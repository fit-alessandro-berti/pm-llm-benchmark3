## Evaluation

The submission matches the required nine-section skeleton, treats POWL as authoritative, separates layout from semantics, plans a primary `POWL → Petri → BPMN` path, and correctly treats process-tree failure as a possible legitimate outcome. That structural competence is real. Under a strict bar, however, precision failures, taxonomy misuse, and incomplete source analysis keep it well below a high score.

### What works

- **Invariant framing** is mostly aligned with the model: register-first, Assess∥Prepare, optional Legal via XOR+tau, join into the loop, binary LOOP with ≥1 `do`, join before Release, Archive finality.
- **Pipeline design** is sound: A as primary, B as stress test with `try/except` and quarantine-on-failure, no invented POWL→BPMN API.
- **Conversion sketch** covers the mandated steps, null-marking checks, diagnostics, and named `save_vis_*` artifacts.
- **Sections 6–9** are the strongest part: layout≠semantics, rejection criteria, canonical POWL/Petri vs derivative BPMN, and residual uncertainty (bounds, tau, BPMN engines) are appropriately cautious.

### Critical flaws

**1. Misuse of “validated preserved” (major methodological error)**  
The allowed terms distinguish `expected preserved` from `validated preserved`. Large parts of §4 mark INV-01/02/04/06/08/09 as **validated** even though no execution evidence is shown—only intended checks. That equates a planned audit with a completed one and contradicts the task’s own warning that returning objects ≠ fidelity. For an unrun proposal, almost every cell should be `expected preserved` / `not tested` / `ambiguous`, not `validated`.

**2. Incomplete source semantics: Legal vs Prepare (real model error)**  
Edges are only `assess → optional_legal` and `assess/prepare → verification_loop`. **Legal does not wait on Prepare.** Valid behavior includes e.g.  
`Register → Assess → Legal → Prepare → Configure → …`  
and Legal overlapping Prepare. The baseline never states Legal∥Prepare; positive witnesses and §5 traces never exercise it. The audit therefore under-specifies the source and cannot catch converters that falsely sequence Prepare before Legal.

**3. Validation suite does not cover stated invariants**  
INV-05 requires orders such as Legal after Configure/Inspect (before Release). §5 positives are mostly “Legal then loop” or “no Legal.” The decisive concurrency witnesses are defined in §1 and omitted from replay—so even a perfect run would not evidence INV-05.

**4. Notation corruption destroys the audit’s precision**  
Throughout tables and prose, critical operators are stripped or blank (`→`, `∥`, `τ`, inequalities, empty `` ``). Pipeline names read as “POWL accepting Petri net BPMN”; footprint claims become unreadable. In a semantic-invariant document this is not cosmetic: several rows cannot be interpreted as written.

**5. API / evidence handwaving**  
- `discover_footprints(net, im, fm)` and reachability-graph tuple indexing are assumed without defense; footprints are primarily log-oriented in PM4Py.  
- Tree column is blanket `conversion failed` without a run—acceptable as expectation, inconsistent with “validated” language elsewhere.  
- Bounded explorer is directionally right (tau-hide, exact FM, anti-cycle) but depends on `petri_utils.fire_transition` and Marking equality details not justified; exhaustiveness disclaimer is good, yet overstated “YES within bound” sits next to incomplete witnesses.

**6. Smaller issues**  
- INV-02/10 “exactly once” / label-uniqueness mix language properties with representation hygiene.  
- INV-09 hedges Archive/FM (“if FM requires Archive”) though the source clearly sequences Archive after Release.  
- True-concurrency vs interleaving is acknowledged, then still paired with strong preservation claims.  
- Some “ambiguous” BPMN cells are fair; several should be `not tested` until footprints/witnesses exist.
