## Critique

### Overall
The submission is unusually thorough, well-structured, and aligned with the required eight-section format. It correctly refuses independent-case simulation, treats calendars and non-stationarity as first-class, keeps service/wait split explicit, treats objections as latent (not observed), avoids illegal PM4Py APIs, and repeatedly disclaims optimality. ### Section-by-section deductions

**1. Model scope (strong, 0.2)**  
Clear boundary, entities, state, queues, calendars, and mandatory cross-case coupling (architect bursts, district batches, latent objection shocks, shared pools). Minor unclarity: “semi-aggregate” pools vs. occasional named-person language; bundle lifecycle/state transitions underspecified.

Correct PM4Py path (`pandas` load  `format_dataframe`  variants/DFG/performance DFG; no `pm4py.read_csv`). Good separation of analytics vs DES.  
Flaws: NHPP × month multipliers anchored to overall average arrival rate without a stated mean-normalization step (volume bias ~30%+ if multipliers average >1); service/wait inversion described at high level but no concrete estimator; building-area / sensitivity proxies left vague.

**3. Assumptions (strong, 0.2)**  
Cleanly factored and honest about missing starts, missing objections, flattened log.  
Minor: D1 “pre-arrival effort KPI” vs cycle-time boundary is fuzzy; appeals “lumped” without a clear generative story; violation flag admitted as partly synthetic then used in hard D2 logic.

**4. Experiment matrix (good, 0.4)**  
B0, D1–D10, packages, and five stresses with trade-off column.  
Gaps: several trade-off cells empty; stress factors not fully crossed with policies; no explicit common-random-number / paired-comparison design column; D5–D9 operational definitions thinner than D1–D4; “expected trade-offs” sometimes speculative rather than mechanism-linked.

**5. Warm-up / replications / uncertainty (strong, 0.3)**  
90-day warm-up, 18–24 months, R30, CRN, MSER/Welch, heavy-tail KPIs, second-order parameter uncertainty.  
Weaknesses: WIP-snapshot initialization asserted without a construction method from the log; CRN described only at seed level (streams not partitioned rigorously); Sobol/Morris named but not tied to concrete parameter ranges; Winsorization caveat slightly muddled.

**6. Validation (strong, 0.2)**

Gap: no quantitative acceptance thresholds agreed a priori beyond loose 10/15% bands; “soft replay” under-specified; partial historical analogues for new policies acknowledged but not designed.

**7. Decision criteria (good, 0.4)**  
Rich metric set (median/tail, SLA by type, fairness, util, rework, backlog, robustness) and sensible filters.  
Flaws: composite formula contains broken notation `0.10·(1rework)` (operator missing); SLA thresholds invented without municipal grounding; “top-3 rank under stress” and “50% of sum of standalone gains” rules are arbitrary; cost model is only a proxy flag; fairness metrics defined but never operationalized into the simulator outputs.

All hard constraints are *verbally* respected. Implementation fidelity lags (especially calendars-in-code, full cross-case engine, and excerpt fidelity), which matters because the skeleton was required to be coherent and faithful, not merely aspirational.

### Summary judgment
Design reasoning, PM grounding, and uncertainty narrative are high quality (roughly 8.5–9.0 material). Code/experiment operationalization, parameter consistency, and several small logical/notational errors prevent a high score under strict grading. Not near-flawless.
