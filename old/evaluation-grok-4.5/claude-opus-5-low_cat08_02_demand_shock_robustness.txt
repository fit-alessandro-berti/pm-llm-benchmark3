## Material flaws (drive the score down)
1. **Broken age-escalation parameter (serious).** R3 promotes Routine at **40h elapsed**. Peak Routine median is already **62h** (normal median 38h). Under peak conditions a majority of Routine would flood the “reserved” pool and compete with Urgent/Complex—i.e., the anti-starvation rule destroys the reservation. Even in normal periods ~half of Routine would escalate. Threshold is mis-calibrated against the supplied medians; the package as specified is not robust.

2. **Urgent 18h objective is not actually engineered end-to-end.** Stated goal is Urgent p90 &lt; 18h. R3 only reserves capacity at **preparation and assay**. Peak medians already show ReceivePre-an 9h and Pre-anPrep 13h (plus registration/courier). Without an Urgent fast lane at courier intake, pre-analytical check, and accession correction—the first binders in S1/S4—prep/assay reservation cannot deliver contractual Urgent performance. Peak Urgent p90 is already 33h; the redesign never closes that gap quantitatively.

3. **Autoverification (R4) and several controls are invented beyond I1–I10** with heavy regulatory/accreditation assumptions. Allowed in spirit as redesign, but the prompt required grounding in supplied candidates; the highest-leverage validation fix is also the least evidenced and introduces a silent quality failure mode the answer underweights relative to its centrality.

4. **Operational fragility vs. its own residual note.** Nine triggered policies with ordering constraints (P3-with-P2, P5-after-P4, paired R2 thresholds, etc.) is acknowledged as a 02:00 failure mode, yet the design still depends on it. That is a self-inflicted brittleness.
