### Critical / high-severity flaws

**1. Wrong process reading of `Damage Assessment Completed → Settlement Decision Recorded` (severe)**  
Labeled “process improbability” / “cannot occur in this fast sequence.” In the given normative model this edge is the *standard* happy path after assessment. The real issues are (a) it is almost never in the `t0` prefix, so it is **future leakage** in an early explanation, and (b) negative SHAP likely marks “clean progression,” not illegality. Inverting possibility vs. leakage is a core PPM mistake.

**2. `Documents Requested → Documents Received` treated as a retainable early signal (severe)**  
At `t0` = immediately after first `Coverage Check Completed`, evidence cycles are generally **not yet enabled**. A full-trace DFG/pattern score is not a prefix feature. Calling it “legitimate process signal” / “seen early” and retaining it contradicts prefix-time availability.

**3. `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` handled too weakly and for the wrong primary reason**  
The strong distinction the task wants: this pattern is **normatively legitimate** after Partial/Deny (unlike `Claim Closed → Damage Assessment`), but still **unavailable at `t0`** and must not be sold as “appeals cause breaches.” The answer mostly scolds causal wording (Medium) and never cleanly separates *semantic legality* from *temporal admissibility*. That is exactly the discrimination quality criteria call out.

**4. Incomplete §1 coverage**  
`final_decision` (Case X, +0.06) never appears in the audit table despite being blatant outcome leakage. Local-only items were required to be covered exhaustively.

**5. Case Y revision conflicts with task norms and its own §3**  
- Drops `claimant_region` solely as fairness-sensitive, against “Do not simply delete every correlated or sensitive feature.” §3 says retain-with-review; §4 deletes it.  
- **`prior_claim_count_before_t0` is ignored** in both credible and removed lists despite §1 calling the lineage High. A repaired count might stay; the broken feature must not silently vanish from the local audit.
