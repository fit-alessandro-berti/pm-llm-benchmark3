## Section-by-section

Critically, later sections do **not** implement Resolution 2: there is no robust emergency detector, no explicit out-of-band authorization handling, and Log Skeleton / DECLARE still behave like a strict “opinion must follow escalation” story without a separated exception path. Choosing a policy and then modeling something else is a material flaw.

### 3. Procedural Backbone — poor

**API / construct problems:**
