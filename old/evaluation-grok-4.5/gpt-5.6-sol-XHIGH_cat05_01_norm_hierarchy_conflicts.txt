## Grading rationale (hypercritical)

### Overall
The submission is unusually thorough, structurally complete, and methodologically self-aware. It follows the required headings, separates facts/assumptions/interpretations, refuses a single total ordering, applies controls at act-time, privileges contemporaneous non-ERP evidence, and refuses automatic cure by later approval. Those are substantial strengths. Under a normal rubric this would sit near the top. Under the mandated hypercritical standard, several recurring clarity, differentiation, and edge-analysis shortfalls prevent a very high score.

### Major strengths (why not lower)

### Deductions (strict)

**1. Rule catalogue trigger column is sloppy (0.35)**  
Triggers are written “Amount  EUR 50,000”, “Amount  EUR 25,000”, “amount  EUR 150,000” with comparison operators dropped. In a formalized catalogue this is an unclarity that forces the reader to reconstruct from pseudo-logic and source text. Pseudo-logic itself is fine; the table is not.

**2. Classification range is artificially compressed (0.40)**  
Seven of eight primaries are NONCOMPLIANT; zero COMPLIANT; zero INDETERMINATE. The method memo reserves INDETERMINATE for “balanced unresolved fact,” then routes pure actor-qualification gaps (P-505/507/508) to NONCOMPLIANT because “missing qualification is not presumed.” That is a defensible stipulation, but it collapses distinct situations:
- affirmative late gate (P-502 Finance/Compliance),
- expired instrument (P-506),
- clear scope failure (P-504),
- unproven role equivalence under D (P-505/507/508).

The last cluster is closer to interpretation-dependent indeterminacy than the first three. Secondary classifications were permitted and would have restored nuance; none appear. Task instruction not to label all deviations noncompliant is only barely satisfied by a single JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.

**3. P-501 under-analyzed on commit authority (0.35)**  
B4 contains two distinct reliefs: (i) commitment may precede PO; (ii) delegated Shift Lead may substitute for an *absent* Plant Manager. Plant Manager was reachable and did not delegate. The answer correctly notes no Shift-Lead-as-Plant path was used, then relies on later Plant_Approve under a “B2 = completion” reading. It does not squarely ask who had authority to *Commit_to_Supplier* at 08:50 with no Plant involvement and pre-ECD. B4’s sequencing relief is not obviously a free-standing grant of commit power to any Shift Lead. JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION remains arguable, but confidence “Medium” and severity “Low” sit on a thinner base than presented. The Section 5 alternative (B2 pre-commit  NONCOMPLIANT) is stronger than the primary write-up admits.

**4. Insufficient differentiation among A2-gap emergency cases (0.30)**  
P-505, P-507, and P-508 are scored nearly identically (NONCOMPLIANT / High / Medium-high) despite material differences:
- P-508 followed D2 exactly (Legal + Compliance + SoD clean + clear line-restoration necessity).
- P-507 had pre-commit Compliance, timely PDF auth record, amount 150k under D1, but zero Finance-shaped event.
- P-505 had late Finance, boundary expiry fight, and no Create_PO in the record.

Treating “D cannot create A2 role-holders” as equally decisive in all three flattens residual risk and the force of D3 (CFO owns Finance interpretation). The alternatives section partially cures this; the primary table does not.

**5. B2 temporal reading is load-bearing and only lightly stress-tested (0.25)**  
The entire P-501 JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION (and part of the local-approval story post-ECD) hangs on reading B2 as lacking pre-commit force because it omits the words A2/A3 use. B5 (“does not expressly waive Finance_Approve”) cuts both ways. The answer flags the ambiguity and puts the contrary reading in alternatives, which is good, but still promotes the lenient reading to primary with limited process-purpose analysis (approval after the company is already bound is a weak control). Acceptable advocacy; slightly thin for “strongest defensible.”

**6. Minor scope/SOP-edge soft spots (0.20)**

**7. Small consistency / presentation nits (0.15)**
