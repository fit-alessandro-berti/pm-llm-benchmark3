Strict grading against the required format, quality bar, analytical task, and “nearly flawless” threshold for high scores. The submission is recognizable and covers the eight headings, but cumulative defects in accuracy, consistency, completeness, precision, and reproducibility are disqualifying under hypercritical review. Minor presentation errors alone would already drop the score; the logical and evidentiary problems compound it.

# 1. Data audit and estimands
Adequate definitions of the opening window, counts, median/p90 service (explicitly not flow time), snapshot WIP, and FTE. Five threats are listed and track the prompt cautions (carry-over, non-identical arrival/completion sets, median  mean flow, complex_pct ambiguity, serial dependence).  
**Deductions:** No explicit units reconciliation (e.g., service in minutes vs. WIP snapshots vs. 12 h day), no labeled assumptions, and the estimands discussion is thin on what can actually be identified from aggregates alone. Still the strongest section.

# 2. Primary regime segmentation
Plausible attempt to isolate pre-mix weekdays, outage, recovery, post-mix, intervention, and weekends; outage/recovery are not collapsed into a “high-throughput” regime. Ranges rather than false point estimates are used.  
**Major defects:**
- Table is corrupted (“p9050”, “WIP falls from 5019”, broken “WIP/  ” strings later). These are not cosmetic; they render cells unreadable.
- Date-range boundaries are written inclusively (e.g., 04-16..04-19, 04-20..04-28, 04-29..05-03) yet the numeric arrival/completion estimates are weekday-only figures that ignore the weekends inside those ranges. The segmentation is therefore not a clean partition and the estimates do not match the stated boundaries.
- Recovery window mixes a clear backlog-clearing weekday with ordinary low-volume weekends without adjustment or note.
- Confidence column is asserted without linking to specific evidence strength or sample size.
- No quantitative derivation of the reported intensity ranges is shown.

One materially different cut is offered and contrasted on simplicity vs. masking of the 04-20–04-28 pressure.

# 5. Burstiness, autocorrelation, and stationarity

The required treatment of serial dependence through backlog is superficial.
