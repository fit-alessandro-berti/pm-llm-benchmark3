## Critical failures (each is independently severe)

### 1. Impossible calendar dates (systemic)
Current time is **Friday 18 September 2026, 15:30**. Monday is therefore **21 Sep**, Tuesday **22 Sep**, etc.

The answer repeatedly uses **“Mon 18 Sep”** as the smooth-path terminal day (scenarios 1–2, forecast summary, earliest completion). That is the same calendar date as “today,” which is Friday. Scenario 4 also invents **“Tue 19 Sep”**, which is chronologically before the alleged Monday finish and inconsistent with the Friday anchor.

This is not a typo in one cell; it is the backbone of the forecast’s wall-clock claims.

### 2. Remaining-hour arithmetic is false
The summary states earliest completion **Mon 12:30 ≈ 21 h from now**, and central **Mon 14:00–17:00 ≈ 22.5–25.5 h**.

Wall-clock from Fri 15:30 → Mon 12:30 is about **69 h**, not 21 h:
- Fri 15:30–24:00 ≈ 8.5 h  
- Sat + Sun ≈ 48 h  
- Mon 00:00–12:30 ≈ 12.5 h  

The answer explicitly says hours are counted from Fri 15:30 to wall-clock finish, so this is not an ambiguous “working hours” reinterpretation. The P80/P90 hour bands are therefore unanchored: a “P50-like” Monday afternoon cannot sit at ~24 elapsed hours.

### 3. Section 6 contradicts the answer’s own join logic
Under the answer’s own smooth-path chemistry finish (**Mon ~10:30**) and no-auth micro readout (**Mon ~08:30–09:30**), the join is already **chemistry-dominated**.

Weekend microbiology authorization that finishes readout **Sunday evening** does **not** advance the parallel join if chemistry still completes Monday mid-morning. The claimed **“~15 h pull-forward of all smooth-path percentiles”** is directionally wrong given the answer’s own bottleneck. The nominated “most forecast-changing next event” is therefore mishandled—the single place where update logic must be sharp.

### 4. Microbiology staffing calendar violated
Scenario 3 allows confirmation readout as early as **Tue 05:00**. Routine microbiologist availability is **Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00**. A continuous incubation ending ~04:30 must wait for staffed readout from **08:00**, not 05:00. That is exactly the kind of calendar error the rubric flags as weak.

### 5. Chemistry Friday dynamics over-claimed and under-modeled
“B-417 cannot be started on Friday” is too absolute. With risk-priority (~45%), B-417 may start after the urgent sample on the second analyst near close; unfinished work would pause at 18:00 and resume Monday—changing residual service, not necessarily “no start.” FIFO with one urgent + three ordinary and two analysts is not seriously queued (service-time overlap, who takes which sample, residual capacity before 18:00). The ±2 h sensitivity claim understates queue-discipline impact on Monday start residual.
