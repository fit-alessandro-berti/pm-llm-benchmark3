## Verdict
An expert-level, unusually complete digital-twin design that satisfies almost every hard constraint (non-stationary demand, cross-case coupling, calendars, latent objections, service/wait separation, no false optimality). Under a hypercritical bar it is clearly not flawless: a few methodological gaps, text–code inconsistencies, and omitted mechanisms from the brief keep it well below a 9+.

## Strengths (why this is still high)

- **Scope & state (§1):** Boundary, entities, concurrent marking for `+(...)`, pending-review join, applicant-as-delay-server, architect bursts, inspection bundles, neighborhood objection shocks, and council-week freezes are all first-class. This is not an independent-case DES.
- **Service vs waiting (§2.3, code):** Explicit refusal to assign inter-completion gaps to service; deconvolution/calibration framing; business-time conversion before fitting; `gap_to_prev` vs observed `service_time_s` in code. Matches the constraint.
- **Objections:** Consistently latent (mixture on notice duration, D9 as value-of-information). Never treated as logged events.
- **PM4Py role:** Correct rejection of `pm4py.read_csv`; `format_dataframe`  descriptive stats  tree/Petri net for conformance only; simulation engine separated. Process-tree limitations (no data/resources/dependencies) are stated clearly.
- **Uncertainty (§5):** Terminating multi-year horizon, WIP snapshot + Welch warm-up, CRN with *per-component* streams, paired CIs, MCB/ranking, calibration posterior, heavy-tail POT/GPD, truncation mass—strong and realistic.
- **Experiments & decisions (§4, §7):** Full D1–D10, combinations, sub-additivity note on D10, stress set, fairness gates, dual clocks (municipal vs total elapsed), risk frontiers for D2/D8.
- **Validation (§6) & closing caveat:** Bidirectional replay idea, temporal hold-out, no publication before B0 validates—appropriate epistemic humility.

## Deductions (hypercritical)

### Medium
1. **Parallel-branch enablement vs waiting inference**  
   `add_timing_columns` and the narrative treat waiting as “previous completion  start.” Under the `+` block (Planning  Fire  Environmental), enablement is the join entry time, not log-order predecessor. Performance-DFG edges and calibrated waits for concurrent reviews are therefore biased. A digital twin aimed at D5/D8/legal–environmental interaction needed an explicit enablement clock from the process-tree marking. Not discussed.

2. **Text vs code on uncertainty propagation**  
   §5.4 promises ~200 posterior draws with combined credible intervals; `ExperimentRunner` does `params_posterior[rep % len(...)]` over 30 reps—confounding MC error with parameter uncertainty and undersampling the posterior. The best part of the design is not what the skeleton actually runs.

3. **Mechanisms present in the brief but thin or missing in the model**  
   - Inspector **no-shows** (“travel and no-shows matter”)—not a modeled failure/retry mode.  
   - **Applicant appeal** after decision/legal—inside the stated boundary and in the rework list, but absent from tree, routing, and experiments.  
   - Council meetings also affect **Public-notice period** (brief); model emphasizes legal outages far more than notice freezes.

### Minor–medium
4. **PM4Py API slips / practicality**  
   §2.1 writes `pm4py.get_case_arrival_avg` (code correctly uses `get_case_arrival_average`). `get_variants_as_tuples` is version-fragile. Full-log `conformance_diagnostics_alignments` on 31.2k cases is not flagged as needing sampling/filtering—unrealistic as written.

5. **Calendar / capacity edge cases**  
   Environmental 40% reservation: excellent note that 1.8 FTE  preemptive blocks for tails, but implementation remains `NotImplementedError` with no sketch of the reservation schedule. Vacation/sick 10–15% is a free parameter without log cross-check plan beyond utilization consistency.

6. **Experiment matrix vs runner**  
   Matrix is rich (E1–E12, S1–S6, fractional D10); `main()` only wires a subset and does not show stress×intervention crossings or the 2 fractional design. Acceptable for a skeleton, but weakens “experiment execution” coherence.

### Minor
7. **Format:** Extra H1 title before the mandated `1. Model scope...` sections (required: exactly those top-level sections).
8. **Operational twin loop:** Prospective monthly scoring is mentioned; little on how the twin is recalibrated from live portal/HR feeds or who owns parameter drift—secondary for this prompt but thin for a municipal “digital twin.”
9. **Slight overclaim tone in places:** e.g. seeding lognormal from median/p90 via ` = ln(p90/median)/1.2816` is fine as a seed, but presented without noting that empirical p90 is contaminated by residual queueing when starts are missing (they know this elsewhere; local inconsistency).
