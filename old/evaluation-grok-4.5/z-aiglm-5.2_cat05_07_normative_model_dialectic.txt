### What is strong

### Material defects (drive the score down hard)

**1. Broken revised POWL semantics (major — §7 is a scored deliverable)**  
M1 forces `checks = (Sanctions  Quality)` *before* `decision` for every path:

- PD-3 auto path and FD-7 emergency path both allow **Quality after provisional activation**.
- Observed conforming overlay traces (e.g. SUP-702, SUP-704) are therefore **not language of M1**.
- Requirement text asked specifically for standard concurrent checks, a low-risk auto route, and an emergency/provisional route. Encoding “quality always before XOR” collapses those routes into a stricter CS-9-only skeleton and does **not** state that limitation; it hides it.
- Auto branch is only `Auto_Approve  Activate` with no post-activation quality in-branch; emergency uses nested retros but still cannot fire until pre-decision Quality completed — internally inconsistent with FD-7/PD-3 and with the brief’s own case judgments (702/704 as justified).

That is a core model-semantics failure, not a nit. A defensible approximation would put Sanctions early, park Quality inside the standard branch (and/or as an optional/post node on auto/emergency), or use explicit silent/optional quality before XOR and document the gap. None of that was done.

**2. Over-confident noncompliance on thin rules**  
- SUP-715 as NONCOMPLIANT: record says no explicit spousal ban; only missing COI *disclosure*. Medium confidence is noted, but dominant label should be closer to MIXED / policy-gap with disclosure failure — treating it like 712 (hard CS-9 same-person rule) overreaches.
- SUP-711 as DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE: same `source_transaction_id` already proves duplicate registration; once deduped the case is determined COMPLIANT. “Indeterminate” is the wrong bucket.

**3. Smaller precision issues**  
- Emergency model invents `Activate_Supplier_Provisional` / retrospective labels without a mapping story to logged `Activate_Supplier` (704/705).  
- Daily monitoring (FD-7 collateral) is absent from both POWL and the overlay enforcement sketch.  
- Prosecution brief is slightly weaker than defense on quantifying how much of the “high deviation rate” is logging vs real breach (aggregates are cited but not weighed).  
- LOOP+XOR RMI approximation is acceptable but never stress-tested against “RMI after partial checks” practice.
