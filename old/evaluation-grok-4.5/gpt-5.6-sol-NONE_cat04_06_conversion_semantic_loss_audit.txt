### Defects and penalties

Section 2 and especially Section 4 mangle required structure (`POWL  accepting Petri net`, `POWLPetri`, `PetriBPMN`, `PetriTree`). The prompt asked for a clean Markdown document with specified headings and readable audit tables. This is sloppy relative to the care shown elsewhere.

**2. Incomplete / slightly inaccurate concurrency invariants**  
Source edges give:
- `optional_legal` only after `assess_risk` (not after `prepare_evidence`);
- `verification_loop` after both `assess_risk` and `prepare_evidence`;
- no order between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`.

Thus after Assess, Legal  Prepare is allowed (e.g. `R,A,L,P,C,I,E,H`), and Legal may start before Prepare finishes. S4/S6 and the witness lists emphasize Legal vs verification and A/P order, but never crystallize LegalPrepare or “Legal does not wait for Prepare” as first-class invariants with dedicated ± witnesses. That is a semantic gap in the baseline the rest of the audit depends on.

**3. Witness set gaps**  
Positive list omits important legal interleavings (`R,A,L,P,…`, `R,A,L,C,…` patterns where legal starts before prepare/verification is fully ready). Negative list is stronger than positive coverage.
