## Material defects (drive the score down)

### 1. S1 busy-time / utilization error (serious)
Direct enumeration of all S1 intervals that must count under A10 (including P21’s 30 min elapsed open service and midpoint imputations for P07/P15) yields:

- Known completed service sum = 505 min  
- + P07 mid 17.5 + P15 mid 17.5 + P21 elapsed 30  **central  570 min** (79%)  
- Optimistic (0+0+30)  535; pessimistic (35+35+30)  605  

The written tables and sensitivity block repeatedly claim **510–580, central ~545 (76%)**. That figure matches the calculation **only if open elapsed service on P21 is omitted**, contradicting A10 (“Busy time includes elapsed portions of open work”) and the code path that sets `s1_end_busy = 14:00` for OPEN. S2 numbers are closer to correct (opens appear to have been included), so treatment is inconsistent across stations. Utilization is a headline station-level metric; publishing an internally contradictory value is a major flaw.

### 2. Text vs code divergence on the same quantities
- Section 4/6 busy minutes  what the supplied code computes when run.  
- Two-hour arrival counts: text puts 14 external arrivals in 08:00–10:00 (includes 10:00); code uses half-open `[lo, hi)` and therefore moves P14 (and the 10:00 S1 completion) into the next bin. The narrative and the “reproducible” artifact do not agree.  
A reproducibility section that does not reproduce the reported numbers fails its own standard.

### 3. Little’s-law cohort arithmetic
Listed sojourns sum to **2050**, not 2055. Consequently \(\bar L = 2050/350 \approx 5.86\), mean sojourn \(\approx 146.4\), not the stated 5.87 / 146.8. The identity still holds, but the published figures are wrong by a full case-five-minutes. Sloppy on a check whose only purpose is exact accounting.

### 4. Secondary precision / consistency nits
- Mean wait/service “about 70–73 / 25–29 / 39–42 / 33–37” are never derived transparently from the case table; given the busy-time error one cannot trust they were computed under a single coherent scenario.  
- Zero-duration service is correctly flagged as operationally extreme yet still enters the optimistic busy bound without a more realistic floor (e.g., 5 min after rounding).  
- Peak-WIP and time-weighted means are asserted (“roughly 4.3”, “peak 7”) with no displayed event-ordered trajectory in the narrative; the code computes them, but the prose does not let a reader verify.  
- A2 ordering convention is reasonable, yet several boundary contacts (P07/P05, P09/P07, P09/P10, etc.) are never stress-tested under the opposite rounding direction (±2.5 min), even though A3 acknowledges the issue.
