## Evaluation

The submission follows the required heading structure, shows real process awareness (act-time application, ECD window, ON-44 evidence weighting, non-curative late approvals), and gets several hard calls roughly right (P-501 pre-ECD, P-502 Compliance, P-504 out-of-scope, P-506 expired directive). It is not close to flawless. Under strict scoring, a central interpretive contradiction plus repeated threshold/SoD mistakes cap it well below the high band.

### Critical failures (consistency and logic)

**1. CFO email vs GPIP-4 A2 is self-refuting.**  
§2 states Finance: “GPIP-4 A2 > CFO email (email can interpret but not repeal)” and that the email “Probably cannot override” segregation/Compliance—and by the same table logic should not repeal mandatory dual pre-commit Finance. §3 R3 then encodes `CFO email 150k` as an exception to pre-commit Finance. §4 uses that exception to drive **JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION** for P-505 and P-507, while P-508 is **NONCOMPLIANT** because amount exceeds EUR 150,000. That is not nuanced layering; it is two incompatible hierarchies. For consistency this alone is disqualifying for any score near the top.

**2. GPIP-4 A2 is collapsed to “Finance only.”**  
A2 requires *both* an independent budget owner *and* a Finance Controller before commitment. Organizational facts state Incident Command are not clearly budget owners. The answer repeatedly treats joint IC authorization (plus CFO gloss) as curing A2, marks R2 “satisfied (uncertain)” on P-505, and never cleanly tests independence of the business approver on P-502/503/506/507/508. That is a systematic misread of the controlling global control, not a minor omission.

**3. Formal catalogue is broken on its face.**  
- R1 behavior reads “Committer final approver” rather than the prohibition that committer/requester must not be final business approver.  
- Triggers lose inequality marks (`50 k`, `Local 25 k`) and pseudo-logic is operator-garbled (`amount(X)  50k`, `new_supplier(X)  amount(X) > 100k`), so OR vs AND for A3 is unrecoverable from the text.  
- `compliant(X) :- ¬violation(X,_)` ignores the paper’s own JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION path.  
A “formalized rule catalogue” that cannot be executed even defeasibly fails the assignment’s formalization demand.
