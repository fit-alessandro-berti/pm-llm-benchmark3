### Major deductions

**1. Pilot power / stratum arithmetic is wrong (material)**  
Section 7 states the “digital non-legal stratum  1,292 cases per six months  108/week” and “~650 per arm over 6 weeks.”  
- 1,292 is \(0.38 \times 3400\) = **all no-originals**, including legal-required cases.  
- Wave 1 is defined as no-originals **and** non-legal. Without an independence/correlation assumption this is not 1,292; a neutral product is \(\approx 0.38 \times 0.60 \approx 23\%\) (\(\sim\)775/six months). Section 9 correctly says “20–25% after” legal exclusion; section 7 contradicts that.  
- Even on the author’s own 108/week figure: \(108 \times 6 = 648\) **total**, not ~650 **per arm**. Per-arm support is ~324. The “detects roughly a 3–4 h median shift at 80% power” claim is therefore unfounded.  
This is not cosmetic: the evaluation rubric explicitly weights pilot design and quantitative scenario analysis.

**2. Site B POWL/legal–supplement loop is incomplete (model fidelity)**  
Site B rule 7 and the DFG require: `Request Supplement`  document work  **re-Assessment**  legal again.  
The code builds  
`LOOP(Legal Review, Request Supplement  Document Check (supplement))`  
with `Assessment` **outside** the loop. Playout will not generate the real rework cycle.  
The prose intervention describes re-assessment; the executable model does not. That is a direct hit on “model fidelity” and on “POWL … must preserve the stated dependencies.” Nested `document_block` vs separate `redo_documents` also splits one activity into two disconnected paths without a clean join back to `Assessment`.

**3. Cross-section inconsistency on first-wave eligibility**  
- §5 S2 / several scenarios: eligible share **38%** for digital-only.  
- §6 Wave 1: intersection of no-originals **and** non-legal **and** low/medium complexity.

A transport answer that lives on stratification cannot blur the primary stratum size across sections.
