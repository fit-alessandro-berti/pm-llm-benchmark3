### Defects that lower the score

**1. Rework/origin attribution is analytically wrong (substantive)**  
Code takes `shift(1)` before `Rework Correction` and treats that as the originating event. In this process the prior event is often Quality Check (or another downstream step), not Document Review / Risk Classification / Eligibility Decision where the error was made. The comment claims “same case/family”; the code does not filter by family or walk back to the likely fault activity. Downstream models of “origin WIP / position / resource” built on this will mis-attribute. This is the largest single flaw in §3.

**2. “Matching” is mislabeled and fragile**  
```python
early.merge(late, on=key)  groupby mean of pairwise diffs
```
Algebraically `E[LE] = E[L]E[E]` within stratum, so the reported mean difference is fine, but (a) it is not coarsened exact matching, (b) it detonates into a Cartesian product, (c) it does not balance or match units. Presenting this as CEM overstates rigor.

**3. Main regression specifies near-collinear regressors without comment**  
`sequence_position_within_shift` and `minutes_since_shift_start` in one OLS, plus `hour` and `shift_type`, without VIF/partialling discussion or a clear horse-race design in the primary model. Elsewhere the answer knows these are confounded; the flagship spec ignores it. Coefficients on “position” and “fatigue clock” from that model are not interpretable as written.

**4. Over-absolute fatigue claim**  
“Fatigue alone cannot produce this: fatigued people are slower” / “fatigue normally slows people down, not speeds them up.” Directionally fair for classic cognitive fatigue, but too categorical: end-of-shift rushing, deadline effects, and speed–accuracy tradeoffs under load are well documented. The data still favor pressure over pure fatigue; the wording overclaims.

**5. Instrument story is oversold**  
Upstream arrival rate is better than own WIP but not cleanly exogenous to case-mix (busy windows often differ in complexity/priority/urgency). Calling it an instrument without that caveat is sloppy relative to the care shown elsewhere.
