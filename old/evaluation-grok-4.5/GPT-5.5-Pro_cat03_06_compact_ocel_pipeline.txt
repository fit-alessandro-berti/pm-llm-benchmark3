### Material defects (score drivers)

1. **`relation_origin` is left inside the live `relations` frame passed to `OCEL(...)`**  
   The audit column is a good human idea, but it is not stripped before construction. That violates the spirit of “mandatory OCEL columns” hygiene and is a real executability/schema risk for PM4Py internals and downstream discovery. The pipeline should build `relations_ocel = relations[mandatory_cols]` (or drop the audit col) and only then construct `OCEL`. This alone blocks a high score.

2. **Provenance claim is only half-true**  
   Section 1 claims the audit column prevents confusing source facts with enrichment. Algorithms still consume enriched rows as ordinary E2O facts; only a human reader of the extra column can tell. Discovery (flattening, OC-DFG, OCPN, interactions) therefore *does* treat lineage enrichment as observation. That undercuts the stated governance story and should have been stated bluntly.

3. **`completes_settlement` for M2 overreaches the narrative**  
   The process text calls M1 *and* M2 “partial payments settling V1.” Coding M2 as completion is a defensible *assumption*, not a stated fact. O2O should have used `partially_settles` for both, or the assumption should have been flagged as interpretive. Small semantic slip, but this task is about semantic fidelity.

4. **“Complete analysis pipeline” stops at `store`**  
   H/I discover DFGs, skeletons, OC-DFG, OTG, ET–OT, OCPN, and interactions, then never print, assert shape, or sanity-check them. For an “executable-looking analysis pipeline,” at least compact summaries (lens of cases, edge counts, interaction rows for P1/V1/O100/O200) should appear. As written, half the analysis is dead code plus prose.

5. **Short / single-case flattening hazards are under-discussed**  
   Invoice is one case (`V1`); Payment flattening is correctly called weak, but Invoice log-skeleton / DFG degeneracy (one trace, artificial `Record Payment  Record Payment` “loop”) is not treated as a methodological failure mode with the same sharpness. Delivery/Package traces are also extremely short; skeleton value is marginal.

6. **Enrichment policy is uneven in ways that affect Section 4 predictions**  
   Confirm Delivery and Dispatch get broad Order/Item/Package lineage links, while Pack P2O100 is marked enriched and Pack P1Orders is “source_stated” only because the NL sentence says so. That is internally documented, but the flattened Order DFG edges advertised in Section 4 are then *policy artifacts*, not robust business facts. The write-up should stress more loudly that those double Pack/Dispatch/Confirm paths on O100 disappear under source-minimal E2O.

7. **Minor quality nits (accumulate under strict grading)**  
   - Awkward qualifier vocabulary (`receiving_order`, mixed noun/verb forms).  
   - Narrative `source_event` strings bloating `events` with no analytical use.

   - OTG prose blurs event-co-occurrence vs O2O paths (Payment–Order).
