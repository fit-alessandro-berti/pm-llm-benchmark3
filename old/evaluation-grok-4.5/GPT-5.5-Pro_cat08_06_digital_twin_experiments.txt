## Evaluation

Strong design document with clear structure, good PM grounding in prose, and a mostly compliant skeleton. It is **not** near-flawless under strict reading: several coherence, correctness, and operationalization gaps are material.

### Strengths
- Sections 1–7 match the required template and hit the hard constraints: non-stationary demand, calendars, rework, latent objections (not treated as observed), service/wait separation, cross-case dependence, no false optimality.
- Entity/state design, estimation plan, assumptions, experiment matrix (incl. stress/combined), validation, and multi-criteria decision rules are unusually complete and realistic.
- Python side correctly avoids `pm4py.read_csv`, uses `pandas` + `pm4py.format_dataframe`, attempts process-tree parse with fallback, and separates PM4Py diagnostics from a simulation layer.
- Explicit handling of missing start times (NaN service/wait rather than treating inter-event time as service) is correct and well explained.

### Major issues (score-driving)
1. **Skeleton vs. claimed DES is incoherent.** Prose insists on calendar-aware, resource-constrained DES with queues, AND-joins, batching, and objection clusters. `PermitDigitalTwinSimulator.run()` is a sequential path builder that accumulates hours and **never** seizes resources, checks calendars, maintains queues, synchronizes parallel reviews, or applies batching. Calendars/pools are built then ignored. That undercuts “do not ignore resource calendars” and “not independent-case only” at the implementation level, even for a skeleton—stubs should still show event/calendar/queue hooks.
2. **D2 logic bug.** `needs_inspection` requires `case.prior_violations is False`, but `prior_violations` defaults to `None` and is never sampled  low-risk skip **never fires**. Intervention D2/D10 are dead in code.
3. **Cross-case machinery missing from code.** `InspectionBundle` / `NeighborhoodObjectionCluster` appear in §1 only. Arrivals lack top-20 burst structure (31% share); batching flags unused; related-case coupling not represented in the engine API.
4. **Experiment matrix  executable scenarios.** E11–E13, S4–S5 (and much of D5/D6/D8) are specified in §4 but absent or inert in `build_scenarios` / `run()`, so the “experiment matrix” cannot support the decision criteria as written.

### Moderate issues
- **Parallel `+()` branch and decision-drafting join** described correctly in text; skeleton runs fire/env/public-notice sequentially with no join state—core control-flow fidelity gap.
- **Performance DFG** used as a diagnostic without stating it mixes wait+service and is a weak calibration target given missing starts (elsewhere they are careful; here they are not).
- **PM4Py API brittleness:** `parse_process_tree` on this exact string, `get_variants`, and related calls are version-sensitive; fallback helps parse only, not diagnostics.
- **D4 “peak-only” inspector** permanently bumps FTE with a comment; not time-varying as claimed.
- **CRN** discussed at scenario level; no multi-stream design (arrivals/services/routing/objections) for proper paired comparisons.
- Decision thresholds (e.g., 5–10% median, 10–15% p90) are arbitrary with thin municipal justification; acceptable as heuristics but over-precise relative to uncalibrated model.
- Compute feasibility of 300–500 reps × multi-year calendar DES with bundling is ignored.

### Minor issues
- Rework operators in the given tree are accepted without reconciling “Completeness rework” / “Inspection rework” as redo activities vs. real loop semantics.
- Applicant pre-submission delay under D1 is parameterized in `Scenario` but not applied in `run()`.
- Utilization/queue summaries returned empty/decorative while §6–7 depend on them.
- Stress S2 legal calendar is a no-op scenario object.

### Rubric-oriented summary
| Dimension | Strict read |
|-----------|-------------|
| Simulation-design reasoning | High in prose; weak operationalization |
| Process-mining grounding | Strong; small DFG/service-wait slip |
| Experiment planning | Excellent table; incomplete/broken execution path |
| Uncertainty treatment | Strong (warm-up, CRN, tails, sensitivity) |
| Realism of assumptions | Strong, especially missing data / latent objections |
| Code plausibility / PM4Py | Compliant loading/diagnostics; DES core and D2 bug hurt |

### Verdict
A high-quality advisory design that would score in the high 8s–9s if the skeleton wired calendars, queues, joins, and dependencies even as stubs and if D2/matrix parity were fixed. Under hypercritical grading—material engine/design mismatch, a disabling intervention bug, and non-executable parts of the experiment plan—**7.5/10**.
