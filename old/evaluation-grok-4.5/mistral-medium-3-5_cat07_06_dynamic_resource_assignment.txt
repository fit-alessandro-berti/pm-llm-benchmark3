However, under hypercritical review it contains numerous material inaccuracies, internal contradictions, data-table misreads, hard-constraint violations, and incomplete logic. These are not minor polish issues; they undermine the core deliverables (policy soundness and the concrete assignment table). Only a low score is defensible.

Consequently the stated algorithm cannot generate the recommendations of Section 3, destroying traceability and explainability.
