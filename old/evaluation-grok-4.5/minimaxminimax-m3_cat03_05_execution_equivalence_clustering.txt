### Critical flaw: preferred equivalence is self-contradictory

Section 1.3 defines \(X \approx_{BS} Y\) as sharing a lifecycle motif that is the **conjunction** of (a) the full `_TS` canonical signature, (b) external-flag multisets, (c) O2O qualifier multisets, and (d) Pack/Dispatch pairing shape. Conjunction with (a) requires identical `_TS` signatures.

Section 3 then places into the same preferred `_BS` cluster:
- **X3 and X4** — different `_TS` signatures (Dispatch multiplicity, Payment multiplicity, interleaving);
- **X5 and X6** — different `_TS` signatures (EXT attached to logistics vs finance events);
- **X7 and X8** — radically different signatures (qualifier-only deviation vs new Return/Inspect activities).

Section 5 explicitly admits the coarsening (“partial-payment qualifier is treated as a within-cluster variant”). The formal definition and the operational clustering are inconsistent. This is not a borderline ambiguity; it is a contradiction at the core of the “preferred” relation the rest of the answer depends on. A correct write-up would define `_BS` as an explicit abstraction / quotient of the signature (e.g., projection onto motif features), not as conjunction with full `_TS` equality.
