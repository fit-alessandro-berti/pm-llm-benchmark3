### §3 Procedural backbone — good with flaws
- Clear disclosure that clarifications are block-structured only before `Classify Risk` and that post-activation bank-change behaviour is excluded.  
- Fresh leaf instances for repeated labels show awareness of process-tree parent constraints.  
**Flaws:**
1. `Operator.LOOP` is constructed with **three** children (`body, tau(), tau()`). Standard PM4Py process-tree loops are binary `(do, redo)`; ternary exit children are non-portable / version-dependent. This is an executable-model defect.
2. `zero_or_more` via `xor(tau, loop(...))` is a reasonable encoding but interacts poorly with the silent exit child, risking empty or non-terminating interpretations depending on the PM4Py version.
3. The reject path permits `Sanctions Escalation` *after* `Reject Vendor`. While R7 (after screening) still holds, the skeleton later asserts `always_before(Classify Risk, Sanctions Escalation)`, creating a silent inconsistency between layers that is never flagged because §9 is missing.
4. Parallel placement of the legal detour with `Manager Approval` / `EDD` is defensible for R5 but makes the “one modeled detour” claim fragile under repeated escalations (delegated to an external validator that is only partially specified).

### §4 DECLARE — solid
Templates are used correctly; response vs. altresponse vs. precedence vs. noncoexistence are distinguished; unconditional `precedence(Obtain Legal Opinion, Activate Vendor)` and `exactly_one` over the two approvals are rightly avoided.  
Minor issues: every confidence is 1.0 with no discussion of vacuity; `succession(Activate, Release)` is stronger than the backbone’s “release follows activation” for incomplete cases; no `absence`/`exactly_one` on terminal activities beyond what the skeleton later adds.

### §5 Log skeleton — material over-constraints
Defensible choices (empty `directly_follows`, frequency sets for at-most-once, never-together pairs) are mixed with unjustified strengthenings:
- `always_before(Sanctions Escalation, Obtain Legal Opinion)` forces every opinion to be preceded by an escalation. **R4 is only the opposite direction.** This rejects traces the DECLARE `response` and the process tree both accept.
- `always_before(Classify Risk, Sanctions Escalation)` exceeds R7 (only after screening). The text calls the tree placement “conservative,” yet encoding the same conservatism in the skeleton turns an approximation into a hard conformance failure.
- `equivalence(Activate Vendor, Release First Payment)` is reasonable only for complete cases; the answer notes the limitation but still emits the constraint without a completeness guard.
- Orientation commentary is present but never reconciled with the DECLARE/procedural disagreements above (again, §9 is missing).

### §6 Temporal profile — correct
Seconds conversion, `zeta = 2.0`, and the “warning not violation” rationale are accurate. Lower-bound truncation at zero is sensible. No deductions beyond the absence of any link to the missing test matrix.

### §7 Resource/data checks — strong executable sketch

Nits:
