**1. Likely violation of the compliance first-wave constraint**

The recommended Bottleneck portfolio (A1, A4, A5, A6) is framed almost entirely as throughput/sync labor relief. The answer never maps the recommended set to this mandatory constraint. That is a direct requirements miss.

**2. Weak use of process-mining evidence**  
Section 4 is generic (“significant reduction,” “lower delays”) and barely cites the supplied metrics or DFG edges. Missing or underused facts include:
- Resolve entity mismatch: **31h** median wait, **38%** exception rate, critical path in **82%** of cases when present; loops `KYC → mismatch → KYC` (1750/1160).

Without these anchors, “process-level thinking” is asserted rather than demonstrated.

**3. Candidate matrix is ordered, not ranked, and thinly justified**  
The table lists A1–A12 in ID order rather than a defended priority rank. Ratings are repetitive Medium/High with little differentiation tied to waiting time, rework, pool size, or DFG criticality. **4. Resource-network and demand-shift analysis is shallow**
