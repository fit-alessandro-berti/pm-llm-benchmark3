## Section-by-section flaws

**#1 (Estimands)**  
Fatal definitional errors.  
- Estimand 1 is written as a conditional probability yet the contribution rules treat open cases >72 h as ordinary right-censoring at 72. An open case at 92 h is an *observed* failure to resolve by the SLA; it is not censored.  
- Estimand 3 inverts the contribution of a late resolution: S03 (resolved at 76 h) is declared “not open at 72 h”. By definition of calendar time, any case whose first terminal event occurs at 76 h *was still open* at hour 72. The stated mapping is simply false.
