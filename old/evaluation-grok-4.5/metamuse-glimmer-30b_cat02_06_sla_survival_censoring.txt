## Critical failures (major score cuts)

### 1. Estimands are under-defined and the required case contributions are incomplete
The task requires defining **at least two** useful estimands and stating **how each of four archetypal cases contributes under each estimand** (resolved 76h, open 92h, withdrawn 27h, transferred 68h).

What is written for E1/E2 is directionally right but not rigorous enough:
- E1 is called “cumulative incidence of resolution by 72h” but never written as a probability \(F_1(72)\) / \(1-F_1(72)\), nor tied cleanly to “breach.”
- E2 mixes “pending at 72” with “any terminal disposition” without a clean survival function or CIF definition.
- Contributions are narrative one-liners; they do **not** systematically state, for each estimand: risk-set membership at 72, event/competing/censor status, and whether the case is a known breach, known non-breach, or still uncertain.
- S03 at 76h under E1 is described as “contributes to risk set up to 76h, not counted as resolved by 72h” — true for continuous-time CIF/KM machinery, but the answer never states the operational implication: this is an **already-observed late resolution / SLA miss** for “not resolved by 72,” which the task explicitly wants distinguished from censoring and from open cases.

The four example statuses are also only partly grounded in IDs (S03/S09/S06/S08 are fine) but the write-up does not cleanly separate **observation** vs **estimand contribution**.
