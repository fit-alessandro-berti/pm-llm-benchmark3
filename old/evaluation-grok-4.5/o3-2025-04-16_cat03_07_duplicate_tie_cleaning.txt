### Critical defects

**1. Section 6 fails the PM4Py contract (largest single penalty).**  
Required items are missing or only named in prose:

- No `pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates` in any justified path.
- No `pm4py.ocel_add_index_based_timedelta` *after* order is fixed.
- No pre-check that materializes candidate duplicate *groups* before library calls.
- No explicit warning on the aggressive `ocel_merge_duplicates` path.
- No concrete preservation of `original_timestamp`, `original_activity`, `source`, `source_event_key` on the event table before mutation.
- `order_index` is never computed, yet the code sorts by it.
- The OCEL is treated as `ocel["events"][eid]` dicts and a mutable relation set. Real PM4Py OCELs are dataframe-centric (`events` / `objects` / `relations`). The helper would not run on a normal `ocel_raw`.
- Imports and call paths (`duplication.ocel_merge_duplicates`, `ordering.ocel_sort_by_additional_column`, `check_ocel_integrity`, `custom_semantic`) are largely invented. “Executable-looking” and “PM4Py-aware” are not satisfied.

**2. Aggressive merge mechanics are factually wrong.**  
With `same_activity=True`, **e10 (`Handover to Carrier`) does not merge with e08/e09 (`Dispatch Package`)**.
