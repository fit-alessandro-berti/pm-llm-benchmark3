## Deductions (strict)

**1. Required format not met (0.6)**  
Spec demanded *exactly* the eight top-level `# 1 …`–`# 8 …` headings. The answer adds a title and “Key Insight,” and uses `##` throughout. That is a direct format miss.

**2. PM4Py plan is partly wrong as code (0.8)**  
Labeled as runnable-style code, but several calls are incorrect or misleading:
- `filter_variants` is used like a path/activity filter with mismatched kwargs; that is not how PM4Py variant filtering works.
- `insert_case_service_waiting_time` in a loop with no assignment/use.
- `filter_case_performance` / `filter_paths_performance` signatures and semantics are hand-wavy.
- Comment claims a log-skeleton/DECLARE model can enforce “Accommodation Review  disability_accommodation_requested”; that flag is a *case attribute*, not an activity constraint discoverable from the control-flow skeleton alone without a joined attribute check.

Credit for “pseudocode” latitude is limited because the section presents concrete `pm4py.*` calls and overclaims what they show.

**3. Imprecise group language (0.3)**  
Repeated “minority groups” for B/C/D. D is defined by risk/prior cases, not minority status. Sloppy in a fairness memo.

**4. Bottom line a bit stronger than the evidence frame (0.4)**  
Opens with “**is** procedurally unfair,” while §5 documents biased controls, unobserved workload, and unmeasured dignity/explanation quality. The later softening helps, but the mandated choice set includes **indeterminate**; given those limits, a headline of *indeterminate with strong implementation concerns* (or equivalent) would be better calibrated than a categorical “unfair.”

**5. Smaller analytical nits (0.2)**  
- Adjusted gaps are used well, but uncertainty (variance, small cells, multiple comparisons) is thin outside a monitoring bullet.
- “More than three times” on cycle times is fine numerically but slightly rhetorical.
- Resource “fairness” leans heavily on headcount concentration without clarifying exposure-normalized workload (cases per FTE).

## What would push toward 9–10
Exact heading contract; PM4Py as honest pseudocode *or* API-correct scripts with attribute-aware checks; no mislabeling of groups; bottom line explicitly in {fair, unfair, indeterminate} with weight on identification limits; tighter uncertainty language.

## Floor (why not lower)
Core fairness logic is coherent, table-grounded, and aligned with the scenario’s hardest instructions. Weaknesses are mostly precision/format/tooling, not a collapse into raw-parity-as-bias or “delete Language Support.”
