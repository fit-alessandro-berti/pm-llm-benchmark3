### Strict critique

The submission is structurally complete and demonstrates solid high-level simulation-design literacy (non-stationarity, calendars, latent objections, multi-criteria decisions, uncertainty quantification). Under the required hypercritical standard, however, multiple concrete failures against the stated checklist, internal inconsistencies, over-precise ungrounded claims, and PM4Py/code deficiencies prevent any high score.

#### Section 1 (Scope)
Adequate boundary, entities, and cross-case dependencies (architect bursts, district bundling, shared objections).  
**Flaws:** Introduces “withdrawal” and an unmotivated `urgency_score` not present in the process or log. Parallel-branch synchronization / join state for `+(Planning, Fire?, Env?)` before Decision drafting is never made an explicit state variable, yet this is central to the given process tree and to several interventions. Cross-case state is described narratively but not operationalized as simulatable state.

#### Section 2 (Parameter estimation)
Correct separation of PM4Py (descriptive) from a DES engine and correct recognition that start timestamps are often missing.  
**Flaws:**  
- Claims both EM/regression imputation and (later) crude median-subtraction; inconsistent.  
- “Kernel density estimation on timestamps” is a weak/inappropriate primary method for non-stationary Poisson intensities; count-based or GLM/time-of-week/season models are standard.  
- No concrete plan for using the supplied 18-month summary tables, variant shares, or resource calendars when the log is incomplete.  
- PM4Py function list is partly aspirational (`get_variants_as_dataframe`, clean `case_statistics.get_case_duration` path).

#### Section 3 (Assumptions)
Properly factored and correctly treats objections as latent (mixture on public-notice duration) rather than observed. Applicant responses separated from municipal service times.  
**Flaws:** Direct contradiction with Section 2 on missing starts (EM vs. “subtract median service time”). The latter collapses service/wait without a rigorous limitation statement. Architect and spatial dependence assumptions are simplistic (fixed 48 h delay) relative to the problem statement.

#### Section 4 (Experiment matrix)
Covers baseline, D1–D10, the required combined policy, and three stress scenarios with a readable table.  
**Flaws:** Fabricates precise effect sizes (“-30 %”, “-45 %”, “+15 %”, “cuts volume by 35 %”, etc.) with no calibration or caveat. This violates the spirit of “do not claim exact optimality / uncalibrated results” and reduces credibility of the trade-off column. Factor levels for several Ds are under-specified.

#### Section 5 (Warm-up / uncertainty)
30 replications, 2-year runs, antithetic variates, bootstrap CIs for tails, Morris + Sobol are excellent on paper.  
**Flaws:** 90-day warm-up is short given Complex-variance p90  260 d and seasonal structure; long-tail and council-calendar states will not have stabilized. No discussion of common random numbers across policies for paired comparisons. Heavy-tail handling is declared but not linked to any specific generation or truncation policy inside the engine.

#### Section 6 (Validation)
All six required validation types are named and the techniques (token replay, KS, utilization vs HR, out-of-sample year split) are appropriate.  
**Flaws:** Remains checklist-level; no concrete acceptance thresholds or what happens if replay fitness is low because of the known log gaps (missing starts, missing objections, flattened inter-case dependence).

#### Section 7 (Decision criteria)
Multi-metric, includes median/tail, SLA by type, fairness, utilization band, rework, backlog, robustness.  
**Flaws:** Absolute day thresholds ( 30 / 60 / 90 / 150) are presented as hard targets without anchoring to the supplied empirical medians/p90s or statutory sources. Utilization 75–85 % band is reasonable but arbitrary.

#### Section 8 (Python skeleton) — largest deductions
Explicit requirements failed:
- Does **not** compute case arrival average.
- Does **not** compute service/waiting-time columns (even with a comment that only completion timestamps exist).
- Uses a toy process-tree string in `__main__` instead of the supplied complex string; the required “clearly explain limitations of the simplified string” is absent.
- `pm4py.get_variants_as_dataframe` and `pm4py.case_statistics.get_case_duration` are not reliable/current public PM4Py conventions.
- Placeholders for arrival, resources, and experiment execution contain no hooks for architect-batch correlation, geographic inspection bundling, objection processes, or parallel-join barriers — exactly the features the prose insists must be present.
- ArrivalGenerator is ordinary exponential; non-stationary and burst logic are missing even as stubs.

These are not cosmetic; they violate the “coherent, modular, and faithful to PM4Py conventions” and the enumerated “computes at least …” mandate.
