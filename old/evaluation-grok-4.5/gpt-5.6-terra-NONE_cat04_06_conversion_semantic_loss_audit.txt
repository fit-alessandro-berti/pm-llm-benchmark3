### Deductions (material under strict scoring)

1. **Validation predicate bug (substantive)**  
   In `source_trace_predicate`, `Correct Defect` between `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial` does not break `pending_configure`, so illegal visible patterns like  
   `Configure Trial  Correct Defect  Inspect Trial`  
   can be accepted. The check  
   `trace.index("Inspect Trial", first_inspect) > release_index`  
   is effectively redundant with the earlier first-inspect check and does not enforce “last inspect before release.”  
   Impact: `completed_traces_violating_source_predicate` can miss faulty round-trips; quarantine is weaker than claimed. This is the largest technical flaw.

2. **Incomplete use of requested comparison toolkit**  
   Section 5 asks for playout “where supported.” Soundness, footprints, reachability, custom exploration, and ± witnesses are present; explicit model/trace playout is not. Minor but explicit gap vs the brief.

3. **Formatting / table hygiene**  
   Pipeline and audit tables lose arrows (`POWL  accepting Petri net`, `POWLPetri`), which hurts scannability against the required schema. Not semantic, but sloppy relative to “nearly flawless.”

4. **Bound / quarantine coupling**  
   `should_quarantine` treats any `truncated=True` as failure. Correct for refusing false equivalence, but with `LOOP_REDO_BOUND = 1` and a fixed expansion cap the reference net itself could be quarantined without distinguishing “incomplete evidence” from “semantic mismatch.” The prose acknowledges non-exhaustiveness; the decision procedure is still blunt.

5. **API / portability soft spots (secondary)**  
   Dual import paths for firing and reliance on place-name `marking_key` are pragmatic, but cross-net place identity and version-skew of `semantics.execute` / `discover_footprints(net, im, fm)` remain fragile. Acceptable given the reminder list; still not airtight.

6. **Small semantic nits**  
   - I6 “Partly” is right, yet the audit still leans hard on trace witnesses without a clearer token-level concurrency criterion.  
   - Activity-label uniqueness is “expected preserved” without a constructive check that generated BPMN/net tasks remain unique before label-based comparison.  
   - Some positive witnesses interleave `Legal Review` inside the trial body (allowed by the POWL edges) while the predicate only weakly constrains legal-vs-verification exclusivity beyond release—consistent with source, but easy for a reader to over-read as fully validated concurrency.

The architecture and honesty are excellent; the executable audit has enough logic gaps that a strict reading cannot award a top-band score.
