### Deductions (strict)

1. **Factual error on S1 volume** — States “Volume +0% but mix rotates…”. Peak throughput is ~3300 cases/week vs ~2300 normal (~+43%). Mix shift is real and important, but denying concurrent volume growth misstates the stress model and weakens migration reasoning for shared stations already near saturation.
2. **Presentation / clarity defects** — Multiple broken tokens in tables and scenario text (`1829%`, `1320%`, `****`, empty effect markers). Rework and directionality cells become hard to read; under strict grading this is not cosmetic when the rubric demands a usable trade-off table.
3. **Underspecified control logic** — WIP cap “~1.5× service rate × service time” is hand-wavy (and dimensionally odd as stated). “One M1 capacity window per shift” and “protected 90-min specialist slot” lack sizing against Complex-panel load (20% of peak, multi-assay). Implementability is asserted more than engineered.
4. **Trade-off table softness** — Required columns exist, but several effect cells collapse to “Same SLA,” “Neutral,” or corrupted symbols instead of directional, evidence-tied impacts (especially vs Urgent p90/18h penalty and Routine 72h). Next-bottleneck and rework columns are uneven across rows.
5. **R6 timing ambiguity** — Correct strategic idea (build skill off-peak), but wording mixes a utilization gate for *training* with *deployment*; 4-week cross-train latency means the gate cannot be reactive. Slight logical smear.
6. **Secondary grounding gaps** — Little quantitative use of absolute DFG flows for rework loops (e.g., recollection and repeat fractions) when arguing magnitude of relief; partner-lab path under I10 is reasoned qualitatively but not stress-tested against Complex p90 210h + 12h transport.
