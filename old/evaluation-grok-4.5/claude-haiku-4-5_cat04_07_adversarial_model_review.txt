## Critical failures (dominant score impact)

**1. Wrong process-tree semantics for Model S (cascades through §§3, 5, 8)**  
In PM4Py/process-tree notation, `+` is **parallel**, not a loop; `*` is the loop. `X(A, tau)` makes A optional once; it does not authorize unbounded repetition.  
Model S is therefore:

- Register  
- then the four activities each optional **once**, freely interleaved  
- then **exactly one** of Manager / Auto Approval  
- then Release  

`Correct Dossier` is **absent** from Model S entirely.

The answer repeatedly calls `+()` a “loop,” claims arbitrary repetitions of checks and approvals, and asserts S can skip approval or take both approvals. Concrete errors:

| Claim | Reality under Model S |
|---|---|
| Trace 3 (no approval) accepted | **Rejected** — approval XOR has no  |
| Trace 5 (both approvals) accepted | **Rejected** — XOR, not AND/loop |
| B8 (second Credit Check, no correction) accepted | **Rejected** — no loop; second CC is a log move |
| Repetition “handles P6” | **False** — Correct Dossier not in the alphabet |

Several probe cells and P2/P5/P6/P7 rows in the comparative audit are therefore wrong or right only by accident. The task explicitly required reasoning from the model expressions; this fails that bar.
