### Critical failures

**1. Primary plan is sequential when it should be concurrent**  
Rank 1 is “Alternate screening → S4 review” with earliest continuation **50–110 minutes** against a **90-minute** deadline. The prompt explicitly invites parallel composites (“alternate screening in parallel with waiting for S4”). Running screening and the S4 wait sequentially burns the SLA window and undermines the claimed €20k–€35k “benefit.” The final recommendation still sequences them. That is a first-order process-monitoring mistake, not a nit.

**2. Benefit/harm columns are decorative, not reasoned**  
Ranks 1–3 all show the same benefit (€20k–€35k) and essentially the same harm (€15k–€80k). That cannot be right:
- Deadline hit probability differs sharply between interrupt-S2, S4@35m, S4@65m, and screening+review at 50–110m.

- €15k–€80k is customer harm from false rejection/prolonged hold, not a generic “delay harm,” yet §3 also uses it as SLA-breach harm.
- Regulatory exposure (€100k–€500k+) is missing from conformant rows’ harm framing and dumped only on A/B.

§4 covers A, B, F, G only. C, D, and E are not mapped to rules 3–9 (mandatory analyst path already done, second officer for risk≥70 / unresolved fuzzy match, four-eyes, approval before execution, SAR≠authorization, etc.). The prompt required a full mapping.
