## Strict Critique

It correctly treats the March raw-fitness collapse as primarily semantic/logging, links post-May rework to Supplier Z, queue growth to staffing, redistribution to the priority rule, and the autumn peak to seasonality, while proposing differentiated monitors. However, the 15–30 June interval is artificially narrow and overlaps the next row; evidence strengths of 90–95 are overstated given purely aggregated monthly data and acknowledged non-uniqueness; the “alternative explanation” column occasionally recycles the primary mechanism rather than offering a genuine rival.
- **§2 Raw-vs-normalized**: Length is acceptable (~230 words) and the core argument is sound.
