## Overall judgment
The submission is broad, well-structured, and hits every required heading with real engagement on non-stationarity, calendars, rework, and interventions. It is **not** near-flawless. Under strict grading it loses heavily for incorrect PM4Py usage, a methodologically wrong steady-state framing for an explicitly non-stationary system, weak service/waiting separation, incomplete cross-case mechanics, and a long skeleton that looks complete but is internally inconsistent and largely non-plausible as PM4Py/sim code.

### 1. Model scope and state variables — adequate, not tight
**Strengths:** Clear boundary, entities, resource table, calendars, and named cross-case links (architect bundling, neighborhood/public-notice coupling). Non-stationary demand is acknowledged.

**Defects:**
- “Public-notice batches” is under-motivated relative to the log (objections missing; duration is a proxy, not a batch entity).
- State variables stay somewhat laundry-list; little on *how* shared objection state, bundle membership, or “wait for all parallel reviews” are stored as first-class state.
- Level of detail claims activity-level DES but does not nail tokens for the `+` (AND) block or applicant-as-resource queues.

### 2. Parameter-estimation plan — serious PM grounding failures
**Strengths:** Right *topics* (NHPP-like arrivals, seasonal/DOW factors, architect bursts, rework from variants, calendars from HR + checks). Correctly avoids treating objections as observed events and flags missing starts.

**Critical issues:**
- Invents or misstates multiple PM4Py APIs: `get_arrival_rate`, `get_branching_factors`, `get_rework_frequency`, `discover_resource_profiles`, `add_service_and_waiting_times`, odd `sojourn_time` usage, nonstandard import/discovery paths. This directly violates “consistent with PM4Py conventions.”
- Service vs waiting: “assume service time  waiting time (conservative)” is confused, not conservative in a useful sense, and still collapses the split without a crisp identification strategy (e.g. busy-time reconstruction, resource-calendar residual, expert priors with sensitivity). Constraint: do not collapse without explaining the limitation — explanation is thin and wrong-headed.
- Building-area / fire threshold and “D3/D5 sensitive because travel is long” are weak proxies (travel  environmental sensitivity).
- Inductive rediscovery is suggested alongside a given tree without a clear “use tree as control-flow prior, estimate only stochastic parameters” discipline.

### 5. Warm-up, replications, uncertainty — conceptually flawed for the stated system
**Strengths:** Long horizon matching seasonality, many replications, tail-aware reporting, sensitivity/Sobol ambitions, mixture idea for applicant delay.

**Major flaws:**
- **Welch warm-up + “steady-state queue lengths” is the wrong primary frame** for mandated non-stationary demand and calendar effects. This should be framed mainly as **terminating** simulations over aligned seasonal windows (or carefully defined cycles), with warm-up only for initialization bias within a season—not classical steady-state.
- **Seeding:** “fixed seed per experiment” plus replications conflicts with independent replications; CI construction becomes meaningless if reps share seeds.
- Batch-means CIs on short seasonal output are hand-wavy; heavy-tail handling is named but not integrated with replication design.
- 100 reps in text vs 5–10 in code — inconsistency.
- “Welche’s” method: sloppy.

### 6. Validation strategy — checklist-complete, statistically naive in places
**Strengths:** Face, replay/alignment idea, distributional, utilization, bottleneck, temporal OOS split — right menu. Bottleneck-shift checks under interventions are good.

**Defects:**
- KS \(p>0.05\) targets on large \(n\) are a known trap (will reject useful models).
- Alignment “2 deviations for 80%” is arbitrary and weakly tied to a tree that is only a simplification.
- Again leans on dubious PM4Py helpers.
- No clear protocol for validating **cross-case** quantities (bundle rates, correlated public-notice extensions) against the flattened log’s limitations.
- Does not stress-test the service/waiting imputation, which is the main measurement gap.

### 8. Python skeleton — ambitious shell, not plausible PM4Py/sim fidelity
**Meets surface requirements:** pandas construction (not `pm4py.read_csv`), `format_dataframe`, tree parse attempt + fallback narrative, variants/DFG/perf DFG/arrival/service-wait attempts, separation of mining vs engine, classes for arrivals/resources/experiments.

**Severe problems (strict):**

- **Service/waiting in `analyze_log`:** pretends enrichment works on completion-only stamps.
- Engine is a partial custom DES, not wrong in spirit, but far from “coherent and faithful” under the stated PM4Py bar.
