Consequently every bottleneck claim (base “no clear bottleneck”, U2 “Electrical/Software”, U3 “Software”, U5 “Validation”) is unreliable. Weighted block-by-block aggregation of M+ME, E+ME+ES, S+ES is never performed. This is basic queueing/process-mining competence and is inexcusable given the scenario’s explicit emphasis on ME/ES.

No bounds or sensitivity on when sequential is preferable appear in the load analysis or table. Handover/setup/preemption costs are listed as assumptions yet never folded into the offered-load arithmetic or the numeric ranges in Section 4.
