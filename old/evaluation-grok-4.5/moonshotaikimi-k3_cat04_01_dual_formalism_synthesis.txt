### Minor issues (hypercritical deductions)

1. **Composite-node interleaving semantics (semantic subtlety, ~0.3).** W2-style traces (QR before the trial region, RR after) are allowed only if concurrent composite nodes shuffle their *internal* events. The answer states this as Assumption 10 and it matches PM4Py’s Petri-net encoding, but it is not proved from first principles; a reader relying on a stricter “node-as-atomic-interval” reading would disagree. This should have been tied more tightly to the conversion semantics.

2. **Tree loses a large, practically common slice of behavior (~0.2).** Early supervisor sign-off and early supplier answers (the entire W3 family) are rejected. The defense is sound, yet a lighter hybrid (e.g., low-risk path allowing SR inside the A-branch, high-risk path forced post-join) was not explored; the binary “T2 vs full duplication” framing slightly overstates the cost of recovering *some* early-approval traces.

3. **Section 2 D15 is a grab-bag (~0.1).** Bundling all intended non-edges into one row weakens the “independent of either implementation” claim; several of those pairs deserved separate rows.

4. **Validation plan is aspirational rather than closed (~0.2).** The `satisfies_spec` predicate and exhaustive topological enumeration are sketched, not supplied as runnable code; POWLnet conversion is correctly caveated as version-dependent, which leaves a hole in the “convert then check_soundness” path. For a modeling task this is acceptable, but it is not airtight evidence.

5. **Tiny surface nits (~0.1).** Compact tree expression uses undeclared short codes before the legend appears; a couple of API calls (`play_out` parameters, exact `check_soundness` signature) are left generic; no indication the snippets were executed.
