**4. Plausible mechanisms** — Meets “4” and includes both legitimate and concerning. Several “concerning” items are plausible but not tightly tied to *identifying variation* in the log (e.g., caseworker subjectivity is asserted more than evidenced). Mild redundancy with §3.
