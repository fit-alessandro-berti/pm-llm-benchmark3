## Defects (score drivers)

1. **Arithmetic error in absence capacity (material in §2)**  
   Without Chen, regular capacity is \(2490 - 450 = 2040\) min, not the ~1995 implied by the table (\(2721/1995 \approx 136.4\%\), \(3442/1995 \approx 172.6\%\)). OT-without-Chen is likewise off. The fluid bounds for U3/U5 are therefore wrong by several points. That undercuts auditability of the “show enough work” requirement.

2. **Mangled objective notation (§1)**  
   \(J_{\text{risk}}\) is corrupted in the markdown (`)*+`, `S*{P2}`, broken positive-part operators). Intent is recoverable; presentation is not “nearly flawless.”

3. **§4 table is weakly coupled to §8**  
   Ranges (e.g. base mean delay 390–800 min, P1 SLA bands) read as narrative/engineering judgment. The code uses \(N=2\) replications and a two-day empty-start/clearance horizon—incapable of stably supporting those bands—yet §4 claims to reflect the scaffold. Requirement allows qualitative ranges, but the implied empiricism is overstated. Delay magnitudes are not tightly reconciled with a ~50–480 min structural gap plus overhead (plausible under the afternoon capacity cliff, but not demonstrated).

4. **DES correctness risk**  
   The simulator is large (minute loop, preemption, calendar/breaks/OT, forced sequential owner, outage release, parallel coordination). Several edge behaviors are easy to get wrong (interval close/open across breaks, double-counting busy time, sequential lock-in vs hybrid overflow, censoring at horizon inflating p90). No runnable outputs are shown. A “well-specified scaffold” is allowed, but confidence in metric tables should be lower than the prose asserts. \(N=2\) is inadequate for the uncertainty rhetoric.

5. **Small internal nits**  
   - Resilience scored as `2–3` in one cell vs integer scale elsewhere.  
   - Strict Intake lane (Ada/Farah/Emil) and Validation lane choices are under-motivated vs the skill matrix.  
   - U2 “nominal work almost unchanged” is fine after renormalization, but skill-mix work shift could be quantified more cleanly.  
   - Jain-on-saturation is discussed well in prose; the table still leans on it as if discriminative under U1/U5.  
   - Farah/OT and multiplier interactions in fluid \(z\) are described as a relaxation then sometimes treated like scenario truth.

6. **What “robust” delivers**  
   The recommendation of H is coherent under \(J_{\text{robust}}\), and the text admits U5 is rationing and that no policy hits 90% SLAs under stated capacity. Still, §4–5 slightly over-sell policy separation on P1 when the binding constraint is missing headcount; routing cannot fix U5. That tension is acknowledged but could discipline the resilience column more hard-headedly.
