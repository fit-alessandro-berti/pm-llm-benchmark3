## Evaluation

### Format and structure

Structure alone does not rescue substance: several mandatory analytical products are missing or only gestured at (case total sojourn for completed cases, an actual queue/WIP path over time, a transparent Little’s-Law calculation, and full uncertainty tables that the prose claims exist).

### Critical quantitative failures
These are not minor slips; they break internal consistency and show weak control of the event log.

1. **Wrong S1 waiting times on required cases**  
   - **P16**: \(12{:}05 - 10{:}25 = 100\) min, reported as **55**.  
   - **P17**: \(12{:}20 - 10{:}45 = 95\) min, reported as **35**.  
   - **P21**: \(13{:}30 - 11{:}50 = 100\) min, reported as **35**.  
   - **P05** S1 wait central should be **35** min (rounding band roughly 30–40), not **20 / 32.5 / 45**.  
   - **P11** S1 wait central should be **75** min, not **65**.  

   These are explicit mandatory rows. Getting them wrong is disqualifying for a high score.

2. **Aggregate “unremarkable case” medians are false**  
   Claimed Ws1 median ≈ 30 min for P01–P04, P06, P08–P14, P18–P20. Actual S1 waits rise from ~5 min early to ~100+ min later; the median is far above 30. This is not rounding noise—it is a fabricated summary.

3. **Station arrival logic is conceptually wrong**  
   S2 “Arrivals” in 08–10 are reported as **0**. Station-2 arrivals are S1 completions entering the S2 queue. By 10:00, multiple cases have finished S1 (P01–P07). Treating S2 arrivals as external arrivals confuses the tandem-queue model the scenario defines.

4. **Busy time vs utilization is arithmetically impossible**  
   S1 08–10: busy-time **320** min with util **0.67–0.71**. Two resources × 120 min ⇒ **240** resource-minutes available. Busy time cannot exceed availability, and \(320 / 240 > 1\) while reported util stays below 0.8. The utilization column is therefore not derived from the busy-time column (or both are wrong). That fails the quality bar on utilization.

5. **Arrival counts are inconsistent with the table**  
   S1 12–14 arrivals reported as **0**, but **P22 arrives 12:10**. S1 10–12 “8 arrivals” is at best boundary-ambiguous and not justified.
