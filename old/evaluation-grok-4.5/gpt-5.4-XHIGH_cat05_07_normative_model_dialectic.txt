## Evaluation

The submission is an outstanding response to a deliberately underspecified, multi-authority conformance problem. It meets the required heading structure, steelmans both sides, separates stipulated/disputed/unproved claims, applies a coherent burden-of-proof rule, classifies every case with a dominant reason, and delivers a layered normative redesign plus executable POWL with explicit limits. Under normal review this would be a 9.5–9.7. Under the requested hypercritical standard, a few real (if narrow) defects keep it from the top band.

### What is genuinely strong

- **Dialectical fairness**: Prosecution correctly anchors on CS-9 as controlled baseline, expiry of PD-3/FD-7, normalization (six August auto-approvals; 14 ineligible + 9 undocumented), and hidden SoD/COI failures (SUP-712, SUP-715). Defense correctly anchors on live overlays, missing loops, scope ambiguity, CRM linkage decay, label equivalence, and reactivation/reject category errors. Each side’s mandatory concessions are real, not cosmetic.
- **Burden of proof (§1)**: The three-part test for `NONCOMPLIANT`, the elevated bar and reverse burden for `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`, and the default to `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` / `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT` when capture or scope is the uncertainty are exactly the right methodology for this fact pattern.
- **Case-level judgment (§5)**: SUP-703/708 as critical noncompliance, SUP-704 as justified FD-7 exception, SUP-705/714 as post-expiry noncompliance, SUP-707/709/710/713 as model/scope defects, and SUP-706 as data-indeterminate are all well-supported. Avoiding `MIXED` as a dump category is disciplined. The dual system conclusion (material operational noncompliance *and* M0 unfit as sole benchmark) is not a false split; it is the only conclusion the evidence supports.
- **Evidence matrix**: Complete, column-correct, and generally traceable to N1–N5 and the case table. “What would reverse” cells are decision-useful.
- **Architecture (§6)**: Correct separation of POWL (route skeleton), DECLARE/skeleton (route-independent precedence), pandas/data (eligibility, amounts, windows, identity), and org controls (overlay registry, COI). Scope types for intake reject / reactivation / withdrawal are necessary and well motivated.
- **POWL (§7)**: Meets the mandatory feature list. Nested `StrictPartialOrder` + `XOR` of full routes is the right shape. Auto route adds `Master_Data_Validation` and post-activation quality; emergency makes quality and manual concurrent after sanctions-gated activate; `LOOP(tau, Request_More_Info)` is a defensible `(RMI)*` approximation with an honest limitation note. Overlay table and collection schema (immutable event/record times, eligibility snapshots, alias graph, notification_id, rule/overlay versions) match the prompt’s data requirements. Code does not read external files.

### Defects that block a higher score

1. **SUP-711 primary classification is slightly wrong**  
   Same source transaction ID + migration duplicate is not true indeterminacy about compliance. Substance is compliant; the log is dirty. Dominant label should be `COMPLIANT` (with data defect) or at worst `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT`/`DATA_QUALITY` as a secondary tag. `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` is the right bucket for SUP-706, not for a proven duplicate row. This is a real taxonomy error on a case the prompt included to test exactly that distinction.

2. **Standard-route over-constraint (model semantics)**  
   CS-9 requires Sanctions and Quality before *activation*, and Manual before *activation*. It does not require Quality before Manual. The revised POWL forces  
   `standard_checks (incl. Quality_Review)  Manual_Procurement_Approval  Activate`.  
   That is stricter than CS-9 and stricter than M0’s spirit (checks, then decision branch). Defensible as a conservative redesign, but not acknowledged as a tightening. A hypercritical model-semantics review marks this.

3. **SUP-702 / PD-3 authority still a soft spot**  
   The brief correctly flags Compliance’s later “had not approved weakening CS-9” statement and gives Medium confidence, but the primary label `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` still treats PD-3 as sufficient authorizing overlay. Given N2’s form (minutes/email, never folded into CS-9) and the observer’s retraction, a stricter adjudication would mark `MIXED` or `NONCOMPLIANT` with mitigation, or would require an explicit holding that “monitored exception” language is enough. Medium confidence mitigates but does not fully cure the jump.

4. **Minor POWL/design nits**  
   - `LOOP(SilentTransition, Request_More_Info)` works for `(RMI)*` but is a slightly awkward encoding; a body/redo arrangement with an explicit exit is clearer and less dependent on reader knowledge of process-tree LOOP polarity.  
   - RMI exists only on the standard quality track; no note on whether emergency/auto paths may need the same loop.  
   - Reactivation deliberately omitted from the XOR (correctly explained), but then the “minimum” model still always demands `Register_Supplier`, so SUP-710-class cases remain structurally impossible without the data-layer `case_type` gate—the dependency should be stated even more sharply next to the code.  
   - Duplicate label objects (`Sanctions_Check` ×4, etc.) are normal for exclusive branches but worth one sentence for log-projection clarity.

5. **Small clarity / completeness gaps**  
   - Evidence matrix “Material risk” and “Missing evidence” cells are uneven in specificity (e.g., SUP-702 master-data proof; SUP-704 critical-supplier source).  
   - §5 severity band “Low to Medium” / “Medium to High” is slightly mushy versus a single ordinal.  
   - DECLARE examples repeat some POWL edges; a cleaner “POWL vs DECLARE ownership” one-liner would tighten §6.  
   - No explicit treatment of withdrawal as a terminal activity in the POWL approximation (only in the overlay table).

### What was *not* wrong (credit against false deductions)

- Treating FD-7 expiry as hard for SUP-705 is correct; “facts on the ground” without writing is not authority.  
- Keeping amount/date/identity/authority out of pure POWL is correct.  
- Not encoding COI/SoD inside POWL is correct.  
- Dual system verdict is earned, not hedged.  
- Required headings and “no questions” constraint are respected.

### Score rationale

The piece is nearly flawless on structure, dialectic, traceability, and redesign quality. The hypercritical deductions are for one mis-applied primary label (SUP-711), one unacknowledged CS-9 over-constraint in the POWL, and residual softness on PD-3’s formal power for SUP-702—plus small encoding/clarity nits. Those are enough to move it off 9.5–10.0 but not enough to drop it into the mid-8s: the core legal/process reasoning and the layered architecture are still excellent.

**Grade: 9.0**
