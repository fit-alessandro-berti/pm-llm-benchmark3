## Major defects (score drivers)

### 1. Required output incomplete (§2)
The brief demands **detailed worked calculations for F02, F04, F07, and F11**. Only F02 is worked; F04/F07/F11 are deferred to “the code.” That is a direct format failure, not a minor omission.

### 2. Decomposition definitions are sloppy and the double-count proof is wrong (§1)
- Notation is broken (“Cases, i {A,B,C}”, missing case index, missing Δ in places).
- The anti–double-count line  
  `sync_wait,i = 3·join_ready – t(c,i_end)`  
  is not a valid identity for a single branch and does not show why **summing** sync waits double-counts elapsed time.  
  The correct point is  
  \(\sum_i( \mathrm{join}-t_{i,\mathrm{end}})=3\cdot\mathrm{join}-\sum_i t_{i,\mathrm{end}}\),  
  which is **not** a single calendar span and must not be added into total flow.  
  Stating “only the max controls progress” is right; the algebra does not support it.

### 3. Aggregate statistics errors / hand-waving (§3)
- **A mean sync wait ≈ 7 min is false.**  
  \(join-A_{end}\) over the 12 cases is  
  \(0,10,15,7,10,15,25,5,15,5,0,25\) → mean **11.0 min**, not 7.
- **B “sync wait imposed on others: 11 min”** is undefined and not reproducible from the table; when B is critical, mean lead over the second-finishing branch is **15 min**, not 11.
- Sync waits are defined as a first-class component but **never appear in the case table**, so the “no double counting” story is asserted, not shown case-by-case.
- `fork_join_elapsed = max(q+s)` is defined then not reported.

### 4. Counterfactuals: right idea, weak execution and overclaim (§4)

- Required **per-case** CF savings are not shown in prose (only vague aggregates / “see code”).

### 5. Python / PM4Py block fails the bar (§6)
