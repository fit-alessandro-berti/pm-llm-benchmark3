## Hypercritical deductions

**1. QA confidence (86) is materially too high ( –0.4)**  
The authors themselves list the appointment semantics (hard / soft / window / ad-hoc override) as the main identifiability problem and note that starting A05 at 09:05 made an on-time A06 impossible. With only two appointment cases and an 8-job sample, an epistemic confidence in the mid-70s would have been more coherent with their own caveats. Inflated confidence violates the instruction that confidence “must reflect evidential ambiguity, not rhetorical certainty.”

**2. QB primary label is cleaner than the evidence ( –0.3)**  
Calling the primary discipline “FIFO by original arrival, with resumed work retaining precedence” under-states the hybrid character.  
- At 10:00 the system selects B05 (p1) rather than resuming B01 or taking older B04 — a clear priority intervention at a free decision point.  
- At 10:28 it selects B04 (p3) over B06 (p2) — pure FIFO / anti-priority.  
These two facts pull in opposite directions. The secondary component (“selective emergency priority”) absorbs some of the tension, yet the primary label still over-privileges FIFO. A primary of “undocumented hybrid (FIFO-resume + selective non-preemptive priority + emergency preemption)” with correspondingly lower confidence would have been more accurate. The 68 confidence partially mitigates, but the categorical primary claim remains slightly over-clean.

**3. Methodological opportunism around decision versus start time ( –0.2)**  
For most queues the authors treat service-start timestamps as decision epochs. For the critical QC 10:32 episode they invoke an unobserved pre-commitment of C04 at 10:18 to rescue an SPT-like reading. The move is explicitly flagged as an assumption and is defensible, yet it is used only where it helps the preferred narrative. A fully even-handed treatment would have applied the same pre-commitment possibility symmetrically to other apparent violations (e.g., B03 at 09:48) or would have lowered QC’s already-modest confidence still further.

**4. Minor presentational and completeness nits ( –0.1)**  
- Section 1 primary-discipline cells for QB and QC are long descriptive paragraphs rather than compact labels, reducing scannability of the required table.  
- No quantitative scorecard (e.g., “6/8 decisions match FIFO, 2/8 match appointment”) is supplied; such a tally would have made the confidence numbers more auditable.  
- The QC column header “appointment / deadline prediction” slightly blurs the candidate set (deadlines are not a listed candidate mechanism).  

None of these are analytical errors, but under a “minor issues  significantly lower” rule they accumulate.

- QB primary explicitly labeled hybrid, with FIFO and priority as co-equal components.  
- Symmetric handling of possible pre-commitment across all queues.  
- A compact per-queue match-count table supporting the confidence figures.

The answer demonstrates genuine process-mining competence, high internal consistency, and exemplary uncertainty hygiene. The deductions above are real but narrow; they do not overturn the overall diagnoses. On a hypercritical rubric the work sits at **9.0**.
