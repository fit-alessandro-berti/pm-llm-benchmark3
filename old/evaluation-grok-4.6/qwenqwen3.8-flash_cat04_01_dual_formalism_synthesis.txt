### Fatal: `Operator.LOOP` is not `body* exit`
PM4Py’s binary loop is the standard process-tree operator

\[
\texttt{LOOP}(P,Q) = P\,(Q\,P)^*
\]

i.e. **do–redo**: `P` at least once, `Q` to repeat, always **finish in `P`**.

The models use `LOOP(fail_cycle, pass_cycle)` under the explicit claim that this is “the common PM4Py unfolding `body* exit`”. That claim is false. Under the real operator:

- the language is `fail (pass fail)*`;
- a first-pass success (`Execute, Inspect, Release`) is **not** in the language;
- the loop **ends on a failing cycle** (`… Adjust`);
- `pass` is the redo, so a passing inspection can be followed by another fail/adjust.

So T1 and T4 are not traces of the constructed tree/POWL, while traces that end `Adjust → Release` (the F3 shape) become easy. The fallback `XOR(pass, fail → LOOP(fail, pass))` is still a body/exit encoding, not do–redo, so it does not save a real PM4Py run.

The natural encoding was available and simpler:

```text
LOOP( ->(Execute Trial, Inspect Trial), Adjust Parameters )
```

language `(EI)(Adj EI)*`: at least one trial, `Adjust` only between cycles, never after the last inspect, no duplicate `Execute`/`Inspect` nodes, no silent pass/fail. Missing that is not a style quibble; it is the standard way to represent this exact repeat-until pattern in PM4Py.

Because both artifacts share this loop, **neither model is faithful on requirements 8–9**, and the witness verdicts are not properties of the code.

### Modeling nits that still matter
- Silent pass/fail plus duplicated `Execute`/`Inspect` nodes are unnecessary given do–redo, and they create the duplicate-label problem the write-up then has to apologize for.
- The tree delay of supplier/approvals until after **both** planning and design is a defensible conservative cut, but it is coarser than required: supplier and risk only need `Analyze`, while trial is the AND-join of `Plan` and `Design`. The text understands that; the tree still lumps them.
- Validation is a plan, not evidence. Given the loop error, playout would have falsified T1 immediately. The self-critique names “loop convention” as a residual risk while the body of the answer treats `body* exit` as fact.

Those tables are internally consistent **only** with the invented loop. Against PM4Py they are not.
