**6.5**

The write-up hits the required eight-section skeleton and the hard constraints in spirit: non-stationary demand, calendars, endogenous waiting, latent objections, no optimality claim, and a PM4Py-versus-engine split. It is still not close to flawless. Under a strict reading, several process-mining and design errors are material.

**What holds up.** Scope is a case-level DES with shared resources, not an independent-case replay. State includes case families, bundles, calendars, and a latent objection flag. Arrivals are framed as an NHPP with weekday/seasonal multipliers and architect bursts. Waiting is treated as emergent except for applicant-driven delays. Objections are explicitly unobserved and proxied. D1–D10 plus four stress cases, Welch-style seasonal warm-up, CRN, bootstrap tails, and a multi-metric decision rule (median, p90, SLA, fairness, utilization, rework, robustness) are all present. The code path `pandas.read_csv` → `pm4py.format_dataframe` → variants/DFG/performance DFG/arrival rate is convention-consistent, and the inter-event column is correctly *not* sold as service time.

**Process-mining grounding is sloppy.** The given process tree is not used. Completeness is rewritten as `*(Completeness check, XOR(...))` instead of a loop around the completeness block with a `Completeness rework` redo; inspection is rewritten as `*(Inspection scheduling, Site inspection)` instead of a sequence looped with `Inspection rework`. Section 2’s tree and Section 8’s tree also disagree. Applicant appeal is named in the source process and then dropped. That is not a parsing caveat; it is a different control-flow model.

**Design holes.** Monday 2.2× arrivals are modeled, but the dedicated Monday portal clerk is never turned into a capacity rule, so the stated completeness-queue spike is only half-specified. D5 (“legal pre-check”) is never pinned to a lifecycle point relative to reviews and decision drafting. `prior_violations` is required for D2 with no estimation plan. WIP initialization (empty system vs seeded open cases) is omitted, which matters if warm-up is a full year. The 12/6 temporal split ignores that the holdout need not be seasonally comparable. The “non-overlapping 95% CI” selection rule is a weak decision procedure (multiple comparisons, practical significance, paired CRN). The claim that missing start timestamps “do not bias” the wait/service split past a sensitivity band is asserted, not justified.

**Code is modular but thin where the twin is distinctive.** Arrival thinning, bundling, calendar assignment, and experiment configuration are `NotImplementedError` stubs with almost no event types or data structures. The estimation plan’s calendar-aware decomposition is not even sketched in `compute_duration_columns`. That is allowed for a skeleton, but it leaves the most important mechanisms untestable and disconnected from the prose.

**Net.** Competent municipal-simulation reasoning with the right sectioning and the right cautions, undermined by an altered process model, several unoperationalized rules, and a statistically naive selection rule. Not a 8–10 under a hypercritical bar.
