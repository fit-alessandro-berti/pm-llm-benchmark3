5.3

The answer’s strategic call is directionally correct, but it is not close to a clean transport analysis. It repeatedly overstates what is known, omits required evidence, and then fails to encode the very constraints it treats as decisive.

**Process structure.** The comparison table hits the twelve named features, but several cells are false by simplification. Site A is not prerequisite-free: registration still precedes verification, and assessment still waits on both checks. Concurrency is not binary “no/yes”; Site A already had 8% overlapping intervals and Site B 6%. Queue huddles are a co-intervention, not part of Site A’s post-change control-flow. The 38% “digital” share is inferred from “originals not legally required,” which is not the same as Site A’s “digital at registration.” Transport-relevance entries are slogans (“Identifies core treatment”) rather than statements about which edges in the partial order can move.

**Transport graph.** Required node names are present, and the prose correctly says a similar diagram does not identify a transport effect. The graph itself is incomplete as a transport diagram. There are no selection nodes into the four capacities, even though Site B’s specialized pools (4 / 7 / 3 / 2) are the main resource contrast with Site A’s 18–20 cross-trained FTE. `role_specialization` never modifies `treatment_parallelization → true_overlap`. `originals_required` never points at cycle time despite an 18-hour receipt delay. Shared-mechanism language is sloppy: Site A has no legal-supplement loop, so “rework loops in both environments” is not a shared structural mechanism. The 180–280 word note is in range but thinner than the graph’s missing edges.

**Evidence transfer.** This section fails an explicit instruction. Missing major before/after rows include high-complexity mix (48→40), p90 (96→78), document-check service (7.2→6.5), 90-day appeal (4.1→4.4), and productive FTE (18→20). Co-occurring changes are not given their own rows: API upgrade, added reviewers, marketing mix shift, huddles, absence of a negative control, and the warning that overlap may be a timestamp artifact. Treating those only as parenthetical interpretations hides how much of Site A is unidentified. Confidence numbers are reasonably low on cycle time, but “transferable component = throughput gains strictly from concurrency” contradicts the causal-interpretation column for the same row.

**Modifiers and scenarios.** The ten required modifiers appear, and one scenario has a better median with a worse tail. That is the high-water mark. Scenario construction then collapses. There is no mapping from Site A’s wait/service times and Site B’s 3.4h / 8.1h / 18h / 20h figures onto the stated ranges. Eligible share is 38% while median change is written as if it were a population effect; those cannot be the same number without dilution. Theoretical overlap among digital cases cannot exceed roughly the shorter verification service time (~3.4h) plus some shared queue wait; optimistic −3 to −5h overall is therefore too large, or else the column is undefined. The IT-lock scenario’s +5h p90 is unmotivated: if work remains sequential, the tail should be near baseline, not systematically worse. “Baseline statutory violation” is a useful failure case, but it is asserted rather than derived.

**Candidate intervention.** Eligibility, statutory hold on originals, WIP gates, and refusal to copy “every case after Register” are the right design moves. The compact partial order then drops `Notify`, replaces the documented supplement path (`Request Supplement → Document Check → Assessment`) with the placeholder “Sequential Rework,” and treats `{Approve, Reject, Legal Review}` as an unordered set rather than a data-dependent exclusive choice. The specification and the later code do not describe the same process.

**Pilot.** Case-level randomization, digital-only eligibility, an intermediate primary outcome, interference, and stop rules are conceptually right. The sizing is impossible. Site B completes 3,400 cases in six months (~130/week). A 38% digital slice is ~50/week. 400 per arm cannot accrue in 4 weeks; it needs on the order of four months, before washout, legal loops, or any 90-day appeal endpoint. Secondary outcomes are not specified. There is no factorial (assignment × lock-bypass), so the claim that overlap failure can be “cleanly” split into mechanism versus implementation is false. Comparing the pilot to “Site A’s digital subset” reimports the confounded 9-hour contrast the rest of the memo correctly rejects. Four weeks also cannot speak to appeal.

**PM4Py / model fidelity.** This is the largest technical failure. The code builds the rejected all-case parallelization: `Register` to both checks, no `Originals Receipt`, no XOR on the 62% statutory path. That path is control-flow and *can* be encoded in POWL; commenting that the model “cannot encode waiting for physical originals” is an admission that a representable constraint was omitted. The LOOP is `Legal Review` ↺ `Request Supplement`, which does not restore document check and assessment. `Operator.SEQUENCE` is not a standard POWL operator (POWL sequence is a strict partial order). Reusing one `direct_decision` object under two parents is an unsafe object graph. `pm4py.check_soundness(net_b)` is the wrong arity; the `except AttributeError` is a mute. Playout and workflow-net checks, even if they ran, would validate the illegal model, not the candidate in §6.

**Recommendation and assumptions.** `conditional go`, digital-first, and refusal to transport the 9-hour drop are correct. Word count is acceptable. Expansion is mis-aimed at “IT-lock fixes in other stages” rather than at new strata or a lock-bypass factorial. Cross-training “union and HR review” is invented. The eight assumptions are the strongest formal artifact: testable, and several have the right failure consequences (fake overlap, statutory breach, downstream congestion). They cannot rescue missing evidence rows, an arithmetic pilot, or a model that parallelizes a legally unstartable task.

**Net.** The memo understands the analogy at slogan level—confounded before/after, no unique transport effect, do not copy the tree, watch the tail—and then under-delivers on every place the prompt asked for identified quantities, complete tables, and a faithful partial order. Under a strict bar that treats those as first-class, this is a middling, error-prone answer, not a high one.
