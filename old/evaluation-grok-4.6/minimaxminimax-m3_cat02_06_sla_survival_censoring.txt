**6.0**

The write-up is a competent process-mining / competing-risk plan with the right headings, estimands-before-estimators, and a mostly serious treatment of transfers, leakage, and immortal time. It is not close to flawless. Several errors hit exactly the distinctions the prompt required.

**Material conceptual and consistency failures**

- Section 1 encodes S09 (open at 92h) as “right-censored at 92h; still at risk over the [72h] window.” For “resolved by 72h” that case is an already-observed non-resolution, not a censored observation still at risk through the horizon. Section 2 later says the opposite. The prompt asked to separate right censoring, competing events, and already-observed breaches; the answer does not hold that line.
- Estimand C’s dedicated encoder only flags cases that are *still open* (and optionally late transfers). Anyone who resolved after 72h (S03, S13, S20, S28) was active at 72h and is dropped. Prose counts S03 as active at 72h; the code does not. `1 − Σ CIF_k(72)` is the right AJ summary; the custom Estimand C construction is not.
- IPCW is named as a third transfer scenario but encoded identically to competing risks. That is not three treatments, only two plus a comment.

**Factual misuse of the table**

- “Six cases are still active” then lists nine IDs (S09, S10, S11, S15, S24, S25, S26, S29, S31). The later “23 closed cases” implies nine open, so the count is internally inconsistent.
- “All Cohort B cases have AutoCheck” and “near-perfect collinearity with cohort” are false: S20, S23, S26, S30 have no AutoCheck; S01 and S15 in A do.
- S03 is placed “still in Review at 72h” and on an “Escalate → Review → Escalate” path. Neither is in the data (prefix is `…Review>Escalate>Review>Resolve`; no timestamps).
- Pooled withdraw+reject “CIF ≈ 0.19 by 24h” is wrong (three terminals by 24h: S05, S12, S23 → about 0.09). ~0.22 is the *overall* W+R share, not a 24h CIF.
- “60%+ never resolve” treats open cases as known non-resolutions.

**Smaller technical nits**

- Greenwood intervals are recommended for Aalen–Johansen (wrong variance).
- Math/operators are repeatedly stripped (`T_resolve  72`, `1 _k CIF_k`), so several definitions are hard to read.
- Snapshot “active at obs” means are printed as if they were survival estimands.
- Completed-case bias is oversimplified as “enriched for fast finishers” (late resolves S13/S28 remain; early W/R pull the other way).

**What still works**

Estimands A/B/C are operationally mapped; transfers are discussed as competing vs independent censoring vs IPCW in words; escalation/rework are correctly barred as baseline covariates; landmarking and AJ are the right primary/secondary pair; case IDs are used throughout; the single Python block builds the table, keeps open cases, stratifies, parses prefixes without fake analysis timestamps, and calls PM4Py variants/prefixes with a fallback. Section 7 does not sell scores as causal effects. Section 9 ranks the right missing data.

Those strengths keep it in the mid range. The S09/already-breached contradiction, the broken Estimand C encoder, the 6-vs-9 count, and invented prefix/timing details are enough that a strict reading cannot go higher.
