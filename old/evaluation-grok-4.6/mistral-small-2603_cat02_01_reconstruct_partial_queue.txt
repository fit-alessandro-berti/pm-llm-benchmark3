**2.8**

The write-up has the required heading skeleton and a few correct point estimates for fully observed complete cases (e.g. P02–P04, P06, P08–P10, P12–P14). That is not enough. The reconstruction that the task actually hinges on is factually wrong, internally inconsistent, and the Python block does not do what was required.

**Data were misread at the exact points the prompt flagged.** Missing S2 starts are P05 and P11; missing S1 starts are P07 and P15. Section 1 A7, the Section 3 notes, and the Python imputations treat P05/P11 as missing S1 starts and leave P05/P11 S2 starts unreconstructed. P07 is described as “S1 start/end missing” even though `s1_end = 10:00` is observed. P15’s S2 start (`13:30`) is treated as unknown waiting. That single inversion invalidates the required special-case rows.

**Section 3 does not bound missing starts and contradicts itself.**
- P05: reported S1 is `09:00–09:25` (wait 35, service 25). S2 start is the missing field; C1 is occupied by P03 until `09:45` and by P07 from `10:20`, so a defensible S2-start window is roughly `[09:45, 10:15]`, not “S2 wait MISSING / service 55”. `09:25→10:15` is 50 minutes of station occupancy, not 55 minutes of service.
- P07: I1 is busy with P05 at `09:00–09:25`, then P09 at `10:05`. Start is in about `[09:25, 10:00]`, wait about `[40, 75]`, service about `[0, 35]` (or `[5, 35]` under a 5-minute minimum). The table leaves both as `MISSING`.
- P11: S1 is fully observed (wait 75, service 25). C2 is on P08 until `12:00`, so S2 start is in about `[12:00, 12:25]`. The table invents an S1-start range and a 60-minute S2 service.
- P15: S1 finished at `12:15`; S1 sojourn is fixed at 125 minutes. Service is not `[105, ∞)`. Note says 125, table says 105. S2 wait is `12:15→13:30 = 75`, not `[90, ∞)`. Elapsed S2 service is 30 minutes, not `OPEN/OPEN` as a wait problem.
- P16: `14:00−12:35 = 85`, not 75; the note’s “≥ 15 min” is a third number for the same quantity.
- P17: S2 wait is `13:55−12:50 = 65` (observed), elapsed service is 5. The table puts wait as `[75, ∞)` and the note calls 5 minutes “elapsed waiting”.
- P18 S1 wait is 100, not 90; S2 elapsed wait is 50, not 90.
- P19/P20 tables say elapsed wait 75; notes say 35 and 15 (the notes are the ones that match the clock).
- P21 wait is observed (`13:30−11:50 = 100`), not `[120, ∞)`. Elapsed S1 service is 30, not 70.
- P22 elapsed wait is 110, not 135.

Rounding is applied only when convenient (P01 wait `[0, 5]`, almost everything else a degenerate `[x, x]`), and A2’s order constraints are written with the inequality signs dropped.

**Section 4 is not a station reconstruction.** Arrivals, completions, busy time, and utilization cannot be reproduced from the log.
- S1 `08:00–10:00`: about 13 arrivals (P01–P13 if 10:00 is excluded) and about 6–7 S1 completions, not 10 and 10.
- S2 `08:00–10:00` completions are P01–P03 (P04 ends `10:05`), not 10.
- S2 `12:00–14:00` completions include P08/P10–P14, not 0. Both C1 and C2 are busy for most of that window (P12/P14/P17 and P11/P13/P15). Busy time `[0, 20]` and utilization `[0%, 5%]` are incompatible with the timestamps.
- S1 `08:00–10:00` busy `[380, 400]` on two servers over 120 minutes (240 server-minutes) is physically impossible; 380/400 = 95% only if the denominator is silently changed. The formula stated in the notes is not the formula used in the numbers.
- Open work is not converted into elapsed busy time. Utilization is therefore not a utilization.

WIP is a single midpoint number with no queue-vs-service split, no simultaneous-event convention actually applied, and no bounds. Arrival/departure *rates* are never computed. Case-level total sojourn for completed cases is absent from the Markdown tables.

**Section 5 follows the broken metrics.** Claiming S2 is “overwhelmed” *because* utilization is 0–5% reverses the meaning of utilization. P08’s 55-minute S2 wait is assigned to the `08:00–10:00` period and blamed on a 15-minute C2 outage; most of that wait is P06 still on C2 until `10:50`. The early-period S1-bottleneck claim is asserted, not measured (no queue-length path, no remaining work, no comparison of residual service to residual capacity). Little’s Law is written in A9 as `L = W` (λ dropped). The one numerical check uses completions as λ on a window where arrivals ≠ completions and never compares `L/λ` to an observed sojourn.

**Section 6 names three scenarios but does not compute them.** Optimistic “reroute around C2 to C1” is not in the data (P08 and P11 stay on C2). Pessimistic infinite service makes means and utilizations undefined, yet bottleneck rank is declared stable. No table of O/C/P waiting, service, WIP, or utilization is given, so “stable vs sensitive” is unearned.

**Section 7 fails the stated contract and would not support the narrative.**
- P05 `s1_start` is coded `MISSING` (it is `09:00`).
- `s2_start` has 23 entries: P17’s `13:55` is shifted onto P18, so the fully-observed subset is also corrupted.
- `MISSING` and blank are both parsed to `None`, against “without treating MISSING, OPEN, and blank as the same thing.”
- Missing starts are replaced by single central timestamps in-place (explicitly forbidden).
- `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` and `pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` are never called, and nothing is labeled case-level vs station-level.
- `pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals` is neither used nor justified as unsafe.

- Completions for P18–P20 are left in the “observed” frame, so `s2_end - s2_start` is not well-defined. P15’s S1 start is still null when busy time is summed.
- The WIP loop counts queue+service, comments call it queue, and missing/open semantics are not scenario-aware.

**What remains.** Headings match. Waiting/service/sojourn are defined in words. A resource-constraint story is *claimed*. Censoring is mentioned. Fully observed complete cases are mostly arithmetically right. Under a strict bar those are formatting and partial credit, not an analysis.
