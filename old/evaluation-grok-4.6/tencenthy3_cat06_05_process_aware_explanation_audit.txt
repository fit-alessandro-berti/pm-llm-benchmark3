**7.5**

The audit is substantively strong: it covers every required item, separates complete-trace leakage from process-impossible order, treats `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` as future-legal rather than illegal, keeps congestion/age/complexity signals, and does not launder full-trace SHAP into early explanations. The Case X/Y revisions avoid leaked outcomes, and Case Z is correctly invalidated on timestamp, mutability, and suffix features.

It is not near-flawless under a strict reading.

The severity column violates the required enum (`low–medium`, `medium–high`). Several `≤ t0` constraints are missing in the text, which makes the validation rules look incomplete. Lineage for `claimant_channel` and `initial_reserve` is assumed rather than marked as unverified. The Broker interpretation slips toward a causal delay story. The four standards (predictive validity, causal interpretation, operational actionability, fairness) are folded into feature classes instead of stated as orthogonal tests that can pass or fail on the same feature. Opaque local “other features” mass is never flagged, and Case X/Y do not say that remaining SHAP pieces cannot be reused without recomputation. Alignment is named more than specified.

These are small relative to the overall quality of the audit, but they are real defects against the stated quality bar.
