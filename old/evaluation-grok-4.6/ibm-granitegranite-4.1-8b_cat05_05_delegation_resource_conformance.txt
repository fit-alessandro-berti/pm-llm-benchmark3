**Case analysis is the core failure.** Several findings invert the facts.

- **CHG-505 is the clearest collapse.** Approve_Change is `u_fl` (Farid) at 13:00 on 11 Aug. D-IC-03 runs 12:00–18:00 that day, so the timestamp is inside the interval, not outside it. - **CHG-504 is legally incoherent.** Asha is the principal QM, not a delegate. A principal acting while on leave is not a “valid substitution.” The real issue is identity: she claims she handled one urgent case during a 09:15–09:40 VPN session, and security cannot exclude credential sharing.
