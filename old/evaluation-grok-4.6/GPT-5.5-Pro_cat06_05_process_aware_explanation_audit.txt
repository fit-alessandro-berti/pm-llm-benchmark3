**8.0**

This is a strong, process-aware audit that hits the main quality bar: it catches both obvious leakage and subtler lineage defects, keeps legitimate congestion/slack signals, treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as later-stage-legitimate rather than impossible, and consistently separates predictive association from causal or actionable claims. It is not nearly flawless.

**What is done well**

Section 1 is comprehensive. Every global top-12 feature, every sequence pattern, and the local-only fields (`claimant_channel`, `final_decision`, `initial_reserve`, `risk_score_current`, `last_resource_role`) are covered, plus employee experience. The hard calls are mostly right: `total_case_duration_hours` as near-label leakage; `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `num_document_requests_total`, and `final_handler_team` as complete-trace/closure leakage; `prior_claim_count_before_t0` as a name-vs-SQL defect; `same_day_document_count_snapshot` as nightly refresh leakage; `queue_length_at_t0`, `initial_damage_complexity`, and `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` as keepable. The same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` pattern is correctly treated as process-suspicious *and* order-ambiguous *and* post-`t0`, which is the distinction the prompt is testing.

Section 2 is conceptually the right suite: point-in-time availability, full-trace vs prefix recomputation, normative enabledness, same-timestamp partial order, and appeal-justified repeated settlement decisions. The DFG caveat is stated clearly.

Section 3 uses the required classes and does not reflexively delete region/channel. Section 4 does not replay future outcomes in the narratives, marks X/Y as `recompute`, and correctly refuses a cleaned local story for Z. Noticing that leaked features contaminate the remaining SHAP values is a real plus. Section 5 covers every requested control.

**Defects that keep this well below a top score**

1. **Severity is sloppy and internally inconsistent.** The table is required to use `critical/high/medium/low`. The answer writes `Medium/High` and `High/Critical`. Worse, process-consistent but post-`t0` patterns are not graded the same way: `Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded` and `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` are Critical, while `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` is only High and documents is hedged. At this exact `t0` they are the same class of defect.

2. **Case Y puts `initial_reserve` in `credible_drivers` while Section 1 says lineage is unverified.** That is a logical contradiction. An unverified reserve belongs in `removed_or_repaired_drivers` until `reserve_effective_at <= t0` is proven. The same optimism appears with `claimant_channel`, which has no lineage note at all.

3. **The required taxonomy is blurred.** The prompt asks to distinguish temporal leakage, process impossibility, mutable lineage, correlated proxies, legitimate signals, and unsupported causal stories. The table invents long custom finding types and mostly audits *features*, not the team’s causal sentences (“Appeals cause breaches,” “The final team determines delay,” “Region is a delay driver”). Those unsupported causal stories are handled later, but not systematically in the audit table.

4. **A small process error.** Evidence cycles are specified as an alternative path *before* damage assessment, with fraud allowed in parallel. Saying evidence occurs “before or alongside later assessment work” flattens that.

5. **Section 2 is not an operational validation suite.** It describes layers well but never specifies concrete failing traces, assertions, or pass/fail artifacts (e.g., `feature(full) != feature(prefix) ⇒ drop`, quarantine of equal-timestamp edges without a sequence index). Alignment/footprint are named more than used.

6. **Revised explanations over-trust original directions.** After contamination, even the signs on queue, region, and channel are not known. Status `recompute` is right; presenting them as settled drivers is not.

7. **Minor looseness.** “Use appeal features where appeal is enabled” would still be zero at enablement. Remaining SLA is just a transform of elapsed time, oversold as a replacement family. Several features are filed in two or three classes without a primary assignment.

The audit would be usable in review. It is not tight enough, consistent enough, or operational enough to score higher under a strict reading.
