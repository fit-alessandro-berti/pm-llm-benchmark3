The answer has the right skeleton and two roughly correct queue labels (QA, QD), but it fails the central requirement of the task: reconstructing decision sets and scoring competing rules against those sets. Several of the comparisons it does attempt are factually wrong. Under a strict reading that is disqualifying for a high score.

**Section 1 is conceptually sloppy even where the label is defensible.**  
“Strongest contradicting episode” is repeatedly used to mean “evidence against a rejected alternative,” not evidence against the stated primary. For QA, A04 waiting behind A02 is *support* for FIFO, not a contradiction of it. That is a basic hypothesis-testing error and it recurs in Section 3. Confidence numbers are also poorly calibrated: QA FIFO-after-appointment is the cleanest single-server pattern in the file and is undersold at 60; QB “pre-emptive priority + SPT tie-break” is oversold at 55 given multiple unaddressed priority violations.

**QA: right headline, thin and partly inaccurate reconstruction.**  
FIFO plus a non-preemptive 08:20 appointment insert is the right primary story. But the 08:20 SPT cell is wrong or at best unreadable: waiting jobs are A02 (20), A03 (10), A04 (7).
