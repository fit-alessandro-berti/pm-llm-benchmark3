## Verdict and causal discipline

“Partly causal through mechanisms” at **75/100** is too strong for this sample. Overlap is thin, High/Nova has two cases, Orion-Day has one case, only completed cases are observed, and resource group is a routing bundle rather than a well-defined treatment. A disciplined answer would have been `not identifiable` or `mainly confounded`, or the same verdict at much lower confidence.

Section 1 does not cleanly separate **total**, **direct**, and **mediated** effects of assignment. Stratified means are treated as if they identify a beneficial direct Orion effect “when case mix is controlled.” That is exactly the move the prompt warned against. Complexity is not a sufficient adjustment set: shift, load, compliance, unobserved risk, and completion-selection remain.

The mechanism story is internally inconsistent. The executive summary cites Orion’s “longer queue waits (19.47 vs 11.12 h in High-complexity cases).” That comparison is false. **19.47 is Orion High; 11.12 is Nova Medium.** Nova High wait is **22.75**, so Orion is *shorter* in the same stratum. The same error is reused in the role table and twice in Section 5. Crude rework (28.6% vs 18.8%) is treated as a harmful Orion mechanism, but within strata the rework gap disappears or reverses (High 50/50; Medium 16.7 vs 25; Low 0 vs 10). Simpson’s paradox is noticed for cycle time and then ignored for the mediators used to justify “partly causal.”

## Graph, roles, and methods

The prompt asked for a **directed adjacency list**. A Graphviz `digraph` is not that. `routing_rule` is collapsed into an edge label instead of a node, which blurs the required distinction between treatment, routing policy, and mechanism bundle.

Several arrows are conceptually wrong: `case_completed → cycle_time` is not a selection structure; `cycle_time → quality_outcome [label="proxy"]` confuses association with causation; `resource_group → compliance_required` is implicit in the front-door proposal even though compliance is a **policy function of complexity**, not a consequence of group. Conditioning on complexity is described as risking “collider bias if mediated by resource_group.” Complexity precedes routing; that sentence is garbled.

The front-door test in hypothesis 4 is invalid. Estimating group → compliance_required → cycle_time treats a confounder-driven policy step as a mediator of treatment. Hypothesis 1 lists *shorter* Orion service time as evidence *for* a harmful skill effect. That is backwards.

Standardization uses **Nova’s** complexity mix. The decision is about moving **Orion** cases, so the policy-relevant weights are Orion’s (ATT-like). High/Nova n=2 still makes either contrast fragile; that is not emphasized.
