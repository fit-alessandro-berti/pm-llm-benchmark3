**7.9**

The write-up is well structured, honest about approximation, and the POWL model plus witness set are strong. It is not nearly flawless under a strict reading of “as faithful as possible.”

**What is solid.** Assumptions resolve the required points (risk XOR, optional supplier via τ, pass/fail as loop exit, no duplicate labels, conservative process-tree policy, overlap as missing order). The dependency table is implementation-independent. The process-tree and POWL code is self-contained, uses the requested constructors, sets parents/children, and wires POWL edges with `add_edge`. Witnesses hit every mandated case: low risk, high risk, supplier, two trial cycles, a tree-only rejection (A5), and a cross-branch forbidden trace (F2) with an earliest-violation reason. The comparison table and self-critique are specific rather than vague.

**Process tree is weaker than the brief asks.** “As faithful as possible” and the allowed duplication policy are not really used. Forcing approvals and supplier to finish before the trial is a clean *safe* under-approximation, but a compact XOR of timing variants (approvals/supplier before the trial vs. concurrent with the trial) would admit A5-style overlap without unsafe release and without inventing extra visible events. Choosing the coarsest safe tree is a real fidelity gap, not a cosmetic one.

**Small semantic slippages.** Section 3 says requirements 6, 7, *and 9* are only approximated. Release synchronization (9) is still enforced; the tree *over-satisfies* it by completing extra work earlier. Requirement 7 is what is actually overconstrained. Silent supplier is still a node that must complete before release; that is correct but only implicit. Nested POWL operators are treated as shuffle-concurrent with siblings; that is the intended reading, but it is not stated up front (only gestured at in the critique).

**Validation nits.** `pm4py.play_out(..., max_events=100)` is not a reliable API match (`no_traces` / `max_trace_length` / parameters dict are the usual knobs). Conversion/soundness/playout are the right checks, but the plan still treats successful construction as more confirmatory than the authors’ own Section 8 allows.

**Net.** High-quality modeling write-up with a faithful POWL model and a well-chosen counterexample set. Deducted mainly for a process tree that is safer and simpler than the task’s fidelity bar, plus a few precise semantic/API inaccuracies. Not in the 9–10 band.
