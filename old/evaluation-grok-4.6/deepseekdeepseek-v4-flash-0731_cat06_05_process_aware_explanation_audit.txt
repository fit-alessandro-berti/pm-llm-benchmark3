**6.8**

The audit is structurally complete and gets the main process-aware distinctions right, but it is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading it has several real inaccuracies, some process-blind remediations, and missing standards the prompt required.

**What it does well.** Section 1 covers every required item (top-12, all five patterns, local-only X/Y/Z features). Obvious full-trace leaks (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`) are correctly marked unusable. Subtler lineage bugs are also caught: extraction-date SQL on `prior_claim_count_before_t0`, the 23:00 `same_day_document_count_snapshot`, mutable `risk_score_current`, and missing sequence index. Legitimate prefix signals (`queue_length_at_t0`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, `initial_damage_complexity`) are preserved rather than discarded. The key quality test is passed: `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated as a *process-legal future* leak, while `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as *impossible / timestamp artifact*. Revised X/Y narratives do not replay leaked outcomes (no Partial, no appeal-close variant).

**Material flaws.**

1. **Invented probabilities.** Case Y’s “true probability is likely lower, perhaps around 0.55–0.60” is methodologically false. You cannot subtract or mentally adjust SHAP masses into a calibrated probability after feature deletion. That number is unsupported and should not appear in an audit of explanations.

2. **Process-blind “repair” of document-rework features.** Replacing `num_document_requests_total` with “count of requests up to t0” is presented as if it salvages an early-warning signal. Immediately after the first `Coverage Check Completed`, document cycles have usually not started, so the prefix count is near-constant zero and *cannot* be the team’s “early warning.” The same applies to `Documents Requested -> Documents Received`. A process-aware audit should say the *repaired* feature is typically uninformative at this prediction point, not merely that the full-trace total leaks.

3. **Required interpretive standards are not actually explained.** The prompt asked to distinguish predictive validity, causal interpretation, operational actionability, and fairness review. Section 3 lists classes and repeats “don’t claim causality,” but never states the four standards. Actionability is almost absent: `queue_length_at_t0` is much closer to an operational lever than `claimant_region`, and that difference is not drawn.

4. **Silent-leakage subtlety is underplayed.** `final_handler_team` matching the t0 team ~65% of the time is exactly the kind of warehouse field that *looks* valid in local explanations. Calling it “High / may not match t0” is directionally right but misses why this feature is especially dangerous.

5. **Lineage overconfidence.** `initial_reserve` has no lineage note in the prompt; treating it as known-at-registration without a caveat is an overclaim. For Case Z, `calendar_week = 52` is recommended as a recomputation input after Section 3 correctly labeled it a holiday/policy drift proxy—inconsistent. The same-day snapshot is only described as look-ahead leakage; a nightly 23:00 join is also *stale* (look-behind) if t0 is during the day.

6. **Smaller inconsistencies.** Complete-trace counts are split arbitrarily between Critical and High. `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` is the one pattern that could be prefix-adjacent (fraud may open right after coverage); it is lumped with unambiguously future patterns with no note. Team causal slogans (“appeals cause breaches,” “the final team determines delay”) are not audited as unsupported causal stories in Section 1.

**Bottom line.** This is a competent, on-rubric audit that would not fail a review: major leaks are identified, legitimate congestion/age signals are kept, and the two sequence patterns are distinguished correctly. It is not a high score because it invents a probability, mischaracterizes what a prefix document-count can do at this t0, and never delivers the four-standard analysis it was asked for. Those are not nits in a hypercritical reading.
