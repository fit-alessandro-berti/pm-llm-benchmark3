**4.5**

The write-up is complete in outline and hits every required heading, but it fails the task’s core demand: treat the POWL as an invariant source and refuse to equate conversion success with semantic fidelity. Several required distinctions are inverted, and the validation code cannot test the model it claims to audit.

**Section 1 is conceptually broken in the columns that matter most.** A negative witness is a forbidden behavior, not a second legal one. INV-02 lists `<Register Change, Prepare Evidence, Assess Risk>` as a negative witness while stating it is valid. INV-03 does the same with the skip path. INV-10’s positive witness never overlaps Legal Review with the verification loop (Legal Review occurs before Prepare Evidence, so verification has not started). After hiding τ, optionality *is* observable: traces with and without `Legal Review`. Marking that “No” confuses unobservability of the silent skip with unobservability of the choice. Loop *scope* is underspecified: nothing forbids `Correct Defect` from being mis-scoped inside `Configure Trial → Inspect Trial`, and nothing says Legal Review cannot precede Assess Risk.

**Section 4 overclaims in a way the prompt explicitly forbids.** Almost every POWL→Petri cell is `validated preserved` with no execution, no markings dump, no witness replay, and no footprints. That is `expected preserved` at best. The prompt already warned that returning an object is not fidelity. Petri→Tree is recorded as `conversion failed` as if observed. INV-10 BPMN round-trip as `lost` is equally unearned. Process trees are said to be unable to represent “true concurrency”; they can (`∧`). The real obstruction is non-block-structured overlap: optional legal depends only on Assess Risk, verification joins Assess Risk *and* Prepare Evidence, both join again at Release.

**Section 3 ignores an explicit constraint and is operationally fragile.** Markings are never checked for `None` before `convert_to_bpmn` / diagnostics. Pipeline 2 and `nets_to_check` assume Pipeline 1 names exist. `save_vis_powl` is never called. Unused object imports and `in locals()` guards are cosmetic, but the missing null-marking checks are not.

**Section 5 is the decisive failure.** Shortest visible complete trace has **seven** activities (`Register`, both unordered prefixes, `Configure Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, `Release`, `Archive`). `max_visible_length=5` makes the accepted-language sets empty, so missing/extra traces are empty and the comparison reports equivalence vacuously. Further defects:

- Visited key is *marking only*, so one interleaving of Assess Risk / Prepare Evidence (and of Legal Review vs. verification) suppresses the others — exactly the concurrency the audit is supposed to protect.
- τ is appended as `None` instead of being hidden.
- Custom firing mutates a copied marking into a plain dict; later `marking == fm` and `get_enabled_transitions` are then type-unsafe.
- Arc-weight, `execute`/`semantics`, and consecutive silent-cycle control are absent.
- Required positive/negative witness replay and playout are not implemented; unused `Trace`/`itertools` imports advertise the gap.

A non-exhaustive bound is acknowledged, but the bound is not merely incomplete — it cannot accept any source-complete trace.

**Sections 6–8 are the strongest parts** (layout ≠ semantics, silent XOR path, false left-to-right order, POWL-as-canonical, regenerate don’t edit). Even here, “process trees cannot represent true concurrency” is repeated, and rejection criterion 5 treats a marking as if it were an activity-order predicate.

**Section 9 is mixed.** Bounded language and BPMN-engine variance are fair residual risks. “May allow more iterations than intended” is not residual uncertainty: the source LOOP is already unbounded. Silent-skip unobservability is restated as if it were a validation hole rather than the definition of τ.

The document looks like a careful audit and is not. The invariant table misuses witnesses, the preservation table fabricates validation, and the round-trip explorer cannot see a single legal complete run. Those are not nits; they invert the method the task asked for.
