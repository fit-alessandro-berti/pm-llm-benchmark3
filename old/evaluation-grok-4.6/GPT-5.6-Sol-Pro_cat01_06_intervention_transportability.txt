**8.0**

The answer is a strong transportability memo: it rejects a literal copy of Site A, keeps statutory gates, treats the 41→32 hour movement as a confounded package association, and designs a segmented three-arm pilot that can separate mechanism failure from lock/implementation failure. Under a hypercritical standard it is not near-flawless. The defects below are real, not stylistic.

**Process and causal slips**

Site B already permits Identity Check and Originals Receipt to start after Register. The DFG confirms that this is not merely a rule on paper: Register→Identity Check (2,050) and Register→Originals Receipt (1,860). The remaining binding constraint on the originals majority is Document Check after receipt, plus the single-owner lock. Calling Identity∥Originals a transferred intervention therefore overstates incremental effect for 62% of volume. The optimistic scenario’s claim that “100% receive some concurrency” inherits that error. A tighter reading is: the only A-like control-flow change is digital/no-original Identity∥Document Check; for originals cases the live treatment is mostly lock-safe *execution* of an already-legal partial order.

The same DFG is underused. Identity Check→Document Check (1,160) is almost exactly the no-original share, which is evidence that digital cases are still sequential and that this—not originals—is the actual Site A analogue. That point should have anchored Sections 1, 5, and 9 more explicitly.

Stop/expansion/abandon thresholds disagree. Section 7 stops if p90 is >15% worse (~+16.5 h). Section 9 abandons at +10 h and expands only if p90 is no more than +5 h. A run can miss the stop rule and still exceed the abandon rule, or vice versa. Rework has the same inversion (stop +3 pp, abandon +2 pp). Decision rules that cannot be applied jointly are a design flaw in a memo whose value is operational.

**Quantitative and graph nits**

The 3.4 h perfect-overlap ceiling is the right calibration; the extra 1–3 h “queue gain” in the digital scenario still smuggles Site A’s confounded waiting-time drop into Site B ranges. Confidence 60 on that scenario is therefore a bit high given untested evidence-independence and unknown digital∩non-legal∩complete intersections.

`assessment_capacity → waiting_before_assessment` conflates time-until-both-checks-complete with assessment-queue delay. `queue_huddles → true_overlap` is a weak, underspecified edge. `API_latency` is signed as a magnitude while `S_API_latency` is a site-selection node; readable, but easy to mis-simulate.

**PM4Py fidelity (Section 8)**

The intended POWL is the right object: XOR of digital Document Check vs Originals≺Document, Identity unordered with that block, Assessment joining both, optional LOOP of Legal Review with a supplement body. Comments on data guards, resources, locks, and time are accurate.

The code is not trustworthy as written. `add_transitive_edges()` and `validate_partial_orders()` are not standard `StrictPartialOrder`/`BinaryRelation` APIs; if they are absent the script dies before conversion. `pm4py.project_on_event_attribute(simulated_log)` typically requires an attribute key. `add_only_if_fm_is_reached` is not a documented `play_out` parameter. Those are not comments—they are executable claims. For a required self-contained construction block, invented helpers are a material miss against “use constructors consistently with the supplied PM4Py manual.”

**What remains excellent**

Section 1 covers the mandated features and draws the correct design implication (do not create Document Check when originals are legally required). Section 3 refuses to identify a unique treatment effect and prices co-interventions (FTE, API, mix, huddles, timestamps) honestly. Sections 4–5 include the required tail-worsens-while-median-improves cases. The candidate intervention, logging spec, and policy-only vs lock-safe arms match the evaluation emphasis. `conditional go` is the only defensible recommendation.

Net: analogical and causal structure is well above competent; one structural mis-attribution, internally inconsistent decision thresholds, and non-runnable PM4Py helpers keep it well short of a 9+.
