## Material legal and logical errors

**1. P-501 misreads B4 (the largest case-level error).**  
B4 grants two independent emergency permissions: (i) commitment may precede the PO, and (ii) an *absent* Plant Manager may be replaced by an *actively delegated* Shift Lead. The answer fuses them. It treats the lack of delegation as defeating the commit-before-PO permission and as rendering SHIFT-1’s commitment “unauthorized.” That is not what B4 says. The emergency predicate is met (four-hour stoppage). Commit-before-PO is therefore allowed. Substitution is about Plant_Approve, not about who may commit. Plant later approved at 15:10; the manager was reachable, so the substitution clause is simply inapplicable.

What remains is B2 (Plant/Finance at €38k) with no express pre-commitment clock, plus GPIP-4 thresholds that this amount does not meet. High-confidence NONCOMPLIANT does not follow. The Section 5 “alternative” that would reclassify P-501 as JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION is in fact the better reading of B4; calling it weak because substitution failed is a non sequitur. This is both a rule error and an inverted alternative.

**2. Pseudo-logic contradicts the catalogue and B5.**  
Rule 2 says `normally require(R-B1,R-B2,R-B3) unless emergency_B4`. B5 expressly preserves Finance_Approve and the approved-supplier rule in emergency. The table for R-B4 admits this; the defeasible logic does not. A formalized catalogue that disagrees with itself is a process-method failure, not a drafting quibble.

**3. COMPLIANT vs JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION is not applied as defined.**  
Section 1: a valid advance overlay that authorized the conduct is COMPLIANT; a baseline deviation covered by competent emergency authority is JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. P-505 is called COMPLIANT because ECD plus D1 cover sequence and Finance. P-507 and P-508 rely on the same overlay for missing Plant/Finance and are called JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. The definitions do not support that split. Either all three are COMPLIANT or all three are justified exceptions. The label is doing rhetorical work, not legal work.

**4. Plant_Approve is treated inconsistently inside the ECD window.**  
P-502 lists missing Plant as a deviation contributing to NONCOMPLIANT. P-507/P-508 treat Plant as an “ordinary local workflow step” superseded by B7/C4. P-502 is the cleanest in-scope ECD purchase in the set. If Plant is ordinary workflow, it is ordinary on 14 July too. If it is a substantive budget control, it is substantive on 16–17 July too. The paper needs one premise and does not have one. (P-502 can still be NONCOMPLIANT on A2/A3 alone; the Plant point is surplus and confusing.)

**5. B2’s timing is promoted from interpretation to rule.**  
R-B2 states Plant/Finance as “inferred before commit by purpose.” A2 *says* “before the supplier commitment.” B2 does not. For P-501 (€38k) that inference is load-bearing, because GPIP A2 never attaches. Presenting it as a normalized rule, then scoring high-confidence breach against it, overclaims.

**6. The central A7 ambiguity is under-played as a cross-cutting alternative.**  
The prompt itself flags that GPIP-4 does not decide whether a COO + Incident Commander directive is an A7 exception, and that prior practice treated signed directives as overlays except in new-supplier Compliance cases. The paper assumes ECD is not A7 and mostly keeps that fight in the hierarchy “defeaters” cell. Section 5 then offers seven case-level forks but never the one premise that would move several holdings together: if ECD-2 *is* a scoped A7 instrument, A2/A3 timing on in-window critical lots looks very different. That is the alternative a skeptical reviewer would actually run.

## Case-level strictures

**P-505 as COMPLIANT is too strong for the paper’s own burden of proof.** Commitment is 29 seconds before expiry on the acknowledgment, ECD recording-within-four-hours is not shown, Create_PO is not in the record, and D1’s phrase “all operational approvals” is self-limited by D3–D4. Medium confidence does not make the *primary* label appropriate. INDETERMINATE or JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION is the classification that matches Assumption G. The Section 5 noncompliant fork is correctly described as moderate-to-strong; that should have disciplined the holding.

**A1 is under-analyzed where actor identity is missing.** P-501 actually has a clean triangle (ENG-1 / SHIFT-1 / later Plant) and A1 may be satisfied; that is not worked. P-502, P-505, P-506, and P-507 do not identify requester, committer, and approver. P-507’s “no self-dealing shown” treats absence of evidence as evidence of absence, which Assumption G forbids.

**C3 is rewritten.** C3 requires two distinct Incident Command members. The catalogue adds independence from requester/committer “by purpose.” That import from A1 is reasonable as interpretation and improper as a formalized rule. It then quietly supports the P-503 holding (which can rest on A1 directly) and the P-507 cleanliness claim (which cannot).

**Unapproved is equated with “new” for A3** without saying so. It does not change P-504 (B3 still bars the supplier) or P-507/P-508 (Compliance was obtained), but it is another silent premise.

**B6 is not applied systematically.** Only P-501 has a form. Other emergency/ECD purchases are not consistently scored against the 24-hour record rule that the catalogue itself lists.

**Receive_Goods** is named as a relevant activity and then ignored. Harmless, but it is an incomplete operational trace.

**Wording slips.** “Finance late cured in advance” (P-505) is incoherent. D1 is sometimes a Finance-meaning rule and sometimes a wand over “all operational approvals,” including Plant, which D3 does not give the CFO.

## Hierarchy and method nits

The hierarchy table is good and should have been the whole method. Two weakenings: (i) ECD “cannot override A2 Finance substance” is then functionally overridden after 15 July via D1, which is a *different* source and should have been stated as a dated, competence-limited qualification of A2 rather than left to the reader to reconcile; (ii) “probably cannot override” is used where the paper in practice treats the bar as categorical (A1, A3). Pick a stance.

The 5–12 lines of pseudo-logic are in the right genre (`normally` / `unless` / `overrides_for`) but line 2 is false, line 3’s `unless integrity_issue` is backwards (ECD never reaches integrity; it does not override-unless), and line 7 smuggles the rewritten C3.

Burden of proof is stated more strictly than it is used. P-505 and P-507 get the benefit of unproven elements (PO time, IC recording, independence). P-506 correctly does not. That double standard is visible.
