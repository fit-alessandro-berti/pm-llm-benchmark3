**6.4**

The write-up is a competent transportability memo that correctly refuses a naive copy of Site A, treats the nine-hour drop as confounded, and keeps statutory `Originals Receipt → Document Check` intact. It is not close to flawless. Under a strict reading of the rubric, model fidelity, quantitative consistency, and a few structural facts are wrong enough to bar any high score.

**What is actually strong.** Sections 1–4 get the main causal story right: Site A’s before/after is a bundle of parallelization, +2 FTE, API latency, huddles, mix shift, and better timestamps; Site B lacks positivity for A’s treatment on the 62% originals-required mass; specialization and the single-owner lock break intent→overlap; legal/supplement loops and a 3+2 assessor/legal pool can eat any verification win. The required feature list is covered. The DAG includes every mandated node plus `S_` selection nodes, and the 180–280 word note correctly says a similar control-flow diagram is not S-admissible. Co-interventions are not waved through. There is a required median-up/tail-down scenario. The recommendation `conditional go` with a digital-first stratum is the right policy shape.

**Process structure is sloppy in a way that matters.** Site B’s *current rules already allow* Identity and Originals Receipt to start after Register. DFG counts (`Register → Identity` 2,050, `Register → Originals` 1,860) show that partial order is already in use. Section 9 then sells `Identity ∥ Originals wait` as “the transportable kernel” of Site A. That is mostly enforcement of an existing legal partial order plus an IT/WIP fix, not a transfer of A’s `+(Identity, Document)`. The real new treatment is full document parallelization on digital cases. The answer knows this in §6 and forgets it when it rhetorically summarizes. Site B concurrency is also mislabeled “sequential convergence”; the interesting fact is permitted concurrency with 6% realized overlap.

**Numbers do not survive a desk check.** Digital share is 38% in §5 and 25–30% (digital ∩ non-legal) in §6–9, with no covariance of channel and legal status. Stratum O is “n~620” while 62% of a 12-week load at 3,400/26 ≈ 131 cases/week is ~970. Power text assigns “130 eligible/week” to stratum D (that is the *site* arrival rate) and claims 500/arm per stratum, which 12 weeks cannot produce. The 5h eligible waiting/cycle gain sits on top of a 3–5h discounted A effect and a 3.4h `min(Identity, Document)` service cap; those three figures are not reconciled. Mix-shift “8pp × 20–30h” is invented. Queue saturation and assessment bottleneck are collapsed into one modifier row. Expansion text “rework  +1.5pp” is missing the inequality. These are not rounding errors; they change whether the pilot is powered and whether the population effect is −2h or noise.

**Section 8 would not run and does not encode the candidate.** `OperatorPOWL.XOR` / `OperatorPOWL.LOOP` are not the PM4Py operators; those live on `pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj.Operator`. `children=[legal_loop, None]` is not a silent transition. `POWL(powl_main)` is not how a POWL is built—`StrictPartialOrder` already *is* a POWL. The same `Transition` instances are reused in the main PO and the supplement redo, which breaks node identity. The LOOP redo includes `t_legal` *and* the body is `t_legal`, so a supplement path reviews legally twice before it can exit. `check_is_workflow_net(..., fm_A_after)` passes the after net’s final marking into the before check. `play_out` is aimed at a POWL object. The Petri net always forces `Originals → Document`, i.e. it is not the segmented intervention of §6 and it is not an XOR of digital vs originals. Comments about what control-flow cannot encode are fine; they do not excuse a non-executable, legally over-constrained model. The prose POWL in §6 is the same mess: Assessment is both outside the SPO node set and the target of its edges; the “equivalent” edge list always requires Originals Receipt.

**Smaller cuts that still count under a hypercritical bar.** `case_complexity → *_capacity` is a category error (service time ≠ server count); `S_identity_capacity` / `S_document_capacity` are missing despite 18–20 vs 4+7 FTE. Copying A’s WIP-6 into specialized pools is asserted, not derived. §9 is at or over the 400-word cap and contains “At At”. Assumption 3/5 typography (`intentexecution`, `17%20%+`) is careless. DFG decision out-edges sum to 3,300 vs 3,400 completed cases and are never discussed.

**Net.** Analogical and transport reasoning is the best part of the script and is why this is not a mid-fail. The evaluation asked for process-faithful models, internally consistent scenario math, and a pilot that could actually be staffed by Site B’s arrival process. Those parts are not reliable. A nearly flawless answer would have shipped a runnable POWL with a digital/originals choice, a LOOP that is `Legal × (Supplement → Document → Assessment)`, one eligibility definition used everywhere, and a power calc on the true stratum base rate. This is a strong draft with a broken artifact and contradictory quantities.
