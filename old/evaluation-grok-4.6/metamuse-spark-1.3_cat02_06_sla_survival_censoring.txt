## What keeps the score from falling further

The memo does not treat withdraw/transfer as ordinary non-informative censoring without discussion, does not restrict analysis to resolved cases, and does not treat `Escalate` as a safe baseline predictor. Case IDs are used throughout. Cohort non-randomization and incomplete AutoCheck compliance are handled as confounding, not as a causal policy effect. Those are the core process-mining requirements, and they are met.

It is a good working analysis with several real errors. It is not a near-flawless one.
