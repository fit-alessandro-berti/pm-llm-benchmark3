**6.5**

The audit is competent, complete in coverage, and correctly handles the obvious leakages. It is not close to flawless. Several required distinctions are blurred, and one repaired-vs-usable contradiction is material.

**What is actually strong.** Every top-12 feature, sequence pattern, and local-only driver is in the Section 1 table. Complete-trace features (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `num_document_requests_total`, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`) are correctly killed. Subtle lineage bugs are seen: nightly `same_day_document_count_snapshot`, `claim_created_at <= extraction_date`, missing validity time on `final_handler_team`, mutable `risk_score_current`. Legitimate prefix signals (`queue_length_at_t0`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, `initial_damage_complexity`) are kept. Region/channel are not naively deleted. X/Y are set to recompute; Z is invalidated. Revised narratives do not replay appeal, variant path, Partial, or Close. Section 5 hits every requested workstream in a sensible order.

**Required distinction is only half-done.** The rubric asked to treat `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` differently from the same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` pattern. Finding-type labels differ, which is something, but the Why text calls the appeal pattern “impossible at t0” and both rows just say discard. That collapses temporal unavailability into process impossibility. The appeal edge is a *legal* continuation (reconsideration after Partial/Deny). The closed-before-assessment edge is either impossible or an unordered same-timestamp artifact. Section 2 later admits repeated settlement decisions after appeal, so the knowledge is in the answer and not applied where it matters.

**Internal contradiction on `prior_claim_count_before_t0`.** Section 1 marks the SQL as High lineage defect. Section 3 then files the same feature under “defensible predictive signal.” Section 4 uses `= 3` as a Case Y credible driver. That violates the instruction to use only information defensibly available at `t0`. The count is not trustworthy until `claim_created_at < t0` is enforced. It belongs in `removed_or_repaired_drivers`, not in the narrative.

**Section 2 does not do what was asked.** Prefix, order, same-timestamp, complete-trace, enabling, and appeal-repeat checks are listed. Alignment, footprint, prefix, and declarative methods are barely named; DFG limitations are omitted. The prompt explicitly warned that a frequent DFG edge is not a normative rule. The suite treats Petri-net/DFG conformance as if frequency or a mined graph could police the normative model.

**Four standards are not actually separated.** Predictive validity, causal interpretation, operational actionability, and fairness review are mashed into a may/may-not table. That is not the same as stating the standards. Queue length can be predictive *and* a candidate staffing lever without being a proven intervention effect. Region can be predictive and still fail fairness/actionability. The text never draws that grid cleanly.

**Revised explanations reintroduce unsupported mechanism stories.** Broker “communication overhead” and a €25,000 reserve implying “scrutiny” are exactly the causal genre the audit is supposed to refuse. `initial_reserve` has no lineage note; calling it Legitimate/Low is overconfident. `final_handler_team` as “35% risk of leakage” understates the defect: the 65% match is coincidental equality without a historical as-of timestamp, so the field is not a reliable `t0` attribute even when it matches.

**Smaller misses that still count.** `same_day_document_count_snapshot` and a repaired document-request count never appear in Section 3’s classes. `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` is under-graded as Medium if the explainer is a complete-trace miner. Case Z’s note ignores week 52 as a documented holiday/policy confounder and ignores same-day document snapshot leakage. `num_document_requests_total` is correctly future-leaked; the parenthetical that a prefix count would be 0 is right and should have been used to kill the “early warning” story more sharply.

This is a solid practitioner writeup with the hard leakages right and the required conceptual work incomplete. Under a strict reading of the spec, that is mid-6s, not 8+.
