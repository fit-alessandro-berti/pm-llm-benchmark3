The causal and process-structural core is strong: Site A’s 9-hour median move is treated as a confounded composite, the statutory originals gate is not parallelized, identity work is correctly hidden under the ~18h originals delay for most of Site B, bottleneck-shift (mean up, tail down) is named, and the recommendation is a redesigned trigger-based intervention plus a stratified T1/T2/C pilot rather than a copy of Site A. **Code and process-model fidelity (largest deduction).** Section 8 is a required artifact and would not be a faithful, runnable construction of the stated Site B candidate.

- `verdict` is built with only `t_legal` in `nodes`, then `verdict.add_edge(t_legal, dec_in_legal)` attaches an `OperatorPOWL` that is not a child of the partial order. That is invalid POWL construction; leftover dead code (`nodes=[t_legal, *([])]`) makes the intent obvious and the fix unapplied.
- The legal/supplement loop is semantically wrong. PM4Py `LOOP(do, redo)` runs `do`, then optionally `redo` then `do` again. Putting `Legal Review → XOR(Approve, Reject)` inside `do` forces a final decision *before* any `Request Supplement`. The DFG is `Legal Review → Request Supplement` (360) without an intervening decision. The compact POWL in §6 repeats the same error (`LegalReview` both before the loop and inside `do`).
- The prompt asked for `pm4py.play_out(...)`. The code calls `playout_alg.apply(..., fm=..., no_traces=...)`, which does not match the usual high-level or algorithm APIs (`fm` is not a standard playout argument; `no_traces` belongs in a parameters dict). `pm4py.check_soundness(...)` is commonly a boolean; indexing `[0]` is unsafe.
- Duplicate-label transitions for digital vs originals `Document Check` are a reasonable XOR stand-in, but the model still always fires `Identity Check` after `Register`, while the DFG has `Register → Identity Check` in only 2,050/3,400 cases. That heterogeneity is never encoded or even flagged as a modeling gap.

**Transport graph.** All listed names appear, but `queue_huddles` is a dead node (`S_queue_huddles → queue_huddles` and nothing else), despite huddles being one of Site A’s main co-interventions and being discussed in prose. `API_latency` is wired; huddles are not. That is a missing confounding path, not a cosmetic omission.

**Quantitative and DFG use.** Anchors (3.4h digital ceiling; 6h wait drop exceeding that ceiling; originals critical path ≈ 26h) are the right ones. The S1 cell formula `0.38×2.0–3.4h + 0.62×1–3h×0.7, 0.7` is opaque. S2 transport confidence 65 is too high given unidentified A effects, the lock, and a 3-assessor merge. DFG structure is used only shallowly: 1,160 `Identity → Document` vs 2,108 `Originals → Document` support the 38/62 split, but the 2,050 identity-after-register mass, 345 supplement returns, and 1,360 legal cases are not used to constrain loop rates, skip rates, or scenario weights.

**Pilot and smaller defects.** T1 vs T2 vs control, backfill exclusion, and stop rules are the right design. Power is asserted (“250/arm, 1.5h”) with no spread, despite p90 = 110h; that MDE is likely optimistic. Prose glitches (`capacityqueue`, `complexityreworkappeal`) look like dropped arrows. WIP/role numbers are directionally right but never turned into even a rough utilization check against 3,400 cases and the 8.1h document stage.

Broken POWL/LOOP semantics, likely non-runnable Section 8, an unused required graph node, and over-precise scenario confidence keep this well below a high score.
