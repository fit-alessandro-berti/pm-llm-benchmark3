**8.5**

This is a strong, rubric-aligned audit. It treats April as a schema/pipeline incident, June as a real mix/routing change that does not imply concept drift, August–September as overlapping process, concept, and population shifts, and October as censoring-dominated. It never treats October’s 0.16 as comparable to mature months, and it triangulates predictive metrics with variant rates, DFG edge times, and Partner Z evidence.

**What holds up.** The diagnosis table separates observed evidence from gaps, and most confidence ratings are earned. Error-direction is used well (April overpredicts with healthy labels; Aug–Sep underpredict; October’s apparent overprediction is an artifact). July is correctly read as V3 drift hidden by aggregate calibration. Mix reconstructions (Jun ~0.16, Aug ~0.31, Sep ~0.34, Oct ~0.33) match the tables. Tests pair competing hypotheses with falsifiers. The staged response is proportionate: embargo April/October, do not retrain on June, escalate the supplier issue operationally, add features, then retrain only on matured vintages. Alert dependencies correctly stop a label-delay alarm from triggering retraining. The six required failure modes are present with mitigations.

**Deductions (strict).**

1. **Test 5 overstates a result it has not yet produced.** Expected matched V3 rates are given as ~0.64 vs ~0.35. Those are unmatched matured V3 rates. If within-V3 mix changed, the matched contrast could be smaller. The test design is right; the numeric forecast is not.

2. **Mix reconstructions are treated more cleanly than the data allow.** Variant shares are over started cases; variant breach rates are over matured labels. At 93% maturity that bias is small; at 82% and 55% it is not. The directional claim still stands, but the point estimates are presented with too little qualification in the table.

3. **The code sketch has a real API trap.** `pm4py.get_variants` often returns variant → list of traces, not counts. Adding `c` as if it were an int will throw, get swallowed by `except Exception: pass`, and leave all shares as `None`. A sketch may be version-tolerant; silent empty buckets are not auditable. The `or` fallback on edge medians also treats a legitimate `0.0` as missing. Duplicate counts are computed and then never returned.

4. **Minor unclarities.** “KIT-style” is unexplained (Kitagawa is the charitable reading). The Jan–Mar 28h→35h edge move is a mild trend, not a clear “early warning.” Stage 1 global/variant recalibration on pre-July months would not fix supplier-driven V3 underprediction; the V3 lead-time offset is the actual containment, and that dependence could be stated more sharply.

No major misdiagnosis, no collapsed single-cause story, and no misuse of October’s observed rate. The remaining defects are precision, one test-result slip, and sketch-level robustness — enough to keep it short of near-flawless.
