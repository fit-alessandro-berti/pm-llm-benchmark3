**5.4**

This is a long, well-organized design that hits the required section list and is clearly not a naïve independent-case DES. It still fails several hard requirements and contains enough process, statistical, and numerical errors that it cannot be scored as strong under a strict rubric.

**What is actually good.** The twin boundary, entities, calendars, queues, and join-before-drafting logic are specified at a usable level of detail. Non-stationary arrivals, architect bursts, district travel, and inspection bundling are in scope rather than waved away. Missing objections are treated as latent and proxied by public-notice duration, not as observed events. Service vs waiting is not silently collapsed: the text states that completion-only timestamps make inter-event times a mixture of queueing and work. The skeleton avoids the nonexistent `pm4py.read_csv`. There is no claim of optimality. Validation types and decision metrics are enumerated in the requested categories.

**Hard requirement failures in the code.** The question required `pm4py.format_dataframe`, `pm4py.parse_process_tree` (or a precise limitation note), and PM4Py computation of variants, DFG, **performance DFG**, and arrival statistics, with PM4Py kept separate from the engine. Section 8 never imports `pm4py`. `format_dataframe_for_pm4py` only parses timestamps and sorts. Variants and the DFG are hand-rolled. Performance DFG is absent. The process tree is never parsed. Section 2’s excuse is also wrong: `parse_process_tree` expects a process-tree string (`->`, `+`, `X`, `*`, `tau`), not “Petri-net formats.” `pm4py.get_variants_as_tuples` is not a reliable public API. Arrival generation and routing exist only as TODOs / callables the runner never defines. Cross-case bundling is described in prose and missing from the event loop. That is not a complete skeleton against the spec; it is a custom pandas rewrite plus an empty DES.

**Broken experiment and initialization logic.** Warm-up is 4 weeks while the authors themselves cite a ~45-day mean cycle time and 140/260-day complex tails. An empty start plus a warm-up shorter than the mean sojourn does not produce a representative backlog. The runner then generates arrivals only after `warm_up_end`, so the warm-up interval contains **no cases at all**. Seed policy contradicts CRN: `42 + 10*exp_id + rep_id` assigns different streams per policy, then the CRN paragraph says to reuse seed 42. Ten replications and normal `±1.96 SE` intervals are thin for p90/SLA-95% on heavy tails; Welch / MSER warm-up diagnostics are missing. Stress cases are counted as five extra rows (16 configs), not crossed with D1–D10, so robustness of interventions is not actually estimated.

**Unrealistic and internally inconsistent quantities.** D1’s expected “median 8d, p90 15d” is incompatible with the supplied variant table (simple clean path already 12/24d; new-residential fire+notice 48/95d). D3’s “inspection service 10 min” and “net cycle time 3d” ignore the 1h inspection plus travel. D10’s 25/50d similarly ignores the complex-variance tail that D1–D4 barely touch. Clustering is stated as both “top 20 architects = 31% of cases” and “3–5% of arrivals from a cluster process.” Routing then forces all New residential through fire + public notice, but those cases are 28% of mix and the fire+notice variant is only 18%. Completeness-check repeats after missing documents are counted as rework; in the given tree that path is the loop body, not `Completeness rework`. D5 is described as removing a legal block **before** decision drafting; in the tree legal is `X(tau,'Legal check')` **after** drafting.

**Assumptions presented as facts.** Council weeks = first week of every month, 15% objection rate, 85/15 public-notice mixture, SLA cutoffs of 30/50/70/150d, utilization bands, and EUR cost figures are invented. The 50d / 70d SLAs sit at or below historical medians (48d, 68d) while requiring 95% compliance against p90s of 95d and 130d — they are not operational targets, they are contradictions. Replay of 500 stratified cases with “actual rework decisions” cannot reproduce historical waits unless the full concurrent workload and calendars are replayed; MAE < 10% of median is an unmeetable acceptance bar given the stated data gaps. Odd/even-month “cross-validation” breaks the non-stationary structure the rest of the design insists on.

**Net.** Competent outline, unsafe numbers, incorrect PM4Py usage, and a skeleton that does not do what the prompt required. Under strict grading that is mid-pack, not a high pass.
