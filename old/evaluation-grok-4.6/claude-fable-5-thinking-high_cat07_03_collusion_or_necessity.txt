**Material methodological errors in §4.** Discovering a DECLARE model from the log and then checking conformance does not test the *normative* rule that every Emergency Override must be followed by Retrospective Justification by a different person. Discovery recovers frequent observed constraints; a often-violated control may be weak or absent in the mined model. The same-resource pandas check is the only part of that block that actually answers the audit question.

High-level helpers such as `filter_four_eyes_principle`, `discover_handover_of_work_network`, and `discover_subcontracting_network` are given a `resource_key` argument that the public signatures generally do not accept. The text calls this analysis, not loose pseudocode, so the API errors count.

Severity labels such as “Medium-High” and “Low-Medium” blur a table that is otherwise precise.
