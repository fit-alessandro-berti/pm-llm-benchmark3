Calling the P1/P2 verdicts a “Formal Proof” is inflated. Non-equivalence of P1 is shown by counterexample, which is enough; it is not a proof of the stated definition.

**Required soundness checking is not done.** Section 5 lists it as step 3 and then only prints place/transition counts, with a comment that process trees are sound by construction. The prompt asked to call `pm4py.check_soundness(...)`. Sound-by-construction is plausible, but it is not what was asked, and it is used to justify a “High (Exhaustive Exploration)” P3 verdict that the document never shows running.

**The explorer is untrusted.** Unique traces are only sampled (`[:3]`), while the prompt asked to print traces unique to each model. Visited keys are `(marking, visible_trace)`. If `Marking.copy()` returns a plain `Counter`, those keys are unhashable and `marking == fm` can fail; the write-up never addresses this. **Section 6 reports a specific EMD (~0.1387) with no run.** Declaring 0.5/0.5 branching does not license a three-decimal distance. That number reads as invented. The qualitative point stands — nonzero EMD refutes equality, low EMD does not prove it, footprints are local — but a fabricated statistic is a serious defect in a formal-comparison write-up.
