**2.0**

This submission has the required skeleton and a few accidentally correct headlines (P1/P3 “not equivalent”; unbounded vs. one `Check Revision`). Almost every technically load-bearing claim is wrong. Under a strict reading it is not a competent formal comparison.

### Fatal semantic errors

`tau` is treated as a **time delay**. In process trees it is a silent transition. That mistake contaminates the equivalence notions, the P1/P2 abstractions, and the discussion of hidden behavior.

The operators are misread throughout:

- `+` is an AND-split, not sequence. In **A**, `Check Revision` is concurrent with `Analyze Revision` inside each iteration, not “after” it. In **B**, `Check Revision` is concurrent with the **entire** loop, not “once before the loop.”
- A is described as having “no concurrency” and “multiple revisions in parallel.” The opposite is true: A’s concurrency is intra-iteration `Analyze ∥ Check`; B is the more concurrent model (`Check` may overlap `Rework` and several analyses).
- B is said to have **no** `Approve Revision`. B contains `X('Approve Revision', tau)` in every loop body.

Loop semantics are also ignored. The redo child is `Rework Case`; after every rework the do-child must run again. A complete trace cannot end `… Rework Case, Close Case`.

### Equivalence notions (Section 1)

None of the four notions is defined precisely enough to decide inclusion.

- Visible-trace equivalence is framed as ignoring delays, not as equality of languages after deleting silent labels.
- P3 is described as **simplifying the trees** to ≤12 events. The bound is on **complete traces**, not on the models.
- P1 is defined so that `Approve Revision` is silent. The brief says the number and placement of approvals stay **visible and relevant**.
- P2 is defined as collapsing approvals to a Boolean. P2 **hides** `Approve Revision` entirely and collapses **repeated checks**, while still recording the **count** of `Analyze Revision`.
- The duplicate-label note contradicts itself (position vs. identity vs. “only identity for P2”).

### Verdicts (Section 3)

| View | Their verdict | Correct direction | Why their reason fails |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | not equivalent | right | B *has* concurrency and approvals; the real gap is **loop-scoped pairing** of check with each analysis |
| P2 | not equivalent | **wrong** | Under P2 both languages abstract to the same observations: Open first, Close last, `n` analyses, check occurred = true, rework iff `n≥2`, approvals hidden |
| P3 | not equivalent | right | The reason (“A exceeds B’s bound”) is incoherent; both are unbounded. They differ on **which** bounded traces they admit |

P2 should be **equivalent only under the stated abstraction**. Calling it “not equivalent” with high confidence is a core mistake.

### Counterexamples (Section 4) — all invalid

Required: ≥2 traces in A∖B and ≥2 in B∖A, plus at least one two-analysis witness.

| ID | Claim | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| T1 `Open, Analyze, Check, Rework, Close` | A yes, B no | **Neither**. After `Rework` the do-child must run again |
| T2 `Open, Analyze, Approve, Check, Close` | A yes, B no | **Inverted**. In A, `Approve` is after the AND-join, so it cannot precede `Check`. In B this interleaving is legal |
| T3 `Open, Analyze, Analyze, Check, Close` | A yes, B no | **Neither**. Two analyses require `Rework` between them; A also requires a check in the first iteration |
| T4 `Open, Check, Analyze, Close` | listed as a difference | **Both** admit it; not a counterexample |

So the answer supplies **zero** valid distinguishing traces. Easy correct witnesses they missed:

- A∖B: `Open, Analyze, Check, Rework, Analyze, Check, Close` (two checks)
- A∖B: `Open, Analyze, Check, Approve, Rework, Analyze, Check, Close`
- B∖A: `Open, Analyze, Approve, Check, Close` (approve before check)
- B∖A: `Open, Analyze, Rework, Analyze, Check, Close` (check after rework / missing per-iteration check)

Optional `Approve` / hidden `tau` are never used to explain why some traces coincide after hiding and others do not.

### Simplified model (Section 8)

The proposed tree is not a simplification of either original and does not preserve P1:

```text
->( 'Open Case', *( ->('Check Revision', 'Rework Case'), 'Close Case' ) )
```

- `Analyze Revision` is deleted — the activity P1 is about.
- `Close Case` is the **redo** child, so “close” can occur in the middle and the process need not end with close.
- Approvals are not retained, silently or otherwise.
- The preservation table is empty/unreadable.
- “Regression” traces are traces the original **rejects**, i.e. failed preservation.
- Structure is never compared to A or B, violating the “do not call it simpler without comparison” rule.

A P1-faithful simplification would stay close to A, e.g. sequentialize the inner AND:

`->(Open, *( ->(Analyze, Check, X(Approve, tau)), Rework), Close)`.

### Recommendation (Section 9)

It recommends replacing B with this broken tree for P1 and P3, and treats A as “more detailed” rather than as the only model that matches P1’s pairing constraint. The bounded-comparison warning is boilerplate and does not say that P3 is evidence, not a proof.

### What keeps it above 1.0

Required headings exist; min/max check counts are right; P1 and P3 verdicts happen to be correct; there is a vague (correct) remark that EMD/footprints do not prove language equality.

That is not enough. The answer would actively mislead on operators, loop exits, P1/P2 observation functions, and every concrete trace. Strictly, this is a **2.0**.
