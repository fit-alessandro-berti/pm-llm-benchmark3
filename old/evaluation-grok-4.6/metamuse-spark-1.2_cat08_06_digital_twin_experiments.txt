**6.3**

The answer is a competent, well-structured digital-twin design that hits the required section contract and the main modeling constraints (non-stationary demand, calendars, cross-case coupling, latent objections, no optimality claim). It is not close to flawless. Under strict grading the technical errors, false PM4Py claims, sloppy experiment writing, and several conceptual shortcuts are enough to keep it well out of the 8–10 band.

**What is actually strong.** Scope and state are at the right grain: activity-level DES, resource pools with calendars, a join/synchronizer before decision drafting, and explicit cross-case state (architect bundles, district inspection buffer, latent objection shock). Arrivals as NHPP with season × DOW × architect clustering is the correct family. Waiting is treated as an emergent queueing outcome rather than a sampled sojourn, and missing start times are handled with deconvolution plus an honest “convolved sojourn” caveat. Objections are a mixture/latent process, not fake observed events. Uncertainty treatment (Welch, WIP seeding, CRN, BCa/percentile intervals, Sobol, heavy-tail metrics, paired differences) is the best part of the write-up. Decision criteria are multi-objective and appropriately refuse a single optimum.

**Process-tree / PM4Py grounding is the clearest failure against the spec.** The required convention is `pm4py.parse_process_tree` on the given `-> / * / + / X / tau` string. That *is* PM4Py process-tree syntax. The answer instead asserts the string is “illustrative pseudocode, NOT valid PTML,” imports an internal `pm4py.objects.process_tree.utils.parse`, expects it to fail, and falls back to inductive discovery. That is a factual error, not a careful limitation note. Section 2 also invents or mangles APIs (`pm4py.discovery.dfg.algorithm`, `pm4py.discovery.process_tree.discover.process_tree_inductive`, `pm4py.conformance.diagnostics_alignments`, `get_resource_profiles`, “footprint rework count”). The skeleton mixes `format_dataframe` output (a DataFrame) with `pm4py.statistics.variants.log` / `pm4py.algo.discovery.dfg` log modules, and passes `variant="frequency"` / `"performance"` as strings rather than `Variants.*`. High-level calls that *do* exist (`discover_process_tree_inductive`, `get_case_arrival_average` with a fallback) do not cancel the false parse claim or the inconsistent internal paths.

**Calendars are designed in prose and then contradicted in code.** Clerks are specified Mon–Fri 08:00–16:00 with a Monday portal clerk; the skeleton sets clerks to `[[1]*24 for _ in range(7)]` (24/7). Fire, inspector, and legal calendars are comments only. Architect burst is advertised on `ArrivalGenerator` and then not applied in `next_arrival`. For a task that forbids ignoring calendars and independent-case arrivals, a skeleton may be incomplete, but it should not implement the opposite of the stated calendar.

**Experiment matrix is conceptually right and editorially broken.** Baseline, D1–D10, a combined policy, and several stresses are present, with real trade-offs (D2 defect risk, D3 first-in-bundle delay, D5 re-audit, D6 priority inversion). Then the text loses operators: `12d  ~7d`, `8d5d`, `130110d`, `1.5d0.8d`, `0.60.35` — the hypotheses become unreadable. The run plan disagrees with the table: “full factorial D1–D4 (16)” is not in the table; D5–D9 have no replication/stress assignment; S4/S5 sit in the table and then disappear from the 4×3 stress block. Effect sizes (−40% missing docs, 60% of Simple eligible, p=0.2 legal re-audit, 30% travel saving) are presented as levels rather than parameters to be elicited or swept.

**Several design choices are sloppy or internally inconsistent.**
- D2 depends on “no prior violations,” which is absent from the log. `violation_history` is listed as an entity field and then never estimated.
- Variant shares (18% + 13%) are misused as if they were P(fire).
- D6 is redefined as a same-planner continuity bonus (−15% rework). That is not the stated intervention.
- Rework is memoryless Bernoulli with a max of 3; appeal/unstructured reasons are waved into the Complex tail.
- Peak months get 1.7× vs a 1.0 shoulder; Jan–Feb vs Jun–Aug/Dec is unargued.
- Extra districts D1–D6 are invented.
- “From pilot 5 runs, warm-up ~90 days” is written as an empirical result that was not obtained.

**Validation overclaims and mixes the wrong objects.** Token-replay fitness >0.85 is process-model conformance, not a simulation-validity test, especially on completion-only logs. χ² p>0.05 as a pass/fail validation criterion is a textbook misuse of significance tests. Trace-driven infinite-capacity replay does not recover enabled times when starts are missing. A last-3-month holdout under strong seasonality is not a clean OOS test unless the seasonal component is frozen and stated. Face validation and utilization bands are fine; the bottleneck and cross-case checks are only sketched.

**Assumptions are mostly labeled, but many numbers are presented as known.** Travel −30%, objection correlation 0.6, tail underestimation “2–3%,” Env 40% third-party, council-week 90% blackout — all acceptable as *explicit* priors, not as calibrated facts. Inter-arrivals are called “not heavy-tailed” while architect clustering is the whole point of the arrival model.

**Code is modular in the right way** (PM4Py confined to extraction; DES placeholders for arrivals, routing, resources, experiments; no `pm4py.read_csv`; sojourn not silently split). It is not faithful enough to the stated PM4Py contract, and the executable fragments that do exist (calendars, bursts, tree parse) undermine the surrounding design.

Net: strong simulation-design instincts and uncertainty thinking, undermined by a missed required PM4Py parse path, incorrect/unstable APIs, calendar/code contradiction, D2 data-gap, and a damaged experiment table. That is a mid-6, not a high-quality reference answer.
