**7.6**

The final write-up follows the required headings, treats the given alignments as authoritative, and does the core job well: it does not equate `raw_cost` with risk, flags the zero-cost P1/P2 failures (CL-609, CL-610), treats `T(tau)` as fitting, and refuses to read a missing event as proof of non-performance. The materiality scale, pattern grouping, competing hypotheses, and section 6 argument are substantively strong. It is not nearly flawless.

**Material errors and sloppy inferences**

- **CL-606 policy overlay is wrong.** P2 requires Fraud Screen and Manual Approve before pay. Those moves are synchronous. The gap is a missing *Assess Loss* log event plus an external adjuster report—a tree/evidence issue, not a P2 miss. “P2 substantively likely met if external report is accepted” misattributes the control.
- **CL-609 over-reads P2.** P1 forbids Auto Approve. P2 does *not* mandate Fraud Screen + Manual Approve for EUR 18k / MEDIUM / no bank change. “Required approval path not followed” is an inference from the XOR in the tree, not from the overlay as written. The severity-4 call is still defensible; the legal basis is not.
- **CL-602 is lumped with CL-606/CL-608** as one “external evidence” pattern. Those are different: possible unperformed fraud control vs. external work that likely occurred vs. a mutable CRM pointer. The per-case text hedges; the pattern table blurs operational failure and logging/correlation failure.

**Remediation gap**

The brief asked for **data-aware conformance** as a detective technique. Priority 2 is operational pre-release guardrails. Useful, but it is not attribute-aware conformance checking on the log. Model repair, correlation, investigation, and control redesign are present; that specific item is not.

**Code (section 7) is not cleanly executable as advertised**

- `print(f"Case {df.iloc[idx//1]['case_id'] if False else ''}")` is leftover junk: case IDs never print, and `iloc[idx]` indexes *events*, not cases.
- `multi_processing=False` is a version-fragile kwarg and may throw.
- `format_dataframe` does not reliably yield an object `conformance_diagnostics_alignments` accepts without `convert_to_event_log` (or an equivalent) on many PM4Py versions.

The hardcoded supplied-move frame, separate `raw_cost` vs `business_severity_1_5`, and the “evidence table, not a formula” disclaimer are correct.

**Smaller issues**

- Severity 5 on CL-610 (EUR 4,500) vs 4 on CL-609 (EUR 18,000) is arguable given the one-hour bank change, but the framework’s amount/exposure criterion is applied unevenly without saying so.
- Section 2 markdown separator does not match the column count.
- “Expected risk reduction” is only High/Medium.

The business reading of CL-601, CL-603–CL-605, CL-607, CL-611, CL-612, the P3 treatment of CL-604, and the five-plus-case demolition of uniform edit distance are good. The grade is held down by the P2 mis-binds, the missing data-aware conformance item, and a reproduction block that would not run or report as claimed.
