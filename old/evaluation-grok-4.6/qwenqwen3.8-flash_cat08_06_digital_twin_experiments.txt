6.5

The final write-up is a complete, on-format digital-twin design: all eight required sections are present, independent-case simulation is rejected, objections are treated as latent, waiting/service are not silently collapsed, optimality is not claimed, and the PM4Py skeleton avoids `pm4py.read_csv` and uses `format_dataframe`, variants, DFG, and a process-tree parse/fallback. Those are the main reasons it is not a low score. Under a strict reading it is still well short of nearly flawless.

**Simulation design.** Scope, entities, queues, and cross-case channels (architect bursts, district bundles, latent objections) are named clearly. They are not specified as an executable state machine. There is no event list, no rule for when a case becomes bundle-eligible, and no cluster process for objections beyond a district-level duration mixture. “Decision drafting waits for all applicable results” is stated, but the given tree already serializes public notice and inspection after the AND-split, so the dependency semantics stay ambiguous. D2’s “no prior violations” flag is used as if it were an observed attribute; it is not in the log and is never estimated.

**Calendars and resources.** The prose takes calendars seriously; the code does not. `WorkingCalendar.is_working()` calls `ts.normalize()`, so hour windows (08:00–16:00, fire Tue–Thu, inspector field days) cannot be enforced. `ResourcePool.acquire()` adds a raw timedelta and ignores overnight/weekend interruptions. Council weeks block all legal work with no urgent exception. That is a direct miss against “do not ignore resource calendars,” even allowing a skeleton.

**Parameter estimation and assumptions.** PM4Py usage is mostly conventional (`format_dataframe`, `get_variant_statistics`, `discover_dfg`, `discover_performance_dfg`, `parse_process_tree` with a documented fallback). Gaps: no mapping from the supplied 18-month tables to fitted objects; no incomplete-case/censoring treatment; no plan for the missing building-area / environmental-sensitivity fields beyond “district priors”; case-mix is treated as stationary. Calling system work “near-zero” contradicts the 5–20 minute Issue-permit summary. Invented policy effects (missing-doc loop 0.4×, inspection rework +5–10 pp, travel −20%) are not tied to an estimation or elicitation method.

**Experiments and uncertainty.** The matrix includes baseline, D1–D10, a combined policy, and three stresses, which meets the letter of the request. S1–S3 are families (“run B0, P4, P10”) rather than well-formed cells, so factors, levels, and replication structure are mixed. A 30-day warm-up is too short relative to 140d / 260d complex tails; the text never defines whether metrics use cases *started* after warm-up, *completed* after warm-up, or a time average, nor how end-of-horizon censoring is handled. One hundred replications plus Sobol-style sensitivity at 30 runs is weak for p95/p99 under heavy tails. Common random numbers are mentioned, but not how they survive interventions that change routing or arrival thinning.

**Validation and decisions.** The validation menu is complete (face, replay, distributions, utilization, bottlenecks, holdout, missing-data). At 31,200 cases, KS/AD tests will reject almost any residual; there is no practical-significance rule or quantitative fitness/precision bar. SLA cutoffs recycle historical p90s (simple ≤ 24d), so baseline already “passes.” Fairness and utilization bands are arbitrary. Post-permit complaints appear as a decision criterion after section 1 excluded that work. There is still no cost model for D4, so the peak inspector cannot be judged.

**Code.** Modular and aligned with the stated PM4Py conventions; service/wait proxy warning is correct; parse-tree limitations are explicit. It remains a stub: `CaseRouter.next_activity` returns `None`, `SimulationEngine.run` returns NaNs, resources are never initialized, and `discover_dfg` is unpacked as a 3-tuple with no version guard (unlike the performance DFG). That is acceptable as a skeleton, but it does not demonstrate that non-stationary demand or cross-case logic can actually run.

Net: strong outline and constraint-awareness, with several methodology errors (warm-up, calendar time-of-day, ungrounded effect sizes, missing attributes, censoring, large-n validation) that a strict judge should not treat as minor.
