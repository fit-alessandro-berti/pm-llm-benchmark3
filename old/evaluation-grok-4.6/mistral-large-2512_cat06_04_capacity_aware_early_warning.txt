## Decision-theoretic errors

The priority score multiplies `adjusted_p_now × value × midpoint_risk_reduction`. The supplied ranges are already **absolute** risk reductions. Multiplying by `p_now` treats a percentage-point effect as a relative effect and systematically distorts ranking toward high-`p` cases. The problem’s own template is `ARR × value − cost − false-alarm burden`. The write-up ignores that template while claiming to use an expected-value rule.

The spillover term is also malformed: `spillover_penalty × 6 × 0.02 × value` after defining `spillover_penalty = 0.02` double-counts the 0.02 and collapses an ~€3,000-per-slot externality to ~€60. Either the formula as written is not the formula used, or the scores are invented. The published scores match neither the stated formula nor the correct EV formula (I-04 is listed at €4,500; a coherent midpoint EV is closer to €9k before spillover; I-08 is listed at €2,000 and is negative under the stated formula).

Section 3 then repeats the same `p × ARR` error, adds the four products to **0.37 total**, labels that “per case,” and inflates it to 0.8–1.2 breaches and €80k–€120k benefit. The working itself produces ~€12.5k total direct benefit, which already cannot generate those ranges (4 × €12.5k = €50k, not €80–120k). Spillover is computed as `4 × 6 × 0.02 × €25,000 = €12,000` and then entered in the table as €3,000–€9,000. Optimistic/base/adverse rows are therefore not auditable. That is a core required deliverable, and it fails.

## Capacity and rule violations

Four slots exist. The table marks **five** cases Intervene (I-01, I-02, I-04, I-07, I-08), the narrative says I-07 “is included,” the selected set is I-01/I-02/I-04/I-08, and a slot is also “reserved.” That is simultaneously 4, 5, and 4+1. A deployable noon policy cannot tell an operator which cases get a swarm.

The repeated-alert rule requires a first alert, a material new event, or a +0.10 move. I-02 is a second alert, no new event, `p` rise of 0.08, and is still selected. The rule is not applied to the live book.

I-08 is a poor slot use even before those contradictions: ARR 0.05–0.15, false-alarm burden €5,000, Strategic Enterprise overprediction, 8 hours of option value left. At the low end the direct net is negative **before** spillover. Selecting it is value-chasing, which Section 5 itself flags, then does anyway.

Time-to-deadline is almost unused. I-07 has 1.5 hours and new evidence; I-04/I-08 have 8–10 hours. Burning scarce pre-diagnosis capacity on long-horizon, high-burden Strategic cases while dropping the urgent SME re-alert is operationally backwards.
