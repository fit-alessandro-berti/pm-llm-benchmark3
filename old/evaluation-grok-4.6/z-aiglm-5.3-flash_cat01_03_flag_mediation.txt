**6.5**

The answer is conceptually strong on the intended evaluation axes—policy-bundle interventions, occurrence vs waiting mediation, interference, exposure-induced confounding, and a knowledge-producing pilot—but it is not nearly flawless. Hypercritically, the required empirical decomposition contains real errors, one of which is used to support a false qualitative claim, and the code/table execution is sloppier than the causal framing.

**What is done well.** Section 1 is the high point: natural effects are rejected for the right reasons (shared queues, cross-world independence, exposure-induced mediator–outcome confounding), and separable/interventional analogues are defined as *different policies*, not as “the direct effect of the flag.” Distinguishing P1 (hide the label) from routing/capacity changes matches the prompt. E3 vs E4 vs E5 correctly splits review *occurrence*, review *waiting*, and escalation. Sections 4–6 and 8–9 generally treat the flag as a bundle, keep CDE as a different intervention (E8), and propose an interference-aware P3 pilot with a spillover guardrail and audit. Reversal conditions hit all six required themes.

**Stage table: material inaccuracies.** Flag vs unflagged headline numbers are mostly right (triage wait ~0.2 vs ~2.5h; review wait ~6.75 vs ~1.75h; cycle ~16.45 vs ~5.75h median). Subgroup cells are not. R03 manual service is 3.5h (18:18–21:48), not 3.0h, so flagged manual mean 2.65 is wrong. Flagged-Medium `triage-done-to-manual-start` median is 5.0h, not 6.0; only **one** Medium flagged case escalates (R06, 4.0h), so 4.5h / 1.5h escalation figures are unsupported. Flagged-High escalation wait median is 8.0h, not 8.5. Flagged high-load wait-to-triage median is 0.2h, not 0.15; escalation wait median is 7.0h, not 8.5. Flagged **normal-load total cycle is reported as 16.45h**, which is the all-flagged median; the subgroup median is 12.25h (mean ~14.3h). Flagged non-escalated cycle is given as 9.4h (actual median 8.8h / mean ~9.2h). Residual-to-close for flag=0 is listed as median 1.1h; it is 1.0h. The table claims medians (means) then mixes them (escalated flagged 21.4h is a mean). Low-complexity is omitted despite n=4 support.

**That error is used.** “Flagged high-load vs normal-load differences are small, so raw load at arrival explains little” follows from the copied 16.45h figure. The correct contrast is not small (~18h vs ~12h median) and is also mixed with complexity. Interval decomposition is therefore not fully correct, which the rubric treats as central.

**Missed process mechanisms.** Overnight/shift closure is visible in many review→escalation gaps and is never separated from queue congestion. Triage resource is **deterministic** with flag (T1/T2 vs T3/T4); lane effects cannot be separated from who works the case, but this is only a light pathway row.

**Code.** The event log correctly omits absent stages and uses `format_dataframe` with a start key. The small-n warning is dead: it sits outside the stage loop, `m`/`c` refer to `cycle_time`, and `c != "cycle_time"` prevents it from firing. Frequency DFGs are computed and discarded; `save_vis_performance_dfg(... if False else None)` is a no-op. Fine for a sketch, not a clean reproduction.

**Other nits.** RDD/McCrary is proposed on n=20 without saying it is infeasible here (positivity appears only later). E6 overlaps E3. Decision-quality claims rest on 3/6 and 2/2 rejections. None of this undoes the causal literacy, but under a near-flawless bar it is a mid-to-high 6, not an 8–10.
