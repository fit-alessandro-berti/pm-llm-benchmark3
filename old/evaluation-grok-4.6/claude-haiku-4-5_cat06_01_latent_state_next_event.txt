**Wrong state for C6-B (serious).** The prefix is already through Customer Evidence Requested and Evidence Received. Normative S3 is the primary state. Labeling it S1 TRIAGED, then hedging that it is “in transition between S1 and S2/S3,” ignores the state machine the prompt defined. Fast Close as rank 1 at 0.50–0.70 compounds the error. The write-up itself asks why evidence was requested if Fast Close were obvious, then still puts Fast Close first with a high interval. That is exactly the DFG misuse the cross-case section later criticizes: using `IT → Fast Close` frequency after the case has already taken another path. After an evidence cycle the coherent terminal label is Close — No Safety Issue, and only after Evidence Review Completed.

**“Next logged” is repeatedly confused with “eventually required.”** Rank-1 choices for A, C, and E are mostly sensible. Ranks 2–3 often are not next events:
