**4.3**

The submission has the right skeleton and some genuine caution on finance, but it repeatedly invents constraints the prompt forbids, misreads its own repair, and treats interpretive guesses as conservative. That is not close to a high score under a strict reading.

**Format and calibration.** All eight sections appear, Bundle A/B are named, some edges are left unresolved, and arithmetic is mostly treated as non-proof. That is the floor, not a virtue. Confidence numbers are asserted rather than derived. “Temporal + prefix ⇒ >0.8” is a slogan, not a combination rule. Identifier similarity, time, amount, customer, route, lifecycle, cardinality, and existing O2O structure are listed, then applied unevenly. Existing O2O is the weakest part of the evidence model: `PK-8 packs IT-A2` is already in the log and is never treated as first-class evidence against stuffing `IT-B1` into the same package.

**Hard factual errors.** P09 cites **`ev16 (PAY-2)`**. There is no `ev16`. The event is `ev14`. Inventing an event ID violates “do not invent new events” and is an unforced data error in the main relation table. E2O qualifiers (`(relates)`, `packet_relation`, `dispatch_relation`, `confirmation_relation`) are invented and do not match the O2O vocabulary (`packs`, `carries`, `bills`, `settles`). That is sloppy OCEL modeling, not a cosmetic naming issue.

**False domain constraints.** Domain rule 5 allows Confirm Delivery to bind one delivery, several deliveries, or only the customer/order. The answer invents “single-delivery confirmation bounds” and uses that fiction to park `ev11→DEL-Y` as unresolved. Domain rule 6 allows an invoice to bill one or more orders; the observed `INV-44 bills ORD-A` edge does **not** contradict `bills ORD-B`. P08’s reject reason (“contradicts single-order billing pattern”) is therefore wrong. P07 `unresolved` at 0.40 and P08 `reject` at 0.30 are the same hypothesis scored two incompatible ways.

**Constraint violation on ev11.** The prompt says not to assign `ev11` uniquely unless alternatives are shown to be weaker. Bundle A maps `ev11` exclusively to `DEL-X`. The stated reason — “closer temporal/route context” — is false. Both deliveries are `NORTH`; `ev09` and `ev10` share the timestamp `10:00`; `N-42*` matches `N-420` and `N-421`. The more conservative reading is the one already in the log: `ev11` relates only to `ORD-A` because the portal lost a clean carrier id. Adding `DEL-X` is an interpretive commitment, not a minimum-change repair. `portal_customer=C17` also makes `ev11→ORD-B` a live candidate; it is never listed.

**Conservative bundle is not conservative.** Domain rule 3 explicitly allows an item-only Pack Item row. Minimum change is to leave `ev08` unresolved or mark a later reconciliation as low-confidence. Prefix `PK8-2` is informative, not decisive (rule 9). Linking `ev08→PK-8` plus `PK-8 packs IT-B1` creates a **mixed-order package** (`IT-A2` from ORD-A is already packed in PK-8). The answer never audits that. It then claims “1-to-1 mapping between items and packages is preserved,” which is false under its own repair: PK-8 would pack two items.

That mixed package breaks Bundle A’s lifecycle story. ORD-A is confirmed at `ev11` while `IT-A2` rides on PK-8/DEL-Y, which Bundle A leaves unconfirmed. So an order is “delivered” while one of its items is still in transit. That is exactly the lifecycle/cardinality failure the rubric asks you to catch. Circular-reasoning language in §5 is noted, then ignored: P02/P04 are just mirrors of P01/P03, scored as if they were independent structure.

**Bundle B is not a coherent alternative.** On `ev10` it offers bulk dispatch via DEL-X *or* `DEL-Y carries PK-8`, then shrugs (“Let us route PK-8 via DEL-Y”). A bundle is a joint interpretation, not a menu. After that waffle, the logistics skeleton of B is almost A, with extra finance edges and a dual confirmation. Missing genuinely distinct bundles: (i) leave `ev08` item-only; (ii) treat `ev10` as bulk on DEL-X and leave DEL-Y unused; (iii) keep `ev11` order-only; (iv) read PAY-1=100 and PAY-2=130 as matching **order declared values**, not an invoice remainder. The last one is sitting in the object table and is never mentioned. Bundle A’s remark that INV-44 is “partial or incomplete” because 130≠230 has the inequality backwards: the invoice is larger than ORD-A, which is what fees/tax would do.

**Downstream section is generic.** OC-DFG / OTG / ET–OT / flattening / clustering are named, but the prompt asked for concrete edges and the three frequency bases (events vs unique objects vs total objects). Those three counting modes are not actually compared. Package, Invoice, and Payment flattenings are thin or absent. “Synchronization loops” for a multi-order invoice is the wrong graph language. The important concrete effect is missed: mixed-package PK-8 plus order-level `Confirm Delivery` will fabricate cross-order directly-follows and equivalent-execution clusters that the unrepaired log does not contain.

**PM4Py outline would not run.** `pm4py.read.read_ocel2_sqlite(None)` is not a constructor. O2O additions use `source`/`target` while E2O uses `ocel:*`; integrity checks then assume `objects_rep['Object ID']` and `o2o_rep['source']`. That will not match a normal PM4Py OCEL 2.0 frame (`ocel:oid`, `ocel:oid_2`). The warning about `ocel_o2o_enrichment` is correct and is one of the few precise methodological notes.

**What is actually good.** Leaving PAY-2 unapplied and refusing to complete every lifecycle in Bundle A; stating that amount sums are defeasible; listing five real over-repair failure modes; noticing (once) that inferred O2O cannot confirm the E2O that produced it. Those points are why this is not a 2–3. They do not offset invented events, invented cardinality laws, a non-conservative “conservative” bundle, a self-contradictory item–package audit, and a unique `ev11` assignment built on a false similarity claim.

A nearly flawless answer would have kept `ev11` non-unique, treated scanner-prefix packing as one optional bundle rather than the default, audited the already-present `PK-8 packs IT-A2` edge, offered a truly minimum-change bundle that adds almost nothing, and named specific OC-DFG/flattening edges. This is a complete-looking writeup with material reasoning defects.
