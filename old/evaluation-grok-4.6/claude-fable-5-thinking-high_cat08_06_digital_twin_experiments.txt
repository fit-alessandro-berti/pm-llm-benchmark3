7.2

The design meets the hard constraints (cross-case coupling, calendars, service/wait not silently collapsed, objections treated as latent, no `pm4py.read_csv`, no optimality claim) and the eight required sections are present and usable. It is still not near-flawless. Several specification, statistical, and fidelity gaps would matter in a real twin.

**What is actually strong.** The boundary is crisp. Waiting is correctly treated as an emergent output rather than a sampled input, with an explicit warning that baking observed waits into every scenario would invalidate intervention comparison. Objections are a two-component duration mixture plus a latent cluster, not fake observed events. Arrivals are non-homogeneous plus an architect burst layer. Inspection bundling, shared-pool interference, and D2/D4 sub-additivity are real simulation-design points, not slogans. CRN, cyclo-stationary collection, outer-loop epistemic uncertainty, and a staged-rollout caveat are the right uncertainty posture.

**Model fidelity holes.** Activity-to-pool mapping is never stated. Decision drafting almost certainly shares the planner pool with Planning review; Inspection scheduling has `scheduler_1` in the log and no calendar; neither is specified. The problem lists rework after Planning review, Legal check, and appeal; the twin only operationalizes completeness and inspection loops, then waves appeals in as an exogenous delay. Public notice is left strictly after the review AND-block because the tree says so; the variant note that reviews are “partly parallel” is not examined, so New-residential cycle time may be structurally overstated. Completeness rework is a labeled tree activity that does not appear in the log (the log repeats Completeness check). No-shows are in the empirical table and almost absent from the state model. D2’s “no prior violations” flag is not in the log and is not in the missing-data section. D7 is a weekday-factor knob, not a capacitated slot system.

**Statistical and experimental nits.** Welch/MSER on a still-seasonal weekly series fights the cyclo-stationary claim. KS/AD against 31k cases will reject any useful model; they are a bad validation gate. SLAs (30/90/120/240d), 85%/75% floors, 1.5 district ratio, 0.8 rework learning, 5% urgent legal, and “<1% emergencies” are invented and written as if given. The nested design (30–100 replications × LHS × 15+ cells) is computationally uncosted. Section 1 refuses to choose between 1.8 FTE and stochastic interruptions for environmental capacity.

**Code.** The PM4Py layer is mostly conventional (`pandas.read_csv` → `format_dataframe` → DFG / performance DFG / arrival average / tree parse, engine separated). It still misses the required service/waiting columns (only `flow_time_h` is added). `conformance_diagnostics_alignments` is invoked on a process tree without conversion to a net. `load_process_tree` returns `None` on failure. `skip_inspection` ignores the violations flag the text defined. The executable matrix omits D5–D9 and S4.

Solid municipal DES design with the right instincts; too many underspecified mechanisms, unearned numbers, and PM4Py/code gaps for a top-band score.
