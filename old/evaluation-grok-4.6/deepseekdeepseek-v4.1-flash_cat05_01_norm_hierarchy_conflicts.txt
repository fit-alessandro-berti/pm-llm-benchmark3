**7.0**

The write-up is a competent, well-structured compliance assessment: it uses the required headings, applies controls at the time of the act, refuses to let ERP timestamps outrun calls/PDFs, does not treat later approvals as curative, and separates facts, assumptions, and recommendations. Scope and time are handled carefully (P-501 before ECD-2, P-504 in-window but out-of-scope, P-505 around expiry, P-506 after expiry). Those are real strengths.

It is not near-flawless, and the main defects are structural rather than typographical.

**Uniform primary labels.** All eight cases are `NONCOMPLIANT`. Severity and confidence vary, and Section 5 shows how some labels could flip, but the allowed set (`JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`, `INDETERMINATE`) is never used as a primary call. That flattens P-507/P-508 (ECD authorization, Compliance, and, in P-508, Legal and SoD all present) onto the same primary outcome as the cafeteria freezer. The brief explicitly warned against treating every deviation as noncompliant and against letting the global instrument automatically defeat every other source. The analysis does both in practice: GPIP-4 A2/A3/A7 win every live contest with ECD-2 and the CFO email.

**A7 silence treated as a negative.** GPIP-4 “does not state whether” ECD-2 counts as an A7 exception; the organization has previously treated signed incident directives as overlays. The memo converts that gap into “ECD cannot waive A2/A3” at High or Medium-High confidence, instead of `INDETERMINATE` on the exception question or `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` where ECD’s own terms are met and the only remaining hole is budget-owner/Finance. The budget-owner reading (IC charter does not make them budget owners; A2 is conjunctive) is a fair reason to stay strict, but it is applied as if it were settled law.

**B2 timing is smuggled in.** SOP B2 requires Plant and Finance approval; it does not say “before commitment.” A2 is the clause that is explicitly pre-commitment and only from EUR 50,000. Several rows treat late or missing Plant/Finance as if B2 had A2’s temporal structure, with only a partial hedge on P-501.

**Catalogue vs. verdicts.** The first five pseudo-logic lines are defeasible rules; lines 6–11 are case holdings. That blurs the required rule layer. Plant Manager is also treated as the “independent budget owner” without consistently marking that as an assumption.

**Smaller gaps.** P-508 is the cleanest alternative-interpretation candidate and is omitted from Section 5. P-505 has no recorded `Create_PO` and no C3 recording; only the latter is flagged. Severity is undefined. GPIP-4 is described as “overriding” weaker local rules; A5 is a floor, not an override. P-504 confidence is High despite SOP-scope ambiguity (cafeteria vs production-support).

The hierarchy table, evidential distinctions (breach vs logging vs late proof of a timely act vs inability to prove), and the proposed exception clause are above average. The score is held down because the central methodological instruction—do not let one source automatically defeat the others, and do not collapse every deviation into a single class—is only honored in the alternatives section, not in the actual classifications.
