6.2

The final write-up (sections 1–7) is a complete, on-prompt response with the required headings, a full six-row matrix, eight identifier-grounded distortions covering every mandated motif, a coherent B decision, and a sensitivity plan that matches the four required checks. It does **not** treat the longest traces as “correct,” and it does not invent events. That is the ceiling. Under a strict reading it is well below a high score because several claims are factually wrong or internally inconsistent with the author’s own definitions.

**What is solid.** Section 1 is the strongest part: case notion is direct E2O membership, shared events are treated as both evidence and copies, and O2O propagation is correctly fenced off as an optional transform. Most Order/Invoice/Payment/Delivery mappings are right (e13 on O2/O3/P3/I21/I31; e14 on O3/O4/P4/I32/I41; e16/e24 on O1–O3/P2/P3/D2; V2 = e19/e22/e25; payments as true singletons). Position B is defensible and the counterargument (copied e13/e14/e16/e17/e19/e22/e24/e25) is the right one.

**Material accuracy failures.**

- **Item flattening is mis-rated.** e13 is copied to I21 and I31; e14/e17/e27 to I32 and I41; e16 and e24 to I12, I21, and I31. Event duplication and convergence are **not** “Low.” This violates the author’s own copy rule.
- **Package flattening is mis-rated.** P2 and P3 both carry e16 and e24 (D2 consolidates them). “Convergence risk: None” and “Event duplication: None” are false.
- **e16 object count is wrong.** Related objects are O1, O2, O3, I12, I21, I31, P2, P3, D2 — **9**, not 7.
- **Durations are invented.** e22→e25 is 20h20m, not “1 day 21h.” e19→e25 is 20h55m, not “1 day 19h.”

**Conceptual slips.** Two sequential Pack events on O3 (e13 then e14) are a **real** split of O3 across P3/P4; the distortion is the *sharing* of those events with O2 and O4, not an “artificial parallel packing pattern.” Sequential V2 instalments are not divergence. Invoice “convergence: Medium” is the same confusion: V1–V3 do not share events. Section 5 ends by saying a single lead is “indefensible,” which is position C’s thesis, not B’s.

**Weaker deliverables.** Five of eight distortions are Order-only, so the matrix errors are not corrected later. The PM4Py block uses non-existent helpers (`get_activity_frequency`, `get_object_type_frequency`), `ocel_flattening.apply(..., case_id_key=...)` instead of an object-type flatten, only three of six flattenings, and no comparison that would stop a reader from treating plots as semantics. That falls short of “executable-looking” and of the instruction not to let visual inspection settle the issues.

A generous reading is a competent, well-structured analysis with several identifier-level mistakes. Strictly, those mistakes — especially the Item/Package ratings and the wrong counts/times — are enough to keep it out of the 7–10 band.
