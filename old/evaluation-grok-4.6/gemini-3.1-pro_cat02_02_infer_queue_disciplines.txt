5.2

The write-up has the required skeleton and some genuine decision-set reasoning, but it is not close to a careful reconstruction. Several headline claims are overconfident, one discrimination argument is simply invalid, and a second one is built on a false implication. Those are not presentation nits; they change what the data can support.

**QA is misdiagnosed and oversold.** Calling the primary rule appointment scheduling at confidence 95 is not supported. The only clean appointment override is A03 at 08:20. The informative later point is 09:05, which is omitted: A05 (`est_min` 15) is started while A06’s 09:15 appointment is imminent, guaranteeing the overrun. That is a challenge to appointment-as-primary, not a harmless consequence of A05 already being non-preemptive. Treating 09:20 as the strongest contradiction therefore blames the wrong decision. The cited “strongest support” (08:30, A02 over A04) supports FIFO, not appointments. After A03 the trace is ordinary FIFO, so appointment-plus-FIFO is observationally almost equivalent to FIFO with a single exception; that identifiability problem is stated and then ignored in the confidence number. The 08:58 eligible set also drops A08 (arrived 08:40).

**QB’s core story is internally inconsistent.** Preemptive priority-1 plus FIFO-for-the-rest does not survive the 09:46–09:48 window. If B05 were visible, preemptive priority-1 should start B05, not B03. The recorded reason is dispatcher reassignment, which the diagnosis underweights. Section 4 then claims a delayed B05 scan would make B03 “the correct FIFO choice among visible jobs.” That is false: without B05 the visible set is B01, B03, B04, and FIFO still resumes B01. Delayed visibility would make B03 the priority choice among remaining jobs, not the FIFO choice. The same section therefore cannot discriminate “priority-1 then FIFO” from a manual override. B05 also never preempts B01, so “preemptive priority (Pri 1)” is already too strong. 10:00 (B05 over residual B01) is the clean priority-1 episode and is not reconstructed.

**QC is the best of the four diagnoses and still too tidy.** 10:32 (C04 over C05 and C06) is the right anti-SPT/anti-EDF observation, and the final warning not to read the opening as SPT/EDF is correct. But the tables keep C01 eligible while the primary label is FIFO, and section 3 upgrades the note “executed last among initial backlog” into a known constraint (missing parts / customer not ready). That note is an outcome description, not evidence of ineligibility. C01 is also skipped after later arrivals C05 and C06, so “last among the initial backlog” does not by itself restore FIFO. Confidence 90 assumes the exception that is in question.

**QD is directionally right, labeled wrongly, and incomplete.** Among skill-eligible jobs the binding sequencing rule is FIFO (D04 over D08 priority-1; D07 over D10 priority-1), with an appointment hold and a do-not-straddle-unavailability rule. Skill matching is a hard filter, not the ranking discipline; calling skill-based routing primary at 95% hides that. The unused D-X idle from 11:20 to 11:30 for D06 is a clearer appointment test than 11:48 and is missing. The 11:36 D-F idle is explained well.

**Required comparisons are incomplete.** The task asked for FIFO, priority, SPT, appointment, and skill-based predictions at each decision. QA/QB omit skill; QC replaces appointment with EDF and omits skill; QD omits appointment. Three decision times per queue meets the letter of the request while avoiding the times that most threaten the chosen labels (QA 09:05, QB 10:00, QC 11:40, QD 11:20).

**Section 4 does not do what it claims.** The QC counterfactual (C0X at 10:04, `est_min` 50) still leaves C02 first under both SPT and FIFO-except-C01, so it does not separate the 10:10 explanations. The QB test rests on the FIFO error above. Neither test isolates the actual ambiguity.

**Exceptions and data needs are thin relative to the prompt.** Meeting-boundary packing, a C01 hold, scan delay, and a manual override are reasonable, but appointment *windows*, noisy/strategic `est_min`, residual time after preemption, and an SLA-triggered policy change are asked for and barely used. Scan delay is invoked to “solidify” a QB rule it cannot solidify. Collection priorities are generic-plus-QB/QC, not matched to the real forks (A06 window vs exact time; whether C01 was in the eligibility set; B01 remaining time vs B03; D-X reservation rule).

**Synthesis fights the table.** QD as most identifiable and “QC as SPT/EDF” as the dangerous inference are fair. QA is given the same 95 as QD despite the write-up’s own observational-equivalence caveat, and QB is called least identifiable while still being summarized as a fairly crisp hybrid. That is rhetorical certainty where the evidence is mixed.

The piece looks structured and process-mining-aware. Under a strict reading it repeatedly picks the convenient eligible set, assigns high confidence to under-identified rules, and then uses an invalid counterfactual and a false FIFO implication as if they were discriminating tests. That is a mid-range analysis, not a near-correct one.
