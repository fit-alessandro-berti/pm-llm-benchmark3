## Material reasoning errors

**1. N3 is misread as adjacency.**  
N3’s *meaning* is “no package may be dispatched *after* that same Order is cancelled.” The OC-DFG row is only a detector. A positive `Cancel Order -> Dispatch Package` pair is decent evidence for those two orders (modulo timestamps and convergence). It is not a complete test of the rule. Intervening events would hide further “after” violations. The write-up says N3 is “stated as a forbidden adjacency, not an eventually-rule” and that DF matching is “close to conclusive.” That inverts the question’s central warning: they apply eventually-vs-DF to N1 (false positives) and not to N3 (false negatives). N4 is similarly under-treated; packing-before-shipment is an ordering obligation, not a proof that the next event must be `Dispatch Package`.

**2. The 98 + 3 arithmetic is ignored.**  
98 orders show `Create Order -> Validate Order` and 3 show `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review`, on a base of 100 newly created orders. If both rows are object-level directly-follows from `Create Order`, that is 101. Possible readings: the 98 is eventually-follows, some orders have multiple create events, or the summary itself is dirty. None of that is raised. Rank 8 and rank 9 are handled as independent facts. That is a failure of the count-and-scope reasoning Section 3 claims to do.

**3. E2 and E6 are the same pattern and are scored differently.**  
`Add Item–Item` 1450, `Add Item–Order` 1448, OTG `(Order, object_interaction, Item)` 1450.  
`Dispatch Package–Package` 200, `Dispatch Package–Delivery` 198, OTG `(Package, object_interaction, Delivery)` 200.  

In both cases the object-to-object edge is complete and the event-anchored edge is short by 2. Dispatch/Delivery is correctly called abstraction mismatch / likely data quality (rank 7). Add Item/Order is called missing mandatory, “item added without order context,” moderate–high (rank 5). Same evidence shape, different legal theory, no justification. T1 is therefore over-escalated relative to T2/E6.

**4. Rank 11 contradicts the invoice counts.**  
“83 of ~98” with “remaining orders pass through invoice/review first” implies about 15 invoice/review exceptions. The same table reports 72 `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice`. Those figures only coexist if the counts are not exclusive DF, there are multiple `Validate Order` events per order, or the metric is not DF at all. The answer uses the 15-remainder story anyway.

**5. Cross-model identity is not checked.**  
One `Order–Payment` cobirth (T6) and one `Record Payment` with Payment but no Invoice (E8) are scored as separate incidents. They may be the same anomalous birth event. Two pack-to-dispatch gaps and two dispatch-to-delivery gaps are only weakly linked in Section 7, not in the ranking. Convergence on N3 (cancel on the Order, dispatch on a Package that may be shared or mis-joined) is a parenthetical in Section 3, not a first-class caveat on a “Critical / High confidence” row.
