6.2

The answer is a competent, well-structured design with the right conservative instincts, but it is not close to flawless. Under a strict reading it has factual errors on the given source facts, an internal contradiction in the ordering theory, an implementation that does not do what the prose promises, and several inconsistencies between pipeline, code, and sensitivity analysis.

**What it gets right.** The decision tree matches the six required categories and assigns defensible terminal actions. It does not collapse same-label/same-time events, does not drop e05 or e14, does not treat ingestion time as the business clock, and does not pretend a total order is the physical order. The `have_common_object=True` discussion is the strongest paragraph in the script: e13 and e14 share O1/V1 while differing on the activity-critical Payment instance. Admissible orders for the two picks, and for e13 vs e14, are correctly left open. Failure modes and the provenance schema cover the mandated ground.

**Factual and logical defects.** Fact 1 says WMS *enriched* the scan with a location. The table nevertheless writes “add `location` from e04”. That inverts the authoritative source and would overwrite or invent the wrong payload. In §3 the authors also violate their own within-source rule: they claim “WMS seq (100) and SCANNER seq (551<552) both put I1-pick before I2-pick.” WMS never recorded the I2 pick, so `source_seq=100` cannot order e03 against e05. Only the scanner sequence (or a lifecycle argument that both picks precede a pack of I1 *and* I2) is valid. Treating e13→e15 as a derivation link while quarantining e14/e15 is inconsistent with fact 8: e15 is an invoice-level balance update after *messages*, i.e. plausibly an aggregate of M1 and M2, not a summary of e13 alone.

**Conservative merge of e11/e16 is only half-justified.** Fact 6 supports identity of the issuance, but also says each system is authoritative for different attributes. A chimeric canonical row is acceptable only if field-level union and conflict logging actually happen. They are specified in the table and absent from the helper, which keeps “canonical row’s core fields” and drops e16. That is exactly “losing source-authoritative attributes,” a failure mode the authors later list.

**The Python block would not implement the pipeline.** `merge_verified_pair` concatenates remapped relations onto the *full* original relation table and never deletes `absorbed_eid` rows, so `check_referential_integrity` must fail. Qualifier relabeling of e08/e09 and e06/e07 is central to §4 and never executed. `ocel_aggressive_pre` is dead; `ocel_merge_duplicates` is called on the *entire* log, including e05 and e14, then those IDs are concatenated back—too late if they were already absorbed and their Item/Payment relations unioned away. `strict_key` / `match_on` / `column=` are invented signatures. The comment claims a “pre-filtered exact-duplicate slice”; the call is on the whole conservative OCEL. If the real `ocel_drop_duplicates` keys on activity+timestamp, the conservative path silently destroys e05 and the split captures. Assigning only `.events` back from a function that returns an OCEL desynchronizes relations. The evidence-based scenario is never copied or run, despite the requirement for independent deep-copied scenarios. Ranks are a hardcoded eid map, not a derivation from the stated rule stack.

**Self-contradiction on synthetic time.** §3 and §9 correctly reject encoding order as microseconds. §6 then calls `ocel_add_index_based_timedelta`, and §7 claims the conservative log has “deterministic order without fabricated precision.” Those three statements cannot be true together. The required library call can be used for tool compatibility if performance metrics stay on `original_timestamp`; that caveat is missing from the sensitivity table.

**Sensitivity is directionally right, sloppy in detail.** Four datasets and the required views are present, and several named edges are real (false `Dispatch Package` self-loop; invoice-edge double count; Item I2 pick disappearing). “Payment-flattened … 2 branches vs 1” disagrees with their own earlier “isolated Capture(M2).” Aggressive event count is a vague “~9–10” and ignores the re-attach logic, which would yield a different number. OTG 1:2 collapsing to 1:1 depends on whether merge unions relations; that is not specified.

**Net.** This is a strong exam outline with genuine process-mining judgment, not a reliable specification. The conceptual core is well above average; the fact error, the source_seq contradiction, the non-working merge helper, the unimplemented refinements, and the timedelta inconsistency keep it far from a high score under a hypercritical standard.
