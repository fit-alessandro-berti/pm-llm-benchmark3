## Classification and calibration problems

E10 is forbidden *as a relation*, but the additional context says it was produced by a join on a reused identifier. The primary class should be **likely data-quality error**, with forbidden only as the syntactic flag. Ranking it as a robust forbidden violation in Section 8, then calling it a suspected join error in the same section, is incoherent.

N3 and T6 are given “None plausible” as a data-quality alternative. That is too strong. Cancel-then-dispatch can be timestamp inversion, object-identity collision, or extract-order artifacts.
