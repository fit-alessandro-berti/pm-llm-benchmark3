The completed case analysis is strong, but the deliverable is unfinished and not nearly flawless. Sections 1–4 are the only fully written parts; section 5 is truncated mid-statement; sections 6 and 7 are absent despite being required headings.

The 11-case table is the best part. TX-301, TX-303, TX-304, TX-307, TX-309 are clearly NONCOMPLIANT on the right triggers. R2’s “standard packet does not cure missing Enhanced_Review” is applied as specified.

The required output was seven exact headings plus one *executable* PM4Py/pandas block.
