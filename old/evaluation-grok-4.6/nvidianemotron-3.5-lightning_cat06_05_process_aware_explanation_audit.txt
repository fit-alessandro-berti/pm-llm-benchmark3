**6.5**

The audit is structurally complete and catches the main leakages plus several lineage defects (full-trace duration/variant/appeals, warehouse `final_handler_team`, nightly `same_day_document_count_snapshot`, `prior_claim_count` vs `extraction_date`, mutable `risk_score_current`, complete-case `last_resource_role`). It also keeps the right survivors (`queue_length_at_t0`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, `initial_damage_complexity`) and does not treat every correlated feature as causal or deletable. That is a competent pass, not a near-flawless one.

The most serious error is **Case X / Case Y `prediction_status`: “retain (with repaired features).”** Those scores are not retainable. In X, `appeal_seen_anywhere` (+0.26) and `final_decision` (+0.06) are a large share of 0.82; in Y, `full_trace_variant_id` alone is +0.31 of 0.71. After those features are removed, the numeric prediction must be **recomputed** (or invalidated). “Retain with repair” is internally contradictory and understates contamination. Z’s “recompute” is the correct call and makes the X/Y choice look inconsistent rather than deliberate.

The answer also fails a stated quality criterion: **`Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` is not treated differently from `Claim Closed → Damage Assessment Completed`.** The first is process-legal (appeal after Partial/Deny can produce a second decision) but unavailable at `t0`. The second is process-inconsistent (closed before assessment), same-timestamp, and missing a sequence index. Section 1 labels both as generic “temporal leakage / critical.” Process impossibility is only half-noticed later for Z.

A related process error: **`Damage Assessment Completed → Settlement Decision Recorded` is called medium because “DA may have completed.”** At this `t0`—immediately after the first `Coverage Check Completed`—assessment is not in the prefix. That pattern is future information, not an open question about whether DA already happened.

A subtler miss: even a **repaired** `num_document_requests_total` or `Documents Requested → Documents Received` is usually **not enabled yet** at this cutoff. Evidence cycles start after coverage check, so a prefix count at `t0` is typically 0 and is not an “early warning” at this prediction time. The write-up treats prefix repair as if it restores the team’s interpretation.

Smaller issues: `initial_reserve` is assumed frozen at registration with no lineage note; finding-type labels are uneven (“Process-order potential”); team causal slogans (“Appeals cause breaches,” “the final team determines delay”) are not called out as unsupported causal stories in the table; `prediction_status` is not one of the three required tokens.

Sections 2, 3, and 5 are the strongest parts: prefix vs complete-trace checks, same-timestamp handling, DFG-is-not-normative, predictive vs causal vs actionable vs fairness, and a complete validation/monitoring plan. Those do not offset the status error or the collapsed process distinctions enough for a high score under a strict rubric.
