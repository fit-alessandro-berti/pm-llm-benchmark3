This submission follows the required eight-heading skeleton and shows a rough grasp of the narrative (start/end, a trial redo-loop, optional supplier, POWL preferred). That is almost all that is salvageable. The models, the dependency table, the witnesses, and the comparison are not merely approximate: they are internally inconsistent, API-invalid, and in several places the opposite of the stated process.

**Assumptions already collapse the problem.** XOR is treated as a way to start concurrent streams. “May overlap” is said to be true concurrency in POWL and then “PARALLEL or XOR” in the tree — XOR is exclusive choice, not interleaving. Duplicate-label policy is confused with the fact that Quality Review and Regulatory Review are *different* activities. The claimed “conservative under-approximation” is never implemented as a policy; the tree both drops required activities and admits unfinished cases. POWL is said to have an `IF` operator; it does not.

**The intended-dependency table is wrong on the relations that make this process hard.** Contain ∥ Analyze is labeled XOR. Plan Verification is incorrectly made dependent on Analyze Root Cause; the whole point of the pattern is that planning waits only on containment. Inspect(pass) → Adjust is polarity-reversed. Release → Quality/Regulatory/Consult is backwards: release is a *join*, not a predecessor of the reviews. Design Corrective Action is never given a clean RCA → Design edge. Mutual exclusion of the two risk routes, optionality of Consult Supplier, and “trial may overlap approvals” are missing as first-class relations. A specification this corrupted cannot ground either implementation.

**The process tree is not a PM4Py tree.** `ProcessTree(Operator.SEQUENCE, […])` binds the list to `parent`, not `children`. Leaves are raw strings, not `ProcessTree(label=…)`. Parent pointers are never set. Design Corrective Action is absent. The top XOR duplicates Register Deviation; one arm ends after Contain ∥ Analyze and never reaches Archive Case, so the tree is not even a single-entry/single-exit case lifecycle. Consult Supplier appears only on the high-risk arm and is mandatory there, then Release is duplicated. Quality and Regulatory are sequential, not concurrent. Approvals are forced before planning/trial, which forbids the required overlap. The two printed trees disagree with each other. The compact expression requested by the prompt is never given.

**The POWL model is not a POWL model.** `StrictPartialOrder(nodes, OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR))` is invented. Edges are added on strings, not on the `Transition` objects. Every activity is a node of one partial order, so Supervisor, Quality, Regulatory, *and* Consult are all mandatory — the risk XOR and the optional supplier skip are simply not modeled. `Inspect → Adjust → Execute` is a cycle inside a *strict partial order*; loops belong in `OperatorPOWL(Operator.LOOP, …)`. There is no `SilentTransition`. Release is ordered *before* the reviews. Analyze → Plan is again incorrectly added. `Operator` is used and not imported. The “unconnected nodes may be concurrent” paragraph treats the two risk routes as concurrent rather than exclusive.

**Witnesses do not test what they claim.** Allowed traces are written with `...` instead of full visible sequences. Trace 4 is marked allowed while skipping Release Material. Trace 3 cannot be “allowed in POWL” under the author’s own node set, because Consult is not optional there and the rest of the trace is unspecified. The tree verdicts are guesses about code that will not construct. No witness is a precise, complete high-risk trace with both Quality and Regulatory overlapping the trial. The required cross-branch negative example is a soup of missing RCA, missing trial, and inverted release edges; the “earliest violated condition” is the author’s own backwards edge.

**Section 6 asserts correctness that the artifacts refute.** Initial streams are not “correctly modeled” by XOR. The tree does not enforce the real split dependencies. POWL does not capture the risk route. Repeat-until is not “correct” as a cyclic partial order. Release is not correctly synchronized when edges point the wrong way. Duplicate-label risk is waved away while the tree duplicates Register Deviation.

**Validation and self-critique are decorative.** Conversion and soundness are named but would fail immediately on constructor errors and cycles. There are no executable positive/negative traces, no silent-skip test, no check that duplicated labels are the same activity. The self-critique mentions flexibility and inspection signaling and ignores inverted causality, missing Design Corrective Action, XOR/parallel confusion, and non-runnable code. That is not a semantic residual; it is a failure to inspect the core deliverable.

What remains is a formatted outline with a few locally correct instincts (redo-loop shape `LOOP(seq(Execute, Inspect), Adjust)`, preference for POWL, mention of Petri-net soundness). Against a rubric that demands faithful models, honest approximation, and counterexamples that actually exercise the models, that is far from enough.
