**Methodological flaws in the discovery design**

- Cosine on complexity-*sum* profiles is largely scale-invariant. If Chen and Dina have the same activity mix on harder vs easier cases, `sim_weighted` will not “fall” as the comment expects. Distinguishing those pairs needs a joint (activity × complexity-band) space, mean complexity per activity, or the categorical mix already in the tables—not cosine of weighted counts.
- `case_complexity >= 0.75` is an invented cutoff, misaligned with the categorical High/Critical labels and with Chen’s median of 0.71. The same block later uses 0.6 as “high.”
- `insert_case_service_waiting_time` is case-level sojourn/wait. It does not measure wait before A6 on critical cases, which is the quantity the substitution story needs.
- `escalation_level` values `[3, 4]` are not defined in the log description.
- Recheck-rate code uses `groupby.apply` returning a variable-length Series and `int(...any())` after the resource’s first A9. That is fragile and not a per-event recheck rate.
- Hana/Erik/Mateo complexity is partly selection (they only enter hard cases). The answer uses the medians well but never names that bias.
