**7.2**

The JSON is process-aware in the ways the rubric rewards: C6-A and C6-B are not cloned from a shared prefix; Rules 2, 4, 5, 6, and 7 are applied with real distinctions (especially lot-cluster vs. unconfirmed look-alikes); completion-only logging, queues, the 16:00 batch, and migration delay are used as evidence rather than decoration. Intervals are coarse, midpoints do not fake a partition of 1, and `process_enabled` is usually the right boolean. That is a solid answer, not a near-flawless one.

**Concrete defects**

1. **Schema slip.** C6-C rank 1 has `"high": "0.60"` (string) while every other bound is a number. The spec asked for numeric ranges inside one valid, uniform object.

2. **Wrong DFG denominator.** “101 of ~356 review-loop exits (~28%)” treats `Evidence Received → Evidence Review Completed` (356) as the outbound mass from review. Outbound review edges in the table are 101 + 173 + 68 = 342 (~29.5%). The number is close; the semantics are not.

3. **Negative evidence from a partial table.** C6-B treats the missing Fast-Close-after-evidence edge as showing that “the office historically resolves such cases through the review-then-close route,” while the cross-case note correctly says a missing DFG edge is not a prohibition. That is an internal contradiction, and the table is a summary, not an exhaustive relation set.

4. **Immediate-next vs. gated successor.** Rank-1 events (review or RA completions) are currently enabled. Ranks 2–3 for A/B/C/E are often not (`process_enabled: false`) because they require that completion first. Assigning 0.10–0.24 to a blocked action as the *next logged line* is only coherent if the prior completion is omitted from the feed or is treated as the same horizon. That ambiguity is never resolved, so the top-three is a blend of “next event now” and “likely follow-on.”

5. **C6-A: mandated RA is missing from the top three.** High severity plus injury makes Technical Investigation *and* Regulatory Assessment obligatory. Rank 3 is TI Completed; RA Completed is never listed, despite reportability-priority on a 12-deep RA queue. For a high-severity injured case that is a real ranking gap.

6. **Queue vs. elapsed-time mismatch.** C6-A claims 1h50m since receipt “sits comfortably inside” the 1–5 h review window. That window is after *queue entry*. Four items are waiting on two reviewers; overtaking is possible, not the default. The same optimism shows up in C6-C (RA 0.40–0.60 with 12 waiting, Medium severity, no injury, only 2h15m since TI, assignment unobserved).

7. **C6-B Fast Close.** Attribute eligibility for Rule 2 is correctly identified. Marking Fast Close `process_enabled: true` at S3, and ranking it above Close - No Safety Issue as the *next* log event, overstates a path the case already left at triage. The 0.10–0.22 band for skipping a logged review is high relative to 356 ER→ERC continuations.

8. **C6-D ranks 2 and 3 are the same hypothesis twice** (delayed ER vs. delayed CER). A distinct process alternative—fresh evidence request after RA, or Disposition Recorded—would have been more informative. Combining two backfill events at 0.12–0.24 and 0.08–0.18 also sits on the high side of an 8–15% delay rate once you multiply by “the task actually occurred.”

9. **Local overclaim.** R-T4 is a *completed* triage assignment, reused on both A and B; using it as support that A is “steered toward full investigation” is weak. “Comfortably,” “factually,” and “all 286 historical Fast Closes hung directly off triage” are sharper than the evidence.

**What still works**

- Latent states and alternatives are mostly the right abstractions (S3 for A/B/E, S4 for C, mixed S4 plus hidden evidence for D).
- Rule 5 is handled with discipline: suspected on C6-A, not triggered on C6-C (different lots), presumed mandatory on C6-E; Fast Close correctly barred on E.
- C6-D uses the worklist snapshot and the migration flag instead of treating silence as non-occurrence.
- Supporting vs. contradicting lists are actually opposed, not paraphrases. Unknowns (lot overlap, RA queue position, duplicate confirmation, attachment vs. request) would change rankings.
- Cross-case discussion of A vs. B, DFG confounding, and residual mass is the right shape of uncertainty.

The answer is clearly above a DFG-copying baseline and below a tight, internally consistent one. Under a hypercritical bar, the JSON type error, the 356/342 mix-up, the gated-vs-next confusion, the omitted RA on C6-A, and the Fast Close enablement stretch keep it well short of the top of the scale.
