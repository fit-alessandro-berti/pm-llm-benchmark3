**Material process errors**

1. **Next logged event vs later successor.** Ranks 2–3 often describe the step *after* the current pending completion, not a competing next log event. That is systematic in C6-B (Fast Close / Close) and C6-E (another evidence request), and partly in C6-A (Technical / Regulatory completions while review of evidence received only ~2 h ago is still the live bottleneck). The schema allows termination outcomes, but the ranking still has to be a next-observation distribution.

2. **C6-B Fast Close is over-ranked.** After `Customer Evidence Requested → Evidence Received`, Fast Close is the S1 path, not the natural S3 continuation. Evidence Review → Close — No Safety Issue is the process-consistent non-investigation ending. Ranking Fast Close above Close, and giving both `process_enabled: true` while review is unfinished, blurs Rule 2, S1, and the evidence cycle. Fast Close can remain a *low-probability* alternative; it should not be the second forecast.

3. **`process_enabled` is used loosely.**  
   - C6-C Supplier Analysis is marked enabled on *different lots* with only a possible shared failure mode. Rule 5 requires same lot or implicated supplier component. The hedge in the prose is better than the boolean.  
   - C6-B Close — No Safety Issue is enabled as if review had already occurred.  
   - C6-E another `Customer Evidence Requested` is enabled before the second review completion is visible.

4. **C6-C rank 2 is internally inconsistent.** Disposition is called not enabled, the S5 alternative is called less likely, then it still gets 0.10–0.25. That is too much mass for a blocked action unless the alternative state is actually given comparable weight.
