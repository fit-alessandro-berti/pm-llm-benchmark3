## Resource and missing-start logic

The task forbids silently replacing missing starts with a single value unless the midpoint is constrained. The answer’s “typical 30 min” rule is applied even when it is infeasible:

- **P11 S2** on C2 cannot start at `11:55`: C2 is busy on P08 `11:15–12:00`. Feasible window is after `12:00` and before end `12:25` (short service, ~20–25 min, not 30).
- **P05 S2** on C1 is squeezed between P03 end `09:45` and P07 start `10:20`, with S2 end `10:15`. A 30-minute service from `09:45` is the *maximum*, not a central midpoint.
- **P07 S1** is the one missing S1 start that *is* well constrained (after P05 `09:25`, before P09 `10:05`, end `10:00`), but that constraint work is not used consistently.

C2 maintenance is discussed, but the only clear outage victim on the log is **P08** (S1 end `10:20`, C2 start `11:15`). The text inflates this into a general S2 collapse without a queue path.

Section 4 is not a reconstruction.
