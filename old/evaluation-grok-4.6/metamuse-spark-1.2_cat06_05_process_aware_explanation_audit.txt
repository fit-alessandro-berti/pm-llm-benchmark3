**7.4**

The audit is substantively strong: it catches both crude leakage and the subtler lineage bugs, keeps the legitimate prefix/congestion signals, and treats `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` as future-feasible rather than process-impossible. It is not nearly flawless. Several judgment errors, one conceptual overclaim, and repeated unclarity keep it well below the top of the scale.

### What is actually good
Coverage of the required inventory is essentially complete: all 12 global features, all five sequence patterns, and the local-only fields (`claimant_channel`, `final_decision`, `initial_reserve`, `risk_score_current`, `last_resource_role`), plus the off-ranking employee-experience join. Severity is mostly well calibrated (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, and `Closed → Assessment` as critical; warehouse overwrite and SQL-as-of defects as high; queue/complexity/elapsed as low).

The process-consistency suite is the best part of the answer. Prefix recomputation, declarative/alignment checks, same-timestamp quarantine, complete-trace detection, enabled vs impossible vs future-possible, and the repeated-decision-after-appeal rule are all present, and the answer correctly refuses to treat a frequent DFG edge as a normative rule. Section 3’s taxonomy and the predictive / causal / actionable / fairness split are the right standards. Section 5 is a coherent P0–P2 plan and hits every mandated theme.

### Material defects

**1. Task violation on correlated features.** The prompt says not to delete every correlated or sensitive feature. Section 3 correctly keeps `claimant_region` and `claimant_channel` as predictive proxies under governance. Section 4 then dumps both into `removed_or_repaired_drivers` beside true leakage (`appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_decision`, `full_trace_variant_id`). That conflates two different failure modes and withholds information that *is* defensibly available at `t0`. The revised narratives were supposed to use available prefix information and fence the claim, not pretend the features do not exist.

**2. `total_case_duration_hours` is not tautological with the label.** Close-time duration includes post-settlement appeal and closure work. The target is a 10-day *settlement* clock. Calling this “label-leakage: `breach = total_duration > 10d`” and “tautological” is too strong. It is severe future leakage and a near-label correlate, not identity with the outcome.

**3. Unverified feature parked as a credible driver.** Case Y puts `initial_reserve` in `credible_drivers` while admitting the as-of timestamp is unproven. That belongs in repaired/conditional, not in the retained-driver list. The hedge inside the interpretation does not fix the classification.

**4. Over-absolute process claims.** “At `t0` zero `Documents Requested` have occurred… If non-zero, indicates logging error” ignores same-timestamp / missing-index disorder, which the rest of the audit otherwise takes seriously. “65% equality… accidental coincidence” is the wrong story: team stability can be real and still fail a point-in-time test. Case Z’s recompute list tells the reader to “drop or historize queue” even though queue is already the one well-timestamped congestion feature and is not a Z driver.

### Clarity and internal consistency
The missing contrast operators are not cosmetic. Phrases such as “Correlation  causation”, “Predictive  causal  actionable”, and “predictive in training period  stable or causal” are the load-bearing sentences of the audit, and they are unreadable as written. Declarative wording is sloppy in places (`AtMostOne(Claim Closed) before close`). Section 3 vs Section 4 on proxies is an internal contradiction, not a nuance.

### Smaller misses
`prediction_status: "recompute"` for X and Y is defensible, but Y’s top local driver is a full-path variant contributing +0.31 of 0.71; “invalidate the current score, then recompute” would have been more precise and more consistent with Z. Prefix document-request counts after lineage repair are almost surely degenerate at this `t0`, which Section 3 still treats as a normal repair target. None of these are fatal; together they show the same pattern: the conceptual skeleton is right, the last mile is loose.

A top-band answer would have kept region/channel as disclosed associations, stated duration leakage without equating it to the label, refused to promote unverified reserves, and written the causal inequalities in actual words. This is a good audit with avoidable errors, not a near-flawless one.
