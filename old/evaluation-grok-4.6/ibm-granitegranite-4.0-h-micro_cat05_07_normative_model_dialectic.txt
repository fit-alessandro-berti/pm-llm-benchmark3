The prosecution’s lead claim is false: Auto_Approve does **not** bypass Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review. PD-3 and every Auto_Approve trace start with sanctions. Auto_Approve substitutes for Manual_Procurement_Approval; quality may be deferred two business days. The brief argues the wrong control.

Burden of proof is inverted: prosecution is told to prove “M0 is materially noncompliant with CS-9.” The charge is that *operations* are noncompliant with M0/CS-9. Defense is not required to prove genuine risk on SUP-703/708, and then does not.

## §6–§7 architecture and code

The layered-architecture *idea* (POWL for control-flow, DECLARE/data checks for eligibility-amount-time, org controls for SoD/COI, explicit overlay governance) is the one competent part of the paper. It is then implemented as prose recitation and a policy table that is mostly tautology (“Supplier meets CS-9 criteria → Proceed to Sanctions_Check”).

The Python is not a revised POWL. Against the given M0 style and the spec:

- `fd7_check` is never defined; the block cannot run.
- `StrictPartialOrder()` with `add_node`/`add_edge` is not the API used in M0 (`nodes=…`, `order.add_edge`).
- `Transition(..., operator=…)` is invalid.
- Sanctions and quality are **sequential**, not concurrent — the one thing M0 already got right is dropped.
- No `Reject_Supplier` path.
- No `Operator.LOOP` (or a stated approximation) for Request_More_Info; a pair of edges is not a loop and can fire after approval.
- Auto-approve and emergency routes skip sanctions, which *every* normative source forbids.
- No `SilentTransition`.
- Duplicate notify/close edges; “Decision_Node” is decorative.

The spec said: if a behavior is awkward in one POWL, state the limitation and approximate. The answer neither states the limitation nor approximates. It hides a broken graph.

Data-collection bullets (stable `notification_id`, rule/version IDs, eligibility snapshots, alias resolution, immutable timestamps) are listed, then not specified at field level. That is a checklist, not a proposal.
