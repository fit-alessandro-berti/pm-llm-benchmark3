6.8

The analytical core (authority, expiry, scope, burden of proof, and case-level restraint) is strong. The revised POWL and overlay table are not. On a rubric that equally weights dialectical fairness and model/data revision, the technical defects are large enough that a high score is unwarranted.

**What holds up.** Section 1 cleanly separates stipulated traces from authority, scope, identity, and logging questions, and the burden rules are usable: authorized overlay plus contemporaneous eligibility to justify an exception; logging doubt yields indeterminacy rather than acquittal or conviction. The briefs are not straw men. Prosecution is right that PD-3 never entered CS-9, that expiry is not a suggestion, and that M0 *under*-reports SoD/conflict failures. Defense is right that a 2025 control-flow skeleton cannot be the sole yardstick for a 2026 pilot, an emergency overlay, a CRM cutover, or an agreed `Request_More_Info` practice. The judgment does not split the difference: SUP-704/702 can be justified while SUP-705/714 are not, and “no transactions before clearance” does not salvage SUP-708. The layered architecture (POWL for structure, DECLARE/windows for precedence, pandas for amount/identity/time, org controls for overlays and case type) is the right decomposition.

**Material defects in the artifact the task asked you to produce.**

1. **The POWL is ill-formed.** A single `sanctions`, `quality`, `activate`, and `manual` object is reused inside mutually exclusive XOR children. In PM4Py POWL those are the same nodes, not same-label copies. A node cannot sit in `std_branch`, `auto_branch`, and `emergency_branch` at once. Distinct `Transition` instances with the same label are required. This is not a comment-level caveat; it is a broken model.

2. **The auto path cannot replay the eligible cases it is meant to legitimize.** `Automated_Master_Data_Validation` is a required member of `auto_checks`. No observed auto-approve trace contains that event. The comment calls it optional; the graph does not (`XOR` with a silent skip is the obvious fix). As written, SUP-702 fails the very branch offered as its justification.

3. **`Request_More_Info` is placed in the wrong region.** The loop sits only between `Register_Supplier` and the XOR. SUP-707 — the sole detailed RMI case — is `Register → Sanctions → RMI → RMI → Quality → …`. The write-up claims this is “the observed desirable position (before decision checks).” That is false. The approximation is explained, but it is explained incorrectly and will still mark the stakeholder-desired trace deviant.

4. **The reject child cannot produce the reject evidence.** `decision` is `XOR(std, auto, emergency, reject)` and `reject` is a bare `Reject_Supplier`. SUP-709 is `Register → Sanctions → Reject → Notify → Close`. Sanctions live only inside the other three children, so a sanctions-then-reject intake is not in the language of `M_rev`. Scoping it out as `INTAKE_LEAD` is a policy choice; it does not satisfy “represent rejection and closure” for the only reject case in the log.

5. **Auto-approve is concurrent with sanctions.** With no edges inside `auto_checks`, `Auto_Approve` may fire before `Sanctions_Check`. PD-3 requires sanctions (and master-data validation) before activation; treating the approval act as unordered with those checks is the wrong partial order.

6. **The overlay table is misaligned.** The CS9-v3 row shifts fields: `authority_id` is filled with eligibility attributes, `amount_cap` becomes “mandatory”, and `quality_window` becomes “Compliance Board”. For a required, versioned control table that is a concrete deliverable failure, not a formatting quibble.

**Smaller analytical nits (each would already block a 9+).** SUP-711 is not indeterminate: same `source_transaction_id` is stipulated, so the right call is data-quality defect resolving to compliant after dedup, not “cannot tell.” The reverse condition for SUP-703 (“still a spend anomaly” if the supplier were actually PD-3-eligible) imports FD-7’s EUR 20k cap into a pilot that has no such cap. SUP-702 as `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` is defensible but generous: Compliance’s later denial that CS-9 was weakened is left as a footnote rather than a residual authority defect. SUP-708’s matrix wording is confused about Quality vs Sanctions order. “MIXED” is used twice; the dominant-reason gloss saves it from being a dodge, but SUP-712/715 could have been `NONCOMPLIANT` with an explicit control-flow-compliant qualifier.

**Net.** This is a capable review that understands M0, refuses informal amendment, and designs the right *kind* of layered control system. It is not near-flawless. An executable normative model that cannot be constructed as specified, cannot replay the justified auto-approve and RMI traces, and ships a scrambled policy table is exactly the class of error this assignment is built to catch.
