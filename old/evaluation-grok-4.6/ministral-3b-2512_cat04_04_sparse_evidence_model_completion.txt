## 1. Evidence ledger

The ledger collapses seven frequency-bearing variants into one row. That erases the facts that actually drive model choice:

- both `CC ≺ ER` (19-ish) and `ER ≺ CC` (11) are common;
- `SC` is absent in **7/30 = 23%** of cases, not a rare glitch;
- `SC` can sit *between* the two checks (`CC → SC → ER`, 2 cases);
- `Expedite Review` appears twice, once on each side of `SC`.

“Include all variant evidence” is not optional. Treating “Variants” as a single medium-reliability blob makes later inductive claims ungrounded.

The required discussion of bidirectional DFG edges is a one-liner. Both directions do **not** prove concurrency because the same DFG arises from (i) an XOR of two sequential variants, (ii) true overlap linearized by completion time, (iii) ingestion-index tie-breaks (S7), or (iv) mixed subpopulations. Frequency `CC→ER: 17` vs `ER→CC: 11` is compatible with all four. The answer never says this.

Reliability labels are sloppy. S1 is marked “High” even though **at least 11 traces violate it**. A stakeholder rule contradicted by the log is not high-reliability evidence; it is a conflicting hypothesis. S2’s “What it does not prove” cell (“No observed interleaving evidence”) is false: both orders *are* the completion-order signature of interleaving. D1’s modeling implication (“Forces linear ordering where possible”) does not follow from start/end anchors. D3 is an AND/precedence constraint (both checks before approval), not “XOR-like constraints on parallelism.” S7 is mislabeled “Logging.”

This is the central conceptual failure the rubric warned against.

## 3–5. The three candidates

**None of the code is valid PM4Py, and none of it assigns `model_conservative` / `model_balanced` / `model_permissive`.**

`Sequence`, `Xor`, and `Operator("Register Order")` are not the process-tree API. Trees are built with `ProcessTree` + `Operator.SEQUENCE` / `Operator.XOR` / `Operator.PARALLEL`, or with `pm4py.parse_process_tree` and the `->`, `X`, `+`, `*` string language. `None` is not a silent transition. The same `xor_cc_er` object is reused in several parents, which is not a tree.

The conservative *text* forces `CC` before `ER` (S1). The conservative *formula* is an XOR of both orders. The conservative *excluded* list forbids concurrency. Those three claims cannot be true together. Worse, a conservative model whose bias is “do not admit unsupported behavior” must **cover** the observed 7-skip-`SC` variants and the 11 `ER`-first variants. Excluding frequent observed behavior is overfitting to a contradicted stakeholder, not conservatism. The task explicitly allows an XOR of the seven variants and requires a discussion of duplicate labels and scalability; that design is not used and the discussion is absent. The formula `XOR(SC|τ , Expedite|τ)` also forbids the observed `SC`+`Expedite` conjunction.

The balanced POWL adds **both** `CC → ER` and `ER → CC` to a `StrictPartialOrder`. That is a cycle. A strict partial order cannot contain both edges; concurrency is the *absence* of an order edge, not a 2-cycle. This is a foundational POWL error. `OperatorPOWL(...)` is not a variadic sequence constructor. Edges are added with string literals instead of `Transition` objects. “90% probability” still does not exist in the formalism. The required paragraph explaining *how* sequence vs. concurrency vs. partial order was chosen for `CC`, `ER`, `SC`, and `Expedite` is not there—only the assertion that the checks are concurrent.

The permissive model labels `SilentTransition("Supplier Confirmation")`. A silent transition is τ (skip), not a renamed activity. XOR(`SC`, τ) makes `SC` optional; a silent transition *named* `SC` does not execute `SC`. The nesting allows skipping `Approve Configuration`, which violates high-confidence D2. One “excluded” item is “`Register Order` before `Archive Case`,” which is the *intended* order (D1). The boundary between reasonable generalization and still-forbidden behavior is never defined.

Because the three artifacts are broken in different accidental ways rather than by controlled inductive bias, they are cosmetic variants of confusion, not competing hypotheses.
