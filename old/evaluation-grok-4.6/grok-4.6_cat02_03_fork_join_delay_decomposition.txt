**7.5**

This is a strong, coherent fork-join analysis with the right semantics, a fully consistent case table, and counterfactuals that correctly refuse to treat non-critical acceleration as a flow-time win. It is not near-flawless. Under a strict bar the numerical slip, the overstated downstream diagnosis, and several smaller precision/spec issues keep it well below the top of the scale.

**What is actually right.** Section 1 defines the pieces correctly: \(J(c)=\max_i t^{\mathrm{end}}(c,i)\), \(T_{\mathrm{FJ}}=\max_i(Q_i+S_i)\), and \(T=U+T_{\mathrm{FJ}}+D+S_F\). The double-counting argument is the right one (sync waits are concurrent slack inside \([\min_i t^{\mathrm{end}},J]\); \(\sum_i W_{\mathrm{sync}}=3T_{\mathrm{FJ}}-\sum_i(Q_i+S_i)\) is not elapsed time). The F02 arithmetic check (45 vs 35; 235 vs 115) is correct. Every row of the 12-case table reconstructs; the four worked cases are accurate, including the important F04 point that C is last because of queue, not long service. Critical-path ranking correctly refuses “A is slowest on service, therefore A controls \(T\)”: B is last in 8/12, owns both 75-minute tails, and accounts for 520/732 of fork-join elapsed. CF1/CF2/CF3 distinguish zero-benefit cases, the A-sojourn floor, and A4 pass-through vs A5 resource-floor masking. Limits of inference (capacity non-identifiability, preemption, \(D\) as queue vs policy) are on target.

**Material accuracy problems.**

1. **Wrong median in the only aggregate service table.** B service times are \(\{30,30,30,35,35,35,40,40,40,45,45,45\}\). Mean 37.5 is right; the median of 12 observations is \((35+40)/2=37.5\), not 35. They averaged the two central values correctly for B queue (20) and C service (20), then used the lower mid-rank only here. The error does not flip the ranking, but it is a clean arithmetic miss in the comparison table the prompt asked for.

2. **“Almost all observed \(D\) is residual delay while a named resource is already free” is false as a description of the log.** Occupancy vs free residual by case: F04 (8 busy + 10 free), F05 (10 busy), F08 (15 busy), F09 (15 busy), F10 (5 busy + 15 free), F11 (20 busy). That is 73 of 176 downstream minutes, and 6 of 12 cases, waiting on a still-busy dedicated server. Free residual is 103 minutes, a majority, not “almost all.” Their *knock-on* calculation (policy-only savings 171, F11 left with 5) is correct and is the interesting claim — apparent occupancy is largely inherited from earlier residuals. They then collapsed that structural point into an incorrect statement about the observed stamps. That matters because Redesign 2 and CF3 are sold on the idea that F is not congested.

**Smaller issues that still count.**

- Required case-table names (`upstream_elapsed`, `A_queue`, …) appear in the Python printout, not in the markdown table the reader is told to use. The markdown uses \(U,Q_A,\ldots\) plus an extra \(T_{\mathrm{FJ}}\) column. Content is there; the mandated schema is not.
- “Case-level elapsed components (no overlap in calendar time)” then lists all three \(Q_i,S_i\), which overlap by construction. The identities later fix this; the heading does not.
- F11’s 20-minute downstream wait is *entirely* F1 occupancy (F09 ends 11:50), not “at least partly.”
- CF2 labels F02/F06 as both “full vs B queue” and “partial-benefit” in the same subsection.
- The Python has dead code (`if False`, `floor` assigned twice) and implements only the crude A5 floor for CF2, not the knock-on / pool variants that carry the Section 4 narrative. The three required CFs are present and the comments on DFG vs AND-join semantics are correct.

**What does not fail.** Assumptions are labeled. Ties are defined deterministically and do not occur in the base log. Half-minute CF2 figures are flagged against a one-minute clock. Downstream masking of F11 (CF1) and F05/F09 (CF2, prev \(F\) held fixed → 70 vs 92.5) checks out. CF3-pool equaling CF3-full on this sample is right: no three join-readies fall inside one 20-minute service window. The two redesigns match the evidence (do not buy faster A or a third F server from \(n=12\)).

The piece would be in the high 9s if the B median were 37.5 and the downstream claim distinguished “observed free residual” from “occupancy that is knock-on of earlier residual.” Those are not cosmetic. They are exactly the kind of over-precision and over-claim the prompt asked the author to avoid.
