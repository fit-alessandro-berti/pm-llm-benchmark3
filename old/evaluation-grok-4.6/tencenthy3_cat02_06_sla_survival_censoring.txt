**6.0**

The final write-up is a competent SLA/survival design: estimands are defined before estimators, withdrawal and transfer are not casually treated as non-informative censoring, immortal-time/leakage on `Escalate`/`Rework` is handled correctly, open cases are kept, and the required nine-heading shape is met. It is not close to flawless. Several mistakes are about the supplied table itself.

**Factual misuse of the extract.** Cohort B has **two** transfers (S21, S32), not three; S08 is A. “B also contains 3 of the 4 observed breaches beyond 72” is false under any defensible reading (open >72 is 2A/2B; duration >72 and not resolved by 72 is 4A vs 5B). The same transfer-count error is repeated in §6 and then used to argue that B’s ranking versus A could reverse. Crude counts do not support a ranking reversal: known-breach tallies move in parallel when S21/S08 are recoded (A 8→9 of 15, B 8→9 of 17). That is a serious evidence error, not a wording slip.

**Risk-set bug.** `at_risk_72 = (duration ≥ 72) & (status != "resolved")` drops late resolutions (S03, S13, S20, S28). Those cases are in the risk set at 72. `fillna(False).mean()` is also labeled a “known breach rate” while treating unknowns as non-breaches, which biases the printed sensitivity downward.

**Incomplete mapping of required objects.** Four estimands are named, but the mandated case walkthrough (76h resolve, 92h open, 27h withdraw, 68h transfer) is only given for three. The state diagram omits the observed `Rework → Resolve` edge (S27). `pm4py.get_variants` is called, but the actual control-flow features are `str.contains` on the prefix column, so requirement 5 is only half-met. §8 never computes a KM/CIF number—only counts plus a comment-level lifelines fallback.

**Smaller defects.** Estimand 1 sometimes mixes the target parameter with what is observed at cutoff. Transfer-before vs transfer-after 72 is handled in code but not in the §2 encoding table. N=32 uncertainty for AJ/CIF is barely discussed. Several τ/inequality symbols are missing in the text.

The survival/process framing (AJ as primary, landmarking/multi-state for post-baseline path, three transfer regimes, open <72 vs ≥72) is the reason this is not lower. The count errors, unsupported reversal, and risk-set definition keep it well below a high score under a strict bar.
