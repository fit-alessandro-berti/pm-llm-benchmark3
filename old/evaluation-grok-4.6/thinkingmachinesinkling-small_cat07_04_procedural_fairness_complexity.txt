The write-up follows the required heading structure, respects every hard constraint, and is generally well calibrated. It is not close to flawless. Several inferential and technical errors are material enough that a high score is not warranted.

**What it gets right.** Raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination. Case-mix differences are not treated as a full excuse. `risk_score_band` is treated as potentially biased. Rights-preserving steps are correctly identified as the main locus of burden rather than as noise. Mitigation does not propose deleting language or accommodation review. The data tables are used in the right places: V3/V4/V5 shares track stated policy, the long waits sit on Language Support Contact and Accommodation Review, and resource concentration (7 bilingual staff, 5 specialists, fraud queue) is the most plausible capacity story. The bottom line (“indeterminate to moderately unfair,” burden inequity rather than proven intent) is appropriately hedged.

**Most serious analytical error: misreading the adjusted model.** The preliminary controls are risk band, documentation completeness, channel, household size, prior-case count, region, and month. They do **not** include `language_preference` or `disability_accommodation_requested`. Group B is 57% language-support and Group C is 62% accommodation-requested; those paths have 3.8d and 4.7d median waits and 19–22 day variant medians. Citing +2.6 / +4.1 days (and extra supervisor-review / missing-doc probabilities) as residual disparity “after controlling for process complexity” therefore still confounds legitimate support routing with unfair delay. A careful answer would have said: the published residuals do **not** hold support need fixed; the clean tests are (i) same-variant comparisons (especially V1/V2), (ii) waits *within* V4/V5, and (iii) models that add language/accommodation flags. Approval residuals are more informative than cycle-time residuals because support steps should not mechanically change eligibility; the answer treats them as parallel evidence.

Section 2 repeats the same incomplete covariate list and never states the required four-way distinction (disparity vs discrimination vs legitimate routing vs uncertainty). “Path/variant comparison” is implemented almost entirely as comparing variant **shares**, not outcomes **conditional on the same path**. That is the highest-leverage design given this log, and it is missing.

**Smaller factual and logical nits.** “V5 share 2–8% for others” mixes variant share (2–3%) with accommodation-request rates. “The burden is unjustified in magnitude” overreaches: long specialized-queue waits are concerning and consistent with scarcity, but service time vs queue time is not separately identified, so necessity of some extra time is not ruled out. Group D’s residuals are the ones estimated *after* the main legitimate-routing covariates (risk, prior cases) and are under-weighted relative to B/C time gaps. “Clustered with 7 bilingual staff” is stronger than “disproportionately assigned.” “Indeterminate to moderately unfair” is an internally unstable verdict.

**PM4Py plan is a function tour, not a correct analysis.** Coverage of the requested APIs is broad, but several usages would not do what the text claims. `filter_variants` is given comma-separated strings rather than activity tuples. `filter_case_performance(..., min_duration=30)` uses the wrong signature and no time unit (PM4Py expects seconds). `filter_paths_performance` is called without a path. Filtering `concept:name == "Language Support Contact"` at event level keeps only those events and destroys the case, which breaks waiting-time decomposition; case-level filtering is required. `get_service_time` is underspecified. Skeleton/Declare is mentioned without stating the actual constraints to test (e.g., language need ⇒ Language Support Contact; accommodation request ⇒ Accommodation Review; Risk Screening ⇒ Fraud Review; violation rates by group).

**Net.** Strong exam-answer shape and the right qualitative story (policy-required extra steps + scarce specialized capacity). Deducted heavily for treating an under-adjusted model as if it isolated unfairness, for omitting same-path contrasts, and for incorrect PM4Py usage. Not a 5, because constraints and structure are solid. Not an 8+, because those are not cosmetic issues.
