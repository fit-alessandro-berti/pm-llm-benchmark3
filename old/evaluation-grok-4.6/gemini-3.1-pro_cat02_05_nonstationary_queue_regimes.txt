The write-up follows the required outline and is stronger on framing than on arithmetic, uncertainty, or stochastic interpretation. It is not close to flawless.

**What is solid.** Estimands are mostly defined correctly: opening-hours counts, service quantiles that exclude wait, snapshot WIP, overnight freeze. **Regime estimates are coarser than claimed.** “Outage & Recovery” is one row while Apr 15 (58 completions, WIP 47, 180 min outage) and Apr 16–17 (129/112 completions, extra FTE) are different mechanisms; the arrival cell quotes only Apr 15.
