Those are exactly the “quality of the PM4Py/pandas checks” items a judge was told to score.

**Net.** Core case math on RR1–RR4 and scope is mostly right and better than a shallow answer. The sick-leave blind spot on CHG-505, the §3/§7 classification clash, the over-certain CHG-509 close, and the non-interpreted networks are enough that this is a solid-but-flawed analysis, not a near-canonical one.
