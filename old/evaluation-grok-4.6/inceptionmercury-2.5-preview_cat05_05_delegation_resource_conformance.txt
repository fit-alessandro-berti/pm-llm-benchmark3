**Business-day counting is wrong in application.** 2026-08-15/16 are the weekend, so 11 Aug is Tuesday. Two business days after Tuesday 11 Aug are Wednesday 12 and Thursday 13. A review at **Friday 14 Aug 16:00 is day three**, not “Review Day 4.” The memo claims weekend exclusion and then counts like calendar days.

**Leave treatment is too loose.** “Valid if credentials are verified” is not in the file. Asha’s VPN session is **contested** (she claims one urgent case; security cannot exclude sharing). That is an evidential gap, not a validation rule.
