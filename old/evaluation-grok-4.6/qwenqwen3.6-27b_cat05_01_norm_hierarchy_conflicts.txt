**5.7**

The write-up follows the required skeleton and is often substantively competent on scope, SoD, and expiry. It is not close to flawless. Several material errors would change or undermine the formal analysis.

**Formalization is internally false.** In §3 the defeasible rules invert the two central thresholds: Finance is written as required *unless* amount ≥ €50k, and Compliance as required *unless* new supplier or >€100k. As stated, large or new-supplier buys would drop the very controls GPIP-4 imposes. A1 is also mis-encoded as a requester≠committer ban; the source bars requester/committer from being *final approver*. The A6 line (“normally post-commitment approval cures, unless A6 applies”) and the default that verbal extensions are valid are likewise backwards. That section is a required deliverable, not decoration.

**Time-of-act discipline fails on D1.** P-502 is a 14 July morning commitment. The CFO email is 15 July 07:20. Treating R2-Fin as satisfied “via D1” applies a later, uncontrolled interpretation to an act that had already occurred. That violates the problem’s own rule that norms are applied at the moment of commitment, and it is the same error the paper correctly avoids on P-501 (commitment at 08:50, ECD-2 at 09:00).

**Hierarchy and case law contradict each other.** §1–2 say ECD-2/D cannot override A2/A3/A1 without a CRO A7 exception, and that D cannot waive integrity controls. P-505, P-507, and P-508 then treat D1 as curing missing Finance (and P-508 as still justified above the €150k cap). JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION is a possible *policy* stance; it is not the stance the hierarchy announced. A2’s independent budget-owner limb is repeatedly collapsed into “Finance.”

**Evidence is mislabeled.** ON-44 is the 14 July 08:30–13:00 ERP outage. It is used to explain a 18 July clock offset (P-505) and a 17 July document-queue lag (P-507). The *principle* (contemporaneous ack/PDF over ERP) is right; the citation is wrong. P-504 lists R4-PO as satisfied even though commit-before-PO has no ECD-2 or clean B4 basis for a cafeteria freezer. P-501’s “unauthorized committer” and “R6-Plant” smear B4’s Plant-Manager substitution into a commitment-authority rule and reuse the wrong rule ID.

**What still works.** Classifications are not uniformly lenient or harsh. P-503 (triple-hat + weak backup-line scope + missing A2/A3), P-504 (outside ECD-2), and P-506 (expiry, unproven verbal extension, late Plant) are the right neighborhood. P-501 correctly refuses to back-date ECD-2. A6 is used as a non-cure. §5 states premise changes rather than vague “another view.” §7 covers authority, scope, thresholds, SoD, new suppliers, late records, expiry, and a real anti-normalization brake.

Those strengths keep the paper out of the lower band. The inverted catalogue, retroactive D1, and hierarchy/case split are enough that a strict judge cannot treat it as a high-quality compliance opinion.
