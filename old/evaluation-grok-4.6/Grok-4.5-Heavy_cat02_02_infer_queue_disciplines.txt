The write-up has the right *shape* of a process-mining diagnosis (decision sets, competing predictors, calibrated confidence, hybrids). It is not close to flawless. Several claims are factually wrong, the QC story is internally inconsistent, and some of the highest-value decision epochs are simply omitted.

## Factual errors

**QB SPT predictions are wrong.** At 10:28 the eligible estimates are B04 = 8, B06 = 20, B07 = 6. SPT selects **B07**, not B04. At 10:00, B01 remaining time is 45 − (14+17) = 14, B05 = 14, B04 = 8; SPT is **B04**, not “residual or B04.” These are exactly the comparisons the method claims to rest on.

**Estimate noise is invented.** Every job’s `est_min` equals realized busy time exactly (including B01’s three segments: 14+17+14 = 45). Section 5.4’s claim that realized times “sometimes match… sometimes not” is false and was checkable from the prompt. That also reverses the implication: SEPT is *more* identifiable here, not less.

**A1 calibration is misused.** A02 ends at 08:52, when calibration *begins*. That is not “interruption avoidance.”

## Important decision epochs omitted

The brief asked for reconstruction at service starts *and* preemptions. Three of the most discriminative instants are missing:

- **QB 09:48** — documented “dispatcher reassignment” off residual B01 onto B03. Without this row, “preemptive priority” looks more systematic than the notes allow.
- **QB 10:14** — resume p3 B01 over p2 B06 and short B04. Second resume-over-priority observation; one is already their strongest contradiction.
- **QD 11:20** — D-X becomes free and **idles 10 minutes** for D06’s appointment, while D03 (elec), D08 (p1 mech) and exclusive D07 are eligible. Stronger appointment evidence than the 11:30 start they do record.
- **QD 11:21** — D-F takes general electrical D03 rather than exclusive D05 (which still fits before the 12:00 meeting: 11:21+30=11:51). This **contradicts** the later “reserve D-F for exclusive work, route electrical to D-X” patch used to excuse the 11:36 idle. D-F had just done the opposite.

Minimum-three tables are not an excuse for dropping the episodes that most stress the claimed rule.

## Weaker but material issues

- **QB primary label is too strong.** Observed automatic preemption is one p1 arrival. Completions at 09:31 and 10:14 prefer the residual p3 job over p2. A tighter primary is “p1-only preemption + resume residual + FIFO,” with one documented manual reassignment. Confidence 60 is in the right band; the name is not.
- **QD “skill-based routing” vs sequencing.** Skills here are mostly *hard eligibility* (D05↔D-F, D07↔D-X). Among eligible jobs, the repeated signal is FIFO (D04 ≻ D08) plus appointment hold. Treating a constraint as the discipline blurs the thing the benchmark is asking to identify.
- **QA contradicting cell is a dodge.** “None major” is not a strongest contradicting episode. A06 started 5 minutes late while A08 (p1) waited is the honest residual tension (non-preemption vs appointment).
- **Sloppy eligible-set wording.** “D05 or D10” for FIFO at 11:36 (FIFO is D05). Residual B01 remaining time never computed. QA 08:30 omits A06 (arrived 08:29) without a scan-delay assumption.
- **§4/§1 mismatch.** The QC discriminator is pure SEPT vs batch-SEPT — not the SLA hybrid advertised in the diagnosis table. Fine as an extra test; it shows the author does not actually believe their own secondary rule.

That is a competent draft with several load-bearing mistakes. Under a strict bar it is clearly not an 8–10.
