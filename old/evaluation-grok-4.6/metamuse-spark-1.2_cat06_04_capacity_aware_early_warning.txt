**6.2**

The memo is structurally complete and clears the obvious failure modes (no swarm on I-03; I-05 suppressed vs I-07 re-entered; spillover and segment calibration are first-class). It is not nearly flawless. Decision-theoretic coherence and auditability — the stated grading axes — are repeatedly broken.

**Internal contradiction on I-01.** Section 1 says: if `Robust_Benefit` is below marginal spillover (~€1,500–2,500 base), defer even if standalone-positive. I-01’s own figure is €920 (mid €1,685). Base spillover per slot at their €18k assumption is \(3 \times 6 \times 0.02 \times 18{,}000\) wait no: **one** slot is \(6 \times 0.02 \times 18{,}000 = €2{,}160\). Mid I-01 net of swarm + false-alarm cost is then \(2{,}385 - 700 - 2{,}160 = -€475\). Adding I-01 to {I-04, I-09} **lowers** base portfolio net (~€8.2k → ~€7.5k). They apply a marginal test to reject a 4th slot and then ignore that same test for the 3rd. That is the central coherence failure.

**The 4th-slot justification is numerically false.** They write that a 4th slot “adds marginal benefit €500–950 (I-02 or I-07).” I-02’s **mid** direct benefit is €2,700 (ARR 0.15 × €25k − €1,050). Under their own base arithmetic, I-02’s marginal net is roughly \(3{,}750 - 1{,}050 - 2{,}160 = +€540\), not a drag to “~€5k.” They mix ARR_low for the rejected case with midpoints for the accepted ones.

**Section 3 is not auditable, despite claiming to be.** Optimistic cash figures (2,763 + 14,100 + 5,400) do not match the ARRs used for breach counts (0.31 × 9k = 2,790; 0.24 × 60k = 14,400). Adverse “€5,200–€8,860” is unexplained: all-low ARR×Value is \(1{,}620+6{,}000+3{,}600=11{,}220\); one-zero is 7,620. Neither bound appears. Stacking 0.03 spillover **and** an extra 0.02–0.04 delay-breaches double-counts the same congestion channel. Column header says “range”; Optimistic and Base are point estimates.

**Calibration is conflated with causal effect.** The brief says Strategic **p** is overpredicted 0.05–0.12, not that ARR is too high. They correctly say “do not inflate ARR,” then write I-02’s “ARR likely over-estimated pre-redesign” and demote a complete, on-stage, high-value case on that leap. Conversely they call I-09’s 0.18–0.30 “credible” while the brief says NNL calibration **and** effect estimates are uncertain. High-epistemic I-09 gets a locked slot; better-measured I-02 is standby. Uncertainty talk and allocation do not match.

**Tiers do not implement the stated score.** I-01 Robust €920 is Tier 1 INTERVENE; I-02 Robust €950 is Tier 2 WAIT. Tiers are a narrative overlay, not a rule. I-04 (10 h, Triage) is treated as must-fire-now rather than a high-priority **wait** that preserves option value for I-06 verification or the regulatory arrival — a weak use of the wait action the template requires.

**False-alarm burden is mis-specified.** The brief attaches the burden to false alarms. Charging the full €250+€2,000+€800 in every scenario, including Optimistic, treats disruption as a fixed fee. Conservative, but it distorts ranking (especially I-04 / I-08 / I-09) and is never sensitivity-tested as \((1-p)\times\) burden.

**Smaller defects that still matter.** Smashed quantities (`0.420.61`, `ARR0`, `n50`, missing Δ); €18k queue value not tied to actual untreated diagnosis-stage cases; no re-allocation if I-06’s queue field returns; no sequencing of three 45-minute swarms on two specialists (parallelism would spike instantaneous congestion); reserve rule (“two cases with Robust >€2,000 and deadline <4 h”) is ad hoc and immediately inconsistent with taking I-04 (10 h). Fairness and monitoring sections are the strongest parts (segment offsets, IPW/holdout labels, placebo at Diagnosis Complete, spillover-by-segment, circuit breaker) but sit on top of an allocation that already starves the marginal-EV logic they preach.

Hits the required six headings and the I-03 / I-05–I-07 / spillover tripwires. Loses the grade on contradictions, unauditable impact math, and p/ARR confusion. Not a 7+: those are not nits.
