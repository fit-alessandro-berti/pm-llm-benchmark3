**5.5**

The write-up is complete and hits the hard quality bars (no swarm on I-03, I-05 suppressed vs I-07 re-eligible, ENB-style scoring, spillover, segment calibration, fairness, monitoring). It is not a `p_now` ranking. It is also not decision-theoretically reliable. Several flaws are load-bearing, not cosmetic.

**The stated rule is not the rule that was used.** Section 1 lets a second alert with no new event proceed only if the risk-reduction midpoint exceeds 0.20. I-02’s midpoint is 0.15 (`0.08–0.22`), yet I-02 is selected. That is a deployable-policy failure: the allocation cannot be audited against the rule that supposedly generated it.

**Calibration is announced and then discarded.** Segment shifts (`+0.065` SME, `−0.085` Strategic Enterprise) are said to be applied “before computing benefit,” but ENB is `ARR × value − cost − false-alarm − spillover`. Adjusted `p` never enters the score. For a 100% Strategic Enterprise portfolio after a known overprediction of `0.05–0.12`, that is not a small omission. I-04 / I-08 / I-02 are exactly the segment where alerts are most likely inflated and where “the model’s reason for alerting was wrong” is most plausible. Labeling the optimistic row “High” confidence is backwards: that is a tail scenario, and this portfolio makes the tail less believable, not more.

**I-08 is a weak second slot.** Eight hours to SLA, still in Triage, ARR only `0.05–0.15`, false-alarm burden €5,000, SE overprediction. Plenty of time remains to wait for evidence. Using a scarce 45-minute senior swarm here is premature. I-01 (Public Sector, first alert, ARR `0.18–0.35`, acceptable calibration) is a cleaner causal candidate and is deferred. Value-maxing is explicit; robustness is not.

**Spillover is under-counted.** The brief is each slot lifting breach probability by `0.01–0.03` on **six** untreated diagnosis-stage cases. The arithmetic uses the three visible diagnosis-stage IDs and ~€17.5k average value (€1.4k–€4.2k). That ignores the stated six, specialist withdrawal (two seniors, three 45-minute swarms), and the 15–30 minute adverse delay. Adverse net impact is still written as a comfortable +€7.4k–€8.9k. A coherent adverse case for this set—lower-bound effects, one null effect, full I-08 disruption, congestion on six cases—can be near zero or negative. The table does not show that.

**Section 3 does not add up.** Optimistic “breaches avoided” uses upper bounds (0.65), while “direct economic benefit range” spans the base midpoint-sum to the optimistic sum (€25,150–€37,300). The optimistic net band therefore overlaps the base case. Notes claim `3 × 3 × 0.01 × €17.5k = €1,575 ≈ €1.4k–€2.8k`. False-alarm burden is in the selection ENB and missing from the impact table (€2,000 + €5,000 + €600 if realized). Intervention cost is only labor (€1,350). Net impact is overstated and not auditable in the way claimed.

**False-alarm treatment is crude.** Subtracting the full burden as a certain cost is not a probability-weighted false-alarm loss, and then that term disappears from portfolio impact. I-07’s wait is consistent with a small ENB (€5k value) but operationally thin: 1.5 hours to SLA, SME underprediction, new log, ARR `0.20–0.32`. The I-05 vs I-07 *language* is correct; the *actions* are both Wait, with no short-horizon re-entry if diagnosis is about to complete.

**Fairness is after-the-fact.** Section 5 correctly names value starvation, NNL uncertainty, calibration gaps, label contamination, and spillover on untreated groups, and the safeguards (quartile quota, monthly segment factors, shadow controls, €2k spillover ceiling) are concrete. None of them bind the live allocation: three of three slots go to Strategic Enterprise; SME, Public Sector, and NNL all wait. A quota that is not applied today is a principle, not a control. Matching “comparable” non-intervened cases after ENB selection will not identify treatment effects.

**What remains solid:** stage gate on I-03; cooldown / 3-alert suppression / new-event re-entry; explicit I-05 vs I-07 contrast; I-06 imputation caution; reserved slot as a preference not a law; monitoring dimensions that match the brief. Those keep the grade out of the failure band. The self-contradiction on I-02, the all-SE book under known overprediction, the mis-specified spillover base, and the incoherent impact table are enough to keep it well below a high score.
