**7.0**

The final report is well structured, cautious, and mostly aligned with the required headings and constraints. It does not allege fraud, does not treat high co-working as suspicious by itself, and correctly frames the log as a mixture of emergency handling, informal specialization, and data-quality problems, with the V-77x bank-change/payment cluster as the highest-sensitivity follow-up. Prioritization, benign alternatives, and the audit-report wording are the strongest parts.

The score is held down by several non-trivial flaws.

The largest conceptual error is conflating same-person four-eyes violations with two-person working pairs. The rule is that one human must not perform both activities. Listing “Buyer_Carla / Approver_Gita” and “VendorMaster_Lena / AP_Kim” as the four-eyes exception pairs treats repeated handover pairs as if they were same-resource SoD breaches. Section 3 later separates the 42 Create/Approve cases more carefully, but Section 1 still blurs two different phenomena.

Section 4 has real tooling errors, not just signature drift. `pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle` keeps cases that *satisfy* four-eyes (different resources), yet the answer stores the result as `violations`. That inverts the filter. `filter_event_attribute_values` is used as if it were event-level resource filtering; the usual default is case-level retain, which would not strip bot events. `filter_paths_performance(..., performance=...)` is not the normal min/max API. `discover_network_analysis` is called without the edge/node columns it needs. The fallback four-eyes function and the custom retrospective check are conceptually better than the PM4Py calls they sit next to.

Smaller issues: severity mixes control risk and data-quality impact in one column with no scale; the working-day check via `bdate_range(...).size - 1` is brittle; and a few API names/parameters are invented while being presented as runnable code.

The evidential judgment is still better than the technical scaffolding. That is why this is a solid but clearly imperfect 7, not a high score.
