**Material flaws.**

1. **§7 uses the wrong ready event.** `wait_between(..., "Inspect", "Batch Cure")` cannot produce the commented 235/220/10 minutes. In the Job DFG, Inspect is *after* Machine Process; Batch Cure *is* the oven processing step. On a correctly related log those timestamps would be near-zero or negative. The code therefore does not operationalize the §2 decomposition and quietly reintroduces a flattening artifact the rest of the answer warns against. `Ready for Test` is correctly flagged as possibly missing; Inspect is not.

2. **The 1.8σ argument is statistically oversold.** The bound \(z_{\max}=(n-1)/\sqrt{n}\approx 2.04\) is for *population* SD with \(n-1\) ties. It does not make a 1.8σ threshold “vacuous,” nor does it imply the test “excludes almost nothing.” With sample SD the cap is \(\sqrt{5}\approx 2.24\). “No job > 1.8σ” at \(n=6\) is weak evidence, but the right point is *coupling equalizes job totals* (waits are transferred, not that the threshold is almost unattainable). Rhetoric here slightly cheats in favor of the OCEL story.

3. **Rank 1 vs §5 Narrative C are not reconciled.** Rank 1 is batch synchronization because 455 job-minutes accrued while oven and rack were free; rank 3 treats J1’s 355 min as a symptom of J4. Narrative C then conjectures that J4’s pack at 19:10 is test-queue delay behind J5 → CAL-3 recalibration → J6, which would make CAL-3 the *proximate* cause of S1/J1 and would reorder ranks 1–3. That conjecture is properly labeled, but the ranking table never states that the order is loss-function dependent (summed B1 job-minutes vs max delivery wait vs last-mile cause) or that rank 1 is conditional on J4’s post-unload interval *not* being TEST-1. “CAL-3, not FIX-A, is the proximate cause” is too strong for an interval with no `Final Test` on J4.

4. **Three different tool mechanisms are collapsed into rank 2.** FIX-A (unrecorded hold through a second job), RACK-7 (cleaning cycle pacing B1→B2), and CAL-3 (mandatory recalibration) are distinct policies. Bundling them as “shared-tool contention” is closer to “shared resources cause delays” than the rest of the paper. The alternative ranking note (RACK-7 first) only partially fixes this.

5. **J2’s 220 min is used and withdrawn.** C1 correctly says J2’s 08:25 readiness is policy-invalid under L7 quarantine until 13:50, so B1 arguably should have been J1+J3 only. The rank-1 headline still uses “455 job-minutes” and “235+220.” The defensible B1 sync wait if policy is respected is J1 waiting on J3 (08:10→11:55 ≈ 225 min), plus a governance incident on J2—not a second 220 min of synchronization.
