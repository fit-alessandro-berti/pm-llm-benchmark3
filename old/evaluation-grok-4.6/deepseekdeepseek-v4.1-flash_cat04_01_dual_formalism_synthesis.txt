**8.5**

The final write-up is a strong, honest modeling solution: the POWL partial order matches the intended cross-branch dependencies, the process tree is a documented conservative under-approximation rather than a disguised exact model, and the eight witnesses actually test the interesting cases (including A5, which correctly separates intended/POWL from the tree).

**What is right**

- Assumptions resolve every required point: XOR risk routes, XOR skip/supplier, loop-as-pass/fail, no duplicate visible labels, under-approximation policy, overlap as missing order.
- POWL structure is the right one: `RD→CM/ARC`, `CM→PV`, `ARC→DCA/risk/supplier`, `DCA∧PV→trial`, `trial∧risk∧supplier→RM→AC`, high-risk as an unordered pair, trial as `LOOP(ET→IT, AP)`.
- The tree `RD → (CM→PV ∥ ARC→DCA) → (trial ∥ risk ∥ supplier) → RM → AC` keeps mandatory precedences and approval/trial overlap, at the cost of delaying risk/supplier until both work products exist. That tradeoff is named and defended.
- A5 is a real approximation witness (SR after ARC, before CM/DCA/PV). F1 is a real cross-branch counterexample (ET before DCA). F2/F3 hit release sync and the AP-must-be-followed-by-a-new-cycle rule.
- Section 6 and the validation plan distinguish exactness from conversion/playout, which is what the task asked for.

**What keeps it from the top**

- The dependency table’s “concurrency (none)” is unclear; “no order” would have been enough.
- Overlap is called “true concurrency” even though both formalisms here are completion-interleaving models.
- The tree is coarser than it needs to be on supplier vs. design/planning (CS cannot overlap DCA/PV at all). The loss is disclosed, but it is a large bite of intended behavior.
- Construction code is plausible and parent/child handling is explicit, but it is untested; `children.append` can fail on some PM4Py `ProcessTree` property implementations.
- Compact tree/`tau` notation is not tied to a `parse_process_tree` path, and silent skip is not discussed as extra tau in a converted net.

Those are presentation and residual-semantics nits on an otherwise coherent, internally consistent answer—not hidden unsoundness in the POWL.
