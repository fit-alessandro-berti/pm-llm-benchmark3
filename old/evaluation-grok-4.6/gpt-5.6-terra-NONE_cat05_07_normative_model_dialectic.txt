7.2

The analytical core (sections 1–5) is strong: both briefs are genuinely steelmanned, burden of proof is stated before it is applied, authority/expiry/scope are treated as distinct from trace fit, and the system-level conclusion is not a bland split. The score is held well below the top band because the formal model revision has several semantic errors, and a few case calls are looser than the answer’s own evidentiary standard.

**What holds up.** Stipulated vs disputed vs unproved is mostly accurate (PD-3 authority, FD-7 after expiry, 709 scope, 710 silence, 706 linkage, 714 lack of amendment). Prosecution correctly treats CS-9 as the baseline, uses 703/708/705/714/712 as independent of M0’s defects, and does not let “desirable” or “no harm” rewrite policy. Defense correctly separates concurrency, overlays, CRM missingness, label mapping, and scope from misconduct, and concedes the same hard cases. The matrix is complete and usable. The layered architecture (POWL vs DECLARE vs data checks vs org controls vs overlay register) is the right decomposition.

**Model semantics — material defects.** Same `Transition` objects (`sanctions`, `quality`, `manual_approval`, `activate`) are reused inside exclusive XOR branches. In PM4Py POWL that is ill-formed: nodes are not reusable subtrees; repeated labels need distinct objects, or common steps must be hoisted. The reject child is a bare `Reject_Supplier`, so M1 both (a) allows rejection with no sanctions and (b) cannot replay SUP-709 (`Sanctions_Check` then reject). The info construct is also misaligned with the motivating trace: the loop sits only after register and before the whole decision block, and each iteration requires a new `Receive_Additional_Evidence` event that the log does not contain. SUP-707 would not fit. The comment that LOOP children are a “redo/exit approximation, depending on PM4Py loop semantics” is an admission that the operator was not pinned down. Auto-route quality is hardcoded *after* activation; PD-3 only *permits* delay, so a compliant quality-first pilot case would fail M1.

**Judgment friction.** The burden section demands affirmative overlay evidence, then SUP-702 is `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` even though PD-3’s required automated master-data validation is absent from the trace (only flagged as missing, medium confidence). That is a self-inflicted inconsistency. SUP-713 as `COMPLIANT` is generous: mapping was approved after execution; `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT` or a qualified mixed call would be tighter. SUP-715 as `MIXED` is allowed and explained, but the facts already show a required COI disclosure was not recorded; the residual uncertainty is overstated. SUP-711 as indeterminate is overly cautious once the shared source transaction ID is stipulated.

**Lesser issues.** Auto/emergency eligibility is correctly pushed out of POWL, but the code never states the placement/replay limits that actually matter (707, 709). Withdrawal/reactivation are described in prose and then omitted from M1 without a second, scoped POWL. None of this sinks the legal analysis; it does keep the artifact well short of “nearly flawless” on the dimensions the judge was told to weight (model semantics and the quality of the proposed revision).
