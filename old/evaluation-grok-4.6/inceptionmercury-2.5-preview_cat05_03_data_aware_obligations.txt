- The embedded CSV **drops** `TX-305` `Settlement_Confirmed`. The prompt said not to read external files *and* to reconstruct the log; omitting the event that makes TX-305 hard is a data error.
- No sort by `(time:timestamp, event_index)` before forward-fill, so same-timestamp causality (TX-311) is not implemented.

- Item 6 is missing: no R4 flag; R3 is “any list update exists”; **no exemption register join**. `exemption_id` is taken from `iloc[0]` (the create row, almost always empty), not from release.
- Item 7 is missing: no evidence timestamps, no unknown-value flags. Diagnostics are four coarse booleans.

- `get_state_at_release` can pull a later same-timestamp update or a NaN `ffill` tail; it does not evaluate “latest known value at this event.”

Coarse flags that ignore review-after-change and evidence quality cannot support the classifications in §3. Hard-coding narrative labels as a lookup was forbidden; this code cannot derive those labels at all.
