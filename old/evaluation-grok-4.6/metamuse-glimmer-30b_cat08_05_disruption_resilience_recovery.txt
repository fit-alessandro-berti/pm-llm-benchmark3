**Material analytical gaps.** T3 is under-used. The case makes partial recovery a first-class regime (intake up, inventory sync and scanning unreliable). That hybrid state is often more dangerous than a clean outage because staff can trust a half-live system. The recovery model is basically full-manual plus post-return reconcile; it does not specify degraded-system rules (when a reservation, scan, or temperature event may be trusted, when it must be dual-written, when the system must be treated as advisory only). Recommendations carry effort/benefit/residual risk but are not ranked, which the prompt asked for. The proposed process invents triggers and timers (30 minutes, 10%, 4h/8h) without saying they are policy choices. Including `Skip authorization [forbidden]` inside the recovery notation is sloppy: a redesigned model should omit that path, not decorate it. T1 is a stub.
