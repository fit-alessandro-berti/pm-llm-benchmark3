**5.4**

The answer is well-structured and gets the headline right: the crude Orion gap is a case-mix / routing artifact, stratification reverses the cycle-time comparison, and a 50% reassignment is a bad idea. That is not enough. Under a strict reading, the analysis contains a core sign error, internal contradictions, several wrong numbers, a cyclic “DAG,” a process-semantics mistake, and code that violates a stated constraint.

**The decisive failure is the queue comparison.** Within every complexity stratum Orion has *shorter* waits (Low 3.45 vs 5.06, Medium 9.25 vs 11.12, High 19.47 vs 22.75). Section 1 asserts the opposite (“Orion still shows longer queue waits”) while printing those same numbers. Hypothesis 2 then invents “Low +1.6 h, Med +1.9 h, High +3.3 h” as Orion *penalties*. That inversion is not a typo: it is used to justify `partly causal through mechanisms`, a “genuine harmful Orion effect on queueing,” and Phase 1’s claim that queueing is “the only mediator that Orion could plausibly worsen.” Longer wait + nearly identical service + shorter cycle cannot hold at once. Anyone adding `wait_before_review_h` to `review_service_h` would have seen the contradiction. That is a process-mining competence failure, not a caveat.

**Arithmetic is sloppy in ways that change the residual story.** Section 4’s table reports a complexity-standardized contrast of Orion 17.3 vs Nova 16.3 (+1.0 h). The immediately following calculation, using the same weights, is Orion 17.14 vs Nova 19.65 (Orion *better* by ~2.5 h). The Orion-mix reweighting is also wrong: \(7.5\cdot\frac{2}{14}+17.17\cdot\frac{6}{14}+31.57\cdot\frac{6}{14}\) recovers ~21.96 h, not 18.6 h; the paired Nova figure should be ~24.7 h, not 22.6 h. Compliance-required means are computed on C03–C14, which includes C04/C06/C08 (`compliance_required=No`); the true Orion required mean is ~26.7 h, not 23.9 h. There are two appeals (C14 and C24), not one.

**Causal discipline is uneven.** Detecting Simpson’s reversal and refusing to treat stratification as identification are strengths. Required distinctions among total, direct, and mediated estimands stay implicit. The adjacency list is not a DAG: `shift → routing_rule` and `routing_rule → shift` form a cycle. Treating 30-day appeals as causing in-process `Request Info` rework reverses time; `outcome_30d` is post-close. `case_completed → (selection on completed)` is not a well-formed edge.

**Code is diagnostic in intent and non-compliant in detail.** It embeds the CSV, reproduces crude/stratified means, standardizes on a stated common distribution, prints overlap cells, warns on \(n<3\), and compares variants. It also fabricates a single timestamp for every event, against the instruction not to invent timestamps, and that choice can scramble directly-follows order if the parser sorts by time. `discover_dfg` is also treated as a dict; current PM4Py returns a tuple, so the DFG block is likely to hit the except path.

**What remains good does not offset this.** Hypotheses 1, 3, 4, and 6 are well posed, with predicted observations. The three-phase plan (capacity first, then a restricted Med randomization, then a holdout policy update) is safer and more informative than the proposed 50% cut. Section 8’s five uncertainties are the strongest part of the memo. Those pieces would support a high score only if the mechanism evidence and numbers were trustworthy. They are not. The verdict is therefore only partly earned, and the mechanism story is not supported by the data the answer itself tabulated.
