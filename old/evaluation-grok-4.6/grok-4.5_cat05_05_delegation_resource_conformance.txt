**7.3**

The write-up is a strong, well-structured investigation that hits the required headings, mostly respects event-time authority, and generally follows RR6. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading, several instruction misses, one internal inconsistency, and a few incomplete legal/control analyses keep it well below the top of the scale.

**What is actually good.** Alias normalization (`b.koch`/`u_bk`) is applied before SoD. Service accounts are treated as non-human, with CHG-509 attributed to Eva and CHG-503 left unmapped. Event-time roles are used (Eva is not QM on 08-09; her IV→QE cutover is dated). D-IC-04 is correctly voided. D-QM-01 is not silently extended to `cooling_system`. The 2-business-day clock is defined explicitly, and 14 Aug is late for CHG-505 under that definition. Later QMS insertion is not allowed to prove an earlier delegation; the 09 Aug PDF is used instead. Collusion is not inferred from the 18/23 pair. CHG-501, 502, 507, 508, and 510 are essentially right.

**Instruction and classification failures.** The task requires *one* primary label, with an optional secondary. CHG-506, CHG-509, and to a lesser extent CHG-505 are written as “UNAUTHORIZED or INDETERMINATE” (and 508 as a slash-compound). That is hedging, not classification. For 506, Section 2 says sick-leave account use is an *identity indicator*, not proof the human acted; the findings then still lead with UNAUTHORIZED. If Ben himself worked sick, D-QM-01 can still be a valid substitution plus an HR/availability breach. If a third party used the account, it is identity failure. The honest primary is INDETERMINATE. Same for 509: they themselves show the absence condition is unproven on a Saturday after leave ended; that is INDETERMINATE, not a coin-flip with UNAUTHORIZED.

**Substantive analytical gap.** Asha’s VPN session on 13 Aug 09:15–09:40 (CHG-504) is analyzed only as an identity risk for *her* approval. It is not applied to D-QM-01’s “while Asha is absent” condition for Ben’s later 13:00 approval of CHG-506. If the principal was working that morning, “absent” is no longer a clean calendar fact. That interaction is material and missing.

**PM4Py requirement miss.** The spec requires `filter_four_eyes_principle` for *both* Draft/Approve *and* Implement/Verify. Only the first pair is called. Using pandas for RR2 because `svc_qms` would falsely satisfy four-eyes is a fair *reason*, not compliance. The implement/verify call should still be made and then discarded with that caveat. Unused `Interval`, a dead first four-eyes result, and encoding D-IC-03 as generic `cooling_system` (CI-44 left “manual”) are smaller code defects. Networks are printed and then barely interpreted in the narrative, which under-serves the requested network reading.

**Smaller inaccuracies / sloppiness.** “IC principal” is placed in the delegated-role column. Calling the 08-12 DB lag on CHG-501 “documentary noise” sits poorly next to the otherwise correct rule that a late SoR row cannot back-date authority. Whether D-QM-01’s “QM *approval*” language even covers `Retrospective_QM_Review` is asserted, not argued. CI-44 linkage for CHG-505 is correctly flagged as missing, but the case row is still hard to score as a single finding.

**Net.** Most case mechanics are right and the control recommendations are sensible. The grade is held down because the answer repeatedly blurs the required taxonomy, contradicts its own sick-leave rule, skips a required PM4Py check, and misses the best cross-case absence/identity conflict in the file. That is a good analyst memo, not a near-flawless one.
