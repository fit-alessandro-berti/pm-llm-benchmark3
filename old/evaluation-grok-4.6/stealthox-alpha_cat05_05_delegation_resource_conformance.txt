**6.3**

This is a strong, well-structured investigation that gets most of the hard parts right: event-time roles, alias normalization, PDF-versus-migration proof, fail-closed service accounts, RR6 discipline, and generally careful alternative explanations. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading it has a clear factual error, a material missed constraint, several classification/code contradictions, and PM4Py checks that would not reproduce the narrative.

**Factual error.** Clara did not draft all ten cases, nor 100% of the extract. `CHG-508` is drafted by `b.koch` (Ben). The matrix, §4.3, and the “universal source node” story are therefore wrong. That is an unacceptable slip in a forensic identity analysis whose own point is that aliases must be read carefully.

**Material miss on `CHG-505`.** Ben’s certified sick leave runs through `2026-08-14 23:59`. The retrospective on `CHG-505` is `2026-08-14 16:00` — the same unavailability the report treats as attribution-breaking for `CHG-506`. The findings discuss lateness and a scope caveat, and never apply the sick-leave/account-use problem to that review. `D-QM-01` is also only for QM *approval* of *production-equipment* work; `Retrospective_QM_Review` of a `cooling_system` case is outside both activity and scope. That is noted in the matrix and then under-weighted in the case row and in the code, which would still call Ben a valid delegate.

**`CHG-509` is classified too high.** `D-QM-02` requires the principal to be absent. 15 August is a weekend, not documented leave. The matrix and the code correctly call the absence condition weak / `MANUAL_REVIEW`; the findings table still says `VALID_SUBSTITUTION`. By the report’s own formalization, the required condition is not evidenced, so `INDETERMINATE` (or fail-closed unauthorized) is the consistent label.

**Code does not match the findings.** The eight requested mechanics are present in outline, but the checker is not a faithful implementation of the analysis:

- `SCOPE_EQUIVALENCE` rewrites `cooling_system` → `production_equipment` before membership is tested. That would *fail* Farid’s `D-IC-03` match (`{cooling_system}`) and *pass* Ben under `D-QM-01` for cooling work — the opposite of the intended IC path.
- `evaluate_approval` is reused for retrospectives without an activity argument, so a delegation limited to `Approve_Change` is treated as authority to review.
- Ben’s sick leave is not in the availability model; only Asha is.
- Approval scoring uses raw `org:resource`, not `actor_canonical`.
- Networks are built on `actor_canonical`, so `CHG-509` remains `svc_qms`; the prose claims that edge is re-pointed to Eva.
- `hn.items()` / `wn.items()` on PM4Py SNA objects is version-fragile; a block sold as executable should not depend on that.

So the “expected flag pattern” is an analyst story, not a reliable output of the listed APIs.

**Smaller but real issues.** Two same-day cooling emergencies (`CHG-505` and `CHG-510`) both plausibly sit inside CI-44; incident-scope is handled as “505 iff CI-44” and not as a two-case problem. `CHG-504` as `INDETERMINATE` versus `CHG-506` as `UNAUTHORIZED` is arguable given the denial and office IP, but it is a sharp distinction for the same class of evidence (account use while the named person is away). The §7 split (40–45 / 25–30 / 25–30) double-counts `CHG-508` and does not add to 100%. RR1–RR3 are written with dropped connectives, which makes the formalization harder to audit than it should be.

**What remains good.** `CHG-501` as `VALID_SUBSTITUTION` on the signed PDF; `CHG-502`/`CHG-508`/`CHG-510` self-approval (the last via void subdelegation) as the real SoD/authority failures; `CHG-503` fail-closed and not credited to Diego; `CHG-507` not back-projected from a later QE title; business-day counting that is convention-robust for the two emergency cases; benign *and* adverse accounts for each indicator; no collusion finding from the Clara–Ben pair alone. Those are the parts that keep the score in the mid-6s rather than lower.

The report reads like a high-quality draft that was not reconciled against the log, the sick-leave interval, or its own checker. That is enough, on a near-flawless bar, to keep it well below a high score.
