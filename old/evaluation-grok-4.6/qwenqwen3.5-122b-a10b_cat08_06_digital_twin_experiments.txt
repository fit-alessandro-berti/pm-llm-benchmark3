**5.6**

The write-up follows the required eight-section scaffold and is conceptually in the right family: a resource-constrained DES digital twin, not a replay of independent traces. It treats objections as an unobserved process, separates applicant delay from municipal service, and includes bundling, seasonal demand, and a baseline-plus-intervention matrix. Those are the main reasons it is not a low score. Under a strict reading of the brief, it is still far from a defensible design.

**Simulation design is incomplete as a twin.** The boundary and entity list are reasonable, but the state model never makes the process tree’s control-flow semantics first-class. There is no AND-join state for “Decision drafting waits for every applicable review,” no explicit council-week / holiday calendar objects, no queue disciplines for D6 aging priority, and no mechanism by which neighborhood objections hit *related* cases rather than stretching one activity’s duration. Architect coupling is reduced to inspection bundles; same-week multi-application contention and shared-architect document quality are not state. Calendars are named, then mostly left as comments. That violates the “not an independent-case sim” and “do not ignore calendars” constraints in spirit even if they are mentioned.

**Process-mining grounding is the weakest part.** Several cited APIs are not PM4Py conventions: `pm4py.log_statistics.get_case_timestamps`, `pm4py.performance.analysis.get_performance`, `pm4py.statistics.transition_occurrence`, `from pm4py.algo.discovery.dfg import apply`, `from pm4py.visualization.dfg.view import visualize`. The required performance DFG is never computed. Service/waiting columns are never created. The process tree is not parsed; the “limitation” note is a shrug, not an account of `->` / `+` / `X` / `*` / `tau` or redo-loop meaning. After `format_dataframe`, the object is treated as an event iterator (`for event in log`), which is a DataFrame column-name loop and makes the arrival-rate calculation wrong. The 18-month empirical service/wait tables are not used as the primary estimator when starts are missing; the text both infers starts from the previous completion (collapsing wait+service) and samples a median service time, without a coherent identification strategy.

**Assumptions are unevenly honest.** The objection-as-mixture treatment is correct. Routing is not: fire/environment/public-notice are treated as known at submission, even though the brief says area thresholds and environmental sensitivity are incomplete. Invented quantities (5% sickness, Weibull applicant delay, 70–85% utilization band, 60d/120d SLAs) are presented as design facts. Arrival modeling contradicts itself (NHPP in §2, Gamma interarrivals in §3). D6 is specified as removing a planner rather than a reservation/priority rule.

**Experiments and uncertainty are generic DES boilerplate.** The table lists D1–D9, D10, and three stresses, which satisfies the outline, but D5/D8/D9 are not operationalized, demand non-stationarity is not a designed factor, and D6/D7 are caricatures. Warm-up of six months plus “18 months calibration + 6 months forecast” is confused for a two-peak seasonal system; there is no initialization-bias diagnostic, no common random numbers for policy comparison, and normal CIs on replication means are offered as if they handled p90 of a 260-day tail. Thirty replications are asserted, not justified.

**Validation and decision criteria miss required items.** “>90% variant coverage” confuses process-model fitness with stochastic predictive validity. Holdout of the last three months cannot test both peak seasons. There is no plan to validate the wait/service split, Monday spikes, district travel, or inter-case dependence. Decision metrics omit **rework** and **backlog** (explicitly required) and architect-level fairness. Thresholds are broken in the text (“Reduction  10%”) and poorly grounded in the provided duration table. No optimality is claimed; that part is fine.

**Code** is modular in shape and correctly avoids `pm4py.read_csv` / uses `format_dataframe` with the right column names. That is not enough: it fails the required computations, uses implausible PM4Py paths, and does not ingest the given excerpt.

In short: a competent outline with several of the right instincts, undermined by incorrect PM4Py usage, missing mandated artifacts, a thin state/calendar model, and decision/validation gaps. Not close to flawless.
