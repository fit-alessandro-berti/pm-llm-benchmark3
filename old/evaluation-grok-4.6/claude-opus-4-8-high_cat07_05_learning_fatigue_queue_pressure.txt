## Material flaws

**1. Automation logic is backwards.** The log says humans *correct Bot_Risk more often* when `queue_length_at_start > 70`. The table explains this as “humans have less slack to catch bot errors.” Less slack implies *missed* errors, i.e. *fewer* corrections. Section 2 then switches to a different story (bot more often wrong-in-context). Those cannot both be the mechanism. A bot also has no reason to become less accurate merely because WIP is high; the likely channels are case-mix into the bot, override behavior, or delayed QC detection. This is a real causal error, not wording.

**2. Fatigue vs circadian identification is muddled in §3.** Section 1 states the design correctly: same clock hour, different time-on-task isolates hours-on-task. Section 3 then says the Day vs Late 14:00–17:00 residual is “fatigue/circadian rather than the hour itself.” Circadian *is* the hour. After holding clock time fixed, the residual is fatigue/load/mix, not circadian. The two sections disagree.

**3. Overclaim that fatigue is secondary.** “Later under low WIP” is not a clean fatigue test. First-20 events are also higher-complexity, higher-priority, higher-backlog events. Juniors’ 5.1% → 4.4% can be remaining case-mix. The stronger natural experiment in the prompt—Early last two hours (error 4.2%, queue falling 61→42) vs Day last two hours (7.5%, queue rising 38→75), similar time-on-task, different load—is underused. Fatigue is probably not primary; it is not as ruled out as the narrative asserts.

**4. Proposed regression is misspecified.** `error_flag ~ position + minutes_since_shift_start + ...` puts two near-collinear time-on-task measures in one linear predictor (the position bins map almost monotonically onto 22, 74, 151, 257, 378, 459 minutes). No VIF/spline/GAM note, and a binary outcome is written as if it were a linear mixed model. Within-resource FE is the right idea; the specification would not estimate it cleanly.

**5. PM4Py is partly wrong, not just incomplete.** `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` does not add hour-of-day/weekday to events; it is a grouped temporal-feature extractor. `hour_of_day` is then used without being created. `discover_performance_dfg` does not measure resource handovers or queue ownership; that needs resource-sequence / handover-of-work analysis. `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` is computed and never used. `get_service_time` is in the allowed list and unused, while a hand-rolled delta is used instead. These are not cosmetic in a PM4Py-oriented section.
