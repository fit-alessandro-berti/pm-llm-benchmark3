**7.0**

The write-up is complete, well structured, and mostly right on the hard points: event-time roles, alias normalization, invalid subdelegation, service-account attribution, and not treating repeated pairs as collusion. It is not near-flawless. Several closed-set, burden-of-proof, and consistency mistakes keep it well below a high score.

**What is solid.** Event-time reasoning is generally correct. Eva is not treated as QM from a later Quality Engineer title. `b.koch`/`u_bk` are merged before RR1, so CHG-508 is a real SoD break. D-IC-04 is rejected because D-IC-03 forbids subdelegation. CHG-503 is left unattributed; CHG-509 is mapped to Eva only with stronger session evidence. CHG-504 and CHG-506 are correctly left indeterminate rather than declared unauthorized from leave or sick-leave logs alone. RR4 lateness for the Friday CHG-505 review is defined and applied coherently. Section 4 gives a benign and an adverse reading plus discriminating evidence. The code hits the required PM4Py/pandas surface and does not auto-call collusion.

**Material defects.**

1. **`VALID_SUBSTITUTION` is never used.** The closed label set exists to separate standing authority from a valid delegate/emergency path. CHG-501 (Ben under D-QM-01) is labeled `AUTHORIZED`. If Farid’s CHG-505 approval is in scope, that path is substitution, not ordinary authority. Collapsing the distinction is a rubric miss, not a wording quibble.

2. **CHG-509 is over-classified.** Unproven weekend absence is not proof the principal was available. D-QM-02 is a one-day Saturday grant; lack of annual-leave on 15 Aug does not establish presence. The correct primary label is `INDETERMINATE` (or a low-confidence substitution), not `UNAUTHORIZED`. Section 7 then omits CHG-509 from the “genuine failure” list, contradicting Section 3.

3. **D-QM-01 is read too narrowly as a scope problem and too loosely as an activity grant.** The instrument authorizes *QM approval for production-equipment change controls*, not retrospective review and not cooling. CHG-505/510 reviews fail on activity type even if someone stretched “production_equipment.” That is under-argued.

4. **RR4’s credibility condition is under-weighted.** Emergency is a flag in the CSV; no safety rationale is in evidence. The exception is treated as available once IC/delegate and timing are discussed. That is weaker than the rule as written.

5. **Code and prose disagree.** `manual_ci44_cases = {"CHG-505"}` asserts the mapping the narrative calls unproven. Emergency approval logic never accepts a QM. Same-person approve+verify on CHG-509 is not flagged in code. Eva’s verifier start is invented as 2025-01-01. Extra kwargs on `filter_activity_done_different_resources` are papered over with `try/except`.

**Smaller issues.** Technical four-eyes vs control-objective strength is noted case-by-case, not applied as a consistent split. CHG-508 could carry a secondary `DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM`. CHG-505’s primary `UNAUTHORIZED` buries a possibly valid IC approval under a failed review. None of these are catastrophic; together they are enough that this is a strong 7, not an 8+.
