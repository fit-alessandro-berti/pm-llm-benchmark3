2. **P1 and P2 are not complements, and the answer only half-notices.** CL-609 sits in the uncovered band: Auto Approve is forbidden by P1 (EUR 18,000, MEDIUM), but P2 does not mandate Fraud Screen + Manual Approve. The text says “P2 is not triggered, but auto-approval is still disallowed” and then stops. It never states the implied control-design hole: the overlay does not specify a required path for this cohort. That is exactly the kind of context-sensitive point a judge is looking for. Severity 3 is defensible; the missing analysis is not.

3. **Like-for-like severity compression.** CL-608 (EUR 250,000, HIGH, all payment controls present, notification only in CRM via a mutable field) and CL-609 (EUR 18,000 policy-ineligible auto-approval) are both a 3.
