**6.5**

The write-up is a real process-mining analysis rather than a label-only guess: it reconstructs decision sets, tests FIFO / priority / SPT / appointments / skills as competing predictors, and mostly avoids the usual error of reading every FIFO violation as priority. The required seven-section format is followed. QA’s FIFO-plus-appointment reading and QB’s P1-only preemption reading are defensible. That is the ceiling of the grade. Several reconstruction errors, one inverted data-quality claim, and overconfident QD/QC statements keep it well below “nearly flawless.”

**Material reconstruction errors**

QA at 08:20 is incomplete. A04 has already arrived (08:13, P1, `est_min` 7). The eligible set is A02, A03, **and A04**, not A02 vs A03. Choosing A03 therefore beats FIFO (A02), priority (A04), and SPT (A04) simultaneously. That is stronger appointment evidence than the table reports, and it is a basic decision-set miss.

QD eligible sets are also incomplete. At 11:25 on D-M, D09 (11:20, mechanical) is waiting; D07 is waiting but skill-ineligible on D-M and should be marked as such. At 11:30 on D-X, D10 has already arrived (11:25, P1, electrical). The observed hold for D06’s 11:30 appointment is appointment-over-FIFO **and** appointment-over-priority, not an “Appointment/FIFO tie-break.”

QC is under-reconstructed after 10:32. SPT is consistent with C02 then C03, then breaks again: at 10:54 C05 (`est` 40) is taken over C06 (`est` 6); at 11:40 C01 (`est` 36, P3) is taken over C07 (`est` 18, P2, deadline 12:00). Primary SPT with C04 as the lone hard contradiction is too tidy. An undocumented hybrid, or SPT only on a restricted subset, is at least as honest.

**Diagnosis and confidence problems**

QD’s primary label should be skill-based routing, with FIFO inside the eligible subset and an appointment reservation. Many “choices” are forced (D02 only on D-F, D05 only on D-F, D01 only mechanical). Confidence 90 and “no significant contradictions” are not earned. D-X idles 11:20–11:30 for D06 (FIFO violation). D-F is idle 11:36–12:00 while D10, a feasible P1 electrical job that would finish before the 12:00 meeting, waits — unexplained by FIFO+skills. That idle is the strongest unidentified QD episode.

QC confidence 55 is appropriately low; the primary_discipline cell is still over-precise relative to the later contradictions.

QB is the best of the four, but 09:31 / 10:14 look as much like “resume the interrupted job unless a P1 is waiting” as like FIFO. The 10:00 choice of B05 over both B04 and paused B01 is the clean separator; it deserved a decision-table row. Treating “dispatcher reassignment” as a standard discipline signal would have been wrong; the write-up mostly avoids that, which is a plus.

**Section 5 is the weakest analytical section**

“Arrival scans can be delayed by up to two minutes” means the log timestamp is **late** relative to true entry (true eligibility earlier), not that logged arrivals may be early. Assumption 1 has the direction backwards. Even under the write-up’s own (wrong) direction, a 2-minute bound cannot remove C06 from the 10:32 choice: C06 is logged at 10:25, seven minutes earlier. Ranking scan delay as a **high**-plausibility explanation of C04≻C06 is inconsistent with the prompt’s own bound.

Strategic-estimate discussion of C05 is muddled: inflating C05’s estimate would make SPT *avoid* it, which matches delay of C05 but does not explain taking C04 over C06. “Skill+FIFO” is a diagnosis, not an operational exception.

**What still works**

A02≻A04 and A06≻A08 are the right QA killers for priority and SPT. A06≻A07 is a real in-sample discriminator of appointment/FIFO vs SPT. QB’s pair (B02 preempts B01; B01 resumes over B03) is used correctly to reject both pure preemptive priority and pure SPT. Calibration, D-F’s meeting vs D05’s 30-minute estimate, and “D-F is the only fully qualified resource” are handled. Section 4’s structure is right even if the second test leans on the invalid scan-delay story. Section 6 is the strongest section: queue-entry vs scan time, remaining estimate on the incumbent, eligibility set, and appointment tolerance are exactly the fields these ambiguities need. Section 7 is directionally right that QC is least identifiable and that inferring priority in QA/QD would be the most dangerous claim — though QA is at least as identifiable as QD, and QD is not “most identifiable” once the idle/appointment holes are counted.

**Net:** competent, format-complete, and often correctly anti-naive, but not trustworthy on the actual waiting sets, on scan-delay semantics, or on how cleanly SPT/FIFO fit QC and QD. That is a mid-6, not an 8+.
