**6.4**

The write-up hits the required six-section shape and the obvious “strong answer” traps: I-03 is refused on stage, I-05 is suppressed while I-07 is allowed to re-enter, effects are not treated as \(p_{\text{now}}\), and spillover is named. That is the floor for a competent answer, not the ceiling. Under a strict reading it is not decision-theoretically tight, not fully deployable, and not operationally sequenced.

The central failure is **rule–allocation incoherence**. Section 1 says to rank by midpoint ANB (with a light Strategic down-weight and a Non-Native co-sign). Midpoint ANBs among non-suppressed cases are roughly I-04 \(\approx\) €8.9k, I-08 \(\approx\) €4.6k, I-09 \(\approx\) €3.6k, I-02 \(\approx\) €2.7k, I-06 \(\approx\) €2.6k, I-01 \(\approx\) €1.7k. The selected book is I-01 / I-02 / I-04. I-01 would not survive the stated rule; I-08 and I-09 would. The later prose (fragile effect on I-08, sparse calibration on I-09) is a *different* rule—maximin / robustness—never written as an operator-reproducible score. A “deployable rule” that does not recover the live book is a serious defect.

**Timing and option value are almost absent.** I-04 has 10 hours in Triage; I-01 has 7 hours in Triage. The swarm remains useful until Diagnosis Complete, so noon consumption of two of three slots on long-fuse triage cases is not justified against Diagnosis Started clocks (I-02 3.0 h, I-09 2.5 h, I-06 4.0 h) or against holding I-04 as a wait. “Wait” is in the action set and then unused where it is most valuable. Related operational miss: two specialists cannot run three 45-minute swarms at once. There is no sequence, no pairing, and no recognition that I-02’s window can expire while a triage swarm is in flight.

**Calibration adjustments are declared and then not used.** SME \(+0.07\) and Strategic \(-0.08\) never appear as \(p_{\text{adj}}\) in the live table, and effect ranges are not robustified even though Strategic over-prediction and Non-Native sparsity should widen or shift the *effect* interval, not only \(p\). I-01 is labeled “high confidence” despite a 0.18–0.35 band and only “moderate” Public Sector samples.

**Section 3 is only partly auditable.** Optimistic points (0.30 / 0.17 / 0.22) are arbitrary interior picks, not a stated functional of each range. Spillover euros (€2–5k / €4–8k / €9–16k) are invented without untreated case values. The “direct economic benefit” column is already net of €1,350 + €2,850, while “intervention cost” sits beside it—column semantics are muddy. Subtracting the *full* false-alarm burden in every scenario (rather than \((1-p)\times\) burden, or a probability-weighted term) is what the prompt’s sketch allows, but it is applied mechanically and then treated as if it were expected cost. Adverse I-02 effect is quietly rewritten from the stated lower bound 0.08 to 0.03; that is allowed by the “wrong reason” clause, but it is not labeled as an extra assumption in the arithmetic line.

**Capacity math is incomplete.** There is no incremental test of slot 3 vs reserve vs a fourth case. After two slots, I-01’s ~€1.7k ANB is in the same order of magnitude as base spillover on six diagnosis-stage cases (\(3\times 6\times 0.02 = 0.36\) probability-points if all three slots are used). Whether the third slot still clears a robust hurdle is asserted, not shown. Non-monetary public-sector / regulatory duty is mentioned only as calibration, not as a constraint that could justify I-01 over I-08 on non-EV grounds.

Smaller defects compound the score cut: suppression inequalities lose the minus sign in places (`p_now p_prev < 0.05`), I-06’s imputation spike is deferred with no 30–60 minute confirmation SLA, and Section 5–6 safeguards (60% segment cap, hold-out cohort, ATT matching) are concrete but generic and unlinked to *this* noon book.

What remains good is real: stage eligibility, I-05 vs I-07, refusal to threshold on \(p_{\text{now}}\), three named scenarios, and a monitoring plan that distinguishes predictive calibration from causal ATT and policy-induced label bias. That is a solid B-draft, not a near-flawless policy.
