**3.0**

The write-up has the required skeleton and a few correct observed intervals (P05/P11 S1, P07 S2, P17, P21 wait), but it fails the quality bar on reconstruction, internal consistency, and the mandated Python. The hard cases are imputed in ways that contradict both the data and the author’s own assumptions.

**Reconstruction errors (decisive).** A4 says a missing S2 start lies in `[max(S1_end, previous completion on that resource), min(S2_end, next start)]`. That is the right idea; the table ignores it.

- **P05:** C1 is on P03 until 09:45, then P05 ends 10:15, then P07 starts 10:20. Feasible S2 start is **[09:45, 10:15]**, so wait **[20, 50]** and service **[0, 30]**. The answer’s wait `[10, 15]` and service `45` imply a start ~09:30, while C1 is still busy. That is exactly the silent point-imputation the prompt forbids.
- **P07 S1:** I1 is on P05 until 09:25, P07 ends 10:00, P09 starts 10:05. Feasible start is **[09:25, 10:00]**, wait **[40, 75]**, service **[0, 35]**. Reported wait `[30, 35]` starts I1 during P05. Wait `[30, 35]` is also inconsistent with the stated 30 min service (that service implies start 09:30 and wait **45**).
- **P11:** C2 runs P08 **11:15–12:00** after the outage, then P11 ends 12:25, P13 starts 12:30. Feasible start is **[12:00, 12:25]**, wait **[55, 80]**, service **[0, 25]**. Wait `[20, 25]` and service `55` put C2 on two cases at once. The note “C2 post-outage” treats 11:10 as free capacity and drops P08.

**Arithmetic and censoring.** P15 elapsed S2 service is 13:30–14:00 = **30 min**, not 80. P16 elapsed S2 wait is 14:00–12:35 = **85 min**, not 95. P21 service is left as `OPEN` with no elapsed time (30 min). P22 elapsed S1 wait (110 min) is omitted. OPEN/WAITING rows are not treated as lower bounds on final wait/service. Rounding (±2.5 min) is announced in A1 and never propagated.

**Section 4 is not computed from the log.** S1 service is not “range [25, 30]”: P01 is **20**. S2 service is not “[40, 55]”: P10 is **25**, P01 is **30**. The S1 wait median 55 / IQR [35, 95] does not match any transparent sample of the 19–21 observed or bounded waits (those medians sit near 70–75). Busy minutes and utilization contradict each other: 620–680 min on two resources over 360 min is **~86–94%** of 720 min (S2: 510–570 / ~705 after the C2 outage is **~72–81%**), not 72–79% and 59–66%. Peak S2 WIP of 5 undercounts the 14:00 state (4 waiting + 2 in service = **6**). Mean WIP 3.8 / 2.9 is unjustified precision with no trajectory. There are no subperiod arrival/departure rates, no case sojourns, and no Little’s-Law numbers anywhere—only a claim that 30-min uncensoring bins are valid, which this transient, accumulating system does not support.

**Bottleneck narrative is not supported by the same data.** Both stations are congested after mid-morning (S1 service ~25–30 min, S2 ~40–50 min, two servers each). At 14:00 S1 has 1 waiter + 1 open service; S2 has **4 waiters + 2 open services**. That is not “S1 becomes the tighter constraint after 11:10.” The outage delays P08 (S1 end 10:20, C2 free only at 11:15) but does not move the afternoon backlog from S2 to S1. Calling that switch “stable under all imputations” is overconfident: the switch is the unstable part.

**Sensitivity is cosmetic.** Optimistic/central/pessimistic only jiggle S2 waits by 5–10 min, far inside the real feasible windows (~30 min for P05, ~25 min for P11, ~35 min for P07/P15). Utilization endpoints just restate the inconsistent Section 4 figures. No scenario table actually recomputes WIP, sojourn, or Little’s Law.

**Python fails the specification.** The CSV is a `...` stub, so the block is not self-contained and does not run. `errors='coerce'` maps MISSING, OPEN, and blank to the same NaT, which the prompt explicitly forbids. Interval events keep only finite start **and** end, discarding OPEN, missing starts, and waiting cases. `insert_case_*` is applied to a COMPLETE-only projection and labelled case-level (the one compliant fragment). Station WIP/utilization is “omitted for brevity”; `convert_log_to_time_intervals` is not called, and the comment is not a real alternative calculation. No compact numeric tables are printed.

**What remains.** Headings match the template. A2–A3, A5–A8, and the wait/service/sojourn definitions are conceptually right. A few fully observed cells are correct. That is not enough: the answer systematically replaces missing starts with typical service lengths, reports utilization that cannot be true given its own busy minutes, misstates the end-of-window queues, and ships non-reproducible code. Under a strict reading this is a formatted outline, not a defensible reconstruction.
