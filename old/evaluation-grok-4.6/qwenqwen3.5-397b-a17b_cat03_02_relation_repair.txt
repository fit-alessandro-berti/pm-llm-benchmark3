**5.8**

The write-up follows the required eight-section scaffold and gets the main missing-edge *candidates* roughly right (ev08/PK-8, ev10/DEL-Y, INV-44/ORD-B, PAY-2/INV-44, ev11 left open). It does not invent events or objects, and it is aware that ev11 should not be uniquely assigned. That is the ceiling. Under a strict reading of the task constraints, the reasoning, calibration, and alternative-bundle design have several material defects.

**Domain rules are restated incorrectly.** Identifier match is labeled a hard invariant (“definitive link unless timestamp-impossible”). The prompt says matching identifiers are informative, not decisive. Arithmetic equality is then treated as an `accept` at 0.95 for `INV-44 bills ORD-B` and `ev12–ORD-B`, despite the explicit instruction that invoice/payment arithmetic is evidence, not proof, and despite the instruction not to claim that matching proves those relations. Calling that a “strong default” in §1 and then promoting it to `accept` in §2 is inconsistent with the evidence model the author claims to use.

**Bundle B is not a coherent alternative interpretation.** A and B share ev08, ev10, INV-44–ORD-B, and PAY-2–INV-44. The only difference is that B attaches ev11 to both deliveries to “close the lifecycle.” That is the move the prompt forbids: repairing in order to complete object lifecycles. The prompt already supplies better rival readings—unapplied cash for PAY-2, fees/taxes so 230 need not cover ORD-B, bulk dispatch, portal confirmation that stays order-only—and none of them is developed as a bundle. P10’s `reject` at 0.40 is also miscalibrated: 0.40 is unresolved, not rejected.

**The lifecycle audit contains a factual error and an internal contradiction.** Package gross weights (PK-7 = 3.1, PK-8 = 2.9) are treated as if they were item weights that might be added into PK-8. Items have no weights in the data; the parenthetical “Wait” is left uncorrected. PK-8 packing two items is one-to-many, not many-to-many. Bundle A leaves ev11–DEL-* unresolved, then §5 asserts that DEL-X is confirmed “via ORD-A (transitive).” That is exactly the circular confirmation the audit claims to avoid.

**Calibration and conservatism are uneven.** `N-421?` is marked uncertain in the log, yet both bundles add ev10–DEL-Y at 0.85. PAY-2 is forced onto INV-44 in both bundles even though rule 7 allows unapplied cash and a conservative repair should be willing to leave it open. E2O qualifiers are flattened to the empty label `relates`, which hides role structure the rest of the analysis pretends to care about. The invented “already sealed package” anti-repair rule has no support in the data.

**Downstream and validation sections are only partly on spec.** §6 names the right artifact types but stays generic (centrality, “stronger edges”) instead of citing concrete OC-DFG/OTG fragments that appear or disappear. The flattening-duplication warning in §8 is a real effect of the author’s own `accept`ed ev12–ORD-B repair, so it is mislabeled as an “aggressive” risk rather than a consequence of Bundle A. The PM4Py block copies tables and avoids `ocel_o2o_enrichment` as proof, but it never implements the required duplicate checks, and the OCEL constructor is positional rather than clearly marked as a named, version-specific call.

**Net:** competent candidate-spotting and a usable conservative JSON core, undermined by a broken evidence hierarchy, a non-alternative “alternative,” a weight/lifecycle error, and over-commitment on the financial edges the prompt told the author not to treat as proven. Not a collapse, but well short of a nearly flawless abductive repair.
