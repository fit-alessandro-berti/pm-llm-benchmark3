**4.1**

The write-up has the right high-level stance (do not copy Site A; statutory originals and IT locks block naive parallelization; `conditional go` on a digital/startable-document subset). Under a strict reading of the spec, that is not enough. Several hard requirements are missed, the causal story is repeatedly imprecise, and the quantitative and modeling sections are weak.

**Format and coverage.** Headings and most table schemas match. Section 2 is about 175 words (under the 180–280 band). Section 3 omits high-complexity share, identity/document service times, and every co-intervention (API upgrade, +2 document reviewers, marketing mix shift, huddles, timestamp artifact, no negative control). The prompt required those rows and forbade treating the 9-hour median drop as an identified effect; “process acceleration” still treats it as such.

**Causal / structural reasoning.** The answer never uses the Site B DFG. The natural eligible set is already visible there (`Identity Check -> Document Check` = 1,160/3,400 ≈ 34%; `Originals Receipt -> Document Check` = 2,108). Instead it uses a crude `100% − 62% = 38%` digital share. Legal review is placed as if it delays first Assessment; in both sites it sits after Assessment and bites via capacity steal and the supplement loop (`360` supplements, `345` back to Document Check). Shared structure is thinner than it should be: both sites already have an AND-join into Assessment. Site-specific mechanisms (role split 4/7/3/2, one-owner lock, 18 h originals delay, 40% legal) are named but not wired into a coherent transport DAG. Required nodes are mixed with extra nodes (`document_channel_constraints`, `operational_efficiency`, …) while important edges are missing (`originals_required`/`IT_case_lock`/`role_specialization` ↛ `true_overlap`; `rework` ↛ `appeal`; huddles/API not into outcomes).

**Scenarios and numbers.** No scenario has median better and p90 worse, which was mandatory. There is almost no mapping from Site A hours to Site B constraints. A serious bound would note that check service times at A barely moved (3.5→3.1, 7.2→6.5), waiting before Assessment did (18→12), mix got easier (48%→40% complex), and FTE rose 18→20. At B, when originals are required the 18 h receipt delay dominates `min(3.4, 8.1)` of possible overlap; 3 assessors and 2 legal reviewers are the plausible next bottlenecks. Ranges are round percentages with no decomposition and overconfident cells (IT-lock “95”).

**Intervention and pilot.** The “modified” design mostly restates Site B as-is: Register already fans out to Identity and Originals Receipt (2,050 and 1,860). The forbidden move is creating Document Check for every case at Register. The useful variant is: create Document Check with Identity only when originals are not legally required or are already in hand; keep the statutory gate otherwise; add WIP, logging, and legal/supplement handling. The POWL sketch marks mandatory AND-joins as “Conditional,” allows both `Assessment → {Approve, Reject}` and `Legal Review → {Approve, Reject}` without an XOR, omits Notify, and cannot represent a supplement loop as a back-edge in a strict partial order.

The pilot aliases treatment with stratum (parallel = digital, sequential = originals), so it cannot estimate a treatment effect inside a stratum or separate mechanism failure from case-mix. Case-level assignment with 4/7/3/2 shared specialists is high interference; “no system lock changes” is not an interference control. Four weeks cannot see 90-day appeals. Power is a bare “200 cases,” not a detectable-effect argument. Fidelity metrics (actual overlap, lock workarounds, back-dated events) are missing.

**PM4Py (large fail).** Site A trees are raw strings, not `pm4py.parse_process_tree(...)`. Operators are quoted as activity names (`'X (Approve, Reject)'`, `'+ (Identity Check, Document Check)'`). `convert_to_petri_net` is called on those strings. `POWLNode` / `POWLEdge` / `POWLModel` are not the PM4Py POWL API (`Transition`, `StrictPartialOrder`, `OperatorPOWL`, `Operator.LOOP`/`XOR`). A cyclic edge on a strict partial order is invalid; the supplement loop needed a LOOP operator. Soundness checks are in `try/except`. Comments on what the model cannot encode are the only solid part of the block.

**Recommendation and assumptions.** `conditional go` and “do not copy the all-cases Document Check spawn” are correct. What should transfer (overlap only when both checks are startable; timestamp discipline; WIP) vs what should not (A’s 9 h, cross-trained pooling, no legal loop, API/huddle/FTE bundle) is only partly stated. Assumption 3 again mis-orders legal review vs Assessment; 2 and 7 duplicate the lock; DFG-informed assumptions are absent.

**Net.** Directionally competent management memo; not a near-flawless transportability analysis. Hard spec misses, unused event-graph evidence, a legally impossible task left conceptually muddy, a non-identified 9-hour effect still leaking into the numbers, a pilot that cannot identify the effect, and non-faithful PM4Py keep this well below a high score.
