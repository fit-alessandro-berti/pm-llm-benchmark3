**6.4**

The write-up hits the required nine-section skeleton, stays conservative on e05/e14, does not treat ingestion as business time, keeps original timestamps, and is honest that a total order is an artifact. Event/E2O arithmetic (17/44, 15/39, 14/37, 14/36, unguarded 10/28) is correct, and the `have_common_object=True` argument for e13/e14 is the right one.

It is not close to flawless.

**Implementation is the largest failure.** `get_dataframe` / `set_dataframe` is not how PM4Py OCEL objects work; a PM4Py-aware solution would operate on `events` and especially `relations`. `merge_verified_pair` never merges attributes, so it contradicts the stated WMS-location / Billing-attribute policy and only unions a string of object ids. After a drop, E2O rows for the discarded eid are never updated or deleted, so the promised referential-integrity check is incomplete. `validate` will fail on its own output: `source_event_ids` is seeded with `e03`/`e04` (eids), then asserted to be a subset of raw `source_event_key` values (`W-77`, `S-901`, …). `assign_order_key` is not a real topological sort; seeding ranks from DataFrame index can bake in arbitrary order. The code runs unguarded `ocel_merge_duplicates`, while §5/§7 sell a “guarded” 14-event library merge that is never implemented. The evidence-based scenario is prose-only.

**Smaller analytical nits.** e11 ≺ e12 is not a given fact and is still compiled into the constraint list. “Scanner device” is not in the payload. Canonical e11 vs e16 is under-justified beyond a source-priority convention. §2 does not actually give alternate rows for the ambiguous pairs (e06/e07, e08/e09), only a conservative line. DFG edge names are garbled (`PickPick`, `Capture PaymentPayment`).

Structure, provenance schema, failure modes, and sensitivity edges are good enough that this is clearly above a mid-pack dump of PM4Py calls. The implementation gaps and the policy/code mismatches are enough, under a strict reading, to keep it well short of 8+.
