**Material weaknesses**

CHG-509 is the weakest call. D-QM-02 requires the principal to be absent. Asha’s recorded leave ends 2026-08-14; 15 August is only a weekend, with no formal unavailability record. RR3 is conjunctive: a delegation is valid *only if* the absence condition holds. Crediting Saturday as absence and classifying **VALID_SUBSTITUTION** (Medium–High) is too firm. The residual caveat does not cure an unevidenced trigger. **INDETERMINATE**, with unauthorized as the contingent if unavailability cannot be shown, is the stricter and better primary.

CHG-505’s primary **VALID_SUBSTITUTION** sits uneasily with RR4. The emergency exception needs *both* a valid IC/delegate approval *and* a timely QM/delegate retrospective review. The review fails on scope, sick leave, and lateness. A secondary UNAUTHORIZED label is allowed and the tension is disclosed, but presenting the case as a valid substitution overstates how much of RR4 actually held. D-QM-01 is also worded as “QM *approval*” for production-equipment controls; the answer never asks whether that instrument covers `Retrospective_QM_Review` at all.

**Smaller issues**

The pandas authority check would print `authority_ok=True` for CHG-506 (instrument in force) while the narrative correctly treats the act as unauthorized because Ben was on certified sick leave, denies the click, and the IP is an office workstation. Sick-leave/denial is left as a comment, so the executable flags and the case table can disagree.

Network output is fragile: `discover_handover_of_work_network` / `discover_working_together_network` often return NetworkX graphs, which have no useful `.items()`. The `hasattr(..., "items")` guard can print nothing, so the required network evidence may not actually appear.

D-IC-03 is “CI-44 only”; the log only says `cooling_system`. Treating CHG-505 as CI-44 is reasonable and flagged as missing evidence, but it remains an assumption that the approval-leg validity depends on.

**Net**

The piece is well above a competent pass: event-time roles, alias SoD, PDF-vs-migration effective time, cooling vs production scope, and the sick-leave identity problem are all handled at a high level. The score is held down by one overconfident delegation call (CHG-509), a soft primary on an incomplete RR4 exception (CHG-505), and a code/narrative gap plus weak network rendering. Those are more than nits under a strict reading, but they do not collapse the analysis.
