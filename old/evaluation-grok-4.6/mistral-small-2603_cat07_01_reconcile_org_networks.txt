**Serious validity problems.**

1. **Case-count contradiction.** “High case count percentile (91%): She touches most claims, but only 22% of all claims” is internally false. Percentile 0.91 means she is high *among resources*, not that she touches most cases. This is a basic misread of the table.

2. **Elapsed time is misattributed to Mira.** Bot_Rules → Mira at 3.5 min is time *until she is reached*. Mira → Omar/Priya at 42–51 min is much more plausibly specialist wait than “she introduces delays.” Treating 3.5–51 min as her delay is a handover-semantics error.

3. **Centrality is treated as a percentage.** 0.82 out-degree centrality is repeatedly written as “82% out-degree,” and the rec even alerts if centrality “exceeds 90%.” That is not what the normalized SNA numbers are.

4. **Working-together is misunderstood.** PM4Py working-together is case-level co-occurrence, not shared duration or a time window. Proposing “longer time windows” and “long-duration activities” to recover collaboration shows the metric was not understood. The real reconciliation is sharper than “ephemeral”: a broker who appears, assigns, and leaves generates many directed transfers and A–B–A loops without joining the same case community as team pairs (Alice–Carla 27%).

Ordinary low-value claims were unaffected; the risk section still talks as if she is a global critical path who could “paralyze” exception handling.
