## Material analytical flaws

**1. QD at 11:21 is reasoned backwards.** D05 started at 11:21 finishes at 11:51, *before* the 12:00 meeting. FIFO, priority (P2 tie), and SPT all already pick D03. The table’s skill-column gloss — “leaves D05 for after the meeting” — and operating-model point 4 (“avoid starting D05 before D-F’s meeting because it cannot be completed non-preemptively”) treat a later infeasibility (11:36+30=12:06) as if it applied earlier. A lookahead scheduler that wanted D05 done before the meeting would have *started D05 at 11:21*. That is a concrete decision-reconstruction error, not a wording quibble.

**2. QA’s primary label overweights one episode.** The distinctive fact is A03 at 08:20. The rest of the day is FIFO; A04 and A08 show priority is not the regime; A05 is started at 09:05 with a visible 15-minute estimate that forces A06 past 09:15. That is FIFO with a soft, late-tolerant appointment override, not appointment scheduling with FIFO fallback. Calling appointment the primary discipline, at confidence 82, inverts the dominant rule and the exception. The diagnosis table even admits A03 is the only uniquely diagnostic appointment.

**3. QC is packaged more tightly than the evidence allows.** C02→C03→C04 is SEPT among *some* of the opening jobs, not of the initial backlog (C01 is in that backlog and is last). Rolling SEPT fails immediately at 10:32 (C06). Priority/EDF fail at 10:10–10:32 and again at 11:40. The secondary bundle — gating, aging, SLA escalation, anti-starvation — can rationalize any leftover residual. Confidence 80 is too high for a story that needs a new mechanism at almost every later decision. Worse, SLA escalation is left standing after C07: if endangered due dates promote work, C07 should have beaten C01 at 11:40. The text shows that counterfactual and then still lists deadline escalation as a hybrid component.

**4. “Scarce-skill protection” is inconsistent with the same log.** D-F *does* take pure electrical work (D03) and then refuses the next pure electrical job (D10). Mechanical staying on D-M is at least as well explained by sticky per-resource FIFO, which the answer elsewhere treats as a hypothesis. Presenting scarce-skill protection as a secondary operating ingredient overstates what the assignments identify.

## What does *not* fail

Format is compliant: seven required headings, diagnosis table with the specified columns, ≥3 decision times per queue, support/contradiction by job ID, ≥2 cross-queue counterfactuals, ≥4 exceptions, prioritized data plan, synthesis with most/least identifiable and most dangerous claim. The writeup consistently separates observation, assumption, and hypothesis and does not treat every FIFO violation as priority. Scan-delay, non-preemption, and skill filters are used as bounds, not as unlimited escape hatches.
