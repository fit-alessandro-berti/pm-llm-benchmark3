**8.2**

The final write-up hits the required structure and the four judgment axes. Timeline T0–T4 is tied to operating state, DFG variants, metrics, and conformance. Patterns A–L are classified with keep/modify/retire logic. Resilience is not equated with throughput; C and G are treated as control loss; K/L are treated as necessary recovery work that is not real-time compliance. The recovery model is concrete (triggers, triage, product/customer hard stops, evidence, backlog scoring, operational vs compliance close). Indicators and the management paragraph are decision-usable.

It is not near-flawless. The H cell is broken English (“Low-risk ambient orders  be acceptable”). C and G are given hybrid “retire unless / retire-replace” labels instead of a clean keep–modify–retire call. Hospital fill of 91% is over-attributed to the fast lane (1,000 of 5,600 cases). Several DFG residuals (~200 cases on temporary release, reservation reconcile, and audit remediation) are ignored. Invented operating numbers (4h/12h snapshot TTL, 70% pharmacy floor, 40/25/15/10/10 backlog weights) are stated as if they were part of the case. “~2.5 days” for T2 and 15-day T4 throughput are asserted without flagging business-vs-calendar ambiguity. Those are small but real defects in precision and evidence hygiene.
