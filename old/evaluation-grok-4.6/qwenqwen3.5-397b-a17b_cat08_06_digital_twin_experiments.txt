**5.4**

The write-up is complete in outline and respects several hard constraints (no `pm4py.read_csv`, objections treated as a proxy, applicant delay separated from municipal service, non-stationary arrivals named, cross-case batching at least mentioned). It is not close to a high score under a strict reading. There are factual PM4Py errors, missing required analyses, and several design choices that do not match the process they claim to twin.

**PM4Py and the skeleton (largest hard fails).**  
The prompt required variants, a DFG, a **performance DFG**, **case arrival average**, and service/waiting columns if timestamps allow. The skeleton computes none of the last three in any real form. `pm4py.get_performance_summary` is not a standard PM4Py API; the conventional call is along the lines of `discover_performance_dfg` / `get_performance_dfg`. `pm4py.discover_dfg` in current PM4Py typically returns `(dfg, start_activities, end_activities)`, so feeding that object into a second invented function is not coherent. `get_variant_statistics` is at best a stale path, not the current public helper.  

Worse, the process-tree claim is wrong. The given string (`->`, `+`, `X`, `*`, `tau`) is essentially PM4Py `parse_process_tree` syntax. Saying PM4Py “expects PNML or a tuple structure” and refusing to parse is an accuracy error, not a careful limitation note. The dummy frame also ignores the supplied excerpt (no `org:resource`, `case_type`, `district`, `architect_id`, start/complete distinction). Calendars in code always return `True`. Arrival generation, routing, batching, and experiment execution are empty `pass` stubs. Modular layout is fine; faithfulness to the stated PM4Py contract is not.

**Scope and state are too thin for the process they describe.**  
Calling this a digital twin while specifying a one-shot offline DES is a conceptual downgrade: no rolling recalibration, no assimilation of new logs, no shared “world” of demand/resources against which D1–D10 are switched. State is mostly “a queue per activity.” Missing first-class state includes related-case sets (same architect/district), pending neighborhood objections that hit **multiple** cases, inspection-bundle membership, remaining calendar-eligible work, legal blackout flags, and planner specialization (two planners Simple-only; Monday portal clerk; env 40% reserved). Neighborhood multi-case interference is in the problem statement and is barely modeled.

**Parameter estimation is directionally right and operationally incomplete.**  
Using the median/p90 table because starts are missing is the correct instinct. There is still no plan to (i) deconvolve wait vs service with calendars rather than treating the table as ground truth, (ii) estimate fire/environment routing when the area threshold and sensitivity flags are incomplete, (iii) identify a public-notice **mixture** (minimum 14d vs objection tail) without fabricating observed objection events, (iv) recover architect burst structure as a marked/clustered point process rather than a plain NHPP, or (v) estimate district travel and batching effects from the log. “Markovian rework” contradicts unstructured, stage-specific rework (planning, inspection, legal, appeal).

**Experiments are present; decision language overclaims.**  
E0–E10 plus three stresses meets the letter of the matrix. Levels are vague (D1 “applicant effort high/low”), extra combined policies beyond D10 are absent, and stresses do not hit the stated shocks (Monday 2.2×, council weeks, D3/D5 travel). “Within 10% of optimal” violates the instruction not to talk as if an uncalibrated simulation yields optimality. SLA “statutory deadlines” are invented. Fairness is districts only; architects are required. Inspection-skip risk (D2) and applicant effort (D1/D7) never become decision metrics.

**Uncertainty treatment does not match the KPIs or the tails.**  
A 3-month warm-up is too short for variants with median 140d and p90 260d; early complex cases will still be censored or biased. Thirty replications and CLT intervals on the **mean** do not underwrite median/p90/SLA decisions on heavy tails and an 8% complex / 7% rare mix. CRN is named, then undercut by “distinct seed per replication” with no shared streams across policies. Sensitivity is one-at-a-time ±20% with no propagation of estimation error. Heavy tails are acknowledged only via CRN.

**Validation is a checklist, not a design.**  
Replay of end times with stochastic service and missing starts is not identified as a weak test. Out-of-sample last 3 months is the wrong holdout under seasonal demand. There are no quantitative acceptance bands (by variant, district, resource, calendar week). Dependency and calendar behavior—the parts the flattened log underrepresents—are not validation targets.

Net: a readable DES experiment memo that hits the section template and avoids the worst forbidden shortcuts, with enough API invention, missing required computations, short warm-up, and optimality talk that it is clearly not a near-flawless design.
