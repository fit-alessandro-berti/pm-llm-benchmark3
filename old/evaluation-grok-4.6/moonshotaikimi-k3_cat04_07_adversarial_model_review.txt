**8.6**

The submission is a strong, board-ready review: it uses the required 11-heading skeleton, refuses to collapse metrics into a score, reasons from concrete traces, separates exploration from enforcement, and offers a third model that is actually safer than S and more maintainable than C. It is not nearly flawless.

**What is solid.** Probe judgments for S and C are correct under standard process-tree semantics (B2/B5/B6 are the right C false rejects; B3/B4 are the right S false accepts). The 48-transition recount and the irreproducible “33” duplicate-label figure are genuine skepticism, not decoration. Pathologies are ranked by decision impact rather than listed. The POWL backbone encodes P2 and P4 instead of tau-optionalizing mandatory work. Validation is a program, not invented numbers. The dissent is a real alternative (C-as-quarantine-now) with falsifiable reversal conditions, and it sits in the 150–250 word window.

**Real flaws.**

1. **Overstated “declared-valid” space.** The 7/20 = 35% attack treats every check-order × approval × SR/ER placement as policy-valid. P3 makes SR attribute-conditioned; several of those 20 traces are only valid given sourcing (or are waste/ambiguous). The fair claim is that C covers 7 of 20 *control-flow* combinations consistent with P1–P5 if SR is treated as optional. B2 and B5 still convict C; the headline percentage is inflated.

2. **Declarative spec and checker disagree.** D2 requires a Correct Dossier *after* the (k−1)-th occurrence of the same check. `posthoc_policy_check` only tests `last_cd == -1`, i.e. whether any CD ever appeared. `CC, CD, CC, CC` violates D2 and is not flagged. The procedural net blocks that trace; the “double-check” does not implement the rule it cites.

3. **Small overclaims and sloppiness.** S’s 0.992 is called hard to reconcile with corrections under standard costs; a few short correction cases are enough to leave fitness that high. The S section is labeled “admissions (5)” and then lists seven traces. Alignment-cost parameter names are guessed (`PARAM_MODEL_MOVE_COST_FUNCTION`); the caveat does not make them correct. Stratified `train_test_split` on tuple labels will die on singleton strata; that is not handled.

4. **Third model still smuggles unresolved policy.** Empty recheck after CD and SR-before-checks are accepted by construction. Both are flagged, so this is a design choice, not a silent flower-model cheat — but they are still unapproved behaviors in an artifact sold as safer than S. A governance reader can fairly ask whether “explicit uncertainty” is just an unstaffed queue (the dissent’s best point is also a hole in the main recommendation).

**Net.** Format, probe matrix, and the third model’s *mandatory* checks are right, which is why this stays high. The quantitative case against C, the P7 checker, and a few unforced wording errors keep it well short of a 9+.
