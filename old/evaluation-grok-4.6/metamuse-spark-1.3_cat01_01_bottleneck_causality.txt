## Material flaws

**Causal graph (Section 2) is not a valid DAG and contains temporal/direction errors.**
- `review_service → rework` and `rework → review_service` are a cycle. A static adjacency list presented as a causal graph cannot contain both.
- `quality_outcome → case_completed` is temporally impossible: the 30-day outcome is post-close.
- `shift → arrival_load` is backwards. Arrival load is an intake condition; night/high-load co-occurrence is a common-cause / routing pattern, not a shift effect.
- `unobserved_reviewer_skill → resource_group` treats group skill mix as a confounder of assignment. The prompt’s fact (“Orion has more certified reviewers”) makes skill a *component of the treatment bundle / mediator*, not a cause of routing. That error is repeated in the role table.

These are not cosmetic. Section 2 is supposed to encode total, direct, and mediated structure. Wrong arrows change which adjustments are legitimate.

**Section 1 overclaims relative to its own caveats.** “Mainly confounded” at 70 is too sharp once High-Nova and Low-Orion are n=2, overlap is broken, and only completed cases exist. “Not identifiable from the supplied evidence” is the more disciplined headline; 70 hides that. The exec summary also asserts that the rework reversal “holds” in every stratum. High rework is 50% vs 50%, not a reversal. The same overstatement reappears in the final recommendation (“cycle, wait and rework”).

**Total / direct / mediated distinction is implied later, not stated in the required exec assessment.** The task asked for that distinction at minimum. Section 1 is almost entirely crude-vs-mix. Direct vs mediated Orion paths (queue, compliance, service, rework) are left to later tables.

**Arithmetic slip.** Compliance-not-required mean is written as `203.1/17 ≈ 11.89h`. That quotient is ≈11.95h. Minor, but the section advertises auditability.

**Section 7 does not reliably do the required DFG comparison.** `parse_event_log_string` is fed a `case_id,activity` dump rather than trace strings (`Register,Classify,...`). That is not the API the prompt specified. The `try/except` then swallows failure, so group DFGs may never print. Variant counts in Pandas do not substitute for “directly-follows relations” via PM4Py.
