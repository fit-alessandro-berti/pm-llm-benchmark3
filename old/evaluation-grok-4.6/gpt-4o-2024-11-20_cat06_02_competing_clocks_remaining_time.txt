**4.2**

The write-up has the required skeleton and avoids the worst failure modes (single point estimate, summing stage means, treating anomalies as independent with no comment). That is not enough. The actual clock arithmetic, queueing, and calendar logic — the things this task is designed to test — are repeatedly wrong, internally inconsistent, or simply not done.

**Chemistry is mishandled in a way that breaks the forecast.** Four samples are waiting, both analysts are busy, remaining Friday capacity is 2.5 h, and service is triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5). B-417 cannot start at 16:00 and finish by 17:30 on any defensible reading. Even under risk-priority the urgent sample still goes first; the first plausible start is after an analyst frees (~16:06–17:30), and P(service ≤ remaining Friday time) is tiny. Under FIFO (~55%) B-417 does not start Friday at all. The smooth-path chemistry line ignores the queue the problem went out of its way to specify. Queue discipline is listed in the ledger and then not used.

**Incubation timing is factually wrong.** Friday 14:00 + 46–54 h is Sunday 12:00–20:00, not Sunday 20:00–22:00. That is a basic elapsed-time error, not a modeling choice.

**The join is never treated as a real max.** The problem states that the pre-join duration is governed by the slower branch. After a correct chemistry analysis, FIFO often makes chemistry the Monday-afternoon clock and micro the Monday-morning clock; risk-priority makes them compete. The answer assumes chemistry is done Friday, so micro “dominates” by construction. That is exactly the chemistry-spillover analysis the quality criteria require, and it is missing.

**Weekend microbiology authorization is over-weighted and mis-reasoned.** Primary incubation is not ready until Sunday. QA has no Sunday hours. Authorization therefore cannot pull the smooth-path join before Monday 08:00; without it, readout is Monday 08:30–09:30. The smooth-path effect is about 0.5–1.5 h, not “accelerating every downstream stage.” Ranking this as uncertainty #1 and as the most forecast-changing next event fails the calendar test the scenario is built around. Authorization *does* matter on the contamination-confirmation path (confirmation incubation can start Sunday rather than Monday). That distinction is not made.

**QA numbers do not add up.** Five batches are already queued; Friday evening plus Saturday supply ~8.5 reviewer-hours against a 1.5 h median and ~3 expected arrivals. A Monday residual around 2–8 is arguable; 8–10 is an unexplained high draw. Then: join ~08:30–09:00, enter queue at 10:00, 8–10 ahead, two Monday reviewers, emerge ~14:00. That wait is far too short for the queue they themselves assumed. Earliest terminal Monday 08:20–08:50 is incompatible with QA opening at 08:00, any residual queue, median 1.5 h review, and 0.25 h release — and it contradicts the summary’s earliest of Monday ~12:00.

**Required output is incomplete or sloppy.** Forecast ranges omit remaining hours (explicitly required). “Wednesday 22 Sept” is the wrong date (Wednesday is 23 Sept). Scenario 4 is not decomposed into the mandated stages (“addifies latency”). Contamination is said to “double” incubation; the data are +20–30 h after indication, not ×2. Rerun timing assumes a Friday first-pass result that almost certainly does not exist; the realistic rerun is first pass Monday, then priority rerun, so earliest Monday 08:00–11:00 is not plausible. Rejection at 15–25% is invented; the log gives investigation and query rates, not a critical-defect rate near that level.

**Uncertainty framing is only half-right.** Dependence from the temperature-excursion flag is correctly flagged and is the one genuinely strong modeling choice. Overlapping scenario bands are correctly not forced to 100%. But the driver ranking is wrong (weekend auth over chemistry discipline, join-time queue, and investigation type), the sensor gap is observation uncertainty as well as dependence, and triangular chemistry service / QA P80 are never used.

What remains is a correctly headed envelope forecast with one good qualitative idea (correlated tails) sitting on broken branch clocks. Under the stated rubric that is not a high score.
