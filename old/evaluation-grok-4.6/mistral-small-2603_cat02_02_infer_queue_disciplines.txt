**3.2**

The write-up has the required skeleton and a few locally correct observations (B01 is interrupted for B02; D05 is pinned to D-F; A1’s calibration exists). That is not enough. The required unit of analysis is the eligible waiting set at each start or preemption. Those sets are repeatedly wrong, so the discipline comparisons, “supporting” episodes, and confidence numbers are not evidence-based.

**Section 1 — wrong primary labels, unjustified confidence**

QA is labeled preemptive priority at 90. QA has no interrupted segment. A04 (priority 1) arrives at 08:13 while A01 (priority 3) runs to 08:20; A01 is not preempted. After A03, the resource takes A02 (priority 3), not A04. After A04, it takes A05, not waiting A08 (priority 1). Service order is A01, A03, A02, A04, A05, A06, A07, A08 — FIFO with one appointment insertion. Calling this preemptive priority inverts the data.

QC is labeled deadline-aware priority. At 10:10 the eligible backlog is C01–C04 (C1 frees at 10:10). The choice is C02 (est 8), not C03 (priority 2, deadline 12:30) and not C01 (oldest / earlier deadline than C02). Then C03 (est 14), then C04 (est 22). That is SPT on the opening backlog, which the C01 note also flags. C05 starts at 10:54, after its 10:50 deadline, so it cannot be read as on-time EDD.

QD is “skill-based routing with priority.” Skill eligibility is a constraint, not the sequencing rule. D-M serves D01 → D04 → D08 → D09: D08 (priority 1) does not jump D04. D-X serves D06 (appointment) → D07 → D10: D10 (priority 1) does not jump D07. Priority as a secondary component is poorly identified.

QB is the only partly defensible row (p1 preemption of B01 for B02). Even there, “FIFO secondary” is underspecified, and B01’s first stop is listed later as a contradiction of preemptive priority — it is the strongest support.

**Section 2 — eligible sets are mostly invalid**

This is the decisive failure.

- QA 08:58 lists A01, A02, A03 as waiting; all three are already complete (08:20, 08:52, 08:30). True waiting set: A04, A05, A06, A07, A08.  
- QA 09:05 and 09:20 repeat the same error. The informative times — 08:20 (A03 vs A02 vs A04) and 08:32 (A02 vs A04 vs A05/A06) — are omitted.  
- QB 09:48 omits B04 (09:35) and B05 (09:46). QB 10:14 omits B06 (10:05). The 09:31 resume of B01 over B03 and the 10:28 choice of B04 (p3) over B06 (p2) are missing.  
- QC 10:10 lists only C01, C02; C03 and C04 have already arrived. QC 10:18 omits C04 and C05. QC 10:54 still lists C04 (just finished) and omits C06 and C07. Deadline prediction “C01 earliest” at 10:10 is false (C03 is 12:30).  
- QD mixes resources and uses finish times as decision times (D04 “11:35” is the end of D04; the start is 11:25). D01/D02 are not waiting for D-F at 11:21. At 12:20, D07/D08/D09 are not waiting on D-F.

Skill columns that say “all jobs” do not apply eligibility. Several “priority” cells are written as ties (“A01/A05”) that a priority rule would not produce.

**Section 3 — support and contradiction inverted**

A08 served last is listed as support for priority; it is a contradiction. A06 starting late does not contradict preemptive priority (there is no preemption to test). B01’s interruption is treated as a contradiction of the stated QB policy; the first stop is labeled “urgent arrival” and matches B02. C01 last is consistent with SPT, not a puzzle for a deadline-priority story unless the opening C02/C03/C04 choices are faced. D08 is called a contradiction because it was “selected over older jobs”; on D-M it is selected after older D04, which is FIFO.

Almost no apparent violation is tested against scan delay (±2 min), remaining time after preemption, or resource calendars.

**Section 4 — tests do not discriminate**

QA at 08:58 cannot be Priority vs Appointment: A03 finished at 08:30. The proposed discriminator (whether A03’s appointment was honored) is already in the log and is not a new observable.

QC at 10:54: “deadline would pick C01” is false. Tightest deadline in system is C05 (10:50, already missed), then C07 (12:00), then C01 (13:00). The test also ignores SPT (C06, est 6), which is the serious competitor.

Both tests stay inside already-observed outcomes instead of naming a missing field that would split observationally equivalent rules.

**Sections 5–7 — generic, mis-ranked, mis-synthesized**

Exceptions are boilerplate. Delayed scans are ranked high but never used to rebuild a decision set. The live alternatives that would change the diagnosis — QA as non-preemptive FIFO + appointment hold; QC as SEPT on the 10:10 backlog with a late C05 insertion; QB as p1-only preemption plus resume-interrupted-job; D-X idling 11:20–11:30 for an appointment — are absent.

Collection plan repeats the prompt’s field list by queue instead of targeting the actual ambiguities (remaining time on B01 at 09:31/09:48; whether B05 was visible at 09:48; appointment slack for 08:20 and 11:30; whether C06 was in the 10:32 eligibility set; D-F idle 11:36–12:00 vs meeting hold for D05).

Final synthesis calls QD most identifiable (fair only for the skill constraint) and QC least identifiable (QC’s opening SPT pattern is one of the cleaner signatures). Appointment is called the most dangerous inference; the dangerous inference on this log is priority/preemptive priority, which the answer itself over-fits from FIFO interruptions.

**Net:** format compliance and a handful of correct local facts cannot offset systematically false waiting sets, inverted evidence, two incorrect primaries, and 75–90 confidence on those calls. Under a strict reading of the quality bar, this is a structured but analytically unreliable solution.
