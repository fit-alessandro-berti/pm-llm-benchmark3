The final write-up hits the required scaffold and is often conceptually on target, but it is not close to flawless. Several quantitative claims in the required comparison matrix are simply wrong, one distortion count contradicts the log, and a few definitions are sloppy. Package flattening is said to yield “4 extra event copies”; e16 and e24 each add one extra copy, so 2 extra, 4 total copies of those two events. **Conceptual and coverage gaps.** Divergence is defined as split-across-subprocesses rather than the usual OC-PM sense (one object, many events of the same activity). Item-level contrast (I11’s private D1 path vs I12 on consolidated D2) is missed. The code outline never computes the duplication ratios the prose relies on, so it does not actually operationalize the warning that visuals will not settle the semantics.

**Net.** This is a coherent, on-spec essay with real engagement with O1–O4, P3/P4, D2, V2, and M2/M3. It is also careless where the task most demanded precision. Wrong cardinalities in the required matrix, a contradictory Pack count, and a nonstandard divergence story keep it well below the “nearly flawless” band.
