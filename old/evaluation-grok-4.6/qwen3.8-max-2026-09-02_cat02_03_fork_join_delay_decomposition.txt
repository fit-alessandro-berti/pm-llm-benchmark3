**8.6**

The final document is a strong, internally consistent fork-join analysis. Definitions, case arithmetic, critical-path ranking, and the required format are almost all correct. Remaining deductions are for incomplete operationalization of a few required influence metrics, unquantified downstream masking, and fragile PM4Py/pandas details—not for invented data or broken identities.

**What is solid**

- Max-based `join_ready`, the elapsed identity \(T=U+E+D+S\), and the double-counting argument (\(Q_i+P_i+W_i=E\) so \(\sum_i=3E\); F07 sync sum 77 vs \(E=75\)) are correct and explicit.
- All 12 case rows, the four worked examples, critical-branch counts (B 8 / A 2 / C 2), and the three counterfactual aggregates (15, 92.5, 176 / 73) check against the log.
- Influence is not confused with mean service: A is 50 min with no queue; B is slower in service but later in queue+service and in the tail (F07/F12). Zero-benefit cases are identified (A-20% in 10/12; B-queue-50% in F01/F04/F08/F11).
- Uncertainty is labeled (potential `join_ready` saving vs realized flow time; CF3 upper bound vs same-resource conflict subset).
- The single Python block matches the spec: in-memory table, pandas decomposition, deterministic A≻B≻C ties, three CFs, interval log, `format_dataframe` + `get_service_time`, and comments that DFG performance does not recover sync wait.

**Issues (why not 9+)**

1. **“Contribution to `join_ready`” is only a proxy.** Mean fork-join elapsed *when critical* (B 65, A 50, C 56) still embeds the other branches’ floor (A is always 50). The natural marginal metric is excess over the second-latest completion (about 5+10 for A, ~15 on average for B, 7 and 5 for C). That quantity is implicit in CF1/CF2 but never tabulated, so the comparison table overstates B’s unique minutes.

2. **Downstream masking is discussed, not measured.** F11 (CF1) and F09 (CF2) are good examples, but there is no F1/F2 replay, so it is unclear how much of 15 or 92.5 minutes would survive the observed final sequence (e.g. F05’s 10 min CF2 cut is fully absorbable if F03 still holds F1 until 10:20). Bounds \([0, \Delta J]\) are honest; a quantified replay was in reach of the same timestamps.

3. **CF3 is not an added-capacity simulation.** Removing all \(D_c\) (176) and crediting same-resource overlap (73) are useful bounds, not a third-server assignment. Residual waits while a named resource looks idle are noted, which is the right caveat.

4. **Code nits.** `datetime.astype('int64')` is version-fragile; `get_service_time` is assumed to be seconds and divided by 60 with no check against the pandas service times. Logic of the custom decomposition does not depend on that.

5. **A vs C ranking is slightly under-specified.** Calling A “slightly ahead” leans on the A-20% CF without a parallel C-service or C-queue experiment in the comparison table. The near-tie itself is reported honestly.

No material arithmetic error, no double-counted total flow time, and no false claim that speeding a noncritical branch shortens the case. The gaps are completeness and metric choice, not contradiction.
