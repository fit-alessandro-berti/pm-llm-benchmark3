The answer ranks parallel mechanisms and never writes the obvious multi-hop chain: J3 holds FIX-A (09:00–11:40) → J4 cannot start on CNC-2 until 11:45 → J4 packs only at 19:10 → S1 holds already-packed J1 from 13:30 to 19:25.
