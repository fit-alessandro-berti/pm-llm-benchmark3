## Factual errors that break the argument

These are not rounding issues. They are wrong timelines, wrong priorities, and an invented downtime.

- **QA “contradiction” is false.** A04 (08:58–09:05) runs *before* A05 (09:05–09:20) *before* A06. The write-up claims A04 started “after A05.” A04 before A06 is exactly FIFO (arrivals 08:13 vs 08:29), so the cited clash does not challenge FIFO at all.
- **QB “strongest support” is false.** B05 arrives 09:46; B03 runs 09:48–10:00. B03 did not complete before B05 arrived. Completing before a later arrival would not identify non-preemption anyway.
- **QD “contradiction” is backwards.** D08 starts 11:35, D09 starts 11:43. D08 did not start later than D09. The real priority clash on D-M is **D04 (P3) before D08 (P1)** at 11:25.
- **QC invents a resource gap 11:18–11:40.** C1 is busy continuously 10:10–12:34 (`10:54–11:34`, `11:34–11:40`, `11:40–12:16`). That gap is used as an identifiability problem and is not in the data.
- **C06 is P3, not P1.** The diagnosis table says C05 was chosen “despite C06 being shorter and P1.”
- **D08 was not “available 11:00.”** It arrives 11:16.
- **`est222`** is a sloppy corruption of C04’s `est_min=22`.

Once supporting/contradicting episodes are wrong, the confidence numbers are rhetoric, not evidential calibration.

## Discipline labels are only partly earned

**QA.** FIFO + appointment override is the right primary. It is not well *shown*. After A03’s 08:20 jump, the rest is ordinary FIFO under the calibration hold (A02 → A04 → A05 → A06 late → A07 → A08). P1 never jumps (A04 waits behind A02; A08 waits behind A05/A06/A07). SPT never jumps (A07 does not beat A05). Calling the main identifiability problem “priority and appointments are conflated” is outdated once 09:05 is reconstructed. “Resource is always available when idle” is false (08:52–08:58 calibration).

**QB.** “Non-preemptive priority” is not tenable as the primary rule.

- 09:16: B02 (P1) *does* preempt B01 — preemptive, with a recorded “urgent arrival” reason.
- 09:31: B01 *resumes* ahead of B03 (P2) — resume preference, not priority.
- 09:48: B03 is chosen while B05 (P1) and B04 (est 8) are in the log; this matches neither priority nor SPT. Scan delay (≤2 min on a 09:46 arrival) is a live hypothesis and is unused.
- 10:14: B01 resumes again ahead of B06 (P2).
- 10:28: **B04 (P3) before B06 (P2)** is a clean priority falsification and a FIFO confirmation.
- B07’s 10:30 appointment is explicitly not honored.

A defensible reading is FIFO with an urgent P1 interrupt, resume of the interrupted job, and a documented dispatcher exception — not non-preemptive priority. Confidence 70 is too high. SPT/priority “observational equivalence (B03 < B05 duration)” is the wrong contrast: if both were waiting at 09:48, priority picks B05, SPT picks B04, and the log picks B03.

**QC.** SEPT as a primary is plausible (C02 then C03 are shortest among jobs already present). The C04-before-C06 puzzle is real, but the write-up does not use the obvious frozen-set hypothesis: C06 arrives 10:25 *during* C03, so a policy that only re-sorts jobs that were waiting when the previous service *started* predicts C04 at 10:32. C05 at 10:54 is a priority/deadline override (the note says the deadline is already at risk), which *contradicts* “priority as tiebreaker” — it is an override when SPT would have taken C06. C01 before C07 at 11:40 then *again* violates SEPT, priority, and EDD. That second violation is ignored. Deadlines are almost unused despite being the distinctive QC field.

**QD.** Skill-based routing is correct as a constraint, not yet as a dispatch rule. Trivial support (D02 on D-F at 11:01, the only firmware job and the only qualified idle tech) does not identify routing. The informative episodes are missing: D-X idle 11:20–11:30 holding for D06’s appointment rather than D08 (P1) or D07 (only D-X can finish mechanical+electrical); D-F idle 11:36–12:00 with D10 (P1, electrical, est 12) waiting, consistent with reserving the only fully qualified tech for D05 ahead of a 12:00 meeting that cannot fit a 30-minute job.
