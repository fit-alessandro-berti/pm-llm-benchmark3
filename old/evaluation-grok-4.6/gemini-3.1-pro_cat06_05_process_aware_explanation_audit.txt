6.3

The audit is structurally complete and avoids the two failure modes named in the quality criteria (treating full-trace SHAP as an early explanation, and reading every correlate as a cause). Almost every required item is in the leakage table, the obvious complete-trace leaks are correctly marked critical, `queue_length_at_t0` and `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` are preserved, Cases X/Y do not narrate future appeals or decisions, and Case Z is invalidated. That is a competent answer, not a near-flawless one. Under a strict reading the gaps below are material.

**Inaccuracies.** `initial_reserve` has no lineage note, yet Section 1 calls it a valid prefix field to “retain as is,” Section 3 lists it as a defensible signal, and Case Y uses it as a primary credible driver. That is an invented availability claim. `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` is described as leakage because “the target event occurs after t0”; the prediction target is the 10-day breach, not the fraud event, so the justification is simply wrong even if the removal decision is mostly right. Queue length is called “actionable for organizational load balancing.” Predictive importance is not an operational warrant; load may be confounded with mix, staffing, and seasonality. Several “retain as is” verdicts overstate cleanliness: the ±5-minute queue join is an as-of risk, and `calendar_week` is not a low-severity “legitimate proxy” once policy change and a holiday window are aligned with those weeks.

**The named quality criterion is only half-met.** `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is tagged “future leakage” and `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is tagged “process impossibility,” which is the right split of labels, but the audit never states the process point: the appeal pattern is a legal reconsideration loop after Partial/Deny, merely unknowable at `t0`, whereas Closed→Assessment is not a legal order and is a timestamp-tie artifact. All five sequence patterns are then collapsed to “remove from the prefix explainer.” That erases the required distinction among impossible, not-yet-enabled, and merely future-possible. After this `t0`, fraud investigation, document cycles, and first settlement are process-legal continuations; they are inadmissible as *features now*, not inherently invalid patterns. `num_document_requests_total` is correctly a complete-trace leak, but the team’s “early warning” story is also process-inconsistent at this cutoff: after Coverage Check, a prefix count is typically zero, so even a repaired feature is usually uninformative.

**Missed lineage subtleties.** `final_handler_team` is not a pure future field: it equals the `t0` team on ~65% of claims and would be repairable as team-at-`t0` if an as-of stamp existed. The mixed legitimate/leaked signal is ignored. Repair paths for prefix document counts, point-in-time risk scores, and same-day snapshots are mentioned only generically. `claimant_channel` is treated as known at `t0` without a lineage caveat (more plausible than reserve, still unverified).

**Section 2 is a checklist, not a suite.** Prefix masking, declarative terminal-event rules, sequence indexes, and “DFG frequency ≠ a norm” are the right ideas, but there are almost no concrete assertions (for example: every training row at this `t0` must have `appeal_seen_anywhere = 0` and no Close/Settlement/Appeal events; same-timestamp pairs without an ingestion index fail; complete-trace aggregations are rejected; repeated Settlement is allowed only after Appeal). Alignment, footprint, and DFG limitations are named more than used.

**Standards are implied, not stated.** Predictive validity, causal interpretation, actionability, and fairness are mixed into class blurbs instead of separated. That is why queue length slips into “actionable,” complexity is called “inherent” in the Case X narrative and then walked back, and region/channel get a fairness flag without a clear rule for when a valid predictor still cannot be an intervention or a claimant-level reason.

**Section 4/5 are serviceable.** “recompute” / “recompute” / “invalidate” is the right status set, leaked drivers are stripped from the narratives, and the recommendation list hits every requested heading. Residual issues: Case Z’s `main_ambiguities` omits `last_resource_role`; calibration is asserted to “drastically” cut AUC as if that were measured; feature-store and historical timestamps are fused into one item with little on event-time vs processing-time or training/serving skew.

Net: a strong student audit with the right skeleton and the right instinct on leakage versus congestion, but too many unsupported retain/actionability claims and too little process-semantic precision to score higher under the stated rubric.
