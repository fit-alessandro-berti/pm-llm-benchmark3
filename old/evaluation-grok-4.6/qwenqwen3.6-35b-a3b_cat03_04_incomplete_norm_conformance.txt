**6.0**

The write-up follows the required eight-section skeleton and gets the main open-world point right: it does not treat `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice`, `Manual Compliance Review -> Customer`, `Package object_codeath Delivery`, or `Reopen Order -> Close Order` as violations merely because they are absent from the norm. Forbidden items N3, T6, and E10 are correctly prioritized, typical invoice exceptions (write-off, open invoices) are handled well, and the uncertainty statement is mostly coherent.

It is not close to flawless. Several errors are exactly the kind of category-mixing the prompt is testing.

**Serious factual mix-up.** The two missing `Pack Package -> Dispatch Package` cases (N4, 198/200) are repeatedly explained with the carrier-reference note. That note applies to two `Dispatch Package` events that lack a **Delivery** binding (E6 / the ET–OT layer), not to an OC-DFG packing-to-dispatch gap. Section 3 states the conflation outright. That is a failure to keep behavior, binding, and abstraction separate.

**Related model-family error.** OTG T2 is satisfied at weight 200. ET–OT E6 is 198. These are different relations. Calling 200 vs 198 “extraction variance” or treating T2 as gapped is wrong.

**E2 under-classified.** `Add Item`–`Item` is 1450 and `Add Item`–`Order` is 1448. E2 is mandatory for every Add Item event, so two events lack a required Order link. Ranking this as a minor/benign tracking issue, then listing the same pair as a satisfied mandatory row (rank 25), is inconsistent.

**E8 is too soft.** The exception applies only to events *explicitly typed* as unapplied cash, and “none are so typed in this snapshot.” One `Record Payment` with Payment but no Invoice is therefore a formal mandatory miss plus a staff explanation, not simply “typical/unspecified.”

**Code sketch fails the stated contract.** `norm_ocdfg` / `norm_otg` / `norm_etot` are given normative structures. The sketch *discovers* them from `ocel` and then checks the same log against that discovered model, which is circular. It also injects mandatory/forbidden flags into `discover_*` parameters, contradicting the requirement that status metadata live *outside* bare graphs. Duplicate tuples and invented diagnostic fields make it look executable without being conceptually valid.

**Smaller issues.** The findings table is bloated with fully conforming edges (E1, E4/E5, T1, …) that are not unusual. Create→Manual Compliance Review (3) is never used as an alternative explanation for the N1 DF gap. Norm-completeness omits the prompt’s own gap: no frequency or tolerance bands. “Halt all dispatch workflows” over-escalates a two-case historical snapshot.

Strong structure and mostly sound forbidden/typical/unregulated splits keep this out of the lower band. The N4/E6 conflation, E2/E8 calibration errors, and broken PM4Py sketch cap it at a middling score under a strict reading.
