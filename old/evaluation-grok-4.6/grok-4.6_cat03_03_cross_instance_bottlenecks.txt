**Substantive analytical flaws.**

1. **FIX-A wait is over-attributed.** C3 leads with 1 h 40 min (10:05 vs 11:45) and 1 h 55 min (09:50 vs 11:45) as the fixture-associated delay. The prompt only documents CNC-2 idle from 10:05–10:47. The 5 min offset 11:40→11:45 is good evidence of a fixture constraint at the *start* instant, and the 42 min idle is good evidence the machine was not binding in that window. Collapsing the whole interval into “the delay plausibly associated with FIX-A” is the single worst quantitative claim.

2. **J3’s fixture cycle is recast as an upstream coupling cause of J3’s own lateness.** J3 *holds* FIX-A 09:00–11:40; that is J3’s processing, not J3 waiting on a shared resource. The coupling effects are (a) J4 blocked and (b) J3 becoming B1’s last arrival because its own CNC work ended late. Narrative A and the rank-1 caveat (“J3 lateness itself has upstream causes”) blur those. Fixture contention explains J4, not J3’s ready time of 11:55.
