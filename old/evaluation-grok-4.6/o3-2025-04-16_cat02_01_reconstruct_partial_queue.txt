The write-up has the required skeleton and a few sound methodological instincts (rounding intervals, resource-precedence constraints for `MISSING` starts, elapsed vs final service for `OPEN`, a time-varying bottleneck story). ## Critical factual errors

**Required case rows are wrong.** P05 and P11 have fully observed S1 starts; S1 wait is 35 min and 75 min. Both rows copy the S2-imputation triple (`20/32.5/45`, `55/65/75`) into `S1_wait`. P16 S1 wait is 100 min (`12:05−10:25`), not 55. P17 is 95 min, not 35. P21 is 100 min, not 35. P22 is waiting at S1 from `12:10`; elapsed wait is 110+ min, not a dash. These are the cases the prompt forbids burying.

**The “unremarkable” summary is false.** Observed S1 waits for P01–P04, P06, P08–P14, P18–P20 are roughly 5, 5, 20, 20, 40, 50, 60, 70, 85, 80, 95, 100, 100, 105 — median about 65 min, not 30. Calling P18–P20 unremarkable is already a stretch; they sit in a 100-minute S1 queue and are still waiting for S2.

**Station counts do not add up.** S1 arrivals in 12–14 are not 0: P22 arrives at 12:10. S2 arrivals in 08–10 are not 0: a case enters the S2 queue when S1 ends, so P01–P06 (and possibly P07 at 10:00) arrive to S2 in that window. That zero is a definition error, not a rounding issue. Interval edges are inconsistent: 14 S1 arrivals in 08–10 implies P14 (`10:00`) is in the first bin, but P14’s S1 completion (`12:00`) is in neither 10–12 (6) nor 12–14 (6). Completions were dropped.

**Busy time and utilization are physically impossible.** Two resources over two hours supply 240 resource-minutes. S1 08–10 busy time 320 and S2 10–12 busy time 330 cannot occur. Reported utilizations (~0.67–0.79 at S1) also fail a direct reading of the log: I1 is essentially occupied from 08:05 through 14:00 and I2 from 08:10 through 13:45. Central S1 utilization is on the order of 0.9+, not two-thirds. The denominator is never defined per subperiod (A7’s 360/345 min is the full window, then reused incoherently).

**Peaks and Little’s Law are unsupported.** S1 queue at 09:30 is about 4–5 waiting (or ~6 station WIP), not 8. Midday S2 queue/WIP is not 9 in any careful count of “entered S2 queue, not yet started / not yet departed.” Little’s-Law check (`L≈5.5`, `λ≈3.5/h`, `W≈1.5 h`) does not match the author’s own 10–12 S2 wait+service (~65 min), names no population (queue vs station vs system), and the claimed 20% break in scenario 3 is asserted, not computed.

## Method and consistency problems

A5 (latest `MISSING` start = end − 5 min) is violated for P15: wait `90/107.5/125` implies a latest start of 12:15 (zero service) while service `5/20/35` implies latest 12:10. Low/central/high service triples are listed in the opposite order from the wait triples that share the same start, so the columns are not a coherent scenario. The 5-minute minimum service is far below every fully observed service (~20–50 min) and is never justified against the data.

Case-level **total sojourn** for completed cases is required and absent. Station sojourn is defined and then not tabulated. Queue length and system WIP are never constructed as functions of time; A2 is stated and unused. Arrival/departure **rates** are mostly raw counts, and the counts are wrong.

Section 5’s qualitative arc (S1 congested while S2 is starved early; congestion moves to S2 after mid-morning, C2 outage aggravates it) is directionally plausible and is the strongest part of the memo. It is undermined by the false S2 arrival count, the false S1 utilization, and an uncounted “eight” S2 cases after 12:15.

Section 6 names three scenarios but does not recompute the station table under them. Stability claims (peak queues, mean service) therefore cannot be checked. Censoring of P15/P17/P21 and residual S2 wait for P16–P20 is acknowledged in flags, then ignored in utilization and Little’s Law.

The block meets the cosmetic requirements (in-memory table, markers kept as strings, `format_dataframe`, a fully-observed interval log, a `convert_log_to_time_intervals` comment) and then fails the analytical ones. Pandas logic covers only central S1 wait/service, skips `OPEN`/`blank` S1 ends (explicitly discarding P21/P22), does not impute S2 `MISSING` starts, does not build queue/WIP paths, and does not implement the three scenarios. `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` is called and never printed; outputs are not labeled as case-level versus station-level except in a caption. `format_dataframe` is not given a start-timestamp key. The printed `head()` fragments cannot reproduce sections 3–6. As executable evidence the code is a stub.

## Format and presentation

The seven headings match the spec. Notation is garbled (`λ`, `μ` dropped). Several cells mix rounding noise, imputation, and censoring without saying which. Precision is unjustified (e.g. 32.5, 0.71) next to impossible busy times.

**Bottom line:** this reads like a competent outline filled with numbers that do not survive contact with the event table. Process-mining definitions (S2 arrival = S1 departure, wait = start − queue entry, utilization ≤ availability, do not drop open cases) are repeatedly broken. A strict grade cannot go higher than the low 3s.
