**6.4**

The answer is well structured, catches the crude-versus-stratified reversal, keeps queueing distinct from service, and recommends against the 50% reassignment. It is not close to flawless. Under a strict reading, several required distinctions are thin, the DAG has real direction errors, one mechanism claim is contradicted by the stratified data, and the required process-mining code is not a valid PM4Py parse.

**Causal verdict.** “Mainly confounded” is defensible, but confidence 70 is too high. High-complexity Nova is *n*=2, low-complexity Orion is *n*=2, Medium-Day Orion and High-Day Orion have zero support, and the sample is completed cases only. Stratification is treated more carefully later than in §1, where “strongly suggests” routing is the primary driver still reads as identification. The required total / direct / mediated split is not executed in the executive assessment: a “direct Orion effect” is mentioned, but there is no explicit estimand for assignment to Orion, for Orion holding mediators fixed, or for paths through wait, compliance, rework, or shift. Two appeals (1/14 vs 1/16) are not evidence against a harmful effect.

**Rework.** Aggregate rework is higher for Orion (4/14 vs 3/16), but within complexity it is not: High 50% vs 50%, Medium 16.7% vs 25%, Low 0% vs 10%. Calling “stricter use of rework” a plausible Orion mechanism repeats the same confounding the cycle-time analysis correctly rejects. §4’s “especially Orion high” is misleading for the same reason.

**DAG.** Several arrows are wrong or missing. `review_service → rework` inverts the measurement: `review_service_h` already includes repeat review, so rework adds service time. `review_service → cycle_time` is absent, so service affects duration only via rework, which is false for the 23 cases with `rework_count=0`. `quality_outcome → case_completed` is temporally implausible (30-day appeal is after Close). `routing_rule → compliance_required` is not in the process description; compliance is a case-policy function, mainly of complexity. `shift → arrival_load` confuses time of arrival with the assigned group’s working hours; `resource_group → shift` is better supported than `routing_rule → shift`. Unobserved skill is drawn as an independent confounder rather than part of the Orion bundle, despite “more certified reviewers.” Missing supported links include `complexity → rework`, `resource_group → rework`, and `unobserved_case_risk → routing_rule` / `resource_group`.

**Roles.** The table hits confounder, mediator, collider, selection, and proxy, but classifies `compliance_required` as a mediator of `resource_group → cycle_time`. That is likely a pre-treatment covariate (or a proxy for risk), not a consequence of which group is assigned. Matching on trace variant, proposed in §5, can block mediation if Request-Info traces are part of the effect.

**Decomposition.** Arithmetic on overall, complexity, night/day, compliance, and wait-versus-service comparisons is largely auditable and correct. The claim that waiting explains “60–80%” of within-stratum gaps is low (the implied shares are closer to 75–95%). “Small fixed overhead” is not small or fixed on rework/high-complexity cases. The code’s complexity-standardized contrast is never brought back into §1 or §4; under the sample mix it would show Orion *shorter* by about 2.5 hours, still not a causal estimate, and still unadjusted for shift, load, and compliance.

**Hypotheses and tests.** The five required families are present and have predicted observations. Rank 2 (harmful Orion) is mostly a restatement of night/load overload and is not cleanly separated from rank 3. A “front-door-like” analysis with compliance as mediator of *group* is not identified: compliance is not a complete intermediary, and backdoors into compliance from risk/complexity remain. Overcontrol risk in matching on traces is not flagged.

**Code.** Crude and stratified means, sample-weighted standardization, variant counts, DFG printing, and `<3` warnings are present. `parse_event_log_string` is called on lines like `C01: Register>Classify>Review>Close`. Default parsing is comma-separated `caseId,activity,activity,...`; this string will not yield the intended traces. Zero-count positivity cells (Medium-Day Orion, High-Day Orion) never enter the warning loop because `groupby.size()` omits them. Group DFGs are printed, not contrasted. No in-code disclaimer that the standardized contrast is descriptive.

**What still works.** The 50% move is correctly treated as a likely backfire if Nova inherits Orion’s mix without Orion’s night/compliance capacity. Phases 2–3 match the spec (restricted randomization on overlap; capacity/compliance without relabeling). Residual uncertainties are concrete and tied to missing fields. No fabricated case facts. The main failure is not ignorance of the reversal; it is overconfidence, several broken causal arrows, a rework story that ignores the stratified table, and a required PM4Py diagnostic that would not parse.
