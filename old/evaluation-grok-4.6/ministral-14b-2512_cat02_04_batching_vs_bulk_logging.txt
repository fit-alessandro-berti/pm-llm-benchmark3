## Signature matrix

Several cells are the wrong signature, so the matrix cannot be used as a decision aid.

- Shared-account work typically produces **high overlap** on one resource ID, not low overlap.
- Exact simultaneous batches do **not** fragment under a tighter tolerance; near-ties and coarsened stamps do.
- Coarsening does not “collapse to a single point” when the data are *already* snapped; a smaller tolerance leaves equals equal.
- Queue accumulation does not generally imply end equality (G8 is the counterexample). G6’s recording lag is ~1 minute, not “high lag.”
- Sequential batching often has *tight* arrivals then chained service (G4, G8), not “spread over time.”
