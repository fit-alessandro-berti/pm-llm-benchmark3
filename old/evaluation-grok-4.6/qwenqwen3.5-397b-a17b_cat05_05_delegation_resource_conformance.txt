The write-up is complete, follows the required headings, and gets several easy cases right (CHG-502 / CHG-508 self-approval after alias merge, CHG-507 Eva with no QM authority, CHG-510 void subdelegation). It also correctly refuses to treat Clara–Ben pairing as proof of collusion. Those are table stakes. The analysis fails on the issues the prompt said would be judged: event-time delegation scope, ambiguous identity, RR4, and the PM4Py checks.

**Delegation scope is mishandled.** D-QM-01 authorizes QM *approval* of *production-equipment* changes, not every QM-flavored act. CHG-505 and CHG-510 are `cooling_system` emergencies; Ben’s `Retrospective_QM_Review` is therefore outside that instrument even when the calendar interval looks open. The answer never applies that limit and treats Ben as a general QM delegate for cooling retro-review. D-IC-03 is limited to Cooling Incident CI-44; two HIGH emergency cooling cases exist the same afternoon (CHG-505, CHG-510). The answer never asks which case is CI-44 and treats Farid’s CHG-505 approval as in-scope by default.

**Identity evidence is over-read, against RR5 and RR6.** CHG-503’s `svc_qms` session is a shared terminal, Diego only badge-entered the room, there is no re-auth, and CCTV is missing. That is not a reliable map to Diego. Classifying the case UNAUTHORIZED at high confidence as an RR2 self-verify is exactly the collapse the prompt forbade. The honest labels are DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM or INDETERMINATE, with Diego-as-verifier as one hypothesis. CHG-506 is similar: Ben denies the act, the IP is an office workstation, sick leave has started. That is unresolved identity, not a proven unauthorized act by Ben. CHG-504 notes leave/VPN but underplays the stated fact that security cannot exclude credential sharing on `u_as`. INDETERMINATE is almost unused, which is a methodological miss on a problem that says there is no canonical reading of ambiguous identity.

**RR4 is incomplete.** Section 2 never defines the counting rule (end of event-day + 2 weekdays vs. 48 business hours vs. calendar days) and never tests emergency-rationale credibility. CHG-505’s review on 14 Aug is late on a standard “two business days after 11 Aug” reading, but the case is flattened to a single UNAUTHORIZED stamp that also buries a possibly valid IC approval. D-QM-02’s “principal must be absent” condition on Saturday 15 Aug, after leave ended 14 Aug, is waved through for CHG-509.

**Classifications are coarse.** CHG-501’s real issue is QE-as-approver under a PDF dated 9 Aug and a QMS row created 12 Aug 09:15. Primary DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM without a VALID_SUBSTITUTION secondary ignores the signed instrument; a later DB insert cannot prove earlier system effectiveness, but it also does not erase the PDF. Several cases need dual labels. CHG-505 should split approval vs. retro-review. Technical conformance and control-objective strength are not consistently separated.

**The code does not do what it claims.** `pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle` retains cases that *satisfy* four-eyes (different resources). Naming that output `*_violations` and inventing `retention_policy='keep_violations'` inverts or breaks the check. `filter_activity_done_different_resources` takes one activity, not a two-activity list. Date-only delegation ends become midnight, so an 14 Aug 16:00 review fails `end >= ts` even when D-QM-01 runs through 23:59. There is no principal-absence predicate, no RR4 business-day logic, and no RR3-vs-RR4 branch. Hard-coding `svc_qms` → Diego and then running SoD automates the judgment the prompt said must stay manual. Networks are called but barely interpreted.

**Smaller faults.** Clara is listed in the matrix as holding an IC delegated role that the same row calls invalid. Executive 50/30/20 splits are fake precision. Weekend HIGH work (CHG-507 Sunday, CHG-509 Saturday) is ignored. Grace verifying CHG-507 with no verifier role is unremarked (allowed if RR2 is pure SoD, but then say so).

Net: a structured, partly correct audit memo that breaks on the hard controls—the exact dimension this task is designed to test. Not a collapse, and not close to a high score.
