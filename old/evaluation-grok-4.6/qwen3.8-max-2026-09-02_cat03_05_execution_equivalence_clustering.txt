**6.8**

The write-up is complete, internally usable, and mostly faithful to the eight traces. It does not treat local IDs as meaningful, does not invent a `Create Order` for X8, and does not pass off activity-label clustering as object-centric. The required eight-section skeleton is present, the assignment table is consistent, and the six mandated pairwise comparisons actually show how two defensible relations can disagree. Those are the strengths. They are not enough for a high score under a strict reading.

**Definitional inconsistency (largest defect).**  
`BS` is described as discarding low-level structure and keeping motifs, multiplicities, external domains, and qualifiers. The implemented signature then hashes the full typed event trace and object lifecycles together with those flags. That makes preferred “business-semantic” equivalence a refinement of typed structural equivalence, not a coarser business quotient. On this dataset the two partitions coincide (only X1=X2 merge). That directly collides with the instruction not to treat the finest partition as best, and with the claim that structural, behavioral, and semantic relations “may produce different answers.” A motif-only key (the flags already in the table) would have been the honest `BS` relation; the code is `TS` plus extras.

**Constraint misses.**  
Prose is well above the 1,700–2,600-word target. Optional `R4` is defined and never used. Compliance monitoring and operational segmentation are both assigned `BS` with little operational distinction.

**Code and PM4Py.**  
Canonicalization of local IDs is the right idea, but `partial_order=True` sorts the entire trace and erases causality rather than building a labeled poset. Hashing `repr` of a dict of flags is a reproducibility footgun. O2O is assumed to arrive as triples; the narrative notes are never given a parsing rule. The PM4Py section hedges (“parameter names may vary,” `NotImplementedError` helpers) instead of stating what the key actually stores. `max_objs` is discussed usefully as a connected-component risk, but as an invented kwarg.

**Smaller semantic gaps.**  
X8’s O2O also contains package replacement; that is not separated from X7’s replacement motif except by the return flag. `SETTLEMENT_PREDICATES` fires on the baseline as well, so it does not earn its place in the signature. First-seen naming is acknowledged as weak under symmetry, but no actual refinement procedure is specified.

**What still works.**  
`AS` grouping of X1/X2/X5/X6/X7 is correct. X3 vs X4 vs X8 are split for the right multiplicity and lifecycle reasons. Borderline write-ups (especially X1–X5/X6/X7 and X4–X8) are the best part of the answer. Flattening comments and the robustness table are specific enough to be reproducible. Recommendations in §8 are directionally right even if two of them collapse.

A nearly flawless answer would have layered three *distinct* quotients (label trace / renamed incidence / motif+qualifier flags), kept `BS` coarser than `TS` by construction, hit the word bound, and described the PM4Py key without stubs. This is a strong student solution with a real equivalence-design error, not a near-perfect one.
