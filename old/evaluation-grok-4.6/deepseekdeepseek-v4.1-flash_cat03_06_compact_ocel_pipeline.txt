**8.0**

The pipeline is complete, internally consistent, and aligned with the required OCEL 2.0 shape. All 17 events, 12 objects, mandatory columns, timezone-aware timestamps, payment amounts on events, merge-based E2O activity/timestamp copying, and the specified O2O facts are present. Validation covers uniqueness, orphans, duplicate E2O rows, and activity/timestamp agreement. Flattening, DFG, log skeleton, OC-DFG, OTG, ET–OT, OC Petri net, and interactions are stored as asked. Interpretation of Order/Package/Delivery/Invoice flattening, shared-event copying, Payment’s one-event traces, and P1/V1/M1/M2/O100/O200 is mostly right.

What keeps it from a high score:

1. **OC-DFG is described like ET–OT / typed incidence.** “Create Order connects to Order” is not a directly-follows fact. An OC-DFG should be stated as per-object-type DFGs (or activity–activity edges annotated by object type). Instance-level claims about P1 and V1 belong in the interactions summary, not in the OC-DFG.

2. **OTG vs O2O vs event co-occurrence is blurred.** The O2O table has no Order–Package or Order–Delivery edge; those appear only if OTG is built from event co-occurrence. That distinction is never made sharp.

3. **Chosen E2O policy fights the provenance rule.** Pack/dispatch/confirm links to Orders are inferable from `contains`/`packs`/`carries`, yet they are stored as observed E2O. Qualifier names help, but source facts and analytical scope are still mixed in the same table.

4. **Order DFG skips the global payment gap.** Wall-clock `Issue Invoice → Record Payment → Confirm Delivery` becomes `Issue Invoice → Confirm Delivery` on Orders. That skipped-event artifact is not called out.

5. **API robustness is incomplete.** Only the OCEL constructor is version-isolated. Unpacking `pm4py.discover_dfg` into three values can fail. Relation `ocel:type` is never checked against `objects`. Log skeletons are computed and then ignored.

6. **Directionality is underspecified in places.** Several O2O lines read as “source object  target object” without a visible arrow, so cardinality tables are slightly harder to audit than they should be.

The construction would still support a serious review; the misses are conceptual and robustness nits, not missing events or broken referential structure.
