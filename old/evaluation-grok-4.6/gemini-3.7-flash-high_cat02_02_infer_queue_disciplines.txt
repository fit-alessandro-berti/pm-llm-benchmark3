5.6

The write-up follows the required skeleton and often reasons from decision sets rather than raw start order, which is the right method. That is not enough. The core task is reconstructing eligible sets and testing competing rules at those moments. Several of those reconstructions are incomplete or internally inconsistent, and a few of the headline diagnoses are stronger than the episodes that supposedly support them.

**Decision-set errors (most serious).**  
QC’s 10:32 choice is omitted from §2 even though it is the cleanest SPT failure: `C06` (est 6) has been waiting since 10:25, yet `C04` (est 22) starts. That episode is more damaging to dynamic SEPT than the 10:54 `C05` override, which can be told as an SLA exception. QB’s 10:14 resumption is also omitted: `B01` (P3) is resumed again while `B06` (P2) is waiting, so the 09:31 “WIP anomaly” is a repeated pattern, not a one-off. QD §2 drops `D03` from D-X’s 11:20 eligible set (electrical, waiting, D-F still on `D02`) and treats D-F’s 11:36 set as `{D05}` only, ignoring `D10` (electrical, P1, arrived 11:25). D-F already executed electrical work (`D03`), so that omission is not a skill constraint. The 11:21 D-F choice (`D03` over `D05`) is never reconstructed; starting `D05` at 11:21 (est 30) would have finished at 11:51, before the 12:00 meeting. Unique-skill priority is then invoked for `D07` on D-X after D-F failed to protect its own unique job. That is selective use of the same principle.

**Diagnoses that outrun the evidence.**  
QA as non-preemptive FIFO plus myopic appointment insertion is the best-supported of the four. The 08:32 `A02`≻`A04` and 09:32 `A07`≻`A08` episodes really do kill priority and SPT. But the table’s “strongest contradicting episode” is `A06` starting late — which does not contradict FIFO at all. The FIFO violation is `A03` at 08:20. Calling `A06` a contradiction is a category error unless the primary claim is appointment scheduling.  
QB is labeled preemptive priority at confidence 75. P1 preemption of `B01` by `B02` is real. P2 does not otherwise beat P3 except after an explicit dispatcher reassignment. After that, 10:28 is FIFO (`B04`≻`B06`≻`B07`), and the appointment is missed. Citing `B06` before `B07` as priority support is weak: FIFO predicts the same pair. The scan-delay story for `B05` at 09:48 is confused. A recorded arrival of 09:46 does not place visibility at 09:48; delayed scans make recorded times late relative to physical arrival, or they make the job visible at the recorded scan. Either way, treating 09:46–09:48 as a reason `B05` was unseen needs an explicit assumption, which is not labeled.  
QC as SEPT plus “batch commitment” plus P1 escalation tries to hold three mechanisms at once. `C02` at 10:10 is good SPT evidence against FIFO, priority, and EDF. `C04`≻`C06` and `C01`≻`C07` are not small residuals: the latter violates SPT, priority, EDF, and slack simultaneously, and inserting `C05`/`C06` ahead of `C01` already breaks a frozen 10:10 batch.  
QD as skill-based routing at confidence 90 overstates a constraint as a discipline. Every job is skill-feasible; the identifying rule among feasible jobs is closer to FIFO-within-resource plus one honored appointment. D-X’s 11:20–11:30 idle is not uniquely lookahead: `D08` (est 8) or `D04` (est 10) would still release D-X by 11:30. The write-up notes the alternative in the identifiability cell, then still uses that idle as the strongest supporting episode and assigns the highest confidence in the table.

**Internal inconsistency.**  
§2 states that at 10:10 QC rejects `C03`’s earlier deadline. §4 then presents 10:10 as an ambiguous SPT-vs-EDF episode that needs a hypothetical `C_test`. The data already discriminate those two rules. The actually ambiguous QC contrasts are dynamic SPT vs frozen batch (10:32) and anti-starvation/`C01` vs every standard index (11:40).

**Uncertainty, exceptions, and data asks.**  
Confidence numbers track rhetoric more than residual ambiguity (QD 90, QB 75). §5’s ranked exceptions are in the right spirit, but “noisy/strategic `est_min`” is offered without checking realized durations: every segment in these tables matches `est_min` exactly, so that hypothesis has no support here. Delayed-scan ranking is fair; the B05 application of it is not. §6 is usable but still generic: it never asks for separate physical queue-entry vs scan time, or for appointment tolerance, both of which are exactly the ambiguities these traces create. Skill-based predictions are missing from the QA/QB/QC tables even though the prompt required that comparison (vacuous is still a prediction and should be stated).

**Synthesis.**  
“Priority is the most dangerous label to read off these logs” is the one cross-queue claim that is clearly earned. “QD most identifiable / QC least” is not: QA’s FIFO-versus-priority contrasts are sharper than QD’s skill matrix plus an under-determined idle. The piece looks like a strong process-mining memo until the eligible sets and the contradicting episodes are checked against the same tables it cites. Those checks do not hold.
