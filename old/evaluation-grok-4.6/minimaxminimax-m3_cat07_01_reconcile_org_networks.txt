**What it does well.** It keeps competing readings open and never treats centrality as intrinsically good or bad. The handover vs working-together contrast is the right organizational puzzle, and the broker/router reading is process-mining valid: high directed transition volume plus short events plus low co-presence is a classic dispatcher signature, not a co-worker signature. Hypothesis D claims case-count percentile **0.91** is “closer to team-lead territory than to adjuster territory.” That is also false: Alice/Bruno are **0.74/0.71**, Carla/Diego **0.80/0.78**, vendors **0.85/0.93**.
