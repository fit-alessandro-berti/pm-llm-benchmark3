The final write-up is a complete, correctly structured seven-section report whose **arithmetic is almost entirely right**. Upstream, queues, services, `join_ready = max(end_i)`, sync waits, downstream queue, final service, totals, critical-branch labels (A: F01/F11; B: 8 cases; C: F04/F08), and the three counterfactual deltas all check out. That is not enough for a high score under a strict bar. Several required or load-bearing pieces are damaged or incomplete.

**Formulas (Section 1).** The required identities are conceptually correct, but the delivered formulas are missing the subtraction operators (and the summation symbols). Section 1 was supposed to *give* the decomposition. A reader can reconstruct the intent; they should not have to.

**Downstream masking — the largest analytical miss.** The task explicitly asked whether final-station congestion masks branch improvements, and the data include `final_resource`. The answer asserts that a shorter `join_ready` with “unchanged downstream queue” shortens total flow time by the same amount. That is not the same as an unchanged *schedule*, and the two assumptions are stated as if they were one. Occupancy contradicts several claimed savings:

- CF1 / F11: `join_ready` 11:30 → 11:20, but F1 is occupied by F09 until 11:50. The 10-minute claim is fully masked.
- CF2 / F05: `join_ready` 10:10 → 10:00, but F1 is occupied by F03 until 10:20. The 10-minute claim is fully masked.

Several other cases (F06, F07, F01) show 8–15 minutes of wait *after* the assigned F resource is already free, which is exactly the queue-vs-policy ambiguity Section 7 later admits. That evidence is not used in Section 4. The counterfactuals therefore overstate direct flow-time benefit and under-use supplied resource information.

**Tail and influence.** Critical frequency, mean queue+service, and mean sync are the right objects, and the B ≫ A > C ranking is defensible. “Tail contribution to `join_ready`” is only qualitative. Calling B “very close” to `join_ready` in every non-critical case overstates F04 (17 min slack) and F08 (20 min).

**Python.** The script meets the structural checklist (in-memory table, pandas decomposition, deterministic tie handling, three CFs, interval log, `format_dataframe`, `get_service_time` / `discover_performance_dfg`, a comment that DFG does not recover sync wait). Stage time is defined but never tabulated. `join_ready` is stored as minutes-from-midnight beside duration columns, which is easy to misread. Assumptions are only partly labeled.
