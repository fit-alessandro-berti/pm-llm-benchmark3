**7.7**

The submission is a strong, structurally complete compliance analysis: it uses the required headings and table schemas, keeps an issue-specific hierarchy rather than a fake total order, applies rules at act-time, refuses to let later approvals or ERP clocks cure earlier defects, and generally separates facts, assumptions, and recommendations. It is not nearly flawless. Several classifications rest on under-argued moves, and the stated method is not applied evenly.

**What is done well.** Source authority is lane-limited rather than ranked by recency or wording. ECD-2 is correctly confined to timing, sequencing, and the supplier register; A1 and A3 are treated as surviving C4/C5 silence; the CFO email is prospective, capped, and unable to touch SoD or Compliance. Temporal traps are handled carefully: P-501’s commitment is kept pre-window, P-506’s verbal extension is rejected, P-505’s supplier acknowledgment at 17:59:31 is preferred to the 18:06 ERP stamp. A6 is applied as mitigation-not-cure. P-502 versus P-507 is principled (same emergency lane; the difference is pre- versus post-commitment clearance). P-503’s alternative is unusually honest: even the best contrary premises cannot waive the missing >€100k clearance. Section 7 actually resolves the live ambiguities (silence ≠ waiver, SoD in joint authorization, written expiry, anti-normalization).

**Material analytical problems.**

P-501 is the weakest primary call. NONCOMPLIANT is built by stacking two contestable readings: (i) B2 is strictly pre-commitment although the SOP is silent on timing, and (ii) SHIFT-1’s *commitment* is treated as an unauthorized Plant Manager substitution. B4’s substitution clause is about standing in for an *absent* Plant Manager, most naturally for Plant_Approve. Plant_Approve later occurs; the committer need not be the Plant Manager. If those two moves fail, the case is COMPLIANT or INDETERMINATE under a genuine 4-hour B4 emergency, timely form, and unused A2/A3. Section 5 states the alternative, but the primary row presents the stretch as a finding. Medium confidence does not fully offset that.

P-505 and P-507 are in tension with the paper’s own burden rule (A-5). A2 requires *both* an independent budget owner and a Finance Controller before commitment. The text repeatedly says IC members are not charter budget owners and that D3 gives the CFO Finance-routing authority only. Yet both cases are JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION while the budget-owner element is “unresolved.” That is exception-friendly. Under the announced claimant-burden rule, unproven coverage of a mandatory A2 element should have produced INDETERMINATE or a qualified NONCOMPLIANT, not a justified exception. P-506 is harsher on the same missing element, which is defensible only because D1 is off; the A2 residue is still treated more gently when ECD-2 is in play.

B6 is announced as a persistent local recording duty (A5 / hierarchy table) and then mostly abandoned. It is scored for P-501, noted as missing for P-502, and ignored for P-505, P-507, and P-508. Either B6 applies to ECD-2 purchases or it does not; the catalogue does not get to pick.

**Smaller defects that still matter.**

- P-504 treats “unapproved supplier” as A3’s “new supplier” without flagging the gap. Amount is below every other threshold; the A3 finding depends on that equation.
- ON-44 is a 14 July recovery rule (ERP stamp ≤ 12 minutes late). P-505’s “clock may have been nine minutes fast” is a different, case-specific claim on 18 July. Calling the latter “ON-44 discipline” blurs an evidential holding the rest of the paper is careful about.
- Finance_Approve (SOP event) is silently identified with GPIP’s Finance Controller. If those are different roles, several A2 “satisfied” cells move.
- P-505 has no Create_PO at all. That is logged as missing evidence, not analyzed as a possible never-created PO versus a logging hole.
- P-508’s C3 four-hour record is never evidenced and is omitted from that row’s missing-evidence cell.
- Hierarchy column “What it can override” is used both for sources a clause defeats and for sources that cannot defeat it (A1 “overriding” the CFO email).
- The pseudo-logic is conceptually right but operators dropped out (`amount50k`, missing ∧/≥), so the formalization is harder to audit than the prose.

**Classification map, briefly.** P-503, P-504, and P-506 are solid. P-502 and P-508 are defensible under the stated premises, with P-508 correctly marked as the closest case. P-505/P-507 are directionally plausible as emergency-lane successes but over-certified relative to A-5 and A2. P-501 is the one primary that may simply be wrong.

The argument is well above a routine paraphrase of the sources. It is not tight enough on one case, on the burden of proof, or on complete rule application to sit in the high 8s or 9s under a strict reading of the rubric.
