**Internal inconsistencies.** Section 2 puts the smooth-path earliest terminal at Monday 13:00 (69.5 h). **Calendar logic errors.** A priority rerun is triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) h. After a Monday ~15:30 FIFO primary, a short rerun can finish before 18:00; the write-up treats ~1.8–3.0 h as if it were a lower bound and sends essentially all FIFO reruns to Tuesday. Contamination earliest (Tuesday 14:30) silently assumes no weekend readout. The P90 lower edge at Thursday 06:30 is not a plausible terminal clock time: QA is closed, and a 3-day laboratory investigation started Monday afternoon does not resolve before Thursday midday at the earliest.

**QA queue at the future join.** The prompt requires an explicit treatment of an uncertain, overdispersed QA queue at a future join. The answer computes a neat weekend balance (8.5 reviewer-hours, ~3 arrivals, ~3–4 left Monday) and then inserts a 0.5–2.0 h wait. With 3–4 batches present at 08:00, two reviewers, median 1.5 h / P80 3.5 h, and λ = 0.35/h, a Monday-morning join wait is more like 2–5 h in expectation and materially wider under the stated overdispersion. Saturday staffing is used only as a point drain, not as a source of Monday backlog variance. This driver is then omitted from the top-five list.

**Dominant-clock claim is too sharp.** On the clean path, microbiology without weekend authorization is typically ready Monday 08:30–09:30; risk-priority chemistry is often 09:30–11:30 and FIFO chemistry 14:30–17:00. Chemistry is frequently the later *lab* input, but the *remaining-time* level is set by the closed weekend, and under risk-priority the branches are co-dominant. The table admits co-dominance; the decomposition then says chemistry “is the dominant clock.” Chemistry-ahead service-time variance (four triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) jobs) is collapsed to ~11.3 analyst-hours, so FIFO start-time uncertainty is understated.

**What remains defensible.** No pre-Monday terminal without an explicit overtime exception; Saturday QA depletes the queue but cannot review B-417; incubation 46–54 h after Friday 14:00 is placed on Sunday; electronic release is treated as a short continuous tail; path bands are allowed to overlap; the severe tail is correctly owned by laboratory investigations and compounded anomalies. The ballpark — nothing useful before Monday, center of mass Monday evening to Tuesday, P80 into midweek, week-plus tail — is in the right region even though several supporting numbers are not.

The piece reads like a strong draft that was not reconciled against itself. Under a strict standard that penalizes even small quantitative and logical defects, that caps the grade well below the “nearly flawless” band.
