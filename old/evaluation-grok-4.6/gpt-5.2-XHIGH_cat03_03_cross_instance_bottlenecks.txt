## Quantitative errors (Section 2)

**B2 is attributed to the wrong binding constraint.** J4 is ready at 13:10, RACK-7 is unavailable until 14:35, J5 is ready at 14:40, and B2 starts at 14:50. The last AND-join input is J5, not the rack. If the rack had been free at 13:10, B2 still could not start until 14:40. The 1h25m “rack unavailability” slice is therefore not the delay that determines start time; at most 5 minutes of the post-13:10 wait is rack-then-partner sequencing, and even that is nested inside waiting for J5. The writeup treats rack cleaning as the main B2 story. That is the same AND-join logic it applied correctly to B1, applied incorrectly here. J5’s 14:40 readiness is also the obvious place to connect L7’s 13:50 release; that link is postponed and never quantified.

**The FIX-A delay uses a window the data do not support.** CNC-2 is idle only from 10:05 to 10:47. Computing `11:40 − 10:05 = 1h35m` as the fixture-gated portion assumes the machine remained available until tool release. After 10:47 the machine’s state is unknown and, on a literal reading, no longer idle. The *observed* overlap of machine idleness and FIX-A hold is 42 minutes, not 1h35m. The 5-minute gap from release (11:40) to process start (11:45) is good evidence of tool gating at the moment of start; stretching that backwards across an undocumented 10:47–11:40 interval is not. The 15 minutes from J4’s arrival (09:50) to the start of recorded idle (10:05) is also omitted.

**B1’s oven is treated as demonstrably non-binding in the morning.** Overall 44% utilization and the later 12:55–14:50 idle window do not establish that OVEN-1 was free between 08:10 and 12:05. For B1 the data show co-readiness, not oven idleness. Saying the controlling constraint is “co-readiness + shared rack” adds rack without a B1 rack-unavailability timestamp. RACK-7 is evidenced as a constraint for B2’s cleaning window, not for B1’s 3h40–3h55 waits.

**J6 is only half-decomposed.** `17:25 − 16:05 = 1h20m` and `17:25 − 16:35 = 50m` are correct arithmetic. The remaining 30 minutes are wait behind J5 on TEST-1 (16:05–16:35) plus a 5-minute residual after recalibration. Ranking machine capacity near the bottom is defensible in aggregate, but this interval is exactly a short tester queue plus a tool-eligibility gap. Collapsing that into “CAL-3, 50m” overstates the tool’s share of J6’s wait.

The required calculations are present and the counterfactual labels are present. Presence is not correctness.

Rank 1 fuses **batch synchronization** and **RACK-7 gating**. The prompt listed those as distinct mechanisms. On B1, synchronization on J3 (11:55) is the only well-supported driver; on B2, J5 readiness dominates the rack. Combining them produces a cleaner “#1” than the evidence warrants.

`E_CAL3_Recal@16:35-17:20 -relates_to-> {CAL-3, TEST-1}` is marked `[obs]`. The source says TEST-1 is idle during recalibration and that Final Test requires CAL-3. It does not say the recalibration event is related to TEST-1. Idle-while-tool-busy is an inference, and the answer’s own observation/inference split is violated here.

L7’s hold (`10:20`–`13:50`, 3h30m) is comparable in duration to B1 waits and is the best explanation of why J5 is late into B2 while J2 is not. The piece reads like a strong outline that was not checked against its own AND-join rule. Completeness, caution about causality, and the flattening discussion keep it well above a failing answer. Strictly, they do not justify a high score.
