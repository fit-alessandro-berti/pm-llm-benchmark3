**Real errors and material omissions**

The most concrete factual slip is in §6: “`Record Payment`’s related invoices from 1 to 2 (B).” There is only one invoice. Unique Invoice support stays 1; what changes is incidence (ev13 already links `INV-44`; Bundle B adds a second `Record Payment` on the same invoice). A clear abductive miss: payments are not just 100+130=230. `PAY-1=100` matches `ORD-B`’s declared value and `PAY-2=130` matches `ORD-A`. That pairing is independent of the sum, cuts toward joint billing *and* toward earmarked installments, and should have been in the evidence model. Using only the total under-uses the amount cue the prompt asked to combine.

Bundle A is labeled “conservative / minimum-change” while **accepting** the mixed-order package (`ev08→PK-8`, `PK-8 packs IT-B1`) at 0.70. That is a large structural claim, not a minimum edit. Rule 3 licenses an item-only pack row; it is not a second independent cue that the missing package is `PK-8`. That sits poorly next to the combination rule (“accept ≥0.7 needs two independent cue types”). Staging P3/P4 as provisional would have matched both the label and the stated evidence policy.

Derived O2O edges are correctly flagged as non-independent, then barely discounted (0.85→0.80). That is a token cap, not the policy they announce.
