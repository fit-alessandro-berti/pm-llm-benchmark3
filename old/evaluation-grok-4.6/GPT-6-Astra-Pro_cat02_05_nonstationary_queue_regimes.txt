**What holds up.** Opening-hours intensity is defined as counts over \(12n_R\) hours, including outage time, and completion intensity is correctly *not* treated as a service rate. **Inaccuracies and over-precision.** Regime intensities are reported to two decimals for \(n=1\) or \(n=2\) (OUT, TRANS, PWD, PWE, HWE). That is more precision than the data support; ranges or integer daily rates would have been more honest. Directional rise versus LWD is fair; treating 44 as a usable occupancy level is not.
