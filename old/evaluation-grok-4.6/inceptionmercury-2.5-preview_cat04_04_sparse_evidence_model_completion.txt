## Fatal modeling errors

**Process trees use `AND` as if it were sequence.** Both `model_conservative` and `model_permissive` wrap start, body, `Release Production`, and `Archive Case` in `AND(...)`. That makes `Register Order` concurrent with approval/release/archive and lets `Approve Configuration` overlap the checks. That violates D1 and D2, which the ledger itself treats as high-confidence. The intended operator is sequence. This is not a notation quibble; it changes the language.

**Conservative formal expression contradicts the conservative code.**  
`(Register (XOR(Eng, Credit)) …)` is a choice of *one* check, not both in either order. The simplified tree uses `XOR` of the two sequences. Those are different languages. The first tree dump is also nested incoherently and is labeled “simplified for demonstration,” so there is no single conservative model.

**Conservative drops observed behavior and then claims exact fit.** Expedite appears in 2/30 traces; V5 (`Credit Check` → `Supplier Confirmation` → `Engineering Review`) appears twice. Conservative excludes Expedite and, in the simplified tree, forces both checks *before* supplier. Section 7 still says fitness is “High (exact match).” Conservative may refuse *unobserved* generalizations; it may not silently drop observed variants and then claim replay.

**Balanced POWL makes optional activities mandatory.** Edges `credit → expedite → approve` and `credit/eng → supplier → approve` with no `OperatorPOWL(XOR, [act, SilentTransition()])` force Expedite and Supplier Confirmation on every case. That fails 28 traces on Expedite and 7 on skipped supplier. Comments say supplier “can happen after either check”; both `credit → supplier` and `eng → supplier` require *both* (the opposite of D5). `StrictPartialOrder()` is constructed without `nodes=[...]`, so the object is not a valid POWL in the usual PM4Py API.

**Permissive tree admits clearly forbidden behavior.** Expedite sits in a sibling `AND` branch, so it can complete after approve/release/archive (D6/S6). Checks sit in another `AND` sibling of `XOR(seq(SC, Approve), Approve)`, so approval need not wait for both checks (D3). `XOR(seq(CC,ER), seq(ER,CC), PARALLEL(CC,ER))` is redundant: the parallel already contains both interleavings. Duplicate labels are used and never discussed, despite the prompt.

**PM4Py surface is not the API in the prompt.** `parse_process_tree` expects `->`, `+`, `X`, `tau` (e.g. `->('Register Order', …)`), not `(SEQUENCE|AND|XOR|PARALLEL|Empty)`. Validation never unpacks `(net, im, fm)`, never calls soundness/fitness/precision/generalization/simplicity, never builds a 30-case log (six unique strings, no multiplicities, seventh variant missing), and replaces the positive/negative suite with `print`. The one correct remark—sampled playout does not prove inclusion—does not salvage a stub.

## What would be needed for a high score

A high score requires three *distinct, executable* languages that match their own bias statements: a conservative tree (or XOR enumeration) that covers all 30 traces and little else, including both Expedite variants and V5; a POWL that uses silent XOR for optional SC/Expedite, a partial order that does not treat two-way DFG edges as proof of overlap, and a written rule for “after at least one check” vs “after both”; a permissive model whose extra traces are listed and classified; a 30-case log; real soundness and alignment metrics; and an audit that does not relabel observed variants as unobserved. This answer does not get there.
