**6.5**

The write-up uses the right method (eligible sets at decision epochs, labeled assumptions, observational equivalence, explicit uncertainty) and matches the required format. The headline diagnoses are mostly in the right neighborhood. It is not close to flawless. Several errors are exactly the failure mode the task warned against: reconstructing start order instead of the waiting set, then over-fitting a discipline to that order.

**Material eligible-set error (QC).** At 10:32 the waiting set is not `{C01, C04, C05}`. C06 arrived at 10:25 (`est_min` 6) while C03 was still in service. SPT’s prediction is **C06**, not C04. Choosing C04 is a clean SPT violation with no SLA story (C06’s deadline is 15:00). The claim that `C02 → C03 → C04` is “highly consistent with SPT over the clearly available backlog” is therefore false. That mistake does real work: it inflates support for the QC primary label, buries a better hybrid (SPT snapshot / committed next-job at 10:18, with later arrivals not re-planned), and wastes the 10:32 discrimination test on C04 vs C05 instead of the actual three-way C04/C05/C06 contrast. Section 3 then cites C06 at 11:34 as SPT support without noticing C06 was already skipped once.

**Internal contradiction (QA).** The 09:32 table correctly has SPT selecting A07 (`est_min` 9 vs 25). The interpretation still says 08:32 **and** 09:32 “strongly reject … SPT.” Only 08:32 rejects SPT. The discriminating SPT rejection is 09:05 (A05 over shorter A07), which is never reconstructed.

**Overstated FIFO in QB.** FIFO-among-waiting is sold using 10:28 (B04 over B06/B07), while 10:00 is omitted. At 10:00 the plausible set is paused B01, B04, and B05; the observed choice is **B05**. That is priority, not FIFO (FIFO: B01 or, if paused jobs are ignored, B04) and not SPT (B04, 8 min). Together with 09:31 (resume B01 over p2 B03) and 10:14 (resume B01 over p2 B06), waiting-time selection is **not** a stable FIFO rule. “Hybrid / discretionary” is fair; “FIFO-like selection among waiting jobs” is not, at the confidence given.

**Missed high-value epochs.**
- QA 09:05: FIFO vs SPT vs priority in one step.
- QA never uses the non-preemption of A04/A08 to kill preemptive priority directly.
- QD 11:25 on D-M: D04 (p3, 10) over D08 (p1, 8), with D07 ineligible on mechanical-only D-M. This is the strongest within-skill FIFO-vs-priority/SPT episode and is absent from §2.

**Smaller inaccuracies.**
- A06 starting at 09:20 is not a serious challenge to appointment gating. A05 is already in service until 09:20; under non-preemption the appointment can only start late. Framing this as a tolerance-window violation is sloppy.
- At 09:31, B01 remaining time is determined: 45 − 14 = 31 min. SPT is not “ambiguous”; it picks B03. Assumption A3 made this computation mandatory.
- D-X at 11:20 could also have taken D08 (p1, mechanical) or D03 (electrical). The idle-until-appointment reading is still the best, but the eligible set is underspecified relative to the rest of QD.
- QC confidence 55 is in the right band; QD at 80 is high given the two long unexplained idles, which the text itself treats as first-class anomalies.

**What is actually strong.** QA as FIFO with appointment gating is the right primary/secondary split; 08:20 vs 08:32 is the correct pair of supporting/contradicting episodes for that split. QB’s 09:16 preemption and resume-in-progress bias are identified without collapsing every FIFO violation into priority. QD as skill-constrained routing with FIFO inside the feasible pool, appointment hold for D06, and meeting-aware deferral of D05 is the best-supported of the four labels. §4’s observable-design (dispatchability timestamp, resume-mode, resource-state log) is competent. §5 ranks data-quality and hidden resource state above estimate-gaming, which the exact `est_min` = realized durations justifies. §7’s warning against inferring strict priority from FIFO exceptions is the correct cross-queue conclusion.

**Net.** This is a coherent, format-compliant process-mining analysis with the right epistemology and three diagnoses that survive the errors. It fails the task’s own quality bar in the place that matters most—one reconstructed decision set is wrong, one table disagrees with its narrative, and one omitted decision undercuts a primary sequencing claim. Under a strict reading that is a mid-6s paper, not an 8–10.
