**6.8**

The reconstruction is mostly competent and internally consistent: wait vs service vs sojourn are separated, OPEN is treated as elapsed (not completed) service, waiting blanks are lower bounds, missing starts are bounded from resource windows, utilization uses in-window busy time (including OPEN elapsed time), and the S1-early / S2-late bottleneck reading is supported by the counts, WIP path, and C2 outage. Most case-level times and the station aggregates I checked (sojourns, subperiod arrival/completion counts, busy minutes 595/570, util 82.6%/80.9%, WIP snapshots) match the data under the stated half-open convention.

It is not close to flawless.

**Required analysis is missing.** A Little’s-Law check is listed in the task and asserted in A9 (“is used only as an illustrative consistency check”), but no \(L \approx \lambda W\) calculation appears in the delivered sections. That is an overclaim plus a dropped requirement, not a minor omission.

**The final write-up still contains draft error.** Section 2 gives P05’s S2 window as `[10:45, 12:15]`, then `[10:45, 10:15]` (empty), then corrects to `[09:45, 10:15]`. The eventual bound is right; leaving the false interval in the report is a clarity and correctness defect.

**Python misses an explicit requirement.** The code builds the table, distinguishes MISSING/OPEN/blank, builds an interval frame, calls `format_dataframe`, and labels the `insert_case_*` calls as case-level. It does **not** compute the optimistic/central/pessimistic scenarios in pandas (only central fill), and it never prints the PM4Py insert outputs—only a statement that they were applied. Collapsing completed cases to one arrival→finish interval also makes `insert_case_service_waiting_time` a weak demo (service ≈ total sojourn).

**Other issues that keep this well below a high score.** Arrival/departure *rates* are mostly implied by 2-hour counts rather than reported as rates in §4. Resource-level busy/util (I1/I2/C1/C2) is not in the station table. “Central” for P05/P11 is the *earliest* feasible start (max service); that is disclosed later but the label is misleading. Scenario O’s 0-minute service is an extreme bound they themselves call non-operational, so the sensitivity span is wider than a plausible range. Some means are reported more precisely than 5-minute rounding supports.

The bottleneck, missingness, and WIP story would support a higher score if Little’s Law had actually been run, the P05 bound text had been cleaned, and the code had implemented and printed the uncertainty scenarios and PM4Py attributes.
