8.5

This is a strong, internally consistent fork–join analysis. The delay identities are correct, the case table matches the timestamps, critical-path ranking does not confuse long service with control of `join_ready`, and the counterfactuals correctly treat noncritical branches and downstream masking.

**What is right.** Upstream, branch queue, branch service, `join_ready = max(end_i)`, sync wait, downstream wait, final service, and total flow are defined without double-counting. The boxed identities \(L=U+\max_i(Q_i+S_i)+Q^F+S^F\) and \(Q_i+S_i+W_i=T\) are the right max-based accounting. The F02 overlap example (10 + 35 = 45 branch-minutes vs 35 elapsed minutes inside a 60-minute stage) is exact. Worked cases F02, F04, F07, and F11 match the log, including F04/F08 as C-critical and F07 where B’s queue, not A’s longer service, sets the join. Branch summaries, exclusive margins, and the 8/12 B-critical count are correct. The three required counterfactuals, plus the F11 A-acceleration masking example, are coherent under the stated replay rules. Section 7 correctly refuses causal and capacity claims the timestamps cannot support.

**Deductions (strict).**

1. **PM4Py requirement is not actually met.** The spec required calling `format_dataframe` and a performance method. The script wraps that in `try/except` and the write-up calls PM4Py “optional.” That is a spec miss, even with honest unavailability and otherwise sound comments that DFG edge times do not recover AND-join sync wait.

2. **Format extras.** A sandbox download path and a GitHub footnote sit outside the required heading set. The Python block itself is complete and the pandas numbers are consistent with the tables.

3. **Replay presented more firmly than the evidence.** The retained-lag / immediate-dispatch models are labeled as assumptions, which is good, but per-case flow gains (including 12.5-minute and 92.5-minute totals) are reported as if they were operational savings. They are arithmetic under a frozen F1/F2 order, no competing work, and continuous availability. Section 7 walks this back; Section 4 still reads like a schedule result.

4. **Small-sample tail statistics.** Linear-interpolation P90s on n = 12 are disclosed, but they are then used as redesign metrics (124.5 → 114.5, etc.). That overstates precision relative to the log.

5. **Utilization figure is window-dependent.** \(240/(2\times220)=54.5\%\) is only occupancy on the chosen 09:00–12:40 interval, not a capacity diagnosis. The text says this, but the number is easy to over-read.

No material arithmetic error was found in the 12-case decomposition or in the three headline counterfactual totals (15 join-minutes / 5 flow-minutes for A −20%; 92.5 for B queue −50% under retained lag; 171 for immediate two-resource dispatch). The remaining gap is completeness against the Python/PM4Py contract and a slightly too-sharp presentation of model-dependent savings.
