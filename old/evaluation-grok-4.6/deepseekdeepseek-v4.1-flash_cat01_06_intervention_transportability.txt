**6.4**

The final 10-section writeup is complete, correctly refuses a unique transport effect, and chooses the right policy stance (`conditional go`). Under a hypercritical reading it is still far from near-flawless: several quantitative, causal, and model-fidelity errors are material.

**What is solid.** Site A’s 9-hour median drop is not treated as an identified effect. Co-interventions (API, two extra reviewers, mix shift, huddles, timestamp inflation, no negative control) are carried through the evidence table. The structural comparison correctly blocks `Document Check` before originals and keeps the legal/supplement loop. The candidate intervention and relation set preserve statutory gates. The bottleneck-shift scenario does allow a better median with a worse p90. The pilot’s fidelity vs mechanism vs effect-modification split is the strongest part of the answer.

**Process structure and analogy.** The answer underuses the Site B DFG. `Register -> Identity Check` (2,050) and `Register -> Originals Receipt` (1,860) already show that identity-after-register is largely permitted; measured overlap is 6%, not structural impossibility. Treating “Identity + Originals concurrently for 100% of cases” as a major new transferable treatment therefore overstates incremental value. The real illegal step is starting `Document Check` at registration for the 62% originals-required mass. That distinction is stated in rules, then blurred in the optimistic scenario and the 100% eligible-share cell.

**Causal graph.** Required nodes and `S_` selection nodes are present, and the 180–280-word band is met. Mediation is messy: `treatment_parallelization` points at `waiting_before_assessment`, `cycle_time`, and `rework` even though `true_overlap` is the intended mediator. Missing high-value edges include `originals_required -> true_overlap` and `digital_documents -> true_overlap`. Service times at A fell (identity 3.5→3.1 h, document 7.2→6.5 h). Parallelization should not shorten activity service; those drops are a negative-control-like signal of confounding. The answer lists them as co-interventions and then fails to use them that way.

**Quantitative scenarios.** Sign convention in the scenario table is unclear: several median cells are written as “2 to 5 h (44 → 39–42)” without a minus, while p90 worsening is marked “+”. Optimistic −2 to −5 h overall is hard to defend once 62% of work is originals-gated, identity can already start after register, and A’s mix shifted 48%→40% high-complexity. A back-of-envelope for mix and extra FTE is missing; throughput 4,800→5,200 with 18→20 FTE is not converted to a productivity check. Digital-only arithmetic (≈38% × 3–6 h ≈ 1–2.5 h overall) is the only well-calibrated row.

**Pilot.** Conceptually right (cluster/stepped-wedge because pools are shared), but over-factored: three treatment versions × six strata × four clusters in ~1,700 cases over 12–16 weeks will not support 150–200 per stratum-arm. That power contradiction is not acknowledged. WIP caps (8/14/6/4) are invented, not derived from A’s six-open-tasks rule or B’s 4/7/3/2 pools.

**PM4Py fidelity.** Trees, Petri conversion, `add_edge` so `Assessment` waits on both checks, optional legal XOR, a LOOP-shaped supplement, soundness checks, and “what the model cannot encode” comments all match the prompt. Two construction errors remain. The same `document` and `assessment` `Transition` objects are reused inside the loop and the main partial order, which is an invalid POWL identity. `OperatorPOWL(Operator.SEQUENCE, …)` is not the reliable way to encode sequence in this API; sequence should be a `StrictPartialOrder`. `play_out(..., num_traces=5)` is also a brittle signature. The digital Identity∥Document variant is only a comment, not a constructed model.

**Recommendation text** is in band and substantively correct, but expansion triggers (`true overlap >50%`, −2 h median) are arbitrary relative to the weaker quantitative section.

Net: a competent transportability memo with the right headline decision, undermined by overstated originals-path gains, unused negative-control metrics, an ambiguous scenario table, an overbuilt pilot, and a POWL model that would not faithfully compile.
