**4.0**

The submission has the required eight headings and names the right themes (non-stationarity, calendars, rework, cross-case effects). That is not enough. Under a strict reading of the brief it is an incomplete outline with invented APIs, a truncated experiment design, and a skeleton that violates the stated PM4Py and timing constraints.

**1. Model scope.** The boundary is a restatement of the prompt. Entities are lists of attributes, not a simulation state. There is no paradigm (DES with shared resources vs. hybrid/agent), no clock/event list, and no operational state for the things that actually distinguish this twin from a case-by-case replay: AND-join readiness for planning/fire/environment/notice/inspection before decision drafting; bundle membership and hold-for-batch flags; council-week and holiday calendar state; inspector location/travel; objection *latent* state (not an observed event); aging/priority for D6; applicant as an exogenous delay source rather than a municipal resource. “FIFO or priority-based” is not a discipline. Cross-case dependence is a bullet, not a coupling mechanism (who waits, who is released, what is shared). Level of detail is never chosen (activity-level DES vs. review-stage aggregate).

**2. Parameter estimation / PM4Py.** This section is the weakest technically. `split_log_to_periods`, `calculate_service_time`, and `calculate_waiting_time` are not part of the PM4Py surface the brief asked you to follow. Fourier “seasonality analysis” is named and never mapped to an arrival process (NHPP, piecewise rates by month×weekday×case type). The plan never says how to estimate interarrival processes, XOR/optional branch probabilities from the tree, loop probabilities for the two `*` constructs, or a performance DFG / arrival average—the artifacts the skeleton was required to compute. Rework “cause analysis using textual patterns” treats unstructured, *explicitly unreliable* reason text as if it were a coding scheme. Missing start timestamps are “adjusted” in one clause; there is no method (sojourn-only model, resource-busy inference, lower/upper bound on service vs. wait). Objections are estimated from notice-duration deviations, which is a proxy, but the section does not keep that proxy out of the “observed routing” estimates.

**3. Assumptions.** The taxonomy is correct; several assumptions are indefensible. Rework independent of prior rework contradicts the long-tail Complex/inspection-rework variants. HR calendars treated as accurate to the day ignore the stated reconstruction bias. “Seasonal multipliers accurate to ±5%” is an unearned precision claim. Inspection batching “assumes similar travel times” skips the D3/D5 travel differential and the first-case wait vs. travel trade-off. There is no assumption set for no-shows, legal blackout weeks, 40% environmental non-permit load, Monday clerk diversion, or the flattened log under-representing related applications. Applicant delay is “heavy-tailed” with no distribution family, truncation, or dependence on case type.

**4. Experiment matrix.** Explicitly incomplete. The brief required baseline, D1–D10 where appropriate, combined policy, and **at least three** stress scenarios, with factors, levels, and trade-offs. The table stops at D5, omits D6–D9, omits D10 (D1+D2+D3+D4), and has **one** stress cell. D4 confuses the intervention (temporary inspector) with a demand setting (“Peak 1.7x”). There are no factor levels (value threshold, batch window, aging cutoff, triage policy, appointment-slot capacity). Trade-offs are slogans, not hypothesized KPI movements (e.g., D3: inspector travel ↓, first-case wait ↑, Simple-renovation sojourn may rise). No KPI column that matches Section 7.

**5. Uncertainty.** One-year warm-up and three-year runs are asserted without a diagnostic (Welch/MSER, backlog by week-of-year). Starting empty after a one-year warm-up still needs a statement of initial condition and of *where* in the seasonal cycle the statistic collection starts. “25 replications with fixed random seeds” does not specify independent streams, common random numbers across policies, or seed-per-replication. Heavy tails are mentioned only as “Beta PERT or bootstrap”—no truncation policy, no extra replications for p90/p95, no separation of input uncertainty (calendar, objection proxy, missing starts) from stochastic uncertainty. Sensitivity is two factors. No interval construction (t-interval on means, quantile CIs, or posterior/credible intervals).

**6. Validation.** Headings are almost the required list, then one sentence each. Resource-utilization validation is missing as a first-class check (only “bottleneck/HR metrics”). Replay is not specified (fitness/precision, alignment, or case-type mix). KS tests on large *n* will reject useful models; no practical-significance or case-type-stratified check. Out-of-sample is “unseen high-demand periods,” not a temporal split of the 18-month log. No statement that counterfactual policies cannot be validated from the log, only the baseline twin.

**7. Decision criteria.** Not usable for selection. No SLA definitions by case type, no numeric thresholds/ranges, no district *and* architect fairness operationalization, no robustness metric (worst stress, regret vs. baseline). “Avoid exceeding 90% utilization” is a single arbitrary cap. Median “case type SLAs” is circular. No rule for conflict (D2 helps Simple, may starve inspectors’ learning/batch density; D10 interactions). Correctly avoids claiming optimality, but also avoids a decision rule.

**8. Python skeleton.** Fails the brief on multiple hard constraints.

- Mixes current high-level calls (`format_dataframe`, `parse_process_tree`) with obsolete 1.x `factory` imports (`inductive`, `pt_converter`, `dfg_discovery`, `case_statistics`) and never `import pm4py`, so `pm4py.format_dataframe` / `pm4py.parse_process_tree` would not run.
- Does not build the provided excerpt; it reads a file. Acceptable only if the excerpt were actually constructed in-code as allowed.
- Process tree is a stub `"->(...simplified tree...)"` with no discussion of the simplified string’s limits (missing appeal, legal after drafting, unstructured rework, AND-join semantics).
- No performance DFG, no case-arrival statistic.
- `service_time = Complete timestamp - Start timestamp` invents columns the log does not have and **collapses wait/service without explaining the known completion-only bias**—a direct constraint violation.
- One empty `SimulationModel.simulate: pass`. Required placeholders for **arrival generation, routing, resources/calendars, and experiment execution** are absent.
- Experiment loop is `Baseline, D1, D2, Combined`—inconsistent with Section 4 and with D1–D10.

**Net.** Conceptually pointed at the right problem class, but it is not a design: dependencies and calendars are not mechanized, half the experiment program is missing, uncertainty/validation/decision sections are placeholders, and the only executable-looking artifact is PM4Py-incoherent and timing-incorrect. That is well below a competent digital-twin design for this prompt.
