The write-up follows the required eight-section scaffold, keeps arithmetic and `ev11` non-unique, and selects a genuinely incomplete Bundle A rather than closing every lifecycle. That is the right abductive posture. It is not nearly flawless. Several claims are false, several statuses contradict the selected repair, and the process-mining consequences are technically unreliable.

**Invented evidence.** P-01 and §5 treat a “package weight budget” as independent support for packing `IT-B1` into `PK-8`. No item weights exist. `route NORTH` is likewise almost worthless here: every package and delivery is `NORTH`. Padding the case with non-evidence is a serious fault in a repair whose value is calibrated uncertainty.

**Over-confident physical repair.** Domain rule 3 says an item-only `Pack Item` row can be a real scanner outage. All three bundles still **accept** `ev08→PK-8` and `PK-8 packs IT-B1` at 0.90, creating a mixed-order package (`ORD-A`/`ORD-B`) that the unrepaired O2O graph does not contain. A conservative bundle should at least leave this provisional or unresolved. `N-421?`→`DEL-Y` at 0.82 **accept** is similarly stiff given the literal `?`. The scoring model in §1 is never applied; the numbers are impressionistic.

**Table vs bundles.** The required table is not a single coherent proposal. P-05/P-06 are `provisional` while Bundle A and the JSON leave `ev11` unresolved. P-12 is `reject`, then Bundle C adopts that same edge. Status labels therefore do not mean what the spec says they mean.

**Broken lifecycle/flattening arithmetic.** Unrepaired E2O gives `ORD-A` five events (`ev01, ev03, ev04, ev11, ev12`) and `ORD-B` two (`ev02, ev05`). The answer says eight and three, silently counting item/package hops. It then says Bundle A flattening on Order “includes” `ev08` and `ev10` for `ORD-B` “via item-package containment,” while also warning not to treat O2O enrichment as semantic proof. Standard `ocel_flattening` uses E2O, not O2O reachability. Calling `ev08`/`ev10` “orphan events” is also wrong: every event already has at least one object. They are cardinality-incomplete, not orphans.

**OC-DFG error.** For `PK-8` the repaired sequence is `Pack(ev07) → Pack(ev08) → Dispatch(ev10)`. That is a `Pack Item` self-follow plus one `Pack→Dispatch` step, not “`Pack Item` → `Dispatch Package` traversed twice.”

**Weaker but real gaps.** HI-3 is over-sold as a hard invariant even though the domain rules are defeasible and confirmation may omit deliveries. PAY-1=`100` and PAY-2=`130` matching the two order values is under-used. Existing independently supported pairs (`ev12`+`INV-44 bills ORD-A`, `ev13`+`PAY-1 settles INV-44`, `ev09`+`DEL-X carries PK-7`) are not used as the template for what “independent E2O and O2O support” actually looks like, even though §5 names circularity correctly for the invoice case. The PM4Py outline meets the prompt’s checklist but does not compare unrepaired vs repaired graphs, so it cannot catch the flattening mistake above.

The selected JSON repair is still a reasonable minimum physical patch. The surrounding argument overstates support, miscounts relations, and misstates how object-centric discovery will behave. That is well short of a high score under a strict reading.
