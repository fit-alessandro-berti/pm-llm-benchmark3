## Fatal empirical error

The supplied strata are unambiguous: Orion is **faster in every complexity band** (Low 7.50 vs 9.64, Medium 17.17 vs 19.38, High 31.57 vs 35.00). The crude gap (21.96 vs 15.24) is composition: Orion is 42.9% high-complexity vs Nova 12.5%. That is the reversal the prompt asks the analyst to see.

Section 1 states that Orion Low is **slower** (7.50 vs 9.64), that Low rework is **higher** (0% vs 10%), and that Low “review service” is longer (3.45 vs 5.06). Those last two numbers are **mean wait**, not service, and 3.45 is shorter. The executive verdict, the “backfire on Low” story, Hypothesis 6, and Phase 3 (send all Low work to Nova) are built on a comparison the author wrote down and then read backwards.

That is not a minor slip. It inverts Simpson’s paradox and turns a mainly compositional pattern into a fake treatment-effect heterogeneity story.

## Section-by-section

**1. Executive assessment.** Verdict `partly causal through mechanisms` at 65% is too sharp given n=30, almost no overlap, and completed-case selection. The prompt required an explicit split of **total vs direct vs mediated** Orion effects. The text never defines those estimands. Quality is also wrong: there is **one** appeal in each group (C14, C24), not “1/6 vs 2/16”.

**2. Causal graph.** The fenced block is not a directed adjacency list. Lines such as `[complexity]  [routing_rule]  [resource_group]` do not specify arrows. Several implied directions contradict the comments: `rework → resource_group` while claiming Orion raises errors; `case_completed → cycle_time` as a “collider” (selection is the other way: we only see completed cases). Supported vs assumed arrows are thin. Missing obvious structure: `complexity → routing_rule → resource_group`, `shift → queue_wait`, `compliance_performed`, `unobserved_case_risk → cycle_time / quality`.

**3. Variable-role table.** Roles are often category errors. Complexity is pre-treatment and a **confounder / effect modifier**, not a mediator of a group effect. Shift is not a collider. Review service and 30-day quality are not colliders. The table asserts Orion review service is “consistently higher across strata”; stratum means are slightly **lower** for Orion. Conditioning risks are named but not tied to a clear estimand (ATE of forced Orion assignment vs effect of the routing policy vs effect of night capacity).

## Causal discipline

Resource group is treated loosely as a treatment that “has mechanisms,” not as what the process description says it is: a **routing-policy output** bundled with night coverage, certified reviewers, and harder case mix. There is almost no positivity discussion (Orion-Day is one Low case; Nova-High is two cases). Completed-case selection is named and then ignored. Queueing vs service is confused in the very paragraph that should separate them. Stratification is alternately over-interpreted and misread; it is never treated as what it is—an association that still leaves unobserved risk, shift, load, and selection on the table.

A template-complete memo that reverses the main contrast, fabricates several comparators, mis-draws the DAG, and ships broken PM4Py is far from “nearly flawless.” Structure and a cautious-sounding recommendation are the only reasons this is not lower.
