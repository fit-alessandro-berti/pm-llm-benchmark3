**6.4**

The audit is substantively on the right side of the task: it catches complete-trace leakage, the 65% `final_handler_team` coincidence, the SQL off-by-one, the 23:00 snapshot, mutable scores without `valid_at`, and it keeps `queue_length_at_t0` and prefix age instead of deleting every correlate. It also treats `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` as semantically legal but post-`t0`, versus `Claim Closed → Damage Assessment Completed` as order-illegal plus timestamp-ambiguous. That is the distinction the rubric cares about. It is not close to a high score under a hypercritical reading.

**Section 1 fails an explicit coverage rule.** The table must include every sequence pattern. Three of five never appear as rows: `Documents Requested → Documents Received`, `Coverage Check Completed → Fraud Investigation Opened`, and `Damage Assessment Completed → Settlement Decision Recorded`. Those are discussed later, so the knowledge exists, but the required artifact is incomplete. In their place the table double-counts `appeal_seen_anywhere` under a sloppy alias. That is not a formatting nit; it erases the chance to separate *process-consistent but leaked* edges from *process-impossible* ones in the one place the grader is told to look.

**Section 2 conflates checks it claims to separate.** Activity-order feasibility is not prefix availability. `Documents Requested → Documents Received` and `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` are order-legal and should pass AOF; they fail PTA. The write-up calls them “not feasible in prefix” and “impossible in prefix” under AOF, then later scores AOF as 1/5 fail. The summary table is right; the check text is not. `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` is also mis-numbered as “activities 7–8” (8 is `Claim Closed`). “Activities 25a” and “Activities 46” are garbled. The invitation to state DFG/alignment/footprint roles and limits is essentially unused; DECLARE is named, not limited. A frequent DFG edge is never distinguished from a normative constraint.

**Signs and causal language are repeatedly sloppy.** Minus signs disappear (`−0.03`, `−0.05`, `−0.12`, `−0.09`). “Correlation  causation” is a broken inequality. Opposite queue effects across X and Y (18 → +0.14, 4 → −0.05) are cited as evidence *against* a causal/state reading; they are the pattern you expect from a consistent congestion signal. Category 1 is titled as if frozen prefix features were causal; `claimant_channel` does not belong there. `calendar_week` is folded into congestion instead of the requested drift/calendar class.

**The revised explanations — the main deliverable — recycle the leaked scores and misstate what drove them.** After correctly removing `appeal_seen_anywhere` (+0.26) and `final_decision` (+0.06), Case X still says the model predicts **82%**, “driven primarily by claim difficulty and system congestion.” That is false on the given SHAP breakdown. The 0.82 figure is not an admissible prefix probability. Case Y still quotes **71%** after deleting a +0.31 full-trace variant. Case Y also invents `initial_damage_complexity` as a local driver; it was not in Y’s explanation. `prior_claim_count_before_t0 = 3` is kept as a credible driver while the SQL is known to be wrong, which violates “only information defensibly available at `t0`.” The residual “base + other” bundles (+0.21 / +0.20) are never treated as potentially leaked. Case Z’s uncertainty note is the strongest of the three local write-ups.

**Smaller process misses that a strict audit should not drop.** A same-day document count of 6 immediately after `Coverage Check Completed` is process-odd even before the nightly job, because evidence cycles are specified *after* `t0`; that is not flagged. Fraud investigation is asserted not to have occurred yet; the spec only says it may run in parallel after a trigger, including possibly around coverage check. `initial_reserve` timing is correctly hedged, then used as if it were a settled prefix feature.

Section 5 is complete relative to the checklist (feature store, PIT timestamps, prefix recompute, conformance, recalibration, stability, fairness, drift) but padded with a generic 12-week program and arbitrary success thresholds. It does not salvage the missing table rows or the contaminated local narratives.

Net: a competent leakage audit with the right high-level instincts, undermined by an incomplete required table, mixed-up process checks, and revised explanations that still sell leaked probabilities and the wrong drivers. That is well above a weak answer, and well below nearly flawless.
