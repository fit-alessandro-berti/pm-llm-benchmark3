## §1 Policy interpretation

R5 and R6 are misstated.

- R5 is not “legal opinion must occur after escalation.” That is R4 (responded existence / response). R5 is a **precedence** constraint: after escalation, `Obtain Legal Opinion` must occur **before** `Activate Vendor`.
- R6 is not “legal opinion may precede activation.” It is the opposite: emergency **activation may precede** the opinion, if jointly authorized **outside the log**, and the opinion must then arrive within 24 hours.

The two “resolutions” therefore rest on inverted semantics. The chosen option (“prioritize R5”) is then immediately undone by an “explicit flag mechanism for emergency activations.” That silently recombines incompatible policies, which the prompt forbids. There is no account of the decisive fact that the log has neither an emergency attribute nor joint authorization, so an activity-only model **cannot** decide T6. Material assumptions about clarification nesting, post-activation bank changes, and “accepted case” vs. “rejected case” are missing.
