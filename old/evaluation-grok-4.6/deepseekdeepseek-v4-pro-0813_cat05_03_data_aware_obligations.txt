**Net.** Sections 1, 6, and 7 and the hard temporal cases (TX-305, TX-306, TX-307, TX-309, TX-311) are well above average. The TX-302 taxonomy error and the TX-310 evidential overreach are enough, on a hypercritical rubric, to block anything in the 8–10 range.
