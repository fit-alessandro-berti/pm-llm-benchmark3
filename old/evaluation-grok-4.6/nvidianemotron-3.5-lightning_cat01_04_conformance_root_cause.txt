**4.6**

The investigative core is directionally competent: it treats the June–July fitness collapse as multi-causal, uses the staged preprocessing recoveries, refuses to indict staff from moves-on-log alone, keeps missing compliance checks and the August policy live as residual risk, and notes that an optional sign-off model can inflate fitness by overgeneralizing. Those are the right instincts. The write-up is nevertheless not close to the standard implied by the spec, and several claims are simply false.

**Format and structure.** Section 4 requires at least eight counterfactual rows and explicitly names high-value stratification, manual audit, and reviewer-level analysis as example repairs. There are only six rows, one per hypothesis. The Section 3 “adjacency list” is whitespace-separated pairs, not a directed list, and it encodes a causal falsehood: `measured_fitness → business_outcomes`. Fitness is a measurement artifact; it does not produce appeal or approval rates. `policy_change` is not linked to `true_process`, so the August rule is treated as a model problem rather than a real process change the model failed to follow. Section 5 never isolates model comparison (normative vs. candidate sign-off tree) as its own decision.

**Factual errors against the supplied tables.** Rank 1 claims fitness “recovers to 81% by label mapping alone.” The alignment summary says label map only → 58%; 81% is label map **plus** `Queue Routed` removal. That is the central staged result, and misstating it collapses the discrimination between hypotheses 3 and 6. Evidence row 1 assigns the UI relabel to the timestamp/order hypothesis. Evidence row 10 says stable outcomes weaken “all hypotheses”; they weaken behavioral deterioration and, more weakly, noncompliance—not label, order, or extraction defects. Section 7 asserts that after remapping, technical-event removal, and source ordering, reversed `Notify` **and** `Manual Override` “largely vanish.” The same table still lists `Manual Override` and unchecked enhanced reviews as dominant residual log moves. Rank 6 at 30 underweights a documented real process change (`Supervisor Sign-off` on high-value claims). Label change and `Queue Routed` are repeatedly bundled, so the ledger and ranking do not cleanly separate semantic relabeling from technical-event injection.

**Evidence ledger.** Twelve rows are present and some reliability notes are sensible (UI audit, 11/70 screenshot check, incomplete incident detection). The single most diagnostic object in the packet—the stepwise fitness path 32 → 58 → 81 → 89, then 66 vs. 86 with a candidate model—is not an evidence item. Variant-frequency structure is not given its own row. “Hidden variable risk” is not one of the six hypotheses the table was asked to support or weaken.

**Diagnostic program.** The four-phase isolation story is the strongest technical prose: DFGs/variants first, alignments second, factorial preprocessing third, stratified audit fourth. Risks of misleading results are named. That is undercut by invented PM4Py calls (`map_regexp_label`, `groupby`, `align_traces_log_model`) and by omitting a model-comparison phase even though the packet’s last alignment row exists specifically for that test.

**Section 6 is a hard fail of a hard requirement.** The spec demanded `pm4py.parse_process_tree(...)`, `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(...)`, and `pm4py.parse_event_log_string(...)`. None of the first or third appears. The tree is assembled with nonexistent modules (`pt_inputs`, `xormodel`, `andmodel`). Sequence (`->`) is encoded as AND, which is the wrong operator and would allow parallel interleaving of `Receive`/`Classify`/`Decision`. The redo-loop `*(Standard Review, Request Info)` is never built. `expand_variants` iterates keys such as `"Apr-May 1"`, splits those keys on commas, and treats the activity tuples as `count`—so the published frequencies 380/180/40/… are never used. Fitness APIs (`conformance_fitness`, `conformance_precision_recall`) are not the usual alignment fitness/precision entry points. The candidate tree duplicates `Decision`, comments on its own mistakes, and is not a coherent optional-sign-off extension. The printed caveats about the EUR 50,000 condition and overgeneralization are the only part of the block that meets the spec. As submitted, the script is not “logically coherent and executable.”

**Conclusion and governance.** Section 7’s split (high confidence that measurement collapse is mostly logging/provenance; moderate residual compliance concern; do not yet claim staff guilt or full compliance) is the right posture, then overstates how clean the residual move set becomes. The six governance rules map onto the six required themes and are usable.

Net: a mid-quality abductive memo with several table-level errors, a missing counterfactual block, a broken causal graph, and a noncompliant PM4Py artifact. That is well below “nearly flawless.”
