**4.6**

The write-up has the right *shape* for this task: six required sections, scenario envelopes instead of a single mean, `max` rather than a sum at the join, calendar pauses named, and temperature excursion treated as a dependence shifter. That is not enough. The forecast is built on elementary clock errors, internal contradictions, and a queue model that the prompt specifically asked not to hand-wave. Under a strict reading those are not polish issues; they are the substance of the grade.

**Material arithmetic failures**

The microbiology ready window is wrong. Forty-six to fifty-four hours after Friday 14:00 is **Sunday 12:00–Sunday 20:00**, not Sunday 16:00–Monday 20:00. The upper bound is off by a full day. Because incubation is continuous, the result is *always* technically ready on the weekend. That fact governs on-call value, Monday readout, and every later confirmation clock. The answer never corrects it.

The contamination path then compounds the error. Even under the answer’s own (wrong) primary window, +20–30 h does not produce “Wed 12:00–Thu 02:00.” Correctly: first readout is Sunday (on-call) or Monday 08:00–09:30; confirmation incubation lands **Monday night–Tuesday afternoon**; second readout is Tuesday in the typical case. The scenario’s earliest terminal on Wednesday, and the P80/P90 bands that inherit it, are about one to two days too late.

Lab-investigation arithmetic is also wrong: Monday + 7 calendar days is the **following Monday**, not Friday. Remaining-hour conversions disagree with the stated clock times (Friday 15:30 → Monday 14:00 is ~70.5 h, not 68 h; other conversions drift by 1–3 h). The claimed triangular mean of 2.75 h does not match the formula the ledger itself writes, `(1.5+4×2.5+4.5)/6 = 2.67`.

**Queueing treatment is the other core miss**

Chemistry is not “spill to Monday 08:00, then 2.75 h, done by 10:30–12:30.” At 15:30 there are four samples ahead and only ~2.5 Friday hours left on two benches. Under FIFO (the 55% discipline) B-417 almost certainly **does not start** Friday, so Monday still carries residual WIP and unstarted work. Starting a fresh service at 08:00 silently deletes that queue. Risk-priority can start B-417 late Friday; leftover service then resumes Monday — a different remaining-time object than a full new draw. The prompt asked for queue waiting versus service; this is the place it mattered, and it was flattened.

QA clearance is overstated the same way. Five in queue + ~3 weekend arrivals versus ~5–6 median completions leaves on the order of **2–3 batches Monday morning**, before overdispersion. “Queue clears by Monday 08:00” and “2 h wait” are written as if they were the same claim. P80 service of 3.5 h makes weekend capacity much tighter than the median drain implies. Future-join queue uncertainty is named, then replaced by a thin 0–2 h wait.

**Internal contradictions**

Scenario 1’s dominant clock is “Microbiology readout & QA service,” but the same scenario’s join is `max(Mon 10:30–12:30, Mon 08:30–09:30)` — **chemistry**. Earliest completion is said to require weekend on-call, which cannot pull the terminal earlier if chemistry is the join clock and cannot finish Friday without overtime. Section 6’s “on-call moves the forecast 8–12 h” repeats that inconsistency. The assumption ledger *picks* risk-priority as the working rule instead of carrying 55/45 as uncertainty, which is the opposite of what “uncertain queue discipline” required.

**Weaker probabilistic and P-level hygiene**

Bundling “rerun + query” and labeling a 20–25% band “QA Deviation Investigation (Lab)” mixes the investigation-opened rate with the lab-vs-documentation split (lab is ~20–40% of opened investigations, i.e. closer to high-single-digit to low-teens percent of all cases). Overlaps are noted, which is good; the labels still over-claim distinct mass. The “P50-like” band Mon 16:00–Tue 12:00 is not a median: it is a scenario smear. If roughly half the mass is the no-rework Monday path, a P50-like clock should sit on Monday, with Tuesday in the upper-central or P80 body. Release at 75–85% and rejection “<5%” is asserted without a defect model; that is acceptable as a judgment, but it is presented as if it were implied by the data.

**What is actually sound**

The ledger format, the four required scenario types, explicit `max` language, weekend close/resume rules, continuous incubation vs staffed readout, non-independence of the temperature flag, and the decision to keep rejection rare are all in the right family. The *qualitative* headline — you mostly wait out the weekend and finish Monday afternoon/evening unless rework or investigation opens a multi-day tail — survives because the Monday staffing gate masks the Sunday-vs-Monday incubation bug on the **smooth** path only. That is why this is not a 2–3. It is also why it cannot be a 6+: the contamination tail, the P80/P90 envelope, the Monday chemistry clock, the QA wait, and the on-call sensitivity are exactly the objects the rubric scores, and they are not reliable.

A nearly flawless answer would have Sunday 12:00–20:00 ready, Monday 08:00 readout without on-call, residual chemistry and QA queues carried across the weekend, competitive Monday join (often chemistry once residual queue is kept), contamination centered on Tuesday, investigations in working hours vs calendar days without the +7-days-equals-Friday slip, and a P50 that is not a 20-hour dump of adjacent scenarios. This answer is far from that.
