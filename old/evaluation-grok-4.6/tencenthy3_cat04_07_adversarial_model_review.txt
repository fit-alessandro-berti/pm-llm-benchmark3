**4.2**

The completed portions (roughly §§1–7) are a competent governance review: use cases are separated, metrics are not averaged, Model S/C are attacked with concrete traces, and B1–B8 are mostly reasoned from the expressions. That is not enough. The required artifact is an 11-section decision document. The submitted text dies mid-table in §8 and never delivers §§9–11. Those missing sections are not decoration; they are the validation program, the board recommendation, and the mandatory dissent.

**Format failure.** The spec demands exactly eleven top-level headings. Eight appear; §8 is truncated at “B2 valid Auto alt”. No adversarial traces of the third model, no experiment design, no PM4Py metric skeletons, no deployment rule, no 150–250 word rebuttal. Against this rubric that is a hard incompleteness, not a polish issue.

**§7 is the other decisive defect.** The third model is advertised as `Correct Dossier` *followed by* a recheck choice, but `corr_block` is a `StrictPartialOrder` with **no edge** from correction to recheck. Those two nodes are concurrent, so a recheck can precede `Correct Dossier` and P7 is not actually enforced. The same `Transition` objects (`re_credit`, `re_tech`) are reused as XOR children and as nodes inside `both`; that is not a well-formed POWL. `OperatorPOWL.CHOICE` / `OperatorPOWL.LOOP` is not the PM4Py construction (`OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR|LOOP, children=...)`). `LOOP(SilentTransition(), corr_block)` is an under-explained hack. Supplier is forced after the whole correction loop without a clear policy justification. The idea (mandatory parallel checks, guarded recheck, XOR approval, post-hoc attribute checks) is the right *kind* of third model; the executable semantics do not match the prose.

**Smaller issues in the finished sections.** S’s `+` as AND of four `X(·,τ)` is the right reading, and the probe matrix is largely correct (C rejects B2/B5/B6; S accepts B3/B4). Still: S’s inability to fire `Correct Dossier` is under-weighted as a modeling hole rather than just a probe cell; C’s eight branches are treated as a closed variant list without noting how process-tree XOR of sequences interacts with alignment (duplicate labels, move-on-model). Precision/TBR pathologies are well ranked; alignment-cost and split-sensitivity stay qualitative. None of that would sink a complete paper; next to a broken `model_third` and three missing required chapters it does not recover the score.

A nearly flawless answer needed a compilable POWL/tree whose edges match P1–P7, a finished P/B/adversarial table, a seed-and-negative-suite evaluation plan without invented numbers, a use-case-split governance call, and a real dissent. This draft has the voice of that review and about two-thirds of the structure. It is not close to 8–10.
