**Fatal model/evaluation contradictions**

The dictionaries and the verdict matrix do not describe the same system.

- `precedence(Obtain Legal Opinion, Activate Vendor)` means: if activation occurs, the opinion must already have occurred. That is not vacuous on ordinary acceptances. T1, T2, T4, and T5 all activate without an opinion, so they must **fail** this rule. The matrix marks them `pass`. Section 9 then repeats the same error: the rule is *not* vacuous “for all non-escalated cases”; it is vacuous only when activation is absent.
- `exactly_one` on `Activate Vendor` and `Release First Payment` forbids the reject path. T3 must **fail** DECLARE. It is marked `pass`.
- `absence` of `Sanctions Escalation` forbids T6 and T7. T7 is marked DECLARE `pass`. The comment “not universal; optional” shows the template itself was misunderstood: `absence` is “must not occur,” not “may be missing.”
- Several rules violate the stated contract that every rule has `support` `1.0` (`absence` 0.7; `exactly_one` / `Release` 0.95).
- T6 is marked Log Skeleton `fail` because “opinion after activation,” but that pair is not in `always_before`, `always_after`, `never_together`, or `directly_follows`. The cell is not entailed by the published skeleton.

A hybrid design is only as good as the claim that each layer’s verdict follows from that layer’s artifacts. Here it does not.
