The sequence rows are not. `t0` is *immediately* after the first coverage check; the given prefixes are only `Claim Registered -> Coverage Check Completed`. `Documents Requested -> Documents Received` and `Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded` therefore cannot be “valid prefix patterns.” Both activities sit after `t0` in the normative model. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated differently from `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed`, but for the wrong reason. The prompt’s semantic note is that a second `Settlement Decision Recorded` after appeal is legitimate. The pattern is complete-trace leakage of a *normatively allowed* continuation, not an illegal order (“Appeal cannot precede Settlement”). Remediation “invert” on the closed-before-assessment edge is also wrong: even the reversed pair is still future information at this `t0`.
