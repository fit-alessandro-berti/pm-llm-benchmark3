**What is strong.** The seven-row mechanism table is the right frame, and the integrated narrative correctly treats the sequence-position curve as a mixture, not a learning curve. **Logical contradiction (serious).** Section 2 claims queue drainage (46 → 31) “mechanically lower[s] service time,” and the learning row treats falling queue as a confounder that helps explain the 18.8 → 13.6 drop. The WIP×complexity table shows the opposite direction: lower WIP is slower (12.7 / 22.4), higher WIP is faster (9.4 / 16.8). That is not a wording slip; it is a mechanism error.

**Requirement miss.** The dashboard must include waiting time. Queue/WIP is not waiting time. Handover is present; waiting time is not.
