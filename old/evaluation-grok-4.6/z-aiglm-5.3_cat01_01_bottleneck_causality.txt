**7.0**

The write-up is a strong causal and process-mining memo: it catches the crude-versus-stratified reversal, keeps stratification from being treated as identification, separates queueing from service, treats the group label as a routing/staffing bundle, and proposes a safer learn-as-you-go intervention. Format compliance is high. It is not near-flawless.

**What holds up.** The `mainly confounded` verdict is defensible for the claim that Orion *lengthens* cycle time: Orion 21.96 vs Nova 15.24 reverses inside every complexity stratum, and a pooled-weight standardized contrast (~2.5 h the other way) is mostly wait (~2.1 h) not service (~0.3 h). Compliance is correctly placed *before* routing, not as a mediator of group. Overlap/positivity holes (no Orion Medium/High day), completed-case selection, and latent router information are named as identification blockers. Hypotheses cover the required types and come with predicted observations. The four-phase plan (instrument → night capacity, not relabeling → capped Medium RCT → adaptive routing) matches the decision problem.

**Accuracy problems.** Low×Night Orion is given as 7.5 h; C01 is 7.2 h (7.5 is the two-case Low Orion mean, pasted into a one-case cell). Low Nova mean service is 3.64 h, not 3.61; crude Nova service is ~5.04 h, not 5.02. These are small, but they sit in the “auditable arithmetic” table.

**Process-mining failure in the required artifact.** The embedded C30 trace is wrong: the source log is `…Compliance Check>Review>Request Info>Review>Close`; the code has `…Compliance Check>Request Info>Review>Close`. That invents a Compliance Check → Request Info edge, drops a Review, and changes the variant set. The same section then asserts identical DFGs / shared process shape — true of the real traces, not of this script. That is evidence the diagnostics were not run, which is a serious hit on section 7 and on process-mining competence.

**Causal-graph slip.** `unobserved_reviewer_skill → resource_group` treats certified-reviewer mix as a case-level confounder. Orion’s skill pool is part of the *treatment bundle* (or a mediator of assignment), not a backdoor into which cases are labeled Orion. That blurs a distinction the prose otherwise gets right.

**Lesser issues.** `shift → arrival_load` is an extra assumption with little support. “Compliance traces ~30 h” mixes High complexity with the compliance step. Confidence 70 sits a bit high next to the claim that residual group effects are not identified.

The committee-facing argument would still be the right one. The grade is held down by a wrong cell mean, a corrupted event log in the reproducible code, an un-run DFG claim, and a mis-specified skill node.
