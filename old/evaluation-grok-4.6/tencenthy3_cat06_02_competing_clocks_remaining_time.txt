**7.2**

The write-up hits the required six-section form, uses scenario envelopes instead of a single mean, treats the join as a maximum, keeps weekend calendars in the critical path, and flags temperature-excursion dependence rather than multiplying anomaly rates as independent. That is the right shape of answer. It is not nearly flawless.

**What is solid.** Chemistry capacity on Friday is handled as analyst-hours, not as four serial samples. Risk-priority versus FIFO is used to open a Monday-morning versus Monday-afternoon chemistry window, and the Friday close is correctly treated as a hard pause (B-417 cannot finish Friday even if it starts ~16:42). Incubation 46–54 h from Friday 14:00 is placed on Sunday 12:00–20:00; without on-call, readout is Monday 08:30–09:30; Saturday QA cannot see a microbiology result because readiness is Sunday. Electronic release after hours is allowed. The assumption ledger is usable. Section 6’s primary update (temperature-excursion flag) and secondary trigger (on-call authorization) are the right pair.

**Material analytical misses.** Under FIFO, long triangular draws on the remaining ahead work plus B-417’s own 4.5 h maximum can push chemistry past 18:00 Monday and onto Tuesday *without* a rerun. The smooth-path tail only names QA congestion. That understates chemistry calendar spillover.

Weekend QA clearance is given as “~2–3 batches.” Friday 4.5 h plus Saturday 4 h is 8.5 reviewer-hours. At the 1.5 h median that is about five to six completions, offset by ~3 arrivals, so a central Monday-morning queue near 2–4, not a leftover of most of the original five. The 2–3 figure is the long-service case presented as the typical case, which biases QA wait high.

**Table and quantile looseness.** “Delayed microbiology” mixes the *baseline* no-on-call path (0.55–0.75) with contamination confirmation. Earliest Monday 09:30 for that row is not a delay relative to the smooth path; the row is not a coherent scenario. Chemistry-rerun “earliest” Monday 18:00 is too late: priority start Friday, short first pass, 1.0 h rerun, thin QA queue can still terminate late Monday morning. Documentation-query earliest Tuesday 09:00 is similarly conservative if the query is only four working hours on a Monday. Section 2’s smooth central band starts Monday 16:00; section 4’s P50 starts Monday 18:00—same forecast, two clocks.

The P80 band through Thursday 17:00 and the P90/severe band Friday 25 Sep–Friday 2 Oct (168–336 h) dump laboratory investigations (a few-percent path) into mid-quantile ranges. A 7–14 day “P90-like” window is too wide to be a P90; it is a mixture of P90 and a worst-case investigation tail.

**Smaller faults.** On-call contamination is written as usable Monday 09:30–19:00; a late Sunday readout plus 30 h lands Tuesday morning. The rerun pause threshold is given as first result after ~16:00; with a 1.0 h minimum, the hard spill is closer to 17:00. QA priority for a high-priority sterile batch is never assumed or rejected. Rejection is mentioned but not given even a rough path probability.

The forecast’s qualitative skeleton is defensible. The numbers and scenario boundaries are not tight enough for a high score under a strict reading.
