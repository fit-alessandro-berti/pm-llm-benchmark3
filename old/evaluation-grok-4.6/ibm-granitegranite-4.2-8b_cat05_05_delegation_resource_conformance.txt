**Critical factual errors**

Aug 15–16 are given as weekend. That makes 11 Aug **Tuesday**, 12 Wed, 13 Thu, 14 Fri, 9 Aug Sunday. The answer treats 11 Aug as Monday and then builds RR4 day-counts on that. The two-business-day logic is therefore untrustworthy even where the conclusion (CHG-505 late / CHG-510 timely) happens to survive a corrected calendar.

D-QM-01 is **production-equipment only**. CHG-505 and CHG-510 are `cooling_system`. Ben’s `Retrospective_QM_Review` on those cases is out of scope. The answer never applies that scope limit. It also misses that the CHG-505 review at 14 Aug 16:00 falls inside Ben’s certified sick leave (from 13 Aug 12:00). Timing is not the only RR4/RR3 failure.

The matrix is too loose on “delegate availability doesn’t matter.” For CHG-506 the evidence is denial plus office workstation, not a policy question about sick delegates. Asha acting on leave is not automatically valid substitution or automatic misconduct; the answer picks authorize and moves on.

**PM4Py / pandas block (fails the stated spec)**

The reconstructed log is not the given log: CHG-503 verify is written as 12:00Z (source 12:30Z); CHG-506 draft as 08:30Z (source 10:00Z). That already breaks “reconstructs the event CSV.”

`pm4py.format_dataframe(..., case_key=...)` is the wrong signature (needs `case_id`, `activity_key`, `timestamp_key`). `filter_four_eyes_principle` is not called as activity-vs-activity (`Draft_Change_Request` vs `Approve_Change`, `Implement_Change` vs `Verify_Implementation`); a fabricated `roles=` argument is used instead. `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is not actually used. Handover/working-together results are read as `.cases` on objects that are not that API. Delegation dicts are inconsistent (`dlg['valid']` will KeyError on entries that omit `valid`). `is_delegation_valid` returns False when the actor is the **principal QM**, so CHG-504 would be flagged as a delegation miss. `within_two_bd` is internally contradictory. Actor mapping is applied twice, the first version can write booleans into `actor`. Sick leave, VPN overlap, Eva’s role cutover, and D-QM-01 scope are not encoded.

The block does **not** meet “executable” or the eight numbered requirements in any strict sense. Service-account handling is at least labeled as partly manual; that is one honest line in a non-running checker.

**Controls and conclusion**

Control ideas (interval + availability in the system of record, alias master, re-auth on `svc_qms`, emergency review clock, no-subdelegation in the template) are directionally right and are the strongest part of the memo. The executive split “40% authority / 30% identity / 30% pattern” underweights credential and master-data risk and overweights pair frequency. Uncertainty is acknowledged in prose and then contradicted by high-confidence AUTHORIZED on the two identity-conflict cases.

**Net:** form and several easy SoD calls are in place; the investigation-grade mistakes (calendar, delegation scope, CHG-504/506 identity, CHG-510 SoD, non-executable PM4Py) are too large for anything in the high band. A near-flawless answer would get the event-time matrix, every case class, RR4 day-count, and a actually runnable four-eyes + interval checker right. This does not.
