7.2

The analysis is substantively strong on event-time roles, alias normalization, delegation scope, and refusal to treat collaboration as collusion. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading, several classification, API, and completeness defects are material.

**What is correct and should be credited.** Event-time evaluation is consistently applied (Eva is not QM on 2026-08-09; Ben is QE except inside D-QM-01; Grace never becomes QM). D-QM-01 is rightly grounded in the 2026-08-09 signed PDF rather than the 2026-08-12 migration stamp, which matters because CHG-501’s approval precedes the database row. D-IC-04 is correctly void as prohibited subdelegation. CHG-508 is caught only after `b.koch`/`u_bk` merge. Service-account handling is conceptually right: CHG-503 unattributed, CHG-509 conditionally Eva. RR4 business-day counting (Tue 08-11 → deadline Thu 08-13; Fri 08-14 late) is a defensible convention. Alternative explanations are actually paired with discriminating evidence. Headings and column sets match the spec.

**Classification errors (not just style).** CHG-504 as `AUTHORIZED` overreaches. The prompt states security cannot exclude credential sharing and that ambiguous identity has no canonical reading; `INDETERMINATE` (secondary `DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM`) is the disciplined label. Medium confidence does not repair a primary label that treats an unproven personal act as established. CHG-509 “leaning `VALID_SUBSTITUTION`” is optimistic: D-QM-02’s absence condition is unevidenced on Saturday after leave ended Friday; burden of proof runs the other way, so pure `INDETERMINATE` or a lean toward unauthorized substitution is tighter. CHG-505/CHG-510 bury a valid IC-path fragment under a single `UNAUTHORIZED` without a secondary `VALID_SUBSTITUTION` for Farid’s in-window CI-44 approval, which blurs RR4’s two-part exception. The 30/30/40 split in the conclusion treats overlapping categories as a partition; CHG-508 is both identity and SoD failure, CHG-510 is authority plus self-approval.

**PM4Py/pandas section is the weakest required block.** `pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle` (classic/high-level API) keeps cases that *satisfy* four-eyes (different resources), not violations. Naming the result `viol_draft_approve` and printing it as violating cases inverts the filter. If executed, CHG-502/508/510 would be the cases *omitted*, not listed. The pandas SoD loop is the actual authority and is largely correct, which makes the PM4Py call cargo-culted. Further gaps: no two-business-day clock for `Retrospective_QM_Review`; IC authority is not gated on `emergency==true`; Ben’s sick-leave interval is not applied in code; D-IC-03 is equated to all `cooling_system` rather than CI-44; `list(handover_net)` emits nodes while the print claims edges. Service-account attribution is at least honestly marked manual.

**Smaller but real unclarities.** Delegate sick-leave is described in narrative and not operationalized as automatic invalidation of D-QM-01 (correct as judgment, incomplete as check). Eva’s post-2026-08-12 Quality Engineer status is never examined as a *control-objective* threat to verifier independence (RR2 is only human-separation, but the brief asked to separate technical conformance from control objective). “Late DB timestamp irrelevant” is too absolute: it is not fatal given the PDF, but it is still a systems-of-record defect. Handover/working-together output is dumped, not interpreted as indicator strength.

**Net.** This is a high-quality investigation memo with the right legal instincts and almost complete case coverage. It is not a 9–10: the four-eyes polarity error, CHG-504 over-authorization, CHG-509 lean, missing RR4/emergency/sick-leave logic in code, and impressionistic percentages are enough, under a hypercritical standard, to keep it well below excellence.
