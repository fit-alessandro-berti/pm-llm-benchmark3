**6.0**

The final write-up follows the required seven-section scaffold, stays inside the given event/object identifiers, does not invent events, and does not treat the longest traces as automatically correct. Those are real strengths. Under a strict reading, they are not enough.

The comparison is systematically incomplete. Order-level duplication is listed as `e13`, `e16`, `e19`, `e22`, `e24`, `e25` across O2/O3. That omits the O3/O4 bundle: `e14`, `e17`, `e27`. The same truncated set reappears in the sensitivity filter. O3 is the hardest case in the log (I31 via P3/D2 and I32 via P4/D3, plus V2’s two payments), yet the matrix and distortions keep centering O1’s split and the O2/O3 pair. That is a material miss, not a rounding error.

Several matrix cells describe the wrong flattening. Invoice “expected event duplication” is written as `e19`/`e22`/`e25` copied onto O2/O3 *if flattened on Order*. Delivery’s duplication cell likewise talks about orders. Those columns were supposed to characterize flattening *on that type*. Package “divergence” is given as P2/P3 sharing D2; that is convergence of two package cases onto `e16`/`e24`. Item convergence is called low and “only at packing,” which ignores shared `e16`, `e17`, `e24`, and `e27`.

Equivalent-execution clustering is misused. O1, O2, and O3 share `e16`/`e24`; they do not have equivalent lifecycles. Using `cluster_equivalent_ocel` to “prove they travelled together” is the wrong tool for that question. That error is repeated in the portfolio, the lead-object argument, and the code.

The PM4Py block is only partly executable-looking. `ocel.log` plus `concept:name` is an event-log API, not an OCEL one. Only Order/Invoice/Package are flattened, so the code does not operationalize the six-way comparison the prose claims to have made. `print(ocel)` and the required discoverers are present; that does not cancel the API mistake.

Smaller defects still count under the stated rubric: DFG arrows are missing in the table (`DispatchDispatch`, `e05e11e15e23`); distortions 5 and 6 largely repeat the payment singleton; the O2O-propagation example admits the relation is already direct; performance bias is asserted more than walked through with timestamps.

Position B is a defensible choice and the argument template (criteria, support, counter, residual risk, reversal conditions) is complete. The piece is competent, identifier-grounded, and internally mostly aligned with that decision. It is not nearly flawless.
