**Material gaps (these are why this is not a 9+).**

1. **Invariant table and validation suite are not aligned.** I4’s negative witness `R,L,A,…` never appears in Section 5. The executable suite therefore cannot fail a model that lets Legal Review precede Assess Risk, which is one of the few hard causal constraints on the optional branch.

2. **I3 and I6 “negative witnesses” are not witnesses.** “A converted model allowing only one order” is a verdict, not a falsifying trace. That breaks the table’s own contract and makes those rows harder to audit than I1, I5, I7–I9.

3. **Section 5 is a logger, not a decision procedure.** It prints soundness, footprints, traces, and replay fragments but never applies Section 7. A net can fail several quarantine rules and still just emit more stdout. The audit therefore stops short of the rejection logic it spent a section defining.

4. **Silent-transition handling is narrower than the conversions it claims to judge.** Only `label is None` is hidden. BPMN round-trips in PM4Py often produce `""` or other non-`None` silent labels. Those would be treated as visible activities and can fabricate language differences or drop real ones.

5. **The preservation table is a pre-commitment sheet, not an audit.** Every BPMN/tree/round-trip cell is `expected preserved` / `ambiguous` / `not tested`. That is honest, but the task asked to catch and *interpret* conversion exceptions. Hypothetical handlers are not an interpretation.

**Smaller but real defects.** Pipeline/table arrows and the Section 4 headers are mashed, which matters in a format-specified deliverable. Footprint comparison via `==` on whatever object PM4Py returns is not a defensible equivalence. Stopping the explorer at the first exact FM visit will miss extra completing traces that leave and re-enter FM if soundness failed or was inconclusive. `play_out` / `fitness_token_based_replay` are version-guessed extras; they are guarded, but they add noise without strengthening the bounded-language argument.
