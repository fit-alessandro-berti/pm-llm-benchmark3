7.1

The submission is a competent digital-twin design: it is not an independent-case replay, calendars and shared resources are first-class, objections are treated as latent, service and waiting are not silently collapsed, and it refuses optimality claims. That is the floor the prompt required, not a reason to score it as nearly flawless. Under a strict reading there are enough reasoning gaps, API slips, and internal contradictions to keep it well below the top band.

**Simulation design.** Scope, entities, and coupling (architect bursts, district bundles, shared pools, correlated latent objections) are the strongest part. The hybrid “DES + lightweight multi-agent” language is loose: what is specified is shared mutable state, not agents. More seriously, the control-flow reading of the given tree is imprecise. `+` synchronizes only Planning / optional Fire / optional Environmental; Public-notice and Inspection are sequential *after* that block, so Decision drafting already waits for them by sequence, not because they sit in the AND. Treating `{planning, fire, environment, public_notice, inspection}` as the `+` join both over-claims the tree and under-specifies a real design choice (whether those stages may overlap). Rework is also thinner than the process text: Planning, Legal, and appeal are named rework sources, but the tree only loops Completeness and Inspection, and Part B never says how appeal or planning-rework re-enter the event graph. Inspection no-shows and applicant unreadiness are in the empirical notes and then largely disappear.

**Process-mining grounding.** The estimation plan is directionally right (NHPP + clusters, flags as lower bounds, HR calendars primary, DFG times as mixed elapsed). The PM4Py usage is not clean. `pm4py.get_start_activities` does not yield arrival timestamps. `pm4py.filter_variants` is not a rework estimator. `pm4py.get_variants_as_tuples` / `pm4py.stats.get_variants` is an uncertain, version-mixing API pair; the public wrapper is `pm4py.get_variants`. The code path is better than the prose (no `read_csv`, `format_dataframe` used correctly, DFG / performance DFG / arrival average present, elapsed time *not* relabeled as service), and the tree-parser caveat is appropriately cautious. That does not erase the textual API errors the prompt explicitly warned against.

**Assumptions and missing data.** The nine-way split is complete and mostly honest: missing starts stay mixtures, objections stay unknown, hidden fire/environment triggers are residual rates, applicant delay is off the municipal calendar. Presentation failures here are not cosmetic. Several key claims are missing operators (“p90  2×median”, “gap  prior waiting”, “submission time  processing start”), so the assumption is not actually stated. Portal 24/7 vs clerk 08:00–16:00 overnight queueing is muddled. Prior violations for D2 default to `False` with no generative story. Council-week frequency is unspecified. These are exactly the places a twin will be silently miscalibrated.

**Experiments.** E0–E10 plus four stresses, interaction language on D10, and a few well-chosen robustness knobs (D2 data completeness, D3 window, D9 lead time) are good. D1’s “loop prob 30%/60%” is not derived from the variant table (19% simple-missing-docs plus mixed others). Several table cells lost their direction of change, so the factor is not identifiable. CRN is proposed without the hard part: when D2/D5/D8 change the event graph, naively reusing seeds does not synchronize clocks or resource contention. That is a real experiment-design hole, not a polish issue.

**Uncertainty and validation.** The non-stationary diagnosis is correct and then contradicted in the same section: warm-up “must span at least one full seasonal cycle,” yet the prescription is to discard ~3–4 months. A 3–4 month discard cannot cover Mar–May *and* Sep–Nov, and there is no log-based WIP snapshot, which is the more twin-like alternative to empty-system warm-up. Importance sampling on rework chains is named, not designed. The 2–3 month holdout is too short for a process whose p90 on Complex is 260 days and whose demand is annual; it also risks holding out only one season. Cross-case structure (burst clustering, co-located long notices) is never a validation target. Face/replay/distribution/utilization/bottleneck coverage is otherwise adequate and correctly refuses to treat conformance fitness as timing validation.

**Decision criteria.** Median/p90 by type, SLA, district and architect fairness, utilization bands, rework non-increase, backlog stability, and rank-stability under stress are the right family. The 10%/20% trade and 75–90% utilization band are arbitrary and not tied to municipal SLA-setting. Applicant effort (D1) and residual risk (D2) stay qualitative footnotes. The anti-optimality framing is correct and should stay.

**Code.** Modular enough and faithful to the required skeleton contract. It is still a sketch: almost every operational method is `NotImplementedError`, D2 is the only coded policy, and `get_variants_as_tuples` may fail on a stock PM4Py install. Acceptable as a skeleton; not evidence the twin is implementable as specified.

**Net.** Stronger than a generic DES write-up, weaker than a design one would hand to a municipality without revision. The warm-up contradiction, tree misread, holdout mismatch to seasonality and tails, and PM4Py/API looseness are disqualifying for a top score. Fix those before treating the rest as a working experiment plan.
