**5.4**

The memo is well structured and gets the central descriptive fact right: the crude Orion delay reverses inside every complexity stratum, so a 50% reassignment is not justified by the aggregate means. That is not enough. Under a strict reading the piece has repeated evidence errors, several causal-role mistakes, and more certainty than the design can support.

**Causal verdict and required distinctions.** `mainly confounded` is defensible for the crude gap, but **85/100 is too high**. The same memo correctly flags failed positivity, n=2 cells, unobserved risk, and completed-case selection. Those facts should have pulled the verdict toward `not identifiable from the supplied evidence`, or at least cut confidence sharply. Section 1 never cleanly separates a **total** effect of assignment, a **direct** effect holding queue/service/rework fixed, and **mediated** paths—an explicit requirement. It also only argues that the 50% move “will backfire,” not how it might succeed, fail, *or* backfire. “Nova will overload uncertified staff” and “trigger increased information requests” are not identified from these 30 completed cases. Capacity, certification by case type, and general-equilibrium queues are assumed, then stated as fact. Section 1 also overruns the 180–300 word cap.

**Use of the evidence (auditable arithmetic fails).** Several numbers presented as calculations are wrong or invented:

- Night × High load is **17/17**, not 15/17 (repeated in §§1, 3, 4, 5).
- Mean wait with vs without compliance is **16.05h vs 6.54h**, not 16.48h vs 6.21h.
- Wait and service shares of mean cycle time are about **58% and 32.5%**, not 56.0% and 31.4%.
- The **~1.4h compliance service** time is not in the data. `wait_before_review_h` already bundles waiting around compliance; no separate compliance service field exists.
- Rework’s incremental wait/service split (**~1.5h and ~2.4h**) does not match the case file (roughly +5.9h wait and +2.1h service).
- Crude gap is **+6.72h**, not +6.71h (minor, but sloppy next to the others).

The stratum means, pooled standardization (−2.51h), day/night means, and rework/appeal counts are largely correct. That makes the wrong derived quantities worse: they look precise, so a committee would treat them as audited.

**Causal graph and variable roles.** `quality_outcome → case_completed` is temporally backwards; the 30-day appeal is post-process. The adjacency list is not a DAG: `review_service ⇄ rework` is a cycle. **`compliance_required` is not a mediator of `resource_group`**. Policy and complexity set the requirement before/alongside routing; the prompt says the check is performed whenever required. Treating compliance as an Orion mechanism (table, hypothesis 3, “>70% of variation”) is a first-order identification error. `queue_wait` collider language is closer to intermediate confounding, which is at least in the right family.

**Hypothesis table logic.** Nova’s high night waits **support** a shift/load story; listing them as contradicting it is reversed. Hypothesis 1’s “statistically significant” IPW ATE on n=30 with empty Day–Orion and High–Nova cells is not a serious estimator. Hypothesis 5’s “falsification” is just restating C02.

**Intervention design.** The RCT-on-medium and queue/compliance phases match the prompt, but Phase 1 “compliance in parallel with classification” is process-incoherent if classify determines whether compliance is required. The 25% Nova medium rework stop rule is the **current** Nova medium rate (1/4), so the pilot can halt on the status quo. Phase 3 bundles certification and load-balancing, so it cannot attribute learning. Rollback at any compliance failure (0.0%) is operationally brittle.

**Code.** Section 7 mostly meets the spec (embedded CSV, crude/stratified means, stated pooled weights, complexity×shift×group overlap, PM4Py DFG, n<3 warnings, no fabricated timestamps). Unused `warnings` import and no warning on the standardized contrast itself are minor. The code is diagnostic, as required. Process-mining competence in the *prose* is thinner: variant/DFG differences are dumped, not interpreted as mix versus execution.

**What remains good.** Simpson reversal without treating stratification as automatic identification (in the decomposition table); queue vs service vs rework vs quality vs selection all appear; resource group is treated as a routing bundle, not an atomic treatment; five hypothesis types are present; residual uncertainties are the right five.

The core operational recommendation is reasonable. The causal discipline, several “exact” numbers, and the graph/roles are not nearly flawless. **5.4**.
