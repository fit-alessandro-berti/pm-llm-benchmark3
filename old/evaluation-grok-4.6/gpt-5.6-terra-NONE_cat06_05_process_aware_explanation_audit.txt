**8.6**

This is a strong, process-aware audit that hits the intended discriminations: complete-trace leakage versus prefix-valid signals, process-impossible order versus retrospectively legal but future patterns, lineage defects versus mere correlation, and predictive association versus causal or actionable claims. It would not receive a top-band score under a strict reading.

**What is done well.** Section 1 is essentially complete. Every global top-12 feature, every sequence pattern, and the local-only fields (`claimant_channel`, `final_decision`, `initial_reserve`, `risk_score_current`, `last_resource_role`) are classified. The important subtleties are present: `final_handler_team` is not rescued by 65% agreement with the t0 team; `prior_claim_count_before_t0` is flagged for the extraction-date predicate; `same_day_document_count_snapshot` is treated as nightly look-ahead; employee experience is caught even though it is off-ranking. The signature contrast is handled correctly: `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is rejected as normatively illegal *and* future *and* order-ambiguous, while `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is allowed retrospectively after Partial/Deny and still barred at t0. Queue length and elapsed pre-coverage time are preserved rather than discarded. Region and channel are kept under a fairness/proxy standard instead of being deleted. Section 2 is a credible validation suite (availability timestamps, complete-trace dependency graph, hard precedence, timestamp-tie indifference classes, enabled vs impossible vs future-possible at t0, decision-instance semantics). The DFG caveat is stated in the required way. The Case X/Y JSON does not narrate appeals, final decisions, or full paths. Case Z is correctly invalidated.

**Why it is not near-flawless.**

1. **Leaked-model attributions are still treated as directional evidence.** Status `recompute` is right, but Case X/Y still promote queue, complexity, channel, reserve, and region with increase/decrease labels taken from the contaminated explainer. Only elapsed time is explicitly said to need re-estimation. In a model dominated by `appeal_seen_anywhere` and `full_trace_variant_id`, remaining SHAP signs and magnitudes are not identified. A stricter audit would retain those *features* as candidates and refuse inherited local directions until refit.

2. **Severity encoding is sloppy relative to the required scale.** `initial_reserve` is `low/medium`. The appeal pattern is `critical for t0 explanation` rather than a clean critical/high/medium/low. Future-but-legal patterns are split between high and critical without an explicit rubric, so the ranking is harder to defend than the underlying analysis.

3. **Section 1 coverage has small holes and category drift.** X/Y “base value and other features” can hide leaked or extraction-dated fields (the note even flags employee experience), yet only Case Z’s residual is tabulated. `calendar_week` and `claimant_region` are not temporal-consistency failures; putting them in the leakage table is acceptable only because finding types are labeled honestly, but the section mixes leakage, proxy, and drift without a separate unsupported-causal-story pass over the team’s own wording (“Appeals cause breaches,” “the final team determines delay”).

4. **Classification in Section 3 overlaps.** `claimant_channel` and repaired `prior_claim_count` appear as defensible signals *and* fairness proxies *and*, for the latter, unrepaired. The dual standard is intellectually right and poorly partitioned. `initial_reserve` is treated as nearly registration-time with no lineage note; the hedge is present but weaker than the uncertainty warrants.

5. **Minor process-text and formalism gaps.** In 2.C the document cycle is written without order markers. Footprint/prefix formalisms are mostly unused (optional, but the section is less tight than it claims). Mixed leakage on `final_handler_team` could have been called out more sharply: partial agreement with the t0 team makes the field look legitimate while still encoding later escalation.

**Bottom line.** Conceptually this is a high-quality audit and the revised narratives stay inside the t0 cone. The remaining defects are methodological (trusting leftover SHAP directions), taxonomic (severity and section membership), and completeness nits—not a collapse into full-trace storytelling or naive causalization. Under a hypercritical bar those are enough to keep it out of the 9s.
