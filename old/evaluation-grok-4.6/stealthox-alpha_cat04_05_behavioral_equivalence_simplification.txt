The four equivalence notions are the right family: full visible language, P3-bounded language, P1 compliance-plus-observability, and P2 dashboard records. Duplicate labels distinguished only by word position is the correct convention.

## Decisive defects

**1. Wrong bounded cardinalities, stated as hand-derived fact.**  
P3 complete traces with `|t| ≤ 12` and `#Analyze ≤ 3` are:

- **A:** `k` iterations contribute `4^k` traces (`2^k` AC/CA orders × `2^k` approval subsets). Length is `3k + 1 + p` with `0 ≤ p ≤ k`. So `k = 1 → 4`, `k = 2 → 16`, `k = 3 → 64` minus the `p = 3` traces of length 13 (`2^3 = 8`). **`|L_A^{P3}| = 76`**, not 83.
- **B:** one `Check` inserted in any of `(L+1)` positions of a sequential loop word. That is 5 (`k=1`) + 20 (`k=2`) + 60 (`k=3`) = **`|L_B^{P3}| = 85`**, not 109. All of these already have length `≤ 11`.
- Common = 4 is right; A-only/B-only are then 72 and 81, not 79 and 105.

These numbers are not a typo: 79 and 105 are consistent with 83 and 109. They are also labeled “hand-derived from tree semantics, independent of the net translation” and used as an oracle the BFS “must reproduce.” Anyone who runs the (otherwise reasonable) BFS will not get 83/109. That is a serious error in a section whose point is exact bounded comparison.

**2. Notation in §1 is damaged enough to fail “precisely enough.”**  
`Σ`, `≡`, `τ`, `≤`, `≥`, `φ`, `α`, `⊆` are missing, leaving definitions such as `Let  = {…}`, `M _L M  L(M) = L(M)`, `#Analyze  1`, `rework=[k2]`. The intent is recoverable, but the observation boundary was supposed to be stated precisely. Several later cells inherit the same gaps (`k  1`, ` 2 analyses`).

**3. P1-equivalence is not an equivalence relation as defined.**  
`M ≡_{P1} M' ⇔ L(M)=L(M')` **and** every trace of both models satisfies `φ1`. Then B is not P1-equivalent to B, because B emits non-compliant traces. Adequacy of a single model was folded into a binary relation. What they actually use in the verdict is coherent (A is adequate, B is not, and languages differ anyway), but the definition as written is logically broken.

P1 is also stricter than the business text in one place and looser in another: “order inside the segment free” is a **predicate on a trace** (either AC or CA is compliant), yet `≡_{P1}` demands full language equality, so a sequential `Analyze → Check` model would be ruled non-equivalent even though it satisfies every P1 bullet. That tension is never discussed, which then inflates the claim that “any structural simplification” of A breaks P1. Sequentializing the AND already yields a smaller compliant tree.

**4. Code that would fail or overclaim.**  
- `{list(k): v for k, v in prob_a.items()}` uses unhashable lists as dict keys and will raise `TypeError` before `compute_emd`.
- §7 calls `simplified_model` / `net_s` before §8 binds them.
- `mkey` hashes `p.name`; if the conversion reuses place names, distinct markings collapse. Using place identity is the safe key.
- Soundness, simplicity scores, and the 83/109 printout are narrated as if obtained. Tree metrics **were** derived; net simplicity was not. That distinction is required and only partly respected.

The BFS design itself is the right approach (marking + visible word, `tau` hidden, final-marking test, visible and Analyze bounds, playout labeled non-exhaustive). Those strengths do not cancel the cardinality fiction they are wired to.
