**7.4**

The write-up is well structured, constraint-compliant, and mostly process-mining literate. It keeps multiple hypotheses open, treats centrality as descriptive rather than good or bad, includes bots and vendors, and refuses to remove Mira without a replacement. The core qualitative reconciliation is right: handover and subcontracting measure transfer and retained control; working-together measures co-membership; a short-touch exception relay will look central in the first two and peripheral in the third.

The central quantitative exhibit is not reliable. The Section 1 table treats co-case rates as percentages of all ~4100 cases (8% → ~328 shared Mira–Carla cases → ~36% of Mira’s 905; Alice–Carla 27% → handovers per shared case ~0.3). That base is inconsistent with the activity table. Alice has only 665 distinct cases; 27% of 4100 is ~1107, which cannot be a co-occurrence count. The same definition cannot underwrite both the Alice–Carla contrast and the Mira conditional rates. The “~1.1 vs ~0.3 handovers per shared case” discriminator, and the later prediction that exception-only working-together will rise to 25–40%, inherit that error. A careful reading would have flagged the base of “co-case rate” as ambiguous (all cases vs. union vs. SNA weight) instead of publishing the table as approximate arithmetic on 4100.

Smaller defects:
- Total load is 960 × **median** 4.2 min (~1 hour/day). Mean service time is unknown and likely higher; the capacity-bottleneck rejection is directionally fair but over-precise.
- “Only human routing to both specialists” over-reads a **top-edge** list.
- “48% at her events” and “she detects problems” lean on unsettled flag semantics and on Bot_Rules’ prior triage.
- 920 Bot_Rules→Mira handovers is not cleanly “one referral per case” given the 62 Bot_Rules–Mira–Bot_Rules loops and large temp/specialist return edges.

Hypotheses, risks, PM4Py follow-ups, and the recommendation/executive summary are strong. The grade is held down by a showcase calculation that should have been caught.
