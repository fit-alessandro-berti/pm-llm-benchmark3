**7.2**

The final write-up is a strong control-objective analysis, not a four-eyes counter. Classifications are mostly non-mechanical, timing is anchored to settlement/irrevocability, and luck is not treated as control. The 8-month tenure error, L3 overclaim, confidence-scale slip, and several code defects keep it well short of near-flawless.

**What is done well**

- Form vs substance is applied correctly: AP-204 fails despite distinct users; AP-209 is withheld; AP-205 is prevented-before-completion; AP-208 fails despite a legitimate outcome.
- Same-user cases are not dumped into `OBJECTIVE_FAILED`. AP-203/AP-207 as `OBJECTIVE_MET` via a complete ED-1 package, AP-201/AP-206 as `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET`, and AP-202/AP-208 as failures track the facts.
- Equivalence is tested on timing, independence, coverage, overrideability, recoverability, evidence quality, and collusion resistance. The rejection of “same expected loss” as the only standard is on point.
- Manager action after `Release_Payment` but before settlement is treated as hybrid, with AP-207 vs AP-208 as the boundary. That matches the prompt.
- Monitoring rules distinguish alert from proof and cover every requested signal.
- The Python sketch meets the seven mechanical requirements and labels output as evidence, not a verdict. The four-eyes vs blocked-attempt split is conceptually right.

**Material defects**

1. **Vendor tenure on AP-208 is wrong.** ED-1 requires ≥180 days (~6 months). Eight months is above that bar. The narrative repeatedly treats “vendor 8mo” as an ED-1 breach (findings table, rationale). The code sets `vendor_days=240` and would mark `tenure_ge_180d=True`, so the prose and the checker contradict each other. AP-208 still fails on amount, bank change, AV-2 outage, post-settlement ratification, and late audit — the classification survives, the justification does not.

2. **MG-4 L3 is assumed, then classified as MET with high confidence.** Additional facts confirm independence for m02/m11/m15, not grade. `OBJECTIVE_MET` for AP-203 (and to a lesser extent AP-207) overstates what is evidenced. Listing “m02 level” as missing evidence does not license High-confidence MET.

3. **Confidence is defined as support for the classification, then used as support that the payment is safe.** AP-209 as `INDETERMINATE` with Low confidence is incoherent under the stated definition; the right reading is high confidence that the file is unresolved.

**Lesser issues**

- AP-204 is framed mainly as collusion / “collusion-resistance ≈ 0.” The decisive fact is a fabricated receipt that two named actors did not stop. Dedicated-cell vs collusion is explicitly open in the source facts; Medium confidence is fair, the zero-resistance wording is not.
- AP-201 mixes an unauthorized exception (no token, convenience) with partial achievement of the *payment* control objective. Independent pre-settlement ratification on a tiny, clean, low-risk item could have been argued as objective-met / rule-broken. `PARTIALLY_MET` is still defensible because review depth is thinner than for m02/m15.
- Code: EventLog fallback uses `CASE.split(":")[0]` → `"case"` instead of the trace case-id key; `same_user and False` is dead; `aw` is computed and never printed; pair-repeat is patched after the fact. `filter_four_eyes_principle` default (keep compliant traces, then `released - kept`) is the usual modern semantics and is rightly cross-checked, but the EventLog branch would not.
- Extra title above the required headings. Harmless, not spec-perfect.

**Bottom line**

Judgment quality on objectives, timing, coverage, independence, recoverability, and residual risk is clearly above a count-the-activities answer. Strictly, a clear arithmetic/policy error on a stated ED-1 threshold, an over-claimed MET, a broken confidence definition, and checker/narrative inconsistency cap the score well below the 9–10 band.
