In the given table **every** cluster’s start/complete is minute-displayed; seconds appear only on some `recorded_at` values. That is table formatting, not PLC fan-out evidence. Ranking genuine simultaneity at 80 while treating display resolution as a serious counter-signal is inconsistent.

The 15:35–15:38 remainder is then used as if the windows align. The second explanation should have been much closer; 80 overstates a clock-dependent causal story.
