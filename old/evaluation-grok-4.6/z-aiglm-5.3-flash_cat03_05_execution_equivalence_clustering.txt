**6.5**

The final write-up hits the required eight-section skeleton, defines three real equivalence relations (not a disguised activity-label clustering), treats local ids as non-semantic except for `EXT`, and gives reproducible cluster assignments for every execution. That is a complete attempt. It is not close to flawless.

**The central consistency error is X7 under typed structure.** R2 is defined as order-preserving bijection with *equal per-event typed incident multisets*. X1’s Dispatch is `{Order, Package, Delivery}`. X7’s Dispatch is `{Order, Item, Package, Delivery}`. That is an E2O difference, not a qualifier-only difference. Putting X7 in T1 with X1/X2, and writing “structurally = X1” / “X7 is ≡ to X1”, contradicts the relation just defined. The problem plants Item on Dispatch *and* `removed_before_dispatch`; a careful answer would split T-clusters (or explicitly weaken R2 to global type counts). The answer does neither, so S5’s claim that the gap is “visible only in qualified O2O” is also false.

**X4 vs X8 in §5 is internally contradictory.** A payment-count view *separates* them (2 vs 1 payments); the text says a finance abstraction merges them and that a payment-count view “also merges them wrongly-by-coincidence.” In the next sentence a “logistics-shape-only view” both merges (two waves) and separates (unplanned rework). The required “two defensible definitions disagree” is asserted, not shown.

**§2 does not deliver the required canonical scheme.** The preferred relation is R3 (flags, settlement, O2O motifs). Section 2 gives a three-line event token and defers the rest to §6. Qualifiers, payment pattern, and motif flags never appear in a closed, order-of-operations signature. That is a missed requirement, not a stylistic choice.

**The PM4Py outline is directionally right and locally wrong.** Using `cluster_equivalent_ocel`, a lead type `Order`, id-stripping, and `EXT` marks is what was asked. The coarsening assert is tautological (`nunique().max() <= len(exact)` always holds); the intended check is `nunique().max() <= 1`. `itertuples` on `ocel:activity` is not a reliable attribute. `max_objs` is discussed as a sampling cap without tying it to how that API actually bounds related-object explosion.

Lesser defects: an extra H1 before the mandated sections; S6’s leftover “(X6 counterpart on logistics quality)”; R1’s tie-break quietly using related types; a preferred partition that is almost discrete (only X1/X2 share a class), with coarsening treated as an afterthought rather than a first-class alternative.

What remains good is real: R1/R2/R3 discard lists and ERP claims; A-clusters `{X1,X2,X5,X6,X7}`, `{X3}`, `{X4}`, `{X8}`; T-split of X5 vs X6; flattening and extract-window fragility; X1–X2 / X1–X5 / X1–X6 / X3–X4 borderlines; purpose-specific recommendations. Those do not cancel a wrong T-assignment on the planted substitution case or a broken pairwise argument. Under a strict reading this is a strong B-level solution, not a high score.
