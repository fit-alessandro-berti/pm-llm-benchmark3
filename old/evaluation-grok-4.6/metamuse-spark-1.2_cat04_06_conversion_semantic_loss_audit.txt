The submission is structurally complete and conceptually stronger than most answers to this prompt: it treats the POWL as authoritative, refuses to invent a POWL→BPMN API, treats process-tree failure as a legitimate outcome, and consistently distinguishes layout from semantics. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading, several defects are material, not cosmetic.

**Overclaiming is the central methodological failure.** Section 4 marks INV-01, INV-02, INV-04, INV-06, INV-08, and INV-09 as `validated preserved` and writes as if bounded exploration and exact final-marking checks already succeeded. Nothing was executed. The allowed term `expected preserved` exists precisely for this situation. Using `validated preserved` equates a proposed test harness with evidence, which is the same category error the prompt warns against (“a conversion function returning an object does not prove semantic fidelity”). Petri→Tree is likewise stamped `conversion failed` without a run; the honest cell is `not tested`.

**The validation suite does not test the invariant the author calls “the most fragile.”** INV-05 is specified correctly in Section 1, including the witness `Configure/Inspect` before `Legal Review`. Section 5 never uses that witness. Every Legal-containing positive trace is `Assess/Prepare` then `Legal` then `Configure`. A converter that illegally inserts `optional_legal → verification_loop` (or `prepare_evidence → optional_legal`) would pass every listed positive and negative witness. The same gap exists for the legal POWL trace `Register, Assess, Legal, Prepare, Configure, …` and for Legal between `Configure` and `Inspect` (concurrent children may interleave). Witness C’s claim that such interleaving is “not expressible linearly” is false.

**The exploration bound contradicts the author’s own coverage claim.** Default `max_visible_len=8` / comparison bound `9` cannot accept the 11-event 2-iteration+Legal traces. The docstring says the bound “covers 1-2 loop iterations including Legal option.” It does not. Language-set comparison therefore does not exercise redo behavior with optionality present, while `accepts()` uses a looser bound—so the two checks are not even comparable.

**Section 5 is not executable against the stated PM4Py surface.** `petri_utils.fire_transition(net, marking, t)` is not in the provided API set and is not a stable public helper; firing belongs on `pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics` (`execute` / `weak_execute`).
