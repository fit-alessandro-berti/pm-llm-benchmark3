The write-up hits the required seven-heading format, reconstructs many decision epochs rather than only start order, and is strongest on QA (appointment + myopic FIFO is well supported) and QD (skills, unique-resource jobs, and D06’s reserved idle window are handled carefully). Sections 4–6 are usable: the discrimination tests name real forks, and the data-collection list is tied to actual ambiguities.

Those strengths do not offset material analytical errors.

The QC reconstruction is the largest failure, and it is exactly the failure the task warned against. At 10:32 the eligible set is not {C01, C04, C05}. C06 arrived at 10:25 (est 6). SPT therefore predicts C06, not C04; the table’s “textbook SPT” row is false. At 10:54 C07 (arrived 10:40) is also omitted. Because the primary QC label is built on a start-order reading of the initial backlog rather than the live decision set, the SPT diagnosis is not earned. C04 over C06/C05/C01 is the episode that most needs explanation; it is not analyzed.

QB has the opposite problem: the decision tables are mostly right, the headline is not. 09:31 (resume B01 over B03) and 10:28 (B04 over higher-priority B06) both contradict ongoing priority and fit FIFO plus priority-1 preemption with resume. The 09:48 start of B03 over waiting B05 is correctly flagged, but it is then used to keep “preemptive-resume priority” as primary instead of treating FIFO+urgent-preempt vs priority as the live contest. Remaining-time arithmetic is also slightly off (B01 remaining at 09:48 is 14, not ~16).

QD is over-called at 91. Twenty-four minutes of D-F idle after 11:36 while D10 is feasible before the meeting is a real work-conservation anomaly; “strict per-resource FIFO, not a contradiction” is one hypothesis, not a closed case. QA’s A05 start at 09:05, which made A06’s 09:15 slot impossible, is a milder appointment lookahead tension that is waved through.

Uncertainty language is uneven: QA/QD are written as if settled, QC’s confidence number is appropriately low but for the wrong reason, and assumptions are discussed more than labeled. Section 7’s “QB least identifiable / QA most identifiable” is defensible, but QC is under-sold as an identifiability problem because the missed arrivals were never treated as such.

Net: a structured, often competent process-mining memo with two queue diagnoses that do not survive their own decision sets. Under a strict bar that is well short of a high score.
