**6.5**

The final write-up hits the required eight-section structure and the hard constraints (cross-case dependence, calendars, no silent service/wait collapse, objections treated as unobserved, no `pm4py.read_csv`, no optimality claim). It is a usable design sketch, not a near-flawless one. Under a strict reading, several methodological and implementation gaps are material.

**What holds up.** Scope and state are at the right grain: case attributes, resource calendars, per-activity queues, architect bursts, inspection bundles, and a latent objection cluster. PM4Py usage is mostly conventional (`format_dataframe` with the named columns, `convert_to_event_log`, `get_variants`, `discover_dfg`, `discover_performance_dfg`, `parse_process_tree`) and the tree is correctly labeled a structural skeleton. The experiment table covers baseline, D1–D10, and three distinct stresses. Warm-up is at least conceptually adapted to non-stationarity. Decision metrics include tails, SLA by type, district/architect fairness, utilization, rework, backlog, and robustness. The code separates log handling from a DES stub and is honest that completion-only stamps do not identify service versus wait.

**Simulation design.** Waiting is specified two ways that are not reconciled: residual of inter-event gaps minus a fitted service draw, versus queue delay generated by calendars and capacity. A digital twin needs an explicit calibration loop that tunes service, capacity, and dependence parameters until simulated waits, utilizations, and variant durations match the log. That loop is missing. Several “effects” are written as known facts (D1 cuts missing-doc loops 40%, D3 cuts travel 30%, D7 uptake 80%, D9 cuts notice from 46d to 20d, rework service 1.2×, max three loops, applicant sd = 4d). Those are uncertain design parameters or hypotheses, not estimates. Treating them as point truths undercuts the later uncertainty section. Architect-batch size, travel-time distributions, council-week capacity loss, and bundle probability are named but not given an estimation procedure from a flattened case log.

**Process-mining grounding.** Extra columns that drive routing (`case_type`, `district`, `architect_id`, `requires_*`, `application_value`) are never mapped to PM4Py case/event attributes, so the analysis objects used for variants/DFG would not actually support the data-dependent router. Seasonal and weekday multipliers are taken from the brief rather than specified as quantities to compute from first-event timestamps (e.g., NHPP or GLM of daily counts). Distribution fitting is named (“log-normal or gamma”) without a selection or goodness-of-fit step. Rework-reason text is correctly discarded, but rework *probabilities* by type and exit condition are left as a count ratio with no handling of right-censoring or incomplete loops.

**Experiments and uncertainty.** The matrix is one-at-a-time plus a single combined policy. There is no factor-level design, no planned interactions beyond D10, and no cost/capacity cost for D4/D6. Thirty replications and an 18-month horizon are thin for p90/p95 of an 8% complex-variance class with 260-day tails and calendar-driven legal freezes; CRN is asserted without saying how shared streams would be aligned under bundling and clustered objections. Warm-up is internally inconsistent (“empty queues” and “seed with historical case mix”). No initialization-bias diagnostic, no separation of input versus stochastic uncertainty beyond a tornado list, and the 3-month holdout ignores that the last quarter of a seasonal series is not a neutral out-of-sample window.

**Code skeleton.** `ArrivalGenerator` is required by the prompt and by `SimulationEngine.__init__` but is never defined. Cross-case bundling exists only as a comment. `log_converter` is imported and unused. `ExperimentRunner` does not apply D1–D10, calendars, or metrics. `ResourceCalendar.is_available` always returns `True`, which is acceptable only as a stub if the arrival and dependence generators also exist as stubs; they do not. `list(variants.keys())[:3]` is version-fragile depending on whether `get_variants` returns a dict.

**Net.** Competent structure and constraint compliance, with enough invented point effects, missing calibration, incomplete PM attribute handling, and an unfinished skeleton that this is clearly not near-flawless. A 6.5 reflects a complete, mostly coherent design that would not survive a hard methodological review without substantial revision.
