**6.5**

The design is complete in outline and hits every required section, but it is not close to flawless. Several process-mining and simulation details are wrong, inconsistent, or underspecified.

**What works.** The twin is not an independent-case replay. Non-stationary arrivals (month and Monday multipliers), architect bursts, district batching, and objection clusters are first-class. Waiting is treated as an emergent queueing outcome rather than a hardcoded input, and missing objection events are treated as a proxy, not as observed events. Calendars, applicant delays, and D1–D10 plus three stress cases are all present. The decision section correctly avoids claiming optimality.

**Process-mining / PM4Py errors (serious).** The skeleton unpacks `dfg_discovery.apply(...)` into `(dfg, start_activities, end_activities)`. That API returns a DFG dict, not a triple; the high-level `pm4py.discover_dfg` / `discover_performance_dfg` would. `variant="performance"` is also not the documented variant object. The estimation plan invents `pm4py.stats.get_arrival_distribution` and `pm4py.algo.filtering.log.rework`. `format_dataframe(..., start_timestamp_key=None)` is risky and can collapse start and complete times. Inter-event sojourn columns are computable from completion timestamps and were required “if timestamps allow”; only case duration is computed. The loop redo labels (`Completeness rework`, `Inspection rework`) are not log activities, and tree-to-engine mapping is left vague.

**Simulation-design gaps.** AND-join semantics for Decision drafting (wait for all applicable reviews/notice/inspection) are not in the state model. Appeals appear in scope and then disappear. Fire “2 FTE, Tue–Thu only” and inspector/legal calendars are not turned into an explicit capacity–calendar mapping. Cross-case logic is described in prose but not represented in the skeleton (no batcher, no objection cluster, no calendar object). `engine.run(24*30)` has no units.

**Uncertainty and validation flaws.** Replication design contradicts itself (100 CRN runs vs. a 50×10 outer Bayesian loop). A 6-month empty-system warmup does not cover a full seasonal cycle, and the start date is unspecified. KS tests on ~31k cases will reject any useful model; that is not mentioned. Utilization validation ignores the missing start timestamps that make utilization poorly identified. D2’s “no prior violations” is treated as log-available; it is a data gap. The 3-month holdout is not aligned with seasonality.

**Assumptions / experiments.** The experiment table and trade-offs are usable, but several numbers (travel adders, 60% missing-doc cut, 70% D2 eligibility, 5% D5 rework increase) are stated as if known. Routing is only a comment in the event loop, not a module.

Solid consulting structure; too many concrete technical mistakes for a high score under a strict rubric.
