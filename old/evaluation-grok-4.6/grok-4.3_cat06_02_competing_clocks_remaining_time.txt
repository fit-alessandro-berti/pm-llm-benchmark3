### Queueing treated as if the waiting line did not exist

### What is actually defensible
Format compliance; refusal to emit one fake point forecast; explicit max at the lab join; some path/queue/calendar/dependence labels; recognition that micro staffing and weekend closure matter *in principle*; TE as a high-leverage observation (direction under-specified); chemistry-status-before-18:00 as a reasonable secondary trigger. Day-*names* Mon → Tue → Wed → Thu–Fri as a qualitative envelope are in the right *order* if one discards the dates and the hour counts — that is not enough.

A forecast whose P50 lands on a closed Sunday, whose dates are a weekend off, and that drops a four-job chemistry queue is not close to operational quality. Strictly, this is a structured but unreliable answer.
