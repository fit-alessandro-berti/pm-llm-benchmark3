The write-up is complete in format and correct on the headline diagnosis (WF-net, unsound; `t_join_waived` does not synchronize with analysis). It then fails several load-bearing requirements.

**What is right.** The net is a workflow net: unique source/sink, and every node lies on a source-to-sink path. Soundness fails because the waiver join is a 1-input transition. **Section 3 misses a required derivation.** The prompt asked to show whether `t_authorize` can occur *before or after* `t_analyze`, and to follow the waiver route. The only firing sequence is

`t_register → t_split → t_analyze → t_waive_review → t_join_waived → t_authorize`

so authorization is shown only *after* analysis. The stronger, and legally enabled, waiver interleaving is never derived:

`t_register → t_split → t_waive_review → t_join_waived → t_authorize`

which reaches `{p_analysis: 1, sink: 1}` with `t_analyze` still enabled. That is the business-rule failure (authorize without completed analysis), not merely an orphan in `p_analysis_done`. **Section 1 understates the same point.** The verdict frames the harm as a leftover token / incomplete consumption. That is true of one interleaving and false as the principal business consequence: authorization is enabled with analysis still pending.

**Repair B is not a repair.** Adding `p_waived → t_join_normal` while leaving `p_review_done → t_join_normal` makes `t_join_normal` an AND of mutually exclusive XOR outcomes. **The firing helper inverts Petri-net semantics.** After an enabledness check, output arcs (`transition → place`) decrement the output place and input arcs (`place → transition`) increment the input place. The helper would not replay the counterexample it is supposed to print. That violates the explicit “check enabledness rather than silently moving tokens” requirement: enabledness is checked, then tokens are moved in the wrong direction.

The required before/after-analysis marking argument, the second distinct *correct* preserve-intent repair, the firing helper, and the soundness/trace claims are not. That is well below a high score under a strict reading of the spec.
