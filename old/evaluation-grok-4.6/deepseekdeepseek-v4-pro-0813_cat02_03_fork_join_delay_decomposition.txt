**8.2**

The final document is analytically strong: the fork–join identities are correct, the 12-case arithmetic checks out, criticality is based on last completion rather than mean service, and the three counterfactuals correctly separate join-ready savings from flow-time savings when `final_start` is fixed.

**What is solid**
- Case-level table and the four worked cases (F02, F04, F07, F11) match the timestamps. Additive check \(U + FJ + D + S_F = T\) holds for every case.
- Critical counts (A 2 / B 8 / C 2), sync means (11.0 / 4.3 / 30.4), queue+service (50 / 56.7 / 30.6), and CF totals (15 / 92.5 / 88) are correct.
- Ranking treats A’s constant 50-minute service as *not* decisive; B’s queue+service and frequency control completion. Partial CF2 cases (F05, F10) and the F01 5-minute (not 10) A cut are handled properly.
- Downstream masking is stated clearly. Limits of inference cover causality, unobserved branch capacity, preemption, and whether final delay is a queue vs. a policy.

**Deductions (strict)**
- Section 1 shows \(\sum_i(Q_i+S_i+W_i)=3\,FJ\), which is the right parallel-overlap idea, but it never *explicitly* sums the three sync waits and shows those intervals sit inside the critical remaining service. That was a required demonstration, not just the triple-count identity.
- Worked cases omit \(W^{\mathrm{sync}}\) even though sync waiting is a named decomposition component.
- CF2 reports 2.5 / 12.5 minutes with no note that the log is minute-resolution; those figures are an unstated modeling choice.
- Mean join-ready when B is critical is 10:41:15, shown as 10:41.
- Section 6 wraps PM4Py in `try/except` and, after `format_dataframe`, still passes `case_id="case"` / `activity_key="activity"`. That is unlikely to be a clean, reproducible PM4Py call.
- “Throughput” in the redesigns is used loosely for a closed 12-case sample; flow-time reduction is not the same as a higher completion rate.
- Final-station utilization (~57%) depends on an implicit 09:08–12:40 window that is not justified as the right capacity denominator.

None of these break the argument. They keep the score well below a near-flawless 9–10.
