**6.8**

The answer is structurally complete, uses the required labels, correctly buries A/B, and treats second review as non-substitutable in the main line. Under a strict reading it is still not near-flawless. The top plan’s timing logic is internally inconsistent, one process hedge is wrong, F’s legal basis is under-analyzed, and several numbers are asserted rather than derived.

**1. Ranked table — mostly right, several sloppy cells**

Ranking a parallel-screen + adaptive S4/S2 plan above pure C, then sequential D, then F/E/G, then A/B is the right *family* of ranking. A and B last, G as an unenabled exception, and D strictly below the parallel composite are all correct.

Defects:

- Rank 1 is labeled `CONFORMANT NOW` while the same S2 interrupt, standing alone, is `CONFORMANT IF STATED CONDITION HOLDS`. Same act, two labels.
- Rank 1 benefit `10–30 k€` is optimistic and not computed. Deadline value is 20–35k only if execution is both timely *and* a true approval. Observational P(approve) is 0.55–0.75; under the S4 branch, review 35–75 min after a 35–65 min wait is often past 90 min. A coherent product is closer to the low single digits to mid-teens, plus option value from screening—not 10–30k of “deadline protection.”
- F’s benefit cell is `0`. Regulatory tail-risk avoided is the *point* of F; stuffing it only into a parenthetical while putting 15–80k entirely in “harm” biases the table against a control action the prose later defends.
- C’s harm band `20–65 k€` mixes P-884 false-reject residual with P-901 spillover without stating the mix. P-901’s loss is never given; “comparable high-value stakes” is invented.
- Eight rows for seven actions is allowed, but D is just the sequential dominated variant of rank 1 and adds little once the composite exists.

**2. Continuations — one real process error**

Top-five traces are compact and have the right approve / reject / hold+SAR spine. The Auto-Clear hedge is not a quibble. After “still perform independent second review” the answer adds that a fresh score satisfying Auto-Clear could open a “lighter path.” Auto-Clear is the *ex ante* bypass when there is no alert and risk &lt; 40, *instead of* Analyst Review. This case already completed Analyst Review and a full evidence cycle under initial risk 78. Alternate screening does not reopen Rule 3. Rule 6 is also state-based (risk ≥ 70 *or* unresolved fuzzy match) before Approve/Executed; a vendor refresh can change whether second review is still mandatory, but it cannot send the case down Auto-Clear. That hedge is exactly the “re-screen substitutes for a control” pattern the rubric flags.

Smaller gaps: Hold+SAR is named without restating that SAR filing does not authorize execution. Transportability is mentioned, not localized (rushed S2 review vs. S4 vs. post-screen). G/A/B are only sketched, which was permitted.

**3. Benefit/harm reasoning — right dominance, weak arithmetic**

The dominance argument against A/B is the strongest part of the memo: unbounded/asymmetric compliance harm plus non-transportable observational predictions means SLA forecasts cannot legalize a skipped control. Information value of a non-substituting re-screen is correctly treated as option value.

What is missing is any first-order expected-value sketch for the *conformant* set. The text restates the given ranges and then jumps to ranking. P-901 spillover is “0.12–0.28 × comparable stakes” with no stake. Rank 1 vs 2 is therefore a qualitative preference to protect P-901, not a shown tradeoff. That is acceptable as a method, but then the table should not pretend to € precision.

**4. Conformance — A/B handled well; F under-argued**

Rules 6, 7, 9, 10 are applied correctly to A, B, and G. The statement that forecasts cannot promote A/B is explicit and right.

F is the miss. The answer reads Rule 6 as applying only before Approve/Executed and concludes reject may skip the second officer if an internal evidence threshold is met. It never confronts Rule 8: “After the required review(s), the case may be Approved, Rejected, or held.” Plural “review(s)” plus risk 72 and an unresolved match is a live reading that second officer is required *before reject as well*. Validity of F therefore needs two conditions, not one: (i) policy threshold for reject-on-unresolved-match actually met by the validated file, and (ii) a determination that Rule 8 does not still require the second review. Only (i) is discussed. G’s enabling conditions are stated correctly.

**5. Spillovers — right sensitivity, broken decision time**

S2-vs-S4, API quota, and “don’t burn the window on an unenabled override” are all present. The 35 vs 65 min sensitivity is the right comparative static.

The operating rule for the winning plan does not survive its own numbers.

- Review is 35–75 min *after* the officer is free, plus 5–15 min to record/execute.
- Waiting for S4 at 45 min already implies a finish around 85–135 min.
- “Decide at ~30–40 min” whether to interrupt S2 is worse: 30–40 min idle + 35–75 min S2 review finishes at 70–130 min, so the fallback often misses P-884 *and* still hits P-901.
- Section 5’s 30–40 min decision point and section 6’s “wait if S4 expected within ~45 min” are not the same policy.

A coherent composite queries S4 ETA at t≈0 (in parallel with the API call) and interrupts S2 immediately unless ETA is on the *low* end of 35–65 *and* a fast review is plausible. As written, the top plan can dominate C only by assuming a wait that its own duration ranges say will miss the deadline.

**6. Final recommendation**

Primary + fallback + medium confidence + rerank triggers (S4 ETA, screen result, stale risk score, P-901, API quota) are the right shape. Confidence is honestly below the conformance labels. The recommendation inherits the timing defect: it still says wait for S4 if expected within ~45 min.

**Net.** This is a strong practitioner memo, not an exam-ceiling one. Hard constraints are respected; A/B are not laundered through EV; parallel gathering and P-901 are in the ranking. It loses a large share of the top band for (1) an Auto-Clear escape hatch that the process does not give, (2) a 30–45 min wait/fallback that is inconsistent with 35–75 min review and a 90 min clock, (3) an incomplete Rule 8 analysis of F, and (4) benefit/harm figures that are not implied by the given ranges. Those are not polish nits on this task.
