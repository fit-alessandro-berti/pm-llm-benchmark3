The answer is structurally complete, correctly refuses a blanket copy of Site A, and is strong on confounders, statutory guards, bottleneck shift, and implementation-versus-mechanism separation. It is not close to flawless. Under a strict reading, several quantitative, causal-graph, and internal-consistency problems keep it well below the top band.

**What holds up.** Section 1 is the best part: the fork–join is treated as the only seriously transportable fragment, originals are distinguished from Document Check, and Site A’s 9-hour median move is never treated as an identified effect. The recommendation (`conditional go`), the guarded eligibility rules, and the dual-owner / blocked-readiness distinction are the right operational conclusions. The pilot’s logging split (created / claimed / started / entered-at / lock state) is the correct way to stop timestamp reform from masquerading as concurrency.

**Causal graph is incomplete relative to the site difference that matters most.** `originals_required` and `digital_documents` only point at `true_overlap`. Site B’s 18-hour originals delay is a first-order driver of waiting and cycle time for 62% of volume *whether or not* anyone parallelizes. `legal_capacity` likewise dies at `waiting_before_assessment` and never reaches `rework`, `appeal`, or `cycle_time`, even though the legal/rework scenario is later used to threaten the tail. The required node list is present, but the graph is an edge list, not an adjacency list, and `legal_review_required` is introduced without being integrated as cleanly as the mandated variables.

**Quantitative transport is asserted more than derived.** The 3.4-hour service-overlap bound is correct and important. Almost nothing else is backed by arithmetic. There is no decomposition of Site A’s 41→32 hour change into mix (48%→40% high-complexity), +2 document FTE, API latency, huddles, and residual overlap. Without that, the scenario ranges (−4 to −7 hours eligible, p90 +4 to +15, legal p90 +5 to +20) are plausible guesses, not mapped estimates. The provided DFG is underused for the same job: 2,108 `Originals Receipt → Document Check` edges match 62% of 3,400 exactly, 1,360 `Assessment → Legal Review` edges match the 40% legal share, and 1,160 `Identity Check → Document Check` edges are direct evidence of already-serial verification. Those counts should have tightened eligible share and slack, not sat in the prompt unused.

**38% is treated as if it were the digital-eligible population.** “Originals not legally required” is not “confirmed complete digital packet.” The modifiers table knows this; the scenario table forgets it and repeatedly uses 38% as the true-parallel share. Phase-1 eligibility (digital ∧ non-legal ∧ not highest complexity) is then incompatible with the power target of 300 digital cases per arm in 18–20 weeks. At ~131 completions/week, a 15–25% first-wave slice cannot support that N, and 500/arm for a p90 claim is even further out of reach. Half-day cohort randomization also does not block spillover through four identity, seven document, three assessor, and two legal staff; the interference controls name the problem more than they solve it.

**Small but real specification cracks.** Pilot WIP is both “six active paired cases in the lane” and “two per identity reviewer” (up to eight). The textual supplement loop can re-require originals; the POWL redo is only `Request Supplement → Document Check → Assessment`. Legal Approve/Reject are modeled as activities after `LOOP(Legal Review, redo)` rather than as exits of Legal Review, which is a fidelity loss relative to the DFG (`Legal Review → Approve/Reject/Supplement`). Appeals in six-month windows are not flagged for 90-day right-censoring. Optimistic eligible savings of 4–7 hours already exceed the service bound and lean on the same confounded Site A waiting drop the essay otherwise refuses to transport.

**Code is directionally right and not a sequential dodge.** Process-tree parse, Petri conversion, explicit POWL edges into Assessment, XOR document routes, and a LOOP legal branch match the brief. Comments on what POWL cannot encode are accurate. Remaining defects are the supplement/originals mismatch, post-loop decisions, and unrun, try/except-wrapped soundness/playout—acceptable illustration, not a verified model.

Net: analogical structure and policy judgment are well above competent; identification discipline is good; quantitative transport, graph completeness, and internal numeric consistency are not tight enough for a high score under the stated rubric.
