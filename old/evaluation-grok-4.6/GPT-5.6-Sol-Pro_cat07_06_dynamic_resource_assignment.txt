## Material flaws

**1. The scoring system is not used.**  
\(U\), slack, \(A\), \(M\), continuity bands, pressure, and the two weight vectors are presented as the policy. The queue is then assigned by qualitative narrative. \(F\) is never defined separately from \(A\). No candidate is scored. No hours are estimated even though the author’s own pressure formula is hours-based. The math is ornamental.

**2. Eva is overloaded by the author’s own rules.**  
Eva already owns two imminent high-priority activities (C005, 8h; C016, 7h) and has 5.0 hours left today, 8 open cases, medium overtime. The table then *adds* C010 finalization and C015 shadowing, putting her at the cap. That directly violates the workload/pressure logic in Section 2. C010 has 20h slack, complexity 0.38, and a legal owner (Bruno, Approval 2). Leaving it with Bruno, or delaying it, is the option the policy’s own overtime *and* fairness rules imply. C015 shadowing can wait 27 hours; putting it on the tightest person is indefensible.

**3. Celine’s load is under-counted in substance.**  
On paper she is at 3–4 duties. In the same batch she executes a repeated diagnosis (C003), a high-complexity high-priority rework with a 6h SLA (C007), coaches C009, remains owner of urgent enterprise Security (C001), and is the escalation path for C016. The text admits coordination cost and then ignores it. C007 alone can consume her day.

**4. The owner/executor split is used to game continuity.**  
For C001 the split is justified (Celine is Security 1; the case needs a specialist; handover cost is high). For C003 it is weaker. This is a repeated diagnosis loop. The person who needs the context is the diagnostician, not a nominal owner who is barred from the work because of overtime. Keeping David as owner so `reassignment_count` stays at 2 is a bookkeeping trick unless the process really has a split accountability model. The log schema does not.

**5. Jonas becomes the residual dump.**  
C006+C012 as a batch is correct and rule-compliant. Adding C013 (new, low, 72h, Eligibility 2) as his fourth slot is lazy. Amal is Eligibility 3, already the owner-pattern worker for routine eligibility, and only has two batch duties. Parking a 72h case on the last Jonas slot reduces reserve for Quality/Rework and is not required by SLA, skill, or continuity.

**6. Training arithmetic is generous, then excused.**  
2/9 = 22.2% only if skill-0 shadowing on C015 counts. Eva and Jonas are both Security 0; they cannot become supervised executors under the stated Security rule (1–2 with Gita/Hugo). The author discloses that hands-on training is 1/9. That honesty is good. Counting C015 anyway to clear the 20% bar, while hanging the shadow on an overloaded Eva, is still a target-seek.
