**7.2**

The write-up is complete, well structured, and mostly process-mining-literate: it hits every required heading, keeps Mira’s role underdetermined, treats centrality as neither automatically good nor bad, keeps vendors/bots in view, and does not recommend removal. The numerical citations from the tables are accurate. Those are necessary conditions, not a near-flawless answer.

**Process-mining validity.** The core SNA story is right in outline (directed brokerage vs. undirected co-production vs. A–B–A re-entry). It is not careful enough at the next level. Working-together rates of 4–8% are treated as “low/modest” without a base-rate correction: Mira is only in 22% of claims, so Mira–Carla at 8% is about 36% of *her* cases, and is close to the 350 Mira→Carla handovers on a ~4,100-case base. The interesting fact is therefore not “they rarely co-occur,” but that co-occurrence is essentially the handoff itself—short, non-repeated, and diluted across many counterparties. The scenario already gives the decisive clue (“disappears from the same case for several events”); the final text under-uses it and leans on a weaker “short events / one-touch” gloss. Exception-flag filtering at `level='event'` is proposed without asking whether the flag is case-level or event-level; truncated traces would distort every subsequent network.

**Hypotheses.** The three mandated frames are present, plus useful extras (automation monitor, gatekeeper, measurement artifact). They are not really competing mechanisms so much as overlapping descriptions of one pattern. Discriminating predictions are thin: several hypotheses forecast “more Mira on exception cases” or “p90 moves when she is absent,” which the log already shows. H6 is the best-calibrated of the set because it is actually bounded by the absence window.

**PM4Py section.** Coverage of the required technique families is there; the snippets are not reliable. `discover_subcontracting_network(log, n=2)` is unlikely to be the public signature; `exception_flag` filtered as the strings `'True'`/`'False'` can silently return an empty log; EventLog and DataFrame are used interchangeably for `get_service_time` / `insert_case_service_waiting_time` without the start/complete lifecycle the service-time metrics need. `selected_variants` is left undefined.

**Recommendation and summary.** Immediate / monitor / do-not-change is the right split, and refusing to delete the coordination function is correct. The executive summary then collapses the multi-hypothesis body into a single label (“human exception switchboard”) and the phrase “limited control over discretionary routing” is ambiguous: it can be read as Mira lacking control, which is the opposite of the compliance point. Word count is in range; vendors drop out of the summary entirely.

**Net.** Strong exam answer, not a tight organizational-mining memo. The missing base-rate on working-together, the non-discriminating hypotheses, the API/method slips, and the single-cause executive summary are enough to keep it well below the top of the scale.
