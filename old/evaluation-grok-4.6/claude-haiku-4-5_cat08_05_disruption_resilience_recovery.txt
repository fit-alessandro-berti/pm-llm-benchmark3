**Material errors.**

1. **Pattern K is mislocated.** Stabilization DFG places `Inventory reservation → Reconcile manual reservation → Pick` (1,400 / 900 / 300). The answer repeatedly treats K as post-dispatch, post-invoice correction. That is L (`Invoice close → Audit remediation`: 2,300). The error contaminates the keep/modify/retire call, Section 3, and the recovery model.

2. **Dual-control is assigned to ambient product.** Dual-control missing 2.9% is a controlled-substance control gap. Saying the gap “likely applies to ambient orders outside the sample” is conceptually wrong and is repeated in Section 3. Ambient product does not carry that control.

3. **DEA Form 106 is the wrong instrument.** Form 106 is theft/loss, not a dispatch or release log. Recommending a “DEA Form 106 equivalent” on routine handoff is a serious compliance error in this setting.

4. **Evidence is invented.** “No rework or complaints reported in this cohort” (C), “reconciliation validated most snapshot accuracy” (D), and “K recovered most traceability” (E) are not in the log. Rework 12.5% and traceability 9.5% cut the other way.

5. **Load comparison is wrong.** 5,600 T2 cases are not “13% of normal load.” Baseline is ~2,000/day over 21 days; T2 daily volume is comparable or higher. The phrase treats a short window’s count as a utilization collapse.
