The answer is strong on the intended substance—confounded before/after evidence, illegal startability, bottleneck shift, and a segmented pilot—but it is not nearly flawless. Several process-mining, quantitative, and API claims are wrong or overprecise, and two hard word limits are missed. Under a strict reading those are not nits.

**What is done well.** The structural comparison table hits every required feature and draws the right design implication: do not create `Document Check` where originals are statutory. Site A’s 9-hour median move is decomposed and never treated as an identified treatment effect. Co-interventions (API, headcount, mix, huddles, timestamps) are separated from the AND-split mechanism. The transport graph includes every required node plus signed mechanism comments. S5 is a genuine “median up, tail down” case. The candidate intervention preserves statutory order, splits digital vs originals concurrency, and the POWL edges correctly AND-join into `Assessment`. The pilot’s fidelity-vs-outcome logic (high overlap + no span cut = mechanism failure; low overlap = implementation failure; span down but cycle time flat = bottleneck shift) is the best part of the answer. `conditional go` is the only defensible recommendation.

**Material errors.**

1. *Medians are treated as linear.* Section 5 states that if 38% of cases improve by 4–7 h, the pooled median moves by ~1.5–3 h (and analogously 53–70% → 2.5–4.5 h). A median is not a weighted average of subgroup effects. Digital cases already skip an 18 h originals delay and likely sit *below* Site B’s 44 h median; making the left tail faster can leave the 50th percentile unchanged. S2’s pooled median range is therefore unsupported, and S1/S6 inherit the same mixture formula. That is a direct miss on “quantitative scenario analysis.”

2. *DFG sum > n is misread as data corruption.* `Register → Identity Check` (2,050) + `Register → Originals Receipt` (1,860) = 3,910 > 3,400 is the expected fingerprint of a parallel split after `Register`, which Site B’s rules already allow. The answer leads with “duplicated Register events, same-timestamp ties, or back-entered events” and freezes a pilot baseline on that. Complementary overclaim: “in the other half identity is done after originals arrive.” Directly-follows counts do not partition the complementary order; intervening steps and true concurrency are unidentified. Assumption 7 then hardens the misreading.

3. *Rework split is over-identified.* 360/3,400 = 10.6 pp is not “6.4 pp non-supplement rework” unless every supplement case is inside the 17% rework definition and the 345 completed loops are a subset without double counting. That figure is then reused as a quality prior.

4. *Load-ratio arithmetic is off.* Identity 3.4 h × 3,400 / 4 FTE vs document 8.1 h × 3,400 / 7 FTE is ~1.36:1, not “roughly 1.5:1.”

**Format and model-fidelity issues.**

- Section 2’s prose is ~310 words against a 180–280 limit; Section 7’s mechanism/implementation note is ~240 against a 220 limit.
- `sound, diagnostics = pm4py.check_soundness(...)` does not match the usual pm4py 2.7 contract (boolean, unless diagnostics are requested). `play_out(..., noTraces=, maxTraceLength=)` is not the public parameters-dict API. Either can throw at runtime, which matters in a section whose job is executable model construction.
- `role_specialization → true_overlap` is underspecified: separate roles *help* within-case parallelism (two people) and *hurt* cross-pool balancing. Cross-training at A can also produce logged “overlap” from one reviewer holding both tasks. That alternative measurement story is not examined.
- Parallelization under a six-task WIP cap consumes two slots per case and can cut case-level WIP in half; that interaction is listed as mitigation, not as a failure mode.
- Power: a 4 h median shift with log-SD ≈ 0.6 is closer to ~600+ per arm than 450, and four treatment versions are powered as if they were a two-arm test. “30 cases per DFG cell” is too thin for the claimed comparisons.
- “Both process trees are sequences of the same labels” is false of Site B (originals, legal, supplement).
- S6’s p90 gain leans on an untested 25–40% supplement cut from a new pre-screen that Site A never ran; confidence 40 is high for that leap.

**What remains intact.** None of the above overturns the core transport result: copy the AND-split only where both checks are startable; keep originals and legal as constraints; do not import the 9 h / 18 h headlines; instrument overlap rather than trust it. The recommendation, eligibility rules, and stop/expansion triggers still follow from the process structure. The grade is held down by the quantitative mixture error, the DFG concurrency miss, the likely-broken soundness/playout calls, and repeated overprecision—not by the analogical frame, which is largely right.
