7.4

This is a strong, well-structured design that meets the required section contract and avoids the main failure modes (independent-case-only simulation, collapsed wait/service, treating missing objections as observed, `pm4py.read_csv`, optimality claims). It is not nearly flawless. Several modeling inconsistencies, underspecified mechanisms, and over-precise decision rules keep it well below a top score.

**What is solid.** The boundary, entities, and clocks are right: municipal service, queue, calendar closure, applicant delay, and third-party delay are separated. Cross-case structure (architect bursts, latent neighborhood objections, inspection bundles with a max wait) is first-class, not an afterthought. Parameter estimation is process-mining-aware: arrivals from submit events, conditional routing rather than raw variant shares, HR calendars as capacity priors, chronological split, cluster-preserving resampling. Assumptions are labeled and mostly testable. The experiment table covers B0, D1–D10, extra packages, and four stress cases with qualitative trade-offs. Uncertainty treatment (Welch warm-up, CRN, paired intervals, outer parameter loop, GPD/mixture tails, “no demonstrated difference”) is professionally framed. Validation and decision sections are complete on paper. The skeleton follows the stated PM4Py conventions, refuses to invent start timestamps, and keeps DES interfaces out of the DFG layer.

**Material flaws and unclarities.**

1. **“Digital twin” is mostly an offline DES study.** Current municipal WIP, live calendars, and rolling recalibration are mentioned only as an initialization option. A twin used to compare interventions should start from today’s open cases and be re-identified as new log data arrive. That operational loop is missing.

2. **Control flow is not reconciled with the narrative.** The given tree has loops only for completeness and inspection. The text says rework also occurs after planning, legal check, and appeal. Section 1 asserts that rework “returns to the appropriate point” but never specifies those points, the state that triggers them, or how an appeal re-enters drafting/legal. The parsed tree is then unused by `Router`, so the executable policy model is unspecified.

3. **Unacknowledged deviation from the given tree.** Section 1 treats public notice as concurrent with planning/fire/environment. The supplied tree places `X(tau, Public-notice)` *after* the `+` block. That may be domain-reasonable; it is not marked as a modeling choice versus the specification.

4. **Prompt-specific resource rules are genericized away.** Monday portal-dedicated clerk, two simple-renovation-only planners, district travel (D3/D5), and 40% environmental reservation are the actual bottlenecks. They appear only as “skill eligibility” / “calendars include…”. Without those predicates, D3, D4, D6, and D7 cannot be identified.

5. **D2/D8 quality is a footnote, not a metric.** Skipping inspections or triaging environment is allowed to win on duration/SLA because false-negative harm is not a simulated outcome—only a “requires review” sentence. The decision rule can therefore select a faster, less safe policy.

6. **Experiment statistics are cookbook and slightly wrong.** S1 lists a Monday multiplier of 2.2× as a *stress* level; that is the historical baseline. 200–500 full-system replications of a multi-year, 31k-case, clustered DES are not costed, and there is no ranking-and-selection / indifference-zone / multiplicity control across 15+ policies. “90% of scenarios / 80% of replications” and “70–85% utilization” are arbitrary precision.

7. **Warm-up is tight for the tail the study cares about.** Complex-variance p90 is 260 days. A 365-day warm-up plus WIP Welch plots can look stable while the age mix of long cases is still filling. One long-case generation is not a conservative design for p95/p99 and aging policies (D6).

8. **Code nits that matter for the “PM4Py conventions” bar.** `discover_performance_dfg` is assigned as one object while `discover_dfg` is unpacked to three (APIs match; the skeleton is inconsistent). `heapq` is unused. `parse_process_tree` success does not feed the engine. `inspection_eligible` is logically correct but only if `prior_violations is False`; unknown violations are handled, yet `prior_violations` is never estimated in §2.

The piece would be in the high 8s if the tree/narrative gap, live-state twin loop, quality outcomes, and resource special cases were specified with the same care as the uncertainty prose. Under a strict reading, those are not nits.
