### Fatal calendar and parallelism errors

Incubation started Friday 14:00 and is technically ready **46–54 h later**, i.e. **Sunday 12:00–20:00**, not “until Monday 08:00” and not Friday evening. Automated incubation runs through the weekend; readout does not. Routine microbiology staffing is Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00. Without on-call authorization, the first legal readout window is **Monday 08:00**. With on-call (probability 0.25–0.45), Sunday afternoon/evening plus 1–3 h is possible. The answer never uses the on-call parameter and never computes Sunday readiness.

QA cannot start until **both** usable branch results exist. A Friday 20:00–20:30 acceptance/release is therefore impossible on every path. Treating Friday 18:00 as the earliest terminal time in **all four** scenarios is not a rounding issue; it is a process-logic failure.

The same section that says microbiology is ready Monday 08:00–09:00 then releases the batch Friday 20:30. That is not an approximation; it is a contradiction.

### Section 2–4 numbers are incoherent

- Scenario times are all Friday night, then Section 3 sometimes moves the same scenarios to Monday, then Section 4 returns to Friday 20:30 as “most plausible.”
- Remaining-time conversions are fake: Friday 22:00–24:00 is written as “48–48 h.” From Friday 15:30 to Friday 22:00 is **6.5 h**, not 48 h. Every band repeats “48–48 h,” including P80/P90 Monday windows. There is no remaining-time calculation.

A defensible envelope, even with crude arithmetic, is roughly: earliest **Monday late morning / midday 22 Sep** if chemistry residual is short and QA is free; central **Monday afternoon–evening** (on the order of **~45–55 calendar hours** from 15:30 Friday); P80 into **Tuesday–Wednesday** via QA queue, documentation query (4–12 **working** hours), or chemistry spillover/rerun; P90/severe tail **several calendar days to more than a week** if a laboratory investigation or confirmed contamination/excursion path realizes. Incubation is still 46–54 h. Confirmation **fattens and lengthens** the rework/investigation tail. That is the opposite update.
