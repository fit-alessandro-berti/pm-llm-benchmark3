**6.2**

The answer’s causal core is strong: it refuses to treat Site A’s 9-hour median drop as an identified effect, rejects copying all-case simultaneous `Document Check` creation, and ties transport to partial-order preconditions rather than a shared control-flow cartoon. Format compliance is mostly exact. Several substantive errors still keep it well short of a high score.

**What holds up.** Sections 1, 3, 4, 6, and 9 do the job the rubric asks for. The comparison table covers the required process features. Co-interventions (API, added FTE, mix shift, huddles, timestamp artifact) are treated as confounders, not as part of the treatment. The candidate intervention keeps statutory `Originals Receipt → Document Check` and only parallelizes what can actually start. `conditional go` with a digital, non-legal first stratum is the right decision class. The eight-assumption register is usable.

**Model fidelity is a clear fail (Section 8).** This is not a cosmetic API quibble. The required POWL construction invents `POWL` / `POWLNode` / `POWLEdge` plus `add_node`/`add_edge`, then buries the guesswork in try/except fallbacks. Current PM4Py POWL is `Transition`, `StrictPartialOrder` (with `order.add_edge`), and `OperatorPOWL` for XOR/LOOP. Because those constructors exist, “POWL cannot encode optional legal review or a supplement loop” is false. The code therefore (i) makes `Legal Review` and a one-shot supplement path effectively mandatory, (ii) collapses `Approve`/`Reject` into `Decision`, and (iii) unrolls a loop instead of encoding one. `convert_to_petri_net(..., variant='accepting')` is also not the real accepting-net API. For a section whose explicit criterion is model fidelity, this is a large deduction.

**Quantitative and structural slips.** The power claim is wrong: a two-sample test with δ = 4h, σ = 20h, 80% power, α = 0.05 is on the order of ~390 per arm, not “~100.” Optimistic scenario “eligible share 100%” overstates transport: 62% of Site B cases cannot start `Document Check` after `Register`. The DFG is underused. `Identity Check → Originals Receipt` (1,020) plus `Register → Originals Receipt` (1,860) already suggest identity often finishes before originals arrive, so “run identity during the 18h originals delay” is not a fresh 3.4h saving for most of that stratum. `originals_required → true_overlap` and `API_latency → true_overlap` in the graph are directionally sloppy: originals block document overlap; API latency changes service time, not concurrency.

**Pilot is conceptually right, operationally loose.** Separating true overlap from recorded overlap is the correct fidelity test. Case-level randomization on shared 4/7/3/2 pools is acknowledged but not fully solved; T1 vs T2 reintroduces the huddle co-intervention the rest of the memo tries to isolate. Stop rules and strata are good.

**Net.** Analogical and transport reasoning is well above average. Invented POWL, a bad sample-size calculation, over-crediting the originals stratum, and a misleading selection graph are enough, under a strict bar, to keep this in the mid-6s rather than the 8–9 range.
