The most serious problem is an internal numerical contradiction the answer both creates and then relies on. Instead the answer uses both sets of numbers as if they were jointly precise.

None of these sink the analysis, but they are exactly the kind of sloppiness the prompt’s PM4Py list was there to discipline.

What remains is still a strong, usable memo: six hypotheses with mechanisms, limits, and log predictions; calibrated risks with no invented misconduct; a sequenced recommendation that preserves the coordination function.
