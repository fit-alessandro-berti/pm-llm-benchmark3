**3.1**

The answer has the required headings and a vaguely sensible high-level instinct (do not copy Site A onto every Site B case). Under the stated rubric that is not enough. Analogical reasoning, causal identification, process structure, and model fidelity are repeatedly wrong.

**Process structure.** Site B is treated as a nearly sequential pipeline. It is not. Identity Check and Originals Receipt may both start after Register; Document Check is blocked by originals *only when originals are legally required*; Assessment already waits on both checks; Legal Review is a 40% post-assessment branch, not a universal successor. The comparison table therefore misstates concurrency, prerequisites, and loops. Site A after is said to drop the six-task WIP cap; the prompt never says that. Site B case locking is listed as “same as Site A”; that is the opposite of the evidence (A supports simultaneous ownership; B’s legacy lock is a first-order barrier). High-complexity at B is given as 40%; 40% is legal-review volume, and high-complexity is 48%. Timestamp reliability, an explicitly required feature, is absent. Overlap is confused with a loop, and Site A’s 8% overlap is attached to the *after* period.

**Transport graph.** The adjacency list inverts the required selection convention (`S_x -> x`) and invents nodes (`false_overlap`, `true_digital_documents`) while omitting almost every mechanism the prompt named. There is no path from `treatment_parallelization` through `true_overlap` and capacities to waiting time and cycle time; no blocking role for `originals_required` or `IT_case_lock`; no confounding arrows from `queue_huddles` or `API_latency`. The prose is far below the 180–280 word requirement and calls queue huddles a shared mechanism even though they are a Site A co-intervention.

**Identification.** Section 3 treats the 9-hour median drop as “parallel verification reduced cycle time.” The prompt forbids that. Mix shift (48% → 40% high-complexity), +2 verification FTE, registry API upgrade, huddles, and better timestamps are not given their own rows and are not treated as confounders. Rework is described as having “decreased slightly from 11.8% to 12.4%”—an arithmetic inversion, then labeled an improvement. Overlap 8% → 71%, p90, document-check service time, and FTE change are missing.

**Modifiers and scenarios.** Required rows for assessment bottleneck and timestamp back-entry are missing. “Digital claims (90%)” is Site A’s channel, not Site B’s. Legal-review “bypass” is not the failure mode implied by the proposed treatment. No scenario has the required signature of a better median and a worse tail. Optimistic and digital-only rows use ~90–100% eligibility and cycle times *below* Site A’s already-confounded after period, which is impossible under B’s 62% originals rule, 18 h originals delay, specialized pools (4/7/3/2), and case lock. The 90% digital-only share is a hard quantitative error; feasible digital-only eligibility is on the order of 38%.

**Intervention and POWL.** Creating Document Check immediately after Register for every case is correctly narrowed, but Originals Receipt is omitted, Legal Review is placed *before* Assessment, and the supplement loop is ordered incorrectly. The partial order does not encode `Document Check` waiting on originals when required, nor XOR close-out after legal review.

**Pilot.** Case-level 50/50 randomization on shared 4/7/3/2 pools without interference controls cannot identify a transport effect. Power/minimum-support, primary vs secondary outcomes, fidelity of *true* overlap vs back-entered timestamps, and statutory-violation monitoring are missing. The write-up does not separate mechanism failure (originals delay dominates; lock prevents overlap; assessors/legal saturate) from implementation failure.

**PM4Py.** The code does not use `pm4py.parse_process_tree` on the given Site A trees, does not call `convert_to_petri_net`, does not build a `StrictPartialOrder`/`OperatorPOWL` model, and does not call `check_is_workflow_net` / `check_soundness`. It imports non-existent APIs, reads fictional XML, and constructs a Petri net from a tree by wishful methods. This section is non-functional.

**Recommendation and assumptions.** “Conditional go” is defensible, but the required 250–400 word argument is a short paragraph with no operational expansion/modify/abandon triggers. Assumption 2 (“originals do not block parallel verification”) is false on the stated law; assumption 8 is circular (the pilot *is* at Site B). Several “consequences if false” do not follow.

Net: correct skeleton, systematically incorrect substance on the quantities, constraints, and causal graph that determine transport.
