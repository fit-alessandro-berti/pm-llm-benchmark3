**7.3**

The design is substantively strong on the criteria that matter most—DES scope, calendars, non-stationary clustered arrivals, latent objections, service/wait identification, experiment structure, and uncertainty—but it is not close to flawless. Several concrete defects keep it well below a high score.

**What is solid.** Sections 1–7 follow the required structure and mostly respect the constraints: the twin is not an independent-case model; resources have calendars; objections are a latent mixture/shared shock rather than observed events; service and waiting are not collapsed; optimality is explicitly refused. Cross-case coupling (architect Neyman–Scott bursts, district inspection bundles, objection groups) is first-class. The experiment matrix covers B0, D1–D10, and four stress scenarios, including a policy×stress overlay. Warm-up as periodic steady state, CRN, two-level Monte Carlo, Morris/Sobol, and Kaplan–Meier for censored heavy tails are the right toolkit. PM4Py usage is largely conventional: `pandas` + `format_dataframe` (no `read_csv`), `get_variants`, `discover_dfg`, `discover_performance_dfg` correctly treated as sojourn, `get_case_arrival_average`, `parse_process_tree` with real limitations called out.

**Inaccuracies and logical flaws.** The simulation clock origin is 2026-01-01 (a Thursday) while `Calendar` documents `Mon=0` and uses `day % 7` with no origin alignment—so fire “Tue–Thu” and inspector “Tue–Fri” would fire on the wrong weekdays. `BASELINE["arrival_params"] = {...}` is a set containing `Ellipsis`, which cannot be `**`-unpacked into `ArrivalGenerator`. `_build_pools` contains leftover `Calendar(8.0 and 8.0, 16.0) if False else ...` junk. `ArrivalGenerator.generate` emits `(t, cluster_id)` but `on_arrival` is typed as `Case`, and nothing samples case type, flags, district, or value. `Router.DEFAULT` will `KeyError` on Commercial/Complex. Stated rules (Monday portal clerk, two Simple-only planners, applicant delay as a non-resource process, district-dependent planning queues, appeal) appear in the prose and then vanish from the engine.

**Unclarities.** Several formulas and thresholds lost operators/symbols (`λ(t) = …`, lognormal `μ`/`σ`, `max/min ≤ 1.25`, utilization `≤ 85%`), so the estimation and decision rules are harder to audit than they should be. Matrix levels and code defaults disagree (D8 20/40% vs `0.3`; D9 0.8/0.6 vs `0.7`). DES handlers are `...`, so “coherent flow” is mostly commentary.

This is a good advisory design with real process-mining grounding. The calendar bug, non-runnable placeholders, missing case-generation path, and garbled quantitative statements are enough that a strict judge should not go higher.
