6.1

The legal/process analysis is competent and mostly well-structured, but it is not close to flawless. Several required technical deliverables are incomplete or incorrect, and there are avoidable errors in classification, model semantics, and presentation.

**What works.** Headings match the spec. Section 1 separates stipulated facts from authority/scope/logging disputes and states a usable burden of proof. The two briefs are recognizably partisan. Case calls on SUP-701–705, 708, 712, and 714 are largely defensible. The judgment does not split the difference: it accepts time-bounded overlays (SUP-702, SUP-704) and still treats post-expiry and ineligible auto-approval as noncompliant. Scope defects (SUP-707, 709, 710) are identified as model problems rather than misconduct.

**Material defects.**

1. **The revised POWL does not do what the write-up claims.** `Request_More_Info` is constructed and then left unused. `quality_with_info` is never wired into `M_REVISED`. There is no `Operator.LOOP`. The required repeatable-info mechanism is comments plus dead code, not an implemented approximation. `SilentTransition` is imported and unused.

2. **POWL semantics are wrong in two structural ways.** The same `Transition` objects (`sanctions`, `quality`, `activate`, `manual_approval`) are reused as nodes inside exclusive XOR children. In PM4Py/POWL that collapses exclusive branches into a shared node. Rejection is a sibling of the full routes, so the model can reject with no sanctions and cannot replay SUP-709 (`Register → Sanctions → Reject`). Reactivation without `Register_Supplier` is also unrepresentable.

3. **Misclassification.** SUP-711 is labeled `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` even though both rows share one source transaction ID. That is a determined logging duplicate, not an open factual question. SUP-713 as `COMPLIANT` is generous: the mapping was approved after the case. SUP-715 as `NONCOMPLIANT` overweights a disclosure rule that is not in CS-9/M0 and is not an explicit prohibition on the relationship.

4. **Briefs miss mandated concessions.** Prosecution barely admits genuine model/data defects. Defense underplays SUP-703/708 and the 14 ineligible auto-approvals. Dialectical fairness is therefore uneven.

5. **Architecture and overlay table are sketches.** Section 6 names layers but does not specify DECLARE/log-skeleton constraints, eligibility/amount/time predicates, or concrete scope rules. The “normalized policy-overlay table” is one schema line, not a table.

6. **Presentation errors corrupt operative text.** “requester  manual approver human”, “requesterapprover”, and “2%  29%” are not cosmetic in a conformance opinion.

The narrative judgment is better than the artifact it proposes. Under a strict reading of model semantics, evidence labels, and the required executable revision, this is a mid-tier answer.
