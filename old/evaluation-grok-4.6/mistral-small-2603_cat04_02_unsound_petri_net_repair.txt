## What is actually wrong with the net

The waiver join is a one-input silent transition. After `t_split` there are tokens in `p_analysis` and `p_review`. On the waiver branch `t_join_waived` can fire as soon as `t_waive_review` has fired. That has two precise consequences the solution never derives:

1. `t_authorize` can fire **while a token is still in `p_analysis`**, i.e. before `t_analyze`.
2. Even if `t_analyze` already fired, `t_join_waived` leaves a token in `p_analysis_done`. That token cannot be consumed: `t_join_normal` still needs `p_review_done`, which is empty on the waiver route. Reaching a token in `sink` is therefore not the same as reaching `fm = [sink]`.

The standard-review route is fine. The net is a workflow net (unique source/sink; every node is on a source–sink path) and is not sound.
