The six-section writeup gets the main diagnosis right: April is a schema/pipeline incident, June is a mix/routing change that does not require concept drift, August–September stack process, concept, and population shift, and October is not comparable because of label maturity. That triangulation is the core of the task, and it is executed clearly.

It is not near-flawless. Several analytical and specification issues keep it well below a 9.

**Logical inconsistency in the August split.** The table and timeline treat the July→August jump (0.19→0.28, +0.09) as roughly half mix and half within-path worsening. The same text treats the gap between the variant-weighted rate (≈0.313) and the reported aggregate (0.28) as evidence that 0.28 is already censoring-deflated. Those two uses of 0.28 cannot both be right. If the matured variant rates are the operational truth, the jump is closer to 0.20→0.31, with a larger concept/process-performance share than mix. Using a contaminated endpoint and then citing the contamination as a separate finding is a real triangulation error, not a rounding quibble.

**Overclaims presented as settled.** May is not “fully traced” to one unbackfilled cache: unknown-activity and missing-queue rates are still elevated. July 98% mature / 36-day delay is inside baseline noise, yet it is folded into “creeping censoring.” June AUROC 0.79 is called “intact” against a 0.81–0.82 baseline. The weighted-aggregate deficit is a clever check, but January already shows ~0.01 slack, unmapped/rare variants are acknowledged in the prompt, and the argument is pushed harder than the denominators support.

**Falsifiers are uneven.** Competing hypotheses in §3 are good. The diagnosis table mostly lists “contradicting or insufficient evidence,” not a crisp falsifier per drift type, which the prompt asked for.

**Recalibration staging is slightly off.** Stage 1 fits V3 on the June vintage (V3 breach still ~0.35) while the live regime is already 0.46–0.64. The later “weekly V3 refresh” does not fully undo that. August is also described as both usable-with-weights and excluded from calibration, which is muddy.

**Code misses part of the spec.** Label normalization, uniform dedup, two cohort semantics, and the refusal to invent a PM4Py drift detector are all correct. `discover_performance_dfg` is called and discarded; watched-edge medians come from pandas. `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` durations are assumed to be seconds. Variant typing by substring is order-dependent and version-fragile. For a required extraction sketch, that is a real shortfall, not polish.

**What still works.** Observed vs hypothesized is usually marked (routing-intent at score time; stale queue feature vs true Y|X). October is not treated as an operational recovery. Alert gating so a maturity alarm cannot auto-retrain is proportionate. The four-signature summary in §2 is the strongest paragraph in the answer.

Major month-level diagnosis is sound; the grade is held down by the inconsistent August decomposition, a few overstated certainties, and incomplete use of the required process-mining API.
