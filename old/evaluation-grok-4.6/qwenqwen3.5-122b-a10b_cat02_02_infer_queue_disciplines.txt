**4.8**

The write-up has the right skeleton and a few genuine process-mining insights, but it is not close to the quality bar. The core requirement is reconstruction of *eligible waiting sets* at decision epochs. Those sets are repeatedly wrong, and several support/contradict labels are inverted. Under a strict reading that is a structural failure, not a polish issue.

**What is actually solid.** The required seven-section layout is present. QA’s 08:20 choice of A03 is correctly treated as an appointment override of both FIFO (A02) and priority/SPT (A04). QC’s C02-before-C01 episode is a real SEPT signal. QD’s D07/D10 episode is a real skill-feasibility story (D-X uniquely covers mechanical+electrical; D05 is correctly noted as D-F-only). QB’s low confidence is appropriate. Section 7’s warning that a center-wide priority rule would be the most dangerous inference is the best paragraph in the document.

**Eligible-set errors (decisive).** The task is to name who was waiting and eligible, then score competing rules. The tables do not do that reliably:

- QA at 08:58 still lists A02, which finished at 08:52. After calibration, FIFO, priority, and SPT all pick A04, so this epoch is non-discriminating; the write-up treats it as if priority were being revealed.
- QA at 09:20 lists A05 (the job that just finished) and omits A08 (P1, arrived 08:40). That hides the real comparison: late appointment A06 versus P1 A08 versus SPT A07.
- QB at 10:28 lists B01 as still resuming after B01 has completed, and omits B07 (appointment 10:30, est. 6).
- QC at 10:54 lists C04 as waiting while C04 is the job ending, and omits C01 and C07.
- QC at 11:40 lists C06 after C06 has finished, and omits C07.
- QD at 11:35 lists D04 as waiting while D04 is ending; FIFO is then said to “predict D04.”
- QD at 12:16 treats D05 as a FIFO candidate for D-X, but D05 requires firmware and is not D-X-eligible. The only eligible leftover is D10, so the row does not discriminate.

Because the decision sets are wrong, the rule-prediction columns cannot be trusted even when the qualitative story is in the right neighborhood.

**Missed discriminating epochs.** The strongest tests are mostly absent:

- QA 08:30: A02 (P3, est. 20) over A04 (P1, est. 7). This is the clean FIFO win over both priority and SPT. The diagnosis table instead cites A04’s delay as the strongest *contradiction* of an appointment+FIFO hybrid; it actually *supports* FIFO.
- QA 09:05: A05 starts and pushes A06’s 09:15 appointment to 09:20. That is the main challenge to “appointment as primary,” and it is not reconstructed.
- QC 10:18: C03 (est. 14) over waiting C05 (P1, deadline 10:50). Dynamic SEPT beats both priority and EDD while the deadline is still the binding one.
- QC 10:32: C04 (est. 22) over already-present C06 (est. 6) and C05. This breaks *dynamic* SPT and is more damaging than the cited C05-before-C06 contrast.
- QD 11:20–11:30: D-X is available and idles until D06’s appointment. Appointment holding is first-class evidence and is barely used.
- QD 11:25: D04 (P3, est. 10) over D08 (P1, est. 8) on the same skill. Skill routing cannot explain that; FIFO can.

**Diagnoses are partly right, over-claimed, or internally inconsistent.**

- **QA.** After A03, every later start is FIFO, including the choice that makes A06 late. Appointment is a one-time override, not the primary discipline. Confidence 85 is not calibrated to that contradiction or to the unused 08:30 test.
- **QB.** FIFO plus ad-hoc preemption is a defensible *label*, but B01→B02 is a FIFO *violation* (preemption by a later P1), yet it is listed as the strongest supporting episode. The 09:31 resume of B01 over B03 is in the table and points to incumbent-resume / non-preemptive completion after a single P1 interrupt; that hybrid is not what Section 1 states. Section 3 then calls B05-after-B03 *support* for priority and also a *contradiction*. Those cannot both be true. Preemptive versus non-preemptive priority are named candidates in the prompt and are never scored cleanly. B07’s broken appointment is almost ignored.
- **QC.** Opening SEPT among the 10:10 backlog is real (C02, C03, then C04). Calling the queue “SPT with deadline override” does not survive 10:32 (C04 over C06) or 11:40 (C01 over shorter, higher-priority, tighter-deadline C07). The C05 story is also backwards on timing: C05 is skipped at 10:18 while the 10:50 deadline is still relevant, then selected at 10:54 *after* the deadline has already failed, with remaining work far past 10:50. That is not a coherent SLA-switch. A frozen opening-backlog SEPT, with C05 inserted later and C01 aged to the end, is at least as good and is not compared.
- **QD.** Skill feasibility plus FIFO is the least-wrong primary, but Section 1 uses the same D07/D10 episode as both strongest support *and* strongest contradiction. The real contradiction of a skill+priority hybrid is D04 over D08. D-F’s idle stretch 11:36–12:00, while P1 electrical D10 is waiting, is an observable already in the log and is not used.

**Required accounting is thin.** Appointment *windows* versus exact times are asserted more than tested (A03 is exact; A06 and B07 are late). The mandated two-minute arrival-scan delay is ranked as a minor data-quality afterthought. It is not: B05 arrives 09:46 and B03 starts 09:48, so a delayed scan could remove B05 from the 09:48 choice set and change the QB priority story; C05 at 10:11 versus a 10:10 start is similarly tight. Preemption/resumption cost is not used to explain B01’s resumes. Deadline versus priority observational equivalence in QC is not worked through. Policy change when an SLA is endangered is invoked rhetorically for C05 and not tested against the earlier skip.

**Later sections are generic.** The two counterfactuals do not take the most ambiguous observed epochs, and one of them invents a hypothetical appointment for A04 instead of pitting two explanations of an actual choice against a single future observable. The D-F-free-at-11:48 test is reasonable in the abstract, but D-F *was* free from 11:36 to 12:00 and did not take D10; that discriminator is already in the data. Section 5 treats documented calibration/meetings as if they were speculative exceptions rather than hard constraints that must be folded into eligibility first. Section 6 lists the prompt’s example fields with only light tailoring to the actual ambiguities (appointment slack on A06/B07/D06, whether QC freezes the 10:10 set, whether QB resume-incumbent is coded, scan-visible time versus raw arrival).

**Net.** This is a complete, sometimes competent draft that repeatedly mis-states who was waiting, mis-tags evidence for and against its own diagnoses, and over-commits on QA and QC. It is well above an empty or purely sequential reading of start order, and well below a nearly flawless decision-set analysis. **4.8.**
