**3.2**

The write-up has the required headings, the right classification vocabulary, and a governance section that names the right controls. That is not enough. The analysis repeatedly misbuilds intervals, then issues **CERTAIN_** labels that are false.

**Interval construction is incomplete or inverted.** HUB’s `[-1 s, +1 s]` offset is dropped (only `±0.5 s` is used). SENSOR’s `[-5 s, +5 s]` is dropped or merged into rounding. VENDOR-NY’s `[-180 s, +180 s]` is omitted entirely, so T-403 Alert is treated as an 08:00 UTC minute instead of roughly `[07:57, 08:04]` UTC. Floor-to-minute is often applied only to the start of the minute; the `59.999 s` end is not clock-corrected. OPS-BER arithmetic is wrong on its face: `08:14:00 − 120 s` is `08:12:00`, not `08:13:00`. Timestamp resolution is not consistently applied *before* clock correction.

**T-404 is reconstructed from the wrong event and invented evidence.** The signed duration attaches to `Investigation_Complete` (10:35 HUB), so start is about `[10:10, 10:17]`, not Containment minus 18–25 minutes. S1 is therefore a possible 15-minute miss, not a certain 30–35 minute miss. `Close_Incident` on 2026-06-02 is not in the evidence; S3 for T-404 is fabricated.

**T-405 DST conversion is wrong, so the headline finding is wrong.** On 2025-10-26 Europe/Berlin the fold is 03:00 CEST → 02:00 CET. Local `02:xx` is either `00:xx` UTC or `01:xx` UTC, not `03:xx`. Ingestion order leaves Alert ≈ `00:50` UTC, Investigation ≈ `01:05` UTC, Containment ≈ `01:42` UTC — about **15 minutes**, not 2 h 15 m. S1 and the Alert–Cont profile are possible/marginal, not certain multi-hour violations. The answer also adds the CET offset instead of subtracting it.

**T-407 reverses delivery latency.** Sent at 14:00 plus `[0, 20]` min is Received in `[14:00, 14:20]`, not `[13:40, 14:00]`. With the optional causal clip, S1 is `[0, 17]` min (possible), not a certain 17–37 minute fail. S2 is then ~38 min from two HUB points (certain pass), not `[38, 58]` min.

**T-408 S3 is a certain fail, reported as a certain pass.** 2026-06-01 16:05 → 2026-06-03 06:30 is **38 h 25 m**, not 14 h 25 m. That also breaks the Cont–Close profile (outside `24 h ± ζ·6 h` for ζ = 1 and 2).

**Profile bands are systematically misstated.** `(Inv, Cont)` at ζ = 1 is `[25, 45]` min, not `[35, 45]`. `(Alert, Cont)` is `[33, 57]` min, not `[45, 75]`. Several “certain” profile calls rest on those bands.

**Coverage and sensitivity do not match the brief.** Many testable pairs are missing (T-401/402/403 Inv–Cont and Alert–Cont, T-408 Alert–Cont and Cont–Close, T-404 Alert–Inv / Inv–Cont under a correct inference). Sensitivity contradicts the author’s own intervals (T-402 S1 at +5 min; T-403 vs ζ = 3). T-409’s weak `[-12, +12]` min estimate is treated as a hard bound and then only half-applied until a causal clip; INDETERMINATE is barely considered even though the problem flags weak evidence. Mobile entry delay is defined as `True ≤ Recorded` and then allowed after Recorded.

**Section 5 is not executable and does not do the six required jobs.** It keeps a handful of midpoint rows, indexes them away from `format_dataframe`, skips interval arithmetic, uses the wrong DST comments, and only sketches `conformance_temporal_profile`. Midpoint-vs-interval comparison is a comment, not a result.

**What remains salvageable:** offset *direction* is stated correctly; SLA vs profile is distinguished; T-408 S1/S2 and a few high-trust HUB-only spans are in the right direction; section 7’s control list is aligned with the prompt. Those do not offset multiple **certain** findings that are the opposite of a correct interval analysis.
