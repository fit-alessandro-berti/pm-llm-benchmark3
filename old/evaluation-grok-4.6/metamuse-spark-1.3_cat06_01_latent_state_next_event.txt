A is only slightly better (high severity can start early), but then a 0.10–0.25 chance that **Tech Completed is the next log line** is too high: Review is the short remaining activity; Tech has 7 waiting, one engineer down, and ~8–9 h historical lag.

Worse, the first Review already chose another evidence cycle (Review → Request), which is evidence *against* investigations already running; that contradiction is underused. **C6-B rank 3 is a contradicted leftover.** The write-up says Tech is “strongly contradicted,” then still predicts it, enabled. Disposition Recorded is the obvious omitted near-term log event on a non-investigation close path.

### Inaccuracies and unclarities
The Review timing model is wrong. Ordinary 1–5 h is **after queue entry**, with 4 waiting and 2 reviewers. A/B are treated as if ~2 h elapsed already means Review is “timely.” That compresses wait time into service time.

Alternative states for B and E are predicted futures (S5 “imminent”, S4 “preparation”), not competing current abstractions. D’s alternative is a slash pile (`S3 / S2` “with S4 also open”), which evades a real state call.

A, B, and E are all in Review at 14:00 and may be among the four waiting items; that cross-case contention is ignored.

D’s rank-2 `Evidence Received` with `process_enabled: true` conflates a delayed past completion with currently enabled work. If the feed is replaying a hidden cycle, `Customer Evidence Requested` or `Evidence Review Completed` are equally plausible next *observations*.

### Calibration
Intervals are not spuriously precise, and midpoints are roughly coherent. The ranking is still too confident where the next *log* line is almost certainly Review (A, B, E) or Regulatory (C), while mass is parked on multi-step outcomes. That is less “honest uncertainty” than blurring the prediction target the prompt defined.

Net: a competent, evidence-using answer with repeated enablement mistakes, one serious miss on E’s supplier obligation, and several state/timing unclarities. Under a strict bar that is well below nearly flawless.
