## Issues that lower the score

The median of all 28 daily medians is 31.5, not 32.5; 32.5 is the weekday-only median of medians. That mixes scopes in the main pooling contrast.

Low-mix Mondays sit at 18–21 WIP; 20 April jumps to 37 with the mix/peak shift, not because overnight stock was still carrying the 15 April shock.
