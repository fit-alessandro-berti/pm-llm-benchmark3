The write-up has the required skeleton and occasionally uses the right vocabulary (decision times, observational equivalence, identifiability). That is not enough. The reconstructions are frequently factually wrong, two of four primary diagnoses are poorly supported by the author’s own tables, and several “explanations” misread the log.

## Fatal reconstruction errors

Eligible sets are the core of this task. They are systematically wrong.

- **QA 08:20:** A01 is finishing, not waiting. A04 (P1, `est_min` 7, arrived 08:13) is omitted. FIFO is not A01; priority is not A01/A02; SPT is A04, not A03. The one decision that could separate appointment, priority, and SPT is therefore unusable.
- **QA 08:58 / 09:20:** A06–A08 are already present at 08:58; A07–A08 are present at 09:20. A08 is P1. Omitting it hides the strongest anti-priority evidence.
- **QB 10:00:** B01 still has remaining work. Treating the choice as `{B04, B05}` is incomplete.
- **QC 11:34:** C01 is still waiting (starts 11:40). FIFO is C06 (arrived 10:25), not C07. The table inverts FIFO and then uses that inversion as analysis.
- **QD 11:21:** D02 is completing. D05 is the material alternative and is missing.
- **QD 11:35:** D04 *started at 11:25*. This is not the decision that chose D04.
- **QD 12:20:** D07 completed at 12:16. Priority of D05 vs D07 is also stated backwards (D05 is P2).

Once waiting sets are wrong, later claims about what FIFO/priority/SPT “would have predicted” are not evidence.

## Misread events

**B05 does not preempt B03.** B03 is a single segment `09:48–10:00`. B05 is `10:00–10:14`. Calling this preemption in the diagnosis table is a log error, not a close call.

**Interruption reasons are attached to the wrong decision.** B01’s *first* stop (09:16) is “urgent arrival.” The *second* stop (09:48) is “dispatcher reassignment.” The 09:31 resume is neither. Using reassignment to excuse B01 over B03 at 09:31 is false pairing.

**D08 after D04 is not explained by skill.** Both jobs are mechanical and both are eligible for D-M. Section 3’s claim that “skill eligibility explains” this is incorrect and converts a real priority violation into a non-issue.

## Wrong or overstated disciplines

**QA is not appointment-plus-priority.** After A03’s 08:20 insertion, start order is A02, A04, A05, A06, A07, A08 — arrival order. A04 (P1) waits behind A02 (P3) for 20 minutes. A08 (P1) waits behind A05–A07. A06’s 09:15 appointment starts late because FIFO continued with A05. That is FIFO with one appointment jump, not priority when “no active appointment.” The “strongest contradicting episode” (A04 waiting while A03 runs) actually *supports* an appointment exception; the real contradiction to their secondary component is A02-over-A04 / A05-over-A08.

**QC is not primarily priority.** At 10:10 the observed choice is C02 (P3, shortest). Strict priority picks C03 (P2). EDD also picks C03. The author’s priority cell (“C02/C03/C04, all P3 except C03 P2”) refuses to name the unique priority prediction. Later, at 11:34, C06 (P3, SPT) is chosen while C07 (P2, tighter deadline) and C01 wait; at 11:40 C01 is chosen over C07. Those are SPT / deferred-long-job / backlog patterns, not priority. C05-over-C06 is the only clean priority/deadline episode, and it is observationally equivalent to both.

**QB is a hybrid, but not the one claimed.** B02 interrupting B01 is preemptive response to a P1 arrival. After that the log is resume-interrupted-first (09:31, 10:14), one dispatcher reassignment onto B03, *no* preemption of B03 by B05, then B04 (P3) before B06 (P2). “Non-preemptive priority” as primary is incompatible with B04 before B06 and with B01 resumed over higher-priority waiters. Confidence 70 is too high.

**QD skill routing is a constraint, not the full discipline.** Routing D02/D05 to D-F and holding D06 for 11:30 are solid. Within a skill class the data look like FIFO (D04 before D08 P1) plus appointment, with an unexplained D-F idle from 11:36–12:00 while D10 (P1, electrical) could have been taken. Confidence 80 overstates what is identified.

## Weaker but still material faults

Section 4’s QC test admits both “competitors” predict C05, then offers an observable that would test *SPT*, not priority vs deadline. That is not a discrimination test.

Section 5 item 4 treats documented calibration/meeting windows as an “exception”; those are baseline constraints. The list never engages the D-F idle, freeze-and-sort of QC’s initial backlog, or resume-first as a policy rather than an exception. “How each would alter the diagnosis” is mostly missing.

Section 6 largely restates the prompt’s example fields. It does not target the actual forks: A04’s visibility at 08:20, B05’s visibility at 09:48, remaining time on interrupted B01, whether QC’s 10:10 backlog was sorted once, appointment tolerance for A06/B07/D06, and why D-F was left idle.

Section 7’s ranking (QD most identifiable, QC least) is defensible, but it sits next to a QC diagnosis of “priority” and a warning that inferring pure priority on QC is dangerous. That is an internal contradiction.

Required prediction columns are incomplete: skill-based is omitted almost everywhere; appointment is omitted on QD; QC swaps in deadline and drops appointment/skill.

## What is actually good

Format compliance; some real decision times (A03 at 08:20, B02 at 09:16, C05 at 10:54, D06 at 11:30); correctly low-ish confidence on QA/QC; recognition that skill constraints mask other rules on QD; and the right instinct that QC is under-identified. Those keep the score above a failing 1–2. They do not offset repeated log misreads and two mislabeled primary policies.

A nearly flawless answer would have gotten waiting sets right, refused to call B05 a preemption, treated A02-over-A04 and C02-over-C03 as decisive, and separated skill *eligibility* from the within-eligible rule. This does not.
