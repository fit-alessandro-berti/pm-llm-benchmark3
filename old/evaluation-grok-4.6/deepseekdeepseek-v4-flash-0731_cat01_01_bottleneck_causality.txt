**7.3**

The final write-up is a strong, format-compliant causal memo: it catches the crude-vs-stratified reversal, refuses to treat stratification as identification, separates queueing from service, treats the group label as a routing/staffing bundle, and proposes a safer learn-while-limiting-risk plan. It is not nearly flawless.

The most serious concrete error is in the required day/night decomposition. “Low night Orion 7.2/8.0” invents a second Orion low-night cycle time. Only C01 is Orion / Low / Night (7.2 h). The 8.0 figure is Nova (C15). That is a misstated data point in a comparison the prompt asked to make auditable.

Causal labeling is sloppy in a way that matters for this task. The mandated single verdict is `not identifiable from the supplied evidence`, immediately glossed as `mainly confounded`, with confidence 85. Those are different claims. Worse, the “evidence against” that verdict is the within-stratum Orion advantage. A stable association is not evidence of identifiability; identifiability would require an instrument, design, or defended assumption set. The same confusion reappears when unobserved risk is ranked first: “Orion gets harder cases” would bias Orion *worse*, so it cannot explain the within-stratum *advantage*. Residual risk is a real uncertainty; it is not a coherent top explanation of the pattern they just documented.

Smaller defects: `unobserved_reviewer_skill → resource_group` treats a constitutive group property as a cause of assignment; the graph also stacks a residual `resource_group → cycle_time` arrow on top of the mediators without a clear residual-direct story; Medium Nova mean wait is given as 11.13 against the supplied 11.12; the standardized contrast cell is worded as “Orion -2.6 h” rather than a signed Orion−Nova contrast.

What remains is still well above a merely competent answer. Arithmetic on the main crude, stratum, wait/service, compliance, and standardization contrasts is almost all correct. The variable-role table actually uses confounder, mediator, collider, selection, and proxy. Hypotheses cover the five required families and attach predicted observations, not method names alone. The intervention table includes both a capped randomized margin pilot and a queue/compliance-capacity experiment that does not relabel the group. The Python block embeds the CSV, reproduces crude and stratified means, states a pooled-complexity weighting, prints overlap and <3-case warnings, and compares variants/DFGs without fake timestamps. Residual uncertainties are specific and tied to a single next measurement or design change.

Those strengths keep the score in the mid-7s. The data error plus the identifiability/verdict confusion are enough, under a strict bar, to rule out anything near 9–10.
