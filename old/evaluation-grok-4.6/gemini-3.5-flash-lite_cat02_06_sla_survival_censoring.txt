## Section 1 — estimands are internally inconsistent

Time origin at `Submit` and horizon at 72h are correct. After that the first estimand collapses.

You define estimand 1 as the probability of **not** being resolved by 72h, then set the target metric to \(1-S(72)\) where \(S(t)\) is “the survival probability of remaining unresolved.” If \(S(t)\) is the probability of still being unresolved, the estimand **is** \(S(72)\), not \(1-S(72)\). If instead the event is Resolve, \(1-S(72)\) is the probability of resolution by 72h — operational success, not SLA tail risk. The text uses both meanings at once. That is not a wording slip; it inverts the quantity the rest of the memo claims to estimate.

The two estimands are also not cleanly separated from estimators. “\(1-S(72)\) in the absence of informative censoring” is an identifying assumption for Kaplan–Meier, not a definition of \(P(\text{not resolved by } 72)\). The latter includes withdrawals, rejections, transfers, late resolutions, and still-open cases; KM-with-censoring does not.

Case contributions mix the estimand with the risk set:

- **S03 (resolved at 76h):** correctly an observed non-resolution *at the 72h horizon*. Calling this “contributes to the risk set up to 76h” is estimator talk. Fine as a secondary remark; it should not replace the horizon contribution.
- **S09 (open at 92h):** this is the most important miss in the section. The prompt required distinguishing right censoring from **already-observed breaches**. S09 is observed through 92h without Resolve. For any 72h functional — \(P(\text{not resolved by } 72)\), \(P(\text{no terminal disposition by } 72)\), or \(1-\mathrm{CIF}_{\text{resolve}}(72)\) — the 72h status is **known**. Censoring at 92h is only the encoding for *eventual* time-to-resolution. Treating S09 as censored for the SLA estimand is wrong.
- **S06 (withdrawn at 27h):** the KM-vs-competing-risks contrast is right for time-to-resolve. For “not resolved by 72h” the case is a known failure of operational success. That is not stated.
- **S08 (transferred at 68h):** internal non-resolution by 72h is known; external resolution is not. The table does not keep those two facts apart.

You also never do the required mapping of estimand → operational decision in this section. CIF of resolution by 72h is a success probability, not a tail-risk functional. A third estimand the prompt flagged — still **no terminal disposition** at 72h, which treats Withdraw/Reject/Transfer as events that *clear* the SLA clock rather than as failures — is absent. That quantity is the one operations would use for “how many cases are still sitting in the queue at the deadline.”
