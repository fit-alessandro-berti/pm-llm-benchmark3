**8.1**

This is a strong, process-aware causal memo with verified arithmetic, real identification discipline, and an intervention plan that matches the decision. It is not near-flawless under a strict reading.

**What holds up.** The crude-versus-stratified reversal is correctly computed (Orion 21.96 vs Nova 15.24; within-stratum gaps −2.14 / −2.21 / −3.43; pooled-complexity standardization 17.14 vs 19.65). The answer does not treat that reversal as identification. Total / direct / mediated estimands are distinguished, and “Orion” is correctly treated as a routing-policy bundle rather than a well-defined treatment. Queue wait is separated from review service; compliance is treated as a policy-fixed path whose *timing* can still mediate; completion is treated as a collider/selection node; the 50% reassignment is treated as an interference/equilibrium estimand, not a case-level contrast. The role table, competing hypotheses (including a genuine harmful-Orion story), and predicted falsification tests are specific enough to be used. The Python block matches the requested diagnostics and does not misuse synthetic PM4Py timestamps for performance claims.

**Material deductions.** Confidence **78** is too high for the object the verdict actually needs. The point-estimate sign reversal is real, but Low-Orion and High-Nova are \(n=2\), every shared complexity×shift cell has an arm with at most two cases, and Orion-night and Orion-high-load are almost the same 13 cases. Those 2–3 hour “advantages” are smaller than ordinary within-cell scatter and have no SE, interval, or even a plain statement that the High contrast is 34 and 36 versus a six-point Orion cloud. Section 1 presents 2.51 h as if it were a stable adjusted fact, then tucks fragility into a list. That is overprecision, not a generic caveat.

The standardized contrast uses the **completed-sample** complexity mix. For a proposal to move future Orion work, the policy-relevant weights are closer to Orion’s mix (or to dual-eligible cases that would actually move), and the analyzed mix is already selected on completion. Same sign, but the memo never says so.

**Smaller defects.** Medium-Nova wait is 11.125 h, reported as both 11.12 and 11.13. The DAG note is at the 120-word cap and includes weakly motivated arrows (`shift → arrival_load`). Phase 0 is instrumentation, not an intervention. The PM4Py footnote/citation before the code is extra to the specified format. “Mainly confounded” is defensible for the *crude* gap, but it sits slightly loose against ranking a beneficial Orion mechanism first; a stricter verdict would have been “not identifiable, with the crude gap mostly mix.”

**Net.** Causal and process competence are clearly above a competent baseline. The remaining gap to a top score is overclaimed numerical precision, incomplete policy weighting, and a few specification nits—not a failure to see the structure of the problem.
