8.6

The audit is unusually complete and process-aware: it covers every required item, separates complete-trace leakage from process-infeasible order, treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as later-legal but t0-inadmissible, preserves queue and prefix-age signals, and keeps region as a reviewed proxy rather than deleting every correlated field. Those are the distinctions the rubric rewards. It is not near-flawless.

**Revised explanations still lean on a contaminated model.** Section 5 correctly says a model trained on leaked features is invalid even if those features are hidden in the UI. Sections 3–4 do not follow that through. Case X/Y `credible_drivers` reuse the leaked model’s signs (high queue, high complexity, short elapsed time, region R4) as if they remain reliable after removing `total_case_duration_hours` (almost the target), `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, and `final_decision`. With a 0.44-importance duration feature and a 0.31 variant-id hit, remaining SHAP values are not transferable. The narratives should have labeled these as *candidate* prefix signals whose direction and magnitude must be re-estimated after prefix-only retraining, not as current drivers of the displayed 0.82 / 0.71 scores.

**Uneven admissibility standard.** `queue_length_at_t0` is kept as a Case X/Y driver despite an explicit ±5 minute join that can select a post-t0 snapshot; `claimant_channel` is removed solely for missing lineage. Channel is at least as plausible at registration as `claimant_region`, which is then promoted to a Case Y driver of a high-risk score that was mostly `REG-COV-DOC-DOC-DMG-DEC-APP-DEC-CLOSE`. That is internally inconsistent and over-credits region as an explanation of the *current* prediction.

**Small factual overreach.** Employee experience is described as “used in the model.” The brief only says it is joined as of extraction date and is absent from the top-12. Presence in the warehouse is not evidence of use in these scores. Case Z’s `prediction_status` also leaves the required `{retain, recompute, invalidate}` set (`invalidate pending prefix-only recomputation`).

**A few missed refinements, not misses of the main task.** The document-cycle pattern’s negative sign is not diagnosed as retrospective survivorship (completed cycles on non-breaching traces). `num_document_requests_total` is correctly banned at this t0, but the write-up underplays that a *prefix* request count can still be informative later, or in deviant traces where requests precede coverage. `final_handler_team` as “high” is slightly soft: the warehouse field is closure-populated for every row; the 65% t0 match does not make 35% of rows merely “unsafe,” it makes the column the wrong variable.

Section 2’s validation suite and the predictive / causal / actionable / fairness split are strong. The grade is held down because the required revised explanations still treat leftover attributions from a leaky ranker as credible early-case reasons.
