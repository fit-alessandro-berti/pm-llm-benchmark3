**6.5**

The final write-up is a competent digital-twin design that hits the required eight sections and the main constraints (non-stationary demand, calendars, cross-case dependence, missing objections as unobserved, no optimality claim, no `pm4py.read_csv`). It is not close to flawless. Several inaccuracies and underspecifications are material for this brief.

**What is solid.** Scope is activity-level DES with a clear boundary. State includes the attributes that actually drive routing (`requires_*`, district, architect, value) plus inspection `bundle_id` and a latent objection flag. Assumptions are split as requested and mostly realistic: NHPP arrivals with architect clusters, service vs applicant delay kept separate, public-notice duration as a proxy rather than observed objections, HR calendars validated against event weekdays. The experiment table covers B, D1–D10, and four stress cases with identifiable levers and trade-offs. Uncertainty treatment (seasonal warm-up, 24-month horizon, CRN, 30–50 reps, percentile CIs for tails, Sobol, truncated lognormals) is appropriate. Validation lists the six required modes and does not pretend start-time-free utilization is ground truth.

**Process-mining / PM4Py flaws.** The skeleton ignores the requested `pm4py.parse_process_tree` and calls a likely nonexistent `pm4py.objects.process_tree.pt_import_string.import_string`. `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg` is assigned as a single object; current PM4Py returns a `(dfg, start, end)` triple. Section 2 invents `pm4py.filtering.filter_log_by_event_attribute_values`. The excerpt is corrupted (`requires_public_notice` = `yyes` on P001). These are exactly the “conventions” the prompt said not to break. Transition gaps are correctly labeled as mixed waiting+service, but estimation then waves at “industrial-engineering estimates” without a reproducible split (no lifecycle handling, no resource-busy reconstruction, no explicit imputation model).

**Design and decision gaps.** Cross-case dependence is described in prose and then almost absent from the engine: no shared bundle queue, no architect-week state, no objection-propagation process. That undercuts the “not an independent-case simulator” constraint. Public-notice’s 14-day minimum and Decision drafting’s wait-for-all-applicable-reviews synchronization are not first-class state. `violation_history` is used for D2 but never estimated (it is not in the log). SLA targets (e.g. 95% Complex within 180d vs empirical median 140d / p90 260d) are invented and unreconciled with the variant table; fairness and robustness lines omit the inequality (`ratio 1.2`, `degradation 20%`). No cost or FTE-budget criterion despite D4/D6 being capacity interventions. Warm-up is internally loose (6 months as “one seasonal shoulder” while there are two peak seasons). Combined policy is only D10; interactions such as D5×legal calendar or D7×Monday clerk dedication are not in the matrix.

**Net.** Strong structure and the right scientific instincts, with enough API errors, missing executable dependence, and sloppy decision thresholds that a strict judge cannot treat it as nearly correct.
