The write-up has the required nine headings and a recognizable specialization–pooling–hybrid argument, but it is not close to the quality bar. Completeness of outline is masking large analytical, tabular, and reproducibility failures.

**Capacity analysis is the core miss.** Offered load is computed on unweighted type shares (ES is treated as ~15% rather than the arrival-weighted ~12%). Ordinary midpoints are used as if they were mixture means, so diagnostic work is understated (Validation, Mechanical, Electrical, and Software tails are ignored). Worse, parallel vs sequential ME/ES is treated as changing *total work* (~1,382 vs ~1,800 min). Parallelism changes the critical path and contention, not offered minutes. That is a process-mining competence error, not a rounding issue. The required time-block × skill view is missing: evening coverage (Emil after 16:00, almost no one after 18:00), Farah’s half-day, Deepa’s 10:00 start, and lunch-break holes are never quantified. U2 is not renormalized; U3/U5 are slogans. The “~90% utilization, already fragile” conclusion is directionally plausible and then built on the wrong object.

**Section 4 does not do what was asked.** The prompt required a *policy-by-scenario* table. The table is one row-band per policy with a single range, so U1 vs U3 vs U5 cannot be compared. The numbers are also unsourced. The text cites “100-repetition simulation bounds”; the code is one seed and cannot have produced those SLAs.
