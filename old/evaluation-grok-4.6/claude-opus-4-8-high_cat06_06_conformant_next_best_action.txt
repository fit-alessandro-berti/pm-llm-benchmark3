**6.5**

The answer is in the right family: it treats conformance as a gate, ranks A/B last, does not let SLA forecasts legalize shortcuts, keeps screening from substituting for second-officer review, parallelizes information-gathering, and accounts for P-901. Under strict grading that is not enough. Two operational errors affect the ranking, and several smaller inaccuracies and unclarities accumulate.

**Fatal-ish operational error: S4’s work is timed as wait + stamp, not wait + review.**  
Action C states second-officer review is 35–75 minutes, then 5–15 minutes to record/execute. Rank 3, the S4 continuation, and §5 all use `S4 available in 35–65m + 5–15m → decision ~40–80m`. That is resource idle time plus recording, with the review duration dropped. The same mistake is explicit in “If S4 at 65m: only ~25m slack remains.” Slack of 25 minutes cannot contain a 35–75 minute review. Consequently:

- “S4 at 35m: strong chance to decide within deadline” is not supported.
- Corrected S4 path is roughly 70–140+ minutes, so it is a lawful *accept-the-SLA-slip* strategy, not a co-equal on-time path.
- The §5/§6 switch (“S4 at ~35m → do not interrupt P-901; Rank 3 rises above C”) is therefore justified only as spillover protection, not as deadline feasibility. The table’s “earliest plausible continuation” column is wrong for ranks 1 and 3.

That is a core decision-support failure on a 90-minute case.

**Counterfactual D1 is nonconformant-adjacent.**  
Rank 1’s first branch says that if alternate screening clears the fuzzy match, “a conformant clear/approval path may open faster” and then `Approve → Payment Executed within deadline`. Rule 6 is disjunctive: score ≥ 70 *or* unresolved match. The live score is 72. Clearing the match does not remove second-officer review, and Auto Clear still requires no alert and score < 40. The hedge “still confirm rule-6 conditions” is not enough: the drawn outcome skips a still-mandatory control. This is exactly the failure mode the prompt warns against, only relocated into the continuation tree.

**Process prefix is noted, then ignored.**  
Framing correctly says Evidence Validated loops to Analyst Review, then second-officer review. Every ranked plan jumps to S2/S4. The missing post-evidence analyst step is never costed, sequenced, or assigned (R17 vs someone else). That understates both delay and four-eyes logistics.

**Conformance labels are used, but not always for the right condition.**

- C is `CONFORMANT NOW` while its own text is “conformant *if* S2 independently reviews” — the same condition that puts D in `CONFORMANT IF STATED CONDITION HOLDS`.
- Rank 3’s “stated condition” is “S4 must actually arrive in time.” Arrival time is operational, not a conformance predicate. The right condition is independent review by someone other than R17.
- Rule 6 is said to apply to F. Rule 6 constrains Approve / Payment Executed, not rejection. F’s live issue is the undocumented rejection threshold (which §4 later gets right).

**Ranking structure is redundant and the required columns are soft.**  
Rank 1 already is “D plus S2 or S4.” Ranks 2 and 3 are those same officer choices again, so A–G are not ranked as a clean set of alternatives. “Expected benefit/harm range” is mostly High/Low prose, not ranges. “Confidence” means “confidence the plan works” for C–G and “confidence it is illegal” for A/B. Rank 1’s harm is “Low–moderate” while the same plan may impose +0.12–0.28 breach probability on another high-value payment.

**§3 mixes the two bounded-loss pots.**  
Deadline operational loss is €20k–€35k; false rejection / prolonged hold is €15k–€80k. E is described as costing “at most” the latter band; F is said to uniquely foreclose the former. Both miss the deadline upside. The tail-dominance argument for ranking A/B last is still correct and is the best part of the memo.

**What remains strong (and why this is not a 5).**  
Exact labels for A, B, F, G; four-eyes and override conditions stated as gates rather than utilities; SAR does not authorize execution and must not be disclosed; observational predictions are not transported uncritically; API quota treated as scarce but parallelizable; G correctly flagged as a time-trap and as misconduct if an incident is invented; fallback never to A/B; rerank triggers (fresh score, screen result, S4 ETA) are the right events.

**Net.** Conformance framing is expert-level; the live ranking is not, because the S4 clock is wrong and the screening branch can be read as waiving a still-mandatory second review. That is too much for 8+, and under the requested strictness it is not a 7.5 either.
