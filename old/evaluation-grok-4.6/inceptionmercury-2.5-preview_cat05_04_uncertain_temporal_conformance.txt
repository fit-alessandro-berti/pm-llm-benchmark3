The answer instead declares flooring to be a **half-width** bound, then writes the offset rule with the minus signs stripped (`True time = Recorded time Clock offset`, `[recorded b, recorded a]`). HUB’s stated `±0.5 s` measurement uncertainty is mostly dropped (HUB is treated as `±1 s` or as an exact second). Ingestion (“start after alert”, “containment after start”) leaves Alert at first 02:50 (≈ `00:50Z`) and Start/Containment on the second pass (≈ `01:05Z` / `01:42Z`).
