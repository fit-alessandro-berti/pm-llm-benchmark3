## What is actually right

The control-flow reading is the right one. Risk is a data-free XOR after analysis; supplier optionality is XOR with a silent skip; pass/fail is loop exit vs. redo; labels are not duplicated; “may overlap” is interleaving/absence of order, not forced simultaneity. `L_int` is stated as a real language (counts, route exclusivity, trial projection `ET IT (AP ET IT)*`, plus the listed precedences), which is what later claims need.

The process tree

```text
SEQ(RD, AND(CM, AR), AND(SEQ(AND(PV, DC), LOOP(SEQ(ET, IT), AP)), APPROVAL, SUPPLIER), RM, AC)
```

is a coherent conservative under-approximation. The extra barrier is exactly `AR < PV`, `CM < DC`, `CM` before the selected reviews, and `CM < CS`. Approval–trial and supplier–trial overlap after that barrier are preserved, which is the more distinctive non-block-structured feature. The N-poset argument on `{AR, SR, PV, ET}` is the right reason an exact unique-label series-parallel tree fails, and the answer correctly refuses to claim impossibility under duplication.

The POWL root edges are the intended covering relation:

- `CM → PV` and `AR → DC` / `AR → APPROVAL` / `AR → SUPPLIER` are split, not joined;
- trial waits for both work products as blocks, which is exact because those products are not repeated;
- approval and supplier stay incomparable with trial and join only at release.

On the stated abstraction, `L_powl = L_int` is plausible. The code shapes match the PM4Py reminders (`parent`/`children` on the tree; `StrictPartialOrder`, `OperatorPOWL`, `Transition`, `SilentTransition`, `.order.add_edge` on POWL). Binary `LOOP(body, AP)` is the right encoding of “adjust only on redo, never after the passing inspection.”

F1–F3 identify an earliest violated condition, and F1 is a genuine cross-branch violation (`PV` before `CM`), not a start/end rule.

## Defects that pull the score down

**1. The allowed-trace set never exhibits the headline split on the planning side.**  
Every allowed witness has `AR` before `PV`. The intended freedom “Plan Verification may start as soon as containment is done, even while analysis is unfinished” is therefore never shown as a positive trace. A5 exposes only the dual extras (`SR`/`CS`/`DC` before `CM`). The tree’s other extra precedence `AR < PV` is argued in prose and never witnessed. For a task whose whole point is split dependencies, that is a real gap in Section 5, not a cosmetic one. A trace such as `RD CM PV AR DC SR ET IT RM AC` was the obvious A5-class counterpart and is missing.

**2. A1–A4 all live inside the tree’s extra barrier.**  
Jointly accepted traces therefore do not illustrate POWL’s extra concurrency except through A5. Approval–trial overlap is shown (A2, A4); the CM/AR successor split is shown only on one side and only as a tree counterexample. “Five representative allowed traces” is weaker than it looks.

**3. Language-equality claims are stronger than the proofs.**  
`L_powl = L_int` rests on a sketch: “projections fit the children, so the root can interleave them.” That skips POWL’s actual block semantics (if `A < B`, *all* of `A` precedes *all* of `B`). It happens to be exact here because `PV`/`DC` are single events and retries do not rewind them, which they do note, but the converse is still hand-wavy. Likewise, “`L_tree` is exactly `L_int` plus those extra precedences” is true for completed visible traces, but the tree also delays the *choice* of risk route and the silent supplier skip until after both `CM` and `AR`. Prefix/performance behavior is therefore more constrained than the visible-language slogan.

**4. Unverifiable experimental assertion.**  
Section 7 states that both nets were converted in PM4Py 2.7.23.8, were sound, and that alignments matched all 16 verdicts. No command, diagnostic, or alignment output is given. The rest of the section correctly treats conversion as evidence to inspect, not as fidelity; this sentence does the opposite. If the runtime claim is false, it is padding; if true, it is still not inspectable. That is a serious evidential defect in a validation plan.

**5. Small specification and wording nits.**  
Assumption 1 says mandatory activities outside the trial occur once, then later carves out route-dependent and optional labels in `L_int` — the tightening should have been in the assumption itself. D12/D13 put “no additional precedence” in a dependency table; useful, but they are absences, not relations, and are easy to misread as edges. Transitive closure on the POWL order is redundant; it should not change the language, but it is extra machinery that the conversion claim never isolates. `ProcessTree()` as tau is idiomatic PM4Py, yet a one-line note that `operator is None` and `label is None` denotes silent would have removed a version-portability doubt.

**6. Approximation policy is defended, not shown to be “as faithful as possible.”**  
The tree could have preserved `CM → PV` and `AR → DC` by joining those arms *before* trial, at the cost of forbidding approval–trial overlap. The answer never compares those two conservative trees. Preferring to keep approval ∥ trial is reasonable, but “as faithful as possible” is asserted rather than argued against the obvious alternative under-approximation.
