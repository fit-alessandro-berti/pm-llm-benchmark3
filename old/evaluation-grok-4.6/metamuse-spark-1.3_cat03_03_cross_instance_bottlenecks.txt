**Calculation and attribution errors (Section 2)**

The S1 arithmetic is left in a broken draft state: “Pack-to-dispatch for J1 = 325 min (5h25? 13:30 to 19:25 = 5h55 = 355 min).” 13:30→19:25 is 355 minutes; 325 and 5h25 are simply wrong. A deliverable that still contains the author’s self-doubt and the discarded figure has not been checked. Later prose then switches among 5h40 (pack→J4 pack), 5h55 (pack→dispatch), and 5h30 (counterfactual saving) without consistently naming the endpoint.

The FIX-A delay is over-attributed. The log documents CNC-2 idle only from 10:05 to 10:47 (42 minutes). The answer treats 10:05–11:45 as continuous machine availability and reports 100 minutes as “the delay plausibly associated with FIX-A.” The 10:47–11:45 interval is not evidenced as idle; it may be other work, setup, or staffing. A careful bound would be ~42 minutes of idle-while-blocked evidence, with 100–115 minutes as an upper counterfactual, not the headline number. The 5-minute 11:40→11:45 handling remark does not repair this.

The L7/J5 aside is confused: “L7 added 50 min upstream to J5 if earliest otherwise 13:10.” Release is 13:50; 13:50−13:10 = 40 minutes of lot hold, while 13:50→14:40 is post-release time and is not lot delay. That sentence mixes two intervals.

B2/RACK-7 is described as if the rack were a binding constraint. RACK-7 is free at 14:35; J5 is ready at 14:40; B2 starts at 14:50. The observed binder is J5 (and whatever made J5 late), not the rack. RACK-7 is a *latent* constraint that would bind only in a counterfactual where J5 is early. The text does not keep that distinction clean.
