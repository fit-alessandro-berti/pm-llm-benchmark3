### Substantive problems

**Downstream modeling (§6) is the weakest part and is partly wrong.** OC-DFG, OTG, and ET–OT are type-level artifacts. Unrepaired data already has Item–Package, Package–Delivery, Invoice–Order, and Payment–Invoice in the OTG, and `Pack Item`–Package plus `Dispatch Package`–Delivery in the ET–OT graph. Bundle A only adds more *instances* of existing type pairs. It does not “add a Delivery node DEL-Y” to the OTG, and it does not newly connect `Pack Item` to Package or `Dispatch Package` to Delivery in the ET–OT graph. Those type edges already exist via `ev06`/`ev07`/`ev09`. The only type-level ET–OT novelty in the whole write-up is Bundle B’s `Confirm Delivery`–Delivery, and that is not isolated as such. OC-DFG is likewise described as if it had instance edges “from IT-B1 to PK-8.” Equivalent-execution clustering is confused with object sharing: after flattening on Order, ORD-A and ORD-B still differ (no confirm/invoice on B), so they would not cluster as equivalent executions merely because they share `PK-8`.

That is not wording noise. The prompt asked for awareness of downstream effects and for concrete edges that appear, disappear, or duplicate. The answer often names instance facts as if they were new type-level structure.

**A salient alternative is missing.** `PAY-2.amount=130` equals `ORD-A.declared_value`; `PAY-1.amount=100` equals `ORD-B.declared_value`. A coherent reading is order-aligned cash with a messy invoice object, not only “remainder of a 230 combined bill” versus “unapplied cash.” Bundle B/C never consider that swap. That is a real abductive hole, especially because `PAY-1` is already tied to `INV-44` while `INV-44` currently bills only ORD-A.

**Uncertainty is not internally calibrated.** `scanner_ref=PK8-2` sits in a clean series (`PK7-1`→`PK-7`, `PK8-1`→`PK-8`) and is stronger than `external_ref=N-421?`, yet P1 is 0.85/provisional and P3 is 0.90/accept. Leaving that open is allowed; barely discussing it is not.

**Smaller factual/clarity defects.** Treating “object creation timestamp” as a hard OCEL invariant is sloppy: packages, deliveries, invoices, and payments have no create events. E2O qualifiers are set to activity names with no argument. `PK-7DEL-X` / `PK-8DEL-Y` concatenation is careless.
