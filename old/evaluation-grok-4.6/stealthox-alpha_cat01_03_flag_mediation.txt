**Factual errors.**

- Section 6 cycle-time numbers are not a coherent estimand. P0 and P2 are *levels* (flagged ~16.5h; P2 “4 to 8h” obtained by subtracting review wait and service). P3’s “flagged 3 to 5h” cannot be a resulting cycle time under retained routing: cutting review wait from 6.5h to ~2–3h yields ~12–13h, not 3–5h. P4’s “escalated cases 3 to 6h” is likewise impossible as a level (those cases are ~21h) and only makes sense as a *reduction*, which is not how P2 is written. A policy table that mixes levels and deltas is not an actionable contrast.

**Causal/graph problems.**
- The DAG has `complexity -> flag` but **omits `risk_score -> flag`**, which is the data-generating relation the prompt asked to respect (sample support 0.28–0.61 vs 0.64–0.93). That is the central treatment mechanism.
- `U_resource_assignment` is marked unobserved even though `triage_resource`/`review_resource` are in the log and perfectly partition on the flag (T1/T2 vs T3/T4). The confounding is observed; the *reason* for the assignment is not.
- `priority_lane -> triage_service` is drawn as a structural effect of the lane. The service gap is unidentified given perfect staff confounding and complexity mix.
- E4 (“all cases reviewed”) is not P3 (protected capacity for flagged cases). E6 sets review to *absent for everyone*, which is stricter than P2 (drop mandatory referral, leave judgment review). Section 1 states the distinction; later estimands and the P1 line “review queue removed” blur it.
