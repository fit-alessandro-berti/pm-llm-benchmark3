**6.8**

This is a structurally complete, process-aware, and mostly well-disciplined memo. It is not close to flawless. Concrete arithmetic errors, a temporally invalid DAG, a broken PM4Py path, and a few role-confusions keep it well below the top band.

**What it gets right.** The crude-versus-stratified reversal is identified and not over-interpreted as identification. Total vs. mediated vs. direct effects are distinguished, and “Orion” is correctly treated as a policy/bundle rather than a portable treatment. Selection on completed cases, positivity failure (n=2 cells, one Orion day case), and equilibrium effects of a 50% move are all named. Sections 5–6 and 8 are the strongest parts: required hypothesis types are present, tests have predicted observations, and the intervention sequence (instrument → capacity → capped RCT → scale) is safer than the proposed reassignment.

**Factual errors (section 4).** Day/night and crude compliance means are wrong, despite being written to two decimals and used as if audited.

- Day mean is **13.98** (n=13), not 15.54. Night mean is **21.74** (n=17), not 20.54. The night–day gap is ~7.8h, not +5h.
- Compliance-required mean is **26.78** (n=13), not 25.21. Not-required is **11.95**, not 11.73. The crude gap is ~14.8h, not 13.48h.

Within-complexity and given-aggregate figures (overall +6.72, stratum waits, rework 4/14 vs 3/16) check out. The errors sit in the hand-computed rows the code never reproduces. That is a serious evidence-use failure in a section that demanded auditable arithmetic.

**Causal-graph flaws.** `quality_outcome → case_completed` is backwards: the 30-day appeal is post-close. `cycle_time → case_completed` treats a completion-conditional duration as a cause of being observed. `complexity → queue_wait` is missing even though wait is the dominant duration channel and rises sharply with complexity; `unobserved_case_risk` and `shift` likewise do not point at queueing. Supported-vs-assumed commentary is otherwise concise and the Request-Info feedback note is good.

**Role slip.** The executive summary lists rework with pre-treatment case mix (“Orion receives … rework cases”). Later tables treat rework as a mediator; the lead paragraph does not. Confidence **78** is high given two-cell strata, one day-Orion case, and no open-case data. `mainly confounded` is defensible for the crude harm claim; residual group effects are closer to not identifiable.

**Section 7.** Crude/stratified contrasts, pooled-complexity standardization, full `complexity × shift × group` overlap (including zeros), variant tables, manual DFRs, small-cell warnings, and a no-causality disclaimer all match the spec. The PM4Py block does not: traces are joined as `>` strings and passed as one string. `parse_event_log_string` expects a collection of comma-separated traces; this either iterates characters or treats each line as a single activity. The `try/except` hides the failure instead of implementing the requested discovery. The standardized contrast (~2.5h Orion advantage under pooled weights) is never used in the narrative.

**Smaller defects.** `OrionNova = +6.72 h`; arrival load is reasoned about but not decomposed; within-stratum service times (nearly identical by complexity) are asserted qualitatively rather than shown.

The recommendation is still the right operational call. The memo would be an 8.5–9.0 if the day/night and compliance numbers were correct, the DAG respected time order and the wait channel, and PM4Py actually parsed the traces. Under strict grading those are not nits.
