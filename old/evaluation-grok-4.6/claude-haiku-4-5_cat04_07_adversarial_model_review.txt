## Fatal operator error

Model S is a sequence of `Register Dossier`, a **parallel** block of four `X(activity, tau)` children, an **XOR** of the two approvals, and `Release Order`. In PM4Py process-tree syntax, `+` is AND/interleaving, not a loop; `*` is the loop. The review repeatedly calls `+()` an “unordered choice loop” that “allows arbitrary repetitions.” That is a regex/process-tree confusion, and it is the load-bearing mistake of the paper.

Consequences:

- Model S can execute each of Credit Check, Technical Review, Supplier Review, and Expedite Review **at most once**, or skip it via `tau`. It cannot repeat them.
- **Correct Dossier is not in Model S at all.** The strongest-case-for-S claim that the loop “handles P6 implicitly” is false. Any trace containing a correction is extra-log behavior, not a silent success.
- Approvals are `X('Manager Approval', 'Auto Approval')`. Model S **requires exactly one** approval. It does not skip both and it does not admit both.

Of the five “concrete undesirable behaviors,” traces 3 and 5 are invalid attacks. The review therefore fails its own requirement of five real admitted traces, and it attacks Model S for the one constraint it actually encoded well.
