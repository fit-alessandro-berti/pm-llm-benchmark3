The answer gets the headline distinction right — Model A can emit one `Check Revision` per loop iteration, Model B emits exactly one check concurrent with the whole loop — and therefore lands the correct coarse verdicts on unbounded traces, P1, and P3. **The fatal operator error.** Model A is `+(Analyze Revision, Check Revision)` inside the loop body. That is interleaving, not sequence. The answer transcribes the `+` correctly and then immediately denies it: “None; all operations sequential,” “immediately after each Analyze,” “Check must immediately follow Analyze.” That single mistake contaminates the P1 definition, the structural table, the T2/T3 explanations, the simplified tree (`->(Analyze, Check)`), and the claim that Model A itself satisfies a “Check after Analyze” rule. Under the answer’s own P1 criteria, Model A is non-compliant, because Check may precede Analyze in the same iteration. The author never notices.
