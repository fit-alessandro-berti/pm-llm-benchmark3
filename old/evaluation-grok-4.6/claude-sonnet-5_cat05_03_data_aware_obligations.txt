## TX-305 is the important miss

R6 says: do not default missing amount; classify `INDETERMINATE` when a decisive trigger cannot be established or excluded, unless other evidence resolves it. R4 says an increase from an unknown amount is not safely classifiable. The log is built to force that issue: create/review/release all omit amount; settlement the next day is 150,000 and is explicitly *not* an immutable release payload.

The answer’s “resolved indeterminacy” move is:

- if amount `< 100k`, R1 is off;
- if amount `≥ 100k`, `Enhanced_Review` already satisfies R1;

therefore `COMPLIANT`.

That is too neat. The review document has no amount. There is no release payload. Settlement is later and only “may” equal the release amount. You cannot establish that the review covered the amount that actually moved, and you cannot exclude a high-value foreign release. Calling the *fact* unknown while calling the *decision* `COMPLIANT` with LOW severity is the wrong control conclusion. The designed label is `INDETERMINATE`. Section 4 almost admits this, then walks it back. That is a core case-reasoning error, not a side dispute.
