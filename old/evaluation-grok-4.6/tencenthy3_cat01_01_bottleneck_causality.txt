**6.4**

The write-up is well structured, hits the required headings, and gets the central descriptive fact right: the crude Orion penalty reverses inside every complexity stratum, wait (not service) carries most of the within-stratum gap, and a 50% label-swap is the wrong intervention. That is the core of the task. It is not close to flawless.

**Causal discipline.** Section 1 never actually distinguishes total, direct, and mediated effects, which the prompt required at minimum. “Partly causal through mechanisms” plus “the crude gap is mainly case mix” is left as a blend rather than three estimands. Confidence of 80 is too high: Orion is almost collinear with night and high load, High-Nova has two cases, Low-Orion has two, and completed-case selection is unobserved. The safer verdict was “not identifiable” or “mainly confounded,” with a *possible* beneficial residual. Stratification is correctly *not* sold as identification in the tables, but the executive verdict overclaims a mechanism effect that shift/load cannot be separated from.

**Graph and roles.** `resource_group → shift` is wrong. Assigning a case to Orion does not make it a night case; Orion’s night coverage and arrival time select the group. That arrow scrambles mediation versus confounding. `case_completed → quality_outcome` is unjustified. `compliance_required` is treated as a mediator of the *group* effect in the role table, while the DAG (correctly) parents it on complexity, not group — an inconsistency. Proxy, collider/selection, and confounder roles are otherwise named.

**Estimand for the actual decision.** Standardization uses the *pooled* complexity distribution. The proposal is to move Orion’s future cases, so the relevant weights are Orion’s mix (or Nova’s stratum means applied to Orion). Under that weighting Nova would look *worse* (~25h vs Orion 22h), which is the number management needs. That calculation is missing. The not-required overall mean is given as 12.2h; it is about 11.9h.

**Interventions and tests.** The three-phase plan, capacity-first phase, and small RCT are the right shape. The Phase 2 stop rule (appeals >2%) is below the observed base rate (~6–7%) and would fire immediately. Several falsification tests are real tests with predicted signs; front-door mediation on n=30 is ornamental.

**Code.** The script embeds the CSV, reproduces crude/stratified means, states a common weighting distribution, prints overlap, and uses the requested PM4Py path without fake timestamps. It prints per-group DFGs rather than a between-group variant/DFG contrast, and it does not warn that the High-stratum contrast itself has n=2.

Strong process-mining instincts and usable recommendations; several causal-graph, estimand, and overconfidence errors keep it well below a high score under a strict reading.
