**4.1**

The answer gets the central pattern right: the crude Orion gap (21.96h vs 15.24h) reverses inside every complexity stratum, so the 50% reassignment is not justified by the overall means. That is the main thing the task was testing. Almost everything else is incomplete, sloppy, or wrong.

**Missing required deliverables.** The specification demanded eight headed sections. The final write-up stops after section 6. There is no reproducible Python block (crude/stratified tables, complexity-standardized contrast, overlap counts, DFG/variant comparison, cell-size warnings) and no five-item residual-uncertainty list. Planning those sections in the scratchpad does not count. That is a hard failure on format, auditability, and process-mining competence.

**Section 1 does not do what was asked.** It never distinguishes total, direct, and mediated Orion effects, which was an explicit minimum. “Mainly confounded” at 75 is too confident for cells of size 2, and the reversal is treated as nearly identifying. Evidence “against” the verdict is mis-aimed: Orion’s within-stratum speed and certified reviewers argue against a *harmful* Orion effect, not against confounding. Completed-case selection and the coarseness of `outcome_30d` are almost absent.

**Section 4 contains polarity and arithmetic errors.** Night means are given as Orion 23.1h vs Nova 17.5h, then described as “Orion still faster.” That comparison shows Orion slower; any Orion advantage appears only after further stratification, and night-only cells remain badly unbalanced. Medium rework is written as “higher for Orion (16.7% vs 25%)” even though 16.7 < 25. The Low-Nova mean is floated as “~9.6–10.5” because 96.4 was summed as 105.4; the supplied aggregate is 9.64. Compliance-required is said to be faster for Orion in Low, Medium, *and* High; there are no Low + compliance-required cases. Queue wait vs review service is not decomposed with auditable group means.

**Graph and roles are incomplete or misspecified.** `shift → routing_rule` / night coverage, `resource_group → queue_wait`, `rework → review_service`, and `complexity → review_service` are missing. `case_completed → selection into analysis sample` is not in the allowed variable set. `quality_outcome` is listed as a possible mediator of cycle time even though the 30-day outcome is downstream. Collider structure is mentioned in passing, not used.

**Sections 5–6 are the strongest parts and still leak invention.** The five required hypothesis types are present and several tests have predicted observations. Then the pilot invents N≈200, four weeks, ±10%, 5%, 35%, 2h, and 2pp as if they were data. That violates “do not invent missing facts.” Positivity is obviously terrible (Low–Orion–Day = 1, High–Nova = 2) and is not treated as a binding identification problem.

The causal story of the proposal is directionally correct. The write-up is not close to a disciplined, complete, or numerically reliable answer.
