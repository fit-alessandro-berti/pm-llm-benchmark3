The answer is complete, internally consistent on X1–X8, and clearly stronger than a label-only clustering. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading, several technical slips, one weak required comparison, and a few definition/implementation gaps keep it well below the top of the scale.

**What it gets right.** The four relations are actual equivalences (keys, not scores). A is correctly labelled as Order-flattening, not object-centric. That matters in a process-mining answer.

**S vs B is stated too cleanly.** On these eight logs B only coarsens S by folding X3 into SEM-STD. In general B is not a coarsening of S: it reads O2O qualifiers that S discards. Two E2O-isomorphic executions could split under B. The text treats a dataset coincidence as a relation inclusion.
