**7.0**

The answer is structurally complete and conceptually on the right side of the task: it uses a max-join rather than a sum, keeps queue wait separate from service, respects the Friday 18:00 / weekend chemistry pause, treats discipline as a discrete regime, and does not invent a single precise completion time. That is necessary, not sufficient. Under strict grading the piece is well below near-flawless: several numbers are wrong, one scenario is mislabelled, the uncertainty ranking contradicts itself, and two interactions the prompt asked for are only half-handled.

### Material inaccuracies

**Analyst free times and Friday capacity are wrong.** Remaining work is 0.4–0.9 h and 1.2–2.0 h from 15:30, so the two chemists free at **15:54–16:24** and **16:42–17:30**, not “~16:25–17:45”. Friday still has **2.5 calendar hours × 2 analysts = 5 analyst-hours**, then ~2.1–3.4 h of residual capacity after in-service jobs. The ledger’s “~2–3 h of Friday capacity left” against an “11 h backlog” collapses servers into calendar time. Downstream FIFO start “Mon ~12:30–14:00” happens to be near a two-server mean (~12:00–12:30), so the error is partly cosmetic—but the ledger and the free-time range are still incorrect, and “start Fri 16:45–17:45” overruns the latest second-server free time (17:30).

**Scenario C is labelled as the delayed-readout case and then given a 5–12% band.** No weekend authorization is the **majority** micro path (0.55–0.75), not a rare tail. The body correctly treats Monday 08:30 as the baseline floor and reserves C for 20–30 h confirmation. The table title and the required “delayed microbiology readout or confirmation” row therefore do not match the probability or the dominant clock. That is not a wording nit; it hides the main staffing-calendar mechanism inside the smooth path.

**Sunday-readout + confirmation cannot be bounded as “Mon ~10:30–18:30”.** Sunday 23:00 + 30 h is Tuesday 05:00, so the second readout is Tuesday 08:00+. The envelope is incomplete.

**“>90–95%” Electronic Release vs “~1–3%” rejection** does not add up and is not derived from the investigation bands (lab investigation already 4–9%, of which only a subset reject). It is an unearned precision band.

### Logical and methodological flaws

**QA queue is not a function of join time.** The task explicitly requires uncertainty in the queue *at the future join*. A static “~4, band 2–7” from Friday–Saturday balance ignores that two reviewers work Monday 08:00 until B-417 arrives. A 16:00 join can see a largely drained queue; a 10:00 join cannot. That induces a **negative correlation** between chemistry delay and QA wait which this forecast never uses. FIFO chemistry is therefore stacked with a still-fat QA wait and an automatic 20:00 spill; some of those paths should still accept Monday if they arrive to a thin queue with ~4 h of QA left. The Monday-evening / Tuesday-morning split is real; the mechanism is overstated.

**Uncertainty ranking is internally inconsistent.** Driver 1 is QA queue (“±half a day”). Driver 2 is investigation, immediately described as the **largest magnitude** driver (hours vs 3–7 days). If magnitude ranks, investigation is first. If impact on P50 ranks, say so. As written, the ranking is confused. Calendar quantization (the ~62 h Friday-18:00→Monday-08:00 floor that dominates *elapsed* remaining time) is buried in fifth place, and the summary never states that ~64.5 h of the 72–92 h P50 is dead calendar, with only ~8–28 h of uncertain process after Monday 08:00.

**Dependence is asserted, not used.** Temperature excursion as a common cause is correctly described, including the useful point that quoted anomaly rates are already marginal. The scenario table still has no joint rerun+contamination+investigation row, and the “smooth” 40–55% band sits *below* a naive independent product (~45–70%) even though a latent common cause **raises** P(all-clear) relative to independent marginals while also fattening the joint tail. Direction of the dependence correction is therefore at least partly wrong. Updated rates in §6 (rerun 0.20–0.35, contamination 0.15–0.25) are invented without a stated mixing model.

**Section 3 does not actually decompose each scenario.** Shared mechanics are good. The per-scenario table is mostly “As A” / “As A/B/C”. Documentation-query and investigation paths never walk working-hour calendars (Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00, Sat 09:00–13:00, Sunday dark) from a concrete first-review timestamp. Earliest Wednesday for an 8 h doc-only investigation after a Monday review is conservative; lab-investigation “central Mon 28 – Thu 01 Oct” is late relative to a Tuesday open + 3–7 calendar days.

### What is actually right (and why this is not a 5)

Chemistry **cannot** finish Friday if urgent takes the first free server and min service is 1.5 h (16:42+1.5 = 18:12): that argument is valid. Join = max is repeated and used. Weekend authorization is often non-binding under FIFO chemistry: that is the correct residual race, even though the grading rubric’s hint that “microbiology dominates the smooth path” is too coarse—FIFO (55%) chemistry finishing mid/late Monday usually *is* the slower branch; priority chemistry (~45%) races the Monday 08:30 micro floor. Treating discipline as weights rather than a blended mean is right. Shortcuts are declared. Overlaps are declared. Earliest ~67–70 h (Monday ~11:00–13:00) correctly hugs the weekend wall. Terminal event is release, not a fake reject-heavy story.

### Smaller defects

- “Banks 0.25–1.25 h” is the 16:45–17:45 envelope, inconsistent with the 16:42 best-case used to prove no Friday finish.
- Lognormal QA mean ~2.4 h from median 1.5 / p80 3.5 is a fair shortcut (σ is actually ~1.0, mean ~2.5 h) but single-reviewer Fri/Sat **blocking** from that tail is ignored.
- FIFO Tuesday spill “~10–15%” is likely low given four triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) jobs on two servers.
- P50 placed inside the smooth-path window while smooth is only 40–55% is slightly optimistic unless overlaps are very carefully ordered.
- Secondary trigger (temperature flag) is well chosen; primary trigger (starts before 18:00) is imminent and calendar-relevant, but it only shifts remaining service by ~0.3–1.3 h plus discipline identification—not obviously a larger update than first chemistry result, first micro readout, or flag confirmation.

**Net:** a competent, calendar-aware envelope with the right architecture and several numbers, labels, and interactions that would not survive a hard review. That is a 7.0, not an 8–9.
