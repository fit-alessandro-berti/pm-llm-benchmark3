The answer is structurally complete and conceptually pointed in the right direction (K-337 is a policy change; C1 reservation does not scale; earlier checks can spill into Assessment). That is not enough. On the criteria this task actually scores — temporal consistency, process feasibility, minimal-change counterfactuals, and distinguishing readiness from completion — it is unreliable, self-contradictory, and often infeasible.

**Feasibility model.** The poset is a flattened sketch, not a case-sensitive partial order. It forces `Triage ≺ Document Check` even when originals gate the check; treats `Originals Receipt` as an optional successor of Triage rather than an external enabling event; and makes Escalation a predecessor of Decision, which the rules do not require. Preliminary compliance is missing as a non-substituting activity. The timing/routing/policy split is wrong: K-104 adds work and a reservation (routing plus capacity allocation), K-411 changes the control-flow AND-split, not “timing only.”

**K-104 is infeasible.** Tue 08:00 C1 still has a 16 h queue. A 08:00–10:00 “skim” is preemption, not a small perturbation, and is not implied by a Wednesday reservation. Booking Wed 09:00–12:00 on Monday steals from an already-queued 20 h Monday / 8 h Wednesday backlog; the write-up notes the externality and then still treats the slot as available. Assessment “40 min faster” is invented. Table (finish Wed 11:30–14:00, 4–9 h) disagrees with the derivation (Decision 12:15; best/base/worst 9/6/3 h). “Cycle time 4 h to 9 h” is unlabeled (level vs delta) and easy to misread against a multi-day factual span.

**K-219 fails the readiness-vs-completion test the prompt flags.** Originals at 17:30 Mon plus a ~5 h D2 wait can pull Document Check into the night (factual D1 already works overnight, so that part is fine). Assessment at 16:00–18:30 Tue still leaves Decision on M1’s Wednesday morning. End-to-end completion should stay near Wed 08:50–09:00 unless M1’s calendar changes. The table’s “Wed 03:30–06:00” is calendar-illegal and contradicts §3 (“Manager is still available 08:30 Wed”). The 2–5 h saving is an arithmetic subtraction from Notify, not a propagation through queues and shifts. The 25% rework rate and +12–24 h add-on are point inventions; a full extra DC+Assessment+external loop is often longer than 24 h.

**K-337 numbers do not add up, and the decision policy is leaked.** After Assessment 2 (Fri 10:30), dropping RMI-2 is the right minimal cut, and keeping a post-assessment C1 review plus four-eyes (A1 ≠ C1) is correct. Local demand “−12 h D1, −8 h A1” is not DC3+A3 (3.5 h + 2.5 h); C1 work is shifted, not deleted.

A honest FIFO path is start ~12:00–13:30, finish mid-to-late afternoon — still overnight before Assessment. Table 3–7 h, §3 Notify 13:20 (≈1 h), and “worst 3 h if the case only waits longer in Assessment” cannot be true together (that worst case is ~0 h). Assumption “(b) Assessment queue unchanged” contradicts the stated congestion mechanism.

Sign-of-effect cells are vaguer than the prose.
