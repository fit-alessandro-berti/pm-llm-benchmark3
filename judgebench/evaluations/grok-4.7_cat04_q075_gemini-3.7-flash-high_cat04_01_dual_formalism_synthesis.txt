7.2

The models are largely the right ones, but the write-up is not reliable enough for a high score. The POWL is close to exact, the process tree is a genuine under-approximation, and the eight witnesses are classified correctly. Several stated claims contradict the models, and the validation plan is not executable as written.

The POWL construction is the strongest part. Cross-branch edges are placed where they belong: containment enables only verification planning, root-cause analysis enables design, approvals, and the supplier choice, the trial loop is gated only by planning and design, and release joins the trial, the chosen approval route, and the optional supplier branch. Loop child order matches repeat-until semantics, so `Adjust Parameters` cannot follow the exiting inspection. High-risk reviews are an unordered partial order inside an XOR, and the supplier skip is a silent XOR child rather than a duplicated label. That is the right use of POWL.

The process tree is also a coherent conservative under-approximation: the artificial join after containment and root-cause analysis excludes early verification planning rather than admitting an illegal trial or release. Parent/child wiring and loop arity look consistent with PM4Py. W-04 correctly exposes that loss.

The approximation is then misdescribed. Assumption 5 says the tree disallows “delaying Plan Verification until Analyze Root Cause completes.” It does the opposite: it forces that delay and rejects the valid case in which planning finishes while analysis is still open. Section 3 and W-04 get this right, so the document contradicts itself on its own policy.

Section 6 repeats the problem. It marks the risk route, approval/trial overlap, and supplier consultation as exact in the tree. The same barrier also stops design, approvals, and supplier consultation until containment finishes. That is additional overconstraint, already admitted in Section 3 and then erased by the comparison table. “Given Phase 2 entry” does not make those rows exact.

Section 4 does not explain every intentionally incomparable pair. Containment versus design, approvals, and supplier, and planning/design versus approvals and supplier, are left implicit. Those pairs are exactly the asymmetric concurrency the POWL is supposed to justify.

The validation plan has concrete defects. `check_soundness` is indexed as `sound_tree[0]` although the usual return is a boolean, so the plan crashes before the witness tests. The limitation text assumes 1000 playout traces, but the call sets no trace or loop bound. The duplicate-label audit only counts silent transitions; it does not test skip behavior, although W-01 would have done so if the function reached the alignments. Soundness is also treated as stronger evidence than it is.

The self-critique mostly restates mandated control-flow abstractions (data-free XOR, unbounded repeat-until) rather than the actual residual errors: the inverted example, the false exactness claims, and the incomplete concurrency argument.
