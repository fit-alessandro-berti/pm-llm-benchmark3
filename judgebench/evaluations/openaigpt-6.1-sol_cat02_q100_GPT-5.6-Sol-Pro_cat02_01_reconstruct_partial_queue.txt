Furthermore, the small residual largely follows from comparing service exposure with the total service durations of departures, with relatively small boundary contributions. The answer acknowledges boundary effects, but should more explicitly explain that agreement is not independent validation of steady-state assumptions. The checks are useful; their interpretation is somewhat overstated.

   The answer honestly labels this distinction, which is important. Nevertheless, it never constructs jointly feasible rounding scenarios enforcing resource non-overlap and shared-event dependencies. Consequently, the very broad utilization envelopes are less operationally informative than they appear. A stronger answer would supplement them with feasible constrained scenarios, or explicitly refrain from presenting them as plausible utilization ranges.
