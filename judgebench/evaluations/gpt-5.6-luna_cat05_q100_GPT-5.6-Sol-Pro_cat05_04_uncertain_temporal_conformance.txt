For T-403, `04:00` EDT means approximately `08:00 UTC`. Applying floor-to-minute uncertainty and the vendor offset bound of `[-180,+180]` seconds gives:

- Recorded clock: `[04:00:00, 04:00:59.999]`
- True local time: `[03:57:00, 04:03:59.999]`
- UTC: `[07:57:00, 08:03:59.999]`

However, the answer treats the upper endpoint as `08:03:59.999Z`, which corresponds to only four minutes of floor uncertainty after correcting the offset in the wrong direction or effectively omitting the full upper range. More importantly, using the stated formula correctly:

- Maximum true local time is `04:03:59.999` only if the recorded displayed value is interpreted as `04:00` with the offset correction applied as ±3 minutes around that displayed minute. Actually, because the floor interval is `[04:00:00,04:00:59.999]`, subtracting the minimum offset `-180 s` yields `[04:03:00,04:03:59.999]`, so the listed upper endpoint is numerically correct.
- But the lower endpoint from subtracting the maximum offset is `03:57:00`, making the full UTC interval `[07:57:00,08:03:59.999]`, as listed.
