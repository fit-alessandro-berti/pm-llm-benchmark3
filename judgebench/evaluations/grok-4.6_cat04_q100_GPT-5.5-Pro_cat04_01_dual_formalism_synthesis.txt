The submission is a strong, honest modeling write-up: both artefacts are internally consistent, the process-tree loss is named rather than disguised, the POWL edges match the crossed dependencies, and the eight witnesses are well chosen. It is not near-flawless.

**What is actually right.** The intended DAG is not series-parallel, so an exact process tree without label duplication is impossible; the stated conservative phase-join (work products, then `{risk ∥ supplier ∥ trial}`, then release) is a coherent under-approximation and the Python tree implements exactly that expression, including binary `LOOP(Execute→Inspect, Adjust)`. The POWL fragment is the right canonical model: `contain→plan`, `analyze→{design, risk, supplier}`, `{plan, design}→trial`, `{trial, risk, supplier}→release`, with Quality/Regulatory unordered inside the high-risk branch. F1 really is a cross-branch violation (`Execute Trial` before `Plan Verification`); A4 really is the tree/POWL split. No duplicate visible labels, optional supplier as `XOR(tau, Consult Supplier)`, pass/fail abstracted by loop position.

**Defects that keep it off the top of the scale.**

1. Assumption 6 mixes “true concurrency” with language-level interleaving. PM4Py trees (and playout) give linear extensions; POWL edges give “complete-before-start” on submodels. Those are not the same, and the write-up pretends they are.
2. The tree policy is not compared with the equally conservative dual (allow reviews/supplier right after RCA, but sequence them before the trial). That is the actual design choice, and it is never argued.
3. Several local inaccuracies: D3 encodes an unordered pair as a directed row; A2’s reason claims both high-risk reviews overlap the trial cycle, but `Regulatory Review` is strictly after `Inspect Trial`; “complete immediately after root-cause analysis” in assumption 5 is the wrong verb (the excluded behaviour is *occurrence/start*, not forced completion).
4. Format drift: leftover `[1]` GitHub citation after §8, informal `SPO{…||…}` prose, and a validation plan that never operationalises the eight witnesses as runnable align/play-out checks (only a soundness one-liner).
5. Self-critique restates the already-advertised tree under-approximation and the data-free inspection abstraction; it does not probe the real residual semantic risk, namely whether `OperatorPOWL` nodes as `StrictPartialOrder` children in this PM4Py version truly interleave inner events (vs. treating each operator as an atomic block). If the implementation is atomic, A2/A4/A5 and the “approvals may overlap trial” claim collapse.

Substance is well above a mediocre answer; the remaining issues are exactly the unclarities and untested semantic assumptions the rubric says should cost a lot.
