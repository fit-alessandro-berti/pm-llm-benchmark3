The most consequential issues are:

- **The occupancy figures fail a basic conservation check.** Mean active count is busy resource-minutes divided by **360 clock minutes**, not by available resource-minutes. Even the answer’s own central S1 busy estimate of 545 minutes would imply 1.51 active cases on average, incompatible with its reported 0.7–0.8. Missing-start imputation changes the split between queue and service, **not station WIP or its peak**, because station entry and exit do not change.
- **The rate table and snapshots use boundaries inconsistently.** With the code’s half-open two-hour bins, external arrivals are **13, 8, 1**, S1 completions are **6, 7, 7**, and S2 completions are **3, 5, 6**. Immediately after the noon events, S2 has received 14 cases and completed nine, so its WIP is **5**, not 6, and total WIP is **12**, not 13. Rounding warrants sensitivity analysis but does not excuse disagreement under the chosen nominal convention.
- **Even the correctly listed completed-case durations are summarized incorrectly.** They sum to **2,050**, not 2,055 case-minutes; their median is **145**, not 155 minutes. The cohort occupancy is therefore about **5.86**. The answer correctly avoids claiming steady state, but its code calculates both sides of the Little’s-Law check from the same duration sum. An independently integrated cohort WIP trajectory would provide an actual implementation check.
- **The PM4Py service/wait demonstration is semantically invalid for this process.** A single synthetic interval from external arrival to final completion erases both queues and encodes the entire sojourn as one activity. Labeling the result “case-level” does not recover active service or waiting. The completed cases’ actual S1 and S2 intervals would preserve service semantics; the external-arrival-to-S1-start wait would then need explicit treatment.
- **Some interpretation exceeds the evidence.** The final queue counts establish that S2 has the larger *residual backlog*, not by themselves that it is the capacity bottleneck. The claim that the estimated completed-service means broadly overlap is also inconsistent with the nominal ranges: about **25.3–28.8 minutes at S1** versus **34.3–38.2 at S2**. A smaller case-table error is that P13 waited 80 minutes at S1, outside the stated P12–P14 range of 85–95.
