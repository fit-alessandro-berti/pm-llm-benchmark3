### Specific Critiques & Deductions

#### 1. Misinterpretation of the Chemistry Pause Rule (-0.4 points)
* **The Prompt's Rule:** *"If B-417 has not started service early enough to finish by 18:00, the unfinished work is paused and resumes Monday at 08:00."*
* **The Flaw:** In the Assumption Ledger, the response states: *"If B-417 outranks the urgent sample, there is only a ~4% corner (start 15:54 + min service 1.5 h) where chemistry finishes Friday ~17:24."* However, in the main analysis (where Urgent > B-417), the response assumes Analyst B frees at 15:54–16:24 and **starts the Urgent sample**, which then gets paused at 18:00. 
* **Why this is a logical flaw:** If the Urgent sample has the same service time distribution as B-417 (min 1.5h, mode 2.5h, max 4.5h)—which the author explicitly assumed in Row 1 of the Assumption Ledger—and starts at ~16:10, it has almost zero chance of finishing by 18:00. If the lab's rule is that work is paused at 18:00 if it cannot finish, that rule exists because *assays cannot be left running unattended* or *analysts go home*. Yet, the author assumes B-417 starts on Analyst A at 16:42–17:30, knowing with 100% certainty it cannot finish by 18:00, "banks" 0.5–1.3h of work, and pauses. While the literal wording allows "partial work banked then paused," in pharmaceutical chemistry (e.g., HPLC runs, titrations), if a run is paused because it didn't start early enough, the prep might be banked, or it might not be started at all to avoid sample degradation. More importantly, the author failed to consider what happens to the *in-service* sample on Analyst A if it hits 18:00, or whether the lab simply holds new starts after 16:30. This nuance was glossed over.

#### 4. Minor Arithmetic/Calendar Imprecision in S4 Working Hours (-0.1 points)
* **The Flaw:** In Section 3 (S1), the central smooth path finishes QA on Monday between 11:00 and 18:00. In S4, a Documentation Query adds "4–12 working hours." Since QA closes at 20:00 on Monday, a query raised at, say, 14:00 with a 12-working-hour resolution time gets 6 working hours on Monday (14:00–20:00) and 6 working hours on Tuesday (08:00–14:00), plus re-review, pushing completion to Tuesday late afternoon. For S4 to finish at Mon 18:00, the join would have to be ~08:30, initial QA done by ~10:00, query raised, resolved in the minimum 4h (14:00), re-reviewed, and released. However, the table combines "QA query / investigation" into one row and lists the central range as "Tue 12:00 – Wed 18:00 for doc-only investigation," completely dropping the central range for the Documentation Query from the table.
