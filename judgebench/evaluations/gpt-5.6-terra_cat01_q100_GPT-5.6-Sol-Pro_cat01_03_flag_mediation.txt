The answer does mention inference/contamination, but the intervention could be stated more formally as a UI-only intervention.

The prose corrects this, but the graph could distinguish proxy measurement more explicitly.

Also, the DFG construction uses completion timestamps for activities, so its performance measures should be interpreted very cautiously relative to explicitly calculated queue waits; the prose warns of this, but the code could make that distinction even sharper.

Given no observed correctness label and the possibility that mandatory review is safety-critical, P3 could reasonably be the safer first pilot. The answer does qualify P2 heavily and gives P3 as fallback, but a stricter recommendation hierarchy might prioritize feasibility and safety evidence before bypassing review.

Overall, this is an unusually complete, process-aware, causally cautious response. Its remaining flaws are mainly terminological precision around mediation estimands, intervention-version ambiguity, and minor implementation/operational concerns rather than substantive misunderstanding.
