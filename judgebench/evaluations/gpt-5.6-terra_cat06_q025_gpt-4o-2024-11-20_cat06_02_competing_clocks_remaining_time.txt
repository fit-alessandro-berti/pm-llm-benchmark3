- **Microbiology timing is materially wrong or inconsistent.** Incubation started Friday 14:00 and becomes ready after 46–54 hours, i.e. roughly **Sunday 12:00–20:00**, not “Sunday 20:00–22:00.” This error propagates into the scenario logic.

Therefore authorization does not generally “accelerate every downstream stage” by a full day; it mostly improves Monday-morning readiness, perhaps by around 0.5–1.5 hours versus Monday readout. The answer overstates this as the most forecast-changing event.
- **The smooth-path scenario is internally inconsistent.**
  - Its table says earliest terminal time is Monday 08:20–08:50, which is impossible because QA still must queue, perform review, accept, and then release.
  - Its decomposition says release Monday 14:15–14:45.

Even under risk-priority, the urgent sample may remain ahead. The answer does not properly build an envelope for FIFO versus priority scheduling.

There are five batches currently, but QA has Friday and Saturday capacity that may reduce the queue. Arrivals occur only during QA opening hours, and service capacity is substantial enough that the current five do not automatically become an 8–10 batch Monday queue.
