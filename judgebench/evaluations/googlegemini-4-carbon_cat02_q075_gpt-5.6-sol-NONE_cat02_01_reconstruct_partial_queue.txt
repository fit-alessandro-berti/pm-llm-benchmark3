Requirement 6 of Section 7 explicitly requires demonstrating `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` and `pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` *"only on a projection for which their case-level semantics are valid"*, and the Quality Bar warns that *"A strong answer will not confuse active service with elapsed station time."*

```python

    "case": "case:concept:name",
    "arrival_s1_ts": "start_timestamp",
    "s2_end_ts": "time:timestamp"
})
...
projection = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(projection)
```
In PM4Py, `insert_case_service_waiting_time` defines case service time as the duration covered by `[start_timestamp, time:timestamp]` intervals within each trace, and case waiting time as trace sojourn minus service time. On this single-interval projection, PM4Py computes:
* `@@service_time` $=$ **total end-to-end case sojourn ($W_1 + B_1 + W_2 + B_2$)**!
* `@@waiting_time` $=$ **`0.0` for every single case**!

This completely invalidates `insert_case_service_waiting_time` by conflating all S1 and S2 queue waiting with active service. To make both PM4Py functions semantically valid on the completed cohort (`P01–P14`), each case must be projected as a multi-event trace containing an instant external-arrival anchor `[arrival_s1, arrival_s1]` followed by the two active service intervals `[s1_start, s1_end]` and `[s2_start, s2_end]` (evaluated across imputation bounds for `P05`, `P07`, and `P11`).
