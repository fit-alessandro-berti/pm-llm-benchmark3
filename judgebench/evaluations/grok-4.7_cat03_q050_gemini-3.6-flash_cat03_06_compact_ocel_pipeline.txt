6.5

The pipeline is complete and mostly referentially sound, but it is not analytically reliable. Several predictions contradict the constructed relations, one required O2O relation is misnamed, and the final checks contain a false assertion.

The required `Package packs Item` relation is encoded as another `contains` link. That collapses Order–Item and Package–Item, so qualifier-level cardinality is no longer meaningful. Section 1 also says logistics events connect to participating items, while dispatch and confirmation do not. Section 3 then says payments radiate into both order processes, although payments are linked only to `M1`/`M2` and `V1`; Section 4 correctly excludes them from order traces.

Flattened trace shapes are largely right, but the Order DFG omits the repeat edges implied by O100’s two `Add Item` and two `Pack Package` events. The Item OC-DFG is wrong: I2 never participates in `Reserve Stock`, so `Add Item → Pack Package` must exist. The interaction summary misses central pairs such as O100–O200, O100–I3, and P1–O100/O200. The Petri-net discussion treats D1 and D2 as type-level transitions and overclaims a uniquely correct concurrency interpretation.

Assertion 6 fails logically: every object is referenced, so the referenced IDs equal the object set, not a strict subset. Assertion 4 also invents an invoice total of 250, which the narrative does not state.
