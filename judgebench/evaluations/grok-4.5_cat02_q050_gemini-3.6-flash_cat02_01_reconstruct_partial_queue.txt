## Major deficiencies (each lowers the score materially)

**Numerical / reconstruction errors**
- Reported station means do not match the data under the paper’s own mid-point rule. S2 completed wait “36.4 min” and S1 completed wait “61.5 min” are inconsistent with direct summation of the table (approx. 33–34 min and ~63–65 min respectively). S2 service “39.2” vs central-imputed “34.6” are swapped/incoherent with the same mid-points.
- Mid-point services of 15 min for P05/P11 are not “central” relative to the empirical S2 distribution (all fully observed S2 services lie in 25–50 min). The justification (“balanced \ldots historical medians”) is asserted, not shown, and contradicts the observed sample.
- Zero-length services are allowed in the pessimistic bound; with 5-minute rounding this is unrealistic and violates a sensible non-negativity + grid constraint.
- Peak/mean WIP figures and the “9 cases (11:50–12:00)” claim are not derived from an event calendar; a quick inventory check at that wall-clock time yields a different total WIP. No time-series of \(L_q(t), L_s(t)\) is ever produced.
- Little’s-Law check claims ~1 % agreement on a hand-picked window using a global mean sojourn rather than the cohort that actually arrived in the window, ignores initial/final inventory, and treats a clearly transient morning as near-steady-state. The precision is overstated.

**Missing analysis required by the prompt**
- Queue-length and total-WIP trajectories over the day are only verbally sketched; the mandated “clearly stated convention for simultaneous rounded events” is not applied to a concrete WIP path.
- Rounding uncertainty (\(\pm 2.5\) min per timestamp) is defined in A2 but never propagated into the metric ranges beyond the four missing starts.
- Case-level total sojourn is given only in scattered notes, not systematically for all completed cases.
- Resource busy-time ranges (560–630 / 535–585) are plausible order-of-magnitude but not reconciled with a minute-by-minute resource timeline that also respects the C2 outage and OPEN elapsed times.
- Open and waiting cases are acknowledged in the table yet effectively dropped from the quantitative station summaries and from the Python metrics (only mid-point imputed started activities enter `df_events`).

**Python block (fails the quality bar)**

- Waiting-for-S1/S2 cases never become rows, so station wait and WIP statistics are incomplete.
- `fully_completed_cases` keeps solely `state == "COMPLETE"`, silently discarding the three `COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START` cases that did finish.
- No WIP/queue profile, no sub-period arrival/departure table, no sensitivity output—yet §4–6 report precise numbers that the code cannot have generated.
- Utilization is a single mid-point ratio; the claimed ranges are not produced.
- `insert_case_service_waiting_time` / `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` are called, but on an incomplete projection and without demonstrating that their case-level semantics match the station-level quantities discussed in the text.

**Other clarity / consistency issues**

- Capacity “1.5 cases/h during outage” mixes units without stating the service-time assumption used.
- Several utilization and WIP figures are given to unjustified decimal places while the underlying timestamps are 5-minute grids.
- The answer never shows that a resource never executes two cases at once under the chosen imputations (only informal inspection).

Under the instructed hypercritical standard these are not minor nits; they break internal consistency and the “reproducible” requirement. Hence well below the near-flawless band.
