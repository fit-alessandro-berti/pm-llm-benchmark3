### 3. Bottleneck interpretation is inverted and unsupported

S2 service is systematically longer than S1 service (roughly 40-45 min vs 25-30 min), so S2 has lower throughput capacity throughout. Both queues grow because arrivals overload the system, but a 15-min **downstream** C2 outage cannot cause **upstream** S1 queue build-up, and removing S2 capacity cannot make S1 "the tighter constraint" after 11:10. After the outage both C1 (P09 11:00-11:40) and C2 (P08 11:15-12:00) are busy, contradicting the claim that S2 utilization "drops" while S1 rises. The "stable" conclusions include the false bottleneck switch, false peak WIP, and false completed-case count.
