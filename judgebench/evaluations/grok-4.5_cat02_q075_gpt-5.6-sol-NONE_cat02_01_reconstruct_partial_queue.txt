## Critical failures (drive the score down hard)

### 1. Internal numerical inconsistency (severe)
§4 claims S1 busy 510–580 min (U ≈ 71–81%) **and** mean active ≈ 0.7–0.8.  
Busy minutes / 360 = mean number in service ⇒ 510/360 ≈ 1.42, 545/360 ≈ 1.51.  
Reported “active 0.7–0.8” is essentially the utilization fraction, not case count. Mean WIP ≈ 4.3 = 3.5 + 0.8 is therefore dimensionally wrong; correct active should be ~1.4–1.6 and mean S1 WIP higher. Same class of error on S2 (busy ~538 ⇒ active ~1.5, not 1.1–1.2).  
Definitions in §2 are right; results in §4 contradict them. That is a core coherence failure.

### 2. Arithmetic errors on easy checks
- Completed sojourns sum to **2050**, not 2055. Mean ≈ **146.4**, not ~147; median of the listed 14 values is **145**, not 155.  
- Hand tally of fixed S1 service minutes (18 known completed + P21 elapsed 30) = **535**, not 510. Ranges should be ~535–605 (central ~570), not 510–580 / 545. S2 is off by ~5 as well.  
These are not rounding disputes; they are plain addition mistakes on data the answer itself lists.

The task required reproducible calculation of the reported quantities. Prose and code diverge.
