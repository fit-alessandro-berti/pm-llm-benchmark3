**The flaw:** The inequality is inverted.

#### 2. Interference Contradiction in the Proposed Pilot Design (Moderate Flaw: −0.4 points)

> *"Assignment: **case-level randomization 50/50** at triage-done: 'mandatory review' (P0) vs 'reviewer-discretion' arm; randomize within time blocks to permit cluster-level spillover estimation."*

**The flaw:** 
1. If you randomize 50/50 *within* every time block (stratified individual randomization with a fixed 0.5 treatment saturation), there is **zero designed variation in treatment saturation across clusters**, which defeats standard two-stage cluster-level spillover estimation! Both P0-arm and P2-arm cases sit in the exact same partially-relieved queue (50% P2 saturation), severely attenuating the P0 vs. P2 waiting-time contrast via mutual interference.
2.
