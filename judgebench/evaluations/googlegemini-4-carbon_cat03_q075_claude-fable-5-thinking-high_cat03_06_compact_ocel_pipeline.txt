### Detailed Critique & Minor Deductions

1. **PM4Py API Version Assumption (`discover_otg` / `discover_etot`) (-0.2):**
   While the prompt explicitly mandates using `pm4py.discover_otg` and `pm4py.discover_etot` (so including them in Section 2 is 100% compliant and must not be penalized in the code itself), Section 1 explicitly asks for *any version-dependent PM4Py assumption*. In reality, standard public releases of PM4Py 2.7.x do **not** expose `pm4py.discover_otg` or `pm4py.discover_etot` at the top-level namespace (`AttributeError: module 'pm4py' has no attribute 'discover_otg'`). Recognising that these two specific functions are non-standard/hypothetical or require a specific wrapper/branch in Section 1 (and Section 7.7) would have elevated the version-awareness discussion from very good to flawless.

2. **Within-Activity E2O Asymmetry (`e07` vs. `e08`) (-0.1):**
   Under your "source-faithful" policy, `e07` (`Pack Package` for `P1`) links to `Order` (`O100`, `O200`) because the narrative explicitly notes *"The event relates to both Orders"*, whereas `e08` (`Pack Package` for `P2`) omits `O100` because the sentence *"Pack Package P2 with I2"* does not re-state the order. While you explicitly document, justify, and trace the consequences of this choice consistently across Sections 1, 3, 4, and 5, from a strict source-system/ERP schema perspective, it creates a *schema-level* irregularity within the exact same activity type (`Pack Package` records `source_order` when multi-order, but drops the foreign key when single-order). A brief acknowledgment in Section 1 or 7 that literal narrative-faithfulness here induces intra-activity schema heterogeneity in the WMS/ERP logging logic would have sharpened the modeling critique.

3. **Duplicate Relation Validation Scope (-0.1):**
   In Section 2.F, the check for duplicate E2O relations is implemented as:
   ```python
   assert not relations.duplicated(subset=["ocel:eid", "ocel:oid"]).any()
   ```
   And `o2o` is checked across all columns (`o2o.duplicated().any()`). While disallowing multiple roles for the same `(eid, oid)` pair holds for this dataset, in strict OCEL 2.0 semantics, the primary key of E2O relations includes `ocel:qualifier` (an object can theoretically participate in one event in two distinct qualified roles, just as two objects in `o2o` could have two distinct qualified relationships). Conversely, in `o2o`, checking `.duplicated()` without `subset=["ocel:oid", "ocel:oid_2"]` allows `M1 -> V1` to simultaneously have both `partially_settles` and `completes_settlement` without triggering the assertion. The uniqueness constraints between `relations` and `o2o` are slightly asymmetric without explanation.
