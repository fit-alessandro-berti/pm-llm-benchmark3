## Critical and material flaws
1. **Direct contradiction in central forecast / CDF claims (most serious)**  
   Section 4 states a “central/P50-like” range of +71–78 h (Mon ~14:30–20:00), a “coin flip” of release before Monday 20:00 (+76.5 h), *and* the cumulative “~40% of mass by +78 h, ~58% by +92 h”. These cannot hold simultaneously. If only ~40% is done by +78 h, the median lies later (in or after the Tuesday lobe), and “coin flip by Monday close” is false. The multimodal gap is acknowledged, yet the headline P50 and coin-flip language ignore the author’s own mass accounting. This is a logical flaw in the primary deliverable, not a rounding quibble.

2. **Earliest terminal times are optimistic about QA**  
   Tables and §4 put plausible earliest at Mon 09:30–11:00 (+66–67.5 h). That requires join ~08:30–09:00 *and* jumping a ~4–5 batch backlog with two reviewers so that acceptance + release finish by ~09:30–11:00. Even “expedite” rarely clears several jobs plus a full review in <1–2 h from Monday open. Mild-expedite language elsewhere (2–4 h waits) is inconsistent with the 09:30 earliest. S1b (chemistry finished Friday) still faces the same Monday QA queue; chemistry removal cannot produce a 09:30 release without strong queue-jump assumptions that are not stress-tested.

3. **Smooth-path probability and Monday-clear mass are loosely reconciled**  
   S1 at 40–55% (no rerun, clean micro, no query, no inv) is plausible as a product of marginals under a mixture, but the fraction of that mass (plus early S2) that actually clears Monday 20:00 is never cleanly integrated. The later CDF numbers (~40% by +78) are more credible than the coin-flip prose; the two were never forced to cohere. Overlaps are declared, yet the disjoint partition and the summary percentages do not fully line up.

4. **QA queue and service approximations are used as if firmer than they are**

Negative correlation (later join → shorter queue) is noted but not quantified into the blended central range.

5. **Chemistry Monday restart and FIFO chain are approximate to the point of fragility**
