### Major problems

1. **The chemistry calculation mishandles the priority case.**  
   Under the stated priority interpretation, the first available analyst takes the urgent sample, and the other can start B-417 on Friday around **16:42–17:30**. That gives B-417 **0.5–1.3 hours of Friday processing**, with the remainder resuming Monday.

Its available Friday capacity is also understated: after the current jobs finish, the two analysts have approximately **2.1–3.4 analyst-hours**, not 1.5–2.5.

   Chemistry can indeed dominate under FIFO; the problem is treating its dominance and late completion as too universal.

2. **Several “earliest plausible” times are not actually earliest plausible times.**  
   A smooth release before Monday 14:00 is feasible with priority processing, short chemistry service, and a favorable QA queue. More seriously, a chemistry rerun does **not** imply a Tuesday earliest release. An early Monday initial result followed by a 1–3-hour priority rerun can still permit Monday QA and release.

   The statement “QA then starts Tuesday” is unjustified when chemistry can finish Monday and QA remains open until 20:00.

4. **The reported hours and dates contradict one another.**
   From Friday 18 September at 15:30:
