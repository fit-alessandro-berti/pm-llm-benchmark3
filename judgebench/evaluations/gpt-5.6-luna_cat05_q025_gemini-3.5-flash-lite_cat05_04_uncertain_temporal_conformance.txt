### 1. Systematic mishandling of timestamp-resolution uncertainty

The answer states that HUB has `U_res = [0,0]`, despite the metadata explicitly specifying measurement uncertainty of ±0.5 seconds. Thus a HUB timestamp with clock offset `[-1,+1]` should be approximately:

\[
T_{\text{true}}\in [T_{\text{displayed}}-1.5,\;T_{\text{displayed}}+1.5].
\]

For example, `2026-06-01 08:00:00` should be:

```text
2026-06-01T07:59:58.500Z to 2026-06-01T08:00:01.500Z
```

not `08:00:00` to `08:00:02`.

The same issue affects the SENSOR timestamps. SENSOR has ±5 seconds of rounding uncertainty **and** a clock offset of ±5 seconds, so `08:51:10` should span approximately `08:51:00` to `08:51:20`, not `08:51:05` to `08:51:15`.

This is a fundamental failure because nearly every derived duration is based on incorrect event intervals.
