### Major defects

1. **Impossible and inconsistent terminal times**
   - Smooth-path “earliest plausible” **Monday 08:20–08:50** is impossible: without weekend authorisation, the earliest microbiology readout is Monday 08:00–09:30, after which QA service—median 1.5 h—and release still remain. 2. **Chemistry queue and closing-time rules are violated**
   - The smooth path claims B-417 starts by 16:00 and finishes Friday by 17:30. That requires jumping four samples ahead—and in particular the urgent sample—and obtaining almost exactly the 1.5 h minimum service time. It contradicts the 45% risk-priority branch, B-417 being only “high” rather than critical, and the prohibition on finishing after 18:00 unless started sufficiently early.

3. **Microbiology timing is wrong**
   - Incubation starting Friday 14:00 and taking 46–54 h becomes ready **Sunday 12:00–20:00**, not Sunday 20:00–22:00.
   - Contamination does **not** “double incubation time”; the specification is an additional 20–30 h plus readout.
   - Weekend authorisation is repeatedly treated as though a Sunday microbiology result accelerates QA on Sunday, although QA has no Sunday hours. Consequently, the claimed benefit to the terminal date is greatly overstated.

4. **QA queue is asserted, not derived**
   - The claim of 8–10 batches awaiting review on Monday morning is not supported by an arrival–service balance. Starting from 5, expected Friday-evening and Saturday arrivals are only about 3, while Friday and Saturday reviewers perform some work. 5. **Probabilities and terminal-event claims are invented or unsupported**

The answer is therefore directionally structured but not temporally or probabilistically reliable. Its central Monday–Wednesday order of magnitude is plausible, but the detailed dates, probabilities, chemistry treatment, QA calculations, and tail claims are sufficiently flawed to prevent a passing high score.
