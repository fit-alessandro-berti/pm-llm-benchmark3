The required six-section format is present, and the answer does not merely add stage means. That is not enough. Several clocks, probabilities, and terminal times are internally inconsistent or incompatible with the stated calendar.

## Material errors
- Incubation readiness is mis-timed. From Friday 14:00, 46–54 h is Sunday 12:00–20:00, not Sunday 20:00–22:00. The forecast appears anchored near 15:30 rather than the recorded start.
- The smooth path has chemistry starting by 16:00 and finishing by 17:30. That ignores one urgent and three ordinary samples, two analysts with only 2.5 h left, and minimum service of 1.5 h. Under FIFO this is implausible; under risk priority the urgent sample should take the first freed analyst, so B-417 likely cannot finish Friday.
- Earliest completion is impossible and contradictory: Monday 08:20–08:50 in §2, about 12:00 in §4, and 14:15–14:45 in §3. QA opens at 08:00, still needs service time, and release takes 0.25–0.75 h.
- “Wednesday 22 Sept” is the wrong date. Tuesday is 22 September; Wednesday is 23 September.
- Weekend microbiologist authorization cannot move the terminal event to Sunday. Technical readiness is Sunday at the earliest, and QA has no Sunday hours. It changes Monday readout timing by hours, not “every downstream stage.”
- Rejection at 15–25% is unsupported. Contamination, queries, and investigations are not stated to be confirmed critical defects. Release should dominate; rejection probability is not identified.
- An 8–10 batch Monday queue is used as the base case, but Friday evening plus Saturday QA capacity makes that a high draw, not a central one. It also contradicts the Monday-noon earliest case.
- The P90 band ending Friday 25 September understates the specified 3–7 calendar-day laboratory-investigation tail.

## Weaker but still important gaps
Scenario 3 conflates ordinary Monday readout delay with a 20–30 h confirmation cycle. Scenario probabilities acknowledge overlap, but the “smooth” 40% case is actually an optimistic chemistry subcase. Dependence is named without any qualified adjustment to rerun, contamination, or investigation probabilities. The decomposition rarely shows an explicit `max(chemistry, microbiology)` calculation, and “addifies” is not analysis. The assumption ledger omits unknown service times for the four samples ahead, urgent-over-high priority, no overtime, and QA’s Sunday closure.

§6 therefore selects the wrong primary update. Temperature-excursion confirmation, or evidence that chemistry will or will not spill past 18:00, would move both the central forecast and the tail more than weekend authorization.
