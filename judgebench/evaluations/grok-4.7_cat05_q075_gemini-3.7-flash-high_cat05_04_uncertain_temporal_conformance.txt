7.5

The interval arithmetic, SLA/profile classifications, and zeta/tolerance flips are largely correct, including several one-second edge cases. That core is not enough for a near-flawless score.

Material defects: Section 6 falsely claims sub-50 ms NTP would shrink T-401 S1 to `[798.5, 856.5]` and make it a certain pass; minute flooring alone still leaves a maximum near `901.5 s`. The required PM4Py block is not executable as written (`conformance_temporal_profile` does not take those keyword arguments), the SLA classifier is dead code, and directly-follows versus eventually-following semantics are ignored. Smaller issues include treating `T(IS) ≥ min T(AR)` as the causal constraint, closed `+60 s` floors, non-ISO timestamps, and no same-clock correlation analysis on the few-second borderline cases.
