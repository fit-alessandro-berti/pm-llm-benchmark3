The submission is an unusually strong, internally consistent process-mining reconstruction. It follows the required seven-heading structure exactly, keeps observations / estimates / bounds / hypotheses distinct, treats MISSING / OPEN / blank as different phenomena, never silently point-imputes missing starts, includes right-censored work in WIP and utilization, and supplies a coherent time-varying bottleneck argument backed by rate, queue, and cutoff-WIP evidence. ## Strengths

## Defects (hypercritical)
1. **Missing-start upper-bound logic (minor methodological softness)**  
   The code takes “next start ≥ own end.” A fully general non-overlap bound should also consider any same-resource start that falls inside `(entry, own_end)` and tighten the upper bound accordingly. The supplied log has no such collision, so results are unaffected, but the stated algorithm is slightly incomplete.

2. **Peak-queue invariance asserted without a full sweep**  
   The claim that peak Q/WIP stays 7/8 and 5/7 under all three scenarios is true for this log (the binding peaks occur while the missing-start cases are still forced to wait), yet the text does not show the short interval-by-interval check that would make the claim obvious. A reader must re-derive it.

3. **Coordinate-wise rounding envelopes are extremely loose**  
   Utilization outer bounds of roughly 61–98 % (S1) are correctly labelled “conservative / not confidence intervals,” but they add little decision value; a joint or resource-feasible tightening would have been stronger. This is a presentational rather than logical flaw.

4. **Runtime / API brittleness in the PM4Py block**  
   Defensive `if updated is not None` handling is sensible, yet the block does not guard against absence or signature change of `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`. Because the benchmark itself names that function, the fault is shared; still, a production-grade script would catch `AttributeError`.

5. **Tiny presentational nits**  
   - Column title `completed_case_total_sojourn` is reused for right-censored ages.  
   - A couple of narrative sentences pack three quantitative claims at once, slightly hurting scannability.  
   - No compact Gantt / resource timeline is printed to let a reader visually confirm non-overlap (not required, but would have removed residual doubt).

None of the above alters any headline number, the bottleneck conclusion, or the stability claims. The work remains near ceiling quality for the stated rubric.
