   - Therefore a third queued sample can sometimes begin Friday, and the first post-current job need not be paused.
   
   This is not a small detail: it changes the FIFO chemistry path, Monday queue-clearing order, the chance of chemistry finishing early Monday, and hence the probability of clearing QA before Monday close.
