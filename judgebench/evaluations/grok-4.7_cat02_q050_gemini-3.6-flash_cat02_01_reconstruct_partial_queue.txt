5.2

The case-level reconstruction is mostly competent, but the station-level results, uncertainty claims, and reproducibility do not hold up under the data.

Case waits, services, and sojourns for fully observed intervals are generally correct, and the resource-constrained windows for P05, P07, P11, and P15 are defensible. Elapsed service is correctly separated from unobserved final service, and completed-case sojourn is rightly treated as invariant to start imputation. The morning arrival-surge versus lower S2 capacity narrative is directionally plausible.

Those strengths are outweighed by material errors. S1 system WIP peaks at 8, not 9; S2 peaks at 7 during 13:45–13:50, not 6 at 14:00. The reported waiting, service, busy-time, and utilization figures are not recoverable from the case table: central S1 busy time is about 575 minutes (about 80% of 720), not 81.9% on a 560–630 range, and central S2 busy time is about 545 of 705 minutes, not 79.4%. Section 6’s service means likewise do not follow from its own imputations. “S1 fully cleared its queue by 13:30” is false because P22 is still waiting. The Little’s Law check uses an incorrect arrival count and an incorrect window WIP (about 6.8, not 6.10) and then overclaims a 1% steady-state confirmation. Sections 3 and 6 also disagree on whether zero service is allowed.

Rounding is named but not propagated; censored means are not identified as downward-biased elapsed values; and the code silently midpoint-imputes, does not calculate WIP or the three scenarios, and would not reproduce the prose utilization. The required format is present, but the numerical core is internally inconsistent.
