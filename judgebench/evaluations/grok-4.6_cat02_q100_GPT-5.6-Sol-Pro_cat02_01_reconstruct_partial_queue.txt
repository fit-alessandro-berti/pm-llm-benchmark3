The submission is a genuinely strong process-mining reconstruction: assumptions are labeled, missing starts are interval-censored rather than silently filled, OPEN/BLANK/MISSING stay distinct, open work is kept in busy-time and WIP, utilization is not inferred from completions alone, and the bottleneck claim is time-varying with evidence separated from hypothesis. Almost every headline number I recomputed (case sojourns, S1/S2 wait and service scenario totals, 2085/360 and 1340/360 mean WIP, rate counts and net WIP, resource busy minutes, Little windows, 14:00 WIP 2 vs 6) matches.

What keeps it well short of 9.5–10 under a strict reading:

- Section 1 math is sloppy (`D_{\text{reported}}-5),,D_{\text{reported}}+5`, extra parentheses around rounding intervals). That is a real clarity defect in the notation section.
- The case table mixes two different uncertainty languages in one column (`d ± 5` rounding envelopes vs imputation ranges `a–b; M=m`). The legend explains it, but it is easy to misread P01’s “5 ± 5” as the same kind of object as P05’s “20–50”.
- WIP snapshots and the 10:00 S2 state are midpoint-only. Under service-light starts, S2 queue at 10:00 is 3 not 2; the text asserts peak invariance but does not show that several interior snapshots are scenario-dependent.
- The 5-minute inter-job gaps persist even with long S1 queues. Treating median service 30/40 min as nominal capacity (~4/h vs ~3/h) without a short sensitivity on “gap is rounding” vs “gap is changeover” is a missed robustness check, even though the time-varying bottleneck conclusion would likely survive.
- Little’s-Law residuals are reported as 0.03 for both stations; S2’s 1.73 vs 1.69 is ~0.04 at the displayed rounding. Windows are reasonable but only weakly motivated.
- The Python meets the required checklist and the pandas reconstruction is the actual source of truth, but `insert_case_*` calls are wrapped in `if updated is not None` and `convert_log_to_time_intervals` is only commented out. That is defensive rather than a clean demonstration, and those branches may not execute as written.

None of these overturn the analysis. Definitions, dependence of wait/service given fixed sojourn, censoring treatment, C2 outage interpretation, and the stable-vs-unstable conclusions in §6 are all at a high professional standard.
