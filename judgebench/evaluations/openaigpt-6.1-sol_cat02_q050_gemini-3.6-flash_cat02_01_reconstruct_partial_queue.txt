### The Little’s-Law validation is invalid

- There are **14 departures**.
- WIP increases from **4 to 7**.

The interval is not shown to be stationary or approximately balanced. Multiplying an arrival rate by an inadequately specified average sojourn time does not establish Little’s Law “with high precision.”
