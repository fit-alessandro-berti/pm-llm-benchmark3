3.5

The required document shape is present, and several intended traces are classified correctly at the business level, but both formal models are non-executable and semantically wrong. Exactness claims contradict the code.

The process tree is not a valid tree and does not implement the stated approximation. `Operator.LEAF` is not a PM4Py operator; leaves must have `operator=None`. The same `risk_xor`, `supplier_xor`, `execute`, and `inspect` objects are inserted under multiple parents, so parent pointers overwrite and the object graph is not a tree. The PM4Py loop is `LOOP(do, redo) = do (redo do)*`; the redo child `Adjust, Execute, Inspect` therefore inserts an extra unadjusted trial. Even after cloning nodes, `Execute Trial` starts only after the whole initial block, including the approval route and supplier choice, so requirement 7 is lost. The expression is not PM4Py syntax and does not match the code. Section 1 calls the policy conservative under-approximation, section 3 calls the parallel an over-approximation, and section 6 calls it sequential under-approximation.

The POWL model misses the central construct. `OperatorPOWL` is imported and never used, despite being required for choice and looping. A `StrictPartialOrder` executes every node exactly once and must be acyclic. The edges `Execute → Inspect → Adjust → Execute` are a cycle and also make `Adjust Parameters` mandatory, so every one-pass trace is excluded and two iterations are impossible. Supervisor, Quality, and Regulatory are all predecessors of release, so there is no low/high-risk XOR. `Consult Supplier` is likewise mandatory; `skip_supplier → Release` does not create a skip. The concurrency explanation is therefore about a model that cannot generate the intended language.

Witness verdicts do not follow from these models. T1–T5 are marked POWL-allowed, but the POWL requires the missing reviews, supplier consultation, and adjustment, and cannot repeat `Execute Trial`. T2’s reason says approvals overlap the trial, yet both reviews complete before `Execute Trial`. T7 cites dependency 3 for a missing `Plan Verification`, which is dependency 2/7. Assumption 4 says visible activities occur once, while T3 and T5 repeat trial labels. Section 6 marks risk choice, optional consultation, repeat-until, and release synchronization as exact for POWL; none of those is exact. The self-critique notices a weak loop exit but not the cycle, the absent XOR, or mandatory adjustment.
