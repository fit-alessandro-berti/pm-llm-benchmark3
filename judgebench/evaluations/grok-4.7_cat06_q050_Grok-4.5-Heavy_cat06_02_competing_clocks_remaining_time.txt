The required structure is present and several hard ideas are handled correctly: join as a maximum, weekend pauses, overlapping scenario bands, temperature-driven dependence, and a Monday earliest bound rather than a false Friday finish. That is not enough to offset material temporal errors.

The delayed-microbiology clock is wrong. A Monday-morning indication plus 20–30 h of confirmation incubation is ready about Tuesday 04:30–15:30 and readable Tuesday, not “Tuesday 05:00–Wednesday 15:00.” The scenario table therefore shifts that path’s central and tail ranges about a day late, and it also misses the left tail in which weekend on-call starts confirmation earlier. The headline mapping is likewise inconsistent: 100 h after Friday 15:30 is Tuesday 19:30, not “early Wednesday,” and 180 h is early Saturday 26 September, not Friday 25 September. Sections 2–4 do not even agree with each other on the smooth central window.

Chemistry queue arithmetic is internally contradictory. Minimum service is 1.5 h, so the claimed 1.5–2.5 residual analyst-hours cannot clear two samples; Analyst A’s Friday slot is also below 1.5 h, while both in-service samples finish Friday with certainty. The in-service versus “four waiting” ambiguity is never ledgered. FIFO is stated as the 55% majority regime, yet the smooth-path start of Monday 09:00–13:00 is a priority-leaning case; the ledger’s own half-day FIFO penalty is not carried into the central decomposition. The unconditional P50 remains anchored on the smooth path even though that path is only given probability 0.30–0.50.

Non-smooth scenarios are not decomposed stage by stage as required, the rerun earliest time (Tuesday 09:00) contradicts the Monday-evening finish allowed in the prose, and rejection is variously “<0.05–0.10,” “low single digits,” and implied 10–15%. The assumption ledger, driver ranking, and temperature-flag update are the strongest parts, but the forecast’s actual clocks are not reliable enough for a higher score.
