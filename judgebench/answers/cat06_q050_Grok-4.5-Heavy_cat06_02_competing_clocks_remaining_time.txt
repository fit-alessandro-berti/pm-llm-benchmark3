## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

**Smooth path (most mass)**  
- Chemistry waiting & service: Friday residual capacity after the two analysts free (≈ 1.5–2.5 analyst-hours) clears 0–2 samples under mixed discipline. Most residual work (2–4 samples) resumes Monday 08:00 with two analysts. Clear time to B-417 start ≈ 1–4 h → start Monday ≈ 09:00–13:00. Service triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) (central ≈ 2.5–3.0 h). Usable result Monday ≈ 11:00–15:00. (Any unfinished at 18:00 pauses until Monday 08:00.)  
- Microbiology incubation & readout: continuous 46–54 h → technical ready Sunday 12:00–20:00. Without on-call (P ≈ 0.55–0.75) readout Monday 08:00 + 0.5–1.5 h → usable Monday 08:30–09:30. On-call (P ≈ 0.25–0.45) can give Sunday evening usable, but this rarely binds.  
- Parallel join: max(chemistry, microbiology) = chemistry time Monday 11:00–15:00 (microbiology is earlier). Maximum, not sum.  
- QA waiting & service: residual queue at join central 1–4; two reviewers from 08:00; wait 0.5–2.5 h + service median 1.5 h (P80 ≈ 3.5 h) → acceptance Monday 14:00–20:00 or Tuesday morning.  
- No rework/investigation.  
- Electronic release 0.25–0.75 h continuous → terminal Monday 16:00 – Tuesday 14:00.  

**Chemistry-rerun path**  
- First chemistry service as above, result typically Monday afternoon. Rerun (P base 0.10–0.20) receives priority, triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0); may finish Monday evening or, if residual hits 18:00, Tuesday morning. Join = later chemistry time.  
- Microbiology still Monday morning → join Monday late / Tuesday.  
- QA then starts Tuesday; normal service → terminal Tuesday–Wednesday. Calendar pauses only if service crosses 18:00/20:00.  

**Delayed-microbiology path**  
- Primary readout Monday morning (or Sunday evening). Contamination indication (base P 0.05–0.12) starts additional continuous 20–30 h incubation. Technical ready Tuesday 05:00 – Wednesday 15:00; staffed readout adds 0.5–1.5 h → usable Tuesday morning – Wednesday afternoon (central Tuesday afternoon).  
- Chemistry already finished Monday → join = microbiology confirmation time.  
- QA starts after that join (Tuesday/Wednesday open hours, two reviewers) → terminal Tuesday late – Thursday.  

**QA-query / doc-only-investigation path**  
- Branches finish as in smooth or mild-anomaly cases (join Monday or early Tuesday).  
- After partial QA review a Documentation Query (P 0.15–0.30) or doc-only investigation (majority of the 0.04–0.10 base investigation probability) is raised. Adds 4–12 working hours (query) or 8–20 working hours (doc-only). Working hours follow QA calendar only → calendar delay typically 0.5–3 days.  
- Resume QA, finish, then electronic release → terminal Wednesday–Friday (or Monday following if late). Laboratory investigation (remaining 20–40 % of investigations) adds 3–7 calendar days and moves into the severe band.  

**Severe correlated path**  
- Temperature confirmation (P 0.20–0.40) simultaneously raises both anomaly probabilities and investigation probability (0.30–0.55). Dual rework + laboratory investigation produces join mid-week and then +3–7 calendar days → terminal next week or beyond. Dependence is treated by conditional inflation rather than independent multiplication.  

In every path the parallel join uses the maximum of the two branch completion times; weekend pauses (chemistry Friday 18:00 → Monday 08:00; microbiology staffing; QA Saturday only 09:00–13:00, no Sunday) are applied explicitly.

## 4. Forecast summary

All figures are ranges (approximate arithmetic from the envelopes above).

- **Plausible earliest completion**: Monday 21 September 2026 ≈ 14:00–16:00 (remaining ≈ 70–74 h). Requires priority discipline, short residual, short service, low QA queue, no rework.  
- **Central / P50-like remaining-time range**: 75–100 h → terminal Monday evening 21 September – Tuesday afternoon / early Wednesday 22–23 September.  
- **P80-like remaining-time range**: 110–160 h → terminal Wednesday 23 September – Friday 25 September.  
- **P90-like / severe-tail range**: 180–350+ h → terminal Friday 25 September through mid-next week (≈ 28 September – 6 October) or later under laboratory investigation.  
- **Most plausible terminal event(s)**: Electronic Release (probability ≳ 0.85–0.90). Batch Rejected occurs only under confirmed critical defect (low single-digit percent overall).

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Chemistry residual queue position, discipline mix and service-time draws** — primarily queue uncertainty + service-time uncertainty. Sets the dominant join clock on the smooth path.  
2. **Temperature-excursion confirmation and the induced dependence on anomaly rates** — dependence uncertainty + observation uncertainty + path uncertainty. Inflates joint rework and investigation probabilities.  
3. **Microbiology contamination indication (and secondary on-call authorisation)** — path uncertainty + calendar uncertainty. Moves the join from Monday into Tuesday–Wednesday.  
4. **QA residual queue length at the (future) join time and arrival overdispersion** — queue uncertainty. Determines whether QA finishes the same day or spills.  
5. **Whether QA raises a documentation query or opens an investigation (especially laboratory)** — path uncertainty. Converts a same-week finish into a multi-day or next-week finish.

Calendar constraints (weekend pauses, limited Saturday QA) amplify the effect of any of the above when they push activity across Friday/Saturday/Sunday boundaries.
