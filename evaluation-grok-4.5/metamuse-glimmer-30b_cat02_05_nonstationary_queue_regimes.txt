## Major defects (score-driving)
1. **Broken / unreadable quantitative text.** §2’s “WIP **475235**” is clearly 47/52/35 smashed together. Operators are missing throughout (“completions arrivals”, “W = L / .”, “FTE  ~3.4”). §6 has empty implied-flow cells (“~ ~”). For a graded analytical memo this is not cosmetic—it destroys checkability.
2. **Outage blended into recovery in the primary table.** Prompt quality bar: do *not* treat outage recovery as a normal high-throughput regime, and treat outage and recovery explicitly. Averaging 2026-04-15 (58 completes, WIP 47) with 16–17 (129/112 completes) into one row with completion mean ~99.7 actively re-hides the outage. Narrative caveats do not fix a misleading regime summary.
3. **Little’s Law time base is under-argued and partly wrong in spirit.** Using \(W = L/\lambda\times 1440\) without a careful open-hours vs calendar-time argument is a real competence gap: service is only 07:00–19:00, overnight carry exists, and `mean_wip` is snapshot-based with an unspecified window. Marking “approximate only” helps, but the matrix still prints point estimates as if the 1440 conversion were settled. Intervention window arithmetic is also slightly off (~463 min at \(L=37,\lambda=115\), not ~466).

6. **Intervention and weekend story incomplete.** Intervention is weekday-only (29 Apr–1 May) while 2–3 May also show higher weekend FTE (3.0 vs 2.5); primary weekend bucket still pools pre- and post-mix weekends even while the text admits service/complex shifted—exactly the pooling failure the answer criticizes elsewhere.
