Under strict grading, repeated consistency errors on `process_enabled`, several “next event” predictions that are really two-step successors, and shaky calibration on C6-D/C6-E pull the score well below the top band.

## Major issues (score-driving)

### 1. `process_enabled` is applied inconsistently
The task requires distinguishing enabled work from the next *logged* event. The answer often collapses them.

- **C6-A rank 2 (TI)**: `process_enabled: true` while the text says TI is enabled only after review and is “not the immediate next log.” That is self-contradictory. Under primary S3, TI is not currently enabled.
- **C6-B rank 2 (Close)**: Same problem unless the alternative (review already finished) is treated as primary; still marked enabled without conditioning.
- **C6-E rank 2 (SA Completed)**: `false` is defensible, but A’s TI is marked `true` under analogous “not yet enabled / maybe already running” logic. The flag is not a coherent semantics across cases.

### 2. Several “next logged” items are successor-after-next
Predictions should be immediate next observations (or honest mixtures under latent-state uncertainty).

- **C6-A**: Rank 1 ERC is right. Ranks 2–3 are mostly *after* ERC, inflated unless P(review already done) is material—which the alternative itself calls less likely under a normal feed.
- **C6-B**: Close as rank 2 is better motivated (duplicate/low-risk alternative), but still partly two-step.
- **C6-E**: **Supplier Analysis Completed** as the second most likely *next log* at 14:00, 40 minutes after second Evidence Received, with SA typically 6–36h and possibly not opened, is temporally weak. A better rank-2/3 set would emphasize another CER, TI, or delayed/concurrent investigation *start visibility*, not SA completion soon.

### 3. C6-D: right story, weak ranking/calibration
Strengths: migration-affected, attachment without CER/ER, RA opened 12:40, concurrent TI/RA.

Problems:
- **RA Completed** as rank 1 at **0.28–0.55** is aggressive: only ~1h20m since open against a 2–14h completion band and a 12-deep RA queue. Early completion is possible with priority, but the upper bound is too high for “next observed.”
- Delayed **Evidence Received / Customer Evidence Requested** are the distinctive migration insight, yet ranked below RA and capped lower, despite the case being explicitly migration-affected and the source already showing an attachment.
- **Evidence Review Completed** is in the latent state as likely done/delayed but never appears in the top three, even though if the evidence cycle ran before TI, review completion is as natural a catch-up event as CER/ER.
- `completed_obligations` improperly lists suspected operational evidence-cycle steps as completed. That blurs completed vs hypothesized.

### 4. Local analytical slips
- **C6-A**: “Evidence Review → TI = 173/356” misuses 356 (that count is ER→Review, not Review out-degree). Outgoing from review in the table is 101+173+68=342. Small, but it shows DFG numerators used as decorative confidence.

- **C6-C alternative (“RA not required”)** sits awkwardly beside the country-rule argument that RA is mandatory; the alternative should be framed mainly as “RA done but not logged / still queued,” which is already partly there, not “obligation absent.”

### 5. Probability coherence (strict view)
Intervals need not sum to 1, but midpoints should be a *top-three next-event* distribution.

- A: ~0.60 / 0.20 / 0.15 — OK if ranks 2–3 are true alternatives for the immediate next mark; weaker if they are multi-step.
- D: RA mid ~0.4 vs delayed evidence combined mid ~0.45 spread across two labels — the split is reasonable in spirit, but ordering and RA ceiling fight the elapsed-time and migration evidence the writeup itself cites.
- E: ERC mid ~0.55 is fine; SA completion mid ~0.15 is high for immediate next given timing.
