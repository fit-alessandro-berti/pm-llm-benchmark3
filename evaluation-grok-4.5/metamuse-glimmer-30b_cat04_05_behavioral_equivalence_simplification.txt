## Critical failures

### 1. Operator semantics are self-contradictory (severe)

Section 2 defines `+` as **AND / parallel** and ties that to the problem’s “concurrently” wording. Under that reading, Model B is

`+(Check Revision, *(Analyze…))`

so **both** branches must complete: there is **no** Check-only branch, and the loop runs at least once ⇒ at least one `Analyze Revision` whenever the case completes.

Yet the answer repeatedly relies on the opposite:

- Trace **B2** `Open, Check, Close` as B-only  
- Verdicts: B “allows Check-only traces without Analyze”  
- Final rec: filter B’s Check-only branch for P2  

That is XOR semantics, not AND. One of the two pillars is wrong; several verdicts, caveats, and counterexamples inherit the error. This is not a wording nit—it breaks P1/P2 reasoning and the counterexample table.

(If instead `+` is standard PM4Py **XOR**, then the “concurrency / parallel” structural story is wrong and Model A does **not** put a check with every analysis. The write-up never resolves this; it asserts both stories.)
