## Grading rationale (strict)

The answer is structurally complete, aligned with the required headings, and substantively strong on fairness framing. It correctly refuses raw-outcome = discrimination, keeps rights-preserving steps as potential burden, treats `risk_score_band` as non-neutral, and avoids “remove support to speed throughput.” Sections 1–2, 4–6, and 8 are mostly high quality. The score is pulled down hard by concrete inaccuracies, weak quantification, and a largely non-executable / statistically invalid PM4Py plan.

### Major deductions

**1. Section 7 (PM4Py) is seriously flawed (1.5 to 2.0)**  
Required as a core deliverable, yet much of the “code” would not run and several analyses are methodologically wrong:

- Variant  approval extraction is nonsensical (`log[case[0].split('_')[0]]`, broken case indexing).
- `discover_performance_dfg` is used as if it returned a NetworkX-like `.graph.edges` object with a simple `'performance'` field; PM4Py’s usual return shape is different.
- `get_service_time`, `insert_case_service_waiting_time`, `discover_declare` are invoked with plausible-sounding but unreliable signatures/usage.
- “Logistic regression” is fit on a tiny set of **stratum-level aggregated rates**, with `group` encoded as a numeric index 0–3 (treated as continuous), and `y > 0.5` as a binary label—statistically invalid for the claimed adjusted group effects.
- Several prompt-listed tools are barely used or omitted in any serious way (`discover_organizational_roles`, `discover_activity_based_resource_similarity`, `filter_case_performance`, `filter_paths_performance`).

A fairness *plan* can be pseudocode, but presenting broken APIs and invalid inference as if they support the assessment is a large defect under strict grading.

**2. Quantitative errors in path/resource analysis (0.8)**  
- Language-support volume: Group B needs language support at 57% of 1250  **713**, not “~400.” V4 is 24%  300. The bottleneck story is directionally right; the arithmetic is wrong.

- Queue wait claim (“57 cases/worker, 1-day service  ~8 days wait; observed 3.8 is lower than expected”) is not valid queueing reasoning (ignores arrival process, concurrency, batching, multiphase work). Invented precision undermines §3.

**3. Over-precise claims not in evidence (0.4)**
