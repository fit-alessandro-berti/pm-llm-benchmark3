## Defects that materially lower the score

### 1. Misuse of `process_enabled` (recurring conceptual error)
Required distinction: whether the **action is currently process-enabled**, not merely whether it can appear later on some path.

- C6-A rank 3 and C6-B rank 3 mark **Customer Evidence Requested** as `process_enabled: true` while the case is still in Evidence Review (Evidence Received has occurred; Review has not completed). A further evidence cycle is only enabled **after** Evidence Review decides to re-request. Marking `true` collapses the required distinction.
- Contrast with their own correct `false` on Tech Investigation Completed / Close / Disposition when prerequisites are unfinished. The inconsistency shows the flag was not applied rigorously.

This alone prevents a top-tier score.

### 2. Weak / process-incoherent rank-3 on C6-C
**Authority Notification** as the third most plausible *next logged* event is a stretch:
- Rule 6 and the DFG both place notification after disposition when reportable (`Disposition Recorded  Authority Notification`).
- Reg completion is not yet visible; disposition is not yet visible.

Reasoning is hedged (“sequencing allows”) but does not justify top-3 status as next log line. This looks like residual “reportability keyword” stuffing.
