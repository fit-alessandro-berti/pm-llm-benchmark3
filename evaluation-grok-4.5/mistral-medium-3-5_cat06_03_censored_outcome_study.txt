**1. Target definition**

Activation, Contract Signed and the start of the 90-day clock all lie *after* that prediction time for the great majority of prefixes. Conditioning the training population on a future event is classic selection bias / look-ahead; the estimand is therefore not identified from the observed early prefixes without an explicit selection model, IPW, or landmarking argument—none of which appear. The text simultaneously claims the target “handles competing risks (transfers/rejections)” while the population statement excludes those cases. The alternative multi-state formulation is mentioned but then ignored; the justification that the primary target “aligns with the business need for every application” is false on its face. This section alone contains fatal inconsistencies.

The cohort-flow table correctly sums to 48 000 and distinguishes matured / immature / competing, but then labels rejects and withdrawals simply “competing outcome (no default)”—exactly the semantic move the instructions forbid when the target clock starts only at activation. Transferred cases are called “partially observed” with no linkage or missingness mechanism. No as-of rule is given for determining whether activation itself has been observed by the prediction time. The table therefore does not support the chosen estimand.

**3. Censoring and missing-label strategy**
