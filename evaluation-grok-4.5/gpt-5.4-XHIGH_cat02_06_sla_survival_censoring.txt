Deductions under a deliberately harsh standard (minor issues only):

- **Section 4 KM wording is slightly imprecise.** Treating non-resolve terminals as censoring makes 1KM an *over*-estimate of cause-specific CIF of resolve (hence an under-statement of “miss” if one naïvely reads 1KM as the resolve probability). Calling it merely an “upper-bound style estimate” without stating *of which quantity* is a small conceptual slip.
