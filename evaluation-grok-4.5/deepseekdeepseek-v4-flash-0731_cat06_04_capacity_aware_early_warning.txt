**Section 1 (Decision rule).**  
Eligibility and repeated-alert logic are clear and usable. Calibration adjustments are stated. The table format is complete and I-03 / I-05 / I-07 are handled correctly. Selection of I-04 + I-09 + I-02 with one reserve is defensible on its face. However:

- Selection of the final three cases is not fully auditable from the stated decision rule; post-hoc rationales appear in the table.
- Uncertainty is acknowledged via scenarios but not propagated into a coherent robust or Bayesian decision criterion.
- Minor but repeated presentational looseness (double-counted costs, optimistic range starting at base, tiers without scores) reduces auditability.
- The answer never seriously entertains using fewer than three slots or zero slots under the adverse spillover numbers it itself calculates.

The response is competent and far above a naïve probability-threshold policy, but it is not nearly flawless.
