### Major defects (score-driving)

1. **§7 hard requirement failure: no PM4Py**  
   The prompt requires PM4Py for process-mining ops (`parse_event_log_string`, group DFGs via `discover_dfg`, etc.). The code only does Pandas string flags (`has_compliance`, trace length). That is a direct miss on a named deliverable, not a style nit.

2. **§2 causal graph is not a usable directed adjacency list**  
   Arrows are missing/garbled; the block is not a clean adjacency list over the allowed vocabulary. Several implied directions are wrong or incoherent (e.g., paths that effectively put `resource_group` downstream of queueing; selection/`case_completed` handled murkily). Supported-vs-assumed commentary is better than the graph itself, but the required artifact is broken.

3. **§7 “complexity-standardized” contrast is the wrong estimand**  
   Inverse-variance pooling of stratum differences is **not** standardization to a stated common complexity distribution (e.g., standard population weights \(P(C)\) or Nova’s mix). For a causal-diagnostics section, this is a real method error, not an equivalent alternative.

4. **Verdict vs body tension**  
   Verdict is `mainly confounded` (OK as a provisional label), but the executive simultaneously treats night queueing, compliance coupling, and rework as “partially genuine” mechanisms and argues Orion is faster in-stratum. That is closer to **`partly causal through mechanisms`** or a sharper “mainly confounded for the crude gap; residual mechanisms uncertain.” Confidence 72 is fine; the labeling is sloppy relative to their own evidence ranking.

5. **Required hypothesis coverage is incomplete/mis-framed**  
   Prompt requires at least one hypothesis that Orion has a **beneficial effect masked by case mix**. The write-up’s narrative implies this, but the ranked hypotheses emphasize harmful Orion, harmful-masked-by-unobserved-risk, shift/load, compliance, selection, variants. Hyp 2 is not cleanly the required “beneficial masked by mix” claim; it partly fights the stratified pattern the answer elsewhere treats as central.
