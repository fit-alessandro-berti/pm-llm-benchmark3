## Section 1 — Drift table

**Serious problems:**

1. **Population shift = August–October is false on the given evidence.** Partner Z is 0% through August and appears in September (24%). August’s V3 jump (22%→35%) and breach spike predate Partner Z; blaming August on population shift confuses supplier-driven mix/performance change with onboarding.

2. **Concept drift is self-undermining.** The row cites parts delays and V3 breach spikes, then says there is “no explicit evidence” the feature–outcome link changed and only **Medium-Low** confidence. If \(P(Y\mid X)\) at diagnosis is unchanged and only path times worsened, that is primarily **process/performance drift** (and missing operational features), not a clean concept-drift call. July (V3 breach 0.46, parts edge 65h after the 10 Jul supplier event) is omitted.

3. **Covariate drift only Sep–Oct is incomplete.** June’s V2 share 35%→52% is a large input-mix/covariate shift; mean predicted risk stays ~0.20 while observed breach falls to 0.15 (ECE 0.060). Partner Z’s higher rural distance/device age are **unmeasured covariates** (population + representation failure), not only a Sep–Oct “reweight predictions” fix.

4. **Label delay only October understates the ramp.** Maturity is already 98%/93%/82% in Jul/Aug/Sep; median label delay rises 36→42→52d. October is the worst confounder, not the only one. Claiming June–Sep distortions “cannot” involve label delay is too absolute for Sep.
