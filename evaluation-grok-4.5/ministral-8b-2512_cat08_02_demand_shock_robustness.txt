The submission follows the exact required section structure and demonstrates basic awareness of bottleneck migration, scenario variation beyond pure volume, and the need for both standing and contingent policies. However, under strict grading it scores only mediocre because of pervasive grounding failures, fabricated quantifications, internal inconsistencies, shallow use of the supplied process-mining artifacts, and several violations of the explicit constraints.

**1. Stress scenarios**  
Four scenarios are listed and they nominally cover the required dimensions (mix shift, machine degradation, specialist absence, courier disruption). The descriptions remain superficial and partly contradictory to the data. “Machine Outage” invents a Routine-heavy mix (+10 % Routine) that is the opposite of the observed peak-period shift toward Urgent/Complex-panel; the courier scenario invents a night-shift strike and a +12 % Repeat-after-failure spike with no supporting frequency evidence. No quantitative linkage is made to the actual DFG arc frequencies or the given p90/median edges. This is already more than a “minor” defect.

**2. Bottleneck-migration analysis**  
Sequences are asserted for each scenario and a few queue numbers from the capacity table are repeated. The analysis never actually walks the frequency DFG (e.g., the 1 260 Repeat-assay arcs, the 610 Hold-for-specialist arcs, the 1 180 Batch-wait arcs in peak) nor the variant statistics (Urgent bypass dropping from 74 % to 58 %, Complex-panel 3.1\times medical-validation probability). Migration claims are generic (“relieving X may push to Y”) and sometimes reverse the direction shown in the performance DFG. The section therefore fails the “use of process-mining evidence” criterion at any rigorous level.

**3. Robust redesign package**  
I6 + I4 + I7 + I2 are offered as always-on. Every claimed quantitative benefit is invented out of thin air:  
- “recollection loops (760  300 cases)”  
- “accession correction 62 %  40 %”  
- “batch wait 6 h  3 h”  
None of these figures appear in, or can be derived from, the supplied logs.  
I2 is declared robust even though the problem statement’s own conflict hint explicitly warns that larger batches delay Urgent cases unless priority exceptions are tightly controlled; the package places the necessary control (I1) only in a scenario-specific policy, creating an immediate logical hole. Downstream overload discussion is perfunctory and is later contradicted by the trade-off table’s “None” entry. This directly violates the constraint “Do not recommend an intervention without discussing which downstream station it may overload.”

**4. Scenario-specific policies**  
Triggers are arbitrary round numbers unrelated to the observed medians or utilizations. The Machine-Outage policy simply restates I3; the Courier policy invents a registration pause never present in the candidate list. Activation/deactivation logic is incomplete (no hysteresis, no multi-signal confirmation). Acceptable at a checklist level, but not robust process reasoning.

**5. Trade-off table**  
The table contains the mandated columns yet is populated with unsupported percentages (“+30 % speed”, “–15 % throughput”, “–1 % rework”, etc.). The core bundle is given “None” under next-bottleneck risk—an assertion contradicted by the migration analysis two sections earlier and by the problem’s conflict hints. Implementation-burden and monitoring-signal columns are vague. The table therefore fails both factual grounding and internal consistency.

**6. Residual vulnerabilities**  
Four vulnerabilities are listed and one is correctly identified as redesign-induced (I1 fragmentation / I3 starvation). This is the strongest section, yet even here the mitigations are after-the-fact patches rather than design features, and the M2 reliability point ignores the already-supplied information that Repeat-after-failure cases cluster after M2 usage.

**7. Minimum monitoring dashboard**  
A usable list of leading versus lagging indicators appears, and a few thresholds echo the peak medians (pre-analytical >9 h, specialist hold >36 h). However, several bands are again invented, the “fragmentation” and “starvation” qualitative rules are not tied back to any DFG or variant statistic, and the dashboard never monitors the very rework and recollection loops that the robust package claims to fix. Adequate but not tight.

**Additional global defects**  
- Systematic fabrication of effect sizes violates the requirement to stay grounded in the given normal/peak summaries, DFGs, queues and variants.  
- The design still quietly adds capacity (evening medical shift, outsourcing) while claiming not to “add capacity everywhere.”  
- No serious treatment of the fact that Urgent, Complex-panel and Routine cannot be simultaneously prioritized—an explicit constraint.  
- Writing is clear but repeatedly over-precise where the data are silent, which is worse than honest qualitative reasoning.

Collectively these are not cosmetic flaws; they undermine the claim of “robust process reasoning” and “bottleneck-migration analysis.” A submission that merely copies the section headings and inserts plausible-sounding but unanchored numbers cannot receive a high score under the instructed hypercritical standard. Hence 5.0.
