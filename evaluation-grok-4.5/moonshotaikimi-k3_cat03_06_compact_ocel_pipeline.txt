## Grading rationale (strict)

### Strengths
- All eight required top-level sections are present and substantive.
- Code is self-contained, constructs all four OCEL tables in memory, covers all 17 events and stated objects, denormalizes activity/timestamp into relations correctly, and includes unusually thorough validation (uniqueness, orphans, type consistency, activity/timestamp equality, duplicates, O2O type directions, payment-amount placement).
- Modeling assumptions are explicit: qualifier vocabulary, O2O direction, direct vs propagated E2O, M2 completion caveat, and the isolated constructor line.
- Flattened-behavior predictions (traces, shared-event copying, omissions, DFG edges, misleading readings) are detailed and mostly consistent with a direct-E2O flattening assumption.
- Alternatives, failure modes (including all named ones), and manual semantic checks exceed the minimum counts and are generally high quality.
- Does not replace the OCEL with independent classic logs; keeps object-centric discovery central; does not claim a unique OC Petri net.

### Deductions (hypercritical)
1. **OC-DFG instance/type confusion (largest issue)**  
   Section 5 attributes O100/O200 and I1/I2/I3 *instance* paths to the OC-DFG. An OC-DFG is object-**type** level (combined Order/Item behavior), while instance paths come from flattening. This is a real conceptual error in the “expected object-centric structures” analysis, not a wording nit.

2. **Tension in “source-scoped” policy**  
   E07Orders is narrative-supported; E08O100 is not stated the same way and is closer to composition-propagated operational involvement. The write-up defends it, but still slightly blurs the line it claims to police between source facts and analytical enrichment.

3. **Over-specific DFG edge lists**  
   Exact edge sets/frequencies are asserted with limited hedging about discovery defaults, filtering, or start/end activity handling. Fine as “likely,” but presented more tightly than warranted.

4. **M2 `completes_settlement`**  
   Acknowledged as non-arithmetic, but still encoded as a first-class O2O fact. Acceptable as a choice; still a soft spot relative to “stated business facts only.”

5. **API executability residual risk**  
   Top-level names follow the prompt (`discover_etot`, `discover_otg`, etc.), and the constructor is isolated, but unpacking `discover_dfg` and assuming EventLog iteration from `ocel_flattening` remain version-sensitive. Not fatal given the prompt, but not bulletproof.

6. **Minor clarity/consistency nits**  
   OC-DFG vs interactions vs OTG responsibilities are mostly separated, but convergence language sometimes slides between instance graph facts (P1/V1) and type-level discovery outputs. Qualifier naming is consistent enough, but long and occasionally awkward (`packs_package` as eventpackage role).

### What would push it toward 9.5+
- Clean separation: instance paths only under flattening; type-level aggregates only under OC-DFG/OTG/ET–OT.
- Either drop E08O100 or mark it explicitly as a deliberate operational enrichment with a distinct qualifier family.
- Slightly more hedging on exact DFG/log-skeleton contents.
- Tighter alignment between “source-scoped” rhetoric and every E2O edge.

### Bottom line
This is a strong, coherent, high-effort solution with excellent validation and analysis breadth. It is **not** nearly flawless under the required hypercritical standard, mainly because of the OC-DFG instance/type mix-up and the residual source-vs-propagated softness around packing/orders. **8.3**.
