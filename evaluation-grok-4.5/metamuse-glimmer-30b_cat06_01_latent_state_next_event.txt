## Major flaws (score killers)

### 1. C6-B: Fast Close as a near-term next log event is poorly defended
Fast Close is normatively gated at triage under low severity / no injury / no multi-device or suspicious lot. Here the case already entered a full evidence cycle (`Customer Evidence Requested` → `Evidence Received`). Treating **Fast Close** as rank-2 next logged outcome with up to 0.40 probability is overconfident and process-sloppy. If closure happens after evidence, **Close — No Safety Issue** (or disposition after review) is the cleaner path; Fast Close looks like DFG-name matching, not control-flow reasoning. The alternative latent state “S1 TRIAGED … Fast Close still enabled” is especially weak after evidence receipt.

### 2. C6-A rank-3 Technical Investigation Completed is barely “next”
At 14:00, evidence arrived 12:10; review is not logged; TI queue is stressed; historical ER→TI median is ~8.5h. Calling TI completion a top-3 **next logged** activity with up to 0.20 is thin. A stronger third candidate would be something still near-term and obligation-linked (e.g., opening path toward supplier analysis after review, or delayed concurrent starts only as *enabled*, not as imminent log events). The answer blurs “eventually required” with “plausibly next in the log.”

### 3. C6-C: Supplier Analysis rank is under-motivated; Disposition is over-ranked
- Four complaints share a **failure mode across different lots**. Rule 5 ties Supplier Analysis to **same lot / supplier component**, not generic mode clustering. Ranking Supplier Analysis Completed third without stronger component/supplier evidence is speculative.
- Rank-2 **Disposition Recorded** at up to 0.50 fights the primary story (reportability signal → Regulatory still open). You cannot both emphasize mandatory RA and give disposition nearly equal mass unless the alternative S5 case is argued much more carefully with split conditions.
- Country-rule reportability is used well for RA, then partly undone by the disposition band.

### 4. C6-D prediction set is incomplete relative to stated state
Worklist says RA opened 12:40; TI completed 13:05; high severity + injury. Top-3 are RA completed + two delayed evidence events. That migration angle is good, but:

### 5. C6-E: Supplier Analysis Completed as next log event is early and soft
Second evidence at 13:20; review not done; SA typical 6–36h. Lot→six complaints correctly flags **mandatory SA obligation**, but rank-3 **Supplier Analysis Completed** confuses obligation with imminent completion. Better third options: Technical Investigation Completed (if opened after review), or a disposition/closure path only after SA—or explicitly “SA opened/in progress, completion later.” Completeness 0.72 supporting another evidence cycle is reasonable; SA completion today is not.
