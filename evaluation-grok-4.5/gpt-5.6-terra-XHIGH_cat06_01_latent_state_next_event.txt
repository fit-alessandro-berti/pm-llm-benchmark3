Once an evidence cycle has been opened, the normative and historical gate is Evidence Review Completed; Technical Investigation / Regulatory Assessment / Supplier Analysis completions appearing *before* that review event require either an unlogged parallel start that finishes first or an event-order anomaly. Those intervals should be materially lower (or the predictions replaced by “Customer Evidence Requested” / another review cycle). The same optimism appears, milder, for Supplier Analysis Completed in C6-E given the 6–36 h typical duration and only 40 min of elapsed time since the second receipt.

In C6-A the two investigation completions are marked enabled while the case is still in primary state S3; the rules state that Evidence Review “may \ldots open investigations,” implying the fork is not yet open. A truly near-flawless submission would have tightened the concurrent-completion probabilities, aligned every process_enabled flag with the normative enablement conditions, supplied non-redundant alternatives, and left explicit residual mass for the evidence-reopen and notification-batch paths.
