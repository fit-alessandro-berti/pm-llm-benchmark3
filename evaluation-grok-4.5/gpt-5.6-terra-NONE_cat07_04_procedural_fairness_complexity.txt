## Grading rationale (strict)

### Strengths
The answer is comprehensive, well-structured, and largely faithful to the required headings and constraints. ### Deductions (hypercritical)

**1. Bottom-line misses the required ternary (moderate)**  
§8 never cleanly selects *fair / unfair / indeterminate* for the process as a whole. “Credible signs of procedurally inequitable design” is thoughtful but evades the specified choice. A strict reading of the required output expects an explicit, calibrated label first, then nuance.

**2. Incomplete use of listed PM4Py surface (minor–moderate)**  
`filter_case_performance` is in the relevant-functions list and never appears in §7. That residual is one of the strongest procedural-unfairness signals in the brief; the answer notes the adjusted gaps but does not sharply leverage them (e.g., as evidence of differential processing rather than starting completeness alone). Same for residual appeal gaps.

**4. Covariate strategy tension (minor–moderate)**

Prose cites CEM/IPW/entropy balancing; code only keeps intersecting strata. That is not wrong as illustration, but it under-delivers on the strategy claimed and invites finite-sample / imbalance errors without diagnostics (SMDs, overlap, weights).
