## Evaluation

The answer is structurally complete, operationally literate in places, and hits the main traps (I-03 ineligible; I-05 ≠ I-07; spillovers; reserve slot; effect ≠ observational \(p\)). Under strict grading it is clearly not near-flawless: several material incoherences, one auditable arithmetic error, and weak operational realism on urgency and fairness-in-action.

### What works
- **Eligibility** correctly gates on pre–Diagnosis Complete and no repeat swarm without a new event; I-03 is refused on stage, not rank.
- **I-05 vs I-07** is handled as required: stale refresh suppressed vs new customer log re-entry.
- **Portfolio thinking** appears: capacity 3+1 reserve, spillover charge, multi-scenario impact, segment calibration notes, intervention flag / uplift monitoring, congestion-triggered recalibration.
- **Base impact arithmetic** for midpoints and costs is largely reconstructible (labour \(3×450\), FA \(2000+5000+600\)).

### Material flaws (score drivers)

**1. Decision rule is decision-theoretically half-built.**  
“Adjusted breach probability” (SME +0.06, SE −0.08, etc.) is defined and never enters the ranking objective. Prioritization is midpoint ARR × value − costs − flat externality. That leaves calibration adjustment as dead machinery and weakly links “known miscalibration” to allocate-or-not. A coherent rule would either adjust the *effect* prior, use value-of-information / robust EV on adjusted risk, or state explicitly that \(p\) only drives eligibility/alerting while ranking is pure uplift-EV (and then stop computing unused adjusted \(p\)).

**2. Required optimistic impact row is internally inconsistent.**  
Stated effects \(0.24 + 0.125 + 0.19 = 0.555\) breaches avoided, but the table claims **0.70**. That is not “approximation”; it fails the auditability the prompt asks for. Confidence labels (“Moderate/Medium/Low”) are decorative without tying to that error.

**3. “Wait” on I-07 (1.5 h to SLA) is operationally incoherent.**  
With a 45-minute swarm and a 1.5 h deadline, the action set is essentially intervene now vs forgo. Parking I-07 as **Wait** while intervening on I-08 (Triage, **8.0 h**, tiny ARR band, €5k FA burden) shows EV formalism without time-to-deadline or option-value of delay. Tiering also collides: I-07 and I-08 share “tier 3” with opposite actions; I-01/I-09 share tier 4—priority is not a total order.

**4. I-08 is a fragile flagship pick under-argued.**  
Lower-bound direct net \(0.05×100\text{k} - 450 - 5000 < 0\) before spillover; stage/timing is the least scarce; FA burden dominates. Adverse scenario correctly zeros I-08, which undercuts calling it a core “intervene” without a robust/minimax or probability-of-loss criterion. Preferring I-08 over a discounted I-09 (stronger ARR band, Diagnosis Started, 2.5 h) is defensible only with a much sharper uncertainty penalty story than “×0.7 then Wait.”

**5. Fairness safeguards do not constrain today’s portfolio.**  
Selected set is **I-04, I-08, I-02**—all Strategic Enterprise. The 20–40% SME/Public budget is nullified by “unless net &lt; 0” after a blunt €1,800 spillover tax that systematically wipes lower-value segments. That is an internal tension: §5 warns about value-starvation and spillover incidence on untreated SME diagnosis work, while §2 implements pure SE concentration. A strict read treats this as policy theater unless the answer shows a binding diversity/learning slot or a revised externality that does not hard-zero small cases.

**6. Spillover / capacity micro-structure is too coarse.**  
€1,800 = \(0.02×6×€15\text{k}\) is a single average-loss fiction; it ignores *which* six diagnosis-stage cases bear risk, congestion dependence, and that two specialists deliver swarms (parallelism, interruption of ordinary diagnosis). Specialist opportunity cost is collapsed into one number used identically for Triage and Diagnosis Started candidates. Adverse delay (15–30 min) is monetized inconsistently vs the 0.01–0.03 probability model.

**7. Smaller but real cuts under a hypercritical bar**
- Imputed I-06: “Wait” is fine; eligibility “Yes” without a data-quality hold or human confirm is loose given the prompt’s imputation warning.
- NNLS: ×0.7 plus “Wait” stacks penalties without a clear intervene threshold if discounted EV still &gt; 0 (borderline under their own numbers).
- False-alarm burden is always fully charged as if every swarm is a false alarm; not wrong as a conservative proxy, but never distinguished from expected disruption conditional on true high risk.
- Non-monetary / regulatory obligations beyond “keep one slot” are thin.
- Persistence rule “10% relative weight” is not operationally specified (weight in what model? who computes?).
- Causal plan (10% hold-out) is good; little on **how** selective swarming biases observational \(p\) used in the live rule between quarterly studies.
- Section 3 header/units are sloppy (`Direct € benefit ()`), undermining “deployable/auditable policy” polish.
