## Strict critique

This is a substantively strong transportability analysis that correctly refuses naive copying, centers partial-order / statutory constraints, and treats Site A’s before/after as confounded. Under a hypercritical standard it is **not** near-flawless: required technical fidelity slips, a few structural imprecisions, and soft quantitative derivation keep it well below the top band.

### What is strong
- **§1**: Meets the 12-feature mandate with the right columns; correctly flags originals prerequisites, legal/supplement loops, lock/timestamp threats, role specialization, and mix/tail non-comparability. Design consequences are actionable.
- **§2–4**: Transport graph has the required nodes and `S_*` selection structure. Shared mechanism is narrow and correctly stated; effect modifiers and failure modes (originals, lock, assessment/legal bunching, fake overlap, rework loops) are the right ones. Co-interventions at A are not laundered into a treatment effect.
- **§5**: Includes the required median-up / tail-down case (bottleneck-shift). Ranges are modest rather than copying A’s 9h / 18h.
- **§6–7, §9–10**: Segmented eligibility (digital/no-originals first; originals only as IdentityReceipt; legal later), WIP/stop rules, and mechanism-vs-implementation separation in the pilot are appropriate. `conditional go` is the defensible call. Eight assumptions are testable and consequential.

### Deductible flaws (strict)

**1. §8 PM4Py code is not faithful enough (major for “model fidelity”).**  
- `Sequence` is imported from `pm4py.objects.powl.obj` and used as a constructor. In standard PM4Py POWL APIs there is no `Sequence` class; sequences are `StrictPartialOrder` nodes with linear edges (or tree operators), and `Operator` is normally taken from `pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj`. As written the block is likely to `ImportError` before any net is built.  
- `StrictPartialOrder([...])` / `add_edge` usage is only approximately right depending on version; playout parameter `no_traces` is version-fragile.  
- Comments on what the model cannot encode are good, but a non-runnable construction fails the explicit requirement to build POWL with Assessment waiting on both branches, convert, and check soundness. This alone blocks a high score.

**2. Eligible-share arithmetic is loose.**  
Optimistic / bottleneck scenarios use ~38% (10.62 originals) as if that share gets clean verification parallelism, without intersecting the 40% legal-review gate, complexity, or lock-safe lanes. Digital-only (20–25%) is more careful; the inconsistency weakens quantitative scenario quality.

**3. Site A WIP attribution is slightly wrong.**  
Context states a six-open-task rule under Site A generally; huddles are the clear co-intervention. The table treats the WIP cap as part of the after package. Minor, but this is exactly the kind of co-intervention bookkeeping the rubric cares about.

**4. Quantitative mapping is asserted more than derived.**  
“Bounded by min(3.4, 8.1)” is the right intuition for ideal overlap savings, but overall median/p90 ranges are not shown as stratum-weighted combinations of A’s *portable* waiting signal vs B’s originals delay, assessor/legal capacity, and rework base rates. Confidences are directionally sane but somewhat decorative.

**5. Small graph / transfer nits.**  
- `digital_documents  true_overlap` is present; capacity  cycle paths are thin; appeal structure is shallow.  
- Transport confidence 55 on pre-Assessment waiting is still generous given staffing, API, huddles, and mix moved together at A.  
- POWL sketch in §6 is conceptually fine (LOOP/XOR/join), but §8 does not reliably implement it.

**6. Hypercritical format / clarity.**  
Tables and headings match the spec. Prose is dense and occasionally blurs “intended concurrency” vs “executed overlap” in scenario assumptions. No fatal logic error on statutory ordering—the answer correctly refuses blanket Document Check after Register—but the code and share math prevent “nearly flawless.”

### Score rationale
Substance on causal transport, co-interventions, segmented design, and refusal of illegal parallelism is high (roughly 8.5–9.0 content). Mandatory model-construction fidelity fails, eligible-share and A-attribution slips are real, and quantification is softer than the rubric’s “quantitative scenario analysis” bar. Under “even minor issues  significantly lower” and “very high only if nearly flawless,” the composite is **7.3**.
