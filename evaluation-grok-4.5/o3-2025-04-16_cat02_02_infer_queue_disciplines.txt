## Evaluation

The submission follows the required seven-section layout, shows real familiarity with dispatching concepts (eligible sets, preemption, scan lag, skill filters, appointments), and is appropriately cautious on QC. Those are real strengths. Under a strict standard, however, it is not close to a high score: several reconstructed decision sets are wrong or incomplete, one primary discipline is poorly supported, prediction tables are mostly empty, and “contradicting episode” columns are sometimes filled with supporting evidence.

### What works

- **QA primary read is directionally right.** After the 08:20 appointment pull-forward of A03, choosing A02 over p1/short A04 is strong evidence for FIFO-with-appointments rather than priority or SPT. Calibration 08:52–08:58 correctly explains why A04 cannot start at 08:30.
- **QD primary read is mostly right.** Skill partitioning plus oldest-first on D-M (D04 before p1 D08) is a clean identifying episode. Meeting-aware non-start of D05 on D-F is acknowledged.
- **Method posture is often good.** Scan-lag is used as an explicit confounder; confidence is not uniformly high; §4 states competing hypotheses and a discriminating observable; §6 matches stated ambiguities.
- **QB preemption of B01 by B02** is correctly treated as evidence of at least selective preemptive priority.

### Critical analytical errors

**1. QB: missed decisive anti-priority episode (B04 before B06).**  
At 10:28, when B01 finishes, waiting jobs include B04 (p3, est 8), B06 (p2, est 20), and B07 (p3, appointment 10:30). Observed choice is **B04**. Strict priority (preemptive or not) predicts **B06**. The write-up never treats this decision. That undercuts “pre-emptive priority” as primary far more than the B03-vs-B05 visibility dispute. A more defensible family is FIFO (or resume-of-suspended) with **priority-1 preemption** (and possibly limited p2>p3 preemption at breakpoints)—not global preemptive priority with an SPT tie-break. Confidence 55 is already modest, but the label is still too strong given ignored contrary evidence.

**2. QC: corrupted eligible set at 10:32 (omits C06).**  
C06 arrives 10:25 with est 6. At 10:32 the waiting set is at least C01(36), C04(22), C05(40), **C06(6)**. Pure SPT predicts **C06**, not C04. The table lists only C01/C04/C05 and treats C02→C03→C04 as “strict SPT.” That is the central supporting pillar of the QC diagnosis, and it is invalid once C06 is included. Downstream, **C01 before C07 at 11:40** also contradicts SPT, priority, and EDF (C07 is shorter, higher priority, and tighter deadline); the answer never reconstructs that decision. “SPT + deadline escalation” is therefore under-identified and overstated even at confidence 45.

**3. QA: wrong SPT prediction at 08:20.**  
Eligible: A02(20), A03(10), A04(7). SPT predicts **A04**, not A03. Marking SPT as selecting A03 is a definitional error in a section whose job is to separate disciplines.

**4. QD: weak / mis-timed decision points.**  
The 12:16 D-X row (D10 after D07 completes) is nearly trivial. The identifying conflict is **11:48: D07 (p3, earlier) vs D10 (p1 electrical)**—FIFO/skill continuity vs priority—yet that moment is not tabled. Claiming D-X “picks the oldest matching job (D07 over newer D10)” in §1 while §2 shows a 12:16 choice of D10 is internally inconsistent. D-F’s idle is also not only the 12:00–12:20 meeting: idle begins 11:36, so the mechanism is “do not start a job that would overrun a known blackout,” not meeting presence alone.

**5. Format / epistemology misuse.**  
For QA, `strongest_contradicting_episode` is filled with “if priority dominated, A04 would start at 08:30”—that **supports** FIFO, it does not contradict it. Several §2 cells that should state each rule’s prediction are blank, so the reader cannot verify head-to-head comparisons. Skill-based is missing as a column in QA–QC tables (acceptable if N/A, but QD’s skill story needed sharper multi-resource eligibility sets).
