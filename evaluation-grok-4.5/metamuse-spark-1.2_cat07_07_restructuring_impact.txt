### Defects (why not higher)

**1. Systematic unclarity from stripped causal direction (serious for §1).**  
Many cells and mermaid labels read as incomplete fragments where increase/decrease or arrows were lost, e.g. “Nominal count , subcontracting count .”, “utilization >85%  waiting time non-linear”, “role clarity : … similarity  within pool,  across pool”, “resilience to absence  ; If pool…”. In a *mechanism map*, missing direction of effect is not cosmetic; the reader must guess the causal claim. That alone blocks a top-tier score.

**2. Mermaid quality is uneven.**  
Nodes mix solid mechanisms with broken shorthand (“entropy , …”, “pings without ownership”). The diagram supports the narrative only if the reader already knows the intended signs.

**3. PM4Py sketch is directionally right but API-loose.**  
Examples: assumed column `__service_waiting_time`; `get_rework_cases_per_activity(..., activity=...)` may not match real signatures; `filter_event_attribute_values` chaining/level handling is underspecified; “role entropy” / silhouette are not standard PM4Py outputs of `discover_organizational_roles`. Acceptable as pseudocode, weaker as an executable analysis plan.

**4. Numeric scenario precision exceeds justification.**  
Figures such as “15–20% need 2+ trips”, “reassignment_count 1.4–1.6”, probabilities 20/50/30% are plausible but largely asserted. Fine for a forecast essay; still a mild overclaim under strict review.

**5. Minor conceptual stretches.**  
- Treating shared-service “role entropy” as a direct PM discoverable without defining the metric.  
- Equating resource similarity &gt;0.7 with role collapse is heuristic, not grounded.  
- Optimistic path assumes language/region routing attributes that are not in the proposed design (only in the hopeful scenario)—should be flagged as a design dependency, not an expected outcome.

**6. Small internal tensions.**  
Adverse scenario has East “also stalls” after relief while pilot logic banks on East benefit—consistent if full merge differs from pilot, but the answer could state that boundary more sharply when using East both as beneficiary and as systemic victim.
