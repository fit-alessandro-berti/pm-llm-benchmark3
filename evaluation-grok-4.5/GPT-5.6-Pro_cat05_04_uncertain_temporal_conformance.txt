## Grading rationale (hypercritical)

### Strengths
The submission is an unusually complete, disciplined treatment of a deliberately adversarial timestamp problem. It follows the required seven-section structure exactly; states interval semantics, offset direction, and resolution-before-correction explicitly; preserves DST scenario structure rather than convex-hulling; keeps T-407 semantically `INDETERMINATE` instead of laundering a proxy into a verdict; treats T-404 inference and T-406 entry-delay as first-class assumptions with alternatives; and runs a real sensitivity matrix whose boundary calls (T-403/=3 still PV by ~1.5s, T-408 profile edge at 55m03s, T-404 S2 by 3s, T-402 S1 not rescued by +5m) show genuine interval reasoning rather than midpoint hand-waving. Governance and evidential-sufficiency sections are operationally sharp and correctly separate hard SLA from profile diagnostics.

### Deductions (strict)
- **Code executability risk (0.4).** `pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile(...)` is not a stable, version-universal top-level API; real deployments typically need `pm4py.algo.conformance.temporal_profile` (or equivalent) and a discovered/normalized profile object. `datetime.fromisoformat` on minute-only strings (`"2026-06-01 10:14"`) is version-sensitive. The adapter comment mitigates shape drift but does not make the block reliably executable as written. Under a “nearly flawless / runnable” bar this matters.
- **Residual formal gaps (0.3).** Independent vs common same-source offsets are discussed narratively (T-405) but the baseline code path is purely eventwise-independent; a fully rigorous joint model would parameterize correlation. Causal truncation of T-409 to `[0, u]` is declared, but the feasible set under “receipt  start” style conditioning is not derived as a constrained joint set in closed form everywhere. Closed/open endpoint conventions (59.999s, inclusive SLA) are consistent internally but never proven against the legal reading of “no later than” at exact equality for every boundary cell.
- **Minor presentation / provenance nits (0.3).** Footnote link carries a `utm_source=chatgpt.com` artifact; severity/confidence scales are reasonable but not anchored to an explicit rubric; the midpoint log correctly refuses multi-interval cases, yet the write-up could more loudly flag that T-407 alert pairs are absent from the PM4Py log by construction (easy to misread as a silent drop).

### What kept it from the 9.5–10 band
No material arithmetic error was found on the checked chains (T-401–T-409 UTC intervals, S1–S3 spans,  bands, DST fold elimination, T-404 inference, T-408 55m±3s edge). The analytical core is excellent. The score is held to **9.0** only because the rubric demands significant penalty for any executability, joint-offset, or endpoint-semantics residual—and because a 10.0 should require a verbatim-runnable PM4Py path plus machine-checked interval proofs, not only careful human reasoning.

### Bottom line
