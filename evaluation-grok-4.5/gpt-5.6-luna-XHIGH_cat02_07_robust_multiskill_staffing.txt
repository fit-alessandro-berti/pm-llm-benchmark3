### Critical flaws (drive the score down hard)

**1. Inverted service-time multipliers (competence error)**  
Prompt: multiplier 1.25  25% *longer*.  
Code: `active = duration / speed(...)`.  
Slower resources (1.35, 1.40) become *faster*. Section 2’s capacity table correctly uses productive/multiplier; the simulator does the opposite. That invalidates policy comparison, utilization, SLA, and any claim the Python “accounts for” the skill matrix.

**2. Dispatch score is not earliest finish**  
`finish = max(ready, free[r]) + setup + 7 + …` omits actual activity duration. Ties and “risk-adjusted earliest finish” rhetoric are not implemented.

**3. Event log timestamps are wrong**  
`base_day` is defined and never applied. Numeric minutes are passed to `pd.to_datetime`, which treats them as ns-since-epoch. PM4Py service/waiting/handover analyses on that log are not meaningful. Required log fields exist in name only for temporal work.

**4. Not a DES; preemption is fake**  
Cases are fully path-scheduled in arrival order. “Preemption” only sometimes adds 10% penalty on the P1 itself—it never interrupts another case’s remaining work. Parallel ME/ES is approximate and can interact badly with sequential `free` updates. Acknowledging “scaffold” does not excuse presenting seed means/SDs as comparable policy metrics while Section 4’s big table is disconnected qualitative fiction.

**5. Section 4 table is not evidence**  
Ranges are asserted planning bands, not tied to the code, not calibrated, and systematically crown H. Under a hypercritical bar this is narrative fit, not audited comparison. `max_u > 1` is fine as overload signaling; the precision theater (e.g. 79–91% P1 under U5) is not.

**6. Sloppiness / inconsistencies**  
- Incomplete/self-contradictory Intake+Validation sum sentence in §2.  
- Policy S home queues under-use listed skills (e.g. Farah on Mechanical).  
- Objective (2) is “used for recommendation” but never scored; §4 doesn’t apply the stated objective function.  
- U2 “lower bound if doubles means extra arrivals” is fine as assumption, then treated unevenly downstream.  
- Overtime limit is stated then barely enforced in the scaffold.  
- Fairness/utilization denominators ignore multi-day spillover vs single-shift capacity.

**7. Python quality bar miss**  
Length  correctness. Required mechanisms are named but several are wrong or hollow (speed, preemption, calendar timestamps, finish scoring). try/except around PM4Py papers over breakage instead of emitting a valid interval log.
