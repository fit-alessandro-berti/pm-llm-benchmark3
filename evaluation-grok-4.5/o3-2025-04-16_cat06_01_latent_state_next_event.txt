### Critical process-semantic errors

**1. Authority Notification before Disposition (C6-C rank 3, C6-D rank 2)**  
Rule 6 and the DFG both place notification after disposition readiness/recording (`Disposition Recorded → Authority Notification`), then closure. Predicting AN as the *next logged* event while disposition is still open (or only hypothesized via delay) inverts the normative order. Marking `process_enabled: true` is wrong: AN is not enabled until reportable disposition logic has run. C6-D even ranks AN above Disposition with a high band (0.15–0.40). Migration can delay *visibility*; it does not make AN a normal predecessor of disposition. This is a serious compliance error, not a minor wording issue.

**2. Fast Close misuse on C6-B**  
After `Evidence Received`, the live path is an evidence cycle, not the triage fast path. Historical `Initial Triage → Fast Close` (286) is cited as support for Fast Close *now*—that edge is from a different prefix and must not be transplanted. Normatively, Fast Close is the early low-risk exit; post-evidence the plausible terminal label is closer to review completion then `Close — No Safety Issue`. Rank-2 Fast Close with `process_enabled: true` and a 0.20–0.45 band overstates both enablement and likelihood. The S5 alternative (“already decided Fast Close”) compounds the same category error.

**3. C6-E rank 3: `Supplier Analysis Completed`**  
Supplier analysis is a *downstream obligation*, not something whose completion is a plausible immediate successor of a 13:20 evidence receipt. Typical completion is 6–36 h; the case shows no SA open/complete event; review has not finished the second package. `process_enabled: true` for *completion* is false if the task is not known to be running. The answer itself says completion today is unlikely, then still ranks it third—internally inconsistent. A better third would be delayed review-driven branch (e.g., investigation path after review), not SA completion.

**4. Weak enablement vs observation distinction (task item 6)**  
Several ranks treat “might appear in the log” as `process_enabled: true` without separating:
- enabled now,
- in progress unseen,
- completion event next.

Examples: C6-A TI Completed while still pre-review; C6-C/D AN; C6-E SA Completed. The brief explicitly grades this distinction; the answer often collapses it.
