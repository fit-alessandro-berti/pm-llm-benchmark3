## Major deductions (why not 9+)

### 1. Section 4 is not evidence from the model in Section 8 (heavy)
The required comparison table is filled with **a priori “engineering risk bands”** that never cite the DES outputs the same document claims to produce. The task allows qualitative ranges, but once a full multi-seed simulator is offered as the reproducibility vehicle, leaving P1_SLA, mean/p90 delay, util CV, and handover burden disconnected from that simulator is a serious internal-consistency failure. A reader cannot tell whether H actually dominates P on P1 under U2/U3 in *their* model. Resilience and main_failure_mode are also largely narrative labels, not metrics.

### 2. Simulation metrics are methodologically fragile (heavy)
- Right-censoring at a fixed horizon treats incomplete cases as finishing at `final_t`, which **biases mean/p90 delay downward** exactly when U1/U3/U5 matter; `censor_rate` is reported but not used to correct SLA or delay summaries.
- N_REPS = 6 is too thin for the SLA quantiles and “p10/p90 of P1_SLA” the summary advertises.
- One-minute global bipartite matching every tick, growing `low_priority_busy_times` lists, and many status flags make correctness hard to trust without a run log; the markdown shows **no actual printed numeric results** from an execution—only code that *would* print them.
- Remaining-time / mode estimates inside scoring (`fastest_mean`, POOLalways parallel, etc.) are coarse relative to the prose decision rules; preemption/handover interleaving is intricate and easy to get wrong on edge cases (outage pause, double pin, setup vs restart).

### 3. Offered-load story still soft on multipliers and dual-use capacity (moderate)
Raw minutes (~2721) vs calendar minutes (2490) is clear, and the text notes multipliers  1. But the block table is raw load / calendar capacity, while assignment always inflates work on slow resources and Intake/Validation compete with diagnostics on the same people. Skill “isolated capacity” paragraphs are presented then correctly caveated, yet the bottleneck table’s utilization figures remain easier to over-read as decisive than they are. Distribution-free bounds (2065–3376) are given once then barely stress-tested in policy comparison.

### 4. Arbitrary control knobs presented as operational design (moderate)
Dispatch credits (continuity 250, overflow 80, aging rules, 3/5/10 min preemption waits, 15 min victim threshold, 75%/90-minute reserve proxy, parallel WIP &lt; 180, etc.) are numerous and mostly unmotivated. Objective \(J_A\)/\(J_B\) weights and \(\lambda\)s are similarly free parameters. That weakens the claim that H is robustly optimal rather than “H with this tuning.”

### 5. Smaller inaccuracies / unclarities (accumulate under strict rubric)
- U2 “aggregate load barely changes” is true under renormalization, but secondary displacement of Software/Validation is asserted more strongly than the ~10.412.3 branch shift supports without queueing dynamics.
