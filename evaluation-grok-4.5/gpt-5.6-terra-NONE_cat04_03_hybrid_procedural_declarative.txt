### 3. Process-tree loop semantics are inverted
```python

```
In PM4Py/process-tree semantics this is \( (Q\!\rightarrow\!RC)\,(\tau\,Q\!\rightarrow\!RC)^* \): **at least one** clarification cycle.

The comment claims “zero or more.” Correct 0..* form is `LOOP([tau, clarification_cycle])`.

Consequences: T1, T3, T4, T6, T7, T8 are all procedural **fails** under the code as written. Matrix marks most of them `pass`. Another hard inconsistency.
