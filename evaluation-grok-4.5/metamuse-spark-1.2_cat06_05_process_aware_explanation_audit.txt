## Evaluation

This is a strong, process-aware audit that hits most of what the rubric rewards: full item coverage, correct critical leakages, the Appeal vs. `Closed→Assessment` distinction, lineage subtleties, preservation of queue/elapsed/complexity, and a clear predictive vs. causal vs. actionable split. It is not near-flawless under a strict bar.

### What is done well

**Section 1** covers all 12 globals, all five sequence patterns, and the local-only drivers (channel, final decision, reserve, risk score, last role), plus employee experience. Severities are mostly well calibrated:

- `total_case_duration_hours` as near-label leakage (not merely “future info”) is sharp.
- Complete-trace aggregates (`appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `num_document_requests_total`) are correctly critical.
- Subtler defects are handled carefully: warehouse `final_handler_team`, `claim_created_at <= extraction_date`, nightly `same_day_document_count_snapshot`, mutable `risk_score_current`.
- Sequence handling is a highlight: `Claim Closed → Damage Assessment` is treated as normative impossibility plus same-timestamp artefact; `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated as process-legitimate later but inadmissible at `t0`. That is exactly the distinction the task asks for.
- Legitimate signals (`queue_length_at_t0`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, `initial_damage_complexity`) are retained rather than purged.

**Section 2** is concrete and usable: PIT recomputation, declarative/alignment checks vs. DFG frequency, same-timestamp/`sequence_index` policy, complete-trace dependency detection, enabled/impossible/future-possible state at the REG–COV prefix, and repeat-decision-after-appeal. Limitations of mined DFGs are stated correctly.

**Section 3** matches the required taxonomy and states claim boundaries cleanly (predictive association ≠ intervention ≠ fairness-cleared use).

**Section 4** does not reintroduce leaked outcomes in narratives; X/Y as `recompute` and Z as invalidate-with-ambiguities is right given driver mix. Z’s ambiguity list and minimum recompute steps are thorough.

**Section 5** is prioritized and covers the required themes (feature store/PIT, prefix gate, conformance CI, calibration after removal, stability, proxies, drift).

### Defects (why this is not 9+)

1. **Explicit instruction miss on correlated features**  
   The task says not to simply delete every correlated/sensitive feature and to apply different standards. Section 3 does that; Section 4 undoes it. For Case X, `claimant_channel` is dumped into `removed_or_repaired_drivers` despite being temporally available. For Case Y, `claimant_region` is removed the same way. Both should remain as **defensible predictive / proxy** drivers with non-causal wording and a fairness flag. That is a real methodological inconsistency, not a nit.

2. **Broken comparison wording (repeated)**  
   Several cells read as corrupted inequalities—“Correlation causation”, “Predictive causal actionable”, “Predictive in training period stable or causal”. The intended “≠” claims are clear to an expert reader but the text is sloppy and sometimes ungrammatical. Under strict grading this is not free.

3. **Small precision / wording issues**
   - “AtMostOne(Claim Closed) before close” is garbled.
   - `appeal_possible_at_t0=0` as remediation is filler.
   - Case Y’s credible set is overly thin once region is wrongly dropped; the narrative could more sharply say the original 0.71 was dominated by an inadmissible variant feature (+0.31), so the point estimate is not trustworthy even before refit.
   - `breach = total_duration > 10d` is directionally right as label leakage but slightly over-precise if breach is tied to settlement time rather than full case close—still critical either way, but the tautology claim is a bit absolute.

4. **Minor taxonomy nits**  
   `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` is listed under both “defensible predictive” and “process-state” without a clean primary home. Harmless, but slightly loose.  
   Treating `claimant_channel` removal as “pending fairness review” blurs “exclude from model” vs. “keep with governance,” which Section 3 had already resolved better.

### What would push it toward 9.5–10

- Keep region/channel (and similarly situated proxies) in revised credible drivers with explicit non-causal + fairness language.  
- Fix the broken ≠ prose.  
- Slightly tighten Case Y’s uncertainty about the numeric prediction after stripping the variant ID.  
- One-line clarification that DFG frequency never licenses impossible edges (already implied, could be sharper in §1).

### Bottom line

Technically strong and clearly above a merely competent audit: leakage, lineage, process impossibility, and causal hygiene are mostly right, and the Appeal vs. same-timestamp pattern distinction is handled with real understanding. The score is held down by the Section 4 proxy deletions (against a stated requirement), repeated broken phrasing, and a few precision slips. Under hypercritical scoring this is **solid excellent work with notable fixable flaws**, not a near-perfect answer.
