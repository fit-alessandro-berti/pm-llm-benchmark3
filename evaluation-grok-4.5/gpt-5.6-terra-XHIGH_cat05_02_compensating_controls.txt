### Material defects

**1. AP-204 misclassified (significant)**  
Facts: fabricated service receipt; high-value consulting payment; repeated pair; shared workstation; late audit.  
A core FEP-1 / payment-integrity objective is preventing erroneous or fraudulent completion. Fabricated receipt evidence means that objective **failed** for the fictitious-goods/services threat.  

Labeling this `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET` is too soft and inconsistent with the answer’s own language (“payment-integrity failure is evidenced,” “do not certify as objective-met”). Partial credit for nominal segregation does not justify a single overall label of PARTIALLY_MET when the payment completed on false support and detective control was late. **`OBJECTIVE_FAILED`** (or at best a clearly argued residual “form met / substance failed” with FAILED overall) is the coherent call. This is a central case in the set; getting the polarity wrong is not a nit.

**2. AP-207 `OBJECTIVE_MET` is over-claimed (moderate)**  
ED-1 needs independent L3+ MG-4, substantive review, and—if ratification is post-release—an **enforceable** pre-settlement stop. The answer repeatedly lists missing L3 evidence and uncertain hold authority, then still marks `OBJECTIVE_MET` with only medium confidence.  

That is internally inconsistent. With missing gate/L3/hold proof, **`OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET` or `INDETERMINATE`** is the disciplined label; MET should require the package to be evidenced, not merely “plausible.” AP-203 (pre-release substantive MG-4) is a much cleaner MET than AP-207.

**3. AP-201 messaging muddle (moderate)**  
Rationale says **“unjustified same-user convenience, not an ED-1 use”** while the classification is `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET`. Low amount + AV-2 pass + manager before stated settlement can support “technically nonconforming but tolerable,” but calling it unjustified while scoring PARTIALLY_MET blurs the required taxonomy. Either:
- PARTIALLY_MET / tolerable with weak exception governance, or  
- closer to failed exception discipline with low residual **financial** risk.  

The write-up wants both without cleanly choosing.

**4. Code: missing bank-change treated as ED-1-satisfying (moderate, real logic bug)**  
```text
bank_change_within_7d = notna & le(7)   # NaN  False
ed_static_conditions  = ... & ~bank_change_within_7d
```  
So **unknown** bank-change history becomes “no change within 7 days” and helps set `ed_static_conditions` true. That is absence-of-evidence-as-evidence-of-absence—the opposite of the answer’s own evidence philosophy elsewhere. NA-aware logic (condition true only when known compliant) was required for “preliminary evidence flags.”

**5. Secondary code / API brittleness (minor–moderate)**
