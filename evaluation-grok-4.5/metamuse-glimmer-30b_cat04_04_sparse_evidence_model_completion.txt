## Critical failures

### 1. Balanced model forces mandatory SC and Exp (task-breaking)
`model_balanced` is a single `StrictPartialOrder` over `{ro, cc, er, sc, exp, ac, rp, arc}`. In POWL/strict partial orders, **every node must occur**. That makes Supplier Confirmation and Expedite Review **mandatory**, which:

- Contradicts observed variants V3–V4 (no SC) and the bulk of cases without Expedite  
- Contradicts D6 (Expedite optional) and the paper’s own narrative that SC/Exp are optional  
- Means expected fitness on the 30-case log is **not** “~100%”; a large share of traces cannot be replayed  

The text waves this away with “optionality via abstraction” but **does not implement** silent/XOR optionality in the assigned object. That is not a minor omission; it is the central modeling claim of the balanced candidate.

### 2. At least one model is wrong on the log (fitness claims false)
Comparative matrix and validation narrative claim ~100% fitness for balanced (and imply sound operational use). With mandatory SC+Exp, fitness on the real log would collapse. Conservative is the only candidate that is clearly log-complete by construction. Permissive *may* fit if `and(..., xor(SC,τ), xor(Exp,τ))` is interpreted as concurrent optional children, but that API/string form is fragile and under-specified (see below).
