### Section 1 — Control-Objective Decomposition
**Solid, not excellent.** Six threat scenarios with a P/D/C/– matrix is on-brief and usable.

**Issues:**
- Threat 1 over-credits AV-2 as **P** for “fake vendor / divert to own account.” Ownership and recent bank-change checks help only for those paths; a stable fake or mule account with no 7-day change can pass AV-2 cleanly. That should be partial/limited P, not blanket P.
- ED-1 is repeatedly scored as a full P/D/C package; that collapses a conditional *permission* into a control that always works. ED-1 only addresses a threat when *all* preconditions hold—otherwise it is the risk path.
- Collusion (row 3): ED-1 as only **D** undersells independent MG-4 when it is real content review before settlement; AV-2 as “–” is right.
- Threat 4 marks AV-2 “– (still runs)”—awkward and inconsistent with AV-2 still being preventive in emergencies.

### Section 2 — Equivalence and Sufficiency Test
**Framework is clear** (fully / partially / merely mitigating / ineffective) and the rejection of pure expected-loss is well argued (deterrence, accountability, forensics, external expectations).

**Material miss vs. instructions:** the six axes list **omits collusion resistance**, which was explicitly required alongside timing, independence, coverage, overrideability, recoverability, and evidence quality. That is not a cosmetic omission for a four-eyes / two-person-cell problem set.

**Other nits:** “Partially equivalent” is defined loosely (“one dimension weakened… low to moderate”) without a decision rule for *which* dimensions are allowed to weaken. The framework is barely linked forward into case verdicts (no case is labeled fully/partially/merely/ineffective).

**Structurally present; substantively incomplete and partly wrong.**

What works: in-memory CSV + `StringIO`, UTC parse, `format_dataframe`, attempt to call `filter_four_eyes_principle`, blocked-case detection via `Automated_Validate_Block`, small facts frame, prose caveat that output is evidence not verdict, version caveat on PM4Py API.

**Required item 6 is only half-done:**
- No **repeated-pair** flag (explicitly required).
- **AV coverage** not really emitted as a per-case evidence flag (only raw events exist).
- **Emergency eligibility** is a bare `emergency_token` boolean, not a condition package (amount, vendor age, bank change, AV pass/no override, MG-4 timing, audit ≤1 bd).

**Logic bugs:**

- Assuming `filter_four_eyes_principle` returns “violations” and that the return is a DataFrame with `case:concept:name` is version-fragile; the note helps but the variable naming asserts a semantics that may be inverted (some PM4Py paths keep *compliant* traces).

For a section that asked for a single executable, judgment-supporting pipeline, this is a noticeable shortfall.
