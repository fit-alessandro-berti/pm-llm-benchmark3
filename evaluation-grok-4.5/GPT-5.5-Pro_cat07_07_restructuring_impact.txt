3. **Scenario block readability / structure:** One ultra-dense row-per-scenario table meets the checklist but buries contrasts; several claims blur estimate vs. forecast.

8. **Fairness operationalization:** Segment gaps are named repeatedly; a precise process-mining fairness definition (e.g., P90 wait or SLA parity by `customer_segment` / `local_expertise_flag` with uncertainty) is only sketched.
