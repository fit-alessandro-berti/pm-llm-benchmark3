## Hypercritical deductions (why not 9.5–10)

1. **“DECLARE” is ad-hoc Python, not a declarative constraint model**  
   The chosen strategy is allowed, but `policy_constraints` + `assess_policy_trace` are a custom checker, not a standard DECLARE set with a documented semantics engine. Integration with the Petri net is informal (two-layer, not a single composed artifact). Minor, but real relative to the option text.

2. **Correction locus is over-constrained**  
   All rework is forced before any Supplier/Expedite. Acknowledged as a limitation, yet it is still a semantic gap versus an open reading of P6 (correction timing unspecified). A fuller design would allow controlled rework later or justify the cut with log evidence.

3. **Small soft/edge inaccuracies**  
   - P5 scored as “mostly” satisfied for all three—unnecessary hedge; all three enforce exactly-one approval structurally.  
   - Pathology label “train/holdout leakage or split sensitivity” overstates leakage; only split sensitivity is evidenced.  
   - `assess_policy_trace` is slightly looser than the tree on a few edges (e.g., Supplier after approval on internal cases not always flagged as order violation; “mandatory before Expedite” is “ever before,” relying on the tree to ensure post-rework freshness).

4. **Silent-loop encoding**  
   `*(tau, body)` is the standard 0..* encoding and behaves correctly on probes, but it injects a mandatory silent step on every case; that can interact with alignment costs and diagnostics in ways never stress-tested in Sec. 9. Worth a short note; absent.

5. **Duplicate-label residual**  
   Credit/Technical still duplicate across initial and rework blocks. Far better than C’s 33, and disclosed—but diagnostics/ownership story is thinner than a design with lifecycle or “recheck” attributes.

6. **Validation skeletons are incomplete as executable harnesses**  
   Adequate as skeletons, but they do not wire `assess_policy_trace` into the decision rule, do not show process-tree parse/round-trip checks, and leave stratified splitting as a comment. For a board pack this is a small operational gap.

None of these reverse the recommendation or break probe behavior. They are why the score is not a 10 under a “minor issues cut significantly” rule.

Safe for exploration only for S; neither original for compliance; third alternative is the correct candidate pending the stated gates. Content quality is excellent; remaining gaps are localized modeling/engineering nits rather than conceptual failures. **Grade: 9.0**.
