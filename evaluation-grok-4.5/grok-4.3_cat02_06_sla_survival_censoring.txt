# 1. SLA estimands and time definitions
The section correctly sets origin at Submit and horizon at 72 h and names two estimands linked to CIF ideas. However, the contribution rules are imprecise and internally inconsistent with later encoding. Declaring S09 (open at 92 h) “right-censored at 72 h under both estimands” is incorrect for a fixed-horizon probability: the case has been observed past 72 h without resolution or terminal disposition, so its status at the horizon is known (it contributes as “not resolved” / “still open”). Clipping every open case to 72 h discards the fact that several ages already exceed the horizon. S03 is described as “contributes an event for E1 after 72 h,” which muddies whether E1 is 1-CIF(resolve) or an indicator of late resolution. The same ambiguity appears for competing-event cases. These definitional slips propagate directly into Sections 2 and 8.
