## Overall judgment

The answer is unusually well structured, format-compliant, and often genuinely decision-set based rather than start-order storytelling. QA and QD are mostly competent; QB’s hybrid reading is defensible; sections 4–7 show real process-mining literacy. Under a strict standard it still falls well short of a high score because of a **core eligibility/prediction error on QC**, a **misfilled “contradicting episode” for QA**, and several **overfitted rescues** that paper over residual contradictions. Those are not cosmetic issues in a task whose quality bar is reconstructing decision sets.

## Critical failures

### 1. QC: C06 is eligible at 10:32; SPT does **not** predict C04

At 10:32 the waiting set includes at least:

- C01 (36), C04 (22), C05 (40), **C06 (6, arrived 10:25)**

Pure SPT predicts **C06**, not C04. Observed selection is **C04**.

The answer is internally inconsistent on this point:

- §1 narrative: waiting set written as C01/C04/C05 only; claims “Ordered by `est_min` exactly.”
- §2 table: **lists C06** in the eligible set, then still writes SPT → **C04 (22)**.

That is not a subtle ambiguity. It is a direct mistake in the central method the prompt demands (eligible set → competing predictions → observed choice). It falsely converts the 10:10 / 10:18 / 10:32 triplet into clean SPT support. Once C06 is handled correctly, **C04 is predicted by none of FIFO, priority, SPT, or EDF**, so “primary = SEPT/SPT” is much weaker than claimed and the §1 supporting episode is invalid.

Knock-on damage:

- Confidence 50 is still too tidy given that the strongest alleged support is wrong.
- Secondary story (SLA override + starvation) is partly motivated by protecting an SPT primary that already failed earlier.
- §3 “three consecutive selections exactly sorted by ascending `est_min`” is false.
- §7 “initial three decisions support SPT perfectly” is false.

A strong answer would have flagged 10:32 as a hard anomaly (or tested a **static/batch SPT on the 10:10 backlog**: C02→C03→C04→C01, with later C05/C06 as interventions). This answer does neither.

### 2. QA table: “strongest_contradicting_episode” does not contradict the primary diagnosis

Primary claim: FIFO + appointment.

Cited “contradiction”: **08:32 chooses A02 over A04**. That **supports** FIFO and **refutes** priority/SPT. It is a supporting discrimination episode mislabeled as contradicting.

§3 then admits there is “no direct FIFO violation” beyond the intentional appointment exception. So the diagnosis table overstates how well the primary was stress-tested. Under strict grading this is a real methodological slip, not a wording quibble.

### 3. QD: D-F idle 11:36–12:00 is under-explained

Meeting look-ahead explains **not starting D05 (30)** in a 24-minute window. It does **not** explain **not starting D10 (12)**, which fits before 12:00.

The answer sometimes blurs this into a generic “non-preemptive block / reservation for D05.” That is possible, but it is an extra undocumented mechanism, and it is stronger evidence against plain work-conserving skill+FIFO than the writeup admits. QD remains the best-supported queue, but confidence **80** is high once D10 is taken seriously.

### QB — reasonable hybrid, heavy special pleading

Strengths:

- Preemption of B01 for B02 is correctly treated as the clearest preemptive-priority signal.
- Resume-to-head (09:31, 10:14) and B04 over B06 (10:28) are rightly used against pure priority.
- Recognition that appointment is not primary (B07) is sound.
- Confidence 55 matches ambiguity better than the other rows.

Issues:

- 09:48 B03 over B05 is rescued primarily by **maximum** 2-minute scan delay (arrival 09:46). Possible, but knife-edge; alternative hypotheses (reassignment logic, incomplete priority implementation) are underdeveloped.
- Claiming SPT would also preempt at 09:16 (remaining ~31 vs new 15) is fair observational-equivalence commentary, but then SPT is not systematically carried as a real competitor through later resumes.
- “Dispatcher reassignment” is an active intervention; treating the queue as a stable hybrid discipline may over-interpret noisy human overrides.
- Primary label is awkwardly narrow (“preemptive priority for p1 urgent arrivals”) while the secondary actually does most ordering work—borderline coherent, but fuzzy.

### QC — weakest queue, diagnosis not earned

Beyond the C06 error:

- Correlation of p1 with earliest deadline/longest est is well noted.
- Still, with 10:32 mis-analyzed, the whole SEPT-primary narrative is not competent enough for this benchmark.

### QD — strongest queue, slightly overconfident

Strengths:

- D05’s long wait and “only D-F fully qualified” is correctly decisive for hard skill constraints.
- Within-partition choices (D04 over D08; D07 over D10) are good anti-priority/anti-SPT evidence.

Issues:

- Appointment vs FIFO for D06 is correctly called confounded.
- “No priority / No SPT” is too absolute given small per-partition choice sets and several non-discriminating moments.
