## Overall judgment
The submission is a strong, process-aware design that correctly refuses the convenient biased cohort (train on 21,600 and call everyone else non-default). It meets the nine-section structure, keeps activation selection explicit, separates undefined outcomes from ordinary censoring, and supplies a concrete PM4Py-oriented skeleton. Under a hypercritical standard it is not near-flawless: several statistical–engineering seams, a few code-level defects, and incomplete realization of the as-of discipline described in the prose keep it well below 9.5–10.

## Section-by-section critique

### 1. Target definition — strong, minor clarity debt
Primary target (90-day default | activation, historical policy) is the right operationally defensible choice; rejected/withdrawn/open are not labeled 0. Estimand, landmark, horizon, and population are stated. The multi-state alternative and the explicitly assumption-heavy counterfactual are appropriate.

Nits: “unit of analysis: application/activated account after resolving duplicates” blurs the distinction between the scoring unit (application at prefix time) and the labeled unit (activated account). The claim that scores may be emitted for all applications while remaining interpretable only on the activation support is correct but underspecified for deployment thresholds. Still clearly above the failure modes the prompt warns against.

### 2. Cohort construction — excellent
Rules are explicit; the flow table exhausts 48,000 and assigns each bucket a statistically honest role (label / competing / delayed / censored). Landmark validity and feed maturity are required for the binary pool. No material flaw.

### 3. Censoring and missing-label strategy — excellent conceptually
The five-way distinction (right censoring, delayed labels, activation selection, transfer, permanent missing feeds) is exactly what the prompt demands. Primary complete-case landmark analysis plus IPW/pattern-mixture/survival/competing-state/transfer-bound sensitivities are appropriate; testable vs. untestable assumptions are called out.

Nit: inverse-probability weighting for feed observability is proposed without a concrete positivity/overlap diagnostic plan or stabilization approach; still acceptable at design level.

### 4. Prefix extraction — good design, incomplete operationalization
Fixed-k and elapsed-time landmarks, no future padding, document-loop retention, and migration canonicalization are all right. Stopping before terminal/decision events is correct for pre-decision warning.

Gap: short-case policy is described (“separate up-to-k model or exclude”) but never pinned to a single primary rule, which will produce irreproducible cohorts.

### 5. Process-aware features — strong
Control-flow, rework, timing, resource/queue, score trajectory, regime indicators, and the train-only DFG/variant restriction are all appropriate. Historical reference features are correctly flagged as requiring point-in-time tables and inner-fold refits.

### 6. Leakage audit — excellent
Table covers every mandated element (`final_limit`, `decision_reason`, `collection_status`, future events, `documents_total`, updated `risk_score`, resource experience, label availability) plus sensible extras. Disposition decisions are almost all correct.

Minor softness: “repair or drop” for resource experience and `documents_total` without a default primary choice leaves implementers room to reintroduce leakage.

### 7. Validation design — strong prose, weaker coupling to code
Temporal, regime-aware splits; explicit rejection of random case splits; calibration, decision-curve, subgroup, and shift monitoring; survival metrics for the sensitivity path—all appropriate. Policy-change and migration eras are isolated.

Hypercritical issue: the text insists on rebuilding labels/features at each historical cutoff, yet the skeleton never parameterizes `AS_OF` inside the split loop. That is a real design–implementation gap for a study whose central claim is as-of honesty.

### 8. Limitations and decision-use boundary — excellent
Clear separation of predictive association, selection, and counterfactual risk; five concrete feedback loops under deployment. No overclaim.

### 9. PM4Py skeleton — usable but not tight
Strengths: CSV + timestamp parsing, `format_dataframe`, case-level maturity/state construction, temporal split by start time, `get_prefixes_from_log`, hard filter on post-prefix activities, forbidden-column assert, train-only DFG/variants, comments pointing to external survival/competing-risk modeling, cohort count printout.

Defects that lower the score under strict grading:
- `was_duplicate` is computed, rows are dropped, then `duplicate_indicators=("was_duplicate","sum")` is aggregated—always zero. Dead/wrong feature.
- Single global `AS_OF = 2026-02-15` is used for every split; the rolling as-of discipline argued in §§2–3–7 is not demonstrated.
- `get_prefixes_from_log` semantics (all lengths 1\ldots K, possible derived case IDs) are only partially handled; the comment admits the issue but the join/eligible logic still assumes stable original case IDs.
- Elapsed-time prefixes are specified in §4 and never shown.
- `case_summary` runs on the pre-`format_dataframe` frame; label/maturity logic is therefore slightly decoupled from the log object used for prefixes.
- No sketch of the activation or feed-observation propensity models that §3 treats as part of the primary strategy.
- Minor robustness: `groupby.apply(case_summary)` and join patterns are fragile to pandas versions; timezone-naive vs. UTC edge cases are ignored.

These are not fatal for a skeleton, but they are concrete inaccuracies relative to the quality bar the prompt set.

## Summary of deductions (hypercritical)
- Design/code mismatch on rolling as-of and label maturity inside backtests (0.5)
- Concrete skeleton bugs / omissions (`was_duplicate`, prefix-ID handling, no elapsed-time path, no propensity sketch) (0.6)
- Small underspecifications (short-prefix primary rule, soft “repair or drop”, unit-of-analysis blur) (0.4)
- Residual risk that a reader could still train a binary model on mature activated cases and then score the full population without the support flags the text recommends (0.0 to 0.2; text is mostly protective)

Net: a high-quality, honest answer that clearly beats the common failure modes, but not the near-flawless artifact required for 9.5+.

**Grade: 8.5**
