## Evaluation

This submission is mostly a heading-compliant skeleton with a few partially correct fragments. Under a strict reading of the task (full interval normalization, exhaustive case–rule classification, real sensitivity work, and executable PM4Py code), it fails on substance almost everywhere.

### Structural compliance
The seven required top-level headings are present. That is nearly the only fully met requirement. Several sections explicitly defer work (`Additional rows follow…`, `# <Implement logic>`, ellipsis governance bullets), which is not an answer.

### Section 1 — Normalization Assumptions
**Partial credit only.** Direction of clock correction (`true = recorded − offset`) is stated correctly, and DST folds / causal constraints are named. Material defects:

- **T-404 inference is wrong:** evidence gives *Investigation_Complete* plus an 18–25 min investigation duration. Start must be inferred from completion − duration, not from *Containment_Complete*.
- **T-406 entry delay:** asserting that the 5 min delay only widens record-time uncertainty, without analyzing the competing “late occurrence” reading the prompt flags as substantive, is under-argued.
- **HUB ±0.5 s measurement uncertainty** is never folded into the pipeline before clock correction, despite the true-time rule.
- **Asymmetric OPS-BER offset [-90 s, +120 s]** is collapsed into “±90 s” thinking later—incorrect.
- No clear policy for **unions vs hulls**, **SENSOR completion semantics**, or **T-407** “receipt ≼ investigation” as assumption vs fact.

### Section 2 — UTC Event-Time Intervals
**Seriously incomplete and factually wrong in places.**

| Issue | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **T-402 Containment_Complete as `08:02:00Z`** | Recorded HUB time is `10:02:00`. This is a hard error, not rounding noise. |
| **± notation instead of proper intervals** | Hides open/closed bounds, floor windows, and asymmetric offsets; not usable for duration arithmetic. |
| **HUB treated as ±1 s** | Omits ±0.5 s measurement uncertainty required before clock correction. |
| **SENSOR `08:51:10` as `±5 s`** | Nearest-10 s rounding implies a 10 s bin *then* ±5 s clock offset; construction is underspecified/wrong. |
| **OPS-BER** | Local floor minute → UTC is roughly right for June (CEST→−2 h), but offset asymmetry and full true-time interval are not. |
| **T-404** | Shows Investigation_Complete; **no inferred Investigation_Start interval**. |
| **T-405** | One vague Alert row; missing Investigation/Containment; **no disjoint DST-fold unions**; no use of ingestion order. |
| **T-406–T-409** | Truncated/missing (table ends mid T-408). No T-407 Alert_Received inference, no T-409 weak NTP case. |
| **Evidence strength** | Generic “High/Medium” without linking to testability. |

A correct table would list every activity with ISO interval **endpoints or unions**, not slogans like `± 1s`.

- All testable temporal-profile pairs with bands `mean ± ζ·sd`.
- Labels **CERTAIN_PASS / CERTAIN_VIOLATION / POSSIBLE_VIOLATION / INDETERMINATE** applied systematically.
- Duration interval arithmetic from endpoint differences (min/max over feasible pairs), including negative/infeasible orderings (e.g. T-401/T-402 containment vs investigation ordering under TZ/offset).
