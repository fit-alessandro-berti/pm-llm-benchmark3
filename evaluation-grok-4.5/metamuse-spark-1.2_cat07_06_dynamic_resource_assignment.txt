## Evaluation

The answer is ambitious, well-structured, and hits every required section with real socio-technical depth. Under a strict reading, that is not enough: the core deliverable (the queue assignment) breaks stated rules, misreports training compliance, and overclaims fairness. Those are not cosmetic issues.

### What works

**§2 Policy design** has the right machinery: eligibility filtering, multi-factor scoring (SLA, skill, continuity, workload/OT, training, resilience), weighted total, tie-breaks, batching, escalation. Weights are explainable and auditable via `assignment_reason`. This is a credible policy sketch.

**§5 Worker explanation** is one of the best parts: plain language, non-punitive, joint accountability for supervision, visible trade-offs, exception path without performance blame.

**§6 Audit indicators** covers the full required set and ties indicators to log fields and review (not punishment).

**§7 PM4Py sketch** names the right families of functions (SNA/handover, roles/similarity, service/waiting, performance DFG, batches, filters, variants) and sketches an audit loop.

**§4 Comparison** correctly refuses a single optimum and contrasts short-term waiting vs. specialist queues vs. balanced policy on the required dimensions.
