## Deductions (strict)
1. **Terminology stretch (“Simpson-like”)** — Absolute weekend WIP lower while WIP/FTE higher is a useful composition point, but it is not demonstrated as a classical Simpson reversal of an association; “Simpson-like” is used more loosely than the benchmark’s own caution invites. Minor but real clarity cost.
2. **Primary segmentation granularity** — ~10 chronological cells, several with \(n=2\), risks over-partitioning even with low confidence labels. A tighter primary (e.g., fewer recovery/post splits) with the factorial design as the main alternative would have been cleaner; the current primary is defensible but not optimally parsimonious.
3. **Table vs prose tension on recovery throughput** — Completion intensities for Apr 16–17 / post-staff days sit in the same numeric columns as ordinary regimes; the critical “inventory-enabled drainage, not a service rate” warning is in prose below, not locked to the table. A reader of the table alone can still misuse those rates.
4. **Pre-intervention elevated FTE under-discussed** — Data show staff_fte already 7.0/6.5 on Apr 16–17, before the named Apr 29 intervention. Treated as recovery capacity (reasonable) but not sharply flagged as a distinct capacity event that confounds a clean “intervention = Apr 29” story.
5. **Dispersion presentation** — Hour-bin Fanos are computed carefully and later caveated, yet the section still leads with “Fano factor” language that can be misread as Poisson overdispersion tests; the caveat should have been welded to the first table.
6. **No explicit sensitivity** on the outage-day denominator beyond discussion, and almost no arrival-weighted service summaries (only median-of-daily-medians), which the answer itself notes is not case-level—fine methodologically, slightly incomplete as robustness.
7. **Tiny presentation nits** — Occasional net sign readability, `daily.index == daily.index` idiom, and very long redundancy across §§2–4. Not wrong, slightly unpolished.

- Explicit call-out of Apr 16–17 FTE as separate from the logged Apr 29 intervention.
- Fano/CV caveats attached at first use; one arrival-weighted or volume-sensitive service summary.
