## Strict evaluation

The answer follows the required eight-section skeleton and references concrete identifiers/timestamps in most places, which is a baseline positive. However, under hypercritical review it contains multiple material inaccuracies, incomplete distinctions between evidence and conjecture, thin quantitative work, API-sketch looseness, and under-development relative to the 1 800–2 800-word target. These flaws compound; none can be dismissed as cosmetic.

### Section-by-section defects

**1. Interaction-graph description**  
Node and edge inventories are adequate and the three motifs are recognisable. The compact edge list is usable. Yet the text never clarifies that “uses” edges for tools/machines are almost entirely inferred from co-presence rather than explicit O2O relations, and the higher-order motifs are described at slogan level without listing the precise participating objects or the temporal windows that make them higher-order. Direct-vs-inferred labelling is present but applied inconsistently (quarantine edges are called “inferred” while acquire/release times for FIX-A are treated as observed).  

**2. Quantitative delay decomposition**  
Five-plus calculations appear, yet two are wrong or poorly counterfactualised.  
- FIX-A/J4: the 1 h 40 min figure assumes CNC-2 is free and set up at 10:05; the supplied evidence only guarantees an idle window until 10:47 and is silent on intervening events. The counterfactual therefore over-states the tool-attributable delay.  
- CAL-3/J6: the claim that “no recalibration \ldots allows start at 16:10” is false. J5 occupies TEST-1/CAL-3 until 16:35; absent recalibration the earliest feasible start is ~16:35, so the recalibration-imposed delay is 50 min, not 1 h 15 min. The stated counterfactual collapses resource exclusivity with the recalibration interval.  
J1/J2 batch waits are numerically correct but the counterfactual (“start at earliest readiness”) ignores the hard policy that B1 contains three jobs and that RACK-7 must be free—precisely the coupling the question asks to analyse. No calculation quantifies the RACK-7 cleaning gap that idles OVEN-1 from 12:55–14:50, an obvious missed opportunity.
