2. **Cross-case dependencies and non-stationarity are named, not designed**  
   Constraint: *do not design a simple independent-case simulation only*. The text mentions architect bursts, district batching, and objection spill-over, but never specifies mechanisms (state variables for bundle windows, coupling rules, event types, synchronization, how Hawkes/cluster arrivals interact with resource calendars). 3. **Service vs waiting collapse is acknowledged then effectively ignored**  
   Constraint: *do not collapse service and waiting without explaining the limitation*. The code computes only `inter_event_days` as a joint proxy. The estimation plan says “waiting = residual after subtracting median service” — a crude bias, not a proper incomplete-data or interval-censoring treatment, and not propagated into the uncertainty design in a serious way.

4. **Missing objection events treated as if duration is enough**  
   Constraint: *do not treat missing objection events as if they were observed*. D9 and S3 still treat objection probability/duration as tunable observed-like parameters (“objection probability −15%”) without a latent-variable / sensitivity framing that keeps the unobserved process explicit.
