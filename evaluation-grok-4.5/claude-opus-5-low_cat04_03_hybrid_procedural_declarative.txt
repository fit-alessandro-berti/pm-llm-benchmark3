## Major defects (heavy deductions)

**1. Procedural model vs. T7 verdict is internally contradictory (severe)**  
The POWL encodes `esc_block  outcome`. Because `outcome` contains the entire high-risk branch (`EDD  Compliance Approval  Activate  Payment`), *any* execution of the escalation body requires `Obtain Legal Opinion` to finish before `Enhanced Due Diligence` may start.  

T7 order: Escalation@6, **EDD@8**, Opinion@40, Compliance@42, Activate@43.  
This is a clear partial-order violation. The matrix nevertheless marks Procedural = `pass` and states “every control-flow layer passes.” The model and the verdict cannot both be right. The intended constraint (R5) only requires opinion before *Activate Vendor*, not before EDD; the POWL over-serializes and the author never re-checked T7 against the edges they wrote. This is the single largest technical failure.

**2. Log-skeleton pair orientation is inverted relative to PM4Py (severe)**  
PM4Py’s conventional reading is `(a, b)  always_before`  *a* is always before *b*. The submission stores pairs as `(later, earlier)` (e.g. `("Classify Risk", "Receive Onboarding Request")`, `("Obtain Legal Opinion", "Sanctions Escalation")`) and documents that private convention. Under real `pm4py.conformance_log_skeleton` those constraints assert the opposite order and would systematically mis-fire. `always_after` is likewise oriented for the author’s private reading, not the library’s. The dictionaries are therefore not safely executable as claimed.

**3. R5 encoding is over-strong in the backbone and clumsy in DECLARE**  
- POWL: opinion forced before the whole outcome block (see above).  
- DECLARE: unconditional `precedence("Obtain Legal Opinion", "Activate Vendor")` fails every normal low/high-risk trace that never escalated; the author must invent an extra-textual “post-filter.” That is an admission that the chosen template cannot express the guarded rule, yet the rule is still left in the model at confidence 0.95. A clean solution would have kept R5 exclusively in the data layer or used a responded-existence + external guard only.
