Calendar reasoning used here: the laboratory closes at 18:00 on weekdays, so any chemistry work not completed by then pauses until Monday or Tuesday. Microbiology incubation runs continuously, but readout requires a microbiologist, so weekend-ready results are read on Monday unless authorization is granted. QA operates Monday–Friday until 20:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00, so QA work that crosses 20:00 pauses until the next working morning.

The maximum operation appears at the parallel join: `join = max(chemistry_usable, microbiology_usable)`. For example, if chemistry is usable Monday 15:00 and microbiology is usable Monday 09:00, the join is Monday 15:00, not Monday 24:00. Within each branch, waiting and service are sequential, but the two branch clocks run concurrently.

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

The single most forecast-changing observation is **confirmation or clearance of the temperature-excursion flag**.

- If the flag is confirmed: rerun and contamination probabilities increase, the investigation probability increases, and the central forecast shifts roughly 0.5–1 day later. The P90 tail becomes substantially more likely.
- If the flag is ruled out: use the base rerun and contamination rates; the central forecast remains Monday evening/Tuesday morning, and the severe tail shrinks.

A good secondary update trigger is the **Monday 08:00 microbiology readout / contamination screen**. A clean readout keeps the smooth path credible; a contamination indication starts the 20–30 h confirmation clock and makes Tuesday the more likely join date.
