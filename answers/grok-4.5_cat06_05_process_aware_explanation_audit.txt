## 2. Process-model consistency checks

Build a validation suite that gates both training features and explanation payloads before they reach users.

**1) Prefix-time availability (as-of / point-in-time)**  
For every feature value attached to prediction time `t0` (first `Coverage Check Completed`):  
- Assert `feature_valid_time <= t0` and source event timestamps  `t0`.  
- Replay feature computation on the event prefix only; compare to offline warehouse values (parity test).  
- Fail closed on missing validity timestamps for mutable attributes (`risk_score_*`, handler, employee attributes, document counters).

**2) Activity-order feasibility (normative + declarative)**  
Encode normative constraints, e.g. Declare/MP-Declare style:  
- *Existence/order:* `Claim Registered` before `Coverage Check Completed`; `Damage Assessment Completed` before first `Settlement Decision Recorded` (except documented exceptions); `Claim Closed` only after a final decision/withdrawal; `Appeal` only after Partial or Deny.  
- *Response/precedence:* `Documents Received` responds to `Documents Requested`; another `Documents Requested` may follow failed validation.  
- Align each full trace and each prefix to the normative model (alignment-based conformance). Record move/remove/insert costs.  
**Limitation:** Alignment fitness flags deviations; it does not by itself say a frequent deviant edge is predictive at `t0`.

**3) Same-timestamp ambiguity**  
When two activities share a timestamp:  
- Require a reliable sequence index / tie-breaker; if missing, mark ordering as unknown.  
- Do not emit DFG directly-follows features for unknown ties.  
- Specifically reject explanations that depend on `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` under tied stamps (Case Z).  
**Limitation:** Even with an index, logging systems may assign arbitrary order; treat tied closed/assessment as data quality, not signal.

**4) Complete-trace information detector**  
Static + dynamic checks:  
- Feature definitions that aggregate without a time upper bound (`*_anywhere`, `*_total` without cutoff, `full_trace_*`, `final_*`, `last_*` on whole case) are banned for early PPM.  
- Sequence explainers must run on prefix DFG/paths only; any pattern whose supporting events are after `t0` is dropped from the local explanation.

**5) Enabled vs impossible vs future-possible at `t0`**  
From the normative model and prefix marking:  
- **Observed:** activities in the prefix (Registered, Coverage Check).  
- **Enabled/future-possible:** Damage Assessment (fast track), Documents Requested, Fraud Investigation (trigger-dependent), later Settlement, etc.  
- **Impossible now:** Appeal, Claim Closed, second Settlement Decision, “final” decision values.  
Explanations may cite *observed* or *measured state at t0* (queue, elapsed, frozen complexity). They must not speak as if future-possible activities have already happened. “Fraud may still open” is scenario language, not a feature value of `Fraud Investigation Opened = 1`.

**6) Repeated `Settlement Decision Recorded` after appeal**  
- Allow a second `Settlement Decision Recorded` only if an `Appeal` (after Partial/Deny) appears in between in the trace.  
- Footprint/DFG checks: `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is a legitimate late pattern; it is still inadmissible as a `t0` early-warning feature.  
- Coarse labels need instance indices (decision #1 vs #2) so explainers do not collapse distinct semantic events.

**Role of DFG / footprint vs normative rules**  
A frequent DFG edge (e.g., some logging quirk ClosedAssessment) is a descriptive statistic of the event log, not a normative permission. Use DFG for discovery and anomaly triage; use declarative/normative rules for explanation admissibility. Prefix automata or process trees can test “is this pattern already realized in the prefix?” before SHAP/LIME/sequence attribution is shown.

**Suite outputs**  
- Hard fail: leakage features in the model matrix.  
- Soft fail / quarantine: same-timestamp patterns, repaired-lineage candidates (`prior_claim_count`, queue tolerance).  
- Explanation firewall: strip non-prefix attributions even if the model was (wrongly) trained with them.

## 3. Alternative feature interpretations

**Defensible predictive signal**  
- Examples after repair: `initial_damage_complexity`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, `initial_reserve` (if frozen), prefix-only prior claim count, `claimant_channel` (predictive use).  
- **May claim:** association with breach risk conditional on information at `t0`; useful for prioritization and early warning.  
- **May not claim:** that changing the feature in isolation would change duration (no causal identification); that the feature is an operational “root cause.”

**Process-state or congestion signal**  
- Examples: `queue_length_at_t0`; optionally handler workload or open-case counts as-of `t0`.  
- **May claim:** current system load correlates with slower settlement; supports capacity management hypotheses.  
- **May not claim:** queue length is the sole cause; or that the claim’s own future path is encoded. Actionability is at operations level (staffing), not by “editing” the feature on one claim.

**Proxy requiring fairness/robustness review**  
- Examples: `claimant_region`, `claimant_channel`, possibly reserve and complexity if they encode protected or sensitive correlates.  
- **May claim:** predictive contribution exists; mediators (repair-network density, language, preferred channel) may explain part of the signal.  
- **May not claim:** region/channel “drives delay” as a blame statement; deploy without disparity testing, slice calibration, and policy review. Prefer replacing with operationally meaningful mediators where possible, but do not delete solely for correlation without analysis.

**Drift/calendar proxy**  
- Examples: `calendar_week`, holiday-aligned weeks, policy-change windows.  
- **May claim:** seasonality and known regime shifts help forecast risk.  
- **May not claim:** intrinsic badness of a week number; stability under new calendars/policies without re-training and monitoring.

**Unusable until lineage is repaired**  
- Examples: `total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_handler_team`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, full-trace sequence patterns, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`, `risk_score_current` without as-of time, `same_day_document_count_snapshot`, buggy `prior_claim_count_before_t0`, extraction-time employee experience, impossible DFG pattern ClosedAssessment.  
- **May claim:** nothing operational at `t0` until recomputed with event-time semantics.  
- **After repair:** some become defensible (prior claims, as-of risk score, as-of team, document counts  `t0`); others remain forever invalid at this prediction point (duration, appeal seen, full variant, final decision).

**Standards (keep distinct)**  
- **Predictive validity:** available at `t0`, non-leaking, improves proper scoring rules / calibration out of sample.  
- **Causal interpretation:** needs identification assumptions, interventions, or experiments—not SHAP rank.  
- **Operational actionability:** lever must be controllable and ethically admissible (queues, staffing, early evidence requests)—not immutable claimant traits alone.  
- **Fairness review:** proxies and disparate impact even when predictively valid.

## 5. Recommendations for explanation validation

**P0 — Stop leaking into production explanations**  
1. Remove immediately from the `t0` model and explainer allowlist: `total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_handler_team`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, full-trace sequence patterns, `last_resource_role`, extraction-time employee fields.  
2. Explanation firewall: generate attributions only from the prefix feature vector actually used at score time; reject payloads that mention activities not in the prefix or not enabled with observed evidence.

**P1 — Feature-store and historical timestamps**  
3. Event-time feature store: every feature has `entity_id`, `feature_name`, `value`, `valid_time`, `transaction_time`; online/offline parity tests.  
4. As-of joins for mutable attributes (risk score, team, role, experience, document counts).  
5. Fix `prior_claim_count` to `claim_created_at < t0`; backfill; unit-test point-in-time correctness.  
6. Replace nightly `same_day_document_count_snapshot` with streaming/event-time counters.  
7. Persist sequence indices; never train directly-follows features on unresolved timestamp ties.

**P2 — Prefix-only recomputation and calibration**  
8. Recompute the entire training matrix from prefixes at first `Coverage Check Completed`.  
9. Retrain; recalibrate probabilities (reliability diagrams, ECE) after feature removal—expect base rates and SHAP ranks to shift sharply once duration/appeal/variant leakages disappear.  
10. Compare old vs new scores; document performance drop that was “fake” lift from leakage.

**P3 — Process-conformance tests in CI**  
11. Automated suite from §2: prefix availability, normative order, appeal–second-decision legitimacy, complete-trace detectors, enabled-activity checks on every explanation batch.  
12. Separate “log DFG frequency” reports from “normative rule” failures so engineers do not promote impossible edges into features.

**P4 — Explanation stability and semantics**  
13. Stability tests: bootstrap/prefix perturbation; attributions should not flip on non-prefix noise.  
14. Ban causal verbs in UI copy (“causes,” “determines”) for SHAP outputs; require templates that state association at `t0`.  
15. Treat `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as a valid *late* pattern for later prediction points, never as an early `t0` driver—productize prediction-time-specific explainer configs.

**P5 — Proxy, fairness, and actionability review**  
16. For `claimant_region`, channel, and related attributes: disparity slices, equalized calibration checks, and mediator analysis (repair network, language).  
17. Governance: predictive use  operational lever on protected/sensitive proxies; prefer congestion and process-state interventions.  
18. Keep legitimate signals (`queue_length_at_t0`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, frozen complexity)—do not purge them in a blanket “correlation bad” sweep.

**P6 — Monitoring for schema and process drift**  
19. Monitor feature validity freshness, null rates, tie-timestamp rates, conformance fitness, and week/policy regime markers.  
20. Alert when new activities, dual settlement-label rates, or holiday/policy calendars shift importance toward pure calendar proxies.  
21. Periodic re-audit of top-k global and local explanations against the normative process and PIT feature definitions.

**Success criteria**  
No top explanation cites post-`t0` outcomes; queue and prefix-age remain if predictive; appeal/decision/variant/duration are gone at early `t0`; Case Z–style impossible patterns never surface; causal and fairness language is constrained by design, not left to model-team interpretation.
