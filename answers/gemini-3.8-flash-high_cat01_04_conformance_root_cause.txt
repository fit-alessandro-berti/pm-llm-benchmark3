## 3. Causal-abductive explanation

```text
UI_release -------------------------> recorded_labels ('Classify' -> 'Risk Screen')
UI_release -------------------------> routing_error_rate (automated risk classification defects)
routing_error_rate -----------------> reviewer_behavior (reviewers intervene to correct queue assignment)
reviewer_behavior ------------------> true_process (operational execution of Manual Override)
reviewer_behavior ------------------> recorded_labels ('Manual Override' logged)
reviewer_behavior ------------------> unlogged_or_bypassed_checks (reviewers skip compliance step under workload)
unlogged_or_bypassed_checks --------> recorded_labels (omission of 'Compliance Check' events)
unlogged_or_bypassed_checks --------> business_outcomes (unrealized legal/regulatory audit liability)
policy_change ----------------------> claim_value_gt_50k (EUR 50,000 regulatory policy rule)
claim_value_gt_50k -----------------> true_process (claims routed to supervisor approval)
true_process -----------------------> recorded_labels ('Supervisor Sign-off' emitted)
true_process -----------------------> business_outcomes (slight cycle time increase from 26.0h to 27.4h)
telemetry_gateway_injection --------> recorded_labels (synthetic 'Queue Routed' inserted)
ETL_change -------------------------> ingestion_network_jitter (asynchronous warehouse arrival order)
ingestion_network_jitter -----------> recorded_order (temporal swap of 'Decision' and 'Notify')
recorded_labels --------------------> alignment_moves (log moves on 'Risk Screen', 'Queue Routed', 'Supervisor Sign-off')
recorded_order ---------------------> alignment_moves (model/log swap moves on 'Decision' vs 'Notify')
normative_model --------------------> alignment_moves (unversioned model penalizes valid new and renamed activities)
alignment_moves --------------------> measured_fitness (measured trace fitness collapses from 93% to 32%)
```

The dramatic collapse in measured conformance from 93% to 32% is an illusion created by the simultaneous convergence of three independent mechanisms: pipeline extraction defects, normative model obsolescence, and a distinct, low-volume compliance vulnerability.

First, the volumetric collapse is overwhelmingly driven by syntactic logging and data pipeline artifacts. UI Release 4.2 renamed an activity without an ETL schema update; the event gateway injected automated infrastructure telemetry (Queue Routed) into the business stream; and ETL modifications introduced warehouse ingestion jitter that inverted the apparent sequence of Decision and Notify. Together, these technical artifacts account for over 85% of alignment penalties. Simultaneously, the August policy introduced a mandatory supervisor sign-off for high-value claims without versioning the normative model, creating severe structural model obsolescence.

Second, the stability of macro business metrics (approval rates at ~71.8% and 30-day appeal rates at ~3.7%) neither proves data integrity nor rules out employee noncompliance. Macro business outcomes are coarse, heavily lagged indicators; they remain completely insensitive to procedural compliance shortcuts, such as bypassing compliance checks on enhanced reviews (~4% of cases). Procedural bypasses rarely produce immediate customer appeals if claims are approved, yet they generate catastrophic regulatory liabilities. Conversely, the stable cycle times refute operational chaos, confirming that the vast majority of observed deviations represent harmless measurement noise rather than widespread organizational failure.

The sudden collapse in measured process conformance from 93% to 32% was overwhelmingly caused by data-provenance defects and normative model obsolescence, rather than systemic employee noncompliance. Systematically correcting these four data and governance factors restores trace fitness to 89% in June-July and 86% in August-September, matching baseline performance. We assign a **99% confidence level** to technical extraction artifacts and model obsolescence as the primary driver of the measured conformance collapse.

However, a critical compliance vulnerability remains unresolved. In June-July (25 traces, 3.8%) and August-September (30 traces, 4.8%), claims undergoing `Enhanced Review` proceeded directly to `Decision` without a recorded `Compliance Check`. ## 8. Model-governance recommendation

1. **Model Versioning and Lifecycle Synchronization**: Establish a formal Process Model Registry where normative process trees and Petri nets are versioned artifacts bound to specific effective date ranges. Any organizational policy update (such as the August 1 EUR 50,000 sign-off requirement) must trigger a mandated change-management workflow requiring an updated, approved process model release before the policy effective date, preventing false-positive obsolescence collapses.
2. **Event-Semantic Schema Contracts**: Enforce strict data contracts between application development teams and the process mining ingestion pipeline. User interface modifications and field relabeling (such as UI release 4.2 renaming `Classify` to `Risk Screen`) must preserve underlying canonical event semantic identifiers (`concept:name`) or register formal alias mappings in the ETL schema catalog prior to deployment.
3. **Telemetry and Infrastructure Event Isolation Policy**: Formulate an architectural policy strictly segregating business domain milestones from technical middleware telemetry. Internal infrastructure events (such as gateway `Queue Routed` logs) must be routed to technical monitoring sinks (e.g., APM/logging pipelines) and filtered from the business event log extracted for regulatory conformance checking unless explicitly certified as human-driven operational tasks.
4. **Monotonic Timestamp Provenance and Multi-Clock Extraction**: Mandate that ETL extraction pipelines preserve primary source-system monotonic sequence numbers alongside millisecond-level execution timestamps recorded at the source database commit level. Warehouse-ingestion time must never serve as the ordering key for asynchronous transactional events (`Decision` vs `Notify`), eliminating ingestion network jitter artifacts.
5. **Data-Aware Petri Nets (DPN) and Guard Constraint Enforcement**: Transition regulatory conformance models from purely control-flow process trees to Data-Aware Petri Nets (DPNs) that incorporate attribute guards and resource authorization rules. Decision points dependent on case-level attributes (such as `claim_amount > EUR 50,000` for `Supervisor Sign-off`) must be formally guarded in the model to avoid overgeneralization and detect both illicit skips on high-value claims and unnecessary delays on low-value claims.
6. **Continuous Post-Release Conformance Regression Testing**: Integrate automated conformance regression testing into CI/CD deployment pipelines. Every UI release, gateway configuration patch, or ETL pipeline modification must automatically trigger a dry-run conformance check on synthetic and staging trace batches; any sudden delta in alignment move frequencies or fitness collapse exceeding 5% must automatically gate production deployment pending cross-functional sign-off from IT, Operations, and Compliance.
