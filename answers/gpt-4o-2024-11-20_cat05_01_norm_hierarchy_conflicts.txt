## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

### **Authority**
- **Facts**: GPIP-4 is a baseline corporate policy, formally board-approved. Local SOP DE-AAC-PROC-17 may impose stricter but not weaker controls. ECD-2 is a temporary incident directive, signed by the COO and Incident Commander, but lacks explicit ratification by the Chief Risk Officer.
- **Assumptions**: ECD-2 represents a legitimate temporary overlay on operational procedures for its stated scope and duration, even if not explicitly ratified by the Chief Risk Officer, as prior practice treats such directives as temporary policies. The CFO email cannot supersede GPIP-4, Compliance controls, or segregation of duties, as emails are not policy-level instruments. CFO authority is limited to Finance control interpretation, not Compliance waiver or vendor approval.

### **Specificity and Timing**
- **Facts**: ECD-2 directly addresses the contamination-related sterilization-line purchases but lacks explicit clarity on some GPIP-4 controls, such as Finance approval and segregation of duties.
- **Assumptions**: A more specific policy (e.g., ECD-2) can temporally override general policies (e.g., GPIP-4) within its stated scope, unless explicitly restricted by higher-order norms or unless GPIP-4 prohibits such temporal overrides for certain areas.

### **Emergency Necessity**
- **Facts**: GPIP-4 and DE-AAC-PROC-17 allow some procedural flexibility in emergencies but do not fully waive integrity controls (e.g., Finance and Compliance approvals).
- **Assumptions**: An emergency exists only when imminent safety harm or production stoppage arises (SOP B-4). Components labeled "critical" under Incident Command should include those necessary to prevent immediate harm or uphold contamination recovery within ECD-2's timeframe.

### **Retrospective Evidence**
- **Facts**: The ERP system outage affects timestamp reliability but does not negate stronger contemporaneous evidence. Actions such as verbal authorizations or signed PDFs during the outage will be considered.
- **Assumptions**: Retrospective records are valid for confirming timely adherence, but acts performed outside appropriate authority, scope, or timing cannot be cured retroactively by later logging.

### **Burden of Proof**
- **Facts**: GPIP-4 and SOP B dictate a general baseline of compliance and control discipline.
- **Assumptions**: Any deviation requires explicit justification rooted in documented exceptions, emergency protocols, or higher authority. The burden of proof lies with the party claiming justified deviation from established norms.
