# Process Redesign for Environmental Impact Reduction in Spare-Parts Distribution

## 1. System Boundary and Assumptions
**Scope:**
- **Included:** All process steps from order receipt to delivery confirmation, including production, packaging, and transport. Environmental impacts measured via energy (kWh), transport emissions (kgCO2e), material scrap (kg), and packaging waste (kg).
- **Excluded:** Supplier upstream impacts (raw material extraction, upstream transport), end-of-life product disposal, and customer-side usage (beyond returns/rework).
- **Assumptions:**
  - Energy and emissions data are accurate at the order level (aggregated by case).
  - Policies SP1, SP2, and Q1 are enforceable via process controls.
  - Customer behavior (e.g., demand variability) is exogenous but can be influenced via lead-time negotiation.
- **Uncertainty:** Rework loops and partial shipments introduce variability; policy violations (e.g., 22% SP1 breaches) suggest enforcement gaps.

## 3. Intervention Options

### S1. Green Shipping Recommender
- **Target:** Standard-stock Service-contract orders (16% of cases, 22% SP1 violations).
- **Mechanism:** Use promised lead time slack and route reliability to recommend ground/consolidated truck instead of air. Prioritize orders with 24h slack.
- **Service/Quality:** Minimal impact; may increase cycle time by 12-24h but reduces late deliveries (current air shipments have 3% late rate vs. 5% for ground).
- **Evidence:** Monitor SP1 conformance and late-delivery rates post-implementation.

### S2. Dynamic Batching with Time Caps
- **Target:** MTO paths using CNC_A/heat treatment (13% of cases, high batch wait times).
- **Mechanism:** Cap batch wait time at 12h for Service-contract orders; allow up to 36h for Regular orders. Use energy savings from larger batches to offset wait-time increases.
- **Service/Quality:** Reduces late deliveries by 5-10% (current heat-treatment batch wait increases late risk by 1.7x). Rework risk may increase if inspections are rushed.
- **Evidence:** Track batch wait time vs. energy/kWh per item and late-delivery rates.

### S3. Shift MTO Production from CNC_B to CNC_A
- **Target:** MTO paths using CNC_B (5% of cases, 18% rework rate).
- **Mechanism:** Route orders to CNC_A when queues allow, leveraging its lower rework rate (9%). Use due-date slack to prioritize.
- **Service/Quality:** Reduces rework by 50% (from 18% to 9%) but may increase cycle time by 10-20% if CNC_A is congested.
- **Evidence:** Monitor rework rates and CNC_A utilization.

### S4. Replace Air for Standard-Stock Service-Contract
- **Target:** Standard-stock Service-contract air shipments (5% of cases).
- **Mechanism:** Use priority picking + ground shipment if promised lead time allows (current median slack is 18h vs. 17h air cycle time).
- **Service/Quality:** No service deterioration; reduces transport emissions by 80% (air: 18.5 kgCO2e  ground: 3.6 kgCO2e).
- **Evidence:** Track SP1 conformance and customer complaints.

### S5. Consolidated Truck Departures Twice Daily
- **Target:** Non-urgent stock orders (42% of cases, ground shipments).
- **Mechanism:** Batch ground shipments into two daily consolidated truck departures (morning/evening) instead of continuous parcel ground.
- **Service/Quality:** Increases cycle time by 12-24h but reduces transport emissions by 35-55%. Late deliveries may rise by 2-3%.
- **Evidence:** Monitor consolidation wait times and late-delivery rates.

### S6. Packaging-Route Compatibility Checks
- **Target:** All reinforced packaging orders (13% of MTO cases).
- **Mechanism:** Automatically validate packaging choice against route_mode and part geometry before shipment. Flag incompatible combinations (e.g., reinforced packaging with parcel ground).
- **Service/Quality:** Reduces packaging rework by 30% (current 260+360 rework events). May increase packaging material use if substitutions are required.
- **Evidence:** Track packaging_rework_flag and material_scrap_kg.

### S7. Inspection Quality Gate for High-Rework Machines
- **Target:** Inspection_cell queues feeding CNC_B (5% of cases, 32% late deliveries).
- **Mechanism:** Add a secondary inspection for parts from CNC_B or heat treatment batches with >10% historical rework. Delay release if defects are suspected.
- **Service/Quality:** Increases cycle time by 4-8h but reduces rework loops by 20-30%.
- **Evidence:** Monitor inspection_cell throughput and rework rates.

### S8. Replace Dedicated Vans with Shared Emergency Couriers
- **Target:** Critical-downtime Service-contract dedicated van shipments (8% of cases).
- **Mechanism:** Use shared emergency courier lanes for precautionary urgent orders (not true downtime). Reserve dedicated vans for confirmed downtime.
- **Service/Quality:** Reduces transport emissions by 30-40% (dedicated van: 52 kgCO2e  shared courier: ~35 kgCO2e). May increase delivery variability.
- **Evidence:** Track justification events for dedicated van use and late deliveries.

### S9. Split Critical-Downtime into Categories
- **Target:** Critical-downtime Service-contract orders (11% of cases).
- **Mechanism:** Classify orders as "true downtime" (immediate) or "precautionary urgent" (within 4h). Use ground/air for precautionary urgent; reserve dedicated vans for true downtime.
- **Service/Quality:** Reduces dedicated van emissions by 50% for precautionary orders. True downtime service levels remain protected.
- **Evidence:** Monitor order classification accuracy and service levels.

### S10. Carbon-Aware Promise-Date Negotiation
- **Target:** Regular MTO customers (22% of cases).
- **Mechanism:** Offer a "green lead time" option with a 10-20% longer promised lead time in exchange for ground/consolidated transport.
- **Service/Quality:** May increase late deliveries for Regular customers by 5-10% but reduces transport emissions by 40-60%.
- **Evidence:** Track customer acceptance rates and late-delivery rates.

### S11. Reduce Partial Shipments
- **Target:** Partial shipment paths (7% of cases, 24% late deliveries).
- **Mechanism:** Change availability-check rules to prioritize full order fulfillment or backorder entire orders if stock is insufficient. Reduce partial shipments by 50%.
- **Service/Quality:** Reduces customer contact and second shipments but may increase order cancellations (current 410 cancellations).
- **Evidence:** Monitor partial shipment rates and cancellation rates.

### S12. Sustainability Exception Log
- **Target:** All policy violations (SP1/SP2/Q1).
- **Mechanism:** Mandate justification events for overrides (e.g., air shipments, dedicated vans, packaging violations). Log reasons and track patterns.
- **Service/Quality:** Improves policy conformance but may increase process complexity.
- **Evidence:** Monitor exception rates and policy compliance.

## 5. Rebound and Displacement Risks

1. **Batching Rebound:**
   - Larger batches reduce energy per item but increase obsolescence (6% of batch-produced items unsold within 14 days). Mitigate by capping batch sizes and improving demand forecasting.

2. **Transport Displacement:**
   - Consolidated trucks reduce emissions but may shift delays to customer sites (e.g., missed delivery windows). Mitigate by offering flexible delivery slots.

3. **Packaging Displacement:**
   - Reinforced packaging reduces damage returns but increases material waste. Mitigate by using recycled materials or reusable packaging where feasible.

4. **Rework Displacement:**
   - Inspection quality gates reduce rework but may increase cycle time, leading to more expedited orders (higher emissions). Mitigate by optimizing inspection queues.

5. **Obsolescence Risk:**
   - Large production batches increase stock holding times, raising obsolescence risk. Mitigate by aligning batch sizes with demand forecasts and implementing dynamic batching.

6. **Customer Behavior Rebound:**
   - Carbon-aware lead-time negotiation may encourage customers to opt for longer lead times, increasing perceived service deterioration. Mitigate by transparently communicating trade-offs.

7. **Resilience Displacement:**
   - Shared emergency couriers reduce emissions but may increase delivery variability during disruptions. Mitigate by maintaining backup carrier contracts.

---

## 6. Balanced Recommendation
**Proposed Redesign Package:**
1. **Tiered Shipping Policy (S1, S4, S5, S9):**
   - Enforce SP1 for Standard-stock Service-contract orders (S1, S4).
   - Implement twice-daily consolidated truck departures for non-urgent stock orders (S5).
   - Split Critical-downtime into true/precautionary categories, reserving dedicated vans for true downtime (S9).

2. **Dynamic Production Optimization (S2, S3, S7):**
   - Cap batch wait times at 12h for Service-contract orders; allow CNC_A to absorb CNC_B orders when feasible (S2, S3).
   - Add inspection quality gates for high-rework machine groups (S7).

3. **Packaging and Exception Governance (S6, S12):**
   - Enforce packaging-route compatibility checks (S6).
   - Mandate justification logs for policy overrides (S12).

**Why This Balances Trade-offs:**
- **Environmental:** Targets high-impact paths (air shipments, dedicated vans, rework) while preserving service for critical orders.
- **Service:** Prioritizes Service-contract customers and critical orders; Regular customers accept longer lead times for sustainability.
- **Quality:** Reduces rework via inspection gates and packaging checks.
- **Resilience:** Maintains alternative routes (e.g., consolidated trucks, shared couriers) and enforces dedicated van justifications.

**Exclusions:**
- Avoids blanket bans on air/dedicated van transport, as these are justified for Critical-downtime orders.
- Does not prioritize lowest emissions at the cost of service (e.g., no forced consolidation for urgent orders).

## 7. Validation and Governance Plan
**Validation:**
1. **Conformance Monitoring:**
   - Track SP1/SP2/Q1 violations pre- and post-implementation. Aim for <5% violations.
   - Monitor exception logs (S12) for patterns in policy overrides.

2. **Segment-Level Service Monitoring:**
   - Late-delivery rates by segment: Target 10% for all.
   - Rework rates by machine group: Target <5% for CNC_A, <8% for CNC_B.
   - Packaging rework events: Target <150/month.

3. **Environmental KPIs:**
   - Avg kgCO2e/order: Target 15% reduction in MTO paths.
   - Avg kWh/order: Target 10% reduction in batch-dependent paths.
   - Obsolescence rate: Target <3% for batch-produced items.

**Governance:**
1. **Process Mining Feedback Loops:**
   - Monthly reviews of event logs to identify new high-impact paths (e.g., emerging rework loops).
   - Adjust batch caps, inspection gates, or shipping rules based on conformance data.

2. **Exception Escalation:**
   - Require senior approval for >3 policy violations per week per segment.
   - Log and analyze justification events for dedicated vans/air shipments.

3. **Customer Communication:**
   - For Regular MTO customers, offer carbon-aware lead-time options with clear trade-offs (S10).
   - For Service-contract customers, provide sustainability reports showing emissions reductions.

4. **Resilience Testing:**
   - Quarterly disruption simulations (e.g., carrier failure) to validate alternative route availability (R1).
