# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The source is a **POWL behavioral specification**, not a drawing. In particular, the source’s explicit edges are dependencies; visual placement is not an ordering constraint.

Below, \(\tau_L\) denotes the silent branch of the optional-legal XOR. The source does not contain an explicitly named silent transition for the verification loop. A converter may introduce loop-control transitions, but their behavior must be checked.

A positive witness is allowed by the relevant invariant. A negative witness is either forbidden by the source or exposes a missing required behavior. For an absence-of-order invariant, rejecting a valid interleaving is a **counterexample to the converted model**, not a counterexample to the source.

All visible labels in the source are unique: `Register Change`, `Assess Risk`, `Prepare Evidence`, `Legal Review`, `Configure Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, `Correct Defect`, `Release Change`, and `Archive Change`.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I01 | `Register Change` occurs exactly once and is the first visible activity. | `[Register Change, …]` | `[Prepare Evidence, Register Change, …]`, or a completed path without registration. | Yes. |
| I02 | `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` each occur once, with no ordering constraint between them. | `[Register, Prepare, Assess, …]` and `[Register, Assess, Prepare, …]`. | A conversion that additionally requires one particular order imposes a constraint absent from the source. | Yes, although visible traces alone cannot establish token-level concurrency. |
| I03 | Verification requires both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence`; neither alone enables it. | With both complete, a legal trial and inspection may occur. | Configure or inspect without completing both prerequisites. | Yes for the required visible activities; hidden routing remains relevant. |
| I04 | Legal review is optional and occurs at most once. XOR chooses either the visible legal route or the silent route. | A completed path with `Legal Review`, and another with no legal-review activity. | Two occurrences of `Legal Review` in one execution. | Partly: the visible count is observable; the silent branch is not. |
| I05 | Optional legal review and verification have no order edge. They may overlap at the model level. | After risk and evidence are complete: `L, Legal, Configure, Inspect`; also `L, Configure, Inspect, Legal`. | Rejecting both visible orders, or imposing a mandatory legal-before-verification or verification-before-legal sequence. | The permitted orders are visible; independence is not established merely by those words. |
| I06 | Every trial iteration executes `Configure Trial` before `Inspect Trial`. | `[Configure, Inspect, …]`. | `[Inspect, Configure, …]`. | Yes. |
| I07 | When another iteration is selected after an inspection, `Correct Defect` separates that inspection from the next configuration. Repetition is not a free repeated `Configure/Inspect` loop. | `[Configure, Inspect, Correct, Configure, Inspect, …]`. | `[Configure, Inspect, Inspect, …]`, or treating `Correct Defect` as a mandatory final activity after every last iteration. | Yes. |
| I08 | At least one complete trial iteration occurs. | Any completed execution contains at least one `Configure Trial` followed by `Inspect Trial`. | Exiting verification without ever configuring and inspecting a trial. | Yes, under unique, stable activity identities. |
| I09 | Release synchronizes on both optional legal and completed verification. Legal may be skipped, but it must first be resolved through its XOR. | `[Register, Assess, Prepare, L, Legal, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive]`. | `[Register, Assess, Prepare, L, Release, Configure, Inspect, Archive]`. | Partly: token/routing state is needed to distinguish a complete join from coincidental trace order. |
| I10 | `Release Change` occurs once after the join; `Archive Change` occurs once afterward and is the last visible activity. | `[…, Release, Archive]`, including paths that terminate the loop after one iteration. | Release before verification, archive before release, or a visible activity after archive. | Yes for visible completion. |
| I11 | Activities have distinct source identities and labels. Register, optional legal, release, and archive occur at most once; configure, inspect, and correct each occur at least once and may repeat. | A full source execution with all nine distinct labels. | A conversion that duplicates, omits, or confuses an activity identity. A net with two transitions labeled `Legal Review` cannot be validated by visible labels alone. | Labels are observable, but a label match does not establish provenance or identity. |
| I12 | The source starts before registration and has a final visible `Archive Change` on every completed execution. Initial and final markings are explicit parts of the accepting-net representation, not recoverable safely from an arbitrary BPMN drawing. | An initial state that enables registration and a completed state with no further visible activity after archive. | Treating an unverified BPMN start/end node as proof of source initial/final semantics. | Partly: hidden initial/final control is not visible. |

Two distinctions matter throughout:

- **True concurrency is not arbitrary interleaving in the Petri-net sense.** The source does not constrain the relative order of assessment and evidence. A serial implementation that always performs assessment before evidence does not add an ordering constraint, although it is more restrictive than an implementation allowing either order. A conversion that forces evidence before assessment adds a new constraint.
- **A loop-control transition is not another activity.** Hiding tau must not change trial cardinality, repair an incorrect join, or make an illegal branch look completed.

A compact block-structured *target* can be written schematically, but this is not evidence that `Petri  process tree` will discover it:

```text
-> (
  Register,
  * (
    -> (Assess, Prepare),
    + (
      -> (Legal, tau_L),
      -> (trial, Correct)*        # explicit at-least-one trial; no trailing Correct
    )
  ),
  + (
    -> (verification, Release),
    -> (Release, Archive)
  )
)
```

That diagram is a possible specification of the intended structure—not a process tree that PM4Py is assumed to produce.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A — primary:** `POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN` | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; then `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Preserves an executable token-firing model, explicit markings, hidden transitions, and the XOR/loop/join structure. BPMN is generated as a derivative view. | The initial net is not automatically correct. BPMN rendering may obscure tau routing, concurrency, loop cardinality, or place-versus-transition semantics. BPMN re-import may reconstruct a different model. | Null results, conversion exceptions, unexplained workflow-net failure, false soundness diagnostics, missing final markings, source-witness rejection, acceptance of counter-witnesses, or a mismatch under a completed bounded comparison. |
| **B — alternative:** `POWL  accepting Petri net  process tree  BPMN` | A’s calls; then `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; on success, `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | May yield a more readable structured representation when a suitable tree exists. | A returned tree does not prove faithfulness. A net-derived `LOOP` may be serialized as a zero-or-more shortcut, losing mandatory first execution. Non-block-structured behavior is not guaranteed to be representable. | A caught tree exception is an explicit alternative-pipeline failure. A returned tree still fails the conversion if it loses optionality, trial cardinality, concurrency, synchronization, or finality. |

**Chosen primary: Pipeline A.**

Pipeline B is allowed to fail. A tree-conversion exception must be recorded and interpreted, not hidden by an empty fallback tree, a guessed tree, or relabeling silent transitions as activities.

# 3. Conversion Code

Run the supplied source definition first, without modifying it. The following functions can then be placed in the same script. The guarded driver in Section 5 calls them and assigns the requested names.

The code deliberately distinguishes:

1. a **conversion exception**;
2. a null or malformed conversion result;
3. a structural/soundness diagnostic;
4. a behavioral witness mismatch;
5. a rendering failure.

A rendering failure is not evidence that the underlying net is wrong.

```python
from pathlib import Path
import copy
import logging

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri.petrinet import Marking


def _accepting_result(value, owner):
    """Check the documented (net, im, fm) result without assuming null safety."""
    if value is None:
        raise ValueError(f"{owner}: converter returned None")

    try:
        net, im, fm = value
    except (TypeError, ValueError) as exc:
        raise ValueError(
            f"{owner}: expected a (net, im, fm) result; got {value!r}"
        ) from exc

    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        raise ValueError(
            f"{owner}: null component; net={net!r}, im={im!r}, fm={fm!r}"
        )

    # PM4Py commonly supplies one Marking. Accept an explicit list of finals.
    if isinstance(fm, Marking):
        finals = [fm]
    elif isinstance(fm, list) and fm:
        finals = fm
    else:
        raise TypeError(
            f"{owner}: final marking must be a Marking or nonempty list of Markings"
        )

    if not all(isinstance(m, Marking) for m in finals):
        raise TypeError(f"{owner}: final markings must contain only Marking objects")

    return net, im, finals


def _checked_bpmn(value, owner):
    if value is None:
        raise ValueError(f"{owner}: BPMN conversion returned None")
    return value


def _diagnostic_call(function, **kwargs):
    """Do not turn a diagnostic exception or None into a successful boolean."""
    try:
        return {"result": function(**kwargs), "error": None}
    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "result": None,
            "error": {
                "type": type(exc).__name__,
                "message": str(exc),
            },
        }


def _register_accepting_net(
    name, net, im, finals, variants, diagnostics
):
    workflow = _diagnostic_call(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net, net=net)
    soundness = _diagnostic_call(
        pm4py.check_soundness,
        net=net,
        im=im,
        fm=finals,
        print_diagnostics=True,
    )

    record = {
        "name": name,
        "net": net,
        "im": im,
        "fm": finals,
        "final_count": len(finals),
        "workflow_check": workflow,
        "soundness_check": soundness,
    }
    variants.append(record)
    diagnostics[name] = {
        "final_count": len(finals),
        "workflow_check": workflow,
        "soundness_check": soundness,
    }

    print(f"\nDiagnostics for {name}")
    print("  workflow check:", workflow)
    print("  soundness check:", soundness)

    if len(finals) > 1:
        print(
            "  NOTE: multiple accepting markings. A structural workflow-net "
            "check may fail even when the marked model has meaningful alternatives."
        )

    return record


def build_models(source_powl, output_directory="pm4py_artifacts"):
    output_directory = Path(output_directory)
    output_directory.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)

    models = []
    accepting_nets = []
    diagnostics = {}
    rendering_errors = []

    def add_model(name, model):
        if model is None:
            raise ValueError(f"{name}: model is None")
        models.append((name, model))

    # ---------------- Primary pipeline ----------------

    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = _accepting_result(
        pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl),
        "POWL -> Petri net",
    )

    _register_accepting_net(
        "net_from_powl",
        net_from_powl,
        im_from_powl,
        fm_from_powl,
        accepting_nets,
        diagnostics,
    )

    bpmn_from_net = _checked_bpmn(
        pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl),
        "Petri net -> BPMN",
    )
    add_model("bpmn_from_net", bpmn_from_net)

    (
        net_from_bpmn_net,
        im_from_bpmn_net,
        fm_from_bpmn_net,
    ) = _accepting_result(
        pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net),
        "bpmn_from_net -> Petri net",
    )

    _register_accepting_net(
        "net_from_bpmn_net",
        net_from_bpmn_net,
        im_from_bpmn_net,
        fm_from_bpmn_net,
        accepting_nets,
        diagnostics,
    )

    # ---------------- Alternative process-tree pipeline ----------------

    tree_from_net = None
    bpmn_from_tree = None
    net_from_tree_bpmn = None
    im_from_tree_bpmn = None
    fm_from_tree_bpmn = None

    try:
        tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
            net_from_powl,
            im_from_powl,
            fm_from_powl,
        )
        if tree_from_net is None:
            raise ValueError("Petri -> process tree returned None")

        add_model("tree_from_net", tree_from_net)

        bpmn_from_tree = _checked_bpmn(
            pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net),
            "process tree -> BPMN",
        )
        add_model("bpmn_from_tree", bpmn_from_tree)

        (
            net_from_tree_bpmn,
            im_from_tree_bpmn,
            fm_from_tree_bpmn,
        ) = _accepting_result(
            pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_tree),
            "bpmn_from_tree -> Petri net",
        )

        _register_accepting_net(
            "net_from_tree_bpmn",
            net_from_tree_bpmn,
            im_from_tree_bpmn,
            fm_from_tree_bpmn,
            accepting_nets,
            diagnostics,
        )

        tree_conversion = {
            "status": "ambiguous",
            "error": None,
            "interpretation": (
                "A tree was returned. This is not evidence of semantic fidelity."
            ),
        }

    except Exception as exc:
        tree_conversion = {
            "status": "conversion failed",
            "error": {
                "type": type(exc).__name__,
                "message": str(exc),
            },
            "interpretation": (
                "The PM4Py process-tree conversion did not return a usable tree. "
                "This may reflect unsupported block structure or another library "
                "limitation. It does not, by itself, prove the source is not "
                "block-structured, and it must not be repaired by guessing a tree."
            ),
        }
        logging.exception(
            "Alternative process-tree pipeline failed; "
            "the primary net-to-BPMN pipeline remains separately recorded."
        )

    # ---------------- Render derivative artifacts ----------------

    def save_visualization(description, function, *args):
        path = output_directory / f"{description}.png"
        try:
            function(*args, str(path))
        except Exception as exc:
            # Missing Graphviz/rendering support is not silently called success.
            rendering_errors.append(
                {
                    "artifact": str(path),
                    "type": type(exc).__name__,
                    "message": str(exc),
                }
            )
            logging.exception("Visualization failed for %s", path)

    save_visualization(
        "source_powl",
        pm4py.save_vis_powl,
        source_powl,
    )

    for name, model in models:
        if name == "tree_from_net":
            save_visualization(
                name,
                pm4py.save_vis_process_tree,
                model,
            )
        else:
            save_visualization(
                name,
                pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
                model,
            )

    for record in accepting_nets:
        save_visualization(
            record["name"],
            pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
            record["net"],
            record["im"],
            record["fm"],
        )

    return {
        "output_directory": output_directory,
        "models": models,
        "accepting_nets": accepting_nets,
        "diagnostics": diagnostics,
        "tree_conversion": tree_conversion,
        "rendering_errors": rendering_errors,
        "named": {
            "net_from_powl": net_from_powl,
            "im_from_powl": im_from_powl,
            "fm_from_powl": fm_from_powl,
            "bpmn_from_net": bpmn_from_net,
            "tree_from_net": tree_from_net,
            "bpmn_from_tree": bpmn_from_tree,
            "net_from_bpmn_net": net_from_bpmn_net,
            "im_from_bpmn_net": im_from_bpmn_net,
            "fm_from_bpmn_net": fm_from_bpmn_net,
            "net_from_tree_bpmn": net_from_tree_bpmn,
            "im_from_tree_bpmn": im_from_tree_bpmn,
            "fm_from_tree_bpmn": fm_from_tree_bpmn,
        },
    }
```

This code intentionally does not use a hypothetical `POWL  BPMN` or `POWL  process tree` function.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

This is a **pre-execution audit**. No conversion result or exception is invented.

For Pipeline B, a caught process-tree exception would be recorded as `conversion failed`. If a tree is returned, its status remains `ambiguous` until the source-relative checks support a stronger conclusion. The BPMN round-trip column is `not tested` until Section 5 is actually run.

Footprints, when available, describe **causal relations between activity occurrences**. They are not a complete test of unrestricted concurrency: non-occurrence of a causal pair does not itself mean both interleavings are admitted.

| Source invariant | POWLPetri | PetriBPMN | PetriTree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I01: one visible registration first | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Inspect initial tokens, enabled visible transitions, and replay a witness beginning with `Prepare Evidence`. The PM4Py contract is not proof. |
| I02: assessment/evidence unordered | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Accept both `R,A,P,…` and `R,P,A,…`; also look for an artificial dependency introduced by routing. |
| I03: verification waits for assessment and evidence | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Reject a path enabling trial before both predecessors. BPMN joins require structural inspection or behavioral replay. |
| I04: optional legal route versus tau | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Inspect XOR successors. Test both legal and no-legal completed executions, and reject repeated visible legal review. |
| I05: no mandatory order between legal and verification | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | With prerequisites complete, accept both visible orders. Inspect whether a gateway/join accidentally serializes them. A layout is not evidence. |
| I06: configure before inspect | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Replay reverse-order witnesses. Inspect the generated loop body and place-token flow. |
| I07: correction separates repeated iterations | ambiguous | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Accept a two-iteration witness, reject consecutive inspections without correction, and do not make a trailing correction mandatory. |
| I08: at least one trial iteration | ambiguous | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Reject completion with no `Configure/Inspect` body. A process-tree zero-or-more shortcut is a serious fidelity risk. |
| I09: legal/verification synchronization before release | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Reject the explicit release-before-inspection witness. The accepting net must not admit release while verification remains incomplete. |
| I10: release then archive, with finality | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Test exact completed traces and exact final-marking equality. A final-looking BPMN end node alone is insufficient. |
| I11: unique activity identities and labels | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Inspect transition names and labels. Distinct autogenerated identifiers do not prove that a label maps to the correct source activity. |
| I12: initial/final markings and soundness | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Non-null markings must be checked. Report every final marking. Multiple finals can make a strict workflow-net check fail; soundness diagnostics and replay still require interpretation. |
| Structural and visual rendering | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | A successful PNG proves only that rendering returned. It does not establish that gateways, place labels, or routing nodes encode the intended BPMN semantics. |

Two particularly important binary-loop concerns are:

1. **The POWL `LOOP` is binary**, not an arbitrary-cardinality language. Its semantics must not be silently generalized.
2. A net-to-process-tree converter can serialize the loop region as a repeat structure. If the source requires a mandatory first trial, a returned `LOOP`/`*` node does not establish that first trial unless the tree actually encodes it.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The following code uses the PM4Py utilities and APIs named in the question. It performs:

- workflow-net and soundness diagnostics;
- footprints and reachability-graph collection;
- bounded visible-trace exploration;
- positive and negative witness replay;
- best-effort model playout.

### What the bounded exploration proves

The default bound is:

- at most 20 visible occurrences;
- at most 4 occurrences of `Inspect Trial`, hence four completed trial iterations in a conforming completed execution;
- at most 64 silent transitions without an intervening visible activity;
- no repeated marking along the silent-only segment between two visible activities;
- a safety cap on exploration work.

A repeated visible marking is **not** pruned: it may represent a legitimate next iteration.

Only hiding transitions whose label is exactly `None` is permitted. A completed execution is recorded only on **exact equality with one of the supplied final markings**.

The exploration is exhaustive only if it reaches the work cap without any visible, iteration, silent, or silent-cycle cut. Even then, its conclusion is scoped to the chosen bound, not to all possible repetitions. A result with any cut is only a bounded subset and must not be used to assert equality.

The process-based playout helper protects against a simulator running indefinitely. A model sample is evidence of an admitted execution only; it is never used as equivalence proof.

```python
from collections import Counter, deque
import copy
import json
import multiprocessing as mp
import os

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri.petrinet import Marking
from pm4py.objects.petri.utils import (
    execute_tpn,
    get_transition_data,
)


def _marking_key(marking):
    return tuple(
        sorted((str(place), int(token_count))
               for place, token_count in marking.items()
    )


def _enabled(net, marking):
    enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
    if enabled is None:
        raise TypeError("get_enabled_transitions returned None")
    return list(enabled)


def _transition_info(net, transition):
    data = get_transition_data(net, transition)
    if data is None:
        raise KeyError(f"No transition data for {transition!r}")

    label = data.get("label")
    if label is not None and not isinstance(label, str):
        raise TypeError(
            f"Expected string or None label, got {label!r} "
            f"for {transition!r}"
        )
    return label, transition


def _visible_trace(records):
    return tuple(label for label, _ in records if label is not None)


def _bounded_completed_executions(
    net,
    im,
    finals,
    max_visible=20,
    max_iterations=4,
    max_silent_between_visible=64,
    max_states=500_000,
):
    """
    Return completed visible traces and evidence about exhaustion.

    It does not claim:
      * equivalence with any other model;
      * exact finality merely because a trace has no enabled transition;
      * coverage of traces beyond the configured bounds.
    """
    final_keys = {_marking_key(f) for f in finals}
    completed = set()
    cuts = Counter()
    unproductive_markings = set()

    initial_key = _marking_key(im)

    # State is: marking, visible trace, inspection count,
    #           silent-only marking history, silent count.
    work = deque([
        (
            im,
            initial_key,
            (),
            0,
            frozenset({initial_key}),
            0,
        )
    ])
    seen_states = set()
    explored_states = 0
    analysis_error = None

    while work:
        if len(seen_states) >= max_states:
            cuts["state_safety_cap"] += 1
            break

        (
            marking,
            marking_key,
            trace,
            inspections,
            silent_history,
            silent_count,
        ) = work.popleft()

        state = (
            marking_key,
            trace,
            inspections,
        )
        if state in seen_states:
            continue
        seen_states.add(state)
        explored_states += 1

        # Acceptance is based only on exact final-marking equality.
        # Expand final states too; a sound model should not have
        # unintended post-completion behavior.
        if marking_key in final_keys:
            completed.add(trace)

        try:
            enabled = sorted(_enabled(net, marking), key=repr)
        except Exception as exc:
            analysis_error = (
                f"Unable to enumerate transitions at {marking_key!r}: "
                f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"
            )
            cuts["enabled_transition_failure"] += 1
            break

        if not enabled:
            if marking_key not in final_keys:
                unproductive_markings.add(marking_key)
            continue

        for transition in enabled:
            try:
                if transition not in net.transitions:
                    raise KeyError(
                        f"Enabled transition is not in net.transitions: "
                        f"{transition!r}"
                    )

                label, transition_id = _transition_info(net, transition)
                next_visible_count = len(trace) + (1 if label is not None else 0)
                next_inspections = inspections + (
                    1 if label == "Inspect Trial" else 0
                )

                if next_visible_count > max_visible:
                    cuts["visible_length_bound"] += 1
                    continue

                if next_inspections > max_iterations:
                    cuts["inspection_iteration_bound"] += 1
                    continue

                if label is None:
                    if silent_count + 1 > max_silent_between_visible:
                        cuts["silent_length_bound"] += 1
                        continue

                # execute_tpn mutates its marking argument.
                next_marking = Marking(marking)
                execute_tpn(net, next_marking, transition)

                if any(tokens < 0 for tokens in next_marking.values()):
                    raise ValueError(
                        f"Negative token count after {transition_id!r}: "
                        f"{next_marking!r}"
                    )

                next_key = _marking_key(next_marking)

                if label is None:
                    if next_key in silent_history:
                        cuts["silent_marking_repetition"] += 1
                        continue
                    next_history = silent_history | {next_key}
                    next_silent_count = silent_count + 1
                else:
                    # A repeated marking across a visible event may be a
                    # legitimate iteration, so reset silent-cycle detection.
                    next_history = frozenset({next_key})
                    next_silent_count = 0

                next_trace = trace + ((label,) if label is not None else ())

                work.append((
                    next_marking,
                    next_key,
                    next_trace,
                    next_inspections,
                    next_history,
                    next_silent_count,
                ))

            except Exception as exc:
                analysis_error = (
                    f"Failure while exploring {transition!r} from "
                    f"{marking_key!r}: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"
                )
                cuts["transition_execution_failure"] += 1

    return {
        "parameters": {
            "max_visible": max_visible,
            "max_iterations": max_iterations,
            "max_silent_between_visible": max_silent_between_visible,
            "max_states": max_states,
        },
        "completed_traces": sorted(completed),
        "completed_trace_count": len(completed),
        "final_markings": sorted(final_keys),
        "unobserved_final_markings": sorted(final_keys - {
            _marking_key(m) for m in finals
        } if False else set()),
        "explored_states": explored_states,
        "truncations": dict(cuts),
        "unproductive_markings": sorted(unproductive_markings),
        "analysis_error": analysis_error,
        "exhaustive_under_bound": (
            analysis_error is None
            and not cuts
            and len(seen_states) < max_states
        ),
        "scope": (
            "Exact final-marking acceptance within the stated bounds. "

def _play_trace(net, im, finals, trace):
    try:
        result = pm4py.play_trace(
            list(trace),
            net,
            Marking(im),
            finals,
        )
        accepted = result[0] if isinstance(result, tuple) else result

        if accepted is True:
            status = "accepted"
        elif accepted is False:
            status = "rejected"
        else:
            status = "ambiguous"

        return {
            "status": status,
            "error": None,
            "result": repr(result),
        }
    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "status": "not tested",
            "error": {
                "type": type(exc).__name__,
                "message": str(exc),
            },
        }


def _model_playout_worker(sender, net, im, finals):
    try:
        generated = pm4py.simulate(net, Marking(im), finals)
        if generated is None:
            raise TypeError("simulate returned None")

        sample = next(iter(generated))
        if (
            not isinstance(sample, (tuple, list))
            or not sample
            or not isinstance(sample[0], (tuple, list))
        ):
            raise TypeError(f"Unexpected simulation result: {sample!r}")

        trace = list(sample[0])
        if any(label is not None and not isinstance(label, str)
               for label in trace):
            raise TypeError(f"Unexpected labels in simulation: {trace!r}")

        visible = [label for label in trace if label is not None]
        inspections = visible.count("Inspect Trial")

        sender.send({
            "status": "not tested",
            "error": None,
            "sample_trace": trace,
            "visible_length": len(visible),
            "inspection_count": inspections,
            "interpretation": (
                "A single simulated sample is an existence witness, not an "
                "equivalence or exhaustiveness test."
            ),
        })

    except Exception as exc:
        try:
            sender.send({
                "status": "not tested",
                "error": {
                    "type": type(exc).__name__,
                    "message": str(exc),
                },
            })
        except Exception:
            # The parent may already have terminated a timed-out worker.
            pass
    finally:
        sender.close()


def _model_playout(net, im, finals, timeout_seconds=10):
    """
    Run one sample in another process because this net can loop forever.
    A timeout is reported, not treated as a semantic test result.
    """
    try:
        context = mp.get_context("spawn")
        receiver, sender = context.Pipe(duplex=False)
        process = context.Process(
            target=_model_playout_worker,
            args=(sender, net, im, finals),
        )
        process.start()
        sender.close()

        if receiver.poll(timeout_seconds):
            result = receiver.recv()
        else:
            result = {
                "status": "not tested",
                "error": {"type": "Timeout", "message": "Simulation timeout"},
            }

        process.join(timeout=1)
        if process.is_alive():
            process.terminate()
            process.join(timeout=1)

        return result

    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "status": "not tested",
            "error": {
                "type": type(exc).__name__,
                "message": str(exc),
            },
        }
    finally:
        try:
            receiver.close()
        except Exception:
            pass


def _initial_final_observations(net, im, finals):
    initial_enabled = _enabled(net, im)
    initial_info = [_transition_info(net, t) for t in initial_enabled]

        "im": _marking_key(im),
        "im_is_final": _marking_key(im) in {_marking_key(f) for f in finals},
        "enabled_at_im": [
            {"transition": str(t), "label": _transition_info(net, t)[0]}
            for t in initial_enabled
        ],
        "finals": final_observations,
        "note": (

            "final token must be Archive. Some correct net encodings use a "

    }

def validate_round_trips(
    artifacts,
    max_visible=20,
    max_iterations=4,
):
    model_reports = []

    for record in artifacts["accepting_nets"]:
        net = record["net"]
        im = record["im"]
        finals = record["fm"]

        transition_info = {}
        visible_labels = []
        for transition in net.transitions:
            label, transition_id = _transition_info(net, transition)
            transition_info[str(transition_id)] = label
            if label is not None:
                visible_labels.append(label)

        label_counts = Counter(visible_labels)
        duplicate_labels = {
            label: count
            for label, count in label_counts.items()
            if count > 1
        }

        workflow = _diagnostic_call(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net, net=net)
        soundness = _diagnostic_call(
            pm4py.check_soundness,
            net=net,
            im=im,
            fm=finals,
            print_diagnostics=True,
        )
        footprints = _diagnostic_call(
            pm4py.discover_footprints,
            net=net,
            im=im,
            fm=finals,
        )
        reachability = _diagnostic_call(
            pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph,
            net=net,
            im=im,
            fm=finals,
        )

        try:
            initial_final = _initial_final_observations(net, im, finals)
        except Exception as exc:
            initial_final = {
                "error": {
                    "type": type(exc).__name__,
                    "message": str(exc),
                }
            }

        if duplicate_labels:
            bounded = {
                "status": "not tested",
                "error": {
                    "type": "DuplicateVisibleLabels",
                    "message": (
                        "Visible-label witness comparison cannot identify "
                        "activities reliably."
                    ),
                    "duplicates": duplicate_labels,
                },
            }
        else:
            try:
                bounded = _bounded_completed_executions(
                    net,
                    im,
                    finals,
                    max_visible=max_visible,
                    max_iterations=max_iterations,
                )
                bounded["status"] = "ambiguous"
            except Exception as exc:
                bounded = {
                    "status": "not tested",
                    "error": {
                        "type": type(exc).__name__,
                        "message": str(exc),
                    },
                }

        positive_replay = [
            {
                "name": name,
                "result": _play_trace(net, im, finals, trace),
            }
            for name, trace in POSITIVE_WITNESSES
        ]
        negative_replay = [
            {
                "name": name,
                "result": _play_trace(net, im, finals, trace),
            }
            for name, trace in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES
        ]

        model_reports.append({
            "name": record["name"],
            "transition_labels": transition_info,
            "duplicate_visible_labels": duplicate_labels,
            "workflow_check": workflow,
            "soundness_check": soundness,
            "initial_final_observations": initial_final,
            "footprints": footprints,
            "reachability_graph": reachability,
            "bounded": bounded,
            "positive_trace_playout": positive_replay,
            "negative_trace_playout": negative_replay,
            "model_playout": _model_playout(net, im, finals),
        })

    comparisons = []
    source_report = model_reports[0]
    source_bounded = source_report["bounded"]
    source_complete = source_bounded.get("exhaustive_under_bound") is True

    for target_report in model_reports[1:]:
        target_bounded = target_report["bounded"]
        target_complete = target_bounded.get("exhaustive_under_bound") is True

        if source_complete and target_complete:
            source_traces = set(source_bounded["completed_traces"])
            target_traces = set(target_bounded["completed_traces"])

            missing = sorted(source_traces - target_traces)
            extra = sorted(target_traces - source_traces)

            verdict = (
                "validated preserved"
                if not missing and not extra
                else "lost"
            )

            comparison = {
                "source": source_report["name"],
                "target": target_report["name"],
                "verdict": verdict,
                "missing_completed_traces": missing,
                "unexpected_completed_traces": extra,
                "scope": (
                    "Equivalence of admitted completed visible traces under "
                    "the explicitly stated exploration bounds."
                ),
            }
        else:
            comparison = {
                "source": source_report["name"],
                "target": target_report["name"],
                "verdict": "ambiguous",
                "missing_completed_traces": not tested,
                "unexpected_completed_traces": not tested,
                "scope": (
                    "The comparison was not exhaustive under its bound. "
                    "A subset agreement is not equivalence."
                ),
            }

        comparisons.append(comparison)

    return {
        "bounds": {
            "max_visible": max_visible,
            "max_iterations": max_iterations,
        },
        "models": model_reports,
        "comparisons": comparisons,
        "note": (
            "A reachability graph and soundness result are additional "
            "diagnostics. They do not turn an incomplete trace comparison "
            "into a complete source-to-derivative proof."
        ),
    }
```

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

Even a behaviorally acceptable net can produce a misleading BPMN rendering.

### Parallel gateways

A correct interpretation of registration requires a fork permitting assessment and evidence, followed by the appropriate synchronization before verification.

Readers may mistakenly interpret a pair of parallel gateways as unrelated routing. They may also mistake the vertical placement of one branch for an edge that does not exist. The join must synchronize the actual predecessors, not merely split the drawing into balanced columns.

### Exclusive gateways

The optional-legal XOR should communicate:

- exactly one branch is chosen;
- one branch contains visible legal review;
- the other contains an invisible no-op route.

An empty branch can be misread as a mandatory activity with an empty name. It must not be relabeled as a visible “Skip Legal” task: that changes the observable activity language.

Conversely, an exclusive gateway alone is not proof of correctness. Multiple outgoing branches and incoming branches must be checked for their actual join semantics.

### Loop visualization

A BPMN rendering may express the loop using gateways, silent routing, or an unrolled visual fragment. The reader needs to know:

- a trial is mandatory before loop exit;
- configure precedes inspect;
- correction lies between repeated trials;
- correction is not required after the final trial;
- the source loop is a two-child loop, not an arbitrary generalized loop.

A loop drawn around a group of activities does not by itself establish which repeat controls are silent or visible.

### Silent routing nodes

Silent transitions can become blank or visually insignificant nodes. Users may then:

- miss a synchronization;
- mistake routing for an activity;
- infer an extra visible step;
- miss an incorrect XOR or join.

Annotations should describe silent nodes as **non-executable routing**, not rename them as activities.

### Graph layout

Left-to-right placement is a reading aid, not a partial order. In particular:

- legal left of verification does not establish legal-before-verification;
- evidence above assessment does not establish exclusivity;
- a visually neat single path does not establish a required join;
- a backward edge in a loop drawing does not mean configure and inspect were reordered.

### Recommended documentation

Every generated BPMN should carry or accompany:

1. a source-version identifier and converter/version information;
2. a statement that `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` are unordered;
3. a statement that optional legal and verification are not ordered;
4. a statement that verification has a mandatory first trial;
5. a statement of the exact loop splice and placement of `Correct Defect`;
6. initial/final-marking semantics;
7. the invariant/witness manifest;
8. the validation bounds and whether exploration was exhaustive;
9. a note that source POOLs, not BPMN layout, define ordering.

Where supported, gateway annotations or documentation can label a routing branch as “silent XOR route.” They should not create a new executable activity.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

A result should be rejected or quarantined under at least the following conditions:

1. **Required conversion failure.** A required net, BPMN, initial-marking, or final-marking result is missing, null, malformed, or raises an exception.
2. **Failed source-witness acceptance.** A validated source execution is rejected.
3. **Accepted counter-witness.** A source-forbidden execution is admitted, including release before verification, a skipped mandatory trial, or a reversed configure/inspect order.
4. **Lost optionality.** Legal review becomes mandatory, disappears as an allowed visible choice, can occur more than once, or the silent branch is turned into a visible activity.
5. **Lost synchronization.** Release can occur before either risk or evidence is complete, before inspection is complete, or before the legal XOR has resolved.
6. **Lost independence or added ordering.** The conversion imposes an additional mandatory legal/verification or assessment/evidence order not present in the source.
7. **Wrong loop semantics.** Verification can complete with no trial; correction is removed between repetitions; configure/inspect are reversed; or correction becomes an unconditional activity after the last iteration.
8. **Failed exact-final-marking test.** A supposedly completed execution is accepted at a different marking, or a purported final state is not among the declared accepting markings.
9. **Undischarged workflow/soundness failure.** `check_soundness` returns false, or a workflow-net check fails without a documented, independently discharged multiple-final-state explanation. A structural workflow-net failure is not automatically a behavioral failure, but it must not be ignored.
10. **Unresolved finality.** Archive can be omitted from a supposedly completed source execution, or visible activity can follow archive.
11. **Activity identity failure.** Activities are omitted, duplicated, or conflated; duplicate visible labels make witness validation ambiguous; or a generated activity identifier is assumed to prove source identity without a mapping.
12. **Unvalidated tree repair.** A process-tree conversion fails, but a guessed, reordered, or tau-rewritten tree is substituted to make the pipeline appear successful.
13. **Incomplete evidence treated as proof.** A bounded comparison encountered a cut, safety cap, diagnostic exception, or missing final marking, yet the result is reported as fully equivalent.
14. **Unvalidated deployment diagram.** A BPMN image is released as the authoritative executable specification without an identified mapping to the validated model.

A **process-tree conversion failure alone does not reject Pipeline A**. It rejects or quarantines Pipeline B as a complete conversion route.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

### Authoritative storage model

Use the **version-controlled source POWL** as the authoritative editable model.

The accompanying semantic manifest should explicitly state:

- source ordering and absence of ordering;
- optional legal behavior;
- binary-loop and mandatory-first-trial semantics;
- correction placement;
- release synchronization;
- unique activity labels and execution-count expectations;
- required positive and negative witnesses;
- initial/final expectations.

Store the source definition and its version. Do not assume an unlisted PM4Py POWL serialization function exists.

### Stakeholder visualization

Use **BPMN as a separate, derivative stakeholder-facing view**, preferably generated through Pipeline A and accompanied by its provenance and validation evidence.

The following are derivative artifacts:

- the generated BPMN;
- its round-tripped net;
- the process tree, if one exists;
- all PNG/PDF renderings.

They must not be independently edited and then treated as the new source without a deliberate semantic-change review.

### Deployment artifact

A validated accepting Petri net can serve as an executable deployment artifact. It remains a compilation of the source POWL, not an independently authoritative second model.

For each derivative, retain:

- source revision and source-content hash;
- PM4Py version and conversion settings;
- all initial and final markings;
- transition names, labels, and silent-transition identities;
- workflow and soundness diagnostics;
- witness results;
- exact-final-marking results;
- exploration bounds and whether the comparison was exhaustive;
- BPMN round-trip status;
- the generated artifact hash.

### Process-tree intermediates

A process-tree intermediate should be allowed only as:

- an auxiliary diagnostic;
- a readable explanation for a behaviorally valid structure;
- an optional derivative, not the storage authority.

A tree returned by PM4Py must not be accepted solely because conversion returned it. In particular, a repeat node must not silently turn a mandatory first trial into an optional one.

Future sources that are not block-structured should not be forced into a tree by adding edges or converting tau into tasks. A manually designed block-structured transformation would require its own semantic proof and versioned mapping; the provided PM4Py net-to-tree API does not supply that guarantee.

### Avoiding round-trip drift

Future edits should:

1. modify the source POWL;
2. regenerate all downstream artifacts;
3. rerun all witnesses and exact-final tests;
4. compare generated models, not the previous BPMN against itself;
5. preserve stable source activity identities and silent-control mappings;
6. retain the loop operator rather than flattening an unbounded loop;
7. never use BPMN re-import as a silent “repair”;
8. treat layout changes and semantic changes as separate changes.

Do not “fix” a failed tree conversion by adding an order edge that the source never had.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if all executed checks pass, the following claims cannot be established by this procedure:

1. **Unbounded semantic equivalence.** A finite witness suite and even a bounded exhaustive exploration do not establish equivalence for all iteration counts unless a stronger formal argument or complete state-space equivalence method is supplied.
2. **Absence of arbitrary silent-only cycling.** The explorer deliberately prunes silent cycles. The source’s visible completions can still be checked, but the code does not claim that every intermediate hidden execution is covered.
3. **That a tree-conversion exception proves non-block-structuredness.** The exception may instead reflect a library limitation, unsupported construct, implementation restriction, or another cause.
4. **That a rendered BPMN is executable with the same routing semantics in every BPMN engine.** Validation here checks PM4Py’s net representation, not every downstream engine’s handling of gateways, silent routing, multiple incoming flows, or completion conditions.
5. **That unique labels prove unique source identities.** Autogenerated transition names and matching labels do not establish provenance. A stronger identifier mapping would be required.
6. **That the net-to-tree implementation is correct for this source.** Successful calls demonstrate that the API returned objects, not that its block decomposition preserved all control structure.
7. **That a BPMN image visually expresses the semantics unambiguously.** The image can omit, hide, or misleadingly place routing information even when the underlying model is acceptable.
8. **That initial and final equivalence follows from endpoint appearance.** A visible start/end event may be a renderer convention. The actual initial and accepting markings are the evidence.
9. **That every preserved behavior is relevant to stakeholders.** Semantic preservation does not establish that a diagram communicates concurrency, optionality, or loop cardinality well.
10. **That model playout establishes completeness or fairness.** A finite sample says only that some execution was produced; it says nothing about all executions or fairness.
11. **That future edits preserve prior semantics.** New downstream artifacts and changed source behavior require a new validation record; a previous successful round trip is not a guarantee for later edits.

The defensible conclusion is therefore evidence-based: use the primary net-based pipeline, preserve the source POWL as authority, require witness and exact-final-marking evidence, and treat the BPMN and process tree as validated derivatives rather than as proof by successful serialization.
