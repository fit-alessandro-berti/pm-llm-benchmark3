## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

Let \(C\) be the calendar timestamp of the usable chemistry result and \(M\) the timestamp of the usable microbiology result. The parallel join is

\[
J=\max(C,M),
\]

not \(C+M\). QA waiting and service begin only at \(J\).

As a scheduling shortcut, the chemistry triangular distribution has a workload average of about 2.8 hours, but this is used only to approximate queue clearance. The forecast retains the minimum/maximum and calendar effects rather than adding unconditional means.

**Smooth path**

- **Chemistry waiting and service:** The two active analysts become free between approximately 15:54–16:24 and 16:42–17:30. Friday has five analyst-hours of capacity left, of which 1.6–2.9 hours are already committed to active work. Thus only about 2.1–3.4 analyst-hours remain for the queue.
  - Under risk priority, the first analyst takes the urgent sample and the next available analyst can take B-417 at roughly 16:42–17:30. B-417 receives only 0.5–1.3 hours before closure, below its 1.5-hour minimum total service, and resumes Monday. A usable result is then plausible around Monday 08:10–12:00.
  - Under FIFO, the four samples ahead consume most of Monday morning. A central B-417 start is around Monday 11:00–15:00, with completion around 13:00–18:00. A long late-starting test can cross 18:00 and finish Tuesday around 08:00–11:00.
  - Queue waiting and service are therefore distinct: much of the elapsed delay is the 62-hour Friday-18:00-to-Monday-08:00 laboratory closure, not active testing.
- **Microbiology incubation and readout:** Technical readiness occurs Sunday 20 September between 12:00 and 20:00.
  - With weekend authorization, probability 0.25–0.45, the recorded result is approximately Sunday 13:00–23:00.
  - Without authorization, probability 0.55–0.75, the recorded result is Monday 08:30–09:30.
- **Parallel join:** Under risk priority, \(J=\max(\text{chemistry Monday 08:10–12:00},\text{microbiology Monday 08:30–09:30 or Sunday})\), giving roughly Monday 08:30–12:00. Under FIFO, chemistry usually wins the maximum, around Monday afternoon.
- **QA waiting and service:** From Friday 15:30 through Saturday, QA has 8.5 open hours. Five current cases plus approximately three average arrivals compete for about five to six median-sized service slots. This suggests a residual Monday backlog around two to four cases, but overdispersion makes zero to seven plausible. An early-Monday join therefore faces roughly 1–5 hours of waiting centrally; a late-afternoon join may face only 0–3 hours because two reviewers have been clearing the queue. Review service is approximately 1–3.5 hours over the central-to-P80 range. A review unfinished at 20:00 pauses until Tuesday 08:00.
- **Optional rework/investigation:** None on this scenario.
- **Final release or rejection:** With normal results, Electronic Release adds 0.25–0.75 hours and can occur after QA closes if acceptance has already occurred. The smooth terminal event is release, mainly Monday afternoon/evening, with a Tuesday-morning calendar-spill tail.

**Chemistry rerun**

- **Chemistry waiting and service:** Initial waiting and testing follow the smooth chemistry cases. Once the borderline result is observed, the priority rerun takes 1–3 hours. If the initial result is available Monday morning or early afternoon, the rerun can finish Monday. If the initial result appears around 16:00–18:00, the rerun is either paused at 18:00 or starts Tuesday 08:00. A usable chemistry result therefore ranges from about Monday 09:10 at the optimistic extreme to Tuesday 11:00 centrally for a late FIFO case.
- **Microbiology incubation and readout:** Assume a normal primary result. It is usable Sunday with call-out or Monday 08:30–09:30 without call-out.
- **Parallel join:** \(J=\max(C_{\text{after rerun}},M)\). Chemistry almost always wins this maximum because the rerun completes after routine microbiology readout.
- **QA waiting and service:** A later join may encounter less residual backlog, but a result after 18:00 can leave too little QA time before the 20:00 close. Central QA waiting plus service is approximately 2–6 working hours, with a 6–12-hour congestion tail.
- **Optional rework/investigation:** The rerun is itself the branch rework. It also moves deviation-investigation probability from the normal 4–10% band to 30–55%. A documentation-only investigation adds 8–20 QA working hours; a laboratory investigation creates the multi-day tail.
- **Final release or rejection:** A resolved rerun normally leads to release after QA plus 0.25–0.75 hours. A rerun that confirms a critical defect leads instead to rejection recording in 0.5–1.0 hour; the probability of that critical outcome is not supplied.

**Delayed microbiology or contamination-confirmation path**

- **Chemistry waiting and service:** Chemistry follows the smooth path and is normally complete by Monday afternoon, with a smaller Tuesday-morning FIFO tail.
- **Microbiology incubation and readout:**
  - With weekend call-out, the primary indication is observed Sunday 13:00–23:00. Adding 20–30 continuous hours gives confirmation readiness from Monday morning to early Tuesday. Staffed confirmation readout gives a usable result roughly Monday 09:30–Tuesday 09:30.
  - Without call-out, the primary result is read Monday 08:30–09:30. Confirmation becomes technically ready Tuesday 04:30–15:30. Results ready before 08:00 wait for staff; the resulting usable confirmation is approximately Tuesday 08:30–17:00.
- **Parallel join:** \(J=\max(C,M_{\text{confirmation}})\). Here microbiology normally wins; chemistry and microbiology must not be added.
- **QA waiting and service:** A Tuesday join likely finds that the initial QA backlog has cleared, but contemporaneous arrivals and overdispersed long reviews still imply about 0–4 hours of central waiting. A confirmation arriving near 17:00 can push review across the 20:00 close and into Wednesday morning.
- **Optional rework/investigation:** An indication raises investigation probability to 30–55%. The excursion flag makes a joint chemistry rerun plus microbiology confirmation more plausible than independence would suggest.
- **Final release or rejection:** If confirmation clears the indication, the batch returns to the release route. If it confirms a critical defect, rejection can be recorded 0.5–1.0 hour after confirmation, potentially earlier than a release that requires QA review.

**Documentation Query or documentation-only-investigation path**

- **Chemistry waiting and service:** Usually the smooth Monday result; a rerun or confirmation can coexist with this scenario and shift its starting point to Tuesday.
- **Microbiology incubation and readout:** Usually the Monday primary result, unless this path overlaps the confirmation scenario.
- **Parallel join:** The join remains the maximum of branch completion times, generally Monday morning to late afternoon.
- **QA waiting and service:** QA first waits for a reviewer and performs enough review to raise the query or investigation. The documentation-completeness score of 0.82 supports the stated 15–30% query probability rather than treating the file as either complete or defective with certainty.
- **Optional rework/investigation:**
  - A Documentation Query adds 4–12 QA working hours. For example, a query raised Monday at 19:00 gets one working hour Monday, then resumes its clock Tuesday at 08:00.
  - A documentation-only investigation adds 8–20 working hours. If opened Monday afternoon, this usually finishes Tuesday or Wednesday. If opened after a Tuesday confirmation, the upper end can reach Thursday.
  - Allow another 0–3 working hours for re-entry and residual review. Query and investigation may both occur, so their tail effects can be sequential.
- **Final release or rejection:** After QA acceptance, release takes 0.25–0.75 hours continuously. This path is most commonly a Tuesday or Wednesday release.

**Laboratory-investigation path**

- **Chemistry waiting and service:** The initiating test results are typically available Monday or Tuesday. A rerun may be the trigger.
- **Microbiology incubation and readout:** A contamination confirmation or confirmed excursion can be the trigger; microbiology may therefore set a Tuesday join.
- **Parallel join:** The initial QA review still starts only after \(J=\max(C,M)\).
- **QA waiting and service:** QA queueing and initial review add several working hours before the deviation is opened.
- **Optional rework/investigation:** The laboratory investigation adds 3–7 calendar days. Unlike documentation work, its clock continues overnight and over the weekend. However, QA resumption remains calendar-constrained: a result returned Saturday after 13:00 or on Sunday generally waits until Monday 08:00. An investigation opened Monday can return Thursday through the following Monday; one opened after Tuesday/Wednesday confirmation can return Friday through the following Wednesday.
- **Final release or rejection:** If the investigation clears the batch, QA resumes and Electronic Release follows. If it confirms a critical defect, rejection recording takes 0.5–1.0 hour. This path generates the late-September/early-October severe tail.

The excursion dependence can be expressed as a latent mixture:

\[
P(R\cap C)=\sum_e P(e)\,P(R\cap C\mid e),
\]

where \(R\) is chemistry rerun and \(C\) is contamination indication. Even if they were conditionally independent within each excursion state, increasing both probabilities in the confirmed-excursion state would make them positively dependent marginally.

## 4. Forecast summary

These are forecast-location bands rather than fitted statistical quantiles.

- **Plausible earliest completion:** approximately **65.5–67 hours remaining**, corresponding to **Monday 21 September, 09:00–10:30**. This requires favorable priority treatment, partial Friday chemistry processing, a normal microbiology result, little or no Monday QA backlog, and short QA service.
- **Central/P50-like remaining-time range:** approximately **72–90 hours**, corresponding to **Monday 21 September, 15:30 through Tuesday 22 September, 09:30**. The lower part represents Monday chemistry and same-day QA; the upper part captures late FIFO chemistry or QA work crossing the 20:00 close.
- **P80-like remaining-time range:** approximately **90–122 hours**, corresponding to **Tuesday 22 September, 09:30 through Wednesday 23 September, 17:30**. This range is driven mainly by a query, documentation-only investigation, contamination confirmation, or a rerun plus QA congestion.
- **P90-like or severe-tail range:** under lower laboratory-investigation assumptions, approximately **115–141 hours**, or **Wednesday 23 September, 10:30 through Thursday 24 September, 12:30**. If laboratory investigations occupy roughly the upper decile, the severe tail expands to about **140–307 hours**, or **Thursday 24 September, 11:30 through Thursday 1 October, 10:30**.
- **Most plausible terminal event(s):** **Electronic Release** is clearly the most plausible terminal event, most likely Monday evening or Tuesday morning. **Batch Rejected** is a lower-probability alternative following a confirmed critical chemistry or microbiology defect. Its probability cannot be defensibly quantified because the chance that an indication ultimately confirms as critical is not supplied.

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

The most forecast-changing observation is **resolution of the upstream temperature-excursion flag**.

- **Flag cleared:** Shift rerun and contamination risks toward their lower bands, restore the 4–10% normal-result investigation band, reduce positive dependence between branch anomalies, and concentrate the forecast around Monday evening to Tuesday morning.
- **Flag confirmed:** Raise both branch-rework risks together, use the 30–55% investigation band even if the eventual test values are otherwise acceptable, increase the chance that both branches are delayed, and move substantial probability from the central range into Wednesday or the multi-day investigation tail.
- **Flag remains unresolved:** Retain the broad latent-mixture forecast; apparent normal progress in one branch would not fully eliminate the correlated tail.

A secondary update trigger is the **primary microbiology readout**: a normal result removes the 20–30-hour confirmation clock, while a contamination indication immediately shifts the likely join to Monday/Tuesday confirmation and raises investigation and rejection risk.
