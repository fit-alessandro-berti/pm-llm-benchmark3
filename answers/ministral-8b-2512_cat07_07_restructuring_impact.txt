# 4. Risks Invisible in Average Cycle Time

1. **Tail Latency Explosion**:
   - Average cycle time may improve, but **99th percentile waits could double** (e.g., East’s tail latency from 22.8d to 45d).
   - *PM4Py check*: `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg()` with `percentile=99`.

2. **High-Complexity Case Failures**:
   - Cases with `local_expertise_flag=1` may see **rework rates jump from 8% to 20%** even if average rework stays flat.
   - *PM4Py check*: `pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(df, "local_expertise_flag", [1])` + `pm4py.get_rework_cases_per_activity()`.

3. **Local Knowledge Erosion**:
   - Local caseworkers may **stop developing technical skills**, leading to **long-term rework spikes** in complex cases.
   - *PM4Py check*: Compare `required_specialty` trends before/after restructuring.

4. **Fairness Erosion**:
   - East may see **faster average processing**, but **higher variance** (e.g., 90th percentile waits  from 18.2d to 25d).
   - *PM4Py check*: `pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe()` by `team`.

5. **Accountability Ambiguity**:
   - Shared queues may **reduce individual accountability**, leading to **more errors in high-stakes cases**.
   - *PM4Py check*: Analyze `outcome` attribute for "failed" cases routed through shared queues.

6. **Learning Loss**:
   - Local teams may **lose institutional knowledge**, causing **chronic rework in edge cases**.
   - *PM4Py check*: Compare `handover_reason` for "missing local context" before/after.

7. **Exception Handling Collapse**:
   - Unusual cases (e.g., `priority=high`) may **face even longer waits** in shared queues.
   - *PM4Py check*: `pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(df, "priority", ["high"])` + `pm4py.get_service_time()`.

# 5. Pilot Design

## **Pilot Scope**
- **Teams**: North (high local expertise) + East (high specialist utilization).
- **Process Segments**:
  - **High-complexity cases** (`case_complexity=high`).
  - **Legal/Risk cases** (`required_specialty in ["legal", "risk"]`).
  - **Cross-team handovers** (`team` changes between events).
- **Time Window**: 8 weeks pre-pilot, 12 weeks post-pilot.

## **Comparison Group**
- **South team** (similar to North but with lower satisfaction).
- **Control group**: Cases routed to **local specialists only** (no shared service).

## **Pre/Post Observation Metrics**
| **Metric**                          | **Pre-Pilot**                          | **Post-Pilot**                          |
|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Handover frequency by type          | `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network()` | Same                                  |
| Median wait time by handover        | `pm4py.get_service_time()`             | Same                                  |
| Rework rate by case complexity      | `pm4py.get_rework_cases_per_activity()` | Same                                  |
| Specialist utilization             | `pm4py.discover_organizational_roles()` | Same                                  |
| Cycle-time distribution             | `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg()`     | Same                                  |
| Customer satisfaction               | Survey (external)                      | Survey (external)                      |
| Cross-team handover frequency       | `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network()` | Same                                  |

## **Segmentation**
- **By Team**: North vs. East vs. South.
- **By Complexity**: `case_complexity` (low/medium/high).
- **By Specialty**: `required_specialty` (none/legal/risk/technical).
- **By Customer Segment**: `customer_segment` (standard/premium/regional).

## **Guardrail Metrics**
- **Stop if**:
  - Rework rate in `local_expertise_flag=1` cases ** >10%**.
  - Specialist queue wait time **>12h for 20% of cases**.
  - Cross-team handovers ** >30%**.
  - East’s 99th percentile cycle time ** >20%**.
- **Pause if**:
  - North/South customer satisfaction ** >0.3 points**.
  - Shared approval queue SLA miss rate ** >5%**.

## **Staff Feedback Collection**
- **Week 4**: Quick pulse survey on **queue wait perceptions**.
- **Week 8**: In-depth interviews on **role clarity and accountability**.
- **Week 12**: Exit survey on **learning and fairness perceptions**.

## **Rollback/Adjustment Triggers**
- **Rollback if**:
  - Adverse scenario metrics met (e.g., rework 15%, tail latency 30%).
  - Staff feedback shows **severe demotivation** or **loss of local expertise**.
- **Adjust if**:
  - Early warning signs appear (e.g., rework 8%, queue waits 4h).
  - **Hybrid model**: Keep some local specialists for complex cases.

# 6. Recommendation

**Conditional Recommendation**: **Pilot the restructuring in North and East teams for 12 weeks**, with **automatic rollback triggers** if:
1. Rework in `local_expertise_flag=1` cases exceeds **12%**.
2. Specialist queue wait times exceed **12 hours for >20% of cases**.
3. Cross-team handovers increase by **>30%**.
4. East’s 99th percentile cycle time increases by **>20%**.
5. North/South customer satisfaction drops by **>0.3 points**.

**Do Not Proceed Full-Scale Without**:
- **Clear evidence that shared queues reduce rework in non-complex cases** (not just average cycle time).
- **No degradation in local expertise** (measured via `local_expertise_flag` rework rates).
- **Fairness across teams** (no widening of cycle-time inequality).
- **Staff buy-in** (role clarity and accountability must improve).

**Management Should Not Infer**:
- That **average cycle time improvements** justify the restructuring without analyzing **tail latency, rework, and fairness**.
- That **specialist utilization gains** (15%) are sustainable if they come at the cost of **local knowledge erosion**.
- That **East’s benefits** (reduced overload) outweigh **North/South’s risks** (lost local expertise) without **segmented analysis**.

**Alternative Path**:
If pilot results show **severe risks**, consider a **hybrid model**:
- Keep **local specialists for high-complexity cases** (`local_expertise_flag=1`).
- Use **shared services only for standard cases**.
- Implement **local "supervisors" for complex cases** to retain accountability.

# 7. PM4Py Analysis Sketch

```python
import pm4py
import pandas as pd

# Load event log
df = pd.read_csv("event_log.csv")

# --- Handover-of-Work Network Analysis ---
def analyze_handover_network(df, before_after="before"):
    # Filter by time period (e.g., pre/post pilot)
    if before_after == "after":
        df = df[df["time:timestamp"] > "2024-05-01"]  # Example cutoff

    # Discover handover network
    handover_network = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(df, attributes=["org:resource", "team"])

    # Extract handover frequencies and waits
    handover_freq = pm4py.get_handover_frequency(handover_network)
    handover_waits = pm4py.get_handover_wait_times(handover_network, df)

    # Filter by handover type (e.g., local caseworker  specialist)
    local_to_specialist = handover_freq[
        (handover_freq["source"] == "local caseworker") &
        (handover_freq["target"].str.contains("specialist"))
    ]

    return handover_freq, handover_waits, local_to_specialist

# --- Working-Together Network (Specialist Utilization) ---
def analyze_specialist_utilization(df, before_after="before"):
    if before_after == "after":
        df = df[df["time:timestamp"] > "2024-05-01"]

    working_network = pm4py.discover_working_together_network(df, attributes=["org:resource"])
    specialist_util = pm4py.get_resource_utilization(working_network, df)

    # Filter for technical specialists
    tech_specialists = specialist_util[specialist_util["resource"].str.contains("Tech")]
    return tech_specialists

# --- Rework Analysis by Case Complexity ---
def analyze_rework_by_complexity(df, before_after="before"):
    if before_after == "after":
        df = df[df["time:timestamp"] > "2024-05-01"]

    # Filter rework cases
    rework_cases = df[df["rework_flag"] == 1]

    # Group by complexity and handover type
    rework_by_complexity = rework_cases.groupby(["case:concept:name", "case_complexity", "handover_reason"]).size().reset_index(name="count")

    # Compare with non-rework cases
    non_rework_cases = df[df["rework_flag"] == 0]
    non_rework_by_complexity = non_rework_cases.groupby(["case:concept:name", "case_complexity", "handover_reason"]).size().reset_index(name="count")

    return rework_by_complexity, non_rework_by_complexity

# --- Cycle-Time Distribution by Team ---
def analyze_cycle_time_by_team(df, before_after="before"):
    if before_after == "after":
        df = df[df["time:timestamp"] > "2024-05-01"]

    # Insert case service times
    df_with_times = pm4py.insert_case_service_time(df)

    # Discover performance DFG
    dfg = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(df_with_times)

    # Extract cycle times by team
    cycle_times = pm4py.get_service_time(df_with_times, attributes=["team"])

    # Calculate percentiles by team
    team_percentiles = cycle_times.groupby("team").quantile([0.5, 0.9, 0.99])

    return team_percentiles

# --- Fairness Analysis (Queue Waits by Team) ---
def analyze_fairness_by_team(df, before_after="after"):
    if before_after == "after":
        df = df[df["time:timestamp"] > "2024-05-01"]

    # Insert waiting times
    df_with_waits = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(df)

    # Filter for shared service waits
    shared_waits = df_with_waits[df_with_waits["service"].str.contains("shared")]

    # Calculate wait time percentiles by team
    team_waits = shared_waits.groupby(["team", "service"]).quantile([0.5, 0.9, 0.99])

    return team_waits

# --- Role Clarity Analysis (Subcontracting Network) ---
def analyze_role_clarity(df, before_after="after"):
    if before_after == "after":
        df = df[df["time:timestamp"] > "2024-05-01"]

    # Discover subcontracting network (shared queues)
    subcontracting_network = pm4py.discover_subcontracting_network(df, attributes=["org:resource"])

    # Identify ambiguous handover paths
    ambiguous_paths = subcontracting_network[
        (subcontracting_network["source"].str.contains("local")) &
        (subcontracting_network["target"].str.contains("shared"))
    ]

    return ambiguous_paths

# --- Example Usage ---
if __name__ == "__main__":
    # Pre-pilot analysis
    handover_before, waits_before, local_to_specialist_before = analyze_handover_network(df, "before")
    specialist_util_before = analyze_specialist_utilization(df, "before")
    rework_before, non_rework_before = analyze_rework_by_complexity(df, "before")
    cycle_times_before = analyze_cycle_time_by_team(df, "before")
    fairness_before = analyze_fairness_by_team(df, "before")
    role_clarity_before = analyze_role_clarity(df, "before")

    # Post-pilot analysis (after cutoff date)
    handover_after, waits_after, local_to_specialist_after = analyze_handover_network(df, "after")
    specialist_util_after = analyze_specialist_utilization(df, "after")
    rework_after, non_rework_after = analyze_rework_by_complexity(df, "after")
    cycle_times_after = analyze_cycle_time_by_team(df, "after")
    fairness_after = analyze_fairness_by_team(df, "after")
    role_clarity_after = analyze_role_clarity(df, "after")

    # Compare key metrics
    print("Local to Specialist Handover Frequency (Before/After):")
    print(local_to_specialist_before, "\n", local_to_specialist_after)

    print("\nSpecialist Utilization (Before/After):")
    print(specialist_util_before, "\n", specialist_util_after)

    print("\nRework Rate by Complexity (Before/After):")
    print(rework_before.groupby("case_complexity").mean(), "\n", rework_after.groupby("case_complexity").mean())

    print("\nCycle-Time Percentiles by Team (Before/After):")
    print(cycle_times_before, "\n", cycle_times_after)

    print("\nFairness: Shared Queue Waits by Team (90th Percentile):")
    print(fairness_before[0.9], "\n", fairness_after[0.9])
```
