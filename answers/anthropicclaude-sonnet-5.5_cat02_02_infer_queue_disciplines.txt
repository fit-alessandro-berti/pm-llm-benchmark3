# 4. Counterfactual discrimination tests

### Test 1: QB at 09:48 (across queues, then QC and QD below)
- **Explanation X:** FIFO + p1 express lane + manual override (the observed stop and start B03 arise from the dispatcher's override).
- **Explanation Y:** preemptive priority with aging, in which B03 (p2, waiting 28 min) was promoted to p1 and beat B05 by FIFO tie-break.
- **Predictions, same situation:** X predicts B05 by rule, with B03 an override. Y predicts B03 by rule. Both predict B03 at 09:48, so the observation does not discriminate.
- **Counterfactual with B03 replaced by a fresh p2 job arriving at 09:45:** X predicts B05. Y predicts B05 (no aging yet). They diverge only if the p2 job has waited more than about 25 min.
- **Discriminating observable:** the effective priority or score of B03 and B05 at the decision time (priority version and aging timestamp), plus the override log entry.

### Test 2: QC at 10:32
- **Explanation X:** SPT with batching (C05 and C06 belong to a later batch that is not yet released). Predicts C04, the observed choice.
- **Explanation Y:** SPT with noisy or strategic estimates, where C06's estimate at dispatch was larger than 22.
- **Predictions:** SPT with current estimates predicts C06. X predicts C04. Y predicts C04 only if the dispatch estimate for C06 exceeded 22.
- **Discriminating observable:** the snapshot of the dispatch list at 10:32, namely queue-entry time of C06 (scan vs physical) and the `est_min` value visible at that moment.
- **Follow-up check:** C06 was chosen at 11:34 over C07 (18), so its estimate must then have been below 18. Y therefore needs estimate revision between 10:32 and 11:34.

### Test 3: QD at 11:36 (D-F)
- **Explanation X:** strict FIFO head-of-line blocking (D05 is head, cannot fit before the meeting, so D-F idles).
- **Explanation Y:** FIFO with backfill (start any job that fits before the meeting).
- **Predictions:** X predicts idle, which was observed. Y predicts D10 at 11:36–11:48 (it fits before 12:00).
- **Discriminating observable:** the dispatch rule version (backfill flag), and whether D-F's recorded state was "available" or "preparing for meeting" between 11:36 and 12:20.

### Test 4: QA at 09:05
- **Explanation X:** FIFO plus appointment at the decision instant, without look-ahead. Predicts A05, observed.
- **Explanation Y:** appointment reservation with look-ahead. Predicts A07 (9 min, finishes 09:14 before 09:15).
- **Discriminating observable:** appointment tolerance and whether the dispatcher checks upcoming appointments when starting a job. The A06 lateness (5 min) is consistent with X and with a tolerance window under Y.

# 7. Final synthesis

- **Most identifiable queue: QA.** It has a single resource, no skill differences, and several decisions where FIFO, priority and SPT give different answers (08:30, 09:05, 09:20, 09:32). It is most consistent with non-preemptive FIFO plus appointment precedence. Priority is rejected there on the evidence of A04 and A08.
- **Least identifiable queue: QC.** No single rule fits. SPT matches only half the decisions, and the C04 choice at 10:32 contradicts every rule tested. The C05 and C01 choices are explained only by post-hoc overrides, and the universal est = realized pattern may mean the estimates were not what the dispatcher saw. QD is also ambiguous, but every QD job start is at least consistent with FIFO plus eligibility, whereas QC has no such baseline.
- **Most dangerous policy to infer from these observations alone: priority scheduling** (especially preemptive priority, read off QB's first preemption and the 10:00 choice). The evidence is an artefact of equivalence: B02 preempting B01 is equally predicted by SRPT, and B05 at 10:00 is also consistent with an express lane. Across queues, p1 jobs (A04, A08, D08, D10, and B05 at 09:48) waited behind lower-priority jobs, and p2 jobs were passed over in three queues. Assuming a working priority rule would therefore misstate service for exactly the urgent jobs. SPT inferred from QC is the runner-up danger: it would starve long jobs such as C01 and leave C07 late, and its support may come from hindsight-filled estimates.
