# 3. Supporting and contradicting cases

### QA

**Support FIFO (+ appointment)**
- A01A03 appointment hold (A03 waits 12min to appt time) supports appointment reservation, not priority/SPT.
- A02 before A04 at 08:32 supports FIFO over priority/SPT (A04 has higher priority and shorter est).
- A05 before A06/A07/A08 at 09:05 supports FIFO over priority (A08 p1) and SPT (A07 9min). Similarly A06 before A08 at 09:20 (appointment due) and A07 before A08 at 09:32.

**Challenge / need exception**
- No direct FIFO violation besides T1, which is *intended* exception (appointment). All other decisions are FIFO-consistent, so no unexplained violation.
- Apparent idle 08:30-08:32 (2min) could be interpreted as violation of work-conserving FIFO, but **explainable by A1 calibration not yet started?** No, calibration 08:52. More plausibly **arrival scan delay (A2)** or setup time not logged. No undocumented exception needed beyond appointment rule.

### QB

**Support preemptive priority**
- B01 preempted at 09:16 for B02 (urgent arrival) - strongest preemptive priority evidence.
- B05 (p1) selected at 10:00 ahead of B01 rem and B04 (both p3) supports priority over FIFO at that instant.
- Second preemption 09:31-09:48 `dispatcher reassignment` indicates operational preemption, not natural completion.

**Challenge**
- **B03 over B05 at 09:48** contradicts strict priority if B05 was scanned by 09:48. Explainable by **A1 scan delay (2min)**: B05 scan 09:46 could be physically 09:48, not in dispatcher's view at 09:48:00. Without delay, requires undocumented exception (e.g., priority aging or SPT tie-break which happens to favor B03 est12 <14). Also contradicts SPT because B04 (8) even shorter not chosen, so SPT cannot rescue.
- **Resume B01 at 09:31 over B03 and at 10:14 over B04/B06** contradicts priority and SPT, but supports FIFO/resumption policy (interrupted job to head). This suggests hybrid: priority only for preemption, not for queue ordering.
- **B04 over B06 at 10:28** (p3 over p2) contradicts priority, supports FIFO (B04 09:35 older than B06 10:05) and contradicts SPT if B07 (6) considered (but B07 appointment 10:30 incomplete, still SPT would pick B07). Requires FIFO secondary.
- **B07 appointment 10:30 not honored (starts 10:56)** contradicts appointment primary; supports FIFO/SPT/priority all predict earlier start if appointment honored, but resource busy until then, so actually no violation - appointment simply not prioritized.

 Diagnosis requires **hybrid preemptive-priority-then-FIFO**, not pure priority.

### QC

**Support SPT**
- C02 (8) over all at 10:10, C03 (14) over C01/C05 at 10:18, C04 (22) over C01/C05 at 10:32 - three consecutive selections exactly sorted by ascending `est_min` among eligible, violating FIFO (oldest C01 never picked), Priority (C05 p1 ignored twice), EDF (earliest deadline C03 vs C05).
- C06 (6) over C01/C07 at 11:34 also SPT.

**Challenge**
- **C05 at 10:54 over C06 (6) and C01 (36)**: SPT predicts C06. Also FIFO predicts C01. Observed C05 matches **Priority p1 and EDF deadline already overdue (10:50)**. Explainable by **SLA-endangered policy switch** (prompt: possibility queue changes policy when SLA becomes endangered). Deadline+priority coincide, indistinguishable.
- **C01 over C07 at 11:40**: SPT predicts C07 (18<36), Priority predicts C07 (p2>p3), EDF predicts C07 (12:00<13:00), FIFO predicts C01. Observed C01 contradicts SPT/Priority/EDF last time, **requires second exception: starvation/aging** (C01 waiting since 10:00, >100min) or **estimate inaccuracy** (C01 estimate inflated). Without aging, SPT primary fails.
- Therefore SPT alone insufficient; needs at least two undocumented exceptions, lowering confidence.

### QD

**Support skill-based routing**
- **D05 73min wait while others served** only explained by skill hard constraint (requires firmware+electrical, only D-F qualified). Same for D07 not eligible for D-M.
- **D-F idle 11:36-12:00 with waiting D10**: If no skill partition, D10 could be served by D-F. Idle indicates look-ahead for D-F meeting, not lack of skill.
- **D-M assignments strictly mechanical-only** (D01,D04,D08,D09) never picks electrical jobs despite earlier arrivals.

**Challenge**
- **FIFO within partition vs Priority**: At 11:25 D-M picks D04 (p3,10) over D08 (p1,8) - contradicts Priority and SPT, supports FIFO within mechanical partition.
- **D07 over D10 at 11:48 on D-X**: D10 p1 12 vs D07 p3 28 - contradicts Priority and SPT, supports FIFO.
- **D06 appointment at 11:30 on D-X over p1 jobs D08/D10**: contradicts Priority, supports Appointment/FIFO tie (oldest eligible).
- **Contradiction to pure FIFO**: D-F not starting D10 at 11:36 violates work-conserving FIFO - explainable by **non-preemptive meeting look-ahead** (won't start job that cannot finish before 12:00) and reservation for D05. Without that exception, skill+FIFO would predict D10 started at 11:36.

 All contradicting cases are explainable by documented skill + meeting constraints, no need to invoke priority/SPT.

# 4. Counterfactual discrimination tests

### Test 1 — QB 09:48 ambiguous (B03 vs B05 vs B04)

**Two strongest competing explanations:**
- H1: **Strict non-preemptive priority** after preemption (queue ordered by priority 1>2>3).
- H2: **FIFO among waiting (with resume-to-head)**.

**Predictions at 09:48 (waiting B01 rem, B03 p2 12, B04 p3 8, B05 p1 14):**
- H1 predicts **B05** (p1, earliest among p1) next, regardless of est or arrival.
- H2 predicts **B03** (oldest arrival 09:20) next, if B05 not yet scanned or if B01 rem considered head but B01 is already preempted dispatcher pulled.
- SPT would predict **B04** (8), which neither H1 nor H2 predicts, providing third contrast.

**Observed:** B03.

**Discrimination:** Observation favors H2 over H1 *if* B05 was eligible; favors H1 if B05 eligibility delayed.

**Additional observable that would discriminate:** `dispatch_decision_time` with millisecond resolution plus `queue_entry_scan_time` vs `true arrival scan latency` and `eligibility_snapshot_at_dispatch`: a log of the exact set the dispatcher saw at 09:48:00. If snapshot shows B05 missing, H2 stands and scan delay explains; if snapshot shows B05 present, H1 falsified and hybrid FIFO confirmed. Also `estimated_remaining_time_at_disruption` for B01 (31 vs 14) would test whether SPT explains resume vs queue.

### Test 2 — QC 10:54 ambiguous (C05 vs C06 vs C01)

**Two strongest competitors:**
- H1: **Pure SPT** (always pick smallest `est_min` among eligible).
- H2: **SPT with EDF/priority expedite when deadline within horizon** (hybrid).

**Predictions at 10:54 (waiting C01 36 dl13:00, C05 40 dl10:50 overdue, C06 6 dl15:00, C07 18 dl12:00):**
- H1 predicts **C06** (6) next.
- H2 predicts **C05** (p1, earliest deadline, SLA endangered) next, despite longest est.

**Observed:** C05. Favors H2.

**Counterfactual:** If next were C06, H1 would be retained, H2 falsified for that threshold.

**Additional observable to discriminate:** `policy_version` or `sla_risk_flag` computed at dispatch (e.g., `deadline - now - est < buffer`), plus `current_estimated_remaining_time` and whether C05's deadline was flagged at risk *before* selection (note says "deadline already at risk when selected" - but we need synchronous log). Also `resource_state = prior job ends 10:10` already explains initial block, but at 11:40 the reverse test (C01 vs C07) discriminates SPT vs aging: logging `waiting_time` and `starvation_counter` for C01 would show whether aging threshold triggered. Without that, 11:40 C01 over C07 cannot separate SPT-with-aging from pure FIFO.

Both tests illustrate observational equivalence: in QB, scan delay mimics FIFO; in QC, priority and deadline coincide, and aging mimics FIFO.

# 5. Operational exceptions and alternative explanations

Ranked by plausibility (1 = most plausible, explains most violations with minimal new mechanism):

**1. Arrival scan delay 2min (Data-quality) - Plausibility Very High**
- Affects QB 09:48 (B05 not observed), QA 08:30-08:32 idle (A05 scan 08:21 could be 08:22), QC minor. Prompt explicitly allows. Alteration: If we assume B05 scan delayed to 09:48:30, QB becomes FIFO-after-preempt, increasing confidence in FIFO secondary from 5570 and removing need for priority-aging. Without this, QB requires undocumented priority inversion.

**2. Appointment reservation with early-arrival holding & late-start tolerance (Operational rule) - High**
- Affects QA (A03 hold 12min until appt, A06 late start) and QD (D06 appt 11:30 held until resource free, but started exactly at appt time, not earlier). Explains T1 QA and D-X choice. If appointments were windows (e.g., ±15min) then A03 could have been taken earlier and A06 could have been taken at 09:05, falsifying FIFO. Diagnosis shifts: QA primary becomes FIFO + strict exact-time appointment, not windowed. Without this rule, QA T1 looks like priority/SPT violation.

**3. Non-preemptive meeting look-ahead / not starting job that overruns unavailability (Resource state) - High**
- Affects QD D-F idle 11:36-12:00 (30min job won't fit 24min window) and QA calibration block. Documented availability exceptions. Alternative interpretation as poor utilization vs intentional avoidance. If scheduler were preemptive, D05 would start 11:36 and preempt at 12:00, but observed idle proves non-preemptive. Diagnosis: QD remains skill+FIFO, but confirms non-preemptive discipline, ruling out preemptive priority for QD. Without this, D-F idle looks like contradiction to any work-conserving discipline.

**4. SLA-endangered policy switch (Priority/EDF expedite when deadline - now < est + buffer) - Moderate-High**
- Affects QC 10:54 (C05 overdue selected despite longest est). Prompt explicitly mentions possibility queue changes policy when SLA becomes endangered. Plausible because note says "deadline already at risk when selected". If we deny this, QC primary SPT is falsified by C05, forcing alternative: noisy estimate (C05 est 40 inflated) or priority was primary all along. Ranking third because requires introducing hybrid mechanism not in candidate list alone, but documented note supports it.

**5. Strategically inaccurate / noisy estimates - Moderate**
- `est_min` may be biased long for C05 (40 vs realized 40 actually accurate) or short for C06 (6). If C05 estimate were actually 6 and C06 40, SPT would predict C05. No evidence estimates are noisy here (realized = est for all QC), so low plausibility for this dataset, but in general could explain QC anomalies without policy switch. Would alter diagnosis: QC would remain pure SPT with measurement error.

**6. Preemption/resumption cost and dispatcher reassignment (Operational preemption) - Moderate**
- Affects QB second interruption ("dispatcher reassignment") and B01 resume. If preemption were free, resuming B01 immediately at 09:31 and 10:14 is optimal; if resumption incurs setup, FIFO resume may be preferred over picking new job. Without accounting, B01 resumes look like FIFO violations of priority. Permits hybrid interpretation to remain.

**7. Skill inaccuracy or cross-training not logged - Low**

# 7. Final synthesis

**Most identifiable: QD (skill-based routing).** Hard skill constraints create a clean partition: D05 waiting 73min despite idle resources is impossible under any non-skill discipline, and per-resource FIFO within partition is violated by priority/SPT at multiple points (11:25 D04 over D08, 11:48 D07 over D10). With skill accounted for, no undocumented exception beyond a standard non-preemptive meeting look-ahead is needed. Observational equivalence is minimal because skill eligibility is orthogonal to arrival order and priority.

**Least identifiable: QC (SPT vs EDF/priority + aging).** Initial three decisions support SPT perfectly, but the two later decisions require two opposite exceptions: an SLA-endangered switch (C05 over C06) that makes priority and EDF indistinguishable (both select C05), and a starvation/FIFO override (C01 over C07) that contradicts SPT, priority and EDF simultaneously. Priority and deadline are correlated (p1 job has earliest deadline but longest est), creating prolonged equivalence windows, and the final violation forces invoking aging without direct evidence. Confidence remains lowest; any inference of pure SPT, pure EDF, or pure priority alone would be wrong.

**Most dangerous to infer alone: QB preemptive priority as *pure* priority and QC SPT as *pure* SPT.** QB appears to be preemptive priority at first glance (B01 preempted by B02), but strict priority fails at 09:48 and 10:28 where FIFO predicts correctly, and appointment/SPT would also partially fit. Inferring pure priority would mispredict the next job after any interruption and misattribute scan delay to policy. Similarly, inferring QC as pure SPT would mispredict SLA-critical expedites and starvation avoidance, leading to missed deadlines if the SLA switch were not replicated. Management claim of a single documented rule is unlikely; the data strongly suggest hybrids: QA = FIFO + exact-time appointments, QB = preemptive priority for entry then FIFO, QC = SPT with SLA-triggered priority/EDF and aging, QD = skill-gated FIFO/appointment.

In sum, reasoning from decision sets rather than start order alone shows FIFO violations are not automatically priority: in QA they are appointments, in QB resumption, in QC SPT, in QD skills. Ignoring eligibility (skill, availability, scan delay) and ignoring policy switches at SLA risk are the two dominant sources of misdiagnosis.
