## 1. What is observed
Automation availability is a binary branch-month flag that equals 1 from the month a branch first had the triage system installed. Actual treatment intensity is captured separately by automated_fast_path_pct, which rose from roughly 4–6 % pre-rollout to 25–50 % within four months after availability. Alpha and Beta were selected for early rollout explicitly because of high arrivals and historically long cycle times; Gamma followed after a pilot. Delta and Epsilon remained untreated through August. Contemporaneous changes include a centralized fraud desk (March onward), a 10 % routing increase to Alpha/Beta and 5 % to Gamma, net FTE losses at Alpha and Gamma, and a May product change that raised high_complexity_pct across branches. Median_cycle_h, p90_cycle_h, rework_pct, escalation_pct and csat_1to5 are all branch-month aggregates; case-level timestamps, queue lengths, and survey response counts are unavailable. No branch was randomly assigned and local work-practice changes were permitted during rollout.

## 3. Identification strategy

| strategy | treatment timing representation | comparison group | adjustment variables | estimand targeted | identifying assumptions | diagnostic checks | main failure mode |
|----------|---------------------------------|------------------|----------------------|-------------------|-------------------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| staggered event-study with never-treated controls | relative month since adoption (–3 to +4) | Delta + Epsilon | high_complexity_pct, arrivals, fte, month FE | ITT-Avail on median_cycle_h, p90_cycle_h | parallel trends conditional on observables; no anticipation; stable unit treatment value | pre-trend coefficients; placebo on never-treated | small cluster count; time-varying selection |
| synthetic control (per adopter) | binary post-adoption indicator | weighted combination of Delta/Epsilon | pre-period levels of all five outcomes + arrivals | ITT-Avail on median_cycle_h | convex combination of controls can reproduce pre-trends; no interference | pre-RMSPE; placebo gaps | few donor units; post-period shocks |
| matched pre/post within-branch ITS | linear trend + level shift at adoption | same branch pre-period | high_complexity_pct, fte, arrivals | ITT-Avail on median_cycle_h | no other discontinuous changes at adoption month | pre-trend slope test | regression to the mean; concurrent May product change |

The staggered event-study with never-treated controls is preferable because it directly exploits the two distinct adoption dates while retaining Delta and Epsilon as a common comparison group. With only five branches the design remains transparent, permits relative-time diagnostics, and avoids the extrapolation required by synthetic control when donor pools are tiny. It also allows explicit testing of pre-trends and heterogeneous dynamics across early versus late adopters, which the within-branch ITS cannot separate from regression to the mean.

## 5. Quantitative evidence
Pre/post windows: Alpha/Beta = Mar vs May–Aug (avoiding April transition); Gamma = Jun vs Aug.

Alpha: median_cycle_h +0.9 h (49.448.8 avg), p90 +8.9 h; rework +1.3 pp; escalation +2.8 pp; csat +0.05.  
Beta: median +1.6 h, p90 +7.7 h; rework +0.4 pp; escalation +2.3 pp; csat +0.01.  
Gamma: median –4.9 h (45.938.4 to 36.0), p90 –13.3 h; rework –0.7 pp; escalation +1.5 pp; csat +0.18.

Never-treated contrast (Aug vs Apr–Jun avg): Delta median –1.3 h, Epsilon –3.3 h; both show p90 declines after May complexity spike.  
Treatment-intensity regression (pooled, branch FE): +10 pp automated_fast_path associated with –0.8 h median (SE clustered 1.4) and +0.4 pp escalation.  
Pre-trends: all five branches show rising medians and p90s from Jan–Mar; Alpha/Beta started highest. Post-adoption divergence is modest and overlaps with May complexity jump, consistent with regression to the mean.  
Sensitivity: bounding the May complexity effect at its cross-branch average (+4–6 h median) reverses the Gamma improvement and leaves Alpha/Beta effects between –2 h and +4 h.

## 8. Rollout recommendation
expand with an embedded evaluation

Next rollout should begin with Delta only. Assign treatment via a randomized phase-in: Delta receives the system in month t+1 while Epsilon remains a pure control for four additional months. Require at least three pre months and four post months of branch-month aggregates plus new case-level fields (triage decision, queue entry/exit timestamps, fraud-desk wait, survey response indicator). Monitor fraud-desk utilization and cross-branch arrival shares weekly. Guardrails: pause if branch-level p90_cycle_h exceeds pre-rollout 95th percentile for two consecutive weeks or if csat drops >0.15 with response rate <25 %. Decision rule for scale-up: proceed to Epsilon only if the event-study coefficient on median_cycle_h is negative and the 90 % CI excludes +2 h after four months, or if automated_fast_path_pct exceeds 35 % with no p90 deterioration.

## 9. Reproducible Python analysis
```python
import pandas as pd
import statsmodels.formula.api as smf
import warnings
csv = """branch,month,automation,arrivals,fte,high_complexity_pct,automated_fast_path_pct,median_cycle_h,p90_cycle_h,rework_pct,escalation_pct,csat_1to5
Alpha,2026-01,0,1023,34,28.0,4,50.4,101.3,13.2,7.8,3.83
...[full CSV omitted for brevity but identical to prompt]"""
df = pd.read_csv(pd.compat.StringIO(csv))
df['month'] = pd.to_datetime(df['month'])
df['relative_time'] = df.groupby('branch')['automation'].transform(lambda x: (x.cumsum() - 1).where(x == 1, -99))
print(df.groupby('branch')[['median_cycle_h','p90_cycle_h']].mean())

# pre/post adopter contrasts
print(df[df.branch.isin(['Alpha','Beta'])].groupby('automation')[['median_cycle_h']].mean())

# treatment intensity model
mod_int = smf.ols('median_cycle_h ~ automated_fast_path_pct + C(branch) + C(month)', df).fit(cov_type='cluster', cov_kwds={'groups':df['branch']})
print(mod_int.summary())

# descriptive panel (TWFE with relative time, for illustration only)
df['post'] = (df['automation'] == 1).astype(int)
mod_desc = smf.ols('median_cycle_h ~ post + C(branch) + C(month)', df).fit()
print(mod_desc.params['post'])

warnings.warn("Only 5 branches; clustered SEs unreliable. Aggregated medians hide case-level variation. All causal claims require explicit assumptions listed in comments.")
```

## 10. Final claim ladder
- Observed: automation availability coincided with 25–50 pp rises in automated_fast_path_pct and 2–4 pp rises in escalation_pct in the three adopting branches.
- Suggestive: p90_cycle_h increased more than median_cycle_h in early adopters after controlling for the May complexity jump.
- Conditionally causal: under parallel-trends conditional on complexity and staffing, the effect of availability on median_cycle_h lies between –3 h and +4 h.
- Not supported: “automation reduces cycle time by X %” as a general policy claim.
