## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

**Shared building blocks before the join**

Chemistry, waiting and service:
- Friday: the second analyst frees at 15:54–16:24, the first at 16:42–17:30. The minimum service time is 1.5 h, so even a sample started at 16:10 finishes by 18:00 with only about 4% probability. B-417 cannot realistically finish on Friday (below 2%, and only if it jumped the urgent sample).
- Prioritised case: the urgent sample takes the first free analyst. B-417 starts at about 17:05, receives 0.5–1.3 h of work, is paused at 18:00 and resumes Mon 08:00 with about 1.9 h left (0.2–4.0 h). Result Mon 08:15–12:00, central 09:30–10:15.
- FIFO case: samples 1 and 2 start on Friday and carry over, completing Monday at about 08:55 and 09:50. Samples 3 and 4 then start. B-417 starts at the third completion, about 11:30 (10:00–13:30), and needs about 2.8 h. Result central Mon 13:00–16:00 (range 11:30–18:00), with under 5% spilling to Tuesday morning.

Microbiology, incubation and readout:
- Incubation is technically ready Sun 12:00–20:00 (+44.5 to +52.5 h).
- Without on-call (0.55–0.75): result Mon 08:30–09:30 (+65 to +66 h).
- With on-call (0.25–0.45): result Sun 13:00–23:00, but QA is closed, so it becomes usable at Mon 08:00.

Parallel join (maximum, not sum):
- Join = max(chemistry result, microbiology result, QA opening).
- About 65 h of the roughly 73 h smooth-path total are fixed by the incubation and the weekend calendar. Nothing can be released before Monday morning even if chemistry were finished on Friday.
- On Monday itself the chemistry carry-over is usually the later of the two results: join ≈ 09:45 (08:30–12:00) if prioritised, ≈ 14:15 (11:30–18:00) under FIFO.
- Chemistry hours and microbiology hours are never added together. The 44–52 h of incubation and the chemistry queue overlap completely.

QA, waiting and service:
- Backlog now 5. Friday 15:30–20:00: about +1.6 arrivals and −1.8 served. Saturday 09:00–13:00: about +1.4 and −1.6. Backlog at Mon 08:00 ≈ 4–5 (2–8).
- From Monday the backlog drains at about 0.45 batches/h net.
- Join at 09:45 meets about 3–4 batches ahead: wait 2.5–4.5 h (0.5–1 h if B-417 is prioritised in QA).
- Join at 14:15 meets about 1–3 ahead: wait 0–2 h.
- A later join meets a shorter queue, so the QA queue partly cancels the chemistry-discipline difference. First QA decision: Mon 12:30–16:30 (prioritised chemistry) or Mon 15:30–18:30 (FIFO), with a long service tail (P80 3.5 h) cut off at 20:00.

Where maxima replace sums:
- Branch join: max(chemistry, microbiology), not chemistry + microbiology.
- A chemistry rerun and a contamination confirmation overlap in time; the confirmation absorbs the rerun.
- Several anomalies feed one investigation trigger. They do not create several investigations in series, but they raise the laboratory share and the rejection chance.
- Calendar gates act as a further maximum: a result that exists on Sunday is worth no more than one that exists at Mon 08:00.

- **Plausible earliest completion:** Mon 21 Sep ≈ 09:30–11:00, about 66–67.5 h remaining. It requires prioritised chemistry, a short Monday residual and near-immediate QA. A terminal event before Mon 08:00 (+64.5 h) is essentially excluded (below 1%) without overtime and Sunday QA.
- **Central / P50-like range:** about 73–91 h remaining, i.e. Mon 21 Sep ≈ 16:30 to Tue 22 Sep ≈ 10:30. The distribution has an overnight gap: roughly 40–55% of the mass lands on Monday afternoon or evening, so the median sits close to the Monday 20:00 QA close and can fall on either side of it.
- **P80-like range:** about 97–117 h remaining, i.e. Tue 22 Sep ≈ 17:00 to Wed 23 Sep ≈ 12:00. This is the query and documentation-only investigation region.
- **P90-like range:** about 120–165 h remaining, i.e. Wed 23 Sep ≈ 15:00 to Fri 25 Sep ≈ 12:00.
- **Severe tail (about P95 and beyond):** about 165–290 h remaining, i.e. Fri 25 Sep to Wed 30 Sep, driven by laboratory investigations and confirmed contamination. Extreme cases reach about 1–2 Oct (above 310 h).
- **Most plausible terminal events:** Electronic Release with probability about 0.92–0.97, most likely Monday 21 Sep afternoon/evening or Tuesday 22 Sep. The rejection share rises to roughly 0.08–0.15 if the temperature excursion is confirmed.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Temperature-excursion flag (unknown, p 0.20–0.40)** — observation uncertainty and dependence uncertainty. It is a common cause that raises rerun, contamination and investigation probabilities together. It moves the overall investigation probability from about 0.12 (cleared) to at least 0.42 (confirmed) and fattens the joint tail beyond what independent anomalies would give.
2. **Whether a Deviation Investigation opens, and whether it needs laboratory work** — path uncertainty. Documentation-only adds about one working day; laboratory work adds 3–7 calendar days. This driver alone determines P90 and beyond.
3. **Queue position at the Monday join: chemistry discipline plus QA backlog, against the 20:00 QA close** — queue uncertainty and calendar uncertainty. It decides whether the bulk of the distribution lands on Monday evening or Tuesday morning, a difference of about 13–16 h for the P50.
4. **Documentation Query (p 0.15–0.30, 4–12 working h)** — path uncertainty. It shifts a release from Monday to Tuesday afternoon, or to Wednesday morning for long queries, and largely sets the P70–P80 region.
5. **Microbiology outcome and readout staffing (contamination indication 0.05–0.12; on-call authorisation 0.25–0.45)** — path uncertainty and calendar uncertainty. The on-call decision has almost no effect on the smooth path, but on the contamination path it moves the confirmation readout by half a day to a day.

Lower-ranked: service-time uncertainty in the Monday chemistry residual and the QA review (±1–2 h on the body, with a larger effect only when it crosses 18:00 or 20:00).

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

**Single most forecast-changing event: resolution of the temperature-excursion flag by engineering.**

- **Flag cleared:**
  - Rerun and contamination probabilities fall to their low ends (about 0.10 and 0.05); the investigation probability falls to about 0.10–0.14, and laboratory investigation to about 0.03.
  - The smooth-path probability rises to about 0.55–0.65.
  - P50 moves firmly to Monday 21 Sep afternoon/evening (about 71–76 h); P80 to Tuesday afternoon (about 95–100 h); P90 to Wednesday (about 115–125 h).
  - Rejection falls to about 2–4%.
- **Flag confirmed:**
  - An anomaly exists by definition, so the investigation probability is 0.30–0.55 even with normal tests.
  - Rerun (0.22–0.35) and contamination (0.12–0.22) become more likely, and more likely to occur together.
  - Only about 0.30–0.40 of cases still finish without query or investigation.
  - P50 moves to Tuesday 22 Sep around midday or afternoon (about 92–100 h); P80 to Wednesday–Thursday (about 120–145 h); P90 into the laboratory-investigation tail, 25–29 Sep (about 165–265 h).
  - Rejection rises to roughly 8–15%.
- **Timing of the news:** if the flag is still unresolved at the Monday join, the forecast shifts later by hours to a day, because QA is likely to hold the batch or raise a query.

**Secondary update trigger: the primary microbiology readout result** (Sunday afternoon/evening if on-call is authorised, otherwise Mon 21 Sep 08:30–09:30).

- A clean readout removes the S4 branch and tightens the forecast toward Monday evening or Tuesday.
- A contamination indication makes microbiology the dominant clock, moves the earliest realistic terminal time to Tuesday 22 Sep midday (unless the on-call path was used), raises the investigation probability to 0.30–0.55 or more, and makes Batch Rejected a material outcome.
