# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

Notation for witnesses: `R`=Register Change, `A`=Assess Risk, `P`=Prepare Evidence, `L`=Legal Review, `C`=Configure Trial, `I`=Inspect Trial, `D`=Correct Defect, `Rel`=Release Change, `Arch`=Archive Change. Silent `skip_legal` tau events are hidden in the traces below. A “negative witness” is a trace or induced constraint that the authoritative POWL must reject.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 | `R` occurs first. | `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `A,R,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` | Yes. `R` is visible and has no predecessor. |
| INV-02 | `A` and `P` are unordered after `R`; both are required. | `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` and `R,P,A,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `R,A,C,I,P,Rel,Arch`: verification starts before `P`. Also reject any converter that forces only one of the two orders. | Partly. Both visible orders appear; true absence of order is not provable from traces alone. |
| INV-03 | `L` is optional via XOR with a silent skip and can occur after `A`. | With legal: `R,A,P,L,C,I,Rel,Arch`; without legal: `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` | Mandatory `L`, e.g. rejecting the no-legal trace, or accepting two `L` occurrences in one case. | Partly. Presence/absence of `L` is visible; the skipped tau branch itself is not. |
| INV-04 | No edge exists between optional legal and verification; they may overlap. | `R,A,P,L,C,I,Rel,Arch`, `R,P,A,C,L,I,Rel,Arch`, `R,A,P,C,I,L,Rel,Arch` | A model forcing `L` always before `C`, or always after `I`, rejects valid source behavior. | Yes when `L` occurs; no when the silent skip is taken. |
| INV-05 | Every verification iteration executes `C` before `I`. | `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `R,A,P,I,C,Rel,Arch` | Yes. |
| INV-06 | Verification loop has binary LOOP semantics: body first, redo child second. Body = `CI`; redo = `D`. | `R,A,P,C,I,D,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `R,A,P,C,I,C,I,Rel,Arch`: second iteration without `D`. | Yes. |
| INV-07 | At least one verification iteration occurs. | `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `R,A,P,Rel,Arch` | Yes. |
| INV-08 | Loop scope is only the verification body/redo; it does not loop `A`, `P`, legal, `Rel`, or `Arch`. | `R,A,P,C,I,D,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `R,A,P,C,I,D,A,C,I,Rel,Arch`: `A` repeated inside the loop. | Yes. |
| INV-09 | `Rel` waits for both optional-legal completion and verification-loop completion. | `R,A,P,C,I,D,C,I,L,Rel,Arch`; skip-legal case `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,L,Arch`: legal after release. `R,A,P,L,Rel,C,I,Arch`: release before verification. | Yes for visible `L`; verification is visible through `C/I`. |
| INV-10 | `Arch` occurs after `Rel` and the case ends there. | `R,A,P,C,I,Rel,Arch` | `R,A,P,C,I,Rel` as a completed trace; `R,A,P,C,I,Arch,Rel` | Yes. |
| INV-11 | The accepting execution must end in the source final marking with no extra tokens. | Source net execution ending with one token in the final place. | Any trace ending with leftover tokens in unreleased parallel branches. | Not from visible labels alone; requires marking-level validation. |

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 — primary**: `POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN` | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` for round trip | Uses the supported POWL-to-Petri bridge; Petri nets can represent the partial order, silent XOR, and loop directly. Most likely to retain the non-blocked structure. | BPMN output may render token concurrency as visually ordered flows; gateways may be ambiguous; round-trip BPMNPetri may change markings or add routing places. | Conversion exception, null markings, unsound round-trip net, failed witness checks, lost interleaving, or release-before-verification trace. |
| P2 — secondary/experimental: `POWL  accepting Petri net  process tree  BPMN` | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)`; `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | If it succeeds, process trees are compact and easier to visualize; tree-to-BPMN often yields cleaner diagrams. | The source is not obviously block-structured: `A` enables optional legal before the `A/P` join, verification needs both `A` and `P`, legal and verification are unordered, and both join only before `Rel`. A tree may fail or force a flow that adds/removes an ordering edge. | Process-tree conversion exception; or a returned tree that fails bounded trace/footprint/witness checks. Failure of this pipeline is acceptable. |
| P3 — optional comparison only: `POWL  accepting Petri net  visualization + reachability audit`, not used as BPMN source | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(...)`; `pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(...)` | Gives a behavior-oriented baseline that is easier to audit than BPMN graphics. | Not a stakeholder BPMN deliverable; does not remove BPMN gateway ambiguity. | Not relevant; this is an audit aid, not a primary conversion claim. |

A successful object return is **not** semantic equivalence. The primary pipeline is P1 because it avoids forcing the source into a block-structured tree before BPMN rendering.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

The cells below use only the required assessment vocabulary. Because no conversion log is available here, I do not mark anything `validated preserved` without running §5. The code in §3 and §5 is what turns `expected preserved` into evidence-backed validation.

| Source invariant | POWLPetri | PetriBPMN | PetriTree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 `R` first | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Source edge `registerassess_risk` and `registerprepare_evidence`; verify with witness traces. |
| INV-02 `A/P` unordered; both before verification | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | Petri net keeps partial order; BPMN may display a visually ordered path. Trace set alone cannot distinguish true concurrency from all interleavings; footprints/reachability needed. |
| INV-03 Optional legal via silent XOR | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Silent `skip_legal` must remain unlabelled/`None`. Hiding tau should leave both `L`-present and `L`-absent traces. |
| INV-04 No edge between legal and verification | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | Source has no order between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`; BPMN layout may imply one. Must test `L` before, during, and after trial steps. |
| INV-05 `C` before `I` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Internal strict order inside `trial_body`. |
| INV-06 Binary LOOP semantics | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | PM4Py LOOP children are `[trial_body, correct_defect]`; at least one body, redo via `D`. BPMN loop gateway rendering may be ambiguous. |
| INV-07 At least one verification iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | LOOP is not XOR-optional around the body; reject `R,A,P,Rel,Arch`. |
| INV-08 Loop scope limited to verification | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Loop children exclude `A`, `P`, legal, `Rel`, and `Arch`; check repeated-activity witnesses. |
| INV-09 AND-join before `Rel` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | `Rel` has incoming order from both `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`; reject release before verification or before chosen legal branch. |
| INV-10 `Arch` final | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Source edge `release_changearchive_change`; exact final marking must have no leftover tokens. |
| INV-11 Initial/final marking integrity | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Source markings are non-null; BPMN round trip may introduce extra places/tokens. Requires exact marking equality and soundness. |
| Activity-label uniqueness | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | All visible source labels are unique. If BPMN conversion renames, splits, or duplicates labels, trace and footprint comparisons become unreliable. |
| Gateway/routing-node meaning | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Petri-to-BPMN may use AND/XOR gateways or implicit routing. Visual gateway shape is not proof of token semantics. |

Key interpretation points:

- **True concurrency versus arbitrary interleaving.** The source partial order and the Petri net express concurrency structurally. A visible trace set only enumerates interleavings: `R,A,P,...` and `R,P,A,...` do not by themselves prove `A || P`. Footprints, reachability summaries, and the Petri net structure are needed. A conversion that replaces concurrency with an XOR-choice of two sequences would pass simple trace examples but lose true concurrency.
- **Lack of an edge between optional legal and verification.** The source permits `L` before verification, between `C` and `I`, or after the loop but before `Rel`. Adding an edge in either direction narrows behavior and must be rejected.
- **Silent optional behavior.** The skip branch is a `SilentTransition`. After hiding tau, absence of `L` is valid and expected. The converter must not expose the tau as a visible activity or make `L` mandatory.
- **Binary-loop semantics.** The loop has two children: the mandatory body `CI` and the redo child `D`. A faithful conversion permits `C,I,(D,C,I)*`, not `(C,I)?`, not `C,I,D*`, and not a loop around the whole process.
- **Join before Release Change.** `Rel` is an AND-join of the legal branch and verification loop. In the silent-skip case, the legal side is already complete invisibly; verification still cannot be bypassed.
- **Activity-label uniqueness.** The audit assumes visible labels uniquely identify activities. If a BPMN artifact duplicates or renames labels, trace comparison must be supplemented with transition-ID mapping or rejected.
- **Initial and final markings.** Non-null markings are checked before conversion. Exact final-marking equality is stronger than “archive was seen”; it rejects leftover tokens in parallel branches.
- **Gateway/routing-node ambiguity in BPMN visualization.** A gateway drawn after `A` may look like a choice between legal and verification even though the source allows overlap. A loop backflow may look like it repeats `A` or `P`. These are rendering risks, not proof of semantic loss.

def clone_marking(marking):
    return Marking({p: n for p, n in marking.items() if n})


def marking_equal(a, b):
    if a is None or b is None:
        return False
    return {p: n for p, n in a.items() if n} == {p: n for p, n in b.items() if n}


def marking_key(marking):
    return tuple(sorted((id(p), n) for p, n in marking.items() if n))


def fire_transition(net, marking, transition):
    """
    Custom firing helper, because the required PM4Py API explicitly includes
    get_enabled_transitions but not a guaranteed top-level execute function.
    """
    new_marking = clone_marking(marking)

    for arc in transition.in_arcs:
        place = arc.source
        weight = arc.weight if arc.weight is not None else 1
        remaining = new_marking.get(place, 0) - weight
        if remaining < 0:
            raise ValueError("negative token count after firing")
        if remaining == 0:
            new_marking.pop(place, None)
        else:
            new_marking[place] = remaining

    for arc in transition.out_arcs:
        place = arc.target
        weight = arc.weight if arc.weight is not None else 1
        new_marking[place] = new_marking.get(place, 0) + weight

    return new_marking


def bounded_visible_traces(net, im, fm):
    """
    Bounded model playout.

    - hides transitions whose label is None;
    - records a trace only when the marking equals fm exactly;
    - avoids infinite silent cycling via tau bound plus visited states;
    - enforces visible-length and loop-iteration bounds.
    """
    if im is None or fm is None:
        raise ValueError("bounded_visible_traces requires non-null im and fm")

    start = (clone_marking(im), (), 0, 0)
    queue = deque([start])
    seen = set()
    accepted = set()
    truncated = False

    while queue:
        marking, trace, loop_count, tau_run = queue.popleft()

        state_key = (marking_key(marking), trace, loop_count, tau_run)
        if state_key in seen:
            continue
        seen.add(state_key)

        if marking_equal(marking, fm):
            accepted.add(trace)
            continue

        enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)

        for transition in enabled:
            label = transition.label

            if label is None:
                if tau_run + 1 > MAX_TAU_STEPS_PER_VISIBLE_PREFIX:
                    truncated = True
                    continue
                next_trace = trace
                next_loop_count = loop_count
                next_tau_run = tau_run + 1
            else:
                if len(trace) + 1 > MAX_VISIBLE_LENGTH:
                    truncated = True
                    continue
                if label in LOOP_MARKER_LABELS and loop_count + 1 > MAX_LOOP_MARKER_COUNT:
                    truncated = True
                    continue
                next_trace = trace + (label,)
                next_loop_count = loop_count + (
                    1 if label in LOOP_MARKER_LABELS else 0
                )
                next_tau_run = 0

            next_marking = fire_transition(net, marking, transition)
            queue.append(
                (next_marking, next_trace, next_loop_count, next_tau_run)
            )

    return accepted, {
        "accepted_trace_count": len(accepted),
        "visited_state_count": len(seen),
        "truncated": truncated,
        "max_visible_length": MAX_VISIBLE_LENGTH,
        "max_loop_marker_count": MAX_LOOP_MARKER_COUNT,
        "loop_marker_labels": tuple(sorted(LOOP_MARKER_LABELS)),
        "max_tau_steps_per_visible_prefix": MAX_TAU_STEPS_PER_VISIBLE_PREFIX,
    }


def footprint_as_dict(fp):
    if fp is None:
        return None
    if hasattr(fp, "footprints") and isinstance(fp.footprints, dict):
        return fp.footprints
    if hasattr(fp, "_footprints") and isinstance(fp._footprints, dict):
        return fp._footprints
    if isinstance(fp, dict):
        return fp
    return dict(getattr(fp, "__dict__", {}))


def reachability_summary(net, im, fm):
    rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)

    if hasattr(rg, "get_nodes"):
        nodes = list(rg.get_nodes())
    else:
        nodes_attr = getattr(rg, "nodes", [])
        nodes = list(nodes_attr() if callable(nodes_attr) else nodes_attr)

    if hasattr(rg, "get_edges"):
        edges = list(rg.get_edges())
    else:
        edges_attr = getattr(rg, "edges", [])
        edges = list(edges_attr() if callable(edges_attr) else edges_attr)

    return {"nodes": len(nodes), "edges": len(edges)}


def soundness_result(model):
    if model.get("im") is None or model.get("fm") is None:
        return "skipped: missing initial or final marking"
    try:
        res = pm4py.check_soundness(
            model["net"], model["im"], model["fm"], print_diagnostics=True
        )
        return bool(res) if isinstance(res, (bool, int)) else repr(res)
    except Exception as exc:
        return f"exception: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"


def event_name(event):
    if hasattr(event, "get_event_name"):
        try:
            return event.get_event_name()
        except Exception:
            pass
    try:
        return event["concept:name"]
    except Exception:
        return str(event)


def pm4py_play_out_sample(model):
    """
    Optional sample playout where supported. This is supporting evidence only;
    it is not treated as exhaustive model equivalence.
    """
    if not hasattr(pm4py, "play_out"):
        return {"supported": False, "reason": "pm4py.play_out not available"}

    if model.get("im") is None or model.get("fm") is None:
        return {"supported": True, "error": "missing markings"}

    try:
        played = pm4py.play_out(model["net"], model["im"], model["fm"])
        traces = set()
        for trace in played:
            traces.add(tuple(event_name(e) for e in trace))
        return {"supported": True, "trace_count": len(traces)}
    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "supported": True,
            "error": f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}",
        }


def printable_trace_set(traces):
    return sorted(traces, key=lambda t: (len(t), t))

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

Even when the converted Petri net is behaviorally acceptable, the BPMN rendering can be misread:

- **Parallel gateways.** An AND-split for `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` may be drawn in a way that looks like ordered branches. The model semantics are token concurrency, not “do the upper branch first.” Similarly, the AND-join before `Release Change` must be read as waiting for both the legal branch and the verification loop, not merely for the visually nearest incoming flow.
- **Exclusive gateways.** The optional legal XOR may render as a choice whose silent branch is easy to overlook. Stakeholders may conclude that Legal Review is mandatory if only the visible branch is labeled. Add a note: “Legal Review is optional; the else branch is silent.”
- **Loop visualization.** The binary LOOP may be rendered as a back edge from Inspect Trial through Correct Defect to Configure Trial. This should be annotated as: “One ConfigureInspect iteration is mandatory; Correct Defect separates repeated iterations; the loop does not include legal review, release, or archive.”
- **Silent routing nodes.** Silent transitions or implicit routing may appear as unlabeled boxes or diamond structures. They should not be interpreted as business activities. Hide or style them distinctly and document that no operational meaning is attached.
- **Apparent ordering from graph layout.** BPMN layout is not semantic. A legal branch drawn above the verification branch, or a loop drawn to the left, does not imply precedence. The absence of a sequence-flow edge between optional legal and verification is the authoritative detail.
- **Recommended annotations.** Add a diagram note or model README containing the invariants from §1, especially: “A/P may occur in either order and may overlap,” “Legal Review is optional and unordered with verification,” “Release waits for both legal branch and verification loop,” and “Loop scope is Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Correct Defect only.”

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a conversion result if any of the following holds:

1. Any conversion call raises an exception that is not explicitly interpreted and resolved.
2. `convert_to_petri_net`, `convert_to_bpmn`, or BPMN round trip returns `None` where an artifact is required.
3. Initial or final markings are missing for a claimed accepting Petri net.
4. `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(...)` returns `False` or raises for a claimed workflow net.
5. `pm4py.check_soundness(...)` fails or cannot be evaluated.
6. A positive witness from §5 is absent from the round-tripped model under the chosen bounds.
7. A negative witness from §5 is admitted, especially release before verification or release before a selected Legal Review.
8. The bounded visible trace set differs from the source net under the same bounds, with unexplained missing or extra traces.
9. A required interleaving is lost, for example Legal Review can no longer occur between Configure Trial and Inspect Trial.
10. Optional Legal Review collapses to mandatory Legal Review, or the silent skip becomes visible.
11. The loop becomes optional, loops the wrong scope, omits Correct Defect between repeated iterations, or permits a second trial without a preceding defect correction.
12. The join before Release Change is weakened so release can occur while verification or the legal branch is still incomplete.
13. Exact final-marking equality fails, including cases with leftover tokens after Archive Change.
14. Footprint or reachability diagnostics materially disagree with the source net and the disagreement is not explainable by harmless identifier renaming.
15. Activity labels are duplicated, renamed, or hidden in a way that invalidates trace-based comparison.
16. The BPMN diagram requires undocumented gateway interpretations to recover the intended semantics.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

Authoritative model for storage: the **source POWL model**, versioned together with the semantic invariant table and executable validation witnesses. It is the source definition and most directly encodes the partial order, silent XOR, and binary LOOP scope.

Derivative artifacts:

- `net_from_powl` with `im_from_powl` and `fm_from_powl`: behavior baseline and validation target.
- `bpmn_from_net`: primary stakeholder visualization.
- Process tree and tree-derived BPMN: optional, experimental derivatives.
- Round-tripped Petri nets from BPMN artifacts: validation artifacts, not authoritative models.

Validation evidence that must accompany each derivative:

- workflow-net and soundness results;
- bounded visible trace set and the exact bound settings;
- positive and negative witness results;
- footprint comparison;
- reachability summary comparison;
- exact initial/final marking status;
- notes on any exception, null marking, or interpretation decision;
- diagram annotations that explain silent routing and loop scope.

Process-tree intermediate policy: allow it only as a non-authoritative experiment. If `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(...)` fails, record the failure and continue with P1. If it succeeds, do not promote the tree or tree-derived BPMN unless §5 evidence shows the required invariants are preserved.

Future edits should be made only in the POWL authority model. Regenerate all Petri nets, BPMN diagrams, process-tree experiments, and validation reports from that edited POWL. Do not edit a generated BPMN and convert it back as if it were canonical; that creates round-trip drift. Stable activity labels and invariant IDs should be retained across versions so witness traces remain comparable.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

1. Bounded trace exploration cannot prove unbounded behavioral equivalence. A mismatch or match beyond 3 verification iterations remains unknown under the chosen bound.
2. Trace sets after hiding tau cannot reveal all internal silent-routing differences. Two nets may have the same visible bounded traces but differ in tau structure or branching topology.
3. Footprint equality and reachability-size equality are not full bisimulation proofs; they can miss state-level semantic differences masked by labels.
4. BPMN gateway rendering does not guarantee that stakeholders will interpret AND/XOR semantics correctly, even if the underlying converted net is sound.
5. `pm4py.play_out` sampling, where supported, is evidence of possible behavior only; it cannot establish language inclusion or equality.
6. PM4Py conversion behavior may vary by version. A converter that succeeds in one environment may fail or produce different routing in another, so environment/version metadata should be stored with validation evidence.
7. The audit assumes unique visible activity labels. If future edits introduce repeated labels, trace-based conclusions become weaker unless transition identities are mapped explicitly.
