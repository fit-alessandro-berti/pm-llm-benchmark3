# 1. System boundary and assumptions

* **Inside the boundary (Gate-to-Customer Delivery + Returns/Rework):**
  * **Manufacturing & Warehouse Operations:** Energy consumption (`energy_kwh`) across `machine_group` (`CNC_A`, `CNC_B`, `heat_treat`, `inspection_cell`) and warehouse picking/packaging.
  * **Materials & Waste:** Production scrap (`material_scrap_kg`), packaging materials (`package_material_kg` for `Package standard` and `Package reinforced`), and unshipped obsolete batch inventory (items unshipped after 14 days).
  * **Outbound Logistics & Rework Loops:** Transport emissions (`transport_kgco2e`) across `route_mode` (`parcel_ground`, `parcel_air`, `dedicated_van`, `consolidated_truck`), including empty return legs for dedicated vans, second shipments from `Partial shipment`, and extra loops from `Rework production` (470 cases) and `Rework packaging` (620 cases total).
* **Outside the boundary:**
  * Upstream raw material extraction (e.g., steel/aluminum mining for parts), manufacturing of capital equipment, customer-side operational emissions, and end-of-life disposal of parts after customer use.
* **Key Assumptions & Uncertainties:**
  * *Embodied carbon of scrap/packaging:* While `energy_kwh` and `transport_kgco2e` are explicitly tracked, the exact $\text{kgCO}_2\text{e}$ per kg of `material_scrap_kg` and `package_material_kg` is unquantified in the log; we assume reducing physical mass directly reduces upstream Scope 3 emissions.
  * * granularity:* The log is flattened by order; multi-line item interactions are approximated via `Partial shipment` (1,010 cases) and `Backorder wait` (220 cases).
  * *Grid mix:* We assume constant carbon intensity per `energy_kwh` across time of day.

# 3. Intervention options

### S1 & S4 (Combined): Green Shipping Recommender + Priority Pick for Service-Contract Stock
* **Target path/segment:** `Standard-stock Service-contract` (16% of orders) and `Stock -> standard pack -> air` (5% share, $18.5\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$ vs. $3.6\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$ for ground). Targets the 22% SP1 violation rate.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Replaces unnecessary `parcel_air` with priority warehouse picking (`Pick from stock`) + `parcel_ground` whenever remaining slack to `promised_lead_time_hours` gives $>85\%$ on-time probability.
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Maintains the 5% late-delivery benchmark for Service-contract stock by trading warehouse wait time for transport time, preserving air capacity for true emergencies.
* **Required process-mining evidence:** Timestamp delta between `Availability check` and `Pick from stock`, `promised_lead_time_hours`, `route_mode`, and SP1 conformance rate.

### S2: Dynamic Batching with Segment Waiting-Time Caps
* **Target path/segment:** `Plan production -> Batch wait -> CNC production` (2,910 cases) and `Heat treatment` (1,820 cases) for `Made-to-order` segments.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Groups items to reach efficient thresholds (`batch_size` $\ge 12$ on `CNC_A` drops energy from $2.8$ to $1.3\text{ kWh/item}$; `heat_treat` $>10$), while capping `Batch wait` (e.g., max 18h for Service-contract, 30h for Regular) and limiting batch size to firm orders + high-certainty short-term demand to prevent the 6% 14-day obsolescence rate.
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Mitigates the $1.7\times$ late-delivery risk in heat treatment and reduces p90 cycle times (currently 180h Regular, 156h Service-contract).
* **Required process-mining evidence:** `batch_id`, `batch_size`, duration of `Batch wait`, `energy_kwh`, and inventory age $>14$ days.

### S3 & S7 (Combined): CNC_B-to-CNC_A Routing Shift + Inspection Quality Gate
* **Target path/segment:** `MTO -> batch wait -> CNC_B -> inspection -> rework -> ground` (5% share, $32.0\text{ kWh}$, $1.8\text{ kg}$ scrap, 32% late) and `Inspection -> Rework production` (470 cases).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Shifts MTO orders with sufficient due-date slack from `CNC_B` (18% rework) to `CNC_A` (9% rework). For orders that must use `CNC_B`, enforces a mandatory quality gate at `inspection_cell` to prevent rushed inspections from missing defects.
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Drastically cuts the 160h median cycle time of the rework variant, improves quality, and prevents downstream packaging/customer returns.
* **Required process-mining evidence:** `machine_group`, queue length at `CNC_A` vs. `CNC_B`, `rework_flag`, `material_scrap_kg`, and `inspection_cell` processing duration.

### S5: Twice-Daily Consolidated Truck Departures for Non-Urgent Stock
* **Target path/segment:** `Standard-stock Regular` (39% of orders, p90 62h) currently using `Ship ground` (5,100 from standard pack) instead of `Consolidated truck` (850 cases).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Shifts eligible ground parcels with $\ge 24\text{h}$ lead-time slack to `consolidated_truck`, cutting `transport_kgco2e` by 35–55% per order.
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Adds 12–24h consolidation wait, slightly increasing median cycle time (from 31h toward 45h), but remains safely within `promised_lead_time_hours`. Maintains `parcel_ground` contract active to satisfy Resilience Policy R1.
* **Required process-mining evidence:** `route_mode`, wait time between `Package standard` and `Consolidated truck`, `transport_kgco2e`, and R1 alternative route availability.

### S6: Automated Packaging–Route Compatibility Check
* **Target path/segment:** `Package standard -> Rework packaging` (260 cases) and `Package reinforced -> Rework packaging` (360 cases), plus heavy MTO parts ($>8\text{ kg}$).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Validates part geometry, weight ($>8\text{ kg}$ rule Q1), and `route_mode` *before* packing begins, eliminating 620 packaging rework loops (saving double `package_material_kg`) and preventing in-transit damage (reinforced pack cuts returns by 40%).
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Removes rework delays, ensures Q1 compliance, and reduces damage-related customer returns.
* **Required process-mining evidence:** Transition frequencies to `Rework packaging`, `package_material_kg`, Q1 conformance rate, and return flags.

### S8 & S9 (Combined): Triage Critical-Downtime + Shared Emergency Courier Lanes
* **Target path/segment:** `Critical-downtime Service-contract` (11% of orders, $38.0\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$ avg) and `Critical -> emergency production -> reinforced pack -> dedicated van` (8% share, $52.0\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$, 31% missing SP2 justification).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Splits `Critical-downtime` into *True Machine-Down* (retains `dedicated_van` with mandatory SP2 log) and *Precautionary Urgent* (routed via shared emergency courier lanes or backhaul-matched vans, reducing empty return legs).
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Protects the 14–15h median cycle time and 3–4% late rate for genuine downtime while curbing unauthorized van dispatches.
* **Required process-mining evidence:** SP2 justification event presence, `expedite_flag`, `route_mode`, and `transport_kgco2e`.

### S10: Carbon-Aware Promise-Date Negotiation for Regular MTO
* **Target path/segment:** `Made-to-order Regular` (22% of orders, 18% late delivery, 94h median / 180h p90 cycle time) and `Availability check -> Cancel due to delay` (410 cases).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Offers Regular MTO customers a green/standard lead-time tier at order intake (`Receive order`) that aligns `promised_lead_time_hours` with `CNC_A` batching cycles and `parcel_ground` transport, avoiding `MTO -> CNC immediately -> ... -> air` ($24\text{ kWh}$, $23\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$).
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Resets unrealistic lead-time expectations upfront, reducing the 18% late rate and 410 initial delay cancellations.
* **Required process-mining evidence:** `promised_lead_time_hours` distribution, `Cancel due to delay` count, and share of immediate-CNC/air variant.

### S11: Smart Availability-Check Rules to Curb Partial Shipments
* **Target path/segment:** `Pick from stock -> Partial shipment` (1,010 cases; 790 pack immediately, 220 enter `Backorder wait`, leading to 60 cancellations). Variant shows 79h median cycle time, 24% late delivery, and double transport ($7.2\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Holds multi-item orders at `Availability check` for a short window if the missing item is in inbound stock or an active batch completing within slack limits, shipping as one consolidated order instead of splitting.
* **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Eliminates second shipments and extra packaging; avoids the high-friction `Backorder wait` path where 27% (60/220) eventually cancel.
* **Required process-mining evidence:** Frequency of `Partial shipment`, `Backorder wait` duration, `package_material_kg` per order, and `transport_kgco2e`.

# 5. Rebound and displacement risks

1. **Batching Energy Savings $\to$ Downstream Expediting & Obsolescence (Batching vs. Transport/Scrap):**
   Forcing `batch_size` to 12–20 on `CNC_A` and `heat_treat` saves $\sim 1.5\text{ kWh/item}$, but increases median `Batch wait` from 4h to 38h. This delay consumes order slack, forcing downstream operations to switch from `parcel_ground` ($5.8\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$) to `parcel_air` ($23.0\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$) to meet promised dates—wiping out the energy carbon savings several times over. Furthermore, padding batches with forecasted items results in 6% obsolescence after 14 days, creating pure material and energy waste.
2. **Truck Consolidation & Priority Ground $\to$ Warehouse Congestion & Emergency Air (Transport vs. Service):**
   Holding `Standard-stock` orders for 12–24h for `Consolidated truck` (S5) or shifting `Service-contract` orders from air to ground (S4) leaves thinner buffer margins. If carrier reliability dips or warehouse picking lags, orders breach `promised_lead_time_hours`, triggering customer escalations and reactive `dedicated_van` or `parcel_air` dispatches.
3. **Lightweight Packaging $\to$ Part Damage, Scrap, and Return Transport (Packaging vs. Rework/Materials):**
   Attempting to reduce `package_material_kg` by using `Package standard` instead of `Package reinforced` on heavy MTO parts ($>8\text{ kg}$, violating Q1) saves packaging mass upfront, but increases transit damage. A single damaged MTO return requires a replacement run ($18.5\text{–}20.0\text{ kWh}$, $0.85\text{–}0.90\text{ kg}$ alloy scrap, plus two-way transport), dwarfing the packaging savings.
4. **Overloading CNC_A $\to$ Queue Starvation & Rushed Inspections (Quality/Rework vs. Cycle Time):**
   Shifting all MTO volume from `CNC_B` to `CNC_A` (S3) to avoid `CNC_B`'s 18% rework rate will saturate `CNC_A` queues. Long queues compress the time left before shipping cutoffs, causing `inspection_cell` operators to rush—which the evidence shows directly triggers missed defects, customer returns, and downstream `Rework packaging` loops.
5. **Strict Partial-Shipment Holds $\to$ Customer Cancellations or Preventive Critical Orders (Customer Behavior):**
   If S11 holds an order at `Availability check` waiting for one out-of-stock line item, and that backorder slips, the customer may cancel the entire order (`Cancel due to delay`) or—worse—learn to game the system by submitting future orders as `Critical-downtime` to force immediate dispatch ($38\text{–}52\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$).
6. **Aggressive Route Consolidation $\to$ Carrier Lock-in (Efficiency vs. Resilience):**
   Concentrating volume into twice-daily `Consolidated truck` lanes (S5) and shared couriers (S8) reduces emissions per order, but if alternative `parcel_ground` or regional courier contracts are dropped due to low volume, a strike or weather disruption leaves `Service-contract` orders stranded (violating Resilience Policy R1).

# 6. Balanced recommendation

To reduce environmental impact without sacrificing service, quality, or resilience, the distributor should implement an integrated **Three-Tier Process Redesign Package** complemented by exception governance (**S12**):

1. **Tier 1: Upstream Intake, Triage & Batching Control (S10 + S9 + S11 + S2)**
   * **At `Receive order` & `Availability check`:** Deploy **S10** to negotiate realistic, batch-aligned lead times for `Made-to-order Regular` customers (curbing the 410 immediate delay cancellations and 18% late rate). Simultaneously apply **S9** to separate `Critical-downtime` into verified *Machine-Down* vs. *Precautionary Urgent*, and **S11** to hold `Partial shipment` orders *only* when the missing item has a confirmed replenishment/batch completion within 24h and sufficient slack.
   * **At `Plan production`:** Implement **S2 (Dynamic Batching with Wait Caps)**. Group `CNC_A` and `heat_treat` batches toward the 10–12 item efficiency sweet spot, **strictly capped by remaining order slack** (e.g., max 16h wait for Service-contract, 28h for Regular) and **restricted to firm orders + high-velocity stock** (eliminating the 6% 14-day obsolescence waste).

2. **Tier 2: Quality-First Manufacturing & Packaging Execution (S3 + S7 + S6)**
   * **At `CNC production` & `Inspection`:** Use **S3** to route MTO orders to `CNC_A` by default when `CNC_A` queue + transport time fits within `promised_lead_time_hours`. Keep `CNC_B` active as a high-speed overflow buffer for tight-slack orders, but pair every `CNC_B` run with **S7 (Mandatory Inspection Quality Gate)** to catch the 18% defect rate before heat treatment or shipping.
   * **At `Package`:** Embed **S6** into the warehouse management system to enforce Q1 (mandatory `Package reinforced` for MTO $>8\text{ kg}$) and verify `route_mode` compatibility before boxing. This eliminates the 620 `Rework packaging` loops and protects heavy parts from the 40% higher return damage rate, accepting slightly higher `package_material_kg` to prevent massive manufacturing scrap and transport rework.

3. **Tier 3: Slack-Driven Logistics & Resilience Preservation (S1 + S4 + S5 + S8)**
   * **At `Ship`:** Deploy the **S1 Green Shipping Recommender**.
     * For `Standard-stock Service-contract`, execute **S4**: trigger priority picking at `Pick from stock` to save 6–12h in the warehouse, enabling `parcel_ground` instead of `parcel_air` whenever on-time delivery probability exceeds 85% (fixing the 22% SP1 violation rate).
     * For `Standard-stock Regular` with $>24\text{h}$ slack, route to **S5 (Twice-Daily Consolidated Trucks)** (35–55% $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$ reduction).
     * For `Critical-downtime`, reserve solo `dedicated_van` ($52\text{ kgCO}_2\text{e}$) strictly for verified *Machine-Down* cases, routing *Precautionary Urgent* cases to **S8 (Shared Emergency Courier Lanes)**.
     * Maintain active baseline allocations across at least two carriers per segment to strictly uphold **Resilience Policy R1**.

# 7. Validation and governance plan

Post-implementation validation will rely on continuous process mining of the enriched event log, organized around three pillars:

1. **Automated Policy Conformance Monitoring (SP1, SP2, Q1, R1):**
   * **SP1 Conformance:** Track the percentage of cases where `on_time_probability_ground > 0.85` at `Package` completion that still execute `Ship air`. Target: reduce SP1 violations in `Standard-stock Service-contract` from **22% to $<3\%$**.
   * **SP2 Conformance:** Configure process-mining conformance checking to flag any trace containing `Dedicated van` that lacks a preceding `Justification_Approved` event attribute. Target: reduce missing justifications from **31% to $0\%$**.
   * **Q1 Conformance:** Audit all MTO traces with `part_weight_kg > 8`. Verify that 100% either pass through `Package reinforced` or carry an explicit `Inspection_Low_Damage_Cert` attribute, correlating this against downstream `Rework packaging` and return rates.
   * **R1 Resilience Check:** Monitor weekly carrier volume share by region; trigger an alert if any single `route_mode` / carrier exceeds 85% of `Service-contract` volume, ensuring backup lanes remain warm.

2. **Segment-Level Multi-Criteria Dashboards (Preventing Rebound):**
   * Track a weekly balanced scorecard segmented by the 5 core case-mix cohorts:
     * *Environmental:* Avg `energy_kwh`, `transport_kgco2e`, `material_scrap_kg`, and 14-day unshipped batch inventory %.
     * *Service & Quality:* Median and p90 cycle time, Late delivery %, `Cancel due to delay` count (benchmark: $<530$), `Rework production` rate (benchmark: $<470$), and `Rework packaging` rate (benchmark: $<620$).
   * **Guardrail Triggers:** If `Batch wait` caps (S2) or `Consolidated truck` holds (S5) cause late deliveries in any segment to rise by $>1.5\%$ over baseline, automatically tighten the recommender slack threshold (e.g., from 85% to 92% on-time probability).

3. **Exception Governance via Sustainability Override Log (S12):**
   * Implement **S12**: Whenever a planner or warehouse operator overrides S1/S4 (e.g., forcing `parcel_air`), S2 (breaking a batch early), or S8 (dispatching an unshared `dedicated_van`), the system requires a structured reason code (`carrier_disruption`, `machine_breakdown`, `customer_escalation`, `inventory_discrepancy`).
   * Review the **S12 Exception Log** in bi-weekly process-mining governance reviews to distinguish legitimate resilience responses (which improve model precision by separating disruption paths from normal operations) from habitual expediting.
