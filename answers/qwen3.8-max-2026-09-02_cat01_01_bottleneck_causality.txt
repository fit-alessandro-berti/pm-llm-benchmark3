| 3 | Queue/compliance capacity intervention: add night compliance capacity, parallelize compliance check, or create priority queue for compliance waiting; do not change resource-group label | queue_wait and compliance_performed bottleneck | shift, queue, or time period in stepped-wedge rollout | compliance policy unchanged; no skipping required checks; audit trail | compliance lead time, wait_before_review_h, queue length, DFG compliance edge frequency, cycle time by period | tests whether delay is mediated by queue/compliance capacity rather than group identity | rollback if compliance wait does not fall after agreed runway or if quality indicators worsen |
| 4 | Targeted, conditional reassignment: if pilot shows Nova non-inferior and queue capacity is sufficient, move only low-risk day/non-compliance cases, not a blanket 50% | transportability of group effect and capacity balancing | case stratum | phased rollout; capacity monitoring; quality gates; retain high/night/compliance work with best-staffed team | group-level DFG drift, cycle distributions, rework, appeals, Nova queue saturation | learns whether pilot effect scales to broader but still selected work | stop expansion if Nova queue_wait rises, cycle worsens, or quality alerts trigger |

Final recommendation: Do not implement an untargeted 50% Orion-to-Nova reassignment now. The supplied evidence is more consistent with a mainly confounded aggregate gap than a harmful Orion effect; within complexity strata Orion is not slower and has lower observed wait and service. However, the data are too sparse and selected to justify a large operational change. I recommend first running a small randomized pilot only in a genuine equipoise stratum—medium complexity, non-compliance, day/normal load—while separately attacking the plausible bottleneck: night compliance/queue capacity. Keep high-complexity, night, compliance-required work with the team best staffed for it until evidence shows safe transfer. Scale reassignment only if the pilot shows non-inferior cycle time and quality, and if queue diagnostics show Nova capacity can absorb the work.

def manual_dfg(sub):
    dfg = {}
    for tr in sub["trace"]:
        acts = tr.split(">")

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. Unobserved true case severity or risk beyond the coarse `complexity` field. Most valuable addition: a pre-routing case-risk score or classifier output recorded at `Classify`.

2. Open, abandoned, or censored cases are missing, so completion may be a collider. Most valuable addition: a case status field with entry date, completion indicator, and censoring time for all routed cases.

3. `wait_before_review_h` conflates classification queue, compliance queue, and other waiting. Most valuable addition: timestamped queue-entry/queue-exit and compliance start/finish events.

4. Reviewer identity, certification, and staffing capacity are not observed. Most valuable addition: reviewer ID plus certification level and shift-level staffing counts.

5. The routing rule and its eligibility criteria are unobserved. Most valuable addition: routing-rule version and assignment score/eligibility flag, or a randomized routing-pilot assignment field.
