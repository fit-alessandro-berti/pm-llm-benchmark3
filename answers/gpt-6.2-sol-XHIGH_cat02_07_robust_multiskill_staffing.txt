# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

**Observation.** Each case visits Intake and Validation once. ME and ES each generate two diagnostic visits and may run on two different resources or in sequence on a single cross-skilled resource. The clock runs from arrival to completed Validation, including breaks, outage time and overnight waits. The deadlines are 120, 480 and 1,440 elapsed minutes for P1, P2 and P3 respectively.

**Demand and service assumptions.** Use independent Poisson arrivals within each two-hour block, with rates equal to the expected counts divided by 120. Draw priority and type independently conditional on block and scenario. Assume both are available to dispatch at arrival. Treat each service range as uniform conditional on ordinary or tail class, with independent tail indicators and base-time draws by activity. Multiply the draw by the assigned worker's speed factor. Dispatch uses the mixture mean and actual progress observed so far. It never sees the hidden draw. In U1 and U5 rescale the remaining P2 and P3 shares in their original ratio. In U2 double the E and ES weights and renormalize the type mix at the original arrival rate. In U4 double the diagnostic tail probabilities only. These assumptions make the experiment reproducible. The prompt gives neither arrival dispersion nor dependence within and between cases.

**Calendar and cost assumptions.** Each replication starts empty, takes one day's arrivals, then drains the cohort on the repeating roster with no new arrivals. In an absence scenario Chen stays out during recovery. The U5 outage occurs once, suspends in-progress Intake and stops new Intake starts, while arrivals continue. Work holds its assignment across breaks and shift ends and resumes without a restart penalty from those calendar pauses. The comparison uses zero overtime under the 30-minute limit. For each resource change from Intake to a branch or from the last diagnostic owner to Validation, model a seven-minute transfer that reserves the recipient's time. Charge five additional minutes for a transfer between the M and E/S teams. For parallel ME, count one cross-team exchange at the join, so the off-resource join delay is 13 minutes for ME and eight for ES. Both branches can incur their own Intake handover. A diagnostic skill switch between consecutive cases means changing among M, E and S, even if an Intake or Validation intervenes. That setup reserves five minutes. A P1 interrupt adds 10% of the victim's remaining active service at the interruption, and the victim resumes with its original worker. A case can be the victim once across all its branches.

**Decisions.** At each release or availability change, choose the next case, the qualified resource, any planned hold or overflow, the order and common owner of sequential branches versus releasing a parallel pair, and whether to interrupt an active lower-priority diagnosis. Roster changes and authorized overtime can be separate planning variables. All policies must respect eligibility, exclusive worker occupancy, the calendar and the fork/join precedence.

- **SLA and tail objective.** For policy $\pi$, scenario $s$ and priority $p$, let $d_{p,s}(\pi)=\max(0,0.90-\Pr_s[T_p\le D_p])$. A P1-first objective minimizes the worst-scenario P1 deficit, then the worst-scenario P2 deficit, then the worst-scenario 90th percentile of elapsed time. Reporting the mean alongside those quantities reveals costs shifted to the rest of the queue.
- **Robust and fair objective.** Minimize $\max_s(5d_{1,s}+2d_{2,s}+d_{3,s})$, breaking close ties with worst-scenario recovery time and the Base spread of schedule-normalized workload. The weights give scarce service to P1 while assigning a material cost to P3 starvation. For planning, reject any Base staffing plan that projects more than 90% occupancy for an individual on regular hours without changing coverage or workload. Compare the fixed-roster policies as shortfall alternatives when that guardrail is infeasible. The simulation's observed occupancy is a stress indicator for this planning constraint.

# 3. Candidate policies

The home roster below provides the fixed queues and the Hybrid's overflow reference. Use the first listed resource on the gross shift and present that day. In M use both Ada and Ben when rostered. A home queue can wait during its assigned worker's break. For a normal change of owner, estimates include handover, setup and calendar time. A fixed worker ID resolves exact ties.

| Queue | Home order |
|---|---|
| Intake | Farah, Ada, Deepa, Emil |
| Mechanical | Ada and Ben, then Deepa, then Farah |
| Electrical | Deepa, Chen, Ada, Emil, Farah |
| Software | Before noon Chen, Farah, Emil. From noon Emil, Chen, Farah. |
| Validation | Ben, Deepa, Emil, Ada, Farah, Chen |

**Fixed specialization.** Dispatch only to the home queue's current coverage, with roster and absence relief following the table. Rank P1 by earliest deadline, then P2 and P3 by class and oldest case arrival. Use the earliest projected finish among eligible free home resources. Fork both branches to distinct workers, except when the two current fixed queues point to the same single worker, in which case keep the pair sequential on that person. A fork can spend time waiting for distinct eligible coverage. There is no workload overflow, protected slot or priority interruption. Fixed home assignments and sequential continuity govern handovers.

**Full Pool.** P1 takes precedence, then dispatch by earliest absolute due time, with queue entry and case ID as tie breakers. Each ready job can go to any free, on-duty, qualified person. Choose the earliest expected completion including calendars, setup and handovers, so keeping the owner is rewarded when it saves time. No slot is held and overflow is immediate. At Intake completion, compare the best projected two-worker join (including join overhead) with both sequential orders on every eligible common owner. Choose the split if earlier, otherwise lock the sequential pair to the selected worker. For a waiting P1 diagnostic, interrupt an active unpreempted P2/P3 case only if this improves the predicted diagnostic finish by at least 15 minutes over the best ordinary resource forecast. Resume the victim on its original worker.

**Hybrid.** Give P1 immediate access to all free qualified workers and use earliest deadline within P1. Next rank P3 that has aged 12 hours or more by deadline, then P2 and other P3 by class and deadline. For a lower priority, allow a flex worker when the stage queue has waited 20 minutes, the flex finish is at least 12 minutes earlier than the best home forecast, or that stage's predicted finish leaves at most 45 minutes to the case deadline. Rank remaining candidates by projected finish plus half a minute per projected busy minute beyond 90% of that person's daily scheduled net minutes. At dispatch, apply a rolling P1 reservation to the on-duty software anchor, Chen first in the morning and Emil first from noon. Use Farah as morning backup and the next on-duty qualified backup later. While a known P1 needing S is in Intake or has a ready S job, refrain from assigning work expected to occupy that anchor for more than 15 minutes to a lower priority. Also protect the anchor from non-S work over 15 minutes when other ready S work totals at least 30 unit-speed minutes, unless the candidate's remaining slack is at most 45 minutes. Otherwise release the slot. Select parallel as in Pool for P1 and require more than eight minutes of projected gain for P2/P3. Interrupt only for a P1 diagnostic whose forecast, with downstream allowance, comes within 10 minutes of its deadline or later, and only for a predicted gain of at least eight minutes. Preserve continuity on the sequential route and charge the same transfers and setup in all forecasts.

The branch forecast looks at currently assigned work and the calendar. It estimates diagnostic readiness and join time. The actual parallel workers are chosen as branches dispatch, with a distinct-worker constraint. Forecasts omit future competition from unassigned jobs and do not explicitly forecast Validation in the split decision. The interruption test approximates downstream work with expected Validation and a transfer, plus the other diagnostic for a first sequential branch. These simplifications make the decisions explainable but can choose the wrong path around a shift boundary or a large join queue.

# 5. Robust recommendation

Use Hybrid as the preferred routing policy for a guarded pilot with a capacity plan. Its trade-off is stronger protection of P3, lower workload spread than Fixed and less reliance on priority interruptions than Pool. Judge it on the maximum weighted SLA shortfall across Base and U1–U5, the cohort tail, and the Base occupancy guardrail. The very high occupancy in both flexible policies means a fairer workload split alone cannot deliver spare capacity. Treat the simulation as a comparison under scarce coverage, and require a separate check before committing to the SLA targets. U5 drives the maximum weighted loss for all three policies. It is 2.84 for Hybrid, 3.34 for Pool and 5.36 for Fixed. The worst-scenario P1 attainment estimates for Hybrid and Pool are 57.4% and 56.4%, a small difference given day variation. The recommendation depends on valuing the broader SLA shortfall, particularly P3.

Size the first capacity change from a deficit of about 480 usable Base worker-minutes at the 90% planning envelope, before overhead or speed loss. This corresponds to at least about 533 additional scheduled net minutes if all incremental productive capacity runs at unit speed with 90% occupancy. Check that new coverage actually matches the constrained skills and times. In particular, plan credible software relief before noon when Chen is unavailable and qualified late-day diagnostic and Validation capacity. An evening all-skill slot and a morning S relief slot solve different problems. Keep overtime within 30 minutes per worker and track it separately. These coverage changes and the suggested mode changes below are planning proposals outside the simulated comparison.

- **Urgent overflow.** If at least one P1 has under 30 minutes of predicted remaining slack and its earliest qualified start is over 15 minutes away, have the duty lead review the next 30-minute window for pooling. In a software absence or a slow restart from the U5 outage, compare Hybrid with the unrestricted Pool forecast, and switch the window to Pool if that improves the endangered P1's forecast by at least 10 minutes. Keep the one-interruption cap and release P1 Intake first on recovery. Escalate for qualified external coverage if no eligible resource can start early enough. Review P3 deadlines before each renewal and return to Hybrid after two 15-minute checks with no threatened P1 and S queue under 30 expected minutes.
- **Afternoon and close.** At 15:00 and 17:00 compare remaining forecast work including Validation and join releases with the next shift's usable qualified minutes. Trigger a coverage review if either E/S or Validation has over 30 forecast minutes more work than qualified capacity over the next hour, or any P1 has no qualified route by its due time. Assign a visible Validation owner when the join queue grows. Confirm who owns unfinished M before Deepa leaves.
- **Alternatives.** Pool is preferable if mean delay and P2 attainment dominate the loss function, or for a short rescue window when a reserved slot would otherwise strand a qualified worker. A well-provisioned, stable single-skill stream with reliable dedicated coverage could favor fixed queues for simpler accountability. The given surge, absence and late roster make fixed queues a poor default.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

| Risk | Control and measurement |
|---|---|
| Gaming service estimates or priority | Generate initial estimates from versioned skill, type and complexity rules. Permit reason-coded overrides and audit estimate error and priority changes by queue and team. Inspect workload and mix before judging workers on estimate variance. |
| P3 starvation | Show oldest P3 and its remaining deadline slack on the dispatch screen, apply the age promotion, and alert at 12 elapsed hours or earlier if the forecast already exceeds 24 hours. Track P3 attainment, tail and missed-case ages especially after urgent overflow. |
| Skill erosion | Record recent practice and quality by skill. Rotate supervised cross-skill work in planned slack and fund refreshers. Reassess backup multipliers and eligibility from completed work and quality checks. |
| Excessive preemption | Record the predicted P1 gain, victim, elapsed work and restart increment. Track interruptions and restart minutes by priority and worker. Review P1 incidents that preempted without beating their nonpreempted forecast, and monitor the victim SLA. |
| Coordination overhead and rework | Use one named case owner and a common branch-status checklist. Charge cost at the decision, then compare predicted and observed transfer/join times and reopened cases. Branch overlap alone gives no measure of join delay. |
| Validation bottleneck | Show branch-complete, join-ready and Validation-ready counts separately. Capacity freed by faster diagnostics can arrive as a wave at Validation. Monitor arrivals, completion rate, active service and queue age at this stage. |
| Fairness and interpretability | Show the ordered reasons for each assignment or hold, including skill, start estimate, continuity, priority, reserve and overflow. Audit net-shift utilization, overtime, missed breaks, restart burden and undesirable shift assignments separately. Record discretionary overrides and ask operators to replay representative cases. |

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

Capture a case ID, arrival, priority, type, eligibility for branches, SLA deadline and clock origin, final disposition and Validation completion. Keep original and revised priorities with timestamps and reasons. Give each Intake, branch and Validation a stable activity-instance ID and each active attempt or interval its own ID. Record lifecycle transitions for ready/queue entry, assignment decision, handover sent and accepted, setup start/end, active start, pause and reason, resume and finish. Record branch release, predecessor dependencies and join-ready time. Store both UTC timestamps and local roster context and preserve SLA elapsed time through pauses.

At decision time, log the qualified set, the on-duty or soon-available subset, excluded candidates with reasons (break, reservation, skill, branch lock), selected resource and actual skill, predicted start and finish, planned active service and its estimate version. Log actual active segment durations, remaining service estimate at pause, restart increment, transfer origin/destination and team, overtime and planned versus actual availability. Store the policy version, operating mode, reservation state, override actor/reason and scenario or shock flags including outage intervals. On production logs use event order or a decision ID to resolve timestamp ties.

- **Flow and conformance.** Format with PM4Py, compare discovered variants and process trees/Petri nets across matched blocks and policy versions, and use conformance checking against Intake → permitted diagnostic branches → Validation. Check a branch's start/finish and explicit dependencies before inferring concurrency or a missing join. A directly-follows graph (DFG) linearizes the observed case order, so a DFG edge between two branches can arise purely from completion order. Use resource and permit checks and interval intersection for actual overlap.
- **Timing and bottleneck migration.** Use PM4Py interval-aware activity service summaries and performance DFGs stratified by type, priority, block, worker and policy. Treat DFG edge time as elapsed between the chosen neighboring events. Compute actual waiting directly from readiness to assignment and assignment to active start, and report pause/restart and join delay separately. Plot queue length, age and workload against on-duty skill supply. A growing V queue with stable V active service points to a capacity or release-pattern change, while an increase in like-for-like V service suggests a service-time change. Compare observed multiplier-adjusted duration distributions, tail rates and pause reasons across versions and shocks.
- **Transfers and total time.** Use PM4Py's handover-of-work network to screen for changing resource adjacency, then verify true transfers with predecessor IDs and recorded sender/recipient. Parallel siblings and split intervals can otherwise produce adjacency unrelated to ownership. On an active-segment input, the case service field in the tested PM4Py calculation sums durations across segments, including simultaneous work. Case sojourn spans earliest logged start to latest end and omits time before the first start unless logged. Its waiting residual equals sojourn minus summed service and can be negative with overlapping intervals. Compute an active-time union if elapsed case time without active work is wanted, and use actual queue events for stage waits. SLA reporting uses case arrival and completed Validation with the full priority-specific denominator.

On a dashboard report SLA and sample size by priority, mean and p90 elapsed, late-case count and case aging, by-stage queue p90 and utilization by worker/block. Add workload spread, percent of shifts above 90%, missed breaks, overtime, reserve held/released time, overflow and preemption counts, restart minutes, transfer/setup minutes, parallel gain and join time, backlog at shift close and recovery time after flags. Use mature cohorts and report incomplete or censored cases explicitly.

DAY, CLOSE, EPS = 1440, 720, 1e-7
NAMES = {"I": "Intake", "M": "Mechanical", "E": "Electrical",
         "S": "Software", "V": "Validation"}

def demand_row(row, shock):
    rate = row["arrivals"]
    priority = [row[p] / 100 for p in TARGET]
    mix = [row[k] / 100 for k in ("M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES")]
    if shock.get("surge") and row["start"] < 240:
        rate *= 1.7
        old_tail = sum(priority[1:])
        priority = [priority[0] + .08] + [p * (old_tail - .08) / old_tail
                                          for p in priority[1:]]
    if shock.get("mix") and 240 <= row["start"] < 480:
        mix[1] *= 2
        mix[4] *= 2
        mix = [p / sum(mix) for p in mix]
    return rate, priority, mix

def case_inputs(shock, seed):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    cases = []
    for _, row in DEMAND.iterrows():
        rate, priority, mix = demand_row(row, shock)
        offset = rng.expovariate(rate / 120)
        while offset < 120:
            kind = rng.choices(("M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"), weights=mix)[0]
            prio = rng.choices(tuple(TARGET), weights=priority)[0]
            draws = {}
            for skill, (lo, hi, tail_lo, tail_hi, probability) in SERVICE.items():
                if shock.get("tail") and skill in DIAG:
                    probability *= 2
                bounds = (tail_lo, tail_hi) if rng.random() < probability else (lo, hi)
                draws[skill] = rng.uniform(*bounds)
            cases.append((row["start"] + offset, prio, kind, draws))
            offset += rng.expovariate(rate / 120)
    return cases

@dataclass
class Case:
    number: int
    arrival: float
    priority: int
    kind: str
    draws: dict
    due: float = field(init=False)
    state: str = "I"
    mode: str = "single"
    second: str = ""
    lock: str = ""
    owners: dict = field(default_factory=dict)
    finished: set = field(default_factory=set)
    preempted: bool = False
    done: float = math.inf

    def __post_init__(self):
        self.due = self.arrival + TARGET[self.priority]

@dataclass
class Job:
    case: Case
    skill: str
    ready: float
    source: str = ""
    previous_skill: str = ""
    owner: str = ""
    phases: list = field(default_factory=list)
    worked: float = 0.0
    planned: float = 0.0
    restart_estimate: float = 0.0

@dataclass
class Worker:
    name: str
    windows: tuple
    capacity: float
    job: object = None
    segment: object = None
    last_diagnostic: tuple = ("", -1)
    busy: float = 0.0
    day_busy: float = 0.0

def worker_calendar(name):
    start, end, pause = SHIFT[name]
    end += OVERTIME
    return ((start, pause[0]), (pause[1], end)) if pause else ((start, end),)

def is_open(worker, now):
    return any(lo <= now % DAY < hi for lo, hi in worker.windows)

def finish_on_calendar(worker, start, work):
    day = math.floor(start / DAY) * DAY
    while True:
        for lo, hi in worker.windows:
            begin = max(start, day + lo)
            if begin < day + hi:
                span = min(work, day + hi - begin)
                work -= span
                if work <= EPS:
                    return begin + span
        day += DAY

def family(skill):
    return "M" if skill == "M" else "ES" if skill in ("E", "S") else ""

class Run:
    def __init__(self, policy, scenario, seed, capture=False):
        self.policy, self.scenario = policy, scenario
        self.shock = SCENARIOS[scenario]
        self.cases = [Case(i, *row) for i, row in
                      enumerate(case_inputs(self.shock, seed))]
        self.workers = {}
        for name in SHIFT:
            if name != self.shock.get("absent"):
                windows = worker_calendar(name)
                self.workers[name] = Worker(name, windows,
                                            sum(hi - lo for lo, hi in windows))
        self.speed = {(r, k): float(SPEED.at[r, k]) for r in self.workers
                      for k in SERVICE if pd.notna(SPEED.at[r, k])}
        self.qualified = {k: [r for r in self.workers if (r, k) in self.speed]
                          for k in SERVICE}
        self.means = {}
        for k, (a, b, c, d, p) in SERVICE.items():
            if self.shock.get("tail") and k in DIAG:
                p *= 2
            self.means[k] = ((1 - p) * (a + b) + p * (c + d)) / 2
        self.pending, self.events, self.assignments = [], [], []
        self.now, self.next_case = 0., 0
        self.capture = capture
        self.burden = self.coordination = 0.

    def primary(self, skill):
        hour = self.now % DAY
        # Fixed coverage changes with roster time, absence and scheduled relief.
        orders = {
            "I": ("Farah", "Ada", "Deepa", "Emil"),
            "M": ("Ada", "Ben", "Deepa", "Farah"),
            "E": ("Deepa", "Chen", "Ada", "Emil", "Farah"),
            "S": ("Chen", "Farah", "Emil") if hour < 240
                 else ("Emil", "Chen", "Farah"),
            "V": ("Ben", "Deepa", "Emil", "Ada", "Farah", "Chen"),
        }
        present = [r for r in orders[skill] if r in self.workers
                   and SHIFT[r][0] <= hour < SHIFT[r][1]]
        if skill == "M" and any(r in present for r in ("Ada", "Ben")):
            return [r for r in present if r in ("Ada", "Ben")]
        return present[:1]

    def eligible(self, job):
        if job.owner:
            return [job.owner]
        case = job.case
        if job.skill in DIAG and case.lock:
            return [case.lock]
        options = self.qualified[job.skill]
        if self.policy == "Fixed":
            options = self.primary(job.skill)
        if job.skill in DIAG and case.mode == "parallel":
            options = [r for r in options if r not in case.owners.values()]
        return options

    def pieces(self, job, name):
        handover = 0
        if job.source and job.source != name:
            handover = HANDOVER
            if {family(job.previous_skill), family(job.skill)} == {"M", "ES"}:
                handover += TEAM
        skill, case = self.workers[name].last_diagnostic
        setup = SETUP if (job.skill in DIAG and skill and case != job.case.number
                          and skill != job.skill) else 0
        return handover, setup

    def expected(self, job, name):
        return self.means[job.skill] * self.speed[name, job.skill]

    def estimate(self, job, name, release=None, replace=False):
        w = self.workers[name]
        start = max(self.now, job.ready, release if release is not None else self.now)
        if w.job is not None and not replace:
            current = w.job
            prep = sum(left for act, left in current.phases if act in
                       ("Handover", "Setup"))
            # In-flight estimate uses known prep and elapsed work, no hidden draw.
            remaining = prep + max(1., current.planned + current.restart_estimate
                                   - current.worked)
            start = max(start, finish_on_calendar(w, self.now, remaining))
        work = (max(1., job.planned + job.restart_estimate - job.worked) if job.owner else
                self.expected(job, name) + sum(self.pieces(job, name)))
        return finish_on_calendar(w, start, work)

    def branch_jobs(self, intake):
        case = intake.case
        case.state = "D"
        first, second = (Job(case, k, self.now, intake.owner, "I")
                         for k in case.kind) if len(case.kind) == 2 else (None, None)
        if first is None:
            self.pending.append(Job(case, case.kind, self.now, intake.owner, "I"))
            return
        if self.policy == "Fixed":
            a, b = [self.primary(j.skill) for j in (first, second)]
            if len(a) == len(b) == 1 and a == b:
                case.mode, case.lock, case.second = "sequential", a[0], second.skill
            else:
                case.mode = "parallel"
        else:
            join = self.join_cost(case)
            pairs = [(max(self.estimate(first, a), self.estimate(second, b)) + join)
                     for a, b in product(self.qualified[first.skill],
                                         self.qualified[second.skill]) if a != b]
            parallel = min(pairs, default=math.inf)
            sequential = []
            for start, end in ((first, second), (second, first)):
                for r in self.qualified[start.skill]:
                    if (r, end.skill) in self.speed:
                        finish = self.estimate(start, r)
                        end_finish = finish_on_calendar(self.workers[r], finish,
                                                        self.expected(end, r))
                        sequential.append((end_finish, r, start.skill, end.skill))
            seq_finish, name, start, end = min(sequential)
            gain = 8 if self.policy == "Hybrid" and case.priority != 1 else 0
            if seq_finish > parallel + gain:
                case.mode = "parallel"
            else:
                case.mode, case.lock, case.second = "sequential", name, end
                if start != first.skill:
                    first, second = second, first
        self.pending.append(first)
        if case.mode == "parallel":
            self.pending.append(second)

    @staticmethod
    def join_cost(case):
        return JOIN + (TEAM if case.kind == "ME" else 0)

    def complete(self, job):
        case = job.case
        if job.skill == "I":
            self.branch_jobs(job)
        elif job.skill == "V":
            case.done, case.state = self.now, "done"
        else:
            case.finished.add(job.skill)
            if len(case.finished) == len(case.kind):
                join = self.join_cost(case) if case.mode == "parallel" else 0
                self.coordination += join
                case.state = "V"
                self.pending.append(Job(case, "V", self.now + join, job.owner, job.skill))
            elif case.mode == "sequential":
                self.pending.append(Job(case, case.second, self.now, job.owner, job.skill))

    def rank(self, job):
        case = job.case
        if case.priority == 1 or self.policy == "Pool":
            return (case.priority != 1, case.due, job.ready, case.number)
        if self.policy == "Hybrid" and case.priority == 3 and self.now - case.arrival >= 720:
            return (1, case.due, job.ready, case.number)
        secondary = case.arrival if self.policy == "Fixed" else case.due
        return (case.priority, secondary, job.ready, case.number)

    def reserve(self):
        preference = ("Chen", "Farah", "Emil") if self.now % DAY < 240 else (
            "Emil", "Chen", "Farah")
        return next((r for r in preference if r in self.workers
                     and is_open(self.workers[r], self.now)), "")

    def select(self, job, eligible):
        case = job.case
        free = [r for r in eligible if not self.workers[r].job
                and is_open(self.workers[r], self.now)]
        if not free:
            return None
        if self.policy != "Hybrid" or case.priority == 1 or job.owner:
            return min(free, key=lambda r: (self.estimate(job, r), r))
        home = set(self.primary(job.skill))
        home_finish = min((self.estimate(job, r) for r in eligible if r in home),
                          default=math.inf)
        reserve = self.reserve()
        urgent_software = any(c.priority == 1 and "S" in c.kind and c.state == "I"
                              for c in self.cases[:self.next_case])
        software_queue = [j for j in self.pending if j.skill == "S"
                          and j.ready <= self.now and j is not job]
        urgent_software |= any(j.case.priority == 1 for j in software_queue)
        software_minutes = sum(self.means["S"] for j in software_queue)

        scores = []
        for r in free:
            finish = self.estimate(job, r)
            age = self.now - job.ready
            work = self.expected(job, r) + sum(self.pieces(job, r))
            if r not in home and not (age >= 20 or finish + 12 <= home_finish
                                      or case.due - finish <= 45):
                continue
            if r == reserve and work > 15 and (
                urgent_software or (job.skill != "S" and software_minutes >= 30
                                    and case.due - finish > 45)):
                continue
            w = self.workers[r]
            above_target = max(0., w.day_busy + work - .90 * w.capacity)
            scores.append((finish + .5 * above_target, r))
        return min(scores)[1] if scores else None

    def assign(self, job, name, eligible):
        if self.capture:
            self.assignments.append({
                "case": job.case.number, "stage": job.skill,
                "queue_entry": job.ready, "assignment": self.now,
                "eligible": ",".join(eligible), "resource": name,
                "resumed": bool(job.owner), "queue_minutes": self.now - job.ready,
                "priority": job.case.priority, "deadline": job.case.due,
                "policy": self.policy, "version": POLICY_VERSION,
                "scenario": self.scenario,
            })
        if not job.owner:
            handover, setup = self.pieces(job, name)
            job.planned = self.expected(job, name)
            job.owner = name
            job.phases = [[act, minutes] for act, minutes in
                          (("Handover", handover), ("Setup", setup),
                           (NAMES[job.skill], job.case.draws[job.skill] *
                            self.speed[name, job.skill])) if minutes > 0]
            self.burden += handover + setup
            if job.skill in DIAG:
                job.case.owners[job.skill] = name
        self.pending.remove(job)
        self.workers[name].job = job

    def flush(self, w, paused_by=""):
        if w.segment is not None:
            if self.capture and self.now - w.segment > EPS:
                j = w.job
                self.events.append({
                    "case:concept:name": str(j.case.number),
                    "concept:name": j.phases[0][0],
                    "org:resource": w.name, "start": w.segment, "end": self.now,
                    "skill": j.skill, "planned_stage_minutes": j.planned,
                    "actual_segment_minutes": self.now - w.segment,
                    "transfer_from": j.source if j.phases[0][0] == "Handover" else "",
                    "priority": j.case.priority, "policy": self.policy,
                    "scenario": self.scenario, "branch_mode": j.case.mode,
                    "version": POLICY_VERSION, "paused_by": paused_by,
                    "arrival_minute": j.case.arrival, "deadline_minute": j.case.due,
                })
            w.segment = None

    def preempt(self, ready):
        if self.policy == "Fixed":
            return
        for job in ready:
            if job not in self.pending or job.case.priority != 1 or job.skill not in DIAG:
                continue
            eligibility = self.eligible(job)
            if not eligibility:
                continue
            normal = min(self.estimate(job, r) for r in eligibility)
            downstream = self.means["V"] + HANDOVER
            if job.case.mode == "sequential" and not job.case.finished:
                downstream += self.means[job.case.second]
            if self.policy == "Hybrid" and normal + downstream <= job.case.due - 10:
                continue
            choices = []
            for r in eligibility:
                w = self.workers[r]
                victim = w.job
                if (not is_open(w, self.now) or w.segment is None or not victim
                    or victim.skill not in DIAG or victim.case.priority == 1 or victim.case.preempted
                    or victim.phases[0][0] != NAMES[victim.skill]):
                    continue
                finish = self.estimate(job, r, replace=True)
                if normal - finish >= (15 if self.policy == "Pool" else 8):
                    choices.append((finish, r))
            if choices:
                _, name = min(choices)
                w = self.workers[name]
                victim = w.job
                self.flush(w, "P1")
                victim.case.preempted = True
                victim.restart_estimate = RESTART * max(0., victim.planned - victim.worked)
                victim.phases[0][1] *= (1 + RESTART)
                victim.ready = self.now
                self.pending.append(victim)
                self.assign(job, name, eligibility)

    def simulate(self):
        last_arrival = len(self.cases)
        while self.next_case < last_arrival or self.pending or any(
                w.job for w in self.workers.values()):
            t = self.now
            if t % DAY == 0:
                for w in self.workers.values():
                    w.day_busy = 0.
            while self.next_case < last_arrival and self.cases[self.next_case].arrival <= t:
                case = self.cases[self.next_case]
                self.pending.append(Job(case, "I", case.arrival))
                self.next_case += 1

            for w in self.workers.values():
                if w.job and w.job.phases[0][1] <= EPS:
                    self.flush(w)
                    w.job.phases.pop(0)
                    if not w.job.phases:
                        job, w.job = w.job, None
                        self.complete(job)
                if not is_open(w, t):
                    self.flush(w, "calendar")

            outage = self.shock.get("outage", (-1, -1))
            ready = sorted((j for j in self.pending if j.ready <= t and
                            not (j.skill == "I" and outage[0] <= t < outage[1])),
                           key=self.rank)
            for j in ready:
                eligible = self.eligible(j)
                name = self.select(j, eligible)
                if name:
                    self.assign(j, name, eligible)
            self.preempt(ready)

            day = math.floor(t / DAY) * DAY
            candidates = [day + DAY]
            candidates += [day + x for w in self.workers.values()
                           for window in w.windows for x in window if day + x > t]
            candidates += [x for x in outage if x > t]
            candidates += [j.ready for j in self.pending if j.ready > t]
            # Wake even when the only change is a Hybrid queue-age threshold.
            if self.policy == "Hybrid":
                candidates += [j.ready + 20 for j in self.pending if j.ready + 20 > t]
                candidates += [j.case.arrival + 720 for j in self.pending
                               if j.case.arrival + 720 > t and j.case.priority == 3]
            if self.next_case < last_arrival:
                candidates.append(self.cases[self.next_case].arrival)

            active = []
            for w in self.workers.values():
                if not w.job or not is_open(w, t):
                    continue
                j = w.job
                if j.skill == "I" and outage[0] <= t < outage[1]:
                    self.flush(w, "IT outage")
                    continue
                if w.segment is None:
                    w.segment = t
                    if j.skill in DIAG and j.phases[0][0] == NAMES[j.skill]:
                        w.last_diagnostic = (j.skill, j.case.number)
                active.append(w)
                candidates.append(t + j.phases[0][1])
            self.now = min(candidates)
            assert self.now < 30 * DAY, "Uncleared cohort or routing deadlock"
            for w in active:
                elapsed = self.now - t
                w.job.phases[0][1] -= elapsed
                if w.job.phases[0][0] == NAMES[w.job.skill]:
                    w.job.worked += elapsed
                w.busy += max(0., min(self.now, CLOSE) - t)
                w.day_busy += elapsed

        delays = [{"priority": c.priority, "delay": c.done - c.arrival,
                   "sla": c.done <= c.due + EPS} for c in self.cases]
        utilities = [100 * w.busy / w.capacity for w in self.workers.values()]
        day = {
            "max_util": max(utilities), "spread": max(utilities) - min(utilities),
            "burden": self.burden / max(1, last_arrival),
            "join": self.coordination / max(1, last_arrival),
            "preempts": sum(c.preempted for c in self.cases),
            "open_20": sum(c.done > CLOSE + EPS for c in self.cases),
            "clear_h": max([0.] + [(c.done - CLOSE) / 60 for c in self.cases]),
        }
        return delays, day

def offered_load():
    result = []
    for scenario, shock in SCENARIOS.items():
        model = Run("Pool", scenario, 0)
        for _, row in DEMAND.iterrows():
            rate, priority, (m, e, s, me, es) = demand_row(row, shock)
            visits = dict(zip("IMESV", (1, m + me, e + me + es, s + es, 1)))
            loads = {k: rate * p * model.means[k] for k, p in visits.items()}
            result.append(dict(scenario=scenario, block=int(row["start"]), n=rate,
                               P1=rate * priority[0], **loads,
                               total=sum(loads.values())))
    return pd.DataFrame(result)

def compare(seeds=range(48)):
    rows = []
    for scenario, policy in product(SCENARIOS, POLICIES):
        case_frames, day_rows = [], []
        for seed in seeds:
            cases, stats = Run(policy, scenario, seed).simulate()
            frame = pd.DataFrame(cases)
            frame["seed"] = seed
            case_frames.append(frame)
            day_rows.append(stats)
        cases = pd.concat(case_frames, ignore_index=True)
        days = pd.DataFrame(day_rows)
        sla = 100 * cases.groupby("priority")["sla"].mean()
        p1_days = 100 * cases[cases.priority == 1].groupby("seed")["sla"].mean()
        seed_means = cases.groupby("seed")["delay"].mean()
        rows.append({
            "scenario": scenario, "policy": policy,
            "mean_delay": cases.delay.mean(), "p90_delay": cases.delay.quantile(.90),
            "P1_SLA": sla.get(1, math.nan), "P2_SLA": sla.get(2, math.nan),
            "P3_SLA": sla.get(3, math.nan), "P1_n": (cases.priority == 1).sum(),
            "max_util": days.max_util.mean(), "spread": days.spread.mean(),
            "burden": days.burden.mean(), "join": days["join"].mean(),
            "preempts": days.preempts.mean(), "open_20": days.open_20.mean(),
            "clear_h": days.clear_h.mean(), "clear_p90_h": days.clear_h.quantile(.90),
            "daily_mean_q10": seed_means.quantile(.10),
            "daily_mean_q90": seed_means.quantile(.90),
            "daily_P1_q10": p1_days.quantile(.10), "daily_P1_q90": p1_days.quantile(.90),
        })
    summary = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    print(summary.iloc[:, :15].round(1).to_string(index=False))
    print(summary[["scenario", "policy", "clear_p90_h", "daily_mean_q10",
                   "daily_mean_q90", "daily_P1_q10", "daily_P1_q90"]]
          .round(1).to_string(index=False))
    return summary

def mining_example():
    import pm4py

    sample = Run("Hybrid", "Base", 0, capture=True)
    sample.simulate()
    intervals = pd.DataFrame(sample.events)
    origin = pd.Timestamp("2000-01-03 08:00", tz="UTC")
    intervals["start_timestamp"] = origin + pd.to_timedelta(intervals.start, unit="m")
    intervals["time:timestamp"] = origin + pd.to_timedelta(intervals.end, unit="m")
    log = pm4py.format_dataframe(intervals, start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")
    active = log[log["concept:name"].isin(NAMES.values())].copy()
    case_spans = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(
        active, start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")
    print("PM4Py case interval aggregates in seconds")
    print(case_spans[["case:concept:name", "@@service_time", "@@waiting_time",
                      "@@sojourn_time"]].drop_duplicates().head().to_string(index=False))
    print("PM4Py mean active segment length in seconds",
          pm4py.get_service_time(active, start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp"))
    network = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(active)
    print("Trace-adjacency handover edge count", len(network.connections))
    # Actual stage service sums split segments by stage. Queue waits come from decisions.
    stage = (active.groupby(["case:concept:name", "skill"])["actual_segment_minutes"]
             .sum().groupby("skill").mean())
    queue = pd.DataFrame(sample.assignments)
    print("Stage mean active minutes", stage.round(1).to_dict())
    print("Queue minutes per dispatch, by stage and whether resuming")
    print(queue.groupby(["stage", "resumed"]).queue_minutes.agg(["mean", "max"])
          .round(1).to_string())
    print("Recorded transfer segments by sender and recipient")
    print(log[log["concept:name"] == "Handover"]
          .groupby(["transfer_from", "org:resource"]).actual_segment_minutes
          .sum().round(1).to_string())
    return log, queue

# 9. Limits and validation plan

These estimates depend on arrivals, duration shapes, correlations, calendars and handover conventions that the averages and ranges do not identify. Bursts within blocks, positive correlation among diagnostic tails in a complex case, priority-type dependence, and repeated shocks can substantially change percentile and deadline results. Some recovery assumptions act in opposite directions. Starting empty and omitting the next day's arrivals frees capacity, while keeping Chen absent during clearance delays recovery if the absence is in fact one day. Recipient-blocking handovers and keeping work with its original owner overnight can also be pessimistic. Sensitivity-test transfer as a nonblocking elapsed lag, E/S as one setup family, one-day absence, qualified handoff at shift end, clustered arrivals, correlated tails, U2 as additional volume and ongoing arrivals with carryover. None of the simulation percentages are forecasts with empirical confidence intervals.

1. **Instrument and backtest.** Verify lifecycle, shift, outage and SLA clocks against a sample of incident records. Replay the same arrival streams and calendars for each policy with paired service draws calibrated by skill, type and worker. Use observed active service, with censoring and restart adjustments, for calibration. Historical assignments affect what services and durations are observed. Treat replay estimates for assignments with poor historical coverage as uncertain and report sensitivity bands. Validate on held-out days including absences or incidents where available. Bootstrap whole days or runs for paired differences and retain the separate P1, P2 and P3 denominators.
2. **Shadow dispatch first.** Compare recommended assignments, forecasts, overrides, and effects on eligible capacity and threatened P1s without changing live routing. Walk through short roster, IT outage with P1 arrivals, and double-tail scenarios with the duty lead. Fix any precursor such as a missing qualification, unlogged transfer or false assurance about deadline slack.
3. **Pilot with protected P1.** Keep current P1 incident command, engineer reliable backup coverage and rehearse escalation before a live trial. Compare bounded Hybrid and Pool windows for lower-priority decisions on matched shifts with the same coverage and day-level assignment where feasible, since queues share workers. Hold out P1 from experimental dispatch and keep its access to any qualified worker. Automatically pause the experiment for a P1 with no feasible route or forecast slack under 15 minutes, an eligibility violation, or a projected Base shift above the 90% planning limit. Record overrides and intention-to-treat outcomes. Stop the window rather than waiting for a missed SLA to confirm a hazard.
4. **Decide against predeclared gates.** Size the trial for precision at the day level and for enough P1 observations to assess a 90% target. Check P1 non-inferiority with a predeclared priority-appropriate margin before exposing P1 to a new policy. Require evidence that the staffed plan can meet all three SLA targets while respecting the Base utilization guardrail, including a credible stress-recovery plan. If the capacity gap persists, report the attainable service level and required qualified coverage before any wider rollout.
