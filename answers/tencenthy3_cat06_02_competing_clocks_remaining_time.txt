## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

*Calculation shortcuts:* triangular means are used only for within-branch service approximations; queue waits are estimated from analyst-hours (2 analysts × remaining open time) and from QA reviewer-hours. QA arrivals are treated as a wide band to reflect overdispersion. The parallel join always uses a **maximum**, not a sum.

**Smooth path**
- Chemistry waiting/service: Four samples ahead plus current work. Under FIFO, ~8–9 h of ahead work remains for Monday, so B-417 starts Monday 12:15–13:00 and finishes Monday 15:00–16:00. Under risk-priority, it starts Friday ~16:42, works 1.25 h before 18:00, resumes Monday 08:00, and finishes Monday 09:00–10:00. Chemistry usable window: **Mon 09:30–16:00**.
- Microbiology incubation/readout: Primary incubation ready Sunday 12:00–20:00. Without on-call (baseline), readout Monday 08:30–09:30. With on-call, usable Sunday 13:00–23:00. 
- Parallel join: `max(chem Mon 09:30–16:00, micro Mon 08:30–09:30)` = **Mon 09:30–16:00**. Not a sum.
- QA waiting: Five batches now; weekend QA clears ~2–3; arrivals ~0–8 by Monday. Two reviewers Monday => wait 0–6 h (central 2–4 h).
- QA service: 1.5–3.5 h.
- Final release: +0.25–0.75 h. Terminal: best Monday late morning; central Monday afternoon–Tuesday midday.

**Chemistry rerun**
- Initial chemistry same as smooth; first usable result Mon 09:30–16:00.
- Rerun triggered (P0.10–0.20), gets priority, takes triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) h. If first result is late (>~16:00), rerun pauses at 18:00 and resumes Tuesday 08:00.
- Microbiology: same as smooth (Mon 08:30–09:30, or Sun if on-call).
- Join: `max(chem rerun usable, micro usable)` = Mon 12:00–18:00 or Tue 10:00–12:00.
- QA wait/service as above; if join Tuesday, QA finishes Tuesday afternoon or Wednesday if congested.

**Delayed microbiology**
- Subcase A – no on-call (baseline): micro usable Monday 08:30–09:30; join dominated by chemistry as in smooth path.
- Subcase B – contamination indication: primary readout Monday 09:30 (or Sunday if on-call); extra 20–30 h continuous incubation + 0.5–1.5 h readout. Without on-call, usable **Tuesday 08:30–15:30**; with on-call, usable Monday 09:30–19:00. Join = `max(chem Mon, micro Tue)` = Tuesday in the no-on-call contamination case. Weekend pause is the dominant delay if no on-call.

**QA query / deviation investigation tail**
- Join assumed as above (Mon–Tue).
- Documentation query (P0.15–0.30) adds 4–12 working hours; if QA review starts Monday, resume Tuesday 08:00–12:00.
- Deviation investigation if opened: doc-only (60–80%) adds 8–20 working hours; lab investigation (20–40%) adds 3–7 calendar days. 
- Because these are working-hour or calendar-day additions, the join-to-release clock is not a simple sum of means but a sequenced calendar pause.

**Correlated temperature-excursion tail**
- If temperature excursion confirmed, chem rerun probability and micro contamination probability both rise; deviation investigation probability rises to ~0.30–0.55.
- Joint effect: chemistry may rerun and microbiology may need confirmation, so join slips to Tuesday–Wednesday. Investigation is more likely, and if lab investigation occurs, 3–7 calendar days are added.
- Probabilities are treated as dependent, so this tail is larger than the product of independent anomalies.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Chemistry queue discipline and Friday residual capacity** — mainly *queue uncertainty* (with calendar pause). Shifts chemistry usable time by ~3–8 h.
2. **Microbiology weekend on-call authorization / staffing calendar** — mainly *calendar uncertainty* (with path). Determines Sunday vs Monday vs Tuesday usability; can shift join by hours to ~2 days if contamination occurs.
3. **Temperature-excursion confirmation and induced correlation** — mainly *dependence uncertainty*. Raises rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities jointly, creating the severe tail.
4. **QA queue at future join** — mainly *queue uncertainty*. Adds 0–10 h wait and can force an overnight/weekend pause.
5. **QA rework type (documentation query vs deviation / lab investigation)** — mainly *path uncertainty* (with calendar for working hours). Adds 4 working hours up to 7 calendar days if a laboratory investigation is opened.
