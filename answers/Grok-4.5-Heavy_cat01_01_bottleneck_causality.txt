## 1. Executive causal assessment
Provisional verdict: mainly confounded. Confidence: 62.

The strongest evidence for this verdict is the clear Simpson’s reversal: Orion’s crude mean cycle time is 21.96 h versus Nova’s 15.24 h, yet within every observed complexity stratum Orion’s point estimate is lower (High 31.57 vs 35.00, Medium 17.17 vs 19.38, Low 7.50 vs 9.64). A complexity-standardized contrast using the overall case-mix weights yields an approximately 2.5 h advantage for Orion. This pattern is reinforced by process facts: the routing engine preferentially assigns difficult cases to Orion (higher certified-reviewer pool), Orion handles nearly all night work, and compliance_required is almost collinear with complexity. Against the verdict are residual imbalances (Orion 93 % night, high arrival_load concentration), thin cells (High-Nova n=2, Low-Orion n=2), possible unobserved case risk that survives recorded complexity, and the fact that only completed cases are observed. Stratification therefore reduces but does not eliminate confounding; it does not identify a pure causal effect of the resource-group label.

The proposed 50 % reassignment of future Orion cases to Nova is likely to fail or backfire. Because Orion’s case mix is systematically harder and more night-heavy, the move would transfer precisely the cases for which Nova shows longer stratified times and for which Nova has limited certified capacity and night coverage. Crude mean cycle time for the remaining Orion portfolio would fall (selection effect), creating an illusory improvement, while the transferred cases and overall system load could lengthen, raise rework, or degrade the coarse 30-day quality signal. Any genuine skill or capacity advantage of Orion would be diluted without corresponding reallocation of reviewers or night staffing.

## 6. Intervention and learning plan
| phase | intervention | target mechanism | assignment unit | safeguards | process-mining measurements | causal learning value | stop or rollback rule |
|-------|--------------|------------------|-----------------|------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | Purely observational: exact/propensity matching on complexity (and shift where positive), overlap & positivity diagnostics, complexity-standardized contrasts, DFG/variant comparison | Confounding by case mix; residual shift/load imbalance; control-flow differences | Existing completed cases | No operational change; results internal only | Cycle, wait, service, rework by matched sets; support counts per cell; group-specific DFGs and variant frequencies; residual cycle − (wait + service) | Quantifies how much of the crude gap survives complexity adjustment; maps positivity failures; supplies baseline for later pilots | None (diagnostic only); if positivity remains near-zero for key cells, escalate caution on any reassignment |
| 2 | Small randomized or quasi-random routing pilot on Medium-complexity cases (region of better potential overlap) | Direct group effect versus residual confounding; skill versus capacity | 20–40 successive Medium cases arriving when both groups have capacity; 50 % random reassignment to the alternate group | Cap pilot volume; real-time quality and cycle monitoring; pre-specified escalation to senior reviewer; retain original group’s certified capacity | Per-arm cycle, wait, service, rework, 30-day outcome; DFG conformance; queue-length snapshots if available | Discriminates “mainly confounded / beneficial skill” from pure label or harmful night effects under partial overlap; supplies experimental contrast | Stop if pilot-arm mean cycle exceeds control by >15 % or any appeal spike; full rollback of assignment rule within 48 h |
| 3 | Capacity-focused intervention: increase night parallel capacity or streamline/parallelize compliance for high-complexity cases; leave Orion/Nova assignment rates unchanged | Queueing and compliance-mediated wait (the largest stratified component) | Night/high-load periods or high-complexity cases system-wide | Capacity added before any volume increase; monitor Nova day load for spill-over; quality gates unchanged | Wait_before_review distributions, compliance-step sojourn (if instrumented), overall and stratum-specific cycle, DFG edge frequencies for Compliance Check → Review | Tests whether queue/compliance mechanisms, rather than the group label itself, drive performance; informs whether label reassignment is even necessary | Rollback added capacity if wait reduction <10 % after two weeks or if secondary queues (day Nova) lengthen materially |

Final recommendation: Do not implement the proposed 50 % reassignment of Orion cases. The data pattern is the classic signature of confounding by case mix (and correlated shift/compliance), not of a harmful causal effect of the Orion label. Blindly moving the harder, night-heavy cases onto Nova risks lengthening those cases, overloading Nova’s thinner certified and night capacity, and producing a misleading improvement in Orion’s remaining crude average. Instead execute the three-phase plan: first lock down the observational diagnostics and positivity map; second run a tightly bounded randomized pilot only on Medium cases where overlap is least catastrophic; third attack the dominant measured component (queue and compliance wait) by adding capacity or parallelizing the compliance step while leaving group assignment rates untouched. Only after these steps produce clear experimental or quasi-experimental contrasts should any permanent change in routing volumes be considered. The plan simultaneously limits operational risk and generates the very data needed to discriminate the competing causal explanations.

    print("=" * 70)

    print("=" * 70)
    strat = df.groupby(["complexity", "group"])[["cycle_time_h", "wait_before_review_h", "review_service_h", "rework_count"]].agg(["mean", "count"])

            print(f"WARNING: complexity={cx}, group={g} has only {n} cases")

    print("   Common weighting distribution = overall observed complexity proportions")

    std_nova = (means["Nova"] * overall_props).sum()
    print(f"Standardized Orion mean cycle: {std_orion:.2f} h")
    print(f"Standardized Nova  mean cycle: {std_nova:.2f} h")
    print(f"Standardized difference (Orion - Nova): {std_orion - std_nova:.2f} h")
    print()

    print("=" * 70)
    print("4. OVERLAP COUNTS: complexity × shift × resource_group")
    print("=" * 70)
    overlap = pd.crosstab([df["complexity"], df["shift"]], df["group"])
    print(overlap)
    for (cx, sh), row in overlap.iterrows():
        for g in ["Orion", "Nova"]:
            n = row.get(g, 0)
            if n < 3:
                warnings.warn(f"Poor support: {cx}/{sh}/{g} n={n}")
                print(f"WARNING: {cx} / {sh} / {g} has only {n} cases")
    print()

    print("=" * 70)
    print("5. TRACE VARIANTS AND DIRECTLY-FOLLOWS (DFG) BY GROUP")
    print("=" * 70)
    for g, sub in df.groupby("group"):
        print(f"\n--- Group: {g} (n={len(sub)}) ---")
        print("Variant frequencies:")
        print(sub["trace"].value_counts().to_string())
        # PM4Py control-flow DFG (timestamps are auto-generated sequential placeholders;
        # they are NOT used for any performance analysis)
        try:
            log = pm4py.parse_event_log_string(sub["trace"].tolist(), sep=">")
            dfg, start_act, end_act = pm4py.discover_dfg(log)
            print("DFG (directly-follows counts):")
            for edge, cnt in sorted(dfg.items(), key=lambda x: -x[1]):
                print(f"  {edge[0]} -> {edge[1]}: {cnt}")
            print(f"Start activities: {dict(start_act)}")
            print(f"End activities:   {dict(end_act)}")
        except Exception as e:
            print(f"PM4Py DFG failed ({e}); falling back to manual DFG")
            dfg = defaultdict(int)
            for t in sub["trace"]:
                acts = t.split(">")
                for i in range(len(acts) - 1):
                    dfg[(acts[i], acts[i + 1])] += 1
            for edge, cnt in sorted(dfg.items(), key=lambda x: -x[1]):
                print(f"  {edge[0]} -> {edge[1]}: {cnt}")

    print("\n" + "=" * 70)
    print("Diagnostics complete. All comparisons are descriptive;")
    print("none constitutes a causal identification result.")
    print("=" * 70)

if __name__ == "__main__":

## 8. Residual uncertainty
1. Unobserved case risk that survives the recorded complexity label. Most valuable addition: a continuous or multi-dimensional risk score (or the raw features the classifier actually uses) measured before routing.
2. Completion / abandonment rates by group and complexity. Most valuable addition: the full population of opened cases, including still-open and abandoned trajectories with their last known state and sojourn times.
3. True night and day capacity (head-count, certified-reviewer hours, parallel slots) under each group. Most valuable design change: instrumented capacity and queue-length snapshots at the moment each case is routed.
4. Within-group reviewer skill heterogeneity and actual reviewer identity. Most valuable addition: anonymized reviewer identifiers linked to each review activity, enabling within-reviewer contrasts.
5. Longer-term quality and downstream cost (beyond the coarse 30-day appeal flag). Most valuable addition: linked claims-outcome data (re-openings, litigation flags, customer effort scores) at 90–180 days, together with an explicit measurement model for post-process confounding.
