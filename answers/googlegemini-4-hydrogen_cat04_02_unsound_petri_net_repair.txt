# 1. Executive Verdict

The given Petri net **is a valid workflow net**: it has a unique source place (`source`), a unique sink place (`sink`), and every place and transition lies on a directed path from `source` to `sink`.

However, it is **not sound**. Its principal defect is an incomplete synchronization on the waiver branch: the silent join `t_join_waived` consumes only `p_waived` and does not require a token from `p_analysis_done`.

Consequently, whenever a review waiver is recorded, `t_authorize` can fire before `t_analyze` completes, violating the mandatory-analysis policy. Moreover, even if `t_analyze` executes, its resulting token in `p_analysis_done` can never be consumed because `t_join_normal` also requires `p_review_done`. Every waiver execution therefore deadlocks with a token in `sink` plus a stranded token in `p_analysis_done`, failing to reach the exact final marking `fm`.

# 4. Minimal-Repair Alternatives

**Explicit edit-cost model:** Let each elementary structural edit have unit cost `1`:
- `+1` per added arc; `+1` per removed arc;
- `+1` per added place; `+1` per removed place;
- `+1` per added transition; `+1` per removed transition.

| Repair | Exact edits | Edit cost | Preserved intent | Changed behavior | New risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Repair A: Two-input waiver synchronization** | Add arc `(p_analysis_done, t_join_waived)` | `1` | Preserves full business intent: mandatory analysis in parallel with review/waiver, synchronization before authorization, clean termination at `fm`. | `t_join_waived` becomes a true AND-join; `t_authorize` cannot fire before `t_analyze`, and `p_analysis_done` is always consumed. | Retains two parallel silent joins (`t_join_normal` and `t_join_waived`) leading to the same `p_decision` place (mild structural redundancy). |
| **Repair B: Rewire waiver to shared synchronization and prune dead branch** | Remove arcs `(t_waive_review, p_waived)`, `(p_waived, t_join_waived)`, `(t_join_waived, p_decision)`; remove place `p_waived` and transition `t_join_waived`; add arc `(t_waive_review, p_review_done)` | `6` | Preserves the exact same visible business intent and trace language as Repair A. | Collapses `p_waived` and `p_review_done` into one XOR-merge place `p_review_done`, synchronized with `p_analysis_done` by a single AND-join `t_join_normal`. | Marking-level distinction between standard-reviewed and waived cases disappears immediately after `t_waive_review` fires, complicating future waiver-specific rules. |
| **Repair C: Policy-changing pre-split waiver (optional analysis)** | Remove arc `(p_review, t_waive_review)`; add arc `(p_ready, t_waive_review)` | `2` | Retains sound workflow execution and mandatory analysis on the standard-review path. | **Explicit policy change:** `t_waive_review` becomes an exclusive choice against `t_split` at `p_ready`, bypassing both `t_analyze` and `t_standard_review` when waived. | Violates the stated business rule that analysis is always mandatory; introduces compliance exposure if a waiver is meant to skip only review. |

### Minimality Justification

- **Repair A (`cost = 1`) is strictly minimal** among all repairs (and uniquely minimal among intent-preserving repairs): `0` edits leaves the net unsound; removing `1` arc cannot connect `p_analysis_done` to the waiver join (and removing any existing arc disconnects a node from `source` or `sink`, breaking the WF-net property); adding any single arc other than `(p_analysis_done, t_join_waived)` either fails to consume `p_analysis_done` on the waiver path or creates a spurious token/deadlock.
- **Repair B (`cost = 6`) is minimal within the single-join family:** redirecting `t_waive_review` to `p_review_done` alone costs `2` edits (remove `(t_waive_review, p_waived)`, add `(t_waive_review, p_review_done)`), but leaves `p_waived` without incoming arcs and `t_join_waived` dead, violating the WF-net source-to-sink path requirement. Removing the unreachable place `p_waived`, dead transition `t_join_waived`, and their two incident arcs adds `4` necessary cleanup edits, for a minimal cost of `6`.
- **Repair C (`cost = 2`) is minimal among policy-changing "waiver skips analysis" repairs:** redirecting `t_waive_review` from `p_review` to `p_ready` requires at least `1` arc removal and `1` arc addition. Note that trying to skip analysis *after* `t_split` (e.g., having `t_waive_review` or `t_join_waived` consume `p_analysis`) would fail because `t_analyze` could fire first, emptying `p_analysis` and deadlocking the waiver path. Moving the choice before `t_split` avoids this race condition at cost `2`.

```python
    return {p.name: count for p, count in sorted(marking.items(), key=lambda kv: kv[0].name) if count > 0}


def fire_named_transition(target_net, marking, transition_name):
    matches = [t for t in target_net.transitions if t.name == transition_name]
    if not matches:
        raise ValueError(f"Transition '{transition_name}' not found in net.")
    transition = matches[0]

    enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(target_net, marking)
    if transition not in enabled:
        enabled_names = sorted(t.name for t in enabled)
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"Transition '{transition_name}' is not enabled at marking {format_marking(marking)}. "
            f"Enabled transitions: {enabled_names}"
        )

    next_marking = Marking(marking)
    for arc in transition.in_arcs:
        next_marking[arc.source] -= arc.weight
        if next_marking[arc.source] == 0:
            del next_marking[arc.source]
    for arc in transition.out_arcs:
        next_marking[arc.target] = next_marking.get(arc.target, 0) + arc.weight
    return next_marking


def run_diagnostics(label, target_net, init_m, final_m):
    print(f"\n=== Diagnostics: {label} ===")
    is_wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(target_net)
    print("Is workflow net:", is_wf)
    soundness_res = pm4py.check_soundness(target_net, init_m, final_m, print_diagnostics=True)
    print("Soundness check result:", soundness_res)
    enabled_init = sorted(t.name for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(target_net, init_m))
    print("Enabled at initial marking:", enabled_init)
    rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(target_net, init_m, final_m)
    print("Reachability graph built:", type(rg).__name__)
    return rg


orig_rg = run_diagnostics("Original Net", net, im, fm)
rep_rg = run_diagnostics("Repaired Net (Repair A)", repaired_net, repaired_im, repaired_fm)

# Replay Counterexample 1 on Original Net
print("\n=== Counterexample Replay on Original Net ===")
m = Marking(im)
print("Start:", format_marking(m))
for t_name in ["t_register", "t_split", "t_waive_review", "t_join_waived", "t_authorize", "t_analyze"]:
    m = fire_named_transition(net, m, t_name)
    enabled_names = sorted(t.name for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m))
    print(f"After {t_name}: marking={format_marking(m)}, enabled={enabled_names}")
print("Reached exact fm on original net?", m == fm)

# Verify premature join is blocked on Repaired Net, while valid order completes cleanly
print("\n=== Verification on Repaired Net ===")
rm = Marking(repaired_im)
for t_name in ["t_register", "t_split", "t_waive_review"]:
    rm = fire_named_transition(repaired_net, rm, t_name)
print("Before analysis on waiver path:", format_marking(rm),
      "enabled:", sorted(t.name for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(repaired_net, rm)))

try:
    fire_named_transition(repaired_net, rm, "t_join_waived")
except RuntimeError as err:
    print("Blocked as expected:", err)

for t_name in ["t_analyze", "t_join_waived", "t_authorize"]:
    rm = fire_named_transition(repaired_net, rm, t_name)
    print(f"After {t_name}: marking={format_marking(rm)}")
print("Reached exact fm on repaired net?", rm == repaired_fm)
```

**Expected qualitative diagnostic differences:**
- `check_is_workflow_net` returns `True` for both `net` and `repaired_net`, confirming that the defect is behavioral rather than a source-to-sink connectivity violation.
- `check_soundness(..., print_diagnostics=True)` fails on `net` because executions taking `t_waive_review` cannot reach `fm = {sink: 1}` and instead terminate improperly at `{p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1}` (a marking containing a token in `sink` alongside a leftover token outside `sink`). On `repaired_net`, `check_soundness` passes: every reachable marking can complete cleanly at `repaired_fm`, there are no dead transitions, and the net is bounded.
- In `convert_to_reachability_graph`: the original reachability graph contains markings where `t_authorize` is enabled while `p_analysis` still holds a token, plus a deadlocked terminal state `{p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1}` distinct from `fm`. The repaired reachability graph has no markings with `t_authorize` enabled prior to `t_analyze`, and every maximal path ends solely at `repaired_fm = {sink: 1}`.

# 7. Repair Trade-Offs and Residual Risks

1. **Distinguishability of waiver vs. standard review:**
   In Repair A, `Perform Standard Review` (`t_standard_review`) and `Record Review Waiver` (`t_waive_review`) remain distinct visible transitions, and their outcomes remain separated in `p_review_done` vs. `p_waived` with dedicated silent joins (`t_join_normal` and `t_join_waived`). Therefore, both event logs and net markings distinguish standard reviews from waivers. (By contrast, Repair B keeps them distinguishable in event logs via the visible transitions, but merges their post-firing states inside `p_review_done`.)

2. **Duplicate behavior and silent-transition confusion:**
   Repair A retains three silent transitions (`t_split`, `t_join_normal`, `t_join_waived`). Because `p_review_done` and `p_waived` are mutually exclusive (fed by conflicting transitions on `p_review`), `t_join_normal` and `t_join_waived` can never both be enabled in the same case and therefore do not introduce duplicate trace behavior or ambiguous routing. The only residual complexity is mild structural redundancy: during alignment-based conformance checking on incomplete logs, two invisible joins feeding the same `p_decision` place yield two silent transitions with identical post-sets (though only one is consistent with whichever visible review/waiver event occurred).

3. **Why soundness alone does not prove business-rule alignment:**
   Soundness checks domain-independent behavioral properties (option to complete, proper completion, absence of dead transitions), not domain semantics. For example, Repair C (where `t_waive_review` skips analysis from `p_ready`) is also a completely sound workflow net, yet it violates the mandatory-analysis policy. Likewise, a sound net in which `t_authorize` executed before `t_analyze` and `t_analyze` cleared its token before `sink` would pass soundness while violating the authorization prerequisite.

4. **Positive and negative regression test traces:**
   - *Positive traces (must complete cleanly at `repaired_fm` with zero alignment cost):*
     1. `["Register Case", "Perform Analysis", "Perform Standard Review", "Authorize Case"]`
     2. `["Register Case", "Perform Standard Review", "Perform Analysis", "Authorize Case"]`
     3. `["Register Case", "Perform Analysis", "Record Review Waiver", "Authorize Case"]`
     4. `["Register Case", "Record Review Waiver", "Perform Analysis", "Authorize Case"]`
   - *Negative traces (must fail replay / be rejected as invalid):*
     1. `["Register Case", "Record Review Waiver", "Authorize Case"]` (missing mandatory analysis)
     2. `["Register Case", "Record Review Waiver", "Authorize Case", "Perform Analysis"]` (authorization before analysis)
     3. `["Register Case", "Perform Standard Review", "Record Review Waiver", "Perform Analysis", "Authorize Case"]` (both review outcomes executed)
     4. `["Register Case", "Perform Analysis", "Authorize Case"]` (neither review nor waiver recorded)

5. **Detecting accidental bypass of mandatory analysis:**
   - *Model-level verification:* Inspect the reachability graph (or check temporal invariants) to assert that in every reachable marking where `t_authorize` is enabled—or where `sink > 0`—neither `p_ready` nor `p_analysis` contains a token and `t_analyze` has fired on that path.
   - *Conformance-level monitoring:* Run alignment or token-based replay on negative synthetic traces (such as `<Register Case, Record Review Waiver, Authorize Case>`) and on production event logs, asserting that `Precedence("Perform Analysis", "Authorize Case")` and `Existence("Perform Analysis")` hold for 100% of authorized cases.

# 8. Final Recommendation

For deployment, I recommend **Repair A (two-input waiver synchronization)**, with **Repair B (shared synchronization via `p_review_done`)** as the fallback.

Repair A fixes both the soundness failure and the mandatory-analysis violation with a minimal edit cost of `1` (adding the single arc `(p_analysis_done, t_join_waived)`). Unlike Repair C, it fully preserves the stated business policy that analysis is mandatory even when review is waived. Compared with Repair B, Repair A avoids deleting existing places and transitions, preserves marking-level traceability between standard-reviewed (`p_review_done`) and waived (`p_waived`) cases, and keeps the door open for future waiver-specific post-processing (such as secondary audit checks) without re-splitting a merged place.

Repair B is the natural fallback if downstream tooling or model-governance standards penalize having two structurally parallel silent joins (`t_join_normal` and `t_join_waived`) and prefer a single canonical AND-join. Because Repair B generates the exact same visible trace language as Repair A, switching to it later requires only structural refactoring with no change to business behavior or event-log compatibility.
