def same_resource_violations(data, activity_a, activity_b, resource_col=RES):
    a = data.loc[data[ACT].eq(activity_a), [CASE, ACT, TS, resource_col,
                                            "vendor_id", "amount_band",
                                            "emergency_flag", "source_system",
                                            "entered_by", "approved_by"]]

                                            "vendor_id", "amount_band",
                                            "emergency_flag", "source_system",

    m = m[m[f"{TS}_a"] <= m[f"{TS}_b"]]

**Declarative rule or log-skeleton check for retrospective justification**

A discovered Declare or log-skeleton model can identify deviations from normal control flow, but the emergency rule also has **resource** and **working-day timing** semantics. Therefore, combine PM4Py conformance with an explicit rule check.

```python

def retrospective_rule_check(data):
    overrides = data[data[ACT].eq("Emergency Override")][[CASE, TS, RES]]
    retros = data[data[ACT].eq("Retrospective Justification")][[CASE, TS, RES]]

    pairs = overrides.merge(retros, on=CASE, suffixes=("_override", "_retro"))
    pairs = pairs[pairs[f"{TS}_retro"] >= pairs[f"{TS}_override"]]

    # Keep first retrospective justification after each override.
    pairs = (
        pairs.sort_values([CASE, f"{TS}_override", f"{TS}_retro"])
        .groupby([CASE, f"{TS}_override"], as_index=False)
        .first()
    )

    pairs["different_resource"] = (
        pairs[f"{RES}_override"] != pairs[f"{RES}_retro"]
    )

    # BusinessDay is an approximation; replace with the organization’s
    # holiday calendar for final audit testing.
    pairs["deadline"] = pairs[f"{TS}_override"] + BusinessDay(3)
    pairs["within_3_working_days"] = pairs[f"{TS}_retro"] <= pairs["deadline"]

    pairs["rule_pass"] = (
        pairs["different_resource"] & pairs["within_3_working_days"]
    )

    return pairs

retro_check = retrospective_rule_check(df_eff)
retro_exceptions = retro_check[~retro_check["rule_pass"]]
```

# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Evidence that would increase concern**

* Bank-detail changes for V-77x lack approved maintenance tickets, vendor confirmations, or bank-verification records.
* Beneficiary account numbers changed shortly before payment and do not match the documented V-77x banking-platform migration.
* The same effective human user, workstation, IP address, or session token appears behind both bank-detail change and payment approval.
* Manual_upload files lack uploader identity, checksum/hash, source approval, or reconciliation to vendor-master tickets.
* Emergency_flag was added after approval, or emergency justification text was created after payment release.
* Retrospective Justification records are late in the original system, not merely in the quarter_close_upload.
* Approver_Gita shared-account records cannot be resolved to individual humans.
* legacy_erp mapping fixes do not reduce apparent Bot_PO/human four-eyes violations.
* Fast approvals are concentrated in specific vendors, regions, or amount bands after controlling for emergency category.
* Subcontracting loops contain no substantive comments, document changes, or decision rationale.

**Evidence that would reduce concern**

* V-77x bank-detail changes tie cleanly to a pre-approved migration ticket, with independent bank verification and payment reconciliation.
* entered_by, approved_by, access logs, and session records show distinct humans for sensitive steps.
* Corrected legacy_erp mapping materially removes bot-related same-resource violations.
* Quarter-close spreadsheet migration explains identical timestamps while original source records show timely, independent justifications.
* Emergency medical-supply and weekend-repair cases have contemporaneous incident records, delegation approvals, and independent retrospective review within 3 working days.
* Routine Aline/Elena and Boris/Felix patterns remain normal after case-mix adjustment.
* Comparable emergency channels show similarly fast timing under documented policy.
* No payment destination, vendor ownership, invoice amount, or contract term changed inconsistently with approved documentation.
* AP_Kim’s role formally covers higher-risk supplier exceptions with compensating review.

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

The procurement-to-payment review identified several control exceptions and unusual process patterns, but the available evidence does not establish fraud, collusion, or intentional misconduct. High co-working frequency in routine buyer/approver pairs appears broadly consistent with team specialization and normal low-to-medium value processing, and should be monitored rather than treated as suspicious by itself. Emergency medical-supply and weekend-repair flows involving Buyer_Carla, Approver_Gita, Buyer_Dan, and Manager_Hugo show unusually fast approvals and repeated handback loops, but these patterns are plausibly explained by urgent operational handling. The main control concern in those cases is whether emergency overrides were properly justified within three working days by a different human resource.

The most concerning subgroup is VendorMaster_Lena → AP_Kim for vendor family V-77x. This cluster combines bank-detail changes, payment approvals, high amount share, high vendor concentration, fast timing, and heavy reliance on manual_upload records. These features do not prove misconduct, especially because V-77x reportedly underwent a legitimate banking-platform change, but they warrant targeted review of vendor-master tickets, bank-verification evidence, beneficiary account changes, payment files, and access logs.

The assessment is materially limited by data-quality issues, including shared “Approver_Gita” account usage, quarter-close spreadsheet migration, identical batch timestamps, missing maintenance-ticket links, and legacy_erp resource-mapping errors involving Bot_PO and human approvers. Internal Audit should therefore prioritize reconciliation of effective human actors and source-system mappings before attributing exceptions to individuals, while promptly reviewing the V-77x manual bank-change/payment cases and unresolved retrospective-justification breaches.
