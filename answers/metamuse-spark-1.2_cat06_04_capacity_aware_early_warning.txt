## 1. Decision rule

No single $p$ threshold is deployable. Intervention is only causal before `Diagnosis Complete` and its value = $ARR \times Value - Cost - Spillover$.

**Step 1: Eligibility filter (hard gate). All must pass:**
1. **Stage-eligible:** Current stage $\in$ {Triage, Diagnosis Started}. `Diagnosis Complete` or later = **ineligible** regardless of $p_{now}$ even if $p_{now}>0.8$. This rules out I-03.
2. **Material change gate for repeated alerts:** A case alerted in last 4h without a swarm is re-eligible *only* if $\geq$ one of: (a) materially new event (new log, state transition, owner change), OR (b) calibration-adjusted $\Delta p >0.10$ on verified features. Refresh-only alerts ($\Delta p$ on same features, no new event) are suppressed.
3. **Data verifiability:** If key live feature missing/imputed and $\Delta p >0.15$ is driven by imputation, status = **Eligible-conditional / Verify First** - do not swarm until feature restored or manual triage confirms deterioration. 
4. **Minimum robust benefit:** Conservative expected direct benefit >0 after calibration robustness:
   `Robust_Benefit = ARR_low × Value - €450 - False-alarm burden`
   Must be >0 at `ARR_low` (lower bound of supplied range). This prevents spending €450 + disruption for low-ARR/high-burden cases on a point estimate.

**Step 2: Calibration robustification (do not use $p_{now}$ raw):**
- SME: add +0.04 to +0.09 to $p_{now}$ for triage framing and for cost-of-omission reasoning, but **do not inflate ARR**. ARR uncertainty remains. Decision uses $p_{adjusted}$ only to avoid under-triage, not to justify benefit.
- Strategic Enterprise: subtract 0.05-0.12 from $p_{now}$ for prioritization; treat any $p_{now}<0.70$ in this segment as potentially $p_{true}\approx 0.55-0.60$. Require stronger ARR or higher Value to compensate.
- Public Sector: use $p_{now}$ as is.
- Non-Native-Language Support / small-N: do not adjust point $p$, widen confidence interval, flag as high-epistemic-uncertainty. Require monitoring and do not learn calibration from intervened cases alone.

Effective decision variable is **not** $p$, but **conservative net portfolio value**:
`Net_portfolio  (ARR × Value) - (€450+ false-alarm burden) - Spillover_harm`

where `Spillover_harm  (slots_used) × 6 × spillover_per_case(0.01-0.03) × avg_Value_at_risk_of_queue`. Base case: 6 other diagnosis-stage cases, mean value ~€15k-20k. Each slot then imposes ~€900-€3,600 expected breach cost on others. Ignoring this biases toward over-intervention.

**Step 3: Prioritization among eligible:**
Rank by tier derived from Robust_Benefit, time-to-deadline urgency (<3h amplifies value of ARR but also reduces realizability if still in Triage), and stage (Diagnosis Started > Triage ceteris paribus because swarm more targeted and less time to recover). After ranking, apply constraints:
- Max 1 swarm per case per episode unless new event.
- Respect queue externality: if Robust_Benefit < expected marginal spillover harm (~€1,500-2,500 base), defer even if positive alone.
- Reserve policy: hold 1 slot for unlisted regulatory arrival <15:00 unless 2 eligible cases have Robust_Benefit >€2,000 and deadline <4h. Reserve is preference, not rule.

Uncertainty treatment: supplied ARR ranges are **observational heterogeneous effects under ideal assignment, not known causal effects**. Use midpoint for Base, lower half for Adverse, never treat upper bound as promise. Sensitivity to calibration and imputation must be shown.

## 4. Repeated alerts and temporal policy

**Cool-down / suppression:** After any alert that does **not** result in swarm, suppress automatic re-alert for same case for **4 hours** unless materially new event occurs. Every 30-min model refresh without new feature change does not reset timer. This prevents I-05-type spam from consuming attention and artificially inflating apparent incidence.

**Re-entry logic:**
- **Material new event** = new customer upload, stage transition, reassignment, SLA edit, priority change, or verified external signal. Then alert re-enters at full priority, $p_{prev}$ is reset to value after event.
- **No new event + p <0.10:** Remains suppressed; show in *monitor list* but no push notification, no swarm eligibility. Logged as `suppressed_refresh`.
- **No new event + p 0.10 on verified features:** Escalate to human triage review (not auto-swarm) to adjudicate if surge is real.

**Escalation:** After 2 suppressed alerts, 3rd alert without new event cannot auto-escalate to swarm at all; requires senior ops approval and explicit justification that effect estimate still applies. After a swarm, 48h blackout for repeat swarm on same case unless new event (prevents bounce).

**Comparison:**
- **I-05 (third alert, no new event, 0.550.57 +0.02):** Classic refresh fatigue. First alert already evaluated, no swarm given (capacity/value). No new information means epistemic state unchanged. Under policy: **suppressed, ineligible today**. Correct action is not another swarm but: (a) keep in monitor, (b) lightweight action (check-in), (c) count as suppressed for training data. Its SME underprediction suggests underlying risk may be higher (true p ~0.61-0.66) - that argues for *earlier* intervention at first alert, not for repeated swarming now.
- **I-07 (third alert, new log, 0.750.72 -0.03):** **Re-eligible** despite lower $p_{now}$ and third-alert status because new log is material information. The slight drop does not mean improvement; model processed new data and stayed high (~0.72). For SME this is likely underpredicted (true ~0.76-0.81) and case is still Triage with 1.5h deadline - so uncertainty is high but information is fresh. Policy: allow eligibility, prioritize by robust benefit, but defer today due to low value/time constraints and offer alternative (rapid desk review of log, not full 45m swarm). If deadline were 6h+, I-07 would outrank I-02.

This distinction avoids treating `count of alerts` as risk and instead treats `information content` as trigger.

## 5. Fairness and feedback-loop risks

**a) Value-based starvation:** Prioritizing by Value×ARR concentrates swarms on Strategic Enterprise (€25-100k). Low-value SME/Public cases I-07 (€5k), I-05 (€7k) systematically lose even when ARR is high (0.20-0.40) and true need high due to underprediction. Over weeks this creates allocative unfairness and contractual risk (SME SLAs may have regulatory penalties not in € value) and morale hazard.
*Safeguard:* Tiered reserve: **At least 1 of 3 daily deployment slots (or 30% of weekly capacity) reserved for non-Strategic / low-value / high-ARR cases** with robust benefit >0, even if raw € value lower. Evaluate weekly by Value decile. Publish share of swarms by segment vs share of breach risk. If Strategic >70% of swarms >2 weeks, trigger fairness review. Use alternative lightweight swarms (15m) for low-value cases to reduce cost/harm.

**b) Non-Native-Language Support uncertainty (I-09):** Small validation sample  both $p$ calibration and ARR estimates have wide intervals. Risk of over-intervening with harmful false alarms (customer language barrier + swarm disruption) or under-serving due to bias.
*Safeguard:* Flag such cases for **human-in-loop triage with interpreter**, do not auto-block. Cap false-alarm burden review: post-swarm CSAT check within 24h. Do not use I-09 outcomes alone to update model without inverse-propensity weighting; pool with external validation and require n50 before tightening ARR interval.

**c) Differential calibration across segments:** Model overpredicts Strategic, underpredicts SME. Naive ranking by $p_{now}$ over-allocates to Strategic (I-02, I-08 appear high) and misses SMEs. Blind threshold would waste slots on overpredicted highs while SMEs breach silently.
*Safeguard:* Apply segment offsets in decision display (show $p_{adjusted}$ band). Require threshold decisions to show both raw and adjusted. Recalibration must be segment-specific (Platt / isotonic per segment) not global. Monitor calibration error (ECE) weekly per segment; freeze auto-threshold changes if ECE >0.07.

**d) Selective intervention  training label drift:** Swarmed cases that avoid breach become `non-breach` labels, making model learn that high-$p$ cases that got help are low risk. Future $p$ will underestimate risk for similar untreated cases (self-negating prophecy). Conversely, we only see outcome under intervention, not counterfactual.
*Safeguard:* Log **two labels**: observed outcome and `intervention indicator + ARR estimate`. Training excludes or IPW-weights intervened cases; maintain holdout **intervention-free cohort** (random 10-15% of eligible cases where swarm withheld by capacity lottery) to estimate true $p$ and causal effect via comparison. Without this, drift is invisible.

**e) Spillover harms systematically fall on untreated groups:** Each swarm adds 8-20m delay to diagnosis queue, breach probability +0.01-0.03 per 6 cases. If queue disproportionately contains SME / Public / low-value cases (staff prioritize Strategic in queue), spillover becomes regressive: helping Strategic starves SMEs doubly (direct starvation + congestion).
*Safeguard:* Monitor **queue demographics of spillover**: track $p$-increase and delay by segment for non-intervened cases within 4h of any swarm. If SME breach rate rises +0.02 after swarm days vs no-swarm days, adjust: reduce slots on high-congestion days, shift swarms to off-peak, or add surge capacity. Report spillover harm in same table as benefit (Section 3) not as footnote.

## 6. Monitoring and recalibration plan

**Operational (daily):**
- Slots used vs reserved, queue length, aggregate delay per slot (target 8-20m, alert if >25m or if diagnosis queue >8 concurrent). Specialist utilization and interruption count.
- Alert persistence: #alerts per case, suppression rate, re-entry rate, % I-05-type refresh alerts.

**Predictive (weekly):**
- Calibration per segment: reliability diagram, ECE, $p_{now}$ vs observed breach rate **in non-intervened holdout**. Track SME (-0.04 to -0.09) and Strategic (+0.05 to +0.12) gaps. If ECE per segment exceeds 0.07 or drifts >0.03, trigger segment-specific recalibration, not global threshold shift.
- Discrimination (AUC) overall and per segment; alert if Non-Native-Language AUC drops >0.05.
- Data-quality: % missing queue feature (I-06), imputation rate, p attributable to imputation.

**Causal-effect (bi-weekly / monthly):**
- Estimate ARR via: (1) randomized holdout comparison (eligible but not swarmed due to capacity lottery) vs swarmed, stratified by stage; (2) before/after Diagnosis Complete natural experiment (I-03-type placebo). Report ARR by segment, stage, time-to-deadline with 95% intervals; never point estimate.
- Sensitivity: optimistic/base/adverse scenarios updated with empirical ARR.
- Detect policy-induced drift: compare observed breach rate for high-$p$ non-swarm cohort over time; if falling due to intervention, do not interpret as model improvement.

**Capacity / spillover (per swarm + weekly):**
- Measure breach rate of 6 nearest non-intervened diagnosis-stage cases within 4h window. Difference-in-differences vs days with 0 swarms. Estimate realized spillover per slot (target 0.01-0.03). If >0.03 persistent, reduce concurrency or require higher Robust_Benefit threshold (+€1,000) for next week.
- Track regulatory arrival prediction error.

**Fairness (weekly):**
- Dashboard: swarms, benefits, spillover harm, false alarms by segment (SME, Strategic, Public, NNL), by value decile, by language support. Metrics: benefit per case, false-alarm per case, spillover delay per segment. Flag if bottom-value quartile receives <15% of swarms despite >25% of risk mass.
- False-alarm burden survey (2-question) after swarms on NNL and high-burden cases (I-08 €5k) to catch customer disruption not in €.

**Decision to change threshold/allocation:**
- **Adjust eligibility threshold** only after calibration is corrected per segment and after causal ARR interval narrowed with 40 swarmed cases per stage.
- **Change tier weights** if fairness monitor shows >2 consecutive weeks of starvation or if spillover monitor shows systematic segment harm.
- **Reserve rule adaptation:** If regulatory arrival <10% of days over 30 days, reserve can be relaxed to 0 with standby call; if >30% or if 2 breached reserves occurred, increase reserve to 1 mandatory + 1 standby.
- **Circuit breaker:** Any swarm that later found to have ARR0 due to data fault (I-06-type) triggers pause on imputed-feature cases until fix validated.

All monitoring artifacts feed a monthly review log with explicit causal disclaimer: `p` is observational risk under no intervention; effect ranges are provisional; net-impact tables must be shown with uncertainty and spillover, not as single ROI number.
