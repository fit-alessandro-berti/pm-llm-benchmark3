**Assessment**: The process is **indeterminate to moderately unfair with respect to procedural fairness**, with evidence of **likely procedural burden inequity** rather than clear discrimination, but with sufficient residual disparity to require intervention.

**Calibrated conclusion**: The process appears to **deliver rights-preserving steps at unequal speed and with unequal procedural burden**, particularly for groups needing language and disability support. This does not justify removing those steps, but it does justify treating **capacity, handover design, and adjusted monitoring** as fairness-critical.
