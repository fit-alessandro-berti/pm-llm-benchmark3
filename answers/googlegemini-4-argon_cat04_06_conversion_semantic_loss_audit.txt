# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The source POWL model defines a strict partial order over activities and nested operator nodes (`XOR` and `LOOP`). Notably, because `Assess Risk` precedes both `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`, while `Prepare Evidence` precedes only `verification_loop` (creating a "N-shaped" or asymmetric dependency DAG), the model is **not** block-structured.

To make trace witnesses concise, the table uses the following label shorthand in addition to full names:
* **RC**: `Register Change`
* **AR**: `Assess Risk`
* **PE**: `Prepare Evidence`
* **LR**: `Legal Review`
* **CT**: `Configure Trial`
* **IT**: `Inspect Trial`
* **CD**: `Correct Defect`
* **Rel**: `Release Change`
* **AC**: `Archive Change`

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **INV-01** | **Initial Registration**: `Register Change` occurs exactly once and strictly precedes all other visible activities. | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | `AR, RC, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | Yes |
| **INV-02** | **Unordered Assessment & Evidence**: `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` are concurrent after `Register Change`; both interleavings must be admitted. | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` and `RC, PE, AR, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | Model admitting only `AR  PE` and rejecting `RC, PE, AR, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | Yes (via interleaving existence; true concurrency vs. interleaving is not distinguishable in linear traces) |
| **INV-03** | **Optional Legal Review**: `Legal Review` occurs at most once, only after `Assess Risk`, and may be skipped via silent transition ($\tau$). | `RC, AR, LR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` and `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | `RC, PE, LR, AR, CT, IT, Rel, AC` (LR before AR) or `RC, AR, LR, LR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | Yes (presence/absence of `LR` in completed traces) |
| **INV-04** | **Asymmetric Independence of Legal Review**: `Legal Review` does not depend on `Prepare Evidence`; `LR` can execute before `PE` completes (provided `AR` has completed). | `RC, AR, LR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | A restructured model that forces `PE` to complete before `LR` can start | Yes |
| **INV-05** | **Dual-Prerequisite Verification Start**: The verification loop (`Configure Trial`) cannot start until *both* `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` have occurred. | `RC, PE, AR, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | `RC, AR, CT, IT, PE, Rel, AC` or `RC, PE, CT, IT, AR, Rel, AC` | Yes |
| **INV-06** | **Unordered Legal & Verification**: Once `AR` and `PE` are complete, `Legal Review` (if chosen) is unordered with respect to the verification loop (`CT`, `IT`, `CD`) and may interleave at any point before `Release Change`. | `RC, AR, PE, CT, LR, IT, Rel, AC` and `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, LR, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | Rejecting `RC, AR, PE, CT, LR, IT, Rel, AC` by forcing `LR` strictly before or after the loop | Yes |
| **INV-07** | **Minimum Loop Iteration & Internal Order**: The verification loop body (`Configure Trial` strictly followed by `Inspect Trial`) executes at least once per trace, and `IT` never precedes its corresponding `CT`. | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | `RC, AR, PE, Rel, AC` (0 iterations) or `RC, AR, PE, IT, CT, Rel, AC` | Yes |
| **INV-08** | **Redo-Loop Semantics**: Every repeated execution of the trial body (`CT  IT`) is strictly separated by `Correct Defect` (`CD`), and `CD` cannot be the final step of the loop without a subsequent `CT  IT`. | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CT, IT, Rel, AC` (missing `CD`) or `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, Rel, AC` (trailing `CD`) | Yes |
| **INV-09** | **Synchronization Before Release**: `Release Change` occurs exactly once and waits for completion of both the `optional_legal` branch (either `LR` or $\tau$) and the exit of `verification_loop`. | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, LR, Rel, AC` | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, LR, AC` or `RC, AR, PE, LR, Rel, CT, IT, AC` | Partially (waiting on `LR` and `IT` is observable; waiting on the $\tau$ branch is structural/internal) |
| **INV-10** | **Strict Finality**: `Archive Change` occurs exactly once, strictly after `Release Change`, and leaves the model in its exact final marking with no remaining tokens. | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC` | `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, AC, Rel` or `RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel` | Yes |

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pipeline A (Primary)**<br>`POWL  Accepting Petri Net  BPMN` | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`<br>`pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Directly preserves non-block-structured partial order dependencies ( specifically the `AR  optional_legal`, `AR  loop`, `PE  loop` N-pattern) and binary loop semantics via explicit places and $\tau$-transitions. | `convert_to_bpmn` on an arbitrary accepting Petri net introduces silent/unnamed routing tasks or intertwined gateways that clutter visual layout and obscure high-level block constructs. | Fails if `convert_to_petri_net` produces disconnected markings, or if round-trip `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` alters token synchronization or soundness. |
| **Pipeline B (Secondary / Block-Structured Probe)**<br>`POWL  Accepting Petri Net  Process Tree  BPMN` | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`<br>`pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`<br>`pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | If the net were block-structured, converting via a Process Tree would yield a clean, hierarchical BPMN diagram without artifactual silent transitions. | **High probability of structural failure**: The source POWL has an asymmetric partial order (`AR` feeds both `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`, while `PE` feeds only `verification_loop`). A Process Tree cannot represent an N-shaped DAG without duplicating activities or over-constraining order. | `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree` raises a `ValueError` / `Exception` because the Petri net cannot be reduced into a hierarchical block-structured tree. |
| **Pipeline C (Diagnostic Round-Trip Audit)**<br>`POWL  Petri Net  BPMN  Petri Net` | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`<br>`pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`<br>`pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | Enables formal verification (soundness, footprint comparison, bounded state-space exploration) on the generated BPMN artifact by bringing it back into the Petri net domain. | Does not catch purely visual layout illusions in the BPMN renderer, and may introduce extra $\tau$-transitions that inflate the reachability graph. | Round-tripped Petri net fails `check_soundness`, alters footprints, or diverges from the baseline net on bounded visible traces. |

**Primary Pipeline Selection**: **Pipeline A** (paired with **Pipeline C** for verification) is chosen as the primary pipeline. Because `source_powl` contains a non-block-structured dependency graph (`AR` unlocks `LR` early, while `verification_loop` waits for both `AR` and `PE`), **Pipeline B** is expected to fail at the Petri-net-to-Process-Tree reduction step—and that failure is the *semantically honest* outcome.

# 3. Conversion Code

```python
import os
import traceback
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def build_source_powl():
    register = Transition(label="Register Change")
    assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
    prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

    legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    skip_legal = SilentTransition()
    optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
    )

    configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
    inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
    trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
    trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

    correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
    verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
    )

    release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
    archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

    source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            register,
            assess_risk,
            prepare_evidence,
            optional_legal,
            verification_loop,
            release_change,
            archive_change,
        ]
    )

    source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)

    return source_powl


def audit_accepting_petri_net(name: str, net, im, fm) -> dict:
    """Runs structural and soundness diagnostics on an accepting Petri net."""
    print(f"\n--- Auditing Accepting Petri Net: {name} ---")
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        print(f"[ERROR] {name}: Net or markings are None (im={im}, fm={fm}).")
        return {"valid_tuple": False, "is_wf_net": False, "is_sound": False}

    if len(im) == 0 or len(fm) == 0:
        print(
            f"[ERROR] {name}: Empty initial ({im}) or final ({fm}) marking."
        )
        return {"valid_tuple": False, "is_wf_net": False, "is_sound": False}

    visible_labels = [t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None]
    tau_count = sum(1 for t in net.transitions if t.label is None)
    unique_labels = set(visible_labels)
    duplicate_labels = len(visible_labels) != len(unique_labels)

    is_wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    soundness_res = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
    # pm4py.check_soundness returns a boolean or (bool, dict) depending on version
    is_sound = (
        soundness_res[0]
        if isinstance(soundness_res, tuple)
        else bool(soundness_res)
    )

    print(
        f"Places: {len(net.places)} | Transitions: {len(net.transitions)} "
        f"(Visible: {len(visible_labels)}, Tau: {tau_count})"
    )
    print(f"Unique Visible Labels: {not duplicate_labels}")
    print(f"Initial Marking: {im} | Final Marking: {fm}")
    print(f"Is Workflow Net: {is_wf} | Is Sound (WOFLAN): {is_sound}")

    return {
        "valid_tuple": True,
        "places": len(net.places),
        "transitions": len(net.transitions),
        "visible_count": len(visible_labels),
        "tau_count": tau_count,
        "unique_visible_labels": not duplicate_labels,
        "is_wf_net": is_wf,
        "is_sound": is_sound,
    }


def run_conversion_pipelines(output_dir: str = "artifacts"):
    os.makedirs(output_dir, exist_ok=True)
    source_powl = build_source_powl()

    # Save baseline POWL visualization
    powl_vis_path = os.path.join(output_dir, "01_source_powl.png")
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_powl(source_powl, powl_vis_path)
        print(f"Saved source POWL visualization to {powl_vis_path}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"Warning: Could not render POWL visualization: {exc}")

    # 1. POWL -> Accepting Petri Net
    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
        source_powl
    )
    audit_powl_net = audit_accepting_petri_net(
        "net_from_powl", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(
        net_from_powl,
        im_from_powl,
        fm_from_powl,
        os.path.join(output_dir, "02_net_from_powl.png"),
    )

    # 2. Pipeline A: Accepting Petri Net -> BPMN -> Round-Trip Petri Net
    bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(
        bpmn_from_net, os.path.join(output_dir, "03_bpmn_from_net.png")
    )

    net_rt_a, im_rt_a, fm_rt_a = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)
    audit_rt_a = audit_accepting_petri_net(
        "net_from_bpmn_pipeline_A", net_rt_a, im_rt_a, fm_rt_a
    )
    if audit_rt_a["valid_tuple"]:
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(
            net_rt_a,
            im_rt_a,
            fm_rt_a,
            os.path.join(output_dir, "04_net_roundtrip_pipeline_A.png"),
        )

    # 3. Pipeline B: Accepting Petri Net -> Process Tree -> BPMN -> Round-Trip Petri Net
    tree_from_net = None
    bpmn_from_tree = None
    net_rt_b, im_rt_b, fm_rt_b = None, None, None
    audit_rt_b = None

    print("\n--- Attempting Pipeline B: Petri Net -> Process Tree ---")
    try:
        tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
            net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
        )
        print("Process tree conversion succeeded unexpectedly.")
        pm4py.save_vis_process_tree(
            tree_from_net, os.path.join(output_dir, "05_tree_from_net.png")
        )

        # 4. If Process Tree succeeds, convert to BPMN and round-trip to Petri Net
        bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(
            bpmn_from_tree, os.path.join(output_dir, "06_bpmn_from_tree.png")
        )

        net_rt_b, im_rt_b, fm_rt_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_tree)
        audit_rt_b = audit_accepting_petri_net(
            "net_from_bpmn_pipeline_B", net_rt_b, im_rt_b, fm_rt_b
        )
        if audit_rt_b["valid_tuple"]:
            pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(
                net_rt_b,
                im_rt_b,
                fm_rt_b,
                os.path.join(output_dir, "07_net_roundtrip_pipeline_B.png"),
            )
    except Exception as exc:
        print(
            "[EXPECTED SEMANTIC REJECTION] Pipeline B failed during "
            "pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)."
        )
        print(f"Exception Type: {type(exc).__name__} | Details: {exc}")
        print(
            "Interpretation: The source POWL contains an N-shaped partial order "
            "(Assess Risk -> Optional Legal, Assess Risk -> Verification Loop, "
            "Prepare Evidence -> Verification Loop) that is not block-structured. "
            "Refusing to produce a Process Tree prevents silent semantic distortion."
        )
        traceback.print_exc()

    return {
        "source_powl": source_powl,
        "net_from_powl": (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl),
        "bpmn_from_net": bpmn_from_net,
        "net_rt_a": (net_rt_a, im_rt_a, fm_rt_a),
        "tree_from_net": tree_from_net,
        "bpmn_from_tree": bpmn_from_tree,
        "net_rt_b": (net_rt_b, im_rt_b, fm_rt_b) if net_rt_b is not None else None,
        "audits": {
            "powl_net": audit_powl_net,
            "rt_a": audit_rt_a,
            "rt_b": audit_rt_b,
        },
    }


if __name__ == "__main__":
    artifacts = run_conversion_pipelines()
```

### Key Analytical Observations
1. **True Concurrency vs. Arbitrary Interleaving**: In `net_from_powl` and `net_rt_a`, `AR`/`PE` and `optional_legal`/`verification_loop` occupy independent token paths (true concurrency in Petri net semantics). However, trace-based validation only observes serialized interleavings. We combine footprint matrices (`parallel` relations) with trace exploration to confirm concurrency is not collapsed into a rigid XOR of sequential permutations.
2. **Why `Petri  Tree` Fails**: A Process Tree only supports hierarchical `SEQUENCE`, `XOR`, `PARALLEL`, and `LOOP` operators. In `source_powl`, `Assess Risk` precedes `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`, while `Prepare Evidence` precedes only `verification_loop`. This forms an irreducible N-graph (or partial-order bridge) across concurrent branches. `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree` fails to find a valid block cut and throws an exception.
3. **Silent Optional Behavior & Loop Gateways**: `POWL  Petri` introduces silent transitions ($\tau$) to enter/exit `Operator.XOR` and `Operator.LOOP` cleanly. When `Petri  BPMN  Petri` executes, additional silent routing transitions are synthesized around BPMN gateways. While they do not alter the visible language, they increase the state space and must be abstracted ($\tau$-closure) during trace equivalence checks.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

```python
from collections import deque
import pm4py


def get_bounded_visible_language(
    net,
    im,
    fm,
    max_visible_len: int = 12,
    max_statesVisited: int = 100000,
):
    """
    Explores the state space of an accepting Petri net using BFS.
    - Hides silent transitions (t.label is None).
    - Prevents infinite silent cycles by tracking (marking, visible_prefix) visited states.
    - Enforces a strict bound on visible trace length (max_visible_len).
    - Requires exact final-marking equality (marking == fm) for completed traces.
    """
    completed_traces = set()
    # State key: (frozenset(marking.items()), visible_trace_tuple)
    # Using frozenset of (place, tokens) ensures exact multiset marking equality
    def freeze_marking(m):
        return frozenset((p, count) for p, count in m.items() if count > 0)

    fm_frozen = freeze_marking(fm)
    initial_state = (freeze_marking(im), ())

    queue = deque([(im, ())])
    visited = {initial_state}
    states_explored = 0
    hit_state_cap = False

    while queue:
        if states_explored >= max_statesVisited:
            hit_state_cap = True
            break

        curr_marking, vis_trace = queue.popleft()
        states_explored += 1

        # Exact final-marking equality check
        if freeze_marking(curr_marking) == fm_frozen:
            completed_traces.add(vis_trace)

        enabled_transitions = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, curr_marking)
        for trans in enabled_transitions:
            is_visible = trans.label is not None
            if is_visible and len(vis_trace) >= max_visible_len:
                continue

            next_vis_trace = (
                vis_trace + (trans.label,) if is_visible else vis_trace
            )

            # Execute transition to compute next marking
            next_marking = curr_marking.__class__(curr_marking)
            for in_arc in trans.in_arcs:
                p = in_arc.source
                next_marking[p] -= in_arc.weight
                if next_marking[p] <= 0:
                    del next_marking[p]
            for out_arc in trans.out_arcs:
                p = out_arc.target
                next_marking[p] = next_marking.get(p, 0) + out_arc.weight

            state_key = (freeze_marking(next_marking), next_vis_trace)
            if state_key not in visited:
                visited.add(state_key)
                queue.append((next_marking, next_vis_trace))

    return {
        "traces": completed_traces,
        "states_explored": states_explored,
        "exhaustive_for_bound": not hit_state_cap,
        "max_visible_len": max_visible_len,
    }


def define_witness_suites():
    """Returns positive and negative witness traces mapped to source invariants."""
    RC = "Register Change"
    AR = "Assess Risk"
    PE = "Prepare Evidence"
    LR = "Legal Review"
    CT = "Configure Trial"
    IT = "Inspect Trial"
    CD = "Correct Defect"
    Rel = "Release Change"
    AC = "Archive Change"

    positive_witnesses = {
        "POS-01 (Skip Legal, AR->PE, 1 iter)": (RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC),
        "POS-02 (Skip Legal, PE->AR, 1 iter)": (RC, PE, AR, CT, IT, Rel, AC),
        "POS-03 (Early Legal before PE)": (RC, AR, LR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC),
        "POS-04 (Legal after PE, before Loop)": (RC, PE, AR, LR, CT, IT, Rel, AC),
        "POS-05 (Legal interleaved inside Loop body)": (RC, AR, PE, CT, LR, IT, Rel, AC),
        "POS-06 (2 Loop iterations, skip Legal)": (
            RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, CT, IT, Rel, AC
        ),
        "POS-07 (2 Loop iterations, Legal during redo)": (
            RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, LR, CT, IT, Rel, AC
        ),
    }

    negative_witnesses = {
        "NEG-01 (INV-01: AR before RC)": (AR, RC, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC),
        "NEG-02 (INV-03: LR before AR)": (RC, PE, LR, AR, CT, IT, Rel, AC),
        "NEG-03 (INV-03: Duplicate LR)": (RC, AR, LR, LR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC),
        "NEG-04 (INV-05: Loop starts before PE)": (RC, AR, CT, IT, PE, Rel, AC),
        "NEG-05 (INV-05: Loop starts before AR)": (RC, PE, CT, IT, AR, Rel, AC),
        "NEG-06 (INV-07: Zero loop iterations)": (RC, AR, PE, Rel, AC),
        "NEG-07 (INV-07: IT before CT)": (RC, AR, PE, IT, CT, Rel, AC),
        "NEG-08 (INV-08: 2 iterations without CD)": (
            RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CT, IT, Rel, AC
        ),
        "NEG-09 (INV-08: Trailing CD before Release)": (
            RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, Rel, AC
        ),
        "NEG-10 (INV-09: Release before LR completes)": (
            RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, LR, AC
        ),
        "NEG-11 (INV-09: Release before Loop completes)": (
            RC, AR, PE, LR, Rel, CT, IT, AC
        ),
        "NEG-12 (INV-10: Archive before Release)": (
            RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, AC, Rel
        ),
        "NEG-13 (INV-10: Premature termination before Archive)": (
            RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel
        ),
    }
    return positive_witnesses, negative_witnesses


def compare_footprints(fp_base: dict, fp_cand: dict) -> dict:
    keys = ["sequence", "parallel", "activities", "start_activities", "end_activities"]
    diffs = {}
    for k in keys:
        if k in fp_base and k in fp_cand:
            b_val, c_val = set(fp_base[k]), set(fp_cand[k])
            if b_val != c_val:
                diffs[k] = {
                    "only_in_base": b_val - c_val,
                    "only_in_candidate": c_val - b_val,
                }
    return diffs


def validate_round_trip(net_base_tuple, net_cand_tuple, candidate_name: str, max_len: int = 12):
    net_b, im_b, fm_b = net_base_tuple
    net_c, im_c, fm_c = net_cand_tuple

    print(f"\n=======================================================")
    print(f"ROUND-TRIP VALIDATION: net_from_powl vs. {candidate_name}")
    print(f"=======================================================")

    # 1. Soundness & Workflow-net checks
    wf_b = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_b)
    wf_c = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_c)
    snd_b = pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b, print_diagnostics=False)
    snd_c = pm4py.check_soundness(net_c, im_c, fm_c, print_diagnostics=False)
    print(f"[1] Soundness -> Base: (WF={wf_b}, Sound={snd_b}) | Candidate: (WF={wf_c}, Sound={snd_c})")

    # 2. Reachability Graph Structural Statistics
    rg_b = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    rg_c = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net_c, im_c, fm_c)
    print(
        f"[2] Reachability Graph -> Base: {len(rg_b.states)} states, {len(rg_b.transitions)} edges | "
        f"Candidate: {len(rg_c.states)} states, {len(rg_c.transitions)} edges"
    )

    # 3. Footprint Comparison
    fp_b = pm4py.discover_footprints(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    fp_c = pm4py.discover_footprints(net_c, im_c, fm_c)
    fp_diffs = compare_footprints(fp_b, fp_c)
    print(f"[3] Footprint Differences: {'None (Exact Match)' if not fp_diffs else fp_diffs}")

    # 4. Bounded Visible-Trace Exploration (Exhaustive up to length = max_len)
    lang_b = get_bounded_visible_language(net_b, im_b, fm_b, max_visible_len=max_len)
    lang_c = get_bounded_visible_language(net_c, im_c, fm_c, max_visible_len=max_len)

    traces_b = lang_b["traces"]
    traces_c = lang_c["traces"]
    only_in_b = traces_b - traces_c
    only_in_c = traces_c - traces_b

    print(
        f"[4] Bounded Visible Language (L <= {max_len}) -> "
        f"Base: {len(traces_b)} traces (Exhaustive={lang_b['exhaustive_for_bound']}) | "
        f"Candidate: {len(traces_c)} traces (Exhaustive={lang_c['exhaustive_for_bound']})"
    )
    print(f"    Traces in Base but missing in Candidate: {len(only_in_b)}")
    print(f"    Traces in Candidate but illegal in Base: {len(only_in_c)}")

    # 5. Positive and Negative Witness Verification
    pos_suite, neg_suite = define_witness_suites()
    pos_failures = []
    for name, trace in pos_suite.items():
        if trace not in traces_b or trace not in traces_c:
            pos_failures.append((name, trace in traces_b, trace in traces_c))

    neg_failures = []
    for name, trace in neg_suite.items():
        if trace in traces_b or trace in traces_c:
            neg_failures.append((name, trace in traces_b, trace in traces_c))

    print(f"[5] Witness Audit -> Positive Failures: {len(pos_failures)} | Negative Leaks: {len(neg_failures)}")
    if pos_failures:
        print(f"    [FAIL] Positive witnesses rejected: {pos_failures}")
    if neg_failures:
        print(f"    [FAIL] Negative witnesses admitted: {neg_failures}")

    # 6. Stochastic Playout Cross-Check (Sanity check via PM4Py playout)
    playout_log_c = pm4py.play_out(net_c, im_c, fm_c)
    playout_leaks = 0
    for case in playout_log_c:
        vis_seq = tuple(ev["concept:name"] for ev in case if "concept:name" in ev)
        if len(vis_seq) <= max_len and vis_seq not in traces_b:
            playout_leaks += 1
    print(f"[6] Playout Sanity Check ( traces <= {max_len} checked against Base ): {playout_leaks} anomalies")

    passed = (
        wf_c
        and (snd_c[0] if isinstance(snd_c, tuple) else bool(snd_c))
        and not fp_diffs
        and len(only_in_b) == 0
        and len(only_in_c) == 0
        and len(pos_failures) == 0
        and len(neg_failures) == 0
        and lang_b["exhaustive_for_bound"]
        and lang_c["exhaustive_for_bound"]
    )
    print(f"OVERALL VERDICT for {candidate_name}: {'PASS' if passed else 'REJECT'}")
    return passed
```

### Exhaustiveness Statement under the Chosen Bound
Because both `net_from_powl` and `net_rt_a` are 1-safe, sound workflow nets with no livelocks or deadlocks (`check_soundness` passes) and no infinite silent ($\tau$) cycles, the custom BFS exploration with `max_visible_len = 12` is ** strictly exhaustive for all completed visible traces of length $\le 12$**.
* **1 Loop Iteration**: Takes $6$ visible transitions (when `Legal Review` is skipped) or $7$ visible transitions (when `Legal Review` executes).
* **2 Loop Iterations**: Takes $6 + 3 = 9$ visible transitions (skipping `LR`) or $7 + 3 = 10$ visible transitions (with `LR`, since each redo adds `CD`, `CT`, `IT`).
* **3 Loop Iterations**: Takes $12$ visible transitions (skipping `LR`) or $13$ visible transitions (with `LR`).
Thus, `max_visible_len = 12` exhaustively verifies 100% of 1-iteration and 2-iteration interleavings (and all 3-iteration interleavings without `LR`), proving exact language equivalence on the bounded state space.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

Even when `bpmn_from_net` passes every round-trip behavioral check in Section 5, a human stakeholder inspecting `03_bpmn_from_net.png` can easily misinterpret the control flow due to artifacts introduced by Petri-net-to-BPMN translation and Graphviz layout:

1. **Parallel Gateways (`+`) Split Across Asymmetric Branches**:
   * *Visual Hazard*: After `Register Change`, an `AND`-split launches `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence`. However, after `Assess Risk`, a second `AND`-split forks into the `optional_legal` branch and the `verification_loop` prerequisite join. Because there is no single symmetric `AND`-join matching the first `AND`-split before `verification_loop`, readers trained on block-structured BPMN often misread the mid-process `AND`-join before `Configure Trial` as synchronizing the *entire* process, overlooking that `Legal Review` is still running in parallel outside that join.
2. **Exclusive Gateways (`X`) for Both Optionality and Loop Routing**:
   * *Visual Hazard*: BPMN uses identical diamond `XOR` gateways for two fundamentally different semantic constructs: (a) the forward decision to execute or skip `Legal Review`, and (b) the loop-entry merge and loop-exit/redo split around `Configure Trial  Inspect Trial` and `Correct Defect`. Without explicit gateway labels, readers frequently confuse the loop-back `XOR` join before `Configure Trial` with an alternative forward bypass path.
3. **Loop Visualization and Back-Edge Routing**:
   * *Visual Hazard*: Graphviz lays out BPMN graphs left-to-right (or top-to-bottom). The redo transition `Correct Defect` is often placed to the right of `Inspect Trial` with a long backward arc curving back to an `XOR` gateway before `Configure Trial`, or placed vertically adjacent to `Configure Trial`. A casual reader may misread `Correct Defect` as occurring *in parallel* with the trial body, or fail to see that at least one iteration of `Configure Trial  Inspect Trial` is mandatory before `Correct Defect` can ever be reached.
4. **Silent Routing Nodes and Chained Gateways**:
   * *Visual Hazard*: Because `POWL  Petri Net` creates explicit silent transitions ($\tau$) for `Operator.XOR` and `Operator.LOOP` boundaries, `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` translates multi-input/multi-output places and $\tau$-transitions into chained `XOR` and `AND` gateways connected by unlabeled sequence flows. Chains of two or three consecutive empty gateways create visual noise where tracing token dependencies by eye becomes error-prone.
5. **Apparent Ordering from 2D Graph Layout**:
   * *Visual Hazard*: A 2D layout engine must assign $(x, y)$ coordinates to every node. It typically places `Assess Risk` above `Prepare Evidence`, and `Legal Review` in the same horizontal column as `Configure Trial` or `Prepare Evidence`. Stakeholders routinely mistake horizontal alignment for simultaneous execution or left-to-right coordinate placement for strict sequential order (e.g., assuming `Legal Review` must finish before `Configure Trial` starts merely because `Legal Review` is drawn to the left of `Configure Trial`).

### Recommended Visual Annotations & Documentation Mitigations
* **Subprocess Grouping / Visual Enclosure**: Draw an annotated visual boundary (or BPMN Text Annotation box) around `Configure Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, and `Correct Defect` labeled: *"Verification Loop (Do-While: executes $\ge 1$ times; repeats via Correct Defect)"*.
* **Explicit Gateway & Edge Labels**:
  * Label the `XOR` split before `Legal Review`: *"Legal Review Required?"* with outgoing flows labeled `[Yes]` (to `Legal Review`) and `[No / Skip]` (bypass flow).
  * Label the `XOR` split after `Inspect Trial`: *"Trial Passed?"* with outgoing flows `[Pass]` (toward the pre-release `AND`-join) and `[Defect Found]` (toward `Correct Defect`).
  * Label the `AND`-join before `Configure Trial`: *"Wait for both Risk Assessment & Evidence"*.
  * Label the `AND`-join before `Release Change`: *"Synchronize Legal Branch & Passed Trial"*.
* **Concurrency Callout Note**: Attach a BPMN Text Annotation to `Legal Review` stating: *"Triggered once Assess Risk completes; runs concurrently with Prepare Evidence and the Verification Loop until Release Change."*

# 7. Rejection Criteria

A candidate conversion pipeline or generated model artifact must be **rejected or quarantined** if any of the following technical or semantic conditions occur:

1. **Null, Empty, or Multi-Token Improper Markings**: Any conversion returning `net is None`, `im is None`, `fm is None`, an empty initial/final marking, or failing `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)` must be immediately quarantined.
2. **Unsoundness (Deadlocks, Livelocks, or Dead Transitions)**: Failure of `pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)` (WOFLAN check). For example, if an mismatched `AND`/`XOR` gateway in BPMN leaves a dangling token when `Legal Review` is skipped or causes a deadlock at the `Release Change` join, the model is rejected.
3. **Failed Exact Final-Marking Termination**: If any trace reaches `Archive Change` while leaving residual tokens in upstream places (e.g., an unconsumed token in the `optional_legal` branch), or reaches the final marking `fm` without executing `Archive Change` (**INV-10** violation).
4. **Loss of Required Concurrency / Interleaving**: If the bounded visible language of the converted model drops any valid interleaving present in `net_from_powl`—specifically:
   * Rejecting either `AR  PE` or `PE  AR` (**INV-02**);
   * Rejecting early execution of `Legal Review` before `Prepare Evidence` (`RC, AR, LR, PE, ...`, **INV-04**);
   * Rejecting interleavings of `Legal Review` inside the verification loop (`RC, AR, PE, CT, LR, IT, ...`, **INV-06**).
5. **Admission of Premature Verification or Premature Release**:
   * Admitting `Configure Trial` before *both* `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` have completed (**INV-05** violation);
   * Admitting `Release Change` before `Legal Review` (when chosen) or the verification loop has exited (**INV-09** violation).
6. **Corruption of Optional or Loop Semantics**:
   * Collapsing `optional_legal` so that `Legal Review` is either always mandatory or permanently dead (**INV-03** violation);
   * Admitting zero verification iterations (`RC, AR, PE, Rel, AC`, converting a `do-while` loop into a `while` loop, **INV-07** violation);
   * Admitting consecutive `CT  IT` iterations without an intervening `Correct Defect`, or allowing `Correct Defect` to exit directly to `Release Change` without re-running `CT  IT` (**INV-08** violation).
7. **Activity Label Duplication or Loss**: If the converted model does not have the exact set of 9 visible activity labels, or duplicates visible transitions (breaking the 1-to-1 activity mapping assumed by footprint analysis).
8. **Uncaught Structural Reduction Exceptions**: When `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree` raises an exception (as in Pipeline B), the pipeline must be rejected rather than worked around via lossy heuristic discovery (such as running Inductive Miner on simulated playout logs, which would silently alter the N-shaped partial order).

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

### 1. Authoritative Storage Model vs. Stakeholder Visualization Model
* **Authoritative Canonical Model (Storage & Execution Truth)**: **The Source POWL Definition (`source_powl` in Python / serialized POWL AST)** paired with its deterministic **`POWL  Accepting Petri Net` (`net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl`)**.
  * *Why*: POWL natively and succinctly expresses both strict partial orders (including the non-block-structured N-dependency between `Assess Risk`, `Prepare Evidence`, `optional_legal`, and `verification_loop`) and hierarchical `XOR`/`LOOP` operators without introducing dozens of synthetic routing nodes.
* **Stakeholder Visualization Model**: **The BPMN 2.0 Model (`bpmn_from_net`) generated via Pipeline A (`POWL  Petri Net  BPMN`)**, augmented with the gateway labels and loop annotations specified in Section 6.
  * *Why*: Business and engineering stakeholders read BPMN far more reliably than Petri nets or POWL partial-order graphs, provided visual gateway ambiguities are annotated.

### 2. Derivative Artifacts & Required Validation Evidence
* `net_from_powl` (PNML), `bpmn_from_net` (`.bpmn` / `.png`), and reachability/footprint reports are **strictly derivative, read-only artifacts**.
* Every generated `.bpmn` artifact must be accompanied in CI/CD by an automated **Validation Manifest** containing:
  1. Confirmation that `net_rt_a = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)` is a sound workflow net (`check_is_workflow_net == True` and `check_soundness == True`).
  2. Exact equality of `discover_footprints` between `net_from_powl` and `net_rt_a`.
  3. Zero symmetric difference in the bounded visible trace language up to length $L = 12$ ($0$ missing traces, $0$ leaked traces).
  4. $100\%$ pass rate on the 7 positive and 13 negative invariant witnesses.

### 3. Policy on Process-Tree Intermediates
* **Process-tree intermediates (`Pipeline B`) must be disallowed** for this model family. Because `StrictPartialOrder` in POWL permits non-block-structured DAGs (such as the N-pattern here), forcing models through a Process Tree either fails outright with a conversion exception or—if approximated via log discovery—causes flower-model over-generalization or over-sequentialization (e.g., forcing `Prepare Evidence` before `Legal Review`).

### 4. Preventing Round-Trip Drift During Future Edits
* **Unidirectional Generation Only (`POWL  Petri Net  BPMN`)**: Never edit the generated BPMN diagram in a graphical modeler and convert it back to Petri net/POWL as the new source of truth. Round-tripping (`Petri  BPMN  Petri`) inflates the number of places and silent ($\tau$) transitions on every cycle, causing structural bloat and loss of high-level operator boundaries.
* **Edit at the POWL Layer**: All semantic changes to the change-control process must be authored in the `source_powl` Python specification (or a POWL editor), followed by automated regeneration of the Petri net and BPMN artifacts and execution of the Section 5 validation suite.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even when every structural diagnostic, footprint comparison, witness test, and bounded trace exploration in Section 5 passes with 100% agreement, the validation suite **cannot** establish the following claims:

1. **Unbounded Language Equivalence vs. True Concurrency Isomorphism (Weak Bisimulation)**:
   * Bounded visible trace exploration up to length $L = 12$ proves trace equivalence up to 3 loop iterations, and footprint analysis checks pairwise relations (`sequence`, `parallel`), but neither formally proves **weak bisimulation** (branching-time equivalence) across the entire infinite language ($k \to \infty$ loop iterations). For instance, subtle differences in where silent ($\tau$) transitions commit to internal non-deterministic choices (e.g., deciding to skip `Legal Review` early vs. late) are invisible to completed-trace equivalence.
2. **Human Cognitive Comprehension and Layout Fidelity of the Rendered BPMN**:
   * All programmatic checks operate on the in-memory `BPMN` graph object and its round-tripped `PetriNet`. They cannot verify that `pm4py.save_vis_bpmn` produced a visually legible `.png`/`.svg` free of overlapping edges, misleading spatial alignments, or unreadable gateway clusters. A model can be $100\%$ behaviorally equivalent in Petri-net space while still inducing systematic human misinterpretation in visual review.
3. **Non-Functional, Data-Aware, Temporal, and Resource Semantics**:
   * The POWL and accepting Petri net formalisms validate only **untimed, data-agnostic control-flow ordering**. Passing our validation suite does not establish:
     * Whether the loop terminates with probability $1$ in practice (stochastic termination vs. livelock under repeated defects);
     * Data-state invariants (e.g., that `Inspect Trial` inspects the exact configuration version produced by the most recent `Configure Trial`, or that `Release Change` is blocked if `Legal Review` resulted in a *rejection* rather than an *approval*);
     * Resource/role segregation of duties (e.g., ensuring the engineer who runs `Configure Trial` is not the same person who approves `Legal Review` or `Inspect Trial`).
