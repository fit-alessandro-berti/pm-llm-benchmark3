Shared causal mechanisms center on critical-path compression and join-point queueing. At both sites, when `Identity Check` and `Document Check` execute concurrently (`true_overlap`), total verification elapsed time shifts from the sum of task durations plus two serial queues toward the maximum of the two branches (`max(T_id, T_doc)`). Simultaneously, `Assessment` acts as an AND-join synchronization barrier: accelerating one branch yields zero downstream gain if the partner branch lags, and burstier completions can inflate `waiting_before_assessment` when `assessment_capacity` is tight.

Site-specific mechanisms break simple equivalence. At Site A, cross-trained staff dynamically rebalance queues, whereas Site A's observed gains were confounded by `S_co_interventions` (`API_latency` reductions, `queue_huddles`, added `document_capacity`, and lower `case_complexity`). Key effect modifiers (`S_`-pointed nodes) include `originals_required`, `digital_documents`, `IT_case_lock`, `role_specialization`, `case_complexity`, `assessment_capacity`, and `legal_capacity`. Because each alters the slope from `treatment_parallelization` to `true_overlap` and `cycle_time`, a superficially similar control-flow diagram cannot guarantee transportability. Control-flow graphs depict permissive task ordering, omitting statutory preconditions, resource contention, synchronization delays, IT locks, and cyclic congestion that determine actual causal effects.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

1. **Decomposing Site A's -9.0h median change:** In sequential execution, Site A's median verification service sum was $3.5 + 7.2 = 10.7\text{h}$. Even under 100% overlap with zero stochastic synchronization penalty, direct service-time overlap cannot save more than $\min(T_{\text{ID}}, T_{\text{Doc}}) \approx 3.1\text{–}3.5\text{h}$, plus eliminating one serial queue wait (~1.0–1.5h) = **3.5–4.5h true structural effect**. The remaining **4.5–5.5h** at Site A came from +11.1% FTE growth outpacing +8.3% volume, an 8 pp reduction in complex cases, lower API latency, and daily huddles.
2. **Projecting onto Site B's segments:**
   - **Digital-only claims (38% of Site B; 1,292 cases):** Currently mostly sequential (`Identity Check -> Document Check` = 1,160 DFG edges). ## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

### Process specification: Segmented Two-Mode Parallelization
1. **Eligibility & routing rules at `Register`:**
   - Classify every registered claim via deterministic statutory metadata `originals_required in {True, False}` and `legal_review_required in {True, False}`.
   - **Mode A (Digital-Sufficient, 38%):** `originals_required == False`. Both `Identity Check` and `Document Check` become immediately eligible after `Register`.
   - **Mode B (Originals-Required, 62%):** `originals_required == True`. `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt` become immediately eligible after `Register`. `Document Check` is **not** created at `Register`; it is instantiated strictly upon completion of `Originals Receipt`.
2. **Concurrent creation and execution:**
   - Deploy a lightweight child-work-item decoupling in the case system so `Identity Check` and `Document Check` (or `Originals Receipt`) hold independent task locks while parent case metadata remains read-accessible.
   - In Mode A, `Identity Check` and `Document Check` execute concurrently. In Mode B, `Identity Check` executes concurrently with external `Originals Receipt` waiting, and may overlap with `Document Check` if `Originals Receipt` finishes before `Identity Check`.
3. **Mandatory statutory prerequisites preserved:**
   - System hard-blocks `Document Check.start` whenever `originals_required == True` and `Originals Receipt.complete` is null.
   - System hard-blocks `Assessment.start` until both `Identity Check.complete` and `Document Check.complete` are recorded.
   - System hard-blocks final decision (`Approve` / `Reject`) on `legal_review_required == True` claims until `Legal Review.complete` is recorded without an active `Request Supplement`.
4. **Queue and WIP controls:**
   - Pull-based role queues with hard active-claim WIP caps per reviewer: **max 4** active tasks in `Identity Check` (4 FTEs), **max 4** in `Document Check` (7 FTEs), and **max 3** in `Assessment` (3 FTEs).
   - **Join-aware priority:** Once one verification branch (`Identity Check` or `Document Check`) completes on a case, the partner open branch receives a +25% aging priority boost to minimize synchronization wait before `Assessment`.
   - **Throttle valve:** If queued ready-for-Assessment cases exceed 18 (6 per assessor), suspend priority boosting on newly registered low-complexity claims and hold daily 10-minute cross-role queue huddles.
5. **Resource responsibilities & anomaly signaling:**
   - Role pools remain specialized for statutory compliance, but if `Identity Check` flags an identity/entity discrepancy, an immediate banner alert is pushed to the open `Document Check` child task so the document reviewer can pause or incorporate the finding rather than completing defective work.
6. **Handling of `Legal Review` and `Request Supplement` loops:**
   - After `Assessment`, cases with `legal_review_required == True` route to `Legal Review`.
   - If `Legal Review` issues `Request Supplement`, `Identity Check` is **not** repeated (unless specifically flagged); upon supplement arrival, `Document Check` and subsequently `Assessment` and `Legal Review` repeat.
   - Loop re-entry tasks (`Document Check [Supplement]` and `Assessment [Supplement]`) enter dedicated high-priority FIFO lanes ahead of fresh Mode A/B arrivals to protect tail latency (p90).
7. **Logging requirements:**
   - Record four immutable system-generated lifecycle events per child task (`created_at`, `claimed_at`, `started_at`, `completed_at`) plus `db_recorded_at` server timestamps. Disable manual retroactive timestamp editing; require an audited exception code for any offline recovery entry.

### Compact POWL / partial-order specification
```text
Let:
  OptOriginals = XOR( 'Originals Receipt', tau )
  DocVerif     = PO( nodes={OptOriginals, 'Document Check'},
                     order={OptOriginals -> 'Document Check'} )

  SuppCycle    = PO( nodes={'Request Supplement', 'Document Check_supp', 'Assessment_supp'},
                     order={'Request Supplement' -> 'Document Check_supp',
                            'Document Check_supp' -> 'Assessment_supp'} )
  LegalLoop    = LOOP( do='Legal Review', redo=SuppCycle )
  OptLegal     = XOR( LegalLoop, tau )
  Decision     = XOR( 'Approve', 'Reject' )

  SiteB_POWL   = PO(
      nodes = { 'Register', 'Identity Check', DocVerif, 'Assessment', OptLegal, Decision, 'Notify' },
      order = {
          'Register'       -> 'Identity Check',
          'Register'       -> DocVerif,
          'Identity Check' -> 'Assessment',
          DocVerif         -> 'Assessment',
          'Assessment'     -> OptLegal,
          OptLegal         -> Decision,
          Decision         -> 'Notify'
      }
  )
```

### Separating mechanism failure from implementation failure
The three-arm stratified design cleanly disentangles **implementation failure** (inability to produce genuine concurrent work) from **mechanism failure** (genuine concurrency fails to shorten end-to-end cycle time).

First, **implementation fidelity** is tested on the `treatment_parallelization -> true_overlap` pathway using immutable database `recorded_at` versus `started_at`/`completed_at` logs. If Arm T1 shows low verified `true_overlap` (<20% on digital claims) or elevated back-entry lag (`recorded_at - started_at > 5 min`) while Arm T2 lifts `true_overlap` above 60% with near-zero back-entry lag, legacy IT locking is confirmed as an implementation blocker. Conversely, if even Arm T2 fails to exceed 25% `true_overlap` because specialized pools are mismatched in arrival pacing, implementation failed operationally.

Second, **mechanism failure** is isolated along `true_overlap -> verification_span -> waiting_before_assessment -> cycle_time` inside Arm T2 versus Control. If Arm T2 achieves high verified `true_overlap` (>60%) and compresses verification span by ~3–4 hours, yet end-to-end `cycle_time` fails to improve (or p90 worsens) because `waiting_before_assessment`, `Legal Review` queue delay, or `Request Supplement` rework rises commensurately, then upstream parallelization works technically but fails causally due to downstream synchronization barriers and bottleneck shifts.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def finalize_partial_order(po: StrictPartialOrder) -> StrictPartialOrder:
    """Ensure transitive closure is populated when required by PM4Py's POWL validator."""
    if hasattr(po.order, "add_transitive_edges"):
        po.order.add_transitive_edges()
    return po


def extract_trace_labels(playout_result, max_traces: int = 5):
    """Extract readable activity sequences whether play_out returns EventLog or DataFrame."""
    if hasattr(playout_result, "columns"):  # Pandas DataFrame
        traces = []
        for _, group in playout_result.groupby("case:concept:name", sort=False):
            traces.append(list(group["concept:name"]))
            if len(traces) >= max_traces:
                break
        return traces
    return [
        [event["concept:name"] for event in trace]
        for trace in playout_result[:max_traces]
    ]


def build_and_verify_models():
    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    # 1 & 2. Site A Before and After Process Trees -> Accepting Petri Nets
    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    tree_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
        "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
    )
    tree_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
        "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
    )

    net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_before)
    net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_after)

    print("=== Site A Verification ===")
    print(
        "Site A Before - Workflow Net:",
        pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_before),
        "| Soundness:",
        pm4py.check_soundness(net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before),
    )
    print(
        "Site A After  - Workflow Net:",
        pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_after),
        "| Soundness:",
        pm4py.check_soundness(net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after),
    )

    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    # 3 & 4. Site B Candidate POWL Model
    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    # Core transitions
    t_register = Transition(label="Register")
    t_id_check = Transition(label="Identity Check")

    # Document verification sub-order: optional Originals Receipt -> Document Check
    t_originals = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
    opt_originals = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR, children=[t_originals, SilentTransition()]
    )
    t_doc_check = Transition(label="Document Check")
    po_doc_verification = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[opt_originals, t_doc_check])
    po_doc_verification.order.add_edge(opt_originals, t_doc_check)
    finalize_partial_order(po_doc_verification)

    # First-pass Assessment (synchronization point waiting for both checks)
    t_assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")

    # Optional Legal Review with Request Supplement rework loop:
    # When Legal Review occurs, it may exit directly or execute:
    # Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment -> Legal Review (repeatedly)
    t_legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    t_req_supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
    t_doc_check_rework = Transition(label="Document Check")
    t_assessment_rework = Transition(label="Assessment")

    po_supplement_rework = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[t_req_supplement, t_doc_check_rework, t_assessment_rework]
    )
    po_supplement_rework.order.add_edge(t_req_supplement, t_doc_check_rework)
    po_supplement_rework.order.add_edge(t_doc_check_rework, t_assessment_rework)
    finalize_partial_order(po_supplement_rework)

    loop_legal_supplement = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[t_legal_review, po_supplement_rework],
    )
    opt_legal_branch = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[loop_legal_supplement, SilentTransition()],
    )

    # Final decision and notification
    xor_decision = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[Transition(label="Approve"), Transition(label="Reject")],
    )
    t_notify = Transition(label="Notify")

    # Top-level StrictPartialOrder for Site B candidate
    powl_site_b = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            t_register,
            t_id_check,
            po_doc_verification,
            t_assessment,
            opt_legal_branch,
            xor_decision,
            t_notify,
        ]
    )

    # Explicit partial-order dependencies:
    # - Both verification streams wait for Register
    # - Assessment waits for BOTH Identity Check and Document Verification
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(t_register, t_id_check)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(t_register, po_doc_verification)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(t_id_check, t_assessment)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(po_doc_verification, t_assessment)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(t_assessment, opt_legal_branch)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(opt_legal_branch, xor_decision)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(xor_decision, t_notify)
    finalize_partial_order(powl_site_b)

    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    # 5 & 6. Convert Site B POWL to Accepting Petri Net and Check Soundness
    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl_site_b)

    print("\n=== Site B POWL Candidate Verification ===")
    print("Site B Candidate - Workflow Net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_b))
    print("Site B Candidate - Soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b))

    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    # 7. Sample Playout Traces
    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    sim_log_b = pm4py.play_out(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    print("\n=== Sample Playout Traces (Site B Candidate) ===")
    for idx, trace in enumerate(extract_trace_labels(sim_log_b, max_traces=6), start=1):
        print(f"Trace {idx}: {' -> '.join(trace)}")

    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    # 8. Modeling Limitations (What Control-Flow POWL / Petri Nets Cannot Encode)
    # -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    # a) Data-dependent statutory guards: `opt_originals` and `opt_legal_branch` are
    #    modeled as untyped XOR choices (`XOR(..., tau)`). Pure POWL/WF-nets cannot
    #    enforce that `originals_required == True` deterministically forces `Originals Receipt`,
    #    nor that `public_sector_or_high_value == True` forces `Legal Review`.
    # b) Resource specialization, pool capacities, and WIP caps: The Petri net permits
    #    unbounded token concurrency across cases, omitting the 4/7/3/2 FTE role pools,
    #    cross-case queueing before `Assessment`, and per-reviewer WIP limits.
    # c) Legacy IT case locking & timestamp artifacts: A token enabled in parallel places
    #    represents logical enablement, not whether single-owner case locks block two
    #    human reviewers from simultaneously starting `Identity Check` and `Document Check`
    #    (atomic transition firing also collapses non-zero start-to-complete overlap intervals).
    # d) Stochastic durations & heavy tails: Standard POWL/Petri playout is untimed and
    #    cannot capture the 18h skewed `Originals Receipt` delay, 20h pre-Assessment wait,
    #    or tail congestion under supplement rework loops.
```

## 9. Recommendation

**Decision: `conditional go`**

Site B should **not** copy Site A’s universal post-registration parallelization, which would violate statutory prerequisites on 62% of claims and collide with single-owner case locking. Instead, Site B should execute a **segmented, IT-enabled conditional rollout**.

**What to transfer:** Transfer the partial-order principle of decoupling independent upstream activities before the `Assessment` synchronization join, paired with explicit per-role WIP caps and structured queue management. Specifically, run `Identity Check` concurrently with `Document Check` when digital documents are legally sufficient, and run `Identity Check` concurrently with `Originals Receipt` (not `Document Check`) when physical originals are mandated.

**What not to copy:** Reject universal creation of `Document Check` immediately after `Register`; spawning legally blocked tasks for 62% of volume floods the 7-FTE document pool with unworkable WIP. Do not assume cross-team load balancing across Site B’s four specialized roles, and do not project Site A’s confounded 9-hour median gain onto Site B.

**First-mover subgroup:** Launch first in **Stratum 1A: digital-eligible, non-legal claims** (~23% of volume), then expand to **Stratum 1B: digital-eligible legal-review claims** (~15%), only after deploying child-task IT unlocking and immutable event-commit timestamps.

**Expected benefits and risks:** In digital-only strata, true concurrency should trim median cycle time by **3.5–5.0 hours** (blended site-wide median improvement of **1.8–3.2 hours** once originals-wait parallelization is standardized). Key risks include shifting queues to the 3-FTE `Assessment` and 2-FTE `Legal Review` pools—improving median duration while worsening p90 tail latency—alongside premature rework if `Document Check` misses identity-mismatch signals, or timestamp distortion from lock workarounds.

**Decision gates:**
- **Expand** to all eligible claims if Stratum 1 achieves >=60% verified `true_overlap`, >=3.0-hour median cycle reduction in digital claims, p90 degradation <=2%, and rework/appeal increases <=1.0 percentage point.
- **Modify** (reallocate/cross-train capacity into `Assessment` or tighten upstream WIP release) if verification span drops >=2.5 hours but `waiting_before_assessment` rises >20%.
- **Abandon** if IT unlocking fails to lift verified `true_overlap` above 25% without manual back-entry, or if rework rises >2.5 percentage points.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Statutory originals status is deterministically known at `Register` —** needed to route the 38% digital-sufficient claims to immediate `Document Check` without risking illegal premature checks on the 62% originals-required claims — **how to test:** audit historical claims to verify whether `originals_required` ever flips from `False` to `True` mid-verification — **consequence if false:** `Document Check` starts prematurely on misclassified claims, causing statutory non-compliance and wasted review effort.
2. **`Identity Check` and `Document Check` have low informational dependency —** needed so executing `Document Check` without prior `Identity Check` results does not generate defective assessments or extra `Request Supplement` cycles — **how to test:** inspect a random sample of 150 historical rework/supplement cases to measure what fraction hinged on identity findings — **consequence if false:** parallelization inflates Site B's 17% rework rate and overloads `Document Check` and `Legal Review`.
3. **Child-task IT unlocking can be configured without full legacy platform replacement —** needed so two specialized reviewers can hold simultaneous active tasks with reliable start/complete timestamps — **how to test:** run a 2-week technical burn-in logging lock-contention errors and `recorded_at - started_at` timestamp lag — **consequence if false:** reviewers revert to single-owner queuing or offline shadow work, collapsing `true_overlap` toward the 6% baseline.
4. **Reported service times (`3.4h` ID, `8.1h` Doc) include intra-task pauses rather than pure continuous touch time —** needed for consistent capacity math, since $3,400 \times 8.1\text{h} = 27,540\text{h}$ exceeds 7 FTEs' raw 6-month clock hours (~7,000h) unless tasks remain open across pauses/queries — **how to test:** shadow 30 tasks or analyze sub-event activity logs to separate active touch minutes from open-task residence time — **consequence if false:** WIP caps and utilization estimates will be miscalibrated, either starving reviewers or inducing severe multitasking slowdowns.
5. **`Originals Receipt` Requirement (62%) and `Legal Review` Requirement (40%) are only moderately correlated —** needed to size Stratum 1A (digital + non-legal, estimated ~23% under near-independence) for pilot statistical power — **how to test:** cross-tabulate `originals_required` by `legal_review_required` in Site B's 3,400 baseline cases — **consequence if false:** if almost all digital claims require `Legal Review` (or vice versa), Stratum 1A shrinks and pilot duration must be extended to hit minimum support.
6. **Downstream `Assessment` (3 FTE) and `Legal Review` (2 FTE) pools operate below critical saturation ($\rho < 0.90$) —** needed so compressed upstream arrivals do not convert verification time savings one-for-one into downstream queue waiting — **how to test:** compute arrival rates, active assessment touch times, and hourly queue-length trajectories over the past 6 months — **consequence if false:** Scenario S3 materializes—median cycle time barely moves while p90 tail latency surges above 120 hours.
7. **External `Originals Receipt` delay (median 18h) is invariant to internal task parallelization —** needed to treat courier/claimant submission latency as exogenous when running `Identity Check || Originals Receipt` — **how to test:** compare `Register -> Originals Receipt` elapsed distributions across pilot arms C, T1, and T2 — **consequence if false:** if claimants delay mailing originals until `Identity Check` prompts them, holding or parallelizing tasks could inadvertently shift mail arrival timing.
8. **Cross-arm spillover in shared downstream pools can be controlled statistically during case-level randomization —** needed because Arms C, T1, and T2 ultimately merge at the 3-FTE `Assessment` and 2-FTE `Legal Review` pools — **how to test:** regress control-arm `waiting_before_assessment` on rolling hourly treated-case share in the join queue — **consequence if false:** unmodeled queue interference biases `(T2 - C)` treatment effect estimates toward zero by slowing Control and Treated claims alike, requiring switchback or cluster-time randomization instead.
