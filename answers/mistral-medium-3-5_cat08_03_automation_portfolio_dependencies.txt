# 2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map

### **Upstream Data Quality Dependencies**
- **A1 (Auto-registration)** improves input quality for **A2 (Classification)** and **A3 (KYC pre-classifier)** by reducing manual errors.
- **A5 (Missing-doc checklist)** reduces rework for **A6 (Normalization)**, which in turn feeds **A8 (Committee pack)**.
- **A3 (KYC)** must precede **A4 (Entity mismatch)** and **A10 (E-signature)** due to legal controls.

### **Legal/Compliance Controls**
- **A3 (KYC)** and **A12 (Remediation workbench)** are critical for compliance. **A9 (Contract generation)** must enforce four-eyes principle (requires **A8** for clean inputs).
- **A11 (Activation bot)** cannot proceed without **A3 (KYC clearance)** and **A9 (Contract controls)**.

### **Human Escalation Capacity**
- **A4 (Entity mismatch)** depends on **Compliance specialists (3 FTE)**, a bottleneck. Automating **A3** reduces their load but requires escalation paths.
- **A6 (Normalization)** offloads **Credit analysts (9 FTE)**, but poor PDF quality may shift work to exceptions.

### **External Valuation Dependency**
- **A7 (Collateral triage)** is limited by external valuers. Parallelizing requests (e.g., via **A7**) helps but doesn’t eliminate wait times.

### **Core-Banking Integration Constraints**
- **A1 (Registration)** and **A9 (Contract generation)** likely require deep integration (2-module limit in first 6 months).
- **A10 (E-signature)** and **A11 (Activation)** may need lightweight integration.

# 3. Portfolio Alternatives

### **Portfolio 1: Quick-Win Portfolio**
**Modules:** A1, A2, A5, A10
**Optimizes:** Immediate labor savings, political visibility (high-frequency tasks).
**Sacrifices:**
- Ignores **KYC/entity mismatch** (critical path delays).
- **A6 (Normalization)** not included  Credit analysts remain bottlenecked.
- Limited compliance improvement (violates "at least one control-strengthening module" constraint).

### **Portfolio 2: Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio**
**Modules:** A3, A4, A6, A8
**Optimizes:**
- **Cycle time**: Reduces **KYC (4h wait)**, **entity mismatch (31h wait)**, and **normalization (20h wait)**.
- **Rework**: **A6** cuts financial-statement loops; **A8** reduces committee packaging errors.
- **Handovers**: Fewer compliance  credit analyst handoffs.
**Sacrifices:**
- Lower direct labor savings (A3/A4 have medium feasibility).
- **A1/A2** not included  Registration/classification remain manual.

### **Portfolio 3: Compliance-First Portfolio**
**Modules:** A3, A9, A12, A10
**Optimizes:**
- **Control violations**: **A3** (KYC), **A9** (contract controls), **A12** (remediation) directly address findings.
- **Auditability**: All include human override trails.
- **Legal risk**: **A9** reduces contract-remediation loops (14% for credit-line renewals).
**Sacrifices:**
- **Throughput**: No direct relief for **financial document review** or **collateral valuation**.
- **Customer friction**: **A10** (e-signature) may face resistance if not paired with **A5**.

- **Rework**: **A5** cuts missing-doc loops (24%  ~15%), but **entity mismatch (38%)** untouched.
- **Handovers**: Fewer back-office  customer loops for documents.
- **Resource demand**: Back office (12 FTE) underutilized post-15:00; Credit analysts still overloaded.
- **Control violations**: No improvement (provisional signing risk remains).

- **Cycle time**: **~30% reduction** (KYC + entity mismatch + normalization are critical path in 82% of cases with entity issues).
- **Waiting time**: **KYC (4h  1h)**, **entity mismatch (31h  10h)**, **normalization (20h  5h)**.
- **Rework**: **A6** reduces financial-statement rework (16%  5%); **A8** cuts committee packaging errors (15%  8%).
- **Handovers**: Compliance  Credit analyst handoffs smoother (fewer KYC blocks).
- **Resource demand**: Compliance specialists (3 FTE) less strained; Credit analysts (9 FTE) see reduced load.
- **Control violations**: **A3** reduces provisional signing (6.5%  2%), but **A9/A12** missing  contract remediation unchanged.

- **Cycle time**: **~20% reduction** (KYC and contract prep faster, but financial review unchanged).
- **Waiting time**: **KYC (4h  1h)**, **contract prep (22h  8h)** (due to **A9**).
- **Rework**: **A12** reduces control remediation loops (8.5%  3%).
- **Handovers**: Legal ops (4 FTE) less overloaded at month-end.
- **Resource demand**: Compliance specialists relieved; Legal ops demand stabilizes.
- **Control violations**: **Four-eyes violations (5.8%  0%)**, **provisional signing (6.5%  1.1%)**, **contract mismatches (14%  5%)**.

  - KYC clearance time (target: <1h median).
  - Control violations (target: <3% provisional signing).
- **Gate Criteria:** A3 false-negative rate <0.5%; A12 reduces remediation loops by 40%.

# 7. Final Recommendation

**Recommended Portfolio:** **Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (A3, A4, A6, A8)**
**Rationale:**
1. **Process-Level Impact**: Addresses the **highest waiting-time activities** (KYC, entity mismatch, normalization) that dominate cycle time (per DFG and variant analysis).
2. **Dependency Alignment**: A3  A4  A6  A8 forms a **critical path chain** (KYC  financials  committee). Automating these sequentially avoids downstream bottlenecks.
3. **Compliance Inclusion**: A3 and A8 indirectly strengthen controls (KYC clearance, consistent committee packs), satisfying the "at least one control module" constraint.
4. **Resource Balance**: Reduces load on **Compliance specialists (3 FTE)** and **Credit analysts (9 FTE)**, the most constrained teams.
5. **Risk Mitigation**: A3/A4 include human escalation; A6/A8 have pre-validation checks.

**Why Not Quick-Wins (A1/A2)?**
- **A1 (Auto-registration)** has high frequency but **exposes KYC/classification bottlenecks** (per process mining: KYC has 4h wait, 11% rework).
- **A2 (Classification)** risks misrouting High-exposure cases, increasing control violations.

**Why Not Compliance-First?**
- While **A3/A9/A12** address controls, they **ignore financial document review** (24% rework, 18h wait), which is a major throughput constraint.

**Trade-Off:**
- **Sacrifices** some direct labor savings (e.g., A1’s high-frequency wins) for **end-to-end cycle-time reduction** and **risk mitigation**.
- **Prioritizes synchronization** (e.g., A4’s low frequency but 31h wait) over volume, aligning with the process-mining insight that "frequency alone is misleading."

**First-Year Rollout:**
- Start with **A3 + A12** (Phase 1) to satisfy compliance and build audit trails.
- Add **A4 + A6** (Phase 2) to tackle the critical path.
- Defer **A8** to Phase 3 to ensure upstream data quality (A6) is stable.
- **A10** (e-signature) is added last as it depends on **A3 (KYC clearance)**.
