## 1. Normalization Assumptions

### **Interval Construction**
- For each recorded timestamp, construct a feasible UTC interval by:
  1. Converting the recorded time to UTC using the system's time zone and DST rules.
  2. Applying the system's clock-offset bound (as a symmetric interval around the UTC-converted time).
  3. Applying timestamp-resolution uncertainty (floor/rounding semantics) as a one-sided or symmetric interval:
     - **Floor semantics**: `[recorded_time, recorded_time + resolution_bound)`.
     - **Round semantics**: `[recorded_time - resolution_bound/2, recorded_time + resolution_bound/2]`.
  4. For DST-ambiguous timestamps (e.g., LEGACY-BER), preserve all feasible intervals consistent with ingestion order and DST rules.
  5. For missing events (e.g., T-404's `Investigation_Start`), infer intervals from other evidence (e.g., `Containment_Complete` minus duration bounds).

### **Clock-Offset Direction**
- Clock offsets are applied as `recorded_time ± offset_bound` in UTC. Negative offsets mean the system clock is slow; positive offsets mean it is fast.

### **Time-Zone Conversion**
- Use `pytz` or `zoneinfo` to convert local times to UTC, accounting for DST where applicable.

### **DST Folds**
- For LEGACY-BER (2025-10-26), the DST transition at 02:00–02:59 creates two possible UTC intervals for local times in this range:
  - **Pre-DST fold**: UTC = local time - 1 hour (e.g., 02:05 local = 01:05 UTC).
  - **Post-DST fold**: UTC = local time - 2 hours (e.g., 02:05 local = 00:05 UTC).
- Ingestion order rules out impossible combinations (e.g., `Investigation_Start` cannot be before `Alert_Received`).

### **Inferred Starts (T-404)**
- For missing `Investigation_Start`, use `Containment_Complete` minus the duration bounds (18–25 minutes) to derive a feasible interval for `Investigation_Start`.

### **Causal Constraints**
- For T-407, assume `Alert_Received` must occur within `[Alert_Sent + 0, Alert_Sent + 20]` minutes (network latency bound). `Investigation_Start` must occur after `Alert_Received`.

### **Mobile Entry Delay (T-406)**
- The user may have entered the timestamp up to 5 minutes late. This shifts the **recorded time** but does not affect the **true occurrence time**. Thus, the feasible interval for `Investigation_Start` is:
  - `[recorded_time - 5 min, recorded_time]` (since the user may have entered it up to 5 minutes after occurrence).

## 7. Governance Recommendation

### **Timestamp Controls**
1. **UTC Storage**: Store all timestamps in UTC with sub-second precision (e.g., ISO 8601 with microseconds).
2. **Raw Retention**: Retain unprocessed timestamps for forensic analysis.
3. **Offset Telemetry**: Log clock-offset bounds and resolution metadata for each system.

### **Clock Management**
- **NTP Synchronization**: Enforce daily NTP checks for high-trust systems (e.g., HUB, OPS-BER).
- **Offset Alerts**: Trigger alerts for offsets exceeding ±30s.

### **Time-Zone/DST Handling**
- **DST Fold Logging**: Log DST transitions and fold ambiguity for systems like LEGACY-BER.
- **Automated Conversion**: Use `zoneinfo` or `pytz` for timezone conversions with DST awareness.

### **Lifecycle Semantics**
- **Start/Complete Clarity**: Distinguish between event start/completion (e.g., "investigation start" vs. "investigation complete").
- **Causal Identifiers**: Link alerts to investigations with unique IDs to enforce causality.

### **Provenance**
- **Source Metadata**: Include system, resolution, and offset bounds in every timestamp.
- **User Entry Policies**: For mobile entries, enforce timestamp validation or delay calibration.

### **Enforcement Policy**
- **Interval Overlap**: Require **non-overlapping intervals** for SLA violations (e.g., `Investigation_Start` must be 15 min after `Alert_Received`).
- **Audit Trails**: Log all timestamp normalizations and interval derivations.

---
This framework ensures defensible incident-response timing analysis while accounting for uncertainty.
