## 1. Control-Objective Decomposition

Assessment is grounded in the uploaded FEP-1 rule, compensating-control definitions, event log, and case facts. 

| FEP-1 objective / threat scenario                                                                                          | AV-2                                                                                                                                                                                             | MG-4                                                                                                                                                        | RA-7                                                                                                 | ED-1                                                                                                                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Segregate initiation from authorization**: prevent the same human from both preparing and releasing a completed payment. | Does **not** establish human independence. It may reduce error/fraud opportunities but cannot prove two independent actors.                                                                      | Can partially substitute only if the manager is independent and reviews before release or at least before settlement.                                       | Detects after the fact; cannot establish pre-release independence.                                   | Permits single-operator handling only under strict emergency conditions; does not make the four-eyes objective disappear.                            |
| **Prevent vendor/bank-account fraud**, especially recent bank changes or compromised payee data.                           | Strong for duplicate invoice, sanctions, bank ownership, recent bank change, operator limit, and basic PO match. Weak or absent when unavailable, overridden, warning-only, or outside coverage. | Strong if invoice, bank details, and receipt/support are reviewed independently before irrevocable settlement. Weak if conflicted, late, or rubber-stamped. | Can detect, and may correct only inside recall/recovery window.                                      | Requires no recent bank change, full AV-2 pass without override, and independent MG-4 before settlement; otherwise not eligible.                     |
| **Prevent commercial-substance failures**, such as payment for services not received or fabricated receipts.               | Fails to address goods/services receipt and commercial reasonableness. A clean AV-2 pass is not evidence of receipt.                                                                             | Potentially strong if the manager actually reviews supporting receipt/service evidence.                                                                     | Can detect fabricated support later, but corrective value depends on timing and recovery.            | Does not itself verify commercial substance except through the required independent manager review.                                                  |
| **Prevent unauthorized override or credential misuse.**                                                                    | Can emit warning or block, but warning override creates risk. It does not prove who controlled a credential.                                                                                     | Strong only if the approving manager is truly independent and approval is not merely credential sharing or ratification.                                    | Detects override misuse later; correction depends on recall and evidence.                            | ED-1 forbids AV-2 override because it requires full AV-2 pass without override.                                                                      |
| **Resist collusion and independence erosion.**                                                                             | Weak: automated checks can reduce simple errors but do not detect collusion, shared workstations, or cozy repeated pairs.                                                                        | Moderate: independent manager review can challenge collusive preparer/releaser behavior, but only if the manager is not conflicted and reviews substance.   | Can identify patterns across cases, repeated pairings, shared workstations, and anomalous approvals. | Weak to moderate: ED-1 narrows exposure by amount, vendor age, bank-change, AV-2, manager, and audit conditions, but it is not a collusion detector. |
| **Preserve recoverability before funds become effectively irrevocable.**                                                   | Preventive if it blocks before release. Less protective if it only warns and is overridden.                                                                                                      | Preventive or hybrid if before bank settlement; mostly detective after settlement.                                                                          | Corrective only if audit occurs soon enough for recall, cancellation, or recovery.                   | Requires RA-7 within one business day and manager approval before settlement, preserving at least some recoverability.                               |
| **Maintain accountable human identity.**                                                                                   | Fails to identify whether a shared or compromised account was controlled by an independent human.                                                                                                | Helps if manager identity, level, independence, and review evidence are reliable.                                                                           | Helps through logs, audit trails, device/session review, and credential investigation.               | Requires pre-existing emergency authorization, not after-the-fact justification.                                                                     |

The key point is that FEP-1 is not simply “two activities occurred” or “one activity was automated.” Its objective is independent human challenge at a point where the transfer can still be stopped.

## 2. Equivalence and Sufficiency Test

A compensating package should be evaluated against a structured sufficiency test:

| Test dimension           | Fully equivalent                                                                                                                      | Partially equivalent                                                            | Merely mitigating                                                                  | Ineffective                                                                             |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Timing**               | Independent approval before Release_Payment, or at minimum before irrevocable settlement where release is not yet economically final. | Approval after release but before settlement with credible stop/recall ability. | Approval after settlement or audit after recall window.                            | No approval, or action after loss is unrecoverable.                                     |
| **Independence**         | Different human actor; not preparer/releaser; no shared account; no unresolved conflict; sufficient authority.                        | Different actor but some contextual independence concerns.                      | Supervisor/conflicted approver or repeated close pair without proof of misconduct. | Same person, shared account, delegated credential, or direct conflict.                  |
| **Coverage**             | Covers vendor, bank account, amount, sanctions, invoice, PO, receipt/service evidence, and commercial plausibility.                   | Covers most payment-validity risks but not all business-substance risks.        | Covers only technical checks such as duplicate/bank/sanctions.                     | AV unavailable, warning overridden, or controls outside the relevant risk.              |
| **Overrideability**      | No override, or override requires independent authorization and leaves strong evidence.                                               | Override exists but is justified and reviewed before settlement.                | Override by operational user, later ratified.                                      | Same user overrides warning using another person’s credential.                          |
| **Recoverability**       | Defect can still be blocked, cancelled, or recalled.                                                                                  | Some recovery possible but uncertain.                                           | Audit occurs, but mainly for future learning.                                      | Audit after recall window or after funds unrecovered.                                   |
| **Evidence quality**     | Timestamped, reviewable, complete evidence of who reviewed what and when.                                                             | Some evidence, but missing level, exact review scope, or token provenance.      | Narrative justification without complete artifacts.                                | Unsigned manual checklist, missing identity evidence, or unresolved credential sharing. |
| **Collusion resistance** | Independent challenge plus pattern monitoring for repeated pairs, shared workstations, unusual approvals, and conflicts.              | Formal independence but repeated-pair or workstation concerns.                  | Automated checks alone.                                                            | Same user or collusive pair can initiate and authorize without meaningful challenge.    |

**Classification standard used here**

* **OBJECTIVE_MET**: either nominal FEP-1 is satisfied with no serious independence/substance warning, or a compensating package is close enough to the FEP-1 objective at the relevant risk boundary.
* **OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET**: meaningful controls reduced risk, but independence, timing, coverage, or evidence gaps prevent equivalence.
* **OBJECTIVE_FAILED**: control objective was not achieved; the case was completed despite severe independence, timing, coverage, or fraud indicators.
* **LOSS_PREVENTED_BEFORE_COMPLETION**: no completed payment because a preventive control stopped the chain.
* **INDETERMINATE**: the answer depends on missing evidence that materially changes the verdict.

“Same expected loss” is not enough because FEP-1 is also a governance, accountability, auditability, and deterrence control. Two packages may have similar expected monetary loss but differ sharply in who can authorize, whether misconduct is visible, whether evidence is reliable, whether a regulator can understand the decision, and whether the organization is resilient to collusion or credential compromise.

## 4. Preventive–Detective–Corrective Comparison

| Control                                   | Primary nature                                                                                              | Strong use                                                                                                                                                                                                | Weak use                                                                                              | Failure mode shown in cases                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **AV-2 — Automated validation**           | Preventive, sometimes warning-only.                                                                         | Full pass before release, no override, service operational, within coverage. Strong in AP-203, AP-205, AP-207.                                                                                            | Warning that can be overridden, or clean pass used as if it proved receipt/commercial reasonableness. | AP-202 warning overridden; AP-208 unavailable; AP-204 clean pass did not detect fabricated receipt.                                              |
| **MG-4 — Independent manager approval**   | Preventive if before release; hybrid if after release but before settlement; detective if after settlement. | Independent manager, L3+, not preparer/releaser, no conflict, substantive review before release or settlement.                                                                                            | Ratification after release, especially after settlement, or approval by a conflicted supervisor.      | AP-202 m09 was u23’s supervisor and ratified late; AP-208 ratification after settlement; AP-201/AP-207 were after release but before settlement. |
| **RA-7 — Retrospective audit and recall** | Detective; corrective only while recall/recovery remains practical.                                         | Audit within one business day and inside recall window, with ability to cancel/recover.                                                                                                                   | Audit after recall window or after settlement with no recovery path.                                  | AP-202 audit after recall window; AP-208 audit four days later.                                                                                  |
| **ED-1 — Emergency permission**           | Conditional exception framework; not a standalone control.                                                  | All seven conditions satisfied: pre-existing token, amount below EUR 100,000, mature vendor, no recent bank change, AV-2 full pass without override, independent manager before settlement, timely audit. | Any missing condition turns it from permission into an unsupported same-user exception.               | AP-203 and AP-207 plausible; AP-206 uncertain because token timing is late; AP-208 clearly ineligible.                                           |

**Manager action after Release_Payment but before Bank_Settlement**

I treat this as **hybrid preventive/detective**. It is detective relative to the internal release event because it occurs after the same-user release has already happened. It is preventive or corrective relative to economic finality if the manager can still stop, hold, cancel, or recall the payment before bank settlement. Therefore AP-201 and AP-207 are not equivalent to clean pre-release four-eyes control, but they are materially stronger than post-settlement ratification.

## 6. Evidence and Monitoring Recommendations

| Monitoring rule                                  | Why it matters                                                                                                 | Alert threshold / handling                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Same-user prepare/release rate**               | Direct FEP-1 nominal violation for completed payments.                                                         | Alert every completed payment where Prepare_Payment and Release_Payment resource intersect; classify separately by ED-1 eligibility and compensating controls.                                          |
| **Attempted same-user release after AV-2 block** | Shows pressure or behavior that would have violated FEP-1 if not blocked.                                      | Track as conduct/process-risk signal, not as completed-payment violation. AP-205 belongs here.                                                                                                          |
| **Override rate and override identity**          | Warning overrides convert AV-2 from preventive to discretionary.                                               | Alert when the releaser overrides their own warning, uses another person’s credential, or override occurs on recent bank change/operator-limit warnings.                                                |
| **Late manager approval / ratification**         | Manager review after settlement is mostly detective and weakly corrective.                                     | Separate: before release, after release-before settlement, after settlement, and after recall window.                                                                                                   |
| **Audit-before-recall-window**                   | RA-7 is corrective only while recall or cancellation remains plausible.                                        | Emergency same-user payments should receive 100% audit within one business day, with an additional operational target inside the 24-hour bank recall window where applicable.                           |
| **Repeated resource pairs**                      | Stable pairs can be efficient dedicated cells or collusion risk.                                               | Alert repeated preparer/releaser pairs above a threshold, especially when paired with shared workstation, high amount, fabricated receipt, or overrides. Do not label misconduct without corroboration. |
| **Shared workstation/session/IP**                | Human independence may be illusory if two accounts are operated from the same endpoint/session.                | Flag same workstation within short time windows, especially for high-value payments and repeat pairs.                                                                                                   |
| **Bank-change proximity**                        | Recent bank changes are high-risk and are explicitly in AV-2/ED-1.                                             | Escalate bank changes within seven days; auto-disallow ED-1; require independent bank-ownership proof.                                                                                                  |
| **Control outages**                              | AV-2 unavailability removes a major preventive layer.                                                          | During AV-2 outage, require signed manual checklist plus independent pre-settlement approval; high-value or recent-bank-change cases should be held unless exception authority is explicit.             |
| **Emergency token timing**                       | ED-1 requires token active before Prepare_Payment.                                                             | Alert token creation after preparation; require system proof of activation time or classify as indeterminate/nonconforming.                                                                             |
| **Manager independence and level**               | MG-4 fails if the manager is conflicted, below authority, or the preparer/releaser’s direct performance rater. | Maintain manager hierarchy, L-level, conflict-of-interest, and delegation records; block conflicted ratification.                                                                                       |
| **Substantive review evidence**                  | A manager action is weak if it does not show review of invoice, bank details, and receipt.                     | Require structured review artifacts, especially for consulting, services, high amount, and emergency cases.                                                                                             |

Alerts should be treated as **risk indicators**, not proof of misconduct. For example, repeated u12/u13 pairings could indicate collusion, but could also reflect a dedicated two-person payment cell; the missing organizational design documents are therefore material.

def first_time(case_id, activities):
    rows = df[
        df["case:concept:name"].eq(case_id)
        & df["concept:name"].isin(activities)
    ].sort_values("time:timestamp")
    return rows["time:timestamp"].iloc[0] if not rows.empty else pd.NaT

def first_resource(case_id, activity):
    rows = df[
        df["case:concept:name"].eq(case_id)
        & df["concept:name"].eq(activity)
    ].sort_values("time:timestamp")
    return rows["org:resource"].iloc[0] if not rows.empty else None

    return set(

This is evidence generation, not the final compliance verdict, because sufficiency still depends on timing, independence, evidence quality, recoverability, and case-specific risk.
