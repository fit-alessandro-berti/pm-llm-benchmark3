# 3. Resilience versus concealed noncompliance

In this incident, **Resilience** is defined as the capacity to maintain critical mission outcomes (hospital supply) while adhering to minimum viable controls. **Concealed Noncompliance** is the accumulation of regulatory debt (missing signatures, temps, auths) that is hidden during the crisis and resolved only after operational metrics appear normal.

*   **True Resilience:** Patterns **A (Hospital fast lane)** combined with **B (Manual auth)** and **I (Defer noncritical)**. These adaptations protected patient safety (91% hospital fill rate) by sacrificing lower-priority throughput (61% pharmacy fill rate) without explicitly bypassing legal controls.
*   **Risky Shortcuts (Concealed Noncompliance):** Patterns **C (Skip auth)**, **G (Dispatch without scan)**, and **L (Audit remediation)**. The T4 DFG shows `Invoice close -> Audit remediation` (2,300 cases). This proves that orders were invoiced and closed *before* compliance was verified. This is not resilience; it is regulatory borrowing. The 2.9% missing dual-control for substances in T2 is a legal violation, not a resilience metric.
*   **Ambiguous Adaptations:** Pattern **E (Manual pick list)**. If paper lists included signature fields for later scanning, it is resilient. If they were plain text spreadsheets requiring manual reconstruction (Pattern K), they create hidden workload. The 12.5% rework rate in T2 suggests the latter occurred.

**Conclusion:** The organization successfully protected patients (Resilience) but funded that protection by accruing compliance debt that required 2,300 audit remediation cases in the Stabilization phase (Concealed Noncompliance). Future plans must decouple these: protect patients *without* bypassing chain-of-custody controls.

# 4. Recovery process model

**Model Name:** Contingency Workflow v1.0 (Pre-Approved Manual Mode)
**Notation:** Structured Process Description (BPMN-inspired)

**1. Trigger Conditions**
*   **Activation:** WMS unavailable > 2 hours OR Cyber Incident Declared.
*   **Deactivation:** WMS integrity verified AND Backlog < 24h volume.

**2. Triage Rules (Entry Gateway)**
*   **Priority 1 (Critical):** Hospital-critical + Cold-chain/Controlled. -> *Route to Manual Lane A.*
*   **Priority 2 (Standard):** Pharmacy-standard + Ambient. -> *Route to Manual Lane B.*
*   **Priority 3 (Bulk):** Wholesale-bulk. -> *Route to Hold Queue (Pattern I).*

**3. Minimum Controls by Risk (Parallel Gateways)**
*   **Controlled Substance:**
    *   Requires **Manual Authorization Check (Pattern B)** via offline approved list.
    *   Requires **Dual Signature** on paper dispatch note (No Pattern G).
    *   *Forbidden:* Skip auth (Pattern C) or Dispatch without scan (Pattern G).
*   **Cold-chain:**
    *   Requires **Manual Temp Log** attached to physical box.
    *   *Forbidden:* Dispatch without temp logger.
*   **Ambient:**
    *   Requires **Customer History Check** (Pattern B light).
    *   Allows **Phone POD (Pattern H)** if signature unavailable.

**4. Manual Evidence Capture**
*   Use **Pre-printed Contingency Forms** (barcoded for later scanning) instead of blank spreadsheets (Pattern E modification).
*   Forms must capture: Order ID, Picker ID, Checker ID, Timestamp, Temp (if applicable).
*   Physical forms travel with the goods until system recovery.

**5. Backlog Prioritization (Recovery Phase)**
*   **Rule:** Critical Unfilled > Cold-chain Pending > Standard Ambient.
*   **Process:** `Backlog prioritization` (Pattern J) feeds into `Validate authorization` before `Inventory reservation`.
*   **Cancel Threshold:** If backlog > 48h, auto-cancel Wholesale-bulk (Pattern I).

**6. Reconciliation and Audit-Remediation Flow**
*   **Constraint:** `Invoice close` is **blocked** until `Audit remediation` is complete for that case.
*   **Flow:**
    1.  System Returns -> Scan Contingency Forms.
    2.  System Validation -> Match Manual Form to Digital Order.
    3.  **Decision:**
        *   Match OK -> `Quality/release check` -> `Dispatch` (System) -> `Invoice close`.
        *   Match Fail/Missing Data -> `Audit remediation` Queue.
        *   **Hard Stop:** Cases in `Audit remediation` cannot proceed to `Invoice close`. (Eliminates T4 DFG deviation where Invoice Close preceded Remediation).

| **Lagging** | **Rework / Correction Rate** | < 5% during Stabilization | T2 was 12.5%. High rework indicates poor manual data capture (Pattern E). |
| **Fairness** | **Cancellation Rate by Segment** | Hospital < 5%, Pharmacy < 10%, Wholesale > 20% | Ensures burden sharing aligns with criticality (Pattern I). |

# 7. Management trade-off statement

Management must accept **delayed fulfillment for non-critical segments** (Wholesale-bulk) to preserve capacity for Hospital-critical orders; this is a valid resilience trade-off evidenced by the 91% hospital fill rate during T2. However, management must **not accept missing chain-of-custody documentation** for Controlled-substance or Cold-chain products to gain speed. The T2 increase in missing dual-controls (0.2% to 2.9%) and the T4 audit remediation queue (2,300 cases) demonstrate that bypassing these controls creates regulatory liability that outweighs throughput benefits. Evidence that would change this recommendation includes a regulatory waiver issued during declared emergencies that explicitly permits temporary suspension of dual-control signatures; absent such waiver, compliance controls are non-negotiable even during cyber incidents.
