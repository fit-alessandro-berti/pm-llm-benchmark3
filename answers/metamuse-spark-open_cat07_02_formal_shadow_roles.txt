# 2. Core versus peripheral activities

**Concept used:** Core = high relative frequency within role + consistent across `case_complexity` strata + performed without substitution + tied to authority/certification. Peripheral = episodic, low-frequency, tied to `temporary_substitution=True`, high complexity outlier, or referral before hand-off.

**R-AUT (Nora):** Core is strictly A1A2 automation chain (1370 events). Zero peripheral. Complexity evidence proves core is bounded to low (median 0.11, zero critical). Authority is system precheck, not decision. Naive reading counts high A1 volume as dominance; correct reading: boundary role that enables human specialization downstream. Handover analysis shows almost exclusive forward handover to FO resources, never receives.

Authority: entitlement certification allows independent Validate. Always apply rarity-weighted analysis by `escalation_level=critical` and product-line risk, and consult risk owners before deprioritizing infrequent but critical paths.

In sum: use mined organizational roles as *diagnostic lens* on enacted behavior – exposing tiered expertise, shadow backups, and brokerage dependencies – then validate against HR authority matrices, certification registries, and quality outcomes before any staffing, training, or automation change.
