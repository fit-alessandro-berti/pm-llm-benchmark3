assess2 = Transition("Assessment")

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Eligibility rules.**
- **Tier 1 (first wave, 23% of volume):** digital claims — all verification data present at registration, originals not required — *and* not public-sector/high-value (no legal review). Both tasks created and executable at Register.
- **Tier 2 (15%):** digital claims requiring legal review. Same concurrency; legal path unchanged.
- **Tier 3 (62%):** originals-required cases. Identity task at Register; document task created **blocked-ready**, auto-released by the Originals Receipt completion event.
- Exclusions: cases under statutory deadlines with their own sequencing; cases currently inside a supplement loop (handled by loop rules).

**Tasks that may be created/executed concurrently.** Identity  Document Check for Tiers 1–2 only; Identity  originals wait for Tier 3. Never: Document Check before Originals Receipt when required; never Assessment before both verifications complete.

**Statutory prerequisites that remain mandatory (unchanged rules 1–8).** Register first; Originals Receipt precedes Document Check when originals are required; Assessment waits for both checks; Legal Review for public-sector/high-value; supplement loop re-check and re-assessment; decision then Notify.

**Queue and WIP controls.** Per-reviewer open-verification cap of 5; FIFO within priority classes (P1 supplement re-checks, P2 originals-released documents — external wait already incurred, P3 standard); assessment-ready WIP cap with paced, oldest-ready-first release to prevent bursts; daily identity-pool monitoring (4 FTE); pilot capped at 50% of pool load; daily huddles introduced only as an explicitly logged co-intervention (or held constant).

**Resource responsibilities.** Registration team creates both tasks and attaches the digital package; identity pool (4) executes identity; document pool (7) executes document checks and supplement re-checks; assessors (3) assess on both-complete release; legal (2) unchanged; a named pilot steward owns eligibility flags and weekly conformance checks.

**Legal-review and supplement loops.** Legal Review sequence untouched. On Request Supplement: re-issue the document re-check at P1 priority; identity re-check only if identity evidence changed; assessment re-runs after the re-check; loop exits on legal Approve/Reject; supplement instances logged as distinct task instances with a loop counter.

**Logging requirements.** Events stamped at actual start/complete (back-entry forbidden or force-flagged); if the case lock persists, sub-case identifiers `CASEID#ID` / `CASEID#DOC` linked to a parent case, with a sync event at both-complete; log eligibility tier, channel, task-creation time, release time, queue join/leave, actor role, WIP snapshots, loop counter; weekly audit sample.

The pilot separates the two failure types through a **measured dose**. Dose is defined from validated timestamps: share of treated cases with both tasks created at registration, share with genuinely overlapping execution intervals, and share released to Assessment exactly when both verifications complete. If dose is high (60% true overlap) yet waiting-before-assessment does not fall, the **mechanism failed to transport** — the saving was absorbed by assessment/legal queues or blocked by the originals gate — and redesign (pacing, segmentation), not retraining, is indicated. If dose is low, **implementation failed** — case lock, workarounds, or training — and the causal question stays open until fidelity is fixed. Back-entry is force-flagged, preventing Site A's overlap-artifact mistake from being replicated. Untouched segments (Notify, ineligible strata) serve as negative controls for trend and mix drift. The switchback overlay quantifies interference: if case-level effects shrink under pooled congestion, expansion requires capacity or pacing changes first. Stratum estimates feed the transport comparison against Site A's structural anchor, with digital, originals, and legal shares as weighting variables.

"""

Requires pm4py >= 2.7.10 (POWL support).
The model encodes CONTROL FLOW only. See the block at the bottom for what it

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import Transition, SilentTransition, Operator, StrictPartialOrder

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1) Site A process trees (before / after)
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
pt_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)
pt_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)
print("Site A before:", pt_a_before)
print("Site A after :", pt_a_after)

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2) convert both trees to accepting Petri nets
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
net_ab, im_ab, fm_ab = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(pt_a_before)
net_aa, im_aa, fm_aa = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(pt_a_after)

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3) Site B candidate as a POWL model
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
reg     = Transition("Register")
iden    = Transition("Identity Check")
orig    = Transition("Originals Receipt")
doc     = Transition("Document Check")
assess  = Transition("Assessment")
legal   = Transition("Legal Review")
supp    = Transition("Request Supplement")
appr    = Transition("Approve")
rej     = Transition("Reject")
notify  = Transition("Notify")

# Document channel: originals path XOR digital skip.
# NOTE: in reality this choice is a DATA GUARD (originals legally required
# for 62% of cases, rule 4). Control flow can only represent it as a free
# (unguarded) choice; conformance checking against the log must enforce it.
channel_gate = Operator(Operator.XOR, [orig, skip])

# Supplement loop: do = Legal Review; redo = Request Supplement ->
# (re-)Document Check -> (re-)Assessment; after redo, control returns to do.
# Separate Transition instances model the re-work executions as new tasks.
doc2     = Transition("Document Check")
assess2  = Transition("Assessment")
redo = StrictPartialOrder([supp, doc2, assess2])
redo.add_edge(supp, doc2)
redo.add_edge(doc2, assess2)
redo.add_edge(supp, assess2)
legal_loop = Operator(Operator.LOOP, [legal, redo])

# Legal review required only for 40% of cases: optional branch (again a
# data guard in reality, a free choice in the model).
legal_gate = Operator(Operator.XOR, [legal_loop, SilentTransition()])

decision = Operator(Operator.XOR, [appr, rej])

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4) strict partial order with explicit edges:
#    Assessment waits for BOTH identity and document verification
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
b_model = StrictPartialOrder(
    [reg, iden, channel_gate, doc, assess, legal_gate, decision, notify]
)
b_model.add_edge(reg, iden)           # identity may start after Register
b_model.add_edge(reg, channel_gate)   # document channel opens after Register
b_model.add_edge(channel_gate, doc)   # Document Check waits for its channel (originals if required)
b_model.add_edge(iden, assess)        # Assessment waits for Identity Check
b_model.add_edge(doc, assess)         # Assessment waits for Document Check
b_model.add_edge(assess, legal_gate)  # legal review (if required) after assessment
b_model.add_edge(legal_gate, decision)
b_model.add_edge(decision, notify)

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 5) POWL -> accepting Petri net
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(b_model)

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 6) workflow-net / soundness checks
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
for name, net, im, fm in [("A before", net_ab, im_ab, fm_ab),
                          ("A after", net_aa, im_aa, fm_aa),
                          ("B candidate", net_b, im_b, fm_b)]:
    try:
        print(f"{name}: workflow net = {pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)}")
    except Exception as e:
        print(f"{name}: workflow-net check unavailable ({e})")
    try:
        print(f"{name}: soundness = {pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)}")
    except Exception as e:
        print(f"{name}: soundness check unavailable ({e})")

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 7) optional playout and sample traces
# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
try:
    log_b = pm4py.play_out(b_model)          # playout from the POWL model
except Exception:
    try:
        log_b = pm4py.play_out(net_b, im_b, fm_b)  # or from the Petri net
    except Exception as e:
        log_b = None
        print("playout unavailable:", e)
if log_b is not None:
    for i, trace in enumerate(log_b[:5]):
        print(f"B sample trace {i + 1}:",
              " -> ".join(e["concept:name"] for e in trace))

# ----------------------------------------------------------------------
# 8) WHAT THIS MODEL CANNOT ENCODE
#  - Data-dependent legal requirements: 'Originals Receipt' is mandatory for
#    62% of cases (rule 4) and 'Legal Review' for 40% (rule 6). POWL/Petri
#    nets without data places model these as free choices; the guards must
#    be enforced by the case system and verified via conformance checking
#    against the real event log (expected guard rates: 62%, 40%, 26.5%
#    supplement rate among legal reviews).
#  - Resource capacity: the 4/7/3/2 specialized pools, per-reviewer WIP
#    caps, and queue priority classes do not exist in the model. Burst and
#    starvation behavior (the bottleneck-shift risk) requires queueing
#    simulation or the pilot itself.
#  - Case locks: the legacy one-active-owner constraint is an IT attribute,
#    not a flow constraint; the model presumes concurrent tasks are ownable.
#  - Durations: no stochastic timing is attached, so cycle-time and p90
#    effects of the partial-order change cannot be derived from the model
#    alone; pair it with interval measurements from the event log.

## 9. Recommendation

**`conditional go`**

Transfer the mechanism, not the package. The only defensible transfer is the partial-order edit applied where its enabling condition holds: document data available at registration. For the 38% digital claims, create identity and document tasks together at Register and release Assessment on the AND-join of both. For the 62% originals-required cases, keep rule 4 absolute: create the document task in a blocked-ready state, released by the Originals Receipt event, while identity starts at registration in parallel with the originals wait. That is an event-based scheduling gain, not parallelization of the two checks — and the DFG suggests partial identity/originals concurrency already exists there.

Do **not** copy: uniform executable document tasks for every case (violates rule 4 and invites preview-based checks and rework); Site A's 9 h as a planning number (it is a confounded composite of structure, API latency, added reviewers, huddles, and a mix shift; the structural share is plausibly 3 to 5 h at 90% digital, rescaling to roughly 1 to 2 h at Site B's mix); overlap percentage as a success KPI without validated timestamps (A's 71% is partly a recording artifact); and an all-teams rollout within two weeks.

First subgroup: digital, non-legal cases (~23% of volume) — the closest structural analog, since roughly 90% of digital cases still run identitydocument sequentially today. Then digital-with-legal; originals-required cases last, under event-based release.

Expected benefits: stratum median 1.5 to 2.5 h; aggregate 1 to 2 h. Risks: identity-queue saturation (4 FTE), assessment/legal burst congestion worsening p90 even as the median improves, compliance incidents from case-lock workarounds, and corrupted measurement from back-entered timestamps.

Expansion triggers: validated overlap 60%, waiting-before-assessment down 2 h, p90 stable, rework and supplement rates flat for four consecutive weeks. Modification triggers (paced release, priority rules, capacity): p90 up >10% while median improves, or identity queue median wait up >50%. Abandonment triggers: any compliance/audit violation from the workaround, or no waiting-time reduction at high measured dose — which would mean the mechanism, not the implementation, failed to transport.

## 10. Assumption register

1. At least ~2 h of Site A's 9 h median reduction is attributable to the partial-order change itself — needed to justify any expected benefit at Site B — test via the stage-level decomposition and the pilot dose-response slope — if false, the expected Site B effect is 0 and the program should stop after Tier 1.
2. Identity and document checks at Site B are informationally independent, so parallel start loses no cross-check inference — needed so parallelism does not trade quality for speed — test via audit samples, error rates, and appeal rates in treated vs ineligible strata — if false, add a mandatory cross-consistency step before Assessment and shrink the net gain estimate.
3. The originals-receipt prerequisite is an immovable statutory gate with no certified-digital substitution — needed to define eligibility and the 62% partial-dose share — test via legal review of the statutory basis and any waiver precedent — if false, the fully eligible share rises toward 100% and the expected benefit roughly doubles.
4. The case lock can be safely bypassed via sub-case splitting without breaking audit trails — needed for implementable true overlap and valid dose measurement — test in a sandbox with audit sign-off before wave 1 — if false, run only a small manually logged pilot and treat full rollout as no-go until the IT change lands.
5. The assessment pool retains 10–15% slack to absorb synchronized completions — needed to avoid converting median gains into tail losses — test via utilization and p90 monitoring with the pre-set stop rules — if false, add paced release or assessor capacity before expanding beyond Tier 1.
6. Intake mix (38% digital, 40% legal-review, 48% high-complexity) remains stable during the pilot — needed for unbiased stratum-level estimates — test via a weekly mix dashboard with drift alarms — if false, re-weight estimates and defer expansion decisions until mix stabilizes.
7. Interference between treated and control cases through shared pools is second-order — needed for case-level randomization to yield interpretable effects — test via the switchback overlay comparing effect estimates under both designs — if false, switch to a phased cluster design and treat case-level estimates as diluted downward.
8. Site A's co-interventions (API upgrade, two added reviewers, daily huddles, marketing mix shift) jointly explain at least ~40% of A's improvement — needed to bound the transferable structural component at 3 to 5 h — test by re-analyzing Site A's stage-level data if accessible, or by comparing pilot effects to the projected structural share — if false, the structural effect is larger than projected (upside), but the risk of over-promising and tail congestion rises accordingly.
