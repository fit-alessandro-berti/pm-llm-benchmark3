## 1. Stipulated Facts, Disputed Propositions, and Burden of Proof

**Stipulated Facts (evidence establishes):**

* M0 semantics are formal: `Register -> (Sanctions || Quality) -> XOR( Manual->Activate, Reject ) -> Notify -> Close`. No loop, no Auto_Approve, no provisional Activate-before-checks, no reactivation or pre-screen variant. Resource SoD not encoded.
* CS-9 is the only controlled, formally approved normative source from 2025-01-01 onward. It requires Sanctions+Quality before activation and Manual before Activate, plus SoD requester != approver.
* PD-3 was a steering-committee decision 2026-06-01 to 2026-07-31, never incorporated into CS-9/M0. Content is documented: LOW-risk DE approved-group may substitute Auto_Approve; Sanctions + automated master-data validation must precede activation; Quality may be up to +2 business days after provisional activation. The Compliance observer's note "monitored exception" is documented; her later denial of approval to weaken CS-9 is documented.
* FD-7 was a written directive 2026-07-10 00:00 to 2026-07-12 23:59 for critical relief suppliers: Sanctions before activation is mandatory; Quality and Manual may follow activation; spend cap EUR 20k until retrospective approval; daily monitoring + Quality within 2 business days.
* From 2026-07-01 Notify moved to CRM, ~70% mirrored, link via `notification_id` mutable for older cases. Notification missingness 2% -> 29%.
* 312 Auto_Approve cases Jun-Jul: 289 appear eligible, 14 clearly ineligible, 9 missing eligibility attributes. 6 Auto_Approve cases first week of Aug after expiry. 47 cases contain Request_More_Info, none activated before requested evidence arrived. Aggregate evidence is stipulated.
* Individual traces and timestamps as in table are stipulated as *observed order* in the log. Underlying real-world occurrence vs logging artifact is not stipulated except where source transaction IDs or CRM evidence exists.

**Disputed Propositions (depends on authority, scope, identity, logging assumptions):**

* **Authority hierarchy:** Can PD-3 or FD-7 create lawful exceptions to CS-9/M0 without formal CS-9 amendment? What weight does "monitored exception" have when Compliance explicitly did not approve weakening? Does expiry extinguish justification for continuation when factual emergency persists?
* **Scope of M0/CS-9:** Do rejected pre-screen leads (SUP-709), reactivations of closed suppliers (SUP-710), and duplicate events from migration (SUP-711) fall within M0's case notion? Document is ambiguous.
* **Identity and label equivalence:** Does alias-duplicated human in SUP-712 violate resource rule? Does QA_Clearance = Quality_Review in SUP-713 at case time vs retroactive mapping? Does spouses/dotted-line in SUP-715 violate SoD/conflict rule when no explicit prohibition but disclosure required?
* **Trace vs event vs behavior:** Is Notify absent in SUP-706 a business omission or 30% CRM non-mirroring? Is duplicated Register in SUP-711 a second execution or same event recorded twice?
* **Substantive misconduct vs conformance deviation:** Does desirable information-request behavior (SUP-707) or no-transaction-before-clearance (SUP-708) mitigate a formal control-flow violation? Does pilot eligibility snapshot vs current attributes determine eligibility?

**Burden of Proof:**

* **To declare NONCOMPLIANT:** Requires (a) applicable authoritative norm at case timestamp, (b) clear scope inclusion, (c) trace evidence that is more likely than not a real execution (not a logging artifact), and (d) deviation not covered by a formally authorized overlay in force at that time. We apply preponderance for low-risk deviations; clear-and-convincing for severe (sanctions bypass, high spend) given consequences. Doubt about logging/identity is resolved as DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE, not as compliance.
* **To accept JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION:** Requires (a) written overlay in effect at case time, (b) eligibility conditions demonstrably met with contemporaneous snapshot attributes, (c) mandatory safeguards of the overlay met (Sanctions before activation always; for FD-7 spend cap + monitoring + timely Quality/approval; for PD-3 Sanctions before activation + timely Quality), and (d) authority traceable (steering committee / emergency issuer) with version/rule IDs. Informal belief, imminent approval, factual emergency without extension, or post-hoc equivalence mapping do not meet burden.
* **Presumptions:** CS-9/M0 control flow is presumed valid unless superseded by controlled amendment. Pilots/emergencies are temporary overlays, not amendments, and must be explicitly time- and scope-bounded. Missing data does not presume compliance nor noncompliance.

## 2. Prosecution Brief
*Strongest case that operation is materially noncompliant and M0/CS-9 must be enforced.*

Formal authority matters. CS-9 is the only controlled standard. M0 faithfully encodes CS-9's control-flow core: both checks before activation and manual approval before activation. PD-3 is meeting minutes + email, explicitly *not* incorporated into CS-9/M0, with contested Compliance sign-off. Defense cannot promote a steering-committee pilot to the level of a standard. The observer's "monitored exception" followed by "had not approved weakening CS-9" shows no consensus to derogate. Allowing PD-3 to create 312 Auto_Approve cases, of which 14 are clearly ineligible and 9 lack evidence, and 6 more after expiry, is exactly normalization of deviance. If exceptions are needed, they must be formally versioned; otherwise auditability collapses.

Expiry is decisive. FD-7's text states automatic expiry. SUP-704 (2026-07-11) meets every FD-7 safeguard and would be a textbook justified provisional activation. SUP-705 (2026-07-13) is materially identical but one day after expiry with no written extension. "Factual emergency still existed" is an operations assertion without authority. Permitting post-expiry continuation invites indefinite emergency powers. The same logic condemns SUP-714 (2026-08-02) — two days after PD-3 expired — and the six post-pilot August cases. Operations "believed permanent approval was imminent" is precisely the risk: behavior changes before policy does.

High-risk severe cases prove material risk is not theoretical. SUP-703: HIGH-risk new foreign supplier using Auto_Approve despite ineligibility, EUR 180k purchase — 9x FD-7 cap, no pilot safeguards intended for this profile. SUP-708: HIGH-risk supplier activated 40 minutes *before* Sanctions_Check, with no emergency or pilot cover. That "no transactions before clearance" is luck, not control; sanctions is a prevent-before-activation control, not a detective control. Audit correctly flags both as severe. Aggregate 14 ineligible Auto_Approves shows systematic eligibility failure, not a single operator error.

M0's blindness hides risk. M0 has no resource perspective, but CS-9 does: requester != approver. SUP-712 fits M0 perfectly yet violates SoD via aliases — a classic hidden noncompliance that conformance checking on control-flow alone would miss. SUP-715 similarly: reviewer/approver are spouses in same reporting line with no disclosure per conflict policy, exploiting that no explicit prohibition exists. Prosecution notes M0 under-reports noncompliance, not over-reports; fixing M0 must *add* constraints, not relax them.

Desirable outcomes do not amend policy retroactively. SUP-707's Request_More_Info loops are process-improvement suggestions, not compliance defenses. That all 47 RMI cases waited for evidence is good, but absence of a loop in M0 does not excuse other violations; it warrants a change request, not unilateral execution. SUP-713's QA_Clearance renaming was mapped *one week after* the case; audit documents show equivalent *content* but the controlled vocabulary at case time was Quality_Review. Retroactive mapping cannot make a past trace compliant at that timestamp; it proves change management failure.

Data incompleteness does not nullify obligations. SUP-706's missing Notify is indeterminate in CRM due to mutable text links entered two days later and only 70% mirroring. The obligation is to produce stable evidence; mutable links with 29% missingness are non-evidence. Operations cannot benefit from its own logging failure. SUP-711's duplicated Register shows migration without idempotent ingestion; without transaction-ID deduplication at analysis time, metrics are inflated, but that is a data-governance failure that prosecution counts as operational noncompliance in its broad sense.

System-level: Even if some deviations are explained, the pattern is enforcement decay — pilot flows normalized (SUP-714), emergency flows continued after expiry (SUP-705), sanctions ordering violated (SUP-708), ineligible auto-approvals with high spend (SUP-703), SoD bypassed via aliases (SUP-712). Conformance rate is low because *behavior* drifted, not only because *model* is old.

## 3. Defense Brief
*Strongest case that M0 is obsolete, incomplete, incorrectly scoped, or contradicted by better evidence.*

M0 was never a faithful representation of intended or practiced normative reality.

**Concurrency and ordering:** M0 forces strict `checks -> decision` and `Manual -> Activate` inside the approved branch. FD-7 and PD-3 both explicitly authorize provisional Activate before Quality and before Manual, with Sanctions remaining before Activate. These overlays are not rogue; FD-7 is a written emergency directive with scope, cap, monitoring, and time window; PD-3 is a steering-committee pilot with documented eligibility and purpose (low-risk DE approved-group, automated master-data validation). To judge SUP-702 against M0 is to judge a 2026 pilot with a 2025 model that the organization itself chose to suspend. SUP-702 on 2026-06-15: LOW-risk DE approved-group, Sanctions before Activate, Auto_Approve instead of Manual, Quality next business day (within 2 days), CRM stable `notification_id` at +2h, pilot eligibility fields complete. Substantively it met every pilot condition; failing it for lacking Manual is formalism over intent. SUP-704 similarly meets every FD-7 safeguard (EUR 18k < 20k, daily monitoring, Quality + approval next business day). M0's rigidity would flag as violation what the emergency directive intentionally permitted to prevent shutdown.

**Missing loop:** M0 has no Request_More_Info. Operations commonly uses 1+ cycles before decision; Compliance agrees it is desirable. 47 cases all waited for evidence; SUP-707 (`RMI,RMI -> Quality -> Manual -> Activate`) is exactly the behavior stakeholders want, yet M0 marks it deviant. A model that penalizes the agreed best practice is obsolete by definition. Trace deviation  business misconduct; here deviation is a model defect.

**Data collection contradicts inference of violation:** From 2026-07-01 Notify is in CRM, 70% mirrored. SUP-702's CRM shows notification at +2h with stable ID — better evidence than the incomplete onboarding log. Prosecution's claim that missing Notify = noncompliance fails when the source of truth moved. SUP-706 (mutable text link entered two days later) is precisely why it must be DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE, not NONCOMPLIANT; no reliable evidence exists to convict, and missingness rose from 2% to 29% at the migration cutover — a system cause, not an operator omission. SUP-711 duplicated Register with *same source transaction ID* is not a second registration; it is a migration bug. Counting it as a violation is counting a logging defect as a business violation.

**Scope and identity:** M0's case notion is undefined. SUP-709 (Register -> Sanctions -> Reject, no Quality) sits on the dispute: process owner says intake cases include rejected leads; model owner says M0 starts after pre-screen acceptance. CS-9 itself says rejected leads use an abbreviated pre-screen but does not state they are M0-governed. Punishing SUP-709 assumes a scope M0 never declared. SUP-710 (Sanctions, Manual, Activate, Notify, Close — no Register, no Quality) is a reactivation after 6 months with prior Quality still valid; CS-9 has *no* reactivation procedure. The log treats it as a new case lacking Register/Quality, but organizationally it is a different object (reopen) with different lifecycle rules. Assessing it against a new-supplier model is category error. SUP-713 (QA_Clearance vs Quality_Review) is a controlled rename with identical content per audit docs one week later; to fail the case for label mismatch is to confuse syntax with semantics.

**Distinguishing control-flow from data/resource rules:** SUP-712 fits M0 but fails SoD via aliases — prosecution is correct this is hidden, but that proves M0 is *incomplete*, not that operations is conformant. Defense accepts the resource violation is real, yet argues it demonstrates M0's insufficiency, not its correctness. Conversely SUP-715 (spouses) violates no explicit CS-9 rule; conflict policy requires *disclosure*, not prohibition, and none was recorded — but M0 cannot capture this data-aware rule at all. Conformance analysis must separate trace conformance from data policy conformance.

**Normalization is not automatically misconduct:** SUP-714 (2026-08-02, LOW-risk eligible, 2 days after expiry) and the six early-August Auto_Approves continue a flow that had been monitored, eligible, and safe (289/312 eligible). The gap is a governance lag between operational success and formal amendment — a familiar process-mining pattern — not evidence of high-risk erosion. No high-risk or high-spend is alleged for SUP-714.

In sum, the high deviation rate cited by Internal Audit is largely artifact: model missing loops, overlays not in model, scope undefined, CRM migration incompleteness, migration duplicates, and label aliases. Judging 2026 operations with a 2025 control-flow-only M0 overstates noncompliance.

## 5. Final Judgment

Defense prevails on substance; prosecution's authority objection noted but steering-committee pilot with monitoring is deemed sufficient temporary overlay for this case. *Reject defense's claim that pilot equals permanent amendment; reject prosecution's claim pilot has no authority.*
* **SUP-703: NONCOMPLIANT** — High confidence, **critical severity**. HIGH-risk foreign supplier, ineligible pilot selection, EUR 180k spend — no overlay covers. Both sides agree misconduct.

### 2. Severity & confidence summary
Severe/critical: SUP-703 (critical), SUP-708 (high). Medium: SUP-705, SUP-712, SUP-714, SUP-715, SUP-710 (scope gap). Low: SUP-702, SUP-704, SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-711, SUP-713, SUP-701. Confidences as above; lowest for SUP-706/709/710/715 due to missing scope or evidence.

### 3. System-level judgment

**Operational compliance: Partially noncompliant with material pockets, not systemically corrupt.** The operation correctly handled standard flows (701), valid emergency activations (704), and pilot-eligible low-risk auto-approvals within window (702). However, material noncompliance exists: (a) sanctions-ordering breach (708), (b) high-risk pilot misuse with large spend (703), (c) unauthorized continuations post-expiry (705, 714 + six early-Aug cases), and (d) SoD/conflict bypasses hidden from control-flow view (712, 715). 14/312 ineligible auto-approvals (4.5%) plus post-expiry drift is not noise; it is systematic eligibility enforcement and governance failure.

**Model validity: Invalid as single normative POWL.** M0 is accurate for CS-9 new-supplier standard route only. It is obsolete/incomplete because it lacks (i) RMI loop (47 cases, stakeholder consensus desirable), (ii) pilot and emergency overlays, (iii) scope definitions for reject pre-screen and reactivation, (iv) label aliasing, and (v) data-aware and resource perspectives. Deviation rate is inflated by these defects and by CRM migration incompleteness (29% missing) and migration duplicates. Rejecting operations wholesale based on M0 alone would be unsound; rejecting model criticism wholesale would hide real risk.

**Where each side is rejected:** Prosecution's claim that every trace deviation equals business noncompliance is rejected for SUP-706/707/709/710/711/713 where defects are model/scope/data. Defense's claim that pilot/emergency/factual continuity informally amends policy is rejected for SUP-703/705/708/714 where authority, eligibility, expiry, or mandatory ordering were violated, and that desirable outcomes (708's no transactions, 714's eligibility) retroactively justify violations.

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

**Principle: Layered controls — POWL for stable ordering, DECLARE/log-skeleton for flexible precedence windows, pandas/data checks for data/identity/amount/time, organizational controls for authority/scope. POWL must not embed volatile policy.**

**A. Revised Control-Flow Model (POWL layer):**
Belongs in POWL. Keep a single maintained POWL that encodes *structural* options approved by Governance Board, not data conditions:

* Start: Register_Supplier (for *new* supplier case; see scope rules)
* Optional repeating Request_More_Info: LOOP with 0..n RMI after Register and before decision-quality checks. This legitimizes SUP-707's desirable loop without masking other deviations.
* Concurrent checks baseline retained for standard route.
* XOR decision with four children: (1) Standard manual branch, (2) Auto-approval branch, (3) Emergency/provisional branch, (4) Reject branch — plus silent close for scope variants handled outside POWL via scope rules. This makes PD-3 and FD-7 visible as modeled paths rather than invisible deviations.
* Always: Notify_Requester -> Close_Case with Notify required before Close (SEQ). Data incompleteness addressed separately.

POWL does NOT decide eligibility for branches; it only permits structure. Branch choice is gated by overlay rules (next layer).

**B. Data-Aware Eligibility / Amount / Time Rules (DECLARE / data-check layer):**
*Not in POWL* — implement as DECLARE constraints and pandas validation:

* DECLARE `Precedence(Sanctions_Check, Activate_Supplier)` — absolute, no violation ever (FD-7 and PD-3 both require). Time-window variant: `Chain` not needed but metric: `Sanctions before Activate`.
* DECLARE `Precedence(Activate_Supplier, Quality_Review)` relaxed with window: For standard branch Quality before Activate; for auto/emergency branch Quality within +2 business days after Activate. Encode as log-skeleton / DECLARE `Response(Activate, Quality) with max interval`.
* Amount rule: pandas check `if branch in (auto, emergency) then provisional spend <=20k until retrospective approval event exists`. FD-7 cap and PD-3 spend monitoring become data checks, not POWL.
* Risk/geo/group rule: `Auto_Approve allowed iff risk=LOW AND geo=DE AND corporate_group_status=approved AND pilot_version active at Activate timestamp AND master_data_validation passed before Activate`. All snapshot at decision time.
* Time validity: `pilot_version effective 2026-06-01—2026-07-31`, `emergency_version 2026-07-10—2026-07-12 per authority_id`. Data check fails if outside window without active extension record.
* Notification: `Response(Decision/Activate, Notify)` with deadline? At minimum Existence constraint before Close.

**C. Resource and Conflict-of-Interest Rules (resource/data layer):**
*Not in pure POWL* — implement as pandas + organizational control:

* Hard SoD: `actor(requester) != actor(manual_approver)` resolved through canonical `person_id`, not alias string. Maintain alias table (`user_alias -> person_id`, validity dates). Check via data join.
* Four-eyes expanded: `quality_reviewer != manual_approver` if policy requires? At minimum conflict disclosure: if reviewer and approver share reporting line or family relationship (HR disclosure registry) then require `conflict_disclosure_id` and independent second approval. Absence = violation (SUP-715).
* Delegation: Allow delegation only with `delegation_id` linked to approval event and delegator != requester.

**D. Policy-Overlay Governance (organizational control layer):**
Pilots and emergencies are versioned overlays, not inline model edits:

* Registry table `policy_overlay (rule_version_id, type [STANDARD/PILOT/EMERGENCY], authority_id, approved_by, effective_from/to, scope_regions, amount_cap, required_safeguards, status)`.
* Every case event carries `applied_rule_version_id` and `eligibility_snapshot` (risk, geo, group at decision time, immutable). Decision UI forces selection of overlay and eligibility check before allowing Auto_Approve or provisional Activate-before-Quality.
* Extensions require new version with explicit approver signature; factual emergency does not auto-extend (governs SUP-705/714). Steering-committee minutes without Compliance sign-off cannot activate a STANDARD-weakening overlay.
* Decommission: auto-expire flows disabled in UI; six post-pilot August cases would be blocked by system, not policy paper.
* Monitoring: daily transaction monitoring and retrospective approval are logged as events (`Transaction_Monitor`, `Retrospective_Approval`) linked to case.

**E. Explicit Scope Rules (scoping layer):**
*Define case notions outside POWL:*

* `Rejected leads`: If `Reject_Supplier` occurs due to sanctions pre-screen failure before Quality assignment, case type = `INTAKE_LEAD`, not `ONBOARDING`. M0/onboarding model does not apply; abbreviated pre-screen workflow applies (log-skeleton: no Quality required). Ambiguity in CS-9 resolved by making M0 applicability predicate: `case_type == ONBOARDING -> both checks required`. SUP-709 becomes COMPLIANT intake lead.
* `Reactivation`: If `supplier_id` previously closed <12mo and `quality_cert_valid_until >= now` then case type = `REACTIVATION`, requires `Register_Reactivation` (not Register_Supplier) and may reuse Quality; else treat as new. Event log must distinguish `Register_Supplier` vs `Register_Reactivation`. SUP-710 becomes scope defect fixed.
* `Withdrawal`: separate closure reason.
* `Duplicate suppression`: ingestion deduplicate on `source_transaction_id`; idempotent event creation prevents SUP-711 double count.

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

*Limitations stated:* POWL cannot enforce amount caps, risk/geo/group eligibility, SoD via aliases, time-windowed Quality (+2 business days), or authority/expiry. It also cannot make Quality optionally before *or* after Activate with a time constraint in one partial order — that requires DECLARE. The POWL below therefore encodes the *structural* permissiveness (all approved variants as XOR branches, plus loop for RMI) while delegating eligibility, timing within window, and resource rules to the overlay and data checks. The RMI mechanism is approximated as a LOOP after Register allowing 0..n `Request_More_Info`; this over-approximates slightly by allowing interleaving only in that position, which is the observed desirable position (before decision checks). A more flexible interleaving would require DECLARE and would hide deviations if put entirely in POWL, so we choose explicit limited loop.

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# --- Core activities (single canonical label; alias handled in data layer) ---
register = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")
rmi = Transition(label="Request_More_Info")
sanctions = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
quality = Transition(label="Quality_Review")  # alias QA_Clearance via mapping table
master_data = Transition(label="Automated_Master_Data_Validation") # pilot safeguard, optional
manual = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
auto = Transition(label="Auto_Approve")
activate = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
reject = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
notify = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")
close = Transition(label="Close_Case")

# --- 1. Repeatable Request_More_Info: 0..n cycles after Register, before checks ---
# LOOP semantics: execute Silent, optionally (rmi + Silent) repeated => 0..n rmi
loop_rmi = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[SilentTransition(), rmi])

# --- 2. Standard branch: (Sanctions || Quality) -> Manual -> Activate ---
checks_std = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, quality])
# concurrent, no edge

std_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[checks_std, manual, activate])
std_branch.order.add_edge(checks_std, manual)
std_branch.order.add_edge(manual, activate)

# --- 3. Low-risk Auto branch (PD-3 style): Sanctions (+ master data) & Auto before Activate, Quality after ---
# Sanctions and Auto both before Activate, Quality after Activate (within 2BD enforced by DECLARE, not POWL)
# We include master_data as optional concurrent check before Activate in auto path
auto_checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, master_data, auto])
# sanctions and master_data concurrent before auto? Simplified: all before activate

auto_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[auto_checks, activate, quality])
auto_branch.order.add_edge(auto_checks, activate)
auto_branch.order.add_edge(activate, quality)

# --- 4. Emergency / provisional branch (FD-7 style): Sanctions before Activate, then Quality || Manual concurrent after ---
emergency_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, activate, quality, manual])
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(sanctions, activate)
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(activate, quality)
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(activate, manual)
# quality and manual concurrent after activation; retrospective approval required (data check)

# --- 5. Decision: choose one route ---
decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[std_branch, auto_branch, emergency_branch, reject])

# --- 6. Top level: Register -> RMI-loop -> Decision -> Notify -> Close ---
M_rev = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, loop_rmi, decision, notify, close])
M_rev.order.add_edge(register, loop_rmi)
M_rev.order.add_edge(loop_rmi, decision)
M_rev.order.add_edge(decision, notify)
M_rev.order.add_edge(notify, close)

# M_rev is the revised normative POWL. Branch eligibility, time windows, amount caps, and SoD are NOT in POWL:
# see overlay table and data-collection proposal below.
```

**Normalized Policy-Overlay Table (versioned, immutable):**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CS9-v3 | STANDARD | Standard onboarding: Sanctions||Quality -> Manual->Activate | 2025-01-01 | NULL (open) | risk, geo, group_status, person_id alias | none | mandatory | before Activate | Compliance Board |
| PD3-v1 | PILOT | Low-risk DE approved-group Auto instead of Manual; master-data before Activate; Quality up to +2BD after | 2026-06-01 | 2026-07-31 | STEER-2026-06 | risk=LOW, geo=DE, group=approved, master_data=pass at decision | none (but monitor) | mandatory | +2 business days after Activate | Steering Committee + Compliance observer (contested) |
| FD7-v1 | EMERGENCY | Critical relief provisional Activate before Quality/Manual | 2026-07-10 00:00 | 2026-07-12 23:59 | EOC-FLOOD-2026-07 | supplier tagged critical_relief, scope=relief | EUR 20k until retrospective_approval | mandatory | +2 business days after Activate | Emergency Ops Center |

Each event carries `applied_rule_version_id` (FK to above), `eligibility_snapshot JSON` (frozen at decision), `actor_person_id` (canonical, not alias string), `delegation_id`, `emergency_tag`.

**Event / Data-Collection Proposal:**

* **Event log schema (immutable, append-only):** `case_id, event_id, activity (canonical), timestamp (effective_time, record_time), source_transaction_id (for idempotency), actor_person_id, actor_alias_raw, applied_rule_version_id, eligibility_snapshot_hash, notification_id (stable UUID), amount_EUR, supplier_id, case_type (ONBOARDING/INTAKE_LEAD/REACTIVATION/WITHDRAWAL), quality_cert_valid_until`
* **Stable notification IDs:** CRM generates UUID at send, onboarding log stores FK `notification_id` immutable; no mutable text. Nightly reconciliation job validates 100% mirroring; missing = data-quality alert, not silent drop. Retroactively fix SUP-706 link by backfill from CRM UUIDs where available; mark remainder indeterminate.
* **Aliases & SoD:** Central `alias_registry (alias -> person_id, valid_from/to)` + `person_registry` + `conflict_registry (person_id, related_person_id, relation, disclosure_id)`. Pandas check `requester_person_id != approver_person_id`; family/line conflict requires disclosure.
* **Label alias:** `activity_alias (QA_Clearance -> Quality_Review, version, effective_from)` with audit equivalence docs; analysis normalizes before conformance.
* **Overlay enforcement:** UI disables Auto_Approve and provisional Activate outside active overlay windows; eligibility snapshot stored before activation; amount check blocks activation if cap exceeded without retrospective approval.
* **Timestamps:** Both `effective_time` (business time) and `record_time` (logging time) stored to detect Sanctions logging lag (SUP-708) and to enforce +2BD windows in business days (calendar table).
* **Deduplication:** Ingestion key `source_transaction_id`; SUP-711 second Register ignored at load.
* **Scope:** Case creation sets `case_type`; intake rejects and reactivations use distinct POWL entry points but share reporting; prevents misclassifying SUP-709/710.

This architecture restores traceability: control-flow conformance is judged against M_rev, but material risk is judged against the combined POWL + DECLARE + data + overlay layers, with evidence anchored in immutable IDs and versioned policy.
