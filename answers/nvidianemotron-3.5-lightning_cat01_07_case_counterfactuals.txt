Assessment-queue bottlenecks migrate downward: K-411’s concurrency pushes earlier Assessment starts, which beneficial for cycle time but risks saturating the assessment pool (A1/A2) during peak hours; with 100 cases, even a 20% shift to earlier starts could extend average assessment wait from the Tue 08:00 snapshot (A1:6h, A2:4h) to >10h for cases arriving after the shift, creating a backlog that propagates to Decision and Notify. Weekend and external-response effects (K-337) become amplified when many cases cluster around supplier delays; correlated slow responses could synchronize reloop entries, creating queue spikes that the single-case analysis masks. Timestamp behavior versus true concurrency (K-411) is the most fragile nonlinearity: if 100 pilots adopt concurrent I/D without robust logging, audit trails degrade, process violations go undetected, and resource scheduling algorithms misallocate capacity, potentially increasing overall makespan despite per-case speed gains. Overall, local benefits do not scale linearly; interference, queue displacement, and bottleneck migration must be modeled explicitly before system-wide adoption.

## 7. Final synthesis (268 words)

The K-411 concurrency intervention appears most robust because it operates entirely within alreadypermitted normative rules (no originals  concurrent Identity and Document Check) and introduces only a protocol enhancement—realtime timestamp logging—to capture true parallelism. Its benefits are measurable without altering decision policy or four-eyes constraints, and the intervention is fully reversible by reverting to sequential task assignment. Spillovers are manageable: earlier Assessment starts may mildly load the assessment queue, but this is detectable and correctable via queue monitoring. In contrast, the K-337 reinfoloop prohibition is the most dangerous despite its large per-cycle time savings, because it explicitly changes the allowed decision policy, risks unresolved discrepancies, and weakens compliance defensibility; at scale, even a 20% appeal/rework rate would generate downstream cases that erase the time gain. The K-104 C1slot reservation at Triage is least transportable systemwide: C1 is a single scarce resource, and early reservation displaces capacity from other urgent highrisk cases, making the intervention contextspecific and potentially harmful when applied broadly. The most valuable additional data are realtime queue timestamps and resource utilization trends per pool (identity, document, assessment, compliance) alongside external supplier response timing; these enable detection of bottleneck migration and concurrency spillovers before any policy rollout, ensuring that local counterfactuals are grounded in systemwide observability rather than isolated case assumptions.

## 8. Assumption audit

1. **Structural:** The partialorder constraints (Register  Triage  …) correctly sequence all mandatory activities across the four case types without hidden cycles.  
2. **Temporal:** Queue forecast values in system snapshots are soft bounds (±20%) and may shift with arrival patterns, but do not override prerequisite constraints.  
3. **Resource:** All resources within a pool are fully interchangeable for the service intervals described, except where senior/faster designations (A1, D1) are explicitly noted; interchangeability holds for capacity pooling.  
4. **Behavioral:** Staff prioritize cases based on queue position and flag status (highrisk/value) when scheduling, but may deviate when resource idle time exists, subject to supervisory override.  
5. **Quality:** The foureyes rule (rule9) is inviolable; no decision may be signed by the same individual who performed the final Assessment or final Compliance Review.  
6. **Interference:** Reserving scarce capacity (e.g., C1 slot, D1 specialization) for a target case reduces effective availability for concurrent nontarget cases in the same scheduling window, with nonlinear spillover effects.  
7. **Temporal:** External supplier response times (Request More Info loops) follow the given uncertainty ranges and are independent across cases; correlated delays would amplify rework propagation.  
8. **Quality:** Preliminary compliance work (rule10) may save 1–3hours in final review only when evidence is partially complete; if evidence is insufficient, final review duration reverts to baseline or increases due to coordination overhead.
