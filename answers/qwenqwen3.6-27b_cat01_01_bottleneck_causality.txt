## 8. Residual uncertainty
1. **Unobserved case urgency/risk:** Complex cases may vary in customer priority or regulatory risk, driving routing and completion decisions. *Most valuable addition:* `case_urgency_score` or `regulatory_risk_flag` at classification.
2. **Queue position and congestion dynamics:** `wait_before_review_h` aggregates queueing and compliance wait, obscuring whether delays stem from arrival spikes or capacity bottlenecks. *Most valuable addition:* `queue_position_at_assignment` and `real_time_queue_length`.
3. **Reviewer skill and fatigue heterogeneity:** Group label masks individual reviewer certification levels and shift fatigue, which may directly affect service time and rework. *Most valuable addition:* `reviewer_id` linked to `shift_hours_worked` and `certification_level`.
4. **Selection bias from completed-only sampling:** Longer cases may be more likely to abandon or timeout, distorting cycle-time distributions by group. *Most valuable addition:* Include `abandoned` or `timeout` cases with partial traces and drop-off timestamps.
5. **Compliance processing vs. waiting time:** The data conflate compliance service time with queue wait, preventing mediation analysis of the compliance step. *Most valuable addition:* Separate `compliance_service_h` from `wait_before_review_h` in the event log.
