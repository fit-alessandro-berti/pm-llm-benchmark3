### **1. Consistency Rules and Evidence Model**
This dataset exhibits multiple sources of ambiguity: missing E2O relations (e.g., `ev10`’s `external_ref=N-421?`), conflicting O2O structures (e.g., `INV-44` vs. `ORD-B`), and lifecycle inconsistencies (e.g., `ev08`’s `scanner_ref=PK8-1` vs. `ev10`’s `PK-8`). Below are the rules used, classified by strength, along with how they interact.

#### **Hard Invariants**
1. **One-to-one event-object mapping for `Create Order` (ev01/ev02)**:
   - Each `Create Order` event must map to exactly one `Order` object (ORD-A or ORD-B). This is enforced by the event’s `external_ref` (A/B) and the observed `ocel.relations` (ORD-A, ORD-B).
   - *Evidence against*: No conflicting `Add Item` or `Pack Item` events suggest a split order, but `ev11`’s `portal_customer=C17` could imply a single customer order.

2. **Cardinality of `Add Item` (ev03–ev05)**:
   - Each `Add Item` event must relate to exactly one `Order` and one `Item`. The `order_hint` field in `IT-A1/IT-A2/IT-B1` aligns with `ORD-A`/`ORD-B`, but `ev08`’s `scanner_ref=PK8-1` (missing `Item`) is a defect.

3. **One-to-one `Dispatch Package` (ev09/ev10)**:
   - Each `Dispatch Package` event must map to exactly one `Package` and one `Delivery`. `ev10`’s `external_ref=N-421?` is ambiguous—it could be a typo for `N-420` (matching `PK-8` and `DEL-X`) or a new `Delivery` (`DEL-Y`).

#### **Strong Defaults**
4. **Route consistency for `Package` and `Delivery`**:
   - `PK-7` and `PK-8` both have `route_hint=NORTH`, and `DEL-X`/`DEL-Y` share `route=NORTH`. This suggests a single `NORTH` route, but `ev10`’s `external_ref=N-421?` could imply a secondary route (e.g., `SOUTH`).
   - *Default*: Assume `ev10`’s `N-421` is a misprint for `N-420` (matching `PK-8` and `DEL-X`).

5. **Invoice-Payment alignment (ev12–ev14)**:
   - `INV-44` (`total=230`) and `PAY-2` (`amount=130`) do not match. The `PAY-1` (`amount=100`) aligns with `INV-44`, but `PAY-2` could be a partial payment or a separate invoice.
   - *Default*: Assume `PAY-2` is a separate `Payment` for a different `Invoice` (e.g., `INV-45` not shown) or that `INV-44` is incorrect.

6. **Order-Item containment (O2O)**:
   - The observed `contains` relations (`ORD-A`  `IT-A1`, `IT-A2`) and (`ORD-B`  `IT-B1`) are consistent with the `Add Item` events. However, `ev11`’s `portal_customer=C17` could imply a single order for the customer, conflicting with `ORD-B`’s `Add Item` events.

#### **Weak Heuristics**
7. **Timestamp ordering**:
   - Events are ordered chronologically, but `ev10` and `ev11` are both at `2026-03-11 10:00`/`14:00`. `ev11`’s `portal_customer=C17` suggests it refers to `ORD-A` (since `ORD-B` has no `portal_customer` in the log).

8. **Customer alignment**:
   - `INV-44` and `PAY-1`/`PAY-2` are linked to `C17`, but `ORD-B` also has `C17`. The invoice total (`230`) matches `ORD-A`’s `declared_value` (`130 + 100`), but `ORD-B`’s `declared_value` (`100`) is missing from the invoice.

9. **Weight consistency**:
   - `PK-7` (`3.1`) and `PK-8` (`2.9`) are plausible for `IT-A1`/`IT-A2` and `IT-B1`. However, `ev08`’s `scanner_ref=PK8-1` is ambiguous—it could be a typo for `PK8-2` (matching `IT-B1`).

#### **Evidence Against a Repair**
- **`ev08` and `PK-8`**: The `scanner_ref=PK8-1` is inconsistent with the `packs` relation (`PK-8`  `IT-A2`). This suggests a defect or missing `Item` for `IT-B1`.
- **`ev10` and `DEL-X/DEL-Y`**: If `ev10`’s `external_ref=N-421` is valid, it implies a second `Delivery` (`DEL-Y`), but `DEL-X` already carries `PK-7`. This could mean `PK-8` is dispatched separately.
- **`ev11` and `ORD-B`**: The `portal_customer=C17` is not tied to `ORD-B`, making it more likely to refer to `ORD-A`.
- **`INV-44` vs. `ORD-B`**: The invoice total (`230`) aligns with `ORD-A`’s `declared_value` (`130 + 100`), but `ORD-B`’s `declared_value` (`100`) is not accounted for in the invoice.

1. **Forcing `ev10` to `N-420`**: This assumes `N-421` is a typo, but it could be valid, creating a second `Delivery` (`DEL-Y`).
2. **Linking `INV-44` only to `ORD-A`**: This ignores `ORD-B`, which may have a separate invoice.
3. **Adding `PK-9` in Bundle B**: This introduces a new `Package` object with no prior evidence.
4. **Treating `ev11` as unique to `ORD-A`**: This ignores the possibility that `ev11` refers to `ORD-B` or multiple orders.
5. **Assuming `PAY-2` settles `INV-44`**: This ignores `PAY-1`’s partial payment, creating a false relation.
