## 2. Counterfactual continuations

**Rank 1 (Composite — parallel screening / S4-first with C-fallback)**
- **Process permits:** Information gathering via vendor screening (rule 5 allows evidence-related controls) and independent second review by a different officer (rules 6, 7); execution only after a valid approval path (rule 9).
- **Continuation tree:**
  - T0: Screening launched; S4 monitored.
  - Branch 1a (~35 min, S4 available): Screening result received (false-positive cleared vs. confirmed). S4 independently reviews validated evidence and risk score (noting 12-min-old timestamp).  **Approve** (probability ~0.55-0.75): record approval, execute by ~60-75 min, never disclose any SAR.  **Reject/Hold/SAR** (~0.25-0.45): case closed or held with confidential SAR filing.
  - Branch 1b (~40-45 min cutoff, S4 absent): Fallback to C. Interrupt P-901; S2 reviews. Same approval/rejection/hold outcomes, but execution/rejection near the 90-min boundary; P-901 spillover realized.
- **Direct benefit to P-884:** Deadline preservation in primary path; information gain reduces false-clear risk.
- **Harm to P-884:** API used; if fallback needed, breach risk rises; if both paths fail, deadline missed and customer harm incurred.
- **Spillover:** P-901 harmed only in fallback.
- **Compliance/non-monetary risk:** Near zero if second review completed; severe if anyone attempts to skip it.
- **Uncertainty:** S4 arrival is a distribution (35-65 min); screening may not change conditions; expedited review quality may differ from normal.

**Rank 2 (C — S2 immediate)**
- **Process permits:** Independent second review completed; then approve/reject/execute per rule 8.
- **Continuation:** S2 reviews evidence (risk 72, match 0.46 unresolved, evidence validated).  Approve  execute (earliest ~45 min, latest ~90 min).  Reject  close.  Hold + SAR filed  hold, no execution (rule 11: non-disclosure required).
- **Benefit:** High deadline preservation probability; regulatory path valid.
- **Harm:** P-901 breach probability +0.12-0.28; if rejected/held, €15k-€80k customer harm.
- **Spillover:** Direct and quantified on P-901.
- **Uncertainty:** Actual review duration within the 35-75 min range; approval decision is observational (0.55-0.75) and may shift under expedited conditions.

**Rank 3 (D — screening then review)**
- **Process permits:** Screening is permissible information gathering; second review still mandatory if conditions remain (rule 6).
- **Continuation:** Screening completes (~15-30 min). If conditions persist (likely), second officer reviews. Then same approve/reject/hold branches.
- **Benefit:** Potential resolution of 0.46 fuzzy match; may update risk perception; execution value similar to Rank 2.
- **Harm:** One API call consumed; added delay pushes timeline closer to breach; if S2 is used, spillover applies later.
- **Spillover:** Contingent on officer selection.
- **Uncertainty:** Screening result is unknown; cumulative time is additive; the 12-min-old risk score may or may not update.

**Rank 4 (F — immediate rejection)**
- **Process permits:** Rejection is permitted after required reviews (rule 8), but the second review has not occurred here. The prompt marks this as conditionally valid.
- **Continuation:** Branch A — documentation threshold satisfied  conformant rejection, case closed in 10-20 min, no execution. Branch B — threshold not satisfied  rejection is nonconformant; must revert to review path with lost time.
- **Benefit:** Zero unlawful-execution risk; rapid closure.
- **Harm:** False-rejection cost €15k-€80k; execution value lost.
- **Spillover:** None.
- **Uncertainty:** Whether the validated evidence package actually meets the documented rejection threshold is unconfirmed.

**Rank 5 (E — additional evidence request)**
- **Process permits:** Extends the evidence-request cycle (rule 5).
- **Continuation:** Customer requested; 6-24 h delay. Deadline missed. Eventually customer responds with exculpatory, inconclusive, or adverse evidence. Then second review proceeds and case resolves.
- **Benefit:** Maximum information; eliminates false-clear risk entirely.
- **Harm:** Guaranteed deadline loss (€20k-€35k) and prolonged hold harm (€15k-€80k).
- **Spillover:** None.
- **Uncertainty:** Customer response time and quality; intervention effect is large (process diverges for 6-24 h).

## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning

The decision environment is **asymmetrically dominated** by regulatory and non-monetary harm. The direct execution value is bounded (€20k-€35k). False-rejection or prolonged-hold harm is also bounded but can reach €80k. By contrast, unlawful execution—given risk 70, an unresolved fuzzy sanctions match (0.46), and no independent approval—exposes the organization to **€100k-€500k+ in regulatory penalties plus non-monetary consequences** (licensing action, criminal referral, reputational damage, and mandatory disclosure obligations). This is an order-of-magnitude larger and not fully capturable in monetary terms. Therefore:

- **A and B are strictly dominated** by every conformant option, regardless of how favorable the deadline forecast appears. A favorable operational forecast does not create the missing second-officer authorization or repair the four-eyes violation; it only changes an expected-value calculation within an invalid state space.
- Among conformant paths, we compare using **expected value weighted by probability** and **portfolio spillover**:
  - **C** maximizes P-884 deadline preservation but imposes a **certain, quantifiable spillover** on P-901.
  - **The Rank-1 composite** achieves similar preservation in the primary branch (S4 available) with **zero spillover**, plus an information gain from screening, at the cost of one scarce API call and a conditional fallback.
  - **D** adds information but delays the mandatory review; it is preferable to pure waiting (E) because screening is faster (15-30 min) than full customer evidence (6-24 h).
  - **F** is reasonable only if the evidence threshold for rejection is clearly met; otherwise it risks false-rejection harm (€15k-€80k) without gaining the information that a second review or screening would provide.
  - **E** is the “safe harbor” for information but is **dominated by D** for this case because the 90-min deadline makes 6-24 h unacceptable and the screening API offers faster information.

**Uncertainty about intervention effects:** The predictions (approval 0.55-0.75; breach 0.48-0.64) are observational and may not transport unchanged under expedited review (C/D) or parallel screening. Expediting may reduce review depth (lower approval accuracy) or screening may change the match status (altering the need for second review). These ranges should be treated as reference points, not fixed utility weights.

## 5. Resource spillovers

**S2 versus S4:**
- **S2** is immediately available but forces a **45-minute interruption of P-901**, a high-value payment with a 70-minute deadline. The estimated spillover is **+0.12 to +0.28 breach probability** for P-901. Given both cases are high-value, this is a material portfolio-level cost.
- **S4** has **no direct spillover** to P-901, but its arrival window is wide (35-65 min). Waiting for S4 introduces schedule risk for P-884.

**Alternate-screening API quota:**
- Three expedited calls remain today. Using one for P-884 is defensible given the €480k amount and the **0.46 unresolved match**, but it reduces resilience for other cases. The information gain justifies the expenditure in this composite plan, but not in a pure “wait and do nothing” strategy.

**Risk of consuming time on the exception path (G):**
- Pursuing G consumes **20-60+ minutes** with a high failure rate. During that window, S2 could have been activated, screening could have run, and S4 might have arrived. If G fails (the likely case), the remaining time is insufficient for C or D. Therefore G should **not** be pursued in parallel with the primary path; it is an “all-in” bet with poor odds and should be avoided.

**How the ranking shifts with S4 timing:**
- **If S4 becomes available in ~35 minutes:** The composite plan is dominant. Wait for S4 with parallel screening; **avoid C and its P-901 spillover entirely**. Action D becomes more attractive because the second review starts promptly.
- **If S4 is not expected until ~65 minutes:** Waiting alone misses the 90-minute deadline (65 min + review + execution > 90 min). The **fallback to C must be triggered early** (cutoff ~35-40 min), or the primary path should shift directly to **C** (accept the spillover) rather than risking a breach. In this scenario, the value of the API call decreases relative to immediate review, and the composite plan still applies but with an earlier, more aggressive fallback.

## 6. Final recommendation and confidence

**Primary recommendation:** Execute the **Rank-1 composite plan** immediately:
1. Launch the **alternate sanctions screening** now (uses one API call; runs in parallel).
2. Monitor **S4 availability** with a **hard decision cutoff at ~40 minutes** from now.
3. **If S4 is available by the cutoff:** Have S4 conduct the **independent second-officer review** of the validated evidence and the risk profile (noting the 12-min-old risk-score timestamp). Proceed to **approve, reject, or hold/file a Suspicious Activity Report** per rules. **Never disclose a SAR to the customer** (rule 11). Execute only after a valid approval is recorded.
4. **If S4 is not available by the cutoff:** Immediately fall back to **Action C**—interrupt P-901 and request expedited independent review by **S2**—then proceed as above.
5. Do **not** execute, approve, or reject without the completed independent second review being documented.

This integrates prediction (approval likelihood 0.55-0.75, breach risk 0.48-0.64), conformance (rules 6, 7, 9), resource management (protect P-901 unless necessary; use API for information), and uncertainty (S4 arrival window, screening result, outdated score).

**Fallback:** If the screening reveals an **unambiguous false positive** and updates materially change conditions, still complete the second review with the first available officer (S4 or S2) before any execution. If both S4 and S2 are unavailable and the deadline is imminent, **consider Action F only if the documented evidence threshold for rejection is clearly satisfied** (currently unconfirmed); otherwise, accept the deadline breach and continue the conformant review/hold path rather than take an invalid shortcut.

**Confidence level:** **Moderate (~70%)**. The plan is structurally sound and fully conformant, but outcomes depend on the **S4 arrival distribution** (35-65 min), the **screening result**, and the **variable review duration** (35-75 min).

**Trigger for immediate reranking:**

- **Compliance Director unexpectedly authorizes with a valid incident ID**  then evaluate G, but still prefer the standard independent-review path unless the emergency is genuinely documented and justified.
