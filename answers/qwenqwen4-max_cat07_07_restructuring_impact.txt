# 4. Risks invisible in average cycle time

1. **Tail latency and variance.** Queueing wait scales with /(1); moving pooled  from 0.74 to 0.90 roughly quadruples expected wait and inflates p99 far more than p50. A +1.3 h/case mean shift is invisible in a 7.5-day mean, yet the same shift can add days to p99. Monitor p90/p95/p99 and the p90/median ratio (currently 2.4–2.65), not the mean.
2. **High-complexity cases pay the bill for average gains.** Complex cases touch technical + legal/risk + approval + difficult QC — four saturated queues instead of one co-located specialist. Simple cases (the majority) get faster and dominate the average; complex cases can lose 20–80% in the tail. Stratify by `case_complexity` and report complexity-weighted tails.
3. **Right-censoring / survivorship in cycle-time statistics.** If backlogs build, slow cases remain open and are excluded from "average cycle time of completed cases". The metric then *improves because the process is failing*. Always report open-case WIP, oldest-open-case age, and a survival/Kaplan–Meier-style estimate alongside completed-case stats.
4. **Local knowledge loss and slow learning decay.** Regional customer conventions currently suppress rework in complex cases. Routing all hard cases away removes the learning substrate; the effect appears in rework and first-time-fix over 6–18 months, long after any pilot's average cycle time has been declared a success. Also invisible: the informal corridor advice that never generates an event — its disappearance shows up only as *more referrals*, which management may misread as "better detection".
5. **Fairness and distributional harm.** Priority queues plus anonymous "pull" systematically disfavour low-priority, small-volume, non-standard-language and accommodation-needing cases. Aggregate SLA can hold at 7.5% while one segment goes to 20%. Fairness must be measured *conditional on need* (wait given complexity/priority), with maxmin gaps and concentration measures — never as an overall rate.
6. **Accountability diffusion.** "Pull from a shared queue" plus one fewer supervisor creates cases with no owner. Symptom set: rising `reassignment_count`, ping-pong edges, orphaned cases with 24 h+ gaps, unstable role partitions, `handover_reason` = "clarification". None of these necessarily lengthen the *average* case, but they lengthen the *difficult* case and destroy escalation traceability.
7. **Exception handling and resilience collapse.** Averages are computed mostly on normal days. A 3-person pool at 74% load has **no** spare server: one absence    1.11  organisation-wide backlog, versus today's team-local damage. Additionally, inconsistent cross-location case notes (already the cause of 18% rework on cross-team cover) become the *normal* interface, not the exception.
8. **Mix-shift / Simpson's paradox and measurement decay.** East improving while North/South deteriorate can net to zero. If referral rules change *which* cases are labelled complex, before/after comparisons are no longer like-for-like. And if staff move advice into unlogged phone calls, the log itself becomes biased — the process looks cleaner because work left the log.

# 6. Recommendation

**Pilot first, with the proposal modified — do not roll out as written.** The idea has a real, quantifiable upside (pooling three specialists at 0.74 could cut expected specialist waiting by ~4× *at unchanged load*, and East is genuinely overloaded at 82%). But three elements of the current design are arithmetically unsafe:

1. **Legal/Risk at 93%/91% receiving +33% referrals is an unstable queue (1.2).** Add ~45% capacity or cap/triage referrals *before* any routing change. This is a precondition, not a guardrail.
2. **Do not centralise difficult Quality Check and legal/risk triage into the same queue in the same step.** That is precisely the "hidden bottleneck" staff warned about, and it is absent from management's handover table.
3. **Do not remove a supervisor while centralising approvals.** Approval volume per remaining approver rises ~75% under the stated frequencies; the assumed 2.43.0 h wait is unsupported.

Additional design conditions: retain **0.2–0.3 FTE local technical anchor per team** (preserves informal advice, regional conventions, learning and absence resilience — and keeps delete-one-resource  below 1); enforce a **named case owner** on every case, with pull-from-queue assigning ownership explicitly; **treat utilisation as a constraint, not a target** — replace "+15% utilisation" with "0.85 utilisation at equal-or-better p90"; instrument queue entry/exit and informal consults before starting.

**Conditional go/no-go:** proceed to Phase 2 only if, after wash-in, _pool 0.85, legal/risk / 0.9, queue-age p99 1.5× baseline, complex-case p90 non-inferior within 10%, no segment worse by >2 pp SLA, complex rework non-inferior within 2 pp, and ownership-clarity scores stable. Full rollout requires Phase 2 (South) to show that a *strong* local team does not lose complex-case quality — East alone cannot demonstrate this, because East has the least local expertise to lose.

**What management must not infer from early averages:**
- That a flat or improved **mean/median cycle time** validates the design — the model predicts only ~+1.3 h/case of average queue wait, i.e. a change too small to detect, while p99 may double.
- That improved averages during weeks 1–4 are structural — they may be transition backlog clearing, novelty/Hawthorne effects, or **right-censoring** (slow cases still open and therefore excluded).
- That **higher utilisation** is a benefit realised; above ~0.88 it is a liability purchased.
- That **fewer distinct handover types** means fewer handovers; total count rises 343359 and weighted wait rises 23%.
- That **more specialist referrals** proves better risk detection; it may be loss of informal local advice and defensive referral behaviour.
- That **aggregate SLA stability** means fairness is preserved; East can gain while North/South complex cases and accommodation-needing segments lose.
- That **12 weeks of clean data** shows learning and resilience are intact; those degrade over 6–18 months and must be tracked with the proxy indicators (L10–L12, G7–G9, G11) long after the pilot closes.

# 7. PM4Py analysis sketch

> Version note: PM4Py signatures vary (some functions return networkx graphs, others dicts; `discover_organizational_roles` may require activity/resource keys). Treat this as executable *sketch* with explicit helper computations rather than a guaranteed drop-in script. Business-calendar conversion is assumed for all hour figures.

```python
import numpy as np, pandas as pd, networkx as nx
import pm4py

CASE, ACT, TS, RES = "case:concept:name", "concept:name", "time:timestamp", "org:resource"

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- load & format
def load(path, period):
    df = pd.read_csv(path, parse_dates=["time:timestamp"])
    df = pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id=CASE, activity_key=ACT, timestamp_key=TS)
    df["period"] = period
    return df

df = pd.concat([load("before.csv", "pre"), load("after.csv", "post")], ignore_index=True)
df = df.sort_values([CASE, TS])

def to_log(frame):
    return pm4py.convert_to_event_log(frame)

# ------------------------------------------------- segmentation (case-level filter)
def slice_log(frame, team=None, complexity=None, specialty=None, segment=None, period=None):
    log = to_log(frame)
    for attr, vals in [("team", team), ("case_complexity", complexity),
                       ("required_specialty", specialty), ("customer_segment", segment)]:
        if vals:
            log = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(log, attr, vals, level="case", retain=True)
    if period:
        cases = frame.loc[frame.period == period, CASE].unique()
        log = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(log, CASE, list(cases), level="case", retain=True)
    return log

SEGMENTS = dict(
    east_complex   = dict(team=["East"],  complexity=["high"]),
    north_complex  = dict(team=["North"], complexity=["high"]),
    south_complex  = dict(team=["South"], complexity=["high"]),
    legal_risk     = dict(specialty=["legal", "risk", "security"]),
    accommodation  = dict(segment=["regional_language", "accommodation_needed"]),
)

# ------------------------------------------- 1. handover-of-work network + edge costs
def handover_table(frame):
    log = to_log(frame)
    how = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(log, resource_key=RES)   # DiGraph
    n_cases = frame[CASE].nunique()

    d = frame.sort_values([CASE, TS]).copy()
    g = d.groupby(CASE)
    d["res_next"]  = g[RES].shift(-1)
    d["act_next"]  = g[ACT].shift(-1)
    d["t_next"]    = g[TS].shift(-1)
    d["rework_next"] = g["rework_flag"].shift(-1)
    h = d[(d[RES] != d["res_next"]) & d["res_next"].notna()].copy()
    h["wait_h"] = (h["t_next"] - h[TS]).dt.total_seconds() / 3600  # TODO business calendar

    edges = (h.groupby([RES, "res_next"])
               .agg(freq=("wait_h", "size"),
                    wait_p50=("wait_h", "median"),
                    wait_p90=("wait_h", lambda s: s.quantile(.90)),
                    wait_p99=("wait_h", lambda s: s.quantile(.99)),
                    rework_rate=("rework_next", "mean"))
               .reset_index())
    edges["freq_per_100"] = 100 * edges.freq / n_cases

    summary = dict(
        handovers_per_100 = edges.freq_per_100.sum(),
        wait_index_h_per_case = (edges.freq_per_100 * edges.wait_p50).sum() / 100,
        rework_exposure_index = (edges.freq_per_100 * edges.rework_rate).sum() / 100,
        distinct_edges = len(edges),
        degree_centralisation = nx.degree_centralization(how.to_directed())
                                if hasattr(nx, "degree_centralization") else None,
        top_betweenness = sorted(nx.betweenness_centrality(how, weight="weight").items(),
                                 key=lambda x: -x[1])[:5],
    )
    return how, edges, summary

how_pre, e_pre, s_pre = handover_table(df[df.period == "pre"])
how_post, e_post, s_post = handover_table(df[df.period == "post"])
edge_diff = e_pre.merge(e_post, on=[RES, "res_next"], how="outer",
                        suffixes=("_pre", "_post")).fillna(0)

# ping-pong / return-to-sender (role clarity)
def pingpong_rate(frame):
    seqs = frame.groupby(CASE)[RES].apply(list)
    def has_pp(s): return any(s[i] == s[i+2] and s[i] != s[i+1] for i in range(len(s)-2))
    return seqs.apply(has_pp).mean()

# --------------------------------- 2. working-together & subcontracting networks
def org_networks(frame):
    log = to_log(frame)
    wt  = pm4py.discover_working_together_network(log, resource_key=RES)
    sc  = pm4py.discover_subcontracting_network(log, resource_key=RES)
    und = wt.to_undirected()
    teams = frame.groupby(RES)["team"].agg(lambda s: s.mode().iat[0]).to_dict()
    nx.set_node_attributes(und, teams, "team")
    comms = nx.algorithms.community.greedy_modularity_communities(und, weight="weight")
    return dict(
        wt_density = nx.density(und),
        wt_modularity_by_team = nx.algorithms.community.modularity(und, comms, weight="weight"),
        sc_centralisation = np.mean(list(dict(sc.degree()).values())),
        sc_max_depth = (max(nx.shortest_path_length(sc, n) for n in sc) if sc.number_of_nodes() else 0),
        sc_top_betweenness = sorted(nx.betweenness_centrality(sc).items(), key=lambda x: -x[1])[:5],
    )

# ------------------------- 3. roles, role stability, resource similarity (learning)
def roles_and_similarity(frame):
    log = to_log(frame)
    roles = pm4py.discover_organizational_roles(log)          # list of resource sets / objects
    sim   = pm4py.discover_activity_based_resource_similarity(log)
    role_sets = [set(r) if not hasattr(r, "resources") else set(r.resources) for r in roles]
    # ownership concentration + role purity
    per_case = frame.groupby(CASE)[RES].value_counts(normalize=True).groupby(level=0).max()
    act_hhi  = frame.groupby([ACT, RES]).size().unstack(fill_value=0)
    act_hhi  = ((act_hhi.div(act_hhi.sum(axis=1), axis=0))**2).sum(axis=1)  # SPOF proxy
    return role_sets, sim, dict(mean_owner_share=per_case.mean(),
                                spof_activities=(act_hhi > 0.5).sum())

def role_stability(sets_a, sets_b):     # Jaccard-best-match average
    return np.mean([max(len(a & b)/len(a | b) for b in sets_b) for a in sets_a])

def deskilling_trend(frame, local_workers, tech_workers):
    out = {}
    for m, sub in frame.groupby(frame[TS].dt.to_period("M")):
        _, sim, _ = roles_and_similarity(sub)
        # sim: dict-of-dicts or matrix -> adapt accessor
        vals = [sim[a][b] for a in local_workers for b in tech_workers if a in sim and b in sim[a]]
        out[str(m)] = float(np.mean(vals)) if vals else np.nan
    return pd.Series(out)   # declining => local technical skill atrophy

# --------------------------------------- 4. variants + performance DFG (path change)
def variant_profile(frame, top_k=20):
    log = to_log(frame)
    variants = pm4py.get_variants(log)                 # {variant: [cases]}
    counts = pd.Series({v: len(c) for v, c in variants.items()}).sort_values(ascending=False)
    p = counts / counts.sum()
    entropy = float(-(p * np.log(p)).sum())
    rework_share = sum(n for v, n in counts.items() if "Rework" in v) / counts.sum()
    keep = list(counts.head(top_k).index)
    top_log = pm4py.filter_variants(log, keep)
    dfg, sa, ea = pm4py.discover_dfg(log)
    pdfg, sa2, ea2 = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(log)   # edge -> {mean/median/...}
    return dict(n_variants=len(counts), top20_share=p.head(top_k).sum(),
                entropy=entropy, rework_variant_share=rework_share), dfg, pdfg, top_log

# ------------------------------------------- 5. service / waiting times (queueing)
def waiting_profile(frame):
    log = to_log(frame)
    log = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(log)      # adds @@service_time / @@waiting_time
    service_times = pm4py.get_service_time(log)
    d = pm4py.convert_to_dataframe(log)
    d["wait_h"] = d["@@waiting_time"] / 3600
    d["serv_h"] = d["@@service_time"] / 3600
    by_queue = (d.groupby([ACT])["wait_h"]
                  .agg(p50="median", p90=lambda s: s.quantile(.9),
                       p99=lambda s: s.quantile(.99), n="size"))
    # utilisation: busy hours / available business hours per resource per week
    d["week"] = d[TS].dt.to_period("W")
    util = d.groupby([RES, "week"])["serv_h"].sum() / 30.0     # 30 productive h/wk assumption
    # weekly queue-age slope = leading bottleneck signal
    slope = (d.groupby([ACT, "week"])["wait_h"].quantile(.9).unstack()
               .T.apply(lambda s: np.polyfit(np.arange(len(s.dropna())), s.dropna(), 1)[0]
                                  if s.notna().sum() > 3 else np.nan))
    return by_queue, util.clip(upper=1.5), slope, d

# ---------------------------------- 6. case outcomes: cycle time, tails, SLA, rework
SLA_DAYS = {"high": 3, "medium": 7, "low": 14}
def case_outcomes(frame, open_cases_snapshot=None):
    log = to_log(frame)
    g = frame.groupby(CASE)
    c = g.agg(start=(TS, "min"), end=(TS, "max"), team=("team", "first"),
              complexity=("case_complexity", "first"), specialty=("required_specialty", "first"),
              segment=("customer_segment", "first"), priority=("priority", "first"),
              rework=("rework_flag", "max"), reassigned=("reassignment_count", "max"),
              n_resources=(RES, "nunique"), n_teams=("team", "nunique"))
    c["cycle_d"] = (c.end - c.start).dt.total_seconds() / 86400
    c["sla_miss"] = [r.cycle_d > SLA_DAYS.get(r.priority, 14) for r in c.itertuples()]
    # censoring: cases still open in the snapshot must NOT be dropped
    if open_cases_snapshot is not None:
        c["censored"] = c.index.isin(open_cases_snapshot)
    rw = pm4py.get_rework_cases_per_activity(log)
    return c, rw

def tail_report(c):
    return (c.groupby(["team", "complexity", "segment"])
              .agg(n=("cycle_d", "size"), p50=("cycle_d", "median"),
                   p90=("cycle_d", lambda s: s.quantile(.9)),
                   p99=("cycle_d", lambda s: s.quantile(.99)),
                   rework=("rework", "mean"), sla=("sla_miss", "mean"),
                   reassigned=("reassigned", "mean"), owners=("n_resources", "mean")))

def fairness_gaps(rep):
    g = rep[rep.n >= 30].groupby(level="segment")[["sla", "p90"]].mean()
    return dict(sla_max_min_gap=g.sla.max() - g.sla.min(),
                p90_max_min_ratio=g.p90.max() / g.p90.min())

# --------------------------------------------- 7. resilience: absence stress test
def absence_stress(frame, resource, capacity_h_per_week=30):
    sub = frame[frame[RES] != resource]                    # remove absent resource's capacity
    remaining = frame[frame[ACT].isin(frame.loc[frame[RES] == resource, ACT].unique())][RES].nunique()
    _, util, _, _ = waiting_profile(frame)
    load = util.loc[resource].mean() * 1.0
    pool_rho_without = (util.mean() * (remaining + 1)) / max(remaining, 1)
    cross = handover_table(sub)[1]
    return dict(pool_rho_without_absent=pool_rho_without,
                unstable=pool_rho_without > 1.0,
                residual_handovers_per_100=cross.freq_per_100.sum())

# -------------------------------------- 8. temporal features / drift & seasonality
def temporal(frame):
    return pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(to_log(frame))

# --------------------------------------------------- 9. before/after comparison run
def compare(frame, seg_name, seg_kwargs):
    rows = {}
    for period in ["pre", "post"]:
        f = frame[frame.period == period]
        log = slice_log(f, period=None, **seg_kwargs)
        f = pm4py.convert_to_dataframe(log)
        _, edges, hs = handover_table(f)
        bq, util, slope, _ = waiting_profile(f)
        c, rw = case_outcomes(f)
        v, dfg, pdfg, _ = variant_profile(f)
        rows[period] = dict(**hs, **v, **org_networks(f),
                            pingpong=pingpong_rate(f),
                            wait_p99_max=bq.p99.max(),
                            util_pool=util.mean(), util_max=util.max(),
                            queue_p90_slope_max=slope.max(),
                            cycle_p50=c.cycle_d.median(), cycle_p90=c.cycle_d.quantile(.9),
                            rework=c.rework.mean(), sla=c.sla_miss.mean(),
                            reassigned=c.reassigned.mean(),
                            **fairness_gaps(tail_report(c)))
    return pd.DataFrame(rows).T.assign(segment=seg_name)

report = pd.concat([compare(df, name, kw) for name, kw in SEGMENTS.items()])
report.to_csv("restructuring_before_after.csv")

# ------------------------------------------------------- 10. guardrail evaluation
GUARDRAILS = {"util_pool": 0.85, "util_hard": 0.90, "wait_p99_ratio": 1.5,
              "cycle_p90_ratio": 1.10, "sla_delta_pp": 0.02, "rework_delta_pp": 0.02}

def guardrail_flags(pre, post):
    f = {}
    f["rollback_util"]   = post.util_pool > GUARDRAILS["util_hard"]
    f["adjust_util"]     = post.util_pool > GUARDRAILS["util_pool"]
    f["tail_wait"]       = post.wait_p99_max > GUARDRAILS["wait_p99_ratio"] * pre.wait_p99_max
    f["tail_cycle"]      = post.cycle_p90   > GUARDRAILS["cycle_p90_ratio"] * pre.cycle_p90
    f["sla_fairness"]    = (post.sla - pre.sla) > GUARDRAILS["sla_delta_pp"]
    f["rework"]          = (post.rework - pre.rework) > GUARDRAILS["rework_delta_pp"]
    f["queue_growing"]   = post.queue_p90_slope_max > 0
    f["role_clarity"]    = (post.pingpong > 1.2 * pre.pingpong) or (post.reassigned > pre.reassigned + .3)
    f["censoring_trap"]  = (post.cycle_p50 <= pre.cycle_p50) and f["queue_growing"]  # FALSE WIN
    return f
```

**How the code answers the question:** `handover_table` exposes the count-vs-cost distinction (343359 vs 5.887.22 h); `waiting_profile` gives per-queue p50/p90/**p99**, utilisation and the **queue-age slope** that discriminates the three scenarios; `org_networks` + `roles_and_similarity` + `pingpong_rate` operationalise central-queue formation, role clarity and de-skilling; `absence_stress` makes the resilience regression explicit (>1 on one absence); `tail_report` + `fairness_gaps` prevent East's gains and simple-case gains from masking North/South complex-case and accommodation-segment harm; and `guardrail_flags["censoring_trap"]` explicitly detects the false win where average cycle time improves while queues grow.
