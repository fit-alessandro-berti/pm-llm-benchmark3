## 1. Normalization Assumptions

At the strict deadlines, the evidence establishes three breaches. T-402 started investigation at least 58.5 seconds late. T-408 completed containment at least 9 min 57 s late and closed at least 2 h 24 min 57 s late. These are lower bounds on lateness over all feasible event times. I use zero SLA tolerance and ζ = 1 for the detailed findings, then vary each parameter separately.

I write A for Alert_Received, I for Investigation_Start, C for Containment_Complete and K for Close_Incident. F means Investigation_Complete and S means Alert_Sent. An arrow denotes elapsed time between the named occurrences in the same case, including pairs with an intervening event.

- **Clock model.** For a displayed time with UTC equivalent r in a given fold, first expand the display to the recorded-clock interval [r + qₗ, r + qᵤ]. Subtract the recorded-minus-true offset [a, b] to obtain [r + qₗ − b, r + qᵤ − a]. A fast clock moves the earliest possible true time earlier. Endpoints are inclusive. A floored minute contributes [0, 59.999] s. I include both half-step ties for nearest rounding since the tie convention is unspecified. HUB contributes ±0.5 s of measurement uncertainty before clock correction.
- **Combined source bounds.** Relative to the displayed instant in UTC, HUB gives [−1.5, +1.5] s, ordinary OPS-BER [−120, +149.999] s, SENSOR [−10, +10] s, VENDOR-NY [−180, +239.999] s, LEGACY-BER [−60, +119.999] s per fold, and MOBILE-LON [−330, +330] s for the time expressed by its clock. June civil offsets are CEST = UTC+02, EDT = UTC−04 and BST = UTC+01. On the October rollback, Berlin fold 0 uses UTC+02 and fold 1 uses UTC+01. None of the displayed rounding bins crosses a transition within its fold.
- **Feasibility.** Offset errors may vary between events within their stated bounds, including on the same system. There is no event-to-event drift bound or attested common offset. This is a bounded-error model without probability weights. For unconstrained endpoints X and Y, Y − X ranges over [Yₗ − Xᵤ, Yᵤ − Xₗ]. For linked times I impose their joint constraints first, take the minimum and maximum difference within each feasible fold assignment, and union the results. Marginal ranges for different pairs need not attain their extremes simultaneously.
- **Order and lifecycle.** I use A ≤ I ≤ C ≤ K for matched occurrences where present, allowing simultaneity at the available precision. This is a substantive order assumption in T-407 and T-409. In T-405 I take the specified ingestion order as occurrence order evidence. C from SENSOR denotes completion. In T-404 the signed record measures elapsed time for the same investigation, so F − I ∈ [1,080, 1,500] s. I retain F, its uncertainty and this constraint.
- **Mobile entry.** In T-406 my primary interpretation is that the timestamp describes when the user made the entry. True occurrence = corrected entry time − delay, with delay in [0, 300] s. Delay moves only the lower bound of occurrence earlier. I also evaluate a field containing a user-supplied occurrence time entered later.
- **Conditional evidence.** In T-407 I evaluate the explicit delivery scenario A = S + latency, latency ∈ [0, 1,200] s, with A ≤ I. A missing acknowledgment leaves delivery itself unverified. In T-409 I provisionally accept the hourly engineering range [−720, +720] s for the OPS offset, superseding the ordinary daily range. The confidence column and sensitivity analysis identify findings that depend on those choices.

## 3. Certain/Possible/Indeterminate Findings

Durations and acceptance regions are in seconds, with inclusive limits. A certain pass requires the whole feasible set to be inside the acceptance region. A certain violation requires every component to miss it. A possible violation has witnesses on both sides of the decision, including the case where one component passes and a disjoint one violates. An untestable pair or an unusable evidence model is indeterminate.

Severity is **High** for an established SLA breach, **Review** for a possible SLA breach, **Diagnostic** for a statistical deviation or possible deviation, and **None** for a pass. Confidence describes the evidence behind the interval. High means direct, well-bounded HUB evidence, Medium means bounded device, clock or signed lifecycle evidence or reliance on order, and Low marks a weak time estimate or an unverified semantic proxy. A certain classification with Low confidence is conditional on that proxy.

- **T-406, occurrence-time field.** If the user typed the actual mobile-clock occurrence time and only submission was late, the corrected field gives I ∈ [2026-06-01T12:08:30Z, 2026-06-01T12:19:30Z]. A→I becomes [508.5, 1,171.5] s and I→C becomes [1,948.5, 2,611.5] s. S2 and profile I→C now pass even at their strictest settings. S1 and profile A→I keep their classifications.
- **T-407, delivery and causality.** If delivery within the latency bound is accepted but A ≤ I is withdrawn, the raw signed I − A is [−183, 1,023] s. A negative lag means the start may have preceded receipt. If such a start counts as an S1 sequencing failure, S1 becomes mixed even at +5 min. The eventually-following A→I test then applies only in worlds with that order, where the lag is [0, 1,023] s. A→C still has the defined range [2,097, 3,303] s and passes all three bands. If proof of receipt is required and delivery itself is unestablished, S1, profile A→I and profile A→C are INDETERMINATE. S2 and profile I→C retain their direct HUB evidence.
- **T-409, missing hourly telemetry.** Before ordering, signed I − A is [−481.5, 1,021.499] s. Enforcing A ≤ I removes those negative lags. If the ±12 min estimate is discarded but the logged I is known to lie between A and C, the HUB endpoint bounds give [0, 2,103] s for each of A→I and I→C. S1 becomes mixed even at +5 min. S2 still certainly passes. Both profile pairs are mixed at all three ζ values. If neither an offset bound nor the ordering of I is defensible, the tests involving I are INDETERMINATE. The two HUB timestamps still establish profile A→C. If recovered telemetry actually validated the ordinary OPS range for this hour, A→I would narrow to [118.5, 391.499] s. S1 would certainly pass, yet the entire duration would be below the ζ = 1 profile minimum of 420 s. That conclusion requires the recovered telemetry.

## 5. PM4Py Temporal-Conformance Code

The code computes the intervals from the raw records. For each DST assignment it bounds time differences jointly, discards inconsistent assignments, and takes the union of the surviving difference intervals. The separate PM4Py log contains one feasible midpoint schedule per case, including the two inferred activities. Its output is a **lossy midpoint approximation**.

The diagnostic adapter uses PM4Py's EventLog result with one deviation list per trace and the two activity names at the start of each deviation record. It prints the raw result if it encounters an unsupported shape. The independent point-band check makes the API comparison inspectable. With these data, the midpoint log yields 10 flagged profile pairs at ζ = 1, two at ζ = 2 and zero at ζ = 3. For example, T-401 A→I has a midpoint near 855 s, flagged at ζ = 1 despite feasible passes. T-406 A→I has a 690 s midpoint that passes despite feasible deviations. T-408 I→C has a midpoint exactly on the inclusive 3,300 s limit at ζ = 2, while the feasible duration can exceed it by 3 s. PM4Py receives point times only.

```python
from datetime import datetime
from itertools import product
from math import inf
from zoneinfo import ZoneInfo

import pandas as pd
import pm4py

A, I, C, K = ("Alert_Received", "Investigation_Start",
              "Containment_Complete", "Close_Incident")
F, S = "Investigation_Complete", "Alert_Sent"
AI, IC, AC, CK = (A, I), (I, C), (A, C), (C, K)
profile = {AI: (600, 180), IC: (2100, 600),
           AC: (2700, 720), CK: (86400, 21600)}
sla = {"S1": (AI, 900), "S2": (IC, 2700), "S3": (CK, 129600)}
names = dict(A=A, I=I, C=C, K=K, F=F, S=S)
sources = dict(H="HUB", B="OPS-BER", D="SENSOR", V="VENDOR-NY",
               L="LEGACY-BER", M="MOBILE-LON")
raw_text = """401 A 2026-06-01 08:00:00 H
401 I 2026-06-01 10:14 B
401 C 2026-06-01 08:51:10 D
401 K 2026-06-02 09:00:00 H
402 A 2026-06-01 09:00:00 H
402 I 2026-06-01 11:18 B
402 C 2026-06-01 10:02:00 H
403 A 2026-06-01 04:00 V
403 I 2026-06-01 08:16:00 H
403 C 2026-06-01 08:58:00 H
404 A 2026-06-01 10:00:00 H
404 F 2026-06-01 10:35:00 H
404 C 2026-06-01 10:55:00 H
405 A 2025-10-26 02:50 L
405 I 2025-10-26 02:05 L
405 C 2025-10-26 02:42 L
406 A 2026-06-01 12:00:00 H
406 I 2026-06-01 13:14 M
406 C 2026-06-01 12:52:00 H
407 S 2026-06-01 14:00:00 H
407 I 2026-06-01 14:17:00 H
407 C 2026-06-01 14:55:00 H
408 A 2026-06-01 15:00:00 H
408 I 2026-06-01 15:10:00 H
408 C 2026-06-01 16:05:00 H
408 K 2026-06-03 06:30:00 H
409 A 2026-06-01 16:00:00 H
409 I 2026-06-01 18:04 B
409 C 2026-06-01 16:35:00 H"""
rows = []
for line in raw_text.splitlines():
    case, act, day, clock, src = line.split()
    rows.append(("T-" + case, names[act], day + " " + clock, sources[src]))
raw = pd.DataFrame(rows, columns=["case", "activity", "recorded", "source"])

# Display uncertainty and then recorded-minus-true offset, in milliseconds.
meta = {
    "HUB": ("UTC", -500, 500, -1000, 1000),
    "OPS-BER": ("Europe/Berlin", 0, 59999, -90000, 120000),
    "SENSOR": ("UTC", -5000, 5000, -5000, 5000),
    "VENDOR-NY": ("America/New_York", 0, 59999, -180000, 180000),
    "LEGACY-BER": ("Europe/Berlin", 0, 59999, -60000, 60000),
    "MOBILE-LON": ("Europe/London", -30000, 30000, -300000, 300000),
}

def normalize(row):
    tz, qlo, qhi, olo, ohi = meta[row.source]
    if (row.case, row.source) == ("T-409", "OPS-BER"):
        olo, ohi = -720000, 720000
    local = datetime.fromisoformat(row.recorded)
    alternatives = set()
    for fold in (0, 1):
        aware = local.replace(tzinfo=ZoneInfo(tz), fold=fold)
        center = pd.Timestamp(aware).value // 1_000_000  # Unix ms
        lo, hi = center + qlo - ohi, center + qhi - olo
        if row.source == "MOBILE-LON":
            lo -= 300000  # occurrence = corrected entry time - delay in [0,5 min]
        alternatives.add((lo, hi))
    return sorted(alternatives)

# Each branch is a system of constraints t[v] - t[u] <= d[u,v].
# Z is a fixed zero (Unix epoch). Shortest paths give tight projected bounds.
Z = "epoch"
def solve(group):
    case = group.iloc[0]["case"]
    obs = {r.activity: normalize(r) for r in group.itertuples()}
    nodes = [Z, *obs]
    if case == "T-404":
        nodes.append(I)
    if case == "T-407":
        nodes.append(A)
    branches = []
    for choice in product(*obs.values()):
        d = {(u, v): (0 if u == v else inf) for u in nodes for v in nodes}
        def bound(u, v, lo, hi):
            d[u, v] = min(d[u, v], hi)
            d[v, u] = min(d[v, u], -lo)
        for act, (lo, hi) in zip(obs, choice):
            bound(Z, act, lo, hi)
        if case == "T-404":
            bound(I, F, 18 * 60000, 25 * 60000)
        if case == "T-407":
            bound(S, A, 0, 20 * 60000)
        for u, v in (AI, IC, CK):
            if u in nodes and v in nodes:
                bound(u, v, 0, inf)
        for k in nodes:
            for u in nodes:
                for v in nodes:
                    d[u, v] = min(d[u, v], d[u, k] + d[k, v])
        if all(d[u, u] >= 0 for u in nodes):
            branches.append(d)
    return nodes[1:], branches

def union(intervals):
    result = []
    for lo, hi in sorted(intervals):
        if result and lo <= result[-1][1]:
            result[-1] = (result[-1][0], max(result[-1][1], hi))
        else:
            result.append((lo, hi))
    return result

def durations(branches, pair):  # ms -> seconds
    u, v = pair
    return union([(-d[v, u] / 1000, d[u, v] / 1000) for d in branches])

def classify(intervals, low, high):
    if not intervals or any(abs(x) == inf for iv in intervals for x in iv):
        return "INDETERMINATE"
    if all(low <= lo and hi <= high for lo, hi in intervals):
        return "CERTAIN_PASS"
    if all(hi < low or lo > high for lo, hi in intervals):
        return "CERTAIN_VIOLATION"
    return "POSSIBLE_VIOLATION"

models = {case: solve(group) for case, group in raw.groupby("case")}
results, midrows = [], []
for case, (acts, branches) in models.items():
    for rule, (pair, limit) in sla.items():
        if set(pair) <= set(acts):
            iv = durations(branches, pair)
            for extra in (0, 2, 5):
                results.append((case, rule, extra, iv,
                                classify(iv, 0, limit + 60 * extra)))
    for pair, (mu, sd) in profile.items():
        if set(pair) <= set(acts):
            iv = durations(branches, pair)
            for z in (1, 2, 3):
                results.append((case, pair, z, iv,
                                classify(iv, mu - z * sd, mu + z * sd)))
    if branches:
        # Choose one feasible fold branch. The average of its earliest and
        # latest feasible schedules also respects every difference constraint.
        d = branches[0]
        for act in acts:
            t_ns = (d[Z, act] - d[act, Z]) * 500_000
            midrows.append((case, act, pd.Timestamp(t_ns, unit="ns", tz="UTC")))

report = pd.DataFrame(results, columns=["case", "rule", "setting", "seconds", "status"])
print(report.to_string(index=False))
mid = pd.DataFrame(midrows, columns=["case", "activity", "midpoint"])
print("LOSSY MIDPOINT APPROXIMATION, conditional on the selected evidence model")
# A non-date-like trace ID prevents date inference on strings such as T-401.
pm_input = mid.assign(case="incident/" + mid["case"])
formatted = pm4py.format_dataframe(pm_input, case_id="case",
                                   activity_key="activity", timestamp_key="midpoint")
log = pm4py.convert_to_event_log(formatted)

def flagged_pairs(diag, log):
    # Standard EventLog result, in trace order. Each deviation starts with
    # (source activity, target activity). Ignore version-specific trailing fields.
    if not isinstance(diag, (list, tuple)) or len(diag) != len(log):
        raise ValueError("Unsupported PM4Py diagnostic container")
    flags = set()
    for trace, deviations in zip(log, diag):
        for item in deviations:
            if not isinstance(item, (tuple, list)) or len(item) < 2:
                raise ValueError("Unsupported PM4Py deviation record")
            case = trace.attributes["concept:name"].removeprefix("incident/")
            flags.add((case, (item[0], item[1])))
    return flags

for z in (1, 2, 3):
    diag = pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile(log, profile, zeta=z)
    try:
        flags = flagged_pairs(diag, log)
    except ValueError as exc:
        print(exc, "Raw PM4Py result:", diag)
        flags = None
    for case, group in mid.groupby("case"):
        times = group.set_index("activity")["midpoint"]
        for pair, (mu, sd) in profile.items():
            u, v = pair
            if u not in times or v not in times:
                continue
            dt = (times[v] - times[u]).value / 1e9
            point = "pass" if mu - z * sd <= dt <= mu + z * sd else "deviation"
            interval = classify(durations(models[case][1], pair), mu - z * sd, mu + z * sd)
            pm = ("unmapped" if flags is None else
                  "deviation" if (case, pair) in flags else "pass")
            print(case, pair, z, "midpoint seconds", dt, "point", point,
                  "PM4Py", pm, "interval", interval)
```

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency

For a maximum L, evidence of duration ≤ L suffices for a certain pass, and evidence of duration > L suffices for a certain breach. For a band [l, h], a pass needs both bounds inside the band. A deviation needs every remaining component outside it, on either side. The extra evidence can be one reliable inequality if the existing evidence already resolves the other boundary. More precision alone may still leave the decision boundary inside the interval.

The table covers mixed findings at any of the tested settings. Thresholds are in seconds. Where several thresholds appear, the evidence need only resolve the one for the setting being adjudicated.

## 7. Governance Recommendation

- **Store occurrence and observation separately.** Store UTC instants or bounded UTC intervals for occurrence, entry and ingestion in separate fields. Retain the original timestamp string, source system and device, IANA zone, numeric UTC offset, DST fold and timezone database version. Preserve alternative UTC intervals whenever a fold cannot be verified.
- **Keep calibration with the event.** Record signed clock offset, measurement method, last synchronization, validity window, drift bound and outages. Retain the clock identity and evidence of shared offsets so elapsed-time calculations can cancel common error where justified. Record timestamp resolution, flooring or rounding rule, tie handling and measurement error.
- **Make lifecycle and causality auditable.** Give alert send, receiver acknowledgment, investigation start, investigation completion, containment completion and closure separate event types. Link them using incident, message, acknowledgment and work-item identifiers. Record monotonic elapsed duration where possible, including whether pauses count. On mobile forms label the time being requested and capture submission time independently.
- **Preserve provenance.** Mark each time as observed, user-entered or inferred. For inferences, retain the input evidence, bounds, correlation and ordering assumptions, derivation version and adjudication history. Label engineering estimates separately from measured clock bounds and flag gaps in calibration.
- **Set the enforcement standard in advance.** Adopt the contractual SLA and any authorized grace period explicitly. Enforce a breach only when every feasible duration exceeds the deadline on evidence accepted for enforcement. Route overlap to review, preserve indeterminate cases as evidence gaps, and record a certain pass when every feasible duration fits. Publish the statistical profile and ζ separately, use deviations to investigate process behavior, and keep midpoint-only results out of breach counts.
