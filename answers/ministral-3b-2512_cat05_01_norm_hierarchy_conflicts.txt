### **1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method**
**Authority Treatment:**
- **Global normative sources (GPIP-4) are primary** unless explicitly overridden by a higher-level directive (e.g., ECD-2) or a formal exception (A7). Local SOP (B) is secondary but may impose stricter controls where not explicitly waived.
- **Later-in-time rules apply only if they are explicitly tied to the act’s timing** (e.g., a 24-hour record deadline). Post-hoc approvals (e.g., Finance_Approve after Commit_to_Supplier) do not cure pre-commitment failures unless explicitly permitted (e.g., A6).
- **Emergency necessity** is assessed by whether the act was *necessary to prevent imminent harm* (B4) and whether the directive (ECD-2) explicitly permits deviations. Verbal authorizations must be recorded within a timeframe (C3, D1) or be supported by contemporaneous evidence (e.g., telephone recordings).
- **Evidential discipline:**
  - **Contemporaneous evidence** (telephone recordings, signed PDFs, access logs) takes precedence over ERP timestamps, which may be delayed by system outages.
  - **Missing evidence** (e.g., no recorded authorization for P-503) is fatal unless a credible alternative justification exists (e.g., a contemporaneous written directive).
  - **Scope of exceptions:** A7 exceptions require written, scoped, time-limited approval by the Chief Risk Officer. COO directives (e.g., ECD-2) are not A7 unless explicitly stated (NORTECH-FACT).

**Assumptions:**
- **GPIP-4’s “local procedures may impose stricter controls” (A5) implies local SOP (B) is binding unless explicitly waived** (e.g., by ECD-2).
- **The “critical sterilization-line lots” in ECD-2 (C6) is interpreted as parts needed within 24 hours** (Internal Audit’s position), not just backup-line components.
- **Finance approval (A2) is a budget owner + Finance Controller** unless waived by Finance controls (D3) or ECD-2 (D1).
- **Compliance_Clearance (A3) is mandatory for new suppliers unless waived by ECD-2 (C5) or a formal exception.**

**Pseudo-Logic:**
1. Normally, `Commit_to_Supplier` requires:
   - Pre-commitment `Create_PO` (B1) unless ECD-2 overrides (C1).
   - For purchases  EUR 50,000: `Finance_Approve` (R1) unless waived by ECD-2 (D1) or Finance controls (D3).
   - For purchases  EUR 100,000 or new suppliers: `Compliance_Clearance` (R2) unless waived by ECD-2 (C5) or A7.
   - For Aachen site purchases  EUR 25,000: `Plant_Approve` (B2) unless waived by ECD-2 (D1) or local emergency SOP (B4).
   - Segregation of duties (A1) applies unless explicitly waived by ECD-2 (no mention) or A7.
   - Supplier must be in approved register unless ECD-2 overrides (C2).
2. In an emergency (B4), `Commit_to_Supplier` may precede `Create_PO` if:
   - Imminent harm is confirmed (e.g., line stoppage within 6 hours).
   - Authorized by two Incident Command members (C3, D1) with contemporaneous recording.
   - ECD-2 explicitly permits deviations (C4).
3. Post-commitment approvals (e.g., Finance_Approve after Commit_to_Supplier) do not cure pre-commitment failures unless explicitly permitted (A6).
4. If ECD-2 does not explicitly waive a control (e.g., Finance approval for P-503), the default rule (GPIP-4) applies.
5. Contemporaneous evidence (e.g., telephone recordings) overrides ERP timestamps if the system was out of service.
6. A CFO email (D) is binding only if it explicitly overrides ECD-2 (D1) or is supported by Finance controls (D3).

### **7. Recommended Policy Clarification**

**Revised Exception Clause (Replaces A7 and ECD-2 Scope):**

> **"EMERGENCY PURCHASE EXCEPTION PROCEDURE"**
> 1. **Scope:** During an active incident directive (e.g., ECD-2), emergency purchases for sterilization-line criticality may deviate from GPIP-4 controls if:
>    - The purchase is necessary to prevent imminent harm (6 hours) and is explicitly authorized by two Incident Command members.
>    - The deviation is documented in a contemporaneous signed directive (telephone recording or email) and recorded within 4 hours.
> 2. **Waived Controls:**
>    - **Supplier registration:** Only if the supplier is critical to the incident resolution (defined by Operations/Internal Audit).
>    - **Compliance_Clearance:** Only if the supplier is pre-approved by Compliance for the incident (not new suppliers).
>    - **Finance approval:** Only if the CFO or Finance team explicitly waives it in writing or via a contemporaneous directive (up to EUR 150k; EUR 150k–250k requires Legal_Clearance).
>    - **Segregation of duties:** Waived only if explicitly delegated by Incident Command in writing.
> 3. **Retroactive Records:**
>    - All deviations must be documented in a controlled repository within 72 hours of the incident.
>    - Pre-commitment approvals are required unless waived as above.
> 4. **Expiry:** The exception applies only during the incident directive window and is automatically revoked if the directive expires or is terminated.
> 5. **Safeguard:** The Chief Risk Officer must approve all deviations for purchases > EUR 250k or involving new suppliers, unless explicitly waived by the CFO.

**Additional Safeguards:**
- **Thresholds:** Define “critical sterilization-line lots” with clear operational/financial thresholds.
- **Evidence:** Require telephone recordings for verbal authorizations and signed PDFs for written directives.
- **Audit Trail:** Maintain a log of all emergency deviations with timestamps, actors, and justification.

This replaces the ambiguity in ECD-2 and A7 while preserving accountability.
