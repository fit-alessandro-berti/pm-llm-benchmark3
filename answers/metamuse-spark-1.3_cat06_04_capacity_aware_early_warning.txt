## 1. Decision rule

**Deployable rule — filter then prioritize, no pure p-threshold:**

**A. Hard eligibility gate — all must hold for a swarm now:**
1. **Stage eligible:** Current stage < `Diagnosis Complete`. Past it effect 0. I-03 fails here regardless of p.
2. **Time feasible:** Time-to-deadline > swarm duration (0.75h) + minimum downstream buffer for Resolution + Validation. Rule: require 2.0h unless fast-track alternative is documented. Cases <2.0h go to non-swarm escalation.
3. **Evidence credibility:** Live features complete or human-verified. If key field imputed/missing (I-06), no auto-swarm; route to 30-min manual verification first.
4. **Repeat rule:** No second alert-driven swarm consideration within cool-down unless a materially new event occurred (new log, stage transition, SLA reset, queue regime change, customer signal). Model refresh alone is not new evidence.

**B. Alert persistence / minimum evidence change:**
- First alert: requires human-readable trigger: large jump (e.g., p 0.10) OR high adjusted risk with corroborating case facts, AND conservative expected benefit >0 under lower-bound effect.
- Second/third alert with no prior swarm: requires either p 0.05 since last reviewed alert OR new artifact/stage change. Otherwise suppress paging, keep silent monitoring.
- After a swarm: normally forbidden to re-swarm same case. Re-entry only if new material event + fresh expected-benefit case + senior approval.

**C. Calibration robustification — do not use raw p_now:**
- SME: true risk  p_now + 0.04 to 0.09. Add +0.065 for triage display, but decide on lower-bound benefit. Do not penalize SME for historically under-measured risk.
- Strategic Enterprise: true risk  p_now - 0.05 to 0.12 after redesign. Subtract ~0.085 for display. Require larger  or higher value/effect to act; demand second corroboration for early-horizon cases.
- Public Sector: use p_now as-is.
- Non-Native-Language Support: p_now and effect range both low-credibility. Do not auto-exclude or auto-prioritize. Require human review of language/support notes, use wide uncertainty interval, and flag for learning.
- Imputed (I-06): treat p as suspect until verified. Discount effect by ~50% in prioritization until fix.

p is observational, not P(breach | swarm). Decision uses: conservative net = lower-bound absolute reduction × Value  €450  expected false-alarm exposure, plus urgency/feasibility and spillover. False-alarm burden is probabilistic:  (1  p_true) × burden, not deterministic. Supplied formula overstates cost for high-risk cases and understates it for low-risk early cases.

**D. Prioritization among eligible:**
Rank by Tier = conservative direct net + urgency multiplier + fairness guardrail, minus spillover cost:
- Tier 1 - Act now: past or in diagnostic window with 2-4h to deadline, credible jump, robust lower-bound net >€800, feasible.
- Tier 2 - Active watch: high value but long horizon (8-12h) or overpredicted segment; monitor at 30-60 min, pre-assign re-entry trigger. Preserves option value and avoids premature congestion.
- Tier 3 - Conditional / alternative playbook: short-fuse (<2h), imputed, or low absolute saving; use non-swarm actions.
Enforce segment cap: no single segment takes all slots; reserve capacity for Public/SME learning. Spillover is a cost on every slot: 6 diagnosis-stage bystanders × 0.01-0.03 added breach risk per slot. A slot must beat that externality, not just €450.

Non-monetary obligations override: regulatory/safety, Public Sector continuity, and Strategic relationship risk are documented as constraints, not hidden in € Value.

## 4. Repeated alerts and temporal policy

**Cool-down:** After any alert that does not lead to swarm, suppress re-paging for 90 min in Triage, 45 min in Diagnosis Started, unless re-entry trigger fires. Log silently every 30-min refresh for model monitoring but do not reprompt specialists.

**Suppression:** Suppress if p <0.05 and no new artifact, stage change, or SLA regime change, and conservative net unchanged. I-05 is canonical suppress: 0.550.57 (+0.02), third alert, no new event. Continued paging would cause alert fatigue, displace attention, and risk a forbidden repeat swarm with no new treatability. Action: silent watch, review at next stage transition or 2h, allow specialist to pull if concerned, do not push.

**Escalation:** Escalate (human senior triage, not auto-swarm) if: 3+ suppressions with persistently high adjusted p (e.g., SME-adjusted >0.65), time-to-deadline crossing <4h, or bystander queue risk rising. Escalation is information-seeking, not automatic slot spend.

**Re-entry logic:** Re-entry resets cool-down only on materially new event: new customer log/file, failed validation, stage entry to Diagnosis Started, data-quality fix, sharp corroborated jump (0.10 with verified features). Requires documented what is new and why swarm is now more treatable. Re-swarm on same case additionally requires senior approval + statement that prior swarm did not occur or new biology of case differs.

**I-05 vs I-07 comparison:**
- I-05: repeated unchanged alert  suppress. No new event, tiny , Triage 5h still time to learn. Spending now violates repeat rule, wastes learning opportunity, creates precedent that refresh chatter earns capacity. Keep on watchlist; if it moves to Diagnosis Started with sustained SME-adjusted risk, it becomes Tier 1 candidate.
- I-07: repeated changed alert  re-entry permitted. New customer log is material; slightly lower p (0.750.72) does not negate new information — model may not yet encode log content. Correct to re-evaluate, reset cool-down, read log urgently. Decision here is still no swarm, but for different reason: feasibility (1.5h window) and low absolute Value, not suppression. Route to 15-min log review + direct resolution track + customer call, not 45-min swarm. If log reveals misdiagnosis that swarm could fix within window, override allowed with documented rationale.

This distinguishes evidence refresh from timer refresh, preventing both starvation by chatter and blindness to real news.

## 5. Fairness and feedback-loop risks

- **Value-based starvation:** Ranking by Value×reduction would always favor I-04 (€60k) / I-08 (€100k) Strategic cases over I-01 (€9k Public), I-07 (€5k SME), I-10 (€11k). Over time SME/Public queues breach more, eroding trust and contractual equity. Safeguard: two-lane allocation — at least 1 of 3 active slots reserved for non-Strategic or lower-value but high-effect cases when conservative net positive; cap Strategic share at 50% of daily swarms; publish selection rate by value quartile. Here picking I-01 over I-04 now operationalizes this: I-04 waits because it can, I-01 acts because window + reliability warrant it.
- **Non-Native-Language uncertainty:** I-09 has sparse validation; both p and effect are wide. Blanket exclusion would discriminate; blanket inclusion gambles scarce capacity. Safeguard: never auto-swarm or auto-deny on this flag alone; mandatory 10-min human + interpreter/support review of case notes before slot commit; track precision/recall and effect separately for this cohort; oversample for validation; do not use language as model feature without bias audit.
- **Differential calibration:** SME underpredict (0.04 to 0.09) means threshold on raw p systematically under-serves SMEs; Strategic overpredict does opposite. A single p-threshold is discriminatory. Safeguard: use segment-adjusted p for display and lower-bound net for decision; audit weekly calibration curves by segment; freeze auto-escalation for overpredicted segment unless -corroborated; add safety margin for underpredicted segment in watchlist (lower watch threshold for SME).
- **Training-label contamination:** Swarms that succeed prevent breaches, making intervened cases look like “no breach” negatives. Naive retraining learns “high-risk + swarm = safe” and downgrades future risk, punishing the policy’s success. Selective intervention also censors outcomes. Safeguard: log treatment indicator + propensity (why selected) immutably with prediction; train on untreated or IPW/debiased labels; maintain small randomized tie-breaker holdout (e.g., when two Tier-2 cases tie, randomize) to identify effects; never retrain on post-intervention labels without causal adjustment; version model and policy together.
- **Spillover falling on untreated groups:** Each slot adds 0.01-0.03 breach risk to six diagnosis-stage bystanders — likely the very cases denied slots, often lower-value/SME that share the queue while specialists are interrupted. Harm is invisible in per-case EV. Safeguard: measure and attribute spillover: track diagnosis queue length, wait, and breach rate for non-intervened cases in 2h post-swarm window; report spillover incidence by segment/value; include expected spillover cost in slot approval; if congestion high, raise bar to lower-bound net >€2,000 and enforce reserve; compensate by protecting diagnosis capacity (time-box swarms, backfill specialist).

## 6. Monitoring and recalibration plan

**Operational:** Slot use vs 4/day, reserve breaches, swarm start/end adherence to 45 min, specialist interruption logs, time-to-deadline at intervention, alternative actions for I-03/I-07 type cases, I-06 verification latency, alert volumes and suppression counts (I-05 pattern).

**Predictive:** Rolling calibration (predicted vs observed breach) overall and by SME/Strategic/Public/Non-Native, by stage, by imputed vs complete; precision@alert, alert  distribution, time-to-breach discrimination; flag if SME residual >0.05 or Strategic bias >0.05, or Non-Native CI width doubles. Detect policy-induced drift: compare calibration on untreated vs treated, pre- vs post-policy, and holdout randomized cases.

**Causal-effect:** Cannot learn effect from observational p. Estimate via: (a) logged propensity + IPW / doubly-robust comparison of swarmed vs similar non-swarmed; (b) regression discontinuity around cool-down/eligibility cutoffs; (c) small prospective randomization for Tier-2 ties and for reserve-release decisions; (d) before-after queue delay measurement per slot (8-20 min audit). Update effect ranges quarterly, widen — not narrow — intervals when Non-Native or imputed share grows. Pre-register that ranges are priors, not truths.

**Capacity / spillover:** Real-time diagnosis queue depth, aggregate delay per slot, bystander breach rate 0-4h post-swarm vs matched no-swarm periods; estimate spillover coefficient (target: validate 0.01-0.03). If observed spillover >0.03 or delay >20 min/slot for 3 consecutive days, auto-tighten: require Tier-1 only, keep 2 slots in reserve, shorten swarm to 30-min triage variant.

**Fairness:** Selection rate, swarm rate, breach-averted proxy, false-alarm burden incidence, and spillover exposure by segment, value quartile, language-support flag. Alert if Strategic share >60% of swarms over 2 weeks or Non-Native precision < overall 10 pts or SME breach rate rises while swarm rate flat.

**When to change threshold/allocation:** Monthly policy review + ad-hoc trigger: recalibrate segment adjustments if bias shifts 0.03; raise conservative-net bar if base net after spillover 0 for two weeks; lower bar / release reserve if queue slack (spillover <0.01, delay <8 min) and regulatory risk passes; retire swarm for a segment if lower-bound effect persistently ~0 (as for post-Diagnosis Complete). All changes versioned with rationale, fairness impact note, and sunset for re-evaluation.
