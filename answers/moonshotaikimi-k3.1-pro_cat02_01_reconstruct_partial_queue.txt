# 1. Assumptions and notation

All durations below are in minutes. Let \(T_0=\) 08:00 and \(T_1=\) 14:00, a 360-minute window.

- **A1 — Queue entries and population.** `arrival_s1` is treated as the S1 queue-entry time, and S1 completion is the instantaneous S2 queue-entry time. The table is assumed to contain all cases and resource work affecting the process in the window, with no pre-08:00 WIP. Otherwise, the reconstructed WIP and busy time would be lower bounds for the listed workload only.
- **A2 — Simultaneous reported events.** At equal reported timestamps, process completions/resource releases first, then the associated S1-to-S2 transfer, then service starts, and finally new external arrivals. Snapshots are taken after all events at that timestamp. This avoids artificial resource conflicts, such as one C1 activity ending and another starting at 11:00. Zero-duration ordering effects do not affect time-averaged WIP.
- **A3 — Rounding.** Calculations initially use the reported timestamps as if exact. An actual timestamp lies approximately within \(\pm2.5\) minutes; consequently, a duration calculated from two rounded timestamps has a worst-case error of approximately \(\pm5\) minutes, before applying ordering and nonnegativity constraints. The numerical ranges below are primarily **missing-start feasibility bounds**, not statistical confidence intervals.
- **A4 — Capacity and service.** Resources are continuously available except for the stated C2 outage, and service is continuous and nonpreemptive on the recorded resource. S1 therefore has 720 available resource-minutes; S2 has \(2(360)-15=705\). Utilization against available capacity is reported; calendar-capacity utilization is also noted where useful.
- **A5 — Missing-start bounds.** For a missing start, the feasible service interval must lie after queue entry, avoid other work on the same resource, respect the outage where relevant, and end at the known completion. The central imputations are hypotheses, not observations:
  - P07 S1: start 09:30;
  - P15 S1: start 11:45;
  - P05 S2: start 09:50;
  - P11 S2: start 12:05.
  
  Each is five minutes after the preceding job on that resource and produces a service duration consistent with the visible scheduling pattern. Earliest/latest feasible starts are used separately as bounds.
- **A6 — Censoring.** OPEN work is truncated at 14:00 for busy-time calculations. Elapsed service is not final service. A case still waiting at 14:00 has an observed waiting time and a longer, unknown final wait. No final service or final sojourn is imputed for these cases.
- **A7 — Subperiods.** For rates, E = [08:00,10:00), M = [10:00,12:00), and L = [12:00,14:00). Counts at period boundaries therefore follow the reported timestamps; rounding can move a boundary event between periods.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

For case \(i\), define:

- **S1 waiting:** \(q_{1i}=s1\_start_i-arrival\_s1_i\).
- **S1 service:** \(s_{1i}=s1\_end_i-s1\_start_i\).
- **S1 station sojourn:** \(r_{1i}=s1\_end_i-arrival\_s1_i=q_{1i}+s_{1i}\) for completed S1 activities.
- **S2 waiting:** \(q_{2i}=s2\_start_i-s1\_end_i\).
- **S2 service:** \(s_{2i}=s2\_end_i-s2\_start_i\).
- **S2 station sojourn:** \(r_{2i}=s2\_end_i-s1\_end_i=q_{2i}+s_{2i}\).
- **Completed-case total sojourn:** \(r_i=s2\_end_i-arrival\_s1_i=r_{1i}+r_{2i}\).

Thus, a missing start changes the estimated waiting/service split but **does not change completed station sojourn or completed-case total sojourn**.

For OPEN service, replace the unknown completion by \(T_1\) only to obtain **elapsed service in the window**. For a case still waiting, use \(T_1-entry\) as observed waiting and retain right censoring. A blank S2 start for P16/P18/P19/P20 is waiting; for P21/P22, S2 has not yet been entered.

For each station, at time \(t\):

- \(Q(t)\): cases entered but not started;
- \(A(t)\): cases in active service;
- \(L(t)=Q(t)+A(t)\): station WIP.

Total system WIP is S1 WIP plus S2 WIP. Time-averaged WIP is

\[
\bar L=\frac{1}{360}\int_{T_0}^{T_1}L(t)\,dt,
\]

and is equivalently obtained by summing each case’s time spent at that station within the window. Busy time \(B\) is the sum of active resource-service minutes, including OPEN service up to 14:00. Utilization is \(U=B/\text{available resource-minutes}\), not completions divided by nominal capacity.

The three missing-start scenarios are:

1. **Early feasible starts:** minimum waiting and maximum service/busy time.
2. **Central pattern:** the four starts in A5.
3. **Latest feasible starts:** maximum waiting and minimum service/busy time. Zero-duration services at this endpoint are mathematical bounds, not plausible assertions that repair work took no time.

Arrival and departure rates are event counts divided by the two-hour subperiod length. Little’s Law, \(\bar L=\lambda W\), is used only for a comparatively balanced active-service subsystem; it is not applied indiscriminately to the nonstationary queues.

| P21 | 100 | 30 elapsed; final >30 | 130 so far | not entered | not entered | not entered | S1 OPEN | Still in S1 service at 14:00; 130 minutes in system |
| P22 | 110 observed; final >110 | not started | 110 so far | not entered | not entered | not entered | S1 wait right-censored | Waiting for S1 since 12:10 |

For the 14 completed cases, total sojourn has mean approximately **146.4 minutes**, median **145 minutes**, and range **60–230 minutes**. This is a completed-cohort summary, not an estimate for all 22 arrivals. Notably, P15 has already reached 230 minutes and remains incomplete.

The feasibility constraints produce especially wide individual uncertainty: for example, P15’s S1 wait can range from 90 to 125 minutes while its service ranges conversely from 35 to 0, but their sum remains the observed 125-minute S1 sojourn.

Flow conservation provides an exact reported-status check:

\[
22\text{ arrivals}=14\text{ completed cases}+8\text{ cases still in system}.
\]

At S1, \(22-20=2\) cases remain; at S2, \(20-14=6\) remain.

**Little’s-Law check.** The least objectionable interval is the S1 **active-service subsystem** during approximately 10:00–12:00, where central starts and completions are balanced and WIP is much more stable than the queue’s waiting times.

- Seven S1 completions give \(\lambda=7/2=3.5\) cases/hour.
- Their mean active service time is \(200/7\approx28.6\) minutes.
- Hence \(\lambda W_{\text{service}}\approx3.5(28.6/60)=1.67\) busy resources.
- The sample-path value is central \(205/120=1.71\), with a missing-start range of approximately \(190/120=1.58\) to \(210/120=1.75\).

The central difference is only about 0.04 resource, and the Little’s-Law value lies inside the imputation range. This supports internal consistency of the active-service reconstruction; it does not validate a station-sojourn Little’s-Law calculation. Such a calculation is not defensible for the full morning because arrivals, waiting times, and WIP are strongly time-varying and several cases are right-censored.

# 5. Bottleneck and temporal interpretation

**Evidence**

- **Early, roughly 08:00–10:00, S1 is the stronger constraint.** Thirteen external arrivals enter in two hours, while only six S1 services complete. S1 WIP and its queue build rapidly. S2 completes only three cases and has much lower early WIP and utilization, partly because it is downstream of a process that cannot yet supply it faster.
- **During 10:00–12:00, both stations are loaded.** S1 receives 8 arrivals and completes 7 services in the reported bins; its WIP remains around 7–8. S2 receives 7 entries but completes only 5 services. Central available-capacity utilization is about 85% at S1 and 87% at S2. It is therefore more consistent with a **time-varying combination** than with one exclusively dominant station.
- **Late, S2 is the current exit constraint.** Only one new external arrival occurs after 12:00. S1 WIP drains from 7 at 12:00 to 2 at 14:00, whereas S2 WIP peaks at 7 and ends at 6. At 14:00 both S2 resources are occupied and four cases are waiting for S2.
- S1 still has a residual problem: P22 remains waiting, and reported I2 capacity is idle after 13:45. That is inconsistent with a simple, work-conserving two-resource queue and could reflect scheduling, unrecorded unavailability, or logging behavior.
- Among the 14 completed cases, cumulative station-sojourn time is 1,070 minutes at S1 and 980 at S2. Thus, even the completed-cohort delay decomposition does not support attributing nearly all delay to one station.

**Hypotheses and interpretation**

- If “bottleneck” means the largest accumulated queue/delay over the full window, S1 has the stronger claim: higher mean WIP and very long waits developed there.
- If it means the constraint visible at the end of the window, S2 has the stronger claim.
- S2’s longer observed service times suggest lower potential capacity when heavily loaded, but service duration alone is not proof of a bottleneck.
- The few-point difference between full-window S1 and S2 utilization is not robust to rounding and imputation. The temporal WIP and flow evidence is more persuasive than that comparison.

**Maintenance interval**

C2’s 15-minute outage removes 15 resource-minutes and temporarily halves S2’s stated resource count from two to one. P08 had entered S2 at 10:20; C2 finished P06 at 10:50, and P08 did not start until 11:15. A hypothesis is that a five-minute-gap schedule would otherwise have started P08 around 10:55, making the observed start approximately 20 minutes later than that counterfactual.

That is not an identified causal estimate: the actual schedule without maintenance is unknown. The outage plausibly aggravated the mid-morning S2 queue, but it cannot explain the entire backlog—S2 entries also exceeded completions, service times were substantial, and S1 had already created a large upstream backlog.

# 6. Missingness, censoring, and sensitivity analysis

The following scenarios change only the four missing starts. All include OPEN elapsed work in busy time and retain waiting cases as censored.

| scenario | missing-start rule | S1 mean wait, started cases | S2 mean wait, completed services | S1 busy / utilization | S2 busy / utilization | mean S1 queue | mean S2 queue |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Early feasible starts | P07 09:25; P15 11:40; P05 09:45; P11 12:00 | 65.2 | 31.8 | 605 / 84.0% | 570 / 80.9% | 4.11 | 2.14 |
| Central pattern | P07 09:30; P15 11:45; P05 09:50; P11 12:05 | 65.7 | 32.5 | 595 / 82.6% | 560 / 79.4% | 4.14 | 2.17 |
| Latest feasible starts | P07 10:00; P15 12:15; P05 10:15; P11 12:25 | 68.6 | 35.7 | 535 / 74.3% | 515 / 73.0% | 4.31 | 2.29 |

The early-start scenario is optimistic about queueing but pessimistic about service effort; the latest-start scenario does the reverse. They should not be interpreted as equally probable statistical scenarios.

**Conclusions stable across these scenarios**

- Completed station sojourns and completed-case total sojourns are unchanged.
- Mean and peak station WIP, total WIP, ending WIP, and the reported event-flow rates are unchanged.
- The waiting/service split for a missing-start case can change substantially even while its station sojourn remains fixed.
- Both stations are highly loaded in the middle of the morning; S1 dominates early backlog formation, while S2 holds the larger terminal backlog.
- Utilization cannot be estimated properly from completed services alone: OPEN activities contribute 30 S1 resource-minutes and 35 S2 resource-minutes within the window.

**Conclusions that are not stable or are not identified**

- Individual missing-case waits and services, completed-service means, and precise utilization rankings are scenario-dependent.
- Exact five-minute waits, short service durations, subperiod boundary counts, and the central five-minute-gap pattern are sensitive to rounding. A reported duration has a worst-case approximately \(\pm5\)-minute endpoint-rounding error.
- The central utilization percentages should not be interpreted as precise to a decimal place. Systematic rounding could also move busy time coherently rather than independently.
- P15 and P17 have final S2 service times exceeding their reported 30 and 5 elapsed minutes; P21’s final S1 service exceeds its 30 elapsed minutes. Their final values are unbounded above using only the supplied evidence.
- Final waits for P16/P18/P19/P20/P22 exceed their observed 85/50/35/15/110 minutes. Consequently, the completed-case mean of 146.4 minutes is not a valid all-case mean and is particularly unrepresentative of later, still-open arrivals.
- A multiple-imputation extension could draw each missing start from its feasible gap, using observed service-duration and setup-gap distributions, reject resource conflicts, and recompute queue and busy-time metrics for every draw. Station WIP and completed sojourn would remain invariant.

# 7. Reproducible Python

```python
import io
import re
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

DAY = "2026-05-12"
T0 = pd.Timestamp(DAY + " 08:00")
T1 = pd.Timestamp(DAY + " 14:00")
WINDOW_MIN = (T1 - T0).total_seconds() / 60

RAW = """case,arrival_s1,s1_start,s1_end,s1_resource,s2_start,s2_end,s2_resource,state_at_14_00
P01,08:00,08:05,08:25,I1,08:30,09:00,C1,COMPLETE
P02,08:05,08:10,08:35,I2,08:40,09:20,C2,COMPLETE
P03,08:10,08:30,08:55,I1,09:05,09:45,C1,COMPLETE
P04,08:20,08:40,09:10,I2,09:25,10:05,C2,COMPLETE
P05,08:25,09:00,09:25,I1,MISSING,10:15,C1,COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P06,08:35,09:15,09:45,I2,10:10,10:50,C2,COMPLETE
P07,08:45,MISSING,10:00,I1,10:20,11:00,C1,COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P08,09:00,09:50,10:20,I2,11:15,12:00,C2,COMPLETE
P09,09:05,10:05,10:35,I1,11:00,11:40,C1,COMPLETE
P10,09:15,10:25,10:55,I2,11:45,12:10,C1,COMPLETE
P11,09:25,10:40,11:05,I1,MISSING,12:25,C2,COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P12,09:35,11:00,11:30,I2,12:15,13:00,C1,COMPLETE
P13,09:50,11:10,11:40,I1,12:30,13:20,C2,COMPLETE
P14,10:00,11:35,12:00,I2,13:05,13:50,C1,COMPLETE
P15,10:10,MISSING,12:15,I1,13:30,OPEN,C2,OPEN_IN_S2
P16,10:25,12:05,12:35,I2,,,,WAITING_FOR_S2
P17,10:45,12:20,12:50,I1,13:55,OPEN,C1,OPEN_IN_S2
P18,11:00,12:40,13:10,I2,,,,WAITING_FOR_S2
P19,11:15,12:55,13:25,I1,,,,WAITING_FOR_S2
P20,11:30,13:15,13:45,I2,,,,WAITING_FOR_S2
P21,11:50,13:30,OPEN,I1,,,,OPEN_IN_S1
P22,12:10,,,,,,,WAITING_FOR_S1
"""

df = pd.read_csv(io.StringIO(RAW), dtype=str, keep_default_na=False)
time_cols = ["arrival_s1", "s1_start", "s1_end", "s2_start", "s2_end"]
clock = re.compile(r"^\d{2}:\d{2}$")

# Preserve status explicitly: MISSING, OPEN, and BLANK are not interchangeable.
for c in time_cols:
    df[c + "_status"] = df[c].map(
        lambda x: x if x in {"MISSING", "OPEN"}
        else ("BLANK" if x == "" else "OBSERVED")
    )
    df[c + "_ts"] = df[c].map(
        lambda x: pd.Timestamp(f"{DAY} {x}") if clock.fullmatch(x) else pd.NaT
    )

print("STATUS-AWARE TIMESTAMP COVERAGE")
for c in time_cols:
    print(c, df[c + "_status"].value_counts().to_dict())

# PM4Py-compatible interval events: only genuinely usable closed intervals.
# Missing starts and OPEN activities remain in df for the pandas reconstruction,
# but are not falsely converted into completed PM4Py intervals.
interval_rows = []
for _, r in df.iterrows():
    for st, activity in [("s1", "Incoming Inspection"), ("s2", "Calibration")]:
        s, e = r[st + "_start_ts"], r[st + "_end_ts"]
        if pd.notna(s) and pd.notna(e):
            interval_rows.append({
                "case:concept:name": r["case"],
                "concept:name": activity,
                "start_timestamp": s,
                "time:timestamp": e,
                "org:resource": r[st + "_resource"]
            })

interval_df = pd.DataFrame(interval_rows)
interval_df = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    interval_df,
    case_id="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp"
)
print("\nUsable closed interval events:", len(interval_df))

def minutes(a, b):
    return (b - a).total_seconds() / 60

# Reported-time arrival/completion rates.
periods = [
    ("E_08_10", T0, T0 + pd.Timedelta(hours=2)),
    ("M_10_12", T0 + pd.Timedelta(hours=2), T0 + pd.Timedelta(hours=4)),
    ("L_12_14", T0 + pd.Timedelta(hours=4), T1)
]
rate_rows = []
for station, entry_col, exit_col in [
    ("S1", "arrival_s1_ts", "s1_end_ts"),
    ("S2", "s1_end_ts", "s2_end_ts")
]:
    for pname, lo, hi in periods:
        arrivals = int(((df[entry_col] >= lo) & (df[entry_col] < hi)).sum())
        completions = int(((df[exit_col] >= lo) & (df[exit_col] < hi)).sum())
        rate_rows.append({
            "station": station, "period": pname,
            "arrivals": arrivals, "completions": completions,
            "arrival_rate_per_h": arrivals / 2,
            "completion_rate_per_h": completions / 2
        })
print("\nSUBPERIOD FLOW")
print(pd.DataFrame(rate_rows).to_string(index=False))

# Completed-case total sojourn does not require either missing start.
completed = df[df["s2_end_ts"].notna()].copy()
completed["total_sojourn_min"] = completed.apply(
    lambda r: minutes(r["arrival_s1_ts"], r["s2_end_ts"]), axis=1
)
print("\nCOMPLETED-CASE SOJOURN")
print(completed[["case", "total_sojourn_min"]].to_string(index=False))
print(completed["total_sojourn_min"].agg(["count", "mean", "median", "min", "max"]))

# Event-driven WIP: exits sort before entries at equal reported timestamps.
# No service-start imputation is needed for station WIP.
def wip_stats(entries, exits):
    events = []
    for t in entries.dropna():
        if T0 <= t <= T1:
            events.append((t, 1))
    for t in exits.dropna():
        if T0 <= t <= T1:
            events.append((t, -1))
    events.sort(key=lambda z: (z[0], z[1]))

    wip, peak, area = 0, 0, 0.0
    last = T0
    for t, delta in events:
        area += wip * minutes(last, t)
        wip += delta
        peak = max(peak, wip)
        last = t
    area += wip * minutes(last, T1)
    return {"area_case_min": area, "mean_wip": area / WINDOW_MIN,
            "peak_wip": peak, "end_wip": wip}

wip_rows = []
for name, entries, exits in [
    ("S1", df["arrival_s1_ts"], df["s1_end_ts"]),
    ("S2", df["s1_end_ts"], df["s2_end_ts"]),
    ("System", df["arrival_s1_ts"], df["s2_end_ts"])
]:
    wip_rows.append({"series": name, **wip_stats(entries, exits)})
wip_table = pd.DataFrame(wip_rows)
print("\nWIP SAMPLE-PATH SUMMARY")
print(wip_table.round(3).to_string(index=False))

# Fully observed completed-service summaries; OPEN and MISSING are excluded here.
service_rows = []
for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
    mask = df[st + "_start_ts"].notna() & df[st + "_end_ts"].notna()
    durations = (df.loc[mask, st + "_end_ts"] -
                 df.loc[mask, st + "_start_ts"]).dt.total_seconds() / 60
    service_rows.append({
        "station": st.upper(), "n_fully_observed": len(durations),
        "mean": durations.mean(), "median": durations.median(),
        "min": durations.min(), "max": durations.max()
    })
print("\nFULLY OBSERVED COMPLETED SERVICES")
print(pd.DataFrame(service_rows).round(2).to_string(index=False))

# Direct station-specific pandas calculations are safer here than
# pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals: queue entry is station-specific, MISSING
# starts need constrained scenarios, OPEN service is right-censored, and equal
# rounded timestamps need the explicit ordering convention above.
def ts(x):
    return pd.Timestamp(f"{DAY} {x}")

scenarios = {
    "early": {
        ("P07", "s1"): ts("09:25"), ("P15", "s1"): ts("11:40"),
        ("P05", "s2"): ts("09:45"), ("P11", "s2"): ts("12:00")
    },
    "central": {
        ("P07", "s1"): ts("09:30"), ("P15", "s1"): ts("11:45"),
        ("P05", "s2"): ts("09:50"), ("P11", "s2"): ts("12:05")
    },
    "latest": {
        ("P07", "s1"): ts("10:00"), ("P15", "s1"): ts("12:15"),
        ("P05", "s2"): ts("10:15"), ("P11", "s2"): ts("12:25")
    }
}

def station_queue_busy(st, starts):
    """Queue area includes time still queued at 14:00; busy includes OPEN elapsed."""
    queue_area, busy = 0.0, 0.0
    started_waits, completed_s2_waits = [], []

    for i, r in df.iterrows():
        entry = r["arrival_s1_ts"] if st == "s1" else r["s1_end_ts"]
        if pd.isna(entry):
            continue

        start = starts.iloc[i]
        if pd.notna(start):
            wait = minutes(entry, start)
            queue_area += wait
            started_waits.append(wait)

            end = r[st + "_end_ts"]
            if pd.isna(end) and r[st + "_end"] == "OPEN":
                end = T1  # truncation only, not an imputed completion
            if pd.notna(end):
                busy += max(0.0, minutes(max(start, T0), min(end, T1)))

            if st == "s2" and pd.notna(r["s2_end_ts"]):
                completed_s2_waits.append(wait)
        else:
            # A genuinely unstarted, still-waiting case. All four MISSING starts
            # have already been replaced by scenario-specific feasible values.
            queue_area += max(0.0, minutes(entry, T1))

    return queue_area, busy, started_waits, completed_s2_waits

scenario_rows = []
for scenario_name, overrides in scenarios.items():
    starts = {
        "s1": df["s1_start_ts"].copy(),
        "s2": df["s2_start_ts"].copy()
    }
    for (case, st), value in overrides.items():
        idx = df.index[df["case"].eq(case)][0]
        starts[st].iloc[idx] = value

    q1, b1, w1, _ = station_queue_busy("s1", starts["s1"])
    q2, b2, _, w2done = station_queue_busy("s2", starts["s2"])

    # Waiting time + active service must reconstruct total station WIP-time.
    assert np.isclose(q1 + b1, wip_table.loc[wip_table.series.eq("S1"),
                                                   "area_case_min"].iloc[0])
    assert np.isclose(q2 + b2, wip_table.loc[wip_table.series.eq("S2"),
                                                   "area_case_min"].iloc[0])

    scenario_rows.append({
        "scenario": scenario_name,
        "S1_started_wait_mean": np.mean(w1),
        "S2_completed_wait_mean": np.mean(w2done),
        "S1_mean_queue": q1 / WINDOW_MIN,
        "S2_mean_queue": q2 / WINDOW_MIN,
        "S1_busy": b1, "S1_util_available": b1 / 720,
        "S2_busy": b2, "S2_util_available": b2 / 705
    })

print("\nMISSING-START UNCERTAINTY SCENARIOS")
print(pd.DataFrame(scenario_rows).round(4).to_string(index=False))

# PM4Py case-level enrichment is demonstrated only on fully observed completed
# cases. A zero-duration arrival marker makes pre-S1 waiting part of the CASE.
# For these cases, whole-case service = S1 service + S2 service and whole-case
# waiting = total case sojourn - active service. These are NOT station metrics.
fully_observed = df[
    df["arrival_s1_ts"].notna() &
    df["s1_start_ts"].notna() & df["s1_end_ts"].notna() &
    df["s2_start_ts"].notna() & df["s2_end_ts"].notna()
]

projection_rows = []
for _, r in fully_observed.iterrows():
    projection_rows.append({
        "case:concept:name": r["case"], "concept:name": "Case arrival",
        "start_timestamp": r["arrival_s1_ts"],
        "time:timestamp": r["arrival_s1_ts"], "org:resource": "EXTERNAL"
    })
    for st, activity in [("s1", "Incoming Inspection"), ("s2", "Calibration")]:
        projection_rows.append({
            "case:concept:name": r["case"], "concept:name": activity,
            "start_timestamp": r[st + "_start_ts"],
            "time:timestamp": r[st + "_end_ts"],
            "org:resource": r[st + "_resource"]
        })

case_projection = pd.DataFrame(projection_rows)
case_projection = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    case_projection,
    case_id="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp"
)

case_level = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(case_projection.copy())
case_level = pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate(case_level)
if isinstance(case_level, tuple):
    case_level = case_level[0]
if not isinstance(case_level, pd.DataFrame):
    case_level = pm4py.convert_to_dataframe(case_level)

new_case_fields = [c for c in case_level.columns if c not in case_projection.columns]
show = ["case:concept:name"] + new_case_fields
print("\nCASE-LEVEL PM4PY OUTPUT -- NOT STATION-LEVEL QUEUE/SERVICE METRICS")
print(case_level[show].drop_duplicates().head(8).to_string(index=False))
print("Projection check for P01: whole-case active service = 50 min, "
      "whole-case waiting = 10 min, total sojourn = 60 min.")
```
