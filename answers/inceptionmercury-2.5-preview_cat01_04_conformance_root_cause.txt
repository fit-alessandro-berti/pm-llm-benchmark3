## 7. Provisional conclusion

The primary explanation for the measured conformance collapse is the accumulation of data artifacts. The UI release changed activity labels from `Classify` to `Risk Screen` without updating the model, while ETL changes replaced source timestamps with warehouse ingestion times. These two issues independently distorted the log, causing alignment fitness to drop by approximately 50% relative to the pre-release period. However, stable appeal rates strongly suggest the process itself did not deteriorate significantly.

Separately, a real compliance problem may remain. The "Enhanced Review" path occasionally lacks a recorded "Compliance Check" or appears without the required "Supervisor Sign-off" for high-value claims. While these gaps could be missing events, the presence of "Manual Override" activity and the new high-value policy indicate potential noncompliance that warrants investigation.

My confidence in the data artifact explanation is High because alignment improvements were immediate upon fixing labels and ordering. My confidence in a real compliance breach is Medium; outcomes are stable but specific traces suggest risk.

Immediate actions:
1. IT: Restore source-sequence timestamps and stop recording technical events like `Queue Routed` in the audit log.
2. Process Owners: Update the normative model to include the mandatory `Supervisor Sign-off` activity.
3. Compliance: Launch a targeted manual audit on enhanced review cases with missing checks.

Do not claim that employees are noncompliant until manual audits confirm that the missing steps were intentional bypasses rather than logging errors.

## 8. Model-governance recommendation

1. Model versioning must be locked to specific release dates, requiring updates whenever UI labels change.
2. Event-semantic contracts must be signed; technical events should be filtered before conformance checks.
3. Timestamp provenance must be standardized; warehouse ingestion time is unacceptable for process discovery.
4. Data-aware constraints must be implemented; model checks cannot rely solely on control flow if attributes are involved.
5. Post-release validation must include automated alignment checks on a pilot group before full conformance reporting.
6. Technical events must be tagged and automatically excluded from fitness calculations unless verified as business activities.
