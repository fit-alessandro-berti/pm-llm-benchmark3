## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Common Initial Conditions (All Scenarios)
- **Reference Start:** Friday 18 September 2026, 15:30 ($t = 0.0\text{ h}$).
- **Chemistry Laboratory Initial State:** 2 analysts active until 18:00 (2.5 h remaining in shift = 5.0 analyst-hours). Analyst 1 has 1.2–2.0 h left on current sample (free at 16:42–17:30); Analyst 2 has 0.4–0.9 h left (free at 15:54–16:24). Waiting queue ahead of B-417 contains 1 urgent sample and 3 ordinary samples.
- **Microbiology Initial State:** Incubation started Friday 14:00 ($t = -1.5\text{ h}$). Continuous primary incubation duration is 46–54 h, reaching technical readiness Sunday 12:00–20:00 ($t = 44.5 - 52.5\text{ h}$).
- **QA Initial State:** 5 batches in queue. 1 reviewer available Friday until 20:00 (4.5 h), 1 reviewer Saturday 09:00–13:00 (4.0 h), 2 reviewers Monday from 08:00. Arrival rate $\lambda = 0.35/\text{h}$ during open hours.

### Scenario 1: Smooth Path (Clean Parallel Branches & Standard QA)

1. **Chemistry Waiting & Service:**
   - *Friday 15:30–18:00:* Analyst 2 finishes active work at ~16:09 and takes the urgent sample. Under Risk-Priority (45%), B-417 is next and is assigned to Analyst 1 when free at ~16:50. B-417 receives ~1.1 h of service before the 18:00 closure. Under FIFO (55%), Analyst 1 takes ordinary sample 1; B-417 does not start.
   - *Weekend Pause:* Friday 18:00 to Monday 08:00 (62.0 calendar hours). Chemistry lab is completely closed.
   - *Monday 08:00 onward:* If started Friday, B-417 resumes at 08:00 with 0.4–3.4 h remaining, completing Monday 08:30–11:30 (mode ~09:30). If queued under FIFO, 2 analysts process remaining work ahead (~8.5 analyst-hours / 2 = ~4.25 h); B-417 begins ~11:30–12:00 and completes Monday 13:30–15:30.
   - *Blended Chemistry Completion:* Monday 21 Sep, 10:30 – 13:30 ($t = 67.0 - 70.0\text{ h}$).

2. **Microbiology Incubation & Readout:**
   - *Incubation:* Automated incubation runs continuously through Friday night, Saturday, and Sunday morning, completing Sunday 12:00–20:00 ($t = 44.5 - 52.5\text{ h}$).
   - *Readout:* If on-call microbiologist authorized (p = 0.25–0.45), readout occurs Sunday 13:00–23:00 ($t = 45.5 - 55.5\text{ h}$). If on-call not authorized (p = 0.55–0.75), sample waits dormant until Monday 08:00, with routine readout completing Monday 08:30–09:30 ($t = 65.0 - 66.0\text{ h}$).
   - *Microbiology Completion:* Monday 21 Sep, 08:30 – 09:30 at the latest ($t = 65.0 - 66.0\text{ h}$).

3. **Parallel Join Synchronization:**
   - The parallel join formula is strictly $T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{chem}}, T_{\text{micro}})$.
   - While Microbiology and the weekend staffing calendar governed the lead time through Sunday, **Chemistry is the binding constraint on Monday morning**:
     $$T_{\text{join}} = \max(\text{Mon 10:30–13:30}, \text{Mon 08:30–09:30}) = \text{Monday 21 Sep, 10:30 – 13:30 } (t = 67.0 - 70.0\text{ h})$$
   - *Operational insight:* Authorizing an on-call microbiologist on Sunday produces 0 hours of lead-time benefit for a smooth batch, because the results must wait for Monday's Chemistry completion.

4. **QA Waiting & Service:**
   - *Queue Dynamics:* Backlog at Friday 15:30 is 5 batches. Reviewers clear ~2.0 batches Friday (4.5 h) and ~1.8 batches Saturday (4.0 h), while ~3.0 new batches arrive during Friday/Saturday open hours. Monday 08:00–11:30 adds 7.0 reviewer-hours (clearing ~3.5 batches) against ~1.2 new arrivals. Backlog when B-417 arrives (~11:30 Monday) is ~1.5–2.5 batches.
   - *Queue Waiting:* With 2 reviewers active (effective capacity ~1.0 batch/h), B-417 waits 1.0–2.5 h. Review starts Monday 12:30–14:00.
   - *Service Duration:* Median 1.5 h, mode ~1.8 h, 80th percentile 3.5 h.
   - *QA Review Completion:* Monday 21 Sep, 14:30 – 17:30 ($t = 71.0 - 74.0\text{ h}$).

5. **Optional Rework / Investigation:** None (clean pass).
6. **Final Step (Electronic Release):** Takes 0.25–0.75 h (continuous 24/7 system).
   - **Terminal Completion:** **Monday 21 Sep, 15:00 – 18:15** ($t = 71.5 - 74.75\text{ h}$).

### Scenario 2: Chemistry Congestion & Borderline Rerun

Priority rerun takes Triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) h, running Monday 12:30–16:30. Chemistry finishes Monday 14:00–17:30 ($t = 70.5 - 76.0\text{ h}$).
2. **Microbiology Incubation & Readout:** Normal completion by Monday 08:30–09:30.
3. **Parallel Join Synchronization:**
   $$T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{chem}}, T_{\text{micro}}) = T_{\text{chem}} = \text{Monday 21 Sep, 14:00 – 17:30 } (t = 70.5 - 76.0\text{ h})$$
   Chemistry strictly dominates the join by 5–8 hours.
4. **QA Waiting & Service:** B-417 enters QA queue late Monday afternoon (~15:30–17:30). QA backlog is ~1–2 batches. Review begins Monday 17:00–18:30. Because QA closes at 20:00 (after which a 12-hour closure occurs until Tuesday 08:00), review either finishes narrowly before 20:00 or pauses overnight to finish Tuesday 08:30–10:00.
5. **Optional Rework / Investigation:** Rerun raises QA scrutiny, but assuming no formal investigation is sustained, documentation sign-off clears.
6. **Final Step:** Electronic Release (0.25–0.75 h).
   - **Terminal Completion:** **Monday 21 Sep, 19:30 to Tuesday 22 Sep, 11:00** ($t = 76.0 - 91.5\text{ h}$).

### Scenario 3: Microbiology Contamination Indication & Secondary Incubation

1. **Chemistry Waiting & Service:** Normal completion Monday 10:30–13:30 ($t = 67.0 - 70.0\text{ h}$).
2. **Microbiology Incubation & Readout:** Primary incubation completes Sunday afternoon. Contamination indication detected during readout (p = 0.05–0.12 baseline, up to 0.35 under confirmed excursion).

   - *If read Monday morning (~09:00):* Secondary incubation completes Tuesday 05:00–15:00. Second readout completes Tuesday 09:30–16:30 ($t = 89.5 - 97.0\text{ h}$).

   $$T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{chem}}, T_{\text{micro}}) = T_{\text{micro}} = \text{Tuesday 22 Sep, 09:30 – 16:30 } (t = 89.5 - 97.0\text{ h})$$
   Microbiology re-establishes complete dominance over the join; Chemistry idle wait is ~22–26 hours.
4. **QA Waiting & Service:** QA review occurs Tuesday afternoon (2 reviewers active, capacity 2.0 batches/h). Wait is 1.0–2.0 h; service takes 1.5–3.5 h.
5. **Optional Rework / Investigation:** A confirmed contamination indication triggers immediate critical defect classification or an emergency deviation.
6. **Final Step (Terminal Event Bifurcation):**
   - *Confirmed Critical Defect (Batch Rejected):* Rejection recording takes 0.5–1.0 h. Terminal completion **Tuesday 22 Sep, 14:00 – 19:30** ($t = 94.5 - 100.0\text{ h}$).

1. **Lab Branches & Join:** Chemistry and Microbiology join normally Monday 11:30–13:30 ($t = 68.0 - 70.0\text{ h}$).
2. **Initial QA Review:** Commences Monday ~13:30; reviewer flags incomplete batch records (completeness score 0.82; query probability 0.15–0.30) at ~15:00.

   - If 12 working hours: 5.0 h consumed Monday (15:00–20:00), 12 h overnight pause, 7.0 h consumed Tuesday (08:00–15:00). Query resolves Tuesday 15:00 ($t = 89.5\text{ h}$).

   - If 8 working hours: runs Tuesday 15:00 to Wednesday 11:00 (accounting for overnight pause at 20:00).
   - If 20 working hours: runs Tuesday 15:00 through Wednesday (12 h) to Thursday 11:00.
5. **Final QA Resumption & Release:** QA completes review in 1.0–2.0 h; electronic release in 0.5 h.
   - **Terminal Completion:** **Wednesday 23 Sep, 11:00 to Thursday 24 Sep, 16:00** ($t = 115.5 - 144.5\text{ h}$).

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

The five most critical uncertainty drivers governing remaining time, ranked by their operational impact on forecast variance:

1. **Calendar Discontinuities & Operating Window Closures**
   - *Classification:* **Calendar uncertainty**
   - *Impact:* The 62-hour Chemistry weekend closure (Fri 18:00 to Mon 08:00) and QA Sunday shutdown create massive discrete threshold effects. A 15-minute delay on Friday afternoon pushes Chemistry completion 64 hours into Monday. Similarly, an investigation completing Saturday afternoon hits the QA weekend closure and incurs a 43-hour dormant penalty until Monday morning.

2. **Upstream Temperature-Excursion Status & Cross-Branch Coupling**
   - *Classification:* **Dependence uncertainty & Observation uncertainty**
   - *Impact:* The unobserved sensor gap creates latent correlation across all downstream stages. If confirmed (prob 0.20–0.40), it concurrently elevates chemistry reruns, microbiology contamination, and QA investigations (from <10% to 30–55%). This common-cause mechanism is the sole driver of the extreme compound tail (>150 h).

3. **QA Deviation Investigation Classification (Documentation vs. Laboratory)**
   - *Classification:* **Path uncertainty**
   - *Impact:* Once a deviation is opened, the process bifurcates into two drastically different operational orders of magnitude: a documentation-only investigation adds 8–20 working hours (resolving in 1–2 days), whereas a laboratory investigation adds 3–7 calendar days (72–168 continuous hours), instantly throwing the batch into late the following week.

4. **Chemistry Queue Discipline & Shift Spillover**
   - *Classification:* **Queue uncertainty & Service-time uncertainty**
   - *Impact:* The 55%/45% split between FIFO and Risk-Priority determines whether B-417 started work on Friday or waits behind four samples on Monday morning. This creates a 3- to 5-hour variance in Monday's Chemistry completion time, directly modulating the QA join time and determining whether QA can finish before Monday's 20:00 closure.

5. **Microbiology Contamination Screen & Secondary Incubation**
   - *Classification:* **Path uncertainty & Service-time uncertainty**
   - *Impact:* While the primary incubation duration has moderate service-time variance (46–54 h), the binary contamination indication branch introduces a massive 20–30 hour continuous secondary incubation loop. This shifts the parallel join from Monday morning to Tuesday afternoon and introduces significant batch rejection risk.
