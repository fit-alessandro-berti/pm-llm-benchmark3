# 1. Review Scope and Assumptions

## 1.1 What is being decided

The board is not choosing "the best model". It is deciding **which artifact may be trusted for which purpose**, and what must be fixed in the *data* and the *policy* before any artifact can be trusted for enforcement. I therefore separate six use cases:

| Use case | Error that hurts | Acceptable trade-off | Extra requirements |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 Exploration / descriptive analytics | Missing a real pattern (false reject) | High fitness + generalization; **low precision tolerable** (F7 is legitimate *here*) | Must be labelled "not a policy model"; must never feed KPI dashboards |
| U2 Conformance enforcement / gating | Admitting forbidden behavior (false accept) | **Precision/specificity on forbidden behavior is a hard constraint**, fitness secondary | Soundness, deterministic verdicts, data attributes, audit trail of rules |
| U3 Audit reporting (non-blocking) | Both, but especially *unfounded certification* | Three-valued verdicts (compliant / non-compliant / **unverifiable**) | Rule provenance, reproducibility, negative-behavior evidence |
| U4 Simulation / capacity planning | Distorted routing probabilities & loop structure | Structural plausibility; precision less critical; **loops and concurrency must be real** | Data-driven branch probabilities, resource/time attributes |
| U5 Stakeholder communication | Confident misunderstanding | Comprehension **accuracy**, not comfort | Task-based comprehension test, not a 1–5 comfort poll |
| U6 Impact analysis / change management | Unowned, duplicated logic | Single point of edit per activity; low label duplication | Versioned model + constraint registry, labelelement map |

The F7/F8 dispute is a **category error on both sides**: F7 defends a U1 artifact using U1 criteria while the board is being asked to bless it for U2; F8 defends a U2 artifact by redefining *all* unseen behavior as non-compliant, which converts a modelling deficiency (extensional enumeration) into a policy claim that the process owner never ratified.

## 1.2 Notation and semantics assumed

- **A1.** `->` = sequence, `X` = exclusive choice, `+` = **parallel/AND** (PM4Py `Operator.PARALLEL`), `*` = loop (`*(body, redo)` = `body (redo body)*`), `tau` = silent transition. Under this reading each `X(a, tau)` in Model S is an *optional, at-most-once* activity, and the four branches interleave freely.
- **A2.** "Accepts" means **language membership modulo silent transitions**, not "alignment fitness above 0.8". A trace with one unmappable event can score 0.86 fitness and still be *rejected*. All probe verdicts below use language membership.
- **A3.** Model C contains no loops and no `tau`; its language is **exactly the 8 listed traces**. Acceptance is therefore decidable by inspection with no uncertainty.
- **A4.** Parsing caveat for S: if `+` were mis-read as inclusive OR, the *only* probe verdict that changes is B3 (rejected, because OR requires 1 branch). Mandatory-check skipping and Expedite mis-ordering survive either reading. The compliance verdict on S is robust to this ambiguity.
- **A5.** The log is a sample of *practice*, not of *policy*. Frequent  permitted; absent  forbidden. P1–P7 plus the adjudicated probes are the specification; the probes are explicitly **not** a complete ground truth.
- **A6.** Two required attributes are absent: `sourcing  {external, internal}` (P3) and `affected_checks  {Credit Check, Technical Review, Supplier Review, Expedite Review}` per correction (P6). **No control-flow model can decide P3 or P6 without them.** Any model that appears to decide them is guessing.
- **A7.** Reported metrics are **not reproducible** (no seeds, preprocessing, cost settings, held-out case IDs, or PM4Py version). They are treated as *ordinal hints*, not measurements. Where I quote numbers below they are either the reported ones (flagged) or **analytic derivations from the model expressions**, with enumeration code in §9 to verify them.
- **A8.** P7 says "suspicious", B8 says "suspicious/forbidden". I treat un-correctioned repetition as **SUSPICIOUS  structural reject with an adjudication/duplicate-event override path**, not as an automatic regulatory violation.
- **A9.** Unresolved policy details are logged as open questions O1–O8 (§7.6) rather than silently resolved by the modeller.

# 4. Adversarial Review of Model C

## 4.1 Strongest case for C

- **No definite-forbidden trace is in its language.** All 8 traces respect P1, P2, P4, P5, P7; the correction branch respects P7. On the definite negatives (B3, B4, B8) it is 3/3 correct, where S is 1/3.
- No silent transitions, no loops  **unambiguous alignments**, trivially decidable membership, and a crisp answer to "which approved variant did this case follow?" — genuinely useful for U3 evidence and for a known-good-path checklist.
- High in-sample precision is *real* in the narrow sense: it does not invent behavior.
- Extensional enumeration is easy to sign off *as a list*, and easy to regression-test.

## 4.2 Strongest case against C

**(a) It is a lookup table, not a model.** |L(C)| = 8. The correction-free policy language implied by P1–P5 is 36 traces (§3.2); C covers **7 of 36  19%**. It rejects, among others:

- `R, TR, CC, AA, RO` (**B2**, explicitly valid) — Auto Approval was only enumerated in CCTR order.
- `R, TR, CC, SR, ER, AA, RO` (**B5**) — the exact combination F4 reports in holdout.
- `R, CC, TR, ER, SR, MA, RO` — **P4 explicitly permits Expedite before Supplier Review**; C only enumerated SRER.
- `R, TR, CC, ER, MA, RO`, `R, CC, TR, SR, AA, RO`, `R, SR, CC, TR, MA, RO` (O1 silence), etc.

These are **false rejects created by taking a cross-product sample instead of composing independent decisions**. The policy has ~5 orthogonal decisions (check order × SR presence/position × ER presence/position × approval type × correction pattern); C enumerates 8 points of that space and calls the rest non-compliant.

**(b) Correction handling is worse than it looks.** One branch, one correction, both checks repeated, in CCTR order only, with no supplier/expedite interaction and no re-review. P6 and F6 explicitly require *only-CC*, *only-TR*, *both*, and repetition. C therefore rejects **B6** and every realistic rework case, and its single correction branch is *unverifiable* anyway without `affected_checks`. C's precision credit for that branch is unearned.

**(c) High precision is plausibly memorization.** Precision measured on training positives only (F5) is maximized by a model whose language equals the training variant set. This is the extensional analogue of overfitting: **C's compliance value is entirely contingent on the log already being compliant.** If a forbidden behavior were frequent, C would happily enumerate it and score 0.99 precision. C encodes *is*, never *ought*. Note also the internal anomaly: an exact enumeration should give training fitness  1.0 and precision  1.0; the reported 0.973/0.941 say the artifact and the measurement do not correspond.

**(d) Holdout degradation is diagnostic, not incidental.** 0.973  0.744 with TBR generalization 0.412 is the signature of variant memorization plus missing-event brittleness (F4). But — and this is where F8 deserves a fair hearing — **the 0.744 cannot be attributed** without adjudicating a sample of rejected holdout cases into {legit-unseen, log-artifact, genuine violation}. That experiment (E5) is the single most informative thing the board can commission.

**(e) Maintainability / ownership (F3) is structurally bad.** 48 labeled leaves, 9 labels, **39 duplicate occurrences** (not the reported 33 — unreproducible). "Where is Credit Check implemented?" has 9 answers. Adding one optional activity, or ratifying one new ordering, multiplies branches: the honest closure of P1–P7 is *infinite* (unbounded corrections), so enumeration cannot be repaired by "adding a few more variants". Edit locality is zero; regression risk per change is total.

**(f) Runtime is a symptom, not a cause.** 0.440 s median comes from search branching over 9 duplicate Credit Check transitions and a wide XOR; it is confounded by size (§6) and will worsen combinatorially with each added variant.

**(g) "Audit-ready" is a mislabel.** Auditability requires *traceable rules* ("mandatory checks before approval, P2, owner X") plus *evidence of testing against forbidden behavior*. C provides neither; it provides a photograph of past practice.

Also inflates apparent non-compliance of external orders | Perturbation tests (E4) with controlled deletion rates; report fitness deltas per model; separate "log-quality violations" from policy violations |
| **5** | **Alignment-cost sensitivity + silent-transition accounting** | Whether tau moves are free, whether log moves cost 1 or 5, whether precision counts tau-enabled escapes, and whether fitness is trace-average or move-weighted | Can move fitness by several points and reverse precision orderings; likely explains the internally inconsistent (0.973, 0.941) pair for C | Publish the cost function; sweep costs (E7); unit-test that the cost override is actually honored by the installed release |
| **6** | **Duplicate-label effects (and the unreproducible "33")** | 9 CC transitions in C  alignment search ambiguity, enabled-label multisets in precision, cold places in TBR generalization; recount gives 39, not 33 | Undermines trust in the whole submission (a number not recomputable from the artifact) and inflates C's runtime and simplicity penalty | Publish the definition; report labeled-transition count, distinct labels, duplicate occurrences, and labelelement map |
| **7** | **Stakeholder-score methodology** | Informal 1–5, unblinded, advocates present, comfort not accuracy | Drives adoption of the model that stakeholders will *misread* (S makes B3/B4 look legal) | Task-based comprehension accuracy on probes, blinded, N15,  reported (E10) |
| **8** | **Simplicity as a size proxy** | Arc-degree  f(average degree); C is penalized for being wide, not for being wrong | Could be mis-used as "C is badly designed, S is well designed" — the opposite of the compliance truth | Report structural maintainability metrics (edit locality, duplication) alongside; keep arc-degree as a descriptive footnote |
| **9** | **Runtime confounding by model size** | 48 transitions + duplicates  larger A* frontier; unknown threads/timeouts | Only affects the operational SLA, and could distort fitness if searches were truncated | Report p50/p95/max, hardware, config, timeout counts; normalize per labeled transition |

Additional cross-cutting pathology: **mixed replay semantics** (alignment fitness + alignment precision + *token-based* generalization). TBR and alignments do not agree on silent-loop handling; disagreements between TBR-fit and alignment-cost-0 should be reported as a measurement-quality signal, not averaged away.

# 7. Third Alternative

**Strategy chosen: block-structured procedural backbone + declarative/data constraint layer, with explicit three-valued verdicts and usage profiles.** Justification: P1, P2, P4, P5, P7 are *control-flow* rules and belong in a sound, compact, single-edit-point net. P3 and P6 are *data-dependent* rules and **cannot** be expressed in any control-flow model given A6; pretending otherwise is exactly what made S unsafe and C brittle. The unit of conformance is the **pair (backbone, constraint set) under a declared profile** — never the net alone.

## 7.1 Compact formal description

Let `OPT(a)  X(a, tau)`.

```
model_third  =  ->(
    R,
    +( OPT(SR),  ->( +(CC, TR), OPT(ER) ) ),        # review phase
    *( ->(CD, +(OPT(CC), OPT(TR), OPT(SR), OPT(ER))), tau ),   # correction phase
    X(MA, AA),
    RO
)
```

Key structural idea: **Supplier Review is placed in parallel with the *whole* mandatory-check-then-expedite sequence**, not in a fixed slot. This simultaneously yields (i) SR before, between, or after the checks (P3 is silent on SR ordering — O1), (ii) SR before *or* after ER (P4 explicitly allows both), (iii) ER strictly after **both** checks, (iv) **one occurrence per label** in the review phase.

**Invariants provable from the structure (no data needed):**

- **I1** R is first, RO is last, nothing follows RO (P1).
- **I2** Every trace contains 1 CC and 1 TR, both before the approval (P2) — the first pass is an **AND, never optionalized**.
- **I3** ER occurs only after both CC and TR have occurred, and strictly before the approval (P4).
- **I4** Exactly one of MA/AA, immediately before RO (P5).
- **I5** Every *repetition* of CC, TR, SR or ER is preceded by a CD (P7 enforced by construction, not by hope).
- **I6** SR is at most once per phase; corrections may repeat unboundedly (P6, "may repeat").
- **I7** The **correction-free fragment of L(model_third) equals exactly the 36-trace policy language** of P1–P5 (+P7 vacuously). Not a superset (unlike S), not a 7-trace sample (unlike C). Verifiable by bounded enumeration (E7).

**Cost:** 13 labeled transitions, 9 distinct labels, **4 duplicate occurrences** (CC, TR, SR, ER each twice: first pass + recheck), 7 silent transitions. Compare S (8/9/0) and C (48/9/39).

**Deliberately *not* enforced by the net** (because they are data-dependent or unratified): `external  SR`, `SR  external`, `recheck set == affected set`, correction budget, SR-on-internal. These live in the constraint layer with **UNVERIFIABLE** as a first-class outcome.

## 7.2 Self-contained PM4Py code

```python
# =====================================================================
# model_third : procedural backbone (T)  --  PM4Py, version-pinned env
# conformance unit = (net_third, POLICY_CONSTRAINTS, profile)
# =====================================================================
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree, Operator

R  = "Register Dossier";  CC = "Credit Check";       TR = "Technical Review"
SR = "Supplier Review";   ER = "Expedite Review";    CD = "Correct Dossier"
MA = "Manager Approval";  AA = "Auto Approval";      RO = "Release Order"

MODEL_THIRD_STR = (
    "->('Register Dossier', "
    "   +( X('Supplier Review', tau), "
    "      ->( +('Credit Check', 'Technical Review'), X('Expedite Review', tau) ) ), "
    "   *( ->('Correct Dossier', +( X('Credit Check', tau), X('Technical Review', tau), "
    "                               X('Supplier Review', tau), X('Expedite Review', tau) )), tau ), "
    "   X('Manager Approval', 'Auto Approval'), "
    "   'Release Order')"
)

model_third = pm4py.parse_process_tree(MODEL_THIRD_STR)
net_third, im_third, fm_third = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(model_third)

# ---- programmatic cross-check of the parsed artifact (defence against
# ---- parser/operator ambiguity, cf. assumption A4) -------------------
def act(lbl): return ProcessTree(label=lbl)
def tau():    return ProcessTree()                      # silent
def nd(op, *kids):
    t = ProcessTree(operator=op)
    for k in kids:
        k.parent = t; t.children.append(k)
    return t
SEQ, XOR, AND, LOOP = (Operator.SEQUENCE, Operator.XOR,
                       Operator.PARALLEL, Operator.LOOP)
def opt(a): return nd(XOR, act(a), tau())

review_phase = nd(AND, opt(SR), nd(SEQ, nd(AND, act(CC), act(TR)), opt(ER)))
recheck      = nd(AND, opt(CC), opt(TR), opt(SR), opt(ER))
corr_phase   = nd(LOOP, nd(SEQ, act(CD), recheck), tau())
built = nd(SEQ, act(R), review_phase, corr_phase, nd(XOR, act(MA), act(AA)), act(RO))
assert str(built).replace(" ", "") == str(model_third).replace(" ", "")

# ---- structural gate: must be sound before any metric is reported ----
try:
    ok = pm4py.check_soundness(net_third, im_third, fm_third, print_diagnostics=True)
except TypeError:                      # signature differs across releases
    ok = pm4py.check_soundness(net_third, im_third, fm_third)
assert ok, "model_third rejected: unsound backbone"

# ---- OPTIONAL stakeholder partial-order view (NOT claimed language-
# ---- equivalent: the correction loop is annotated, not encoded) ------
def stakeholder_powl_view():
    from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (Transition, SilentTransition,
                                        OperatorPOWL, StrictPartialOrder)
    tR, tCC, tTR = Transition(R), Transition(CC), Transition(TR)
    tSR, tER, tRO = Transition(SR), Transition(ER), Transition(RO)
    tMA, tAA = Transition(MA), Transition(AA)
    checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[tCC, tTR])          # no edges => concurrent
    oSR = OperatorPOWL(operator=XOR, children=[tSR, SilentTransition()])
    oER = OperatorPOWL(operator=XOR, children=[tER, SilentTransition()])
    app = OperatorPOWL(operator=XOR, children=[tMA, tAA])
    po = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[tR, checks, oER, oSR, app, tRO])
    po.order.add_edge(tR, checks); po.order.add_edge(tR, oSR)
    po.order.add_edge(checks, oER)
    po.order.add_edge(oER, app);   po.order.add_edge(oSR, app)
    po.order.add_edge(app, tRO)
    return po   # annotate: "Correct Dossier may re-open affected checks (see D7)"
```

## 7.3 Declarative / data constraint dictionary and checker

```python
VERDICT_ORDER = ["COMPLIANT", "COMPLIANT_WITH_WARNINGS", "UNVERIFIABLE", "NONCOMPLIANT"]

POLICY_CONSTRAINTS = {
 "D1":  dict(template="existence", activity=CC, min=1, scope="before_approval",
             severity="VIOLATION", enforced_by="backbone(I2)", source="P2"),
 "D2":  dict(template="existence", activity=TR, min=1, scope="before_approval",
             severity="VIOLATION", enforced_by="backbone(I2)", source="P2"),
 "D3":  dict(template="precedence", before=[CC, TR], after=ER, also_before_approval=True,
             severity="VIOLATION", enforced_by="backbone(I3)", source="P4"),
 "D4":  dict(template="data_precondition", if_activity=SR,
             requires="case.sourcing == 'external'", severity="VIOLATION",
             on_missing_attribute="UNVERIFIABLE", source="P3", open_question="O2"),
 "D5":  dict(template="data_response", if_condition="case.sourcing == 'external'",
             requires_activity=SR, severity="VIOLATION",
             on_missing_attribute="UNVERIFIABLE", source="P3"),
 "D6":  dict(template="exactly_one", activities=[MA, AA], severity="VIOLATION",
             enforced_by="backbone(I4)", source="P5"),
 "D7":  dict(template="recheck_coverage", trigger=CD,
             requires="set(rechecks_after_trigger) == set(case.affected_checks[i])",
             severity="VIOLATION", on_missing_attribute="UNVERIFIABLE",
             source="P6/F6", open_question="O3,O5"),
 "D8":  dict(template="no_repeat_without_correction", activities=[CC, TR, SR, ER],
             severity="SUSPICIOUS", enforced_by="backbone(I5)", source="P7",
             override="adjudication or D11 log-artifact finding"),
 "D9":  dict(template="data_forbid", if_condition="case.sourcing == 'internal'",
             forbids_activity=SR, severity="WARNING", source="O2 (unratified)"),
 "D10": dict(template="budget", activity=CD, max=3, severity="WARNING",
             source="O7 (proposed guard on the unbounded loop)"),
 "D11": dict(template="duplicate_event_artifact", same=["concept:name", "org:resource"],
             window_seconds=2, severity="LOG_QUALITY",
             action="flag, never silently delete"),
 "D12": dict(template="boundary", first=R, last=RO, severity="VIOLATION",
             enforced_by="backbone(I1)", source="P1"),
}

CHECKS = [CC, TR, SR, ER]
APPROVALS = [MA, AA]

def seq(trace):  return [e["concept:name"] for e in trace]

def correction_segments(s):
    """(i, CD index, set of rechecks before next CD / approval / end)."""
    out, idx = [], [k for k, a in enumerate(s) if a == CD]
    for i, k in enumerate(idx):
        stop = idx[i + 1] if i + 1 < len(idx) else len(s)
        for j in range(k + 1, stop):
            if s[j] in APPROVALS: stop = j; break
        out.append((i, k, {a for a in s[k + 1:stop] if a in CHECKS}))
    return out

def check_constraints(trace, attrs):
    """attrs: {'sourcing': 'external'|'internal'|None,
               'affected_checks': [set(...), ...] | None}  (one entry per CD)"""
    s, findings = seq(trace), []
    add = lambda rid, st, msg: findings.append(dict(rule=rid, status=st, detail=msg))

    # --- defence in depth: re-assert the structural invariants on the trace
    for rid, ok in (("D12", s and s[0] == R and s[-1] == RO and R not in s[1:]
                            and RO not in s[:-1]),
                    ("D1", CC in s), ("D2", TR in s),
                    ("D6", sum(a in APPROVALS for a in s) == 1)):
        if not ok: add(rid, "VIOLATION", "structural invariant violated on trace")
    for k, a in enumerate(s):
        if a == ER and not (CC in s[:k] and TR in s[:k]):
            add("D3", "VIOLATION", "Expedite before both mandatory checks")
        if a in CHECKS and a in s[:k]:
            if CD not in s[:k]: add("D8", "VIOLATION", f"repeat of {a} without correction")

    # --- data-dependent rules: three-valued, never guessed
    sourcing = attrs.get("sourcing")
    if SR in s:
        if sourcing is None:  add("D4", "UNVERIFIABLE", "sourcing attribute absent")
        elif sourcing != "external": add("D4", "VIOLATION", "Supplier Review on non-external")
    if sourcing is None:
        if SR not in s: add("D5", "UNVERIFIABLE", "cannot confirm externalSupplier Review")
    elif sourcing == "external":
        if SR not in s: add("D5", "VIOLATION", "external order without Supplier Review")
        else: pass
    if sourcing == "internal" and SR in s:
        add("D9", "WARNING", "O2 unratified: SR on internal order")

    segs = correction_segments(s)
    affected = attrs.get("affected_checks")
    for i, k, rechecks in segs:
        if affected is None or i >= len(affected) or affected[i] is None:
            add("D7", "UNVERIFIABLE", f"correction #{i}: affected_checks absent")
        elif rechecks != set(affected[i]):
            add("D7", "VIOLATION",
                f"correction #{i}: rechecks {sorted(rechecks)} != affected {sorted(affected[i])}")
    if len(segs) > POLICY_CONSTRAINTS["D10"]["max"]:
        add("D10", "WARNING", "correction budget exceeded (proposed guard)")

    # --- log-artifact detection (kept orthogonal to compliance)
    for k in range(1, len(s)):
        if s[k] == s[k-1]:
            t1, t0 = trace[k].get("time:timestamp"), trace[k-1].get("time:timestamp")
            close = (t1 is not None and t0 is not None
                     and abs((t1 - t0).total_seconds()) <= 2)
            same_res = trace[k].get("org:resource") == trace[k-1].get("org:resource")
            if close and same_res:
                add("D11", "LOG_QUALITY", f"probable duplicate event {s[k]} at {k}")
    return findings

def verdict(findings, profile="AUDIT"):
    st = {f["status"] for f in findings}
    if "VIOLATION" in st: return "NONCOMPLIANT"
    if "UNVERIFIABLE" in st:
        return "NONCOMPLIANT_HOLD" if profile == "GATING" else "UNVERIFIABLE"
    if "WARNING" in st: return "COMPLIANT_WITH_WARNINGS"
    return "COMPLIANT"
```

|---|---|---|---|

| `AUDIT` | net + D1–D12 | `UNVERIFIABLE`, routed to a data-remediation queue | U3 |
| `GATING` | net + D1–D12 | `NONCOMPLIANT_HOLD` (manual adjudication, never auto-release) | U2 |

## 7.4 Explicit semantics for each contested requirement

- **Mandatory checks (P2):** `+(CC, TR)` in the first pass — an **AND**, so both are unavoidable and either order is accepted, with **one label occurrence each**. No tau alternative exists on a mandatory activity. This is the single decisive fix versus S.
- **Optional Supplier Review (P3):** `OPT(SR)` in parallel with the checkexpedite sequence. Optionality is *control-flow*; the **requirement** ("external  SR") is D5, and the **precondition** ("SR  external") is D4. Missing `sourcing` yields UNVERIFIABLE, never a silent pass.
- **Expedite ordering (P4):** structurally after **both** checks and before the approval (I3); relative to SR **both orders are allowed** because SR is a parallel sibling of the whole sequence — this is what C could not express without more branches.
- **Exactly-one approval (P5):** `X(MA, AA)` immediately before RO; asserted again by D6. Auto Approval is *not* conditioned on check order, sourcing or urgency (C's spurious coupling removed); if the owner wants an auto-approval eligibility rule, it is a **data rule** (new D13), not a new branch.
- **Correction / recheck (P6, P7):** unbounded loop `*(->(CD, recheck), tau)` where `recheck` allows any subset of the four reviews in any order, including the empty subset. **The first pass is not optionalized; only the *rechecks* are**, because which checks must repeat is exactly the missing `affected_checks` attribute. D7 enforces set equality when the attribute exists and returns UNVERIFIABLE otherwise. P7 is enforced *structurally* (I5): a repeat is impossible without an intervening CD.

## 7.5 Limitations caused by the missing attributes (stated, not hidden)

1. **P3 cannot be decided.** B1 on an external order (SR missing) and B7 on an internal order are both accepted by the net; only D4/D5 catch them, and only with `sourcing`. Until then a measurable share of cases is UNVERIFIABLE — this is a **data defect surfaced as a verdict**, not a modelling success.
2. **P6 cannot be decided.** B6 is accepted structurally and returned UNVERIFIABLE. Under GATING it is *held*, which will generate appeals; the hold rate is a deployment gate (G3), not an inconvenience to be tuned away.
3. **The net alone is over-permissive on the correction phase** (`CD` then approval, or rechecking the wrong subset). Therefore **net-only conformance reporting is prohibited** (§10): anyone computing `pm4py.fitness_alignments` on `net_third` and publishing it as "compliance" reproduces S's pathology in a new costume.
4. **Unbounded loop** admits arbitrarily many corrections; D10 is a *proposed* guard, not ratified policy, and simulation (U4) needs an empirical correction-count distribution.
5. **Recheck-phase ordering is deliberately free** (e.g., ER re-review before a CC recheck). If the owner ratifies an ordering (O8), tighten the loop body — a one-place edit.

## 7.6 Open policy questions to the process owner (must be answered, not assumed)

**O1** May SR precede/interleave the mandatory checks? (P3 silent; the backbone allows it, C forbids it.) **O2** Is SR on an *internal* order forbidden or merely unnecessary? **O3** Which activities count as "checks" for recheck coverage — CC/TR only, or SR/ER too? **O4** May a correction occur *after* approval, and does it force re-approval? (Backbone currently places all corrections before approval; also: may CD occur *between* the two mandatory checks? Currently no.) **O5** Is an empty `affected_checks` (cosmetic correction, no recheck) legitimate? **O6** Is P7 "suspicious" a hard reject or an adjudication trigger, and what duplicate-event tolerance applies? **O7** Correction budget / escalation. **O8** Ordering constraints inside the recheck phase.

## 7.7 Documented variants (defensible alternatives, not hedging)

- **T-strict-SR:** nest SR after the checks (`->(+(CC,TR), +(OPT(SR),OPT(ER)))`) **iff O1 is ratified as "SR after checks"**. Cost: rejects 16 of the 36 currently-permitted correction-free shapes; benefit: fewer escaping edges, higher precision. Decide with E6/E5 evidence, not taste.
- **T-strict-recheck:** replace the recheck AND with `X(+(CC,TR), CC, TR)` to force 1 mandatory recheck procedurally. Only correct if O5 is answered "no cosmetic corrections"; otherwise it manufactures false rejects. Costs 2 extra duplicate occurrences per mandatory label.
- **T-attribute-conditioned:** two backbones (external / internal) sharing one template, selected by `sourcing`, once the attribute exists with 99% completeness. This removes D4/D5 from the unverifiable bucket and is the *target state*; it is premature today.

## E1 — Reproducible splits, published preprocessing

```python
import hashlib, json, platform, pm4py
from pm4py.objects.log.obj import EventLog, Trace, Event

SEED = 20240517
def h(seed, key): return hashlib.sha256(f"{seed}|{key}".encode()).hexdigest()

def variant_key(tr): return tuple(e["concept:name"] for e in tr)

def split_case_random(log, seed=SEED, frac=0.8):
    ids = sorted({t.attributes["concept:name"] for t in log})
    train_ids = {c for c in ids if int(h(seed, c)[:8], 16) / 0xFFFFFFFF < frac}
    tr, te = EventLog(), EventLog()
    for t in log: (tr if t.attributes["concept:name"] in train_ids else te).append(t)
    return tr, te

def split_variant_disjoint(log, seed=SEED, frac=0.8):
    """No variant appears on both sides: kills variant leakage."""
    vs = sorted({variant_key(t) for t in log}, key=lambda v: h(seed, str(v)))
    cut = int(frac * len(vs)); keep = set(vs[:cut])
    tr, te = EventLog(), EventLog()
    for t in log: (tr if variant_key(t) in keep else te).append(t)
    return tr, te

def split_stratified(log, key_fn, seed=SEED, frac=0.8):
    """Attribute- or variant-stratified 80/20 *within* each stratum."""
    from collections import defaultdict
    buckets = defaultdict(list)
    for t in log: buckets[key_fn(t)].append(t)
    tr, te = EventLog(), EventLog()
    for k, ts in buckets.items():
```

```python
def tree_language(t, max_loop=2, max_len=14):
    from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
    if t.operator is None: return {()} if t.label is None else {(t.label,)}
    kids = [tree_language(c, max_loop, max_len) for c in t.children]
    if t.operator == Operator.XOR:  return set().union(*kids)
    if t.operator == Operator.SEQUENCE:
        out = {()}
        for k in kids: out = {a+b for a in out for b in k}
        return {s for s in out if len(s) <= max_len}
    if t.operator == Operator.PARALLEL:
        def interleave(A, B):
            res = set()
            for a in A:
                for b in B:
                    if not a: res.add(b); continue
                    if not b: res.add(a); continue
                    for rest in interleave({a[1:]}, {b}): res.add((a[0],)+rest)
                    for rest in interleave({a}, {b[1:]}): res.add((b[0],)+rest)
            return res
        out = {()}
        for k in kids: out = interleave(out, k)
        return {s for s in out if len(s) <= max_len}
    if t.operator == Operator.LOOP:
        body, redo = kids[0], kids[1]
        out, cur = set(body), set(body)
        for _ in range(max_loop):
            cur = {a+r+b for a in cur for r in redo for b in body}
            out |= {s for s in cur if len(s) <= max_len}
        return out
    raise ValueError(t.operator)
```

## E12 — Decision rule (lexicographic, with veto)

A model is **rejected for enforcement regardless of any aggregate score** if any gate fails:

- **G1** Soundness verified; bounded state space; no dead labeled transitions.
- **G2** **Zero false accepts on the definite-forbidden suite** (N1–N7 + generated negatives). No tolerance, no trade-off.
- **G3** UNVERIFIABLE rate on holdout  board-agreed threshold (proposed 10%) **with a funded remediation path**; and no UNVERIFIABLE case auto-released under GATING.
- **G4** Reproducibility pack complete (seeds, preprocessing, costs, versions, held-out IDs, model hash); every reported number recomputable from the artifact — the "33" failure mode is a gate, not a typo.
- **G5** Recall  0.90 on adjudicated unseen-valid combinations (E6) and false-reject rate  0.05 on adjudicated legit-unseen holdout (E5).
- **G6** Stakeholder accuracy  0.85 on mandatory-activity and ordering tasks (E10); p95 runtime within SLA (E9).

Only after G1–G6 pass may the board Pareto-compare fitness, holdout precision, negative-suite specificity, generalization-by-unseen-recall, simplicity and runtime — **disaggregated, with CIs**.

# 11. Dissenting View

**Rebuttal (strongest case against my own recommendation, ~220 words).**

In a regulated dossier process the loss function is brutally asymmetric: one released order without a Credit Check can cost more than a thousand delayed legitimate orders. On that reading F8 is right and my recommendation is reckless. Model C admits *no* definite-forbidden trace; my Model T admits `Correct Dossier  approval` with no recheck, and rechecks of the wrong subset, and calls them "UNVERIFIABLE" — a category that, in production, becomes a queue, then a backlog, then a rubber stamp. I have not removed S's permissiveness; I have relocated it into a Python dictionary that no auditor can see in the process diagram, no impact-analysis tool can index, and no version-controlled net can constrain. F3's complaint about duplicate labels is trivial next to *shadow logic outside the model*. My "invariant I7" is proved against **my own reading** of P1–P7 and my own hand-curated negative suite — the same reading that produced the model. That is question-begging, not validation. C, by contrast, is a single, finite, enumerable artifact that a process owner can literally sign, line by line, and whose rejections are conservative by construction. Its 0.744 holdout fitness may be *mostly genuine non-compliance* that my "generalization" would legalize. And I dismissed the reported metrics as unreproducible while offering zero measurements of my own.

**What would change my decision.**

1. **Adjudicated holdout rejections (E5):** if a blinded, owner-labelled sample of C's rejections shows 70% `genuine-violation` or `log-artifact-that-is-actually-non-compliance`, then C's low fitness is a *feature*, the asymmetry argument wins, and I would recommend C (or T-strict variants) for GATING with an explicit waiver workflow instead of an UNVERIFIABLE bucket.
2. **Quantified costs:** a signed cost model from Compliance/Risk showing false-accept : false-reject cost ratios  50:1 would justify maximally conservative enumeration plus manual approval of every new combination — i.e., C as a whitelist, with my backbone demoted to a *proposal generator* for whitelist extensions.
3. **Shadow-mode telemetry:** if T's UNVERIFIABLE rate exceeds ~20%, or if >5% of UNVERIFIABLE cases are released without documented evidence, my three-valued design is empirically permissive and must be replaced by T-strict-recheck / T-strict-SR or by C-plus-waiver.
4. **Attribute completeness 99%** for `sourcing` and `affected_checks`: this collapses most UNVERIFIABLE cases, makes the attribute-conditioned variant family the best option, and would move me off the hybrid entirely.
5. **Evidence that C is maintainable in practice:** a parameterized generator that regenerates the variant catalog from a ratified rule set, with provenance and diffable output, would dissolve F3 and much of my maintainability objection — C would then be a *compiled* artifact, not a hand-maintained one.
6. **A precision/specificity result** showing T's held-out precision and negative-suite specificity are close to S's, or a soundness/reachability failure in the silent-loop correction phase, would invalidate my central claim and force redesign.
7. **Regulator/auditor statement** that a third verdict value is unacceptable in evidence: then the honest options are C-plus-waiver or "no automated conformance until the data exists" — and I would recommend the latter over any model that guesses.
