**A2 — Rounding.** All reported times are rounded to the nearest 5 minutes, so a reported timestamp `t` may represent actual time in approximately `[t-2.5, t+2.5]` minutes. I therefore do not treat differences smaller than about 5 minutes as precise. **Scenario O — optimistic for waiting and service.** For MISSING starts, use the latest feasible start that gives nonnegative service: P05 S2 start 10:15 with service 0; P07 S1 start 10:00 with service 0; P11 S2 start 12:25 with service 0; P15 S1 start 12:15 with service 0. This minimizes missing-case waiting and service. It is an extreme bound because zero-minute service is not credible operationally.

**Scenario C — central.** Use plausible service durations: P05 30 min, P07 30 min, P11 25 min, P15 30 min, with resource-compatible starts 09:45, 09:30, 12:00, 11:45 respectively. This is the main reconstruction above.

**Scenario P — pessimistic for waiting and service.** Use earliest feasible starts: P05 start 09:45, service 30; P07 start 09:25, service 35; P11 start 12:00, service 25; P15 start 11:40, service 35. This maximizes missing-case waiting and service within the observed resource windows.

Effects:
- Missing S1 starts P07 and P15 affect S1 waiting/service. Under central, S1 mean waiting for started cases is 65.7 min. If both are moved to latest feasible, the S1 mean rises by about 3 min; if moved to earliest feasible, it changes by less than 1 min because central already uses plausible service. So S1 mean waiting is stable to within a few minutes.
- Missing S2 starts P05 and P11 affect S2 waiting. Central S2 mean waiting for started cases is 36.6 min. Moving both to latest feasible raises it by about 3–4 min. Moving to earliest feasible is essentially central for P05 and P11. So the S2 mean waiting is also stable within a few minutes.
- Service-time means are less stable: if missing starts are given zero service, S1 mean service drops by up to about 3 min and S2 mean completed service drops by about 4 min. But the qualitative comparison remains: S2 service is longer than S1 service.
- OPEN cases P15, P17, and P21 are right-censored. Their final service times are unknown. They increase S2/S1 busy time beyond 14:00 but do not change within-window utilization except through elapsed service. They do bias completed-service means downward if treated as completed at 14:00; I therefore separate elapsed from final service.
- Waiting cases P16, P18, P19, P20, and P22 have only lower-bound waiting times at 14:00. Their final waits may be much longer. This strengthens, rather than weakens, the conclusion that S2 is the late bottleneck and that queues remain at 14:00.
- C2 outage sensitivity: if the outage is ignored for capacity, S2 whole-window utilization would be about 570/720 = 79.2% rather than 80.9%, and the 10:00–12:00 S2 utilization would fall from about 88.9% to about 83.3%. The outage tightens S2, but the backlog conclusion survives without it.
- Rounding sensitivity: ±2.5 minutes per timestamp means differences such as short waits are uncertain by about ±5 minutes. Long waits and the S1-early/S2-late pattern are stable. Exact mean waiting times and exact utilization percentages should be treated as approximate to within a few minutes or a few percentage points.

Stable conclusions: S1 is the early queue builder; S2 becomes the late bottleneck; total WIP peaks around late morning; S2 has longer service times and larger late waits; missingness and censoring do not reverse these conclusions.

Unstable conclusions: exact mean waiting/service times; exact peak WIP by one case depending on rounded simultaneous events; exact utilization within a few percentage points; final service/sojourn for OPEN cases.

# 7. Reproducible Python

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

OBS_START = pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 08:00:00")
OBS_END = pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 14:00:00")

rows = [
    ["P01","08:00","08:05","08:25","I1","08:30","09:00","C1","COMPLETE"],
    ["P02","08:05","08:10","08:35","I2","08:40","09:20","C2","COMPLETE"],
    ["P03","08:10","08:30","08:55","I1","09:05","09:45","C1","COMPLETE"],
    ["P04","08:20","08:40","09:10","I2","09:25","10:05","C2","COMPLETE"],
    ["P05","08:25","09:00","09:25","I1","MISSING","10:15","C1","COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START"],
    ["P06","08:35","09:15","09:45","I2","10:10","10:50","C2","COMPLETE"],
    ["P07","08:45","MISSING","10:00","I1","10:20","11:00","C1","COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START"],
    ["P08","09:00","09:50","10:20","I2","11:15","12:00","C2","COMPLETE"],
    ["P09","09:05","10:05","10:35","I1","11:00","11:40","C1","COMPLETE"],
    ["P10","09:15","10:25","10:55","I2","11:45","12:10","C1","COMPLETE"],
    ["P11","09:25","10:40","11:05","I1","MISSING","12:25","C2","COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START"],
    ["P12","09:35","11:00","11:30","I2","12:15","13:00","C1","COMPLETE"],
    ["P13","09:50","11:10","11:40","I1","12:30","13:20","C2","COMPLETE"],
    ["P14","10:00","11:35","12:00","I2","13:05","13:50","C1","COMPLETE"],
    ["P15","10:10","MISSING","12:15","I1","13:30","OPEN","C2","OPEN_IN_S2"],
    ["P16","10:25","12:05","12:35","I2","","","","WAITING_FOR_S2"],
    ["P17","10:45","12:20","12:50","I1","13:55","OPEN","C1","OPEN_IN_S2"],
    ["P18","11:00","12:40","13:10","I2","","","","WAITING_FOR_S2"],
    ["P19","11:15","12:55","13:25","I1","","","","WAITING_FOR_S2"],
    ["P20","11:30","13:15","13:45","I2","","","","WAITING_FOR_S2"],
    ["P21","11:50","13:30","OPEN","I1","","","","OPEN_IN_S1"],
    ["P22","12:10","","","","","","","WAITING_FOR_S1"],
]
cols = ["case","arrival_s1","s1_start","s1_end","s1_resource",
        "s2_start","s2_end","s2_resource","state_at_14_00"]
df = pd.DataFrame(rows, columns=cols)

def parse_t(x):
    if pd.isna(x) or x in ["", "MISSING", "OPEN"]:
        return pd.NaT
    return pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 " + x)

for c in ["arrival_s1","s1_start","s1_end","s2_start","s2_end"]:
    df[c + "_dt"] = df[c].apply(parse_t)

# Central imputation for MISSING starts only. OPEN and blank remain NaT.
central = {
    ("P05","s2_start"): "09:45",
    ("P07","s1_start"): "09:30",
    ("P11","s2_start"): "12:00",
    ("P15","s1_start"): "11:45",
}
for (case, col), t in central.items():
    mask = df["case"] == case
    df.loc[mask, col + "_dt"] = pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 " + t)

# Elapsed service columns: OPEN activities count only up to observation end.
df["s1_end_elapsed"] = df["s1_end_dt"]
df.loc[df["s1_end"] == "OPEN", "s1_end_elapsed"] = OBS_END
df["s2_end_elapsed"] = df["s2_end_dt"]
df.loc[df["s2_end"] == "OPEN", "s2_end_elapsed"] = OBS_END

def minutes(a, b):
    return (a - b).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0

df["S1_wait"] = minutes(df["s1_start_dt"], df["arrival_s1_dt"])
df["S1_service"] = minutes(df["s1_end_elapsed"], df["s1_start_dt"])
df["S2_wait"] = minutes(df["s2_start_dt"], df["s1_end_dt"])
df["S2_service"] = minutes(df["s2_end_elapsed"], df["s2_start_dt"])
df["total_sojourn"] = minutes(df["s2_end_elapsed"], df["arrival_s1_dt"])

print("Case-level central metrics (minutes after 08:00):")
print(df[["case","S1_wait","S1_service","S2_wait","S2_service",
          "total_sojourn","state_at_14_00"]].round(1).to_string(index=False))

# WIP snapshots using half-open intervals [start, end).
times = pd.date_range(OBS_START, OBS_END, freq="30min")
wip_rows = []
for t in times:
    s1 = ((df["arrival_s1_dt"] <= t) &
          ((df["s1_end_dt"].isna()) | (df["s1_end_dt"] > t))).sum()
    s2 = ((df["s1_end_dt"].notna()) &
          (df["s1_end_dt"] <= t) &
          ((df["s2_end_dt"].isna()) | (df["s2_end_dt"] > t))).sum()
    wip_rows.append({"time": t.strftime("%H:%M"), "S1_WIP": s1,
                     "S2_WIP": s2, "Total_WIP": s1 + s2})
wip_df = pd.DataFrame(wip_rows)
print("\nWIP at 30-minute grid (central imputation):")
print(wip_df.to_string(index=False))

# PM4Py-compatible interval-event dataframe for usable observations.
event_rows = []
for _, r in df.iterrows():
    if pd.notna(r["s1_start_dt"]) and (pd.notna(r["s1_end_dt"]) or r["s1_end"] == "OPEN"):
        end = r["s1_end_dt"] if pd.notna(r["s1_end_dt"]) else OBS_END
        event_rows.append({
            "case:concept:name": r["case"],
            "concept:name": "S1",
            "start_timestamp": r["s1_start_dt"],
            "time:timestamp": end,
            "org:resource": r["s1_resource"],
        })
    if pd.notna(r["s2_start_dt"]) and (pd.notna(r["s2_end_dt"]) or r["s2_end"] == "OPEN"):
        end = r["s2_end_dt"] if pd.notna(r["s2_end_dt"]) else OBS_END
        event_rows.append({
            "case:concept:name": r["case"],
            "concept:name": "S2",
            "start_timestamp": r["s2_start_dt"],
            "time:timestamp": end,
            "org:resource": r["s2_resource"],
        })

event_df = pd.DataFrame(event_rows)
formatted_event_df = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    event_df,
    case_id="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp",
)
print("\nPM4Py formatted interval-event dataframe shape:", formatted_event_df.shape)

# PM4Py case-level functions are valid only on a case-level projection.
# Use completed cases only: arrival to final S2 completion.
completed = df[df["s2_end_dt"].notna()].copy()
case_proj = pd.DataFrame({
    "case:concept:name": completed["case"],
    "concept:name": "completed_case",
    "start_timestamp": completed["arrival_s1_dt"],
    "time:timestamp": completed["s2_end_dt"],
    "org:resource": "SYSTEM",
})
case_proj_fmt = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    case_proj,
    case_id="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp",
)
case_log = pm4py.convert_to_event_log(case_proj_fmt)
case_log = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(case_log)
case_log = pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate(case_log)
print("PM4Py insert_case_service_waiting_time and insert_case_arrival_finish_rate applied.")
print("These PM4Py outputs are case-level (arrival-to-completion), not station-level S1/S2 metrics.")

# Direct station-specific pandas calculation is safer here than pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals,
# because S1 and S2 WIP need separate half-open definitions and censored/open/waiting handling.
# pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals would treat intervals generically and cannot distinguish
# "waiting for S1", "in S1 service", "waiting for S2", "in S2 service", and right-censoring.

def overlap_minutes(start, end, w_start, w_end):
    if pd.isna(start) or pd.isna(end):
        return 0.0
    s = max(start, w_start)
    e = min(end, w_end)
    return max(0.0, (e - s).total_seconds() / 60.0)

def busy_for(resource, w_start, w_end):
    total = 0.0
    for _, r in df.iterrows():
        if r["s1_resource"] == resource:
            total += overlap_minutes(r["s1_start_dt"], r["s1_end_elapsed"], w_start, w_end)
        if r["s2_resource"] == resource:
            total += overlap_minutes(r["s2_start_dt"], r["s2_end_elapsed"], w_start, w_end)
    return total

periods = [
    ("08:00-10:00", OBS_START, pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 10:00:00")),
    ("10:00-12:00", pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 10:00:00"), pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 12:00:00")),
    ("12:00-14:00", pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 12:00:00"), OBS_END),
]

print("\nBusy time by resource and period (minutes, central imputation):")
for label, a, b in periods:
    vals = {res: round(busy_for(res, a, b), 1) for res in ["I1","I2","C1","C2"]}
    print(label, vals)

print("\nWhole-window busy time (minutes):")
print({res: round(busy_for(res, OBS_START, OBS_END), 1)
       for res in ["I1","I2","C1","C2"]})
```
