## 1. Executive causal assessment
Mainly confounded. Confidence: 65.

Strongest evidence for: within every complexity stratum Orion shows equal or lower mean and median cycle time than Nova; the crude 6.7 h gap disappears once complexity (and its correlates night shift and compliance) is held fixed. Strongest evidence against: only two low-complexity Orion cases exist, night coverage is almost entirely Orion, and completed-case selection may truncate long Orion tails.

The 50 % reassignment proposal is likely to fail or backfire. Because Orion’s higher aggregate mean is produced by a harder case mix plus night load, simply relabeling half its volume will not shorten queueing or service on those cases; it may increase Nova’s night backlog and compliance workload, raising cycle times for both groups while leaving the underlying routing policy untouched.

## 6. Intervention and learning plan

| phase | intervention | target mechanism | assignment unit | safeguards | process-mining measurements | causal learning value | stop or rollback rule |
|-------|--------------|------------------|-----------------|------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | 10 % random reroute of Medium cases to Nova for 4 weeks | routing policy | case after Classify | maintain night coverage, cap Nova load at +15 % | DFG per group, queue_wait by variant | isolates routing effect from reviewer skill | >20 % increase in Nova median wait |
| 2 | Add one compliance reviewer to night queue | queueing & compliance capacity | shift-level capacity | keep existing routing | wait_before_review_h by compliance flag | tests queue mechanism without changing group label | no reduction in wait after 3 weeks |
| 3 | Staggered rollout of revised routing rule on High cases | routing + skill bundle | week-level clusters | parallel control group of unchanged weeks | cycle_time, rework, outcome_30d by complexity | estimates policy effect under realistic load | any stratum shows >10 % cycle increase |

Final recommendation: Run the 10 % randomized reroute pilot first while simultaneously adding night compliance capacity. The pilot directly tests whether the routing rule itself lengthens cycle times; the capacity intervention addresses the dominant observed mechanism (queue_wait) without touching reviewer assignment. Stop both after four weeks if any monitored stratum shows >15 % deterioration in median cycle_time or if overlap diagnostics reveal <3 cases in any complexity–shift cell. This sequence learns the causal contribution of routing versus capacity while limiting operational exposure.
