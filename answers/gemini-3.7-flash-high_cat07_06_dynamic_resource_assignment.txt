# 1. Objectives and constraints

Dynamic resource assignment in knowledge-intensive service processes is fundamentally a multi-criteria optimization problem under operational uncertainty. Rather than maximizing instantaneous throughput at the expense of long-term workforce health and process quality, an explainable policy must balance multiple competing objectives and adhere to strict regulatory, safety, and fairness constraints.

---

### 1.1 Optimization Objectives

The assignment policy optimizes a composite objective across six core dimensions:

1. **SLA Risk Minimization & Timely Delivery**: Minimize the probability of SLA breaches by prioritizing cases with tight deadlines and high complexity, routing them to resources capable of completing them within the remaining time buffer.
2. **Quality & First-Time-Right Execution**: Minimize downstream rework, handback loops, and customer appeals by ensuring that complex and high-risk tasks are executed by qualified personnel or supervised pairs.
3. **Continuity of Case Ownership & Friction Reduction**: Preserve case ownership to minimize context switching, cognitive reload overhead, and handover delays (which empirically add a median of $2.7\text{ hours}$ on high-complexity cases).
4. **Workload Fairness & Burnout Prevention**: Balance open case volumes and remaining working hours across the team while actively preventing the assignment of additional tasks to staff exhibiting high overtime risk.
5. **Organizational Resilience & Skill Development**: Systematically invest in workforce cross-skilling by assigning safe training opportunities to trainees under structured supervision, eliminating single points of failure (e.g., bottlenecks in Security and Approval).
6. **Explainability & Procedural Transparency**: Ensure every assignment decision can be mapped to clear, auditable scoring rules and expressed in plain language to caseworkers and team leads.

---

### 1.2 Hard Constraints (Safety, Compliance, and Boundary Rules)

Hard constraints are non-negotiable boundaries that prune ineligible (case, resource) pairs prior to scoring.

* **HC1 — Batch Capacity Limit**: No resource shall receive more than $4$ new task assignments within the current decision batch ($\Delta N_r \le 4$).
* **HC2 — High-Priority Competency Floor**: No case with priority `urgent` or `high` may be assigned to a resource with skill level $< 2$ (trainee) unless paired with an explicit, designated specialist supervisor (skill level $3$).
* **HC3 — Security Review Governance**: `Security Review` activities must strictly be performed by a specialist (skill level $3$), or by a trainee/competent resource (skill level $1$–$2$) under direct, documented supervision by either `R7_Gita` or `R8_Hugo`.
* **HC4 — Final Approval Authorization**: `Final Approval` must be finalized and signed off by a resource with skill level $2$ or $3$. Trainees (skill level $1$) may assist in preparation but are legally prohibited from final sign-off.
* **HC5 — Absolute Incompetence Prohibition**: A resource with skill level $0$ cannot be assigned an activity independently under any circumstance.
* **HC6 — Quality Batching Gate**: Activity batching for `Quality Check` is permitted **only** when case priority is `normal` or `low` **and** SLA time remaining is $> 24\text{ hours}$.

---

### 1.3 Soft Constraints and Preference Trade-offs

Soft constraints represent trade-offs modeled as continuous penalty/reward functions within the assignment score:

* **SC1 — Overtime Risk Mitigation**: Heavily penalize assignments to resources with `overtime risk = "high"` (e.g., $R2$, $R4$, $R9$), permitting assignment only when an urgent SLA cannot be met by any other qualified resource.
* **SC2 — Continuity vs. Specialization Trade-off**:
  * For high-complexity cases ($\text{complexity} \ge 0.60$) with high handover costs, retaining the current owner is strongly preferred if their skill level is $\ge 2$.
  * If the current owner has skill level $\le 1$, the policy trades off continuity in favor of routing to a specialist or initiating a supervised pairing, as historical data shows that retaining a weak-skilled owner adds $3.3\text{ hours}$ of service time and inflates rework.
* **SC3 — Reassignment Loop Suppression**: Heavily penalize reassigning cases that already have $\text{reassignment\_count} \ge 2$, because historical evidence proves $> 2$ reassignments result in a $1.8\times$ higher rework rate and $1.4\times$ higher appeal rate.
* **SC4 — Skill Development Allocation Target**: At least $20\%$ of `normal` and `low` priority cases must be allocated to support documented training priorities, provided the SLA buffer exceeds $12\text{ hours}$.
* **SC5 — Workload Leveling & Intra-Team Affinity**: Favor resources with fewer open cases and more remaining hours, while favoring intra-team handovers over inter-team handovers when transfers are necessary.

# 2. Assignment policy

The proposed policy is a **Multi-Criteria Dynamic Assignment Algorithm with Supervised Pairing (MCDA-SP)**. It executes in four sequential stages: (1) Candidate Pruning, (2) Urgency & Complexity Weight Adaptation, (3) Multi-Factor Utility Scoring, and (4) Global Stable Matching with Exception Handling.

```
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                              QUEUE SNAPSHOT                             |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                     |
                                     v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| STAGE 1: Hard Constraint Pruning & Pair Generation                      |
| - Filter skill=0, verify HC1-HC6                                        |
| - Generate singletons (r) and supervised pairs (r_trainee, r_supervisor)|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                     |
                                     v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| STAGE 2: Dynamic Weight Adaptation                                      |
| - Calculate SLA Urgency Factor U(c) & Handover Sensitivity H(c)         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                     |
                                     v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| STAGE 3: Multi-Factor Utility Scoring                                   |
| - Score = S_skill + S_cont - S_handover - S_load - S_ot + S_train + S_bt|
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                     |
                                     v
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| STAGE 4: Constrained Greedy Assignment & Fallback Escalation            |
| - Sort cases by priority & SLA; assign max score; update batch counters |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

---

### 2.1 Formal Scoring Model

For a case $c$ and an eligible candidate resource or supervised pair $R$:

$$\text{Score}(c, R) = w_{\text{skill}} \cdot S_{\text{skill}}(c, R) + w_{\text{cont}} \cdot S_{\text{cont}}(c, R) - w_{\text{ho}} \cdot P_{\text{ho}}(c, R) - w_{\text{load}} \cdot P_{\text{load}}(R) - w_{\text{ot}} \cdot P_{\text{ot}}(R) + w_{\text{train}} \cdot S_{\text{train}}(c, R) + w_{\text{batch}} \cdot S_{\text{batch}}(c, R)$$

#### 1. Skill Match Score ($S_{\text{skill}}$)
* Let $\text{eff\_skill}(c, R)$ be the effective skill level of the candidate:
  * For single resource $r$: $\text{eff\_skill} = \text{Skill}(r, \text{act}(c))$.
  * For supervised pair $(r_{\text{trainee}}, r_{\text{sup}})$: $\text{eff\_skill} = \min(3, \text{Skill}(r_{\text{trainee}}) + 1.5)$.
* Scale:
  $$\text{Skill Level } 3 \implies +100 \quad | \quad \text{Skill Level } 2 \implies +65 \quad | \quad \text{Supervised Pair } (1 + 3) \implies +80 \quad | \quad \text{Skill Level } 1 \implies +20$$

#### 2. Continuity & Handover Penalties ($S_{\text{cont}}$, $P_{\text{ho}}$)
* **Continuity Bonus**: If $R$ contains $\text{CurrentOwner}(c)$, $S_{\text{cont}}(c, R) = +70 \times \text{Complexity}(c)$.
* **Handover Penalty**: If $R$ does not contain $\text{CurrentOwner}(c)$ and $\text{CurrentOwner}(c) \ne \text{None}$:
  $$P_{\text{ho}}(c, R) = \text{CostBandFactor}(\text{band}(c)) \times \Big(1 + 0.8 \cdot \text{ReassignCount}(c)\Big) \times \text{Complexity}(c)$$
  *(CostBandFactor: `high` = 60, `medium` = 35, `low` = 15, `none` = 0)*.

#### 3. Workload & Overtime Fairness Penalties ($P_{\text{load}}$, $P_{\text{ot}}$)
* **Workload Index**:
  $$P_{\text{load}}(r) = 15 \times \left(\frac{\text{OpenCases}(r) + \Delta N_r}{\text{HoursRemaining}(r)}\right)$$
* **Overtime Risk Penalty**:
  $$P_{\text{ot}}(r) = \begin{cases} 80, & \text{if OvertimeRisk}(r) = \text{"high"} \\ 25, & \text{if OvertimeRisk}(r) = \text{"medium"} \\ 0, & \text{if OvertimeRisk}(r) = \text{"low"} \end{cases}$$
* For supervised pairs, $P_{\text{load}}$ and $P_{\text{ot}}$ are evaluated as $0.7 \cdot P(r_{\text{trainee}}) + 0.3 \cdot P(r_{\text{sup}})$.

#### 4. Training / Development Bonus ($S_{\text{train}}$)
* If $\text{Priority}(c) \in \{\text{"normal"}, \text{"low"}\}$, $\text{SLA\_Remaining}(c) \ge 12\text{h}$, and $\text{act}(c) = \text{TrainingPriority}(r)$:
  $$S_{\text{train}}(c, R) = +60 \quad (\text{if supervised or trainee skill safe})$$
* If $\text{Priority}(c) \in \{\text{"urgent"}, \text{"high"}\}$, $S_{\text{train}}(c, R) = 0$ (learning is subordinated to SLA security).

#### 5. Batching Bonus ($S_{\text{batch}}$)
* If $\text{act}(c) = \text{"Quality Check"}$, $\text{Priority}(c) \in \{\text{"normal"}, \text{"low"}\}$, $\text{SLA} > 24\text{h}$, and $r$ is already assigned another Quality Check in this cycle:
  $$S_{\text{batch}}(c, r) = +35$$

### 2.2 Dynamic Weight Adaptation

Weights dynamically shift based on case urgency:

| Priority Class | $w_{\text{skill}}$ | $w_{\text{cont}}$ | $w_{\text{ho}}$ | $w_{\text{load}}$ | $w_{\text{ot}}$ | $w_{\text{train}}$ | $w_{\text{batch}}$ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Urgent** ($\text{SLA} \le 4\text{h}$) | $1.5$ | $0.8$ | $0.5$ | $0.4$ | $0.3$ | $0.0$ | $0.0$ |
| **High** ($4\text{h} < \text{SLA} \le 10\text{h}$) | $1.2$ | $1.2$ | $1.0$ | $0.7$ | $0.8$ | $0.2$ | $0.0$ |
| **Normal** ($10\text{h} < \text{SLA} \le 36\text{h}$) | $0.8$ | $0.9$ | $0.8$ | $1.0$ | $1.0$ | $1.2$ | $1.0$ |
| **Low** ($\text{SLA} > 36\text{h}$) | $0.6$ | $0.6$ | $0.6$ | $1.2$ | $1.1$ | $1.5$ | $1.2$ |

### 2.3 Algorithmic Execution Logic (Pseudocode)

```python
def assign_queue(queue_snapshot, resources, skill_matrix):
    # Step 1: Sort queue by priority rank (urgent > high > normal > low), then SLA asc, then complexity desc
    sorted_queue = sort_cases_by_urgency(queue_snapshot)
    
    batch_assignments = {r.id: [] for r in resources}
    training_assignments_count = 0
    normal_low_count = sum(1 for c in sorted_queue if c.priority in ['normal', 'low'])
    
    for case in sorted_queue:
        candidate_options = []
        
        # Identify singleton candidates and supervised pairs
        for r in resources:
            if len(batch_assignments[r.id]) >= 4:  # HC1: Batch limit
                continue
                
            skill = skill_matrix[r.id][case.required_skill]
            
            # HC5: No skill 0 unassisted
            if skill == 0:
                continue
                
            # HC2 & HC7: High priority / Approval checks
            if case.priority in ['urgent', 'high'] and skill < 2:
                continue
            if case.next_activity == 'Final Approval' and skill < 2:
                continue
                
            # HC6: Security Review singleton rule
            if case.next_activity == 'Security Review' and skill < 3:
                continue  # Skill 1-2 requires explicit supervisor
                
            # Evaluate valid singleton
            score = evaluate_utility(case, r, supervisor=None, weights=get_weights(case.priority))
            candidate_options.append({'candidate': r, 'supervisor': None, 'score': score})
            
        # Evaluate Supervised Pairing Candidates for Security, Approval, or Training
        if case.next_activity == 'Security Review' or case.priority in ['normal', 'low']:
            supervisors = [s for s in resources if skill_matrix[s.id][case.required_skill] == 3 
                           and len(batch_assignments[s.id]) < 4]
            trainees = [t for t in resources if t.training_priority == case.required_skill.lower()
                        and len(batch_assignments[t.id]) < 4]
            
            for t in trainees:
                for s in supervisors:
                    if t.id == s.id: continue
                    # HC6: Security review supervisors must be Gita or Hugo
                    if case.next_activity == 'Security Review' and s.id not in ['R7_Gita', 'R8_Hugo']:
                        continue
                    score = evaluate_utility(case, t, supervisor=s, weights=get_weights(case.priority))
                    candidate_options.append({'candidate': t, 'supervisor': s, 'score': score})
        
        # Step 2: Select highest utility candidate
        best_choice = max(candidate_options, key=lambda x: x['score'])
        
        # Step 3: Register assignment
        assignee = best_choice['candidate']
        supervisor = best_choice['supervisor']
        
        batch_assignments[assignee.id].append(case.id)
        if supervisor:
            batch_assignments[supervisor.id].append(f"{case.id}_sup")
            
        if case.priority in ['normal', 'low'] and (assignee.training_priority == case.required_skill.lower()):
            training_assignments_count += 1

    return batch_assignments
```

### 2.4 Tie-Breaking and Exception Handling

* **Tie-Breaker Hierarchy**:
  1. **Lowest Normalized Workload**: Prefer the resource with the lowest ratio of $(\text{Open Cases} / \text{Hours Remaining})$.
  2. **Team Locality Affinity**: Prefer a candidate in the same operational team as the current case owner to minimize cross-silo handover friction.
  3. **Training Balance**: Prefer the resource who has received fewer training assignments during the current operating week.
  4. **FIFO Log Timestamp**: Deterministic fallback to resource alphabetical ID.
* **Exception Escalation**: If all eligible candidates violate overtime or batch constraints during an `urgent` case arrival, trigger an **Emergency Override Exception**: allocate to the highest-skilled resource regardless of overtime risk, log an auditable `assignment_reason = "EMERGENCY_SLA_OVERRIDE"`, and automatically notify the team supervisor.

### 5.1 Communication Policy and Core Principles

* **No Black-Box Scoring**: Workers have real-time visibility into the assignment criteria: current workload balance, skill growth objectives, case continuity, and deadline urgency.

* **Human-in-the-Loop Override**: Any caseworker or team lead can request a temporary freeze or case trade through their dashboard if unanticipated complexity arises.

```
| 6. SLA Breach & Buffer Burn  | On-time completion % and SLA buffer consumption rate    |
| 7. Post-Assignment Rework    | Frequency of Rework Correction following handovers      |
| 8. Training Share Compliance | % Normal/Low cases allocated to documented trainees     |
| 9. Specialist Bottleneck Q   | Mean waiting time before Security Review & Approval     |
| 10. Manual Override Ratio    | Frequency & rationale of manual supervisor overrides    |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 6.1 Indicator Definitions and Formulas

#### 1. Handover Frequency & Network Density ($I_{\text{ho}}$)
* **Definition**: Measures the rate of resource transfers between consecutive activities within a case.
* **Formula**:
  $$I_{\text{ho}} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbb{I}(\text{org:resource}_{i} \ne \text{org:resource}_{i-1} \wedge \text{case}_{i} = \text{case}_{i-1})}{N - |C|}$$
* **Log Columns**: `case:concept:name`, `org:resource`, `time:timestamp`.
* **Target / Threshold**: Inter-resource handovers $< 1.2$ per case on average.

#### 2. Reassignment Ping-Pong Index ($I_{\text{loop}}$)
* **Definition**: Proportion of cases exceeding the critical threshold of $2$ reassignments.
* **Formula**:
  $$I_{\text{loop}} = \frac{\sum_{c \in C} \mathbb{I}(\text{reassignment\_count}(c) > 2)}{|C|} \times 100\%$$
* **Log Columns**: `case:concept:name`, `reassignment_count`.
* **Target / Threshold**: $< 5\%$ of active cases.

#### 3. Skill Mismatch & Governance Violation Rate ($I_{\text{mismatch}}$)
* **Definition**: Percentage of events where an activity was executed by a resource below required skill level without documented supervision.
* **Formula**:
  $$I_{\text{mismatch}} = \frac{\sum_{e \in E} \mathbb{I}(\text{Skill}(\text{org:resource}_e, \text{act}_e) < 2 \wedge \text{is\_supervised}_e = \text{False})}{|E|} \times 100\%$$
* **Log Columns**: `org:resource`, `concept:name`, `required_skill`, `assignment_reason`.
* **Target / Threshold**: Strictly $0.0\%$ (Hard Compliance).

#### 4. Workload Equity (Gini Coefficient) ($I_{\text{gini}}$)
* **Definition**: Measures the statistical dispersion of active case burden across the operational workforce.
* **Formula**:
  $$G = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n} |w_i - w_j|}{2n\sum_{i=1}^{n} w_i}, \quad \text{where } w_i = \frac{\text{workload\_snapshot}(r_i)}{\text{HoursRemaining}(r_i)}$$
* **Log Columns**: `org:resource`, `workload_snapshot`, `time:timestamp`.
* **Target / Threshold**: $G \le 0.22$ (indicating equitable load distribution).

#### 5. Overtime Risk Exposure ($I_{\text{ot\_exp}}$)
* **Definition**: Proportion of new task assignments routed to resources flagged with `overtime risk = "high"`.
* **Formula**:
  $$I_{\text{ot\_exp}} = \frac{\sum_{e \in E_{\text{new}}} \mathbb{I}(\text{OvertimeRisk}(\text{org:resource}_e) = \text{"high"} \wedge \text{case\_priority}_e \ne \text{"urgent"})}{|E_{\text{new}}|} \times 100\%$$
* **Log Columns**: `org:resource`, `case_priority`, `time:timestamp`.
* **Target / Threshold**: $0.0\%$ for non-urgent cases; $< 2\%$ overall.

#### 6. SLA Breach & Buffer Consumption Rate ($I_{\text{sla}}$)
* **Definition**: Ratio of cases completed past `sla_deadline` and median SLA buffer consumed at completion.
* **Formula**:
  $$\text{SLA Miss Rate} = \frac{\sum_{c \in C} \mathbb{I}(\text{time:timestamp}_{\text{end}}(c) > \text{sla\_deadline}(c))}{|C|} \times 100\%$$
* **Log Columns**: `time:timestamp`, `sla_deadline`, `case_priority`.
* **Target / Threshold**: $0\%$ on urgent/high; $< 2.5\%$ on normal/low.

#### 7. Post-Assignment Rework & Appeal Escalation ($I_{\text{rework}}$)
* **Definition**: Probability of a case entering `Rework Correction` as a function of the number of prior handovers and skill level of the assignee.
* **Formula**:
  $$P(\text{Rework} \mid \text{Handovers} > 2) \quad \text{vs.} \quad P(\text{Rework} \mid \text{Handovers} \le 1)$$
* **Log Columns**: `case:concept:name`, `concept:name`, `handover_cost_band`, `reassignment_count`.
* **Target / Threshold**: Ratio $< 1.1\times$ (verifying that reassignments do not induce quality failures).

#### 8. Training Assignment Share ($I_{\text{train}}$)
* **Definition**: Percentage of normal and low priority cases allocated to fulfill documented training priorities.
* **Formula**:
  $$I_{\text{train}} = \frac{\sum_{c \in C_{\text{norm/low}}} \mathbb{I}(\text{assignment\_reason} = \text{"TRAINING\_PAIR"} \vee \text{TRAINING\_SOLO})}{|C_{\text{norm/low}}|} \times 100\%$$
* **Log Columns**: `case_priority`, `assignment_reason`, `required_skill`.
* **Target / Threshold**: $\ge 20.0\%$ across rolling 7-day windows.

#### 9. Specialist Bottleneck Queue Accumulation ($I_{\text{bottleneck}}$)
* **Definition**: Waiting time preceding `Security Review` and `Final Approval` relative to general queue waiting time.
* **Formula**:
  $$\text{QueueRatio}_{\text{specialist}} = \frac{\text{MeanWaitingTime}(\text{"Security Review"}, \text{"Final Approval"})}{\text{MeanWaitingTime}(\text{All Other Activities})}$$
* **Log Columns**: `concept:name`, `time:timestamp`, `case:concept:name`.
* **Target / Threshold**: Ratio $\le 1.35$.

#### 10. Policy Compliance & Override Ratio ($I_{\text{override}}$)
* **Definition**: Proportion of assignments where the algorithmic recommendation was manually overridden by a supervisor.
* **Formula**:
  $$I_{\text{override}} = \frac{\sum_{e \in E} \mathbb{I}(\text{assignment\_reason} = \text{"MANUAL\_OVERRIDE"})}{|E|} \times 100\%$$
* **Log Columns**: `assignment_reason`, `org:resource`, `time:timestamp`.
* **Target / Threshold**: $< 3.0\%$ of total assignment volume.

# 7. PM4Py implementation sketch

The Python module below demonstrates how event log data is processed using PM4Py and pandas to audit the policy, analyze handover networks, discover organizational roles, calculate waiting/service times, and detect bottlenecks.

```python
import pm4py
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np

def audit_dynamic_assignment_policy(log_path_or_df):
    """
    Comprehensive PM4Py audit suite for dynamic resource assignment policy.
    Analyzes SNA handover networks, role discovery, performance DFGs,
    waiting times, skill mismatches, and training compliance.
    """
    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    # 1. Log Ingestion and Schema Formatting
    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    if isinstance(log_path_or_df, str):
        df = pd.read_csv(log_path_or_df)
    else:
        df = log_path_or_df.copy()
        
    # Standardize column naming and timestamp parsing via PM4Py
    df['time:timestamp'] = pd.to_datetime(df['time:timestamp'])
    event_log = pm4py.format_dataframe(
        df,
        case_id='case:concept:name',
        activity_key='concept:name',
        timestamp_key='time:timestamp'
    )
    
    print(f"Loaded event log with {len(event_log)} events across {event_log['case:concept:name'].nunique()} cases.")

    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    # 2. Social Network Analysis: Handover of Work & Collaboration
    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    print("\n--- Social Network Analysis ---")
    
    # Discover Handover of Work Network (identifies handover friction & silos)
    handover_network = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(event_log)
    print("Handover of Work Matrix discovered.")
    
    # Discover Working-Together Network (evaluates supervised training pairs)
    working_together = pm4py.discover_working_together_network(event_log)
    print("Working Together Network discovered.")
    
    # Discover Activity-Based Resource Similarity (checks role alignment)
    resource_similarity = pm4py.discover_activity_based_resource_similarity(event_log)
    print("Resource Similarity Matrix computed.")
    
    # Organizational Role Discovery
    roles = pm4py.discover_organizational_roles(event_log)
    print(f"Discovered {len(roles)} distinct organizational roles across workforce.")

    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    # 3. Performance Analysis: Service Time, Waiting Time, & DFGs
    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    print("\n--- Performance & Time Analysis ---")
    
    # Enrich event log with case waiting and service times
    enriched_log = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(
        event_log,
        case_id_key='case:concept:name',
        timestamp_key='time:timestamp'
    )
    
    # Discover Performance Directly-Follows Graph (DFG)
    perf_dfg, start_acts, end_acts = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(
        enriched_log,
        case_id_key='case:concept:name',
        activity_key='concept:name',
        timestamp_key='time:timestamp'
    )
    print("Performance DFG generated (tracks bottleneck transitions).")
    
    # Discover Activity Batches (Audits Quality Check batching rule HC6)
    batches = pm4py.discover_batches(
        event_log,
        activity_key='concept:name',
        timestamp_key='time:timestamp',
        resource_key='org:resource'
    )
    print(f"Identified {len(batches)} activity execution batches in log.")

    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    # 4. Policy Compliance & Audit Metrics Computation
    # ---------------------------------------------------------
    print("\n--- Policy Compliance Indicators ---")
    
    # A. Reassignment & Handover Penalty Audit
    high_reassign_cases = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(
        enriched_log,
        'reassignment_count',
        [3, 4, 5, 6],
        level='case'
    )
    loop_rate = (high_reassign_cases['case:concept:name'].nunique() / 
                 enriched_log['case:concept:name'].nunique()) * 100.0
    print(f"Indicator 2 (Reassignment Loops >2): {loop_rate:.2f}%")
    
    # B. SLA Breach Rate
    if 'sla_deadline' in enriched_log.columns:
        enriched_log['sla_deadline'] = pd.to_datetime(enriched_log['sla_deadline'])
        # Group by case to evaluate final event timestamp vs deadline
        case_end = enriched_log.groupby('case:concept:name').agg({
            'time:timestamp': 'max',
            'sla_deadline': 'first',
            'case_priority': 'first'
        })
        case_end['sla_miss'] = case_end['time:timestamp'] > case_end['sla_deadline']
        overall_sla_miss = case_end['sla_miss'].mean() * 100.0
        urgent_sla_miss = case_end[case_end['case_priority'] == 'urgent']['sla_miss'].mean() * 100.0
        print(f"Indicator 6 (Overall SLA Miss Rate): {overall_sla_miss:.2f}%")
        print(f"Indicator 6 (Urgent SLA Miss Rate): {urgent_sla_miss:.2f}%")

    # C. Training Share Audit
    if 'assignment_reason' in enriched_log.columns:
        norm_low_events = enriched_log[enriched_log['case_priority'].isin(['normal', 'low'])]
        training_events = norm_low_events[norm_low_events['assignment_reason'].str.contains('TRAIN', na=False)]
        train_share = (len(training_events) / max(1, len(norm_low_events))) * 100.0
        print(f"Indicator 8 (Training Share in Normal/Low Cases): {train_share:.2f}% (Target: >=20%)")

    # D. Workload Fairness (Gini Index Calculation)
    if 'workload_snapshot' in enriched_log.columns:
        latest_workloads = enriched_log.groupby('org:resource')['workload_snapshot'].last().values
        def gini(array):
            array = np.array(array, dtype=np.float64)
            if np.amin(array) < 0:
                array -= np.amin(array)
            array += 0.0000001
            array = np.sort(array)
            index = np.arange(1, array.shape[0] + 1)
            n = array.shape[0]
            return ((np.sum((2 * index - n - 1) * array)) / (n * np.sum(array)))
        print(f"Indicator 4 (Workload Gini Coefficient): {gini(latest_workloads):.3f}")

    # E. Variant Analysis
    variants = pm4py.get_variants(enriched_log)
    print(f"Process Variant Count: {len(variants)} execution paths observed.")
    
    return {
        "handover_network": handover_network,
        "working_together": working_together,
        "resource_similarity": resource_similarity,
        "roles": roles,
        "performance_dfg": perf_dfg,
        "batches": batches
    }
```

The implementation integrates directly with process event logs to provide closed-loop operational governance:
1. **SNA Handover Mining** continuously flags emerging communication silos between functional teams.
2. **Performance DFG Analysis** isolates transitions where handover delays exceed the $2.7\text{h}$ benchmark.
3. **Role & Batch Discovery** automatically verifies that `Quality Check` batching and `Security Review` supervisory mandates are strictly enforced.
4. **Attribute Filtering** extracts real-time compliance metrics, alerting leadership whenever training allocations or workload fairness drift outside established operational parameters.
