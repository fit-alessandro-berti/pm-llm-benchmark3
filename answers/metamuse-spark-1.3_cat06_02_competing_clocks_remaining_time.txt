## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

General rule: parallel join = max(chemistry usable, microbiology usable) in calendar time, not sum. QA cannot start before join. All Friday 18:00 / 20:00 and weekend stops are pauses, not service.

**S1 Smooth:**
- *Chemistry waiting+service:* Priority (45%): start ~16:10-17:30 Fri, 0.5-1.3h worked Fri, pause, resume Mon 08:00, finish Mon 08:30-11:30 mid ~10:00. FIFO (55%): behind 4 jobs, total ahead ~13.5 analyst-hours; 2 servers clear in ~6.8h continuous but interrupted; resume Mon 08:00, B-417 start Mon 11:00-15:30, finish Mon 13:00-18:00 mid ~15:30. Rare fast track (~5-10%): start 15:54-16:12, service 1.5h, finish Fri 17:25-17:45.
- *Microbiology incubation+readout:* Ready Sun 12:00-20:00. Authorized (~35%): Sun 13:00-23:00 usable. Not authorized (~65%): Mon 08:30-10:00 usable.
- *Join:* max. Typical: priority+no-auth = max(Mon10:00, Mon09:00)=Mon10:00. FIFO+no-auth = max(Mon15:30, Mon09:00)=Mon15:30. Rare Fri+Sun = Sun eve, but QA closed so effective Mon 08:00.
- *QA waiting+service:* Backlog logic: 5 now + ~3-6 arrivals Fri15:30-Mon join  ~4 completions Fri eve+Sat = 4-7 queued Mon 08:00. With 2 reviewers, ~1 completion/h. B-417 wait ~2-7h mid ~4h. Service 1.5 median, 3.5 80th. E.g. join Mon10 +4+2 = accept Mon16:00. Join Mon15:30 +4+2 = Mon21:30  spills past 20:00 close to Tue 08:00-10:00.
- *Rework/investigation:* none.
- *Release:* +0.25-0.75h continuous. Smooth release Mon afternoon to Tue midday.

**S2 Chemistry rerun:**
- *Chemistry:* Same as S1 to first result Mon 10:00 (priority) or Mon 15:30 (FIFO), then borderline flag. Priority rerun 1.0-3.0h mid 1.9h. Morning first result  rerun finishes same day Mon 11:00-14:00. Afternoon first result near 16:00-18:00  cannot finish by 18:00, pauses to Tue 08:00 +1-2h = Tue 09:00-11:00 usable.
- *Microbiology:* As S1, Mon 09:00 or Sun eve, now non-dominant; waits for chemistry.
- *Join:* max shifts to chemistry rerun completion: Mon midday to Tue ~10:00.
- *QA:* Join Tue morning faces shorter queue (Monday drain) but still 1-4h wait; service as S1. Tue accept.
- *Investigation uplift:* Investigation prob now 0.30-0.55, so S2 often flows into S4/S5. Pure S2 without further query central Tue.
- *Release:* +0.5h.

**S3 Delayed microbiology:**
- *Chemistry:* As S1.
- *Microbiology S3a (simple weekend delay):* Ready Sun 12-20, no on-call, read Mon 08:30-10:00. This is the common case; join still chemistry-dominated unless chemistry was rare Friday finish, in which case join = Mon 09:00.
- *Microbiology S3b (contamination):* First read Mon 09:00 flags indication (prob higher if excursion). Second incubation 20-30h continuous: Sun12+20h=Mon08:00 earliest to Sun20+30h=Tue02:00 latest technically ready. Re-read 0.5-1.5h in staffing hours  usable Mon 09:00-Tue 10:00 mid Tue morning. Weekend on-call irrelevant for second read if weekday.
- *Join:* S3a join = chemistry time Mon10-15:30. S3b join = max(chem Mon10-15:30, micro Tue09:00) = Tue morning, micro becomes dominant.
- *QA:* Tue join  wait 1-4h (queue drained Monday), service 1.5-3.5h  accept Tue afternoon to Wed morning.
- *Release:* +0.5h  Tue-Wed.

**S4 QA query / doc-only investigation:**
- *Up to join:* As S1/S2/S3a (join Mon-Tue).
- *QA first pass:* Wait+service as above to Mon eve-Tue.
- *Query:* P 0.15-0.30 (score 0.82 supports mid range). +4-12 working hours: e.g. QA flags Tue 10:00, 8 working-hours = Tue 10:00-20:00 (10h open) + Wed 08:00-10:00  resume Wed morning. Calendar +1-2d.
- *Doc-only investigation:* 60-80% of investigations. +8-20 working hours = 1-2 working days. E.g. accept-pass paused Wed, resume Thu-Fri. Over Sat (only 4h) adds calendar stall: Thu eve  Sat 09:00-13:00  Mon.
- *QA second pass:* Re-review ~1-2h.
- *Release:* Wed-Thu typical, Fri if Sat intervenes.

**S5 Lab investigation / correlated tail:**
- *Up to QA:* As S2+S3b often: rerun Mon-Tue + contamination to Tue + query. Excursion confirmed acts as common cause.
- *Lab investigation:* 20-40% of investigations. +3-7 calendar days continuous lab work, independent of QA hours. Starting Tue-Wed  usable Fri 26 Sep to Wed 01 Oct.
- *QA acceptance + release:* After lab result returns, short QA close +0.5h release, or rejection recording 0.5-1.0h if critical defect confirmed (timing similar, outcome different).

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Weekend microbiology staffing authorization** — mainly *calendar uncertainty* (with queue/service component). Binary 0.25-0.45 decision shifts micro usable from Sun eve to Mon 09:00 (+10-16h calendar). Sets floor for join; interacts with QA Sunday closure.
2. **Unknown temperature-excursion flag and correlated rework** — mainly *dependence uncertainty* (+ path). P 0.20-0.40 confirmation jointly lifts rerun, contamination, investigation. Turns independent small tails into joint 10-20% severe tail; most tail-widening.
3. **QA queue at future Monday join + QA service skew** — mainly *queue uncertainty* (+ service-time). Initial 5 + overdispersed arrivals  weekend completions = 4-7 backlog; 2 reviewers Monday; wait 2-7h plus skewed service (median 1.5, 80th 3.5). Determines Mon-eve vs Tue spill past 20:00.
4. **Chemistry queue discipline and spillover past 18:00** — mainly *queue uncertainty* (+ calendar). FIFO 55% vs priority 45% moves B-417 start from Fri eve to Mon afternoon (±5h work, ±20h calendar with weekend pause). Decides whether chemistry or microbiology is max.
5. **Investigation type: documentation-only vs laboratory 3-7d** — mainly *path uncertainty* (+ service-time). 60-80% vs 20-40% split is uncertain; difference is 8-20 working hours vs 72-168 calendar hours. Single biggest jump from P80 to P90.

Secondary: chemistry service variability (service-time), contamination base rate (path), observation gap on excursion sensor (observation).
