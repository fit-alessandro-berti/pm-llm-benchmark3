### Self-Contained Reproducible Decision-Set Reconstructor (Python / `pandas`)

Standard PM4Py control-flow discovery (`discover_petri_net_inductive`, etc.) treats cases independently and cannot represent cross-case waiting-set competition, skill eligibility, or appointment maturity. Accordingly, `pandas` is the appropriate tool to verify waiting sets at each decision timestamp:

```python
import pandas as pd

def to_min(t):
    return int(t[:2]) * 60 + int(t[3:]) if t else None

qa_data = [
    ("A01", "08:00", "", 3, "general", 18, [("08:02", "08:20")]),
    ("A02", "08:04", "", 3, "general", 20, [("08:32", "08:52")]),
    ("A03", "08:08", "08:20", 3, "general", 10, [("08:20", "08:30")]),
    ("A04", "08:13", "", 1, "general", 7, [("08:58", "09:05")]),
    ("A05", "08:21", "", 3, "general", 15, [("09:05", "09:20")]),
    ("A06", "08:29", "09:15", 2, "general", 12, [("09:20", "09:32")]),
    ("A07", "08:35", "", 3, "general", 9, [("09:32", "09:41")]),
    ("A08", "08:40", "", 1, "general", 25, [("09:41", "10:06")]),
]

df_qa = pd.DataFrame(qa_data, columns=["job", "arr", "appt", "prio", "skill", "est", "segs"])
df_qa["arr_m"] = df_qa["arr"].map(to_min)
df_qa["appt_m"] = df_qa["appt"].map(to_min)
df_qa["first_start_m"] = df_qa["segs"].map(lambda s: to_min(s[0][0]))

for _, chosen in df_qa.sort_values("first_start_m").iterrows():
    t = chosen["first_start_m"]
    # Include jobs scanned by t (or within 2-min scan delay bound t+2) not yet started
    waiting = df_qa[(df_qa["arr_m"] <= t) & (df_qa["first_start_m"] >= t)]
    print(f"t={t//60:02d}:{t%60:02d} | chosen={chosen['job']} | waiting={list(waiting['job'])}")
```

# 4. Counterfactual discrimination tests

### Test 1: Queue QC at `10:54` (`C05` selection — Priority-With-Aging vs. Deadline-Breach Override)
- **Ambiguity:** At `10:54`, SPT is overridden to run `C05` (arr 10:11, P1, dl 10:50, est 40) ahead of `C06` (est 6) and `C07` (est 18, P2, dl 12:00). Because `C05` is *both* Priority 1 (having waited 43 min) and 4 minutes past its `10:50` deadline, **H1 (SPT + P1 wait-time cap $\approx 40\text{ min}$)** and **H2 (SPT + overdue-deadline override when $t \ge \text{deadline}$)** are observationally equivalent here.
- **Counterfactual scenario:** Hold arrival, `est_min`, and resources constant, but perturb `C05` to **Priority 3, deadline `10:50`**, and introduce a waiting job `C05b` (arr 10:11, **Priority 1, deadline `14:00`**, est 40) alongside `C06` (est 6).
  - **Prediction under H1 (P1 aging override):** Selects **`C05b`** at `10:54` (P1 waited 43 min; P3 `C05` waits).
  - **Prediction under H2 (Deadline-breach override):** Selects **`C05`** at `10:54` ($10:54 \ge 10:50$; `C05b` has ample slack until 14:00).
- **Discriminating observable:** A **`dispatch_trigger_reason` / `sla_escalation_flag`** logged at decision time (recording whether rule evaluation branched on `priority == 1 AND wait > threshold` versus `now >= deadline`).

### Test 2: Queue QD at `11:36` & `11:48` (`D10` wait — Global FIFO with HoL Blocking vs. Dedicated Sub-Queue / Specialist Reservation)
- **Ambiguity:** At `11:36`, `D-F` idles rather than taking `D10` (`electrical`, P1, est 12), and at `11:48`, `D-X` takes `D07` (`mechanical+electrical`, P3, arr 11:14, est 28) ahead of `D10` (arr 11:25). Two competing hypotheses explain both choices:
  - **H1 (Shared-pool FIFO with strict HoL blocking):** All jobs sit in one FIFO queue; at 11:36 `D05` (arr 11:07) blocks `D-F` because $30 > 24\text{ min}$ and overtaking is forbidden; at 11:48 `D07` (11:14) beats `D10` (11:25) by arrival order.
  - **H2 (Arrival-time static sub-queue routing + composite-skill priority):** Single-skill `electrical` job `D10` was routed at 11:25 to `D-X`'s sub-queue because `D-F` was already holding composite `D05`, and `D-X` prioritizes exclusive `mechanical+electrical` jobs (`D07`) over single-skill `electrical` jobs (`D10`).
- **Counterfactual scenario:** Swap arrival times of `D07` and `D10` (`D10` arrives `11:14`, `D07` arrives `11:25`), AND extend `D-F`'s meeting start from `12:00` to `12:10` (giving 34 min at 11:36, so `D05` fits on `D-F` at `11:36–12:06`). Now evaluate `D-X` at `11:48`:
  - **Prediction under H1 (FIFO):** `D-X` selects **`D10`** at `11:48` (`11:14 < 11:25`).
  - **Prediction under H2 (Composite-skill priority on `D-X`):** `D-X` still selects **`D07`** at `11:48` (`mechanical+electrical` outranks single-skill `electrical`).
- **Discriminating observable:** The **per-resource `candidate_queue_id` / `eligibility_set` snapshot** at `11:25` and `11:36` (showing whether `D10` was ever in `D-F`'s candidate pool at `11:36`).

### Test 3: Queue QB at `09:31` & `10:28` (P1-Preemptive FIFO vs. Preemptive Priority with Resume-Lock & Wait-Aging)
- **Ambiguity:** `B01` (P3) resumes over `B03` (P2) at `09:31`, and unstarted `B04` (P3, arr 09:35, wait 53m) starts over `B06` (P2, arr 10:05, wait 23m) at `10:28`.
  - **H1 (Two-tier P1-preemptive + FIFO base):** P2 and P3 are treated identically in FIFO; only P1 has priority.
  - **H2 (Three-tier Priority + non-preemptible P2 + 30-min aging boost):** P2 normally beats P3, except in-progress jobs resume ahead of P2 (`09:31`) and P3 jobs waiting $>30\text{ min}$ promote above fresh P2 jobs (`09:48` for `B03`, `10:28` for `B04`).
- **Counterfactual scenario:** At `10:28`, suppose `B04` (P3) had arrived at **`10:02`** (waiting 26 min) and `B06` (P2) arrived at **`10:05`** (waiting 23 min).
  - **Prediction under H1 (P1-preemptive + FIFO):** Selects **`B04`** (`10:02 < 10:05`).
  - **Prediction under H2 (Priority + $>30\text{ min}$ aging):** Selects **`B06`** (P2 > un-aged P3).
- **Discriminating observable:** **`effective_priority` / `aging_score`** computed by the scheduler at `10:28`.

# 5. Operational exceptions and alternative explanations

Ranked from **most plausible (1)** to **least plausible (5)** based on direct log evidence:

1. **Unrecorded setup, safe-stop preemption lag, and pre-unavailability buffers (High plausibility)**
   - *Evidence:* `QA` shows 2-min start lags (`08:00–08:02`, `08:30–08:32`); `QB` shows identical 2-min lags between P1 arrivals (`09:14`, `09:46`) and `B01` stops (`09:16`, `09:48`); `QD` idles `D-X` (`11:20–11:30`) before `D06`'s appointment and `D-F` (`11:36–12:00`) before its meeting.
   - *Diagnostic impact:* Explains idle intervals in `QD` and `QA` without inventing complex routing rules—if `D-F` actually entered pre-meeting prep at `11:45` (leaving 9 min at 11:36), `D10` (est 12) was *genuinely infeasible* on `D-F`, eliminating the main contradiction against work-conserving Skill+FIFO in `QD`.

2. **Ad-hoc human dispatcher overrides and batch staging lock-in (High plausibility)**
   - *Evidence:* Explicitly documented in `QB` (`"second stop: dispatcher reassignment"` at 09:48 where P2 `B03` jumps P1 `B05`) and strongly implied in `QC` (`C04` beating 6-min `C06` at 10:32).
   - *Diagnostic impact:* Shifts `QB` and `QC` away from clean closed-form hybrid algorithms toward *loosely governed base rules* (FIFO in `QB`, SPT in `QC`) periodically overridden by human dispatchers managing complaints or pre-staged kitting carts.

3. **Delayed arrival scans up to 2 minutes (Moderate-high plausibility; confirmed system property)**
   - *Evidence:* Stated in prompt. Critically affects boundary visibility at `10:10` in `QC` (`C05` scanned `10:11` missed the `10:10` dispatch—the only slot where its 40-min work could meet its `10:50` deadline).
   - *Diagnostic impact:* Does *not* overturn any FIFO inversion in `QA`, `QB`, or `QD` (where inverted arrivals are separated by $7–30\text{ min} \gg 2\text{ min}$), but rescues `QC` from appearing to ignore an imminently failing deadline at `10:10`.

4. **Retrospective backfilling or time-boxing of `est_min` (Moderate plausibility; data-quality anomaly)**
   - *Evidence:* Across all 32 jobs in 4 queues—including twice-preempted `B01` ($14+17+14=45$)—realized active duration equals `est_min` to the exact minute (zero variance, zero resumption rework time).
   - *Diagnostic impact:* If `est_min` in this extract was overwritten upon completion with realized duration (a common ERP/MES logging bug), then dispatchers at arrival may have seen *different* estimates! Apparent SPT violations in `QC` (e.g., `C04` vs `C06` at 10:32, or `C01` vs `C07` at 11:40) could actually have been pure SPT under the *true* arrival-time estimates.

5. **Dynamic policy switching under queue congestion / SLA state (Moderate-low plausibility)**
   - *Evidence:* `QC` runs SPT (`10:10–10:54`), switches to deadline/P1 recovery (`10:54` for `C05`), returns to SPT (`11:34` for `C06`), then clears oldest backlog (`11:40` for `C01`, abandoning `C07`'s 12:00 deadline).
   - *Diagnostic impact:* If `QC` formally toggles between *Normal Mode (SPT)* and *Recovery Mode (FIFO/Urgency)*, inferring a single stationary rule mischaracterizes queue behavior under load.
