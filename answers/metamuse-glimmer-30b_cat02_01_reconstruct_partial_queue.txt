# 1. Assumptions and notation
A1 Event order convention. Reported times are nominal. With rounding to the nearest 5 min an actual time t_reported satisfies t_actual  [t_reported-2.5min, t_reported+2.5min]. For ordering we use the reported times and treat simultaneous reported times as potentially overlapping. Tie break: start before end on the same resource, otherwise order is indeterminate within ±5 min.

A2 Capacity. One case per resource at a time, no pre-emption. S1 resources I1,I2; S2 resources C1,C2. C2 unavailable 10:55–11:10.

A3 Queue semantics. S1 waiting = s1_start – arrival_s1. S2 waiting = s2_start – s1_end. Station sojourn = waiting + service. Service = end – start. Total sojourn for completed cases = s2_end – arrival_s1.

A4 Missing start. MISSING means activity occurred, resource and completion known, start not recorded. Start is bounded by max(arrival or previous activity completion on same resource, s1_end for S2)  start  end and by the next activity on the same resource. Service 0.

A5 Censoring. OPEN means started but not completed by 14:00. Elapsed service = 14:00 – start, final service  elapsed. Blank start/end means waiting at 14:00.

A6 Rounding. All interval bounds are reported ±2.5 min. Central estimates use reported times as point estimates.

Notation: W = waiting, Svc = service, WIP = work in process.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas
Waiting
S1_W = s1_start – arrival_s1
S2_W = s2_start – s1_end

Service
S1_Svc = s1_end – s1_start
S2_Svc = s2_end – s2_start

Station sojourn
S1_soj = S1_W + S1_Svc = s1_end – arrival_s1
S2_soj = S2_W + S2_Svc = s2_end – s1_end

Case total sojourn completed
Tot = s2_end – arrival_s1

Throughput
Arrivals per subperiod = count(arrival_s1 in period)
Departures per subperiod = count(s2_end completed in period)

WIP bounds
WIP(t)  number of cases with s1_start  t < s1_end or s1_end  t < s2_start or s2_start  t < s2_end, accounting for rounding intervals.

Utilization
Busy_time_resource =  (end – start) over activities assigned to resource intersected with observation window 08:00–14:00.

Afternoon 12:00–14:00: S1 waiting remains 95–105 min, S2 waiting 45–75 min and three cases are waiting for S2 at 14:00. S1 queue is the dominant contributor to total sojourn; S2 is busy but its waiting is largely inherited from S1 backlog.

# --- 3. Station metrics with pandas, uncertainty scenarios ---
