## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Parallel Branch Clocks (Pre-Join)

#### 1. Chemistry Waiting and Service
* **Current state (Fri 15:30):** Two analysts (A1, A2) have $2.5\text{ h}$ of shift remaining until 18:00. A1 is busy for $1.2\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$ (free Fri $16:50\text{–}17:30$); A2 is busy for $0.4\text{–}0.9\text{ h}$ (free Fri $15:54\text{–}16:24$). Queue has 1 urgent ($U_1$) and 3 ordinary ($O_1, O_2, O_3$) samples ahead of B-417.
* **Sub-case 1a: Risk-Priority Discipline (45% prob):**
  * B-417 jumps ahead of $O_1\text{–}O_3$. $U_1$ takes A2 at $15:54\text{–}16:24$. B-417 takes A1 at $16:50\text{–}17:30$.
  * B-417 completes $0.5\text{–}1.1\text{ h}$ of service on Friday before the 18:00 weekend shutdown ($62\text{ h}$ pause).
  * **Resume Monday 08:00:** Remaining service is $(1.5\text{ to }4.5\text{ h}) - (0.5\text{ to }1.1\text{ h}) \approx 0.4\text{–}4.0\text{ h}$ (central $1.5\text{–}2.2\text{ h}$).
  * **Smooth Completion ($C_{\text{chem}}$):** Monday **08:25 – 12:00** (central **09:30 – 10:15**).
  * **With Borderline Rerun ($+1.0\text{ to }3.0\text{ h}$, mode $1.8\text{ h}$):** Monday **09:45 – 15:00** (central **11:30 – 12:30**).
* **Sub-case 1b: FIFO Discipline (55% prob):**
  * B-417 is 5th in line. Total work ahead of B-417: remaining current jobs ($1.6\text{–}2.9\text{ analyst-hours}$) + 4 waiting jobs ($4 \times \text{Tri}(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) \approx 7.5\text{–}15.5\text{ analyst-hours}$, central $11.3\text{ h}$) $= 9.1\text{–}18.4\text{ analyst-hours}$ (central $13.6\text{ h}$).
  * Two analysts provide $5.0\text{ analyst-hours}$ on Friday ($15:30\text{–}18:00$). Unfinished queue work carried to Monday 08:00 is $4.1\text{–}13.4\text{ analyst-hours}$ (central $8.6\text{ h}$), requiring $2.0\text{–}6.7\text{ clock-hours}$ (central $4.3\text{ h}$) with 2 analysts on Monday.
  * **B-417 Service Start:** Monday **10:00 – 14:40** (central **12:15**).
  * **Smooth Completion ($C_{\text{chem}}$):** Adding $\text{Tri}(1.5, 2.5, 4.5)\text{ h}$ gives Monday **12:00 – 18:00**, or spilling over the Monday 18:00 close to **Tuesday 08:00 – 09:15** (central **Monday 14:30 – 15:30**).
  * **With Borderline Rerun:** Adding $\text{Tri}(1.0, 1.8, 3.0)\text{ h}$ pushes completion past Monday 18:00 in roughly half of FIFO cases, finishing **Monday 14:00 – Tuesday 11:30** (central **Monday 16:45 – Tuesday 08:30**).

#### 2. Microbiology Incubation and Readout
* **Primary Incubation:** Starts Friday 14:00, runs continuously for $46\text{–}54\text{ h}$, becoming technically ready **Sunday 12:00 – 20:00** (central **Sunday 16:00**).
* **Sub-case 2a: On-Call Weekend Readout Authorized (25–45% prob):**
  * Dispatch and readout take $1.0\text{–}3.0\text{ h}$ after technical readiness.
  * **Smooth Completion ($C_{\text{micro}}$):** **Sunday 13:00 – 23:00** (central **Sunday 18:00**).
* **Sub-case 2b: No On-Call Authorization / Routine Readout (55–75% prob):**
  * Waits for routine staffing at Monday 08:00. Readout takes $0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$.
  * **Smooth Completion ($C_{\text{micro}}$):** **Monday 08:30 – 09:30** (central **Monday 09:00**).
* **Sub-case 2c: Contamination Confirmation Required (5–12% prob):**
  * After primary readout (Sunday afternoon or Monday 08:30–09:30), an additional $20\text{–}30\text{ h}$ continuous incubation begins, finishing **Monday 09:00 – Tuesday 15:30** (central **Monday 20:00 – Tuesday 11:00**).
  * Second readout ($0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$ during Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00 staffing; if incubation finishes Monday night after 18:00, readout waits until Tuesday 08:00) completes ($C_{\text{micro}}$) **Monday 10:00 – Tuesday 17:00** (central **Tuesday 08:30 – 12:30**).

#### 3. Parallel Join Time ($T_{\text{join}} = \max(C_{\text{chem}}, C_{\text{micro}}, \text{QA Open})$)
Because QA can only start when *both* branches have usable results *and* QA is open (Monday 08:00 onward, since Sunday has no QA hours), the join is governed by the maximum operator, not a sum:
* **Scenario A (Smooth Path):**
  * Under *Risk-Priority Chem* ($\text{Mon } 08:25\text{–}12:00$) and *Routine/On-Call Micro* ($\text{Sun } 13:00\text{–}\text{Mon } 09:30$): $T_{\text{join}} = \max(\text{Mon } 08:25\text{–}12:00, \text{Sun } 13:00\text{–}\text{Mon } 09:30) =$ **Monday 08:30 – 12:00** (central **09:30 – 10:15**). Note that Sunday on-call micro only saves time if Chemistry gets Risk-Priority *and* finishes before Monday 09:30 (a narrow $30\text{–}60\text{ min}$ gain).
  * Under *FIFO Chem* ($\text{Mon } 12:00\text{–}\text{Tue } 09:15$): Chemistry strictly dominates Microbiology. $T_{\text{join}} =$ **Monday 12:00 – Tuesday 09:15** (central **Monday 14:30 – 15:30**).
* **Scenario B (Chemistry Rerun):** Chemistry strictly dominates ($C_{\text{chem}} > C_{\text{micro}}$). $T_{\text{join}} =$ **Monday 09:45 – Tuesday 11:30** (central **Monday 12:00 – 17:00**).
* **Scenario C (Microbiology Confirmation):** Microbiology strictly dominates ($C_{\text{micro}} > C_{\text{chem}}$) in $>90\%$ of cases. $T_{\text{join}} =$ **Monday 11:00 – Tuesday 17:00** (central **Tuesday 08:30 – 12:30**).

### Post-Join Clocks (QA Review, Rework, and Release)

#### 4. QA Waiting and Initial Service
* **Queue at Join Time:** Between Friday 15:30 and Monday 08:00, QA works $4.5\text{ h}$ (Fri $15:30\text{–}20:00$) + $4.0\text{ h}$ (Sat $09:00\text{–}13:00$) $= 8.5\text{ reviewer-hours}$. At median service $1.5\text{ h}$ ($P_{80} = 3.5\text{ h}$, mean $\approx 2.2\text{ h}$), the single reviewer clears $2\text{–}5$ of the 5 initial batches. Meanwhile, $8.5\text{ h} \times 0.35\text{ arrivals/h} \approx 3$ new batches arrive (range $0\text{–}7$ due to overdispersion). Thus, on Monday at 08:00, the QA queue holds **$1\text{–}6$ batches** (central **$3\text{–}4$ batches**).
* **Waiting Time ($W_{\text{QA}}$):** From Monday 08:00, **two** QA reviewers are active (effective clearing rate $\approx 1\text{ batch every } 0.75\text{–}1.1\text{ h}$, net of $0.35/\text{h}$ new arrivals).
  * Best case (1 batch ahead or cleared before join): $W_{\text{QA}} \approx 0.0\text{–}0.5\text{ h}$.
  * Central case ($3\text{–}4$ batches ahead): $W_{\text{QA}} \approx 1.5\text{–}3.5\text{ h}$.
  * High-congestion tail ($6+$ batches or heavy-tailed reviews ahead): $W_{\text{QA}} \approx 4.0\text{–}7.5\text{ h}$.
* **Initial QA Service ($S_{\text{QA}}$):** $0.8\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$ (fast/median), $1.5\text{–}3.5\text{ h}$ (central to $P_{80}$), $3.5\text{–}6.0\text{ h}$ (tail).
* **Total Initial QA Elapsed Time ($W_{\text{QA}} + S_{\text{QA}}$):** **$1.0\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$** (best), **$3.0\text{–}6.0\text{ h}$** (central), **$6.5\text{–}12.0\text{ h}$** (tail; if exceeding Monday 20:00 close, pauses $12\text{ h}$ overnight to Tuesday 08:00).

#### 5. Optional Rework / Investigation (Scenario D)
* **Documentation Query (15–30% prob):** Triggered during initial QA service. Adds $4\text{–}12\text{ working hours}$ (QA calendar: $12\text{ h/day}$ Mon–Fri) plus $0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$ QA re-review. Because a Monday afternoon query ($4\text{–}12\text{ working hours}$) hits the 20:00 QA close, it incurs at least one $12\text{ h}$ overnight pause, adding **$16\text{–}26\text{ calendar hours}$** (finishing **Tuesday morning to Wednesday afternoon**).
* **Deviation Investigation — Documentation-Only (60–80% of investigations):** Adds $8\text{–}20\text{ working hours}$ ($0.7\text{–}1.7\text{ QA working days}$) + $1.0\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$ QA sign-off. Across overnight pauses, this adds **$20\text{–}45\text{ calendar hours}$**, pushing completion to **Tuesday afternoon – Thursday afternoon**.
* **Deviation Investigation — Laboratory Work (20–40% of investigations):** Adds $3\text{–}7\text{ calendar days}$ ($72\text{–}168\text{ h}$) + $1.0\text{–}2.5\text{ h}$ QA sign-off (and weekend lab/QA pauses if the $3\text{–}7\text{ days}$ end on Sat/Sun). Pushes completion to **Friday 25 Sep – Wednesday 30 Sep**.

#### 6. Final Release or Rejection
* **Electronic Release (24/7):** Adds **$0.25\text{–}0.75\text{ h}$** immediately upon QA acceptance.
* **Batch Rejection:** If contamination or lab investigation confirms a critical defect (estimated **2% – 6%** overall probability), adds **$0.5\text{–}1.0\text{ h}$** after QA/investigation closure.

1. **Deviation Investigation Trigger & Type (Doc-Only vs. Lab Investigation) — *Path Uncertainty***
   * *Impact:* Adds $+8\text{ to }20\text{ working hours}$ (1–2 calendar days) for documentation-only, or $+72\text{ to }168\text{ calendar hours}$ (3–7 days) for laboratory work. This single branching point drives almost the entire difference between a 3-day turnaround and an 11-day turnaround.
2. **Upstream Temperature-Excursion Flag Resolution — *Dependence & Observation Uncertainty***
   * *Impact:* Currently unobserved ("unknown" due to sensor gap, 20–40% prob of confirmation). If confirmed, it induces positive correlation across branches: simultaneously raising borderline chemistry rerun risk, microbiology contamination risk, and jumping the QA Deviation Investigation probability from $4\text{–}10\%$ to $30\text{–}55\%$.
3. **Microbiology Contamination Indication & Confirmation Incubation — *Path & Service-Time Uncertainty***
   * *Impact:* A $5\text{–}12\%$ probability event that adds $20\text{–}30\text{ h}$ of continuous incubation plus a second readout (and overnight staffing pauses), shifting the parallel join from Monday morning/afternoon to Tuesday morning/afternoon and likely triggering a QA investigation.
4. **Chemistry Queue Discipline (FIFO vs. Risk-Priority) & Rerun Spillover — *Queue & Calendar Uncertainty***
   * *Impact:* Because Friday only has $2.5\text{ h}$ of lab time left, Risk-Priority allows B-417 to start Friday and finish Monday morning ($\sim 09:30$), whereas FIFO puts 4 batches ahead of B-417, pushing Chemistry completion to Monday afternoon ($\sim 15:00$) or Tuesday morning (if a rerun occurs). Under FIFO, Chemistry overtakes Microbiology as the dominant pre-join clock.
5. **QA Documentation Query & Monday Queue Congestion — *Path & Queue Uncertainty***

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

### Primary Update Trigger: Resolution of the Upstream Temperature-Excursion Flag (or Primary Microbiology Readout if the flag is resolved late)
While the **Primary Microbiology Readout** (occurring Sunday afternoon or Monday 08:30–09:30) is the most decisive *normative process event*, **engineering resolution of the unknown temperature-excursion sensor gap** is the single observation that can happen *earliest* and shifts the entire joint probability distribution across both lab branches and QA:
* **If the excursion is CONFIRMED (20–40% prior):**
  * **Direction of update:** Severe upward shift in remaining time (median shifts from **Monday evening [+74 h]** to **Wednesday/Thursday [+115 to +140 h]**, and the P90 tail expands toward **8–11 days**).
  * **Mechanism:** Immediately updates the QA Deviation Investigation probability to $30\text{–}55\%$ *even before lab tests finish*, while simultaneously increasing the conditional probabilities of a Chemistry rerun and a Microbiology contamination confirmation. It also substantially increases the probability that the terminal event is **Batch Rejected**.
* **If the excursion is RULED OUT / NORMAL (60–80% prior):**
  * **Direction of update:** Sharp compression of the right tail and modest reduction in the median (P90 shrinks by $2\text{–}4\text{ days}$).
  * **Mechanism:** Drops the baseline Deviation Investigation probability to $4\text{–}10\%$ (unless an independent lab anomaly occurs) and keeps Chemistry rerun and Microbiology contamination probabilities near their lower bounds ($\sim 10\%$ and $\sim 5\%$, respectively).

### Secondary Update Trigger: Chemistry Service Start Timestamp on Friday Afternoon (between 15:54 and 17:30)
* **Why it matters:** Observing whether B-417 enters Chemistry service before the Friday 18:00 close immediately reveals the **queue discipline** in effect:
  * **If B-417 starts Friday (Risk-Priority confirmed):** Eliminates $4\text{–}6\text{ hours}$ of Monday queueing delay, ensuring Chemistry finishes Monday morning ($\sim 09:30\text{–}10:30$ if no rerun) and making a Sunday on-call microbiology authorization actually useful for achieving an early Monday morning QA join.
  * **If B-417 does NOT start by Friday 18:00 (FIFO confirmed):** Guarantees that Chemistry will not finish until Monday afternoon ($12:00\text{–}18:00$) or Tuesday morning. This renders a Sunday on-call microbiology readout completely redundant (since QA will have to wait for Chemistry on Monday afternoon anyway) and shifts the smooth-path completion from Monday afternoon to Monday night / Tuesday morning.
