# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The authoritative artifact is the POWL object `source_powl`. Everything below is read off the partial order and the two operator nodes, not off any rendering.

Notation for witnesses: traces list **visible** labels only. `R`=Register Change, `A`=Assess Risk, `P`=Prepare Evidence, `L`=Legal Review, `C`=Configure Trial, `I`=Inspect Trial, `D`=Correct Defect, `RE`=Release Change, `AR`=Archive Change.

One subtlety that governs the whole audit: a `StrictPartialOrder` is an *unfolding* semantics — a node is enabled once all its predecessors have **completed**, and unordered nodes are genuinely concurrent (their executions may interleave arbitrarily, and for composite nodes may interleave *internally*, not only as atomic blocks). This is the strongest claim in the source and the one most at risk in conversion.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | `Register Change` is the unique start; nothing visible precedes it | `R,A,P,C,I,RE,AR` | `A,R,P,C,I,RE,AR` | Yes |
| I2 | `Assess Risk` after `Register Change` | `R,A,...` | any trace with `A` before `R` | Yes |
| I3 | `Prepare Evidence` after `Register Change` | `R,P,...` | any trace with `P` before `R` | Yes |
| I4 | `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` are **unordered**: both orders are legal | `R,A,P,...` and `R,P,A,...` | a model admitting only one of the two | Yes (both orders must be replayable) |
| I5 | `Legal Review` is optional (XOR with a silent transition) | `R,A,P,C,I,RE,AR` (skipped) | a model that forces `L` in every trace | Partly: skip is only observable as *absence* of `L`, i.e. by the existence of a complete trace without `L` |
| I6 | If executed, `Legal Review` occurs after `Assess Risk` | `R,A,L,P,C,I,RE,AR` | `R,L,A,...` | Yes |
| I7 | Verification requires **both** `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` complete (AND-join) | `R,A,P,C,...` | `R,A,C,I,P,RE,AR` (verification started with `P` pending) | Yes |
| I8 | The optional-legal branch and the verification loop are **unordered** and may interleave | `R,A,P,C,L,I,RE,AR` and `R,A,L,P,C,I,RE,AR` | a model where `L` must precede `C` or must follow `I` | Yes (interleaving witness must replay) |
| I9 | Every iteration executes `Configure Trial` before `Inspect Trial` | `R,A,P,C,I,RE,AR` | `R,A,P,I,C,RE,AR` | Yes |
| I10 | Loop scope is exactly `{Configure Trial, Inspect Trial}` as the do-part; `Correct Defect` is the redo-part | `R,A,P,C,I,D,C,I,RE,AR` | `R,A,P,C,I,D,I,RE,AR`; also `...,D,` immediately before `RE` | Yes |
| I11 | At least one iteration occurs (`LOOP` in PM4Py is `*(do, redo)` = do·(redo·do)*) | every complete trace contains `C,I` | a complete trace with no `C`/`I` | Yes |
| I12 | Loop exit is always after a `do` execution, never after `redo` | `...C,I,D,C,I,RE...` | `...C,I,D,RE...` | Yes |
| I13 | `Release Change` synchronizes: it waits for **both** the optional-legal node and the loop node | `...L,...,I,RE,...` | `R,A,L,RE,...` (release before verification) or `R,A,P,C,I,RE,L,AR` (release before legal branch resolved) | Yes for the verification side; the legal side is only weakly observable when the silent branch is taken |
| I14 | `Archive Change` occurs after `Release Change` and is the unique last visible activity | `...,RE,AR` | `...,AR,RE` | Yes |
| I15 | Proper completion: exactly one final marking, reached after `Archive Change`; no visible activity may fire afterwards | `R,A,P,C,I,RE,AR` reaches `fm` exactly | any trace where `fm` is reached with tokens elsewhere, or where `AR` can repeat | Yes, but only via exact final-marking equality, not via label sequence |
| I16 | All visible labels are pairwise distinct (7 labels, 7 visible transitions) | label multiset has no duplicates | a converted net with two transitions labelled `Legal Review` | Yes — but a converter is free to duplicate transitions, which breaks label-based identity even when behavior is preserved |

Invariants I4, I8 and I13 (legal side) are the fragile ones. I5 and I13 depend on silent transitions, so after tau-hiding they degrade from "structural" to "existential" claims.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 (primary)** POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` → `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Only the officially supported POWL export path; the Petri net is a full-behavior artifact preserving true concurrency; BPMN is generated from a marked net so the marking is respected | Petri→BPMN introduces routing nodes and silent tasks; concurrency is rendered via gateways that may not be block-structured; tau-labelled transitions become unlabelled BPMN tasks/gateways that visually suggest work | Petri net is not a workflow net; `check_soundness` fails; BPMN conversion raises or returns a model whose re-conversion has a null/ambiguous marking |
| **P2 (secondary, allowed to fail)** POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net` → `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` (may raise) → `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | If it succeeds, yields a block-structured, trivially sound, cleanly renderable model with a canonical BPMN layout | The source is **not** block-structured: the partial order `A→{legal, loop}`, `P→loop`, `{legal, loop}→RE` is a non-series-parallel dependency pattern (`A` feeds two successors, `P` feeds only one, and both successors re-join). Reduction to a process tree must either fail or *approximate* by adding order | `convert_to_process_tree` raises (typically `Exception("The Petri net cannot be converted into a process tree")`); or it "succeeds" only after an implicit relaxation, in which case the tree admits or forbids traces the source does not |
| **P3 (control/reference)** POWL → Petri net → BPMN → Petri net (round trip) | `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)` | The only way to test whether BPMN export is information-preserving rather than merely renderable | Round-tripped net has different place/transition identities, extra taus, possibly different marking cardinality; naive structural comparison is meaningless | Round-tripped net is unsound, or the bounded visible-language comparison against `net_from_powl` diverges, or its `im`/`fm` are empty |
| **P4 (rejected)** direct POWL → BPMN | — | none | **No such API is provided.** Inventing `convert_powl_to_bpmn` would be fabrication | n/a — not attempted |

**Primary pipeline: P1**, with P3 as its mandatory validation harness. P2 is executed as a *diagnostic probe*: its failure is informative evidence that the source is not block-structured, and its success would require the extra scrutiny described in §4.

# 3. Conversion Code

```python
"""
POWL -> Petri net -> BPMN conversion with an explicit semantic-loss audit.

Nothing here assumes a conversion is faithful. Every produced accepting net is
diagnosed; every marking is checked for being non-null before use.
"""

import os
import traceback

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

OUT = "conversion_audit"
os.makedirs(OUT, exist_ok=True)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 0. Source model (authoritative)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def build_source_powl():
    register = Transition(label="Register Change")
    assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
    prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

    legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    skip_legal = SilentTransition()
    optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
    )

    configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
    inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
    trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
    trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

    correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
    verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
    )

    release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
    archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

    powl = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            register,
            assess_risk,
            prepare_evidence,
            optional_legal,
            verification_loop,
            release_change,
            archive_change,
        ]
    )
    powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
    powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
    powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
    powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
    powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
    powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
    powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
    powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)
    return powl


source_powl = build_source_powl()


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Diagnostics helper -- applied to EVERY accepting net we ever hold
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def diagnose_net(name, net, im, fm):
    """Return a dict of diagnostics. Never assumes im/fm are usable."""
    report = {
        "name": name,
        "places": len(net.places) if net is not None else None,
        "transitions": len(net.transitions) if net is not None else None,
        "silent_transitions": None,
        "visible_labels": None,
        "duplicate_labels": None,
        "im_ok": bool(im is not None and len(im) > 0),
        "fm_ok": bool(fm is not None and len(fm) > 0),
        "is_workflow_net": None,
        "sound": None,
        "error": None,
    }
    if net is None:
        report["error"] = "net is None"
        return report

    labels = [t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None]
    report["silent_transitions"] = sum(1 for t in net.transitions if t.label is None)
    report["visible_labels"] = sorted(set(labels))
    report["duplicate_labels"] = sorted(
        {lab for lab in labels if labels.count(lab) > 1}
    )

    try:
        report["is_workflow_net"] = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    except Exception as exc:                      # noqa: BLE001 - diagnostic only
        report["error"] = f"check_is_workflow_net: {exc}"

    if not (report["im_ok"] and report["fm_ok"]):
        # Soundness is undefined without both markings; do not fabricate them.
        report["sound"] = "not tested (null or empty marking)"
        return report

    try:
        report["sound"] = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
    except Exception as exc:                      # noqa: BLE001 - diagnostic only
        report["sound"] = f"error: {exc}"
    return report


def print_report(report):
    print(f"\n=== diagnostics: {report['name']} ===")
    for key, value in report.items():
        if key != "name":
            print(f"  {key}: {value}")


REPORTS = {}
ARTIFACTS = {}


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Pipeline P1: POWL -> accepting Petri net -> BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)
REPORTS["net_from_powl"] = diagnose_net(
    "net_from_powl (POWL -> Petri)", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
)
print_report(REPORTS["net_from_powl"])

bpmn_from_net = None
try:
    bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
    ARTIFACTS["bpmn_from_net"] = bpmn_from_net
except Exception as exc:                          # noqa: BLE001
    print("[P1] Petri -> BPMN FAILED:", exc)
    traceback.print_exc()


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Pipeline P2 (probe, allowed to fail):
#    POWL -> Petri -> process tree -> BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

tree_from_net = None
tree_conversion_error = None
try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    print("[P2] Petri -> process tree SUCCEEDED:", tree_from_net)
except Exception as exc:                          # noqa: BLE001 - expected outcome
    tree_conversion_error = exc
    print(
        "[P2] Petri -> process tree FAILED (this is an informative, not fatal, "
        "outcome): "
        f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"
    )
    traceback.print_exc()

bpmn_from_tree = None
if tree_from_net is not None:
    try:
        bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
        ARTIFACTS["bpmn_from_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
    except Exception as exc:                      # noqa: BLE001
        print("[P2] tree -> BPMN FAILED:", exc)
        traceback.print_exc()


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Pipeline P3: BPMN -> accepting Petri net (round trip) for every BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

ROUND_TRIPPED = {}   # name -> (net, im, fm)

for bpmn_name, bpmn_obj in ARTIFACTS.items():
    try:
        rt_net, rt_im, rt_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_obj)
    except Exception as exc:                      # noqa: BLE001
        print(f"[P3] {bpmn_name} -> Petri FAILED: {exc}")
        traceback.print_exc()
        continue
    key = f"net_from_{bpmn_name}"
    ROUND_TRIPPED[key] = (rt_net, rt_im, rt_fm)
    REPORTS[key] = diagnose_net(key, rt_net, rt_im, rt_fm)
    print_report(REPORTS[key])

# The process tree, if it exists, is itself convertible to a marked net.
if tree_from_net is not None:
    try:
        t_net, t_im, t_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net)
        ROUND_TRIPPED["net_from_tree"] = (t_net, t_im, t_fm)
        REPORTS["net_from_tree"] = diagnose_net("net_from_tree", t_net, t_im, t_fm)
        print_report(REPORTS["net_from_tree"])
    except Exception as exc:                      # noqa: BLE001
        print("[P2] tree -> Petri FAILED:", exc)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. Visualizations -- filenames state provenance, not just content
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def safe_save(fn, *args, **kwargs):
    try:
        fn(*args, **kwargs)
    except Exception as exc:                      # noqa: BLE001
        print(f"[viz] {fn.__name__} failed: {exc}")


safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_powl, source_powl,
          os.path.join(OUT, "00_source_powl_AUTHORITATIVE.png"))
safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl,
          os.path.join(OUT, "01_net_from_powl.png"))
if bpmn_from_net is not None:
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, bpmn_from_net,
              os.path.join(OUT, "02_bpmn_from_net_DERIVATIVE.png"))
if tree_from_net is not None:
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_process_tree, tree_from_net,
              os.path.join(OUT, "03_tree_from_net_PROBE.png"))
if bpmn_from_tree is not None:
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, bpmn_from_tree,
              os.path.join(OUT, "04_bpmn_from_tree_DERIVATIVE.png"))
for key, (n, i, f) in ROUND_TRIPPED.items():
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, n, i, f,
              os.path.join(OUT, f"05_{key}_ROUNDTRIP.png"))
```

Notes on the code as written:

- No `convert_powl_to_bpmn` is used; POWL leaves the POWL world only through `convert_to_petri_net`.
- Every `im`/`fm` pair is checked with `len(...) > 0` before soundness is attempted; a null or empty marking downgrades the verdict to `not tested` rather than silently passing.
- The process-tree exception is caught, printed with its type, and recorded — not swallowed. Downstream steps are guarded by `if tree_from_net is not None`.
- Duplicate-label detection is part of the standard diagnostic, because label duplication is the main threat to invariant I16.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

Assessment terms are restricted to: `expected preserved`, `validated preserved`, `approximated`, `ambiguous`, `lost`, `conversion failed`, `not tested`.

"`validated preserved`" is used only where the round-trip harness in §5 actually decides the question (bounded visible-language equality plus exact final-marking equality). Where the harness is bounded and the property is existential over an unbounded space, the honest term is `expected preserved`.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 unique start | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | `check_is_workflow_net` on both nets + single-place `im` |
| I2/I3 `R` precedes `A`,`P` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | negative witnesses `A,R,...` rejected by bounded replay |
| I4 `A` ‖ `P` (both orders) | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | both `R,A,P,...` and `R,P,A,...` replay to exact `fm` |
| I5 optional legal (silent skip) | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | validated preserved *as language*, `ambiguous` *as structure* | Language: a complete trace without `L` exists in both nets. Structure: BPMN renders the skip as an unlabelled element, so "optional" is only inferable, not stated |
| I6 `L` after `A` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | negative witness `R,L,A,...` rejected |
| I7 AND-join before verification | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | negative witness `R,A,C,I,P,RE,AR` rejected |
| I8 legal ‖ loop, may interleave | expected preserved | **ambiguous** | conversion failed | validated preserved (language) / ambiguous (visual) | Interleaving witness `R,A,P,C,L,I,RE,AR` must replay. If it does not, the pipeline is **rejected** (§7). Visually, BPMN gateway layout tends to draw the two branches as sequential-looking lanes |
| I9 `C` before `I` per iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | negative witness `...I,C...` rejected |
| I10 loop scope `{C,I}` / redo `D` | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | validated preserved up to bound | `...,D,I,...` and `...,D,RE` rejected; exhaustive only up to 3 iterations |
| I11 ≥1 iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | no complete trace without `C,I` found within the bound |
| I12 exit only after `do` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved up to bound | trace `...C,I,D,RE,AR` must be rejected |
| I13 join before `RE` | expected preserved | expected preserved (verification side) / approximated (legal side) | conversion failed | validated preserved for the verification side; `ambiguous` for the legal side when the tau branch fires | With the silent skip, "waiting for the legal branch" is unobservable in the visible language; only the reachability graph / marking evidence distinguishes it |
| I14 `AR` after `RE`, last | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | negative witness `AR,RE` rejected; no visible transition enabled at `fm` |
| I15 proper completion / exact `fm` | expected preserved | **ambiguous until checked** | conversion failed | validated preserved *if* `rt_im`/`rt_fm` non-empty and `check_soundness` true | BPMN→Petri may return markings that are empty or that encode start/end events differently; this is exactly why the code tests `len(im)>0` |
| I16 label uniqueness | expected preserved | **lost or ambiguous** | conversion failed | ambiguous | Petri→BPMN commonly duplicates routing structure and may emit multiple unlabelled tasks; and any structural unfolding of the loop can duplicate `Configure Trial`. Language equality survives duplication; *identity-based* reasoning does not |

## Required discussion points

**True concurrency vs. arbitrary interleaving.** The POWL partial order asserts *absence of order*. A Petri net with an AND-split expresses the same thing directly, so P1 preserves it structurally. But every validation technique available here — footprints, reachability graphs, trace replay — observes only *interleavings*. Two models can have identical interleaving languages and different concurrency structure (e.g. a genuine AND-split vs. an explicit XOR over both orderings). So the audit can confirm that both orders are *possible*, never that they are *causally independent*. For I4 and I8 the honest ceiling is "interleaving-equivalent", not "concurrency-equivalent".

**The missing edge between Optional Legal and verification.** This is an *absence* in the source, and absences are the hardest thing to preserve. Any converter that serializes the two — or any downstream tree approximation that wraps them in a `->` — will still produce a model that replays the "legal first" and "verification first" traces if it happens to place them in the right order, and will only be caught by the interleaved witness `R,A,P,C,L,I,RE,AR` where `L` occurs *strictly between* `C` and `I`. That witness is therefore a mandatory test, not an optional one. Without it, a serialized approximation passes.

**Silent optional behavior.** The XOR-with-tau is the mechanism for optionality. After tau-hiding, "optional" degrades to "there exists a complete trace lacking `L`". That is testable, and it is what §5 tests. What is *not* recoverable is the modeler's intent that the skip is a deliberate branch rather than, say, an invisible-but-mandatory step that happens to be unlabelled. In BPMN the tau becomes either an unlabelled task or a routing node; a reader may misread it as work.

**Binary-loop semantics.** PM4Py's `LOOP(do, redo)` means `do · (redo · do)*` — at least one `do`, exit only after a `do`. This is *not* the same as a BPMN "repeat until" drawn as a back-edge from a gateway, which a reader may interpret as zero-or-more. Preservation of I11 and I12 must be tested by explicit negative witnesses (`RE` with no `C,I`; `...D,RE`), because both nets will look identical in a rendering.

**The join before Release Change.** `RE` has two predecessors, so the net gets an AND-join. On the verification side this is directly observable. On the legal side, when the silent branch fires, the synchronization consumes a token produced by a tau — invisible in the trace language. So I13 is only half-validated by language comparison; the other half rests on the structural claim that the round-tripped net is sound and has the same reachable marking structure.

**Activity-label uniqueness.** The source has seven distinct visible labels and seven visible transitions. Conversions may duplicate transitions (structural unfolding, gateway expansion, tree-to-net translation). Duplication does not change the language but does break any downstream analysis keyed on label→node identity (e.g. attaching performance data, or diffing models). Treat `duplicate_labels` non-empty as a quarantine trigger for storage, not necessarily for behavior.

**Initial and final markings.** POWL→Petri yields a genuine accepting net with a single source and single sink. BPMN→Petri reconstructs markings from start/end events; if the BPMN has no explicit end event, `fm` may come back empty. An empty `fm` makes soundness undefined and makes "completed trace" untestable — hence the `im_ok`/`fm_ok` guards and the `not tested` verdict rather than a pass.

**Gateway / routing-node ambiguity in BPMN.** The generated BPMN will contain unlabelled tasks or gateways originating from taus, plus gateways whose *type* (exclusive vs. parallel) is the only carrier of I4/I8. If the converter emits a generic routing node, or if a reader confuses a diamond with an X for a diamond with a `+`, the concurrency claim is silently inverted. This is a *reading* risk with no behavioral counterpart — see §6.

**Petri→Tree column.** Every row is marked `conversion failed` under the expectation established in §2: the dependency pattern `A→{legal, loop}`, `P→loop`, `{legal, loop}→RE` is not series-parallel, so no process tree has exactly this behavior. If `convert_to_process_tree` nevertheless returns an object, the correct response is **not** relief: the returned tree must be pushed through §5 against `net_from_powl`, and any relaxation (e.g. `P` also constraining `legal`, or `legal` and `loop` forced into a `+` block whose semantics differ at the join) must be reported as `approximated`, with the specific witness that separates them.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

```python
"""
Round-trip validation: compare net_from_powl against every round-tripped net.

Techniques, in increasing order of strength:
  (a) soundness / workflow-net structural checks
  (b) footprint comparison           -- necessary, not sufficient
  (c) reachability-graph size        -- coarse fingerprint, tau-sensitive
  (d) bounded visible-trace exploration with EXACT final-marking equality
  (e) explicit positive / negative witness replay
"""

from itertools import product

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics import ClassicSemantics

SEM = ClassicSemantics()

MAX_VISIBLE = 12          # visible-length bound
MAX_LOOP_ITER = 3         # bound on Configure Trial repetitions
MAX_TAU_RUN = 12          # consecutive silent steps before we abandon a branch


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# (d) bounded visible-trace exploration
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def bounded_visible_language(net, im, fm,
                             max_visible=MAX_VISIBLE,
                             max_loop_iter=MAX_LOOP_ITER,
                             max_tau_run=MAX_TAU_RUN,
                             loop_marker="Configure Trial"):
    """
    Enumerate visible-label sequences that reach EXACTLY fm.

    - transitions with label None are hidden (fired, not recorded)
    - a trace counts as COMPLETE only when the marking equals fm exactly
    - consecutive silent firings are capped (max_tau_run) to avoid tau-cycling
    - the loop_marker occurrence count is capped (max_loop_iter)
    - visited-state memo is keyed on (marking, trace) to allow revisiting a
      marking under a different visible prefix, while the bounds guarantee
      termination
    """
    if im is None or fm is None or len(im) == 0 or len(fm) == 0:
        return None, "not tested: null or empty marking"

    complete = set()
    seen = set()
    stack = [(im, tuple(), 0)]

    while stack:
        marking, trace, tau_run = stack.pop()

        if marking == fm:
            complete.add(trace)
            # do NOT stop: fm may have outgoing transitions (a soundness bug we
            # want to observe), so continue exploring from here.

        if len(trace) >= max_visible:
            continue

        key = (marking, trace, tau_run)
        if key in seen:
            continue
        seen.add(key)

        try:
            enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
        except Exception:                          # noqa: BLE001
            enabled = SEM.enabled_transitions(net, marking)

        for t in enabled:
            new_marking = SEM.execute(t, net, marking)
            if new_marking is None:
                continue
            if t.label is None:
                if tau_run + 1 > max_tau_run:
                    continue                       # abandon silent cycling
                stack.append((new_marking, trace, tau_run + 1))
            else:
                if t.label == loop_marker and trace.count(loop_marker) + 1 > max_loop_iter:
                    continue
                stack.append((new_marking, trace + (t.label,), 0))

    return complete, "ok"


def replay_trace(net, im, fm, labels,
                 max_tau_run=MAX_TAU_RUN):
    """
    True iff `labels` can be replayed and the run ENDS EXACTLY in fm.
    Silent transitions may be inserted anywhere, bounded by max_tau_run.
    """
    if im is None or fm is None or len(im) == 0 or len(fm) == 0:
        return None
    stack = [(im, 0, 0)]
    seen = set()
    while stack:
        marking, idx, tau_run = stack.pop()
        if idx == len(labels) and marking == fm:
            return True
        key = (marking, idx, tau_run)
        if key in seen:
            continue
        seen.add(key)
        try:
            enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
        except Exception:                          # noqa: BLE001
            enabled = SEM.enabled_transitions(net, marking)
        for t in enabled:
            nm = SEM.execute(t, net, marking)
            if nm is None:
                continue
            if t.label is None:
                if tau_run + 1 <= max_tau_run:
                    stack.append((nm, idx, tau_run + 1))
            elif idx < len(labels) and t.label == labels[idx]:
                stack.append((nm, idx + 1, 0))
    return False
```

**Exhaustiveness statement.** The comparison is **exhaustive relative to the bound and not otherwise**. Concretely: it enumerates every complete visible trace of visible length ≤ 12 containing ≤ 3 occurrences of `Configure Trial`, under a cap of 12 consecutive silent firings, and requires exact equality with `fm` for completeness. Within that envelope, `lang_ref == lang_cand` is a genuine language-equality result. Outside it — longer loop unrollings, models needing >12 consecutive taus, and any behavior that only diverges after the 12th visible event — nothing is established. It also decides nothing about true concurrency (only interleavings), nothing about data or resources, and nothing about unbounded nets: if either net were unbounded, the reachability-graph step would blow up or fail, and the marking-keyed memo would grow without the length bound stopping it in reasonable time. The soundness check is the guard against that case, so a `sound=False` result must be treated as invalidating the language comparison rather than as one more failing row.

Footprints are used only as a *necessary* condition: equal footprints do not imply equal languages (they abstract over loops and long-distance dependencies), and unequal footprints are a strong reject signal. Reachability-graph state counts are a fingerprint only — they differ legitimately whenever a converter adds tau transitions, so a mismatch there is not by itself a defect.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN diagram can be a faithful rendering of a behaviorally correct net and still be systematically misread, because BPMN layout encodes *reading order* on the same axis it encodes *causal order*.

**Parallel gateways.** The `+` gateway pair around `Assess Risk` / `Prepare Evidence`, and around the legal branch / verification loop, is the only glyph carrying invariants I4 and I8. Readers routinely (a) miss the difference between `+` and `X` at small zoom, and (b) read the upper branch as "happens first" because it is drawn first. Nothing in the diagram states that `Legal Review` may occur *between* `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial`, yet that interleaving is legal in the source — arguably the single most misread property of this model.

**Exclusive gateways.** The optional-legal XOR becomes an `X` split with one branch going to `Legal Review` and one branch bypassing it. If the converter renders the bypass as an unlabelled *task* rather than a plain edge, a reader sees an unnamed activity and may assume undocumented work. If it renders as a bare edge, the reader may not notice the branch has no condition attached — BPMN conditions are absent because POWL has no data perspective, not because the choice is unconstrained-by-oversight.

**Loop visualization.** The binary loop appears as a back-edge from a gateway after `Inspect Trial` through `Correct Defect` and back to `Configure Trial`. Visually this is indistinguishable from a zero-or-more loop and from a while-do loop. The two facts that must be read *into* the picture are: at least one iteration always happens (I11), and the process never exits immediately after `Correct Defect` (I12). Neither is visible.

**Silent routing nodes.** Petri→BPMN typically emits several unlabelled elements: the XOR skip, the AND-split/join taus, and loop-entry/exit taus. Each is a modelling artifact. Collectively they inflate the perceived size of the process and invite questions like "what is this empty box?" — and, worse, invite an editor to *name* one of them, which silently changes the language.

**Apparent ordering of graph layout.** Graphviz-style left-to-right layout puts everything on a timeline. Two concurrent branches get a top branch and a bottom branch; a reader infers priority, or even ordering, from vertical position. The layout also places the legal branch physically before or after the loop, suggesting the very edge that the source deliberately omits.

**Annotations that reduce ambiguity.** Ship the BPMN only as part of a bundle:

1. A caption block on the diagram: "Derived artifact. Authoritative model: `source_powl` (POWL). Generated by POWL→Petri→BPMN on <date>, PM4Py <version>."
2. A legend distinguishing `+` from `X` in words, plus an explicit sentence: "Branches joined by `+` are unordered and may interleave in any way, including one branch's activities occurring between two activities of the other."
3. Text annotations attached to the loop gateway: "at least one Configure Trial → Inspect Trial iteration; exit occurs only after Inspect Trial, never directly after Correct Defect."
4. A text annotation on each unlabelled element: "routing artifact — no work performed" (and for the skip: "represents skipping Legal Review").
5. A note on the release join: "Release Change requires completion of both the legal decision (review or skip) and the verification loop."
6. A note disclaiming layout: "Vertical position carries no meaning. Left-to-right position implies order only along a directed edge path."
7. The invariant table from §1 shipped alongside as the acceptance criteria the diagram is asserted to satisfy.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

A conversion result must be rejected or quarantined if any of the following holds.

1. **Unsoundness.** `pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)` is false for any produced accepting net, or `check_is_workflow_net` is false (no unique source/sink, or unconnected nodes). Deadlocks, dead transitions, or improper completion invalidate every downstream claim.
2. **Null, empty, or unusable markings.** `im` or `fm` returned by a conversion is `None` or empty. Completion cannot be defined, exact-final-marking tests cannot run, and soundness is undefined. Quarantine rather than substitute a guessed marking.
3. **Failed exact-final-marking test.** A positive witness replays as a label sequence but does not terminate exactly in `fm` (tokens left over, or `fm` reachable with extra tokens). Label-sequence acceptance without marking equality is not acceptance.
4. **Loss of a required interleaving.** The witness `R, A, P, Configure Trial, Legal Review, Inspect Trial, RE, AR` fails to replay. This means the converter serialized the optional-legal branch against the verification loop, inventing an edge the source deliberately omits (I8). Same verdict if `R,P,A,...` fails (I4).
5. **Admission of release before verification.** Any negative witness replays: notably `R, A, Legal Review, Release Change, Archive Change` (I13) or `R, A, P, Release Change, Archive Change` (I11). Under-constrained models are as defective as over-constrained ones and are more dangerous, since they pass casual review.
6. **Collapse of optional behavior.** Either no complete trace without `Legal Review` exists (optionality lost — legal review became mandatory), or `Legal Review` appears in a position violating I6, or the XOR degenerated so that `Legal Review` can occur more than once.
7. **Loop-semantics drift.** A complete trace exists with zero `Configure Trial`/`Inspect Trial` (I11 lost), or `...Correct Defect, Release Change...` replays (I12 lost), or `...Correct Defect, Inspect Trial...` replays without a preceding `Configure Trial` (I10 lost).
8. **Bounded-language divergence.** `lang_ref != lang_cand` under the §5 bound, in either direction. Extra traces mean the conversion under-constrains; missing traces mean it over-constrains.
9. **Label-set corruption.** The visible label set changes (an activity dropped, renamed, or an unlabelled element acquiring a label), or duplicate labels appear where the source has seven unique ones. Duplication quarantines the artifact for *storage and analytics* purposes even when the language is unchanged.
10. **Silent acceptance of a process tree.** `convert_to_process_tree` returns an object that has not been put through the full §5 harness. A tree that "succeeded" but relaxes the partial order is a semantic mismatch masquerading as a success, and must be quarantined until a distinguishing witness is either found (reject) or provably absent under the bound (accept as `approximated`).
11. **Suppressed exceptions.** Any pipeline stage whose exception was caught and discarded without being recorded in the audit record. An unreported failure is treated as a failed conversion.
12. **State-space blow-up.** The bounded exploration or reachability-graph construction does not terminate within the configured bound, indicating possible unboundedness. No positive claim may be issued from an incomplete exploration.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

**Authoritative representation: the POWL model `source_powl`**, stored as the code that constructs it (or an equivalent serialization), under version control.

Rationale: POWL is the only representation here that states the *absence* of order directly. A partial order says "`Legal Review` and the verification loop are unordered" as a first-class fact; a Petri net says it as an emergent consequence of token flow, and BPMN says it as a gateway glyph plus a layout. Every other artifact is a lossy or at best equal-information projection, and all of them are *derivable* from the POWL — while none of them can regenerate it, since no POWL importer exists in the pipeline. Storing the derivative and discarding the source would be irreversible.

**Stakeholder visualization: the BPMN produced by pipeline P1** (`bpmn_from_net`), shipped with the annotation bundle from §6. BPMN is what business stakeholders read; the POWL diagram is precise but unfamiliar, and the Petri net is precise but reads as engineering notation.

**Derivative artifacts** (all regenerated from the POWL on every release, never hand-edited): `net_from_powl`/`im`/`fm`, `bpmn_from_net`, any process tree, all `.png`/`.svg` renderings, and all round-tripped nets. Each derivative filename should carry its provenance, as in the §3 code (`_DERIVATIVE`, `_ROUNDTRIP`, `_PROBE`).

**Validation evidence that must accompany each derivative:**

| Derivative | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| `net_from_powl` | `check_is_workflow_net` true; `check_soundness` true with non-empty `im`/`fm`; full positive/negative witness table passing; bounded language recorded (trace count + bound parameters) |
| `bpmn_from_net` | Its round-tripped net passes soundness; bounded-language equality with `net_from_powl` under a stated bound; the full witness table; a duplicate-label report |
| Process tree (if any) | Distinguishing-witness search result; explicit `approximated`/`lost` verdicts per invariant; must never be published without them |
| Renderings | The annotation bundle (§6 items 1–7) and the PM4Py version + conversion date |

**Should a process-tree intermediate be allowed?** Not on the production path. It is valuable as a *diagnostic probe* — a failure is positive evidence that the model is genuinely non-block-structured, which is itself worth recording in the audit trail — but a process tree cannot represent this partial order faithfully, so any tree that does emerge is an approximation. Permit it under three conditions: it is labelled `approximated` in every artifact, the specific relaxation is documented with a witness trace, and it is never used as the source for the stakeholder BPMN when P1's BPMN is available.

**Avoiding round-trip drift.** Enforce a one-way pipeline: POWL → everything else, never back. Concretely:

1. All edits are made to the POWL construction code and reviewed as code.
2. Derivatives are regenerated in CI, never edited; a CI step fails the build if a derivative in the repository differs from a freshly generated one.
3. Every regeneration runs §5 and fails the build on any §7 rejection criterion.
4. The invariant table from §1 lives next to the model as executable witnesses, so adding a behavior to the model requires consciously adding or amending a witness — a change in the witness table is the review signal that semantics changed.
5. The PM4Py version is pinned; a version bump reruns the full harness before the pin moves, since conversion internals are the thing most likely to shift silently.
6. If a stakeholder requests a change on the BPMN diagram, it is transcribed into the POWL by a modeler; imported BPMN edits are rejected outright.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even with every test in §5 passing on every artifact, the following remain unestablished.

1. **True concurrency.** All evidence is interleaving-based. A converted model that replaces the AND-construct with an explicit choice between the two orderings would pass every test while asserting something categorically different about causal independence — and about what a resource model or a simulation would predict.
2. **Behavior beyond the bound.** Nothing is known about traces with more than 12 visible events, more than 3 loop iterations, or divergence that requires more than 12 consecutive silent steps to expose. Bounded language equality is not language equality.
3. **That the POWL itself matches the real-world process.** The whole audit takes `source_powl` as authoritative by construction. If the modeler mis-encoded the intent — say, the business actually requires legal review before any trial configuration — every conversion faithfully preserves the wrong thing, and no amount of round-tripping detects it.
4. **Branching / failure equivalence.** Trace-language equality does not imply bisimulation. Two models can have identical languages and differ in *when* a choice is committed (e.g. an early XOR vs. a late one). For the optional-legal branch this determines whether the decision point is observable to a participant, which matters for process control.
5. **Semantics of unlabelled elements in the derived BPMN.** The tests confirm that taus are behaviorally invisible; they cannot confirm that a downstream tool, engine, or human will treat them as invisible. A BPMN engine may attach forms, timers, or assignments to an unlabelled task.
6. **Non-control-flow perspectives.** Data conditions on the legal-review choice, the actual termination criterion for the verification loop, resources, roles, timing, and cost are absent from the source model and therefore absent from every derivative. Their absence is not validated — it is simply out of scope, and readers of the BPMN will nonetheless infer some of them from layout and naming.
7. **Stability across PM4Py versions.** The verdicts apply to the specific conversion implementations exercised at the recorded version. A future version could legitimately change the structure of the generated net or BPMN in ways that alter duplicate-label behavior or marking construction without any change to the source.
