## 1. Decision rule

**Eligibility & Stage Gate:**
Intervention is only authorized if the case is in `Triage` or `Diagnosis Started`. Cases at `Diagnosis Complete` (I-03) or later are ineligible as the swarm has negligible effect. Cases with missing critical data (I-06) are suspended until quality is restored.

**Calibration Adjustment:**
We do not use `p_now` directly for decision thresholds. Instead, we apply calibration offsets to the estimated Absolute Risk Reduction (ARR) range to account for segment bias:
*   **Strategic Enterprise:** Reduce ARR estimate by 0.05–0.10 (overprediction).
*   **SME:** Increase ARR estimate by 0.04–0.09 (underprediction).
*   **Public Sector:** No adjustment, but widen confidence intervals.
*   **Non-Native Language:** Do not intervene unless ARR lower bound > 0.15 and data quality is verified.

**Alert Persistence & Re-entry:**
*   **Repeat Alerts:** If a case triggers a repeated alert without a `Materially New Event` (e.g., new log, status change, evidence upload), the case is suppressed for 2 hours from the last alert. I-05 is suppressed. I-07 is re-eligible because a new log was uploaded.
*   **Minimum Evidence Change:** A delta of `p_now` > `p_prev` + 0.10 is preferred to avoid noise.

**Prioritization (Score):**
We calculate a **Robust Priority Score (RPS)**:
$$RPS = (ARR_{mid} \times Value) - (Cost_{slot} + Cost_{burden}) - (0.02 \times 6 \times Avg_{QueueValue})$$
The spillover term (€5,400 for 3 slots) is distributed across the portfolio. Cases with high `RPS` are selected.

**Capacity Constraint:**
Maximum 3 slots utilized per day to maintain 1 slot reserve for regulatory incidents. If 4 slots are available, the 4th is reserved unless a regulatory incident is confirmed.

## 5. Fairness and feedback-loop risks

**Value-Based Prioritization:**
*   **Risk:** Prioritizing I-04 (€60k) and I-02 (€25k) may starve lower-value SME cases (e.g., I-01, I-07) despite higher absolute risk.
*   **Safeguard:** Implement a "Fairness Cap": No more than 40% of slots can be allocated to Strategic Enterprise cases. I-01 (Public/SME) is prioritized to ensure compliance and customer trust.

**Non-Native-Language Support:**
*   **Risk:** I-09 has sparse calibration. Intervening on I-09 could waste a slot on a false positive due to language barriers in data.
*   **Safeguard:** I-09 is excluded from Tier 1. Intervention requires a human-in-the-loop validation of the log before swarm allocation.

**Differential Calibration:**
*   **Risk:** Strategic cases are overpredicted, leading to "alert fatigue" where real risks are ignored, while SME cases are underpredicted, leading to missed interventions.
*   **Safeguard:** The Decision Rule applies calibration offsets (SME +p, Strategic -p). We track actual breach rates by segment separately from model predictions.

**Feedback Loops:**
*   **Risk:** Intervening on cases that do not breach (e.g., I-01 in Adverse scenario) creates negative training labels ("False Positive Intervention"), degrading the model's ability to predict breach risk accurately.
*   **Safeguard:** Label cases as "Intervention Required vs. Breach Occurred". Use inverse probability weighting in retraining to account for the intervention's effect on the outcome.

**Spillover Harms:**
*   **Risk:** Untreated cases (I-06, I-09) suffer congestion delays (0.01–0.03 risk increase). This systematically harms lower-value or less-prioritized cases.
*   **Safeguard:** Monitor spillover impact on Diagnosis Stage queue. If spillover risk > 0.03 on average, pause swarm allocation until capacity increases.

## 6. Monitoring and recalibration plan

**Operational Monitoring:**
*   **Metric:** Slot utilization vs. Reserve usage.
*   **Alert:** If Reserve Slot is used >1 time, trigger policy review.
*   **Metric:** Queue wait times for Diagnosis Stage (measure spillover).

**Predictive Monitoring:**
*   **Metric:** Calibration plots by Segment (SME, Strategic, Public).
*   **Threshold:** If Brier Score drifts > 0.02 for any segment, trigger recalibration.
*   **Metric:** False Alarm Rate (Intervention triggered but no breach).

**Causal-Effect Monitoring:**
*   **Metric:** Estimated ARR per slot.
*   **Method:** Propensity Score Matching. Compare treated cases (Swarm) vs. matched untreated cases (No Swarm) on breach outcome.
*   **Trigger:** If observed ARR < 0.10 for any segment, pause allocation to that segment.

**Capacity & Fairness Monitoring:**
*   **Metric:** Breach rate by Segment (Actual vs. Predicted).
*   **Metric:** Spillover Cost (€) per intervention.
*   **Review:** Monthly review of "Tier 1" allocation to ensure Strategic Enterprise cases do not exceed 40% of slots.

**Recalibration Plan:**
*   **Quarterly:** Re-estimate segment calibration offsets (SME/Strategic).
*   **Ad-hoc:** If Regulatory Incident occurs and Reserve Slot is used, evaluate if the slot was truly needed (Post-Mortem).
*   **Rule Change:** If 3 consecutive months show negative Net-Impact in Base Scenario, trigger a policy default to "Wait" until ARR > 0.20.
