## 8. Residual uncertainty
1. Unobserved case risk driving routing and outcomes. Most valuable: a pre-Classify risk score or case complexity sub-components.
2. True service time vs queue time separation. Most valuable: timestamps per activity and reviewer id.
3. Abandoned or still-open cases causing selection bias. Most valuable: inclusion of incomplete cases with censoring time.
4. Reviewer-level skill and workload heterogeneity within groups. Most valuable: reviewer id and daily workload per resource group.
5. Post-process factors affecting 30-day appeals. Most valuable: appeal reason and timestamp of appeal relative to close.
