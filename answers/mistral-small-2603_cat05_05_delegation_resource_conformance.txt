## 2. Rule Interpretation

### RR1 — Drafter/approver separation
- **Formalization**: The human performing `Draft_Change_Request` must not perform `Approve_Change` for the same case.
- **Counting business days**: Weekends/holidays excluded (RR4 uses same definition).
- **Treatment of principal on leave**: Actions by a principal while on leave (e.g., Asha’s VPN session) are treated as unauthorized unless explicitly delegated and documented *before* the action.

### RR2 — Implementer/verifier separation
- **Formalization**: The human performing `Implement_Change` must not perform `Verify_Implementation` for the same case.
- **Service accounts**: Must map to an authorized human via session evidence (RR5).

### RR3 — High-risk approval authority
- **Formalization**: For HIGH-risk cases, `Approve_Change` must be performed by QM or a valid QM delegate. Delegation must:
  1. Cover the event timestamp.
  2. Include the case scope.
  3. Require principal absence (or formal unavailability).
  4. Prohibit subdelegation.
- **Database record timing**: A delegation recorded *after* the action cannot validate it (e.g., D-QM-01’s QMS record created on 2026-08-12 cannot justify Asha’s approval on 2026-08-13).

### RR4 — Emergency substitution
- **Formalization**: HIGH-risk emergency changes may be approved by IC or valid IC delegate, but QM must perform `Retrospective_QM_Review` within 2 business days.
- **Business days**: Exclude weekends/holidays (e.g., CHG-505’s review on 2026-08-14 is within 2 days of 2026-08-11).
- **Emergency flag**: Must be credible (e.g., CHG-510 lacks emergency flag but was approved by Clara under invalid delegation).

### RR5 — Identity evidence
- **Service accounts**: Must have session-level human attribution (e.g., badge/FIDO logs). Shared workstations without re-authentication weaken attribution.
- **Aliases**: Normalized before SoD checks (e.g., `u_bk`/`b.koch`  Ben Koch).

### RR6 — Suspicion versus proof
- **Indicators**: Repeated pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, concentrated handovers are *indicators*, not proof. Require supporting evidence (e.g., logs, emails) for collusion.

## 4. Collusion and Alternative-Explanation Analysis

### Repeated Pairs (Clara & Ben)
- **Indicator**: 18/23 HIGH-risk production changes drafted by Clara and approved by Ben.
  - **Benign**: Efficient workflow; no financial benefit; no formal pair-rotation policy.
  - **Adverse**: Potential SoD circumvention; repeated pairs may indicate collusion.
  - **Discriminating evidence**: Session logs showing Clara and Ben working together outside approvals; emails discussing approvals.

### Reciprocal Approvals (Ben drafts, Clara approves for LOW-risk training)
- **Indicator**: Ben drafted 4 LOW-risk training changes approved by Clara.
  - **Benign**: Training changes may have lower risk; reciprocal approvals are common in training.
  - **Adverse**: Could mask approval of higher-risk changes under LOW-risk guise.
  - **Discriminating evidence**: Content of training changes; approval justifications.

### Shared Workstation (CHG-503)
- **Indicator**: `svc_qms` session from Diego’s workstation; no re-authentication.
  - **Benign**: Shared terminals in maintenance areas.
  - **Adverse**: Weak session attribution; potential for unauthorized actions.

## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation Creation**:
   - Require delegations to be recorded *before* the effective interval.
   - Automate delegation scope validation (e.g., match case scope).
   - Prohibit subdelegation in delegation templates.

2. **Effective-Time Recording**:
   - Use UTC timestamps for all delegation intervals.
   - Log principal absence electronically (e.g., HR system integration).

3. **Principal Availability**:
   - Enforce multi-factor authentication for principals on leave.
   - Disable VPN access during leave unless explicitly approved.

4. **Alias Management**:
   - Automate alias normalization in identity systems.
   - Require periodic reconciliation of aliases.

5. **Individual Authentication**:
   - Enforce re-authentication for shared workstations.
   - Log all service-account sessions with human attribution.

6. **Emergency Review**:
   - Automate tracking of `Retrospective_QM_Review` deadlines.
   - Require QM sign-off for emergency changes *before* implementation.

7. **Pair Rotation**:
   - Implement random pair rotation for HIGH-risk approvals.
   - Monitor repeated pairs for policy compliance.

8. **Network Monitoring**:
   - Alert on repeated pairs or reciprocal approvals.
   - Correlate badge logs with approval events.

9. **Evidential Retention**:
   - Retain delegation documents for 7 years.
   - Log all approval justifications.
