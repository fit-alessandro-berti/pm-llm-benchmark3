## **1. Conformance Semantics**

### **Distinguishing Deviation Classes**
The classification of deviations depends on the **normative status** (mandatory, forbidden, typical, unspecified) and the **scope** of the relation. The key distinctions are:

1. **Forbidden Behavior**
   - Explicitly prohibited by the norm (e.g., `N3: Cancel Order  Dispatch Package`).
   - **Closed-world assumption applies**: Any occurrence is a violation.
   - **Severity**: Critical (immediate remediation required).

2. **Missing Mandatory Behavior**
   - A required relation is absent where the norm’s scope applies (e.g., `N1: Create Order  Validate Order` for 2/100 orders).
   - **Open-world assumption**: The norm does not guarantee *how* the relation is satisfied (e.g., intermediate events may exist).
   - **Severity**: High (investigation needed, but not all gaps are violations).

3. **Unexpected but Unregulated Behavior**
   - Relations not in the norm but not forbidden (e.g., `Validate Order  Issue Invoice`).
   - **Open-world assumption**: The norm does not prohibit these, but their legitimacy depends on business context.
   - **Severity**: Low (unless they indicate process drift).

4. **Typical-Pattern Absence**
   - Common but not required relations are missing (e.g., `N2: Validate Order  Close Order` for 17/100 orders).
   - **Open-world assumption**: Exceptions are allowed.
   - **Severity**: Informational (may indicate inefficiency but not noncompliance).

5. **Likely Data-Quality Error**
   - Artifacts of incorrect joins, mislabeled events, or sensor noise (e.g., `Sensor Heartbeat  Invoice`).
   - **Severity**: Low (requires data cleaning, not process change).

6. **Abstraction Mismatch**
   - The norm uses a "directly-follows" abstraction, but the real process has intermediate steps (e.g., `Create Order  [unrecorded review]  Validate Order`).
   - **Severity**: Medium (investigate if the abstraction is too strict).

7. **Insufficient Evidence**
   - Relations are absent but could be missing due to incomplete logging (e.g., 14/110 invoices with no successor).
   - **Severity**: Low (requires process audit, not immediate action).

### **Open-World vs. Closed-World Assumptions**
- **Closed-world** applies only to **forbidden** relations and **mandatory** relations *within their stated scope*.
  - Example: `N3` is strictly forbidden; any occurrence is a violation.
  - Example: `N1` is mandatory *for standard orders*, but exceptions (e.g., manual reviews) may exist.
- **Open-world** applies to **typical** and **unspecified** relations.
  - Example: `N2` allows intermediate events; missing `Validate  Close` is not a violation.
  - Example: `N6` (Reopen Order) is unspecified; its behavior is outside the norm’s scope.

## **3. Detailed Reasoning by Model Family**

### **OC-DFG (Object-Centric Directly-Follows Graph)**
**What it can establish:**
- **Mandatory relations** (e.g., `Create Order  Validate Order` must hold for standard orders).
- **Forbidden relations** (e.g., `Cancel Order  Dispatch Package` is never allowed).
- **Typical patterns** (e.g., `Validate Order  Close Order` is common but not required).

**What it cannot establish:**
- **Intermediate steps**: The norm uses a "directly-follows" abstraction, but real processes may have unlogged events (e.g., manual reviews).
- **Concurrent or overlapping lifecycles**: `object_codeath` relations are not modeled.
- **Data-quality issues**: Spurious links (e.g., `Sensor Heartbeat  Invoice`) appear as valid edges.

**Counts and scopes affect interpretation:**
- **2/100 missing `Create Order  Validate Order`**:
  - **Possible violations**: If no intermediate review exists, this is a gap.
  - **Possible abstraction mismatch**: If reviews are unlogged, the norm’s "directly-follows" assumption is too strict.
- **198/200 `Pack Package  Dispatch Package`**:
  - **High compliance**, but 2 missing cases require investigation (data gap or process error).

**Danger of interpreting "eventually expected" as "directly-follows":**
- The norm says `Create Order` should **eventually** lead to `Validate Order`, but the OC-DFG enforces a **direct** edge.
- **Risk**: False positives if intermediate steps exist but are unlogged.
- **Solution**: Use **temporal constraints** (e.g., "within 24 hours") or **eventual consistency checks** instead of strict directly-follows.

---

### **OTG (Object-Type Graph)**
**What it can establish:**
- **Mandatory interactions** (e.g., `Order  Item`, `Package  Delivery`).
- **Forbidden cobirths** (e.g., `Order` and `Payment` cannot be created in the same event).

**What it cannot establish:**
- **Temporal ordering**: OTG only shows *which* objects interact, not *when*.
- **Cardinality**: How many interactions are "enough" (norm does not specify minimums).
- **Indirect relations**: E.g., `Invoice  Payment` may require `Issue Invoice  Record Payment`.

**Counts and scopes affect interpretation:**
- **1450 `Order  Item`**: Fully compliant with `T1`.
- **1 `Order cobirth Payment`**: **Critical violation** of `T6` (forbidden).
- **92 `Invoice  Payment`**: Mostly compliant, but 1 missing link (data issue or unapplied cash).

**How a relation can be conforming at one abstraction but suspicious at another:**
- Example: `Dispatch Package` lacks `Delivery` relation (`T2` violation).
  - **OTG**: Missing edge  **violation**.
  - **OC-DFG**: Carrier reference exists in event attributes  **data-quality issue**, not a process violation.

---

### **ET–OT (Event-Object Typing)**
**What it can establish:**
- **Mandatory event-object links** (e.g., `Add Item` must create `Item` and link to `Order`).
- **Forbidden event-object links** (e.g., `Sensor Heartbeat` must not link to `Invoice`).

**What it cannot establish:**
- **Object lifecycles**: ET–OT shows *which* objects are created/updated, not their transitions.
- **Missing but expected objects**: E.g., `Dispatch Package` may lack a `Delivery` object due to data error.

**Counts and scopes affect interpretation:**
- **100 `Create Order  Order`**: Fully compliant (`E1`).
- **1 `Sensor Heartbeat  Invoice`**: **Forbidden** (`E10`), likely a data error.
- **1 `Record Payment` with no `Invoice`**: **Possible violation** of `E8` (unless it’s unapplied cash, but not labeled as such).

**Abstraction mismatches:**
- The norm says `Record Payment` **must** link to an `Invoice` (`E8`), but finance allows unapplied cash.
- **Risk**: Over-enforcement if the norm does not account for exceptions.
- **Solution**: Clarify whether unapplied cash should be a separate event type.

    else:
        return "Unregulated" if deviation not in norm_metadata else "Data-Quality Error"

## **7. Investigation and Remediation Plan**

### **Prioritized Actions**

#### **1. Critical: `Cancel Order  Dispatch Package` (Rank 1)**
- **Immediate Containment**:
  - **Block dispatch** for any order with a `Cancel Order` event in the system.
  - **Audit last 30 days** of cancellations to identify false positives (e.g., partial cancellations).
- **Data Repair**:
  - If false positives exist, **retroactively void** the dispatched packages.
- **Process Investigation**:
  - Review **cancellation logic** to ensure no dispatch occurs post-cancellation.
  - **Automate checks** in the dispatch workflow.
- **Norm Revision**:
  - **Clarify scope**: Should cancellations include partial orders? If yes, define rules.
  - **Status**: **Forbidden** (no change needed).

#### **2. High: Missing `Create Order  Validate Order` (Rank 2)**
- **Immediate Containment**:
  - **Flag the 2 orders** for manual review.
- **Data Repair**:
  - Check if **unlogged review events** exist (e.g., in a separate system).
- **Process Investigation**:
  - **Audit validation steps** to ensure no orders bypass validation.
  - **Improve logging** for manual reviews.
- **Norm Revision**:
  - **Add temporal constraint**: "Orders must enter validation within 24 hours."
  - **Status**: **Mandatory** (with time window).

#### **3. Medium: Missing `Pack Package  Dispatch Package` (Rank 3)**
- **Immediate Containment**:
  - **Verify packing status** before dispatch for the 2 missing cases.
- **Data Repair**:
  - If packing occurred outside the observed window, **log it retroactively**.
- **Process Investigation**:
  - **Check warehouse systems** for unlogged packing events.
- **Norm Revision**:
  - **No change** (norm is clear, but enforcement needs improvement).

#### **4. Low: Data-Quality Issues (`Sensor Heartbeat  Invoice`, `Dispatch` without `Delivery`)**

  - **Filter out sensor data** from financial object joins.
  - **Ensure `Delivery` objects are created** for all dispatches.

  - **Clean pipeline** to prevent reused IDs.
  - **Backfill missing `Delivery` objects** for the 2 cases.
- **Process Investigation**:
  - **Audit ID assignment** logic to prevent collisions.
- **Norm Revision**:
  - **No change** (these are data issues, not process gaps).

## **8. Uncertainty Statement**

### **Robust Conclusions**
- **Forbidden violations** (`Cancel Order  Dispatch Package`, `Order cobirth Payment`) are **definitive** and require immediate action.
- **Missing mandatory relations** (`Create Order  Validate Order`, `Pack  Dispatch`) are **high-risk** but may have data or abstraction explanations.
- **Typical-pattern absences** (`Validate  Close`, `Issue Invoice  Payment`) are **not violations** but may indicate inefficiencies.

### **Norm-Dependent Conclusions**
- **Abstraction mismatches** (e.g., unlogged reviews) depend on whether the norm’s "directly-follows" assumption is too strict.
- **Unregulated behavior** (e.g., `Reopen Order`) cannot be classified without further policy.
- **Unapplied cash** depends on whether the norm should explicitly allow `Record Payment` without `Invoice`.

### **Data-Quality-Dependent Conclusions**
- **Spurious links** (`Sensor Heartbeat  Invoice`) are likely **data errors**, not process issues.
- **Missing `Delivery` objects** may be **process gaps** or **logging errors**.

### **Limitations of Graph Summaries Alone**
- **Temporal nuances** (e.g., "eventually" vs. "directly-follows") cannot be resolved without event-level data.
- **Business context** (e.g., why an invoice has no payment) requires finance team input.
- **Concurrent lifecycles** (e.g., `object_codeath`) are invisible in directly-follows graphs.

---
**Final Note**: The norm is **intentionally incomplete**, and some deviations (e.g., `Reopen Order`) are **legitimate but unmodeled**. Focus remediation on **forbidden violations** and **high-risk mandatory gaps**, while treating typical absences and data errors as secondary.
