## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

Layered architecture:

1. **Process Core (POWL - strict control flow)**:
   - Nodes: Register, Sanctions_Check (SC), Quality_Review (QR), Manual_Approval (MA), Activate, Reject.
   - StrictPartialOrder: Register  (SC and QR concurrent)  Decision (XOR: [MA  Activate] or Reject).
   - Notification: Notify_Requester after Decision (before Close), but not mandatory in model (enforced by organizational control).
   - No loops; Request_More_Info is modeled as a "wait" state with data check: cannot Activate until Request_More_Info completed AND evidence received (enforced by data rule, not control flow).

## 5. Final Judgment

1. **Primary classification per case** (using exact labels; no "mixed" substitution):
   - COMPLIANT: SUP-701, SUP-709, SUP-713, SUP-707
   - JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION: SUP-702 (PD-2 active), SUP-704 (FD-7 active), SUP-714 (short-term post-PD-2 normalization)
   - NONCOMPLIANT: SUP-703 (high-risk pilot misuse), SUP-708 (activate before SC)
   - MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT: SUP-706 (log migration), SUP-710 (reactivation scope), SUP-711 (data migration duplicate)
   - DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE: SUP-705 (FD-7 post-expiry), SUP-712 (COI disclosure), SUP-715 (COI disclosure)

2. **Severity and confidence**:
   - HIGH severity: SUP-703 (high-risk + auto-approve before QR), SUP-708 (SC after Activate for high-risk)  **confidence HIGH** (clear unconditional policy violation; no overlay applies)
   - MEDIUM severity: FD-7 cases (SUP-704/705), COI uncertainty (SUP-712/715)  **confidence MEDIUM** (overlay applicability borderline for post-expiry; data missing for disclosure)
   - LOW severity: pilot normalization post-expiry (SUP-714), log completeness (SUP-706)  **confidence HIGH** for loop safety, LOW for log stability (but process likely compliant)
   - Overall judgment confidence: **HIGH** for clear noncompliant cases (SUP-703, SUP-708); **MEDIUM** for indeterminate cases (SUP-705, SUP-712); **LOW** uncertainty only for labeling, not factual violation.

3. **System-level judgment**:
   - **Operational compliance**: Mixed but not materially failing. ~15% of recent cases (SUP-703, SUP-708) are HIGH-severity noncompliant; ~35% are indeterminate due to missing data (SUP-705, SUP-712); majority (50%) compliant or justified. The operation reasonably uses overlays during their effective windows (PD-2/FD-7), and observed practice mostly respects unconditional rules (e.g., SC before activation). However, continuing FD-7-like steps post-expiry without documentation and using pilot for high-risk cases represent real risks that require overlay verification. Thus, **enforcing M0 strictly without checking active overlays leads to unjustified NONCOMPLIANT calls for justified cases and misses real risks for indeterminate cases**. The current practice is operationally acceptable under overlays but needs formal overlay verification at activation.
   - **Model validity**: M0 is incomplete for natural loops (Request_More_Info) and does not handle policy overlays, scope ambiguities (rejected/reactivated cases), or resource rules. It correctly models concurrency (SC/QR) and exclusive decision (approved vs. reject), but lacks data-aware conditions (e.g., auto-approve eligibility). Thus **M0 alone cannot determine compliance; it should be a baseline control-flow model only, with policy overlays and data rules enforced separately**.

4. **Reject each side’s argument**:
   - Reject prosecution’s claim that *all* pilot/emergency deviations are noncompliant: Evidence shows PD-2 and FD-7 explicitly permit deviations under monitoring when active and eligible (e.g., SUP-702, SUP-704). Automatic expiry (FD-7) is correct, but continuing emergency practice shortly after expiry without a written prohibition is reasonable; compliance depends on overlay verification, not blanket enforcement. Labeling SUP-714 as noncompliant ignores short-term normalization under expected policy change (no permanent policy yet).
   - Reject defense’s claim that M0 is obsolete: M0 correctly captures the mandatory sequence for accepted onboarding (SC/QR before decision, MA before Activate in standard route). It is valid for compliant cases. However, defense overstates by calling all overlay deviations "justified" without verifying eligibility at timestamp; some cases (SUP-703) clearly violate PD-2 eligibility, so not justified. Also, continuing FD-7 steps post-expiry strictly per dates is noncompliant (SUP-705), though reasonable operationally.
   - **Dominant reason**: Policy effective dates (inclusive) are hard boundaries for overlays. Overlay applicability must be checked at case time using documented eligibility (e.g., LOW-risk DE group for PD-2). Without overlay verification, M0 enforcement misclassifies justified cases as noncompliant and fails to detect high-risk violations. Data gaps (disclosure records, extension docs) necessitate indeterminate labels, not automatic compliance.

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

**Layered design (clear separation of concerns):**

1. **Process Core (POWL - strict control flow only)**:
   - Transitions: `Register_Supplier`, `Sanctions_Check`, `Quality_Review`, `Manual_Procurement_Approval`, `Activate_Supplier`, `Reject_Supplier`, `Notify_Requester`, `Close_Case`.
   - Concurrent checks: `StrictPartialOrder([Sanctions_Check, Quality_Review])`.
   - Decision: `OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [StrictPartialOrder([Manual_Procurement_Approval, Activate_Supplier]), Reject_Supplier])`.
   - Chain: `Register`  checks  decision  `Notify_Requester`  `Close_Case`.
   - *No policy conditions, data rules, or loops in POWL.* (Approximation: Request_More_Info is **not** in POWL; handled by data rule.)
   - *Rationale:* POWL must be a minimal, unambiguous control-flow skeleton. Loops and conditionals belong to data/organizational layers.

2. **Policy Overlay (Versioned Overlay Table)**:
   - Table `PolicyOverlay` (organizational/data layer):
     | `overlay_id` | `effective_start` | `effective_end` | `scope_type` | `scope_criteria` | `auto_approve_allowed` | `quality_after_activation_allowed` | `spend_cap_eur` | `emergency_type` | `monitoring_required` |
     |---------------|-------------------|-----------------|---------------|------------------|------------------------|------------------------------------|------------------|------------------|------------------------|
     | `CS-9-base` | 2025-01-01 | 9999-12-31 | `accepted` | all suppliers accepted after SC pass | `false` | `false` | `none` | none | `true` |
     | `PD-2-auto` | 2026-06-01 | 2026-07-31 | `low_risk_de` | `risk_level = LOW` AND `corporate_group = approved` AND `country = DE` | `true` | `true` (2 biz days) | `20,000` | none | `true` |
     | `FD-7-prov` | 2026-07-10 | 2026-07-12 | `critical_relief` | `emergency = flood` AND `supplier_type = pump/critical` | `true` (provisional) | `true` (2 biz days) | `20,000` | `flood` | `true` |
   - `scope_criteria` is machine-readable (e.g., `risk_level`, `corporate_group_approved` flags from data).
   - Overlay IDs are immutable; new overlays get new ID. Effective dates are inclusive (`>= start`, `<= end`).
   - Governance: Steering committee records overlays in meeting minutes + email; a simple form captures `scope_criteria`, `spend_cap`, `monitoring checklist`. No amendment to CS-9; overlays are exceptions.

3. **Data-Aware Checks (pandas/validation rules)**:
   - At `Register_Supplier`, create a **stable snapshot** with immutable fields:
     - `notification_id` (UUIDv4, generated once, reused for `Notify_Requester`), stored in event log and CRM.
     - `supplier_risk_level` (`HIGH`/`LOW`, derived from internal risk matrix), `corporate_group_approved` (`true`/`false`), `emergency_flag` (`true` if current date in FD-7 window), `coi_disclosure` (`true`/`false`), `info_requested` list (`[]`), `info_status` dict (`{item_id: "pending"|"received"}`).
     - `policy_snapshot`: list of active overlays (`overlay_id`, `effective_start`, `effective_end`) by comparing `register_date` to overlay dates (inclusive).
   - Validation rules (executed at each step; e.g., in onboarding app logic):
     - **Sanctions_Check**: Must complete before any `Activate_Supplier` attempt (unconditional; enforced by rule).
     - **Activate_Supplier**: Before execution, check:
       1. `Sanctions_Check` completed?  `true` required.
       2. Determine active overlays: pick overlays where `effective_start <= activate_date <= effective_end`.
       3. For each active overlay:
          - If `auto_approve_allowed = true` **AND** `scope_criteria` matches (`e.g., risk_level=LOW and corporate_group_approved=true`), then `Manual_Procurement_Approval` step may be skipped (i.e., allow `Activate` without `MA`), **but only if** `info_requested` is empty or all `info_status == "received"`.
          - Else (no matching auto-approve overlay), require `Manual_Procurement_Approval` performed (`ma_performed = true`).
       4. Check `spend_cap_eur`: if `spend > spend_cap`, block `Activate` and log `status = "pending_retrospective"`. Allow `Activate` only after retrospective approval logged.
       5. Check `quality_after_activation_allowed`: if `auto_approve_allowed = true` or `emergency_type = flood`, allow `Quality_Review` to occur up to 2 business days after `Activate` (rule checks `qr_due_date <= activate_date + 2 biz days`; no enforcement at `Activate`, but UI shows deadline).
       6. COI check: if `corporate_group_same_line = true` (requester and approver in same reporting line per alias resolution) AND `coi_disclosure = false`, block `MA` approval; require third-party approver or set `coi_disclosure = true` (recorded in snapshot).
       7. Request_More_Info: `Request_More_Info` transition (not in POWL) adds items to `info_requested`, sets `info_status = "pending"`. `Activate` allowed only if `all(info_status.values()) == {"received"}`. `QR` may be scheduled within 2 biz days post-Activate if overlay allows.
     - **Reject_Supplier**: Only `Sanctions_Check` required; `QR` skipped (matches N1’s abbreviated pre-screen). No MA needed.
     - **Notify_Requester**: Use `notification_id`; CRM stores it. Mutable text fields for old logs are metadata only (no process enforcement).
     - **Close_Case**: Only after `Notify_Requester` logged (per M0), and all mandatory checks (`SC` for accepted, `SC` for rejected) completed.
   - *Rationale:* Data rules check overlay applicability at decision time using timestamps; no logic in POWL. Stable `notification_id` ensures traceability. COI disclosure is recorded; skips MA only if disclosed.

4. **Organizational Controls**:
   - **Overlay Governance**: Steering committee submits overlay via simple template (scope criteria, dates, monitoring checklist). Template auto-generates `overlay_id` and checks for overlap (e.g., PD-2 + FD-7 cannot overlap for same supplier type without review). Approval requires meeting minutes reference.
   - **Monitoring Checklist**: For FD-7/PD-2 cases, daily transaction log (auto-generated) and QR completion timestamp must be recorded within 2 biz days. System shows a reminder if QR >1 biz day post-Activate.
   - **COI Process**: When requester/approver resolve to same line (detected via alias resolution), UI prompts: “Disclosure required? Yes/No (record reason).” If “No,” block MA approval; require third party or delay. Disclosure status (`coi_disclosure`) is part of snapshot.
   - **Rejected Leads & Reactivation Scope Rules** (explicit):
     - M0 (`Register  checks  decision`) applies **only to accepted suppliers** (i.e., after `Sanctions_Check` passes and not rejected). Rejected leads follow `Register  Sanctions_Check  Reject`; no `QR` or `MA`.
     - Reactivation: if prior `Quality_Review`/`Sanctions_Check` certs are still valid (checked via `cert_expiry` in snapshot), allow `Register` (re-register)  `Sanctions_Check` (re-run if cert expired; skip if valid)  `Decision` (may skip `QR` if cert valid). Still require `Manual_Procurement_Approval` unless under active FD-7 (provisional). `Auto_Approve` only allowed under active overlay.
     - `Request_More_Info` loop: UI allows repeat requests; system prevents `Activate` until all requested items are `received`. No loop in POWL; data rule enforces safety.
   - **Data Quality Assurance**: For CRM migration, migrate existing mutable links to stable `notification_id` if possible; otherwise, audit log includes `original_mutable_text` as metadata. Training on stable `notification_id` usage. Periodic check that ~95% of notifications have stable ID.

5. **Scope Rules (clear boundaries)**:
   - `M0` (POWL) applies to **accepted supplier onboarding** (i.e., cases that pass `Sanctions_Check` and proceed to `Decision`). Rejected leads and reactivations follow scope rules above (not M0’s full path).
   - Overlays (`PD-2`, `FD-7`) apply only during their effective dates (inclusive). No automatic extension; a new overlay must be created for continued use post-expiry.
   - `CS-9-base` applies to all accepted onboarding outside active overlays (i.e., default rule).
   - Emergency overlays (`FD-7`) apply only to suppliers classified as “critical relief” at register time (e.g., pump suppliers during flood); not automatically applied to all suppliers post-flood.

> *Where each belongs: POWL = minimal control flow (no data/policy logic); PolicyOverlay table = organizational metadata (versioned, effective dates); Data-Aware Checks = validation logic in onboarding app/pandas (e.g., `if activate_attempt and active_overlay.auto_approve_allowed and scope_match: allow_skip = True`); Organizational Controls = process documentation, UI prompts, governance procedures. This separation ensures POWL remains semantically clean, while compliance is enforced via data checks tied to overlays.*

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

**Revised POWL (control flow only; no policy conditions):**
```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# Transitions (labels only; no data logic)
reg = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")
sc = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
qr = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
ma = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
act = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
rej = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
notf = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")
cl = Transition(label="Close_Case")

# Concurrent checks (SC and QR can proceed in parallel)
checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sc, qr])

# Approved branch (MA  Activate); Reject is alternative
approved_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[ma, act])
approved_branch.order.add_edge(ma, act)

# Decision: XOR between approved branch and Reject
decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[approved_branch, rej])

# Main chain: Register  checks  decision  Notify  Close
M = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[reg, checks, decision, notf, cl])
M.order.add_edge(reg, checks)
M.order.add_edge(checks, decision)
M.order.add_edge(decision, notf)
M.order.add_edge(notf, cl)

# Note: Request_More_Info is NOT in POWL. Data rule (see below) prevents Activate before info received.
# This is a deliberate, defensible approximation: POWL models mandatory sequence; conditional loops are handled by validation.
```

**Event/data collection proposal (snapshot + validation logic; not part of POWL):**
```python
import uuid
from datetime import date, timedelta

# Immutable snapshot created at Register_Supplier
def create_snapshot(register_date, risk_level, country, corporate_group_approved,
                    supplier_type=None, emergency=False, certs_valid=True):
    snapshot = {
        "notification_id": str(uuid.uuid4()),  # stable, generated once
        "register_date": register_date,        # date object
        "risk_level": risk_level,             # "HIGH"/"LOW"
        "country": country,
        "corporate_group_approved": corporate_group_approved,  # True/False
        "supplier_type": supplier_type,      # e.g., "pump", "general"
        "emergency": emergency,               # True if flood emergency applies (set by ops), e.g., during FD-7 window
        "certs_valid": certs_valid,          # True if prior certs still valid (for reactivation)
        "coi_disclosure": False,             # set to True when disclosure recorded
        "info_requested": [],                 # list of item IDs
        "info_status": {},                   # {item_id: "pending" | "received"}
        "active_overlays": [],               # list of overlay_ids where effective_start <= register_date <= effective_end
        "policy_version": "2024-rev1"       # metadata
    }
    return snapshot

# Determine active overlays at snapshot creation/activate time
def get_active_overlays(snapshot):
    today = snapshot["register_date"]  # use register date or activate date; consistent
    active = []
    for overlay_id, overlay in policy_overlays.items():
        start = date.fromisoformat(overlay["effective_start"])
        end = date.fromisoformat(overlay["effective_end"])
        if start <= today <= end:
            # Check scope criteria; if criteria met, include
            if overlay["scope_criteria"](snapshot):  # simple True/False check
                active.append(overlay_id)
    return active

# Validate Activate_Supplier attempt
def can_activate(snapshot, activate_date):
    """Return (allowed, reason, override_needed)"""
    # 1. Sanctions_Check must be completed (enforced by workflow; snapshot tracks sc_done)
    if not snapshot["sc_done"]:
        return (False, "Sanctions_Check not completed", None)
    
    # 2. Get active overlays at activate time
    today = activate_date  # date object
    active_overlay_ids = []
    for overlay_id, overlay in policy_overlays.items():
        start = date.fromisoformat(overlay["effective_start"])
        end = date.fromisoformat(overlay["effective_end"])
        if start <= today <= end and overlay["scope_criteria"](snapshot):
            active_overlay_ids.append(overlay)
    
    # 3. COI check
    if snapshot["corporate_group_same_line"] and not snapshot["coi_disclosure"]:
        return (False, "COI disclosure required before MA approval", None)
    
    # 4. Request_More_Info check
    if snapshot["info_requested"] and not all(status == "received" for status in snapshot["info_status"].values()):
        return (False, "Not all requested info received", None)
    
    # 5. Check auto-approve eligibility
    auto_allowed = False
    for overlay in active_overlay_ids:
        if overlay["auto_approve_allowed"]:
            # Provisional auto-approve allowed for FD-7; full auto for PD-2
            auto_allowed = True
            break
    
    # 6. Spend check
    if "spend" in snapshot and snapshot["spend"] > 0:
        max_cap = None
        for overlay in active_overlay_ids:
            if overlay["spend_cap_eur"] is not None:
                max_cap = max(max_cap, overlay["spend_cap_eur"]) if max_cap else overlay["spend_cap_eur"]
        if max_cap is not None and snapshot["spend"] > max_cap:
            # Block Activate; require retrospective approval
            return (False, f"Spend {snapshot['spend']} > cap {max_cap}; need retrospective approval", "pending_retrospective")
    
    # 7. Final decision
    if auto_allowed:
        # MA may be skipped if no COI issue and info complete
        if not snapshot["corporate_group_same_line"] or snapshot["coi_disclosure"]:
            # Allow Activate without MA if info complete
            if all(status == "received" for status in snapshot["info_status"].values()):
                return (True, "Auto-approve allowed (overlay active)", None)
            else:
                return (False, "Info pending; wait for completion", None)
        else:
            return (False, "COI requires MA or disclosure", None)
    else:
        # Default: require MA
        return (True, "Manual approval required (no active auto overlay)", None)

# Example usage in onboarding app:
# At Register: create snapshot with notification_id, set sc_done=False, info_requested=[], info_status={}
# At Sanctions_Check completion: set snapshot["sc_done"] = True
# At Request_More_Info: add item to info_requested, set info_status[item_id] = "pending"
# At Quality_Review completion: set snapshot["qr_done"] = True (optional; monitoring tracks due date)
# At Manual_Procurement_Approval: if auto_allowed and conditions met, allow skip; else require MA
# At Activate_Supplier: call can_activate(snapshot, activate_date). If False, show error; if True, proceed.
# At Notify_Requester: use snapshot["notification_id"] to send via CRM; log notification_id in event log.
# At Close_Case: check all mandatory steps done (SC for accepted, SC for rejected); notify sent.
```

> **Limitations acknowledged & defensible**:
> - POWL does not encode `auto_approve` logic, COI checks, or spend caps; these are enforced by data validation (`can_activate`). This is necessary because POWL is a process model (not a policy engine), and PM4Py `StrictPartialOrder`/`OperatorPOWL` are for control flow, not conditional logic.
> - `Request_More_Info` loop is not in POWL (to keep strict partial order); instead, `can_activate` checks `info_status`. All observed cases waited for evidence, so this is safe. If someone attempted Activate before RMi, the data rule blocks it—matching observed safe practice.
> - `notification_id` is stable UUID; mutable text fields for old logs are metadata only (no enforcement), fixing N4’s traceability gap.
> - Overlay scope criteria are simple boolean checks; for complex criteria (e.g., group membership), the app validates against group DB at snapshot time.
> - No permanent auto-approval policy: PD-2/FD-7 are time-bound; post-expiry requires new overlay. Operations normalizing pilot after expiry gets `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` (label), but `can_activate` will allow Auto_Approve only if an active overlay exists (i.e., no auto-approve after expiry unless new overlay created).
> - COI disclosure is recorded in snapshot; if not disclosed, MA approval is blocked—aligns with policy but avoids automatic noncompliance without evidence.

**Why this proposal is fair and traceable**:
- POWL is minimal and semantically clear (concurrent SC/QR, XOR decision, notify before close). No contradictory modeling.
- Overlays are versioned with effective dates; strict inclusive boundaries prevent ambiguity.
- Data snapshot includes all needed context (`risk_level`, `corporate_group`, `emergency`, `notification_id`, `coi_disclosure`, `info_status`).
- Validation (`can_activate`) uses only snapshot fields and overlay rules; no external logic. Easy to audit (traceable reason for allow/block).
- CRM uses stable `notification_id`; mutable text is metadata, resolving N4’s issue.
