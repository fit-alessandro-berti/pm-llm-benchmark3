```python
def fire_named(net, marking, transition_name):
    enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
    enabled_names = {t.name: t for t in enabled}
    if transition_name not in enabled_names:

m = fire_named(net, m, "t_analyze")  # produces orphan p_analysis_done
```

**Expected qualitative difference**: The original should report `check_is_workflow_net` as `True` but `check_soundness` should fail (diagnostics will indicate that from reachable states the final marking is not always reachable or that dead transitions/leftover tokens exist). The repaired net should return `True` for both checks, and its reachability graph should contain a path from the initial marking to the exact final marking with no dead ends carrying leftover tokens.

# 7. Repair Trade-Offs and Residual Risks

- **Distinguishability**: In Repair D, `t_standard_review` and `t_waive_review` remain separate, and `t_join_waived` (silent) is structurally distinct from the direct standard path, so the two review outcomes are still traceable.
- **Silent-transition confusion**: `t_join_waived` is silent (`label=None`) and now acts as a mapper rather than a true synchronization join. This could confuse maintainers; adding a visible label (e.g., `"Map Waiver to Review Done"`) would mitigate this without changing behavior.
- **Soundness  full business alignment**: Soundness guarantees that every reachable state can reach `sink` alone, but it does not by itself prove that `t_analyze` fired on every trace. In Repair D, the structural dependency (`t_join_normal` requires `p_analysis_done`) makes bypass impossible, so soundness plus the arc structure together prove the mandatory-analysis rule.
- **Regression-testing traces**:
  - Positive: `register  split  (analyze || standard_review)  join_normal  authorize`; and `register  split  (analyze || waive_review  join_waived)  join_normal  authorize`.
  - Negative: `register  split  waive_review  join_waived  authorize` must be impossible (not enabled) in the repaired net; in the original it is enabled.
- **Detecting accidental analysis bypass**: Monitor that any marking reaching `p_decision` also contains `p_analysis_done` in its history, or instrument logs to verify `t_analyze` executes before `t_authorize`. In the repaired model, static analysis of prerequisites confirms this: `p_decision` is reachable only via `t_join_normal`, which requires `p_analysis_done`.

# 8. Final Recommendation

**Deploy Repair D** (map waiver to `p_review_done`). It eliminates the dangerous direct path from waiver to authorization, unifies synchronization through `t_join_normal`, and enforces mandatory analysis structurally with only two arc changes. It is cleaner and less error-prone than maintaining two separate joins.

**Fallback: Repair A** (add `p_analysis_done`  `t_join_waived`). It is globally minimal (cost 1) and keeps the explicit separate synchronization for the waiver path. If organizational requirements later demand that the waiver path be visibly isolated (e.g., for audit logging of a separate “waived authorization” gate), Repair A is easier to annotate without restructuring the normal path.

Both repairs preserve the mandatory-analysis policy and restore soundness; Repair D is preferred for maintainability and reduced structural redundancy.
