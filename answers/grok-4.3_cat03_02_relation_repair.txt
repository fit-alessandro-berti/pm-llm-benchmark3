## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model
Hard invariants: (1) Create Order relates to exactly one Order (cardinality from activity definition); (2) Add Item relates to one Order and one Item; (3) Record Payment relates to exactly one Payment; (4) O2O qualifiers are directional and cannot be reversed. Strong defaults: Pack Item relates to one Item and one Package (scanner outage may omit package); Dispatch Package relates to one Package and one Delivery unless bulk dispatch is signaled; Issue Invoice relates to one Invoice and one or more Orders. Weak heuristics: identifier prefix similarity (N-42*), route matching (NORTH), timestamp proximity, and declared_value/total arithmetic serve only as supporting signals. Evidence against a repair: missing qualifier in observed_related_objects, conflicting route or customer values, or violation of observed O2O directionality. Evidence is combined by first enforcing hard invariants on E2O, then checking consistency with existing O2O edges, then weighing timestamp/route/customer signals only when multiple candidates remain; unresolved status is preferred over forced completion when signals conflict or are absent.

## 3. Alternative repair bundles
Bundle A (conservative/minimum-change) adds only P01 and P04 (PK-8–IT-B1 via ev08), leaves ev10 linked solely to PK-8 with DEL-Y unresolved, treats ev11 as customer-only acknowledgment without delivery object, adds no O2O for INV-44–ORD-B, and leaves PAY-2 unlinked to INV-44. This yields coherent but incomplete lifecycles: PK-8 now contains two items, DEL-Y and PAY-2 remain open, ORD-B ends without invoice. Bundle B adds P01/P04, assigns ev10 to DEL-Y, adds a weak ev11–DEL-X link plus DEL-X–PK-8 O2O, adds INV-44–ORD-B O2O, and links PAY-2 to INV-44. The bundle produces fuller lifecycles for Delivery and Invoice objects but introduces a possible many-to-one invoice-to-order pattern and risks circular confirmation between E2O and O2O edges. Both bundles preserve the observed O2O directionality and avoid fabricating new objects.

## 5. Lifecycle and cardinality audit
Order objects receive complete lifecycles under both bundles; no orphan events. Package objects gain a plausible two-item lifecycle only after P01/P04; PK-7 remains single-item. Delivery objects stay partially orphaned in Bundle A (DEL-Y without package link) and gain a one-to-many carries relation in Bundle B. Invoice receives a one-to-two bills relation only in Bundle B, which is suspicious but cardinality-legal. No circular reasoning occurs because O2O edges are added only when supported by direct E2O scanner evidence, not retrofitted from assumed invoice totals. PAY-2 remains an open payment in both bundles, correctly reflecting possible unapplied cash.

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects
The unrepaired log produces an OC-DFG missing the Pack Item–Package edge for IT-B1 and an OTG with isolated DEL-Y and PAY-2 nodes. Bundle A adds one E2O and one O2O edge, creating a single new Package–Item fragment without altering Order or Delivery flattenings; activity frequencies on Package rise by one object count but not by event count. Bundle B adds four edges, duplicating the Dispatch–Delivery edge for DEL-Y and introducing an Invoice–Order many-to-one path that appears in Order flattening but not in Delivery flattening. ET–OT graph gains a Confirm Delivery–Delivery link only in Bundle B. Equivalent-execution clustering around Order remains stable in Bundle A but splits into two clusters in Bundle B once ORD-B receives an invoice edge. No flattening on Payment is affected because PAY-2 stays unresolved.

## 8. Over-repair warnings
1. Forcing ev11–DEL-X would fabricate a delivery confirmation that never appears in observed_related_objects, creating a spurious Delivery–Order edge visible in every flattening. 2. Linking PAY-2 to INV-44 solely on amount match would duplicate the settles relation and inflate Payment frequency counts in Invoice flattening. 3. Adding DEL-Y to ev10 without scanner evidence would create a false many-to-one Dispatch–Delivery pattern that disappears when route_hint is later contradicted. 4. Inventing an O2O from INV-44 to ORD-B on arithmetic alone would produce a one-to-many bills relation unsupported by any E2O row, biasing equivalent-execution clustering. 5. Completing PK-8–IT-B1 via inferred O2O without the ev08 E2O qualifier would allow the OTG to show a Package–Item edge that is actually an artifact of the repair rather than observed data.
