## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Eligibility and routing**

- Every registered case may begin Identity Check.
- Full Identity–Document concurrency is eligible only when originals are not legally required and the document channel confirms that verification can begin immediately.
- For originals-required cases, start Identity Check and Originals Receipt concurrently. Document Check remains blocked until a verified receipt event is recorded. A logically blocked placeholder may exist, but it must not count as active Document work.
- The first implementation stratum should be no-originals, digitally checkable, non-legal, standard-complexity cases. Legal cases and originals-required cases enter later through separate gates.
- Public-sector and high-value cases retain mandatory Legal Review.

**Concurrent and sequential work**

- Eligible no-original cases: Identity Check and Document Check may execute concurrently after registration.
- Originals-required cases: Identity Check and Originals Receipt may execute concurrently; Document Check begins only after receipt.
- Assessment begins only after both Identity Check and Document Check are complete.
- No legal decision or final approval may occur before the applicable legal gate.

**Queue and WIP controls**

- Begin with one executable case per worker in each role: four identity, seven document, three assessment, and two legal ready-WIP positions. Increase only after a pre-specified stability period.
- Cases waiting for originals do not consume document-reviewer active WIP.
- Keep control and treatment queues separate during the pilot. Prohibit cross-queue borrowing and batch release.
- Use age-based escalation, but do not introduce treatment-only huddles, staffing changes, or API upgrades.
- Monitor stage-level ready queue, queue age, service time, active ownership, and utilization separately.

**Resource responsibilities**

- Identity reviewers own identity work; document reviewers own document work; assessors own Assessment; legal reviewers own Legal Review.
- An originals coordinator may monitor receipt evidence but may not perform Document Check.
- Cross-role task switching requires explicit handoff and should not be assumed from Site A’s cross-trained model.
- Legal and assessment capacity remain fixed during the initial causal comparison.

**Legal and supplement handling**

- Legal Review may issue Approve, Reject, or Request Supplement.
- A supplement reopens Document Check, then Assessment, then Legal Review. It must not bypass the legal gate.
- Supplement cause, prior decision, rework owner, and regenerated timestamps must be linked to the original case.

**Logging requirements**

- Record task ready, start, end, owner, predecessor evidence, original-receipt proof, WIP, queue entry/exit, event-entry time, re-open count, decision, and appeal.
- Distinguish task creation, execution readiness, actual start, and retrospective event entry.
- Compute overlap from intersecting active-execution intervals and audit simultaneous owner IDs.
- Preserve the legacy case identifier across task and event records.
- Log all API, staffing, queue-management, and case-mix changes during the pilot.

A compact partial-order relation set is:

```text
R  -> I
R  -> O_req                                  [originals-required branch only]
O_req -> D
R  -> D                                     [no-originals branch only]
I  -> Asmt
D  -> Asmt
Asmt -> L                                   [legal subset]
Asmt -> X                                   [non-legal subset]
L  -> X | S
S  -> D_repeat -> Asmt_repeat -> L
X  -> Notify
X  is Approve or Reject
```

For no-original cases, \(I\parallel D\); for originals-required cases, \(I\parallel O_{\text{req}}\) and \(D\) follows \(O_{\text{req}}\). In both cases, Assessment waits for \(I\) and \(D\).

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

The factorial separates scheduling from measurement: all arms receive the same case mix and capacity rules, while parallel versus current order and real-time versus ordinary event capture are independently varied. A rise in recorded overlap without concurrent active owners, or with execution timestamps entered later, identifies an instrumentation artifact. If true overlap rises but waiting before Assessment does not fall, mediator and queue data distinguish an assessment-capacity shift from a broken implementation. If overlap and waiting both change but cycle time does not, the effect is being absorbed downstream. Absence of overlap in a technically capable arm is an implementation or IT failure, not evidence against the partial-order mechanism; overlap in an incapable arm is not a valid per-protocol success. Legal and originals strata are staged only after the core stratum passes safety and fidelity checks. Analysis remains intention-to-treat, with per-protocol estimates clearly secondary and sensitivity analyses for spillovers, workarounds, and selective missingness. Site A’s nine-hour contrast is motivation only, never a calibration target.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    Activity,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Operator,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# Helpers
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
def make_strict_order(top_level_nodes, edges):
    """
    Construct a PM4Py POWL StrictPartialOrder.

    OperatorPOWL instances own their children through POWL parent/child
    relationships. StrictPartialOrder.add() establishes the same relation
    for top-level children. order_adding contains explicit precedence edges.
    """
    order = StrictPartialOrder()
    for node in top_level_nodes:
        order.add(node)
    order.order_adding.extend(edges)
    return order


def report_properties(name, net):
    """Run soundness/workflow checks without assuming a particular PM4Py version's return type."""
    try:
        workflow_result = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
        print(f"{name}: is_workflow_net = {workflow_result}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"{name}: workflow-net check could not be completed: {exc}")

    try:
        soundness_result = pm4py.check_soundness(net)
        print(f"{name}: soundness = {soundness_result}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"{name}: soundness check could not be completed: {exc}")


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1-2. Site A before and after process trees, converted to accepting PNs
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
site_a_before_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

site_a_after_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), "
    "'Assessment', X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

# The returned initial and final markings make these accepting Petri nets.
site_a_before_net, site_a_before_initial, site_a_before_final = (
    pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_before_tree)
)
site_a_after_net, site_a_after_initial, site_a_after_final = (
    pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_after_tree)
)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3-5. Site B candidate POWL
#
# Main control flow:
#   Register -> Identity -------------------------------> Assessment
#           -> Originals-or-no-originals -> Document -> Assessment
#   Assessment -> optional Legal Review -> legal outcome
#   legal outcome -> Approve/Reject -> Notify
#                 -> Request Supplement -> Document(repeat)
#                                      -> Assessment(repeat)
#                                      -> Legal Review(repeat)
#                                      -> legal outcome
#
# The supplemental path is an intentional cycle.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
register = Activity("Register")
identity = Activity("Identity Check")

originals = Activity("Originals Receipt")
no_originals = SilentTransition()
receipt_route = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [originals, no_originals]
)

document = Activity("Document Check")
assessment = Activity("Assessment")

legal_review = Activity("Legal Review")
no_legal = SilentTransition()
legal_route = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [legal_review, no_legal]
)

approve = Activity("Approve")
reject = Activity("Reject")
final_choice = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [approve, reject]
)

supplement = Activity("Request Supplement")
legal_outcome = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [final_choice, supplement]
)

repeat_assessment = Activity("Assessment - repeat")
repeat_legal = Activity("Legal Review - repeat")
notify = Activity("Notify")

site_b_candidate = make_strict_order(
    top_level_nodes=[
        register,
        identity,
        receipt_route,
        document,
        assessment,
        legal_route,
        legal_outcome,
        repeat_document,
        repeat_assessment,
        repeat_legal,
        notify,
    ],
    edges=[
        # Entry.
        (site_b_candidate_start := None, register),  # replaced below
    ]
)

# Using assignment above would obscure the intended source transition.
# Rebuild the order cleanly with StrictPartialOrder.start and .end.
site_b_candidate = make_strict_order(
    top_level_nodes=[
        register,
        identity,
        receipt_route,
        document,
        assessment,
        legal_route,
        legal_outcome,
        repeat_document,
        repeat_assessment,
        repeat_legal,
        notify,
    ],
    edges=[
        (site_b_candidate.start, register),

        # Identity and receipt acquisition may proceed concurrently.
        (register, identity),
        (register, receipt_route),

        # Document starts only after the receipt route has resolved.
        (receipt_route, document),

        # Assessment explicitly waits for BOTH identity and document.
        (identity, assessment),
        (document, assessment),

        # Optional legal route. The static XOR over-approximates the
        # data-dependent legal population; runtime routing must guard it.
        (assessment, legal_route),
        (legal_route, legal_outcome),

        # Legal outcome: final decision or supplement.
        (final_choice, notify),
        (supplement, repeat_document),

        # Mandatory legal review after a repeated assessment.
        (repeat_document, repeat_assessment),

        (repeat_legal, legal_outcome),

# faithfully encode:
#   * which individual cases require originals or Legal Review;
```

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go**

Transfer the partial-order mechanism, not Site A’s nine-hour estimate. At Site B, create only work that is legally ready: identity may overlap document checking for no-original claims, and identity may overlap originals receipt for original-required claims. Document verification remains blocked until receipt; assessment remains blocked until both identity and document completion. Do not copy Site A’s all-case immediate start, six-task WIP rule, API gain, added reviewers, huddles, or simpler case mix.

The first pilot cohort should be digitally checkable, no-originals, non-legal-review cases of standard complexity. This maximizes genuine overlap and avoids confusing the scheduling mechanism with receipt, assessment, or legal congestion. Later cohorts can add legal cases and then original-required cases, where the target is identity-receipt overlap rather than prohibited identity-document overlap.

The central scenario is a 1–3 hour median reduction, little automatic p90 gain, and roughly neutral quality. Risks are assessment/legal queue migration, bursty document arrivals, rework from supplements, hidden task switching, and back-entered timestamps. Legal review must remain downstream, and any supplement must reopen document checking, assessment, and legal review.

Expand only if the randomized cohort shows at least a 2-hour reduction in median end-to-end cycle time or wait before assessment, p90 no more than 5% worse than control, rework no more than 1 percentage point worse, no adverse appeal signal, true concurrent execution in at least 90% of technically eligible cases, and event completeness above 98%. Modify rather than expand if benefit is confined to no-original claims, if original-required cases show only safe identity-receipt overlap, or if the assessment queue is the new constraint. Suspend the affected arm for any pre-receipt document work, p90 deterioration above 10%, rework deterioration above 3 points, or statutory-control failure. Abandon full parallelization if fidelity is achieved yet neither wait nor cycle time improves, or if quality deteriorates persistently.

## 10. Assumption register

1. 2. 4.
