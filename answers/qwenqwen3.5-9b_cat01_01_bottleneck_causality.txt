Do not reassign 50% of Orion cases blindly. The data shows Orion is actually faster within complexity strata, meaning the aggregate delay is a confounding artifact of case mix and shift assignment. Moving Orion's high-complexity and night-shift workload to Nova (optimized for day/low complexity) risks increasing cycle times due to shift mismatch and compliance overloads. A safer intervention is a randomized pilot on 10% of cases to isolate the direct group effect while maintaining the current routing for the majority. Simultaneously, optimize night-shift and compliance capacity to reduce queueing, which drives the majority of the cycle time. This approach learns the true causal impact of resource group assignment while mitigating operational risk.

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Unobserved Reviewer Skill**: Individual performance varies; `resource_group` is a proxy for skill distribution.
   *Valuable Data*: Individual reviewer ID linked to case performance.
2. **Abandoned Cases**: The dataset includes only completed cases; high-risk cases may be abandoned before classification.
   *Valuable Data*: Time-to-abandonment or abandonment flag for all cases.
3. **Load Spikes**: Arrival load affects queueing but is aggregated; spikes may cause non-linear delays.
   *Valuable Design Change*: Granular timestamped arrival data to reconstruct queue state.
4. **Compliance Latency**: Compliance check duration is assumed constant; actual processing time may vary by case.
   *Valuable Data*: Actual timestamp for `Compliance Check` start and end.
5. **Quality vs. Time Tradeoff**: Faster cases may be less accurate (e.g., Appeal rate); speed may be at cost of quality.
   *Valuable Data*: Detailed quality metrics per case beyond binary appeal outcome.
