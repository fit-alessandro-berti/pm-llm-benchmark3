Notes on the figures used above.

- Load index = cases × median service hours ÷ (FTE × about 910 productive hours per half-year). Values above 1 show that the "service" intervals are open-task time, not touch time, which matches A's cap of six open tasks. Medians understate means, so the index is a relative indicator only. B's document range is without and with the 345 supplement re-checks.
- Reading of B's DFG: Originals Receipt → Document Check equals the 62% originals share exactly, so the statutory prerequisite is honored. Identity Check → Document Check (1,160) is about 90% of the 1,292 no-originals cases, so the digital segment runs sequentially today. Identity Check → Originals Receipt (1,020) shows identity finishing inside the originals wait in about half of the originals cases.
- The DFG is indicative only: the arcs leaving Register sum to 3,910 for 3,400 cases, and 2,880 arcs enter Originals Receipt against 2,108 leaving it. That implies repeated events, mixed lifecycle transitions or back-entered events.

**Shared mechanisms.** At both sites parallelization can shorten a case only through `treatment_parallelization → true_overlap → verification_lead_time → cycle_time`. Assessment is an AND-join, so the saving per case is at most the lead time of the shorter branch: identity, about 3.4 h of service plus its queue. The quality path `true_overlap → rework → appeal` is also plausibly shared, because checks done side by side cannot use each other's findings.

**Site-specific mechanisms.** At A, digital documents, cross-trained reviewers and simultaneous task ownership let the treatment become overlap (8% to 71% recorded). At B, `originals_required` and the originals delay put the document branch on the critical path for 62% of cases, `IT_case_lock` blocks or falsifies overlap, separate pools cannot flex, and legal review adds a supplement loop that A does not have. Queue huddles, the API upgrade, added reviewers and the simpler mix belong to A's period, not to the treatment.

**Effect modifiers.** `digital_documents`, `originals_required`, `IT_case_lock`, `role_specialization`, the four capacities, `case_complexity` and legal review change the size or sign of the effect. Each has an `S_` node pointing into it, so A's pooled effect cannot be carried over. Only stratum-specific effects re-weighted to B's mix are candidates, and A never observed the strata B has most of.

**Why similar diagrams are not enough.** A control-flow model states which orders are permitted. The effect depends on which branch is critical, who is free to do the work, whether the system lets two people hold a case, and what waits downstream. B's actual partial order also differs: Identity Check ∥ (Originals Receipt → Document Check), followed by a legal loop.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

Reasoning from Site A evidence and Site B constraints to the ranges:

1. **What A's 9 h can support.** Service times fell 1.1 h in total (API, staffing, mix); parallel work does not shorten service. Waiting before Assessment fell 6 h, which is more naturally explained by huddles, a 13% lower load index and a simpler mix. Structurally, parallelization saves at most the identity-branch lead time (3.1–3.5 h plus queue), and at most 63 points of cases became newly overlapping. That gives a working prior of 1–5 h of the 9 h for parallelization at A, or about 1.6–8 h per newly overlapping case. This is a prior, not an estimate.
2. **Saving per eligible case at B.** Δ = L × f × (1 − a), where L is identity-branch lead time (3.4 h service plus 1–3 h queue, so 4.4–6.4 h), f is the share of eligible cases that truly overlap (0.5–0.85), and a is the share of the saving absorbed downstream (0.1–0.5). Low 4.4 × 0.5 × 0.5 ≈ 1.1 h; central 5.4 × 0.7 × 0.65 ≈ 2.5 h; high 6.4 × 0.85 × 0.9 ≈ 4.9 h. This agrees with A's implied 1.6–8 h per overlapping case scaled by fidelity.
3. **Eligible share.** Only no-originals cases change their partial order: 38%. If originals and legal status are independent, the no-originals, non-legal segment is 0.38 × 0.60 ≈ 23% (plausibly 15–28%).
4. **Population effect.** Share × Δ gives 0.4–1.9 h on the mean for all digital cases (central about 1 h, roughly 2% of 44 h), against A's naive 22%. The overall median moves that much only if digital cases straddle 44 h; if they already finish faster, it barely moves.
5. **Tail.** B's top decile is 340 cases; supplements alone are 360 cases, and originals delay is highly variable. Parallelization removes none of these and can add to them through priority transfer, extra supplements and speculative document work.

Confidence is my credence that the stated ranges contain the outcome if that version is deployed under those conditions; scenarios are not mutually exclusive and do not sum to 100.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Name: segmented early release of verification.** It replaces "create both tasks for every case" with rules per segment.

**Eligibility rules (decided at Register from case data).**
- Segment D0: originals not required and no legal review. Concurrent creation and execution of Identity Check and Document Check. First wave.
- Segment D1: originals not required, legal review required. Same concurrency, second wave, only after D0 passes its gates.
- Segment O: originals required (62%). No concurrent Document Check. Not part of the parallelization treatment.
- Exclusions, which stay sequential: fraud or risk flags, known identity discrepancies, and document types whose check legally depends on the identity result.

**Tasks that may be created or executed concurrently.**
- D0 and D1: Identity Check and Document Check are both created at Register and may be executed at the same time by different reviewers.
- Segment O: Identity Check and the originals request are released at Register. The Document Check task is created in a blocked state for planning and is released automatically by the logged Originals Receipt event.
- If Identity Check fails or raises a discrepancy, an open Document Check is suspended and the document reviewer is notified.

**Statutory prerequisites that remain mandatory.**
- Register first.
- Originals Receipt before Document Check wherever originals are legally required.
- Both checks complete before Assessment.
- Legal Review for public-sector or high-value cases before the final decision.
- Approve or Reject before Notify.

**Queue and WIP controls.**
- Cap of six open verification tasks per reviewer to start, as at A; review after four weeks.
- CONWIP release: concurrent Document Check tasks are released only while the document pool has fewer than 35 open tasks (7 reviewers × 5). Above that, new eligible cases fall back to the sequential path.
- All queues sort by case registration age, not task creation time, so early-released digital cases do not overtake originals and supplement cases.
- Supplement re-checks have a reserved priority lane in the document and assessment queues.
- Throttle: if median waiting before Assessment exceeds 24 h for five working days, the early-release share is halved.
- Daily queue huddles are introduced for all cases before the pilot, so they are not confounded with the treatment.

**Resource responsibilities.**
- Identity reviewers (4) own Identity Check; document reviewers (7) own Document Check; no cross-role execution.
- A rotating verification coordinator owns the join, the fallback decisions and cross-finding messages between the two checks.
- Assessors (3) pull only cases with both checks complete.
- Legal reviewers (2) see the legal flag from Register for planning but start only after Assessment.
- IT provides paired sub-tasks under one case, each with its own lock, so no workaround is needed.

**Legal-review and supplement loops.**
- Legal Review follows Assessment for flagged cases.
- Request Supplement returns the case to Document Check (after receipt of new originals where the supplement requires them), then Assessment, then Legal Review again. Identity Check is not repeated unless the supplement changes the identity basis.
- Loop count is recorded; a second supplement request escalates to a senior legal reviewer.

**Logging requirements.**
- System-generated created, released, start and complete timestamps for every task, with resource ID.
- Entry time stored separately from event time; lags over 30 minutes are flagged.
- Case attributes fixed at Register: originals required, legal required, channel, complexity, value band, pilot arm and treatment version.
- Lock and unlock events, originals requested and received, identity outcome, suspension events, supplement loop index and rework reason codes.

**Partial-order (POWL-style) description.**

```text
Nodes
  R    = Register
  IC   = Identity Check
  XO   = XOR( Originals Receipt , tau )          guard: Originals Receipt iff originals_required
  DC   = Document Check
  AS   = Assessment
  XL   = XOR( LOOP( Legal Review ,
                    PO{ Request Supplement < XOR( PO{ Document Check' < Assessment' } , tau ) } ) ,
              tau )                              guard: loop iff public_sector or high_value
  XD   = XOR( Approve , Reject )
  N    = Notify

Order (strict partial order, transitive closure implied)
  R < IC        R < XO        XO < DC
  IC < AS       DC < AS
  AS < XL       XL < XD       XD < N

Concurrency (unordered pairs)
  IC || XO      IC || DC

Segment-specific refinements (data guards, outside POWL)
  D0, D1 : XO = tau, so IC || DC from Register        (new behaviour)
  O      : XO = Originals Receipt, so IC || (Originals Receipt < DC)   (already legal today)
  Excluded cases: add IC < DC                          (sequential fallback)
```

| Interference controls | Queues sorted by case age in all arms; no arm-specific priority; spillover estimated from the saturation contrast; staffing frozen or logged daily | Shared reviewers make control cases react to treated ones; this measures it instead of assuming it away | Coordinator logs every fallback and throttle event | Waiting times of control and untreated cases by week saturation |
| Power or minimum-support logic | Log cycle times have a standard deviation of about 0.7 (from p90/median = 2.5). A 25% fall in verification lead time needs about 100 cases per arm (D0 reaches this around week 12). A 15% fall in cycle time needs about 300 per arm; D0 alone (about 225 per arm) detects about 18%; D0 plus D1 reaches about 315. Rework differences under 8 points are not detectable and are treated as guardrails | States in advance what the pilot can and cannot show | Count the real D0 and D1 volumes in Phase 0; extend the phase if volume is lower | Weekly cumulative sample by stratum and arm |
| Duration | Weeks 1–2 A/A; 3–4 V1 probe; 5–10 D0 randomized; 11–19 D0 and D1 randomized; then 90 days of appeal follow-up | Covers several full saturation blocks and the lagging quality outcome | Decision points at weeks 4, 10 and 19 | Time series of all outcomes with phase markers |
| Stop rules | Immediate halt: any Document Check start before logged Originals Receipt in an originals case. Pause: rework in treated cases more than 5 points above control with 90% posterior probability. Throttle: waiting before Assessment above 24 h for five working days, or p90 of untreated strata more than 10% worse in 100% weeks than in 0% weeks. Fidelity stop: true overlap below 40% after four weeks of V2. Data stop: more than 10% of verification events with entry lag over 30 minutes | Protects legality, quality and the tail; prevents estimating an effect on an undelivered treatment | Stop decisions belong to a named process owner and legal counsel | Conformance checking against the segment model; guardrail dashboards |
| Transportability analysis | Estimate stratum-specific effects at B by channel, legal status and complexity. Estimate the first stage (assignment to true overlap) and the effect per truly overlapped case with assignment as the instrument. Compare that per-case saving with the structural bound (identity-branch lead time) and with A's implied 1.6–8 h. Re-weight only across strata that were piloted; make no extrapolation to originals cases. Ask A for its event log to estimate stratum effects there | Tests whether the shared mechanism has the same size once modifiers are held fixed | Publish the modifier table before unblinding | Stratified performance spectra; critical-path share of identity; variant comparison between arms |

The pilot measures the causal chain link by link. Link 1, assignment to true overlap, is an implementation question. If treated cases do not show overlapping system-stamped intervals (target at least 70%, failure below 40%), or overlap appears only in back-entered events, the intervention was not delivered and nothing has been learned about the mechanism. The V1 probe shows how much of this is the legacy lock.

Link 2, true overlap to verification lead time, is the mechanism. Among cases that truly overlapped, estimated with assignment as the instrument and not by comparing self-selected cases, the saving should approach the identity-branch lead time. High fidelity with no saving means identity was not on the critical path, so the mechanism does not hold at B.

Link 3, verification lead time to cycle time, is absorption. A verification saving that disappears before Notify, together with longer waits before Assessment or slower control cases in 100% weeks, shows a bottleneck shift and not a failed idea.

Link 4, overlap to rework and supplements, is the quality cost. The A/A phase shows what the new logging alone does to every metric, so measurement change is not mistaken for either kind of failure.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def to_net_and_check(name, model):
    """Convert a process tree or POWL model to an accepting Petri net and verify it."""
    net, im, fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(model)
    is_wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    res = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)          # bool or (bool, diagnostics), depending on version
    is_sound = res[0] if isinstance(res, tuple) else bool(res)
    print(f"{name}: places={len(net.places)} transitions={len(net.transitions)} "
          f"workflow_net={is_wf} sound={is_sound}")
    return net, im, fm


def sample_traces(net, im, fm, k=8):
    """Play out the net and return the distinct label sequences (EventLog or DataFrame output)."""
    log = pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm)
    if hasattr(log, "groupby"):                        # DataFrame in some configurations
        traces = [tuple(g["concept:name"]) for _, g in log.groupby("case:concept:name", sort=False)]
    else:
        traces = [tuple(ev["concept:name"] for ev in trace) for trace in log]
    distinct = sorted(set(traces), key=lambda t: (len(t), t))
    for t in distinct[:k]:
        print("   ", " > ".join(t))
    return distinct


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Site A (process trees)
site_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', X( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )")
site_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->( 'Register', +( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', X( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )")

net_a0, im_a0, fm_a0 = to_net_and_check("Site A before", site_a_before)
net_a1, im_a1, fm_a1 = to_net_and_check("Site A after ", site_a_after)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Site B candidate (POWL)
register = Transition(label="Register")
identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")
originals = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
doc_check = Transition(label="Document Check")
assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")
notify = Transition(label="Notify")

# Originals Receipt happens only when originals are legally required (62%); otherwise it is skipped.
originals_if_required = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[originals, SilentTransition()])

# Supplement loop body: Request Supplement, then (optionally) a repeated Document Check -> Assessment.
# Repeated activities need their own Transition objects (same label, different node).
legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
request_supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
doc_recheck = Transition(label="Document Check")
reassessment = Transition(label="Assessment")

repeat_work = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[doc_recheck, reassessment])
repeat_work.order.add_edge(doc_recheck, reassessment)
repeat_if_needed = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[repeat_work, SilentTransition()])

supplement = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[request_supplement, repeat_if_needed])
supplement.order.add_edge(request_supplement, repeat_if_needed)

# LOOP(do, redo): Legal Review; then either exit or run the supplement body and return to Legal Review.
legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[legal_review, supplement])
# Legal branch is optional: only public-sector / high-value cases (40%) take it.
legal_if_required = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[legal_loop, SilentTransition()])

decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR,
                        children=[Transition(label="Approve"), Transition(label="Reject")])

site_b = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, identity, originals_if_required, doc_check,
                                   assessment, legal_if_required, decision, notify])
order = site_b.order
order.add_edge(register, identity)                  # rule 2: identity may start after registration
order.add_edge(register, originals_if_required)     # rule 3: originals receipt may start after registration
order.add_edge(originals_if_required, doc_check)    # rule 4: no document check before originals (if required)
order.add_edge(identity, assessment)                # rule 5: assessment waits for identity ...
order.add_edge(doc_check, assessment)               # rule 5: ... AND for document verification
order.add_edge(assessment, legal_if_required)       # rule 6: legal review (if required) after assessment
order.add_edge(legal_if_required, decision)         # rule 8: final decision after the legal branch
order.add_edge(decision, notify)                    # rule 8: notify last
# Deliberately NO edge between identity and {originals_if_required, doc_check}: that is the concurrency.
if hasattr(order, "add_transitive_edges"):
    order.add_transitive_edges()                    # make the relation an explicit strict partial order
assert order.is_irreflexive()

print("Site B candidate POWL:", site_b)
net_b, im_b, fm_b = to_net_and_check("Site B candidate", site_b)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- Playout and dependency checks
print("Site A after, distinct traces:")
sample_traces(net_a1, im_a1, fm_a1)
print("Site B candidate, sample of distinct traces:")
traces_b = sample_traces(net_b, im_b, fm_b)

for t in traces_b:
    first_assessment = t.index("Assessment")
    assert t[0] == "Register" and t[-1] == "Notify"
    assert "Identity Check" in t[:first_assessment] and "Document Check" in t[:first_assessment]
    if "Originals Receipt" in t:
        assert t.index("Originals Receipt") < t.index("Document Check")
    if "Request Supplement" in t:
        assert t.index("Legal Review") < t.index("Request Supplement")
orders = {(t.index("Identity Check") < t.index("Document Check")) for t in traces_b}
print("Identity/Document Check observed in both orders:", orders == {True, False})

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- What the model cannot encode
# * Data-dependent routing: the two XORs are free choices. The model does not know that Originals
#   Receipt is mandatory when originals are legally required, or that Legal Review is mandatory for
#   public-sector / high-value cases. These need guards (data Petri net / decision rules) or
#   per-segment models, and conformance must be checked per segment.
# * Resources: no pools (4 identity, 7 document, 3 assessors, 2 legal), no role separation,
#   no WIP caps, no queue discipline. Concurrency here is permission, not capacity.
# * Case locks: the legacy single-owner lock serializes what the net allows in parallel; true overlap
#   must be measured from start/complete timestamps, not inferred from the model.
# * Time: no service times, originals delay, or waiting; sound does not mean fast.
# * Activities are atomic (no start/complete lifecycle), so "overlap" is only interleaving.
# * Loop semantics: the supplement loop is unbounded and memoryless; an identity failure cannot
#   cancel a running Document Check; a supplement cannot trigger a new Originals Receipt here.
```

## 9. Recommendation

`conditional go`

**What should be transferred.** Transfer the mechanism, not the result: where both checks are legally startable at registration and independent of each other, release them together and let Assessment wait for both. Also transfer the conditions that made this work at A, namely a cap on open verification tasks, lock-free task ownership with system timestamps, and daily queue huddles. The huddles and the cap are worth introducing for all cases on their own merits.

**What should not be copied.** Do not create both tasks for every case. For the 62% of cases needing originals, Document Check cannot legally start, and identity can already run during the originals wait, so the proposal adds nothing and invites checks on copies. Do not copy A's nine-hour expectation; it combines an API upgrade, two extra reviewers, a simpler mix, huddles and better timestamps. Do not rely on lock workarounds, and do not assume cross-trained capacity that B does not have.

**Which subgroup enters first.** Cases without an originals requirement and without legal review, roughly 23% of volume. Digital cases with legal review follow only if the first group passes its gates.

**Expected benefits and risks.** A plausible gain is 2–4 hours within the first segment and about 0.3–1 hour (1–2%) on the overall median, with p90 essentially unchanged. The risks are slower originals and supplement cases through queue displacement, more supplement requests, wasted document work on cases that fail identity, and a recorded improvement that is only a logging artefact.

**Evidence that triggers the next decision.**
- Expansion to digital legal cases: true overlap of at least 70%, a verification lead-time saving of at least 2 hours per treated case, no detectable slowdown of untreated strata, no stop-rule breach, and a rework point estimate at most 2 points above control.
- Modification: good fidelity but the saving is absorbed before Assessment or displaced onto other cases. Then address assessor capacity and queue priority before widening.
- Abandonment of parallelization: IT cannot deliver lock-free sub-tasks, true overlap stays below 40%, identity proves not to be on the critical path, or rework and supplements rise beyond the stop rule. In that case keep the logging, the WIP cap and the huddles, and direct effort at originals delay and the supplement loop, which drive B's tail.

## 10. Assumption register

1. The reported "service" hours are open-task intervals, not touch time — the load indices (about 3.1 at A, 4.3–4.8 for B's document pool) and the structural saving bound depend on it — compare logged intervals with a one-week work sample of touch time per task — the capacity comparison is invalid and the saving bound per case shrinks to touch time, lowering every scenario.
2. "Waiting before Assessment" is the time from completion of the last verification task to the start of Assessment — it underlies attributing A's six-hour fall to co-interventions and not to parallelization — recompute the metric from A's and B's event logs under both definitions — if it includes join waiting, more of A's gain may be due to parallelization and the prior of 1–5 hours should move up.
3. Originals requirement and legal-review requirement are roughly independent, so the first-wave segment is about 23% of volume — it sets the eligible share, the population-level effect and the pilot duration — cross-tabulate both attributes in B's six-month log during Phase 0 — a smaller segment lengthens the pilot and reduces the overall benefit; a larger one does the opposite.
4. For no-originals cases at B, Document Check does not legally or evidentially depend on the Identity Check result — concurrent execution is only safe and lawful if this holds — written opinion from legal counsel plus a review of 100 past cases for identity findings that changed the document outcome — the dependent case types must be excluded, or the segment stays sequential.
5. IT can provide paired sub-tasks with separate locks and system-generated start and complete timestamps on the legacy system — without it only task creation (V1) is possible — a technical spike before Phase 0 and the two-week V1 probe — expect the IT-lock failure scenario; parallelization is not rolled out and only the queue measures proceed.
6. B's document, assessment and legal queues are stable, with utilisation below one — upstream savings reach the end of the process only through stable queues — backlog trend and queue-length mining over the six-month log — capacity or demand must be addressed first, because parallelization then yields no lasting gain.
7. B's DFG counts are approximately right although they do not close (3,910 arcs leave Register for 3,400 cases) — the reading that about 90% of no-originals cases run sequentially today, and hence the size of the opportunity, rests on them — rebuild the DFG from raw lifecycle events and check for duplicates, repeated activities and back-entered events — if digital cases already overlap in practice, the achievable gain is smaller than every scenario states.
8. Parallelization at A caused some but not all of the nine-hour fall, about 1–5 hours — it is the prior that anchors the scenario ranges — a stratified interrupted time series on A's event log by team, complexity and overlap status, using the two-week staggered rollout — if the true share is near zero, B should expect the confounding-only scenario and may limit itself to queue measures; if it is near nine hours, the ranges are too low, although the structural bound makes that unlikely.
