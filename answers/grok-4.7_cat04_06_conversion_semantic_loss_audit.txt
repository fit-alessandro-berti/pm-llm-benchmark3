# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The authoritative specification is the given POWL object, read with ordinary POWL operator meaning. The prose list in the task and the code agree. `Register Change` is the only node with no predecessor. `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` both follow registration and have no edge between them. `optional_legal` is an XOR of `Legal Review` and a silent transition, and its only predecessor is `Assess Risk`. `verification_loop` is a binary loop whose do-child is the chain `Configure Trial` then `Inspect Trial` and whose redo-child is `Correct Defect`; the loop waits for both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence`. There is no edge either way between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`. `Release Change` waits for both of those nodes. `Archive Change` follows release and is the unique maximal node.

Loop meaning used below is the binary POWL/process-tree law: do, then zero or more times (redo then do). That is one or more iterations of `Configure Trial` then `Inspect Trial`, with `Correct Defect` only as the separator between iterations. Activities are atomic. “Overlap” means co-enabledness in some reachable marking and free interleaving of the visible labels, including `Legal Review` in the interior of an iteration.

Abbreviations for witnesses: R = Register Change, A = Assess Risk, P = Prepare Evidence, L = Legal Review, C = Configure Trial, I = Inspect Trial, D = Correct Defect, Rel = Release Change, Arc = Archive Change. A witness is a complete visible trace. Prefixes are not complete.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I1 | R is the unique visible start and precedes every other visible activity | R A P C I Rel Arc | A R P C I Rel Arc | Yes |
| I2 | A and P each occur once, both after R, and neither precedes the other | R A P C I Rel Arc and R P A C I Rel Arc | R A C I P Rel Arc | Yes, as the two orders |
| I3 | L is optional, at most once, and only after A. The other XOR child is a silent skip | With L: R A L P C I Rel Arc. Without L: R A P C I Rel Arc | R A L L P C I Rel Arc and R L A P C I Rel Arc | Partially. Presence and absence of L are visible. The skip firing itself is hidden. A silent join is not separable from an empty branch by visible words alone |
| I4 | The first C waits for both A and P | R P A C I Rel Arc | R A C I P Rel Arc and R P C I A Rel Arc | Yes |
| I5 | No order edge between the optional-legal branch and the verification loop. L may occur before P, between C and I, or after the loop, and in all of those cases before Rel | R A L P C I Rel Arc and R A P C L I Rel Arc and R A P C I L Rel Arc | R A P C I Rel L Arc | Yes for those interleavings. The missing edge is observed by the survival of all three orders |
| I6 | Each iteration is C then I. At least one iteration occurs. A later iteration exists only after D, and D is always followed by another full iteration | One iteration: R A P C I Rel Arc. Two iterations: R A P C I D C I Rel Arc. L between separator and next iteration: R A P C I D L C I Rel Arc | Zero iterations: R A P Rel Arc. End on redo: R A P C I D Rel Arc. Two iterations with no separator: R A P C I C I Rel Arc. Reversed body: R A P I C Rel Arc. D first: R A P D C I Rel Arc | Yes for the visible words. The loop-exit routing transition is hidden; exit is seen as I moving on to L or Rel with no extra visible exit label |

| I9 | The visible alphabet is exactly the nine business labels, each transition label unique. Distinct silent copies are hidden |
| I10 | One initial marking and one final marking. A completed trace is accepted only when the marking equals that final marking exactly | A positive witness that consumes the word and lands on the final marking | The proper prefix R A P C I Rel, which matches a positive prefix and still fails exact final-marking equality | No. Markings are structural. Only the visible start and end labels remain after hiding tau |
| I11 | Unordered visible work is true concurrency: some reachable marking co-enables the pair. It is a separate claim from the set of linear extensions | Co-enabled pairs that must exist: {A, P}, {L, P}, {L, C}, {L, I}, {L, D} | Pairs that must not be co-enabled: {C, I}, {A, L}, {P, C}, {L, Rel}, {Rel, Arc}, and R with any other visible label | No. Interleavings can match while an exclusive choice of total orders never co-enables the pair |

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

Primary pipeline: Pipeline 1. The accepting Petri net is the execution image of this POWL. The outer partial order contains an N-shaped suborder on Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, the legal branch, and verification: Legal Review depends on Assess Risk and not on Prepare Evidence, while verification depends on both, and the legal branch stays unordered relative to verification. That suborder is already present in a single legal iteration, so the loop is not the only obstacle. Process trees are series-parallel blocks. Pipeline 2 is therefore allowed to fail, and that failure is the faithful outcome for a tree intermediate.

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pipeline 1, primary: POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN, then BPMN back to an accepting net for audit | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)`; `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net`; `pm4py.check_soundness`; `pm4py.save_vis_powl`; `pm4py.save_vis_petri_net`; `pm4py.save_vis_bpmn` | The net can keep the N-shaped partial order, the silent XOR, and the binary loop. BPMN can show parallel joins, exclusive choice, and a cyclic verification region without first forcing a process tree. The round trip gives a second net whose visible behavior can be compared | A returned BPMN object only shows that the call completed. Gateway synthesis can turn the N-order into a block, replace the release join with an exclusive join, drop mid-iteration interleavings, promote tau to a drawn task, or duplicate labels. Some PM4Py versions implement Petri-net-to-BPMN by way of a process tree; on this net that route refuses the model. Layout then adds apparent order. Initial or final markings can come back null | Any of those calls raises; a marking is null or empty; the net is unsound or not a workflow net; the visible alphabet changes; a positive witness fails exact final-marking equality; a negative witness reaches the final marking; required co-enabledness disappears; the BPMN object introduces an inclusive gateway or a duplicated task label |
| Pipeline 2: POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN, then back to a net if a tree exists | Same Petri call; `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` inside `try/except`; on success only, `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)`, `pm4py.save_vis_process_tree`, `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net` on that BPMN | A tree would give a compact stakeholder hierarchy and a block-structured BPMN | This behavior is not series-parallel. A structural converter should refuse it. A surprising success would be a different language, a cloned-label encoding, or an exclusive menu of linearizations that preserves some traces and loses co-enabledness | Expected: `convert_to_process_tree` raises or returns `None`. Also a failure if it returns a tree whose derived net misses a witness, admits a negative witness, changes the alphabet, or loses a required co-enabled pair. Swallowing the exception and inventing a tree is an audit failure |

POWL is not sent to a direct POWL-to-BPMN call. The supported BPMN entries used here are a marked Petri net and, only if a tree exists, a process tree.

```python
from __future__ import annotations

import contextlib

import os
from collections import Counter, deque

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    OperatorPOWL,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    Transition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

OUT_DIR = "conversion_audit_artifacts"
MAX_RG_SUMMARY = 50


def build_source_powl():
    register = Transition(label="Register Change")
    assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
    prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

    legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    skip_legal = SilentTransition()
    optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
    )

    configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
    inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
    trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
    trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

    correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
    verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
    )

    release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
    archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

    source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            register,
            assess_risk,
            prepare_evidence,
            optional_legal,
            verification_loop,
            release_change,
            archive_change,
        ]
    )
    source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)
    return source_powl


SOURCE_LABELS = (
    "Register Change",
    "Assess Risk",
    "Prepare Evidence",
    "Legal Review",
    "Configure Trial",
    "Inspect Trial",
    "Correct Defect",
    "Release Change",
    "Archive Change",
)


def fire_transition(transition, net, marking):
    """Fire one transition. This is Petri-net execution, not a conversion API."""
    try:
        from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics as petri_semantics
        return petri_semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
    except Exception:
        from pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics import ClassicSemantics
        return ClassicSemantics().execute(transition, net, marking)


def visible_label(transition):
    """Hide only real silent transitions. A string label is never treated as tau."""
    label = getattr(transition, "label", None)
    if label is None:
        return None
    return label


def marking_key(marking):
    if marking is None:
        return None
    return tuple(
        sorted(
            (id(place), int(tokens))
            for place, tokens in marking.items()
            if tokens
        )
    )


def markings_equal(left, right):
    if left is None or right is None:
        return False
    return marking_key(left) == marking_key(right)


def json_ready(value):
    if isinstance(value, dict):
        return {str(k): json_ready(v) for k, v in value.items()}
    if isinstance(value, (set, frozenset, tuple, list)):
        return [json_ready(v) for v in value]
    if isinstance(value, (str, int, float, bool)) or value is None:
        return value
    return str(value)


def marking_profile(marking, role):
    if marking is None:
        return {"role": role, "present": False, "places": [], "total_tokens": 0}
    places = []
    total = 0
    for place, tokens in marking.items():
        if tokens:
            places.append({"place": getattr(place, "name", str(place)), "tokens": int(tokens)})
            total += int(tokens)
    return {
        "role": role,
        "present": True,
        "n_places": len(places),
        "total_tokens": total,
        "places": places,
    }


def net_alphabet(net):
    visible = []
    silent = 0
    unlabeled_empty = 0
    for transition in net.transitions:
        label = getattr(transition, "label", None)
        if label is None:
            silent += 1
        elif label == "":
            unlabeled_empty += 1
        else:
            visible.append(label)
    counts = Counter(visible)
    return {
        "visible_counts": dict(counts),
        "duplicates": sorted([label for label, count in counts.items() if count > 1]),
        "silent_transitions": silent,
        "empty_string_labels": unlabeled_empty,
        "matches_source_alphabet": (
            set(counts) == set(SOURCE_LABELS)
            and all(count == 1 for count in counts.values())
            and unlabeled_empty == 0
        ),
    }


def workflow_net_flag(net, im, fm):
    try:
        return {"ok": bool(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)), "arity": 1}
    except TypeError:
        try:
            return {"ok": bool(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net, im, fm)), "arity": 3}
        except Exception as exc:
            return {"ok": None, "error": "%s: %s" % (type(exc).__name__, exc)}
    except Exception as exc:
        return {"ok": None, "error": "%s: %s" % (type(exc).__name__, exc)}


def soundness_report(net, im, fm):
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        return {"status": "not tested", "reason": "net or marking is None"}
    buffer = io.StringIO()
    try:
        with contextlib.redirect_stdout(buffer):
            try:
                result = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
            except TypeError:
                result = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)
        return {
            "status": "returned",
            "result": result,
            "diagnostics": buffer.getvalue(),
        }
    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "status": "error",
            "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
            "message": str(exc),
            "diagnostics": buffer.getvalue(),
        }


def structural_ends(net):
    sources = [place.name for place in net.places if len(place.in_arcs) == 0]
    sinks = [place.name for place in net.places if len(place.out_arcs) == 0]
    return {"source_places": sources, "sink_places": sinks}


def save_called(name, saver, path, args):
    try:
        saver(*args, path)
        return {"artifact": name, "path": path, "saved": True}
    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "artifact": name,
            "path": path,
            "saved": False,
            "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
            "message": str(exc),
        }


def summarize_bpmn(bpmn):
    if bpmn is None:
        return {"present": False}
    nodes = list(bpmn.get_nodes())
    flows = list(bpmn.get_flows())
    kinds = Counter(type(node).__name__ for node in nodes)
    names = []
    for node in nodes:
        getter = getattr(node, "get_name", None)
        if getter is None:
            continue
        name = getter()
        if name:
            names.append(name)
    name_counts = Counter(names)
    succ = {id(node): [] for node in nodes}
    for flow in flows:
        source = flow.get_source()
        target = flow.get_target()
        succ.setdefault(id(source), []).append(id(target))
    color = {}

    def has_back_edge(node_id):
        color[node_id] = 1
        for nxt in succ.get(node_id, []):
            state = color.get(nxt, 0)
            if state == 0 and has_back_edge(nxt):
                return True
            if state == 1:
                return True
        color[node_id] = 2
        return False

    cyclic = any(has_back_edge(node_id) for node_id in list(succ) if color.get(node_id, 0) == 0)
    interesting = {
        kind: count
        for kind, count in kinds.items()
        if "Gateway" in kind or "Event" in kind or "Task" in kind or "SubProcess" in kind
    }
    return {
        "present": True,
        "node_kinds": dict(kinds),
        "routing_kinds": interesting,
        "named_nodes": names,
        "duplicate_names": sorted([name for name, count in name_counts.items() if count > 1]),
        "has_sequence_flow_cycle": cyclic,
        "inclusive_gateway_count": sum(
            count for kind, count in kinds.items() if "Inclusive" in kind
        ),
        "parallel_gateway_count": sum(
            count for kind, count in kinds.items() if "Parallel" in kind
        ),
        "exclusive_gateway_count": sum(
            count for kind, count in kinds.items() if "Exclusive" in kind or kind.endswith("XORGateway")
        ),
    }


def diagnose_accepting_net(net, im, fm, name):
    report = {
        "name": name,
        "net_present": net is not None,
        "places": None if net is None else len(net.places),
        "transitions": None if net is None else len(net.transitions),
        "arcs": None if net is None else len(net.arcs),
        "initial_marking": marking_profile(im, "initial"),
        "final_marking": marking_profile(fm, "final"),
        "markings_usable": im is not None and fm is not None and len(marking_key(im) or ()) > 0 and len(marking_key(fm) or ()) > 0,
    }
    if net is None:
        report["workflow_net"] = {"ok": None, "reason": "net is None"}
        report["soundness"] = {"status": "not tested", "reason": "net is None"}
        return report
    report["alphabet"] = net_alphabet(net)
    report["ends"] = structural_ends(net)
    report["workflow_net"] = workflow_net_flag(net, im, fm)
    report["soundness"] = soundness_report(net, im, fm)
    if report["markings_usable"]:
        try:
            rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
            states = getattr(rg, "states", None)
            transitions = getattr(rg, "transitions", None)
            if states is None and hasattr(rg, "number_of_nodes"):
                report["reachability_graph"] = {
                    "status": "returned",
                    "kind": type(rg).__name__,
                    "nodes": rg.number_of_nodes(),
                    "edges": rg.number_of_edges(),
                }
            else:
                report["reachability_graph"] = {
                    "status": "returned",
                    "kind": type(rg).__name__,
                    "states": None if states is None else len(states),
                    "edges": None if transitions is None else len(transitions),
                }
        except Exception as exc:
            report["reachability_graph"] = {
                "status": "error",
                "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
                "message": str(exc),
            }
        try:
            footprints = pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm)
            report["footprints"] = normalize_footprints(footprints)
        except Exception as exc:
            report["footprints"] = {
                "status": "error",
                "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
                "message": str(exc),
            }
    else:
        report["reachability_graph"] = {"status": "not tested", "reason": "marking is null or empty"}
        report["footprints"] = {"status": "not tested", "reason": "marking is null or empty"}
    return report


def normalize_footprints(footprints):
    if footprints is None:
        return {"status": "not tested", "reason": "footprints is None"}
    if not isinstance(footprints, dict):
        return {"status": "returned", "raw_type": type(footprints).__name__, "raw": str(footprints)}
    normalized = {"status": "returned"}
    for key, value in footprints.items():
        if isinstance(value, dict):
            pairs = []
            for pair, weight in value.items():
                if weight:
                    pairs.append(pair if isinstance(pair, tuple) else (pair, weight))
            normalized[key] = sorted(str(pair) for pair in pairs)
        elif isinstance(value, (set, frozenset, list, tuple)):
            normalized[key] = sorted(str(item) for item in value if item is not None)
        else:
            normalized[key] = value if isinstance(value, (str, int, float, bool)) or value is None else str(value)
    return normalized


def interpret_tree_failure(exc):
    return {
        "status": "conversion failed",
        "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
        "message": str(exc),
        "interpretation": (
            "Process-tree conversion refused the accepting net or could not reduce it. "
            "Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, the optional-legal branch, and verification "
            "form an N-shaped partial order, which is outside the series-parallel blocks "
            "a process tree can represent. This failure is the faithful Pipeline 2 outcome. "
            "The accepting Petri net remains the execution model."
        ),
    }


def interpret_bpmn_failure(exc):
    return {
        "status": "conversion failed",
        "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
        "message": str(exc),
        "interpretation": (
            "Petri-net to BPMN conversion raised. A known implementation route rebuilds "
            "a process tree first. This partial order is not series-parallel, so that route "
            "refuses the net. The accepting Petri net is unchanged as the execution model, "
            "and there is no BPMN derivative to treat as a picture of record."
        ),
    }


def run_conversion():
    os.makedirs(OUT_DIR, exist_ok=True)
    source_powl = build_source_powl()
    artifacts = {
        "pm4py_version": getattr(pm4py, "__version__", "unknown"),
        "source_powl": source_powl,
        "visualizations": [],
        "pipelines": {},
    }
    artifacts["visualizations"].append(
        save_called(
            "source_powl",
            pm4py.save_vis_powl,
            os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "01_source_powl.png"),
            (source_powl,),
        )
    )

    try:
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)
        powl_net_status = "returned"
        powl_net_error = None
    except Exception as exc:
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = None, None, None
        powl_net_status = "conversion failed"
        powl_net_error = "%s: %s" % (type(exc).__name__, exc)

    artifacts["net_from_powl"] = net_from_powl
    artifacts["im_from_powl"] = im_from_powl
    artifacts["fm_from_powl"] = fm_from_powl
    artifacts["pipelines"]["powl_to_petri"] = {
        "status": powl_net_status,
        "error": powl_net_error,
        "diagnosis": diagnose_accepting_net(
            net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl, "net_from_powl"
        ),
    }
    if net_from_powl is not None and im_from_powl is not None and fm_from_powl is not None:
        artifacts["visualizations"].append(
            save_called(
                "net_from_powl",
                pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
                os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "02_net_from_powl.png"),
                (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl),
            )
        )

    bpmn_from_net = None
    if net_from_powl is None or im_from_powl is None or fm_from_powl is None:
        artifacts["pipelines"]["petri_to_bpmn"] = {
            "status": "not tested",
            "reason": "accepting Petri net or a marking is missing",
        }
    else:
        try:
            bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
            artifacts["pipelines"]["petri_to_bpmn"] = {
                "status": "returned",
                "bpmn_summary": summarize_bpmn(bpmn_from_net),
            }
        except Exception as exc:
            artifacts["pipelines"]["petri_to_bpmn"] = interpret_bpmn_failure(exc)
    artifacts["bpmn_from_net"] = bpmn_from_net
    if bpmn_from_net is not None:
        artifacts["visualizations"].append(
            save_called(
                "bpmn_from_net",
                pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
                os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "03_bpmn_from_net.png"),
                (bpmn_from_net,),
            )
        )
        try:
            round_net, round_im, round_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)
            round_status = "returned"
            round_error = None
        except Exception as exc:
            round_net, round_im, round_fm = None, None, None
            round_status = "conversion failed"
            round_error = "%s: %s" % (type(exc).__name__, exc)
        artifacts["net_roundtrip_bpmn"] = round_net
        artifacts["im_roundtrip_bpmn"] = round_im
        artifacts["fm_roundtrip_bpmn"] = round_fm
        artifacts["pipelines"]["bpmn_round_trip"] = {
            "status": round_status,
            "error": round_error,
            "diagnosis": diagnose_accepting_net(
                round_net, round_im, round_fm, "net_roundtrip_bpmn"
            ),
        }
        if round_net is not None and round_im is not None and round_fm is not None:
            artifacts["visualizations"].append(
                save_called(
                    "net_roundtrip_bpmn",
                    pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
                    os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "04_net_roundtrip_bpmn.png"),
                    (round_net, round_im, round_fm),
                )
            )
    else:
        artifacts["net_roundtrip_bpmn"] = None
        artifacts["im_roundtrip_bpmn"] = None
        artifacts["fm_roundtrip_bpmn"] = None

    tree = None
    tree_report = None
    if net_from_powl is None or im_from_powl is None or fm_from_powl is None:
        tree_report = {
```

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

The table is a pre-execution audit. A conversion call that returns an object establishes that the call completed. It does not move a cell to `validated preserved`. The script in sections 3 and 5 is what can later write that term into `audit_report.json` from witnesses, exact final markings, footprints, and co-enabledness. Nothing in this document was given that status, because the library was not executed here.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I1 Register first | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | POWL has one minimal node. The net encoding is expected to keep a single visible start. BPMN gateway synthesis and layout can place another task visually first |
| I2 A and P unordered, both required | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | No POWL edge between them. Trace preservation and co-enabledness are separate; BPMN may keep both orders while drawing one above the other |
| I3 silent optional Legal Review | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | XOR with `SilentTransition` is the source. A BPMN empty branch preserves the visible language and still hides the skip. A converter that names the skip, or that always draws L, changes the contract |
| I4 verification waits for A and P | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Two incoming POWL edges. An exclusive menu of sequences can drop this join or move it |
| I5 no edge between legal and verification | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | The three positive interleavings, especially L between C and I, are the language test. Block structuring typically keeps L entirely before the loop or entirely after it and loses the interior word |
| I6 binary loop, minimum one iteration | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Do-child is the chain C then I; redo-child is D. A BPMN loop marker or a free backward arc can admit zero iterations or a word that ends on D |
| I7 join before Release | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Two incoming edges. An XOR-join here admits Rel after only one branch. Visible tests catch a missing verification obligation; they are weak on the silent skip half of the join |
| I8 Archive final | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Unique maximal POWL node. Exact final-marking equality is the acceptance test for complete traces |
| I9 unique activity labels | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Each business label is one POWL transition. Tree conversion sometimes clones labels when it is forced to block-structure a net; the expected refusal avoids that clone. BPMN task names still need an alphabet check |
| I10 initial and final markings | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | `convert_to_petri_net` is expected to return both markings. Every later call has to re-check for null. Round-trip places will differ, so equality is per net against its own final marking |
| I11 true concurrency | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Expected in the POWL net as co-enabled pairs. An interleaving encoding can keep I2's words and still fail {A, P} co-enabledness. That outcome is `approximated`, and the script reports it separately from the trace fragment |

True concurrency and arbitrary interleaving are different contracts. For two atomic activities, the words `A P` and `P A` can be produced by a parallel split or by an exclusive choice of the two sequences. The same is false once a third activity can sit inside a longer block: `C L I` is a word of the source, and it disappears if Legal Review is placed wholly before verification or wholly after it. Pairwise co-enabledness is the check that distinguishes those encodings. Footprints are weaker still: mutual direct-follows can come from a loop, and optionality of L is invisible to a directly-follows pair set because the pair set does not say whether a label can be absent.

The absent edge between optional legal and verification is a positive concurrency fact. Adding either direction is a different process. The dangerous “helpful” repairs are exactly the block-structured ones: finish all of `SEQ(A, XOR(L, tau))` and `P` before the loop, or start the loop in parallel with legal only after both A and P. The first repair forbids L inside and after the loop in the way the source allows relative to P. The second forbids `R A L P C I Rel Arc`.

Silent optional behavior survives in the visible language as the pair of complete words, one with L and one without it. It does not survive as a labeled skip task. After tau is hidden, a silent join before release is easy to confuse with an ordinary optional activity. The marking-level join still matters for any encoding that can enable Rel while the legal choice is unresolved.

Binary-loop scope is local to verification. `D` is outside each trial body and inside the loop. The source law is `C I (D C I)*`, with the trial chain repeated as a pair. Words that end on D, skip the first iteration, or repeat C I without D are outside the language even when every activity name is familiar.

The release join is an AND of two predecessors. One predecessor can be silent. A sound net with the wrong join can still be sound. Soundness is a filter, and the witness `R A P L Rel Arc` is the filter for “release before verification.”

Label uniqueness is part of the contract because a cloned `Configure Trial` would make the visible language look richer while a diagram still “has the right names.” Initial and final markings have to be checked on every returned triple. A later BPMN-to-Petri call is a new net with its own marking pair; a null final marking makes soundness, footprints, and exact acceptance `not tested`.

Gateway and routing ambiguity belongs to the BPMN derivative. A parallel join and an exclusive join can be drawn at similar diamonds. The section 6 review is about that picture. A cycle in the BPMN flow graph is consistent with the verification loop and is also consistent with a wrongly reversed body, so the cycle flag is a review prompt.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

Append these functions to the section 3 module and call `main()`. The comparison oracle is the bounded explorer plus exact final-marking equality. `discover_footprints` and playout are recorded beside it. Footprint `parallel` uses a library-specific abstraction, so a parallel-relation mismatch is reported as `ambiguous` and does not by itself overwrite a trace result. Playout is a sample.

The fragment is every complete visible word with at most three verification iterations (at most three `Inspect Trial` labels, hence at most two `Correct Defect` labels) and at most 16 visible labels. Inside that fragment the enumeration is exhaustive when it finishes under the state cap and the silent-step cap. The silent cap is applied to the shortest firing sequence that reaches a `(marking, visible word)` state; repeating a marking on the same visible word is treated as a silent cycle and is not expanded. The full language is infinite because the loop can grow, so a passing fragment is not a proof for four or more iterations. Co-enabledness is different: it walks markings once, and on a finite net that walk is exhaustive when the state cap is not hit.

```python
def enabled_set(net, marking):

def accepts_visible_trace(net, im, fm, visible_trace, max_silent_steps=FRAGMENT_MAX_SILENT, max_states=FRAGMENT_MAX_STATES):
    """Return ACCEPT, REJECT, or UNCERTAIN_BOUND. Silent labels are skipped."""
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        return {"verdict": "not tested", "reason": "net or marking is None"}
    target = tuple(visible_trace)
    queue = deque([(im, 0, 0)])
    seen = {(marking_key(im), 0): 0}
    expansions = 0
    while queue:
        marking, index, silent_steps = queue.popleft()
        if index == len(target) and markings_equal(marking, fm):
            return {"verdict": "ACCEPT", "expansions": expansions}
        if expansions >= max_states:
            return {"verdict": "UNCERTAIN_BOUND", "reason": "state cap", "expansions": expansions}
        expansions += 1
        for transition in enabled_set(net, marking):
            label = visible_label(transition)
            new_marking = fire_transition(transition, net, marking)
            if label is None:
                nxt_silent = silent_steps + 1
                if nxt_silent > max_silent_steps:
                    return {
                        "verdict": "UNCERTAIN_BOUND",
                        "reason": "silent cap on shortest explored path",
                        "expansions": expansions,
                    }
                state = (marking_key(new_marking), index)
                best = seen.get(state)
                if best is not None and best <= nxt_silent:
                    continue
                seen[state] = nxt_silent
                queue.append((new_marking, index, nxt_silent))
            elif index < len(target) and label == target[index]:
                state = (marking_key(new_marking), index + 1)
                if state in seen and seen[state] == 0:
                    continue
                seen[state] = 0
                queue.append((new_marking, index + 1, 0))
    return {"verdict": "REJECT", "expansions": expansions}


def enumerate_visible_fragment(net, im, fm):
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        return {
            "status": "not tested",
            "reason": "net or marking is None",
            "traces": [],
            "exhaustive_under_bound": False,
        }
    queue = deque([(im, tuple(), 0, 0)])
    seen = {(marking_key(im), tuple()): 0}
    completed = set()
    hit_state_cap = False
    hit_silent_cap = False
    expansions = 0
    while queue:
        if expansions >= FRAGMENT_MAX_STATES:
            hit_state_cap = True
            break
        marking, trace, inspects, silent_steps = queue.popleft()
        expansions += 1
        if markings_equal(marking, fm):
            completed.add(trace)
            continue
        for transition in enabled_set(net, marking):
            label = visible_label(transition)
            if label is not None:
                if len(trace) + 1 > FRAGMENT_MAX_VISIBLE:
                    continue
                nxt_inspects = inspects + (1 if label == "Inspect Trial" else 0)
                if nxt_inspects > FRAGMENT_MAX_ITERATIONS:
                    continue
                new_trace = trace + (label,)
                new_marking = fire_transition(transition, net, marking)
                state = (marking_key(new_marking), new_trace)
                if state in seen:
                    continue
                seen[state] = 0
                queue.append((new_marking, new_trace, nxt_inspects, 0))
            else:
                nxt_silent = silent_steps + 1
                if nxt_silent > FRAGMENT_MAX_SILENT:
                    hit_silent_cap = True
                    continue
                new_marking = fire_transition(transition, net, marking)
                state = (marking_key(new_marking), trace)
                best = seen.get(state)
                if best is not None and best <= nxt_silent:
                    continue
                seen[state] = nxt_silent
                queue.append((new_marking, trace, inspects, nxt_silent))
    return {
        "status": "returned",
        "traces": completed,
        "n_traces": len(completed),
        "expansions": expansions,
        "hit_state_cap": hit_state_cap,
        "hit_silent_cap": hit_silent_cap,
        "exhaustive_under_bound": not hit_state_cap and not hit_silent_cap,
        "bound": {
            "max_iterations": FRAGMENT_MAX_ITERATIONS,
            "max_visible_len": FRAGMENT_MAX_VISIBLE,
            "max_silent_steps": FRAGMENT_MAX_SILENT,
            "max_states": FRAGMENT_MAX_STATES,
        },
    }


def dfg_from_traces(traces):
    pairs, starts, ends = set(), set(), set()
    for trace in traces:
        if not trace:
            starts.add("<empty>")
            ends.add("<empty>")
            continue
        starts.add(trace[0])
        ends.add(trace[-1])
        pairs.update(zip(trace, trace[1:]))
    return pairs, starts, ends


def first_visible_labels(net, im, max_states=10000):
    found = set()
    seen = {marking_key(im)}
    stack = [im]
    capped = False
    while stack:
        marking = stack.pop()
        for transition in enabled_set(net, marking):
            label = visible_label(transition)
            new_marking = fire_transition(transition, net, marking)
            if label is None:
                key = marking_key(new_marking)
                if key in seen:
                    continue
                if len(seen) >= max_states:
                    capped = True
                    continue
                seen.add(key)
                stack.append(new_marking)
            else:
                found.add(label)
    return {"labels": found, "exhaustive": not capped}


def concurrency_profile(net, im, max_states=FRAGMENT_MAX_STATES):
    if net is None or im is None:
        return {"status": "not tested", "reason": "net or initial marking is None"}
    seen = {marking_key(im)}
    queue = deque([im])
    pairs = set()
    ever_enabled = set()
    capped = False
    while queue:
        if len(seen) >= max_states:
            capped = True
            break
        marking = queue.popleft()
        enabled = enabled_set(net, marking)
        visible_now = []
        for transition in enabled:
            label = visible_label(transition)
            if label is not None:
                visible_now.append(label)
                ever_enabled.add(label)
            new_marking = fire_transition(transition, net, marking)
            key = marking_key(new_marking)
            if key not in seen:
                seen.add(key)
                queue.append(new_marking)
        for i, left in enumerate(visible_now):
            for right in visible_now[i + 1:]:
                pairs.add(frozenset((left, right)))
    all_visible = {
        visible_label(transition)
        for transition in net.transitions
        if visible_label(transition) is not None
    }
    return {
        "status": "returned",
        "pairs": pairs,
        "never_enabled_visible": all_visible - ever_enabled,
        "markings_visited": len(seen),
        "exhaustive": not capped,
        "missing_required_pairs": sorted(
            sorted(pair) for pair in REQUIRED_COENABLED - pairs
        ),
        "present_forbidden_pairs": sorted(
            sorted(pair) for pair in FORBIDDEN_COENABLED & pairs
        ),
    }


def try_playout(net, im, fm, no_traces=20, max_len=16):
    """Sample the net. Absence of a word in the sample proves nothing."""
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        return {"status": "not tested", "reason": "net or marking is None", "traces": []}
    try:
        from pm4py.algo.simulation.playout.petri_net import algorithm as playout_algo
        params = {}
        basic = playout_algo.Variants.BASIC_PLAYOUT.value
        params[basic.Parameters.NO_TRACES] = no_traces
        params[basic.Parameters.MAX_TRACE_LENGTH] = max_len
        log = playout_algo.apply(net, im, fm, parameters=params)
    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "status": "not tested",
            "reason": "%s: %s" % (type(exc).__name__, exc),
            "traces": [],
        }
    traces = []
    try:
        for trace in log:
            labels = []
            for event in trace:
                if isinstance(event, dict):
                    label = event.get("concept:name")
                else:
                    label = event.get("concept:name") if hasattr(event, "get") else None
                if label is not None:
                    labels.append(label)
            traces.append(labels)
    except Exception as exc:
        return {
            "status": "not tested",
            "reason": "playout returned an unreadable log: %s" % exc,
            "traces": [],
        }
    return {"status": "returned", "traces": traces, "n_traces": len(traces)}


def classify_witnesses(net, im, fm):
    rows = []
    uncertain = False
    for polarity, suite in (("positive", WITNESS_POSITIVE), ("negative", WITNESS_NEGATIVE)):
        for trace in suite:
            result = accepts_visible_trace(net, im, fm, trace)
            verdict = result["verdict"]
            if verdict == "UNCERTAIN_BOUND":
                uncertain = True
                outcome = "not tested"
            elif polarity == "positive" and verdict == "ACCEPT":
                outcome = "validated preserved"
            elif polarity == "negative" and verdict == "REJECT":
                outcome = "validated preserved"
            elif verdict in ("ACCEPT", "REJECT"):
                outcome = "lost"
            else:
                outcome = "not tested"
                uncertain = True
            rows.append({
                "polarity": polarity,
                "trace": trace,
                "explorer": result,
                "assessment": outcome,
            })
    if uncertain:
        suite_status = "not tested"
    elif any(row["assessment"] == "lost" for row in rows):
        suite_status = "lost"
    else:
        suite_status = "validated preserved"
    return {"suite_status": suite_status, "rows": rows}


def compare_fragments(source_fragment, other_fragment):
    if not source_fragment.get("exhaustive_under_bound") or not other_fragment.get("exhaustive_under_bound"):
        return {
            "assessment": "not tested",
            "reason": "fragment enumeration hit a cap or a marking was missing",
            "exhaustive_under_bound": False,
        }
    source_traces = set(map(tuple, source_fragment["traces"]))
    other_traces = set(map(tuple, other_fragment["traces"]))
    missing = sorted(source_traces - other_traces)
    extra = sorted(other_traces - source_traces)
    src_df, src_start, src_end = dfg_from_traces(source_traces)
    dst_df, dst_start, dst_end = dfg_from_traces(other_traces)
    same = not missing and not extra
    return {
        "assessment": "validated preserved" if same else "lost",
        "exhaustive_under_bound": True,
        "n_source": len(source_traces),
        "n_other": len(other_traces),
        "missing_from_other": missing,
        "extra_in_other": extra,
        "start_activities_source": sorted(src_start),
        "start_activities_other": sorted(dst_start),
        "end_activities_source": sorted(src_end),
        "end_activities_other": sorted(dst_end),
        "df_only_source": sorted(src_df - dst_df),
        "df_only_other": sorted(dst_df - src_df),
    }


def compare_concurrency(source_profile, other_profile):
    if source_profile.get("status") != "returned" or other_profile.get("status") != "returned":
        return {"assessment": "not tested"}
    if not source_profile.get("exhaustive") or not other_profile.get("exhaustive"):
        return {"assessment": "not tested", "reason": "co-enabledness search hit the state cap"}
    source_pairs = set(map(frozenset, source_profile["pairs"]))
    other_pairs = set(map(frozenset, other_profile["pairs"]))
    required_lost = [sorted(pair) for pair in REQUIRED_COENABLED if pair in source_pairs and pair not in other_pairs]
    forbidden_gained = [sorted(pair) for pair in FORBIDDEN_COENABLED if pair in other_pairs]
    if forbidden_gained or source_profile["present_forbidden_pairs"] or other_profile["missing_required_pairs"]:
        assessment = "lost"
    elif required_lost:
        assessment = "approximated"
    elif source_pairs != other_pairs:
        assessment = "ambiguous"
    else:
        assessment = "validated preserved"
    return {
        "assessment": assessment,
        "required_pairs_lost_by_other": required_lost,
        "forbidden_pairs_present_in_other": forbidden_gained,
        "pairs_only_source": sorted(sorted(pair) for pair in source_pairs - other_pairs),
        "pairs_only_other": sorted(sorted(pair) for pair in other_pairs - source_pairs),
    }


def footprint_activities(diagnosis):
    footprints = (diagnosis or {}).get("footprints") or {}
    if footprints.get("status") != "returned":
        return None
    return footprints


def check_playout_against(source_net, source_im, source_fm, sample):
    if sample.get("status") != "returned":
        return {"assessment": "not tested", "reason": sample.get("reason")}
    surprises = []
    for trace in sample["traces"]:
        result = accepts_visible_trace(source_net, source_im, source_fm, trace)
        if result["verdict"] == "REJECT":
            surprises.append(trace)
        elif result["verdict"] != "ACCEPT":
            return {"assessment": "not tested", "reason": "membership hit a bound", "trace": trace}
    if surprises:
        return {"assessment": "lost", "rejected_sample_traces": surprises}
    return {"assessment": "validated preserved", "n_checked": len(sample["traces"]), "scope": "sample only"}

def run_round_trip_validation(artifacts):
    source_net = artifacts.get("net_from_powl")
    source_im = artifacts.get("im_from_powl")
    source_fm = artifacts.get("fm_from_powl")
    report = {
        "exhaustive_language": False,
        "fragment_definition": (
            "Complete visible traces with at most 3 Inspect Trial events and "
            "at most 16 visible events. Exhaustive only when both enumerations "
            "report exhaustive_under_bound. The loop language beyond that bound is unchecked."
        ),
        "source": {},
        "derivatives": {},
    }
    if source_net is None or source_im is None or source_fm is None:
        report["source"] = {"status": "conversion failed", "reason": "source accepting net is missing"}
        return report

    source_fragment = enumerate_visible_fragment(source_net, source_im, source_fm)
    source_concurrency = concurrency_profile(source_net, source_im)
    source_witnesses = classify_witnesses(source_net, source_im, source_fm)
    source_first = first_visible_labels(source_net, source_im)
    source_playout = try_playout(source_net, source_im, source_fm)
    report["source"] = {
        "fragment": {
            "n_traces": source_fragment.get("n_traces"),
            "exhaustive_under_bound": source_fragment.get("exhaustive_under_bound"),
            "hit_state_cap": source_fragment.get("hit_state_cap"),
            "hit_silent_cap": source_fragment.get("hit_silent_cap"),
            "bound": source_fragment.get("bound"),
        },
        "first_visible": {
            "labels": sorted(source_first["labels"]),
            "exhaustive": source_first["exhaustive"],
            "assessment": (
                "validated preserved"
                if source_first["exhaustive"] and source_first["labels"] == {"Register Change"}
                else "lost" if source_first["exhaustive"] else "not tested"
            ),
        },
        "witnesses": source_witnesses,
        "concurrency": {
            "exhaustive": source_concurrency.get("exhaustive"),
            "missing_required_pairs": source_concurrency.get("missing_required_pairs"),
            "present_forbidden_pairs": source_concurrency.get("present_forbidden_pairs"),
            "never_enabled_visible": sorted(source_concurrency.get("never_enabled_visible") or []),
            "assessment": (
                "lost"
                if source_concurrency.get("exhaustive") and (
                    source_concurrency.get("missing_required_pairs")
                    or source_concurrency.get("present_forbidden_pairs")
                    or source_concurrency.get("never_enabled_visible")
                )
                else "validated preserved"
                if source_concurrency.get("exhaustive")
                else "not tested"
            ),
        },
        "playout": {
            "status": source_playout.get("status"),
            "n_traces": source_playout.get("n_traces"),
            "self_check": check_playout_against(source_net, source_im, source_fm, source_playout),
        },
        "stored_footprints": (artifacts["pipelines"].get("powl_to_petri") or {}).get("diagnosis", {}).get("footprints"),
        "stored_soundness": (artifacts["pipelines"].get("powl_to_petri") or {}).get("diagnosis", {}).get("soundness"),
    }

    derivative_specs = [
        ("bpmn_round_trip", "net_roundtrip_bpmn", "im_roundtrip_bpmn", "fm_roundtrip_bpmn"),
        ("tree_bpmn_round_trip", "net_roundtrip_tree", "im_roundtrip_tree", "fm_roundtrip_tree"),
    ]
    for label, net_key, im_key, fm_key in derivative_specs:
        net = artifacts.get(net_key)
        im = artifacts.get(im_key)
        fm = artifacts.get(fm_key)
        pipeline_status = (artifacts["pipelines"].get(label) or {}).get("status")
        if label == "tree_bpmn_round_trip" and artifacts.get("process_tree") is None:
            report["derivatives"][label] = {
                "assessment": "conversion failed",
                "reason": "process-tree pipeline produced no BPMN to round-trip",
            }
            continue
        if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
            report["derivatives"][label] = {
                "assessment": "conversion failed" if pipeline_status == "conversion failed" else "not tested",
                "reason": "round-trip accepting net or marking is missing",
            }
            continue
        fragment = enumerate_visible_fragment(net, im, fm)
        concurrency = concurrency_profile(net, im)
        witnesses = classify_witnesses(net, im, fm)
        playout = try_playout(net, im, fm)
        diagnosis = (artifacts["pipelines"].get(label) or {}).get("diagnosis") or {}
        report["derivatives"][label] = {
            "alphabet": (diagnosis.get("alphabet") if diagnosis else net_alphabet(net)),
            "soundness": diagnosis.get("soundness") if diagnosis else soundness_report(net, im, fm),
            "workflow_net": diagnosis.get("workflow_net") if diagnosis else workflow_net_flag(net, im, fm),
            "marking_profiles": {
                "initial": marking_profile(im, "initial"),
                "final": marking_profile(fm, "final"),
            },
            "fragment_comparison": compare_fragments(source_fragment, fragment),
            "concurrency_comparison": compare_concurrency(source_concurrency, concurrency),
            "witnesses": witnesses,
            "playout_against_source": check_playout_against(source_net, source_im, source_fm, playout),
            "library_footprints": diagnosis.get("footprints"),
            "first_visible": first_visible_labels(net, im),
        }
    return report
```

Read a run in this order. If the source net's own witness suite or required co-enabled pairs fail, the execution encoding itself is quarantined and derivative comparisons stay informative only. If a derivative cell says `conversion failed`, that pipeline produced no behavioral claim. If `exhaustive_under_bound` is false, the fragment comparison stays `not tested` and is not a pass. Playout agreement covers only the sampled words. Library footprints are kept so a reviewer can see tau-sensitive differences; the pass/fail language comparison is the explorer's own direct-follows set on complete fragment traces.

The comparison is exhaustive for the stated finite fragment when both sides report `exhaustive_under_bound`. It is not an exhaustive comparison of the infinite languages.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN drawing can be misread while the round-tripped net still accepts every fragment witness. Coordinates, lane order, and edge curvature are layout. Precedence lives in sequence-flow connectivity and gateway type.

For this process the likely misreadings are concrete. After `Register Change`, a parallel split toward `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` is often drawn with Assess Risk on the upper lane. Readers then treat the upper lane as the earlier task. Both orders are required behavior. The N-shaped synchronization needs a second split after `Assess Risk`: one branch toward the legal XOR, the other toward an AND-join with `Prepare Evidence` that opens verification. Crossing flows in that picture look like a corrupted sequence even when the tokens implement the partial order.

The legal XOR has one labeled task and one empty branch. An empty branch is easy to miss, and then `Legal Review` looks mandatory. A label such as `tau` or `skip` on that branch creates a tenth activity the source does not have. The exclusive diamond and the parallel diamond are visually close; reading the release join as exclusive admits release after legal alone or after verification alone.

The verification loop is usually a backward sequence flow, sometimes a subprocess loop marker. A backward arrow from the end of `Inspect Trial` toward `Configure Trial` is readable as “Inspect, then Configure.” `Correct Defect` sitting on the return path is readable as an optional note, as a task that may follow the last inspection straight into `Release Change`, or as a task that may start the loop. The source allows none of those readings. A loop marker with no cardinality is readable as zero iterations. A cycle flag from the script only says a cycle exists; it does not say the cycle body is `C I` with redo `D`.

Silent routing nodes between these regions have no business name. If they are dropped from the reading, the remaining straightest path often looks like `Prepare Evidence` leads to `Legal Review` and then to `Release Change`, which invents an order the POWL does not have and hides the verification obligation. Graphviz rank order can also place `Legal Review` to the left of `Configure Trial` whenever one of the legal-before-verification traces is a convenient layering, which suggests that order is required.

Annotations that should travel with the picture, in the diagram and in a sidecar note:

- On the Assess/Prepare split: “Concurrent after Register Change. Both orders are required. Vertical position is layout.”
- On the legal XOR: “At most one Legal Review, and only after Assess Risk. The empty branch is a silent skip, not a task.”
- Between the legal branch and the verification region: “No precedence. Legal Review may occur before Prepare Evidence completes, between Configure Trial and Inspect Trial, or after the loop. It finishes before Release Change when it occurs.”
- On the verification region: “At least one iteration. Each iteration is Configure Trial then Inspect Trial. Another iteration occurs only after Correct Defect, and Correct Defect is followed by a full iteration.”
- On the release join: “Parallel join. Release Change waits for the legal branch and for the verification loop.”
- Footer: “This figure is derived from the POWL source through an accepting Petri net. Geometry is not precedence. Edit the POWL, then regenerate the figure.”

An inclusive gateway, a duplicated task name, or a task named `tau`/`skip` is enough to quarantine the picture even before trace replay. Those checks are in `summarize_bpmn`.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a derivative when any of the following holds. An inconclusive bound is a quarantine, not a pass. A saved PNG is not a pass.

1. `convert_to_petri_net` or `convert_to_bpmn` raises, or either marking is null or empty. Soundness, footprints, and exact acceptance are then unusable.
2. The net fails `check_is_workflow_net`, fails `check_soundness`, has a final marking that is unreachable, or leaves a source business label never enabled.
3. The visible alphabet is not exactly the nine source labels. That includes a duplicated business label, a renamed task, an empty-string label, or a silent skip promoted to `tau`, `skip`, or `"None"`.
4. Any positive witness returns REJECT, or returns ACCEPT on a marking that fails exact equality with that net's own final marking. The proper prefix that stops at `Release Change` must fail this test.
5. Any negative witness reaches the final marking. The high-priority mismatches are release before verification (`R A P L Rel Arc`), zero iterations, `Legal Review` before `Assess Risk`, `Inspect Trial` before `Configure Trial`, a loop that ends on `Correct Defect`, two iterations with no `Correct Defect`, and anything after `Archive Change`.
6. A required interleaving is missing from an exhaustive fragment: `Prepare Evidence` before `Assess Risk`, `Legal Review` before `Prepare Evidence`, `Legal Review` between `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial`, or `Legal Review` after a finished iteration and before `Release Change`.
7. Required co-enabledness fails. Loss of `{Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence}` or `{Legal Review, Configure Trial}` means the encoding collapsed concurrency. If the fragment language still matches, classify concurrency as `approximated` and keep the derivative out of the authoritative store.
8. A forbidden co-enabled pair appears, in particular `{Configure Trial, Inspect Trial}`, `{Legal Review, Release Change}`, or `Register Change` together with any other visible label.
9. Pipeline 2's exception is swallowed, or a returned process tree is published without the witness and co-enabledness results. A tree failure quarantines only the tree derivative. A tree success that loses I5 or I11 quarantines the tree and its BPMN.
10. The BPMN object contains an inclusive gateway, a duplicated task name, or a sequence-flow story that the sidecar annotations do not correct. Behavioral tests on the round trip do not repair a misleading picture.
11. Fragment enumeration hits the state cap or the silent cap. The result stays `not tested`. A silent cycle that blocks the final marking, reported by soundness or by failure of every positive witness, quarantines the net.
12. Library footprints, when they expose start activities, end activities, or visible direct-follows, move the start off `Register Change`, move the end off `Archive Change`, or add a direct pair such as `(Prepare Evidence, Release Change)`, `(Correct Defect, Release Change)`, or `(Inspect Trial, Configure Trial)`. Investigate before trusting the picture; the explorer's own fragment DF is the language comparison.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

Store the POWL source as the authoritative specification, together with the invariant table and the witness lists. Store `net_from_powl` with `im_from_powl` and `fm_from_powl` as the execution encoding, and treat that triple as authoritative only after a run reports workflow-net structure, soundness, the source alphabet, the positive and negative witnesses, exhaustive fragment status, and the required and forbidden co-enabled pairs. Regenerate that triple from the POWL. A hand-edited net, and every round-tripped net, stay derivative.

Stakeholder visualization uses `bpmn_from_net` and `03_bpmn_from_net.png`, with the section 6 annotations in the diagram and in a sidecar. The PNG and the BPMN XML are derivative views. Pipeline 2 does not get a stakeholder artifact for this process: a process-tree intermediate is disallowed because the partial order is not series-parallel, and a converter refusal is the result the audit expects. If a future library returns a tree, that tree remains quarantined unless the full section 5 report preserves both the fragment language and the co-enabled pairs, and even then it is a derived view.

Each derivative should carry the PM4Py version, the conversion exception text if any, the soundness and workflow-net results, the alphabet comparison, the witness verdicts, the fragment diff with `exhaustive_under_bound`, the co-enabledness diff, and the BPMN gateway summary. A picture without that report is not evidence.

Future edits belong in the POWL. After each edit, regenerate the Petri net and the BPMN and rerun the audit. Do not import a rearranged BPMN, a process tree, or a round-tripped net back into the store as the new source. Stakeholder redlines on the drawing are change requests against specific invariant IDs. Pin the PM4Py version in the report so a later converter change is visible. Round-trip drift is avoided by keeping one writer, the POWL, and by refusing to promote a derivative whose section 5 assessment is anything other than a passing run.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

These limits remain even when soundness, footprints, the reachability-graph summary, every listed witness, playout samples, and an exhaustive fragment comparison all succeed.

1. The fragment stops at three iterations. Words with four or more iterations are unchecked. The script does not build the epsilon-closed visible automaton and does not decide language equivalence on that automaton, which is the check that would cover the infinite loop language on a finite net.
2. Pairwise visible co-enabledness plus the fragment language still leaves the silent skeleton free. Extra tau transitions, and the precise marking at which the silent legal skip fires, can change while every visible word and every visible co-enabled pair stays the same. Net isomorphism, equal place counts, and equal silent-transition counts are not established.
3. A passing round trip says the BPMN semantics accepted the fragment. It says nothing about how a person will order the lanes, read an empty XOR branch, or interpret the backward verification arc. Layout fidelity is outside the behavioral tests.
4. No event log is part of the source. Model-to-model agreement leaves fitness to historical change records, and any data, time, cost, or organizational rule, unexamined. The labels are control-flow names.
5. `check_soundness` has varied between a boolean easy-soundness result and a fuller diagnostic across PM4Py versions. A true result from that function, and a stored PNG from the same version, can age into a different converter's behavior. The report pins one version; it does not bind the next one.
