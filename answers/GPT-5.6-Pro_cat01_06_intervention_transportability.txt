The shared mechanism is a fork–join critical-path effect: where Identity Check and Document Check use independent information, both are ready after Register, and Assessment waits for both, concurrent execution can replace roughly the sum of branch times with their maximum and reduce pre-Assessment waiting. That mechanism exists at both sites for Site B’s no-original, document-complete cases. For Site B’s originals-required cases, the transferable mechanism is narrower: Identity Check can overlap with Originals Receipt, while Document Check remains legally gated.

Site-specific mechanisms are substantial. Site A combined parallelization with mostly digital intake, cross-trained reviewers, simultaneous ownership, a six-task WIP rule, added document capacity, an API upgrade, queue huddles, a simpler case mix, and possibly better timestamp capture. Site B adds specialized pools, a single-owner lock, variable originals delay, mandatory legal review, supplement loops, and thinner assessment/legal capacity.

Document channel, complexity, role specialization, IT locking, each pool’s capacity, legal-review status, queue discipline, and evidence dependence are effect modifiers because they determine whether treatment produces true overlap and whether saved verification time remains on the end-to-end critical path. A similar control-flow picture therefore does not establish transportability: legal guards, data readiness, resource contention, feedback loops, and logging semantics can generate different causal and observed processes even when activity names and joins look alike.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

For calibration, Site A’s direct service-path gain from perfect overlap is only about 3.1–3.5 hours: sequential verification would take `3.1 + 6.5 = 9.6` hours after the change versus `max(3.1, 6.5) = 6.5`, or `3.5 + 7.2 − 7.2 = 3.5` hours using pre-period service times. At Site B, the corresponding no-original ceiling is `3.4 + 8.1 − 8.1 = 3.4` hours. Applying that mechanically to the 38% no-original share gives roughly 1.3 hours of average-like direct service-path gain, although medians are not linearly weighted. Larger gains require queue-delay reductions, which were confounded at Site A.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Eligibility rules.** At Register, set immutable routing attributes for `originals_required`, `documents_complete`, `legal_review_required`, `complexity_band`, and `known_check_dependency`.

* **Digital route:** no originals required, all mandatory digital documents available, and no known dependence of Document Check on the identity result. Initially prioritize cases without mandatory Legal Review while retaining complexity strata for learning.
* **Originals route:** originals legally required. These cases are eligible for Identity Check–Originals Receipt concurrency, but not immediate Document Check.
* **Excluded or deferred route:** unresolved legal classification, incomplete registration data, known identity-to-document dependency, or categories where concurrent work has previously caused cancellation or rework.

**Concurrent execution.**

* Digital route: release `Identity Check` and `Document Check` immediately after Register.
* Originals route: release `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt` immediately after Register. Release `Document Check` only after the receipt event.
* `Assessment` is enabled only after both the relevant identity and document branches complete.

**Mandatory dependencies.** Originals Receipt remains a prerequisite where legally required. Mandatory Legal Review remains after Assessment. Approve or Reject remains after either Assessment for non-legal cases or completed Legal Review for legal cases. Notify remains last.

**Queue and WIP controls.** Treat release and claimed work as different states. Start with claimed-WIP caps of four tasks per identity/document reviewer and two per assessor/legal reviewer; waiting tasks remain unclaimed. Pause incremental treated-case release when downstream queue age exceeds a pre-specified baseline threshold. Use the same queue-huddle policy across pilot arms so it does not silently become a treatment co-intervention.

**Responsibilities.** Specialized roles remain unchanged. A queue coordinator manages routing, stale cases, and lock exceptions but does not perform substantive checks. Reviewers record suspension rather than holding an inactive task as active work.

**Legal and supplement handling.** A legal Request Supplement creates a new loop episode: `Request Supplement → Document Check (rework) → Assessment (rework) → Legal Review`. Preserve the original treatment assignment. Record the supplement reason and escalate after two repeated episodes rather than silently looping indefinitely.

**Logging.** Record task creation, eligibility, queue entry, actual start, suspension, resumption, completion, resource, ownership changes, lock conflicts, original-receipt time, and Assessment-ready time. Store event-entry time separately from execution time. Back-entered or corrected timestamps must be explicitly flagged.

```text
DocPath =
    X(
        DocumentCheck_Digital,
        PO(
            nodes={OriginalsReceipt, DocumentCheck_Originals},
            order={OriginalsReceipt ≺ DocumentCheck_Originals}
        )
    )

SupplementCycle =
    PO(
        nodes={RequestSupplement, DocumentRecheck, Reassessment},
        order={
            RequestSupplement ≺ DocumentRecheck,
            DocumentRecheck ≺ Reassessment,
            RequestSupplement ≺ Reassessment
        }
    )

LegalPath = *(LegalReview, SupplementCycle)
OptionalLegal = X(tau, LegalPath)
Decision = X(Approve, Reject)

SiteB =
    PO(
        nodes={
            Register,
            IdentityCheck,
            DocPath,
            Assessment,
            OptionalLegal,
            Decision,
            Notify
        },
        order={
            Register ≺ IdentityCheck,
            Register ≺ DocPath,
            IdentityCheck ≺ Assessment,
            DocPath ≺ Assessment,
            Assessment ≺ OptionalLegal,
            OptionalLegal ≺ Decision,
            Decision ≺ Notify,
            plus the transitive closure
        }
    )
```

The `X(tau, LegalPath)` is a control-flow abstraction. Operational routing must select the branch from the statutory legal-review flag rather than by unconstrained choice.

| Stop rules                         | Stop or pause for any statutory-order breach; duplicate/lost task; rework increase above 3 points; p90 more than 15% worse for two reporting periods; severe lock failure; or downstream queue-age breach                           | Protects legality, quality, and tail performance                                        | Independent review of stop-rule triggers                                                  | Automated exception dashboard with auditable numerator and denominator                      |
| Transportability analysis          | Estimate intention-to-treat effects and heterogeneity by complexity, document channel, legal status, load, and implementation version; standardize supported-arm effects to B’s target mix only where positivity exists             | Learns which A mechanism transports and to which B population                           | Do not extrapolate into unobserved combinations of originals, legal status, or complexity | Selection-variable balance, stratum-specific effects, mediation funnel, and overlap support |

The three-arm structure distinguishes policy from implementation. If the policy-only arm is assigned to parallelize but the lock prevents genuine temporal overlap, its intention-to-treat effect can be null while the supported arm shows increased true overlap and shorter time to Assessment-ready; that pattern indicates implementation failure. If both treatment arms achieve comparable verified overlap yet neither reduces Assessment-ready time, the core critical-path mechanism has failed or the checks are not actually independent.

Process-mining fidelity measures—server-side starts/completions, overlap minutes, lock conflicts, dormant tasks, queue entry/exit, and WIP—locate the break between assignment, task release, execution, join completion, and final outcome. Dedicated queue lanes and capacity quotas reduce contamination; queue-age and utilization measurements reveal whether a benefit is displaced into assessment or legal waiting. Stratified estimates show whether failure is confined to complexity or legal-status groups. An assignment-as-instrument analysis for true overlap can supplement, but not replace, intention-to-treat results. Quality stop rules prevent an apparent speed gain from being accepted when it is purchased through rework, supplements, or appeals.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

The code follows the current PM4Py object relationships: `StrictPartialOrder(nodes=...)` with order edges, `OperatorPOWL` for XOR and LOOP, conversion through `convert_to_petri_net`, top-level workflow-net and soundness checks, and Petri-net playout. ([GitHub][1])

```python
from __future__ import annotations

import random
from typing import Iterable, Sequence, Tuple

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    OperatorPOWL,
    POWL,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    Transition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def make_strict_partial_order(
    nodes: Sequence[POWL],
    immediate_edges: Iterable[Tuple[POWL, POWL]],
) -> StrictPartialOrder:
    """
    Build a valid StrictPartialOrder.

    PM4Py stores the strict partial-order relation, not merely an arbitrary
    directed graph. After adding the intended immediate dependencies, add the
    transitive closure and validate irreflexivity/transitivity.
    """
    model = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=list(nodes))

    for source, target in immediate_edges:
        model.order.add_edge(source, target)

    model.order.add_transitive_edges()
    model.validate_partial_orders()
    return model


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Site A process trees
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

site_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    """
    -> (
        'Register',
        'Identity Check',
        'Document Check',
        'Assessment',
        X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ),
        'Notify'
    )
    """
)

site_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    """
    -> (
        'Register',
        + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ),
        'Assessment',
        X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ),
        'Notify'
    )
    """
)

a_before_net, a_before_im, a_before_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
    site_a_before
)
a_after_net, a_after_im, a_after_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
    site_a_after
)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Site B POWL candidate
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

register = Transition(label="Register")
identity_check = Transition(label="Identity Check")

# Digital/no-original route: Document Check can start after Register.
document_check_digital = Transition(
    label="Document Check [digital/no originals]"
)

# Originals route: receipt must strictly precede Document Check.
originals_receipt = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
document_check_originals = Transition(
    label="Document Check [after originals]"
)

originals_document_path = make_strict_partial_order(
    nodes=[originals_receipt, document_check_originals],
    immediate_edges=[
        (originals_receipt, document_check_originals),
    ],
)

# The XOR abstracts a data-dependent route:
# in execution, originals_required must determine which child is selected.
document_path = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[
        document_check_digital,
        originals_document_path,
    ],
)

assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")

# Supplement/rework body:
# Request Supplement -> repeated Document Check -> repeated Assessment.
request_supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
document_recheck = Transition(label="Document Check [supplement]")
reassessment = Transition(label="Assessment [supplement]")

supplement_cycle = make_strict_partial_order(
    nodes=[request_supplement, document_recheck, reassessment],
    immediate_edges=[
        (request_supplement, document_recheck),
        (document_recheck, reassessment),
    ],
)

# LOOP semantics:
# execute Legal Review once; optionally execute the supplement body and
# then return to Legal Review, repeating as necessary.
legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
legal_review_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP,
    children=[legal_review, supplement_cycle],
)

# Non-legal cases take tau; legally required cases take the legal loop.
# POWL itself does not encode the data guard deciding between them.
optional_legal_review = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[
        SilentTransition(),
        legal_review_loop,
    ],
)

decision = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[
        Transition(label="Approve"),
        Transition(label="Reject"),
    ],
)

notify = Transition(label="Notify")

# Root partial order:
# - Register precedes both verification routes.
# - Identity and document verification remain unordered relative to each other,
#   allowing true concurrency.
# - Assessment explicitly waits for BOTH identity_check and document_path.
# - Legal review is optional but, when selected, precedes the final decision.
site_b_candidate = make_strict_partial_order(
    nodes=[
        register,
        identity_check,
        document_path,
        assessment,
        optional_legal_review,
        decision,
        notify,
    ],
    immediate_edges=[
        (register, identity_check),
        (register, document_path),

        # Explicit fork-join dependencies:
        (identity_check, assessment),
        (document_path, assessment),

        (assessment, optional_legal_review),
        (optional_legal_review, decision),
        (decision, notify),
    ],
)

site_b_candidate.validate_partial_orders()

b_net, b_im, b_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_b_candidate)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Workflow-net and soundness checks
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def report_model_properties(name, net, initial_marking, final_marking) -> None:
    is_workflow_net = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)

    soundness_result = pm4py.check_soundness(
        net,
        initial_marking,
        final_marking,
    )

    # Current PM4Py returns (is_sound, diagnostics). This fallback makes the
    # example tolerate versions that return only a Boolean.
    if isinstance(soundness_result, tuple):
        is_sound, diagnostics = soundness_result
    else:
        is_sound, diagnostics = bool(soundness_result), {}

    print(
        f"{name}: workflow_net={is_workflow_net}, "
        f"sound={is_sound}, "
        f"diagnostic_keys={list(diagnostics)[:5]}"
    )


report_model_properties(
    "Site A before",
    a_before_net,
    a_before_im,
    a_before_fm,
)
report_model_properties(
    "Site A after",
    a_after_net,
    a_after_im,
    a_after_fm,
)
report_model_properties(
    "Site B candidate",
    b_net,
    b_im,
    b_fm,
)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Optional playout and sample traces
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

random.seed(7)

simulated_log = pm4py.play_out(
    b_net,
    b_im,
    b_fm,
    parameters={
        "noTraces": 10,
        "maxTraceLength": 40,
        "add_only_if_fm_is_reached": True,
    },
)

sample_traces = pm4py.project_on_event_attribute(simulated_log)

print("\nSample Site B traces:")
for trace in sample_traces[:5]:
    print(" -> ".join(trace))

# Model limitations
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

# This POWL/Petri-net model expresses control-flow possibilities and ordering.
# It does NOT by itself encode:
#
# 1. Data-dependent guards:
#    - originals_required selecting the digital versus originals route;
#    - legal_review_required selecting tau versus Legal Review.
#
# 2. Resource capacities:
#    - 4 identity, 7 document, 3 assessment, and 2 legal reviewers;
#    - reviewer calendars, utilization, skills, or WIP limits.
#
# 3. Legacy case locking:
#    - single active owner, lock conflicts, workarounds, or delayed event entry.
#
# 4. Time:
#    - service durations, queue waiting, the 18-hour originals delay,
#      heavy-tailed arrivals, or priority rules.
#
# 5. Probabilities and quality:
#    - the probability of legal review, supplements, approval/rejection,
#      rework, or appeal.
#
# These require data-aware routing, organizational/resource models,
# simulation parameters, and an event-log measurement design in addition
# to the control-flow model.
```

## 9. Recommendation

conditional go

Site B should transfer the partial-order principle, not Site A’s literal rollout or its nine-hour before/after difference. For cases where all required documents are digitally available at registration and the two checks do not depend on each other’s results, Identity Check and Document Check should be released to separate roles at the same time. For originals-required cases, the valid concurrency is Identity Check with Originals Receipt; Document Check must remain downstream of receipt. Assessment must continue to join identity and document completion, and mandatory Legal Review plus the supplement–document–assessment loop must remain intact.

The first cohort should be no-original, document-complete cases without mandatory legal review, stratified by complexity. This cohort is no more than 38% of volume and may be materially smaller because the intersections are unknown. A reasonable initial expectation is a four-to-six-hour reduction within the eligible subgroup and roughly one-to-three hours at whole-site level, with little immediate improvement in p90 because originals, legal review, and rework dominate the tail. Those are planning ranges, not identified effects.

Site B should not copy Site A’s six-open-task limit, cross-training assumptions, universal simultaneous task creation, or claim that 41-to-32 hours was caused by parallelization. The principal risks are false overlap caused by back-entered timestamps, dormant legally blocked tasks, lock workarounds, document or assessment queue saturation, and accelerated arrival into the two-person legal pool.

Expansion should require a pre-specified increase of at least 35 percentage points in verified true overlap, at least three hours less to Assessment-ready, at least two hours less total median cycle time, p90 no more than five hours worse, rework no more than one percentage point worse, no statutory-order violations, and no material 90-day appeal increase. If overlap is achieved but downstream queues erase the gain, modify release caps or capacity before expanding. Abandon universalization if the supported IT version still cannot create true overlap, if p90 worsens by ten hours or more, if rework rises by two points, or if any legal prerequisite is bypassed.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **The originals-required flag is accurate and available at Register** — needed to choose the legally valid document route before task release — test against statutory classification and later case-file review — if false, cases may be incorrectly parallelized or unnecessarily delayed.

2. **All mandatory digital documents can be identified as complete at registration** — needed to ensure an immediately released Document Check has usable inputs — test document-completeness predictions against reviewer findings and cancellation rates — if false, dormant tasks and rework will dilute or reverse the benefit.

3. **Identity and document evidence are operationally independent in the eligible stratum** — needed for simultaneous work to avoid invalidation or duplication — test whether one check’s result changes, restarts, or cancels the other — if false, narrow eligibility or retain the precedence edge for affected claim types.

4. **The supported IT treatment permits genuine simultaneous ownership without compromising access control** — needed for assignment to produce true overlap — test lock logs, overlapping server-side intervals, and user audit trails — if false, the treatment is an implementation no-op and should not expand.

5. **Server-side task-start and completion timestamps reflect execution rather than later data entry** — needed to measure the mediator and waiting decomposition — test entry-time differences and audit sampled cases — if false, overlap-based conclusions are unreliable.

6. **Assessment and legal capacity can absorb at least part of the earlier arrivals under WIP control** — needed for verification savings to survive end to end — test queue-age, utilization, and arrival bursts during the pilot — if false, reduce release rates or add downstream capacity.

7. **Treatment and control capacity can be sufficiently separated to limit queue interference** — needed for a meaningful causal contrast — test cross-lane transfers, reviewer allocation, and control-queue deterioration — if false, use cluster-period assignment or model saturation spillovers explicitly.

8. **Rework, supplements, and 90-day appeals are consistently linked to the originating claim and treatment assignment** — needed to evaluate quality rather than speed alone — test linkage completeness and outcome follow-up — if false, expansion decisions must wait for a reliable quality-outcome pipeline.
