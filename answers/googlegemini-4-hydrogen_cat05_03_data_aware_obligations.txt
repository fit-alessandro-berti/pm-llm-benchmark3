## 1. Temporal Data Semantics

Compliance assessment over mutable case attributes requires strict **event-time state reconstruction** rather than final-case projection:

1. **Deterministic Ordering (`(time:timestamp, event_index)`)**: Events within each case are totally ordered by the lexicographic pair `(time:timestamp, event_index)`. Where two events share the exact same UTC timestamp (e.g., `TX-311` at `2026-04-06T16:20:00Z`), `event_index` establishes causal precedence: `event_index = 3` (`Enhanced_Review`) precedes `event_index = 4` (`Update_Jurisdiction`). Consequently, `Enhanced_Review` evaluated the pre-update `US` state, and the `DE` jurisdiction update took effect *after* the review and *before* `Release_Funds`.
2. **State Reconstruction & Blank Handling**: Case state (`amount`, `risk_level`, `jurisdiction`, `customer_type`, `exemption_id`) is initialized to `UNKNOWN` before the first event and updated strictly forward in `(time:timestamp, event_index)` order. A blank cell means **"no update at this event"**—never "overwrite with null" and never "inherit from future events." Leading blanks before any populated update remain `UNKNOWN` (e.g., `amount` in `TX-305` at creation, review, and release).
3. **Prohibition on Final-State Leakage**: Future events (such as `Settlement_Confirmed` in `TX-305` or `Record_Exemption` in `TX-302` / `TX-310`) must never be copied backward to supply missing state at `Release_Funds`. Post-release events may only serve as **diagnostic or corroborating evidence** (e.g., confirming whether an approval or settlement occurred), subject to R5 and R6.
4. **Material Changes**:
   - **Amount materiality (R4)**: Comparing the latest amount prior to/at `Release_Funds` against the amount active at the latest `Standard_Review` or `Enhanced_Review`: an increase $> 20\%$ ($\frac{A_{\text{new}} - A_{\text{review}}}{A_{\text{review}}} > 0.20$) invalidates that review for amount-dependent obligations; a decrease ($\le 0\%$) or increase $\le 20\%$ does not by itself invalidate it. Any increase from an `UNKNOWN` amount is unclassifiable (`INDETERMINATE`) without independent evidence.
   - **Non-amount material changes**: Reclassification to `risk_level = HIGH`, change to `customer_type = PEP`, jurisdiction changes affecting domestic/foreign status (`DE` vs. non-`DE`), or addition of the active jurisdiction to the sanctions list activate or alter obligations at the event time of the update.
5. **Four Distinct Temporal Dimensions**:
   - **Event time** (`time:timestamp`): When the business/system activity occurred in the operational log.
   - **Effective time** (`effective_from`): When a legal status or exemption begins to apply (defaults to event time unless authoritative evidence establishes another time, e.g., `EX-77` effective `09:00`).
   - **Record time**: When a record is persisted in a database (e.g., `Record_Exemption` at `11:00` after an outage in `TX-302` and `TX-310`). Late record time does not by itself nullify an earlier decision, nor does it prove it.
   - **Evidence time**: When independent, tamper-evident proof was generated (e.g., qualified digital signature timestamp `09:05` for `EX-77` vs. `10:30` for `EX-88`).

### Formal Pseudo-Logic (R1–R7)

Define strict causal order over events $e_1, e_2$ in case $c$:
$$e_1 \prec e_2 \iff \big(t(e_1) < t(e_2)\big) \;\lor\; \big(t(e_1) = t(e_2) \land \text{idx}(e_1) < \text{idx}(e_2)\big)$$
and $e_1 \preceq e_2 \iff e_1 \prec e_2 \lor e_1 = e_2$.

Let $\text{State}(c, e)$ be the forward-only attribute snapshot immediately after applying any updates at event $e$, with $\bot$ denoting `UNKNOWN`:
- **R7 (Forward-Only State)**:
  $$\text{attr}(c, e) = \begin{cases} v(e) & \text{if } e \text{ explicitly sets } \text{attr} = v(e) \neq \text{blank} \\ \text{attr}(c, e_{\text{prev}}) & \text{if blank and } \exists e_{\text{prev}} \prec e \\ \bot & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
- **R4 (Review Validity for Amount)**: For a review event $r \in \{\text{Standard\_Review}, \text{Enhanced\_Review}\}$ and release event $rel$ with $r \prec rel$:
  $$\text{AmtValid}(r, rel) \iff \text{amt}(r) \neq \bot \land \text{amt}(rel) \neq \bot \land \forall u \big(r \prec u \preceq rel \land \text{updates\_amt}(u) \implies \frac{\text{amt}(u) - \text{amt}(r)}{\text{amt}(r)} \le 0.20\big)$$
  If $\text{amt}(r) = \bot$ or $\text{amt}(rel) = \bot$ (when amount is material), $\text{AmtValid}(r, rel) = \bot$ (`INDETERMINATE`).
- **R5 (Exemption Validity)**: An exemption $x$ waives rule $R$ at trigger $rel$ iff:
  $$\text{ValidEx}(x, R, rel) \iff \text{approver}(x)=\text{Head\_Compliance} \land t_{\text{eff}}(x) \le t(rel) \le t_{\text{exp}}(x) \land R \in \text{waives}(x) \land \text{ScopeMatch}(x, \text{State}(c, rel)) \land \text{TimelyProof}(x, rel)$$
  where $\text{TimelyProof}(x, rel)$ requires reliable evidence that approval existed at or before $rel$ (a late DB insert or backdated display field alone is insufficient).
- **R1 (Foreign High-Value)**: At $rel = \text{Release\_Funds}$:
  $$\text{Trig}_{R1}(rel) \iff \text{jur}(rel) \neq \text{DE} \land \text{amt}(rel) \ge 100{,}000$$
  $$\text{Sat}_{R1}(rel) \iff \exists r \big(r \prec rel \land \text{act}(r)=\text{Enhanced\_Review} \land \text{AmtValid}(r, rel) \land \neg\exists m (r \prec m \prec rel \land \text{MaterialTriggerChange}(m))\big) \;\lor\; \exists x\,\text{ValidEx}(x, R1, rel)$$
- **R2 (High-Risk / PEP)**: At $rel = \text{Release\_Funds}$:
  $$\text{Trig}_{R2}(rel) \iff \text{risk}(rel)=\text{HIGH} \lor \text{ctype}(rel)=\text{PEP}$$
  $$\text{Sat}_{R2}(rel) \iff \exists r, a \big(r \prec a \prec rel \land \text{act}(r)=\text{Enhanced\_Review} \land \text{act}(a)=\text{Compliance\_Approve} \land \neg\text{StandardPacketOnly}(a) \land \neg\exists m (r \prec m \prec rel \land \text{R2MaterialChange}(m))\big)$$
- **R3 (Sanctions List Update)**: At $rel = \text{Release\_Funds}$:
  $$\text{Viol}_{R3}(rel) \iff \exists u \big(u \prec rel \land \text{act}(u)=\text{Sanctions\_List\_Update} \land \text{jur\_added}(u)=\text{jur}(rel) \land \neg\exists s (u \prec s \prec rel \land \text{act}(s)=\text{Sanctions\_Screen})\big)$$
- **R6 (Missing Data)**: If a decisive trigger predicate evaluates to $\bot$ (e.g., $\text{jur}(rel) \neq \text{DE} \land \text{amt}(rel)=\bot$ for R1) and no non-speculative evidence resolves it, the obligation status is `INDETERMINATE`.

## 4. Ambiguities and Alternative Readings

### TX-302 (Late Database Record vs. Early Cryptographic Proof & Existing Enhanced Review)
- **Current reading (`COMPLIANT`)**: `TX-302` satisfies R1 independently through `Enhanced_Review` at `09:20` (after `09:00` creation, with no subsequent material change, before `09:40` release). Even if `Enhanced_Review` were absent, `EX-77` is supported by strong corroborating evidence: a valid qualified digital signature at `09:05`, effective `09:00`, matching scope (`GB`, `CORPORATE`, $\le 250\text{k}$), and a documented outage explaining the `11:00` `Record_Exemption` insert.
- **What would change the result**:
  - If `Enhanced_Review` had been absent, `TX-302` would shift from `COMPLIANT` to `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`.
  - If forensic investigation showed the outage log was fabricated or the QES hash did not match `EX-77` (and `Enhanced_Review` were absent), it would shift to `NONCOMPLIANT`.
  - A naive control-flow or record-time-only checker ignoring the QES timestamp would wrongly flag `EX-77` as post-hoc because `Record_Exemption` (`11:00`) follows `Release_Funds` (`09:40`).

### TX-305 (Missing Release Amount & Post-Release Settlement Evidence)
- **Current reading (`INDETERMINATE`)**: At `Create_Transaction`, `Enhanced_Review`, and `Release_Funds`, `amount` is missing. Under R6 and R7, we cannot project the next-day `Settlement_Confirmed` (`150000`) backward as if it were the release state, especially given the absence of an immutable release payload. Moreover, the `Enhanced_Review` document itself lacked an amount, so even if `150000` were released, we cannot verify that no $>20\%$ increase occurred between `11:15` and `11:30` (R4) or that the reviewer could meaningfully assess an amount-dependent obligation.
- **What would change the result**:
  - **To `COMPLIANT`**: Immutable payment-gateway/SWIFT release message plus review workpapers proving `amount` was `< 100000` at release (R1 not triggered), OR proving `amount = 150000` was known and verified during `Enhanced_Review` at `11:15` with no change before `11:30`.
  - **To `NONCOMPLIANT`**: Payment-gateway audit trail showing `amount` was $\ge 100000$ at release while `Enhanced_Review` never evaluated amount, or showing an amount jump $> 20\%$ between `11:15` and `11:30`.

### TX-310 (Contradictory Evidence for Pre-Release Exemption)
- **Current reading (`INDETERMINATE`)**: Only `Standard_Review` occurred (`09:20`), so compliance for the `200000` `AE` transfer hinges entirely on `EX-88`. The evidence is directly contradictory: the PDF displays `08:50` and the transaction requester claims oral approval at `08:45`, whereas the qualified signing certificate was created at `10:30` (30 minutes *after* `10:00` release) and database insertion occurred at `11:00`. Because an interested witness and a backdated display field cannot establish timely approval under R5, `EX-88` is unverified; yet an out-of-band oral approval before formal signing remains factually possible pending investigation.
- **Alternative reading (`NONCOMPLIANT`)**: Under a strict burden-of-proof interpretation of R5 (*"effective only if... the evidence supports that approval existed at the relevant time"*), failing to produce reliable pre-release proof at audit time already constitutes a control failure, yielding `NONCOMPLIANT`.
- **What would change the result**:
  - **To `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`**: Contemporaneous, immutable pre-`10:00` communication from `Head_Compliance` (e.g., signed email, chat audit log, or calendar/ticket approval timestamped `< 10:00`) confirming `08:45` approval before the `10:30` formal certificate signing.
  - **To `NONCOMPLIANT` (definitively)**: `Head_Compliance` testifying or system logs showing the request for `EX-88` was first submitted at or after `10:00`.

### TX-311 (Same-Timestamp Tie-Breaking via `event_index`)
- **Current reading (`COMPLIANT`)**: Using `event_index` at `16:20:00Z`, `Enhanced_Review` (`idx=3`) occurred while `jurisdiction=US` and `amount=115000`, and `Update_Jurisdiction` (`idx=4`) changed `jurisdiction` to `DE` afterward. At `Release_Funds` (`16:30`), `jurisdiction=DE`, so R1 is not triggered.
- **What would change the result**:
  - If the jurisdiction transition had been reversed (`DE -> US` at `16:20`, `idx=4`) after `Enhanced_Review` (`idx=3`), a timestamp-only engine ignoring `event_index` could misread `Enhanced_Review` as occurring *after* or *concurrently with* the `US` change, masking a material post-review shift to a foreign jurisdiction.
  - If `Update_Jurisdiction=DE` were erroneous (e.g., actual destination remained `US`), `TX-311` would still remain `COMPLIANT` under R1 because `Enhanced_Review` (`idx=3`) actually completed after the `16:10` amount update (`115000`) and before `16:30` release.

### TX-306 (Additional Case: Materiality Boundary for Non-Amount vs. Amount Changes)
- **Current reading (`COMPLIANT`)**: The amount increase at `12:30` ($220\text{k} \to 230\text{k}$, $+4.55\%$) is below R4's $20\%$ invalidation threshold, leaving `Enhanced_Review` (`12:15`) and `Compliance_Approve` (`12:25`) valid.
- **What would change the result**: If an internal bank rule treated *any* post-`Compliance_Approve` increase (even $<20\%$) as requiring fresh `Head_Compliance` sign-off under R2—or if `230000` crossed an internal escalation tier above `225000`—the case would flip to `NONCOMPLIANT`.

## 6. Why Control-Flow-Only Conformance Is Insufficient

1. **Final-State Leakage (Static Case-Attribute Fallacy)**: Control-flow conformance tools that attach case attributes globally (often taking the final or latest attribute value, such as `Settlement_Confirmed = 150000` in `TX-305` or `jurisdiction = DE` in `TX-311`) project future state backward in time. This either creates false obligations on events that occurred before a threshold was crossed or fabricates certainty where data was missing at release.
2. **Post-Hoc Exemptions and Out-of-Order Persistence**: In `TX-302` and `TX-310`, `Record_Exemption` appears *after* `Release_Funds` in the control flow. A control-flow model requiring `Record_Exemption < Release_Funds` falsely condemns `TX-302` (which had a valid `09:05` QES delayed by an outage), whereas a model allowing `Record_Exemption` after release fails to distinguish legitimate outage backfills (`TX-302`) from unsubstantiated post-release approvals (`TX-310`).
3. **Material Data Changes After Review (R4 Invalidation)**: A control-flow precedence constraint (`Enhanced_Review -> Release_Funds`) passes both `TX-306` ($+4.55\%$ increase) and `TX-309` ($+22.88\%$ increase). Only arithmetic comparison across event-time snapshots distinguishes non-material adjustments (`TX-306`, `TX-308`) from review-invalidating jumps (`TX-301`, `TX-309`).
4. **Conditional Rule Activation**: Obligations R1, R2, and R3 are data-guarded. `Enhanced_Review` is mandatory in `TX-301` (`US`, `125k`) and `TX-307` (`PEP`), optional/untriggered in `TX-304` (`60k`, `MEDIUM`) and `TX-311` (`DE` at release), and insufficient without `Compliance_Approve` in `TX-303` (`HIGH`). Static Petri nets, BPMN, or untyped DECLARE templates cannot separate allowed vs. forbidden traces without evaluating event-time predicates.
5. **Missing Data and Epistemic Uncertainty (R6)**: Control-flow conformance outputs binary/numeric fitness (`fit` vs. `deviation`). When decisive data is absent (`TX-305`), control-flow alignment either defaults silently to "untriggered" (false negative) or forces an arbitrary assumption, rather than returning `INDETERMINATE`.
6. **Same-Timestamp Ordering Collisions (R7)**: Many process-mining parsers sort solely by timestamp or use unstable sorts when timestamps collide. Without `event_index`, concurrent events (`TX-311` at `16:20:00Z`) can be inverted, misinterpreting which attribute state a review actually examined.
7. **Semantic Scope and Packet Sufficiency**: Control-flow labels like `Compliance_Approve` look identical in `TX-306` and `TX-307`, yet `TX-307`'s `Compliance_Approve` reviewed only a `Standard_Review` packet. Similarly, `Enhanced_Review` in `TX-305` had no amount data. Control-flow conformance cannot verify whether an activity's *payload* matched the legal prerequisite.

## 7. Recommended Logging and Control Improvements

1. **Immutable Release Payloads**: At `Release_Funds`, persist a cryptographically hashed, non-nullable release snapshot containing `amount`, `currency`, `risk_level`, `jurisdiction`, `customer_type`, and `exemption_id` (plus hard pre-release validation blocking release when decisive fields are missing, eliminating `TX-305`-style ambiguity).
2. **Separation of Event, Effective, Record, and Signature Timestamps**: Log four explicit UTC timestamp fields on every compliance/exemption event—`event_time`, `effective_time`, `record_insert_time`, and `qes_signature_time`—plus an `outage_or_backfill_reason` code when `record_insert_time > event_time`.
3. **Rule Version & Decision Snapshot Logging**: Record `policy_rule_version` (e.g., `AML-2026.04`) and the exact evaluated rule outcomes (`R1..R3` triggered/satisfied/waived flags) at `Release_Funds` so audits are reproducible even if thresholds later change.
4. **Structured Exemption Scope & Cryptographic Binding**: Store structured exemption attributes (`approved_by_role`, `scope_jurisdictions`, `scope_customer_types`, `scope_max_amount`, `waived_rule_ids`, `expires_at`, `qes_cert_serial`, `doc_hash`) in an append-only register and require a valid pre-release QES hash reference on `Release_Funds` before allowing R1 waiver.
5. **Change Provenance & Review Invalidation Links**: On every `Update_*` or `Risk_Reclassify` event, log `previous_value`, `new_value`, `pct_delta`, `change_source`, and `invalidates_review_ids`. On `Review` and `Compliance_Approve` events, log `reviewed_snapshot_hash` and `referenced_review_id` so a `Compliance_Approve` bound to a `Standard_Review` cannot be misread as approving an `Enhanced_Review` (`TX-307`).
6. **Stable, Monotonic Event Ordering**: Assign a globally monotonic per-case `event_sequence_id` (and microsecond/nanosecond UTC timestamps synchronized via NTP/PTP) across all upstream microservices so concurrent updates never rely on fragile tie-breaking.
