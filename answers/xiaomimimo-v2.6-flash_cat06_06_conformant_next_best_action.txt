**Dominance arguments (used instead of exact monetisation):**

1. **(iii) dominates (i).** The *low* end of (iii), €100k, is 2.9× the *high* end of (i), €35k. Even if the probability of detection/sanction were only 0.25, expected (iii) = €25k–125k+, which is at or above the entire upside of meeting the deadline.
