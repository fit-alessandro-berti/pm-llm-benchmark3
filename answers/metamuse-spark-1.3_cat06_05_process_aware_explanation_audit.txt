## 2. Process-model consistency checks

Concrete prefix-aware validation suite to run before any explanation is accepted:

1. **Prefix-truncation / point-in-time replay:** Truncate every trace to events with `event_time  t0` (first Coverage Check Completed) using reliable timestamps, not ingestion order. Recompute every feature from truncated prefix only. Any feature whose recomputed value  stored value fails availability. This catches `total_duration, appeal_seen, full_variant, num_requests_total, final_decision, last_role`.
2. **Complete-trace detector:** Flag any feature definition that scans `entire case`, `anywhere`, `total`, `final`, `last`, `variant_id` without `t0` predicate, or uses warehouse closure fields. Requires code + data-lineage inspection.
3. **Enabled / future-possible / impossible classifier at t0:** From normative model, at t0 after REG-COV the enabled set is {Damage Assessment (fast track), Documents Requested, Fraud trigger}. Appeal, second Decision, Close are *future-possible but not enabled and not observed*; DamageDecision and RequestReceive are *legitimate eventual* but future; CloseAssessment is *never enabled*. An explanation referencing an observed occurrence of a not-yet-observed activity = leakage; referencing impossible order = ordering or conformance bug.
4. **Activity-order feasibility: footprint + declarative + alignment:** Build normative footprint (follows/precedes/exclusive/parallel). Check: Coverage before Assessment/Request; Request before Receive before Validate; Decision after Assessment; Close after final Decision; Appeal only after Partial/Deny; Fraud parallel after trigger. Run trace alignment (cost-based conformance) to detect deviations. Limitation: footprint/DFG shows observed frequency, not permission – a frequent DFG edge (e.g. CloseAssessment due to same-timestamp misorder) is not a rule. Declarative constraints (e.g. `Response(Request,Receive)`, `Precedence(Partial/Deny,Appeal)`, `NotCoExistence` where needed) give interpretable pass/fail but need expert thresholds.
5. **Same-timestamp ambiguity test:** List all pairs with equal timestamps and missing sequence index. Require deterministic tie-breaker (e.g. normative order + index) or mark order unknown and suppress order-dependent patterns. Case Z Close/Assessment pair must be flagged unknown, not explained as CloseAssessment cause.
6. **Repeated-activity semantics check:** Allow two `Settlement Decision Recorded` events iff trace contains Appeal between them with first decision  {Partial,Deny}. Otherwise flag duplication error. This is why `AppealDecision` is legitimate later while `CloseAssessment` remains illegitimate.
7. **Mutable / batch-refresh audit:** For each feature store field record `valid_time, ingestion_time, refresh_cadence`. Fail if `valid_time > t0` or batch window (e.g. 23:00 snapshot, extraction-date join, current score) can include post-t0 updates. Covers `same_day_count, prior_count, risk_score_current, employee experience, final_handler_team`.
8. **Explanation-time conformance:** Every local pattern contribution must pass 1-7. If pattern refers to future events, suppress even if SHAP is large.

## 3. Alternative feature interpretations

**Defensible predictive signal – may claim association with breach risk at t0, may not claim cause or lever:**
`initial_damage_complexity`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`. Both frozen/past-only, process-plausible (complexity  more work; age  less slack). May say “higher predicted risk in this subgroup.” May not say “changing complexity label” or “resetting clock” would causally reduce breach, nor that importance = causal effect.

**Process-state or congestion signal – may claim system load / state, may not claim individual fault:**
`queue_length_at_t0` (±5 min snapshot). May say “prediction made under high queue, historically associated with higher breach rate; triage/monitor.” May not say queue *causes* this claim’s breach or that intervening on queue guarantees prevention; queue is shared, endogenous, and tolerance-bound.

**Proxy requiring fairness/robustness review – may use for prediction only after review, must not present causally or act on directly:**
`claimant_region`, `claimant_channel`, `initial_reserve` (if verified t0). Region/channel/reserve correlate with network coverage, language, broker process, socioeconomic factors and adjuster judgement. May say “model associates R4 / Broker / €25k reserve with higher risk in historical data.” May not say “region/channel causes delay,” use for differential prioritisation, or imply changing channel/region/reserve is an intervention. Requires disaggregated performance, counterfactual stability, and search for less discriminatory mediators (e.g. repair capacity, language support).

**Drift/calendar proxy – may claim temporal heterogeneity, may not claim operational causality:**
`calendar_week`. Known at t0 but confounded by policy change + holidays. May say “week 52 historically higher risk, likely reflecting holidays/policy regime.” May not say “week 52 is operationally worse” as stable actionable cause. Requires adding holiday/policy indicators, regime-split validation, drift monitoring.

**Unusable until lineage is repaired – may not use in t0 explanations or decisions at all now; may become defensible after point-in-time fix:**
`prior_claim_count_before_t0`, `same_day_document_count_snapshot`, `risk_score_current  risk_score_at_t0`, `final_handler_team  team_at_t0`, `last_resource_role  role_at_t0`, employee experience, and by definition `total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision` and all full-trace sequence patterns at t0. The first group is *repairable* to prefix-only versions; the second group is *inherently future* at t0 and can only be used for later prediction points. No predictive, causal or operational claim permitted until recomputed from prefix replay with historical timestamps and re-calibrated.

General boundary: predictive importance  causal effect  actionability. Fairness review is separate from predictive validity – a feature can be validly predictive yet unfair/unstable to act on.

## 5. Recommendations for explanation validation

**P0 – Stop leakage now:** Block `total_case_duration, appeal_seen, full_trace_variant, num_requests_total, final_decision, last_role, future patterns` from t0 feature set and dashboards. Invalidate Cases X/Y/Z scores; require recompute. Communicate that team stories (“long cases breach”, “appeals cause”, “final team determines”) are invalid causal readings of leaked SHAP.

**P0 – Feature store with historical validity:** Every feature needs `event_time, valid_time, ingestion_time, refresh_cadence`. Enforce point-in-time joins (` t0`), event-time cutoffs for counts, versioned risk scores and reserve, timestamped team/role assignment logs. Fix `prior_claim` SQL to `< t0`, `same_day_count` to `upload_time  t0`, employee experience to `as_of t0`.

**P1 – Prefix-only recomputation pipeline:** Standard replay: truncate to first Coverage Check, recompute all features, re-train, re-evaluate (AUC, calibration, decision-curve) and regenerate SHAP from retrained prefix-only model. Report performance drop as expected cost of removing leakage. Recalibrate after removal – leaked models are miscalibrated.

**P1 – Process-conformance gate:** Integrate checks from §2 into CI: prefix test, complete-trace grep, enabled/future/impossible classifier, alignment + declarative rules, same-timestamp quarantine, appealsecond-decision legitimacy rule. Fail build on violation. Remember DFG frequency  normative permission; require expert-approved normative model.

**P1 – Explanation stability and fidelity:** Test SHAP stability under ±5 min queue tolerance, timestamp tie-breaking, and prefix perturbation; report variance. Verify that top contributors are all prefix-available; measure faithfulness on prefix-only model.

**P2 – Proxy, fairness and robustness:** For `region, channel, reserve, calendar_week`: disaggregated error rates, counterfactual / group-stability tests, mediation analysis (repair capacity, language, policy regime). Add explicit holiday/policy features. Prohibit operational action on sensitive proxies without review; prefer mediators (e.g. add capacity, language support) as interventions.

**P2 – Monitoring for drift and schema change:** Monitor `calendar_week` regime shifts, queue distribution, complexity mix, appeal/decision rates, sequence-index missingness, batch delays, and feature-store freshness. Alert on new activities, repeated Decision without Appeal, or reappearance of CloseAssessment edges. Version normative model and re-validate on change.
