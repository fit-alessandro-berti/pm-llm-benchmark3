Four-eyes filtering. The helper below treats cases returned by `filter_four_eyes_principle` as compliant and examines the complement among cases that actually contain both activities.

```python
def cases_with_both_activities(event_log, a1, a2):
    d = pm4py.convert_to_dataframe(event_log)
    sub = d[d["concept:name"].isin([a1, a2])]
    counts = sub.groupby("case:concept:name")["concept:name"].nunique()
    return set(counts[counts == 2].index)

def four_eyes_violations(event_log, a1, a2):
    compliant_log = pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(event_log, a1, a2)
    compliant_cases = set(pm4py.convert_to_dataframe(compliant_log)["case:concept:name"])
    both = cases_with_both_activities(event_log, a1, a2)
    return both - compliant_cases
```

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Based on the log alone, the observed patterns are most consistent with a mixture of emergency handling, informal role specialization, control exceptions, and data-quality artefacts; they do not establish fraud or collusion. High co-working between Buyer_Aline/Approver_Elena and Buyer_Boris/Approver_Felix appears consistent with routine team assignment, and the fast Carla/Gita and Dan/Hugo channels align with documented emergency medical and facilities processes.
