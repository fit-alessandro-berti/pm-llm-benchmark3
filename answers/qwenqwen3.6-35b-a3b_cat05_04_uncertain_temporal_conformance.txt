## 1. Normalization Assumptions

- **Interval Construction & Clock Offset Direction**: `clock_offset = recorded_clock - true_clock`. Therefore, `true_clock = recorded_clock - offset`. For an offset bound `[a, b]`, true time is `[recorded - b, recorded - a]`. Resolution/rounding uncertainty is applied to the `recorded_clock` first, then the offset bound is subtracted.
- **Rounding/Floor Semantics**: 
  - `floor to minute` (OPS-BER, VENDOR-NY, LEGACY-BER): Displayed `HH:MM` implies recorded time in `[HH:MM:00, HH:MM:59.999]`.
  - `round to nearest N` (SENSOR ±10s, MOBILE-LON ±1m): Implies recorded time in `[displayed - N/2, displayed + N/2)`.

## 7. Governance Recommendation

- **UTC Storage & Raw Retention**: All systems must store raw timestamps in UTC with sub-second precision. Display/localized times must be derived, never stored as authoritative.
- **Source Offset Telemetry**: Every event source must attach a telemetry payload containing `clock_offset_bounds`, `last_ntp_sync`, and `resolution_metadata`. Central SIEM/ITSM must ingest and validate these before SLA evaluation.
- **Resolution & Rounding Metadata**: Explicitly log whether timestamps are floored, rounded, or exact. Downstream analytics must apply correction intervals per Section 1 semantics.
- **Lifecycle & Causal Identifiers**: Enforce strict `case_id` propagation across systems. Log ingestion order and causal flags (`Alert_Sent` vs `Alert_Received`) to prevent semantic conflation.
- **DST Fold Handling**: Time-zone libraries must use IANA database with explicit fold-aware parsing. Ambiguous intervals must be preserved as unions until disambiguated by downstream context or secondary sync.
- **Enforcement Policy**: Interval overlap is **insufficient** for CERTAIN_PASS. Compliance dashboards must require `CERTAIN_PASS` classification. `POSSIBLE_VIOLATION` triggers mandatory review; `CERTAIN_VIOLATION` triggers auto-remediation or escalation.
- **Provenance & Auditability**: Retain original source payloads, offset bounds, and normalization logs for a minimum of 7 years. Enable replay of interval-conformance checks against historical data as clock drift or DST policies change.
