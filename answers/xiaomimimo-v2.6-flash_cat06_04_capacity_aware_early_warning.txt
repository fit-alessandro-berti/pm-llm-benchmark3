- **Cool-down**: after any swarm on a case, no re-swarm for 120 min unless a material event occurs; max 1 re-swarm per case per24h, duty-manager approved.
- **Suppression**: a case in cool-down is suppressed from the alert queue; it retains a standing claim (score) that refreshes each cycle but does not escalate.
- **Escalation**: to Tier A requires NB_stress > 0, admissible alert, eligibility. Escalation to duty manager for: must-not-fail override, re-swarm, reserve release, fairness-quota shortfall.
- **Re-entry**: stage change back, new material event, model version change with documented cause, or cool-down expiry.
- **Expiry**: claim expires at Diagnosis Complete (ineligible) or when deadline < 45 min (cannot fit a swarm) or when p* < 0.15.
- **Anti-gaming**: never rank by alert count; event provenance hash required; a refresh with unchanged features yields p* = 0.
