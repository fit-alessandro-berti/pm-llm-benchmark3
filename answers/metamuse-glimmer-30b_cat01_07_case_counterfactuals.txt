D2 could start Mon evening with queue offset. Earlier Document completion moves Assessment readiness earlier, but Assessment queue at Tue 08:00 is A1 9h A2 6h. Earlier readiness may be absorbed by queue. If D2 requests clarification, a Request More Info loop adds Document and Assessment.
Process-feasibility check:
Originals Receipt precedes Document Check satisfied. Identity Check already complete. No high-risk compliance required. Resource constraint respected.
Best/base/worst case:
Best -18h to -24h with clean D2 check. Base small gain absorbed by assessment queue. Worst +24h to +48h with clarification and rework.
Evidence that would reverse the conclusion:
Data showing D2 clarification rate >30% on handwritten standard documents or D2 queue consistently >12h after originals receipt.

### K-337
Factual mechanism:
Supplier inconsistency triggered Request More Info1, then remaining inconsistency triggered Request More Info2 with weekend delay, leading to Document Check3 and Assessment3 before Compliance.
Minimal intervention:
After Assessment2 prohibit second Request More Info and require decision on evidence available.
Temporal propagation:
Removing Document Check3 Mon 13:00-16:30 and Assessment3 Tue 08:00-10:30 removes ~2.5 days including weekend external wait. Compliance can be scheduled after Assessment2 Fri 10:30 subject to C1 availability, moving Decision from Wed to Mon/Tue.
Process-feasibility check:
High risk/high value requires Compliance Review after latest Assessment before Decision. Four-eyes holds A1 vs C1. Decision policy change is allowed by intervention.
Best/base/worst case:
Best -72h with immediate C1 availability. Base -48h with C1 queue. Worst decision changes to reject or conditional approval increasing appeal risk.
Evidence that would reverse the conclusion:
Evidence that residual discrepancy materially changes loss exposure or that regulators require full supplier confirmation for high-risk cases.

### K-411
Factual mechanism:
Triage created only identity task, case lock prevented document ownership until identity complete, forcing sequential Identity Tue 11:30-13:20 then Document Tue 15:00-19:30.
Minimal intervention:
At Triage create both verification tasks with controlled workaround allowing I2 and D2 to work concurrently with real-time timestamps.
Temporal propagation:
Document Check can start at Triage completion Tue 09:35 rather than after Identity. Both checks finish by ~Tue 19:30. Assessment readiness moves from Wed 09:30 to Wed morning, but Assessment queue had grown Wed 08:00 A1 7h A2 5h, so gain may be absorbed.
Process-feasibility check:
No originals required, concurrency allowed. Order Identity & Document < Assessment preserved. No compliance required.
Best/base/worst case:
Best -12h if assessment slot moves earlier. Base -4h to -6h. Worst 0h if assessment bottleneck absorbs gain or workaround creates false concurrency.
Evidence that would reverse the conclusion:
Evidence that true concurrency is not achievable due to case lock data integrity or that assessment queue consistently absorbs all verification gains.

## 4. Capacity and interference analysis
| intervention | local resource demand change | queue likely to shrink | queue likely to grow | affected non-target cases | sign of system-wide effect | key nonlinearity |
| K-104 early escalation/compliance prep | increases early S1 and C1 demand; reserves C1 | Compliance queue for target case | Compliance queue for other high-risk cases | urgent compliance cases needing C1 | ambiguous to negative | reservation creates priority inversion and incomplete packet waste |
| K-219 different document resource | shifts Document Check from D1 to D2 earlier | D1 queue | D2 queue; Assessment queue if rework | D1-bound complex cases benefit; D2-bound standard cases delayed | ambiguous | rework probability nonlinear with handwriting difficulty |
| K-337 suppress second rework loop | reduces Document Check and Assessment demand, moves Compliance earlier | Document queue; Assessment queue; Compliance queue | none | cases that would use freed capacity benefit | positive short term | decision quality vs time trade-off |
| K-411 parallelize verification | increases parallel verification demand | Identity queue; Document queue for target | Assessment queue | cases waiting for assessment | neutral to negative | bottleneck migration from verification to assessment |

A 100-case weekly rollout amplifies interference. Reserving C1 for K-104 style pre-work would consume the single certified reviewer’s window, displacing urgent high-risk cases and potentially increasing their cycle time by hours to days. Substitution of D1 by D2 for K-219 reduces D1 queue but raises D2 utilization and rework risk; with 100 cases the clarification rate dominates, generating additional Request More Info loops that feed back into Document and Assessment queues and create weekend external-response delays. Suppressing rework for K-337 frees Document and Assessment capacity but shifts risk to decision quality; at scale, a higher rate of conditional approvals raises appeal and loss exposure non-linearly. Parallel verification for K-411 moves work earlier into Assessment, which is already a bottleneck on Tue-Wed; system-wide effect is bottleneck migration, with timestamp behavior diverging from true concurrency if logging is batch. Weekend effects matter for external supplier responses and for C1 availability, making Friday interventions particularly sensitive.

## 7. Final synthesis
K-411 parallelizing verification appears most robust. It preserves order constraints, does not change decision policy, and is reversible with minimal compliance risk. Gains are modest and may be absorbed by the assessment bottleneck, but the intervention is unlikely to create adverse spillovers beyond queue migration. K-337 suppressing a second rework loop is most dangerous despite potential speed. It trades time for decision quality and compliance defensibility on high-risk high-value cases with slow external suppliers; at scale it raises appeal risk and loss exposure non-linearly and is hard to reverse. Local counterfactuals are least transportable to system-wide policy for K-104 early compliance preparation. The benefit depends on a single scarce resource C1 and on packet completeness; a case-level saving can become system-wide delay through reservation displacement. K-219 resource substitution also loses transportability because rework probability is case-specific and queue effects flip with volume. Valuable additional data are event-level queue occupancy and actual service times by resource and document complexity, timestamp fidelity for true concurrency versus batch entry, and external response time distributions by supplier and day of week to calibrate rework risk.

## 8. Assumption audit
1. Partial order relations are hard constraints. structural
2. Four-eyes rule prohibits same person for final Assessment and final Compliance Review. structural
3. Queue estimates are forecasts, actual wait depends on arrivals. temporal
4. Preliminary compliance work saves 1-3h later without requiring full evidence. temporal
5. D2 is slower than D1 on complex handwriting and has higher clarification probability. resource
6. External supplier response times are exogenous and unaffected by internal policy. behavioral
7. Decision policy permits conditional approval with documented residual discrepancy. quality
8. Reserving C1 capacity displaces other cases rather than adding capacity. interference
