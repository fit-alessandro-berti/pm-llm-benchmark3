### **1. Decision Rule**
**Eligibility Criteria:**
- **Stage:** Only cases in `Triage` or `Diagnosis Started` (exclude `Diagnosis Complete` or later, e.g., I-03).
- **Alert Persistence:**
  - **First alert:** Always eligible if other criteria are met.
  - **Repeated alerts:**
    - *No new event:* Suppress if `p_now`  `p_prev` < 0.05 (e.g., I-05, where  = +0.02).
    - *New event:* Re-evaluate if `p_now`  `p_prev`  0.05 *or* new diagnostic data (e.g., I-07, new log uploaded).
- **Minimum Evidence Change:** Require   0.05 or qualitative justification (e.g., new logs, imputation flags) to avoid overreacting to noise.

**Probability Adjustments for Calibration:**
- **SME:** Add +0.065 (midpoint of 0.04–0.09 underprediction) to `p_now`.
- **Strategic Enterprise:** Subtract 0.085 (midpoint of 0.05–0.12 overprediction) from `p_now`.
- **Non-Native-Language Support:** Use raw `p_now` but flag for higher uncertainty (no adjustment due to sparse data).
- **Public Sector:** No adjustment (calibration acceptable).

**Prioritization Score:**
For eligible cases, compute:
`Score = (Adjusted p_now) × (Value of avoiding breach) × (Midpoint of absolute risk reduction)  (False-alarm burden + €450)`
- **Tiebreaker:** Prefer cases with:
  1. Higher time urgency (shorter time to SLA).
  2. Lower uncertainty in risk reduction (narrower ranges).
  3. Higher value-to-cost ratio (`Value / (False-alarm burden + €450)`).

**Intervention Threshold:**
- Intervene if `Score > 0` *and* the case is in the top 4 by score (or top 3 if reserving a slot).
- **Robustness Check:** Exclude cases where the *lower bound* of risk reduction × value < €450 + false-alarm burden (e.g., I-08’s lower bound: 0.05 × €100,000 = €5,000 > €5,450, but spillover may tip the balance).

### **5. Fairness and Feedback-Loop Risks**
**Risks:**
1. **Value-Based Starvation:** High-value cases (e.g., I-04, I-08) may crowd out lower-value but high-risk cases (e.g., I-01, I-07).
   - *Safeguard:* Reserve 1 slot for cases with `Value < €20k` if their `Score` is in the top 5.
2. **Non-Native-Language Uncertainty:** I-09’s sparse calibration may lead to over/under-intervention.
   - *Safeguard:* Require manual approval for Non-Native-Language cases; default to "wait" unless risk reduction lower bound > 0.20.
3. **Differential Calibration:**
   - SME: Underprediction  may miss interventions. *Safeguard:* Add +0.065 to `p_now` for SME.
   - Strategic Enterprise: Overprediction  may over-intervene. *Safeguard:* Subtract 0.085 from `p_now`.
4. **Feedback Loops:**
   - Intervening on high-risk cases may bias future models to associate high `p_now` with "needs swarm," reducing generalizability.
   - *Safeguard:* Randomly withhold intervention on 10% of eligible cases to maintain observational data.
5. **Spillover Harm:**
   - Untreated diagnosis-stage cases (e.g., I-04, I-08) may suffer disproportionately.
   - *Safeguard:* Track spillover harm by segment; if >5% of untreated cases breach due to delay, reduce slots by 1.

**Concrete Actions:**
- **Audit:** Weekly review of intervention rates by segment to detect starvation.
- **Threshold Adjustment:** If >30% of interventions are on Strategic Enterprise, increase their `p_now` adjustment to 0.10.

### **6. Monitoring and Recalibration Plan**
**Operational Monitoring:**
- **Queue Metrics:** Track average diagnosis time and breach rates for treated vs. untreated cases.
- **Slot Utilization:** Alert if >80% of slots are used on a single segment for 3+ days.

**Predictive Monitoring:**
- **Calibration Drift:** Monthly backtests by segment; flag if absolute calibration error >0.10.
- **Feature Stability:** Monitor imputation rates (e.g., I-06’s missing queue feature); retrain if >5% of cases have imputed fields.

**Causal-Effect Monitoring:**
- **A/B Testing:** Randomly assign 10% of eligible cases to "no swarm" to estimate true risk reduction.
- **Spillover Tracking:** For each used slot, measure breach rate changes in the 6 nearest untreated diagnosis-stage cases.

**Capacity Monitoring:**
- **Delay Impact:** If aggregate delay per slot exceeds 25 minutes, reduce slots by 1.
- **Cost Tracking:** If false-alarm burdens exceed €1k/case on average, tighten the `Score` threshold.

**Fairness Monitoring:**
- **Segment Parity:** Ensure intervention rates for Public Sector/SME are within 20% of their breach rate share.
- **Feedback Loop:** Compare model predictions for cases with/without historical interventions; retrain if divergence >0.05.

**Recalibration Triggers:**
- Change the `Score` threshold if:
  - Net impact (from Section 3) drops below €20k in the Base scenario for 2+ weeks.
  - Spillover harm exceeds €10k/week.
- Adjust segment-specific probability corrections if calibration error exceeds 0.15.

**Policy Review:**
- **Biweekly:** Review Sections 3–5 with stakeholders; adjust rules if spillover or fairness metrics degrade.
- **Quarterly:** Full policy overhaul with updated causal-effect estimates.
