# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

Use the POWL definition as the specification. Write `R` for Register Change, `A` for Assess Risk, `E` for Prepare Evidence, `L` for Legal Review, `C` for Configure Trial, `I` for Inspect Trial, `D` for Correct Defect, `U` for Release Change and `Z` for Archive Change. Juxtaposition denotes successive visible events of one case. A completed witness starts at the initial marking and ends at exactly the final marking. For a freedom such as “either order is possible,” losing a required word is a negative test of the model. There is no forbidden ordering between those two activities.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | `R` occurs exactly once, first. Both `A` and `E` occur exactly once after it. | `RAECIUZ` | Reject `ARECIUZ` and `RAAECIUZ`. | Yes, in visible words. |
| I2 | `A` and `E` are unordered. Neither requires completion of the other. | Admit both `RAECIUZ` and `REACIUZ`. After `R`, look for a reachable marking where `A` and `E` can fire as one concurrent step. | Losing either word introduces a visible restriction. An exclusive choice of two serial schedules could pass both word tests. | Both orders are visible. Independence needs evidence about enabling and conflict. |
| I3 | The legal branch chooses `L` once or a silent skip. `L` requires `A` and can precede `E`. | `RAECIUZ` skips legal. `RALECIUZ` takes legal before evidence. | Reject `RELACIUZ` and `RAELLCIUZ`. Losing all skip words makes legal mandatory. | Yes for the choice of zero or one `L` and its visible prerequisites. The skip firing is hidden. |
| I4 | The first `C` waits for both `A` and `E`. | `RAECIUZ` and `REACIUZ` | Reject `RACEIUZ` and `RECAIUZ`, each of which starts verification with one prerequisite outstanding. | Yes for visible precedence. |
| I5 | There is no precedence edge in either direction between the optional-legal child and the verification child. Their own prerequisites still apply. | `RAELCIUZ`, `RAECLIUZ` and `RAECILUZ` put legal before, within and after verification. | Losing `RAECLIUZ` by forcing the two children to execute as indivisible serial blocks violates this freedom. | Interleavings are visible. A common concurrent state and the time of a silent branch decision cannot be inferred from these words. |
| I6 | Verification executes the body at least once. Its first two verification events are `CI`. | `RAECIUZ` | Reject `RAEUZ` and `RAEICUZ`. | Yes. |
| I7 | The projection of every completed case onto verification labels is exactly `CI(DCI)*`. `D` is the redo child. Each redo is followed by the whole body again. | `RAECIDCIUZ` and `RAECIDCLIDCIUZ`. In the latter, `L` interleaves with the second body. | Reject `RAECICIUZ` (missing redo), `RAECIDUZ` (exit after redo) and `RAECIDICUZ` (reversed second body). | Yes for the projected word. The loop boundary is structural information. |
| I8 | `U` occurs once after both children finish. On the legal-skip path the legal child also completes. Verification exits after an `I`. | `RAECILUZ` takes legal late. `RAECIUZ` releases after a skip. | Reject `RAECUILZ` (early release) and `RAECIULZ` (legal after release). | Visible ordering is testable. Waiting for the hidden skip token needs routing or marking evidence. |
| I9 | `Z` occurs once, after `U`, as the last event. Successful completion leaves exactly the designated final marking. | `RAECIUZ` ending at that marking | Reject `RAECIZU`, `RAECIU` and `RAECIUZZ` as complete words. A token in the sink plus a token elsewhere also fails the final-marking test. | The visible ending is observable. Proper completion needs the accepting markings. |

The complete language has a short independent construction. Choose `AE` or `EA`, prepend `R`, append `CI(DCI)*` and then `UZ`. Either omit `L` or insert it at any one boundary after `A` and before `U`. This construction uses the transitive consequences of the supplied precedence edges. It imposes no dependency from `E` to `L`.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary** POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`, then `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`. Audit with `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)`. | A marked net can express the two different prerequisite sets directly. This route avoids requiring a process-tree decomposition. | Translating places, silent transitions and token synchronization to gateways can obscure or change a dependency. | A required conversion or marking is missing, the result fails diagnostics, or the audit finds a missing behavior, extra behavior or unresolved synchronization. |
| **Conditional** POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN | Same first call, then `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` and, on success, `convert_to_bpmn(tree)`. Back-convert this BPMN separately. | A faithful tree could make the optional choice and body/redo structure easier to inspect. | At the child level the dependencies `A < legal`, `A < verification` and `E < verification` form an N-shaped order. Grouping `A` and `E` under a single fork/join before both successors would delay legal. | Tree reduction raises an exception, or a returned tree or its BPMN fails fidelity checks. Preserve the exception and continue auditing the primary route. |

The N-shaped order is an obstacle to a simple series/parallel decomposition with one occurrence of each child. A reduction failure is evidence about that converter on that net. Conversely, a converter could encode schedules with duplicated labels and exclusive choices, making a tree worth checking even if it passes all word tests.

# 3. Conversion Code

Run the following after the supplied definition of `source_powl`. Section 5 continues in the same Python session. The source net and each BPMN back-conversion get their own markings and diagnostics. Each run writes figures into a fresh directory so that an old tree figure cannot be mistaken for a successful conversion.

```python
from collections import Counter, deque
from pathlib import Path
from tempfile import mkdtemp
from time import monotonic

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics

out = Path(mkdtemp(prefix="change_audit_", dir="."))
print("PM4Py", pm4py.__version__, "output", out.resolve())
errors = {}

def record(stage, exc):
    errors[stage] = f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"
    print(stage, errors[stage])

def attempt(stage, fn, *args, **kwargs):
    try:
        return fn(*args, **kwargs)
    except Exception as exc:
        record(stage, exc)
        return None

def required(obj, kind):
    if obj is None:
        raise ValueError(f"Converter returned no {kind}")
    return obj

def accepting(obj):
    net, im, fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(obj)
    required(net, "net")
    if im is None or fm is None:
        raise ValueError(f"Incomplete accepting net: im={im!r}, fm={fm!r}")
    return net, im, fm

def make_bpmn(*args):
    return required(pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(*args), "BPMN")

attempt("draw source POWL", pm4py.save_vis_powl, source_powl,
        str(out / "01_source_powl.svg"))
try:
    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = accepting(source_powl)
except Exception as exc:
    record("POWL to accepting net", exc)
    raise RuntimeError("Both routes require this accepting net") from exc

baseline = (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
nets = {"02_from_powl": baseline}
bpmns = {}
bpmn_from_net = attempt("net to BPMN", make_bpmn, *baseline)
if bpmn_from_net is not None:
    bpmns["03_via_net"] = bpmn_from_net

try:
    tree = required(pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(*baseline), "process tree")
except Exception as exc:
    record("net to process tree", exc)
    print("Tree route unavailable. Inspect the reduction diagnostic and the "
          "cross-branch prerequisites before attributing a cause.")
else:
    attempt("draw process tree", pm4py.save_vis_process_tree, tree,
            str(out / "04_candidate_tree.svg"))
    bpmn_from_tree = attempt("tree to BPMN", make_bpmn, tree)
    if bpmn_from_tree is not None:
        bpmns["05_via_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
    # Isolate losses at the tree step from losses in its BPMN export.
    tree_net = attempt("tree to audit net", accepting, tree)
    if tree_net is not None:
        nets["04_tree_audit"] = tree_net

for route, bpmn in bpmns.items():
    attempt("draw " + route, pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, bpmn,
            str(out / f"{route}_bpmn.svg"))
    back = attempt(route + " BPMN to accepting net", accepting, bpmn)
    if back is not None:
        nets[route + "_roundtrip"] = back

def sound_flag(net, im, fm):
    result = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
    if isinstance(result, tuple):
        ok, detail = result
        print("soundness detail", detail)
    else:
        ok = result
    if not isinstance(ok, bool):
        raise TypeError(f"Unrecognized soundness flag: {ok!r}")
    return ok

diagnostics = {}
for tag, (net, im, fm) in nets.items():
    print(tag)
    workflow = attempt(tag + " workflow net", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net, net)
    sound = attempt(tag + " soundness", sound_flag, net, im, fm)
    sources = [p for p in net.places if not p.in_arcs]
    sinks = [p for p in net.places if not p.out_arcs]
    boundary_ok = (len(sources) == len(sinks) == 1
                   and dict(im) == {sources[0]: 1}
                   and dict(fm) == {sinks[0]: 1})
    diagnostics[tag] = (workflow, sound, boundary_ok)
    print("workflow, sound, one-token case boundaries:", diagnostics[tag])
    print("visible label counts:", Counter(t.label for t in net.transitions
                                           if t.label is not None))
    attempt("draw " + tag, pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, net, im, fm,
            str(out / f"{tag}_net.svg"))
```

A workflow-net result concerns the topology. Soundness concerns executions with the supplied markings, including the possibility of completing properly. Neither establishes that this particular change process was preserved. The boundary check is the one-case workflow convention expected here. An alternate marking convention needs its own justification. A drawing error is logged separately from a conversion error.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

These are pre-execution assessments. The BPMN column describes the primary marked-net export. The round-trip column covers the separately tested BPMN routes. A missing result earns `conversion failed` at the failing stage and `not tested` downstream. Use `validated preserved` only with an explicit evidence scope, such as completed words through 14 visible events.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1, I4 — registration and both verification prerequisites | expected preserved | expected preserved | not tested | not tested | Check both orders and both one-prerequisite counterexamples, including executable prefixes ending at an early `C`. |
| I2, I5 — unordered pairs and absence of a legal/verification edge | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Need the legal-before-evidence and legal-inside-body words. Joint enabling distinguishes local independence from an arbitrary choice of serial schedules. |
| I3 — legal choice and silent skip | expected preserved | expected preserved | not tested | not tested | Confirm accepting paths with zero and one `L`. Inspect bypass routing and token arrival at the release join. The number and names of silent transitions can change. |
| I6, I7 — mandatory first body and binary redo | expected preserved | expected preserved | not tested | not tested | Check exit after the first inspection and repeated `DCI` suffixes. A back edge to inspection alone or an exit immediately after correction changes the language. |
| I8 — release waits for both completed children | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Test an executable early release even if its path later deadlocks. Trace the legal-taken, legal-skip and loop-exit flows into the synchronization. |
| I9 — archive and accepting boundaries | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | BPMN uses start/end events and flow semantics. Inspect each derived net's initial/final token distributions and use exact equality at completion. Place identity has meaning only within its own net. |
| Identity of the nine visible activities | expected preserved | expected preserved | not tested | not tested | Compare exact label spellings, multiplicities and node mappings. Loop repetitions revisit source activities. Duplicate transitions with the same label in a derivative can hide choices in a word comparison. |
| Explicit POWL child boundaries and their interpretation in the diagram | approximated | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Firing rules can realize the child boundaries even when the net flattens the POWL hierarchy. Recovering the identity of a child from places, gateways or routing-node counts requires a mapping. |

A visible word orders the recorded events even when both were independently enabled. A stronger local test is whether two transitions can consume their combined input tokens at the same reachable marking. Section 5 checks selected contexts in the ordinary converted nets. To validate the actual release join, follow token production and consumption through silent routing as well. A legal skip followed by verification can have the same visible word as a faulty implementation that omitted the legal dependency entirely.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The independent oracle includes **all 45 completed source words of length at most 14**. These cover one, two and three iterations, including every placement of legal. The shortest four-iteration completion has length 16. The search uses a visible-length bound only, so a malformed candidate can expose extra body firings within that same budget. Prefixes are tested separately because an illegal firing can lead to a deadlock without ever producing an illegal completed word.

```python
names = dict(R="Register Change", A="Assess Risk", E="Prepare Evidence",
             L="Legal Review", C="Configure Trial", I="Inspect Trial",
             D="Correct Defect", U="Release Change", Z="Archive Change")
def word(code):
    return tuple(names[c] for c in code)

LIMIT = 14
def source_words(n):
    for order in ("AE", "EA"):
        head = "R" + order + "CI" + "DCI" * (n - 1)
        yield word(head + "UZ")
        for pos in range(head.index("A") + 1, len(head) + 1):
            yield word(head[:pos] + "L" + head[pos:] + "UZ")

expected, expected_prefixes = set(), set()
# A short prefix may extend only to completions LONGER than LIMIT.
# LIMIT+1 iterations suffice to extend any prefix of length <= LIMIT.
for n in range(1, LIMIT + 2):
    for w in source_words(n):
        if len(w) <= LIMIT:
            expected.add(w)
        expected_prefixes.update(w[:i] for i in range(min(LIMIT, len(w)) + 1))
assert len(expected) == 45

positive = ("RAECIUZ", "REACIUZ", "RALECIUZ", "RAELCIUZ", "RAECLIUZ",
            "RAECILUZ", "RAECIDCIUZ", "RAECIDCLIDCIUZ")
negative = ("ARECIUZ", "RAAECIUZ", "RELACIUZ", "RAELLCIUZ", "RACEIUZ",
            "RECAIUZ", "RAEUZ", "RAEICUZ", "RAECICIUZ", "RAECIDUZ",
            "RAECIDICUZ", "RAECUILZ", "RAECIULZ", "RAECIZU", "RAECIU",
            "RAECIUZZ")
assert all(word(s) in expected for s in positive)
assert all(word(s) not in expected for s in negative)

# Each probe asks for an existential joint step after this visible prefix
# (including any reachable silent routing at the same prefix).
probes = {word("R"): word("AE"), word("RA"): word("EL"),
          word("RAE"): word("LC")}
def joint_step(enabled, marking, pair):
    for t in enabled:
        if t.label != pair[0]:
            continue
        for u in enabled:
            if u.label != pair[1]:
                continue
            need = Counter()
            for arc in list(t.in_arcs) + list(u.in_arcs):
                need[arc.source] += arc.weight
            if all(marking[p] >= count for p, count in need.items()):
                return True
    return False

def explore(net, im, fm, limit, cap=250_000, seconds=60):
    queue = deque([(im, ())])
    seen = {(frozenset(im.items()), ())}
    complete, prefixes, steps = set(), set(), set()
    bad_deadlock, final_exit, residual = None, None, None
    deadline = monotonic() + seconds
    while queue:
        if monotonic() > deadline:
            raise RuntimeError("Incomplete exploration: time limit")
        m, w = queue.popleft()
        prefixes.add(w)
        enabled = tuple(pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m))
        if m == fm:
            complete.add(w)
            if enabled and final_exit is None:
                final_exit = w
        else:
            if not enabled and bad_deadlock is None:
                bad_deadlock = w
            if residual is None and fm and all(m[p] >= k for p, k in fm.items()):
                residual = (w, repr(m))

        if w in probes and w not in steps and joint_step(enabled, m, probes[w]):
            steps.add(w)
        for t in enabled:
            if t.label is None:
                v = w
            elif len(w) < limit:
                v = w + (t.label,)
            else:
                continue
            nxt = semantics.execute(t, net, m)
            if nxt is None:
                raise RuntimeError("Enabled transition failed to fire")
            key = (frozenset(nxt.items()), v)
            if key in seen:
                continue
            if len(seen) >= cap:
                raise RuntimeError("Incomplete exploration: state/prefix cap")
            seen.add(key)
            queue.append((nxt, v))
    return dict(complete=complete, prefixes=prefixes, steps=steps,
                bad_deadlock=bad_deadlock, final_exit=final_exit, residual=residual)

def compare(tag, want, got):
    missing, extra = want - got, got - want
    def shortest(words):
        return min(words, key=lambda w: (len(w), repr(w)), default=None)
    print(tag, "missing", len(missing), shortest(missing),
          "extra", len(extra), shortest(extra))
    return not missing and not extra

reports = {}
for tag, triple in nets.items():
    labels = Counter(t.label for t in triple[0].transitions if t.label is not None)
    wanted = Counter(names.values())
    print(tag, "missing labels", wanted - labels, "extra label occurrences",
          labels - wanted)
    r = attempt(tag + " bounded search", explore, *triple, LIMIT)
    if r is None:
        print(tag, "bounded verdict unavailable")
        continue
    reports[tag] = r
    compare(tag + " complete vs source", expected, r["complete"])
    compare(tag + " prefixes vs source", expected_prefixes, r["prefixes"])
    compare(tag + " joint-step contexts vs source", set(probes), r["steps"])
    print(tag, "nonfinal deadlock prefix", r["bad_deadlock"],
          "enabled exit at exact final", r["final_exit"],
          "final tokens plus residue", r["residual"])
    print(tag, "missing positive witnesses",
          [s for s in positive if word(s) not in r["complete"]],
          "admitted negative witnesses",
          [s for s in negative if word(s) in r["complete"]])

base_tag = "02_from_powl"
for tag in nets.keys() - {base_tag}:
    if tag not in reports or base_tag not in reports:
        print(tag, "pair comparison unavailable")
        continue
    for field in ("complete", "prefixes", "steps"):
        compare(f"{base_tag} vs {tag} {field}",
                reports[base_tag][field], reports[tag][field])

# Secondary diagnostics on sound, single-case workflow nets. Reachability
# construction and some footprint algorithms can be expensive on other nets.
def supplemental(tag, triple):
    net, im, fm = triple
    fp = attempt(tag + " footprints", lambda: pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm))
    rg = attempt(tag + " reachability",
                 lambda: pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm))
    if rg is not None:
        print(tag, "reachability representation", type(rg).__name__)
        if hasattr(rg, "states") and hasattr(rg, "transitions"):
            print(tag, "states", len(rg.states), "edges", len(rg.transitions))
    def sample():
        log = pm4py.convert_to_event_log(pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm))
        return [tuple(e["concept:name"] for e in trace) for trace in list(log)[:3]]
    print(tag, "sampled words", attempt(tag + " playout", sample))
    return fp

fps = {}
for tag, triple in nets.items():
    if diagnostics[tag] == (True, True, True):
        fps[tag] = supplemental(tag, triple)
    else:
        print(tag, "supplemental checks skipped after diagnostics")

for tag, fp in fps.items():
    ref = fps.get(base_tag)
    if tag != base_tag and isinstance(ref, dict) and isinstance(fp, dict):
        for rel in ("sequence", "parallel"):
            if rel in ref and rel in fp:
                compare(f"{tag} footprint {rel}", set(ref[rel]), set(fp[rel]))
print("stage errors", errors)
```

For ordinary place/transition nets, future firing depends on the marking. Keeping the pair *(marking, visible word)* preserves distinct histories that reach the same marking, while merging silent cycles that return to the same pair. Tau successors are explored even at length 14, including silent paths to the exact final marking. An unbounded sequence of distinct silent markings hits a resource limit and yields an inconclusive search. When the queue drains, the comparison is exhaustive for visible prefixes and exact-final completed words through the bound, with existential semantics over silent paths. The joint-step tests cover only the listed contexts. If a probe fails, inspect whether routing moved a choice or serialized work, and require further concurrency evidence.

Compare each candidate to both the oracle and the source net. Agreement between two translations could otherwise mask a defect in their common first conversion. Footprints summarize relations between labels. Reachability graphs can acquire extra silent states, and graph sizes alone do not establish equivalence. Playout supplies examples whose termination may depend on the sampler. Only the marking-based test above certifies that a reported completed word actually reached the designated final marking in that net.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

- **Parallel gateways.** Trace the incoming flows at the start of verification. It needs risk and evidence. Legal needs risk alone. A single horizontal bar or a symmetric fork in the drawing can suggest that evidence also gates legal. Annotate the two entry conditions and the release condition beside their respective gateways.
- **Exclusive gateways.** Mark the legal alternatives “perform legal review” and “skip legal review.” Show where the alternatives merge before the legal branch contributes to the release synchronization. An XOR merge accepts the chosen alternative. An AND join across those two alternatives could wait for a token that never comes.
- **Loop.** Mark “first pass required” at configure, “exit or redo” after inspect, and “correct → configure” on the return path. A looping arrow can look like permission to repeat inspection alone. Keep legal outside the loop boundary even if its lane runs beside the return path.
- **Silent routing.** Give routing gateways stable identifiers and document which implement choice, bypass, loop exit and synchronization. Use a legend identifying unlabeled routing as control flow. Label the visible work with the nine source activity names.
- **Layout.** Vertical alignment, left-to-right placement, crossing connectors and swimlanes do not establish execution order or elapsed time. A short note with `RALECIUZ` and `RAECLIUZ` documents two cases a reader might incorrectly rule out. Keep a mapping from POWL children to the relevant BPMN task and gateway IDs alongside the drawing.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

1. Quarantine the affected route if a converter raises, returns no object, or supplies a net with a missing marking. Retain the stage, input version and exception. A tree failure leaves the direct-net route available for its own tests.
2. Reject a single-case workflow derivative with failed workflow-net or soundness diagnostics, an unexplained initial/final token distribution, a reachable nonfinal deadlock, or further firing enabled from its intended terminal state. A diagnostic exception leaves the verdict open.
3. Reject any missing required source word, including one risk/evidence order, legal before evidence, or legal between configure and inspect. Quarantine a claimed concurrency-preserving derivative if it provides only a choice among serial schedules or lacks evidence of the required independence.
4. Reject loss of either legal alternative, a mandatory visible “skip” task, a second `L`, or legal before risk.
5. Reject verification starting before either prerequisite, any path that skips the first body, or a projection with missing, extra or misplaced correction.
6. Reject an executable prefix with premature release, even if that prefix cannot be completed. Reject a release join that can fire with a required branch outstanding or that deadlocks when legal is skipped.
7. Reject a model that treats a matching visible ending or a marked sink as sufficient completion while residual tokens remain. Reject missing archive, archive before release or visible work after archive.
8. Quarantine missing, renamed or duplicate visible labels until the event mapping is reconciled. Quarantine incomplete searches and inconclusive routing evidence. A rendering failure also blocks publication of that figure.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

Store the versioned POWL definition as authoritative, including stable activity identities, the partial-order edges and the ordered body/redo children. Keep the invariant table and the source-language oracle with it. Use the primary BPMN for stakeholder visualization after its audit passes. The accepting nets with their markings, BPMN files, optional tree and rendered figures are generated derivatives.

For each released derivative retain its source revision and artifact identity, PM4Py version, exact conversion route and parameters, label/node mapping, workflow-net and soundness results for its associated net, and the actual initial/final markings. Attach the bound, completed-search status, word and prefix differences or counterexamples, concurrency probes, and review of the skip, loop and release routing. Bind each figure to the audited model it depicts. Distinguish a failed stage, a skipped check and an observed mismatch in that record.

Allow the tree as an optional audited export. Require evidence for the tree's accepting-net interpretation as well as its BPMN round trip. Do not serialize a formerly independent pair simply to obtain a tree. Make future semantic edits in POWL, review the affected invariants, regenerate derivatives and rerun the audit. Bring a stakeholder's proposed BPMN change back as an explicit POWL change for review.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

- Equality through length 14 leaves behavior at greater loop depths open. Even a sound implementation can make a different choice after the tested iterations.
- Equal visible languages and the three local step probes leave global causal equivalence, conflict structure, branching equivalence and silent divergence unresolved. Different hidden decisions can produce exactly the same visible words.
- A BPMN round trip exercises PM4Py's interpretation in both directions. It does not independently certify the BPMN token semantics in another execution engine or catch every pair of compensating translator errors.
- A trace records activity labels. It cannot establish wall-clock overlap, task duration, resource availability, or business conditions for requiring legal review or correcting a defect. The source specifies control flow.
- The proposed code has no run results attached here. Whether this PM4Py installation reduces this particular net to a tree, and whether either generated BPMN passes the audit, remain empirical questions.
