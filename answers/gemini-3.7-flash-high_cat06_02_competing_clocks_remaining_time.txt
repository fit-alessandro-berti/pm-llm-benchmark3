## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

- **Friday 18:00** ($t = 2.5$ h): Chemistry lab closes.
- **Friday 20:00** ($t = 4.5$ h): QA closes (1 reviewer worked 4.5 h).
- **Saturday 09:00–13:00** ($t = 17.5$ to $21.5$ h): QA open (1 reviewer works 4.0 h).

- **Monday 08:00** ($t = 64.5$ h): Chemistry lab opens (2 analysts), Microbiology routine staffing opens, QA opens (2 reviewers).

### Scenario 1: Smooth Path (Clean Parallel Tests $\rightarrow$ Routine QA $\rightarrow$ Release)

1. **Chemistry Branch:**
   - *Friday 15:30–18:00 (2.5 h capacity):* Analyst 2 finishes ongoing work at ~16:10 (0.4–0.9 h rem), Analyst 1 at ~17:10 (1.2–2.0 h rem).
   - *Under Risk-Priority (45%):* Urgent sample starts on Analyst 2 at ~16:10, working until 18:00 pause. B-417 starts on Analyst 1 at ~17:10, completing ~0.8 h before 18:00 pause. On Monday 08:00, B-417 resumes with ~1.7 h remaining (mode 2.5 h total), finishing **Monday 09:30–11:30** ($t = 66.0$–$68.0$ h).
   - *Under FIFO (55%):* 4 samples ahead total ~11.3 analyst-hours. After Friday’s 2.6 analyst-hours of queue work, ~8.7 analyst-hours remain across 2 analysts on Monday (~4.3 h queue wait). B-417 starts ~12:20 Monday, finishing **Monday 14:30–17:00** ($t = 71.0$–$73.5$ h).
2. **Microbiology Branch:**
   - Primary incubation completes Sunday 12:00–20:00 ($t = 44.5$–$52.5$ h).
   - *If weekend on-call authorized (25–45%):* Readout completed Sunday 14:00–21:00 ($t = 46.5$–$53.5$ h).
   - *If routine Monday staff (55–75%):* Readout completed **Monday 08:30–09:30** ($t = 65.0$–$66.0$ h).
3. **Parallel Join ($\max(\text{Chem}, \text{Micro})$):**
   - Even if Microbiology finishes Sunday ($t \approx 48$ h) or Monday morning ($t \approx 65.5$ h), QA review cannot start until Chemistry is ready.
   - Join Time $= \max(\text{Chem}, \text{Micro}) = \text{Chemistry completion} = \mathbf{\text{Monday 09:30–11:30}}$ (Risk-Priority) or $\mathbf{\text{Monday 14:30–17:00}}$ (FIFO). Chemistry is the dominant clock.
4. **QA Waiting & Service:**
   - Over the weekend, 1 reviewer provided 8.5 reviewer-hours, clearing ~4 batches while ~3 arrived. At Monday 08:00, 2 reviewers clear backlog at net rate ~0.55 batches/h.
   - When B-417 arrives on Monday, QA queue wait is ~0.5–2.0 h. QA review duration is median 1.5 h (mean ~2.2 h).
   - QA completes **Monday 13:00–16:00** (Risk-Priority) or **Monday 17:30–19:30** (FIFO). If FIFO finishes near 17:00 and QA queue has an 80th-percentile tail, QA review pauses at 20:00 and completes **Tuesday 08:30–09:30**.
5. **Final Step:**
   - Automated Electronic Release (0.25–0.75 h) $\rightarrow$ **Terminal Completion: Monday 21 Sep 15:00 – 19:30** (central), with earliest at **Monday 13:00** and FIFO/QA-spillover tail at **Tuesday 22 Sep 09:30**.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Laboratory Weekend Calendars vs. Continuous Incubation (Calendar Uncertainty — Rank 1):**
   - *Reasoning:* While automated microbiology runs continuously across the 62-hour weekend gap, routine chemistry and QA close for ~60+ hours. This creates an enforced batching barrier at Monday 08:00, preventing any intermediate release between Friday 20:00 and Monday 11:30 regardless of assay speed.
2. **Investigation Scope: Doc-Only vs. Laboratory Investigation (Path Uncertainty — Rank 2):**
   - *Reasoning:* Dictates the extreme bifurcation of the right tail. A documentation-only deviation adds 8–20 working hours (resolving by Wednesday), whereas a laboratory re-testing investigation adds 3–7 calendar days (72–168 h), pushing the forecast into the following week.
3. **Chemistry Queue Discipline & Prioritization (Queue Uncertainty — Rank 3):**
   - *Reasoning:* Whether the chemistry lab applies Risk-Priority (B-417 is 2nd) or FIFO (B-417 is 5th behind 4 samples) swings Chemistry completion by 5.0–6.0 working hours on Monday (09:30 vs. 15:30), directly determining whether the batch can be released on Monday evening before QA closes at 20:00.
4. **Upstream Temperature-Excursion Status (Dependence / Observation Uncertainty — Rank 4):**
   - *Reasoning:* Represents a latent common cause. If confirmed (P = 0.20–0.40), it simultaneously triples the risk of chemistry reruns, doubles micro contamination risk, and multiplies deviation probability five-fold (to 30–55%), shifting probability mass directly from the P50 band into the P80/P90 tail.
5. **Microbiology Secondary Confirmation Requirement (Path Uncertainty — Rank 5):**
   - *Reasoning:* A primary contamination indication (5–12% baseline) overrides chemistry as the pacing clock and immediately injects an unavoidable 20–30 h continuous incubation plus next-day staffing readout, guaranteeing delay to at least Tuesday afternoon and introducing substantial batch rejection risk.
