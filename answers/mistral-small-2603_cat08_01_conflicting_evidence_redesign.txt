# Warranty Process Redesign Analysis

## 1. Current-process diagnosis

**Core Problems Identified:**
1. **Fast-path quality paradox**: The most frequent path (Auto eligibility  Fast approval  Dispatch replacement) is attractive on speed (median 8-28 hours) and cost (EUR 0.20 + EUR 34 for auto screen) but generates 14.1% complaint/reopen rates versus 3.3% for repair paths. This contradicts the assumption that faster paths are inherently better (Fast approval cases are 31% cheaper before rework but only 9% cheaper after accounting for downstream costs).

2. **Compliance violations**: Token-based replay shows 32% of traces violate normative rules (R1: EUR >400 claims skipping manual/fraud review; R2: replacement before evidence completeness; R3: reopened claims bypassing manual eligibility). The log fitness of 0.91 masks that only 68% of traces fully conform.

3. **Resource bottlenecks**: Manual eligibility review queue grows when utilization exceeds 82%, with median cycle times of 5-13 hours for Premium cases vs. 10-16 hours for Standard. Fraud/risk review is a Monday bottleneck after marketing campaigns.

4. **Rework amplification**: 26% of cases entering "Request missing evidence" require it twice, and 12.5% of high-value repairs require rework (vs. 4% for low-risk devices). Wrong replacement selection (EUR 95-410 cost) drives 96-180 hour complaint/reopen cycles.

5. **Segment misalignment**: Premium customers (18% of cases) have 22% complaint rates on the fast path vs. 5% on repair paths, yet their service-credit exposure is high if cycle time exceeds 24 hours. The auto-screen rules are outdated and not segment-aware.

**Key Artifacts Referenced:**
- DFG shows Fast approval  Customer complaint/reopen in 140 cases (mean 72 hours) vs. Repair quote path at 80 cases (mean 30 hours)
- Performance DFG: Manual eligibility review  Repair quote has p90 60 hours (Premium cases)
- Conformance findings: 32% policy violations despite 0.91 log fitness
- Rework statistics: Fast approval cases have 14.1% complaint rate vs. 3.3% for repair paths
- Resource data: Manual review queue pressure at >82% utilization

## 2. Redesign A: Segment-Aware Fast-Track with Pre-Approval Gates

**Target:** Premium low-risk accessories (18% of cases) and Standard low-risk accessories (25%) where speed is critical but compliance risk is low.

**Redesigned Flow:**
1. **Intake claim**  **Auto eligibility screen** (updated rules)
   - New rules: Auto-approve Premium low-risk accessories if defect type is accessory and claim < EUR 200
   - Standard low-risk accessories auto-approved if claim < EUR 100
2. **Auto eligibility screen**  **Pre-dispatch quality gate** (new activity)
   - Lightweight check (6-hour median) using AI to verify part compatibility
   - Only for auto-approved claims
3. **Pre-dispatch quality gate**  **Dispatch replacement** (if passed)
4. **Pre-dispatch quality gate**  **Manual eligibility review** (if failed)
5. **Manual eligibility review**  **Evidence completion** (if needed)  **Technical decision**

**Mechanism of Improvement:**
- Reduces manual review queue pressure by auto-approving 43% of cases (Premium + Standard low-risk accessories)
- Pre-dispatch gate catches 30-45% of wrong replacement selections (based on evidence portal potential)
- Maintains compliance by routing failed gates to manual review (addressing R2 violations)

**Sacrifices/Unresolved:**
- Leaves Standard high-value devices (21%) and Premium high-value devices (14%) on slower paths
- Pre-dispatch gate adds 6-18 hours for auto-approved cases (but still faster than manual review)
- Digital literacy issues may increase abandonment for some customers

## 3. Redesign B: Risk-Based Parallel Processing

**Target:** All high-value device claims (Premium 14% + Standard 21% = 35% of cases) and unclear defect/missing evidence cases (22%).

**Redesigned Flow:**
1. **Intake claim**  **Parallel eligibility & evidence collection**
   - Auto screen runs in parallel with evidence portal invitation
   - Evidence portal uses dynamic questionnaires based on defect type
2. **Parallel paths:**
   - **Fast approval path** (if auto screen passes AND evidence is complete)
   - **Risk review path** (if auto screen flags risk OR evidence incomplete)
3. **Risk review path:**
   - Fraud/risk review + Manual eligibility review run concurrently
   - Evidence completion continues in background
4. **Technical decision**  **Fulfillment/repair** with segment-aware SLA:
   - Premium high-value: 48-hour SLA for repair quote
   - Standard high-value: 72-hour SLA for repair quote
5. **Quality/compliance close** with mandatory post-fulfillment audit for high-value devices

**Mechanism of Improvement:**
- Reduces cycle time for high-value devices by 30-50% (parallelizing eligibility and evidence)
- Catches R1 violations (EUR >400 claims) by forcing concurrent risk review
- Dynamic evidence portal reduces repeated requests (target 30-45% reduction)
- Post-fulfillment audit catches wrong replacement selections

**Sacrifices/Unresolved:**
- Increases resource pressure on fraud/risk reviewers (2 specialists) and manual reviewers
- Evidence portal may increase abandonment for low-digital-literacy customers
- Adds complexity to case management (parallel paths require coordination)

## 4. Redesign C: Complaint-Driven Escalation Prevention

**Target:** All cases with complaint/reopen risk (especially Fast approval path cases with 14.1% complaint rate).

**Redesigned Flow:**
1. **Intake claim**  **Auto eligibility screen** (with risk scoring)
   - Risk scoring uses defect type, customer segment, and historical complaint data
   - High-risk cases (e.g., Premium high-value devices with defect type "screen") routed to manual review
2. **Eligibility assessment**  **Predictive quality gate** (new activity)
   - AI model predicts likelihood of wrong replacement selection
   - High-risk cases routed to manual eligibility review
3. **Technical decision**  **Fulfillment with mandatory quality check**
   - All high-value device replacements require pre-dispatch quality check
   - Low-risk accessories skip quality check
4. **Customer experience layer:**
   - Real-time status updates with ETA for all Premium customers
   - Proactive replacement part verification for high-risk cases
5. **Post-fulfillment monitoring:**
   - Automated complaint detection within 24 hours of fulfillment
   - Immediate escalation for high-risk cases

**Mechanism of Improvement:**
- Reduces complaint/reopen rates by 40-60% (targeting the 14.1% Fast approval rate)
- Predictive quality gate catches wrong replacement selections before dispatch
- Real-time status updates improve customer experience for Premium customers
- Proactive monitoring reduces escalation board load

**Sacrifices/Unresolved:**
- Increases cycle time for high-risk cases (added quality gate)
- AI model training requires historical data and may have false positives
- Adds complexity to case management (predictive routing)

## 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

**Redesign A:**
- **Failure modes:**
  1. Pre-dispatch quality gate may miss some wrong replacement selections, leading to downstream complaints
  2. Auto-approval rules may approve ineligible claims if defect type classification is wrong
- **Displaced bottleneck:** Manual eligibility review queue pressure decreases, but pre-dispatch gate may create a new queue if utilization exceeds 85%

**Redesign B:**
- **Failure modes:**
  1. Parallel evidence collection may overwhelm customers with complex questionnaires
  2. Concurrent fraud/risk review may delay high-priority cases if specialists are unavailable
- **Displaced bottleneck:** Fraud/risk review specialists become the new bottleneck, especially on Mondays

**Redesign C:**
- **Failure modes:**
  1. Predictive quality gate may have false positives, routing low-risk cases to manual review unnecessarily
  2. Real-time status updates may increase customer expectations beyond what can be delivered
- **Displaced bottleneck:** Manual eligibility review queue may increase if predictive routing is too aggressive

## 7. Recommended experiment sequence

**Phase 1: Pilot Redesign A (Segment-Aware Fast-Track)**
- **Pilot group:** Premium low-risk accessories (18% of cases)
- **Metrics to monitor:**
  - Cycle time (target: <24 hours for 90% of cases)
  - Complaint/reopen rate (target: <5%)
  - Manual eligibility review queue length (target: utilization <80%)
  - Abandonment rate (target: <5%)
- **Stop/go thresholds:**
  - Stop if complaint/reopen rate >8% or abandonment rate >7%
  - Go if cycle time <20 hours and complaint/reopen rate <5%
- **Learning objective:** Validate that segment-aware auto-approval doesn't increase compliance violations

**Phase 2: Scale Redesign A to Standard low-risk accessories**
- **Expand pilot to Standard low-risk accessories (25% of cases)**
- **Metrics to monitor:**
  - Same as Phase 1, plus service-credit exposure (target: <2% of cases)
- **Stop/go thresholds:**
  - Stop if service-credit exposure >3% or cycle time >72 hours
  - Go if complaint/reopen rate <6% and abandonment rate <6%

**Phase 3: Pilot Redesign B (Risk-Based Parallel Processing)**
- **Pilot group:** Premium high-value devices (14% of cases)
- **Metrics to monitor:**
  - Cycle time for repair quote (target: <48 hours for 90% of cases)
  - Compliance violations (target: <5%)
  - Fraud/risk reviewer utilization (target: <85%)
- **Stop/go thresholds:**
  - Stop if fraud/risk reviewer utilization >90% or compliance violations >8%
  - Go if cycle time <40 hours and compliance violations <5%

**Phase 4: Scale Redesign B to Standard high-value devices**
- **Expand pilot to Standard high-value devices (21% of cases)**
- **Metrics to monitor:**
  - Same as Phase 3, plus cost per case (target: <EUR 150)
- **Stop/go thresholds:**
  - Stop if cost per case >EUR 160 or cycle time >96 hours
  - Go if cost per case <EUR 140 and cycle time <80 hours

**Phase 5: Pilot Redesign C (Complaint-Driven Escalation Prevention)**
- **Pilot group:** All cases with Fast approval path (3600 cases)
- **Metrics to monitor:**
  - Complaint/reopen rate (target: <8%)
  - Cycle time (target: <36 hours for 90% of cases)
  - Predictive quality gate accuracy (target: >85%)
- **Stop/go thresholds:**
  - Stop if cycle time >48 hours or complaint/reopen rate >10%
  - Go if complaint/reopen rate <6% and cycle time <30 hours

**Phase 6: Full implementation decision**
- **Criteria for full implementation:**
  - At least two redesigns show statistically significant improvements in complaint/reopen rates and cycle time
  - No redesign shows >10% increase in resource pressure or compliance violations
  - ROI analysis (based on reduced rework and service credits) supports implementation
- **Learning objective:** Determine whether improvements are real or shifted downstream (e.g., from Fast approval to Repair quote path)
