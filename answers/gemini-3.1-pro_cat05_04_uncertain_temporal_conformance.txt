## 1. Normalization Assumptions
- **Interval Construction**: `true_time = recorded_time_resolved - clock_offset`. If resolution is `[r1, r2]` and offset is `[o1, o2]`, the true time interval is `[r1 - o2, r2 - o1]`.
- **Clock-Offset Direction**: Defined as `recorded_clock - true_clock`. Subtracting the offset bounds from the resolved time yields true time.
- **Rounding**:
  - Exact to displayed second: `[T - 0.5s, T + 0.5s]`
  - Floor to minute: `[T, T + 59.999s]`
  - Round to nearest 10s: `[T - 5s, T + 4.999s]`
  - Round to nearest minute: `[T - 30s, T + 29.999s]`
- **Time-Zone Conversion**: Local times are converted to UTC before applying offsets.
- **DST Folds**: For LEGACY-BER on 2025-10-26, 02:00-02:59 maps to both UTC+2 (CEST) and UTC+1 (CET).
- **Inferred Starts**: For T-404, `Investigation_Start` is missing and inferred as `Investigation_Complete` minus the duration interval `[18m, 25m]`.
- **Causal Constraints**: For T-405, the ingestion sequence (Alert < Start < Complete) logically eliminates all DST combinations except Alert in CEST, Start in CET, and Complete in CET. For T-407, `Alert_Received` must occur on or after `Alert_Sent` and on or before `Investigation_Start`.
- **Mobile Entry Delay**: For T-406, the user entry delay of up to 5 minutes implies the actual event occurred up to 5 minutes *before* the recorded time. This shifts the estimate of occurrence time earlier, adding `[0, 300s]` to the past.
