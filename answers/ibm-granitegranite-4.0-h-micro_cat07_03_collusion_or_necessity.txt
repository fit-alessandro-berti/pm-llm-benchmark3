1. **Emergency coordination**: In high-priority cases (Buyer_Carla, Buyer_Dan), the same approver may be used to speed up decision-making during urgent vendor negotiations. Evidence: timestamps align with emergency windows; no other approvers present.
2. **Role design**: Roles for Buyer_Carla and Buyer_Dan include emergency handling; the policy allows same-person sign-off for emergency overrides. Evidence: emergency_flag=True for all cases; no Retrospective Justification required.

## Unusually fast approvals
1. **Batch processing**: Some approvals (e.g., VendorMaster_LenaAP_Kim) occurred during quarter-end batch uploads. Evidence: source_system="quarter_close_upload" + identical timestamps.
2. **Automation**: Bot_PO and Bot_Match often create PO and match invoices automatically, with human approval shortly thereafter. Evidence: high volume of Bot-generated PO events; human approval within 6 minutes.

## Repeated subcontracting loops
1. **Clarification loops**: Approver_Gita may act as a liaison between Buyer_Carla and vendor specialists. Evidence: frequent timestamps, same approver, emergency_flag=False for majority.
2. **Managerial oversight**: Manager_Hugo’s loops may be part of standard audit trail for high-value cases. Evidence: Manager_Hugo’s role as emergency override authorizer; no malicious intent indicated.

## Vendor concentration
1. **Strategic partnership**: VendorMaster_Lena and VendorMaster_Marc may represent a preferred supplier network optimized for cost and speed. Evidence: high volume of transactions with these vendors, low price variance.
2. **Data migration**: Recent banking platform changes may have caused temporary manual uploads. Evidence: timestamp correlation with platform change dates.

## Manual source-system concentration
1. **Transition period**: The ERP migration resulted in a temporary shift to manual uploads for critical transactions. Evidence: timestamps align with migration schedule; source_system="manual_upload" dominates.
2. **Operational fallback**: Manual systems were used to ensure business continuity during ERP issues. Evidence: no errors in transactions; manual entries corrected post-upload.

## Shared-account or mapping problems
1. **Data entry error**: The shared "Approver_Gita" account may be a data entry error rather than actual collusion. Evidence: identical "org:resource" for Retrospective Justifications; audit trail shows separate entered_by values.
2. **Legacy ERP mapping**: In legacy_erp source_system, the bot and approver mappings were incorrect, causing apparent collusion. Evidence: source_system="legacy_erp" events show bot and human resource mismatch.
