# 2. Dependency and prerequisite map
**Upstream data quality dependencies (garbage-in chain):**
A2 classification is the router. Errors (8%) misroute to wrong KYC path, wrong collateral path (71% Equipment vs 18% Working-capital need valuation), wrong committee cadence (weekly for high-exposure). All downstream — A3, A6, A7, A8, A9 — inherit this error. A1 without A2 accelerates misrouting: Receive 12000 -> Register 11880 -> Classify 11680 is near-total flow, so speeding Register only moves the queue to Classify/KYC/Financial review.
A5  A6  Credit analysis is a serial prerequisite chain in DFG: Financial review -> Missing docs 3300 -> back 2800 -> Normalize 5050 -> Credit 5000. Automating Normalize (A6) on incomplete document sets wastes analyst validation on low-quality PDFs (16% exception, poor performance noted). A8 pack auto-assembly is a pure join: DFG shows Wait valuation -> Pack 1980 converges with Credit -> Pack 2700 into Pack -> Decision 3900. If any input (KYC status, normalized statements, valuation, credit notes) is stale, A8 produces garbage-in/garbage-out and increases Packaging 15% rework and Decision rework.

**Concurrency misrecorded as sequence:**
System logs Classify -> KYC 7250 and Classify -> Financial 3940 as alternatives plus KYC -> Credit 4200, but practice is concurrent KYC + Financial review. Automation assuming strict sequence will mis-estimate waiting (4h + 18h not additive) and mis-design handoffs. Portfolio must preserve parallel execution and synchronize only at Committee packaging and Contract preparation, not force sequential KYC-then-analysis.

**Legal/compliance controls as hard gates:**
- KYC clearance must precede Customer signing. Provisional signing 6.5% with 1.1% remediation proves gate is bypassed under pressure. A10 (e-sign reminders) and A9 (contract generation) must enforce a system block, not a warning, when KYC != cleared. Resolve mismatch sits after KYC but before Contract prep; DFG Mismatch -> Contract 380 shows bypass path that must be closed unless KYC re-cleared (Mismatch -> KYC 1160 loop is the compliant path).
- Contract terms must match Committee decision. Loop Contract -> Remediate 650 -> Contract 510 (8.5%, 14% renewal) shows decision-data inconsistency. A9 must compare generated terms to decision record field-by-field and block signing on mismatch.
- Four-eyes: Credit analysis and Packaging must be different persons in High-exposure. 5.8% violation in month-end rush. Any automation that auto-assigns or suggests assignees must enforce SoD; A8/A12 must log actor IDs. Automation never removes compliance responsibility — human owner + override log required.
- Audit trail: every model suggestion (A2, A3, A6), human override, final decision must be immutable. This is prerequisite for A3/A2/A6 acceptance.

**Human escalation capacity:**
- Compliance specialists 3 FTE with handover concentration on 2 seniors: A3 pre-classifier reduces routine hits but false-positive tuning directly determines whether specialists drown. Without protected escalation SLA and threshold tuning, A3 shifts work rather than reduces it.
- Credit analysts 9 FTE central in working-together network: A5 increases clean inflow. If A6 does not simultaneously reduce 75-min normalization effort, analysts overload, Credit 28h wait + 110-min service worsens. Must pair inflow-increasing with capacity-releasing automation.
- Legal ops 4 FTE underutilized when KYC slow, overloaded month-end: faster upstream (A4/A6) without leveling will create month-end spikes in Contract prep 22h wait. Need WIP leveling + rule maintenance capacity.
- Relationship managers trigger missing-doc loops via incomplete instructions: A5 must redesign RM instructions, not just nudge customers, or RMs will resist rigid friction.

**External valuation dependency:**
Collateral request 2400 -> Wait 2350 (58h wait, 0 service, 19% late) is a synchronization delay, not labor. Internal automation cannot remove external time; only triage (waive/scope early via A2+A7), parallelize (request immediately after Credit analysis starts, not after it ends), and route to responsive valuers + track. Benefit is cycle-time variance reduction, not FTE saving. A7 alone does not fix pack synchronization if KYC/statements late.

**Core-banking integration constraints:**
Only two deep integrations in first six months. Deep candidates: A3 (screening/compliance hub), A6 (credit model/financial store), A9 (contract/decision store + core), A11 (activation posting). Light candidates: A1, A2 recommender (sidecar), A4 assistant (case UI), A5 portal (CRM/portal), A7 routing (workflow + email/API to valuers), A10 reminders, A12 workbench (overlay). Phasing must put at most two deep in H1, reserve others for H2 after gates.

# 6. Recommended phased rollout
**Principle:** max four modules year 1; max two deep integrations in H1; at least one control-strengthening module in first wave; no downstream bot before upstream gates; measure process-level, not just labor.

**Phase 0 — Foundations (Months 0-2, no deep integration):**
Deploy A5 (portal checklist + RM instruction redesign, light) + A2 sidecar recommender in shadow mode (suggests, human confirms). Start logging concurrency correctly (parallel KYCFinancial timestamps), baseline metrics: end-to-end cycle p50/p90 by product/exposure, waits per activity, rework rates (2800/3300, 1160/1750, 510/650), handover counts, analyst/specialist/legal utilization, control violations (four-eyes %, provisional %, remediation %), override rates.
*Gate to Phase 1:* A2 precision on High-exposure  agreed threshold + RM acceptance of checklists (drop-off not worsened); audit-log schema live.

**Phase 1 — Bottleneck relief + control gate (Months 3-6, up to two deep):**
Deep 1: A6 extraction/normalization integrated to credit store (with confidence + analyst override UI). Light: A4 mismatch assistant (case UI + playbook for 3 specialists). Deep capacity reserved; A9 design + rule-library build starts in background but not yet live as deep.
Pairing rationale: A5 (Phase 0) increases clean inflow; A6 prevents analyst overload; A4 unblocks KYC critical path. Monitor analyst queue (target: Credit wait 28h  without overtime surge), mismatch wait 31h , missing-doc repeat 52% .
*Gate to Phase 2:* analyst override rate stable/decreasing, extraction QA pass rate met, specialist queue not breached, no increase in provisional signings.

**Phase 2 — Enforce controls at scale (Months 7-9, second deep wave):**
Deep 2: A9 contract generation with control-rule checks (KYC-clearance token + terms-vs-decision match + SoD check) integrated to core/document store. A7 triage/parallel valuation request goes live (light workflow). A8 remains manual checklist aided by clean artifacts; A10 reminders piloted only with KYC gate enforced.
*Gate to Phase 3:* contract remediation 650 rate  (especially renewal 14%  target ), zero activations without KYC token, four-eyes violations  near zero with block log, rule-library ownership + monthly review cadence proven.

**Phase 3 — Harden + expand (Months 10-12):**
Operationalize A12 workbench overlay (or A3 pre-classifier tuned conservatively if specialist capacity allows) as the Compliance first-wave control evidence; decide on A3 vs A12 based on Phase 1-2 data. Defer A1 auto-registration scale-up and A11 activation bot to Year 2 — A1 only after downstream SLAs stable; A11 only after A9/A12 gates demonstrate sustained control reliability. A10 full rollout only after provisional-signing gate proven.
Ongoing monitoring: cycle p50/p90, wait per activity, rework loops via DFG, handover network density, resource utilization by pool/time-of-day, violation rates, model confidence/drift, customer friction. Monthly governance with Compliance veto on threshold changes.

# 7. Final recommendation
**Recommend a hybrid Bottleneck-plus-Control portfolio for Year 1: A5 + A6 + A4 + A9.**

This is Portfolio B amended with A9 replacing A7 (A7 follows in Phase 2 as light addition if capacity allows, or early Year 2). It fits four-module budget, uses exactly two deep integrations in sequence (A6 then A9), and satisfies Compliance with A9's blocking control checks plus A4's structured KYC resolution — controls are strengthened, not just labor cut.

Why this trade-off wins:
- It attacks where process mining shows time and risk concentrate, not where counts are highest: 31h mismatch block on 82% critical path, 52% repeated doc loops feeding 18h+20h waits and 75-min normalization, 110-min credit analysis queued 28h on 9 central analysts, and 650 contract remediations gating 7300 signings. Fixing these moves p90 cycle and rework; speeding Register (1.5h wait, idle back office) does not.
- It respects dependencies: A5 cleans input  A6 can trust extraction  credit notes + KYC clearance (via A4) feed A9 clean data  A9 can safely enforce terms-vs-decision and KYC-before-signing gates. No garbage-in automation, no downstream bot on unreliable controls (hence defer A11, gate A10).
- It accounts for shifted demand: A5 alone would overload analysts; paired with A6 it nets relief. A4 protects 3 specialists via structured escalation instead of A3's broad auto-triage which would flood them. A9 levels Legal month-end by blocking incomplete packs early rather than remediating later.
- It balances quick wins with prudence: A5/A4 show fast wait/rework drops; A6 shows analyst hours saved; A9 shows remediation and violation drops — enough political visibility without the illusion of A1.

**Why the highest-frequency activity (A1 auto-registration, 11880) should not be automated first:** frequency misleads. Registration is low-complexity, low-wait, already 72% digital, handled by a 12-FTE pool idle after 15:00 — not the constraint. Automating it first in isolation is locally efficient but systemically harmful: it accelerates misrouted cases (8% classify errors), swamps the true constraints (3 specialists, 9 analysts), exposes KYC/document bottlenecks, and — via faster bad-data contracts/signings — risks more provisional signings and remediation loops. Process-level thinking says: de-constrain and control the middle (docs, statements, mismatch, contract gate) before widening the front door, and never automate activation downstream until those gates are proven.
