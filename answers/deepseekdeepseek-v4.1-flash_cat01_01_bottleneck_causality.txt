    def make_event_log_string(sub):
        lines = ["case:concept:name,concept:name,time:timestamp"]
        for i, row in sub.reset_index(drop=True).iterrows():
            activities = [a.strip() for a in str(row["trace"]).split(">")]

                # Dummy timestamps only to satisfy the parser; not used for performance analysis.
                ts = (
                    pd.Timestamp("2020-01-01 00:00:00")
                    + pd.Timedelta(minutes=i, seconds=j)

            print(f"  start: {start_acts}")
            print(f"  end: {end_acts}")

## 8. Residual uncertainty
1. Unmeasured case risk and triage severity. Most valuable addition: a pre-routing risk score or blinded severity rating.
2. Completed-case selection and censoring. Most valuable addition: open, abandoned, and still-open cases with status and censoring times.
3. Queueing versus service and compliance waiting. Most valuable addition: activity-level timestamps for Classify, Compliance start/end, Review start/end, Request Info, and Close, plus queue lengths.
4. Whether `resource_group` is a treatment or a proxy/bundle for routing policy, skill, and capacity. Most valuable addition: routing engine logs with eligibility rules, overrides, assigned reviewer IDs, certification, and concurrent caseload.
5. Whether 30-day quality is a valid endpoint or post-process artifact. Most valuable addition: post-close appeal/adjudication timeline and reasons.
