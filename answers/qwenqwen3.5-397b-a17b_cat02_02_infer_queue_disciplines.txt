# 6. Additional event data needed

To resolve the ambiguities identified (specifically QB queue selection and QC deadline logic), the following data fields should be captured at the **Dispatch Decision Event**:

1.  **`decision_timestamp`**: Distinct from `service_start`. Allows measurement of decision latency vs. scan delay.
2.  **`eligible_job_set_snapshot`**: List of Job IDs waiting at the exact moment of decision. Crucial for verifying FIFO vs. Priority selection (e.g., was B06 truly visible at 10:28?).
3.  **`resource_state_code`**: Explicit state (Idle, Busy, Setup, Down, Calibration). Resolves the QA 08:30-08:58 gap.
4.  **`preemption_trigger_id`**: Link the preempted segment to the specific job/reason that caused it. Distinguishes "Urgent Arrival" from "Dispatcher Reassignment".
5.  **`policy_version_id`**: Rules may change over time. Knowing the active policy helps interpret hybrid behaviors.
6.  **`remaining_time_estimate`**: At decision time. Distinguishes SPT (based on original estimate) from SRPT (Shortest Remaining Processing Time).
7.  **`appointment_tolerance_window`**: Minutes early/late allowed. Explains A03 early arrival and B07 late start.
