*   **Emergency proportionality:** High-value Carla/Gita and Dan/Hugo cases retain emergency_flag=True with supporting clinical/facilities incident reports, off-hours occurrence, and independent justification; late-justification rate drops to routine ~1% after calendar correction and excluding batch artifact.
*   **Historical baseline normalizes pattern:** Prior quarters show similar vendor concentration for V-77x and similar manual_upload usage during platform migration, with no payment errors; working-together communities for Aline/Elena etc. replicate pattern, showing specialization is structural.

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Our review of procurement event logs for the last quarter identified control exceptions that require targeted follow-up, but we did not find direct evidence of misconduct and do not make determinations of fraud or collusion.

Frequent, rapid collaboration between buyers and approvers is largely consistent with informal specialization and legitimate emergency handling. High-volume pairs Buyer_AlineApprover_Elena and Buyer_BorisApprover_Felix exhibit expected routings and normal approval times. Pairs Buyer_CarlaApprover_Gita and Buyer_DanManager_Hugo handle predominantly emergency medical and facilities cases with appropriately short approval intervals; the observed segregation exceptions in this group are substantially explained by emergency procedures, though retrospective justifications were late in approximately 18–21% of cases compared with 1% baseline.

The most concerning subgroup is the VendorMaster_LenaAP_Kim cluster. This small group (44 cases) combines change of vendor bank details followed by rapid payment approval (median 17 minutes), high-value transactions, concentration in vendor family V-77x (72%), and heavy reliance on manual uploads outside automated controls (83%). The linkage to recently authorized mass-maintenance for that vendor family could not be validated from the log alone.

Assessment is limited by data-quality issues, including shared approval accounts, bot-to-human mapping errors, and batch migration of justifications that obscure true timing and attribution. We recommend immediate substantive testing of the V-77x bank-change-to-payment chain, reconstruction of attribution using entered-by and access logs, and process remediation for manual-upload and retrospective-justification controls.
