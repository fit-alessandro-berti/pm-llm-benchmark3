## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model

To evaluate missing relations in this OCEL 2.0 dataset without inventing certainty where the log is ambiguous, we classify domain semantics and data signals into four tiers:

### Hard Invariants (Must never be violated)
* **Referential Integrity:** Every E2O and O2O relation must reference existing `ocel:eid` and `ocel:oid` values in the dataset. No phantom objects (e.g., an unlogged shipping container or second invoice) may be instantiated.
* **Directional O2O Typing:** O2O qualifiers are strictly typed and directional: `Order --contains--> Item`, `Package --packs--> Item`, `Delivery --carries--> Package`, `Invoice --bills--> Order`, and `Payment --settles--> Invoice`. Inverses or cross-type bypasses (e.g., `Delivery --carries--> Order`) are invalid.
* **Temporal Precedence of Object Creation:** An object cannot participate in downstream lifecycle events prior to its creation event if that creation event is captured in the log window (e.g., `Add Item` cannot precede `Create Order` for the same order).

### Strong Defaults (Defeasible only with explicit anomaly evidence)
* **Activity-Object Cardinality:**
  * `Pack Item` links exactly 1 `Item` and 1 `Package`.
  * `Dispatch Package` links at least 1 `Package` and 1 `Delivery`.
  * `Issue Invoice` links 1 `Invoice` and $\ge 1$ `Order`.
  * `Record Payment` links 1 `Payment` and at most 1 `Invoice` (0 if unapplied cash).
* **E2O/O2O Co-occurrence:** When an activity establishes a structural relationship between two objects (e.g., `Pack Item` linking `IT-A1` and `PK-7`), the corresponding O2O edge (`PK-7 --packs--> IT-A1`) should exist, and vice versa.
* **Customer Homogeneity:** An `Invoice` should only bill `Orders` belonging to the same `customer` attribute (`C17`).

### Weak Heuristics (Suggestive signals requiring corroboration)
* **Identifier & Attribute String Matching:** Scanner references (`scanner_ref=PK8-2` pointing to `PK-8`), carrier references (`external_ref=N-421?` pointing to `DEL-Y` with `carrier_ref=N-421`), and wildcards (`external_ref=N-42*`).
* **Arithmetic Summation:** `INV-44.total (230) == ORD-A.declared_value (130) + ORD-B.declared_value (100)`, and `PAY-1.amount (100) + PAY-2.amount (130) == INV-44.total (230)`. Because freight charges, taxes, restocking fees, or partial credit memos are unmodeled, arithmetic alignment is treated as abductive support rather than proof.
* **Temporal Proximity & Route Alignment:** Co-temporal events (`ev07` at 09:04 and `ev08` at 09:05; `ev09` and `ev10` both at 10:00) and matching `route=NORTH` attributes support grouping or parallel processing.

### Evidence Against a Repair (Penalties and red flags)
* **Explicit Uncertainty Markers:** The trailing question mark in `ev10` (`external_ref=N-421?`) and the wildcard in `ev11` (`external_ref=N-42*`) indicate data corruption, OCR failure, or human guesswork at the source.
* **Cross-Order Contamination Without Downstream Alignment:** Packing `IT-B1` (from `ORD-B`) into `PK-8` (which holds `IT-A2` from `ORD-A`) creates a multi-order package. If `PK-8` is mixed, any downstream delivery confirmation or billing event touching `PK-8` would logically implicate `ORD-B`, yet `ev11` and `ev12` only observe `ORD-A`.
* **Orphaned Object Temptation:** The mere fact that `DEL-Y` has zero observed E2O/O2O relations is *not* independent evidence that `ev10` or `ev11` must connect to it; unused pre-allocated labels (`DEL-Y`) are common in warehouse logistics when packages are consolidated onto a single truck (`DEL-X`).

### Evidence Synthesis Model
We evaluate candidate repairs by combining independent signals. A high-confidence repair requires alignment between a **Strong Default** (e.g., cardinality deficit in `Pack Item`) and a **Weak Heuristic** (e.g., exact prefix match `PK8-2` + 1-minute temporal proximity) without triggering **Evidence Against**. When arithmetic matches conflict with missing upstream fulfillment confirmations (as with `ORD-B` and `INV-44`), we cap confidence in the `provisional` range ($0.50\text{--}0.75$) and separate the choices into distinct repair bundles rather than forcing a single deterministic graph.

## 3. Alternative repair bundles

Because the ambiguities in warehouse packing, carrier dispatch, and financial settlement interact across object lifecycles, edges cannot be repaired in isolation. We define three coherent bundles representing distinct interpretations of what happened to Customer `C17`'s orders on March 11, 2026.

### Bundle A: Conservative / Minimum-Change Repair (Selected Baseline)
* **Hypothesis:** The warehouse scanner suffered a brief association glitch during packing (`ev08`), which can be safely repaired via strong sequential scanner metadata (`PK8-1` $\rightarrow$ `PK8-2`). However, downstream carrier assignment (`ev10`), portal confirmation (`ev11`), and back-office financial matching (`ev12`, `ev14`) reflect genuine operational ambiguity or incomplete reconciliation that must not be overwritten.
* **Handling of key ambiguities:**
  * **`ev08` and `PK-8`:** **Repaired** (**P01**, **P02**). `ev08` is linked to `PK-8` (E2O) and `PK-8 --packs--> IT-B1` (O2O). `scanner_ref=PK8-2` one minute after `PK8-1` is strong physical-layer evidence of a dropped foreign key during a continuous packing session.
  * **`ev10` and `DEL-X`/`DEL-Y`:** **Left Unresolved** (**P03**–**P06** omitted). The literal question mark in `external_ref=N-421?` means the source system itself could not verify whether `PK-8` went onto truck `DEL-Y` or was consolidated onto `DEL-X` at 10:00. `DEL-Y` remains an orphan object (representing a pre-printed manifest that may or may not have been used).
  * **`ev11` and delivery objects:** **Left Unresolved** (**P07**–**P09** omitted). Per Rule 5, the customer portal lost the exact carrier reference (`N-42*`) and logged only `ORD-A`. We do not force `DEL-X` or `DEL-Y` onto `ev11`.
  * **`INV-44` and `ORD-B`:** **Left Unresolved** (**P10**, **P11** omitted). While $130 + 100 = 230$ is suggestive, `ORD-B` was never confirmed delivered at `ev11`. In many ERP systems, an order cannot be invoiced before delivery confirmation; thus, the extra 100 on `INV-44` could plausibly be rush shipping/handling for fragile item `IT-A1` (`sku=GLASS-1`).
  * **`PAY-2` and `INV-44`:** **Left Unresolved** (**P12**, **P13** omitted). `PAY-2` (`ev14`) is treated as **unapplied cash** (Rule 7) arriving at 16:00 without a remittance advice linking it to `INV-44`.
* **Lifecycle Coherence:** Highly faithful to raw system state. Every `Item` is cleanly packed into a `Package`, avoiding dangling warehouse items. Incomplete lifecycles for `DEL-Y`, `ORD-B`, and `PAY-2` accurately reflect unconfirmed deliveries and unreconciled cash at the end of the log extract.

### Bundle B: Consolidated Operational & Financial Fulfillment Repair
* **Hypothesis:** Customer `C17`'s two orders (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`) were processed together across the entire fulfillment and billing pipeline, and the missing links are purely logging omissions (scanner drop at `ev08`, OCR uncertainty `?` at `ev10`, truncated wildcard `*` at `ev11`, and dropped join keys at `ev12`/`ev14`).
* **Handling of key ambiguities:**
  * **`ev08` and `PK-8`:** **Repaired** (**P01**, **P02**), identical to Bundle A.
  * **`ev10` and `DEL-X`/`DEL-Y`:** **Repaired to `DEL-Y`** (**P03**, **P04**). Treats `N-421?` as a true scan of `DEL-Y` (`carrier_ref=N-421`) where the scanner flagged a low-confidence barcode read.
  * **`ev11` and delivery objects:** **Repaired to both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y`** (**P07**, **P08**). Since `ORD-A` spans both `PK-7` (`IT-A1` on `DEL-X`) and `PK-8` (`IT-A2` on `DEL-Y`), and the portal logged `external_ref=N-42*` while confirming `ORD-A`, Bundle B interprets `ev11` as a joint confirmation of both `N-420` (`DEL-X`) and `N-421` (`DEL-Y`). (Note: `ORD-B` is still omitted from `ev11` because the portal user only clicked confirm on `ORD-A`).
  * **`INV-44` and `ORD-B`:** **Repaired** (**P10**, **P11**). Accepts the arithmetic match ($130 + 100 = 230$) and same-customer context (`C17`) as a consolidated invoice covering both `ORD-A` and `ORD-B`.
  * **`PAY-2` and `INV-44`:** **Repaired** (**P12**, **P13**). Treats `PAY-1` (100) and `PAY-2` (130) as split order-level payments by `C17` that together fully settle `INV-44` (230).
* **Lifecycle Coherence:** Produces fully closed, end-to-end lifecycles for all 12 objects. However, it risks fabricating process regularity—specifically by assuming `DEL-Y` actually departed and arrived, and that `PAY-2` was automatically reconciled at 16:00.

### Bundle C: Single-Carrier Physical Consolidation (`DEL-X` Bulk Dispatch)
* **Hypothesis:** When two packages (`PK-7`, `PK-8`) for the same customer (`C17`) and route (`NORTH`) reached the dock at exactly `10:00`, the driver loaded both onto truck `DEL-X` (`N-420`), rendering pre-allocated label `DEL-Y` (`N-421`) void.
* **Handling of key ambiguities:**
  * **`ev08` and `PK-8`:** **Repaired** (**P01**, **P02**).
  * **`ev10` and `DEL-X`/`DEL-Y`:** **Repaired to `DEL-X`** (**P05**, **P06**). The `?` in `N-421?` represents the dock worker realizing label `N-421` was superseded and placing `PK-8` onto `DEL-X` alongside `PK-7` at `10:00`. `DEL-Y` is left as an unused orphan object.
  * **`ev11` and delivery objects:** **Repaired to `DEL-X` only** (**P07**). Because both `PK-7` and `PK-8` traveled on `DEL-X`, `ev11` confirming `ORD-A` (`N-42*`) uniquely confirms `DEL-X`.
  * **`INV-44` and `ORD-B` / `PAY-2` and `INV-44`:** **Repaired** (**P10**–**P13**), matching Bundle B's financial consolidation since physical delivery of `PK-8` (containing `IT-B1`) on `DEL-X` is now complete at `14:00`, making `ORD-B` eligible for invoicing at `15:00`.
* **Lifecycle Coherence:** Resolves the temporal coincidence of `ev09` and `ev10` (`10:00`) and explains why `ORD-B` can be invoiced at `15:00` (its package arrived on `DEL-X` at `14:00`), at the cost of overriding the string hint `N-421?`.

## 5. Lifecycle and cardinality audit

We audit all six object types under the selected **Bundle A** repair (with comparisons to **Bundle B** where relevant), checking cardinality constraints, orphan status, and circularity between E2O and O2O evidence.

### 1. Order (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`)
* **Lifecycle plausibility:**
  * `ORD-A`: `Create Order (ev01) -> Add Item (ev03) -> Add Item (ev04) -> Confirm Delivery (ev11) -> Issue Invoice (ev12)`. Complete, well-ordered lifecycle.
  * `ORD-B`: `Create Order (ev02) -> Add Item (ev05)`. Under Bundle A, `ORD-B` terminates after item addition because packing/shipping occurs at the `Item`/`Package` level and neither `ev11` nor `ev12` explicitly references `ORD-B`. This is a plausible partial lifecycle (an order fulfilled in the warehouse whose portal confirmation and billing are still pending or unlinked).
* **Orphans & Cardinalities:** No orphan orders. `ORD-A` has 1:2 `contains` O2O relations (`IT-A1`, `IT-A2`); `ORD-B` has 1:1 (`IT-B1`). Under Bundle B, `INV-44 --bills--> Order` becomes 1:2 (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`), which is valid under Rule 6.
* **E2O/O2O Independence:** Both `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` have fully observed, mutually consistent E2O (`ev03`–`ev05`) and O2O (`contains`) links. Under Bundle B, adding `INV-44 --bills--> ORD-B` (`P11`) and `ev12 -> ORD-B` (`P10`) relies on the exact same arithmetic cue ($130+100=230$); neither is independent observed confirmation of the other.

### 2. Item (`IT-A1`, `IT-A2`, `IT-B1`)
* **Lifecycle plausibility:** All three items follow `Add Item -> Pack Item` (`IT-A1`: `ev03 -> ev06`; `IT-A2`: `ev04 -> ev07`; `IT-B1`: `ev05 -> ev08`).
* **Orphans & Cardinalities:** No orphans. Every item belongs to exactly one `Order` and is packed into exactly one `Package` (once **P01/P02** are applied).
* **Circular Reasoning Audit (`ev08` / `PK-8` / `IT-B1`):** In the raw log, `IT-B1` has `ev08` (`Pack Item`) with `scanner_ref=PK8-2`, but *neither* the E2O link `(ev08, PK-8)` nor the O2O link `(PK-8, packs, IT-B1)` is observed. We infer `(ev08, PK-8)` from the event attribute `scanner_ref=PK8-2` and then add `(PK-8, packs, IT-B1)` to maintain structural consistency. **Crucial distinction:** The inferred O2O edge `PK-8 --packs--> IT-B1` is a *consequence* of the E2O inference, **not** independent evidence supporting it. Treating **P02** as corroboration for **P01** would be circular reasoning.

### 3. Package (`PK-7`, `PK-8`)
* **Lifecycle plausibility:**
  * `PK-7`: `Pack Item (ev06) -> Dispatch Package (ev09)`. Complete warehouse lifecycle.
  * `PK-8`: Under Bundle A, `Pack Item (ev07) -> Pack Item (ev08) -> Dispatch Package (ev10)`. Adding `ev08` makes `PK-8` a multi-item package (`IT-A2` and `IT-B1`).
* **Orphans & Cardinalities:** No orphans. `PK-8` exhibits a 1:2 `packs` cardinality (`IT-A2`, `IT-B1`) and spans two orders (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`), creating an indirect many-to-many relationship between `Order` and `Package` (`ORD-A` uses `PK-7` and `PK-8`; `PK-8` serves `ORD-A` and `ORD-B`). While structurally important for process mining (causing convergence/divergence), multi-order consolidation for the same customer (`C17`) and route (`NORTH`) is operationally sound.
* **E2O/O2O Independence:** `PK-7 --packs--> IT-A1` and `PK-8 --packs--> IT-A2` are independently present in both E2O and O2O tables. `PK-8 --packs--> IT-B1` is co-inferred (**P01/P02**).

### 4. Delivery (`DEL-X`, `DEL-Y`)
* **Lifecycle plausibility:**
  * `DEL-X`: `Dispatch Package (ev09)` (and `Confirm Delivery (ev11)` only in Bundles B/C).
  * `DEL-Y`: **Complete Orphan Object** in the raw log and Bundle A (0 E2O, 0 O2O relations). In Bundle B, it follows `Dispatch Package (ev10) -> Confirm Delivery (ev11)`.
* **Orphans & Cardinalities:** Leaving `DEL-Y` as an orphan in Bundle A is an intentional feature of calibrated uncertainty: if `PK-8` was consolidated onto `DEL-X` (Bundle C) or `ev10` failed to bind, forcing `DEL-Y` into the graph fabricates a second truck run.
* **Circular Reasoning Audit (`DEL-Y`):** One must not argue: *"DEL-Y carries PK-8 (O2O), therefore ev10 must link to DEL-Y (E2O),"* nor *"DEL-Y exists in the objects table, so it must have been dispatched at ev10."* Both `P03` (E2O) and `P04` (O2O) stem solely from the uncertain string `external_ref=N-421?`.

### 5. Invoice (`INV-44`)
* **Lifecycle plausibility:** `Issue Invoice (ev12) -> Record Payment (ev13)` (plus `Record Payment (ev14)` in Bundles B/C). Completely plausible financial lifecycle.
* **Orphans & Cardinalities:** No orphan invoices. In Bundle A, `INV-44` bills 1 `Order` (`ORD-A`) and receives 1 `Payment` (`PAY-1`). In Bundle B, `INV-44` has a 1:2 `bills` relation (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`) and a 2:1 `settles` relation (`PAY-1`, `PAY-2`).
* **E2O/O2O Independence:** `INV-44 --bills--> ORD-A` and `PAY-1 --settles--> INV-44` are attested in both E2O and O2O tables. The candidate links to `ORD-B` and `PAY-2` lack both E2O and O2O records in the raw data.

### 6. Payment (`PAY-1`, `PAY-2`)
* **Lifecycle plausibility:**Each payment participates in a single `Record Payment` event (`PAY-1` in `ev13`, `PAY-2` in `ev14`).
* **Orphans & Cardinalities:** No event-level orphans (`PAY-2` is tied to `ev14`), but under Bundle A, `PAY-2` is an **O2O orphan** (not linked to any `Invoice`). In accounting domain semantics (Rule 7), an O2O-orphan `Payment` linked to a `Record Payment` event accurately models **unapplied cash** awaiting reconciliation.
* **Circular Reasoning Audit (`PAY-2`):** Inferring `PAY-2 --settles--> INV-44` (O2O) from arithmetic ($100 + 130 = 230$) and then using that O2O edge to justify adding `INV-44` to `ev14` (E2O) is circular; both edges depend on the unverified assumption that `PAY-2` was applied to `INV-44` at 16:00.

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects

Choosing between the **Unrepaired Log**, **Bundle A**, and **Bundle B** substantially alters structural discovery, frequency metrics, and trace clustering in PM4Py.

### 1. Object-Centric Directly-Follows Graph (OC-DFG)
* **Unrepaired Log:**
  * Along the `Package` dimension, `PK-8` follows `Pack Item (ev07) -> Dispatch Package (ev10)`. `ev08` has no `Package` object, so there is no `Pack Item -> Pack Item` self-loop for `PK-8`.
  * Along `Delivery`, `DEL-X` has a single node `Dispatch Package (ev09)` with no outgoing edge; `DEL-Y` does not appear in the OC-DFG at all.
  * Along `Invoice`, `INV-44` traces `Issue Invoice (ev12) -> Record Payment (ev13)`. `ev14` is isolated on `PAY-2` with no incoming edge from `ev12` or `ev13`.
* **Bundle A:**
  * Linking `PK-8` to `ev08` **creates a new `Pack Item -> Pack Item` self-loop** on the `Package` object type (`ev07 -> ev08`) and routes the `Pack Item -> Dispatch Package` edge for `PK-8` through `ev08 -> ev10` instead of `ev07 -> ev10`.
  * Delivery, Order, and Invoice paths remain clean and uninflated.
* **Bundle B:**
  * **Delivery edges created:** `DEL-X` gains `Dispatch Package (ev09) -> Confirm Delivery (ev11)`, and `DEL-Y` enters the OC-DFG with `Dispatch Package (ev10) -> Confirm Delivery (ev11)`. Thus, the `Delivery` edge `Dispatch Package -> Confirm Delivery` jumps from frequency **0** (Unrepaired/Bundle A) to **2** (Bundle B).
  * **Order edge created:** `ORD-B` gains an edge directly from `Add Item (ev05) -> Issue Invoice (ev12)` (skipping `Confirm Delivery`, since `ev11` only linked to `ORD-A`, `DEL-X`, and `DEL-Y`). This introduces a suspicious bypass edge `Add Item -> Issue Invoice` on the `Order` lifecycle!
  * **Invoice self-loop created:** Linking `INV-44` to `ev14` creates a `Record Payment -> Record Payment` self-loop (`ev13 -> ev14`) along the `Invoice` object type, shifting the `Invoice` lifecycle from single-payment settlement to installment/split-payment settlement.

### 2. Object Type Graph (OTG) and ET–OT Graph
* **OTG (Object-to-Object Type Graph):**
  * All three versions contain the core type edges: `Order -> Item`, `Package -> Item`, `Delivery -> Package`, `Invoice -> Order`, and `Payment -> Invoice` (because at least one observed O2O instance exists for each type pair in the raw log).
  * However, **instance cardinalities** on the OTG change: Bundle A increases `Package -> Item` max out-degree from 1 to 2 (`PK-8` packs 2 items) and induces an indirect two-hop link between `PK-8` and two distinct `Orders`. Bundle B increases `Invoice -> Order` out-degree from 1 to 2 and `Payment -> Invoice` in-degree from 1 to 2.
* **ET–OT Graph (Event-Type to Object-Type Cardinalities):**
  * **Unrepaired:** `Pack Item` -- `Package` exhibits a `0..1` cardinality (`ev08` has 0 packages), `Dispatch Package` -- `Delivery` is `0..1` (`ev10` has 0 deliveries), `Confirm Delivery` -- `Delivery` is `0` (`ev11` only links to `Order`), and `Record Payment` -- `Invoice` is `0..1` (`ev14` has 0 invoices).
  * **Bundle A:** Normalizes `Pack Item` -- `Package` to strictly `1..1`, while preserving the `0..1` and `0` cardinalities on `Dispatch Package`, `Confirm Delivery`, and `Record Payment`.
  * **Bundle B:** Forces `Dispatch Package` -- `Delivery` to `1..1`, `Record Payment` -- `Invoice` to `1..1`, `Issue Invoice` -- `Order` to `1..2`, and adds a brand-new ET–OT edge between **`Confirm Delivery` and `Delivery`** (`0..2` across the log, `2` at `ev11`).

### 3. Flattenings on Individual Object Types
* **Order Flattening:**
  * *Unrepaired & Bundle A:* `ORD-A` trace is `<Create Order, Add Item, Add Item, Confirm Delivery, Issue Invoice>` (length 5); `ORD-B` trace is `<Create Order, Add Item>` (length 2).
  * *Bundle B:* `ORD-B` becomes `<Create Order, Add Item, Issue Invoice>` (length 3), creating a variant where invoicing occurs without a preceding delivery confirmation event.
* **Package Flattening:**
  * *Unrepaired:* `PK-7` and `PK-8` both have trace `<Pack Item, Dispatch Package>` (2 identical traces of length 2).
  * *Bundle A & Bundle B:* `PK-8` becomes `<Pack Item, Pack Item, Dispatch Package>` (length 3), revealing multi-item packing loops.
* **Delivery Flattening:**
  * *Unrepaired & Bundle A:* `DEL-X` has `<Dispatch Package>` (length 1); `DEL-Y` has an empty trace `<>` (dropped during flattening).
  * *Bundle B:* Both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y` have trace `<Dispatch Package, Confirm Delivery>` (2 identical traces of length 2). Notice also a classic **convergence/duplication distortion**: single event `ev11` (`Confirm Delivery`) is duplicated into both `DEL-X`'s and `DEL-Y`'s flattened traces.
* **Invoice Flattening:**
  * *Unrepaired & Bundle A:* `INV-44` has `<Issue Invoice, Record Payment>`.
  * *Bundle B:* `INV-44` has `<Issue Invoice, Record Payment, Record Payment>`.
* **Payment Flattening:**
  * Across all bundles, `PAY-1` and `PAY-2` each have trace `<Record Payment>` (length 1).

### 5. Equivalent-Execution Clustering Around `Order`
When extracting object-centric process executions (connected components of objects via O2O or shared E2O relationships anchored on `Order`):
* **Unrepaired Log:** `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` form **two completely disconnected execution subgraphs**. `ORD-A` connects to `IT-A1, IT-A2, PK-7, PK-8, DEL-X, INV-44, PAY-1`. `ORD-B` connects only to `IT-B1` (stopping at `ev08`). They represent two distinct process variants (one complete two-item order, one truncated single-item order).
* **Bundle A:** Because `ev08` links `IT-B1` (from `ORD-B`) into `PK-8` (which already contains `IT-A2` from `ORD-A`), **`ORD-A` and `ORD-B` become coupled in the warehouse layer via shared package `PK-8`**. If execution extraction traverses all connected object types, `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` collapse into a single intertwined multi-order execution component. If scoped strictly to direct 1-hop `Order` neighbors, they remain distinct.
* **Bundle B:** `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` are doubly fused—both physically via `PK-8` and financially via `INV-44`. Any execution clustering merges them into a single giant component, masking individual order variance.

## 8. Over-repair warnings

Aggressive "completionist" repairs—driven by a desire to eliminate every orphan object and close every lifecycle—can corrupt downstream process analytics in at least six specific ways in this dataset:

1. **Fabricating Delivery Without Confirmation (`ORD-B` Invoicing Bypass):**
   Linking `INV-44` to `ORD-B` at `ev12` (**P10**) without a prior `Confirm Delivery` event for `ORD-B` creates a direct `Add Item (ev05) -> Issue Invoice (ev12)` edge in the `Order` OC-DFG and flattened `Order` log. A compliance audit model would falsely conclude that the organization violates internal controls by invoicing orders before they are packed or confirmed delivered—when in reality the extra `100` on `INV-44` may simply be fragile-glass shipping/insurance fees on `ORD-A`.
2. **Erasing Unapplied Cash Delays (`PAY-2` to `INV-44`):**
   Forcing `PAY-2` (`ev14`) onto `INV-44` (**P12/P13**) solely because $100 + 130 = 230$ hides the existence of unapplied cash (Rule 7). In Accounts Receivable process mining, unlinked payments represent a critical bottleneck (remittance matching failure requiring manual clerk intervention). Over-repairing `ev14` makes `PAY-2` look like an instant, automated split-payment settlement at `16:00`, distorting payment-to-invoice lead times and straight-through processing (STP) rates.
3. **Animating a Dead Shipping Manifest (`DEL-Y` Phantom Shipment):**
   Because `DEL-Y` sits in the `objects` table with `carrier_ref=N-421`, it is tempting to force `ev10` (`N-421?`) and `ev11` (`N-42*`) to link to `DEL-Y` (**P03, P08**). However, `ev09` and `ev10` occur at the exact same timestamp (`10:00`) for the same customer (`C17`) and route (`NORTH`). If the dock worker actually consolidated `PK-8` onto truck `DEL-X` after voiding label `N-421`, linking `DEL-Y` fabricates a second vehicle run, doubles the apparent carrier dispatch volume on the `NORTH` route, and understates package consolidation efficiency.
4. **Creating False Convergence on Portal Confirmation (`ev11` Multi-Delivery Binding):**
   If an analyst connects `ev11` (`external_ref=N-42*`) to both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y` (**P07 + P08**), `ev11` becomes a synchronization point across two separate deliveries. When flattening the log on `Delivery`, `ev11` is duplicated into both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y` traces (the classic OCEL *convergence* distortion), and in the OC-DFG, both deliveries appear to arrive at the exact same millisecond (`14:00:00`), creating an artificial batch-arrival pattern.
5. **Cross-Order Execution Contamination via `PK-8` Transitive Propagation:**
   Once `IT-B1` is placed into `PK-8` (`ev08`, **P01**), `PK-8` physically bridges `ORD-A` (`IT-A2`) and `ORD-B` (`IT-B1`). If an aggressive repair subsequently uses `PK-8`'s presence to infer that `ORD-B` must also participate in `ev10` (`Dispatch Package`), `ev11` (`Confirm Delivery`), and `ev12` (`Issue Invoice`), the repair conflates physical package consolidation with order-level administrative milestones, destroying the distinction between warehouse handling and customer-facing order state.
6. **Direct E2O Flattening Bypass (Linking `Order` Directly to Warehouse Events):**
   To make flattened `Order` process maps show `Pack Item` (`ev06`–`ev08`) and `Dispatch Package` (`ev09`–`ev10`), analysts sometimes propagate O2O transitively into E2O (`Order -> Item -> Package -> Event`, **P14**). In OCEL 2.0, this causes severe *divergence* (repeated `Pack Item` and `Dispatch Package` loops per `Order`), violates domain cardinality rules (Rules 3 and 4), and destroys the multi-level abstraction hierarchy captured by the ET–OT graph.
