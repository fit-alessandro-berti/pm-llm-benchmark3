## 1. Stipulated Facts, Disputed Propositions, and Burden of Proof

### Stipulated Facts
1. **Model Baseline**: Model $M_0$ defines a partial order requiring `Sanctions_Check` and `Quality_Review` to complete concurrently before an exclusive choice (`XOR`) between an approved branch (`Manual_Procurement_Approval` $\rightarrow$ `Activate_Supplier`) and `Reject_Supplier`. Every case must end with `Notify_Requester` followed by `Close_Case`.
2. **Normative Governance Baseline**: Compliance Standard CS-9 (effective 2025-01-01) requires both `Sanctions_Check` and `Quality_Review` prior to supplier activation, manual procurement approval prior to activation, and distinct natural persons fulfilling the requester and manual approver roles.
3. **Execution Contexts**:
   - Pilot Decision PD-3 was recorded in steering committee minutes (effective 2026-06-01 to 2026-07-31) allowing `Auto_Approve` for LOW-risk DE suppliers in approved corporate groups, deferring `Quality_Review` up to two business days post-activation.
   - Emergency Directive FD-7 (effective 2026-07-10 00:00 to 2026-07-12 23:59) permitted provisional activation for critical flood relief suppliers post-`Sanctions_Check`, capping spend at EUR 20,000 pending retrospective approval and quality review within two business days.
4. **Data Operations & Logging Systems**:
   - On 2026-07-01, notification handling shifted to a CRM system, reducing local onboarding log mirroring and introducing a 29% missingness rate in local log captures.
   - Case SUP-711 contains duplicate `Register_Supplier` records sharing identical source transaction IDs due to migration issues.
   - Case SUP-713 executed `QA_Clearance`, which operational management approved as a functional rename of `Quality_Review` one week post-event.

### Disputed Propositions
1. **Normative Status of Pilot and Emergency Directives**: The Prosecution asserts that PD-3 and FD-7 are unauthorized or expired overrides that cannot supersede CS-9 without formal baseline amendments. The Defense asserts that PD-3 and FD-7 represent valid operational policy overlays issued by executive governance.
2. **Scope of Baseline Model $M_0$**: The Process Owner asserts that pre-screen rejections (e.g., SUP-709) and reactivations (e.g., SUP-710) are valid operational paths improperly penalized by $M_0$. The Model Owner maintains that $M_0$ exclusively governs new full-onboarding lifecycles starting post-intake clearance.
3. **Validity of Post-Expiration Operations**: Operations asserts that post-directive execution (SUP-705 during ongoing flood recovery, SUP-714 post-PD-3 expiry) is justified by ongoing business necessity or pending policy updates. Compliance maintains these represent unauthorized policy breaches.
4. **Data Integrity vs. Process Noncompliance**: The Prosecution treats missing log notifications (e.g., SUP-706) as control failures. The Defense argues these are log-synchronization defects arising from CRM decouplings.

### Burden of Proof Framework
- **Proving Noncompliance**: The Prosecution bears the burden of establishing that an observed event sequence violates an active, applicable normative standard (CS-9 or valid active overlay) using corroborated log and system data.
- **Establishing a Justified Exception**: The Defense bears the burden of demonstrating that a deviation:
  1. Falls within the explicit temporal, geographical, and risk scope of a formally authorized policy overlay (PD-3 or FD-7);
  2. Adheres strictly to associated operational guardrails (e.g., spend caps, delay windows, mandatory pre-activation sanctions); and
  3. Is supported by auditable, immutable system records.

## 2. Prosecution Brief

### 1. Superiority of CS-9 and Lack of Formal Baseline Revisions
Compliance Standard CS-9 is the sole formally controlled normative standard governing supplier onboarding. Steering committee minutes (PD-3) and emergency emails (FD-7) were never incorporated into CS-9. The Compliance observer explicitly noted that PD-3 was a "monitored exception" and denied authorizing any permanent weakening of CS-9. Informal executive consensus cannot override controlled compliance standards.

### 2. Unauthorized Operational Drift and Normalization of Deviations
Allowing unratified exceptions breeds systemic drift:
- **SUP-703**: Demonstrates the danger of unmonitored pilot mechanisms. An operator selected the pilot pathway for a HIGH-risk foreign supplier, executing `Auto_Approve` and committing EUR 180,000 in spend—violating both risk and spend boundaries.
- **SUP-705**: Operations unilaterally extended Emergency Directive FD-7 post-expiration without written authorization. Factual emergency conditions do not grant self-issued regulatory exemptions.
- **SUP-714**: Operations continued executing `Auto_Approve` after PD-3 expired on 2026-07-31, assuming permanent adoption. Operational expectation is not legal authorization.


### 3. Severe Control Violations Hidden by Process Timing
- **SUP-708**: `Activate_Supplier` occurred 40 minutes *before* `Sanctions_Check`. Activating an unvetted entity exposes the enterprise to legal penalties under international sanctions regimes, regardless of whether immediate transactions occurred.
- **SUP-712**: The requester and manual approver were the same individual acting under two user aliases, violating CS-9’s explicit Segregation of Duties (SoD) mandate. $M_0$ fails to catch this because it checks only trace syntax, masking severe governance fraud.

### 4. Rejection of Retroactive Rationalization
Operations argues that SUP-707 (iteration loops) represents desirable business behavior. However, unmodeled iteration allows unchecked delays and arbitrary approval cycles. Desirable outcomes cannot retroactively validate noncompliant execution.

## 3. Defense Brief

### 1. Structural Obsolescence and Incompleteness of Model $M_0$
Model $M_0$ is fundamentally flawed and structurally incapable of reflecting actual enterprise business processes:
- **Absence of Essential Iteration (SUP-707)**: $M_0$ models onboarding as a strict directed acyclic graph (DAG), omitting the `Request_More_Info` clarification loop. When business operations require supplementary documentation, following $M_0$ forces a choice between rejecting viable suppliers or violating process structure.
- **Scope Misalignment (SUP-709 & SUP-710)**: $M_0$ forces all cases into a single fixed flow. Intake leads rejected during initial pre-screening (SUP-709) do not require a `Quality_Review`. Reactivated suppliers (SUP-710) possess valid prior quality certifications. Penalizing these cases as control failures misinterprets scope limits as operational noncompliance.

```
Actual Business Operations: Intake Pre-Screen  [Failed Sanctions]  Reject & Close (SUP-709)
                                                                           
Model M0 Constraint:        Register  Sanctions  Quality  Decision  Reject (Forces Invalid Quality)
```

### 2. Valid Executive Overlays and Authorized Emergency Directives
- **SUP-702**: Executed within the authorized timeframe of PD-3, satisfying all eligibility criteria (LOW-risk DE supplier, approved corporate group). Post-activation quality review completed within one business day.
- **SUP-704**: Executed during active flooding under Emergency Directive FD-7. `Sanctions_Check` completed prior to activation, initial spend was kept under the EUR 20,000 threshold (EUR 18,000), and retrospective reviews completed within one business day.

### 3. Logging Defects and Systems Integration Issues
- **SUP-706**: CRM migration shifted `Notify_Requester` logging out of the primary onboarding log. Cross-system audit trails confirm the notification sent at +3 hours; the delay in updating the mutable case link is a log synchronization lag, not a failure to notify.
- **SUP-711**: The duplicate `Register_Supplier` entry stems from a database migration glitch sharing identical transaction IDs, representing technical noise rather than duplicate process execution.
- **SUP-713**: System UI remapping renamed `Quality_Review` to `QA_Clearance`. The control objective was fully satisfied; rejecting the trace over label naming confuses semantic consistency with control failure.

### 4. Distinguishing Process Deviations from Misconduct
In SUP-705, Operations managed critical flood recovery under ongoing emergency conditions with capped spend (EUR 12,000). While technically post-expiry, this represents a low-risk, good-faith response to operational necessity, distinct from malicious noncompliance or high-risk exposure like SUP-703.

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

To resolve the structural defects of $M_0$ and account for multi-layered business rules, the normative architecture is expanded into a five-tier framework:

```

 Layer 5: Organizational & COI Controls (HR Alias Checks, COI Disclosures)

 Layer 4: Lifecycle & Scope Boundaries (Intake, Full Onboarding, Reactiv)

 Layer 3: Policy Overlay Governance (PD-3 Pilot, FD-7 Emergency Rules)   

 Layer 2: Data-Aware Constraints (Spend Caps, Country Risk, Time Limits)  

 Layer 1: Control-Flow Model M1 (POWL Syntax: Loops, Branches, State)    

```

### 1. Layer 1: Revised Control-Flow Model ($M_1$)
Encoded using POWL syntax to support:
- Concurrent standard checks with integrated `Request_More_Info` loops.
- Exclusive choices between Standard Approval, Low-Risk Auto-Approve, Emergency Provisional Activation, and Early Rejection paths.
- Mandatory downstream `Notify_Requester` and `Close_Case`.

### 2. Layer 2: Data-Aware Constraints (Declarative Rules)
- **Spend Constraints**: `Spend_Amount` $\le$ EUR 20,000 for Emergency Provisional Activation paths.
- **Temporal Windows**: Deferred `Quality_Review` must execute within $\le$ 2 business days (48 hours) post-activation for Pilot and Emergency routes.
- **Pre-Activation Mandate**: `Sanctions_Check` completion timestamp $<$ `Activate_Supplier` timestamp across all execution paths without exception.

### 3. Layer 3: Policy Overlay Governance
- **Pilot Overlay (PD-3)**: Valid only between 2026-06-01 and 2026-07-31. Requires `Supplier_Risk_Level == LOW`, `Supplier_Country == DE`, and `Corporate_Group_Approved == True`.
- **Emergency Overlay (FD-7)**: Valid only between 2026-07-10 00:00 and 2026-07-12 23:59. Requires `Emergency_Flag == True`, `Critical_Relief_Category == True`, and daily transaction monitoring.

### 4. Layer 4: Lifecycle Scope Rules
- **Intake Pre-Screen Scope**: Cases failing early pre-screening terminate directly at `Reject_Supplier` $\rightarrow$ `Notify_Requester` $\rightarrow$ `Close_Case` without requiring `Quality_Review`.
- **Reactivation Scope**: Reactivation of closed suppliers skips `Register_Supplier` and `Quality_Review` provided `Prior_Quality_Cert_Valid == True` and cert expiration date $>$ current date.

### 5. Layer 5: Resource and Governance Integrity Rules
- **Segregation of Duties (SoD)**: `User_ID(Requester)` $\neq$ `User_ID(Manual_Approver)` (mapped across all primary accounts and HR aliases).
- **Conflict of Interest (COI)**: `Manager_Line(Quality_Reviewer)` $\neq$ `Manager_Line(Manual_Approver)` unless an approved `COI_Disclosure_Waiver` ID is attached to the case.

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

### 1. Revised Executable POWL Model ($M_1$)

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    OperatorPOWL,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    Transition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# Define standard activity transitions
register = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")
sanctions = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
quality = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
request_info = Transition(label="Request_More_Info")
manual_approval = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
activate = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
auto_approve = Transition(label="Auto_Approve")
reject = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
notify = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")
close = Transition(label="Close_Case")

# Silent transition for optional choices
skip_info = SilentTransition()

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Standard Review Loop & Path (Quality Review + Info Loop -> Manual Approval -> Activation)
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Loop structure: Quality_Review followed optionally by Request_More_Info
info_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[quality, request_info])

# Concurrent execution of Sanctions Check and Quality Review loop for standard route
std_checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, info_loop])

std_path = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[std_checks, manual_approval, activate])
std_path.order.add_edge(std_checks, manual_approval)
std_path.order.add_edge(manual_approval, activate)

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Pilot Low-Risk Path (Sanctions -> Auto Approve -> Activate -> Post-Activation Quality)
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
pilot_post_quality = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
pilot_path = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[sanctions, auto_approve, activate, pilot_post_quality]
)
pilot_path.order.add_edge(sanctions, auto_approve)
pilot_path.order.add_edge(auto_approve, activate)
pilot_path.order.add_edge(activate, pilot_post_quality)

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Emergency Relief Path (Sanctions -> Provisional Activation -> Deferred Approval & Quality)
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
emerg_quality = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
emerg_approval = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
emerg_post_checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[emerg_quality, emerg_approval])

emerg_path = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, activate, emerg_post_checks])
emerg_path.order.add_edge(sanctions, activate)
emerg_path.order.add_edge(activate, emerg_post_checks)

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Intake Pre-Screen Rejection Path (Sanctions -> Reject)
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
reject_path = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, reject])
reject_path.order.add_edge(sanctions, reject)

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. Top-Level Architectural Choice Assembly
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
onboarding_choice = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[std_path, pilot_path, emerg_path, reject_path],
)

# Overall Lifecycle Assembly
M1 = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, onboarding_choice, notify, close])
M1.order.add_edge(register, onboarding_choice)
M1.order.add_edge(onboarding_choice, notify)
M1.order.add_edge(notify, close)
```

#### Technical Limitations of POWL Representation
POWL cannot natively enforce data-driven conditions, calendar durations, monetary caps, or identity checks. Specifically:
1. **Data Constraints**: Spend caps (EUR 20,000) and risk thresholds (LOW risk DE) cannot be expressed within POWL transitions and require Layer 2 validation.
2. **Resource Constraints**: User alias identity resolution (SoD) and spousal relationship checks (COI) require Layer 5 governance checks.
3. **Temporal Scope**: Post-activation 48-hour completion targets require log timestamp evaluations outside the control-flow structure.

### 3. Data Collection and Event Logging Standard

To prevent missing log data, linking failures, and identity obfuscation, event data collection must be restructured to enforce the following schema:

```
                              
                                  Event Log Record Schema       
                              
                               Case_ID (UUIDv4)                 
                               Event_ID (UUIDv4)                
                               Activity_Name (Enum)             
                               Event_Timestamp (ISO-8601 UTC)   
                               Record_Timestamp (Immutable DB)  
                               Notification_ID (Stable Foreign) 
                               User_ID (Global Identity Key)    
                               HR_Alias_ID (Resolved Master ID) 
                               Policy_Overlay_ID (FK Table)     
                               Risk_Level_Snapshot (Enum)       
                               Supplier_Country (ISO 2-letter)  
                               Committed_Spend_EUR (Decimal)    
                               COI_Disclosure_ID (Nullable FK)  
                              
```

#### Key Implementation Specifications:
1. **Immutable Dual Timestamps**: Record both `Event_Timestamp` (when the operational action took place) and `Record_Timestamp` (when the log entry was committed to the database) to catch backdated logs.
2. **Canonical Identity Keys**: Replace free-text user strings with a unified `User_ID` linked to a master HR identity registry, resolving multi-alias collisions (addressing SUP-712).
3. **Deterministic System Foreign Keys**: Enforce mandatory UUID-based `Notification_ID` generation at notification dispatch, eliminating mutable text links across CRM systems (addressing SUP-706).
4. **Context Snapshots**: Append immutable snapshots of supplier risk level, country of origin, committed spend, and policy overlay ID directly to every case record at the time of process execution.
