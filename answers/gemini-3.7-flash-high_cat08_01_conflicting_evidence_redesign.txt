# Executive Advisory: Warranty Process Diagnostic and Redesign Strategy

## 1. Current-process diagnosis

### 1.1 The Core Operational Contradiction: Phantom Speed and Hidden Failure Costs
The organization's warranty process exhibits a fundamental contradiction between localized front-end velocity and end-to-end system effectiveness:
* **The "Fast Path" Illusion:** The dominant process variant—`Intake claim -> Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement -> Quality/compliance close`—handles 2,550 of 5,000 cases (51%) with rapid front-end execution (median cycle time of ~5 hours from intake to dispatch). Before rework, this path appears **31% cheaper** than reviewed paths.
* **Downstream Quality and Financial Erosion:** Fast approval cases experience a **14.1% customer complaint/reopen rate**, compared to only **3.3%** for the structured repair path (`Repair quote -> Customer approval -> Repair device`). After accounting for replacement errors (costing EUR 95 for accessories and EUR 410 for high-value devices), secondary shipments, customer support escalations, and SLA service credits, the net cost advantage collapses from **31% down to a meager 9%**.
* **Segment-Specific Failure:** For Premium High-Value devices (14% of total volume), the fast path generates an unacceptable **22% complaint/reopen rate** (versus 5% on reviewed paths). For Standard High-Value devices (21% volume), it produces an **18% complaint rate** (versus 6% on reviewed paths). In contrast, pushing Premium low-risk accessories to manual review creates a 46-hour median cycle time that breaches the strict 24-hour SLA, triggering heavy service-credit liabilities.

### 1.2 Five Key Process-Mining Artifacts and Conformance Findings

1. **Conformance Metrics and Token Replay Diagnostics:**
   * Token-based replay against the normative Petri net reveals a **log fitness of 0.91**, but only **68% of traces are fully fitting**. This means 32% of all processed claims (1,600 cases) violate internal operating rules.
   * Frequent alignment deviations (move-on-log) confirm widespread non-compliance:
     * **Violation of Rule R1:** Claims > EUR 400 bypass required Manual eligibility review or Fraud/risk review, flowing directly from Auto eligibility screen to Fast approval.
     * **Violation of Rule R2:** Replacement dispatch occurs before evidence completeness is verified for non-exempt segments (only Premium low-risk accessories are legally exempt).
     * **Violation of Rule R3:** Reopened claims bypass mandatory Manual eligibility review, routing straight to ad-hoc resolutions or premature closure.

2. **Performance DFG Long-Tail Latencies and Temporal Profiling:**
   * **Evidence Loop Latency:** `Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` is a severe drag, averaging **mean 34.0h, median 26.0h, and p90 88.0h**.
   * **Quoting Delays:** `Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote` reaches a **p90 of 60.0h (mean 22.0h)** due to technicians waiting for manual part-price confirmations.
   * **Delayed Downstream Friction:** `Fast approval -> Customer complaint/reopen` takes **median 50.0h (p90 150.0h)**, while `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` takes **median 70.0h (p90 180.0h, mean 96.0h)**. This long-tail delay masks dispatch errors (wrong replacement parts) until days after initial case closure.

3. **Rework and Abandonment Statistics:**
   * **Evidence Ping-Pong:** `Request missing evidence` occurs at least twice in **26% of cases** that enter the state, driving the Unclear Defect segment (22% of all claims) to an unsustainable median cycle time of **140 hours**.
   * **Customer Drop-Off:** 390 claims terminate in `Claim abandoned` (320 directly from missing evidence requests, 70 post-repair quote).
   * **Repair Defect Rate:** Repaired high-value devices experience a **12.5% rework rate** (`Quality check -> Rework repair`), compared to 4.0% for low-risk devices, pointing to inadequate technical triage and diagnostic tools upfront.

4. **Model Precision and Control-Flow Entropy:**
   * Process discovery shows **low precision**, caused by an over-permissive, chaotic execution topology. Numerous under-regulated, low-frequency paths interleave auto-approval, fraud review, repair quoting, and complaint escalations. This indicates that operational staff frequently bypass standard operating procedures to resolve ad-hoc queue blockages.

5. **Resource Utilization and Capacity Fragility:**
   * **Manual Review Queue Explosion:** 7 manual reviewers (252 productive hours/week at EUR 34/h) handle initial intake (1,400 cases), returned evidence (890 cases), and reopened complaints (310 cases)—a total of **2,600 case touches**. Queue delays grow non-linearly whenever utilization crosses the **82% tipping point**.
   * **Specialist Bottlenecks:** Fraud/risk review (2 FTEs, EUR 52/h) becomes severely backlogged on Mondays and following marketing campaigns (mean wait time 11.0h to 18.0h).
   * **Escalation Constraint:** The Escalation Board is restricted to a single **daily 45-minute slot**, creating an artificial bottleneck for 120 high-friction customer disputes (`mean 54.0h, p90 130.0h`).
   * **Misallocated Inspection Capacity:** 2 Quality Check inspectors (EUR 38/h) remain underutilized (handling only 360 repair checks) while uninspected dispatch replacements generate 260 costly customer complaints.

## 2. Redesign A: Segment-Tiered Risk-Based Triage & Automated Guardrails

### 2.1 Process Flow Description
Redesign A implements a deterministic, multi-track intake engine that classifies claims at entry based on Customer Tier (Premium vs. Standard), Asset Value (Accessory < EUR 400 vs. High-Value Device > EUR 400), and Defect Clarity.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                      CLAIM INTAKE & TRIAGE ENGINE                                 |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
           |                                                 |                             |
 [Premium / Std Accessory]                              [High-Value Device]        [Unclear Defect]
 (< EUR 400, 43% Volume)                                (> EUR 400, 35% Volume)     (22% Volume)
           |                                                 |                             |
           v                                                 v                             v
+-----------------------+                         +---------------------+       +-------------------+
| Automated SKU Check   |                         | Mandatory Technical |       | Tiered Resolution:|
| & Serial Validation   |                         | & Compliance Review |       | - Prem: Agent Call|
+-----------------------+                         +---------------------+       | - Std: Mini-Portal|
           |                                                 |                  +-------------------+
           v                                                 v                             |
+-----------------------+                         +---------------------+                  v
| Instant Auto-Approval |                         | Integrated Part-API |       +-------------------+
| & Dispatch            |                         | Automated Quoting   |       | Fast-Track Re-Eval|
+-----------------------+                         +---------------------+       +-------------------+
           |                                                 |                             |
           v                                                 v                             v
+-----------------------+                         +---------------------+       +-------------------+
| Fast Close (< 12 hrs) |                         | Repair / Controlled |       | Conformance Close |
| (100% R2 Compliance)  |                         | Dispatch Close      |       | (100% R3 Comply)  |
+-----------------------+                         +---------------------+       +-------------------+
```

1. **Track 1: Low-Risk Accessories (43% volume):**
   * *Premium Accessories (18%):* Instant auto-approval and immediate replacement dispatch without upfront evidence checks (leveraging the normative R2 exemption). Cycle time target: < 12 hours.
   * *Standard Accessories (25%):* Automated SKU verification against purchase history. Approval executes instantly upon basic photo upload; dispatch occurs within 24 hours.
2. **Track 2: High-Value Devices (35% volume):**
   * Hard automated guardrail: Direct `Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval` is programmatically blocked for all assets > EUR 400, enforcing 100% compliance with Rules R1 and R2.
   * Claims route to a specialized *High-Value Technical Triage Queue*. Reviewers are equipped with an automated Part-Price Lookup API, eliminating the 22h manual quote delay.
3. **Track 3: Unclear Evidence / Missing Data (22% volume):**
   * *Premium Customers:* Immediate white-glove phone/chat outreach by an intake specialist within 4 hours to prevent SLA breach.
   * *Standard Customers:* Dynamic, guided digital form with mandatory photo criteria, eliminating unstructured email ping-pong.

### 2.2 Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
* **Target Segments:** Premium Low-Risk Accessories (18%), Premium High-Value Devices (14%), Standard High-Value Devices (21%).
* **Target Activities/Paths:** Replaces generic `Auto eligibility screen` and `Manual eligibility review` with automated attribute-based routing; eliminates unregulated `Auto screen -> Fast approval` for > EUR 400 devices.

### 2.3 Mechanism of Improvement
* **Compliance Restoration:** Enforces R1 and R2 systematically in code. Log fitness rises toward > 0.98, eliminating unauthorized high-value bypasses.
* **Eradication of Wrong-Replacement Losses:** Requiring technical verification for high-value units prevents the EUR 410 replacement errors, slashing the 18–22% high-value complaint rate down to ~5%.
* **Queue Protection:** Diverting 43% of total claim volume (all accessories) completely away from manual review unburdens the 7 manual reviewers. Total review touches drop from 2,600 to ~1,100, driving reviewer utilization safely below the 82% tipping point and stabilizing queue times.

### 2.4 Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
* **Sacrifices High-Value Speed:** High-value devices will no longer achieve 20–28 hour turnaround times; median cycle times for high-value claims will intentionally increase to 48–72 hours to ensure quality and compliance.
* **Leaves Workshop Rework Unresolved:** Does not re-engineer internal workshop repair mechanics (12.5% high-value repair rework remains unaddressed).
* **Administrative Overhead:** Requires continuous maintenance and governance of SKU risk tables and pricing rules.

## 3. Redesign B: Diagnostic-Driven Quality Assurance & Pre-Dispatch Verification

### 3.1 Process Flow Description
Redesign B adopts an industrial quality-engineering paradigm: "Verify upfront, inspect before release." It treats warranty claims as technical diagnostic workflows rather than administrative eligibility reviews.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                 TECHNICAL DIAGNOSTIC INTAKE (ERP-LINKED)                          |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                                            |
                                            v
                         +-------------------------------------+
                         | Structured Fault-Code Entry &       |
                         | Instant Real-Time Part-Price Engine |
                         +-------------------------------------+
                                            |
                                            v
                         +-------------------------------------+
                         | Repair-First Assessment             |
                         | (3 Technicians, Auto-Quoted)        |
                         +-------------------------------------+
                                     /             \
                   [Replacement Route]             [Repair Route]
                            /                               \
                           v                                 v
        +------------------------------------+    +------------------------------------+
        | Mandatory Pre-Dispatch Gate:       |    | Two-Stage High-Value Repair        |
        | Barcode & Serial Match (QC Team)   |    | Quality Inspection (2 Inspectors)  |
        +------------------------------------+    +------------------------------------+
                           |                                 |
                           v                                 v
        +------------------------------------+    +------------------------------------+
        | Verified Dispatch & Close          |    | Quality-Certified Close            |
        +------------------------------------+    +------------------------------------+
```

1. **Diagnostic Intake & Automated Part-Pricing Engine:**
   * Claims are ingested using structured technical fault codes. An integrated enterprise catalog API instantly validates part pricing and availability, completely eliminating the technician quoting backlog (`Manual review -> Repair quote` drops from 22h mean to real-time).
2. **Mandatory Pre-Dispatch Verification Gate:**
   * Before any replacement device or accessory is shipped, it must pass a physical/digital verification scan conducted by the Quality Check team (repurposing the 2 underutilized Quality Inspectors).
   * The scanner matches the customer's exact device model, revision, and regional carrier configuration against the replacement unit barcode, intercepting incorrect parts before carrier handover.
3. **Dual-Stage Repair Quality Inspection:**
   * Reallocates QC inspection capacity to mandate a full functional diagnostic re-test for 100% of repaired high-value devices prior to closure.
4. **Dedicated Technical Complaint Resolvers:**
   * Replaces the daily 45-minute Escalation Board slot with 2 empowered Senior Technical Resolvers who handle reopened cases continuously within 4 hours, satisfying Rule R3 without batch delays.

### 3.2 Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
* **Target Segments:** High-Value Devices (both Premium and Standard, 35% total share), Repaired Units, and Reopened/Escalated Claims.
* **Target Activities/Paths:** `Repair quote`, `Quality check`, `Dispatch replacement -> Quality/compliance close`, `Customer complaint/reopen -> Escalation board`.

### 3.3 Mechanism of Improvement
* **Eradication of Shipping Errors:** Intercepts wrong replacement selections before dispatch, directly eliminating the root cause of the 260 post-dispatch customer complaints (`mean 96.0h latency`) and saving EUR 95 to EUR 410 per incident.
* **Reduction of Repair Rework:** Dual-stage QC inspection suppresses the 12.5% high-value repair rework rate down to < 3%, preventing post-repair returns.
* **Elimination of Quoting Delays:** Automated ERP part-pricing integration removes the manual quoting queue (`p90 60.0h`), shrinking the repair quote approval timeline.
* **Resource Realignment:** Fully utilizes the underworked Quality Check team (2 inspectors at EUR 38/h) to protect customer experience, while replacing the congested Escalation Board with continuous technical resolution.

### 3.4 Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
* **Sacrifices Immediate Dispatch Velocity:** Adding a mandatory pre-dispatch verification gate adds 6 to 12 hours of operational buffer to replacement dispatches.
* **Higher Labor Touch per Case:** Increases the proportion of claims subjected to hands-on technician and inspector verification.
* **Leaves Customer Evidence Ping-Pong Unresolved:** Does not address customer submission friction or abandonment in the 22% Unclear Defect segment.

## 4. Redesign C: Customer-Centric Digital Self-Service & Trust-Based Ex-Post Assurance

### 4.1 Process Flow Description
Redesign C shifts the operating paradigm from front-end gating to decentralized digital self-service backed by algorithmic trust and ex-post auditing.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                                INTERACTIVE SELF-SERVICE SMART PORTAL                              |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
                     |                                                    |
         [Digital-First Customers]                            [Low Digital Literacy]
                     |                                                    |
                     v                                                    v
+-----------------------------------------+              +----------------------------------+
| AI Guided Diagnostics, Video Upload     |              | Assisted Intake Hub              |
| & Serial Barcode Scan                   |              | (Omnichannel Call/Chat Support)  |
+-----------------------------------------+              +----------------------------------+
                     |                                                    |
                     +--------------------------+-------------------------+
                                                |
                                                v
                             +--------------------------------------+
                             | Algorithmic Fraud/Risk Scoring       |
                             +--------------------------------------+
                                           /          \
                     [Score <= Threshold]            [Score > Threshold]
                              /                                \
                             v                                  v
+-----------------------------------------------+   +---------------------------------------+
| Instant Approval & Advance Replacement        |   | Targeted Front-End Risk Review        |
| (Soft Credit Card Hold for High-Value Units)  |   | (Fraud Specialists, 2 FTEs)           |
+-----------------------------------------------+   +---------------------------------------+
                     |                                                  |
                     v                                                  v
+-----------------------------------------------+   +---------------------------------------+
| Carrier Fast-Track Delivery (< 18 hrs)        |   | Standard Fulfillment Route            |
+-----------------------------------------------+   +---------------------------------------+
                     |
                     v
+-----------------------------------------------+
| Ex-Post Reverse Logistics Dock Inspection     |
| (Manual Reviewers Reassigned to Ex-Post Audit)|
+-----------------------------------------------+
                     |
                     v
+-----------------------------------------------+
| Release Card Hold & Final Compliance Close    |
+-----------------------------------------------+
```

1. **Interactive Self-Service Smart Portal:**
   * Customers submit claims via an intelligent mobile web portal featuring interactive symptom-tree troubleshooting, guided camera-based serial number scanning, and automated short video upload.
   * Eliminates incomplete submissions at the source, reducing repeat evidence requests by 45%.
2. **Assisted Intake Fallback Hub:**
   * An omnichannel assisted-intake channel (phone/chat) provides agent-supported submission for customers with low digital literacy, preventing portal-induced abandonment.
3. **Algorithmic Risk Scoring and Advance Replacement:**
   * Automated risk scoring evaluates claim history, customer tenure, device telemetry, and defect characteristics.
   * Low-risk and Premium high-value claims receive immediate advance replacement dispatch upon placing a temporary **soft credit card pre-authorization hold** for the device value. This satisfies financial risk controls and the commercial intent of R1/R2 without front-end queue delays.
4. **Ex-Post Reverse Logistics Dock Audit:**
   * Front-end manual review is eliminated for 75% of claims. The 7 manual reviewers are reallocated to an *Ex-Post Assurance & Returns Audit Team*.
   * When defective devices are returned in prepaid packaging, reviewers scan and inspect them to confirm genuine defects, releasing the credit card hold and executing formal compliance closure (R3 compliant).

### 4.2 Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
* **Target Segments:** Unclear Defect / Missing Evidence segment (22%), Premium and Standard High-Value Devices, Digital-native customers.
* **Target Activities/Paths:** Replaces `Request missing evidence`, `Manual eligibility review`, and front-end `Fraud/risk review` with digital intake and ex-post dock audits.

### 4.3 Mechanism of Improvement
* **Slashes Evidence Latencies and Abandonment:** Interactive guided diagnostics compress the 140-hour median cycle time for unclear defects down to < 24 hours, while assisted intake prevents the 320 abandoned claims.
* **Combines Sub-24h Speed with Asset Protection:** Advance replacement with pre-authorization holds allows Premium customers to receive replacements in < 18 hours while fully mitigating financial leakage and compliance risk.
* **Decongests Front-End Queues:** Shifts the workload from synchronous, time-critical front-end approvals to asynchronous, batch-processed reverse logistics inspections, smoothing labor utilization throughout the week.

### 4.4 Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
* **Customer Friction on Financial Holds:** Some standard customers may object to credit card pre-authorizations for warranty service.
* **Downstream Reverse Logistics Exposure:** High dependency on customer return compliance; requires robust dispute-handling operations for disputed damage assessments upon dock inspection.
* **High Upfront Implementation Investment:** Demands substantial software engineering effort to build the portal, AI diagnostic models, and payment gateway integrations.

## 6. Failure Modes and Displaced Bottlenecks

### 6.1 Redesign A: Segment-Tiered Risk Triage & Guardrails

```
[Intake Triage Engine] ---> (Failure 1: Misclassification / Gaming)
         |
         v
[Auto-Screen Thresholds] ---> (Failure 2: Over-Conservative Rules -> Inflow +35%)
         |
         v
[Displaced Bottleneck] ====> [High-Value Technical & Fraud Review Queues (2 FTEs)]
```

* **Failure Mode 1: Claim Misclassification and Upfront Gaming:** Customers or customer service representatives may intentionally misclassify high-value devices or complex defects as "low-risk accessories" to force claims onto the automated fast-track, bypassing technical review and reintroducing compliance violations.
* **Failure Mode 2: Over-Conservative Rule Thresholds Triggering Manual Inundation:** If auto-screen eligibility parameters are set too conservatively during the two-week update, manual-review inflow could surge by 15% to 35% (as highlighted in the resource data). This would push reviewer utilization beyond the 82% breaking point, re-triggering severe queue delays.
* **Displaced Bottleneck:** The primary bottleneck shifts from the initial manual eligibility screen to the **Fraud/Risk Review Specialists (2 FTEs)** and the **High-Value Technical Review Queue**, particularly during post-marketing campaign surges and on Mondays.

### 6.2 Redesign B: Diagnostic QA & Pre-Dispatch Verification

```
[Dispatch Staging] ---> (Failure 1: High-Volume Queue Explosion at QC Station)
         |
         v
[Repair Diagnostic Flow] ---> (Failure 2: Non-Standard Hardware Quoting Lockup)
         |
         v
[Displaced Bottleneck] ====> [Pre-Dispatch Physical Inspection Station (2 QC FTEs)]
```

* **Failure Mode 1: Pre-Dispatch Inspection Station Congestion:** If daily dispatch volume spikes (e.g., after holiday promotional periods), the 2 Quality Check inspectors may become overwhelmed. The physical verification gate could back up from its planned 6-hour window to > 24 hours, eroding customer goodwill.
* **Failure Mode 2: Diagnostic Catalog Gaps on Legacy/Non-Standard Devices:** If the automated part-pricing engine encounters uncataloged component SKUs or discontinued models, claims will drop into a manual quoting exception state, causing technician quoting delays to spike back toward p90 60.0h.
* **Displaced Bottleneck:** The system bottleneck migrates from downstream customer support reopens directly to the **Pre-Dispatch Quality Inspection Station**, creating a physical queue right before carrier pickup.

### 6.3 Redesign C: Digital Self-Service & Ex-Post Assurance

```
[Customer Self-Service Portal] ---> (Failure 1: Low Digital Literacy -> Abandonment Surge)
              |
              v
[Dock Reverse Logistics] ---> (Failure 2: Return Delinquency & Chargeback Disputes)
              |
              v
[Displaced Bottleneck] ========> [Returns Receiving Dock & Payment Dispute Operations]
```

* **Failure Mode 1: Digital Divide and Abandonment Escalation:** If the self-service web portal is too rigid or unintuitive, customers with low digital literacy or non-standard defects will abandon their claims rather than uploading video evidence. Without an adequate assisted-intake safety net, the baseline 320 abandoned claims could increase significantly.
* **Failure Mode 2: Reverse Logistics Deficit and Dispute Backlog:** Customers may fail to return defective units, return incorrect items, or return units with unverified customer-induced damage. Enforcing soft-hold charges will trigger high-friction credit card chargebacks and customer service escalations.
* **Displaced Bottleneck:** The operational bottleneck shifts entirely out of the front-end intake process and lands on the **Reverse Logistics Inbound Receiving Dock** and the **Billing/Dispute Resolution Team**.

### 7.1 Stage 1: The High-Value Guardrail & Pre-Dispatch Pilot (Weeks 1–4)
* **Target Cohort:** 100% of Premium High-Value Devices (14% of volume, ~700 cases annualized) and a 50% randomized sample of replacement dispatches.
* **Intervention:** 
  1. Implement the programmatic R1/R2 intake guardrail (Redesign A), routing all high-value devices through technical eligibility.
  2. Implement the barcode pre-dispatch verification gate (Redesign B) using the existing 2 underutilized Quality Check inspectors.
* **Primary Objective:** Immediately eliminate EUR 410 wrong-replacement errors, halt compliance infractions, and protect the organization's highest-value customer relationships.

### 7.2 Stage 2: The Digital Evidence Portal & Assisted Intake Pilot (Weeks 5–8)
* **Target Cohort:** 50% randomized traffic of the Unclear Defect / Missing Evidence segment (22% of total volume).
* **Intervention:** Deploy the Smart Mobile Evidence Portal alongside a dedicated phone-assisted intake line (Redesign C).
* **Primary Objective:** Test whether interactive photo/video capture compresses the 140-hour cycle time and reduces the 26% repeated evidence request rate without driving up customer abandonment.

### 7.3 Key Metrics to Monitor (The Balanced Control Dashboard)
To verify that process interventions create true system improvements rather than shifting defects downstream, monitor the following paired metrics:

1. **Velocity & Throughput:**
   * End-to-end cycle time by segment (mean, median, p90).
   * Queue wait times at Manual Review and Fraud/Risk Review.
2. **Quality & Downstream Rework (60-Day Window):**
   * Customer complaint/reopen rate at 30, 60, and 90 days post-closure (critical to capture the 70–96h delayed complaint latency).
   * Frequency of repeated evidence loops (target: reduce 26% baseline down to < 8%).
   * Repaired high-value device rework rate (target: reduce 12.5% baseline down to < 3%).
3. **Regulatory Conformance:**
   * Token-based replay fitness against normative Petri net (target: > 0.98; fitting traces > 95%).
   * Total count of Rule R1, R2, and R3 infractions (target: exactly 0).
4. **Financial Health & Customer Friction:**
   * Net cost per completed claim (including secondary freight, replacement costs, and service credits).
   * Claim abandonment rate (guardrail: must not exceed baseline 7.8% / 390 cases).
   * Customer CSAT / Net Promoter Score (NPS) across Premium and Standard cohorts.

### 7.4 Stop / Go Decision Thresholds

| Decision Threshold | Metric / Condition | Action Required |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RED LIGHT (Stop & Rollback)** | Manual review queue utilization exceeds **85% for > 3 consecutive days**, or queue wait exceeds **24 hours**. | Immediately revert auto-screen rule sensitivity; activate overtime surge capacity. |
| **RED LIGHT (Stop & Rollback)** | Claim abandonment rate in the pilot portal cohort increases by **> 2.0 percentage points** above baseline (9.8%). | Pause self-service portal gating; route all ambiguous cases to the Assisted Intake Hub. |
| **YELLOW LIGHT (Pause & Refine)** | Pre-dispatch verification gate adds **> 18 hours** to total cycle time. | Streamline scanning protocols; reallocate 1 additional FTE from manual review to dispatch inspection. |
| **YELLOW LIGHT (Pause & Refine)** | Repeated evidence request rate fails to drop by at least **25% relative to baseline** (remains > 19.5%). | Redesign digital intake upload prompts and improve dynamic defect diagnostic trees. |
| **GREEN LIGHT (Scale & Expand)** | Token fitness reaches **>= 0.98** with zero R1/R2 violations; High-value complaint rate drops from **22% to <= 6%**; Net claim cost drops by **>= 15%**. | Full production rollout across all customer tiers and product categories (Stage 3). |

### 7.5 Long-Tail Verification: Preventing Downstream Distortion
Because the performance DFG reveals that customer complaints and replacement selection errors take up to **180 hours (p90)** to manifest, pilot evaluation must not conclude upon initial case closure. 
* **60-Day Cohort Hold:** Every pilot batch must be tracked across a full 60-day post-closure window.
* **Paired Counter-Metrics:** Speed gains at intake must be explicitly evaluated against downstream warranty reserve claims, reverse-logistics freight costs, and credit card chargeback rates. A process acceleration is only certified as a genuine improvement if the combined cost of intake, fulfillment, and 60-day downstream rework demonstrates a statistically significant net reduction.
