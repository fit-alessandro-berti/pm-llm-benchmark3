## 1. Normalization Assumptions

1. **Resolution & Rounding Semantics (Pre-Offset):**
   Let $t_{\text{disp}}$ be the displayed local timestamp converted to a point in local time. We first expand $t_{\text{disp}}$ into the recorded clock interval $[c_{\min}, c_{\max}]$ before applying clock offsets or time-zone shifts:
   - **Exact +/- 0.5 s (`HUB`):** $[t_{\text{disp}} - 0.5\text{ s},\; t_{\text{disp}} + 0.5\text{ s}]$.
   - **Floor to minute (`OPS-BER`, `VENDOR-NY`, `LEGACY-BER`):** $[t_{\text{disp}},\; t_{\text{disp}} + 60\text{ s}]$ (representing the half-open continuous minute $[t_{\text{disp}}, t_{\text{disp}} + 59.999\dots\text{ s}]$; closed upper bounds are used for interval arithmetic with explicit note on strict boundary ties).
   - **Round to nearest 10 s (`SENSOR`):** $[t_{\text{disp}} - 5\text{ s},\; t_{\text{disp}} + 5\text{ s}]$.
   - **Round to nearest minute (`MOBILE-LON`):** $[t_{\text{disp}} - 30\text{ s},\; t_{\text{disp}} + 30\text{ s}]$.

2. **Clock-Offset Direction:**
   Given $\text{clock\_offset} = t_{\text{recorded}} - t_{\text{true}} \in [a, b]$, we have $t_{\text{true}} = t_{\text{recorded}} - \text{clock\_offset}$. Applying interval subtraction:
   $$[t_{\text{true},\min},\; t_{\text{true},\max}] = [c_{\min} - b,\; c_{\max} - a]$$
   For example, `OPS-BER` has $[a, b] = [-90\text{ s}, +120\text{ s}]$, so $c_{\min}$ shifts by $-120\text{ s}$ and $c_{\max}$ shifts by $-(-90\text{ s}) = +90\text{ s}$.

3. **Time-Zone Conversion & DST Fold (`T-405`):**
   - `CEST` (`Europe/Berlin` in June) = UTC+2; `EDT` (`America/New_York` in June) = UTC-4; `BST` (`Europe/London` in June) = UTC+1.
   - On `2025-10-26`, `Europe/Berlin` transitions from CEST (UTC+2, fold=0) to CET (UTC+1, fold=1) at 03:00 local time, repeating 02:00–02:59. Without fold metadata, a local timestamp in `02:xx` generates a disjoint union of two intervals ($F_0$ in UTC+2, $F_1$ in UTC+1).
   - **Ingestion-Order Pruning (`T-405`):** Ingestion order places `Alert_Received` (02:50) $<$ `Investigation_Start` (02:05) $<$ `Containment_Complete` (02:42). Because all three events are logged on the *same* `LEGACY-BER` system (or even under independent `[-60s, +60s]` bounds, since $02:50 - 02:05 = 45\text{ min} \gg 2\text{ min}$ max drift), `Alert_Received` at 02:50 **must** be in fold 0 (CEST, ~00:50 UTC) and `Investigation_Start` at 02:05 **must** be in fold 1 (CET, ~01:05 UTC). Otherwise, `Investigation_Start` would precede `Alert_Received` by ~45 minutes. Consequently, `Containment_Complete` at 02:42 following `Investigation_Start` (01:05 UTC) **must** also be in fold 1 (CET, ~01:42 UTC), as fold 0 (~00:42 UTC) would precede `Investigation_Start` by ~23 minutes. Thus, causal/ingestion sequence uniquely resolves the folds to $(F_0, F_1, F_1)$ while preserving the `[-60s, +120s]` minute-floor + clock-offset uncertainty on each event.

4. **Inferred Start Times (`T-404`):**
   `Investigation_Complete` on `HUB` is $[10:34:59.5, 10:35:00.5]$ UTC. The signed work record bounds duration $D \in [18, 25]\text{ min} = [1080, 1500]\text{ s}$ (treated as exact continuous bounds; if the work record itself was rounded to minutes, uncertainty would widen by $\pm 30\text{ s}$—noted in sensitivity). Subtracting intervals:
   $$t_{\text{start}} = [10:34:59.5 - 1500\text{ s},\; 10:35:00.5 - 1080\text{ s}] = [10:09:59.5,\; 10:17:00.5]\text{ UTC}.$$

5. **Mobile Entry Delay (`T-406`):**
   "User may have entered the displayed time up to five minutes late." **Primary Assumption (Occurrence-Shift / Auto-Populated Clock):** In mobile incident apps, users typically tap "Log Start" up to 5 minutes *after* physically starting work, causing the device's current clock to be captured late ($t_{\text{occurrence}} = t_{\text{device\_capture}} - d$, $d \in [0, 300]\text{ s}$). Combined with nearest-minute rounding $[-30\text{ s}, +30\text{ s}]$ and clock offset $[-300\text{ s}, +300\text{ s}]$, the true occurrence shifts earlier: $[-30 - 300 - 300,\; +30 + 300 - 0] = [-630\text{ s},\; +330\text{ s}]$ around 12:14:00 UTC $\rightarrow [12:03:30,\; 12:19:30]$ UTC.
   *Alternative Assumption (Manual Memory Entry):* If the user *typed* a remembered time 5 minutes later, record-creation delay does not shift the displayed timestamp at all ($[12:08:30,\; 12:19:30]$ UTC). Notice that **both** assumptions share the exact same upper bound (`12:19:30` UTC), which governs S1 maximum duration and S2 minimum duration! We track the lower-bound difference explicitly.

6. **Missing Receipt & Causal Truncation (`T-407`):**
   `Alert_Sent` is $[13:59:59.5,\; 14:00:00.5]$ UTC. Network latency $[0, 1200]\text{ s}$ yields an *unconstrained* delivery window of $[13:59:59.5,\; 14:20:00.5]$ UTC, but **no receipt acknowledgment exists** (delivery could have failed or been bypassed via out-of-band phone call).
   - Under **strict evidential semantics**, `Alert_Received` is unverified; any rule requiring it without assuming delivery + causality is `INDETERMINATE` (or `POSSIBLE_VIOLATION` under unverified network bounds).
   - Under **causal-truncation semantics** (assuming the alert *was* delivered and triggered `Investigation_Start` at $[14:16:59.5, 14:17:00.5]$, so $t_{\text{rec}} \le t_{\text{inv}}$), `Alert_Received` truncates to $[13:59:59.5,\; 14:17:00.5]$, forcing $D(\text{Alert\_Received}, \text{Investigation\_Start}) \in [0,\; 1021]\text{ s}$. Because `Alert_Received` is missing and causality is unlogged, we report both the **Causal-Assumed** interval and the **Unconstrained Network** interval, classifying S1 as `POSSIBLE_VIOLATION` (with low confidence / borderline `INDETERMINATE` without delivery proof).

7. **Missing NTP Diagnostics (`T-409`):**
   `OPS-BER` at `18:04` CEST (`16:04` UTC) has floor $[0, 60]\text{ s}$ and weak engineering offset $[-720\text{ s}, +720\text{ s}]$, giving $[15:52:00,\; 16:17:00]$ UTC. Note this permits `Investigation_Start` before `Alert_Received` (`16:00` UTC) by up to 8 minutes if untruncated; even with causal truncation ($t_{\text{inv}} \ge t_{\text{alert}}$), the upper bound remains `16:17:00` UTC.

8. **SLA & Temporal Profile Evaluation:**
   - **Hard SLAs:** Upper-bound rules on duration $D$: S1 $\le 900\text{ s}$ (15m), S2 $\le 2700\text{ s}$ (45m), S3 $\le 129,600\text{ s}$ (36h). Note: Negative feasible durations (from wide clock offsets where causality isn't enforced) satisfy upper-bound SLAs mathematically, though we also note causal truncation $D \ge 0$.
   - **Temporal Profile Baseline ($\zeta = 2$):** Section 3 uses $\zeta = 2$ (standard $2\sigma$ band) as the baseline acceptance region $[\mu - 2\sigma,\; \mu + 2\sigma]$, while Section 4 evaluates $\zeta \in \{1, 2, 3\}$ in full:
     - `P1 (Alert_Received -> Investigation_Start)`: $600 \pm 180\zeta\text{ s}$ ($\zeta=2 \Rightarrow [240, 960]\text{ s}$).
     - `P2 (Investigation_Start -> Containment_Complete)`: $2100 \pm 600\zeta\text{ s}$ ($\zeta=2 \Rightarrow [900, 3300]\text{ s}$).
     - `P3 (Alert_Received -> Containment_Complete)`: $2700 \pm 720\zeta\text{ s}$ ($\zeta=2 \Rightarrow [1260, 4140]\text{ s}$).
     - `P4 (Containment_Complete -> Close_Incident)`: $86400 \pm 21600\zeta\text{ s}$ ($\zeta=2 \Rightarrow [43200, 129600]\text{ s}$).
   - **Missing Lifecycle Events:** Unlogged `Close_Incident` in T-402 through T-407 and T-409 is treated as *not yet occurred / out of extract scope* (omitted from testable pairs rather than `INDETERMINATE`), whereas missing intermediate events (`Investigation_Start` in T-404, `Alert_Received` in T-407) *are* evaluated because downstream events prove the phase occurred.

            base_utc = aware_local.astimezone(dt.timezone.utc)
            # True time = recorded_clock - clock_offset; apply resolution first
            # [base + res_lo - off_hi, base + res_hi - off_lo]
            lo_shift = res_lo - off_hi
            hi_shift = res_hi - off_lo
            # Apply occurrence-shift mobile entry delay if present: true = captured - delay
            if "entry_delay_s" in extra:
                d_lo, d_hi = extra["entry_delay_s"]
                lo_shift -= d_hi
                hi_shift -= d_lo
            t_min = base_utc + dt.timedelta(seconds=lo_shift)
            t_max = base_utc + dt.timedelta(seconds=hi_shift)
            intervals.append((t_min, t_max))

        # Deduplicate identical intervals (when fold=0 and fold=1 don't differ)
        intervals = sorted(list(set(intervals)))
        case_events.setdefault(cid, {})[act] = {
            "intervals": intervals,
            "unverified": False,
            "seq": extra.get("seq"),
            "extra": extra,
        }

    # Post-process special cases:
    # T-404: Infer Investigation_Start from Investigation_Complete - [1080, 1500]s
    if "T-404" in case_events and "Investigation_Complete" in case_events["T-404"]:
        ic_info = case_events["T-404"]["Investigation_Complete"]
        d_lo, d_hi = ic_info["extra"]["infer_start_dur_s"]
        ic_min, ic_max = ic_info["intervals"][0]
        case_events["T-404"]["Investigation_Start"] = {
            "intervals": [(ic_min - dt.timedelta(seconds=d_hi), ic_max - dt.timedelta(seconds=d_lo))],
            "unverified": False,
            "seq": None,
            "extra": {"inferred": True},
        }

    # T-405: Prune DST fold combinations violating ingestion sequence
    if "T-405" in case_events:
        ordered_acts = sorted(
            [(v["seq"], k, v["intervals"]) for k, v in case_events["T-405"].items() if v["seq"] is not None]
        )
        # Find feasible tuple of intervals (i0, i1, i2) where i0_min <= i1_max and i1_min <= i2_max
        valid_combos = []
        for iv0 in ordered_acts[0][2]:
            for iv1 in ordered_acts[1][2]:
                if iv0[0] <= iv1[1]:  # Feasible t0 <= t1
                    for iv2 in ordered_acts[2][2]:
                        if iv1[0] <= iv2[1]:  # Feasible t1 <= t2
                            valid_combos.append((iv0, iv1, iv2))
        for idx, (_, act_name, _) in enumerate(ordered_acts):
            surviving = sorted(list({combo[idx] for combo in valid_combos}))
            case_events["T-405"][act_name]["intervals"] = surviving

    # T-407: Derive Alert_Received proxy from Alert_Sent + [0, 1200]s (with causal truncation at Inv_Start)
    if "T-407" in case_events and "Alert_Sent" in case_events["T-407"]:
        sent_info = case_events["T-407"]["Alert_Sent"]
        lat_lo, lat_hi = sent_info["extra"]["net_latency_s"]
        s_min, s_max = sent_info["intervals"][0]
        inv_max = case_events["T-407"]["Investigation_Start"]["intervals"][0][1]
        rec_min = s_min + dt.timedelta(seconds=lat_lo)
        rec_max = min(s_max + dt.timedelta(seconds=lat_hi), inv_max)
        case_events["T-407"]["Alert_Received"] = {
            "intervals": [(rec_min, rec_max)],
            "unverified": True,  # Flagged because receipt ack is missing
            "seq": None,
            "extra": {"causal_truncated": True},
        }

    return case_events

normalized_events = build_event_intervals(raw_df)

# 4. FEASIBLE DURATION INTERVALS & INTERVAL-BASED CLASSIFICATIONS

def duration_unions(iv_a_list, iv_b_list):
    """Pairwise duration intervals (b - a) in seconds."""
    durs = []
    for a_min, a_max in iv_a_list:
        for b_min, b_max in iv_b_list:
            d_min = (b_min - a_max).total_seconds()
            d_max = (b_max - a_min).total_seconds()
            durs.append((d_min, d_max))
    return sorted(durs)

def classify_two_sided(dur_intervals, band_lo, band_hi, unverified=False):
    if unverified:
        # Also compute conditional classification if delivery is assumed
        cond = classify_two_sided(dur_intervals, band_lo, band_hi, unverified=False)
        return f"INDETERMINATE (cond: {cond})"
    all_inside = all(d_lo >= band_lo and d_hi <= band_hi for d_lo, d_hi in dur_intervals)
    all_outside = all(d_hi < band_lo or d_lo > band_hi for d_lo, d_hi in dur_intervals)
    if all_inside:
        return "CERTAIN_PASS"
    if all_outside:
        return "CERTAIN_VIOLATION"
    return "POSSIBLE_VIOLATION"

def classify_sla(dur_intervals, max_allowed, unverified=False):
    if unverified:
        cond = classify_sla(dur_intervals, max_allowed, unverified=False)
        return f"INDETERMINATE (cond: {cond})"
    if all(d_hi <= max_allowed for _, d_hi in dur_intervals):
        return "CERTAIN_PASS"
    if all(d_lo > max_allowed for d_lo, _ in dur_intervals):
        return "CERTAIN_VIOLATION"
    return "POSSIBLE_VIOLATION"

interval_profile_records = []
for cid, acts in sorted(normalized_events.items()):
    for (act_a, act_b), (mu, sigma) in TEMPORAL_PROFILE.items():
        if act_a in acts and act_b in acts:
            durs = duration_unions(acts[act_a]["intervals"], acts[act_b]["intervals"])
            unv = acts[act_a]["unverified"] or acts[act_b]["unverified"]
            rec = {"case_id": cid, "pair": f"{act_a} -> {act_b}", "durs_s": durs}
            for zeta in (1, 2, 3):
                lo, hi = mu - zeta * sigma, mu + zeta * sigma
                rec[f"interval_z{zeta}"] = classify_two_sided(durs, lo, hi, unverified=unv)
            interval_profile_records.append(rec)

interval_df = pd.DataFrame(interval_profile_records)

def extract_pm4py_violations(conf_result, case_ids):
    """
    Isolated parser for pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile output.
    Across PM4Py 2.7.x+, conformance_temporal_profile returns a list (aligned with
    traces ordered by appearance in the formatted dataframe/event log), where each
    trace element is a list of violating tuple records:
      (act_source, act_target, time_passed_s, zeta_score)
    If a future PM4Py version returns a DataFrame, we handle that branch explicitly.
    """
    violating_pairs = set()
    if isinstance(conf_result, pd.DataFrame):
        for _, r in conf_result.iterrows():
            violating_pairs.add((r["case_id"], f"{r['act_a']} -> {r['act_b']}"))
    else:
        for cid, trace_diags in zip(case_ids, conf_result):
            for diag in trace_diags:
                act_a, act_b = diag[0], diag[1]
                violating_pairs.add((cid, f"{act_a} -> {act_b}"))
    return violating_pairs

**Why Midpoint PM4Py Diagnostics Diverge from Interval Reasoning:**
PM4Py's `conformance_temporal_profile` evaluates point durations against $[\mu - \zeta\sigma, \mu + \zeta\sigma]$. At $\zeta = 2$, collapsing intervals to midpoints causes **false passes** across nearly every uncertain boundary case:
- `T-401 P1` (midpoint ~`855s` $\le 960\text{s}$), `T-403 P1` (midpoint ~`930s` $\le 960\text{s}$), `T-404 P1` (midpoint `810s` $\le 960\text{s}$), `T-405 P1` (midpoint `900s` $\le 960\text{s}$), `T-406 P1` (midpoint `690s` $\le 960\text{s}$), `T-407 P1` (midpoint `511.5s`), `T-408 P2` (midpoint `3300s` right on the boundary), and `T-409 P1` (midpoint `270s` inside `[240, 960]s`) **all** appear compliant under midpoint reduction despite having substantial `POSSIBLE_VIOLATION` (or `INDETERMINATE`) mass! Conversely, `T-402 P1` (`[958.5, 1231.5]s`) has midpoint `1095s > 960s`, masking the tiny `1.5s` feasible pass window at `[958.5, 960]s`.

## 7. Governance Recommendation

To eliminate timestamp ambiguity, unverified lifecycle transitions, and legal/audit disputes across distributed incident-response systems, adopt the following seven-pillar telemetry and enforcement standard:

1. **Dual Timestamp Storage (Canonical UTC + Raw Provenance):**
   - Store all event timestamps in **ISO-8601 UTC with microsecond/millisecond precision** (`YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MM:SS.ffffffZ`), alongside the **immutable raw source string**, IANA time-zone identifier (`Europe/Berlin`, never ambiguous abbreviations alone), and **server ingestion UTC timestamp** (`t_ingested`).
2. **Explicit DST Fold & Offset Logging (`LEGACY-BER` Remediation):**
   - Require every local-time logger that cannot emit native UTC to serialize numeric UTC offset (`+02:00` vs `+01:00`) or PEP-495 `fold  {0, 1}` metadata, plus a monotonic per-source sequence counter (`seq_id`). Never store bare local wall-clock strings across DST transitions.
3. **Source Clock-Offset Telemetry & Drift Bounds (`OPS-BER`, `VENDOR-NY`, `T-409` Remediation):**
   - Attach NTP/PTP synchronization health to event envelopes (`ntp_offset_ms`, `root_dispersion_ms`, `stratum`, `time_since_last_sync_s`). When hourly NTP diagnostics drop (`T-409`), derive formal bounds from hardware oscillator drift rates bounded between adjacent verified sync points rather than ad-hoc engineering guesses. Require external vendors (`VENDOR-NY`) to sign webhook payloads with TLS/HTTP `Date` headers captured at the perimeter gateway (`HUB`).
4. **Explicit Resolution & Rounding Metadata (`SENSOR`, `OPS-BER` Remediation):**
   - Prohibit silent minute-flooring or 10-second bucketing in upstream schemas. Where legacy devices cannot be upgraded, register machine-readable schema attributes (`resolution_unit="1m"`, `rounding_mode="FLOOR"`) in a central Data Contract Registry so downstream analytics automatically construct intervals rather than point estimates.
5. **Lifecycle & Start/Complete Event Semantics (`T-404`, `T-406`, `SENSOR` Remediation):**
   - Enforce transactional lifecycle state transitions (`start` vs `complete` per XES lifecycle standard). Require mobile/UI clients (`MOBILE-LON`) to separate `t_occurred_user_claimed` from `t_captured_device_clock` and `t_received_server`, explicitly flagging retrospective manual entries. Prohibit closing or completing an investigation (`T-404`) without an immutable `Investigation_Start` state-transition event.
6. **Causal Identifiers & Delivery Receipts (`T-407` Remediation):**
   - Propagate W3C Trace Context / correlation IDs (`alert_id`, `parent_event_id`) from `Alert_Sent` through message-broker delivery acknowledgment (`Alert_Received`) to `Investigation_Start`. Outlaw substituting dispatch timestamps for receipt timestamps in SLA calculations.
7. **Interval-Based Enforcement & Evidential Burden Policy:**
   - **Regulatory / Contractual Enforcement (`Hard SLAs`):** Adopt a two-tier policy:
     - **Punitive / Financial SLA Penalties** require **`CERTAIN_VIOLATION`** (100% of feasible interval exceeds the SLA threshold, e.g., `T-402 S1`, `T-408 S2/S3`). Interval overlap (`POSSIBLE_VIOLATION`) is **insufficient** for automated financial penalties when clock/rounding uncertainty originates in internal infrastructure, because the burden of instrumentation rests on the platform operator.
     - **Operational Review & Telemetry Remediation** are triggered on **`POSSIBLE_VIOLATION`** or **`INDETERMINATE`** (`T-401`, `T-403`–`T-407`, `T-409`), routing the case to engineering QA and logging a "Telemetry SLO Breach" against the offending source system.
   - **Statistical Monitoring (`Temporal Profiles`):** Never feed lossy midpoint approximations into point-based conformance checkers without accompanying interval bounds; run anomaly triage at $\zeta = 2$ (`POSSIBLE_VIOLATION` $\to$ warning queue) and escalate high-confidence outliers (`CERTAIN_VIOLATION` at $\zeta = 3$) for root-cause investigation.
