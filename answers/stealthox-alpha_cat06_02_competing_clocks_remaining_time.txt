## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

**Shared stage mechanics**

- **Chemistry (waiting vs service).** Analysts free at ~16:25–17:45 Friday; only ~2–3 h of capacity remains before the 18:00 stop against an ~11 h backlog. *Risk-priority (~45%):* only the urgent job precedes B-417, which starts Fri 16:45–17:45, banks 0.25–1.25 h, pauses over the weekend, and completes **Mon 08:15–12:15** (mode ~09:30–10:30). *FIFO (~55%):* B-417 starts Mon ~12:30–14:00 after the drained backlog and completes **Mon ~14:30–18:00** (mode ~15:30–16:30), with a ~10–15% sub-chance of spilling to Tue 08:00–10:00. **Weekend pause is unavoidable** (best case finishes 18:12 Friday > 18:00). Rerun (p = 0.10–0.20) adds tri(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) h at priority: same-day if the original ends  ~15:00, otherwise an overnight pause  Tue 08:00 + remainder.
- **Microbiology (incubation vs readout).** Technically ready **Sun 12:00–20:00** — entirely inside the weekend, so the *staffing calendar, not incubation*, sets the clock. Authorized weekend readout (p = 0.25–0.45): result **Sun ~13:30–23:00**. Otherwise the result waits, fully grown, until **Mon 08:30–09:30**. Contamination indication (p = 0.05–0.12) adds 20–30 h *continuous* incubation (runs through Sunday/Monday nights) plus a second staffed readout  result **Mon ~10:30–18:30** (if first readout was Sunday) or **Tue ~08:30–16:30** (if Monday 08:30).
- **Parallel join = MAX, not sum.** Join = max(chem, micro). Smooth paths: micro supplies a Mon-08:30 floor (or Sunday result), chemistry usually sets the actual join (**Mon ~10:00–16:00**). Only in contamination paths does microbiology become the binding maximum (Tue). Summing the branches would double-count ~an entire day — the classic error this decomposition avoids.
- **QA waiting vs service.** Queue at join  4 (band 2–7). With two reviewers Monday, wait  1.2 h per batch ahead  **~2.5–7 h**; the 20:00 closure can truncate service and force resumption Tue 08:00 (bimodal: Monday-evening vs Tuesday-morning acceptance). Service itself: median 1.5 h, p80 3.5 h.
- **Rework/investigation (sequential additions on the QA calendar).** Query +4–12 working h; doc-only investigation +8–20 working h; lab investigation +3–7 *calendar* days. These are **sums** appended after first review, unlike the branch join.
- **Final step.** Electronic release +0.25–0.75 h, available 24/7 (so it can land Sunday–Monday nights if reached); rejection recording +0.5–1 h at investigation conclusion.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers (ranked)

1. **QA queue depth at join + QA service variability** — *queue uncertainty (with service-time component).* Controls the 2.5–7 h wait and whether review crosses the 20:00 closure; worth ±half a day and creates the Monday/Tuesday bimodality.
2. **Investigation occurrence and type (doc-only vs laboratory)** — *path uncertainty.* Largest magnitude driver: +8–20 working hours vs +3–7 calendar days; alone moves the tail by ~3–5 days.
3. **Chemistry queue discipline and Friday spillover** — *queue + calendar uncertainty.* Decides whether the join is Mon ~09:00–12:00 (priority) or Mon ~15:00–18:00 (FIFO), cascading into the QA closure boundary.
4. **Temperature-excursion confirmation** — *dependence uncertainty.* A latent common cause that jointly raises rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities; ignoring the correlation understates the joint tail.
5. **Calendar quantization (weekend stops; 18:00/20:00 closures; micro weekend authorization)** — *calendar uncertainty.* Produces discrete jumps (Sun vs Mon readout; Mon evening vs Tue morning release) rather than smooth shifts.
