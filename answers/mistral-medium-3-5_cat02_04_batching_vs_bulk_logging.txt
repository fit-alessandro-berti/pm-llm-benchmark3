# 6. Data-collection redesign

**Prioritized Recommendations**:
1. **Execution Timestamps**:
   - Record `start`/`complete` at **sub-second precision** (e.g., PLCs).
   - For manual steps (G3, G6), log **pause/resume** events.
2. **Recording Timestamps**:
   - Separate `recorded_at` from `complete` to distinguish logging delays.
3. **Source Clocks**:
   - Synchronize all systems (e.g., G8’s scanner vs. safety-check clock).
4. **Resource Identity**:
   - Replace shared accounts (G5) with **badge-level tracking**.
5. **Batch Identifiers**:
   - Explicitly log **batch IDs** (e.g., G1’s fixture load).
6. **Workstation Capacity**:
   - Document constraints (e.g., G2’s single "begin group" button).
7. **Timestamp Precision Metadata**:
   - Store **true accuracy** (not just documented precision).
