# 2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map

*   **Upstream Data Quality:** 
    *   **A6 (Normalization)** depends on accurate **A5 (Missing documents)** handling. If customers submit incomplete docs, A6 fails or generates low-quality inputs for Credit Analysis.
    *   **A4 (Entity Mismatch)** depends on **KYC check** outputs. Without clean entity resolution, **Contract preparation** and **Customer signing** remain blocked.
*   **Legal/Compliance Controls:** 
    *   **A9 (Contract generation)** requires strict adherence to **Committee decision** inputs. Automating contract prep without automated rule checks (A9) risks violating the "Contract matches decision" control (currently 8.5% remediation rate).
    *   **A3 (KYC pre-classifier)** must ensure no cases proceed to **Customer signing** without clearance (violated in 6.5% of cases currently).
*   **Human Escalation Capacity:** 
    *   Automation that reduces routine work (e.g., A4, A6) shifts Compliance Specialists and Credit Analysts to exceptions. With only 3 Compliance FTEs, A4 must route complex mismatches efficiently.
    *   **A6** risks overloading Credit Analysts (9 FTE) if normalization increases clean inflow without increasing analyst capacity.
*   **External Valuation Dependency:** 
    *   **A7 (Triage)** does not reduce external wait time (58h) but can optimize triage. It relies on external valuer API availability.
*   **Core-Banking Integration Constraints:** 
    *   Only two modules can be deeply integrated in the first six months. **A1, A9, and A11** typically require deep core banking access for registration, contract terms, and activation. Priority must be given to modules controlling data integrity over those merely capturing frequency.

# 3. Portfolio Alternatives

### Portfolio 1: Quick-Win (Frequency-Focused)
*   **Modules:** A1, A2, A5, A10
*   **Optimizes:** Immediate volume reduction for Back Office (Register), customer satisfaction (Signing/Nudges).
*   **Sacrifices:** Compliance (no control-strengthening module), Critical path flow (ignores A4 entity mismatch). Risks accelerating bottlenecks where A1 feeds faster into A3/A4 queues.

### Portfolio 2: Bottleneck & Synchronization
*   **Modules:** A4, A6, A7, A8
*   **Optimizes:** Critical path (A4 waits 31h), analyst load (A6), and pack assembly (A8). Reduces end-to-end cycle time significantly.
*   **Sacrifices:** Control integrity (none of these explicitly strengthen rules like A9). Requires careful resource planning for Compliance Specialists.

### Portfolio 3: Compliance & Control-First (Recommended Hybrid)
*   **Modules:** A4, A6, A9, A5
*   **Optimizes:** Critical path clearance (A4), Analyst capacity (A6), and Risk/Control compliance (A9). A5 ensures clean input for A6.
*   **Sacrifices:** Initial user-facing automation (A1 registration, A10 signing). Requires deeper integration effort in first half of Year 1.
*   **Constraint Check:** Includes A9 (Control-strengthening). Includes 4 modules max.

# 4. Expected Process-Level Effects

### Portfolio 1 (Quick-Win)
*   **Cycle Time:** Decreases slightly (3-5 min per case), but total lead time unchanged due to downstream waits.
*   **Waiting Time:** **Increases** at Compliance and Credit queues as upstream speed increases. A1 reduces 0.2h wait at entry but blocks downstream queues.
*   **Rework:** Unchanged (A2 and A5 do not fix entity mismatch or contract remediation).
*   **Control Violations:** Potential increase as throughput overwhelms manual checks.

### Portfolio 2 (Bottleneck)
*   **Cycle Time:** Significant reduction (A4 reduces 31h wait; A8 reduces 26h wait).
*   **Waiting Time:** Reduces median wait before **Credit analysis** and **Contract preparation**.
*   **Rework:** Reduced via better artifact reconciliation (A8).
*   **Resource Demand:** High risk on Credit Analysts (A6) if upstream document quality isn't managed.

### Portfolio 3 (Compliance & Control-First)
*   **Cycle Time:** Reduced by clearing the A4 critical path blocker (82% of critical path).
*   **Waiting Time:** Stabilizes KYC to Contract flow; A5 reduces 18h Financial Review wait by forcing complete docs upfront.
*   **Rework:** Substantially reduced (A9 fixes 8.5% contract loops; A4 fixes 38% exception loops).
*   **Handovers:** Reduces handover friction between Compliance and Legal (A4 -> A9).
*   **Resource Demand:** Balances Credit Analyst load (A6) and Legal Ops load (A9), reducing month-end spikes.

# 5. Risks and Compensating Controls

| Risk Introduced | Compensating Control |
| :--- | :--- |
| **False Negatives (KYC):** Automation A3 missing a bad actor. | **Human Escalation:** A3 routes "unknown confidence" cases to senior specialists; mandatory audit trail. |
| **Four-Eyes Violation:** A6/A8 allowing analyst to complete pack without Committee separation. | **System Rule Enforcement:** A8 blocks pack assembly unless Credit Analyst and RM IDs differ; flag High-exposure cases. |
| **Model Drift:** A9 contract rules becoming outdated. | **Quarterly Review:** Legal Ops reviews rule library updates; "change request" workflow required. |
| **Overload of Analysts:** A6 increasing volume faster than A6 capability. | **Capping Logic:** A6 limits normalization rate to analyst utilization cap (e.g., 80%); triggers alerts on queue size. |
| **Contract Remediation Loops:** A9 generating contracts that still mismatch decision. | **Dual-Check:** A9 generates draft, requires RM to validate against decision data before commit. |

# 6. Recommended Phased Rollout

**Year 1 Budget: 4 Modules (A4, A5, A6, A9)**

### Phase 1 (Months 1-6): Foundation & Integration
*   **Modules:** A9 (Contract Generation) and A6 (Normalization).
*   **Integration:** Deep integration 1: Core Banking (A9 for contract rules/decision data). Deep integration 2: Data Lake (A6 for statement extraction).
*   **Monitoring:** 
    *   Contract remediation rate (Target: <5%).
    *   Credit Analyst utilization (Target: <85%).
*   **Gate Criteria:** A9 rules validated by Legal; A6 extraction accuracy >90% on sample dataset.

### Phase 2 (Months 7-12): Flow & Control
*   **Modules:** A4 (Entity Mismatch) and A5 (Missing Docs).
*   **Integration:** API-based portal nudges (A5) and internal compliance system (A4).
*   **Monitoring:**
    *   Critical path wait times (Target: <20h before Contract).
    *   Customer document submission completeness (Target: >95%).
*   **Gate Criteria:** A4 resolution rate >60% automated; A5 customer feedback positive.

# 7. Final Recommendation

**Recommended Portfolio:** Portfolio 3 (Compliance & Control-First): **A4, A5, A6, A9.**

**Trade-Off Argument:**
Senior management’s preference to automate the highest-frequency activity (A1: Registration) would technically save the most direct labor hours but fails on process-level reasoning. Automation without downstream capacity planning acts as a "flow accelerator" that exacerbates existing bottlenecks. Automating A1 without addressing **A4 (Resolve entity mismatch)** or **A6 (Normalize financial statements)** would simply push applicants faster into the 31-hour entity mismatch queue and the 18-hour document review queue, potentially increasing total cycle time due to queue buildup and error discovery.

**A4** is prioritized over A1 because it sits on the critical path for 82% of its cases. Automating it reduces the longest wait (31h) and prevents blocking **Contract preparation**.

**A9** is selected to satisfy the compliance requirement. It directly reduces the 8.5% contract remediation loop and enforces the "Contract matches Decision" control, preventing financial exposure.

**A6** and **A5** are paired to ensure Credit Analysts are not overloaded by poor-quality documents. Without A5 ensuring complete docs, A6 (Normalization) would fail or increase rework, violating the resource utilization constraints.

This portfolio balances immediate control reinforcement with systemic flow improvement, adhering to the constraint on deep integrations by focusing Phase 1 on high-value internal systems rather than customer-facing registration automation.
