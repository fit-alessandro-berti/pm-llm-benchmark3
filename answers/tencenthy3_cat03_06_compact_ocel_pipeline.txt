Section 6: Modeling alternatives.
At least three defensible alternative E2O policies:
1. Source-minimal relations: only the most direct objects per event (e.g., Create Order->Order; Add Item->Item only, not Order; Reserve->Items only; Pack->Package only; Invoice->Invoice only; etc.). Let O2O propagate all else. This yields sparser E2O, discovery relies on O2O; reduces relation inflation but hides operational sharing (e.g., Pack P1 would not directly show Order convergence).
2. Full operational involvement: add direct E2O from every event to all objects transitively involved (e.g., Record Payment also linked to O100/O200; Dispatch linked to Orders/Items). Maximizes direct visibility but creates relation inflation and risks confusing analytical propagation with source facts.
3. O2O-propagated analytical relations: keep source E2O minimal but create a separate analytical relation table (or annotate) that adds derived E2O edges for analysis only, not in source `relations`. This separates facts from views; discovery on analytical graph shows full convergence but raw OCEL remains clean.
