## 8. Falsification and additional data
Observations that would weaken top-ranked batch synchronization explanation:
* Batch start times coincide with fixed operator shift changes independent of job readiness.
* RACK-7 cleaning time 12:55-14:35 is mandatory and always occurs between batches, making idle inevitable.
* J1 and J2 readiness timestamps are recorded before component staging completes, so readiness is not trustworthy.
* B1 start 12:05 is triggered by a quality hold on J3 not captured in object log.
* Shipment dispatch times are driven by carrier cut-off, not partner packing.
* FIX-A release at 11:40 is actually a logging delay; tool was free earlier.

Additional fields or event types to discriminate hypotheses:
* Explicit Wait for Batch, Wait for Tool, Wait for Shipment activities with start/end timestamps.
* Tool reservation events and actual physical possession timestamps for FIX-A,RACK-7,CAL-3.
* Batch release policy parameters: minimum batch size, max lateness, scheduled start windows.
* Lot event reason codes and quality test results linking to ComponentLot events.
* Shipment partner deadline and carrier cut-off timestamps per Shipment object.
* Supervisor override audit trail with reason and timestamp for J2 lot bypass.
* Component staging completion timestamp separate from job ready timestamp.
