## 5. Rebound and displacement risks
* Batching rebound: Capping batch wait reduces energy per item efficiency and may increase obsolescence if large batches are forced later.
* Transport displacement: Shifting Standard-stock Service-contract from air to ground increases parcel_ground volume, potentially raising peak congestion and late delivery during disruptions.
* Consolidation wait rebound: 12-24h consolidation wait can push non-urgent orders into expedited air to meet promised lead time, increasing transport emissions.
* Packaging trade-off: Enforcing reinforced packaging per Q1 reduces damage returns but doubles package_material_kg and increases transport emissions for heavy parcels.
* Rework displacement: Improving inspection quality gates raises inspection time and energy, which may increase batch wait downstream and late delivery.
* Customer behavior: Carbon-aware promise date negotiation for Regular MTO may shift demand to Service-contract tier, increasing overall transport emissions.
* Obsolescence displacement: Larger batches reduce energy per item but increase inventory hold and 6% obsolescence when forecasts are wrong, creating hidden material waste.
* Dedicated van sharing risk: Replacing dedicated vans with shared emergency lanes reduces empty return trips but may reduce resilience for true Critical-downtime during carrier disruption.

## 6. Balanced recommendation
A coherent package is S4 + S5 + S2 with caps + S3 + S6 + S7 + S9 + S12.

* Protect service for Critical-downtime by splitting true downtime from precautionary urgent and keeping dedicated van with explicit SP2 justification. This avoids unjustified 52 kgCO2e/order transport for precautionary cases while preserving 3% late delivery for true downtime.
* Reduce transport emissions for Standard-stock without unacceptable service loss via priority pick + ground for Service-contract and twice-daily consolidated trucks for non-urgent stock, governed by SP1 >85% on-time probability and R1 alternative route.
* Address production impact through dynamic batching with segment-specific wait caps to avoid the 1.7x late-delivery multiplier from heat-treatment batch wait and the 6% obsolescence from oversized batches, accepting modest energy per item increase.
* Improve quality and material efficiency by shifting MTO to CNC_A when slack allows, enforcing packaging-route compatibility, and adding inspection gates for high-rework combos to cut rework-driven energy and scrap.
* Govern all changes with a mandatory sustainability exception log for SP1/SP2/Q1 overrides to prevent rebound and enable learning.

This balances lower transport kgCO2e and material scrap with stable median cycle times and late delivery for each segment, and maintains resilience via alternative routes and justified exceptions.

## 7. Validation and governance plan
* Conformance monitoring: Monthly process mining checks for SP1 use of ground/consolidated when promised lead time allows, SP2 dedicated van justification event presence, and Q1 reinforced packaging for MTO >8kg. Track violation rates by segment.
* Segment-level service monitoring: Median and p90 cycle time, late delivery % for Standard-stock Regular, Standard-stock Service-contract, Made-to-order Regular/Service-contract, Critical-downtime Service-contract. Compare pre/post with control limits.
* Sustainability KPIs: Energy kWh/order, transport kgCO2e/order, material_scrap_kg/order, package_material_kg/order, rework_flag rate, batch wait hours, partial shipment share. Report by path and segment.
* Exception governance: Sustainability exception log reviewed weekly; overrides must record business reason, estimated impact on service/quality/resilience, and post-delivery outcome. Use for continuous refinement of recommender thresholds.
* Resilience check: Simulated carrier disruption scenario to verify at least one alternative route remains available for Service-contract per R1 after consolidation changes.
* Feedback loop: Quarterly DFG and performance path update to detect displacement, e.g., increase in air use after consolidation or rise in packaging rework after routing changes.
