## 1. Normalization Assumptions

Interval construction is two-stage. A displayed timestamp is first expanded to a **recorded-clock interval** using the source’s rounding or measurement rule. Clock correction is applied after that. Source metadata define `clock_offset = recorded_clock − true_clock` with offset in `[a, b]`, so every recorded instant `R` maps to true time `R − offset`. For a recorded interval `[R_lo, R_hi]` the true interval is `[R_lo − b, R_hi − a]`. Offsets of different systems are treated as independent. Midpoints are never used as the primary event time.

Rounding and resolution:

- **HUB**: displayed second `T` has measurement uncertainty `±0.5 s`, then offset `[-1, +1] s`, so true time is `[T − 1.5 s, T + 1.5 s]`.
- **Floor to the minute** (OPS-BER, VENDOR-NY, LEGACY-BER): displayed `HH:MM` means recorded clock in `[HH:MM:00, HH:MM+1min)`.
- **SENSOR**, nearest 10 s: displayed `T` means recorded clock in `[T − 5 s, T + 5 s]`.
- **MOBILE-LON**, nearest minute: displayed `T` means recorded clock in `[T − 30 s, T + 30 s]`.

Time zones use IANA rules. In June 2026: `Europe/Berlin = CEST = UTC+2`, `America/New_York = EDT = UTC−4`, `Europe/London = BST = UTC+1`. Conversion is applied to the recorded-clock interval before offset expansion (equivalent for a pure duration offset).

**DST fold (T-405).** On 2025-10-26, Berlin local `02:00–02:59` occurs twice: first as CEST (`UTC+2` → `00:00–00:59Z`), then as CET (`UTC+1` → `01:00–01:59Z`). Each folded stamp is a **union of two UTC intervals**. Ingestion order is treated as occurrence order (`Alert_Received` before `Investigation_Start` before `Containment_Complete`). That constraint eliminates seven of eight fold triples and leaves only AR in the first pass and IS/CC in the second pass. The surviving stamps are still minute-floor plus `±60 s` offset; they are not exact. If ingestion order were refused as a causal constraint, T-405 would be indeterminate.

**Inferred start (T-404).** `Investigation_Start` is not logged. A signed duration of 18–25 minutes is treated as a true-time bound on `Investigation_Complete − Investigation_Start`. Combined with the HUB interval on completion this yields `IS ∈ [IC_lo − 25 min, IC_hi − 18 min]`. The duration record is not given extra clock error.

**T-406 mobile entry delay.** The extra “up to five minutes late” is treated as **late recording of occurrence**, not as a shift of only the audit stamp. After minute-rounding, occurrence on the phone clock is up to 300 s earlier than the entered display interval; clock offset `[-300, +300] s` is then applied. The alternative — delay affects only record provenance, not occurrence — is material for S2 and is reported in §4. Primary results use the occurrence-shift reading (more conservative for containment delay).

**T-407.** `Alert_Sent` is not `Alert_Received`. Delivery latency `[0, 20] min` without an ack does not prove receipt. The causal rule `receipt ≤ investigation start` is plausible and not logged. Primary S1 / AR-profile calls are **INDETERMINATE**. Conditional intervals under “delivered within 20 min” and under that plus `AR ≤ IS` are shown but not treated as certain evidence.

**T-409.** Daily NTP bounds are missing. The weak engineering estimate `[-12, +12] min` **replaces** the table bound `[-90, +120] s`. If that estimate is not accepted as a hard bound, IS is unbounded and T-409 S1 / AR→IS become indeterminate.

**SLA vs profile.** S1–S3 are one-sided legal maxima (0 s floor is not required for a pass; a negative delay still satisfies “no later than”). The temporal profile is a two-sided statistical band `average ± zeta · stdev`. Primary table uses **tolerance 0** and **zeta = 2**. Classifications:

- `CERTAIN_PASS`: every feasible duration satisfies the rule/band.
- `CERTAIN_VIOLATION`: every feasible duration fails (for the profile: the interval lies wholly below or wholly above the band).
- `POSSIBLE_VIOLATION`: the feasible set meets both outcomes.
- `INDETERMINATE`: missing activity, unaccepted causal leap, or unbounded/untrusted clock.

If feasible time is a union, the union is preserved. Joint constraints (T-405 folds; optional T-407 causality) are applied before duration arithmetic. Otherwise duration of `B − A` is `[B_lo − A_hi, B_hi − A_lo]`.

Material assumptions: T-405 occurrence-order from ingest; T-406 delay-as-occurrence; T-407 refusal to equate send with receive; T-409 use of the weak offset as a bound; T-404 duration as true-time.

Findings that actually change enforcement posture:

- **T-402 S1** is the only certain SLA miss that **softens** under a 2-minute grace (certain → possible). It never becomes a certain pass by +5 min.
- **T-401 S1** is the cleanest “possible only because of OPS-BER granularity” result: +2 min grace makes it a certain pass without touching the evidence.
- **T-404 S2 and T-408 IS→CC (zeta=2)** are 3-second knife-edges from HUB ±1.5 s. They should not drive policy on their own.
- **T-402 AR→CC and T-408 AR→CC** are certain profile violations at zeta=1 and certain passes at zeta=2. Zeta is a policy choice, not a fact.
- **T-408 CC→CI** is a certain profile violation at zeta=1 and 2 and a pass at 3; the SLA miss is stable. Do not let a large zeta wash out a 36-hour legal cap.
- **T-406 S2** changes more from the entry-delay semantics than from zeta.
- **T-403 AR→IS at zeta=3** remains possible by 1.5 s. Calling that a pass would be a silent midpoint-style rounding of the evidence.

@dataclass(frozen=True)
class Interval:
    """Closed-open when end_exclusive, otherwise closed."""

    start: datetime
    end: datetime
    end_exclusive: bool = False

    def lo(self) -> datetime:
        return self.start

    def hi(self) -> datetime:
        return self.end - timedelta(microseconds=1) if self.end_exclusive else self.end

    def midpoint(self) -> datetime:
        a = self.lo()
        b = self.hi()
        return a + (b - a) / 2

    def __str__(self) -> str:
        br = ")" if self.end_exclusive else "]"
        return f"[{self.start.isoformat()}, {self.end.isoformat()}{br}"


def apply_offset(recorded: Interval, off_lo: float, off_hi: float) -> Interval:
    """true = recorded - offset, offset in [off_lo, off_hi] seconds."""
    return Interval(
        recorded.lo() - timedelta(seconds=off_hi),
        recorded.hi() - timedelta(seconds=off_lo),
        end_exclusive=False,
    )


def hub(t: datetime) -> Interval:
    rec = Interval(t - timedelta(milliseconds=500), t + timedelta(milliseconds=500))
    return apply_offset(rec, -1.0, 1.0)


def floor_minute_local(local_naive: datetime, zone, fold: Optional[int] = None) -> Interval:
    kw = dict(tzinfo=zone)
    if fold is not None:
        local = datetime(
            local_naive.year, local_naive.month, local_naive.day,
            local_naive.hour, local_naive.minute, 0, 0, tzinfo=zone, fold=fold,
        )
    else:
        local = local_naive.replace(second=0, microsecond=0, tzinfo=zone)
    rec = Interval(local.astimezone(UTC), local.astimezone(UTC) + timedelta(minutes=1), True)
    return rec


def nearest_minute_local(local_naive: datetime, zone) -> Interval:
    local = local_naive.replace(second=0, microsecond=0, tzinfo=zone)
    rec = Interval(local.astimezone(UTC) - timedelta(seconds=30), local.astimezone(UTC) + timedelta(seconds=30))
    return rec


def nearest_10s_utc(t: datetime) -> Interval:
    return Interval(t - timedelta(seconds=5), t + timedelta(seconds=5))


def duration_bounds(a: Interval, b: Interval) -> tuple[float, float]:
    dmin = (b.lo() - a.hi()).total_seconds()

def classify_sla(dmin: float, dmax: float, limit: float) -> str:

    return "POSSIBLE_VIOLATION"

    """Isolate version-dependent PM4Py diagnostic shapes."""

    if isinstance(diag, pd.DataFrame):

    if isinstance(diag, dict):
        try:
            return pd.DataFrame(diag)
        except Exception:
            return pd.DataFrame([{"raw": repr(diag)}])
    if isinstance(diag, (list, tuple)):
        if not diag:
            return pd.DataFrame()
        if isinstance(diag[0], pd.DataFrame):

        try:
            return pd.DataFrame(diag)

            return pd.DataFrame({"raw": [repr(x) for x in diag]})

The midpoint log is a collapsed statistic. It will typically **pass** T-401 S1 (~855 s), T-404 S1 (~810 s), T-405 S1 (exactly 900 s), T-406 S1 (~690 s), and T-409 S1 (~270 s), all of which are interval **possible** SLA or order problems. PM4Py, seeing only those points, cannot report those possibles. It will agree with the interval layer on T-402 S1 and T-408 S2/S3, where even the interval minimum is past the cut. That agreement is coincidental tightness of evidence, not a reason to treat PM4Py as an uncertainty engine.

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency

| Result | Minimum additional evidence that would make it certain | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| T-401 S1 POSSIBLE | OPS-BER second-resolution stamp, or that hour’s NTP residual inside ~±15 s rather than `[-90, +120] s` | stronger synchronization / resolution |
| T-401 AR→IS possible (zeta=2) | Same, or a documented zeta≥3 policy plus the same tighter IS stamp | synchronization or policy |
| T-402 S2 POSSIBLE | Second-resolution IS, or NTP showing offset ≥ −30 s so that IS cannot reach 09:16Z | stronger synchronization |
| T-402 AR→IS possible at zeta=2/3 | Not resolvable into a pass: even the minimum 958.5 s is outside zeta=1 and only 1.5 s inside zeta=2. A zeta≥3 **and** a tighter early bound on IS would be required for a certain profile pass | statistical policy + synchronization |
| T-403 S1 / AR→IS possible | Vendor receipt ack with UTC seconds and a measured offset, or HUB-side `Alert_Received` | stronger synchronization; this is an external source |
| T-404 S1 possible | Logged `Investigation_Start`, or a signed duration whose upper bound is ≤ 15 min minus AR uncertainty (~14.9 min) | missing activity or tighter duration evidence |
| T-404 S2 possible (3 s) | Either treat HUB ±1.5 s as non-enforcement noise, or log IS directly so the 25 min start cannot pair with a +1.5 s CC | missing activity / clock policy |
| T-405 S1 / AR→IS possible | Fold indicator (CEST vs CET) on each LEGACY row, or UTC storage from the source | DST semantic evidence; sync already secondary |
| T-406 S1 possible | Device occurrence time (not user text), NTP for that device, and a statement that entry delay is provenance-only | synchronization + semantic evidence |
| T-406 S2 possible | Same, **or** an explicit rule that user delay does not move occurrence (that alone makes S2 certain pass) | semantic evidence |
| T-407 S1 / AR profiles INDETERMINATE | Receipt acknowledgment (or HUB `Alert_Received`). Latency alone is not enough. A logged causal id linking the sent alert to the investigation would justify `AR ≤ IS` but still needs a receipt time | missing activity + semantic/causal evidence |
| T-408 IS→CC possible at zeta=2 (3 s) | Nothing operational: this is HUB quantization. A zeta=3 policy makes it a certain pass; zeta=1 makes it a certain violation | policy, not new evidence |
| T-409 S1 / AR→IS possible | That hour’s NTP residual, or a HUB/OPS second stamp for IS. If the ±12 min note is withdrawn as a bound, the result is indeterminate until a bound exists | stronger synchronization; without it the weak estimate is not enough for certainty |

“Interval overlap” is not missing evidence; it is the correct state given the evidence. Overlap becomes certainty only when one side of the duration interval is cut off by a tighter stamp, a trusted offset, a missing activity, or an explicit semantic rule.

## 7. Governance Recommendation

Store **UTC instants** as the system of record, with the original source timestamp, source time zone, and UTC offset **at the moment of the event** retained beside them. Do not overwrite raw values with converted or rounded displays. Persist resolution metadata (`second`, `minute-floor`, `nearest-10s`, `user-entered`) and lifecycle (`start` vs `complete`) on every event. SENSOR already marks completion; HUB should do the same for investigation and containment.

Collect **source offset telemetry** (NTP residual, step history) on the same cadence as the tightest SLA you intend to enforce. A 15-minute S1 cannot be decided on a clock whose hourly bound is ±12 minutes (T-409) or on a phone with ±5 minutes plus unmodelled entry delay (T-406). When diagnostics are missing, mark the stream `offset_unbounded` and treat SLA tests as non-evaluable, not as passes.

**DST:** never log local civil time in a fold without `fold`/`is_dst` and a monotonic UTC companion. LEGACY-BER T-405 is the failure mode. Prefer UTC-only writers through the transition.

**Causal provenance:** give every alert a delivery id; record `Alert_Sent`, `Alert_Received`, and the investigation that consumed that id as separate activities. T-407 is not an S1 case until receipt exists. Inferring start from a duration range (T-404) is allowed only if the duration artifact is stored and the inferred interval, not a midpoint, is what conformance sees.

**Enforcement policy.** Do not treat interval overlap as a proven breach. Recommended ladder:

1. **CERTAIN_VIOLATION** on high-trust clocks (HUB–HUB, or any pair whose interval minimum exceeds the SLA after published offset bounds): eligible for automatic enforcement.
2. **POSSIBLE_VIOLATION**: queue for review; request better stamps before penalty. Grace of 2 minutes is enough to clear several granularity artefacts (T-401 S1, T-402 S2, T-404 S2) and is **not** enough to clear T-402 S1 or T-408 S2/S3 — which is the desired separation.
3. **INDETERMINATE**: not a pass. Block closure or mark `sla_not_evaluable` until receipt, fold, or offset evidence arrives.
4. Never use midpoint logs or PM4Py temporal-profile deviations as the legal record. They are exploratory diagnostics on a lossy projection.
5. Keep SLA and temporal profile as separate controls. A large zeta must not relax S3. A statistical pass must not excuse a certain 36-hour miss.

Those controls — UTC storage, raw retention, offset telemetry, resolution and lifecycle fields, DST fold tags, causal ids, and an overlap-is-not-proof rule — are the minimum under which the classifications above could be defended rather than merely computed.
