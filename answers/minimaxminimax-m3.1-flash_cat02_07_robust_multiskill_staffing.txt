# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Observations from the supplied process

- Every case requires `Intake` and `Validation`.
- `M`, `E`, and `S` cases require one diagnostic activity.
- `ME` requires Mechanical and Electrical; `ES` requires Electrical and Software.
- A complex case may run its two branches in parallel or sequentially.
- Parallel complex cases incur 8 minutes of coordination before Validation.
- A resource change between activities costs 7 minutes; a move between the Mechanical family and the Electrical/Software family costs another 5 minutes unless the same cross-skilled resource retains the case.
- A resource changing diagnostic family between consecutive cases incurs 5 minutes of setup.
- P1 work may preempt lower-priority diagnostic work once per interrupted case, with a restart charge of 10% of the remaining active service time.
- Overtime is capped at 30 minutes per resource.
- The base staffing pattern is temporally mismatched: Farah leaves at 12:00, Deepa arrives at 10:00, Emil arrives at 12:00, and Chen covers several scarce skills.

## Modeling assumptions

These assumptions are needed because the prompt gives ranges and expected counts rather than a complete stochastic model.

1. **Arrival process.** The two-hour arrival figures are interpreted as expected arrival counts. The Python model approximates them with Poisson counts and uniform arrival times within each block. This assumption affects queueing variability but not the offered-load calculations.
2. **Service distributions.** Within each ordinary or complex-tail range, service time is treated as uniformly distributed. For activity \(a\),
   \[
   \mu_a=(1-p_a)\frac{o_{a,L}+o_{a,U}}2+
   p_a\frac{t_{a,L}+t_{a,U}}2.
   \]
   This gives Intake 6.86, Mechanical 26.30, Electrical 33.00, Software 38.00, and Validation 16.62 base minutes.
3. **Tail independence.** The tail indicator is drawn independently for each activity. U4 doubles only the Mechanical, Electrical, and Software tail probabilities. Intake and Validation retain their stated probabilities.
4. **Priority and case type.** Because only marginal percentages are supplied, priority and case type are assumed independent within a block. A real deployment should estimate their joint distribution.
5. **Resource productivity.** A multiplier \(m\) means \(m\) elapsed resource minutes are required for one base service minute. Capacity is also reported below in “standard minutes,” equal to available clock time divided by the applicable multiplier.
6. **Breaks and shifts.** Breaks are unavailable time. Shifts are assumed to repeat on the following day so that late cases can be evaluated against the 24-hour P3 target. If shifts do not repeat, cases remaining after 20:00 must instead be counted as unresolved/failed.
7. **Overtime.** Overtime is used only when work is waiting and only up to 30 minutes per resource per day. It is not included in the scheduled-capacity ratios in Section 2.
8. **Preemption.** Only active diagnostic work is preempted. If \(r\) active minutes remain, the resumed task receives \(\lceil0.10r\rceil\) additional minutes. The interrupted case is not preempted again.
9. **Queueing and elapsed delay.** SLA and reported delay start at case arrival and end at Validation completion. Waiting for branch completion, handover, setup, coordination, breaks, and shifts is therefore included.
10. **Parallelism.** Parallel execution is used only when two distinct eligible resources can start the two branches without forcing one branch to wait. The expected branch-duration saving must exceed the 8-minute coordination charge, plus any policy-specific safety margin.

## Decision variables

Operational decisions include:

- assignment of each activity to an eligible resource;
- routing of complex branches to parallel or sequential execution;
- sequencing of complex branches;
- reservation of capacity for P1;
- preemption and restart decisions;
- use of handover-prone cross-skill resources;
- use of the 30-minute overtime allowance;
- age, workload, and SLA-risk thresholds for overflow.

## Objective formulations

### Objective A: mean/tail delay and SLA risk

A normalized objective could be

\[
J_{\text{tail}}
=
E[D]
+\lambda_1 E\!\left[\frac{(D-120)_+}{120}\mid P1\right]
+\lambda_2 E\!\left[\frac{(D-480)_+}{480}\mid P2\right]
+\lambda_3 E\!\left[\frac{(D-1440)_+}{1440}\mid P3\right]
+\lambda_4 \operatorname{CVaR}_{0.90}(D),
\]

where \(D\) is arrival-to-Validation elapsed time. The SLA shortfall terms prevent a policy from appearing good merely because it minimizes the mean.

### Objective B: fairness and robustness across scenarios

For scenario \(s\), policy \(p\), and resource \(r\), let \(u_{s,p,r}\) be utilization, \(J_{s,p}\) a Jain fairness index, and \(A_{s,p,q}\) SLA attainment for priority \(q\). A robust objective is

\[
J_{\text{robust}}
=
\max_s
\left[
w_1\max_q(0.90-A_{s,p,q})
+w_2\max_r(0.90-u_{s,p,r})
\right]
+\gamma\sum_s(1-J_{s,p})
+\eta\sum_s \operatorname{CVaR}_{0.90}(D_{s,p}).
\]

Robustness here means good worst-case and near-worst-case behavior over Base, U1-U5, not merely superior Base-case mean delay.

# 3. Candidate policies

## Policy 1: strict specialization with fixed queues

### Eligibility

Use fixed activity queues:

- Intake: Ada, Ben, Farah.
- Mechanical: Ada, Ben, Deepa.
- Electrical: Ada, Chen, Deepa, Emil.
- Software: Chen, Emil, Farah.
- Validation: Ben, Chen, Deepa, Emil.

Resources do not take work outside these queues. Within a queue, P1 is dispatched before P2, which is dispatched before P3; within a priority class, dispatch is first-come-first-served.

### Parallelization and preemption

- ME and ES are always sequential.
- ME follows Mechanical then Electrical; ES follows Electrical then Software.
- A common cross-skilled resource is retained where possible.
- P1 can preempt a lower-priority diagnostic task only in the same fixed queue.
- Preemption occurs only when the P1 case otherwise has no immediately eligible resource and is projected to miss the 120-minute target.

### Handover, setup, and overflow

- Handover and setup penalties are accepted as the price of fixed specialization.
- There is no cross-queue overflow.
- Resource selection within a queue favors low current load, but never moves work to a different queue.

### Expected strengths and weaknesses

This policy is auditable and protects specialized teams, but it cannot use Farah for Mechanical/Electrical, Ada for Intake/Validation overflow, or the cross-skilled capacity of Ada, Deepa, Chen, and Emil for complex branch balancing. U3 exposes the Software problem particularly clearly.

## Policy 2: full pooling with an earliest-completion dispatcher

### Eligibility and dispatch

Any resource qualified for the activity may receive the work.

Dispatch is lexicographic:

1. P1 before P2 before P3.
2. For P1, smallest projected SLA slack:
   \[
   \text{arrival}+120-
   (\text{now}+\text{projected service}+\text{handover/setup}).
   \]
3. For P2/P3, shortest projected service completion, with an aging bonus after 30 minutes of waiting.
4. Ties within two minutes go to the resource with lower recent utilization, then to FIFO.

The projected service time includes the candidate resource's multiplier, 7-minute handover, possible 5-minute family change, and 5-minute setup.

### Parallelization and preemption

- ME/ES use parallel execution when two distinct eligible resources are immediately available and the branch-duration saving is greater than 8 minutes.
- Otherwise the branches run sequentially, preferring a common cross-skilled resource.
- P1 may preempt an eligible lower-priority diagnostic activity regardless of its original queue.
- Preemption is limited to one interruption per lower-priority case, with the 10% remaining-time penalty.

### Handover, setup, and overflow

- Every assignment considers handover and setup costs.
- Full pooling is also the overflow mechanism: there are no fixed boundaries.
- A utilization tie-break prevents exclusive use of fast Chen/Emil resources, but this does not guarantee fairness.

### Expected strengths and weaknesses

This policy has the best chance of minimizing base-case mean delay and can exploit parallel branches. Its risks are setup churn, assignment to slow resources when they are idle, and concentration of work on nominally flexible resources.

## Policy 3: hybrid policy with reserved P1 capacity and controlled overflow

### Primary queues and P1 reservation

Use the same primary queues as the strict policy, with these operating differences:

- Each resource reserves 20% of its scheduled shift time for P1 work.
- P2/P3 work cannot consume that reserve.
- Unused reserve is released when the shift ends, or near shift end when no P1 case is waiting.
- P1 work can use every qualified resource, including secondary queues.

A 20% reservation is operationally plausible because U1 raises the stressed-period P1 share to 18%-23%, while Base P1 is about 13% overall. It should nevertheless be recalibrated from observed P1 workload.

### Overflow triggers

A secondary resource is used when at least one of the following holds:

- P1 has waited at least 5 minutes;
- any case has waited at least 15 minutes;
- the two-hour skill pressure index exceeds 85%;
- no primary resource is currently available and the SLA projection is at risk.

The dispatcher still includes handover/setup penalties, so overflow does not mean indiscriminate cross-skill assignment.

### Parallelization and preemption

- P1 complex cases may parallelize when the branch saving exceeds 8 minutes.
- P2/P3 complex cases require a saving greater than 15 minutes, covering the 8-minute coordination cost plus a safety margin.
- P1 may preempt lower-priority diagnostic work on a primary resource, or on an overflow resource when the P1 SLA is at risk.
- Preemption requires a projected P1 completion above 110 minutes, leaving a ten-minute contingency.

### Fairness and P3 protection

- P3 work receives an aging bonus after 30 minutes.
- P3 may use residual P1 capacity if the P1 reserve is unused.
- A resource is not selected solely because it is fastest when an equally competitive resource has materially lower utilization.

This policy deliberately gives up some mean throughput in Base and U1 in exchange for a larger P1 reserve and faster overflow.

# 5. Robust recommendation

## Primary recommendation: hybrid policy with surge mode

Use the hybrid policy as the normal operating mode. It does not strictly dominate full pooling on Base mean delay, but it offers the best supplied-evidence trade-off across the robustness objective:

- materially better P1 attainment than strict specialization in every scenario;
- better utilization fairness and lower overload than strict specialization;
- better P1 protection than full pooling in U1, U3, U4, and U5;
- only a modest expected mean-delay penalty relative to full pooling in Base;
- explicit overflow and aging rules that are understandable to operators.

The key advantage is controlled degradation. Strict specialization fails locally because work cannot cross queues; full pooling fails because pooling cannot overcome skill qualification and may introduce setup churn. The hybrid policy reserves scarce P1 capacity and releases it only under explicit conditions.

## Fallback mode: full-pooling surge mode

Switch from normal hybrid dispatch to a full-pooling surge mode when any of the following holds:

1. A P1 task has waited at least 5 minutes.
2. A P1 projection exceeds 110 elapsed minutes.
3. A non-P1 task has waited at least 15 minutes.
4. The current skill pressure index exceeds 85%.
5. The 10:00-12:00 or 14:00-16:00 resource utilization projection exceeds 95%.

In surge mode:

- release the P1 reserve to any qualified resource;
- allow controlled cross-skill overflow;
- permit P1 preemption;
- parallelize P1 complex branches whenever the branch saving exceeds 8 minutes;
- allocate overtime only after qualified idle resources have been exhausted;
- use a maximum of the permitted 30 overtime minutes per resource.

Return to normal hybrid mode only after the overload trigger is cleared for two consecutive ten-minute intervals and no P1 case is awaiting assignment.

## Cases in which another policy is preferable

- **Strict specialization** is preferable when immutable team boundaries, independent quality assurance, or minimal cross-training are more important than delay and SLA attainment.
- **Full pooling** is preferable if the objective is strictly Base/U2 mean delay, observed skill flexibility is high, and setup/handover costs are demonstrably small.
- **No existing routing policy is sufficient** if the U5 P1 target of 90% within 120 minutes is non-negotiable. That requires temporary capacity, demand deferral, or explicit triage in addition to routing.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates:** Dispatchers may understate remaining work to obtain a faster resource or overstate it to retain a case. Use system-derived elapsed service, capped manual adjustments, and audit overrides.
- **P3 starvation:** Hard P1 reservation and aging-based overflow can indefinitely delay P3. Apply a P3 aging bonus after 30 minutes and allow residual reserve to be used when no P1 work is waiting.
- **Skill erosion:** Repeatedly sending Mechanical work to Farah or Software work to Chen can reduce proficiency and make observed multipliers drift upward. Monitor performance by resource and skill, not only by activity.
- **Excessive preemption:** Every preemption creates restart work, context switching, and a less predictable tail. Restrict preemption to active diagnostic work, require P1 SLA risk, and enforce the once-per-case limit.
- **Coordination overhead:** Parallel branches can lose their apparent advantage when one branch waits, setup is triggered, or Validation requires another handover. Measure realized parallel savings rather than assuming the nominal 8-minute coordination charge.
- **Validation bottleneck migration:** Faster diagnostics can move the queue to Validation, especially at 14:00-16:00. Monitor stage-specific utilization and age, not just end-to-end duration.
- **Fairness:** Fast-resource bias can protect the average while overloading Chen, Emil, or Farah. Compare resource-day utilization and P3 age, and include a fairness term or guardrail.
- **Human interpretability:** A dispatcher needs a short explanation such as “P1, 7-minute wait, Ada is eligible, projected completion 94 minutes, selected over Deepa at 101 minutes.” An opaque learned score would be difficult to audit and could not be validated from the supplied evidence.
- **Preemption fairness:** A lower-priority case should not be repeatedly interrupted merely because new P1 work arrives. The once-per-case rule must be case-level, not activity-level.

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

## Deployment indicators

Track by priority, case type, resource, hour, branch mode, policy version, and shock flag:

- arrival-to-Validation mean, p50, p90, p95, and maximum;
- queue waiting separately for Intake, each diagnostic skill, and Validation;
- P1/P2/P3 SLA attainment and SLA-clock pause reasons;
- resource utilization over 10-, 15-, and 60-minute windows;
- maximum resource utilization and overtime minutes;
- Jain or another auditable fairness index;
- estimated versus actual service by activity and resource;
- tail incidence and tail magnitude;
- parallelization rate and realized parallel-versus-sequential savings;
- handover count, handover minutes, setup minutes, and their causes;
- preemption count, interrupted remaining service, and restart penalty;
- P3 maximum queue age and cases aged over 30/60 minutes;
- changes in bottleneck activity over time.

## Required event attributes

Every process activity should contain:

- `case:concept:name`;
- `concept:name`;
- `start_timestamp`;
- `time:timestamp` and `end_timestamp`;
- `org:resource`;
- case arrival, priority, and case type;
- diagnostic skill and complex branch identifier;
- planned base service, resource multiplier, planned actual service, and observed actual service;
- queue-entry timestamp;
- assignment timestamp;
- eligible-resource set;
- selected resource;
- assignment decision and dispatch score;
- P1 projected completion and SLA deadline;
- handover reason and duration;
- setup reason and duration;
- coordination duration;
- pause/resume sequence and cumulative restart penalty;
- policy version;
- scenario/shock flag;
- outcome and validation completion timestamp.

Assignment decisions can be represented as attributes on the activity-start event. Pause and resume events should preserve the same task identifier so PM4Py does not mistake a restart for unrelated work.

## Relevant PM4Py analyses

- Use `insert_case_service_waiting_time` for case-level service and residual waiting, but label it carefully. It is not a substitute for stage-specific queue waiting.
- Use `get_service_time` for activity/resource service-time summaries. Because explicit Handover, Setup, and Coordination activities are logged, case service will include those overhead activities unless they are separately filtered.
- Use `discover_handover_of_work_network` to identify resource-to-resource handoff patterns.
- Compare variants by priority and case type to determine whether complex cases or particular skills dominate delay.
- Use resource/activity statistics and time-binned workload views to detect bottleneck migration.
- Compare service-time distributions and tail frequencies across weeks to detect changes in the service ranges or resource multipliers.
- Use token-based replay with the policy version to estimate where an alternative policy would have changed waiting and preemption.
- Calculate queue-specific metrics directly from `queue_entry_timestamp` and assignment/start timestamps; PM4Py's residual waiting can be distorted by explicit overhead activities and repeated resource assignments.

def mean_service(activity, scenario):
    row = SERVICE.loc[activity]
    p = float(row["tail_p"])
    if scenario == "U4" and activity in {"Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"}:
        p *= 2.0
    ordinary = (float(row["ordinary_low"]) + float(row["ordinary_high"])) / 2.0
    tail = (float(row["tail_low"]) + float(row["tail_high"])) / 2.0
    return (1.0 - p) * ordinary + p * tail


def scheduled_minutes(resource, day=0):
    data = RESOURCE[resource]
    total = data["shift_end"] - data["shift_start"]
    for left, right in data["breaks"]:
        overlap = max(0, min(data["shift_end"], right) - max(data["shift_start"], left))
        total -= overlap
    return total


def resource_available(resource, minute, scenario):
    """Calendar availability, with at most 30 minutes of overtime."""
    day, mod = divmod(int(minute), 1440)
    data = RESOURCE[resource]

    # Chen is absent for the scenario day, not the following comparison day.
    if resource == "Chen" and scenario in {"U3", "U5"} and day == 0:
        return False

    if data["shift_start"] <= mod < data["shift_end"]:
        return not any(left <= mod < right for left, right in data["breaks"])

    if data["shift_end"] <= mod < data["shift_end"] + data["ot_minutes"]:
        return True

    return False


def intake_system_available(minute, scenario):
    """U5 has a global Intake outage from 09:30 to 10:00 on day zero."""
    if scenario == "U5":
        day, mod = divmod(int(minute), 1440)
        if day == 0 and 570 <= mod < 600:
            return False
    return True


def block_capacity(scenario, block_index, activity):
    """Scheduled standard-minute capacity for a skill in a two-hour block."""
    start = 8 * 60 + 2 * 60 * block_index
    end = start + 120
    capacity = 0.0

    for resource in QUALIFIED[activity]:
        if resource == "Chen" and scenario in {"U3", "U5"}:
            continue
        data = RESOURCE[resource]
        overlap = max(0, min(data["shift_end"], end) - max(data["shift_start"], start))
        for left, right in data["breaks"]:
            overlap -= max(0, min(data["shift_end"], right, end) - max(data["shift_start"], left, start))
        overlap = max(0, overlap)
        multiplier = data["multiplier"][activity]
        capacity += overlap / multiplier

    return capacity


def skill_pressure(scenario, block_index, activity):
    """Expected offered work divided by all-qualified scheduled capacity."""
    d = scenario_demand(scenario)
    row = d.iloc[block_index]
    offered = 0.0

    if activity in {"Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"}:
        contributing_types = {
            "Mechanical": {"M", "ME"},
            "Electrical": {"E", "ME", "ES"},
            "Software": {"S", "ES"},
        }[activity]
        for case_type in contributing_types:
            count = float(row["expected_arrivals"]) * float(row[f"{case_type}_pct"]) / 100.0
            offered += count * mean_service(activity, scenario)
    else:
        offered = float(row["expected_arrivals"]) * mean_service(activity, scenario)

    capacity = block_capacity(scenario, block_index, activity)
    return offered / capacity if capacity > 0 else float("inf")

def draw_service(activity, scenario, rng):
    row = SERVICE.loc[activity]
    p = float(row["tail_p"])
    if scenario == "U4" and activity in {"Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"}:
        p *= 2.0
    if rng.random() < p:
        value = rng.uniform(float(row["tail_low"]), float(row["tail_high"]))
    else:
        value = rng.uniform(float(row["ordinary_low"]), float(row["ordinary_high"]))
    return max(1, int(math.ceil(value)))


def generate_cases(scenario, seed):
    """Poisson arrivals; uniform within block; independent priority/type."""
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    d = scenario_demand(scenario)
    cases = []
    case_number = 0

    for block_index, row in d.iterrows():
        start = 8 * 60 + 2 * 60 * block_index
        end = start + 120
        count = int(rng.poisson(float(row["expected_arrivals"])))

        type_probs = row[TYPE_COLUMNS].astype(float).to_numpy()
        type_probs /= type_probs.sum()
        priority_probs = row[["P1_pct", "P2_pct", "P3_pct"]].astype(float).to_numpy()
        priority_probs /= priority_probs.sum()

        for _ in range(count):
            case_number += 1
            arrival = int(rng.integers(start, end))
            priority = str(rng.choice(PRIORITIES, p=priority_probs))
            case_type = str(rng.choice(["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"], p=type_probs))

            activities = ["Intake", "Validation"]
            if case_type in {"M", "ME"}:
                activities.append("Mechanical")
            if case_type in {"E", "ME", "ES"}:
                activities.append("Electrical")
            if case_type in {"S", "ES"}:
                activities.append("Software")

            draws = {activity: draw_service(activity, scenario, rng) for activity in activities}
            cases.append(
                Case(
                    case_id=f"C{case_number:06d}",
                    arrival=arrival,
                    priority=priority,
                    case_type=case_type,
                    draws=draws,
                )
            )

    return sorted(cases, key=lambda c: c.arrival)


class Simulator:
    def __init__(self, cases, scenario, policy):
        self.cases = cases
        self.scenario = scenario
        self.policy = policy
        self.case_map = {case.case_id: case for case in cases}
        self.tasks = {}
        self.active = {}
        self.events = []
        self.busy = Counter()
        self.non_p1_busy = Counter()
        self.last_diagnostic_family = {}
        self.last_diagnostic_case = {}
        self.total_handover = 0
        self.total_setup = 0
        self.preemption_count = 0
        self.overtime_minutes = 0
        self.next_task_id = 1

    # ----- event logging -------------------------------------------------

    def add_interval(self, case_id, concept, start, end, resource, **attributes):
        row = {
            "case:concept:name": case_id,
            "concept:name": concept,
            "start_timestamp": timestamp(start),
            "time:timestamp": timestamp(end),
            "end_timestamp": timestamp(end),
            "org:resource": resource,
            "activity_type": attributes.pop("activity_type", "process"),
            "policy_version": f"{self.policy}-v1",
            "scenario_shock_flag": self.scenario,
        }
        row.update(attributes)
        self.events.append(row)

    def emit_service_segment(self, task, start, end, resource):
        if end <= start:
            return
        case = self.case_map[task.case_id]
        task.segments.append((start, end, resource))
        self.add_interval(
            task.case_id,
            task.activity,
            start,
            end,
            resource,
            activity_type="process",
            task_id=task.task_id,
            priority=case.priority,
            case_type=case.case_type,
            branch_mode=case.branch_mode or "not_applicable",
            skill_used=task.activity,
            planned_service_minutes=task.base_minutes,
            resource_multiplier=task.multiplier,
            actual_service_minutes=end - start,
            queue_entry_timestamp=timestamp(task.queue_entry),
            assignment_timestamp=timestamp(task.assigned_at) if task.assigned_at is not None else None,
            queue_wait_minutes=task.current_queue_wait,
            eligible_resource_set=task.eligible_set,
            assignment_decision=task.dispatch_reason,
            pause_resume_count=case.preemptions,
            restart_penalty_minutes=task.restart_penalty,
            sla_clock_start=timestamp(case.arrival),
            sla_deadline=timestamp(case.arrival + sla_minutes(case.priority)),
        )

    # ----- task creation and eligibility -------------------------------

    def add_task(self, case, activity, ready_at, queue_entry=None, preferred=None,
                 predecessor_resource=None, predecessor_activity=None):
        task = Task(
            task_id=self.next_task_id,
            case_id=case.case_id,
            activity=activity,
            base_minutes=case.draws[activity],
            remaining=case.draws[activity],
            queue_entry=ready_at if queue_entry is None else queue_entry,
            ready_at=ready_at,
            preferred_resource=preferred,
            predecessor_resource=case.last_resource if predecessor_resource is None else predecessor_resource,
            predecessor_activity=case.last_activity if predecessor_activity is None else predecessor_activity,
        )
        self.next_task_id += 1
        self.tasks[task.task_id] = task
        return task

    def base_pool(self, activity):
        if self.policy == "STRICT":
            return STRICT_POOL[activity].copy()
        if self.policy == "HYBRID":
            return HYBRID_PRIMARY[activity].copy()
        return QUALIFIED[activity].copy()

    def current_block_index(self, minute):
        day, mod = divmod(int(minute), 1440)
        if 480 <= mod < 1200 and day == 0:
            return int((mod - 480) // 120)
        return None

    def overflow_triggered(self, case, task, minute):
        wait = minute - task.queue_entry
        if case.priority == "P1" and wait >= 5:
            return True
        if wait >= 15:
            return True
        block = self.current_block_index(minute)
        if block is not None and diagnostic_family(task.activity):
            pressure = skill_pressure(self.scenario, block, task.activity)
            if pressure >= 0.85:
                return True
        return False

    def candidate_sets(self, case, task, minute):
        qualified = QUALIFIED[task.activity].copy()
        if self.policy == "STRICT":
            return set(STRICT_POOL[task.activity]) & qualified, set()
        if self.policy == "FULL":
            return qualified, set()

        primary = set(HYBRID_PRIMARY[task.activity]) & qualified
        secondary = qualified - primary
        if self.overflow_triggered(case, task, minute):
            return primary, secondary
        return primary, set()

    # ----- overheads and assignment ------------------------------------

    def handover_amount(self, case, task, resource):
        predecessor_resource = task.interrupted_resource or task.predecessor_resource
        predecessor_activity = task.predecessor_activity
        if not predecessor_resource or predecessor_resource == resource:
            return 0, 0

        amount = 7
        extra_team_move = 0
        old_family = diagnostic_family(predecessor_activity or "")
        new_family = diagnostic_family(task.activity)
        if old_family and new_family and old_family != new_family:
            extra_team_move = 5
        return amount, extra_team_move

    def setup_amount(self, case, task, resource):
        family = diagnostic_family(task.activity)
        if not family:
            return 0
        old_family = self.last_diagnostic_family.get(resource)
        old_case = self.last_diagnostic_case.get(resource)
        if old_family and old_family != family and old_case != case.case_id:
            return 5
        return 0

    def hybrid_reservation_blocks(self, resource, minute, estimated_service):
        if self.policy != "HYBRID":
            return False
        day, mod = divmod(int(minute), 1440)
        capacity = scheduled_minutes(resource, day)
        used = self.non_p1_busy[(resource, day)]
        if used + estimated_service <= 0.80 * capacity:
            return False

        # Near shift end, release unused reserve if no P1 is waiting.
        p1_waiting = any(
            task.status == "waiting" and self.case_map[task.case_id].priority == "P1"
            for task in self.tasks.values()
        )
        shift_end = RESOURCE[resource]["shift_end"]
        return not (not p1_waiting and mod >= shift_end - 30)

    def assignment_score(self, case, task, resource, minute):
        multiplier = RESOURCE[resource]["multiplier"][task.activity]
        service = math.ceil(task.base_minutes * multiplier)
        handover, extra = self.handover_amount(case, task, resource)
        setup = self.setup_amount(case, task, resource)
        projected = service + handover + extra + setup
        load = self.busy[resource] / max(1, scheduled_minutes(resource))
        return projected, load, handover + extra + setup

    def choose_resource(self, case, task, minute):
        primary, secondary = self.candidate_sets(case, task, minute)
        idle = set(RESOURCE) - set(self.active)
        available = {
            resource for resource in idle
            if resource_available(resource, minute, self.scenario)
            and not (task.activity == "Intake" and not intake_system_available(minute, self.scenario))
        }

        # A preferred resource is used for sequential complex branches when
        # it is eligible; this minimizes handovers and family changes.
        preferred = task.preferred_resource
        if preferred in available and preferred in primary:
            return preferred, "retained cross-skilled resource"

        candidates = [resource for resource in available if resource in primary]
        if not candidates:
            candidates = [resource for resource in available if resource in secondary]
            if not candidates:
                return None, ""
        elif secondary:
            secondary_available = [resource for resource in available if resource in secondary]
            if secondary_available:
                best_primary = min(
                    candidates,
                    key=lambda r: self.assignment_score(case, task, r, minute),
                )
                best_secondary = min(
                    secondary_available,
                    key=lambda r: self.assignment_score(case, task, r, minute),
                )
                # Controlled overflow: secondary must be materially better.
                if self.assignment_score(case, task, best_secondary, minute)[0] + 8 < \
                        self.assignment_score(case, task, best_primary, minute)[0]:
                    candidates = [best_secondary]

        return min(
            candidates,
            key=lambda r: self.assignment_score(case, task, r, minute),
        ), "priority score with handover/setup and utilization tie-break"

    def assign_task(self, task, resource, reason, minute):
        case = self.case_map[task.case_id]
        multiplier = RESOURCE[resource]["multiplier"][task.activity]
        handover, extra = self.handover_amount(case, task, resource)
        setup = self.setup_amount(case, task, resource)

        task.status = "active"
        task.resource = resource
        task.assigned_at = minute
        task.multiplier = multiplier
        task.remaining = max(1, int(math.ceil(task.base_minutes * multiplier)))
        task.current_queue_wait = max(0, minute - task.queue_entry)
        task.handover_total = handover + extra
        task.handover_left = task.handover_total
        task.handover_start = minute if task.handover_total else None
        task.setup_total = setup
        task.setup_left = setup
        task.setup_start = minute if setup else None
        task.eligible_set = ";".join(sorted(QUALIFIED[task.activity]))
        task.dispatch_reason = (
            f"{case.priority}; {reason}; multiplier={multiplier:.2f}; "
            f"handover={task.handover_total}; setup={setup}"
        )

        self.active[resource] = task
        self.total_handover += task.handover_total
        self.total_setup += setup

    def task_order(self, minute):
        priority_rank = {"P1": 0, "P2": 1, "P3": 2}

        def key(task):
            case = self.case_map[task.case_id]
            wait = minute - task.queue_entry
            if self.policy == "STRICT":
                secondary = task.queue_entry
            elif case.priority == "P1":
                secondary = case.arrival + 120 - (minute + task.base_minutes)
            else:
                # Shortest expected service, with an aging bonus after 30 min.
                secondary = task.base_minutes - min(wait, 60) * 0.2
            return priority_rank[case.priority], secondary, task.queue_entry

        return sorted(
            [task for task in self.tasks.values()
             if task.status == "waiting" and task.ready_at <= minute],
            key=key,
        )

    def assign_ready(self, minute):
        while True:
            made_assignment = False
            for task in self.task_order(minute):
                if task.status != "waiting":
                    continue
                case = self.case_map[task.case_id]
                resource, reason = self.choose_resource(case, task, minute)
                if resource is None:
                    continue
                estimated = math.ceil(
                    task.base_minutes * RESOURCE[resource]["multiplier"][task.activity]
                )
                if self.hybrid_reservation_blocks(resource, minute, estimated):
                    continue
                self.assign_task(task, resource, reason, minute)
                made_assignment = True
                break
            if not made_assignment:
                break

    def preempt_for_p1(self, minute):
        """Preempt at most one lower-priority diagnostic case per interruption."""
        for p1_task in self.task_order(minute):
            if p1_task.status != "waiting":
                continue
            p1_case = self.case_map[p1_task.case_id]
            if p1_case.priority != "P1":
                continue

            candidates = []
            for resource, active_task in list(self.active.items()):
                lower_case = self.case_map[active_task.case_id]
                if lower_case.priority == "P1" or lower_case.preemptions >= 1:
                    continue
                if diagnostic_family(active_task.activity) is None:
                    continue
                if resource not in QUALIFIED[p1_task.activity]:
                    continue

                if self.policy == "STRICT" and resource not in STRICT_POOL[p1_task.activity]:
                    continue
                if self.policy == "HYBRID":
                    primary = HYBRID_PRIMARY[p1_task.activity]
                    if resource not in primary and not self.overflow_triggered(p1_case, p1_task, minute):
                        continue

                candidates.append((active_task.remaining, resource, active_task))

            if not candidates:
                continue

            _, resource, active_task = max(candidates, key=lambda item: item[0])
            lower_case = self.case_map[active_task.case_id]

            if active_task.service_start is not None:
                self.emit_service_segment(active_task, active_task.service_start, minute, resource)

            penalty = int(math.ceil(0.10 * active_task.remaining))
            active_task.remaining += penalty
            active_task.restart_penalty += penalty
            active_task.status = "waiting"
            active_task.resource = None
            active_task.assigned_at = None
            active_task.service_start = None
            active_task.queue_entry = minute
            active_task.ready_at = minute
            active_task.interrupted_resource = resource
            active_task.dispatch_reason += f"; preempted by {p1_task.case_id}; restart_penalty={penalty}"

            lower_case.preemptions += 1
            self.preemption_count += 1
            del self.active[resource]
            break

    # ----- branch decision and process flow ----------------------------

    def decide_branch_mode(self, case, minute):
        if case.case_type == "ME":
            branches = ["Mechanical", "Electrical"]
        elif case.case_type == "ES":
            branches = ["Electrical", "Software"]
        else:
            return "single", None

        if self.policy == "STRICT":
            parallel = False
        else:
            idle = set(RESOURCE) - set(self.active)
            pair_candidates = []
            for first_resource in self.base_pool(branches[0]) & idle:
                for second_resource in self.base_pool(branches[1]) & idle:
                    if first_resource == second_resource:
                        continue
                    first_duration = math.ceil(
                        case.draws[branches[0]] *
                        RESOURCE[first_resource]["multiplier"][branches[0]]
                    )
                    second_duration = math.ceil(
                        case.draws[branches[1]] *
                        RESOURCE[second_resource]["multiplier"][branches[1]]
                    )
                    saving = first_duration + second_duration - max(first_duration, second_duration)
                    pair_candidates.append((saving, max(first_duration, second_duration), first_resource, second_resource))

            parallel = False
            if pair_candidates:
                best = max(pair_candidates)
                threshold = 8 if (self.policy == "FULL" or case.priority == "P1") else 15
                parallel = best[0] > threshold

        if parallel:
            return "parallel", None

        # Prefer a common cross-skilled resource for sequential execution.
        common = QUALIFIED[branches[0]] & QUALIFIED[branches[1]]
        idle = set(RESOURCE) - set(self.active)
        preferred = None
        if common & idle:
            preferred = min(
                common & idle,
                key=lambda r: sum(
                    case.draws[activity] * RESOURCE[r]["multiplier"][activity]
                    for activity in branches
                ),
            )
        return "sequential", preferred

    def complete_task(self, task, end_minute):
        resource = task.resource
        case = self.case_map[task.case_id]

        if task.service_start is not None:
            self.emit_service_segment(task, task.service_start, end_minute, resource)

        task.status = "done"
        if resource in self.active and self.active[resource] is task:
            del self.active[resource]

        case.last_resource = resource
        case.last_activity = task.activity

        family = diagnostic_family(task.activity)
        if family:
            self.last_diagnostic_family[resource] = family
            self.last_diagnostic_case[resource] = case.case_id

        if task.activity == "Intake":
            if case.case_type in {"M", "E", "S"}:
                diagnostic = {"M": "Mechanical", "E": "Electrical", "S": "Software"}[case.case_type]
                self.add_task(case, diagnostic, end_minute)
            else:
                mode, preferred = self.decide_branch_mode(case, end_minute)
                case.branch_mode = mode
                if case.case_type == "ME":
                    case.branch_order = ["Mechanical", "Electrical"]
                else:
                    case.branch_order = ["Electrical", "Software"]

                predecessor_resource = case.last_resource
                predecessor_activity = case.last_activity
                if mode == "parallel":
                    self.add_task(case, case.branch_order[0], end_minute,
                                 predecessor_resource=predecessor_resource,
                                 predecessor_activity=predecessor_activity)
                    self.add_task(case, case.branch_order[1], end_minute,
                                 predecessor_resource=predecessor_resource,
                                 predecessor_activity=predecessor_activity)
                else:
                    self.add_task(case, case.branch_order[0], end_minute,
                                 preferred=preferred,
                                 predecessor_resource=predecessor_resource,
                                 predecessor_activity=predecessor_activity)

        elif task.activity in {"Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"}:
            if case.case_type in {"ME", "ES"}:
                if case.branch_mode == "parallel":
                    case.branch_done.add(task.activity)
                    if len(case.branch_done) == 2:
                        coordination_end = end_minute + 8
                        self.add_interval(
                            case.case_id,
                            "Coordination",
                            end_minute,
                            coordination_end,
                            case.last_resource,
                            activity_type="overhead",
                            task_id=task.task_id,
                            priority=case.priority,
                            case_type=case.case_type,
                            delay_minutes=8,
                            assignment_decision="parallel branch coordination",
                        )
                        self.add_task(case, "Validation", coordination_end, queue_entry=end_minute)
                else:
                    if task.activity == case.branch_order[0]:
                        self.add_task(case, case.branch_order[1], end_minute)
                    else:
                        self.add_task(case, "Validation", end_minute)
            else:
                self.add_task(case, "Validation", end_minute)

        elif task.activity == "Validation":
            case.completed_at = end_minute

    # ----- execution ----------------------------------------------------

    def work_one_minute(self, minute):
        to_complete = []

        for resource, task in list(self.active.items()):
            if not resource_available(resource, minute, self.scenario):
                continue
            if task.activity == "Intake" and not intake_system_available(minute, self.scenario):
                continue

            case = self.case_map[task.case_id]
            self.busy[resource] += 1
            day, mod = divmod(minute, 1440)
            self.busy_by_day = getattr(self, "busy_by_day", Counter())
            self.busy_by_day[(resource, day)] += 1

            if case.priority != "P1":
                self.non_p1_busy[(resource, day)] += 1

            if mod >= RESOURCE[resource]["shift_end"]:
                self.overtime_minutes += 1

            if task.handover_left > 0:
                task.handover_left -= 1
                if task.handover_left == 0:
                    task.handover_end = minute + 1
                    self.add_interval(
                        task.case_id,
                        "Handover",
                        task.handover_start,
                        task.handover_end,
                        resource,
                        activity_type="overhead",
                        task_id=task.task_id,
                        priority=case.priority,
                        case_type=case.case_type,
                        delay_minutes=task.handover_total,
                        assignment_decision=task.dispatch_reason,
                    )
                    if task.setup_total:
                        task.setup_start = minute + 1
                continue

            if task.setup_left > 0:
                task.setup_left -= 1
                if task.setup_left == 0:
                    task.setup_end = minute + 1
                    self.add_interval(
                        task.case_id,
                        "Setup",
                        task.setup_start,
                        task.setup_end,
                        resource,
                        activity_type="overhead",
                        task_id=task.task_id,
                        priority=case.priority,
                        case_type=case.case_type,
                        delay_minutes=task.setup_total,
                        assignment_decision=task.dispatch_reason,
                    )
                continue

            if task.service_start is None:
                task.service_start = minute
            task.remaining -= 1
            if task.remaining <= 0:
                to_complete.append((task, minute + 1))

        for task, end_minute in to_complete:
            self.complete_task(task, end_minute)

    # ----- metrics -------------------------------------------------------

    def summary(self):
        completed = [case for case in self.cases if case.completed_at is not None]
        delays = np.array([case.completed_at - case.arrival for case in completed], dtype=float)

        def attainment(priority):
            cases = [case for case in self.cases if case.priority == priority]
            if not cases:
                return float("nan")
            return 100.0 * sum(
                case.completed_at is not None and
                case.completed_at - case.arrival <= sla_minutes(priority)
                for case in cases
            ) / len(cases)

        busy_by_day = getattr(self, "busy_by_day", Counter())
        utilization_ratios = []
        for resource in RESOURCE:
            for day in [0, 1]:
                if resource == "Chen" and day == 0 and self.scenario in {"U3", "U5"}:
                    continue
                denominator = scheduled_minutes(resource, day)
                if denominator > 0:
                    utilization_ratios.append(100.0 * busy_by_day[(resource, day)] / denominator)

        if utilization_ratios:
            max_util = max(utilization_ratios)
            squared_sum = sum(value * value for value in utilization_ratios)
            fairness = (
                sum(utilization_ratios) ** 2 /
                (len(utilization_ratios) * squared_sum)
                if squared_sum > 0 else 0.0
            )
        else:
            max_util = float("nan")
            fairness = float("nan")

        denominator = max(1, len(completed))
        return {
            "mean_delay_h": float(np.mean(delays) / 60.0) if len(delays) else float("nan"),
            "p90_delay_h": float(np.percentile(delays, 90) / 60.0) if len(delays) else float("nan"),
            "P1_SLA_pct": attainment("P1"),
            "P2_SLA_pct": attainment("P2"),
            "P3_SLA_pct": attainment("P3"),
            "max_resource_utilization_pct": max_util,
            "utilization_fairness_jain": fairness,
            "handover_min_per_case": self.total_handover / denominator,
            "setup_min_per_case": self.total_setup / denominator,
            "preemptions": self.preemption_count,
            "overtime_minutes": self.overtime_minutes,
            "completed_cases": len(completed),
            "total_cases": len(self.cases),
        }


def run_simulation(cases, scenario, policy):
    simulator = Simulator(copy.deepcopy(cases), scenario, policy)

    # Arrivals are on day zero. The horizon extends beyond the following
    # 20:00 shift so P3 cases have a full 24-hour SLA observation window.
    horizon = 2 * 1440
    arrival_index = 0

    for minute in range(8 * 60, horizon + 1):
        while arrival_index < len(simulator.cases) and simulator.cases[arrival_index].arrival <= minute:
            case = simulator.cases[arrival_index]
            simulator.add_task(case, "Intake", case.arrival)
            arrival_index += 1

        simulator.assign_ready(minute)
        simulator.preempt_for_p1(minute)
        simulator.assign_ready(minute)
        simulator.work_one_minute(minute)

    return simulator

def aggregate_runs(run_rows):
    metric_names = [
        "mean_delay_h",
        "p90_delay_h",
        "P1_SLA_pct",
        "P2_SLA_pct",
        "P3_SLA_pct",
        "max_resource_utilization_pct",
        "utilization_fairness_jain",
        "handover_min_per_case",
        "setup_min_per_case",
        "preemptions",
        "overtime_minutes",
        "completed_cases",
    ]

    output = []
    for (scenario, policy), group in pd.DataFrame(run_rows).groupby(["scenario", "policy"]):
        row = {"scenario": scenario, "policy": policy, "seeds": len(group)}
        for metric in metric_names:
            values = pd.to_numeric(group[metric], errors="coerce").dropna().to_numpy()
            if len(values):
                row[f"{metric}_mean"] = float(np.mean(values))
                row[f"{metric}_p10"] = float(np.percentile(values, 10))
                row[f"{metric}_p90"] = float(np.percentile(values, 90))

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## What cannot be concluded from the supplied information

The expected arrival table and service ranges do not establish:

- arrival-count variance, burstiness, or within-block arrival shape;
- whether priority and case type are independent;
- the empirical service distribution, autocorrelation, or whether tail probability applies per activity or per case;
- realistic queue discipline, dispatcher behavior, or estimates of remaining work;
- the frequency with which parallel branches are operationally feasible;
- demand for overtime, employee skills outside the matrix, or work crossing resource teams;
- whether validation times are correlated with diagnostic severity;
- whether next-day shifts repeat and whether weekend/calendar effects matter;
- how much handover/setup time is already embedded in measured service times.

Therefore, the numerical screening ranges and the Python outputs should not be treated as forecasts until calibrated.

## Backtest

1. Reconstruct at least several weeks of interval logs using the event schema in Section 7.
2. Validate event completeness and reconstruction:
   - every case has exactly one Intake and Validation;
   - ME/ES branch membership is unambiguous;
   - pauses and resumes preserve the same task;
   - service durations exclude calendar unavailability unless that is the organization's intended definition.
3. Fit empirical distributions by activity and resource. Compare fitted medians, p90s, and tail frequencies with the supplied ranges.
4. Estimate the joint distribution of priority and case type rather than assuming independence.
5. Replay Base, U1-U5, and the three policies with observed arrival timestamps and fitted service distributions.
6. Use common random numbers and report confidence intervals for SLA differences, maximum utilization, and workload concentration.

## Controlled pilot

- Shadow all three policies first; dispatchers continue using the current rule while each policy recommends assignments.
- Compare recommendations on identical live cases without applying them.
- Then pilot full pooling and hybrid dispatch by matched teams or alternating time windows, while retaining a P1-safe mode.
- Do not experimentally withhold qualified capacity from P1 cases.
- Predefine guardrails:
  - no increase in P1 SLA misses beyond the confidence interval observed in shadow mode;
  - no resource above 95% planned utilization without an explicit fallback;
  - no material increase in restart penalties or P3 age;
  - no unlogged handover or setup;
  - an immediate switch-back for skill-safety or data-quality failures.
- Increase the treated share only after one full operating cycle demonstrates that P1 protection, fairness, and event logging remain stable.

The pilot should distinguish policies on their intended dimensions: mean and tail delay, P1 protection, P3 starvation, setup/handover burden, utilization concentration, and operational interpretability. A policy should not advance merely because it has the lowest Base-case mean.
