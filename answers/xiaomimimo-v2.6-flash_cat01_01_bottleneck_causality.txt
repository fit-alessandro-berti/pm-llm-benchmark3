## 6. Intervention and learning plan

| phase | intervention | target mechanism | assignment unit | safeguards | process-mining measurements | causal learning value | stop or rollback rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Instrument and adjust (weeks 1–3, no reassignment)** | Add pre-routing and queue fields: `queue_state_at_classify` (open cases per group, reviewers on duty), `routing_reason`/rule version, `reviewer_id` + certification, and retain **all** cases with arrival/last-event timestamps and open/closed status. Freeze `routing_rule` version during the window | Measurement and selection: separates queueing from service, skill from label, and completed from censored cases | None (observational, all cases) | No operational change; version freeze; weekly data-quality audit | DFG and variant discovery per group; directly-follows edge frequencies (`Compliance Check`, `Request Info`); wait decomposition (queue vs compliance dwell vs service); variant mix drift; censoring/abandonment rate | Determines whether the 2.51 h reversal survives finer risk control and censoring-aware estimation; quantifies how much of wait is compliance dwell | Routing-rule version changes mid-window  pause analysis and restart the window (time-varying policy confound) |
| **2 — Queue/capacity relief without touching labels (weeks 4–8)** | Pool the post-classification queue across groups for eligible cases and/or open a dedicated night compliance lane with cross-trained reviewers; keep High-complexity cases with certified reviewers | `queue_wait` — the component carrying ~86% of the standardized contrast and 60% of the crude gap | Shift-day blocks (cluster), stepped-wedge or alternating blocks across weeks | Certified-reviewer requirement retained for High; nightly cap on pooled queue size; weekly quality guard (rework, appeal, reopen) | Queue-wait distribution by complexity and block; compliance dwell; service time; cycle time; variant/conformance comparison vs Phase-1 baseline; rework edge rate | If cycle time falls while group labels are unchanged, queueing (not the group) is causal — a genuine mechanism result independent of routing | Either group's median cycle time or quality worsens beyond the pre-registered margin (e.g., +10% cycle, +2 pp rework), or pooled queue exceeds the cap |
| **3 — Small stratified randomized routing pilot (weeks 9–14)** | Randomize **10–15%** of eligible cases (Low and Medium complexity only; exclude High, exclude cases requiring specialist certification) between Orion and Nova, stratified by complexity × shift × load, with randomization at `Classify` | The treatment itself: total effect of assignment under genuine overlap | Case (individually randomized within stratum) | Eligibility restricted to strata where both groups are credentialled; balanced night/day allocation; hard 15% cap to limit queue perturbation; intent-to-treat with contamination log; real-time guard on appeals/reopens | ITT cycle time by stratum; wait vs service decomposition; rework rate; variant mix and DFG conformance per arm; crossover/override rate | Identifies the causal effect the observational analysis cannot: resolves H1 vs H2 directly, and tests whether effects run through queue or service | Non-inferiority breach: reassignment arm cycle time worse by >10% (or >2 h) in any stratum, **or** either group's p90 cycle time rises >20%, **or** any quality/safety signal  immediate rollback to current routing |
| **4 — Decision and staged scaling (weeks 15+)** | Only if Phase 3 ITT is favourable with quality non-inferiority: ramp reassignment 10%  25%  50% with sequential monitoring; otherwise keep routing and continue the queue intervention | Load-dependent queueing under scaled transfer | Group of cases per step | Pre-registered sequential boundaries; capacity check on Nova night/High before each step; keep compliance and certified-reviewer coverage with whichever group receives High cases | Queue-state vs load curve per group; cycle-time control charts by complexity; variant drift; capacity utilization | Converts a point estimate into a dose-response curve, revealing the load threshold at which Nova's queue degrades | Any step breaches the sequential boundary on cycle time or quality, or Nova night queue exceeds its capacity cap  revert to previous step |

**Final recommendation:** Do not reassign 50% of Orion's work now. The crude gap motivating the proposal (+6.72 h) reverses under complexity standardization (2.51 h), rests on cells with little or no overlap, and is dominated by queue wait — a property of load, shift staffing and compliance dwell, not of the group label. A 50% transfer would be a large, hard-to-reverse shock to a night-heavy queue where Nova has almost no demonstrated capacity (2 High cases, 4 night cases), could inflate Nova's wait non-linearly, could strand compliance and certified-reviewer coverage in Orion, and would teach nothing because case mix changes at the same time. Instead: instrument queue state, routing reason, reviewer identity and open cases; relieve the queue/compliance bottleneck without changing labels; then run the stratified randomized routing pilot with pre-registered non-inferiority and rollback rules. Scale in steps only on intent-to-treat evidence.

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **How much unobserved case risk remains inside each `complexity` level?** Most valuable addition: a granular **pre-routing case-risk score recorded at `Classify`** (cause of loss, claim amount, sub-flags) — it is measured before assignment, so it can close (or quantify) the residual backdoor that 3-level complexity leaves open.
2. **Is the wait gap queueing, compliance dwell, or staffing?** Most valuable addition: a per-case **`queue_state_at_classify` field** (open cases in each group's queue plus reviewers on duty at that moment) — it lets `wait_before_review_h` be decomposed and lets group be compared at equal queue pressure.
3. **How does selection into the completed-case sample distort both groups?** Most valuable design change: **retain all cases — open, abandoned and completed — with arrival time, last-event time and status**, enabling censoring-aware (survival) estimation instead of conditioning on `case_completed`.
4. **What is the treatment actually: the label, the routing rule, or the bundled skill/staffing?** Most valuable design change: the **Phase-3 stratified randomized routing pilot**, which moves the label while (initially) leaving staffing fixed, separating the label effect from the bundle that observational data cannot untangle.
5. **Is the 30-day outcome a valid quality endpoint?** Most valuable addition: a **blind QA score or 90-day reopen/re-inspection field independent of appeal** — appeal is post-process, group-linked through delay, and observed only twice, so it cannot currently serve as a safety guard for any reassignment.
