# 2. Benign alternative explanations

**Pattern A — Carla  Gita fast PO approve / emergency loops**

1. **Informal specialization + emergency medical channel**  
   Carla is noted as specialized in emergency medical supplies; Gita is delegated urgent approver. Fast medians and 88% `emergency_flag=True` fit a designed rush path.  
   - Support: SOPs naming this channel; on-call rosters; clinical urgency tickets; different humans on retrospective justification within 3 days; low vendor concentration.  
   - Refute: Missing/late justifications by independent parties; approvals without emergency documentation; amount/category outside medical emergency scope; shared-account use hiding true actor.

2. **Data-quality / shared account**  
   `org:resource="Approver_Gita"` used by assistants; mapping may collapse distinct actors.  
   - Support: `entered_by` / `approved_by` differ from `org:resource`; access logs show assistants; identity-management tickets.  
   - Refute: `entered_by` equals buyer or same individual across create and approve with no assistant trail.

**Pattern B — Dan  Hugo weekend repair overrides**

1. **Emergency facilities handling**  
   Dan specialized in facilities repairs; Hugo handles exceptions; weekend cases explain speed and loops.  
   - Support: Work-order / incident tickets; after-hours facilities logs; retrospective justification by a third party within policy window.  
   - Refute: No incident record; justification by same person only; personal benefit vendors; repeated overrides without close-out.

2. **Managerial coverage when Team A/B approvers unavailable**  
   - Support: Calendar/out-of-office; delegation matrix; Hugo only on weekends/holidays.  
   - Refute: Hugo also dense on routine weekday low-urgency cases with Dan.

**Pattern C — Lena  Kim bank change + payment (V-77x)**

1. **Legitimate mass bank-platform migration**  
   Notes state V-77x changed banking platform; mass maintenance may be valid but poorly linked in the log.  
   - Support: Change tickets, vendor confirmations, dual control in vendor-master system outside this log, AP callback evidence, bank letters, consistent IBAN changes across non-procurement systems.  
   - Refute: No ticket; payments to new accounts before vendor confirmation; only Lena–Kim path; split invoices; no independent payment release.

2. **Role design / queue routing for “higher-risk suppliers”**  
   Kim specializes in higher-risk suppliers; Lena owns bank changes—routing creates repeated pairs without collusion.  
   - Support: Work-allocation rules; similar timing for other high-risk vendors not in V-77x; segregation in underlying app (different system users).  
   - Refute: Only V-77x shows sub-20 min changepay; same person effectively controlling both steps end-to-end; manual_upload without secondary review.

**Pattern D — Same-person retrospective justification (17 cases)**

1. **Batch migration / quarter-close data-quality**  
   12/17 identical-to-the-minute timestamps from `quarter_close_upload` spreadsheet.  
   - Support: Migration runbook; spreadsheet artifact; true justifier in source columns not mapped to `org:resource`.  
   - Refute: Native interactive timestamps during quarter; same person as override actor in immutable audit trail; no independent reviewer field populated.

2. **Policy misunderstanding (justification treated as administrative close)**  
   - Support: Training materials allowing preparer entry “on behalf of”; later countersignature elsewhere.  
   - Refute: Policy text requiring different human; no countersignature in any system.

**Pattern E — Aline–Elena / Boris–Felix high volume**

1. **Team-based informal specialization (Team A / Team B)**  
   - Support: Org chart; stable queues; correction loops with normal comments; amounts low/medium; ~2.5h medians.  
   - Refute: Hidden four-eyes breaks when bot mapping assigns buyer identity to approve events; favoritism by vendor with anomalous terms.

2. **Automation handoff labeling**  
   Bot_PO create with human approve mis-mapped as same resource in `legacy_erp`.  
   - Support: Mapping table defects; `approved_by` human vs `org:resource` bot.  
   - Refute: Clean mapping and still same human on both activities.

# 3. Case prioritization

## High-priority case clusters

1. **VendorMaster_Lena + AP_Kim with Change Vendor Bank Details  Payment Approval** (especially 9 four-eyes violations; ~16 ultra-fast; V-77x; `manual_upload`)  
   - **Reason:** Highest fraud-relevant control combination (payee diversion), vendor concentration, manual channel, weak emergency narrative (only 16% emergency).  
   - **Inspect first:** Bank change audit trail (old/new account, who authorized in vendor master), payment beneficiary snapshot at release, vendor master dual-control logs, maintenance tickets for V-77x platform change, `entered_by` / `approved_by`, AP callback notes, goods receipt vs. invoice match integrity, whether Release Payment was independent of Kim, access logs for Lena and Kim on those timestamps, source_system payload for `manual_upload`.

2. **Same-person Emergency Override + Retrospective Justification not explained by `quarter_close_upload` batch** (subset of 17; focus on 5 non-batch if separable)  
   - **Reason:** Direct breach of stated detective control; batch subset may be DQ.  
   - **Inspect first:** Native UI audit timestamps vs. upload job, identity of override vs. justification, whether a different human appears in any secondary system, case emergency evidence, amount and vendor.

3. **Create PO + Approve PO four-eyes violations where emergency_flag=False or retrospective justification missing/late** (slice of 42)  
   - **Reason:** Emergency exception may not apply; residual SoD failure.  
   - **Inspect first:** `emergency_flag` provenance, justification deadline (3 working days), distinct justifier, contract_type / purchase_category consistency, `approved_by` vs `org:resource`.

## Medium-priority case clusters

1. **Carla–Gita emergency medical path** (fast approvals, GitaCarlaGita loops, high emergency rate)  
   - **Reason:** Plausible specialization/emergency handling, but shared-account risk and 18% late retrospective justification need sample testing.  
   - **Inspect first:** Clinical/emergency requisitions, delegation matrix for Gita, `entered_by` on approvals, sample of 36 subcontracting loops (comment text / rework reason), justification timeliness.

2. **Dan–Hugo weekend facilities path** (similar logic; 21% late justification)  
   - **Inspect first:** Facilities incident tickets, after-hours schedules, independent justification, vendor legitimacy for repairs.

3. **Lena/Marc same-person Vendor Check + Bank Change (31)**  
   - **Reason:** Role-design vs. weak master-data control; escalate if overlapping with payment influence.  
   - **Inspect first:** Vendor-master SoD config, maker-checker in MDG system, overlap with V-77x and Kim payments.

## Low-priority or monitoring-only clusters

1. **Aline–Elena and Boris–Felix routine pairs** (high co-case, normal speed, low emergency/manual)  
   - **Reason:** Expected team routing; high frequency alone is not suspicious.  
   - **Monitor:** Drift in four-eyes mapping errors (`legacy_erp`), sudden vendor concentration, or median time collapse.

2. **Bot_Match  AP_Kim exception path** (210 cases, 6 min)  
   - **Reason:** Likely designed exception queue; sample for rubber-stamping quality, not assume collusion.  
   - **Inspect first:** Exception codes, override reasons, whether Kim also changed vendor master on same cases (join to high-priority set).

3. **Temp_Nina  Carla end-of-quarter backlog**  
   - **Reason:** Seasonal support pattern; monitor for Nina performing controlled activities beyond data entry.

4. **Batch quarter_close_upload justifications (12)**  
   - **Reason:** Primarily data-quality remediation candidates until proven otherwise.  
   - **Inspect first:** Migration package, original spreadsheet columns, whether business still owes independent justification.

def retrospective_control_breaks(dataframe):
    rows = []
    for case_id, g in dataframe.sort_values("time:timestamp").groupby("case:concept:name"):
        ov = g[g["concept:name"] == "Emergency Override"]
        ju = g[g["concept:name"] == "Retrospective Justification"]
        if len(ov) == 0:
            continue
        if len(ju) == 0:
            rows.append((case_id, "missing_justification", None, None))
            continue
        # take first override / first justification after it
        t0, r0 = ov.iloc[0]["time:timestamp"], ov.iloc[0]["org:resource"]
        ju_after = ju[ju["time:timestamp"] >= t0]
        if ju_after.empty:
            rows.append((case_id, "justification_before_only", r0, None))
            continue
        t1, r1 = ju_after.iloc[0]["time:timestamp"], ju_after.iloc[0]["org:resource"]
        same = r0 == r1
        delta_days = (t1 - t0).total_seconds() / 86400
        status = []
        if same:
            status.append("same_resource")
        if delta_days > 3:  # refine with business-day calendar
            status.append("late")
        rows.append((case_id, "|".join(status) or "ok", r0, r1, delta_days,
                     ju_after.iloc[0].get("source_system")))
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Internal audit reviewed procurement-to-payment process-mining output for segregation-of-duties exceptions, handover concentration, approval cycle times, and case attributes. The patterns do not, by themselves, establish misconduct. Several signals are equally or more consistent with emergency handling, informal specialization by commodity team, and known data-quality issues (shared approver accounts, legacy resource mapping, quarter-close spreadsheet uploads, and incomplete linkage of vendor bank mass-maintenance to procurement cases).

Control exceptions are nonetheless real and require disposition. The most concerning cluster is the VendorMaster_Lena and AP_Kim path involving vendor bank-detail changes followed by payment approval—particularly cases on vendor family V-77x, manual_upload sources, short change-to-payment intervals, and the small set of apparent four-eyes breaches on that activity pair. This cluster combines payee-modification risk with payment authority and elevated vendor concentration; it should be prioritized for documentary and system-of-record testing, not treated as proof of collusion.

Secondary focus is emergency override cases lacking a timely retrospective justification by a different human, after isolating batch upload artifacts. Fast Carla–Gita and Dan–Hugo approvals align materially with stated emergency specialization and should be sampled against SOPs and supporting incident or medical documentation rather than presumed improper. High-volume Aline–Elena and Boris–Felix pairing is consistent with team routing and is monitoring-only unless mapping errors recreate false SoD breaks.

We recommend targeted follow-up: (1) reconstruct identity (`org:resource` vs `entered_by` / `approved_by` / SSO); (2) test V-77x bank changes and payments end-to-end; (3) validate emergency justification effectiveness; (4) remediate event-log source mapping before any personnel action. Findings should be framed as control design, execution, and data-integrity issues until case-level evidence indicates otherwise.
