## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model

**Hard invariants**
- Events and objects referenced by any proposed edge must already exist; no new IDs.
- O2O qualifiers are directional (`packs`, `carries`, `contains`, `bills`, `settles`) and must not be inverted.
- E2O cardinality for `Create Order` is exactly one Order in this log (already satisfied).
- An Item is contained in at most one Order (already given by O2O/`order_hint`).

**Strong defaults**
- `Add Item`  one Order + one Item (satisfied).
- `Pack Item`  one Item + one Package; an item-only row is allowed under scanner outage and should be reconciled if a later package pointer exists (`scanner_ref`, existing `packs`).
- `Dispatch Package`  one Package + one Delivery, unless clear bulk-dispatch evidence.
- `Record Payment`  exactly one Payment and normally one Invoice; unapplied cash is allowed but should be marked unresolved rather than forced.
- `Issue Invoice`  one Invoice and one or more Orders; amount equality is supporting evidence only.
- Lifecycle order: Create/Add  Pack  Dispatch  Confirm; Invoice after (or near) fulfillment; Payment after Invoice.

**Weak heuristics**
- Identifier prefixes: `scanner_ref=PK8-*`  PK-8; `external_ref=N-421?`  DEL-Y (`carrier_ref=N-421`); `N-42*` on confirm is ambiguous between N-420/N-421/both.
- Same customer (C17), same route (NORTH), matching timestamps (ev09/ev10 both 10:00), and declared_value sums (130+100=230) are soft corroboration.
- Partial O2O already present (e.g., PK-8 packs IT-A2, DEL-X carries PK-7) anchors local neighborhoods.

**Evidence against a repair**
- Forcing every object into a closed lifecycle (especially DEL-Y, ORD-B on confirm, PAY-2) when the portal/carrier fields are deliberately noisy.
- Treating invoice/payment arithmetic as proof.
- Assigning ev11 uniquely to one Delivery without explaining why the wildcard and order-only portal row are weaker alternatives.
- Inferring O2O solely from a hypothesized E2O and then citing that O2O as independent confirmation (circular).

**Combination policy**  
Prioritize (1) existing O2O structure and explicit attribute pointers (`scanner_ref`, `carrier_ref`, `order_hint`), (2) activity cardinality defaults, (3) lifecycle monotonicity, (4) customer/route/time co-occurrence, (5) amount sums as non-decisive support. When only (4)–(5) remain, mark provisional or unresolved. Prefer minimum-change bundles that restore local Pack/Dispatch consistency without completing every Payment/Delivery path.

## 3. Alternative repair bundles

### Bundle A — conservative / minimum-change
- **ev08 and PK-8:** Accept P1–P2. `scanner_ref=PK8-2` is the strongest local pointer; reconcile the item-only pack row to PK-8 and add `PK-8 packs IT-B1`. PK-8 then packs IT-A2 and IT-B1 (multi-order package).
- **ev10 and DEL-X/DEL-Y:** Accept P3–P4; reject P12/P14. Pair PK-8 with DEL-Y via N-421; leave DEL-X solely with PK-7.
- **ev11 and deliveries:** Do **not** uniquely bind. Keep observed E2O to ORD-A only; optionally mark P5/P6 provisional without promoting both to accept. Portal row may be order-level ack with lost carrier id.
- **INV-44 and ORD-B:** Leave O2O `bills ORD-B` provisional or unresolved (P8–P9 not accepted as fact). Invoice remains explicitly tied to ORD-A; note 230 as soft hint only.
- **PAY-2 and INV-44:** Leave P10–P11 provisional/unresolved; PAY-2 may be unapplied cash. Do not force settlement completeness.

**Lifecycle coherence:** Orders and items complete through pack; PK-7/DEL-X full dispatch path; PK-8/DEL-Y dispatch path restored; Confirm stays order-centric and ambiguous; Invoice/Payment partially open. Minimal invention; coherent local pack/dispatch neighborhoods.

### Bundle B — fulfillment-complete, finance still soft
- **ev08/PK-8:** Same as A (P1–P2).
- **ev10/DEL-Y:** Same as A (P3–P4).
- **ev11:** Accept both P5 and P6 (multi-delivery confirm under N-42* and shared customer/route), still without forcing ORD-B on ev11. Interprets confirm as batch ack of NORTH legs serving C17/ORD-A material (A1 on DEL-X, A2 on DEL-Y).
- **INV-44/ORD-B:** Promote P8–P9 to provisional-high: invoice covers both orders because declared values sum to 230 and customer matches; still not “proved.”
- **PAY-2:** Promote P10–P11 to accept-lite (confidence ~0.65): second payment closes residual 130 after PAY-1’s 100.

**Lifecycle coherence:** Both packages and both deliveries enter confirm; finance graph closes ORD-A+ORD-B  INV-44  PAY-1+PAY-2. Risk: over-complete finance and dual confirm edges that the portal row does not literally show. Still no new objects/events.

### Bundle C — split-package skepticism (optional contrast)
- Treat `scanner_ref=PK8-2` as suggestive but leave E2O ev08PK-8 and O2O packs IT-B1 **unresolved** (scanner outage not fully reconciled).
- Still pair ev10DEL-Y for PK-8’s known content (IT-A2 only).
- ev11 order-only; finance fully unresolved for ORD-B/PAY-2.
- Use when prioritizing “do not invent contents” over scanner mnemonic. Yields orphan-ish IT-B1 pack and thinner PK-8 lifecycle—valid uncertainty preservation, weaker operational story than A/B.

**Selected for JSON below: Bundle A** (conservative).

## 5. Lifecycle and cardinality audit

**Order.** ORD-A: Create  Add×2  (items packed)  Confirm  Invoice. Plausible. ORD-B: Create  Add  item packed (via Bundle A)  no confirm/invoice edge accepted. Intentionally incomplete; not forced.

**Item.** IT-A1: Add  Pack(PK-7) with matching O2O. IT-A2: Add  Pack(PK-8). IT-B1: Add  Pack(PK-8) under A. No orphans after A. Cardinality: each item one order (O2O contains); PK-8 packs two items from two orders—suspicious but allowed (shared package); supported by independent scanner_ref on ev08, not only by assuming O2O first. **Anti-circularity:** E2O ev08PK-8 is justified by `scanner_ref` before O2O `packs` is added; O2O is not used as prior proof of that E2O.

**Package.** PK-7: pack A1  dispatch DEL-X  carried O2O. Complete. PK-8: pack A2 (+B1 in A)  dispatch DEL-Y. Complete under A. No package without dispatch after repair.

**Delivery.** DEL-X: full E2O+O2O with PK-7; confirm optional. DEL-Y: gains carries PK-8 and ev10; confirm unresolved. Neither delivery is required to appear on ev11 in Bundle A—avoids false confirm completeness. Orphan risk only if one refused P3–P4 (not in A).

**Invoice.** INV-44: Issue with ORD-A; bills ORD-A O2O; PAY-1 settles. ORD-B billing and PAY-2 left open  invoice not artificially closed. One-to-many orders possible but not asserted as fact.

**Payment.** PAY-1: full path to INV-44. PAY-2: object+event exist; settlement unresolved  acceptable unapplied cash island, not an integrity error.

**Suspicious cardinalities.** PK-8 multi-item multi-order; INV-44 potentially multi-order; ev11 potentially multi-delivery—flagged provisional. No many-to-many explosion introduced in A.

**E2OO2O independence.** PK-7/IT-A1 and DEL-X/PK-7 already dual-supported pre-repair. Bundle A’s new dual support for PK-8/IT-B1 and DEL-Y/PK-8 uses attribute evidence (scanner/carrier) plus activity defaults—not mutual bootstrapping alone. Finance dual edges deliberately not dual-accepted to avoid circular “sum  edge  sum confirmed.”

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects

**Unrepaired log.** OC-DFG: Pack Item may show Item-only dangling activity; Dispatch Package from PK-8 lacks Delivery OT co-object; Confirm Delivery only on Order; Issue Invoice Order+Invoice; second Record Payment Payment-only. OTG: missing PK-8–IT-B1, DEL-Y–PK-8, optional finance edges. ET–OT: Pack Item underruns Package; Dispatch underruns Delivery; Record Payment underruns Invoice. ## 8. Over-repair warnings

1. **Forcing PAY-2INV-44 solely because 100+130=230** invents a clean InvoicePayment DFG and hides legitimate unapplied-cash or multi-invoice behavior the schema explicitly allows.

2. **Binding ev11 uniquely to DEL-X (or only DEL-Y)** turns a wildcard portal ack into a single-carrier confirm, fabricating ConfirmDelivery exclusivity and skewing Delivery flattening and on-time carrier KPIs.

3. **Adding ORD-B to ev11 because customer=C17** creates a false joint delivery confirmation across orders and collapses distinct order clusters into one “equivalent execution.”

4. **Linking both packages to DEL-X as bulk dispatch** despite N-420 vs N-421 duplicates Dispatch cardinality, erases DEL-Y’s lifecycle, and produces a wrong one-delivery/two-package OTG core.

5. **Placing IT-B1 into PK-7** against `scanner_ref=PK8-2` invents cross-package contents, pollutes PK-7 weight/route analytics, and can create impossible pack-after-dispatch orders relative to ev09.

6. **Accepting INV-44 bills ORD-B as certain from declared_value sums** materializes Issue Invoice multi-order edges and Order–Invoice OC-DFG paths that may be fee/tax coincidence—downstream cost allocation and order-to-cash conformance then look better than the evidence warrants.

7. **Completing every object “because OCEL likes closed lifecycles”** (e.g., inventing missing confirms for DEL-Y or payments for residual amounts) removes diagnostically valuable incompleteness and yields overconfident OC-DFG frequencies versus the unrepaired reality.
