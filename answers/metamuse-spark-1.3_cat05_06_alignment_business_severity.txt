## 1. Materiality Framework

**Purpose:** Raw alignment cost counts edits under a uniform cost function (1 per visible log/model move). Business materiality asks a different question: did the control objective fail, with what exposure, irreversibility and harm, and how reliable is the evidence?

**Severity 1-5 (business risk, not technical cost):**

* **1 - No control failure / harmless:** Fitting trace, technical noise, synonym/label gap, or out-of-scope model gap with no payment, no customer harm, no regulatory breach. Example: telemetry, retry duplicate, denied/withdrawn with no Pay.
* **2 - Weak assurance / evidence gap:** Substantive control likely performed but not provably retained in linked evidence, or low-harm timing/notice defect. Payment if any was to correct party after required checks. Requires detective fix, not investigation for misconduct.
* **3 - Control bypass with moderate exposure:** Required scrutiny level was not applied (e.g., Auto Approve outside P1) but without the highest-risk triggers (HIGH, >50k, bank change, out-of-sequence payment). Exposure is real but reversible in principle and fraud signal is not acute.
* **4 - High-risk control failure:** P2 trigger (HIGH, >50k, recent bank change) auto-approved or paid without Fraud Screen + Manual Approve, or payment to newly-changed account. Even if amount is small, fraud / account-takeover risk and audit failure are high. Payment may already be irreversible.
* **5 - Severe / critical control failure:** Large-value and/or HIGH-risk payment made without required Fraud Screen, or Pay before applicable approval (P3). Irreversible funds transfer, potential fraud loss, regulatory reportability, and personal accountability of approver. Requires immediate investigation, containment and control redesign.

**Dimensions applied to every case:**

1. **Control objective:** Was the *purpose* met (right payee, right amount, fraud considered, informed claimant) vs. was the *model path* followed?
2. **Amount / exposure:** EUR value + fraud-risk level + bank-change flag. EUR 480k HIGH >> EUR 800 LOW.
3. **Reversibility:** Pay Claim is largely irreversible once bank accepts; Notify Claimant is remediable; Assess Loss can be re-performed.
4. **Timing / sequence:** Pay at 10:03 before Manual Approve at 10:20 is not the same as Pay after Approve, even if both events exist. Alignment can reorder via L+M pair cheaply.
5. **Customer / regulatory harm:** Non-payment, misdirected payment, delayed notice, discriminatory auto-denial.
6. **Recurrence:** Single vs. 74x retry connector vs. 4x silent auto-approval violations vs. 9x external-adjuster gaps.
7. **Evidence reliability:** Core log vs. external signed report vs. verbal claim vs. mutable text-field link vs. same message-ID.
8. **Model scope:** M0 was designed for payable claims. Denied pre-screen leads and claimant withdrawals are disputed scope (P6) - high alignment cost there is model incompleteness, not misconduct.

**Confidence vs. severity:**

* **Severity** = how bad *if the narrative is true*.
* **Confidence** = how strongly the supplied alignment + context + corroborating evidence supports that narrative. A missing log event `M(A)` is **not proof A did not happen**; a synchronous `S(A)` is not proof it was done well; an `L(A)` is not proof it was business-relevant. Confidence is High only with independent corroboration (timestamps, message IDs, external documents, stable IDs, policy attributes). Confidence is Low/Medium when we rely on absence-of-evidence, mutable links, or disputed labels/scope. High severity + low confidence still requires investigation, but not the same action as high severity + high confidence.

Uniform cost 1 per move, no data-awareness, no distinction between reversible telemetry and irreversible payment, is why cost and risk diverge.

## 3. Repeated Deviation Patterns

**Pattern R1 - UI telemetry + transport retry noise (CL-601, CL-603)**
* Frequency: CL-601 (3x telemetry) + CL-603 (1x retry) + month evidence: 74 cases with `L(Notify Claimant)` immediately after synchronous Notify with same message ID, all from one retrying connector.
* Business risk: Nil. No control, financial or customer impact. Risk is analyst fatigue and inflated average cost.
* Likely cause: Unfiltered `Open Workbench/Autosave/Close Workbench` UI events ingested as business events; connector retry without idempotence key handling in conformance pre-processing. Operational/logging cause, not misconduct. Confidence High.
* Owner: Logging / Integration owner + Process analyst (filter + deduplicate on message ID before alignment).

**Pattern R2 - External-adjuster assessment gap `M(Assess Loss)` (CL-606)**
* Frequency: 9 high-value cases in month with `M(Assess Loss)` + external adjuster document; 2 lack stable document-to-case identifier.
* Business risk: Low if document valid and linked (Severity 2), High audit risk if 2 unlinked cases cannot prove assessment for large/HIGH claims.
* Likely cause: Assessment done in external licensed system; core log not interfaced; fragile manual linkage. Missing evidence, not proven skip. Confidence Medium (High where immutable ID exists, Low where not).
* Owner: Claims Operations + IT Integration (ingest adjuster report as event with immutable ID).

**Pattern R3 - Label synonym `L(Assess Damage) + M(Assess Loss)` (CL-611)**
* Frequency: At least CL-611; mapping table updated after case suggests systemic lexicon drift.
* Business risk: Nil for this case (correct checks done). Systemic risk is false non-conformance and wasted investigation.
* Likely cause: Core code `Assess Damage` vs. procedure/UI `Assess Loss`; late mapping update. Event-label mismatch. Confidence High.
* Owner: Data Governance / Process owner (canonical ontology + alias table applied pre-alignment).

**Pattern R4 - Out-of-scope denial / withdrawal high-cost (CL-607, CL-612)**
* Frequency: CL-607 (cost 4), CL-612 (cost 4); month note: denied + withdrawn account for most alignments with raw cost >=4.
* Business risk: Nil to low (no payment). Governance risk: disputed scope (P6) hides real violations in average-cost dashboards.
* Likely cause: M0 designed for payable claims; intake includes denied leads and claimant withdrawals with no modelled exit. Process-model mismatch. Confidence High on facts, Low on verdict until scope decided.
* Owner: Process Owner + Model Owner jointly (scope decision; add `Deny Claim` and `Withdraw Claim` exit branches if in scope).

**Pattern R5 - Data-aware Auto Approve violations with zero cost (CL-609, CL-610)**
* Frequency: 4 cases in month fully synchronous Auto Approve despite violating P1 or P2; supplied examples CL-609 (18k MEDIUM) and CL-610 (4.5k + 1h bank change).
* Business risk: High to critical. Tree allows Auto, data policy forbids it. CL-610 is potential account-takeover with irreversible payment. This is the most dangerous pattern because monitoring on raw cost = 0 misses it entirely.
* Likely cause: Control redesign failure: amount/risk/bank-change guards not encoded in tree nor enforced as system hard block; operators treat model path as permission. Data-aware policy failure. Confidence High (attributes are structured, not inferred from absence).
* Owner: Risk/Control owner + System owner (data-aware guard + hard block), not just modeler.

**Pattern R6 - Skipped / out-of-sequence Fraud-Manual-Pay (CL-602, CL-604; CL-608 is payment-compliant contrast)**
* Frequency: At least 2 severe cases: CL-602 `M(Fraud Screen)` + verbal-only, CL-604 `L(Pay)+M(Fraud)+M(Pay)` out-of-sequence. Both involve P2 triggers (HIGH/>50k/bank change).
* Business risk: Critical (Severity 5). Large irreversible payments without mandated fraud scrutiny; P3 Pay-before-approval.
* Likely cause: Operational deviation (bypass, premature release, post-hoc approval) + weak preventive control (payment can execute without approval token). Confidence High for sequence (timestamps), High-Medium for skip (verbal claim without evidence fails evidence standard even if conversation occurred).
* Owner: Fraud/Risk + Finance Operations (preventive payment hold + detective review) + Internal Audit investigation.

## 6. Why Alignment Cost Is Not Business Risk

Alignment cost was configured uniformly at 1 per visible move with no business weighting, no data conditions, no sequence penalty beyond extra moves, and payable-only scope. Five+ cases prove the divergence:

1. **Zero cost can be critical (CL-610, CL-609):** Both are fully synchronous, cost 0, yet violate P1/P2 data guards. CL-610 auto-approved EUR 4,500 to an account changed 1 hour earlier - textbook redirection signal - and CL-609 auto-approved EUR 18,000 MEDIUM (P1 limit 5k LOW). The tree's `X(Fraud+Manual, Auto)` has no amount/risk/bank-change guard, so data-aware failures are invisible. Cost-function design ignores data; risk does not.
2. **Cost 1 can be catastrophic (CL-602):** One `M(Fraud Screen)` costs 1, same as one duplicate Notify. Business difference: EUR 480,000 HIGH + 1-day bank change paid without fraud evidence vs. a retry. Uniform weighting equates irreversible fraud exposure with harmless duplication. Irreversibility and amount are absent from cost.
3. **Cheap repair hides sequence (CL-604):** `L(Pay)+M(Fraud)+M(Pay)` costs 3 to realign Pay after approval on paper. In reality bank accepted funds at 10:03 before approval at 10:20 - P3 severe control failure. Alignment is order-repairing; business risk is order-dependent. Timestamps, not edit count, prove the breach.
4. **Scope drives high cost without risk (CL-607, CL-612):** Costs 4 each tie for highest, yet neither paid anything. Denied pre-screen and claimant withdrawal have no modelled exit, so the model demands a fictitious Assess/Auto/Pay tail. The note confirms denied/withdrawn dominate cost>=4. Scope incompleteness, not misconduct, inflates cost.
5. **Granularity/label and duplication inflate cost (CL-611, CL-601, CL-603):** CL-611 costs 2 for one real assessment logged as `Assess Damage` vs. `Assess Loss` - synonym counted twice. CL-601 costs 3 for UI telemetry that P5 explicitly excludes. CL-603 costs 1 for a same-message-ID retry that proves reliable delivery. Activity granularity and idempotence are modelling choices, not risk.
6. **Missing-but-external and hidden moves (CL-606, CL-605, CL-608):** CL-606 `M(Assess Loss)` costs 1 but a signed external report at 10:05 likely satisfies substance - absence  non-occurrence. CL-605 `T(tau)` costs 0 and is correctly fitting routing, not a skip - hidden moves must be narrated, not mistaken for gaps. CL-608 `M(Notify)` costs 1 but a +3h portal message may satisfy P4 if linkage were immutable - evidence reliability, not existence, is the issue.

In short: cost counts *edits*; risk weighs *control purpose, data conditions, timing, irreversibility, harm, recurrence and proof quality*. Prioritizing by raw cost would chase denied/withdrawn/telemetry (cost 3-4, severity 1) while missing auto-approved bank-change fraud (cost 0, severity 4-5).
