## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

### Intervention Specification
- **Eligibility Rules**: Parallel verification allowed for cases without original documents required.
- **Concurrent Tasks**: Identity Check and Document Check can be created and executed concurrently after registration.
- **Statutory Prerequisites**: Legal Review remains mandatory for high-value cases.
- **Queue and WIP Controls**: WIP limit of 6 tasks per reviewer; daily queue huddles retained.
- **Resource Responsibilities**: Identity reviewers and document reviewers operate independently; assessors handle verification results.
- **Legal Review & Supplement Loops**: Legal Review followed by Assessment; Supplement loop triggered if legal review requests supplement.
- **Logging Requirements**: Detailed logging of verification intervals and legal review outcomes.

### POWL Description
```
Register -> (Identity Check, Document Check)
Identity Check -> Assessment
Document Check -> Assessment
Assessment -> (Approve, Legal Review)
Legal Review -> Request Supplement (if required)
Request Supplement -> Document Check (repeat)
Document Check -> Assessment
Assessment -> Reject
```

### Explanation
The pilot design isolates the effect of parallel verification while controlling for confounding factors. It ensures that any observed improvements can be attributed to the intervention rather than other changes. The pilot will provide evidence on whether the intervention can be safely scaled to Site B.

## 9. Recommendation

**Conditional Go**

### Explanation
While parallel verification shows promise in improving median cycle times, the legal review requirement and original documents need careful handling in Site B. A conditional go allows us to pilot the intervention in a controlled manner, focusing on digital claims and cases without legal review. This approach minimizes risk while providing valuable data on process efficiency. Expansion should be contingent on successful pilot outcomes and further evidence of reduced rework and appeal rates.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Assumption**: Parallel verification reduces median cycle time.  
   **Why Needed**: To justify the intervention's primary benefit.  
   **How to Test**: Compare median cycle times before and after intervention.  
   **Consequence if False**: Intervention may not show expected efficiency gains.

2. **Assumption**: Original documents do not block parallel verification.  
   **Why Needed**: Originals receipt delay could negate benefits.  
   **How to Test**: Monitor original document processing times.  
   **Consequence if False**: Legal review may increase significantly.

   **Why Needed**: Legal review cannot be bypassed.  
   **How to Test**: Track legal review instances.  
   **Consequence if False**: Potential legal complications.

4. **Assumption**: Resource capacity can support parallel tasks.  
   **Why Needed**: Resource constraints could limit parallelism.  
   **How to Test**: Monitor reviewer workload.
