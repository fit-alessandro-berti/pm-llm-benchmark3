## Scope, terminology, and labelled assumptions

**Terminology used throughout:** an *operational batch* is a batch in the physics of production (items genuinely processed together or as one engineered cycle); a *logging batch* is a batch-shaped impression created purely by how timestamps were captured (shared buttons, bulk forms, rounding, backfilled lifecycle fields). The two produce **identical temporal signatures**, so this dossier always triangulates three evidence streams: (i) the timestamp geometry itself, (ii) field semantics (`recorded_at`, `sequence_no`, `precision`), (iii) collateral process context supplied in the scenario. PM4Py batch labels are treated as *temporal pattern detections only*, never as proof of physical execution semantics.

**Labelled assumptions:**

- **A1 — Resource identity**: identifiers map to one worker except `analyst_pool`, which the scenario declares a shared login with up to 3 analysts. Needed because concurrency-impossibility arguments (e.g., 4 overlapping intervals for one human) depend on identity exclusivity.
- **A2 — Point treatment of interval-censored stamps**: `HH:MM` stamps are computed as point values (start-of-minute), while their interval nature ([t, t+tick)) is tracked in the flags and Discussion §5. Needed because arithmetic on points is computationally tractable; conclusions derived from gaps smaller than the tick are consciously suppressed.
- **A3 — Adjacency tolerance 61 s**: a "chained" pair satisfies |start<sub>i+1</sub>  complete<sub>i</sub>|  61 s, i.e., one coarse tick + margin. Needed to make adjacency detection robust to 1-minute-precision sources without rubber-stamping larger gaps.
- **A4 — Safety-system clock skew left symbolic ()**: the scenario only asserts that safety and repair clocks differ; the magnitude is unknown, so the holdrestart link in G8 is treated as directional evidence, not a calibrated delay.
- **A5 — Single site/day clock domain**: rows are assumed commensurable unless internal contradictions (G8) force qualification.

## Per-cluster reading notes

- **G1**: Third-ranked rivals (start batching, queue accumulation) fail because completions are *also* fused and arrivals are tightly gathered, which start-batching alone does not predict and an unloaded queue cannot produce with a 2–6 min deliberate staging gap. Confidence is capped because "fused within 1 s" is a *bound*, not an observation of simultaneity.
- **G2**: The mirror twin of G3: G2 fuses the *beginning*, G3 fuses the *end*. Note that observed "service time" grows with start rank (827 min), whereas in G3 it *shrinks* (286 min) — opposite regression directions arising from opposite fusion points. Either regression, if published naively, is an artifact of logging, not labor.
- **G3**: The strongest datum is the *shape*, not the volume: service estimates exactly anti-ranked with start order is what consolidated submission generates mechanically; randomized genuine completion times would not track start order so cleanly.
- **G4**: This cluster may simply be healthy serial work — the point is that its *structure* (perfect chaining) is isomorphic to what template-inheritance would fabricate, so provenance must be verified before celebrating it as clean ground truth. Its queue signal (rising waits, arrival rate 1/min vs capacity 1/4min) is the most trustworthy backlog picture in the dataset.
- **G5**: No allocation of the four open windows to 3 badges with all-parallel semantics exists (peak 4 > 3), so *something* in the windows is inflated (switching dwell, suspended sessions, or heterogeneous stamping). Exactly *what* is unrecoverable from this log.
- **G6**: Held at high confidence as a *recording artifact diagnosis*; what remains unknown is only the physical fate of the tickets between arrival and 17:00.
- **G7**: Coarsening is diagnosed from the data itself (negative waits are an internal contradiction), independent of the MES remark. Residual confidence concerns only the *label*, not the underlying mode, which is unrecoverable.
- **G8**: Structurally a sibling of G4 — the discriminator is the exogenous pre-gap corroborated (directionally, modulo ) by the independent safety system.

**Why G6-type data manufactures inflated waiting *and* near-zero service simultaneously.** The bulk form writes both lifecycle stamps from the same keystroke at submission time (17:00). Consequently: (1) *waiting* is computed as start  arrival = entry time  arrival, which folds together genuine queue time, unobserved touch time, and idle parking — 35–175 minutes of phantom "wait"; (2) *service* is complete  start = 0 by construction, because the two fields are copies of one another; (3) *utilization* collapses to zero because no interval length survives; (4) *throughput* acquires a spurious burst (7 completions in one minute) that poisons hourly profiles and any Little's Law instantiation (L = W blows up while the implied service rate goes to infinity). One defective field-writing habit therefore biases all four foundational queues/server quantities **in opposite directions at once** — this is the archetype of a logging batch masquerading as operations, and exactly the mistake equating "identical timestamps  simultaneous work" commits.

**Cross-cutting bias laws** (recurrent across clusters):

- *Batch-size dilution* (G1, partly G2): when b items share one operational cycle, per-case service overstates unit consumption by b unless divided through.
- *Fusion corridors* (G2, G3, G6, G7): whenever many events are stamped at one instant (button, form, snap), variance at the fused edge collapses and spurious correlations with arrival rank appear (positive under start-fusion, negative under end-fusion).
- *Purge cliffs* (G6, G8's restart): end-of-period dumps fake sudden capacity and refresh the queue; transient-outage drains (G8) make utilization look bimodal and waits look demand-driven.
- *Snapping floors* (G7, boundary-minute effects in G4/G8): truncation raises apparent occupancy to full bins, zero-lengthens short touches, and can invert event order (negative waits) — worse than missing data, because it is confidently wrong.
- *Conformance contamination*: mining sees instant parallel transitions or duplicated instants, yielding falsely loose models; batch constructs must be modeled explicitly rather than filtered away as "noise," since the filter deletes real structure.

# 4. PM4Py and pandas analysis

Design notes before the code:

1. **Event log layout**: completion is exposed as `time:timestamp` (the event's ledger timestamp) and start as `start_timestamp`, giving PM4Py interval semantics; case, activity, and resource follow standard keys. Each cluster is one `(activity, resource)` combination, so PM4Py's batch discovery — which groups events by that pair — maps almost one-to-one onto clusters; this makes the comparison legible.
2. **Parameter grid**: batch discovery is run over at least two `merge_distance` (60 s, 300 s, 900 s) × two `min_batch_size` (2, 4) settings. Expectations (verifiable by running the script, not asserted as output): with tight merge distance G1/G6/G7 appear as simultaneous batches (fused walls); G2 stays "overlapping" because its finishes diverge far more than 60 s; chains in G4/G8 register as sequential batching; enlarging `min_batch_size` to 4 drops the four-strong pairs and keeps only 4-member groups.
3. **Extraction robustness**: PM4Py's batch result container has drifted across versions (lists of tuples/dicts/enums). The summarizer below reads categories and cluster membership defensively via string scanning, and isolates failures per configuration rather than crashing the diagnostics.
4. **Preservation policy**: no row is dropped. Because the supplied table contains no blank lifecycle fields, "preservation of suspect semantics" is demonstrated *behaviorally*: zero-length (G6), negative-wait (G7), and shared-identity (G5) rows are retained and surfaced through derived flags (`ZERO_LEN_ALL`, `NEG_WAIT`, `SHARED_LOGIN`, `COARSE>=15min`, `MASS_ONE_REC_MIN`, `CHAIN_ADJACENT`) plus a per-cluster disposition suggestion.
5. Independent pandas diagnostics computed: pairwise strict-overlap rate per resource, coincident-interval rate (fused *walls* — stronger than mere overlap), peak concurrency, uniqueness of start/end, recording-lag statistics, second-alignment and quarter-grid alignment shares, queue build-up (max open backlog at start times), waiting statistics with negative-wait counts, and the wait-versus-arrival-rank slope.

## Temporal patterns supporting multiple physical explanations

1. **Fully fused walls (identical start *and* end)** — seen in G1, G6, G7. Supports, depending only on actor/source context: a genuine machine batch (G1), end-of-shift bulk entry (G6), or wholesale quarter-hour snapping (G7). Three ontologically different realities, byte-identical data.
2. **Perfect start = previous-complete chains** — G4 and G8. Supports: (a) authentic serial drain of a queued single server; (b) template/cascaded auto-fill, where the system copies the predecessor's completion into the successor's start (H9); (c) in G8 additionally, externally gated resumption rather than organic demand-paced work. Only provenance auditing (was a human present at each junction?) separates these.
3. **Single-edge fusion, mirrored** — G2 (all starts equal, ends staggered) and G3 (starts staggered, ends equal). The same family "common endpoint, private counterpart" supports: shared begin-button UX logging (G2), consolidated form submission (G3), but also honest shared setups or honest synchronized wrap-ups. Direction of fusion identifies the *mechanism class* only probabilistically.
4. *(Bonus)* **Dense interleave on a lone identifier** — G5 vs G3. Nearly the same overlap topology with opposite root causes: hidden multi-humans vs documented single submitter. Without provenance fields, even overlap-heavy topologies under-determine the world.

# 6. Data-collection redesign

Priorities reflect expected bias reduction per engineering euro; acceptance criteria make the fixes auditable.

- **P0 — Execution timestamps at the point of action.** Emit begin/finish per item at the workstation itself (button-press, scan, PLC edge), never backfilled. Mandate *reason codes* for any manual override. Acceptance: 1 s deviation from independent observer audit on a sampled shift; zero backfilled fields. Directly kills G2/G3/G6 pathology classes.
- **P0 — Timestamp-precision metadata, enforced.** Store documented resolution *and* achieved-latency percentiles per source; flag any event whose emitted value sits exactly on a rounding boundary beyond chance. Acceptance: share of minute-aligned event stamps inconsistent with declared precision < 1%. Kills the G7 illusion and exposes minute-truncation like the suspected G4/G8 boundary effect.
- **P0 — Source-clock governance.** NTP-sync all subsystems (MES, safety PLC, shop scanners); log clock offset/jump history alongside events; embed the authoritative clock ID per row. Acceptance: inter-system skew bound published and < stated decision tolerance (e.g., 5 s). Required before G8's hold-causality can ever be certified rather than hypothesized.
- **P1 — Resource/badge identity.** One badge/token per person; forbid shared credentials technically; map sessionsindividuals at ingestion. Acceptance: zero events with shared-login attribution; spot audits reconcile. Restores the entire utilization/productivity layer now void at G5 (and keeps multi-operator stations honest elsewhere).
- **P1 — Explicit batch/group identifiers.** When work is intentionally grouped, write `group_id`, member cardinality, slot/position, and group begin/finish. Acceptance: every operational batch queryable without geometric inference. Converts G1-style cycles and G2-style group runs from *guessed* to *declared* semantics, letting analytics divide by batch size instead of conflating unit and cycle.
- **P1 — Workstation/process state machine (capacity & holds).** Model stations with capacity and states (up, held, down, paused) emitting state-transition events — the safety hold (G8) becomes first-class data with its own clock-tagged timestamps. Acceptance: every wait decomposable into queue-wait vs unavailable-state wait.
- **P2 — Pause/resume lifecycle.** Support suspend/resume on long or interruptible activities; accumulate active vs elapsed time separately. Eliminates the G5 inflation channel (switch-away dwell booked as service) and protects knowledge work from elapsed-time lies.
- **P2 — Recording provenance separation.** Keep `recorded_at` (and `sequence_no`) immutable and separate from event semantics forever; monitor insertion-lag distributions per source; alert on single-minute mass waves (the G6 signature would trigger an alarm day one). Acceptance: lag outliers page a data steward, not silently enter reports.
- **Ongoing — automated lifecycle validation gates** (derived, not discretionary): reject or quarantine events with `complete < start` or arrival-after-start; zero-duration pairs require a positive reason code; concurrency per accountable human capped by registered staffing. The script's flag vocabulary in §4 sketches exactly this gate set.

**Tier 1 — interpretable with high confidence (use for performance analytics, with stated corrections).**

- **G4**: use directly as a sequential drain; waits (719 min) and 4-min takt are sound conditional on one stamp-provenance verification (operator scan vs template inheritance). Best-quality substrate in the dataset.
- **G8**: adopt the outage-then-drain reading for service/utilization and post-restart throughput; *pre-restart waits must be decomposed* — they blend genuine scarcity with the documented hold, and the hold linkage carries symbol- clock uncertainty. - **G1**: interpret as a four-up operational batch (confidence 78) *for throughput purposes* (unit  45 s of cycle), but mark the parallel-vs-serial-in-cycle question open; only intra-cycle telemetry settles it, and the difference matters for unit-cost, not throughput.

**Tier 2 — exclude or interval-censor in queue/waiting analysis.**

- **G6**: exclude outright from waiting, service, utilization, and any Little's-Law application as recorded; replace fields with true close events or drop. Nothing in the cluster constrains physical process at all (confidence 92 that it is a recording construct).
- **G7**: interval-censor everything to 15-min bins; all point arithmetic is currently invalid (four negative waits prove it internally). Until native-resolution capture exists, permit only bucket-level throughput claims.
- **G3**: retain starts (pickups look genuine) but censor completion in [start, 11:30]; all completion-derived metrics are submission artifacts until finish/submit are separated.
- **G2**: censor each piece's start to [10:00, own finish]; trusts the completion staircase, discards the fused onset.
