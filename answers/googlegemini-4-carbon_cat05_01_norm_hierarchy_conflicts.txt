## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

### Separation of Epistemic Categories
*   **Established Facts:** GPIP-4 is board-approved; SOP DE-AAC-PROC-17 (SOP-17) is local; ECD-2 was signed by the COO and Incident Commander (IC) for 2026-07-14 09:00 to 2026-07-18 18:00; the CRO was unavailable and never issued an A7 exception; the CFO owns the Finance control framework and sent Source D on 2026-07-15 at 07:20; ERP outage ON-44 occurred 07-14 08:30–13:00.
*   **Interpretations:** How source authority interacts across domains (operational vs. financial vs. risk/compliance); the meaning of undefined terms ("critical sterilization-line lots", "ordinary local workflow steps", "new" vs. "unapproved" supplier).
*   **Assumptions:** Standard corporate governance principles apply where texts are silent (e.g., an executive cannot unilaterally waive a control owned by a peer/board-mandated function; non-retroactivity of informal guidance; real-world execution timestamps govern over delayed system-ingestion timestamps).

### Core Analytical Principles
1.  **Domain-Specific Authority (Rejection of Naive Total Ordering):** Neither *lex superior* (GPIP-4 wins all), *lex posterior* (CFO email wins all), nor *lex specialis* (ECD-2 wins all) suffices alone. Authority is domain-partitioned:
    *   *Board/CRO Domain (Integrity & Third-Party Risk):* GPIP-4 Segregation of Duties (SoD, A1) and Compliance Clearance (A3) are core integrity controls. Under A4/A7, neither the COO (ECD-2) nor the CFO (Source D) holds delegated authority to waive them, and past precedent explicitly excludes Compliance clearance from incident overlays.
    *   *COO/IC Domain (Operational Workflow & Local Register):* Via SOP-17 clause B7, ECD-2 validly supersedes Aachen SOP-17’s PO-first rule (B1), Plant_Approve (B2), 6-hour horizon (B4), and local approved-supplier register (B3) within ECD-2's scope and time window.
    *   *CFO Domain (Financial Controls & Budget Authority):* Because the CFO explicitly owns the Finance control framework (Fact) and sits on the executive committee, Source D functions as a valid administrative interpretation/standing functional approval for `Finance_Approve` (A2/B2) and recognizes joint IC members as temporary `budget owners` (A2) from 2026-07-15 07:20 until ECD-2 expiry—subject to its own conditions (EUR 150k cap or `Legal_Clearance`, and D4 preservation of integrity controls like SoD). It is *not* retroactive to 07-14.
2.  **Terminological Alignment ("Unapproved" vs. "New" Supplier):** SOP-17 B3 restricts purchases to the local "approved-supplier register." GPIP-4 A3 triggers `Compliance_Clearance` for a "new supplier." **Interpretation:** Any supplier outside the approved-supplier register ("unapproved") lacks vetted onboarding status and therefore triggers GPIP-4 A3 ("new supplier") unless proven otherwise. ECD-2 C2 waives the *prohibition* on using unapproved suppliers (B3), while C4 waives full *supplier-onboarding* workflows, but per C5 and A4, it cannot waive pre-commitment *Compliance_Clearance* (A3).
3.  **Scope of "Critical Sterilization-Line Lots" (ECD-2 C6):** Internal Audit’s strict 24-hour cutoff conflates ECD-2 with SOP-17’s 6-hour rule and ignores procurement lead times during a multi-day contamination event. Operations' view that *any* backup part qualifies risks unlimited scope creep. **Interpretation:** Scope covers components directly required to restore, contain, or prevent imminent failure of active or actively relied-upon backup sterilization lines during the incident window, *provided* existing stock does not comfortably outlast normal replenishment or the directive window. Non-production items (cafeteria) are strictly excluded.
4.  **Temporality & Point-in-Time Evaluation:** Compliance is evaluated at the moment of `Commit_to_Supplier` (the legally binding external act). Rules effective only *after* commitment (e.g., ECD-2 at 09:00 for an 08:50 commitment; CFO email on 07-15 for a 07-14 commitment) cannot retroactively validate an unauthorized act. Post-hoc approvals mitigate severity (A6) but do not cure breach.
5.  **Evidential Weight & Burden of Proof:** External, contemporaneous records (supplier email acknowledgments, call logs, cryptographically/system-dated PDFs) defeat delayed ERP entry timestamps, especially during documented outages (ON-44 or document-queue delays). However, the burden of proof for invoking an emergency exception rests on the buyer: uncorroborated verbal claims of directive extensions fail.
6.  **Classification Taxonomy:**
    *   `COMPLIANT`: Satisfies baseline rules OR satisfies all conditions of a *formally valid* override (e.g., ECD-2 + CFO Email within their true authority, plus GPIP-4 A1/A3).
    *   `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`: Violates a formal rule where an override lacked strict legal authority or suffered a minor non-integrity procedural defect, *but* operational necessity was acute, good-faith reliance on executive directives existed, and core integrity controls (SoD, Compliance where high-risk) were respected or mitigated.
    *   `NONCOMPLIANT`: Breaches core non-waivable controls (SoD, pre-commitment Compliance clearance without excuse), acts wholly outside emergency scope, or acts after directive expiry without valid emergency conditions.
    *   `INDETERMINATE`: Material classification hinges on an unverified factual/technical ambiguity that available evidence cannot resolve.

### Defeasible Pseudo-Logic

```prolog
% 1. Segregation of Duties (Non-defeasible without CRO A7)
normally prohibited(approve(Actor, Case)) :- 
    (requested(Actor, Case) ; committed(Actor, Case)), 
    counts_as_final_approver(Actor, Case),
    unless(supported_by(cro_written_exception_A7(Case))).

% 2. Compliance Clearance (Strict Risk Control)
normally required_before(compliance_clearance(Case), commit(Case)) :-
    (amount(Case, Amt), Amt > 100000 ; supplier_status(Case, unapproved_or_new)),
    unless(supported_by(cro_written_exception_A7(Case))).

% 3. ECD-2 Operational & Supplier-Register Override
overrides_for(ecd2, [po_before_commit, plant_approve, approved_supplier_ban], Scope, Time) :-
    in_scope(Case, critical_sterilization_lot),
    commit_time(Case, T), T >= '2026-07-14T09:00', T =< '2026-07-18T18:00',
    supported_by(joint_ic_auth_before_commit(Case, IC1, IC2)), IC1 \= IC2,
    supported_by(recorded_within_4h(Case, AuthTime)),
    Scope = Case, Time = T.

% 4. CFO Functional Override for Finance & Budget Owner Approvals
overrides_for(cfo_email, [finance_approve, budget_owner_approve], Case, T) :-
    overrides_for(ecd2, _, Case, T),
    T >= '2026-07-15T07:20', T =< '2026-07-18T18:00',
    not_violated(segregation_of_duties(Case)),
    ( (amount(Case, Amt), Amt =< 150000) ; 
      (amount(Case, Amt), Amt > 150000, supported_by(pre_commit_legal_clearance(Case))) ).

% 5. Local SOP Emergency Fallback (When ECD-2 Inapplicable)
overrides_for(sop_b4, [po_before_commit], Case, T) :-
    (expected_stoppage_hours(Case, H), H =< 6 ; safety_harm_imminent(Case)),
    supported_by(emergency_form_within_24h(Case, T)).

% 6. Shift Lead Substitution Constraint
normally invalid(shift_lead_substitution(Lead, Case)) :-
    plant_manager_reachable(Case) ; not(formally_delegated(Lead, Case)).

% 7. Evidential Priority Under System Outage / Clock Skew
effective_time(Act, T_ext) :-
    supported_by(external_or_cryptographic_evidence(Act, T_ext)),
    erp_timestamp(Act, T_erp), T_erp > T_ext,
    supported_by(documented_outage_or_clock_skew(T_ext, T_erp)).

% 8. Non-Curative Post-Hoc Approval
mitigates_severity_only(ApproveAct, Case) :-
    required_before(ApproveAct, commit(Case)),
    effective_time(ApproveAct, T_app), effective_time(commit(Case), T_com), T_app > T_com.
```

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

1.  **Cases P-507 & P-508 — Strict *Lex Superior* / Anti-Email Formalism (`COMPLIANT` / `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` $\rightarrow$ `NONCOMPLIANT`):**
    *   *Premise Changed:* Reject the premise that the CFO’s uncontrolled email (Source D) can operationalize or substitute for GPIP-4 A2’s explicit requirement of pre-commitment approval by an "independent budget owner and a Finance Controller." Under strict textualism, GPIP-4 A4/A7 states *all* non-tendering controls remain unless waived in writing by the CRO, and an ordinary email outside the policy repository cannot alter a board-approved policy—especially since IC members are not chartered budget owners.
    *   *Credibility Assessment:* **Moderately Credible legally, Weak operationally.** Textually, A4 and A7 strongly support this for audit purists. However, it is weak as an accountability standard because the CFO *owns* the Finance control framework; penalizing buyers who obtained Compliance, Legal, and joint IC sign-offs in reliance on explicit written instructions from the Chief Financial Officer confuses executive policy-drafting friction with process noncompliance.

2.  **Case P-505 — Strict System-of-Record / Expiration Cut-Off (`COMPLIANT` $\rightarrow$ `NONCOMPLIANT` or `INDETERMINATE`):**
    *   *Premise Changed:* Treat the moment of internal system entry (`Commit_to_Supplier` at 18:06 in ERP) as constitutive of an authorized corporate commitment, OR treat the unverified clock-skew hypothesis ("ERP clock *may* have been nine minutes fast") as insufficient without proved synchronization logs, while holding that an external supplier email timestamp (17:59:31) could reflect a manipulated or non-CEST client header unless server-verified. Additionally, note that `Create_PO` and the 4-hour IC recording are absent from the case extract.
    *   *Credibility Assessment:* **Weak on commitment timing; Credible on missing post-commit artifacts (`INDETERMINATE`).** Legally, a contract/commitment forms upon external communication with the supplier (call at 17:57, supplier ack at 17:59:31), not subsequent ERP data entry—so 17:59:31 beats 18:06 even *without* ERP clock skew. However, shifting to `INDETERMINATE` is credible if the judge treats the silence in the case table regarding `Create_PO` and the 4-hour written IC log as potentially missing steps rather than omitted uncontroversial facts.

3.  **Case P-501 — Substantive Emergency Necessity & Outage Impossibility (`NONCOMPLIANT` $\rightarrow$ `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`):**
    *   *Premise Changed:* Apply an *operational necessity / ultra posse nemo obligatur* principle during the 20-minute gap before ECD-2 took effect (08:42–09:00) concurrent with the onset of ERP Outage ON-44 (started 08:30). ## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

> **GPIP-4 Clause A7 (Replacement) — Emergency Directives, Norm Hierarchy, and Non-Waivable Controls**
>
> **A7.1 Exclusive Authority & Activation:** A temporary Emergency Continuity Directive (ECD) may be issued jointly in writing by the COO and Chief Risk Officer (CRO)—or, if the CRO is unreachable for >30 minutes during an active Tier-1 safety or contamination incident, by the COO, CFO, and a designated Deputy Risk/Compliance Officer. Uncontrolled emails or verbal statements outside the policy repository cannot create, expand, or extend an ECD.
>
> **A7.2 Explicit Scope & Inventory Horizon:** Every ECD must define eligible asset lines and a maximum stock-depletion horizon (default: components required to prevent line stoppage within **48 hours**; contingency restocking where on-hand stock exceeds 48 hours or non-production/facility repairs are strictly excluded).
>
> **A7.3 Tiered Thresholds & Delegated Approvals During an Active ECD:**
> *   **Operational & PO Timing:** Commitments may precede PO creation, and two distinct Incident Command (IC) members are formally designated as temporary operational/budget approvers overriding local SOP plant approvals.
> *   **Financial Controls:** Joint IC authorization satisfies `Finance_Approve` up to **EUR 50,000**. Purchases **EUR 50,000–150,000** require pre-commitment electronic/recorded sign-off by a Finance Controller (unless expressly delegated in the signed ECD by the CFO). Purchases **> EUR 150,000** additionally require pre-commitment `Legal_Clearance`.
>
> **A7.4 Non-Waivable Integrity & Third-Party Risk Controls:** No ECD or executive instruction may waive:
> 1.  *Segregation of Duties:* Neither the requester nor the committer may serve as one of the required IC or functional approvers.
> 2.  *Expedited Compliance Screening:* Purchases **> EUR 100,000** or from **any supplier not on the active approved-supplier register** (treated as a "new supplier") require pre-commitment `Compliance_Clearance` (an expedited 1-hour sanctions/integrity desktop screen is authorized during an ECD in lieu of full onboarding).
>
> **A7.5 Outage Evidence & Retrospective Recording:** Verbal approvals permitted during an ECD or verified IT outage must be logged via timestamped audio, email, or signed PDF within **4 hours** of commitment and entered into the ERP within **12 hours** of system recovery. Verifiable contemporaneous external or cryptographic timestamps govern over delayed ERP ingestion timestamps.
>
> **A7.6 Hard Expiry & Anti-Normalization Safeguard:** An ECD automatically expires after **96 hours** (hard stop; zero verbal extensions). Any renewal requires a fresh written authorization co-signed by the CRO. **Safeguard:** Every ECD activation triggers a mandatory Internal Audit post-mortem within 10 business days; if >15% of ECD transactions breach scope or if Aachen invokes >2 emergency directives in any rolling 6-month period, local emergency self-authorization privileges are automatically suspended pending Audit Committee review.
