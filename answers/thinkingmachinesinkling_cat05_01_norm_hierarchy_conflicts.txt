Let's draft the clause.

"Emergency Procurement Exception Framework (EPEF)
1. Authority: Only a written, scoped, time-limited exception by the Chief Risk Officer (GPIP-4 A7) may waive GPIP-4 A1-A3. A jointly signed COO/Incident Commander directive (ECD-type) may supersede local SOP B timing and supplier-register rules (B1, B3, B4 delegation) but may never waive GPIP-4 A1, A2, A3, or A6.
2. Scope: "Critical sterilization-line lots" means parts required for lines with confirmed or projected stoppage within 24 hours. Backup-line contingency parts with stock coverage exceeding 24 hours are excluded unless separately designated by the CRO.
3. Thresholds: A2 (EUR 50k: independent budget owner + Finance Controller), A3 (>EUR 100k or unapproved supplier: Compliance_Clearance), and B2 (EUR 25k: Plant + Finance) apply in full during any emergency. ## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Proposed replacement clause: Emergency Procurement Integrity and Exception Framework (EPIEF)**

> **1. Authority and exception exclusivity.** Only the Chief Risk Officer may issue a written, scoped, time-limited exception to GPIP-4 A1, A2, A3, or A6 under GPIP-4 A7. A jointly signed COO/Incident Commander directive (ECD-type) may supersede local SOP B timing rules (B1), supplier-register requirements (B3), and local Plant-approval substitution (B4 delegation) within its explicit time window and scope, but it **never** waives GPIP-4 A1 (segregation), A2 (budget owner + Finance Controller), A3 (Compliance_Clearance), or A6 (no retrospective cure).
>
> **2. Scope definition.** “Critical sterilization-line lots required to contain the current contamination event” means parts required for the affected sterilization line with a confirmed or projected stoppage within **24 hours**. Backup-line contingency parts with verified stock coverage exceeding 24 hours are excluded unless separately designated in writing by the Chief Risk Officer.
>
> **3. Thresholds and approvals (unaffected by emergency).** For all purchases:
> -  EUR 25,000 (Aachen): Plant_Approve and Finance_Approve required before commitment (B2), except that an actively delegated Shift Lead may substitute for an unreachable Plant Manager (B4).
> -  EUR 50,000 (global): independent budget owner and Finance Controller approval required before commitment (A2).
> - > EUR 100,000 or any unapproved/new supplier: Compliance_Clearance required before commitment (A3).
> Joint Incident Command authorization satisfies only the local Plant-approval substitution (when B4 applies) and the directive-specific authorization form (C3); it does not satisfy A2 budget-owner/Finance-Controller requirements or A3.
>
> **4. Segregation of duties (absolute).** The individual who requests the purchase, the individual who commits to the supplier, and any final business approver—including any Incident Command authorizer—must be **three distinct persons**. No single individual may hold more than one of these roles for a single purchase.
>
> **5. New suppliers and supplier onboarding.** Use of a supplier outside the approved register is permitted only within a valid ECD-type window and scope (C2), but **Compliance_Clearance (A3) remains mandatory before commitment** for any such supplier, regardless of amount. No verbal or post-commitment clearance satisfies this rule.
>
> **6. Timing, expiry, and extension.** ECD-type directives are valid only within the explicit start and end timestamps stated in the directive. Any extension requires a **new jointly signed authorization** specifying a new time window. The new authorization must be recorded within 4 hours. **Verbal extensions are invalid.** After expiry, all ordinary rules (B1, B2, B3, A2, A3) apply in full.
>
> **7. Retrospective evidence and cure prohibition.** Post-commitment approvals, including those recorded in ERP after recovery, mitigate organizational consequences but **do not cure** missing pre-commitment approvals (A6). Where ERP timestamps conflict with contemporaneous manual records (signed PDFs, supplier acknowledgments, telephone recordings, access logs), the independent contemporaneous record governs for proving the time of the act. During any system outage, procurement personnel must create a manual, time-stamped authorization record within 4 hours.
>
> **8. Anti-normalization safeguard.** If any Aachen site uses emergency authorization for more than **three purchases in a rolling 30-day period**, or for any single purchase exceeding EUR 150,000 without a valid CRO A7 exception, local emergency authorization authority is **automatically suspended**, and a mandatory board-level audit must be completed within **5 business days** before any further emergency commitments are permitted.

This clause resolves authority (CRO exclusivity for core waivers), scope (24-hour definition), thresholds (explicit retention of A2/A3/B2), segregation (three-role separation), new suppliers (C2 permitted but A3 retained), retrospective records (independent evidence governs, no cure), and expiry (written joint extension only, with anti-normalization trigger).
