## 1. System boundary and assumptions

### System boundary
The environmental and operational assessment covers a **gate-to-customer-delivery** boundary for spare-part order fulfillment, bounded by the activities and attributes captured in the enriched event log:
* **Inside the boundary:**
  * **Order intake, planning, and inventory allocation:** `Receive order`, `Availability check`, `Pick from stock`, `Partial shipment`, `Backorder wait`, `Batch wait`, and `Cancel due to delay`.
  * **Shop-floor manufacturing and quality control:** `CNC production` (`CNC_A`, `CNC_B`), `Emergency production`, `Heat treatment` (`heat_treat`), `Inspection` (`inspection_cell`), and `Rework production`, captured via `energy_kwh`, `material_scrap_kg`, `batch_id`, `batch_size`, and `rework_flag`.
  * **Packaging and packaging rework:** `Package standard`, `Package reinforced`, and `Rework packaging`, captured via `package_material_kg`.
  * **Outbound distribution:** `Ship ground` (`parcel_ground`), `Ship air` (`parcel_air`), `Dedicated van` (`dedicated_van`), and `Consolidated truck` (`consolidated_truck`), including second-leg shipments from `Partial shipment`, captured via `transport_kgco2e` and `expedite_flag`.
  * **Short-term inventory waste:** Batch-produced items remaining unshipped after 14 days (6% obsolescence rate).
* **Outside the boundary:**
  * Upstream raw-material extraction, steel/alloy smelting, and embodied emissions of purchased spare parts and packaging stock.
  * Facility baseline energy (HVAC, lighting, compressed-air idle losses) not allocated to order-level `energy_kwh`.
  * Reverse-logistics transport and disposal/recycling emissions for customer returns (damage returns are linked qualitatively to Q1 violations, but return legs are not separately logged).
  * Downstream environmental or economic impacts of customer equipment downtime.

### Key assumptions and remaining uncertainties
* **Order-level flattening:** Multi-line orders are flattened to a single case, masking SKU-level weight/geometry heterogeneity, shared `batch_id` composition across different customer segments, and how `Partial shipment` (1,010 cases) splits physical parcels.
* **Unmeasured transport utilization:** Dedicated vans "often return empty," and consolidated trucks rely on fill thresholds, but load factors, empty-backhaul allocation rules in `transport_kgco2e`, and regional carrier availability (relevant to R1) are not directly observed.
* **Physical units without universal conversion factors:** `energy_kwh`, `material_scrap_kg`, and `package_material_kg` are logged in physical units without grid-intensity or material-emission factors; therefore, energy, material, and transport impacts should be evaluated multi-dimensionally rather than collapsed into an assumed single CO2e figure.
* **Low model precision on exception paths:** Disruption handling generates varied combinations of emergency production, partial shipments, and rework loops, creating forecast and routing uncertainty when rules interact.

## 3. Intervention options

Ten candidate interventions from S1–S12 are evaluated below across process paths, impact mechanisms, trade-offs, and monitoring requirements.

### 1. S1 & S4: Slack-aware green shipping recommender and priority-pick + ground replacement for Standard-stock Service-contract
* **Target path or segment:** `Standard-stock Service-contract` (16% of orders, 7.8 kgCO2e avg) and `Package standard/reinforced -> Ship air` (1,420 total air cases), especially the 22% violating SP1.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Uses `promised_lead_time_hours` minus estimated pick/pack/ground transit time (and route reliability) to replace `parcel_air` with priority picking + `parcel_ground`, cutting transport emissions by ~4–6x per shifted order (e.g., 18.5 to 3.6 kgCO2e on stock paths).
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Preserves the 5% late-delivery threshold for `Standard-stock Service-contract` by reallocating lead-time buffer from transit (`Ship air` saves ~24h) to warehouse picking priority; retains `parcel_air` when slack is genuinely insufficient, protecting service and R1 resilience.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** `Pick from stock -> Package standard` queueing time, SP1 conformance rate by `customer_type`, `parcel_air` vs. `parcel_ground` cycle-time distributions, and segment late-delivery rates.

### 2. S5: Twice-daily consolidated truck departures for non-urgent stock orders
* **Target path or segment:** `Standard-stock Regular` (39% of orders) on `Package standard -> Ship ground` (5,100 cases) where `promised_lead_time_hours` provides sufficient slack beyond the current 31h median cycle time.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Shifts individual `parcel_ground` shipments to `Consolidated truck` (currently only 850 cases), reducing transport emissions by ~35–55% per shifted order.
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Adds a 12–24h consolidation wait; safe for `Regular` orders with slack, but risks pushing p90 cycle time (62h) and late deliveries (7%) upward if applied blindly to tight-deadline orders or single-carrier lanes (R1 risk).
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** Pre-ship consolidation dwell time (`Package standard -> Consolidated truck`), truck fill rates, `Standard-stock Regular` late-delivery rate, and backup carrier availability (R1).

### 3. S2: Dynamic batching with segment-specific maximum waiting-time caps
* **Target path or segment:** `Plan production -> Batch wait -> CNC production -> Heat treatment` (3,020 `Batch wait` cases; `Made-to-order` segments representing 34% of orders).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Captures energy-per-item reductions on `CNC_A` (2.8 to 1.3 kWh) and `heat_treat` when demand naturally clusters, while capping wait times prevents extreme delays that otherwise trigger `Emergency production` (760 cases) or `Ship air` recovery shipments, and limits over-batched 14-day obsolescence (6%).
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Directly reduces the 1.7x late-delivery penalty on `heat_treat` batch waits and prevents MTO Regular late delivery (18%) and MTO cancellations (`Cancel due to delay`) from worsening.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** `Batch wait` duration percentiles by `batch_id`, `batch_size` distribution, `energy_kwh` per unit, `heat_treat` late-delivery odds ratio, and 14-day unshipped stock rate.

### 4. S3: Shift Made-to-order production from `CNC_B` to `CNC_A` when slack allows
* **Target path or segment:** `Made-to-order` orders routed via `CNC_B` (e.g., `MTO -> batch wait -> CNC_B -> inspection -> rework -> ground`, 5% of all orders).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** `CNC_B` has double the rework rate of `CNC_A` (18% vs. 9%). Shifting slack-tolerant orders to `CNC_A` avoids the high-waste `Rework production` loop (470 cases), where energy reaches 32.0 kWh and scrap reaches 1.8 kg vs. 16.0 kWh and 0.70 kg on `CNC_A`.
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Improves quality and reduces the 32% late-delivery rate caused by rework loops; however, `CNC_A` is slower and can congest if all MTO volume shifts indiscriminately, creating single-machine resilience risk.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** Queue lengths and utilization on `CNC_A` vs. `CNC_B`, `rework_flag` rates by `machine_group` and part type, `material_scrap_kg`, and p90 MTO cycle times.

### 5. S7: Add an inspection quality gate for high-rework machine/part combinations
* **Target path or segment:** `CNC production / Heat treatment -> Inspection` (`inspection_cell`), targeting the 470 `Inspection -> Rework production` and downstream `Rework packaging`/return loops.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Prevents rushed `inspection_cell` checks from letting defective MTO parts reach packaging or customers, eliminating secondary `material_scrap_kg`, `package_material_kg`, and duplicate `transport_kgco2e` on returns/replacements.
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Adds minor `inspection_cell` dwell time for high-risk `CNC_B` or complex parts, but reduces total end-to-end rework cycle time (160h on rework paths) and lowers Q1-related customer returns.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** `inspection_cell` queue wait and service time, first-pass yield, `Rework production` count (470 baseline), and post-delivery return/rework correlations.

### 6. S6: Pre-shipment packaging-route compatibility and Q1 verification checks
* **Target path or segment:** `Package standard` (260 `Rework packaging` loops) and `Package reinforced` (360 `Rework packaging` loops), plus MTO parts >8 kg subject to Q1.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Eliminates the 620 `Rework packaging` loops (wasted `package_material_kg` and labor/energy) caused by mismatches between packaging, part geometry, and `route_mode`, while enforcing Q1 prevents the 40% damage-return elevation on heavy MTO items without defaulting light items to doubled `Package reinforced` mass.
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Cuts packaging rework delays and customer damage claims with minimal check overhead; maintains quality without unnecessary packaging over-specification.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** Transition counts for `Package standard/reinforced -> Rework packaging`, `package_material_kg` per order, Q1 violation rate, and damage-return rates by part weight and `route_mode`.

### 7. S8 & S9: Split `Critical-downtime` into true downtime vs. precautionary urgent, and shift eligible lanes to shared emergency couriers
* **Target path or segment:** `Critical-downtime Service-contract` (11% of orders; 38.0 kgCO2e avg; `Dedicated van` path at 52.0 kgCO2e) and the 31% of `dedicated_van` orders lacking SP2 justification.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Eliminates unnecessary `Dedicated van` dispatches (which often return empty) for precautionary urgent orders, replacing them with shared emergency courier lanes or priority `parcel_ground`/`parcel_air` depending on slack.
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Protects true line-stoppage resilience (keeping median 14–15h cycle time and 4% late delivery) by freeing dedicated vans for verified emergencies; carries minor risk if precautionary orders are misclassified or shared couriers lack guaranteed SLA headroom.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** SP2 justification event completeness (closing the 31% gap), `Critical-downtime` sub-tier shares, empty-backhaul incidence, and late-delivery rates across sub-tiers.

### 8. S11: Reduce partial shipments by revising availability-check and stock-allocation rules
* **Target path or segment:** `Availability check -> Pick from stock -> Partial shipment` (1,010 cases, 14.3% of stock picks), including the 220 `Backorder wait` cases and 60 subsequent cancellations.
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Consolidates multi-line or near-ready stock orders (e.g., waiting briefly when incoming replenishment is imminent and slack exists) to avoid shipping two physical packages, cutting duplicate `package_material_kg` and transport emissions (`7.2 kgCO2e` on partial+ground vs. `3.6 kgCO2e` on single ground).
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Reduces customer friction (79h cycle time, 24% late delivery on the partial path) when replenishment is reliable, though holding an entire `Service-contract` order for one delayed line item risks breaching `promised_lead_time_hours` if not gated by slack.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** `Pick from stock -> Partial shipment` frequency, `Backorder wait` duration and cancellation conversion (60/220 baseline), and multi-shipment `transport_kgco2e` per order.

### 9. S10: Carbon-aware promise-date negotiation for `Made-to-order Regular` customers
* **Target path or segment:** `Made-to-order Regular` (22% of orders; 94h median cycle time, 18% late delivery, 18.5 kWh, 8.0 kgCO2e).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Offers slightly longer, realistic `promised_lead_time_hours` windows at order intake (`Receive order`) so MTO Regular orders can batch on `CNC_A` (16.0 vs. 24.0 kWh) and ship via `parcel_ground` or `Consolidated truck` rather than immediate CNC or `Ship air` (23.0 kgCO2e).
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Aligns customer expectations to reduce the 18% late-delivery rate and `Cancel due to delay` risk without degrading actual manufacturing quality; requires commercial buy-in so Regular customers do not defect.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** Accepted `promised_lead_time_hours` distribution, MTO Regular `expedite_flag` rate, late-delivery rate, and `Cancel due to delay` share.

### 10. S12: Structured sustainability exception log for SP1, SP2, and Q1 overrides
* **Target path or segment:** Cross-cutting across all override events (`parcel_air` under SP1, `dedicated_van` under SP2, unreinforced heavy MTO under Q1).
* **Mechanism of environmental improvement:** Creates visibility and accountability for high-impact exceptions, systematically shrinking unjustified overrides (22% SP1 violations, 31% SP2 missing justifications) through root-cause feedback rather than blind bans.
* **Likely service/quality/resilience effect:** Preserves operational flexibility during genuine disruptions (maintaining R1 resilience and service SLAs) while preventing exception creep.
* **Required process-mining evidence to monitor:** Override event counts, reason codes, conformance rates for SP1/SP2/Q1, and post-override delivery/quality outcomes.

## 5. Rebound and displacement risks

1. **Batching-to-expedite rebound:** Chasing maximum energy efficiency on `CNC_A` (1.3 kWh at batch 12–20) or `heat_treat` (>10) extends median `Batch wait` from 4h to 38h and increases late-delivery risk 1.7x. When cases lose their buffer, schedulers trigger `Emergency production` (760 cases), `Ship air` (23.0 kgCO2e), or `Dedicated van` (52.0 kgCO2e), displacing modest shop-floor kWh savings into a 4–6x surge in transport emissions.
2. **Transport-consolidation and mode-shift displacement:** Pushing `Standard-stock` orders into `Consolidated truck` (+12–24h wait) or `Ship ground` without priority picking can erode slack on tight `Service-contract` orders, increasing `Cancel due to delay` (530 cases), customer complaints, and last-minute air rescues. Over-consolidating onto a single carrier also erodes R1 route redundancy during disruptions.
3. **Packaging-lightweighting rebound (Q1 displacement):** Attempting to cut `package_material_kg` by skipping `Package reinforced` on heavy MTO parts (>8 kg) saves packaging mass at dispatch, but increases damage-related returns by ~40% and feeds `Rework packaging` (620 cases). Each damaged heavy MTO part then incurs a full second round of `energy_kwh` (16–32 kWh), `material_scrap_kg` (0.7–1.8 kg), and duplicate `transport_kgco2e`.
4. **Rework and bottleneck displacement across machines:** Restricting `CNC_B` abruptly to eliminate its 18% rework rate without wait/capacity limits can overload `CNC_A` and `inspection_cell`. Conversely, pushing `inspection_cell` to clear queues faster causes missed defects—displacing minor inspection time into `Rework production` (470 cases, 32.0 kWh, 1.8 kg scrap, 160h cycle time) and post-shipment returns.
5. **Batch obsolescence displacement:** Building large MTO or semi-standard batches (`batch_size` 12–20) to lower unit `energy_kwh` relies on demand forecasts. When forecasts miss, 6% of batch-produced items sit unshipped past 14 days—turning "efficient" machining energy and raw material into dead-stock scrap.
6. **Customer and internal gaming behavior:** Tightening SP2 rules (`Dedicated van` restricted to `Critical-downtime`) and limiting `Ship air` under SP1 can incentivize account managers or `Service-contract` customers to misclassify precautionary orders as `Critical-downtime`, inflate urgency flags (`expedite_flag`), or split multi-line orders to game availability checks—inflating high-emission paths (`38–52 kgCO2e/order`) unless audited.

## 6. Balanced recommendation

Rather than deploying isolated green rules that shift emissions or risk elsewhere, the distributor should implement a **four-pillar, slack- and quality-gated process redesign**:

### Pillar 1: Slack-gated fulfillment and mode routing (S1 + S4 + S5 + S11)
* Deploy a real-time green shipping recommender (`S1`) driven by remaining slack (`promised_lead_time_hours` minus expected process/transit time).
* Eliminate the 22% SP1 violations in `Standard-stock Service-contract` by routing eligible orders through **priority `Pick from stock` + `Ship ground`** (`S4`), preserving the 5% late-delivery SLA while avoiding 18.5 kgCO2e air legs.
* Route `Standard-stock Regular` orders with 24h slack to **twice-daily `Consolidated truck` departures** (`S5`), while keeping `Ship ground` and multi-carrier backup lanes active to satisfy R1.
* Revise `Availability check` (`S11`) to hold `Partial shipment` orders *only* when missing lines have confirmed replenishment within lead-time slack, cutting the 1,010 partial-shipment double-legs without risking `Service-contract` lateness.

### Pillar 2: Quality-first, time-capped manufacturing (S2 + S3 + S7 + S10)
* Replace rigid batch-size targets with **dynamic batching (`S2`) governed by segment-specific maximum `Batch wait` caps** (shorter caps for `Service-contract` and `heat_treat` queues to neutralize the 1.7x late-delivery penalty and curb the 6% 14-day obsolescence).
* Route MTO orders to **`CNC_A` over `CNC_B` (`S3`) whenever `CNC_A` queue + lead-time slack allows**, and use **`S10` carbon-aware promise-date negotiation** with `MTO Regular` customers to create the slack needed for `CNC_A` batching and `parcel_ground`/`Consolidated truck` shipping.
* Add a targeted **`inspection_cell` quality gate (`S7`)** on `CNC_B` outputs and complex/heavy parts so defects are caught before `Heat treatment` or packaging, directly shrinking the 470 `Rework production` loops.

### Pillar 3: Compatibility-verified packaging and tiered critical response (S6 + S8 + S9)
* Embed automated **packaging-route and geometry checks (`S6`)** plus strict Q1 enforcement (>8 kg MTO -> `Package reinforced` unless certified low risk) prior to packing to eliminate the 620 `Rework packaging` events and avoid the 40% damage-return penalty.
* Split `Critical-downtime` into **true line-stoppage vs. precautionary urgent (`S9`)**. Protect `Dedicated van` and `Emergency production` for verified line-stoppage cases (sustaining the 15h median / 4% late-delivery SLA), while routing precautionary urgent orders via **shared emergency courier lanes (`S8`)** or priority `parcel_air`/`parcel_ground` based on slack.

### Pillar 4: Exception transparency (S12)
* Implement a structured **override and exception log (`S12`)** for SP1, SP2, and Q1 rather than hard blocks, ensuring operations can still expedite during genuine supply or carrier disruptions while creating auditability.

### Why this package is balanced
* **Environmental impact:** Targets the largest, most avoidable emissions and waste drivers—unjustified `Ship air` (4–6x CO2e), empty-backhaul `Dedicated van` dispatches (52 kgCO2e), double-leg `Partial shipment` (7.2 kgCO2e), `CNC_B` rework loops (32 kWh, 1.8 kg scrap), and `Rework packaging` (620 events)—without chasing brittle batch-size extremes that cause obsolescence or expedite rebounds.
* **Service & quality:** Protects `Critical-downtime` and `Service-contract` SLAs via priority picking, wait caps, and explicit slack gates, while improving MTO Regular reliability (18% late) through quality-gate rework reduction and realistic promise dates.
* **Resilience:** Preserves R1 multi-carrier backup routes, frees emergency van/air capacity for true crises, avoids single-machine lock-in on `CNC_A` by retaining `CNC_B` for peak overflow under `S7` inspection gating, and governs exceptions transparently via `S12`.

## 7. Validation and governance plan

### 1. Post-implementation event-log validation
* **Phased rollout & counterfactual comparison:** Pilot by customer segment / route lane (e.g., `S4` + `S6` first on `Standard-stock` and packaging; `S2` + `S3` + `S7` on MTO; `S8` + `S9` on `Critical-downtime`), comparing pre/post enriched event logs matched by `case_type`, `customer_type`, and order volume.
* **End-to-end path & lifecycle checks:** Recompute DFG frequencies and variant-level KPIs (`energy_kwh`, `transport_kgco2e`, `material_scrap_kg`, `package_material_kg`, rework loops, partial shipments) to verify that reductions in one stage do not trigger rebound increases in `Emergency production`, `Ship air`, `Cancel due to delay` (baseline 530), or 14-day unshipped batch stock (baseline 6%).

### 2. Automated conformance monitoring (SP1, SP2, Q1, R1)
* **SP1 (Mode selection):** Run daily replay checks comparing selected `route_mode` against `promised_lead_time_hours` and empirical ground/consolidated transit p85 distributions. Target reducing `Standard-stock Service-contract` SP1 non-conformance from 22% to <5% without breaching the 5% late-delivery threshold.
* **SP2 (Dedicated-van justification):** Enforce a mandatory pre-dispatch event (`Critical_justification_logged` with tier classification under `S9`) before `Dedicated van` dispatch, driving the 31% missing-justification rate to 0% and tracking `Dedicated van` vs. shared-courier utilization.
* **Q1 (Heavy MTO packaging):** Monitor compliance on MTO parts >8 kg (`Package reinforced` or `Inspection_low_risk_certified`), alongside `Rework packaging` transitions (620 baseline) and damage-return rates.
* **R1 (Resilience & model precision):** Track active backup carrier availability per `Service-contract` region and monitor process-discovery precision/variant drift during disruption weeks to ensure exception paths remain manageable.

### 3. Segment-level service and sustainability guardrails
Establish explicit stop/recalibration triggers per segment:
* `Critical-downtime Service-contract`: Late delivery  4%, median cycle time  15h, p90  30h.
* `Standard-stock Service-contract`: Late delivery  5%, median  18h, while reducing avg `transport_kgco2e` below 7.8 kg.
* `Standard-stock Regular`: Late delivery  7%, `Cancel due to delay` non-increasing, while shifting slack-rich ground orders to `Consolidated truck`.
* `Made-to-order (Regular & Service-contract)`: Rework rate strictly < 10–11%, late delivery improving below 18% (Regular) / 13% (Service-contract), and 14-day batch obsolescence < 6%.

### 4. Exception governance (`S12`)
* Require structured metadata on every SP1, SP2, or Q1 override (`override_reason_code`: e.g., *carrier disruption*, *customer line-stoppage*, *replenishment delay*, *approver_id*, and *expected_slack_shortfall_hours*).
* Conduct bi-weekly process-mining governance reviews of the top override-generating SKUs, `customer_type` accounts, and `machine_group` bottlenecks to recalibrate `Batch wait` caps, `Consolidated truck` cutoffs, and `S9` tier criteria, preventing customer misclassification and exception creep.
