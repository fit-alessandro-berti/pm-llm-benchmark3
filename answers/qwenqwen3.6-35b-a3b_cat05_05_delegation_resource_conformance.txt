## 2. Rule Interpretation

- **RR1 (Drafter/Approver separation):** Strict segregation. The canonical human performing `Draft_Change_Request` must not be the same canonical human performing `Approve_Change` for the same `case:concept:name`. Applies to all risk levels unless explicitly waived by policy (none stated).
- **RR2 (Implementer/Verifier separation):** Strict segregation. The canonical human performing `Implement_Change` must not be the same canonical human performing `Verify_Implementation` for the same case.
- **RR3 (High-risk approval authority):** For `risk=HIGH`, `Approve_Change` requires the standing QM or a valid QM delegate. Validity requires: (1) event timestamp  delegation interval, (2) case `scope`  delegation scope, (3) principal absence/unavailability confirmed per HR/IT records, (4) no prohibited subdelegation in the chain. Standing QM role satisfies this without delegation.
- **RR4 (Emergency substitution):** For `emergency=true` AND `risk=HIGH`, `Approve_Change` may be performed by the IC or a valid IC delegate. A `Retrospective_QM_Review` must be completed by the QM or valid QM delegate **within two business days** (counting forward from the approval/implementation date, excluding weekends/holidays per site calendar). 2026-08-11 (Tue)  12 (Wed), 13 (Thu) = exactly 2 business days; 14 (Fri) = day 3. Scope of the QM delegate must cover the change.
- **RR5 (Identity evidence):** Service accounts are non-human. Conformance requires reliable session evidence mapping the action to an authorized human. Aliases must be normalized before any SoD or delegation check.
- **RR6 (Suspicion versus proof):** Repeated pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, and concentrated handovers are analytical indicators. They trigger deeper session/audit review but do not constitute proof of collusion without independent corroboration.
- **Principal on leave acting:** A standing role remains valid during leave, but concurrent leave + remote session triggers credential-sharing/absence-policy review. Does not automatically invalidate authority but flags for RR6 investigation.
- **Later database entry proving earlier delegation:** A database record created after the effective interval cannot retroactively validate a delegation for events prior to its creation, unless the system supports cryptographically signed backdating. Here, the signed PDF (08-09) precedes the interval, so it validates D-QM-01. The 08-12 DB record is a migration artifact and unnecessary for validity.
