# Board Review of Competing Process Models — Custom-Order Dossier Case

# 1. Review Scope and Assumptions

Before interpreting any number, I fix the *purpose* of each candidate model, because fitness/precision trade-offs are only meaningful relative to use.

**Intended use cases distinguished:**

| Use case | What matters | Acceptable trade-off |
|---|---|---|
| **Exploration / discussion base** | Coverage of logged variety, legibility, speed, cheap updates | High fitness tolerated even at low precision; rejection errors harmless |
| **Conformance enforcement** | Correct accept/reject on *known-policy* cases; auditable rejection reasons | High precision against a curated **negative suite**, moderate fitness; missing log content must be declared |
| **Simulation / what-if** | Structurally faithful branching (external sourcing, corrections), stable timings | Balanced fitness and precision; explicit fork/join structure preferred over flat XOR lists |
| **Stakeholder communication** | Map people can annotate and challenge | Human-legible structure dominates; metric scores nearly irrelevant |

Under exploration, Model S's profile is defensible (F7). Under enforcement, both Model S and Model C are unsafe, for opposite reasons: S cannot say no to the *forbidden*, C cannot say yes to the *valid-yet-unseen*. Under simulation, Model C's variant enumeration collapses combinatorially as soon as attribute dependencies are added. A governance board therefore cannot accept a single aggregate claim ("robust"/"audit-ready") without pinning the use case first.

Two standing assumptions for this review: (a) the process tree strings are interpreted with standard POWL/tree semantics (`->` sequence, `+` parallel, `X` exclusive choice, `*` loop, tau = silent move); (b) "acceptance" of a probe means *fitness 1.0 alignment exists*, verified by reasoning and, where cited, by PM4Py playback/alignment code supplied in §9.

Verdict: S's advocacy (F7) is coherent only for the exploration seat. It must be barred from any enforcement role.

# 4. Adversarial Review of Model C

**Strongest case for C.** Reject-by-default is a defensible enforcement posture (F8): probes B3, B4, B8 are refused; language is fully enumerable, which simplifies variant sign-off; on the training window it looks tight (precision 0.941).

**Strongest case against C.**

1. **Branch enumeration is the pathology, not the cure.** Eight hand/frequency-selected variants sit inside a cross-product space (check order × supplier  {yes,no} × expedite  {absent, before/after supplier} × approval  {M,A} × correction pattern  {none, [CC], [TR], [CC;TR]} × multiplicity). The captured set is a sparse, frequency-biased sample of that lattice; benign holes are inevitable: B2 (TR,CC,**Auto**) and B5-class (alternate order + Supp + Exp + Auto) are refused despite policy validity. Unseen-combination brittleness (F2) follows mechanically.
2. **Duplicate labels (33, definition undisclosed)** stem from literal re-encoding of "Credit Check" etc. in every variant branch. Diagnostics lose provenance (which occurrence fired?), simplicity craters (0.281), and maintenance requires synchronized edits across copies (F3).
3. **Holdout degradation (0.744)** is exactly the memorization signature — but the leaked/unpublished split leaves room to contest its magnitude.
4. **Correction handling is rigid**: only the CCTR re-do both shape exists (variant 7). Policy-dependent-valid B6 (correct then *only* TR rechecked) is refused; multi-cycle corrections are unexpressible. Neither the scope nor the repetition semantics (P6/P7) is represented — merely one frozen template.
5. **High precision  memorizing training variants.** With one exhaustive branch per frequent trace, positive-log precision trivially rises; F5 confirms no negatives were tested. The "audit-ready" epithet rests on a measure structurally blind to Type-II error.
6. **Maintainability/clarity scored worst on both instruments** available (simplicity 0.281; comprehension 1.9) despite marketing as precision tooling.

Verdict: unacceptable for enforcement until generalized; even its precision advantage evaporates outside the training window.
