## 1. Control-Objective Decomposition

**The important distinction is between an activity-level separation rule, an authorized exception, and effective protection against an erroneous or fraudulent payment.** FEP-1 requires different human preparers and releasers; ED-1 permits a narrowly conditioned single-operator route without removing the underlying control objective. A successful payment outcome does not retrospectively establish that its controls were sufficient.  

I use the following decomposition. “Prevent” refers to stopping the relevant failure before the chosen commitment boundary; “correct” requires an actionable stop, recall, or recovery mechanism—not simply a review.

| Objective or threat scenario                                                            | AV-2                                                                                                                          | MG-4                                                                                                                                   | RA-7                                                                                                 | ED-1                                                                                                             |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Prevent unilateral initiation and authorization**                                     | Constrains certain transaction attributes, but does not supply an independent human authorization.                            | Can provide substantive independent authorization, subject to grade, identity, conflicts, evidence reviewed, and effective veto power. | Can detect unauthorized handling afterward; cannot erase the original unilateral authorization.      | Permits single-operator handling only through the complete conditional package. A token alone is insufficient.   |
| **Prevent duplicates, incorrect amounts, limit breaches, and sanctions-related errors** | Direct preventive coverage for its specified checks when operational and enforced. An overridden warning is not a clean pass. | Can challenge inconsistencies in the invoice and payment, but approval alone does not prove equivalent technical coverage.             | May detect defects; correction depends on timing and bank action.                                    | Requires a full AV-2 pass without override; does not substitute for missing checks.                              |
| **Prevent bank-account diversion or payment to an unsuitable destination**              | Covers ownership and recent changes, but not who actually controls a compromised account.                                     | Review of bank details provides an additional challenge; verification quality matters.                                                 | Can discover diversion, but delayed discovery can leave funds unrecoverable.                         | Excludes recent bank changes and insufficiently established vendors, reducing—but not eliminating—this exposure. |
| **Prevent payment for fictitious, unreceived, or commercially unreasonable services**   | Explicitly does not establish receipt or commercial reasonableness. The coverage limitations and qualification requirements above come directly from the supplied control definitions. The functions described as possible contributions of a review remain conditional on evidence that the reviewer actually performed them. 

## 2. Equivalence and Sufficiency Test

### Observation boundary and evidence standard

I assess three distinct boundaries:

**Release:** Could one person issue the payment instruction without effective independent authorization?

**Settlement:** Could an independent reviewer still prevent the instruction from becoming irrevocable?

**Recovery:** After settlement, was there a demonstrably effective route to recover funds?

A package can therefore be equivalent for preventing an irrevocable transfer without reproducing the literal Prepare/Release separation. Conversely, merely reviewing a payment before its settlement timestamp does not establish prevention: the reviewer must have had a usable veto, and enough time to exercise it. The scenario treats settlement as normally irrevocable for internal-control purposes, so an audit within 24 hours cannot automatically be credited with corrective capacity after settlement.  

### Structured assessment

| Test dimension                         | Required assessment                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Timing**                             | Identify preparation, validation, release, approval, settlement, audit completion, and any stop/recall action. Distinguish effective time from recording time.                                                |
| **Independence and authority**         | Resolve actual humans, L3-or-above status, conflicts, delegation, credential control, and ability to refuse authorization.                                                                                    |
| **Coverage**                           | Map each material threat to an actually performed check. Do not extend an AV-2 pass to receipt, commercial reasonableness, or collusion.                                                                      |
| **Overrideability**                    | Establish who could bypass each barrier, under what conditions, and whether the same person controlled initiation and bypass.                                                                                 |
| **Recoverability**                     | Verify bank-specific cutoffs, operational response time, and successful stop/recall capability. A theoretical recovery option is weaker than an enforceable hold.                                             |
| **Evidence quality**                   | Require reliable timestamps, complete audit lifecycle, transaction-version binding, review contents, and trustworthy identity evidence.                                                                       |
| **Collusion resistance**               | Assess substantive organizational independence and independent evidence sources, not merely account inequality. Compare with reasonable independent review—not an impossible guarantee against all collusion. |
| **Case risk and exception legitimacy** | Consider vendor history, recent bank changes, documentary quality, necessity, amount, control outages, and concentration of authority together.                                                               |

For ED-1, the eligibility test is conjunctive:

$$
\begin{aligned}
ED1 ={}& Token_{\text{before preparation}}
\land Amount<100{,}000
\land VendorAge\ge180\text{ days}\\
&\land NoRecentBankChange
\land FullAVPassWithoutOverride\\
&\land QualifiedIndependentMG_{\text{before settlement}}
\land CompletedAudit_{\text{within one business day}}.
\end{aligned}
$$

A confirmed false condition defeats eligibility. Missing critical evidence produces **unknown**, not true. AP-206 needs two separate records: its token timing fails **as recorded**, while its actual effective timing remains disputed. These distinctions follow the supplied exception conditions and case facts.  

### Equivalence grades

| Grade                                    | Meaning                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ---------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fully equivalent**                     | For the stated boundary and risk class, the package provides comparable reasonable assurance across material objectives, with no unmet mandatory condition and no unresolved critical evidence gap. Literal FEP-1 nonconformance may still remain. |
| **Partially equivalent**                 | Some objectives receive comparable protection, but another material objective, exposure interval, or assurance requirement remains weaker.                                                                                                         |
| **Merely mitigating**                    | The package reduces some exposure without adequately replacing independent authorization—for example, a late audit or unverified ratification.                                                                                                     |
| **Ineffective for the relevant failure** | The supposed barrier was unavailable, bypassed, outside its coverage, or too late to interrupt that failure mechanism. It might still have value against another threat.                                                                           |

**Equal expected loss is not the sole standard.** Even an illustrative equality in average loss could conceal a larger unrecoverable tail, weakened accountability, unauthorized exceptions, correlated credential failures, or normalization of bypasses. Conversely, genuine production or safety emergencies justify considering delay costs—but do not cancel ED-1’s explicit conditions.

For the findings below, I use a conservative certification standard: **I do not assume unreported manager grades or bank-stop powers.** Consequently, AP-203 and AP-207 remain indeterminate for unconditional certification, despite strong favorable evidence. This is an evidence limitation, not a judgment that their risk reduction was weak.

## 4. Preventive–Detective–Corrective Comparison

| Control and timing                           | Preventive contribution                                                                     | Detective contribution                                    | Corrective contribution                                      | Principal limitation                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **AV-2 hard block before release**           | Stops release for a covered defect                                                          | Identifies the covered anomaly                            | Avoids the need for recovery                                 | Limited coverage; effectiveness depends on enforcement and availability               |
| **AV-2 warning with override**               | Conditional; lost against the warned-about issue when bypassed without adequate replacement | Warning remains evidence of an identified concern         | None by itself                                               | Override authorization and actual human control are critical                          |
| **MG-4 before release**                      | Strong if independent review is an enforceable prerequisite to release                      | Finds defects in proposed payment                         | Can require correction before issuing the instruction        | Grade, independence, substantive review, and payment-version binding                  |
| **MG-4 after release but before settlement** | Preventive against irrevocable settlement **only with an effective veto**                   | Detective relative to the already-issued instruction      | Can cancel or amend an in-flight instruction where supported | Timestamp ordering alone does not establish stop capability                           |
| **MG-4 after settlement**                    | Cannot prevent the already completed commitment                                             | Retrospective detection and accountability                | Only through an actually available recovery mechanism        | Ratification does not retroactively authorize or prevent the original exposure        |
| **RA-7**                                     | Not preventive for the original release                                                     | Detects errors, unsupported payments, or control breaches | Conditional on actionable stopping, recall, or recovery      | Audit completion, scope, independence, and response latency—not merely audit presence |
| **ED-1**                                     | A governed exception architecture incorporating preventive controls                         | Requires retrospective audit                              | Depends on constituent controls and available bank actions   | Permission is conjunctive; emergency status is not a compensating control by itself   |

This comparison applies the supplied control definitions rather than assuming that every recorded control action was effective. 

**Chosen semantics for pre-settlement ratification:** it is a **hybrid**—detective relative to Release_Payment and potentially preventive relative to irrevocable settlement. Cancelling an already-issued instruction is also corrective relative to that instruction. These labels describe different boundaries, not contradictory findings.

AP-201’s ratification occurred 24 minutes after release and 90 minutes before settlement. AP-207’s occurred 35 minutes after release and 80 minutes before settlement. Those are observed opportunity intervals, not demonstrated intervention capabilities.   

There are also **two different timing tests**:

* **Audit-service target:** completed audit within the applicable business-day definition.
* **Loss-intervention target:** detection **plus action** before the actual bank cutoff.

AP-201’s audit event is 24 hours 54 minutes after release, and the narrative describes an audit beginning—not necessarily completing—around that time. Meeting a next-business-day interpretation would not establish timely corrective action.

## 6. Evidence and Monitoring Recommendations

### Evidence collection

Preserve a linked record of **payment version, human identity, control execution, and bank state**. Priority evidence includes effective manager grade and authority, conflicts and delegation, authentication/session records, the exact invoice/bank/receipt versions reviewed, AV check-level results, token activation and ingestion times, bank hold and settlement acknowledgments, and audit start/completion plus corrective-action timestamps.

For AP-206, collect effective-time and recording-time evidence separately. Manager approval precedes AV-2 by one minute; that is not prohibited by the supplied rules, but the approval must be shown to cover the final validated payment rather than an earlier version. 

For AP-204/AP-209, obtain payment-cell design and access/session evidence before concluding that frequent pairing or a shared workstation defeats human independence. The supplied facts explicitly allow a dedicated-cell explanation. 

### Monitoring rules

| Indicator                      | Definition and recommended response                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Same-user rate**             | Same actual human preparing and releasing / completed released payments. Report account-level and identity-resolved rates separately. Split verified ED-1 uses, ineligible uses, and unresolved uses.     |
| **Override rate**              | Overridden warnings / warnings, with reason, actual operator, credential owner, authorization, and subsequent outcome. Distinguish authorized overrides from borrowed-credential use.                     |
| **Late manager approval**      | Separate pre-release, post-release/pre-settlement, post-settlement, missing, and unknown-settlement cases. For the middle category, require evidence of an effective veto.                                |
| **Audit-before-recall-window** | Measure completed audit **and actionable response** before the bank-specific intervention deadline. Report a separate release-plus-24-hour proxy where the true deadline is unknown.                      |
| **Emergency audit coverage**   | Emergency same-operator payments with complete, adequately scoped audits within policy deadline / all such payments. An Audit_Review event alone is insufficient evidence of completion or full coverage. |
| **Repeated resource pairs**    | Monitor pair concentration, reciprocity, duration, exception rates, defect rates, and peer-normalized patterns. Compare dedicated cells with comparable operating units.                                  |
| **Shared workstations**        | Correlate device/session use with separate authentication, overlapping sessions, credential switching, and actual human control. Shared equipment alone is not misconduct.                                |
| **Bank-change proximity**      | Flag changes in the previous seven days and shorter high-risk intervals. Link the changer, preparer, releaser, verification method, and payment version.                                                  |
| **Control outages**            | Identify release while AV-2 is unavailable, incomplete, or degraded. Record fail-open authority and alternative evidence; prevent silent equivalence between an unsigned checklist and full validation.   |
| **Exception preconditions**    | Test token activation before preparation, amount threshold, vendor-age threshold, unchanged bank details, unoverridden AV pass, qualified manager timing, and audit completion separately.                |

For this selected extract, the account-level same-user result is **6/8 released payments, or 75%**. AP-205 is excluded from that denominator. Three of the six same-user payments have audit-event timestamps within 24 hours of release, but this does **not** establish completed audits with usable recovery capacity. These are descriptive results for the supplied extract, not estimates of enterprise-wide performance. 

**An alert is a request for evidence, not proof of misconduct.** Combine pattern alerts with independently established defects and authentication evidence. Monitor the redesign’s operational costs as well: authorization waiting time, genuine-emergency delays, manual workload, and attempts to bypass newly introduced controls.

## 7. PM4Py and pandas Checking Code

The inspected PM4Py API defaults to `keep_violations=False`, which removes violations. The code therefore explicitly uses `keep_violations=True`. It independently checks the result with pandas rather than trusting filter direction implicitly. 

The inspected pandas backend selects the last occurrence of the second activity during matching. For that reason, this example rejects repeated activities within a case rather than claiming robust attempt-level semantics for arbitrary rework logs. Production logs with retries require explicit payment-attempt/version matching; behavior should be regression-tested against the installed PM4Py version. ([GitHub][1])

The code preserves unknown values, supplements the two settlement times supplied only in the narrative, separates audit events from audit completion, and labels the weekday calculation as a proxy. No external files or services are read.

        "Bank_Settlement",
    }
    blank = events[RES].isna() | events[RES].fillna("").str.strip().eq("")
    if (blank & ~events[ACT].isin(system_activities)).any():
        raise ValueError("Missing human resource; do not replace with SYSTEM.")
    events.loc[blank, RES] = "SYSTEM"
    if events[[CASE, ACT, TIME, RES]].isna().any().any():
        raise ValueError("Missing mandatory event data.")
    return events.sort_values([CASE, TIME], kind="stable").reset_index(drop=True)


def make_facts() -> pd.DataFrame:
    # None means unknown, NOT false. Ages are threshold facts, not invented dates.
    columns = [CASE, "amount_eur", "vendor_ge180d", "bank_change7d",
               "token_present", "token_recorded_at", "token_time_disputed",
               "av_result", "manager_independent", "manager_substantive",
               "audit_1bd_reported", "settlement_fact", "shared_workstation",
               "pair_count30d_reported", "override_credential_owner"]
    rows = [
        ("AP-201", 1800, True, False, False, None, False, "PASS", True, None,
         None, "2026-05-04T10:00:00Z", None, None, None),
        ("AP-202", 480000, None, True, False, None, False, "WARNING", None, None,
         False, None, None, None, "m09"),
        ("AP-203", 75000, True, False, True, "2026-05-04T09:55:00Z", False,
         "PASS", True, True, True, "2026-05-04T12:00:00Z", None, None, None),
        ("AP-204", 220000, True, False, False, None, False, "PASS", None, None,
         False, None, True, 37, None),
        ("AP-205", 12000, None, None, False, None, False, "BLOCK", None, None,
         None, None, None, None, None),
        ("AP-206", 95000, True, False, True, "2026-05-04T13:02:00Z", True,
         "PASS", True, None, True, None, None, None, None),
        ("AP-207", 35000, True, False, True, "2026-05-04T13:50:00Z", False,
         "PASS", True, True, True, None, None, None, None),
        ("AP-208", 900000, True, True, True, None, False, "UNAVAILABLE",
         None, None, False, None, None, None, None),
        ("AP-209", 42000, True, False, False, None, False, "PASS", None, None,
         True, None, True, None, None),
    ]
    facts = pd.DataFrame(rows, columns=columns).set_index(CASE)
    for col in ["vendor_ge180d", "bank_change7d", "token_present",
                "token_time_disputed", "manager_independent",
                "manager_substantive", "audit_1bd_reported", "shared_workstation"]:
        facts[col] = facts[col].astype("boolean")
    for col in ["token_recorded_at", "settlement_fact"]:
        facts[col] = pd.to_datetime(facts[col], utc=True)
    facts["manager_L3plus"] = pd.Series(pd.NA, index=facts.index, dtype="boolean")
    # Audit_Review has no lifecycle. AP-201 is explicitly described as a start.
    facts["audit_event_is_completion"] = pd.Series(
        pd.NA, index=facts.index, dtype="boolean"
    )
    facts.loc["AP-201", "audit_event_is_completion"] = False
    return facts


def before(a: pd.Series, b: pd.Series) -> pd.Series:
    """Unknown endpoints remain unknown rather than silently becoming False."""
    return a.lt(b).astype("boolean").mask(a.isna() | b.isna(), pd.NA)


def case_evidence(events: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    # Deliberately reject repeats: this example has one payment attempt per case.
    # Production retries require payment-version/attempt identifiers and matching.
    if events.duplicated([CASE, ACT]).any():
        raise ValueError("Repeated activity: define attempt-level matching first.")
    times = events.pivot(index=CASE, columns=ACT, values=TIME)
    people = events.pivot(index=CASE, columns=ACT, values=RES)
    facts = make_facts()
    facts.index = facts.index.astype(times.index.dtype)
    if set(times.index) != set(facts.index):
        raise ValueError("Case-fact join is incomplete or contains extra cases.")
    e = pd.DataFrame(index=times.index).join(facts, validate="one_to_one")
    for col, activity in {
        "prepare_at": "Prepare_Payment", "release_at": "Release_Payment",
        "av_pass_at": "Automated_Validate", "audit_event_at": "Audit_Review",
        "attempt_at": "Attempt_Release", "block_at": "Automated_Validate_Block",
        "cancel_at": "Cancel_Payment", "override_at": "Override_Warning",
    }.items():
        e[col] = times[activity]
    e["preparer"] = people["Prepare_Payment"]
    e["releaser"] = people["Release_Payment"]
    e["manager_at"] = times["Manager_Approve"].combine_first(times["Manager_Ratify"])
    e["manager"] = people["Manager_Approve"].combine_first(people["Manager_Ratify"])
    bank_event = times["Bank_Settlement"]
    conflict = (bank_event.notna() & e["settlement_fact"].notna()
                & bank_event.ne(e["settlement_fact"]))
    if conflict.any():
        raise ValueError("Conflicting settlement evidence; do not silently reconcile.")
    e["settlement_at"] = bank_event.combine_first(e["settlement_fact"])
    e["settlement_source"] = "UNKNOWN"
    e.loc[e["settlement_fact"].notna(), "settlement_source"] = "CASE_FACT"
    e.loc[bank_event.notna(), "settlement_source"] = "EVENT"
    e["released"] = e["release_at"].notna()
    if e["prepare_at"].isna().any():
        raise ValueError("Missing preparation event.")
    if not before(e["prepare_at"], e["release_at"])[e["released"]].all():
        raise ValueError("Release is not strictly after preparation.")
    e["same_account"] = e["preparer"].eq(e["releaser"]).astype("boolean")
    e.loc[~e["released"], "same_account"] = pd.NA
    e["attempted_blocked_no_release"] = (
        ~e["released"] & e["attempt_at"].notna()
        & before(e["block_at"], e["attempt_at"]).fillna(False)
        & before(e["attempt_at"], e["cancel_at"]).fillna(False)
    )
    e["override_observed"] = e["override_at"].notna()
    e["av_full_pass_pre_release"] = (
        e["av_result"].eq("PASS") & e["av_pass_at"].notna()
        & before(e["av_pass_at"], e["release_at"])
        & ~e["override_observed"]
    ).astype("boolean")
    e.loc[~e["released"], "av_full_pass_pre_release"] = pd.NA
    e["av_receipt_coverage"] = False
    e["av_collusion_coverage"] = False
    e["av_outage"] = times["Validation_Service_Unavailable"].notna()
    e["manager_before_settlement"] = before(e["manager_at"], e["settlement_at"])
    e.loc[e["manager_at"].isna(), "manager_before_settlement"] = False
    e["manager_distinct_account"] = (
        e["manager"].ne(e["preparer"]) & e["manager"].ne(e["releaser"])
    ).astype("boolean")
    e.loc[e["manager"].isna(), "manager_distinct_account"] = False

    def manager_timing(row: pd.Series) -> str:
        if pd.isna(row["manager_at"]):
            return "NO_MANAGER_EVENT"
        if row["manager_at"] < row["release_at"]:
            return "PRE_RELEASE"
        if pd.isna(row["settlement_at"]):
            return "POST_RELEASE_SETTLEMENT_UNKNOWN"
        return ("POST_RELEASE_PRE_SETTLEMENT"
                if row["manager_at"] < row["settlement_at"] else "AT_OR_POST_SETTLEMENT")

    e["manager_timing"] = e.apply(manager_timing, axis=1)
    e["manager_to_settlement_minutes"] = (
        e["settlement_at"] - e["manager_at"]
    ).dt.total_seconds() / 60
    e["token_recorded_before_prepare"] = before(e["token_recorded_at"], e["prepare_at"])
    e.loc[~e["token_present"], "token_recorded_before_prepare"] = False
    # AP-206: recorded timing fails, but actual effective timing is disputed.
    e["token_effective_before_prepare"] = e["token_recorded_before_prepare"].mask(
        e["token_time_disputed"], pd.NA
    )
    e["audit_event_hours_after_release"] = (
        e["audit_event_at"] - e["release_at"]
    ).dt.total_seconds() / 3600
    e["audit_event_within24h_release_proxy"] = (
        e["audit_event_hours_after_release"].between(0, 24).astype("boolean")
        .mask(e["audit_event_at"].isna() | ~e["released"], pd.NA)
    )
    # Illustrative calendar only: Mon-Fri, UTC, no holidays, end of next weekday.
    # It is NOT the undefined policy meaning of 'within one business day'.
    e["next_weekday_end_proxy"] = e["release_at"].map(
        lambda t: (t.normalize() + pd.offsets.BDay(1) + pd.Timedelta(days=1)
                   - pd.Timedelta(nanoseconds=1)) if pd.notna(t) else pd.NaT
    )
    e["audit_event_by_weekday_end_proxy"] = before(
        e["audit_event_at"], e["next_weekday_end_proxy"]
    )
    e["audit_event_after_settlement"] = before(e["settlement_at"], e["audit_event_at"])
    e["audit_completion_within24h_supported"] = (
        e["audit_event_within24h_release_proxy"] & e["audit_event_is_completion"]
    )
    e["bank_stop_capability_verified"] = pd.Series(pd.NA, index=e.index, dtype="boolean")

    gates = pd.DataFrame({
        "token": e["token_effective_before_prepare"],
        "amount": e["amount_eur"].lt(100000).astype("boolean"),
        "vendor_age": e["vendor_ge180d"],
        "unchanged_bank": ~e["bank_change7d"],
        "AV": e["av_full_pass_pre_release"],
        "MG": (e["manager_before_settlement"] & e["manager_distinct_account"]
               & e["manager_independent"] & e["manager_L3plus"]
               & e["manager_substantive"]),
        # Reported timing is kept separate from event-lifecycle verification.
        "RA_reported": e["audit_1bd_reported"],
    }, index=e.index).astype("boolean")
    e["ED1_failed_gates"] = gates.apply(
        lambda r: ",".join(c for c, v in r.items() if pd.notna(v) and not bool(v)), axis=1
    )
    e["ED1_unknown_gates"] = gates.apply(
        lambda r: ",".join(c for c, v in r.items() if pd.isna(v)), axis=1
    )
    e["ED1_evidence"] = "ALL_GATES_SUPPORTED_PENDING_ASSURANCE"
    e.loc[e["ED1_unknown_gates"].ne(""), "ED1_evidence"] = "UNRESOLVED"
    e.loc[e["ED1_failed_gates"].ne(""), "ED1_evidence"] = "KNOWN_INELIGIBLE"
    e.loc[~e["released"], "ED1_evidence"] = "NOT_APPLICABLE_NO_RELEASE"
    distinct = e["released"] & ~e["same_account"].fillna(False)
    e.loc[distinct, "ED1_evidence"] = "NOT_NEEDED_DISTINCT_ACCOUNTS"
    e.loc[~e["released"] | distinct, ["ED1_failed_gates", "ED1_unknown_gates"]] = ""
    pair_counts = e.loc[e["released"]].groupby(["preparer", "releaser"]).size()
    e["pair_count_in_extract"] = pd.Series(pd.NA, index=e.index, dtype="Int64")
    for case in e.index[e["released"]]:
        pair = (e.at[case, "preparer"], e.at[case, "releaser"])
        e.at[case, "pair_count_in_extract"] = int(pair_counts.loc[pair])
    e["repeated_distinct_pair_alert"] = (
        e["released"] & ~e["same_account"].fillna(False)
        & e["pair_count_in_extract"].ge(2).fillna(False)
    )
    return e


def main() -> None:
    try:
        import pm4py
    except ImportError as exc:
        raise RuntimeError("Install pandas and PM4Py before running this script.") from exc
    if not hasattr(pm4py, "filter_four_eyes_principle"):
        raise RuntimeError("This PM4Py version lacks filter_four_eyes_principle.")
    events = pm4py.format_dataframe(
        load_events(), case_id=CASE, activity_key=ACT, timestamp_key=TIME
    )
    evidence = case_evidence(events)
    released_ids = set(evidence.index[evidence["released"]])
    # Only completed-release cases belong in the nominal FEP denominator.
    log = events.loc[events[CASE].isin(released_ids)].copy()
    # The default removes violations. Explicit True retains the violating cases.
    violations = pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(
        log, "Prepare_Payment", "Release_Payment", keep_violations=True
    )
    if not isinstance(violations, pd.DataFrame):
        violations = pm4py.convert_to_dataframe(violations)
    violating_ids = set(violations[CASE].astype(str))
    pandas_ids = set(evidence.index[evidence["same_account"].fillna(False)])
    if violating_ids != pandas_ids:
        raise RuntimeError("PM4Py/pandas disagreement: inspect installed filter semantics.")
    evidence["nominal_FEP"] = "NOT_APPLICABLE_NO_RELEASE"
    evidence.loc[evidence["released"], "nominal_FEP"] = "DISTINCT_ACCOUNTS"
    evidence.loc[evidence.index.isin(violating_ids), "nominal_FEP"] = "SAME_ACCOUNT_VIOLATION"
    print("EVIDENCE FOR JUDGMENT — NOT A FINAL COMPLIANCE OR MISCONDUCT VERDICT")
    print("PM4Py:", pm4py.__version__, "pandas:", pd.__version__)
    print("Nominal violations:", sorted(violating_ids))
    print("Blocked attempts:", list(evidence.index[evidence["attempted_blocked_no_release"]]))
    print("Same-account rate:", f"{len(violating_ids)}/{len(released_ids)}")
    groups = {
        "Nominal and emergency evidence": ["nominal_FEP", "token_recorded_before_prepare",
            "token_time_disputed", "ED1_evidence", "ED1_failed_gates", "ED1_unknown_gates"],
        "Validation and manager evidence": ["av_result", "av_full_pass_pre_release",
            "av_receipt_coverage", "av_collusion_coverage", "av_outage", "bank_change7d",
            "override_observed", "override_credential_owner", "manager_timing",
            "manager_independent", "manager_L3plus", "manager_substantive"],
        "Audit and recoverability evidence": ["settlement_source", "audit_event_hours_after_release",
            "audit_event_within24h_release_proxy", "audit_event_is_completion",
            "audit_completion_within24h_supported", "audit_event_by_weekday_end_proxy",
            "audit_1bd_reported", "audit_event_after_settlement", "bank_stop_capability_verified"],
        "Pair-pattern evidence": ["preparer", "releaser", "pair_count_in_extract",
            "pair_count30d_reported", "repeated_distinct_pair_alert", "shared_workstation"],
    }
    for title, columns in groups.items():
        print("\n" + title)
        print(evidence[columns].to_string())
    print("\nUnknown manager grade is not approval; a 24-hour flag is not proof of recallability.")


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
