# 1. Assumptions and Interpretation

1. A case is one complete, visible trace from Register Deviation through Archive Case. Each label records completion. An edge from one activity to another means the predecessor must **finish before the successor may start**. Parallel branches are independently enabled and may genuinely overlap. Their completion events appear in either order in a trace. A completion sequence alone cannot establish that their execution intervals overlapped.
2. After Analyze Root Cause, an exclusive choice selects either Supervisor Review alone or both Quality Review and Regulatory Review. The two high-risk reviews have independent starts and must both finish. The model abstracts the risk flag by allowing either route, once per case.
3. After Analyze Root Cause, a separate exclusive choice selects one Consult Supplier or a silent skip. Selecting consultation creates a release prerequisite even if the consultation starts late. With no data predicates given, the control flow permits each supplier choice with each risk route.
4. Each iteration has one Execute Trial and one Inspect Trial. At the inspection, exiting the loop represents a pass. Taking the redo branch represents failure and requires one Adjust Parameters before the next execution. Other branches may complete activities between any of these events. Every *completed* case has finitely many failures and one final pass. The model places no bound on retries and supplies no guarantee of eventual success.
5. Each model contains one visible node per activity label. Repeating a trial reuses a node. Silent transitions and repeated *occurrences* of a label in a trace are permitted.
6. The tree is a conservative under-approximation. I preserve both work-product streams, the earliest trial start, and concurrency throughout the trial. To do so in a tree with unique visible labels, I delay the review and supplier branches until both work products finish. I compare the models on complete visible traces after hiding silent steps.

# 2. Intended Dependency Specification

The trace notation uses the following symbols.

| Symbol | Activity | Symbol | Activity |
|---|---|---|---|
| R | Register Deviation | C | Contain Material |
| A | Analyze Root Cause | P | Plan Verification |
| D | Design Corrective Action | V | Supervisor Review |
| Q | Quality Review | G | Regulatory Review |
| U | Consult Supplier | E | Execute Trial |
| I | Inspect Trial | J | Adjust Parameters |
| L | Release Material | Z | Archive Case |

Let B denote the selected review route, O the supplier choice, and T the whole trial loop. An arrow involving a submodel refers to its entry or its completion as appropriate.

| ID | Source activity or submodel | Target activity or submodel | Relation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Case start | R | Mandatory first activity | Registration opens every case. |
| 2 | R | C and A | Enable both, C ∥ A | Both activities are required. Either may finish first. |
| 3 | C | P | Finish before start | Planning can proceed while analysis is still running. |
| 4 | A | D | Finish before start | Design can proceed while containment is still running. |
| 5 | A | B | Finish before route entry | Analysis enables the review decision. Neither work product is a prerequisite. |
| 6 | B entry | V or {Q, G} | Exclusive choice, Q ∥ G | Low risk requires only V. High risk requires both Q and G in either order. |
| 7 | A | O | Finish before choice of U or silent skip | If selected, U runs exactly once and may overlap P, D, B or T. |
| 8 | P and D | First E in T | Join both work products | Trial work is enabled immediately after both finish, independently of B and O. |
| 9 | E in iteration k | I in iteration k | Mandatory sequence | Each execution gets exactly one inspection. |
| 10 | I in iteration k | J then E in iteration k+1, or loop exit | Exclusive redo or exit | Failure requires adjustment and a fresh trial. A pass ends T and disables further adjustment. |
| 11 | T, B and O | L | Join all three completions | Release needs a pass, the entire chosen review route, and consultation if selected. These branches may finish in any order. |
| 12 | L | Z then case end | Mandatory final sequence | Archive closes the released case. |

# 3. PM4Py Process-Tree Model

Here SEQ orders its children, AND runs all its children in parallel, XOR selects one child, and LOOP(do, redo) runs its do child once and repeats it after each redo. The symbol tau denotes a silent leaf.

```text
SEQ(R,
    AND(SEQ(C, P), SEQ(A, D)),
    AND(XOR(V, AND(Q, G)), XOR(tau, U), LOOP(SEQ(E, I), J)),
    L, Z)
```

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree, Operator


def leaf(label):
    return ProcessTree(label=label)


def branch(operator, *children):
    parent = ProcessTree(operator=operator)
    for child in children:
        parent.children.append(child)
        child.parent = parent
    return parent


work_products = branch(
    Operator.PARALLEL,
    branch(Operator.SEQUENCE, leaf("Contain Material"), leaf("Plan Verification")),
    branch(Operator.SEQUENCE, leaf("Analyze Root Cause"), leaf("Design Corrective Action")),
)
reviews = branch(
    Operator.XOR,
    leaf("Supervisor Review"),
    branch(Operator.PARALLEL, leaf("Quality Review"), leaf("Regulatory Review")),
)
supplier = branch(Operator.XOR, ProcessTree(), leaf("Consult Supplier"))
trial = branch(
    Operator.LOOP,
    branch(Operator.SEQUENCE, leaf("Execute Trial"), leaf("Inspect Trial")),
    leaf("Adjust Parameters"),
)
tree = branch(
    Operator.SEQUENCE,
    leaf("Register Deviation"),
    work_products,
    branch(Operator.PARALLEL, reviews, supplier, trial),
    leaf("Release Material"),
    leaf("Archive Case"),
)
```

The only added ordering rule is the barrier from completion of **both P and D** to entry of **both B and O**. Thus no review or selected consultation can finish during planning, design or unfinished containment. This narrows the timing in requirements 5 and 6. It preserves the route contents, supplier optionality, trial start and release conditions. A structural reason for the compromise is visible even in a low-risk, single-trial run. On the four activities V, A, E and P the only intended comparabilities are A < V, A < E and P < E. These form the four-element N order, which has no decomposition into sequence and parallel blocks. This explains the difficulty with a single block decomposition. Enumerating different interleavings in exclusive branches would pose a different modeling choice and could require multiple nodes with the same label.

# 4. PM4Py POWL Model

```text
B = XOR(V, PO({Q, G}, no edges))
O = XOR(tau, U)
T = LOOP(PO({E, I}, E -> I), J)
powl = PO({R, C, A, P, D, B, O, T, L, Z}, covers below)
  R -> C, A
  C -> P
  A -> D, B, O
  P -> T
  D -> T
  B -> L
  O -> L
  T -> L
  L -> Z
```

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition,
)

register = Transition(label="Register Deviation")
contain = Transition(label="Contain Material")
analyze = Transition(label="Analyze Root Cause")
plan = Transition(label="Plan Verification")
design = Transition(label="Design Corrective Action")
release = Transition(label="Release Material")
archive = Transition(label="Archive Case")

high = StrictPartialOrder([
    Transition(label="Quality Review"),
    Transition(label="Regulatory Review"),
])
reviews = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [Transition(label="Supervisor Review"), high])
supplier = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR, [SilentTransition(), Transition(label="Consult Supplier")]
)

execute = Transition(label="Execute Trial")
inspect = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
iteration = StrictPartialOrder([execute, inspect])
iteration.order.add_edge(execute, inspect)
trial = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.LOOP, [iteration, Transition(label="Adjust Parameters")]
)

powl = StrictPartialOrder([
    register, contain, analyze, plan, design, reviews, supplier, trial, release, archive,
])

# Store the full strict relation, including transitive consequences of the covers.
successors = [
    (register, [contain, analyze, plan, design, reviews, supplier, trial, release, archive]),
    (contain, [plan, trial, release, archive]),
    (analyze, [design, reviews, supplier, trial, release, archive]),
    (plan, [trial, release, archive]),
    (design, [trial, release, archive]),
    (reviews, [release, archive]),
    (supplier, [release, archive]),
    (trial, [release, archive]),
    (release, [archive]),
]
for source_node, targets in successors:
    for target_node in targets:
        powl.order.add_edge(source_node, target_node)
```

The incomparable pairs at the root can be accounted for in three groups.

- Each of C and P is incomparable with each of A, D, B and O. The containment stream has no handoff to the analysis stream. Each activity still waits for its own predecessors.
- D is incomparable with B and O. All three need analysis, and none consumes another's result.
- B, O and T are pairwise incomparable. They meet only at release. In particular, one high-risk review can finish between E and I while the other finishes after the pass.

Within the high-risk branch, the empty order permits Q and G to overlap and requires both. Inside XOR the unchosen alternative has no execution. Inside the loop the body and redo obey loop order. For any intended trace, choose B from its review labels, choose O from the presence or absence of U, and take the redo branch once per J. The remaining events then form an interleaving of those branches respecting every cover edge. Conversely, the choices and loop fix the required activity occurrences, and the edges enforce their prerequisites and the release join. This gives equality with the intended complete visible traces under the stated abstractions.

# 5. Behavioral Witnesses

Each row is a complete visible sequence. For a rejected sequence, the reason identifies the earliest event after which no valid completion can extend the prefix.

| Trace ID | Visible trace | Intended verdict | Process-tree verdict | POWL verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `R C P A D V E I L Z` | Allow | Allow | Allow | Planning finishes before analysis. The low-risk route uses V and skips U. |
| 2 | `R A D C P G E Q U I L Z` | Allow | Allow | Allow | Design finishes before containment. High-risk G precedes Q, and Q and U both finish between execution and inspection. |
| 3 | `R C A D P E Q I J E I U G L Z` | Allow | Allow | Allow | The first inspection takes the redo, the second passes. Q precedes G, and release waits for U and G after the pass. |
| 4 | `R A V D C P E I L Z` | Allow | Reject | Allow | A already enables V. The tree first blocks at V because the work products are pending. |
| 5 | `R C P A U D E I V L Z` | Allow | Reject | Allow | U may finish immediately after A. The tree first blocks at U because D is pending. |
| 6 | `R A D C E P I V L Z` | Reject | Reject | Reject | At E, design is complete but the required plan is missing. The later P arrives too late. |
| 7 | `R A C D P Q E I L Z` | Reject | Reject | Reject | Q commits to high risk. L is the first violation because G is still missing. |
| 8 | `R C A P D V E I J L Z` | Reject | Reject | Reject | J is legal and identifies the preceding inspection as a failure. L then occurs before the required new E and passing I. |

# 6. Semantic Comparison

| Requirement | Intended semantics | Process tree | POWL | Overconstraint or underconstraint | Business consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Initial work streams | R enables C and A independently | Parallel streams after R | R precedes both, C and A incomparable | Exact in both | Neither stream must wait for the other to begin. |
| Split into P and D | C enables P, A enables D | Parallel sequences C–P and A–D | Separate C < P and A < D edges | Exact in both | Each team can deliver its work product while the other is still working. |
| Risk route | After A, choose V or both Q and G | Same XOR and parallel reviews, enabled after P and D | B enabled after A | Tree overconstrains onset only | The tree flags early reviews as deviations and a scheduler using it would delay them. |
| Review and trial overlap | P and D enable T regardless of review progress | T and B are parallel after the work products | B and T incomparable, with distinct predecessors | Exact trial gate and overlap during T in both. Only the tree loses the reviews' head start. | Trial may begin with all approvals outstanding. |
| Optional supplier | After A, skip or perform U once by release | Correct cardinality, choice delayed until P and D finish | O follows A and joins at L | Tree overconstrains onset only | A legitimate supplier completion during design or planning can fail tree-based conformance. |
| Repeat until pass | E I followed by zero or more J E I repetitions, projected onto trial labels | Binary loop | Binary loop with ordered body | Exact in both under the outcome abstraction | Every adjustment requires another inspected trial before release. |
| Release and closure | Pass, all selected reviews and selected U completed before L, then Z | Parallel join before L, then Z | B, O and T precede L, then L < Z | Exact gate in both | Neither model allows release with an outstanding obligation. |
| Duplicate-label risk | Event names identify activities, with recurring trial events | One leaf per visible label | One transition node per visible label | No extra traces or exclusions from label duplication | A repeated I denotes another inspection occurrence. The node identity stays consistent across iterations. |

# 7. Preferred Representation and Validation Plan

Use POWL as the canonical control-flow specification. The incoming edges of T are exactly the two work-product dependencies in the cover relation. The distinct incoming edges of L account for all three release obligations. This lets a conformance check accept traces 4 and 5 while still rejecting 6 and 7.

1. Convert each model separately with `net, im, fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(model)` in a PM4Py release supporting that model. Keep both markings with the net. Run `pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)` on each result. Check for the option to complete, proper completion and dead transitions.
2. Test the eight witnesses against each accepting net. Start from the silent closure of the initial marking. For each event, fire all matching enabled transitions from the current set, keep every possible successor, and close under silent firings again. Accept exactly when the set after the last event contains the final marking. Use visited markings within each closure. This tests exact membership even when a label can be reached by several silent paths.
3. For bounded playout, cap E at three occurrences and J at two. Branch over both risk choices, supplier present or absent, and one through three iterations. Independently unroll Section 2 for each combination. Give each E and I an iteration index internally, join the required reviews and optional U at L, and enumerate topological orders with the indices erased in the emitted trace. Compare this reference language in both directions with visible, final-marking-reaching net playout at the same iteration bound. Expect equality for POWL. For the tree, expect exactly the reference traces in which every review and U occurs after both P and D.
4. Check silent behavior explicitly. Exercise the supplier skip with no visible U. Insert a second U in a valid supplier trace and require rejection. On the projection to E, I and J, require at least one E I and one J between successive iterations. Check the converted nets' visible-label counts against the 14 labels and inspect any copies introduced during conversion. Distinguish a second model transition from firing the same transition twice.
5. A sound net can still put a dependency in the wrong place. Finite playout leaves longer retry histories untested, and completion-only logs cannot verify start times, risk classification, supplier-selection data or the real inspection outcome.

I ran these checks with PM4Py 2.7.23.4. Both conversions preserved one visible transition per label. Both nets passed the eight witness tests, the silent-skip test, and the repeated-consultation rejection test. The soundness checker returned true for both. An independent traversal of every reachable marking (48 for the tree, 114 for POWL) confirmed that each could reach the final marking, that reaching the final place left no tokens elsewhere and enabled no further firing, and that every transition could fire on some run. Within the playout bound, POWL admitted exactly the 20,952 distinct reference traces. The tree admitted exactly the 10,152 traces satisfying its additional barrier.

# 8. Final Self-Critique

1. The tree delays a potentially long consultation or review. Its conformance verdict can therefore penalize a case that followed the intended business process.
2. The POWL model infers failure from a redo and pass from loop exit. A case that releases after a *real* failed inspection may look valid if the inspection outcome is omitted from the log.
3. Completion-order tests can miss an activity that started too soon but finished after its prerequisite. Checking the finish-before-start interpretation against actual operations would require lifecycle timestamps.
