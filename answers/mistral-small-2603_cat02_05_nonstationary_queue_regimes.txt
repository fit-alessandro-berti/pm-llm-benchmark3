Queueing quantities and units:
- arrivals, completed: counts during opening hours (07:00–19:00)
- median_service_min, p90_service_min: active handling time in minutes (excluding waiting)
- mean_wip: mean number of cases in system from 15-min snapshots (unitless count)
- staff_fte: mean concurrently available full-time-equivalent capacity
- complex_pct: percentage of arrivals following multi-step complex variant
- outage_min: minutes of outage during opening hours
- peak_08_10_pct: percentage of daily arrivals between 08:00–09:59

Opening-hours convention: All metrics are computed only during 07:00–19:00.
Cases can carry over overnight; no service occurs while closed.

Limitations of aggregates:
1. No backlog at 07:00 is supplied  cannot compute net inflow or true daily throughput.
2. Completed cases may have arrived on prior days  daily completion  daily arrival.
3. Median and p90 service times  mean flow time  Little’s Law cannot use median service as flow time.
4. Complex_pct may reflect coding change, not real mix shift  interpret with caution.
5. Representative hourly vectors are single-day snapshots  not robust to day-to-day variation.
6. Daily rows are not independent  weekday/weekend patterns and carry-over create serial dependence.
7. Outage on 2026-04-15 is logged but timing is approximate  recovery period unclear.
8. Staffing intervention began on 2026-04-29 with no logged implementation time  change point uncertain.
9. WIP is from 15-min snapshots  may miss short-lived bursts.
10. Peak_08_10_pct uses 08:00–09:59, but hourly vectors show peak often in 09:00–10:00 bin  slight misalignment.

Threats to naive inference:
- Simpson’s paradox: pooling across regimes with different case mixes can reverse observed effects.
- Backlog carry-over: high WIP may reflect prior-day backlog, not current capacity pressure.
- Outlier days (e.g., 2026-04-15 outage, 2026-04-21 bursty) dominate pooled averages.
