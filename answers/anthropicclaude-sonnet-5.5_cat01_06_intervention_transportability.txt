**Shared mechanisms.** Removing a non-statutory precedence between checks that use independent data shortens the verification critical path from I+D to max(I,D) and removes one queue hop. The join at Assessment exists at both sites, so a fast branch cannot help while the slow branch runs. This mechanism plausibly holds wherever the checks are independent and both branches have free capacity.

**Site-specific mechanisms.** At B, originals gate Document Check in 62% of cases, so the binding branch is an external delay. Separate roles remove pooling. The single-owner lock decides whether true overlap exists. Legal review and supplement loops create a rework pathway that A lacks. A's API upgrade, added reviewers, huddles and mix shift act on the same mediator, `waiting_before_assessment`.

**Effect modifiers.** These are `originals_required`, `digital_documents`, `case_complexity`, `role_specialization`, `IT_case_lock`, the three capacities, and `legal_review_required`. The last of these mainly modifies tail and rework effects.

**Why similar control flow is insufficient.** The tree only labels activities and orderings. Transport also depends on which edges are enabled per case (data-dependent guards), which resources serve them, the joint distribution of branch durations (a max depends on variance and tails, not medians), the recording process, and the shared queues coupling tasks. Identical labels can hide different enabled partial orders, different bottlenecks and different measurement error.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Name.** Staged conditional verification concurrency with early-start for originals cases.

**Eligibility rules.**
- **Track P (parallel verification).**
  - `originals_required = false`, determined at registration from the legal criteria.
  - Digital completeness check passed at registration.
  - Wave 1 also requires no public-sector and no high-value flag. Wave 2 adds legal-review cases.
  - No known identity-document discrepancy flag.
- **Track E (early start, not parallel Document Check).**
  - All `originals_required = true` cases.
  - Identity Check and the originals request are triggered at registration, which rules 2–3 already permit.
  - Document Check is created only on the Originals Receipt event.
- **Control.** Legacy process for randomized controls and any case failing eligibility checks.

**Concurrency.**
- Track P: Identity Check  Document Check after Register.
- Track E: Identity Check  Originals Receipt, then Document Check after receipt.
- Assessment after both checks.
- Legal Review (when required) after Assessment.

**Statutory prerequisites that remain mandatory.**
- Register first.
- Document Check after Originals Receipt when originals are legally required.
- Assessment after both checks complete.
- Legal Review for public-sector or high-value cases.
- Decision after Assessment and Legal Review, then Notify.
- No "pre-screen of scanned copies" is permitted as a substitute for Document Check. Legal counsel must approve any separate non-decisional pre-screen, and it must be logged as a distinct activity.

**Queue and WIP controls (starting values, to be tuned).**
- Keep the per-reviewer cap of 6 open tasks per role.
- Create Document Check tasks on a pull basis: only if document-pool open tasks  30 (~70% of the cap of 42). Otherwise fall back to sequential order (identity first).
- Release to Assessment only if the assessor ready-queue is below about one working day of capacity.
- Use age-based FIFO within class across arms. Supplement returns get expedite priority.
- Never count idle, blocked tasks against WIP. Do not create them.

**Resource responsibilities.**
- Identity reviewers own the identity task only.
- Document reviewers own document tasks.
- A case coordinator role owns the join and monitors fallbacks, ages and back-entry.
- Assessors are released only when the join condition holds.
- Legal reviewers handle legal review and supplement requests.
- A pilot option is to cross-train 1–2 identity reviewers for document checks.

**Legal-review and supplement loops.**
- Request Supplement  Document Check (and originals if newly required)  Assessment  Legal Review.
- Identity is re-run only if a discrepancy flag is raised by the document check (rework type R_ID).
- Log supplement cause and time lost.

**Logging requirements.**
- System-generated lifecycle events (created, assigned, start, complete) for every task.
- Event time and record time, with a back-entry flag.
- Case attributes: `originals_required` and basis, digital completeness, public-sector, high-value, complexity, stratum, arm and version.
- Owner ID, lock events, pool WIP snapshots, assessor queue length, discrepancy flags, supplement cause.

**POWL-style partial-order relation set (Track P / general model).**

```text
Nodes: Register, Identity, X(OriginalsReceipt, tau) = Orig_opt, DocCheck, Assessment,
       X(tau, *(LegalReview, PO(RequestSupplement -> DocCheck' -> Assessment'))) = Legal_opt,
       X(Approve, Reject) = Decision, Notify
Order: Register < Identity
       Register < Orig_opt < DocCheck
       Identity < Assessment
       DocCheck < Assessment
       Assessment < Legal_opt < Decision < Notify
Concurrency: Identity || (Orig_opt -> DocCheck) until the join at Assessment
Track guards (outside POWL): Orig_opt = OriginalsReceipt iff originals_required,
       and DocCheck task is created on the receipt event; in Track P Orig_opt = tau
Loop: after Legal Review, optional redo PO(RequestSupplement -> DocCheck' -> Assessment') then back to Legal Review
```

**How the pilot separates mechanism failure from implementation failure (220 words).**

Implementation failure means the intervention was not delivered as designed. Mechanism failure means it was delivered but did not produce the expected change. The pilot keeps these apart by measuring treatment received, not only treatment assigned.

1. Fidelity metrics come from system-generated events: two tasks concurrently open with distinct owners, near-zero lag between event and record, no blocked idle tasks, no statutory violations. Randomized assignment is used as an instrument, so compliance-adjusted (CACE) effects can be estimated alongside intention-to-treat.
2. If assigned cases do not achieve true overlap, that is an implementation failure (lock, WIP cap, staffing). Fix it and retest before judging the idea.
3. If true overlap is achieved but verification span falls little, the critical path lies elsewhere (originals, document branch dominance). That is a mechanism limit for that stratum.
4. If verification span falls but waiting before Assessment and cycle time do not, the bottleneck has moved downstream. That is a mechanism-plus-capacity interaction, visible in assessor queue data.
5. If the cycle time falls but rework, supplement rate or p90 worsens, the mechanism has side effects for that subgroup.

Each link of the mediator chain is tested separately, so failures are attributed to the step where the chain breaks.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# ---------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Site A process trees (before / after)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------
pt_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)
pt_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Convert to accepting Petri nets
# ---------------------------------------------------------------
net_a0, im_a0, fm_a0 = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(pt_before)
net_a1, im_a1, fm_a1 = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(pt_after)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Site B candidate as POWL
#    (flat strict partial order at the root; nested XOR / LOOP operators)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------
register   = Transition(label="Register")
identity   = Transition(label="Identity Check")
originals  = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
doc_check  = Transition(label="Document Check")
assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")
legal      = Transition(label="Legal Review")
supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
approve    = Transition(label="Approve")
reject     = Transition(label="Reject")
notify     = Transition(label="Notify")

# Originals Receipt is optional in the model (required only for ~62% of cases);
# when skipped, Document Check may start right after Register.
orig_opt = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR,
                        children=[originals, SilentTransition()])

# Supplement loop: do = Legal Review;
# redo = Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment (then Legal Review again)
doc_check_redo   = Transition(label="Document Check")
assessment_redo  = Transition(label="Assessment")
redo_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[supplement, doc_check_redo, assessment_redo])
redo_body.order.add_edge(supplement, doc_check_redo)
redo_body.order.add_edge(doc_check_redo, assessment_redo)
legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[legal, redo_body])

# Legal review is optional (public-sector or high-value cases only)
legal_opt = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR,
                         children=[SilentTransition(), legal_loop])

decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[approve, reject])

root = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[
    register, identity, orig_opt, doc_check,
    assessment, legal_opt, decision, notify
])

# 4. Explicit order edges
root.order.add_edge(register, identity)        # identity may start after Register
root.order.add_edge(register, orig_opt)        # originals may start after Register
root.order.add_edge(orig_opt, doc_check)       # Document Check after Originals Receipt (when present)
root.order.add_edge(identity, assessment)      # Assessment waits for identity ...
root.order.add_edge(doc_check, assessment)     # ... AND for document verification
root.order.add_edge(assessment, legal_opt)
root.order.add_edge(legal_opt, decision)
root.order.add_edge(decision, notify)
# Identity and Document Check have no mutual edge -> they are concurrent.

try:
    print("Root order irreflexive:", root.order.is_irreflexive())
except AttributeError:
    pass

# ---------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. POWL -> accepting Petri net
# ---------------------------------------------------------------
net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(root)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------
# 6. Workflow-net / soundness checks
# ---------------------------------------------------------------
def report(name, net, im, fm):
    wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    res = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)
    sound = res[0] if isinstance(res, tuple) else res
    print(f"{name}: workflow net = {wf}, sound = {sound}")
```

## 9. Recommendation

**Decision: `conditional go`.**

Transfer the mechanism, not the diagram. Remove non-statutory precedence between Identity Check and Document Check where both can legitimately start at registration. Implement it with true concurrent task ownership, system-generated timestamps and a hard join before Assessment. Also transfer the measurement discipline that Site A lacked: lifecycle-event overlap, a negative-control process, and mediator tracking (verification span, waiting before Assessment).

Do not copy creating Document Check at registration for every case. For the 62% with legally required originals, Document Check cannot start before receipt. Forcing it creates blocked tasks that consume WIP, invites review of copies, and risks statutory breach. Do not copy the all-teams-in-two-weeks rollout, the bundled co-interventions, the pooled cross-trained staffing assumption, or case-lock workarounds with back-entered events. Do not use the nine-hour median drop as an expected benefit.

Start with originals-not-required, digitally complete, non-legal cases: about 20% of volume, ~110–120 cases a month. They avoid external delay and legal loops and isolate the pure mechanism. Wave 2 adds no-originals legal-review cases. Originals-required cases get an early-start (identity and the originals request at registration, already allowed by rules 2–3), tested separately.

Expected benefit: in-stratum median 10% to 20% (~3–7 h), overall median only about 1 to 3 h (2% to 7%), and a small p90 change. Risks: the document pool is near its cap (~33 of 42 open tasks from service time alone), the bottleneck can shift to the three assessors, supplement loops can worsen the tail, and lock workarounds can create false overlap.

Expand when:
- true overlap is  80% in treated cases and back-entry is < 5%;
- in-stratum median falls  10% with a confidence interval excluding zero;
- p90 is not worse by more than 10%;
- rework stays within +3 pp;
- the assessor queue is stable.

Modify when parallelization works but assessor or legal queues grow (add metering or capacity), or when overlap is not achieved (fix IT before judging the idea).

Abandon if waiting before Assessment does not fall despite  70% true overlap, if rework or appeals exceed their margins, or if statutory ordering violations recur after a fix.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Originals and legal flags are known at registration and independent enough for the eligible share to be ~20%** — needed for safe eligibility and for the sample-size estimate — test: audit ~200 cases against the legal criteria and cross-tabulate originals × legal × digital — consequence if false: ineligible cases could be parallelized (statutory breach) or the eligible stratum is smaller and the pilot takes longer.
2. **Identity and document checks at B use sufficiently independent data** — needed for the max(I,D) mechanism — test: measure the discrepancy rate where document results change identity outcomes — consequence if false: parallel work is redone and rework rises.
3. **The case system can support sub-work-item ownership without workarounds** — needed for true overlap — test: sandbox with two simultaneous owners and timestamp lag checks — consequence if false: S4 (recorded but not real overlap) and possible harm.
4. **Touch-time and utilization by pool are measurable** (elapsed service times here are not effort) — needed for capacity, Little's Law and Kingman reasoning — test: time study or task-level active time logging per role — consequence if false: the bottleneck and WIP cap predictions are wrong.
5. **A's parallelization effect is nonzero and positive, about a third to two-thirds of the 9 h** — needed only for a discounted prior — test: re-analyze A by complexity strata with interrupted time series and a negative-control process — consequence if false: the prior should be dropped and B's expectations lowered.
6. **The rework increase at A (+0.6 pp) is not caused by parallelization** — needed to treat rework as a secondary concern rather than a primary harm — test: A's rework by type and mix-adjusted comparison — consequence if false: expect rework to rise at B, and the loop costs are larger.
7. **Case-level randomization with shared pools has limited interference** — needed for causal interpretability — test: compare case-level estimates with switchback estimates and monitor queue spillover — consequence if false: effects are biased (the control arm benefits or suffers from the treated arm).
8. **B's logs are reliable enough to measure overlap and durations** (current DFG counts are inconsistent) — needed for all process-mining outcomes and the fidelity gate — test: reconcile the DFG counts and quantify back-entry lag — consequence if false: overlap and cycle time comparisons are unreliable and the pilot's fidelity claims cannot be verified.
