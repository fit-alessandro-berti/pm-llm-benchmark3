Sum 6.71  | Standardized: wait 9.65 vs 11.80 (2.15, **85%** of 2.51); service 5.80 vs 6.07 (0.27); compliance overhead identical (0.61 each); rework 0.28 vs 0.39 (0.10) | "The label difference lives in queueing and mix, not in review effort" | "Orion reviewers are faster/better." Rework is a mediator/collider; the near-zero re-review increment contradicts the stated definition |
| 30-day quality | Appeals 1/14 (7.1%) Orion vs 1/16 (6.3%) Nova  +0.9 pp | Orion appeal = C14 (High, compliance, rework, 33.5 h); Nova appeal = C24 (Low, no compliance, no rework, Day/High load, wait 7.0 = max of Low) | "Two events, opposite risk profiles: no usable quality signal" | Any non-inferiority or superiority claim; appeal is post-process and cannot validate a cycle-time trade-off at n=2 |
| Measurement structure | cycle  wait  service = 0.8 (plain), 2.2 (compliance), 2.3 (rework), 3.6–3.7 (both) for 29/30 cases (C26=0.7) | Implied additive law: cycle  0.8 + wait + service + 1.4·compliance + 1.5·rework | "Cycle time carries no independent group information; group differences must be explained by wait, service, or variant mix" | Treating cycle_time as an independent outcome that could reveal hidden Orion delay; the constant overheads suggest derived or rounded fields |

## 5. Competing causal explanations and falsification tests

| rank | hypothesis | process mechanism | evidence supporting it | evidence contradicting it | discriminating analysis or falsification test | predicted observation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Case-mix confounding (Simpson reversal); no Orion harm** | Routing sends complex, compliance-heavy, night, high-load cases to Orion; complexity drives wait+service | Reversal in all 3 strata; standardized 2.51 h; crude gap = 4.01 wait + 2.00 service + 0.70 variant overhead | Complexity is coarse; nothing rules out offsetting unmeasured severity | Exact matching on complexity (then +load, +shift where overlap exists), plus standardized contrasts under 3 weighting targets; E-value-style sensitivity for an unmeasured severity confounder | Matched/standardized contrast stays  2 to 3 h across weightings; an unmeasured confounder would need to shift within-stratum cycle time by >3 h *and* be strongly imbalanced to restore a positive Orion effect |
| 2 | **Orion genuinely beneficial, masked by mix** (certified skill + queue priority) | More certified reviewers  slightly less service time; Orion queue may be prioritized/preempting | Service advantage in every stratum (0.39/0.23/0.13 h); wait advantage 1.6/1.9/3.3 h | Skill cannot plausibly cut *queueing* by 3 h; effect concentrated in wait, not effort | Within-reviewer and within-certification-level comparisons of service; queue-state reconstruction (queue position at Classify, priority flag, staffing per hour) to see whether Orion cases jump the queue; front-door-style decomposition group{skill, priority}{service, wait} | If skill: service gap survives within certification level. If priority: wait gap vanishes after conditioning on queue position/priority flag  benefit is a *policy* artifact and is destroyed, not transferred, by relabeling |
| 3 | **Genuine Orion harm through night queueing/capacity (mediated harm)** | Night skeleton staffing + 100% High arrival load  long post-Classify queues; harm exists but is swamped by mix in the crude number | Orion 92.9% night, 100% High load; wait is 60% of crude gap; Nova night Medium/High essentially unobserved | Within every stratum Orion waits *less* than Nova, including night-vs-night Low (3.3 vs 3.8/5.1) | Queue reconstruction with hourly staffing and arrival counts; compare Orion vs Nova at equal complexity×shift×load×staffing; then a stepped-wedge or randomized overflow pilot at night | If harm: at equal staffing/load Orion queues are longer and the pilot's Nova-routed night cases improve. Current data predict the opposite (pilot Nova night waits rise 2 h); a null pilot with wide CIs would leave harm unresolved, not excluded |
| 4 | **Compliance / rework mediation, or measurement folding of compliance waiting** | Compliance step and Request-Info loop add waiting; `wait_before_review_h` mixes queue and compliance waiting; rework adds re-review | Compliance 64.3% vs 25%; rework 28.6% vs 18.8%; fixed +1.4/+1.5 h overheads | Within Medium both groups 50% compliance; compliance-required cases wait *less* (8.57 vs 9.93; 10.35 vs 11.90); rework  Nova in all strata; re-review adds ~0–0.3 h | Timestamp split of wait into post-Classify queue vs compliance-adjacent wait; test groupcompliance_required within complexity (should be null by policy); conformance check required-vs-performed; recompute service excluding re-review | No groupcompliance association within complexity (Medium 50/50)  compliance is a complexity marker, not a group mediator. If the split shows compliance-adjacent waiting dominates Orion's wait, mediation is revived; if re-review time is truly ~0.2 h, the `review_service` definition is wrong |
| 5 | **Selection / survivorship and coarse measurement** | Long, difficult Orion night cases abandon or stay open and are excluded; conditioning on `case_completed`=1 opens collider paths | Prompt states only completed cases present; `case_completed` has parents group, wait, complexity, risk; appeal indicator coarse and post-process | Completed-case Orion advantage is large and consistent, which survivor bias alone would have to manufacture | Reconstruct all *registered* cases incl. open/abandoned; completion-rate table by group×complexity×shift; time-to-completion survival model with censoring + IPCW; Lee/Manski bounds on the group contrast | If Orion censors more long cases, its completed mean is biased downward and bounds straddle 0 or turn positive; equal completion hazards would largely defuse this explanation |
| 6 | **Group is a proxy for a routing-policy bundle, not a well-defined treatment** | Label carries reviewer pool, certification, priority, shift coverage, escalation rights; "move 50%" changes several things at once | Prompt: routing engine after Classify, certification asymmetry, night coverage; empty Orion Day×Medium/High cells | Nothing in the data isolates a single mechanism | Positivity/overlap diagnostics on complexity×shift×load×group; enumerate treatment versions (label only vs label+capacity+priority); specify the policy estimand (E4) instead of the label estimand (E1) | Diagnostics show 0 cases in Orion Day Medium/High and n=1 Nova Night Medium/High  the proposed intervention region is unsupported; version enumeration shows effects are not transportable without capacity transfer |
| 7 | **Residual within-stratum severity confounding favouring Orion** | Routing may also balance load, sending *easier-within-band* cases to Orion, or severity is measured with error | Complexity has only 3 bands; routing inputs unlogged; High band spans 29.5–36.0 h | Routing is stated to favour Orion for *difficult* cases, which biases against Orion, not for it | Negative-control outcome: pre-assignment duration (RegisterClassify), which group cannot cause; add granular severity (claim amount, claimant history, documents missing) and re-match; within-route variant comparison | Negative control shows no group difference (here overhead 0.8 h for all 30 cases  consistent with no pre-assignment artefact). If granular severity shrinks the 2.5 h toward 0, hypothesis 2 weakens and hypothesis 1 becomes "confounded in both directions" |

## 6. Intervention and learning plan

| phase | intervention | target mechanism | assignment unit | safeguards | process-mining measurements | causal learning value | stop or rollback rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Instrument (2–3 weeks, no routing change)** | Log per-case event timestamps, routing-rule inputs/score, reviewer ID + certification, hourly staffing, queue position/priority flag, compliance start/end, Request-Info send/reply, and **all registered cases incl. open/abandoned** | Measurement, selection, queue-vs-service separation | none (observational) | read-only; no SLA impact | variant/conformance rates (required vs performed compliance), wait decomposition, DFG per group×shift, completion/censoring table | Converts E1 from unidentifiable to candidate-identifiable; enables queue reconstruction, negative controls, IPCW; validates the suspicious additive cycle-time law | abort if <95% of cases have compliant timestamps or if open-case backlog cannot be reconstructed |
| **1. Capacity/queue & compliance fix (4–6 weeks)** | Add night certified review capacity and run Compliance Check in parallel with queueing; pre-fetch missing info at Classify. **Group labels unchanged** | `queue_wait`, compliance-adjacent waiting, rework trigger | shift×hour block (stepped-wedge across night blocks) | keep certification requirements; cap WIP; daily backlog monitor | queue-state reconstruction (wait vs staffing/load), compliance wait share, rework rate, variant mix, standardized cycle time by complexity | Tests hypothesis 3/4 directly: if night waiting is capacity-driven, wait falls for *both* groups  the "Orion effect" was a staffing mechanism, and relabeling is unnecessary | roll back a block if night queue length or >SLA share rises >20% vs pre-period baseline for 3 consecutive nights |
| **2. Small randomized routing pilot (6–8 weeks)** | Randomize **10–15% of eligible cases** to Nova instead of Orion, blocked by complexity×shift×arrival_load; start with Medium and Low, night included only after Phase 1 adds night certified capacity. Pre-register MDE (e.g. 2 h) and analysis (stratum-standardized ITT) | isolate `resource_group` label from routing selection; test skill vs priority | individual case, block-randomized; overflow decisions logged | exclude cases whose required certification Nova lacks; automatic overflow back to Orion on queue breach; senior-review escalation; independent quality monitor | ITT cycle time and its wait/service split; per-protocol conformance; rework and Request-Info loop rates; queue position of control vs treated; appeals as *guardrail only* | First genuine E1 estimate in supported strata; distinguishes "Orion harmful" (treated improve) from "Orion beneficial/priority" (treated worsen); measures the real transportability penalty at night | pause if treated-arm mean wait exceeds control by >3 h, or any severity-3 quality event, or >2 appeals/50 treated cases; halt if night backlog >110% of baseline |
| **3. Decide, then scale adaptively** | If Phase 2 shows benefit, expand as a *routing-policy* change with capacity and priority transferred explicitly; if null/harmful, keep routing and scale Phase 1 | policy bundle (routing_rule), not label | policy version rolled out by complexity×shift cell | staged rollout with holdout cells; weekly standardized reporting with support flags; no change to High-complexity night routing until n50/arm | standardized (pooled-mix) cycle time with cell counts, overlap dashboard, variant drift, censoring rates, rework/quality trend | Estimates E4 (policy) rather than an ambiguous label effect; preserves a permanent randomized holdout for future routing changes | revert any cell whose standardized cycle time or appeal rate worsens beyond pre-set limits for 2 weeks; never scale into cells with <10 historical cases |

**Final recommendation.** Do not approve the blanket 50% reassignment. Its premise — Orion's higher overall mean — is a mix artifact: the sign reverses in all three complexity strata and under three standardization targets (2.3 to 2.7 h), and roughly 85% of Orion's within-mix advantage sits in queueing, not review effort. Worse, the proposal targets exactly the cells with no data: Orion has zero Day Medium/High cases, and Nova has one Night Medium and one Night High case. Moving night, compliance-heavy, high-complexity work into a day-oriented, less-certified pool is an extrapolation that could lengthen queues, increase rework and erode quality, with only two appeal events available as a guardrail. Approve Phase 0 instrumentation and Phase 1 night capacity plus parallel compliance instead: they attack the dominant, plausibly causal mechanism (waiting) without touching routing. Then run the blocked randomized pilot to learn the label's actual effect in supported strata. Report all future comparisons standardized to a stated case mix, with cell counts and positivity flags attached.

## 7. Reproducible Python diagnostics

```python
"""
Diagnostic (NOT causal) analysis of Orion vs Nova claims-review cases.
- Reproduces crude + complexity-stratified cycle-time comparisons.
- Complexity-standardized contrast under three explicit weighting targets.
- Overlap/positivity counts for complexity x shift x resource_group.
- Variant + directly-follows (structure only) comparison via PM4Py.
No event timestamps are fabricated for performance analysis: all durations come
from the supplied numeric columns; PM4Py is used for ORDER/STRUCTURE only.
"""
import io

import pandas as pd

CSV = """case_id,trace,complexity,group,shift,arrival_load,compliance_required,wait_before_review_h,review_service_h,rework_count,cycle_time_h,outcome_30d

C13,Register>Classify>Compliance Check>Review>Close,High,Orion,Night,High,Yes,21.4,9.2,0,32.8,No appeal
C14,Register>Classify>Compliance Check>Review>Request Info>Review>Close,High,Orion,Night,High,Yes,20.4,9.4,1,33.5,Appeal

C17,Register>Classify>Review>Close,Low,Nova,Day,Normal,No,4.2,3.9,0,8.9,No appeal
C18,Register>Classify>Review>Close,Low,Nova,Day,High,No,5.0,3.4,0,9.2,No appeal
C19,Register>Classify>Review>Close,Low,Nova,Day,Normal,No,5.0,3.7,0,9.5,No appeal
C20,Register>Classify>Review>Close,Low,Nova,Night,High,No,5.1,3.9,0,9.8,No appeal
C21,Register>Classify>Review>Close,Low,Nova,Day,High,No,5.9,3.4,0,10.1,No appeal

C24,Register>Classify>Review>Close,Low,Nova,Day,High,No,7.0,3.4,0,11.2,Appeal

C28,Register>Classify>Review>Request Info>Review>Close,Medium,Nova,Day,High,No,12.0,6.2,1,20.5,No appeal

C30,Register>Classify>Compliance Check>Review>Request Info>Review>Close,High,Nova,Day,Normal,Yes,22.9,9.4,1,36.0,No appeal
"""

MIN_CELL = 3
COMPLEXITY_ORDER = ["Low", "Medium", "High"]


def hdr(t):
    print("\n" + "=" * 78 + f"\n{t}\n" + "=" * 78)


def support(label, n):
    if n == 0:
        print(f"[POSITIVITY] {label}: n=0 -> structural non-positivity; no estimate possible.")
    elif n < MIN_CELL:
        print(f"[SUPPORT]    {label}: n={n} (<{MIN_CELL}) -> unstable; report descriptively only.")


def main():
    df = pd.read_csv(io.StringIO(CSV))
    df["complexity"] = pd.Categorical(df["complexity"], COMPLEXITY_ORDER, ordered=True)
    df["appeal"] = (df["outcome_30d"].str.strip().str.lower() == "appeal").astype(int)
    df["compliance_flag"] = (df["compliance_required"] == "Yes").astype(int)
    df["has_compliance_activity"] = df["trace"].str.contains("Compliance Check").astype(int)
    df["has_request_info"] = df["trace"].str.contains("Request Info").astype(int)
    df["residual_h"] = (df.cycle_time_h - df.wait_before_review_h - df.review_service_h).round(3)

    # ---------------- 1. crude ----------------
    hdr("1. CRUDE (unadjusted) group comparison -- association only")
    crude = df.groupby("group").agg(
        cases=("case_id", "size"),
        mean_cycle=("cycle_time_h", "mean"),
        median_cycle=("cycle_time_h", "median"),
        mean_wait=("wait_before_review_h", "mean"),
        mean_service=("review_service_h", "mean"),
        high_pct=("complexity", lambda s: 100 * (s == "High").mean()),
        night_pct=("shift", lambda s: 100 * (s == "Night").mean()),
        highload_pct=("arrival_load", lambda s: 100 * (s == "High").mean()),
        compliance_pct=("compliance_flag", lambda s: 100 * s.mean()),
        rework_pct=("rework_count", lambda s: 100 * (s > 0).mean()),
        appeals=("appeal", "sum"),
    ).round(3)
    print(crude.to_string())
    crude_diff = crude.loc["Orion", "mean_cycle"] - crude.loc["Nova", "mean_cycle"]
    print(f"\nCrude Orion - Nova cycle time = {crude_diff:+.3f} h")
    for g in crude.index:
        support(f"appeal events, {g}", int(crude.loc[g, "appeals"]))

    # crude gap decomposition (auditable, additive)
    print("\nCrude gap decomposition (Orion - Nova), hours:")
    for col, name in [("wait_before_review_h", "queue+compliance wait"),
                      ("review_service_h", "review service")]:
        d = df.loc[df.group == "Orion", col].mean() - df.loc[df.group == "Nova", col].mean()
        print(f"  {name:24s} {d:+.3f}")
    for col, name in [("compliance_flag", "compliance presence"),
                      ("rework_count", "rework presence")]:
        d = df.loc[df.group == "Orion", col].mean() - df.loc[df.group == "Nova", col].mean()
        print(f"  {name:24s} {d:+.3f} (rate difference; overhead fitted below)")

    # additive measurement law: cycle ~ wait + service + a + b*compliance + c*rework
    X = np.column_stack([np.ones(len(df)), df.compliance_flag, (df.rework_count > 0).astype(int)])
    beta, *_ = np.linalg.lstsq(X, df.residual_h.values, rcond=None)
    pred = X @ beta
    print(f"\nResidual (cycle - wait - service) ~ {beta[0]:.3f} + {beta[1]:.3f}*compliance "
          f"+ {beta[2]:.3f}*rework ; max abs error = {np.abs(pred - df.residual_h).max():.3f} h")
    print("  -> cycle_time is an almost deterministic composite: no independent group signal.")
    print("  -> re-review adds ~0-0.3 h to review_service despite rework: check field semantics.")

    # ---------------- 2. stratified ----------------
    hdr("2. COMPLEXITY-STRATIFIED comparison (strata are NOT exchangeable sets)")
    strat = df.groupby(["complexity", "group"], observed=True).agg(
        cases=("case_id", "size"),
        mean_cycle=("cycle_time_h", "mean"),
        median_cycle=("cycle_time_h", "median"),
        mean_wait=("wait_before_review_h", "mean"),
        mean_service=("review_service_h", "mean"),
        compliance_pct=("compliance_flag", lambda s: 100 * s.mean()),
        rework_pct=("rework_count", lambda s: 100 * (s > 0).mean()),
        night_pct=("shift", lambda s: 100 * (s == "Night").mean()),
    ).round(3)
    print(strat.to_string())
    for c in COMPLEXITY_ORDER:
        sub = strat.loc[c]
        d = sub.loc["Orion", "mean_cycle"] - sub.loc["Nova", "mean_cycle"]
        dw = sub.loc["Orion", "mean_wait"] - sub.loc["Nova", "mean_wait"]
        ds = sub.loc["Orion", "mean_service"] - sub.loc["Nova", "mean_service"]
        print(f"  {c:6s}: Orion-Nova cycle {d:+.2f} h = wait {dw:+.2f} + service {ds:+.2f} + variant mix")
        for g in ["Orion", "Nova"]:
            support(f"{c} x {g}", int(sub.loc[g, "cases"]))

    # ---------------- 3. standardization ----------------
    hdr("3. COMPLEXITY-STANDARDIZED contrasts (explicit common weights)")
    m = df.groupby(["complexity", "group"], observed=True)["cycle_time_h"].mean().unstack("group")
    n = df.groupby(["complexity", "group"], observed=True).size().unstack("group").fillna(0)
    targets = {
        "POOLED (primary: all 30 cases)": df["complexity"].value_counts(normalize=True),
        "ORION mix (ATT-like)": n["Orion"] / n["Orion"].sum(),
        "NOVA mix (ATU-like)": n["Nova"] / n["Nova"].sum(),
    }
    for name, w in targets.items():
        w = w.reindex(COMPLEXITY_ORDER).fillna(0)
        ok = m.notna().all(axis=1)
        if not ok.all():
            print(f"[POSITIVITY] {name}: empty strata {list(m.index[~ok])} -> weights renormalized.")
        ww = w[ok] / w[ok].sum()
        o = float((m.loc[ok, "Orion"] * ww).sum())
        v = float((m.loc[ok, "Nova"] * ww).sum())
        print(f"\n{name}: weights = " + ", ".join(f"{k}:{ww[k]:.3f}" for k in ww.index))
        print(f"  standardized Orion = {o:.3f} h ; Nova = {v:.3f} h ; contrast = {o - v:+.3f} h")
    print("\nSign flips vs crude (+6.71 h) => Simpson reversal. NOT a causal estimate:")
    print("no exchangeability, unmeasured severity, selection on completion, empty shift cells.")

    # standardized component split under pooled weights
    w = df["complexity"].value_counts(normalize=True).reindex(COMPLEXITY_ORDER)
    print("\nPooled-standardized components (Orion vs Nova):")
    for col in ["wait_before_review_h", "review_service_h"]:
        mm = df.groupby(["complexity", "group"], observed=True)[col].mean().unstack("group")
        o, v = float((mm["Orion"] * w).sum()), float((mm["Nova"] * w).sum())
        print(f"  {col:24s} {o:6.3f} vs {v:6.3f} -> {o - v:+.3f} h")

    # ---------------- 4. overlap ----------------
    hdr("4. OVERLAP / POSITIVITY: complexity x shift x resource_group")
    keys = list(itertools.product(COMPLEXITY_ORDER, ["Day", "Night"], ["Orion", "Nova"]))
    idx = pd.MultiIndex.from_tuples(keys, names=["complexity", "shift", "group"])
    counts = df.groupby(["complexity", "shift", "group"], observed=True).size().reindex(idx, fill_value=0)
    print(counts.to_string())
    for k, v in counts.items():
        support(" x ".join(map(str, k)), int(v))
    print("\nExtended overlap (adds arrival_load) -- the proposed reassignment region:")
    keys2 = list(itertools.product(COMPLEXITY_ORDER, ["Day", "Night"], ["Orion", "Nova"], ["Normal", "High"]))
    idx2 = pd.MultiIndex.from_tuples(keys2, names=["complexity", "shift", "group", "arrival_load"])
    c2 = df.groupby(["complexity", "shift", "group", "arrival_load"], observed=True).size().reindex(idx2, fill_value=0)
    print(c2.to_string())
    print(f"Empty extended cells: {int((c2 == 0).sum())} / {len(c2)}")
    print("[WARN] Orion has NO Day Medium/High cases; Nova Night Medium/High are singletons.")
    print("       'Move 50% of Orion work to Nova' targets largely UNOBSERVED conditions.")

    # ---------------- 5. shift / compliance / rework / quality ----------------
    hdr("5. OTHER STRATIFIED VIEWS (shift, compliance, rework, quality)")
    for var in ["shift", "compliance_required"]:
        t = df.groupby([var, "group"], observed=True)["cycle_time_h"].agg(["size", "mean"]).round(2)
        print(f"\nBy {var}:\n{t.to_string()}")
        for lev in df[var].unique():
            s = df[df[var] == lev]
            print(f"  {var}={lev}: crude mean {s.cycle_time_h.mean():.2f} h; "
                  f"within-complexity means " +
                  str(s.groupby('complexity', observed=True)['cycle_time_h'].mean().round(2).to_dict()))
            for g in ["Orion", "Nova"]:
                support(f"{var}={lev} x {g}", int((s.group == g).sum()))
    print("\nCompliance within Medium (only stratum with overlap):")
    med = df[df.complexity == "Medium"]
    print(med.groupby(["group", "compliance_required"], observed=True)[
        ["cycle_time_h", "wait_before_review_h"]].agg(["size", "mean"]).round(3).to_string())
    print("Rework and appeals by stratum:")
    print(df.groupby(["complexity", "group"], observed=True).agg(
        n=("case_id", "size"), rework_cases=("rework_count", lambda s: int((s > 0).sum())),
        appeals=("appeal", "sum")).to_string())
    print("[WARN] 2 appeals total -> quality is a guardrail, never an endpoint for this decision.")

    # ---------------- 6. variants + conformance ----------------
    hdr("6. TRACE VARIANTS AND CONFORMANCE (structure only)")
    var = df.groupby(["trace", "group"], observed=True).size().unstack("group").fillna(0).astype(int)
    print(var.to_string())
    print("\nVariant share within group (%):")
    print((100 * var / var.sum(axis=0)).round(1).to_string())
    bad_c = df[df.compliance_flag != df.has_compliance_activity]
    bad_r = df[(df.rework_count > 0) != (df.has_request_info == 1)]
    print(f"\nConformance: compliance required-vs-present mismatches = {len(bad_c)}; "
          f"rework_count-vs-RequestInfo mismatches = {len(bad_r)}")
    if len(bad_c) or len(bad_r):
        print(pd.concat([bad_c, bad_r])[["case_id", "group", "trace", "compliance_required", "rework_count"]].to_string())
    print("Note: identical variant SETS across groups; only frequencies differ -> mix, not conformance.")

    # ---------------- 7. PM4Py DFGs ----------------
    hdr("7. PM4Py DIRECTLY-FOLLOWS GRAPHS per group (ORDER only, no timestamps used)")
    manual = {}
    for g, sub in df.groupby("group", observed=True):
        edges = {}
        for tr in sub["trace"]:
            acts = tr.split(">")
            for a, b in zip(acts[:-1], acts[1:]):
                edges[(a, b)] = edges.get((a, b), 0) + 1
        manual[g] = edges
    try:
        import pm4py
        for g, sub in df.groupby("group", observed=True):
            log_string = "\n".join(sub["trace"].tolist())
            try:
                log = pm4py.parse_event_log_string(log_string, sep=">")
            except TypeError:
                log = pm4py.parse_event_log_string(log_string)
            dfg, sa, ea = pm4py.discover_dfg(log)
            print(f"\n[{g}] n_cases={len(sub)}  PM4Py DFG (count, per-case rate):")
            for (a, b), c in sorted(dfg.items(), key=lambda kv: -kv[1]):
                print(f"   {a:18s} -> {b:18s} {c:3d}   {c/len(sub):.2f}")
            print(f"   start={dict(sa)} end={dict(ea)}")
            pm_edges = {k: int(v) for k, v in dfg.items()}
            if pm_edges != manual[g]:
                print("   [WARN] PM4Py edge counts differ from manual pandas recount; inspect parsing.")
            else:
                print("   [OK] matches manual pandas recount.")
    except Exception as e:  # pm4py absent / API drift -> transparent fallback
        print(f"[WARN] PM4Py unavailable or API mismatch ({type(e).__name__}: {e}).")
        print("       Falling back to a pandas directly-follows recount (structure only).")
        for g, edges in manual.items():
            n_g = int((df.group == g).sum())
            print(f"\n[{g}] n_cases={n_g}")
            for (a, b), c in sorted(edges.items(), key=lambda kv: -kv[1]):
                print(f"   {a:18s} -> {b:18s} {c:3d}   {c/n_g:.2f}")

    eo, en = set(manual.get("Orion", {})), set(manual.get("Nova", {}))
    print(f"\nEdges only in Orion: {sorted(eo - en)}")
    print(f"Edges only in Nova : {sorted(en - eo)}")
    for g, edges in manual.items():
        n_g = int((df.group == g).sum())
        for e, c in edges.items():
            support(f"DFG edge {g}: {e[0]}->{e[1]}", c)
        print(f"  {g}: Classify->Compliance Check rate = "
              f"{edges.get(('Classify','Compliance Check'),0)/n_g:.2f}; "
              f"Review->Request Info rate = {edges.get(('Review','Request Info'),0)/n_g:.2f}")

    hdr("INTERPRETATION LIMITS")
    print("All numbers above are DESCRIPTIVE. Standardization removes measured complexity mix only.")
    print("Unmeasured severity, shift/load collinearity, empty cells, and conditioning on completed")
    print("cases prevent any causal reading. Use the pilot + queue reconstruction in the plan.")
```

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Is the within-stratum Orion advantage real, or residual severity confounding inside three coarse complexity bands?** Most valuable addition: the **routing engine's continuous difficulty/severity score and its input features at Classify** (logged per case), enabling matching on the actual assignment variable rather than a proxy.
2. **Does Orion's advantage come from reviewer capability or from queue priority/preemption?** Most valuable addition: a per-case **queue-state record (queue position at Classify end, priority flag, and reviewers-on-shift per hour)**, so waiting can be modelled against staffing and priority instead of group label.
3. **How much of `wait_before_review_h` is post-Classify queueing versus compliance-adjacent waiting (and is the near-constant 1.4/1.5 h overhead real or imputed)?** Most valuable addition: **per-activity start/end timestamps** (especially Compliance Check start/end and each Review instance), which also lets `review_service_h` be verified against the re-review it claims to include.
4. **Is the completed-case sample selected differentially by group (abandonment / still-open long night cases)?** Most valuable design change: **extract all registered cases with status and censoring time**, i.e. an outcome-free registry denominator, enabling completion-hazard comparison and IPCW/Lee bounds.
5. **What happens to quality and to Nova's night capability if work actually moves — the region with zero/tiny support?** Most valuable design change: a **pre-registered, block-randomized routing pilot with linked, adjudicated quality outcomes (appeal reason and severity) and mandatory certification logging**, powered for cycle time and monitored sequentially for quality as a guardrail.
