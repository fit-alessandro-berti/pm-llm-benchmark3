**Explanation (250 words).** Shared mechanisms are structural and queueing-theoretic: replacing a sequence with an unordered pair turns the verification path from `Identity + Document` into `max(Identity, Document)` before the Assessment join, and WIP/queue discipline governs how much of that saving survives downstream. Those mechanisms are site-invariant *in form*. Site-specific mechanisms are the ones that determine whether the form is instantiable at B: `statutory_gate` (62% of cases cannot legally start Document Check), `originals_receipt_delay` (an exogenous, capacity-free wait that already dominates the critical path), `legal_review_required` with its supplement loop, `role_specialization` handoffs, and `IT_case_lock`, which blocks true simultaneity and corrupts `measured_overlap` through back-entry. Effect modifiers are `digital_documents`, `originals_required`, `case_complexity`, `role_specialization`, `IT_case_lock`, and the four capacity nodes — especially `assessment_capacity`, because parallelization changes not only the mean but the *burstiness* of arrivals at the join (`assessment_arrival_burstiness`), which can raise `waiting_before_assessment` and the tail even when verification shortens. `queue_huddles` and `API_latency` at A are co-interventions on the same outcome, not part of the treatment. A similar control-flow diagram therefore guarantees nothing: identical arcs can hide different enabling guards (data-dependent statutory ordering), different resource semantics (pools vs cross-trained reviewers), different measurement fidelity (`timestamp_quality`), and different mixes. Site A's before/after contrast additionally lacks any identification strategy — no control, no negative control, two-week universal rollout, capacity and mix shifts — so even the *magnitude* at A is unresolved, let alone its transport.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Name:** *Statutorily-gated partial-order verification with pull release to Assessment* (not "copy Site A").

**Eligibility rules (evaluated and logged at Register).**
- `E1` (pilot core): documents fully digital and authenticated at Register (`originals_required = false`) **and** `legal_required = false`.
- `E2` (stage 2): digital **and** `legal_required = true`.
- `E3` (stage 3, all originals cases): **identity-only head start**. Identity may run concurrently with the receipt wait (already permitted); Document Check is *not* created as claimable work.
- Excluded from all arms: cases with coupled identity/document data fields flagged by the dependency map (sequential fallback), and cases already in a supplement loop at entry.

**Concurrency.** For E1/E2, Register creates two *claimable, separately owned* tasks: `Identity Check` (identity pool) and `Document Check` (document pool). They are unordered. `Assessment` is released only when **both** complete (AND-join), via a pull queue, not push. For E3, Register creates `Identity Check` plus a system-owned `Await Originals` milestone; the receipt-scan event spawns `Document Check`.

**Statutory prerequisites that remain mandatory.** `Document Check.start  Originals Receipt.occurred` whenever originals are required (hard system gate, no override, breach = immediate stop); `Assessment.start  max(Identity.complete, Document.complete)`; `Legal Review` after Assessment for public-sector/high-value cases; decision after Legal where mandated; `Notify` after decision. Nothing in the pilot relaxes legislation.

**Queue and WIP controls.** Identity 5 open tasks/reviewer (4 reviewers  20); Document 4/reviewer (7  28); `Await Originals` lane excluded from reviewer WIP but aged with 48 h escalation; Assessment CONWIP release buffer 20 ready cases, levelled hourly; dispatch rule = oldest statutorily-ready first with complexity aging boost; 20% of document hours reserved for rework; treated cases capped at 30% of each pool's hours to limit interference. Daily huddles run in **both** arms (balanced background practice, not a co-intervention).

**Resource responsibilities.** Identity reviewers: identity only. Document reviewers: document only. Assessors: pull from ready buffer, own the join. Legal reviewers: post-assessment mandated review. A named case coordinator owns end-to-end status without holding an exclusive task lock. Optional separate arm: one cross-trained floater (tests Site A's role mechanism).

**Legal-review and supplement loops.** `Request Supplement` returns the case to the document pool tagged `rework`, with reserved capacity and preserved priority age. Identity results are reused if within a 30-day validity window and no identity-bearing field changed; otherwise identity is re-run. Loop counter cap = 2, then senior-assessor escalation. Identity is never re-run merely because documents were re-checked.

**Logging requirements.** Immutable server-side `claim/start/complete` events with **dual timestamps** (`occurred_at`, `recorded_at`) and actor IDs; multi-owner task ownership with lock-version audit; late entries permitted only with reason codes and excluded from per-protocol dose; eligibility flag, stratum, randomized arm, statutory gate evidence (receipt scan ID), queue enter/exit events, and active-touch time capture (to convert intervals into effort); no manual editing of overlap-relevant timestamps.

**Compact POWL-style / partial-order specification.**

```text
Activities: R=Register, I=Identity Check, O=Originals Receipt, D=Document Check,
            A=Assessment, L=Legal Review, S=Request Supplement, D'=Document Check(repeat),
            Ap=Approve, Rj=Reject, N=Notify, =skip

Guard g_orig  {true,false}   (originals legally required)
Guard g_leg   {true,false}   (public-sector or high-value)

GATE  = X(, O)                       # originals required  O; else silent skip
LEGAL = X(, L)                       # mandated  L; else skip
BODY  = ->(A, LEGAL)
REDO  = ->(S, D')
LOOP  = *(BODY, REDO)                 # body first, then optional redo+body
DEC   = X(Ap, Rj)

ROOT  = StrictPartialOrder{ R, I, GATE, D, LOOP, DEC, N } with order edges:
  R  I
  R  GATE
  GATE  D
  I  LOOP        # AND-join leg 1 (LOOP's entry node is A)
  D  LOOP        # AND-join leg 2
  LOOP  DEC
  DEC  N
Unordered (concurrent) pairs: {I, GATE}, {I, D} when GATE selects  (digital).
Forbidden edges: I  D, D  I, and any path enabling D before O when g_orig = true.
E3 variant: GATE fixed to O; I  O remains; D still gated by O.
```

**Separating mechanism failure from implementation failure (210 words).** The design creates three observable links in the chain *treatment  true overlap  verification-phase time  cycle time*, and each has a dedicated contrast. If `T1` (task creation under the legacy lock) shows logged overlap rising but server-timestamp overlap and phase time flat, the binding constraint is **implementation** (the case lock and back-entry), not the mechanism; the remedy is the multi-owner patch, and `T2` tests it. If `T2` achieves true overlap >60% and phase time falls 2.5–3.4 h but cycle time does not move, the mechanism works locally and fails to transport to the outcome: the gain is absorbed by the Assessment join or the legal/supplement loop — a **downstream mechanism failure** diagnosed by queue enter/exit decomposition and arrival CV². If phase time does not fall even at high true overlap, the theorized critical-path model is wrong (identity was already off the critical path, or data coupling forces re-verification) — a **core mechanism failure**. The E3 placebo stratum should show 0 effect; a non-zero placebo estimate, or a change in identity service time or `Notifypayment`, indicates confounding or measurement drift rather than any mechanism. Dose–response across arms plus occurred-vs-recorded lag distributions distinguishes genuine concurrency from timestamp artifact, which is precisely the ambiguity Site A left unresolved.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
"""

Tested against the PM4Py 2.7.x public API; defensive fallbacks are used because
some helper names differ across minor versions.

  * The POWL model encodes CONTROL FLOW ONLY. The guards g_orig (originals legally
    required, 62%) and g_leg (public-sector/high-value, 40%) are represented as
    NON-DETERMINISTIC XOR choices, not data-dependent predicates. Soundness therefore
    means "for every resolution of the guards", not "the legally correct branch is taken".
  * Resource pools (4 identity / 7 document / 3 assessors / 2 legal), utilization,
    WIP caps, CONWIP release, priority/aging and the legacy single-owner CASE LOCK
    are NOT expressible in a Petri net/POWL; they need a resource-aware simulation
    or a queueing model layered on top.
  * Durations (identity 3.4h, document 8.1h, originals receipt median 18h with a heavy
    tail) and costs are absent: the net cannot reproduce median/p90 cycle time.
  * 'Originals Receipt' is an exogenous arrival, not reviewer work; here it is a
    transition, which wrongly implies it is schedulable unless the simulator marks it
    as an uncontrollable timer.
  * Duplicate labels ('Document Check' in the main path and in the supplement loop)
    are distinct nodes; this is fine for playout but complicates alignment/conformance.
"""

import itertools
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition,
)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 1) SITE A trees
A_BEFORE = ("->('Register','Identity Check','Document Check','Assessment',"
            "X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')")

            "X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')")

tree_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(A_BEFORE)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 2) trees -> nets

# ------------------------------------------------- 3) SITE B candidate POWL model
def build_site_b_powl():
    R  = Transition(label='Register')
    I  = Transition(label='Identity Check')
    O  = Transition(label='Originals Receipt')      # exogenous arrival (timer)
    D  = Transition(label='Document Check')
    A  = Transition(label='Assessment')
    L  = Transition(label='Legal Review')
    S  = Transition(label='Request Supplement')
    D2 = Transition(label='Document Check')         # repeat after supplement
    AP = Transition(label='Approve')
    RJ = Transition(label='Reject')
    N  = Transition(label='Notify')
    skip_o = SilentTransition()                     # digital: no originals needed
    skip_l = SilentTransition()                     # legal review not mandated

    gate  = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[skip_o, O])   # g_orig
    legal = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[skip_l, L])   # g_leg

    body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[A, legal])     # Assessment then (optional) Legal
    body.order.add_edge(A, legal)

    redo = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[S, D2])        # supplement -> re-check documents
    redo.order.add_edge(S, D2)

    loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[body, redo])  # body first
    dec  = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[AP, RJ])

    root = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[R, I, gate, D, loop, dec, N])
    root.order.add_edge(R, I)
    root.order.add_edge(R, gate)
    root.order.add_edge(gate, D)      # STATUTORY GATE: D after O when originals required
    # ---- 4) EXPLICIT AND-JOIN: Assessment (entry node of `loop`) waits for BOTH ----
    root.order.add_edge(I, loop)      # join leg 1: identity complete
    root.order.add_edge(D, loop)      # join leg 2: document complete
    # --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    root.order.add_edge(loop, dec)
    root.order.add_edge(dec, N)

    edges = [(R, I), (R, gate), (gate, D), (I, loop), (D, loop), (loop, dec), (dec, N)]
    return root, dict(R=R, I=I, gate=gate, D=D, loop=loop, dec=dec, N=N), edges


powl_b, nodes_b, edges_b = build_site_b_powl()

# Structural assertions on the declared partial order (model-fidelity checks that
# do not depend on the converter): I and D must be UNORDERED (concurrent), and both
# must precede the Assessment block.
def closure(edges):
    reach = {}
    for a, b in edges:
        reach.setdefault(a, set()).add(b)
    changed = True
    while changed:
        changed = False
        for a in list(reach):
            for b in list(reach[a]):
                for c in reach.get(b, ()):
                    if c not in reach[a]:
                        reach[a].add(c); changed = True
    return reach

reach = closure(edges_b)
I, D, gate, loop = nodes_b['I'], nodes_b['D'], nodes_b['gate'], nodes_b['loop']
assert D not in reach.get(I, set()) and I not in reach.get(D, set()), "I and D must be concurrent"
assert loop in reach[I] and loop in reach[D], "Assessment must wait for BOTH checks"
assert D in reach[gate], "Document Check must be gated by the originals choice"
assert I not in reach.get(gate, set()), "Identity must not wait for originals"
print("Partial-order invariants OK: {Identity, Document} unordered; AND-join at Assessment.")

def wf_and_soundness(net, im, fm, name):
    is_wf = None
    fn = getattr(pm4py, "check_is_workflow_net", None)
    if callable(fn):
        try:
            is_wf = bool(fn(net, im, fm))
        except TypeError:
            is_wf = bool(fn(net))
    if is_wf is None:                      # manual structural fallback
        try:
            from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils.check_soundness import check_is_workflow_net
            is_wf = bool(check_is_workflow_net(net))
        except Exception:
            src = [p for p in net.places if not p.in_arcs]
            snk = [p for p in net.places if not p.out_arcs]
            nodes = set(net.places) | set(net.transitions)
            fwd = {n: set() for n in nodes}
            for a in net.arcs:
                fwd[a.source].add(a.target)
            seen, stack = set(), list(src)
            while stack:
                x = stack.pop()
                if x in seen: continue
                seen.add(x); stack.extend(fwd[x] - seen)
            is_wf = (len(src) == 1 and len(snk) == 1 and nodes <= seen
                     and len(im) == 1 and len(fm) == 1)

    sound = None
    fn = getattr(pm4py, "check_soundness", None)
    if callable(fn):
        try:
            sound = fn(net, im, fm)
        except Exception:
            sound = None
    if sound is None:
        try:
            from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils.check_soundness import check_soundness_shortcut
            sound = check_soundness_shortcut(net, im, fm)
        except Exception:
            sound = "unavailable in this PM4Py version"
    print(f"{name}: places={len(net.places)} transitions={len(net.transitions)} "
          f"arcs={len(net.arcs)} | workflow_net={is_wf} | soundness={sound}")
    return is_wf, sound
```

## 9. Recommendation

**`conditional go`**

Transfer the *structural principle*, not Site A's rule or its headline. What travels is the replacement of an unnecessary sequential chain by a partial order whose AND-join sits at Assessment, plus Site A's enabling disciplines: WIP caps, pull release, clean multi-owner event logging. What must not be copied is "create Identity and Document tasks simultaneously for every case": for 62% of Site B's volume Document Check is legally unstartable, and identity (3.4 h) already sits off the critical path behind receipt (18 h) plus document (8.1 h) — the structural gain there is 0. Also do not import the 9-hour median claim: at A only 3.1–3.5 h is arithmetically attributable to concurrency; the rest is the API upgrade, two added reviewers (+11% capacity), a marketing-driven mix shift, huddles, and possibly better timestamp recording. A's rework (+0.6 pp) and appeal (+0.3 pp) movements are each within one standard error — inconclusive, not reassuring.

Enter first with **E1: digital-at-registration, non-public-sector/non-high-value cases (~23% of volume, ~775 per six months)**, under a multi-owner IT patch with server-side occurred/recorded timestamps, per-reviewer WIP caps, and a CONWIP release buffer protecting the three assessors (0.8, the tightest pool). Originals cases serve as a placebo stratum receiving only an identity head start.

Expected benefit: treated verification-phase median 2.5 to 3.4 h; because eligibility is below 50%, the all-case median moves only 1 h and p90 essentially not at all. Risks: bottleneck shift (mean 1 h with p90 +8–18 h), phantom WIP and priority inversion under a naive rollout, rework inflation from 17%, and false-positive "success" from back-entered overlap.

**Expand** to E2 (digital  legal) if, over 12 weeks with 150 cases/arm: true server-timestamp overlap 60%, phase-time reduction 2 h, all-case p90 non-inferior (+5%), assessment wait +1 h, rework +2 pp, zero statutory breaches. **Modify** (add assessor capacity, tighten release levelling, restrict eligibility) if overlap is high but cycle time is flat or the tail degrades. **Abandon** the parallelization arm if true overlap cannot exceed ~20% without lock workarounds, if p90 worsens >15%, if the rework/appeal harm posterior exceeds 0.9, or on any illegal Document start.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **`Originals Receipt  Document Check` = 2,108 = 0.62 × 3,400 identifies the originals-required cohort, leaving ~1,292 digital cases — why needed: it fixes the eligible share and hence the ceiling on any all-case effect — how to test: case-level join of statutory flag to receipt events, plus Register-time document-channel audit — consequence if false: eligibility, power, and the mixture-median calculation (1 h) are all wrong; re-derive before piloting.**
2. **Reported "service h" are task interval durations, not reviewer effort (A's 5,200 × 9.6 h  20 FTE capacity) — why needed: prevents absurd utilization estimates and separates waiting from touch time — how to test: time-and-motion / active-window sampling of 100 tasks per pool — consequence if false: the  estimates (47/65/79/74%) and the bottleneck-shift scenario S3 are mis-specified; capacity conclusions must be rebuilt.**
3. **900 productive hours per FTE per six months, with touch times 0.5/1.1/0.65/0.9 h for identity/document/assessment/legal — why needed: converts volumes into utilization for scenarios — how to test: payroll/roster productive-hour reconciliation plus assumption 2's sampling — consequence if false: assessment may not be the bottleneck; the binding pool (possibly legal, 2 FTE) changes the mitigation and the expected p90 range.**
4. **Originals requirement and legal-review requirement are both determinable at Register — why needed: eligibility routing and stratified randomization depend on knowing guards before task creation — how to test: retrospective coding of 300 cases against Register-time fields; measure misclassification — consequence if false: late re-routing contaminates arms, creates illegal task creation risk, and biases ITT toward null.**
5. **Digital status is approximately independent of legal-review mandate (digital  non-legal  60% of digital  775 cases) — why needed: sets E1 sample size and pilot duration — how to test: cross-tab of channel × mandate on the 3,400 cases — consequence if false: E1 may be far smaller (e.g., digital claims are mostly low-value), extending the pilot beyond 12 weeks or forcing E2 entry first.**
6. **Log cycle time is approximately normal with   0.72 (from p90/median = 110/44 = 2.5), stable across strata — why needed: power/MDE arithmetic (304/arm for 15%, 161/arm for 20%) and quantile-shift math — how to test: fit distributions per stratum on baseline logs; check tail index and mixture structure — consequence if false: heavier or multimodal tails invalidate the MDE; switch to quantile/bootstrap or rank-based endpoints and re-size.**
7. **Identity results are independent of document content and remain valid across supplement loops (30-day window) — why needed: concurrency is only safe if checks use consistent, non-coupled data, and loops must not force identity re-runs — how to test: field-level dependency map plus review of 100 rework cases for identity-invalidating supplements — consequence if false: concurrency raises inconsistency-driven rework and appeals; coupled-field cases must fall back to sequential, shrinking eligibility.**
8. **A multi-owner case-lock patch with immutable server-side occurred/recorded timestamps is deliverable for the pilot, and treated workload can be ring-fenced at 30% of pool hours — why needed: without it, true overlap is unattainable and dose is unmeasurable (Site A's exact ambiguity), and shared pools violate SUTVA — how to test: IT feasibility spike in a sandbox; audit lock-version and timestamp lag; monitor pool-hour quotas weekly — consequence if false: only T1 is runnable, the mechanism is untestable, and the recommendation degrades to `no-go` for parallelization pending IT remediation.**
