## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **QA rework path — the Deviation Investigation (opened? and if so, documentationonly vs laboratory?)** — *mainly **path uncertainty***. Marginal P(opened)  0.13–0.30 and the doconly/laboratory split (60–80 % / 20–40 %) is the difference between +8–20 QA working hours and +3–7 **calendar** days. This single choice moves the forecast by 1–7 days and constitutes almost the entire P80+ mass.
2. **Temperatureexcursion confirmation (the sensor gap)** — *mainly **dependence uncertainty**, with **observation uncertainty*** on the flag itself. It is a shared cause: it inflates P(rerun) 0.15~0.35, P(contamination) 0.085~0.22 and P(investigation) 0.07~0.42, so rerun/contamination/investigation must **not** be multiplied as independent rates. Roughly two thirds of the total investigation probability originates from this one unknown.
3. **Chemistry queue discipline (FIFO vs riskpriority) and backlog depth** — *mainly **queue uncertainty***. It decides whether B417 is the 7th job (result Mon 14:35) or the 2nd (result Mon 08:55), i.e. ±4–6 h on the join — and, because the QA review then straddles Mon 20:00 or not, effectively ±12–24 h on the terminal time. A Tuesday spill of the chemistry backlog (allmax services) is a further +24 h tail.
4. **Microbiology contamination  confirmation incubation (20–30 h + second readout)** — *mainly **path uncertainty***. The largest single branch clock in the tail; it is also the mechanism by which the microbiology branch dominates the join by 1–2 days and by which the investigation probability jumps.
5. **QA queue depth at the join + QA review duration tail + the QA calendar cliffs** — *mainly **queue uncertainty** and **servicetime uncertainty**, interacting with **calendar uncertainty***. Waiting 2–8 h (8–14 h in an overdispersed burst), a lognormal review with p95  7.9 h, and the 20:00/ Sunday closures turn small differences into 12–43 h pauses.

*Close sixth:* the **weekend microbiologist / oncall authorization** — *mainly **calendar uncertainty*** (with **observation uncertainty** on the authorization decision). It is worth 12–20 h but only binds in the ~10–20 % of cases where chemistry is fast; it is, however, the reason the *floor* of the forecast is Monday rather than Sunday.
