# 1. Assumptions and notation

**Notation.** All times on 20260512; minutes are the unit. `W0 = 08:00`, `W1 = 14:00`, window length `T = 360`. For case *i* and station *k*  {S1,S2}: `a_ik` = queueentry time, `s_ik` = service start, `e_ik` = service end, `q_ik = s_ik  a_ik` (queue wait), `v_ik = e_ik  s_ik` (active service), `soj_ik = e_ik  a_ik = q_ik + v_ik` (station sojourn). `S_ik` denotes the *station* interval, `R_ik` the resource. "c" = central estimate, `[lo–hi]` = bound, `x` = rightcensored lower bound.

- **A1 (clock/window).** The log is complete for the window 08:00–14:00; the process is observed from an empty system (no case is in service at 08:00, P01 arrives exactly at 08:00). Needed to close WIP balances.
- **A2 (rounding model).** Each reported timestamp *t* stands for a true time in *[t2.5, t+2.5]*, modelled as independent Uniform(2.5,+2.5) jitter. A duration built from two rounded endpoints therefore has error in *[5,+5]* with a triangularish density (sd  2.0 min); a sum of *n* durations has hard error ±5n but sd  2.04·n. Both a hard bound and a probabilistic band are reported. The external arrival feed is rounded identically but independently, so arrivalstart differences carry the same ±5 error.
- **A3 (eventorder convention).** Intervals are halfopen `[start, end)`. At identical reported timestamps I apply (1) activity ends / system departures, (2) activity starts / queue exits, (3) external arrivals. WIP and queue lengths are reported as the rightcontinuous value **just after all events at t** ("t+"). Consequence: a zerogap handover (P07 end 11:00 = P09 start 11:00 on C1) is **not** a resource conflict. Instantaneous peaks are conventionsensitive by ±1 case; timeintegrals (means, areas) are **not** sensitive at all, because ties have measure zero.
- **A4 (station entry).** A case enters the S2 queue at the instant S1 completes (stated in the prompt); no transport buffer is modelled. Therefore `a_i2 = e_i1` and S2 "waiting" absorbs any transfer/setup latency. Observed minimum S2 wait is 5 min and minimum S1 wait is 5 min *even when both resources were idle* (P01, P02), so a fixed 5min intake/handover latency is plausible (see A12).
- **A5 (capacity).** I1, I2, C1, C2 are singletask; no preemption, no multitasking, no rework, no station skipping. Apparent simultaneity 5 min is treated as a rounding artifact, not as proof of undocumented assistance (the latter remains a hypothesis).
- **A6 (queue discipline).** FCFS by queue entry is the *baseline* used only to narrow imputation brackets, not as an asserted fact. The data violate it at least once (P09, ready 10:35, starts 11:00 on C1 while P08, ready 10:20, waits for C2 until 11:15). Where the FCFS bracket is empty/inverted it is discarded (this happens for P11).
- **A7 (MISSING).** Start unknown but the activity happened, resource and end are known. The start is bracketed by `max(previous end on the same resource, queue entry)  s  e`. Service is **never** replaced by a single silent value.
- **A8 (OPEN).** Start known, end unknown, `e > W1`. Elapsed service `W1  s` is a **lower bound** on final service, not an estimate of it. A soft upper bound uses the station's empirical service maximum (S1 30, S2 50); this is a prior, not a bound.
- **A9 (blank).** Blank start+end with completed upstream = still waiting at W1  wait is rightcensored (` W1  a`), service not started (0 elapsed, final service unknown). Blank S1 with an arrival = waiting for S1.
- **A10 (arrival completeness).** No arrivals occurred after 12:10. **This is an assumption, not an observation**: an arrival feed truncated at 12:10 would look identical. Sensitivity is discussed in §6; it is the single assumption most able to change the "S1 drains" narrative.
- **A11 (maintenance).** C2 is unavailable 10:55–11:10 exactly (±2.5 under A2). Availabilityadjusted denominators subtract 15 min from C2 (and from S2's 720 resourceminutes  705). The data respect the outage with a 5min margin on both sides (P06 ends 10:50, P08 starts 11:15), so no violation even under rounding.
- **A12 (5min gaps).** The nearuniversal 5min intercase gap on every resource is interpreted as handover/setup **and/or** quantization of a smaller real gap. Utilization is therefore reported on three denominators (full window, availabilityadjusted, saturatedcore span) rather than one.
- **A13 (empirical service priors).** Fully observed services: S1 n=18, min 20, median 30, max 30, mean 28.06; S2 n=12, min 25, median 40, max 50, mean 40.0. These are used as *soft* floors/ceilings to tighten imputation brackets. Needed because the pure logical bounds (service  0) are operationally meaningless.
- **A14 (imputation scenarios).** Three deterministic scenarios plus a MonteCarlo ensemble (§6): `late_min` (start as late as the empirical floor allows  shortest service, most idle), `central` (midpoint of resource  empirical  FCFS bracket), `early_max` (workconserving: start as soon as the resource is free  longest service, least idle).
- **A15 (censoring mechanism).** For OPEN cases I assume the eventual service duration is drawn from the same distribution as observed services at that station (censoring independent of duration). This is needed for any forward projection and is explicitly flagged as fragile (n=3 open cases).
- **A16 (routing).** No eligibility matrix is supplied. An observed early pattern (even casesC2, oddC1, breaking at P10/P12) is treated as a *hypothesis* (family/fixture affinity), used only to bound the outage's causal effect.
- **A17 (invariance properties used, not assumed).** Because every started activity has a known end (observed or censored at W1) and every entry time is known, (i) station WIP trajectories, (ii) station sojourns, and (iii) completedcase total sojourns are **invariant to start imputation**. Only the wait/service *split*, idle attribution and utilization move with imputation. This is the backbone of the uncertainty strategy.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

**Percase quantities.** `q = s  a`, `v = e  s`, `soj = e  a`. For fully observed pairs these are point estimates ±5 (A2). For MISSING starts I propagate bounds:
`s  [s_lo, s_hi]` with `s_lo = max(prev end on R, a)`, `s_hi = e`  `v  [e  s_hi, e  s_lo]`, `q  [s_lo  a, s_hi  a]`, with `q + v = soj` fixed. Rounding widens each hard bound by 2.5–5 min. Refinement: intersect with `[e  max_obs_service, e  min_obs_service]` (A13) and, if nonempty and ordered, with the FCFS neighbour bracket `[a + q_prev, a + q_next]` (A6). Central = bracket midpoint. For OPEN: `v  W1  s`, `q` observed. For blank: `q  W1  a`, `v` undefined.

**Total sojourn.** Completed cases: `S_i = e_i2  arrival_i` (imputationfree). Open cases: `S_i  W1  arrival_i` (censored). I never average completed and censored cases into one "mean sojourn".

**WIP / queue.** Station WIP is built from *entry/exit* events only: `+1` at `a_ik`, `1` at `e_ik` when observed (OPEN and blank contribute no exit). `WIP_k(t)` is the rightcontinuous step function under A3; `WIP_total = WIP_S1 + WIP_S2`. Inservice count `B_k(t)` is built from `[s, e_eff)` intervals (`e_eff = W1` for OPEN) under a chosen scenario. Queue length `Q_k(t) = WIP_k(t)
