The June collapse is overdetermined. The UI release replaced the label `Classify` with `Risk Screen` without updating conformance mappings, so every otherwise compliant case incurred a synchronous move mismatch (log `Risk Screen` vs model `Classify`). Simultaneously the gateway began emitting `Queue Routed`, a technical routing event never in the normative net, creating an insertion on the log, and the ETL switch to warehouse-ingestion time inverted the order of `Decision` and `Notify` in ~10% of cases, producing artificial swaps that appear as a model move `Decision` plus log move `Notify` and the DFG cycle Standard Review->Notify->Decision. Each defect targets a different alignment move type, so their staged repair recovers fitness additively 32%58%81%89%. That recovery pattern is diagnostic of logging/provenance, not behavior: true process change would not be repaired by label mapping or reordering.

In August a distinct mechanism dominates: the policy adds `Supervisor Sign-off` for high-value claims but the normative tree still encodes Decision directly after review/check. The control-flow-only model therefore flags compliant sign-off cases as deviations, dropping normalized fitness to 66% until the model is extended. Because the model is data-unaware it cannot distinguish required vs optional sign-off, so an optional-sign-off candidate improves fitness (86%) by overgeneralizing.

Unchanged approval rates, appeal rates and cycle times do not settle the debate. They are insensitive to logging inserts and order swaps, so stability corroborates the artifact hypothesis. However they are also insensitive to undetected compliance bypass: compliance itself warns incident detection is incomplete, appeals lag, and 55 Enhanced Review cases without checks remain unaudited. Rate stability thus neither proves pure data error nor rules out a residual, concentrated noncompliance risk requiring targeted audit, especially around `Manual Override` and missing `Compliance Check` events.

## 7. Provisional conclusion

The primary explanation for the measured conformance collapse is artifactual, not behavioral. In June-July the staged pipeline isolates three independent logging/provenance changes that jointly account for almost all lost fitness: the `Classify``Risk Screen` label rename (32%58% fitting), the intrusive technical event `Queue Routed` (58%81%), and the ETL ordering defect using warehouse ingestion time for `Decision`/`Notify` (81%89%, DFG reversal and 11/11 screenshot contradictions). Performance and volume stability (median cycle 26.026.5h, approval ~72%, appeal ~3.7%) and the audit confirming semantic identity further corroborate that the real process likely remained largely unchanged. I assign ~88-94% confidence that logging/provenance defects are the dominant cause of the June metric swing. The remainder of the August-September drop after full normalization (66% fitting) is explained by normative model obsolescence: the 2026-08-01 policy requires `Supervisor Sign-off` for claims >€50k, which the control-flow-only tree does not encode. Allowing optional sign-off restores fitness to 86%, confirming obsolescence with ~80% confidence. Neither mechanism implies widespread employee misconduct as a driver of the dashboard.

A separate, lower-confidence but material compliance concern remains. After all cleaning, 55 cases (25 Jun-Jul +30 Aug-Sep) show Enhanced ReviewDecision without a recorded `Compliance Check`, and 55-60 cases use `Manual Override`. No reliable audit has determined whether these are true bypasses or incomplete logging, and incident detection is acknowledged as incomplete. Rate stability therefore provides only weak reassurance. With ~40% plausibility of concentrated noncompliance, this cannot be dismissed and must not be averaged away by aggregate fitness. The supervisor memo and `Override` routing corrections may be benign workarounds or may hide bypasses; reviewer-level clustering is unknown.

Immediate actions: IT must restore label synonym mapping (`Risk Screen`= `Classify`), filter or mark `Queue Routed` as technical, and revert ordering to the source-system sequence number (not ingestion time), with regression validation. Process owners must version the normative model to include a data-aware sign-off rule (>€50k mandatory) rather than a permissive optional task. Compliance should prioritize a targeted audit of the 55 missing-check cases and `Manual Override` cases, stratified by high-value attribute and reviewer, before any claims about employee noncompliance. What must not be claimed yet: that employees systematically bypass checks, that the optional sign-off model proves compliance, or that stable appeal rates prove absence of risk.

## 8. Model-governance recommendation

1. **Version the normative model with policy effective dates.** No policy or UI change deploys without a corresponding model version, change log, and fitness/precision regression on a normalized reference log; keep control-flow plus data-aware constraints.
2. **Enforce event-semantic contracts.** Renames, splits, or new activities require a registered synonym/ontology mapping (`Risk Screen` = `Classify` v4.2) approved by process owner and consumed by conformance jobs; unmapped labels must fail the pipeline, not silently score as deviations.
3. **Govern technical events explicitly.** Every event type declared as business vs technical/hidden; technical events (`Queue Routed`) default to filtered or silent transitions, with provenance, retention policy, and explicit inclusion criteria before they affect alignments.
4. **Preserve timestamp provenance and ordering authority.** Store both event time, ingestion time, and monotonic source sequence number; conformance ordering uses the source sequence as authority, with automated checks for DecisionNotify inversions and ingestion drift alerts.
5. **Make data-aware constraints first-class.** Encode attribute rules (e.g., amount > €50k  `Supervisor Sign-off` mandatory; authorizations) as DMN/declare constraints separate from control-flow, so optional control-flow does not overgeneralize and compliance checks remain verifiable.
6. **Mandate post-release conformance validation with attribute stratification.** Any UI, ETL, gateway, or model release triggers automated pre/post DFG diff, staged alignment repair (label  technical  order  model), high-value vs low-value stratification, and sampled provenance audit before dashboards are published or personnel conclusions drawn.
