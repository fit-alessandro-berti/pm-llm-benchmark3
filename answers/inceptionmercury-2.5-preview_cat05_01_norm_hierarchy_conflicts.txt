

## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

**Decision Method:**
I treat the Global Procurement and Integrity Policy (GPIP-4) as the baseline control framework. Local SOPs must not silently weaken it. Incident directives (ECD-2) override local SOPs but do not automatically waive Global Policy requirements unless explicitly authorized. The Chief Risk Officer (CRO) is the designated authority for GPIP exceptions under clause A7. The CFO has authority over Finance controls but explicitly cannot waive Compliance or Segregation of Duties (SoD) per the document facts. I prioritize evidence of action at the time of commitment over retrospective timestamps. Late approvals (A6) do not cure pre-commitment violations.

**Assumptions:**
*   The "emergency" definition in SOP B4 applies only if a formal emergency declaration occurred prior to the act; ECD-2 serves as this declaration for ECD-2 acts.
*   ECD-2's scope ("critical sterilization-line lots") must be interpreted restrictively based on production impact.
*   CFO Email D1 (IC auth sufficient) applies to Finance approvals where the CFO has authority, but does not override GPIP-4 A1/A3 or local SOP requirements not delegated to the CFO.
*   ERP delays during the outage (12 min max) are accounted for when comparing commitment times to policy windows.

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

**P-503 (140k):** *Alternative:* ECD-2 overrides GPIP-4 SoD (A1) during the incident.
*Premise:* If "ordinary purchasing steps" in ECD-2 C4 includes SoD, then IC authorization replaces Finance approval and SoD. *Weakness:* GPIP-4 A7 reserves exceptions for CRO, not IC. CFO Email D3 explicitly denies authority to waive SoD.

**P-505 (88k):** *Alternative:* Commit occurred *before* ECD-2 expiry (18:00).
*Premise:* ERP timestamp 18:06 is delayed; actual commit was 17:57. *Weakness:* Finance approval was still post-commit (19:00). A6 states post-hoc approval does not cure missing pre-commit approval regardless of timing relative to ECD-2 expiry.

**P-507 (110k):** *Alternative:* CFO Email D1 (IC auth sufficient up to 150k) waives R2 (Finance pre-commit).
*Premise:* CFO interprets Finance controls (D3) and D1 explicitly authorizes IC to approve operational purchases. *Weakness:* D3 says not meant to remove essential controls. GPIP A2 requires specific actors (Finance Controller), not just "operational approval."

**P-504 (24k):** *Alternative:* Cafeteria freezer critical for staff continuity (ECD-2 scope).
*Premise:* "Critical sterilization-line lots" could be read as any line critical to facility operation. *Weakness:* Audit interpretation restricts scope to production impact. "Sterilization-line" implies specific equipment, not general facilities.

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency and Remediation

**Evidential Sufficiency:**
*   **Policy Breach:** Cases P-503 (SoD) and P-501 (Timing/SOP) show clear breaches.
*   **Logging Defect:** Cases P-501, P-506 show ERP timestamps that lag reality but confirm timing sequence (commit before approve).
*   **Late Evidence:** P-502 and P-507 have compliance clearance or IC auth but timing relative to commitment is critical. The ERP outage requires verifying audio/PDF records for exact times.

**Remediation:**
1.  **Immediate:** Halt non-emergency purchases at Aachen until explicit GPIP exceptions (A7) are issued.
2.  **Case-Level:**
    *   P-502, P-507: Review if new suppliers were critical (P-502) and document risk justification for lack of pre-commit compliance.
    *   P-503: Initiate independent audit on Segregation of Duties (SoD) violations.
3.  **Monitoring:** Implement a "Commitment Lock" in ERP that prevents financial approval processing if GPIP pre-commit controls are unmet.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Replacement Clause for ECD-2 Exceptions (A7):**

> "During a declared incident, Incident Command may authorize exceptions to * timing (SOP B1, GPIP B4) and supplier-onboarding (SOP B3) *only* for the explicitly defined scope. Any exception to GPIP-4 A1 (SoD), A2 (Finance Pre-commit), or A3 (Compliance Pre-commit) requires a written waiver signed by the Chief Risk Officer (or Acting CFO acting as proxy if CRO is unavailable) **before** commitment.
>
> Post-commit approval for GPIP controls is permitted only where:
> 1.  Financial Controller and Business Owner sign a Risk Acceptance Form within 48 hours.
> 2.  For GPIP A1 violations, the approver must be a separate executive not involved in the purchase request.
> 3.  For GPIP A3 violations, Compliance Clearance is performed immediately post-commit with a documented impact review.
>
> All waivers expire 24 hours after incident termination unless renewed. Incident directives do not constitute written exceptions under A7 unless signed by the CRO."
