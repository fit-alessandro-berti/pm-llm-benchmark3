## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

Elapsed hours below are from **Friday 18 Sep 2026, 15:30**. Weekend structure: chemistry and routine micro staff off after Friday 18:00; QA Saturday 09:00–13:00 only; automated incubation continues; Monday is **21 September**.

Shortcut used throughout: analyst-hour / reviewer-hour balances and triangular means, not a stochastic simulation. Parallelism is always a **maximum of branch ready-times**, never a sum.

### Shared prefix to the parallel join

**Chemistry waiting and first service**

- Now–18:00: 2.5 h × 2 analysts = 5 analyst-h. Current WIP 1.6–2.9 analyst-h. About 2.1–3.4 analyst-h reach the waiting line Friday.
- **Risk-priority (~45%)**: first free analyst (~15:54–16:24) takes the urgent job; second (~16:42–17:30) starts B-417, works 0.5–1.3 h, pauses at 18:00. Remaining triangular service resumes Monday 08:00. First-run chemistry typically **Monday 08:15–12:00** (mean remaining ~1.8 h after ~1 h Friday work).
- **FIFO (~55%)**: four jobs ahead, mean 11.3 analyst-h, ~2.7 h burned Friday, ~8.6 h left Monday. Two analysts imply B-417 *start* ~Monday 11:30–13:30. First-run finish **Monday 13:30–17:00** centrally; a long queued set plus a 4.5 h test started after ~15:30 **pauses at 18:00 and finishes Tuesday 08:00–11:30**.
- Mixture without rerun: chemistry token typically ready **Monday 10:00–15:30**.

**Microbiology incubation and readout**

- Technically ready 46–54 h after Friday 14:00 → **Sunday 20 Sep 12:00–20:00**.
- No authorization (0.55–0.75): readout Monday 08:00 + 0.5–1.5 h → **Monday 08:30–09:30**.
- Authorization (0.25–0.45): dispatch + readout 1–3 h after readiness → **Sunday 13:00–23:00**. This clears micro *before* chemistry, so it does not set the join.

**Parallel join (maximum, not sum)**

- Clean micro Monday 08:30–09:30 vs chemistry Monday 10:00–15:30 → join **= chemistry** on most smooth draws.
- Fast priority chemistry (~08:30) vs routine Monday readout → join **= micro** ~08:30–09:30.
- Sunday on-call readout vs Monday chemistry → join **= chemistry**.
- Therefore the smooth-path join sits **Monday 21 Sep ~09:00–16:00**, not Friday, not Saturday, and not Sunday.

### Scenario A — smooth path

- Join: Monday 09:00–16:00 as above.
- QA waiting: Friday 15:30–20:00 (~4.5 reviewer-h) and Saturday 09:00–13:00 (~4 reviewer-h) should retire much of the current five-batch queue despite ~3 expected arrivals. Central leftover Monday 08:00 ≈ 2–4 batches; overdispersion → 0–8. Two reviewers from 08:00. At a Monday midday join, wait is typically **0–3 h**, occasionally **4–8 h**.
- QA service: 1.5 h central, ~3.5 h P80. A 12:00–15:00 start usually finishes **Monday 14:00–20:00**. A late join plus a long review **spills to Tuesday 08:00–12:00**.
- Rework: none by definition.
- Final: electronic release +0.25–0.75 h, continuous. Terminal **Monday afternoon/evening or Tuesday late morning**, remaining about **70–95 h**.
- Calendar pauses that matter: Friday 18:00 chemistry stop; Sunday dead for both labs and QA. Saturday QA helps only by draining *other* batches.

### Scenario B — chemistry rerun

- First run as in the shared prefix (Monday, sometimes Tuesday morning).
- Borderline with baseline probability 0.10–0.20, higher if the temperature flag confirms. Rerun is priority, triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) h, mean ~1.93 h, **cannot cross 18:00**.
- If the first run ends Monday morning/midday, the rerun usually finishes **the same Monday**. If the first run ends after ~15:30, the rerun **starts Monday and finishes Tuesday 08:00–11:00**.
- Micro is still typically ready Monday 08:30–09:30, so the join **moves with the rerun**, not with a sum of the two branches.
- QA then starts Monday late or Tuesday. Elevated investigation probability (0.30–0.55) means a large overlap with D/E: a “rerun only” release is Tuesday; a rerun + doc investigation is mid-week.
- Final without investigation: **Tuesday 22 Sep**, remaining ~88–105 h. With documentation investigation, see D.

### Scenario C — delayed microbiology readout or confirmation

- Primary incubation still ends Sunday 12:00–20:00.
- Indication probability 0.05–0.12 baseline, higher under a confirmed excursion. Indication is **not** treated as the usable join token.
- Extra 20–30 h continuous incubation from the indication/readout epoch:
  - Indication Monday ~09:00 → technically ready **Tuesday 05:00 – Wednesday 15:00**. Staffed readout **Tuesday 08:00–09:30** or **Tuesday 15:30–16:30** or **Wednesday 15:30–16:30**.
  - Sunday on-call indication ~16:00 → ready **Monday 12:00 – Tuesday 22:00**, readout Monday afternoon or Wednesday 08:00.
- Chemistry is usually already done, so the join **= confirmation readout**. This is the case where micro, not chemistry, is the slower branch.
- QA sees a late Tuesday/Wednesday token and, with high probability, an investigation. Clean-QA finish **Tuesday evening–Thursday**. Laboratory investigation pushes this row into E.
- Weekend staffing matters *here*: a confirmation that becomes ready Saturday night waits until Monday 08:00 unless on-call is re-authorized.

### Scenario D — QA query or documentation-only investigation

- Join still Monday (smooth or rerun) or Tuesday/Wednesday (if stacked with C).
- Documentation query 0.15–0.30 from completeness 0.82: **+4–12 QA working hours**, then a short residual review.
  - 4 working hours after Monday 16:00 → Tuesday 08:00 resume.
  - 12 working hours after Monday 16:00 → Wednesday ~16:00 resume (Mon 16:00–20:00 = 4 h, Tue 08:00–20:00 = 8 h).
- Documentation-only investigation (about 60–80% of opened files): **+8–20 QA working hours**.
  - 8 h after Monday evening → Tuesday afternoon.
  - 20 h after Monday evening → Wednesday afternoon, or Thursday if the join was already Tuesday.
- Saturday contributes only 4 QA hours; Sunday none. That is why a 20 h investigation opened late in the week can appear to “lose” a weekend.
- Final: **Tuesday–Thursday** centrally; **Friday 25 Sep or Monday 28 Sep** if a long investigation sits across the weekend.
- Electronic release still 0.25–0.75 h after the last acceptance.

### Scenario E — laboratory investigation / dependence tail

- Trigger: confirmed temperature excursion and/or rerun and/or contamination, then the 20–40% laboratory track. Approximate mixture: P(open investigation) ≈ 0.20–0.28 using mid-band rates and the common-cause structure; P(lab track) ≈ 0.04–0.12.
- After the join and an initial QA look, **+3–7 calendar days** of extra laboratory work (not QA working hours). Example: investigation opened Monday 21 evening → earliest report Thursday 24, latest Monday 28. Opened Wednesday 23 → Saturday 26–Wednesday 30; QA cannot close on Sunday.
- Then residual QA (hours, not days) + release 0.25–0.75 h, or rejection recording 0.5–1.0 h if a critical defect is confirmed.
- This tail is where **adding stage means would most mislead**: the 3–7 day clock replaces, rather than sits beside, a 1.5 h review.

### Dependence (applies across B–E)

Do not multiply baseline rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities as if independent. A confirmed excursion raises all three together, so P(rerun ∩ contamination ∩ investigation) is larger than the independent product. A cleared flag does the reverse and returns the forecast toward A.

A single-point “about 80 hours / Monday 18:00” would overstate precision. The forecast is a **Monday–Tuesday centre with a working-day tail into the following week**.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Whether QA opens a deviation and whether that investigation is laboratory-type** — *path uncertainty*. This is the step that turns a 1–2 day remaining-time problem into a 3–10 day one. The 0.04–0.10 vs 0.30–0.55 switch, and the 3–7 calendar-day lab track, dominate the upper percentiles.
2. **Unknown temperature-excursion flag** — *observation uncertainty* and *dependence uncertainty*. One unresolved binary jointly lifts rerun risk, contamination risk, and investigation risk. Treating those events as independent would understate the stacked tail.
3. **Chemistry queue discipline plus unobserved work ahead** — *queue uncertainty* (with a service-time component). FIFO vs risk-priority is a 55/45 coin that decides whether first-run chemistry is Monday breakfast or Monday mid-afternoon, and whether a long job spills to Tuesday.
4. **QA backlog at the future join time** — *queue uncertainty*. Today’s five batches are not the Monday queue. Overdispersed arrivals at 0.35/h over Friday evening, Saturday, and Monday morning can leave anything from an idle reviewer to a multi-cycle wait for two staff.
5. **Microbiology confirmation path and the staffing calendar around it** — *path uncertainty* plus *calendar uncertainty*. A clean primary readout is a Monday-morning add-on; an indication inserts 20–30 continuous hours and then waits for a microbiologist. Weekend authorization is second-order on the smooth path but first-order once confirmation readiness falls on a Saturday or Sunday.

Honourable mention, not in the top five: chemistry and QA service-time draws (triangular / right-skew) and the exact 46–54 h incubation window. Those move the result by hours, not by days, unless they interact with an 18:00 or weekend close.

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

**Resolution of the temperature-excursion flag** (confirmed vs cleared).

- **Cleared (probability mass 0.60–0.80):** rerun and contamination return to baseline; investigation stays in the 0.04–0.10 band if both tests are clean. Probability piles onto Scenario A. The P50 window stays **Monday evening–Tuesday afternoon**; the P90 thins dramatically.
- **Confirmed (0.20–0.40):** rerun and indication probabilities rise together; investigation moves into the 0.30–0.55 band even if both tests later look acceptable. Mass shifts from A into B, C, D, and especially E. The P50 slides later in the week (often **Tuesday–Thursday**); the severe tail (**next week, laboratory track**) becomes a first-class planning case rather than a footnote.

**Secondary trigger:** the **primary microbiology readout outcome** once a microbiologist is present (Sunday if on-call; otherwise Monday 08:00–09:30). A clean readout leaves chemistry and QA as the live clocks. A contamination indication starts the 20–30 h confirmation timer and, with high probability, an investigation — immediately replacing the Monday-join story with a Tuesday/Wednesday join and a much thicker tail.

A useful runner-up observation is the **Friday 18:00 chemistry snapshot** (did B-417 enter service, and how much work remains?). That single timestamp largely resolves FIFO vs risk-priority and tells whether first-run chemistry is a Monday-morning job or a Monday-afternoon/Tuesday job. It changes the centre by hours; the temperature flag and the micro readout change the centre *and* the path.
