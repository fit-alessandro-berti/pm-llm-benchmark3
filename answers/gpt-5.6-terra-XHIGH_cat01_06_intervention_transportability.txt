The shared causal mechanism is a guarded fork–join reduction: when Identity Check and Document Check are both legally enabled, based on sufficiently independent information, and can be staffed simultaneously, verification completion can move from approximately a serial sum toward the maximum of the two branches. Both sites retain an Assessment join requiring completed identity and document verification, so this local structural mechanism is transferable for eligible cases.

Several mechanisms are site-specific. At Site A, digital documents, cross-trained reviewers, simultaneous ownership, added document capacity, API improvement, and queue huddles jointly enabled the observed change. At Site B, originals-required cases cannot start Document Check early; case locking can prevent real overlap; separate role pools can move congestion downstream; and Legal Review plus supplement loops create a path absent at A.

Important effect modifiers are digital-document status, originals requirement, legal-review requirement, complexity, lock status, identity/document/assessment/legal capacity, and queue discipline. These modify both treatment fidelity and the causal effect conditional on fidelity.

A similar control-flow diagram does not establish transportability because diagrams usually omit data guards, statutory constraints, resource calendars, queueing, IT ownership rules, and measurement quality. Site A’s observed nine-hour median reduction is a bundled before/after association, not an identified effect of parallelization. At Site B, the same nominal fork can be legally impossible, technically serialized, or operationally harmful if it increases downstream Assessment or Legal Review congestion.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

The recommended intervention is a **guarded, channel-aware fork–join**, not a universal simultaneous creation of executable Identity Check and Document Check tasks.

**Eligibility rules.** Phase 1 should include cases that are: confirmed digital at registration, not subject to an originals requirement, not public-sector/high-value, not already on an exception hold, and not classified as highest complexity. Phase 2 may add confirmed-digital legal-review cases after queue and quality gates are met. Originals-required cases are not eligible for concurrent Document Check.

**Concurrent work.** For eligible digital cases, create and permit execution of Identity Check and Document Check concurrently immediately after Register. For originals-required cases, permit Identity Check and Originals Receipt to proceed concurrently after Register; a Document Check work item may exist as a blocked readiness record, but cannot be claimed, started, or completed until Originals Receipt is logged.

**Mandatory prerequisites.** Assessment remains blocked until both Identity Check and the applicable Document Check are complete. Legal Review remains mandatory for statutory public-sector/high-value cases. No task configuration may override originals, legal, or assessment prerequisites.

**Queue and WIP controls.** Start with no more than six active paired digital verification cases in the pilot lane, no more than two active paired cases per identity reviewer, and release no new paired case when ready-to-assess treated WIP exceeds one case per available assessor. Apply the same priority, aged-work escalation, and daily huddle rules to treatment and control cases.

**Resource responsibilities.** Identity and document reviewers retain separate ownership. The coordinator controls paired-case admission; assessors and legal reviewers are not expected to absorb unplanned bursts. Treatment requires auditable dual-task ownership; no manual lock workaround is permitted.

**Legal and supplement loops.** A Request Supplement invalidates the current document-clearance state, closes or supersedes obsolete tasks, and returns the case through required receipt, Document Check, Assessment, and Legal Review steps. It does not reopen unrelated completed identity work unless the supplement changes identity evidence.

**Logging requirements.** Record channel, originals requirement, legal flag, complexity, task-created time, claim time, actual start/finish time, entered-at time, owner, lock state, document version, loop number, API latency, queue age, and reason for every repeat task.

A compact partial-order specification is:

```text
R = Register; I = Identity Check; O = Originals Receipt; D = Document Check;
A = Assessment; L = Legal Review; S = Request Supplement; N = Notify.

Digital case:
R < I
R < D
{I, D} < A

Originals-required case:
R < I
R < O < D
{I, D} < A

No-legal branch:
A < X(Approve, Reject) < N

Legal branch:
A < L0
For each k >= 0:
Lk < X(Approve, Reject, Sk)
Sk < [Ok if legally required] < D(k+1) < A(k+1) < L(k+1)
Approve < N
Reject < N
```

The pilot distinguishes mechanism failure from implementation failure by measuring assignment, task creation, actual task claim intervals, lock state, and completion timestamps separately. If T1 is assigned but true overlap remains low because the legacy lock serializes work, the result is an implementation failure rather than evidence that the fork–join mechanism has no value. If true overlap is high among digital cases but waiting before Assessment and cycle time do not improve, the mechanism is failing operationally, likely because document, assessment, or legal queues offset the saved verification time.

If waiting before Assessment improves but p90 worsens, the mechanism worked locally while congestion shifted downstream. Stratification by digital status, legal status, complexity, and queue load identifies whether the effect is limited to the subgroup structurally comparable with Site A. Enhanced logging in both control and treatment arms also prevents a timestamp-recording change from being mistaken for genuine concurrency.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
"""
Illustrative PM4Py construction of:
1. Site A before/after process trees;
2. accepting Petri nets for those trees;
3. a guarded Site B POWL candidate with:
   - digital versus originals-required document routes,
   - a strict partial order that makes Assessment wait for both checks,
   - an optional Legal Review branch,
   - a supplement loop that redoes Document Check and Assessment.

This is a control-flow model. It intentionally does NOT encode data guards,
statutory eligibility predicates, resource capacities, case locks, priorities,
durations, queueing, API latency, or timestamp back-entry behavior.
"""

import pm4py

from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1) Site A before/after process trees
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

site_a_before_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', "
    "'Assessment', X( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

site_a_after_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->( 'Register', +( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), "
    "'Assessment', X( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2) Convert Site A trees to accepting Petri nets
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

a_before_net, a_before_im, a_before_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
    site_a_before_tree
)
a_after_net, a_after_im, a_after_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
    site_a_after_tree
)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Utility: create a POWL strict sequence using an explicit strict partial order
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def strict_sequence(*nodes):
    """Returns a StrictPartialOrder whose nodes form a sequence."""
    po = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=list(nodes))
    for left, right in zip(nodes[:-1], nodes[1:]):
        po.order.add_edge(left, right)
    return po


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3) Site B candidate POWL model
#
# Each occurrence below is a distinct POWL object. For example, the repeat
# Document Check has the same visible label but is a different node occurrence.
# This avoids reusing a POWL node under multiple parents.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

register = Transition(label="Register")

identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")

# Digital route: Document Check can start immediately after Register.
document_digital = Transition(label="Document Check")

# Originals route: Document Check starts only after Originals Receipt.
originals_receipt = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
document_originals = Transition(label="Document Check")
originals_document_route = strict_sequence(
    originals_receipt,
    document_originals
)

# Exclusive data-dependent route. The model cannot itself decide which branch
# applies; operational data guards must select digital vs originals-required.
document_route = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [document_digital, originals_document_route]
)

assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")

# Non-legal direct-decision path.
approve_direct = Transition(label="Approve")
reject_direct = Transition(label="Reject")
no_legal_decision = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [approve_direct, reject_direct]
)

# Legal path with a supplement loop:
# Legal Review; optionally Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment;
# then Legal Review repeats. When the loop exits, legal decision is issued.
legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
request_supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
document_repeat = Transition(label="Document Check")
assessment_repeat = Transition(label="Assessment")

supplement_redo = strict_sequence(
    request_supplement,
    document_repeat,
    assessment_repeat
)

# POWL LOOP semantics: do child first; then optionally redo child and do again.
legal_supplement_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.LOOP,
    [legal_review, supplement_redo]
)

approve_legal = Transition(label="Approve")
reject_legal = Transition(label="Reject")
legal_decision = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [approve_legal, reject_legal]
)

legal_path = strict_sequence(
    legal_supplement_loop,
    legal_decision
)

# Optional legal-review branch. In production, statutory case data—not the
# XOR itself—must determine whether a case takes the legal path.
decision_route = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR,
    [no_legal_decision, legal_path]
)

notify = Transition(label="Notify")

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4) Top-level Site B strict partial order
#
# Register enables Identity and the document route in parallel.
# The explicit two edges below make Assessment wait for BOTH Identity and
# whichever document route was selected.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

site_b_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        register,
        identity,
        document_route,
        assessment,
        decision_route,
        notify,
    ]
)

site_b_powl.order.add_edge(register, identity)
site_b_powl.order.add_edge(register, document_route)

# Critical fork-join dependencies:
site_b_powl.order.add_edge(identity, assessment)
site_b_powl.order.add_edge(document_route, assessment)

site_b_powl.order.add_edge(assessment, decision_route)
site_b_powl.order.add_edge(decision_route, notify)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
```

## 9. Recommendation

conditional go

Transfer the **guarded fork–join principle**, not Site A’s blanket implementation or its observed nine-hour median reduction. The defensible transferable mechanism is narrow: when a case is confirmed digital, both verification checks are legally enabled after Register, their evidence sources are sufficiently independent, two active owners are technically possible, and downstream Assessment capacity is protected, starting Identity and Document checks together should reduce verification completion time and some waiting before Assessment.

Do not copy the proposed “create both tasks immediately for every case” design. It is legally invalid for the 62% of cases requiring originals, operationally risky under the one-owner legacy lock, and likely to create misleading overlap measures through back-entry. It also ignores Site B’s separate role pools, limited Assessment and Legal capacity, and legal supplement loops. Site A’s improvement was confounded by added document reviewers, registry API improvement, a simpler digital mix, queue huddles, and an all-team before/after rollout.

The first subgroup should be confirmed-digital, non-public-sector/non-high-value, lower-to-medium-complexity cases with no unresolved intake hold. This group is structurally closest to the portion of Site A where parallelization was feasible, while avoiding statutory originals and the legal-review tail. Expected benefit is modest at portfolio level: roughly zero to three hours lower overall median cycle time, with potentially three to five hours for eligible cases. The principal risk is not failed concurrency alone; it is shifting work into the three-assessor pool and worsening p90 performance.

Expand to digital legal-review cases only if true overlap exceeds a pre-specified fidelity threshold, Assessment queue age is stable, p90 does not worsen materially, rework remains within one percentage point of control, and no statutory or lock-control violations occur. Modify the design if overlap is real but Assessment queues rise: lower paired-case admission, stagger releases, or add protected assessment capacity. Abandon the intervention for a stratum if it depends on manual lock workarounds, creates unauthorized early document work, raises rework or appeals materially, or causes sustained tail deterioration despite high implementation fidelity.

## 10. Assumption register

1. Confirmed digital status means Document Check is legally and evidentially ready at Register — why needed: it defines the true parallelizable population — how to test: audit registration flags against document completeness and legal requirements — consequence if false: premature work, rework, and invalid treatment assignment.

2. The legacy system can support two auditable active task owners for treated digital cases — why needed: nominal task creation is not real parallel execution — how to test: inspect lock, claim, and audit logs during a technical dry run — consequence if false: the intervention becomes serialization with misleading timestamps.

3. Identity and document evidence are sufficiently independent for eligible cases — why needed: concurrent review should not create avoidable duplicate or contradictory work — how to test: review dependency reasons and escalation rates in pilot cases — consequence if false: overlap increases rework or quality risk.

4. Originals-required cases can be reliably identified at registration — why needed: statutory ordering cannot be violated — how to test: compare intake classification with later legal/document determinations — consequence if false: unauthorized early Document Check or contaminated eligibility strata.

5. Assessment capacity is adequate under the pilot WIP cap — why needed: saved verification time must not simply become Assessment waiting — how to test: monitor ready-to-assess age, assessor utilization, and p90 by week — consequence if false: median may improve while tail performance worsens.

6. Legal-review demand and supplement-loop rates remain stable enough during the pilot — why needed: legal congestion is a major downstream confounder — how to test: compare legal arrivals, loop counts, and reviewer availability across arms and weeks — consequence if false: apparent treatment effects may be legal-capacity shocks.

7. Enhanced timestamps represent actual work timing rather than later data entry — why needed: true overlap is the mechanism-fidelity measure — how to test: reconcile task audit times, claim logs, and user-entry timestamps — consequence if false: overlap-based mediation and adoption estimates are biased.

8. Pilot effects in digital non-legal cases are not assumed to generalize automatically to originals or legal cases — why needed: transportability is conditional on structural similarity — how to test: estimate effects separately by channel, legal status, complexity, and true-overlap fidelity — consequence if false: expansion could impose a beneficial niche intervention on harmful or ineligible populations.
