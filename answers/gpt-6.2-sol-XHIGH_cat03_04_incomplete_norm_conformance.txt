## 1. Conformance semantics

- **Forbidden behavior** satisfies an explicit prohibition in its stated scope. A forbidden edge in a graph is a recorded hit. A business breach requires evidence that the edge represents the prohibited act or relationship.
- **Missing mandatory behavior** occurs when an eligible instance lacks a required action or link at the point when the obligation is due. Verify eligibility, observation coverage, and the unit of obligation before declaring a behavioral breach. An identifiable event can have a confirmed missing link in the log while its real-world association remains unresolved.
- **Unexpected but unregulated behavior** is observed outside the clauses that govern this norm. Its rarity or omission from the reference graph carries no adverse verdict. Another policy may apply.
- **Typical-pattern absence** is a departure from a desirable pattern. It can motivate investigation of a trend or an individual case. It does not establish a breach.
- **Likely data-quality error** is a suspect representation supported by evidence such as a reused join key or an omitted link. The recorded deviation and the probable cause are separate judgments.
- **Abstraction mismatch** occurs when the summary tests a different predicate from the business rule, such as strict adjacency in place of eventual occurrence.
- **Insufficient evidence** means the available records cannot establish scope, timing, identity, completeness, or an applicable exception well enough to settle the case.

Closed-world judgments apply to explicit prohibitions and to required behavior within mandatory scopes. The remaining vocabulary is open-world. Severity below reflects potential impact and urgency. Confidence distinguishes the recorded observation from its business interpretation. E10 ranks ahead of T6 because the known join provenance supports immediate containment while the cobirth still needs lifecycle verification.

## 3. Detailed reasoning by model family

### OC-DFG

An OC-DFG aggregates activity adjacency from individual object histories by object type. The counts indicate observed Order, Package, or Invoice support as described in the summary. They cannot recover complete histories, disjoint cohorts, or causal explanations. For N1, testing an **eventual** obligation with directly-follows creates false positives when a logged review or other event intervenes before a valid later validation. A review absent from the underlying log cannot itself break recorded adjacency, so investigate what is actually recorded, including missing or misattributed validation and right-censoring. Standard status and sufficient follow-up are prerequisites to a missing-validation verdict. With one Create per Order and a strict single successor on the same 100-Order population, 98 Validate successors and 3 Review successors would be inconsistent. Check recurrence, ties, and counting scope before attributing the two gaps to those three reviews.

For N3, the two adjacent hits warrant checking any later dispatch on the same cancelled Order. Intervening events can hide additional violations from adjacency counts. N6 supplies no exception to N3. For N4, verify both external intent and actual Pack/Dispatch history, and resolve whether adjacency or packing before dispatch is required. ET–OT weights of 200 for Pack–Package and Dispatch–Package cannot establish this history. For N2, the counts of 72 and 83 need neither be disjoint nor share a known denominator. N5’s 92 Issue → Record Payment paths, 4 write-offs, and 14 without an observed successor reflect a snapshot. The 14 cannot be called unpaid or overdue without ledger and cutoff evidence.

### OTG

An OTG establishes that typed relationships were found under the discovery rules. Weights may count pairs or other support units. Even a weight equal to an event total cannot establish that every scoped interaction was represented. For T1 and T2, inspect the participants jointly at each Add Item or Dispatch. T2’s aggregate edge can coexist with the two E6 misses because links elsewhere can contribute to the same type edge. For T6, test actual creation in the same business event. Common first visibility may be an artefact of extraction. The weight of 92 under T3 is consistent with common Invoice–Payment interaction and leaves individual E8 obligations to event-level checking. T4, T5 and codeath provide context without imposing coverage requirements. Equal weights across projections do not establish that they refer to the same instances.

### ET–OT

An ET–OT edge shows that events of a given type were linked to objects of a given type. Universal requirements need event-ID checks against the event table, including events with no relations. Relation weights can differ because one event may have multiple objects of a type. The Add Item weight difference therefore leaves the number of E2 misses unresolved, and the Item weight does not prove E3 coverage. The E1, E4, E5 and E7 weights of 100, 200, 200 and 93 show expected link types are present. Neither valid event links nor matching totals establish sequence or shared object identity across events. Context independently identifies the E6 and E8 gaps. A carrier reference may support E6 repair, and a properly typed unapplied-cash event would be outside E8. E10 fails the recorded-link rule even if the source event was a legitimate heartbeat unrelated to finance.

## 4. Mandatory, forbidden, typical, and unregulated behavior

**Mandatory**

- N1 and N4 require checking the 2 Order and 2 Package histories in scope. A missing direct edge becomes a behavioral breach only when evidence establishes failure of the actual obligation.
- T1/T2 and E1–E8 require coverage per scoped event or interaction. E2 remains unverified, E6 has 2 recorded gaps, and E8 has 1. If review establishes and correctly records the unapplied-cash exception, that payment falls outside E8. If a Delivery or Invoice exists and the link was lost, repair the representation.

**Forbidden**

- N3 and T6 have 2 and 1 recorded hits respectively. Confirm effective sequencing or true shared creation before assigning a business breach.
- E10 has 1 prohibited recorded link. If source evidence confirms the join defect, remediate it as a linkage error.

**Typical**

- N2’s 83 direct closures are observed typical behavior. Invoicing can be part of an indirect closure path.
- N5 and T3 support a common payment pattern, with 92 reported in each view. The 4 write-offs are allowed and the 14 with no observed successor need maturity and follow-up context. Neither typical relation waives E8.

**Unregulated**

- N6, T4, T5, E9, Review–Customer and Package–Delivery codeath are outside scope. Create → Review and Validate → Issue Invoice are unregulated transitions. In-scope validation remains due after review.

## 5. PM4Py conformance code sketch

```python
import pm4py

# Assumes a PM4Py build exposing the named APIs, with conformance(log, reference).
observed = {
    "ocdfg": pm4py.discover_ocdfg(ocel),
    "otg": pm4py.discover_otg(ocel),
    "etot": pm4py.discover_etot(ocel),
}
raw = {
    "ocdfg": pm4py.conformance_ocdfg(ocel, norm_ocdfg),
    "otg": pm4py.conformance_otg(ocel, norm_otg),
    "etot": pm4py.conformance_etot(ocel, norm_etot),
}

# Version policy statuses, eligibility, exceptions and temporal predicates
# outside the reference graphs. An absent graph edge carries no policy status.
policy = {
    "N1": "mandatory", "N2": "typical", "N3": "forbidden",
    "N4": "mandatory", "N5": "typical", "N6": "unspecified",
    "T1": "mandatory", "T2": "mandatory", "T3": "typical",
    "T4": "unspecified", "T5": "unspecified", "T6": "forbidden",
    **{f"E{i}": "mandatory" for i in range(1, 9)},
    "E9": "unspecified", "E10": "forbidden",
}

classes = {
    ("mandatory", "missing_link"): "recorded mandatory link gap",
    ("mandatory", "proxy_gap"): "scope or abstraction review required",
    ("forbidden", "hit"): "recorded forbidden hit; verify provenance",
    ("typical", "absence"): "typical-pattern absence",
    ("unspecified", "observed"): "unregulated observation",
}


def interpret(norm_id, signal, evidence):
    status = policy[norm_id]
    return {
        "norm_id": norm_id,
        "status": status,
        "class": classes.get((status, signal), "no gap in this check"),
        "evidence": evidence,
    }


# Use instance-level evidence for universal event-link obligations. Joining to
# the object table excludes dangling references from valid linked event IDs.
eid, oid = ocel.event_id_column, ocel.object_id_column
activity, object_type = ocel.event_activity, ocel.object_type_column
events = ocel.events.groupby(activity)[eid].agg(set).to_dict()
typed_links = ocel.relations[[eid, oid]].dropna().merge(
    ocel.objects[[oid, object_type]].dropna(), on=oid, how="inner"
)
linked = typed_links.groupby(object_type)[eid].agg(set).to_dict()
event_rules = {
    "E1": ("Create Order", "Order"),
    "E2": ("Add Item", "Order"),
    "E3": ("Add Item", "Item"),
    "E4": ("Pack Package", "Package"),
    "E5": ("Dispatch Package", "Package"),
    "E6": ("Dispatch Package", "Delivery"),
    "E7": ("Record Payment", "Payment"),
    # No explicitly typed unapplied-cash events in this snapshot.
    "E8": ("Record Payment", "Invoice"),
    "E10": ("Sensor Heartbeat", "Invoice"),
}

semantic = []
for norm_id, (event_type, required_type) in event_rules.items():
    eligible = events.get(event_type, set())
    with_link = eligible & linked.get(required_type, set())
    if policy[norm_id] == "forbidden":
        affected = with_link
        signal = "hit" if affected else "clear"
    else:
        affected = eligible - with_link
        signal = "missing_link" if affected else "clear"
    semantic.append(interpret(norm_id, signal, {
        "affected_event_ids": sorted(affected), "checked_count": len(eligible),
    }))

# Selected graph observations supplied by the snapshot. In an operational run,
# populate these from normalized diagnostics and scoped instance investigations.
for norm_id, signal, evidence in [
    ("N1", "proxy_gap", {"adjacent": 98, "new_orders": 100}),
    ("N3", "hit", {"orders": 2, "verify": "effective times and package attribution"}),
    ("N4", "proxy_gap", {"adjacent": 198, "external_packages": 200}),
    ("N5", "absence", {"no_observed_successor": 14}),
    ("T6", "hit", {"weight": 1, "verify": "actual creation in the same event"}),
    ("T5", "observed", {"weight": 3}),
]:
    semantic.append(interpret(norm_id, signal, evidence))

result = {"observed": observed, "raw_diagnostics": raw, "semantic_findings": semantic}
```

## 6. Norm-completeness assessment

| Gap | Operational risk | Clarification and status |
|---|---|---|
| N1 standard eligibility and validation horizon | Open cases and indirect paths inflate misses, while indefinitely pending cases escape review | **Mandatory.** Define standard status, qualifying validation, and the due point. Permit intermediates and track cases still within the window. |
| N3 effective cancellation and dispatch | Timestamp artefacts create false alerts and intervening events hide actual dispatches | **Forbidden.** Define authoritative cancellation and handoff times and same-Order attribution. Specify how any authorized reinstatement affects the prohibition. |
| N4 external intent and ordering | Direct adjacency can penalize legitimate warehouse steps and mask packing after release | **Mandatory.** Define external shipment intent and require evidenced packing before dispatch, with staging allowed. |
| T1/T2 and E1–E8 aggregation | Healthy aggregate counts hide events with missing or invalid participants | **Mandatory.** Audit each eligible event and valid, qualified participant types, with joint participation for scoped interactions. Define how to resolve a carrier reference to a Delivery link. |
| E8 cash exception | Staff may add fictitious Invoice links or leave valid unapplied cash indistinguishable from lost allocations | **Mandatory.** Require the Invoice link or explicit, auditable unapplied-cash typing under the defined exception. |
| T6 birth semantics | Import and first visibility can falsely imply a control breach | **Forbidden.** Prohibit actual co-creation and define authoritative creation evidence and event identity. |
| E10 financial linkage provenance | Reused identifiers can silently contaminate financial associations | **Forbidden.** Define the prohibited stream-to-finance predicate and require namespaced identity and provenance in its detection. |
| N2/N5/T3 evaluation windows | Snapshots can generate misleading pattern alarms and obscure changes in mature cohorts | **Typical.** Define cohort, denominator and ageing for monitoring, including recognized alternative dispositions. |
| Review–Customer and Package–Delivery codeath coverage | Teams may penalize legitimate rare links or assume compliance under a separate policy | **Explicitly unspecified.** Document these boundaries and identify an owning policy where one exists. |
