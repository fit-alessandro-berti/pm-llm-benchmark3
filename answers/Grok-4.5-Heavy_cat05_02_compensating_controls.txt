## 1. Control-Objective Decomposition

FEP-1 requires that the human resource performing Prepare_Payment differs from the human resource performing Release_Payment (shared service accounts do not count). Its purpose is to prevent one person from both initiating and authorizing an erroneous or fraudulent transfer. We decompose this into six interrelated threat scenarios / sub-objectives:

1. **Single-actor unintentional error** (wrong amount, vendor, bank details, or unsupported invoice through carelessness).  
   AV-2 prevents a defined subset (duplicates, operator amount limit, basic PO match, bank-account ownership/changes in prior 7 days, sanctions) and is strong for those checks only; it fails on goods/services receipt confirmation and commercial reasonableness. MG-4 prevents when an independent L3+ manager substantively reviews invoice, bank details and supporting evidence before irrevocable settlement (weak if rubber-stamped). RA-7 detects after the fact and becomes partly corrective only while bank recall (~24 h) remains feasible. ED-1 does not independently prevent; it requires the other conditions (especially AV full pass + MG + timely RA). Incomplete conditions leave the threat open.

2. **Single-actor intentional fraud / diversion** (fictitious or inflated invoice, diverted bank account, self-dealing).  
   AV-2 offers partial prevention (bank ownership/change, sanctions, amount limit) but explicitly fails on collusion, commercial reasonableness, goods receipt, and attribution of who controlled a compromised account; same-actor override nullifies it. MG-4 is strong preventive if truly independent, pre-settlement and evidence-based; it fails under conflict of interest or credential sharing. RA-7 is detective and corrective only inside the recall window; post-window recovery is uncertain. ED-1 mitigates via strict preconditions (vendor ≥180 days, no recent bank change, amount < EUR 100 000, AV full no-override, independent MG before settlement, RA within one business day) but does not dissolve the underlying objective.

3. **Credential compromise or shared-account control**.  
   AV-2 has limited value (cannot reliably establish who controlled the account). MG-4 mitigates by requiring a second independent human before settlement. RA-7 is detective. ED-1 / FEP-1 explicitly treat shared service accounts as non-independent and require independent MG.

4. **Collusion or non-independent dual actors** (preparer and releaser collude, share workstation/credentials, form a persistent pair, or involve a conflicted manager). Nominal four-eyes form may hold while the independence objective fails.  
   AV-2 fails to detect collusion or shared control. MG-4 can detect/prevent if the manager is independent of both and reviews thoroughly; it fails if the manager is a direct supervisor or rubber-stamps. RA-7 can detect patterns (repeated pairs, fabricated documentation, shared workstations) retrospectively. ED-1 still requires independent MG and remains vulnerable to preparer–releaser collusion under an emergency token.

5. **Irrecoverable settlement of a defective payment**.  
   AV-2 is strong preventive (blocks or warns pre-release). MG-4 is preventive if before release, hybrid (detective of upstream defects + residual preventive of settlement) if after release but before settlement, and purely detective if after settlement. RA-7 is detective + partly corrective only inside ~24 h; after that recovery is uncertain and exceptional. ED-1 explicitly requires MG before settlement and RA within one business day to preserve recoverability.

6. **Absence of commercial substance / goods-or-services confirmation and bank/vendor integrity**.  
   AV-2 is strong on automated bank ownership, recent changes, sanctions, duplicates and PO match but fails on goods/services receipt and commercial reasonableness. MG-4 addresses reasonableness and receipt review when evidence is examined. RA-7 detects fabricated receipts or post-facto anomalies. ED-1 leverages AV + MG conditions plus vendor-tenure and no-bank-change rules.

These map directly onto the evaluation dimensions of timing, coverage, independence, recoverability and case risk.

## 2. Equivalence and Sufficiency Test

A compensating package (AV-2 + MG-4 + RA-7, whether under ED-1 or otherwise) is assessed for equivalence to nominal independent dual-human control along seven dimensions:

- **Timing** of intervention relative to Release_Payment and Bank_Settlement (preventive force requires action before release or, at latest, before irrevocable settlement).  
- **Independence** of compensating actors (distinct from preparer/releaser, no performance-rating conflict, no shared credentials or workstations that collapse separation).  
- **Coverage** of the six threat scenarios (AV-2 only for its listed automated checks; MG-4 must be substantive review of invoice + bank + receipt).  
- **Overrideability** (same-actor override of a warning, or escalation that bypasses independence, weakens the package).  
- **Recoverability** (ability to stop or recall before irrevocable settlement / within ~24 h).  
- **Evidence quality** (documented review of supporting materials versus mere timestamped ratification; audit findings of fabrication or related-party accounts).  
- **Collusion resistance** (pattern monitoring of pairs, workstation sharing, credential assignment).

**Sufficiency categories**:
- **Fully equivalent**: restores (or exceeds) the protective force of independent dual human control across all major threat scenarios, with comparable preventive timing, true independence, non-overridable core checks, preserved recoverability, high evidence quality and collusion resistance. Rare for same-user emergency paths.  
- **Partially equivalent**: most threats addressed with good timing and independence, yet residual gaps remain (hybrid post-release pre-settlement MG-4; AV coverage limits on receipt; minor independence questions). Residual risk materially lower than uncontrolled same-user action but higher than perfect FEP-1.  
- **Merely mitigating**: some risk dimensions reduced but significant residual remains on fraud, collusion or recoverability because of compromised timing, independence or override.  
- **Ineffective**: one or more core threats left unaddressed or the compensating controls themselves compromised (same-user warning override, post-settlement manager, conflicted supervisor, AV outage without alternative evidence, audit after recall window accompanied by loss).

“Same expected loss” is not a sufficient sole standard. Expected monetary loss is an important outcome measure but does not capture (a) the intrinsic process-integrity and deterrence value of segregation of duties, (b) differences in loss distribution and fat-tail / unknown-attack robustness, (c) regulatory, auditability and reputational requirements that evaluate control design itself, (d) cultural erosion from repeated convenience violations, and (e) the ability of independent dual control to surface defects that automated or post-hoc checks miss. Two packages with identical expected loss can still differ materially on independence, timing, evidence quality and collusion resistance; judgment is therefore multi-dimensional and calibrated to case-specific risk (amount, vendor tenure, bank-change proximity, emergency justification strength, anomaly evidence).

## 4. Preventive–Detective–Corrective Comparison

| Control | Primary Nature | Timing Relative to Release / Settlement | Preventive Contribution | Detective Contribution | Corrective Capacity | Key Limitations |
|---------|----------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------|------------------------|---------------------|-----------------|
| AV-2 | Preventive (with detection) | Pre-Release_Payment | Blocks or warns on covered checks (duplicate, sanctions, bank ownership/change 7 d, amount limit, PO match) | Flags anomalies for human attention | None beyond block; override converts to warning | Narrow coverage (no goods receipt, no collusion, no reasonableness, no account-control attribution); outage leaves gap; override by same actor weakens |
| MG-4 | Timing-dependent: Preventive / Hybrid / Detective | Pre-Release: preventive; Post-Release but pre-Settlement: hybrid; Post-Settlement: detective | Independent L3+ review of invoice/bank/receipt can stop release or settlement | Identifies defects in prepared package | Can stop settlement if still possible; otherwise none | Rubber-stamp weak; conflict of interest (supervisor) destroys independence; requires evidence of actual review |
| RA-7 | Detective (+ conditional corrective) | Post-Release; ideal within 1 BD / 24 h recall | None | Full retrospective review possible | Recall/recovery feasible ~24 h; after that uncertain | Purely after-the-fact; delayed = weak recovery; does not prevent initial loss |
| ED-1 | Conditional permission (not standalone control) | Preconditions must hold prospectively | Bundles AV + MG + amount/vendor/bank limits to constrain same-user risk | Requires timely RA | Depends on bundled RA timing | Any condition fail renders same-user unauthorized; does not make FEP-1 objective disappear |

**Semantics for manager action after Release_Payment but before Bank_Settlement**: Hybrid. It detects defects that survived prepare/release while still offering residual preventive force against irrevocability of settlement (the prompt explicitly values “before release or before irrevocable bank settlement”). Pure post-settlement ratification is detective only. Settlement is treated as the internal-control irrevocability threshold. AV-2 and pre-settlement MG-4 are the main preventives; RA-7 supplies detection and limited correction. Packages succeed only when these layers are independent, timely and cover the relevant threats.

## 5. Counterfactual and Residual-Risk Analysis

**AP-201 (OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET)**: Most plausible loss mechanism = low-value same-user error or opportunistic diversion on an established vendor. Causal chain broken primarily by AV-2 full pass (bank ownership/change, amount limit, PO) + independent hybrid MG-4 (m04 pre-settlement). Residual exposure: commercial reasonableness / goods not received (AV silent); single-actor fraud residual low given amount/vendor. A truly independent second releaser would restore nominal FEP-1, eliminating convenience risk with negligible change to residual (already low). Residual after package: Low.

**AP-202 (OBJECTIVE_FAILED)**: Mechanism = intentional high-value diversion to a relative via new vendor + recent bank change, enabled by same-user AV-warning override using supervisor credential. No effective break: AV overridable by the actor, MG conflicted and post-recall, RA far outside window. Residual = realized unrecovered loss (High/Critical). An independent second releaser (non-supervisor, no credential share) would force escalation of the bank-change warning, prevent self-override and require proper independent MG → high probability of blocking or detecting pre-settlement. Residual would fall to Low-Medium.

**AP-204 (OBJECTIVE_FAILED)**: Mechanism = non-independent dual control (shared workstation + 37 joint payments in 30 days) enabling a fabricated service receipt on high-value consulting. Nominal form satisfied; AV passed (no coverage of receipt); no MG-4 present; RA detected late. Residual High (220 k exposure, defect realized). A truly independent second releaser (rotated pairs, separate workstations, no long-term dedicated cell without oversight) raises friction and detection probability for fabrication substantially. Residual would drop to Medium (sophisticated collusion still possible but harder).

**AP-206 (INDETERMINATE)**: Mechanism = same-user action under ambiguous emergency (token 2 min after Prepare; outage claimed from 12:40). Chain largely broken by AV full + independent MG-4 pre-release + vendor/amount/bank conditions. Residual Medium due to precondition timing ambiguity (possible process weakness or logging delay). An independent second releaser makes ED-1 unnecessary and removes token-timing doubt; residual similar on receipt issues. Package mitigates most threats but leaves ED-1 validity open → indeterminate overall.

**AP-208 (OBJECTIVE_FAILED)**: Mechanism = high-value transfer (900 k) with recent bank change under AV outage + oversized “emergency” token, no contemporaneous ownership evidence. Chain unbroken: no AV, MG post-settlement (detective only), RA 4 days late (no recall). Residual High (even though legitimate outcome, exposure unrecoverable and evidence gap). An independent second releaser + operational AV would enforce bank-ownership/change checks pre-release and block or demand stronger evidence; residual falls sharply to Low for this threat class. Outage + amount ≫ ED-1 limit = package failure.

**AP-207 (OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET, bonus)**: Mechanism = same-user under safety emergency. Broken by ED-1 preconditions + AV + hybrid MG (reviewed evidence pre-settlement) + timely RA. Residual Low. An independent second releaser converts to pure FEP-1 with little residual change.

**AP-203 (OBJECTIVE_MET)**: Fully covered by ED-1 (token pre, MG pre-release reviewing evidence); residual near-zero for covered threats.

Compensating packages approximate FEP-1 best on automated-checkable, low-collusion, pre-settlement threats with true independence. They systematically under-perform on collusion, override abuse, control outages, post-irrevocable timing and goods-receipt gaps. An independent second releaser remains the cleanest break for single-actor and many collusion paths. Qualitative residual ranges (Low/Medium/High) are used; no precise probabilities are invented.

## 6. Evidence and Monitoring Recommendations

Collect and monitor the following as risk indicators that trigger investigation, never as automatic proof of misconduct:

- Same-user Prepare+Release rate overall and by user; any same-user transaction without documented full ED-1 condition set (token timestamp before Prepare, amount < EUR 100 000, vendor ≥180 days, no bank change in 7 days, AV full pass with no override, independent MG before settlement, RA ≤1 business day).  
- AV override rate, especially of bank-change or amount-limit warnings, and whether the override actor equals the preparer/releaser.  
- Manager ratification after Bank_Settlement or more than a few hours after Release.  
- Emergency same-user cases with RA-7 >1 business day or >24 h from release.  
- Repeated resource pairs (e.g., u12/u13) exceeding a threshold of joint completed payments in 30 days; co-occurrence with shared-workstation or same-terminal indicators.  
- Bank-account change within 7 days of payment (already partially covered by AV-2, but useful for pattern analysis).  
- AV-2 service unavailable / Validation_Service_Unavailable co-occurring with any Release.  
- High-amount same-user or emergency-token use above the ED-1 limit.  
- Presence of Override_Warning by the same user who prepared/released.  
- Audit findings of fabricated receipts or related-party bank accounts.

**Alert versus proof**: repeated pairs may be a legitimate dedicated payment cell if organizational design documents establish compensating independence (rotation, dual-location review, separate credentials, manager oversight); overrides may be justified with evidence; late manager action may still be hybrid if pre-settlement. Alerts trigger enhanced audit or process review, not automatic nonconformance findings.

Additional evidence to retain: what the manager actually reviewed (invoice, bank details, receipt); emergency-token activation with precise UTC timestamp and requester; workstation/session ID for Prepare and Release to detect sharing; post-audit recovery success rate. Track same-user rate, override rate, late manager approval, audit-before-recall-window compliance, repeated resource pairs, shared workstations, bank-change proximity and control outages as ongoing monitoring metrics.

# 6. Emit preliminary evidence flags
pairs = pd.DataFrame({'prep': prep, 'release': rel}).dropna()
pairs['pair'] = pairs['prep'] + '|' + pairs['release']
pair_counts = pairs['pair'].value_counts()

ts = df.groupby(['case:concept:name', 'concept:name'])['time:timestamp'].first().unstack()

def hours_between(a, b):
    if pd.isna(a) or pd.isna(b):
        return None
    return (b - a).total_seconds() / 3600.0

flags = []
for case in sorted(df['case:concept:name'].unique()):
    f = {'case': case}
    f['has_prepare'] = case in prep.index
    f['has_release'] = case in rel.index
    f['has_attempt'] = case in attempt.index
    f['completed'] = f['has_prepare'] and f['has_release']
    f['same_user'] = (prep.get(case) == rel.get(case)) if f['completed'] else (
        prep.get(case) == attempt.get(case) if f['has_attempt'] else None)
    f['prep_resource'] = prep.get(case)
    f['release_or_attempt_resource'] = rel.get(case) if f['has_release'] else attempt.get(case)
    f['av_full_pass'] = case in set(df[df['concept:name']=='Automated_Validate']['case:concept:name'])
    f['av_warning'] = case in set(df[df['concept:name']=='Automated_Validate_Warning']['case:concept:name'])
    f['av_block'] = case in set(df[df['concept:name']=='Automated_Validate_Block']['case:concept:name'])
    f['av_unavailable'] = case in set(df[df['concept:name']=='Validation_Service_Unavailable']['case:concept:name'])
    f['override_by_same'] = case in set(df[df['concept:name']=='Override_Warning']['case:concept:name']) and f['same_user']
    # Manager timing
    mgr_ts = None
    for act in ['Manager_Approve', 'Manager_Ratify']:
        if act in ts.columns and case in ts.index and pd.notna(ts.loc[case].get(act)):
            mgr_ts = ts.loc[case, act]
            break
    rel_ts = ts.loc[case, 'Release_Payment'] if (case in ts.index and 'Release_Payment' in ts.columns) else None
    settle_ts = ts.loc[case, 'Bank_Settlement'] if (case in ts.index and 'Bank_Settlement' in ts.columns) else None
    audit_ts = ts.loc[case, 'Audit_Review'] if (case in ts.index and 'Audit_Review' in ts.columns) else None
    f['manager_present'] = mgr_ts is not None and pd.notna(mgr_ts)
    f['manager_before_release'] = (mgr_ts < rel_ts) if (f['manager_present'] and rel_ts is not None) else None
    # When settlement not logged, use facts-based knowledge for hybrid assessment
    f['manager_before_settlement'] = (mgr_ts < settle_ts) if (f['manager_present'] and settle_ts is not None) else (
        True if f['manager_present'] and settle_ts is None else None)
    f['manager_after_settlement'] = (mgr_ts > settle_ts) if (f['manager_present'] and settle_ts is not None) else None
    f['audit_hours_after_release'] = hours_between(rel_ts, audit_ts) if (rel_ts is not None and audit_ts is not None) else None
    f['audit_within_approx_24h'] = (f['audit_hours_after_release'] is not None and f['audit_hours_after_release'] <= 24)
    f['audit_within_1bd_approx'] = (f['audit_hours_after_release'] is not None and f['audit_hours_after_release'] <= 36)
    if case in case_facts.index:
        cf = case_facts.loc[case]
        f['amount_eur'] = cf['amount_eur']
        f['bank_change_recent'] = cf['bank_change_recent']
        f['emergency_token_before_prepare'] = cf['emergency_token_before_prepare']
        f['av2_result_fact'] = cf['av2_result']
        f['manager_independent_fact'] = cf['manager_independent']
        f['outcome_defect'] = cf['outcome_defect']
        f['ed1_amount_ok'] = cf['amount_eur'] < 100000
        f['ed1_vendor_ok_approx'] = cf['vendor_active_days_approx'] >= 180
        f['ed1_bank_ok'] = not cf['bank_change_recent']
        f['ed1_av_ok'] = cf['av2_result'] == 'full_pass'
        f['ed1_token_ok'] = cf['emergency_token_before_prepare']
        f['ed1_mg_timing_ok'] = f['manager_before_settlement'] is True or f['manager_before_release'] is True
        f['ed1_audit_ok'] = f['audit_within_1bd_approx'] is True
    if f['completed']:
        p = str(f['prep_resource']) + '|' + str(f['release_or_attempt_resource'])
        f['pair'] = p
        f['pair_count_in_log'] = int(pair_counts.get(p, 0))
        f['repeated_pair_flag'] = f['pair_count_in_log'] >= 2
    else:
        f['pair'] = None
        f['pair_count_in_log'] = 0
        f['repeated_pair_flag'] = False
    flags.append(f)

flags_df = pd.DataFrame(flags).set_index('case')

print("\n=== PRELIMINARY EVIDENCE FLAGS (for human judgment only; NOT compliance verdict) ===")
print(flags_df[['same_user', 'av_full_pass', 'av_warning', 'av_block', 'av_unavailable', 'override_by_same',
                'manager_present', 'manager_before_release', 'manager_before_settlement', 'manager_after_settlement',
                'audit_hours_after_release', 'audit_within_approx_24h', 'amount_eur', 'bank_change_recent',
                'emergency_token_before_prepare', 'ed1_amount_ok', 'ed1_token_ok', 'ed1_av_ok',
                'repeated_pair_flag', 'pair', 'outcome_defect']].to_string())

print("\n=== Pair counts in this log (evidence of repeated pairing) ===")
print(pair_counts)

print("\n=== NOTES ON PM4Py BEHAVIOR ===")
print("pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle (v2.7.x):")
print("  - Default keep_violations=False returns cases that do NOT violate (compliant).")
print("  - keep_violations=True returns cases that violate (same resource on the two activities).")
print("  - Signature and return type (DataFrame vs EventLog) can vary across minor versions;")
print("    pure-pandas resource comparison is therefore used as the robust cross-check.")
print("  - format_dataframe (this version) does not accept resource_key; it preserves existing columns.")
print("  - No external files or services are used; all data is embedded.")
print("\n=== END OF EVIDENCE EXTRACTION ===")
print("These flags support (but do not replace) the control-objective analysis of timing,")
print("independence, coverage, recoverability, and case risk. Final classifications are")
print("human/LLM-judged multi-dimensional assessments, not mechanical outputs of this script.")

**PM4Py caveats**: `filter_four_eyes_principle` (pm4py 2.7.x) uses `keep_violations=True` to return violating cases; the default returns compliant cases. Signature and return type (DataFrame versus EventLog) can differ across minor versions; pure-pandas resource comparison is therefore the reliable cross-check. `format_dataframe` in this version does not accept a `resource_key` argument and simply preserves the existing `org:resource` column. Timestamps are forced to UTC. No external files or network services are used. All emitted flags are strictly preliminary process-mining and facts-join evidence for subsequent human judgment of control objectives; they do not constitute the compliance verdict.
