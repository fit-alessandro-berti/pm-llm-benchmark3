## 1. Current-process diagnosis

The warranty process is not slow on average - it is slow *conditionally* and fast *incorrectly*. The dominant behavior is a high-volume, low-friction auto-route that hides quality, compliance and downstream cost problems.

**The central contradiction:** The most frequent path `Intake claim (5000) -> Auto eligibility screen (3600) -> Fast approval (2550) -> Dispatch replacement (2300) -> Quality/compliance close (2320)` is dominant in the **frequency DFG** and is the best performer on speed/cost in isolation, but is the worst on quality/compliance when viewed end-to-end.

- **Performance DFG:** `Intake -> Auto (mean 0.8h) + Auto -> Fast approval (1.0h) + Fast -> Dispatch (3.0h) + Dispatch -> Close (5.0h)` yields a theoretical median of ~8-13h for low-risk accessories, confirmed in **customer segment outcomes** (Premium low-risk 8h median on fast path, Standard low-risk 13h). Auto screen cost is `EUR 0.20` and Fast approval cases are `31% cheaper before rework`.
- **Rework statistics reverses the advantage:** `Customer complaint/reopen` occurs in `9.2%` of all cases, but `14.1%` of Fast approval cases vs `3.3%` for `Repair quote -> Customer approval -> Repair device`. `Fast -> Complaint` mean is `72h` and `Dispatch -> Complaint` mean is `96h` with `p90 150-180h`. After complaints, reshipments and credits, Fast approval is only `9% cheaper` - the cost saving is largely illusory. Wrong replacement cost is `EUR 95` (low-risk) but `EUR 410` (high-value), making the fast error expensive precisely where it is most frequent.
- **Segment outcomes break the average:** For `Premium high-value (14% share)` complaint rate is `22% on fast path vs 5% on reviewed/repair path`; for `Standard high-value (21% share)` it is `18% vs 6%`. For `Premium low-risk (18%)` the delta is small (`6% vs 3%`) and service-credit exposure if `>24h` is the dominant CX risk. Aggregating average cycle time therefore optimizes the wrong segment.

**Additional performance, quality, compliance and CX problems:**

1.  **An evidence completion trap.** `Request missing evidence` is entered by `650 (Auto) + 780 (Manual)` cases, but `890` loop back to `Manual eligibility review` with mean `34h / median 26h / p90 88h`. `26%` of cases that enter it do so at least twice. The **Unclear defect / missing evidence segment (22% share)** has `median 140h`, highest abandonment (`Claim abandoned 390` total, `320` directly from `Request missing evidence`), and repeated evidence requests. **Temporal profile deviation** confirms `Manual -> Repair quote` is too slow for Premium, and `Request missing evidence -> Manual` is the long-tail driver.

2.  **Compliance failure at scale.** **Token-based replay** shows `log fitness 0.91` but `only 68% fitting traces` - 32% of cases deviate from the normative model. **Alignment diagnostics** show the three frequent `move-on-log` deviations are: Fast approval before evidence completeness (violates **R2**), Dispatch without risk review for high-value devices (violates **R1**: claims `>EUR 400` must have Manual or Fraud/risk review before Fast approval), and Customer complaint/reopen after close bypassing Manual review (violates **R3**). **Precision is low**: the discovered model allows combinations of auto approval + risk review + repair that policy forbids, indicating ad-hoc workarounds rather than controlled flexibility. High-value segments carry high compliance risk exactly where R1/R2 are violated.

3.  **Rework loops are not contained.** Beyond evidence loops, `Repair device -> Quality check (360) -> Rework repair (45) -> Repair device (42)` creates a repair rework loop, affecting `12.5%` of high-value repaired devices vs `4.0%` low-risk. `Customer complaint/reopen (total 510: 140 from Fast, 260 from Dispatch, 80 from Repair quote, 30 from Repair)` feeds `310 -> Manual eligibility review` and `120 -> Escalation board`. The `Escalation board` has only one `45-min slot/day` - strong satisfaction effect but unscalable, and `Customer complaint -> Escalation mean 54h / p90 130h`.

4.  **Resource asymmetry and bottleneck fragility.** **Resource info:** `Manual eligibility 7 reviewers, 36h/week, EUR 34/h, queue grows sharply >82% utilization`; `Fraud/risk 2 specialists EUR 52/h, bottleneck Mondays/post-campaign`; `Repair quote 3 technicians EUR 45/h, waiting for part-price confirmation (mean 22h)`; `Quality check 2 inspectors EUR 38/h, underused except after campaigns`. The fast path avoids these bottlenecks today only by violating rules. Any correction without capacity redesign will immediately migrate the bottleneck to Manual/Fraud.

5.  **Customer experience is segmented, not average.** Premium customers have strict expectations but different tolerances: Premium low-risk cannot tolerate `>24h` (credit exposure) while Standard low-risk tolerates `72h` if visibility is good. Fast path meets Premium SLA on median but fails on complaint tail (`p90 150h+` after complaint). Reviewed/repair paths meet quality (`5-6% complaint for high-value`) but violate timeliness (`median 96-120h for high-value reviewed/repair`), creating opposite CX failure modes.

In short: optimizing for mean speed/cost pushes cases through Auto -> Fast -> Dispatch, which maximizes fitness violations and downstream complaint/reopen work, while the slower `Manual -> Fraud -> Repair quote -> Customer approval (mean 30h) -> Repair (20h) -> Quality check (9h)` path is slower and more expensive upfront but dominant on quality/compliance for high-value and unclear defects.

## 2. Redesign A: Segment-Aware Smart Triage with Enforced Compliance Gates

**Redesigned process flow:**
`Intake claim` -> **Segment & Value Classifier** (new automated decision: Premium/Standard x low-risk/high-value x defect clarity, with EUR 400 check) ->
- Branch A1 [Low-risk accessory, evidence complete, `EUR 400`]: `Auto eligibility screen (updated rules)` -> `Fast approval` -> `Dispatch replacement` -> `Quality/compliance close`. R2 exception for Premium low-risk accessory is explicitly coded.
- Branch A2 [High-value device OR >EUR 400 OR history/risk flag]: `Auto eligibility screen` -> **mandatory** `Manual eligibility review` OR `Fraud/risk review` (parallelized where possible) -> `Repair quote` OR `Fast approval` only after gate passes -> fulfillment. No bypass allowed; system blocks `Fast approval` and `Dispatch` if fitness check fails.
- Branch A3 [Unclear defect / missing evidence flagged at Intake]: Immediate `Request missing evidence` with single structured portal task, not a loop to Manual until evidence completeness is system-confirmed.

Evidence completeness becomes a system state, not a human judgment, to enforce R1/R2 by design.

**Targets:** All high-value devices (35% of volume: 14% Premium + 21% Standard) and any claim >EUR 400. Explicitly preserves the fast path for low-risk accessories (43% of volume) where the quality delta is small.

**Mechanism of improvement:** Shifts cost/quality trade-off from *post-hoc rework* to *ex-ante selection*. By updating auto-screen rules (deployable in 2 weeks) to be segment-aware, it prevents the 22% complaint rate for Premium high-value on fast path and eliminates the most frequent alignment deviations (Fast before evidence, Dispatch without risk review). Frequency DFG logic: reduces the 2550 Auto->Fast volume for high-value, diverts ~15-35% more cases to Manual/Fraud as predicted, but cuts `Dispatch -> Complaint (260)` and `Fast -> Complaint (140)` tail. Compliance risk drops because R1/R2 are enforced in workflow engine; fitness % fitting should rise from 68% without manual auditing.

**Deliberately sacrifices / leaves unresolved:**
- **Speed and cost for high-value:** Median cycle time for high-value moves from 20-28h (fast path) to 96-120h (reviewed/repair). Does not solve the 34h evidence loop or 30h customer approval wait; may worsen them if volume diverted without capacity increase.
- **Resource pressure:** Deliberately overloads Manual (7) and Fraud (2) specialists. Will breach 82% utilization threshold rapidly on Mondays/campaign peaks unless mitigated by redesign B/C scheduling. Does not address part-price delay (Repair quote 22h mean) or Abandonment for unclear defects.
- **CX for Premium high-value:** Sacrifices the 24h credit boundary to gain quality; requires expectation management instead of speed.

## 3. Redesign B: Evidence-First & Pre-Dispatch Assurance Gateway

**Redesigned process flow:**
`Intake claim` -> `Eligibility assessment (Auto or Manual)` -> **if evidence incomplete: Guided Evidence Portal** (new: mobile-first, checklist by defect-type, completeness validation, human-assisted chat for low digital literacy) -> **Evidence completeness = true** gate -> `Technical decision` ->
-> **Lightweight Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate** (new: 6-18h, pooled reviewer/inspector checks part number vs defect vs claim history; sampling 100% for high-value, 20% for low-risk) -> `Dispatch replacement` OR `Repair quote` -> `Quality/compliance close`.

Crucially, retains the *possibility* of Auto -> Fast -> Dispatch for all segments, but inserts two friction points that add short, bounded delay to prevent unbounded long-tail delay.

**Targets:** The `22% Unclear defect / missing evidence` segment (median 140h, 26% repeat requests, highest abandonment) and all `Dispatch replacement (2300 cases)` at risk of wrong-part selection (Dispatch->Complaint is the longest tail: mean 96h, p90 180h, linked to incorrect part). Secondarily targets `Request missing evidence -> Manual` loop (890 cases, 34h mean).

**Mechanism of improvement:** Attacks rework at its source rather than routing around it. Portal reduces repeated evidence requests by intended 30-45% by making requirements explicit upfront and validating completeness before the case returns to `Manual eligibility review`, cutting the 14h Manual->Request and 34h Request->Manual cycle. Pre-dispatch gate catches wrong replacement selection before shipment, trading `6-18h` deterministic delay for `96h` complaint delay + `EUR 410` replacement error + escalation load. Uses underused `Quality check` inspectors (EUR 38/h, idle outside campaigns) in a pooled pre-dispatch role, avoiding new FTEs. Leverages performance DFG insight that 5h Dispatch->Close is cheap if correct, but 96h Dispatch->Complaint dominates total flow time variance.

**Deliberately sacrifices / leaves unresolved:**
- **Fast-path speed:** Deliberately slows the fastest cases: Premium low-risk median moves from 8h to ~14-26h (8h + gate). Will breach 24h service-credit threshold more often; sacrifices the fastest SLA for predictability. Standard low-risk customers tolerate 72h if visibility is good, so impact is smaller there.
- **Compliance:** Does not structurally enforce R1 (Manual/Fraud before Fast for >EUR 400) unless combined with A. Leaves high-value fast-track risk review bypass unresolved; may still allow compliant-but-low-quality auto approvals.
- **Inclusivity vs efficiency:** Portal may increase abandonment for low digital literacy customers (as warned) and does not address the 30h `Repair quote -> Customer approval` wait or Fraud specialist bottleneck.

## 4. Redesign C: Repair-First Containment for High-Value with Scaled Quality and Async Escalation

**Redesigned process flow:**
`Intake claim` -> Classification (high-value vs low-risk) ->
- For **high-value devices**: Default = `Fraud/risk review (triage, not full investigation)` -> `Repair quote (with pre-fetched part-price catalog to cut waiting)` -> `Customer approval` -> `Repair device` -> **Mandatory `Quality check` -> `Quality/compliance close`**. `Dispatch replacement` for high-value requires Escalation Board approval only (exception, not default). `Fast approval -> Dispatch` is removed as a normal path for high-value.
- For **low-risk accessories**: Retains `Auto -> Fast -> Dispatch` as today.
- **Escalation board redesign:** Replace single daily 45-min slot with async pooled senior review (two parallel lanes: Premium vs Standard) plus daily 15-min standup for exceptions only; `Customer complaint/reopen` goes to `Manual eligibility review` per R3 but with dedicated complaint handlers, not the general Manual queue.

**Targets:** Premium and Standard high-value devices (35% of cases) where repair path complaint rate (`3.3%` overall, `5-6%` for high-value) is dramatically lower than dispatch path (`14.1%` overall, `18-22%` for high-value) and where wrong replacement cost (`EUR 410`) dwarfs repair labor. Also targets `Escalation board (120 cases)` scalability and `Repair device -> Quality check -> Rework (12.5% high-value)` containment.

**Mechanism of improvement:** Changes fulfillment philosophy from *replace* to *repair* where quality data favors repair. Frequency DFG shows `Repair quote (570 total incoming: 260+310) -> Customer approval (460)` is a low-complaint island; scaling it contains downstream volatility. Mandatory `Quality check` (currently 345 closes, 360 inspections) moves from underused to critical control, catching rework before `Rework repair (45)` escapes to customer. Async escalation decouples complaint recovery from a single bottleneck, reducing `Customer complaint -> Escalation 54h mean / p90 130h` and `Complaint -> Manual 40h mean` queue interference with new claims.

**Deliberately sacrifices / leaves unresolved:**
- **Cycle time and resource intensity:** Repair path median `96h (Premium high-value) to 120h (Standard high-value)` + `Repair device 20h + Quality check 9h + Rework 14h` is 4-5x slower than fast dispatch. Deliberately sacrifices speed for cost-of-quality; Premium SLA breach is certain without separate promise management. Requires technician capacity (3 techs) and inspector capacity (2) to scale, shifting cost from replacement logistics to labor.
- **Customer preference:** Some Standard high-value customers prefer replacement over repair; `Customer approval` abandonment (`70 cases`) and waiting time (`30h mean`) remain. Does not fix evidence loop (26% repeat) or auto-rule outdatedness; if evidence is unclear, repair quote is still delayed (temporal deviation: `Manual->Repair 22h mean, p90 60h`).
- **Low-risk efficiency:** Leaves low-risk accessory path untouched, so R2 violations or repeat evidence requests for that segment persist.

## 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

**Redesign A - Smart Triage:**
- **Failure Mode 1: Manual/Fraud collapse.** Rule update increases Manual inflow 15-35% per prompt. With 7 reviewers at 82% threshold and 2 fraud specialists bottlenecked Mondays/post-campaign, queue for `Manual eligibility review (mean 7.5h, p90 18h)` explodes to `p90 60h+` for `Manual -> Repair quote`, violating Premium temporal expectation and spiking `Request missing evidence` abandonment. Mitigation requires load leveling, not just routing.
- **Failure Mode 2: Misclassification at the boundary.** EUR 400 threshold + segment classifier mislabels low-risk high-price accessories or premium devices with accessory defect, sending them to slow thorough lane unnecessarily, increasing cycle time with no quality gain (complaint delta only 3% for Premium low-risk) and triggering service credits.
- **Displaced Bottleneck:** From `Auto -> Fast` throughput to `Intake -> Manual eligibility review` and `Manual -> Fraud/risk review (mean 18h)`. Also `Request missing evidence -> Manual (890 cases, 34h mean)` becomes secondary bottleneck as Manual queue backs up, amplifying the 140h unclear-defect loop rather than relieving it.

**Redesign B - Evidence-First & Pre-Dispatch Gateway:**
- **Failure Mode 1: Portal-induced abandonment.** 22% unclear-defect segment has low digital literacy; portal reduces repeat requests 30-45% for digital users but increases `Claim abandoned (320 from Request)` for assisted-need customers, trading rework reduction for loss of legitimate claims and CX harm. Evidence completeness validation may be too strict, creating false-negative blocks.
- **Failure Mode 2: Gate becomes performative delay.** 6-18h gate staffed by pooled QC/inspectors catches <50% of wrong-part selections due to sampling or part-data quality issues, adding delay without reducing `Dispatch -> Complaint 96h` tail. Cases wait for gate during peak dispatch (2300 dispatches) and gate p90 extends to 18h+, reproducing the same SLA breach it was meant to avoid.
- **Displaced Bottleneck:** From `Dispatch -> Complaint` (post-fulfillment rework) to **pre-dispatch gate queue**. `Fraud/risk` and `Manual` pressure eases from evidence loop, but `Quality check` shifts from underused to overused (2 inspectors now covering 2300 dispatches + 390 repairs). `Repair quote -> Customer approval 30h mean` becomes more visible as gate holding amplifies customer wait perception.

**Redesign C - Repair-First Containment:**
- **Failure Mode 1: Repair capacity and quote latency failure.** Defaulting high-value (35% volume  1750 cases) to `Repair quote (3 techs, EUR 45/h, 22h mean waiting for part-price)` overwhelms technicians; part-price confirmation delay (p90 60h) + `Customer approval 30h mean / p90 80h` pushes cycle time beyond 120h, causing abandonment (70 already) and Premium churn. `Repair device -> Quality check 9h` then stalls on 2 inspectors.
- **Failure Mode 2: Customer rejection of repair.** High-value customers expect replacement, not repair; `Customer approval` fallout increases (`Customer complaint/reopen 80 from Repair quote` may rise) and Escalation Board async lane floods, recreating complaints upstream. Rework repair (12.5% high-value) still occurs, but now with higher volume, so absolute rework count increases even if rate decreases.
- **Displaced Bottleneck:** From `Dispatch replacement` logistics + `Escalation board single slot` to `Repair quote` + `Repair device -> Quality check -> Rework` loop. `Fraud/risk review` load drops (no longer gate to dispatch) but `Technician` and `Inspector` become the system constraints, and `Customer approval` waiting becomes the customer-perceived bottleneck.

## 7. Recommended experiment sequence

**Sequencing logic:** De-risk fastest-to-deploy, lowest-regret lever first (rules), then attack the highest-variance loop (evidence), then test the highest-impact fulfillment change (repair-first) which needs most capacity and promise change. Isolate effects to avoid attributing rework reduction to triage when it was actually gate-driven. Treat speed vs quality as segment-conditional hypotheses, not global averages.

**Stage 1 - First pilot (Weeks 1-4): Redesign A light (Segment-aware rule update) on limited high-value slice.**
- **Scope:** Update auto-screen rules to enforce R1/R2 for claims >EUR 400 *only* for Standard high-value (21%) in one region/channel. Keep Premium high-value on current process as control. Deploy classifier with hard block: Auto->Fast disabled if value >400 without Manual/Fraud.
- **Metrics to monitor (leading/lagging):**
  - Leading: % Auto->Fast blocked, Manual queue depth & utilization vs 82%, Fraud queue Mondays, `Intake->Manual` median/p90.
  - Process: `Dispatch without risk review` count, alignment fitness % fitting, precision change, `Manual->Repair quote` temporal compliance.
  - Outcome: Median/p90 cycle time by segment, complaint/reopen % at 7/14/30 days, wrong replacement cost (EUR 410 proxy via reshipment), service credits for Premium boundary test, abandonment rate.
- **Stop/Go thresholds (qualitative/ranges):**
  - **Stop/rollback if:** Manual utilization sustains >85-90% for >1 week OR `Manual->Repair` p90 exceeds 60h for >20% of Premium cases OR abandonment increases >2-3 pp vs control without complaint reduction.
  - **Go to Stage 2 if:** High-value complaint rate drops directionally toward 5-8% range (vs 18-22% baseline) AND fitting traces rise from 68% toward 75-80% AND downstream reshipment/EUR 410 cost drops, even if median cycle time rises to 50-90h range (expected). Do not require cost neutrality at this stage; require that 31%9% cost gap narrows further (net cost improves vs current).
- **Learning guard:** Use difference-in-differences: Standard high-value pilot vs Premium high-value control. Track whether complaints are *prevented* or *delayed*: monitor `Fast->Complaint 72h` and `Dispatch->Complaint 96h` tails at 30 days, not just 7 days, to ensure reduction is not shifted downstream beyond Close (check `Customer complaint after close` alignment deviation).

**Stage 2 (Weeks 5-10): Redesign B evidence portal + sampling gate, layered on top of Stage 1.**
- **Scope:** Keep triage pilot running. Add portal for Unclear defect segment (22%) in same pilot region; A/B test portal (self-serve vs assisted chat) and introduce pre-dispatch gate for high-value dispatches only (100% sampling high-value, 20% low-risk). Use pooled QC inspectors; do not add headcount.
- **Metrics:** Repeat `Request missing evidence` rate (target 26%  ~15-18%), `Request->Manual` loop time (34h mean), abandonment by digital-literacy proxy (age/channel), gate catch rate (flagged wrong-part / total dispatches), gate latency (6-18h actual), `Dispatch->Complaint` rate, evidence completeness time.
- **Thresholds:**
  - **Stop portal if:** Abandonment rises >30-45% reduction benefit (e.g., +3 pp abandon) OR repeat evidence not reduced by at least 15-20% absolute. Switch to assisted-only mode.
  - **Stop gate if:** Catch rate <30% of historic wrong-part rate OR median gate wait >15h for >25% cases without complaint delta.
  - **Go if:** Evidence loop p90 drops from 88h toward 40-50h, Dispatch->Complaint mean drops from 96h toward 40-60h range, and overall complaint/reopen for portal cohort drops 3-5 pp vs Stage 1 alone. Use quality-adjusted cycle time (median + complaint tail probability × 96h) rather than raw median.
- **How to know improvement is real not shifted:** Extend observation window to 45 days post-close to capture `Customer complaint/reopen after Quality/compliance close` (R3 violation pattern). Track rework repair (12.5%) and escalation board entries; if portal+gate merely pushes cases to `Manual eligibility review` after complaint (310 path), complaint count will be flat but `Complaint->Manual 40h` volume will rise - that is shifted, not solved. Require both complaint rate *and* total touch time per claim (including rework) to fall.

**Stage 3 (Weeks 10-18): Redesign C repair-first controlled trial for a sub-segment.**
- **Scope:** Only if Stage 1-2 did not collapse capacity. Randomize Standard high-value devices where part-price catalog is available (avoid waiting) to `Repair quote -> Approval -> Repair + mandatory QC` vs `Enhanced dispatch (with Stage B gate)`. Keep Premium high-value on triage+gate, not repair-first, until promise reset agreed with sponsor. Run with temporary technician overtime or part-price automation.
- **Metrics:** `Repair quote -> Customer approval` conversion vs abandonment (70 baseline), `Customer approval -> Repair 20h` adherence, `Repair->Quality check->Rework` rate (12.5% baseline), final complaint 30-day (target 5-6% vs 18%), net cost per claim (labor EUR 45/h + QC EUR 38/h vs EUR 410 replacement error), cycle time distribution and SLA breach rate.
- **Thresholds:**
  - **Stop if:** Abandonment after quote rises >5 pp vs dispatch control OR technician queue makes `Manual->Repair` p90 >60h sustained OR net cost per claim higher than gate-enhanced dispatch despite lower complaint (i.e., repair labor cost exceeds avoided EUR 410).
  - **Go/scale if:** Repair-first achieves complaint <7% with mandatory QC escape <4% AND net cost per high-value claim  vs Stage 2 (even with  cycle time) AND async escalation reduces `Complaint->Escalation 54h` to <30h median. Scale only with catalog automation and added QC/technician capacity plan; otherwise keep as segmented option, not default.
- **Learning to separate real improvement:** Compare not just complaint % but *full journey* cost-time: include reshipment, rework, service credit, and second-order `Customer complaint -> Manual/Escalation` loops. Require that total customer-touch hours per 100 claims (including all loops) decreases; if median improves but total hours per 100 claims is flat, work has been displaced to repair/rework. Validate with conformance re-run: fitness should stay >0.85 and R1/R2 violations near zero for repair-first cohort, distinguishing compliant slowness from ad-hoc slowness.

**Cross-stage governance:** All stages report by segment (Premium/Standard x low-risk/high-value x unclear defect) - never by overall average. Dashboard must show median, p90, and rework-adjusted mean together, plus resource utilization vs 82% threshold, to prevent optimizing average cycle time at expense of tails.
