## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning
Deadline loss (€20k–€35k) and customer false-rejection/hold harm (€15k–€80k) are in the same order of magnitude and partially offsetting; they can be traded with ordinary operational caution. Regulatory/compliance exposure (€100k–€500k plus license, criminal, and reputational non-monetary consequences) is asymmetric and right-tailed: a single unlawful execution (missing mandatory second-officer review or four-eyes) dominates any plausible deadline saving.  

Dominance arguments:  
- Any CURRENTLY NONCONFORMANT path (A, B) is dominated once regulatory tail is admitted, regardless of point-estimate SLA predictions.  
- Between conformant paths, prefer those that keep P-884 inside the 90 min window while preserving an independent second review (ranks 1–3).  
- F closes the regulatory tail immediately but pays the full false-rejection expectation; it is attractive only if the unresolved match already meets the firm’s written rejection standard.  
- E is dominated for a live deadline case unless both C and D-S4 become infeasible.  
- Intervention predictions are observational; I therefore treat approval probability 0.55–0.75 and breach ranges as wide intervals and avoid razor-thin expected-value rankings.
