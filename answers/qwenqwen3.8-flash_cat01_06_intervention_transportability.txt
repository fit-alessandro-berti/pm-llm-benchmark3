## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

Management should not copy “create Identity Check and Document Check tasks simultaneously immediately after Register for every case.” The modified intervention is a segmented partial-order redesign:

1. **Eligibility rules**
   - **Tier 1: digital/no-originals, non-legal, low/medium complexity.** Eligible for full Identity Check and Document Check concurrency immediately after Register.
   - **Tier 2: originals-required, non-legal.** Eligible only for Identity Check and Originals Receipt concurrency after Register. Document Check is created only after the Originals Receipt event.
   - **Tier 3: legal/high-value.** Eligible for Identity Check and Originals Receipt concurrency if originals are required, or Identity and Document concurrency if digital/no-originals, but only after supplement-loop and legal-capacity controls are active.
   - **Exclusions:** cases with incomplete registration data, suspected fraud requiring sequential review, or cases where statutory rules require identity before document receipt confirmation.

2. **Tasks that may be created or executed concurrently**
   - Register must complete first.
   - For Tier 1: create Identity Check and Document Check as separate parallel tasks.
   - For Tier 2: create Identity Check and Originals Receipt as parallel tasks; do not create Document Check until Originals Receipt is logged.
   - Assessment is a join task that starts only after both Identity Check and Document Check are complete.
   - Legal Review starts after Assessment for legal-required cases.
   - Approve or Reject starts after the required decision path is complete.
   - Notify follows Approve or Reject.

3. **Statutory prerequisites that remain mandatory**
   - Originals Receipt before Document Check for originals-required cases.
   - Both Identity Check and Document Check before Assessment.
   - Legal Review before final decision for public-sector or high-value cases.
   - Request Supplement must restart Document Check and Assessment before Legal Review can close.

4. **Queue and WIP controls**
   - Separate queues: Identity, Originals Receipt, Document, Assessment, Legal, Supplement.
   - Initial WIP limits:
     - Identity reviewers: no more than 6 open Identity Check tasks per reviewer.
     - Document reviewers: no more than 5 open Document Check tasks per reviewer.
     - Assessors: no more than 4 open Assessment tasks per assessor.
     - Legal reviewers: no more than 3 open Legal Review tasks per legal reviewer.
   - If Assessment queue exceeds 80% of capacity or Legal Review queue exceeds 70%, throttle release of new Document Check tasks for Tier 2.
   - If supplement count exceeds 1.2 per legal case over a rolling two-week period, pause Tier 3 expansion.

5. **Resource responsibilities**
   - Identity reviewers perform Identity Check only.
   - Document reviewers perform Document Check only.
   - Assessors perform Assessment only.
   - Legal reviewers perform Legal Review and decide Request Supplement, Approve, or Reject.
   - Queue owners, not individual reviewers, control task creation and WIP.

6. **Handling of legal-review and supplement loops**
   - Legal Review may produce Approve, Reject, or Request Supplement.
   - Request Supplement creates a new Document Check repeat task and a new Assessment repeat task.
   - After Assessment repeat, the case returns to Legal Review repeat.
   - Maximum two supplement iterations before escalation to a joint legal/operations review.
   - Supplement loops are monitored separately from first-pass cycle time.

7. **Logging requirements**
   - Log task creation, start, end, owner, and queue entry/exit times.
   - Log Originals Receipt as a distinct event with source and delay.
   - Log case-lock overrides with reason code.
   - Log back-entry or delayed event entry with timestamp difference.
   - Log supplement iteration number.
   - Log eligibility tier and whether the intervention was correctly applied.

Compact POWL-style partial-order relation set:

```text
Register < IdentityCheck
Register < OriginalsReceipt

If digital_no_originals:
  IdentityCheck < Assessment
  DocumentCheck < Assessment
  No edge between IdentityCheck and DocumentCheck, allowing concurrency.

If originals_required:
  OriginalsReceipt < DocumentCheck
  IdentityCheck < Assessment
  DocumentCheck < Assessment
  No edge between IdentityCheck and OriginalsReceipt, allowing concurrency.

Assessment < LegalReview       if legal_required
Assessment < FinalDecision     if not legal_required

LegalReview < RequestSupplement
LegalReview < FinalDecision

RequestSupplement < DocumentCheckRepeat
DocumentCheckRepeat < AssessmentRepeat
AssessmentRepeat < LegalReviewRepeat

FinalDecision < Notify
```

The pilot separates mechanism failure from implementation failure by comparing cases that were correctly randomized and correctly executed with cases where execution deviated. Mechanism failure is inferred when eligible cases that achieved true overlap still show no reduction in waiting_before_assessment or cycle_time, or when p90 worsens because assessment/legal queues become saturated. Implementation failure is inferred when intended parallel tasks are not created, are back-entered, are serialized by case locks, or are diverted to manual workarounds; these cases should be classified as non-adherent but retained in intention-to-treat analysis. The design records task creation, start/end, owner, lock overrides, and supplement counts, allowing a process-mining classifier for true_overlap, back-entry, and bottleneck shift. A per-protocol analysis on cases with clean timestamps and real concurrency estimates the causal mechanism; an intention-to-treat analysis estimates the transportable operational effect. If ITT shows little benefit but per-protocol shows benefit, the problem is implementation. If both show little benefit but p90 worsens, the mechanism is bottleneck shift. If rework/appeal rise only in legal/high-value cases, the mechanism is loop congestion. Stop rules trigger on quality, not just speed.

def unique_nodes(nodes, order):
    """Return nodes in a stable unique order, including order endpoints."""
    seen_ids = set()
    out = []

    def add(node):
        key = id(node)
        if key not in seen_ids:
            seen_ids.add(key)
            out.append(node)

    for node in nodes:
        add(node)

    for source, targets in order.items():
        add(source)
        for target in targets:
            add(target)

    return out


def make_spo(nodes, order):
    """
    Construct a StrictPartialOrder.

    order is a dict: source -> iterable of targets.
    This encodes explicit partial-order edges.
    """
    order = {source: list(targets) for source, targets in order.items()}
    nodes = unique_nodes(nodes, order)

    last_error = None

    # Try several plausible constructor signatures.
    attempts = [
        ((nodes, order), {}),
        ((order,), {}),
        ((), {"nodes": nodes, "order": order}),
        ((), {"nodes": nodes, "order": order}),
    ]

    for args, kwargs in attempts:
        try:

def convert_to_petri(model):

        result = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(model)

        raise RuntimeError(f"Could not convert model to Petri net: {exc}") from exc

        if len(result) >= 3:
            net, initial_marking, final_marking = result[0], result[1], result[2]
        elif len(result) == 2:
            net, initial_marking = result[0], result[1]
            final_marking = None
        else:
            net = result[0]
            initial_marking = None
            final_marking = None
    else:
        net = result
        initial_marking = None
        final_marking = None

    if initial_marking is None:
        try:
            initial_marking = pm4py.get_initial_marking(net)
        except Exception:
            initial_marking = None

    if final_marking is None:
        try:
            final_marking = pm4py.get_final_marking(net)
        except Exception:
            final_marking = None

    return net, initial_marking, final_marking

#   - Document Check is gated by Originals Receipt in the originals branch.

#   - Legal Review is optional through an exclusive choice.
#   - A supplement loop is represented, with fallback to a one-iteration
#     loop-like path if the PM4Py version does not expose Loop.
#
# Important limitation:
#   POWL/Petri nets encode control flow, not data-dependent eligibility,
#   resource capacity, case locks, queue discipline, durations, or whether
#   a case is legally required to have originals.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

reg = make_activity("Register")

# Digital/no-originals branch:
# Identity Check and Document Check may run concurrently, then join at Assessment.
idc_d = make_activity("Identity_Check")
doc_d = make_activity("Document_Check")
assess_d = make_activity("Assessment")

digital_stage = make_spo(
    [idc_d, doc_d, assess_d],
    {
        idc_d: {assess_d},
        doc_d: {assess_d},
    },
)

# Originals-required branch:
# Identity Check and Originals Receipt may run concurrently.
# Document Check cannot start before Originals Receipt.
# Assessment waits for Identity Check and Document Check.
idc_o = make_activity("Identity_Check")
orig = make_activity("Originals_Receipt")
doc_o = make_activity("Document_Check")
assess_o = make_activity("Assessment")

originals_stage = make_spo(
    [idc_o, orig, doc_o, assess_o],
    {
        idc_o: {assess_o},
        orig: {doc_o},
        doc_o: {assess_o},
    },
)

verification_choice = make_choice([digital_stage, originals_stage])

# Post-assessment branch:
# Direct decision path.
approve_direct = make_activity("Approve")
reject_direct = make_activity("Reject")
direct_path = make_choice([approve_direct, reject_direct])

# Legal-review path with supplement loop.
legal = make_activity("Legal_Review")
supp = make_activity("Request_Supplement")
doc_rep = make_activity("Document_Check_Repeat")
assess_rep = make_activity("Assessment_Repeat")
legal_rep = make_activity("Legal_Review_Repeat")

approve_legal = make_activity("Approve")
reject_legal = make_activity("Reject")

legal_exit = make_choice([approve_legal, reject_legal])

# One loop body:
# Request Supplement -> Document Check repeat -> Assessment repeat -> Legal Review repeat.
# The loop exit is the final legal decision.
legal_cycle = make_spo(
    [supp, doc_rep, assess_rep, legal_rep],
    {
        supp: {doc_rep},
        doc_rep: {assess_rep},
        assess_rep: {legal_rep},
    },
)

legal_loop = make_loop(legal_cycle, legal_exit)

if legal_loop is not None:
    legal_path = make_spo(
        [legal, legal_loop],
        {
            legal: {legal_loop},
        },
    )
else:
    # Fallback: one-iteration supplement path.
    # This is not a true unbounded loop, but it preserves the dependency
    # Legal Review -> Request Supplement -> Document Check repeat ->
    # Assessment repeat -> Legal Review repeat -> decision.
    legal_path = make_spo(
        [legal, supp, doc_rep, assess_rep, legal_rep, approve_legal, reject_legal],
        {
            legal: {supp, approve_legal, reject_legal},
            supp: {doc_rep},
            doc_rep: {assess_rep},
            assess_rep: {legal_rep},
            legal_rep: {supp, approve_legal, reject_legal},
        },
    )

post_assessment_choice = make_choice([direct_path, legal_path])

notify = make_activity("Notify")

try:
    site_b_model = make_spo(
        [reg, verification_choice, post_assessment_choice, notify],
        {
            reg: {verification_choice},
            verification_choice: {post_assessment_choice},
            post_assessment_choice: {notify},
        },
    )
except Exception as exc:
    print(
        "Composite top-level StrictPartialOrder failed; using flattened but "
        "still concurrent Site B model."
    )
    print("Error:", exc)

    approve_f = make_activity("Approve")
    reject_f = make_activity("Reject")

    site_b_model = make_spo(
        [
            reg,
            idc_o,
            orig,
            doc_o,
            assess_o,
            legal,
            approve_f,
            reject_f,
            notify,
        ],
        {
            reg: {idc_o, orig},
            idc_o: {assess_o},
            orig: {doc_o},
            doc_o: {assess_o},
            assess_o: {legal, approve_f, reject_f},
            legal: {approve_f, reject_f},
            approve_f: {notify},
            reject_f: {notify},
        },
    )

print("\n=== Site B candidate ===")
print("Model type:", type(site_b_model).__name__)

try:
    net_b = convert_to_petri(site_b_model)
    print("Workflow net:", check_workflow_net(net_b))
    print("Sound:", check_soundness(net_b))
    print("Sample traces:", play_out_traces(net_b, max_traces=3))
except Exception as exc:
    print("Could not convert Site B POWL model to Petri net:", exc)

## 9. Recommendation

Recommendation: conditional go.

Transfer the mechanism, not the Site A rollout. The transportable idea is to remove unnecessary serialization only where the partial order permits it: for digital/no-originals cases, start Identity Check and Document Check concurrently; for originals-required cases, start Identity Check concurrently with Originals Receipt, but do not start Document Check before legal receipt. This preserves statutory prerequisites while reducing the join time before Assessment. Do not copy the all-team, two-week rollout, the assumption of cross-trained reviewers, the six-task WIP rule, or the 9-hour median reduction. Site A’s estimate is confounded by added document reviewers, an API latency improvement, simpler incoming mix, daily huddles, and improved timestamp recording.

The first subgroup should be non-legal, medium/low-complexity digital claims with no originals requirement. This stratum has the cleanest structural transfer: the two verification tasks are both independent and legally startable, the document pool has reasonable capacity, and legal-review loops cannot amplify the change. Next stage should add originals-required non-legal cases only for Identity Check and Originals Receipt concurrency, with Document Check still gated by receipt. Legal/high-value cases should enter last, with supplement-loop caps and legal-capacity monitoring.

Expected benefits are modest: median cycle-time reduction of roughly 1–3 hours overall and 2–4 hours in eligible digital cases, mainly through reduced waiting before Assessment. Expected risks are queue shift to Assessment/Legal, higher rework if back-entry or lock workarounds create duplicate events, and worse p90 if external originals delay or legal capacity becomes the binding constraint.

Expansion should require: true overlap in the eligible stratum, lower waiting_before_assessment, no increase in rework or appeals, and no worsening of p90 after controlling for mix. Modification is triggered if median improves but p90 worsens, or if document/assessment queues saturate. Abandonment is triggered if overlap is only timestamp artifact, case locks force serialization, or supplement loops increase legal backlog.

## 10. Assumption register

1. `The partial-order join before Assessment is the main target mechanism — parallelization only matters if Assessment waits for both identity and document verification — test by measuring waiting_before_assessment and task completion timestamps — if false, the intervention may create parallel tasks without reducing the critical join.`

2. `Site A's 9-hour median reduction is not a pure treatment effect — before/after changes are confounded by mix, capacity, API, huddles, and timestamp recording — test by decomposing service time, waiting, mix, and overlap quality — if false, Site B may overestimate achievable gains and design an overly aggressive rollout.`

3. `Originals-required cases cannot have full verification parallelization — legal receipt precedes Document Check for 62% of Site B cases — test by auditing Document Check start times relative to Originals Receipt — if false, statutory violations or invalid task creation may occur.`

4. `Digital/no-originals cases are the best initial transport segment — they most closely match Site A's high digital share and independent verification structure — test by comparing Tier 1 pilot outcomes with Site A mechanism paths — if false, the pilot may show no benefit even when implementation is correct.`

5. `Role specialization limits absorptive capacity — Site B cannot shift identity reviewers to document or legal work as Site A could — test by monitoring queue lengths and utilization by role — if false, parallelization may overload one pool while another idles.`

6. `Legacy case locks threaten true concurrency — intended parallel tasks may be serialized or back-entered — test by comparing logged overlap with task ownership, lock overrides, and manual observation — if false, observed overlap may be an artifact and operational harm may be hidden.`

7. `Legal supplement loops can turn early completion into later congestion — faster verification can increase Assessment and Legal Review demand — test by measuring legal queue length, supplement count, and p90 by legal status — if false, average cycle time may improve while tail latency and rework worsen.`

8. `Transportability requires segment-specific evidence, not a single site-level effect — Site A and Site B differ in mix, legislation, capacity, and IT constraints — test by estimating effects within digital, originals-required, non-legal, and legal/high-value strata — if false, a site-wide average may mask harmful subgroup effects.`
