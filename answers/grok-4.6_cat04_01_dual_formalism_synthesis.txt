# 1. Assumptions and Interpretation

1. **Risk route.** “Low risk” versus “high risk” is a pure exclusive choice after `Analyze Root Cause`. There is no decision activity and no data attribute. Exactly one alternative is taken: either `Supervisor Review`, or the pair `{Quality Review, Regulatory Review}`. A case that performs supervisor review together with either quality or regulatory review is not an intended trace.

2. **Optional supplier consultation.** `Consult Supplier` is an XOR against a silent skip: it occurs zero or one time. The choice stands for missing case data (“supplier involved”). If the skip is taken, no supplier event appears. If the activity is taken, it is enabled only after `Analyze Root Cause` and must complete before `Release Material`.

3. **Passing inspection.** Inspection outcome is not a label. A passing inspection is the *exit* of a loop whose do-part is `Execute Trial → Inspect Trial` and whose redo-part is `Adjust Parameters`. A failing inspection is a redo: `Adjust Parameters` and then another do-part. Consequently `Adjust Parameters` cannot follow the last (accepted) `Inspect Trial`, and a passing inspection is never an extra visible event.

4. **Duplicate visible labels.** Not permitted. Every business activity appears once as a labeled node. Duplication would make frequency, waiting-time, and alignment diagnostics count the same step twice, so it is rejected even where it could encode more interleavings.

5. **Process-tree approximation policy.** Conservative under-approximation of the intended *completion* language. Every hard precedence, exclusive choice, “at most once” bound, obligatory trial, and release synchronization is preserved. The tree therefore never admits a completion sequence that the narrative forbids. What it sacrifices is *early enablement*: `Supervisor Review` / `{Quality Review, Regulatory Review}` and `Consult Supplier` become enabled only after *both* `Plan Verification` and `Design Corrective Action`, not immediately after `Analyze Root Cause`. A permissive tree (putting the trial in the same AND as planning/design) would allow `Execute Trial` before its work products exist and is rejected. A duplication-based tree is rejected by assumption 4.

6. **Meaning of “may overlap”.** Activities are recorded at completion and are treated as atomic. “May overlap” means *no happens-before constraint* between those completion events, i.e. every interleaving of the unconstrained completions is allowed. Interval-true concurrency (start/complete life-cycle transitions) is not modeled.

7. **Start/end.** Every case begins with `Register Deviation` and ends with `Archive Case`. No activity is enabled before registration.

8. **Atomicity of nested blocks.** In both models, XOR and LOOP are single control-flow nodes. The silent skip of the supplier XOR is an invisible move; it does not add a visible constraint beyond “the supplier alternative, if chosen, is the skip.”

9. **No inclusive OR.** High-risk review is a mandatory pair, not an optional combination of quality and regulatory review.

# 2. Intended Dependency Specification

| ID | Source activity or submodel | Target activity or submodel | Relation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | (start) | Register Deviation | precedence / source | Every case starts here. |
| R2 | Register Deviation | Contain Material | precedence | Containment is a first work stream after registration. |
| R3 | Register Deviation | Analyze Root Cause | precedence | Root-cause analysis is the other first work stream. |
| R4 | Contain Material | Analyze Root Cause | concurrency | The two streams may proceed independently and overlap. |
| R5 | Contain Material | Plan Verification | precedence | Verification planning waits only for containment. |
| R6 | Analyze Root Cause | Design Corrective Action | precedence | Corrective-action design waits only for root-cause analysis. |
| R7 | Analyze Root Cause | risk-route XOR | precedence + exclusive choice | Route is selected after root-cause analysis. |
| R8 | risk-route (low) | Supervisor Review | exclusive alternative | Low-risk obligation is a single supervisory review. |
| R9 | risk-route (high) | Quality Review ∧ Regulatory Review | exclusive alternative + concurrency | High-risk obligation is both reviews, any completion order. |
| R10 | Analyze Root Cause | Consult Supplier | optional precedence (0..1) | Supplier consult is data-driven, at most once, and cannot start before root-cause analysis. |
| R11 | Consult Supplier | Plan Verification, Design Corrective Action, risk route, trial loop | concurrency | When selected, consultation may overlap planning, design, approvals, and trial work. |
| R12 | Plan Verification ∧ Design Corrective Action | trial loop | synchronization / precedence | Execute Trial waits for both work products; it does *not* wait for approvals or supplier. |
| R13 | risk route | trial loop | concurrency | Approvals may run during the trial cycle. |
| R14 | trial body | Execute Trial → Inspect Trial | sequence | One cycle is execute then inspect. |
| R15 | Inspect Trial (fail) | Adjust Parameters → trial body | loop redo | Failed inspection is redo; parameters are adjusted only between cycles. |
| R16 | Inspect Trial (pass) | (exit loop) | loop exit | Passing inspection ends the trial; Adjust Parameters must not follow it. |
| R17 | passing Inspect Trial ∧ completed risk route ∧ (Consult Supplier if selected) | Release Material | synchronization | Release is the join of trial success, approvals, and optional supplier work. |
| R18 | Consult Supplier | Release Material | precedence if selected | A selected consultation is a release precondition. |
| R19 | Release Material | Archive Case | precedence | Archiving is strictly last. |
| R20 | Archive Case | (end) | sink | Every case ends here. |

# 3. PM4Py Process-Tree Model

Compact expression (PM4Py parse syntax):

```text
->(
  'Register Deviation',
  +(
    ->('Contain Material', 'Plan Verification'),
    ->('Analyze Root Cause', 'Design Corrective Action')
  ),
  +(
    *->('Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial'), 'Adjust Parameters'),
    X('Supervisor Review', +('Quality Review', 'Regulatory Review')),
    X(tau, 'Consult Supplier')
  ),
  'Release Material',
  'Archive Case'
)
```

Mapping: the first sequence child is the mandatory start; the first AND is the two work streams with their *split* successors (`Plan Verification` only under containment, `Design Corrective Action` only under root cause); the second AND is the trial loop in true concurrency with the risk XOR and the optional supplier; the last two sequence children are the release–archive suffix. The join *between* the two AND-blocks is the approximation (see below).

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree, Operator


def act(label):
    return ProcessTree(label=label)


def op(operator, children):
    node = ProcessTree(operator=operator, children=list(children))
    for child in node.children:
        child.parent = node
    return node


rd = act("Register Deviation")
cm = act("Contain Material")
arc = act("Analyze Root Cause")
pv = act("Plan Verification")
dca = act("Design Corrective Action")
sr = act("Supervisor Review")
qr = act("Quality Review")
rr = act("Regulatory Review")
cs = act("Consult Supplier")
et = act("Execute Trial")
it = act("Inspect Trial")
ap = act("Adjust Parameters")
rm = act("Release Material")
ac = act("Archive Case")
tau = ProcessTree(label=None)

contain_then_plan = op(Operator.SEQUENCE, [cm, pv])
analyze_then_design = op(Operator.SEQUENCE, [arc, dca])
initial_streams = op(Operator.PARALLEL, [contain_then_plan, analyze_then_design])

trial_body = op(Operator.SEQUENCE, [et, it])
trial_loop = op(Operator.LOOP, [trial_body, ap])
high_risk = op(Operator.PARALLEL, [qr, rr])
risk_route = op(Operator.XOR, [sr, high_risk])
supplier_choice = op(Operator.XOR, [tau, cs])
mid_overlap = op(Operator.PARALLEL, [trial_loop, risk_route, supplier_choice])

tree = op(Operator.SEQUENCE, [rd, initial_streams, mid_overlap, rm, ac])
```

The tree is exact for start/end, the two initial streams, the split `Contain Material → Plan Verification` and `Analyze Root Cause → Design Corrective Action` dependencies, exclusive risk routing, optional single supplier consultation, the repeat-until trial with `Adjust Parameters` only as redo, overlap of *already enabled* approvals/supplier with the trial, and the release join. It is **not** exact for enablement of the risk route or of `Consult Supplier`: both are structurally placed *after* the AND-join of planning and design, so the tree excludes intended traces in which a review or a supplier consult completes while `Plan Verification` or `Design Corrective Action` is still outstanding. That is a deliberate conservative under-approximation, not an encoding of extra data conditions.

# 4. PM4Py POWL Model

Compact description:

```text
StrictPartialOrder
  nodes:
    RD = Register Deviation
    CM = Contain Material
    ARC = Analyze Root Cause
    PV = Plan Verification
    DCA = Design Corrective Action
    risk = XOR( Supervisor Review,
                StrictPartialOrder{ Quality Review, Regulatory Review } )
    supplier = XOR( τ, Consult Supplier )
    trial = LOOP( StrictPartialOrder{ Execute Trial < Inspect Trial },
                  Adjust Parameters )
    RM = Release Material
    AC = Archive Case
  covering edges:
    RD < CM, RD < ARC
    CM < PV
    ARC < DCA, ARC < risk, ARC < supplier
    PV < trial, DCA < trial
    trial < RM, risk < RM, supplier < RM
    RM < AC
```

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)

rd = Transition(label="Register Deviation")
cm = Transition(label="Contain Material")
arc = Transition(label="Analyze Root Cause")
pv = Transition(label="Plan Verification")
dca = Transition(label="Design Corrective Action")
sr = Transition(label="Supervisor Review")
qr = Transition(label="Quality Review")
rr = Transition(label="Regulatory Review")
cs = Transition(label="Consult Supplier")
et = Transition(label="Execute Trial")
it = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
ap = Transition(label="Adjust Parameters")
rm = Transition(label="Release Material")
ac = Transition(label="Archive Case")
skip_cs = SilentTransition()

high_risk = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[qr, rr])
risk_route = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[sr, high_risk])
supplier_choice = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[skip_cs, cs])

trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[et, it])
trial_body.order.add_edge(et, it)
trial_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[trial_body, ap])

powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        rd,
        cm,
        arc,
        pv,
        dca,
        risk_route,
        supplier_choice,
        trial_loop,
        rm,
        ac,
    ]
)
powl.order.add_edge(rd, cm)
powl.order.add_edge(rd, arc)
powl.order.add_edge(cm, pv)
powl.order.add_edge(arc, dca)
powl.order.add_edge(arc, risk_route)
powl.order.add_edge(arc, supplier_choice)
powl.order.add_edge(pv, trial_loop)
powl.order.add_edge(dca, trial_loop)
powl.order.add_edge(trial_loop, rm)
powl.order.add_edge(risk_route, rm)
powl.order.add_edge(supplier_choice, rm)
powl.order.add_edge(rm, ac)
```

Pairs with no path in either direction are intentionally concurrent completions:

- `Contain Material` vs `Analyze Root Cause`, and therefore vs every ARC-successor that does not later join on that stream (`Design Corrective Action`, the risk route, supplier choice).
- `Plan Verification` vs `Analyze Root Cause`, `Design Corrective Action`, the risk route, and supplier choice (planning depends only on containment).
- `Design Corrective Action` vs `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, the risk route, and supplier choice (design depends only on root-cause analysis; approvals and supplier do not wait for the design artifact).
- the risk-route node vs the trial loop (approvals may overlap the trial).
- the supplier-choice node vs planning, design, the risk route, and the trial loop (consultation may overlap all of them).
- `Quality Review` vs `Regulatory Review` inside the high-risk suborder (either completion order).

Every *connected* covering pair above is a hard narrative obligation; omitting any of `PV → trial`, `DCA → trial`, `risk → RM`, or `supplier → RM` would under-constrain release or the trial guard.

# 6. Semantic Comparison

| Requirement | Intended semantics | Process tree | POWL | Overconstraint or underconstraint | Business consequence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Two initial work streams | After RD, CM and ARC are concurrent | Exact: top-level AND of the two sequences | Exact: RD≺CM, RD≺ARC, no CM–ARC edge | None | Containment and analysis can be staffed in parallel. |
| Split planning / design dependencies | PV waits only for CM; DCA waits only for ARC | Exact inside the first AND | Exact: CM≺PV, ARC≺DCA only | None in either model for these two edges | Verification can start as soon as material is contained, even if analysis is unfinished. |
| Risk route | XOR after ARC: SR vs QR‖RR | Choice is exact; *enablement* is late (after PV and DCA) | Exact: ARC≺risk, XOR(SR, PO{QR, RR}) | Tree overconstrains enablement; POWL matches | Tree delays supervisory/regulatory work until both artifacts exist; real high-risk cases may sit idle. |
| Approval ‖ trial | Route need not finish before ET; any interleaving until RM | Exact *after* the late join | Exact: no risk–trial edge; both precede RM | Tree overconstrains only the start of approvals, not the overlap with trial | Once planning and design are done, both models allow review during the trial. |
| Optional supplier consult | 0..1, after ARC, before RM, overlapping design/plan/approvals/trial | 0..1 and RM-guard are exact; enablement is late (misses overlap with design/plan) | Exact: ARC≺supplier≺RM, no other edges | Tree overconstrains CS vs PV/DCA; neither underconstrains “at most once” | Tree forbids consulting the supplier while planning/design are still open, which the narrative allows. |
| Repeat-until trial | ET→IT, then exit or AP then repeat; no AP after the passing IT | Exact LOOP(do=ET→IT, redo=AP) | Exact same operator over a two-node PO | None | Failed trials are rework; a passing inspection cannot be followed by parameter tweaking. |
| Release synchronization | RM waits for passing IT, the chosen route, and CS if selected | Exact relative to the nodes the tree actually enables | Exact on the three predecessors of RM | Tree does not under-join; it only shrinks the language feeding the join | Neither model can release unfinished material; the tree simply never reaches some legal pre-release states. |
| Duplicate-label risk | Each activity is a single business step | No duplicated visible labels | No duplicated visible labels | None from duplication; residual risk is silent skips/exits after Petri conversion | Frequency and waiting-time analyses will not double-count reviews or trials. |

# 7. Preferred Representation and Validation Plan

**Canonical representation: the POWL model.** The narrative is a non-block-structured partial order with a local XOR and a local LOOP. POWL encodes those cross-branch edges directly (`CM≺PV`, `ARC≺{DCA, risk, supplier}`, `{PV, DCA}≺trial`, `{trial, risk, supplier}≺RM`) without late AND-joins or duplicated labels. The process tree is retained as a conservative diagnostic projection: any trace it accepts is intended; any intended trace it rejects is a known enablement loss, not an implementation bug.

**Validation plan**

1. **Accepting Petri-net conversion.** `net_t, im_t, fm_t = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)` and `net_p, im_p, fm_p = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl)`. Inspect the nets for the expected silent structure: XOR-skip for CS, LOOP-exit tau, and *no* extra visible copies of the 14 labels.

2. **Soundness.** `pm4py.check_soundness(net_t, im_t, fm_t, print_diagnostics=True)` and the same for the POWL net. Require option-to-complete, proper completion, and no dead transitions other than those justified by mutually exclusive XOR branches (a dead `Consult Supplier` would be a defect; a live skip tau is expected).

3. **Bounded / sampled playout.** `pm4py.play_out(powl, no_traces=500)` and the same for `tree` (and/or the converted nets). Record whether A1–A4 appear in both samples and whether A5 appears only in the POWL sample. Treat absence from a finite sample as inconclusive, not as a counterexample.

4. **Explicit positive and negative tests.** Build an event log with the eight witnesses. Align each trace to both nets (`pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_alignments` or the alignments algorithm). Expected fitness 1.0: A1–A4 on both models, A5 on POWL only. Expected fitness < 1.0: F1–F3 on both, A5 on the tree. Confirm that the first non-synchronous move of F1 is PV, of F2 is ET, and of F3 is RM (or a model move of the missing RR/SR).

5. **Silent behavior and duplicated labels.** (i) Collect the multiset of *visible* transition labels in each net; assert it equals the 14 activity names, each once. (ii) Align A1 (no CS) and A3 (with CS): A1 must use exactly one model-move on the supplier-skip tau and no move on `Consult Supplier`; A3 the reverse. (iii) Align A4 and check that AP is a synchronous move only *between* the two ET–IT pairs, never after the last IT. (iv) Replay a crafted trace that repeats `Consult Supplier`; both models must reject it.

6. **Limitations of the plan.** Soundness of a converted net is not fidelity to the narrative. Finite playout undersamples concurrent interleavings and will miss A5-style witnesses unless they are tested explicitly. Alignments explain deviations with silent moves, so a missing CS can be “fixed” by the skip tau and must be interpreted with the label-multiset check. Conversion may introduce extra silent structure that is not in the POWL/tree source. The plan never reconstructs interval overlap, because both models are completion-atomic by assumption 6. Finally, the tree will *correctly* fail A5; that failure validates the documented under-approximation and must not be “fixed” by loosening the tree.

# 8. Final Self-Critique

1. **Semantic (enablement vs. obligation).** The process tree preserves every release *obligation* but systematically late-binds approvals and supplier consultation. That is safe, yet it changes operational meaning: a quality reviewer cannot legally start, according to the tree, until planning and design have both finished. If this model were used for work-instruction generation rather than for conformance of already-completed logs, it would block intended parallel staffing.

2. **Semantic (choice vs. data).** Encoding risk and supplier involvement as free XOR branches makes the *language* right under assumption 11 of the brief, but it cannot say that two cases with the same unmodeled attributes must take the same branch. Conformance on a log that mixes routes for “the same” material family will look perfectly fitting. The models also accept a high-risk-shaped trace (`QR` then `RR`) even when the real case was classified low-risk and simply over-reviewed.

3. **Silent LOOP/XOR residue after conversion.** Even the POWL source, which I treat as exact on visible completions, will typically become a Petri net with silent loop-exit and XOR-skip transitions. Alignments can spend those taus to mask a missing `Adjust Parameters`/`Consult Supplier` or to shuffle the apparent moment of the risk decision. The validation plan detects some of those tricks, but it does not give a tau-free observable semantics, and I did not execute the constructors against a live PM4Py version in this answer.
