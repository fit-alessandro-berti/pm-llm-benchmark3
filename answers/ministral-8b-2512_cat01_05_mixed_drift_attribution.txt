## 9. Provisional Attribution

### Strongest Conclusions:
1. **System migration (March)** caused **logging/semantic drift** (evidenced by `Eligibility Screen` emergence and missing resources). This is **not behavioral drift** but a **provenance issue** recoverable via label mapping.
2. **Supplier Z disruption (May–June)** drove **performance drift** (higher rework rates) and **population drift** (non-random case mix). The spike in `Request Info` loops for `Z` cases is **directly attributable** to incomplete documents.
3. **Staffing cuts (June)** exacerbated **queueing delays** (inferred from `cycle_time` stagnation despite modest `Assess` service time increases). The **redistribution of work** likely increased `Senior Assessment` usage without fully offsetting delays.

### Plausible but Less Strong:
4. **Priority rule (July)** introduced **control-flow redistribution**, reducing waiting for high-complexity cases but **increasing it for standard cases** (not fully visible in aggregates). The **interaction with staffing cuts** may have limited its effectiveness.
5. **Seasonal renewal (October)** amplified **population drift** (higher complexity) and **behavioral drift** (more `Senior` cases). The **priority rule** may have partially mitigated delays for high-complexity cases.

### Mediation/Interaction Issues:
- **Supplier Z + Staffing Cuts**: The **combined effect** of incomplete documents and reduced capacity created a **feedback loop** of rework and delays. December improvements (overtime + campaigns) suggest **intervention** broke this loop.
- **Priority Rule + Seasonality**: The rule’s impact is **confounded** by seasonal load and staffing constraints. Without pre-July data, we cannot isolate its effect.

### Weakest Conclusions:
- **Persistent behavioral adaptation** (e.g., rework rates not fully recovering) may reflect **residual supplier effects** or **staffing constraints**. December data suggests **partial recovery**, but long-term trends are unclear.
- **Measurement missingness** (March) is **isolated to schema artifacts** and does not imply broader process drift.

### What Would Revise the Account:
- **Pre-migration logs** to confirm baseline DFG stability.
- **Resource-level data** to distinguish random vs. systematic missingness.
- **Priority rule logs** (pre-July) to isolate its effect from seasonality.
