## 2. Competing explanations and timeline

**Jan-Mar: stable baseline.** Cases 3,200-3,410, 100% mature, observed 0.18-0.19 vs predicted 0.18-0.19, AUROC 0.81-0.82, Brier ~0.13, ECE 0.022-0.025, Unknown 0.3% Missing 1%, variants 45/35/20 stable, `Order Part->Part Received` 28-35h, V-conditional breach stable (V1 ~0.15-0.16, V2 0.10-0.11, V3 0.30-0.32). This is the only clean training reference.

**April: schema/pipeline incident, not real drift.** 1 Apr migration renames prediction-time event; pipeline misses it. Prefixes built at wrong event or dropped, duplicates + `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY`. Result: 18% Unmapped, Unknown 17%, Missing 14%, mean predicted 0.28 (+9pp overprediction), ECE 0.11, AUROC 0.67. Crucial triangulation: overall observed breach stays 0.19 and performance-DFG stays 34h — process did not get worse, predictions got worse. Treating Apr as concept drift would trigger pointless retraining on corrupt features.

**May: patched but not repaired.** 6 May mapping patch + backfill restores most labels (Unknown 2%, Unmapped 1%) and AUROC to 0.78, but cached “events before diagnosis” not recomputed and pre-20 Apr duplicates remain. Hence Brier 0.146, ECE 0.050 still 2× baseline. Partial recovery proves pipeline cause; residual proves incomplete lineage repair. May usable only after full recompute.

**June: real intentional process/covariate mix change, no concept drift.** New remote-routing policy: V2 34%->52%, V1 43%->30%. Mechanically overall breach falls to 0.15 because V2 is lowest-risk (0.09). Model trained on 35% V2 overpredicts (0.20, ECE 0.060) but discrimination intact (0.79). V-conditional breaches flat, Remote Fix edge 3.8-4.6h flat, data quality clean. This is textbook covariate/process-mix shift: P(X) changed, P(Y|X) did not. Recalibration/reweighting suffices; full retraining as if model broke would be overreaction and would learn the policy as if permanent without a policy flag.

**July: transition — early performance/concept warning.** V3 share 22% (normal) but V3 breach jumps to 0.46 and edge time to 65h (+55% vs Jun). Overall breach 0.19 masked by V2-heavy mix; AUROC slips to 0.76, Brier 0.151. Supplier issue starts 10 Jul. This is the falsification of “June miscalibration will self-correct”: same-path outcome worsened.

**August-September: overlapping severe supplier-driven performance + concept drift plus (Sep) population shift.** Aug has no pipeline or maturity excuse (Unknown 0.7% Missing 2% maturity 93%) yet observed 0.28, ECE 0.12, AUROC 0.68, edge 130h, V3 breach 0.64, V3 share 35%. This is genuine: longer waits cause more breaches *and* breach probability conditional on V3 rises beyond what wait alone predicted historically. Sep adds second layer: Partner Z 24%, 58% V3, missing rural/device-age features, edge 148h, V3 42%, observed 0.31 ECE 0.14. Sep’s 0.31 is real but confounded — 82% mature, Z slower to mature, so Z-vs-other gap and absolute level will shift as vintages mature. Attributing all of Sep to Z, or all to supplier, both fail: Aug already severe with 0% Z; Sep non-Z V3 36% still elevated and V3-conditional 0.61 high for all.

**October: label-maturity collapse dominates, uninterpretable as performance.** Backlog + 52d median delay + 55% maturity, longer V3 paths censored first. Observed 0.16 *falls* while predicted 0.23 rises and edge time peaks at 155h — impossible as true improvement. AUROC 0.64/Brier 0.205/ECE 0.13 reflect evaluated-on-available selection bias, not true discrimination. Oct must be maturity-gated, not compared to mature months and never used for retraining yet.

In short: April = bad features, June = different mix, Aug-Sep = worse process + worse conditional outcomes + new people, October = unseen outcomes. One homogeneous “drift” label would prescribe one wrong fix.

## 3. Diagnostic tests

- **Label-maturity curves + fixed-vintage backtesting.** Plot observed breach, AUROC/Brier/ECE vs days-since-case-start by monthly cohort; overlay maturity %. Backtest only cases with 60d (or 90d) follow-up at cutoff. *Expect:* Under censoring hypothesis Oct curve still rising at cutoff and fixed-vintage Oct breach >>0.16 and closer to/above Sep; under true-improvement hypothesis curve plateaus at 0.16 even at full maturity. Aug (93%) should be stable across vintages if real; Sep should revise upward as Z matures if Z-risk real.
- **Recomputation after activity-label normalization + duplicate cleaning.** Normalize `Diagnostic Assessment Completed` -> `Initial Diagnosis Completed` (both spellings), drop exact duplicates on (case, activity, timestamp) with source-aware rule (apply pre-20 Apr rule retroactively), recompute all derived features (especially cached count-before-diagnosis, queue features), re-derive prefixes strictly after diagnosis, re-score frozen model. *Expect:* Under pipeline hypothesis Apr ECE 0.11->~0.03, AUROC 0.67->~0.80, Unmapped 18%->~0% and Jun-Sep unchanged; under real-drift hypothesis Apr metrics stay degraded after repair.
- **Calibration and discrimination stratified by variant × source × month.** Reliability curves, ECE, AUROC/Brier per V1/V2/V3 and Z/non-Z per month; report predicted vs observed gap within strata. *Expect:* Under pure covariate hypothesis Jun overall miscalibration disappears within strata (V1/V2/V3 calibrated) but overall miscalibrated; under concept hypothesis Aug-Sep V3 miscalibrated *within* V3 (predicted ~0.19 vs observed 0.64) while V1/V2 stay calibrated.
- **Process-variant + DFG / performance-DFG comparison (case-start vs event-calendar cohorts).** Monthly `get_variants`, `discover_dfg` (counts) and `discover_performance_dfg` (median/mean edge times), especially `Order Part->Part Received` and diagnosis->`Remote Fix`. Use case-start cohort (all cases started in month, Vintage-controlled) for outcome linkage and event-calendar cohort (all events in month) for operational load; note calendar mixes vintages. *Expect:* Under real process drift Jun V2 edge-frequency jump persists in both cohorts and Remote Fix time flat; Apr Unmapped edges vanish after normalization (proving artifact). Under supplier hypothesis parts-edge time distribution shifts right from Jul with growing variance, while non-parts edges flat.
- **Test whether outcome relationship conditional on same path changed.** Compare matured breach conditional on (variant, parts-wait bin, triage/queue bin) over time; logistic model `breach ~ variant + wait + month + variant×month + wait×month`; or delay-standardized expected breach (apply Jan-Mar wait-specific rates to Jul-Sep waits). *Expect:* Under pure delay-mediated effect, month effect 0 after conditioning on wait; under concept drift, Aug-Sep month/V3×month odds ratios stay >1 even within same wait bin — threshold/SLA became less forgiving or unmeasured severity rose.
- **Population reweighting / transport-style checks for Partner Z.** (a) Non-Z-only Sep trend vs Aug; (b) reweight Sep to Jan-Mar joint distribution of (variant, queue, diagnosis features) via IPS/standardization; (c) Z vs non-Z within V3 and within wait bins; (d) add rural distance/device age if logged, test incremental AUROC/lift. *Expect:* Under pure population-shift hypothesis reweighted Sep breach falls to ~0.19-0.20 and Z-gap persists within strata; under supplier-concept hypothesis non-Z Sep  overall Sep and reweighted gap remains large, Z-gap collapses after V3/wait stratification.
- **Feature missingness + lineage audit.** Monthly missingness by feature (queue, counts, diagnosis-derived), lineage of which source/patch version produced each feature, prefix-event correctness rate (was prefix timestamp == true diagnosis?), duplicate rate, `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` rate. Correlate missingness with error (residual = predicted – outcome). *Expect:* Under pipeline hypothesis Apr-May errors concentrate in Unknown/missing/incorrect-prefix cases and vanish post-repair; under genuine drift errors spread evenly across complete-data cases in Aug-Sep.

## 4. Recalibration-versus-retraining decision

**Stage 0 — Immediate containment (now, no refit):** Freeze automated retraining. Quarantine Apr (and Oct, Sep) from training/eval. Publish maturity-gated dashboards; flag predictions for V3 / long-wait / Partner-Z cases as low-confidence. Route operationally: expedite parts, adjust promise dates, add manual review for V3 — model fix alone cannot fix supplier delay. Fix pipeline first because any recalibration on corrupt Apr features bakes in bug.

**Stage 1 — Short-term recalibration (once pipeline repaired, before retraining):** After normalization+recompute, refit *calibration only* (Platt/isotonic or temperature) **per variant** (and ideally per variant×period) on mature data. Jun needs this: overall intercept overpredicts due to V2 mix; variant-specific calibration preserves discrimination (0.79) cheaply. For Aug-Sep, recalibration mitigates ECE but will not restore AUROC (0.68/0.66) because missing drivers (parts-wait regime, Z features) are not in score — recalibration is a bandage to keep thresholds usable while features are built. Do not globally recalibrate on Oct/immature Sep; use fixed-vintage mature cases only.

**Stage 2 — Feature repair + enrichment (prerequisite to retraining):** Fully backfill lineage (counts, queue, prefix timestamps), enforce uniform dedup, add: (i) supplier-regime / actual `Order Part->Part Received` wait or expected wait at prediction time + backlog indicator, (ii) routing-policy flag, (iii) source/Partner-Z flag + rural distance/device age if available, (iv) interaction V3×wait. Validate that V3-conditional lift is captured. Without (i)-(iii), retraining just relearns biased intercept.

**Stage 3 — Full retraining (only when labels mature):**
- **Safe to train (after repair):** Jan-Mar (gold), Jun-Jul (mature, clean; include with variant/policy weights), May (only after recompute), Aug (93% mature — usable with vintage check, weight recent regime).
- **Unsafe until matured/repaired:** Apr until recomputed (exclude or include only repaired version with flag), Sep (82%, Z slow — hold out as validation of transport, train only after 95% mature or with survival weighting), Oct (55% — never train; use only for nowcasting with censoring-aware methods).
- Use time-weighted / regime-aware training (emphasize post-Jul for current regime) + fixed-vintage validation (train on cases with complete follow-up, validate on next vintage). Consider censoring-aware (survival) formulation given 30-50d delays.
- **Segment-specific model justified?** Yes, partially: a V3-specific submodel or variant-interacted model is justified because P(Y|X) diverged by path (V3 0.61-0.64 vs V1/V2 stable) and drivers differ (parts-wait irrelevant for V2). A separate Partner-Z model is *not yet* justified — prefer shared model + source/rural/device-age covariates + segment-specific thresholds, unless transport test shows residual Z effect after enrichment and sufficient mature Z sample. Otherwise fragmentation harms sample size and masks supplier fix.

## 5. Monitoring architecture

Layered monitors with gating so upstream failures suppress downstream alarms:

- **Predictive monitor:** monthly AUROC, Brier, log-loss, PR-AUC on fixed-vintage mature cohort + on available-labels (flagged preliminary). Alert only if maturity-gated.
- **Calibration monitor:** ECE, reliability curves overall + by variant/source/decile, mean predicted vs observed gap.
- **Label-maturity monitor:** % mature, median delay, maturity curves by variant/source, outcome-API backlog depth. *Gate:* if maturity <90% (warn) / <70% (block), suppress predictive/concept alerts, emit “immature — do not retrain” state.
- **Data-quality monitor:** Unknown share, missingness per feature, duplicate rate, prefix-correctness, schema-change detector (new activity strings), lineage version. *Gate:* if Unknown/missing/unmapped spike, route to pipeline incident, suppress concept/covariate retraining alerts.
- **Process-variant monitor:** variant shares, `get_variants` drift, DFG edge frequency deltas (control-flow).
- **Performance-DFG monitor:** median/p90 edge times (`Order Part->Part Received`, diagnosis->Remote Fix/Dispatch), queueing.
- **Population monitor:** source mix (Partner Z %), rural distance/device-age distributions, PSI/KS vs baseline.
- **Intervention/deployment monitor:** log of policy/supplier/source changes (routing policy 1 Jun, supplier 10 Jul, Partner Z 1 Sep, mapping patch 6 May), model version, calibration version, threshold changes; annotate all charts.

**Alert dependencies:** `data-quality alarm  hold concept/covariate alarms, trigger pipeline runbook`; `maturity alarm  hold retraining/recalibration automation, trigger vintage re-evaluation`; `process/population alarm + stable within-strata calibration  trigger reweight/recalibrate, not full retrain`; `within-strata miscalibration + performance-DFG shift  trigger concept/feature-enrichment path`. Every alert links to cohort definition and vintage.

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

# 1. Load + normalize renamed activity BEFORE any prefix/feature logic
df = pd.read_csv("field_service_events.csv", parse_dates=["timestamp"])
# audit raw quality
print(df["activity"].value_counts(dropna=False).head(20))

LABEL_MAP = {
    "Diagnostic Assessment Completed": "Initial Diagnosis Completed",
    "UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY": None  # flag, don't silently drop
}
df["activity_raw"] = df["activity"]
df["activity"] = df["activity"].replace(
    {k: v for k, v in LABEL_MAP.items() if v is not None}
)
df["is_unknown"] = df["activity_raw"] == "UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY"

# 2. Duplicate-handling: sort then dedupe; retro-apply post-20-Apr rule to all
df = df.sort_values(["case_id", "timestamp", "activity_raw"])
df_dedup = df.drop_duplicates(
    subset=["case_id", "activity_raw", "timestamp"], keep="first"
)
# keep audit trail of removed dups
n_removed = len(df) - len(df_dedup)
print(f"duplicates removed: {n_removed}")

# 3. Format for pm4py AFTER normalization/dedup
log = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    df_dedup, case_id="case_id",
    activity_key="activity", timestamp_key="timestamp"
)

# 4. Two cohorts with different interpretations
# case-start cohort: cases STARTED in month M (for outcome linkage)
# event-calendar cohort: events OCCURRING in month M (for load/ops view)
def monthly_summaries(log, month_start: str):
    m_start = pd.to_datetime(month_start)
    m_end = (m_start + pd.offsets.MonthEnd(0)).normalize() + pd.Timedelta(days=1) - pd.Timedelta(seconds=1)
    # calendar: events in month
    cal = pm4py.filter_time_range(log, str(m_start), str(m_end), mode="events")
    # case-start: traces contained/started in month (use contained for vintage clarity)
    started = pm4py.filter_time_range(log, str(m_start), str(m_end), mode="traces_contained")
    out = {"month": month_start,
           "n_events_cal": len(cal), "n_cases_cal": cal["case_id"].nunique(),
           "n_cases_started": started["case_id"].nunique(),
           "unknown_share": float(cal["is_unknown"].mean()) if len(cal) else None,
           "missing_queue": float(cal["queue_feature"].isna().mean()) if "queue_feature" in cal else None}
    for name, sub in [("cal", cal), ("started", started)]:
        if len(sub) == 0:
            continue
        dfg = pm4py.discover_dfg(sub)  # control-flow frequencies
        perf = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(sub)  # median/mean edge times
        variants = pm4py.get_variants(sub)  # variant distribution
        out[f"{name}_dfg_OrderPart_PartReceived_count"] = dfg[0].get(("Order Part", "Part Received"), 0)
        out[f"{name}_perf_OrderPart_PartReceived"] = perf[0].get(("Order Part", "Part Received"), {}).get("median", None)
        out[f"{name}_n_variants"] = len(variants)
        # temporal features for audit (not for modelling here)
        tmp = pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(sub)
        out[f"{name}_tmp_cols"] = list(tmp.columns)[:5]
    return out

months = pd.date_range("2026-01-01", "2026-10-01", freq="MS").strftime("%Y-%m-%d")
summary = pd.DataFrame([monthly_summaries(log, m) for m in months])
print(summary.to_string())
# NOTE: returns auditable counts/times/variant stats only; no drift detector, no model fitting.
# Interpret 'started' for breach linkage (needs maturity lag) and 'cal' for ops load.
```

## 6. Risks of acting on the wrong diagnosis

- **Retraining on immature labels (Oct/Sep as if mature).** *Failure:* model learns that Oct low observed 0.16 = improvement and that long V3 paths are safe, because censored breaches are treated as non-breaches; AUROC 0.64 baked in, thresholds lowered just as supplier delay peaks, causing mass promise-date misses when labels mature. *Mitigation:* maturity-gated training (fixed-vintage only, e.g., 95% mature), survival/censoring-aware labels, hold out Sep-Oct from fit until matured; monitor maturity curves and backlog before any fit.
- **Calibrating around a pipeline bug (recalibrating April/May without repair).** *Failure:* global Platt scaling learns to offset +9pp overprediction caused by wrong prefixes/missing queue, then systematically underpredicts once labels/features fixed; bug becomes invisible in metrics while root lineage stays corrupt. *Mitigation:* lineage-first rule — data-quality gate blocks recalibration; require normalization+dedup+recompute and demonstration that Apr ECE/AUROC recover with frozen model before any recalibration.
- **Ignoring genuine supplier-driven concept drift (treating Aug-Sep as transient noise or pure mix).** *Failure:* reweighting or Jun-style recalibration keeps discrimination at 0.66-0.68, V3 cases massively under-scored (0.19 vs 0.64), SLAs and staffing unchanged while parts waits triple; continued breaches + contractual penalties. *Mitigation:* within-variant conditioning test + performance-DFG alert forces feature enrichment (wait-time predictor) and operational response; variant-specific recalibration/thresholds and supplier-regime flag until resolved.
- **Attributing Partner-Z population shift to unfair model behavior (or vice versa) without stratified evidence.** *Failure A:* naively constraining model for parity across Z/non-Z without adjusting for V3/rural/device-age and maturity differences penalizes correct risk signals and hides missing-feature problem. *Failure B:* dismissing Z gap as “just V3” misses genuine transport failure from unseen rural/device-age features. *Mitigation:* stratified calibration/discrimination by source×variant×wait + reweighting/transport analysis on mature vintages only; add rural/device-age features, use segment-specific thresholds, and report Z-gap with maturity disclaimer; involve fairness review only after confounders ruled out.
