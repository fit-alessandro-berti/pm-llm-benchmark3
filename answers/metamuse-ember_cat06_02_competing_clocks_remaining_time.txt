QA cannot start until join time, defined as max(chemistry usable result, microbiology usable result). Terminal time is QA completion plus 0.25-0.75h for release or 0.5-1.0h for rejection recording. Durations are not summed across parallel branches.

**Smooth path.** Chemistry: analysts free Friday 15:54-16:24 and 16:42-17:30. Final: Electronic Release Monday evening centrally, Tuesday in the tail.

**Chemistry rerun.** Chemistry initial result is Monday 09:00-16:00 as in the smooth path. Rerun takes 1-3h with priority: if the initial result is Monday morning, rerun finishes Monday midday; if initial is Monday afternoon, rerun finishes Monday evening or pauses to Tuesday 08:00-09:30. Microbiology is already usable Monday 08:30-09:30 unless contamination also occurs, so join equals rerun completion. QA then starts Monday afternoon or Tuesday morning; a Monday-afternoon rerun finish often misses productive QA time before 20:00, pushing release to Tuesday. Final terminal is Monday evening at the earliest favourable end, Tuesday afternoon centrally, Wednesday if a query or documentation deviation is also triggered.

**Delayed microbiology readout or confirmation.** Baseline readout delay: primary ready Sunday 12:00-20:00, but routine microbiology staffing resumes Monday 08:00, so result Monday 08:30-09:30.
