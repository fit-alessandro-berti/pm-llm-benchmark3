## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Process specification:**
- **Eligibility rules**: Digital claims only (38% of volume); exclude legal-review cases (60% of digital claims); exclude high-complexity cases (48% of volume). Final eligible share: ~23%.
- **Concurrent tasks**: `Identity Check` and `Document Check` may be created simultaneously after `Register`. `Originals Receipt` remains sequential if required.
- **Statutory prerequisites**: `Document Check` cannot begin until `Originals Receipt` is complete for originals-required cases (excluded here).
- **Queue and WIP controls**: Limit to 4 open verification tasks per reviewer; daily queue huddles to prioritize cases.
- **Resource responsibilities**: Cross-train identity and document reviewers for eligible cases; assessors remain specialized.
- **Legal-review and supplement loops**: Excluded from parallelization; handled sequentially.
- **Logging requirements**: Automated timestamps for all task start/end events; manual back-entry prohibited.

**POWL-style description:**
```text
strict_partial_order = {
    Register -> Identity Check,
    Register -> Document Check,
    Identity Check -> Assessment,
    Document Check -> Assessment,
    Assessment -> (Legal Review | Approve | Reject),
    Legal Review -> (Request Supplement | Approve | Reject),
    Request Supplement -> Document Check
}

optional_parallel = {
    (Identity Check, Document Check)  # Only for digital, non-legal cases
}
```

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

| design element               | specification                                                                 | causal purpose                                                                 | operational safeguard                                                                 | process-mining measurement                                                                 |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

| Randomization                | Blocked by team; 50% treatment, 50% control                                  | Control for team-level confounders                                            | Balance case mix across teams                                                        | Team-level cycle time and rework rates                                                   |
| Eligible strata              | Digital claims, no legal review, low complexity                              | Ensure transportability                                                       | Exclude high-risk cases                                                              | Stratum-specific cycle time and rework                                                   |
| Treatment versions           | Parallel (treatment) vs. sequential (control)                                | Test parallelization effect                                                   | None                                                                                 | Overlap percentage and waiting time before assessment                                    |
| Primary outcome              | Median cycle time                                                            | Direct transportability                                                       | Monitor for assessment bottleneck                                                    | Case-level cycle time                                                                    |
| Secondary outcomes           | P90 cycle time, rework rate, appeal rate                                     | Unintended consequences                                                       | Stop rules for rework/appeal increases                                               | Rework and appeal rates by stratum                                                       |
| Interference controls        | WIP limits (4 tasks/reviewer); exclude legal-review cases                    | Prevent bottleneck shift                                                      | Monitor assessment queue                                                             | Assessment queue length and waiting time                                                 |
| Power logic                  | 80% power to detect 6h median cycle time reduction (=0.05)                  | Ensure statistical significance                                               | Minimum 400 cases per arm                                                            | Sample size calculation                                                                  |
| Duration                     | 3 months                                                                     | Capture steady-state effects                                                  | None                                                                                 | Weekly cycle time trends                                                                 |
| Stop rules                   | Rework >15% or appeal >6% in treatment arm                                   | Safety                                                                         | Immediate pause for review                                                           | Rework and appeal rates by week                                                          |
| Transportability analysis    | Compare overlap, waiting time, and cycle time by stratum                     | Isolate mechanism failure                                                      | None                                                                                 | Stratum-specific causal diagrams                                                         |

**Explanation:**
The pilot separates mechanism failure (parallelization doesn’t reduce waiting time) from implementation failure (IT locks, role specialization, or queue saturation prevent parallelization) by:
1. **Stratification**: Digital, non-legal, low-complexity cases isolate the parallelization mechanism.
2. **Randomization**: Blocks by team to control for team-level confounders.
3. **Operational safeguards**: WIP limits and stop rules prevent bottleneck shifts or quality degradation.
4. **Process mining**: Overlap measurement and waiting time analysis distinguish true parallelization from false overlap.
5. **Secondary outcomes**: Rework and appeal rates detect quality degradation, while assessment queue monitoring detects bottleneck shifts.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree import util as pt_util
from pm4py.objects.process_tree import obj as pt_obj
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import POWL, StrictPartialOrder, Optional
from pm4py.objects.conversion.powl import converter as powl_converter
from pm4py.objects.conversion.process_tree import converter as pt_converter
from pm4py.simulation.playout import playout
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils import check_soundness, check_is_workflow_net

# Site A before: sequential
tree_a_before = pt_util.parse("->(Register, Identity_Check, Document_Check, Assessment, X(Append, Reject), Notify)")
net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pt_converter.apply(tree_a_before, variant=pt_converter.Variants.TO_PETRI_NET)
print("Site A before is workflow net:", check_is_workflow_net(net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before))

# Site A after: parallel
tree_a_after = pt_util.parse("->(Register, +(Identity_Check, Document_Check), Assessment, X(Append, Reject), Notify)")
net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pt_converter.apply(tree_a_after, variant=pt_converter.Variants.TO_PETRI_NET)
print("Site A after is workflow net:", check_is_workflow_net(net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after))

# Site B candidate: POWL model
register = POWL("Register")
identity_check = POWL("Identity_Check")
document_check = POWL("Document_Check")
originals_receipt = POWL("Originals_Receipt")
assessment = POWL("Assessment")
legal_review = POWL("Legal_Review")
request_supplement = POWL("Request_Supplement")
approve = POWL("Append")
reject = POWL("Reject")
notify = POWL("Notify")

# Strict partial order for mandatory dependencies
strict_order = StrictPartialOrder()
strict_order.add_edge(register, identity_check)
strict_order.add_edge(register, originals_receipt)
strict_order.add_edge(identity_check, assessment)
strict_order.add_edge(originals_receipt, document_check)
strict_order.add_edge(document_check, assessment)
strict_order.add_edge(assessment, legal_review)
strict_order.add_edge(assessment, approve)
strict_order.add_edge(assessment, reject)
strict_order.add_edge(legal_review, request_supplement)
strict_order.add_edge(legal_review, approve)
strict_order.add_edge(legal_review, reject)
strict_order.add_edge(request_supplement, document_check)
strict_order.add_edge(approve, notify)
strict_order.add_edge(reject, notify)

# Optional parallelization for digital claims
optional_parallel = Optional()
optional_parallel.add_child(identity_check)
optional_parallel.add_child(document_check)

# Combine into POWL
powl_model = POWL()
powl_model.add_child(register)
powl_model.add_child(strict_order)
powl_model.add_child(optional_parallel)

# Convert to Petri net
net_b, im_b, fm_b = powl_converter.apply(powl_model)
print("Site B candidate is workflow net:", check_is_workflow_net(net_b, im_b, fm_b))
print("Site B candidate is sound:", check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b))

# Playout sample traces
log = playout.apply(net_b, im_b, fm_b, parameters={playout.Parameters.NO_TRACES: 5})
print("Sample traces:")
for trace in log:
    print([event["concept:name"] for event in trace])

# Limitations
print("\nModel limitations:")
print("- Cannot encode data-dependent legal requirements (e.g., originals required for some cases)")
print("- Cannot model resource capacity or case locks")
print("- Cannot represent durations or queueing dynamics")
```

## 9. Recommendation

**Conditional go**

**What should be transferred:**
Site B should adopt parallelization for **digital, non-legal, low-complexity cases** (approximately 23% of volume). This subgroup mirrors Site A’s conditions (digital documents, no legal review, cross-trained reviewers) and avoids statutory constraints (originals requirements) and operational bottlenecks (legal review, rework loops). The intervention should include:
1. **Process change**: Create `Identity Check` and `Document Check` tasks simultaneously after `Register` for eligible cases.
2. **Queue management**: Implement WIP limits (4 open tasks per reviewer) and daily queue huddles to prevent assessment bottlenecks.
3. **Resource adjustments**: Cross-train identity and document reviewers for eligible cases to enable parallelization.
4. **IT fixes**: Automate timestamp logging to ensure overlap measurement reflects true concurrency.

**What should not be copied:**
- **Originals-required cases**: Statutory constraints block parallelization.
- **Legal-review cases**: Adds a bottleneck; parallelization may worsen rework.
- **High-complexity cases**: Likely to see no benefit or increased rework.
- **Full-scale rollout**: Site A’s intervention was implemented site-wide without controls; Site B should pilot first.

**Subgroup to enter first:**
Digital, non-legal, low-complexity cases (23% of volume). This subgroup is:
- **Legally permissible**: No originals requirements or legal review.
- **Operationally feasible**: Cross-trained reviewers can handle parallel tasks.
- **Low risk**: Minimal rework or appeal risk.

**Expected benefits:**
- **Median cycle time reduction**: 5–9 hours for eligible cases, based on Site A’s 9-hour reduction (scaled for Site B’s higher baseline and role specialization).
- **Tail performance**: P90 cycle time reduction of 5–10 hours for eligible cases.
- **Queue efficiency**: Reduced waiting time before assessment, freeing up assessor capacity.

**Risks:**
- **Bottleneck shift**: Parallelization may overwhelm assessors, worsening tail performance. Mitigate with WIP limits and queue huddles.
- **Quality degradation**: Rushed checks may increase rework or appeals. Mitigate with stop rules (pause if rework >15% or appeals >6%).
- **IT constraints**: Case locking may prevent true parallelization. Mitigate with manual timestamp back-entry or IT modifications.
- **Equity concerns**: Non-eligible cases may see relative delays. Mitigate by monitoring non-eligible case performance.

**Evidence to trigger expansion, modification, or abandonment:**
- **Expansion**: If the pilot shows >6-hour median cycle time reduction with stable rework/appeals, expand to all digital, non-legal cases (38% of volume).
- **Modification**: If rework or appeals increase, exclude high-complexity cases or add quality checks. If assessment becomes a bottleneck, add assessors or segment cases.
- **Abandonment**: If cycle time does not improve or tail performance worsens, abandon parallelization and focus on queue management or IT fixes.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Assumption**: Digital claims at Site B are structurally similar to Site A’s digital claims.
   - **Why needed**: Parallelization benefits depend on document type and complexity.
   - **How to test**: Compare document types, complexity, and rework rates between sites.
   - **Consequence if false**: Parallelization may not reduce cycle time if Site B’s digital claims are more complex.

2. **Assumption**: Cross-training identity and document reviewers is feasible at Site B.
   - **Why needed**: Role specialization may limit parallelization benefits.
   - **How to test**: Pilot cross-training with a small team; measure task completion times.
   - **Consequence if false**: Parallelization benefits may be smaller than expected.

3. **Assumption**: IT case locking can be worked around via manual timestamp back-entry.
   - **Why needed**: True parallelization requires simultaneous task ownership.
   - **How to test**: Audit overlap measurement in pilot; compare to manual timestamps.
   - **Consequence if false**: Overlap may be measured but not executed, reducing benefits.

4. **Assumption**: Assessment capacity is sufficient to handle increased throughput.
   - **Why needed**: Parallelization may shift bottleneck to assessment.
   - **How to test**: Monitor assessment queue length and waiting time in pilot.
   - **Consequence if false**: Tail performance may worsen; assessors may become overwhelmed.

5. **Assumption**: Legal-review cases can be excluded without operational disruption.
   - **Why needed**: Legal review adds a bottleneck; parallelization may not help.
   - **How to test**: Monitor legal-review queue in pilot; ensure exclusion doesn’t delay other cases.
   - **Consequence if false**: Legal-review cases may see relative delays.

6. **Assumption**: Queue huddles and WIP limits will prevent bottleneck shifts.
   - **Why needed**: Site A’s co-interventions may have contributed to cycle time reduction.
   - **How to test**: Compare queue lengths and waiting times with/without huddles in pilot.
   - **Consequence if false**: Assessment bottleneck may worsen, negating parallelization benefits.

7. **Assumption**: Rework and appeal rates are stable or improve with parallelization.
   - **Why needed**: Quality degradation would offset cycle time benefits.
   - **How to test**: Track rework and appeal rates in pilot; compare to baseline.
   - **Consequence if false**: Parallelization may be abandoned due to quality concerns.

8. **Assumption**: Site B’s case mix is stable during the pilot.
   - **Why needed**: Changes in case mix (e.g., more complex cases) could confound results.
   - **How to test**: Monitor case mix weekly; compare to baseline.
   - **Consequence if false**: Results may reflect case mix changes rather than parallelization.
