## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model

Repairing an incomplete OCEL 2.0 dataset requires separating non-negotiable metamodel invariants from defeasible operational expectations. Rather than forcing every object into a closed lifecycle, we classify rules into four epistemic tiers and score candidate edges by weighing multi-modal signals against competing domain explanations.

### Rule classification

1. **Hard invariants (metamodel and physical causality laws):**
   - **H1 — Referential Integrity:** Every `E2O` and `O2O` edge must reference existing IDs in `events` and `objects`; no synthetic events or objects may be invented.
   - **H2 — O2O Directionality and Typing (Rule 8):** `O2O` qualifiers are strictly directional: `Order --contains--> Item`, `Package --packs--> Item`, `Delivery --carries--> Package`, `Invoice --bills--> Order`, and `Payment --settles--> Invoice`. Reversed edges (`PK-7 carries DEL-X`) or schema-skipping shortcuts (`PAY-2 settles ORD-A`) are invalid.
   - **H3 — Temporal Causality:** Along any object's trajectory, event timestamps must follow causal order: `Create Order` $\le$ `Add Item` $\le$ `Pack Item` $\le$ `Dispatch Package` $\le$ `Confirm Delivery`, and `Issue Invoice` $\le$ `Record Payment`.
   - **H4 — Item Containment Uniqueness:** Without an unpack/repack activity, each `Item` belongs to one `Order` (`contains`) and is placed into at most one physical `Package` (`packs`).

2. **Strong defaults (normal operational semantics, defeasible under documented anomalies):**
   - **S1 — Activity Participation Signatures (Rules 1–7):** `Create Order` takes 1 `Order`; `Add Item` takes 1 `Order` + 1 `Item`; `Pack Item` takes 1 `Item` + 1 `Package` (scanner drops yield item-only rows); `Dispatch Package` takes $\ge 1$ `Package` + 1 `Delivery`; `Confirm Delivery` takes $\ge 1$ `Delivery` and/or `Order` (portal carrier loss yields `Order`-only rows); `Issue Invoice` takes 1 `Invoice` + $\ge 1$ `Order`; `Record Payment` takes 1 `Payment` + 1 `Invoice` (unless held as unapplied cash).
   - **S2 — E2O/O2O Co-Evolution:** Activities establishing structural relationships (`Add Item`, `Pack Item`, `Dispatch Package`, `Issue Invoice`, `Record Payment`) normally generate matching `E2O` and `O2O` edges.
   - **S3 — Scanner Session Continuity:** Contiguous scanner tokens within one minute at the same station (`scanner_ref=PK8-1` at `09:04` on `PK-8`, then `scanner_ref=PK8-2` at `09:05` on `ev08`) strongly indicate packing into the same open container.

3. **Weak heuristics (corroborating signals, never decisive alone):**
   - **W1 — Arithmetic Equality (Rule 6):** `ORD-A (130) + ORD-B (100) = INV-44 (230)` and `PAY-1 (100) + PAY-2 (130) = INV-44 (230)` support multi-order billing and split settlement, but fees, taxes, or fragile handling on `GLASS-1` can produce the same sum.
   - **W2 — Degraded Identifier Prefixes (Rule 9):** `external_ref=N-421?` (`ev10`), wildcard `N-42*` (`ev11`), and sequential bank refs `B-771`/`B-772` narrow candidates but carry explicit ambiguity.
   - **W3 — Shared Context and Timestamps (Rule 9):** Shared `customer=C17`, shared `route=NORTH`, and simultaneous `10:00` dispatch timestamps (`ev09`, `ev10`) establish compatibility, not identity.

4. **Evidence against a repair (defeaters and anti-signals):**
   - **A1 — Explicit Uncertainty Markers:** Trailing `?` (`N-421?`) and `*` (`N-42*`) show that the capture system lacked a verified foreign key.
   - **A2 — Legitimate Open Domain States:** Unapplied cash (`Rule 7`), portal carrier loss (`Rule 5`), and fee-bearing single-order invoices (`Rule 6`) explain missing links without data corruption.
   - **A3 — Dual E2O+O2O Absence:** When both `E2O` and `O2O` edges are missing (`ev08`/`PK-8`, `ev10`/`DEL-Y`, `ev12`/`ORD-B`, `ev14`/`INV-44`), neither table independently confirms the other.
   - **A4 — Cross-Order Entanglement:** Packing `IT-B1` (`ORD-B`) into `PK-8` (with `IT-A2` from `ORD-A`) merges two orders into one container and requires strong local evidence (`S3`).

### Evidence combination model

Candidates are scored in $[0,1]$ across seven dimensions: (1) signature/cardinality fit (`S1`, `H2`, `H4`), (2) lifecycle timestamp order (`H3`), (3) identifier match quality (`S3` vs. `W2`/`A1`), (4) temporal proximity, (5) attribute alignment (`customer=C17`, `route=NORTH`, weights, amounts `W1`/`W3`), (6) existing `O2O`/`E2O` corroboration (`S2` vs. `A3`), and (7) competing explanation weight (`A2`, `A4`). Thresholds map scores to `accept` ($\ge 0.80$), `provisional` ($0.55\text{–}0.79$), `unresolved` ($0.25\text{–}0.54$), and `reject` ($< 0.25$).

## 3. Alternative repair bundles

Candidate repairs interact across fulfillment and billing layers and must be evaluated as coherent bundles.

### Bundle A: Conservative / minimum-change repair (Selected Baseline)

Bundle A commits only repairs supported by strong local physical continuity and a documented capture failure mode, preserving source-flagged ambiguities and valid open states:
- **`ev08` and `PK-8`:** **Repaired (`P01`, `P02` accepted).** `scanner_ref=PK8-2` at `09:05` immediately after `PK8-1` (`09:04` on `PK-8`) instantiates Rule 3's scanner-outage pattern, packing `IT-B1` into `PK-8`.
- **`ev10` and `DEL-X`/`DEL-Y`:** **Left unresolved.** Because `external_ref=N-421?` is flagged uncertain and co-occurs with `ev09` at `10:00`, `ev10` stays linked only to `PK-8`, leaving `DEL-Y` as an unreferenced manifest.
- **`ev11` and the delivery objects:** **Left unresolved.** Under Rule 5, wildcard `N-42*` reflects a portal confirmation that lost carrier granularity and legitimately links only to `ORD-A`.
- **`INV-44` and `ORD-B`:** **Left unresolved.** Per Rule 6, `130 + 100 = 230` is not proof; `INV-44` bills `ORD-A` (`130` base + `100` fragile glass freight/tax), consistent with `ORD-B` lacking delivery confirmation.
- **`PAY-2` and `INV-44`:** **Left unresolved.** Per Rule 7, `ev14` (`PAY-2`, `130`) remains a valid unapplied cash receipt at `16:00`.
- *Lifecycle coherence:* All three `Item` objects complete `Add Item -> Pack Item`, and both `Package` objects complete `Pack Item -> Dispatch Package`, while open downstream states on `DEL-Y`, `ORD-B`, and `PAY-2` remain transparent.

### Bundle B: Consolidated customer-episode repair (Plausible Maximal Alternative)

Bundle B interprets the log as a single consolidated episode for customer `C17` on route `NORTH`:
- **`ev08` and `PK-8`:** **Repaired (`P01`, `P02`).** `PK-8` consolidates `IT-A2` (`ORD-A`) and `IT-B1` (`ORD-B`).
- **`ev10` and `DEL-X`/`DEL-Y`:** **Repaired to `DEL-Y` (`P03`, `P04`).** `N-421?` resolves to `DEL-Y` (`N-421`).
- **`ev11` and the delivery objects:** **Repaired to joint delivery confirmation (`P06`, `P07`).** Because `ORD-A` spans `PK-7` (`DEL-X`, `N-420`) and `PK-8` (`DEL-Y`, `N-421`), portal wildcard `N-42*` acknowledges simultaneous arrival of both deliveries at `14:00`.
- **`INV-44` and `ORD-B`:** **Repaired (`P09`, `P10`).** With both packages delivered, `ev12` (`15:00`) bills both `ORD-A` (`130`) and `ORD-B` (`100`) on `INV-44` (`230`).
- **`PAY-2` and `INV-44`:** **Repaired (`P11`, `P12`).** Customer `C17` settles `INV-44` in two transfers matching the order sub-totals (`PAY-1=100` at `15:30`, `PAY-2=130` at `16:00`).
- *Lifecycle coherence:* Every object achieves a closed end-to-end lifecycle with zero orphans, and the physical coupling inside `PK-8` explains why both deliveries, orders, and payments converge on `INV-44`.

### Bundle C: Partial delivery / stalled second shipment alternative

Bundle C explains why `ORD-B` is absent from `ev11` and `ev12`:
- **`ev08` and `PK-8`:** **Repaired (`P01`, `P02`).**
- **`ev10` and `DEL-X`/`DEL-Y`:** **`PK-8` provisionally assigned to `DEL-Y` (`P03`, `P04`), which suffers a transit delay.**
- **`ev11` and the delivery objects:** **Bound uniquely to `DEL-X` (`P06` only; `P07` rejected).** Only `DEL-X` (`N-420`, carrying `PK-7` with `IT-A1` of `ORD-A`) arrived by `14:00`, while `DEL-Y` (`PK-8`, holding `IT-A2` and `ORD-B`'s `IT-B1`) did not.
- **`INV-44` and `ORD-B`:** **Excluded (`P09`, `P10` rejected).** Because `ORD-B` is undelivered, `INV-44` (`230`) bills `ORD-A` only (`130` + `100` express fragile surcharge) and is settled in two installments by `PAY-1` and `PAY-2` (**`P11`, `P12` included**).
- *Lifecycle coherence:* Accounts for `ORD-A`-only confirmation and billing without fabricating completion for `DEL-Y` or `ORD-B`.

## 5. Lifecycle and cardinality audit

Auditing each object type under **Bundle A** (contrasted with Bundle B) shows:

- **`Order` (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`):** `ORD-A` follows a complete 5-event trajectory (`ev01 -> ev03 -> ev04 -> ev11 -> ev12`). `ORD-B` executes `Create Order (ev02) -> Add Item (ev05)` and remains open downstream in Bundle A (closing via `ev12` in Bundle B). Neither is orphaned. `ORD-A` has a valid `1:2` `contains` link to `Item` (`IT-A1`, `IT-A2`). Raw links for `ORD-A` (`IT-A1`, `IT-A2`, `INV-44`) and `ORD-B` (`IT-B1`) have independent `E2O` and `O2O` support, whereas `INV-44 bills ORD-B` (Bundle B) lacks raw edges in both tables.
- **`Item` (`IT-A1`, `IT-A2`, `IT-B1`):** All three execute `Add Item -> Pack Item`, with `1:1` containment in `Order` and `1:1` packing into `Package`. No orphans exist. `IT-A1` and `IT-A2` have dual raw `E2O`/`O2O` support for `Order` and `Package`; `IT-B1` has dual raw support for `ORD-B` (`order_hint=ORD-B`), while its `PK-8` link stems from repairing `ev08`.
- **`Package` (`PK-7`, `PK-8`):** `PK-7` executes `ev06 -> ev09`; under Bundle A, `PK-8` executes `ev07 -> ev08 -> ev10`. Neither is orphaned. Repairing `ev08`/`PK-8` creates an **indirect many-to-many (`M:N`) coupling between `Order` and `Package`**: `ORD-A` splits across two packages (`PK-7`, `PK-8`), while `PK-8` merges items from two orders (`IT-A2` from `ORD-A`, `IT-B1` from `ORD-B`).
- **`Delivery` (`DEL-X`, `DEL-Y`):** `DEL-X` executes `Dispatch Package (ev09)` and has dual raw `E2O`/`O2O` support with `PK-7`. Under Bundle A, **`DEL-Y` remains a complete orphan object** (zero `E2O` and zero `O2O` edges), representing an unverified manifest (`N-421?`). In Bundle B, `DEL-Y` executes `ev10 -> ev11`, and `ev11` becomes a `2:1` synchronization event across `{DEL-X, DEL-Y}`.
- **`Invoice` (`INV-44`):** Executes `Issue Invoice (ev12) -> Record Payment (ev13)` in Bundle A (adding `-> Record Payment (ev14)` in Bundle B). Not orphaned; links to `ORD-A` and `PAY-1` are supported in both raw tables. In Bundle B, `INV-44` becomes a `2:1:2` hub billing 2 `Orders` and settled by 2 `Payments`.
- **`Payment` (`PAY-1`, `PAY-2`):** Each executes one `Record Payment` event (`ev13`, `ev14`), so neither is an event-orphan. Under Bundle A, `PAY-2` is an **`O2O`-isolated object** (no `settles` edge), modeling unapplied cash (`Rule 7`).

### Circular reasoning audit

Three circularity fallacies must be avoided:
1. **`E2O(ev08, PK-8)` $\leftrightarrow$ `O2O(PK-8, packs, IT-B1)` bootstrap circularity:** Inferring `O2O(PK-8, packs, IT-B1)` from `ev08.scanner_ref=PK8-2` and then citing that inferred `O2O` edge as "independent structural confirmation" of `E2O(ev08, PK-8)` double-counts one observation.
2. **Invoice–Payment arithmetic circularity:** Using `PAY-1 (100) + PAY-2 (130) = 230` to prove `INV-44` bills `ORD-B (100)`, and simultaneously using `INV-44 bills ORD-B` to prove `PAY-2 (130)` settles `INV-44`, treats mutually dependent conjectures as independent facts.
3. **Cascade conditioning (`ev10 -> DEL-Y` $\Rightarrow$ `ev11 -> DEL-Y`):** Linking `ev11` (`ORD-A`) to `DEL-Y` (`P07`) assumes `DEL-Y` carried `ORD-A`'s package `PK-8`, which itself relies on provisional repair `P03`.

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects

Comparing the **Unrepaired Log**, **Bundle A**, and **Bundle B** shows how relation repair alters downstream models:

1. **OC-DFG:**
   - *Unrepaired:* `PK-8` transitions directly `Pack Item (ev07) -> Dispatch Package (ev10)`, bypassing `ev08`; `DEL-X` is a singleton node at `Dispatch Package (ev09)` and `DEL-Y` is absent; `ORD-B` halts at `Add Item (ev05)`; `INV-44` halts at `Record Payment (ev13)`.
   - *Bundle A:* Creates a **`Package` self-loop `Pack Item (ev07) -> Pack Item (ev08)`** and routes `PK-8` through `Pack Item (ev08) -> Dispatch Package (ev10)`, restoring `Item`–`Package` synchronization at `ev08`.
   - *Bundle B:* Adds the `Delivery` edge `Dispatch Package -> Confirm Delivery` with frequency 2 (`DEL-X`: `ev09 -> ev11`; `DEL-Y`: `ev10 -> ev11`), adds `Add Item (ev05) -> Issue Invoice (ev12)` on `Order` (`ORD-B` joining `ORD-A` at `ev12`), and creates an **`Invoice` self-loop `Record Payment (ev13) -> Record Payment (ev14)`** on `INV-44`.

2. **Object Type Graph (OTG):**
   - *Unrepaired:* Splits into 4 disconnected instance components: `{ORD-A, IT-A1, IT-A2, PK-7, PK-8, DEL-X, INV-44, PAY-1}`, `{ORD-B, IT-B1}`, isolated `{DEL-Y}`, and isolated `{PAY-2}`.
   - *Bundle A:* `O2O(PK-8, packs, IT-B1)` bridges `{ORD-B, IT-B1}` into the main component via `PK-8`, leaving `{DEL-Y}` and `{PAY-2}` isolated.
   - *Bundle B:* Connects all 12 objects into a single component via `DEL-Y carries PK-8`, `INV-44 bills ORD-B`, and `PAY-2 settles INV-44`.

3. **ET–OT Graph:**
   - *Unrepaired:* `(Pack Item, Package)`, `(Dispatch Package, Delivery)`, and `(Record Payment, Invoice)` show `0..1` cardinality, while `(Confirm Delivery, Delivery)` is absent (`0..0`).
   - *Bundle A:* Restores `(Pack Item, Package)` to strict `1..1` invariance while keeping `0..1` / `0..0` where uncertainty is real.
   - *Bundle B:* Upgrades `(Dispatch Package, Delivery)` and `(Record Payment, Invoice)` to `1..1`, adds `(Confirm Delivery, Delivery)` at `2..2` (`ev11`), and widens `(Issue Invoice, Order)` from `1..1` to `2..2` (`ev12`).

   - *`Order`:* Unrepaired and Bundle A produce `ORD-A` $\langle\text{ev01}, \text{ev03}, \text{ev04}, \text{ev11}, \text{ev12}\rangle$ and `ORD-B` $\langle\text{ev02}, \text{ev05}\rangle$. Bundle B extends `ORD-B` to $\langle\text{ev02}, \text{ev05}, \text{ev12}\rangle$, **duplicating `ev12` (`Issue Invoice`) across both flattened `Order` traces (convergence)**.
   - *`Package`:* Unrepaired drops `ev08` (`PK-8`: $\langle\text{ev07}, \text{ev10}\rangle$). Bundles A and B recover `ev08` (`PK-8`: $\langle\text{ev07}, \text{ev08}, \text{ev10}\rangle$; `PK-7`: $\langle\text{ev06}, \text{ev09}\rangle$).
   - *`Delivery`:* Unrepaired and Bundle A yield 1 case (`DEL-X`: $\langle\text{ev09}\rangle$; `DEL-Y` empty). Bundle B yields 2 cases (`DEL-X`: $\langle\text{ev09}, \text{ev11}\rangle$; `DEL-Y`: $\langle\text{ev10}, \text{ev11}\rangle$), **duplicating `ev11` (`Confirm Delivery`) across both `Delivery` cases**.
   - *`Invoice`:* Unrepaired and Bundle A yield `INV-44` $\langle\text{ev12}, \text{ev13}\rangle$; Bundle B yields $\langle\text{ev12}, \text{ev13}, \text{ev14}\rangle$.

## 8. Over-repair warnings

Aggressive repair strategies that force completeness onto every ambiguous record introduce at least six specific process-mining distortions in this dataset:

1. **False multi-delivery convergence at `Confirm Delivery` (`ev11` $\to$ `{DEL-X, DEL-Y}`):** Binding wildcard `N-42*` to both deliveries duplicates `ev11` across both `Delivery`-flattened traces, fabricating a 100% on-time 4-hour delivery SLA (`10:00` $\to$ `14:00`) and masking a potentially unconfirmed shipment (`DEL-Y`).
2. **Masking unapplied-cash compliance exceptions (`ev14` $\to$ `INV-44`):** Forcing `PAY-2` (`130`) onto `INV-44` erases the legitimate unapplied-cash state (`Rule 7`), hiding open accounts-receivable suspense balances at `16:00` and creating a synthetic `Record Payment -> Record Payment` loop on `INV-44`.
3. **Spurious multi-order billing synchronization (`ev12` $\to$ `ORD-B`):** Treating `130 + 100 = 230` as deterministic proof links `ORD-B` to `ev12`, duplicating `Issue Invoice` across `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` upon `Order` flattening and fabricating an `Add Item -> Issue Invoice` transition for `ORD-B` that skips delivery confirmation.
4. **Divergence via transitive `O2O`-to-`E2O` propagation (`Order` $\to$ `Pack Item` / `Dispatch Package`):** Propagating `ORD-A` across `O2O` links onto item/package events (`ev06`–`ev10`) causes classic **divergence**: because `ORD-A` has two items in two packages, flattening on `Order` yields false rework loops (`Pack Item -> Pack Item`, `Dispatch Package -> Dispatch Package`).

6. **Metamodel-violating `Payment`-to-`Order` shortcuts (`PAY-1` $\to$ `ORD-B`, `PAY-2` $\to$ `ORD-A`):** Matching payment amounts (`100`, `130`) directly to order values bypasses the `Invoice` intermediary (`Rule 7`) and contradicts the observed link between `PAY-1` (`100`) and `INV-44` (`ORD-A`).
