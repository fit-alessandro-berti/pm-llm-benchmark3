Two mechanisms are shared. Both gate Assessment on identity and document checks, then approve or reject and notify. Independent checks can be partially ordered so the shorter one leaves the critical path. Site A’s overlap rise from 8% to 71%, and the fall in wait before Assessment from 18h to 12h, match that mechanism. Earlier enablement cuts cycle time only when downstream servers have slack.

The rest of Site A’s change is separate. An API cut identity latency, verification FTE rose from 18 to 20, marketing cut the high-complexity share from 48% to 40%, and managers added huddles. All teams switched within two weeks, without randomization or a negative control. The nine-hour median reduction is a mixture, not an identified parallelism effect.

Site B has modifiers Site A lacks. Originals precede Document Check in 62% of cases, median delay 18h. Legal Review covers 40%, and about a quarter of those reviews request a supplement that repeats document checking and assessment. Roles are split, and one owner is locked. Parallel creation need not yield true overlap, and overlap can shift waiting onto assessment (3 FTE) and legal review (2 FTE). Complexity and back-entry change size and sign.

A similar diagram is not transportability. A process tree records label order, not selection nodes that make an order legal, pool slack, the IT state that allows two owners, or Site A’s co-interventions. The same parallelism operator is reachable in Site A’s digital, cross-trained process and often illegal or locked at Site B. The shared piece is narrower: overlap identity with an independent document check or the originals wait, and keep every statutory edge.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

Service-overlap ceiling at A: before the change, median identity plus document service was 3.5 + 7.2 = 10.7h against a parallel maximum of 7.2h, a gap of 3.5h. Afterward the same gap is 3.1 + 6.5 versus 6.5, or 3.1h. That gap is the most that overlapping the shorter check can remove from the critical path when both start together and the longer check stays binding. A’s median cycle time fell by 9h and wait before Assessment by 6h. Both exceed this ceiling, so several hours of the published improvement sit outside a pure sequencing effect. Those hours line up with the concurrent shocks: verification FTE 18 → 20, high-complexity share 48% → 40%, an API latency cut, and new huddles. The transported sequencing effect used below is therefore about 0 to 4h per case where identity truly leaves the critical path, not 9h. The optimistic stratum range of 3 to 5h allows the 3.4h identity median at B plus a small sequential queue, and it is not a portfolio promise.

At B the same arithmetic is: digital sequential service 3.4 + 8.1 = 11.5h versus parallel max 8.1h, delta 3.4h. For originals, if identity currently sits outside the wait, sequential verification-plus-wait is 3.4 + 18 + 8.1 = 29.5h against max(3.4, 18) + 8.1 = 26.1h, delta 3.4h. If identity already runs during the 18h wait, the delta is about 0. Because 62% exceeds half the volume and those cases carry the long external delay, the portfolio median is an originals case unless the joint distribution is surprising. Improving only the digital minority mostly moves cases that are already left of the median.

Elapsed medians cannot be turned into utilization. Identity elapsed time of 3.4h on 3,400 cases would be about 11,600 hours, far above four reviewers’ available time, so the medians include external wait. Slack at assessment and legal review is unmeasured. Scenarios that need slack state that as an assumption. A’s rework and appeal rates moved slightly the wrong way (+0.6 and +0.3 percentage points); no quality gain is carried over. B’s supplement load is 360/1,360 of legal cases, about 11% of all cases, each re-entering document check and assessment.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

Reject the proposal to create Identity Check and Document Check together, immediately after Register, for every case.

Eligibility and concurrency:

- Wave eligibility is an operating rule on top of the target model. The model below is the allowed control flow, not a statement that every branch is offered on day one.
- Every case: create Identity Check after Register. An identity reviewer may execute it immediately.
- Originals not required (digital): create Document Check at the same moment as Identity Check. The two tasks may execute concurrently. A document reviewer owns the document task.
- Originals required: do not create Document Check at Register. Track Originals Receipt concurrently with Identity Check. Create and execute Document Check only after originals are recorded as received.
- Assessment is created only when both Identity Check and Document Check are complete. Assessors do not perform verification.
- Public-sector or high-value cases: after Assessment, Legal Review is mandatory. It is not concurrent with verification.
- Other cases: the legal stage is skipped. The case goes to an exclusive Approve or Reject, then Notify.
- Request Supplement returns the case to Document Check, then Assessment, then Legal Review again. Supplement work is prioritized over new work of the same role. A further physical receipt, if the statute requires it for the supplement, is enforced by the same originals precondition before that repeated Document Check.

Statutory edges that stay mandatory: Register before both verification branches; Originals Receipt before Document Check whenever originals are required; both checks before Assessment; Assessment before Legal Review when legal review is required; decision before Notify.

Queue and WIP, starting values to tune from queue age: identity 8 open tasks, document 14, assessment 6, legal 4, about two per current FTE. Pull, not push. No hiring, no API change, and no new huddle ritual in the same step. Rework and aged cases outrank new arrivals inside a pool so the supplement loop cannot stretch the tail unchecked.

Logging: case attributes `originals_required`, `legal_required`, and complexity band at the point of eligibility; enablement time, real start, real complete, role, and user; a back-entry flag; lock-conflict events. Overlap is the intersection of identity and document intervals on events that are not back-entered. Originals-receipt delay is logged and is not an effect metric.

Target partial order, using `X` for exclusive choice, `*` for a loop whose body runs at least once, and `τ` for a silent skip. Incomparable activities may interleave.

```text
Register ≺ Identity Check
Register ≺ DocumentChannel
Identity Check ≺ Assessment
DocumentChannel ≺ Assessment
Assessment ≺ LegalGate
LegalGate ≺ Decision
Decision ≺ Notify

DocumentChannel = X(
    Document Check,
    Originals Receipt ≺ Document Check
)

LegalGate = X(
    τ,
    *( Legal Review , Request Supplement ≺ Document Check ≺ Assessment )
)

Decision = X( Approve , Reject )

Concurrent when both occur:
Identity Check || digital Document Check
Identity Check || Originals Receipt
Identity Check || Document Check that follows Originals Receipt
```

The data predicates that choose the document channel (62%) and the legal branch (40%) are attributes, not free choices. The pilot may offer the concurrent digital branch only to non-legal digital cases at first, even though the target model allows the other legal combinations.

Later phases open only by the expansion rule, not by calendar enthusiasm. | Separates instrumentation change from the partial-order treatment. | Phase 0 ends only after back-entry and dual-ownership tests pass. No second stratum opens during phase 1. | Conformance of phase-0 traces to the old order; overlap should stay near 6% in phase 0. |
| Eligible strata | Stratum D: originals not required, legal review not required. Stratum O: originals required, legal not required. Stratum L: legal review required. Complexity band is a blocking factor inside D. Joint shares are unknown; plan on D being roughly one fifth to one third. | D is the only stratum with a control flow close to A after the change. O and L are different mechanisms. | Screen attributes before task creation. Mis-classified originals cases are a statutory incident, not a protocol deviation to analyze away. | Discover the realized DFG per stratum and arm. Confirm Document Check never precedes Originals Receipt in O. |
| Treatment versions | Control: current sequential practice. Treatment in D: create both checks after Register. Treatment in O, later: Identity Check beside the receipt wait only. No “both tasks for every case” arm. | Estimates the mechanism that is actually legal, rather than the infeasible copy of A. | System rule blocks Document Check before receipt even if a user requests it. | Trace-level fidelity: prescribed order present, forbidden order absent, true interval overlap on unflagged events. |
| Primary and secondary outcomes | Primary: median hours from Register to Assessment enablement, and median cycle time, inside the randomized stratum. Secondary: p90 cycle time, rework, 90-day appeals, assessment and legal queue age. | Enablement tests the mechanism directly. Cycle time tests whether the gain survives downstream queues. | Appeals are safety endpoints. A median gain does not offset a p90 or appeal breach. | Compare enablement, start, and complete. Report waiting before Assessment split into verification sojourn and assessment queue. |
| Interference controls | Staffing, API, intake rules, and huddles frozen. WIP caps identical for both arms. Watch control cases and untreated strata that share assessors and legal reviewers. | Stops B from recreating A’s co-interventions. Detects reshuffling of a shared queue. | If median assessment wait on control cases rises by ≥3h versus baseline, tighten WIP before any expansion. | Weekly queue-age and WIP by role and by arm; originals delay as a negative control that should not fall. |
| Power or minimum-support logic | Portfolio flow is about 131 cases a week. If D is 25%, a 1:1 draw yields about 16 cases per arm per week. For a 4h shift and a within-D SD near 12h, a normal two-sample calculation needs on the order of 140 completed cases per arm; if SD is nearer 20h, nearer 400. | Avoids stopping on a small DFG fluctuation, and avoids a sample too small to see a 3–5h effect. | Do not expand on fewer than 150 completed cases per arm. Efficacy look is not taken before that support. A 6-week look is safety and fidelity only. | Publish case counts per variant. Do not interpret a DFG built on a thin variant. |
| Duration | Up to 16 weeks of enrollment in stratum D, or until 150 per arm, whichever is later. Appeal endpoint continues to 90 days after decision for cases decided in the first 8 weeks. | Cycle time is observable at completion; appeals are not. | Safety review every 2 weeks can pause enrollment without waiting for 16 weeks. | Time-bounded event logs per review, with the same cohort definitions. |
| Stop rules | Pause if in any week more than 1% of originals-required treated cases start Document Check before receipt. Pause if after 4 weeks fewer than 40% of targeted D cases show true overlap. Stop or modify if treatment rework or appeals exceed control by ≥3pp, or if p90 on treatment or on the legal queue rises by ≥10% versus baseline. | Separates illegal execution and implementation failure from a harmful mechanism. | A pause for low fidelity triggers an IT or training repair, not an automatic conclusion that parallelism cannot work. | Fidelity dashboard from the log: forbidden precedence, overlap on clean timestamps, p90, rework, queue age. |
| Transportability analysis | Estimate effects inside D, O, and L separately. Compare B’s D effect with the 3–5h structural ceiling, not with A’s 9h. Report modifiers: originals delay, legal queue age, lock conflicts, complexity. | Asks whether the shared mechanism survived B’s selection nodes. | Expansion to O requires a successful D estimate plus stable p90 and stable legal queue. L is last. | Stratified cycle-time distributions, conformance to the partial order, and the negative-control series. |

The pilot separates mechanism failure from implementation failure by scoring fidelity and outcome on the same randomized cases, with staffing, huddles, and the registry interface held fixed.

Fidelity asks whether the prescribed partial order occurred. Digital treated cases need overlapping identity and document intervals, both after registration. Originals cases may overlap identity with the receipt wait, but Document Check must not start before receipt. Back-entered events, lock conflicts, WIP breaches, and ineligible task creation are implementation failures. They do not show that parallelism itself is useless.

The mechanism outcome is time from registration to assessment enablement, then cycle time, inside the stratum. High fidelity with no shorter pre-assessment sojourn means the mechanism is not binding: originals delay or a downstream queue dominates. Low fidelity with no cycle-time change is inconclusive; repair the lock or the logging and rerun before judging transport.

Control cases and untreated strata that share assessment and legal staff reveal interference. A gain on treated cases matched by a loss on controls is spillover, not net benefit. Originals-receipt delay is a negative control and must not be credited to the intervention. Site A’s nine-hour shift is not the success bar. Expand only with high fidelity, a stratum cycle-time gain, stable p90, stable rework and appeals, and no legal-queue spillover.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
"""Site A trees and a Site B candidate POWL model.

The Site B net encodes the modified partial order, not the rejected
blanket parallelization. It cannot encode:
- data-dependent statutory choice (originals required on 62% of cases;
  legal review required on 40%), because XOR here is a free choice;
- resource pools, WIP caps, role specialization, or queueing;
- the legacy one-active-owner case lock;
- activity durations, originals-delay distributions, or service-time overlap;
- measurement error from back-entered timestamps.
A sound partial order is not evidence that the effect at Site A transports.
"""

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    OperatorPOWL,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    Transition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def summarize_net(label, net, im, fm):
    print(f"\n== {label} ==")
    try:
        print("workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    except TypeError:
        print("workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net, im, fm))
    try:
        print("sound:", pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm))
    except Exception as exc:
        print("soundness check raised:", type(exc).__name__, exc)


def print_traces(label, net, im, fm, limit=6):
    print(f"\n-- sample traces: {label} --")
    try:
        log = pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm, parameters={"noTraces": limit})
    except Exception as exc:
        print("play_out raised:", type(exc).__name__, exc)
        return
    for i, trace in enumerate(log):
        if i >= limit:
            break
        names = [event["concept:name"] for event in trace]
        print(f"{i + 1}: " + " -> ".join(names))


def site_a_models():
    before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
        "->( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
        "X( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
    )
    after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
        "->( 'Register', +( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', "
        "X( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
    )
    net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(before)
    net_a, im_a, fm_a = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(after)
    summarize_net("Site A before", net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    summarize_net("Site A after", net_a, im_a, fm_a)
    return before, after


def site_b_candidate():
    register = Transition(label="Register")
    identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")
    originals = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
    # Distinct objects, same task type. Equality in POWL is by identity,
    # which is also what allows several silent transitions to coexist.
    doc_digital = Transition(label="Document Check")
    doc_originals = Transition(label="Document Check")
    doc_supplement = Transition(label="Document Check")
    assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")
    assessment_supplement = Transition(label="Assessment")
    legal = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
    approve = Transition(label="Approve")
    reject = Transition(label="Reject")
    notify = Transition(label="Notify")
    skip_legal = SilentTransition()

    # Statutory channel: originals must precede document checking.
    originals_path = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[originals, doc_originals])
    originals_path.order.add_edge(originals, doc_originals)

    # Digital channel has no originals predecessor. XOR is the attribute
    # split the net cannot enforce; operations must.
    document_channel = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[doc_digital, originals_path],
    )

    # Supplement redo: request, then document check, then assessment.
    # LOOP body is Legal Review, executed at least once; the redo is optional.
    supplement_redo = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[supplement, doc_supplement, assessment_supplement]
    )
    supplement_redo.order.add_edge(supplement, doc_supplement)
    supplement_redo.order.add_edge(doc_supplement, assessment_supplement)
    legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[legal, supplement_redo],
    )
    legal_gate = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[skip_legal, legal_loop],
    )
    decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[approve, reject])

    # Assessment waits for BOTH identity and the document channel.
    # No edge between identity and document_channel: they are concurrent.
    # On the originals branch that concurrency includes Originals Receipt,
    # while Document Check remains ordered after receipt inside the branch.
    root = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            register,
            identity,
            document_channel,
            assessment,
            legal_gate,
            decision,
            notify,
        ]
    )
    root.order.add_edge(register, identity)
    root.order.add_edge(register, document_channel)
    root.order.add_edge(identity, assessment)
    root.order.add_edge(document_channel, assessment)
    root.order.add_edge(assessment, legal_gate)
    root.order.add_edge(legal_gate, decision)
    root.order.add_edge(decision, notify)

    net, im, fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(root)
    summarize_net("Site B candidate", net, im, fm)
    print_traces("Site B candidate", net, im, fm)
    return root, net, im, fm


if __name__ == "__main__":
    site_a_models()
    site_b_candidate()
```

## 9. Recommendation

conditional go

Transfer only the partial order Site B can execute legally and log honestly. Do not create both checks for every case at registration, and do not budget Site A’s nine-hour median cut as the effect.

If originals are not required, create both checks after Register and run them concurrently, with Assessment waiting on both. If originals are required, start Identity Check at registration so it can sit inside the receipt delay, and start Document Check only after originals are recorded. Keep Legal Review on the public-sector and high-value stream, and keep the supplement loop through document checking and assessment. Keep a WIP cap sized to the specialist pools, not Site A’s cap of six tasks for cross-trained staff.

Do not transfer the surrounding package: the API upgrade, the rise from 18 to 20 verification FTE, the mix shift from 48% to 40% high complexity, and the huddles, all inside an uncontrolled two-week rollout. Site B’s case lock, separate roles, 18-hour originals delay, two legal reviewers, and 17% rework can move the queue or fake the overlap.

Enter first on digital cases that need neither originals nor legal review. That is the only flow close to Site A after the change. Direct Identity-to-Document precedence is 1,160 of 3,400 cases and originals cover 62%, so the wave is about one fifth to one third of volume. Expect about 3 to 5 hours off that stratum’s median, close to the identity sojourn that can leave the critical path. The portfolio median should move less because it sits among originals cases. Risks are a worse p90 if the three assessors or two legal reviewers saturate, a breach if document checks precede originals, extra rework, and a false win from back-entered times.

Expand to originals-required non-legal cases only after high true overlap, a stratum median gain whose interval excludes zero, a stable p90, and stable rework and appeals. If earlier enablement is consumed by the assessment queue, tighten assessment WIP before opening the next stratum. Admit legal-review cases last, and only while legal queue age is stable. Abandon parallelism if fidelity is high and sojourn does not fall, if statutory order is breached, or if controls or the legal queue pass the stop rule. A low-fidelity null means repair the lock or logging and rerun.

## 10. Assumption register

1. Identity and document median “service” hours are elapsed durations that include external wait, not pure reviewer labor — needed because treating them as labor makes identity demand incompatible with four reviewers, so utilization and slack are otherwise unidentified — test by splitting each activity into touch time and external wait from interaction logs or a short time study — if false and the times are pure labor, the pools are already beyond capacity or the volume data are wrong, and parallelism cannot create slack that the staffing numbers deny.

2. The portfolio median case at Site B is originals-required — needed because the headline median is then insensitive to gains that hit only the faster digital minority — test by publishing median cycle time stratified on the originals attribute — if false, a digital-only success will move the all-case median by more than the −0.5 to −2h range, and the expansion rule should watch the portfolio median as well as the stratum median.

3. On digital cases, identity evidence and document evidence are independent enough that concurrent review does not create a new contradiction loop — needed to treat rework as a small risk rather than the main effect — test by the randomized difference in contradiction codes, rework, and appeals inside stratum D — if false, keep a sequential handoff on digital cases and abandon concurrency even where it is legal.

4. Where originals are required, identity sojourn usually fits inside the originals delay and is not already nested there — needed for a nonzero critical-path gain on 62% of cases; the gain is zero if nesting already happens or if delay is often shorter than identity sojourn — test the joint distribution of the two intervals and their current order on baseline traces — if false, do not expand to stratum O, because the statutory design would change little.

5. Assessment (3 FTE) and legal review (2 FTE) are the downstream resources most likely to saturate if verification enablement moves earlier — needed to justify WIP caps and the rule that a median gain with a worse p90 is failure — test queue age, WIP, and completions per productive hour by role during phase 1, including control cases — if both pools show large slack and p90 does not move, the tail scenarios are pessimistic and expansion can proceed sooner.

6. The case lock can be changed so two tasks are genuinely active, and start timestamps can be written at the real start — needed because otherwise the treatment marking is unreachable and the overlap metric repeats Site A’s recording ambiguity — test in a sandbox on a handful of cases before phase 1, then audit back-entry rate weekly — if false, stop at phase 0 and do not run an outcome comparison until the system can represent the partial order.

7. `originals_required` and `legal_required` are known accurately when tasks are created, not only after the path has been seen — needed to block illegal Document Check creation and to stratify the trial — test attribute completeness and agreement with the eventual path on historical cases, plus a weekly mismatch audit — if false, eligibility error will both breach the statute and bias the stratum estimates, so the pilot waits on the data definition.

8. Case-level randomization interferes through shared pools in a way that is partial and measurable, not a pure reshuffle — needed to read an arm difference as a net effect at that treatment dose — test whether control-case assessment wait and out-of-stratum legal queue age move relative to the phase-0 baseline — if interference accounts for the whole treated-case gain, switch later strata to a clustered or stepped-wedge assignment and do not expand on the case-level contrast.
