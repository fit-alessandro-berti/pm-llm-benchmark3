## 1. Control-Objective Decomposition

FEP-1 nominal rule: `Prepare_Payment resource != Release_Payment resource`, human, for every *completed* payment. Its purpose is not form - it is to prevent one person from both initiating and authorizing an erroneous or fraudulent irrevocable transfer.

Decomposed into objectives / threat scenarios:

**O1 / T1 - Honest single-operator error:** typo in amount, wrong vendor selected, duplicate invoice, wrong bank line.
- AV-2: **Prevent** duplicates, amount-vs-limit, basic PO-match; **Detect** if only warning. Cannot judge commercial typo if within limits. No receipt check.
- MG-4: **Prevent** if independent review of invoice + amount + supporting doc before release/settlement; **Fail** if rubber-stamp.
- RA-7: **Detect** after the fact; **Correct** only if before irrevocable settlement / within ~24h recall window.
- ED-1: **No control** by itself; permission that *preserves* AV-2+MG-4+RA-7 under strict conditions.

**O2 / T2 - Single-operator intentional diversion / limit abuse:** preparer chooses attacker-controlled payee, splits or inflates amount, abuses authority.
- AV-2: **Partial prevent/detect** for sanctions, bank-ownership, limit, PO-match. **Fails** to establish who controlled account if compromised, commercial reasonableness, collusion. Overrideable warning = prevent becomes advisory.
- MG-4: **Prevent** if independent, competent, reviews bank details + business rationale before release/settlement; **Fail** if conflicted, subordinate, or after settlement.
- RA-7: **Detect**; **Correct** only if recall still possible. After 24h, recovery uncertain.
- ED-1: **Constrain** exposure via <EUR 100k, vendor >=180 days, no recent bank change, no override, pre-settlement MG-4, 1-day audit. Does not eliminate insider intent.

**O3 / T3 - Bank-detail subversion:** legitimate vendor, but bank account changed/hijacked shortly before payment.
This is the highest-value external+insider vector.
- AV-2: **Prevent** if operational: checks bank change in prior 7 days, bank-ownership, sanctions. **Fail entirely** if unavailable, or if warning overridden. Clean pass is strong evidence *only* for checks covered.
- MG-4: **Prevent** only if manager explicitly verifies bank change via independent source, not just invoice face. Rubber-stamp fails.
- RA-7: **Detect**; rarely **Correct** because attacker quickly extracts funds after settlement; recall window is the only corrective lever.
- ED-1: **Prevent by prohibition**: condition 4 forbids ED-1 if any bank change in 7 days, condition 5 forbids override/unavailability.

**O4 / T4 - Collusion / independence failure:** two nominally distinct users act as one, shared workstation/credential, supervisor pressure, shared service account.
- AV-2: **Fail** - explicitly does not detect collusion or who controlled a compromised account.
- MG-4: **Fail** if manager is conflicted (e.g. sets preparer's rating), shares credential, or is socially captured; **Prevent** only if L3+, truly independent, no unresolved conflict.
- RA-7: **Detect** via pairing analytics, workstation logs; **Correct** only if timely.
- ED-1: **Fail** if independence assumed from titles; requires substantive independence.

Form-conformance (two names) without substantive independence = objective failure.

**O5 / T5 - Commercial substance failure:** no goods/services received, fabricated receipt, unreasonable price, no PO linkage.
- AV-2: **Fail** - does not establish receipt, does not validate commercial reasonableness. Full pass must not be treated as receipt evidence.
- MG-4: **Prevent** only if reviews service report / goods receipt / repair evidence, not just invoice+bank.
- RA-7: **Detect** (e.g. AP-204 fabricated receipt found on audit); **Correct** via recovery/claim only if timely, otherwise loss remains.
- ED-1: **No coverage**; vendor age + amount limit only proxy for familiarity, not proof of delivery.

**O6 / T6 - Irrecoverability / timing failure:** control occurs but after the point of no return.
Bank settlement is normally irrevocable for internal-control purposes.
- AV-2: **Prevent** only if before release and blocking/ respected.
- MG-4: **Prevent / interceptive** if before release *or* before settlement with power to stop/hold settlement; **Detective only** if after settlement.
- RA-7: **Detective** always; **Partly corrective** only when audit < ~24h and recall attempted. 100% audit within 1 business day for emergency same-user is the ED-1 corrective backstop.
- ED-1: Explicit timing chain: token before Prepare, AV before release without override, MG-4 before settlement, RA-7 within 1 business day. Break any link and permission fails, even if objective might still be partially met on other grounds.

> Summary: AV-2 covers data-consistency; MG-4 covers judgment/independence; RA-7 covers detection/recall; ED-1 covers *permission* under bounded risk, not substitution for the objective.

## 2. Equivalence and Sufficiency Test

A compensating package is not judged by "a control occurred" or amount alone, but by whether it restores the *causal protection* of a truly independent second releaser before irrevocable harm.

Proposed four-tier test. All criteria must be assessed; failure on timing or independence is normally dispositive:

**A. Fully equivalent - OBJECTIVE_MET:**
1. **Timing:** Independent second eyes with authority to stop *before* release, or at minimum before irrevocable bank settlement, plus timely audit within recall window. After-settlement ratification alone is never fully equivalent.
2. **Independence:** Different human, L3+ for manager, no shared account/credential/workstation dominance, no unresolved conflict (e.g. rating power, family link), no evidence of collusion/pairing capture.
3. **Coverage:** AV-2 fully operational + passed without override *and* manager reviewed the risk-relevant evidence for the case (invoice, bank details, receipt/service report where substance at issue). Gaps must be irrelevant to the case risk.
4. **Overrideability:** No warning overridden by conflicted party, no shared override credential, no AV outage with unsigned manual workaround for high-risk attributes.
5. **Recoverability:** Audit within 1 business day / 24h recall window preserved; or payment stopped before settlement.
6. **Evidence quality:** Contemporaneous, reviewable artifacts (what was checked, when), not post-hoc assertion of legitimacy.
7. **Collusion resistance:** No repeated-pairing + shared workstation + high frequency suggesting a single decision unit; or if such pattern exists, additional independent evidence.

**B. Partially equivalent - OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET / tolerable deviation:**
Package materially reduces risk and harm was interceptable, but one pillar is weakened: e.g. manager before settlement but not before release, audit 25h not 24h, emergency context genuine but token late/documented imperfectly, low-risk vendor/amount limiting exposure. Residual risk low-medium. Requires remediation, not sanction, but cannot be treated as conforming precedent.

**C. Merely mitigating - still OBJECTIVE_FAILED but with mitigation:**
Some detective control operated (late audit, post-settlement ratification) but could not prevent or recall loss. Reduces severity narrative, not verdict. E.g. legitimate payment despite high exposure (AP-208) - luck is not control.

**D. Ineffective - OBJECTIVE_FAILED / unjustified:**
Preventive bypassed/overridden/unavailable, independence compromised, audit after recall window, bank-change + new vendor + no receipt combined. Or nominal distinct users but colluding unit.

Additional dimensions:
- **Timing > existence:** A manager approval after settlement is detective history, not prevention.
- **Coverage > pass/fail:** Clean AV-2 pass on 6 checks says nothing about receipt, collusion, reasonableness.
- **Independence > distinct IDs:** u12 != u13 means nothing if same workstation + 37 joint payments in 30 days.
- **Recoverability > detection:** Audit that finds a non-recoverable diversion is not corrective.

**Why "same expected loss" is not the only standard:**
Expected loss = probability x amount averages away what controls must govern: (a) tail risk and irreversibility - EUR 900k with no bank-ownership evidence can be survivable 99 times and catastrophic once; (b) adversarial adaptation - fraudsters optimize against overrideable warnings and late audits; (c) auditability and accountability - a control must produce evidence a third party can re-perform, not just a good outcome by luck; (d) moral hazard and precedent - tolerating convenience same-user handling teaches circumvention; (e) legal/regulatory duty of segregation - deontological requirement, not utilitarian trade; (f) uncertainty - with missing workstation design docs, unresolved credential sharing, and AV outage, probabilities are unknowable. Therefore equivalence requires *mechanism* equivalence (independent check before harm with coverage of case risk), not just low ex-post loss.

## 4. Preventive–Detective–Corrective Comparison

| Control | Timing in log | Type | What it stops/detects/corrects | Strength driver | Weakness driver |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AV-2 Automated validation, blocking | Before Release, automatic | **Preventive** (if block respected) | Duplicate, sanctions, bank-ownership, 7-day bank change, limit, PO-match | Fully operational, no override, coverage relevant to case risk | Warning + override credential, outage, coverage gaps (receipt, collusion, reasonableness) |
| AV-2 Warning + Override | Before Release, human override | **Preventive attempted, degraded to advisory** | Same as above, but decision delegated to overrider | Independent overrider with evidence | Same-user override (AP-202) = self-authorization; conflicted overrider = no control |
| MG-4 Manager_Approve before Release (AP-203, AP-206) | Before Release | **Preventive** (gold standard) | Error, diversion, bank hijack, substance - if scope reviewed | Independent L3+, reviews invoice+bank+receipt, power to deny release | Rubber-stamp, conflict, shared credential |
| MG-4 Manager_Ratify between Release and Bank_Settlement (AP-201, AP-207) | After Release, before irrevocable settlement | **Hybrid: detective re Release, preventive/interceptive re harm** (chosen semantics) | Can still hold/stop settlement, demand recall, correct payee before funds leave | Independent, timely, with settlement-hold authority and full evidence review | No hold authority, cursory review, settlement already executed |
| MG-4 Manager_Ratify after Settlement (AP-202 late, AP-208) | After irrevocable point | **Detective only** (history) | Learns for next time; cannot prevent this loss | Thorough root-cause + recovery action | Often confused with prevention; creates false comfort |
| RA-7 Audit_Review <24h / 1 business day | After Release, ideally before/within recall window | **Detective, partly corrective** | Detects all O1-O5; corrects only if recall/stop still possible | 100% for emergencies, within recall window, with recall attempt | Late audit (AP-202 8d, AP-208 4d, AP-204 4d, AP-201 25h) = detection without correction |
| RA-7 Audit_Review late | After recall window | **Detective only** | Lessons, disciplinary, claim | Still valuable for pattern (AP-204) | No corrective capacity |
| ED-1 Emergency permission | Token before Prepare through audit | **Permission / gate, not a control** | Bounds risk (amount, vendor age, bank stability, AV, MG-4, audit) to make same-user tolerable | All 7 conditions met + genuine emergency | Any condition fails = no permission; does not itself check substance |

**Semantics choice:** We adopt *harm-centric* semantics: the legally/economically irrevocable point is `Bank_Settlement`, not `Release_Payment`. `Release_Payment` is authorization; settlement is harm. Therefore a manager action after Release but before Settlement with authority to block settlement is *preventive in effect* (interceptive), even though workflow-centric logging calls it "ratify" (suggesting detective). We label it hybrid to avoid overstating: detective as to the authorization breach, preventive as to loss. Without proof of hold authority and timely review, we downgrade it to detective-only. This explains why AP-207 (ratify 14:40 before 16:00 settlement with full review) can be MET, while AP-208 (ratify next day after settlement) is failed, even though both are logged as `Manager_Ratify`.

## 5. Counterfactual and Residual-Risk Analysis

Qualitative scale: Negligible < Low < Medium < High < Very High. No precise probabilities asserted; any numbers would be illustrative only and are omitted.

**AP-202 - diversion via bank-change + override:**
- Most plausible loss mechanism: u23 (alone or with relative) creates/uses new vendor, changes bank 2h before payment, prepares large payment exceeding own limit, uses m09 override credential to silence AV-2 warning, releases to relative-controlled account. Supervisor ratification delayed and conflicted.
- Which control breaks chain: Respect for AV-2 block/warning (no override without independent second person re-validating bank ownership via independent channel) *or* pre-release independent MG-4 reviewing bank-change proximity + receipt *or* audit within 24h with immediate recall attempt.
- Remains exposed: Funds unrecovered; credential-sharing unresolved; whether m09 complicit or negligent unknown; other payments by pair unreviewed.
- With truly independent second releaser: Second human would have to co-approve release in system (not just lend credential), seeing warning + new vendor + 480k + no receipt; refusal or escalation highly likely. Residual would drop from realized Very High loss to Low (attempt blocked or recalled). Collusion would still require two-person conspiracy, raising barrier substantially.

**AP-204 - substance fraud under nominal separation:**
- Mechanism: u12 prepares consulting invoice with fabricated service receipt; u13 releases without substantive check; AV-2 passes because PO-match/bank checks do not cover receipt truth; no manager; audit 4 days later detects fabrication but after use of funds/services dispute.
- Breaks chain: Independent manager/second eyes reviewing service evidence (timesheets, deliverables, goods receipt) before release, or AV+MG coverage extended to receipt, or timely audit with hold on future payments to vendor.
- Remains exposed: 37 joint payments suggest systematic capture; single detection does not prove scope; recovery of consulting fees uncertain; same pair continues in AP-209.
- With truly independent second releaser (rotated, no shared workstation, no joint history): Independent releaser with duty to verify receipt would query fabrication; collusion would need explicit agreement, not tacit routine. Residual drops from High (proven defect + systemic) to Low-Medium (isolated error still possible but collusion-resistant).

**AP-201 - convenience same-user, low-risk:**
- Mechanism: u17 hurries office-supply payment, skips seeking releaser; typo/wrong vendor possible but AV-2 + pre-settlement manager catch.
- Breaks chain: AV-2 duplicate/bank/PO checks + m04 pre-settlement review before 10:00 settlement already broke chain; no defect existed.
- Remains exposed: Precedent risk - if tolerated, operators learn same-user is acceptable; 25h audit misses recall window by 1h, so a real diversion would have been unrecoverable. Necessity absent.
- With independent second releaser: No material change to this payment outcome (already correct), but restores deterrence and audit timeliness discipline; residual stays Low, confidence in MET would rise from partial to full.

**AP-208 - high-exposure legitimate payment during outage:**
- Mechanism (near-miss, not realized): 900k to 8-month vendor whose bank changed 3 days earlier, released with no AV-2 bank-ownership evidence and unsigned manual checklist; if bank details had been hijacked, full amount would be irrevocably settled before next-day ratification/4-day audit.
- Breaks chain: Prohibit release during AV outage for payments with bank-change proximity or >100k; require dual manual sign-off with independent bank confirmation call before release; hold settlement until MG-4 pre-settlement review with evidence.
- Remains exposed: No reliable evidence at release time what was true; retrospective legitimacy does not retroactively create control; outage handling procedure still missing.
- With independent second releaser: Second releaser alone insufficient without AV data - both would be blind. Need *both* independence *and* information (bank-ownership proof). Proper counterfactual is independent releaser empowered to refuse release during outage; payment delayed 1 day until AV restored/bank confirmed. Residual drops from Very High exposure to Low (delay cost vs loss risk).

**AP-206 vs AP-207 - emergency timing:**
- Mechanism: Genuine outage/safety pressure tempts skipping token or post-dating justification; error/diversion could hide behind urgency.
- Breaks chain: Pre-release MG-4 (both cases) + operational AV + timely audit already break error/diversion chain; token timing is permission hygiene.
- Remains exposed in AP-206: 2-minute inversion creates doubt whether emergency was pre-authorized or rationalized; if logging delay, negligible; if back-dated, permission culture failure (Medium). AP-207 has no such doubt, residual Low.
- With independent second releaser in normal (non-emergency) process: Emergency delay eliminated, token irrelevant, same preventive effect with cleaner evidence. Lesson: emergencies need pre-issued tokens and clock-synced logging, otherwise strong substance is tainted by weak permission proof.

## 6. Evidence and Monitoring Recommendations

Distinguish **alert** (triage trigger) from **proof of misconduct** (forensics + HR/legal finding). All rules below generate alerts; none alone proves fraud.

**Required evidence per payment (to re-perform judgment):**
- Immutable Prepare/Release/Approve/Ratify/Settlement/Audit timestamps (UTC, synced), resources (human vs SYSTEM), workstation ID, credential used for override.
- AV-2 input snapshot: which checks ran, version, pass/warning/block, values (bank hash, change date, limit, PO-match score), outage flag + manual checklist sign-off.
- Manager artifact: what was reviewed (invoice, bank evidence, receipt/service report), L-level, independence attestation, decision + rationale, before/after settlement flag.
- Emergency artifact: token ID, activation time, requestor, reason, outage ticket if any.
- Audit artifact: start time vs release (hours), recall attempted Y/N + bank response, finding, recovery amount.
- Vendor master: activation date, bank-change history with timestamps, receipt confirmation.

**Monitoring rules (alert thresholds, tune after baseline):**
1. **Same-user rate:** alert if `Prepare==Release` for completed payments >0 without active ED-1 token; dashboard same-user % by dept/user; any repeat same-user by same operator >1/month = review.
2. **Override rate:** alert on every `Automated_Validate_Warning -> Override_Warning -> Release` chain; especially overrider == preparer or override credential owner != physical overrider. Override rate >5% or any limit/bank-change override = immediate manager+ audit review.
3. **Late manager approval:** alert if `Manager_Approve/Ratify` after `Release` (workflow-late) and critical alert if after `Bank_Settlement` (harm-late). Track median Release->Manager hours; AP-201/207 pattern (before settlement) needs settlement-hold proof, not just timestamp.
4. **Audit-before-recall-window:** SLA 100% emergency same-user audit within 1 business day and Release->Audit <24h where recall possible. Alert on breach (AP-201 25h marginal, AP-202 8d, AP-208 4d, AP-204 4d). Measure Audit->Recall-Attempt lag.
5. **Repeated resource pairs:** alert on Prepare-Release pair frequency (e.g. >5 joint payments in 30 days, or pair share >50% of either user's volume). AP-12/13 37 in 30 days = freeze rotation + independent re-audit. Do not punish dedicated cells without proof - rotate and compare defect rates.
6. **Shared workstations / credentials:** alert on same workstation for both legs, shared service account use, override credential used by non-owner. Require workstation log join; unresolved sharing = independence failure.
7. **Bank-change proximity:** hard block or dual approval if bank change within 7 days (ED-1 forbids single-operator); alert if within 30 days + amount >50k or vendor <1y or no receipt. AP-202 2h + AP-208 3d would have been blocked/held.
8. **Control outages:** alert on `Validation_Service_Unavailable` + subsequent Release; require release freeze for high-risk attributes until AV restored or dual manual sign-off with independent bank verification. Track outage duration, payments released during outage, checklist completion.

**Governance:**
- Monthly four-eyes dashboard by case with nominal result vs objective classification, residual risk, and missing evidence.
- Quarterly pairing + workstation + override analytics; annual independence attestation for L3+ managers.
- Clear policy: convenience same-user (AP-201) is violation even if low loss; emergency without token is violation; post-settlement ratification never cures prevention failure.
- Whistleblower + vendor-bank confirmation channel independent of preparer.
