### Identity Normalization and Service-Account Attribution Methodology

1. **Alias Normalization (`b.koch` \(\to\) `u_bk` / Ben Koch)**:
   - Automated Segregation of Duties (SoD) and Four-Eyes Principle controls frequently fail when users hold multiple system identifiers (e.g., legacy usernames vs. single sign-on IDs).
   - Before executing any SoD, transition, or organizational network analytics, all event logs are processed through an alias mapping dictionary: `{'b.koch': 'u_bk'}`.
   - This ensures that transactions where Ben Koch drafted under `b.koch` and approved under `u_bk` (such as CHG-508) are correctly identified as single-human self-approvals rather than compliant multi-person workflows.

2. **Service-Account Attribution (`svc_qms`)**:
   - Under pharmaceutical data integrity regulations (21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11, ALCOA+ principles), a generic service account cannot provide legal attributable accountability without deterministic linkage to an authenticated natural person.
   - We evaluate corroborating ambient session logs across three evidentiary tiers:
     - **Tier 1 (Cryptographic / Multi-Factor Attribution - Conclusive)**: In CHG-509, `svc_qms` was invoked from dedicated verifier workstation `WS-IV-02` with an individual FIDO2 hardware token and badge log identifying Eva Novak at 11:58 UTC. This achieves deterministic attribution to Eva Novak (`u_en`), allowing full SoD verification.
     - **Tier 2 (Location / Badge Access Attribution - Inconclusive / Suspicious)**: In CHG-503, `svc_qms` was invoked from workstation `WS-DM-04`, assigned to Maintenance Lead Diego Martins. Badge records show Diego entered the room, but the workstation was shared, CCTV was unavailable, and no individual re-authentication or PIN challenge occurred. While this creates a strong presumption that Diego performed self-verification of his own implementation, the lack of individual authentication renders the record technically non-attributable and an audit-trail failure.
     - **Tier 3 (Uncorrelated Service Account - Non-Conformant)**: Any service account action lacking physical or digital session attribution violates RR5 and is treated as non-compliant.

## 2. Rule Interpretation

### Formalization of Resource Rules RR1–RR6

- **RR1 — Drafter/Approver Separation (Four-Eyes Principle)**:
  \[
  \forall c \in \text{Cases}, \quad \text{Actor}(\text{Draft\_Change\_Request}, c) \cap \text{Actor}(\text{Approve\_Change}, c) = \emptyset
  \]
  - *Interpretation*: The human identity (after alias normalization) who authors/drafts a change request is strictly barred from approving the same change request, regardless of whether the risk classification is LOW or HIGH. Self-approval completely negates the primary preventive control against unauthorized alterations.

- **RR2 — Implementer/Verifier Separation (Execution Independence)**:
  \[
  \forall c \in \text{Cases}, \quad \text{Actor}(\text{Implement\_Change}, c) \cap \text{Actor}(\text{Verify\_Implementation}, c) = \emptyset
  \]
  - *Interpretation*: The human who executes physical or software modifications cannot verify or qualify those modifications. Independent verification is mandatory across all risk tiers. Shared service accounts without individual session attribution fail this check automatically.

- **RR3 — High-Risk Approval Authority**:
  \[
  \forall c \in \text{Cases} \text{ where } \text{Risk}(c) = \text{HIGH} \land \text{Emergency}(c) = \text{false}:
  \]
  \[
  \text{Actor}(\text{Approve\_Change}, c) \in \{\text{Principal QM}\} \cup \text{ValidDelegates}(\text{QM}, t_{\text{event}}, \text{Scope}(c))
  \]
  - *Validity Conditions for Delegation \(D = (P \to A, [t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}], \text{Scope}, \text{Conditions})\)*:
    1. *Temporal coverage*: \(t_{\text{event}} \in [t_{\text{start}}, t_{\text{end}}]\).
    2. *Scope alignment*: \(\text{Scope}(c) \subseteq \text{Scope}(D)\).
    3. *Principal absence*: If stipulated, Principal \(P\) must be on recorded leave or formally unavailable at \(t_{\text{event}}\).
    4. *Anti-subdelegation*: Delegate \(A\) must hold authority directly from \(P\); subdelegation is void unless expressly empowered in the head instrument.
    5. *Contemporaneous execution*: The legal instrument establishing \(D\) must be executed prior to \(t_{\text{event}}\).

- **RR4 — Emergency Substitution & Retrospective QM Review**:
  \[
  \forall c \in \text{Cases} \text{ where } \text{Risk}(c) = \text{HIGH} \land \text{Emergency}(c) = \text{true}:
  \]
  1. \(\text{Actor}(\text{Approve\_Change}, c) \in \{\text{Principal IC}\} \cup \text{ValidDelegates}(\text{IC}, t_{\text{event}}, \text{Scope}(c))\)
  2. \(\exists e = \text{Retrospective\_QM\_Review} \text{ such that } \text{Actor}(e, c) \in \{\text{Principal QM}\} \cup \text{ValidDelegates}(\text{QM}, t_e, \text{Scope}(c))\)
  3. \(t_e \le \text{AddBusinessDays}(t_{\text{Implement\_Change}}, 2)\)
  4. The emergency classification and safety rationale must be substantiated by an active incident record.

- **RR5 — Identity Evidence and Normalization**:
  - System accounts (`svc_qms`) possess no legal human agency. They can satisfy internal controls only when bound to an individual human via multi-factor authentication, physical telemetry, or cryptographic session tokens.
  - Multiple digital aliases belonging to the same individual must be normalized to a singular canonical identity prior to evaluating RR1 and RR2.

- **RR6 — Suspicion versus Proof**:
  - Statistical concentrations (e.g., dyad recurrence, reciprocal approvals, shared physical workstations) establish risk indicators and investigative priority, but do not constitute legal or regulatory proof of collusion without direct corroborating factual evidence (e.g., shared passwords, forged logs, intentional bypass).

### Detailed Operational Definitions and Edge-Case Governance

1. **Business-Day Counting Protocol for RR4**:
   - *Definition*: Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding official site calendar holidays. Saturdays and Sundays are non-business days.
   - *Computation Formula*: For an implementation completing at timestamp \(t_{\text{impl}}\) on day \(D_0\) (UTC):
     - Business Day 1 ends at 23:59:59 UTC on the first subsequent business day (\(D_1\)).
     - Business Day 2 ends at 23:59:59 UTC on the second subsequent business day (\(D_2\)).
   - *Application to CHG-505*:
     - Implementation: Tuesday, 2026-08-11 13:10 UTC.
     - Business Day 1: Wednesday, 2026-08-12.
     - Business Day 2 Deadline: Thursday, 2026-08-13 23:59:59 UTC.
     - Actual Retrospective Review: Friday, 2026-08-14 16:00 UTC (Business Day 3).
     - *Finding*: **Non-compliant (Overdue by ~16 hours)**.
   - *Application to CHG-510*:
     - Implementation: Tuesday, 2026-08-11 14:30 UTC.
     - Retrospective Review: Wednesday, 2026-08-12 10:00 UTC (< 20 hours later; within Business Day 1).
     - *Finding*: **Compliant with timing deadline**, though non-compliant on initial approval authority.

2. **Actions by a Principal While on Leave (Asha Rao on CHG-504)**:
   - Under corporate and regulatory governance, an officer's statutory authority derives from their corporate appointment and job description, not their physical presence. Approved annual leave suspends operational duty obligations but does not legally extinguish statutory signing authority.
   - When Principal QM Asha Rao logged into the corporate VPN and approved CHG-504:
     - Her direct action was **fully authorized and legally effective** (standing role paramountcy).
     - The active delegation (D-QM-01 to Ben Koch) was not invalidated for other matters, but Asha's direct intervention superseded the delegate's necessity for CHG-504.
     - *Forensic requirement*: Because leave status increases the risk of credential lending, security must verify VPN MFA telemetry (IP geolocation, device certificates, biometrics) to confirm that Asha personally executed the transaction.

3. **Evidentiary Validity of Subsequent Database Records (D-QM-01 Migration)**:
   - In regulated GxP environments, electronic records must satisfy contemporaneous recording requirements (ALCOA+).
   - For D-QM-01, the primary legal instrument is the signed PDF executed with a cryptographic/system timestamp of `2026-08-09 18:00 UTC`, well before the effective start date (`2026-08-10 00:00 UTC`).
   - The creation of a QMS database record on `2026-08-12 09:15 UTC` during a system migration is a secondary technical synchronization event. It does **not** invalidate the delegation, provided the underlying signed PDF exists, is tamper-evident, and was registered prior to the delegated acts.
   - Conversely, an uncorroborated post-hoc database record created after an event with no pre-existing signed instrument cannot retroactively cure an unauthorized act.

## 6. Control Improvements

### Preventive, Detective, and Evidentiary Recommendations

1. **Electronic Delegation of Authority (DoA) Lifecycle Management**:
   - *Automated Workflow Integration*: Replace paper forms, signed PDFs, and email delegations with a structured, digital DoA module inside the QMS.
   - *Pre-Execution Database Registration*: Mandate that all delegations be approved and committed to the transactional QMS database prior to the effective start timestamp. Prohibit post-hoc synchronization.
   - *Anti-Subdelegation Enforcement*: Implement programmatic constraints that block any delegated user from creating child delegations unless the master instrument contains an explicit `allow_subdelegation = True` attribute.

2. **Dynamic Segregation of Duties (SoD) & Alias Hard-Blocking**:
   - *Database-Level SoD Interlocks*: Hardcode blocking rules at the API layer: `IF current_user == drafter THEN disable_approve_button()`, applied universally to both LOW and HIGH risk workflows.
   - *Identity & Access Management (IAM) Synchronization*: Unify all legacy account identifiers (`b.koch`, `u_bk`) under a single immutable enterprise Employee ID (`person_uuid`). Ensure SoD validation checks `person_uuid` rather than application usernames.

3. **HRIS Leave & Access Control Integration**:
   - *Automated Delegation Activation*: Link QMS delegation availability directly to the enterprise HR Information System (HRIS). A delegation conditioned on principal absence should automatically activate only when HRIS marks the principal as "On Leave."
   - *Immediate Sick-Leave Suspension*: Automatically lock an employee's signing permissions and suspend active delegations the moment certified sick leave is logged in HRIS.
   - *Formal Remote Override Protocol*: If an absent manager must act during leave (as in CHG-504), require a structured "Emergency Remote Override" workflow prompting for justification and step-up biometric/push MFA, automatically logging the incident for post-leave audit.

4. **Phase-Out of Shared Service Accounts & Mandatory Individual Authentication**:
   - *Deprecation of Interactive `svc_qms`*: Restrict service accounts to machine-to-machine background APIs. Completely eliminate generic interactive logins on shop-floor kiosks.
   - *Hardware-Backed MFA (FIDO2 / WebAuthn)*: Equip all shop-floor and verifier workstations with smart-card or FIDO2 hardware token readers. Mandate individual touch/PIN re-authentication at the exact moment of executing verification or implementation sign-offs (21 CFR Part 11 compliant).
   - *Aggressive Terminal Auto-Lock*: Configure workstation inactivity timeouts to 2 minutes on shop-floor terminals to prevent walk-up credential hijacking.

5. **Emergency Workflow Timers & Escalation**:
   - *Automated RR4 Compliance Countdown*: Introduce automated countdown timers in the QMS dashboard for all emergency changes approved under IC authority.
   - *Automated Escalations*: Issue automated alerts to the QM pool at 24 hours post-implementation. If a Retrospective QM Review is not executed within 48 business hours, automatically lock the change record and generate a critical CAPA deviation.

6. **Continuous Process Mining & Organizational Network Monitoring**:
   - *Automated Dyad Concentration Alerts*: Embed continuous PM4Py analytics into the QMS compliance reporting engine to compute monthly handover and collaboration matrices.
   - *Threshold-Based Review Triggers*: Configure automated audit triggers when any single drafter-approver dyad exceeds 50% of approvals in a 60-day rolling window, initiating supervisory quality reviews without declaring misconduct.

7. **Evidential Retention & Forensic Audit Trails**:
   - *Unified Time Synchronization*: Enforce high-precision Network Time Protocol (NTP) across all physical badge readers, workstation operating systems, VPN gateways, and QMS database servers.
   - *WORM Storage for Audit Trails*: Store all transactional and authentication logs on immutable Write-Once-Read-Many (WORM) cloud repositories to ensure tamper-evident forensic readiness.
