## 2. Raw-versus-normalized interpretation

The 0.35–0.39 raw–mapped gap from March onward is a provenance artifact, not evidence of process breakdown. Three migration effects create it: `Eligibility Screen` is an unseen label, duplicated `Open Case` events violate the model's expected start, and missing resources weaken resource-aware checks. That mapping restores fitness to 0.90 in March — while normalized edge rates sit at January values (eligibilitydocuments 98 per 100 cases on both sides; fragment L1 distance 3, the rounding noise floor) and cycle time and rework are flat — shows the business flow was essentially unchanged. The gap measures semantic migration; it does not imply nothing else changed. Mapped fitness itself slides from 0.92 to 0.86–0.87, and that deficit tracks rework share almost linearly (r  0.94): real behavioral deviation that no label map can repair. Conversely, mapping recovers conformance without proving the renamed check is performed identically — a shallower eligibility screen would conform equally well — so a map validates label equivalence, not semantic equivalence of the activity. Duplicates and missing resources damage different analyses: duplicates corrupt DFGs, variant counts, and fitness (spurious `Open CaseRegister`, 26 cases in March) but barely move durations when timestamps coincide with case start; missing resources disable handoff, workload, and per-assessor throughput analyses while leaving control-flow conformance untouched. Relabeling cannot generate timestamp-based queue growth or supplier-timed rework; later drift is real, layered on the March shift. Rework and cycle metrics also come from operational tables computed independently of migrated log labels, further insulating them from the semantic change.

## 6. Segmentation plan

| segment level | variables | purpose | minimum support rule | comparisons enabled | risk of conditioning bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Case-start cohort | cohort month (case start) | anchor all drift metrics to intake, removing completion-month survivorship bias | all cases; report cycle metrics on completed cases only, with censoring noted | month-over-month onset timing at weekly resolution | low; right-censoring at window end must be handled explicitly |
| Supplier stratum | cohort month × supplier (Z / non-Z) | separate supplier-driven rework from internal change | 60 cases per cell, else merge to quarter | Z vs non-Z rework and cycle DiD around 2026-05-05 and 2026-09-10 | high: Z selection non-random; conditioning on supplier mediates through complexity — report complexity-adjusted contrasts |
| Complexity stratum | cohort month × complexity class | separate mix shift from within-class behavior | 60 cases per cell | within-class rework, senior assessment, cycle; mix decomposition | moderate: complexity coding drift; collinear with priority class after July |
| Priority class | cohort month × priority class (defined by pre-rule complexity to keep it time-invariant) | detect redistribution of waiting after 2026-07-01 | 60 cases per cell | class-specific waiting DiD around 2026-07-01 | high: the class definition is entangled with the rule change itself |
| Resource/team | cohort month × team (events with resource present) | staffing and attrition throughput effects | 300 events per team-month, else merge teams | per-assessor throughput, service time, reassignment handoff networks | high: missing resources non-random; reassignment endogenous to case type |
| Label form | month × raw label form (`Validate Eligibility` vs `Eligibility Screen`) | validate semantic-mapping equivalence | 20 cases per form-month | duration and outcome equivalence under the mapping | low: adoption timing varies; check duration equivalence, not just label frequency |
| Seasonal overlay | calendar week × season (renewal vs off-season) | distinguish recurring seasonal drift from novel drift | 150 cases per week-cell, else pool weeks | year-over-year same-week comparisons | moderate: one season observed; December interventions overlap |

**Primary monitoring population:** case-start monthly cohorts over the full log, stratified by supplier (Z/non-Z) × complexity class, with priority class nested under complexity from July onward. Rationale: (a) cohort indexing removes the survivorship/completion bias that contaminates completion-month aggregates during backlog growth; (b) supplier and complexity are the two strongest mix drivers observed; (c) the non-Z × standard-complexity cell (~200–260 cases per month at observed volumes, satisfying the 60 support rule) serves as the reference stratum — exposed to shared mechanisms (migration, staffing, priority) but insulated from supplier and seasonal-complexity mechanisms, so drift appearing there is more likely internal behavioral or organizational change.

**Seasonality handling.** Reference windows are day-of-year matched to prior years where available; with only one observed year, fit robust weekly seasonal decomposition (STL) or week-of-year indicators on Jan–Sep, widen control limits for Oct–Nov, and never compare October directly against September. Renewal-season metrics carry their own seasonal baselines so recurrence is the null hypothesis, not an alert.

**Multiple testing.** Monitors are grouped into families (data quality, control flow, performance, population). Within a family, alerts are combined under Benjamini–Hochberg FDR at 5%. Across families, hierarchical gating applies: active data-quality alerts quarantine flow and performance alerts until the log is revalidated, because schema drift generates correlated downstream false alarms (exactly the March pattern). Noisy statistics (temporal profile, service time) additionally require two consecutive out-of-limit periods. Thresholds are calibrated per monitor on baseline MAD/quantiles — never one universal constant — and alert precision is reviewed quarterly with retuning.

LABEL_MAP = {"Eligibility Screen": "Validate Eligibility"}   # semantic mapping
TECHNICAL_ACTIVITIES = {"Open Case"}                          # duplicated technical event

def _require_pm4py():
    if pm4py is None:
        raise RuntimeError("Part B requires pm4py (pip install pm4py).")

def prepare_log(df: pd.DataFrame, case_col: str, act_col: str,
                ts_col: str, res_col: str | None = None) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Format an event dataframe for PM4Py without mutating the input."""
    _require_pm4py()
    out = df.copy()
    kwargs = dict(case_id=case_col, activity_key=act_col, timestamp_key=ts_col)
    if res_col:
        kwargs["resource_key"] = res_col
    return pm4py.format_dataframe(out, **kwargs)

def normalize_labels(log_df: pd.DataFrame,
                     drop_all_technical: bool = False) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Return a *copy* with the semantic mapping applied and duplicated
    technical events removed. The original dataframe is never overwritten."""
    out = log_df.copy()
    out["concept:name"] = out["concept:name"].replace(LABEL_MAP)
    if drop_all_technical:
        out = out[~out["concept:name"].isin(TECHNICAL_ACTIVITIES)]
    else:
        tech = out["concept:name"].isin(TECHNICAL_ACTIVITIES)
        dup = out.duplicated(subset=["case:concept:name", "concept:name",
                                     "time:timestamp"], keep="first")
        out = out[~(tech & dup)]
    return out.reset_index(drop=True)

def slice_window(log_df: pd.DataFrame, start: str, end: str,
                 mode: str = "traces_contained") -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Time slicing; 'traces_contained' keeps whole traces inside [start, end]."""
    _require_pm4py()
    return pm4py.filter_time_range(log_df, start, end, mode=mode)

def tag_cohort_month(log_df: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Tag each case with its start month. Cohort (intake) indexing avoids the
    survivorship bias of completion-month aggregation."""
    starts = (log_df.groupby("case:concept:name")["time:timestamp"].min()
              .dt.to_period("M").astype(str).rename("case:cohort_month"))
    return log_df.merge(starts, left_on="case:concept:name",
                        right_index=True, how="left")

def select_cohort(log_df: pd.DataFrame, month: str) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """All events of cases that STARTED in `month` (whole-trace cohort)."""
    _require_pm4py()
    return pm4py.filter_trace_attribute_values(log_df, "case:cohort_month",
                                               [month], level="case", retain=True)

def monthly_dfg(log_df: pd.DataFrame) -> dict:
    """Discover a directly-follows graph per start-month cohort."""
    _require_pm4py()
    return {m: pm4py.discover_dfg(select_cohort(log_df, m))
            for m in sorted(log_df["case:cohort_month"].dropna().unique())}

def monthly_variants(log_df: pd.DataFrame) -> dict:
    """Variant table per start-month cohort (variant tuple -> count)."""
    _require_pm4py()
    return {m: pm4py.get_variants(select_cohort(log_df, m))
            for m in sorted(log_df["case:cohort_month"].dropna().unique())}

def temporal_profile_by_segment(log_df: pd.DataFrame, segment_col: str) -> dict:
    """pm4py temporal profile per segment: edge-wise min/max/mean/stdev of time
    differences; use pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile(log, tp, z_threshold)
    on new data to flag out-of-band edge timings."""
    _require_pm4py()
    out = {}
    for seg in log_df[segment_col].dropna().unique():
        sub = log_df[log_df[segment_col] == seg]
        out[seg] = pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(sub)
    return out

def optional_constraint_models(log_df: pd.DataFrame) -> dict:
    """Optional declarative models: log skeleton and DECLARE constraints.
    Availability depends on the pm4py version, hence the guards."""
    _require_pm4py()
    models = {}
    try:
        models["log_skeleton"] = pm4py.discover_log_skeleton(log_df, noise_threshold=0.0)
    except AttributeError:
        pass  # log-skeleton discovery not exposed in this pm4py build
    try:
        models["declare"] = pm4py.discover_declare(log_df)
    except AttributeError:
        pass  # DECLARE discovery not available in this pm4py build
    return models

def compare_raw_vs_normalized(df: pd.DataFrame, case_col: str, act_col: str,
                              ts_col: str, res_col: str | None = None) -> dict:
    """Side-by-side DFG/variant discovery on raw vs normalized labels.
    Returns a report; original frames are left untouched."""
    _require_pm4py()
    raw = prepare_log(df, case_col, act_col, ts_col, res_col)
    norm = normalize_labels(raw)
    return {
        "raw_labels": sorted(raw["concept:name"].unique()),
        "normalized_labels": sorted(norm["concept:name"].unique()),
        "raw_dfg": pm4py.discover_dfg(raw),
        "normalized_dfg": pm4py.discover_dfg(norm),
        "raw_variants": pm4py.get_variants(raw),
        "normalized_variants": pm4py.get_variants(norm),
        "removed_technical_events": int(len(raw) - len(norm)),
    }

**Strongest conclusions.** (1) The March fitness collapse is logging/semantic drift from the migration: raw fitness fell 0.920.55 while mapped fitness fell only ~0.02, normalized edge rates were unchanged (fragment L1 distance 3, the rounding noise floor), durations and rework were flat, and the raw DFG reconciles exactly (500+140 = 640 registrations; 26 duplicated `Open Case` events  4%). **Plausible but not established.** The June–July deterioration is best read as attrition-driven queueing compounded by supplier backlog: in June rework rose only 3pp while the median rose 4.3h, and Assess service rose just 0.6h against a +14h median drift by August, so queueing plausibly carries 60–70%. **Mediation and interaction.** Rework mediates suppliercycle time; queueing mediates attritioncycle time; complexity mediates seasonsenior assessments; the Z-mix shift mediates part of the apparent supplier effect. Attrition, backlog, and priority interacted May–August, so the August peak (median 45h, p90 110h) admits no single-mechanism attribution, and "persistent behavioral adaptation" cannot be separated from seasonal rework without post-September supplier-stratified rates.

**Revision triggers.** Supplier-stratified rework loops, class-split waiting around July 1, per-assessor throughput, Request-Info reason codes, prior-year October baselines, verification of the Z-share jump in intake data, and weekly December interrupted-time-series would each materially reorder this attribution.

## 10. Monitoring decision rules

1. `IF raw-model fitness drops by more than 0.2 within one month while mapped fitness, normalized DFG edge rates, and cycle-time quantiles remain within their baseline bands, THEN quarantine the month from drift reporting, update the label-mapping and duplicate-filter specification, and republish conformance before any operational escalation, BECAUSE the March signature (0.920.55 raw vs 0.90 mapped, edge rates unchanged) shows schema migration masquerading as process failure.`
2. `IF a new activity label exceeds 1% of monthly events, or duplicated technical events exceed 1% of cases, or resource missingness steps up more than 2 percentage points concentrated in specific activities, THEN freeze DFG, variant, and resource-based dashboards and trigger an extraction audit before interpreting performance changes, BECAUSE schema and provenance drift silently invalidate every downstream comparison and generate correlated false alarms.`
