# 2. Benign alternative explanations

1. **Emergency handling / highpriority cases**  
   *Explanation*: Fast approvals (e.g., Buyer_Carla  Approver_Gita) likely reflect legitimate emergency medicalsupply requests requiring urgent processing.  
   *Evidence to support*: Review the `emergency_flag` and case descriptions; confirm that the approval timestamps precede the stated urgency and that retrospective justifications were completed within the 3day window.  

2. **Informal specialization / role design**  
   *Explanation*: Repeated loops such as Approver_Gita  Buyer_Carla may be the result of a specialized workflow where emergency caseworkers are routinely consulted by a subset of buyers.  
   *Evidence to support*: Documented SOPs showing that emergency medicalsupply requests are routed to these pairs; absence of policy violations in similar nonemergency cases.  

3. **Legitimate vendormaster mass maintenance**  
   *Explanation*: High vendor concentration (VendorMaster_Lena  AP_Kim) may stem from a recent bankplatform migration where many vendors under family V77x required coordinated bankdetail updates.  
   *Evidence to refute*: Lack of linking tickets between procurement cases and the migration project; presence of mismatched timestamps indicating possible oversight.  

4. **Dataquality / mapping errors**  
   *Explanation*: The appearing of Approver_Gita for emergency approvals despite assistant entries could be an error in the `source_system="legacy_erp"` mapping table, where multiple org:resource identifiers are collapsed.  
   *Evidence to refute*: Audit of the legacy ERP mapping schema; correct entries showing distinct users for those periods.
