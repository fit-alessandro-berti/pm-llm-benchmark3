# 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

### 1.1 What is fact (given) and what is interpretation (mine)

- **[FACT]** Source characteristics (status, effective dates, wording, signatories, thresholds) and the case event data, subject to the ON44 evidential caveat.
- **[FACT]** GPIP4 **A7 reserves written, scoped, timelimited exceptions to the Chief Risk Officer** and **expressly does not decide** whether a jointly signed COO/Incident Commander directive counts as an A7 exception (open question left by the instrument itself).
- **[FACT]** The CFO has **explicit authority to interpret Finance controls**; **no document** gives the CFO power to waive Compliance controls or GPIP4 segregation of duties (D3).
- **[FACT]** Organizational practice: signed incident directives have been treated as **temporary policy overlays, but never to displace newsupplier Compliance clearance**.
- **[INTERPRETATION]** Everything below about the relative weight of A/B/C/D, the meaning of "ordinary," the scope term "critical sterilization-line lots," the effect of silence, and the classification of each case.

### 1.2 Authority (competence, not rank alone)

Authority is **issue-specific and competence-based**, not a single ladder.

1. **[INTERPRETATION]** GPIP4 is the **board-level integrity floor**. Its non-negotiable core is: **A1 (segregation of duties), A3 (Compliance clearance for >EUR 100,000 and for new suppliers), A6 (no automatic cure), A7 (only the CRO may except)**. Only an A7 instrument can displace this core.
2. **[INTERPRETATION]** **ECD2 is an operational incident directive**, not an A7 exception: it is not signed by the CRO, it is silent on every GPIP4 control at issue (C5), and the CRO never ratified it. Its *valid* reach is exactly what it claims and what B7 lets it claim: **purchasing timing and supplier-onboarding requirements** within its window and subject-matter scope.
3. **[INTERPRETATION]** **The CFO email (D) has low formal weight but real interpretive weight on Finance-approval modality** from 20260715 07:20, because competence is documented (D3). It is **prospective only**, cannot extend ECD2's window or scope, and cannot reach A1 or A3 (D3/D4).
4. **[INTERPRETATION]** The **local SOP** may be stricter (A5) and may define operational evidence, but B7's supersession clause **cannot be used to weaken GPIP4** (A5 forbids silent weakening).

### 1.3 Specificity, later-in-time, and the express-waiver canon

- **[INTERPRETATION — EXPRESS-WAIVER CANON]** Silence is not waiver. A4 ("emergency status waives competitive tendering **only**"), B5 (emergency procedure does **not expressly** waive Finance/approved-supplier), C5 (ECD2 silent on GPIP4), and D4 ("not meant to remove essential integrity controls") all point one way: **a control survives unless an authorized instrument expressly displaces it, or displacement follows by necessary implication from the instrument's own words.** ECD2 expressly displaced the PO-timing rule (C1) and the approved-supplier rule (C2). It did **not** expressly displace Finance approval, Compliance clearance, thresholds, or SoD.
- **[INTERPRETATION — TIME OF THE ACT]** Each act is tested against instruments **in force at the moment of the act** (mainly the moment of `Commit_to_Supplier`, the moment company money is put at risk). Later instruments have **no retroactive effect**; a later approval **mitigates but does not cure** (A6).
- **[INTERPRETATION — SPECIFICITY]** A more specific instrument prevails **only inside its own issue, scope and time window**, and only if the general instrument has not **reserved** the issue (as A7 reserves exceptions).

### 1.4 Scope of ECD2 (C6) — adopted two-limb test

**[ASSUMPTION]** "Critical sterilization-line lots required to contain the current contamination event" means: **(i) asset nexus** — the item serves the affected sterilization line or its containment function; **and (ii) necessity nexus** — the item is needed to contain/restore that function **within the incident horizon**, evidenced by a need-by assessment (engineering/production), not by an Incident Command label alone. A need-by of  24 h strongly evidences (ii); multi-day contingency stock for a *backup* line does not, absent evidence the backup line is itself part of containment. This is a mean between Operations (backup-line = critical) and Internal Audit (24 h only): the 24 h figure is treated as **strong evidence of necessity, not as a hard definition**.

### 1.5 Emergency necessity and its limits

- **[INTERPRETATION]** "Emergency" is a **status with defined content**, not a general dispensing power. B4 defines it locally (imminent safety harm or production stoppage within 6 hours). A4 confines the GPIP4 effect of emergency status to **competitive tendering**. Therefore a plea of "we had to" **cannot waive** A1/A2/A3; it can only explain why a *permitted* emergency route was used, or mitigate severity.
- **[INTERPRETATION]** Where an exception has **conditions** (e.g., B4's Shift Lead substitution requires an *absent* Plant Manager and an *active* delegation), the proponent must prove the conditions. An unmet condition means the exception is unavailable — mitigation is not the same as authorization.

### 1.6 Retrospective evidence and evidential weight

- **[INTERPRETATION]** **Late database entry  late act.** Where a contemporaneous durable record exists (signed PDF metadata, telephone recording, supplier acknowledgment, access log), it **defeats** a later or skewed ERP timestamp (ON44 expressly warns of up to 12 minutes' skew and up to 9 minutes' clock drift). A logging delay is a **logging defect**, not a substantive breach.
- **[INTERPRETATION]** But a record that is itself **the control** (e.g., C3's "recorded within four hours") must be shown to have been **created** within the window — creation time, not entry time, is decisive if creation is independently verifiable.

### 1.7 Burden of proof

- **Positive duties** (approvals, clearances, dual authorization, records, forms): the proponent of compliance must produce the record. **No record = unproven compliance** (not automatically a breach, but not COMPLIANT either where the duty is material).
- **Prohibitions** (A1 SoD): breach must be evidenced by the prohibited configuration (same person as committer and final approver). Mere absence of actor identities is a **logging failure** requiring remediation, not proof of breach.
- **Exceptions are affirmative defences**: time, scope, authority, form and the exception's own conditions must all be proved by the party relying on them.

### 1.8 Classification convention (so the labels are not ad hoc)

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **COMPLIANT** | All applicable requirements met; requirements validly **displaced** by a competent, in-scope, in-time instrument count as not applicable; no material unexcused deviation. |
| **JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION** | An ordinary control was departed from and the departure is **authorized or justified in substance** (valid derogation, or derogation exercised under conditions imperfectly met but supported by genuine necessity, proportionality and contemporaneous authorization); residual defect immaterial/mitigated. |
| **NONCOMPLIANT** | Material control failure that **no competent instrument reaches** (wrong time, wrong scope, wrong authority, silence). |
| **INDETERMINATE** | Classification turns on a decisive fact or premise not resolvable on the record. |

Materiality test: a deviation is material if it defeats a control's purpose (unauthorized commitment, missing integrity clearance, collapsed independence, reliance on a regime that had lapsed or never covered the purchase). Pure form/sequence irregularities with intact substance are immaterial but are logged.

### Issue-specific hierarchies (deliberately not one total order)

1. **Integrity / anti-abuse (SoD, new suppliers, thresholds, exception authority):** GPIP4 A1–A3, A6, A7 **>** ECD2 (no competence) **** D (expressly excluded) **>** B (may only be stricter). Near-total order.
2. **Workflow sequencing & supplier onboarding:** **ECD2 (in window + in scope) > B1/B3 > GPIP4 (silent/neutral)**. B7 is the local gateway; ON44 governs proof of *when* the step happened.
3. **Approval modality & thresholds:** **GPIP4 A2 substance > D (modality; EUR 150,000 cap; Legal gate above) > B2 (local steps)**, powered by ECD2 C3's two-person authorization.
4. **Documentation & evidence:** **A5 + B6 (local evidence definition) + ON44 weighting > C3's 4-hour record duty > ERP entry timestamps (weakest)**.
5. **Time:** time-of-act **beats** later-in-time; a later instrument governs only later acts; later approval mitigates, never cures (A6).
