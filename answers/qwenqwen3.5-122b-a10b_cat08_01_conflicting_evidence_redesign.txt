# 1. Current-process diagnosis

The current warranty process exhibits a critical **efficiency-quality paradox**: the dominant workflow maximizes throughput and minimizes immediate labor cost while systematically degrading long-term value through rework, compliance breaches, and customer dissatisfaction.

**Key Performance & Quality Problems**
*   **The "Fast Path" Trap:** The most frequent path (`Intake`  `Auto eligibility`  `Fast approval`  `Dispatch`) handles 2,550 of 5,000 cases (51%). While this path achieves a median cycle time of roughly 5–8 hours compared to 46+ hours for manual paths, it generates a **14.1% complaint/reopen rate**, significantly higher than the 3.3% rate for the repair-quote path.
*   **Hidden Cost Inversion:** Although Fast Approval cases appear **31% cheaper** in direct labor/materials, this advantage collapses to only **9%** after accounting for downstream costs (reshipments, service credits, rework). The cost of a wrong replacement for high-value devices (EUR 410) outweighs the savings from skipping manual review.
*   **Compliance Erosion:** Conformance analysis reveals a **fitness of 0.91 but only 68% fitting traces**. Frequent deviations include `Fast approval` occurring before `Evidence completeness` (violating R2) and high-value devices bypassing required `Manual eligibility` or `Fraud/risk review` (violating R1).
*   **Evidence Loop Friction:** Cases entering `Request missing evidence` have a **26% recurrence rate** (requested at least twice). This creates a massive temporal drag: `Request missing evidence`  `Manual eligibility review` takes a median of 26 hours and p90 of 88 hours, contributing to the 140-hour median cycle time for the "Unclear defect" segment (22% of volume).
*   **Resource Imbalance:** `Manual eligibility review` acts as a capacity constraint. With 7 reviewers available (252 productive hours/week), queues grow sharply when utilization exceeds 82%. Currently, 1,400 cases are forced here manually, plus 890 looping back from evidence requests, creating volatility.

**The Core Contradiction**
Management faces a false dichotomy between the "Fast/Cheap" path and the "Slow/Quality" path. The data shows the Fast path is **fast but fragile**; it fails compliance checks (R1/R2) and incurs high rework costs. The Manual path is robust but **slow and scarce**; it cannot handle the 2,550 cases currently routed through Auto without collapsing under queue pressure. The redesign must not simply choose one path but structurally separate cases where speed is safe from cases where quality gates are mandatory.

# 2. Redesign A: Segment-Aware Risk Gating

**Description**
This redesign modifies the `Auto eligibility screen` logic to enforce a **dynamic routing strategy** based on claim value, device risk, and customer segment, rather than a binary auto/manual split. It strictly enforces Normative Rules R1 and R2 at the intake stage.

**Process Flow Changes**
1.  **Intake Claim:** Enhanced data capture for `Device Value` and `Defect Type`.
2.  **Dynamic Classifier:** Replaces static Auto Screen.
    *   *Route 1 (Safe):* Low-risk accessory + Standard Segment  `Auto eligibility`  `Fast approval`  `Dispatch`.
    *   *Route 2 (Controlled):* High-value device (>EUR 400) OR Premium Segment  Bypass `Fast approval`. Route to `Fraud/risk review` (for speed) or `Manual eligibility review` (for depth).
    *   *Route 3 (Exception):* Unclear defect/Evidence gaps  Direct to `Evidence Portal` (see Redesign B integration) before eligibility decision.
3.  **Fulfillment:** `Dispatch replacement` is blocked unless `Evidence completeness` flag is TRUE (enforcing R2).

**Target Segments/Paths**
*   Specifically targets **Premium High-Value** (currently 22% complaint rate on fast path) and **Standard High-Value** (18% complaint rate).
*   Reduces inflow to `Manual eligibility review` by filtering out low-risk cases that currently get stuck there due to generic rules.

**Mechanism of Improvement**
*   **Compliance:** Eliminates R1 violations by preventing >EUR 400 claims from reaching `Fast approval` without review.
*   **Quality:** Reduces wrong-part shipments by ensuring high-value cases undergo `Fraud/risk` or `Manual` assessment (median 12–16h delay vs 1h, but prevents EUR 410 errors).
*   **Cost:** Shifts cost upstream (review time) but eliminates downstream rework costs (EUR 410 per error).

**Sacrifices/Unresolved Issues**
*   **Speed:** Cycle time for High-Value Premium cases increases (Median 20h  ~32h) to ensure compliance.
*   **Capacity:** Increases load on `Fraud/risk review` (currently bottlenecked on Mondays). Requires careful scheduling or temporary staffing.
*   **Legacy Tech:** Requires rule engine update (estimated 2 weeks deployment), but may increase manual-review inflow by 15–35% initially.

# 3. Redesign B: Evidence Integrity First

**Description**
This redesign attacks the **26% repeat evidence request rate** and the 140-hour cycle time of the "Unclear defect" segment by moving evidence validation upstream. It implements a **Digital Evidence Portal with AI-assisted validation** before the eligibility decision occurs.

**Process Flow Changes**
1.  **Intake Claim:** Immediately redirects customers to the **Evidence Portal**.
2.  **AI Validation Layer:** Automated checks on uploaded photos/documents (e.g., serial number legibility, damage visibility).
    *   *Pass:* Proceeds to `Auto eligibility` or `Manual review` with "Evidence Complete" flag.
    *   *Fail:* Instant feedback loop to customer ("Blurry photo," "Missing receipt") *before* entering the agent queue.
3.  **Eligibility:** If evidence passes AI check, skip `Request missing evidence` loop entirely. Only cases failing AI validation or requiring complex judgment enter `Manual eligibility review`.

**Target Segments/Paths**
*   Targets the **"Unclear defect / missing evidence"** segment (22% of cases, 140h median cycle).
*   Targets the `Request missing evidence`  `Manual eligibility review` loop (890 cases).

**Mechanism of Improvement**
*   **Cycle Time:** Removes the 34h median lag for evidence correction loops.
*   **Abandonment:** While digital literacy is a risk, instant feedback reduces frustration compared to waiting days for a "missing evidence" email.
*   **Quality:** Ensures `Evidence completeness` (R2) is met before `Fast approval`, reducing downstream disputes caused by insufficient proof.

**Sacrifices/Unresolved Issues**
*   **Accessibility:** May increase abandonment for low-digital-literacy customers (risk noted in prompt).
*   **Tech Dependency:** Relies on AI accuracy; false negatives will frustrate users.
*   **Compliance:** Does not inherently solve R1 (High Value bypass); must be paired with Redesign A logic or used as a prerequisite for all paths.

# 4. Redesign C: Targeted Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate

**Description**
This redesign accepts the speed of the `Fast approval` path but inserts a **lightweight pre-dispatch quality gate** specifically for high-risk items. It shifts the quality control point from "Eligibility Decision" to "Fulfillment Preparation."

**Process Flow Changes**
1.  **Intake  Eligibility:** Maintains existing `Auto eligibility`  `Fast approval` flow for speed.
2.  **Gate Trigger:** Before `Dispatch replacement`, system checks `Device Value` and `Segment`.
    *   *High-Value/Premium:* Route to **Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate** (new activity).
    *   *Low-Risk:* Skip gate, proceed to `Dispatch`.
3.  **Quality Gate:** Technician verifies part compatibility against claim description (6–18 hour hold).
4.  **Dispatch:** Only released after Gate clearance.

**Target Segments/Paths**
*   Targets **High-Value Devices** (where wrong replacement cost is EUR 410).
*   Preserves speed for **Low-Risk Accessories** (where wrong part cost is EUR 95).

**Mechanism of Improvement**
*   **Cost:** Captures wrong-part errors (EUR 410) before shipment, preserving the 31% labor saving of the Fast Path while mitigating the rework penalty.
*   **Compliance:** Satisfies R2 (Evidence completeness) implicitly by requiring gate sign-off before dispatch.
*   **Experience:** Maintains speed perception for low-risk customers; ensures reliability for high-risk customers.

**Sacrifices/Unresolved Issues**
*   **Bottleneck Risk:** Adds a new resource requirement (Technicians/Inspectors). If not sized correctly, the Gate becomes the new bottleneck.
*   **Complexity:** Requires integration between Eligibility System and Warehouse Management.
*   **Speed Penalty:** Adds 6–18 hours to High-Value cycle time, though less than full Manual Review (22h+).

# 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

**Redesign A (Risk Gating)**
*   **Failure Mode 1:** *Rule Over-Sensitivity.* If the classifier routes too many borderline cases to Manual Review, the `Manual eligibility review` queue (already >82% util) will explode, causing SLA breaches for legitimate manual cases.
*   **Failure Mode 2:** *Fraud Bottleneck.* Moving High-Value cases to `Fraud/risk review` may overwhelm the 2 specialists (bottlenecked Mondays), delaying Premium customers who expect speed.
*   **Displaced Bottleneck:** Capacity shifts from `Auto eligibility` (unlimited) to `Fraud/risk review` and `Manual eligibility review`.

**Redesign B (Evidence First)**
*   **Failure Mode 1:** *Digital Exclusion.* Customers with low digital literacy may abandon the process during the mandatory portal upload, increasing the `Claim abandoned` rate (currently 320 cases).
*   **Failure Mode 2:** *AI False Positives.* If the AI rejects valid evidence too often, customers face immediate friction, potentially driving complaints even before the case enters the queue.
*   **Displaced Bottleneck:** Shifts friction from internal `Request missing evidence` loops to external customer effort at the start of the process.

**Redesign C (Pre-Dispatch Gate)**
*   **Failure Mode 1:** *Warehouse Congestion.* The new `Quality Gate` activity requires physical space and personnel. If not sized for the 2,550 Fast Approval volume (even if only 14% are high value), it creates a physical backlog.
*   **Failure Mode 2:** *Gate Leakage.* If the gate is rushed to meet speed targets, it may fail to catch wrong parts, rendering the EUR 410 risk mitigation ineffective while adding the 6–18h delay.
*   **Displaced Bottleneck:** Shifts delay from `Manual eligibility review` (back office) to `Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate` (warehouse/logistics).

# 7. Recommended experiment sequence

To mitigate risk while addressing the core contradictions, execute the following staged plan:

**Phase 1: Policy Enforcement (Redesign A Pilot)**
*   **Pilot Scope:** Apply strict R1 enforcement only for **Premium High-Value** devices (14% of volume). Do not touch Standard cases yet.
*   **Metrics to Monitor:**
    *   Manual Review Queue Utilization (Must stay <85%).
    *   Premium High-Value Complaint Rate (Target: <10%, down from 22%).
    *   Cycle Time Variance (Ensure no >48h outliers).
*   **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
    *   *Stop:* If Manual queue wait time exceeds 24 hours for >3 consecutive days.
    *   *Go:* If Complaint Rate drops by 50% without impacting overall Manual Util >85%.
*   **Sequencing Logic:** Lowest technical risk (rule change vs. software build). Validates whether removing Fast Approval from High-Value actually improves quality without breaking capacity.

**Phase 2: Evidence Validation (Redesign B Integration)**
*   **Pilot Scope:** Roll out Evidence Portal for the **"Unclear defect"** segment (22% of volume) only.
*   **Metrics to Monitor:**
    *   Evidence Request Loop Rate (Target: Reduce 26% repeat requests to <15%).
    *   Abandonment Rate (Watch for spikes >5% above baseline).
    *   Median Cycle Time for Unclear Defects (Target: <100h).
*   **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
    *   *Stop:* If Abandonment Rate increases by >2 percentage points.
    *   *Go:* If Loop Rate decreases by >20% and Abandonment remains stable.
*   **Sequencing Logic:** Addresses the longest tail (140h cycle). Can run parallel to Phase 1 as it targets different segments.

**Phase 3: Fulfillment Safety Net (Redesign C)**
*   **Pilot Scope:** Implement Pre-Dispatch Gate for **Standard High-Value** devices (if Phase 1 leaves these on Fast Path).
*   **Metrics to Monitor:**
    *   Wrong Replacement Rate (Target: <1%).
    *   Gate Throughput Time (Target: <12h median).
    *   Cost Savings (Verify EUR 410 error reduction offsets Gate labor).
*   **Learning Real Improvements:** Compare downstream complaint rates and reshipment costs between the Pilot Group (Gate applied) and Control Group (Fast Path only). If complaints drop but costs rise disproportionately to labor savings, the Gate is over-engineered.

**Validation of Downstream Shift:**
Monitor `Customer complaint/reopen` metrics specifically for the `Escalation board` path. If complaints decrease in `Fast approval` but increase in `Escalation board` (due to stricter initial denial or longer waits), the problem has been shifted, not solved. True improvement requires a net reduction in total `Customer complaint/reopen` volume across all paths.
