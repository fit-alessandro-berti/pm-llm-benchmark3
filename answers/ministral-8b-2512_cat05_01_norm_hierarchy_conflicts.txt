### **1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method**

#### **Facts (non-controversial)**
- **Normative hierarchy**: GPIP-4 is board-approved policy; DE-AAC-PROC-17 is a controlled local SOP; ECD-2 is an incident directive; the CFO email is informal.
- **Temporal scope**: ECD-2’s effective window is explicit (2026-07-14 09:00–18:00 CEST); post-ECD-2 actions must comply with pre-existing norms unless explicitly extended.
- **Evidential hierarchy**: Contemporaneous records (telephone logs, signed PDFs, supplier acknowledgments) outweigh ERP timestamps during outages. Written records (e.g., signed PDFs) are stronger than verbal authorizations unless explicitly allowed.
- **Authority constraints**:
  - **GPIP-4** reserves formal exceptions to the **Chief Risk Officer (CRO)** (A7). ECD-2 is not signed by the CRO.
  - **CFO** can interpret Finance controls (D3) but lacks explicit authority to waive Compliance or segregation-of-duties rules.
  - **Incident Command** can override "ordinary local workflow steps" (B7) but does not have inherent authority to waive GPIP-4’s mandatory controls (e.g., Compliance clearance for new suppliers).
- **Emergency necessity**: ECD-2’s scope is limited to "critical sterilization-line lots" (C1). Non-production-related purchases (e.g., cafeteria freezer) are not covered.

#### **Assumptions (defensible but contestable)**
- **Incident directives as temporary policy overlays**: ECD-2’s authority is limited to the stated scope and does not implicitly override GPIP-4’s segregation-of-duties or Compliance requirements unless explicitly stated. The organization’s prior treatment of signed directives as "temporary policy" does not extend to new-supplier Compliance clearance.
- **Retrospective records**: A signed PDF queued during a system outage (e.g., P-507) is valid if created within the time window and signed by authorized parties, but verbal authorizations without contemporaneous records are insufficient.
- **Finance approval**: The CFO’s email (D1–D2) does not waive Compliance clearance or segregation-of-duties but may imply that Finance approval is deferred to post-emergency review if not feasible during the incident.
- **Approved-supplier rule**: ECD-2’s waiver of the approved-supplier requirement (C2) is limited to "critical sterilization-line lots." Non-critical purchases (e.g., cafeteria repairs) remain subject to B3.

#### **Decision Method**
- **Norm application**: Rules are applied at the time of the act, not in hindsight. Emergency directives override ordinary workflows only where explicitly stated (e.g., timing, supplier onboarding).
- **Burden of proof**: The organization must demonstrate that deviations were justified by the controlling directive’s scope and that all mandatory controls (e.g., Compliance clearance for new suppliers) were either waived by the correct authority or met retroactively.
- **Severity grading**: Deviations are classified by:
  1. **Critical controls** (GPIP-4 A2–A3, B3, A1 segregation-of-duties) that are not waived.
  2. **Emergency-specific relaxations** (e.g., supplier commitment before PO) that are within ECD-2’s scope.
  3. **Informal deviations** (e.g., verbal authorizations without records) that lack explicit authority.

### **6. Evidential Sufficiency and Remediation**

#### **Policy Breach vs. Logging Defect**
- **P-501, P-502, P-505, P-507, P-508**: **Policy breach** (deviations from GPIP-4 or ECD-2’s scope). Remediation:
  - Retroactively obtain missing approvals (Compliance, Finance) for post-ECD-2 cases.
  - Document Incident Command’s justification for scope interpretations (e.g., "critical lot" definition).
- **P-503, P-504, P-506**: **Policy breach** (non-compliance with GPIP-4’s mandatory controls).
  - **P-503**: Require formal delegation logs for future emergencies.
  - **P-504**: Exclude non-production purchases from ECD-2 scope in future directives.
  - **P-506**: Mandate written extensions for ECD-2.

#### **Late Evidence of Timely Acts**
- **P-507**: Signed PDF at 12:40 is valid despite ERP delay; no breach.
- **P-505**: ERP timestamp may be unreliable; supplier acknowledgment at 17:59:31 is decisive.

#### **Inability to Prove Compliance**
- **P-506**: No written extension; reliance on verbal authorization is insufficient.

#### **Immediate Remediation Actions**
1. **Audit all post-ECD-2 cases** (P-506) for compliance with GPIP-4.
2. **Clarify "critical sterilization-line lots"** in ECD-2’s scope (e.g., define time window for backup-line components).
3. **Require signed PDFs for verbal Incident Command authorizations** during emergencies.
4. **Train Procurement on segregation-of-duties** (R-001) even during emergencies.

#### **Monitoring Actions**
- **ERP system redundancy**: Ensure real-time logging during outages.
- **Incident Command charter**: Explicitly define authority limits (e.g., cannot waive Compliance clearance).
- **Post-ECD-2 review**: Mandate compliance audits for all emergency purchases.

### **7. Recommended Policy Clarification**

**Proposed Amendment to GPIP-4 (A7) and ECD-2 Template**:

---
**GPIP-4, Section A7 (Exceptions)** *(Revised)*:
> *"The Chief Risk Officer may issue written, scoped, time-limited exceptions to any control in this policy. **Emergency incident directives (e.g., ECD-2) may override ordinary purchasing workflows only if:**
> 1. **Signed jointly by the COO and Incident Commander;**
> 2. **Explicitly state which controls are waived (e.g., supplier onboarding, timing), and which remain mandatory (e.g., segregation-of-duties, Compliance clearance for new suppliers);**
> 3. **Define the scope of "critical" purchases (e.g., time window, production impact);**
> 4. **Require contemporaneous records for verbal authorizations (e.g., signed PDFs within 4 hours).*
> *Exceptions must not weaken mandatory controls without explicit CRO approval."*

---
**ECD-2 Template Addendum**:
> *"**Scope Definition:**
> - 'Critical sterilization-line lots' means components required to:
>   - Restore production within [X] hours of contamination/event onset, or
>   - Prevent safety hazards directly tied to the affected line.
> - **Non-production purchases (e.g., cafeteria, utilities) are excluded.**
>
> **Control Waivers:**
> - [ ] Supplier onboarding requirements (B3)
> - [ ] PO creation timing (B1)
> - [ ] Finance approval (A2/B2) [Deferral only; must be retroactively obtained]
> - [ ] Compliance clearance for new suppliers (A3) [Only if CRO-exempted]
> - [ ] Segregation-of-duties (A1) [Never waivable]
>
> **Evidential Requirements:**
> - Verbal authorizations must be recorded in a signed PDF within 4 hours.
> - All waived controls must be retroactively satisfied post-emergency unless explicitly exempted."*

---
**Safeguard Against Emergency Normalization**:
> *"Post-emergency, all purchases made under ECD-2 must undergo a compliance audit within 72 hours. Deviations from mandatory controls (e.g., Compliance clearance, segregation-of-duties) require formal CRO ratification or corrective action."*

---
**Rationale**:
- **Explicit hierarchy**: Separates waivable (timing, supplier onboarding) from non-waivable (Compliance, segregation-of-duties) controls.
- **Scope precision**: Defines "critical" to prevent overreach (e.g., cafeteria repairs).
- **Evidential discipline**: Requires contemporaneous records to prevent retrospective justification.
- **Post-emergency accountability**: Ensures no permanent normalization of emergency deviations.
