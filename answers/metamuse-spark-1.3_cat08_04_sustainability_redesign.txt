# 1. System boundary and assumptions
**Environmental scope used:** Gate-to-delivery operational footprint per spare-part order, calculated from enriched event-log attributes. This is not a full ISO-LCA.
- **Inside boundary:**
  - Production energy: `energy_kwh` at `CNC_A, CNC_B, heat_treat, inspection_cell`, including `Batch wait -> CNC production` and `Heat treatment -> Inspection` loops and `Rework production -> CNC production` (390 cases) + `Inspection -> Rework production` (470 cases).
  - Material waste: `material_scrap_kg` from production + `package_material_kg` from `Package standard / Package reinforced / Rework packaging`, including `Rework packaging -> Package reinforced (420) / Package standard (130)`.
  - Outbound transport: `transport_kgco2e` by `route_mode` (`parcel_ground, parcel_air, dedicated_van, consolidated_truck`) for paths `Ship ground (6660 total), Ship air (1420), Dedicated van (610), Consolidated truck (830)` to `Confirm delivery`.
  - Failure demand: second shipments from `Partial shipment (1010) -> Backorder wait (220)` and damage returns avoided by reinforced packaging, and cancellations `Cancel due to delay (530)`.
- **Outside boundary:** Upstream raw material extraction, inbound supply to stock, customer use-phase and end-of-life of parts, warehousing HVAC, empty return of `dedicated_van`, and production of packaging materials beyond weight proxy. `Close -> END (11200)` stops observation.
- **Key assumptions and uncertainty:**
  - Log is flattened by order; multi-line orders share `batch_id, energy_kwh, transport_kgco2e`. Per-item allocation assumes equal split unless `batch_size` available. This understates variance for Made-to-order (MTO) heavy parts.
  - Batch energy is allocated per order, not metered per part; `CNC_A 2.8 kWh/item at size 1-3 to 1.3 kWh/item at 12-20` is an average, sensitive to machine idle and heat-treat batch fill.
  - Transport `kgCO2e/order` uses route_mode factors; load factor and empty van returns are not fully logged, so dedicated-van impact (avg 52.0 kgCO2e for Critical path) is likely underestimated.
  - Obsolescence after `Close` is only partially visible: `6% of batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days` signals waste but post-log scrapping is unobserved.
  - Service is measured as `median / p90 cycle time` vs `promised_lead_time_hours` resulting in `Late delivery`, not customer downtime cost. Critical-downtime 15h median / 30h p90 has low tolerance for added wait.

# 2. Sustainability-related process indicators
All indicators sliceable by `case_type x customer_type x route_mode x machine_group` and by DFG variant.

**Energy:**
- E1: Avg `energy_kwh / order` and `/ item` by segment and by `machine_group + batch_size` bin. Baseline: Standard-stock 0.8-0.9, MTO Regular 18.5, MTO Service-contract 20.0, Critical 4.5.
- E2: Batch efficiency: `energy_kwh / item` vs `Batch wait` hours for `CNC_A, CNC_B, heat_treat`. Tracks trade-off 4h to 38h wait.

**Transport:**
- T1: Avg `transport_kgCO2e / order` and share by `route_mode`. Baseline: Standard-stock Regular 4.2, Service-contract 7.8, MTO 8.0-12.0, Critical 38.0.
- T2: SP1-conform ground-eligible share: % orders where `promised_lead_time_hours` allowed ground/consolidated with >85% on-time probability but air/van used. Currently SP1 violated in 22% of Standard-stock Service-contract cases.
- T3: Consolidation rate: % non-urgent stock orders via `Consolidated truck`, avg fill, consolidation wait (12-24h).

**Materials and packaging:**
- M1: Avg `material_scrap_kg / order` (MTO 0.85-0.90, rework loop 1.8) + `package_material_kg / order` by `Package standard vs reinforced`.
- M2: Obsolescence rate: % batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days. Baseline 6% when forecasts wrong.
- M3: Packaging failure rate: `Rework packaging (620 total: 260+360)` / all packages, split by `route_mode x part geometry`, and Q1 violation rate for MTO >8kg without reinforced pack.

**Rework and batching:**
- R1: Rework rate: `rework_flag=true` % (Standard 3-4%, MTO 10-11%, Critical 8%) and `Inspection -> Rework production` loop rate by `machine_group`.
- R2: CNC-specific first-pass yield: CNC_B 18% rework vs CNC_A 9% for MTO; heat-treat batch >10 efficiency vs 1.7x late risk.
- R3: Batch discipline: median `Batch wait` time, % capped by max-wait, batch_size distribution.

**Service:**
- S1: Median and p90 cycle time vs `promised_lead_time_hours` by segment and variant (e.g., Stock-standard-ground 34h median 8% late; Stock-standard-air 17h 3% late; Critical-emergency-van 14h 3% late).
- S2: Late delivery % and `Cancel due to delay` % (410 from Availability check + 60 from Backorder wait + 60 other = 530 END). Partial-shipment path 79h median 24% late.
- S3: `expedite_flag` and `Emergency production (760)` rate by segment.

**Quality:**
- Q1: Damage-related return / packaging-rework rate. Reinforced reduces returns 40% for MTO heavy parts.
- Q2: Inspection escape rate: defects found in packaging/customer stage after rushed `inspection_cell` queue.

**Resilience:**
- RES1: Alternative-route availability for Service-contract during disruption (R1): % cases with feasible second `route_mode` without model precision collapse.
- RES2: Share of Critical orders dependent on single lane (dedicated van only) and SP2 justification completeness (31% missing). Carrier-disruption late % for Service-contract segments.

# 3. Intervention options
## S1. Green shipping recommender using promised lead time, reliability, and segment
- **Target path/segment:** All `Package -> Ship` choices, especially Stock-standard-air (5%, 18.5 kgCO2e) and MTO-reinforced-air (part of 6% MTO immediate-air variant, 23.0 kgCO2e).
- **Mechanism:** At shipping, compute slack = `promised_lead_time_hours - expected ground/consolidated time (p85)`; default to ground/consolidated if SP1 >85% on-time probability met. Requires `route_mode` reliability table by lane.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Neutral if slack respected; small +2-6h cycle vs air saving 24h median. Protects R1 by keeping air/van as fallback. Risk if reliability estimates stale.
- **Evidence to monitor:** SP1 conformance, `route_mode` mix, late % by slack bin, `expedite_flag` overrides.

## S2. Dynamic batching for CNC and heat treatment with max-wait caps by segment
- **Target:** `Plan production (3780) -> Batch wait (3020) -> CNC production (2910)` + heat-treat batching; 13% efficient variant `MTO-batch-CNC_A-heat-inspection-ground (118h, 16.0 kWh, 5.8 kgCO2e)`.
- **Mechanism:** Batch to size 12-20 for CNC_A (1.3 vs 2.8 kWh/item) and >10 for heat-treat, but cap wait: e.g., Standard MTO Regular 36h, MTO Service-contract 24h, Critical 0-4h bypass. Release incomplete batch at cap.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Energy per item down, but Batch wait 4h to 38h and heat-wait 1.7x late risk. Caps prevent 180h p90 tail and 21-32% late. No direct quality loss.
- **Evidence to monitor:** `batch_size` histogram, `Batch wait` duration, `energy_kwh/item`, late % by cap, obsolescence 14-day non-shipment.

## S3. Shift MTO from CNC_B to CNC_A when queue and slack allow
- **Target:** 5% rework-dominated variant `MTO-batch-CNC_B-inspection-rework-ground (160h, 32.0 kWh, 1.8kg scrap, 32% late)` vs CNC_A 9% vs CNC_B 18% rework.
- **Mechanism:** Route to CNC_A if queue + due-date slack permits; reserve CNC_B for true rush. Avoids `Inspection -> Rework production (470) -> CNC production (390)` loop which doubles energy and scrap.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Cycle +~10-20h waiting for CNC_A but late risk net down by avoiding 160h rework tail; quality up, scrap down. Resilience down slightly if CNC_A becomes bottleneck — must keep CNC_B qualified.
- **Evidence to monitor:** `machine_group` share, queue time, `rework_flag` by machine, `material_scrap_kg`, `energy_kwh`.

## S4. Replace air for Standard-stock Service-contract with priority pick + ground unless slack insufficient
- **Target:** Standard-stock Service-contract (16%, 18h median, 7.8 kgCO2e vs 4.2 Regular) where SP1 violated in 22%; Stock-standard-air path.
- **Mechanism:** `Pick from stock (7050) -> Package standard (6040)` accelerated by priority picking, then `Ship ground` instead of `Ship air`. Same `promised_lead_time_hours` met without 4-6x air factor (3.6 vs 18.5 kgCO2e).
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Saves ~14.9 kgCO2e/order when applied; cycle +~10-17h vs air but within 18h median / 41h p90 envelope for this segment. Late risk flat if slack check enforced. No quality change.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Air share in this segment, pick-to-ship time, SP1 conformance, late %, `promised_lead_time_hours` adherence.

## S5. Consolidate truck departures twice daily for non-urgent stock orders
- **Target:** Stock-standard-ground 42% (34h, 3.6 kgCO2e) eligible for `Consolidated truck (830 cases)`; excludes Critical and tight-slack Service-contract.
- **Mechanism:** Hold non-urgent `Package standard` 12h max for twice-daily truck; 35-55% transport cut vs individual ground parcels.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Cycle +12-24h consolidation wait; unsuitable for 15h Critical or <24h promise. Late risk up if misclassified. Resilience up via fuller trucks, down if single consolidation point fails — need split departures.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Consolidation wait, truck fill, `transport_kgCO2e/order`, late % by promise bin, R1 alternative retained.

## S6. Improve packaging-route compatibility checks before shipment
- **Target:** `Package standard -> Rework packaging (260)` + `Package reinforced -> Rework packaging (360)`; total 620 reworks; Q1 violations for MTO >8kg.
- **Mechanism:** Hard check: part weight/geometry + `route_mode` => required pack type before `Ship`. Prevents incompatible choice and double handling (`Rework packaging -> Package reinforced 420`).
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Cycle - rework loop (often 1-2 days), late down, `package_material_kg` net down despite reinforced doubling material where needed because 40% damage returns avoided. No resilience loss.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Rework packaging count, Q1 conformance, return rate, `package_material_kg` + extra transport for returns.

## S7. Add inspection quality gate for high-rework machine/part combinations
- **Target:** `CNC production (3670 incl. 720 emergency + 390 rework) -> Heat (1820) / Inspection (1710+1780) -> Rework (470)`; rushed `inspection_cell` queues causing escapes.
- **Mechanism:** Mandatory extended inspection for CNC_B + heavy MTO + heat-treat parts; do not skip under time pressure. Catches defects before `Package reinforced` and shipment.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Adds 2-6h inspection but avoids 90h+ rework tail (160h vs 70-118h variants) and 32 kWh / 1.8kg scrap rework variant. Service net improves p90. Quality up.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Inspection queue time, escape rate, `rework_flag`, `material_scrap_kg`, `energy_kwh` per completed order including loops.

## S9. Split Critical-downtime into true downtime and precautionary urgent
- **Target:** Critical Service-contract 11% (15h median, 38.0 kgCO2e); 8% emergency-van path (14h, 7.0 kWh, 52.0 kgCO2e) vs 3% stock/ground exception (23h, 4.8 kgCO2e, 9% late).
- **Mechanism:** Triage at `Receive order -> Availability check`: true machine-down keeps `Emergency production (760) + Dedicated van`; precautionary uses stock/expedited ground or shared lane. Fixes 31% SP2 missing justification.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Protects true downtime service (3% late) while cutting unjustified van emissions. Mis-triage risk is high cost — needs explicit sign-off and fast escalation to van.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Split share, SP2 justification completeness, late % for true vs precautionary, `transport_kgCO2e`, escalation time.

## S11. Reduce partial shipments by changing availability-check rules
- **Target:** `Pick from stock -> Partial shipment (1010) -> Package standard (790) + Backorder wait (220) -> Cancel (60)`; 7% path 79h, 24% late, 7.2 kgCO2e (2x shipments) + 1.4 kWh.
- **Mechanism:** At `Availability check`, if full availability within 24-48h, hold and ship complete via ground/consolidated; partial only if customer confirms downtime need or backorder > threshold. Prevents second `Ship ground` + packaging.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Cycle median down from 79h for held orders, late 24% down, contact/return risk down. Short-term perceived service (first item fast) down — needs customer agreement. Resilience neutral.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Partial rate, backorder wait duration, late %, cancel %, `transport_kgCO2e` (shipments/order), customer acceptance.

## S12. Create sustainability exception log for SP1/SP2/Q1 overrides
- **Target:** Cross-cutting governance for SP1 22% violation, SP2 31% missing, Q1 return loops; low precision due to emergency + partial combinations.
- **Mechanism:** Require structured override event (reason, slack, approver) when choosing air/van/non-reinforced against policy; mines conformance directly.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** No direct environmental saving; enables learning and prevents blanket bans that would harm service/resilience. Small admin time.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Override rate by segment, conformance trends, late/return outcomes of overrides vs conforming cases.

# 5. Rebound and displacement risks
- **Batching  waiting  air displacement:** Larger CNC_A (12-20) and heat >10 batches cut `energy_kwh/item` but push Batch wait 4h38h and 1.7x late risk; planners compensate with `Ship air (620+800)` or `Emergency production (760)`, displacing production energy saving into 4-6x transport emissions (MTO immediate-air variant 24.0 kWh + 23.0 kgCO2e).
- **Batching  obsolescence:** Efficient 13% MTO-ground variant (16.0 kWh) looks green per shipped order, but 6% of batch-produced items not shipped in 14 days when forecasts wrong shifts scrap/energy to unlogged dead stock, raising true `material_scrap_kg` and negating per-item gains.
- **Transport consolidation  delay  express rebound:** Consolidated truck -35 to -55% kgCO2e requires 12-24h wait; applied to tight-slack Service-contract or MTO orders it triggers late delivery then `expedite_flag`, dedicated van, or partial shipment second leg (7.2 kgCO2e), worse than direct ground.
- **Light packaging  damage  double footprint:** Cutting `package_material_kg` by using standard instead of reinforced for MTO >8kg (Q1 violation) saves material once but increases damage returns by ~67% (inverse of 40% reduction), causing return transport + `Rework packaging (360)` + re-production energy/scrap.
- **Rework avoidance via rushed inspection  escape:** Skipping extended `inspection_cell` to save 2-6h and meet promise creates escapes that later surface as packaging rework or customer returns; the 5% CNC_B-rework-ground variant shows how 470 rework loops drive 32.0 kWh + 1.8kg scrap, far exceeding inspection cost.
- **Partial-shipment ban  cancellation / emergency order:** Holding for complete shipment to save second 7.2 kgCO2e shipment may frustrate downtime customers, leading to `Cancel due to delay (530 total)` followed by new emergency order with `Emergency production + Dedicated van (52.0 kgCO2e)`, multiplying impact.
- **Customer behavior  promise tightening:** If green options lengthen quoted lead times (e.g., S10 carbon-aware dates, S5 consolidation), Regular MTO customers (22%, 18% late, 180h p90) may inflate urgency, misdeclare Critical-downtime, or split orders, increasing precautionary van/air use and eroding SP2 control. Requires S9 triage and S12 override logging to detect.

# 6. Balanced recommendation
**Coherent package, phased, not lowest-emission-at-all-costs:** Do not force all Critical to stock/ground (23h, 9% late vs 14h, 3% late — unacceptable downtime risk) nor all stock to consolidated truck (12-24h wait breaks 15-18h Critical/Service promises). Distinguish by slack and segment.

**Core layer (do first, low service risk, high evidence): S1 + S4 + S6 + S7 + S12.**
- Deploy S1 recommender enforcing SP1 (>85% on-time) to convert only slack-sufficient air to ground, starting with Standard-stock Service-contract where 22% SP1 violations indicate unjustified air (S4 with priority pick). Keep air when slack insufficient — this respects that not all air is unjustified.
- Add S6 compatibility check to eliminate 620 packaging reworks and enforce Q1 for >8kg MTO; paired with S7 inspection gate for CNC_B/heavy combos to kill the 5% 32 kWh / 1.8kg / 32% late rework tail. Together they cut hidden material waste without adding resilience risk and actually improve p90 (160h~118h).
- Underpin with S12 exception log so every SP1/SP2/Q1 override is mined; this prevents brittle bans and addresses low precision from emergency/partial combinations by making them explicit.

**Batching layer (controlled efficiency): S2 + S3.**
- Implement S2 dynamic batching with segment caps (Critical bypass, Service-contract tighter caps than Regular) to capture CNC_A 2.81.3 kWh/item and heat >10 gains while capping 38h waits and 1.7x late risk. Couple with S3 preferential CNC_A routing when queue/slack allows to halve rework 18%9%, but keep CNC_B qualified and monitored to preserve resilience and avoid CNC_A bottleneck. Monitor 6% obsolescence guardrail: if 14-day non-shipment rises, shrink batch targets.

**Transport and availability layer (selective): S5 (narrow) + S11 (narrow) + S9 + shared lane.**
- Limit S5 consolidation to non-urgent Standard-stock Regular with >24h slack and twice-daily departures on two lanes (supports R1). Exclude Critical and tight MTO Service-contract.
- Limit S11 to holding partials only when full availability <48h and customer is Regular non-downtime; keep immediate partial for true downtime with explicit consent.
- Implement S9 triage to separate true downtime (retain emergency + dedicated van with SP2 justification) from precautionary urgent (shift to expedited ground/shared emergency courier lane per S8 idea). This cuts the 38.0 avg and 52.0 van peaks where unjustified, without violating service for true 11% Critical. Require escalation path <2h if precautionary deteriorates.

Why balanced: Energy and transport fall where slack exists (Standard-stock air, consolidatable ground, avoidable rework energy), materials fall via scrap + packaging-returns + obsolescence control, while service (late %, p90) is protected by slack-gated rules, quality (Q1, inspection) improves, and resilience (R1 alternative route, dual CNC, dual truck lane, retained air/van fallback, exception governance) is explicitly preserved. Pilot in Standard-stock first (55% share, low risk), then MTO Regular with promise-date negotiation buffer, last Critical triage with close monitoring.

# 7. Validation and governance plan
**Event-log validation (before/after + control):**
- Re-mine DFG monthly: compare `Pick/Plan/Batch wait/CNC/Inspection/Rework/Pack/Ship` frequencies and variant shares (42% ground, 5% air, 7% partial, 13% efficient MTO-ground, 6% MTO-air, 5% rework, 8% van). Expect: air share down in Standard-stock Service-contract, consolidated up in non-urgent stock, CNC_A share up, `Rework production (470)` and `Rework packaging (620)` down, `Batch wait` mean up modestly but p90 down.
- Segment dashboards using original attributes: `energy_kwh/order, transport_kgCO2e/order, material_scrap_kg/order + package_material_kg`, median/p90 cycle, late % (`promised_lead_time_hours` vs actual), `rework_flag` %, `expedite_flag` %, `batch_size` distribution, `route_mode` mix, obsolescence 14-day rate, cancellation rate. Use difference-in-differences vs untouched lane/customer as control; watch for displacement (energy down but transport up).
- Attribute completeness: mandate `batch_id/batch_size, machine_group, route_mode, energy_kwh, transport_kgCO2e, material_scrap_kg, package_material_kg, rework_flag, expedite_flag, promised_lead_time_hours` on every relevant event; reject cases with missing SP2 justification.

**Conformance monitoring for SP1/SP2/Q1 (+R1):**
- SP1: replay `Ship air / Dedicated van` events with slack model; alert if ground/consolidated p(on-time)>85% but air/van chosen without S12 override. Target: halve 22% violation in Standard-stock Service-contract in 3 months without late % increase.
- SP2: check every `Dedicated van (610)` has preceding Critical-downtime justification + S9 true/precautionary label; target missing 31%<5%. Mine escalation from precautionary to van.
- Q1: check MTO >8kg `Package standard` without inspection low-risk cert; correlate with returns. Target zero violations + 40% damage-return reduction sustained.
- R1: quarterly disruption drill — verify alternative `route_mode` exists for Service-contract lanes; monitor precision: if emergency+partial combos spike, review triage rules.

**Segment-level service monitoring and exception governance:**
- Guardrails per segment: Standard-stock Regular late 7%, Service-contract 5%, MTO Regular 18%, MTO Service-contract 13%, Critical 4%; p90 caps 62h/41h/180h/156h/30h. Any intervention breaching guardrail for two consecutive weeks auto-reverts to prior rule for that segment.
- S12 board: weekly review of overrides (reason, approver, outcome late/return/kgCO2e); monthly sustainability-process review to promote repeated justified overrides into policy (e.g., adjust caps, add shared lane) and to flag gaming (e.g., precautionary  true downtime inflation).
- Resilience log: track carrier-failure periods, CNC downtime, inspection queue length; ensure at least one alternative route/machine remains qualified. Publish balanced scorecard jointly showing environmental, service, quality, resilience — no single KPI optimized alone.
