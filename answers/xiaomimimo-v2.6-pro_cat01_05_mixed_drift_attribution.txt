def robust_step_flags(s: pd.Series, z_thresh=3.5, rel_thresh=0.10, min_run=2):
    d = s.diff()
    mad = (d - d.median()).abs().median()
    scale = 1.4826*mad if mad > 0 else max(1e-9, d.abs().median())

def validate_inputs(m: pd.DataFrame, r: pd.DataFrame):
    msgs, warns = [], []
    req_m = {"month","cases","median_cycle_h","p90_cycle_h","raw_model_fitness",
             "mapped_model_fitness","distinct_activity_labels"} | set(PCT)
    req_r = {"month"} | set(EDGE_COLS)
    if not req_m.issubset(m.columns): msgs.append(f"missing monthly columns: {sorted(req_m - set(m.columns))}")
    if not req_r.issubset(r.columns): msgs.append(f"missing rate columns: {sorted(req_r - set(r.columns))}")
    if msgs:
        return {"status":"FAIL","errors":msgs,"warnings":warns}
    if m["month"].duplicated().any(): msgs.append("duplicate months in monthly table")
    if list(m["month"]) != sorted(m["month"]): msgs.append("months not sorted")
    for c in PCT:
        if not m[c].between(0,100).all(): msgs.append(f"{c} outside [0,100]")
    for c in FIT:
        if not m[c].between(0,1).all(): msgs.append(f"{c} outside [0,1]")
    if (m["mapped_model_fitness"] < m["raw_model_fitness"] - 1e-9).any():
        warns.append("mapped fitness below raw somewhere: the harmonisation map would be harmful there")
    if (m["p90_cycle_h"] < m["median_cycle_h"]).any(): msgs.append("p90 < median")
    if (m["cases"] <= 0).any(): msgs.append("non-positive case count")
    if not (r["reg_to_elig"] == 100).all():
        warns.append("reg_to_elig != 100 for some months (fragment is not 1 event per case)")
    if (r["requestinfo_to_collectdocs"] > r["assess_to_requestinfo"]).any():
        msgs.append("more RequestInfo->CollectDocs than Assess->RequestInfo (impossible loop accounting)")
    if r[EDGE_COLS].min().min() < 0: msgs.append("negative DFG rates")
    return {"status": "PASS" if not msgs else "FAIL", "errors": msgs, "warnings": warns}

```python
def simple_changepoint_flags(s: pd.Series, months: pd.Series, z_thresh=3.5, rel_thresh=0.10):
    """Explainable two-rule detector on month-over-month deltas:
 R1 (step): |delta - median(delta)| > z_thresh * 1.4826*MAD(delta)
       R2 (drift): |rel delta| > rel_thresh AND same sign as the previous delta (>=2 month run)"""
    s = pd.Series(np.asarray(s, float), index=np.asarray(months))
    d = s.diff()
    med = d.median()
    mad = (d - med).abs().median()
    scale = 1.4826 * mad
    if not np.isfinite(scale) or scale < 1e-9:
        scale = max(1e-9, d.abs().median())
    z = (d - med) / scale
    rel = d / s.shift(1).abs().replace(0, np.nan)
    step = z.abs() > z_thresh
    same_sign = np.sign(d) == np.sign(d.shift(1))
    drift = (rel.abs() > rel_thresh) & same_sign.fillna(False)
    return pd.DataFrame({"value": s, "delta": d, "robust_z": z.round(2),
                         "rel_delta": rel.round(3), "RULE1_step": step,
                         "RULE2_sustained_drift": drift})
```

def temporal_profile(df_fmt, business_hours=False):
    """pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(log) -> (temporal_profile[(a1,a2)] = (mean, stdev),
                                                seconds_distribution[(a1,a2)] = EmpiricalCumulativeDistribution)"""
    tp, secs = pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(df_fmt, business_hours=business_hours)
    tp_df = pd.DataFrame([(a1, a2, mu, sd) for (a1, a2), (mu, sd) in tp.items()],

    tp_df["cv"] = tp_df["stdev_seconds"] / tp_df["mean_seconds"].replace(0, np.nan)
    return tp_df.sort_values("mean_seconds", ascending=False), tp, secs

## 2. Raw-versus-normalized interpretation

**(1) What the gap implies.** The raw series is not a behavioural signal after 2026-03-01. The rawmapped gap opens in one step (0.35) and then stays flat (0.32–0.40) for ten months. A step-and-plateau is the signature of a one-off re-binding of labels plus duplicated technical events, not of accumulating behaviour change. 86–96 % of every month's apparent raw-fitness loss is recovered by harmonisation alone.

**(2) What it does not imply.** That the business process was unchanged. Mapping `Eligibility Screen``Validate Eligibility` only shows the relabelled trace language is still acceptable under the old normative model. It cannot detect a silently cheaper activity under the old name, changed eligibility criteria, changed decision authority, or changed service level — all of which stay model-conformant.

**(3) Missing resources and duplicated `Open Case` do not hit the same analyses.** They corrupt resource- and handover-level work (work distribution, in-basket queues, per-assessor waiting) and inflate variants and DFG counts, but barely move case durations or start/end statistics. Conformance fitness is exquisitely sensitive to them; cycle-time and queue-wait statistics are largely immune.

**(4) Post-May patterns cannot be dismissed as logging drift.** After harmonisation, mapped fitness still falls 0.930.86; normalized DFG distance rises from 0.7 % to 10–16 % of baseline edge volume; `AssessRequestInfo` doubles in the *normalized* relations; and median and p90 durations — computed from timestamps — rise 45–78 % while recorded `Assess` service time rises 12 %. Semantic drift does not lengthen a clock.

## 6. Segmentation plan

| segment level | variables | purpose | minimum support rule | comparisons enabled | risk of conditioning bias |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| S0 — intake cohort, monthly | registration month (`Register`) | global drift screen **without completion censoring** |  300 cases / month (met in all 12 months) | month vs month; vs baseline; change-point detection | pooled aggregates conflate mix and behaviour (ecological fallacy) |
| S1 — cohort × supplier exposure | Supplier Z flag, region | isolate the supplier mechanism |  50 cases **and**  200 events per cell-month; merge region cells otherwise | DiD pre/post 0505 and 1009; stratum DFGs | Z is non-random by region/customer (selection); supplier can change mid-case (time-varying exposure) |
| S2 — cohort × complexity | HC / standard flag | separate mix from behaviour; enable standardisation |  50 cases per cell-month | mix-standardised distances; within-stratum constraint strength | complexity is a mediator of the supplier effect and may be re-coded after 0107 |
| S3 — cohort × priority class / queue route | priority class, senior route flag | detect **redistribution** of delay rather than net queue growth |  50 cases per class-month, else 3-month rolling | wait-by-class before/after 0107; redistribution identity | priority is assigned from complexity  post-treatment / mediator conditioning |
| S4 — cohort × handling team (rolling quarter) | team derived from `org:resource` | capacity and behavioural heterogeneity |  60 cases **and**  80 % resource completeness per team-quarter | team fixed effects; handover networks | team assignment was altered by the redistribution  endogenous; MNAR resource missingness |
| S5 — change-window panels | windows around 0103, 0505, 1506, 0107, 1009, 0110, 0512 | verify/deny each candidate change point |  4 weeks and  200 cases on each side | event studies / interrupted time series | overlapping windows share observations  dependent estimates |
| S6 — completion cohort (closed cases) | closure date | **only** outcome and total-cost metrics |  100 closures per month | outcome mix, total rework cost per case | right-censoring and survivorship: excludes the slowest cases exactly when drift is worst |
| S7 — region × intake channel | region, channel | distinguish composition shift from supplier behaviour |  40 cases per cell-month, else collapse to region level | mix decomposition of the distance | high dimensionality  tiny cells and multiple testing |

**Primary monitoring population: the S0 monthly registration cohort, stratified into the S1 × S2 panel (Supplier Z × complexity, four strata), with the non-Z / standard-complexity stratum designated the sentinel reference series.** Reason: (i) an intake (registration) cohort removes the month-boundary right-censoring that otherwise fakes outcome drift whenever cycle times lengthen; (ii) the four-stratum panel keeps every cell well above support limits (non-Z standard  350–500 cases/month even at peak season) while holding the two dominant mediators — supplier exposure and intrinsic path length — approximately fixed; (iii) the non-Z / standard stratum is the only segment in which a change in waiting, service or loop rate cannot be attributed to supplier contamination or to complexity inflation, so it is the cleanest available detector of capacity/queueing and genuine behavioural drift. Composition drift is then read off the *stratum weights*, behavioural drift off the *within-stratum* rates, and logging drift off artefacts (labels, skeleton, resource completeness) that are invariant to both.

**Seasonality.** Do **not** estimate a seasonal factor from 12 points — it is not identifiable. Instead use a *pre-specified* business calendar (documented renewal season Oct–Nov) to build month-of-year factors from prior years' operational tables, and express alerts as deviations from a **season-naive reference** (same calendar month of the prior year once  2 years exist; before that, the trailing 12-month robust envelope with the Oct–Nov window excluded from baseline estimation). M5, M7, M9 and M12 are the seasonally-exposed monitors and therefore get season-matched references; M1–M3, M10 and M13 get plain rolling references because schema, ordering and completeness should be season-invariant — that asymmetry is itself diagnostic.

**Multiple testing.** Thirteen monitors × weekly cadence is a large family. (i) Group monitors into five families (schema M1–M3, structure M4–M5, time M6–M8, resources M9–M10, outcomes M11–M13) and apply **Benjamini–Hochberg at q = 0.10 within family**, not globally. (ii) Use **monitor-specific** thresholds (robust z on MAD for continuous metrics, Wilson/CUSUM for proportions, negative-binomial prediction intervals for counts, quantile bands for p90) — never one universal threshold. (iii) Escalate to operational action only on **two-family corroboration**, e.g. a structure alert plus a time alert; single-family alerts get a diagnostic follow-up only. (iv) Maintain a **drift-signature registry** — fingerprint = (artefact family, onset type, direction, affected strata) — so a repeat of a known fingerprint is tagged *recurrence* and only escalated if it exceeds the historical envelope, while a new fingerprint opens a fresh RCA. This is what separates "the renewal season again" from "a new March".

def validate_inputs(m: pd.DataFrame, r: pd.DataFrame) -> dict:
    """Structural + semantic checks. Returns {'status','errors','warnings'}."""
    errors, warns = [], []
    req_m = {"month", "cases", "median_cycle_h", "p90_cycle_h", "distinct_activity_labels",
             "raw_model_fitness", "mapped_model_fitness"} | set(PCT_COLS)
    req_r = {"month"} | set(EDGE_COLS)
    if not req_m.issubset(m.columns):
        errors.append(f"missing monthly columns: {sorted(req_m - set(m.columns))}")
    if not req_r.issubset(r.columns):
        errors.append(f"missing rate columns: {sorted(req_r - set(r.columns))}")
    if errors:
        return {"status": "FAIL", "errors": errors, "warnings": warns}

    if m["month"].duplicated().any():
        errors.append("duplicate months in the monthly table")
    if list(m["month"]) != sorted(m["month"]):
        errors.append("months are not sorted")
    if len(m) != 12:

    for c in PCT_COLS:
        if not m[c].between(0, 100).all():
            errors.append(f"{c} outside [0,100]")
    for c in FIT_COLS:
        if not m[c].between(0, 1).all():
            errors.append(f"{c} outside [0,1]")
    if (m["p90_cycle_h"] < m["median_cycle_h"]).any():
        errors.append("p90 < median for at least one month")
    if (m["cases"] <= 0).any():
        errors.append("non-positive case count")
    if (m["mapped_model_fitness"] + 1e-9 < m["raw_model_fitness"]).any():
        warns.append("mapped < raw fitness somewhere: the harmonisation map would be harmful there")
    if not (r["reg_to_elig"] == 100).all():

    if (r["requestinfo_to_collectdocs"] > r["assess_to_requestinfo"]).any():
        errors.append("more RequestInfo->CollectDocs than Assess->RequestInfo (impossible loop accounting)")
    if r[EDGE_COLS].min().min() < 0:

    if sorted(r["month"]) != list(r["month"]):

    return {"status": "PASS" if not errors else "FAIL", "errors": errors, "warnings": warns}

# --- A5. simple, explainable change-point flags -------------------------------
def simple_changepoint_flags(s: pd.Series, months: pd.Series, z_thresh=3.5, rel_thresh=0.10):
    """Two transparent rules on month-over-month deltas:
       RULE1 (step)  : |delta - median(delta)| > z_thresh * 1.4826 * MAD(delta)
       RULE2 (drift) : |relative delta| > rel_thresh AND the sign repeats the previous
                       month (>= 2-month sustained run).
    Deliberately not a black-box PELT; used only to nominate candidate change points."""
    s = pd.Series(np.asarray(s, float), index=np.asarray(months))
    d = s.diff()
    med = d.median()
    mad = (d - med).abs().median()
    scale = 1.4826 * mad
    if not np.isfinite(scale) or scale < 1e-9:
        scale = max(1e-9, float(d.abs().median()))
    z = (d - med) / scale
    rel = d / s.shift(1).abs().replace(0, np.nan)
    same_sign = (np.sign(d) == np.sign(d.shift(1))).fillna(False)
    return pd.DataFrame({"value": s, "delta": d.round(3), "robust_z": z.round(2),
                         "rel_delta": rel.round(3),
                         "RULE1_step": (z.abs() > z_thresh),
                         "RULE2_sustained_drift": (rel.abs() > rel_thresh) & same_sign})

# PART B — REUSABLE PM4Py PIPELINE FOR THE (FUTURE) EVENT-LEVEL LOG

import pm4py
from pm4py.algo.discovery.log_skeleton import algorithm as lsk_disc
from pm4py.algo.conformance.log_skeleton import algorithm as lsk_conf

CASE_KEY = "case:concept:name"
ACT_KEY  = "concept:name"
TS_KEY   = "time:timestamp"
RES_KEY  = "org:resource"

LABEL_MAP = {"Eligibility Screen": "Validate Eligibility"}   # normalised view only
TECHNICAL_ACTIVITIES = ["Open Case"]                          # duplicated by the 2026-03-01 migration
TIME_MODES = ("events", "contained", "traces", "intersecting")


# B1. formatting -------------------------------------------------------------
def format_log(df: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """B1. Wrap a raw dataframe as an event-log-compatible dataframe.

    pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id_key=, activity_key=, timestamp_key=, resource_key=)
    adds 'case:concept:name', 'concept:name', 'time:timestamp' and sorts the log.
    Returns a NEW frame; the caller's frame is untouched."""
    df = df.copy(deep=True)
    if RES_KEY not in df.columns and "resource" in df.columns:
        df = df.rename(columns={"resource": RES_KEY})
    df[TS_KEY] = pd.to_datetime(df[TS_KEY], errors="coerce")   # naive/UTC-normalised upstream
    df = df.dropna(subset=[CASE_KEY, ACT_KEY, TS_KEY])
    df = df.sort_values([CASE_KEY, TS_KEY], kind="mergesort").reset_index(drop=True)
    return pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id_key=CASE_KEY, activity_key=ACT_KEY,
                                  timestamp_key=TS_KEY, resource_key=RES_KEY)


def to_event_log(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame):
    """pm4py.convert_to_event_log: DataFrame -> EventLog (needed by skeleton/DECLARE)."""
    return pm4py.convert_to_event_log(df_fmt)


# B2. time slicing -----------------------------------------------------------
def slice_window(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, start, end, mode: str = "contained") -> pd.DataFrame:
    """B2. pm4py.filter_time_range(log, dt1, dt2, mode=...).

    mode in {'events','contained','traces','intersecting'}:
      'contained'   -> traces wholly inside the window (no partial traces)  [default]
      'events'      -> events inside the window (CREATES truncated traces -> biased variants)
      'intersecting'-> any trace overlapping the window (can leak pre/post events)
    'contained' is the safe default for variant/DFG work; report the mode with every number."""
    if mode not in TIME_MODES:
        raise ValueError(f"mode must be one of {TIME_MODES}")
    return pm4py.filter_time_range(df_fmt, start, end, mode=mode)


def _month_windows(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame):
    """Yield (start_iso, end_iso, 'YYYY-MM') covering the log's months."""
    per = df_fmt[TS_KEY].dt.to_period("M")
    for p in sorted(per.dropna().unique()):
        yield (p.start_time.isoformat(),
               (p.end_time + pd.Timedelta(milliseconds=1)).isoformat(),
               str(p))


def monthly_slices(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, mode: str = "contained") -> dict:
    return {tag: slice_window(df_fmt, s, e, mode=mode) for s, e, tag in _month_windows(df_fmt)}


# B3. monthly DFGs -----------------------------------------------------------
def monthly_dfgs(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, mode: str = "contained") -> dict:
    """B3. pm4py.discover_dfg(log) -> (dfg {(act1,act2): count}, start_activities, end_activities)
    computed per month. NOTE: 'contained' drops cases spanning a month boundary, which biases the
    month downward exactly when cycle times are long -> always report n_events/n_cases alongside."""
    out = {}
    for tag, sub in monthly_slices(df_fmt, mode=mode).items():
        dfg, sa, ea = pm4py.discover_dfg(sub)
        out[tag] = {"dfg": dfg, "start_activities": sa, "end_activities": ea,
                    "n_events": len(sub), "n_cases": sub[CASE_KEY].nunique(), "mode": mode}
    return out


def dfg_rate_table(monthly_dfgs: dict) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Normalise monthly DFG counts to occurrences per 100 cases (comparable to Part A)."""
    rows = []
    for tag, d in monthly_dfgs.items():
        n = max(d["n_cases"], 1)
        for (a1, a2), c in d["dfg"].items():
            rows.append({"month": tag, "relation": f"{a1} -> {a2}",
                         "count": c, "per_100_cases": 100.0 * c / n})
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)


# B4. variants ----------------------------------------------------------------
def variant_table(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, top_n: int = 25):
    """B4. pm4py.get_variants(log) -> {tuple(activities): [traces]}; returns (table, raw dict)."""
    variants = pm4py.get_variants(df_fmt)
    tab = pd.Series({ " -> ".join(k): len(v) for k, v in variants.items() },
                    name="n_traces").sort_values(ascending=False).to_frame()
    tab["share"] = tab["n_traces"] / tab["n_traces"].sum()
    tab["cum_share"] = tab["share"].cumsum()
    return tab.head(top_n), variants


# B5. temporal profiles --------------------------------------------------------
def temporal_profile(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, business_hours: bool = False):
    """B5. pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(log) ->
    (temporal_profile[(a1,a2)] = (mean_seconds, stdev_seconds),
     seconds_distribution[(a1,a2)] = empirical cdf object)."""
    tp, secs = pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(df_fmt, business_hours=business_hours)
    tab = pd.DataFrame([(a1, a2, mu, sd) for (a1, a2), (mu, sd) in tp.items()],
                       columns=["act1", "act2", "mean_seconds", "stdev_seconds"])
    tab["cv"] = tab["stdev_seconds"] / tab["mean_seconds"].replace(0, np.nan)
    tab["mean_hours"] = tab["mean_seconds"] / 3600.0
    return tab.sort_values("mean_hours", ascending=False), tp, secs


def monthly_temporal_profiles(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, mode: str = "contained") -> dict:
    out = {}
    for tag, sub in monthly_slices(df_fmt, mode=mode).items():
        tab, tp, secs = temporal_profile(sub)
        out[tag] = {"table": tab, "profile": tp, "seconds_distribution": secs}
    return out


# B6. log skeleton (stable) and DECLARE (optional) ------------------------------
def log_skeleton_constraints(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, noise_threshold: float = 0.0):
    """B6a. Log-skeleton discovery over the EventLog: constraint types include
    'always_coerce', 'exactly_one', 'never_follows', 'never_together',
    'always_precedes', 'always_follows', 'directly_follows', 'activ_freq'."""
    log = to_event_log(df_fmt)
    return lsk_disc.apply(log, parameters={lsk_disc.Parameters.NOISE_THRESHOLD: noise_threshold})


def log_skeleton_deviations(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, skeleton: dict, noise_threshold: float = 0.0):
    """B6a'. pm4py log-skeleton conformance: {constraint_type: [(trace indices, violated constraints)]}."""
    log = to_event_log(df_fmt)
    return lsk_conf.apply(log, skeleton,
                          parameters={lsk_conf.Parameters.NOISE_THRESHOLD: noise_threshold})


def declare_model(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, templates=None):
    """B6b. OPTIONAL. DECLARE discovery has no single stable pm4py.* top-level name across
    releases; where the DECLARE plug-in ships it is reached as
    pm4py.discover_declare_model(log) or pm4py.algo.discovery.declare.algorithm.apply(log, ...).
    We probe for either name and otherwise fall back to the log skeleton (same constraint
    family, documented API) so the pipeline never hard-fails."""
    log = to_event_log(df_fmt)
    top_level = getattr(pm4py, "discover_declare_model", None)
    if top_level is not None:
        return top_level(log) if templates is None else top_level(log, templates)
    try:
        from pm4py.algo.discovery.declare import algorithm as declare_discovery
    except Exception as exc:                       # pragma: no cover
        print(f"[DECLARE] plug-in unavailable ({exc}); falling back to the log skeleton.")
        return log_skeleton_constraints(df_fmt)
    params = {} if not templates else {"templates": tuple(templates)}
    return declare_discovery.apply(log, parameters=params)


# B7. raw vs normalized comparison (NON-DESTRUCTIVE) ---------------------------
def normalized_view(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, label_map=None, technical_activities=None,
                    drop_duplicated_technical: bool = True) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """B7a. Returns a NEW dataframe: (1) alias labels, (2) drop technical events,
    (3) collapse duplicated (case, activity, timestamp) rows. The raw view is hash-verified
    to be unchanged."""
    label_map = LABEL_MAP if label_map is None else label_map
    technical_activities = TECHNICAL_ACTIVITIES if technical_activities is None else technical_activities

    cols = sorted(df_fmt.columns)
    fingerprint_before = pd.util.hash_pandas_object(df_fmt[cols]).sum()

    df_n = df_fmt.copy(deep=True)
    df_n[ACT_KEY] = df_n[ACT_KEY].replace(label_map)                       # 1. label harmonisation
    df_n = df_n[~df_n[ACT_KEY].isin(technical_activities)]                 # 2. drop technical events
    if drop_duplicated_technical:
        df_n = df_n.drop_duplicates(subset=[CASE_KEY, ACT_KEY, TS_KEY], keep="first")
    df_n = df_n.sort_values([CASE_KEY, TS_KEY], kind="mergesort").reset_index(drop=True)

    fingerprint_after = pd.util.hash_pandas_object(df_fmt[cols]).sum()
    assert fingerprint_before == fingerprint_after, "the raw view was mutated!"
    return df_n


def compare_raw_vs_normalized(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame, label_map=None, technical_activities=None,
                              model=None, noise_threshold: float = 0.0) -> dict:
    """B7b. Runs the identical artefact set on the raw and the normalised view so that
    'semantic' drift and 'structural' drift can be separated. Never overwrites the raw log.

    model: optional (net, im, fm) from pm4py.discover_petri_net_inductive(...) to obtain
    alignment fitness on both views (raw label set -> low fitness; mapped -> high fitness)."""
    raw = df_fmt
    norm = normalized_view(df_fmt, label_map, technical_activities)
    out = {}
    for tag, view in (("raw", raw), ("normalized", norm)):
        dfg, sa, ea = pm4py.discover_dfg(view)
        tp, secs = pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(view)
        _, variants = variant_table(view, top_n=10 ** 6)
        entry = {
            "n_events": len(view),
            "n_cases": view[CASE_KEY].nunique(),
            "n_labels": view[ACT_KEY].nunique(),
            "label_counts": view[ACT_KEY].value_counts().to_dict(),
            "dfg": dfg, "start_activities": sa, "end_activities": ea,
            "variants": {" -> ".join(k): len(v) for k, v in variants.items()},
            "temporal_profile": tp, "seconds_distribution": secs,
            "skeleton": log_skeleton_constraints(view, noise_threshold=noise_threshold),
            "missing_resource_pct": (100.0 * view[RES_KEY].isna().mean()) if RES_KEY in view else np.nan,
        }
        if model is not None:
            net, im, fm = model
            aligned_traces, fitness = pm4py.conformance_alignments(view, net, im, fm)
            entry["alignment_fitness"] = fitness        # 'log_fitness','average_trace_fitness', ...
            entry["aligned_traces"] = aligned_traces
        out[tag] = entry

    out["dfg_edges_raw_only"] = sorted(set(out["raw"]["dfg"]) - set(out["normalized"]["dfg"]))
    out["dfg_edges_norm_only"] = sorted(set(out["normalized"]["dfg"]) - set(out["raw"]["dfg"]))
    out["fitness_gap_raw_minus_normalized"] = (
        out["raw"].get("alignment_fitness", {}).get("log_fitness", np.nan) -
        out["normalized"].get("alignment_fitness", {}).get("log_fitness", np.nan))
    return out


def reference_model(df_fmt: pd.DataFrame):
    """Convenience: (petri_net, initial_marking, final_marking) via the inductive miner."""
    return pm4py.discover_petri_net_inductive(df_fmt)

## 9. Provisional attribution

**Strongest conclusions (high confidence).** (a) *The March fitness collapse is semantic and provenance-related* (confidence 0.9): raw fitness falls 0.9250.55 while mapped fitness falls 0.025 and median cycle moves 0.05 h; 86–96 % of every later month's apparent raw-fitness loss disappears under relabelling and de-duplication, and the gap then plateaus (0.32–0.40). (b) *May's rework explosion is supplier-mediated* (0.85): `AssessRequestInfo` doubles 1224 per 100 in the month Supplier Z jumps 1843 %, and both reverse after 10 September (Z 24 %, rework 20 %). (c) *The June–August queueing surge is capacity-driven* (0.8): recorded `Assess` service time rises 12 % while reconstructed waiting rises much more, and the median shifts although only 27–29 % of cases are in rework variants — a tail effect cannot move the median.

**Plausible but not established.** The priority rule's redistribution of delay is consistent with p90/median rising 2.182.47 and `Senior Assessment` routing 722 per 100 (0.55), but monthly aggregates cannot see queue classes. The doubling of `Senior Assessment` relative to HC share (0.270.54) is ambiguous between real routing and improved label capture (0.5). October–November deterioration is genuine seasonal population drift — volume +29 %, HC 20.545 % — on a depleted capacity base (0.75).

**Mediation and interaction.** Supplier Z mediates complexity (Z cases were harder before May), so "supplier effect" and "case-mix effect" cannot be added cleanly; staffing loss amplifies supplier rework since every extra loop consumes scarce assessor time; priority scheduling redistributes rather than creates delay. December's improvement is unidentifiable: volume 13 %, HC 11 pp and two interventions start on 5 December (0.4 for any intervention effect).

**What would make me revise.** I would move toward "mostly logging" if stratum-level alignments showed no post-May log-move growth. I would move toward "mostly capacity" if team-level temporal profiles showed waiting flat within teams while composition shifted across teams. Priority attribution collapses if queue-class data show no asymmetry. Everything weakens if registration-cohort re-analysis removes the decision-rate decline — which is why that re-analysis is the first monitoring action.

## 10. Monitoring decision rules

1. **IF** raw model fitness falls while the rawmapped gap widens to a constant offset and mapped fitness is flat, **THEN** declare a schema/semantic incident, freeze all behavioural alarms and open a label-map review, **BECAUSE** a step-and-plateau rawmapped gap is generated by label re-binding plus duplicated technical events, and attributing it to process change would create a false operational escalation (as in March 2026).

2. **IF** the `AssessRequestInfo` loop rate rises by  8 per 100 cases within four weeks of a supplier completeness alert and the increase is concentrated in the supplier-exposed stratum, **THEN** open a joint supplier/process root-cause analysis and hold capacity escalation in abeyance, **BECAUSE** incomplete document bundles mechanically generate `Request Info` loops, so the signature should be stratum-localised and should decay with supplier recovery.

3. **IF** median queue waiting rises  25 % while activity service times move < 10 % and events per case are flat, **THEN** trigger a capacity action (overtime, redistribution, backfill) rather than a training or process-redesign action, **BECAUSE** waiting-time growth with flat service time and flat path length is the signature of utilisation-driven queueing from capacity loss, which process redesign cannot fix.

4. **IF** the p90/median cycle ratio rises while the median is flat and the shift is confined to standard-priority cases, **THEN** review the priority-rule threshold and cap and re-measure wait by priority class, **BECAUSE** priority scheduling redistributes delay from complex to standard cases and can inflate tail metrics without any change in total work.

5. **IF** a monthly alert matches a stored drift signature (artefact family, onset type, direction, affected strata) **and** its magnitude sits inside the pre-specified seasonal envelope for that month, **THEN** tag it *recurrence*, refresh the reference envelope and escalate only on an envelope breach; **ELSE** open a new RCA, **BECAUSE** the renewal season raises volume and complexity every October–November and pooled aggregates move without any within-stratum behavioural change, whereas a new fingerprint indicates genuinely new drift.

6. **IF** `Senior Assessment` share diverges from the high-complexity share by > 30 % of its trailing median ratio **or** missing-resource rate exceeds its trailing envelope or shows an activity×team association, **THEN** mark that month's outcome and resource metrics provisional and reroute them to the measurement-quality queue, **BECAUSE** historically inconsistent senior-assessment labelling and non-random resource missingness bias both outcome attribution and per-resource performance measurement.

7. **IF** the in-month decision rate per 100 registrations falls while the open-case stock and its age distribution rise, **THEN** recompute all monthly rates on registration cohorts and report the backlog stock beside them, **BECAUSE** lengthening cycle times right-censor long cases at month boundaries and mechanically mimic outcome drift exactly when performance is worst.
