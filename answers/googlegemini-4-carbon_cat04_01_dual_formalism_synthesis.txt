# 1. Assumptions and Interpretation

1. **Risk-route representation (low risk vs. high risk):** Because case risk level is an unmodeled data attribute (Rule 11), the risk decision after `Analyze Root Cause` is modeled as a control-flow exclusive choice (`Operator.XOR`) between two branches: (a) the atomic activity `Supervisor Review` (abstracting low-risk cases) and (b) a concurrent submodel containing both `Quality Review` and `Regulatory Review` with no ordering edge between them (abstracting high-risk cases). Once the high-risk branch is entered, *both* reviews are mandatory before `Release Material`.
2. **Optional supplier consultation representation:** Whether a supplier is involved depends on unmodeled case data (Rule 6). This optionality is modeled as an exclusive choice (`Operator.XOR`) between the visible transition `Consult Supplier` and a silent skip transition ($\tau$). Because it is not enclosed in a loop, `Consult Supplier` occurs at most once. When chosen, it is enabled immediately after `Analyze Root Cause` (in the exact specification and POWL model) and must complete before `Release Material`.
3. **Abstraction of passing vs. failing trial inspections:** Inspection pass/fail outcomes are data conditions rather than distinct event labels (Rules 8 and 11). We model the trial cycle using structured repeat-until loop semantics (`Operator.LOOP(do, redo)`), where `do` is the strict sequence `Execute Trial -> Inspect Trial` and `redo` is `Adjust Parameters`. Under PM4Py `LOOP` semantics, `do` executes at least once; each execution of `redo` (`Adjust Parameters`) represents a failed inspection and forces a subsequent execution of `do` (`Execute Trial -> Inspect Trial`); exiting the loop immediately after `Inspect Trial` represents a passing inspection. Consequently, `Adjust Parameters` can never occur without a prior failed inspection and can never occur after the final (passing) `Inspect Trial`.
4. **Prohibition of duplicate visible activity labels:** Duplicate visible labels are **not permitted** in either constructed model; each of the 14 activity names appears on at most one leaf/transition node. Duplicating subtrees or activities (such as `Trial` or `Analyze Root Cause`) to mimic non-block dependencies would either fail on the unbounded trial loop or sever single-activity identity in Petri-net conversions, corrupting conformance diagnostics, bottleneck statistics, and alignment precision.
5. **Process-tree approximation policy:** The intended causal graph contains an embedded **N-poset** (specifically among `Plan Verification`, `Analyze Root Cause`, `Execute Trial`, and the post-analysis `Approval` / `Consult Supplier` branches), which is mathematically non-series-parallel and therefore impossible to express exactly in a duplicate-free `ProcessTree`. We adopt a **strict conservative under-approximation** policy ($L(\text{tree}) \subsetneq L(\text{intended})$): we synchronize the two preparation pipelines—`Contain Material -> Plan Verification` and `Analyze Root Cause -> Design Corrective Action`—before enabling the parallel block of `{Trial Loop, Risk Approval, Optional Consult Supplier}`. This preserves 100% of safety/precedence rules, full concurrency between the two initial pipelines, and full overlap of approvals and supplier consultation with the trial loop, while excluding only traces in which approvals or `Consult Supplier` complete before `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, and `Design Corrective Action` have all finished.
6. **Interpretation of “may overlap” under completion-time logging:** By Rule 11, events represent atomic activity *completions*. At the **event-log (trace) level**, “may overlap” is observed as the absence of an ordering constraint between completion timestamps (both interleaving orders are admitted). At the **model semantics level**, we represent overlapping obligations via true causal independence (`Operator.PARALLEL` in the process tree and unconnected nodes in `StrictPartialOrder` / independent Petri-net places in POWL), meaning enabled activities neither block nor preempt one another during execution.
7. **Scope of trial rework:** When `Inspect Trial` fails, rework is confined to `Adjust Parameters -> Execute Trial -> Inspect Trial`; prior upstream activities (`Analyze Root Cause`, `Design Corrective Action`, `Plan Verification`) and governance reviews are not repeated per trial failure.

# 3. PM4Py Process-Tree Model

### 3.1 Compact Process-Tree Expression

```text
->(
  'Register Deviation',
  +(
    ->( 'Contain Material', 'Plan Verification' ),
    ->( 'Analyze Root Cause', 'Design Corrective Action' )
  ),
  +(
    *( ->( 'Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial' ), 'Adjust Parameters' ),
    X(
      'Supervisor Review',
      +( 'Quality Review', 'Regulatory Review' )
    ),
    X( 'Consult Supplier', tau )
  ),
  'Release Material',
  'Archive Case'
)
```

### 3.2 Self-Contained Python Construction

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator, ProcessTree

# Root sequence: Register Deviation -> Prep Streams -> Trial/Approvals/Supplier -> Release -> Archive
tree = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE)

t_register = ProcessTree(label="Register Deviation")

# Phase 1: Concurrent preparation streams
p_prep = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.PARALLEL)

seq_contain_plan = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE)
t_contain = ProcessTree(label="Contain Material")
t_plan = ProcessTree(label="Plan Verification")
for child in (t_contain, t_plan):
    child.parent = seq_contain_plan
    seq_contain_plan.children.append(child)

seq_analyze_design = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE)
t_analyze = ProcessTree(label="Analyze Root Cause")
t_design = ProcessTree(label="Design Corrective Action")
for child in (t_analyze, t_design):
    child.parent = seq_analyze_design
    seq_analyze_design.children.append(child)

for child in (seq_contain_plan, seq_analyze_design):
    child.parent = p_prep
    p_prep.children.append(child)

# Phase 2: Concurrent trial loop, risk-route reviews, and optional supplier consultation
p_exec_gov = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.PARALLEL)

# 2a. Repeat-until trial cycle: do (Execute Trial -> Inspect Trial), redo (Adjust Parameters)
loop_trial = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.LOOP)
seq_trial_body = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE)
t_exec_trial = ProcessTree(label="Execute Trial")
t_insp_trial = ProcessTree(label="Inspect Trial")
for child in (t_exec_trial, t_insp_trial):
    child.parent = seq_trial_body
    seq_trial_body.children.append(child)

t_adjust = ProcessTree(label="Adjust Parameters")
for child in (seq_trial_body, t_adjust):
    child.parent = loop_trial
    loop_trial.children.append(child)

# 2b. Risk route: XOR(Supervisor Review, PARALLEL(Quality Review, Regulatory Review))
xor_risk = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.XOR)
t_sup_rev = ProcessTree(label="Supervisor Review")
p_high_risk = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.PARALLEL)
t_qual_rev = ProcessTree(label="Quality Review")
t_reg_rev = ProcessTree(label="Regulatory Review")
for child in (t_qual_rev, t_reg_rev):
    child.parent = p_high_risk
    p_high_risk.children.append(child)

for child in (t_sup_rev, p_high_risk):
    child.parent = xor_risk
    xor_risk.children.append(child)

# 2c. Optional supplier consultation: XOR(Consult Supplier, tau)
xor_supplier = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.XOR)
t_consult = ProcessTree(label="Consult Supplier")
t_skip_consult = ProcessTree(operator=None, label=None)  # Silent transition (tau)
for child in (t_consult, t_skip_consult):
    child.parent = xor_supplier
    xor_supplier.children.append(child)

for child in (loop_trial, xor_risk, xor_supplier):
    child.parent = p_exec_gov
    p_exec_gov.children.append(child)

# Closing sequence
t_release = ProcessTree(label="Release Material")
t_archive = ProcessTree(label="Archive Case")

for child in (t_register, p_prep, p_exec_gov, t_release, t_archive):
    child.parent = tree
    tree.children.append(child)
```

### 3.3 Representational Limits and Approximation Analysis

A duplicate-free `ProcessTree` can represent only series-parallel (N-free) causal partial orders, whereas the intended specification induces a non-series-parallel **N-poset** on `{Plan Verification, Analyze Root Cause, Trial Loop, Risk Approval}` (and analogously with `Consult Supplier`): `Plan Verification` precedes `Trial Loop`, `Analyze Root Cause` precedes `Trial Loop` (via `Design Corrective Action`), and `Analyze Root Cause` precedes `Risk Approval` / `Consult Supplier`, while `Plan Verification` is concurrent with `Analyze Root Cause`, `Plan Verification` is concurrent with `Risk Approval` / `Consult Supplier`, and `Trial Loop` is concurrent with `Risk Approval` / `Consult Supplier`. Because `Trial Loop` synchronizes a sub-branch of containment (`Plan Verification`) with a sub-branch of root-cause analysis (`Design Corrective Action`) *without* synchronizing the sibling sub-branches of root-cause analysis (`Risk Approval` and `Consult Supplier`) until `Release Material`, no nesting of `SEQUENCE` and `PARALLEL` can capture all intended interleavings simultaneously. Under our conservative under-approximation (`tree`), **Requirements 5 and 6 (early start/overlap of risk reviews and supplier consultation)** are over-constrained: `Supervisor Review`, `Quality Review`, `Regulatory Review`, and `Consult Supplier` cannot complete immediately after `Analyze Root Cause` while `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, or `Design Corrective Action` is still incomplete. Instead, `tree` imposes artificial precedence edges `{Contain Material, Plan Verification, Design Corrective Action} -> {Supervisor Review, Quality Review, Regulatory Review, Consult Supplier}`. All other requirements—including full independence of `Contain Material -> Plan Verification` and `Analyze Root Cause -> Design Corrective Action`, full overlap of approvals and `Consult Supplier` with the multi-iteration `Trial Loop`, exact loop-exit semantics, and release synchronization—are represented without unsafe behavior.

# 4. PM4Py POWL Model

### 4.1 Compact POWL Description

```text
Nodes of root StrictPartialOrder:
  n1 = 'Register Deviation'
  n2 = 'Contain Material'
  n3 = 'Analyze Root Cause'
  n4 = 'Plan Verification'
  n5 = 'Design Corrective Action'
  n6 = X( 'Supervisor Review', PO( nodes={'Quality Review', 'Regulatory Review'}, edges={} ) )
  n7 = X( 'Consult Supplier', tau )
  n8 = *( PO( nodes={'Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial'}, edges={'Execute Trial' -> 'Inspect Trial'} ),
          'Adjust Parameters' )
  n9 = 'Release Material'
  n10 = 'Archive Case'

Direct partial-order edges in root:
  n1 -> n2,  n1 -> n3
  n2 -> n4
  n3 -> n5,  n3 -> n6,  n3 -> n7
  n4 -> n8,  n5 -> n8
  n6 -> n9,  n7 -> n9,  n8 -> n9
  n9 -> n10
```

### 4.2 Self-Contained Python Construction

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    OperatorPOWL,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    Transition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# Top-level atomic transitions
reg = Transition(label="Register Deviation")
contain = Transition(label="Contain Material")
analyze = Transition(label="Analyze Root Cause")
plan_ver = Transition(label="Plan Verification")
design_ca = Transition(label="Design Corrective Action")
release = Transition(label="Release Material")
archive = Transition(label="Archive Case")

# Risk-route submodel: XOR(Supervisor Review, StrictPartialOrder({Quality Review, Regulatory Review}))
sup_rev = Transition(label="Supervisor Review")
qual_rev = Transition(label="Quality Review")
reg_rev = Transition(label="Regulatory Review")
high_risk_po = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[qual_rev, reg_rev])
approval_route = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [sup_rev, high_risk_po])

# Optional supplier consultation: XOR(Consult Supplier, SilentTransition)
consult = Transition(label="Consult Supplier")
skip_consult = SilentTransition()
supplier_opt = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [consult, skip_consult])

# Repeat-until trial loop: LOOP(do=(Execute Trial -> Inspect Trial), redo=Adjust Parameters)
exec_trial = Transition(label="Execute Trial")
insp_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[exec_trial, insp_trial])
trial_body.order.add_edge(exec_trial, insp_trial)

adjust = Transition(label="Adjust Parameters")
trial_loop = OperatorPOWL(Operator.LOOP, [trial_body, adjust])

# Root StrictPartialOrder encoding cross-branch causal dependencies directly
powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        reg,
        contain,
        analyze,
        plan_ver,
        design_ca,
        approval_route,
        supplier_opt,
        trial_loop,
        release,
        archive,
    ]
)

# Direct causal precedence relations (Hasse diagram of the intended partial order)
powl.order.add_edge(reg, contain)
powl.order.add_edge(reg, analyze)

powl.order.add_edge(contain, plan_ver)

powl.order.add_edge(analyze, design_ca)
powl.order.add_edge(analyze, approval_route)
powl.order.add_edge(analyze, supplier_opt)

powl.order.add_edge(plan_ver, trial_loop)
powl.order.add_edge(design_ca, trial_loop)

powl.order.add_edge(approval_route, release)
powl.order.add_edge(supplier_opt, release)
powl.order.add_edge(trial_loop, release)

powl.order.add_edge(release, archive)
```

### 4.3 Justification of Intentionally Unconnected (Concurrent) Node Pairs

In `powl`, `reg` transitively precedes all other 9 root nodes and `archive` transitively succeeds `release` and all 7 interior nodes, leaving exactly **13 unordered incomparable pairs** among the 7 interior root nodes plus **1 unordered pair** inside `high_risk_po`, all of which must remain concurrent by specification:

1. **Containment/planning pipeline vs. root-cause/design pipeline — 4 pairs** `(contain, analyze)`, `(contain, design_ca)`, `(plan_ver, analyze)`, `(plan_ver, design_ca)`: Rules 2–4 state that `Contain Material -> Plan Verification` and `Analyze Root Cause -> Design Corrective Action` progress independently and synchronize only when `Execute Trial` (`trial_loop`) begins.
2. **Containment/planning pipeline vs. approvals and supplier consultation — 4 pairs** `(contain, approval_route)`, `(contain, supplier_opt)`, `(plan_ver, approval_route)`, `(plan_ver, supplier_opt)`: Rules 5 and 6 require only `Analyze Root Cause` before risk reviews or `Consult Supplier` begin; neither waits for physical containment or verification planning.
3. **Corrective-action design and trial loop vs. approvals and supplier consultation — 4 pairs** `(design_ca, approval_route)`, `(design_ca, supplier_opt)`, `(trial_loop, approval_route)`, `(trial_loop, supplier_opt)`: Rules 6 and 7 explicitly permit the chosen approval route and `Consult Supplier` to overlap corrective-action design and the entire trial/inspection/adjustment loop, synchronizing only at `Release Material`.
4. **Approvals vs. supplier consultation — 1 pair** `(approval_route, supplier_opt)`: Rule 6 states `Consult Supplier` may overlap approvals, with both independently gated after `Analyze Root Cause` and joined at `Release Material`.
5. **High-risk dual reviews — 1 pair inside `high_risk_po`** `(qual_rev, reg_rev)`: Rule 5 explicitly states that `Quality Review` and `Regulatory Review` may execute concurrently and complete in either order.

# 7. Preferred Representation and Validation Plan

### 7.1 Canonical Representation Choice and Defense

**`powl` (the PM4Py POWL model) is selected as the canonical representation.**
1. **Exact behavioral fidelity without duplication:** `powl` expresses the embedded N-poset directly via `StrictPartialOrder` edges while retaining structured `OperatorPOWL` submodels for exclusive choice (`Risk Approval`, `Optional Supplier`) and structured repeat-until iteration (`Trial Loop`). Consequently, $L(\text{powl}) = L(\text{intended})$ with all 14 visible labels appearing exactly once.
2. **Guaranteed workflow soundness by construction:** Unlike arbitrary hand-wired Petri nets—where cross-branch places easily introduce deadlocks or orphan tokens on XOR branches—every `StrictPartialOrder` of valid `POWL` nodes and every `OperatorPOWL(XOR / LOOP)` composes single-entry/single-exit sound sub-nets, guaranteeing a sound workflow net upon Petri-net conversion.
3. **Role of `tree`:** `tree` should be retained solely as an explicitly documented conservative companion when downstream tooling requires a pure `ProcessTree` input, knowing $L(\text{tree}) \subsetneq L(\text{powl})$.

### 7.2 Concrete Validation Plan

1. **Conversion to Accepting Petri Nets:**
   Convert both `powl` and `tree` using `net_p, im_p, fm_p = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl)` and `net_t, im_t, fm_t = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)`. Inspect `len(im_p) == 1` and `len(fm_p) == 1` to confirm proper single-source/single-sink workflow-net structure.
2. **Formal Soundness Verification:**
   Run `is_sound_p = pm4py.check_soundness(net_p, im_p, fm_p, print_diagnostics=True)` and `is_sound_t = pm4py.check_soundness(net_t, im_t, fm_t, print_diagnostics=True)`. Require both calls to return `True`, confirming: (a) workflow-net structure, (b) option to complete from every reachable marking, (c) proper completion (no residual tokens when `fm` is reached), and (d) absence of dead transitions.
3. **Bounded Playout and Invariant Checking:**
   Generate sampled traces via `log_p = pm4py.play_out(net_p, im_p, fm_p, parameters={"no_traces": 1000, "max_trace_length": 40})` (bounding `max_trace_length` so the `Adjust Parameters` loop terminates finitely). Programmatically assert over every generated trace $t \in \text{log\_p}$:
   - `t[0] == "Register Deviation"` and `t[-1] == "Archive Case"` and `t[-2] == "Release Material"`;
   - every precedence pair in Section 2 (`Contain Material < Plan Verification`, `Analyze Root Cause < Design Corrective Action`, `{Plan Verification, Design Corrective Action} < first Execute Trial`, etc.) holds;
   - `Consult Supplier` appears $\le 1$ time;
   - `Supervisor Review` is mutually exclusive with `{Quality Review, Regulatory Review}`, and `Quality Review in t` iff `Regulatory Review in t`;
   - every occurrence of `Adjust Parameters` is preceded by `Inspect Trial` and followed by `Execute Trial`.
   Also verify existential coverage in `log_p` of early supplier/review traces (`index("Consult Supplier") < index("Contain Material")`) to confirm `powl` genuinely admits the cross-branch concurrency.
4. **Explicit Positive and Negative Witness Replay (`W1`–`W8`):**
   Build an `EventLog` containing `W1`–`W8` and evaluate exact conformance via `pm4py.fitness_alignments(witness_log, net, im, fm)`:
   - On `(net_p, im_p, fm_p)`: assert `fitness == 1.0` (zero non-sync moves) for `W1`–`W5`, and `fitness < 1.0` for `W6`–`W8`.
   - On `(net_t, im_t, fm_t)`: assert `fitness == 1.0` for `W1, W2, W3, W5`, and `fitness < 1.0` for `W4` (confirming the documented conservative under-approximation) as well as `W6`–`W8`.
5. **Dedicated Check for Duplicated Labels and Silent-Transition ($\tau$) Anomalies:**
   - *Bijective visible-label census:* Collect `[t.label for t in net_p.transitions if t.label is not None]`. Assert `len(visible_labels) == 14` and `len(set(visible_labels)) == 14`, proving zero duplicated or missing visible labels introduced during conversion.
   - *Silent-transition ($\tau$) cycle and bypass audit:* Build the $\tau$-only subgraph of `net_p` (retaining only transitions with `t.label is None` and their connected places) and check that it is acyclic (ruling out livelocks / infinite $\tau$-loops), and test a synthetic trace omitting `Supervisor Review`, `Quality Review`, and `Regulatory Review` to verify that `skip_consult` ($\tau$) only skips `Consult Supplier` and cannot silently bypass `approval_route` or `trial_loop`.
6. **Limitations of the Validation Plan:**
   - *Unbounded loop state space:* Bounded playout (`max_trace_length=40`) and finite witness sets (`W1`–`W8`) cannot exhaustively enumerate arbitrarily many `Adjust Parameters` iterations; structural soundness (`check_soundness`) proves termination *reachability* rather than statistical loop convergence.
   - *Data-predicate blindness:* Control-flow replay cannot detect data-level violations—for example, a truly *high-risk* batch routed through `Supervisor Review`, a mandatory vendor issue skipping `Consult Supplier`, or `Release Material` executed after a *failed* inspection because `Adjust Parameters` was omitted in reality.
   - *Completion-only timestamp granularity:* Validating completion-ordered traces cannot verify whether `Plan Verification` illegally *started* before `Contain Material` completed, only that it *completed* later.

# 8. Final Self-Critique

1. **Completion-time atomicity masks start-before-complete violations (Semantic limitation):** Following Rule 11, both `powl` and `tree` treat activities as instantaneous completion events. Consequently, "B may start only after A is complete" (e.g., `Contain Material -> Plan Verification` or `{Plan Verification, Design Corrective Action} -> Execute Trial`) is relaxed in trace semantics to "B completes after A completes." In reality, if `Plan Verification` started *before* `Contain Material` finished and merely completed afterward, a completion-only model would falsely accept the trace; enforcing true start-after-complete barriers requires dual `start` + `complete` lifecycle transitions.
2. **Memoryless control-flow abstraction severs data-driven correlations and retry bounds (Semantic limitation):** Modeling risk routing (`XOR`), supplier consultation (`XOR` with $\tau`), and trial pass/fail (`LOOP`) as independent, memoryless non-deterministic choices ignores domain correlations—such as high-risk deviations requiring supplier consultation when a vendor batch is implicated, or organizational policies capping `Adjust Parameters` at $k$ failed trials before mandatory material scrap rather than permitting unbounded retries or only `Release Material` as the sole exit.
3. **Incomparable conservative ProcessTree approximations (Modeling trade-off):** Our `tree` places the synchronization barrier after `PARALLEL(->(Contain, Plan), ->(Analyze, Design))`, preserving full concurrency between the two preparation pipelines at the cost of delaying `Risk Approval` and `Consult Supplier` behind both `Plan Verification` and `Design Corrective Action`. An alternative conservative tree—synchronizing `PARALLEL(->(Contain, Plan), Analyze)` first and then running `PARALLEL(->(Design, TrialLoop), RiskApproval, ConsultOpt)`—would allow approvals and supplier consultation to overlap `Design Corrective Action`, but would falsely block `Design Corrective Action` behind `Contain Material -> Plan Verification`. Because the two under-approximations admit incomparable subsets of $L(\text{powl})$, neither tree is universally superior without empirical trace-frequency weights from historical logs.
