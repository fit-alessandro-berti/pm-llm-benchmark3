## 1. Executive causal assessment

**Provisional verdict: mainly confounded. Confidence: 75/100** that case mix substantially explains Orion's unfavorable aggregate association. This confidence concerns that explanation; the causal effect of the proposed reassignment remains unidentified.

The strongest supporting evidence is a Simpson's-paradox pattern: Orion averages 21.96 hours versus Nova's 15.24, yet Orion is faster within low, medium, and high complexity by approximately 2.14, 2.21, and 3.43 hours. Orion handles substantially more high-complexity cases and night work. Standardizing both groups to the pooled complexity distribution changes the Orion-minus-Nova contrast from +6.71 to −2.51 hours. This is descriptive standardization, not causal identification.

The strongest evidence against a purely confounded explanation is Orion's consistently shorter waiting and review-service times within complexity strata: an actual benefit from skill, prioritization, or coordination is plausible. Conversely, coarse complexity, unrecorded risk, routing priorities, and completion selection could conceal a harmful effect. Orion has only one day case, Nova only two high-complexity cases, and several joint comparison cells are empty. Shift, load, and group therefore cannot be reliably separated here.

Moving half of Orion's work could succeed if it relieves a real congestion constraint and Nova has appropriately skilled spare capacity at the necessary hours. It could fail if case difficulty determines duration, or backfire by overloading Nova, delaying night cases until daytime, increasing information requests, or reducing review quality. A transfer this large also changes other cases' queues. I recommend a small, capacity-limited routing experiment alongside a separate queue/compliance-capacity intervention, with required checks preserved and all arrivals followed.

Routing after classification, preferential difficult-case assignment, requirement-to-performance, and review/rework contributions to completion time are supported. Other arrows are hypotheses, including routing priority affecting queues and duration affecting inclusion at extraction. Here `routing_rule` denotes the applied routing decision; `unobserved_reviewer_skill` denotes the skill of the reviewer received, so assignment selects skill exposure. Shift and arrival load are assumed pre-assignment; if shift records actual service, its direction could reverse. No group-to-compliance-requirement arrow is assumed. A static DAG suppresses earlier review causing information requests, later review increasing workload, and evolving queues influencing subsequent routing. Those relations require time-indexed events and cross-case dependencies.

## 3. Variable-role table

Define the **total assignment effect** as E[T(Orion) − T(Nova)] for future cases eligible for either group, assigned just after `Classify`, under specified staffing and coverage. It includes changes in reviewer exposure, waiting, repeated review, and other downstream work. The pre-assignment portion of total cycle time cannot be caused by that assignment. A post-classification outcome would isolate the relevant time more precisely.

A **direct effect** must specify mechanisms held fixed: for example, a controlled direct effect at common queue wait, total review service, and rework. The graph's direct group-to-cycle arrow allows other handling or closing delays. Such controlled combinations may be infeasible. **Mediated effects** include paths through queueing, reviewer skill, service, and rework; rework also changes total review service. Regressing cycle time on these downstream variables does not identify those effects. Natural direct/indirect effects need additional mediator-confounding and cross-world assumptions unsupported here.

The **50% routing-policy effect** additionally includes congestion spillovers, capacity changes, and which cases are moved. A small individual assignment effect at current load does not identify that policy effect.

With 30 observations, exact support tables are more informative than a flexible propensity model: deterministic-looking cells invite separation and extreme weights. Joint cells defined by complexity, shift, load, and requirement contain both groups for only 20/30 cases; every such cell has fewer than three cases in at least one group. Restrict an observational estimand to genuine shared support and report the excluded population. None of the mechanisms satisfies an established front-door identification argument: risk can confound mediator–outcome relations, and group has multiple possible pathways to completion. Sequentially randomized mechanism interventions would provide stronger evidence. Survival or censoring-weighted analyses also require explicit assumptions; abandoned cases should not simply be treated as independently censored eventual completions.

## 6. Intervention and learning plan

The numerical thresholds below are proposed pilot safeguards to agree before launch, not estimates derived from these 30 cases.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**Final recommendation.** Do not approve a blanket 50% reassignment on the current evidence. The crude ranking is unsuitable for that decision, and complexity standardization reverses it without resolving causal uncertainty. Keep mandatory compliance intact and establish an all-arrival baseline with comparable clocks, routing reasons, reviewer eligibility, and queue states.

Then run a small randomized transfer pilot for cases both groups can safely handle during shared coverage. Evaluate assignment as implemented, using intention-to-treat comparisons and explicit monitoring of the queues of other cases. Separately test added capacity at the step where event data show backlog accumulating. Distinguishing compliance-related waiting, ready-for-review waiting, information-response delays, and active review will make the result operationally useful.

Expand only when the pilot demonstrates a practically worthwhile system benefit with acceptable quality and sufficiently complete follow-up. Two recorded appeals cannot establish quality equivalence. If the benefit comes from staffing or compliance access, deploy that mechanism where needed. If Nova cannot absorb eligible work without growing its queue, retain the allocation and address the constrained capacity directly.

            print(f"WARNING [{label}]: fragile comparison at {cell!r}")


crude = df.groupby("resource_group").agg(
    n=("case_id", "size"),
    mean_cycle_h=("cycle_time_h", "mean"),
    median_cycle_h=("cycle_time_h", "median"),
).reindex(groups)
print("\nCrude completed-case comparison")
print(crude.round(4).to_string())
print("Orion - Nova (h):",
      round(crude.loc["Orion", "mean_cycle_h"]
            - crude.loc["Nova", "mean_cycle_h"], 4))
warn_counts(pd.DataFrame([crude["n"].to_dict()], index=["overall"]),
            "overall")

strat = df.groupby(["complexity", "resource_group"]).agg(
    n=("case_id", "size"),
    mean_cycle_h=("cycle_time_h", "mean"),
    median_cycle_h=("cycle_time_h", "median"),
    mean_wait_h=("wait_before_review_h", "mean"),
    mean_service_h=("review_service_h", "mean"),
)
strat = strat.reindex(pd.MultiIndex.from_product(
    [levels, groups], names=["complexity", "resource_group"]))
print("\nComplexity-stratified completed-case comparison")
print(strat.round(4).to_string())
means = strat["mean_cycle_h"].unstack("resource_group").reindex(columns=groups)
counts = strat["n"].unstack("resource_group").reindex(columns=groups)
warn_counts(counts.fillna(0), "complexity")
print("\nWithin-complexity Orion - Nova (h)")
print((means["Orion"] - means["Nova"]).round(4).to_string())

# Fixed common target: empirical pooled distribution among the 30 completers.
weights = df["complexity"].value_counts(normalize=True).reindex(levels)
print("\nCommon pooled-completer complexity weights")
print(weights.to_string())
standardized = means.mul(weights, axis="index").sum(axis=0, min_count=len(levels))
print("\nComplexity-standardized means (h)")
print(standardized.round(4).to_string())
print("Standardized Orion - Nova (h):",
      round(standardized["Orion"] - standardized["Nova"], 4))
print("WARNING: Standardization adjusts only recorded complexity; sparse cells, "
      "unmeasured routing factors, completion selection, and interference remain.")

# Include explicit zeros: all complexity x shift combinations, both groups.
grid = pd.MultiIndex.from_product(
    [levels, ["Day", "Night"]], names=["complexity", "shift"])
overlap = (df.groupby(["complexity", "shift", "resource_group"])
           .size().unstack("resource_group", fill_value=0)
           .reindex(index=grid, columns=groups, fill_value=0).astype(int))
print("\nOverlap counts: complexity x shift x resource_group")
print(overlap.to_string())
warn_counts(overlap, "complexity x shift")

# Further joint support on candidate pre-assignment variables.
keys = ["complexity", "shift", "arrival_load", "compliance_required"]
joint = (df.groupby(keys + ["resource_group"]).size()
         .unstack("resource_group", fill_value=0)
         .reindex(columns=groups, fill_value=0).astype(int))
print("\nJoint support including load and baseline compliance requirement")
print(joint.to_string())
warn_counts(joint, "joint baseline cells")
shared = (joint[groups] > 0).all(axis=1)
print("Cases in cells with both groups:", int(joint.loc[shared].sum().sum()),
      "of", len(df))
print("WARNING: Shared observed cells do not establish exchangeability or "
      "future eligibility; actual service shift may be post-assignment.")
if not ((df["shift"] == "Night") & (df["arrival_load"] == "Normal")).any():
    print("WARNING: No night/normal-load cases; shift and load lack full support.")

# Arithmetic component comparison; the residual has no assigned activity label.
df["residual_h"] = (df["cycle_time_h"] - df["wait_before_review_h"]
                    - df["review_service_h"])
print("\nMean duration components by complexity and group")
print(df.groupby(["complexity", "resource_group"])[
    ["cycle_time_h", "wait_before_review_h", "review_service_h", "residual_h"]

# PM4Py structural operations only. Never discover a performance DFG here.
logs, variants, dfgs = {}, {}, {}
for group in groups:
    cases = df.loc[df["resource_group"] == group]
    trace_strings = cases["trace"].str.replace(">", ",", regex=False).tolist()
    parsed = pm4py.parse_event_log_string(trace_strings)
    # Normalize to case-oriented EventLog; some versions parse to a DataFrame.
    logs[group] = pm4py.convert_to_event_log(parsed)
    assert len(logs[group]) == len(cases)
    raw_variants = pm4py.get_variants_as_tuples(logs[group])
    # PM4Py versions may return frequencies or collections of traces.
    variants[group] = {
        tuple(variant): int(value) if isinstance(value, Integral) else len(value)
        for variant, value in raw_variants.items()
    }
    dfgs[group], starts, ends = pm4py.discover_dfg(logs[group])
    assert sum(variants[group].values()) == len(cases)
    print(f"\n{group} structural start/end counts:", dict(starts), dict(ends))

variant_rows = []
for variant in sorted(set(variants["Orion"]) | set(variants["Nova"])):
    row = {"variant": ">".join(variant)}
    for group in groups:
        n = variants[group].get(variant, 0)
        row[group] = n
        row[f"{group}_share"] = n / len(logs[group])
    variant_rows.append(row)
variant_table = pd.DataFrame(variant_rows).set_index("variant")
print("\nTrace variants: case counts and within-group shares")
print(variant_table.round(4).to_string())
warn_counts(variant_table[groups], "variant")

edge_rows = []
for edge in sorted(set(dfgs["Orion"]) | set(dfgs["Nova"])):
    row = {"edge": " -> ".join(edge)}
    for group in groups:
        count = int(dfgs[group].get(edge, 0))
        # Count supporting cases separately from repeated edge occurrences.
        case_count = sum(
            1 for trace in logs[group]
            if any((a["concept:name"], b["concept:name"]) == edge
                   for a, b in zip(trace, trace[1:]))
        )
        row[f"{group}_occurrences"] = count
        row[f"{group}_occurrences_per_case"] = count / len(logs[group])
        row[f"{group}_supporting_cases"] = case_count
    edge_rows.append(row)
edge_table = pd.DataFrame(edge_rows).set_index("edge")
print("\nDirectly-follows edges: occurrence counts, rates, and case support")
print(edge_table.round(4).to_string())
edge_support = edge_table[[f"{g}_supporting_cases" for g in groups]].copy()
edge_support.columns = groups
warn_counts(edge_support, "DFG case support")

# Case-level outcomes: sparse events warrant an explicit warning too.
for outcome_column in ["rework_count", "outcome_30d"]:
    outcome_counts = pd.crosstab(df[outcome_column], df["resource_group"])
    outcome_counts = outcome_counts.reindex(columns=groups, fill_value=0)
    print(f"\n{outcome_column} counts")
    print(outcome_counts.to_string())
    warn_counts(outcome_counts, outcome_column)

print("\nWARNING: These are completed-case descriptive diagnostics, not causal "
      "estimates. Trace frequencies do not measure activity durations; no "
      "event timestamps are fabricated for performance analysis.")

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Residual routing confounding and the sign of the assignment effect.** Most valuable design change: randomized routing among cases certified as eligible for both groups, blocked on baseline risk and coverage.
2. **Which step actually constrains throughput.** Most valuable additional field: an event lifecycle timestamp keyed to each activity instance and transition, including queue entry, service start, and completion, to separate waiting from execution.
3. **Capacity spillovers from transferring a large share of work.** Most valuable design change: a randomized routing-saturation experiment across sufficiently long queue/team blocks, with outcomes measured for every affected case.
4. **Bias from observing only completed cases.** Most valuable design change: prospective follow-up of an inception cohort containing every arrival through completion, abandonment, or a defined extraction horizon.
5. **Whether speed changes preserve substantive quality.** Most valuable additional field: a blinded, standardized review-quality assessment at a fixed follow-up horizon for every eligible case, with missing follow-up explicitly recorded.
