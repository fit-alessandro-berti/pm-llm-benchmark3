## 1. Stipulated Facts, Disputed Propositions, and Burden of Proof

### Stipulated Facts (Established by the Record)
1. **Baseline Authority:** Compliance Standard CS-9 (effective 2025-01-01 onward) is the only formally controlled and approved enterprise standard. It mandates `Sanctions_Check` and `Quality_Review` prior to activation, `Manual_Procurement_Approval` prior to `Activate_Supplier`, and human segregation of duties (`requester != manual_approver`).
2. **Model Gaps in M0:** M0 encodes CS-9’s basic control flow (`Register_Supplier -> {Sanctions_Check || Quality_Review} -> XOR(Manual_Procurement_Approval -> Activate_Supplier, Reject_Supplier) -> Notify_Requester -> Close_Case`) but omits CS-9’s segregation-of-duties rule, has no `Request_More_Info` loop, has no reactivation path, and leaves rejected pre-screen scope ambiguous.
3. **Temporary Overlays and Expiry:**
   - **PD-3** (2026-06-01 through 2026-07-31) appeared in steering-committee minutes/email, was never codified into CS-9 or M0, and expired on 2026-07-31.
   - **FD-7** (2026-07-10 00:00 through 2026-07-12 23:59) authorized provisional activation for critical flood-relief suppliers subject to pre-activation `Sanctions_Check`, a EUR 20,000 spend cap, daily transaction monitoring, and `Quality_Review` within two business days. It expired automatically with no written extension.
4. **Aggregate Patterns:**
   - Of 312 June–July `Auto_Approve` cases, 289 appear PD-3-eligible, 14 are clearly ineligible, and 9 lack eligibility attributes.
   - 6 post-pilot `Auto_Approve` cases occurred in early August (e.g., SUP-714).
   - 47 cases used `Request_More_Info`; none activated before requested evidence arrived.
   - Post-2026-07-01 CRM migration increased missing onboarding-log notifications from 2% to 29%.
5. **Case-Level Facts:** SUP-701 met all rules; SUP-703 (high-risk, EUR 180k) and SUP-708 (high-risk, activated 40 minutes before `Sanctions_Check`) bypassed mandatory controls without eligibility; SUP-711 has duplicate `Register_Supplier` rows with the same source transaction ID; SUP-712 shares a human actor across aliases; SUP-713 used equivalent `QA_Clearance` prior to formal mapping; SUP-715 involved undisclosed spousal reviewer/approver roles in the same reporting line.

### Disputed Propositions
1. **Legal Sufficiency of PD-3:** Whether steering-committee minutes plus the Compliance observer’s note (“pilot may proceed as a monitored exception”) validly authorized bypassing CS-9 for eligible cases, given her later statement that she had not approved weakening CS-9.
2. **Factual Necessity After Sunset:** Whether ongoing flood recovery on 2026-07-13 (SUP-705) or anticipated permanent approval on 2026-08-02 (SUP-714) mitigates or excuses execution past explicit expiration timestamps.
3. **Scope of M0 for Rejected Leads and Reactivations:** Whether pre-screen rejections (SUP-709) and 6-month reactivations (SUP-710) fall outside M0’s governance boundary, and whether a 6-month-old quality certification satisfies CS-9.

5. **Unwritten Conflict-of-Interest Prohibitions:** Whether SUP-715 represents a substantive approval defect when CS-9 contains no explicit anti-nepotism bar, beyond the clear failure to disclose.

### Burden-of-Proof Standard
- **To Declare Operational Noncompliance:** The reviewer bears the burden of establishing, via corroborated event and attribute evidence, either (a) breach of an unambiguous, active mandatory control (e.g., pre-activation sanctions, segregation of duties, spend cap, unauthorized post-expiry bypass) or (b) absence of required authority at execution time. - **To Accept an Exception or Defense:** Operations bears the burden of producing contemporaneous, immutable evidence of (a) valid authority active at the event timestamp, (b) satisfaction of every substantive condition (eligibility, cap, SLA, monitoring), and (c) verified alternative logging where primary logs are incomplete. Unwritten extensions, expected future policies, and mutable retroactive links fail this burden.

## 2. Prosecution Brief

From a compliance-assurance standpoint, the operation exhibits **material noncompliance and control erosion**, requiring strict enforcement of CS-9 and formal change control rather than post-hoc rationalization of M0 deviations.

1. **Formal Authority and Policy Primacy:** CS-9 is the sole controlled, formally approved standard. PD-3 was never incorporated into CS-9 or M0, and the Compliance observer explicitly denied approving a weakening of CS-9. Steering-committee minutes cannot informally supersede an enterprise compliance standard. Even treating PD-3 and FD-7 as valid temporary variances, both contained explicit expiration dates (2026-07-31 and 2026-07-12 23:59).
2. **Normalization of Deviance and Expiry Violations:** Operations repeatedly treated temporary exceptions as permanent entitlements. **SUP-705** bypassed pre-activation `Quality_Review` and `Manual_Procurement_Approval` on 2026-07-13 after FD-7 expired automatically without written extension. **SUP-714** (along with 5 other early-August cases) continued `Auto_Approve` after PD-3 expired merely because staff assumed permanent approval was imminent. In governance terms, an unrenewed sunset clause terminates authority immediately.
3. **Failure of Pilot Controls and High-Risk Breaches:** Even during PD-3’s nominal window, preventive controls failed: 14 of 312 `Auto_Approve` cases were clearly ineligible and 9 lacked eligibility attributes. **SUP-703** demonstrates the resulting exposure: a HIGH-risk new foreign supplier was misrouted into `Auto_Approve` and incurred EUR 180,000 in initial spend without manual procurement approval or prior quality review. **SUP-708** is equally severe: a HIGH-risk supplier was activated 40 minutes *before* `Sanctions_Check` and without `Manual_Procurement_Approval` under no pilot or emergency authority. That no transactions occurred during those 40 minutes is fortuitous luck, not a control.
4. **Violations Concealed by M0:** Relying solely on M0 creates dangerous false negatives. **SUP-712** satisfies M0’s trace perfectly yet violates CS-9’s core segregation-of-duties rule through two actor aliases resolving to the same human. **SUP-715** likewise conforms to M0’s control flow while breaching conflict-of-interest disclosure policy between spouses in the same reporting line.
5. **Desirable Outcomes Do Not Amend Policy:** Benign outcomes in SUP-705, SUP-708, or SUP-714 do not retroactively confer authority. Compliance audits ex-ante control integrity; allowing operators to skip approvals whenever they judge risk to be low or urgent destroys preventive assurance.

**Prosecution Acknowledgment of Genuine Model and Data Defects:** The prosecution concedes that raw M0 nonconformance rates inflate apparent misconduct. **SUP-711** is a migration ETL duplicate sharing one source transaction ID, not a double registration. **SUP-702**’s notification occurred within two hours in CRM with a stable `notification_id`, reflecting the 29% CRM mirroring defect rather than operational omission. **SUP-707**’s `Request_More_Info` loop is a genuine expressiveness gap in M0 that improved pre-approval due diligence. **SUP-713** reflects a one-week governance lag in mapping `QA_Clearance` to `Quality_Review`, and **SUP-709**/**SUP-710** expose genuine scope ambiguities in CS-9 and M0.

## 3. Defense Brief

From a process-science and operational perspective, **M0 is obsolete, incomplete, incorrectly scoped, and evaluated against degraded event data**, causing routine, risk-mitigating, and authorized work to register falsely as noncompliance.

1. **Structural Blindness of M0:** M0 is an overly rigid partial order incapable of representing real-world onboarding. Across 47 cases (e.g., **SUP-707**), staff used `Request_More_Info` loops to obtain missing certificates before approval, and zero cases activated prematurely. Compliance agrees this behavior is desirable, yet M0 marks every one of those 47 traces nonconforming. Conversely, M0 passes **SUP-712** and **SUP-715** as 100% conformant despite severe identity and disclosure breaches, proving M0 is neither necessary nor sufficient for actual compliance.
2. **Authorized Overlays Excluded from M0:** Both PD-3 and FD-7 were documented organizational decisions with explicit eligibility, spend, monitoring, and SLA guardrails. **SUP-702** met every PD-3 criterion (LOW-risk DE, approved group, within window, `Quality_Review` next business day, CRM notification at +2 hours). **SUP-704** met every FD-7 criterion (critical relief supplier, EUR 18,000 spend < EUR 20,000 cap, daily monitoring, next-day `Quality_Review` and `Manual_Procurement_Approval`). Flagging these cases as deviations measures M0’s staleness, not operational failure.
3. **Data-Collection Defects and Label Drift:** Following the 2026-07-01 CRM migration, notification missingness jumped from 2% to 29% solely because the onboarding log fails to mirror CRM events reliably. Treating a broken integration pipeline as employee noncompliance in **SUP-702** (and conflating **SUP-711**’s identical-ID ETL duplicate with process failure) misattributes IT defects to operations. Similarly, **SUP-713** (`QA_Clearance`) reflects an approved UI rename whose semantic equivalence is confirmed by audit documents; a seven-day mapping lag is a metadata-management issue, not an unreviewed supplier.
4. **Scope Ambiguity for Rejected Leads and Reactivations:** CS-9 explicitly permits an abbreviated pre-screen for rejected leads, yet M0 enforces `Quality_Review` on all cases. **SUP-709** is an intake rejection following failed `Sanctions_Check`, not an activation bypass. **SUP-710** is a reactivation of a supplier closed six months prior with valid quality certification; CS-9 and M0 simply omit lifecycle reactivation rules.
5. **Trace Deviation vs. Business Misconduct:** Even where formal deadlines lapsed, **SUP-705** (active flood recovery, EUR 12,000 spend, next-day quality and approval) and **SUP-714** (eligible LOW-risk supplier two days post-pilot) reflect administrative renewal lag rather than reckless risk appetite.

**Defense Acknowledgment of Genuine Risk and Limits on Informal Practice:** The defense concedes that model defects cannot excuse genuine control failures. **SUP-703** (EUR 180k high-risk misrouting into `Auto_Approve`) and **SUP-708** (pre-sanctions activation of a high-risk supplier) are indefensible under any model or policy. The defense further acknowledges that alias-masked self-approval (**SUP-712**), undisclosed familial conflicts (**SUP-715**), unverified mutable links (**SUP-706**), unmonitored pilot leakage (14 ineligible and 9 missing-attribute cases), and continuing temporary overlays past explicit sunset dates without written extensions (**SUP-705**, **SUP-714**) exceed the legitimate bounds of informal operational discretion.

## 5. Final Judgment

### 1 & 2. Case-by-Case Classifications, Severity, and Confidence

1. **SUP-701 — `COMPLIANT`** (Severity: None | Confidence: High). Satisfies both M0 and all CS-9 substantive/resource rules.
2. **SUP-702 — `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`** (Severity: Low | Confidence: Medium-High). *Dominant reason:* Executed strictly within PD-3’s active window with complete eligibility attributes, next-day `Quality_Review`, and verifiable +2h CRM notification (`notification_id`). While PD-3 was not codified into CS-9, steering-committee minutes plus Compliance’s contemporaneous “may proceed as a monitored exception” constitutes sufficient operational authority for the pilot window.
3. **SUP-703 — `NONCOMPLIANT`** (Severity: Critical | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Ineligible high-risk new foreign supplier bypassed `Manual_Procurement_Approval` and pre-activation `Quality_Review`, resulting in EUR 180,000 initial spend. Violates CS-9, M0, and PD-3.
4. **SUP-704 — `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`** (Severity: Low | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Validly invoked FD-7 within its active window and satisfied every guardrail (pre-activation `Sanctions_Check`, EUR 18k < EUR 20k cap, daily monitoring, next-day quality and approval).
5. **SUP-705 — `NONCOMPLIANT`** (Severity: Medium | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Executed after FD-7’s explicit automatic expiration (2026-07-12 23:59) without written extension. Ongoing flood conditions and EUR 12k spend mitigate severity, not the absence of authority.
6. **SUP-706 — `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE`** (Severity: Low-Medium uncertainty | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Core pre-activation onboarding controls complied, but timely notification cannot be proved or disproved because the CRM link is a mutable text field entered two days later.
7. **SUP-707 — `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT`** (Severity: None | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* All CS-9 requirements were met before activation; nonconformance exists solely because M0 cannot express the desirable `Request_More_Info` loop.
8. **SUP-708 — `NONCOMPLIANT`** (Severity: High | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* High-risk supplier activated 40 minutes before `Sanctions_Check` and without `Manual_Procurement_Approval` with no pilot or emergency justification.
9. **SUP-709 — `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT`** (Severity: None | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* CS-9 permits abbreviated pre-screening for rejected leads, whereas M0 enforces `Quality_Review` unconditionally due to unresolved scope boundaries.
10. **SUP-710 — `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT`** (Severity: Medium | Confidence: Medium). *Dominant reason:* Neither CS-9 nor M0 defines a reactivation lifecycle or rules for reusing prior quality certifications, leaving a genuine normative gap (though substantive adequacy of the 6-month certificate remains unproved).
11. **SUP-711 — `COMPLIANT`** (Severity: None | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Identical source transaction IDs prove only one registration occurred; the duplicate row is purely a migration ETL artifact.
12. **SUP-712 — `NONCOMPLIANT`** (Severity: High | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Direct violation of CS-9 human segregation of duties masked by actor aliases and invisible to M0.
13. **SUP-713 — `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT`** (Severity: Low | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Audit documentation proves `QA_Clearance` had equivalent content to `Quality_Review`; deviation reflects UI label drift outpacing model ontology mapping by one week.
14. **SUP-714 — `NONCOMPLIANT`** (Severity: Low-Medium | Confidence: High). *Dominant reason:* Unauthorized continuation of `Auto_Approve` after PD-3’s explicit 2026-07-31 expiration without permanent policy amendment; prior pilot eligibility mitigates risk but does not supply authority.
15. **SUP-715 — `NONCOMPLIANT`** (Severity: Medium | Confidence: Medium-High). *Dominant reason:* Violated mandatory conflict-of-interest disclosure rules between spouses in the same reporting line. (Note: substantive prohibition of spousal reviewer/approver pairs is unproved in CS-9 itself, making non-disclosure the established breach.)

### 3. System-Level Judgment
- **Operational Compliance:** **Materially deficient in preventive controls and exception governance, though not systemically broken in core onboarding.** While many apparent deviations reflect model/ETL defects, the operation suffered two severe high-risk bypasses (**SUP-703**, **SUP-708**), 14 clearly ineligible and 9 unverified pilot cases, alias-masked self-approval (**SUP-712**), undisclosed familial conflict (**SUP-715**), and unauthorized post-expiry continuation (**SUP-705**, **SUP-714**).
- **Model Validity:** **M0 is invalid as a standalone conformance oracle.** It generates high false-positive noise on benign loops, authorized overlays, scope omissions, and CRM/ETL artifacts while producing dangerous false negatives on SoD, COI, spend, risk-tier, and expiry rules.

### 4. Where Each Side’s Argument Is Rejected
- **Rejected from the Prosecution:** I reject treating M0 deviations or formal CS-9 lag as operational noncompliance where documented pilot/emergency authority existed (**SUP-702**, **SUP-704**), where controls were value-adding (**SUP-707**), where CS-9 itself allowed pre-screen rejection (**SUP-709**), or where IT/label artifacts explain the trace (**SUP-711**, **SUP-713**). I also reject inferring missing notifications from log mirroring gaps when immutable CRM `notification_id` evidence exists (**SUP-702**).
- **Rejected from the Defense:** I reject the claim that “factual necessity” (**SUP-705**) or “imminent permanent approval” (**SUP-714**) overrides explicit written expiration timestamps without formal renewal. I reject treating M0’s obsolescence as an excuse for missing hard eligibility gates (**SUP-703**, 14 ineligible pilot cases), pre-sanctions activation (**SUP-708**), alias self-approval (**SUP-712**), or undisclosed COI (**SUP-715**).

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

A single control-flow diagram cannot enforce multi-perspective compliance. Compliance must be governed through a **five-layer architecture**:

| Layer | Governed Requirements | Implementation Technology | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Control-Flow Layer** | Concurrent `Sanctions_Check` & `Quality_Review` (standard); `Manual_Procurement_Approval`, `Auto_Approve`, and `Emergency_Provisional` branches; repeatable `Request_More_Info` loop; abbreviated rejection path; `Notify_Requester` before `Close_Case`. | **POWL (`M1`)** | POWL cleanly expresses partial orders, exclusive routing, and structured loops, while preventing false positives on SUP-702, SUP-704, SUP-707, SUP-709, and SUP-713. |
| **2. Temporal & Ordering Constraints** | Global `Precedence(Sanctions_Check, Activate_Supplier)` across *all* routes; `Response(Activate_Supplier, Quality_Review)` within 2 business days (`<= 48 business hours`) for pilot/emergency; zero spend transactions before `Sanctions_Check`; evidence receipt before approval after `Request_More_Info`. | **DECLARE / Log-Skeleton / LTL Checks** | Declarative temporal constraints enforce cross-branch invariants and metric time SLAs that POWL alone cannot express. |
| **3. Data-Aware Eligibility, Amount & Time Rules** | Active overlay window check (`effective_start <= ts <= effective_end`); PD-3 gate (`risk == LOW`, `country == DE`, `approved_group == True`); FD-7 gate (`flood_critical == True`, `spend <= EUR 20,000`, `daily_monitoring == True`). | **Pandas / SQL / DMN Rule Engine** | Prevents SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-714, and missing-attribute leakage via deterministic attribute predicates evaluated against frozen snapshots. |
| **4. Resource, Identity & COI Layer** | Canonical identity resolution (`alias -> canonical_person_id`) enforcing `requester != manual_approver`; mandatory COI disclosure lookup across reporting-line/familial pairs before approval. | **IAM Graph + Pandas Checks + HR/COI Register** | Catches SUP-712 (alias masking) and SUP-715 (undisclosed COI) regardless of trace conformance. |
| **5. Overlay Governance & Lifecycle Scope** | Hard system expiration at `effective_end` requiring signed extension; formal Compliance sign-off for pilot-to-permanent promotion; explicit sub-processes for **Rejected Leads** (`Intake_PreScreen`), **Reactivations** (`cert_age <= 180d` + risk-delta check + `Initiate_Reactivation`), and **Withdrawals** (`Withdraw_Request -> Close_Case`). | **Organizational Controls + Process/UI Gates** | Eliminates informal normalization of deviance and resolves CS-9/M0 scope ambiguities (SUP-709, SUP-710). |

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

### Limitations of Encoding in a Single POWL
POWL captures control-flow structure only. The model `M1` below **cannot** enforce:
1. **Route Guards:** Restricting `pilot_branch` to `LOW`-risk `DE` group suppliers within an active window, or `emerg_branch` to declared disasters within EUR 20,000 (so SUP-703 and SUP-714 remain control-flow valid in `M1` and must be blocked by the data/overlay layer).
2. **Metric Time & Cadence:** The 2-business-day SLA for post-activation `Quality_Review` and continuous `Daily_Transaction_Monitoring` (omitted from `emerg_branch` as an event so onboarding traces like SUP-704 replay cleanly without unlogged background jobs; enforced instead via `monitoring_active` data checks).
3. **Loop-Evidence Coupling:** `Request_More_Info` is approximated as a `0..*` loop (`Operator.LOOP(tau, Request_More_Info)`), which cannot guarantee that requested certificates actually arrived prior to approval.
4. **Lifecycle Scope:** Reactivations (SUP-710) and withdrawals are kept outside `M1` rather than making `Register_Supplier` optional, because weakening registration in `M1` would mask genuine unlogged onboarding starts; reactivations should use a dedicated `Reactivate_Supplier` workflow or explicit start event.
5. **Resource & COI Rules:** Actor alias resolution and spousal COI checks lie outside POWL.

### Executable Revised POWL (`M1`)

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def make_info_loop():
    """Approximates zero or more Request_More_Info cycles prior to decision."""
    return OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[SilentTransition(), Transition(label="Request_More_Info")],
    )


register = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")

# 1. Standard Manual-Approval Route (CS-9 baseline + Request_More_Info loop)
sanctions_std = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
quality_std = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
info_std = make_info_loop()
checks_std = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions_std, quality_std, info_std])

manual_std = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
activate_std = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
notify_std = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")

std_branch = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[checks_std, manual_std, activate_std, notify_std]
)
std_branch.order.add_edge(checks_std, manual_std)
std_branch.order.add_edge(manual_std, activate_std)
std_branch.order.add_edge(activate_std, notify_std)

# 2. Low-Risk Auto-Approval Pilot Route (PD-3 overlay)
sanctions_pilot = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
master_data_pilot = Transition(label="Automated_Master_Data_Validation")
info_pilot = make_info_loop()
pre_pilot = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions_pilot, master_data_pilot, info_pilot])

auto_approve = Transition(label="Auto_Approve")
activate_pilot = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
quality_pilot = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
notify_pilot = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")

pilot_branch = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[pre_pilot, auto_approve, activate_pilot, quality_pilot, notify_pilot]
)
pilot_branch.order.add_edge(pre_pilot, auto_approve)
pilot_branch.order.add_edge(auto_approve, activate_pilot)
pilot_branch.order.add_edge(activate_pilot, quality_pilot)
pilot_branch.order.add_edge(activate_pilot, notify_pilot)

# 3. Emergency / Provisional Route (FD-7 overlay)
sanctions_emerg = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
activate_emerg = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
quality_emerg = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
manual_emerg = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
notify_emerg = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")

emerg_branch = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[sanctions_emerg, activate_emerg, quality_emerg, manual_emerg, notify_emerg]
)
emerg_branch.order.add_edge(sanctions_emerg, activate_emerg)
emerg_branch.order.add_edge(activate_emerg, quality_emerg)
emerg_branch.order.add_edge(activate_emerg, manual_emerg)
emerg_branch.order.add_edge(activate_emerg, notify_emerg)

# 4. Rejection Route (supports full checks or CS-9 abbreviated pre-screen)
sanctions_rej = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
opt_quality_rej = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[Transition(label="Quality_Review"), SilentTransition()],
)
info_rej = make_info_loop()
pre_rej = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions_rej, opt_quality_rej, info_rej])

reject = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
notify_rej = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")

reject_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[pre_rej, reject, notify_rej])
reject_branch.order.add_edge(pre_rej, reject)
reject_branch.order.add_edge(reject, notify_rej)

# Combine routes and enforce Notify_Requester (inside each branch) -> Close_Case
route_choice = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[std_branch, pilot_branch, emerg_branch, reject_branch],
)
close = Transition(label="Close_Case")

M1 = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, route_choice, close])
M1.order.add_edge(register, route_choice)
M1.order.add_edge(route_choice, close)
```

### Event & Data-Collection Proposal (Normalized Schema)

1. **Core Event Log (`onboarding_events` — Append-Only):**
   - `event_id` (UUID), `case_id`, `source_tx_id` (unique constraint per `(case_id, raw_activity, source_tx_id)` to eliminate migration duplicates like SUP-711).
   - `raw_activity` + `canonical_activity` + `label_mapping_version_id` (resolves UI renames such as `QA_Clearance -> Quality_Review` in SUP-713 without retroactive guesswork).
   - `effective_ts_utc` (when the business action occurred) and `recorded_ts_utc` (immutable ingestion timestamp—both append-only).
   - `actor_alias_id`, `canonical_person_id` (resolved at event time via IAM), `delegation_id` (nullable FK to signed delegation records).
   - `policy_version_id`, `overlay_id` (nullable FK to active overlay at `effective_ts_utc`).
2. **Frozen Eligibility & Risk Snapshot (`case_eligibility_snapshots`):**
   - `snapshot_id`, `case_id`, `captured_ts_utc`, `risk_tier`, `country_code`, `approved_corporate_group_flag`, `critical_relief_flag`, `initial_spend_cap_eur`, `prior_qa_cert_id`, `prior_qa_valid_until_utc`. Prevents retroactive attribute edits and eliminates the 9 missing-attribute pilot gaps.
3. **Immutable Notification & Evidence Bridge (`notification_events`):**
   - `notification_id` (UUID generated by CRM at send time, FK-bound to `case_id`—replacing mutable text links as in SUP-706), `sent_ts_utc`, `mirrored_to_log_ts_utc`, `delivery_status`.
4. **Identity & COI Attestation Table (`actor_coi_checks`):**
   - `check_id`, `case_id`, `requester_person_id`, `qa_person_id`, `approver_person_id`, `sod_verified_flag`, `coi_disclosure_id` (required before `Manual_Procurement_Approval` whenever org-graph/HR flags reporting-line or familial ties, closing SUP-712 and SUP-715).
