# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 | **Register Change** occurs first (no visible action precedes it). | `⟨Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release Change, Archive Change⟩` | Any accepted trace starting with a different label, e.g. `⟨Assess Risk, …⟩` | yes |
| INV-02 | **Assess Risk** and **Prepare Evidence** are unordered after registration: both orders must be possible. | Both must exist: `⟨Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, …⟩` **and** `⟨Register Change, Prepare Evidence, Assess Risk, …⟩` | One of the two order-witnesses is impossible (converter silently imposes an order). | yes |
| INV-03 | **Legal Review** is optional and can only occur after **Assess Risk**; it occurs at most once. | With legal: `⟨…, Assess Risk, Legal Review, …⟩` and without legal: `⟨…, Assess Risk, … (no Legal Review) …⟩` | `⟨Legal Review, Assess Risk, …⟩` or any trace containing two `Legal Review` occurrences | yes |
| INV-04 | **Verification loop cannot start** until both **Assess Risk** and **Prepare Evidence** complete (AND-join before loop entry). | `⟨Register Change, Prepare Evidence, Assess Risk, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, …⟩` | `⟨Register Change, Assess Risk, Configure Trial, …⟩` (Configure before Prepare) or `⟨Register Change, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial, …⟩` (Configure before Assess) | yes |
| INV-05 | **Optional Legal** and the **verification loop** are unordered: legal may occur before, during (interleaved with), or after loop activity, as long as both finish before release. | Legal interleaving exists, e.g. `⟨…, Configure Trial, Legal Review, Inspect Trial, …⟩` and also `⟨…, Legal Review, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, …⟩` | All accepted traces force `Legal Review` entirely before loop (or entirely after loop), eliminating one interleaving witness. | yes |
| INV-06 | In every verification iteration, **Configure Trial** precedes **Inspect Trial**. | `⟨…, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, …⟩` | Any accepted trace with `⟨…, Inspect Trial, Configure Trial, …⟩` or `Inspect Trial` without a prior `Configure Trial` in that iteration-prefix | yes |
| INV-07 | **Correct Defect** occurs only between iterations: if it occurs, it must be after an `Inspect Trial` and must be followed by another `Configure Trial` (start of next iteration). | Two-iteration witness: `⟨…, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Correct Defect, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, …⟩` | `⟨…, Correct Defect, Configure Trial, …⟩` (Correct before any Inspect) or a trace ending `…, Correct Defect⟩` with no subsequent `Configure Trial` | yes |
| INV-08 | **At least one verification iteration occurs** (minimum one `Configure Trial`+`Inspect Trial` pair). | Any accepted completion trace containing `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial` at least once before `Release Change`. | An accepted completion trace with `⟨…, Release Change, …⟩` but no `Configure Trial`/`Inspect Trial` anywhere | yes |
| INV-09 | **Release Change** waits for completion of both (i) the optional-legal branch and (ii) the verification loop (AND-join before release). | `⟨…, (Legal Review)?, … Inspect Trial, … Release Change, Archive Change⟩` | `⟨…, Release Change, Inspect Trial, …⟩` or `⟨…, Release Change, Legal Review, …⟩` (if legal is taken) | yes |
| INV-10 | **Archive Change** occurs after **Release Change** and is terminal in the visible trace language. | `⟨…, Release Change, Archive Change⟩` | Any accepted trace with `⟨…, Archive Change, Release Change, …⟩` or with any visible activity after `Archive Change` | yes |
| INV-11 | **Visible activity-label uniqueness**: each business activity label maps to exactly one visible transition (no duplicate labels that blur semantics). | Label set equals: `{Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Legal Review, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Correct Defect, Release Change, Archive Change}` | Any conversion that duplicates/merges labels (e.g., two transitions labeled `Release Change`, or `Correct Defect` disappears) | yes |
| INV-12 | **Exact completion**: completion requires reaching the accepting final state (after `Archive Change`), not earlier. | Every completed trace ends with `Archive Change` and reaches the final marking. | A trace is considered “complete” without reaching final marking, or ends earlier (e.g., after `Release Change`) | partially (final-marking test is marking-level) |

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 (Primary): POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN** | `net,im,fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` → `bpmn = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Petri net can represent the non-block-structured partial order + true concurrency; BPMN artifact is derivable for stakeholders. | BPMN may be structurally noisy (many gateways / tau routing), layout may imply false ordering; gateways may be hard to interpret. | Any step raises; `im`/`fm` missing; net not a WF-net; soundness fails; round-trip admits forbidden witness (e.g., release before verification). |
| **P2: POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN** | `net,im,fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` → `tree = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` (try/except) → `bpmn = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | If it succeeds, yields a block-structured intermediate and often a simpler BPMN for communication. | Likely **fails** or **approximates**: the source partial order is not cleanly nestable (optional legal “hangs across” the verification join), so tree may force extra ordering (semantic drift). | `convert_to_process_tree` throws; or tree/BPMN round-trip loses any invariant witness (e.g., eliminates legal/verification interleaving). |

# 3. Conversion Code

```python
import os
import pm4py

from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def build_source_powl():
    register = Transition(label="Register Change")
    assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
    prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

    legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    skip_legal = SilentTransition()
    optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
    )

    configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
    inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
    trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
    trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

    correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
    verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
    )

    release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
    archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

    source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            register,
            assess_risk,
            prepare_evidence,
            optional_legal,
            verification_loop,
            release_change,
            archive_change,
        ]
    )

    source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
    source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)

    return source_powl


def safe_call(tag, fn, *args, **kwargs):
    try:
        return fn(*args, **kwargs)
    except Exception as e:
        print(f"[WARN] {tag} failed: {e}")
        return None


def diag_accepting_net(tag, net, im, fm):
    print(f"\n== Diagnostics: {tag} ==")
    if net is None:
        print("[WARN] net is None")
        return
    wf = safe_call(f"{tag}.check_is_workflow_net", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net, net)
    print(f"workflow_net={wf}")
    if im is None or fm is None:
        print("[WARN] Missing initial/final marking; skipping soundness.")
        return
    safe_call(
        f"{tag}.check_soundness",
        pm4py.check_soundness,
        net,
        im,
        fm,
        print_diagnostics=True,
    )


OUT_DIR = os.path.join("artifacts", "cat04_06_conversion_audit")
os.makedirs(OUT_DIR, exist_ok=True)

source_powl = build_source_powl()

# Save the authoritative source visualization
safe_call(
    "save_vis_powl",
    pm4py.save_vis_powl,
    source_powl,
    os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "01_source_powl.png"),
)

# 1) POWL -> accepting Petri net
net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)

safe_call(
    "save_vis_petri_net.powl_to_petri",
    pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
    os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "02_powl_to_accepting_petri.png"),
)
diag_accepting_net("net_from_powl", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)

# 2) accepting Petri net -> BPMN (primary)
bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
safe_call(
    "save_vis_bpmn.petri_to_bpmn",
    pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
    bpmn_from_net,
    os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "03_petri_to_bpmn.png"),
)

# 3-4) attempt Petri -> process tree -> BPMN (secondary; may fail)
tree_from_net = None
bpmn_from_tree = None
try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
    safe_call(
        "save_vis_process_tree.petri_to_tree",
        pm4py.save_vis_process_tree,
        tree_from_net,
        os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "04_petri_to_process_tree.png"),
    )

    # PM4Py supports tree -> BPMN via the same convert_to_bpmn entrypoint
    bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
    safe_call(
        "save_vis_bpmn.tree_to_bpmn",
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
        bpmn_from_tree,
        os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "05_process_tree_to_bpmn.png"),
    )
except Exception as e:
    print(f"[INFO] Petri -> process tree conversion failed (this can be the correct outcome): {e}")

# 5) Convert each produced BPMN back to accepting Petri net where possible
roundtripped_nets = {}

def try_bpmn_roundtrip(tag, bpmn):
    if bpmn is None:
        return
    try:
        n, i, f = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)
        roundtripped_nets[tag] = (n, i, f)
        safe_call(
            f"save_vis_petri_net.{tag}",
            pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
            n,
            i,
            f,
            os.path.join(OUT_DIR, f"06_roundtrip_{tag}_to_petri.png"),
        )
        diag_accepting_net(f"roundtrip_{tag}", n, i, f)
    except Exception as e:
        print(f"[WARN] BPMN -> Petri roundtrip failed for {tag}: {e}")

try_bpmn_roundtrip("bpmn_from_net", bpmn_from_net)
try_bpmn_roundtrip("bpmn_from_tree", bpmn_from_tree)

# 6) Workflow-net + soundness already executed above for every accepting net we could obtain

# 7) Visualizations were saved with clear filenames in OUT_DIR
print("\nProduced artifacts in:", OUT_DIR)
print("Round-tripped nets:", list(roundtripped_nets.keys()))
```

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 (Register first) | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Validate by negative witness `⟨Assess Risk, …⟩` rejection and by trace enumeration from `im` (no visible prefix before Register). |
| INV-02 (Assess/Prepare unordered) | validated preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | validated preserved | Language property can be checked by existence of both order witnesses; “true concurrency” vs nondeterministic interleaving remains ambiguous. |
| INV-03 (Optional legal after assess) | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Presence/absence of `Legal Review` in accepted traces; tau-skip may be rendered as empty routing in BPMN and misread. |
| INV-04 (Join before verification) | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Check that no trace contains `Configure Trial` before both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence`. |
| INV-05 (No order between legal and verification) | validated preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | validated preserved | BPMN layout/gateway nesting can accidentally imply order; tree intermediate typically cannot encode “legal starts after assess but may overlap verification.” |
| INV-06 (Configure before Inspect) | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Check with negative witness `⟨…, Inspect Trial, Configure Trial, …⟩`. |
| INV-07 (Correct Defect only between iterations) | validated preserved | approximated | conversion failed | validated preserved | BPMN may represent LOOP with a marker or gateway cycle that weakens the “redo part” placement; round-trip trace checks can re-validate language-level constraints under bounds. |
| INV-08 (≥1 verification iteration) | validated preserved | approximated | conversion failed | validated preserved | BPMN loop depiction might allow zero-iteration misreading; validate by rejecting any accepted completion trace missing both `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial`. |
| INV-09 (Join before Release) | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Critical safety check: reject any accepted trace where `Release Change` precedes (i) last `Inspect Trial` or (ii) `Legal Review` when legal is taken. |
| INV-10 (Archive after Release; terminal) | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Validate by rejecting any trace with visible activity after `Archive Change`, and any `Archive` before `Release`. |
| INV-11 (Unique labels) | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | ambiguous | Round-trip may duplicate labels via split/merge; validate by counting visible transitions by label and ensuring 1:1 mapping per label. |
| INV-12 (Exact completion via final marking) | validated preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | validated preserved | Requires marking-level equality checks; BPMN “end events” may not correspond 1:1 to accepting final marking. |

Key semantic-loss risks to explicitly audit:

- **True concurrency vs. arbitrary interleaving**: Petri nets can encode concurrent enabling; BPMN may show parallel gateways, but both (i) real concurrency and (ii) nondeterministic interleavings yield the same set of linear traces. Footprints can hint at concurrency, but cannot prove it.
- **No edge between Optional Legal and verification**: Any tree/block-structured intermediate tends to force nesting, which typically **creates an order** (either “legal before verification” or “legal after verification”), violating INV-05.
- **Silent optional behavior**: The tau-skip for legal can be collapsed into BPMN routing nodes (unlabeled gateways/sequence flows). This can lead to misinterpretation (“legal must happen but is unlabeled”) unless annotated.
- **Binary-loop semantics (do/redo)**: The POWL `LOOP(A,B)` means at least one `A`, with `B` only between iterations. BPMN conversions may represent looping with (a) a loop marker on a subprocess, (b) explicit gateways, or (c) back-edges; some of these can accidentally allow zero iterations or allow `Correct Defect` to be skipped/misplaced.
- **Join before Release**: The release is a synchronization point for *both* optional legal and verification completion. In BPMN, an incorrect join type (XOR vs AND) or mis-nesting can admit `Release Change` early.
- **Activity-label uniqueness**: Conversions can introduce multiple visible transitions with the same label (e.g., duplicating `Release Change` across branches), breaking label-based audits unless explicitly checked.
- **Initial/final markings**: A successful serialization does not guarantee the accepting marking is preserved; always verify that a completed run reaches `fm` exactly, not just “some end event.”
- **Gateway/routing-node ambiguity in BPMN visualization**: Unlabeled gateways, implicit merges, and layout crossings can cause humans to read ordering that is not semantically required.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The comparison below is **exhaustive under the chosen bound** iff:

- the exploration completes without hitting the caps (`max_states`, `max_traces`), and
- it reports `exhaustive_under_bound=True` for both the baseline and each round-tripped net.

Otherwise, it is a **sound but incomplete** search (it may miss long behaviors or deep silent routings).

```python
import pm4py

try:
    from pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics import execute as fire_transition
except Exception:
    # Fallback for some PM4Py layouts
    from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics as _semantics
    fire_transition = _semantics.execute


def _place_id(p):
    return getattr(p, "name", repr(p))


def marking_key(marking):
    if marking is None:
        return None
    return tuple(sorted((_place_id(p), int(w)) for p, w in marking.items()))


def is_tau(t):
    return getattr(t, "label", None) is None


def t_label(t):
    return getattr(t, "label", None)


def enumerate_visible_traces(net, im, fm, max_visible_len=12, max_iterations=3, max_states=200000, max_traces=200000):
    """
    Bounded, language-based exploration:
    - Hides transitions with label None (tau).
    - Accepts a trace only if the run ends in EXACT final marking equality.
    - Bounds visible length AND bounds verification iterations via counting 'Configure Trial'.
    - Avoids infinite silent cycling via visited-state pruning on (marking, trace, iter_count).
    """
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        return set(), False

    fm_key = marking_key(fm)
    init_state = (im, tuple(), 0)

    stack = [init_state]
    visited = set()
    traces = set()

    exhaustive = True
    steps = 0

    while stack:
        if len(visited) >= max_states or len(traces) >= max_traces:
            exhaustive = False
            break

        marking, vis_trace, iter_count = stack.pop()
        state_key = (marking_key(marking), vis_trace, iter_count)
        if state_key in visited:
            continue
        visited.add(state_key)

        # Record completed traces only if final marking matches exactly
        if marking_key(marking) == fm_key:
            traces.add(vis_trace)
            # Do not stop here; tau may still be enabled in some nets (should be rejected separately)

        # Bound visible length and iteration count
        if len(vis_trace) >= max_visible_len:
            continue
        if iter_count > max_iterations:
            continue

        enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
        for t in enabled:
            try:
                new_marking = fire_transition(t, net, marking)
            except Exception:
                continue

            if is_tau(t):
                # tau does not change the visible trace or iteration count
                stack.append((new_marking, vis_trace, iter_count))
            else:
                lab = t_label(t)
                if lab is None:
                    stack.append((new_marking, vis_trace, iter_count))
                else:
                    new_iter = iter_count + (1 if lab == "Configure Trial" else 0)
                    stack.append((new_marking, vis_trace + (lab,), new_iter))

        steps += 1

    return traces, exhaustive


def accepts_witness(net, im, fm, witness, **kwargs):
    traces, exhaustive = enumerate_visible_traces(net, im, fm, **kwargs)
    return (tuple(witness) in traces), exhaustive


POS_WITNESSES = {

def compare_footprints(tag_a, net_a, im_a, fm_a, tag_b, net_b, im_b, fm_b):
    """
    Footprints are heuristic; treat mismatches as signals, not proofs.
    """
    out = {"ok": False}
    if None in (net_a, im_a, fm_a, net_b, im_b, fm_b):
        return out
    try:
        fp_a = pm4py.discover_footprints(net_a, im_a, fm_a)
        fp_b = pm4py.discover_footprints(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
        out = {"ok": True, "fp_a_type": type(fp_a).__name__, "fp_b_type": type(fp_b).__name__}
        out["equal_repr"] = (repr(fp_a) == repr(fp_b))
    except Exception as e:
        out = {"ok": False, "error": str(e)}
    return out


def reachability_summary(net, im, fm):
    if None in (net, im, fm):
        return None
    try:
        rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
        # Different PM4Py versions expose different attributes; keep it robust.
        n_states = getattr(rg, "number_of_states", None) or getattr(rg, "number_of_nodes", None)
        n_trans = getattr(rg, "number_of_transitions", None) or getattr(rg, "number_of_edges", None)
        return {"type": type(rg).__name__, "states": n_states, "edges": n_trans}
    except Exception as e:
        return {"error": str(e)}


def compare_nets_language(baseline, candidate, bounds):
    net0, im0, fm0 = baseline
    net1, im1, fm1 = candidate

    traces0, ex0 = enumerate_visible_traces(net0, im0, fm0, **bounds)
    traces1, ex1 = enumerate_visible_traces(net1, im1, fm1, **bounds)

    only0 = traces0 - traces1
    only1 = traces1 - traces0

    return {
        "exhaustive_under_bound": bool(ex0 and ex1),
        "n_traces_baseline": len(traces0),
        "n_traces_candidate": len(traces1),
        "n_only_baseline": len(only0),
        "n_only_candidate": len(only1),
        "sample_only_baseline": list(sorted(only0))[:5],
        "sample_only_candidate": list(sorted(only1))[:5],
    }


def run_witness_suite(tag, net, im, fm, bounds):
    res = {"positive": {}, "negative": {}}
    for k, w in POS_WITNESSES.items():
        ok, exhaustive = accepts_witness(net, im, fm, w, **bounds)
        res["positive"][k] = {"accepted": ok, "exhaustive_under_bound": exhaustive}
    for k, w in NEG_WITNESSES.items():
        ok, exhaustive = accepts_witness(net, im, fm, w, **bounds)
        res["negative"][k] = {"accepted": ok, "exhaustive_under_bound": exhaustive}
    return res


def audit_roundtrip_equivalence(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl, roundtripped_nets):
    baseline = (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)

    bounds = {
        "max_visible_len": 14,
        "max_iterations": 3,
        "max_states": 250000,
        "max_traces": 250000,
    }

    print("\n== Baseline diagnostics ==")
    pm4py.check_soundness(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl, print_diagnostics=True)
    print("baseline reachability:", reachability_summary(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl))

    for tag, (net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt) in roundtripped_nets.items():
        print(f"\n== Round-trip candidate: {tag} ==")
        if None in (net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt):
            print("[WARN] Missing net/im/fm; skipping")
            continue

        pm4py.check_soundness(net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt, print_diagnostics=True)
        print("candidate reachability:", reachability_summary(net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt))

        fp_cmp = compare_footprints("baseline", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl, tag, net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt)
        print("footprints compare:", fp_cmp)

        lang_cmp = compare_nets_language(baseline, (net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt), bounds)
        print("language compare under bound:", {k: v for k, v in lang_cmp.items() if not k.startswith("sample_")})
        if lang_cmp["n_only_baseline"] or lang_cmp["n_only_candidate"]:
            print("sample only-baseline traces:", lang_cmp["sample_only_baseline"])
```

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

Even if the BPMN artifact is behaviorally acceptable (i.e., it round-trips to a net that matches the baseline under audit), the **rendering can be misread**:

- **Parallel gateways and “implied order” from layout**: Left-to-right placement often suggests sequence, but BPMN execution semantics are defined by gateways/flows, not by geometry. A converter may lay out two parallel branches vertically yet still route them through extra gateways that obscure true independence.
- **Exclusive gateways vs silent routing**: The optional legal XOR with a tau branch can become an unlabeled XOR gateway where one outgoing branch looks like a “missing activity.” Stakeholders may misinterpret this as “Legal Review happens but is unnamed,” rather than “Legal Review is optional.”
- **Loop visualization mismatch**: The POWL LOOP is a do/redo loop (`trial_body` then optionally `correct_defect` then repeat). BPMN may depict this as:
  - a loop marker on a subprocess (often read as “repeat zero or more times”), or
  - a gateway cycle (often read as a while-loop),
  either of which can be read differently from “at least once; redo part only between iterations.”
- **Silent routing nodes**: Conversions from Petri nets commonly introduce many unlabeled gateways to represent places/transitions. These are semantically necessary for faithful routing but are cognitively noisy.
- **Join-node ambiguity**: The join before `Release Change` must be an AND-join. If the diagram uses a merge that looks exclusive (or if lines cross), readers may assume release can happen after only one branch finishes.

Mitigations (recommended annotations/documentation):

- Add explicit **text annotations**: “Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence are unordered,” “Legal Review is optional,” “Release waits for (Legal + Verification).”
- Label gateways (when the tool allows) as **AND-split/AND-join** and **XOR-split/XOR-join**.
- For the loop, prefer an explicit gateway-based loop with a note: **“At least one iteration; Correct Defect only between iterations.”**
- Provide the **invariant table (Section 1)** alongside the BPMN diagram; treat BPMN as a view, not the semantic source.
- In stakeholder docs, include two or three **illustrative interleavings** (especially INV-05) to prevent accidental “legal must be before verification” assumptions.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a conversion result if any of the following holds:

1. The conversion step raises an exception (other than the explicitly “allowed-to-fail” Petri→Tree attempt), or returns `None` for `im`/`fm` where an accepting net is required.
2. The produced accepting net is not a workflow net (`pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)` is false/throws) or `pm4py.check_soundness(...)` indicates unsoundness, deadlocks, or improper completion.
3. Any **negative witness** becomes accepted, especially:
   - `Release Change` before verification completes,
   - `Inspect Trial` before `Configure Trial`,
   - `Legal Review` before `Assess Risk`,
   - `Correct Defect` without a subsequent new iteration.
4. Any **required positive witness** is lost (e.g., no trace exists with `Prepare Evidence` before `Assess Risk`, or no interleaving exists where legal occurs between configure and inspect).
5. The BPMN round-trip net fails the **exact-final-marking acceptance test** for completed traces (completion without reaching `fm`, or multiple “ends” that do not correspond to the accepting marking).
6. Loop semantics drift: the conversion admits **zero verification iterations**, or admits `Correct Defect` in positions other than “between iterations.”
7. Label mapping drift: visible activity labels are duplicated, merged, renamed, or dropped (INV-11 violated), making label-based audits unreliable.
8. The bounded trace-language comparison reports large asymmetric differences (many traces only in baseline or only in candidate) under reasonable bounds, or exploration is non-exhaustive due to runaway silent routing (state explosion), indicating the artifact is too unstable for stakeholder use.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

**Authoritative canonical for storage (semantics)**: **accepting Petri net + explicit initial/final markings** (`net_from_powl`, `im_from_powl`, `fm_from_powl`), with the POWL code kept as **provenance and editable source**.

- **Authoritative**: The accepting Petri net is the canonical interchange/analysis form because it can represent:
  - the non-block-structured partial order (INV-05 risk),
  - true concurrency (as far as the notation can express),
  - precise acceptance via \(im,fm\),
  and it is directly compatible with soundness/reachability tooling.
- **Derivative artifacts**:
  - `bpmn_from_net` is **derivative** for stakeholder visualization.
  - Any `process_tree` or `bpmn_from_tree` is **derivative** and must be treated as “best-effort” unless it passes the same invariant suite.
- **Validation evidence required per derivative**:
  - soundness/workflow-net diagnostics for the derivative’s round-tripped net,
  - bounded visible-trace equivalence report (Section 5),
  - explicit pass/fail of positive/negative witness suite,
  - a label uniqueness report (no duplicated visible labels).
- **Should a process-tree intermediate be allowed?** Only as an optional *view-optimization*:
  - If `convert_to_process_tree` fails: record “conversion failed” and do not force it.
  - If it succeeds: still treat it as potentially **approximated** and require the same witness/trace checks before using its BPMN for communication.
- **Avoiding future round-trip drift**:
  - Never “edit back” from BPMN into the canonical model.
  - Apply all edits in the canonical form (Petri net or upstream POWL), regenerate BPMN, and re-run the invariant+round-trip audit.
  - Version artifacts together with their validation report (hash of canonical net + bounds used + witness outcomes).

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if all proposed tests pass, the audit cannot establish (at least) the following:

1. **True concurrency vs. trace-equivalent interleaving**: A trace-language match (even exhaustive under a bounded length/iteration cap) cannot prove the converted BPMN enforces concurrency rather than simulating it via interleavings or scheduling assumptions.
2. **Unbounded behavioral equivalence**: With loops, any bounded exploration cannot guarantee equality of languages for all lengths/iteration counts, nor can it rule out rare long-run divergences introduced by silent routing.
3. **Engine-specific BPMN execution semantics**: Different BPMN engines interpret gateways, merges, and termination/end events with subtle differences; a net round-trip equivalence does not guarantee identical runtime behavior in every BPMN tool.
4. **Human interpretation fidelity**: Even if behavior is preserved, stakeholders may still misread the BPMN due to layout, unlabeled routing nodes, or loop depiction; this is a communication risk beyond formal validation.
