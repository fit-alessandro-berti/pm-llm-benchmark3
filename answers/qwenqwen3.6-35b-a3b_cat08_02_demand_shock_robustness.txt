    *   *Failure Mode:* If Urgent bypass triggers too frequently, batch efficiency gains are lost, and machine schedules fragment. Mitigation: Monitor "Urgent interruption rate"; cap Urgent interruptions per batch cycle.

    *   *Trigger:* **Hold for specialist review queue > 20h**.
    *   *Action:* Auto-route Complex-panel cases with low-risk markers to a "Pre-validated" pool for rapid Medical review (skipping full specialist review if criteria met). Notify partner lab for overflow (I10) with expedited courier.
    *   *Deactivation:* Queue < 15h.
    *   *Rationale:* Reduces Specialist Review load (36h queue) for cases that don't need deep review. Uses I10 selectively to avoid transport delay penalties for routine cases.

*   **P4: Recollection Alert & Clerk Reallocation**
    *   *Trigger:* **Recollection rate > 10%** OR **Accession Correction queue > 12h**.
    *   *Action:* Pause non-urgent registration. Redirect Registration clerks to assist with recollection data entry and physician communication. Implement "Sample Kit Check" digital prompt for clerks.
    *   *Deactivation:* Recollection rate < 8% for 4 hours.
    *   *Rationale:* Addresses S4 bottleneck where clerks are overwhelmed by recollection loops. Frees up intake capacity.

### 6. Residual Vulnerabilities

*   **Technical Validation Capacity Hard Limit:**
    *   *Vulnerability:* Technical Validation is at 101% utilization in peak. No candidate intervention increases Tech capacity. I4 (Cross-train Tech to Accession) would worsen this. The redesign package relies on flow optimization, but Tech Validation remains the hard constraint. If Tech Validation does not improve, Medical Validation shifts (I5) will be underutilized during Tech bottlenecks.
    *   *Redesign-induced risk:* I6 and I7 increase valid flow into the system. If Tech Validation cannot keep up, the queue will shift entirely to Pre-analytical/Preparation, causing WIP accumulation without improving release times.

*   **Urgent Bypass Fragmentation:**
    *   *Vulnerability:* I2 increases batch size, relying on Urgent bypass to protect SLAs. If Urgent volume spikes (S1), the bypass may trigger frequently, fragmenting machine schedules and reducing the efficiency gains of larger batches. This could lead to Prep bench becoming unstable during high Urgent mix.

*   **Recollection Dependency on Courier:**
    *   *Vulnerability:* I6 rejects poor samples early, generating recollection requests. If the courier system is delayed (S4), these recollection samples cannot return quickly, extending the loop. The redesign reduces loop frequency but does not solve the courier dependency for recollections.

*   **Specialist Review Scalability:**
    *   *Vulnerability:* Specialist Review has a 36h queue and only one specialist unavailable every second Friday. P3 attempts to triage, but Complex-panel cases (20% peak) may still overwhelm the specialist. There is no robust intervention to increase specialist capacity or automate review.

### 7. Minimum Monitoring Dashboard

Daily monitoring during peak periods should focus on leading indicators that predict bottlenecks before SLAs are breached.

*   **Leading Indicators (Warning Bands):**
    *   **Recollection Rate:** > 8% (Normal < 5%). *Signal:* Pre-analytical quality is degrading; loop risk increasing.
    *   **Courier Arrival Variance:** > 20% deviation from schedule. *Signal:* Intake spike imminent; Pre-analytical queue will grow.
    *   **Technical Validation Queue Depth:** > 8 hours. *Signal:* Tech bottleneck active; Medical shift (I5) may be ineffective; Urgent cases piling up.
    *   **M2 Failure Rate:** > 2% over 4 hours. *Signal:* M2 degradation; P2 protocol may need activation.
    *   **Urgent Pipeline Age:** > 10 hours. *Signal:* Urgent SLA risk; Check if bypass is working or if Med queue is blocking.
    *   **Accession Correction Queue:** > 8 hours. *Signal:* Intake errors rising; Clerks may need reallocation (P4).

*   **Lagging Outcome Metrics (Review Weekly):**
    *   **Urgent SLA Breach Rate:** % of cases > 18h.
    *   **Routine p90 Cycle Time:** Target < 68h.
    *   **Overall Rework Rate:** Target < 7.5%.
    *   **Release Result Throughput:** Cases per hour.
    *   **Specialist Review Turnaround:** Median hours.

*   **Dashboard Action:**
    *   If **Tech Queue > 8h** AND **Med Queue < 10h**: Deactivate I5 (Evening Shift) to save cost; focus on Tech efficiency.
    *   If **Recollection > 8%** AND **Courier Variance > 20%**: Activate P4; alert management to courier issues.
    *   If **Urgent Mix > 25%**: Activate P1; monitor Routine SLA impact.
