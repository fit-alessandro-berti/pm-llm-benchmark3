Forecast anchor: "now" = Friday 18 Sep 2026, 15:30. All remaining-time figures are hours from this anchor (Mon 21 Sep 15:30 = 72 h, Tue 22 Sep 15:30 = 96 h, Wed 23 Sep 15:30 = 120 h, Thu 24 Sep 15:30 = 144 h, Mon 28 Sep 15:30 = 240 h).

Calculation shortcuts used throughout (stated once): triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) chemistry service has mean ≈ 2.8 h; rerun triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) has mean ≈ 1.9 h; QA service with median 1.5 h and P80 ≈ 3.5 h is treated as roughly lognormal (σ ≈ 1.0), giving a mean of ≈ 2.3–2.5 h, so two reviewers drain ≈ 0.8–0.9 batches/h; QA queue evolution is approximated as a fluid model (drain rate minus arrival rate) with a ±2–3 batch overdispersion band; probabilities of compound events are multiplied only after conditioning on the temperature-excursion state; all stage combinations use max() at the parallel join and only sum sequential stages after the join.

## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

Common facts for every scenario

- Chemistry waiting: at 15:30 two analysts are busy; the second frees ≈15:55–16:25, the first ≈16:40–17:30. Under risk-priority the urgent sample takes the first free analyst and B-417 takes the second (start ≈16:40–17:30, 0.5–1.3 h of work done before 18:00). Under FIFO, B-417 is 5th; two samples begin today, two more begin Monday morning, B-417 begins Monday ≈11:30–14:30.
- Chemistry service (triangular 1.5/2.5/4.5, mean ≈2.8 h): priority path finishes Monday ≈08:15–12:00 (central ≈10:00); FIFO path finishes Monday ≈13:00–17:00 (central ≈15:00), with a small chance (long service) of spilling to Tuesday morning.
- Microbiology incubation: technically ready Sunday 12:00–20:00 regardless of anything else. Readout 0.5–1.5 h: without weekend authorisation (55–75 %) the result is available Monday 08:30–09:30; with authorisation (25–45 %) Sunday 13:00–23:00. Because QA does not work Sunday, both variants deliver an equivalent "usable at Monday 08:00–09:30" input on the smooth path.
- Parallel join = max(chemistry finish, microbiology usable). The two Monday-morning clocks are close: microbiology sets a floor at ≈08:30–09:30, chemistry under priority lands ≈08:15–12:00 (either can be the slower one), chemistry under FIFO lands ≈13:00–17:00 and is clearly the slower branch. So the weekend calendar dominates the location (Monday), and the queue discipline decides which branch is the binding clock within Monday. A sum of branch durations (≈2.8 h chemistry + ≈50 h microbiology + …) would be wrong: the branches overlap almost entirely.
- QA queue at the join: Monday 08:00 backlog ≈3–7 (central 4–5). Two reviewers drain ≈0.8–0.9 batches/h against 0.35/h arrivals, net ≈0.5/h. Number ahead of B-417 at join time t ≈ backlog − 0.5 × (hours since 08:00), floored near 1–2 by arrival noise. Waiting ≈ (number ahead) / 0.85 per hour.

- Microbiology: usable Mon 08:30–09:30 (or Sunday evening if authorised).

S6 Laboratory investigation or confirmed critical defect

- Stages up to the investigation decision as in S2/S3/S5 (Mon afternoon – Tue afternoon).
- Laboratory investigation adds 3–7 calendar days (runs through the weekend of 26–27 Sep as elapsed time, but the closing QA review must land in QA hours): decision Tue 22 Sep + 3–7 days → Fri 25 Sep – Tue 29 Sep, then QA re-review ≈1–3 h in QA hours and release.

Where the maximum replaces the sum: only at the Monday join (chemistry vs microbiology) and again in S3 (chemistry vs microbiology confirmation). Everything downstream of the join—QA waiting, QA service, query/investigation loops, release—is sequential and is summed, with calendar pauses inserted (Mon 20:00 → Tue 08:00 QA closure; Mon 18:00 → Tue 08:00 chemistry closure; Sat/Sun microbiology staffing gap).

## 4. Forecast summary

- Plausible earliest completion: Monday 21 Sep ≈11:00–13:00 (≈67.5–69.5 h). Requires risk-priority chemistry pickup today, no rerun, clean microbiology readout Monday 08:30–09:30, a short or expedited QA queue and a sub-median review. A release before Monday morning is effectively impossible because chemistry cannot close today, microbiology is not readable before Sunday afternoon, and QA is closed Sunday.
- Central / P50-like remaining time: ≈73–91 h, i.e. Monday 21 Sep ≈16:30 to Tuesday 22 Sep ≈11:00. The band is deliberately wide and bimodal: roughly 45–50 % of probability mass lands Monday afternoon/evening (S1 and fast S2), and the QA overnight closure (Mon 20:00 → Tue 08:00) pushes the next tranche to Tuesday morning, so the median sits on that gap.
- P80-like remaining time: ≈96–117 h, i.e. Tuesday 22 Sep ≈16:00 to Wednesday 23 Sep ≈12:00 (documentation query, rerun with spill, documentation-only investigation, or a refuted contamination indication).
- P90-like / severe tail: ≈122–150 h (Wednesday 23 Sep evening to Thursday 24 Sep) for compounded working-hour rework; the 5–12 % severe tail (laboratory investigation, confirmed contamination) extends to ≈144–240 h (Thu 24 – Mon 28 Sep) with an outer edge near ≈260–290 h (Tue 29 – Wed 30 Sep).
- Most plausible terminal event: Electronic Release, ≈90–96 %. Batch Rejected ≈4–10 % (confirmed contamination ≈2–6 % after a 0.05–0.20 indication with partial confirmation; plus a share of laboratory investigations). Rejection, if it happens, is most likely recorded Tuesday 22 – Wednesday 23 Sep, or late the following week after a laboratory investigation.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. Temperature-excursion flag (0.20–0.40 confirmed) — dependence uncertainty (with observation uncertainty from the sensor gap). It is the common cause that simultaneously raises rerun, contamination and investigation probabilities; it decides whether the investigation probability is ≈0.05–0.10 or ≈0.30–0.55 and thereby how much mass sits beyond Tuesday.
2. Deviation Investigation path (documentation-only vs laboratory, 8–20 working hours vs 3–7 calendar days) — path uncertainty. This single branch governs the P90 and severe tail (Wednesday vs following week).
3. Chemistry queue discipline realised at ≈16:40–17:30 today (FIFO vs risk-priority) — queue uncertainty. It moves the Monday join from ≈10:00 to ≈15:00, flips which branch is the binding clock, and raises the probability that a rerun spills past Monday 18:00 into Tuesday.
4. QA backlog at the Monday join (≈3–7 batches, overdispersed arrivals, possible expedite) — queue uncertainty. Each batch ahead adds ≈1.1–1.3 h; combined with the P80 3.5 h service tail it decides whether Monday's review finishes before the 20:00 close or slips to Tuesday morning.
5. Microbiology outcome and readout staffing (contamination indication 0.05–0.20; weekend authorisation 0.25–0.45) — path uncertainty coupled with calendar uncertainty. A low-probability indication adds ≈1–1.5 days and carries most of the rejection risk; authorisation matters mainly by shortening that contamination path, not the smooth path.

(Service-time uncertainty of the chemistry triangular and QA lognormal is present but ranks below these: it shifts results by hours within a day rather than across calendar days.)
