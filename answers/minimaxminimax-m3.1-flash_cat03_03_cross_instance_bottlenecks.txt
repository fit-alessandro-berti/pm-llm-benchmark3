## 8. Falsification and additional data

Observations that would weaken the top-ranked batch-synchronization explanation include:

1. The 12:05 `Batch Cure` timestamp denotes cure completion rather than batch start.
2. “Ready for batch” timestamps were entered retrospectively or mean enqueue time rather than material-ready time.
3. OVEN-1 or RACK-7 was demonstrably unavailable before 12:05.
4. B1 membership was recorded retrospectively and did not actually gate processing.
5. Immediate independent processing was technically, legally, or economically infeasible.
6. Similar non-batch jobs show equal or greater waiting.
7. J1/J2 were delayed primarily by missing components or upstream quality release.
8. Batch waiting is a one-off event rather than a recurring policy outcome.

Additional discriminating fields or event types are:
