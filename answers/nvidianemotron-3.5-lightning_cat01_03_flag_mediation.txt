**Pilot selection**: P3 (retain current routing + add protected manualreview capacity for flagged cases) is selected for a limited pilot.

- **Eligibility**: All new cases entering the system during the pilot period; flagged cases (risk_score above current threshold) are eligible for protected manual review; unflagged cases continue under existing routing.
- **Randomization or assignment rule**: Cases are assigned to “protected capacity” arm or “standard capacity” arm based on a 1:1 random allocation at intake, stratified by complexity (Low/Medium/High) and arrival_load (High/Normal). The protected arm receives a dedicated manual-review resource (e.g., a senior clerk) unavailable to the standard arm.
- **Queuecapacity guardrail**: The protected capacity is capped at 20% of total daily manualreview slots to avoid overall system capacity depletion. If protected arm utilization exceeds 85% for three consecutive days, the guardrail triggers a temporary suspension of new protected assignments.
- **Primary outcomes**: (a) median triagedonetomanual-start time for flagged cases; (b) total cycle time for flagged cases; (c) proportion of flagged cases receiving manual review (should remain ~100%).
- **Secondary outcomes**: (d) total cycle time for unflagged cases (to guard against spillover); (e) escalation rate and wait time for flagged cases; (f) decision outcome quality (Approve/Reject consistency with risk_score).
- **Minimum process data needed**: Timestamps for Register, Triage start/done, Manual review start/done (if any), Escalation start/done (if any), Close, risk_score, flag, complexity, arrival_load, triage_resource, review_resource. Eventlevel logs compatible with PM4Py (as in Section 7).
- **Stopping conditions**: Pilot halted if (a) total cycle time for flagged cases does not improve by at least 15% relative to P0 baseline after 4 weeks; (b) unflagged total cycle time increases by >10%; (c) adverse event rate (e.g., missed escalations flagged by compliance audit) rises; or (d) protected capacity guardrail triggers repeated suspensions.
