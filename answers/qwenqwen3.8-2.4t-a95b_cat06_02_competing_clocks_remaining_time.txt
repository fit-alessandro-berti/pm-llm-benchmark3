| **QA query / investigation tail** | **25–55%**; overlaps strongly with rerun/contamination/excursion | QA working-hour rework and investigation calendar | **Mon 21 Sep ~16:30** for a very short query | **Wed 23 Sep 12:00 – Fri 25 Sep 12:00** | **Mon 28 Sep – Wed 30 Sep** for laboratory investigation; rejection possible |
| **Correlated excursion / critical defect tail** | Severe excursion-driven tail **5–15%**; rejection roughly **2–10%** | Investigation/decision clock, not simple branch max | **Tue 22 Sep ~12:00** if critical defect is immediately confirmed | **Thu 24 Sep – Tue 29 Sep** | **Beyond Wed 30 Sep** if external/lab work is prolonged |

The probability bands are **not additive**. For example, “delayed microbiology readout” can occur inside an otherwise smooth path if the only issue is lack of weekend authorization. A chemistry rerun can co-occur with a QA deviation, especially if the temperature excursion is confirmed. The severe excursion tail overlaps with rerun, contamination, and investigation scenarios.

## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Scenario 1: Smooth path, no rework

**Chemistry waiting and service**

- At Friday 15:30, two analysts are busy:
  - Analyst A free in about **0.4–0.9 h**, i.e. **15:54–16:24**.
  - Analyst B free in about **1.2–2.0 h**, i.e. **16:42–17:30**.
- There are four samples ahead: one urgent and three ordinary.
- Under FIFO, B-417 probably does not start Friday.
- Under risk-priority, B-417 may move ahead of ordinary samples but probably still behind the urgent sample. It may start late Friday and be paused at 18:00.
- Chemistry service time is triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) h.
- Practical chemistry usable-result window:
  - Very favorable: **Friday 18:00** if B-417 starts early and service is near the minimum. Low probability.
  - Central: **Monday 09:00–15:00** after resume/queue processing.
  - Delayed but still smooth: **Monday 16:00–18:00**.

**Microbiology incubation and readout**

- Incubation started Friday 14:00.
- Technical readiness is 46–54 h later:
  - Earliest: **Sunday 20 Sep 12:00**.
  - Latest: **Sunday 20 Sep 20:00**.
- If on-call authorization is granted, readout occurs about 1–3 h after readiness:
  - **Sunday ~13:00–23:00**.
- If not authorized, routine readout begins Monday:
  - **Monday ~08:30–11:00**, allowing 0.5–1.5 h readout and a small queue.
- No contamination indication.

**Parallel join time**

- The join uses a **maximum**, not a sum:
  - `Join  max(chemistry usable time, microbiology usable time)`.
- If chemistry finishes Friday and micro is read Sunday, the technical join is Sunday, but QA is closed Sunday, so QA can start only **Monday 08:00**.
- If chemistry finishes Monday morning/afternoon, the join is **Monday 09:00–15:00**, because micro is either already available Sunday or available Monday morning.

**QA waiting and service**

- Initial QA queue: 5 batches at Friday 15:30.
- QA capacity before Monday:
  - Friday 15:30–20:00: 4.5 reviewer-hours.
  - Saturday 09:00–13:00: 4 reviewer-hours.
- Expected arrivals over Friday/Saturday opening hours: about `0.35 × 8.5  3` batches, with overdispersion.
- By Monday 08:00, the residual QA queue is plausibly **0–4 batches**, but bursty arrivals or long services could make it higher.
- Monday has two reviewers. B-417 wait is plausibly **0–4 h** in the central case, with a tail of perhaps **6+ h** if the queue is congested.
- QA service time: median 1.5 h, 80th percentile about 3.5 h. Use roughly **1–3.5 h** for central planning.

**Optional rework/investigation**

- None in this scenario: no documentation query, no deviation investigation.

**Final release**

- Electronic Release takes 0.25–0.75 h and is available continuously after QA acceptance.
- Terminal window:
  - Earliest plausible: **Monday 21 Sep ~09:30** if chemistry was Friday, micro read Sunday, QA queue is minimal, and QA service is short.
  - Central: **Monday 21 Sep 18:00 to Tuesday 22 Sep 13:00**.
  - Smooth-path tail: **Tuesday 22 Sep ~18:00** if QA queue or service is longer.

### Scenario 2: Chemistry rerun

**Chemistry waiting and initial service**

- Initial queue dynamics are the same as in Scenario 1.
- Initial chemistry completion is plausibly **Monday 09:00–16:00**, with a rare Friday completion.
- Borderline result probability:
  - Base: **0.10–0.20**.
  - If temperature excursion is confirmed: higher, plausibly **0.25–0.40**.

**Chemistry rerun service and calendar pause**

- Rerun gets priority and takes triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) h.
- Rerun cannot continue after chemistry lab closes at 18:00.
- If initial result is available Monday morning, rerun may finish Monday afternoon.
- If rerun would cross 18:00, it pauses and resumes Tuesday 08:00.
- Practical chemistry usable-result window with rerun:
  - Favorable: **Monday 12:00–18:00**.
  - Central: **Tuesday 08:00–12:00**.
  - Delayed: **Tuesday afternoon** if rerun is long or queued.

**Microbiology incubation and readout**

- In this scenario, microbiology is assumed not to have a contamination confirmation unless overlapping with the delayed-micro scenario.
- Micro usable time remains:
  - Sunday 13:00–23:00 if authorized.
  - Monday 08:30–11:00 if not authorized.

**Parallel join time**

- Join is again `max(chemistry rerun completion, microbiology usable time)`.
- Because chemistry rerun usually moves chemistry to Monday afternoon or Tuesday morning, chemistry is often the binding branch.

**QA waiting and service**

- If join is Monday afternoon, QA may still review Monday evening if queue is low.
- If join is Tuesday morning, QA review occurs Tuesday.
- QA wait: central **0–5 h**, tail longer if queue bursts occur.
- QA service: **1–3.5 h** central.

**Optional rework/investigation**

- A chemistry rerun makes a deviation investigation more likely: investigation probability rises to **0.30–0.55** if rerun, contamination, or confirmed excursion exists.
- Central rerun scenario assumes either no investigation or a short documentation-only investigation.
- Tail includes documentation-only investigation adding roughly **8–20 working hours**.

**Final release or rejection**

- Release central: **Tuesday 22 Sep 09:00–18:00**.
- Tail release: **Wednesday 23 Sep 12:00–18:00**.
- Rejection is possible but not central unless the rerun reveals a critical defect.

**Microbiology contamination confirmation**

  - Base: **0.05–0.12**.

Approximate timing:

### Scenario 4: QA documentation query or investigation tail

**Branches and join time**

- The join time may come from Scenario 1, 2, or 3.

**Final release or rejection**

### Scenario 5: Correlated temperature-excursion / critical-defect tail

**Dependence structure**

- Temperature excursion confirmation probability: **0.20–0.40**.
- If confirmed:
  - Chemistry rerun probability increases.
  - Microbiology contamination probability increases.
  - QA deviation/investigation probability increases to **0.30–0.55**.
- This creates positive dependence: bad outcomes cluster. A simple independent model would understate the probability of simultaneous rerun, contamination, and investigation.

**Operational consequence**

- The dominant clock shifts from the simple parallel-branch maximum to the investigation and decision process.
- If both branches have anomalies, the join may already be Tuesday/Wednesday.
- If a laboratory investigation is opened, add 3–7 calendar days.

**Terminal outcomes**

- Release is still possible if investigation clears the batch.
- Batch Rejected becomes more plausible if a critical defect is confirmed, especially with sterile-product contamination or excursion-related quality impact.
- Central severe-tail window: **Thu 24 Sep to Tue 29 Sep**.
- Extreme tail: beyond **Wed 30 Sep**.
