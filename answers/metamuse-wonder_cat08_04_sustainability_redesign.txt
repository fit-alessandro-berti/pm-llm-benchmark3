## 1. System boundary and assumptions

**Inside the boundary - order-fulfilment lifecycle in the log:**
Receive order -> Availability check -> Pick from stock / Plan production / Emergency production -> Batch wait -> CNC production -> Heat treatment -> Inspection -> Rework production -> Package standard / reinforced -> Rework packaging -> Ship ground / air / Dedicated van / Consolidated truck -> Confirm delivery -> Close, plus Partial shipment, Backorder wait, and Cancel due to delay.

Environmental scope is operational, per-order and per-item where `batch_id / batch_size` permits allocation:
- Production: `energy_kwh` by `machine_group` (CNC_A, CNC_B, heat_treat, inspection_cell), `material_scrap_kg`, rework loops
- Fulfilment: `package_material_kg`, packaging rework
- Transport: `transport_kgco2e` by `route_mode` (parcel_ground, parcel_air, dedicated_van, consolidated_truck)
- Waste / ineffectiveness: scrap from rework, obsolescence of batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days (6%), damage returns linked to packaging, second shipments from partial shipments, cancellations after resources consumed

Stratified throughout by `case_type` (Standard-stock, Made-to-order, Critical-downtime), `customer_type` (Regular, Service-contract), `promised_lead_time_hours`, `expedite_flag`, `rework_flag`.

**Outside the boundary / not claimed:**
Upstream raw-material extraction, machine manufacture, customer use-phase and end-of-life of spare parts, carrier fleet lifecycle, energy grid mix behind `energy_kwh`, and returns transport not captured in the log. Do not convert kWh to CO2e or sum kgCO2e + kg scrap into a single score without explicit factors - none are given.

**Assumptions and uncertainty:**
- Log is flattened by order, not line item: partial shipment (1,010 cases) and multi-item packaging/transport allocation is approximate.
- Discovered model precision is low because emergency paths and partial shipments combine in many ways during disruptions; variant-level, not model-level, analysis is safer.
- `transport_kgco2e` and `energy_kwh` are observed attributes, assumed comparable within a `route_mode` / `machine_group`, not audited LCA values.
- Late risk for SP1 - ground/consolidated feasible if met with probability >85% - must be estimated by segment and slack, not average cycle time. Median vs p90 matters: e.g. Made-to-order Regular 94h median / 180h p90, 18% late.
- No intervention is assumed service-neutral by default; Critical-downtime and Service-contract resilience policy R1 constrains all transport changes.

## 2. Sustainability-related process indicators

Monitor monthly and per segment / variant, median and p90, not only averages:

**Energy**
- `energy_kwh` / order and / shipped item, by `machine_group`, `batch_size` band (1-3, 4-11, 12-20, >20), and path. Benchmark: CNC_A 2.8 kWh/item at 1-3 to 1.3 at 12-20.
- Heat-treatment kWh/item above/below batch size 10; share of energy consumed by cases later cancelled or reworked.

**Transport**
- `transport_kgco2e` / order and / on-time delivered order, by `route_mode`. Benchmarks: stock-ground 3.6, stock-air 18.5, Critical dedicated van variant avg 52.0 within Critical segment avg 38.0.
- Air share and dedicated-van share by segment and slack to `promised_lead_time_hours`; consolidated_truck share for non-urgent stock.
- Shipments per order - captures partial shipment + backorder second shipment (variant: 7.2 kgCO2e, 79h, 24% late).

**Materials**
- `material_scrap_kg` / order and / good item, esp. MTO rework variant 1.8 kg vs 0.70 kg efficient MTO; `package_material_kg` / order by standard vs reinforced.
- Obsolescence rate: batch-produced items not shipped in 14 days; scrap + package kg on cancelled / returned cases.

**Rework and quality**
- `rework_flag` rate, Inspection -> Rework production loops (470, with 390 returning to CNC), Rework packaging loops (620 entries), by `machine_group` x part weight / geometry. CNC_B 18% vs CNC_A 9% rework.
- Q1 compliance: MTO >8kg without reinforced pack and without low-risk certification; damage-return rate by packaging choice - reinforced reduces damage returns ~40% for heavy MTO but doubles material.

**Batching**
- Batch wait duration, `batch_size` distribution, wait-to-processing ratio, late-delivery rate by wait band. CNC_A wait 4h at small batch to 38h at 12-20; heat-batch waiters 1.7x late risk.

**Service**
- Cycle time median/p90, late-delivery % vs `promised_lead_time_hours`, Cancel due to delay rate (530/12,000), by segment in the case-mix table. Guardrails e.g. Standard-stock Regular 7% late, Critical Service-contract 4% late - must not regress materially.

**Resilience**
- Share of Service-contract orders with a viable alternative route (R1), dedicated-van justification completeness, route concentration, expedite rate during disruption periods, emergency production share (760 from Availability check).

All sustainability KPIs reported *per successfully delivered, non-returned order* to avoid rewarding cancellations, partial shipments, or damage returns.

## 3. Intervention options

**S1+S4. Green shipping recommender + priority pick/ground instead of automatic air for Standard-stock Service-contract**
- Target: Stock -> standard pack -> air (5%, 18.5 kgCO2e, 17h); SP1 violated in 22% of Standard-stock Service-contract cases. This segment is 16% of orders, 18h median, but 7.8 kgCO2e vs 4.2 for Regular.
- Mechanism: At Ship decision, compute slack to `promised_lead_time_hours` and route reliability; if ground/consolidated meets promise with >85% probability (SP1), use priority pick + ground. Air retained when slack insufficient.
- Service/quality/resilience: Small cycle increase for switched cases, late risk controlled by 85% gate; no quality effect; keep air as R1 fallback, do not remove it.
- Evidence: `route_mode, expedite_flag, promised_lead_time_hours`, SP1 conformance, late % and p90 by slack band, air override rate.

**S5. Consolidated truck twice daily for non-urgent stock orders**
- Target: Package standard -> Ship ground 5,100; stock-ground variant 42%.
- Mechanism: 35-55% lower `transport_kgco2e` vs individual ground parcels per prompt evidence.
- Effect: Adds 12-24h consolidation wait; therefore only where slack allows. Risk of bunching missing departure; twice daily limits wait. Preserves ground/air alternatives for R1.
- Evidence: Consolidated share, wait at Ship, late %, transport/order, missed-departure upgrades to air.

**S2. Dynamic batching for CNC and heat treatment with max wait caps by segment**
- Target: Plan production -> Batch wait 3,020 -> CNC 2,910; MTO batch variant 13%, 118h, 21% late; heat treatment 1,820.
- Mechanism: Batch towards 12-20 on CNC_A / >10 for heat to approach 1.3 kWh/item, but cap wait by segment/slack - e.g. release earlier for Service-contract / low-slack, wait longer for Regular with slack - and forecast-aware batch sizing to cut 6% obsolescence.
- Effect: Energy/item down, cycle and late risk up if caps are loose. Caps trade a small energy penalty for service protection. Quality neutral if inspection not rushed.
- Evidence: `batch_size, batch_id`, Batch wait duration, kWh/item, 14-day non-shipment rate, late % by batch band.

**S3. Shift MTO from CNC_B to CNC_A when queue and due-date slack allow**
- Target: MTO -> batch wait -> CNC_B -> inspection -> rework (5%, 32.0 kWh, 1.8 kg scrap, 32% late, 160h).
- Mechanism: Avoid CNC_B's 18% vs 9% rework; each avoided Rework production -> CNC loop saves repeat energy, scrap, inspection, and packaging.
- Effect: Often largest scrap/energy saving per affected case, but CNC_A queue can grow and create lateness / temptation to expedite by air later. Gate on queue + slack, not blanket shift.
- Evidence: `machine_group`, queue wait, `rework_flag`, scrap/order, downstream Ship air rate for shifted cases.

**S7. Inspection quality gate for high-rework machine/part combinations**
- Target: Inspection -> Rework production 470; inspection_cell queues; rushed inspection missing defects causing packaging/customer returns.
- Mechanism: Risk-based extra inspection for CNC_B output, heavy/complex parts, prior rework; root-cause feedback to CNC parameters.
- Effect: Adds inspection energy/time upfront, reduces hidden material waste, rework loops, returns, and emergency reshipments. If applied to all, becomes a bottleneck; apply selectively.
- Evidence: Rework loops per `machine_group` x part, defects found at Inspection vs at Package/Customer, inspection wait, scrap per good item.

**S6. Packaging-route compatibility check before shipment, respecting Q1**
- Target: Package standard/reinforced -> Rework packaging (260+360); Rework packaging -> re-pack 550.
- Mechanism: Pre-pack rule checking part geometry/weight vs packaging vs `route_mode`; enforce Q1: >8kg MTO reinforced unless inspection certifies low damage risk.
- Effect: Prevents double packaging material and repacking time. Must not be used to downgrade heavy parts to standard packing - reinforced halves material efficiency but cuts damage returns ~40% there. Light, low-risk parts can safely avoid reinforced.
- Evidence: `package_material_kg`, rework packaging rate, Q1 violations, damage-return rate by weight band and pack type.

**S8+S9. Split Critical-downtime and shared emergency courier lanes**
- Target: Critical -> emergency -> reinforced -> dedicated van (8%, 52.0 kgCO2e, 14h, 3% late); SP2 justification missing in 31% of van cases; Critical stock/ground exception (3%, 4.8 kgCO2e, 23h, 9% late) shows not all are equal.
- Mechanism: Triage at Receive/Availability into true downtime (van/air justified, fast decision) vs precautionary urgent (shared courier, consolidated emergency lane, or ground with priority handling if slack allows). Shared lanes reduce empty dedicated returns.
- Effect: Protects true downtime service - do not force ground there. Precautionary cases accept modest cycle increase. Shared lane pooling is a resilience gain if multiple carriers/lanes retained, a risk if it becomes sole route.
- Evidence: Category at triage, justification event, `route_mode, transport_kgco2e`, late/downtime outcome by category, empty/pooled utilisation if logged, mis-triage rate.

**S10+S12. Carbon-aware promise-date negotiation for Regular MTO + sustainability exception log**
- Target: MTO Regular (22%, 94h/180h, 18% late) and immediate-CNC + air variant (6%, 24 kWh, 23 kgCO2e).
- Mechanism: At Receive order, offer longer `promised_lead_time_hours` option enabling batching + ground instead of immediate CNC + air. Log every SP1/SP2/Q1 override with reason in exception log (S12).
- Effect: Enables S2/S1 without covert service deterioration because promise is explicit. Risk: longer promises lost to competitors / cancellations if pushed indiscriminately; keep optional and Regular-only initially.
- Evidence: Acceptance rate, promise change, Cancel due to delay, batch/air avoided, late vs revised promise.

**S11. Reduce partial shipments by changing availability-check rules**
- Target: Pick -> Partial shipment 1,010 -> Package 790 / Backorder wait 220 -> re-Pick 160 / Cancel 60; variant 79h, 24% late, double transport.
- Mechanism: Hold-and-complete when remainder ETA is short and slack allows, split only when first item is critically needed or remainder ETA long; improve stock accuracy at Availability check.
- Effect: One shipment instead of two, less packaging/contact. Can delay first item if rule is rigid - segment by criticality.
- Evidence: Shipments/order, Backorder wait, cancellation, cycle for held vs split, customer expedites after hold.

## 5. Rebound and displacement risks

1. **Batching rebound - obsolescence and expedite displacement:** Larger CNC/heat batches save kWh/item but wait rises 4h to 38h and forecast error leaves 6% not shipped in 14 days - embodied energy/scrap without a delivery. Late batch cases may then be upgraded to Ship air or Emergency production, wiping out production savings with transport/emergency energy.
2. **Transport rebound - consolidation/ground causing late-driven air:** Consolidated truck saves 35-55% but 12-24h wait, or ground instead of air, can miss a departure/promise and trigger a second expedited shipment, partial shipment, or Cancel due to delay after picking/packing resources are already spent. Net kgCO2e per *delivered* order can rise.
3. **Packaging displacement:** Cutting reinforced packaging saves `package_material_kg` but violates Q1 logic for >8kg MTO: damage returns rise - reinforced already cuts them ~40% - generating return transport, scrap, rework production and reshipment far larger than packaging saved. Conversely blanket reinforced packing doubles material on low-risk light parts.
4. **Rework / quality displacement:** Shifting all CNC_B work to CNC_A, or adding universal inspection gates, creates CNC_A / inspection_cell queues. Rushed inspection/handling then misses defects, defects surface at Package or customer, creating Rework packaging, Rework production -> CNC loops and returns - impact moves downstream and becomes less visible in `material_scrap_kg` at first inspection.
5. **Obsolescence and stock displacement from S11/S2:** Holding partial orders or building larger batches/forecast stock reduces shipments in the short term but increases inventory obsolescence and Backorder wait cancellations (60 of 220) if stock/ETA data is wrong. Saving is counted at Ship, waste appears later outside a narrow transport KPI.
6. **Customer behaviour rebound:** Carbon-aware longer promises (S10), ground downgrades, or held partial shipments perceived as service loss can cause Regular customers to split orders, order precautionary duplicates, demand `expedite_flag`, or classify orders as Critical-downtime / precautionary urgent to regain vans/air. Order inflation increases total cases, picking, packaging and transport even if per-order impact falls.
7. **Critical reclassification and resilience displacement:** S9 mis-triaging true downtime to shared/ground lanes repeats the 9% late, 23h exception path vs 3% late van path and can extend customer downtime. Removing dedicated-van / air capacity to book savings also breaks R1 during carrier disruption - low precision emergency variants proliferate, with combined partial + emergency + air paths.
8. **Machine/route concentration:** CNC_A-only or consolidated-only policies improve average efficiency but reduce alternative capacity. A CNC_A outage or truck departure failure forces Emergency production, CNC_B rework-prone overflow, and dedicated vans at peak impact.

## 6. Balanced recommendation

Recommend a gated, segment-differentiated package - not blanket bans on air, vans, or small batches:

**Package A - transport by slack, protect the critical core (S1, S4, S5, S8, S9, S12):**
Implement green shipping recommender enforcing SP1 (>85% on-time probability) and SP2 justification. Default Standard-stock Service-contract to priority pick + ground/consolidated, air only on insufficient slack - targeting the 22% SP1 violations. Consolidate non-urgent stock twice daily with ground/air fallback for R1. Split Critical-downtime at intake: true downtime keeps Emergency production -> reinforced -> dedicated van/air unimpeded; precautionary urgent uses shared emergency lanes / priority ground. Every override logged (S12). This takes the largest transport deltas (18.5 vs 3.6; 52.0 van) where evidence shows slack exists, while explicitly *not* applying the lowest-emission stock/ground exception to true downtime.

**Package B - produce right-first-time, batch within time caps (S2, S3, S7, S6):**
Dynamic batching to CNC_A 12-20 / heat >10 only within segment wait caps and forecast checks; shift CNC_B->CNC_A gated by queue + slack, retaining CNC_B as overflow; risk-based inspection gate for CNC_B and high-rework parts; pre-pack compatibility + Q1 enforcement. This attacks the dominant MTO burden - 18.5-20.0 kWh, 0.85-0.90 kg scrap, 10-11% rework, rework variant 32 kWh / 1.8 kg - without trading packaging material for damage returns.

**Package C - fewer ineffective orders (S10, S11):**
Optional carbon-aware promise for Regular MTO only, and partial-shipment hold/split rules by remainder ETA and criticality. This reduces system volume of second shipments, immediate-CNC + air variants, and cancellations, rather than optimising impact per shipment while shipments multiply.

Why balanced: environmental gains come from the three evidenced hotspots - unjustified air/van, rework loops, and inefficient small/rushed batches - each gated by `promised_lead_time_hours`, segment, and queue. Service is protected by slack thresholds, wait caps, and true-downtime exemption; quality by Q1 and inspection gates; resilience by retaining air, van, CNC_B, and ground as documented fallbacks under R1, avoiding single-route/machine concentration.

Sequence: A quick-win + S12 logging first (no physical capacity change), then S6/S7, then S2/S3 with caps, then S9/S10 with customer communication. Pilot by segment before scaling.

## 7. Validation and governance plan

**Event-log validation design:**
- Re-mine DFG and variants after implementation, stratified by `case_type x customer_type x route_mode x machine_group x batch_size` band. Compare pre/post median and p90 cycle time, late %, Cancel due to delay, and all Section 2 indicators *per delivered, non-returned order*. Use pilot vs non-pilot segments / phased rollout where possible; account for case-mix shift - e.g. more precautionary orders reclassified as Critical would falsely worsen Critical averages.
- Success requires joint movement: e.g. air share and `transport_kgco2e` down in Standard-stock Service-contract **with** late % and p90 not materially above 5% / 41h baseline; MTO scrap and kWh/good item down **with** Batch wait, obsolescence, and 21-32% late variants not rising; Critical true-downtime late stays near 4% segment / 3% van variant.
- Monitor rebound KPIs explicitly: upgrades ground->air after consolidation miss, Emergency production rate, shipments/order, 14-day obsolescence, damage returns, expedite_flag rate, order-splitting / Critical reclassification rate per customer.

**Conformance monitoring:**
- SP1: automated check at Ship - ground/consolidated chosen or, if air/van chosen, logged slack calculation showing <85% ground feasibility. Track violation rate from 22% baseline in Standard-stock Service-contract.
- SP2: dedicated van without Critical justification event = violation; track from 31% missing baseline, by true vs precautionary category, with mis-triage audits.
- Q1: >8kg MTO without reinforced pack and without inspection low-risk certificate = violation; correlate with damage returns/rework as before.
- R1: periodic check that each Service-contract segment retains at least one tested alternative route; log carrier-disruption episodes separately rather than judging green rules on disruption weeks.
- New rules: batch wait cap breaches, CNC_B->A gate breaches, packaging compatibility check skipped.

**Exception governance (S12):**
Sustainability exception log mandatory for SP1/SP2/Q1 overrides: case, segment, slack/justification, approver, outcome (late, rework, return). Review weekly operationally, monthly for policy: recurring override reasons become rule changes, not individual blame. Set stop/rollback triggers in advance - e.g. segment late %, p90, rework, or Critical late breaching agreed guardrail for two periods, or rebound air-upgrades offsetting consolidated savings - pause that intervention for that segment and revert to fallback route/machine.

**Data quality to close:**
Add line-item ID, return/damage event, van utilisation/empty-return flag, inspection outcome reason, and batch-item ship date to resolve flattening, obsolescence, and return attribution uncertainty before claiming lifecycle totals.
