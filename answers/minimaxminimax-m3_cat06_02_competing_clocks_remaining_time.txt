## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

**Calendar reference:** Friday 18 Sep 2026 15:30 = t0. Mon 21 Sep 08:00 = t0 + ~64.5 h.

### S1 — Smooth path

- **Chemistry waiting + service**
  - Analysts' remaining work: best case both finish by 17:00 (1.6 h from t0); worst case one finishes 17:24, the other 17:54. Assume the queueing residual of 4 samples (1 urgent + 3 ordinary) is partially absorbed by the finishing analysts.
  - Probability B-417 starts by 16:30  0.55 (priority lane helps); by 17:30  0.85; by 18:00  0.97.
  - Service time: triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5)  mean  2.67 h, P80  3.4 h.
  - If start  ~15:30 (impossible) + 4.5 h  18:00; practically any start after 13:30 needs the work to continue Mon if > 2.5 h remain at 18:00.
  - Central: starts ~16:30–17:00, finishes 18:30–20:30 of the same day OR pauses and finishes Mon ~09:30–11:00. Use Mon 10:00 as central finish.
- **Microbiology incubation + readout**
  - Incubation window: 46–54 h from Fri 14:00  ready Sat 12:00 – Sun 20:00.
  - With p  0.35 no weekend authorization  readout Mon 08:00 onward; with p  0.65 authorized  readout Sat 12:00 – Sun 23:00 (1–3 h dispatch+readout).
  - Central assumption: no on-call  Mon 08:00 ready, readout + recording 0.5–1.5 h, done by Mon ~09:30.
- **Parallel join time = max(chem, micro)**
  - Chem central finish Mon 10:00; micro central Mon 09:30. Max  Mon 10:00.
- **QA waiting + service**
  - Future queue at Mon 10:00: 5 current + ~ (64.5 h  2.5 h lab closing overnight) × 0.35/h  21 expected arrivals; with overdispersion, P50  22, P80  30. With 2 reviewers from Mon 08:00, capacity  10–12 batches/day. Wait P50  2 days, P80  3 days.
  - Service time: median 1.5 h, P80 3.5 h.
  - QA finishes: Mon 10:00 + ~2 d wait + 1.5–3.5 h service  Wed 22 Sep 13:00 – Thu 23 Sep 17:00.
- **Optional rework**: none (smooth).
- **Final release**: 0.25–0.75 h, available continuously. Add 0.5 h.
- **Terminal time**: **Tue 22 Sep ~13:00 – Wed 23 Sep ~17:00** central; tail Wed eve – Thu.

**Maximum vs sum:** microbiology incubation forces a fixed ~46–54 h calendar delay; chemistry is ~0.5–6 h of active work, so the join  max(T_chem, T_micro)  T_micro + small adjustment.

### S2 — Chemistry rerun

### S4 — QA query or investigation tail

- **Terminal**: Wed 23 Sep – Fri 25 Sep (doc query / doc-only inv); Mon 28 Sep – Wed 30 Sep (lab inv).

- Rejection recording 0.5–1.0 h.
- **Terminal**: Mon 28 Sep – Mon 5 Oct.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Microbiology weekend staffing authorization (p  0.25–0.45)** — *calendar / observation uncertainty*. A 0.20 swing in authorization probability moves P50 by roughly 16–24 h (a full working day).
2. **Future QA queue depth at the join time** — *queue uncertainty*. With overdispersion, the P50 wait can be 1.5–3 days; a 50% miss on arrival rate moves P50 by ~0.5–1.0 day and P80 by 1.5 days.
3. **Temperature-excursion flag confirmation (unknown, p  0.30)** — *dependence / path uncertainty*. It simultaneously raises chemistry rerun odds (0.150.25) and microbiology contamination odds (0.0850.13), creating correlated adverse paths; tail mass shifts by 1–3 calendar days.
4. **Chemistry queue discipline (FIFO vs risk-priority) and analyst availability** — *queue uncertainty*. Determines whether B-417 starts by 16:30 (smooth Fri finish) or is paused to Monday; a single-hour shift can move the join by 12–16 h.
5. **Investigation type mix (documentation-only vs laboratory)** — *path uncertainty*. A laboratory investigation adds 3–7 calendar days vs. 1–2.5 working days for documentation-only, and is the single largest tail-driver in the upper deciles.

(Honorable mentions: rerun probability, contamination confirmation probability, QA documentation query.)
