## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

**Facts.** GPIP-4 was board-approved and applied throughout the incident. Aachen's controlled SOP gives corporate incident directives limited precedence. The COO and Incident Commander signed ECD-2. The Chief Risk Officer (CRO) was unavailable and did not ratify it. The CFO can interpret Finance controls. The Incident Command charter establishes operational authority and leaves budget-owner status unstated.

**Primary interpretation.** Give precedence by issue. ECD-2 is a valid temporary override of local PO sequencing and supplier-register eligibility for covered commitments. B7 supplies the local basis, and C1, C2 and C4 expressly address those controls. GPIP-4's segregation, role-specific approvals and Compliance gates continue to apply. A7 identifies the CRO as the exception authority. ECD-2's signatures, silence on those gates and the prior practice of accepting incident overlays supply insufficient grounds to treat it as an A7 exception. Specificity and recency resolve overlap within the issuer's authority. Emergency necessity supports use of an applicable exception and affects remediation priority.

**Scope and timing assumptions.** Read the SOP as covering site maintenance, including repair of a site asset, as well as production support. Read ECD-2 as covering lots with a demonstrated operational link to containing the current incident. Backup equipment can qualify when a credible failure or stockout scenario makes it critical during containment. Neither the “critical contingency” label nor a universal 24-hour cutoff settles that question. Assess scope and the directive window when the company becomes bound to the supplier. A timely commitment can carry later PO and recording obligations past expiry. Infer July 14 for P-502 from the outage context. Treat B2 as requiring both named approvals without an express pre-commitment deadline. Where A2 applies, it supplies a separate pre-commitment requirement. Apply B6 to use or claimed use of the local emergency process, including where an ECD purchase also follows that process. C3 and B6 have distinct purposes and deadlines.

**Evidence and classification.** Evaluate contemporaneous evidence for contents, provenance, actor authority and clock accuracy. An ERP timestamp can represent occurrence, transcription or synchronization. A timely act under another activity label can satisfy a role-specific approval if the actor exercised the required authority and approved the particular purchase. The party relying on an exception must support its authority, scope, timing and conditions. Routine role-coded events support the stated action, subject to actor validation. An established sequence failure or an inapplicable exception to an established departure warrants **NONCOMPLIANT**. Use **INDETERMINATE** where compliance turns on a substantive act or condition and the record supplies insufficient evidence either way. Use **JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION** for a substantiated, permitted departure with no established breach and **COMPLIANT** for established conformance on the ordinary path. Severity reflects control exposure. Confidence reflects evidence for the disposition, including confidence that a gap remains unresolved.

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

- **P-501 → NONCOMPLIANT.** Read B2 as an implied commitment gate. Plant and Finance approval protects against an unauthorized obligation only if meaningful review takes place before it. On that reading B4 excuses the PO sequence and allows a qualified substitute, but both approvals here came later. The protective purpose supports the implication. B2's silence on sequence and the explicit timing in B1 and A2 weigh against it. The documented Plant approval is late on this reading regardless of whether SHIFT-1 was eligible to substitute.
- **P-504 → INDETERMINATE.** Read “maintenance and production-support purchases” as manufacturing maintenance and production support, excluding ancillary facilities like the cafeteria. The demonstrated B1 and B3 failures then drop out. ECD-2 remains inapplicable, and GPIP-4 compliance depends in part on supplier history and any A3 clearance. The manufacturing context supports the narrower category. The unqualified reference to maintenance supports the primary reading. Unapproved-register status leaves newness unresolved.
- **P-505 → INDETERMINATE.** A broader emergency-overlay reading treats timely joint authorization as substantive business and Finance approval up to EUR 150,000, supported by D1 and past acceptance of signed overlays. Coupled with commitment before 18:00, that reading treats 19:00 Finance as subsequent confirmation and removes the demonstrated A2 sequence failure. Role segregation and timely C3 recording would still need evidence. This requires reading the COO and CFO's combined emergency authority as sufficient to substitute for A2's named roles and reading B7 to reach local approval steps. A7's reservation, the missing budget-owner authority and D4 make it weaker than the primary reading.
- **P-507 → NONCOMPLIANT.** Read C3 recording as controlled filing, with the signed PDF treated as a finished document awaiting filing. ERP appearance at 14:30 then puts the first qualifying record after the 12:55 deadline. This is credible if a controlling procedure designates the system as the official record and provides no queue-based filing route. C3 specifies no medium, and a verifiably signed PDF in a contemporaneous queue supports the primary reading.
- **P-508 → NONCOMPLIANT.** Read B3 as substantive supplier qualification beyond the ordinary requirements that B7 lets a directive supersede. B3 then independently bars the commitment even with Compliance and Legal clearance. B3's categorical eligibility requirement supports distinguishing qualification from onboarding administration. The express language of C2 and C4 and the SOP's invitation to incident directives support the primary override. A different, permissive reading of D2 treats “as well” as extending D1's joint-approval substitution above EUR 150,000 when Legal concurs. Even that reading needs authority to reach A2 and B2 and timely C3 recording before it can support JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.

For P-503, accepting the Operations view that this backup lot was critical would remove the demonstrated PO sequence breach if C3 was satisfied. It would still leave serious concerns about the missing Board-control trail and PROC-3's role. On a functional reading of A1, an indispensable vote is a final approval if the vote jointly releases the purchase. A merely advisory vote followed by a documented, independent final decision would lead to a different A1 finding.

Keep separate findings for unverified controls. Document subsequent approvals at their real times and assess their mitigating effect. Retain the original sequence in the audit trail.
- **P-501 and P-504.** Preserve P-501's engineering forecast, form and approval identities. Confirm that an authorized person performed the Plant approval and that the final approval was independent. Correct P-504's emergency designation, confirm supplier history and assess supplier eligibility and any required clearance before further use. Preserve the form and establish its recording time.
- **P-502, P-503 and P-506.** Reconcile underlying approval records and delegations against each binding commitment. For P-502, review the late new-supplier clearance against any product received or used and make any necessary qualification or traceability decisions. For P-503, establish the contingency risk, PROC-3's decision role and whether qualifying clearances existed. For P-506, investigate the purported extension and validate Controller and budget-owner authority. Have the responsible control owners review continuing supplier obligations and subsequent releases.
- **P-505.** Preserve the full supplier acknowledgment and call record, ERP audit history and independent clock references. Determine when agreement became binding and when the joint authorization was first recorded. Resolve the A2 approval trail regardless of where commitment falls relative to 18:00.
- **P-507 and P-508.** Authenticate P-507's signed PDF and its queue history. A timely original is evidence of a timely record. Delayed ERP ingestion is a logging defect if the system obscures that original time. Preserve both timestamps. Establish the time of P-508's first C3 record. Verify who exercised budget, Finance and Plant authority in both cases, what they approved and when. A later-produced record can corroborate a timely act if its provenance supports the claimed time. - **Monitoring.** Capture binding commitment time and its source, approval occurrence and entry times, effective role assignments, supplier newness and register status, and the directive ID and expiry. Check amount-based gates at release and alert on missing approvals, overdue C3 or B6 records, and post-expiry commitments. Review repeated exceptions and affected goods or supplier exposure with Procurement, Finance, Compliance and Quality.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

> **Emergency procurement authority.** A site emergency permits departures defined in the controlled SOP, including early commitment relative to PO creation when its emergency test is met. A signed corporate incident directive may vary only the local requirements it names and that the controlled SOP authorizes it to vary. It must identify the incident, sites, covered lots or objective criticality criteria, effective times and required evidence. A departure from GPIP-4 requires a written CRO exception operative before commitment, identifying the clause, transactions or class, expiry and compensating controls. This authority requires the stated written instrument, including during a continuity incident.
>
> **Approval and supplier gates.** Each final business approver, including each indispensable joint final approver, must be distinct from the requester and committer. Unless expressly varied by a valid exception, Aachen purchases of EUR 25,000 or more require Plant and Finance approval before commitment, and all purchases of EUR 50,000 or more require prior independent budget-owner and Finance Controller approval. Purchases above EUR 100,000 or from any new supplier require prior Compliance clearance. A directive permitting an unregistered supplier varies local register eligibility only. Directive purchases above EUR 150,000 also require prior Legal clearance. Joint Incident Command approval counts toward a named role only if that authorizer holds and exercises the role and meets its independence requirement.
>
> **Records, expiry and review.** Two distinct Incident Command members must authorize before binding commitment. Record authorization within four hours of the authorization and complete any applicable site emergency form within 24 hours of commitment. A traceable signed offline record satisfies timely recording when it captures the actors, approvals and actual times. Upload it at recovery with both original and ingestion timestamps preserved. Treat later approvals as mitigation and retain original compliance findings. The directive ends at its stated cutoff, with the end time exclusive. Any extension requires fresh written authorization before expiry by the authority for each affected control. Accrued PO and documentation duties survive expiry. Review every directive after closeout, including repeated use by category, and require a fresh necessity finding for any renewal.
