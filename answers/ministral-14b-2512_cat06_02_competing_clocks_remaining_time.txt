   - **Rerun risk:** 15%  ignored in smooth path.

2. **Microbiology Incubation/Readout:**

   - **Readout:** Requires microbiologist. Weekend on-call probability = 0.35  **50% chance of Monday 08:00 readout**, 50% chance of **Monday 08:00 + 0.5–1.5 h**.

3. **Parallel Join:**
   - Chemistry finishes **Monday 10:30**, microbiology by **Monday 10:00**  **join at Monday 10:30**.

4. **QA Waiting/Service:**

5. **Terminal Event:**
