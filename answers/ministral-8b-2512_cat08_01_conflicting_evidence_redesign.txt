# **Consumer Electronics Warranty Process Redesign**

---

## **1. Current-Process Diagnosis**

### **Key Performance Problems**
1. **Speed vs. Quality Trade-off**:
   - The **fast path (Auto eligibility  Fast approval  Dispatch replacement)** is the most frequent (2,550/5,000 cases) and fastest (median **0.4h** for auto screen + **1.8h** dispatch), but leads to **higher rework (14.1% complaint/reopen rate)** and **compliance violations (e.g., R2 violations for high-value devices)**.
   - The **manual/reviewed path** is slower (median **5–15h** for eligibility + **16–60h** for repair quotes) but has **lower rework (3.3–6% complaint/reopen)** and **better compliance adherence**.

2. **Compliance and Risk Gaps**:
   - **R1 violations**: High-value claims (>EUR 400) often bypass manual review or fraud checks, increasing fraud risk.
   - **R2 violations**: **23% of Fast approvals** dispatch replacements without evidence completeness (per alignment diagnostics).
   - **R3 violations**: **31% of reopened claims** lack manual eligibility review before final resolution.

3. **Customer-Specific Pain Points**:
   - **Premium customers** tolerate speed but **abandon if cycle time >24h** (high service-credit exposure).
   - **High-value devices** suffer from **wrong replacement errors** (EUR 410 cost) due to rushed dispatch.
   - **Unclear defect/missing evidence cases (22%)** have a **median 140h cycle time** and **high abandonment**.

4. **Resource Bottlenecks**:
   - **Fraud/risk review** is a bottleneck (2 specialists, EUR 52/hour) but only handles **400+ cases/month** (low capacity relative to fraud risk).
   - **Repair quote** delays (median **21h**) are caused by **part-price confirmation waits**, not labor.
   - **Quality check** is underutilized but critical for catching **rework (12.5% of high-value repairs)**.

5. **Data-Driven Contradictions**:
   - **Fast approval is 31% cheaper pre-rework but only 9% cheaper post-rework** (due to complaints/reshipments).
   - **Manual paths reduce rework by ~70% for high-value devices** but increase cycle time by **5–10x**.
   - **Premium low-risk accessories** have **lower complaint rates on manual paths (3% vs. 6%)** despite longer cycle times.

---
### **Process-Mining Evidence Supporting Diagnosis**
1. **Frequency DFG**:
   - **Fast approval  Dispatch replacement (2,300 cases)** dominates but has **260 complaints** (11.3% of this path).
   - **Manual eligibility  Repair quote (260 cases)** has **only 80 complaints (3.1%)**, proving better quality but at higher cost.

2. **Performance DFG**:
   - **Fast approval  Customer complaint/reopen (median 50h)** is a **long-tail disaster** (p90=150h), far worse than manual paths.
   - **Dispatch replacement  Quality/compliance close (median 3.2h)** vs. **Repair device  Quality check (median 6.5h)** shows repair paths are slower but more controlled.

3. **Conformance Findings**:
   - **Log fitness = 0.91, 68% fitting traces**  **32% of cases violate normative rules**, mostly due to **Fast approval before evidence completeness**.
   - **Precision is low** because **auto-screen rules are outdated** (e.g., don’t distinguish Premium vs. Standard).

4. **Rework Statistics**:
   - **26% of "Request missing evidence" cases loop twice**  **digital portal could reduce this by 30–45%** but may increase abandonment for low-literacy users.
   - **Rework repair rate is 12.5% for high-value devices**  **quality gate before dispatch could catch 60–80% of these**.

5. **Customer Segment Outcomes**:
   - **Premium high-value devices** have **22% complaint rate on fast path vs. 5% on manual**  **speed is not worth the risk**.
   - **Standard low-risk accessories** tolerate **72h completion if status visibility is good**  **manual review may not be justified here**.

## **2. Redesign A: "Segment-Specific Tiered Approval"**
**Target**: Balance speed/cost for low-risk cases while enforcing manual review for high-risk/compliance-sensitive cases.

### **Redesigned Process Flow**
1. **Intake Claim  Segment Classification** (auto, rules-based):
   - **Premium High-Value Device**: **Mandatory Manual Eligibility + Fraud Review** (bypasses auto screen).
   - **Standard High-Value Device**: **Auto Screen  Fraud Review** (if risk score > threshold).
   - **Low-Risk Accessories (Premium/Standard)**: **Auto Screen  Fast Approval** (unless evidence missing).
   - **Unclear Defect/Missing Evidence**: **Auto Screen  Evidence Portal + Manual Escalation if unresolved**.

2. **Evidence Portal Integration**:
   - Replace **Request Missing Evidence** with a **self-service portal** (reduces loops by 30–45%).
   - **Premium customers** get **priority support** (dedicated chatbot).
   - **Low-literacy users** get **guided prompts** (reduces abandonment).

3. **Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate** (lightweight):
   - For **Fast Approval  Dispatch Replacement**, add a **6h automated check** (no human review) to verify:
     - Evidence completeness (R2 compliance).
     - Replacement part correctness (reduces EUR 410 errors).
   - **False positives** escalate to **Quality Check team (2 inspectors)**.

4. **Repair Path Optimization**:
   - **Repair Quote  Customer Approval** gets a **24h SLA** (current median is 21h).
   - **Repair Device  Quality Check** adds a **real-time defect log** to flag rework early.

### **Mechanism of Improvement**
- **Reduces compliance violations** by **enforcing R1/R2 for high-value cases**.
- **Cuts rework** by **catching 60–80% of wrong replacements** via pre-dispatch gate.
- **Improves Premium experience** by **guaranteeing <24h for low-risk cases**.
- **Lowers resource pressure** by **offloading evidence requests to portal** (reduces manual review workload).

### **What It Sacrifices/Leaves Unresolved**
- **Increases cycle time for Standard High-Value Devices** (now goes through fraud review).
- **Portal may increase abandonment for low-literacy users** (mitigated by guided prompts).
- **Quality gate adds 6–18h delay** (but reduces long-tail complaints).

---
## **3. Redesign B: "Dynamic Risk-Based Routing with Predictive Escalation"**
**Target**: Use **predictive modeling** to route claims based on **likelihood of fraud/rework**, not just segment.

### **Redesigned Process Flow**
1. **Intake Claim  Predictive Risk Score** (ML model trained on:
   - Defect type, segment, evidence completeness, historical rework rates).
   - **Low Risk (<10% rework probability)**: **Auto Screen  Fast Approval**.
   - **Medium Risk (10–30%)**: **Auto Screen  Fraud Review (if >EUR 200) or Evidence Portal**.
   - **High Risk (>30%)**: **Manual Eligibility + Fraud Review**.

2. **Evidence Portal with AI Guidance**:
   - **For Medium-Risk cases**, the portal **flags likely missing docs** (e.g., "Your invoice is incomplete—here’s a template").
   - **If unresolved after 2 attempts**, auto-escalates to **manual review**.

3. **Real-Time Complaint Prediction**:
   - After **Fast Approval**, monitor **customer behavior** (e.g., delayed returns, multiple complaints on similar claims).
   - If **complaint risk >15%**, **pause dispatch** and reroute to **Repair Quote path**.

4. **Repair Path with Parallel Review**:
   - **Repair Quote  Customer Approval** now has a **parallel fraud check** (2 specialists work in shifts).
   - **If approved**, dispatch with **pre-dispatch quality gate**.

### **Mechanism of Improvement**
- **Reduces false positives in auto approval** by **routing only low-risk cases fast**.
- **Catches fraud/rework early** via **predictive routing** (reduces EUR 410 replacement errors).
- **Lowers resource pressure** by **letting ML handle routing** (no need to manually review all high-value cases).

### **What It Sacrifices/Leaves Unresolved**
- **Requires ML model training** (2–4 weeks to deploy).
- **May misclassify some cases** (false positives/negatives in risk scoring).
- **Fraud review team still bottleneck** (but now only handles high-risk cases).

---
## **4. Redesign C: "Premium-Only Fast Track with Guaranteed Manual Review for High-Value"**
**Target**: **Premium customers get speed; Standard customers get cost control.**

### **Redesigned Process Flow**
1. **Segment-Based Intake**:
   - **Premium Claims**:
     - **Low-Risk Accessories**: **Auto Screen  Fast Approval  Dispatch (with 6h quality gate)**.
     - **High-Value Devices**: **Auto Screen  Fraud Review  Manual Eligibility  Repair Quote**.
   - **Standard Claims**:
     - **All cases  Manual Eligibility + Fraud Review** (unless <EUR 100, then auto).

2. **Evidence Portal with Tiered Support**:
   - **Premium**: **Priority chatbot + human override**.
   - **Standard**: **Basic portal + manual escalation if needed**.

3. **Repair Path Standardization**:
   - **All repair quotes** go through **parallel fraud review** (no more bottlenecks).
   - **Quality check** becomes **mandatory for all high-value repairs**.

4. **Complaint Handling**:
   - **Premium complaints** get **escalation board access**.
   - **Standard complaints** are **auto-routed to manual review**.

### **Mechanism of Improvement**
- **Premium customers get <24h for low-risk cases** (meets their expectations).
- **Standard customers avoid fast-path rework** (lower complaint rates).
- **Compliance risk drops** (R1/R2 always enforced for high-value).

### **What It Sacrifices/Leaves Unresolved**
- **Standard customers pay for slower processing** (but get better quality).
- **Manual review workload increases** (7 reviewers may need to hire 1 more).
- **High-value Premium cases are slower** (but complaint rate drops from 22%  5%).

## **6. Failure Modes and Displaced Bottlenecks**

### **Redesign A: Segment-Specific Tiered Approval**
- **Failure 1**: **Portal increases abandonment for low-literacy users**  Mitigation: **Add guided prompts + phone support**.
- **Failure 2**: **Quality gate adds 6–18h delay**  Mitigation: **Prioritize high-value cases first**.
- **Bottleneck Migration**:
  - **Original**: Fraud review bottleneck.
  - **New**: **Quality gate inspectors** (2 people) become bottleneck if wrong replacement rate rises.

### **Redesign B: Dynamic Risk-Based Routing**
- **Failure 1**: **ML model misclassifies risk**  Mitigation: **Start with conservative thresholds**.
- **Failure 2**: **Fraud review team still overwhelmed**  Mitigation: **Hire 1 more specialist**.
- **Bottleneck Migration**:
  - **Original**: Fraud review bottleneck.
  - **New**: **ML training data quality** (if model is wrong, rework increases).

### **Redesign C: Premium-Only Fast Track**
- **Failure 1**: **Manual review workload spikes**  Mitigation: **Hire 1 more reviewer**.
- **Failure 2**: **Premium customers still get complaints**  Mitigation: **Add real-time complaint prediction**.
- **Bottleneck Migration**:
  - **Original**: Fraud review bottleneck.
  - **New**: **Repair quote technicians** (if parallel fraud review slows them down).

## **7. Recommended Experiment Sequence**

### **Phase 1: Pilot "Segment-Specific Tiered Approval" (Redesign A)**
- **Target Segment**: **Premium Low-Risk Accessories (18% of cases)**.
- **Metrics to Monitor**:
  - **Cycle time**: Median <24h (vs. current 8h fast path).
  - **Complaint rate**: <6% (vs. current 6%).
  - **Portal abandonment**: <10% (vs. current 320 abandoned cases).
  - **Quality gate false positives**: <5% (escalated to manual review).
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - **Go if**: Complaint rate drops by **>20%** or portal reduces loops by **>30%**.
  - **Stop if**: Cycle time increases by **>30%** or manual review workload rises by **>20%**.

### **Phase 2: Expand to "Standard High-Value Devices"**
- **Target Segment**: **Standard High-Value Devices (21%)**.
- **Metrics**:
  - **Complaint rate**: <10% (vs. current 18%).
  - **Fraud review cycle time**: <12h (vs. current median 13h).
  - **Cost per claim**: <EUR 150 (vs. current EUR 180).
- **Stop/Go**:
  - **Go if**: Complaint rate drops by **>30%** or cost reduces by **>10%**.
  - **Stop if**: Manual review queue exceeds **85% utilization**.

### **Phase 3: Test "Predictive Risk Routing" (Redesign B)**
- **Target Segment**: **Unclear Defect Cases (22%)**.
- **Metrics**:
  - **Rework rate**: <8% (vs. current 12.5%).
  - **ML accuracy**: >85% (precision/recall).
  - **Fraud review cases reduced by**: >30%.
- **Stop/Go**:
  - **Go if**: Rework drops by **>25%** or fraud review cases drop by **>20%**.
  - **Stop if**: False negatives cause **>5% increase in complaints**.

### **Phase 4: Full Rollout of "Premium-Only Fast Track" (Redesign C)**
- **Target Segment**: **All Premium Customers (32%)**.
- **Metrics**:
  - **Premium complaint rate**: <5% (vs. current 6–22%).
  - **Standard complaint rate**: <8% (vs. current 8–18%).
  - **Manual review cost**: <EUR 200/claim (Standard).
- **Stop/Go**:
  - **Go if**: Premium satisfaction scores rise by **>15%** and Standard rework drops by **>20%**.
  - **Stop if**: Manual review team utilization exceeds **90%**.

---
### **How to Learn if Improvements Are Real (Not Shifted Downstream)**
1. **Track "Complaint Reopen" Rates**:
   - If **Redesign A** reduces complaints in Phase 1 but **Phase 2 sees a spike in repair rework**, the issue may be **poor quality gates**.
2. **Monitor "Quality Check" Failures**:
   - If **Redesign C** reduces complaints but **Quality Check rework rises**, the **pre-dispatch gate may be too lenient**.
3. **Compare "Abandoned Cases" Before/After Portal**:
   - If **Redesign A** reduces loops but **abandonments rise**, the **portal may be too complex**.
4. **Use "Control Charts" for Key Metrics**:
   - Plot **complaint rates, cycle times, and costs** over time to detect **shifted bottlenecks**.

---
### **Final Recommendation**
**Start with Redesign A (Segment-Specific Tiered Approval)** because:
- **Lowest risk** (no ML dependency).
- **Directly addresses Premium customer pain**.
- **Provides clear stop/go signals** before scaling.

**If successful, expand to Redesign B (Predictive Routing)** for **unclear defect cases**, then **Redesign C (Premium-Focused)** for full segmentation.

**Avoid Redesign C first** because it **increases manual review workload** without proving the case for Premium-only speed.
