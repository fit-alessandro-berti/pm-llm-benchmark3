The shared causal core is a join mechanism: Assessment cannot start until both verification branches finish. Removing an unnecessary precedence constraint can shorten the join time when both branches are ready, have capacity, and truly execute concurrently. The same mechanism can operate at B for no-original cases and, more narrowly, when Identity remains unfinished after originals arrive.

Site-specific mechanisms dominate elsewhere. At A, digital availability, cross-trained reviewers, simultaneous ownership, the registry upgrade, added document capacity, and queue huddles all supported or mimicked the treatment. At B, statutory originals gates, specialist queues, a case lock, two legal reviewers, and supplement loops can block overlap or move delay downstream.

The principal effect modifiers are `originals_required`, `digital_documents`, complexity, role specialization, the IT lock, and all four capacity variables. Legal-review status modifies final-cycle effects even if verification waiting improves. Timestamp reliability modifies the observed implementation measure rather than necessarily changing true work.

Thus, similar activity labels and an AND-join do not establish transportability. A control-flow diagram omits data-dependent eligibility, resource contention, queue policies, duration distributions, legal loops, and measurement error. The graph’s arrows are unsigned: for example, parallelization may reduce join waiting while simultaneously increasing queue load or handoff rework. Site B therefore needs mechanism-specific, subgroup-specific effects rather than a transported nine-hour estimate.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

The intervention should implement **safe partial-order release**, not universal simultaneous execution.

- **Eligibility rules**
  - At registration, record whether originals are legally required, expected legal-review status, channel, and complexity.
  - Phase 1 eligibility: no-original, nonlegal, low/moderate-complexity cases.
  - Phase 2: original-required cases, but only for concurrent Identity Check and Originals Receipt.
  - Legal and high-complexity cases enter only after downstream capacity and loop handling pass pilot gates.
- **Concurrent task rules**
  - No-original cases: create and execute Identity Check and Document Check concurrently after Register.
  - Original-required cases: execute Identity Check while awaiting Originals Receipt. A blocked document placeholder may be created, but it cannot be assigned or started until receipt is validated.
  - If Identity is still incomplete after originals arrive, Identity and Document Check may then execute concurrently.
- **Mandatory dependencies**
  - Document Check follows Originals Receipt whenever originals are required.
  - Assessment waits for completed Identity and Document Checks.
  - Applicable cases must complete Legal Review before Approve or Reject.
- **Queue and WIP controls**
  - Separate blocked, ready, active, and completed states.
  - Initial active-WIP ceilings: 16 identity tasks, 21 document tasks, 9 assessments, and 4 legal reviews; tune from observed utilization.
  - Blocked originals cases do not count as active WIP. Use pull queues and daily aging review held constant across pilot arms.
- **Responsibilities**
  - Specialist pools retain responsibility for their task type. The case coordinator resolves cross-pool discrepancies but does not silently complete another role’s task.
- **Legal and supplement loops**
  - A Request Supplement creates a versioned supplement round. After any required receipt, Document Check repeats, then Assessment repeats, then Legal Review resumes. Repeat Identity only if the supplement changes identity evidence.
- **Logging**
  - Record immutable task-created, legally-enabled, claimed, started, suspended, completed, and entered-at timestamps; resource and role; original requirement; document version; lock errors; API calls; supplement-round ID; and reason for any manual back-entry.

A compact POWL-style specification is:

```text
No-original verification =
    Register ; (Identity Check || Document Check) ; Assessment

Original-required verification =
    Register ; (Identity Check || (Originals Receipt ; Document Check)) ; Assessment

Post-assessment =
    XOR(
        nonlegal: Decision,
        legal:
            LOOP(
                Legal Review,
                Request Supplement ;
                Optional(Supplement Originals Receipt) ;
                Repeat Document Check ;
                Repeat Assessment
            ) ;
            Decision
    )

Decision = XOR(Approve, Reject)
Post-assessment ; Decision ; Notify
```

Equivalently, the essential order relations are `Register  Identity`, `Register  document-path`, `Originals Receipt  Document Check` when required, `Identity  Assessment`, `Document Check  Assessment`, `Assessment  Legal Review` when applicable, `Request Supplement  Repeat Document Check  Repeat Assessment  Legal Review`, and `Decision  Notify`.

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

| design element | specification | causal purpose | operational safeguard | process-mining measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unit of assignment | Case at registration in stage 1; queue-lane-by-week in a later system-load stage | Estimate direct case effects first, then equilibrium queue effects | Immutable assignment before any verification starts | Assignment event linked to case ID and lane |
| Randomization or phased assignment | Stratified 1:1:1 randomization to control, safe legacy scheduling, or safe scheduling with lock-safe dual ownership; best version then tested in randomized switchback weeks | Separates policy, IT implementation, and scale effects | Concealed allocation and pre-specified switch dates | Conformance against the assigned model version |
| Eligible strata | Digital/no-original first; then original-required; legal status and complexity retained as strata | Estimate effect modification rather than averaging incompatible structures | No Document Check before legally valid receipt | Attribute and event-based subgroup validation |
| Treatment versions | C: current; T1: safe partial order under legacy lock; T2: same order with dual-task support and native timestamps | Distinguish implementation failure from failure of concurrency itself | Huddles, staffing, and API releases held equal | Assigned version, realized overlap, lock errors, and deviations |
| Primary outcomes | Register-to-first-Assessment-ready time and final cycle time | Test proximal mechanism and total benefit | Analyze all assigned cases, including failures | Case durations, join-ready timestamp, and survival/quantile views |
| Secondary outcomes | p90, service and queue components, throughput, true overlap, rework, supplements, appeals, utilization | Detect bottleneck shifts and quality effects | Pre-specified noninferiority limits | Token-based replay, variants, DFGs, WIP, and resource performance |
| Interference controls | Ring-fenced virtual task slots during case randomization; balanced reviewer-hours; later full-system switchback | Limit cross-arm queue spillovers and then measure them explicitly | No reviewer simultaneously owns treatment and control tasks | Cross-lane handoffs, queue lengths, and concurrent active cases |
| Power or minimum support | Bootstrap baseline logs for a 4-hour median effect; seek at least 300 cases per arm, 250 digital cases per key contrast, 250 legal cases, and 60 supplement loops per version | Avoid unsupported subgroup and p90 claims | Extend enrollment if rare-loop support is inadequate | Variant counts and effective cluster size reported before estimation |
| Duration | At least 16 weeks enrollment, optional 6–8-week load stage, completion clearance, and 90-day appeal follow-up | Cover queue cycles and mature quality outcomes | Avoid major statutory or API releases during comparison periods | Calendar drift, backlog carryover, and censoring checks |
| Stop rules | Immediate stop for statutory violation; pause for over 5% unauditable timestamps, over 15 h p90 harm for two weeks, or material rework/appeal breach | Protect legality, tail performance, and quality | Independent weekly safety review | Automated conformance and control charts |
| Transportability analysis | Estimate interactions by originals, legal status, complexity, channel, lock fidelity, and utilization; standardize effects to B’s target mix | Identify where Site A’s mechanism does and does not operate | No expansion based only on pooled median | Causal contrasts, mediation by true overlap, and mix-standardized estimates |

Random assignment estimates the effect of offering each treatment version, while detailed event data reveal whether the assigned version was implemented. The causal chain is tested as assignment  lock-safe execution  true overlap  earlier join readiness  final cycle time.

If T1 shows little trustworthy overlap but T2 shows overlap and shorter waiting, the legacy lock caused implementation failure. If both versions create overlap but join readiness does not improve, the concurrency mechanism is weak for that stratum—for example because originals or one long document branch dominate. If join readiness improves while final cycle does not, assessment or legal congestion is an offsetting downstream mechanism rather than failure of verification parallelism.

Conformance violations, back-entry rates, lock errors, and eligible-task start rates provide fidelity measures. Randomized switchback weeks then test whether benefits survive full queue loading; this distinguishes an individually effective intervention from one that fails at system equilibrium.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py

from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
    OperatorPOWL,
    StrictPartialOrder,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Site A process trees exactly reflecting the stated before/after
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
site_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', "
    "'Assessment', X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

site_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), "
    "'Assessment', X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

a_before_net, a_before_im, a_before_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
    site_a_before
)
a_after_net, a_after_im, a_after_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
    site_a_after
)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Site B candidate as a POWL model
#
# The first XOR abstracts a data-dependent choice:
#   - silent branch: documents are already legally available;
#   - Originals Receipt branch: originals are required.
#
# Identity Check is unordered with the document-preparation path.
# Assessment has explicit predecessors from BOTH Identity Check and
# Document Check, implementing the synchronization requirement.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
register = Transition(label="Register")
identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")

digital_documents_ready = SilentTransition()
originals_receipt = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
document_gate = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[digital_documents_ready, originals_receipt],
)

document_check = Transition(label="Document Check")
assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Optional legal-review branch with a supplement/rework loop
#
# LOOP(body, redo) means:
#   execute Legal Review once;
#   optionally execute the supplement round and then Legal Review again.
#
# The supplement-originals XOR is another abstraction of a
# data-dependent legal/document requirement.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")

request_supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
no_supplement_originals_needed = SilentTransition()
supplement_originals_receipt = Transition(
    label="Supplement Originals Receipt"
)
supplement_originals_gate = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[
        no_supplement_originals_needed,
        supplement_originals_receipt,
    ],
)

repeat_document_check = Transition(label="Repeat Document Check")
repeat_assessment = Transition(label="Repeat Assessment")

supplement_round = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        request_supplement,
        supplement_originals_gate,
        repeat_document_check,
        repeat_assessment,
    ]
)
supplement_round.order.add_edge(
    request_supplement, supplement_originals_gate
)
supplement_round.order.add_edge(
    supplement_originals_gate, repeat_document_check
)
supplement_round.order.add_edge(
    repeat_document_check, repeat_assessment
)

legal_with_supplement_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP,
    children=[legal_review, supplement_round],
)

skip_legal_review = SilentTransition()
optional_legal_branch = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[skip_legal_review, legal_with_supplement_loop],
)


# Final decision and notification
approve = Transition(label="Approve")
reject = Transition(label="Reject")
decision = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[approve, reject],
)
notify = Transition(label="Notify")


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Top-level strict partial order
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------
site_b_candidate = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        register,
        identity,
        document_gate,
        document_check,
        assessment,
        optional_legal_branch,
        decision,
        notify,
    ]
)

# Registration enables the two potentially concurrent paths.
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(register, identity)
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(register, document_gate)

# Document Check waits for either digital availability (silent branch)
# or an actual Originals Receipt event.
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(document_gate, document_check)

# Explicit AND-join dependencies: Assessment waits for BOTH checks.
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(identity, assessment)
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(document_check, assessment)

# Conditional legal branch, decision, then notification.
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(assessment, optional_legal_branch)
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(optional_legal_branch, decision)
site_b_candidate.order.add_edge(decision, notify)
```

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go**

Transfer the **partial-order principle**: remove only precedence constraints that are neither statutory nor informationally necessary. Do not copy Site A’s literal intervention, its six-task WIP rule, or its observed nine-hour reduction. At B, an active Document Check must not be created as immediately executable for the 62% of cases requiring originals. Site A’s cross-trained staffing, dual ownership, digital mix, API improvement, added reviewers, and huddles are part of the observed bundle and cannot be assumed at B.

The first subgroup should be no-original, nonlegal, low/moderate-complexity cases. For these cases, Identity and Document Checks can execute concurrently after registration and most closely reproduce A’s structural mechanism. A reasonable initial expectation is a three-to-six-hour median reduction within this subgroup and only a one-to-three-hour whole-portfolio reduction. Original-required cases should initially receive the narrower intervention of concurrent Identity Check and Originals Receipt tracking, with Document Check remaining legally gated. Legal and high-complexity cases should enter only after assessment and legal queues demonstrate adequate capacity.

The main risks are false overlap caused by the case lock or retrospective timestamps, polluted document queues containing unavailable files, specialist-pool imbalance, an assessment bottleneck, and worsening p90 from legal or supplement congestion. Rework and appeals must be treated as safety outcomes because neither improved at A.

Expansion should require zero statutory ordering violations, auditable native timestamps, at least a substantial increase in true overlap among eligible digital cases, a clinically operational reduction in Assessment-ready time, and no material deterioration in p90, rework, supplement frequency, or appeals. If overlap is low only under the legacy version but rises under dual ownership, modify the IT implementation before expansion. If join readiness improves but final cycle does not, throttle release or add downstream capacity rather than declaring parallelization ineffective. If true overlap is high yet neither join time nor cycle time improves across eligible strata, the mechanism is not valuable at B. Abandon expansion if legal violations occur, quality limits are breached, or sustained tail congestion outweighs central-case gains.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Originals and legal-review requirements can be classified correctly at registration** — needed to select the lawful partial order — test classification against completed legal files and exception audits — if false, use conservative gating and delay subgroup expansion.
2. **Identity and document evidence are sufficiently independent for concurrent work** — needed to avoid one check repeatedly invalidating the other — test discrepancy causes and document rework conditional on identity changes — if false, restrict concurrency to evidence-independent claim types.
3. **The reported 62% originals and 40% legal shares approximate the pilot-period mix** — needed for capacity and scenario calculations — test weekly intake proportions and seasonality — if false, reweight estimates and resize downstream capacity.
4. **A lock-safe dual-task implementation can preserve authorization and audit controls** — needed for true rather than nominal overlap — test security review, lock-event logs, and user acceptance — if false, limit the intervention to Identity–receipt scheduling until IT is replaced.
5. **Productive capacity remains approximately stable across randomized arms** — needed to separate scheduling from staffing effects — test productive hours, absences, utilization, and reviewer rotation — if false, adjust estimates and repeat under balanced capacity.
6. **Supplement-loop events are completely and consistently recorded** — needed to assess legal congestion and repeated work — test trace audits against legal correspondence and document versions — if false, tail and rework conclusions are unreliable.
7. **Rework and 90-day appeals are comparable across arms with complete follow-up** — needed for quality noninferiority decisions — test coding consistency, censoring, and appeal-reason distributions — if false, delay expansion until mature quality data exist.
8. **Interference can be limited or measured during the pilot** — needed because treatment cases share specialist queues with controls — test cross-lane ownership, queue spillovers, and switchback carryover — if false, rely on cluster-period system estimates rather than case-level effects.
