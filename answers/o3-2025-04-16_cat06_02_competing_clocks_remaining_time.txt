## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition  

Below the calendar clock always starts at Fri 18 Sep 15:30.

### Scenario 1 – “Smooth”  
1. Chemistry waiting: both analysts busy until 16:15 and 17:00. Urgent sample occupies one analyst 16:15–17:50 (min)  B-417 cannot be started on Friday.  
2. Chemistry service: Mon 08:00 – 10:30 (triangular mode 2.5 h).  
3. Microbiology: primary incubation ends Sun 12:00–20:00. No weekend call  readout starts Mon 08:00, ends Mon 08:30–09:30.  
4. Parallel join: max(chem 10:30, micro 09:00)  10:30.  
5. QA queue on Monday: five older batches + weekend inflow 3. Reviewer capacity 2. M/M/2 waiting P50 1 h; take 0.5–2 h band. Service 1.0–3.0 h. QA done 12:30–14:30. No query / investigation.  
6. Electronic release 0.25–0.75 h  12:45–15:15.

### Scenario 2 – “Chemistry rerun”  
1–3. Same until chemistry first result (Mon 10:30). Borderline  1–3 h rerun, priority slot, completes 11:30–13:30.  
4. Join  max(chem 13:30 , micro 09:00)  13:30.  
5. QA now faces slightly larger queue; waiting 1–3 h, service 1.0–3.0 h  QA finishes 16:00–19:00.  
6. Release 0.25–0.75 h  16:15–19:45.

### Scenario 3 – “Microbiology confirmation”  
1. Chemistry identical to Scenario 1 (finish 10:30 Mon).  
2. Microbiology readout Mon 08:30; contamination indication (p0.120.24 if excursion). Extra incubation 20–30 h  finishes Tue 04:30–14:30. Readout 0.5–1.5 h  Tue 05:00–16:00.  
3. Join Tue max(chem Mon 10:30 , micro Tue 10:00)  Tue 10:00.  
4. QA Tuesday: two reviewers, backlog thinner. Waiting 1 – 3 h; service 1–3 h  QA 14:30–17:00 Tue (earlier) or Wed morning if confirmation late.  
5. Release Tue 14:45–17:45 or Wed. No investigation assumed here.

### Scenario 4 – “QA tail”  
1–4. Same timings as Scenario 1 (join 10:30 Mon).  
5a. Documentation query (p0.22) adds 4–12 QA working hours. If triggered 12:30 Monday:  
   • 4 h return  QA resumes 16:30 Mon  finish 18:00 Mon.  
   • 12 h return  resumes Tue 10:30  finish 12:30 Tue.  
5b. Deviation investigation (p0.07 baseline or 0.4 with anomalies). Take documentation-only median 14 h working  stretches into Wed. Lab investigation (30 %) adds 3–7 calendar days; we use 5 d centre.  
6. Release 0.25–0.75 h after QA close. Hence:  
   • Query path: Mon 17:30–20:00 or Tue 12:45–15:15.  
   • Doc-only investigation: Wed 17:00 ±4 h.  
   • Lab investigation: Mon 28 Sep ±1 day.
