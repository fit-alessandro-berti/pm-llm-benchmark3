## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Parallel Join Mechanics
The core governing principle for the parallel phase is $\max(t_{\text{chemistry}}, t_{\text{microbiology}})$. Because microbiology incubation requires 46–54 hours, it universally dominates the critical path unless catastrophic chemistry queue delays or repeated reruns push chemistry completion past Monday noon.

### Scenario-by-Scenario Breakdown

1. **Smooth Path**
   - **Chemistry:** Active service begins around Friday 16:30 (after clearing the urgent sample and active items). Service takes ~2.5 h, finishing Friday at 19:00 (within Chemistry hours: 08:00–18:00 + minor spillover/buffer, completed safely before Friday 18:00 if prioritized, or pausing until Monday 08:00 and finishing by 10:30). Let's assume normal finish Friday 17:30. Usable chemistry result: Friday 17:30.
   - **Microbiology:** Incubation started Friday 14:00. Ready between Sunday 12:00 and Saturday 16:00 (46–54 h). Assuming weekend on-call authorization occurs (prob ~0.35), readout happens Saturday at 15:00. Without authorization, readout is Monday at 08:30–09:30.
   - **Parallel Join Time:** Governed by Microbiology $\rightarrow$ Saturday 15:00 (if on-call) or Monday 09:30 (if routine staffing).
   - **QA Waiting & Service:** QA is open Saturday 09:00–13:00 (closed when Sat micro finishes; reopens Monday 08:00). Thus, QA review begins Monday at 08:00. Review takes ~1.5–3.5 h, finishing Monday at 11:30.
   - **Optional Rework:** None.
   - **Final Release:** Electronic release takes 0.5 h. Terminal event: **Monday, 21 Sep 2026, ~12:00**.

2. **Chemistry Rerun**
   - **Chemistry:** Initial test finishes Friday 17:00. Borderline result triggers rerun (prob ~0.15). Rerun cannot finish before lab closes at 18:00, so it pauses and resumes Monday 08:00, taking another ~1.5–2.0 h. Usable result: Monday, 10:30.
   - **Microbiology:** Completes readout Monday at 09:30.
   - **Parallel Join Time:** Monday at 10:30 (governed by Chemistry rerun).
   - **QA Waiting & Service:** QA review starts Monday at 10:30, finishes by 14:00.
   - **Optional Rework:** None (rerun resolved).
   - **Final Release:** Terminal event: **Monday, 21 Sep 2026, ~14:30**.

3. **Delayed Microbiology Readout or Confirmation**
   - **Chemistry:** Completed Friday 17:30.
   - **Microbiology:** Primary incubation finishes Sunday. Weekend on-call is *not* authorized. Contamination indication occurs (prob ~0.08), requiring an additional 24 h of continuous incubation (finishing Monday ~14:00) plus 1 h readout (finishing Monday 15:00).
   - **Parallel Join Time:** Monday at 15:00.
   - **QA Waiting & Service:** QA review starts Monday 15:00, queues behind Monday arrivals, completes Tuesday at 10:00.
   - **Optional Rework:** Included in confirmation delay.
   - **Final Release:** Terminal event: **Tuesday, 22 Sep 2026, ~11:00**.

4. **QA Query or Investigation Tail**
   - **Chemistry & Micro:** Normal parallel completion by Monday 10:00.
   - **Parallel Join Time:** Monday 10:00.
   - **QA Waiting & Service:** QA review starts Monday 10:00. A Documentation Query is raised (adds ~8 working hours) or a Deviation Investigation is opened (documentation-only adds ~14 working hours, spanning into Tuesday/Wednesday). If a laboratory investigation is triggered, it adds 3–7 calendar days.
   - **Terminal Event:** Ranges from Wednesday, 23 Sep 2026 (Documentation Query) to Monday, 28 Sep 2026 (Lab Investigation).

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Microbiology Weekend On-Call Authorization & Readout Timing** *(Calendar & Path Uncertainty)* — Determines whether the 48-hour incubation batch is read out during the weekend or delayed until Monday morning.
2. **Upstream Temperature Excursion Confirmation** *(Dependence Uncertainty)* — A confirmed excursion simultaneously increases the probability of chemistry reruns and microbiology contamination indications.
3. **QA Deviation Investigation vs. Documentation Query** *(Path Uncertainty)* — Whether QA opens a quick document review or a 3–7 day laboratory investigation drastically alters the tail distribution.
4. **Chemistry Queue Discipline & Borderline Rerun Spillage** *(Queue & Service-Time Uncertainty)* — Uncertainty over whether B-417 is prioritized past ordinary samples and whether its test spills over past Friday 18:00.
5. **QA Queue Congestion at Future Join Time** *(Queue Uncertainty)* — Incoming batch arrival rate ($\lambda = 0.35\text{/hr}$) combined with variable reviewer staffing creates queuing delays upon branch join.
