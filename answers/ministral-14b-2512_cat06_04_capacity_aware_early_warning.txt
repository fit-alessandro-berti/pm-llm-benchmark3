### **1. Decision Rule**
**Eligibility:**
- **Stage:** Only cases in `Triage` or `Diagnosis Started` (i.e., before `Diagnosis Complete`). Cases like I-03 are ineligible.
- **Alert persistence:** Require **material new evidence** (e.g., new customer logs, diagnostic updates) for repeated alerts. For I-05 (no new event), suppress further alerts unless `p_now` exceeds a higher threshold (e.g., 0.70). For I-07 (new log), allow re-evaluation.
- **Minimum evidence change:** If `p_now` increases by 0.10 from `p_prev` *and* there is a documented new event, treat as a fresh alert. Otherwise, suppress for 2 hours (cool-down period).
- **Calibration adjustments:**
  - **SME:** Add 0.065 to `p_now` (midpoint of underprediction range).
  - **Strategic Enterprise:** Subtract 0.075 from `p_now` (midpoint of overprediction).
  - **Public Sector:** No adjustment (calibration acceptable).
  - **Non-Native-Language Support:** Use the lower half of effect ranges (0.05–0.15) due to sparse calibration.

**Prioritization:**
Compute a **weighted score** for eligible cases:
`Score = (Adjusted p_now × Value of avoiding breach)  (False-alarm burden + Swarm cost)  (Spillover harm estimate)`
- **Adjusted p_now:** Calibration-adjusted probability.
- **Spillover harm estimate:** `0.02 × 6 cases × Value of avoiding breach` (midpoint spillover × untreated cases × average value).
- **Tiering:**
  - **Tier 1 (Intervene):** Score > €5,000 net benefit.
  - **Tier 2 (Wait):** Score between €2,000–€5,000; monitor for new evidence.
  - **Tier 3 (Do not intervene):** Score < €2,000 or ineligible.

### **4. Repeated Alerts and Temporal Policy**
- **Cool-down:** Suppress alerts for **2 hours** after the first alert unless `p_now` increases by 0.10 *and* new evidence is documented.
  - **I-05:** No new event  suppress indefinitely (or until `p_now`  0.70).
  - **I-07:** New customer log  treat as fresh alert; eligible for intervention.
- **Escalation:**
  - If a case remains in `Diagnosis Started` > 4 hours with `p_now` > 0.70, auto-escalate to Tier 1 (unless suppressed).
- **Re-entry:**
  - Cases that breached after a prior swarm are **permanently ineligible** for future swarms (feedback loop risk).
- **Alert history review:**
  - Log all suppressed alerts for post-mortem if the case breaches (to detect false negatives).

### **5. Fairness and Feedback-Loop Risks**
**Risks:**
1. **Value-based starvation:**
   - Lower-value cases (e.g., I-10) may never receive intervention. **Mitigation:** Reserve 1 slot/week for cases with `adjusted p_now` > 0.80 *regardless of value*.
2. **Non-Native-Language Support:**
   - Uncertain effects may lead to under-intervention. **Mitigation:** Cap spillover harm estimate at 50% for these cases.
3. **Differential calibration:**
   - SME cases are underpredicted, leading to over-intervention. **Mitigation:** Apply stricter Tier 1 threshold (e.g., score > €7,000) for SME.
4. **Selective intervention bias:**
   - Treated cases may appear "easier" in training data, reducing future model accuracy. **Mitigation:** Randomly withhold 10% of swarms for A/B testing of effect estimates.
5. **Spillover harm equity:**
   - Untreated cases in `Diagnosis Started` (e.g., I-04, I-08) may disproportionately bear spillover harm. **Mitigation:** Prioritize swarms for cases with the **highest `p_now × time to deadline` ratio** to minimize spillover exposure.

**Concrete Safeguards:**
- **Segment-specific thresholds:** Use different `adjusted p_now` cutoffs per segment (e.g., 0.60 for SME, 0.55 for Strategic Enterprise).
- **Spillover budget:** Limit total spillover harm to <20% of direct benefits in any scenario.
- **Fairness audit:** Monthly review of breach rates for untreated cases by segment to detect systematic harm.

### **6. Monitoring and Recalibration Plan**
**Operational Monitoring:**
- **Daily:** Track swarm utilization, spillover delay metrics, and breach rates for treated vs. untreated cases.
- **Weekly:** Compare actual breach rates to predicted rates (by segment) to detect calibration drift.

**Predictive Monitoring:**
- **Monthly:** Backtest model predictions against actual breaches, stratified by segment and intervention status. Adjust calibration offsets if bias exceeds ±0.05.
- **Quarterly:** Re-estimate intervention effect ranges using a **difference-in-differences** design (compare swarmed vs. non-swarmed cases matched on `p_now` and time to deadline).

**Causal-Effect Monitoring:**
- **Semi-annual:** Run a **randomized experiment** (e.g., 20% of Tier 2 cases randomly swarmed) to estimate spillover harm and effect heterogeneity.
- **Trigger-based:** If >30% of swarms show no breach reduction, suspend the policy for 2 weeks and recalibrate.

**Capacity Monitoring:**
- **Real-time:** Track diagnosis queue length and specialist availability. If queue >10 cases, reduce swarm slots to 3 to limit spillover.
- **Adaptive:** Dynamically adjust spillover harm estimate based on recent queue delays (e.g., if delays exceed 20 mins/slot, reduce swarm slots).

**Fairness Monitoring:**
- **Monthly:** Compute **equity ratios** (breach rate for untreated cases / breach rate for treated cases) by segment. Flag if ratio >1.5 for any group.
- **Annual:** Conduct a **causal fairness audit** to test if spillover harm disproportionately affects any segment.

**Recalibration Triggers:**
- Change the decision rule if:
  - Calibration bias exceeds ±0.10 for any segment.
  - Spillover harm exceeds 30% of direct benefits in >2 consecutive months.
  - Intervention effects shrink by >50% (e.g., from 0.20 to 0.10).

**Example Recalibration:**
- If **Strategic Enterprise** overprediction grows to 0.15, increase the `adjusted p_now` threshold to 0.60 (from 0.55) for that segment.
