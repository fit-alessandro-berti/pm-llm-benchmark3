
### Time-ordered process-mechanism diagram
```
arrival -> triage_wait -> triage_service -> (optional) manual_queue_wait -> manual_service -> (optional) escalation_queue_wait -> senior_service -> close
arrival_load affects triage_wait, manual_queue_wait, escalation_queue_wait
manual_review mediates triage -> manual_queue_wait
escalation depends on manual_review -> escalation_queue_wait -> senior_service
```

### Explanation
- **Interference**: Shared queues for manual review and escalation introduce competition effects across flagged/unflagged cases.
- **Exposure-induced confounding**: Flag influences triage priority, altering downstream queue times.
- **Feedback loops**: Flagged conditions alter queue congestion, feeding back into system delays.

## 5. Identification strategy and falsification

| question                          | proposed design or analysis        | adjustment or stratification set          | assumptions                                | diagnostic or falsification test        | result that would change your conclusion |
|-----------------------------------|------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| Does the flag truly accelerate triage? | Local threshold or discontinuity at flag-risk boundary | Risk_score cutoff strata                  | Risk score affects only flag assignment   | Compare triage times near cutoff        | Overlap between Flag=0/1 disappears      |
| Do shared queues interfere?       | Queue-state-aware analysis         | Arrival_load, queued case distribution    | Queue state reliably observed            | Inspect queued service correlation      | No effect of queue state on times        |
| How does manual review affect delay? | Mediator-outcome analysis         | Risk_score, manual completion, flagged    | Mediator-treatment interaction accounted  | Compare mediated and direct pathways    | Substantial manual-completion variance   |
| Hidden risk confounds outcomes?   | Negative control using risk-score-adjusted cases | Complexity and risk_score overlap regions | Observed risk covers hidden confounding   | Test flagged versus unflagged quality   | Flag case outcomes diverge substantially |
| Sensitivity to escalation capacity | Sensitivity to assigned escalation | Risk_score, escalation resources allocated | System stable across escalation within-pilot | Trace escalation-cycle variability      | No queue increases despite trial queues  |
