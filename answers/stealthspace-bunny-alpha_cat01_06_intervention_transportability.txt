**Reasoning and arithmetic.**

1. **Structural opportunity:** Identity service at B is 3.4 h; document service is 8.1 h. For original-exempt cases, a simple no-queue comparison changes `Identity + Document` from 11.5 h to `max(Identity, Document) = 8.1 h`—a diagnostic difference of 3.4 h.

2. **Original-required cases:** Using the supplied 18 h receipt delay, the illustrative clock changes from `Identity + Receipt + Document = 29.5 h` to `max(Identity, Receipt + Document) = 26.1 h`, again a 3.4 h component difference. Receipt delay is external, so this does not disappear; it merely overlaps with identity work. These calculations use medians as simple clock inputs and are **not distributional bounds**.

3. **Why nine hours is not the forecast:** A’s nine-hour median contrast coincided with an API improvement, two additional reviewers, huddles, easier claims, and more FTE. Its six-hour waiting reduction also cannot be subtracted directly from B’s 20-hour waiting figure without fork and queue timestamps.

4. **Legal and rework magnitude:** Supplements are 360/1,360, or about 26.5%, of legal cases and 360/3,400, or about 10.6%, of all cases. The 345 recorded document rechecks must not simply be added to the 17% rework rate: they may overlap. Their queue effect nevertheless makes tail harm plausible.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

### Process specification

**Eligibility and rollout**

- Initially enroll simpler, confirmed original-exempt/digital cases with no known exceptional dependency.
- Retain representation of original-required, complex, public-sector, and high-value cases in the learning pilot; do not permanently exclude Legal Review cases.
- All registered cases may use the new readiness and logging architecture, but admission must respect pilot WIP headroom.
- Use an initial ceiling of approximately 10 eligible cases per day, subject to measured service capacity. This is below B’s historical throughput of approximately 18.9 cases per day and must be coordinated with intake; it is not a claim that the small WIP caps can support unrestricted intake.

**Permitted concurrency**

- Create Identity Check and Originals Receipt, when required, after Register.
- For confirmed original-exempt claims, create Identity Check and Document Check together after Register.
- For original-required claims, create Document Check in a blocked/pending state if useful, but execute it only after an original-document-received event.
- Identity and Document Check have no unnecessary dependency on each other. A document check may start after receipt while identity checking continues.
- Start Assessment only when identity checking and the current, final required document-check version are complete.

**Mandatory dependencies**

Retain Register first, the original-document prerequisite, the Identity/Document join before Assessment, mandatory legal review, and Approve/Reject followed by Notify. No management rule can waive these dependencies.

**Queue and WIP controls**

- Initial pool limits: **6 open identity cases, 8 document cases, 4 assessment cases, and 2 legal cases**.
- These are conservative pilot defaults, not inferred optimal capacities. No individual may exceed six open tasks.
- Count both executable and prerequisite-blocked work in the relevant workload measure; otherwise hidden queues will be understated.
- Hold new admissions when a pool reaches its limit. Prioritize cases whose next task is executable rather than merely creating more blocked tickets.
- Supervisor queue huddles, if adopted, must be a separately evaluated intervention.

**Resource responsibilities**

Registration staff own the receipt event. Identity reviewers own Identity Check, document reviewers own Document Check, assessors own Assessment, and legal reviewers own Legal Review. A supervisor manages release priorities but does not perform unqualified legal or verification work. Cross-trained support may be used only where competence and legislation permit.

**Legal and supplement handling**

- Route the specified 40% to Legal Review after Assessment.
- Legal Review may directly approve/reject or request a supplement.
- A supplement creates a new document-check version and, when required, a new assessment version. Completed identity work may be reused unless identity information changes.
- Do not discard the original task history or count the repeat as a new claim.

**Logging**

Record case and task version, Register, receipt-required flag, original-received time, prerequisite-ready time, actual owner, task start/end, queue-entry time, Legal Review disposition, supplement request, repeat links, Assessment join, decision, Notify, and appeal linkage. Use source-system timestamps and log any back-entry.

### Compact POWL-style partial-order description

```text
Register -> IdentityCheck
Register -> OriginalsReceipt                 [when legally required]
Register -> DocumentCheck                    [when originals are not required]
OriginalsReceipt -> DocumentCheck             [when legally required]

IdentityCheck -> Assessment
DocumentCheck -> Assessment

Assessment -> Approve_or_Reject
Assessment -> LegalReview                     [when legally required]

LegalReview -> LegalApprove_or_Reject
LegalReview -> RequestSupplement
RequestSupplement -> DocumentCheck_repeat
DocumentCheck_repeat -> Assessment_repeat
Assessment_repeat -> Approve_or_Reject
Assessment_repeat -> LegalReview_repeat      [if still required]
LegalReview_repeat -> Approve_or_Reject
LegalReview_repeat -> RequestSupplement_repeat

Final_Approve_or_Reject -> Notify -> End
```

The original-exempt branch makes the receipt dependency vacuous; the original-required branch retains it. The same logic is repeated after each supplement.

The factorial design estimates the incremental effect of readiness scheduling with contemporaneous controls, rather than crediting a pre/post mix shift. Huddles are separately randomized, so an apparent parallelization benefit is not automatically attributed to the task diagram.

Fidelity measures separate permission from execution. High task-creation rates with low eligible overlap, excessive back-entry, or lock violations indicate implementation failure. Genuine early overlap with unchanged cycle time suggests that downstream work merely shifted the queue. In the latter case, capacity, legal review, or assessment—not concurrency—may be the limiting mechanism.

Stratified estimates prevent a large original-exempt subgroup from hiding deterioration among original-required or legal cases. Conversely, under-supported strata remain exploratory. Expansion requires both a credible eligible-case cycle effect and acceptable tail and quality guardrails; failure on only the latter would favor redesign rather than abandonment of concurrency altogether.

    model = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)
    for source, target in edges:
        model.order.add_edge(source, target)
    return model

# ------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Construct a reasonable Site B candidate.
#
# Register starts Identity Check and the document-channel branch.
# The receipt branch has two model alternatives:
#   * a required Originals Receipt, which gates Document Check;

# Identity and Document Check remain independent. Their explicit

# Legal Review is optional at the control-flow level. A legal

    (assessment_1, post_assessment_1),
    (post_assessment_1, legal_decision_1),
    (legal_decision_1, document_2),

    # Identity Check 1 is already complete and can be reused.
    (document_2, assessment_2),
    (assessment_2, post_assessment_2),
    (post_assessment_2, legal_decision_2),
    (legal_decision_2, document_3),

    # Second supplement loop.
    (document_3, assessment_3),
    (assessment_3, post_assessment_3),
    (post_assessment_3, legal_decision_3),

    # Exactly one Notify on each accepted path.
    (legal_decision_3, notify),
    (end, notify),  # temporary edge, removed below
]

# The first/last legal-decision and assessment choices terminate
# directly at Notify. XOR choices have no independent completion
# transition, so their predecessor/Notify edges are set explicitly.
edges = []
for source, target in b_edges:
    if (source, end) and target is notify:
        continue
    if target is end:
        continue
    edges.append((source, target))

# Direct decisions and legal decisions after each assessment all
# terminate through Notify.
for source in [
    post_assessment_1,
    post_assessment_2,
    post_assessment_3,
    legal_decision_1,
    legal_decision_2,
    legal_decision_3,

edges = [(source, target) for source, target in edges if target is not end]

    (source, target)
    for source, target in edges
    if not (target is notify and source is end)

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go**

Transfer the **partial-order and queue-management idea**: create eligible verification branches promptly, retain explicit joins, prioritize work that can actually execute, and timestamp events faithfully. Do not copy the claim that every Document Check may start immediately. For B, Identity Check and Originals Receipt can overlap, and confirmed original-exempt Document Check can overlap Identity Check. Original-required Document Check must remain blocked until receipt. Mandatory Assessment joins and Legal Review remain unchanged.

The first implementation subgroup should be confirmed original-exempt, preferably digital, lower-complexity claims. The learning pilot must also include original-required, complex, public-sector, and high-value cases so that 40% Legal Review and supplement loops are not omitted from transport assessment. A’s nine-hour improvement must not be promised. A simple clock comparison identifies only a 3.4-hour scheduling component under current service inputs; queueing, external receipt delays, and downstream capacity determine whether that opportunity materializes.

The principal benefits are earlier eligible work, better use of original-exempt capacity, and clearer queue priorities. The principal risks are workarounds around the case lock, document or legal saturation, hidden blocked queues, and a better median accompanied by a worse p90. A’s capacity increase, API change, staffing, case mix, and huddles cannot all be transferred as a single “parallelization” claim.

Expand only if the randomized scheduling component improves eligible-case cycle time relative to concurrent controls, produces verified—not merely logged—overlap, and has an upper confidence bound below a prespecified p90 deterioration margin, provisionally +5 h. Rework and appeals should show no material adverse signal, and the legal subgroup should not show congestion-driven deterioration. Modify the design if fidelity is good but benefits arise only for original-exempt cases, or if document throughput improves while legal/assessment tails worsen. Pause immediately for any statutory-order violation, record corruption, or sustained WIP-limit breach. Abandon the causal intervention claim if lawful early execution cannot be achieved or if the controlled pilot shows no cycle benefit and unacceptable downstream effects. Do not seek expansion merely from another favorable before/after comparison.
