# 1. Current-process diagnosis

The warranty process suffers from **unsegmented routing**, where a single outdated automated triage engine conflates low-risk, time-sensitive claims with high-value, technically complex claims. Rather than facing a binary choice between "fast and sloppy" versus "slow and high-quality," process-mining evidence shows that **both dominant pathways are misapplied across customer and product segments**.

### Synthesis of Process-Mining Artifacts and Core Problems
1. **Frequency DFG (Volume & Structural Bypassing):** Of 5,000 claims, 72% (`3,600`) enter `Auto eligibility screen` and `2,550` flow straight to `Fast approval`, making `Intake -> Auto screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement -> Quality/compliance close` the dominant happy-looking highway (`2,300` dispatches). Conversely, cases hitting `Request missing evidence` (`650` from Auto, `780` from Manual) suffer severe attrition and looping: `890` loop back into `Manual eligibility review` and `320` end in `Claim abandoned` (out of `390` total abandonments). Furthermore, `Customer complaint/reopen -> Escalation board` (`120` cases) routes directly to `Dispatch replacement` (`65`), `Repair device` (`45`), and `Claim denied` (`10`), structurally bypassing `Manual eligibility review`.
2. **Performance DFG (Bimodal Latency & Long-Tail Failure):** The fast path moves from `Intake` through `Quality/compliance close` in roughly 6 hours of sum-of-medians (`0.5h + 0.4h + 1.8h + 3.2h`). By contrast, the reviewed/repair path accumulates massive queueing and external wait times: `Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` takes a mean of `34.0h` (`p90 88.0h`), `Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote` takes `22.0h` (`p90 60.0h`, driven by part-price confirmation waits), and `Repair quote -> Customer approval` adds `30.0h` (`p90 80.0h`, triggering `70` abandonments and `80` complaints). Crucially, fast-path speed is partly illusory: `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` (`260` cases) exhibits a hidden long tail with a `96.0h` mean and `180.0h` p90.
3. **Token-Based Replay vs. Trace Fitness:** Token-based replay reports a deceptively high `0.91` log fitness, yet **only `68%` of traces are fully conformant**. Because token replay penalizes a single skipped gate lightly across a multi-step trace, the `0.91` score masks systemic policy breaches in nearly one-third of all claims.
4. **Alignment Diagnostics & Model Precision:** Alignments expose three critical move-on-log violations: `Fast approval` before evidence completeness (violating **R2**), `Dispatch replacement` without risk review on >EUR 400 devices (violating **R1**), and `Customer complaint/reopen` occurring *after* `Quality/compliance close` (explaining why `3,015` closes plus `220` denials and `390` abandonments do not cleanly terminate all `5,000` cases without post-close reopens, alongside censored in-flight cases where `Manual eligibility review` receives `2,600` inflows against `1,400` completed primary outflows). Meanwhile, low model precision reflects ad-hoc combinations of auto-approval, risk review, and repair—and direct **R3** violations when the `Escalation board` denies or resolves reopened claims without `Manual eligibility review`.
5. **Temporal Profile Deviations, Rework, & Segment Economics:** Temporal diagnostics link `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` directly to **wrong replacement part selection**, and flag `Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote` as chronically too slow for Premium SLAs. In the rework ledger, `Request missing evidence` repeats $\ge 2$ times in `26%` of cases entering it, while `Rework repair` hits `12.5%` of repaired high-value devices (vs. `4.0%` low-risk).

### Resolving the Fast-vs.-Slow Contradiction
The apparent contradiction—that `Fast approval` is `31%` cheaper upfront and vastly faster, yet generates a `14.1%` complaint/reopen rate (vs. `3.3%` on the repair path) that erodes its net cost advantage to just **`9%`**—is an **aggregation artifact (Simpson’s paradox across segments)**:
* **Where the Fast Path Wins:** For **Premium low-risk accessories** (`18%` of volume), **R2** explicitly permits dispatch before evidence completion. The fast path delivers an `8h` median cycle time (protecting against steep >`24h` service credits) with only a `6%` complaint rate (vs. `3%` manual) and low error severity (`EUR 95` per wrong replacement). Routing these to the `46h` manual path destroys customer value and wastes scarce reviewer hours (`EUR 34/h`).
* **Where the Fast Path Destroys Value:** Outdated auto-screen rules leak **Premium (`14%`) and Standard (`21%`) high-value devices** onto the fast path, violating **R1/R2** and driving `22%` and `18%` complaint/reopen rates, respectively, at **`EUR 410` per wrong replacement**. Here, reviewed/repair paths cut complaints to `5%–6%`, but their `96h–120h` median cycle times breach Premium expectations and create heavy labor costs.
* **The Capacity Trap:** Simply tightening auto-screen rules increases `Manual eligibility review` inflow by `15%–35%`, pushing the 7 reviewers past their **`82%` utilization cliff** where queues explode nonlinearly. Any viable redesign must balance risk controls against this capacity boundary.

# 2. Redesign A: Segment-Aware Risk Triage & Repurposed Pre-Dispatch SKU Gate

### Redesigned Process Flow
1. **Updated Segment- & Value-Aware Auto Screen (`Intake -> Auto screen`):** Rules are updated (2-week deployment) to cross-check product value (EUR 400 threshold), segment tier, and initial evidence flags:
   * *Premium Low-Risk Accessories:* Immediate `Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` (no evidence hold, **R2**-compliant).
   * *Standard Low-Risk Accessories:* Automated completeness check; if complete, `Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement`; if incomplete, `Request missing evidence`.
   * *All Claims > EUR 400:* Hard-blocked from direct `Auto -> Fast approval` (enforcing **R1**). Anomaly-scored claims route to `Fraud/risk review`; clean >EUR 400 claims route to a streamlined `Manual eligibility review`.
2. **Pre-Dispatch SKU/Part Verification Gate (`Fast approval -> Pre-dispatch gate -> Dispatch replacement`):** Underutilized `Quality check` inspectors (2 FTEs at `EUR 38/h`, currently idle outside repair campaigns) are cross-assigned to run a lightweight `6h–12h` pre-dispatch verification on all replacement orders for high-value devices and complex SKUs before warehouse release.
3. **Mandatory Compliance Sign-Off on Reopens:** `Escalation board` outputs (`120` cases) are routed through a mandatory 10-minute `Manual eligibility review` compliance sign-off before `Claim denied` or fulfillment close, eliminating **R3** violations.

### Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
* **Segments:** High-value devices (`35%` combined share) leaking onto the fast path, while ring-fencing Premium low-risk accessories (`18%`) from added friction.
* **Paths/Activities:** Eliminates non-conformant `Auto screen -> Fast approval` for >EUR 400 claims, inserts a control gate before `Dispatch replacement` (`260` post-dispatch complaints), and closes the `Escalation board` **R3** loophole.

### Mechanism of Improvement
* **Compliance & Error Prevention:** Enforces **R1**, **R2**, and **R3** deterministically (lifting trace fitness well above `68%`). Catching wrong replacement part selections *before* shipment via underused `Quality check` capacity (`EUR 38/h`) prevents `EUR 410` high-value replacement errors, reshipments, and the `96h` mean (`p90 180h`) `Dispatch -> Complaint/reopen` tail.
* **Capacity Netting:** While updating auto-screen rules pushes `+15%–35%` more initial cases into `Manual eligibility review`, preventing downstream `Customer complaint/reopen -> Manual eligibility review` loops (currently `310` re-entries) partially offsets reviewer load.

### Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
* **Deliberately Sacrifices Replacement Speed on Mid/High-Value Claims:** Adds `6h–18h` of pre-dispatch gate latency plus manual/risk review time, abandoning the `20h–28h` (non-compliant) fast-path cycle time on high-value devices.
* **Leaves Unresolved:** Does **not** fix the `22%` "Unclear defect / missing evidence" segment (`140h` median cycle time, `26%` repeat loops) or the slow `Repair quote -> Customer approval` repair chain (`52h` combined mean wait). Runs dangerously close to the `82%` utilization cliff for the 7 manual reviewers during transition.

# 3. Redesign B: Guided Front-End Evidence Intake & Pre-Priced Repair Compression

### Redesigned Process Flow
1. **Guided Digital Evidence Portal at Intake (`START -> Guided Intake`):** Replaces passive intake with a defect-specific digital wizard (with optional agent-assisted phone upload for non-digital users) that validates serial numbers, proof-of-purchase, and diagnostic photos *before* eligibility routing, alongside updated **R1/R2** auto-screen rules.
2. **Pre-Priced Modular Repair Matrix & Upfront Consent:** Replaces ad-hoc technician part-price lookups in `Repair quote` (3 techs at `EUR 45/h` waiting on part-price confirmation) with a pre-approved standard part/tier price catalog. During `Guided Intake`, customers pre-authorize covered repairs or capped deductible tiers, allowing `Manual/Fraud review` to route qualifying cases **directly to `Repair device`**, bypassing `Repair quote -> Customer approval` (`460` cases) except for non-standard out-of-warranty damage.
3. **In-Line High-Value Repair Dual-Inspection:** Reallocates idle `Quality check` hours to an intermediate diagnostic check inside `Repair device` specifically for high-value devices to cut the `12.5%` `Rework repair` loop.

### Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
* **Segments:** Unclear defect / missing evidence (`22%` share, `140h` median time) and High-Value devices routed through repair (`35%` share, `96h–120h` reviewed/repair median).
* **Paths/Activities:** Compresses `Auto/Manual -> Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` (`1,430` requests, `890` manual loops at `34.0h` mean) and collapses `Manual/Fraud -> Repair quote` (`22.0h`/`21.0h` mean) and `Repair quote -> Customer approval` (`30.0h` mean).

### Mechanism of Improvement
* **Unlocking Reviewer Capacity by Eliminating Rework:** Cutting repeated evidence loops by `30%–45%` via the portal removes hundreds of `Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` re-entries (`890` edges) and `14.0h` manual evidence-request prep steps. This **frees existing `Manual eligibility review` capacity**, allowing the 7 reviewers to absorb **R1**-mandated high-value reviews *without* breaching the `82%` utilization cliff.
* **Making the High-Quality Path Fast:** Because the repair path already achieves the lowest complaint rate (`3.3%` overall; `5%–6%` high-value), stripping `~52h` of non-value-added quote/approval waiting time makes compliant repair fast enough for Premium high-value cases while cutting mid-process quote abandonments (`70`) and quote complaints (`80`).

### Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
* **Deliberately Sacrifices Intake Frictionless Simplicity:** Shifts effort to the customer at `Intake`; customers with low digital literacy face higher **front-end abandonment risk** unless rescued by assisted channels.
* **Leaves Unresolved:** Higher IT/catalog implementation complexity and longer lead time than Redesign A. For cases still fulfilled via `Dispatch replacement` rather than repair, wrong part selection is not directly gated unless paired with SKU rules.

# 4. Redesign C: SLA-Decoupled Dual Network (Concierge Replace-First vs. Load-Leveled Standard)

### Redesigned Process Flow
1. **Hard Network Split at Intake by Customer Tier & SLA:**
   * **Track 1 — Premium Concierge Express (`32%` total volume: `18%` Low-Risk Accessory + `14%` High-Value Device):**
     * *Premium Low-Risk:* Instant `Auto screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` (`<8h` median).
     * *Premium High-Value:* Routed to a dedicated **Priority Rapid Pod** (1 dedicated Manual Reviewer + scheduled morning/afternoon Fraud specialist micro-slots + 1 Quality Inspector verifying exact replacement SKU). Instead of waiting `96h` for customer-specific quoting and repair, approved Premium high-value claims receive **Immediate Verified Replacement (`<24h` target)** (**R1/R2** compliant). Returned defective units are routed *offline* to `Repair device -> Quality check` to replenish refurbished replacement stock, decoupling repair latency from customer wait time.
   * **Track 2 — Load-Leveled Standard Network (`46%` Standard volume + `22%` Unclear/Missing Evidence):**
     * Standard claims receive immediate automated status tracking with an explicit **`48h–72h` completion promise** (matching Standard customers' `72h` tolerance with good visibility).
     * Claims enter a **WIP-capped smoothing buffer** that releases work into `Manual eligibility review` and `Fraud/risk review` only when reviewer utilization sits `<78%`, flattening Monday and post-campaign spikes.
     * Standard High-Value claims (`21%`) default to the high-accuracy reviewed/repair path (protecting against `EUR 410` replacement errors where speed earns no service-credit premium).
2. **R3 Governance:** Reopened claims enter the Standard/Priority `Manual eligibility review` queue prior to `Escalation board` execution or denial.

### Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
* **Segments:** Explicitly separates **Premium high-value (`14%`)** and **Premium low-risk (`18%`)** from **Standard low-risk (`25%`)** and **Standard high-value (`21%`)**.
* **Paths/Activities:** Eliminates customer-facing `Repair quote -> Repair device` waits for Premium claims; introduces queue load-leveling ahead of `Fraud/risk review` (Monday bottleneck) and `Manual eligibility review` (`82%` utilization cliff).

### Mechanism of Improvement
* **Queueing Physics & Peak Smoothing:** Fraud (`2` specialists) and Manual Review (`7` reviewers) fail primarily on variance (Mondays, marketing campaigns, >`82%` utilization). By intentionally holding tolerant Standard Low-Risk (`25%`) and Standard High-Value (`21%`) claims for `12h–36h` inside a transparent status-tracked buffer, arrival peaks are shaved into troughs—preventing nonlinear queue blowups without hiring scarce specialists.
* **Economic Arbitrage on Premium High-Value:** Replaces `22%` fast-path complaints (`EUR 410` wrong-part cost) *and* `96h` repair SLA breaches with `<24h` verified replacement, recovering asset value asynchronously via offline refurbishment when repair/QC shops have slack capacity.

### Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
* **Deliberately Sacrifices Standard Low-Risk Speed & Working Capital:** Standard low-risk accessory median cycle time intentionally slows from `13h` toward `36h–60h` (within their `72h` tolerance), and maintaining a high-value replacement/refurbishment buffer ties up inventory capital and carries depreciation risk.
* **Leaves Unresolved:** Does not resolve the `26%` repeat evidence loop in the `22%` missing-evidence segment, nor does it speed up external part-price confirmation for out-of-warranty Standard repairs.

# 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

### Redesign A: Segment-Aware Risk Triage & Repurposed Pre-Dispatch SKU Gate
* **Failure Mode 1 (Manual Reviewer Queue Collapse):** If the auto-screen rule update triggers the upper end of the `+15%–35%` inflow surge before downstream complaint loops (`310` cases) subside—since `Dispatch -> Complaint` takes a `70h` median to materialize—the 7 manual reviewers will cross **`82%` utilization**, causing `Intake -> Manual review` (`5.0h` median) to snowball past `48h+`.
* **Failure Mode 2 (Rubber-Stamping at the Pre-Dispatch Gate):** `Quality check` inspectors are trained to inspect physical repairs, not verify subtle SKU/entitlement compatibility from claim logs; without crisp SKU lookup tables, the `6h–18h` gate adds latency while still leaking `EUR 410` wrong replacements.
* **Bottleneck Migration:** Migrates from downstream complaint handling (`Escalation board` / reopens) upstream to **`Fraud/risk review` (2 specialists on Mondays/post-campaigns)** and **`Quality check` during simultaneous repair + replacement campaigns**, when inspectors can no longer cover both `Repair device -> Quality check` and pre-dispatch gating.

### Redesign B: Guided Front-End Evidence Intake & Pre-Priced Repair Compression
* **Failure Mode 1 (Silent Pre-Intake Abandonment):** Customers with low digital literacy or intermittent defects (who cannot capture clear diagnostic proof upfront) drop out *before* submitting `Intake claim`. Reported post-intake `Claim abandoned` (`390` cases) falls, creating a mirage of improvement while hidden customer churn and brand damage rise.
* **Failure Mode 2 (Margin Erosion or Scope Disputes from Pre-Priced Tiers):** When technicians open devices in `Repair device` and discover unquoted liquid/impact damage not caught by intake photos, bypassing `Repair quote` triggers mid-bench halts, angry exception calls, or unrecovered part costs.
* **Bottleneck Migration:** Removing the `52h` combined `Repair quote -> Customer approval` buffer floods **`Repair device` physical bench capacity and spare-parts inventory** immediately, shifting waiting time from administrative quote queues onto the physical repair floor.

### Redesign C: SLA-Decoupled Dual Network (Concierge Replace-First vs. Load-Leveled Standard)
* **Failure Mode 1 (Refurbished Buffer Stockout Spiral):** After a product defect wave or marketing campaign, the Premium High-Value pool runs out of verified replacement stock; Premium claims suddenly fall back to the `96h` repair path, triggering mass SLA service-credit payouts.
* **Failure Mode 2 (Visibility Failure Turning Buffering into Complaints):** If proactive status notifications fail or lack concrete milestone updates, Standard customers interpret `36h–60h` load-leveling holds as lost claims, generating duplicate inquiries and `Customer complaint/reopen` spikes.
* **Bottleneck Migration:** Migrates from Monday `Fraud/risk review` queues to **reverse-logistics recovery and offline refurbishment (`Repair device -> Rework repair`)**, where unreturned defective Premium devices or the `12.5%` high-value repair rework rate starve the forward replacement pool.

  * *Downstream outcome:* 14-day cohort `Customer complaint/reopen` rate (%) and Premium `>24h` SLA credit incidence.
* **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
  * **GO to full high-value rollout:** Manual utilization remains **`<80%`**, pre-dispatch gate adds **`<12h` median**, trace fitness on piloted cohort exceeds **`92%`**, and high-value fast/replacement complaint rate drops from `18%–22%` into the **`<8%–10%` range**.
  * **PAUSE / ADJUST:** Manual utilization breaches **`82%`** for `>3` consecutive days, Monday `Fraud/risk` p90 exceeds **`40h`**, or Premium service-credit payouts rise **`>10%`** due to gate latency.

### Stage 2 (Weeks 5–8): Load-Leveled Standard Buffering & Priority Premium Pod (Core of Redesign C, Contingent on Capacity Pressure)
* **Sequencing Logic:** Scaling Stage 1 rules to *all* `35%` high-value claims (`+15%–35%` manual inflow) will likely crowd the `82%` reviewer utilization cliff before long-lead IT portals exist. Before scaling Stage 1 to 100%, introduce **Standard-segment status visibility + dynamic queue smoothing (`<72h` cap)** to create immediate headroom on Mondays and campaigns, paired with a small pilot buffer of replacement stock for Premium high-value claims.
* **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
  * **GO:** Standard low-risk complaint rate stays **`\le 5%–6%`** despite median cycle time rising to `36h–60h`, Monday queue variance drops **`>25%`**, and Premium high-value median cycle time holds **`<24h`**.
  * **STOP:** Standard abandonment or status-inquiry contacts rise **`>15%`**, or unreturned/unrepairable core devices exceed **`10%–15%`** of Premium replacements.

### Stage 3 (Weeks 9–16): A/B Test of Guided Evidence Portal & Pre-Priced Repair Matrix (Core of Redesign B)
* **Sequencing Logic:** Once compliance bleeding (**R1–R3**) and `EUR 410` shipment errors are contained by Stages 1–2, test the capital-intensive digital portal and pre-priced catalog via a **50/50 randomized A/B split** on incoming Standard and missing-evidence-prone defect categories (with a visible "Finish by Phone" rescue button).
* **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
  * **GO:** Repeat `Request missing evidence` loops fall by **`\ge 30%`**, `Manual -> Repair device` end-to-end median drops **`\ge 30h`**, and **total funnel abandonment (pre-intake start-to-submit drop-off + post-intake `Claim abandoned`)** increases by **`<2%`** relative to control.
  * **STOP:** Pre-intake drop-off rises **`>5%`** among non-digital segments, or mid-repair unquoted damage exceptions exceed **`10%`** of pre-priced repairs.

### Verifying Real Improvements vs. Shifted Downstream Problems
1. **14-Day Minimum Cohort Maturation Window:** Because `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` has a `p90` of **`180 hours` (`7.5 days`)** and alignment diagnostics show reopens occurring *after* `Quality/compliance close`, never declare success at case close. Track every pilot cohort for **at least 14 days post-close** to verify net cost and reopen reductions are real.
2. **Full-Funnel Denominator Auditing:** Measure abandonment starting from **web/phone session initiation (`Pre-Intake`)**, not `Intake claim`, ensuring portal friction does not disguise customer churn as lower `Claim abandoned` counts.
3. **End-to-End Trace & Token-Replay Re-Mining:** Re-run alignment diagnostics and precision checks biweekly to confirm that frontline staff are not inventing informal bypass paths (e.g., classifying >EUR 400 devices as accessories or closing claims prematurely to meet SLA clocks) that shift work into unlogged service channels.
