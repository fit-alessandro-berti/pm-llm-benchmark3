# Executive Advisory Report: Warranty Process Redesign

---

## 1. Current-Process Diagnosis

### Strategic Dilemma: Speed/Cost Paradox vs. Quality & Compliance
The executive team faces a classic operational paradox: the current high-volume fast path creates an illusion of high efficiency while generating substantial downstream financial and customer equity erosion. 

```
                                  +-----------------------+
                                  |     Intake Claim      |
                                  |        (5,000)        |
                                  +-----------+-----------+
                                              |

                   | (72% / 3,600 cases)                                 | (28% / 1,400 cases)
                   v                                                     v
      +-------------------------+                           +-------------------------+
      |  Auto Eligibility Screen |                           | Manual Eligibility Rev. |
      +------------+------------+                           +------------+------------+
                   | (70.8% / 2,550 cases)                               |
                   v                                                     |
        +---------------------+                                          |
        |    Fast Approval    |                                          |
        +----------+----------+                                          |
                   |                                                     |
       +-----------+-----------------------+                             |
       | (90.2% / 2,300 cases)             | (5.5% / 140 cases)          |
       v                                   v                             |
+---------------------+         +---------------------+                  |
| Dispatch Replacement|         | Complaint / Reopen  |                  |

           v                                             | (40.0 mean hrs)
+---------------------+                                  v
| Complaint / Reopen  |                       +-------------------------+

```

The process mining data shows that claims taking the automated fast path (**Intake claim $\rightarrow$ Auto eligibility screen $\rightarrow$ Fast approval $\rightarrow$ Dispatch replacement**) move swiftly (median execution under 4 hours) at an initial processing cost of just **EUR 0.20 per case**. However, this path triggers high downstream failure rates:
* **Customer Complaint / Reopen Rates:** 14.1% for Fast Approval cases overall, rising to **22% for Premium high-value devices** and **18% for Standard high-value devices**. By contrast, cases routed through **Repair quote $\rightarrow$ Customer approval $\rightarrow$ Repair device** suffer only a **3.3% complaint rate**.
* **Erosion of Cost Advantage:** While Fast Approval appears **31% cheaper upfront**, high complaint rates, incorrect part selections, reshipments (costing **EUR 410 per wrong high-value unit** and **EUR 95 per wrong low-risk unit**), and service credits erode this gap. The net cost advantage drops to just **9%**—even before accounting for long-term customer attrition.

### Key Process-Mining Diagnostic Evidence

#### 1. DFG Frequency & Flow Fragment Diagnostics
* **Total Volume & Routing:** Out of 5,000 cases entering **Intake claim**, 3,600 (72%) flow to **Auto eligibility screen**, which directly yields 2,550 **Fast approval** decisions. 
* **Downstream Rework Loops:** Fast approval sends 2,300 cases directly to **Dispatch replacement**. This link produces 260 **Dispatch replacement $\rightarrow$ Customer complaint/reopen** transitions and 140 **Fast approval $\rightarrow$ Customer complaint/reopen** transitions. 
* **Reopen Recalls:** 310 complaint cases loop back via **Customer complaint/reopen $\rightarrow$ Manual eligibility review**, while 120 go to the **Escalation board**. This demonstrates that skipping thorough upfront reviews merely defers manual work into a more costly, multi-touch recovery process.

#### 2. Performance DFG & Bottleneck Latencies
* **Upfront vs. Reopen Latency:** **Intake claim $\rightarrow$ Auto eligibility screen** takes a mean of 0.8 hours (median 0.5h, p90 1.8h). However, when errors occur, **Fast approval $\rightarrow$ Customer complaint/reopen** adds a mean of 72.0 hours (p90 150.0h), and **Dispatch replacement $\rightarrow$ Customer complaint/reopen** adds a mean of 96.0 hours (median 70.0h, p90 180.0h).
* **Evidence Collection Delay:** The path **Request missing evidence $\rightarrow$ Manual eligibility review** requires a mean of 34.0 hours (median 26.0h, p90 88.0h). 
* **Escalation Latency:** **Customer complaint/reopen $\rightarrow$ Escalation board** averages 54.0 hours (p90 130.0h), severely impacting time-sensitive Premium claims.

#### 3. Conformance & Alignment Diagnostics
* **Conformance Summary:** Token-based replay yields a log fitness of **0.91** with only **68% fitting traces**. Discovered model precision is low because low-frequency paths merge automated approvals, fraud checks, and repairs in ways unconstrained by policy.
* **Normative Violations:**
  * **Rule R1 Breach:** Claims over EUR 400 frequently receive **Fast approval** without passing **Manual eligibility review** or **Fraud/risk review**.
  * **Rule R2 Breach:** Alignments reveal **Fast approval** and **Dispatch replacement** occurring prior to evidence completeness on non-accessory high-value devices.
  * **Rule R3 Breach:** Alignments show move-on-log deviations where **Customer complaint/reopen** traces close without passing through **Manual eligibility review**.

#### 4. Rework & Waste Statistics
* **Repeated Loops:** **Request missing evidence** occurs at least twice in **26%** of cases that enter evidence collection.
* **Repair Quality Issues:** **Rework repair** occurs in **12.5%** of repaired high-value devices compared to **4.0%** of low-risk devices, pointing to technical diagnostic gaps prior to repair execution.
* **Abandonment Waste:** 390 claims end in **Claim abandoned** (320 directly from **Request missing evidence $\rightarrow$ Claim abandoned**), representing a major drop-off point caused by poor friction management during evidence intake.

#### 5. Segment Discrepancies & Resource Constraints
* **Unclear Defect / Missing Evidence Segment:** Accounts for **22% of total volume** with a median cycle time of **140 hours**, driving the highest abandonment rates.
* **Resource Capacity Constraints:** Manual eligibility review relies on 7 reviewers; when queue utilization exceeds 82%, waiting times spike exponentially (**Manual eligibility review $\rightarrow$ Repair quote** averages 22.0 hours, p90 60.0h). Fraud/risk review (2 specialists @ EUR 52/h) forms a critical bottleneck on Mondays and post-campaign periods, delaying risk checks (**Auto screen $\rightarrow$ Fraud review** p90 is 28.0h).

## 2. Redesign A: Segment-Driven Strict Routing & Tiered Fast-Track

### Process Flow Narrative
Redesign A removes high-value devices from the single-stream automated path and establishes strict, rule-governed routing based on risk level and customer tier at the intake point.

```
                           +------------------------+
                           |  Intake Claim (5,000)  |
                           +-----------+------------+
                                       |
           +---------------------------+---------------------------+
           |                                                       |
[Low-Risk Accessories (43%)]                             [High-Value Devices (35%)]
           v                                                       v
+-----------------------+                               +-----------------------+
| Segment Auto-Screen   |                               | Mandatory Risk/Manual |
| (Rules Engine)        |                               | Eligibility Filter    |
+-----------+-----------+                               +-----------+-----------+
            |                                                               |
            v                                                               v
+-----------------------+                               +-----------------------+
| Instant Dispatch      |                               | Technical Diagnostic  |
| (Rules R1/R2 Enforced)|                               | & Verified Dispatch   |
+-----------------------+                               +-----------------------+
```

1. **Intake & Automated Classification:** Claims enter a segment-aware rules engine.
2. **Path 1 — Low-Risk Accessories (43% of cases):** Routed to an automated fast-track. Claims meeting automated criteria receive instant approval and dispatch. Standard cases require a basic digital receipt validation step before approval.
3. **Path 2 — High-Value Devices (35% of cases):** Bypass fast approval entirely. Claims over EUR 400 are routed to **Manual eligibility review** or **Fraud/risk review**, enforcing Normative Rule R1.
4. **Path 3 — Unclear Defect / Missing Evidence (22% of cases):** Routed to a dynamic evidence intake workflow before screening.

### Targeted Segments, Paths, & Activities
* **Target Segments:** Premium High-Value (14%), Standard High-Value (21%), Premium Low-Risk (18%), Standard Low-Risk (25%).
* **Targeted Paths:** **Intake claim $\rightarrow$ Auto eligibility screen $\rightarrow$ Fast approval** and **Manual eligibility review $\rightarrow$ Fraud/risk review**.
* **Targeted Activities:** Automated eligibility screening, fraud/risk routing, and replacement dispatch.

### Mechanism of Improvement
* **Compliance:** Hard-coded workflow rules force claims over EUR 400 through manual or fraud reviews before approval, eliminating Rule R1 and R2 violations.
* **Quality & Expense Reduction:** Preventing unverified fast approvals on high-value devices avoids incorrect replacement dispatches (saving EUR 410 per incident). This is expected to lower high-value complaint rates from 18–22% toward the 5–6% baseline seen on reviewed paths.
* **Targeted Speed:** Low-risk accessory claims maintain rapid turnaround times (median <8 hours) without creating excessive financial risk for the business.

### Intentional Sacrifices & Unresolved Issues
* **Manual Bottleneck Pressure:** Transferring high-value cases to manual review increases inflow by 15–35%, driving reviewer utilization past the 82% threshold and increasing manual queue wait times.
* **Cycle Time Inflation for High-Value Devices:** Average cycle time for high-value claims increases upfront due to mandatory manual reviews.
* **Evidence Friction Unaddressed:** Does not resolve friction or customer drop-off within the missing evidence workflow (22% of total cases).

## 3. Redesign B: Interactive Evidence Portal & Pre-Dispatch Verification Gate

### Process Flow Narrative
Redesign B focuses on evidence completeness prior to review, combined with physical verification before replacement dispatch.

```
                           +------------------------+
                           |  Intake Claim (5,000)  |
                           +-----------+------------+
                                       |
                                       v
                           +------------------------+
                           | Dynamic Smart Portal   |
                           | (Serial & Photo Gate)  |
                           +-----------+------------+
                                       |
                                       v
                           +------------------------+
                           | Auto/Manual Screen     |
                           +-----------+------------+
                                       |
                                       v
                           +------------------------+
                           | Pre-Dispatch Gate      |
                           | (2-Min Physical Verification)
                           +-----------+------------+
                                       |
                                       v
                           +------------------------+
                           | Verified Dispatch /    |
                           | Close                  |
                           +------------------------+
```

1. **Interactive Evidence Portal Intake:** All incoming claims pass through a digital intake portal that validates serial numbers, defect descriptions, and photo evidence before initial screening.
2. **Screening & Processing:** Verified complete claims move to **Auto eligibility screen** or **Manual eligibility review**. Incomplete submissions receive real-time system guidance, preventing manual rework loops.
3. **Pre-Dispatch Verification Gate:** Approved replacement dispatches pass through a 2-minute warehouse verification gate. Logistics staff verify part SKUs against the diagnosed fault code before releasing shipments.
4. **Fulfillment & Closure:** Verified items are dispatched and closed immediately, reducing post-close complaint processing.

### Targeted Segments, Paths, & Activities
* **Target Segments:** Unclear Defect / Missing Evidence (22%), Standard High-Value (21%), Standard Low-Risk (25%).
* **Targeted Paths:** **Request missing evidence $\rightarrow$ Manual eligibility review** and **Dispatch replacement $\rightarrow$ Customer complaint/reopen**.
* **Targeted Activities:** Intake evidence collection, replacement fulfillment, and post-dispatch quality validation.

### Mechanism of Improvement
* **Evidence Rework Reduction:** Dynamic evidence intake addresses the root cause of repeated evidence requests (present in 26% of evidence-bound cases), cutting missing evidence cycle times down from 140 hours.
* **Misfire Prevention:** The pre-dispatch verification gate intercepts incorrect replacement shipments, directly addressing the long-tail complaint pattern (**Dispatch replacement $\rightarrow$ Customer complaint/reopen**, mean 96.0h). This reduces replacement error costs (EUR 410 high-value / EUR 95 low-risk).

### Intentional Sacrifices & Unresolved Issues
* **Friction & Abandonment Risk:** Upfront evidence requirements may increase drop-off among customers with low digital literacy, potentially increasing claim abandonment beyond the 390-case baseline.
* **Dispatch Speed Impact:** Introducing a physical verification gate adds 6 to 18 hours to total dispatch turnaround time.
* **Resource Cost:** Gate verification requires additional logistics coordination and operational oversight.

## 4. Redesign C: Premium VIP Fast-Track Pod & Triage-Based Repair Routing

### Process Flow Narrative
Redesign C establishes segment-based operational routing, prioritizing high-value Premium customers through dedicated cross-functional teams while restructuring the repair process for Standard customers.

```
                           +------------------------+
                           |  Intake Claim (5,000)  |
                           +-----------+------------+
                                       |
           +---------------------------+---------------------------+
           |                                                       |
[Premium Segment (32%)]                                   [Standard Segment (46%)]
           v                                                       v
+-----------------------+                               +-----------------------+
| Express VIP Pod       |                               | Standard Triage       |
| (Dedicated Cross-     |                               | (Automated Pricing &  |
| Functional Team)      |                               | Batch Technical Audit)|
+-----------+-----------+                               +-----------+-----------+
            |                                                       |
            v                                                       v
+-----------------------+                               +-----------------------+
| Direct Resolution &   |                               | Standard Fulfillment /|
| Fast-Track Escalation |                               | Controlled Repair     |
+-----------------------+                               +-----------------------+
```

1. **Intake & Customer Segment Triage:** Claims are separated by customer tier (**Premium** vs. **Standard**) at intake.
2. **Premium Express Pod:** Premium claims (32% total; 18% low-risk, 14% high-value) route directly to an integrated VIP team combining eligibility, fraud, and technical specialists. This team handles cases end-to-end, bypassing general queues and the standard daily **Escalation board**.
3. **Standard Triage & Automated Repair Quotes:** Standard claims move to an automated repair workflow. Pre-approved catalog pricing eliminates technician waiting times for part price confirmation (**Manual eligibility review $\rightarrow$ Repair quote**, mean 22.0h).
4. **Quality Inspection & Escalation Management:** High-value repairs undergo mandatory technical quality audits to prevent rework (**Rework repair** baseline 12.5%). Standard escalations move to a decentralized review pool.

### Targeted Segments, Paths, & Activities
* **Target Segments:** Premium Low-Risk (18%), Premium High-Value (14%), Standard High-Value (21%).
* **Targeted Paths:** **Manual eligibility review $\rightarrow$ Repair quote**, **Customer complaint/reopen $\rightarrow$ Escalation board**, and **Quality check $\rightarrow$ Rework repair**.
* **Targeted Activities:** Manual review queue management, part pricing approvals, repair quality control, and escalation processing.

### Mechanism of Improvement
* **SLA Protection:** Dedicated resources ensure Premium claims stay within target response windows (under 24 hours), minimizing service credit liabilities.
* **Repair Bottleneck Removal:** Automated part pricing cuts latency in the **Manual eligibility review $\rightarrow$ Repair quote** path.
* **First-Time Fix Improvement:** Quality inspections for high-value repairs reduce the 12.5% rework rate, decreasing secondary customer complaints.

### Intentional Sacrifices & Unresolved Issues
* **Higher Operating Expenses:** Maintaining dedicated VIP staff increases fixed labor costs across specialist roles (EUR 34–52/h).
* **Standard Queue Latency:** Prioritizing Premium claims may extend wait times for Standard customers when overall volume spikes.
* **Higher Fraud/Risk Exposure:** Auto-approved repair pricing under set thresholds accepts minor fraud leakage to maximize processing speed.

## 6. Failure Modes and Displaced Bottlenecks

```
                               CURRENT PROCESS
              [Manual Review Queue] ----> Spikes >82% Utilization
                                      |
                                      v
                                 REDESIGN A
              [Manual Review Queue] ----> OVERLOADED (Inflow +15-35%)
              * Migration: Eligibility queue expands; high-value cycle time increases.
                                      |
                                      v
                                 REDESIGN B
              [Warehouse Gate]      ----> NEW BOTTLENECK (Physical Inspection)
              * Migration: Logistics processing delay; staging queue expands.
                                      |
                                      v
                                 REDESIGN C
              [Standard Queue]      ----> LATENCY SPIKE (Resource Depletion)
              * Migration: Standard manual review wait times increase.
```

### Redesign A: Segment-Driven Strict Routing & Tiered Fast-Track

#### Failure Modes
1. **Manual Review Queue Collapse:** Routing all high-value devices to manual review increases queue volume by 15–35%. Because the current staff of 7 reviewers already experiences queue growth at >82% utilization, this inflow increase can cause manual review times to jump from 7.5 hours to over 48 hours.
2. **Segment Misclassification Gaming:** If rules rely on customer-entered defect types to determine routing, customers may intentionally select low-risk accessory codes for high-value items to bypass manual review, undermining Rule R1 enforcement.

#### Displaced Bottleneck
* **Migration Point:** The system bottleneck shifts from downstream complaint processing to the **Manual eligibility review** stage. Queue depth increases significantly, extending wait times for standard high-value claims (**Manual eligibility review $\rightarrow$ Repair quote**).

---

### Redesign B: Interactive Evidence Portal & Pre-Dispatch Verification Gate

#### Failure Modes
1. **Digital Intake Customer Abandonment:** Requiring detailed photo evidence and serial validation via the dynamic portal may increase customer friction, causing drop-off rates among less digitally literate users to rise above the current baseline of 390 abandoned cases.
2. **Pre-Dispatch Warehouse Staging Congestion:** If warehouse staff cannot complete physical verification checks within 2 minutes during peak dispatch windows, staging queues will back up, delaying shipping carriers and creating fulfillment delays.

#### Displaced Bottleneck
* **Migration Point:** Processing queues shift from customer service back-offices to physical logisitics. The main operational bottleneck becomes the **Dispatch replacement pre-check gate**, particularly during Monday morning processing peaks.

---

### Redesign C: Premium VIP Fast-Track Pod & Triage-Based Repair Routing

#### Failure Modes
1. **Standard Segment Resource Starvation:** Ring-fencing top reviewers and technicians for the Premium VIP Pod reduces capacity for standard cases, causing standard high-value claim cycle times to exceed 140 hours.
2. **Auto-Approved Repair Cost Overruns:** Automatically approving repair quotes below preset price thresholds without technician validation may lead to cost inflation from inaccurate part estimates or vendor overcharging.

#### Displaced Bottleneck
* **Migration Point:** Operational friction shifts to the **Standard Manual Eligibility & Technical Queue**, where reduced staffing levels create longer wait times for non-premium customers.

## 7. Recommended Experiment Sequence

To manage risk and validate theoretical operational gains, implement a phased rollout schedule.

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 1: PILOT TESTING (Weeks 1-4)                                                |
| - Scope: Premium High-Value (14%) & Unclear Evidence (22%)                        |
| - Deploy: Redesign A Rules + Redesign B Smart Portal                             |
+-----------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 2: TARGETED SCALE & PRE-DISPATCH (Weeks 5-8)                                |
| - Scope: Add Standard High-Value (21%)                                            |
| - Deploy: Redesign B Pre-Dispatch Gate + Redesign C Repair Triage                 |
+-----------------------------------------+-----------------------------------------+
                                          |
                                          v
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PHASE 3: FULL SYSTEM INTEGRATION (Weeks 9-12)                                     |
| - Scope: 100% Total Claim Volume                                                  |
| - Deploy: Integrated VIP Pods + Automated Escalations                             |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### Stage 1: Initial Pilot (Weeks 1–4)
* **Target Scope:** 100% of **Premium High-Value** claims (14% of total volume) and a 20% random sample of **Unclear Defect / Missing Evidence** claims.
* **Deployed Mechanisms:** Combine Redesign A (strict R1/R2 screening rules) with Redesign B (dynamic evidence intake portal).
* **Key Operational Metrics:** 
  * 30-day Post-Close Complaint Rate
  * Manual Eligibility Review Queue Utilization (%)
  * Portal Drop-off / Abandonment Rate
  * First-Time Evidence Completeness Rate (%)
* **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
  * *Proceed:* Premium complaint rate drops below **8%** (from 22%), and missing evidence rework loops fall by at least **30%** without portal abandonment rising above **10%**.
  * *Pause/Pivot:* Manual review queue utilization exceeds **90%**, or overall portal abandonment increases by more than **15%**.

---

### Stage 2: Expanded Scale & Warehouse Pilot (Weeks 5–8)
* **Target Scope:** Expand to include **Standard High-Value** claims (21% of volume) and introduce the pre-dispatch gate at the primary logistics center.
* **Deployed Mechanisms:** Roll out Redesign B's Pre-Dispatch Verification Gate and Redesign C's pre-approved repair pricing catalog.
* **Key Operational Metrics:**
  * Incorrect Replacement Part Rate (Target: <1%)
  * Mean Repair Quote Generation Latency (Target: <10 hours, down from 22h)
  * Escalation Board Referral Rate (Target: <1.5%, down from 2.4%)
* **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
  * *Proceed:* Pre-dispatch verification catches >90% of part mismatches while adding less than **12 hours** to total dispatch time; repair quote turnaround drops below **12 hours**.
  * *Pause/Pivot:* Dispatch gate processing times exceed **24 hours**, or vendor pricing discrepancies exceed budget allocations by >5%.

---

### Stage 3: Full Enterprise Integration (Weeks 9–12)
* **Target Scope:** All claim segments (100% volume; 5,000 cases/period).
* **Deployed Mechanisms:** Full implementation of segment-based routing, the VIP Express Pod for Premium claims, and dynamic evidence intake across all channels.

### Verification Strategy: Preventing Downstream Cost/Defect Shifting
To ensure operational adjustments resolve issues rather than shifting costs downstream, track the following metrics throughout rollout:

```
                            +-----------------------+
                            |  HOLISTIC COST MATRIX |
                            +-----------+-----------+
                                        |
           +----------------------------+----------------------------+
           |                                                         |
           v                                                         v
+-----------------------+                               +-----------------------+
| Upfront Savings Check |                               | Secondary Expense Check|
| - Initial Processing  |                               | - Service Credit Exposure
| - Labor Cost / Case   |                               | - Return Logistics    |
|                       |                               | - Part Reshipment     |
+-----------+-----------+                               +-----------+-----------+
            |                                                       |
            +---------------------------+---------------------------+
                                        |
                                        v
                            +-----------------------+
                            | Net True Cost / Claim |
                            |  (Must trend downward)|
                            +-----------------------+
```

1. **30-Day Post-Close Reopen Tracking:** Track customer contact and claim reopen rates for 30 days post-closure to ensure quick closures do not mask unresolved product issues.
2. **Net Unit Cost Calculation:** Measure total cost per claim using a combined expense formula:
   $$\text{Net Cost} = \text{Processing Labor} + \text{Fulfillment Cost} + \text{Reshipment Cost} + \text{Service Credits} + \text{Scrap/Rework}$$
   *Success Criterion:* Fast-path processing must maintain a true net cost advantage of **>25%** over fully reviewed paths (up from the current baseline of 9%).
3. **Physical Inventory Return Audits:** Perform monthly audits matching returned defective hardware against logged failure codes to verify diagnostic accuracy and track underlying supplier quality issues.
