# 3. Supporting and contradicting cases

## QA
- **Support:** A03 (appointment 08:20 honored at 08:20); A02A04A05A06A07A08 follows arrival order (08:04, 08:13, 08:21, 08:29, 08:35, 08:40); A06 starts near appointment 09:15 (late by 5 min due to non-preemptive A05).  
- **Contradict:** A04 (priority 1) delayed behind A02 (priority 3) and A03 (appointment). A08 (priority 1) served last. **Explanation:** Non-preemptive FIFO; no interruption mechanism is recorded; calibration gap (08:52–08:58) physically prevents earlier A04 start if A02 is running.

## QB
- **Support:** B02 (priority 1) interrupts B01 at 09:16; B01 resumes after B02; B03 served before B05 if B05 not visible by 09:48; B05 served before B04 (priority 1 jumping lower-priority arrival).  
- **Contradict:** B01 resumes over B03 (priority 2) and B06 (priority 2); B07 appointment 10:30 not honored until 10:56. **Explanation:** Interruption reasons are operational (“urgent arrival”, “dispatcher reassignment”), not automatic priority logic; 2-min scan delay could hide B05; B01’s chunks may be required to avoid restart cost; B07’s delay is consistent with FIFO backlog.

## QC
- **Support:** C02C03C04C05C06 follow arrival (10:0310:0510:0710:1110:25); C01 explicitly deferred; single resource eliminates routing choice.  
- **Contradict:** C05 (priority 1, deadline 10:50) starts at 10:54 after C04; C07 (deadline 12:00) starts after C01 (13:00). **Explanation:** FIFO dominates over priority/deadline; deadlines are “soft” (C05 note says “already at risk when selected”); C01 deferral is documented by note, suggesting a deliberate longest-job/oldest-job exception.

## QD
- **Support:** D-M sequence by arrival; D-F FIFO (D02, D03); D06 starts at appointment 11:30; D05 starts at 12:20 to avoid D-F meeting interruption; D07 only eligible for D-X.  
- **Contradict:** D08 (priority 1) after D04; D10 (priority 1) after D07. **Explanation:** Skill routing and per-resource FIFO dominate; D08 is mechanical only, so it waits for D-M’s FIFO turn; D10 is electrical and could go to D-F, but D-F is reserved for D05 and unavailable 12:00–12:20, so D-X handles it after skill-constrained D07.

# 4. Counterfactual discrimination tests

## Episode 1 — QB at ~09:48 (B01 interrupted; B03 starts; B05 possibly unseen)
- **Competing explanations:**  
  - (A) *Strict preemptive priority*: Once B05 (pri1) is visible, it should preempt B01 and be served before B03 (pri2). Next-job = B05.  
  - (B) *FIFO of visible jobs + resume-interrupted rule*: B05 not yet scanned (arrival 09:46 + up to 2 min delay); B03 (arr 09:20) is earliest visible; B01 resumes/interrupted only by dispatcher reassignment. Next-job = B03.  
- **Discriminating observable:** Exact queue-entry timestamp for B05 relative to 09:48. If entry >09:48, (B) is validated; if entry 09:48, (A) fails and a hybrid (dispatcher discretion / resumption cost) is required.

## Episode 2 — QC at 10:32 (C04 starts; C05 waits)
- **Competing explanations:**  
  - (A) *FIFO by arrival*: C04 arrived 10:07 < C05 10:11, so C04 gets next slot. Next-job = C04.  
  - (B) *Priority / deadline urgency*: C05 is pri1 and deadline 10:50 (18 min away); C04 is pri3, dl 14:30. Next-job = C05.  
- **Discriminating observable:** Dispatcher’s estimated remaining time for C04 at 10:32 plus C05’s recorded queue-entry time. If C05 entered at 10:13 (after scan delay) and C04 was already in service, FIFO is validated; if C05 was visible at 10:11 and dispatcher still chose 22-min C04 over 40-min C05, then deadline/priority is not operational and FIFO (or SPT) is dominant.

# 6. Additional event data needed

Prioritized collection plan, tailored to observed ambiguities:

1. **True queue-entry timestamp** (separate from arrival scan). Needed because 2-min delay makes priority/deadline ordering uncertain in QB, QA, and QC.  
2. **Dispatch-decision timestamp + eligibility set** (list of waiting jobs with visible priority, skill, appointment, deadline, current estimated remaining time). Needed to compare observed choice against predictions at each interval, not just start order.  
3. **Resource-state log** (calibration, meeting, prior-job end, break) with expected return time. Needed to separate forced gaps from discretionary scheduling (QA calib, D-F meeting, C1 end).  
4. **Appointment tolerance / policy version** (hard exact time vs ± window; is appointment preemptive?). Needed because QA A03 is exact, A06 is late; QB B07 is missed.  
5. **Estimated remaining time for interrupted jobs** (e.g., B01 at 09:16 and 09:48). Needed to test whether resumption is cost-driven or policy-driven.  
6. **Preemption reason and dispatcher authorization** (operator ID / rule invoked). Needed because QB interruption reasons are qualitative; without them, “urgent arrival” is an external label, not a derived mechanism.  
7. **Skill-match matrix and resource qualification** (full qualification for combined skills like fw+elec). Needed to confirm QD routing and explain why D05 waits.  
8. **SLA / deadline-at-risk flag at decision time**. Needed to test if QC switches policy when C05’s 10:50 deadline approaches.

# 7. Final synthesis

**Most identifiable:** **QD** — skill-based routing is almost deterministic (D-M = mechanical only; D-F = fw/elec; D-X = mech+elec). The per-resource sequences match arrival order, and the meeting-avoidance for D05 is a forced scheduling event, not ambiguity.

**Least identifiable:** **QB** — the long interrupted job (B01), external interruption reasons, and plausible scan-delay for B05 mean that “preemptive priority” and “FIFO with resumption” are observationally equivalent for several decisions. No single discipline predicts both the B01 chunks and the B05/B03 ordering without untestable assumptions.

**Most dangerous claimed policy to infer from these observations alone:** **Strict non-preemptive (or preemptive) priority** — QA’s A04 and A08 are priority 1 but served after lower-priority arrivals; QB’s B05 (priority 1) may or may not jump B03; QC’s C05 (priority 1, closest deadline) is served after C04 and misses its deadline. If management claims “priority scheduling,” the evidence directly contradicts it in three of four queues. The safer cross-queue conclusion is: **FIFO sequencing within resource and skill constraints, with non-preemptive execution, appointment insertion at exact times when feasible, and dispatcher-driven interruption/resumption for urgent or long jobs** — not a pure documented rule, but an undocumented hybrid in which arrival order and resource availability dominate over priority and deadlines.
