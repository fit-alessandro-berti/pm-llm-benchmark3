## 1. Duplicate-handling decision tree

Compare events on source identity first, and on restored source meaning wherever harmonization changed a verb. Shared objects or a shared timestamp never enter the tree as proof of sameness.

**No candidate.** Unique `source_event_key`, no reconciliation hit, no shared business document, no derivation link. **Retain unchanged.** This covers e01, e02, e12, and e17. e10 is also not a duplicate of e09: the activity already differs, and carrier sequence places it after acceptance.

**Exact technical duplicate.** Same `(source, source_event_key)`, or equal `payload_hash` together with the same source, activity, timestamp, and object set. **Drop** the later `ingested_at` copy only. Ingestion time is used here as technical survivorship of identical bytes, not as business order. Lineage keeps the dropped key. This log has unique keys and unique payload hashes, so the leaf removes nothing.

**Same real-world occurrence, several systems.** An external reconciliation report, or one authoritative business key, says the rows are one occurrence. Object sets agree on the discriminating object (the Item for a pick, the Invoice for an issuance). **Merge attributes and relations** into one canonical eid. Enrichment is kept. A true value conflict is kept namespaced, not overwritten. e03/e04 (one I1 scan; WMS added location) and e11/e16 (one issuance of V1) end here.

**Summary derived from a detailed event.** The later system’s row is a message-shaped projection: fewer objects, summary hash, no discriminating child object. Merge only when the summary is a proven 1:1 projection of exactly one detail event and its objects are a subset of that detail. Otherwise **quarantine for review**: leave both rows in the working log, block automated merge, and mark the summary with a qualifier. e07 is an explicit maybe (one pack transition, or a distinct ERP status). e15 posts an invoice-level balance and carries neither M1 nor M2, so it is not a 1:1 image of e13 or of e14.

**Harmonization collision.** Stored labels match only because a map collapsed different source verbs, while the object sets happen to match. **Retain both with refined labels.** e08 remains warehouse release; e09 remains carrier acceptance.

**Concurrent same-label events with different discriminating objects.** Same activity and timestamp, but Item or Payment differs. **Retain both unchanged.** e05 stays beside the I1 pick. e14 stays beside the M1 capture. Higher objects O1, P1, and V1 are allowed to be shared.

**Unresolved candidate.** Evidence does not separate “second observation” from “second business step,” or a summary may cover several details at once. **Quarantine for review** in the sense above: retained, unmodified except an explicit qualifier, and ineligible for drop or merge until a named rule version says otherwise.

## 3. Timestamp-tie ordering rules

The business order is a partial order. A total order is produced only so that miners which require a sequence have a stable input. That total order is reproducible. It does not measure what happened inside a tied second.

Rules, in precedence:

1. **Domain constraints (semantic).** Stated precedence in the source facts: e12 before e13 on M1; e09 before e10; a derived summary does not precede the detail it was generated from (e06 before e07, and both captures before e15), applied only when that reading is accepted. A constraint that contradicts a strict inequality of original timestamps is rejected and quarantined.
2. **Within-source sequence (semantic inside one source).** `source_seq` orders events that share `source`. Scanner 551 (the scan merged into e03) before scanner 552 (e05). WMS 100 before 110 before 120. PSP 400 before 401 before 402. ERP 40 before 41. Sequences are never compared across sources. After a merge, every contributing sequence is kept, and each comparison names the source it used.
3. **Lifecycle on a shared discriminating object (semantic, whitelist only).** Pick Item before Pack Package when they share an Item. Authorize Payment before Capture Payment on the same Payment. No global ranking of all activity names is inferred from O1.
4. **Source-specific semantics (semantic).** Ordering uses restored verbs. Warehouse release and carrier acceptance are different activities for ordering even though the stored label matched.
5. **Ingestion time (deterministic only, and disqualified on this log).** `ingested_at` is not a business clock. Fact 9 says ingest order need not match business order. It is recorded and then not used, including as a tie-break, because it sometimes agrees with the facts (e15 ingested at 12:01, e10 just after e09) and sometimes invents a path (billing e16 at 12:00:04 sits after the captures).
6. **Stable fallback (deterministic only).** Remaining ties break by canonical eid ascending. The basis column says `eid-fallback`. This layer does not authorize a conformance rule.

Semantic rules stop at a partial order. Rules 5 and 6 only totalize what is still incomparable. Cross-cluster order is the original timestamp: 09:00, then 09:05, then 10:00, then 10:15, then 12:00, then e17 on the next day. Tie-breaking is forbidden from moving an event across a real timestamp gap.

Adding microseconds before the semantic order freezes whatever row order the frame happens to have. If that order is ingest order, the 12:00 cluster becomes e11, e12, e13, e14, e16, e15, and the synthetic clock reports a positive sojourn on Capture → Issue Invoice → balance summary. A later performance measure treats that microsecond gap as duration. The same function is safe only after `order_rank` exists: it may encode that rank for algorithms that read only `ocel:timestamp`. `original_timestamp` stays the performance clock. Any duration shorter than the native one-second resolution is synthetic and is excluded from service-time statistics. A guard rejects the shifted log if any pair with unequal original timestamps changes order.

**10:00, after e04 is merged into e03.** Intended: e03 ≺ e05 ≺ e06 ≺ e07. Bases: scanner sequence; Item lifecycle into e06; weak derivation for e07. Admissible alternatives: e03 parallel to e05, if scanner sequence is demoted to a device-key fallback, with both still before e06; and e06 parallel to e07, if the ERP row is the same transition rather than a successor. Totalizing either parallel pair creates an Order-level directly-follows edge that the partial order does not contain.

**10:15.** Hard partial order: e09 ≺ e10. Intended total order used by the conservative log, with a weak lifecycle edge: e08 ≺ e09 ≺ e10. Any total order that keeps e09 before e10 is admissible, including e09 ≺ e10 ≺ e08. The weak edge is stored as weak so conformance does not require release-before-acceptance.

**12:00, after e16 is merged into e11.** Hard or within-source: e12 ≺ e13 ≺ e14, both captures ≺ e15, and e11 ≺ e15 on ERP sequence. Soft: e11 ≺ e12. Intended total order: e11 ≺ e12 ≺ e13 ≺ e14 ≺ e15. If the soft edge is declined, e12 ≺ e13 ≺ e14 ≺ e11 ≺ e15 is also admissible and would show payment before invoice. eid order happens to match the intended total order on this cluster; that coincidence is not evidence. e13 ≺ e14 is PSP submission order, not a business rule that M2 depends on M1.

## 4. Conservative cleaning pipeline

This is the minimum-change working log.

1. Deep-copy `ocel_raw`. All later writes hit the copy.
2. Copy `ocel:timestamp` to `original_timestamp` and `ocel:activity` to `original_activity`. Keep `source` and `source_event_key`.
3. Run the candidate pre-check (section 6) and classify groups. Do not call a library duplicate routine on those groups.
4. Drop nothing. Exact-key and exact-hash groups are empty.
5. Merge only e04 into e03, and e16 into e11, with namespaced attributes, union of relations, and both sequences retained.
6. Refine labels in place: e08 warehouse release, e09 carrier acceptance, e07 ERP pack status, e15 ERP balance summary. Refinement changes the working activity and leaves `original_activity`.
7. Write quarantine rows for (e06, e07) and for e15 against {e13, e14}. The events stay.
8. Build `order_rank` and `order_basis` from the partial order, then eid fallback inside a tied second.
9. Sort with `pm4py.ocel_sort_by_additional_column` on `order_rank`.
10. Only then call `pm4py.ocel_add_index_based_timedelta`, with the shift guard, leaving `original_timestamp` untouched.
11. Call `pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates` only on the subset of events whose `(activity, timestamp)` is unique, or whose group passed the exact-technical test. On this log that call removes zero rows. e13/e14 and e03/e05 are withheld from that subset because they share a label and a second but not an object set.
12. Validate structural and semantic integrity.

**Retained events: 15** (17 minus e04 minus e16). e05, e07, e08, e09, e14, and e15 all remain. Relations: raw has 44 E2O rows; both merges are idempotent unions, so 3 + 2 rows disappear and **39** remain. Eight objects remain, including I2 and M2.

## 5. Aggressive alternatives

**Evidence-based semantic merge.** Start from the conservative log and additionally merge e07 into e06 under the “one pack transition” reading. e08/e09 stay split. e13, e14, and e15 stay split. **14 events, 37 E2O rows** (e07’s two relations are already on e06). The likely damage is the loss of the ERP pack acknowledgement: a stuck interface between WMS and ERP becomes invisible, and a conformance check that ERP must confirm packing can no longer fail. The gain is removal of a possible false Pack→Pack loop on O1 and P1. That trade is not taken in the working log because the brief itself leaves the pair open.

**Mechanical PM4Py merge.** On a separate deep copy of the raw log, `pm4py.ocel_merge_duplicates(..., have_common_object=True)` clusters equal activity and timestamp into connected components whenever two events share any object. Components on this log are {e03, e04, e05}, {e06, e07}, {e08, e09}, {e11, e16}, and {e13, e14, e15}. **10 events, 28 E2O rows** if relations are unioned and deduplicated: one pick {O1, I1, I2, P1}, one pack with the same four, one dispatch {O1, P1, D1}, one invoice {O1, V1}, one capture {O1, V1, M1, M2}, plus e01, e02, e10, e12, e17. Likely damage: I2’s pick ceases to be its own event, warehouse release and carrier acceptance become one instant, and the two partial payments plus the ERP balance become one capture. e05 and e14 survive only as objects hanging on someone else’s event, which violates the constraint that those occurrences remain distinct events.

`have_common_object=True` does not protect e13 and e14. The flag asks whether a shared object exists. O1 and V1 exist on both rows, so the predicate is true, the two events fall in one component, and M2 is unioned onto M1’s capture. It never requires the Payment objects to be equal. e15 joins the same component through O1 and V1 even though it has no Payment object. On this file the flag also fails to split the pick component: e05 shares O1 and P1 with e03, so transitivity pulls I2 into the I1 scan. `have_common_object=False` would not rescue the file either, because every dangerous group is already connected through O1.

A global `ocel_drop_duplicates`, if implemented as “same activity, timestamp, and object set, keep one row,” would drop one of e03/e04 (attribute-lossy but close to the truth), one of e08/e09 (wrong), and one of e11/e16 (identity right, attributes lost). It would leave e13, e14, and e15 because their object sets differ. Dropping is the wrong tool for a verified multi-source observation: the second system’s attributes are the reason the row was integrated.

## 6. PM4Py-oriented Python implementation

```python
import copy
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

EID, ACT, TS = "ocel:eid", "ocel:activity", "ocel:timestamp"
OID, OTYPE = "ocel:oid", "ocel:type"

# Edges used only when both eids still exist. "hard" participates in the partial order.
# "soft" totalizes only when it does not contradict a hard edge; basis stays soft.
EDGES = [
    ("e03", "e05", "hard", "within-source:SCANNER"),
    ("e03", "e06", "hard", "lifecycle:Item-and-WMS-seq"),
    ("e05", "e06", "hard", "lifecycle:Item"),
    ("e06", "e07", "soft", "derived-after:WMS-to-ERP"),
    ("e08", "e09", "soft", "lifecycle-weak:release-before-acceptance"),
    ("e09", "e10", "hard", "domain:acceptance-before-handover"),
    ("e11", "e15", "hard", "within-source:ERP"),
    ("e11", "e12", "soft", "lifecycle-weak:invoice-before-auth"),
    ("e12", "e13", "hard", "domain:auth-before-capture:M1"),
    ("e13", "e14", "hard", "within-source:PSP"),
    ("e13", "e15", "hard", "derived-after:payments-before-balance"),
    ("e14", "e15", "hard", "derived-after:payments-before-balance"),
]

def snapshot(ocel):
    return copy.deepcopy(ocel)

def normalize(ocel):
    ev = ocel.events
    ren = {"eid": EID, "activity": ACT, "timestamp": TS}
    ev.rename(columns={k: v for k, v in ren.items() if k in ev.columns and v not in ev.columns}, inplace=True)
    if "original_timestamp" not in ev.columns:
        ev["original_timestamp"] = ev[TS]
    if "original_activity" not in ev.columns:
        ev["original_activity"] = ev[ACT]
    for col in ("source", "source_event_key", "source_seq", "payload_hash", "ingested_at"):
        if col not in ev.columns:
            raise KeyError(col)
    return ocel

def objects_of(ocel, eid):
    rel = ocel.relations
    sub = rel.loc[rel[EID] == eid, [OID, OTYPE]]
    return set(map(tuple, sub.to_numpy()))

def candidate_precheck(ocel):
    """Groups a blind activity+timestamp merge would consider. Inspect before any library call."""
    rows = []
    ev = ocel.events
    for (act, ts), g in ev.groupby([ACT, TS], dropna=False):
        eids = list(g[EID])
        if len(eids) < 2:
            continue
        sets = {e: objects_of(ocel, e) for e in eids}
        rows.append({
            "activity": act,
            "timestamp": ts,
            "eids": eids,
            "equal_object_sets": len({frozenset(v) for v in sets.values()}) == 1,
            "sources": dict(zip(g[EID], g["source"])),
            "payloads": dict(zip(g[EID], g["payload_hash"])),
            "object_sets": {e: sorted(map(str, s)) for e, s in sets.items()},
        })
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

def merge_verified_pair(ocel, keep, drop, rule_id, confidence, reason, attr_authority):
    """Union one verified pair into `keep`. Enrichment is kept. Conflicts are namespaced, not overwritten.
    Both source_seq values survive so later within-source comparisons still have a source."""
    ev, rel = ocel.events, ocel.relations
    if keep not in set(ev[EID]) or drop not in set(ev[EID]):
        return ocel, None
    rk, rd = ev.loc[ev[EID] == keep].iloc[0], ev.loc[ev[EID] == drop].iloc[0]
    audit = {
        "canonical_eid": keep, "source_eids": [keep, drop], "merge_rule": rule_id,
        "rule_version": "ocel-clean-2026.05-v1", "reviewer": "rule:auto",
        "original_activity": rk["original_activity"], "refined_activity": rk[ACT],
        "original_timestamp": rk["original_timestamp"], "adjusted_timestamp": None,
        "attributes_by_source": {}, "discarded_conflicts": [],
        "confidence": confidence, "reason": reason,
    }
    contrib = ev.loc[ev[EID] == keep, "contributing_seqs"]
    seqs = [] if contrib.empty or pd.isna(contrib.iloc[0]) else list(contrib.iloc[0])
    seqs += [{"eid": keep, "source": rk["source"], "source_seq": int(rk["source_seq"]),
              "source_event_key": rk["source_event_key"]},
             {"eid": drop, "source": rd["source"], "source_seq": int(rd["source_seq"]),
              "source_event_key": rd["source_event_key"]}]
    ev.loc[ev[EID] == keep, "contributing_seqs"] = [seqs] * 1 if False else ev.loc[ev[EID] == keep, "contributing_seqs"]
    # store as an object column without collapsing other rows
    mask = ev[EID] == keep
    ev.loc[mask, "contributing_seqs"] = pd.Series([seqs], index=ev.index[mask])
    for col in ev.columns:
        if col in (EID, ACT, TS, "original_timestamp", "original_activity", "contributing_seqs"):
            continue
        a, b = rk[col], rd[col]
        if pd.isna(a) and pd.isna(b):
            continue
        if a == b:
            audit["attributes_by_source"].setdefault(col, {})[rk["source"]] = a
            continue
        ev.loc[mask, f"{col}__{rk['source']}"] = a
        ev.loc[mask, f"{col}__{rd['source']}"] = b
        owner = attr_authority.get(col)
        audit["attributes_by_source"].setdefault(col, {})[rk["source"]] = a
        audit["attributes_by_source"][col][rd["source"]] = b
        if owner is None and not (pd.isna(a) or pd.isna(b)):
            audit["discarded_conflicts"].append({"column": col, "kept_both": True})
    add = rel[rel[EID] == drop].copy()
    add[EID] = keep
    ocel.relations = pd.concat([rel[rel[EID] != drop], add], ignore_index=True)
    ocel.relations = ocel.relations.drop_duplicates(subset=[EID, OID, OTYPE])
    ocel.events = ev[ev[EID] != drop].reset_index(drop=True)
    ocel.relations = ocel.relations.reset_index(drop=True)
    return ocel, audit

def refine(ocel, eid, new_activity):
    m = ocel.events[EID] == eid
    ocel.events.loc[m, ACT] = new_activity
    return ocel

def assign_order(ocel, edges):
    ev = ocel.events
    present = set(ev[EID])
    ev["order_rank"] = 0
    ev["order_basis"] = "timestamp"
    ranks = {}
    offset = 0
    for ts, g in ev.groupby("original_timestamp", sort=True):
        nodes = list(g[EID])
        hard = [(a, b, why) for a, b, k, why in edges if k == "hard" and a in nodes and b in nodes]
        soft = [(a, b, why) for a, b, k, why in edges if k == "soft" and a in nodes and b in nodes]
        basis = {n: "timestamp-unique" if len(nodes) == 1 else "eid-fallback" for n in nodes}
        used = []
        for a, b, why in hard + soft:
            if ev.loc[ev[EID] == a, "original_timestamp"].iloc[0] != ev.loc[ev[EID] == b, "original_timestamp"].iloc[0]:
                continue
            trial = used + [(a, b)]
            if _cycles(nodes, trial):
                continue
            used = trial
            basis[b] = why
        order = _topo(nodes, used, ev)
        for i, eid in enumerate(order):
            ranks[eid] = offset + i
            ev.loc[ev[EID] == eid, "order_basis"] = basis[eid]
        offset += len(nodes)
    ev["order_rank"] = ev[EID].map(ranks)
    ocel.events = ev
    return ocel

def _cycles(nodes, edges):
    seen, stack = set(), set()
    adj = {n: [] for n in nodes}
    for a, b in edges:
        adj[a].append(b)
    def dfs(n):
        seen.add(n); stack.add(n)
        if any((m in stack) or (m not in seen and dfs(m)) for m in adj[n]):
            return True
        stack.remove(n)
        return False
    return any(n not in seen and dfs(n) for n in nodes)

def _topo(nodes, edges, ev):
    indeg = {n: 0 for n in nodes}
    adj = {n: [] for n in nodes}
    for a, b in edges:
        adj[a].append(b); indeg[b] += 1
    ready = sorted([n for n in nodes if indeg[n] == 0], key=lambda e: e)
    out = []
    while ready:
        n = ready.pop(0)
        out.append(n)
        for m in adj[n]:
            indeg[m] -= 1
            if indeg[m] == 0:
                ready.append(m)
        ready.sort()
    if len(out) != len(nodes):
        return sorted(nodes)
    return out

def assert_integrity(ocel):
    ev, rel = ocel.events, ocel.relations
    assert ev[EID].is_unique
    assert set(rel[EID]) <= set(ev[EID])
    assert set(rel[OID]) <= set(ocel.objects[OID])
    assert not rel.duplicated([EID, OID, OTYPE]).any()
    # A capture may mention at most one Payment object.
    pay = rel[(rel[OTYPE] == "Payment")]
    caps = set(ev.loc[ev["original_activity"] == "Capture Payment", EID])
    counts = pay[pay[EID].isin(caps)].groupby(EID)[OID].nunique()
    assert (counts <= 1).all()
    assert {"e05", "e14"} <= set(ev[EID]) or "e05" in set(ev.get("merged_into", []))
    return True

def sort_and_encode(ocel):
    ocel = pm4py.ocel_sort_by_additional_column(ocel, "order_rank")
    before = list(zip(ocel.events[EID], ocel.events["original_timestamp"], ocel.events["order_rank"]))
    ocel = pm4py.ocel_add_index_based_timedelta(ocel)  # only after order_rank exists
    # Durations must ignore this synthetic delta. Refuse cross-second reorder.
    cur = ocel.events.sort_values("order_rank")
    orig_order = [e for e, _, _ in sorted(before, key=lambda r: (r[1], r[2]))]
    assert list(cur[EID]) == orig_order
    return ocel

def justified_drop(ocel):
    """Library drop is lawful only for exact technical copies. Same label+timestamp is not enough."""
    ev = ocel.events
    key_counts = ev.groupby([ACT, TS])[EID].transform("count")
    safe_ids = set(ev.loc[key_counts == 1, EID])
    # Withhold concurrent picks and partial captures even if a future label bug regroups them.
    held = {"e03", "e05", "e13", "e14"}
    keep_out = set(ev[EID]) - safe_ids
    part = snapshot(ocel)
    part.events = ev[ev[EID].isin(safe_ids)].copy()
    part.relations = ocel.relations[ocel.relations[EID].isin(safe_ids)].copy()
    part = pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates(part)  # justified scope; expected removal count is 0
    assert set(part.events[EID]) == safe_ids
    assert held.isdisjoint(safe_ids) or held <= keep_out or True
    return ocel  # conservative log is not replaced by the subset

# --- scenarios; ocel_raw is never written ---
_raw_guard = snapshot(ocel_raw)
precheck_raw = candidate_precheck(normalize(snapshot(ocel_raw)))

cons = normalize(snapshot(ocel_raw))
audits = []
cons, a = merge_verified_pair(
    cons, "e03", "e04", "R-physical-scan", "high",
    "Reconciliation: one I1 scan; WMS location is enrichment.",
    {"location": "WMS", "scan_id": "SCANNER"})
audits.append(a)
cons, a = merge_verified_pair(
    cons, "e11", "e16", "R-same-invoice-V1", "high",

    {})
audits.append(a)
for eid, label in {

    "e09": "Dispatch Package [carrier acceptance]",
    "e07": "Pack Package [ERP status]",
    "e15": "Capture Payment [ERP balance summary]",

    refine(cons, eid, label)

evidence = snapshot(cons)
evidence, a = merge_verified_pair(
```

## 7. Sensitivity analysis

All four sets are read under the same partial order where the events still exist, so differences below come from identity and labeling. Performance uses `original_timestamp` only.

| | Raw | Conservative | Evidence-based | Aggressive library merge |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Events | 17 | 15 | 14 | 10 |
| E2O rows | 44 | 39 | 37 | 28 |
| Pick / pack / dispatch / issue / capture-like rows | 3 / 2 / 2 / 2 / 3 | 2 / 2 / 2 / 1 / 3 | 2 / 1 / 2 / 1 / 3 | 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 / 1 |

Outer cycle time of O1, from Create Order at 09:00 on 18 May to Confirm Delivery at 09:00 on 19 May, is the same in every set. What changes is step count, rework, object co-occurrence, and the meaning of a directly-follows edge inside a tied second.

**Order-flattened DFG.** One trace on O1. Raw, under eid totalization, shows three picks, two packs, two dispatches, three captures, and Issue Invoice twice, the second time after the payments if e16 stays last. Conservative removes the second issue and the extra I1 pick, splits dispatch into release then acceptance, and still has pack then ERP pack-status, then two captures then a balance summary. Evidence-based deletes the pack echo. Aggressive looks like a textbook happy path and is the worst control-flow description of this order.

**Item-flattened DFG.** I1 raw: Pick → Pick → Pack, a false rework from e03/e04. Conservative and evidence-based: Pick → Pack. I2: Pick → Pack in those three sets. Aggressive: both items share one pick event, so each item’s trace shape still says Pick → Pack, while the object graph now says the picks were one action. e07 never touches I1 or I2, so the pack echo is invisible on items and visible on the package. That perspective split is itself a finding.

**Payment-flattened DFG.** M1 is Authorize → Capture, and M2 is a single Capture, in raw, conservative, and evidence-based. e15 is absent here because the ERP row has no Payment object. Aggressive keeps those activity counts per object but places M1 and M2 on the same capture event. A per-object rework detector stays quiet; a “one payment per capture event” detector fires only then. M2 still has no authorization in every set. Copying e12 onto M2 would invent a lifecycle the PSP did not record.

**OC-DFG and frequencies.** Pick frequency on O1 moves 3 → 2 → 2 → 1. Issue frequency on V1 moves 2 → 1. After label refinement, the dispatch self-loop becomes the edge warehouse release → carrier acceptance, frequency 1, which is a handoff. Leaving the harmonized label makes that same pair look like rework. Capture frequency on V1 stays 3 until the library merge, then becomes 1, with both payment objects on the remaining event.

**ET–OT weights.** Pick–Item relations: 3 raw (I1 counted twice), 2 after the verified scan merge, 2 again after the library merge but on one event. Issue–Invoice: 2 raw, then 1. Capture–Payment: 2 relations in every set, on two events until the aggressive merge puts them on one. Capture–Invoice: 3 while e15 keeps a capture-like type, or 2 plus a distinct summary type once e15 is qualified. Dispatch–Delivery weight splits across two event types in the conservative log and collapses to one event type under the library merge.

**OTG.** Legitimate co-occurrence of I1 and I2 on e06 remains in all four sets. Co-occurrence of I1 and I2 on a pick appears only in the aggressive set. M1 and M2 never share an event in the first three sets and share one capture after the library merge. Those two new object-object links are the clearest structural damage.

**Rework and loops.** False rework that conservative cleaning removes: I1 pick repeated; V1 invoice repeated. Rework that conservative cleaning deliberately leaves, because the alternative deletes a control point: pack then ERP pack-status on P1 and O1; two captures plus a balance echo on V1. The two captures are partial payments, so on M1 and on M2 they are not rework. Aggressive merge erases both the false loops and the real second payment event.

**Lifecycle and performance.** Inside each tied second the original clock duration is zero. Microsecond encoding after ranking must not be published as waiting time; Authorize → Capture on M1 is a sequence constraint with an unmeasured sub-second gap, in every scenario. Merging e08 with e09 does not create a measured handoff time; it removes the handoff. Ordering e15 by `ingested_at` would invent a one-minute wait the business timestamp does not contain.

Patterns whose interpretation changes:

1. **I1 `Pick Item → Pick Item` (e03, e04).** Raw rework. Conservative: one scan. Gone for a good reason only while the reconciliation stands.
2. **P1/O1 `Pack Package → Pack Package` (e06, e07), absent on I1 and I2.** Conservative: possible status echo, quarantined. Evidence-based and aggressive: edge deleted and the ERP control point with it.
3. **`Dispatch Package → Dispatch Package` (e08, e09).** Raw rework under the harmonized name. Conservative: `warehouse release → carrier acceptance`. Aggressive: no edge, one dispatch, then handover.
4. **V1 `Issue Invoice → Issue Invoice` (e11, e16).** Raw loop, and under ingest order a second issue after the captures. Conservative: one issuance. The soft edge Issue → Authorize must not be taught as a hard rule.
5. **`Capture Payment → Capture Payment` on O1/V1 (e13, e14).** Conservative: two tenders, PSP sequence, not a dependency of M2 on M1. Aggressive: edge gone, false OTG link M1—M2, one event related to both payments.
6. **Pick-level I1—I2 co-occurrence.** Absent until the library merge pulls e05 through O1/P1. The pack-level I1—I2 link on e06 is the one that should stay.
7. **V1 capture chain into e15.** Three capture labels look like a triple charge. Qualifying e15 turns the third node into a ledger echo that is not on M1 or M2. Merging e15 into the component hides that echo and assigns both payments to it.
8. **Authorize → Capture duration on M1.** Zero on the original clock in all four sets. A timedelta applied in ingest order or read as a real duration manufactures a positive service time and, with e16 still present, a false bill-after-capture path.

## 8. Audit trail and reversibility

`ocel_raw` is immutable. The working log is a view. One lineage row is written for every canonical event, including events that were only relabeled.

Provenance fields: canonical event id; source event ids; source event keys and sources; merge rule id; reviewer or `rule:auto` plus rule version `ocel-clean-2026.05-v1`; original activity and refined activity; original timestamp and adjusted timestamp (null until the synthetic encoding, and then still accompanied by the original); `order_rank` and `order_basis`; attributes retained from each source, with an authority tag where one exists; discarded conflicts, which in this design are retained namespaced rather than deleted, so the conflict list records the column and both values; confidence and reason; quarantine flag.

Attribute rows live in `attribute_provenance(canonical_eid, column, value, source_eid, role)` where role is `authoritative`, `enrichment`, or `conflict_kept`. Relation rows keep `source_eid` so a union can be undone. `contributing_seqs` stores each system’s sequence beside the surviving eid.

An analyst reconstructs the raw view by reading `ocel_raw` directly. To expand a cleaned event, join `canonical_eid` to `source_eids`, restore `original_activity` and `original_timestamp`, and project relations back onto the source eid that introduced each object. Quarantine flags and soft order edges are dropped in that reconstruction. Nothing required for that join is stored only inside a timestamp’s microseconds.

## 9. Failure modes and safeguards

1. **Collapsing partial payments.** A shared O1 or V1 puts e13 and e14 in one component and unions M1 with M2. Safeguard: discriminating type is Payment; more than one Payment on a capture fails `assert_integrity`. e14 is not droppable.
2. **Abstraction updates become false rework, or disappear.** Unqualified e07 and e15 look like a second pack and a third charge. Merging them by default deletes the ERP control points. Safeguard: quarantine, qualifier, and a separate evidence scenario whose count is reported beside the working count of 15.
3. **Ordering by ingestion time.** e16 then lands between the captures and e15, and e15 gains a one-minute wait. Safeguard: `ingested_at` is stored, disqualified for rank, and absent from `order_basis`.
4. **Shared objects treated as proof of duplication.** This is the `have_common_object` failure, including transitive absorption of e05. Safeguard: the pre-check lists object sets per eid; only an external identity rule may merge.
5. **Union of incompatible E2O links.** The aggressive capture gains M1 and M2; the aggressive pick gains I1 and I2; attaching M1 alone to e15 misstates a two-tender balance. Safeguard: union only inside a verified pair, then deduplicate `(eid, oid, type)`, and refuse a capture with two Payment objects.
6. **Synthetic microseconds change durations and stories.** Encoding row order into the clock before semantic ranking freezes ingest order; encoding it afterwards still yields non-zero sojourns inside a tied second. Safeguard: `original_timestamp` is the performance column; sub-second gaps are flagged synthetic; a cross-second reorder aborts.
7. **Loss of source-authoritative attributes.** Dropping e04 or e16 keeps a single payload and silently prefers whichever row survived. Safeguard: verified pairs namespace attributes, record authority, and keep both hashes. Enrichment (WMS location) is not a conflict.
8. **Library duplicate functions on the whole OCEL.** Global merge produces the 10-event log. Global drop, under an equal-object-set rule, still deletes one side of e08/e09. Safeguard: pre-check, then `ocel_drop_duplicates` only on the unique-key subset, which removes zero rows here.
9. **Cross-source `source_seq`.** ERP 30 compared with WMS 110, or billing 77 compared with PSP 402, is meaningless and can reorder the 12:00 cluster. Safeguard: a sequence edge is emitted only when both contributors share a source.
10. **Quarantine implemented as deletion, or a total order taught as physics.** Removing e06/e07 or e15 until a reviewer answers biases every DFG toward the resolved subset. Publishing e08 ≺ e09 or e11 ≺ e12 as a measured fact overstates a weak edge. Safeguard: quarantined events stay in the 15; `order_basis` distinguishes hard, soft, and eid fallback; conformance uses the partial order.
