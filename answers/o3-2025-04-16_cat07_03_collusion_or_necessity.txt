# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

During the review of the procurement-to-payment event log for Q4, Internal Audit noted several control exceptions and data-quality issues. Most routine transactions processed by Buyer-Approver pairs Aline/Elena and Boris/Felix follow expected timings and separation-of-duties; they will remain under periodic monitoring only.

A higher-risk cluster involves 44 cases in which VendorMaster_Lena both modified vendor bank details and AP_Kim approved the subsequent payment within the same day. The majority of these cases relate to vendor family V-77x and were uploaded manually rather than processed through standard workflow. Because the normal four-eyes control was bypassed and approval times were markedly faster than the company median, further examination of supporting tickets, vendor confirmations and access logs is recommended.

Fast-track emergency purchases handled by Buyer_Carla / Approver_Gita and Buyer_Dan / Manager_Hugo appear consistent with the documented emergency procedure; however, in 42 instances the retrospective justification step was performed by the same individual who executed the override, contrary to policy. Twelve of these justifications originated from a quarter-close spreadsheet upload, suggesting that data-migration shortcomings may mask the true level of independent review. We advise sampling underlying correspondence to confirm that a second-level review did in fact occur.

Overall, while the observed patterns could stem from legitimate specialization and time-critical operations, the concentration of bank-detail changes and payments, together with known data-quality limitations, warrants targeted follow-up rather than broad-brush allegations. Management should ensure (1) completeness of audit trails for manual uploads, (2) enforcement of retrospective justifications by an independent party, and (3) remediation of shared-account and mapping issues so that automated controls can operate effectively.
