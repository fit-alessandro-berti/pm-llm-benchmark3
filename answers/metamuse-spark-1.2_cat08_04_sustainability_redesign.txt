# 1. System boundary and assumptions

**Environmental scope used:** Gate-to-gate extended with embodied waste from failures. Based on available event attributes:
*Inside boundary:* `energy_kwh` (CNC_A, CNC_B, heat_treat, inspection_cell via `machine_group`), `transport_kgco2e` + `route_mode` (parcel_ground, parcel_air, dedicated_van, consolidated_truck), `material_scrap_kg` (subtractive production scrap), `package_material_kg` + `package` type (standard vs reinforced) + `rework_flag` / `rework packaging` loops, and hidden waste from obsolescence, partial shipments (second `Ship` event), and damage-returns. Includes empty return leg of `dedicated_van` where logged in `transport_kgco2e`.

*Outside boundary:* Upstream raw material extraction, customer use of spare part, downstream end-of-life disposal/recycling, and facility HVAC/overhead not allocated to `energy_kwh`. Courier fleet manufacturing excluded. Customer behavior outside the order-to-close process (e.g., ordering extra safety stock due to slower delivery) tracked only as rebound risk, not in direct footprint.

**Key assumptions:**
- `energy_kwh`, `material_scrap_kg`, `transport_kgco2e` are measured per order event and allocated per item via `batch_id/batch_size` where applicable.
- Promised `promised_lead_time_hours` and `expedite_flag` reflect contractual service constraint; meeting it avoids a larger footprint from alternative sourcing by customer.
- Reinforced packaging doubles `package_material_kg` vs standard but is justified if damage-return rate falls (observed 40% reduction for MTO heavy parts >8kg).
- 6% obsolescence rate for batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days is treated as full material+energy waste for those items, even if scrap not yet logged.

**Uncertainty remains:**
- Whether `energy_kwh` includes idle energy during `Batch wait` / `Backorder wait`.
- Full lifecycle impact of double handling in `Partial shipment` (1010 cases, 220 -> Backorder wait) and `Rework packaging` (620 cases) vs customer return outside log.
- `transport_kgco2e` calculation method for consolidated_truck (allocation per order vs per kg-km) and dedicated_van empty return factor.
- Forecast accuracy driving obsolescence waste; not directly in log.
- Quality impact of rushed `inspection_cell` queues causing later field failures not captured as `rework_production`.

# 2. Sustainability-related process indicators

All indicators build directly from event attributes and DFG paths, segmented by `case_type` x `customer_type` and by variant.

**Energy:**
- `Avg energy_kwh / order` and `energy_kwh / item` (using `batch_size`) by `machine_group` and batch size bucket. Tracks CNC_A vs CNC_B trade-off.
- `Energy per good item` = `energy_kwh / (1 - scrap + rework rate)` to capture rework loops.
- `Batch wait time` median/p90 + `Heat treatment batch wait` time.

**Transport:**
- `Avg transport_kgCO2e / order` by `route_mode` and by segment.
- `SP1-compliant ground/consolidated share` = % cases where `route_mode` in [parcel_ground, consolidated_truck] when `promised_lead_time_hours - predicted ground time` allows >85% on-time probability.
- `SP2-justified dedicated_van rate` = % `dedicated_van` with justification event.
- `Consolidation utilization` = orders per `consolidated_truck` departure + empty return indicator.

**Materials & Waste:**
- `Avg material_scrap_kg / order` + `scrap_kg / good item` including `rework_production` scrap (1.8 kg in MTO CNC_B rework loop vs 0.70 kg in efficient MTO path).
- `Package_material_kg / order` and `Rework packaging rate` (Rework packaging -> Package reinforced/standard = 620 / 11000 packages = 5.6%).
- `Obsolescence waste rate` = % batch-produced items with no `Ship` within 14 days after `CNC production` (currently 6% for large batches).
- `Partial shipment rate` = Partial shipment (1010) / Pick from stock (7050) = 14.3% plus second shipment footprint.

**Batching efficiency:**
- `Median batch_size` vs `energy_kwh/item` curve (CNC_A 2.8 -> 1.3 kWh at 12-20) + `Batch wait` contribution to cycle time.
- `Rework rate by machine_group` (CNC_B 18% vs CNC_A 9% for MTO).

**Service / Quality:**
- `Cycle time` median/p90 and `Late delivery %` (`Confirm delivery` vs promised_lead_time_hours) per segment/variant. Critical-downtime 15h/4% vs MTO Regular 94h/18%.
- `Cancellation due to delay` rate = 530/12000 = 4.4% (410 direct + 60 after backorder).
- `Rework_production rate` = 470 Inspections + 390 loops / 3670 CNC productions  12.8% overall, and `Return/damage rate` proxy via `Rework packaging`.

**Resilience:**
- `Alternative route availability` for Service-contract cases during disruption (R1 conformance).
- `Emergency production share` (760/12000 = 6.3%) + `Dedicated van dependency` concentration per carrier.
- `Model precision risk` = frequency of non-standard combinations (emergency + partial shipment) indicating flexibility vs uncontrolled variance.

# 3. Intervention options

**S1. Green shipping recommender using promised lead time, route reliability, customer segment**
- Target path/segment: All `Ship` choices, especially Standard-stock Service-contract where SP1 violated in 22% and air used (5% stock->air, 6% MTO->air) even when ground feasible.
- Mechanism: At `Package -> Ship` decision, compute slack = `promised_lead_time_hours - predicted route time (p85)`. Recommend lowest `transport_kgCO2e` route_mode that keeps on-time probability >85% (SP1). Preserves `parcel_air` / `dedicated_van` only when slack insufficient.
- Service/quality/resilience: Late delivery risk neutral if 85% threshold calibrated; reduces unjustified air but retains expedite when needed. No packaging change. Resilience requires R1: recommender must offer at least one fallback `route_mode`.
- Evidence to monitor: `expedite_flag`, `route_mode` vs slack, `SP1 conformance`, late delivery % and `transport_kgCO2e` per segment, violation log S12.

**S2. Dynamic batching for CNC and heat treatment with maximum waiting-time caps by segment**
- Target path: `Plan production -> Batch wait -> CNC production` (3020/3780 MTO regular) and `Heat treatment` batches.
- Mechanism: Allow energy-optimal batch (12-20 for CNC_A, >10 for heat) up to a cap: e.g., Standard-stock wait cap short, MTO Regular max 24h, Critical-downtime bypass batch (Emergency production). Trades 1.5 kWh/item energy saving against waiting energy is small vs waiting time.
- Service/quality/resilience: Reduces p90 cycle time and 1.7x late risk from heat wait; avoids starvation of small urgent jobs. Slightly higher `energy_kwh/item` vs maximal batching but avoids obsolescence of large batches.
- Evidence: `batch_id`, `batch_size`, `Batch wait` duration, `energy_kwh/item`, `late delivery %`, `obsolescence waste rate` (items not shipped in 14d), `CNC production` queue time.

**S3. Shift Made-to-order from CNC_B to CNC_A when queue and due-date slack allow**
- Target path: 5% variant `Batch wait -> CNC_B -> inspection -> rework -> ground` (32% late, 32 kWh, 1.8 kg scrap, 18% rework) vs CNC_A path (9% rework, 16 kWh, 0.70 kg).
- Mechanism: Routing rule at `Plan production`/`Batch wait`: if `CNC_A` queue + slack permits on-time completion, route to CNC_A; reserve CNC_B for true capacity overflow. Avoids rework-driven 2x energy and >2x scrap.
- Service/quality/resilience: Median cycle time may increase 4-10h vs CNC_B but late risk drops from 32% to ~21% due to fewer rework loops (160h vs 118h total). Quality improves. Resilience improves by preserving CNC_B as overflow.
- Evidence: `machine_group`, `rework_flag`, `material_scrap_kg`, `energy_kwh`, `Inspection -> Rework production` rate, cycle time by machine, queue length.

**S4. Replace air for Standard-stock Service-contract with priority pick + ground unless slack insufficient**
- Target segment: Standard-stock Service-contract (16% share, 7.8 kgCO2e vs 4.2 for Regular, median 18h) where air used despite promise allowing ground.
- Mechanism: At `Pick from stock` prioritize queue position for Service-contract, then ship `parcel_ground` or `consolidated_truck`. Only use `Ship air` if recommender (S1) shows insufficient slack. Addresses SP1 violations directly.
- Service/quality/resilience: Cycle time increases median ~+5-8h but stays within 41h p90 vs promised lead time; late risk stays ~5-6% if priority picking enforced. No quality impact. Resilience: ground carrier diversification needed.
- Evidence: `customer_type`, `Pick from stock` waiting time, `route_mode`, `transport_kgCO2e`, late delivery %, SP1 conformance shift.

**S5. Consolidate truck departures twice daily for non-urgent stock orders**
- Target path: `Stock -> standard pack -> ground` (42% share, 3.6 kgCO2e) vs `consolidated_truck` (currently 850 orders only).
- Mechanism: Batch `Package standard` orders with slack >24h into `consolidated_truck` at fixed windows (e.g., 12h and 24h). Reported 35-55% kgCO2e reduction vs individual ground parcel.
- Service/quality/resilience: Adds 12-24h consolidation wait  cycle time +12h median, but acceptable for 39% Standard-stock Regular (late only 7%, p90 62h). Must exclude Critical-downtime and low-slack MTO. Resilience risk if single consolidator fails  need alternative ground lane (R1).
- Evidence: `consolidated_truck` load factor, `Package -> Ship` wait time, `transport_kgCO2e/order`, late delivery by slack bucket, alternative route event availability.

**S6. Improve packaging-route compatibility checks before shipment**
- Target path: `Package standard/reinforced -> Rework packaging` (620 cases; 260 from standard, 360 from reinforced; triggers 550 re-packages).
- Mechanism: Hard check at `Package` event: verify `package type` vs `route_mode` and `case_type` weight/geometry + Q1 rule (>8kg MTO needs reinforced unless certified). Blocks incompatible combinations causing rework loops.
- Service/quality/resilience: Reduces double material use (package_material_kg x2), extra handling energy, and 1-2 day delay from Repackage. Slightly increases initial `package_material_kg` where upgraded to reinforced but prevents return damage. Resilience neutral.
- Evidence: `package_material_kg`, `rework_flag` at packaging, `Rework packaging` frequency, damage return rate, Q1 conformance.

**S7. Add inspection quality gate for high-rework machine/part combinations**
- Target path: `CNC production -> Inspection -> Rework production -> CNC production` loops (470 + 390 loops; inspection_cell queue rushing).
- Mechanism: Enforced dwell/inspection time for CNC_B and heavy MTO parts; add capacity or priority rule so inspection not rushed when queue high. Might add `inspection_cell` staffing during peaks.
- Service/quality/resilience: Adds 2-4h inspection time but avoids 390 rework cycles that each adds ~30-50h and 1.1 kg extra scrap and 16 kWh extra. Net cycle time and late risk improve (rework variant 160h/32% late vs 118h/21%). Quality improves.
- Evidence: `inspection_cell` queue time, `rework_flag`, `material_scrap_kg`, `energy_kwh`, rework loop count per `machine_group` and part family.

**S8. Replace some dedicated vans with shared emergency courier lanes**
- Target path: `Critical -> emergency production -> dedicated_van` (8% share, 52 kgCO2e, strong service 14h/3% late but empty returns).
- Mechanism: For Critical-downtime that passes S9 triage as "precautionary urgent" (not true line-down), offer guaranteed 4-6h shared courier lane aggregating multiple urgent orders vs dedicated single-order van. Keeps true downtime on dedicated_van with SP2 justification.
- Service/quality/resilience: Adds 2-5h vs dedicated van but still well under 30h p90; transport kgCO2e drops ~40-60% by sharing. Resilience improves via pooled fleet vs single van dependency. Must not apply to true downtime without slack.
- Evidence: `dedicated_van` justification event (31% missing), `transport_kgCO2e`, `Confirm delivery` time for Critical, `expedite_flag`, empty return flag.

**S9. Split Critical-downtime into true downtime and precautionary urgent categories**
- Target segment: Critical-downtime Service-contract 11% (currently all treated as emergency). Evidence: 3% stock/ground exception meets service with 23h/9% late at 4.8 kgCO2e vs 14h/3% at 52 kgCO2e.
- Mechanism: Triage at `Receive order` using downtime signal + customer confirmation. True downtime keeps `Emergency production -> dedicated van`; precautionary uses priority stock/ground or consolidated emergency lane (S8).
- Service/quality/resilience: Allows differentiated service promise; precautionary gets slightly higher late risk (9% vs 3%) but still acceptable with priority handling, while cutting emissions ~90%. Requires clear R1 exception handling for true downtime.
- Evidence: `case_type` sub-category, `Emergency production` share, `dedicated_van` rate, `transport_kgCO2e`, late delivery split by new sub-type, customer satisfaction/cancellation.

**S10. Carbon-aware promise-date negotiation for Regular made-to-order customers**
- Target path: MTO Regular 22% (94h median, 18% late, 18.5 kWh, 8.0 kgCO2e) where promise drives `CNC immediately -> air` (6% variant, 70h but 23 kgCO2e + 24 kWh).
- Mechanism: At `Availability check`, offer later `promised_lead_time_hours` (e.g., +24-48h) with price incentive to allow batching and ground shipment.
- Service/quality/resilience: Increases quoted lead time but decreases late risk from 18% to ~13% due to feasible batching; avoids rush and air. Risk is order loss/cancellation if customer rejects — must monitor conversion. Resilience improves with buffered production.
- Evidence: `promised_lead_time_hours` offered vs accepted, `Batch wait` vs immediate CNC, `route_mode` shift, late delivery, `Cancel due to delay` rate, `energy_kwh/item`.

**S11. Reduce partial shipments by changing availability-check rules**
- Target path: `Pick from stock -> Partial shipment` (1010) -> second shipment + `Backorder wait -> Cancel` (60).
- Mechanism: Require availability threshold for multi-item orders: hold for complete shipment within 24h if `promised_lead_time` allows, else split only if slack critical. Aligns with S5 consolidation.
- Service/quality/resilience: Median cycle time for partial path 79h with 24% late vs 34h for complete shipment — reducing splits improves service, cuts second `transport_kgCO2e` (7.2 vs 3.6) and double `package_material_kg`. Resilience needs safety stock visibility.
- Evidence: `Partial shipment` rate, second `Ship` event per case, `transport_kgCO2e` per case with partial, `Backorder wait` duration, late delivery.

**S12. Create sustainability exception log for SP1/SP2/Q1 overrides**
- Enabler for all. Adds mandatory event `Sustainability override` with reason code when deviating from SP1/SP2/Q1 (replacing 31% missing justifications). Does not reduce impact directly but enables governance and prevents silent rebound to air/van.
- Evidence: Conformance checking on `SP1/SP2/Q1` events, exception rate, audit of reason codes vs `promised_lead_time_hours` and `customer_type`.

# 5. Rebound and displacement risks

1. **Batching  Waiting + Obsolescence displacement (energy vs time/material):** Pushing batch_size to 12-20 saves 2.81.3 kWh/item on CNC_A and heat but creates 438h Batch wait and 1.7x late risk. Late orders then trigger `Rework production` rush or `Ship air` (4-6x CO2) and 6% obsolescence waste where batch items never ship — wiping out energy gains with transport + material waste.

2. **Transport mode shift  Service failure  Second shipment (transport vs transport + material):** Moving `parcel_air``parcel_ground` or `consolidated_truck` without slack check (S1/S5) adds 12-24h. If predicted p85 on-time <85%, late deliveries jump (8%21% in MTO batch path)  customers cancel (410 cases) and re-order via express, creating duplicate `energy_kwh` and `transport_kgCO2e`.

3. **Packaging light-weighting  Damage returns (material vs material+transport):** Reducing `reinforced` to `standard` pack to save `package_material_kg` (2x difference) violates Q1 for >8kg MTO parts. 40% damage-return reduction is lost  `Rework packaging` loops (360 cases) + return shipment doubles package and transport footprint.

4. **Rework reduction via slower machine  Throughput displacement (quality vs energy):** Shifting all MTO to CNC_A (S3) without cap overwhelms CNC_A, extending queues  more `Emergency production` and air shipments to catch up. Energy/scrap saved on single item displaced to system-wide waiting and expedite emissions. Need CNC_B as overflow with monitoring.

5. **Obsolescence from forecast-driven batches (batching vs material waste):** Energy-efficient large batches based on forecast rather than firm orders reduce kWh/item but increase unsold stock. Log shows 6% of batch items not shipped in 14 days  hidden `material_scrap_kg` and embodied heat-treatment energy wasted, not visible in order-level `energy_kwh`.

6. **Customer behavior rebound to precautionary urgency (service vs transport):** If standard service slows due to ground/consolidation, Service-contract customers may inflate `case_type` to `Critical-downtime` or demand dedicated vans to protect SLA, increasing `dedicated_van` share (already 610 cases) and empty returns, raising system `transport_kgCO2e` despite per-order savings. S9 triage and S12 exception log needed to prevent gaming.

7. **Inspection speedup  Hidden rework loop (quality vs energy/scrap):** Pressuring `inspection_cell` to cut cycle time saves hours but causes rushed inspection  missed defect  later `Rework production` (470 loops) or field return with much larger scrap (1.8 kg vs 0.70 kg) and repeat CNC energy (32 kWh vs 16 kWh). Appears as service improvement but increases lifecycle impact.

8. **Partial shipment avoidance  Stockout wait and cancellation (transport vs cancellation):** Holding orders for complete shipment (S11) without accurate `Availability check` stock data extends `Backorder wait` (220 cases)  60 cancellations. Cancelled orders may be sourced externally with higher footprint or cause downtime cost that outweighs saved second shipment.

# 6. Balanced recommendation

**Coherent package: S1 + S4 + S9 + S8 + S2 + S3 + S7 + S6 + S5 (selective) + S11 + S12, with R1 resilience guardrails.** Rationale: address the three largest impact hotspots without unacceptable service loss — unjustified air/dedicated-van transport (38 kgCO2e for Critical, 12-18.5 kgCO2e for Service-contract), rework-driven energy/scrap (32 kWh/1.8 kg loops), and rework-packaging/partial-shipment waste — while protecting Critical-downtime SLA.

**A. Differentiate by segment and slack — do not treat all air/vans equally:**
- Deploy **S1 Green recommender** as backbone: at `Availability check  Plan production/Pick  Package  Ship` decision, compute slack vs `promised_lead_time_hours`. Enforce SP1 (>85% on-time) and SP2 (Critical justification). This automatically preserves `parcel_air` for 5% stock-air path where cycle 17h vs promised <24h and MTO immediate-CNC-air (6%) when true slack insufficient, and protects Critical true-downtime dedicated vans, while cutting the 22% SP1 violations in Standard-stock Service-contract.
- Pair with **S4** (priority pick + ground) for Standard-stock Service-contract: achieves low transport (3.6-5.5 kgCO2e) with still 18-25h median, well within 41h p90, so service deterioration acceptable.
- Pair with **S9 + S8**: triage Critical into true downtime (keep 14h emergency + dedicated van, ~8% core) vs precautionary urgent (shift to stock/ground exception path at 23h/4.8 kgCO2e or shared courier lane). This captures ~90% CO2 reduction for ~maybe 30-50% of current Critical volume without violating downtime protection, maintaining resilience via pooled lane.

**B. Fix quality-driven waste before accelerating flow:**
- Implement **S7 inspection gate** + **S3 CNC routing** together: route MTO to CNC_A when slack allows; reserve CNC_B for overflow and enforce inspection dwell for CNC_B parts. This attacks the highest environmental multiplier: rework loops that double energy and scrap (1632 kWh, 0.701.8 kg) and drive late delivery 21%32%. Net service *improves* (160h118h) despite slight initial slowdown, so no service trade-off.
- Add **S6 packaging compatibility + Q1 enforcement** before `Ship`: prevents 5.6% Rework packaging double-material and damage returns that otherwise offset transport savings. Slight increase in reinforced use for >8kg MTO is justified by 40% damage reduction — material increase is lower than return-loop material.

**C. Optimize batching with hard service caps, not maximal efficiency:**
- Deploy **S2 dynamic batching** with segment-specific max wait (e.g., Critical 0h/bypass, Service-contract 12h, Standard-stock Regular 24h for consolidation). Keeps energy benefit of 12-20 batch (1.3 vs 2.8 kWh) while capping heat-treatment 1.7x late risk and obsolescence 6% waste. Complement with **S10 optional** carbon-aware promise negotiation for MTO Regular only where customer accepts — do not impose longer promise universally.

**D. Selective consolidation, not blanket:**
- Apply **S5 consolidated truck** only to non-urgent Standard-stock Regular with slack >24h (42% ground path cohort), twice daily, with dual carrier to satisfy R1. Avoid for MTO low-slack and Critical. This yields 35-55% transport cut without pushing overall late risk unacceptable.
- Apply **S11 partial-shipment rule** with 24h hold threshold linked to slack predictor: cuts second shipment (7.2 vs 3.6 kgCO2e) and backorder cancellations, but requires accurate stock ATP to avoid extended waits.

**Why balanced:**
- Environmental: Cuts dominant `transport_kgCO2e` (air 4-6x, dedicated van 52 kg) and dominant `energy/scrap` (rework loops) with measurable ranges, while accounting for hidden obsolescence and return waste — no burden shifting to material.
- Service: Service-contract and Critical true-downtime SLAs protected via slack-based recommender and triage; MTO late risk actually falls by removing rework; only controlled +12-24h added where promise allows.
- Quality: Reinforced packaging and inspection gates strengthen Q1, reducing field failures that create larger lifecycle impact than packaging material saved.
- Resilience: R1 preserved by mandating alternative route, preserving CNC_B as overflow (S3), pooling emergency lanes (S8), and exception governance (S12) preventing brittle lean model (low precision due to emergency paths is retained as deliberate flexibility, not eliminated).

Do not adopt lowest-emission extreme: e.g., forcing all shipments to consolidated truck or banning all air/vans would breach Critical-downtime 15h SLA and raise cancellations (currently 4.4%), displacing impact to customer downtime sourcing.

# 7. Validation and governance plan

**A. Event-log validation — pre/post with segment-level process mining:**

- **Baseline vs post-change process discovery:** Re-run DFG and variant analysis monthly (same case-mix buckets). Compare frequencies: `Ship air`, `Dedicated van`, `Batch wait`, `Rework production`, `Rework packaging`, `Partial shipment`, `Backorder wait`, `Cancel due to delay`. Use 12k order cohort; maintain control chart per segment (Standard-stock, MTO, Critical x Regular/Service-contract). Require minimum 2,000 cases per segment for significance.

- **Conformance monitoring for SP1/SP2/Q1 (automated checks on enriched log):**
  - SP1: Replay each `Ship` event: compute `slack = promised_lead_time_hours - predicted p85 route time` using historic `route_mode` performance. Flag violation if `route_mode` = air/dedicated_van while ground/consolidated meets >85% on-time. Track SP1 violation rate target: 22%  <8% for Standard-stock Service-contract, with no increase in `Late delivery %` >2 pp per segment.
  - SP2: Check `dedicated_van` cases have preceding `justification` event and `case_type` = Critical-downtime true sub-type. Target 31% missing  <5%. Correlate with S12 exception log.
  - Q1: Check MTO >8kg with `package = standard` and no `inspection certifies low damage risk` event. Flag Q1 violation and link to subsequent `Rework packaging` or return. Target Q1 violations  near zero and damage return -30% within 3 months.
  - Visual: Conformance dashboard with case-level `trace` highlighting violating paths (e.g., `Package standard -> Ship air` despite slack).

- **Sustainability KPIs per S-indicator set, monitored weekly:**
  - Energy: `energy_kwh/order` and `/good item` by `machine_group` and `batch_size` bucket; heat treatment per item.
  - Transport: `transport_kgCO2e/order` by `route_mode`; consolidation load factor; empty return rate.
  - Material: `material_scrap_kg/good item`, `package_material_kg/order`, `Rework packaging rate`, `Partial shipment rate`, `obsolescence waste rate` (14-day no-ship check joining production batch to shipment).
  - Service/Quality/Resilience: median/p90 cycle time, `Late delivery %`, `Cancel due to delay %`, `Rework_production %`, alternative route availability event during simulated carrier disruption.

- **A/B or phased rollout validation:**
  - Pilot S1+S4+S6+S12 on Standard-stock Service-contract first (22% violation cohort) for 4 weeks, compare to holdout warehouse/region.
  - Pilot S2+S3+S7 on MTO production cell CNC_A/B for one product family before full rollout; measure counterfactual: energy/scrap vs wait time distribution.
  - For Critical (S9+S8), shadow-mode triage (log new sub-type but keep old route) for 1 month to validate classification accuracy before switching transport.

- **Drift and rebound detection:**
  - Monitor `Critical-downtime` share and `expedite_flag` inflation (customer gaming), `Batch wait` p90, and `obsolescence waste rate`. Alert if consolidated truck or batching causes late risk > threshold leading to secondary air expedites (detect `Ship ground/consolidated -> late -> Ship air` compensating events within same case).

**B. Governance:**

- **S12 Sustainability exception log:** Every SP1/SP2/Q1 override must create `Sustainability override` event with `reason_code` (customer SLA, stockout, carrier failure, quality exception), `promised_lead_time_hours`, `slack_hours`, and approver role. Reviewed weekly by operations + sustainability owner; exceptions >2 weeks require re-approval. Prevents silent relapse and provides audit trail for precision trade-offs.

- **Segment-level service gates:** No intervention is kept if per-segment late delivery or cancel rate degrades >2-3 pp vs baseline or Critical-downtime p90 exceeds 30h — rollback rule. Resilience test quarterly: simulate carrier outage and verify R1 fallback route execution in log.

- **Quality gate:** Any increase in `Rework production` or damage returns >1 pp triggers pause on batching/consolidation changes and review of inspection_cell capacity.

- **Continuous process mining loop:** Monthly mining of enriched log  update recommender thresholds (route time p85), batch caps, and CNC routing rules based on observed queues and scrap rates, ensuring improvements remain grounded in actual paths and attributes, not generic targets.
