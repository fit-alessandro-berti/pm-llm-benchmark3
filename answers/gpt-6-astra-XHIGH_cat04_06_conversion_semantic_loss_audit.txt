# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The supplied POWL is authoritative. Its edges constrain execution of whole child models; their transitive consequences also apply. Missing edges between incomparable children permit concurrency, rather than prescribing a preferred order.

Use these abbreviations for **visible** traces:

`r` = Register Change; `a` = Assess Risk; `e` = Prepare Evidence; `l` = Legal Review; `c` = Configure Trial; `i` = Inspect Trial; `d` = Correct Defect; `u` = Release Change; `z` = Archive Change.

A word in the table denotes a complete trace after hiding transitions labeled `None`. For an absence-of-order invariant, a negative witness means **rejecting a required trace**, because adding an ordering constraint usually removes behavior rather than admitting a forbidden word.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | Registration occurs first. Registration, assessment, evidence preparation, release, and archive each occur exactly once per completed case. | `r a e c i u z` | Accepting `a r e c i u z`, or repeating registration within a case. | Yes, for completed visible traces. |
| I2 | Assessment and evidence preparation are unordered after registration; neither is optional. | Both `r a e c i u z` and `r e a c i u z`. | Rejecting either ordering; accepting `r a c i u z` with no evidence preparation. | Both orders are observable. Actual concurrent enabling/overlap is not established by words alone. |
| I3 | Legal review requires assessment, but does not require evidence preparation. | `r a l e c i u z` | Accepting `r l a e c i u z`; rejecting the positive witness solely because evidence is unfinished. | Yes for these ordering constraints; silent branch execution itself is hidden. |
| I4 | Legal review is an exclusive choice between one review and a silent skip. | Both `r a e l c i u z` and `r a e c i u z`. | Accepting `r a e l l c i u z`; eliminating all traces with review or all traces without it. | Review presence/absence is observable. The identity and timing of the skip transition are not. |
| I5 | Verification starts only after both assessment and evidence preparation finish. | `r e a c i u z` | Accepting `r a c i e u z` or `r e c i a u z`. | Yes for the visible start condition. |
| I6 | Optional legal review and verification have no ordering edge between them. When their prerequisites hold, either can proceed; review can occur between verification steps. | `r a e l c i u z`, `r a e c l i u z`, and `r a e c i l u z`. | Losing any of these because a converter serializes the two child models. | Their interleavings are observable; true concurrency needs additional causal/enabling evidence. |
| I7 | The verification projection is exactly `(c i)(d c i)*`. Each repeated body is preceded by one correction; correction is outside the body. The loop does not repeat assessment, evidence preparation, or the legal choice. | `r a e c i d c l i u z` | Accepting `r a e c i c i u z`, `r a e c d i c i u z`, or `r a e c i d a e c i u z`. | Yes for activity order/counts and visible loop scope; not for the placement of silent loop routing. |
| I8 | At least one complete verification body executes; zero corrections are allowed. | `r a e c i u z` | Accepting `r a e u z`, or requiring correction on every case. | Yes. |
| I9 | Release waits for the verification loop to exit and the optional-legal child to complete, including its skip alternative. | `r a e c i l u z` and `r a e c i u z`. | Accepting `r a e c u i z` or `r a e c i u l z`. | Partly: premature visible release is observable, but completion of a silent skip requires marking/routing inspection. |
| I10 | Archive follows release and is the last visible activity. A completed accepting-net execution must reach exactly its declared final marking. | `r a e c i u z`, reaching exactly `fm`. | Accepting `r a e c i z u` or `r a e c i u z l`; treating `fm` plus residual tokens as successful completion. | Visible finality is observable; exact marking equality and residual tokens are not properties of the word alone. |

For any iteration count `n >= 1`, choose either zero or one `l`. The verification chain is `c i (d c i)^(n-1)`. Enumerate all linear extensions satisfying the table's dependencies, with `r` first and `u z` last. This defines an independent reference for the completed visible language. It does not by itself define a true-concurrency equivalence criterion.

The source has nine distinct non-null activity labels. Repeated `c`, `i`, and `d` events are repeated executions of loop activities, not distinct source activities sharing a label. The binary POWL loop means **body, then zero or more redo/body pairs**; it is not a zero-or-more body loop, and its second child is not an exit activity.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1, **primary**: POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN; validate BPMN → accepting Petri net | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | Avoids imposing a process-tree structure on the source's general partial order. Provides an executable acceptance reference and a stakeholder diagram. | Silent routing, nontrivial split/join structure, and reconstructed BPMN start/end behavior may obscure or change acceptance. | Conversion exception, missing/invalid markings, failed diagnostics, or invariant/round-trip mismatch. |
| P2, conditional: POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN; also validate tree → net and BPMN → net | `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; if successful, `convert_to_petri_net(tree)` and `convert_to_bpmn(tree)`; then `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | May yield a clearer structured diagram if a faithful tree is actually obtainable. The extra tree-to-net conversion isolates the tree stage for testing. | A tree converter may reject the overlapping dependency structure, serialize it, or introduce duplicated activities/choices whose causal meaning differs. | A tree-conversion exception is a legitimate pipeline failure. Successful construction still fails the audit if behavior or required concurrency changes. |

There is an N-shaped dependency pattern among assessment, evidence preparation, optional legal review, and verification: assessment precedes both legal review and verification; evidence precedes verification; evidence and legal review are incomparable. This is not a straightforward nesting of sequence and parallel blocks over those same child occurrences. It explains why a structural net-to-tree conversion may fail. It does **not** prove that no tree with duplication or enumerated alternatives could reproduce the visible trace language. Such a tree could still change causal/branching behavior.

No direct POWL-to-BPMN call is assumed.

# 3. Conversion Code

Run this code after the authoritative question code has constructed `source_powl`. Section 5 continues in the same Python module/session. The code retains all returned nets, records exceptions with tracebacks, checks markings explicitly, and distinguishes failed conversion from failed rendering. It does not invent markings or repair a failed conversion silently.

```python
from pathlib import Path
from collections import Counter
from numbers import Integral
import json
import traceback

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet, Marking

OUT = Path("conversion_audit")
OUT.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
NETS = {}
BPMNS = {}
DIAGNOSTICS = {}
EVENTS = []
FAILED = object()


def event(stage, outcome, **details):
    row = {"stage": stage, "outcome": outcome, **details}
    EVENTS.append(row)
    print(json.dumps(row, default=str))


def failed(stage, exc):
    event(stage, "failed", exception_type=type(exc).__name__,
          message=str(exc), traceback=traceback.format_exc())


def attempt(stage, fn):
    try:
        result = fn()
        event(stage, "returned")
        return result
    except Exception as exc:
        failed(stage, exc)
        return FAILED


def save_report():
    report = {
        "pm4py_version": getattr(pm4py, "__version__", "unknown"),
        "events": EVENTS,
        "net_diagnostics": DIAGNOSTICS,
    }
    (OUT / "audit_report.json").write_text(
        json.dumps(report, indent=2, default=str), encoding="utf-8"
    )


def marking_error(net, marking):
    if marking is None:
        return "marking is None"
    if not isinstance(marking, Marking):
        return "not a PM4Py Marking"
    for place, tokens in marking.items():
        if place not in net.places:
            return "marking refers to a place outside this net"
        if not isinstance(tokens, Integral) or tokens < 0:
            return "token counts must be nonnegative integers"
    # An empty Marking is not None. Its suitability is checked by soundness
    # and the source-language/finality tests, rather than by truthiness.
    return None


def boolean_verdict(result):
    # check_soundness may return (bool, diagnostics). bool((False, ...))
    # would incorrectly report success, so never coerce the whole tuple.
    value = result[0] if isinstance(result, tuple) and result else result
    return value if isinstance(value, bool) else None


def audit_net(name, net, im, fm):
    record = {}
    wf = attempt(name + ":workflow_net",
                 lambda: pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    record["workflow_net"] = None if wf is FAILED else boolean_verdict(wf)
    record["initial_marking_error"] = marking_error(net, im)
    record["final_marking_error"] = marking_error(net, fm)
    record["markings_usable"] = (
        record["initial_marking_error"] is None
        and record["final_marking_error"] is None
    )
    if record["markings_usable"]:
        sound = attempt(
            name + ":soundness",
            lambda: pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm,
                                          print_diagnostics=True),
        )
        record["sound"] = (
            None if sound is FAILED else boolean_verdict(sound)
        )
        record["soundness_detail"] = (
            "diagnostic call failed" if sound is FAILED else repr(sound)
        )
    else:
        record["sound"] = None
        record["soundness_detail"] = "not tested: invalid/missing marking"
        event(name + ":soundness", "not tested",
              reason="No valid accepting marking pair; net quarantined")
    record["visible_transition_counts"] = dict(Counter(
        t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None
    ))
    record["silent_transition_count"] = sum(
        t.label is None for t in net.transitions
    )
    DIAGNOSTICS[name] = record


def keep_net(name, result):
    if result is FAILED:
        return None
    if not isinstance(result, (tuple, list)) or len(result) != 3:
        event(name, "failed", reason="Expected (net, im, fm)")
        return None
    net, im, fm = result
    if not isinstance(net, PetriNet):
        event(name, "failed", reason="Missing or invalid Petri net")
        return None
    NETS[name] = (net, im, fm)
    # Run the workflow diagnostic even when accepting markings are unusable.
    audit_net(name, net, im, fm)
    if DIAGNOSTICS[name]["markings_usable"]:
        attempt(name + ":save_visualization",
                lambda: pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(
                    net, im, fm, str(OUT / (name + ".png"))))
    return NETS[name]


def keep_bpmn(name, result):
    if result is FAILED or result is None:
        if result is None:
            event(name, "failed", reason="Converter returned None")
        return None
    BPMNS[name] = result
    attempt(name + ":save_visualization",
            lambda: pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(
                result, str(OUT / (name + ".png"))))
    return result


attempt("source_powl:save_visualization",
        lambda: pm4py.save_vis_powl(
            source_powl, str(OUT / "00_source_powl.png")))

source_result = attempt(
    "POWL_to_net", lambda: pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)
)
source_accepting_net = keep_net("01_net_from_powl", source_result)
if source_accepting_net is None:
    save_report()
    raise RuntimeError("POWL conversion failed; inspect audit_report.json")

net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = source_accepting_net
if not DIAGNOSTICS["01_net_from_powl"]["markings_usable"]:
    save_report()
    raise RuntimeError("Source net lacks valid accepting markings")

bpmn_from_net = keep_bpmn(
    "02_bpmn_from_net",
    attempt("net_to_BPMN", lambda: pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)),
)

# P2 failure is recorded explicitly and does not prevent auditing P1.
tree_from_net = None
try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    if tree_from_net is None:
        raise RuntimeError("Process-tree converter returned None")
except Exception as exc:
    tree_from_net = None
    failed("net_to_process_tree", exc)
    event("P2", "conversion failed",
          interpretation=("No usable tree produced. Inspect the exception: "
                          "structural rejection differs from an API or "
                          "environment failure. P2 is unavailable."))
else:
    event("net_to_process_tree", "returned")
    attempt("tree:save_visualization",
            lambda: pm4py.save_vis_process_tree(
                tree_from_net, str(OUT / "03_tree_from_net.png")))
    keep_net(
        "04_net_from_tree",
        attempt("tree_to_net",
                lambda: pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net)),
    )
    bpmn_from_tree = keep_bpmn(
        "05_bpmn_from_tree",
        attempt("tree_to_BPMN",
                lambda: pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)),
    )

# Every successfully produced BPMN gets its own round-trip attempt.
for bpmn_name, bpmn in list(BPMNS.items()):
    keep_net(
        bpmn_name + "_roundtrip_net",
        attempt(bpmn_name + ":BPMN_to_net",
                lambda bpmn=bpmn: pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)),
    )

save_report()
```

A failed workflow-net or soundness diagnostic quarantines the artifact even if later conversions are attempted for investigation. Graphviz/rendering failures affect review readiness and are recorded separately from behavioral conversion failures. This answer supplies reproducible checks; it does not report an executed PM4Py conversion or a successful diagnostic result.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

The stage cells below are prospective assessments, conditional on a converter producing a usable result. No cell is `validated preserved`, because no PM4Py conversion or model diagnostic was executed here. In an executed audit, replace assessments with observed results: a failed stage becomes `conversion failed`, and its unavailable downstream stages become `not tested`. The round-trip column covers each produced BPMN separately; P2 also inherits uncertainty from its tree stage.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1: mandatory activities and registration first | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | Compare accepted words and activity occurrences against the independent baseline. |
| I2: both assessment/evidence orders | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | Both orders must remain possible. A tree extraction may impose a sequence. |
| I3: legal after assessment, possibly before evidence | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | The witness with review before evidence detects an overbroad synchronization barrier. |
| I4: exactly one review or silent skip | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | Require both visible alternatives. Hiding tau does not justify dropping its enabling constraints. |
| I5: verification waits for assessment and evidence | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | Reject early configuration; inspect synchronization, including silent routing. |
| I6: legal/verification incomparability and true concurrency | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | Trace interleavings establish absence of a fixed order, not causal concurrency. |
| I7: body/redo loop scope | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | Check the `(c i)(d c i)*` projection, including legal review interleaved within it. |
| I8: at least one body execution | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | Zero-body acceptance is a counterexample. |
| I9: release synchronization | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | A visually plausible merge may implement the wrong token behavior. |
| I10: archive last and proper completion | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | Exact final marking, residual-token checks, and soundness are required. |
| Visible activity identity and label mapping | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | Duplicated target nodes may share a label. Record their provenance rather than assuming one node per activity. |
| Initial/final acceptance boundary | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | ambiguous | BPMN start/end events and regenerated markings must represent the same accepted executions. |

**Concurrency.** A net that offers an exclusive choice between two serializations can accept the same two words as two concurrent activities. Thus bidirectional trace inclusion, even unbounded, is weaker than preservation of true concurrency. For a stronger audit, examine reachable enabling states, transition independence/conflict, and causal behavior, allowing for silent routing refinements. Immediately enabled visible transitions need not line up across different encodings; silent closures and an explicit correspondence are needed.

**The absent legal/verification edge.** Legal review must not become a prerequisite for configuration, and completion of verification must not become a prerequisite for review. Nor should the diagram accidentally force evidence to precede legal review. Preserve the mixed dependency pattern, including a review between configuration and inspection. The POWL expresses possible overlap, not measured activity durations or a requirement to overlap on every case.

**Silent optional behavior and the join.** Tau is invisible, not semantically inert: it can consume and produce tokens that record a choice or satisfy synchronization. A skip still completes the optional-legal child before release. An AND join expecting tokens from both mutually exclusive alternatives can deadlock; an exclusive merge where independent branches must synchronize can release too early.

**Loops.** The body executes first, then either exits or performs correction followed by another whole body. Review remains an independent, once-per-case optional activity; it is not copied into every iteration. A BPMN loop marker, backward sequence flow, or group of gateways is acceptable only if its execution semantics realizes this structure.

**Labels and markings.** Compare labels exactly; only `None` is hidden. Empty strings are not automatically tau. Label equality is a good observation mapping for this source, but target node duplication requires an activity-provenance map and may conceal changed causality. Extra nodes with the same label are not automatically a behavioral defect. Conversely, renamed visible activities are not automatically equivalent. Acceptance uses each net's own initial and final markings; place identifiers and the literal shape of markings need not match across nets. A non-null marking is necessary but does not establish correct acceptance.

**BPMN routing.** Gateway type, sequence-flow direction, and token behavior matter. The diagram's appearance is insufficient evidence, and the BPMN-to-net converter provides an interpretation that also needs auditing. A shared misunderstanding between forward and reverse converters can survive a round trip.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

This continues Section 3. It compares every usable generated net—including the tree intermediate and both possible BPMN round trips—with the source net and an independently enumerated POWL trace language.

The domain is completed visible words of length at most 14, with at most three configurations, three inspections, and two corrections. The source therefore contributes all cases with one to three verification iterations, with and without review. Bounding the individual loop labels also prevents malformed target loops from consuming unlimited exploration. Other visible labels remain observable and count toward the length bound.

Exploration follows silent transitions even when the visible-length limit is reached. A visited key contains **both marking and visible prefix**: marking alone would lose distinct trace histories. Token/state limits are resource safeguards; hitting either makes the result incomplete, not equivalent. Normalizing away explicit zero-token entries preserves mathematical marking equality and does not change positive token counts.

```python
from collections import deque
from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics

LABEL = {
    "r": "Register Change", "a": "Assess Risk", "e": "Prepare Evidence",
    "l": "Legal Review", "c": "Configure Trial", "i": "Inspect Trial",
    "d": "Correct Defect", "u": "Release Change", "z": "Archive Change",
}
MAX_ITERATIONS = 3
MAX_VISIBLE = 3 * MAX_ITERATIONS + 5  # 14, including optional review
MAX_STATES = 200_000
MAX_TOKENS = 100
LOOP_CAPS = {
    LABEL["c"]: MAX_ITERATIONS,
    LABEL["i"]: MAX_ITERATIONS,
    LABEL["d"]: MAX_ITERATIONS - 1,
}


def word(short):
    return tuple(LABEL[x] for x in short.split())


def canonical_marking(marking):
    return Marking({p: int(n) for p, n in marking.items() if n != 0})


def bounded_visible_language(net, im, fm):
    # The supplied converters are expected to produce ordinary P/T nets.
    # Do not silently apply these firing semantics to reset/inhibitor nets.
    if type(net) is not PetriNet:
        raise TypeError("Explorer requires an ordinary PetriNet")
    for arc in net.arcs:
        if (type(arc) is not PetriNet.Arc
                or not isinstance(arc.weight, Integral)
                or arc.weight <= 0
                or arc.properties.get("arctype") is not None):
            raise TypeError("Explorer requires ordinary positive-weight arcs")
    if any(t.label is not None and not isinstance(t.label, str)
           for t in net.transitions):
        raise TypeError("Expected string visible labels, or None for tau")
    for marking in (im, fm):
        issue = marking_error(net, marking)
        if issue is not None:
            raise ValueError(issue)

    # Coordinates are local to this net; cross-net comparisons use words.
    places = tuple(net.places)

    def key(marking):
        return tuple(marking.get(p, 0) for p in places)

    initial = canonical_marking(im)
    final = canonical_marking(fm)
    completed = set()
    cuts = set()
    final_with_enabled_transitions = False
    if sum(initial.values()) > MAX_TOKENS:
        return {"traces": completed, "exhaustive": False,
                "cuts": ["initial token cap"], "states": 0,
                "final_with_enabled_transitions": False}

    queue = deque([(initial, ())])
    seen = {(key(initial), ())}
    while queue:
        marking, prefix = queue.popleft()
        enabled = list(pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking))
        # Equality, not coverage, subset inclusion, or absence of enabled work.
        if marking == final:
            completed.add(prefix)
            if enabled:
                final_with_enabled_transitions = True
            # Continue: accepted continuations can expose improper finality.

        for transition in enabled:
            label = transition.label
            if label is None:
                next_prefix = prefix
            else:
                if len(prefix) >= MAX_VISIBLE:
                    continue  # Outside the declared comparison domain.
                if (label in LOOP_CAPS
                        and prefix.count(label) >= LOOP_CAPS[label]):
                    continue  # Outside the declared comparison domain.
                next_prefix = prefix + (label,)

            successor = semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
            if successor is None:
                raise RuntimeError("Enabled transition could not be executed")
            successor = canonical_marking(successor)
            if sum(successor.values()) > MAX_TOKENS:
                cuts.add("token cap")
                continue
            state = (key(successor), next_prefix)
            if state in seen:
                continue  # Includes silent cycles returning to the same state.
            if len(seen) >= MAX_STATES:
                cuts.add("state cap")
                continue
            seen.add(state)
            queue.append((successor, next_prefix))

    return {"traces": completed, "exhaustive": not cuts,
            "cuts": sorted(cuts), "states": len(seen),
            "final_with_enabled_transitions": final_with_enabled_transitions}


def expected_source_language():
    result = set()
    for iterations in range(1, MAX_ITERATIONS + 1):
        for has_legal in (False, True):
            labels = {x: LABEL[x] for x in ("r", "a", "e", "u", "z")}
            if has_legal:
                labels["l"] = LABEL["l"]
            chain = []
            for iteration in range(1, iterations + 1):
                if iteration > 1:
                    redo = "d" + str(iteration - 1)
                    labels[redo] = LABEL["d"]
                    chain.append(redo)
                for activity in ("c", "i"):
                    occurrence = activity + str(iteration)
                    labels[occurrence] = LABEL[activity]
                    chain.append(occurrence)

            predecessors = {node: set() for node in labels}

            def edge(before, after):
                predecessors[after].add(before)

            edge("r", "a")
            edge("r", "e")
            edge("a", chain[0])
            edge("e", chain[0])
            for before, after in zip(chain, chain[1:]):
                edge(before, after)
            edge(chain[-1], "u")
            if has_legal:
                edge("a", "l")
                edge("l", "u")
            edge("u", "z")

            def enumerate_orders(done, prefix):
                if len(done) == len(labels):
                    if len(prefix) <= MAX_VISIBLE:
                        result.add(prefix)
                    return
                for node in labels:
                    if node not in done and predecessors[node] <= done:
                        enumerate_orders(done | {node},
                                         prefix + (labels[node],))

            enumerate_orders(set(), ())
    return result


POSITIVE = [word(x) for x in (
    "r a e c i u z",
    "r e a c i u z",
    "r a l e c i u z",
    "r a e l c i u z",
    "r a e c l i u z",
    "r a e c i l u z",
    "r a e c i d l c i u z",
    "r a e c i d c l i u z",
    "r e a c i d c i d c i u z",
)]
NEGATIVE = [word(x) for x in (
    "a r e c i u z",
    "r l a e c i u z",
    "r a c i e u z",
    "r e c i a u z",
    "r a e u z",
    "r a e i c u z",
    "r a e d c i u z",
    "r a e c i c i u z",
    "r a e c d i c i u z",
    "r a e c i d u z",
    "r a e l l c i u z",
    "r a e c u i z",
    "r a e c i u l z",
    "r a e c i z u",
    "r a e c i u z l",
    "r a e c i d a e c i u z",
)]
EXPECTED = expected_source_language()
assert set(POSITIVE) <= EXPECTED
assert not (set(NEGATIVE) & EXPECTED)
assert all(len(w) <= MAX_VISIBLE for w in POSITIVE + NEGATIVE)
assert all(all(w.count(label) <= cap for label, cap in LOOP_CAPS.items())
           for w in POSITIVE + NEGATIVE)


def sample_words(words, limit=10):
    return [list(w) for w in sorted(words)[:limit]]


def witness_results(exploration):
    accepted = exploration["traces"]
    complete = exploration["exhaustive"]
    return {
        "positive": [
            {"trace": list(w), "result": (
                "pass" if w in accepted else "fail" if complete
                else "inconclusive")}
            for w in POSITIVE
        ],
        "negative": [
            {"trace": list(w), "result": (
                "fail" if w in accepted else "pass" if complete
                else "inconclusive")}
            for w in NEGATIVE
        ],
    }


EXPLORATIONS = {}
FOOTPRINTS = {}
# Full reachability can be expensive. Enable only for nets whose ordinary
# workflow-net soundness/acceptance diagnostics have succeeded.
RUN_REACHABILITY = False
# Playout is supplementary sampling, never an acceptance/equivalence proof.
RUN_PLAYOUT = False

for name, (net, im, fm) in NETS.items():
    if not DIAGNOSTICS[name]["markings_usable"]:
        event(name + ":validation", "not tested",
              reason="Missing/invalid accepting markings")
        continue

    fp = attempt(name + ":footprints",
                 lambda: pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm))
    if fp is not FAILED:
        FOOTPRINTS[name] = fp
        event(name + ":footprint_data", "recorded", value=repr(fp))

    exploration = attempt(
```

If the queue is exhausted **without any resource cut**, this algorithm is exhaustive for the declared bounded completed visible language of that ordinary net: all enabled transitions are explored, and histories are merged only when both their marking and visible word agree. Silent cycles returning to the same marking/prefix add no new finite words and are safely deduplicated. Silent cycles that create new markings eventually hit a resource limit if necessary, producing an explicitly incomplete result. An incomplete source/target comparison cannot establish absence of a witness or trace-language equality; an actually observed forbidden completion is still a counterexample.

The independent reference enumeration is exhaustive for the source within the chosen bound and yields 45 distinct visible words. Equality with the source net alone would permit a shared POWL-to-net conversion error; requiring agreement with this reference reduces that risk. It still checks finite words, not branching or causal equivalence. Passing tests should therefore be recorded as `validated preserved` only with their scope, such as “completed visible traces through three verification iterations.”

Soundness and workflow-net results from Section 3 accompany every net comparison. Soundness addresses completion/dead behavior under the diagnostic's supported semantics; it does not prove equivalence to POWL, and it does not require every possible looping execution to terminate. Footprints summarize relations and can expose discrepancies, but loops and choices can make those relations misleading as concurrency evidence.

The optional reachability call is disabled by default because an unguarded full-state construction can be impractical. A successfully generated full reachability graph can support stronger state-based checks, but equal state counts are not equivalence, and different state counts may merely reflect silent routing. A full proof would compare properly accepting, tau-abstracted behavior using a stated equivalence relation. Playout is also optional: record samples, and establish completion by exact-marking replay/exploration before treating them as accepted traces. Sample absence proves nothing; a sampled prefix outside the bounded language is not automatically a forbidden completed trace.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

Parallel gateways should communicate independent branches and the synchronization they require. In particular, evidence preparation must not block legal review, while it must block verification. A large parallel split followed by one broad join can falsely suggest that all downstream activities share the same prerequisites. Review the actual sequence flows and token dependencies, not just the gateway symbols.

Exclusive gateways implement alternatives such as legal review versus skip. An exclusive merge is not a synchronization barrier. Label the legal alternatives “Review” and “Skip review,” and explain that skipping completes the optional branch. The source specifies nondeterministic optionality, not a business rule determining when review is required; no guard condition should be invented during conversion.

A backward flow, multiple gateways, or an activity loop marker may obscure the loop's scope. Annotate the region: “Configure → Inspect; optionally Correct → repeat Configure → Inspect; at least one body execution.” Keep legal review visually separate from this region while documenting that it may occur during verification.

Silent transitions may become gateways or other routing constructs. Hiding them in a simplified diagram must not hide a necessary choice or join. Conversely, routing nodes should not be presented as additional human tasks. Retain a detailed technical view and a mapping from generated nodes to source activities and operators.

Horizontal/vertical placement is not an ordering relation. Put a note on the stakeholder diagram: “Position does not impose execution order; directed flows and gateways define dependencies.” Explicitly annotate assessment/evidence as unordered, and legal review/verification as unordered after their respective prerequisites. Annotate release with “Wait for verification exit AND completion of review-or-skip.” Include the canonical source version and validation-report identifier so the visual cannot silently outlive its evidence.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a result when any of the following applies:

1. A converter raises an exception, returns `None`, or produces an unexpected object/tuple. Record the stage and traceback. Failure of P2 does not invalidate an independently validated P1.
2. Initial or final markings are missing, malformed, or refer to another net's places. Do not guess replacements.
3. Workflow-net or soundness checks fail; required diagnostics are unsupported or raise exceptions. An unavailable check is unresolved evidence, not a pass.
4. A required ordering/interleaving disappears, including evidence before assessment, legal review before evidence, or legal review between configuration and inspection.
5. Verification can start before either prerequisite, or release can occur before verification completes or before a chosen review completes.
6. Optional review becomes mandatory, impossible, repeated, or unable to complete its skip branch.
7. The loop permits zero bodies, correction before the first body, consecutive bodies without correction, correction followed by exit without another body, or repetition of activities outside the source loop.
8. Archive can precede release, another visible activity can follow archive, or the claimed final marking has enabled transitions inconsistent with terminal completion.
9. Completion is inferred from covering final places, reaching a deadlock, or exhausting the visible-length budget instead of exact final-marking equality. Residual tokens at a supposed completion are a failure.
10. A bounded comparison finds a forbidden accepted word or, when exhaustive, fails to find a required word. If state/token limits are hit, quarantine pending a completed check rather than treating missing observations as established rejection.
11. Labels are lost/changed, visible routing activities are introduced, or duplicated labels cannot be reconciled with source activity identity and behavior.
12. The diagram or deployment engine interprets routing differently from the validated net, or only serialization/rendering success is offered as evidence.

A matching bounded trace set does not override an unsoundness result, a concurrency mismatch, or invalid acceptance markings.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

Store the **POWL model as canonical**, with a stable serialization of activity identities, labels, operator/child structure, and the strict partial order, together with the invariant specification. Retain the supplied construction code or equivalent reproducible source, its version/hash, and the PM4Py version used for generation.

Use **BPMN generated through P1 for stakeholder visualization**, provided it passes the audit and carries the concurrency/loop annotations. The accepting Petri net is the executable validation reference derived from POWL; it is not a replacement authority. Tree models, BPMN models, round-tripped nets, screenshots, and layout files are derivative artifacts.

Each derivative should carry source provenance, conversion pipeline and software version, activity/node mapping, explicit accepting markings for its validation net, workflow/soundness diagnostics, bounded-language and witness results with bounds and cutoff status, and a visual-semantics review. Record unavailable tests and unresolved issues. Native model artifacts and mappings should accompany pictures; a PNG is not a recoverable semantic model.

Allow a process-tree intermediate only conditionally: conversion must succeed, the tree-to-net and tree-derived BPMN round trip must independently pass validation, and any required causal-concurrency claim must receive evidence beyond word equality. Treat a structural conversion exception as a reason to retain P1, not to force a “simplified” tree into the canonical workflow.

Make future behavioral edits in the canonical POWL, update invariants/witnesses, and regenerate derivatives. Keep cosmetic layout changes separately. If stakeholders propose a BPMN behavior change, translate it into an explicit POWL revision and review the resulting semantic difference before regeneration. Do not repeatedly convert edited derivative models back into the authority; that invites cumulative round-trip drift.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every enabled check passes:

- Bounded trace equality does not prove equality for four or more verification iterations, words beyond the visible bound, or malformed behaviors excluded by the individual loop-label bounds.
- Visible trace equality does not prove true-concurrency preservation, branching/weak bisimulation, or preservation of decision points. Serial alternatives can mimic concurrent interleavings.
- Deduplicating silent cycles preserves finite visible-language exploration but does not prove divergence sensitivity, fairness, or inevitable termination. The source itself permits indefinitely repeated verification.
- Soundness and round-trip agreement do not independently prove that every BPMN engine implements the generated diagram as the reverse converter does. Shared conversion assumptions can conceal errors.
- The source and checks contain no timing, resources, data conditions, real-world legal-review criteria, or correction-effectiveness rules; they cannot validate those properties.
- Trace and structural checks do not establish that stakeholders will interpret the diagram correctly, or that duplicated target nodes preserve source activity identity without a reviewed provenance mapping.
