# 1. Current-process diagnosis

The warranty process exhibits a classic "false efficiency" paradox where the most frequent path optimizes for upstream throughput at the expense of downstream stability, compliance, and total cost.

*   **Performance vs. Quality Contradiction:** The dominant path (Intake -> Auto -> Fast Approval -> Dispatch) handles 2,300 of 5,000 cases with a median cycle time of roughly 5 hours (0.8 + 1.0 + 3.0h). However, this path generates a **14.1% complaint/reopen rate**, compared to only **3.3%** for the slower Repair Quote path. While Fast Approval cases are **31% cheaper upfront**, they are only **9% cheaper after accounting for rework**, erasing the efficiency gain.
*   **Compliance Violations:** Conformance checking reveals a **log fitness of 0.91** but only **68% fitting traces**, indicating widespread deviation from policy. Specifically, **Rule R1** (High-value >EUR 400 requires Manual/Fraud review) is frequently violated via "Fast approval before Evidence completeness" and "Dispatch replacement without risk review for high-value devices." This exposes the organization to significant financial risk (EUR 410 per wrong replacement on high-value devices).
*   **Evidence Loop Inefficiency:** The "Request missing evidence" activity is a major bottleneck. **26% of cases** entering this loop request evidence at least twice, and **320 cases abandon** here. The loop back to Manual Eligibility Review (890 cases) adds a mean delay of **34 hours**, contributing to the **140-hour median cycle time** for the "Unclear defect" segment.
*   **Resource Bottlenecks:** Manual Eligibility Reviewers are utilized near the **82% threshold** where queues grow sharply. Fraud/Risk Review is a known bottleneck on Mondays. Pushing more cases to manual review without segmentation risks collapsing the service level for Premium customers who tolerate only **24 hours** for low-risk accessories.
*   **Downstream Failure Costs:** The transition **Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen** (260 cases) has a mean lag of **96 hours**. This indicates that errors are not detected until the customer receives the wrong item, triggering expensive reshipments and service credits, particularly for Premium segments where service-credit exposure is high.

# 2. Redesign A: Segment-Aware Dynamic Triage

*   **Redesigned Process Flow:** Modify the **Intake claim -> Auto eligibility screen** decision logic to incorporate segment attributes (Device Value, Customer Tier) before routing.
    *   **High-Value Devices (>EUR 400):** Bypass Auto Eligibility -> Fast Approval. Route directly to **Manual Eligibility Review** or **Fraud/Risk Review** to enforce Compliance Rule R1.
    *   **Premium Low-Risk Accessories:** Route to a "Fast Track" lane that allows **Dispatch replacement** even if evidence is partially missing (enforcing Rule R2 exception) to meet the 24-hour expectation.
    *   **Standard Low-Risk:** Remain on current Auto path but with updated rules.
*   **Target Segments/Paths:** Targets the **Premium high-value device (14% share)** and **Standard high-value device (21% share)** segments currently leaking through the Auto screen. Also targets **Premium low-risk accessory (18% share)** to protect SLA.
*   **Mechanism of Improvement:** By enforcing R1 upstream, we prevent the **EUR 410 wrong replacement cost** and reduce the **22% complaint rate** on high-value fast paths. By fast-tracking Premium low-risk, we reduce service-credit exposure caused by exceeding 24 hours.
*   **Deliberate Sacrifices/Unresolved:**
    *   **Speed for High-Value:** Median cycle time for high-value claims will increase from **20 hours to ~96 hours** (reviewed/repair path times).
    *   **Resource Pressure:** Updating auto-screen rules may increase manual-review inflow by **15-35%**, risking queue collapse if the 7 reviewers exceed **82% utilization**.
    *   **Unresolved:** Does not address the **320 abandonments** in the evidence loop for standard customers.

# 3. Redesign B: Upstream Evidence Integrity Portal

*   **Redesigned Process Flow:** Insert a mandatory **Digital Evidence Portal** step before **Intake claim** completion for segments with historically missing evidence.
    *   **Logic:** If defect type = "Unclear" or segment = "Standard High-Value," the customer must upload photos/serials via the portal before the claim ID is generated.
    *   **AI Validation:** Implement an automated check at intake to reject incomplete evidence immediately, preventing the **Intake -> Auto -> Request missing evidence** loop.
*   **Target Segments/Paths:** Targets the **Unclear defect / missing evidence (22% share)** segment and the **Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review (890 cases)** loop.
*   **Mechanism of Improvement:** Reduces the **26% case rework rate** where evidence is requested twice. Eliminates the **34-hour mean delay** for evidence loops back to manual review. Reduces the **320 claim abandonments** caused by prolonged uncertainty (by failing fast upfront instead of late).
*   **Deliberate Sacrifices/Unresolved:**
    *   **Upfront Friction:** May increase abandonment for customers with **low digital literacy**, potentially shifting abandonment from mid-process (320 cases) to intake.
    *   **Implementation Complexity:** Requires development of the portal and AI validation logic; cannot be deployed in the **two-week window** available for rule updates.
    *   **Unresolved:** Does not fix compliance violations (R1) where evidence is present but risk review is skipped.

# 4. Redesign C: Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate

*   **Redesigned Process Flow:** Insert a mandatory **Quality Check** activity between **Fast approval** and **Dispatch replacement** for specific high-risk combinations.
    *   **Logic:** If Device Value > EUR 400 OR Customer Tier = Premium, route to **Quality Check** (utilizing the 2 underused inspectors) before dispatch.
    *   **Flow:** Fast approval -> Quality Check -> Dispatch replacement.
    *   **Exception:** Low-risk accessories bypass this gate to maintain speed.
*   **Target Segments/Paths:** Targets the **Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen (260 cases)** transition and the **High-Value** segment where wrong replacement costs **EUR 410**.
*   **Mechanism of Improvement:** Catches incorrect part selection before it reaches the customer, converting a **96-hour mean complaint resolution** cycle into a contained internal rework. Leverages **underused inspectors** (currently idle except after repair campaigns). Reduces the **14.1% complaint rate** on fast approval paths.
*   **Deliberate Sacrifices/Unresolved:**
    *   **Cycle Time:** Adds **6-18 hours** to the dispatch process, potentially violating the **24-hour SLA** for Premium low-risk accessories if not segmented correctly.
    *   **Cost:** Increases touch cost per case (EUR 38/hour labor), though offset by avoided EUR 410 errors.
    *   **Unresolved:** Does not address the root cause of eligibility errors (Redesign A) or missing evidence (Redesign B).

# 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

*   **Redesign A (Dynamic Triage):**
    *   **Failure Mode 1:** **Manual Review Queue Collapse.** If the **15-35% increase in inflow** pushes reviewer utilization beyond **82%**, queue times will spike exponentially (from mean 7.5h to potentially >40h), violating Premium SLAs.
    *   **Failure Mode 2:** **Fraud Bottleneck.** Routing high-value claims to Fraud/Risk Review may exacerbate the **Monday bottleneck** caused by marketing campaigns, delaying decisions beyond the **p90 of 44 hours**.
    *   **Bottleneck Migration:** Moves from downstream Complaints/Returns to upstream Manual Eligibility Review.

*   **Redesign B (Evidence Portal):**
    *   **Failure Mode 1:** **Intake Abandonment Spike.** Customers with **low digital literacy** may drop off before claim creation, increasing abandonment beyond the current **320 cases** in the evidence loop, resulting in lost revenue/customer goodwill.
    *   **Failure Mode 2:** **False Rejections.** AI validation may incorrectly reject valid evidence, forcing customers into the **Manual eligibility review** path unnecessarily, increasing cost without quality gain.
    *   **Bottleneck Migration:** Moves from mid-process Evidence Requests to upfront Intake conversion rate.

*   **Redesign C (Quality Gate):**
    *   **Failure Mode 1:** **Inspector Bottleneck.** The **2 inspectors** are currently underused, but if **260+ cases** are routed to them plus existing repair checks, capacity may saturate, causing the **6-18 hour** add-on to stretch to **p90 36+ hours**.
    *   **Failure Mode 2:** **SLA Breach.** Applying the gate to Premium Low-Risk accessories by mistake would violate the **24-hour service-credit exposure** threshold, increasing costs more than the gate saves.
    *   **Bottleneck Migration:** Moves from downstream Customer Complaints to upstream Dispatch latency.

# 7. Recommended experiment sequence

To validate improvements without destabilizing the process, execute the following staged plan:

1.  **First Pilot: Redesign A (Dynamic Triage) on High-Value Only.**
    *   **Logic:** Lowest implementation complexity (**2-week rule update**) and highest compliance risk mitigation (R1).
    *   **Metrics:** Monitor **Manual Review utilization** (keep <82%), **High-Value complaint rate** (target <10%), and **Cycle time p90** for High-Value.
    *   **Stop/Go Threshold:** **Stop** if Manual Review queue time exceeds **24 hours** (median). **Go** if High-Value complaint rate drops by >50% without queue collapse.
    *   **Downstream Check:** Compare **Customer complaint/reopen** volumes 4 weeks post-implementation to ensure complaints aren't just delayed.

2.  **Second Pilot: Redesign C (Quality Gate) on Standard High-Value.**
    *   **Logic:** Once High-Value routing is stable, address the **EUR 410 error cost**. Exclude Premium Low-Risk to protect SLA.
    *   **Metrics:** **Wrong replacement rate**, **Inspector utilization**, and **Dispatch latency**.
    *   **Stop/Go Threshold:** **Stop** if Dispatch latency p90 exceeds **48 hours**. **Go** if reshipment costs drop by >20%.
    *   **Downstream Check:** Track **Service-credit exposure** to ensure latency doesn't trigger penalties.

3.  **Third Pilot: Redesign B (Evidence Portal) on Standard Segment.**
    *   **Logic:** Highest complexity/risk. Test on Standard customers first (lower SLA risk) before Premium.
    *   **Metrics:** **Intake abandonment rate**, **Evidence loop frequency** (target <10% double requests), and **Digital literacy support tickets**.
    *   **Stop/Go Threshold:** **Stop** if Intake abandonment increases by >5% relative to baseline. **Go** if evidence loop cases drop by >30%.
    *   **Downstream Check:** Verify that reduced evidence loops do not correlate with increased **Claim denied -> END** due to stricter upfront filtering.

**Learning Validation:** To ensure improvements are real and not shifted downstream, maintain a **cohort analysis** of claims closed per week. Track the **total cost per closed claim** (including downstream service credits and reshipments allocated to the original claim ID) for 90 days post-close. If upstream metrics improve but total cost per cohort remains flat, the redesign is merely displacing work, not eliminating it.
