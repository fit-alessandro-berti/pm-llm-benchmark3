# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

Notation: `R` = Register Change, `A` = Assess Risk, `P` = Prepare Evidence, `L` = Legal Review, `C` = Configure Trial, `I` = Inspect Trial, `D` = Correct Defect, `X` = Release Change, and `Z` = Archive Change. A trace is a visible sequence after hiding `tau`. `…` denotes the rest of a trace. Negative witnesses are behaviors a faithful conversion must reject, or interleavings it must not rule out.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | `R` is first and precedes `A` and `P`. | `R,A,P,C,I,X,Z` | A complete trace beginning `A,R,…` or `P,R,…` | Yes, for visible events. |
| S2 | `A` and `P` are unordered after `R`; either may occur first. | `R,A,P,…` and `R,P,A,…` | A model that permits only one of those two orders | Partly: both interleavings are visible, but traces alone do not prove true concurrency. |
| S3 | Legal Review is optional (zero or one execution) and, when present, follows `A`. Its absence is the XOR silent branch. | `R,A,P,C,I,X,Z` and `R,A,L,P,C,I,X,Z` | A trace with `L` before `A`, two `L` occurrences, or a model requiring `L` | Yes for the presence/absence of `L`; the silent branch itself is hidden. |
| S4 | Each verification iteration contains `C` before `I`. | `…,C,I,…` | `…,I,C,…` | Yes. |
| S5 | At least one verification iteration occurs. Each additional iteration is preceded by `D`; the intended visible loop language is `(C,I)(D,C,I)*`. There is no trailing `D` after the final iteration. | `…,C,I,X,Z` and `…,C,I,D,C,I,X,Z` | A complete trace with no `C,I`, `…,C,I,D,X,Z`, or `…,C,I,D,C,I,D,X,Z` | Yes. A converter still has to get the binary LOOP interpretation right. |
| S6 | The optional legal branch and verification loop have no ordering edge between them; they may overlap, subject to `A` before `L` and `A,P` before verification. | `R,A,P,C,L,I,X,Z` (legal work interleaves within a verification iteration) | A model that forces all of `L` before the loop, or forces all of the loop before `L` | Partly: an interleaving can witness availability, but absence of a trace does not alone identify the cause. |
| S7 | `X` waits for completion of both the optional-legal branch (either `L` or its silent skip) and the entire verification loop. | `R,A,L,P,C,I,X,Z`; also the same trace with `L` omitted | `R,A,P,C,X,I,Z` or, when `L` was selected, `R,A,P,C,I,X,L,Z` | Partly: release order is visible; completion of the silent skip is not. |
| S8 | `Z` follows `X`; archive is the last visible activity. A completed execution reaches the exact final marking. | `…,X,Z` and exact equality with the accepting net's final marking | A complete trace ending at `X`, or `…,X,Z,L` | Visible order: yes. Exact marking equality is not visible in the trace. |

The positive interleaving in S6 makes the missing edge between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop` concrete. A trace language can show that an interleaving is possible, but cannot by itself distinguish concurrent transitions from a control-flow construction that happens to permit the same linearizations.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary: POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN → accepting Petri net** | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | The accepting net is a useful behavioral intermediate for preserving synchronization and concurrency; BPMN provides a familiar stakeholder view. Round-tripping exposes conversion drift. | BPMN layout and gateway mapping can obscure semantics; silent routing, gateways, and joins may be transformed; accepting markings may be absent; a successful conversion is not equivalence evidence. | Any conversion exception or null marking needed by the next call; not a workflow net or unsound net; failed witness, exact-final-marking, bounded-trace, or other required semantic check. |
| POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN → accepting Petri net | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; if successful, `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)`; then `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | A process tree can make structured choices, sequences, and loops easier to explain and render. | The source's partially ordered branches and interleaving of legal work with loop internals may not be representable without changing behavior. The Petri-net-to-tree conversion is not guaranteed to succeed; success still needs auditing. | Catch and record a process-tree conversion exception as `conversion failed`; also reject/quarantine a successful result if its BPMN round trip fails semantic checks. |

The primary route does not assume a direct POWL-to-BPMN API. It uses only the stated POWL-to-accepting-net and accepting-net-to-BPMN conversions.

# 3. Conversion Code

This standalone script reconstructs the supplied `source_powl`, checks markings before using them, attempts the process-tree branch without suppressing exceptions, converts every produced BPMN model back to an accepting net, diagnoses every accepting net, and attempts PM4Py visualizations. Visualization errors (for example, a missing renderer) are reported separately from conversion results.

```python
from pathlib import Path
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# Authoritative POWL source from the question.
register = Transition(label="Register Change")
assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()
optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
)

configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP,
    children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
)

release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        register,
        assess_risk,
        prepare_evidence,
        optional_legal,
        verification_loop,
        release_change,
        archive_change,
    ]
)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)

artifact_dir = Path("artifacts")
artifact_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)


def unpack_accepting_net(result, name):
    if not isinstance(result, (tuple, list)) or len(result) != 3:
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"{name}: expected (net, initial_marking, final_marking), "
            f"received {type(result).__name__}"
        )
    return result[0], result[1], result[2]


def diagnose_accepting_net(name, net, im, fm):
    print(f"\\nDiagnostics: {name}")
    if net is None:
        print("  no Petri net")
        return

    visible_labels = [t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None]
    duplicate_labels = sorted(
        label for label in set(visible_labels)
        if visible_labels.count(label) > 1
    )
    print(f"  duplicate visible labels: {duplicate_labels or 'none'}")

    try:
        print(f"  workflow net: {pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"  workflow-net check raised {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")

    if im is None or fm is None:
        print("  soundness check skipped: initial or final marking is None")
        return
    try:
        sound = pm4py.check_soundness(
            net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True
        )
        print(f"  soundness result: {sound}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"  soundness check raised {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")


def save_petri_visual(name, net, im, fm):
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        print(f"  skipped {name} visualization: net or marking is None")
        return
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(
            net, im, fm, str(artifact_dir / f"{name}.pn.png")
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"  saving {name} Petri visualization failed: {exc}")


def accepting_net_from_bpmn(name, bpmn):
    try:
        net, im, fm = unpack_accepting_net(
            pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn), f"{name} BPMN round trip"
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"{name} BPMN-to-net conversion failed: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")
        return None, None, None
    diagnose_accepting_net(name, net, im, fm)
    save_petri_visual(name, net, im, fm)
    return net, im, fm


# POWL → accepting Petri net.
net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = unpack_accepting_net(
    pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl), "POWL-to-Petri-net conversion"
)
diagnose_accepting_net("net_from_powl", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
save_petri_visual("net_from_powl", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)

# Source visualization is useful context, but not semantic evidence.
try:
    pm4py.save_vis_powl(source_powl, str(artifact_dir / "source_powl.png"))
except Exception as exc:
    print(f"Saving source POWL visualization failed: {exc}")

# Accepting Petri net → BPMN, only when the markings needed by the API exist.
bpmn_from_net = None
if im_from_powl is not None and fm_from_powl is not None:
    try:
        bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
            net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
        )
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(
            bpmn_from_net, str(artifact_dir / "bpmn_from_net.bpmn.png")
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"Petri-net-to-BPMN conversion or save failed: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")
else:
    print("Petri-net-to-BPMN skipped: initial or final marking is None")

# BPMN → accepting Petri net round trip.
net_from_bpmn, im_from_bpmn, fm_from_bpmn = (None, None, None)
if bpmn_from_net is not None:
    net_from_bpmn, im_from_bpmn, fm_from_bpmn = accepting_net_from_bpmn(
        "net_roundtrip_from_bpmn", bpmn_from_net
    )

# Try POWL → Petri net → process tree. Preserve the exception as audit evidence.
tree_from_net = None
bpmn_from_tree = None
try:
    if im_from_powl is None or fm_from_powl is None:
        raise ValueError("process-tree conversion needs non-null markings")
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_process_tree(
            tree_from_net, str(artifact_dir / "tree_from_net.pt.png")
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"Saving process-tree visualization failed: {exc}")

    try:
        bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(
            bpmn_from_tree, str(artifact_dir / "bpmn_from_tree.bpmn.png")
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"Process-tree-to-BPMN conversion or save failed: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")
except Exception as exc:
    print(f"Petri-net-to-process-tree conversion failed: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")

# Round-trip every BPMN model that was successfully produced.
net_from_tree_bpmn, im_from_tree_bpmn, fm_from_tree_bpmn = (None, None, None)
if bpmn_from_tree is not None:
    net_from_tree_bpmn, im_from_tree_bpmn, fm_from_tree_bpmn = accepting_net_from_bpmn(
        "net_roundtrip_from_tree_bpmn", bpmn_from_tree
    )
```

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

The statuses below are pre-execution expectations, not claims that a particular installed PM4Py version has passed validation. `validated preserved` should be assigned only after running the checks against the actual artifacts. Each assessment cell uses one of the required terms.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `R` before `A` and `P` | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | not tested | Check visible witness traces and the net's causal structure; a drawing's left-to-right placement is not evidence. |
| `A` and `P` unordered | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Require both `R,A,P,…` and `R,P,A,…`; both traces alone still do not prove true concurrency. Inspect the net for independent enabled work / synchronization structure. |
| Optional `L` after `A`, or silent skip | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Check traces both with and without `L`; verify the skip branch is silent and not a visible invented activity. Hiding tau removes the branch's identity from traces. |
| `C` precedes `I` in each iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | not tested | Check witness and counterexample traces, and ensure repeated iterations do not reverse this order. |
| Binary loop means one or more `(C,I)` iterations, separated by `D` only between iterations | ambiguous | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Inspect the converted net and test against `(C,I)(D,C,I)*`; do not infer loop language merely from a rendered loop arrow. |
| `optional_legal` and verification have no edge and may interleave | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | The POWL source has no edge between these nodes. Check a witness with `L` between `C` and `I` as well as legal work before or after verification. A block-structured tree may serialize them. |
| Release waits for both legal resolution and loop completion | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Inspect the join and reject release traces before `I`/loop completion. The legal skip resolves a branch without visible `L`. |
| `Z` follows `X`; exact accepting final marking | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Compare final-marking equality during trace exploration. Event traces ending in `Z` do not establish exact marking equality. |
| Visible activity labels remain unique and stable | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Source labels are unique; converters may duplicate, relabel, or conflate labels, making trace and event mapping ambiguous. |
| Initial and final markings are present and respected | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Check both for `None`, pass actual markings to converters/diagnostics, and require exact equality at completion. |

True concurrency must be distinguished from arbitrary interleaving. A Petri net can express independent transitions enabled together, while its visible traces are linearizations of those firings. Footprints and a set of interleavings can support a concurrency claim but do not alone establish equivalence of branching behavior. The two source branches are not ordered with respect to one another; adding an order between the optional legal branch and the verification loop loses some allowed executions even if all individual activities remain present.

The optional legal XOR includes a silent branch. Hiding `tau` makes the absent-legal path look like a direct transition between surrounding visible activities. Check both trace alternatives and the converted net structure. The two-child LOOP is especially risky: require the exact intended pattern—at least one trial body, `Correct Defect` between repetitions, and no correction after the final trial—rather than assuming a loop symbol has the desired semantics.

The Release Change join must wait for both branch completions, including the silent legal skip. A converter may render gateways as parallel or exclusive gateways, but a gateway label or shape alone does not establish correct split/join behavior. BPMN notation may include routing nodes that become invisible Petri-net transitions. The source's visible labels are unique, but round trips still need a label mapping audit. Initial and final markings must be carried explicitly; a visually plausible start/end event does not prove that the accepting markings were preserved.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

This bounded comparison uses PM4Py to obtain enabled transitions, then fires ordinary Petri-net arcs explicitly so it can enforce an exact final-marking test. It hides every transition whose label is `None`, suppresses repeated states to avoid silent-cycle spinning, and enforces visible-length, correction/iteration, token, and state bounds. It compares `net_from_powl` with each available BPMN-round-tripped accepting net. The results are exhaustive only within the specified finite bounds if no state/token cutoff was hit; they are not exhaustive for the source's unbounded repetition language.

```python
from collections import deque
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet, Marking


def marking_key(marking):
    return tuple(sorted(
        (place.name, int(count))
        for place, count in marking.items()
        if count
    ))


def fire_ordinary_transition(net, marking, transition):
    """Fire a normal weighted Petri-net transition; reject nonordinary arcs."""
    result = Marking()
    for place, count in marking.items():
        if count:
            result[place] = int(count)

    for arc in transition.in_arcs:
        if not isinstance(arc.source, PetriNet.Place):
            raise TypeError("Expected a place-to-transition input arc")
        if result.get(arc.source, 0) < arc.weight:
            raise RuntimeError("PM4Py returned a transition that is not enabled")
        result[arc.source] -= arc.weight
        if result[arc.source] == 0:
            del result[arc.source]

    for arc in transition.out_arcs:
        if not isinstance(arc.target, PetriNet.Place):
            raise TypeError("Expected a transition-to-place output arc")
        result[arc.target] = result.get(arc.target, 0) + arc.weight
    return result


def bounded_visible_traces(
    net, im, fm, *, max_visible_length=14, max_loop_iterations=3,
    max_states=100000, max_total_tokens=64,
):
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        raise ValueError("A net and non-null initial/final markings are required")
    if max_loop_iterations < 1:
        raise ValueError("At least one verification iteration must be allowed")

    # Revisiting a marking with the same visible prefix through tau cannot add
    # a new future behavior. This closes silent cycles without hiding cutoffs.
    start_trace = ()
    start_key = (marking_key(im), start_trace)
    seen = {start_key}
    queue = deque([(im, start_trace)])
    completed = set()
    truncated = False

    while queue:
        marking, visible = queue.popleft()
        if marking == fm:  # exact whole-marking equality, not subset inclusion
            completed.add(visible)
            continue

        try:
            enabled = list(pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking))
        except Exception as exc:
            raise RuntimeError(
                f"get_enabled_transitions failed: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"
            ) from exc

        enabled.sort(key=lambda t: ("" if t.label is None else str(t.label), t.name))
        for transition in enabled:
            label = transition.label
            next_visible = visible
            if label is not None:
                if len(visible) >= max_visible_length:
                    truncated = True
                    continue
                # Here each Correct Defect separates two iterations.
                defects_so_far = visible.count("Correct Defect")
                if (
                    label == "Correct Defect"
                    and defects_so_far >= max_loop_iterations - 1
                ):
                    truncated = True
                    continue
                next_visible = visible + (str(label),)

            next_marking = fire_ordinary_transition(net, marking, transition)
            total_tokens = sum(int(count) for count in next_marking.values())
            if total_tokens > max_total_tokens:
                truncated = True
                continue

            key = (marking_key(next_marking), next_visible)
            if key in seen:
                continue
            if len(seen) >= max_states:
                truncated = True
                queue.clear()
                break
            seen.add(key)
            queue.append((next_marking, next_visible))

    return {
        "completed_traces": completed,
        "truncated": truncated,
        "visited_states": len(seen),
        "bounds": {
            "max_visible_length": max_visible_length,
            "max_loop_iterations": max_loop_iterations,
            "max_states": max_states,
            "max_total_tokens": max_total_tokens,
        },
    }


def footprint_signature(net, im, fm):
    footprints = pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm)
    # A coarse summary, not an equivalence certificate.
    keys = (
        "activities", "start_activities", "end_activities",
        "sequence", "parallel",
    )
    return {
        key: frozenset(repr(item) for item in footprints.get(key, ()))
        for key in keys
    }


def compare_accepting_nets(name, candidate):
    net, im, fm = candidate
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        print(f"{name}: not comparable; net or marking is None")
        return

    print(f"\\n{name}")
    try:
        print("  workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"  workflow-net check error: {exc}")
    try:
        print("  soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(
            net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True
        ))
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"  soundness check error: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")

    try:
        base_fp = footprint_signature(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
        candidate_fp = footprint_signature(net, im, fm)
        print("  footprint signatures equal:", base_fp == candidate_fp)
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"  footprint comparison unavailable: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")

    base = bounded_visible_traces(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
    other = bounded_visible_traces(net, im, fm)
    base_traces = base["completed_traces"]
    other_traces = other["completed_traces"]
    print("  baseline bounded completed traces:", len(base_traces))
    print("  candidate bounded completed traces:", len(other_traces))
    print("  bounds:", base["bounds"])
    print("  any bound cutoff:", base["truncated"] or other["truncated"])
    print("  traces only in baseline:", len(base_traces - other_traces))
    print("  traces only in candidate:", len(other_traces - base_traces))
    print("  bounded trace sets equal:", base_traces == other_traces)

    positives = [
        # Legal branch skipped; one verification iteration.
        ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
         "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
        # Legal activity interleaves between configure and inspect.
        ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
         "Configure Trial", "Legal Review", "Inspect Trial",
         "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
        # Assess and evidence occur in the other allowed order.
        ("Register Change", "Prepare Evidence", "Assess Risk",
         "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
        # Two trials with one correction between them.
        ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
         "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect",
         "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ]
    negatives = [
        # Release cannot precede verification completion.
        ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
         "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
        # Configure must precede inspect.
        ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
         "Inspect Trial", "Configure Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
        # A completed trace must not stop before archive.
        ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
         "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change"),
        # Correction cannot trail the final iteration before release.
        ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
         "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect",
         "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ]
    print("  positive witnesses present:", [t in other_traces for t in positives])
    print("  negative witnesses absent:", [t not in other_traces for t in negatives])

    # State identifiers are implementation-specific; graph construction alone
    # is not a canonical graph-isomorphism or equivalence check.
    try:
        pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
        print("  reachability graph: constructed")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"  reachability graph unavailable: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")


roundtrips = {
    "BPMN round trip": (net_from_bpmn, im_from_bpmn, fm_from_bpmn),
    "Tree-BPMN round trip": (
        net_from_tree_bpmn, im_from_tree_bpmn, fm_from_tree_bpmn
    ),
}
for comparison_name, accepting_net in roundtrips.items():
    if accepting_net[0] is not None:
        compare_accepting_nets(comparison_name, accepting_net)
```

The bounded comparison is not exhaustive for all source executions because the loop can repeat without limit. Even within its configured bounds, a reported token/state/length/iteration cutoff makes the corresponding comparison incomplete. Footprints are coarse, soundness and workflow-net checks are not equivalence checks, and reachability graph construction is not graph equivalence. These methods complement rather than replace structural inspection and the witness suite. Random/model playout can add examples, but sampled playout is weaker than bounded systematic exploration and must not be treated as proof.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN picture can be misread even when its converted accepting net behaves acceptably. A parallel gateway may represent a real fork/join, or may be a routing artifact introduced by conversion. An exclusive gateway may render the optional legal choice, but the skip may be an unlabeled route whose meaning is easy to miss. A loop marker can suggest that `Correct Defect` happens after every inspection, including the final one, unless the loop's exit behavior is explained. Silent Petri-net transitions may appear as unlabeled BPMN routing nodes or disappear from the drawing entirely.

Graph layout is not a semantic ordering relation. A left-to-right placement of Legal Review before the loop does not prove that the legal branch must finish first; crossing edges or parallel columns do not prove concurrency. Review BPMN control-flow semantics and the accepting net, not just the geometry.

Add a legend stating that unlabeled routing nodes represent silent behavior, annotate the XOR as “Legal Review or skip,” annotate the loop as “one or more Configure Trial → Inspect Trial iterations; Correct Defect only between iterations,” and label the release join as waiting for both legal resolution and verification completion. Add a note that Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence are unordered and that the legal branch may overlap verification. Preserve a mapping from BPMN nodes/gateways to source POWL nodes and Petri-net transitions, including labels and tau transitions.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a derivative if any of these conditions holds:

1. A conversion raises an exception, returns an unusable model, or omits an initial or final marking required for subsequent conversion or validation.
2. The accepting net is not a workflow net when the chosen soundness criteria require one, or a soundness check reports unsoundness, dead transitions, deadlocks, or improper completion.
3. Either allowed `Assess Risk`/`Prepare Evidence` order is lost, or the optional legal branch and verification are forced into an order that rules out a source-allowed interleaving.
4. Release Change can occur before both legal resolution (including the silent skip) and completion of the verification loop.
5. Optional behavior collapses: Legal Review becomes mandatory, impossible, duplicated, or its skip is made visible as an invented business activity.
6. The loop permits zero verification iterations, omits `Correct Defect` between repeated iterations, places correction after the final iteration, or reverses Configure Trial and Inspect Trial.
7. A candidate admits a negative witness or rejects a required positive witness; in particular, any completed trace fails exact equality with the accepting final marking.
8. Visible labels are duplicated, conflated, or changed so source activities cannot be mapped unambiguously for validation or stakeholder review.
9. Initial/final markings, silent routing semantics, or gateway behavior are not documented well enough to interpret the converted artifact.
10. A bounded trace comparison reports a mismatch, or a cutoff is mistaken for exhaustive evidence. A cutoff is an unresolved validation result, not a pass.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

Keep the supplied POWL model authoritative because it explicitly records the partial order, optional XOR, and loop structure that define the source semantics. Store the POWL definition and its versioned source alongside the accepted interpretation of the binary loop and the invariant/witness suite.

Treat the accepting Petri net as a generated behavioral derivative and BPMN as a generated stakeholder visualization. A process tree should be allowed only as a conditional derivative: if conversion fails, record `conversion failed` and stop that branch; if it succeeds, still require the same invariant, soundness, marking, witness, and round-trip checks before distributing it. A successful tree conversion alone is not evidence that a non-block-structured source was preserved.

Each derivative should carry its PM4Py version, conversion calls and options, conversion logs/exceptions, initial and final markings, activity-label mapping, workflow-net and soundness results, bounded comparison limits and cutoff flag, witness outcomes, and visualization annotations. Future edits should begin from POWL, regenerate the derivatives, rerun the audit, and review semantic differences before replacing artifacts. Do not use a round-tripped BPMN or tree as the new canonical source without explicitly reconciling the change into POWL and approving the changed invariants.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every proposed check passes, these claims remain unestablished:

- The bounded exploration cannot prove behavior for arbitrarily many loop iterations; the source loop admits an unbounded family of traces.
- Matching visible traces and footprints does not prove equivalence of branching behavior, true concurrency, or all partial-order executions. Different nets can share a bounded trace set.
- A BPMN rendering cannot establish what a stakeholder will infer from its gateways, loop annotation, or layout; visual ambiguity requires human review.
- Soundness and workflow-net status do not prove behavioral equivalence to the POWL source.
- Hiding tau makes the silent legal-skip route unobservable in visible traces; trace equality cannot establish that its internal routing is the intended XOR skip.
- Successful conversion and serialization do not establish that a particular PM4Py release implements the intended binary LOOP semantics; inspect the produced net and retain version-specific evidence.
