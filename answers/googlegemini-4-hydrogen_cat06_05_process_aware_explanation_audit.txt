## 2. Process-model consistency checks

An automated validation suite must gate both training pipelines and explanation generation before any SHAP values or sequence patterns reach users.

### 1. Prefix-time availability check
- **Mechanism:** Enforce a hard event-log truncation filter at `t0 = timestamp(first(Coverage Check Completed))` using event-time and ingestion/recording-time (`recorded_at <= t0`). For tabular/feature-store joins, require `feature_valid_from <= t0 < feature_valid_to` (or `snapshot_ts <= t0`, disallowing `±5 min` lookahead).
- **Target failures caught:** Rejects `total_case_duration_hours`, `final_decision`, `same_day_document_count_snapshot` (23:00), `prior_claim_count_before_t0` (`<= extraction_date`), `risk_score_current`, and all post-`t0` patterns.
- **Role & limitations:** Essential first line of defense, but cannot detect clock skew, missing timestamps, or mutable warehouse columns (`final_handler_team`) that lack history tables.

### 2. Complete-trace information audit
- **Mechanism:** Static and runtime lineage assertions tagging every feature with `aggregation_scope` (`prefix_only`, `point_in_time_snapshot`, `full_trace`, `mutable_current`). Any feature with `full_trace` or `mutable_current` scope—or SQL aggregates without `WHERE event_ts <= t0`—fails CI/CD.
- **Target failures caught:** Catches `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `last_resource_role`, and `employee_experience`.
- **Role & limitations:** Guarantees structural provenance in code/SQL, but requires metadata discipline and bitemporal logging from upstream databases.

### 3. Same-timestamp ambiguity and ordering check
- **Mechanism:** Detect consecutive events `(e_i, e_{i+1})` where `ts(e_i) == ts(e_{i+1})`. Require a deterministic monotonic `sequence_index` from the source system. If `sequence_index` is missing:
  - Flag the trace as `order_ambiguous`.
  - Resolve order against a normative partial-order / behavioral profile only for diagnostic auditing, and **suppress** any directly-follows pattern derived from tied timestamps during model training and explanation.
- **Target failures caught:** Directly blocks `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` in Case Z where missing sequence indices inverted tied timestamps.
- **Role & limitations:** Prevents spurious DFG edges caused by batch commits or coarse timestamp granularity, though it reduces usable sequence coverage until upstream ingestion emits sequence IDs.

### 4. Activity-order feasibility and semantic loop checks (Alignment, Footprint, Declarative)
- **Mechanism:** Validate observed traces and explanation patterns against the normative process model (Petri net / BPMN + declarative constraints):
  - **Footprint matrix check:** Check whether any edge `A -> B` violates `never-follows` (`#`) relations in the normative model (e.g., `Claim Closed -> *` is forbidden because `Claim Closed` is terminal).
  - **Alignment-based conformance:** Align prefixes and full traces against the normative model to quantify fitness and isolate log moves/model moves (detecting skipped or inverted milestones).
  - **Declarative (DECLARE / LTL) rules for semantic exceptions:** Encode context-dependent cardinality and precedence rules, specifically allowing `Settlement Decision Recorded` to repeat **only if** preceded by `Settlement Decision Recorded (Partial | Deny) -> Appeal`. Thus, `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` passes normative semantics (legitimate reconsideration), whereas `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` fails `End(Claim Closed)` / `Precedence(Damage Assessment Completed, Claim Closed)`.
- **Why DFG frequency is insufficient:** A Directly-Follows Graph (DFG) edge frequency reflects empirical log occurrence, not normative validity. Rare valid loops (e.g., `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded`) may have low frequency yet be fully compliant, while systematic ETL bugs (e.g., same-timestamp inversion of `Claim Closed` and `Damage Assessment Completed`) can generate high-frequency spurious DFG edges.

### 5. Explanation-time activity status check (`enabled` vs. `impossible` vs. `future-possible`)
- **Mechanism:** Given the normative state `S(t0)` reached by the prefix at `t0` (typically after `Claim Registered -> Coverage Check Completed`), classify every activity or pattern referenced in an explanation:
  - **Already occurred (`<= t0`):** Admissible in `t0` explanations (e.g., `Claim Registered`, `Coverage Check Completed`).
  - **Currently enabled at `t0`:** `Documents Requested`, `Damage Assessment Completed`, or `Fraud Investigation Opened` (parallel trigger). Admissible *only* as forward-looking process possibilities (e.g., expected next step), **never** as observed facts.
  - **Merely future-possible (not yet enabled at `t0`):** `Documents Received`, `Documents Validated`, `Settlement Decision Recorded`, `Appeal`, `Claim Closed`. Strictly inadmissible as observed drivers in a `t0` explanation (`Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded`, `final_decision = Partial`).
  - **Normatively impossible:** Transitions violating the normative model regardless of time (`Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed`). Must trigger an immediate explanation invalidation alert.
- **Role & limitations:** Bridges explainability (XAI) and process semantics so explanations cannot hallucinate future or impossible milestones as present causes.

## 3. Alternative feature interpretations

### 1. Defensible predictive signals
- **Features:** `initial_damage_complexity`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, `initial_reserve` (once immutability at `<= t0` is verified), `claimant_channel` (once immutability at registration is verified).
- **What may be claimed:** Statistical association with downstream breach probability given information known at `t0` (e.g., higher initial complexity or reserve correlates with heavier assessment/evidence workloads; higher elapsed time before `t0` leaves less remaining time inside the 10-day window).
- **What may NOT be claimed:** That the feature itself *causes* the breach (e.g., changing the recorded `initial_reserve` number will not speed up settlement), or that these features justify automated adverse decisions without domain review.

### 2. Process-state or congestion signals
- **Features:** `queue_length_at_t0` (tightened to `<= t0`); repaired `same_day_document_count_at_t0` (strictly `<= t0`).
- **What may be claimed:** Operational workload and queue state at the exact prediction boundary correlate with elevated breach risk and provide plausible capacity-monitoring context (e.g., backlog at `t0` signals constrained handler bandwidth).
- **What may NOT be claimed:** Exact causal effect size of adding/removing staff from single-row SHAP values (queue length is confounded by arrival bursts, seasonal complexity mix, and shift staffing).

### 3. Proxies requiring fairness / robustness review
- **Features:** `claimant_region` (and potentially `claimant_channel`).
- **What may be claimed:** Predictive correlation across geographic or intake segments reflecting bundled differences in repair-network density, broker vs. direct intake workflows, or regional claim mix.
- **What may NOT be claimed:** That "region is a delay driver" or that claimants in a region cause delays. Must not be surfaced as an operational reason code without decomposing into actionable operational factors (e.g., local repair-shop capacity) and auditing for socioeconomic, linguistic, or protected-group disparate impact.

### 4. Drift / calendar proxies
- **Features:** `calendar_week`.
- **What may be claimed:** Captures historical temporal correlation tied to specific past weeks (holidays, operating-policy regimes).
- **What may NOT be claimed:** Stable causal seasonality ("Week X is inherently worse") or future generalizability; high sensitivity to `calendar_week` indicates model fragility under policy changes and calendar drift.

### 5. Unusable until lineage is repaired (and permanently excluded post-`t0` variables)
- **Repairable (currently unusable):** `prior_claim_count_before_t0` (buggy `<= extraction_date`), `same_day_document_count_snapshot` (23:00 batch refresh), `final_handler_team` (must be replaced by `handler_team_at_t0`), `risk_score_current` (must be replaced by bitemporal `risk_score_at_t0`), `employee_experience` (must be joined as of `t0`).
- **Permanently excluded (unrepairable as `t0` predictors):** `total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`, and all post-`t0` sequence patterns.
- **What may be claimed:** Nothing for `t0` risk scoring or explanation until point-in-time lineage is fixed and the model is retrained.

## 5. Recommendations for explanation validation

### Phase 1 — Immediate Blocking Controls (P0)
1. **Prefix-only recomputation & quarantine of leaked predictions:** Immediately suspend production use of the current model and explanations where predictions depend on full-trace or mutable fields (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`, `final_handler_team`, and all post-`t0` patterns). Retrain solely on event prefixes truncated strictly at `first(Coverage Check Completed)` (`<= t0`).
2. **Process-conformance CI/CD gates:** Implement automated tests validating:
   - Prefix cutoffs (`event_ts <= t0`, `recorded_at <= t0`);
   - Normative conformance checks (alignment + footprint `never-follows` + declarative rules distinguishing valid post-`t0` `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` loops from impossible `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` inversions);
   - Same-timestamp sequence-index completeness (`sequence_index IS NOT NULL` whenever timestamps tie);
   - An explanation vocabulary guard preventing any `future-possible` or `impossible` activity from appearing as an observed `t0` explanation factor.

### Phase 2 — Feature Store & Data Lineage Reconstruction (P1)
3. **Bitemporal feature-store design:** Migrate from ad-hoc warehouse joins to a point-in-time feature store enforcing `valid_from` and `recorded_at` timestamps on every feature. Enforce strict point-in-time joins (`feature_ts <= t0`) with zero lookahead tolerance (tightening `queue_length_at_t0` to `<= t0` and replacing `same_day_document_count_snapshot` with streaming/event-time counts `<= t0`).
4. **Historical feature snapshots & SQL repair:** Fix `prior_claim_count_before_t0` (`claim_created_at < t0`), implement SCD Type 2 history tables for `handler_team_at_t0`, `employee_experience_at_t0`, and `risk_score_at_t0`, and store an audit flag confirming `initial_reserve` and `claimant_channel` are immutable after registration.
5. **Post-removal probability calibration:** Removing strong future leaks will substantially shift predicted score distributions and reduce apparent AUC. Re-calibrate the leakage-free model (e.g., isotonic regression or Platt scaling on out-of-time validation splits) and evaluate Brier score, Expected Calibration Error (ECE), and reliability diagrams across risk deciles.

### Phase 3 — Explanation Quality, Fairness & Drift Governance (P2)
6. **Explanation stability & causal boundary standards:** Test local SHAP/attribution stability across bootstrap resamples, correlated-feature groupings (e.g., `initial_reserve` vs. `initial_damage_complexity`), and small prefix perturbations. Enforce UI/report templates separating *predictive indicators* from *causal/actionable interventions*.
7. **Proxy & fairness analysis:** Audit `claimant_region` and `claimant_channel` for disparate impact, false-positive/false-negative parity, and calibration parity across regions and socioeconomic/language proxies. Where possible, replace coarse regional identifiers with direct operational capacity metrics (e.g., local repair-network backlog at `t0`).
8. **Drift & conformance monitoring:** Continuously monitor input distribution shifts (especially around `calendar_week`, holidays, and policy rollouts), feature-freshness SLA violations (missing timestamps or sequence indices), and process drift (shifts in DFG transition probabilities, new activity labels, or rising alignment-conformance deviations). Trigger automatic model/explanation review whenever calibration error or conformance violations exceed defined thresholds.
