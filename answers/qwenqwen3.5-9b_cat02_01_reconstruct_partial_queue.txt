# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

**Waiting Time ($W$):**
*   $W_{S1} = S1\_Start - Arrival$
*   $W_{S2} = S2\_Start - S1\_End$

**Service Time ($S$):**
*   $S_{S1} = S1\_End - S1\_Start$
*   $S_{S2} = S2\_End - S2\_Start$

**Sojourn Time ($T$):**
*   $T = S1\_End - Arrival$ (Total time in system to completion)

**WIP (Work In Progress):**
*   WIP at time $t$: Count of cases with $Arrival \le t$ AND $Departure > t$ (or $State \neq COMPLETE$).
*   For `OPEN` cases, $Departure = \infty$.

**Utilization ($U$):**
*   $U = \frac{Busy\_Time}{Total\_Time}$.
*   $Busy\_Time = \sum Service\_Time$.
*   Total Time = 6 hours (08:00 - 14:00).

**Throughput ($\lambda$):**
*   $\lambda = \frac{Completed\_Cases}{Total\_Time}$.

**Little's Law ($L = \lambda W$):**
*   Used to check consistency between Average WIP ($L$), Throughput ($\lambda$), and Average Sojourn Time ($W$).

**Estimates:**
*   **Lower/Upper Bounds:** Derived from rounding intervals ($\pm 2.5$ min) and resource constraints.
*   **Central Estimate:** Midpoint of the valid range.

# 5. Bottleneck and temporal interpretation

**Evidence:**
*   S1 resources (I1, I2) show consistent service times and short waiting times (mostly < 20 mins).
*   S2 resources (C1, C2) show increasing waiting times after 11:00 (P08: 55m, P14: 65m, P16: 85m).
*   C2 was under maintenance 10:55-11:10.
*   P15-P20 show significant S2 waiting times (65-85 mins).

**Hypothesis:**
*   S2 is the bottleneck.
*   The bottleneck is exacerbated by the C2 outage.
*   S1 can process cases faster than S2 can accept them (S1 service ~30m, S2 service ~40m).
*   The queue builds up at S2 after 11:00.

**Maintenance Interval:**
*   C2 outage (10:55-11:10) likely delayed P08 (S2_start 11:15 vs S1_end 10:20). If C2 was available, P08 could have started at 10:25.
*   The outage reduced S2 capacity by 50% (1 resource) for 15 mins, but the effect propagated due to S2's longer service times and S2 queue formation.

**Conclusion:**
*   S2 is the bottleneck.
*   S1 is not the bottleneck.
*   The C2 outage contributed to the queue buildup but was not the sole cause (S2 utilization was high even after 11:10).
