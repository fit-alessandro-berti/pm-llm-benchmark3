6. Residual vulnerabilities

Even after the redesign, several fragilities remain—some are inherent, one is **caused by the redesign itself**.

**Inherent structural fragilities**
- **Cyclical specialist absence:** The “every second Friday” absence is structural. I10 and I5 provide coverage, but if two consecutive Fridays coincide with high Complex-panel volume, the partner pipeline and evening shift may be insufficient. There is no permanent specialist pool.
- **Only two accession-correction specialists:** Even with I4 guardrails, the base pool is tiny. A single absence (not modeled in the scenarios but plausible) collapses correction regardless of cross-training.
- **M2 failure-rate dependence:** The smart-scheduling layer relies on M2 being usable but monitored. If M2 degrades suddenly without telemetry, Routine is pushed onto a failing machine before the failure-rate gate triggers.

**Vulnerability caused by the redesign**
- **Accelerated upstream flow with hidden downstream accumulation:** By smoothing intake (I7), automating registration (I8), and breaking loops (I6), the redesign reduces visible queues at registration and pre-analytical check. However, because assay (98%) and technical validation (101%) are already near saturation in peak, accelerating flow into them creates **silent WIP growth** in preparation and assay queues. The monitoring dashboard must therefore shift attention **away from upstream queues** (which look healthy) toward **assay and validation queues** (which may worsen without immediate visibility). If managers continue to optimize upstream metrics (registration time, intake queue), they will unintentionally overload the assay-technical-validation-medical-validation chain—exactly the “intervention overloads the next step” problem the redesign seeks to avoid.

- **Triage-to-correction staff competition:** I6 requires staff at pre-analytical triage. If P-Surge and P-Cascade activate together with P-Validation, staff may be pulled in three directions (triage, cross-trained correction, evening validation). The redesign assumes a flexible float pool; without it, activating all layers simultaneously collapses the middle of the process.

- **Partner dependency fragility:** I10 introduces an external dependency. If partner turnaround slips or labeling errors occur, Complex-panel results may be released with incorrect traceability, requiring local rework that the redesigned process is not staffed to absorb.
