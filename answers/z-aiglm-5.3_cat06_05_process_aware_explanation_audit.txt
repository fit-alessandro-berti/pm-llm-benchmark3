Where the index is missing, enumerate all orderings consistent with the normative constraints, recompute pattern features under each, and flag unstable patterns (contribution sign or magnitude flips, as it would for `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` vs the reverse). Define and document a canonical tie-break policy (e.g., normative precedence, `Claim Closed` last) and report the share of ties resolved by policy. *Limitation:* constraint-based tie-breaking embeds the normative model's assumptions; genuine concurrency cannot be resolved by policy alone.

**V4 — Complete-trace usage detection.** Beyond V1: (a) perturbation test — shuffle post-`t0` events; any feature that changes is a function of the future; (b) single-feature label separation (an AUC near 1, as expected for `total_case_duration_hours`, signals label leakage); (c) prefix-only retraining gap — an implausibly large performance drop when moving to lawful features indicates the original model was trading on the future.

**V5 — Enabledness classification at `t0`.** From the state immediately after the first Coverage Check Completed, classify every activity cited in any explanation:
- *Enabled now:* `Documents Requested`; `Damage Assessment Completed` (fast track); `Fraud Investigation Opened` (if the trigger condition is already satisfied at coverage check).
- *Future-possible, not enabled:* `Settlement Decision Recorded` (requires assessment), `Claim Closed` (requires final decision/withdrawal), a second decision (requires an appeal), `Documents Received/Validated` (require a request).
- *Impossible at `t0`:* `Appeal` (no prior decision exists), any event after `Claim Closed`, and the edge `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed`.
Rule: explanations may use *enabled-now* activities as observed features; *future-possible* activities may appear only as **model-predicted** quantities (e.g., predicted investigation propensity), never as observed inputs; *impossible* references invalidate the explanation and open a data-quality ticket.

**V6 — Repeated Settlement Decision legitimacy.** Declarative checks per trace: a second `Settlement Decision Recorded` requires an `Appeal` between the two decisions; an `Appeal` requires the preceding decision to be Partial or Deny; no `Claim Closed` before the final decision; `Claim Closed` is terminal. Additionally verify the label semantics — whether the 10-day target runs to the *first* or *final* decision and from which start event. If it runs to the final decision, appeal cycles are partly definitional in the label, which further undercuts any causal story about appeals; document the definition in the model card.

**V7 — Explanation admissibility gate.** Maintain a manifest of admissible `t0` features; automatically reject any global or local explanation citing a non-manifest feature at model release and at explanation generation time.

## 3. Alternative feature interpretations

**Four standards, applied separately.** *Predictive validity* means improving calibrated, out-of-time performance when features are computed strictly from the `t0` information state — importance measured on a leaked matrix is not predictive validity. *Causal interpretation* requires interventional or quasi-experimental evidence; SHAP and sequence contributions attribute correlation, and several top features here (SIU role, final team, appeals) are plausibly *consequences* of the case trajectory, i.e., reverse causation. *Operational actionability* requires a lever available at/after `t0` whose effect is validated — a feature can be predictive but not actionable (complexity), actionable-but-unproven (queue/staffing), or actionable but undesirable (region-based triage). *Fairness review* applies to features correlated with protected or protected-adjacent attributes: subgroup performance and calibration, disparate impact, feedback loops, and claimant-facing harm.

**Defensible predictive signal** — `initial_damage_complexity`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, `claimant_channel` (with caveat), `initial_reserve` (pending verification).
*May claim:* measured association on lawful data; plausible mechanisms where they exist (slack arithmetic for elapsed time; complexity  more process steps); use for risk-based prioritization.
*May not claim:* causal effects or effect sizes; that reclassifying complexity or the channel would change outcomes; claimant-facing attribution for channel without review.

**Process-state or congestion signal** — `queue_length_at_t0`; after repair also `team_at_t0` / `resource_role_at_t0`, workload/WIP at `t0`, fraud indicators available at coverage check, and *modeled* quantities such as predicted investigation propensity.
*May claim:* association with operational plausibility; status as candidate levers (staffing, routing) subject to validation; use as monitored leading indicators.
*May not claim:* causality from SHAP; may not ignore endogeneity (queues form around complex cases — reverse causation); may not use snapshots whose tolerance reaches forward in time past `t0`.

**Proxy requiring fairness/robustness review** — `claimant_region`, `prior_claim_count_before_t0` (after SQL repair), `claimant_channel` (partially).
*May claim:* predictive use if robust across subgroups and time and no direct mediator is available; subgroup performance reporting.
*May not claim:* that claimants' region or history *cause* delay; use in claimant-facing explanations; deployment of triage rules that create the very delay they predict (feedback loop). Prefer replacing the proxy with measured mediators (repair-network coverage, distance to assessor, language, channel) via mediation analysis.

**Drift/calendar proxy** — `calendar_week`.
*May claim:* in-sample period effects; recurring holiday-capacity effects once decomposed and validated year over year.
*May not claim:* invariant "bad weeks"; silent absorption of a one-off policy change (structural break) into weekly effects; generalization without post-policy re-evaluation.

**Unusable until lineage repaired** — `total_case_duration_hours` (unusable at this prediction point *by definition*; replace with elapsed time at `t0`), `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_decision`, `num_document_requests_total` (no lawful variance at `t0`), `full_trace_variant_id` (lawful value is constant at `t0`), `final_handler_team` ( `team_at_t0`), `last_resource_role` ( `role_at_t0`), `risk_score_current` ( versioned as-of-`t0` snapshots), `same_day_document_count_snapshot` ( event-time count  `t0`), `prior_claim_count` ( corrected SQL), all five sequence patterns as currently computed (observed post-`t0` events; the `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment` pattern is additionally footprint-impossible), employee experience ( as-of event date).
*May claim:* use in clearly labeled **post-hoc diagnostics of closed cases** (what correlated with breach) and as design input for later-checkpoint models.
*May not claim:* any role in a `t0` score or explanation until the repaired lineage passes checks V1–V7. Note that future events can be re-legitimized as *predictions* (e.g., P(fraud investigation), expected evidence cycles) — that is a model output, not an observation, and must be evaluated honestly.

## 5. Recommendations for explanation validation

**P0 — Stop the bleeding (days).**
1. Quarantine current model outputs from operational decisions; audit which decisions already consumed leaked scores.
2. Remove outcome-encoding and full-trace features (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_decision`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `num_document_requests_total`, `final_handler_team`, `last_resource_role`, all five sequence patterns as currently computed, unversioned `risk_score_current`, nightly document snapshot).
3. Fix deterministic defects now: prior-claim SQL (`< t0`, exclude current claim), backward-only snapshot tolerance, event ordering / sequence-index capture at ingestion, label semantics (clock start; first vs final decision).
4. Retrain prefix-only, recalibrate (Platt/isotonic on out-of-time data), accept the honest performance drop, recompute all open-case scores, and re-baseline the breach-alarm threshold.

**P1 — Feature-store design and lineage (weeks).**
5. Bitemporal feature store: every value carries event time and knowledge/ingestion time; an as-of join API is the *only* access path for training and serving; mutable scores are versioned, never overwritten; closure-time warehouse fields are excluded from early-warning features unless historicalized (`valid_from`/`valid_to`); employee experience joined as-of event date.
6. Lineage registry per feature: computation SQL, source system, valid-time semantics, tolerance, owner — plus CI gates: prefix replay equality tests, impossibility canaries (features that must be constant at `t0`), and single-feature label-separation tests.

**P1/P2 — Process-conformance testing (weeks, then continuous).**
7. Conformance suite in CI: footprint and alignment checks against the normative model; terminal-closure check; appeal-precondition and second-decision-requires-appeal checks; enabledness tagging of every activity cited in explanations (V5); same-timestamp tie-break policy with pattern-stability testing under admissible permutations. Treat DFG edges as descriptive only — a frequent edge is never a rule. Review systematic deviations with process owners (e.g., genuine reopenings) and update the normative model deliberately, with versioning.

**P2 — Explanation stability and admissibility.**
8. Admissibility gate: local and global explanations may cite only features in the `t0` manifest; automatically lint SHAP and sequence-explainer output at release time.
9. Stability testing: recompute explanations under bootstrap resampling, seed variation, and tie-break permutations; report top-k agreement; flag or abstain on unstable explanations. Mandate explicit "recompute" status whenever a released explanation contained inadmissible features.

**P2 — Proxy and fairness analysis.**
10. Audit `claimant_region`, `claimant_channel`, and (repaired) `prior_claim_count`: subgroup calibration and performance, disparate-impact testing, mediation analysis replacing raw proxies with operational mediators (repair-network coverage, distance to assessor, language, channel); a claimant-facing explanation policy prohibiting attribution of delay to claimant attributes; monitoring of triage rules for self-fulfilling feedback loops.

**P3 — Drift and schema monitoring (ongoing).**
11. Monitor schema drift (activity taxonomy changes, sequence-index availability), process/control-flow drift (variant distribution, conformance drift, new edges after closure), and feature drift (PSI). Decompose calendar effects into knowable components (holidays, planned capacity) plus an explicit policy-regime indicator; treat the policy change as a known structural break and validate the model separately on post-policy data. Schedule annual re-derivation of calendar features and out-of-time revalidation, with drift alerts routed to model and process owners for sign-off.
