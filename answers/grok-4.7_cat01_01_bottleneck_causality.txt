## 1. Executive causal assessment

Provisional verdict: mainly confounded. Confidence: 64 out of 100.

Management reads Orion's mean cycle time of 21.96 hours against Nova's 15.24 hours (crude gap about +6.7 hours) as a reason to move work. That figure is a confounded total association. The total association, a direct effect of the group label, and a queue-mediated effect are different estimands, and only the first is positive in this extract. Inside each complexity level the sign reverses. On the supplied stratum means, Orion is faster by 2.14 hours when complexity is low, 2.21 hours when medium, and 3.43 hours when high. A Kitagawa split that prices Orion's mix at Nova's within-stratum rates attributes about +9.4 hours to composition and about −2.7 hours to rate differences. Most of each within-stratum cycle-time gap sits in pre-review wait (about 1.6, 1.9, and 3.3 hours). Review service is only 0.1–0.4 hours shorter for Orion and scales with complexity.

The reversal is the strongest evidence against a harmful Orion effect. What blocks reading that reversal as a causal benefit is the design. Orion is night and high-load on 13 of 14 cases. Nova contributes two high-complexity cases and four night cases. No complexity-by-shift cell contains three cases from both groups. Residual difficulty can still drive routing after the three-level label. The file contains only completed cases. Compliance and rework gaps largely follow complexity, so they do not carry a separate harmful group effect.

Reassigning half of future Orion cases to Nova follows the confounded sign and can backfire. Nova would receive night, high-complexity, compliance-heavy work outside its observed support, while Orion's certified capacity stayed in place. Queue time dominates the stratified gap, so moving volume without night and compliance capacity would be expected to lengthen waits for transferred cases and for Nova's current workload.

Leave the 50% transfer unimplemented. The crude 6.7-hour Orion penalty is the wrong estimand for that decision: case mix accounts for roughly 9 hours in the opposite direction, and completed Orion cases are faster inside every complexity band, mostly through shorter pre-review wait. That within-band pattern is still not a license to declare Orion causally faster. Night shift and high load travel with the Orion label, Nova's support in the hard cells is one or two cases, and only finished cases are visible.

Repair the event log so queue time, compliance time, and open cases are separable. Add bounded night-queue or compliance capacity while holding the resource-group label fixed, which tests the wait mechanism directly. Randomize only a capped block of low- and medium-complexity cases on shifts both groups already cover, and track waits of cases that were not rerouted. Expand routing toward Nova only if that local contrast favors Nova by enough to offset spillover, or if the capacity trial shows staffing removes the gap. Otherwise keep the current rule and manage complexity mix and night coverage as the levers the present evidence actually supports.

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. Residual difficulty after the three-level complexity label can still steer routing. The single most valuable addition is the router's pre-assignment feature vector or score, retained even when a pilot overrides the recommendation.

2. Shift may be either a cause of routing or a consequence of which group is staffed. The single most valuable addition is an arrival timestamp, an assignment timestamp, and the roster that was already on duty before that case arrived.

3. `wait_before_review_h` mixes queueing with compliance waiting, and review service does not increase when Request Info occurs. The single most valuable addition is event start and end timestamps for Classify, Compliance Check, each Review, and Request Info.

4. The extract keeps only completed cases, so a slow hidden tail could manufacture Orion's within-band advantage. The single most valuable addition is a row for every arrival, with a completion indicator and the age of still-open cases.

5. A 50% volume move would change the queues of cases that are not reassigned. The single most valuable addition is certified hours rostered by group and shift, together with queue length at Classify, so capacity and congestion can be separated from the group label.
