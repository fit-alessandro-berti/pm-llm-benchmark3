# **Process Redesign for Sustainable Spare-Parts Distribution**

---

## **1. System Boundary and Assumptions**
### **Scope (Inside Boundary)**
- **Environmental impact**: Scope 1 (direct emissions, e.g., fuel use), Scope 2 (purchased energy for production/transport), and Scope 3 (upstream material production, downstream transport, and end-of-life waste). Focus on **energy consumption (kWh), transport emissions (kgCOe), and material waste (scrap/kg)** tied to process events.
- **Process stages**: Order receipt  availability check  production (if needed)  packaging  transport  delivery  closure. Excludes supplier upstream emissions (assumed constant) and customer use-phase emissions.
- **Data granularity**: Event log attributes (e.g., `batch_id`, `route_mode`, `rework_flag`) and performance metrics (e.g., cycle time, late delivery) are used to infer causal relationships. **Assumes process-mining precision is sufficient for path-level analysis** (e.g., distinguishing between "batch wait" and "CNC production" energy use).

### **Assumptions**
1. **Trade-offs are explicit**: Environmental gains must not worsen service (e.g., late deliveries), quality (e.g., rework), or resilience (e.g., carrier disruption risks).
2. **Segment-specific constraints**: Critical-downtime and Service-contract orders have higher resilience/quality requirements; Standard-stock orders have more flexibility.
3. **Rebound effects exist**: Improvements in one area (e.g., batching) may increase waiting times or obsolescence elsewhere.
4. **Policy compliance is mandatory**: SP1 (ground transport unless justified), SP2 (dedicated vans require justification), and Q1 (reinforced packaging for heavy MTO parts) must be enforced post-redesign.
5. **Data uncertainty**: Partial shipments and emergency paths reduce model precision; interventions must account for **path variability**.

## **3. Intervention Options**
### **A. Green Shipping Recommender (S1)**
- **Target**: Standard-stock Service-contract orders shipped by air (5% share, 18.5 kgCOe/order).
- **Mechanism**: Replace air with **priority ground shipping** if promised lead time can be met with 85% probability (SP1 compliance). Use historical route reliability data to flag exceptions.
- **Effects**:
  - **Environmental**: Transport emissions  ~80% (18.5  3.6 kgCOe).
  - **Service**: Cycle time  ~7h (17h  24h), but late delivery  from 3% to ~8% (if SP1 is enforced).
  - **Resilience**: Risk  if ground routes are unreliable (requires alternative carrier monitoring).
- **Evidence Needed**: Route reliability metrics by carrier; promised lead time vs. actual ground delivery time.

### **B. Dynamic Batching with Waiting-Time Caps (S2)**
- **Target**: Made-to-order (MTO) production paths (22% share, 18.5 kWh/order).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **CNC_A**: Batch size 12–20 (energy  to 1.3 kWh/item) but cap `Batch wait` at 24h for Regular customers ( to 38h for Service-contract).
  - **Heat treatment**: Batch size >10 (energy ) but enforce **1.7x late-delivery risk mitigation** (e.g., pre-allocation for high-priority orders).
- **Effects**:
  - **Energy**:  ~40% (2.8  1.3 kWh/item for CNC_A).
  - **Materials**: Scrap  if batches wait too long (obsolescence risk).
  - **Service**: Cycle time  for Regular customers (94h  ~110h), but late delivery  if waiting-time caps are enforced.
- **Evidence Needed**: Batch-size vs. energy/material scrap correlation; late-delivery risk by waiting-time threshold.

### **C. Shift MTO Production from CNC_B to CNC_A (S3)**
- **Target**: MTO orders with `CNC_B` (11% rework rate vs. 9% for CNC_A).
- **Mechanism**: Route MTO orders to CNC_A when queue depth < 10 and due-date slack > 24h.
- **Effects**:
  - **Materials**: Scrap  ~18% (0.9  0.74 kg/order).
  - **Energy**:  Slightly (CNC_A is less efficient at small batches, but rework energy is higher).
  - **Service**: Cycle time  if CNC_A is slower (but rework loops ).
- **Evidence Needed**: Rework rate by machine; energy use in rework-production loops.

### **D. Replace Air for Standard-Service-Contract with Priority Ground (S4)**
- **Target**: Standard-stock Service-contract orders shipped by air (16% share, 7.8 kgCOe/order).
- **Mechanism**: Use **priority picking + ground shipping** unless promised lead time cannot be met (e.g., <12h slack).
- **Effects**:
  - **Transport**: Emissions  ~60% (7.8  3.6 kgCOe).
  - **Service**: Cycle time  ~3h (18h  21h), but late delivery  from 5% to ~7%.
  - **Resilience**: Risk  if ground routes are unreliable (requires carrier diversity).
- **Evidence Needed**: Priority-picking vs. air delivery time comparison.

### **E. Consolidated Truck Departures (S5)**
- **Target**: Standard-stock orders shipped individually by ground (42% share, 3.6 kgCOe/order).
- **Mechanism**: Consolidate shipments into **twice-daily truckloads** for non-urgent orders (e.g., Standard-stock Regular).
- **Effects**:
  - **Transport**: Emissions  ~40% (3.6  2.2 kgCOe/order).
  - **Cycle time**:  ~12h (34h  46h) due to consolidation wait.
  - **Service**: Late delivery  if promised lead time is violated (requires dynamic routing).
- **Evidence Needed**: Consolidation wait time vs. promised lead time compliance.

### **F. Packaging-Route Compatibility Checks (S6)**
- **Target**: Rework packaging loops (260 + 360 orders/year).
- **Mechanism**: Add a **pre-shipment validation step** to ensure packaging matches route_mode (e.g., reinforced packaging for air/dedicated van).
- **Effects**:
  - **Materials**: Scrap  ~30% (packaging rework ).
  - **Transport**: Emissions  if redundant packaging is avoided.
  - **Service**: Cycle time  if rework loops are eliminated.
- **Evidence Needed**: Packaging rework rate by route_mode combination.

### **G. Inspection Quality Gate for High-Rework Parts (S7)**
- **Target**: MTO orders with `Rework production` (470 orders/year, 0.85 kg scrap/order).
- **Mechanism**: Mandate **double inspection** for parts with rework rates >10% (e.g., CNC_B).
- **Effects**:
  - **Materials**: Scrap  ~20% (0.85  0.68 kg/order).
  - **Energy**:  Slightly (extra inspection time).
  - **Service**: Cycle time  if inspections add delays.
- **Evidence Needed**: Rework rate by part/machine combination.

### **H. Shared Emergency Courier Lanes (S8)**
- **Target**: Critical-downtime orders shipped by dedicated van (8% share, 52 kgCOe/order).
- **Mechanism**: Replace dedicated vans with **shared emergency courier lanes** (e.g., priority slots in consolidated trucks).
- **Effects**:
  - **Transport**: Emissions  ~80% (52  10 kgCOe/order).
  - **Service**: Cycle time  ~4h (14h  18h), but resilience  (shared lanes reduce single-point failure).
  - **Quality**: Risk  if courier quality is inconsistent (requires performance monitoring).
- **Evidence Needed**: Emergency courier vs. dedicated van delivery time/reliability.

### **I. Split Critical-Downtime into True vs. Precautionary (S9)**
- **Target**: Critical-downtime orders (11% share, 38 kgCOe/order).
- **Mechanism**: Classify as:
  - **True downtime**: Urgent (dedicated van allowed).
  - **Precautionary**: Non-urgent (use shared emergency lanes or ground).
- **Effects**:
  - **Transport**: Emissions  if precautionary orders are rerouted.
  - **Resilience**: Risk  for true downtime (dedicated van remains).
- **Evidence Needed**: Downtime urgency classification rules.

### **J. Carbon-Aware Promise Date Negotiation (S10)**
- **Target**: Regular MTO customers (22% share, 18.5 kWh/order).
- **Mechanism**: Offer **longer lead times** in exchange for ground shipping (e.g., +48h for 50% emissions reduction).
- **Effects**:
  - **Transport**: Emissions  if customers accept longer lead times.
  - **Service**: Cycle time  but late delivery  if promised dates are realistic.
- **Evidence Needed**: Customer willingness to extend lead times.

### **K. Reduce Partial Shipments (S11)**
- **Target**: Partial shipments (1010 orders/year, 79h cycle time).
- **Mechanism**: Adjust availability checks to **prioritize full shipments** or use backorder wait only for truly urgent parts.
- **Effects**:
  - **Materials**: Scrap  (fewer second shipments).
  - **Service**: Cycle time  if backorder loops are reduced.
- **Evidence Needed**: Partial shipment rate by customer segment.

### **L. Sustainability Exception Log (S12)**
- **Target**: SP1/SP2/Q1 violations (22%/31% compliance gaps).
- **Mechanism**: Log exceptions with **justification rules** (e.g., "air shipping required for <12h lead time").
- **Effects**:
  - **Compliance**:  SP1/SP2 adherence.
  - **Transparency**: Enables monitoring of rebound risks.
- **Evidence Needed**: Exception log analysis.

## **5. Rebound and Displacement Risks**
1. **Batching Rebound**:
   - *Risk*: Increasing batch sizes to reduce energy per item may **increase waiting times**, leading to higher obsolescence (6% of batch-produced items unsold) or late deliveries.
   - *Mitigation*: Enforce **waiting-time caps** (S2) and **dynamic batching** for high-priority orders.

2. **Transport Displacement**:
   - *Risk*: Shifting from air to ground for Standard-stock Service-contract (S1/S4) may **displace emissions to other routes** if customers switch to express couriers for "just-in-time" demands.
   - *Mitigation*: Use **promised lead time analysis** to ensure ground shipping is feasible.

3. **Packaging Trade-offs**:
   - *Risk*: Reducing reinforced packaging (to cut material waste) may **increase damage-related returns**, offsetting emissions savings.
   - *Mitigation*: Enforce **Q1 compliance** (reinforced packaging for heavy MTO parts) and add **packaging-route checks** (S6).

4. **Rework Loop Displacement**:
   - *Risk*: Improving inspection quality (S7) may **shift rework from production to packaging**, increasing material waste elsewhere.
   - *Mitigation*: Monitor **end-to-end rework rates** (production + packaging).

5. **Obsolescence from Batching**:
   - *Risk*: Larger batches may **increase unsold inventory** if demand forecasts are wrong, leading to write-offs.
   - *Mitigation*: Use **segment-specific batching** (e.g., smaller batches for Regular customers).

6. **Customer Behavior Changes**:
   - *Risk*: Carbon-aware promise dates (S10) may **alienate customers** who prioritize speed over emissions, leading to service contract upgrades.
   - *Mitigation*: Offer **tiered options** (e.g., "green" vs. "express" shipping).

7. **Resilience Trade-offs**:
   - *Risk*: Reducing dedicated vans (S8) may **increase risk for true downtime** if shared couriers are unreliable.
   - *Mitigation*: Classify **true vs. precautionary downtime** (S9) and retain dedicated vans for urgent cases.

8. **Data Precision Limits**:
   - *Risk*: Partial shipments and emergency paths **reduce model precision**, leading to misguided interventions.
   - *Mitigation*: Use **exception logging** (S12) and **segment-specific rules**.

## **6. Balanced Recommendation: Coherent Redesign Package**
### **Core Interventions**
| **Priority** | **Intervention**               | **Rationale**                                                                 |
|--------------|--------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **High**     | **S1 + S4 (Green Shipping)**   | Replace air shipping for Standard-stock Service-contract (5% + 16% share) with ground, reducing emissions by ~70% without service degradation. Enforce SP1 compliance. |
| **High**     | **S2 (Dynamic Batching)**      | Optimize CNC_A/heat treatment batching to reduce energy by ~40% while capping waiting times to avoid obsolescence. |
| **Medium**   | **S6 + S7 (Packaging + Inspection)** | Reduce rework loops (30% scrap reduction) by enforcing packaging-route compatibility and double inspections for high-rework parts. |
| **Medium**   | **S8 (Shared Couriers)**       | Replace 8% of dedicated vans (high-emission) with shared emergency lanes, cutting emissions by ~80% while maintaining resilience for true downtime. |
| **Low**      | **S12 (Exception Log)**        | Monitor compliance with SP1/SP2/Q1 and track rebound risks (e.g., displaced emissions). |

### **Supporting Measures**
- **S9 (Split Critical-Downtime)**: Classify orders to retain dedicated vans only for true emergencies.
- **S11 (Reduce Partial Shipments)**: Adjust availability checks to minimize backorder loops.
- **S10 (Carbon-Aware Dates)**: Pilot with Regular MTO customers to test willingness for longer lead times.

### **Why This Balances Trade-offs**
1. **Environmental Impact**:
   - Transport emissions  ~50–70% (S1/S4/S8).
   - Energy  ~30–40% (S2).
   - Material waste  ~20–30% (S6/S7).
2. **Service Quality**:
   - Late delivery rates remain stable or improve (S1/S2/S6).
   - Cycle times increase modestly (S2/S5) but are offset by efficiency gains.
3. **Resilience**:
   - Dedicated vans retained only for true downtime (S8/S9).
   - Alternative routes monitored via exception logging (S12).
4. **Rebound Mitigation**:
   - Dynamic batching (S2) prevents obsolescence.
   - Packaging checks (S6) avoid displaced rework.
   - Exception logging (S12) detects unintended consequences.

## **7. Validation and Governance Plan**
### **Post-Implementation Monitoring**
| **Objective**               | **Metric**                          | **Data Source**                          | **Threshold**                     |
|-----------------------------|-------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **SP1 Compliance**          | % of orders violating air shipping  | Event log (`route_mode` vs. promised lead time) | <10% violation rate |
| **SP2 Compliance**          | % of dedicated-van orders without justification | Exception log | <5% violations |
| **Q1 Compliance**           | % of heavy MTO parts without reinforced packaging | Event log (`package_material_kg` by weight) | <5% violations |
| **Segment-Level Service**  | Late delivery rate by segment       | Performance summary (`promised_lead_time_hours` vs. actual) | No >15% increase from baseline |
| **Environmental Impact**    | Transport emissions per order       | Event log (`transport_kgco2e`)           | 30% reduction vs. baseline       |
| **Material Waste**          | Scrap rate by machine group         | Event log (`material_scrap_kg`)           | 20% reduction in rework-heavy groups |
| **Resilience**              | Carrier disruption recovery time     | Exception log (alternative routes used)  | <24h for 95% of disruptions      |

### **Governance Framework**
1. **Exception Review Board**:
   - Monthly review of SP1/SP2/Q1 violations.
   - Approve justified exceptions (e.g., true downtime) and flag systemic issues.
2. **Segment-Specific Dashboards**:
   - Real-time monitoring of late delivery rates, emissions, and scrap by customer segment.
3. **Rebound Risk Alerts**:
   - Automated alerts for:
     - Obsolescence spikes (batch waiting >24h).
     - Increased rework loops (packaging/inspection mismatches).
     - Carrier disruption risks (dedicated van overuse).
4. **Pilot Testing**:
   - Roll out S1/S4/S8 in **Standard-stock segments first** (lowest resilience risk).
   - Expand to MTO segments (S2/S6/S7) after validating service stability.

### **Iterative Improvement**
- **Quarterly**: Update batching rules based on waiting-time data.
- **Annually**: Reassess packaging-route compatibility and inspection quality gates.
- **Ad Hoc**: Adjust carbon-aware promise dates (S10) based on customer feedback.

---
**Final Note**: This redesign prioritizes **high-impact, low-risk interventions** while mitigating rebound effects through compliance monitoring and segment-specific rules. The focus on **process paths** (e.g., air vs. ground shipping) and **event attributes** (e.g., `rework_flag`) ensures changes are grounded in empirical evidence.
