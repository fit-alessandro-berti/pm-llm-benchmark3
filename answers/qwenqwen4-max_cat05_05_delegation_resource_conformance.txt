## 2. Rule Interpretation

Let `H(r, t)` = the human principal reliably attributable to resource `r` at time `t` (alias-normalized; `` if unattributed). Let `Role(h, t)` be evaluated from **role intervals**, never from the current master.

**RR1 — Drafter/approver separation.**
For case *c*: `H(drafter)    H(approver)    H(drafter)  H(approver)`.
If either side is ``, the result is **UNPROVEN** (treated as non-conformant for assurance, but classified as an identity problem, not misconduct).

**RR2 — Implementer/verifier separation.** Identical structure over `Implement_Change` / `Verify_Implementation`. Passing RR2 is *necessary but not sufficient* for the control objective: approver = verifier (CHG-509) and an unqualified verifier (CHG-507) satisfy the letter while defeating independent challenge.

**RR3 — High-risk approval authority.** For `risk = HIGH  emergency = false`:
`Approve(c)` is conformant iff `QM  Role(H(approver), t)` **or** a delegation `D` exists with all of:
1. `t  [D.start, D.end]` (UTC, inclusive);
2. `D.delegate = H(approver)`;
3. `case.scope  D.scope` **and** the activity type is within `D`'s granted activity class;
4. the absence/unavailability condition is **affirmatively evidenced at `t`** (silence  absence);
5. no prohibited subdelegation in the chain (a chain containing a forbidden hop is void *ab initio*, not merely voidable);
6. the instrument is verifiable as existing **before** first use.
Failure of (1),(2),(3),(5),(6)  **UNAUTHORIZED**. Failure of (4) only, with no counter-evidence  **INDETERMINATE**.

**RR4 — Emergency substitution.** For `risk = HIGH  emergency = true`:
approval by `IC  Role(h,t)` or a valid IC delegate (same six tests), **then**  `Retrospective_QM_Review` by QM/valid QM delegate with `t_review  BD2(t_approval)`. The exception is *justified* only if the emergency flag **and** a credible safety rationale are evidenced — a boolean column is a claim, not evidence. An invalid approval is not cured by a timely review; a valid approval is not cured of lateness by a competent reviewer.

**Business-day counting (RR4).** Site calendar = Mon–Fri, no holidays in the window. The trigger day (day of the IC approval) is **excluded**; the deadline is **23:59 UTC of the second following business day**. A stricter clock-time variant (same time of day, +2 business days) is also reported.
- CHG-505: approve Tue 08-11 13:00  deadline **Thu 08-13 23:59** (strict variant 08-13 13:00). Actual Fri 08-14 16:00 = **3 business days / 75 elapsed hours  LATE under every reasonable convention.**
- CHG-510: approve Tue 08-11 14:20  deadline Thu 08-13 23:59. Actual Wed 08-12 10:00 = **1 business day  TIMELY.**

**Principal acting while on leave.** Leave is an HR/availability fact, **not a revocation of standing authority**. Asha's 08-13 09:22 approval is therefore *technically authorized*. Three consequences: (a) the absence-condition of any conditional delegation is **suspended during demonstrated presence windows**; (b) the leave record's reliability as an absence proxy for the remainder of that day is degraded (rebuttable, not automatically void); (c) if credential sharing cannot be excluded, the action's attribution — not its authority — is the open question.

**Can a later database entry prove an earlier delegation?** **No — and it also does not disprove one.** Authority provenance is the *earliest verifiable instrument*, not the row-creation timestamp. D-QM-01's signed PDF (08-09 18:00) predates first use (08-11 09:00); the 08-12 09:15 QMS row is consistent with a migration backfill and is a **registration artifact**. Acceptance requires signature validation / trusted timestamp / version history; without those, the delegation is *plausible but not proven*, and confidence drops from High to Medium. Conversely, D-QM-02's pre-interval DB record proves **existence**, not **condition satisfaction**. Nothing created after an event can retro-authorize it (decisive for CHG-507).

**RR5 — Identity evidence.** Service accounts are never humans. Conformance requires a session chain terminating in an individually authenticated human (token/biometric/re-auth), not merely workstation assignment or room badge presence. Attribution strength is graded **strong / weak / none** and propagated into every downstream finding.

**RR6 — Suspicion versus proof.** Pair concentration, reciprocity, shared workstations and handover density are **indicators**. Escalation to a collusion finding requires corroboration: communications, benefit, intent, falsified records, or coordinated timing that survives base-rate conditioning. Repeated collaboration is explicitly **not** treated as evidence of innocence either.

## 4. Collusion and Alternative-Explanation Analysis

**Base-rate discipline first.** Clara is described as a frequent drafter and shares a production cell with Ben; Asha was on leave for the whole 08-1008-14 week, leaving Ben as the *only* valid QM delegate for production equipment. Under those conditions a high ClaraBen share is **structurally expected**. The 18/23 statistic is a **marginal** rate; what matters is the **conditional** rate: P(approver = Ben | drafter = Clara) versus P(approver = Ben | drafter  Clara), stratified by shift, cell, risk and Asha's availability. Without that stratification, 78% is not evidence of coordination.

def pandas_four_eyes(frame, res_col, a1, a2):
    """Ground truth: PASS / FAIL / UNPROVEN (service account unattributed)."""
    out = {}
    for cid, g in frame.groupby("case:concept:name"):
        r1 = set(g.loc[g["concept:name"] == a1, res_col])
        r2 = set(g.loc[g["concept:name"] == a2, res_col])
        if not r1 or not r2:
            out[cid] = "MISSING_ACTIVITY"
        elif SERVICE in (r1 | r2):
            out[cid] = "UNPROVEN_SERVICE_ACCOUNT"
        elif r1 & r2:
            out[cid] = "FAIL_SAME_HUMAN"
        else:
            out[cid] = "PASS"
    return out

def pm4py_four_eyes(frame, res_col, a1, a2):
    """pm4py filter; semantics vary by version, so we report the returned set
       and its complement and reconcile against the pandas ground truth."""
    if not HAVE_PM4PY:
        return None, None
    f = frame.copy()
    f["org:resource"] = f[res_col]
    f = pm4py.format_dataframe(f, case_id="case:concept:name",
                               activity_key="concept:name",
                               timestamp_key="time:timestamp")
    log = pm4py.convert_to_event_log(f)
    try:
        kept = pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(log, a1, a2)
    except Exception as e:
        print(f"[warn] filter_four_eyes_principle({a1},{a2}) failed: {e}")
        return None, None
    if isinstance(kept, pd.DataFrame):
        ids = set(kept["case:concept:name"].unique())
    else:
        ids = {t.attributes["concept:name"] for t in kept}
    allids = set(frame["case:concept:name"].unique())
    return ids, allids - ids

        return "UNPROVEN_SERVICE_ACCOUNT", []

        return "AUTHORIZED_STANDING_IC_RR4", []
    cands = delegation_candidates(h, "Approve_Change", scope, t, "IC" if emerg else "QM")
    if not cands and emerg:                       # QM delegation may also cover emergencies
        cands = delegation_candidates(h, "Approve_Change", scope, t, "QM")
    if not cands:
        return "UNAUTHORIZED_NO_ROLE_NO_DELEGATION", []
    best = "UNAUTHORIZED_DELEGATION_INVALID"
    for did, c in cands:
        hard_fail = (c["scope"] == "MISMATCH" or not c["subdelegation_ok"]
                     or not c["instrument_valid"] or not c["instrument_before_use"])
        soft = (c["absence"].startswith("UNPROVEN") or c["scope"] == "AMBIGUOUS"
                or c["incident_link"].startswith("UNPROVEN"))
        if hard_fail:
            continue
        best = "INDETERMINATE_CONDITION_UNPROVEN" if soft else "VALID_DELEGATION"
        if best == "VALID_DELEGATION":
            break
    return best, cands

## 6. Control Improvements

**Delegation creation.** Issue delegations only through the QMS workflow with a cryptographically signed, trusted-timestamped instrument; prohibit email-only delegation outright. Encode scope as structured fields (activity class × change scope × incident ID × risk band), not free text — this alone removes the "does production-equipment cover a cooling retrospective review?" ambiguity. Enforce `no_subdelegation` as a hard system constraint so a D-IC-04 chain cannot be created, let alone relied upon.

**Effective-time recording.** Store `effective_from/to` (UTC) separately from `record_created_at` and `instrument_signed_at`; make the earlier of the verifiable instrument times the authority provenance. Migration backfills must write a preserved original-timestamp field plus a migration audit entry, so a 08-12 row for a 08-10 delegation is self-explaining rather than suspicious.

**Principal availability.** Replace "on leave" as a proxy for absence with a computed availability state (leave record  active session/VPN/badge presence). Condition-checked delegations should auto-suspend during evidenced principal presence and require re-attestation afterwards. Define explicitly whether non-working days constitute formal unavailability; if they do, say so in the delegation text and the calendar. Require delegate availability/fitness confirmation too — a delegate on certified sick leave should not be an active approval principal.

**Alias management.** Maintain a single canonical person ID with an alias table; resolve aliases at ingestion, before any SoD rule runs. Block same-human approval at the workflow layer using the canonical ID, and periodically census dual-account users. Emit an alert whenever a four-eyes pass depends on unresolved aliases.

**Individual authentication.** Eliminate shared-terminal service-account actions for human judgement steps (verification, approval, retrospective review). Require step-up authentication (FIDO/biometric) bound to the e-signature at the moment of the action; a room badge plus a workstation assignment must never be sufficient. If `svc_qms` legitimately performs automated checks, model it as a distinct automated activity that cannot substitute for `Verify_Implementation`.

**Emergency review.** Make RR4 a system-enforced timer: on IC approval, create a scheduled QM-review task with a computed business-day deadline, escalate at 50%/100% of the window, and block `Close_Change` until a conformant review exists. Require a mandatory incident ID and safety-rationale field at emergency declaration; validate the emergency flag against the incident record.

**Pair rotation.** Introduce a risk-based rotation constraint for HIGH-risk drafter/approver and implementer/verifier pairs, with a qualified-deputy pool so that a single absence (Asha's leave) does not force 100% of approvals through one delegate. Add a rule prohibiting approver = verifier and, for HIGH risk, drafter = implementer, and define who may close a change (currently the drafter closes CHG-501 unchallenged).

**Network monitoring.** Run handover/working-together mining monthly with **conditional** rates stratified by cell, shift, risk and approver availability; alert on statistically anomalous concentration or reciprocity, not on raw counts. Treat every alert as a triage item requiring corroboration.

**Evidential retention.** Retain per-action authentication artefacts, session-to-human mappings, badge/CCTV for shared-terminal areas (with a defined retention period covering the change lifecycle), delegation instruments with signature-validation results, and immutable event-time role snapshots so future audits never depend on the current user master.

## 7. Executive Conclusion

Of ten cases, only **two (CHG-501, CHG-504)** are cleanly conformant on event-time authority, and both carry caveats (instrument authenticity; an availability record contradicted by a VPN session). **Four (CHG-502, CHG-507, CHG-508, CHG-510)** are genuine, evidence-backed authority/SoD failures — two self-approvals (one hidden by aliasing), one HIGH-risk approval by a person with no QM role or delegation, and one emergency approval under a void subdelegation where a compliant path was demonstrably available 70 minutes earlier. **One (CHG-505)** is a genuine RR4 timeliness failure (3 business days / 75 hours against a 2-business-day limit) compounded by a scope gap and a reviewer on certified sick leave. **Three (CHG-503, CHG-506, CHG-509)** cannot be resolved on the current record: they are limited by identity attribution or by an undefined absence condition, not by ambiguity in the rules.

Systemically, the dominant failure mode is **delegation governance** (scope text, subdelegation, absence semantics, registration-versus-creation timestamps), followed closely by **identity attribution quality** (service accounts on shared terminals, dual aliases, accounts active during certified absence). Organizational-pattern risk is real but **structural rather than proven misconduct**: with no rotation policy and the principal QM on leave for the entire week, a 78% ClaraBen share is close to what the resource pool predicts. **No collusion finding is supported** by the present evidence, and repeated collaboration is not treated as exculpatory either. Residual uncertainty is concentrated in three places: the authenticity of the D-QM-01 PDF, the meaning of "absent" on a weekend, and who actually operated `u_bk` and `svc_qms`.
