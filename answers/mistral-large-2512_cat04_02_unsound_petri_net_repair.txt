# 1. Executive Verdict

The given Petri net is a **workflow net**: it has a unique source place (`source`), a unique sink place (`sink`), and every node lies on a path from source to sink.

However, **it is not sound**. The principal defect is **incorrect synchronization of the waiver path**: the `join_waived` transition only consumes a token from `p_waived` but does not wait for the analysis to complete, violating the business rule that authorization requires *both* analysis and review (or waiver) to be complete. This creates a **race condition** where authorization can occur prematurely if the waiver completes before analysis.

**Business consequence**: Cases may be authorized without completed analysis, risking compliance violations and incorrect decisions. The net also leaves leftover tokens in `p_analysis_done` when the waiver path is taken, violating proper completion.

# 3. Marking-Based Counterexample

**Firing sequence exposing the defect (waiver path)**:
1. `t_register`
2. `t_split`
3. `t_waive_review` (waiver completes before analysis)
4. `t_join_waived` (premature synchronization)
5. `t_authorize` (case authorized without analysis)
6. `t_analyze` (analysis completes *after* authorization)

**Marking table**:

| Step | Fired transition   | Resulting nonzero marking                          | Enabled transitions                     | Observation                                                                 |
|------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 0    | -                  | `{source: 1}`                                      | `t_register`                            | Initial marking                                                            |
| 1    | `t_register`       | `{p_ready: 1}`                                     | `t_split`                               | Case registered                                                             |
| 2    | `t_split`          | `{p_analysis: 1, p_review: 1}`                     | `t_analyze`, `t_standard_review`, `t_waive_review` | Parallel branches started                                                  |
| 3    | `t_waive_review`   | `{p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1}`                     | `t_analyze`, `t_join_waived`            | Waiver recorded; analysis still pending                                     |
| 4    | `t_join_waived`    | `{p_analysis: 1, p_decision: 1}`                   | `t_analyze`, `t_authorize`              | **Defect**: `p_analysis` token ignored; `t_authorize` enabled prematurely   |
| 5    | `t_authorize`      | `{p_analysis: 1, sink: 1}`                         | `t_analyze`                             | Case authorized; `p_analysis` token leftover                                |
| 6    | `t_analyze`        | `{p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1}`                    | None                                    | Analysis completes; token stuck in `p_analysis_done`                        |

**Key observations**:
- `t_authorize` fires **before** `t_analyze`, violating the business rule.
- The final marking `{p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1}`  `fm` (`{sink: 1}`), leaving a leftover token.
- The leftover token cannot be consumed because no transition reads `p_analysis_done` after `t_authorize`.

# 4. Minimal-Repair Alternatives

**Edit-cost model**: Count of added/removed arcs, places, or transitions (1 unit per arc/place/transition).

| Repair                          | Exact edits                                                                 | Edit cost | Preserved intent                                                                 | Changed behavior                                                                 | New risk                                                                 |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Repair 1: Waiver sync fix**   | Add arc `p_analysis_done  join_waived`                                     | 1         | Analysis remains mandatory on waiver path; waiver and standard review distinct  | `join_waived` now waits for analysis; no premature authorization                 | Silent `join_waived` may obscure waiver/standard review distinction      |
| **Repair 2: Dedicated waiver join** | Remove `join_waived`; add new transition `join_waiver` with arcs from `p_analysis_done` and `p_waived` to `join_waiver`, and `join_waiver  p_decision` | 3 (remove 1 transition, add 1 transition + 2 arcs) | Analysis mandatory; waiver path explicitly synchronized                        | Clearer separation of waiver/standard review paths                            | Slightly higher structural complexity                                      |
| **Repair 3: Optional analysis on waiver** | Add arc `p_waived  p_decision`; remove `join_waived`                       | 2 (add 1 arc, remove 1 transition) | Waiver path bypasses analysis (policy change)                                  | Analysis no longer mandatory for waivers                                       | **Policy violation**: waivers may authorize cases without analysis        |

**Justification of minimality**:
- Repair 1 is minimal (cost=1) and preserves the business rule.
- Repair 2 is slightly more costly (cost=3) but improves clarity.
- Repair 3 is minimal (cost=2) but changes the business policy, so it is only valid if the policy is explicitly relaxed.

import pm4py

def fire_and_print(net, marking, transition_name):
    """Fire a transition by name and print the resulting marking."""
    enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
    transition = next(t for t in net.transitions if t.name == transition_name)
    if transition not in enabled:
        raise ValueError(f"Transition {transition_name} not enabled")
    new_marking = pm4py.petri_net.utils.add_markings(marking, pm4py.petri_net.utils.remove_marking(marking, transition))
    print(f"Fired {transition_name}: {new_marking}")
    return new_marking

# 7. Repair Trade-Offs and Residual Risks

1. **Waiver/standard review distinguishability**:
   - **Repair 1**: Preserved. Both paths remain distinct, but `join_waived` is silent, which may obscure the waiver in logs.
   - **Repair 2**: Explicitly separates the paths, improving traceability.

2. **Silent transitions**:
   - `join_normal` and `join_waived` are silent, which may complicate process mining. However, this is inherent to the synchronization pattern.

3. **Soundness vs. business alignment**:
   - Soundness ensures proper completion and no leftover tokens, but does not guarantee that the net enforces *all* business rules (e.g., mandatory analysis). Additional checks (e.g., trace alignment) are needed.

4. **Regression testing**:
   - **Positive traces**:
     - Standard review: `t_register  t_split  t_analyze  t_standard_review  t_join_normal  t_authorize`.
     - Waiver: `t_register  t_split  t_analyze  t_waive_review  t_join_waived  t_authorize`.
   - **Negative traces**:
     - Waiver without analysis: `t_register  t_split  t_waive_review  t_join_waived` (should fail in repaired net).
     - Premature authorization: `t_register  t_split  t_waive_review  t_join_waived  t_authorize` (should fail in repaired net).

5. **Detecting analysis bypass**:
   - Monitor for traces where `t_authorize` occurs without `t_analyze` in the log.
   - Use conformance checking to detect deviations from the repaired model.

# 8. Final Recommendation

**Primary choice**: **Repair 1 (Waiver sync fix)**.
- **Rationale**: Minimal edit cost (1 arc), preserves the business rule, and fixes the defect without adding complexity. The silent `join_waived` is a minor trade-off for simplicity.

**Fallback choice**: **Repair 2 (Dedicated waiver join)**.
- **Rationale**: If traceability is critical (e.g., audits require explicit waiver paths), the additional complexity is justified. The cost is still low (3 edits), and the separation of paths reduces ambiguity.

**Avoid Repair 3**: It changes the business policy (making analysis optional for waivers), which is not aligned with the stated intent. Only consider this if the policy is explicitly revised.
