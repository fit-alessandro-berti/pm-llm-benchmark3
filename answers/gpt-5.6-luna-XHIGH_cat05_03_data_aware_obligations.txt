## 1. Temporal Data Semantics

For each case, events are ordered by the pair `(time:timestamp, event_index)`. Write `e1 ≺ e2` when `e1` has an earlier timestamp, or when the timestamps are equal and `e1.event_index < e2.event_index`. State at event `e` is the fold of all attribute updates for that case with event order less than or equal to `e`. A blank attribute is not an assignment: it leaves the previous known value unchanged. An update is effective at its event timestamp unless reliable evidence gives it another effective time.

Thus, a release uses the state immediately after the `Release_Funds` event's preceding updates in this total order. It does not use values from later events, such as settlement data or a later database insertion. The final case state is only the state after the last event and must not be copied backward to a review or release.

I treat a trigger-affecting jurisdiction, risk-level, or customer-type update as a material data change. For amount-dependent review freshness, the explicit R4 threshold controls: a known increase greater than 20% after the review is material and invalidating; a decrease does not invalidate; a change from an unknown prior amount is not safely classifiable. A smaller known increase does not invalidate under R4, although it remains an auditable amount update. The initial transaction values establish the initial state, not a post-review change.

Four times must be kept separate:

- Event time is when the source event is ordered in the event log.
- Effective time is when an attribute or exemption legally takes effect. It defaults to event time here unless contrary evidence is established.
- Record time is when a database row is inserted or displayed. It may be later than the underlying approval or effective time.
- Evidence time is the timestamp carried by reliable evidence, such as a qualified signature or authenticated approval log. A displayed backdated field alone does not prove that the approval existed at that time.

Equal timestamps do not erase causality. For TX-311, event index 3 is before event index 4, so the Enhanced_Review sees the pre-update jurisdiction and the jurisdiction is DE only after the review.

## 2. Normalized Rule Table

| Rule | Trigger event and condition | Required prior/subsequent event | Ordering constraint | State snapshot used | Exception | Missing-data treatment | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | At `Release_Funds`, known current jurisdiction is not DE and known current amount is at least EUR 100,000 | Enhanced review before release and after the latest material data change, unless a valid matching exemption waives R1 | `material_change ≺ Enhanced_Review ≺ Release_Funds`; the review may be same-timestamp only when its event index is earlier | State at release for jurisdiction and amount; state at each change and review for freshness | R5 exemption | Unknown jurisdiction or amount leaves the trigger unresolved unless another reliable payload resolves it | Immutable release attributes, review evidence, material-change provenance, or valid exemption evidence |
| R2 | At `Release_Funds`, current risk is HIGH or current customer type is PEP | Enhanced review and Compliance approval | `Enhanced_Review ≺ Compliance_Approve ≺ Release_Funds`; approval must follow the relevant enhanced review | Current risk and customer type at release; review/approval event states | No R5 exemption is stated for R2 | Unknown risk or customer type leaves the OR trigger unresolved unless the other known field establishes the trigger or excludes it | Release snapshot, enhanced-review scope, approval content and timestamp |
| R3 | Current jurisdiction was added to the sanctions list after the latest screen and before release | A new Sanctions_Screen after the list update and before release | `latest Sanctions_Screen ≺ Sanctions_List_Update ≺ new Sanctions_Screen ≺ Release_Funds` | Current release jurisdiction and jurisdiction named by the list update | None stated | Missing jurisdiction, screen, or list-update identity prevents a decisive result | Signed list update, screen result, jurisdiction identity, timestamps |
| R4 | A known amount increase greater than 20% occurs after the latest Standard_Review or Enhanced_Review | No compensating review event is prescribed by R4, but the affected review cannot satisfy amount-dependent obligations | `Review ≺ Amount_Update ≺ Release_Funds` invalidates that review; a decrease does not | Amount immediately before and after each update, not final amount alone | No invalidation for a decrease; no safe classification from an unknown prior amount | Unknown prior amount produces an unknown invalidation status | Old/new amount, effective time, update provenance, immutable review amount |
| R5 | An exemption is invoked for an obligation | Head_Compliance approval and reliable evidence that it existed at the trigger; matching scope and validity window | `effective_from ≤ trigger`; reliable approval evidence must establish existence by the relevant time; record insertion may be later | State at the trigger for jurisdiction, customer type, and amount; exemption register/evidence at the time | Only the obligations explicitly waived by the exemption, here R1 | Unknown scope or inconclusive approval evidence makes exemption validity indeterminate | Approval identity, qualified signature/authenticated log, effective/expiry times, exact scope, record provenance |
| R6 | Any decisive trigger depends on a missing amount, risk, jurisdiction, or customer type | Evidence resolution, if available, must be tied to the relevant event time | Later evidence is not automatically retroactive | Latest known state at the obligation event | None; do not default to low risk | Classify INDETERMINATE when the trigger cannot be established or excluded | Immutable event payload or reliable contemporaneous evidence |
| R7 | Every obligation is evaluated at its relevant event | All state transitions and control events must be retained in order | Timestamp first, then `event_index` for ties; no backward propagation from later events | Event-local snapshot | None | Unknown remains unknown at that event | Source ordering, stable event ID/index, effective-time metadata |

Let `S(c,e,a)` be the latest known value of attribute `a` for case `c` at event `e`, including updates at `e`; let `≺` be the total order above. Let `M(c,e)` mean an obligation-relevant material change. Let `fresh(x,r)` mean `x ≺ r` and there is no `m` such that `M(c,m)` and `x ≺ m ≺ r`. Let `known(v)` mean that the value is resolved by event-time evidence.

```text
R1_trigger(c,r) ⇔ activity(r)=Release_Funds ∧ known(S(c,r,jurisdiction))
                   ∧ known(S(c,r,amount)) ∧ S(c,r,jurisdiction) ≠ DE
                   ∧ S(c,r,amount) ≥ 100000

R1_ok(c,r) ⇔ ¬R1_trigger(c,r)
              ∨ ∃x [ValidExemption(x,c,r) ∧ waives(x,R1)]
              ∨ ∃h [activity(h)=Enhanced_Review ∧ fresh(h,r)]

R2_trigger(c,r) ⇔ activity(r)=Release_Funds ∧
                   (S(c,r,risk_level)=HIGH ∨ S(c,r,customer_type)=PEP)

R2_ok(c,r) ⇔ ¬R2_trigger(c,r)
              ∨ ∃h,a [activity(h)=Enhanced_Review ∧ activity(a)=Compliance_Approve
                       ∧ h ≺ a ∧ a ≺ r ∧ ¬InvalidForAmount(h,r)]

R3_trigger(c,r) ⇔ ∃s,u [activity(s)=Sanctions_Screen ∧ activity(u)=Sanctions_List_Update
                         ∧ s is the latest screen with s ≺ u
                         ∧ u ≺ r ∧ S(c,u,jurisdiction)=S(c,r,jurisdiction)]

R3_ok(c,r) ⇔ ¬R3_trigger(c,r)
              ∨ ∃s2,u [R3-triggering u ∧ activity(s2)=Sanctions_Screen
                        ∧ u ≺ s2 ∧ s2 ≺ r]

InvalidForAmount(h,r) ⇔ ∃u [activity(u)=Update_Amount ∧ h ≺ u ≺ r
                             ∧ known(old_amount(u)) ∧ known(new_amount(u))
                             ∧ new_amount(u) > 1.20 × old_amount(u)]

UnknownAmountChange(h,r) ⇔ ∃u [activity(u)=Update_Amount ∧ h ≺ u ≺ r
                                ∧ ¬known(old_amount(u))]

ValidExemption(x,c,t) ⇔ approved_by(x)=Head_Compliance
                         ∧ effective_from(x) ≤ t ∧ not_expired(x,t)
                         ∧ scope_matches(x,S(c,t))
                         ∧ reliable_evidence_proves_approval_by(x,t)

R6_indeterminate(c,r) ⇔ a decisive trigger or invalidation predicate contains an
                         unresolved value and no event-time evidence resolves it.
```

The relations in the pseudo-logic are directional: `h ≺ a` means the enhanced review occurred before the approval, and `a ≺ r` means the approval occurred before release. A missing prior amount in `UnknownAmountChange` is not treated as zero.

## 4. Ambiguities and Alternative Readings

- **TX-302:** The late `Record_Exemption` does not by itself make the exemption late. Under R5, the qualified digital signature at 09:05 is strong evidence that approval existed before the 09:40 release, so the result is JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. If that timestamp were merely a database display time, or if the signature/hash could not be authenticated, exemption validity would become INDETERMINATE; if no timely approval could then be proven, TX-302 would be NONCOMPLIANT for R1. EX-77 waives R1 only and could not cure an R2 obligation.

- **TX-305:** The settlement amount of 150,000 is reliable at settlement time but is not an immutable release payload. If a signed release payload showed an amount below 100,000, R1 would be excluded. If it showed 150,000 effective at or before release and showed that the Enhanced_Review covered that amount with no intervening material change, R1 could be satisfied. If it showed 150,000 but also showed an unreviewed amount change after the review, the result would be NONCOMPLIANT. The settlement record alone cannot select among those readings.

- **TX-306:** The finding treats R4's explicit 20% threshold as the amount-materiality threshold for amount-dependent review freshness. If an organization instead defines every amount update, including 220,000 to 230,000, as a material change requiring a new R1 review, R1 would be deficient even though R2 remains satisfied. That interpretation should be fixed in the policy version and logging schema; it is not supported by the case note that the increase is below 20%.

- **TX-310:** An authenticated Head_Compliance approval log, qualified signature with a signing time no later than 10:00, or reliable contemporaneous evidence linking the witness statement to an actual approval would support JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. Evidence that the certificate creation time is the true approval time and that no earlier approval existed would make it NONCOMPLIANT, because the only review was Standard_Review. A displayed backdated field alone is insufficient either way.

- **TX-311:** The same-timestamp order is decisive for the review snapshot: index 3 is before index 4. If index 4 were actually ordered first, the Enhanced_Review would see DE rather than US. The release still has DE under the supplied release-time update, so R1 remains inactive. The result would change if the release payload preserved a non-DE jurisdiction, if the update's effective time were after release, or if policy imposed a re-review for every jurisdiction change even when the final jurisdiction no longer triggers R1. The last interpretation is a stricter policy than the stated conditional rule.

- **TX-309:** The result does not depend on copying the final amount backward: each increase is compared with the amount known immediately before that update and with the review most recently preceding it. If the second increase were proven to have been effective before the Enhanced_Review rather than at 15:28, the enhanced review would not be invalidated by that update, and R1 could be satisfied. The supplied event-time evidence places it after the review, so the noncompliance finding is strong.

```python
    if pd.isna(jurisdiction):
        r1_trigger = None
    elif jurisdiction == "DE":
        r1_trigger = False
    elif pd.isna(amount):
        r1_trigger = None
    else:
        r1_trigger = bool(amount >= 100000)

    # R2 is an OR trigger; one known triggering operand is enough.
    if risk == "HIGH" or customer_type == "PEP":
        r2_trigger = True
    elif pd.notna(risk) and pd.notna(customer_type):
        r2_trigger = False
    else:
        r2_trigger = None

    latest_standard = latest_event(before, "Standard_Review")
    latest_enhanced = latest_event(before, "Enhanced_Review")
    latest_approval = latest_event(before, "Compliance_Approve")
    latest_screen = latest_event(before, "Sanctions_Screen")
    latest_list_update = latest_event(before, "Sanctions_List_Update")
    latest_material = before.loc[before["material_data_change"]]
    latest_material = None if latest_material.empty else latest_material.iloc[-1]

    standard_seq = None if latest_standard is None else int(latest_standard["case_seq"])
    enhanced_seq = None if latest_enhanced is None else int(latest_enhanced["case_seq"])

    def post_review_amount_status(review_seq):
        if review_seq is None:
            return False, False
        after = case_events.loc[
            (case_events["case_seq"] > review_seq)
            & (case_events["case_seq"] < release_seq)
        ]
        return bool(after["amount_increase_gt20"].any()), bool(
            after["amount_change_unclassifiable"].any()
        )

    standard_invalid, standard_unknown = post_review_amount_status(standard_seq)
    enhanced_invalid, enhanced_unknown = post_review_amount_status(enhanced_seq)
    r4_unknown = standard_unknown or enhanced_unknown

    last_material_seq = 0 if latest_material is None else int(latest_material["case_seq"])
    enhanced_fresh = (
        enhanced_seq is not None and last_material_seq <= enhanced_seq
    )
    # Amount invalidation is also applied to the enhanced review for the
    # amount-dependent R1/R2 checks; the policy version should make this scope explicit.
    enhanced_usable = (
        enhanced_seq is not None and not enhanced_invalid and not enhanced_unknown
    )

    # Exemption validity is computed from scope, validity window, and evidence time.
    exemption_claimed = pd.notna(rel["release_exemption_id"])
    register_matched = pd.notna(rel["register_exemption_id"])
    scope_known = all(pd.notna(rel[x]) for x in [
        "release_amount", "release_jurisdiction", "release_customer_type"
    ])
    scope_match = bool(
        register_matched and scope_known
        and rel["release_jurisdiction"] == rel["scope_jurisdiction"]
        and rel["release_customer_type"] == rel["scope_customer_type"]
        and rel["release_amount"] <= rel["scope_max_amount"]
    )
    window_ok = bool(
        register_matched
        and pd.notna(rel["effective_from"])
        and pd.notna(rel["expires"])
        and rel["effective_from"] <= release_time <= rel["expires"]
    )
    evidence_proven = bool(
        register_matched
        and bool(rel["evidence_sufficient"])
        and pd.notna(rel["approval_evidence_time"])
        and rel["approval_evidence_time"] <= release_time
        and rel["approved_by"] == "Head_Compliance"
    )
    exemption_valid = scope_match and window_ok and evidence_proven
    # A claimed, scope/window-matching exemption with inconclusive approval
    # evidence is preserved for human review rather than forced to invalid.
    exemption_human_review = bool(
        exemption_claimed and register_matched and scope_match and window_ok
        and not evidence_proven
    )

    if r1_trigger is True:
        r1_ok = bool((exemption_valid and bool(rel["waives_r1"]))
                     or (enhanced_usable and enhanced_fresh))
    elif r1_trigger is False:
        r1_ok = True
    else:
        r1_ok = None

    approvals_after_enhanced = before.loc[
        before[ACTIVITY].eq("Compliance_Approve")
        & (before["case_seq"] > (enhanced_seq or 10**9))
    ]
    r2_order_ok = not approvals_after_enhanced.empty and enhanced_seq is not None
    if r2_trigger is True:
        r2_ok = bool(enhanced_usable and r2_order_ok)
    elif r2_trigger is False:
        r2_ok = True
    else:
        r2_ok = None

    # R3: identify a list update for the release jurisdiction after the latest screen.
    if pd.isna(jurisdiction):
        relevant_updates = before.loc[before[ACTIVITY].eq("Sanctions_List_Update")]
        r3_trigger = None if not relevant_updates.empty else False
    else:
        relevant_updates = before.loc[
            before[ACTIVITY].eq("Sanctions_List_Update")
            & before["state_jurisdiction"].eq(jurisdiction)
        ]
        screen_seq = None if latest_screen is None else int(latest_screen["case_seq"])
        if relevant_updates.empty:
            r3_trigger = False
        elif screen_seq is None:
            r3_trigger = None
        else:
            r3_trigger = bool(
                (relevant_updates["case_seq"] > screen_seq).any()
            )
    if r3_trigger is True:
        latest_triggering_update = relevant_updates.loc[
            relevant_updates["case_seq"] > int(latest_screen["case_seq"])
        ].iloc[-1]
        later_screens = before.loc[
            before[ACTIVITY].eq("Sanctions_Screen")
            & (before["case_seq"] > int(latest_triggering_update["case_seq"]))
        ]
        r3_ok = not later_screens.empty
    elif r3_trigger is False:
        r3_ok = True
    else:
        r3_ok = None

    unknown_flags = []
    if r1_trigger is None:
        unknown_flags.append("R1 amount/jurisdiction")
    if r2_trigger is None:
        unknown_flags.append("R2 risk/customer_type")
    if r3_trigger is None:
        unknown_flags.append("R3 screen/list/jurisdiction")
    if r4_unknown:
        unknown_flags.append("R4 prior amount")
    if r1_trigger is True and exemption_human_review:
        unknown_flags.append("exemption approval evidence")

    hard_violation = (
        (r1_trigger is True and not r1_ok and not exemption_human_review)
        or (r2_trigger is True and not r2_ok)
        or (r3_trigger is True and not r3_ok)
    )
    if unknown_flags:
        coarse_classification = "REVIEW_REQUIRED"
    elif hard_violation:
        coarse_classification = "NONCOMPLIANT"
    elif r1_trigger is True and exemption_valid and bool(rel["waives_r1"]):
        coarse_classification = "JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION"
    else:
        coarse_classification = "COMPLIANT"

    def t_and_i(activity):
        return event_time_and_index(latest_event(before, activity))

    mat_time, mat_index = event_time_and_index(latest_material)
    diagnostic_rows.append({
        CASE: case,
        "release_time": release_time,
        "release_event_index": rel["event_index"],
        "release_amount": amount,
        "release_risk_level": risk,
        "release_jurisdiction": jurisdiction,
        "release_customer_type": customer_type,
        "release_exemption_id": rel["release_exemption_id"],
        "last_standard_review_time": t_and_i("Standard_Review")[0],
        "last_enhanced_review_time": t_and_i("Enhanced_Review")[0],
        "last_compliance_approve_time": t_and_i("Compliance_Approve")[0],
        "last_sanctions_screen_time": t_and_i("Sanctions_Screen")[0],
        "last_sanctions_list_update_time": t_and_i("Sanctions_List_Update")[0],
        "last_material_change_time": mat_time,
        "last_material_change_event_index": mat_index,
        "exemption_record_time": rel["record_time"],
        "exemption_approval_evidence_time": rel["approval_evidence_time"],
        "r1_trigger": tri_string(r1_trigger),
        "r1_ok": tri_string(r1_ok),
        "r2_trigger": tri_string(r2_trigger),
        "r2_ok": tri_string(r2_ok),
        "r3_trigger": tri_string(r3_trigger),
        "r3_ok": tri_string(r3_ok),
        "standard_invalidated_r4": standard_invalid,
        "enhanced_invalidated_r4": enhanced_invalid,
        "exemption_scope_match": scope_match,
        "exemption_evidence_proven_by_release": evidence_proven,
        "exemption_valid_coarse": exemption_valid,
        "unknown_value_flags": "; ".join(unknown_flags),
        "coarse_classification": coarse_classification,
    })

diagnostics = pd.DataFrame(diagnostic_rows).sort_values(CASE).reset_index(drop=True)

print("State at each release:")
print(release_state[[CASE, "event_index", TIME] + [f"release_{a}" for a in attrs]])
print("\nCase-level diagnostics:")
print(diagnostics.to_string(index=False))
```

The code intentionally preserves the settlement amount as a later event and therefore does not fill it backward into TX-305's release. It also keeps the material-change event index, so a same-timestamp change after a review is not confused with one before it. The exemption join exposes record and evidence timestamps separately.

## 6. Why Control-Flow-Only Conformance Is Insufficient

Control-flow conformance can identify whether an activity appears in a trace, but it cannot faithfully decide these obligations for at least the following reasons:

1. **Final-state leakage:** a trace model may attach the final amount, jurisdiction, or risk to every event, making an earlier review appear to have assessed a later state.
2. **Post-hoc exemptions:** an `EX-77` or `EX-88` label in a release trace says nothing about whether approval existed before release, whether it was signed, or whether a late record is trustworthy.
3. **Material changes after review:** the same sequence `Enhanced_Review → Release_Funds` can be compliant or noncompliant depending on the amount change between them and the old/new values.
4. **Conditional activation:** R1, R2, and R3 are activated by release-time values and intervening changes. A control-flow model cannot infer a predicate such as “non-DE and at least EUR 100,000” from activity order alone.
5. **Missing data:** an absent amount is not a low amount. TX-305 has the same visible review/release pattern as a complete case, but its decisive trigger is unknown.
6. **Same-timestamp ordering:** TX-311 requires `event_index` to distinguish a review before a jurisdiction update from a review after it. Timestamp-only traces lose that distinction.
7. **Scope and semantics:** Standard_Review and Enhanced_Review are different control types, and an approval can cover a standard packet rather than the enhanced obligation. Activity labels without scope/content cannot establish equivalence.
8. **Evidence and effective time:** record time, effective time, signature time, and event time can disagree. A control-flow trace cannot assess qualified-signature validity, expiry, scope matching, or whether an update was merely recorded late.
9. **Amount comparison:** R4 requires arithmetic against the immediately preceding known amount and the review's position, including an unknown-prior branch. A Petri-net path generally does not carry enough data context unless it is extended with guards and data state.

Accordingly, a trace-order check is useful as a coarse screen for impossible sequences, but the compliance decision needs event-time state, data predicates, provenance, and evidence adjudication.

## 7. Recommended Logging and Control Improvements

- Emit an immutable release payload containing amount, risk level, jurisdiction, customer type, exemption ID, policy decision, and the exact state version used for the release. Store a hash of that payload.
- Record separate `effective_time`, `event_time`, `record_time`, and, for approvals, `signature_time` or authenticated evidence time. Do not overload one timestamp for all meanings.
- Give every event a globally unique ID and a stable per-case sequence or source offset. Preserve `event_index` for equal timestamps and record corrections as new events rather than overwriting history.
- Log every attribute change with old value, new value, effective time, actor/system, reason, source, confidence, and provenance. For amounts, retain the comparison baseline needed for the R4 percentage test.
- Store review scope and snapshot hashes: amount assessed, jurisdiction, risk, customer type, rule version, reviewer, completion time, and whether the review was Standard or Enhanced.
- Store approval scope and decision content, including the enhanced-review ID it approves, approver identity, signature evidence, and the approval's effective time.
- Make exemption records immutable and explicit: exemption ID, approving authority, signature/evidence chain, effective-from and expiry times, exact jurisdiction/customer-type/amount scope, waived rule IDs, and both record time and evidence time.
- Record sanctions-list updates with version, jurisdiction, source, publication/effective time, ingestion time, and the screen version against which the case was checked.
- Version the policy and threshold interpretation used for each decision, including whether a sub-20% amount update is considered material for R1 freshness.
- Fail closed or route to review when a decisive release attribute is missing. Preserve an explicit `UNKNOWN` value and an evidence-resolution workflow instead of substituting a low-risk default.
- Add automated pre-release guards that recompute event-time state, require fresh reviews after qualifying changes, verify approval ordering, and block or escalate when exemption evidence is late or contradictory.
