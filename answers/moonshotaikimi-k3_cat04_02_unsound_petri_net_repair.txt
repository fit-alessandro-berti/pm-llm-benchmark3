from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking

def fire_by_name(net, marking, transition_name):
    """Fire `transition_name` if enabled; raise otherwise. Never moves tokens blindly."""
    t = next(t for t in net.transitions if t.name == transition_name)
    enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
    if t not in enabled:
        raise RuntimeError(f"{transition_name} is NOT enabled at {dict(marking)}")
    result = Marking()
    for p, c in marking.items():
        if c > 0:
            result[p] = c
    for arc in t.in_arcs:
        remaining = result.get(arc.source, 0) - arc.weight
        if remaining > 0:
            result[arc.source] = remaining
        elif arc.source in result:
            del result[arc.source]
    for arc in t.out_arcs:
        result[arc.target] = result.get(arc.target, 0) + arc.weight
    return result

def show(marking):
    return {p.name: c for p, c in marking.items()} or "{empty}"

def run_diagnostics(tag, n, i, f):
    print(f"===== {tag} =====")
    print("is_workflow_net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(n))
    print("check_soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(n, i, f, print_diagnostics=True))
    print("enabled at initial marking:", sorted(t.name for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(n, i)))
    rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(n, i, f)
    print("reachability-graph states:", len(rg.states))

run_diagnostics("ORIGINAL", net, im, fm)
run_diagnostics("REPAIRED", repaired_net, repaired_im, repaired_fm)

# Replay the waiver-path counterexample on the original net.

# 1. Executive Verdict

The net **is a workflow net**: it has a unique source (`source`), a unique sink (`sink`), and every place and transition lies on a source-to-sink path, so `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net` returns `True`. It should nevertheless be expected to be **unsound**: `pm4py.check_soundness` will return `False`. The principal defect is that `t_join_waived` is a degenerate "join" with a single input (`p_waived`); unlike `t_join_normal`, it never waits for `p_analysis_done`. Therefore, on the waiver route, `t_authorize` can fire before — or entirely without — `t_analyze`, and the analysis token is stranded in `p_analysis`/`p_analysis_done` with no possible consumer. The exact final marking `{sink: 1}` is unreachable on every waiver interleaving. Business consequence: waived cases can be authorized without the mandatory analysis ever completing, and such cases never terminate cleanly, leaving stuck tokens that corrupt completion monitoring and audit state.

Here `t_authorize` occurs **after** `t_analyze`, yet the case still deadlocks at `{p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1}` with a permanently stranded token. Hence **no** waiver interleaving reaches `fm`.

**Control (positive) trace.** `t_register  t_split  t_analyze  t_standard_review  t_join_normal  t_authorize` yields exactly `{sink: 1} = fm`. The standard-review path is fine; the defect is isolated to the waiver synchronization.

# 4. Minimal-Repair Alternatives

**Edit-cost model.** Adding or removing one arc costs 1. Adding or removing one place or one transition costs 2 (a node is useless without connecting arcs). Label/name edits cost 0 and are not accepted as repairs. Cost of a repair = total units.

| Repair | Exact edits | Edit cost | Preserved intent | Changed behavior | New risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **R1 — give the waiver path its own proper two-input synchronization** | Add arc `p_analysis_done  t_join_waived` | **1** (one arc added) | Full intent: analysis mandatory on both paths; review outcomes remain distinguishable; single authorization; exact `fm` | `t_join_waived` becomes a true AND-join over `{p_analysis_done, p_waived}`, mirroring `t_join_normal`; `t_authorize` on the waiver route only after analysis completes | Two silent joins (`t_join_normal` / `t_join_waived`) duplicate synchronization logic; -steps are indistinguishable in label-only logs/alignments; maintainers must update both joins if the post-review process changes |
| **R2 — rewire the waiver outcome into the normal synchronization (single shared AND-join)** | Add arc `t_waive_review  p_review_done`; remove arc `t_waive_review  p_waived`; remove place `p_waived`; remove arcs `p_waived  t_join_waived` and `t_join_waived  p_decision`; remove transition `t_join_waived` | 1+1+2+1+1+2 = **8** | Full intent: analysis mandatory on both paths; single join enforces `{p_analysis_done, p_review_done}` | Both review outcomes merge into `p_review_done`; one join, no duplicated logic; outcomes still distinguishable via visible labels ("Perform Standard Review" vs "Record Review Waiver") | `p_review_done` now has two producers; relies on the upstream XOR for 1-boundedness — a future change allowing *both* review types reintroduces stranded tokens; larger diff to review |
| **R3 — explicit policy change: waiver cancels pending analysis** | Add arc `p_analysis  t_waive_review` (recording the waiver consumes, i.e., cancels, the not-yet-done analysis) | **1** (one arc added) | Partial only: parallel start, XOR review choice, single authorization, exact `fm` (net is sound: 9 reachable markings, all lead to `{sink: 1}`) | **POLICY CHANGE, stated explicitly: analysis is no longer mandatory under waiver.** Side effect: `t_waive_review` now needs the `p_analysis` token, so waiver becomes unavailable once `t_analyze` has fired — a timing race; the trace *analyze-then-waive* disappears | Authorization with **no analysis at all** becomes legal; waiver availability depends on execution timing; requires explicit business sign-off; audit exposure |

**Minimality justification.** The defect is exactly one missing dependency: "waiver join waits for analysis completion." In arc terms, any correct repair must either (a) add that dependency ( 1 arc into the join), or (b) guarantee the analysis token cannot outlive the waiver decision ( 1 consuming arc), or (c) restructure ( 2 for a node, plus arcs). No 0-cost repair exists, so cost 1 is the lower bound, achieved by R1 and R3. Not every cost-1 edit is valid: e.g., *replacing* the `p_waived` input of `t_join_waived` with `p_analysis_done` (cost 2 as add+remove, but the point generalizes) would let authorization happen with **no review outcome at all**, violating intent; and deleting `t_join_waived`/`p_waived` without rerouting disconnects the waiver path, breaking the workflow-net path condition. **Tie-break between R1 and R3 (both cost 1):** R1 preserves the stated business intent, while R3 spends the same single arc on a policy change and adds a race; under the given intent, R1 strictly dominates. R2 is not minimal under this cost model (8) but is structurally the cleanest (one join, no silent-transition duplication) and cheapest to extend (each new review outcome costs 1 arc instead of place + transition + arcs).

# 6. Diagnostic and Reachability Code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking


def show(marking):
    """Human-readable nonzero marking."""
    return {p.name: c for p, c in marking.items() if c > 0} or "{empty}"


def fire_by_name(net, marking, transition_name):
    """Fire `transition_name` iff enabled; never moves tokens blindly."""
    t = next((t for t in net.transitions if t.name == transition_name), None)
    if t is None:
        raise KeyError(f"no transition named {transition_name}")
    if t not in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking):
        raise RuntimeError(f"{transition_name} is NOT enabled at {show(marking)}")
    result = Marking()
    for p, c in marking.items():
        if c > 0:
            result[p] = c
    for arc in t.in_arcs:
        remaining = result.get(arc.source, 0) - arc.weight
        if remaining > 0:
            result[arc.source] = remaining
        elif arc.source in result:
            del result[arc.source]
    for arc in t.out_arcs:
        result[arc.target] = result.get(arc.target, 0) + arc.weight
    return result


def run_diagnostics(tag, n, i, f):
    print(f"===== {tag} =====")
    print("is_workflow_net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(n))
    print("check_soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(n, i, f, print_diagnostics=True))
    print("enabled at im:", sorted(t.name for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(n, i)))
    rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(n, i, f)
    print("reachability-graph states:", len(rg.states))
    sink_place = next(p for p in n.places if p.name == "sink")
    improper = [s for s in rg.states if s.get(sink_place, 0) > 0 and s != f]
    deadlocks = [s for s in rg.states
                 if s != f and not pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(n, s)]
    print("sink-marked states != fm:", [show(s) for s in improper])
    print("non-final deadlock states:", [show(s) for s in deadlocks])


run_diagnostics("ORIGINAL", net, im, fm)
run_diagnostics("REPAIRED", repaired_net, repaired_im, repaired_fm)

# --- Replay the waiver-path counterexample on the ORIGINAL net ---
m = im
print("ORIGINAL start:", show(m))
for step in ["t_register", "t_split", "t_waive_review",
             "t_join_waived", "t_authorize", "t_analyze"]:
    m = fire_by_name(net, m, step)
    print(f"after {step}: {show(m)} | enabled:",
          sorted(t.name for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m)))
print("original: sink token present but marking == fm ?", m == fm)  # False

# --- Same interleaving attempted on the REPAIRED net ---
m = repaired_im
for step in ["t_register", "t_split", "t_waive_review"]:
    m = fire_by_name(repaired_net, m, step)
print("REPAIRED after waiver:", show(m), "| enabled:",
      sorted(t.name for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(repaired_net, m)))
try:
    m = fire_by_name(repaired_net, m, "t_join_waived")
except RuntimeError as exc:
    print("expected refusal:", exc)
for step in ["t_analyze", "t_join_waived", "t_authorize"]:
    m = fire_by_name(repaired_net, m, step)
print("repaired final marking:", show(m), "| == fm ?", m == repaired_fm)  # True
```

**Expected qualitative difference** (diagnostic wording varies by PM4Py version; no specific WOFLAN keys assumed): for the **original**, `check_is_workflow_net`  `True` but `check_soundness`  `False`, and the diagnostics indicate behavioral unsoundness on a structurally valid workflow net; the reachability graph contains sink-marked states that are not `fm` (`{p_analysis, sink}`, `{p_analysis_done, sink}`) and a non-final deadlock (`{p_analysis_done, sink}`). For the **repaired** net, both checks return `True`, no sink-marked state differs from `fm`, and the only token-free-of-work state is `fm` itself. The decisive single-marking difference: at `{p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1}` the original enables `{t_analyze, t_join_waived}` while the repaired net enables `{t_analyze}` only. State-space sizes, derived by explicit enumeration (not invented): the original has **14** reachable states — `{source}`, `{p_ready}`, `{p_analysis,p_review}`, `{p_analysis_done,p_review}`, `{p_analysis,p_review_done}`, `{p_analysis,p_waived}`, `{p_analysis_done,p_review_done}`, `{p_analysis_done,p_waived}`, `{p_decision}`, `{p_analysis,p_decision}`, `{p_analysis_done,p_decision}`, `{sink}`, `{p_analysis,sink}`, `{p_analysis_done,sink}` — and the repaired net has **10** (the same list minus the last four defective states, keeping `{sink}`).

# 7. Repair Trade-Offs and Residual Risks

- **Distinguishability of waiver vs. standard review.** Under R1, the outcomes remain distinct both in the net (separate places `p_review_done`/`p_waived`, separate joins) and in any event log (visible labels "Perform Standard Review" vs. "Record Review Waiver"). Under R2 the control flow merges after the choice, so distinguishability rests solely on the visible activity labels — adequate for logging, weaker for model-based auditing.
- **Silent-transition duplication/confusion.** R1 keeps two -joins with near-identical names. They produce no duplicate *visible* behavior (the visible trace language is exactly the intended one), but label-only logs cannot attribute a -move to either join, and conformance tools may align -steps ambiguously. Mitigation: comments/naming discipline now; adopt R2 in a planned refactor.
- **Soundness  business alignment.** R3 is sound (verified by enumeration: 9 reachable markings, all lead to exactly `fm`) yet permits authorization with *no analysis at all* — a direct breach of "analysis is always mandatory." Soundness guarantees completion hygiene (option to complete, proper completion, no dead transitions); it says nothing about ordering rules such as "authorize only after analysis" or "a waiver must be formally recorded." Compliance needs separate trace-level checks.
- **Regression traces.** Positive: `Register, , Perform Analysis, Perform Standard Review, , Authorize`; `Register, , Record Review Waiver, Perform Analysis, , Authorize`; `Register, , Perform Analysis, Record Review Waiver, , Authorize` — all must reach exactly `fm`. Negative: `Register, , Record Review Waiver, Authorize` (analysis skipped) must be non-fitting; any waiver trace with `Authorize` before `Perform Analysis`; any trace whose alignment leaves tokens (fitness < 1); any case firing *both* review alternatives.
- **Detecting an accidental bypass of mandatory analysis.** Statically: assert that every transition producing into `p_decision` has `p_analysis_done` among its inputs. Dynamically: enumerate the reachability graph and assert no reachable marking marks `p_decision` (or `sink`) together with `p_analysis` or `p_analysis_done` — this exact scan fails on the original and passes on the repaired net. At runtime: alignment-based conformance checking on waiver-case logs, plus a log query for cases containing "Record Review Waiver" and "Authorize Case" but no preceding "Perform Analysis."

# 8. Final Recommendation

**Deploy R1** (add the single arc `p_analysis_done  t_join_waived`). It is minimal under the stated edit-cost model (cost 1, the provable lower bound for an arc-based fix), preserves the entire business intent — mandatory analysis on both review routes, a single authorization point, exact proper completion — and keeps waiver and standard review fully distinguishable. Its correctness is established independently of any tool: the enumerated 10-state reachability graph shows every reachable marking leads to exactly `{sink: 1}`, no reachable marking marks `p_decision` or `sink` alongside an unfinished analysis token, and all eight transitions remain live. The PM4Py diagnostics merely confirm this (`is_workflow_net` and `check_soundness` both `True`).

**Fallback: R2** (merge both review outcomes into `p_review_done` and keep one shared AND-join; cost 8). It is behaviorally equivalent with respect to intent and is the better long-term structure: a single join removes the duplicated silent synchronization, and adding a future third review outcome costs one arc instead of a place, a transition, and three arcs. Adopt it when the model next undergoes a planned refactor.

**Do not deploy R3** (waiver cancels analysis) unless the business explicitly changes the policy to make analysis optional under waiver; although minimal and sound, it legalizes authorization without any analysis and makes waiver availability race with analysis completion — an audit liability, not just a modeling choice.
