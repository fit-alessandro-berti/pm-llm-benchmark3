## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Clause X: Emergency Overlays and Exceptions (draft)**

1. **Hierarchy.** GPIP-4 prevails over local SOPs and incident directives on segregation of duties, dual approval, Compliance clearance and thresholds. Local SOPs may be stricter. An incident directive may override only expressly listed local sequencing and register rules.
2. **Who may waive.** Only the CRO, or a named alternate when the CRO is unavailable (e.g. General Counsel with the Chief Compliance Officer), may issue a written, scoped, time-limited exception. A COO/Incident Commander directive is operative on sequencing and register rules but is not an exception to GPIP-4 unless countersigned in writing by the CRO or alternate.
3. **Minimum emergency controls.** Even under an exception:
   - no requester or committer may be a final approver;
   - a same-day Compliance screen, by an on-call officer, is required before commitment for any new supplier or any purchase above €100,000;
   - a Finance Controller or delegate gives pre-commit approval, by phone if needed and recorded;
   - at least one approver must be independent of the requester and committer.
4. **Scope.** "Critical lots" means components whose absence is expected to stop the affected line within 72 hours, as designated on a named list signed by the Incident Commander. Unlisted items are out of scope.
5. **Thresholds.** The €50k and €100k thresholds and the €150k CFO-guidance limit must be stated in one controlled table. CFO emails may interpret Finance controls but must be filed in the repository and cannot waive GPIP-4.
6. **Retrospective records.** A verbal authorization must be recorded within 4 hours by a trusted-timestamped artifact. Independent contemporaneous evidence outranks system-entry time for timing. Logging delays and outages do not shift the legal time of the act.
7. **Expiry.** An act after expiry is outside the overlay. Extensions require a written joint instruction before expiry. Verbal extensions are void unless recorded within 4 hours with a timestamped artifact.
8. **Post-hoc approval.** Later approval mitigates but does not cure a missing pre-commit GPIP-4 approval. It is logged as a breach with mitigation.
9. **Anti-normalization safeguards.**
   - Emergency directives auto-expire after 72 hours and may be renewed once, only with CRO sign-off.
   - Emergency purchases are reported monthly to the Audit Committee, with a cap on share of spend.
   - Breach counts are tracked per requester.
   - A post-incident review of every emergency commitment is required within 10 business days.
