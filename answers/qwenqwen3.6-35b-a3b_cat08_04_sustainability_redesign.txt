### 3. Intervention options

#### S1. Dynamic Green Shipping Recommender (SP1 Enforcement)
*   **Target:** Standard-stock Service-contract, Standard-stock Regular.
*   **Mechanism:** Implement a recommender that checks `promised_lead_time_hours` vs. route reliability. Replaces air with ground/consolidated truck when slack exists. Uses `expedite_flag` only when necessary.
*   **Effect:** Reduces transport CO2 significantly (air is 4–6× ground). Service maintained via priority picking for Service-contracts to offset ground wait.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor SP1 compliance rate (currently 78%); track CO2 reduction per order; compare late delivery rates pre/post.

#### S2. Dynamic Batching with Segment-Specific Caps
*   **Target:** MTO production (CNC_A, Heat treatment).
*   **Mechanism:** Batch size optimization to reach energy sweet spot (batch 12 for CNC_A reduces energy/item from 2.8 to 1.3 kWh). Apply hard caps on `Batch wait` based on segment: e.g., max 20h for Service-contract, max 10h for Regular.
*   **Effect:** Lowers production energy per item. Caps prevent late-delivery spikes (heat treatment has 1.7× late risk with large waits) and reduce obsolescence risk.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `batch_size` distribution, `Batch wait` hours, energy/item, and late delivery correlation with batch size.

#### S3. Machine Migration to CNC_A with Quality Gates
*   **Target:** Made-to-order cases currently on CNC_B.
*   **Mechanism:** Shift MTO production to CNC_A when queue slack allows. CNC_A has 9% rework vs 18% on CNC_B and better energy scaling. Add inspection quality gate for high-risk part/machine combos to prevent rework loops.
*   **Effect:** Reduces `material_scrap_kg` (MTO scrap is 0.85–0.90 kg; rework adds more). Reduces rework loops and associated energy. Slightly increases cycle time but improves quality.
*   **Evidence:** Track `machine_group` distribution, `material_scrap_kg`, `rework_flag`, and rework loop counts.

#### S4. Consolidated Truck Frequency & Priority Picking
*   **Target:** Non-urgent Standard-stock and MTO orders.
*   **Mechanism:** Increase consolidated truck departures to twice daily. For Service-contract orders, combine with priority picking to reduce internal wait, allowing ground/consolidated shipping without service loss.
*   **Effect:** Consolidated trucks reduce CO2 by 35–55% vs ground parcels. Priority picking maintains service levels. Reduces air usage.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `route_mode` shift to consolidated truck, `transport_kgco2e`, and median cycle time for Service-contract.

#### S5. Packaging-Route Compatibility Engine
*   **Target:** All orders, especially MTO heavy parts and rework paths.
*   **Mechanism:** Validate `package_material_kg` and geometry against `route_mode` before shipping. Enforce Q1 (reinforced for >8kg MTO) automatically. Prevent incompatible packaging that causes rework.
*   **Effect:** Reduces `rework_flag` for packaging, lowers `package_material_kg` by avoiding over-packaging, and cuts returns.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `rework_flag` (packaging sub-type), `package_material_kg`, and return/rework loops.

#### S6. Critical Downtime Tiering & Shared Emergency Lanes
*   **Target:** Critical-downtime Service-contract.
*   **Mechanism:** Split `case_type` into "True Downtime" and "Precautionary Urgent". True downtime gets dedicated van/air; Precautionary gets shared emergency courier or expedited ground. Audit dedicated van usage to ensure justification event exists (fix SP2 violation).
*   **Effect:** Reduces empty leg CO2 and high-emission transport for non-critical cases. Protects service for true downtime.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `case_type` distribution, `route_mode` share, `transport_kgco2e` for Critical segment, and SP2 compliance.

#### S7. Partial Shipment Prevention via Availability Logic
*   **Target:** Partial shipment path (7% of cases, 24% late).
*   **Mechanism:** Update availability check to bundle items or delay shipment slightly to avoid partial shipments, unless `expedite_flag` is set. Partial shipments double transport emissions and increase late risk.
*   **Effect:** Reduces transport CO2 (fewer shipments), lowers late delivery rate, improves customer experience.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `Partial shipment` count, `transport_kgco2e`, and late delivery rate.

#### S8. Carbon-Aware Promise Date Negotiation
*   **Target:** Made-to-order Regular customers.
*   **Mechanism:** Offer negotiated lead times that align with green batching and consolidated shipping windows. Regular customers are more price/lead-time sensitive and may accept slightly longer dates for green options.
*   **Effect:** Enables efficient batching and transport without service penalty. Reduces emergency production and air freight.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `promised_lead_time_hours`, adherence to green paths, and customer acceptance rate.

### 5. Rebound and displacement risks

1.  **Batching Obsolescence Rebound:** Aggressive batching to save energy may increase inventory age. If demand forecasts are wrong, 6% of batch items become obsolete, wasting material and energy. *Mitigation:* Enforce batch caps and monitor obsolescence rate.
2.  **Transport Displacement to Air:** Consolidation or ground shipping may cause delays for Service-contract customers, triggering emergency air shipments or cancellations. *Mitigation:* Use priority picking for Service-contracts and dynamic rerouting.
3.  **Packaging Material Displacement:** Reinforced packaging reduces returns but doubles material. If applied to low-risk parts, material impact worsens. *Mitigation:* Use compatibility engine to apply reinforcement only when needed (Q1 enforcement).
4.  **Rework Rebound via Inspection:** Adding inspection gates to reduce rework may increase cycle time. If customers perceive delays, they may cancel or demand air. *Mitigation:* Optimize inspection_cell queues and use gates only for high-risk combos.
5.  **Service Contract Resource Strain:** Priority picking for Service-contracts to offset ground wait may strain internal resources, affecting Regular customers. *Mitigation:* Monitor resource utilization and balance priority rules.
6.  **Critical Downtime Misclassification:** Tiering may lead to "Precautionary" orders using shared lanes that are too slow, causing actual downtime. *Mitigation:* Strict classification criteria and automated alerts for true downtime.
7.  **CNC_A Bottleneck Displacement:** Migrating CNC_B to CNC_A may create queues, increasing batch waits and negating energy benefits. *Mitigation:* Monitor CNC_A queue length and allow CNC_B fallback for urgent cases.

For MTO, use dynamic batching with strict caps (max 20h wait for Service, 10h for Regular) to capture energy savings without late risk.
2.  **Quality & Machine Optimization:** Execute S3 and S5. Migrate MTO production to CNC_A to halve rework and scrap. Deploy the packaging compatibility engine to eliminate rework loops and optimize material use.
3.  **Critical Downtime Rationalization:** Apply S6. Split Critical cases and audit dedicated van usage. This addresses the highest transport CO2 segment while protecting true downtime.
4.  **Governance & Continuous Improvement:** Implement S12 (Sustainability Exception Log) and S8 (Carbon-Aware Promise) for Regular MTO customers to engage the market in sustainability.

**Why it balances:**
*   **Environmental:** Targets high-impact areas: air freight (S1/S4), batching energy (S2), scrap/rework (S3/S5), and critical transport (S6). Expected significant CO2 and scrap reduction.
*   **Service:** Priority picking and caps protect Service-contract service levels. Partial prevention (S7) improves delivery reliability.
*   **Quality:** CNC_A migration and inspection gates reduce rework and scrap, improving quality.
*   **Resilience:** SP1/SP2 compliance and alt-route monitoring enhance resilience. Caps prevent over-optimization that could cause systemic delays. Obsolescence monitoring prevents waste from batching.
*   **Risk Management:** Rebound risks are mitigated by caps, priority rules, and strict classification. The package avoids lowest-emission extremes (e.g., no unlimited batching, no air ban for true downtime).
