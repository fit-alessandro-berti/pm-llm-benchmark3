# 1. Assumptions and notation

**A1. Scope and clocks.** I reconstruct the 22 listed cases on May 12. I assume the extract covers the work assigned to the four named resources in this window and the two timestamp systems share a time basis. The reported-time path has no carry-in before 08:00. The first recorded arrival, at 08:00, could in fact have occurred just before the window. Unlisted work or a systematic clock offset would require a different reconstruction.

**A2. Time and ties.** Durations are in minutes. The primary reconstruction uses the reported clock. In continuous time I allow each recorded timestamp to move by approximately 2.5 minutes in either direction, subject to precedence and capacity. Each S1 completion and corresponding S2 entry are the same event. Queueing and service occupy half-open intervals. At each recorded time I apply all changes together and report the state immediately afterward. A handoff at 11:00 therefore takes no extra server slot. Rate bins include their left endpoint and exclude their right endpoint, with 14:00 included in the last bin.

**A3. Capacity.** Each visit has one nonpreemptive service interval on its named resource. S2 queueing begins immediately on S1 completion. I use two available servers at each station except during the scheduled C2 outage, treated as fixed at [10:55, 11:10). I allow idle gaps and overtaking. I assume neither first-come-first-served dispatch nor immediate dispatch to an idle server. Busy time measures time executing a listed activity.

**A4. Missing starts.** On the reported clock I bound each start by queue entry, freedom of the named resource, the calendar, and completion. I evaluate earliest, midpoint and latest feasible starts. The midpoint bisects the available interval and assumes no probability distribution. A start at completion is the limiting case of arbitrarily short positive service because there is no supplied minimum duration. The rounding stress tests in section 6 vary recorded endpoints as well.

**A5. Censoring.** The status at the fixed cutoff H = 14:00 is authoritative. An open activity contributes service from its start to H. A queued case contributes waiting time from entry to H. Neither has an observed final station sojourn. I assume no completion time, residual duration, or future dispatch order beyond H.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

For a station visit let a be queue entry, s service start and e service end. At S1, a is external arrival. At S2, a is the S1 end. For a completed visit, waiting time W = s − a, active service B = e − s, and station sojourn T = e − a = W + B. Total completed case sojourn is S2 end minus external arrival, also T₁ + T₂. If s is bounded by [l,u], the wait is in [l − a,u − a] and service in [e − u,e − l]. Increasing the imputed wait by a minute reduces service by the same minute.

Let A(t), J(t) and D(t) be cumulative station entries, starts and completions through t. Queue length Q(t) = A(t) − J(t), busy servers = J(t) − D(t), and station WIP N(t) = A(t) − D(t). System WIP is N₁ + N₂, equivalently external arrivals minus S2 completions. For each change time, multiply the state after the change by the minutes until the next change. Dividing the summed area by the interval length gives a time mean. The peak is the maximum of that step function. Starts move cases between queue and service without changing WIP.

Arrival rate and departure throughput are entry and completion counts divided by elapsed hours. Utilization is the sum of in-window service minutes, including partial service, divided by available server-minutes. The denominators are 720 at S1 and 360 + 345 = 705 at S2. Waiting-time means below use visits that started by H, including open services. Service-time and station-sojourn means use visits that finished that station by H. I report queue and WIP exposure for **all** entered visits.

**Little's-Law check.** For a queue with matched entrants and service starts in an interval of length h, empty of waiting cases at both boundaries, the finite-interval relation is mean Q = (entries/h) × mean wait. From 08:10 to 08:50 the S2 queue clears both P01 and P02, with no other entrants or boundary-crossing waits. The queue path has two five-minute plateaus at one, so mean Q = 10/40 = 0.25. The arrival rate is 2/40 per minute and the mean wait is 5 minutes, giving 0.25 independently. This is a check of queue exposure for a cleared cohort. For the six-hour window there is startup, changing inflow and unfinished work. The exact accounting is completed stay plus elapsed stay of unfinished visits. For example, S2 contributes 980 + 360 = 1,340 case-minutes. Using six-hour departure throughput times the completed mean stay alone would omit those 360 minutes.

**Observed pressure.** Inspection accumulated seven more entries than completions before 10:00. Its average queue approached six in the next two hours. Calibration also accumulated WIP in *every* two-hour bin. Its first-bin throughput includes the startup period before the first S2 entry at 08:25. **Maintenance.** C2 finished P06 at 10:50 and started P08 at 11:15. Fifteen minutes of this 25-minute gap have a known cause. The outage reduced scheduled capacity by 15 server-minutes while there was demand. A specific delay attribution to P08, or a number of lost completions, would require a counterfactual dispatch and service schedule.

**Interpretation.** The evidence supports a time-varying bottleneck. S1 was the main source of waiting during the arrival surge, and S2 increasingly constrained the clearance of released work. Calibration took longer per finished visit across all three missing-start scenarios, and its backlog persisted after C2 returned. Both stations had queues concurrently. Resource occupancy averaged over all six hours cannot establish a permanent capacity bottleneck. For example, P17 passed the still-waiting P16 at S2, and I2 had no listed service after 13:45 while P22 waited. Eligibility rules, setup time or delayed dispatch could explain such gaps, but the extract gives no reason to select one.

# 6. Missingness, censoring, and sensitivity analysis

For each missing start, the binding lower bound is the later of queue entry and release of the named resource, also respecting maintenance. The observed end gives the upper bound. The next known assignment on that resource starts after the end in all four cases.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The last column gives marginal outer bounds. For example, P11 could start as early as 11:57:30 if P08 ended at the early rounding edge. Its queue entry could be as late as 11:07:30, giving a 50-minute minimum wait. Release from maintenance at 11:10 alone is insufficient because P08 subsequently occupied C2. Maximum wait and maximum service cannot be realized together within a visit.

Earlier starts always lower Q and increase accumulated busy time. Hence the endpoint scenarios also bound mixed choices across cases. All three give the same station WIP paths and per-station queue peaks. Missing starts have a larger effect on the inferred service of individual visits. Even the **longest** nominal P11 service is 25 minutes, versus a mean of 40 among the 12 S2 completions with recorded starts. Applying that observed mean to P11 would overlap P08. Relative utilization of S1 and S2 is unresolved since their ranges overlap and missing starts at the two stations can vary independently.

**Rounding sensitivity.** A difference between two recorded times can change by up to about five minutes, subject to precedence. For elapsed time measured against the fixed H the uncertainty is about 2.5 minutes. For example, P17 has about 2.5–7.5 minutes of S2 service *already performed* and no finite upper bound on final service. A shared S1 finish affects both stations and must be shifted only once. In particular, the P07-to-P09 handoff on C1 admits coincident endpoints or a short gap under A3. A literal overlap from jittering both endpoints independently is infeasible.

Two coherent clock stress tests keep external arrivals fixed. Shorten recorded services at both ends and choose latest missing starts. This gives about **442.5 / 450 busy minutes** at S1 / S2, or **61.5% / 63.8%**. Lengthen recorded services at both ends and use earliest missing starts, keeping the shared C1 handoff at 11:00. This gives **697.5 / 630 busy minutes**, or **96.9% / 89.4%**. These deliberately aligned clock errors illustrate sensitivity, without assigning likelihood to the extremes. True peaks near ties, minute-specific queue membership and rates near bin boundaries are less certain than closing stocks. For instance, the rounded 12:00 S1 finish could belong to either adjacent bin.

**Stable conclusions and limits.** WIP depends only on entry and exit. Even allowing rounding, the station WIP areas can change by at most about 2.5 × (22 + 20) = 105 case-minutes at S1 and 2.5 × (20 + 14) = 85 at S2, using the same listed cases and clipping exposure to the window. Thus S1's larger whole-window mean WIP is robust. The observed closing split, early external surge and later transfer of backlog downstream also survive. Exact utilization rankings, a precise crossover minute, eventual means for all arrivals, and the effect of removing maintenance are unidentified. The eight censored cases have no supplied upper bound on remaining time.

# 7. Reproducible Python

The observed interval log contains 30 station visits with both endpoints recorded. The separate completed-case projection inserts a zero-duration external arrival so PM4Py's case sojourn includes the S1 queue. Its service, sojourn and waiting features are in seconds. Its arrival and finish features describe successive gaps in seconds within those 14 cases. Station arrival and departure rates above come from counting station events per hour.

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

cols = "case arrival_s1 s1_start s1_end s1_resource s2_start s2_end s2_resource state_at_14_00".split()
raw = """P01 08:00 08:05 08:25 I1 08:30 09:00 C1 COMPLETE
P02 08:05 08:10 08:35 I2 08:40 09:20 C2 COMPLETE
P03 08:10 08:30 08:55 I1 09:05 09:45 C1 COMPLETE
P04 08:20 08:40 09:10 I2 09:25 10:05 C2 COMPLETE
P05 08:25 09:00 09:25 I1 MISSING 10:15 C1 COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P06 08:35 09:15 09:45 I2 10:10 10:50 C2 COMPLETE
P07 08:45 MISSING 10:00 I1 10:20 11:00 C1 COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P08 09:00 09:50 10:20 I2 11:15 12:00 C2 COMPLETE
P09 09:05 10:05 10:35 I1 11:00 11:40 C1 COMPLETE
P10 09:15 10:25 10:55 I2 11:45 12:10 C1 COMPLETE
P11 09:25 10:40 11:05 I1 MISSING 12:25 C2 COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P12 09:35 11:00 11:30 I2 12:15 13:00 C1 COMPLETE
P13 09:50 11:10 11:40 I1 12:30 13:20 C2 COMPLETE
P14 10:00 11:35 12:00 I2 13:05 13:50 C1 COMPLETE
P15 10:10 MISSING 12:15 I1 13:30 OPEN C2 OPEN_IN_S2
P16 10:25 12:05 12:35 I2 - - - WAITING_FOR_S2
P17 10:45 12:20 12:50 I1 13:55 OPEN C1 OPEN_IN_S2
P18 11:00 12:40 13:10 I2 - - - WAITING_FOR_S2
P19 11:15 12:55 13:25 I1 - - - WAITING_FOR_S2
P20 11:30 13:15 13:45 I2 - - - WAITING_FOR_S2
P21 11:50 13:30 OPEN I1 - - - OPEN_IN_S1
P22 12:10 - - - - - - WAITING_FOR_S1"""
wide = pd.DataFrame([r.split() for r in raw.splitlines()], columns=cols).replace("-", "")
def stamp(hm):
    return pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 " + hm)
L, H = stamp("08:00"), stamp("14:00")
minute = pd.Timedelta(minutes=1)
def time_col(col):
    return pd.to_datetime("2026-05-12 " + col, format="%Y-%m-%d %H:%M", errors="coerce")

parts = []
for st, prefix, entry in [("S1", "s1_", "arrival_s1"), ("S2", "s2_", "s1_end")]:
    s, e = wide[prefix+"start"], wide[prefix+"end"]
    parts.append(pd.DataFrame(dict(case=wide.case, station=st, a=time_col(wide[entry]),
        s=time_col(s), e=time_col(e), res=wide[prefix+"resource"],
        start_status=s.replace({"": "unstarted"}).where(s.isin(["", "MISSING"]), "recorded"),
        end_status=e.replace({"": "unstarted"}).where(e.isin(["", "OPEN"]), "recorded"))))
# A row first becomes a station visit when its entry is observed.
v = pd.concat(parts, ignore_index=True).dropna(subset=["a"]).copy()
def bounds(base):
    v = base.copy()
    v["lo"], v["hi"] = v["s"], v["s"]
    for i, row in v[v.start_status.eq("MISSING")].iterrows():
        same = v[v.res.eq(row.res)]
        prior = same.loc[same.e.lt(row.e), "e"]
        lower = max([row.a] + prior.tolist())
        if row.res == "C2" and row.e > stamp("11:10"):
            lower = max(lower, stamp("11:10"))
        following = same.loc[same.e.fillna(H).gt(row.e), "s"].dropna()
        assert lower <= row.e and (following.empty or row.e <= following.min())
        v.loc[i, ["lo", "hi"]] = [lower, row.e]
    return v

def reconstruct(fraction, base=v):
    d = bounds(base)
    d["s"] = d.lo + fraction * (d.hi - d.lo)
    d["wait"] = (d.s - d.a) / minute                 # admitted to service
    d["service"] = (d.e - d.s) / minute              # completed service
    d["stay"] = (d.e - d.a) / minute                 # completed station visit
    d["wait_obs"] = (d.s.fillna(H) - d.a) / minute
    d["busy"] = ((d.e.fillna(H) - d.s) / minute).fillna(0)
    return d

def path(d):
    # +1 at the left, -1 at the right of every half-open interval.
    # Group equal times before cumulatively summing.
    changes = pd.concat([
        pd.DataFrame({"t": d.a, "q": 1, "n": 1}),
        pd.DataFrame({"t": d.s.dropna(), "q": -1, "n": 0}),
        pd.DataFrame({"t": d.e.dropna(), "q": 0, "n": -1}),
        pd.DataFrame({"t": [L, H], "q": 0, "n": 0})])
    p = changes.groupby("t")[["q", "n"]].sum().sort_index().cumsum()
    p["dt"] = -p.index.to_series().diff(-1).fillna(pd.Timedelta(0)) / minute
    return p

summaries, resources = [], []
for scenario, f in [("early", 0.), ("middle", .5), ("late", 1.)]:
    d = reconstruct(f)
    assert ((d.wait.dropna() >= 0).all() and (d.service.dropna() >= 0).all())
    for st, g in d.groupby("station"):
        p = path(g)
        T = (H-L)/minute
        assert abs((p.q*p.dt).sum() - g.wait_obs.sum()) < 1e-8
        assert abs((p.n*p.dt).sum() - g.wait_obs.sum() - g.busy.sum()) < 1e-8
        available = 2*T - (15 if st == "S2" else 0)
        summaries.append([scenario, st, len(g), g.s.count(), g.e.count(), g.wait.mean(),
            g.service.mean(), g.stay.mean(), (p.q*p.dt).sum()/T, p.q.max(),
            (p.n*p.dt).sum()/T, p.n.max(), g.busy.sum(), 100*g.busy.sum()/available])
    for res, total in d[d.s.notna()].groupby("res").busy.sum().items():
        resources.append([scenario, res, total, 100*total/(345 if res == "C2" else 360)])
print(pd.DataFrame(summaries, columns=("scenario station entries starts ends mean_wait "
    "mean_service mean_stay mean_Q peak_Q mean_N peak_N busy_min util_pct").split())
    .round(2).to_string(index=False))
print(pd.DataFrame(resources, columns=["scenario", "resource", "busy_min", "util_pct"])
    .round(1).to_string(index=False))

mid = reconstruct(.5)
periods = []
for st, g in mid.groupby("station"):
    p = path(g)
    for left, right in [("08:00","10:00"), ("10:00","12:00"), ("12:00","14:00")]:
        l, r = stamp(left), stamp(right)
        def count(col):
            return int((col.ge(l) & (col.le(r) if r == H else col.lt(r))).sum())
        cuts = p.index.union(pd.DatetimeIndex([l,r])).sort_values()
        segment = p[["q","n"]].reindex(cuts).ffill().loc[l:r]
        dt = -segment.index.to_series().diff(-1).fillna(pd.Timedelta(0))/minute
        periods.append([st, left, count(g.a)/2, count(g.e)/2,
                        (segment.q*dt).sum()/120, (segment.n*dt).sum()/120])
print(pd.DataFrame(periods, columns=["station","from","in/h","out/h","avg_Q","avg_N"])
    .round(2).to_string(index=False))
paths = pd.concat({st:path(g)[["q","n"]] for st,g in mid.groupby("station")}, axis=1, sort=True)
hourly = pd.date_range(L, H, freq="h")
paths = paths.reindex(paths.index.union(hourly)).sort_index().ffill().astype(int)
paths["system", "n"] = paths["S1", "n"] + paths["S2", "n"]
print(paths.loc[hourly])
cohort = wide[wide.state_at_14_00.str.startswith("COMPLETE")]
total = (time_col(cohort.s2_end) - time_col(cohort.arrival_s1))/minute
print("Completed total stays (min):", dict(zip(cohort.case, total)))
print("System peak WIP:", paths["system", "n"].max())

# Coherent clock stress tests. Keep each S1 finish identical to its S2 entry.
# +/-2.5 represents the limiting edge of the rounding bin.
for name, shift, fraction in [("short", -2.5, 1.), ("long", 2.5, 0.)]:
    edge = v.copy()
    edge["s"] = edge.s - shift*minute
    edge["e"] = edge.e + shift*minute
    if name == "long":
        t = stamp("11:00")
        edge.loc[edge.case.eq("P07") & edge.station.eq("S2"), "e"] = t
        edge.loc[edge.case.eq("P09") & edge.station.eq("S2"), "s"] = t
    s1_end = edge[edge.station.eq("S1")].set_index("case").e
    s2 = edge.station.eq("S2")
    edge.loc[s2, "a"] = edge.loc[s2, "case"].map(s1_end)
    x = reconstruct(fraction, edge)
    assert x.wait.dropna().ge(0).all() and x.service.dropna().ge(0).all()
    for res, g in x[x.s.notna()].groupby("res"):
        g = g.sort_values(["s", "e"])
        assert (g.s.iloc[1:].to_numpy() >= g.e.iloc[:-1].to_numpy()).all()
        if res == "C2":
            assert (g.e.le(stamp("10:55")) | g.s.ge(stamp("11:10"))).all()
    print(name, "busy minutes:", x.groupby("station").busy.sum().to_dict())

# Finite-interval queue check with empty waiting rooms at both boundaries.
l, r = stamp("08:10"), stamp("08:50")
g = mid[mid.station.eq("S2")]
p = path(g)
cuts = p.index.union(pd.DatetimeIndex([l,r])).sort_values()
q = p.q.reindex(cuts).ffill().loc[l:r]
dt = -q.index.to_series().diff(-1).fillna(pd.Timedelta(0))/minute
entrants = g[g.a.ge(l) & g.a.lt(r)]
duration = (r-l)/minute
assert q.iloc[0] == q.iloc[-1] == 0 and entrants.s.lt(r).all()
print("S2 cleared queue: mean Q, arrival rate per min x mean W:",
      (q*dt).sum()/duration, len(entrants)/duration*entrants.wait.mean())

# Interval log of all observed start/end pairs. Censored visits remain in mid above.
keys = {"case":"case:concept:name", "station":"concept:name", "s":"start_timestamp",
        "e":"time:timestamp", "res":"org:resource"}
def intervals(rows):
    return rows[list(keys)].rename(columns=keys).copy()
observed = pm4py.format_dataframe(intervals(v.dropna(subset=["s","e"])))
print("Fully observed station intervals:", len(observed))

# Completed CASE projection, with midpoint-imputed starts and a zero-duration
# external arrival marker. Each case now spans external arrival to S2 completion.
ids = wide.loc[wide.state_at_14_00.str.startswith("COMPLETE"), "case"]
visits = mid[mid.case.isin(ids)]
arr = visits[visits.station.eq("S1")].copy()
arr["s"], arr["e"], arr["station"], arr["res"] = arr.a, arr.a, "Arrival", "external"

features = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(log, start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")
features = pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate(features, start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")
```
