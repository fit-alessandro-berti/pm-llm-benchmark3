## 6. Intervention and learning plan
| phase | intervention | target mechanism | assignment unit | safeguards | process-mining measurements | causal learning value | stop or rollback rule |
|-------|--------------|------------------|-----------------|------------|-----------------------------|-----------------------|-----------------------|
| 1 | Quasi-random routing pilot (10% of Orion cases rerouted to Nova post-Classify) | Isolate routing policy effect from case mix | Case-level after Classify | Cap volume; maintain Nova day-shift capacity; monitor compliance bandwidth | Cycle time, wait/service decomposition, rework rate, 30d quality | A/B comparison within complexity strata; estimates direct effect of group assignment | Rollback if Nova wait increases >20% or appeal rate rises >5% |

**Final Recommendation:** Do not reassign 50% of Orion’s cases to Nova. The crude cycle-time difference is a confounding artifact driven by routing policy, night-shift coverage, and compliance load, not a causal penalty. Instead, implement a small randomized routing pilot to isolate the group effect, simultaneously expand compliance and queue capacity to address the primary bottleneck, and monitor night-shift coverage to prevent operational disruption. This phased approach learns the true causal mechanisms while limiting risk, ensuring that any volume transfer is evidence-based and operationally sustainable.

## 8. Residual uncertainty
1. **Unobserved case risk/complexity nuance** – Add `risk_score` or `claim_amount` to adjust for residual heterogeneity within complexity bins.
2. **Queue state dynamics and timing** – Add `timestamp` for each process step to reconstruct queue depth and isolate service vs wait accurately.
3. **Reviewer skill heterogeneity** – Add `reviewer_id` and certification/tenure to control for unobserved skill differences within groups.
4. **Selection bias from incomplete cases** – Add `status` (open/abandoned) and `exit_code` to model completion probability and apply inverse weighting.
5. **Compliance wait vs general queue wait** – Add `compliance_wait_h` as a separate field to disentangle compliance-mediated delay from classification queue congestion.
