**4. Pretrends and regression to the mean.** Prewindow medians: Alpha flathigh (50.4, 46.9, 49.4), Beta gently rising (45.446.4), Gamma steeply worsening (36.445.9, +26% in six months) *before* treatment — a textbook RTM setup, since Gamma was admitted after a pilot and Alpha/Beta were chosen for weakness. Against pure RTM: (a) DiD differences out branchspecific levels; (b) Alpha/Beta absorbed +23% arrivals and +29–19% load per FTE while holding medians  flat, which RTM alone cannot produce; (c) Gamma, *not* selected on poor performance, shows the largest drop — but its prepeak embodies the May product change and a June FTE loss whose natural reversion is visible in untreated Epsilon (8.6h median, JuneAugust, dose constant at 4). Verdict: RTM is implausible as the whole story for Alpha/Beta (their "post" sits at pre levels, not below transient lows), but for Gamma the Juneanchored contrast is largely shockreversion plus demand easing; the honest premeananchored contrast is only 3.4h.

**5. Sensitivity / bounded conclusions (separating arithmetic from causal interpretation).**
- *Median cycle (mature).* Arithmetic anchors: adopter  vs control  (S1, controldrift counterfactual) gives Alpha 6.9h, Beta 2.8h, Gamma 3.9h; a "nodrift" counterfactual (S2) gives 3.3, +0.9, 3.4h; Gamma's shockreversion upper bound approaches +1h. *Bounded conclusion*: program effect on medians of roughly **2h to 7h ( 4% to 13%) for Alpha and Gamma; statistically indistinguishable from zero for Beta**.
- *p90 cycle.* Arithmetic: absolute deterioration +7 to +10h (adopters) and +15 to +17h (controls) over comparable windows. *Bounded conclusion*: effect vs. counterfactual lies between about 9h and +4h — i.e., no demonstrated improvement, plausibly meaningful cushioning.
- *Escalation.* Three specifications (withinbranch step +0.8/+1.0/+1.0pp; fullwindow DiD +0.95pp; controls' zero steps) agree: **persistent +0.8–1.2pp**, essentially mechanical.
- *CSAT.* DiD  +0.09 in two window alignments, but with unknown response composition the defensible bound is ±0.15  **indistinguishable from zero**.

## 8. Rollout recommendation

`expand with an embedded evaluation`

Leadership may expand, but only through a designed phasein that turns the next rollout into the identification strategy. **Sites and assignment.** Enter both Delta and Epsilon; stratified coin toss assigns one to go live 20261001 and the other 20270101. Epsilon is the highervalue first mover (heaviest case mix, most strained p90, weakest CSAT); Delta is the safer pilot. Randomizing the order converts managerial horsetrading into designbased identification, and while one branch runs, the other is a genuine concurrent control — prohibit routine crossbranch routing changes to the control wave and log any exception. **Measurement minimums.** At least 6 months of instrumented preperiod (Sep 2026–Feb 2027) with the new pipeline live but triage automation disabled, and at least 6 postmonths: observed adopters needed ~5 months to reach a dose plateau, so judge nothing before month 6 and only once fastpath share has been 30% for 3 consecutive months. **Processmining outcomes and queue state.** Collect complete percase event logs (timestamps and resources for Register  Triage  Review  optional rework loops  Resolve  Close), variant frequencies, loop mining on rework triggers, triagesuggestion overrides with misroute audit samples, and perhandover queue records for specialist review and the central fraud desk tagged by branch origin; add daily queuestate snapshots (WIP by stage, arrivals, service rates, ageing buckets), and branchmonth survey n and response rate. Maintain a changelog of every local practice edit. **Spillover monitoring.** Record frauddesk waiting times per branch before and after each golive; dashboard controlwave arrivals and medians with an alert if they shift >5% concurrently with the partner's adoption. **Guardrails.** Automatic percase fallback to manual triage if misroute audits exceed 5%; p90 alert at +15% over the branch's own baseline for 2 consecutive months; CSAT 0.2 for 2 months; rework +3pp; escalation +4pp; weekly incident review. **Decision rule (preregistered).** Scale to remaining sites when, at maturity, the intenttotreat median improvement is 4% versus matched control with randomizationinference p<0.10, all guardrails clear, rework noninferior (+0.5pp), and plateau reached without emergency overrides; otherwise extend one quarter or redesign (classifier quality/specialist capacity) before wider rollout.

```python
# ------------------ 4) RELATIVE-TIME INDICATORS (k bins) --------------
conds = [df["rel_time"].le(-3), df["rel_time"].eq(-2), df["rel_time"].eq(-1),
         df["rel_time"].eq(0), df["rel_time"].eq(1), df["rel_time"].eq(2),
         df["rel_time"].ge(3)]
ch = ["k_pre3up", "k_pre2", "k_ref_minus1", "k_launch", "k_plus1",
      "k_plus2", "k_plus3up"]
df["rel_bin"] = np.select(conds, ch, default="ctrl_never")
D = pd.get_dummies(df["rel_bin"], dtype=float)
k_cols = [c for c in D.columns if c.startswith("k_") and c != "k_ref_minus1"]
print("\n[RELTIME] event-time bin counts:\n", df["rel_bin"].value_counts())
print("[NOTE] k>=2 support comes ONLY from the 2026-04 cohort; pre<-2 support "
      "ONLY from Gamma; never-treated branches are absorbed by branch FEs.")

# --------------------- 5) MODELS --------------------------------------
df["log_med"] = np.log(df["median_cycle_h"])
df["log_p90"] = np.log(df["p90_cycle_h"])
df["log_arr"] = np.log(df["arrivals"])
base = df[df.automation == 0].groupby("branch")["automated_fast_path_pct"].mean()
df["afp_base"] = df["branch"].map(base).fillna(0.0)
df["afp_c"] = df["automated_fast_path_pct"] - df["afp_base"]   # centering handles the 4-6% floor artifact
clu = pd.factorize(df["branch"])[0]

try:
    import statsmodels.formula.api as smf

    # MODEL 1 -- DESCRIPTIVE static two-way FE ("TWFE-like").
    # COMMENT: assumes a CONSTANT effect; with staggered timing and dynamic
    # learning this uses already-treated spells as controls (forbidden-
    # comparison problem). Reported as an ASSOCIATION, not a causal effect.
    m1 = smf.ols("log_med ~ C(branch) + C(cm) + automation", df)\
             .fit(cov_type="cluster", cov_kwds={"groups": clu})
    print("\n[MODEL 1 :: DESCRIPTIVE static TWFE] log(median) on automation:")
    print(m1.params[["automation"]].round(4).to_string(),
          "| 95% CI:", m1.conf_int().loc["automation"].round(4).tolist())

    # MODEL 2 -- DESCRIPTIVE stacked-cohort EVENT-STUDY on relative time.
    # COMMENT: dynamic ATT(k)-flavored descriptive coefficients; unbalanced
    # event-time support (see NOTE above); still descriptive, not causal.
    f2 = "log_med ~ " + " + ".join(k_cols) + " + C(branch) + C(cm)"
    m2 = smf.ols(f2, df).fit(cov_type="cluster", cov_kwds={"groups": clu})
    print("\n[MODEL 2 :: DESCRIPTIVE event-study bins] log(median):")
    print(m2.params[k_cols].round(4).to_string())

    # MODEL 3 -- TREATMENT-INTENSITY (dose) model.
    # COMMENT: identification rides on within-adopter dose swings being
    # conditionally exogenous (assumption A5) -- doubtful. Association only.
    for y in ["log_med", "log_p90", "csat_1to5"]:
        m3 = smf.ols(y + " ~ afp_c + log_arr + fte + high_complexity_pct"
                     " + C(branch) + C(cm)", df)\
                 .fit(cov_type="cluster", cov_kwds={"groups": clu})
        ci = m3.conf_int().loc["afp_c"].round(4).tolist()
        print(f"\n[MODEL 3 :: INTENSITY] {y} on centered dose afp_c:",
              f"coef={m3.params['afp_c']:.4f}, 95% CI={ci}")
except ImportError:
    print("[WARNING] statsmodels unavailable -- models skipped, "
          "contrasts/tables above are complete.")

# ------------- 6/7) CLUSTERING LIMITATIONS & PRINTED WARNINGS ---------
print("""
==================== SMALL-SAMPLE & MEASUREMENT WARNINGS ====================
[WARNING] Only 5 clusters (3 adopters, 2 controls). Cluster-robust SEs are
          severely anti-conservative at this n. Use randomization inference
          (permute adoption schedules across branches) or a wild cluster
          bootstrap; do NOT trust the printed CIs for decisions.
[WARNING] Outcomes are branch-month AGGREGATES (medians/p90), not case-level
          quantiles: quantile treatment effects are unidentifiable, and
          median and p90 must never be collapsed into one 'cycle-time effect'.
[WARNING] rework_pct/escalation_pct appear step-coded; effective information
          << nominal observations.
[WARNING] Co-occurring events (Mar fraud desk, Apr routing campaign, May
          product change, FTE shifts) are only partly removable by month
          FE; branch-specific exposure to them remains confounded.
============================================================================
""")
```

## 10. Final claim ladder

- **Observed:** Once the system was available, actual fast-path use ramped from ~25% at golive to 46–50% within five months (Alpha/Beta; 2532% for Gamma); escalations stepped up ~0.8–1.0pp at each golive; adopters carried 19–37% more cases per productive FTE; and by August adopter medians sat at or below their Jan–Mar averages while every branch's p90 remained above prelevels.
- **Suggestive:** Relative to nevertreated branches over matched calendar windows, adopters' medians drifted ~4–6h lower at maturity and their p90 rose ~6–10h less, with no durable rework penalty and CSAT unmoved — a pattern consistent with genuine capacity absorption, though demand normalization and the shared product change could generate similar signatures.
- **Conditionally causal:** *If* conditional parallel trends (net of the frauddesk era, routing campaign, and May product change) and negligible net spillovers are granted, branchlevel availability of automation caused matureperiod median cycletime reductions of roughly 3–10% (2–6h; clear for Alpha and Gamma, ~zero for Beta) and shielded tails without improving them absolutely — a cohort and horizonspecific statement, with no defensible single "X%".
- **Not supported:** One universal effect size; any absolute tail (`p90`) improvement; any durable rework reduction; any customersatisfaction change in either direction; toolonly (algorithmic) effects separated from the bundled routing/staffing package; and extrapolation of Alpha/Beta/Gamma experience to coldstart Delta or Epsilon performance.
