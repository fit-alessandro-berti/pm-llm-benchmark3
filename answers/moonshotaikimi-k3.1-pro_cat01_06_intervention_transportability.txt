## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Intervention: gated, capacity-controlled verification orchestration.**

- **Initial eligibility:** registration complete; documents digitally complete; no statutory originals required; identity and document inputs confirmed independent; no known public-sector/high-value legal requirement; low/medium complexity; no active supplement loop. If originals status or completeness is uncertain, use the originals-safe path. The initial eligible share is unknown and bounded above by 38%.
- **Digital path:** after Register, Identity Check and Document Check may be created and actually started concurrently, using separate role owners. Simultaneous creation without executable capacity is not counted as treatment fidelity.
- **Originals path:** Identity Check and Originals Receipt may proceed concurrently after Register. Originals Receipt should be a mailroom/coordinator milestone rather than a document reviewer’s active task. Document Check remains dormant or uncreated until required originals are received and validated. It may then overlap an unfinished Identity Check.
- **Mandatory dependencies:** no document execution before legally required originals; no Assessment before both checks are complete; no bypass of Legal Review; Notify only after a final Approve/Reject decision.
- **Queue and WIP controls:** begin with at most two open executable verification cases per reviewer, rather than copying A’s six-task setting. Use one executing task per reviewer, age/complexity-aware FIFO, and no pilot-case priority over urgent non-pilot work. Pending originals are tracked separately. Pause new parallel starts when the assessment-ready queue exceeds one day of measured assessor capacity or its p90 age breaches the pilot threshold.
- **Resources:** the 4 identity, 7 document, 3 assessment, and 2 legal reviewers remain in their roles. No implicit cross-cover, extra staffing, new huddles, or API changes are bundled with the ordering intervention.
- **Legal/supplement loops:** after Assessment, route mandatory cases to Legal Review. A Request Supplement freezes the final decision and creates a versioned supplement path. Any required supplemental originals must arrive before Document Check repeats; Assessment and Legal Review then repeat as required. Identity Check repeats only when legally or factually necessary.
- **IT and logging:** supported concurrent ownership is required for the full digital path; informal lock workarounds are not acceptable. Log task creation, eligibility, executable time, actual start/end, owner, queue entry/exit, receipt milestones, legal rationale, supplement versions, lock overrides, event time, and entry time.

A compact partial-order specification is:

```text

A = Assessment; L = Legal Review; Q = Request Supplement; N = Notify
G = tau for a digitally complete/no-originals case, otherwise O

Decision = X(Approve, Reject)

G < D

I and G are unordered; on the digital path, I and D may execute concurrently.

Z = X(
      Decision,                                        # no legal review required
      L . (Q . Gs . D2 . A2 . L)* . Decision          # legal branch + supplements
    )

D2 and A2 are controlled repetitions of Document Check and Assessment.
```

Assignment is not evidence of overlap. **Implementation failure** is indicated when T does not produce lawful, contemporaneously logged overlap because of locking, unavailable reviewers, pending originals, or ownership workarounds. The response is repair and rerun, not a conclusion that ordering cannot work.

**Mechanism failure** is indicated when fidelity targets are met, staffing and queue policies remain comparable, actual overlap increases substantially, yet Register-to-verification-complete does not shorten. If that stage shortens but cycle time does not, the verification mechanism works while transport fails downstream. If medians improve while assessment queues or p90 worsen, the result is bottleneck displacement, not successful transport.

Because C receives the same IT, logging, WIP, and queue-management conditions, TC avoids A’s mixture of API, staffing, mix, and huddle effects. Randomized intention-to-treat remains the causal estimate; high-fidelity and per-protocol analyses diagnose implementation. A supplies only weak priors for scenario ranges, not the pilot’s transport estimand.

    return OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=list(children))

    """Build a POWL strict partial order and explicitly add its order edges."""
    model = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)
    for source, target in edges:
        model.order.add_edge(source, target)
    return model

## 9. Recommendation

conditional go

Transfer the principle that legally independent verification activities may overlap, together with explicit synchronization, capacity control, and fidelity logging. Do not copy simultaneous active Document Check creation for every case, Site A’s six-task WIP setting, or its raw nine-hour median and eighteen-hour p90 improvements. Those outcomes combine changing case mix, additional reviewers, API latency, huddles, staffing, and possible timestamp changes.

The first entrants should be digitally complete, non-original, non-public-sector, non-high-value, low/medium-complexity claims with independent verification inputs. Their share is unknown and cannot exceed the 38% that do not require originals. Under the digital-only scenario, a reasonable planning expectation is a 2–4-hour improvement on treated verification paths, but only approximately 0–2 hours in site-wide median cycle time. The p90 may remain unchanged or worsen if assessment, document, or legal queues absorb the benefit.

Expansion should require zero statutory or legal-review bypasses, supported concurrent ownership, contemporaneous timestamps, a material increase in actual overlap, and a randomized improvement in Register-to-verification-complete time. Cycle-time and assessment-queue p90 must not materially deteriorate, non-pilot cases must not be harmed, and rework/appeal monitoring must show no persuasive adverse signal. Expansion should then proceed to additional digital complexity strata, followed by originals-safe identity/receipt overlap. Legal-review cases should enter only after supplement loops and legal capacity have been tested.

If verification time improves but downstream queues or p90 worsen, modify the intervention through release throttling, lower WIP, or separately justified downstream capacity changes. If high-fidelity overlap produces no direct stage benefit, the critical-path rationale is unsupported. Persistent lock failure, inability to enforce document prerequisites, repeated quality harm, or tail deterioration despite throttling should trigger abandonment rather than broader rollout.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **A non-trivial set of digitally complete, non-original cases can lawfully start both checks immediately with independent inputs** — needed for full identity/document parallelism and the `e  38%` scenarios — audit registration documents, legal prerequisites, and input dependencies — if false, the eligible share and expected benefit are smaller, and only originals-safe overlap may remain.

2. **Sequential verification is on the critical path for enough eligible cases, and the service metrics are sufficiently comparable for the 3.4-hour envelope** — needed to expect overlap to reduce calendar time — reconstruct case-level critical paths and separate touch time, elapsed service, and waiting — if false, true overlap can increase without meaningful cycle-time improvement.

3. **At least part of A’s raw six-hour waiting reduction is plausibly attributable to ordering** — needed only to use A as a weak quantitative prior — compare A variants, queue histories, staffing/API periods, and any credible negative control; test the mechanism directly at B — if false, the scenario ranges are too optimistic, although B could still have an independent benefit.

4. **B can implement supported concurrent ownership and reliable contemporaneous logging** — needed for both treatment fidelity and measurement — test the remediated workflow with ownership audits, event-time/entry-time comparisons, and direct observation — if false, measured overlap is untrustworthy and full digital parallelization should not proceed.

5. **Specialist and downstream pools have sufficient slack, or release throttling can prevent harmful congestion** — needed to preserve gains and protect the tail — measure utilization, touch time, queue length, and capacity by role; stress-test WIP policies — if false, benefits will be offset or displaced into document, assessment, or legal queues.

6. **Originals and legal requirements are identifiable in time to enforce routing, and the supplement-loop specification covers material variants** — needed for statutory compliance and workload modeling — audit legal rules against discovered variants and supplement histories — if false, uncertain cases must default to safer routing and the structural model requires revision.

7. **Team-week assignment and operational controls adequately limit interference, while staffing and other management practices remain stable** — needed to interpret randomized contrasts — monitor reviewer sharing, control/non-pilot queues, arm contamination, and concurrent changes — if false, use higher-level phased assignment or treat results as total operational effects rather than isolated case effects.

8. **Case-level support and follow-up will be sufficient for distributional, heterogeneity, and quality conclusions** — needed to move from a feasibility pilot to transport decisions — calculate cluster-aware power, tail support, stratum coverage, and 90-day outcome completeness — if false, report only mechanism/fidelity findings and do not standardize or extrapolate to site-wide medians, p90s, or appeals.
