# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Assumptions

The following assumptions are needed because the prompt provides expected arrivals and service-time ranges rather than an event log or fully specified stochastic model.

1. **Arrival process — modeling assumption.** Arrivals are Poisson within each two-hour block and uniformly distributed inside the block. The given expected arrivals are not treated as exact counts.
2. **Priority/type dependence — modeling assumption.** Priority and case type are sampled independently within a block because only marginal percentages are supplied. Positive correlation between P1 and multi-branch or complex cases would reduce P1 attainment.
3. **Service-time distribution — modeling assumption.** Conditional on ordinary versus complex-tail status, service is uniform over the stated range. Tail indicators are independent by activity and case. The resulting pre-multiplier means are:

   | Activity | Uniform-mixture mean, min | Distribution-free mean bound from supplied ranges, min |
   |---|---:|---:|
   | Intake | 6.94 | 5.40–8.48 |
   | Mechanical | 26.30 | 20.55–32.05 |
   | Electrical | 32.82 | 25.24–40.40 |
   | Software | 38.50 | 29.00–48.00 |
   | Validation | 15.74 | 11.44–20.04 |

   For example, Mechanical has mean \(0.85(23)+0.15(45)=26.3\) under uniform draws. Resource multipliers scale both ordinary and tail draws.

4. **Calendar — modeling assumption.** Breaks are unavailable time. Up to 30 minutes immediately after each shift may be used as overtime. SLA clocks continue during breaks, off-shift periods, outages, handovers, and overnight waits.
5. **Carryover — modeling assumption.** A single arrival day may be cleared on subsequent days using the same calendars, but no further arrivals are added during clearance. Chen is absent on the arrival day in U3/U5 and returns on a later clearance day. This produces finite-horizon estimates, not a steady-state queue.
6. **Overhead semantics — modeling assumption.**
   - A 7-minute handover and the additional 5-minute cross-team transfer are elapsed delays but do not occupy a resource.
   - A 5-minute skill-family setup occupies the receiving resource.
   - The 8-minute parallel coordination period is elapsed delay and does not occupy a diagnostic resource.
   - If these delays actually require staffed meetings, the capacity estimates below are optimistic.
7. **Parallelism.** Parallel branches may start at different times. Validation becomes eligible after both finish and coordination is completed. Sequential ME/ES is assigned to one common-qualified resource, avoids the 8-minute coordination delay, and preserves continuity.
8. **Preemption.** Only a waiting P1 diagnostic task can interrupt a lower-priority diagnostic task. The victim case may be interrupted once; 10% of its remaining active work is added. Preemption is rejected if the expected P1 benefit does not exceed the restart, setup, and transition burden.
9. **U2 interpretation.** Total arrivals remain fixed and E and ES type weights are doubled before renormalization. A literal interpretation in which E/ES counts double without fixed totals would add approximately seven cases and about 467 raw minutes during 12:00–16:00; that materially harsher interpretation should be included as a sensitivity test.
10. **Utilization and fairness.** Resource utilization is active service plus setup divided by regular available minutes excluding breaks. Overtime can therefore produce utilization over 100%. Fairness is represented by utilization coefficient of variation, supplemented by the 90% individual-utilization rule.

## Decision variables

Operational decisions are:

- resource eligibility and assignment \(x_{case,activity,resource,time}\);
- queue priority and aging score;
- primary versus overflow resource use;
- amount and location of P1-reserved capacity;
- parallel versus sequential mode for ME/ES;
- whether and whom to preempt;
- continuation versus handover;
- use of overtime, subject to 30 minutes per resource;
- activation of normal versus stress dispatch mode.

## Objective formulations

### Objective A: SLA- and tail-sensitive performance

A suitable lexicographic objective is:

1. minimize P1 SLA shortfall;
2. minimize P2 SLA shortfall;
3. minimize weighted CVaR or p95 elapsed completion time;
4. minimize mean elapsed completion time and overhead.

A scalar version is:

\[
J_A(\pi)=
8(0.90-A_{P1})_+
+3(0.90-A_{P2})_+
+(0.90-A_{P3})_+
+0.02\,CVaR_{0.90}(T/60)
+0.005\,E[T/60],
\]

where \(A_p\) is attainment for priority \(p\), \(T\) is arrival-to-Validation completion, and \(\pi\) is the policy. The high P1 weight reflects its short target and the irreversibility of overnight failure.

### Objective B: robust and fair operation

For scenarios \(\mathcal S=\{\text{Base},U1,\ldots,U5\}\):

\[
J_B(\pi)=
\max_{s\in\mathcal S}
\left[
8(0.90-A_{P1,s})_+
+3(0.90-A_{P2,s})_+
+CVaR_{0.90}(T_s/120)
\right]
+\lambda_1\max_r(U_r-0.90)_+
+\lambda_2CV_r(U_r).
\]

This objective penalizes worst-scenario degradation, concentration of work on one employee, and utilization above the fairness threshold. Under the center-of-range service assumption, no routing policy can clear the expected base workload within regular capacity, so fairness, SLA performance, and same-day clearance cannot all be achieved simultaneously.

## Parallel versus sequential branches

Ignoring queues:

- ME sequential on Deepa is approximately \(1.25(26.3)+1.0(32.82)=65.7\) minutes.
- Parallel ME on fast branch resources is approximately \(\max(26.3,32.82)+8+5=45.8\) minutes, before ordinary handovers.
- ES sequential is about 81 minutes on either Chen or Emil.
- Parallel ES is approximately \(\max(32.82,38.5)+8=46.5\) minutes.

Parallelism can therefore save roughly 20–35 critical-path minutes for a P1 case. It does not reduce labor demand and can worsen queues by occupying two scarce resources concurrently. The auditable decision test should compare projected completion times:

\[
\max(q_1+d_1,q_2+d_2)+8+\text{cross-team cost}
<
q_c+d_{1,c}+d_{2,c},
\]

where \(q\) is projected resource availability and \(c\) is a common-qualified sequential resource. P1 usually favors parallelism; old P2 may; P3 generally favors sequential continuity during congestion.

# 3. Candidate policies

## Policy S: strict specialization with fixed queues

**Eligibility**

- Intake/Validation home pool: Ben, Farah, then Emil.
- Mechanical: Ada, Ben, Deepa.
- Electrical: Chen, Deepa.
- Software: Chen, Emil.
- Farah is not routine diagnostic overflow; Ada and Emil do not cross into Electrical.
- Sequential ME is retained by Deepa; sequential ES by Chen.

**Dispatch score**

Within each fixed queue: priority class, then FCFS. A P3 aging rule should apply after four hours to avoid indefinite starvation, but it does not override waiting P1 cases.

**Reservation and preemption**

No reserved P1 capacity. A P1 may preempt a lower-priority diagnostic only after 10 minutes of diagnostic waiting, only when remaining victim work exceeds 15 minutes, and only once per victim case.

**Parallelization**

ME and ES are sequential on the designated common-qualified resource. This avoids coordination and minimizes handovers but makes Deepa and Chen single points of failure.

**Handover/setup control**

Cases remain with their fixed owner whenever possible. Family switches may be batched, but batching must not delay a P1.

**Overflow trigger**

There is no cross-skill overflow except an explicit supervisor-declared emergency mode. This makes the policy transparent but brittle.

## Policy P: full qualified-resource pooling

**Eligibility**

Every activity may go to every physically qualified resource. Resource multipliers, calendars, setup state, continuity, and projected availability are included in dispatch.

**Dispatch score**

For every eligible task-resource pair, compute:

1. priority guardrail;
2. normalized SLA slack;
3. expected remaining processing time;
4. resource-adjusted task duration;
5. expected handover/setup penalty;
6. aging credit.

Choose the pair with the lowest predicted risk-adjusted completion score. The score and eligible set are logged for audit.

**Reservation and preemption**

No hard reservation. P1 tasks have a strong score advantage and may preempt after five diagnostic waiting minutes if the net time saved is positive.

**Parallelization**

ME and ES use parallel branches whenever projected parallel completion, including coordination and cross-team delay, is earlier than the best sequential completion. This will include most uncongested cases, not only P1.

**Handover/setup control**

Continuity and setup are score penalties rather than hard constraints. Farah is used diagnostically only if her slower service still improves projected completion.

**Overflow trigger**

Not applicable: the pool is always open. This policy is strongest for average flow and mix changes but may generate more handovers and consume both branches’ capacity for low-priority cases.

## Policy H: reserved-P1 hybrid with controlled overflow

**Primary eligibility**

- Intake/Validation: Ben, Farah, Emil.
- Mechanical: Ada, Ben.
- Electrical: Chen, Deepa.
- Software: Chen, Emil.

**Controlled overflow**

- P1: immediate access to all physically qualified resources.
- P2: overflow after 20 minutes of stage waiting or when projected SLA slack falls below 30 minutes.
- P3: overflow after 60 minutes, subject to P1 reserve and aging.
- Deepa is Mechanical overflow; Ada/Emil are Electrical overflow; Farah is last-resort diagnostic overflow.

**Dispatch score**

Priority guardrail plus normalized SLA slack, expected remaining processing time, continuity, setup, speed, and aging. A P3 waiting over four hours gains aging credit but cannot displace a P1 at risk.

**Reservation**

Maintain a rotating rapid-response reserve on a skill-compatible flex resource. Operationally, no more than 75% of its rolling 120-minute capacity is committed to lower-priority work unless no alternative work placement exists. Suggested rotation:

- Ada during 08:00–10:00;
- Deepa during 10:00–16:00;
- Emil after 16:00.

This is a soft reserve: unused capacity is released when no P1 pressure exists.

**Preemption**

A waiting P1 may preempt after three minutes when:

- no qualified capacity will become free soon enough;
- the victim has more than 15 minutes remaining;
- the estimated P1 gain exceeds restart plus setup/handover cost;
- the victim case has not already been preempted.

**Parallelization**

- P1 ME/ES: parallel unless one branch would wait so long that sequential is faster.
- P2: parallel only when the projected saving exceeds 15 minutes and diagnostic workload over the next hour is below 90% of available capacity.
- P3: sequential by default.
- A parallel decision is reassessed before either branch starts; once one branch starts, mode changes require supervisor approval.

**Handover/setup control**

Continuity receives a strong preference. Family batching is allowed only when no P1 has waited 10 minutes and no P2 is within 60 minutes of its SLA threshold.

This policy intentionally gives up some base-case mean efficiency to protect tail risk, continuity, and shock response.

# 5. Robust recommendation

## Primary recommendation

Use **Policy H, the reserved-P1 hybrid**, optimized under Objective B.

Its advantage is not necessarily the lowest base-case mean. Full pooling may have a lower mean when demand is balanced and handover/setup costs are small. The hybrid is preferred because it:

- retains most pooling benefits during U2 and Chen absence;
- protects a rapid-response path for P1 rather than relying only on queue rank;
- parallelizes the cases for which critical-path reduction matters most;
- avoids spending two scarce resources on routine P3 parallelism;
- limits slow cross-skill assignments unless waiting or SLA risk justifies them;
- produces less handover/setup burden than unrestricted pooling;
- avoids the fixed-queue single points of failure in strict specialization;
- makes decisions explainable through explicit slack, eligibility, and overflow thresholds.

## Normal-to-stress mode triggers

Switch from normal hybrid mode to stress mode when any of the following occurs:

1. a P1 has waited 10 minutes for Intake or 5 minutes for diagnostics;
2. a P1’s projected p90 completion exceeds 105 elapsed minutes, leaving less than 15 minutes of safety;
3. base-equivalent E/S queue workload exceeds 90% of qualified capacity available over the next 120 minutes;
4. total remaining work exceeds 90% of all available capacity over the next four hours;
5. two or more P1 cases simultaneously need the same scarce skill;
6. Chen’s absence or the U1/U5 surge is known;
7. Intake backlog reaches three cases after an outage.

Stress mode should:

- parallelize all feasible P1 ME/ES cases;
- prohibit P3 starts on the protected flex resource while a P1 diagnostic is waiting;
- use P1-only preemption with the net-benefit test;
- route P2 by critical ratio rather than FCFS;
- keep Emil primarily on Software after 16:00 unless an Electrical P1 is at risk;
- keep Deepa primarily on Electrical, with Mechanical overflow based on queue workload;
- activate overtime deliberately rather than implicitly;
- expose P3 carryover rather than hiding it through unsafe utilization.

## Capacity escalation

Under the uniform-mixture estimate, base work already exceeds maximum same-day capacity before speed and setup penalties. Therefore, when the four-hour workload/capacity ratio exceeds 1.0, management must choose among:

- supplemental qualified capacity;
- demand deferral or appointment shaping where incidents permit;
- explicit P3 carryover;
- acceptance of lower P2/P3 attainment.

These are capacity decisions, not routing improvements. Under U5, a credible P1 90% guarantee would require extra capacity or demand protection beyond the listed staff.

## When another policy is preferable

- **Full pooling:** preferable if the objective is primarily mean flow time, demand mix changes frequently, setup/handover costs prove small, and P1 pressure is low.
- **Strict specialization:** defensible only at materially lower load, where skill preservation, simple ownership, or costly real handovers outweigh resilience. It is inappropriate for U3/U5.
- **Hybrid stress mode:** preferable to ordinary hybrid during known surges, absence, or outage recovery, but it should not become permanent because P3 starvation and skill concentration would follow.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates.** Staff may classify work as complex or inflate remaining time to avoid preemption or gain priority. Preserve original estimates, revisions, revision reasons, and estimator identity. Audit systematic bias by resource and priority.
- **P3 starvation.** P1 reservation and critical-ratio routing can leave P3 work overnight. Use an aging credit, a visible maximum P3 wait, and a rule that at least one non-protected slot serves old P3 work whenever no P1 is at immediate risk.
- **Skill erosion.** Primary routing may reduce Ada’s Electrical exposure, Emil’s Electrical exposure, or Farah’s diagnostic practice. Schedule controlled low-risk cross-skill work during low-load periods and monitor competency separately from production utilization.
- **Excessive preemption.** A 10% restart penalty is only the explicit cost; cognitive recovery and setup may be larger. Enforce one preemption per victim case, a minimum remaining-work threshold, and a positive net-benefit test.
- **Coordination overhead.** Parallelization may look attractive from branch means while adding two handovers, synchronized Validation demand, and an 8-minute coordination period. Compare actual rather than planned critical-path savings.
- **Validation bottleneck.** Better diagnostic routing can merely create a burst at Validation, particularly after the U1 or outage wave. Validation workload and projected release times must be part of the dispatch score.
- **Fairness.** Equal utilization is not automatically fair because slow-resource assignments and family switching impose different cognitive loads. Report active utilization, overtime, setup count, preemptions suffered, complex cases, and undesirable shift-end carryover.
- **Interpretability.** A mathematically optimal score that operators cannot reproduce will be distrusted. Each assignment should display priority, SLA slack, eligible resources, continuity effect, expected setup/handover cost, and the triggered overflow rule.
- **False precision.** Remaining-time estimates from interval ranges are weak, especially during U4. Use bands and sensitivity tests rather than a single deterministic completion prediction.
- **Reservation waste.** A hard idle reserve can worsen already inadequate aggregate capacity. The reserve should be rolling and soft, with automatic release when no P1 risk signal exists.

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

## Required event and decision attributes

At minimum, capture:

- `case:concept:name`;
- `concept:name`;
- `start_timestamp`;
- `time:timestamp`;
- `org:resource`;
- case type and priority;
- arrival block and actual arrival time;
- **queue-entry timestamp** for each stage;
- **assignment decision timestamp and score components**;
- **eligible-resource set** at decision time;
- **skill used** and resource multiplier;
- **planned and actual service**;
- ordinary/tail classification where available;
- **pause/resume**, preempting case, victim case, remaining work, and restart penalty;
- **handover** source, destination, reason, and duration;
- **setup** old family, new family, and duration;
- branch mode, branch readiness, and coordination interval;
- **policy version** and normal/stress mode;
- **scenario/shock flag**, including outage and absence;
- shift, break, and overtime status;
- **SLA clock** at queue entry, assignment, start, pause, resume, and completion;
- estimate revisions and revision reason.

## Deployment indicators

Monitor by priority, type, stage, resource, and two-hour block:

- arrival count and deviation from forecast;
- base-equivalent WIP by skill;
- stage queue wait p50/p90/p95;
- arrival-to-completion p90/p95 and SLA attainment with confidence intervals;
- censored open cases, rather than excluding them;
- planned versus actual service ratio and tail frequency;
- handovers, cross-team moves, setups, and coordination minutes per case;
- number and net benefit of preemptions;
- resource utilization, overtime, utilization CV, and maximum utilization;
- sequential versus parallel critical-path saving;
- post-diagnostic Validation queue growth;
- P3 aging and overnight carryover.

## PM4Py analyses

- `pm4py.format_dataframe` should standardize interval-log columns before discovery.
- `pm4py.get_service_time` can track activity service-time drift by rolling period. For resource-specific drift, split or group by activity and resource rather than relying only on the overall activity mean.
- `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` produces case-level service, sojourn, and waiting measures according to PM4Py’s interval semantics. These are not queue-specific waits and may be repeated on all events of a case. SLA elapsed time should still be calculated directly from arrival to Validation completion.
- Queue-specific waiting must be calculated separately as first activity start minus the logged queue-entry timestamp for each task instance.
- `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network` reveals resource succession and concentration. In parallel traces, directly-follows order can create apparent handovers between concurrent branches, so compare the network with explicit logged `Handover` events.
- Performance DFGs and time-sliced variants can show whether the bottleneck migrates from Intake to Electrical/Software or Validation.
- Variant analysis can compare sequential versus parallel ME/ES behavior and identify unauthorized routing.
- Conformance checking against the intended Intake–diagnostic–Validation model can detect missing Validation, branch duplication, or preemption without a resume.
- Rolling service-time and waiting-time comparisons by policy version can distinguish genuine demand drift from dispatch-induced change.

# Explicit process constants rather than hidden penalties.
HANDOVER_MIN = 7
CROSS_TEAM_MIN = 5
SETUP_MIN = 5
PARALLEL_COORD_MIN = 8
RESTART_FRACTION = 0.10
OVERTIME_MIN = 30
SLA_MIN = {"P1": 120, "P2": 480, "P3": 1440}
PRIORITY_RANK = {"P1": 0, "P2": 1, "P3": 2}
DIAGNOSTICS = ["Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"]
SERVICE_ACTIVITIES = ["Intake"] + DIAGNOSTICS + ["Validation"]
TYPE_BRANCHES = {
    "M": ["Mechanical"],
    "E": ["Electrical"],
    "S": ["Software"],
    "ME": ["Mechanical", "Electrical"],
    "ES": ["Electrical", "Software"],
}
FAMILY = {
    "Intake": "ADMIN", "Validation": "ADMIN",
    "Mechanical": "M", "Electrical": "ES", "Software": "ES"
}

def hhmm_to_min(x):
    h, m = map(int, x.split(":"))
    return 60 * h + m

calendar = {}
for r, row in skill_df.iterrows():
    s, e = row["shift"].split("-")
    if row["break/unavailability"]:
        bs, be = row["break/unavailability"].split("-")
        br = (hhmm_to_min(bs), hhmm_to_min(be))
    else:
        br = None
    calendar[r] = {
        "start": hhmm_to_min(s),
        "end": hhmm_to_min(e),
        "break": br
    }

resources = list(skill_df.index)
T0 = pd.Timestamp("2025-01-06 00:00:00", tz="UTC")

def mixture_mean(activity, tail_multiplier=1.0):
    row = service_df.loc[activity]
    p = min(1.0, row["tail_probability"] * tail_multiplier)
    ordinary_mean = (row["ordinary_lo"] + row["ordinary_hi"]) / 2
    tail_mean = (row["tail_lo"] + row["tail_hi"]) / 2
    return (1 - p) * ordinary_mean + p * tail_mean

BASE_MEAN = {a: mixture_mean(a) for a in service_df.index}

# Fixed/home queues used by the policies.
SILO_HOME = {
    "Intake": ["Ben", "Farah", "Emil"],
    "Validation": ["Ben", "Farah", "Emil"],
    "Mechanical": ["Ada", "Ben", "Deepa"],
    "Electrical": ["Chen", "Deepa"],
    "Software": ["Chen", "Emil"],
}
HYBRID_PRIMARY = {
    "Intake": ["Ben", "Farah", "Emil"],
    "Validation": ["Ben", "Farah", "Emil"],
    "Mechanical": ["Ada", "Ben"],
    "Electrical": ["Chen", "Deepa"],
    "Software": ["Chen", "Emil"],
}

def physically_qualified(resource, activity):
    return activity in skill_df.columns and pd.notna(skill_df.loc[resource, activity])

def regular_capacity(resource):
    c = calendar[resource]
    cap = c["end"] - c["start"]
    if c["break"] is not None:
        cap -= c["break"][1] - c["break"][0]
    return cap

def simulate(policy, scenario, seed, return_log=False):
    """
    Transparent one-minute discrete-event simulation.

    Explicit assumptions:
    - One arrival day; later days only clear backlog.
    - Chen is absent only on arrival day in U3/U5.
    - Overtime is immediately after shift and is available up to 30 minutes.
    - Handover/cross-team/coordination are elapsed delays, not resource work.
    - Setup consumes resource time.
    - A task assigned near shift end pauses over off-shift time.
    - One-minute discretization can overstate a task by less than one minute.
    """
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    cfg = scenario_df.loc[scenario]
    cases, tasks = {}, {}
    raw_log = []
    task_counter = 0
    case_counter = 0
    overhead = defaultdict(float)
    busy_by_resource_day = defaultdict(float)
    low_priority_busy_times = defaultdict(list)

    state = {
        r: {
            "current": None,
            "setup_remaining": 0,
            "setup_name": None,
            "setup_start": None,
            "segment_start": None,
            "last_diag_family": None,
            "last_diag_case": None,
        } for r in resources
    }

    def draw_service(activity):
        row = service_df.loc[activity]
        p = row["tail_probability"]
        if activity in DIAGNOSTICS:
            p = min(1.0, p * cfg["diagnostic_tail_multiplier"])
        if rng.random() < p:
            return float(rng.uniform(row["tail_lo"], row["tail_hi"]))
        return float(rng.uniform(row["ordinary_lo"], row["ordinary_hi"]))

    def new_task(cid, activity, base_work, ready, prev_resource=None,
                 prev_family=None, pinned=None, required_common=None,
                 seq_next=None):
        nonlocal task_counter
        task_counter += 1
        tid = f"T{task_counter}"
        tasks[tid] = {
            "task_id": tid,
            "case": cid,
            "activity": activity,
            "family": FAMILY[activity],
            "remaining": float(base_work),
            "ready": int(ready),
            "queue_entry": int(ready),
            "prev_resource": prev_resource,
            "prev_family": prev_family,
            "pinned": pinned,
            "required_common": required_common,
            "seq_next": seq_next,
            "status": "ready",
            "assigned": None,
            "handover_done": prev_resource is None,
            "assignment_score": np.nan,
            "eligible_set": "",
            "planned_at_assignment": np.nan,
        }
        return tid

    # Generate stochastic arrivals from all supplied blocks.
    for _, row in demand_df.iterrows():
        start_txt, end_txt = row["block"].split("-")
        block_start, block_end = hhmm_to_min(start_txt), hhmm_to_min(end_txt)
        morning = block_start < 720
        lam = row["expected_arrivals"]
        if morning:
            lam *= cfg["morning_multiplier"]
        n = rng.poisson(lam)

        p1 = row["P1_pct"] / 100
        p2 = row["P2_pct"] / 100
        p3 = row["P3_pct"] / 100
        if morning and cfg["morning_P1_delta"] > 0:
            new_p1 = min(1.0, p1 + cfg["morning_P1_delta"])
            remainder = 1 - new_p1
            old_non_p1 = p2 + p3
            p2, p3, p1 = (
                remainder * p2 / old_non_p1,
                remainder * p3 / old_non_p1,
                new_p1,
            )
        priority_probs = np.array([p1, p2, p3], dtype=float)
        priority_probs /= priority_probs.sum()

        type_probs = np.array([
            row["M_pct"], row["E_pct"], row["S_pct"],
            row["ME_pct"], row["ES_pct"]
        ], dtype=float)
        if cfg["reweight_E_ES"] and 720 <= block_start < 960:
            type_probs[1] *= 2  # E
            type_probs[4] *= 2  # ES
        type_probs /= type_probs.sum()

        for _ in range(n):
            case_counter += 1
            cid = f"{scenario}_{policy}_{seed}_{case_counter:03d}"
            arrival = int(rng.integers(block_start, block_end))
            priority = rng.choice(["P1", "P2", "P3"], p=priority_probs)
            case_type = rng.choice(["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"], p=type_probs)
            cases[cid] = {
                "arrival": arrival,
                "priority": priority,
                "type": case_type,
                "sla": SLA_MIN[priority],
                "validation_work": draw_service("Validation"),
                "mode": None,
                "diag_done": {},
                "completed": None,
                "preempted": False,
                "active_service": 0.0,
            }
            new_task(cid, "Intake", draw_service("Intake"), arrival)

    def emit(cid, activity, start, end, resource, task=None, **extra):
        if not return_log:
            return
        c = cases.get(cid, {})
        rec = {
            "case:concept:name": cid,
            "concept:name": activity,
            "_start_min": start,
            "_end_min": end,
            "org:resource": resource,
            "priority": c.get("priority"),
            "case_type": c.get("type"),
            "branch_mode": c.get("mode"),
            "policy_version": f"{policy}-v1",
            "scenario_shock_flag": scenario,
            "arrival_minute": c.get("arrival"),
            "sla_target_minutes": c.get("sla"),
            "sla_clock_minutes": (end - c["arrival"]) if c else np.nan,
            "task_instance": task["task_id"] if task else None,
            "queue_entry_minute": task["queue_entry"] if task else np.nan,
            "skill_used": task["activity"] if task else None,
            "planned_service_minutes": (
                task.get("planned_at_assignment", np.nan) if task else np.nan
            ),
            "assignment_decision": (
                f"{policy}:score={task.get('assignment_score', np.nan):.2f}"
                if task else None
            ),
            "eligible_resource_set": task.get("eligible_set", "") if task else "",
            "actual_segment_minutes": end - start,
        }
        rec.update(extra)
        raw_log.append(rec)

    def calendar_available(resource, t):
        day, local = divmod(t, 1440)
        if cfg["chen_absent"] and resource == "Chen" and day == 0:
            return False
        c = calendar[resource]
        if not (c["start"] <= local < c["end"] + OVERTIME_MIN):
            return False
        if c["break"] is not None and c["break"][0] <= local < c["break"][1]:
            return False
        return True

    def intake_outage(t):
        return bool(cfg["intake_outage"] and 570 <= t < 600)

    def fastest_mean(activity):
        vals = [
            BASE_MEAN[activity] * skill_df.loc[r, activity]
            for r in resources if physically_qualified(r, activity)
        ]
        return min(vals)

    def estimated_remaining(task, resource, t):
        c = cases[task["case"]]
        act = task["activity"]
        own = task["remaining"] * skill_df.loc[resource, act]
        if act == "Intake":
            branches = TYPE_BRANCHES[c["type"]]
            diag = [fastest_mean(a) for a in branches]
            if len(diag) == 2 and (
                policy == "POOL" or c["priority"] == "P1"
            ):
                diag_est = max(diag) + PARALLEL_COORD_MIN
                if c["type"] == "ME":
                    diag_est += CROSS_TEAM_MIN
            else:
                diag_est = sum(diag)
            return own + diag_est + fastest_mean("Validation")
        if act in DIAGNOSTICS:
            return own + fastest_mean("Validation")
        return own

    def protected_resource(t):
        local = t % 1440
        if 480 <= local < 600:
            return "Ada"
        if 600 <= local < 960:
            return "Deepa"
        return "Emil"

    def policy_allowed(task, resource, t):
        act = task["activity"]
        if not calendar_available(resource, t):
            return False
        if intake_outage(t) and act == "Intake":
            return False
        if not physically_qualified(resource, act):
            return False
        if task["required_common"] is not None and resource not in task["required_common"]:
            return False
        if task["pinned"] is not None:
            return resource == task["pinned"]

        c = cases[task["case"]]
        wait = t - task["queue_entry"]

        if policy == "SILO":
            return resource in SILO_HOME[act]

        if policy == "HYBRID":
            if resource not in HYBRID_PRIMARY[act]:
                threshold = {"P1": 0, "P2": 20, "P3": 60}[c["priority"]]
                slack = c["sla"] - (t - c["arrival"]) - estimated_remaining(task, resource, t)
                if wait < threshold and slack > 30:
                    return False

            # Explicit rolling reserve: after 90 lower-priority busy minutes
            # in 120 minutes, the rotating flex resource is protected.
            if c["priority"] != "P1" and resource == protected_resource(t):
                recent = sum(x > t - 120 for x in low_priority_busy_times[resource])
                if recent >= 90:
                    return False

        return True  # Full pool or accepted hybrid pair.

    def score_pair(task, resource, t):
        c = cases[task["case"]]
        wait = t - task["queue_entry"]
        duration = task["remaining"] * skill_df.loc[resource, task["activity"]]
        continuity = task["prev_resource"] == resource
        setup_needed = (
            task["activity"] in DIAGNOSTICS
            and state[resource]["last_diag_family"] is not None
            and state[resource]["last_diag_family"] != task["family"]
            and state[resource]["last_diag_case"] != task["case"]
        )
        handover_needed = task["prev_resource"] not in (None, resource)
        slack = (
            c["sla"]
            - (t - c["arrival"])
            - estimated_remaining(task, resource, t)
        )

        if policy == "SILO":
            # Fixed-queue priority then FCFS, with continuity as tie-breaker.
            return (
                PRIORITY_RANK[c["priority"]] * 1_000_000
                + task["queue_entry"]
                - (100 if continuity else 0)
            )

        priority_guard = {"P1": -100_000, "P2": -10_000, "P3": 0}[c["priority"]]
        aging = min(wait, 600) * 4
        if c["priority"] == "P3" and wait >= 240:
            aging += 4_000
        transition = (
            (HANDOVER_MIN if handover_needed else 0)
            + (SETUP_MIN if setup_needed else 0)
        )
        continuity_credit = 250 if continuity else 0
        overflow_penalty = 0
        if policy == "HYBRID" and resource not in HYBRID_PRIMARY[task["activity"]]:
            overflow_penalty = 80

        return (
            priority_guard
            + 10 * slack
            + duration
            + 8 * transition
            + overflow_penalty
            - aging
            - continuity_credit
        )

    def close_service_segment(resource, end):
        st = state[resource]
        if st["current"] is None or st["segment_start"] is None:
            return
        task = tasks[st["current"]]
        if end > st["segment_start"]:
            emit(task["case"], task["activity"], st["segment_start"], end,
                 resource, task=task,
                 resource_multiplier=skill_df.loc[resource, task["activity"]])
        st["segment_start"] = None

    def close_setup_segment(resource, end):
        st = state[resource]
        if st["current"] is None or st["setup_start"] is None:
            return
        task = tasks[st["current"]]
        if end > st["setup_start"]:
            emit(task["case"], st["setup_name"], st["setup_start"], end,
                 resource, task=task, setup_family=task["family"])
        st["setup_start"] = None

    def choose_mode(cid):
        c = cases[cid]
        if len(TYPE_BRANCHES[c["type"]]) == 1:
            return "single"
        if policy == "SILO":
            return "sequential"
        if policy == "POOL":
            return "parallel"
        if c["priority"] == "P1":
            return "parallel"
        diag_wip = sum(
            x["remaining"] for x in tasks.values()
            if x["status"] != "done" and x["activity"] in DIAGNOSTICS
        )
        if c["priority"] == "P2" and diag_wip < 180:
            return "parallel"
        return "sequential"

    def common_resources(branches, selected_policy):
        common = [
            r for r in resources
            if all(physically_qualified(r, a) for a in branches)
        ]
        if selected_policy == "SILO":
            if branches == ["Mechanical", "Electrical"]:
                return ["Deepa"]
            if branches == ["Electrical", "Software"]:
                return ["Chen"]
        return common

    def complete_task(task, end, resource):
        cid = task["case"]
        c = cases[cid]
        act = task["activity"]

        if act == "Intake":
            c["mode"] = choose_mode(cid)
            branches = TYPE_BRANCHES[c["type"]]
            if len(branches) == 1:
                new_task(cid, branches[0], draw_service(branches[0]), end,
                         prev_resource=resource, prev_family="ADMIN")
            elif c["mode"] == "parallel":
                for branch in branches:
                    new_task(cid, branch, draw_service(branch), end,
                             prev_resource=resource, prev_family="ADMIN")
            else:
                first, second = branches
                common = common_resources(branches, policy)
                new_task(cid, first, draw_service(first), end,
                         prev_resource=resource, prev_family="ADMIN",
                         required_common=common, seq_next=second)
            return

        if act in DIAGNOSTICS:
            c["diag_done"][act] = (end, resource)
            if task["seq_next"] is not None:
                nxt = task["seq_next"]
                new_task(cid, nxt, draw_service(nxt), end,
                         prev_resource=resource, prev_family=task["family"],
                         pinned=resource, seq_next=None)
                return

            required = set(TYPE_BRANCHES[c["type"]])
            if required.issubset(c["diag_done"].keys()):
                latest_act = max(required, key=lambda a: c["diag_done"][a][0])
                latest_end, latest_resource = c["diag_done"][latest_act]
                ready = latest_end

                if c["mode"] == "parallel":
                    emit(cid, "Parallel_Coordination", ready,
                         ready + PARALLEL_COORD_MIN, "SYSTEM")
                    overhead["coord_min"] += PARALLEL_COORD_MIN
                    ready += PARALLEL_COORD_MIN

                    # ME parallel work combines mechanical and E/S teams.
                    used_resources = {
                        c["diag_done"][a][1] for a in required
                    }
                    if c["type"] == "ME" and len(used_resources) > 1:
                        emit(cid, "CrossTeamTransfer", ready,
                             ready + CROSS_TEAM_MIN, "SYSTEM")
                        overhead["cross_min"] += CROSS_TEAM_MIN
                        ready += CROSS_TEAM_MIN

                new_task(cid, "Validation", c["validation_work"], ready,
                         prev_resource=latest_resource,
                         prev_family=FAMILY[latest_act])
            return

        if act == "Validation":
            c["completed"] = end

    def initiate_handover(task, resource, t):
        delay = HANDOVER_MIN
        if (
            task["prev_family"] in {"M", "ES"}
            and task["family"] in {"M", "ES"}
            and task["prev_family"] != task["family"]
        ):
            delay += CROSS_TEAM_MIN
            overhead["cross_min"] += CROSS_TEAM_MIN
        emit(task["case"], "Handover", t, t + delay, "SYSTEM", task=task,
             handover_from=task["prev_resource"], handover_to=resource)
        overhead["handover_min"] += HANDOVER_MIN
        task["ready"] = t + delay
        task["pinned"] = resource
        task["handover_done"] = True

    def try_preemption(t):
        threshold = {"SILO": 10, "POOL": 5, "HYBRID": 3}[policy]
        waiting_p1 = sorted([
            x for x in tasks.values()
            if x["status"] == "ready"
            and x["ready"] <= t
            and x["activity"] in DIAGNOSTICS
            and cases[x["case"]]["priority"] == "P1"
            and t - x["queue_entry"] >= threshold
        ], key=lambda x: x["queue_entry"])

        for p1task in waiting_p1:
            free_exists = any(
                state[r]["current"] is None and policy_allowed(p1task, r, t)
                for r in resources
            )
            if free_exists:
                continue

            victims = []
            for r in resources:
                st = state[r]
                if st["current"] is None or st["setup_remaining"] > 0:
                    continue
                victim = tasks[st["current"]]
                vc = cases[victim["case"]]
                if (
                    victim["activity"] in DIAGNOSTICS
                    and PRIORITY_RANK[vc["priority"]] > PRIORITY_RANK["P1"]
                    and not vc["preempted"]
                    and victim["remaining"] * skill_df.loc[r, victim["activity"]] > 15
                    and policy_allowed(p1task, r, t)
                ):
                    victims.append((r, victim))

            if not victims:
                continue

            # Prefer interrupting the task with the largest remaining service.
            r, victim = max(
                victims,
                key=lambda rv: rv[1]["remaining"]
                * skill_df.loc[rv[0], rv[1]["activity"]]
            )

            # Handover is initiated first; interruption waits until P1 is ready.
            if not p1task["handover_done"]:
                if p1task["prev_resource"] not in (None, r):
                    initiate_handover(p1task, r, t)
                    continue
                p1task["handover_done"] = True
                p1task["pinned"] = r

            close_service_segment(r, t)
            victim["remaining"] *= (1 + RESTART_FRACTION)
            victim["status"] = "ready"
            victim["ready"] = t
            victim["assigned"] = None
            victim["pinned"] = r
            victim["handover_done"] = True
            cases[victim["case"]]["preempted"] = True
            emit(victim["case"], "Pause_Preempted", t, t, r, task=victim,
                 restart_fraction=RESTART_FRACTION,
                 preempting_case=p1task["case"])
            overhead["preemptions"] += 1
            state[r]["current"] = None
            state[r]["setup_remaining"] = 0
            state[r]["setup_start"] = None
            state[r]["segment_start"] = None

    # Long enough to clear one arrival day in most simulated realizations.
    horizon = 480 + 4 * 1440
    final_t = horizon

    for t in range(480, horizon):
        # U5: release resources from in-progress Intake during the IT outage.
        if cfg["intake_outage"] and t == 570:
            for r in resources:
                st = state[r]
                if st["current"] is not None:
                    task = tasks[st["current"]]
                    if task["activity"] == "Intake":
                        close_service_segment(r, t)
                        task["status"] = "ready"
                        task["ready"] = 600
                        task["assigned"] = None
                        task["pinned"] = r
                        task["handover_done"] = True
                        emit(task["case"], "IT_Outage_Pause", 570, 600,
                             "SYSTEM", task=task)
                        st["current"] = None

        try_preemption(t)

        # Global pair dispatch: repeat until no free-resource/task pair remains.
        while True:
            pairs = []
            for task in tasks.values():
                if task["status"] != "ready" or task["ready"] > t:
                    continue
                for r in resources:
                    if state[r]["current"] is None and policy_allowed(task, r, t):
                        pairs.append((score_pair(task, r, t), task["task_id"], r))
            if not pairs:
                break

            pair_score, tid, r = min(pairs, key=lambda x: (x[0], x[1], x[2]))
            task = tasks[tid]

            if (
                not task["handover_done"]
                and task["prev_resource"] not in (None, r)
            ):
                initiate_handover(task, r, t)
                continue

            task["handover_done"] = True
            task["status"] = "running"
            task["assigned"] = r
            task["assignment_score"] = pair_score
            task["eligible_set"] = "|".join([
                q for q in resources if policy_allowed(task, q, t)
            ])
            task["planned_at_assignment"] = (
                task["remaining"] * skill_df.loc[r, task["activity"]]
            )

            st = state[r]
            st["current"] = tid
            st["segment_start"] = None
            st["setup_start"] = None
            st["setup_remaining"] = 0
            st["setup_name"] = None

            if task["activity"] in DIAGNOSTICS:
                needs_setup = (
                    st["last_diag_family"] is not None
                    and st["last_diag_family"] != task["family"]
                    and st["last_diag_case"] != task["case"]
                )
                if needs_setup:
                    st["setup_remaining"] = SETUP_MIN
                    st["setup_name"] = (
                        f"Setup_{st['last_diag_family']}_to_{task['family']}"
                    )
                    overhead["setup_min"] += SETUP_MIN
                st["last_diag_family"] = task["family"]
                st["last_diag_case"] = task["case"]

        # Work one minute on every assigned resource.
        for r in resources:
            st = state[r]
            if st["current"] is None:
                continue
            task = tasks[st["current"]]

            if not calendar_available(r, t) or (
                task["activity"] == "Intake" and intake_outage(t)
            ):
                close_service_segment(r, t)
                close_setup_segment(r, t)
                continue

            day = t // 1440

            if st["setup_remaining"] > 0:
                if st["setup_start"] is None:
                    st["setup_start"] = t
                st["setup_remaining"] -= 1
                busy_by_resource_day[(r, day)] += 1
                if cases[task["case"]]["priority"] != "P1":
                    low_priority_busy_times[r].append(t)
                if st["setup_remaining"] == 0:
                    close_setup_segment(r, t + 1)
                continue

            if st["segment_start"] is None:
                st["segment_start"] = t
                emit(task["case"], "Resume" if task["remaining"] <
                     task["planned_at_assignment"] else "Start",
                     t, t, r, task=task)

            multiplier = skill_df.loc[r, task["activity"]]
            task["remaining"] -= 1.0 / multiplier
            busy_by_resource_day[(r, day)] += 1
            cases[task["case"]]["active_service"] += 1
            if cases[task["case"]]["priority"] != "P1":
                low_priority_busy_times[r].append(t)

            if task["remaining"] <= 1e-9:
                close_service_segment(r, t + 1)
                task["status"] = "done"
                task["assigned"] = None
                st["current"] = None
                st["segment_start"] = None
                complete_task(task, t + 1, r)

        if cases and all(c["completed"] is not None for c in cases.values()):
            final_t = t + 1
            break

    # Right-censor incomplete cases at the simulation horizon.
    flows = {}
    sla_hit = {}
    for cid, c in cases.items():
        end = c["completed"] if c["completed"] is not None else final_t
        flows[cid] = end - c["arrival"]
        sla_hit[cid] = (
            c["completed"] is not None and flows[cid] <= c["sla"]
        )

    n_cases = max(len(cases), 1)
    flow_values = np.array(list(flows.values()), dtype=float) if cases else np.array([np.nan])

    # Max utilization over resource-days; fairness CV uses arrival day.
    util_by_resource_day = {}
    for (r, day), busy in busy_by_resource_day.items():
        if cfg["chen_absent"] and r == "Chen" and day == 0:
            continue
        util_by_resource_day[(r, day)] = busy / regular_capacity(r)

    arrival_day_utils = []
    for r in resources:
        if cfg["chen_absent"] and r == "Chen":
            continue
        arrival_day_utils.append(
            busy_by_resource_day.get((r, 0), 0.0) / regular_capacity(r)
        )
    fairness_cv = (
        float(np.std(arrival_day_utils) / np.mean(arrival_day_utils))
        if np.mean(arrival_day_utils) > 0 else np.nan
    )

    metrics = {
        "policy": policy,
        "scenario": scenario,
        "seed": seed,
        "cases": len(cases),
        "mean_delay_h": float(np.nanmean(flow_values) / 60),
        "p90_delay_h": float(np.nanquantile(flow_values, 0.90) / 60),
        "P1_SLA": np.mean([
            sla_hit[cid] for cid, c in cases.items() if c["priority"] == "P1"
        ]) if any(c["priority"] == "P1" for c in cases.values()) else np.nan,
        "P2_SLA": np.mean([
            sla_hit[cid] for cid, c in cases.items() if c["priority"] == "P2"
        ]) if any(c["priority"] == "P2" for c in cases.values()) else np.nan,
        "P3_SLA": np.mean([
            sla_hit[cid] for cid, c in cases.items() if c["priority"] == "P3"
        ]) if any(c["priority"] == "P3" for c in cases.values()) else np.nan,
        "max_resource_utilization": (
            max(util_by_resource_day.values())
            if util_by_resource_day else np.nan
        ),
        "utilization_fairness_cv": fairness_cv,
        "handover_setup_min_per_case": (
            overhead["handover_min"] + overhead["cross_min"]
            + overhead["setup_min"]
        ) / n_cases,
        "coord_min_per_case": overhead["coord_min"] / n_cases,
        "preemptions_per_case": overhead["preemptions"] / n_cases,
        "censor_rate": np.mean([
            c["completed"] is None for c in cases.values()
        ]) if cases else np.nan,
    }

    if return_log:
        log_df = pd.DataFrame(raw_log)
        if not log_df.empty:
            log_df["start_timestamp"] = (
                T0 + pd.to_timedelta(log_df.pop("_start_min"), unit="m")
            )
            log_df["time:timestamp"] = (
                T0 + pd.to_timedelta(log_df.pop("_end_min"), unit="m")
            )
            log_df["queue_entry_timestamp"] = (
                T0 + pd.to_timedelta(log_df["queue_entry_minute"], unit="m")
            )
            required = [
                "case:concept:name", "concept:name", "start_timestamp",
                "time:timestamp", "org:resource"
            ]
            other = [c for c in log_df.columns if c not in required]
            log_df = log_df[required + other].sort_values(
                ["case:concept:name", "start_timestamp", "time:timestamp"]
            ).reset_index(drop=True)
        return metrics, log_df

    return metrics

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## Limits

The supplied information is insufficient to conclude:

- the actual within-range service distribution;
- whether tail status is correlated across activities within a case;
- whether P1 cases are more likely to be multi-branch or complex;
- whether arrivals are Poisson, clustered, or scheduled;
- the initial WIP at 08:00;
- whether handover and coordination delays consume staff time;
- whether interrupted work can resume on another resource without further loss;
- the empirical setup frequency and true cognitive switching cost;
- overnight staffing and the fate of unfinished cases;
- absence duration beyond the stated day;
- actual learning, fatigue, quality, or rework effects;
- exact SLA attainment from expected arrivals alone.

The scenario table therefore expresses relative risk, not validated forecasts. The simulation also uses one-minute discretization, a single arrival day, automatic availability of permitted overtime, and simplified forecasts of remaining work.

## Validation plan

1. **Instrument before changing dispatch.** Collect at least several weeks of interval events, queue entries, eligible sets, estimates, breaks, setups, handovers, and SLA clocks under the current policy.
2. **Calibrate distributions.** Fit arrival dispersion by block, service distributions by activity/resource/type, cross-activity tail correlation, setup frequency, and transition duration. Compare uniform, triangular, and empirical bootstrap models.
3. **Historical replay.** Replay the same realized arrivals and sampled/observed service requirements under all policies. Use common random numbers so differences reflect policy rather than different random days.
4. **Shadow mode.** Run Policy H without executing its decisions. Record recommended assignments, disagreement with operators, predicted SLA risk, and whether the recommendation was physically feasible.
5. **Protected pilot.** Use a stepped-wedge pilot by day or shift. Keep the hybrid P1 guardrail active everywhere; randomize only P2/P3 overflow and parallelization rules initially.
6. **Safety stops.** Revert to supervisor dispatch if:
   - a P1 waits 10 minutes for Intake or 5 minutes for diagnostics;
   - projected P1 completion exceeds 105 minutes;
   - preemptions exceed one per 20 cases;
   - any resource exceeds planned 90% utilization without approved stress mode;
   - old P3 WIP exceeds a predefined carryover limit.
7. **Evaluation.** Compare P1/P2/P3 attainment, p90/p95 flow, queue-specific waits, utilization CV, overtime, preemption, handover/setup burden, and quality/rework. Use day-level bootstrap intervals because cases on the same day share congestion.
8. **Shock exercises.** Validate U1–U5 through replay or controlled tabletop tests before using stress mode in production. U5 should be treated as a capacity-contingency exercise, not evidence that routing can guarantee the targets.
9. **Decision rule.** Promote the hybrid only if its one-sided confidence bound for P1 SLA is non-inferior to the current policy and it materially improves worst-scenario P1 risk without unacceptable P3 starvation or fairness deterioration.
