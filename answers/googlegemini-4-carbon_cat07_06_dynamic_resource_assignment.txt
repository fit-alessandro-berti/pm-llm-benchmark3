# 1. Objectives and constraints

Resource assignment in knowledge-intensive service processes has no single mathematical optimum. Minimizing immediate waiting time pushes work toward whoever is free (eroding quality and case continuity), whereas routing strictly to top specialists minimizes immediate errors at the cost of severe queues, specialist burnout, and stagnant junior development. Accordingly, the policy is designed as a **governed multi-criteria decision system** that makes trade-offs explicit, bounded, and auditable.

### Optimization Objectives
1. **SLA Risk Mitigation:** Prioritize urgent and short-horizon cases so service plus handover delays do not breach `sla_deadline`.
2. **Quality & Throughput Balance:** Match task complexity to resource proficiency to avoid both the `+3.3h` weak-skill service-time penalty and unnecessary rework loops, while exploiting safe setup-saving batches in Quality Check.
3. **Continuity of Ownership:** Preserve `current_owner` where context matters, avoiding the `+2.7h` waiting penalty on high-complexity handovers and the `1.8x` rework / `1.4x` appeal surge seen when `reassignment_count > 2`.
4. **Workload Fairness & Burnout Prevention:** Balance open work relative to remaining shift hours and shield workers flagged with high overtime risk.
5. **Skill Development & Organizational Resilience:** Deliberately invest slack SLA capacity in supervised training so thin specialist pools (especially **Security** and **Approval**) do not remain single points of failure.

### Hard Safety, Compliance, and Capacity Constraints
Hard constraints act as strict eligibility gates before scoring:
* **H1 — Skill Prohibition (`Skill = 0`):** No resource with skill level `0` may independently execute an activity. *(Note on apprenticeship: where a resource has `Skill = 0` in their documented `training_priority`—such as Eva or Jonas in Security—they may only be attached as a shadow/co-preparer alongside an eligible specialist who formally executes and signs off the task.)*
* **H2 — Security Review Governance:** Must be performed by a level `3` specialist (`R7_Gita` or `R8_Hugo`), or by level `1–2` strictly under explicit supervision by `R7_Gita` or `R8_Hugo`.
* **H3 — Final Approval Governance:** Must be finalized by skill level `2–3`. Skill level `1` may prepare the approval package under supervision, but may not finalize.
* **H4 — High/Urgent Priority Protection:** Cases with `case_priority` in `{urgent, high}` require skill level `>= 2`, unless explicitly paired with a supervisor of skill `3`.
* **H5 — Decision-Batch Cap:** No resource may receive more than `4` new involvements (counting both primary execution and formal supervision/shadowing) in a single assignment batch, nor exceed reasonable shift capacity (`hours_remaining`).
* **H6 — Batching Eligibility:** `Quality Check` tasks may be batched only when `case_priority` is `{normal, low}` and `sla_deadline` remaining `> 24h`.

### Soft Constraints and Managed Preference Trade-offs
Soft constraints may be traded off only under explicit conditions that are logged in `assignment_reason`:
* **S1 — High Overtime-Risk Shield:** Resources with `overtime_risk = "high"` (`R2_Bruno`, `R4_David`, `R9_Iris`) are frozen from new assignments—and have non-urgent queued work actively offloaded—unless a case is `urgent` and no compliant medium/low-risk alternative exists.
* **S2 — High-Complexity Continuity Preference:** When `case_complexity >= 0.70` and `handover_cost_band = "high"`, keeping `current_owner` is strongly preferred unless the owner has skill `0–1` and either supervision is infeasible or the `+3.3h` weak-skill duration would breach a tight SLA (e.g., urgent `C001`).
* **S3 — Developmental Quota (`>= 20%`):** At least `20%` of `{normal, low}` priority assignments in the batch should advance documented `training_priority` goals (or structured level-`1` bottleneck preparation) whenever SLA buffer is sufficient (`>= 18h`) and a mentor is available.
* **S4 — Bottleneck Scarcity Preservation:** Specialist hours in constrained skills (`Security`, `Approval`) carry a scarcity penalty when routing non-bottleneck work (`Eligibility`, `Quality`, `Rework`), unless all non-bottleneck peers are constrained.

# 2. Assignment policy

To remain explainable to workers and auditable by process analysts, the policy uses **two-phase triage with transparent additive scoring** (bounded `0–100` scale before scarcity/overtime adjustments) rather than an opaque black-box solver. Every score component maps directly to an observable reason code.

### Algorithm / Pseudocode

```python
def assign_queue_batch(cases, resources, config):
    # PHASE 1: Triage & Queue Ordering
    # Score urgency first so scarce specialists are reserved for SLA-critical work
    for c in cases:
        c.urgency_score = compute_sla_urgency(c)
    ordered_cases = sort_descending(cases, key=lambda c: (
        c.priority_rank,          # urgent(4) > high(3) > normal(2) > low(1)
        c.urgency_score,          # function of SLA remaining vs expected time
        c.complexity              # tie-breaker: harder cases placed earlier
    ))

    normal_low_cases = [c for c in cases if c.priority in ("normal", "low")]
    min_training_target = ceil(0.20 * len(normal_low_cases))
    training_assigned_count = 0
    batch_counts = {r.id: 0 for r in resources}
    assignments = {}

    # Identify eligible Quality Check batches upfront (Rule H6)
    qc_batch_pool = [
        c for c in ordered_cases
        if c.activity == "Quality Check"
        and c.priority in ("normal", "low")
        and c.sla_hours > 24
    ]

    # PHASE 2: Sequential Candidate Evaluation & Scoring
    for c in ordered_cases:
        # 1. Build valid solo and supervised-pair candidates (Rules H1-H5)
        candidates = generate_eligible_candidates(c, resources, batch_counts)

        # Apply Soft Freeze on High Overtime Risk (Rule S1)
        safe_candidates = [cand for cand in candidates if not cand.uses_high_ot_resource]
        if safe_candidates:
            candidates = safe_candidates
        elif c.priority != "urgent":
            # Hold in short buffer or escalate to team lead rather than burning out high-OT staff
            assignments[c.id] = escalate_exception(c, reason="NO_NON_HIGH_OT_RESOURCE")
            continue

        # 2. Score each candidate transparently
        scored = []
        for cand in candidates:
            s_skill = score_skill_match(c, cand)          # [0 .. 25]
            s_cont  = score_continuity(c, cand)           # [-15 .. +25]
            s_fair  = score_workload_fairness(c, cand, batch_counts, qc_batch_pool) # [-20 .. +25]
            s_train = score_training(c, cand, training_assigned_count < min_training_target) # [0 .. 20]
            s_resil = score_resilience_scarcity(c, cand)  # [-15 .. +5]

            # Weight SLA speed more heavily when SLA is tight (< 8h)
            urgency_mult = 1.4 if c.sla_hours <= 8 else 1.0
            total_score = (urgency_mult * s_skill) + s_cont + s_fair + s_train + s_resil
            scored.append((total_score, cand))

        # 3. Tie-breaking & Selection
        winner = break_ties(scored)  # 1) Higher total_score, 2) Continuity, 3) More hours_remaining, 4) Lower open_cases
        assignments[c.id] = record_decision(c, winner)

        # 4. Update dynamic batch state
        for r in winner.involved_resources:
            batch_counts[r.id] += 1
        update_remaining_hours(winner, c)
        if c.priority in ("normal", "low") and winner.supports_documented_training:
            training_assigned_count += 1

    return assignments
```

### Component Scoring & Rule Mechanics

1. **Eligibility Filtering (`generate_eligible_candidates`):**
   * Excludes any resource `r` with `batch_counts[r] >= 4` or `r.hours_remaining < est_task_hours(c)`.
   * *Solo candidates:* Must satisfy `skill >= 1` (`>= 2` if `priority in {high, urgent}`; `>= 2` for `Final Approval`; `== 3` for `Security Review`).
   * *Supervised pairs `(trainee, mentor)`:* Generated when `c.sla_hours >= 15` (safe buffer for review overhead) or when required by rule.
     * `Security Review`: `trainee` has skill `1–2` (or skill `0` as shadow apprentice if `training_priority == "security"`) paired with `R7_Gita` or `R8_Hugo` executing sign-off.
     * `Final Approval`: `trainee` has skill `1` (preparer) paired with a skill `2–3` finalizer.
     * General training: `trainee` has skill `1` in `c.required_skill` paired with a skill `3` mentor (preferably same team).

2. **SLA Urgency Scoring (`compute_sla_urgency`):**
   * `slack_ratio = c.sla_hours / (est_service_hours + est_handover_delay)`.
   * Urgent (`<= 4h` or `slack_ratio < 1.5`) triggers `urgency_mult = 1.4` on skill-match speed and blocks developmental pairings that add review latency.

3. **Skill-Match Scoring (`s_skill`, `0` to `25` pts):**
   * High complexity (`>= 0.65`) or repeat loops (`reassignment_count >= 2`): Skill `3` = `+25`, Skill `2` = `+14`, Supervised Trainee = `+8`, Solo Skill `1` = `-20` (avoids the `+3.3h` weak-skill drag).
   * Routine/moderate complexity (`< 0.65`): Skill `2` = `+22`, Skill `3` = `+18` (slight penalty vs. Skill `2` to prevent over-qualification waste), Supervised Trainee = `+20`.

4. **Continuity & Handover-Cost Scoring (`s_cont`, `-15` to `+25` pts):**
   * Retaining `current_owner` (solo or as supervised trainee): `+25` if `handover_cost == "high"` and `complexity >= 0.70`; `+15` if `medium`; `+8` if `low`.
   * Additional `+10` retention bonus if `reassignment_count >= 2` *provided* `current_owner` has skill `>= 2` (prevents crossing the `>2` reassignment rework cliff, as in `C007`).
   * Changing owner when `handover_cost == "high"` incurs `-15`, overridden only when keeping a skill `1` owner threatens SLA breach (as in `C001`).

5. **Workload, Batching & Overtime Fairness (`s_fair`, `-20` to `+25` pts):**
   * Base score `= 20 * (r.hours_remaining / 6.5) - 1.2 * r.open_cases - 3.0 * batch_counts[r]`.
   * Penalty `-8` for `overtime_risk == "medium"`, `0` for `"low"`, and `-50` emergency penalty if `"high"` had to be unlocked for an urgent fallback.
   * **Batching bonus (`+12`):** Awarded when assigning an eligible `Quality Check` (`C006`, `C012`) to a level `3` Quality resource already holding or receiving another batch-eligible `Quality Check`.

6. **Training & Development Scoring (`s_train`, `0` to `20` pts):**
   * On `{normal, low}` cases with `sla_hours >= 18`: `+20` if candidate advances a documented `training_priority` while below the `20%` batch quota (`+12` once quota is met); `+10` if it develops a level-`1` preparer in a bottleneck skill (`Approval` or `Security`). `0` on urgent/high cases.

7. **Resilience & Bottleneck Avoidance (`s_resil`, `-15` to `+5` pts):**
   * Applies `-12` scarcity penalty when assigning a `Security=3` (`R7`, `R8`) or `Approval=3` (`R6`) specialist to a non-bottleneck activity (`Eligibility`, `Quality`, `Rework`), *unless* non-bottleneck specialists are unavailable or high-OT constrained (e.g., `R8_Hugo` absorbing `C014` Rework because `R4_David` and `R9_Iris` are in high-OT freeze).
   * Intra-team mentoring/handover bonus `+5` when trainee and mentor share `team`.

8. **Tie-Breaking & Exception Handling:**
   * Ties within `1.0` point are broken deterministically by: (1) preserving `current_owner`, (2) higher `hours_remaining`, (3) lower `open_cases`, (4) lexical resource ID.
   * If all compliant candidates violate `H5` (cap of 4) or `S1` (high OT), the policy never silently forces an unsafe match: urgent cases trigger a supervisor-approved high-OT override (`REASON: URGENT_OT_OVERRIDE`), while normal/low cases remain in the dispatch buffer until the next shift window (`REASON: DEFERRED_CAPACITY_PROTECTION`).

# 5. Explanation to workers

> ### How Work Routing Works on Our Team — Clear, Fair, and Built Around Context
>
> Our assignment tool is designed to support how we work together, not to chase raw speed at your expense. Whenever a case is routed to you—or moved to a colleague—there is a clear, visible reason recorded on the case card.
>
> **1. Keeping cases with the person who already knows the story**  
> Switching owners mid-stream forces everyone to re-read files and slows customers down. Whenever you already own a case and have the time and permissions to finish the next step, the system tries to keep it with you—especially on complex cases or cases that have already bounced around.
>
> **2. Protecting your end-of-day capacity**  
> If you are carrying a heavy open caseload or running short on hours today, the router pauses new non-urgent assignments to you and shifts waiting items to teammates with more breathing room. **Having a case reassigned away from your queue is never a penalty or a mark on your performance**—it is how we prevent involuntary overtime and protect urgent deadlines (for example, when a 3-hour Security check needs an available Risk specialist right away).
>
> **3. Making real room for growth—never "sink or swim"**  
> Everyone should get to build new skills without risking customer SLAs or feeling unsupported. When a case has a comfortable deadline, the router looks for matches with your Personal Training Priority and pairs you with a specialist mentor (such as co-reviewing a Diagnosis, preparing an Approval package, or shadowing a Security Review). Both the learner's and the mentor's effort count toward batch workload limits so mentoring isn't invisible extra labor.
>
> **4. Smart grouping on routine checks**  
> When two low-urgency Quality Checks have plenty of time left (`> 24h`), they may be grouped together for one reviewer so you don't lose focus switching contexts back and forth.
>
> **5. You stay in the loop (human override)**  
> Every assignment shows a plain-language tag (e.g., *Continuity kept*, *Workload relief*, *Paired training*, or *Urgent SLA specialist*). If a case turns out to be more complex than its score suggests, or if you need a quick peer consult, you can flag it to your team lead—overrides are logged to improve the routing rules, never to penalize you.

# 6. Process-mining audit indicators

Using the event log schema, governance reviews should track the following **10 process-mining indicators** continuously (comparing pre/post-deployment cohorts and team segments):

1. **Handover Frequency (per Case & Activity Pair):**
   * *Definition:* Mean number of distinct `org:resource` transitions along a trace (`pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network`), stratified by `case_complexity` (`>= 0.70` vs. `< 0.70`).
   * *Audit Goal:* Verify fewer unforced handovers on high-complexity cases (`handover_cost_band = "high"`) and quantify post-handover waiting time (`+2.7h` benchmark).
2. **High-Churn Reassignment Rate (`reassignment_count > 2`):**
   * *Definition:* Share of completed cases where `max(reassignment_count) > 2`, plus average `reassignment_count` per priority tier.
   * *Audit Goal:* Keep `>2` reassignment share declining; alert if workload-relief reassignments inadvertently push normal cases into chronic ping-pong loops.
3. **Skill Mismatch & Unsupervised Execution Rate:**
   * *Definition:* Join `org:resource` and `required_skill` against the historical skill matrix at event `time:timestamp`. Flag (a) any solo execution with `Skill = 0`, (b) `{high, urgent}` execution with `Skill < 2` lacking a logged supervisor event/co-owner, and (c) `Security` or `Approval` rule breaches.
   * *Audit Goal:* **0%** hard compliance breaches; track service-time inflation (`get_service_time`) when `Skill 1` vs. `Skill 3` executes solo.
4. **Workload Distribution Inequality:**
   * *Definition:* Gini coefficient and coefficient of variation of active `workload_snapshot` and daily assigned service hours across `org:resource` (accounting for both primary and mentor events).
   * *Audit Goal:* Detect hidden concentration on conscientious mid-tier workers (`R3`, `R5`, `R8`) or uncounted supervisory work.
5. **Overtime-Risk Exposure Rate:**
   * *Definition:* Proportion of assignment events routed to resources in the top quartile of `workload_snapshot` / flagged high-overtime windows, segmented by `case_priority` and `assignment_reason`.
   * *Audit Goal:* Confirm `0%` non-urgent routing to high-OT resources and verify whether emergency `URGENT_OT_OVERRIDE` events stay rare (`< 2%`).
6. **SLA Miss Rate & Buffer Erosion:**
   * *Definition:* Percentage of cases completing after `sla_deadline` (`time:timestamp > sla_deadline`), plus median remaining buffer at completion, broken down by `case_priority` and whether the case involved a handover or training pair.
   * *Audit Goal:* Ensure developmental pairings and batching (`Quality Check`) do not erode SLA compliance.
7. **Post-Assignment Rework & Appeal Rate:**
   * *Definition:* Share of cases exhibiting activity self-loops or backward edges in the DFG (e.g., returning to `Technical Diagnosis` or triggering `Rework Correction`), correlated with `assignment_reason`, `reassignment_count`, and skill level.
   * *Audit Goal:* Validate that keeping `current_owner` on cases like `C007` and escalating loops like `C003` actually lowers downstream rework.
8. **Documented Training Assignment Share:**
   * *Definition:* Percentage of `{normal, low}` priority events bearing `assignment_reason` in `{TRAINING_SUPERVISED, TRAINING_SHADOW}` mapped against each worker's `training_priority`, plus longitudinal progression of worker skill scores (`012`).
   * *Audit Goal:* Confirm sustained achievement of the `>= 20%` floor without crowding out certain teams (e.g., ensuring high-OT workers like `R2`/`R4`/`R9` eventually get workload relief so they too can access training).
9. **Specialist Bottleneck & Queueing Ratio:**
   * *Definition:* Ratio of waiting time to service time (`waiting_time / service_time` via `insert_case_service_waiting_time`) entering `Security Review` and `Final Approval`, combined with SNA centrality concentration on `R6`, `R7`, and `R8`.
   * *Audit Goal:* Measure whether cross-training (`R10` in Security, `R7` in Approval) reduces queue waiting times ahead of bottleneck activities over successive months.
10. **Policy Deviation & Manual Override Rate:**
    * *Definition:* Share of events where actual `org:resource` differs from the policy's recommended resource or carries `assignment_reason = MANUAL_OVERRIDE_*`.
    * *Audit Goal:* High override rates (`> 10–15%`) in a specific activity are treated not as worker non-compliance, but as a diagnostic signal that `case_complexity`, skill scores, or time estimates are miscalibrated.

# 7. PM4Py implementation sketch

The Python sketch below demonstrates how to ingest the process event log with **PM4Py**, enrich waiting/service times, mine organizational networks/roles/batches, inspect performance bottlenecks, and compute policy audit KPIs.

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Load & Format Event Log
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
raw_df = pd.read_csv("service_process_event_log.csv")
raw_df["sla_deadline"] = pd.to_datetime(raw_df["sla_deadline"], utc=True)

# Standardize PM4Py event-log columns (case ID, activity, timestamp)
log_df = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    raw_df,
    case_id="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    timest_format=None,  # ISO8601 auto-parsed
)

# If start timestamps exist (or lifecycle transitions), compute exact service & waiting times
log_df = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(
    log_df,
    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
)
avg_service_times = pm4py.get_service_time(
    log_df,
    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
)
print("Median/Mean Activity Service Times (sec):", avg_service_times)

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Resource Networks, Organizational Roles & Batch Discovery
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Audit Indicator 1: Handover-of-Work network (context-switching & ping-pong paths)
hw_metric = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(
    log_df,
    resource_key="org:resource",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
)
pm4py.save_vis_sna(hw_metric, "audit_handover_network.png")
# Interactive inspection option: pm4py.view_sna(hw_metric)

# Audit Indicator 8 & Mentoring Verification: Working-Together network
# Confirms trainees (e.g., R1_Amal, R10_Jonas) actually co-work cases with mentors (R3, R8)
wt_metric = pm4py.discover_working_together_network(
    log_df,
    resource_key="org:resource",
    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
)
pm4py.save_vis_sna(wt_metric, "audit_mentoring_co_work_network.png")

# Role & Skill Similarity Discovery:
# Detects empirical skill profiles and checks if cross-training widens bottleneck roles
res_similarity = pm4py.discover_activity_based_resource_similarity(
    log_df,
    resource_key="org:resource",
    activity_key="concept:name",
)
org_roles = pm4py.discover_organizational_roles(
    log_df,
    resource_key="org:resource",
    activity_key="concept:name",
)

# Audit Rule H6: Discover batch execution behavior and check Quality Check compliance
batches = pm4py.discover_batches(
    log_df,
    activity_key="concept:name",
    resource_key="org:resource",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
)
for act_res, batch_stats in batches:
    print(f"Detected batching pattern on {act_res}: {batch_stats}")

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Performance DFG, Variant Rework Loops & Attribute/Performance Filtering
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Slice high-complexity cases to calibrate the +2.7h handover waiting-time parameter
high_comp_df = log_df[log_df["case_complexity"] >= 0.70]
high_handover_df = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(
    high_comp_df,
    attribute_key="handover_cost_band",
    values=["high"],
    level="event",
    retain=True,
)

# Discover Performance DFG to spot queue buildup ahead of Security Review & Final Approval
perf_dfg, start_acts, end_acts = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(
    log_df,
    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
)

# Isolate slow tail cases (> 24 hours = 86,400 sec) and inspect rework loop variants
slow_cases_df = pm4py.filter_case_performance(
    log_df,
    min_performance=86400,
    max_performance=float("inf"),
    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
)
slow_variants = pm4py.get_variants(
    slow_cases_df,
    activity_key="concept:name",
    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
)

# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Policy Governance & Compliance KPI Audit Table
# -------------------------------------------------------------------------
def run_policy_governance_audit(df: pd.DataFrame, skill_matrix: pd.DataFrame) -> dict:
    # Join historical skill level per (org:resource, required_skill)
    merged = df.merge(
        skill_matrix,
        on=["org:resource", "required_skill"],
        how="left",
    )

    # Case-level outcomes
    case_end = merged.groupby("case:concept:name").agg(
        end_time=("time:timestamp", "max"),
        sla_deadline=("sla_deadline", "last"),
        max_reassignments=("reassignment_count", "max"),
        had_rework=("concept:name", lambda s: (s == "Rework Correction").any()),
    )
    sla_miss_rate = (case_end["end_time"] > case_end["sla_deadline"]).mean()
    high_churn_share = (case_end["max_reassignments"] > 2).mean()
    rework_rate_high_churn = case_end.loc[
        case_end["max_reassignments"] > 2, "had_rework"
    ].mean()

    # Event-level compliance checks
    is_supervised = merged["assignment_reason"].str.contains("SUPERVISED|SHADOW", na=False)
    unsupervised_priority_mismatch = merged[
        merged["case_priority"].isin(["high", "urgent"])
        & (merged["skill_level"] < 2)
        & (~is_supervised)
    ]

    # Training quota check on normal/low cases
    normal_low = merged[merged["case_priority"].isin(["normal", "low"])]
    training_share = normal_low["assignment_reason"].str.contains(
        "TRAINING_DOC_PRIORITY", na=False
    ).mean()

    # Policy override rate
    override_rate = merged["assignment_reason"].str.startswith(
        "MANUAL_OVERRIDE", na=False
    ).mean()

    return {
        "sla_miss_rate": round(float(sla_miss_rate), 4),
        "cases_gt_2_reassignments_share": round(float(high_churn_share), 4),
        "rework_rate_when_gt_2_reassignments": round(float(rework_rate_high_churn), 4),
        "unsupervised_high_prio_skill_violations": int(len(unsupervised_priority_mismatch)),
        "normal_low_documented_training_share": round(float(training_share), 4),
        "manual_override_rate": round(float(override_rate), 4),
    }
```
