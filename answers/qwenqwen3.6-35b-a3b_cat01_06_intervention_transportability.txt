## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Process Specification:**
- **Eligibility:** All cases enter parallel creation. Document Check is conditionally gated: if legally required, it waits for `Originals Receipt`; if digital, it starts immediately.
- **Concurrency:** `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt` are created simultaneously at `Register`. `Document Check` starts at `Originals Receipt` (or immediately if digital). `Legal Review` may start at `Assessment` completion where permissible.
- **Statutory Prerequisites:** `Originals Receipt` must precede `Document Check` when originals are legally required. No statutory constraint blocks `Identity Check` parallelism.
- **Queue & WIP Controls:** Max 4 concurrent open tasks per case. Dynamic WIP caps per pool; if utilization >85%, triage routes to parallel assessors.
- **Resource Responsibilities:** Dedicated pools remain. Assessors are cross-trained to handle initial verification triage. Legal reviewers operate independently.
- **Legal/Supplement Handling:** `Legal Review` runs in parallel with final verification where allowed. `Request Supplement` triggers a loop that restarts `Document Check` and `Assessment` without blocking other cases.
- **Logging:** Automated event timestamps enforced. Back-entry disabled. System logs task ownership and overlap intervals.

| transportability analysis | propensity score matching + subgroup consistency | validate transfer | external audit | treatment effect heterogeneity reports |

The pilot isolates mechanism failure from implementation failure by decoupling planned concurrency from actual concurrency. Process mining tracks event timestamps and task ownership to verify whether parallelization reduces true idle time or merely creates administrative overlap. If concurrency metrics align with design but cycle times do not improve, the failure is causal (mechanism): capacity constraints, legal loops, or originals delays block the critical path. If concurrency metrics diverge from design—evidenced by late event entry, task-lock workarounds, or queue starvation—the failure is operational (implementation). Stratified randomization and team-level assignment prevent interference, while continuous monitoring of p90 and rework distinguishes distributional shifts from systemic breakdowns. This design ensures that observed effects are attributed to either structural capacity limits or execution fidelity, enabling precise intervention refinement and preventing misguided scaling.
