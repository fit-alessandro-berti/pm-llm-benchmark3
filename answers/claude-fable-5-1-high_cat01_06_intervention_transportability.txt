## 1. Process-structural comparison

Preliminary arithmetic used throughout (Site B, 3,400 cases): originals legally required in 62% = 2,108 cases (matches `Originals Receipt -> Document Check` = 2,108); digital-eligible 38% = 1,292 cases; Legal Review 40% = 1,360 cases; `Legal Review -> Request Supplement` = 360 = 26.5% of legal cases = 10.6% of all cases, so roughly 6.4 percentage points of the 17% rework rate are non-supplement rework. `Identity Check -> Originals Receipt` = 1,020 means identity was already finished before originals arrived in about half of the originals cases; in the other half identity is done after originals arrive and therefore sits on the critical path together with Document Check. Note that `Register -> Identity Check` (2,050) + `Register -> Originals Receipt` (1,860) = 3,910 > 3,400, so the log contains duplicated Register events, same-timestamp ties, or back-entered events; the exact split must be re-derived from the raw log before any pilot baseline is frozen.

Shared mechanisms. The only mechanism that plausibly travels is the path `treatment_parallelization -> true_overlap -> verification_span -> cycle_time`: when two checks are both startable and both have capacity, the shorter one leaves the critical path. This is a structural fact about AND-splits and holds at any site. The AND-join before Assessment, the decision structure, and the downstream Notify step are also invariant.

Site-specific mechanisms. `originals_required -> originals_delay -> document_check_start` exists only at B and disables the shared mechanism for 62% of cases. The legal loop (`legal_review_required -> supplement_loop -> rework`) exists only at B and governs its tail. `IT_case_lock -> true_overlap` is a B-only suppressor, and `IT_case_lock -> timestamp_backentry` corrupts B's measurement layer. The co-interventions at A (`API_latency`, `queue_huddles`, added document capacity, mix shift through `case_complexity`) are separate causal paths that were bundled with the treatment; none is caused by parallelization.

Effect modifiers. `digital_documents`, `originals_required`, `IT_case_lock`, `role_specialization`, `document_capacity`, `assessment_capacity`, `legal_capacity`, `case_complexity`, and `legal_review_required` all sit on paths that change the sign or magnitude of the treatment effect: parallelization is worth up to the identity task plus its handoff wait when the document pool has slack, and roughly zero when the document or assessment pool is the binding constraint.

Why a similar diagram is not enough. Both process trees are sequences of the same labels, but the control-flow view abstracts away startability conditions (data-dependent prerequisites), resource pools, locking, and load. Transportability requires that the S-nodes pointing into the mechanism be either absent or adjusted for; at B, four of them (`S_originals_required`, `S_IT_case_lock`, `S_role_specialization`, `S_legal_requirement`) point directly into the effect path.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

Reasoning chain. Baseline Site B: median 44 h, p90 110 h, waiting before Assessment 20 h. The mechanical ceiling of identity ∥ document for a digital case is the identity service (3.4 h) plus the identity-to-document handoff wait; assuming a handoff wait of 1–4 h gives 4–7 h per digital case. For originals cases where identity currently runs after originals arrive (roughly 15–32% of all volume, exact figure to be re-derived), moving identity into the originals wait removes 3–5 h from the critical path. Eligible shares: digital 38%; originals-with-late-identity 15–32%; both together 53–70%. A pooled median moves by less than the subgroup effect; with 38% of cases improving by 4–7 h the pooled median moves by roughly 1.5–3 h, with 53–70% improving by 3–7 h it moves by roughly 2.5–4.5 h. The p90 at B is populated by originals-delay and supplement-loop cases that the intervention does not touch, so p90 movement without loop measures is small. Site A's rework drift (+0.6 pp) is taken as the quality prior for a well-implemented change; congestion and phantom tasks add to it.

Mapping notes. S1 applies Site A's parallelization component (2–5 h) scaled up slightly because B's handoff wait is likely longer, but it reaches only the startable subgroups. S2 restricts to the population that matches Site A's 90%-digital context; its per-case range is the direct analogue and its pooled effect is diluted by 0.38. S3 takes B's 20 h waiting before Assessment (worse than A-before at 18 h with fewer assessors) and B's document pool load ratio as evidence that the constraint sits downstream, so gains are relocated rather than realised. ## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

Specification.

- Eligibility rules. Stratum D (digital): `originals_required = false`; both verification tasks are created at Register. Stratum O (originals): `originals_required = true`; Identity Check is created at Register, the case enters a non-owning state `Awaiting Originals`, and Document Check is created only by the `Originals Receipt` event. Stratum L (legal): `public_sector OR high_value`; in stage 2 only, a time-boxed `Legal Completeness Pre-screen` is created at Register for cases in L∩O and runs during the originals wait.
- Concurrent tasks. Identity Check may execute concurrently with Document Check (stratum D) or with the originals wait and, in stage 2, with the pre-screen (stratum O). Nothing else runs concurrently. Assessment, Legal Review, decision, and Notify remain serial.
- Statutory prerequisites kept. Document Check starts only after Originals Receipt where originals are required. Assessment starts only after both checks are complete (including repeated checks after a supplement). Legal Review follows Assessment for all L cases and precedes the decision. Request Supplement forces repeat of Document Check and Assessment before the decision.
- Queue and WIP controls. Six open verification tasks per reviewer (imported from A); FIFO within stratum with an age-based escalation at 24 h; a cap on the Assessment queue (if more than 40 cases sync-complete and waiting, identity and document reviewers stop pulling new digital cases and the assessment lead is alerted); daily 10-minute queue huddle covering all four pools.
- Resource responsibilities. Identity reviewers own Identity Check only; document reviewers own Document Check and receive a structured identity-outcome flag when identity completes before them; assessors own Assessment; legal reviewers own Legal Review and, in stage 2, the pre-screen limited to 15 minutes per case; a process owner reviews fidelity metrics weekly.
- Legal-review and supplement loops. Supplement requests re-open Document Check with a "supplement" reason code and route back to Assessment then Legal Review; repeat instances are logged as new events on the same case, never overwritten. Pre-screen output is a checklist attached to the case so that the first Document Check addresses legal completeness.
- Logging requirements. Task creation, start, complete, reassignment, and lock-conflict events with system timestamps; a back-entry flag for any manually dated event; stratum attributes (`originals_required`, `legal_required`, `complexity`) recorded at Register; originals arrival timestamp from the mailroom system; pilot arm recorded at Register.

Partial-order specification (POWL-style).

```text
Atoms: R=Register, I=Identity Check, O=Originals Receipt, D=Document Check, A=Assessment,
       L=Legal Review, S=Request Supplement, Ap=Approve, Rj=Reject, N=Notify, PS=Legal Completeness Pre-screen (stage 2)

DocBranch  = PO{ XOR(O, tau_digital) < D }            # O selected iff originals_required
RedoBody   = PO{ S < D' < A' }                        # repeated document check and assessment
LegalBranch= XOR( LOOP(do = L, redo = RedoBody), tau_no_legal )   # selected iff public_sector or high_value
Decision   = XOR(Ap, Rj)

Top-level strict partial order P0 over {R, I, DocBranch, A, LegalBranch, Decision, N}:
  R < I,  R < DocBranch,  I < A,  DocBranch < A,  A < LegalBranch,  LegalBranch < Decision,  Decision < N
  I || DocBranch                                     # the only intended concurrency

Stage-2 extension for L∩O cases: add PS with  R < PS,  PS < D  (PS runs inside the originals wait; PS || I)
```

| Stop rules | Stop treatment arm if rework +3 pp sustained 4 weeks, p90 +10% in the treated stratum, back-entry >15%, or Assessment queue cap breached 3 days running; stop stage 2 if legal wait +20% | Protects quality and tail; prevents measurement-corrupted conclusions | Decision rights with process owner and legal lead | Automated alerts from event-log metrics |
| Transportability analysis | Estimate the T1 effect in D and compare with the 2–5 h component inferred at A; estimate T2−T1 to price co-interventions; use assignment as instrument for a complier-average effect given fidelity; effect-modification analysis by document-queue load and complexity; re-fit the S-node graph with observed B values | Tests whether the shared mechanism reproduces and quantifies site-specific modification | Pre-registered estimands | Stratified DFG/performance comparison; conformance to the POWL model; overlap validated on system timestamps only |

The pilot separates mechanism failure from implementation failure by measuring the manipulation, not only the outcome. Fidelity variables (system-timestamped true overlap, task creation within 10 minutes of Register, lock-conflict count, back-entry rate, WIP-cap compliance) are recorded for every treated case. If fidelity is high (true overlap above 60% in stratum D, back-entry below 5%) and the verification span does not fall, the mechanism has failed at B: the identity task was not on the critical path, or the document queue absorbed the saving; queue-length series by pool show which. If fidelity is low, the result is an implementation failure and says nothing about the mechanism; the complier-average effect estimated with random assignment as the instrument gives the effect among cases that actually received concurrency. A third pattern, verification span falls but Register-to-Notify does not, with waiting before Assessment rising, identifies bottleneck shift, which is neither mechanism nor implementation failure but a capacity constraint. Negative-control outcomes (originals delay, decision-to-notify) guard against secular trends, and the logging upgrade applied to all arms prevents the recording artefact that clouds Site A's overlap metric. Finally, comparing T2 with T1 prices the huddles and WIP cap separately, so B learns what part of A's headline was concurrency and what part was operational co-intervention.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
"""
Site A before/after as process trees -> accepting Petri nets.
Site B candidate as a POWL model (strict partial order + XOR + LOOP) -> accepting Petri net.
Written against the pm4py 2.7.x API (POWL classes are available from pm4py 2.7.5).
"""
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Site A process trees (as reported before and after the intervention)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
A_BEFORE = ("-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
            "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )")
A_AFTER = ("-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', "
           "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )")

tree_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(A_BEFORE)
tree_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(A_AFTER)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Convert both trees to accepting Petri nets
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
net_ab, im_ab, fm_ab = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_before)
net_aa, im_aa, fm_aa = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_after)


def report(name, net, im, fm):
    is_wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    sound, diagnostics = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)
    print(f"{name}: places={len(net.places)} transitions={len(net.transitions)} "
          f"arcs={len(net.arcs)} workflow_net={is_wf} sound={sound}")
    return is_wf, sound, diagnostics


report("Site A before", net_ab, im_ab, fm_ab)
report("Site A after ", net_aa, im_aa, fm_aa)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Site B candidate as POWL
#    Structure (see Section 6):
#      P0 = { Register, Identity Check, DocBranch, Assessment, LegalBranch, Decision, Notify }
#      DocBranch   = PO{ XOR(Originals Receipt, tau) < Document Check }
#      LegalBranch = XOR( LOOP(Legal Review, PO{ Request Supplement < Document Check < Assessment }), tau )
#      Decision    = XOR(Approve, Reject)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
register = Transition(label="Register")
identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")
originals = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
skip_originals = SilentTransition()               # digital case: no originals step
document = Transition(label="Document Check")
assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")
legal = Transition(label="Legal Review")
supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
document_redo = Transition(label="Document Check")  # repeated check after a supplement
assessment_redo = Transition(label="Assessment")    # repeated assessment after a supplement
skip_legal = SilentTransition()                   # case without legal-review requirement
approve = Transition(label="Approve")
reject = Transition(label="Reject")
notify = Transition(label="Notify")

# Document branch: statutory prerequisite preserved (Originals Receipt before Document Check when required)
originals_choice = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[originals, skip_originals])
doc_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[originals_choice, document])
doc_branch.order.add_edge(originals_choice, document)

# Supplement loop: Legal Review; optionally (Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment) then Legal Review again
redo_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[supplement, document_redo, assessment_redo])
redo_body.order.add_edge(supplement, document_redo)
redo_body.order.add_edge(document_redo, assessment_redo)
legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[legal, redo_body])
legal_branch = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[legal_loop, skip_legal])

decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[approve, reject])

site_b = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, identity, doc_branch, assessment,
                                   legal_branch, decision, notify])
site_b.order.add_edge(register, identity)
site_b.order.add_edge(register, doc_branch)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Assessment waits for BOTH verification branches (AND-join); identity || doc_branch is the only concurrency
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
site_b.order.add_edge(identity, assessment)
site_b.order.add_edge(doc_branch, assessment)
site_b.order.add_edge(assessment, legal_branch)
site_b.order.add_edge(legal_branch, decision)
site_b.order.add_edge(decision, notify)

# Sanity checks on the partial order (transitive closure, irreflexivity)
for po in (doc_branch, redo_body, site_b):
    if hasattr(po.order, "add_transitive_edges"):
        po.order.add_transitive_edges()
    if hasattr(po.order, "is_strict_partial_order"):
        assert po.order.is_strict_partial_order(), "order relation is not a strict partial order"

print("\nSite B POWL model:")
print(site_b)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. Convert the POWL model to an accepting Petri net
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_b)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 6. Workflow-net and soundness checks
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
is_wf_b, sound_b, diag_b = report("Site B POWL  ", net_b, im_b, fm_b)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 7. Playout: sample traces from each model
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
def sample_traces(net, im, fm, n=6, max_len=40):
    log = pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm, noTraces=n, maxTraceLength=max_len)
    out = []
    for i, trace in enumerate(log):
        if i >= n:
            break
        out.append([e["concept:name"] for e in trace])
    return out


print("\nSample traces, Site A after:")
for t in sample_traces(net_aa, im_aa, fm_aa, n=3):
    print("  ", " -> ".join(t))

print("\nSample traces, Site B candidate:")
for t in sample_traces(net_b, im_b, fm_b, n=8):
    print("  ", " -> ".join(t))

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 8. What these models cannot encode
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# - Data-dependent routing: the XOR between 'Originals Receipt' and tau, and between the legal loop and tau,
#   is a free choice in the model. In reality it is determined by case attributes (originals_required,
#   public_sector, high_value). Conformance checking against the model will therefore accept a digital
#   route for an originals case; the eligibility rule must be checked with data-aware conformance or filters.
# - Resource capacity and roles: 4 identity, 7 document, 3 assessors, 2 legal reviewers, the six-task WIP cap,
#   and the fact that roles cannot substitute for each other are invisible. The Petri net allows unbounded
#   concurrent cases; queueing, bottleneck shift, and interference between pilot arms need a simulation
#   with resource pools or empirical performance analysis, not this control-flow model.
# - Case locking: the legacy system's single-owner lock is a resource/IT constraint that may prevent the
#   modelled concurrency (identity || document) from occurring; the model states what is permitted, not
#   what the system allows.
# - Durations and delays: originals arrival delay (median 18 h, heavy tail), service times, and waiting
#   times are not represented; playout traces are order-only and equiprobable across choices, which is
#   not the observed 62%/40%/26.5% branching.
# - Repeated activities are modelled by separate transitions with the same label ('Document Check',
#   'Assessment' in the redo body); alignment-based conformance will handle this, but the label
#   duplication means the Petri net is not a free-choice, label-unique net.
# - The redo body assumes Legal Review is repeated after a supplement; if B's practice sends the repeated
#   assessment straight to a decision, the loop should be redefined with the redo body ending the loop
#   instead of returning to Legal Review.
```

## 9. Recommendation

Decision: `conditional go`.

What should be transferred is the mechanism, not the metric. The transferable content of Site A's experience is that when two verification tasks are both startable and the pool serving the shorter task has slack, moving the shorter task off the critical path saves roughly its service time plus the handoff wait, which at Site A was plausibly 2–5 hours of the 9-hour headline. The cheap-to-import operational components are the six-task WIP cap, daily queue huddles, system-generated timestamps for concurrent tasks, and adding capacity where load is heaviest.

What should not be copied is the literal proposal "create both tasks after Register for every case". For 62% of Site B's volume Document Check cannot legally start until originals arrive, so the task would be phantom WIP inviting partial reviews and re-checks. Nor should Site B expect the 9-hour or 18-hour reductions: those include an easier case mix, two extra document reviewers, an API upgrade, and a measurement change that will not recur, while Site B has an assessment queue (20 h, three assessors) and a legal loop the intervention does not touch.

The digital, non-legal stratum should enter first (roughly a fifth of volume, 38% × 60%, less if channel and legal requirement are correlated). It is the closest analogue to Site A's population and the cleanest test of whether the mechanism reproduces under specialised roles and the case lock. The originals stratum follows with the different, lawful concurrency of identity work inside the originals wait. Legal cases are included only in stage 2 with the completeness pre-screen.

Expected benefits are a 4–7 hour reduction in the digital stratum, 2.5–4.5 hours pooled if both lawful concurrencies work, and a 4–12 hour p90 gain only if the supplement loop is reduced. Risks are bottleneck shift to assessment, phantom tasks, back-entered timestamps that fabricate an effect, and rework drift as at Site A.

Expansion trigger: true overlap above 60% in treated digital cases with verification span down at least 3 hours, waiting before Assessment not rising, rework within 1 point of control after 450 cases per arm. Modification trigger: fidelity achieved but span unchanged (address document capacity), or span down while waiting before Assessment rises (address assessors). Abandonment trigger: rework up 3 points or p90 up 10% for four weeks, or back-entry above 15% after the IT fix.

## 10. Assumption register

1. Identity and document checks at Site B use largely independent information — why needed: parallel execution is only safe if the document check does not depend on identity findings — how to test: audit 100 document checks for use of identity outcomes; compare verification-failure rework between arms — consequence if false: parallelization raises rework and appeals, as hinted at Site A.
2. The case-management lock can be reconfigured to allow two active owners or a non-owning "awaiting originals" state — why needed: without it no true overlap occurs — how to test: two-week technical dry run measuring lock-conflict events and back-entry rate — consequence if false: implementation failure; only the WIP cap and huddles can be transferred.
3. The document pool is not fully saturated at current volume — why needed: the saving from removing identity from the critical path is realised only if document reviewers can start sooner — how to test: document queue wait and reviewer utilisation from the log, and the control-arm queue during the pilot — consequence if false: bottleneck shift with no span reduction (scenario S3).
4. Assessment capacity can absorb earlier, burstier arrivals — why needed: otherwise waiting before Assessment grows and cancels the gain — how to test: queue-length series before Assessment by arm; pre-period 20 h baseline — consequence if false: net cycle-time change near zero; assessor capacity becomes the intervention.
5. Site A's parallelization component is 2–5 hours of the 9-hour median change — why needed: sets the prior for Site B's digital stratum and the pilot's detectable effect — how to test: obtain A's case-level log and stratify by complexity, team, and period; estimate identity-to-document handoff wait — consequence if false (smaller): the pilot is underpowered and expectations must be lowered; if larger, expansion can be faster.
6. Channel (`originals_required`) and legal requirement are recorded reliably at Register and are only weakly correlated — why needed: eligibility and stratification depend on them; the first-wave stratum size depends on their overlap — how to test: attribute completeness and cross-tabulation on the last six months — consequence if false: first-wave volume is too small for power, or cases are misrouted.
7. The Site B DFG inconsistencies (Register out-degree 3,910 for 3,400 cases) reflect timestamp ties and back-entry rather than genuinely different process behaviour — why needed: the baseline and overlap metrics rest on the log — how to test: re-extract the raw log, inspect duplicate Register events and same-timestamp ties — consequence if false: baseline is wrong and pre/post comparisons are invalid until re-derived.
8. A time-boxed legal completeness pre-screen can reduce supplement requests by 25–40% without lengthening legal review — why needed: it is the only lever in the package that reaches Site B's tail — how to test: stage-2 randomization within legal-plus-originals cases, tracking supplement rate and legal wait — consequence if false: p90 gains do not materialise and the pre-screen is withdrawn to protect the two legal reviewers.
