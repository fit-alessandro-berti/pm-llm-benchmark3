## 5. Fairness and feedback-loop risks
* Value-based prioritization can starve low-value SME cases. Safeguard: urgency multiplier and a minimum share quota per segment, e.g. 20% of swarms to SME/Public Sector.
* Non-Native-Language Support: sparse calibration and effect estimates. Safeguard: confidence penalty 0.7 in score, require human review, and cap share until sample grows.
* Differential calibration: SME under-prediction  true risk higher than shown; Strategic Enterprise over-prediction  over-selection. Safeguard: segment-adjusted `p_adj` in eligibility and monthly recalibration.
* Selective intervention changes training labels: intervened cases have artificially lowered breach rates, causing future under-prediction if intervention flag is not included as a treatment covariate. Safeguard: always record `swarmed` and `swarm time` in the label history and train with inverse-propensity weighting.
* Spillover harms fall systematically on untreated diagnosis cases, which may be lower-value. Safeguard: monitor distribution of spillover by segment/value, add spillover penalty to score, and rotate reserve slot to protect heavily loaded queue periods.
