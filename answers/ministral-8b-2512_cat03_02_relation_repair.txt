# **Conservative and Alternative Repairs for the OCEL 2.0 Dataset**

This analysis proposes repairs for ambiguous relations in the given dataset while preserving uncertainty and avoiding over-repair. The approach balances **lifecycle consistency**, **cardinality constraints**, and **downstream modeling effects** (e.g., OC-DFG, OTG, ET–OT graphs).

## **1. Consistency Rules and Evidence Model**

### **Hard Invariants**
1. **Create Order**  **exactly one Order** (ev01, ev02).
2. **Add Item**  **one Order + one Item** (ev03–ev05).
3. **Pack Item**  **one Item + one Package** (ev06–ev08), but ev08 lacks a Package (scanner outage).
4. **Dispatch Package**  **one Package + one Delivery** (ev09–ev10), but ev10 is ambiguous.
5. **Confirm Delivery**  **may reference one or more Deliveries** (ev11).
6. **Issue Invoice**  **one Invoice + one or more Orders** (ev12), but INV-44 only bills ORD-A.
7. **Record Payment**  **one Payment + one Invoice** (ev13–ev14), but PAY-2 (130) does not match INV-44 (230).

### **Strong Defaults**
- **E2O relations** are prioritized over O2O inferences (e.g., `Pack Item`  `PK-7 packs IT-A1` is stronger than inferring it from `ev06`).
- **Timestamp order** suggests causality (e.g., `ev06`  `ev09`  `ev11`).
- **Identifier prefixes** (e.g., `PK7-1`, `PK8-1`) imply grouping.

### **Weak Heuristics**
- **Amount matching** (PAY-1 = 100, PAY-2 = 130) suggests partial payment, but INV-44 = 230.
- **Route consistency** (PK-7, PK-8, DEL-X, DEL-Y all `NORTH`) suggests DEL-X may carry PK-8.
- **Customer alignment** (ORD-A, ORD-B both `C17`) suggests INV-44 may cover both.

### **Evidence Against a Repair**
- **ev10** lacks a `carrier_ref` (only `N-421?`), making it ambiguous.
- **ev11** uses `N-42*` (wildcard), but DEL-X = `N-420`, DEL-Y = `N-421`.
- **PAY-2 (130)** does not match INV-44 (230), suggesting partial payment or another invoice.

## **3. Alternative Repair Bundles**

### **Bundle A: Conservative/Minimum-Change Repair**
- **ev08 & PK-8**: Leave `ev08` unresolved (no PK-8). Assume `IT-B1` was packed but not recorded.
- **ev10 & DEL-X/DEL-Y**: Assign `ev10` to `DEL-Y` (since `PK-8` was packed but not dispatched to `DEL-X`).
- **ev11 & Delivery**: Confirm only `DEL-X` (since `N-42*` is ambiguous but `DEL-X` is explicitly linked).
- **INV-44 & ORD-B**: Do not link (only `ORD-A` is explicitly billed).
- **PAY-2 & INV-44**: Do not link (amount mismatch).

**Lifecycle Implications**:
- `ORD-B` remains uninvoiced (plausible if partial shipment).
- `PAY-2` remains unassigned (could be for another order or future invoice).

### **Bundle B: Plausible Alternative Interpretation**
- **ev08 & PK-8**: Assume `PK-8` was created but mislabeled in `ev08` (infer `PK-8 packs IT-B1`).
- **ev10 & DEL-X/DEL-Y**: Assign `ev10` to `DEL-X` (bulk dispatch).
- **ev11 & Delivery**: Confirm both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y` (wildcard `N-42*`).
- **INV-44 & ORD-B**: Link `INV-44` to both `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` (total = 230, but `ORD-A` = 130 + 100 = 230, `ORD-B` = 100).
- **PAY-2 & INV-44**: Assume partial payment (130/230).

**Lifecycle Implications**:
- `ORD-B` is now invoiced (but total does not match).
- `PAY-2` is linked to `INV-44` (partial payment).

## **8. Over-Repair Warnings**

1. **Forcing `PAY-2` to `INV-44`** could misrepresent partial payments.
2. **Linking `INV-44` to `ORD-B`** assumes both orders are billed together, which may not be true.
3. **Inferring `PK-8` from `ev08`** assumes no scanner outage, breaking `Pack Item` rule.
4. **Assigning `ev10` to `DEL-X`** ignores `DEL-Y` possibility, missing a delivery.
5. **Confirming both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y` in `ev11`** may split a single delivery confirmation.

---
**Conclusion**: The repairs preserve uncertainty while ensuring lifecycle consistency. Bundle A is conservative, while Bundle B introduces plausible alternatives. Downstream models should account for unresolved relations to avoid false process behavior.
