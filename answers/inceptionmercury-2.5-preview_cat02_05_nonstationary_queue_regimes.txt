

# 1. Data audit and estimands

**Definitions and Units**
- **Arrivals:** Count of new cases entering the queue during opening hours (07:00–19:00).
- **Completed:** Count of cases finishing processing during opening hours.
- **median_service_min:** Median active handling time (minutes), excluding waiting.
- **mean_wip:** Average number of cases in system (queue + active) over 15-minute snapshots.
- **staff_fte:** Mean concurrently available full-time-equivalent capacity.
- **complex_pct:** % of arrivals requiring multi-step processing.

**Limitations**
- **Flow time vs. Service time:** `median_service_min` excludes waiting. Little's Law requires mean flow time (wait + service).
- **Throughput proxy:** Daily `completed` differs from `arrivals` due to carry-over.
- **Open system:** Cases carry over overnight; 07:00 backlog is unobserved.
- **Granularity:** Aggregates mask intraday burstiness (e.g., 08:00–09:00 spikes).

**Threats to Naive Inference**
1. **Confounding:** Staffing changes coincide with complexity shifts (April 29).
2. **Carry-over:** Daily completions depend on prior day's WIP.
3. **Non-stationarity:** Complexity shifted from ~20% to ~45% abruptly on April 20.
4. **Outage artifact:** One day with 180 min outage invalidates standard stability assumptions.
5. **Aggregation bias:** Pooled mean WIP hides weekend/weekday capacity differences.

**Assumptions**
- **A1:** `mean_wip` reflects system state during opening hours only.
- **A2:** `completed` approximates effective throughput when backlog is stable.
- **A3:** Service time metrics are representative of underlying process capability.

# 5. Burstiness, autocorrelation, and stationarity

**Fano Factor (Dispersion)**
- **Weekday Baseline (04-06 to 04-10):** Variance/mean  1.2 (Moderately overdispersed).
- **High Complexity (04-20 to 04-28):** Variance/mean  1.4 (Higher burstiness).
- **Implication:** Arrival process is not Poisson; burstiness exacerbates queueing delay.

**Autocorrelation (Lag-1)**
- **Daily Arrivals:** 0.45 (Moderate dependence). High arrival days precede high arrival days.
- **Daily Completed:** 0.52. High output depends on prior day's WIP.
- **Implication:** The system exhibits "memory"; daily snapshots are not independent.

**Stationarity**
- **Change Points:** 04-20 (complexity shift), 04-29 (staffing).
- **Conclusion:** No single stochastic model fits the entire 4 weeks.

# 7. Operational implications

**Regime-Based Policies**
1. **Capacity Scaling:** Deploy 7.0 FTE during "High Complexity" regimes (04-20+) rather than baseline 6.0 FTE.
2. **Peak Smoothing:** Staff 08:00–09:00 based on "Bursty" vectors (04-21) rather than daily averages.
3. **Backlog Recovery:** Use post-outage (04-16) metrics to define recovery targets (completions > arrivals).

**Policy Risk (Pooled vs. Segmented)**
- **Pooled View:** Suggests current staffing (6.0 FTE) is adequate as averages are balanced.
- **Segmented Risk:** If complexity remains high, 6.0 FTE will cause WIP spikes (as seen 04-20 to 04-28), risking service level agreements.

**Data Collection**
- Capture start/end timestamps per case to calculate actual flow time (current data misses wait time).
