# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Demand and service assumptions

**A1 (labelled).** Arrivals within each two-hour block are treated as a homogeneous Poisson process with rate = `expected_arrivals / 120` per minute. Within-block mix (priority × type) is multinomial with the given percentages. This is needed because only block expectations are supplied.

**A2.** Ordinary and complex-tail service times are drawn independently per activity as Uniform on the stated ranges; with probability \(p_{\text{tail}}\) the complex range is used. Resource multipliers scale the drawn base time: \(T = T_{\text{base}} \times m_{r,a}\).

**A3.** Expected base service (before multiplier) uses range midpoints:
\[
\mathbb{E}[T] \approx (1-p)\frac{a+b}{2} + p\frac{c+d}{2}.
\]
Full simulation (Section 8) samples uniforms; load analysis uses midpoints.

**A4 (ME/ES parallelism).** Under specialization and hybrid default, ME/ES run **sequentially** on one eligible resource when a cross-skilled worker is assigned, avoiding the 8 min coordination penalty; parallel mode is used only when (i) policy allows it, (ii) two distinct qualified resources are free within a short wait threshold (policy parameter, default 5 min), and (iii) critical-path estimate favors parallel after +8 min coordination. Sequential order: Mechanical before Electrical for ME; Electrical before Software for ES.

**A5.** Preemption: only P1 may preempt a lower-priority diagnostic activity; at most one preemption per case; restart penalty = 10% of remaining active service at interrupt. Intake and Validation are not preempted (assumption needed for tractability; not contradicted by the brief).

**A6.** Handover: +7 min when resource changes between Intake / diagnostic / Validation. +5 min extra when the case crosses mechanical  electrical/software teams unless the same cross-skilled resource retains it. Setup: +5 min when a resource switches diagnostic skill family between consecutive cases.

**A7.** Shifts and breaks as tabled; overtime  30 min past shift end. Work not started by end of overtime waits until next day (out of scope for same-day SLA clock except as open work). Farah works 08:00–12:00 only.

**A8.** SLA clock is elapsed time from arrival to Validation completion. Same-day horizon 08:00–20:30 for capacity accounting.

## Decision variables

- Routing/eligibility (who may do which activity)
- Priority score and preemption rights
- P1 capacity reservation
- Parallel vs sequential branch rule and threshold
- Overflow / cross-skill triggers
- Queue discipline (FCFS within priority, or SERPT-like)

## Objectives (at least two)

**O1 — Tail/SLA risk (primary robustness objective).**  
Minimize a weighted SLA shortfall plus tail delay:
\[
J_{\text{SLA}} = \sum_{s \in \{U0..U5\}} w_s \Big[
5\cdot(0.90 - \widehat{\text{P1\_SLA}}_s)^+ +
2\cdot(0.90 - \widehat{\text{P2\_SLA}}_s)^+ +
1\cdot(0.90 - \widehat{\text{P3\_SLA}}_s)^+ +
0.01\cdot \widehat{p90\_delay}_s
\Big]
\]
with equal scenario weights \(w_s = 1/6\) unless noted. “Robust”  low \(J_{\text{SLA}}\) and no scenario with P1_SLA collapse below ~70%.

**O2 — Fairness-aware efficiency.**  
Minimize mean delay subject to max individual utilization  90% in base, and utilization Gini (or maxmin utilization gap) below a threshold; penalize handover/setup minutes per case.

Secondary: resilience = degradation of P1_SLA from base to worst stress scenario (smaller drop is better).

# 3. Candidate policies

## Policy P-Spec — Strict specialization, fixed queues

- **Eligibility:** Intake  {Ada, Ben, Chen, Deepa, Emil, Farah} by shift only, FCFS.  
  M-queue  {Ada, Ben, Deepa} (Farah if M and morning).  
  E-queue  {Chen, Deepa} primary; Ada only if multiplier accepted and others busy >15 min (still “specialized” overflow off). *Strict mode:* E  Chen, Deepa only; Ada/Emil not used for E to protect their other work — actually strict means named primary skills: Ada/Ben Mechanical; Chen Electrical+Software preference split 50/50 static; Deepa E preferred over M; Emil Software preferred; Farah any morning short jobs.  
  **Operational rule (strict):** each diagnostic family has a dedicated queue; resource works only primary skill: Ben=M, Ada=M (E only if M queue empty), Chen=E then S, Deepa=E then M, Emil=S then E, Farah=shortest queue morning.  
- **Priority:** P1 > P2 > P3 within queue; no cross-queue steal.  
- **Reservation:** none.  
- **Preemption:** P1 may preempt P2/P3 on same resource’s diagnostic only.  
- **Parallelization:** never (sequential ME/ES on one resource if that resource has both skills; else hand to second queue).  
- **Handover minimization:** prefer same resource for Validation if skilled and free.  
- **Overflow:** none (fixed queues).

## Policy P-Pool — Full pooling, transparent dispatch

- **Eligibility:** any resource with finite multiplier for the activity.  
- **Priority score** (lower first):  
  \( s = \alpha \cdot \text{prio_weight} + \beta \cdot \text{expected_remaining} + \gamma \cdot \text{wait_so_far} \),  
  with prio_weight P1=0, P2=100, P3=300; \(\alpha=1,\beta=0.5,\gamma=-0.3\) (aging).  
  Dispatch: among idle eligible, minimize \( m_{r,a} \times \hat{T} + h_{\text{handover}} + h_{\text{setup}} \) (transparent greedy).  
- **Reservation:** none.  
- **Preemption:** P1 preempts non-P1 diagnostic if only eligible busy resources are on lower priority and expected P1 wait > 15 min.  
- **Parallelization:** ME/ES parallel if two eligible free within 5 min and  
  \(\max(T_1,T_2)+8 < T_1+T_2\) (usually true) and both teams available.  
- **Overflow:** N/A (already full pool).  
- **Handover:** explicit cost in assignment score.

## Policy P-Hybrid — Reserved P1 capacity + controlled cross-skill overflow

- **Eligibility:** primary teams as P-Spec, plus overflow.  
- **P1 reservation:** keep at least one “fast” resource path:  
  - Before 12:00: reserve Ada or Ben preference for incoming P1 (token: 1 P1-ready mechanical slot).  
  - Electrical/Software P1: Chen (or Emil after 12) not assigned P3 if any P1 E/S waiting or predicted within 20 min (simple arrival-rate look-ahead).  
- **Overflow triggers:**  
  1. P1 wait for primary > 10 min  any eligible including slow multipliers.  
  2. P2 wait > 45 min or queue length  3  cross-skill overflow.  
  3. P3 overflow only if donor util < 75% and no P1/P2 primary work.  
- **Priority score:** P1 absolute head-of-line on assigned resource; else FCFS within priority.  
- **Preemption:** P1 preempts P3 always if needed; preempts P2 only if P2 remaining > 20 min and P1 SLA risk (elapsed > 60 min). Max one preemption.  
- **Parallelization:** threshold-based — parallel ME/ES for P1 always when second resource free; for P2 if both free and coordination 8 min < 0.3 × sequential sum; P3 sequential to save coordination and staff.  
- **Handover minimization:** sticky resource — after Intake, prefer Intake resource for first diagnostic skill if qualified; Validation sticky to last diagnostic resource.  
- **Setup control:** prefer not to switch skill family unless overflow trigger fires.

## Optional fourth (mentioned in comparison): P-SERPT  
Pool like P-Pool but order by shortest expected remaining processing time within priority class — useful under U4 variance; higher gaming risk (Section 6).

# 5. Robust recommendation

**Primary policy: P-Hybrid** (reserved P1 capacity + controlled cross-skill overflow + sticky handovers + threshold parallelization for P1/P2).

**Why it best serves O1 (and acceptable O2).**  
- Protects P1 under U1/U5 via reservation and selective preemption without P-Spec’s immobility.  
- Overflow triggers unlock pooling when queues prove stress, capturing most of P-Pool’s flexibility.  
- Sticky assignment and skill-family stability cut handover/setup versus full pool — material when multipliers already inflate service.  
- Parallelization limited to where SLA value is high avoids Validation pile-up from excess coordination and dual-resource lock-in.  
- Utilization fairness stays nearer “Good” in base (respects 90% fragility guideline better than P-Spec’s Chen/Emil hotspot).

**Fallback mode: “Emergency Pool” (P-Pool rules)**  
**Triggers (any):**  
1. Chen or Emil unexpected absence (U3-like);  
2. Rolling 60 min Intake queue > 6 or P1 median wait > 20 min;  
3. Two consecutive blocks with predicted E-skill offered load > 0.95 × on-shift E capacity;  
4. IT outage cleared with backlog > 8 cases.

**Return to Hybrid when:** for 90 min, P1 wait p50 < 10 min and no resource util > 95%, and absent staff restored.

**When another policy is preferable.**  
- **P-Pool** if the workforce is highly cross-trained, handover is systemically reduced (same IT workspace), and audit wants one score rule — or after skill-depth hire on Software.  
- **P-Spec** only for training days or if gaming/estimate manipulation under pool scores becomes severe (Section 6).  
- **P-SERPT variant** under sustained U4-type long-tail days if estimators are trusted.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates:** Pool/SERPT scores using “expected remaining” invite under-reporting complexity. Mitigate with historical posterior service by (activity, type, resource), not self-report.  
- **P3 starvation:** Hybrid overflow denies P3 when donors busy; long P3 tails after 16:00. Mitigate with P3 aging boost after 4 h and end-of-day sweep on Emil/Deepa.  
- **Skill erosion:** Specialization atrophy if Hybrid always overflows S to Emil; rotate non-P1 S work to Chen deliberately.  
- **Excessive preemption:** Restart 10% remaining + setup can destroy P2 productivity in U1. Cap: one preemption per resource per hour; prefer redirect P1 to idle overflow.  
- **Coordination overhead:** Parallel ME/ES +8 min and dual busy resources can create **Validation** as new bottleneck (pool under U2). Monitor Validation queue separately.  
- **Fairness:** Farah’s short shift and slow mult (1.4) can dump hard jobs on her under naive pool — Hybrid should assign Farah short Intake/Validation preferentially.  
- **Human interpretability:** Hybrid triggers (10 min P1, 45 min P2, queue3) are auditable; pure weighted scores need a dashboard explanation string per assignment.  
- **Overtime fragility:** 30 min OT insufficient for U5 backlog — policy must include explicit deferral and next-day P1 carry with warm handover note (process design).

    s = SERVICE[act]

            m, e, s, me, es = mix
            e *= 2
            es *= 2
            mix = np.array([m, e, s, me, es], dtype=float)
            mix /= mix.sum()
        else:
            mix = mix / mix.sum()
        blocks.append(dict(start=start, end=end, n=n, p1=p1, p2=p2, p3=p3, mix=mix))
    return blocks


def sample_arrivals(scenario: str, rng: np.random.Generator):
    cases = []
    cid = 0
    types = ["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"]
    absent = set()
    if scenario in ("U3", "U5"):
        absent.add("Chen")
    intake_block = None
    if scenario == "U5":
        intake_block = (90.0, 120.0)  # 09:30-10:00 from 08:00
    tail_mult = 2.0 if scenario == "U4" else 1.0

    for b in scenario_blocks(scenario):
        n = rng.poisson(b["n"])
        for _ in range(n):
            t = float(rng.uniform(b["start"], b["end"]))
            u = rng.random()
            if u < b["p1"]:
                pr = "P1"
            elif u < b["p1"] + b["p2"]:
                pr = "P2"
            else:
                pr = "P3"
            ctype = types[int(rng.choice(5, p=b["mix"]))]
            cases.append(dict(
                id=f"C{cid:04d}", arr=t, priority=pr, ctype=ctype,
                tail_mult=tail_mult, intake_block=intake_block
            ))
            cid += 1
    cases.sort(key=lambda x: x["arr"])
    return cases, absent

# 3. Lightweight DES
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
@dataclass
order: bool = True
class ActivityExec:
    case_id: str
    activity: str
    priority: str
    remaining: float
    total_service: float
    resource: Optional[str] = None
    preempted: bool = False
    start_t: Optional[float] = None


@dataclass
class CaseState:
    id: str
    arr: float
    priority: str
    ctype: str
    tail_mult: float
    intake_block: Optional[Tuple[float, float]]
    stage: str = "intake_wait"  # pipeline stage
    last_resource: Optional[str] = None
    last_team: Optional[str] = None
    diag_queue: List[str] = field(default_factory=list)
    parallel: bool = False
    done_diag: set = field(default_factory=set)
    completion: Optional[float] = None
    preempt_count: int = 0
    handover_burden: float = 0.0
    setup_burden: float = 0.0
    coord_burden: float = 0.0


class Simulator:
    def __init__(self, policy: str, scenario: str, seed: int = 0):
        self.policy = policy
        self.scenario = scenario
        self.rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
        self.cases_raw, self.absent = sample_arrivals(scenario, self.rng)
        self.resources = [r for r in SKILL if r not in self.absent]
        self.busy_until: Dict[str, float] = {r: 0.0 for r in self.resources}
        self.busy_job: Dict[str, Optional[ActivityExec]] = {r: None for r in self.resources}
        self.last_skill_fam: Dict[str, Optional[str]] = {r: None for r in self.resources}
        self.util_busy: Dict[str, float] = {r: 0.0 for r in self.resources}
        self.events: List[dict] = []
        self.cases: Dict[str, CaseState] = {}
        self.wait_queues: Dict[str, List[str]] = {
            "Intake": [], "Mechanical": [], "Electrical": [], "Software": [], "Validation": []
        }
        # pending parallel partners
        self.metrics_handover = []
        self.preempt_events = 0

    def eligible(self, act: str, t: float, for_priority: str) -> List[str]:
        out = []
        for r in self.resources:
            if SKILL[r][act] is None:
                continue
            if not resource_available(r, t) and not (self.busy_job[r] and self.busy_job[r].case_id):
                # allow finish OT already started
                if t >= SHIFTS[r][1] + OT_LIMIT or t < SHIFTS[r][0]:
                    continue
            out.append(r)
        if self.policy == "P-Spec":
            # primary specialization filters
            primary = {
                "Mechanical": ["Ben", "Ada", "Deepa", "Farah"],
                "Electrical": ["Chen", "Deepa", "Emil", "Ada"],
                "Software": ["Emil", "Chen", "Farah"],
                "Intake": list(self.resources),
                "Validation": list(self.resources),
            }
            pref = [r for r in primary.get(act, out) if r in out]
            # strict: only primary if any available soon; else empty forces wait
            if act in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                # Farah only if morning for diagnostics in strict
                pref = [r for r in pref if not (r == "Farah" and t >= 240 and act != "Intake")]
                # Ada electrical only if no Chen/Deepa in list for strict primary first
                if act == "Electrical":
                    core = [r for r in pref if r in ("Chen", "Deepa")]
                    if core:
                        pref = core + [r for r in pref if r not in core]
                if act == "Software":
                    core = [r for r in pref if r in ("Emil", "Chen")]
                    if core:
                        pref = core
                return [r for r in pref if r in self.resources]
            return pref
        if self.policy == "P-Hybrid":
            # primary first; overflow added by caller via flag
            return out
        return out

    def assignment_cost(self, r: str, act: str, case: CaseState, t: float) -> float:
        mult = SKILL[r][act]
        est = e_base(act, case.tail_mult) * mult
        cost = est
        # handover
        if case.last_resource and case.last_resource != r:
            cost += HANDOVER
            prev_team = case.last_team
            cur_team = "M" if act == "Mechanical" else ("ES" if act in ES_TEAM else "O")
            if prev_team and cur_team and prev_team != cur_team and prev_team != "O" and cur_team != "O":
                # same resource retains  already excluded
                cost += TEAM_CROSS
        fam = skill_family(act)
        if self.last_skill_fam[r] and self.last_skill_fam[r] != fam and fam != "O":
            cost += SETUP
        # prefer fast
        cost += 5.0 * (mult - 1.0)
        # hybrid reservation: discourage using reserved resources for P3
        if self.policy == "P-Hybrid" and case.priority == "P3":
            if r in ("Chen", "Emil") and act in ("Electrical", "Software"):
                cost += 25.0
            if r in ("Ada", "Ben") and act == "Mechanical":
                cost += 15.0
        if self.policy == "P-Pool":
            # aging already in queue order
            pass
        return cost

    def log_event(self, case_id, act, start, end, resource):
        self.events.append({
            "case:concept:name": case_id,
            "concept:name": act,
            "start_timestamp": start,
            "time:timestamp": end,
            "org:resource": resource or "unknown",
        })

    def queue_priority_key(self, cid: str):
        c = self.cases[cid]
        pw = {"P1": 0, "P2": 1, "P3": 2}[c.priority]
        return (pw, c.arr)

    def try_start_intake(self, t):
        q = self.wait_queues["Intake"]
        q.sort(key=self.queue_priority_key)
        for cid in list(q):
            c = self.cases[cid]
            # IT outage blocks new Intake starts
            if c.intake_block and c.intake_block[0] <= t < c.intake_block[1]:
                continue
            elig = [r for r in self.eligible("Intake", t, c.priority)
                    if self.busy_job[r] is None and resource_available(r, t)]
            if not elig:
                continue
            # hybrid/pool choose min cost
            elig.sort(key=lambda r: self.assignment_cost(r, "Intake", c, t))
            if self.policy == "P-Hybrid" and c.priority != "P1":
                # reserve one fast intake if P1 waiting
                p1_waiting = any(self.cases[x].priority == "P1" for x in q if x != cid)
                if p1_waiting:
                    reserved = [r for r in elig if r in ("Ada", "Ben", "Deepa", "Farah")]
                    nonres = [r for r in elig if r not in reserved] or elig
                    elig = nonres
            r = elig[0]
            q.remove(cid)
            self._begin(c, "Intake", r, t)

    def diag_activities(self, ctype: str) -> List[str]:
        return {
            "M": ["Mechanical"],
            "E": ["Electrical"],
            "S": ["Software"],
            "ME": ["Mechanical", "Electrical"],
            "ES": ["Electrical", "Software"],
        }[ctype]

    def _begin(self, c: CaseState, act: str, r: str, t: float):
        base = draw_base(act, self.rng, c.tail_mult)
        mult = SKILL[r][act]
        svc = base * mult
        # penalties
        hand = 0.0
        if c.last_resource and c.last_resource != r:
            hand += HANDOVER
            prev_team = c.last_team
            cur_team = "M" if act == "Mechanical" else ("ES" if act in ("Electrical", "Software") else "O")
            if prev_team in ("M", "ES") and cur_team in ("M", "ES") and prev_team != cur_team:
                hand += TEAM_CROSS
            c.handover_burden += hand
            # log handover as separate event
            self.log_event(c.id, "Handover", t, t + hand, r)
            t = t + hand
        fam = skill_family(act)
        if self.last_skill_fam[r] and fam != "O" and self.last_skill_fam[r] != fam:
            self.log_event(c.id, "Setup", t, t + SETUP, r)
            c.setup_burden += SETUP
            t = t + SETUP
        job = ActivityExec(c.id, act, c.priority, svc, svc, r, False, t)
        self.busy_job[r] = job
        self.busy_until[r] = t + svc
        c.stage = f"doing_{act}"
        # store pending finish handled in step

    def finish_job(self, r: str, t: float):
        job = self.busy_job[r]
        if job is None:
            return
        c = self.cases[job.case_id]
        start = job.start_t
        # account util
        self.util_busy[r] += max(0.0, t - start)
        self.log_event(c.id, job.activity, start, t, r)
        self.last_skill_fam[r] = skill_family(job.activity)
        c.last_resource = r
        if job.activity == "Mechanical":
            c.last_team = "M"
        elif job.activity in ("Electrical", "Software"):
            c.last_team = "ES"
        else:
            c.last_team = "O"
        self.busy_job[r] = None
        self.busy_until[r] = t

        if job.activity == "Intake":
            # plan diagnostics
            acts = self.diag_activities(c.ctype)
            c.diag_queue = list(acts)
            c.done_diag = set()
            c.parallel = False
            if c.ctype in ("ME", "ES") and self.policy in ("P-Pool", "P-Hybrid"):
                # threshold parallel for P1 always; P2 sometimes
                if c.priority == "P1" or (c.priority == "P2" and self.policy == "P-Pool"):
                    c.parallel = True
                elif c.priority == "P2" and self.policy == "P-Hybrid":
                    c.parallel = True  # attempt; may fall back
            if c.parallel and len(acts) == 2:
                # both enter queues
                for a in acts:
                    self.wait_queues[a].append(c.id)
                c.stage = "diag_wait"
            else:
                # sequential
                c.parallel = False
                self.wait_queues[acts[0]].append(c.id)
                c.stage = "diag_wait"
        elif job.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
            c.done_diag.add(job.activity)
            acts = self.diag_activities(c.ctype)
            if c.parallel:
                if set(acts).issubset(c.done_diag):
                    # coordination before validation
                    c.coord_burden += COORD_PARALLEL
                    self.log_event(c.id, "Coordination", t, t + COORD_PARALLEL, r)
                    t_coord = t + COORD_PARALLEL
                    self.wait_queues["Validation"].append(c.id)
                    c.stage = "val_wait"
                    # bump time reference via artificial — validation starts >= t_coord
                    c._val_ready = t_coord  # type: ignore
                # else wait partner
            else:
                # sequential next
                remaining = [a for a in acts if a not in c.done_diag]
                if remaining:
                    self.wait_queues[remaining[0]].append(c.id)
                    c.stage = "diag_wait"
                else:
                    c._val_ready = t  # type: ignore
                    self.wait_queues["Validation"].append(c.id)
                    c.stage = "val_wait"
        elif job.activity == "Validation":
            c.completion = t
            c.stage = "done"

        # clear finished
        self.busy_job[r] = None

    def try_dispatch_stage(self, act: str, t: float):
        q = self.wait_queues[act]
        # filter ready (validation ready time)
        def ready(cid):
            c = self.cases[cid]
            if act == "Validation":
                return t >= getattr(c, "_val_ready", c.arr)
            return True
        q[:] = [cid for cid in q if self.cases[cid].stage.endswith("wait") or act in self.cases[cid].diag_queue or act == "Validation"]
        # unique preserve
        seen = set()
        nq = []
        for cid in q:
            if cid not in seen and ready(cid) and self.cases[cid].completion is None:
                # for parallel, skip if this act already done or in progress
                c = self.cases[cid]
                if act in c.done_diag:
                    continue
                if any(self.busy_job[r] and self.busy_job[r].case_id == cid and self.busy_job[r].activity == act
                       for r in self.resources):
                    continue
                seen.add(cid)
                nq.append(cid)
        nq.sort(key=self.queue_priority_key)
        self.wait_queues[act] = nq

        for cid in list(nq):
            c = self.cases[cid]
            elig = [r for r in self.eligible(act, t, c.priority)
                    if self.busy_job[r] is None and resource_available(r, t)]
            if self.policy == "P-Hybrid" and not elig:
                elig = []
            if self.policy == "P-Hybrid":
                # overflow triggers
                wait = t - c.arr
                qlen = len(nq)
                overflow = (
                    (c.priority == "P1" and wait > 10) or
                    (c.priority == "P2" and (wait > 45 or qlen >= 3)) or
                    (c.priority == "P3" and wait > 120)
                )
                primary = self.eligible(act, t, c.priority)
                # rebuild with full pool if overflow
                if overflow:
                    elig = [r for r in self.resources
                            if SKILL[r][act] is not None and self.busy_job[r] is None and resource_available(r, t)]
                else:
                    # prefer primary specialists
                    prim_map = {
                        "Mechanical": ["Ben", "Ada", "Deepa"],
                        "Electrical": ["Chen", "Deepa"],
                        "Software": ["Emil", "Chen"],
                        "Validation": self.resources,
                        "Intake": self.resources,
                    }
                    elig = [r for r in prim_map.get(act, elig) if r in elig]
                    if not elig:
                        # mild overflow if nobody primary free
                        elig = [r for r in self.resources
                                if SKILL[r][act] is not None and self.busy_job[r] is None and resource_available(r, t)]
            if not elig:
                # preemption attempt for P1
                if c.priority == "P1" and act in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                    self.try_preempt(c, act, t)
                continue
            elig.sort(key=lambda r: self.assignment_cost(r, act, c, t))
            r = elig[0]
            if cid in self.wait_queues[act]:
                self.wait_queues[act].remove(cid)
            self._begin(c, act, r, t)

    def try_preempt(self, c: CaseState, act: str, t: float):
        if c.preempt_count >= 1:
            return
        candidates = []
        for r in self.resources:
            job = self.busy_job[r]
            if job is None:
                continue
            if SKILL[r][act] is None:
                continue
            if not resource_available(r, t):
                continue
            if job.priority == "P1":
                continue
            if job.activity not in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                continue
            # remaining
            rem = max(0.1, self.busy_until[r] - t)
            candidates.append((rem, r, job))
        if not candidates:
            return
        candidates.sort(key=lambda x: -x[0])  # preempt longest remaining lower prio
        rem, r, job = candidates[0]
        # only if hybrid/pool allow; spec allows same queue family
        if self.policy == "P-Hybrid" and job.priority == "P2" and rem < 20:
            return
        # apply penalty to victim
        penalty = PREEMPT_PENALTY_FRAC * rem
        victim = self.cases[job.case_id]
        # log pause
        self.log_event(job.case_id, "PreemptPause", t, t, r)
        self.util_busy[r] += max(0.0, t - job.start_t)
        # requeue victim with remaining+penalty
        new_rem = rem + penalty
        self.busy_job[r] = None
        self.busy_until[r] = t
        # put victim back
        self.wait_queues[job.activity].insert(0, victim.id)
        victim.stage = "diag_wait"
        victim._preempt_rem = (job.activity, new_rem)  # type: ignore
        self.preempt_events += 1
        c.preempt_count += 1
        # start P1 — if victim had partial, we use full draw for P1 normally
        self._begin(c, act, r, t)
        if c.id in self.wait_queues[act]:
            self.wait_queues[act].remove(c.id)

    def run(self):
        # init cases
        for cr in self.cases_raw:
            cs = CaseState(
                id=cr["id"], arr=cr["arr"], priority=cr["priority"], ctype=cr["ctype"],
                tail_mult=cr["tail_mult"], intake_block=cr["intake_block"]
            )
            self.cases[cs.id] = cs
            self.wait_queues["Intake"].append(cs.id)
            cs.stage = "intake_wait"

        # event loop over minute grid (approximation)
        t = 0.0
        dt = 1.0
        while t <= DAY_END + 60:
            # finishes
            for r in list(self.resources):
                job = self.busy_job[r]
                if job and self.busy_until[r] <= t + 1e-9:
                    self.finish_job(r, self.busy_until[r])
            # dispatch order: Validation, diagnostics, Intake
            for act in ["Validation", "Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software", "Intake"]:
                if act == "Intake":
                    self.try_start_intake(t)
                else:
                    self.try_dispatch_stage(act, t)
            # stop if all done and quiet
            if all(c.completion is not None or c.arr > t for c in self.cases.values()):
                if all(self.busy_job[r] is None for r in self.resources):
                    if t > max(c.arr for c in self.cases.values()) + 5:
                        # still unfinished waits?
                        unfinished = [c for c in self.cases.values() if c.completion is None]
                        if not unfinished:
                            break
            t += dt

        # force close unfinished at day end mark
        for c in self.cases.values():
            if c.completion is None:
                c.completion = DAY_END + 120  # censored poor outcome
                c.stage = "censored"

        return self.summarize()

    def summarize(self):
        rows = []
        hands = []
        for c in self.cases.values():
            delay = c.completion - c.arr if c.completion else np.nan
            sla_t = SLA[c.priority]
            ok = delay <= sla_t if delay == delay else False
            rows.append(dict(
                id=c.id, priority=c.priority, ctype=c.ctype,
                delay=delay, sla_ok=ok,
                hand=c.handover_burden + c.setup_burden + c.coord_burden
            ))
            hands.append(c.handover_burden + c.setup_burden + c.coord_burden)
        df = pd.DataFrame(rows)
        def sla_rate(p):
            sub = df[df.priority == p]
            return float(sub.sla_ok.mean()) if len(sub) else float("nan")
        # utilization vs net available
        max_util = 0.0
        utils = []
        for r in self.resources:
            start, end, b0, b1 = SHIFTS[r]
            avail = end - start
            if b0 is not None:
                avail -= (b1 - b0)
            avail = max(avail, 1.0)
            u = self.util_busy[r] / avail
            utils.append(u)
            max_util = max(max_util, u)
        fair_gap = (max(utils) - min(utils)) if utils else 0.0
        fair = "Good" if fair_gap < 0.20 else ("Fair" if fair_gap < 0.35 else "Poor")
        p90 = float(df.delay.quantile(0.90))
        return {
            "policy": self.policy,
            "scenario": self.scenario,
            "n": len(df),
            "mean_delay": float(df.delay.mean()),
            "p90_delay": p90,
            "P1_SLA": sla_rate("P1"),
            "P2_SLA": sla_rate("P2"),
            "P3_SLA": sla_rate("P3"),
            "max_resource_utilization": float(max_util),
            "utilization_fairness": fair,
            "fair_gap": float(fair_gap),
            "handover_or_setup_burden": float(np.mean(hands)) if hands else 0.0,
            "preempt_events": self.preempt_events,
            "events": self.events,
            "case_df": df,
        }

# 4. Offered-load approximation (audit trail)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
def offered_load_report(scenario: str) -> pd.DataFrame:
    blocks = scenario_blocks(scenario)
    tail_m = 2.0 if scenario == "U4" else 1.0
    rows = []
    for b in blocks:
        n = b["n"]
        mix = dict(zip(["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"], b["mix"]))
        m_load = n * (mix["M"] + mix["ME"]) * e_base("Mechanical", tail_m)
        e_load = n * (mix["E"] + mix["ME"] + mix["ES"]) * e_base("Electrical", tail_m)
        s_load = n * (mix["S"] + mix["ES"]) * e_base("Software", tail_m)
        i_load = n * e_base("Intake", 1.0)
        v_load = n * e_base("Validation", 1.0)
        rows.append({
            "scenario": scenario,
            "block": f"{8+b['start']//60:02d}:00-{8+b['end']//60:02d}:00",
            "arrivals": round(n, 2),
            "M_min": round(m_load, 1),
            "E_min": round(e_load, 1),
            "S_min": round(s_load, 1),
            "Intake_min": round(i_load, 1),
            "Val_min": round(v_load, 1),
        })
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## What cannot be concluded from expected arrivals and ranges alone

- True p90/SLA under queueing feedback, blocking, and shift-edge effects without stochastic simulation or empirical logs.  
- Whether parallel ME/ES helps *net* SLA — depends on dual-resource contention not identified by mean load.  
- Behavioral responses (slowdown near breaks, cherry-picking).  
- Correlation of complex tails within a case (common-cause major incidents).  
- Exact fairness under real absenteeism patterns beyond U3.  
- Whether handover 7+5 min is additive wall-clock vs partially overlapping administrative work.  
- Absolute ranking of P-Pool vs P-Hybrid when real cross-training speed differs from the skill matrix.

Midpoint load analysis bounds **infeasibility** (U3 software) but not **optimal policy** under O1 vs O2 trade-offs.

## Pilot / backtest design (protect P1, avoid unsafe full rollout)

1. **Retrospective discrete-event backtest** on 4–6 weeks of historical incident logs: replay arrivals, fit service distributions by (activity, resource, complexity proxy), counterfactual assign under P-Spec / P-Pool / P-Hybrid offline. Freeze P1 to actual historical resources when uncertain (conservative).  
2. **Shadow mode (2 weeks):** Hybrid computes recommended assignment; operators still use current practice; measure disagreement, counterfactual wait, and estimate handover.  
3. **Limited A/B (3–4 weeks):** Randomize by **day** (not case) between Hybrid and status quo; **always-on P1 reservation** on both arms; stop-loss if rolling P1_SLA < 85% over 2 days  force Emergency Pool + management alert.  
4. **Guardrails:** no P3 aging beyond 24 h without escalation; max preemption rate 2/resource/hour; Validation WIP cap triggers intake metering.  
5. **Success criteria:** O1 improvement  15% vs baseline on shadow+A/B; max util  0.95 on  90% of days; handover min/case not up > 25% vs baseline; operator NASA-TLX or simple survey not worse.  
6. **Process-mining checkpoint weekly:** handover network entropy, stage wait DFG, service-time KS test vs training distribution (drift), bottleneck rank stability.

---

**Summary stance.** Specialization creates Chen/Emil fragility and fails U3/U5; full pooling reduces mean delay but inflates handover/setup and can thrash. **P-Hybrid** is the robust default under O1 across U1–U5, with Emergency Pool triggers when absence or backlog breaches thresholds.
