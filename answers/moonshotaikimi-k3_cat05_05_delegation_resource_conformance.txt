## 6. Control Improvements

- **Delegation creation & effective time:** require a single controlled record created *before* the effective interval (as in DQM02); prohibit backdated/migrationcreated rows from setting effective time; store the signed instrument hash and effective interval together so a later DB row cannot be mistaken for the start (DQM01).
- **Principal availability:** record *formal unavailability* explicitly (including weekends/afterhours) so conditions like “principal must be absent” are objectively testable (resolves the DQM02 Saturday question); when a principal briefly acts while on leave (Asha’s 0813 VPN), log it as a limited return and state its effect on concurrent delegations.
- **Delegate capacity:** hardblock an account whose owner is on certified sick/annual leave from exercising delegations (would have stopped u_bk at 0813 13:00 and 0814 16:00); require positive reproof of delegate availability at execution time.
- **Alias management:** enforce one human = one identity; retire or hardlink `b.koch`/`u_bk` so SoD cannot be evaded by stringdistinct IDs (CHG508); run SoD on canonical `human_id` only.
- **Individual authentication:** eliminate shared/serviceaccount execution for SoDrelevant activities; where a service account is unavoidable, require peraction FIDO/MFA reauthentication and applicationlevel user context (CHG503 failed this; CHG509 shows badge+FIDO working).
- **Emergency review (RR4):** preregister incidentscoped delegations (DIC03 worked); make subdelegation technically impossible (DIC04 should not be issuable); guarantee an inscope QM delegate for *every* scope (there was no coolingscope QM delegate, dooming both CHG505 and CHG510 retrospective reviews); alert when the 2businessday deadline is at risk.
- **Pair rotation:** introduce rotation/secondapprover sampling for highfrequency draftapprove pairs (ClaraBen 18/23) and monitor reciprocal approvals (BenClara), with thresholds that trigger review — not accusation.
- **Network monitoring:** run handover/workingtogether networks on canonical identities with service accounts resolved; alert on *changes* in concentration and on crossabsence anomalies (account active while owner on leave), always routed to human review.
- **Evidential retention:** retain signed delegation instruments, VPN/MFA/device posture, badge/CCTV, and session recordings long enough to adjudicate eventtime authority; preserve the premigration QMS audit trail.
