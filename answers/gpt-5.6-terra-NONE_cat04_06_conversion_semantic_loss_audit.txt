# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The POWL definition is authoritative. The table below describes semantic obligations, not merely what a rendered diagram may suggest.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | `Register Change` occurs before every other visible business activity. | `Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release Change, Archive Change` | `Assess Risk, Register Change, ...` | Yes |
| I2 | `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` occur after registration and have no required order between them. | `Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, ...`; also `Register Change, Prepare Evidence, Assess Risk, ...` | A model permitting `Prepare Evidence` before `Register Change` | Yes |
| I3 | `Legal Review` is optional after `Assess Risk`. | Trace containing `..., Assess Risk, Legal Review, ...`; trace containing `..., Assess Risk, ...` without `Legal Review` | A model requiring `Legal Review` in every completed trace | Yes; the silent skip itself is hidden |
| I4 | `Legal Review` cannot occur before `Assess Risk`. | `Register Change, Assess Risk, Legal Review, ...` | `Register Change, Legal Review, Assess Risk, ...` | Yes |
| I5 | Verification starts only after both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` complete. | `Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, ...` | `Register Change, Assess Risk, Configure Trial, Prepare Evidence, ...` | Yes |
| I6 | Optional legal review and the verification loop are unordered after their respective prerequisites; neither has a direct precedence constraint over the other. | `..., Assess Risk, Legal Review, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial, ...`; also `..., Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Legal Review, ...` | A model forcing all verification before legal review, or all legal review before verification | Partly: visible trace interleavings can demonstrate absence of forced visible order, but cannot fully prove true concurrency |
| I7 | Every verification iteration performs `Configure Trial` before `Inspect Trial`. | `..., Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, ...` | `..., Inspect Trial, Configure Trial, ...` | Yes |
| I8 | The verification loop has at least one iteration. | `..., Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release Change, ...` | `..., Release Change, ...` with no configure/inspect pair | Yes |
| I9 | A repeated verification iteration is separated by `Correct Defect`. | `..., Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Correct Defect, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, ...` | `..., Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, ...` | Yes |
| I10 | `Correct Defect` is only meaningful as loop redo behavior after an inspected trial and before another iteration. | `Inspect Trial, Correct Defect, Configure Trial` | `Correct Defect` before the first `Configure Trial`, or after `Release Change` | Yes |
| I11 | `Release Change` waits for both completion of the optional-legal branch and completion of the verification loop. | `..., Legal Review, ..., Inspect Trial, Release Change, ...`; and skip-legal completion followed by verification completion then release | `..., Inspect Trial, Release Change, Legal Review, ...`; or release before any verification iteration | Yes |
| I12 | `Archive Change` occurs after `Release Change` and is the final visible business action. | `..., Release Change, Archive Change` | `..., Archive Change, Release Change`; or a visible activity after archive | Yes |
| I13 | Successful completion corresponds to the accepting final marking, not merely reaching `Archive Change` in a partially completed branch. | A firing sequence ending exactly in the final marking after archive | A trace with `Archive Change` whose marking still enables unfinished legal or verification work | Partly; exact final-marking equality requires net-state inspection |

“True concurrency” is stronger than observing two visible traces in opposite orders. Trace observations can demonstrate permitted interleavings, but they cannot by themselves prove that the conversion retains a concurrent token-flow representation rather than encoding the same interleavings through other routing structures.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary: POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN** | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Uses the supported POWL conversion directly and retains accepting-net markings as the semantic checkpoint before BPMN export. | BPMN gateway structure may visually imply sequencing or exclusive choice where the source expresses partial order and silent routing. BPMN conversion can introduce routing nodes and tau-like behavior. | Reject or quarantine if POWL net is unsound/non-workflow, BPMN conversion raises, BPMN cannot be converted back to an accepting net, or bounded witness/marking checks fail. |
| **Secondary: POWL  accepting Petri net  process tree  BPMN** | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | A successful tree can provide compact block-structured stakeholder documentation and may make loop/XOR structure easier to explain. | The source includes partial-order concurrency plus an independent optional branch and a loop. A faithful process tree may not exist, or conversion may normalize/approximate behavior. | Process-tree conversion raises an exception; generated tree lacks expected operators; tree-derived BPMN fails round-trip witness checks; or bounded trace comparison finds mismatches. |
| **Audit-only: POWL  accepting Petri net  reachability/footprints/trace exploration** | `convert_to_petri_net`; `check_soundness`; `discover_footprints`; `convert_to_reachability_graph`; custom exploration | Establishes behavioral evidence without treating visualization success as correctness. | Bounded state/trace exploration is not global language equivalence, especially in the presence of loops and silent transitions. | State-space construction fails or becomes impractical; required positive traces are absent; prohibited traces are admitted; exact final-marking completion is not reached. |

The primary pipeline is **POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN**. The accepting Petri net is the principal converted semantic artifact and the BPMN is a derivative visualization/interchange artifact.

# 3. Conversion Code

```python
import os
from typing import Any, Dict, Optional, Tuple

import pm4py

# source_powl is assumed to be defined exactly as in the authoritative source code.

OUTPUT_DIR = "conversion_artifacts"
os.makedirs(OUTPUT_DIR, exist_ok=True)


def normalize_accepting_net_result(result: Any) -> Tuple[Any, Any, Any]:
    """
    Normalize a PM4Py accepting-net conversion result.

    PM4Py conversion APIs commonly return (net, initial_marking, final_marking).
    This helper fails explicitly if the expected accepting-net shape is absent.
    """
    if not isinstance(result, tuple) or len(result) != 3:
        raise TypeError(
            "Expected an accepting Petri net tuple "
            "(net, initial_marking, final_marking), got: {!r}".format(result)
        )

    net, initial_marking, final_marking = result

    if net is None:
        raise ValueError("Conversion returned a null Petri net.")
    if initial_marking is None:
        raise ValueError("Conversion returned a null initial marking.")
    if final_marking is None:
        raise ValueError("Conversion returned a null final marking.")

    return net, initial_marking, final_marking


def diagnose_accepting_net(
    name: str,
    net: Any,
    initial_marking: Any,
    final_marking: Any,
) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """
    Run workflow-net and soundness diagnostics without treating diagnostics
    as proof of semantic equivalence.
    """
    result: Dict[str, Any] = {
        "name": name,
        "workflow_net": None,
        "soundness": None,
        "soundness_error": None,
    }

    try:
        result["workflow_net"] = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    except Exception as exc:
        result["workflow_net"] = False
        result["workflow_net_error"] = repr(exc)

    try:
        result["soundness"] = pm4py.check_soundness(
            net,
            initial_marking,
            final_marking,
            print_diagnostics=True,
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        result["soundness"] = False
        result["soundness_error"] = repr(exc)

    print(f"\n[{name}] workflow-net: {result['workflow_net']}")
    print(f"[{name}] soundness: {result['soundness']}")
    if result.get("workflow_net_error"):
        print(f"[{name}] workflow-net diagnostic error: "
              f"{result['workflow_net_error']}")
    if result["soundness_error"]:
        print(f"[{name}] soundness diagnostic error: "
              f"{result['soundness_error']}")

    return result


def save_accepting_net_visualization(
    name: str,
    net: Any,
    initial_marking: Any,
    final_marking: Any,
) -> None:
    path = os.path.join(OUTPUT_DIR, f"{name}.png")
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(net, initial_marking, final_marking, path)
        print(f"Saved Petri-net visualization: {path}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"Could not save Petri-net visualization for {name}: {exc!r}")


def save_bpmn_visualization(name: str, bpmn: Any) -> None:
    path = os.path.join(OUTPUT_DIR, f"{name}.png")
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(bpmn, path)
        print(f"Saved BPMN visualization: {path}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"Could not save BPMN visualization for {name}: {exc!r}")


# Save the authoritative source visualization separately from derivative artifacts.
try:
    pm4py.save_vis_powl(
        source_powl,
        os.path.join(OUTPUT_DIR, "00_source_powl.png"),
    )
    print("Saved source POWL visualization.")
except Exception as exc:
    print(f"Could not save source POWL visualization: {exc!r}")


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Pipeline 1 (primary): POWL -> accepting Petri net -> BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = normalize_accepting_net_result(
    pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)
)

diagnostics: Dict[str, Dict[str, Any]] = {}
diagnostics["net_from_powl"] = diagnose_accepting_net(
    "net_from_powl",
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
)

save_accepting_net_visualization(
    "01_net_from_powl",
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
)

bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
)
if bpmn_from_net is None:
    raise ValueError("Petri-net-to-BPMN conversion returned None.")

save_bpmn_visualization("02_bpmn_from_net", bpmn_from_net)

roundtrip_nets: Dict[str, Tuple[Any, Any, Any]] = {}

try:
    net_from_bpmn, im_from_bpmn, fm_from_bpmn = normalize_accepting_net_result(
        pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)
    )
    roundtrip_nets["bpmn_from_net"] = (
        net_from_bpmn,
        im_from_bpmn,
        fm_from_bpmn,
    )

    diagnostics["net_from_bpmn"] = diagnose_accepting_net(
        "net_from_bpmn",
        net_from_bpmn,
        im_from_bpmn,
        fm_from_bpmn,
    )

    save_accepting_net_visualization(
        "03_net_roundtrip_from_bpmn",
        net_from_bpmn,
        im_from_bpmn,
        fm_from_bpmn,
    )
except Exception as exc:
    print(f"BPMN -> accepting Petri net round trip failed: {exc!r}")


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Pipeline 2 (conditional): POWL -> accepting Petri net -> process tree -> BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

tree_from_net: Optional[Any] = None
bpmn_from_tree: Optional[Any] = None

try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl,
        im_from_powl,
        fm_from_powl,
    )

    if tree_from_net is None:
        raise ValueError("Petri-net-to-process-tree conversion returned None.")

    print("Process-tree conversion succeeded.")
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_process_tree(
            tree_from_net,
            os.path.join(OUTPUT_DIR, "04_tree_from_net.png"),
        )
        print("Saved process-tree visualization.")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"Could not save process-tree visualization: {exc!r}")

    bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
    if bpmn_from_tree is None:
        raise ValueError("Process-tree-to-BPMN conversion returned None.")

    save_bpmn_visualization("05_bpmn_from_tree", bpmn_from_tree)

    try:
        net_from_tree_bpmn, im_from_tree_bpmn, fm_from_tree_bpmn = (
            normalize_accepting_net_result(
                pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_tree)
            )
        )

        roundtrip_nets["bpmn_from_tree"] = (
            net_from_tree_bpmn,
            im_from_tree_bpmn,
            fm_from_tree_bpmn,
        )

        diagnostics["net_from_tree_bpmn"] = diagnose_accepting_net(
            "net_from_tree_bpmn",
            net_from_tree_bpmn,
            im_from_tree_bpmn,
            fm_from_tree_bpmn,
        )

        save_accepting_net_visualization(
            "06_net_roundtrip_from_tree_bpmn",
            net_from_tree_bpmn,
            im_from_tree_bpmn,
            fm_from_tree_bpmn,
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        print(
            "Tree-derived BPMN -> accepting Petri net round trip failed: "
            f"{exc!r}"
        )

except Exception as exc:
    # This is a meaningful result, not a condition to suppress silently.
    print(
        "Petri-net -> process-tree conversion failed or was rejected. "
        "The source behavior may not admit a faithful block-structured tree: "
        f"{exc!r}"
    )
    tree_from_net = None
    bpmn_from_tree = None


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Optional reachability diagnostics for every available accepting net.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

all_accepting_nets: Dict[str, Tuple[Any, Any, Any]] = {
    "net_from_powl": (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl),
    **roundtrip_nets,
}

for name, (net, initial_marking, final_marking) in all_accepting_nets.items():
    try:
        reachability_graph = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(
            net,
            initial_marking,
            final_marking,
        )
        print(f"[{name}] reachability graph created: {reachability_graph!r}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"[{name}] reachability-graph conversion failed: {exc!r}")

    try:
        footprints = pm4py.discover_footprints(
            net,
            initial_marking,
            final_marking,
        )
        print(f"[{name}] footprints: {footprints!r}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"[{name}] footprint discovery failed: {exc!r}")

print("\nAvailable accepting nets:")
for name in all_accepting_nets:
    print(f" - {name}")
```

The code intentionally treats a failed process-tree conversion as an audit outcome. It does not silently replace it with a different model or claim that a tree exists.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

Assessment terms are restricted to the requested vocabulary. “Validated preserved” requires actual execution of the validation code and successful relevant checks; before execution, the strongest honest assessment is generally “expected preserved” or “not tested.”

| Source invariant | POWLPetri | PetriBPMN | PetriTree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Register occurs first | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | The POWL-to-net conversion should encode the source precedence. Positive/negative trace checks can validate it under the selected bound. |
| Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence are unordered | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Opposite visible-order witnesses are useful evidence. They do not establish true token-level concurrency by themselves. |
| No direct order between optional legal review and verification | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | The source has no edge between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`; a conversion must not introduce a precedence dependency beyond release synchronization. |
| Legal review is optional through XOR with tau skip | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | The silent skip may become BPMN routing structure. Visible traces should include both legal-present and legal-absent completions. |
| Configure precedes Inspect within each iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Verify with positive and negative bounded visible traces. |
| Binary-loop redo uses Correct Defect | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Process-tree loop conventions and BPMN loop rendering may differ visually; ensure `Correct Defect` separates iterations and is not optional arbitrary work. |
| At least one verification iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | A completed trace without `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial` must be rejected. |
| Release joins legal completion and verification completion | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | The Petri net should contain synchronization behavior. BPMN gateway rendering can obscure whether it is AND-join versus another routing pattern. |
| Archive is final visible activity | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Exact final marking must be reached after archive; archive alone is insufficient evidence if residual tokens remain. |
| Activity-label uniqueness | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | not tested | Source labels are unique. Check generated nets for duplicate visible labels before using label-based trace comparison. |
| Initial and final markings are retained as acceptance semantics | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | BPMN itself may not carry the same explicit marking representation; round-trip conversion must produce non-null markings and exact completion tests. |
| BPMN gateways/routing nodes render faithfully | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | A gateway drawing is not a proof of source semantics. Inspect generated BPMN XML/model plus behavioral checks. |

Key audit observations:

- **True concurrency versus arbitrary interleaving:** The source partial order permits `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` in either order. It also leaves legal review and verification unordered once enabled. A BPMN conversion may preserve the visible trace language while representing it with routing that is visually or structurally different. Bounded traces showing both orders are evidence of permitted interleaving, not a complete proof of preserved concurrency semantics.

- **No edge between optional legal review and verification:** This is semantically important. Legal review depends on `Assess Risk`; verification depends on both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence`; neither branch depends on completion of the other until release. A conversion that forces legal completion before verification, or verification completion before legal review, changes the source behavior.

- **Silent optional behavior:** The legal skip is a `SilentTransition`, so its branch-selection event is invisible in business traces. Validation must distinguish “legal absent” from “legal impossible”: completed traces both with and without `Legal Review` should be admitted.

- **Binary-loop semantics:** The POWL loop has `trial_body` as the do part and `Correct Defect` as the redo part. At least one body execution is required. A second body execution should require a redo occurrence. BPMN loop markers can be misleading because they may not visibly show the redo activity as the loop-back condition.

- **Join before Release Change:** Release must not occur merely because one branch completes. The key semantic check is that any complete release/archive trace has completed the verification body at least once and has resolved the optional legal branch, whether by legal review or hidden skip.

- **Activity-label uniqueness:** The given source has unique visible labels. That makes label-based witness tests defensible. If a conversion duplicates a visible label through expansion or duplicate task nodes, simple trace-label comparison becomes ambiguous and should be quarantined or strengthened with transition identity checks.

- **Initial and final markings:** Accepting Petri-net semantics are defined by `(net, initial_marking, final_marking)`. Non-null markings are necessary but not sufficient. Completion should be tested using exact equality with the final marking, not “archive fired” or “a final-looking place was marked.”

- **BPMN routing ambiguity:** Gateways, events, and generated routing nodes may not carry business labels. Their visual placement, shape, and graph direction can suggest an order not imposed by the original POWL. BPMN should therefore be described as a derivative representation, not as the authoritative semantic source.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The following code performs bounded visible-trace exploration. It hides transitions with `label is None`, requires exact final-marking equality for completed traces, tracks silent steps to avoid infinite tau cycling, and bounds visible length and `Correct Defect` occurrences.

The comparison is **exhaustive only within the selected exploration bounds and marking/tau limits**. It is not a proof of unbounded language equivalence.

```python
from collections import deque
from typing import Any, Dict, Iterable, List, Optional, Sequence, Set, Tuple

import pm4py


VISIBLE_LENGTH_BOUND = 12
LOOP_REDO_BOUND = 1
TAU_STEPS_BOUND = 40
STATE_EXPANSION_BOUND = 50000


def marking_key(marking: Any) -> Tuple[Tuple[str, int], ...]:
    """
    Hashable marking representation based on place name and token count.

    If generated places have duplicate/empty names, repr(place) keeps the key
    usable for bounded exploration, although it is not intended for cross-net
    place identity comparison.
    """
    return tuple(
        sorted(
            (
                str(getattr(place, "name", repr(place))),
                int(tokens),
            )
            for place, tokens in marking.items()
            if tokens
        )
    )


def fire_transition(net: Any, marking: Any, transition: Any) -> Any:
    """
    PM4Py exposes enabled-transition discovery directly. The low-level firing
    utility location can vary by PM4Py version, so import it explicitly and
    fail clearly if unavailable.
    """
    try:
        from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics
        return semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
    except Exception as first_exc:
        try:
            from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils import petri_utils
            return petri_utils.execute(transition, net, marking)
        except Exception as second_exc:
            raise RuntimeError(
                "Could not execute a Petri-net transition using this PM4Py "
                "installation."
            ) from second_exc


def visible_label(transition: Any) -> Optional[str]:
    label = getattr(transition, "label", None)
    return label if label is not None else None


def enumerate_completed_visible_traces(
    net: Any,
    initial_marking: Any,
    final_marking: Any,
    visible_length_bound: int = VISIBLE_LENGTH_BOUND,
    loop_redo_bound: int = LOOP_REDO_BOUND,
    tau_steps_bound: int = TAU_STEPS_BOUND,
    state_expansion_bound: int = STATE_EXPANSION_BOUND,
) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """
    Enumerate bounded completed visible traces.

    Properties:
    - transitions with label None are hidden from returned visible traces;
    - a trace is completed only when the current marking equals final_marking;
    - silent-step count is bounded to avoid infinite tau cycling;
    - visible trace length is bounded;
    - Correct Defect count is bounded as a proxy for loop iteration count;
    - visited state includes marking and bounded-control dimensions.

    This does not prove equivalence beyond these bounds.
    """
    if initial_marking is None or final_marking is None:
        raise ValueError("Exact final-marking validation requires both markings.")

    final_key = marking_key(final_marking)
    queue = deque()
    queue.append((initial_marking, tuple(), 0, 0))

    completed: Set[Tuple[str, ...]] = set()
    visited: Set[Tuple[Any, Tuple[str, ...], int, int]] = set()
    expansions = 0
    truncated = False

    while queue:
        marking, trace, tau_steps, redo_count = queue.popleft()

        state_key = (
            marking_key(marking),
            trace,
            tau_steps,
            redo_count,
        )
        if state_key in visited:
            continue
        visited.add(state_key)

        if marking_key(marking) == final_key:
            completed.add(trace)
            # Do not continue beyond exact completion. This avoids treating
            # post-final behavior as acceptable completion.
            continue

        if expansions >= state_expansion_bound:
            truncated = True
            break
        expansions += 1

        try:
            enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
        except Exception as exc:
            raise RuntimeError(
                "Could not obtain enabled transitions during exploration."
            ) from exc

        for transition in enabled:
            label = visible_label(transition)

            next_trace = trace
            next_tau_steps = tau_steps
            next_redo_count = redo_count

            if label is None:
                if tau_steps >= tau_steps_bound:
                    continue
                next_tau_steps += 1
            else:
                if len(trace) >= visible_length_bound:
                    continue
                if label == "Correct Defect":
                    if redo_count >= loop_redo_bound:
                        continue
                    next_redo_count += 1
                next_trace = trace + (label,)

            next_marking = fire_transition(net, marking, transition)
            queue.append(
                (
                    next_marking,
                    next_trace,
                    next_tau_steps,
                    next_redo_count,
                )
            )

    return {
        "completed_traces": completed,
        "visited_states": len(visited),
        "expansions": expansions,
        "truncated": truncated,
        "visible_length_bound": visible_length_bound,
        "loop_redo_bound": loop_redo_bound,
        "tau_steps_bound": tau_steps_bound,
    }


def contains_order(trace: Sequence[str], first: str, second: str) -> bool:
    try:
        return trace.index(first) < trace.index(second)
    except ValueError:
        return False


def count_label(trace: Sequence[str], label: str) -> int:
    return sum(1 for item in trace if item == label)


def source_trace_predicate(trace: Sequence[str]) -> bool:
    """
    A visible-trace-level predicate for the principal source obligations.
    It is intentionally conservative: it checks what can be checked from
    labels, not hidden branch tokens or true concurrency.
    """
    required = {
        "Register Change",
        "Assess Risk",
        "Prepare Evidence",
        "Configure Trial",
        "Inspect Trial",
        "Release Change",
        "Archive Change",
    }

    if not required.issubset(set(trace)):
        return False

    # Source has unique visible activities except repeated loop occurrences.
    if count_label(trace, "Register Change") != 1:
        return False
    if count_label(trace, "Assess Risk") != 1:
        return False
    if count_label(trace, "Prepare Evidence") != 1:
        return False
    if count_label(trace, "Release Change") != 1:
        return False
    if count_label(trace, "Archive Change") != 1:
        return False
    if count_label(trace, "Legal Review") > 1:
        return False

    if trace[0] != "Register Change":
        return False
    if trace[-1] != "Archive Change":
        return False

    if not contains_order(trace, "Register Change", "Assess Risk"):
        return False
    if not contains_order(trace, "Register Change", "Prepare Evidence"):
        return False
    if not contains_order(trace, "Release Change", "Archive Change"):
        return False

    if "Legal Review" in trace:
        if not contains_order(trace, "Assess Risk", "Legal Review"):
            return False
        if not contains_order(trace, "Legal Review", "Release Change"):
            return False

    first_configure = trace.index("Configure Trial")
    first_inspect = trace.index("Inspect Trial")
    release_index = trace.index("Release Change")

    # Verification starts after both prerequisites.
    if trace.index("Assess Risk") > first_configure:
        return False
    if trace.index("Prepare Evidence") > first_configure:
        return False

    # At least one ordered Configure/Inspect pair before release.
    if first_configure > first_inspect:
        return False
    if first_inspect > release_index:
        return False

    # Each Configure must be followed by an Inspect before the next Configure
    # or before release.
    pending_configure = False
    for label in trace:
        if label == "Configure Trial":
            if pending_configure:
                return False
            pending_configure = True
        elif label == "Inspect Trial":
            if not pending_configure:
                return False
            pending_configure = False
        elif label == "Release Change":
            if pending_configure:
                return False

    # Repetition requires Correct Defect between iterations.
    config_positions = [
        index for index, label in enumerate(trace)
        if label == "Configure Trial"
    ]
    for previous, current in zip(config_positions, config_positions[1:]):
        if "Correct Defect" not in trace[previous + 1:current]:
            return False

    # Correct Defect must be between an inspect and a later configure.
    for index, label in enumerate(trace):
        if label == "Correct Defect":
            has_prior_inspect = "Inspect Trial" in trace[:index]
            has_later_configure = "Configure Trial" in trace[index + 1:]
            if not (has_prior_inspect and has_later_configure):
                return False

    # Release must be after completion of the final inspected iteration.
    if trace.index("Inspect Trial", first_inspect) > release_index:
        return False

    return True

def validate_net_against_witnesses(
    name: str,
    net: Any,
    initial_marking: Any,
    final_marking: Any,
) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """
    Perform bounded language evidence checks for one accepting net.
    """
    result: Dict[str, Any] = {
        "name": name,
        "workflow_net": None,
        "soundness": None,
        "footprints": None,
        "reachability_graph_created": False,
        "exploration": None,
        "missing_positive_witnesses": set(),
        "admitted_negative_witnesses": set(),
        "completed_traces_violating_source_predicate": set(),
    }

    try:
        result["workflow_net"] = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    except Exception as exc:
        result["workflow_net"] = False
        result["workflow_net_error"] = repr(exc)

    try:
        result["soundness"] = pm4py.check_soundness(
            net,
            initial_marking,
            final_marking,
            print_diagnostics=True,
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        result["soundness"] = False
        result["soundness_error"] = repr(exc)

    try:
        result["footprints"] = pm4py.discover_footprints(
            net,
            initial_marking,
            final_marking,
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        result["footprints_error"] = repr(exc)

    try:
        pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(
            net,
            initial_marking,
            final_marking,
        )
        result["reachability_graph_created"] = True
    except Exception as exc:
        result["reachability_graph_error"] = repr(exc)

    exploration = enumerate_completed_visible_traces(
        net,
        initial_marking,
        final_marking,
    )
    result["exploration"] = exploration

    completed = exploration["completed_traces"]
    result["missing_positive_witnesses"] = {
        witness for witness in POSITIVE_WITNESSES
        if witness not in completed
    }
    result["admitted_negative_witnesses"] = {
        witness for witness in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES
        if witness in completed
    }
    result["completed_traces_violating_source_predicate"] = {
        trace for trace in completed
        if not source_trace_predicate(trace)
    }

    print(f"\nValidation report for {name}")
    print(f"  workflow-net: {result['workflow_net']}")
    print(f"  soundness: {result['soundness']}")
    print(
        "  completed visible traces within bound: "
        f"{len(completed)}"
    )
    print(
        "  exploration truncated: "
        f"{exploration['truncated']}"
    )
    print(
        "  missing positive witnesses: "
        f"{len(result['missing_positive_witnesses'])}"
    )
    print(
        "  admitted negative witnesses: "
        f"{len(result['admitted_negative_witnesses'])}"
    )
    print(
        "  completed traces violating source predicate: "
        f"{len(result['completed_traces_violating_source_predicate'])}"
    )

    return result


def compare_bounded_languages(
    reference_name: str,
    reference_result: Dict[str, Any],
    candidate_name: str,
    candidate_result: Dict[str, Any],
) -> Dict[str, Set[Tuple[str, ...]]]:
    """
    Compare completed visible trace sets only under the chosen bounds.
    """
    reference_traces = reference_result["exploration"]["completed_traces"]
    candidate_traces = candidate_result["exploration"]["completed_traces"]

    comparison = {
        "only_in_reference": reference_traces - candidate_traces,
        "only_in_candidate": candidate_traces - reference_traces,
    }

    print(f"\nBounded language comparison: {reference_name} vs {candidate_name}")
    print(f"  only in {reference_name}: "
          f"{len(comparison['only_in_reference'])}")
    print(f"  only in {candidate_name}: "
          f"{len(comparison['only_in_candidate'])}")

    return comparison

# - roundtrip_nets: mapping name -> (net, im, fm)

reference_validation = validate_net_against_witnesses(
```

Important interpretation rules:

1. `check_soundness(...)` is a structural/behavioral net diagnostic, not proof that the net matches the source POWL.
2. Footprints are useful comparative evidence but are not, by themselves, a complete equivalence proof.
3. A reachability graph may be expensive or fail for large/unbounded state spaces.
4. The custom enumerator uses exact final-marking equality, but its language comparison is bounded by:
   - visible trace length;
   - `Correct Defect` count;
   - tau-step count;
   - total state expansions.
5. If exploration reports `truncated=True`, absence of a trace is inconclusive. The result should be quarantined rather than treated as a successful equivalence check.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN rendering can be behaviorally acceptable while still being easy to misread.

- **Parallel gateways:** A BPMN parallel split/join may be the appropriate rendering for the partial-order portions of the source. However, visual branch placement can imply that one branch “comes first” merely because it is drawn above or left of another. Stakeholders may incorrectly infer that `Assess Risk` precedes `Prepare Evidence`, or that legal review precedes verification.

- **Exclusive gateways:** The optional legal review is represented semantically by an XOR between `Legal Review` and a silent transition. In BPMN, the silent branch may appear as an unlabeled path, an empty routing node, or a direct bypass. Readers may mistake this for an error, a missing task, or an unconditional bypass rather than an intentional “legal review not required” decision.

- **Loop visualization:** A BPMN loop marker, backward sequence flow, or gateway cycle may not visibly communicate the POWL binary-loop rule. The required semantics are: execute `Configure Trial` then `Inspect Trial` at least once; if another iteration occurs, execute `Correct Defect` before restarting the trial body. A generic loop marker can be misread as “retry any task arbitrarily” or “correct defect is optional after every inspection.”

- **Silent routing nodes:** Generated BPMN may contain gateways, start/end events, or unnamed routing elements that correspond to Petri-net tau transitions. These are semantically relevant for routing but not business work. They should not be presented as missing business activities.

- **Graph-layout ordering:** Top-to-bottom, left-to-right, and line-crossing conventions are layout decisions. They are not precedence semantics unless supported by the underlying sequence-flow and gateway behavior. A diagram where legal review is drawn above the verification loop does not prove legal review must finish first.

Recommended documentation and annotations:

1. Add a legend stating that unlabeled routing paths represent silent control-flow, including the legal-review skip.
2. Annotate the legal branch: “Legal Review optional after Assess Risk; release waits for branch resolution.”
3. Annotate the verification loop: “At least one trial iteration; repeat only after Correct Defect.”
4. Annotate the release gateway/join: “Release requires legal-branch completion and verification-loop completion.”
5. Add a note near `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence`: “No imposed order; may proceed independently after Register Change.”
6. Publish the POWL source and accepting Petri-net validation report alongside BPMN so that the rendered diagram is not the sole semantic authority.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a conversion result if any of the following occurs:

1. `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` fails, returns null net/markings, or cannot produce an accepting net.
2. The converted net is not recognized as a workflow net, where workflow-net structure is required by the intended deployment.
3. Soundness diagnostics fail, including deadlocks, improper completion, unreachable final marking, or remaining tokens after supposed completion.
4. BPMN conversion fails, produces `None`, or BPMN cannot be converted back to an accepting Petri net for audit.
5. Process-tree conversion raises an exception, but a tree-derived artifact is nevertheless claimed to be faithful.
6. A required positive witness is absent within a non-truncated bound that should cover it, such as:
   - evidence-before-risk ordering;
   - risk-before-evidence ordering;
   - legal-present completion;
   - legal-absent completion;
   - one-iteration completion;
   - two-iteration completion separated by `Correct Defect`.
7. A prohibited witness is admitted, especially:
   - release before the first completed verification iteration;
   - verification beginning before both risk and evidence complete;
   - archive before release;
   - legal review after release;
   - repeated configuration/inspection without `Correct Defect`.
8. The conversion forces legal review before verification, or forces verification completion before legal review, thereby losing the source absence of order.
9. Optional legal behavior collapses: legal review becomes mandatory, impossible, or appears as visible extra work on the skip branch.
10. The round-tripped net reaches a trace ending in `Archive Change` but fails exact equality with the final marking.
11. Visible labels are duplicated, renamed, dropped, or merged in a way that makes business-activity trace comparison ambiguous.
12. Bounded exploration truncates, but the artifact is nevertheless certified as behaviorally equivalent.
13. BPMN gateway structure cannot be interpreted consistently with the required AND-style synchronization before release.
14. A loop rendering permits zero verification iterations, permits `Correct Defect` before the first inspection, or permits a repeated trial body without correction.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

The canonical stored model should be the **source POWL definition**, including its explicit operators, partial-order edges, silent transition, and loop structure.

Reasons:

- The POWL expresses the intended semantic distinctions directly:
  - partial order rather than merely diagrammed branches;
  - optionality via XOR with a silent transition;
  - loop body and redo scope;
  - synchronization constraints;
  - absence of an edge between legal review and verification.
- It is the only representation in this task explicitly designated as authoritative.
- It avoids treating BPMN layout or generated routing nodes as the source of truth.

Recommended artifact hierarchy:

| Artifact | Status | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Version-controlled POWL source code | Authoritative | Semantic definition and controlled-change source model |
| POWL visualization | Derivative | Technical review aid |
| POWL-derived accepting Petri net with initial/final markings | Derivative but semantic audit checkpoint | Soundness, reachability, witness, and round-trip validation |
| BPMN from accepting Petri net | Derivative stakeholder/interchange model | Communication, BPMN-compatible tooling |
| Process tree, if conversion succeeds | Optional derivative | Compact block-structured explanation only |
| BPMN from process tree | Optional derivative | Secondary visualization, never presumed equivalent solely because generation succeeded |
| Validation report, trace sets, tool version, conversion parameters | Required evidence | Reproducibility and acceptance decision |

For stakeholder visualization, use the **BPMN generated from the POWL-derived accepting Petri net**, accompanied by explicit annotations about optional legal review, trial-loop semantics, and the release join.

A process-tree intermediate should be allowed only as a conditional, non-authoritative derivative:

- It may be generated and reviewed if `convert_to_process_tree(...)` succeeds.
- It must pass the same round-trip and bounded witness checks.
- Failure to derive a tree is acceptable and should not be “repaired” by manually simplifying away concurrency or loop semantics.
- A successful tree should not replace POWL as canonical because tree conversion may normalize behavior or hide distinctions important to the original partial order.

To avoid round-trip drift:

1. Edit only the canonical POWL source.
2. Regenerate Petri, BPMN, and optional tree artifacts after every source edit.
3. Store PM4Py version, conversion code revision, generated artifact hashes, and validation results.
4. Compare each generated net to the freshly generated POWL-derived reference under the declared bounds.
5. Do not edit generated BPMN as the master model. If a stakeholder-requested BPMN edit is needed, translate the intended change back into POWL, review it semantically, then regenerate all derivatives.
6. Require manual semantic review whenever conversion structure, PM4Py version, or validation bounds change.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if all diagnostics and bounded tests pass, the proposed validation cannot establish all of the following:

1. **Unbounded behavioral equivalence:** Bounded trace exploration cannot prove equality of the complete languages for arbitrary numbers of verification-loop repetitions or arbitrary tau behavior.

2. **True concurrency preservation:** Observing both visible interleavings does not prove that the BPMN or round-tripped Petri net preserves the same causal/concurrent token semantics as the source partial order.

3. **Correctness of PM4Py conversion implementation:** Successful conversion and diagnostics do not prove the converter has no implementation defect or version-specific semantic discrepancy.

4. **Stakeholder interpretation:** Behavioral correctness of the underlying model cannot ensure that readers will correctly understand generated BPMN gateways, bypass paths, loop markers, or layout.

5. **Domain-policy adequacy:** The source itself may omit real-world constraints, such as legal-review eligibility criteria, defect severity rules, approval roles, timing constraints, resource constraints, or exception handling. Conversion validation cannot establish requirements that are not represented in the source.

6. **Semantic identity of labels:** Label-based trace checks assume that labels retain their intended business meaning. They cannot establish that a renamed or visually similar activity still represents the same organizational action.
