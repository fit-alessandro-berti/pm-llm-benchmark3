# 1. Assumptions and notation

All times are 2026-05-12. Reported time $\hat t$ is used for calculations unless stated. For a case $i$:

- $a_i$: external arrival, S1 queue entry
- $s_{1i},e_{1i}$: reported S1 start/end; $s_{2i},e_{2i}$: reported S2 start/end
- $W_1=s_1-a$, $X_1=e_1-s_1$, sojourn in S1 $=e_1-a=W_1+X_1$
- $W_2=s_2-e_1$, $X_2=e_2-s_2$, sojourn in S2 $=e_2-e_1=W_2+X_2$
- total sojourn for a completed case $=e_2-a$

**A1 Event order and ties.** Intervals are $[t,\text{next }t)$. Events reported at $t$ are applied at $t$ before the bin starting at $t$ is evaluated. Simultaneous arrival and start therefore do not count as queueing at that instant. This affects instantaneous snapshots by at most one case but does not affect time-integrated WIP except at measure-zero instants. Boundary events at 10:00/12:00 shift interval counts by one if the opposite tie rule is used.

**A2 Rounding.** A reported $t$ means actual time approximately in $[t-2.5,t+2.5]$ minutes. A difference of two reported times has worst-case rounding error $\pm5$ min and, under independent uniform rounding, SD about $2.0$ min. Arrival is from a different system but rounded identically, so no smaller error is claimed for waits. Ranges below are first given on the reported scale; add $\pm5$ min worst case to any single difference.

**A3 Capacity and availability.** S1 has I1,I2; S2 has C1,C2, each single-task. They are available 08:00–14:00 except C2 unavailable 10:55–11:10 (15 min). Available resource-minutes are therefore S1 $2\times360=720$, S2 $360+345=705$.

**A4 No bypass, no FIFO assumption.** Every case must pass S1 then S2. Queue discipline is not assumed; only recorded order is used. S1 completion is S2 queue entry; S2 completion is system departure.

**A5 Status semantics.** MISSING start means the activity occurred, resource and completion are known, start is not. OPEN means started, not completed by 14:00: elapsed service is a lower bound, final service is right-censored. Blank S2 with completed S1 means waiting for S2 at 14:00. Blank S1 means waiting for S1 at 14:00.

**A6 Single-task bound for MISSING starts.** For a missing start on a resource, the start cannot precede queue entry or the previous completion on that same resource, and cannot follow its own completion. Resource sequencing is taken from reported completions on that resource. Apparent violations by more than rounding would require undocumented help, shared execution, or logging delay; that possibility is treated as a hypothesis, not silently assumed away.

**A7 Turnaround gaps.** Time between a resource completion and the next start is not counted as service. It may be setup, idle, logging, or rounding. Whether a systematic 5-minute turnaround exists is tested as Scenario B, not assumed for logical bounds.

**A8 No other downtime.** No breaks or unreported outages are assumed. Idle time while the station queue is non-empty is therefore informative about setup/assignment, not about lack of work.

**Imputation labels.** I1 = logical bounds only. I2 = pattern-central: missing start $=$ previous completion on same resource $+5$ min, clipped to I1 bounds. I3 = typical-service imputation, truncated to I1 bounds, or, in a diagnostic variant, allowed to overlap the previous case. Central values in tables are I2 unless stated; logical ranges are always shown beside them.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

Waiting, service and sojourn are as defined in Section 1. For missing starts, $W$ and $X$ are complementary: $W+X$ equals the known station sojourn, so the split is bounded but the sojourn is not uncertain (except rounding).

For open activities, elapsed service $=14{:}00-s$ is reported and final service is $\ge$ elapsed. For blank activities, elapsed wait $=14{:}00-$ queue-entry is a lower bound and service elapsed is zero.

WIP at time $t$: number arrived with $a\le t$ and not yet departed S2. Station WIP is defined analogously between queue entry and station completion or 14:00. For missing starts the total station WIP is known; the queue-versus-in-service split is uncertain by at most one case in the interval $[$earliest start, completion$]$.

Time-integrated mean WIP over window length $T=360$:
$$\bar L=\frac1T\sum_i \big(\min(\text{departure}_i,14{:}00)-a_i\big)$$
and similarly per station using station entry/exit. This is an accounting identity, exact on the reported scale up to rounding.

Throughput in an interval $=$ completions in interval $/$ interval length. Busy time is the sum of service intervals clipped to the window; open service contributes only elapsed time to 14:00. Utilization $=$ busy resource-minutes $/$ available resource-minutes from A3. Utilization from completed cases alone would discard open and missing service and is not used.

Little's Law $L=\lambda W$ in steady state is used only as a check in Section 5/6 discussion, because the window starts empty, ends with 8 cases in system, and arrivals are not balanced with departures. The integrated identity above is the defensible version here.

Lower/central/upper estimates: I1 logical low/high; I2 central; I3 and overlap variants as sensitivity. Monte-Carlo multiple imputation would, under I1, draw each missing start uniformly in its feasible interval (respecting $W+X=$ constant), draw open final services from the observed service distribution truncated below at elapsed, and propagate rounding by adding independent $U(-2.5,2.5)$ to each reported timestamp. The code implements the deterministic bounds and the three point scenarios; the resulting WIP and sojourn totals are invariant to missing-start draws, only queue/service splits and busy times move.

# 5. Bottleneck and temporal interpretation

## Evidence

Both stations are heavily loaded, so a single utilization number cannot identify one bottleneck. The time pattern can.

Early window. External arrivals of 7/h in the first hour exceed the theoretical capacity of both stations: S1 $2\times60/28\approx4.3$/h and S2 $2\times60/40=3.0$/h. S1 waits rise from 5 min (P01,P02) to 35–50 min by P05–P08, and S1 queue reaches 6 by 10:00 while S2 queue is only 2–3. S2 is initially starved: C1 first starts 08:30 and C2 08:40, because no case has cleared S1 before then, giving S2 only 65% central utilization in 08–10 despite the arrival surge. Observably, S1 is the first binding constraint.

Middle window (10–12). S1 remains dominant on every queue measure: mean S1 WIP 7.58 versus S2 3.88, S1 queue peak 7, S1 cohort waits averaging 78 min (P09–P14) and reaching 95–100 min. S2 waits are also rising (cohort mean 37.5, with P08 55 and P10 50) and S2 central utilization is 89% of available time, so S2 is saturated too, but its backlog is smaller because S1 releases cases at only 3.5/h.

Late window (12–14). The bottleneck observably shifts. External arrivals fall to one case, S1 completions continue at 3.5/h from backlog, S1 mean WIP falls to 4.92 and S1 queue falls from 6 to 1, while S2 mean WIP rises to 5.38, S2 completions reach 3.0/h (the nominal S2 capacity), S2 cohort waits average 56 min, and at 14:00 four of the eight cases in system are waiting for S2 versus one waiting for S1. The backlog has transferred downstream.

## Hypotheses

Structurally, S2 is the slower station per server (service about 40 versus 28 min), so in sustained operation above about 3/h it would be the long-run bottleneck. S1 manifests first because the arrival surge hits the front station first and because S1 releases the initial cases. A systematic turnaround gap would strengthen this reading: 25 of 27 known adjacent same-resource pairs are separated by exactly 5 reported minutes, exceptions being the documented C2 outage, one 10-minute C2 gap, and one 0-minute C1 touch. If that 5 minutes is real setup, effective cycle capacity is about $2\times60/33\approx3.6$/h for S1 and $2\times60/45\approx2.7$/h for S2. The pattern is consistent enough to use as a central scenario, but rounding alone could also manufacture apparent 5-minute gaps, so it is not proof of setup.

## Maintenance interval

C2 finishes P06 at 10:50, is unavailable 10:55–11:10, and starts P08 at 11:15. P08 had been ready since 10:20. The 25-minute C2 idle gap is therefore mostly accounted for by the outage plus the usual 5-minute gaps before and after it. A counterfactual start at about 10:55 would have given P08 a wait near 35 instead of 55 min and an end near 11:40 instead of 12:00, shifting at most about 20 case-minutes of S2 WIP out of 10–12. That is a direct effect on P08 and probably on P11's start, not the cause of the overall queues: S2 was already overloaded by load, and C1 was simultaneously busy. No evidence links the outage to the S1 queue.

Stable conclusions across all scenarios: station sojourns for P05,P07,P11,P15 (because $W+X$ is known); total WIP integrals (mean 9.51 overall; S1 5.79, S2 3.72) and total peak 13; arrival and completion rates; the rising wait trends; utilization above roughly 70% at both stations; the late shift of backlog to S2 (at 14:00, 4 waiting for S2 versus 1 for S1, independent of missing starts); and the conclusion that completed-case means understate true sojourn because long cases are censored.

Unstable or only scenario-dependent conclusions: the exact wait/service split for the four missing starts; utilization to within about 5 percentage points (plus rounding); whether P05 and especially P11 had genuinely short S2 services or overlapped/shared a resource; the exact S2 queue at 12:00 (3 or 4); and mean S2 service, since excluding P05/P11 (whose feasible services are at most 30/25) inflates the observed mean of 40 if missingness is related to short service.

Right censoring is material. Final total sojourns for P15–P22 are all strictly greater than the elapsed values in Section 3. If open services finish with typical durations, P15 would finish roughly 10–15 min after 14:00, P17 roughly 35–40 min after, and P21 near 14:00–14:05; these are illustrative, not observed. The four cases waiting for S2 at 14:00 will see their S2 waits increase by at least the time until a C1/C2 slot frees.

Rounding sensitivity: individual waits/services carry $\pm5$ min worst case. Means over 12–19 cases carry roughly $\pm1$–2 min under independent rounding, far smaller than the observed trends (S1 waits 5 to about 100 min). Time-integrated mean WIP has worst-case rounding error about $\pm0.3$ cases. The main conclusions survive rounding; exact starts for touching events (P07 end/P09 start at 11:00) and P17's 5-minute elapsed service do not.

Little's-Law check: naively applying $\lambda W$ to completed cases only gives $2.33/\text{h}\times146\text{ min}\approx5.7$, versus actual mean WIP 9.51, because completed cases are early and short and 8 cases remain in system. For S1, completed-only gives $20/360\times92.25\approx5.13$ versus integrated mean 5.79, the gap being exactly the censored P21/P22 time. In the near-plateau 10–12 (total WIP 11–13), S1 entry rate 4/h and mean S1 WIP 7.58 imply mean S1 sojourn about 114 min, close to observed late S1 sojourns of 110–130 min; S2 entry 3.5/h and mean 3.88 imply about 66 min, close to observed S2 sojourns of 50–100 min. This is an order-of-magnitude consistency check only: arrivals and departures are not balanced (8 arrivals versus 5 S2 completions in 10–12), so steady-state Little's Law is not defensible as an exact identity in any subperiod.

            x_low.append(minutes(r[s_col], r[e_col])); x_high.append(minutes(r[s_col], r[e_col]))
            w_cent.append(minutes(entry_i, r[s_col])); x_cent.append(minutes(r[s_col], r[e_col]))
        elif s_status == "MISSING":
            w_low.append(minutes(entry_i, r[f"s{st}_start_low"])); w_high.append(minutes(entry_i, r[f"s{st}_start_high"]))
            x_low.append(minutes(r[f"s{st}_start_high"], r[e_col])); x_high.append(minutes(r[f"s{st}_start_low"], r[e_col]))
            w_cent.append(minutes(entry_i, r[f"s{st}_start_central"])); x_cent.append(minutes(r[f"s{st}_start_central"], r[e_col]))
        elif s_status == "KNOWN" and e_status == "OPEN":
            w_low.append(minutes(entry_i, r[s_col])); w_high.append(minutes(entry_i, r[s_col]))
            elapsed = minutes(r[s_col], END_WINDOW)
            x_low.append(elapsed); x_high.append(elapsed)  # busy-to-14:00 only; final service is censored
            w_cent.append(minutes(entry_i, r[s_col])); x_cent.append(elapsed)
        elif s_status == "BLANK":
            elapsed_w = minutes(entry_i, END_WINDOW)
            w_low.append(elapsed_w); w_high.append(elapsed_w); w_cent.append(elapsed_w)
            x_low.append(0.0); x_high.append(0.0); x_cent.append(0.0)
        else:
            w_low.append(np.nan); w_high.append(np.nan); x_low.append(np.nan); x_high.append(np.nan)
            w_cent.append(np.nan); x_cent.append(np.nan)
    df[f"s{st}_wait_low"] = w_low; df[f"s{st}_wait_high"] = w_high; df[f"s{st}_wait_central"] = w_cent
    df[f"s{st}_service_low"] = x_low; df[f"s{st}_service_high"] = x_high; df[f"s{st}_service_central"] = x_cent

df["total_sojourn_or_elapsed"] = [minutes(r["arrival"], r["s2_end"] if pd.notna(r["s2_end"]) else END_WINDOW) for _, r in df.iterrows()]
print("CASE-LEVEL (reported minutes; low/high are logical bounds, central is pattern I2)")
print(df[["case","s1_wait_low","s1_wait_high","s1_service_low","s1_service_high","s2_wait_low","s2_wait_high",
          "s2_service_low","s2_service_high","total_sojourn_or_elapsed","state"]].to_string(index=False))

for st in [1, 2]:
    observed = df[(df[f"s{st}_start_status"] == "KNOWN") & (df[f"s{st}_end_status"] == "KNOWN")]
    w = (observed[f"s{st}_start"] - (observed["arrival"] if st == 1 else observed["s1_end"])).dt.total_seconds() / 60
    x = (observed[f"s{st}_end"] - observed[f"s{st}_start"]).dt.total_seconds() / 60
    print(f"S{st} observed wait mean/median/n: {w.mean():.1f}/{w.median():.1f}/{len(w)}; service mean/median/n: {x.mean():.1f}/{x.median():.1f}/{len(x)}")

# WIP grid, events at t applied at t; central starts split queue vs service, total WIP is start-invariant
grid = pd.date_range(START_WINDOW, END_WINDOW, freq="5min")
rows = []
for t in grid:
    arrived = df[df["arrival"] <= t]
    departed = arrived[arrived["s2_end"].notna() & (arrived["s2_end"] <= t)]
    in_system = arrived.drop(departed.index)
    def in_service(col_start_c, col_end):
        s = in_system[col_start_c]
        e = in_system[col_end]
        return int(((s <= t) & ((e.isna()) | (e > t))).sum())
    q1 = int(len(in_system) - in_service("s1_start_central", "s1_end") - in_service("s2_start_central", "s2_end") - int(((in_system["s1_end"].notna()) & (in_system["s2_start_central"].isna())).sum()) - 0)
    # queue counts computed directly for the two named cases at 14:00 and generally as entered-not-started
    q1_direct = int(((in_system["arrival"] <= t) & ((in_system["s1_start_central"].isna()) | (in_system["s1_start_central"] > t))).sum())
    q2_direct = int(((in_system["s1_end"].notna()) & (in_system["s1_end"] <= t) & ((in_system["s2_start_central"].isna()) | (in_system["s2_start_central"] > t))).sum())
    rows.append({"t": t, "WIP_total": len(in_system), "S1_queue_central": q1_direct, "S1_service_central": in_service("s1_start_central", "s1_end"),
                 "S2_queue_central": q2_direct, "S2_service_central": in_service("s2_start_central", "s2_end")})
wip = pd.DataFrame(rows)
print("WIP SNAPSHOTS (central split; total WIP is exact on reported scale)")
print(wip[wip["t"].isin(pd.to_datetime(['2026-05-12 08:00','2026-05-12 09:00','2026-05-12 10:00','2026-05-12 11:00','2026-05-12 12:00','2026-05-12 13:00','2026-05-12 14:00']))].to_string(index=False))
print("Peak total WIP:", int(wip["WIP_total"].max()), "at", wip.loc[wip["WIP_total"].idxmax(), "t"])

def clip_minutes(start, end, a, b):
    if pd.isna(start) or pd.isna(end):
        return 0.0
    return max(0.0, (min(end, b) - max(start, a)).total_seconds() / 60.0)

intervals = [(pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 08:00'), pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 10:00')),
             (pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 10:00'), pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 12:00')),
             (pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 12:00'), pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 14:00')),
             (START_WINDOW, END_WINDOW)]
print("SUBPERIOD MEAN WIP (integrated; S1 segment arrival-to-S1end, S2 segment S1end-to-S2end, censored at 14:00)")
for a, b in intervals:
    s1_min = sum(clip_minutes(r["arrival"], r["s1_end"] if pd.notna(r["s1_end"]) else END_WINDOW, a, b) for _, r in df.iterrows())
    s2_min = sum(clip_minutes(r["s1_end"], r["s2_end"] if pd.notna(r["s2_end"]) else END_WINDOW, a, b) for _, r in df.iterrows() if pd.notna(r["s1_end"]))
    dur = (b - a).total_seconds() / 60
    print(a.strftime('%H:%M'), b.strftime('%H:%M'), f"S1 mean {s1_min/dur:.2f} S2 mean {s2_min/dur:.2f} total {(s1_min+s2_min)/dur:.2f}")

print("UTILIZATION SCENARIOS (busy-to-14:00; open contributes elapsed only)")
for st, avail in [(1, 720), (2, 705)]:
