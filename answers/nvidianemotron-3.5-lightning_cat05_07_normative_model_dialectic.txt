M0 is obsolete, incomplete, incorrectly scoped, and contradicted by better evidence of operational practice. The defense concludes that M0 is a narrow control-flow model that does not incorporate pilot/emergency overlays, data-aware eligibility, resource rules, or iterative practice. Model revision, not strict enforcement of an incomplete standard, is the appropriate corrective path.

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

A layered architecture is proposed, consisting of five levels, each residing in the most appropriate formalism:

**Level 1 – Revised Control-Flow Model (POWL)**  
- Represents the core, acyclic control-flow: Register_Supplier  (concurrent Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review)  XOR decision among standard-approved, low-risk auto, emergency-provisional, and reject branches  Notify_Requester  Close_Case.  
- Encodes the four substantive routes: (a) standard manual-approval route (Manual_Procurement_Approval  Activate_Supplier); (b) low-risk auto-approval route (Auto_Approve  Activate_Supplier, eligibility constrained by overlay); (c) emergency/provisional route (Sanctions_Check  provisional Activate_Supplier, with Quality_Review and Manual_Procurement_Approval permissible afterward per overlay constraints); (d) rejection and closure path.  
- **Belongs in POWL:** Pure control-flow semantics; sequential, branching, and notification/closure steps.

**Level 2 – Data-Aware Eligibility/Amount/Time Rules (pandas/datacheck constraints)**  
- Enforces: risk-level restrictions (Auto_Approve only for LOW-risk DE in approved corporate group); spend caps (EUR 20,000 until retrospective approval under FD-7); effective/expire timestamps (PD-3: 2026-06-01 to 2026-07-31; FD-7: 2026-07-10 to 2026-07-12); Quality_Review timing limits (2 business days after provisional activation per PD-7; 2 business days daily monitoring).  
- Implemented as pandasbased eventvalidators or DECLARE-style logupdates triggered at event time; not expressible in POWL alone.  
- **Belongs in pandas/datachecks:** Conditional, data-dependent enforcement; immutable timestamp comparisons; eligibility snapshot validation.

**Level 3 – Resource and Conflict-of-Interest Rules (organizational controls)**  
- Mandates: requester and manual approver must be different humans; no spouse/close-relative relationship in same reporting line without documented waiver; alias resolution with evidence trail.  
- Enforced via organizational policy (HR onboarding checks, disclosure forms), not via process-model semantics.  
- **Belongs in organizational controls:** Humanresource procedures, disclosure management, aliasresolution audit logs.

**Level 4 – Policy-Overlay Governance for Pilots and Emergencies (DECLARE/logskeleton constraints)**  
- Captures time-bounded, condition-specific overlays: PD-3 (LOW-risk DE in approved corporate group Auto_Approve; sanctions + master-data validation before activate; Quality_Review 2bdays post-provisional), FD-7 (provisional activation allowed; sanctions before activate; spend EUR 20k; daily monitoring; Quality_Review 2bdays). Each overlay has immutable effective_start, effective_end, authority_chain, and monitoring_requirements.  
- Implemented as DECLAREstyle temporal constraints or a policytable keyed by rule_version_id and event timestamp.  
- **Belongs in DECLARE/logskeleton:** Temporal, conditional rules with start/end dates and authority predicates.

**Level 5 – Explicit Scope Rules for Rejected Leads, Reactivations, and Withdrawals (separate procedure definitions)**  
- Defines: whether M0 applies pre- or post-pre-screen acceptance for rejected leads; reactivation procedure (whether prior Quality_Certification suffices, or full re-onboarding with Register_Supplier and Quality_Review is required); withdrawal handling (case closure without Notify_Requester/Close_Case if withdrawn predecision).  
- Implemented as separate procedure documents referenced by the main model, with clear entry/exit conditions and linkage to M0 decision nodes.  
- **Belongs in procedural policy/organizational controls:** Standalone SOPs, not embedded in the control-flow model.

**Crosscutting data proposal (immutable audit trail):**  
Every event log entry must carry: stable `notification_id` (UUID, created at Register_Supplier, never mutated); `rule_version_id` (identifier of the policy version active at event time); `eligibility_snapshot` (dict with risk_level, country, corporate_group, effective_date); `actor_aliases` (list of human IDs with mapping evidence, primary alias used for resource checks); `delegation_evidence` (optional, for approved approver substitutions); `emergency_pilot_authority` (flag: NONE, PD-3, FD-7, indicating which overlay was in effect); `effective_timestamp` (immutable UTC when the rule version took effect); `record_timestamp` (immutable UTC of event creation). This schema supports all five levels and enables automated conformance checking beyond POWL’s control-flow scope.

# Concurrent checks: Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review are unordered (concurrent)

# No explicit edge between sanctions and quality means they are concurrent in the partial order.

standard_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[manual_approval, activate])
standard_branch.order.add_edge(manual_approval, activate)

# ---- Event/data-collection proposal (in-memory schema) ----
# Each event log entry should include, at minimum, the following fields:
# - notification_id: stable UUID, immutable upon creation, linked across CRM and onboarding log.
# - rule_version_id: identifier of the policy version in effect at event time (corresponds to policy_overlay rule_id).
# - eligibility_snapshot: dict with keys risk_level, country, corporate_group, effective_date.
# - actor_aliases: list of human identifiers with alias-mapping evidence; primary alias used for resource checks.
# - delegation_evidence: optional, for approved approver substitutions (e.g., signed delegation form).
# - emergency_pilot_authority: flag indicating FD-7 or PD-3 in effect at event timestamp (values: NONE, PD-3, FD-7).
# - effective_timestamp: immutable UTC timestamp of when the rule version took effect (for audit).
# - record_timestamp: immutable UTC timestamp of event creation (for traceability).
# These fields enable automated conformance checking against Levels 25 and support the
# policy-overlay governance described in Section 6.

# ---- Limitation statement ----
# POWL by itself cannot enforce amount (EUR 20k spend cap), risk (LOW-risk restriction for Auto_Approve),
# date (effective/expire windows), identity (actor aliases distinct), or authority (emergency pilot authorization)
# conditions. The revised model above approximates control-flow; all conditional, data-aware, and authority
# rules are encoded in the policy_overlay table and accompanying data-collection proposal. If a requested
# behavior is awkward or unsafe to encode in a single POWL (e.g., repeatable Request_More_Info with
# data-dependent termination), the Loop approximation is used with a clear statement that full semantics
# require data-aware checking outside POWL. The code implements the most defensible approximation rather
# than hiding the limitation behind an overly complex or unsafe POWL construct.
