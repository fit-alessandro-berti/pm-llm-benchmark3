

# 1. Objectives and constraints

**Optimization Objectives**
1.  **SLA Protection:** Prioritize urgent cases and those near deadline.
2.  **Service Quality:** Minimize rework and appeal risk by enforcing skill requirements.
3.  **Operational Efficiency:** Balance throughput with continuity to reduce handover costs.
4.  **Workload Fairness:** Distribute assignments evenly while respecting overtime risk limits.
5.  **Capability Building:** Ensure a quota of assignments supports documented training priorities.

**Hard Constraints (Compliance)**
1.  **Skill Thresholds:** High-priority cases require skill level  2 (or supervised). Security Review requires skill 3 (or supervised). Final Approval requires skill 2–3.
2.  **Batch Limit:** No resource receives more than 4 new assignments in a single batch.
3.  **Overtime Limits:** Resources with "high" overtime risk cannot receive new assignments unless the case is urgent and no safer alternative exists.
4.  **Continuity Thresholds:** Do not break continuity on high-complexity/high-cost cases unless the current owner is skill 0/1 without supervision.

**Soft Constraints (Preferences)**
1.  **Training Quota:** At least 20% of normal/low-priority assignments must support documented training priorities.
2.  **Reassignment Limits:** Avoid adding to cases with >2 reassignments unless necessary for SLA.
3.  **Batching:** Route Quality Check cases with SLA >24h to batchable resources.

# 2. Assignment policy

**Algorithm: Weighted Multi-Criteria Decision**

1.  **Eligibility Filtering**
    *   Filter resources by `required_skill` matrix (Score > 0).
    *   Apply Security/Approval hard rules (Skill 3 for Security; Skill 2-3 for Approval).
    *   Exclude "high overtime risk" resources for non-urgent cases.

2.  **Scoring Function**
    For each eligible resource $r$ for case $c$:
    *   **SLA Urgency Score:** $1 / (SLA\_remaining\_hours + 1)$
    *   **Skill Match Score:** $Skill(r, Activity) \times 10$
    *   **Continuity Score:** $+15$ if $r == current\_owner$ (unless Skill $\le 1$ and no supervision)
    *   **Workload Score:** $-5 \times (Open\_Cases(r) + New\_Assignments(r))$
    *   **Training Score:** $+20$ if $r$ has `Training Priority` matching $Activity$
    *   **Resilience Score:** $+5$ if $r$ is not the sole specialist (avoids bottleneck)

3.  **Selection & Tie-Breaking**
    *   Select highest total score.
    *   If tied, prefer current owner to minimize handover cost.
    *   Verify `Batch Limit` (4 new assignments). If exceeded, move to next best.
    *   Ensure `Training Quota` is on for the batch; if not, override selection for normal/low cases to match training priority.

4.  **Supervision Check**
    *   If a lower-skill resource (1–2) is selected for a high-skill requirement (Security/Approval), verify a supervisor (Skill 3) is available to monitor.

# 5. Explanation to workers

> **Why these assignments?**
> We are balancing speed with quality. Urgent cases like C001 go directly to specialists (R8) to meet the 3-hour deadline, even if it moves the case from the current owner. For ongoing work like C003 and C007, we keep the same person to avoid the time and errors that happen when cases switch hands repeatedly.
>
> We also make room for development: Cases like C009 and C012 are assigned to staff with the right learning goals. This requires supervision by a lead resource to ensure you stay on track. Finally, we are capping new assignments per person to prevent burnout—this policy protects everyone's capacity.
