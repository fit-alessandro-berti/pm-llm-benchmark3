## 1. Conformance semantics

Each observation is judged on three separate axes: what the norm says (status and scope), what probably happened in the business, and how trustworthy the record is. A finding keeps its formal class even when a data-quality explanation is likely; the alternative is recorded beside it, not instead of it.

- **Forbidden behavior**: an observed relation that matches a row marked forbidden (N3, T6, E10). One occurrence is enough, because the norm sets no tolerance band. It stays a formal violation until a data defect is proven.
- **Missing mandatory behavior**: an in-scope object or event that lacks a relation marked mandatory (N1, N4, T1, T2, E1–E8). It needs a denominator ("2 of 200") and a scope check before it counts.
- **Unexpected but unregulated behavior**: a relation that is absent from the norm or marked unspecified (N6, T4, T5, E9). It is not a deviation, at most a candidate for extending the norm.
- **Typical-pattern absence**: a typical relation (N2, N5, T3) that some objects lack. It prompts a check for a named exception and is not a violation.
- **Likely data-quality error**: the record contradicts the norm, but there is independent evidence of an extraction, join, timestamp or labeling defect.
- **Abstraction mismatch**: the business rule and the measured relation differ in kind, for example "eventually" versus "directly follows", or object relation versus event attribute.
- **Insufficient evidence**: the summary cannot distinguish the alternatives because denominators, scopes or object-level traces are missing.

**Open versus closed world.** The norm is closed-world in only two places:

- Forbidden rows: any occurrence counts.
- Mandatory rows within their stated scope: any in-scope gap counts.

Everywhere else it is open-world, so absence from the norm implies nothing. An "additional edge" in a graph comparison is a violation only if metadata marks it forbidden, and a "present edge" does not prove compliance with a mandatory row, because type-level presence says nothing about per-object coverage.

## 3. Detailed reasoning by model family

### OC-DFG

- **Can establish**: for each object type, which activity pairs occur adjacently in object lifecycles, and for how many objects.
- **Cannot establish**: eventual ordering, per-object completeness without a denominator, causes, or whether the objects were in scope ("standard" Orders in N1, external shipment in N4).
- **Counts and scope**: 98 of 100 (N1) and 198 of 200 (N4) carry denominators; the 83 for N2 carries none, so no rate can be stated. The sums 98 + 3 and 72 + 83 exceed 100, which is impossible if each Order has one Create and one Validate event in strict order, so the count basis is unresolved (rank 9).
- **Conforming at one level, suspicious at another**: the edge `Create Order -> Validate Order` exists, so a graph comparison reports it as present, yet two objects lack it.

**Eventually versus directly follows.** Reading an "eventually" rule as a directly-follows edge misleads in both directions:

- For N1 it over-reports. An Order with `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review -> Validate Order` satisfies the business rule and fails the edge check, so the reviewed Orders and the two with a possibly unrecorded review may all be compliant.
- For N3 it under-reports. A dispatch that follows cancellation after any intermediate event does not appear on the `Cancel Order -> Dispatch Package` edge, so 2 is a minimum.

Neither count is final. N4 has no "eventually" wording, so its gap has firmer formal footing than N1's.

### OTG

- **Can establish**: that object types interact or share birth or death events at least once, with an aggregate weight.
- **Cannot establish**: which event type produced the interaction, or whether every required event carries it.
- **Counts and scope**: T1 and T2 are scoped to Add Item and Dispatch Package interactions, but OTG weights are not tied to those events. For forbidden T6, weight 1 suffices; codeath is simply outside the norm.
- **Conforming at one level, suspicious at another**: `(Package, object_interaction, Delivery)` has weight 200 and looks fully conforming, while ET–OT shows only 198 Dispatch–Delivery relations. Other events, such as the confirmation behind the 7 codeath edges, can supply interactions that mask the dispatch gap. The same applies to T1 at weight 1450 against E2 at 1448.

### ET–OT

- **Can establish**: which event types reference which object types, and how many relations exist.
- **Cannot establish**: ordering, object identity across events, or per-event completeness when an event can relate to several objects of one type.
- **Counts and scope**: comparing weights for the same event type exposes gaps (200 vs 198, 93 vs 92, 1450 vs 1448). The first two are confirmed at event level by the context notes; the third is not. E8's exception requires an explicit unapplied-cash type, absent from this snapshot, so the invoice-less payment is formally in scope.
- **Conforming at one level, suspicious at another**: Record Payment–Payment (E7) is fully conforming at 93, while the same events show an E8 gap. The E10 edge is forbidden at type level but probably never happened in the business.

## 4. Mandatory, forbidden, typical, and unregulated behavior

**Mandatory**
- Conforming: E1 (100), E3 (1450), E4 (200), E5 (200), E7 (93).
- N4: 2 of 200 lack the direct edge. Abstraction mismatch if an intermediate event intervenes; out of scope if the packages were not external shipments.
- E6 and T2: 2 Dispatch events lack a Delivery. Becomes a data-quality repair if the carrier reference maps to a real delivery.
- E8: 1 invoice-less payment. Becomes a legitimate exception once typed as unapplied cash.
- E2 and T1: the 1448 vs 1450 gap is a violation only if events lacking an Order are confirmed.
- N1: 2 of 100. Compliant if Validate Order occurs later; missing mandatory if it never does and the Orders are standard.

**Forbidden**
- N3: 2 Orders. Reclassified only if timestamps or package links are proven wrong.
- T6: 1 cobirth. Reclassified only if the events were merged in extraction.
- E10: 1 relation. Formally forbidden; handled as a data-quality incident once the join defect is confirmed.

**Typical**
- N5: 92 of 110 paid; 4 write-offs are named exceptions; 14 open items become a concern only when past credit terms.
- N2: 83 Orders on the direct edge.
- T3: weight 92, consistent with N5.

**Unregulated**
- By design: N6 (1), T4 (100), T5 (3), E9 (3).
- Absent from the norm: `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice` (72), `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review` (3), Manual Compliance Review–Customer (3), Package–Delivery codeath (7). These change class only if the norm is revised.

## 5. PM4Py conformance code sketch

```python
import copy
import pm4py

# --- 1. Observed abstractions (kept for inspection, never edited) -----------------
obs_ocdfg = pm4py.discover_ocdfg(ocel)   # dict; edges["unique_objects"][ot][(a, b)] -> set of objects
obs_otg = pm4py.discover_otg(ocel)       # (object_types, {(ot1, relationship, ot2): weight})
obs_etot = pm4py.discover_etot(ocel)     # (activities, object_types, edges, {(act, ot): weight})

# --- 2. Raw PM4Py diagnostics: structural graph comparison only -------------------
# norm_ocdfg / norm_otg / norm_etot hold ONLY the positively expected edges
# (mandatory + typical). Forbidden and unspecified relations must NOT be put in them:
# a bare graph can only say "edge expected", so they live in NORM_META below.
raw = {
    "ocdfg": pm4py.conformance_ocdfg(ocel, norm_ocdfg),
    "otg": pm4py.conformance_otg(ocel, norm_otg),
    "etot": pm4py.conformance_etot(ocel, norm_etot),
}
raw_snapshot = copy.deepcopy(raw)        # proves later that the raw layer was not overwritten

# --- 3. Norm metadata maintained OUTSIDE the graph structures ----------------------
# Status, semantics and scope cannot be encoded in an OC-DFG dict or an OTG/ET-OT tuple.
# Version this table together with the norm (IDs N*, T*, E*).
NORM_META = {
    ("ocdfg", ("Order", "Create Order", "Validate Order")):     {"id": "N1", "status": "mandatory", "semantics": "eventually"},
    ("ocdfg", ("Order", "Validate Order", "Close Order")):      {"id": "N2", "status": "typical", "semantics": "eventually"},
    ("ocdfg", ("Order", "Cancel Order", "Dispatch Package")):   {"id": "N3", "status": "forbidden", "semantics": "eventually"},
    ("ocdfg", ("Package", "Pack Package", "Dispatch Package")): {"id": "N4", "status": "mandatory", "semantics": "directly",
                                                                 "scope": "external shipment only"},
    ("ocdfg", ("Invoice", "Issue Invoice", "Record Payment")):  {"id": "N5", "status": "typical", "semantics": "eventually"},
    ("ocdfg", ("Order", "Reopen Order", "Close Order")):        {"id": "N6", "status": "unspecified"},
    ("otg", ("Order", "object_interaction", "Item")):           {"id": "T1", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("otg", ("Package", "object_interaction", "Delivery")):     {"id": "T2", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("otg", ("Invoice", "object_interaction", "Payment")):      {"id": "T3", "status": "typical"},
    ("otg", ("Order", "object_interaction", "Customer")):       {"id": "T4", "status": "unspecified"},
    ("otg", ("Order", "object_interaction", "RiskCase")):       {"id": "T5", "status": "unspecified"},
    ("otg", ("Order", "object_cobirth", "Payment")):            {"id": "T6", "status": "forbidden"},
    ("etot", ("Create Order", "Order")):                        {"id": "E1", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("etot", ("Add Item", "Order")):                            {"id": "E2", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("etot", ("Add Item", "Item")):                             {"id": "E3", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("etot", ("Pack Package", "Package")):                      {"id": "E4", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("etot", ("Dispatch Package", "Package")):                  {"id": "E5", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("etot", ("Dispatch Package", "Delivery")):                 {"id": "E6", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("etot", ("Record Payment", "Payment")):                    {"id": "E7", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("etot", ("Record Payment", "Invoice")):                    {"id": "E8", "status": "mandatory",
                                                                 "scope": "except events typed as unapplied cash"},
    ("etot", ("Manual Compliance Review", "RiskCase")):         {"id": "E9", "status": "unspecified"},
    ("etot", ("Sensor Heartbeat", "Invoice")):                  {"id": "E10", "status": "forbidden"},
}

# Analyst annotations (data-quality suspicions, context) are also external metadata.
DQ_NOTES = {
    ("etot", ("Sensor Heartbeat", "Invoice")): "join on reused numeric identifier suspected",
    ("etot", ("Dispatch Package", "Delivery")): "carrier reference present as event attribute",
    ("etot", ("Record Payment", "Invoice")): "possible unapplied cash, event not typed as such",
}

# --- 4. Object/event-level evidence that graph weights cannot provide --------------
EID, ACT, OID, OT = ocel.event_id_column, ocel.event_activity, ocel.object_id_column, ocel.object_type_column
rel = ocel.relations.sort_values([ocel.event_timestamp, EID])


def lifecycle_coverage(ot, a, b):
    """Per object of type `ot` containing `a`: is `b` reached directly / eventually?"""
    out = {"in_scope": 0, "directly": 0, "eventually": 0, "objects_without": []}
    for oid, acts in rel[rel[OT] == ot].groupby(OID)[ACT].apply(list).items():
        if a not in acts:
            continue
        out["in_scope"] += 1      # scope filters (standard Order, external shipment) belong here
        i = acts.index(a)
        direct = i + 1 < len(acts) and acts[i + 1] == b
        later = b in acts[i + 1:]
        out["directly"] += int(direct)
        out["eventually"] += int(later)
        if not later:
            out["objects_without"].append(oid)
    return out


def events_without_type(act, ot):
    """Events of `act` with no related object of type `ot` (event-level view of E-rules)."""
    all_events = set(ocel.events.loc[ocel.events[ACT] == act, EID])
    linked = set(rel.loc[(rel[ACT] == act) & (rel[OT] == ot), EID])
    return sorted(all_events - linked)


# --- 5. Semantic classification layer (separate from `raw`) ------------------------
observed = {}
for ot, flows in obs_ocdfg["edges"]["unique_objects"].items():   # keeps the object type, which the
    for (a, b), objs in flows.items():                           # raw flow diagnostics do not expose
        observed[("ocdfg", (ot, a, b))] = len(objs)
for edge, w in obs_otg[1].items():
    observed[("otg", edge)] = w
for edge, w in obs_etot[3].items():
    observed[("etot", edge)] = w

semantic = []
for key in sorted(set(observed) | set(NORM_META), key=str):
    family, edge = key
    meta = NORM_META.get(key)
    weight = observed.get(key, 0)
    finding = {"family": family, "edge": edge, "weight": weight,
               "norm_id": meta["id"] if meta else None,
               "status": meta["status"] if meta else "not_in_norm",
               "dq_note": DQ_NOTES.get(key), "evidence": None}

    if meta is None:
        finding["class"] = "unexpected_unregulated" if weight else "n/a"       # open world: no violation
    elif meta["status"] == "unspecified":
        finding["class"] = "unregulated_by_design" if weight else "n/a"
    elif meta["status"] == "forbidden":
        finding["class"] = "forbidden_observed" if weight else "ok"            # closed world
        if family == "ocdfg":                                                  # N3 means "after", not "directly after"
            finding["evidence"] = lifecycle_coverage(*edge)
            if finding["evidence"]["eventually"]:
                finding["class"] = "forbidden_observed"
    elif meta["status"] == "typical":
        finding["class"] = "typical_present" if weight else "typical_absent"
        if family == "ocdfg":
            finding["evidence"] = lifecycle_coverage(*edge)                    # exceptions are judged by analysts
    else:                                                                      # mandatory, closed world within scope
        if family == "ocdfg":
            cov = lifecycle_coverage(*edge)
            finding["evidence"] = cov
            target = cov["directly"] if meta.get("semantics") == "directly" else cov["eventually"]
            if target == cov["in_scope"]:
                finding["class"] = "ok"
            elif cov["eventually"] == cov["in_scope"]:
                finding["class"] = "abstraction_mismatch_candidate"            # eventual rule met, adjacency not
            else:
                finding["class"] = "missing_mandatory"
        elif family == "etot":
            missing = events_without_type(*edge)
            finding["evidence"] = {"events_without_relation": missing}
            finding["class"] = "ok" if not missing else "missing_mandatory"
        else:   # OTG: type-level presence only; event-level coverage is checked through the E-rules
            finding["class"] = "ok_at_type_level" if weight else "missing_mandatory_if_scope_occurs"

    if finding["dq_note"] and finding["class"] not in ("ok", "n/a"):
        finding["class"] += "|possible_data_quality"                          # annotate, never downgrade silently
    semantic.append(finding)

assert raw == raw_snapshot, "raw PM4Py diagnostics must stay untouched"
report = {"raw_pm4py": raw, "semantic_layer": semantic, "norm_meta": NORM_META}
```

The raw diagnostics compare edge sets and weights structurally: they list the forbidden `Cancel Order -> Dispatch Package` and the benign `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice` identically as additional flows and report N1 and N4 as present. They inform the interpretation; they are not a business or legal conclusion.

## 7. Investigation and remediation plan

**Immediate containment**
- N3 (rank 1): identify the two Orders and their Packages; stop or recall the shipments if still possible; confirm that the dispatch system checks cancellation status; hold related invoicing.
- T6 (rank 2): place the order–payment pair under finance review and hold any refund or release until its origin is known.
- E10 (rank 3): quarantine the relation in the analysis layer and keep the source data unchanged.

**Data repair**
- Fix the reused-identifier join with type-qualified keys, re-extract, and re-confirm N3 and T6 afterwards.
- Materialize Delivery objects for the two dispatches after verifying the carrier references (E6, T2).
- Type or allocate the invoice-less payment (E8).
- Resolve the Add Item gap (E2) and the unreconciled counts (rank 9) at event level.

**Process investigation**
- Reconstruct object-level traces for ranks 1, 4 and 5 and test whether they are the same two shipments; three separate "2" counts suggest it, but the summaries cannot prove it.
- Test whether the T6 payment is the invoice-less one.
- Check whether the two N1 Orders and the three reviewed Orders were eventually validated.

**Norm revision**
- Adopt the clarifications in section 6, starting with gaps 1 to 6.
- Store status, semantics and scope as versioned metadata beside the graphs.

**Monitoring**
- Alert on any occurrence of N3 (eventually-follows version), T6 and E10.
- Track per-object and per-event coverage for N1, N4, E2, E6 and E8 with denominators.
- Report invoice aging against credit terms for N5.
- Trend, but do not alert on, reviews, reopenings, write-offs and codeath edges; rarity is not a deviation.

## 8. Uncertainty statement

- **Robust**: forbidden relations N3, T6 and E10 are recorded in the data. E6 (2 events) and E8 (1 event) are incomplete at event level. `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice`, `Reopen Order -> Close Order`, Manual Compliance Review–Customer, the risk-review relations, the write-offs and the codeath edges are not violations.
- **Dependent on the norm's incompleteness**: whether the N1 and N4 gaps are violations, whether the invoice-less payment is acceptable, whether the 14 open invoices matter, and how to read T6 for prepayments.
- **Dependent on suspected data quality**: E10 certainly; possibly T6, the N3 timestamps and links, the E2 gap, and the unreconciled counts.
- **Undecidable from graph summaries alone**: whether goods physically shipped after cancellation, whether the anomalies share the same objects, whether Orders are eventually validated, true rates where denominators are missing, and any question of intent or cause.
