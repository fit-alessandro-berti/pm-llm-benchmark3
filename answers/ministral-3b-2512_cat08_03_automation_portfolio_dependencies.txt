## **2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map**
### **Upstream Data Quality Dependencies**
- **A1 (Auto-registration)** depends on clean portal data to avoid misrouting cases into KYC/classification.
- **A2 (Classification recommender)** requires accurate product/exposure data to avoid wrong control paths (e.g., misclassifying a Working-capital as Equipment finance).
- **A3 (KYC pre-classifier)** assumes entity-mismatch resolution capacity is not overwhelmed (A4 may help).
- **A6 (Financial statement extraction)** fails on low-quality PDFs, so upstream document review (A5) is critical.
- **A8 (Committee pack assembly)** depends on:
  - Clean KYC (A3/A4),
  - Normalized financials (A6),
  - Collateral data (A7).

### **Legal/Compliance Controls**
- **Four-eyes principle**: Credit analysis and Committee packaging must be separate for High-exposure cases. **A8 and A9** must enforce this.
- **KYC clearance before signing**: **A9 (Contract generation)** cannot proceed without KYC clearance (A3/A4).
- **Contract terms match Committee decision**: **A9** must validate against the decision (no remediation loops).
- **Audit trail**: All automated suggestions (A2, A3, A6, A9) require human overrides logged.

### **Human Escalation Capacity**
- **Compliance specialists (3 FTE)**: **A3 (KYC pre-classifier)** and **A4 (entity mismatch)** must not overwhelm them.
- **Credit analysts (9 FTE)**: **A6 (normalization)** and **A8 (pack assembly)** must not overload them unless upstream bottlenecks (e.g., A5) are resolved.
- **Relationship managers**: **A5 (missing-document nudges)** must avoid increasing friction; RM training is needed.

### **External Valuation Dependency**
- **A7 (Collateral triage)** reduces wait time but depends on external valuer capacity. Parallelization (e.g., triaging high-risk cases) may help.

### **Core-Banking Integration Constraints**
- **Two-deep integration limit**: Only **A1 (auto-registration)** and **A9 (contract generation)** can be deeply integrated in Year 1.
- **A2, A3, A6** require shallow integration (APIs) but may need deeper integration later.

## **3. Portfolio Alternatives**
### **A. Quick-Win Portfolio (Political Appeal)**
**Modules**: **A1 (Auto-registration), A5 (Missing-document checklist), A10 (E-signature reminders)**
- **Optimizes**: High-frequency labor savings (12k + 3.3k + 7.3k cases/day).
- **Sacrifices**:
  - No control strengthening (violates compliance requirement).
  - **A5** may increase RM friction; **A10** risks customer identity issues.
  - **A1** exposes downstream bottlenecks (KYC/classification errors).

### **B. Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio**
**Modules**: **A3 (KYC pre-classifier), A4 (Entity-mismatch resolution), A6 (Financial statement extraction)**
- **Optimizes**:
  - Eliminates **38% of KYC delays** (A4) and **11% of rework** (A3).
  - Reduces Credit analyst workload by **75%+** (A6).
  - Aligns concurrency (KYC/Financial review) by pre-filtering high-risk cases.
- **Sacrifices**:
  - **A3/A4** are low-frequency but critical for synchronization.
  - Requires **compliance specialist capacity** (3 FTE may be strained).
  - **A6** risks poor performance on low-quality docs.

### **C. Compliance-First Portfolio**
**Modules**: **A2 (Classification recommender), A8 (Committee pack assembly), A9 (Contract generation with controls), A12 (Control remediation workbench)**
- **Optimizes**:
  - Strengthens controls (four-eyes, audit trails, remediation).
  - Reduces **8.5% contract remediation loops** (A12).
  - Ensures **KYC clearance before signing** (A9).
  - Reduces **High-exposure violations** (A8 enforces separation).
- **Sacrifices**:
  - Lower direct labor savings (medium-high vs. high).
  - **A2/A8** require upstream data quality (e.g., A5 must improve).
  - **A9** may delay signing if controls are incomplete.

## **6. Recommended Phased Rollout**
### **Phase 1 (Months 1–3): Compliance-First Pilot**
**Modules**: **A3 (KYC pre-classifier), A8 (Committee pack assembly)**
- **Order**: A3  A8 (requires clean KYC data).
- **Monitoring Metrics**:
  - **KYC clearance time** (target: 2.0h).
  - **Entity mismatch resolution time** (target: <1h).
  - **Committee decision time** (target: 12h).
- **Gate Criteria**:
  - **A3** must reduce KYC rework by **>30%**.
  - **A8** must reduce handoffs between analysts/relationship managers by **>40%**.
- **IT Integration**: Shallow (APIs for KYC data, Committee artifacts).

### **Phase 2 (Months 4–6): Bottleneck Reduction**
**Modules**: **A6 (Financial statement extraction), A9 (Contract generation with controls)**
- **Order**: A6  A9 (requires normalized financials).
- **Monitoring Metrics**:
  - **Credit analysis time** (target: 15h).
  - **Contract generation time** (target: 10h).
  - **Contract remediation rate** (target: <5%).
- **Gate Criteria**:
  - **A6** must normalize **>90% of cases** without manual intervention.
  - **A9** must reduce contract remediation loops by **>50%**.
- **IT Integration**: Deep (core banking for contract generation).

### **Phase 3 (Months 7–12): Scaling and Optimization**
**Modules**: **A2 (Classification recommender), A4 (Entity-mismatch resolution), A10 (E-signature reminders)**
- **Order**: A2  A4  A10 (requires clean classification data).
- **Monitoring Metrics**:
  - **Classification error rate** (target: <5%).
  - **Entity mismatch resolution time** (target: <30min).
  - **Customer signing time** (target: 22h).
- **Gate Criteria**:
  - **A2** must reduce classification errors by **>40%**.
  - **A10** must reduce reminder loops by **>60%**.
- **IT Integration**: Mixed (APIs for classification, portal for e-sign).

## **7. Final Recommendation**
**Portfolio**: **Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (A3 + A4 + A6)**
**Why Not Quick-Win (A1 + A5 + A10)?**
- **Process-mining evidence** shows that **low-frequency but critical activities (A3/A4/A6)** dominate end-to-end performance. Automating high-frequency tasks (A1) without addressing synchronization delays (e.g., KYC mismatches) risks **no improvement in cycle time** and **higher rework**.
- **Compliance requirement** is violated by the quick-win portfolio (no control strengthening).
- **Resource demand** would be unbalanced (e.g., A5 nudges may overwhelm RMs).

**Why Not Compliance-First (A2 + A8 + A9 + A12)?**
- **Budget constraint**: Four modules exceed the limit for Year 1.
- **Prerequisites**: A2 and A8 require upstream data quality (e.g., A5 must improve), which is not prioritized in this phase.
- **Risk of overcomplication**: A3/A4/A6 provide immediate synchronization wins with lower risk.

**Defensible Trade-Off**:
- **Automating high-frequency tasks (A1) first is politically appealing but misleading**—it does not address the **synchronization bottlenecks** that dominate cycle time. The bank’s process-mining data reveals that **low-frequency exceptions (e.g., entity mismatches) block 82% of critical paths**, making them the true leverage points.
- **A3 (KYC pre-classifier) and A4 (entity mismatch resolution)** are the most impactful because:
  - They **eliminate 38% of KYC delays** (A4) and **reduce rework by 11%** (A3).
  - They **align concurrency** (KYC/Financial review) without requiring deep IT integration.
  - They **preserve compliance** (audit trails, human escalation) while reducing resource strain.

**Phased Rollout Justification**:
- **Phase 1 (A3 + A8)**: Proves the synchronization hypothesis by reducing KYC delays and improving Committee decision times.
- **Phase 2 (A6 + A9)**: Scales normalization and contract generation while enforcing controls.
- **Phase 3 (A2 + A4 + A10)**: Refines classification and e-signature adoption with feedback loops.

**Final Answer**: **Prioritize the Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (A3 + A4 + A6)**. It addresses the root causes of end-to-end delays, strengthens controls indirectly, and avoids the pitfalls of high-frequency automation without addressing synchronization. The phased approach ensures incremental value while managing risk.
