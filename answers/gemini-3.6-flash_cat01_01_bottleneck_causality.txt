## 1. Executive causal assessment

* **Provisional verdict:** `mainly confounded`
* **Confidence level:** 85 / 100
* **Strongest evidence for verdict:** Within every observed complexity stratum (Low, Medium, High), cases routed to resource group Orion exhibit shorter mean cycle times than those routed to Nova (Low: 7.50h vs 9.64h; Medium: 17.17h vs 19.38h; High: 31.57h vs 35.00h). Orion's overall higher crude mean cycle time (21.96h vs 15.24h) is a textbook instance of Simpson's Paradox resulting from heavy selection bias: 42.9% of Orion's volume consists of High-complexity cases, compared to only 12.5% for Nova.
* **Strongest evidence against verdict:** True unconfoundedness cannot be asserted from stratified means alone because `resource_group` is near-perfectly confounded with operational shift and arrival load. Orion handles 92.9% of night-shift cases and 100% of high-arrival-load night cases, whereas Nova operates primarily on day shifts (75.0%). Additionally, the dataset conditions strictly on completed cases (`case_completed = 1`), leaving potential sample truncation or abandonment unobserved.
* **Why the proposed 50% reassignment may fail or backfire:** Management's proposal relies on a naive comparison of crude averages. Shifting 50% of Orion's workload to Nova would transfer complex, night-arriving claims to a group that operates mostly during the day and has fewer certified reviewers. Drowning Nova in complex claims under misaligned shift coverage will cause severe queue congestion, inflate wait times, increase rework, and ultimately worsen organization-wide cycle times.

## 6. Intervention and learning plan

### Final Recommendation
Management must halt the proposed blanket 50% reassignment of Orion cases to Nova. Reassigning complex, night-arriving claims to a day-focused resource group with fewer certified reviewers is based on a flawed interpretation of crude averages and will cause severe operational queueing bottlenecks, elevated rework, and degraded service times.

Instead, execute a phased, process-aware intervention. First, address the primary driver of delay by rebalancing reviewer shift coverage and decoupling compliance queues during high-arrival night windows. Second, implement a micro-randomized routing pilot on 15% of Low and Medium complexity day claims to gather unconfounded experimental data on true group capability. Third, transition the routing engine from static group allocation to dynamic, skill- and queue-aware assignment. This approach mitigates operational risk, protects SLA compliance, and yields rigorous causal estimates to guide future operational restructuring.

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Unobserved Shift-Specific Queueing Dynamics:**
   * *Most valuable data field / design change:* Timestamped event logs recording exact queue entry and dispatch times (`queue_start_timestamp`, `review_start_timestamp`) to isolate shift-level arrival bottlenecks from active processing times.
2. **Unmeasured In-Stratum Claim Risk & Complexity:**
   * *Most valuable data field / design change:* Continuous claim risk score or financial indemnity amount (`claim_estimated_value_usd`) to control for residual case-mix variation within Low, Medium, and High complexity tiers.
3. **Truncation and Right-Censoring of Incomplete Claims:**
   * *Most valuable data field / design change:* Longitudinal intake log capturing all open, abandoned, and rejected claims (`case_status`, `intake_timestamp`) over a 90-day window to eliminate completed-case selection bias.
4. **Individual Reviewer Skill Confounding:**
   * *Most valuable data field / design change:* Anonymized reviewer identifier and tenure metric (`reviewer_id`, `reviewer_certification_level`) to separate individual skill effects from group-level operational performance.
5. **Post-Process Decision Quality and Long-Term Outcomes:**
   * *Most valuable data field / design change:* Extended 180-day audit outcome log tracking payout adjustments and litigation flags (`reopen_flag`, `financial_adjustment_amount`) to evaluate true decision accuracy beyond coarse 30-day appeal counts.
