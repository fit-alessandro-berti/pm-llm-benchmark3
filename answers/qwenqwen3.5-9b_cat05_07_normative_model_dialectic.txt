**Event/Data Collection Proposal**
To address N4 (CRM migration) and N5 (Identity):
1.  **Immutable Notification IDs:** All `Notify_Requester` events must generate a `notification_id` at creation. If the CRM link is mutable, the system must capture the `notification_id` in the onboarding log *at the time of notification*, not just later.
2.  **Identity Resolution Table:** Store `requester_id` and `approver_id` with `alias_mapping` (e.g., "john.doe" -> "john.doe@email.com"). Compute `is_conflict` automatically.
3.  **Eligibility Snapshot:** Capture `risk_level` and `supplier_group` at `Register_Supplier` timestamp. Compare against `Activate_Supplier` timestamp to detect Pilot/Standard drift.
4.  **Directive Log:** Every case must reference a `directive_id` (CS-9, PD-3, FD-7) at the `Decision` step. If `directive_id` is null, default to CS-9.
