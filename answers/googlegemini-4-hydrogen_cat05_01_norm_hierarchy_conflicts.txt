**Undisputed Facts**

**Controlling Interpretive Assumptions**
1. **Dimension-Specific Authority (No Single Total Ordering)**: Rather than assuming GPIP-4 invalidates ECD-2 and Source D in toto—or that ECD-2 sweeps away all Board controls—authority is allocated by control domain:

ECD-2 is silent on both (C5), CFO authority does not extend to Compliance or SoD (D3–D4), and organizational precedent expressly excludes new-supplier Compliance clearance.

5. **Evidential Discipline and Burden of Proof**: Contemporaneous external records (telephone logs, supplier email acknowledgments, timestamped signed PDFs) override delayed or skewed ERP timestamps. ## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

> **Proposed Clause GPIP-4 § 7A — Emergency Norm Hierarchy, Delegated Incident Overlays, and Anti-Normalization Safeguards**
>
> 1. **Authority and Hierarchy**: During a declared site or enterprise disruption, a written **Emergency Continuity Directive (ECD)** jointly signed by the COO and Incident Commander—and countersigned by the **CRO (or designated Deputy CRO)** within 12 hours of issuance—constitutes a valid scoped exception under Clause A7. Ordinary emails (including executive guidance) cannot modify GPIP-4 thresholds or substitute for an ECD; any Finance-control interpretation by the CFO must be issued as a dated addendum to the active ECD in the controlled policy repository.
> 2. **Non-Waivable Core Controls**: No ECD or emergency status may waive **(a) Segregation of Duties (A1)**—no requester, committer, or buyer may serve as one of the joint authorizers—or **(b) pre-commitment Compliance screening** for new/unapproved suppliers or purchases above EUR 100,000 (A3). Compliance must maintain an expedited 2-hour incident SLA for pre-commitment sanctions/integrity screening.
> 3. **Scope and Threshold Alignment**: An ECD applies strictly to direct containment, safety, or restoration items required within **24 hours** to prevent imminent harm or halt-in-progress production; multi-day backup stock and non-production facilities (e.g., catering/offices) are excluded. Within a valid ECD: (i) purchases up to **EUR 150,000** may substitute `Plant_Approve` and `Finance_Approve` with pre-commitment joint authorization by two independent IC members (at least one holding delegated budget authority); (ii) purchases **above EUR 150,000** require pre-commitment `Finance_Approve` *and* `Legal_Clearance`; and (iii) `Create_PO` may follow `Commit_to_Supplier` within 24 hours.
> 4. **Retrospective Records and Outage Resilience**: Verbal IC authorizations are valid only if logged via timestamped signed PDF, recorded call, or ERP entry within **4 hours** of authorization. During ERP/system outages, contemporaneous external timestamps (signed PDFs, email acknowledgments, call logs) govern, provided they are reconciled to the ERP within 24 hours of system recovery.
> 5. **Strict Expiry and Anti-Normalization Safeguard**: Every ECD automatically expires at its stated end timestamp. **Verbal extensions are void.** Any extension requires a new written amendment signed by the COO, IC, CFO, and CRO before expiry, capped at **72 hours per renewal**, and any cumulative emergency overlay exceeding **7 days** triggers a mandatory Internal Audit and Board Risk Committee review to prevent emergency normalization.
