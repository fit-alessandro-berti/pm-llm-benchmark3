# Redesigned Diagnostic-Lab Sample Process for Robustness

---

## 1. Stress scenarios

| Scenario | Description | Key Stressors | Case Mix Shift | Disruption Type |
|----------|-------------|---------------|----------------|-----------------|
| **Urgent Surge** | Winter infection wave with high Urgent demand | +40% Urgent, +20% Complex-panel, -30% Routine | Urgent 40%, Complex-panel 25%, Routine 30%, Repeat 5% | Demand mix shift, courier delays (spikes at 10:00/17:00 compressed) |
| **Machine Degradation** | M2 outage (higher failure rate machine fails) | -50% assay capacity, +30% repeats | Urgent 25%, Complex-panel 20%, Routine 45%, Repeat 10% | Equipment outage, increased rework |
| **Specialist Shortage** | Medical validator and specialist review absent (e.g., flu outbreak) | -60% Medical validation capacity, -100% Specialist review | Urgent 20%, Complex-panel 30%, Routine 40%, Repeat 10% | Staff absence, Complex-panel backlog |
| **Courier Chaos** | Extreme weather delays all courier arrivals by 6–12h | +50% intake queue, +20% pre-analytical failures (sample degradation) | Urgent 25%, Complex-panel 15%, Routine 50%, Repeat 10% | Courier delay, sample quality degradation |
| **Complex-Panel Spike** | Outbreak requiring rare tests | +100% Complex-panel, +50% Urgent | Complex-panel 35%, Urgent 30%, Routine 25%, Repeat 10% | Demand mix shift, specialist review bottleneck |

## 2. Bottleneck-migration analysis

### **Urgent Surge**
- **Initial Bottleneck**: **Pre-analytical check** (96% peak utilization, 9.5h median queue) and **Assay machines** (98% utilization, 18h queue) due to Urgent cases bypassing batch wait but fragmenting schedules.
- **Migration**:
  - Urgent-first dispatch (I1) relieves Urgent at **Assay run** but starves **Routine** and **Complex-panel**, increasing **Technical validation** queue (101% utilization in peak).
  - **Medical validation** (93% utilization, 22h queue) becomes next bottleneck as Urgent and Complex-panel cases require it.
  - If **Specialist review** is needed (48h median in peak), it clogs further.
- **Downstream Risk**: **Release result** delays as **Medical validation** backlog grows.

### **Machine Degradation (M2 Outage)**
- **Initial Bottleneck**: **Assay machines** (M1 alone at ~140% load, repeats 30%).
- **Migration**:
  - **Repeat assay** loop (26h median in peak) exacerbates **Assay run** queue.
  - **Technical validation** (101% utilization) struggles with higher repeat volume.
  - **Preparation benches** (89% utilization) may stall if batches are held for M1.
- **Downstream Risk**: **Medical validation** overwhelmed by repeats and Complex-panel cases.

### **Specialist Shortage**
- **Initial Bottleneck**: **Medical validation** (93%  150%+ utilization) and **Hold for specialist review** (36h  72h+ queue).
- **Migration**:
  - **Technical validation** (101% utilization) backs up as cases wait for Medical validation.
  - **Assay run** may slow if **Technical validation** cannot keep up.
- **Downstream Risk**: **Complex-panel** cases pile up in **Hold for specialist review**, increasing cancellations.

### **Courier Chaos**
- **Initial Bottleneck**: **Receive sample** (courier intake 88%  130%+ utilization, 7.5h  20h+ queue).
- **Migration**:
  - **Pre-analytical check** (96% utilization) worsens as samples degrade, increasing **Request recollection** (7h median in peak).
  - **Registration clerks** (85% utilization) spend time on recollection explanations, reducing intake capacity.
- **Downstream Risk**: **Test preparation** starved for samples, but **Assay machines** idle if intake collapses.

### **Complex-Panel Spike**
- **Initial Bottleneck**: **Medical validation** (93%  150%+) and **Specialist review** (87%  130%+).
- **Migration**:
  - **Assay machines** (98% utilization) struggle with multiple assays per Complex-panel case.
  - **Technical validation** (101% utilization) backs up as more cases need Medical validation.
- **Downstream Risk**: **Release result** delayed as **Medical validation** becomes the long pole.

## 3. Robust redesign package

### **Core Interventions (Always Active)**
1. **I6: Early Pre-Analytical Defect Triage**
   - **Why Robust**: Reduces recollection loops (760 normal  780 peak cases) and **Request recollection** queue (7h median in peak). Prevents downstream waste in **Assay run** and **Medical validation**.
   - **Assumptions**: Triage can reject 10–15% of poor samples within 2h without false positives.
   - **Failure Mode**: If triage is too aggressive, may increase **Cancel order** rate (280 normal  540 peak).

2. **I7: Shift Courier Pickup Slots**
   - **Why Robust**: Smooths **Receive sample** spikes (1.0h  7.5h queue in peak). Reduces **Pre-analytical check** congestion.
   - **Assumptions**: Couriers can adjust routes; samples remain stable with delayed pickup.
   - **Failure Mode**: If weather delays persist, may not fully resolve intake compression.

3. **I8: Automate Registration for Digital Orders**
   - **Why Robust**: Reduces **Registration clerks** load (85% peak utilization), freeing them for recollection explanations.
   - **Assumptions**: 30–40% of orders have complete digital metadata.
   - **Failure Mode**: If digital metadata is incomplete, may increase **Manual accession correction** (14h queue in peak).

4. **I4: Cross-Train Technical Validators for Accession Correction**
   - **Why Robust**: **Accession correction specialists** (91% peak utilization, 14h queue) are a critical choke point. Cross-training adds flexibility.
   - **Assumptions**: 2 validators can be trained in 4 weeks; no net loss in **Technical validation** capacity.
   - **Failure Mode**: If **Technical validation** demand spikes (e.g., during **Machine Degradation**), may create a new bottleneck.

### **Conditional Interventions (Scenario-Specific)**
- **I10: Outsource Complex-Panel Overflow** (for **Specialist Shortage** or **Complex-Panel Spike**)
- **I5: Temporary Evening Medical-Validation Shift** (for **Urgent Surge** or **Specialist Shortage**)
- **I3: Dedicate M1 to Urgent/Complex-Panel** (for **Machine Degradation**)

### **Avoided Interventions**
- **I1 (Urgent-first dispatch)**: Causes **Routine** starvation and **Technical validation** overload.
- **I2 (Larger batches)**: Hurts **Urgent** cases (58% bypass rate in peak drops if batches grow).
- **I9 (Batch release)**: Adds unnecessary **Release result** delay.

## 4. Scenario-specific policies

| Scenario | Policy | Trigger On | Trigger Off | Rationale |
|----------|--------|------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Urgent Surge** | Activate **I5 (Evening Medical-Validation Shift)** + **I1 (Urgent-first dispatch at Assay only)** | Urgent cases >30% of mix **AND** Medical validation queue >24h | Urgent cases <20% **OR** Medical validation queue <12h | Prevents **Medical validation** collapse while limiting **Routine** starvation. |
| **Machine Degradation** | Activate **I3 (Dedicate M1 to Urgent/Complex-panel)** + **I6 (Triage)** | M2 outage **OR** Assay machine utilization >95% for >4h | M2 restored **AND** Assay utilization <90% | Protects critical cases; **I6** reduces repeats. |
| **Specialist Shortage** | Activate **I10 (Outsource Complex-Panel)** + **I5 (Evening Medical-Validation Shift)** | Specialist review queue >36h **OR** Medical validation utilization >120% | Specialist review queue <24h **AND** Medical validation utilization <100% | Offloads **Complex-panel** to prevent **Medical validation** gridlock. |
| **Courier Chaos** | Activate **I7 (Shift Courier Slots)** + **I6 (Triage)** | Courier intake queue >10h **OR** >20% samples degraded | Courier queue <5h **AND** degradation <10% | Smooths intake and filters bad samples early. |
| **Complex-Panel Spike** | Activate **I10 (Outsource Complex-Panel)** + **I3 (Dedicate M1)** | Complex-panel >25% of mix **AND** Specialist review queue >48h | Complex-panel <15% **OR** Specialist queue <24h | Prevents **Specialist review** from becoming the long pole. |

## 6. Residual vulnerabilities

1. **Medical Validation as a Universal Choke Point**
   - **Cause**: **Complex-panel** and **Urgent** cases both require it, and **Technical validation** (101% peak utilization) feeds into it. Even with **I5**, a severe **Specialist Shortage** or **Complex-Panel Spike** can overwhelm it.
   - **Redesign-Induced Risk**: **I10 (Outsourcing)** reduces **Complex-panel** load but may not help if **Urgent** cases also surge.

2. **Assay Machine Fragility**
   - **Cause**: **M2** has a higher failure rate; if **M1** is dedicated to **Urgent/Complex-panel** (I3), **Routine** cases on **M2** may face more repeats, worsening **Assay run**  **Repeat assay** loops.

3. **Cross-Training Side Effects (I4)**
   - **Cause**: If **Technical validators** are pulled to **Accession correction**, **Technical validation** (already at 101% in peak) may become the new bottleneck.

4. **Courier Slot Shifts (I7) May Not Scale**
   - **Cause**: If **Courier Chaos** is due to external factors (e.g., road closures), rescheduling slots may not help.

5. **Outsourcing Traceability (I10)**
   - **Cause**: 12h transport delay + partner lab variability may introduce **Complex-panel** result inconsistencies, increasing **Medical validation** rework.

## 7. Minimum monitoring dashboard

### **Leading Indicators (Daily, Peak Periods)**
| Metric | Threshold / Warning Band | Rationale |
|--------|---------------------------|-----------|
| **Pre-analytical check queue time** | >6h (warning), >12h (critical) | Early sign of **Receive sample** or **Courier** issues. |
| **Assay machine utilization (M1 + M2)** | >90% (warning), >95% (critical) | Predicts **Repeat assay** and **Technical validation** backlogs. |
| **Medical validation queue time** | >18h (warning), >24h (critical) | Leading indicator for **Complex-panel** and **Urgent** delays. |
| **Specialist review queue time** | >30h (warning), >48h (critical) | Triggers **I10** or **I5** activation. |
| **Urgent case % in Assay run queue** | >40% (warning), >50% (critical) | Signals **Urgent Surge** or **I1** misconfiguration. |
| **Sample degradation rate (pre-analytical)** | >10% (warning), >15% (critical) | Triggers **I6** (triage) or **I7** (courier slots). |
| **Repeat assay rate** | >10% (warning), >15% (critical) | Indicates **Assay machine** issues (M2 failure) or **Pre-analytical** problems. |

### **Lagging Outcome Metrics (Weekly)**
- **Urgent case p90 cycle time** (Target: <18h)
- **Routine case p90 cycle time** (Target: <72h)
- **Complex-panel p90 cycle time** (Target: <120h)
- **Overall rework rate** (Target: <10%)
- **Cases with >12h queue** (Target: <30%)

### **Policy Triggers**
- **Activate I5**: Medical validation queue >24h **OR** Urgent cases >30%.
- **Activate I3**: Assay utilization >95% for >4h **OR** M2 outage.
- **Activate I10**: Specialist review queue >36h **OR** Complex-panel >25%.
- **Activate I7**: Courier queue >10h **OR** degradation >15%.
