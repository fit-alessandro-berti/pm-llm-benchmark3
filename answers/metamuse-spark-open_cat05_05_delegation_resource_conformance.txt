## 2. Rule Interpretation

**General principles**
* Event-time principle: role, delegation, and absence are evaluated at `time:timestamp` of the *human-action* event, not current DB view. This matters for Eva’s role transition 08-1108-12 and Diego’s IC elevation 2026-07-01.
* Delegation proof: effective interval is defined by the **legally signed instrument timestamp**, not by QMS database creation time. A later DB entry created during migration (D-QM-01 DB record 2026-08-12 09:15) cannot retroactively invalidate a signed PDF 2026-08-09 18:00 that precedes the interval. Conversely, an unregistered email purporting to delegate (D-IC-04) has no effect if the parent delegation prohibits subdelegation.
* Later DB entry  later effective time. Stale master data must be reconciled to the earliest authoritative evidence.

**RR1 – Drafter/approver separation**
` case c: resource(Draft_Change_Request,c) normalized  resource(Approve_Change,c) normalized`. Applies to all risks; no LOW-risk exemption is stated. Alias-normalized human equality.

**RR2 – Implementer/verifier separation**
` case c: resource(Implement_Change,c)  resource(Verify_Implementation,c)` after alias normalization and after reliable `svc_qms` attribution. Service-account unresolved = fail RR2 per RR5.

**RR3 – High-risk approval authority**
`If risk(c)=HIGH then resource(Approve_Change,c)  {QM}  {valid QM delegates}`.
Valid delegate iff:
1. `event_time  delegation.effective_interval`
2. `case.scope  delegation.scope`
3. principal-absence condition satisfied *if required by delegation text*
4. no prohibited subdelegation in chain.
QM principal acting personally while on leave is still QM-authorized (leave is administrative, not revocation), but creates authenticity question.

**RR4 – Emergency substitution**
`HIGH-risk  emergency=true  approved by IC/valid IC delegate  Implement allowed, BUT  Retrospective_QM_Review by QM/valid QM delegate within 2 business days`. 
*Business day definition (site calendar, per evidence):*
* Business days = Mon-Fri; Sat-Sun excluded (2026-08-15/16 are weekend; no holiday in period).
* Counting: **Conservative interpretation adopted:** Day of emergency implement = Day 0; review due by end of 2nd *following* business day (exclusive). E.g., emergency Tue 08-11 13:10  due by EOD Thu 08-13. 
* Alternative inclusive counting (Tue counts as Day 1  due EOD Wed 08-12) is stricter; we report both. Both interpretations make CHG-505 late (review 08-14) and CHG-510 on-time (review 08-12), but CHG-505 fails anyway on scope.

**RR5 – Identity evidence**
`svc_qms` is never a human. Counts as conforming only with reliable session evidence (individual re-auth, FIDO, etc.) mapping to an authorized human at that time. `u_bk`/`b.koch` aliases must be merged before SoD; failure to do so is a data-quality false negative.

**RR6 – Suspicion versus proof**
Repeated pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, concentrated handovers are **indicators**, not proof of collusion. Require supporting evidence (content quality, timing, benefit, access logs) before adverse inference.

**Principal on leave, delegation validity:**
If Asha personally approves during D-QM-01 window (CHG-504 09:22), that act is QM-authorized if authentic. Its existence is evidence she was *not* effectively absent at that moment, which can undermine the “principal absent” condition for *other* delegated acts at the same time, but does not retroactively void an earlier valid delegated approval when she was absent. Authenticity of the VPN session remains indeterminate without MFA/device proof.

## 6. Control Improvements

**1. Delegation creation & prohibition enforcement**
* Single authoritative delegation registry (HR+QMS) with mandatory scope vocabulary (`production_equipment`, `cooling_system`, `training_document`) and boolean flags: `principal_absence_required`, `subdelegation_prohibited`. System blocks D-IC-04-style subdelegation at entry if parent prohibits it; email-only delegations not executable.

**2. Effective-time recording**
* Legally effective time = digital signature timestamp, not DB insertion time. Freeze delegation PDF hash on signing; QMS migration must preserve `effective_from/to` as immutable metadata. Back-dated DB creation (D-QM-01 08-12) flagged as data-quality incident with audit note.

**3. Principal availability truth source**
* Integrate HR leave, on-call, and VPN/MFA availability feed. Delegation UI requires real-time check: “principal recorded absent” pulls from HR API; if principal authenticates (VPN) during interval, auto-alert to Quality and suspend further delegated approvals pending confirmation. Post-leave debrief mandatory.

**4. Alias master management**
* Canonical HumanId in IAM; alias `b.koch`  `u_bk` retirement plan. All QMS approvals resolve to HumanId before SoD check. Quarterly IAM-HR reconciliation; orphan aliases disabled. PM4Py checks must run on `human_id`, not `org:resource`.

**5. Individual authentication & anti-sharing**
* Disable `svc_qms` as shared writer. Enforce FIDO2/phishing-resistant MFA for Draft/Approve/Verify; requirement for individual re-authentication on shared workstations (WS-DM-04, WS-IV-02) with 2-minute idle lock. Badge tap + FIDO binding for Verify station already proven effective in CHG-509 – roll out site-wide. Log `session_id  human_id`.

**6. Emergency review control**
* Emergency path requires structured form: incident ID (CI-44), safety rationale, approving IC identity (system-validated), and auto-creates retrospective QM Review task due in 2 business days (exclusive count, EOD, with weekend/holiday calendar). Escalation at 75% of due time; overdue blocks Close. QM delegate scope validated against case scope at task creation – prevents production-equipment delegate reviewing cooling-system cases.

**7. Pair rotation & four-eyes hardening**
* Enforce QMS workflow: system rejects `Draft human == Approve human` (RR1) and `Implement human == Verify human` (RR2) at submit, after alias resolution and service-account blocking. For HIGH-risk, rotate approver pool (rule: no single approver >40% of requestor’s cases per quarter) to reduce 18/23 concentration. Keep exception audit trail if rotation not feasible.

**8. Network monitoring as leading indicator, not verdict**
* Monthly pm4py handover/working-together dashboards for QA oversight, with baselines adjusted for cell assignment. Triggers (e.g., sustained Clara-Ben dyad >60% of cell output) open *coaching* and sampling review, not disciplinary action. Document RR6 adherence in SOP.

**9. Evidential retention & forensics**
* Retain VPN device fingerprints, workstation OS session logs, badge tap logs, and FIDO challenge logs for 2 years; CCTV retention 90 days for GMP areas. All HIGH-risk approvals capture approver comment and second-factor evidence. Post-incident forensic playbook for sick-leave/leave-period authentications.

## 7. Executive Conclusion

**Systemic judgment:** The log shows a **mixed failure mode**, not a single root cause.

* **Genuine authority failures  40-50% of HIGH-risk non-conformance:** CHG-507 (no delegation at all), CHG-510 (void subdelegation + self-approval), CHG-509 (D-QM-02 absence condition missed), and CHG-502/508 (self-approvals). These are *control-design/user-understanding* failures: scope vocabulary and subdelegation prohibition were not system-enforced.
* **Identity/data-quality failures  30-35%:** CHG-503 (unattributable `svc_qms` verify  potential self-verify), CHG-506 (sick-leave account use  compromised identity), CHG-504 (leave-period VPN authenticity indeterminate), and CHG-508 (alias duplication). Technical conformance cannot be asserted/denied without forensic evidence; per RR5 these count as non-conforming.
* **Organizational-pattern risk  20-25%:** Clara-Ben repeated dyad (18/23 HIGH) and reciprocal LOW approvals are *explained* by cell co-location and small QM pool, but amplify all other risks because independent challenge is weakened. Networks will highlight this dyad, but that is correlation, not collusion proof.

Uncertainty remains **high** for three cases (CHG-504, CHG-503, CHG-506) where authenticity depends on forensics outside the event log.
