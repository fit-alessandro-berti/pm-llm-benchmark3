## 2. Competing explanations and timeline

**January–March (stable baseline).**  
V1/V2/V3 mix ~45/35/20, breach ~0.18–0.19, mean predicted risk matched, AUROC ~0.81, Brier ~0.13, ECE ~0.02–0.025, unknown activity 0.3%, parts edge ~28–35h. Matured variant breaches stable (V1 ~0.15–0.16, V2 ~0.10–0.11, V3 ~0.30–0.32). No material drift—use as reference operating point.

**April — schema/pipeline incident, not “the model forgot how to predict.”**  
Migration renamed the decision-time activity; prefixes were built at the wrong event or skipped; duplicates and `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` inflated unmapped share to 18%. Metrics (AUROC 0.67, ECE 0.110, mean pred 0.28 vs 0.19) are consistent with garbage prefixes and missing queue features (14%), not with a sudden business concept change: observed breach stayed ~0.19 and matured variant rates only mildly up. **Treat April as data-pipeline failure.** May’s partial recovery after the mapping patch (unknown 2%, AUROC 0.78, ECE 0.050) confirms the mechanism; residual May error is incomplete feature backfill—not a new drift regime.

**May — repair lag.**  
Mostly pipeline residual. Process mix nearly restored. Safe to use for monitoring baselines only after feature recomputation; still unsafe as clean training data until cached diagnosis-relative features are rebuilt.

**June — real process/routing mix change; weak evidence for concept drift.**  
Policy increased V2 to 52%; overall observed breach dropped to 0.15 in line with V2’s low breach. Mean predicted risk 0.20 (mild over-prediction, ECE 0.060) is expected if the model’s prior mix or calibration still reflects higher-V3 history. AUROC 0.79 is near baseline—**discrimination held while calibration lagged mix**. Parts edge only ~42h; V3 matured breach 0.35 (slight up, not the later crisis). **Do not fold June into the same failure as April or August.**

**July — onset of supplier-driven process performance drift on V3.**  
Parts edge 65h; V3 matured breach 0.46; overall breach back to 0.19 (mix of more V2 and worse V3). AUROC 0.76, mild maturity slip (98%). This is the start of **process drift on a critical edge**, beginning to induce **concept/covariate stress** for parts-dependent cases.

**August–September — overlapping process, concept, and (Sep) population shifts; not one knob.**  
- **August:** Parts edge 130h; V3 share 35%; V3 breach 0.64; overall breach 0.28 vs pred 0.19; AUROC 0.68; ECE 0.120. Dominant story: supplier failure lengthening V3 and breaking the learned outcome relationship for parts paths. Maturity 93%—real signal, mild censoring.  
- **September:** Parts edge still severe (148h); V3 42%; Partner Z 24% with more rural/V3 mix and slower label maturity; observed breach 0.31 with only 82% maturity. Population shift **compounds** process/concept problems; new relevant features (travel, device age) absent. Pooled metrics entangle supplier shock + new segment + incomplete labels.

**October — label maturity/censoring dominates the scorecard.**  
Maturity 55%, median delay 52d, backlog hits long paths hardest. Observed breach 0.16 is **not comparable** to mature Aug–Sep rates; it likely understates true breach (V3/Z under-represented among matured labels). AUROC 0.64 / ECE 0.130 / pred 0.23 vs obs 0.16 cannot be read as clean concept drift or as recovery. Process evidence (parts edge 155h, V3 ~41%, Z ~27%) says operational risk remains high.

**Why not one homogeneous failure:** April is mostly broken inputs; June is successful routing mix with calibration lag; Aug–Sep are genuine path-performance and segment shifts; October’s dashboard is censored. Collapsing them invites the wrong fix (e.g., retrain on Oct labels or “recalibrate April”).

## 3. Diagnostic tests

**A. Label-maturity curves and fixed-vintage backtesting**  
- Plot, for each case-start month, observed breach rate and Brier/AUROC vs days-since-cutoff (or % labels arrived).  
- Freeze a vintage (e.g., “labels as of T+60”) and recompute metrics for all months on the same maturity rule.  
*Expected:* Under **label censoring (Oct/Sep)**, early cutoffs show depressed breach and optimistic/unstable calibration that rise toward Aug-like levels as long paths mature. Under **true concept improvement**, maturity curves stay flat at low breach. Under **pipeline failure (Apr)**, even 100%-mature metrics stay poor until features are repaired.

**B. Recomputation after activity-label normalization and duplicate cleaning**  
- Map both diagnosis labels to one canonical name; drop exact duplicate events (same case, activity, timestamp) with a documented policy; recompute diagnosis-relative features (counts before diagnosis, queue at decision time); regenerate prefixes at true “diagnosis completed.”  
*Expected:* Under **pipeline failure**, Apr (and residual May) AUROC/ECE return toward Jan–Mar; mean predicted risk falls toward ~0.19. Under **concept/process drift**, Apr changes little after repair (but Apr business rates already suggest repair will help). Under **label delay**, Oct unchanged by feature repair alone.

**C. Calibration and discrimination stratified by variant, source, and month**  
- AUROC, Brier, ECE, and reliability plots for V1/V2/V3 × {Partner Z, other} × month on maturity-qualified sets only.  
*Expected:* **Process mix (Jun):** pooled calibration off, V2-stratum still well calibrated. **Concept drift on parts:** V3 ECE/Brier collapse Jul–Sep; V2 remains stable (remote edge stable). **Population shift:** Z stratum worse than non-Z at same variant. **Pipeline:** all variants bad in Apr, including V2.

**D. Process variants and DFG / performance-DFG edges**  
- Monthly variant shares; performance-DFG on `Order Part  Part Received`, diagnosis  `Remote Fix`, diagnosis  dispatch, etc.  
*Expected:* **Process drift:** large shift in edge times and/or variant mass (observed). **Covariate drift without process change:** feature shifts with flat DFG (not the Aug story). **Pipeline:** spike in unmapped/other (Apr 18%), not true V3 lengthening.

**E. Conditional outcome given the same path**  
- Match prefixes (e.g., reached `Order Part` within similar pre-diagnosis features); compare P(breach \| path, month) and waiting-time distributions. Optionally residual model: breach ~ wait_time + controls, test month effects.  
*Expected:* **Concept drift:** month effect remains after controlling for wait time. **Pure process/covariate (delay as feature):** wait time absorbs the Jul–Sep V3 breach rise; month coefficient ~0. **Population:** Z effect remains after path and wait controls  missing features / segment shift.

**F. Population reweighting / transport-style checks for Partner Z**  
- Reweight non-Z Sep–Oct to Z’s covariate/variant distribution (or classify propensity of “being Z”); compare model risk and outcomes on common support; evaluate metrics on non-Z only vs pooled.  
*Expected:* **Pure population shift with shared concept:** reweighted non-Z metrics match Z. **New concept or missing features for Z:** gap remains after reweighting. **Label delay:** Z gap shrinks as Z labels mature.

**G. Feature missingness and lineage audits**  
- Rate of missing queue/diagnosis-relative features by source system and week; verify backfill job coverage for Apr–May; check whether prediction time aligns with canonical diagnosis event.  
*Expected:* **Pipeline:** missingness spike Apr, partial May, correlated with error. **True drift months:** missingness near baseline while metrics still bad (Aug).

**H. Discrimination vs calibration decomposition under fixed maturity**  
- After B and A, check whether Aug–Sep harm is mostly calibration (recalibration sufficient) or rank-order failure within V3 (needs new features/retraining).  
*Expected:* If parts wait is omitted, within-V3 AUROC drops (retrain/features). If wait is present but mis-scaled, ECE drops more than AUROC (recalibration/segmented calibration may suffice short-term).

## 4. Recalibration-versus-retraining decision

**Stage 0 — Immediate containment (days)**  
- Stop automated actions that trust raw scores for Apr-built prefixes and for any case with `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` / unmapped diagnosis.  
- Dashboard: split metrics by maturity (95% cohort only), variant, and source; **do not page “model decay” from Oct pooled AUROC alone**.  
- Operational: escalate supplier SLA and V3 capacity (process), independent of ML.

**Stage 1 — Feature/pipeline repair (before any learning)**  
- Canonicalize diagnosis labels; dedupe; recompute all decision-time features for Apr–May (and verify Jun+).  
- Only after repair, decide whether Apr is usable; default: **exclude unrepaired Apr from train/calibrate**.

**Stage 2 — Short-term recalibration (1–2 weeks, maturity-safe data)**  
- Isotonic/Platt **within stratum**: variant (at least V2 vs V3) and, when volume allows, source.  
- Use **fixed-vintage mature labels** from periods where pipeline is clean: prefer **Jan–Mar + post-repair May–Jun** for baseline; add **Jul** only with V3-aware calibration.  
- **Unsafe for calibration/training until repaired/mature:** raw Apr; Oct; Sep until maturity curves plateau; any backlog-censored slice.  
- Recalibration alone is **insufficient** for Aug–Sep V3 if parts delay and Z features are missing—use as containment for V1/V2 and as temporary V3 offset while features ship.

**Stage 3 — Feature enrichment + full retrain**  
- Add: realized/expected parts wait, supplier performance, variant probabilities, travel/rural, device age, source partner.  
- Train on repaired data with **mature outcomes**; **exclude Oct** and immature Sep tails; carefully down-weight or separately validate Apr even after repair.  
- Walk-forward validation by case-start month with maturity gates.  
- **Segment/variant-specific models:** justified for **V3 vs V2** (different mechanisms and the supplier shock is V3-local); a **Partner Z adapter or separate calibration** is justified once mature Z labels exist and common-support checks fail—start with shared model + Z-specific calibration/features before full split (volume and label delay).

**Safe vs unsafe months (training targets)**  

| Month | Training use |
|---|---|
| Jan–Mar | Safe baseline |
| Apr | Unsafe until full recompute; then optional with pipeline QC flag |
| May | Conditional on backfill verification |
| Jun | Safe for mix-shifted regime; good for V2 representation |
| Jul | Usable with V3 upweight; mark supplier-onset |
| Aug | Usable only with mature labels + parts features; strong V3 signal |
| Sep | Wait for maturity; stratify Z; do not pool blindly |
| Oct | **Unsafe** until labels mature; process features OK for unsupervised monitoring |

**Decision rule:** If after repair + maturity filter, within-V3 AUROC remains materially below baseline  **retrain with new features**. If AUROC OK but ECE high  **stratified recalibration**. If only pooled metrics move when variant mix moves  **process monitor + mix-aware calibration**, not full retrain.

## 5. Monitoring architecture

**Layers and alert dependencies**

1. **Data-quality monitors (upstream gate)**  
   Unknown/renamed activity rate; duplicate rate; missing decision-time features; prefix built at canonical diagnosis (yes/no); unmapped variant share.  
   *On fire  suppress model-performance alerts; ticket to pipeline; no retrain.*

2. **Label-maturity monitors**  
   % labels available by case-start week; median label delay; backlog age; maturity by variant/source (V3/Z vs V2).  
   *On fire  holdout “official” AUROC/Brier; no auto-retrain/recalibrate.*

3. **Process-variant monitors**  
   Share V1/V2/V3/other vs baseline band; alert on policy-sized shifts (e.g., V2 +10pp).

4. **Performance-DFG monitors**  
   Median/p90 on `Order Part  Part Received`, diagnosisRemote Fix, diagnosisRepair Completed; compare to control limits.  
   *Parts-edge alarm  expect V3 calibration drift; open ops + targeted model review, not global retrain alone.*

5. **Population monitors**  
   Partner/source mix; rural distance/device age distributions; propensity overlap with training population.

6. **Predictive & calibration monitors (downstream, gated)**  
   AUROC, Brier, ECE, mean predicted risk vs matured breach—**only on maturity-qualified, DQ-passed cohorts**, stratified by variant and source.  
   *Dependency:* run only if DQ OK and maturity  threshold (e.g., 95% or fixed T+45/T+60).

7. **Intervention/deployment monitors**  
   Score distribution shift; override rate; action take-up; post-action outcomes—to detect feedback loops after policy changes (e.g., Jun remote routing).

**Alert logic (sketch):**  
`if dq_alarm: page(data), suppress(perf_alarms)`  
`elif maturity_alarm: page(analytics), suppress(retrain)`  
`elif process_or_population_alarm and perf_alarm: recommend(feature/segment review, stratified recal)`  
`elif perf_alarm only on mature clean data: recommend(calibrate vs retrain per §4)`  

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

DIAG_ALIASES = {
    "Initial Diagnosis Completed": "DIAGNOSIS_COMPLETED",
    "Diagnostic Assessment Completed": "DIAGNOSIS_COMPLETED",
}

def monthly_process_evidence(path="field_service_events.csv"):
    df = pd.read_csv(path)
    df = pm4py.format_dataframe(
        df,
        case_id="case_id",
        activity_key="activity",
        timestamp_key="timestamp",
    )
    # Normalize renamed diagnosis labels before any feature / prefix logic
    df["concept:name"] = df["concept:name"].replace(DIAG_ALIASES)

    # Duplicate-handling: exact same case+activity+timestamp -> keep one
    df = (
        df.sort_values(["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp"])
        .drop_duplicates(
            subset=["case:concept:name", "concept:name", "time:timestamp"],
            keep="first",
        )
    )
    df["month"] = df["time:timestamp"].dt.to_period("M").astype(str)
    case_starts = (
        df.groupby("case:concept:name")["time:timestamp"].min().rename("case_start")
    )
    df = df.join(case_starts, on="case:concept:name")
    df["start_month"] = df["case_start"].dt.to_period("M").astype(str)

    summaries = {}
    # Event-calendar cohorts: activity volume / DFG in calendar month
    # Case-start cohorts: process of cases that began that month (lead-time honest)
    for cohort_name, month_col in [
        ("event_calendar", "month"),
        ("case_start", "start_month"),
    ]:
        rows = []
        for m, sub in df.groupby(month_col):
            # Bound events to a window for calendar view; case_start uses full case trace
            if cohort_name == "event_calendar":
                start, end = pd.Timestamp(m + "-01"), (
                    pd.Timestamp(m + "-01") + pd.offsets.MonthEnd(1)
                )
                sub_ev = pm4py.filter_time_range(
                    sub, start.to_pydatetime(), end.to_pydatetime(),
                    mode="events"
                )
            else:
                # all events of cases started in m (may extend past month)
                sub_ev = sub

            dfg, sa, ea = pm4py.discover_dfg(sub_ev)
            pdfg, psa, pea = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(sub_ev)
            variants = pm4py.get_variants(sub_ev)
            # temporal features optional audit extract (not a model fit)
            try:
                tfeat = pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(sub_ev)
                n_tfeat_cases = tfeat.shape[0]
            except Exception:
                n_tfeat_cases = None

            parts_edge = ("Order Part", "Part Received")
            remote_edge = ("DIAGNOSIS_COMPLETED", "Remote Fix")
            rows.append({
                "cohort": cohort_name,
                "month": m,
                "n_events": len(sub_ev),
                "n_cases": sub_ev["case:concept:name"].nunique(),
                "n_variants": len(variants),
                "top_variants": tuple(list(variants.items())[:5]),
                "unknown_or_unmapped_share": float(
                    sub_ev["concept:name"].isin(["UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY"]).mean()
                ),
                "parts_wait_sec": pdfg.get(parts_edge),
                "remote_fix_sec": pdfg.get(remote_edge),
                "dfg_parts_count": dfg.get(parts_edge),
                "temporal_feature_rows": n_tfeat_cases,
            })
        summaries[cohort_name] = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    return summaries
```

*Interpretation note:* event-calendar DFGs describe work-in-period congestion; case-start cohorts describe the journey of a monthly intake class—use the latter for breach-rate alignment, the former for supplier shock timing.

## 6. Risks of acting on the wrong diagnosis

**1. Retraining on immature October (and partial September) labels**  
*Failure:* Model learns that “recent cases rarely breach” because long V3/Z paths are still open  systematic under-prediction just as parts delays peak.  
*Mitigation:* Maturity gates and fixed-vintage labels; ban Oct targets until curves stabilize; train on matured Aug only with explicit delay features.

**2. Calibrating around the April pipeline bug**  
*Failure:* Global recalibration maps healthy score ranges to Apr’s broken prefix distribution; after repair, calibration is worse. Masks the need for feature recompute.  
*Mitigation:* DQ gate before any calibrator update; drop/repair Apr; never fit one calibrator across Apr and Jan–Mar without stratum for “pipeline_ok.”

**3. Ignoring genuine supplier-driven concept/process drift (Jul–Sep V3)**  
*Failure:* Treat Aug metrics as “another April” or as pure label noise  only restart pipelines while V3 breach stays ~0.6; field misses SLA.  
*Mitigation:* Performance-DFG and matured V3 breach as independent ops KPIs; path-conditioned tests; retrain/add parts-supplier features even when DQ is green.

**4. Attributing Partner Z’s metrics to unfair/biased model behavior without stratification**  
*Failure:* Pooled Sep deterioration blamed on “bias against rural customers,” triggering inappropriate fairness patches or disabling the model, while main drivers are V3 mix, parts delay, missing travel/device features, and slower label maturity.  
*Mitigation:* Metrics by source × variant; transport/reweight checks; add legitimate operational features; compare Z vs non-Z on matched paths before governance actions.

**5. Global retrain in response to June’s V2 mix shift**  
*Failure:* Overfit to a temporary remote-heavy mix; damage V3 ranking right before supplier crisis; unnecessary churn.  
*Mitigation:* Variant-stratified monitoring; mix-aware calibration first; retrain only if within-variant discrimination fails.

**6. Suppressing all model work because October looks “fine” on observed breach (0.16)**  
*Failure:* False comfort from censoring; under-investment in V3/Z features while pred risk 0.23 and parts edge 155h say risk remains.  
*Mitigation:* Never compare raw Oct observed breach to mature months; pair scorecard with process and population monitors.

---

**Summary stance:** April  pipeline/schema failure; June  process mix (remote policy) with calibration lag; July–September  parts **process** drift inducing **concept** stress on V3, plus September **population** shift (Partner Z); October  **label delay/censoring** confounding the metrics. Remediation is gated: repair features  maturity-aware stratified calibration  feature-rich retrain, with process and population monitors empowered to block naive retraining.
