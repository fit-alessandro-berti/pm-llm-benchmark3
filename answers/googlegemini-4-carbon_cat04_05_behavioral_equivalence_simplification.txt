# 1. Equivalence Notions

Let $\Sigma = \{\text{Open Case}, \text{Analyze Revision}, \text{Check Revision}, \text{Approve Revision}, \text{Rework Case}, \text{Close Case}\}$ be the alphabet of visible activity labels, and let $\tau \notin \Sigma$ denote an unobservable (silent) step. For an execution sequence $\rho \in (\Sigma \cup \{\tau\})^*$ reaching the final state/marking of model $M$, let $\pi_\Sigma(\rho) \in \Sigma^*$ be the visible projection obtained by erasing all $\tau$ occurrences. The complete visible trace language of $M$ is $L_{\text{vis}}(M) = \{\pi_\Sigma(\rho) \mid \rho \text{ is a complete execution of } M\}$.

**Label, position, and identity convention:** Neither Model A nor Model B has duplicate visible leaves in its static syntax tree, though loop iteration dynamically generates multiple runtime occurrences of the same label. Across all four equivalence notions below, events carry **no hidden object or transition identity**: two occurrences of `'Check Revision'` are distinguished solely by their **label** and—prior to P2 aggregation—their **ordinal position** $i \in \{1, \dots, |\sigma|\}$ in the visible sequence $\sigma = \langle e_1, \dots, e_n \rangle$.

1. **Visible trace equivalence after hiding `tau` ($\equiv_{\text{vis}}$):**
   $$M_A \equiv_{\text{vis}} M_B \iff L_{\text{vis}}(M_A) = L_{\text{vis}}(M_B).$$
   Silent steps $\tau$ are unobservable, except indirectly when firing $\tau$ enables or disables subsequent visible ordering choices (divergence/branching structure is collapsed to set equality over $\Sigma^*$).

2. **Bounded visible-language equivalence under P3 ($\equiv_{P3}$):**
   Define the P3 filter predicate $B_{P3}(\sigma) \iff |\sigma| \le 12 \land \#(\text{Analyze Revision}, \sigma) \le 3$, where $\#(a, \sigma)$ counts occurrences of label $a$ in $\sigma$. Let $L_{P3}(M) = \{\sigma \in L_{\text{vis}}(M) \mid B_{P3}(\sigma)\}$. Then:
   $$M_A \equiv_{P3} M_B \iff L_{P3}(M_A) = L_{P3}(M_B).$$
   Notice that $M_A \not\equiv_{P3} M_B \implies M_A \not\equiv_{\text{vis}} M_B$ (sound refutation), whereas $M_A \equiv_{P3} M_B \not\implies M_A \equiv_{\text{vis}} M_B$ (bounded evidence is not an unbounded proof).

3. **Business-relevant equivalence under P1 ($\equiv_{P1}$):**
   P1 treats `'Open Case'`, `'Close Case'`, `'Analyze Revision'`, `'Check Revision'`, `'Rework Case'`, and `'Approve Revision'` (both count and placement) as operationally observable, while hiding $\tau$. Furthermore, P1 imposes an epoch-level compliance invariant: every valid trace must decompose around `'Rework Case'` boundaries as
   $$\sigma = \langle \text{Open Case} \rangle \cdot \eta_1 \cdot \langle \text{Rework Case} \rangle \cdot \eta_2 \cdots \langle \text{Rework Case} \rangle \cdot \eta_k \cdot \langle \text{Close Case} \rangle \quad (k \ge 1),$$
   where each revision epoch $\eta_j \in \{\text{Analyze Revision}, \text{Check Revision}, \text{Approve Revision}\}^*$ contains exactly one `'Analyze Revision'` and its dedicated `'Check Revision'` before the next `'Rework Case'` or `'Close Case'`. Because P1 retains all labels in $\Sigma$ and treats approval placement and per-epoch check placement as compliance-relevant, two models are P1-equivalent iff they satisfy the same epoch compliance predicates and admit the same visible trace set: $M_A \equiv_{P1} M_B \iff L_{\text{vis}}(M_A) = L_{\text{vis}}(M_B)$.

4. **Abstracted dashboard equivalence under P2 ($\equiv_{P2}$):**
   Define the observation abstraction $\alpha_{P2} : \Sigma^+ \to \mathbb{B}^4 \times \mathbb{N} \times \mathbb{B}$ mapping each complete visible trace $\sigma = \langle e_1, \dots, e_n \rangle$ to the dashboard record:
   $$\alpha_{P2}(\sigma) = \Big(\underbrace{e_1 = \text{Open Case} \land \text{Open Case} \notin \sigma_{2..n}}_{\text{proper open}},\; \underbrace{e_n = \text{Close Case} \land \text{Close Case} \notin \sigma_{1..n-1}}_{\text{proper close}},\; \underbrace{\#(\text{Analyze Revision}, \sigma) \ge 1}_{\text{has analysis}},\; \underbrace{\#(\text{Check Revision}, \sigma) \ge 1}_{\text{check occurred}},\; \underbrace{\#(\text{Analyze Revision}, \sigma)}_{\text{analysis count}},\; \underbrace{\#(\text{Rework Case}, \sigma) \ge 1}_{\text{rework occurred}}\Big).$$
   Notice that $\alpha_{P2}$ erases $\tau$ and `'Approve Revision'`, collapses positive `'Check Revision'` multiplicity ($1, 2, 3, \dots \mapsto \text{True}$), collapses positive `'Rework Case'` multiplicity to a Boolean, and discards the relative interleaving of `'Check Revision'` with `'Analyze Revision'` and `'Rework Case'`. Thus:
   $$M_A \equiv_{P2} M_B \iff \{\alpha_{P2}(\sigma) \mid \sigma \in L_{\text{vis}}(M_A)\} = \{\alpha_{P2}(\sigma) \mid \sigma \in L_{\text{vis}}(M_B)\}.$$
   *(Even under a stricter P2 reading that also retains the projected revision-backbone sequence $\pi_{\{\text{Open Case}, \text{Analyze Revision}, \text{Rework Case}, \text{Close Case}\}}(\sigma)$, both models yield the exact same regular set $\{\langle \text{Open Case}, \text{Analyze Revision} \rangle \cdot \langle \text{Rework Case}, \text{Analyze Revision} \rangle^{k-1} \cdot \langle \text{Close Case} \rangle \mid k \ge 1\}$ paired with $\text{check\_occurred} = \text{True}$.)*

# 2. Structural Comparison

Let $k \ge 1$ denote the number of executions of the first child ("do" body) of the `*` loop in a complete run (so `'Rework Case'` executes $k - 1$ times).

| Feature | Model A | Model B | Semantic consequence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Loop scope** | `*` encloses `'Analyze Revision'`, `'Check Revision'`, and `X('Approve Revision', tau)` in its redoable body, with `'Rework Case'` as redo child. | `*` encloses only `'Analyze Revision'` and `X('Approve Revision', tau)` with `'Rework Case'` as redo child; `'Check Revision'` sits outside `*`. | Model A repeats `'Check Revision'` on every rework cycle; Model B executes the entire revision/rework loop independently of `'Check Revision'`. |
| **Min / max `'Check Revision'` occurrences** | Minimum $1$, maximum $\infty$ (unbounded; strictly $\#(\text{Check Revision}) = k = \#(\text{Analyze Revision})$). | Minimum $1$, maximum $1$ (constant $\#(\text{Check Revision}) = 1$ regardless of $k$). | For every multi-attempt run ($k \ge 2$), Model A and Model B have disjoint `'Check Revision'` counts ($k$ vs. $1$). |
| **Possible placement of `'Check Revision'`** | Within each epoch $j \in \{1, \dots, k\}$, after `'Open Case'` (or $(j-1)$-th `'Rework Case'`) and strictly **before** epoch $j$'s optional `'Approve Revision'`, next `'Rework Case'`, and `'Close Case'`. | Anywhere after `'Open Case'` and before `'Close Case'`—including **after** `'Approve Revision'` or **after** one or more `'Rework Case'` events. | Even on single-attempt runs ($k = 1$), Model B permits `'Approve Revision'` before `'Check Revision'`, which Model A forbids. |
| **Optional approval occurrences** | $0 \le \#(\text{Approve Revision}) \le k$; at most one per iteration, enabled only after **both** `'Analyze Revision'` and `'Check Revision'` complete in that iteration. | $0 \le \#(\text{Approve Revision}) \le k$; at most one per iteration, enabled as soon as `'Analyze Revision'` completes in that iteration (without waiting for `'Check Revision'`). | Model B allows an un-checked revision to be approved (`'Analyze Revision'` $\to$ `'Approve Revision'` $\to$ `'Check Revision'`); Model A enforces check-before-approval. |
| **Concurrency (`+`)** | Scoped inside the loop body: `+('Analyze Revision', 'Check Revision')` synchronizes before `X('Approve Revision', tau)`. | Scoped outside the loop: `'Check Revision'` runs concurrently with the entire `*(...)` sub-process. | Model A has $k$ tight barrier synchronizations (one per iteration); Model B has one wide concurrent region spanning all $k$ iterations. |
| **Join before `'Close Case'`** | Sequential exit from `*`: `'Close Case'` awaits completion of the $k$-th iteration's `X('Approve Revision', tau)` (which already joined `'Analyze Revision'` and `'Check Revision'`). | Parallel AND-join (`+`) before `'Close Case'`: awaits both the single `'Check Revision'` branch and the exit of the `*(...)` branch. | Both guarantee `'Close Case'` is last and preceded by $\ge 1$ check and $\ge 1$ analysis, but Model B defers check synchronization all the way to case closure. |

### Role of Optional `'Approve Revision'` and Hidden `tau`

1. **In $k = 1$ runs (CE-3), visible `'Approve Revision'` exposes concurrency scope:** If `tau` is selected instead of `'Approve Revision'` on a single-iteration run ($k = 1$), both models yield the exact same two visible traces: `Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Close Case` and `Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Close Case`. Because $\tau$ leaves no footprint in the visible trace, an observer cannot see whether the model exited `X('Approve Revision', tau)` before or after `'Check Revision'`. Selecting the visible `'Approve Revision'` branch acts as an observable causal marker that separates Model B (`Approve Revision` before `Check Revision` in **CE-3**) from Model A.
2. **In $k \ge 2$ runs (CE-4), hidden `tau` enables premature rework in Model B:** In **CE-4**, the $\tau$ branch inside `X('Approve Revision', tau)` allows Model B to silently complete its first loop body immediately after `'Analyze Revision'` and fire `'Rework Case'` at Event 3 with zero approvals and zero checks so far—violating P1's compliance rule even when no `'Approve Revision'` appears anywhere in the trace.

# 5. Bounded Comparison Code

The script below uses **exhaustive breadth-first state-space exploration** over `(marking, visible_prefix)` pairs rather than random playout. Key subtleties handled explicitly:
- **Silent transitions (`t.label is None`):** Allowed to fire even when `len(vis_trace) == 12` or `an_count == 3`, because WF-net conversions of `+` and `*` require silent join/exit transitions *after* visible events to reach the final marking `fm`.
- **Repeated markings & $\tau$-cycles:** Pruning on `marking` alone would erroneously discard distinct visible prefixes reaching the same loop marking. Pruning on `(freeze_marking(marking), vis_trace)` eliminates redundant silent paths and $\tau$-cycles while preserving every distinct visible trace within the P3 bound.
- **Playout contrast:** `pm4py.play_out(...)` draws finite random or prefix-bounded samples and can miss valid interleavings (false equivalence) when concurrency or loop branching grows; our BFS explores 100% of P3-bounded executions.

```python
from collections import deque
import pm4py

TREE_A_STR = """->(
  'Open Case',
  *(
    ->(
      +('Analyze Revision', 'Check Revision'),
      X('Approve Revision', tau)
    ),
    'Rework Case'
  ),
  'Close Case'
)"""

TREE_B_STR = """->(
  'Open Case',
  +(
    'Check Revision',
    *(
      ->(
        'Analyze Revision',
        X('Approve Revision', tau)
      ),
      'Rework Case'
    )
  ),
  'Close Case'
)"""


def normalize_tree_str(s: str) -> str:
    return " ".join(line.strip() for line in s.splitlines())


def freeze_marking(marking) -> frozenset:
    """Canonical hashable representation ignoring zero-token entries."""
    return frozenset((place, tokens) for place, tokens in marking.items() if tokens > 0)


def fire_transition(marking, transition):
    """Pure marking update without mutating existing Marking objects."""
    next_m = marking.__class__(marking)
    for arc in transition.in_arcs:
        next_m[arc.source] -= arc.weight
        if next_m[arc.source] == 0:
            del next_m[arc.source]
    for arc in transition.out_arcs:
        next_m[arc.target] = next_m.get(arc.target, 0) + arc.weight
    return next_m


def exhaustive_p3_visible_traces(
    net, im, fm, max_vis_len: int = 12, max_analyze: int = 3
) -> set[tuple[str, ...]]:
    """
    Exhaustively enumerates all complete visible traces satisfying P3:
      - len(visible_trace) <= max_vis_len
      - visible_trace.count('Analyze Revision') <= max_analyze
    """
    fm_key = freeze_marking(fm)
    initial_state = (freeze_marking(im), ())
    visited = {initial_state}
    queue = deque([(im, ())])
    accepted_traces: set[tuple[str, ...]] = set()

    while queue:
        current_m, vis_trace = queue.popleft()

        # Check exact final-marking equality
        if freeze_marking(current_m) == fm_key and current_m == fm:
            accepted_traces.add(vis_trace)

        for trans in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, current_m):
            if trans.label is None:
                # Silent tau step: visible length and Analyze count do not increase
                next_trace = vis_trace
            else:
                next_trace = vis_trace + (trans.label,)
                if len(next_trace) > max_vis_len:
                    continue
                if next_trace.count("Analyze Revision") > max_analyze:
                    continue

            next_m = fire_transition(current_m, trans)
            state_key = (freeze_marking(next_m), next_trace)
            if state_key not in visited:
                visited.add(state_key)
                queue.append((next_m, next_trace))

    return accepted_traces


def main():
    # 1. Parse process trees
    tree_a = pm4py.parse_process_tree(normalize_tree_str(TREE_A_STR))
    tree_b = pm4py.parse_process_tree(normalize_tree_str(TREE_B_STR))

    # 2. Convert to accepting Petri nets
    net_a, im_a, fm_a = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a)
    net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_b)

    # 3. Check workflow-net soundness
    sound_a = pm4py.check_soundness(net_a, im_a, fm_a, print_diagnostics=True)
    sound_b = pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b, print_diagnostics=True)
    print(f"Soundness -> Model A: {sound_a}, Model B: {sound_b}\n")

    # 4 & 5. Exhaustive bounded exploration under P3 (not random playout)
    traces_a = exhaustive_p3_visible_traces(net_a, im_a, fm_a, max_vis_len=12, max_analyze=3)
    traces_b = exhaustive_p3_visible_traces(net_b, im_b, fm_b, max_vis_len=12, max_analyze=3)
```

# 6. Stochastic and Footprint Comparison

### Behavioral Evidence Added—and Fundamental Limits

1. **`pm4py.discover_footprints(...)`:**
   - **How it adds evidence:** Footprints summarize pairwise direct-succession (`sequence`), concurrency (`parallel`), and mutual exclusion (`Choice`) relations. Here, footprints *already* catch an asymmetry: in Model B, `'Check Revision'` is parallel (`||`) to `'Approve Revision'`, so `('Approve Revision', 'Check Revision')` appears in direct succession; in Model A, `'Approve Revision'` can only follow `'Check Revision'` within an iteration and is separated from the next iteration's `'Check Revision'` by `'Rework Case'`, so `('Approve Revision', 'Check Revision')` never occurs!
   - **Why equal footprints do not establish trace equivalence:** Footprints are a first-order Markovian (pairwise) abstraction. They discard **cardinality** (1 check vs. $k$ checks) and **long-range history**. Notice also how hidden $\tau$ creates deceptive pairwise relations: in Model A, skipping `'Approve Revision'` via $\tau$ allows `'Check Revision'` $\to$ `'Rework Case'`, while entering Iteration 2 allows `'Rework Case'` $\to$ `'Check Revision'`, causing `('Check Revision', 'Rework Case')` to appear bidirectional (`||`) in a trace-derived footprint even though they never execute concurrently in Model A.

2. **`pm4py.get_stochastic_language(...)` and `pm4py.compute_emd(...)`:**
   - **Declared stochastic assumption:** Neither process tree defines transition probabilities. To compare stochastic languages without inventing hidden business rates, one must explicitly declare a **uniform weight policy** ($w(t) = 1.0$ for all Petri-net transitions) and a **finite unfolding/stopping horizon** (required because unbounded loops have infinite support).
   - **How they add evidence:** Under identical declared weights, a strictly positive Earth Mover's Distance ($\text{EMD} > 0$) proves that the truncated stochastic languages differ and quantifies how much probability mass must be transported across normalized string-edit (Levenshtein) distances.
   - **Why low EMD does not establish trace equivalence:**
     1. *Edit-distance smoothing:* Traces `Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Approve Revision, Close Case` (valid in A) and `Open Case, Analyze Revision, Approve Revision, Check Revision, Close Case` (invalid in A, valid in B, **CE-3**) differ by a single adjacent transposition of length-5 strings, yielding small normalized edit cost despite a hard P1 compliance violation.
     2. *Probability weighting and truncation:* If loop redo (`'Rework Case'`) or explicit approval (`'Approve Revision'`) has low firing probability, counterexample mass approaches $0$, driving $\text{EMD} \to 0$ even though qualitative counterexamples remain reachable. Even $\text{EMD} = 0$ on a horizon-truncated stochastic language only certifies bounded agreement up to the stopping threshold.

```python
import pm4py

# Assumes net_a, im_a, fm_a and net_b, im_b, fm_b from Section 5
fp_a = pm4py.discover_footprints(tree_a)
fp_b = pm4py.discover_footprints(tree_b)
print("Footprint parallel pairs equal?", fp_a.get("parallel") == fp_b.get("parallel"))
print("In B parallel but not A:", fp_b.get("parallel", set()) - fp_a.get("parallel", set()))

# Explicitly declare uniform transition weights = 1.0 (not business data!)
uniform_weights_a = {t: 1.0 for t in net_a.transitions}
uniform_weights_b = {t: 1.0 for t in net_b.transitions}

# Finite stochastic approximation (stopping probability / max trace length truncation)
slang_a = pm4py.get_stochastic_language(
    net_a, im_a, fm_a, weight_map=uniform_weights_a, stopping_probability=0.15
)
slang_b = pm4py.get_stochastic_language(
    net_b, im_b, fm_b, weight_map=uniform_weights_b, stopping_probability=0.15
)

emd_val = pm4py.compute_emd(slang_a, slang_b)
print(f"Truncated uniform-weight EMD(A, B) = {emd_val:.6f}")
```

# 7. Complexity Comparison

### Derived Exact Tree Metrics

Define **edge-depth** of a tree node as the number of parent-to-child edges from the root (root at depth $0$), and **duplicate-label count** as $|\text{visible\_leaves}| - |\text{set}(\text{visible\_leaves})|$.

- **Model A syntax tree:**
  - Depth $0$: `->`
  - Depth $1$: `'Open Case'`, `*`, `'Close Case'`
  - Depth $2$: `->`, `'Rework Case'`
  - Depth $3$: `+`, `X`
  - Depth $4$: `'Analyze Revision'`, `'Check Revision'`, `'Approve Revision'`, `tau`
  - Counts: **5** operator nodes + **7** leaf nodes (**6** visible, **1** silent) = **12** total nodes; max edge-depth = **4**; duplicate visible labels = $6 - 6 = \mathbf{0}$.
- **Model B syntax tree:**
  - Depth $0$: `->`
  - Depth $1$: `'Open Case'`, `+`, `'Close Case'`
  - Depth $2$: `'Check Revision'`, `*`
  - Depth $3$: `->`, `'Rework Case'`
  - Depth $4$: `'Analyze Revision'`, `X`
  - Depth $5$: `'Approve Revision'`, `tau`
  - Counts: **5** operator nodes + **7** leaf nodes (**6** visible, **1** silent) = **12** total nodes; max edge-depth = **5**; duplicate visible labels = $6 - 6 = \mathbf{0}$.

| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Process-tree node count** | **12** | **12** | Identical total size (5 operators + 7 leaves). |
| **Tree depth (edges from root)** | **4** | **5** | Model B nests `*` inside `+`, adding one level of depth. |

### Structural Complexity Computation Code

```python
import pm4py


def tree_metrics(tree) -> dict:
    def walk(node, depth=0):
        if not node.children:
            is_vis = node.label is not None
            return {
                "nodes": 1,
                "max_depth": depth,
                "vis_labels": [node.label] if is_vis else [],
                "tau_leaves": 0 if is_vis else 1,
            }
        sub = [walk(ch, depth + 1) for ch in node.children]
        vis = [lbl for s in sub for lbl in s["vis_labels"]]
        return {
            "nodes": 1 + sum(s["nodes"] for s in sub),
            "max_depth": max(s["max_depth"] for s in sub),
            "vis_labels": vis,
            "tau_leaves": sum(s["tau_leaves"] for s in sub),
        }

    res = walk(tree, 0)
    vis = res["vis_labels"]
    net, im, fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)
    return {
        "node_count": res["nodes"],
        "edge_depth": res["max_depth"],
        "visible_leaves": len(vis),
        "silent_leaves": res["tau_leaves"],
        "duplicate_visible_labels": len(vis) - len(set(vis)),
        "petri_places": len(net.places),
        "petri_transitions": len(net.transitions),
        "petri_simplicity": pm4py.simplicity_petri_net(net, im, fm),
    }


for name, t_str in [("Model A", TREE_A_STR), ("Model B", TREE_B_STR)]:
    t = pm4py.parse_process_tree(" ".join(l.strip() for l in t_str.splitlines()))
    print(name, "->", tree_metrics(t))
```

# 8. Simplified Model

### Target View Selection: P2 (Executive Dashboard)

A single simplified model **cannot simultaneously preserve P1, P2, and P3**: Models A and B already disagree under P1 and P3, and preserving P1 (for Model A) requires keeping all 6 visible activities, the explicit `tau` skip for optional approval, concurrency between analysis and check, and per-epoch synchronization—meaning none of Model A's 12 tree nodes are redundant under P1.

By contrast, **P2** is the natural target for semantics-preserving simplification because both original models are already equivalent under $\alpha_{P2}$, while both carry structural machinery (`+` concurrency and `X('Approve Revision', tau)` choice) that P2 completely projects away.

### 1. Compact Process-Tree Expression

```text
->(
  'Open Case',
  'Check Revision',
  *(
    'Analyze Revision',
    'Rework Case'
  ),
  'Close Case'
)
```

**Structural comparison against Originals A and B:**
- **Process-tree node count:** **7** nodes (2 operators `->`, `*` + 5 visible leaves), down from **12** in both Model A and Model B (**41.7% reduction**).
- **Edge-depth:** **2**, down from **4** in Model A and **5** in Model B.
- **Leaves:** **5** visible leaves and **0** silent (`tau`) leaves (vs. 6 visible and 1 `tau` in A and B), with **0** duplicate labels.
- **Converted Petri-net structure:** Eliminates the parallel AND-split/join (`+`) and the optional approval XOR-split/join (`X`), reducing higher-degree split/join places/transitions and strictly improving or matching arc-degree simplicity (`pm4py.simplicity_petri_net`).

### 2. Self-Contained PM4Py Code (`simplified_model`)

```python
import pm4py

SIMPLIFIED_TREE_STR = (
    "->('Open Case', 'Check Revision', *('Analyze Revision', 'Rework Case'), 'Close Case')"
)

simplified_model = pm4py.parse_process_tree(SIMPLIFIED_TREE_STR)
s_net, s_im, s_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(simplified_model)

is_sound = pm4py.check_soundness(s_net, s_im, s_fm, print_diagnostics=True)
simplicity = pm4py.simplicity_petri_net(s_net, s_im, s_fm)
print(f"Simplified model sound: {is_sound}, Petri-net simplicity: {simplicity:.4f}")
```

### 3. Semantic Preservation Table

| Behavioral / Structural Property | Status | Explanation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`Open Case` strictly first, `Close Case` strictly last** | **Exact** | Enforced by root sequence `->('Open Case', ..., 'Close Case')`. |
| **Existence of $\ge 1$ `Analyze Revision` and $\ge 1$ `Check Revision`** | **Exact** | `'Check Revision'` executes once unconditionally; `*` executes `'Analyze Revision'` $k \ge 1$ times. |
| **P2 image $\{\alpha_{P2}(\sigma)\}$ (`#Analyze = k`, Boolean check, Boolean rework)** | **Exact** | Generates $\{(\text{True}, \text{True}, \text{True}, \text{True}, k, \mathbb{I}[k \ge 2]) \mid k \ge 1\}$, identical to both Model A and Model B. |
| **Alternating revision/rework backbone (`Analyze` / `Rework`)** | **Exact** | Every trace projects onto `Open Case, Analyze Revision, (Rework Case, Analyze Revision)^(k-1), Close Case`. |
| **`Check Revision` multiplicity** | **Abstracted** | Collapsed to a single occurrence ($\#(\text{Check Revision}) = 1$), matching P2's Boolean check flag and Model B's count, while dropping Model A's per-epoch repetition. |
| **Interleaving of `Check Revision` and `Analyze Revision`** | **Weakened** | Sequentializes `'Check Revision'` immediately after `'Open Case'` (a strict subset of both Model A's $k=1$ interleavings and Model B's concurrent interleavings). |
| **Optional `Approve Revision` count and placement (P1 / P3)** | **Lost** | `'Approve Revision'` and its `tau` bypass are removed from the alphabet, matching P2's hiding rule. |
| **Per-revision check-after-rework compliance (P1 on Model A)** | **Lost** | Reworked iterations ($j \ge 2$) do not execute additional `'Check Revision'` events. |

### 4. Regression and Negative Traces for `simplified_model`

**Regression traces (must be admitted by `simplified_model`):**
1. **REG-1 ($k = 1$, no rework):** `Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Close Case` — verifies minimum run ($\#(\text{Analyze Revision}) = 1$, $\text{rework} = \text{False}$, $\text{check} = \text{True}$).
2. **REG-2 ($k = 2$, single rework):** `Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Close Case` — verifies loop redo and Boolean rework transition ($\#(\text{Analyze Revision}) = 2$, $\text{rework} = \text{True}$).
3. **REG-3 ($k = 3$, repeated rework):** `Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Close Case` — verifies exact preservation of `#(Analyze Revision)` for $k > 2$ while collapsing rework and check flags to `True`.

**Negative traces (must be rejected by `simplified_model`):**
1. **NEG-1 (missing analysis / premature rework):** `Open Case, Check Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Close Case` — rejected because `*('Analyze Revision', 'Rework Case')` requires executing the first loop child before `'Rework Case'`.
2. **NEG-2 (unremoved approval / repeated check):** `Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Approve Revision, Close Case` (and `Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Close Case`) — rejected because `'Approve Revision'` is excluded from the P2 alphabet and `'Check Revision'` cannot fire twice.

# 9. Final Recommendation

- **For P1 (Operational compliance):** Choose **Model A** exclusively. Model A is the only model that guarantees every revision epoch receives its own `'Check Revision'` before `'Approve Revision'`, `'Rework Case'`, or `'Close Case'`. **Model B must be rejected for P1**, as it allows revisions to be approved or sent to rework without being checked and performs only one check across arbitrarily many rework cycles.
- **For P2 (Executive dashboard):** Both **Model A** and **Model B** are suitable and behaviorally indistinguishable under the P2 abstraction $\alpha_{P2}$. If the model is used *solely* to specify, simulate, or validate P2 dashboard KPIs, **`simplified_model` can safely replace either original**, cutting syntax-tree size from 12 to 7 nodes, depth from 4–5 to 2, and eliminating unnecessary concurrency and silent-choice branching. However, **`simplified_model` must not replace Model A as the operational workflow engine or P1/P3 compliance oracle**, because it intentionally discards `'Approve Revision'` and per-iteration `'Check Revision'` enforcement.
- **For P3 (Bounded testing):** Neither original model can substitute for the other, nor can `simplified_model` substitute for either, because P3 retains all visible labels and interleavings where A, B, and `simplified_model` are pairwise non-equivalent.

> **Warning on the Limits of Bounded Comparison:** In this problem, exhaustive P3 exploration ($|\sigma| \le 12$, $\#(\text{Analyze Revision}) \le 3$) succeeded as a **refutation tool** because counterexamples occurred as early as length 5 ($k = 1$) and length 6 ($k = 2$). In general, however, **passing** a bounded visible-trace check—or observing identical footprints or $\text{EMD} = 0$ on truncated playouts—is **never a proof of unbounded equivalence**. For example, if a flawed model enforced per-epoch checks for the first three iterations and silently dropped checks starting on the fourth revision ($k = 4$), it would be 100% P3-equivalent to Model A while still violating P1 in unbounded operation. Unbounded equivalence requires formal bisimulation or finite-automata language-equivalence proofs over the reachability graphs.
