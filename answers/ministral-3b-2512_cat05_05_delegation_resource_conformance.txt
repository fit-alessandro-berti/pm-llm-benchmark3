### **2. Rule Interpretation**
**RR1 (Drafter/approver separation):**
- **Violation:** Same human cannot draft and approve a case.
- **Application:** Check if `org:resource` for `Draft_Change_Request` and `Approve_Change` differ at event time, or if aliases normalize to the same identity.
- **Edge case:** If a principal performs an action while on leave (e.g., Asha’s VPN session), the delegation must explicitly cover the event.

**RR2 (Implementer/verifier separation):**
- **Violation:** Same human cannot implement and verify.
- **Application:** `Implement_Change` and `Verify_Implementation` must be performed by different identities (normalized).

**RR3 (High-risk approval authority):**
- **Valid delegation:** QM or QM delegate must approve HIGH-risk cases.
- **Conditions:**
  1. Delegation interval covers the event.
  2. Scope matches the change (e.g., D-QM-01 restricts production-equipment changes).
  3. Principal is absent or formally unavailable (e.g., Asha’s leave).
  4. No prohibited subdelegation (e.g., D-IC-04 violates D-IC-03).
- **Business days:** Count calendar days (e.g., 2026-08-15–16 are weekend; no holiday).

**RR4 (Emergency substitution):**
- **Valid emergency:** Approval by IC or IC delegate + QM review within 2 business days.
- **Evidence required:** Emergency flag + safety rationale (e.g., Cooling Incident CI-44).
- **Business days:** Start counting from the day after the emergency action (e.g., CHG-505’s QM review by 2026-08-16).

**RR5 (Identity evidence):**
- **Service accounts:** Require session evidence (e.g., WS-DM-04 linked to Diego). Aliases must be normalized (e.g., `u_bk`/`b.koch`  Ben Koch).

**RR6 (Suspicion vs. proof):**
- **Indicators:** Repeated pairs, shared workstations, concentrated handoffs.
- **Not proof:** Without evidence (e.g., credential sharing, lack of re-authentication).
