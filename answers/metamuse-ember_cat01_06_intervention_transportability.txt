Shared mechanisms are work-conserving overlap and queue shortening: when identity and document work are independent, starting both removes serial service and the handoff wait that precedes Assessment, so waiting_before_assessment and then cycle_time should fall, provided downstream capacity exists. These mechanisms are plausible at both sites because Assessment gates on both checks in each process and median service times are of similar magnitude.

Site-specific mechanisms dominate the remainder. At A, registry API latency, two added document reviewers, daily queue huddles, cross-trained reviewers, timestamp improvements, and a simpler digital mix can each shorten waiting or recorded cycle time without any true_overlap. At B, statutory originals, legal review, supplement loops, specialized pools, the single-owner case lock, and variable originals delay create serial constraints that A did not face.

Effect modifiers are digital_documents, originals_required, case_complexity, role_specialization, IT_case_lock, and the four capacity nodes, especially legal_capacity and assessment_capacity. A similar control-flow diagram does not guarantee transportability because the diagram encodes only ordering, not eligibility, resources, queues, data-dependent branches, or measurement. Parallel operators with different prerequisites, capacities, and confounding co-interventions identify different causal effects, so the A diagram is an analogy for a mechanism, not evidence that the same effect will occur at B.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Eligibility rules.** Stratify every case at Register by two flags known at intake: originals required or not, and legal review likely or not based on public-sector or high-value status. Full parallelization applies only to no-originals cases. Originals-required cases use identity plus originals concurrency. High-complexity cases are included but flagged and analyzed separately, never pooled silently with simple digital cases.

**Tasks that may be created or executed concurrently.** After Register, create Identity Check for all cases and Originals Receipt for required cases at the same time. For no-originals cases, also create Document Check at the same time. For originals-required cases, create Document Check only on the Originals Receipt event, and only when a document reviewer slot is available under the pull rule below. Never create an executable Document Check before originals are received where they are legally required.

**Statutory prerequisites that remain mandatory.** Register is first. Originals Receipt precedes Document Check when originals are legally required. Assessment requires completed Identity Check and completed Document Check. Legal Review, where required, follows Assessment. Approve or Reject follows Assessment or Legal Review as applicable. Notify follows the decision. No statutory ordering is waived in the pilot.

**Queue and WIP controls.** Use pull release by role. A task enters a ready pool unassigned until a reviewer in that role pulls it. Set per-reviewer ready WIP caps adapted to pool size, for example no more than five ready tasks per identity or document reviewer and a smaller cap for assessors and legal reviewers, and a global CONWIP limit on cases in verification. If a pool is above its utilization threshold, new parallel tasks stay in the ready pool and are not assigned, preventing batch flooding of Assessment or Legal Review.

**Resource responsibilities.** Identity reviewers own Identity Check. Document reviewers own Originals Receipt verification and Document Check. Assessors own Assessment. Legal reviewers own Legal Review and supplement decisions. A case receives a two-owner lock exception only for the verification pair, with both owner IDs logged. Cross-cover is allowed only as a logged exception, not as an assumption.

**Legal-review and supplement loops.** If Legal Review issues Request Supplement, reopen Document Check, and where the supplement concerns identity, reopen Identity Check, then repeat Assessment and return to Legal Review. Do not re-run the full parallel release automatically. Track loop number, reason, and time in each loop iteration.

**Logging requirements.** Log system timestamps for task created, ready, started, completed, and queue entry and exit, with owner ID, role, lock-exception ID, originals-required flag, legal flag, complexity, external latency, and supplement reason. Manual back-entry is prohibited for the pilot cases. Audit a weekly random sample against source systems.

**Compact POWL-style partial-order description.** Register precedes Identity Check and Originals Receipt. Identity Check is parallel with Originals Receipt. Originals Receipt precedes Document Check when required. Identity Check precedes Assessment and Document Check precedes Assessment. Assessment precedes Legal Review when flagged. Legal Review may precede Request Supplement, which precedes a repeated Document Check and repeated Assessment and returns to Legal Review. Assessment or Legal Review precedes the exclusive choice of Approve or Reject, which precedes Notify. In relation form: Register < Identity, Register < Originals, Originals < Document if required, Identity < Assessment, Document < Assessment, Assessment < Legal if flagged, Legal < Supplement < Document < Assessment as a loop, Assessment < Decision, Legal < Decision, Decision < Notify, with Identity parallel to Originals and, only when originals are not required, Identity parallel to Document.

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

| design element | specification | causal purpose | operational safeguard | process-mining measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unit of assignment | Individual case, assigned at Register after flags are known | Avoids ecological bias and allows stratum-specific effects | Assignment is system-generated and cannot be overridden without a logged reason | Case ID linked to all events, flags, and arm |

| Treatment versions | Control is current sequential creation. Intervention is the segmented model from Section 6. | Transportability analysis | Pre-register effect modification by originals, legal, complexity, and pool utilization, and test whether waiting mediates cycle effects | Converts a local pilot into evidence about where the effect transports | Publish stratum results even when the pooled effect is null | Causal mediation through true overlap and waiting, with negative-control outcomes |

Mechanism failure means true_overlap occurs in logs, queues are worked as intended, but cycle time does not improve in the eligible stratum, implying overlap does not remove the critical path at B. Implementation failure means tasks are not truly concurrent - lock prevents simultaneous ownership, document tasks are created before originals arrive, reviewers do not pull work, or timestamps are back-entered - so true_overlap stays low and any null result is uninformative about the mechanism. The pilot therefore measures fidelity first: proportion of pilot cases with overlapping start-complete intervals by system timestamps, waiting before Assessment, resource utilization, WIP, and loop counts, by stratum. If fidelity is high and waiting falls but cycle time and p90 do not, the mechanism is ineffective or displaced downstream, supporting adaptation or abandonment. If fidelity is low, the null reflects implementation, prompting IT and queue fixes and a retest rather than a conclusion that parallelization cannot work at B. Negative-control outcomes and pre-registered subgroup contrasts prevent attributing co-interventions to treatment. This separation protects both internal validity and transport learning.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import Activity, PartialOrder, StrictPartialOrder, Choice, Loop

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Site A: process trees before and after the intervention.
# The before tree is a strict sequence. The after tree uses the parallel
# operator (+) over Identity Check and Document Check.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
site_a_before_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

site_a_after_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

# Convert both Site A process trees to accepting Petri nets.
a_before_net, a_before_im, a_before_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_before_tree)
a_after_net, a_after_im, a_after_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_after_tree)

print("Site A before is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(a_before_net))
print("Site A after is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(a_after_net))

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Site B candidate POWL model.
# Activities are POWL nodes. PartialOrder encodes concurrency with explicit
# order edges. StrictPartialOrder encodes sequential composition. Choice
# encodes the exclusive Approve/Reject decision and the optional legal
# branch. Loop encodes Legal Review -> Request Supplement -> (repeat).
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
register = Activity("Register")
identity = Activity("Identity Check")
originals = Activity("Originals Receipt")
document = Activity("Document Check")
assessment = Activity("Assessment")
legal_review = Activity("Legal Review")
supplement = Activity("Request Supplement")
notify = Activity("Notify")


def make_decision():
    # Fresh Activity objects are used for each occurrence of a label so that
    # parent and child relationships are not shared between two branches.
    return Choice(nodes=[Activity("Approve"), Activity("Reject")])


# Optional legal branch with a supplement loop. The Loop node takes the
# legal review as its do-part and the supplement request as its redo-part.
legal_loop = Loop(nodes=[legal_review, supplement])

# A legal case goes through the loop and then reaches a decision. A
# non-legal case reaches a decision directly. This Choice is the optional
# legal-review branch.
legal_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[legal_loop, make_decision()])
legal_or_direct = Choice(nodes=[legal_branch, make_decision()])

# Verification partial order. The order edges are explicit: Originals
# Receipt must precede Document Check, and Assessment must wait for BOTH
# Identity Check and Document Check. Identity Check and the
# Originals -> Document chain are otherwise concurrent.
verification = PartialOrder(
    nodes=[identity, originals, document, assessment],
    order=[
        (originals, document),
        (identity, assessment),
        (document, assessment),
    ],
)

# Set the order edges explicitly as well, so the Assessment gate is not
# left implicit in the construction.
verification.order = [
    (originals, document),
    (identity, assessment),
    (document, assessment),
]

# Top-level Site B model: Register, then verification with its Assessment
# gate, then the optional legal branch or direct decision, then Notify.
# Because verification is a single sequential node here, Assessment inside
# it completes only after both verification activities complete.
site_b_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[register, verification, legal_or_direct, notify]
)

# Convert the POWL model to an accepting Petri net.
powl_net, powl_im, powl_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_b_powl)
```

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go** — Pilot a segmented version at Site B; do not copy Site A's blanket parallelization for every case.

What should be transferred is the mechanism, not the diagram: independent work should be released together when its statutory prerequisites are already satisfied, downstream queues are managed, and real concurrent execution is logged. For cases where originals are not legally required, most notably digital claims, Identity Check and Document Check can be created simultaneously after Register, because neither depends on the other and both are required before Assessment. For originals-required cases, what should be transferred is concurrency only between Identity Check and Originals Receipt; Document Check must remain blocked until originals are received. Assessment must continue to require both checks, and legal review, where required, must remain after Assessment with its supplement loop intact. What should not be copied is immediate creation of a Document Check task for every case, cross-training assumptions, Site A's WIP limit without regard to Site B's specialized pools, or the expectation of a nine-hour gain.

Enter the no-originals-required stratum first, initially excluding, or separately stratifying, public-sector and high-value cases likely to need legal review, because those cases add a downstream bottleneck, a 26% supplement rate among legal cases, and much of the tail. Within that first stratum, use pull-based WIP caps by role and require system timestamps and a lock exception that permits two owners. Expected benefits are modest and concentrated: perhaps two to five hours from the population median if utilization is controlled, larger for eligible digital cases, with little change for originals-required cases. Risks include assessment batching, legal congestion, inflated overlap from back-entry, and rework if document work starts on incomplete originals.

Expansion should require high true overlap, lower waiting before Assessment, no deterioration in p90, rework, or appeals, and stable legal queues in the pilot stratum. Modification should follow if waiting falls but p90 worsens, indicating bottleneck shift. Abandonment should follow if fidelity is high yet waiting and cycle time do not improve, or if harmful signatures appear despite fixes. This sequence tests transportability where the mechanism is structurally possible before exposing legally constrained and congested subgroups.

## 10. Assumption register

1. No-originals digital cases are structurally parallelizable because Identity Check and Document Check are independent and both are prerequisites for Assessment — Why needed: this is the only subgroup where Site A's mechanism can operate unchanged at Site B — How to test: verify prerequisites in the case rules and measure true overlap and waiting in the pilot stratum — Consequence if false: the first stratum is not eligible and the pilot must be redesigned around a different concurrency pair.
2. A two-owner case-lock exception can be implemented with auditable system timestamps — Why needed: without it, parallel creation does not produce parallel execution and overlap cannot be measured — How to test: audit lock-exception use and compare system timestamps with source records weekly — Consequence if false: the pilot suffers implementation failure and any null result is uninformative about the mechanism.
3. Assessment and legal capacity are not saturated at pilot volume under WIP caps — Why needed: parallel verification otherwise shifts the queue downstream and can worsen the tail — How to test: monitor utilization, queue length, and waiting by role throughout the pilot — Consequence if false: median gains may coexist with p90 deterioration, requiring capacity or release smoothing before expansion.
4. Originals delay is independent of identity work, so identity can be completed during the originals wait — Why needed: this is the only saving available to originals-required cases in the segmented design — How to test: compare identity completion time with originals receipt time and post-receipt waiting — Consequence if false: originals-required cases gain nothing and should not be included in the expected benefit.
5. System event timestamps reflect actual task start and completion rather than back-entry — Why needed: recorded overlap is the fidelity mediator and the basis for process mining — How to test: weekly audit of a random sample against source systems and reviewer attestation — Consequence if false: overlap is inflated, mechanism and implementation failure cannot be distinguished, and the pilot data are invalid.
6. The legal-review flag is known accurately at Register — Why needed: stratification, eligibility, and the optional legal branch all depend on knowing which cases will need legal review — How to test: compare the Register flag with the actual legal review outcome and measure misclassification — Consequence if false: legal cases contaminate the first stratum and downstream congestion is misattributed to parallelization.
7. Starting Document Check on complete originals does not increase rework or supplement rates — Why needed: a waiting saving that creates repeated work is not a net benefit, especially with 17% baseline rework — How to test: track rework, supplement rate, and loop reasons by arm and stratum with pre-set stop rules — Consequence if false: the intervention harms quality and must be gated more strictly or abandoned.
8. Staffing, huddles, API changes, and incoming case mix remain constant across pilot arms — Why needed: these were the confounders that made Site A's before-after evidence non-identified — How to test: log staffing, huddle practice, external latency, and weekly incoming mix, with a negative-control outcome — Consequence if false: pilot effects are confounded in the same way as Site A's and cannot support a transport decision.
