## 4. Forecast summary

- **Plausible earliest completion:** 22:00 Friday (smooth path).
- **Central/P50-like remaining-time range:** 22:00–24:00 Friday (48–48 h).
- **P80-like remaining-time range:** 22:00–00:00 Monday (48–48 h).
- **P90-like or severe-tail range:** 00:00–04:00 Monday (48–48 h).
- **Most plausible terminal event(s):** Release of batch B-417 on Friday 20:30.
- **Severe tail:** Batch release on Monday 20:30 due to contamination or investigation.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Microbiology branch completion** (dominant path uncertainty): The incubation start time and readout confirmation are highly uncertain, especially with the unknown temperature excursion.
2. **QA queue congestion** (queue uncertainty): The arrival rate and overdispersion affect how quickly QA can process B-417, potentially causing delays.
3. **Documentation Query or investigation** (service-time uncertainty): The probability and duration of queries or investigations can significantly extend the timeline.
4. **Temperature excursion flag** (dependence uncertainty): The unknown status of the flag introduces ambiguity in the microbiology pathway, affecting the likelihood of contamination or investigation.
5. **Calendar constraints** (calendar uncertainty): Weekend pauses for QA and microbiology staffing create periods where no progress can be made, adding to the tail risk.

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

The **confirmation of the temperature excursion** would most change the forecast. If confirmed, the microbiology pathway shortens (readout proceeds immediately). If not confirmed, the branch remains in incubation until Monday, extending the timeline significantly. A secondary update trigger would be the arrival of new samples in the QA queue, which could increase congestion and delay the join time.
