## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning

**Do not monetize across the compliance boundary.** The €20k–35k deadline value and the €15k–80k customer-harm range are ordinary operational losses; the €100k–500k regulatory range is a *tail with non-monetary components* (licence conditions, correspondent-banking de-risking, criminal exposure, personal liability for R17). These are not commensurable, so I apply a **lexicographic filter first** (drop A, B; drop G until enabled), then compare expected operational loss among survivors.

Even inside a purely monetary frame, A fails: its upside is capped at 0.75 × €35k  **€26k** (the payment must still have been approvable), while its expected regulatory cost at the *most favourable* corner is 0.15 × €100k = **€15k**, and at midrange  0.30 × €300k = **€90k**. Dominance holds across nearly the whole parameter box, and the non-monetary terms break any residual tie.

Among conformant plans, the P1-vs-P2 comparison is genuinely close:
- P2 buys P-884 roughly **+€3k–8k** of expected avoided deadline loss.
- P2 imposes on P-901 roughly **€2.4k–11k+** of expected loss, *plus* unpriced review-quality degradation and the risk of pushing a second high-value case into deadline panic (the systemic pathway by which A/B get chosen elsewhere).

The ranges overlap, so no numeric dominance. Four tie-breakers favour P1:
1. **Slack asymmetry**: 45 min of interruption consumes ~64% of P-901's remaining 70-min window, versus ~50% of P-884's 90.
2. **Approval uncertainty**: 25–45% of worlds end in rejection/hold — burning shared capacity to accelerate a case that may not be approvable is poor portfolio policy.
3. **Real options**: waiting for S4 *preserves* the S2 interrupt; interrupting P-901 is irreversible. The option decays (S2 must start by ~t=20–25 to be useful), which is exactly why the gate is time-boxed rather than open-ended.
4. **Information value**: screening improves the decision in *every* branch, including rejection quality — it reduces false-rejection harm (0.25–0.45  ~0.10–0.25), which is worth €1.5k–16k and is invisible to a deadline-only objective.

**Deadline loss is an acceptable outcome.** If no conformant path can execute by t=90, the correct answer is a documented breach plus a non-tipping-off customer notification, not a shortcut.
