## 2. Rule Interpretation

- **RR1 (Drafter/approver separation):** The human who performs `Draft_Change_Request` must not perform `Approve_Change`. Checked after alias normalization.
- **RR2 (Implementer/verifier separation):** The human who performs `Implement_Change` must not perform `Verify_Implementation`. Checked after alias normalization and service account attribution.
- **RR3 (High-risk approval authority):** HIGH-risk cases require QM or valid QM delegate approval. A delegation is valid if the event timestamp falls within the effective interval, the scope matches, the principal is absent, and no prohibited subdelegation occurred. A later database entry (e.g., D-QM-01 entered 08-12) can prove an earlier delegation if supported by prior signed evidence (PDF on 08-09). Action by a principal while on leave (Asha on 08-13) temporarily suspends the "principal absent" condition for that specific action, but does not invalidate the delegation for other actions unless the principal officially returns.
- **RR4 (Emergency substitution):** Emergency HIGH-risk changes require IC approval, followed by QM `Retrospective_QM_Review` within 2 business days. Business days exclude weekends (Saturday, Sunday) and site holidays. Day 1 starts the business day following the emergency approval.
- **RR5 (Identity evidence):** Service accounts are attributed to humans based on session/badge logs. Aliases are normalized to human entities.
- **RR6 (Suspicion versus proof):** Indicators of suspicion (repeated pairs, shared workstations) are noted but not treated as definitive proof of collusion without corroborating evidence (e.g., financial benefit, explicit policy violation).

## 4. Collusion and Alternative-Explanation Analysis

- **Repeated pairs (Clara drafting, Ben approving):**
  - *Benign:* They are in the same production cell, so they naturally work together on most changes.
  - *Adverse:* They are colluding to bypass scrutiny on production changes.
  - *Discriminator:* Compare approval rejection rates and review times for this pair vs. others; check if they rotate when requested.
- **Reciprocal behavior (Ben drafting LOW, Clara approving):**
  - *Benign:* Normal team cross-training or workload sharing for low-risk documents.
  - *Adverse:* Quid pro quo for Ben approving Clara's HIGH-risk changes.
  - *Discriminator:* Evidence of direct benefit, quality of the LOW-risk changes.
- **Shared workstations (WS-DM-04):**
  - *Benign:* Standard shop-floor practice where users don't log out to save time.
  - *Adverse:* Intentional use of shared terminals to mask identity for unauthorized verifications (e.g., CHG-503).
  - *Discriminator:* CCTV footage, implementation of forced re-authentication.
- **Absence conflicts (Asha VPN, Ben sick leave):**
  - *Benign:* Dedicated employees working briefly while on leave to handle urgent tasks.
  - *Adverse:* Credential sharing (Asha) or credential compromise (Ben's office workstation used while he is sick).
  - *Discriminator:* VPN origin IP/device fingerprint for Asha; CCTV or badge logs for Ben's office workstation.

## 6. Control Improvements

- **Delegation creation:** Require system-enforced delegation rules (no subdelegation, scope limits) rather than relying on PDF/email.
- **Effective-time recording:** Ensure delegations are active in the system before use.
- **Principal availability:** Integrate HR leave system with QMS to automatically activate delegations and suspend principal access (or flag overrides).
- **Alias management:** Enforce single identity per human across all systems.
- **Individual authentication:** Eliminate shared accounts; mandate individual logins or FIDO tokens at shared workstations.
- **Emergency review:** Implement automated alerts for pending retrospective reviews to prevent missing the 2-day SLA.
- **Pair rotation:** Introduce system-enforced rotation for drafter/approver pairs to reduce collusion risk.
- **Network monitoring:** Regularly run PM4Py network analysis to detect unusual collaboration patterns.
- **Evidential retention:** Ensure CCTV and badge logs are retained longer for critical areas.
