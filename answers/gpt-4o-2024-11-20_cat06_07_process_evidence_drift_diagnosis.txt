## 2. Competing explanations and timeline

**January–March (Stable Baseline)**: Metrics indicate moderate but stable performance (e.g., observed breach rate ~0.18, mean predicted risk ~0.19). All variants are within expected ranges. The pipeline is fundamentally sound with no identified major data-quality issues.

**April (Pipeline Failure)**: The renaming of `Initial Diagnosis Completed` disrupts the event-sequencing pipeline. Predictive quality collapses (AUROC drops to 0.67, observed breach rate increases to 0.19). Evidence of unmapped records (`UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` spikes to 17%) and incomplete feature backfilling (14% "missing queue" features) confirm this as a primarily pipeline-driven incident, not genuine systemic or model deterioration.

**May (Partial Recovery)**: Data-pipeline issues are mitigated by the patch, with significantly fewer `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` records (2%) and an AUROC recovery to 0.78. Observed breach rates return to prior levels (~0.18), meaning pipeline-driven distortions are largely resolved for May records.

**June (Process Change)**: The new remote-routing policy significantly shifts case composition toward V2, which now constitutes 52% of all cases. This improves overall breach rates (0.15) and provides more stable predictions (Brier = 0.139). No explicit evidence of concept drift; process change alone explains the improvements.

**August–September (Overlapping Shifts)**: 
- Severe delays in parts delivery drastically increase V3 breach rates (August: 0.64, September: 0.61). The median delay for V3's `Order Part -> Part Received` grows from 42 hours in June to 148 hours in September, validating concept drift within this path.
- Partner Z onboarding in September introduces additional population shift, evidenced by rural characteristics, older devices, high routing through V3, and a breach rate (0.45) nearly double that of other sources (0.27).
- AUROC declines over these months (August: 0.68, September: 0.66), indicating model difficulty in capturing these changes.

**October (Label Delay Confounds)**: Widespread outcome API delays heavily distort breach rates and quality metrics. For example, observed breach rates (0.16) are significantly lower than expected due to delayed resolution of long-path cases like V3, which are disproportionately mature in later phases.

## 3. Diagnostic tests

1. **Label maturity curves and fixed-vintage backtesting**:
   - **Expected under label delay**: October metrics (observed breach, AUROC, etc.) are unreliably low for immature cohorts.
   - **Expected under genuine process failure**: Fixed-vintage backtesting shows predictable stability across outcome maturities.

2. **Recomputation post `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` decontamination**:
   - **Expected if pipeline error is root cause**: April AUROC and Brier scores improve with corrected features.
   - **Expected otherwise**: No major effect on April metrics.

3. **Calibration and discrimination stratified by variant, source (incl. Partner Z)**:
   - **Expected under population shift**: Discrimination/calibration degraded for Partner Z in V3.
   - **Expected under process stability**: Metrics remain stable across all sources.

4. **DFG and performance-DFG temporal analyses**:
   - **Expected under process changes**: Variants like V3 show increasing resolution delays.
   - **Expected under pipeline failure only**: No significant temporal differences in process-dependency times.

5. **Outcome-relationship tests**:
   - **Expected under concept drift (V3-specific)**: New feature-performance relationships emerge for V3 breaches, especially parts delays.
   - **Expected under population shift only**: No internal feature-outcome changes; shifts are entirely cohort-based.

6. **Reweighting/transport-style tests for Partner Z**:
   - **Expected under population shift**: Reweighted comparisons reduce Partner Z predictive anomalies.
   - **Expected under concept drift**: Anomalies persist despite reweighting.

7. **Feature missingness and lineage audits**:
   - **Expected under pipeline issues**: April anomalies stem directly from feature-gen errors.
   - **Expected otherwise**: All months show random or stable variation in feature availability.

## 4. Recalibration-versus-retraining decision

**Immediate containment**: Halt October reliance on emerging metrics until label backlog clears; recalibrate predictions temporarily based on historical Partner Z proportions and pathways.

**Short-term recalibration**:
- Exclude April and October cohorts from recalibration due to severe distortions.
- Adjust V3-related components (e.g., parts delays) in recalibration weights.

**Feature repair**: Fully backfill cached April features, clean duplicates, and re-extract process features.

**Full retraining**: Suggested post-October to incorporate Partner Z pathways from Sept., ensuring observable and matured outcomes. Segment-specific modeling for V3 and Partner Z may enhance quality.

## 5. Monitoring architecture

Proposed architecture includes:

- **Predictive metrics**: AUROC, Brier stratified by source/variant.
- **Calibration**: ECE stratified by cohort maturity.
- **Label maturity**: Fixed-vintage metrics and lag thresholds.
- **Data quality**: Unknown activity tracking, feature completeness.
- **Process variant**: Distribution and edge durations (e.g., V3 delays).
- **Performance-DFG**: Median operational times by variant/partner.
- **Population**: Partner Z weight shifts and rural-case shares.
- **Alert links**: Delayed-label alarms pause retraining; quality drops only trigger intervention post-validation.

### Python code block (process-evidence extraction):
```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

# Load and preprocess event data
df = pd.read_csv("field_service_events.csv")
df = pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id="case_id", activity_key="activity", timestamp_key="timestamp")

# Normalize labels
df['activity'] = df['activity'].replace({"Diagnostic Assessment Completed": "Initial Diagnosis Completed"})

# Remove duplicates
df = df.loc[~df.duplicated(subset=["case_id", "activity", "timestamp"])]

# Cohorting
event_calendar = pm4py.filter_time_range(df, start_timestamp="2026-01-01", end_timestamp="2026-10-31")
case_start = df[df['activity'] == 'Case Created']

# Extract process measures
daily_dfg = pm4py.discover_dfg(event_calendar)
perf_dfg = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(event_calendar)
variant_stats = pm4py.get_variants(event_calendar)
temporal_features = pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(df)

# Generate auditable summaries
summary = {
    "event_counts": event_calendar.groupby('activity').size(),
    "case_variants": pd.Series(variant_stats).value_counts(),
    "dfg_edge_times": perf_dfg
}
summary
```

## 6. Risks of acting on the wrong diagnosis

1. **Retraining on immature labels**:
   - **Risk**: Model performance degraded due to incomplete feedback loops.
   - **Mitigation**: Use fixed-vintage validation windows.

2. **Calibrating around pipeline bugs**:
   - **Risk**: Persistent April-like distortions in downstream tasks.
   - **Mitigation**: Recreate improperly computed April-May features.

3. **Ignoring genuine supplier-driven concept drift**:
   - **Risk**: Loss of predictive value for V3 cases under parts delays.
   - **Mitigation**: Monitor path-specific performance and retrain segments.

4. **Misattributing Partner Z as unfair bias**:
   - **Risk**: Population shifts mistaken for systemic modeling flaws.
   - **Mitigation**: Perform feature-wise comparison against Partner-Z-neutral baselines.
