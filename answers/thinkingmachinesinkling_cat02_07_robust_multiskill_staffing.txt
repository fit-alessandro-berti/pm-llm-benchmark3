# 3. Candidate policies

All policies share these common mechanics: shifts/breaks enforced; overtime up to 30 min allowed; handover (+7 min) and inter-team setup (+5 min) applied explicitly; coordination delay (+8 min) applied only under parallel execution for ME/ES; preemption permitted only on diagnostic activities, once per case.

- **Dispatch score:** First-come-first-served within priority tier; P1 always first within pool.

**Policy P — Full pooling with transparent shortest-expected-processing-time (SEPT) dispatch.**

- **Parallelization:** For ME/ES, always attempt parallel: assign Mechanical and Electrical (or Electrical and Software) to the two qualified resources with the lowest individual `E_rem`, provided both are available within 5 minutes of each other; otherwise fall back to sequential on the fastest single qualified resource.

- **Handover/setup:** Applied mechanically (7 min on change; +5 min inter-team). Because resources float, these occur frequently; they are logged as separate delay events in the simulation.

**PM4Py analyses and semantics.**
- `pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id='case:concept:name', activity_key='concept:name', timestamp_key='start_timestamp')` converts the synthetic log to PM4Py format.
- `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(log)` computes for each case: `service_time` (sum of `service_actual_minutes`) and `waiting_time` (time between previous `time:timestamp` and next `start_timestamp`, excluding coordination/setup/handover events that are logged separately). This separates value-added time from queue and transition delays.
- `pm4py.get_service_time(log)` returns stage-level service statistics; we apply it separately to Intake, Mechanical, Electrical, Software, and Validation to detect service-time drift (e.g., U4 increasing complex-tail draws).
- `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(log)` reveals resource-to-resource transfers. Under Policy P we expect a dense network (many edges); under S a sparse network confined within pools; under H an intermediate density with strong AdaDeepa and ChenEmil edges.
- `pm4py.discover_dfg(log, activity_key='concept:name', timestamp_key='start_timestamp', case_id='case:concept:name')` shows the direct-follow graph; for ME/ES it should reveal parallel structures when `parallel_branch` attributes are aggregated.
- `pm4py.discover_bpmn(log, ...)` can be compared across policy versions to visualize coordination and handover paths.

*Labeling note:* `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` operates at the case level; for stage-specific metrics (e.g., “waiting time before Mechanical”), the log should be filtered by `concept:name` before calling, or calculated separately via pandas groupby on `queue_entry_timestamp` and `start_timestamp`.
