## 2. Rule Interpretation

**RR1 Drafter/Approver separation:** `human(Draft_Change_Request) != human(Approve_Change)` per case. Applied **after** alias normalization. Risk-agnostic. No exception for LOW risk.

**RR2 Implementer/Verifier separation:** `human(Implement_Change) != human(Verify_Implementation)` per case. After attribution. Service account unattributed = inability to prove separation  `DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM` not proof of separation.

**RR3 High-risk approval authority:** If `risk==HIGH` and `Approve_Change` occurs, performer must be at that timestamp either (a) `role==QM` (Asha from 2026-01-01) or (b) holder of a **valid** QM-delegation (D-QM-01/02) that passes all four conjuncts: inside interval, scope covers `scope`, principal absent/unavailable if condition present, no prohibited subdelegation. Delegation condition is evaluated at event time from HR/absence logs, not from DB `created_at`.

*Stale master data:* `Eva` is **not** QM merely because current user master says Quality Engineering. `Eva` on 2026-08-11 23:59 `Independent Verifier`; from 2026-08-12 `Quality Engineer`. Requires delegation for HIGH QM approval. Similarly Grace never acquires QM.

*Later database entry:* A QMS record created **after** the approval cannot create authority ex nihilo, but also does not invalidate authority if an earlier signed artifact (PDF 2026-08-09) proves the delegation existed and was effective at event time. Effective-time is interval, not `created_at`. `D-QM-01` is valid from 2026-08-10 despite DB migration on 2026-08-12.

*Principal on leave but acts:* A principal who is formally on leave but authenticates and acts personally performs an **authoritative** act as principal if identity is proven. That act is valid QM approval; it does not retroactively void delegate acts before/after, but during the exact window of proven presence the `principal-absence` condition for a delegate is **not** satisfied. Credential-sharing uncertainty (Asha VPN)  we cannot exclude delegate misuse, so that window is `INDETERMINATE` for identity provenance but not automatically `UNAUTHORIZED`.

**RR4 Emergency substitution:** `IF risk==HIGH AND emergency==true AND scope==cooling_system AND safety rationale credible AND IC_or_valid_IC_delegate approved, THEN implementation before QM retrospective is a justified exception **iff** a QM / valid QM-delegate performs `Retrospective_QM_Review` within **two business days** after emergency approval.

*Business day count used:* Exclusive of approval day, count Mon-Fri only, no holidays in period, deadline = EOD (23:59 UTC) of second business day after `Approve_Change`. Example: approve Mon 2026-08-11  business day 1 = Tue 12, day 2 = Wed 13  deadline 2026-08-13 23:59. Weekend days 2026-08-15/16 are excluded; if deadline falls on weekend it rolls forward to next business day. This is stated in code as configurable; alternative inclusive counting would shift deadline by one day and is noted as judgment.

**RR5 Identity evidence:** `svc_qms` is never a human. Four-eyes checks must run on normalized human IDs; unattributed service events are flagged `UNATTRIBUTED` and excluded from automatic pass. Strong badge+FIDO in same minute at same workstation = strong attribution; badge-only on shared workstation with no re-auth = weak/unattributed.

**RR6 Suspicion versus proof:** Repeated resource pairs (ClaraBen 18/23), reciprocal approvals, shared workstation handover, concentration metrics from `discover_handover_of_work_network` / `discover_working_together_network` are **indicators**, not proof of collusion. Collusion requires supporting evidence (e.g., instruction, benefit, credential sharing). Network interpretation must not infer misconduct from frequency alone.

## 4. Collusion and Alternative-Explanation Analysis

All network / frequency signals below are **indicators** under RR6, not proof. Each needs discriminating evidence.

**a) Repeated pair Clara (draft)  Ben (approve) 18/23 HIGH production in 60 days (CHG-501/503/505-retro/506/508/510-retro). Concentration seen in handover network ClaraBen.**
* Benign: Same production cell staffing; Clara is frequent drafter, Ben is designated QM-delegate in August and prior informal cover; no rotation policy exists  natural concentration. Handover metric `p21` high reflects workflow design.
* Adverse: Reciprocal favoritism / auto-approval ring; Ben approves Clara's drafts without independent review.
* Discriminate: Sample 18 approvals for independent technical comments, time-spent review evidence, rejection rate vs Asha's 3 approvals, segregation of approval across cells, blind re-review quality.

**b) Reciprocal pattern: Ben drafted 4 LOW training changes that Clara approved (vs. ClaraBen).**
* Benign: Training docs are low-risk and cell colleagues cross-approve as peers.
* Adverse: Tit-for-tat "you approve mine, I approve yours" bypass of independent oversight.
* Discriminate: Content independence, whether Clara had competence to approve training docs, financial/benefit trail (none known), rotation of training approvals after policy change.

**c) Shared workstation / handover concentration: CHG-503 `svc_qms` on WS-DM-04 (Diego's WS) with Diego badge but no re-auth. Handover network Diegosvc_qms / DiegoEva edges inflate. CHG-509 WS-IV-02 shared verifier station.**
* Benign: Verifier station is legitimately shared; terminal timeout left logged-in service account; legitimate verifier performed verify but failed to re-authenticate.
* Adverse: Implementer (Diego) performed self-verification via service account to close change faster (RR2 breach); CHG-503 self-verification.
* Discriminate: Retrieve FIDO/token logs for WS-DM-04 at 12:30, keystroke biometrics, verification checklist content, CCTV recovery, interview of who was physically at terminal.

**d) Absence-conflict credential use: Ben sick leave 12:00 08-13 vs `u_bk` approval 13:00 (CHG-506) from office IP not home; Asha VPN 09:15-09:40 vs leave.**
* Benign: Ben attended office briefly despite sick note; Asha genuinely worked one case remotely on leave (as claimed).
* Adverse: Credential sharing / shared account use: colleague used Ben's `u_bk` to keep QM approvals flowing during sick leave; Asha credential shared to allow delegates to act as QM.
* Discriminate: Device fingerprint of VPN/office WS, badge logs for Ben at 13:00, HR sick-leave verification, Asha phone interview with specifics of CHG-504 decision rationale, IP geolocation vs home.

**e) Emergency subdelegation chain DiegoFaridClara (D-IC-04 email only).**
* Benign: Emergency pressure led Farid to believe he could delegate onward to maintain coverage during incident; misunderstanding of no-subdelegation clause.
* Adverse: Intentional creation of informal authority bypass to allow drafter to self-approve emergency (CHG-510 RR1 + RR3 failure).
* Discriminate: Email content, incident command log for CI-44 staffing, whether Farid was actually unavailable 14:00-16:00, training on delegation policy.

**Network interpretation guardrails applied in code:** `discover_handover_of_work_network` and `discover_working_together_network` quantify frequency/direction; we **do not** label high handover as collusion. We report top 3 handovers and require qualitative evidence before suspicion escalates.

## 6. Control Improvements

**Delegation creation & effective-time recording:**
* Require dual-signature delegation PDF to be registered in QMS **before** effective start; block `Approve` if `created_at > effective_start` without secondary PDF evidence. Add immutable `effective_from/to` separate from `created_at`; never use `created_at` as authority proof.

**Principal availability:**
* Define "absent or formally unavailable" in policy: includes certified leave, off-site without remote access; explicitly state whether weekend/non-working hours counts. Require real-time HR calendar integration; if principal logs in (VPN/badge) during delegation, system suspends delegate authority and alerts. Require principal to formally revoke/pause delegation rather than informal leave.

**Alias management:**
* Eliminate duplicate accounts (`b.koch` vs `u_bk`). Single identity per human with alias table mastered in IAM; enforce normalized human ID in all workflow logs. Historical alias map must be versioned and applied retroactively for audits. Block approval if `human(draft)==human(approve)` after normalization.

**Individual authentication:**
* Enforce re-authentication (FIDO2/password) for every `Approve` and `Verify` step, especially at shared workstations (WS-DM-04, WS-IV-02). Disable generic `svc_qms` for human actions; service account may only perform system steps (e.g., `Close_Change` automation) with `on_behalf_of` human field populated. Session token must be bound to human, not terminal.

**Emergency review (RR4):**
* Codify business-day calculator in QMS (configurable EOD, holiday calendar, inclusive/exclusive). Auto-create `Retrospective_QM_Review` task with deadline + escalation to Site Director if overdue. Require QM retro to record safety rationale review; block `Close_Change` until retro completed or formally waived with risk assessment.

**Pair rotation & independent review:**
* Introduce rotation/ randomization for HIGH-risk approvals; forbid same drafter/approver pair > 50% in rolling 30/60 days without QA oversight. Require secondary spot-check and documented review comments/time. Monitor reciprocal low-risk approvals.

**Network monitoring & retention:**
* Continuous `handover_of_work` and `working_together` dashboards as **detective** controls only; alert on concentration but require human investigation. Retain for 2 years: badge, FIDO, IP, VPN device fingerprint, workstation video stills, delegation PDFs, approval comments. Preserve immutable audit log of `effective interval` vs `DB creation` to detect stale master data.

**Evidential retention:**
* Mandate session evidence retention for every `svc_qms` use; if missing, treat as control deficiency not evidence of compliance.
