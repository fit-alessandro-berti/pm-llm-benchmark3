# 7. Final synthesis

The most identifiable queue is **QA**. Despite the appointment-related exception for A03 and the late A06 appointment, QA largely preserves arrival order: A02, A04, A05, A06, A07, and A08 follow a FIFO pattern once the appointment insertion is treated as a localized hybrid component. The main remaining ambiguity is whether the appointment mechanism is primary with FIFO fill-in, or FIFO primary with appointment windows.

The least identifiable queue is **QB**. B01’s two interruptions and resumptions create a moving decision context. The log contains evidence consistent with preemptive priority, FIFO, SPT, manual dispatcher reassignment, and preempted-job aging, often in adjacent decisions. Without a dispatch-decision log and explicit preemption/override reasons, QB cannot be confidently assigned to a single documented mechanism.

The most dangerous claimed policy to infer from these observations alone is **systematic preemptive priority**, especially for QB. The 09:16 preemption of B01 by B02 is salient and could be mistaken for a stable priority rule, but the later resumptions of B01, the selection of B04 over B06, and the explicit “dispatcher reassignment” note suggest manual exceptions or a hybrid policy. Treating this as a documented preemptive-priority rule could encourage unnecessary preemptions, underestimate resumption costs, and create false expectations for high-priority jobs.
