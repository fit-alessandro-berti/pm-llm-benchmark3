

## 1. Normalization Assumptions
**Interval Construction:** For each recorded value `R` with resolution uncertainty `res` and clock offset `o` in `[a,b]`, the true time interval is `[R - res_lower - b, R + res_upper - a]`. Rounding/ flooring errors are treated as half-width bounds.
**Clock Offset Direction:** True time = Recorded time  Clock offset. Thus for offset `[a,b]`, the corrected interval is `[recorded  b, recorded  a]`.
**Time-Zone Conversion:** All times are normalized to UTC. Fixed offsets for 20260601: CEST=UTC+2, EDT=UTC4, BST=UTC+1. For LEGACYBER, two UTC intervals are constructed to cover both fold possibilities.
**DST Folds:** For events recorded during the missinghour fold, both interpretations are retained as disjoint intervals.
**Inferred Starts:** Missing Investigation_Start times are inferred from Completion minus duration bounds, with all rounding/offset uncertainties preserved.
**Causal Constraints:** Event precedence is enforced by taking intersections of durationinferred intervals with causal bounds; when constraints produce an empty set, the case is marked INDETERMINATE.
**Mobile Entry Delay:** For MOBILELON, the displayed timestamp may be up to 300s early due to user entry lag. This delay shifts the possible occurrence earlier: occurrence = recorded  entry_delay  clock_offset. The entry delay is treated as a nondeterministic uncertainty (0 to 300s), not a fixed shift.

## 7. Governance Recommendation
**Timestamp Controls:** Store all event times in UTC with microsecond precision. Retain raw vendor timestamps plus metadata (source, offset, resolution).
**Clock Telemetry:** Require NTP offset telemetry per host; reject events when offset exceeds ±10s or synchronization source is unknown.
**TimeZone & DST:** Log both local and UTC values with explicit timezone identifier. Disable automatic DST conversions during ingestion; validate against calendar rules.
**Lifecycle Semantics:** Standardize start/complete definitions and forbid inferred timestamps in production logs. For missing events, flag cases for manual review rather than imputation.
**Provenance:** Attach a signed receipt or delivery acknowledgement to alert events to enable causal validation.
**Policy:** For enforcement, require intervals to be fully inside the SLA band after tolerance additions; otherwise classify as possible violation and trigger manual adjudication.
