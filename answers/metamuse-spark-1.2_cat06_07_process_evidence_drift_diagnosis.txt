## 2. Competing explanations and timeline

**Jan-Mar: Stable baseline.** Process variants ~45/35/20, `Order->Part` 28-35h, breach 0.18-0.19, predicted 0.18-0.19, AUROC ~0.81, ECE ~0.02, data-quality ~1% missing/0.2% unknown. Model well-calibrated and discriminative on mature labels. Process and population stationary.

**April: Pipeline/system schema failure masquerading as drift.** 1 Apr migration renamed diagnosis; pipeline counted only old label. Effect: prefixes built at wrong point, many cases unmappable  Unmapped 18%, Unknown 17%, Missing queue 14%. Predictive symptoms (AUROC -0.14, ECE +0.086, mean predicted +0.09) occur **without** change in true process performance (median 34h) or large change in matured V3 breach (0.34). Label delay/maturity normal  not censoring. No process or population intervention. Distinct because discrimination collapses exactly where features are missing, not where outcome relationship changed. May would have been falsely labeled concept drift if process evidence ignored.

**May: Incomplete recovery - still pipeline.** 6 May patch remapped label + backfilled most records  Unknown 2%, Unmapped 1% but cached `number of events before diagnosis` not recomputed. ECE remains 0.050 (2x baseline), AUROC 0.78 (vs 0.81). Suggests residual feature staleness, not new drift. Median delay normal, variant mix normalizes to 43/34/22. If this were retrained naively on April-uncorrected features, model would learn artifact.

**June: Real process (control-flow) drift  covariate shift, not concept drift.** 1 Jun remote-routing policy  V2 34%52%, V1 43%30%, V3 stable 18-22%. `Order->Part` 42h stable, other edges stable. Overall observed breach drops to 0.15 (mature 100%) because V2 has lowest breach (0.09-0.11) vs V3 0.35. Model over-predicts (0.20 vs 0.15)  ECE 0.060. Crucially, stratified breach by variant unchanged (V2 0.09 vs 0.11, V1 0.16 vs 0.15)  **P(y|X) stable, P(X) changed**. AUROC stable 0.79 because ranking within variants preserved. Treating June as concept drift would trigger unnecessary retraining; correct response is reweight/recalibrate for new mixture.

**July: Transition - performance drift begins to convert to concept drift.** Median `Order->Part` 4265h (+55%), V3 breach 0.350.46 early supplier miss. Observed breach 0.19 vs predicted 0.18 slight underprediction; AUROC slips to 0.76, ECE 0.042 low-moderate, maturity 98%  mostly real, not censoring. Population still 0% Z. First evidence that a post-prediction bottleneck is altering outcome conditional on pre-diagnosis state. Homogeneous “June-July failure” wrong: June mixture benefit, July parts risk - opposite directions.

**Aug-Sep: Overlapping genuine deterioration - performance + concept + population shift.** Supplier issue severe: edge 130h Aug, 148h Sep. V3 conditional breach 0.64 Aug, 0.61 Sep (~2x baseline)  strong concept drift localized to V3. V3 share rises 22%35%42% (both because more cases get stuck waiting and new population). 1 Sep Partner Z 24%  58% V3 vs 36% non-Z, plus unmodeled rural/age features  additional covariate/population shift whose observed 0.45 vs 0.27 raw breach is confounded by slower maturity. Predictive metrics: AUROC 0.680.66, Brier 0.1970.214, ECE 0.120.14, mean predicted under by 0.09-0.10 on 93-82% maturity  discrimination and calibration both degraded, but **for different reason than April** (here because true conditional risk changed, not because features missing). Data-quality clean (Unknown <1%). Misdiagnosing Aug-Sep as pipeline or pure label delay would miss needed business intervention and feature upgrade.

**October: Label censoring dominates and confounds measurement.** Outcome API backlog  maturity 55%, delay 52d (+10d), precisely when resolution paths longest (155h). Observed breach spuriously halves to 0.16 while performance worsens and V3 stays 41%  differential censoring: non-breaches resolve faster, breaches censored. Brier/ECE/AUROC on 55% cohort invalid vs mature months. No mature variant breach estimate. Treating Oct as improvement or as worse model would be wrong; it is a measurement artifact on top of underlying Aug-Sep issues. Requires vintage backtesting, not retraining.

**Why not homogeneous:** April = pre-prediction feature corruption (overprediction, high missing/unknown, normal conditional risk). June = decision-policy-induced P(X) shift (overprediction due to low-risk V2 up-weight, stable conditional risk). Aug-Sep = post-prediction bottleneck  increases P(y|V3) (underprediction, conditional risk shift) + new subpopulation with unobserved covariates. Oct = censoring-induced apparent reversal (under-observed breaches). Different signs of calibration (over vs under), different edges, different maturity - one label cannot capture.

## 3. Diagnostic tests

**1. Label-maturity curves and fixed-vintage backtesting**
*Method:* For each month (case-start cohort), compute maturity % and observed breach as function of days since case start / cutoff lag. Kaplan-Meier completion and time-to-breach hazard by variant. Recompute AUROC/Brier/ECE on **mature vintage** e.g., cases started  15 Aug evaluated 45 days later vs contemporaneous reporting cutoff. Compare Oct 55% cutoff vs 45-day mature cohort; estimate final breach via inverse-probability-of-censoring weighting.
*Expected under censoring hypothesis:* Oct observed 0.16 rises toward ~0.30-0.35 when vintage fixed; maturity curve still rising at cutoff, median delay longer for V3/breach cases. Calibration improves slightly after vintage fix but Aug-Sep underprediction remains. *Under real improvement hypothesis:* maturity curve flat at cutoff and vintage-corrected Oct breach stays ~0.16 with equal delay by outcome.

**2. Recomputation after activity-label normalization and duplicate cleaning**
*Method:* Reload `field_service_events.csv`, map `Diagnostic Assessment Completed`  `Initial Diagnosis Completed`, deduplicate on (case_id, activity, timestamp) with tolerance, apply both 1 Apr mapping and 6 May backfill uniformly, recompute `number of events before diagnosis`, queue features, variant assignment, then re-score held-out model or replay prefixes `pm4py.get_variants` / `discover_dfg`.
*Expected under pipeline hypothesis:* April Unknown 17%<1%, Unmapped 18%~0%, Missing queue 14%1-2%, AUROC recovers 0.67~0.78-0.81, mean predicted 0.280.19 on recomputed features; May residual ECE/Brier drops to baseline. *Under concept drift hypothesis:* recomputation leaves Aug-Sep metrics unchanged (Unknown already <1%). Falsifies pipeline for Aug-Oct.

**3. Calibration and discrimination stratified by variant, source, month**
*Method:* Reliability diagrams, ECE/Brier/AUROC per V1/V2/V3 and Partner Z vs non-Z per month (mature cohorts only). Also PR-AUC. Compare overall vs within-stratum.
*Expected under covariate/process-mix hypothesis (June):* Overall ECE 0.060 high but within-stratum ECE ~0.02-0.03, V2 vs V1 vs V3 AUROC stable. Overprediction is weighted average of well-calibrated strata. *Under concept drift hypothesis (Aug-Sep):* V3 ECE 0.12-0.14 high and V3 AUROC degrades even within-stratum, while V1/V2 ECE remains near 0.03. Partner Z stratification shows ECE gap shrinks after conditioning on V3 but not fully if rural/age matters.

**4. Comparison of process variants and DFG / performance-DFG edges**
*Method:* `pm4py.discover_dfg` and `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg` (median/mean sojourn) per month on normalized log; trace variant share ( `pm4py.get_variants` ), edge frequency `Order Part -> Part Received`, choice rate at diagnosis. Statistical test for edge duration (Mann-Whitney) vs Mar baseline.
*Expected under process drift:* June: DFG arc DiagnosisRemote Fix frequency +~18pp, p<0.001; performance DFG unchanged. July-Oct: `Order->Part` sojourn distribution shifts right (median 35130-155h, p<0.001) with increased variance, while DiagnosisRemote Fix unchanged. *Under pipeline hypothesis:* April DFG shows phantom `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` self-loops and missing Diagnostic edge, performance DFG artificially truncated - disappears after normalization.

**5. Test of whether outcome relationship conditional on same path changed (concept drift test)**
*Method:* Logistic regression breach ~ features_at_diagnosis + V3 indicator + month + month×V3 interaction on mature cases; or direct comparison of matured breach rate by variant with Wilson CI, and model residual analysis (observed - predicted) by month×variant. Conditional calibration: E[y | pred=p, V3] curve. Also causal check: use pre-July model to predict Jul-Sep, test Hosmer-Lemeshow by variant.
*Expected under concept drift:* Interaction V3×Aug significant OR~3-4, residual per V3 positive 0.2-0.3 in Aug-Sep, V3 conditional calibration curve shifted up (predicted 0.3  observed 0.5-0.6). V1/V2 residuals ~0. *Under pure covariate drift:* No interaction, residuals ~0 within each variant across months including June.

**6. Population reweighting / transport-style checks for Partner Z**
*Method:* (a) Direct standardization: reweight Sep population to Jan-Mar variant mix and to non-Z covariate distribution, compute expected breach 0.18*weight - compare to observed Sep non-Z 0.27 vs Z 0.45. (b) Fit propensity P(Partner Z | features), IPTW to balance rural distance/age (if logged) and compare adjusted breach rates. (c) Train simple model on non-Z Sep mature cases, test on Z Sep mature cases (transport AUROC/ECE gap).
*Expected under population covariate shift only:* After reweighting to baseline P(V), expected breach ~0.20-0.22, remaining excess ~0.05-0.09 is supplier concept effect; Z vs non-Z gap 0.45-0.27 shrinks to <0.08 after adjusting for V3/distance/age, and transport ECE similar. *Under Partner Z higher conditional risk (concept/bias):* Gap persists after conditioning, transport AUROC drops and ECE high for Z even within V3, suggesting missing risk factor or fairness issue.

**7. Feature missingness and lineage audits**
*Method:* Monthly missingness heatmap for queue, diagnosis count, travel distance, device age; lineage audit join between source workflow tables and feature store timestamps (check April renaming, caching). Correlate missingness with prediction error (|y-p|). `pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe` lineage before/after normalization.
*Expected under pipeline failure:* April missing queue 14% and cached diagnosis count stale correlate with prediction error (overprediction cluster where count wrong/unknown), missingness pattern aligns with migration cutover 1 Apr - 20 Apr. *Under drift:* Aug-Sep missingness 2-3% uniform, error correlates with V3/supplier delay not missingness. If May May missingness normalizes but error remains elevated for April cases only, confirms partial backfill.

## 4. Recalibration-versus-retraining decision

**Staged decision - proportionate, gated by pipeline and maturity:**

**Immediate containment (Days): Do not retrain.**
- **Block auto-retraining** until pipeline fix and vintage maturity gates pass.
- Roll back to *normalized* pre-prediction: enforce diagnosis label normalization + deduplication + full feature recompute for April-May in production scoring; if live predictions on April logic still active, apply temporary override (e.g., suppress high-risk flags where `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` present or queue missing).
- Business intervention for parts delay (alternate supplier, expediting) - model cannot fix physical bottleneck.
- **Reporting:** publish maturity-adjusted breach ( vintage 45d ) not raw October 0.16; annotate dashboards “Oct 55% mature - not comparable.” Alert dependency: predictive degradations do not fire PagerDuty if maturity <90%.

**Short-term recalibration (1-2 weeks, after pipeline recompute):**
- **Recalibration (Platt / isotonic or temperature scaling) useful but insufficient alone.**
  - *Where recalibration helps:* June covariate shift - overall calibration drift due to new V2 weight. Variant-stratified recalibration or simple intercept adjustment on Jun mature cohort (100% mature) would correct 0.05 overprediction without retraining features. Also may correct mild residual May miscalibration.
  - *Where recalibration fails:* Aug-Sep V3 concept drift: uniform recalibration would lift all predictions ~0.10, over-predicting V1/V2 (stable 0.16/0.11) and still under-discriminating V3 (AUROC loss not fixed by monotone transform). Supplier delay changed rank-order, not just intercept. **Decision:** Deploy *segment-specific calibration* (V1/V2/V3 separately) as band-aid while retraining model with new features. Do not globally recalibrate Oct on immature labels - would calibrate to downward-biased 0.16.

**Feature repair (2-4 weeks, required before full retraining):**
- Add at prediction time: expected parts lead time (supplier SLA tracker), current queue for parts, Partner Z flag, rural distance, device age, variant likelihood (from routing policy). These make post-diagnosis delay observable at prediction point and convert concept drift into learnable covariate.
- Fix lineage: backfill April `num_events_before_diagnosis` and queue features, re-validate variant assignment, document workflow version.
- Validate on fixed-vintage cohorts: use **Jan-Mar + May-recomputed + Jun-Jul mature** as near-stable reference with new features - test if V3 conditional risk now predictable (interaction captured).

**Full retraining (4-8 weeks, after maturity and feature readiness):**
- **Training data eligibility:**
  - **Safe:** Jan-Mar 2026 (clean, mature, representative pre-drift), May 2026 *after* full recompute (check ECE recovery), June 2026 (mature, useful to learn new V2 mix - include with weighting), July 2026 (98% mature, early signal of new V3 risk - include).
  - **Use with care:** Aug 2026 (93% mature - nearly safe, but regime already shifted; include as *new regime* sample, not as outlier to exclude, upweight for recency).
  - **Unsafe until labels mature & process stable:** Sep 2026 (82% mature + differential Partner Z censoring  exclude latest 18% unmature cases or use IPCW; do not train on raw Sep until >95% mature ~ mid-Nov), Oct 2026 (55% mature  **exclude entirely** from training until maturity, otherwise learns censored target, underestimates risk for V3/long paths).
  - **Exclude stale-feature April** unless fully recomputed; if recomputed passes test #2, it may be included but down-weighted as old regime. Never train on immature Oct or raw Sep.
- **Strategy:** Time-based split with embargo and variant stratification; use rolling vintage window (45-day label lag). Consider **segment-specific or hierarchical model** justified: V3 parts-dependent breach mechanism (duration-sensitive) vs V2 remote (fast, low breach) have different feature importance and calibration. Evidence: V3 breach 3-6x V2, drift localized to V3, Partner Z concentrated in V3. A single global model underfits without interaction. Justified to train variant-aware model (either multitask or separate calibrators, or include variant×delay interaction) and source-aware (Partner Z distance/age). Not justified to split solely by month - split by *mechanism*.

**Decision gates:** No retraining until (a) pipeline recompute passes (Unknown <1%, missing <2% across months), (b) label maturity >95% for training cohort, (c) new features extracted. If supplier issue is transient, retraining without supplier feature would overfit temporary spike; prefer augmented feature + recent window.

## 5. Monitoring architecture

**Architecture - 8 monitors with dependencies (gating to avoid false retraining):**

1. **Predictive-performance monitor:** Monthly AUROC, AUPRC, Brier, ECE on *fixed-vintage* mature cohort only (45-day lag), stratified by variant and Partner Z. Uses `pm4py.filter_time_range` case-start cohort. Alert: AUROC drop >0.05 or ECE >0.05 sustained 2 vintages.
2. **Calibration monitor (conditional):** Reliability diagrams, intercept/slope drift, stratified calibration (V3). Dependent on maturity gate (only mature).
3. **Label-maturity / censoring monitor:** Maturity %, median label delay, differential delay by variant/outcome (breach vs non-breach). Kaplan-Meier. **Master gate:** If maturity <90% or delay spike >10d, suppress downstream predictive/process auto-alerts, annotate dashboard “labels immature”.
4. **Data-quality / pipeline monitor:** Unknown activity share, missing feature rate, duplicate rate, schema version check (diagnosis label), lineage lag. Alert immediate on Unknown >1% or Missing queue >3%. Triggers pipeline fix workflow, not model retraining.
5. **Process-variant (control-flow) monitor:** Variant shares (V1/V2/V3/unmapped), DFG edge frequencies, `pm4py.get_variants` distribution drift (Jensen-Shannon), new activity appearance. Alert on shift >5pp or new node `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY`.
6. **Performance-DFG monitor:** Sojourn time per edge (especially `Order Part -> Part Received`, DiagnosisRemote Fix), queue length before diagnosis. Uses `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg`. Alert on median edge increase >30% vs baseline.
7. **Population / shift monitor:** Partner Z share, rural distance, device age distribution (PSI, KS test), P(Variant | Source). Covariate PSI >0.2 triggers population investigation.
8. **Intervention / deployment monitor:** Logs of workflow migration, mapping patches, routing policy, supplier SLAs, partner onboarding, API backlog deployments. Provides annotation and causal attribution; downstream monitors adjust baselines after approved interventions.

**Alert dependencies:** Data-quality alarm (4) or Label-maturity alarm (3)  *hold* predictive retraining trigger (1,2); instead fire pipeline/data engineering incident. Process-performance drift (6) + conditional breach rise (2 stratified)  fire concept drift investigation. Population shift (7) alone  trigger reweight analysis before labeling concept drift.

**Python sketch - auditable monthly summaries (not model fitting):**

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

# 1. Load & normalize renamed activity BEFORE any prefix / feature logic
df = pd.read_csv("field_service_events.csv", parse_dates=["timestamp", "case_start_time"])
# Normalize diagnosis label across migration
df["activity"] = df["activity"].replace({"Diagnostic Assessment Completed": "Initial Diagnosis Completed"})
# Duplicate handling: keep first of exact (case, activity, timestamp) - pipeline bug after 20 Apr duplicated
df = df.sort_values("timestamp").drop_duplicates(subset=["case_id","activity","timestamp"], keep="first")

# 2. PM4Py formatting with explicit case/time/activity
df = pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id="case_id", activity_key="activity", timestamp_key="timestamp")

# 3. Derive monthly cohorts with different interpretation
# event-calendar: prediction opportunities happening in month (for real-time monitoring)
# case-start: cases born in month (for mature outcome evaluation)
df["month"] = df["timestamp"].dt.to_period("M")
auditable = []
for month, g in df.groupby(df["timestamp"].dt.to_period("M")):
    # Alternative cohort slices
    cal_cohort = pm4py.filter_time_range(df, 
                  f"{month.start_time:%Y-%m-%d}", f"{month.end_time:%Y-%m-%d}")  # events in month
    start_cohort_ids = df[df["case_start_time"].dt.to_period("M")==month]["case_id"].unique()
    start_cohort = df[df["case_id"].isin(start_cohort_ids)]  # cases started in month

    # Process evidence per cohort
    variants = pm4py.get_variants(start_cohort)  # control-flow shares
    dfg = pm4py.discover_dfg(start_cohort)       # frequency DFG
    perf_dfg = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(start_cohort)  # median sojourn, to capture Order->Part delay
    # temporal features for lineage audit (do not fit model here)
    tmp = pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(start_cohort)

    # Data-quality per month
    unknown_share = (start_cohort["activity"]=="UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY").mean()
    missing_queue = start_cohort["queue_length_at_diagnosis"].isna().mean()

    auditable.append({
      "month": str(month),
      "cases_started": len(start_cohort_ids),
      "variant_shares": {k: len(v)/len(start_cohort_ids) for k,v in variants.items()},
      "dfg_Order_Part_median_h": perf_dfg[("Order Part","Part Received")]["median"] if ("Order Part","Part Received") in perf_dfg else None,
      "unknown_share": unknown_share,
      "missing_queue": missing_queue,
      "cohort_note": "event-calendar = monitoring at diagnosis time; case-start = vintage for outcome maturity"
    })

summary = pd.DataFrame(auditable)  # return auditable monthly summaries, no model training
```

*Code avoids inventing PM4Py drift detectors; uses documented `format_dataframe`, `filter_time_range`, `discover_dfg`, `discover_performance_dfg`, `get_variants`, `extract_temporal_features_dataframe`. Normalization precedes feature extraction, deduplication is explicit, and two cohorts are annotated with interpretation.*

## 6. Risks of acting on the wrong diagnosis

**1. Retraining on immature / censored labels (treating Oct 0.16 as truth)**
*Failure:* Training on 55% Oct cohort teaches model that V3 41% mix has low breach risk (censored breaches excluded), systematically under-predicting future mature breaches, worse for long rural Partner Z cases - dangerous under-allocation of technicians. AUROC on censored data is also biased (early resolvers are easier).

**2. Calibrating around a pipeline bug (recalibrating April/May without fixing features)**
