**Shared mechanisms**: Parallel execution of independent verification tasks (`treatment_parallelization`  `true_overlap`) reduces waiting time before assessment, improving cycle time when prerequisites allow. Both sites benefit from reduced queueing when capacity is available.

**Site-specific mechanisms**:
- **Site B**: `originals_required` and `role_specialization` block parallel document checks; `legal_capacity` introduces a mandatory sequential bottleneck.
- **Site A**: Cross-trained roles and digital-first workflow reduce constraints.

**Effect modifiers**:
- `originals_required`, `case_complexity`, `role_specialization`, `IT_case_lock`, `legal_capacity`, `assessment_capacity`.

**Why control-flow similarity isn’t enough**: Site A’s process tree allows parallelism by design, but Site B’s statutory and system constraints create irreducible sequential dependencies. A similar tree structure would violate legal requirements or system limitations, making transport invalid without modification.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Eligibility rules**:
- Only cases with digital documents (38% of volume) are eligible for parallel identity/document checks.
- Cases requiring originals must follow current sequential process.

**Concurrent tasks**:
- After `Register`, create `Identity Check` and `Document Check` tasks simultaneously **only if** digital documents are confirmed or originals are not legally required.
- `Originals Receipt` task remains mandatory for physical cases; `Document Check` cannot start until receipt is logged.

**Statutory prerequisites**:
- `Assessment` still requires both identity and document checks to complete.
- Legal review remains mandatory for 40% of cases; supplement loops unchanged.

**Queue and WIP controls**:
- Limit parallel verification tasks to 4 per reviewer (identity or document) to prevent overload.
- Introduce daily queue huddles for eligible cases only.

**Resource responsibilities**:
- Identity and document reviewers remain specialized; no cross-training required.
- Assessor and legal reviewer roles unchanged.

**Legal-review and supplement handling**:
- No change to legal-review process or supplement loops.
- Supplement cases return to document check queue; no parallelization allowed during rework.

**Logging requirements**:
- Timestamp every task start/completion.
- Log document type (digital/physical) at registration.
- Track overlap intervals and rework triggers.

**POWL-style partial order**:
```
Register  (Identity Check || Document Check) [if digital]
Register  Identity Check  Document Check [if physical]
Document Check  Assessment
Assessment  (Legal Review [if required]  (Approve | Reject))
Assessment  (Approve | Reject)
Legal Review  Request Supplement  Document Check [if supplement issued]
```

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

| design element | specification | causal purpose | operational safeguard | process-mining measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unit of assignment** | Individual case | Isolate treatment effect | Assign by case ID | Trace by case ID |
| **Randomization** | Stratified block randomization by document type (digital/physical) and complexity (high/low) | Balance confounders | Use permuted blocks; 1:1 allocation | Stratified balance checks |
| **Eligible strata** | Digital cases only; high/low complexity | Focus on feasible subgroup | Exclude physical cases | Case mix tracking |
| **Treatment versions** | Two: standard (sequential) vs. parallel (digital only) | Compare mechanisms | Mask assessors to treatment | Treatment flag in logs |
| **Primary outcome** | Median cycle time (hours) | Measure throughput improvement | Automated extraction from logs | Daily dashboard |
| **Secondary outcomes** | p90 cycle time, rework %, overlap %, legal-review queue length | Assess tail behavior and quality | Real-time monitoring | Weekly reports |
| **Interference controls** | Limit parallel cases to 20% of daily volume; stagger start times | Prevent queue saturation | Capacity buffer; daily huddles | Queue length logs |
| **Power** | Detect 5h median cycle time reduction with 80% power, =0.05, assuming =12h | Adequate signal detection | 300 cases per arm | Interim analysis at 150 cases/arm |
| **Duration** | 12 weeks (3 months) | Capture seasonal effects and stabilize | Fixed calendar period | Enrollment log |
| **Stop rules** | Futility: <2h median change after 6 weeks; harm: rework  >5% or appeal  >1% | Safety and efficiency | DSMB review every 4 weeks | Predefined thresholds |
| **Transportability analysis** | Compare effect by document type, complexity, and role utilization | Identify effect modifiers | Stratified treatment effect estimation | Subgroup analysis report |

**Separating mechanism vs. implementation failure**:
The pilot randomizes within eligible cases, ensuring that any observed improvement in the digital subgroup can be attributed to parallel execution rather than case-mix shifts or co-interventions. By comparing digital cases only, we isolate the mechanism of parallel verification. If no benefit is seen despite high overlap, the failure is likely structural (e.g., IT lock, role specialization). If benefits appear but are smaller than expected, implementation issues (e.g., reviewer overload, poor logging) are likely. The physical-case control group ensures that external trends (e.g., seasonal volume) do not confound results.

## 8. PM4py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import POWL, POWLNode, OrderingRelation
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import POWLLoop, POWLXor, POWLAnd
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils import petri_utils

# --- Site A Before ---
tree_a_before = ProcessTree()
register = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Register")])
identity = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Identity Check")])
document = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Document Check")])
assessment = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Assessment")])
decision = ProcessTree(operator="X", children=[
    ProcessTree(label="Approve"),
    ProcessTree(label="Reject")
])
notify = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Notify")])

register.children[0].children = [identity]
identity.children[0].children = [document]
document.children[0].children = [assessment]
assessment.children[0].children = [decision]
decision.children = [notify]

net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_before)
print("Site A Before is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_before))

# --- Site A After ---
tree_a_after = ProcessTree()
register_after = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Register")])
parallel = ProcessTree(operator="+", children=[
    ProcessTree(label="Identity Check"),
    ProcessTree(label="Document Check")
])
assessment_after = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Assessment")])
decision_after = ProcessTree(operator="X", children=[
    ProcessTree(label="Approve"),
    ProcessTree(label="Reject")
])
notify_after = ProcessTree(operator="", children=[ProcessTree(label="Notify")])

register_after.children[0].children = [parallel]
parallel.children[0].children = [assessment_after]
assessment_after.children[0].children = [decision_after]
decision_after.children = [notify_after]

net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_after)
print("Site A After is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_after))

# --- Site B Candidate (POWL) ---
powl_b = POWL()

# Nodes
register_b = POWLNode("Register")
identity_b = POWLNode("Identity Check")
originals_b = POWLNode("Originals Receipt")
document_b = POWLNode("Document Check")
assessment_b = POWLNode("Assessment")
legal_b = POWLNode("Legal Review")
approve_b = POWLNode("Approve")
reject_b = POWLNode("Reject")
request_supplement_b = POWLNode("Request Supplement")
notify_b = POWLNode("Notify")

# Structure
powl_b.add_node(register_b)
powl_b.add_node(identity_b)
powl_b.add_node(originals_b)
powl_b.add_node(document_b)
powl_b.add_node(assessment_b)
powl_b.add_node(legal_b)
powl_b.add_node(approve_b)
powl_b.add_node(reject_b)
powl_b.add_node(request_supplement_b)
powl_b.add_node(notify_b)

# Ordering: Register  (Identity Check || Originals Receipt)
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(register_b, identity_b))
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(register_b, originals_b))

# Identity Check  Document Check (only if originals received; but POWL can't encode data condition)
# We enforce: Document Check requires both Identity Check and Originals Receipt
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(identity_b, document_b))
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(originals_b, document_b))

# Document Check  Assessment
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(document_b, assessment_b))

# Assessment  (Legal Review or Approve/Reject)
assessment_to_decision = POWLXor(children=[legal_b, approve_b, reject_b])
powl_b.add_node(assessment_to_decision)
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(assessment_b, assessment_to_decision))

# Legal Review  Request Supplement  Document Check (loop)
supplement_loop = POWLLoop(children=[request_supplement_b])
powl_b.add_node(supplement_loop)
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(legal_b, supplement_loop))
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(supplement_loop, document_b))

# Decision to Notify
decision_to_notify = POWLXor(children=[approve_b, reject_b])
powl_b.add_node(decision_to_notify)
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(assessment_to_decision, decision_to_notify))
powl_b.add_ordering_relation(OrderingRelation(decision_to_notify, notify_b))

# Convert POWL to Petri net
net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl_b)
print("Site B POWL converted to Petri net. Is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_b))
```

## 9. Recommendation
**conditional go**

Transfer the **parallel execution of identity and document checks for digital-only cases**, but **not** the full-site parallelization as proposed. This respects legal constraints and avoids exacerbating bottlenecks.

**What to transfer**:
- The principle: execute independent verification tasks in parallel when legally permissible.
- Eligibility rules: restrict to digital claims.
- Queue and WIP controls: limit parallel tasks per reviewer.
- Logging improvements: timestamp accuracy and overlap tracking.

**What not to copy**:
- Full-site parallelization (violates legal requirements for physical documents).
- Cross-training of reviewers (not feasible under role specialization).
- Staffing increases (not transferable).

**Pilot subgroup**:
Start with digital claims only, stratified by complexity (high/low). This subgroup is most likely to benefit and avoids confounding from physical-document delays.

**Expected benefits**:
- Median cycle time reduction of 6–12 hours for digital cases.
- Lower p90 for digital claims.
- Potential rework reduction if parallel checks improve accuracy.

**Risks**:
- Legal-review bottleneck may worsen if assessment inflow increases.
- Rework loops could rise if coordination errors increase.
- IT system may not support true parallelism (case locks).

**Evidence triggers**:
- **Expand**: If digital cases show >5h median cycle time reduction and rework 15%, roll out to all digital claims and consider system upgrade.
- **Modify**: If legal-review queue grows >20% or p90 worsens, introduce pre-assessment triage or increase legal capacity.
- **Abandon**: If overlap <30% due to IT locks or rework  >5%, halt and redesign.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Assumption**: Digital claims can be verified in parallel with identity checks without violating legal requirements.
   - *Why needed*: Parallelization must be legally permissible.
   - *How to test*: Legal review confirms no statutory violation for digital claims.
   - *Consequence if false*: Intervention is illegal; must be halted.

2. **Assumption**: The legacy case system can log true task start/end times for digital cases.
   - *Why needed*: Overlap measurement and performance analysis depend on accurate timestamps.
   - *How to test*: Audit 50 digital cases for timestamp accuracy and overlap logging.
   - *Consequence if false*: Misleading performance signals; pilot inconclusive.

3. **Assumption**: Role specialization prevents reviewers from performing both identity and document checks.
   - *Why needed*: Parallelization requires independent reviewers.
   - *How to test*: Reviewer role definitions and system permissions.
   - *Consequence if false*: Cross-training could enable full parallelization, but not feasible under current policy.

4. **Assumption**: Assessment capacity is sufficient to handle increased inflow from parallelization.
   - *Why needed*: Parallelism increases inflow to assessment.
   - *How to test*: Monitor assessor queue length and service time during pilot.
   - *Consequence if false*: Assessment bottleneck worsens cycle time.

5. **Assumption**: Legal-review capacity is adequate for the increased inflow.
   - *Why needed*: 40% of cases require legal review.
   - *How to test*: Track legal-review queue length and supplement rate.
   - *Consequence if false*: Legal bottleneck negates benefits.

6. **Assumption**: Rework rates will not increase significantly due to parallel execution.
   - *Why needed*: Parallelism may increase coordination errors.
   - *How to test*: Compare rework % in digital vs. physical cases during pilot.
   - *Consequence if false*: Quality degradation; appeals may rise.

7. **Assumption**: Physical-document receipt delays are irreducible and outside intervention scope.
   - *Why needed*: Parallelization cannot eliminate external delays.
   - *How to test*: Measure originals receipt delay distribution.
   - *Consequence if false*: Underestimates baseline cycle time; overestimates potential gain.

8. **Assumption**: Daily queue huddles improve queue discipline and can be implemented for eligible cases.
   - *Why needed*: Management practice may transfer.
   - *How to test*: Pilot huddles for digital cases only; measure queue length reduction.
   - *Consequence if false*: Missed opportunity to reduce waiting time.
