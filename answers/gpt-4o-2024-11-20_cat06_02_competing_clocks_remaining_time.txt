## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Scenario 1: Smooth path
- **Chemistry (wait + service):** B-417 starts by 16:00, finishes Friday by 17:30. No rerun is needed.
- **Microbiology (incubation + readout):** Incubation ends Sunday 20:00–22:00, readout starts Monday ~08:30.
- **Parallel join:** QA receives both results by Monday ~08:30–09:00.
- **QA (wait + service):** 8–10 batch queue forms over the weekend; B-417 enters queue Monday 10:00, emerges ~14:00. No queries or investigations.
- **Release:** Electronic release ~Monday 14:15–14:45.

---

### Scenario 2: Chemistry rerun
- **Chemistry (wait + service):** Service starts Friday but rerun delays remaining work past lab closing. Rerun resumes Monday 08:00, finishes 11:00–12:00.
- **Microbiology (incubation + readout):** As in smooth path.
- **Parallel join:** Chemistry result delays QA start until Monday ~12:00.
- **QA (wait + service):** QA congestion pushes completion to Monday ~16:00–18:00.
- **Release:** Electronic release Monday or early Tuesday.

---

### Scenario 3: Delayed microbiology readout or contamination confirmation
- **Chemistry (wait + service):** As in smooth path; result ready Friday.
- **Microbiology (incubation + readout):** If no weekend authorization, readout begins Monday morning. Contamination doubles incubation time, delaying final result to Tuesday evening.
- **Parallel join:** QA delayed until Microbiology clears Tuesday evening or early Wednesday.
- **QA (wait + service):** Queue congestion pushes start to Wednesday morning or afternoon.
- **Release:** Late Wednesday to early Friday.

---

### Scenario 4: QA query or investigation tail
- **Chemistry and Microbiology:** Any branch delays compound QA entry timing.
- **QA (wait + service):** Query/investigation pauses QA for 4–12 working hours (query) or 8–20 hours/3–7 days (investigation). Scenario cascades addifies latency.
- **Release:** Ranges Wednesday late evening to Friday, or next week for longest investigation paths.
