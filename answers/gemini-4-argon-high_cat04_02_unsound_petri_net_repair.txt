# 1. Executive Verdict

The given accepting Petri net **is a valid workflow net (WF-net)** structurally: it has a unique source place (`source` with empty preset), a unique sink place (`sink` with empty postset), and every place and transition lies on a directed path from `source` to `sink`.

However, the net is **unsound** and fails to realize the business intent. Its principal defect is an **unsynchronized join on the waiver path**: `t_split` concurrently populates `p_analysis` and `p_review`, whereas `t_join_waived` consumes only from `p_waived` (`•t_join_waived = {p_waived}`) and omits `p_analysis_done`.

This defect causes two business violations:
1. **Premature authorization**: a waived case can execute `t_authorize` *before* mandatory analysis (`t_analyze`) completes.
2. **Improper completion**: the parallel analysis token remains stranded in `p_analysis_done` (`[p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1]`), so the clean final marking `fm = [sink: 1]` is never reached.

# 3. Marking-Based Counterexample

Because `t_join_waived` does not depend on `p_analysis_done`, the waiver route exhibits two distinct behavioral symptoms depending on interleaving:
1. **Counterexample 1 (`t_authorize` before `t_analyze`)**: Authorization occurs before mandatory analysis has even executed, and completion is improper.
2. **Counterexample 2 (`t_authorize` after `t_analyze`)**: Even if analysis executes first, completion is still improper because the token in `p_analysis_done` cannot be consumed.

### Counterexample 1: Premature Authorization Before Analysis (`t_authorize` before `t_analyze`)

Legal firing sequence using transition names:
$$\sigma_1 = \langle \texttt{t\_register},\; \texttt{t\_split},\; \texttt{t\_waive\_review},\; \texttt{t\_join\_waived},\; \texttt{t\_authorize},\; \texttt{t\_analyze} \rangle$$

| Step | Fired transition | Resulting nonzero marking | Enabled transitions | Observation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | — *(initial)* | `[source: 1]` | `{t_register}` | Exact initial marking `im`. |
| 1 | `t_register` | `[p_ready: 1]` | `{t_split}` | Case registered (`"Register Case"`). |
| 2 | `t_split` | `[p_analysis: 1, p_review: 1]` | `{t_analyze, t_standard_review, t_waive_review}` | Silent AND-split creates one token in the analysis branch (`p_analysis`) and one token in the review branch (`p_review`). |
| 3 | `t_waive_review` | `[p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1]` | `{t_analyze, t_join_waived}` | Waiver recorded (`"Record Review Waiver"`). Because `•t_join_waived = {p_waived}`, `t_join_waived` is immediately enabled while `p_analysis` still holds its unanalyzed token. |
| 4 | `t_join_waived` | `[p_analysis: 1, p_decision: 1]` | `{t_analyze, t_authorize}` | Silent transition moves the waiver token into `p_decision` without waiting for `p_analysis_done`. |
| 5 | `t_authorize` | `[p_analysis: 1, sink: 1]` | `{t_analyze}` | **Premature authorization and improper completion**: `"Authorize Case"` fires *before* `t_analyze`. A token now exists in `sink` ($M(\texttt{sink}) = 1$), but $M \neq fm$ because `p_analysis` still contains 1 token ($M > fm$). |
| 6 | `t_analyze` | `[p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1]` | `∅` | Analysis completes *after* authorization. The net reaches a deadlocked terminal marking `[p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1] != fm`. |

### Counterexample 2: Analysis Completed First, Yet Final Marking `fm` Remains Unreachable (`t_authorize` after `t_analyze`)

Legal firing sequence using transition names:
$$\sigma_2 = \langle \texttt{t\_register},\; \texttt{t\_split},\; \texttt{t\_analyze},\; \texttt{t\_waive\_review},\; \texttt{t\_join\_waived},\; \texttt{t\_authorize} \rangle$$

| Step | Fired transition | Resulting nonzero marking | Enabled transitions | Observation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | — *(initial)* | `[source: 1]` | `{t_register}` | Exact initial marking `im`. |
| 1 | `t_register` | `[p_ready: 1]` | `{t_split}` | Case registered. |
| 2 | `t_split` | `[p_analysis: 1, p_review: 1]` | `{t_analyze, t_standard_review, t_waive_review}` | Parallel tokens created in `p_analysis` and `p_review`. |
| 3 | `t_analyze` | `[p_analysis_done: 1, p_review: 1]` | `{t_standard_review, t_waive_review}` | Mandatory analysis completes first; token waits in `p_analysis_done`. |
| 4 | `t_waive_review` | `[p_analysis_done: 1, p_waived: 1]` | `{t_join_waived}` | Waiver recorded. `t_join_normal` is not enabled because `M(p_review_done) = 0`. |
| 5 | `t_join_waived` | `[p_analysis_done: 1, p_decision: 1]` | `{t_authorize}` | `t_join_waived` consumes only `p_waived`, leaving the token in `p_analysis_done` untouched. |
| 6 | `t_authorize` | `[p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1]` | `∅` | **Token in `sink` vs. exact `fm`**: A token exists in `sink` ($M(\texttt{sink}) = 1$), but the exact final marking `fm = [sink: 1]` has **not** been reached because $M(\texttt{p\_analysis\_done}) = 1$. |

### Proof That the Leftover Token in `p_analysis_done` Cannot Be Consumed

At the resulting marking $M_{\text{dead}} = [\texttt{p\_analysis\_done}: 1, \texttt{sink}: 1]$:
1. The postset of `sink` is $\texttt{sink}\bullet = \emptyset$, so the token in `sink` cannot enable any transition.
2. The postset of `p_analysis_done` is $\texttt{p\_analysis\_done}\bullet = \{\texttt{t\_join\_normal}\}$, so `t_join_normal` is the only transition in the net capable of consuming a token from `p_analysis_done`.
3. The preset of `t_join_normal` is $\bullet\texttt{t\_join\_normal} = \{\texttt{p\_analysis\_done}, \texttt{p\_review\_done}\}$. By the Petri net firing rule, `t_join_normal` is enabled at $M$ if and only if $M(\texttt{p\_analysis\_done}) \ge 1$ and $M(\texttt{p\_review\_done}) \ge 1$.
4. The only transition with `p_review_done` in its postset is `t_standard_review` ($\bullet\texttt{p\_review\_done} = \{\texttt{t\_standard\_review}\}$), whose preset is $\bullet\texttt{t\_standard\_review} = \{\texttt{p\_review}\}$.
5. Because `M_dead` has 0 tokens in `source`, `p_ready`, `p_review`, and `p_review_done`, and no transition is enabled at `M_dead`, `M(p_review_done)` remains permanently $0$. Therefore, `t_join_normal` can never fire, the token in `p_analysis_done` can never be consumed, and `fm = [sink: 1]` is unreachable from every marking following `t_waive_review` (violating both the **option to complete** and **proper completion** conditions of soundness).

# 4. Minimal-Repair Alternatives

### Explicit Edit-Cost Model

Let a Petri net be $N = (P, T, F)$ with places $P$, transitions $T$, and directed arcs $F \subseteq (P \times T) \cup (T \times P)$. We define the structural edit cost of transforming $N$ into $N' = (P', T', F')$ as the unweighted count of elementary graph edits (unit cost $= 1$ per added or removed arc, place, or transition):
$$\text{Cost}(N \to N') = \Delta A + \Delta P + \Delta T = |F \triangle F'| + |P \triangle P'| + |T \triangle T'|$$
where $\triangle$ denotes symmetric difference, $\Delta A = |F' \setminus F| + |F \setminus F'|$, $\Delta P = |P' \setminus P| + |P \setminus P'|$, and $\Delta T = |T' \setminus T| + |T \setminus T'|$.

### Comparison of Distinct Minimal Repairs

| Repair | Exact edits | Edit cost | Preserved intent | Changed behavior | New risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Repair 1: Two-Input Waiver Join** *(Intent-preserving, global minimum cost)* | **Add 1 arc**: `(p_analysis_done, t_join_waived)` | **1** ($\Delta A=1, \Delta P=0, \Delta T=0$) | Retains mandatory analysis in parallel with both standard review and waiver; keeps `p_waived` and `t_join_waived` distinct from `p_review_done` and `t_join_normal`. | `t_join_waived` now has preset `{p_analysis_done, p_waived}`. It blocks until both `t_analyze` and `t_waive_review` have fired, then consumes both tokens to produce 1 token in `p_decision`. | Makes the net **non-free-choice**: `p_analysis_done` has two silent outgoing transitions (`t_join_normal`, `t_join_waived`) with different presets. Safe here because `p_review_done` and `p_waived` are mutually exclusive, but rejected by tools requiring free-choice nets. |
| **Repair 2: Silent Merge into `p_review_done`** *(Intent-preserving, free-choice)* | **Remove 1 arc**: `(t_join_waived, p_decision)`<br>**Add 1 arc**: `(t_join_waived, p_review_done)`<br>*(Note: a canonical pruned variant **2b** redirects `(t_waive_review, p_review_done)` directly and deletes `p_waived`, `t_join_waived`, and their 3 incident arcs at $\text{Cost}=6$: $\Delta A=4, \Delta P=1, \Delta T=1$.)* | **2** ($\Delta A=2, \Delta P=0, \Delta T=0$) | Retains mandatory analysis on both paths and preserves the exact same visible trace language as Repair 1. | `t_join_waived` forwards the token from `p_waived` into `p_review_done`; a single AND-join (`t_join_normal`) then synchronizes `p_analysis_done` and `p_review_done`. The net is strictly **free-choice**. | Chained silent transitions (`t_join_waived` followed by `t_join_normal`) on the waiver route; semantic overloading of `p_review_done` to represent both standard completion and waived completion after `t_join_waived` fires. |
| **Repair 3: Pre-Split Waiver Fast-Track** *(Explicit policy change: waiver skips analysis)* | **Remove 1 arc**: `(p_review, t_waive_review)`<br>**Add 1 arc**: `(p_ready, t_waive_review)` | **2** ($\Delta A=2, \Delta P=0, \Delta T=0$) | Preserves parallel mandatory analysis and standard review on the non-waived path, and achieves a sound, free-choice WF-net. | **Policy change**: The decision to waive occurs at `p_ready` *before* `t_split`. If `t_waive_review` fires, `t_split` is bypassed and `t_analyze` is never executed ($\langle \text{Register Case}, \text{Record Review Waiver}, \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$). | Violates the original mandate that analysis is always required; only admissible if business stakeholders explicitly revise policy so a waiver exempts the case from analysis. |

### Justification of Minimality and Tie Analysis

1. **Why Repair 1 ($\text{Cost} = 1$) is strictly minimal across all valid repairs**:
   - A repair of $\text{Cost} = 0$ leaves the net unchanged and unsound, so $\text{Cost} \ge 1$ is a lower bound.
   - Any single-place or single-transition edit ($\Delta P = 1$ or $\Delta T = 1$ with $\Delta A = 0$) either creates an isolated node (violating WF-net connectivity) or leaves broken arc endpoints.
   - Removing any single existing arc ($\Delta A = 1$ removal) either disconnects a place/transition from source-to-sink paths or fails to synchronize `p_analysis_done`.
   - Adding the single arc `(p_analysis_done, t_join_waived)` achieves $\text{Cost} = 1$, preserves all original places, transitions, and labels, and restores full soundness while enforcing mandatory analysis on the waiver path.
   - *(Note on an invalid $\text{Cost} = 1$ candidate)*: Adding `(p_analysis, t_join_waived)` to let `t_join_waived` consume the unanalyzed token from `p_analysis` also has $\text{Cost} = 1$, but it is **unsound**: at `[p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1]`, `t_analyze` is concurrently enabled, and firing `t_analyze` reaches `[p_analysis_done: 1, p_waived: 1]`, where neither `t_join_waived` nor `t_join_normal` is enabled (total deadlock).
2. **Why Repair 2 and Repair 3 tie at $\text{Cost} = 2$**:
   - Under the free-choice constraint (where any two transitions sharing an input place must have identical presets), `t_join_normal` and `t_join_waived` cannot both consume from `p_analysis_done` while having distinct second inputs (`p_review_done` vs. `p_waived`).
   - Achieving a free-choice sound net without adding/removing nodes requires rewiring one endpoint of an existing arc (1 arc removal + 1 arc addition $\implies \text{Cost} = 2$):
     - **Repair 2** moves the target of `(t_join_waived, p_decision)` to `p_review_done` ($\text{Cost} = 2$), merging the XOR review sub-branches *before* the single AND-join `t_join_normal` and preserving mandatory analysis.
     - **Repair 3** moves the source of `(p_review, t_waive_review)` to `p_ready` ($\text{Cost} = 2$), moving the XOR waiver branch *before* the AND-split `t_split` so that only one token exists along the waiver route. Thus Repair 2 and Repair 3 tie at $\text{Cost} = 2$ as minimal single-arc-reroute repairs for free-choice intent preservation and policy-changing fast-track waiver, respectively.

# 6. Diagnostic and Reachability Code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking


def format_marking(marking):
    nonzero = {p.name: cnt for p, cnt in marking.items() if cnt > 0}
    return "{" + ", ".join(f"{k}: {v}" for k, v in sorted(nonzero.items())) + "}"


def format_enabled(net_obj, marking):
    enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net_obj, marking)
    return sorted(t.name for t in enabled)


def fire_named_transition(net_obj, marking, transition_name):
    """Fire a transition by name only after verifying enabledness via PM4Py."""
    by_name = {t.name: t for t in net_obj.transitions}
    if transition_name not in by_name:
        raise KeyError(f"Transition '{transition_name}' does not exist in net '{net_obj.name}'.")

    target_t = by_name[transition_name]
    enabled_set = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net_obj, marking)
    if target_t not in enabled_set:
        enabled_names = sorted(t.name for t in enabled_set)
        raise ValueError(
            f"Transition '{transition_name}' is NOT enabled at marking {format_marking(marking)}. "
            f"Currently enabled: {enabled_names}"
        )

    next_marking = Marking()
    for place, count in marking.items():
        if count > 0:
            next_marking[place] = count

    for in_arc in target_t.in_arcs:
        next_marking[in_arc.source] -= in_arc.weight
        if next_marking[in_arc.source] <= 0:
            del next_marking[in_arc.source]

    for out_arc in target_t.out_arcs:
        next_marking[out_arc.target] = next_marking.get(out_arc.target, 0) + out_arc.weight

    return next_marking


def replay_sequence(net_obj, initial_marking, final_marking, sequence, title):
    print(f"\n=== {title} ({net_obj.name}) ===")
    current = Marking(initial_marking)
    print(
        f"Step 0 [init]: marking={format_marking(current)}, "
        f"enabled={format_enabled(net_obj, current)}"
    )
    for step_idx, t_name in enumerate(sequence, start=1):
        try:
            current = fire_named_transition(net_obj, current, t_name)
            print(
                f"Step {step_idx} [fired {t_name}]: marking={format_marking(current)}, "
                f"enabled={format_enabled(net_obj, current)}, "
                f"equals_fm={current == final_marking}"
            )
        except ValueError as err:
            print(f"Step {step_idx} [BLOCKED {t_name}]: {err}")
            break


# 1. Structural WF-net and behavioral soundness checks

print(
```

### Expected Qualitative (and Derived Reachability) Differences

1. **`pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(...)`**:
   - Returns `True` for **both** `net` and `repaired_net`. Both nets have a unique source place, a unique sink place, and every node on a directed path from `source` to `sink`.
2. **`pm4py.check_soundness(..., print_diagnostics=True)`**:
   - **Original net**: Reports **unsound** (`False` boolean indicator). The diagnostics flag that proper completion and option to complete fail because markings exist where `sink` is marked alongside `p_analysis` or `p_analysis_done`, and `[p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1]` is a deadlocked marking from which `fm` cannot be reached.
   - **Repaired net**: Reports **sound** (`True` boolean indicator), with no dead transitions, bounded (1-safe) places, and `repaired_fm = [sink: 1]` reachable from every reachable marking.
3. **`pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(...)` along the counterexample**:
   - After firing `t_register`, `t_split`, and `t_waive_review` (reaching `[p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1]`), the **original net** returns `{t_analyze, t_join_waived}`, allowing `t_join_waived` and `t_authorize` to fire before `t_analyze`.
   - At the exact same marking `[p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1]`, the **repaired net** returns only `{t_analyze}`; `fire_named_transition` blocks `t_join_waived` with a `ValueError` until `t_analyze` deposits a token in `p_analysis_done`.
4. **`pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(...)`**:
   - **Original net** (derived in Section 3): Has **14 reachable states** and **17 transition edges**, with **two terminal (dead) states** (`[sink: 1]` on the standard path and `[p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1]` on the waiver path) because `p_waived -> p_decision -> sink` can progress in both `p_analysis` and `p_analysis_done` states ($2 + 2 \times 5 + 2 = 14$ states).
   - **Repaired net**: Eliminates the 4 premature/improper waiver states (`[p_analysis: 1, p_decision: 1]`, `[p_analysis: 1, sink: 1]`, `[p_analysis_done: 1, p_decision: 1]`, and `[p_analysis_done: 1, sink: 1]`), contracting the reachability graph to **10 reachable states**, **12 transition edges**, and a **single terminal state** (`[sink: 1] == repaired_fm`).

# 7. Repair Trade-Offs and Residual Risks

1. **Distinguishability of Waiver vs. Standard Review**:
   - **Event-log / trace level**: In Repairs 1, 2, and 3, `t_standard_review` (`"Perform Standard Review"`) and `t_waive_review` (`"Record Review Waiver"`) retain distinct visible labels, so standard reviews and waivers remain fully distinguishable in event logs.
   - **State / marking level**: **Repair 1** also preserves place-level distinction right up to synchronization (`p_review_done` vs. `p_waived`, consumed by `t_join_normal` vs. `t_join_waived`). By contrast, **Repair 2** routes `p_waived` via `t_join_waived` into `p_review_done` (and **Repair 2b** eliminates `p_waived` altogether), collapsing the post-decision state so a token in `p_review_done` no longer distinguishes whether a standard review or a waiver occurred without inspecting firing history.
2. **Duplicate Behavior or Confusion via Silent Transitions**:
   - Neither Repair 1 nor Repair 2 introduces duplicate visible transitions, and neither alters the visible language of valid traces.
   - However, **Repair 1** introduces a **non-free-choice share** at `p_analysis_done`: its two outgoing silent transitions `t_join_normal` (`•t_join_normal = {p_analysis_done, p_review_done}`) and `t_join_waived` (`•t_join_waived = {p_analysis_done, p_waived}`) share `p_analysis_done` without having identical presets. In general Petri nets, asymmetric presets can cause *confusion* (conflict coupled with concurrency). Here, no runtime confusion or nondeterministic deadlock can arise because `p_review_done` and `p_waived` are mutually exclusive descendants of the single token in `p_review`. Still, process mining algorithms or workflow engines that require free-choice nets (e.g., certain block-structured BPMN converters or alpha-family assumptions) may flag this construct.
   - **Repair 2** avoids non-free-choice conflict by keeping `t_join_normal` as the sole consumer of `p_analysis_done`, at the cost of firing two sequential silent transitions (`t_join_waived` then `t_join_normal`) on the waiver branch.
3. **Why Soundness Alone Does Not Prove Alignment with Business Rules**:
   - Classical WF-net soundness verifies only control-flow consistency (option to complete, proper completion at `fm`, and absence of dead transitions). It is agnostic to *domain semantics* and *task ordering policies*.
   - **Repair 3** is a concrete proof: moving `t_waive_review` to `p_ready` produces a net that passes `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net` and `pm4py.check_soundness` with `True`, yet it allows traces without `"Perform Analysis"`, violating the business rule that analysis is always mandatory. Similarly, a net that placed analysis and review strictly in sequence rather than in parallel, or swapped transition labels, would still be 100% sound while violating business requirements.
4. **Regression Test Suite (Positive and Negative Traces)**:
   - **Positive traces (must replay cleanly to exact marking `fm = [sink: 1]` with zero missing/remaining tokens)**:
     - $t^+_1 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Perform Analysis},\; \text{Perform Standard Review},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$
     - $t^+_2 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Perform Standard Review},\; \text{Perform Analysis},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$
     - $t^+_3 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Perform Analysis},\; \text{Record Review Waiver},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$
     - $t^+_4 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Record Review Waiver},\; \text{Perform Analysis},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$
   - **Negative traces (must be rejected / fail enabledness or proper completion)**:
     - $t^-_1 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Record Review Waiver},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$ *(waiver bypassing mandatory analysis — differentiates Repair 1/2 from Repair 3)*
     - $t^-_2 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Record Review Waiver},\; \text{Authorize Case},\; \text{Perform Analysis} \rangle$ *(premature authorization before analysis — exposes the original bug)*
     - $t^-_3 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Perform Standard Review},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$ *(standard review bypassing mandatory analysis)*
     - $t^-_4 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Perform Analysis},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$ *(analysis without any review or waiver outcome)*
     - $t^-_5 = \langle \text{Register Case},\; \text{Perform Analysis},\; \text{Perform Standard Review},\; \text{Record Review Waiver},\; \text{Authorize Case} \rangle$ *(both mutually exclusive review outcomes executed in one case)*
5. **Detecting an Accidental Bypass of Mandatory Analysis**:
   - **Model-level verification**:
     1. *Reachability / language check*: Enumerate all accepting paths from `im` to `fm` in `pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(...)` and assert that every path contains at least one edge labeled `"Perform Analysis"` and that `"Perform Analysis"` precedes `"Authorize Case"` on every path.
     2. *Place-invariant (S-invariant) check*: Verify the conservation law
        $$M(\texttt{source}) + M(\texttt{p\_ready}) + M(\texttt{p\_analysis}) + M(\texttt{p\_analysis\_done}) + M(\texttt{p\_decision}) + M(\texttt{sink}) = 1$$
        which holds in Repair 1 and Repair 2 only because every transition moving a token into `p_decision` consumes one token from `p_analysis_done`.
   - **Runtime / event-log verification**:
     Run LTL/Declare conformance rules requiring $\text{Existence}_1(\text{Perform Analysis})$ and $\text{Precedence}(\text{Perform Analysis}, \text{Authorize Case})$ across all completed cases, combined with token-based replay or alignments to flag any missing tokens on `p_analysis_done`.

# 8. Final Recommendation

- **Deploy: Repair 1 (Add synchronization arc `(p_analysis_done, t_join_waived)`, $\text{Cost} = 1$)**.
- **Fallback: Repair 2 (Reroute `(t_join_waived, p_decision)` to `(t_join_waived, p_review_done)`, $\text{Cost} = 2$ — or its pruned single-join variant Repair 2b if refactoring is permitted)**.

**Rationale**:
Repair 1 is the unique global-minimum edit ($\text{Cost} = 1$) that restores WF-net soundness while faithfully preserving every business requirement: mandatory analysis executes in parallel with either review outcome, authorization is strictly blocked until both branches finish, and `p_waived` remains semantically separate from `p_review_done` right up to synchronization. It directly fixes the modeler's omission on line 70 (where `t_join_waived` was created as a waiver synchronization transition but given only one of its two required input arcs) without altering any existing place, transition, or label.

If the target execution engine, BPMN translator, or verification pipeline enforces a strict **free-choice** structural constraint—rejecting nets where `p_analysis_done` feeds two transitions (`t_join_normal` and `t_join_waived`) with distinct presets—deploy **Repair 2** as the fallback. At $\text{Cost} = 2$, Repair 2 merges the waiver branch into `p_review_done` ahead of the single two-input AND-join `t_join_normal`, guaranteeing free-choice structure and the exact same four valid visible traces. **Repair 3** should not be deployed unless business governance formally changes the policy to exempt waived cases from analysis.
