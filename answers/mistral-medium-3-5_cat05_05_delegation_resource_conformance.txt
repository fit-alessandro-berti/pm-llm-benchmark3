## 2. Rule Interpretation

### Formalized Rules:
1. **RR1 (Drafter/Approver Separation)**
   - **Violation**: If `Draft_Change_Request` and `Approve_Change` for the same case are performed by the **same human** (after alias normalization).
   - **Exception**: None.

2. **RR2 (Implementer/Verifier Separation)**
   - **Violation**: If `Implement_Change` and `Verify_Implementation` for the same case are performed by the **same human**.

3. **RR3 (High-Risk Approval Authority)**
   - **Valid Approver**: QM (Asha) **or** a delegate with:
     - Delegation interval **covering the event timestamp**.
     - Scope **covering the change type** (e.g., production equipment).
     - Principal (Asha) **absent or formally unavailable** (e.g., leave, VPN session not counted as availability).
     - **No prohibited subdelegation** (e.g., D-IC-04 invalidates Farid  Clara).
   - **Database Timestamps**: A delegation record created **after** the effective interval (e.g., D-QM-01’s DB record on 2026-08-12) is **not valid** for events before that time (e.g., CHG-501 on 2026-08-11). The **signed PDF timestamp (2026-08-09)** is authoritative.

4. **RR4 (Emergency Substitution)**
   - **Valid Emergency Approval**: IC (Diego) or **valid IC delegate** (Farid via D-IC-03) may approve `HIGH`-risk `emergency=true` changes.
   - **Mandatory Review**: `Retrospective_QM_Review` must be performed by QM or **valid QM delegate** within **2 business days** (weekends excluded; 2026-08-15–16 are weekends).
   - **Credibility Check**: `emergency` flag and safety rationale must be **documented and plausible**.

5. **RR5 (Identity Evidence)**
   - Service accounts (`svc_qms`) **do not count as humans** unless session evidence (badge/FIDO) attributes to a specific user.
   - Aliases must be **normalized** before SoD checks (e.g., `u_bk` = `b.koch` = Ben Koch).

6. **RR6 (Suspicion vs. Proof)**
   - **Indicators** (not proof):
     - Repeated pairs (Clara  Ben: 18/23 HIGH-risk cases).
     - Reciprocal approvals (Ben drafts, Clara approves LOW-risk).
     - Shared workstations (WS-DM-04, WS-IV-02).
     - Absence conflicts (Ben’s account used during sick leave).
   - **Proof Requires**: Direct evidence (e.g., CCTV, multi-factor auth logs, or confession).

**Business Days for RR4**:
- Count **weekdays only** (Mon–Fri). For CHG-505 (approved 2026-08-11 13:00), the deadline is **2026-08-13 23:59** (2 business days: 11th  12th  13th). CHG-505’s review on 2026-08-14 **fails** this deadline.
- For CHG-510 (approved 2026-08-11 14:20), the deadline is **2026-08-13 23:59**; review on 2026-08-12 **passes**.

**Principal on Leave**:
- If the principal (Asha) is on leave but **actively uses credentials** (e.g., VPN on 2026-08-13), they are **not considered absent** for delegation purposes. Thus, D-QM-01 is **invalid** for CHG-504 (approved by Asha herself).

**Database vs. Signed Evidence**:
- Delegation validity is determined by the **earliest authoritative timestamp** (e.g., D-QM-01’s signed PDF on 2026-08-09). Later DB entries (e.g., 2026-08-12) **do not retroactively validate** earlier events.
