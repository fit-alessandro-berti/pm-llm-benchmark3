**Disputed propositions — turn on authority, scope, identity, or logging assumptions:**

5. *Desirability vs normativity:* Do Request_More_Info cycles (SUP-707), provisional activation with full retrospective compliance, or absence of transactions before sanctions clearance (SUP-708) make trace deviation non-material?

**Burden-of-proof approach:**

- *To declare NONCOMPLIANT:* Require (a) applicable norm with authority at case time, (b) trace deviation from that norm on reliable evidence, (c) no valid exception with authority covering the deviation. Formal authority controls over informal belief or outcome. Missing evidence alone does not prove compliance; it yields DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE unless corroborated.
- *To accept JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION or MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT:* Require (a) written authority with effective window/scope or proof of model/scope/data defect independent of outcome, (b) substantive eligibility conditions met on contemporaneous evidence (risk, geography, group, sanctions-before-activation, amount/time limits), (c) compensating controls evidenced (monitoring, retrospective review). Desirability, low loss, or imminent approval alone do not meet burden. Retrospective label mapping or mutable CRM link does not retroactively create contemporaneous proof.
- Default: ambiguity about scope/identity/logging is resolved against declaring substantive misconduct but also against declaring compliance — recorded as MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT or DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE with explicit missing evidence and reversal condition.

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

Propose layered governance — no single POWL can carry control-flow + data + resources + authority:

**Layer 1 — Revised control-flow (POWL):** Covers ordering only. Include: Register -> repeatable Request_More_Info loop -> XOR{ Standard manual route with concurrent Sanctions||Quality before Manual->Activate; Low-risk auto route with Sanctions-before-Auto->Activate and Quality allowed before or after (within 2 business days); Emergency provisional route with Sanctions-before-Activate then Quality+Manual after; Abbreviated reject route (Sanctions->Reject) } -> Notify -> Close. POWL expresses concurrency (StrictPartialOrder), exclusive choice (XOR), and repetition (LOOP). It must *over-approximate* (allow more orderings than policy) and leave precise guards to lower layers.

**Layer 2 — Data-aware eligibility/amount/time rules (DECLARE / log-skeleton + pandas checks, NOT POWL):**
- DECLARE constraints: `Precedence(Sanctions, Activate)` absolute; `Precedence(Quality, Activate)` only for standard route; `Response(Activate, Quality within 2 business days)` for auto/emergency; `Response(Activate, Manual within 2 business days)` for emergency; `NotCoExistence(Manual, Auto)` per case; `ExactlyOnce(Register deduplicated, Notify, Close)`.
- pandas/data checks: risk==LOW + country==DE + group_approved==True + date in pilot/policy window + master_data_validated==True for auto route; spend <=20k until retrospective approval + daily_monitoring==True + critical_relief==True + date in emergency window for emergency route; amount thresholds, retrospective approval timeliness, Quality timeliness in business days.
- Rationale: POWL cannot enforce attributes, caps, or windows; encoding them as branches without guards invites misuse (703, 714). Keep POWL permissive, enforce guards in data layer with rule/version IDs.

**Layer 3 — Resource and conflict-of-interest rules (organizational controls + data checks, NOT POWL):**
- SoD: requester != manual approver, requester != quality reviewer where applicable, enforced via HR identity resolution (alias registry), not activity order. Four-eyes on activation above threshold.
- Disclosure/recusal: reviewer/approver relationship (family, reporting line, financial interest) requires pre-decision disclosure + recusal/delegation record. SUP-715 breach is disclosure failure, not control-flow failure.
- Delegation evidence: any alias/delegate must link to immutable delegation record. POWL cannot express identity; do not try.

**Layer 4 — Policy-overlay governance for pilots and emergencies:**
- Every overlay needs: written authority ID, version, effective/record timestamps (immutable), scope (risk/geography/group/amount), required compensating controls, monitoring, expiry + handover/extension procedure (no “factual” extension as in 705). Cases must snapshot eligibility attributes + rule_version at execution. PD-3 failure was missing incorporation + missing snapshots (9 cases) + post-expiry normalization (6 cases). FD-7 failure was cliff expiry without recovery handover.
- Organizational control: pilot/emergency routes disabled by default in workflow engine; enabled only with valid authority token + eligibility snapshot.

**Layer 5 — Explicit scope rules:**
- Rejected leads: intake pre-screen (Register_intake -> Sanctions -> XOR[Reject->Notify->Close, Accept -> handover to M1 onboarding]) is a separate case type or phase; M1 onboarding begins after acceptance. Resolves 709.
- Reactivations: if prior cert valid + within defined period + no material change, abbreviated reactivation case links to prior case ID (Sanctions + Manual + Activate + Notify + Close, Quality by reference); otherwise de novo onboarding. Resolves 710.
- Withdrawals/cancellations: explicit withdraw branch (Notify->Close without Activate/Reject) to avoid forcing Reject.
- Duplicates/renames: deduplicate on source transaction ID; maintain label ontology (Quality_Review = QA_Clearance from mapping version) with contemporaneous mapping.

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

POWL limitation statement: POWL can express order/concurrency/choice/repetition but cannot enforce risk/geography/group, amount caps, date windows, SoD/disclosure, authority validity, or timeliness. Encoding auto/emergency as pure XOR without guards would legitimize ineligible use (703) and post-expiry reuse (714). The defensible approximation is to make the revised POWL *permissive on order* (allow provisional patterns) and enforce all guards in the accompanying policy-overlay table + data checks. Request_More_Info repetition is approximated with LOOP(do=silent, redo=Request_More_Info) to allow zero or more cycles; notification-before-closure is enforced as strict sequence.

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# --- Top-level anchors ---
register = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")
notify = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")
close = Transition(label="Close_Case")

# --- Repeatable Request_More_Info: zero or more cycles (LOOP approximation) ---
# LOOP semantics: do (silent) then optionally (redo + do) repeatedly.
# This allows 0,1,2,... Request_More_Info events before proceeding.
skip_info = SilentTransition()
request_info = Transition(label="Request_More_Info")
info_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[skip_info, request_info])

# --- Standard manual route: concurrent sanctions + quality before manual -> activate ---
s_std = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
q_std = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
m_std = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
a_std = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
standard_route = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s_std, q_std, m_std, a_std])
standard_route.order.add_edge(s_std, m_std)
standard_route.order.add_edge(q_std, m_std)
standard_route.order.add_edge(m_std, a_std)
# s_std and q_std have no edge between them => concurrent

# --- Low-risk auto route: sanctions before auto -> activate; quality allowed before or after ---
# Approximation: quality only constrained to be after sanctions, concurrent with auto/activate.
# Timeliness (within 2 business days after) enforced in data layer, not POWL.
s_auto = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
auto = Transition(label="Auto_Approve")
a_auto = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
q_auto = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
auto_route = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s_auto, auto, a_auto, q_auto])
auto_route.order.add_edge(s_auto, auto)
auto_route.order.add_edge(auto, a_auto)
auto_route.order.add_edge(s_auto, q_auto)
# q_auto unordered vs auto/a_auto => may occur before or after activation (pilot flexibility)

# --- Emergency / provisional route: sanctions before activate, then quality + manual after ---
s_em = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
a_em = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
q_em = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
m_em = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
emergency_route = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s_em, a_em, q_em, m_em])
emergency_route.order.add_edge(s_em, a_em)
emergency_route.order.add_edge(a_em, q_em)
emergency_route.order.add_edge(a_em, m_em)
# q_em and m_em concurrent after activation

# --- Abbreviated rejection route: sanctions -> reject (no quality required for pre-screen rejects) ---
# Full rejection with quality can use standard checks then reject; this branch covers intake scope.
s_rej = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
reject = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
reject_route = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s_rej, reject])
reject_route.order.add_edge(s_rej, reject)

# --- Decision as exclusive choice among routes ---
decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[standard_route, auto_route, emergency_route, reject_route])

# --- Top-level: register -> info_loop -> decision -> notify -> close ---
M1 = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, info_loop, decision, notify, close])
M1.order.add_edge(register, info_loop)
M1.order.add_edge(info_loop, decision)
M1.order.add_edge(decision, notify)
M1.order.add_edge(notify, close)
```
