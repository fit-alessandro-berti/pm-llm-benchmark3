# 1. Assumptions and notation

**A1 (scope).** The 22 listed cases are the observed population; there is no unlisted work already in either station at 08:00. The six-hour window is [08:00, 14:00]. This is needed to reconstruct absolute WIP. All quantities below are in minutes unless stated otherwise.

**A2 (rounded time).** Calculations use the printed five-minute timestamps as nominal event times. An actual printed endpoint can differ by about 2.5 minutes; therefore a difference of two printed endpoints has about a ±5-minute timing envelope. These are uncertainty envelopes, not confidence intervals. Exact boundary ties and small apparent gaps should not be read as precise physical timing.

**A3 (event order).** Service intervals are half-open [start, end). At a printed time, completions and the resulting S2 entries are applied, then other arrivals, then starts. The reported queue and WIP are the state *after* all events at that time. A case starting at its entry time has zero queue time. Two-hour rate bins are half-open, so an event printed at 10:00 belongs to 10:00–12:00.

**A4 (resources and missing starts).** For nominal bounds, I1, I2, C1 and C2 each serve at most one case at a time, without preemption, in the recorded resource order. A MISSING start occurred but is unknown. Its nominal feasible interval is bounded by station entry, the prior completion on its resource, its own completion, and the next recorded start on that resource. Zero service at an interval endpoint is a mathematical limiting bound, not a plausible literal repair operation. Rounding or unlogged assistance can relax the nominal bounds slightly.

**A5 (central scenario).** For descriptive point estimates only, impute P05 S2 at 09:50, P07 S1 at 09:30, P11 S2 at 12:05, and P15 S1 at 11:45. Each gives a positive, roughly comparable service duration and obeys the nominal resource constraints. The other two scenarios use all four earliest or all four latest feasible starts; no single imputation is treated as observed.

**A6 (censoring).** At 14:00 an OPEN job is still in service: its elapsed service is known nominally, while its final service and station sojourn are only lower-bounded. A waiting case has a lower bound on queue time and zero observed service. The 14:00 boundary is treated as exact for elapsed-time reporting; operational event endpoints retain A2 uncertainty.

**A7 (availability).** S1 has 720 resource-minutes and S2 has 705 staffed resource-minutes over the window, subtracting C2's 15-minute outage. Busy time counts work observed through 14:00, including elapsed OPEN work. It does not count maintenance as productive service. No shift breaks or other outages are supplied.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

For case \(i\), let \(a_{1i}\) be external arrival, \(b_{ji}\) service start, and \(d_{ji}\) completion at station \(j\). Set \(a_{2i}=d_{1i}\). For a completed station visit, queue wait is \(q_{ji}=b_{ji}-a_{ji}\), active service is \(s_{ji}=d_{ji}-b_{ji}\), and station sojourn is \(v_{ji}=d_{ji}-a_{ji}=q_{ji}+s_{ji}\). For a fully completed case, total sojourn is \(d_{2i}-a_{1i}\). This includes both queue waits and both services. For a waiting visit, only \(14{:}00-a_{ji}\) of waiting has elapsed. For OPEN service, \(14{:}00-b_{ji}\) is elapsed service, not final service.

At time \(t\), \(Q_j(t)\) counts station entries with no start yet, \(B_j(t)\) counts started visits without completion, and \(W_j(t)=Q_j(t)+B_j(t)\). Total process WIP is \(W_1(t)+W_2(t)\), since each case occupies exactly one station state. Time means integrate the post-event step functions over 360 minutes. A station's arrival and completion rates in a bin are its respective event counts divided by bin hours. Nominal utilization is observed busy resource-minutes divided by staffed resource-minutes; it is not a completed-case-only ratio.

The nominal missing-start bounds, derived under A4, are:

| Missing start | Station entry / prior resource completion | Own completion / next resource start | Feasible start | Central start |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P05 S2, C1 | 09:25 / 09:45 | 10:15 / 10:20 | 09:45–10:15 | 09:50 |
| P07 S1, I1 | 08:45 / 09:25 | 10:00 / 10:05 | 09:25–10:00 | 09:30 |
| P11 S2, C2 | 11:05 / 12:00 | 12:25 / 12:30 | 12:00–12:25 | 12:05 |
| P15 S1, I1 | 10:10 / 11:40 | 12:15 / 12:20 | 11:40–12:15 | 11:45 |

Lower and upper estimates below come from these feasible intervals, with the paired wait and service changing in opposite directions. A2 adds roughly ±5 minutes to individual two-endpoint durations; it is not correct to choose all independent endpoints adversarially and call the result a likely schedule.

# 4. Station-level metrics

The summaries use all supplied cases, A5 for the central values, and the full 08:00–14:00 interval. Waiting means are among **started** visits; completed-service means are among **completed** visits. Queue means include unfinished waiting time. `Q/WIP` means waiting queue / waiting plus active service.

| Station | Analysis interval | Arrivals; completed services | Waiting-time summary | Active-service summary | Mean / peak Q and WIP | Busy time / plausible nominal utilization | Data coverage |
|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|---|

The central total time-mean WIP is **9.51 cases** (S1 5.79 + S2 3.72); its nominal event-time peak is **13**, around 11:30–11:50. The endpoint identity is 22 external arrivals − 14 completed cases = 8 unfinished cases. At 14:00 those are one S1 OPEN, one S1 waiting, two S2 OPEN, and four S2 waiting.

| Nominal bin | S1 arrivals / h | S1 completions / h = S2 arrivals / h | S2 completions / h |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 08:00–10:00 | 13 / 2 = 6.5 | 6 / 2 = 3.0 | 3 / 2 = 1.5 |
| 10:00–12:00 | 8 / 2 = 4.0 | 7 / 2 = 3.5 | 5 / 2 = 2.5 |
| 12:00–14:00 | 1 / 2 = 0.5 | 7 / 2 = 3.5 | 6 / 2 = 3.0 |

Events printed exactly at 10:00 or 12:00 could cross these bin boundaries after rounding. The counts are nominal, while the six-hour totals and backlog trend are more robust.

A classical steady-state Little's Law estimate is **not justified** for any broad bin: the external arrival rate changes sharply, WIP builds and then redistributes, and many cases cross bin boundaries or remain censored. A finite-window Little's-Law-style accounting check is exact under A1–A3: total process WIP area is **3,425 case-minutes**, equal to **2,050 minutes** of arrival-to-finish time for 14 completed cases plus **1,375 minutes** of observed age for eight unfinished cases. Thus 3,425/360 = **9.51**. Station checks give S1 **2,085 = 1,845 completed-visit sojourn + 130 OPEN age + 110 waiting age** and S2 **1,340 = 980 completed-visit sojourn + 105 + 70 OPEN ages + 185 waiting ages**. Using only completed cases in \(L=\lambda W\) would give about 5.69 total cases and miss the censored WIP; that discrepancy is precisely why a steady-state interpretation would be misleading here.

# 5. Bottleneck and temporal interpretation

**Observed early:** From 08:00–10:00, external S1 arrivals run at 6.5/h against 3.0/h S1 completions. S1's queue reaches six by 10:00, versus two at S2. The main *location of accumulating waiting* is S1. This reflects an arrival surge relative to observed S1 output; it does not by itself establish that S1 has the lower inherent capacity.

**Observed later:** From 10:00–14:00, S1 sends 14 cases to S2 but S2 completes 11. S1's queue falls to one by 14:00 as external arrivals taper, while S2 still has four waiting and two in service. Completed S2 services average about 37.5 minutes versus 28.25 at S1, with two nominal resources each. This supports S2 as the **downstream capacity constraint later in the window**, though the observed completion rates are affected by transient backlog, imputation, and censoring. A rough two-server capacity comparison using those selected completed-case means is about 4.2/h for S1 versus 3.2/h for S2 before outages; these are descriptive, not stable service-rate estimates.

**Maintenance:** C2 is unavailable 10:55–11:10, a 15-minute capacity loss. P08 had entered S2 at 10:20, starts on C2 at 11:15, and waited 55 minutes. C2's prior job ended at 10:50; with nonpreemptive service, a 45-minute calibration could not fit before maintenance, so the outage plausibly contributes to that wait and the contemporaneous queue. The log does not identify a dispatch rule, so it cannot allocate all of P08's 55 minutes causally to maintenance. S2 backlog persists well after 11:10, making the outage only one contributor to the later S2 constraint.

# 6. Missingness, censoring, and sensitivity analysis

| Scenario | Missing-start choice | S1 started wait mean / observed busy / Q mean | S2 started wait mean / observed busy / Q mean | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Short waits, long services | All four earliest feasible starts | 65.2 / 605 / 4.11 | 36.6 / 570 / 2.14 | Most busy time; zero-duration endpoint avoided. |
| Central | 09:50, 09:30, 12:05, 11:45 for P05, P07, P11, P15 | 65.7 / 595 / 4.14 | 37.2 / 560 / 2.17 | Positive durations consistent with neighboring work. |
| Long waits, short services | All four latest feasible starts | 68.6 / 535 / 4.31 | 40.0 / 515 / 2.29 | Includes zero-service mathematical limits; not a literal operational scenario. |

The **stable conclusions** are the 20 S1 and 14 S2 completions, eight unfinished cases, mean WIP (because entries and completions are unchanged by start imputation), the early S1 buildup, and the later S2 backlog. In contrast, queue-versus-service decomposition, utilization, and the exact instantaneous queue near an imputed start are sensitive. At noon, S1's nominal Q can be 6–7 and S2's 3–4, while WIP remains 7 and 5 respectively.

Rounding can move a two-endpoint wait or service by about five minutes and move an event across a rate-bin cutpoint. If all duration errors were combined independently in the most adverse direction, busy-time envelopes would be extremely loose—roughly 433–708 S1 resource-minutes and 440–645 S2 resource-minutes before enforcing all physical constraints. They should not be interpreted as likely utilization ranges. The nominal imputation ranges in the station table are more informative, but neither supports a precise percentage. Rounded apparent overlaps or gaps warrant checking the raw logs before assigning idle or busy time to a person.

Right censoring is more consequential for eventual service and sojourn means. P15 and P17 have only 30 and 5 minutes of S2 service observed; P21 has only 30 minutes of S1 service observed. The four S2 waiters and P22 can wait longer after 14:00. Treating any of these as completed at the cutoff would bias final durations downward. The 14-case total-sojourn mean describes the selected cases that finished by 14:00; the 22-case eventual mean is not identifiable, although its nominal lower bound exceeds the completed-case mean.

# 7. Reproducible Python

The script prints missing-start bounds, three station scenarios, two-hour flow rates, and a small complete-case PM4Py diagnostic. PM4Py's case-level `@@arrival_rate` and `@@finish_rate` fields are **gaps in seconds between case starts or finishes**, not station throughput rates. Its case sojourn starts at the first S1 **service start**, so it omits external pre-S1 queue time; the pandas calculations above retain it.

```python
import io
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

DATA = '''case,arrival_s1,s1_start,s1_end,s1_resource,s2_start,s2_end,s2_resource,state
P01,08:00,08:05,08:25,I1,08:30,09:00,C1,COMPLETE
P02,08:05,08:10,08:35,I2,08:40,09:20,C2,COMPLETE
P03,08:10,08:30,08:55,I1,09:05,09:45,C1,COMPLETE
P04,08:20,08:40,09:10,I2,09:25,10:05,C2,COMPLETE
P05,08:25,09:00,09:25,I1,MISSING,10:15,C1,COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P06,08:35,09:15,09:45,I2,10:10,10:50,C2,COMPLETE
P07,08:45,MISSING,10:00,I1,10:20,11:00,C1,COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P08,09:00,09:50,10:20,I2,11:15,12:00,C2,COMPLETE
P09,09:05,10:05,10:35,I1,11:00,11:40,C1,COMPLETE
P10,09:15,10:25,10:55,I2,11:45,12:10,C1,COMPLETE
P11,09:25,10:40,11:05,I1,MISSING,12:25,C2,COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P12,09:35,11:00,11:30,I2,12:15,13:00,C1,COMPLETE
P13,09:50,11:10,11:40,I1,12:30,13:20,C2,COMPLETE
P14,10:00,11:35,12:00,I2,13:05,13:50,C1,COMPLETE
P15,10:10,MISSING,12:15,I1,13:30,OPEN,C2,OPEN_IN_S2
P16,10:25,12:05,12:35,I2,,, ,WAITING_FOR_S2
P17,10:45,12:20,12:50,I1,13:55,OPEN,C1,OPEN_IN_S2
P18,11:00,12:40,13:10,I2,,, ,WAITING_FOR_S2
P19,11:15,12:55,13:25,I1,,, ,WAITING_FOR_S2
P20,11:30,13:15,13:45,I2,,, ,WAITING_FOR_S2
P21,11:50,13:30,OPEN,I1,,, ,OPEN_IN_S1
P22,12:10,,, ,,, ,WAITING_FOR_S1'''
raw = pd.read_csv(io.StringIO(DATA), dtype=str, keep_default_na=False)
raw = raw.apply(lambda col: col.str.strip())
base = pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 08:00')
horizon = base + pd.Timedelta(hours=6)

def timestamp_status(value):
    if value == 'MISSING':
        return 'missing_start'  # occurred; completion is known
    if value == 'OPEN':
        return 'open_end'       # started; incomplete at cutoff
    if value == '':
        return 'blank'          # activity has not started
    return 'observed'

def parse_time(value):
    return (pd.Timestamp('2026-05-12 ' + value)
            if timestamp_status(value) == 'observed' else pd.NaT)

for col in ('arrival_s1', 's1_start', 's1_end', 's2_start', 's2_end'):
    raw[col + '_status'] = raw[col].map(timestamp_status)
    raw[col + '_ts'] = pd.to_datetime(raw[col].map(parse_time))

missing = [(row['case'], station) for _, row in raw.iterrows()
           for station in (1, 2)
           if row[f's{station}_start_status'] == 'missing_start']
bounds = {}
for case, station in missing:
    row = raw.loc[raw.case.eq(case)].iloc[0]
    resource = row[f's{station}_resource']
    same = raw.loc[raw[f's{station}_resource'].eq(resource)].reset_index(drop=True)
    pos = same.index[same.case.eq(case)][0]
    entry = row['arrival_s1_ts'] if station == 1 else row['s1_end_ts']
    prev_end = same.loc[pos-1, f's{station}_end_ts'] if pos else base
    next_start = (same.loc[pos+1, f's{station}_start_ts']
                  if pos + 1 < len(same) else horizon)
    lo = max(entry, prev_end)
    hi = min(row[f's{station}_end_ts'], next_start)
    assert lo <= hi
    bounds[(case, station)] = (lo, hi)

central = {('P05', 2): parse_time('09:50'),
           ('P07', 1): parse_time('09:30'),
           ('P11', 2): parse_time('12:05'),
           ('P15', 1): parse_time('11:45')}
scenarios = {
    'short_wait_long_service': {k: v[0] for k, v in bounds.items()},
    'central': central,
    'long_wait_short_service': {k: v[1] for k, v in bounds.items()},
}

def minutes(delta):
    return delta.total_seconds() / 60

def reconstruct(chosen):
    df = raw.copy()
    for (case, station), timestamp in chosen.items():
        lo, hi = bounds[(case, station)]
        assert lo <= timestamp <= hi
        df.loc[df.case.eq(case), f's{station}_start_ts'] = timestamp
    return df

def station_cases(df, station):
    entry_col = 'arrival_s1_ts' if station == 1 else 's1_end_ts'
    out = df.loc[df[entry_col].notna(), ['case', entry_col,
        f's{station}_start_ts', f's{station}_end_ts']].copy()
    out.columns = ['case', 'entry', 'start', 'end']
    return out

def station_summary(df, station):
    x = station_cases(df, station)
    started = x.start.notna()
    finished = x.end.notna()
    wait = (x.start.fillna(horizon) - x.entry).dt.total_seconds() / 60
    service = (x.end.fillna(horizon) - x.start).dt.total_seconds() / 60
    service = service.where(started, 0)
    times = sorted(set([base, horizon]) | set(x.entry.dropna()) |
                   set(x.start.dropna()) | set(x.end.dropna()))
    area_q = area_w = 0.0
    peak_q = peak_w = 0
    snapshots = {}
    for t, nxt in zip(times, times[1:] + [horizon]):
        # Post-event state: departures, arrivals, starts at t are all applied.
        q = int(((x.entry <= t) & (x.start.isna() | (x.start > t))).sum())
        w = int(((x.entry <= t) & (x.end.isna() | (x.end > t))).sum())
        dt = minutes(nxt - t)
        area_q += q * dt
        area_w += w * dt
        peak_q, peak_w = max(peak_q, q), max(peak_w, w)
        if t == horizon:
            snapshots['14:00'] = (q, w)
    for label, hh in [('10:00', 2), ('12:00', 4)]:
        t = base + pd.Timedelta(hours=hh)
        snapshots[label] = (
            int(((x.entry <= t) & (x.start.isna() | (x.start > t))).sum()),
            int(((x.entry <= t) & (x.end.isna() | (x.end > t))).sum()))
    staffed = 720 if station == 1 else 705  # C2 unavailable 15 min
    return {'station': f'S{station}', 'arrivals': len(x),
            'completed': int(finished.sum()), 'started': int(started.sum()),
            'mean_wait_started_min': round(wait[started].mean(), 1),
            'mean_service_completed_min': round(service[finished].mean(), 1),
            'busy_elapsed_min': round(service.sum()),
            'util_pct': round(100 * service.sum()/staffed, 1),
            'mean_Q': round(area_q/360, 2), 'mean_WIP': round(area_w/360, 2),
            'peak_Q': peak_q, 'peak_WIP': peak_w,
            'Q_WIP_at_10_12_14': str([snapshots[k] for k in
                                   ('10:00', '12:00', '14:00')])}

print('Missing-start nominal feasible bounds:')
print(pd.DataFrame([{'case': c, 'station': f'S{s}',
                     'earliest': lo.strftime('%H:%M'),
                     'latest': hi.strftime('%H:%M')}
                    for (c, s), (lo, hi) in bounds.items()]).to_string(index=False))
print('\nScenario station summaries (minutes except WIP and utilization):')
summary = []
for name, chosen in scenarios.items():
    df = reconstruct(chosen)
    for station in (1, 2):
        summary.append({'scenario': name, **station_summary(df, station)})
print(pd.DataFrame(summary).to_string(index=False))

central_df = reconstruct(central)
rates = []
for start_hour in (0, 2, 4):
    lo = base + pd.Timedelta(hours=start_hour)
    hi = lo + pd.Timedelta(hours=2)
    for station in (1, 2):
        x = station_cases(central_df, station)
        arrivals = int(((x.entry >= lo) & (x.entry < hi)).sum())
        completions = int(((x.end >= lo) & (x.end < hi)).sum())
        rates.append({'window': f'{lo:%H:%M}-{hi:%H:%M}',
                      'station': f'S{station}', 'arrivals': arrivals,
                      'completed': completions,
                      'arrival_per_hour': arrivals/2,
                      'completion_per_hour': completions/2})
print('\nTwo-hour flow rates:')
print(pd.DataFrame(rates).to_string(index=False))

 # Complete intervals only; OPEN has no end. Imputed starts are scenario-tagged.
intervals = []
for _, row in central_df.iterrows():
    for station, activity in ((1, 'Incoming Inspection'), (2, 'Calibration')):
        start = row[f's{station}_start_ts']
        end = row[f's{station}_end_ts']
        if pd.notna(start) and pd.notna(end):
            intervals.append({'case:concept:name': row.case,
                              'concept:name': activity,
                              'start_timestamp': start,
                              'time:timestamp': end,
                              'org:resource': row[f's{station}_resource'],
                              'imputed_start': (row.case, station) in bounds})
events = pd.DataFrame(intervals)
formatted = pm4py.format_dataframe(events, case_id='case:concept:name',
    activity_key='concept:name', timestamp_key='time:timestamp',
    start_timestamp_key='start_timestamp')
complete_ids = set(central_df.loc[central_df.s2_end_ts.notna(), 'case'])
projection = formatted.loc[formatted['case:concept:name'].isin(complete_ids)].copy()
 # These helpers have CASE-level semantics on complete two-station traces only.
case_times = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(
    projection.copy(), start_timestamp_key='start_timestamp')
case_rates = pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate(
    projection.copy(), start_timestamp_key='start_timestamp')
first = case_times.drop_duplicates('case:concept:name').sort_values('case:concept:name')
show = first[['case:concept:name', '@@service_time', '@@waiting_time',
              '@@sojourn_time']].copy()
for col in show.columns[1:]:
    show[col] = (show[col] / 60).round(1)
print('\nPM4Py case-level complete-trace service/gap/sojourn minutes (first 5):')
print(show.head().to_string(index=False))
rate_first = case_rates.drop_duplicates('case:concept:name').sort_values('case:concept:name')
print('\nPM4Py case-level inter-start/inter-finish gaps in minutes (first 5):')
print((rate_first[['case:concept:name', '@@arrival_rate', '@@finish_rate']]
```
