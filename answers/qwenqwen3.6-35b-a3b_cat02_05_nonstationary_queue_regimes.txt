# 1. Data audit and estimands

**Queueing quantities & units**
- `` (arrival intensity): cases/day or cases/hour. Derived from daily `arrivals` or hourly vectors.
- `` (service capacity): cases/hour. Implied by `staff_fte` × 12 h × (1/mean_service_min). Mean service time is not supplied; only median and p90 are available.
- `L` (mean WIP): dimensionless count. Provided as `mean_wip` from 15-min snapshots.
- `W` (mean flow time): hours or minutes. Not directly observed. Can be implied via Little’s Law if steady-state assumptions hold.
- `` (capacity pressure): ` / (staff_fte × _per_fte)`. Proxy: `mean_wip / staff_fte` or `arrivals / completed`.
- `S_median`, `S_p90`: active handling times (minutes), excluding queue wait.

**Opening-hours convention**
- System operates 07:00–19:00 (12 hours/day). No service occurs outside this window.
- Daily aggregates collapse intra-day dynamics. Hourly vectors are representative, not exhaustive.

**Limitations & threats to naive inference**
1. **Backlog carry-over**: `completed`  `arrivals` on many days. Completions include cases from prior days, biasing throughput estimates if `arrivals` is used as .
2. **Median/p90 service time  mean flow time**: Queueing laws require mean flow time. Using median service time in L=W violates the law and underestimates W.
3. **Intraday burstiness**: Hourly vectors show non-uniform demand (e.g., 08–09 spikes). Steady-state M/M/1 or M/G/1 assumptions fail without explicit burstiness modeling.
4. **Unobserved case-mix shift**: `complex_pct` jumps from ~20% to ~44% around Apr 20. Without knowing the service-time multiplier for complex cases, capacity utilization estimates are confounded.
5. **Transient outage/recovery dynamics**: Apr 15–16 exhibits backlog accumulation and clearance. Treating these days as steady-state inflates perceived throughput and masks system fragility.

*Assumption 1*: `staff_fte` represents effective productive capacity during opening hours.  
*Assumption 2*: Complex cases increase mean service time proportionally, but the exact ratio is unknown; I treat `complex_pct` as a mix-intensity proxy.  
*Assumption 3*: 15-minute WIP snapshots are representative of the daily mean.

# 5. Little's Law implied flow time for chosen windows
print("\n=== LITTLE'S LAW IMPLIED FLOW TIME ===")
def little_flow_time(df, label):
    # lambda = completed / 12 hr^-1
    lam = df['completed'].mean() / 12.0
    L = df['mean_wip'].mean()
    W_hr = L / lam
    W_min = W_hr * 60
    print(f"{label}: L={L:.1f}, ={lam:.2f} hr¹, W={W_hr:.2f} hr  {W_min:.0f} min")
