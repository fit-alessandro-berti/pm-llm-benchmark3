# 1. Current-process diagnosis

The current warranty process exhibits a fundamental contradiction between operational efficiency and long-term value creation, driven by the dominance of the "Fast Track" (Auto eligibility + Fast Approval + Dispatch).

**Performance & Cost Contradiction**
The Frequency DFG shows the Fast Track (Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement) handles approximately 50% of all cases (3600 + 2550 + 2300 edges). While this path appears attractive on a per-case basis (31% cheaper before rework), the process mining data reveals it is significantly more expensive *after* accounting for downstream failures. Fast approval cases incur a 14.1% complaint/reopen rate compared to 3.3% for the Repair Quote path. The "Customer complaint/reopen" edge has a mean duration of 72.0 hours, indicating high operational drag when the fast path fails.

**Quality & Compliance Risks**
The process mining evidence highlights severe compliance deviations. The normative rules (R1, R2, R3) are frequently violated:
*   **R1 Violation:** Fast approval often occurs without Manual eligibility review or Fraud/risk review for high-value claims (EUR > 400).
*   **R2 Violation:** Dispatch replacement frequently precedes evidence completeness confirmation.
*   **R3 Violation:** Reopened claims often bypass Manual eligibility review, leading to "Customer complaint/reopen -> Manual eligibility review" loops (mean 40.0 hours).
The conformance score (68% fitness) confirms that the "fast" path is structurally misaligned with policy, creating regulatory and reputational risk.

**Customer Experience & Rework**
The "Unclear defect / missing evidence" segment represents 22% of volume but consumes a median cycle time of 140 hours. The "Request missing evidence" node creates a feedback loop where evidence is requested twice in 26% of cases, consuming resources and delaying closure. Furthermore, the "Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen" edge (mean 96.0 hours) suggests that incorrect part selection (linked to the 14.1% complaint rate) drives the longest delays.

**Resource Pressure**
Manual eligibility review is a bottleneck (7 reviewers, 82% utilization threshold). However, the process currently overloads this resource with low-risk cases that could be automated, while underutilizing the Fraud/risk review specialists who are the only filter for high-value compliance.

# 2. Redesign A: Segmented Dual-Track Routing

**Redesigned Process Flow**
Intake Claim -> **Segment & Risk Classifier** -> **Path Selection** -> (Standard Track: Auto Screen -> Fast Approval -> Dispatch) OR (Premium/High-Value Track: Manual Review/Risk Review -> Repair Quote -> Repair Device) -> Quality Check -> Close.

**Targeted Segments/Paths**
*   **Standard Low-Risk Accessories (25% share):** Routed to the Fast Track.
*   **Premium Low-Risk Accessories (18% share):** Routed to Manual Review/Risk Review.
*   **All High-Value Devices (35% share):** Routed to Manual Review/Risk Review.
*   **Unclear Defects (22% share):** Routed to Manual Review for Evidence Gathering.

**Mechanism of Improvement**
This redesign explicitly decouples process speed from customer segment. By pre-classifying cases at Intake based on the Segment attributes (Premium/Standard) and Defect Risk, the system prevents high-value or Premium claims from entering the Fast Track. This ensures that claims requiring strict compliance (R1/R2) are routed to the Manual/Risk Review path upfront, eliminating the "Fast approval before Evidence completeness" violation. It aligns the 3.3% complaint rate of the Repair path with the high-risk segments.

**Deliberate Sacrifices**
*   **Standard Customer Speed:** Standard customers will experience slightly longer cycle times (median 13 hours vs 8 hours) if evidence is incomplete, but this reduces the 140h abandonment risk for unclear defects.
*   **Manual Resource Load:** Routing Premium cases to Manual Review increases the load on the 7 reviewers. This requires capacity planning to ensure the 82% utilization threshold is not exceeded.

# 3. Redesign B: Evidence-Gated Fast-Track

**Redesigned Process Flow**
Intake Claim -> **Evidence Completeness Check** (Mandatory for all) -> (If Complete: Auto Screen -> Fast Approval) OR (If Incomplete: Request Evidence) -> Technical Decision -> Fulfillment -> Close.
*Note: The "Request missing evidence" loop is collapsed into a single, evidence-portal-driven step.*

**Targeted Segments/Paths**
*   **All Segments:** Mandatory evidence validation before any approval.
*   **Unclear Defect Segment:** Specifically targeted to reduce the 140h median cycle time.
*   **Premium/High-Value:** Fast Track allowed only if Evidence Completeness is confirmed.

**Mechanism of Improvement**
This redesign attacks the root cause of the "Request missing evidence" loop (26% of cases entering this node). By mandating the new Evidence Portal usage *before* the Auto eligibility screen, we prevent the "Fast approval -> Customer complaint/reopen" failure mode. If evidence is missing, the system cannot approve, forcing the "Manual eligibility review" path only when necessary. This directly addresses the R2 violation (Dispatch before evidence completeness) by making Evidence Completeness a hard prerequisite for the Fast Approval node.

**Deliberate Sacrifices**
*   **Low Digital Literacy Abandonment:** The Evidence Portal requirement may increase abandonment for low-digital-literacy customers (Standard segment).
*   **Upfront Cost:** The initial evidence gathering phase increases upfront labor cost (Manual Review cost EUR 34/hr) before the technical decision is made, potentially increasing the median cycle time for the 22% "Unclear defect" segment.

# 4. Redesign C: Risk-Triggered Repair-Quote Mandate

**Redesigned Process Flow**
Intake Claim -> Auto Screen -> **Risk Scoring Engine** -> (High Risk: Repair Quote) OR (Low Risk: Fast Approval) -> Fulfillment.
*   **Rule:** If Defect Type = High Value OR Segment = Premium -> Force **Repair Quote** path.
*   **Rule:** If Defect Type = Low Risk -> Allow **Fast Approval** path.
*   **Quality Gate:** Pre-Dispatch Quality Check (6-18 hours) for all Repair Quotes.

**Targeted Segments/Paths**
*   **Premium High-Value Device (14% share):** Forced into Repair Quote.
*   **Standard High-Value Device (21% share):** Forced into Repair Quote.
*   **Low-Risk Accessories:** Remain on Fast Track.

**Mechanism of Improvement**
This redesign focuses on the "Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen" edge (mean 96.0 hours) caused by wrong replacement selection. By forcing the "Repair Quote -> Customer Approval -> Repair Device" path for High-Value devices, we utilize the 3.3% complaint rate path instead of the 14.1% Fast path. The Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate catches part-selection errors before the device leaves the warehouse, directly addressing the "Replacement cost error" (EUR 410 for high-value) without waiting for a customer complaint.

**Deliberate Sacrifices**
*   **Cycle Time for High Value:** High-Value devices will see a significant cycle time increase (median 20h to 96h) due to the Repair Quote and Quality Check steps.
*   **Resource Scalability:** The Repair Quote path relies on 3 technicians. If volume spikes (e.g., post-marketing campaign), this path may become a bottleneck, requiring temporary capacity scaling.

# 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

**Redesign A (Segmented)**
*   **Failure Mode 1:** "Premium Misclassification." If the Segment Classifier incorrectly labels a Standard case as Premium, the customer suffers unnecessary delay (Complaint risk).
*   **Failure Mode 2:** Manual Review Bottleneck. If the 7 reviewers are overloaded, the Premium queue will back up, delaying the 18% Premium segment beyond their 24h SLA.
*   **Bottleneck Migration:** The bottleneck shifts from Auto Screen to **Manual Eligibility Review** capacity.

**Redesign B (Evidence-Gated)**
*   **Failure Mode 1:** "Portal Abandonment." Low digital literacy customers may abandon the claim during the evidence step (increasing the 390 "Claim abandoned" count).
*   **Failure Mode 2:** "Incomplete Evidence Loop." If the Evidence Portal does not capture all defect context, the case loops back to Manual Review, creating a new "Request Evidence" loop.
*   **Bottleneck Migration:** The bottleneck shifts from "Request Missing Evidence" to **Evidence Portal Administration** and **Manual Review** (for validation).

**Redesign C (Repair-Mandate)**
*   **Failure Mode 1:** "Quote Delay." The 3 technicians (EUR 45/hr) may become a bottleneck, causing the Repair Quote step to exceed the 96h median, frustrating customers expecting speed.
*   **Failure Mode 2:** "Quality Gate False Negatives." The Pre-Dispatch Quality Check may miss complex part-selection errors, leading to the same complaints as the current Fast Track.
*   **Bottleneck Migration:** The bottleneck shifts from "Fast Approval" to **Repair Quote & Quality Check** capacity.

# 7. Recommended experiment sequence

To validate these redesigns without disrupting the entire process, a staged experiment plan is required.

**Phase 1: Pilot Redesign A (Segmented Routing)**
*   **Logic:** Implement the Segment Classifier on a 10% sample of Intake claims.
*   **Metrics:** Compare Median Cycle Time and Complaint Rate for Standard vs. Premium segments against baseline.
*   **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
    *   *Go:* Premium segment complaint rate drops below 6% (current 22% for fast path).
    *   *Stop:* Standard segment cycle time exceeds 24 hours (SLA breach).
*   **Sequencing:** Run for 4 weeks. Monitor Manual Review queue utilization.

**Phase 2: Pilot Redesign B (Evidence-Gated)**
*   **Logic:** Enroll 15% of "Unclear Defect" cases into the Evidence Portal workflow (Mandatory Evidence before Auto Screen).
*   **Metrics:** Abandonment Rate and "Request Missing Evidence" loop frequency.
*   **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
    *   *Go:* Abandonment rate remains < 5% of current baseline.
    *   *Stop:* Abandonment rate increases by > 2% or cycle time for Unclear Defects exceeds 100 hours.
*   **Sequencing:** Run for 4 weeks. Check if Evidence Portal reduces the "Request missing evidence" node frequency.

**Phase 3: Pilot Redesign C (Repair-Mandate)**
*   **Logic:** Force the Repair Quote path for all High-Value devices (35% of volume) for 2 weeks.
*   **Metrics:** Complaint Rate (Target < 5%) and Quality Check Pass Rate.
*   **Stop/Go Thresholds:**
    *   *Go:* Complaint rate drops below 8% (current 18-22%).
    *   *Stop:* Repair Quote queue time exceeds 48 hours.
*   **Sequencing:** Run for 2 weeks (high impact, short duration).

**Learning Logic**
To ensure improvements are real rather than shifted downstream, monitor the **"Escalation Board"** usage. If Redesign A/A/B reduces complaints but increases Escalations significantly, the issue has not been solved, merely moved. Additionally, track **Cost per Closed Claim** (including rework costs) rather than just Cycle Time to ensure the "Fast Path" cost savings are not eroded by hidden complaint costs.
