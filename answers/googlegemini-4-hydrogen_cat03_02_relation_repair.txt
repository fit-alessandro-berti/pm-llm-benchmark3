## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model

Repairing an incomplete OCEL 2.0 log requires separating structural constraints from defeasible operational clues. Treating soft heuristics as axioms manufactures false certainty, whereas treating every missing link as unrecoverable discards reliable local context. Below, each rule is classified by epistemic weight.

### Hard invariants
1. **Referential integrity and no invented entities:** Every repaired E2O edge `(eid, oid, qualifier)` and O2O edge `(oid, oid_2, qualifier)` must reference existing events and objects in the input tables.
2. **O2O directionality and domain/range typing:** O2O qualifiers are strictly directional: `Order contains Item`, `Package packs Item`, `Delivery carries Package`, `Invoice bills Order`, and `Payment settles Invoice`. Reversing direction (e.g., `PK-8 carries DEL-Y`) or crossing incompatible types (e.g., `Payment settles Order`) is invalid under the schema.
3. **Tuple uniqueness:** No duplicate `(eid, oid, qualifier)` or `(oid, oid_2, qualifier)` rows may be introduced.

### Strong defaults
1. **Activity–object participation and cardinality:** `Create Order` links to one `Order`; `Add Item` links to one `Order` and one `Item`; `Pack Item` normally links to one `Item` and one `Package` (unless a scanner outage drops the `Package` reference); `Dispatch Package` normally links to one `Package` and one `Delivery`; `Issue Invoice` links to one `Invoice` and at least one `Order`; `Record Payment` links to one `Payment` and zero or one `Invoice`.
2. **Monotonic lifecycle order:** For a given object chain, `Add Item` precedes `Pack Item`, which precedes `Dispatch Package`, which precedes `Confirm Delivery`. Financial settlement (`Record Payment`) normally follows `Issue Invoice`.
3. **Customer and route compatibility:** Linked `Order` and `Invoice` objects share `customer` (`C17`), and linked `Package` (`route_hint=NORTH`) and `Delivery` (`route=NORTH`) objects share routing compatibility unless explicit re-routing is documented.

### Weak heuristics
1. **Identifier similarity and scanner sequencing:** Prefix and sequence patterns (`scanner_ref=PK8-2` immediately following `PK8-1`; `external_ref=N-421?` resembling `DEL-Y.carrier_ref=N-421`; `bank_ref=B-772` following `B-771`) suggest continuity but can reflect typos, reused labels, or provisional drafts.
2. **Temporal proximity:** Short inter-event gaps (`ev07` at `09:04` and `ev08` at `09:05`; `ev09` and `ev10` both at `10:00`) corroborate shared batch operations or concurrent dispatches, but co-occurrence in time alone never proves shared object participation.
3. **Arithmetic alignment:** Summing `ORD-A` (`130`) plus `ORD-B` (`100`) to `230` (`INV-44.total`), or `PAY-1` (`100`) plus `PAY-2` (`130`) to `230`, provides suggestive numerical compatibility.

### Evidence against a repair
1. **Explicit ambiguity markers:** Question marks (`N-421?`) and wildcards (`N-42*`) indicate unverified or truncated identifiers that match multiple candidates (`DEL-X` and `DEL-Y`) or reflect tentative carrier assignments.
2. **Valid alternative domain explanations:** Fees, taxes, or price adjustments can make a single order (`ORD-A`, `declared_value=130`) produce an invoice of `230`. Unapplied cash, prepayments, or unlogged invoices can leave `PAY-2` (`130`) without an `Invoice` link. Portal confirmation loss (`Confirm Delivery`) legitimately omits carrier IDs.
3. **Non-independent structural mirroring:** Inferring an O2O edge solely from a hypothesized E2O edge—and then citing that O2O edge to raise confidence in the E2O edge—is circular and counts as evidence against treating the pair as independently corroborated.

### Evidence combination model
Evidence is combined via defeasible abductive weighting rather than additive scoring. A candidate is accepted in a conservative repair only when (a) no hard invariant is violated, (b) at least one strong default or specific operational artifact (such as a scanner sequence tied to a documented outage mode) points to a unique target, and (c) no plausible alternative business explanation accounts equally well for the observed record. Where multiple candidates or legitimate exceptions remain competitive, the proposal is marked `provisional` (for exploratory bundles) or `unresolved`.

- **INV-44 and ORD-B:** Leaves `ev12 -> ORD-B` and `INV-44 bills ORD-B` unresolved, respecting Rule 6 (tax/fee/surcharge explanations for `230` cannot be excluded).
- **PAY-2 and INV-44:** Leaves `ev14 -> INV-44` and `PAY-2 settles INV-44` unresolved, respecting Rule 7 (unapplied cash or unlogged invoices cannot be excluded).

**Coherence:** Bundle A yields partial but honest lifecycles: `PK-8` gains its missing `Pack Item` step without fabricating downstream carrier, delivery-confirmation, or billing closures that the raw evidence cannot prove.

### Bundle B: Coherent consolidated-fulfillment and split-settlement repair
Bundle B tests the hypothesis that customer `C17`'s same-day orders (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`) underwent consolidated packing (`PK-8`), split delivery (`DEL-X`, `DEL-Y`) jointly acknowledged in the portal (`ev11`), consolidated invoicing (`INV-44`), and split payment (`PAY-1`, `PAY-2`):

## 5. Lifecycle and cardinality audit

Auditing the selected repair (**Bundle A**) by object type highlights where lifecycles remain legitimately incomplete and where structural asymmetries arise:

- **Order (`ORD-A`, `ORD-B`):** `ORD-A` follows `Create Order -> Add Item (x2) -> Confirm Delivery -> Issue Invoice`. Notice the jump from `Add Item` directly to `Confirm Delivery`: because `Pack Item` and `Dispatch Package` attach to `Item`, `Package`, and `Delivery` rather than `Order`, `Order` skips fulfillment events unless projected through O2O/E2O joins. `ORD-B` stops after `Create Order -> Add Item`, which is an incomplete lifecycle by design rather than an error.
- **Item (`IT-A1`, `IT-A2`, `IT-B1`):** All three items now share a plausible lifecycle (`Add Item -> Pack Item`), with `IT-B1` gaining its missing package context via `ev08 -> PK-8`. Each `Item` still belongs to exactly one `Order` and one `Pack Item` event.
- **Package (`PK-7`, `PK-8`):** `PK-7` has `Pack Item (ev06) -> Dispatch Package (ev09)`. `PK-8` now has `Pack Item (ev07) -> Pack Item (ev08) -> Dispatch Package (ev10)`. Notably, `PK-8` exhibits a **one-to-many relation across orders**: it packs `IT-A2` (`ORD-A`) and `IT-B1` (`ORD-B`). While plausible for same-customer (`C17`), same-route (`NORTH`) consolidation, it is a suspicious cross-order merge if downstream analysts assume single-order packages.
- **Delivery (`DEL-X`, `DEL-Y`):** `DEL-X` participates only in `Dispatch Package (ev09)` (`Confirm Delivery` remains unlinked). **`DEL-Y` is an orphan object** in Bundle A: it appears in `OBJECTS` with `carrier_ref=N-421, route=NORTH` but has zero E2O and zero O2O relations. Leaving `DEL-Y` orphaned reflects honest uncertainty around `N-421?` rather than an oversight. No completely orphan *events* exist (every event retains at least one observed object), though `ev10`, `ev11`, and `ev14` lack secondary role objects.
- **Invoice (`INV-44`):** Participates in `Issue Invoice (ev12) -> Record Payment (ev13)`. The discrepancy between `INV-44.total=230` and `ORD-A.declared_value=130` (and `PAY-1.amount=100`) remains visibly flagged rather than smoothed away.
- **Payment (`PAY-1`, `PAY-2`):** `PAY-1` participates in `Record Payment (ev13)` with `INV-44`; `PAY-2` participates in `Record Payment (ev14)` alone, representing valid unapplied or unreconciled cash.

**Independent E2O vs. O2O support and circularity:** Only the original eight O2O edges (`ORD-A contains IT-A1/A2`, `ORD-B contains IT-B1`, `PK-7 packs IT-A1`, `PK-8 packs IT-A2`, `DEL-X carries PK-7`, `INV-44 bills ORD-A`, `PAY-1 settles INV-44`) are corroborated by both E2O and O2O evidence. By contrast, Bundle A's addition (`ev08 -> PK-8`) is supported solely by E2O scanner evidence. Adding `PK-8 packs IT-B1` (as Bundle B does) solely because `ev08 -> PK-8` was hypothesized—and then citing that O2O edge as independent confirmation of `ev08 -> PK-8`—would be circular reasoning. The same circularity applies in Bundle B to `DEL-Y carries PK-8` (derived from `ev10 -> DEL-Y`), `INV-44 bills ORD-B` (derived from `ev12 -> ORD-B`), and `PAY-2 settles INV-44` (derived from `ev14 -> INV-44`).

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects

Comparing the **Unrepaired log**, **Bundle A**, and **Bundle B** shows how seemingly small repairs alter process discovery:

- **OC-DFG:**
  - *Unrepaired:* On `Package`, `PK-7` yields `Pack Item (ev06) -> Dispatch Package (ev09)` and `PK-8` yields `Pack Item (ev07) -> Dispatch Package (ev10)`. On `Delivery`, `DEL-X` shows only an isolated `Dispatch Package (ev09)` node. On `Order`, `ORD-A` follows `Create Order -> Add Item -> Add Item -> Confirm Delivery -> Issue Invoice`, while `ORD-B` stops at `Create Order -> Add Item`.
  - *Bundle A:* Linking `ev08 -> PK-8` changes `PK-8`'s trace to `<ev07, ev08, ev10>`. This **creates** a `Pack Item -> Pack Item` self-loop (`ev07 -> ev08`) on `Package`, **removes** the direct `ev07 -> ev10` transition, and **shifts** `PK-8`'s `Pack Item -> Dispatch Package` edge to `ev08 -> ev10`.
  - *Bundle B:* On `Delivery`, `Dispatch Package -> Confirm Delivery` **appears** twice (`ev09 -> ev11` on `DEL-X`, `ev10 -> ev11` on `DEL-Y`). On `Order`, `ORD-B` **gains** `Add Item -> Confirm Delivery -> Issue Invoice`. On `Invoice`, `INV-44` **gains** a `Record Payment -> Record Payment` self-loop (`ev13 -> ev14`).
- **OTG (Object Type Graph):** When derived from observed O2O relations, the Unrepaired log and Bundle A have identical OTGs (`Order -> Item`, `Package -> Item`, `Delivery -> Package`, `Invoice -> Order`, `Payment -> Invoice`), though `Package -> Item` (`PK-8 packs IT-B1`) and `Delivery -> Package` (`DEL-Y carries PK-8`) have lower instance support than in Bundle B. If an OTG variant uses event co-occurrence, Bundle B **adds** a brand-new `(Delivery, Order)` coupling via `ev11` (`ORD-A, ORD-B, DEL-X, DEL-Y`) that is absent in both Unrepaired and Bundle A.
- **ET–OT graph:** In Unrepaired and Bundle A, `(Confirm Delivery, Delivery)` is **absent** (`ev11` only links to `Order`). Bundle A strengthens the existing `(Pack Item, Package)` edge from 2/3 to 3/3 `Pack Item` events. Bundle B **introduces** `(Confirm Delivery, Delivery)` for the first time via `ev11`.
- **Flattenings:**
  - *Order:* Unrepaired and Bundle A yield identical flattened logs (`ORD-A` with 5 events; `ORD-B` with 2 events). Bundle B expands `ORD-B` to 4 events (`Create Order, Add Item, Confirm Delivery, Issue Invoice`).
  - *Package:* Unrepaired gives two 2-event traces (`PK-7`, `PK-8`). Bundle A and Bundle B lengthen `PK-8` to 3 events (`Pack Item, Pack Item, Dispatch Package`).
  - *Delivery:* Unrepaired and Bundle A yield only one 1-event trace (`DEL-X: Dispatch Package`), dropping `DEL-Y`. Bundle B yields two 2-event traces (`DEL-X` and `DEL-Y`: `Dispatch Package, Confirm Delivery`).
  - *Invoice:* Unrepaired and Bundle A yield `INV-44: Issue Invoice, Record Payment`. Bundle B extends `INV-44` to `Issue Invoice, Record Payment, Record Payment`.
  - *Payment:* Identical across all three (`PAY-1` and `PAY-2` each have `Record Payment`).
- **Activity frequencies (events vs. unique objects vs. total E2O involvements):** Event counts never change (`14` total events). However, total E2O involvements per activity shift: `Pack Item` rises from `5` (Unrepaired) to `6` (Bundle A & B), while unique objects per `Pack Item` remains `5` (`IT-A1, IT-A2, IT-B1, PK-7, PK-8`). In Bundle B, `Dispatch Package` rises from `3` to `4` involvements (`+DEL-Y`), `Confirm Delivery` jumps from `1` to `4` (`+ORD-B, DEL-X, DEL-Y`), `Issue Invoice` rises from `2` to `3` (`+ORD-B`), and `Record Payment` rises from `3` to `4` involvements while remaining at `3` unique objects (`PAY-1, PAY-2, INV-44`).
- **Equivalent-execution clustering around Order:** In the Unrepaired log, `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` reside in completely disconnected object-graph components. In Bundle A, `ev08 -> PK-8` **merges** `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` into a single connected component via `ORD-A -- IT-A2 -- PK-8 -- IT-B1 -- ORD-B`. In Bundle B, they become tightly entangled across packing (`PK-8`), delivery confirmation (`ev11`), and billing (`INV-44`), effectively collapsing Order-centric clustering into a Customer-level (`C17`) cluster.

## 8. Over-repair warnings

1. **Treating `230 = 130 + 100` as proof of consolidated invoicing (`ev12 -> ORD-B`, `INV-44 bills ORD-B`):** If `INV-44` actually billed `ORD-A` (`130`) plus `100` in taxes, rush shipping, or penalties while `ORD-B` was cancelled or billed later, forcing `ORD-B` onto `INV-44` fabricates revenue recognition for `ORD-B` and conceals unexpected surcharges on `ORD-A`.
2. **Treating `100 + 130 = 230` as proof of full payment (`ev14 -> INV-44`, `PAY-2 settles INV-44`):** If `PAY-2` is unapplied cash, a deposit, or settlement for an unrecorded invoice, linking it to `INV-44` hides an open `130` accounts-receivable balance and masks payment-reconciliation exceptions.
3. **Forcing `ev11` (`N-42*`) onto a single delivery (`DEL-X` or `DEL-Y` alone):** Choosing `DEL-X` alone because `ORD-A` appears on `ev11` and `DEL-X` carries `IT-A1` fabricates a clean delivery cycle for `DEL-X` while leaving `DEL-Y` artificially incomplete—even if `ev11` actually confirmed `DEL-Y` or both deliveries. Conversely, forcing both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y` onto `ev11` hides genuine partial-delivery delays if one carrier leg had not yet arrived.
4. **Resolving `N-421?` in `ev10` without preserving uncertainty:** Unconditionally linking `ev10` to `DEL-Y` turns a tentative or corrupted scan (`?`) into a completed carrier dispatch, concealing potential manifest errors or bulk loading onto `DEL-X` (and vice versa if `DEL-X` is forced).
5. **Auto-generating O2O edges from hypothesized E2O edges:** Mirroring every provisional E2O link into `o2o` (`PK-8 packs IT-B1`, `DEL-Y carries PK-8`, `INV-44 bills ORD-B`, `PAY-2 settles INV-44`) creates an illusion of independent multi-source corroboration and permanently fuses `ORD-A` and `ORD-B` into one fulfillment-and-billing cluster across all object-centric metrics.
6. **Direct payment-to-order matching (`PAY-2 settles ORD-A`):** Linking `PAY-2` (`130`) directly to `ORD-A` (`declared_value=130`) bypasses `Invoice` altogether, hallucinating a non-standard cash-on-order shortcut in the discovered ET–OT graph and OTG.
