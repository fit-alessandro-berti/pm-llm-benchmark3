## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model

The following rules were used to evaluate repairs. They are classified as requested.

- **Hard invariant – referential integrity and directionality.** Every `ocel:eid` and `ocel:oid` used in E2O or O2O relations must already exist in the event or object tables. O2O qualifiers are directional (rule 8). No new events or objects may be invented.
- **Hard invariant – lifecycle ordering.** An event cannot legitimately relate to an object whose creation timestamp is later. In this log all observed timestamps respect order: `Create Order` before `Add Item`, before `Pack Item`, before `Dispatch Package`, before `Confirm Delivery`, `Issue Invoice`, and `Record Payment`.
- **Strong default – activity cardinality (rules 1–4, 7).** `Create Order` relates to exactly one Order; `Add Item` to one Order and one Item; `Pack Item` to one Item and one Package; `Dispatch Package` to one Package and one Delivery, unless bulk dispatch is explicitly indicated; `Record Payment` relates to exactly one Payment and normally one Invoice.
- **Strong default – identifier matching.** `scanner_ref=PK8-2` on `ev08` is a strong, though defeasible, hint that the missing Package is `PK-8`. Similarly, `external_ref=N-421?` on `ev10` matches `DEL-Y.carrier_ref=N-421` and `route=NORTH`, making `DEL-Y` the natural missing Delivery.
- **Weak heuristic – amount alignment.** `INV-44.total=230` equals `ORD-A.declared_value=130` plus `ORD-B.declared_value=100`. `PAY-1.amount=100` plus `PAY-2.amount=130` equals `230`. Per rules 6 and 7, this is evidence, not proof; fees, discounts, taxes, or missing invoices could explain the numbers.
- **Weak heuristic – shared customer, route, and day.** Same `customer=C17`, same `route=NORTH`, and same-day timestamps make bundling of orders, packages, deliveries, and payments plausible but not necessary.
- **Evidence against a repair – forced completeness.** Treating every missing relation as an error, or using `pm4py.ocel_o2o_enrichment` to auto-derive O2O edges and then treating those derived edges as independent business confirmation, is explicitly rejected. Rule 5 allows `Confirm Delivery` to reference only a customer/order when the carrier identifier is lost.

**Combining evidence.** Identifier similarity is used first because it is least ambiguous. Lifecycle order and existing O2O structure then check plausibility. Amount, customer, and route are used only to support alternative bundles, never as sole proof. Cardinality is respected: a Package may pack multiple Items, and a Delivery may carry one Package in the observed data, but bulk dispatch is possible.

## 8. Over-repair warnings

1. **Assigning `ev11` uniquely to DEL-X** would ignore that ORD-A is split across PK-7DEL-X and PK-8DEL-Y. It would create a false partial delivery confirmation and distort the OTG.
2. **Adding `INV-44 bills ORD-B` solely because 130+100=230** treats arithmetic equality as proof, violating rule 6 and hiding possible fees, discounts, or a separate invoice.

4. **Forcing IT-B1 into PK-7** based on weight guesses invents cross-order packaging not supported by `scanner_ref=PK8-2`; it would misrepresent the package composition.
