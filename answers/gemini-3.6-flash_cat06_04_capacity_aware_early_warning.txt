## 1. Decision Rule

### 1.1 Scope and Eligibility Filter
An incident $i$ is **eligible** for a Senior Diagnostic Swarm at time $t$ if and only if:
1. **Process Stage Eligibility:** Current stage $\in \{\text{Triage}, \text{Diagnosis Started}\}$. Cases in $\text{Diagnosis Complete}$ or later are hard-excluded (intervention effect $\Delta p \approx 0$).
2. **Execution Feasibility:** Remaining time to SLA deadline $T_i > 0.75\text{ hours}$ (minimum window required to schedule and execute a 45-minute swarm).
3. **Data Quality Gate:** Key features must be present. If live queue features are missing/imputed (e.g., I-06), the case is flagged for verification before slot commitment, and its estimated risk reduction is discounted by 25% to account for imputation uncertainty.

### 1.2 Calibration Adjustment
Observed raw model outputs ($p_{\text{now}}$) are adjusted to account for known segment-level calibration bias before making allocation decisions:
*   **SME Segment:** $p_{\text{adj}} = \min\left(1.0, \, p_{\text{now}} + 0.065\right)$ (median correction for 0.04–0.09 underprediction).
*   **Strategic Enterprise:** $p_{\text{adj}} = \max\left(0.0, \, p_{\text{now}} - 0.085\right)$ (median correction for 0.05–0.12 overprediction).
*   **Public Sector:** $p_{\text{adj}} = p_{\text{now}}$ (unbiased, acceptable calibration).
*   **Non-Native-Language Support:** $p_{\text{adj}} = p_{\text{now}}$, but treatment effect uncertainty bounds are widened by $\pm 20\%$.

### 1.3 Alert Persistence and Minimum Evidence Change
To prevent alert fatigue and redundant interventions:
*   **Suppression:** If a case has generated an alert in a prior 30-minute refresh cycle and received no intervention, subsequent alerts are suppressed **unless**:
    $$\text{New Event Flag} = \text{TRUE} \quad \text{OR} \quad (p_{\text{adj, now}} - p_{\text{adj, prev}}) \ge +0.15$$
*   **Hard Restraint on Repeat Swarms:** A case that has already received a Senior Diagnostic Swarm cannot receive another within the same lifecycle unless a verified, materially new customer event occurs (e.g., structural scope change, new diagnostic logs).

### 1.4 Prioritization Metric: Net Expected Policy Value ($EV_{\text{net}}$)
Eligible cases are ranked using the Net Expected Value formula, explicit for direct benefit, operational costs, false-alarm burden, and queue spillover externalities:

$$EV_{\text{net}, i} = \left[ \Delta \hat{p}_i \times V_{\text{breach}, i} \right] - C_{\text{swarm}} - B_{\text{FA}, i} - E[\text{Spillover Harm}_i]$$

Where:
*   $\Delta \hat{p}_i$: Estimated absolute risk reduction (midpoint of context-adjusted range).
*   $V_{\text{breach}, i}$: Financial and contractual value of avoiding breach.
*   $C_{\text{swarm}} = €450$ (fixed labor/coordination cost).
*   $B_{\text{FA}, i}$: False-alarm disruption burden incurred if the case would not have breached absent intervention, approximated as $(1 - p_{\text{adj}, i}) \times \text{False-Alarm Burden}_i$.
*   $E[\text{Spillover Harm}_i]$: Expected penalty imposed on untreated diagnosis cases due to senior specialist interruption:
    $$E[\text{Spillover Harm}] = N_{\text{untreated, diag}} \times \Delta p_{\text{spillover}} \times \bar{V}_{\text{diag\_queue}} \approx 6 \times 0.02 \times €15,000 = €1,800 \text{ per slot}$$

## 5. Fairness and Feedback-Loop Risks

### 5.1 Systemic Risks Identified
1.  **Lower-Value Account Starvation:** Prioritizing purely by $EV_{\text{net}}$ concentrates scarce senior swarms onto Strategic Enterprise accounts (e.g., I-04, I-08), systematically starving SME and Public Sector accounts of intervention support.
2.  **Uncertainty Disadvantage (Non-Native Language Support):** Sparse validation data yields wider confidence intervals. Risk-averse policies under Adverse scenarios will penalize these cases unless explicit variance bounds are factored in.
3.  **Differential Calibration Distortion:** Unadjusted model usage would over-allocate swarms to Strategic Enterprise (overpredicted risk) and under-allocate to SMEs (underpredicted risk).
4.  **Selective Intervention / Feedback Loop in Training Labels:** Intervening on high-risk cases lowers their observed breach rate. Retraining future predictive models on this observational data without intervention indicators will cause the model to incorrectly learn that high-risk features lead to low breach rates (treatment-confounding bias).
5.  **Queue Externalities (Spillover Inequity):** Swarming selected cases delays standard diagnosis for non-swarmed cases in the shared queue, disproportionately raising breach risks for lower-priority cases that are already operating near SLA thresholds.

### 5.2 Concrete Policy Safeguards
*   **Equity Allocation Floor (SME / Public Sector Reserve):** Reserve at least 1 of the daily swarm slots specifically for non-Strategic Enterprise cases that meet a minimum risk threshold ($p_{\text{adj}} \ge 0.50$).
*   **Causal Counterfactual Logging:** Store explicit binary flags `Intervention_Applied` and `Intervention_Timestamp` alongside model predictions in the feature store. Require causal inference modeling (e.g., Inverse Propensity Weighting or S-Learners) during model retraining cycles to prevent label distortion.
*   **Epsilon-Greedy Exploration (Off-Policy Learning):** Allocate 5–10% of monthly swarm slots to a randomized control protocol across medium-risk cases ($0.35 \le p_{\text{adj}} \le 0.60$) to continuously measure true CATE (Conditional Average Treatment Effect) across sparse segments like Non-Native-Language Support.
*   **Spillover Penalty Integration:** Mandate the subtraction of $E[\text{Spillover Harm}]$ in the priority score calculation to prevent interventions whose direct benefit is offset by portfolio-wide queue degradation.

## 6. Monitoring and Recalibration Plan

### 6.1 Multi-Tier Monitoring Matrix

```
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                            MONITORING DASHBOARD                                   |
+----------------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| OPERATIONAL METRICS              | PREDICTIVE & CALIBRATION                       |
| - Swarm Slot Utilization Rate    | - Segment Brier Scores & Calibration Slope     |
| - Queue Delay Added (Min/Slot)   | - Feature Attribution Drift (PSI / CSI)        |
| - Specialist Interruption Hours  | - Imputation Error Rates (Live Queue Features) |
+----------------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
| CAUSAL & POLICY EFFECTIVENESS    | EQUITY & FAIRNESS                              |
| - CATE Estimates by Segment      | - SLA Breach Rate Ratio (SME vs Enterprise)    |
| - False Alarm Disruption Rate    | - Swarm Allocation Share per Segment           |
| - Reserve Slot Burn Rate         | - Spillover Burden Distribution                |
+----------------------------------+------------------------------------------------+
```

*   **Operational Monitoring (Real-Time):** Track swarm slot burn rate, average delay added to non-swarmed cases in the diagnosis queue (alert trigger if delay > 25 min/slot), and reserve slot consumption prior to 15:00.
*   **Predictive Monitoring (Weekly):** Track Brier score, reliability curves, and Expected Calibration Error (ECE) across SME, Strategic Enterprise, Public Sector, and Non-Native Support segments. Flag feature drift using Population Stability Index (PSI).
*   **Causal-Effect Monitoring (Monthly):** Estimate true treatment efficacy ($\Delta p$) using Quasi-Experimental designs (e.g., Regression Discontinuity around the policy eligibility threshold) combined with randomized holdouts.
*   **Fairness & Spillover Audit (Bi-weekly):** Compute the ratio of breach rates between untreated SME cases and untreated Strategic Enterprise cases to ensure queue externalities are not causing disproportionate harm to smaller accounts.

### 6.2 Recalibration and Policy Update Triggers
1.  **Automatic Model Recalibration:** Triggered if segment ECE exceeds $0.05$ or if a segment’s mean prediction error exceeds $0.04$ over a 14-day rolling window.
2.  **Allocation Threshold Adjustment:** If daily average queue congestion adds $> 20\text{ minutes}$ of aggregate delay per slot, automatically raise the minimum required $EV_{\text{net}}$ threshold for intervention by 25% to account for heightened spillover penalties.
3.  **Dynamic Reserve Modification:** If reserve slot usage for unlisted/regulatory incidents falls below 30% over a rolling 30-day period, release the mandatory reserve slot for general portfolio allocation after 14:00 daily.
