# 5. Limitations of available attributes

- **No capture of "genuine need" vs. "request" for support**: The log records `language_preference`, `disability_accommodation_requested`, and variant steps (e.g., Language Support Contact), but does not record whether the request was made by the applicant or auto-triggered by system rules, nor whether the request aligns with actual need (e.g., applicant needs language support but didn't request it). - **Missing applicant-perceived wait/burden data**: `time:timestamp` gives objective timing, but not applicant experience (e.g., "felt waited long", "had to repeat explanation"). A 3.8-day wait for Eligibility after Language Support Contact may be acceptable if communicated clearly; it may feel burdensome if applicant receives no status update. Log has no field for communication (e.g., "sent notification", "contacted applicant"), so we cannot assess quality/accessibility of support beyond step presence and wait time. Thus, "quality/accessibility" dimension can only observe step occurrence and wait length, not perceived fairness.
