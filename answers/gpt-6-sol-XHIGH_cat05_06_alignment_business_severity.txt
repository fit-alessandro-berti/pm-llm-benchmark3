## 1. Materiality Framework

Use the alignment to identify *where the recorded trace differs from the payable-claim model*, then assess the business facts separately. Severity is an ordinal judgment, not a conversion of edit cost or a claim that the full amount was lost:

| Severity | Business meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 — Critical | Payment was released before a mandatory control, or a high-consequence mandatory control was bypassed; immediate exposure or diversion is plausible. |
| 4 — High | A paid claim used an ineligible approval route, with material exposure, but without evidence of an earlier bank release than the recorded approval. |
| 3 — Moderate | A meaningful control or customer-communication evidence gap needs prompt resolution; the underlying activity may have occurred. |
| 2 — Low | Likely model, mapping, or correlation defect, or a limited unresolved obligation, with no demonstrated improper payment. |
| 1 — Minimal | Benign telemetry, retry duplication, or a fitting silent routing step. |

For each case, consider the control objective and whether the *applicable* approval preceded bank release; amount and possible loss, not merely claim face value; whether payment can be reversed; timing relative to the seven-day bank-change and 24-hour notification windows; claimant, fraud, regulatory, and audit effects; recurrence; reliability of source records and identifiers; and whether the case belongs in a model built for payable claims. Recent bank changes can make even a small payment urgent. A later approval does not cure an earlier release. Severity describes the consequence and urgency *if the best-supported interpretation is true*. Confidence describes how strongly the evidence supports that interpretation and its proposed cause: a low-confidence, high-severity case still warrants investigation. Missing log events establish an evidence gap, not proof that an activity never happened.

## 6. Why Alignment Cost Is Not Business Risk

The configured cost is the sum of unit penalties for *visible unmatched log and model moves* under one control-flow tree. It has no amount, risk, beneficiary history, payment finality, customer harm, source reliability, or policy-weighted term. It is a diagnostic distance, not a probability of breach or monetary loss.

CL-609 and CL-610 cost zero because all activities match the tree, yet their Auto Approve decisions violate P1; CL-610 also violates P2/P3 in the bank-change window. CL-605 also costs zero, but for a different reason: its `tau` is a fitting hidden transition and the low-value auto path is eligible. A zero cost therefore needs a data-aware policy check. CL-601 costs 3 solely for UI telemetry, and CL-603 costs 1 for a same-ID transport retry; activity granularity and duplicate logging inflate distance without comparable harm. CL-607 and CL-612 cost 4 because a payable-only model lacks denial/withdrawal endings; model scope explains most of those edits. CL-611 costs 2 for a possible label alias, while CL-606 costs 1 for a core event absent despite an external signed report: naming and cross-system evidence determine the meaning of those moves. CL-608 likewise may have a valid +3-hour portal notice, but its mutable link lowers evidential confidence. In contrast, CL-602's single missing Fraud Screen is critical in a EUR 480,000 HIGH-risk changed-bank claim. CL-604's cost 3 includes a bank-accepted payment before controls; the payment's timing and possible irreversibility matter far more than three unit edits. Alternative optimal alignments can distribute edits differently, but the supplied alignments remain the diagnostic inputs here.

## 7. PM4Py Reproduction and Aggregation Code

The code uses only the supplied traces and manually entered supplied move summaries. The diagnostic alignments it computes can use different optimal choices or formatting from the authoritative alignments above. The sample aggregates cover these 12 cases; the separate full-month counts in section 3 are not inferred from this sample.

```python
from collections import Counter
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

tree_text = (
    "-> ( 'Register Claim', 'Validate Coverage', 'Assess Loss', "
    "X ( -> ( 'Fraud Screen', 'Manual Approve' ), 'Auto Approve' ), "
    "'Pay Claim', 'Notify Claimant' )"
)
tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(tree_text)
net, initial_marking, final_marking = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)

traces = {
    "CL-601": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Open Workbench", "Assess Loss", "Autosave", "Auto Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant", "Close Workbench"],
    "CL-602": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Manual Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-603": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Auto Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-604": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Pay Claim", "Manual Approve", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-605": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Auto Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-606": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Fraud Screen", "Manual Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-607": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Deny Claim", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-608": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Fraud Screen", "Manual Approve", "Pay Claim"],
    "CL-609": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Auto Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-610": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Auto Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-611": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Damage", "Fraud Screen", "Manual Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant"],
    "CL-612": ["Register Claim", "Validate Coverage", "Assess Loss", "Withdraw Claim"],
}
origin = pd.Timestamp("2026-01-01T00:00:00Z")
events = [
    {"case:concept:name": case, "concept:name": activity,
     "time:timestamp": origin + pd.Timedelta(days=i, minutes=j)}
    for i, (case, trace) in enumerate(traces.items())
    for j, activity in enumerate(trace)
]
log = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    pd.DataFrame(events), case_id="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name", timestamp_key="time:timestamp"
)
diagnostics = pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_alignments(
    log, net, initial_marking, final_marking
)
print("PM4Py diagnostic count:", len(diagnostics))
print("First PM4Py diagnostic:", diagnostics[0] if len(diagnostics) else None)
print("PM4Py may return alternative optimal alignments or a different move format;")
print("use the supplied moves and costs below for the business assessment.")

# These are the supplied authoritative move summaries, not parsed from PM4Py output.
# A severity number is an explicit business judgment; it is not calculated from cost.
def record(case, amount, risk, pattern, log_moves=(), model_moves=(), tau=0,
           raw_cost=0, flags=(), severity=1, confidence="High"):
    assert raw_cost == len(log_moves) + len(model_moves)
    return dict(case=case, amount_eur=amount, risk_level=risk, pattern=pattern,
                log_moves=list(log_moves), model_moves=list(model_moves),
                silent_tau=tau, raw_cost=raw_cost, policy_flags=list(flags),
                severity_judgment=severity, confidence=confidence)

supplied = [
    record("CL-601", 1200, "LOW", "UI telemetry", ["Open Workbench", "Autosave", "Close Workbench"], raw_cost=3),
    record("CL-602", 480000, "HIGH", "prepayment control evidence", model_moves=["Fraud Screen"], raw_cost=1, flags=["P2_screen_unverified"], severity=5, confidence="Medium"),
    record("CL-603", 3200, "LOW", "notification retry", log_moves=["Notify Claimant"], raw_cost=1),
    record("CL-604", 90000, "MEDIUM", "premature payment", log_moves=["Pay Claim"], model_moves=["Fraud Screen", "Pay Claim"], raw_cost=3, flags=["P2_screen_unverified", "P3_early_pay_confirmed"], severity=5),
    record("CL-605", 800, "LOW", "silent routing", tau=1),
    record("CL-606", 75000, "HIGH", "external assessment", model_moves=["Assess Loss"], raw_cost=1, flags=["external_evidence_check"], severity=2, confidence="Medium-high"),
    record("CL-607", None, "not stated", "nonpayable outcome", log_moves=["Deny Claim"], model_moves=["Assess Loss", "Auto Approve", "Pay Claim"], raw_cost=4, flags=["scope_disputed"], severity=2, confidence="Medium"),
    record("CL-608", 250000, "HIGH", "cross-channel notice", model_moves=["Notify Claimant"], raw_cost=1, flags=["P4_link_unverified"], severity=3, confidence="Medium"),
    record("CL-609", 18000, "MEDIUM", "ineligible auto approval", raw_cost=0, flags=["P1_breach"], severity=4),
    record("CL-610", 4500, "LOW", "ineligible auto approval", raw_cost=0, flags=["P1_breach", "P2_breach", "P3_breach", "recent_bank_change"], severity=5),
    record("CL-611", 62000, "HIGH", "assessment label mapping", log_moves=["Assess Damage"], model_moves=["Assess Loss"], raw_cost=2, flags=["label_equivalence_check"], severity=2, confidence="Medium-high"),
    record("CL-612", 2000, "LOW", "nonpayable outcome", log_moves=["Withdraw Claim"], model_moves=["Auto Approve", "Pay Claim", "Notify Claimant"], raw_cost=4, flags=["scope_disputed", "P4_applicability_unknown"], severity=2, confidence="Medium"),
]
cases = pd.DataFrame(supplied)
cases["move_signature"] = cases.apply(
    lambda r: " | ".join(["L:" + x for x in r.log_moves] +
                         ["M:" + x for x in r.model_moves] +
                         (["T:tau"] * r.silent_tau)) or "all synchronous", axis=1
)
print("\nSupplied move summaries:")
print(cases[["case", "move_signature", "raw_cost", "amount_eur", "risk_level",
             "policy_flags", "severity_judgment", "confidence"]].to_string(index=False))

move_counts = Counter(
    ("L", move) for row in supplied for move in row["log_moves"]
) + Counter(("M", move) for row in supplied for move in row["model_moves"])
print("\nVisible move counts:", dict(move_counts))

pattern_evidence = cases.groupby("pattern", as_index=False).agg(
    sample_cases=("case", "count"),
    case_ids=("case", lambda s: ", ".join(s)),
    total_raw_cost=("raw_cost", "sum"),
    raw_cost_range=("raw_cost", lambda s: f"{s.min()}–{s.max()}"),
    stated_amount_eur=("amount_eur", "sum"),
    risk_levels=("risk_level", lambda s: ", ".join(sorted(set(s)))),
    policy_flags=("policy_flags", lambda s: ", ".join(sorted(set(
        flag for flags in s for flag in flags))) or "none"),
    severity_range=("severity_judgment", lambda s: f"{s.min()}–{s.max()}"),
)
print("\nSample pattern evidence (amount sum excludes unstated amounts):")
print(pattern_evidence.to_string(index=False))

# The policy flags are also aggregated independently of alignment move pattern.
flagged = cases.explode("policy_flags")
flagged["policy_flags"] = flagged["policy_flags"].fillna("none")
print("\nPolicy-overlay flag counts:")
print(flagged.groupby("policy_flags")["case"].nunique().to_string())

# Evidence table for human prioritization: sorting displays the stated judgment;
# it does not derive severity from amount, risk, flags, or alignment cost.
risk_evidence = cases[["case", "severity_judgment", "confidence", "raw_cost",
                       "amount_eur", "risk_level", "policy_flags", "pattern"]]
risk_evidence = risk_evidence.sort_values(
    ["severity_judgment", "case"], ascending=[False, True]
)
print("\nRisk-prioritization evidence table:")
print(risk_evidence.to_string(index=False))
```
