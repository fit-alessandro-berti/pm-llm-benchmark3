*Explanation (120words):*  
Supported arrows: `complexity  routing_rule` (routing engine selects group after classification), `routing_rule  resource_group` (assignment), `arrival_load  queue_wait` and `shift  queue_wait` (higher load or night shift increase queueing), `complexity  compliance_required` (policy links compliance to complexity), `compliance_required  compliance_performed` (always performed when required), `compliance_performed  queue_wait` (compliance step can add waiting), `resource_group  review_service` and `resource_group  rework` (group composition can affect service speed and need for rework), `queue_wait  cycle_time`, `review_service  cycle_time`, `rework  cycle_time`. Assumptions: `unobserved_reviewer_skill  review_service` (skill not measured), `unobserved_case_risk  compliance_required` (risk may trigger extra compliance), `case_completed  cycle_time` (selection bias – only completed cases observed). A plausible feedback not captured by the static DAG: if queues grow large, the routing rule might preferentially assign future cases to underloaded groups, creating a timevarying relationship between queue length and `resource_group` that the DAG suppresses.

**Final recommendation (120200words):**  
Do **not** implement the proposed 50% blanket reassignment of Orion cases to Nova. Instead, launch Phase1 (small randomized pilot) that moves ~10% of night Orion cases to Nova while preserving all other routing rules and staffing. Simultaneously run Phase2 (temporary daytime Orion reviewers) to relieve nightqueue pressure. Use the builtin processmining measurements (`wait_before_review_h`, `review_service_h`, `cycle_time_h`, `rework_count`) and propensityscore matching to estimate the conditional effect of group assignment on cycle time by complexity and shift. Apply clear rollback thresholds (20% cycle increase for Novareceiving Orion night cases *or* rework >30%). After the pilot (2weeks), examine overlap diagnostics; if the conditional Orion effect is consistently positive (i.e., Orion adds time) for nightcomplex cases, consider a targeted policy that only reassigns lowcomplexity Orion night cases to Nova. If Orion appears neutral or beneficial for complex night work, keep Orion night capacity intact. This staged learning approach reduces operational risk, generates causal evidence under realistic conditions, and avoids a blunt rule that could disrupt needed specialist coverage.

## 8. Residual uncertainty
1. **Unobserved reviewer skill** – Adding a field `reviewer_skill_level` (e.g., certified vs experienced) would clarify whether Orion’s advantage comes from higher skill.  
2. **Arrivalload granularity** – Recording a numeric load index (e.g., 0–2 scale) rather than categorical “High/Normal” would improve mediation analysis of queue dynamics.  
3. **Abandonment status** – A binary `case_status` (completed vs abandoned/open) would allow estimation of selection bias on cycle time.  
4. **Nightqueue state** – Measuring `queue_wait_start_h` or `queue_length_at_classification` would enable frontdoorstyle tests of shiftmediated effects.  
5. **Compliance difficulty** – A short `compliance_complexity_score` (e.g., 1–3) would refine mediation through requestinfo loops and reduce ambiguity around `compliance_required`.

Each uncertainty corresponds to a single, actionable data field or design change.
