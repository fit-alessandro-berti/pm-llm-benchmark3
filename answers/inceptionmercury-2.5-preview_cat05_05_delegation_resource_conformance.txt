## 2. Rule Interpretation

*   **RR1:** Draft/Approve resources must differ at case level.
*   **RR2:** Implement/Verify resources must differ at case level.
*   **RR3:** HIGH-risk Approval: QM or valid delegate. Delegation requires active interval, scope match, principal absence, and no subdelegation.
*   **RR4:** Emergency HIGH: IC/IC-delegate approval OK. Retrospective QM review must occur within two business days post-event.
*   **RR5:** Service accounts require explicit human attribution (e.g., FIDO token, verified workstation).
*   **RR6:** Patterns indicate suspicion but do not prove collusion.

**Business Days:** Counts exclude weekends (Sat/Sun). 2026-08-12 to 14 are business days.
**Leave:** An action by a principal on leave is valid if credentials are verified and authorized.
**Database Creation:** Post-event record creation does not prove prior validity unless timestamped/signed before the event.
