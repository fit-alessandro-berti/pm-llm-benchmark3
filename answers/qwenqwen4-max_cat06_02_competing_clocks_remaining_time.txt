## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **QA backlog and discipline at the future join** — *queue uncertainty* (with a service-time component). Expected 3–6 open batches Monday 08:00; expedite vs FIFO swings the P50 by **5–15 h** and decides whether the 20:00 cliff is hit. Largest effect on the central mass.
2. **Chemistry queue discipline + Friday spillover** — *queue uncertainty / calendar uncertainty*. FIFO vs risk-priority moves chemistry usable from Mon ~09:00 to Mon ~15:30, i.e. moves the **identity of the binding branch** and ~6 h of P50, plus a 10–15% Tuesday spillover.
3. **Unknown temperature excursion (common cause)** — *dependence uncertainty* (observation uncertainty in origin). Confirmed  rerun ~2.5×, contamination ~3×, investigation 0.070.42. Moves the mean ~+20–25 h and **P90 by 1–2 days**; independence would understate joint-anomaly probability by ~2×.
4. **Rework path selection: query vs doc-only vs lab investigation** — *path uncertainty*. Lab investigation (4–9%) is the only mechanism reaching >150 h; it single-handedly sets P95–P99. Doc-only vs lab split (60–80%) is itself uncertain.
5. **Microbiology staffing/authorization and contamination confirmation** — *calendar uncertainty* + path uncertainty. Authorization is nearly worthless on the smooth path (QA closed Sunday) but worth ~24 h once contamination is indicated; Monday-morning readout contention (0–1.5 h) can make micro bind even in the priority-chemistry case.

(Just outside the top five: QA service-time upper tail, p90 5.5 h, and Monday chemistry arrivals/preemption.)
