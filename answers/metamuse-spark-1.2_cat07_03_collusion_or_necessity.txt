# 2. Benign alternative explanations

**Pattern A: Buyer_Carla  Approver_Gita and Buyer_Dan  Manager_Hugo - fast 8-10 min approvals, four-eyes violations, 79-88% emergency_flag, subcontracting loops**

1.  *Emergency handling / informal specialization as designed rush channel.* Carla specializes in emergency medical supplies, Dan in facilities repairs; Gita is delegated approver for urgent cases, Hugo for exceptions/weekend. Fast approvals and repeated pairing reflect on-call roster and triage, not collusion. Holland loops are clarification under time pressure.
    *   *Supports:* `emergency_flag=True`, `purchase_category`=emergency medical / facilities repair, weekend/evening timestamps, low vendor concentration, amount_band mixed, documented emergency procedure allowing same-day approval.
    *   *Refutes:* Missing `Emergency Override` event, `emergency_flag=False` but still fast, approval timestamps outside business hours without on-call record, no linked clinical/maintenance ticket, `Retrospective Justification` late (>3 days) or absent for >20% of cases.
2.  *Data-quality / shared account inflation.* Four-eyes violations overstated because `org:resource="Approver_Gita"` is a shared account; true approver in `entered_by` / `approved_by` may be different person, and Bot_PO mapping error creates spurious same-resource appearance.
    *   *Supports:* `org:resource` != `entered_by`, `source_system="legacy_erp"`, assistants listed in detailed audit trail, training/support logs showing account sharing.
    *   *Refutes:* `entered_by` = same person as creator, or `approved_by` field confirms same human across both activities, access logs show single-user login.

**Pattern B: VendorMaster_Lena  AP_Kim - Change Vendor Bank Details  Payment Approval in 17 min, 9 four-eyes violations, 72% V-77x, 83% manual_upload, 68% high amount**

1.  *Legitimate mass-maintenance due to bank platform change.* Vendor family V-77x changed banking platform; many bank-detail changes were legitimate, centrally requested, manually uploaded in bulk, and payments were due, causing short intervals. Concentration and manual upload reflect project work, not targeting.
    *   *Supports:* External bank migration notice dated before changes, maintenance ticket for V-77x bulk update, identical requested-by/request reason across cases, split across regions (suggests central project not localized manipulation), `entered_by`  beneficiary, bank details match published vendor master.
    *   *Refutes:* No maintenance ticket linkage in log, bank details vary per case to different accounts, beneficiary name mismatches vendor register, payments to newly created accounts with no prior history, regions/accounts inconsistent with vendor's stated bank.

2.  *Role/process design and manual upload artifact.* Lena performs corrections after Kim's payment rejections, creating tight loop (LenaKimLena 11). `manual_upload` reflects migration of historic payments, not bypass. Fast timing is artefact of upload date, not actual processing time.
    *   *Supports:* `lifecycle:transition` shows rework/correction, invoice and payment events have same `time:timestamp` as upload date, `source_system="manual_upload"` and `quarter_close_upload` batch, original bank change timestamp in legacy system is earlier.
    *   *Refutes:* Original source timestamps still show <30 min gap, `Change Vendor Bank Details` created after `Invoice Match` but before payment with no correction reason, AP_Kim approved without independent vendor verification record.

**Pattern C: Emergency Override  Retrospective Justification by same person (17 cases, 12 batch-entered)**

1.  *Administrative catch-up / data-entry backlog.* Justifications were done verbally/on-time but batch-entered into system after quarter close by one person from a spreadsheet, creating identical timestamps and same `org:resource`.
    *   *Supports:* Spreadsheet with original dates found, `source_system="quarter_close_upload"`, identical timestamp to minute, `entered_by` vs `org:resource` mismatch, email/Teams evidence of timely review by second person.
    *   *Refutes:* No spreadsheet or independent evidence, justification text is generic/copy-pasted, no second-party signature, `entered_by` = same person who did override per access logs.

2.  *Workload/resource constraint on second approver.* Delegated approver Gita/Hugo covered both roles due to staff absence, with intent to remediate but no available alternate. Reflects resource gap, not concealment.
    *   *Supports:* Roster showing absence, delegation approval email, justification content substantive and case-specific.
    *   *Refutes:* No delegation record, repeated pattern beyond absence period.

**Pattern D: Temp_Nina  Buyer_Carla (65 cases, 4 min) Create Request  Assign Buyer**

1.  *End-of-quarter surge support.* Nina was end-of-quarter temp for data entry; 4-min handover reflects queue processing for emergency backlog, not preferential routing.
    *   *Supports:* `time:timestamp` clustered at quarter-end, `purchase_category` = emergency backlog, assignment follows round-robin but Carla had emergency specialization.
    *   *Refutes:* Assignment bypasses rule-based routing for non-emergency cases or disproportionately high-value cases to Carla.

# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Evidence that would INCREASE concern:**

*   **Field-level proof of same human control:** `entered_by` = `approved_by` = same human for Create PO + Approve PO or Change Bank Details + Payment Approval, despite `org:resource` vs Bot mapping, with access logs confirming single login, no shared-account defence.
*   **Vendor master audit:** `vendor_id` V-77x bank changes show new account holder name  registered vendor, or accounts belong to employees/related parties, or multiple vendors converge to same account, verified via vendor master history and bank confirmation letters.
*   **Missing authorization:** Emergency cases lack `Emergency Override` event or `Retrospective Justification` text is absent/generic/copy-pasted, or justification delay >3 working days with no delegation record, or `emergency_flag=True` without clinical/maintenance evidence.
*   **Source-system bypass:** `manual_upload` files have no bulk-maintenance ticket linkage, uploader = beneficiary approver, timestamps in legacy system still show <20 min bank-change-to-payment, and payment blocked-match exceptions were manually overridden by same approver (`approved_by`).
*   **Financial divergence:** `amount_band=high` payments to V-77x are split across regions in amounts just below approval thresholds, or invoice amounts  PO/goods receipt, with `Invoice Match` overridden by AP_Kim.
*   **Behavioral concealment:** Access logs show VendorMaster_Lena's account used from AP_Kim's workstation, or retrospective justifications back-dated.

**Evidence that would REDUCE concern:**

*   **Shared-account / data-quality confirmation:** `entered_by` clearly shows different humans than `org:resource`; 23 Approver_Gita events resolve to assistants; Bot_PO mapping error explains Create PO four-eyes violations; `quarter_close_upload` spreadsheet provides original timely dates for 12 justifications.
*   **Legitimate V-77x project:** Dated bank platform migration notice + approved mass-maintenance ticket linked to all 44 Lena/Kim cases, with old/new bank details uniform per vendor and validated by treasury, explaining concentration and manual upload.
*   **Emergency legitimacy:** Emergency cases have linked medical urgency or facilities work orders, on-call roster matches Carla/Gita and Dan/Hugo pairings, weekend timestamps, and `Retrospective Justification` by different qualified manager within SLA.
*   **Process design clarification:** Role documentation shows Vendor Check and Change Bank Details intentionally combined under VendorMaster role with compensating detective control elsewhere (e.g., payment requires Kim + second AP), and subcontracting loops correlate with documented rework (`lifecycle:transition=redo`).
*   **Timing explained by automation/queue:** Fast paths correspond to `lifecycle:transition=complete` after automated `Bot_Match` pre-check, waiting time before assignment was long but approval itself short, not indicative of insufficient review.

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Our review of the procurement-to-payment event log identified control exceptions requiring targeted follow-up, but no direct evidence of misconduct or collusion. Exceptions are unevenly distributed across resource pairs and are better explained by distinct mechanisms rather than a single pattern.

The most concerning subgroup is VendorMaster_Lena with AP_Kim involving vendor family V-77x. Nine cases breach the segregation between changing vendor bank details and payment approval, sixteen show unusually fast bank-change-to-payment (median 17 minutes versus 7.8 hours globally), and 83% originate from manual uploads with high amounts and concentrated vendor exposure. This combination increases potential impact and is not explained by emergency handling. Review of vendor master history, bank-change tickets, and source-system linkage for the V-77x migration is recommended.

Separate emergency-related exceptions were observed for Buyer_Carla with delegated approver Gita and Buyer_Dan with Manager_Hugo, including fast approvals (8-10 minutes) and 42 create-to-approve segregation breaches, largely flagged as emergencies. High co-working frequency in these pairs and in routine pairings Aline/Elena and Boris/Felix is consistent with informal specialization and on-call teaming, not by itself indicative of collusion. Seventeen retrospective justifications performed by the same person who made the emergency override breach the compensating control, but twelve were batch-entered after quarter close, limiting timestamp reliability.

Data-quality limitations constrain attribution: shared use of the Approver_Gita account, Bot-to-human mapping errors in the legacy ERP, and missing links between mass-maintenance tickets and procurement cases materially affect metrics. Findings represent control exceptions that merit verification; they do not establish intent. We recommend prioritized case-level testing of the V-77x manual-upload cluster first, sample testing of emergency approvals with independent `entered_by` verification, and remediation of account-mapping and timely justification controls.
