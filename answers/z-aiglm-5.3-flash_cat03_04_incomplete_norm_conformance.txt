## 1. Conformance semantics

The controlling principle is that **conformance status is a property of the norm, not of the mining tool**. PM4Py diagnostics tell us *what* relations exist and with what multiplicity; the norm tells us *what those multiplicities mean*. I distinguish seven classes:

- **Forbidden behavior.** These relations are the *only* truly closed-world part of the specification. Any observed occurrence is noncompliant as stated (N3, T6, E10), regardless of frequency, business plausibility, or suspected innocent cause. A plausible data-quality explanation changes the *remediation* (repair the pipeline vs. discipline the process) but never the initial conformance verdict.
- **Missing mandatory behavior.** Mandatory relations create a presumption of deviation only when (a) the stated scope demonstrably applies — e.g., N4 covers packages "intended for external shipment," not all packages; N1 covers "standard" orders — and (b) the abstraction used to test the rule is the right one. Where the summary tests an "eventually" rule with a directly-follows statistic (as N1 admits), the finding is provisional pending per-object verification.
- **Unexpected but unregulated behavior.** Relations absent from the norm sit in the open-world zone: they are annotated and monitored, never flagged as violations (T4, T5, N6, Package codeath Delivery).
- **Typical-pattern absence.** For N2, N5, T3, absence or non-directly-follows behavior is *conforming* when a recognized exception applies (write-off, credit terms, unapplied cash). These become findings only as "exceptions requiring documented rationale," not defects.
- **Likely data-quality error.** Evidence of a derivation artifact (joins on reused identifiers, dropped object references) rather than business behavior. This class can *cause* apparent violations (E10) or *mask* real ones (dropped Order references during Add Item).
- **Abstraction mismatch.** The summary construct (directly-follows, aggregate counts) cannot faithfully encode the norm's temporal or per-object semantics; the mismatch is charged to the measurement, not the process, until proven otherwise.
- **Insufficient evidence.** Aggregate counts cannot establish per-object chronology, identity of violating instances, or scope membership; these findings are parked pending drill-down.

Open-world vs. closed-world: for everything not marked forbidden — and for everything not inside a mandatory column within its scope — **absence from the norm is permission**, and presence is merely an observation. Only the three forbidden cells invert this: presence is condemnation, absence is silence.

## 3. Detailed reasoning by model family

### OC-DFG

An OC-DFG establishes, per object type, how frequently activity A directly precedes activity B, aggregated over all objects of that type. It can therefore substantiate N3-type prohibitions well, because the harm condition (dispatch following cancellation *on the same order*) is almost isomorphic to a directly-follows fact — the 2-orders count is actionable evidence. It cannot establish eventual properties: it cannot say whether the 2 Orders without CreateValidate *ever* validated, nor identify which physical orders or packages are implicated, nor verify scope qualifiers ("standard," "external") that determine the denominator. Denominators such as "98 of 100" and "198 of 200 externally shipped" are analyst-supplied annotations, not outputs of the graph; the graph alone yields raw follow frequencies.

The central hazard, made explicit by N1's own scoping note, is reading an **"eventually expected" rule as a directly-follows rule**. Validation delayed by an unrecorded or informal compliance review produces two phantom deviations at once: a missing CreateValidate edge and a spurious CreateManual Compliance Review edge. Conversely, the same object can be perfectly conforming at the eventual abstraction and alarming at the DF abstraction, or vice versa: ValidateIssue Invoice (72) looks like an unsanctioned shortcut under a naive reading of N2, yet N2's scope note declares interposition legitimate. Counts compound this: 198/200 could reflect two deviant packages, or three deviant ones with one masked by reordering. Sequence confidence must therefore decay as activity density between endpoints grows.

### OTG

The OTG establishes which object types stand in which relationship kinds and with what weight. Its strength is atomicity for T1/T2/T6: the single cobirth edge is visible here and nowhere else at this reliability. Its weaknesses: it cannot say *which event* produced an edge, whether temporal order within an interaction was compliant, or why family-level totals disagree. The instructive case is Package–Delivery: OTG interaction weight is 200 (fully satisfying T2) while ET-OT DispatchDelivery shows 198. The most parsimonious reconciliation is that two Deliveries attach through some other event type — at OTG level the object graph conforms, at ET-OT level a mandatory-column gap appears. Thus one abstraction certifies what another indicts, and only event-level drill-down resolves which is truthful. Cobirth edges likewise require the underlying event: the OTG proves a shared birth event exists; only the event determines whether it was a control-bypassing business step or a modeling artifact.

### ET–OT

ET–OT is the most atomic audit surface: each cell states whether events of a type touch objects of a type. Within stated scope, it makes E1–E8 gaps nearly indisputable — Add Item without Order (1448 vs. 1450) and Record Payment without Invoice (93 vs. 92) are structural, not aggregation, facts. It cannot express order, sequence, or repetition: a fully populated ET–OT is compatible with every N3 violation occurring. It is also where E10 bites hardest: the Sensor Heartbeat–Invoice cell violates E10 regardless of what the DF or OTG views show — and, being a *join artifact*, demonstrates how a data-quality defect manufactures closed-world guilt. Direction matters too: conforming at ET–OT (all E-cells filled) but suspicious at OC-DFG (packeddispatch breaks) describes finding 5; suspicious at ET–OT (E6, E2 gaps) but conforming at OTG (T1, T2 weights intact) describes findings 4 and 6. Triangulating the three families is not optional; each hides what another exposes.

## 6. Norm-completeness assessment

1. **No formal interposition semantics (N1/N2).** The DF-vs-eventual tension is only a prose aside. *Risk:* systematic false positives whenever informally recorded events intervene. *Clarification:* give every mandatory relation an `allowed_interpositions` list and an abstraction tag (`directly_follows` | `eventual`). Status: **mandatory-with-scoping**.
2. **Undefined scope qualifiers.** "Standard Order," "externally shipped," "intended for external shipment" lack attribute definitions, so denominators (100, 200) are unverifiable. *Risk:* mandatory gaps asserted against the wrong population. *Clarification:* bind each scope to named OCEL object attributes. Status: **mandatory within refined scope, unspecified otherwise**.
3. **Unapplied cash has no machine-readable convention** despite E8 referencing it. *Risk:* every legitimate unapplied payment registers as an E8 violation. *Clarification:* define event subtype/value carrying an exception code. Status: **mandatory with explicit exception carve-out**.
4. **Cobirth/codeath semantics declared only once (T6)**; codeath wholly ungoverned. *Risk:* ad-hoc verdicts on the 7 Package–Delivery codeath edges. *Clarification:* enumerate allowed relationship-kind × type-pair combinations; mark Package–Delivery codeath as sanctioned joint termination. Status: **typical** for that pair.
5. **Cancelled-order lifecycle underspecified.** N3 forbids dispatch after cancel but requires nothing. *Risk:* cancelled orders linger open; refunds untimely; the 2 violations were found by luck, not by design. *Clarification:* Cancel  (Close Order | Refund Recorded) eventually. Status: **typical** initially, **mandatory** once defined.
6. **No construction/derivation policy for edges.** E10 was violated by an analytical join, not by the operational system. *Risk:* conformance artifacts fabricated downstream. *Clarification:* forbid inferring edges into financial object types from non-source joins. Status: **forbidden as a derivation rule**.
7. **No cross-family reconciliation rules.** OTG 200 vs. ET-OT 198 Deliveries has no adjudication procedure. *Risk:* contradictory evidence treated as noise. *Clarification:* specify precedence and mandatory triage when families disagree. Status: **typical** investigative duty.
8. **Reopen loop (N6) left entirely open.** *Risk:* infinite reopen cycles pass unnoticed. *Clarification:* cap or track reopen chains per order. Status: start as **unspecified by design**, revisit with data.
