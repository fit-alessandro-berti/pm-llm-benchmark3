We compare P-884 SLA loss, false rejection, and asymmetric regulatory risk.
*   **Operational Loss (P-884):** Expected value €20k–€35k. Harm from delay (€15k–€80k) is lower than maximum compliance risk.
*   **Compliance Risk:** Asymmetric. Execution without Second Officer Review (Action A/B) carries €100k–€500k+ regulatory penalties plus non-monetary sanctions. This dominates any operational gain.
*   **False Rejection Harm:** Estimated €15k–€80k. This is strictly less than the maximum compliance risk but comparable to the compliance downside if a violation occurs.
*   **Dominance Argument:** Actions A and B are dominated due to non-conformance. Action C dominates Action D on time efficiency. Action F dominates G on certainty and speed. 
*   **Resource Cost:** P-901 breach probability increases by 0.12–0.28 (approximate value risk). If P-901 exposure exceeds €150k, Action F becomes preferred. Otherwise, C is optimal.
