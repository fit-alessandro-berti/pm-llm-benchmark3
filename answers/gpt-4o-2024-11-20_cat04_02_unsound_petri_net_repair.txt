# 1. Executive Verdict

- **Workflow Net**: The net satisfies structural conditions for a workflow net (unique source, unique sink, and all components reachable from source and lead to the sink).
- **Soundness**: The net is **not sound** because it permits scenarios where leftover tokens exist after completing the process. Specifically, waiver-based reviews can bypass mandatory synchronization with the analysis result, allowing premature authorization or leftover tokens.
- **Principal Defect**: Premature synchronization occurs when the waiver path (`p_waived`) bypasses `p_analysis_done` via `t_join_waived`.
- **Business Consequence**: The defect allows the case to proceed to authorization (`t_authorize`) without ensuring that `t_analyze` has completed, which violates the business rule requiring mandatory analysis.

---

# 2. Structural Diagnosis

| **Element or subnet** | **Intended role**                              | **Actual structural effect**                                | **Potential symptom**                                            |
|------------------------|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `t_split`             | Parallel start for analysis and review         | Properly splits tokens into `p_analysis` and `p_review`    | None                                                            |
| `t_analyze`           | Mandatory analysis execution                   | Functions correctly in isolation                            | May be skipped due to asynchronous `t_join_waived` handling.     |
| `t_standard_review`   | Standard review execution                      | Functions correctly                                         | None                                                            |
| `t_waive_review`      | Record waiver of review                       | Functions correctly                                         | None                                                            |
| `t_join_normal`       | Synchronize analysis and standard review       | Ensures synchronization when standard review is chosen      | None, properly handles normal path.                             |
| `t_join_waived`       | Synchronize analysis and waiver path           | Fails to require analysis completion before waiver-syncs    | Premature progression to `p_decision` when waiver bypasses `t_analyze` |
| Initial/final marking | Start or end state of the net                  | Proper initial and final markings defined                  | Leftover tokens possible in `p_analysis`, `p_review`, or `p_waived`. |
| Source-to-sink paths  | All nodes should lead from source to sink      | All elements are on a source-to-sink path                  | None                                                            |

# 3. Marking-Based Counterexample

### Counterexample 1: Premature authorization via waiver
Firing sequence:
1. `t_register`  `t_split`  `t_waive_review`  `t_join_waived`  `t_authorize`

Marking table:

| **Step** | **Fired transition** | **Resulting nonzero marking**       | **Enabled transitions**                     | **Observation**                                       |
|----------|-----------------------|-------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| 1        | `t_register`          | `{p_ready: 1}`                     | `t_split`                                   | Case registered, ready for parallel execution        |
| 2        | `t_split`             | `{p_analysis: 1, p_review: 1}`     | `t_analyze`, `t_standard_review`, `t_waive_review` | Both parallel branches initialized                  |
| 3        | `t_waive_review`      | `{p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1}`     | `t_analyze`, `t_join_waived`                | Waiver path selected, token moved to `p_waived`     |
| 4        | `t_join_waived`       | `{p_analysis: 1, p_decision: 1}`   | `t_analyze`, `t_authorize`                  | Bypass mandatory analysis, proceeding prematurely   |
| 5        | `t_authorize`         | `{sink: 1, p_analysis: 1}`         | None                                        | Process ends with leftover tokens in `p_analysis`. |

### Additional Defect: Analysis can lag behind
Here, `p_analysis` retains a token, violating the requirement for no remaining tokens at completion.

# 4. Minimal-Repair Alternatives

| **Repair** | **Exact edits**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | **Edit cost** | **Preserved intent**                             | **Changed behavior**                                    | **New risk**                                               |
|------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------|---------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1          | Merge waiver (`p_waived`) into `p_review_done` and remove `t_join_waived`.                                                                                                                                                                              | 1 arc removal, 1 arc addition, 1 transition removal | Mandatory analysis and review paths remain distinct     | All paths synchronize at `t_join_normal`                  | Misalignment if waiver needed a distinct end state          |
| 2          | Replace `t_join_waived` with a two-input transition that requires tokens from both `p_analysis_done` and `p_waived`.                                                                                                                                   | 1 transition modification, 1 arc added            | Mandatory analysis remains enforceable for waiver path | Waiver synchronization explicitly checks analysis token   | Complicates reachability analysis and execution tracking   |
| 3          | Make analysis optional only for waiver by adding an XOR-split before `p_analysis`, routing waiver directly to `p_decision` without synchronization.                                                                                                                                           | 2 transitions added, 3 arcs modified              | Simplifies waiver option                                | Analysis is no longer guaranteed for waiver conditions    | Business rule violation if analysis is later deemed critical |

### Justification of Minimality
The edit-cost model considers additions, modifications, or removals of arcs, places, and transitions. **Repair 1** has the lowest cost while maintaining the business rule. **Repair 2** is behaviorally distinct, with stricter synchronization for waiver paths, while **Repair 3** changes the business policy by making analysis optional under waiver.

# 5. Corrected PM4Py Code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet, Marking
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils import petri_utils

# Create the repaired net
repaired_net = PetriNet("parallel_review_with_waiver_repaired")

# Places
source = PetriNet.Place("source")
p_ready = PetriNet.Place("p_ready")
p_analysis = PetriNet.Place("p_analysis")
p_review = PetriNet.Place("p_review")
p_analysis_done = PetriNet.Place("p_analysis_done")
p_review_done = PetriNet.Place("p_review_done")
p_decision = PetriNet.Place("p_decision")
sink = PetriNet.Place("sink")

for p in {source, p_ready, p_analysis, p_review, p_analysis_done, p_review_done, p_decision, sink}:
    repaired_net.places.add(p)

# Transitions
register = PetriNet.Transition("t_register", "Register Case")
split = PetriNet.Transition("t_split", None)
analyze = PetriNet.Transition("t_analyze", "Perform Analysis")
standard_review = PetriNet.Transition("t_standard_review", "Perform Standard Review")
waive_review = PetriNet.Transition("t_waive_review", "Record Review Waiver")
join_normal = PetriNet.Transition("t_join_normal", None)
authorize = PetriNet.Transition("t_authorize", "Authorize Case")

for t in {register, split, analyze, standard_review, waive_review, join_normal, authorize}:
    repaired_net.transitions.add(t)

# Arcs
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(source, register, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(register, p_ready, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_ready, split, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(split, p_analysis, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(split, p_review, repaired_net)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_analysis, analyze, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(analyze, p_analysis_done, repaired_net)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_review, standard_review, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(standard_review, p_review_done, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_review, waive_review, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(waive_review, p_review_done, repaired_net)  # Waiver merged into review_done

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_analysis_done, join_normal, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_review_done, join_normal, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(join_normal, p_decision, repaired_net)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_decision, authorize, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(authorize, sink, repaired_net)

# Markings
repaired_im = Marking()
repaired_im[source] = 1

repaired_fm = Marking()
repaired_fm[sink] = 1
```

from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils import petri_utils
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet, Marking

# Helper to fire a transition and log marking changes
def fire_transition_check_enabled(net, marking, transition_name):
    enabled_transitions = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
    for transition in enabled_transitions:
        if transition.name == transition_name:
            petri_utils.execute_transition(net, transition, marking)
            print(f"Fired: {transition_name}, New marking: {marking}")
            return marking
    raise ValueError(f"Transition {transition_name} not enabled under marking {marking}")

# Original net diagnostics
print("\nOriginal Net Diagnostics:")
print(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)

### Expected Difference
The original net allows tokens to bypass analysis, violating business rules, whereas the repaired net enforces synchronization, ensuring no premature progression. The reachability graph of the repaired net should no longer have paths leading to leftover tokens.

# 7. Repair Trade-Offs and Residual Risks

1. **Waiver and Standard Review Distinction**: Both repairs retain this distinction but merge behaviors in post-review synchronization in **Repair 1**.
2. **Silent Transition Confusion**: The removal of `t_join_waived` in **Repair 1** reduces redundant transitions, simplifying the net.
3. **Soundness and Business Rule Alignment**: Soundness verification confirms structural and behavioral correctness but does not prove alignment with business rules. Regression testing must ensure cases always involve analysis.
4. **Testing**:
   - Positive traces: Parallel execution with analysis and review, waiver handling cases, normal synchronization behavior.
   - Negative traces: Premature authorization, token bypass scenarios.
5. **Bypass Detection**: Introduce unit tests verifying `t_authorize` requires tokens from both `p_analysis_done` and `p_review_done`.

---

# 8. Final Recommendation

**Selected Repair**: Repair 1 (waiver merged into `p_review_done`) is most deployable due to its simplicity, minimal edit cost, and clear enforcement of synchronization between review (standard or waiver) and analysis. 

**Fallback**: Repair 2, with strict waiver-analytics synchronization, is preferred where explicit safeguards are needed.

Deploy **Repair 1** if efficiency and minimalism are critical; use **Repair 2** for stringent policy enforcement at higher implementation cost.
