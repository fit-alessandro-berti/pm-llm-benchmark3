# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The supplied `source_powl` is authoritative. Its transitions are atomic events; “unordered” permits different schedules and concurrent enablement, without specifying task durations or proving simultaneous real-world execution. All nine visible activity labels are unique in the source. Marking-related invariants below are obligations on an accepting-net realization: the POWL itself defines completion of its nodes and contains no Petri-net marking pair.

Use these abbreviations in traces: `R` = Register Change, `A` = Assess Risk, `E` = Prepare Evidence, `L` = Legal Review, `C` = Configure Trial, `I` = Inspect Trial, `D` = Correct Defect, `X` = Release Change, and `H` = Archive Change. `tau` means a transition whose label is `None`. Witnesses below are complete visible traces unless explicitly described as a state or execution property. A positive witness demonstrates an allowed case; it does not by itself prove the invariant universally.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Registration occurs exactly once, before all other visible activities. | `R A E C I X H` | `A R E C I X H`; `R R A E C I X H` | Yes, for visible completed executions. |
| S2 | Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence each occur once after registration, with no order between them. | Both `R A E C I X H` and `R E A C I X H`; a reachable state enabling A and E together. | A model forbidding either witness adds an order. `R A C I X H` omits required E. | Both orders are observable. True concurrency needs state/step or structural evidence beyond a trace set. |
| S3 | If Legal Review occurs, it occurs once, after Assess Risk. | `R A L E C I X H` | `R L A E C I X H`; `R A E L L C I X H` | Yes. |
| S4 | The optional-legal XOR chooses exactly one of L and the silent skip. Skipping legal remains possible. | Both `R A E L C I X H` and `R A E C I X H` | A model requiring L in every complete execution, or permitting both XOR branches in one execution. | Presence/absence of L is observable; the exact silent choice and its timing are not. |
| S5 | Verification starts only after both A and E complete. | `R E A C I X H` | `R A C E I X H`; `R E C A I X H` | Yes, through precedence of A and E before the first C. |
| S6 | There is no order between optional legal and verification. E and L are also unordered once A is complete. | `R A L E C I X H`, `R A E C L I X H`, and `R E A C I L X H`; states enabling E/L and L/C together. | A model requiring L before C, L after I, or E before L excludes an allowed schedule. | Interleavings are observable. Independence and concurrent enablement need additional evidence. |
| S7 | Every verification iteration performs C followed by I; unrelated L can occur between them. | `R A E C L I X H` | `R A E I C X H`; `R A E C C I X H` | Yes. “Followed by” does not require adjacency in the global trace. |
| S8 | The binary loop has visible language `C I (D C I)*` after projecting away L. D separates successive complete bodies; it is neither an initial action nor an exit action. | `R A E C I D C I X H` | `R A E D C I X H`; `R A E C I C I X H`; `R A E C I D X H` | Yes for visible loop scope; internal silent entry/exit routing is hidden. |
| S9 | At least one verification body occurs. | `R A E C I X H` | `R A E X H` | Yes. |
| S10 | Release occurs once and waits for both the optional-legal subprocess and the loop to terminate. If L is chosen, L precedes X; the last loop body precedes X. | `R A E C I L X H`; a completed skip execution reaching the join before X. | `R A E C X I H`; `R A E C I X L H`; release with an unfinished redo branch. | Visible precedence is observable. Completion of the silent skip and absence of leftover branch tokens require marking/state checks. |
| S11 | Archive occurs once after release and is the final visible activity. A successful execution reaches exactly the accepting final marking, without residual tokens. | `R A E C I X H`, followed by any required silent completion routing and exact final marking. | `R A E C I H X`; `R A E C I X H L`; a marking containing the final token plus another token. | Visible finality is observable; exact completion and residual tokens are not visible in labels alone. |

An independent visible-language specification is: choose `n >= 1` iterations and either zero or one L; order the verification chain as `C I (D C I)^(n-1)`; place A and E after R and before the first C; place L, if chosen, after A and before X, anywhere relative to E and the verification chain; finish with X then H. This specification will serve as an oracle independent of the POWL-to-net converter.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary:** POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN → accepting Petri net | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | Uses the provided POWL conversion interface; Petri nets can retain the partial-order dependencies, optional skip, and loop. BPMN offers a stakeholder diagram. | Gateway translation or marking reconstruction may change behavior; silent routing can obscure dependencies. | Exception, missing markings, failed diagnostics, or invariant/language mismatch. Successful object construction alone is insufficient. |
| **Conditional alternative:** POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN → accepting Petri net | Same initial net conversion; `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_bpmn(tree)`; `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | A successfully validated tree can offer simpler hierarchical presentation. | The overlapping dependencies are not naturally block structured; restructuring, activity duplication, or serialization can change concurrency or identity. | Tree conversion raises an exception, or a produced tree/BPMN fails the same semantic audit. No fallback that silently changes the source is allowed. |

The induced order on A, E, L, and the first C, when L is chosen, contains `A < L`, `A < C`, and `E < C`, but no order between A/E, E/L, or L/C. This is an N-shaped partial order, which is an obstruction to representing the same event structure with a simple sequence/parallel hierarchy having one occurrence per activity. Optionality and looping do not remove that obstruction.

A process-tree conversion exception is therefore plausible and acceptable. It establishes that the converter did not obtain a supported tree, not that no expression with duplicated activities could reproduce the visible language. Enumerating alternative interleavings with XOR can preserve some trace language while changing concurrency and activity identity. The primary pipeline avoids making a tree a prerequisite. No direct POWL-to-BPMN API is assumed.

```python
from collections import Counter
from dataclasses import dataclass
from pathlib import Path
from typing import Any, Optional
import json
import traceback
import pm4py


@dataclass
class NetArtifact:
    name: str
    net: Any
    im: Any
    fm: Any
    workflow_net: Optional[bool] = None
    sound: Optional[bool] = None
    soundness_details: Any = None

    @property
    def marked(self):
        return self.net is not None and self.im is not None and self.fm is not None


@dataclass
class ConversionBundle:
    nets: dict
    bpmn_models: dict
    tree: Any
    failures: list
    output_dir: Path


def soundness_flag(result):
    # PM4Py commonly returns (boolean, diagnostics). Do not bool() that tuple:
    # bool((False, details)) would incorrectly report success.
    flag = result[0] if isinstance(result, (tuple, list)) and result else result
    if not isinstance(flag, bool):
        raise TypeError("Unrecognized check_soundness result: " + repr(result))
    return flag


def convert_and_audit(source_powl, output_dir="audit_artifacts"):
    out = Path(output_dir)
    out.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)
    nets, bpmn_models, failures = {}, {}, []
    tree_from_net = None

    def failure(stage, exc, interpretation):
        entry = {
            "stage": stage,
            "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
            "message": str(exc),
            "interpretation": interpretation,
            "traceback": traceback.format_exc(),
        }
        failures.append(entry)
        print(f"[{stage}] {entry['exception_type']}: {entry['message']}")
        print(interpretation)

    def save(stage, function, *args):
        try:
            function(*args)
        except Exception as exc:
            failure(stage, exc, "Visualization failed; no image is evidence of fidelity.")

    def register_net(name, net, im, fm, filename):
        artifact = NetArtifact(name, net, im, fm)
        nets[name] = artifact
        if net is None:
            failure(name, ValueError("Conversion returned no net"),
                    "Quarantine this artifact; structural and behavioral tests cannot run.")
            return artifact
        try:
            wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
            if not isinstance(wf, bool):
                raise TypeError("Unrecognized workflow-net result: " + repr(wf))
            artifact.workflow_net = wf
            print(name, "workflow_net:", wf)
        except Exception as exc:
            failure(name + ":workflow", exc, "Workflow-net status is unknown.")

        if not artifact.marked:
            failure(name + ":markings", ValueError("Initial or final marking is None"),
                    "This is not a usable accepting net. Do not infer or replace markings.")
            return artifact

        # Even a structurally non-workflow net receives a soundness diagnostic;
        # an exception or False result is retained, never converted into success.
        try:
            raw = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
            artifact.soundness_details = raw
            artifact.sound = soundness_flag(raw)
            print(name, "sound:", artifact.sound)
        except Exception as exc:
            failure(name + ":soundness", exc, "Soundness is unknown; quarantine pending review.")

        if artifact.workflow_net is not True or artifact.sound is not True:
            print(name, "is quarantined by diagnostics; generated views remain candidates.")
        save(name + ":visualization", pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
             net, im, fm, str(out / filename))
        return artifact

    save("source:visualization", pm4py.save_vis_powl,
         source_powl, str(out / "source_powl.svg"))

    try:
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)
    except Exception as exc:
        failure("powl_to_net", exc, "The required primary accepting net was not produced.")
        raise

    baseline = register_net("powl_net", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl,
                            "powl_accepting_net.svg")
    if not baseline.marked:
        raise ValueError("Cannot continue from a POWL net with missing markings")

    # Direct marked-net route. Its result is still subject to round-trip tests.
    try:
        bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
        if bpmn_from_net is None:
            raise ValueError("BPMN conversion returned None")
        bpmn_models["direct"] = bpmn_from_net
        save("direct_bpmn:visualization", pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
             bpmn_from_net, str(out / "bpmn_direct_from_powl_net.svg"))
    except Exception as exc:
        failure("net_to_bpmn", exc, "The primary BPMN route failed; retain the source and net.")

    # A failed tree conversion is an explicit outcome, not a reason to serialize
    # unordered source branches or manufacture an approximate tree.
    try:
        tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
            net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
        )
        if tree_from_net is None:
            raise ValueError("Process-tree conversion returned None")
    except Exception as exc:
        tree_from_net = None
        failure("net_to_tree", exc,
                "No supported tree was obtained. Inspect the exception and the N-shaped "
                "dependencies; this does not prove universal non-expressibility.")

    if tree_from_net is not None:
        save("tree:visualization", pm4py.save_vis_process_tree,
             tree_from_net, str(out / "tree_from_powl_net.svg"))
        # Also audit the tree's accepting-net semantics independently of its BPMN.
        try:
            nt, it, ft = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net)
            register_net("tree_net", nt, it, ft, "tree_accepting_net.svg")
        except Exception as exc:
            failure("tree_to_net", exc, "The produced tree lacks an audited accepting-net view.")
        try:
            bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
            if bpmn_from_tree is None:
                raise ValueError("Tree-to-BPMN conversion returned None")
            bpmn_models["via_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
            save("tree_bpmn:visualization", pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
                 bpmn_from_tree, str(out / "bpmn_via_process_tree.svg"))
        except Exception as exc:
            failure("tree_to_bpmn", exc, "The alternative BPMN route failed.")

    # Attempt every produced BPMN, including any later quarantined by validation.
    for route, bpmn_model in bpmn_models.items():
        try:
            nr, ir, fr = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_model)
            register_net("roundtrip_" + route, nr, ir, fr,
                         "roundtrip_" + route + "_accepting_net.svg")
        except Exception as exc:
            failure("bpmn_to_net:" + route, exc,
                    "This BPMN cannot undergo accepting-net round-trip validation.")

    summary = {
        "nets": {
            name: {
                "markings_present": artifact.marked,
                "initial_marking": repr(artifact.im),
                "final_marking": repr(artifact.fm),
                "workflow_net": artifact.workflow_net,
                "sound": artifact.sound,
                "soundness_details": repr(artifact.soundness_details),
                "visible_label_counts": dict(Counter(
                    t.label for t in artifact.net.transitions if t.label is not None
                )) if artifact.net is not None else {},
            }
            for name, artifact in nets.items()
        },
        "tree_produced": tree_from_net is not None,
        "bpmn_routes_produced": list(bpmn_models),
        "failures": failures,
    }
    (out / "conversion_diagnostics.json").write_text(
        json.dumps(summary, indent=2, default=repr), encoding="utf-8"
    )
    return ConversionBundle(nets, bpmn_models, tree_from_net, failures, out)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    conversion_bundle = convert_and_audit(source_powl)
```

The following table is a prospective assessment. No conversion has been executed for this answer, so nothing is labeled `validated preserved`. If tree conversion fails, its entire column becomes `conversion failed`; failure downstream must likewise be recorded explicitly.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Registration, required single A/E, and final X/H order | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Check all complete traces against the source oracle, plus required-event counts. |
| A/E independence and both relative orders | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Two trace orders establish scheduling freedom; concurrent-step witnesses add evidence of genuine concurrent enablement. |
| Legal occurs only after A; E/L remain unordered | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | `R A L E C I X H` must survive. A conventional parallel-block rewrite may incorrectly put E before L. |
| No order between optional legal and verification | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Permit L before C, between C/I, between iterations, and after the last I; check an L/C concurrent step. |
| XOR with a silent skip; L appears zero or one time | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Both legal-present and legal-absent complete traces must exist. An invisible branch cannot be assessed from the picture alone. |
| Verification waits for A and E | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | An AND-style synchronization must require both tokens, rather than treating the incoming alternatives as XOR. |
| Binary loop `body (redo body)*`, body C before I | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | The body is obligatory, D is the redo, and redo must return to C. A silent exit introduced by a tree is harmless only if it preserves this language. |
| At least one body; no trailing D or missing I | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | Reject zero-iteration acceptance and completion after D without another C/I body. |

| Archive finality and no residual tokens at acceptance | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | H must be last; the final token alone is insufficient if another token remains. |
| Visible labels and activity identity | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Check label inventory and multiplicities. Duplicated labels make trace-based event identity ambiguous even when strings match. |
| Initial/final marking interpretation | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | BPMN represents start/end routing, not an explicit Petri marking pair. Verify returned markings and their accepted language; do not compare place names across nets. |
| Meaning of gateways and invisible routing | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | not tested | Multiple equivalent routing structures may render differently; graph layout and gateway appearance do not prove synchronization or choice semantics. |

`expected preserved` expresses the converter's intended obligation, not a measured guarantee. `ambiguous` marks a representation or algorithmic risk that still needs evidence. If an experiment demonstrates only bounded completed-trace equality, qualify any `validated preserved` assessment with that scope. Use `approximated` for an explicitly documented weakened representation, and `lost` for an observed violation; neither is an acceptable authoritative replacement here.

A net supporting traces `A E` and `E A` could implement an XOR between two sequences instead of concurrency. Complete visible-trace equality alone does not distinguish those implementations. A jointly enabled, jointly fireable pair with commuting firing results supplies stronger local concurrency evidence, although it still does not prove an isomorphism of complete event structures.

The lack of an edge between optional legal and verification is a semantic fact, not permission to choose an arbitrary display order. In particular, “run A and E, then parallelize legal and verification” wrongly prevents L before E. Conversely, a “legal then verification” block loses L/C interleavings. A binary-loop conversion must preserve the entire C/I body, compulsory first execution, and D-only redo; it must not reinterpret the redo as an optional exit or a separately repeated task.

Visible legal-present/legal-absent tests validate optional behavior, but they cannot by themselves establish that the review and silent skip were never both taken internally. Inspect or formally analyze the exclusive routing and branch completion; exact final-marking equality alone does not establish internal XOR exclusivity if an incorrect implementation joins both paths cleanly.

All source labels are unique, so visible witnesses have clear activity meanings. Count label occurrences in derivative nets and document any many-to-one label mapping. `None` alone is hidden: an empty string, “tau,” or another visible label is not automatically silent. Finally, correctness depends on the initial/final marking pair, not just net topology. A diagram can look right while its recovered final marking accepts an unfinished branch or rejects valid completion.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The code below compares every produced accepting net to the independent source oracle, then compares each BPMN round trip to `net_from_powl`. It also checks the optional tree net. Its default domain is all completed visible traces with at most three C occurrences and at most fourteen visible events. For the source, fourteen is `8 + 3*(3-1)`: one legal-present iteration uses eight events, and each further iteration adds D/C/I. The independent oracle has 45 traces in this domain.

The exploration processes silent transitions even at the visible-length boundary, tests exact token-for-token final-marking equality, and deduplicates `(marking, visible prefix, iteration count)` states. Repeated silent cycles returning to the same state therefore cannot run forever. Silent token growth, excessive state growth, or a deadline triggers an explicit incomplete result. Bound-related pruning defines the comparison domain; resource-related pruning prevents a claim of exhaustiveness.

```python
# Append after Section 3 and the question's source_powl definition.
from collections import deque
from dataclasses import dataclass
from itertools import product
from queue import Empty
import multiprocessing as mp
import time
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking
from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics


R = "Register Change"
A = "Assess Risk"
E = "Prepare Evidence"
L = "Legal Review"
C = "Configure Trial"
I = "Inspect Trial"
D = "Correct Defect"
X = "Release Change"
H = "Archive Change"
SOURCE_LABELS = {R, A, E, L, C, I, D, X, H}
REQUIRED_STEPS = {tuple(sorted(pair)) for pair in [(A, E), (E, L), (L, C)]}
MAX_ITERATIONS = 3
MAX_VISIBLE = 8 + 3 * (MAX_ITERATIONS - 1)


def source_trace_oracle(max_iterations, max_visible):
    """Linear extensions of the specified dependencies, not of a converted net."""
    language = set()
    for iterations in range(1, max_iterations + 1):
        for include_legal in (False, True):
            labels = [R, A, E]
            predecessors = [set(), {0}, {0}]
            legal_index = None
            if include_legal:
                legal_index = len(labels)
                labels.append(L)
                predecessors.append({1})  # Only A precedes legal.

            last = None
            for iteration in range(iterations):
                if iteration > 0:
                    labels.append(D)
                    predecessors.append({last})
                    last = len(labels) - 1
                labels.append(C)
                predecessors.append({1, 2} if last is None else {last})
                last = len(labels) - 1
                labels.append(I)
                predecessors.append({last})
                last = len(labels) - 1

            release_predecessors = {last}
            if legal_index is not None:
                release_predecessors.add(legal_index)
            labels.append(X)
            predecessors.append(release_predecessors)
            release_index = len(labels) - 1
            labels.append(H)
            predecessors.append({release_index})
            if len(labels) > max_visible:
                continue

            def enumerate_extensions(done, prefix):
                if len(done) == len(labels):
                    language.add(prefix)
                    return
                for node in range(len(labels)):
                    if node not in done and predecessors[node] <= done:
                        enumerate_extensions(done | {node}, prefix + (labels[node],))

            enumerate_extensions(set(), ())
    return language


def clean_marking(net, marking):
    """Normalize zero entries; preserve the full token vector, not a subset."""
    if marking is None:
        raise ValueError("Missing accepting marking")
    result = Marking()
    for place, count in marking.items():
        if place not in net.places:
            raise ValueError("Marking refers to a place outside this net")
        if isinstance(count, bool) or not isinstance(count, int) or count < 0:
            raise ValueError("Expected non-negative integral token counts")
        if count:
            result[place] = count
    return result


def fire_enabled(net, marking, transition):
    result = semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
    if result is None:
        raise RuntimeError("An allegedly enabled transition could not fire")
    return clean_marking(net, result)


def concurrently_fireable(net, marking, first, second):
    # Ordinary weighted P/T-net semantics: a step must have enough tokens for
    # both transitions together, not merely enable each separately.
    if first is second:
        return False
    required = Counter()
    for transition in (first, second):
        for arc in transition.in_arcs:
            required[arc.source] += arc.weight
    if any(marking.get(place, 0) < count for place, count in required.items()):
        return False
    after_first = fire_enabled(net, marking, first)
    after_second = fire_enabled(net, marking, second)
    if second not in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, after_first):
        return False
    if first not in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, after_second):
        return False
    return (fire_enabled(net, after_first, second)
            == fire_enabled(net, after_second, first))


@dataclass
class Exploration:
    completed: set
    complete_within_bound: bool
    visited_states: int
    resource_cutoffs: dict
    bound_prunes: int
    concurrent_steps: set
    nonterminal_final_states: int
    deadlock_examples: list


def bounded_visible_language(artifact, max_visible=MAX_VISIBLE,
                             max_iterations=MAX_ITERATIONS,
                             max_states=250000, max_tokens=64,
                             timeout_seconds=30):
    net = artifact.net
    initial = clean_marking(net, artifact.im)
    final = clean_marking(net, artifact.fm)
    places = tuple(net.places)  # A fixed index within this net, not across nets.
    for transition in net.transitions:
        if transition.label is not None and not isinstance(transition.label, str):
            raise TypeError("Expected string labels or None")

    def signature(marking):
        return tuple(marking.get(place, 0) for place in places)

    # Call this on ordinary P/T nets. Do not apply ordinary execute/step rules
    # to inhibitor/reset nets or data-dependent transitions without adaptation.
    queue = deque([(initial, (), 0)])
    seen = {(signature(initial), (), 0)}
    completed, steps = set(), set()
    cutoffs = Counter()
    bound_prunes = 0
    final_with_enabled = 0
    deadlocks = []
    deadline = time.monotonic() + timeout_seconds
    if sum(initial.values()) > max_tokens:
        return Exploration(set(), False, 1, {"token_cap": 1}, 0, set(), 0, [])

    stop = False
    while queue and not stop:
        if time.monotonic() >= deadline:
            cutoffs["deadline"] += 1
            break
        marking, trace, iterations = queue.popleft()
        enabled = list(pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking))
        if marking == final:  # Exact equality; a final-place token is not enough.
            completed.add(trace)
            if enabled:
                final_with_enabled += 1
        elif not enabled and len(deadlocks) < 20:
            deadlocks.append(trace)

        by_label = {}
        for transition in enabled:
            by_label.setdefault(transition.label, []).append(transition)
        for pair in REQUIRED_STEPS - steps:
            first_label, second_label = pair
            if any(concurrently_fireable(net, marking, first, second)
                   for first, second in product(by_label.get(first_label, []),
                                                by_label.get(second_label, []))):
                steps.add(pair)

        for transition in enabled:
            # Only None is silent. Preserve empty strings and other labels.
            silent = transition.label is None
            next_trace = trace if silent else trace + (transition.label,)
            next_iterations = iterations + int(transition.label == C)
            if len(next_trace) > max_visible or next_iterations > max_iterations:
                bound_prunes += 1
                continue
            next_marking = fire_enabled(net, marking, transition)
            if sum(next_marking.values()) > max_tokens:
                cutoffs["token_cap"] += 1
                continue
            state = (signature(next_marking), next_trace, next_iterations)
            if state in seen:
                continue  # Also stops silent cycles with no marking change.
            if len(seen) >= max_states:
                cutoffs["state_cap"] += 1
                stop = True
                break
            seen.add(state)
            queue.append((next_marking, next_trace, next_iterations))

    return Exploration(
        completed, not cutoffs and not queue, len(seen), dict(cutoffs),
        bound_prunes, steps, final_with_enabled, deadlocks
    )

def witness_decision(exploration, trace):

# Potentially expensive PM4Py probes run in spawned workers with a timeout.
# Full reachability and Petri-net footprints are attempted only for nets whose
# workflow-net and soundness diagnostics passed. Their failures remain visible.
def _probe_worker(kind, net, im, fm, output_queue):
    try:
        if kind == "footprints":
            raw = pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm)
            if not isinstance(raw, dict):
                raise TypeError("Expected a Petri-net footprint dictionary")
            keys = ("activities", "sequence", "parallel", "start_activities",
                    "end_activities", "min_trace_length")
            payload = {key: raw[key] for key in keys if key in raw}
        elif kind == "reachability":
            graph = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
            payload = {"states": len(graph.states), "edges": len(graph.transitions)}
        elif kind == "playout":
            if not callable(getattr(pm4py, "play_out", None)):
                raise NotImplementedError("This PM4Py installation lacks play_out")
            log = pm4py.play_out(
                net, im, fm,
                parameters={"noTraces": 50, "maxTraceLength": MAX_VISIBLE}
            )
            payload = {"sampled_visible_traces": sorted(set(
                tuple(event["concept:name"] for event in trace) for trace in log
            ))}
        else:
            raise ValueError("Unknown probe: " + kind)
        output_queue.put({"status": "ok", "result": payload})
    except Exception as exc:
        output_queue.put({"status": "error", "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
                          "message": str(exc), "traceback": traceback.format_exc()})


def timed_probe(kind, artifact, timeout_seconds=30):
    if artifact.workflow_net is not True or artifact.sound is not True:
        return {"status": "not tested", "reason": "Workflow/soundness not confirmed"}
    context = mp.get_context("spawn")
    output_queue = context.Queue()
    process = context.Process(target=_probe_worker,
                              args=(kind, artifact.net, artifact.im, artifact.fm,
                                    output_queue))
    started = False
    try:
        process.start()
        started = True
        try:
            result = output_queue.get(timeout=timeout_seconds)
        except Empty:
            result = {"status": "incomplete",
                      "reason": "Timeout or worker exited without a result",
                      "worker_exitcode": process.exitcode}
        process.join(timeout=1)
        return result
    except Exception as exc:
        return {"status": "error", "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
                "message": str(exc)}
    finally:
        if started and process.is_alive():
            process.terminate()
            process.join(timeout=1)
        output_queue.close()


def validate_bundle(bundle):
    expected = source_trace_oracle(MAX_ITERATIONS, MAX_VISIBLE)
    assert all(trace in expected for trace in POSITIVES.values())
    assert all(trace not in expected for trace in NEGATIVES.values())
    results, reports, probes = {}, {}, {}

    for name, artifact in bundle.nets.items():
        if not artifact.marked:
            reports[name] = {"status": "quarantined", "reason": "Missing markings"}
            continue
        try:
            explored = bounded_visible_language(artifact)
            results[name] = explored
        except Exception as exc:
            reports[name] = {"status": "quarantined",
                             "exception_type": type(exc).__name__, "message": str(exc)}
            print(name, "exploration failed:", repr(exc))
            continue

        visible_counts = Counter(t.label for t in artifact.net.transitions
                                 if t.label is not None)
        extra = explored.completed - expected
        missing = expected - explored.completed
        reasons = []
        if artifact.workflow_net is not True or artifact.sound is not True:
            reasons.append("Workflow-net/soundness diagnostic did not pass")
        if not explored.complete_within_bound:
            reasons.append("Bounded exploration incomplete")
        if extra or (explored.complete_within_bound and missing):
            reasons.append("Bounded source-language mismatch")
        if set(visible_counts) != SOURCE_LABELS:
            reasons.append("Visible label inventory changed")
        if any(count != 1 for count in visible_counts.values()):
            reasons.append("Duplicated activity identity requires explicit mapping/review")
        if explored.nonterminal_final_states:
            reasons.append("Exact final marking still enables transitions")
        if REQUIRED_STEPS - explored.concurrent_steps:
            reasons.append("Required local concurrency witnesses not established")

        reports[name] = {
            "status": "quarantined" if reasons else "passes this bounded audit",
            "reasons": reasons,
            "workflow_net": artifact.workflow_net,
            "sound": artifact.sound,
            "visible_label_counts": dict(visible_counts),
            "complete_within_bound": explored.complete_within_bound,
            "visited_states": explored.visited_states,
            "resource_cutoffs": explored.resource_cutoffs,
            "bound_prunes": explored.bound_prunes,
            "completed_visible_traces": sorted(explored.completed),
            "extra_source_traces": sorted(extra),
            "missing_source_traces": sorted(missing) if explored.complete_within_bound else None,
            "source_language_equal_within_bound": (not extra and not missing)
                if explored.complete_within_bound else None,
            "concurrent_step_witnesses": sorted(explored.concurrent_steps),
            "nonterminal_final_states": explored.nonterminal_final_states,
            "deadlock_examples": explored.deadlock_examples,
            "positive_witnesses": {key: witness_decision(explored, trace)
                                   for key, trace in POSITIVES.items()},
            "negative_witnesses": {key: witness_decision(explored, trace)
                                   for key, trace in NEGATIVES.items()},
        }
        probes[name] = {kind: timed_probe(kind, artifact)
                        for kind in ("footprints", "reachability", "playout")}
        reports[name]["probes"] = probes[name]
        sample_probe = probes[name]["playout"]
        if sample_probe["status"] == "ok":
            samples = sample_probe["result"]["sampled_visible_traces"]
            reports[name]["sampled_traces_with_exact_completion_witness"] = [
                trace for trace in samples if tuple(trace) in explored.completed
            ]
            # Other sampled traces may be truncated, outside our iteration bound,
            # or incorrect. Sampling alone does not establish their final marking.
        print(name, reports[name]["status"], "reasons:", reasons)
        print(name, "probe statuses:", {k: v["status"] for k, v in probes[name].items()})

    comparisons = {}
    baseline = results.get("powl_net")
    for name in bundle.nets:
        if not name.startswith("roundtrip_"):
            continue
        candidate = results.get(name)
        if baseline is None or candidate is None:
            comparisons[name] = {"status": "not tested", "reason": "Exploration unavailable"}
            continue
        exhaustive = baseline.complete_within_bound and candidate.complete_within_bound
        comparison = {
            "complete_within_bound": exhaustive,
            "same_completed_visible_language":
                baseline.completed == candidate.completed if exhaustive else None,
            "lost_traces": sorted(baseline.completed - candidate.completed)
                if exhaustive else None,
            "added_traces": sorted(candidate.completed - baseline.completed)
                if exhaustive else None,
        }
        base_fp = probes["powl_net"]["footprints"]
        candidate_fp = probes[name]["footprints"]
        comparison["same_selected_footprints"] = (
            base_fp["result"] == candidate_fp["result"]
            if base_fp["status"] == candidate_fp["status"] == "ok" else None
        )
        comparisons[name] = comparison
        print(name, "comparison to POWL net:", comparison)

    report = {
        "pm4py_version": getattr(pm4py, "__version__", "unknown"),
        "bounds": {"max_visible": MAX_VISIBLE, "max_configure_occurrences": MAX_ITERATIONS,
                   "max_states": 250000, "max_tokens": 64, "exploration_seconds": 30},
        "source_oracle_complete_traces": sorted(expected),
        "artifacts": reports,
        "bpmn_roundtrip_comparisons": comparisons,
    }
    (bundle.output_dir / "validation_report.json").write_text(
        json.dumps(report, indent=2, default=repr), encoding="utf-8"
    )
    return report
```

For the source, counting C occurrences measures iteration starts. For a faulty derivative it is simply a label-occurrence bound, not an assumption that its loops are correct. The visible-length bound also limits malformed sequences that repeat I or D without C.

If `complete_within_bound` is true, the exploration is exhaustive for the specified domain on the ordinary P/T-net semantics used here. It considers every enabled transition and all silent continuations, merging states only when their marking and visible prefix are equivalent. It is not exhaustive for arbitrarily many visible iterations. If a resource cap fires or a worker fails, missing traces are unknown rather than proven absent. Known extra accepted traces remain counterexamples even during an incomplete exploration.

The oracle comparison is necessary: comparing only two nets could certify that both share the same initial conversion defect. Soundness is necessary but compatible with many incorrect source behaviors. Footprints supply coarse relations and can disagree for useful reasons, but footprint equality cannot certify language or concurrency equivalence. Reachability-graph state/edge counts are recorded for inspection; equal counts are not a graph-equivalence proof, and unequal counts can result from harmless silent routing. A stronger unbounded check would compare labeled state spaces under an explicitly selected weak/branching or concurrency-sensitive equivalence, rather than compare graph sizes.

Playout uses PM4Py's model simulator where available. Sampled traces are additionally tested for membership in the exact-completion trace set. A logged trace without that witness may be truncated or outside the bound; its log alone does not report an exact terminal marking. The positive/negative witnesses already receive a deterministic trace-acceptance test through the exhaustive bounded search, so random sampling does not replace replay or negative tests. Retain simulator exceptions and record seeds and effective parameters when relying on sampled evidence.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A correct net can produce a confusing BPMN picture. Review node types and sequence-flow semantics in the BPMN object as well as the rendered image.

- **Parallel gateways:** A split after registration should allow risk and evidence to proceed independently. Risk completion enables the legal choice and contributes to the verification prerequisite. Verification needs both risk and evidence; release needs legal completion and loop completion. These joins can span overlapping dependencies rather than form visually nested split/join blocks. Label joins “requires Risk AND Evidence” and “requires Optional Legal AND Verification completion.”
- **Exclusive gateways:** Legal review and bypass must be alternatives. An exclusive merge does not synchronize two mandatory incoming branches; using it at either required join can allow premature verification or release. Mark the legal choice as “Legal Review XOR skip.” The source supplies nondeterministic choice, not business conditions for taking either route.
- **Loop visualization:** Backward arrows or gateways can suggest repetition of one activity or optional execution of the body. Enclose or annotate the entire verification body with “Configure then Inspect; at least once; repeat only via Correct Defect.” Legal may interleave with any part of that chain; a drawn loop boundary must not imply an additional synchronization with legal.
- **Silent routing:** BPMN can represent a Petri-net tau by a direct bypass, routing gateways, or additional conversion nodes. Document that these are control-flow routing and carry no business activity. Check that the legal bypass remains exclusive and completes the optional branch before release.
- **Graph layout:** Horizontal position, lane placement, proximity, and crossing edges are presentation choices. A legal task drawn to the left of Configure does not establish precedence. Add “Legal and verification are unordered after their prerequisites; Legal may also precede Evidence.” Only directed sequence flows and node semantics define control flow.

Include the invariant IDs, allowed examples `R A L E C I X H` and `R A E C L I X H`, a routing legend, the source identifier/version, the converter version, and the validation bound in accompanying documentation. Do not introduce BPMN message flows, participant handoffs, task-duration claims, or legal-skip conditions that are absent from the source. A pleasant-looking rendering and a semantically acceptable accepting-net round trip are different review outcomes.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject a result as a faithful derivative, or quarantine it while evidence is incomplete, under any of these conditions:

1. A required conversion raises an exception, returns `None`, produces an unusable object, or fails to produce the requested downstream artifact. A failed alternative tree route can be abandoned while the independently validated primary route remains usable.
2. Initial or final markings are missing, invalid, refer to foreign places, or have been guessed instead of preserved or justified. The source requires nonempty start/completion states; arbitrary empty markings are not an acceptable repair.
3. Workflow-net or soundness diagnostics return false, raise an exception, or remain unknown. Dead transitions, deadlocks, improper completion, and token leakage require investigation.
4. Any source-oracle trace is lost, including reversed A/E order, L before E, L between C and I, a legal skip, or a valid repeated iteration.
5. Both visible orders survive only through serialized XOR alternatives, while required concurrent-step witnesses or the intended event identities are lost.
6. An extra completed trace admits C before either prerequisite, X before verification completes, X before a chosen L, or H before X.
7. The legal branch is mandatory, permanently skipped, can execute twice, or can execute both review and bypass in the same XOR instance.
8. The loop admits zero bodies, I before C, D before the first body, repetition without D, completion immediately after D, or repetition of only part of C/I.
9. Completion is inferred from reaching an end place or a prefix instead of exact final-marking equality. A final token with residual tokens, no exact final marking after H, or enabled behavior from the exact final marking fails the completion contract.
10. Activity labels disappear, change, become unexpectedly visible routing tasks, or are duplicated without an explicit identity mapping and semantic justification.
11. A state/token/time cap, simulator truncation, footprint exception, or unsupported API is reported as a successful exhaustive validation. Missing evidence warrants quarantine, not an equivalence claim.
12. Manual BPMN or tree edits add precedence, remove synchronization, or change gateway types without regeneration and revalidation against the authoritative source.

Visualization failure alone is not proof of semantic loss, but it prevents approval of the stakeholder visualization deliverable. Conversely, a successfully saved image or serialized model cannot override a failed semantic check.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

Store the exact POWL definition as the authoritative model, including node identities, visible labels, silent nodes, operators, and all partial-order edges. Keep a versioned, lossless serialization where supported, or the deterministic construction code plus a pinned PM4Py environment. The POWL expresses the intended unordered dependencies directly. Its accepting Petri net is a derived executable validation reference, not a replacement whose conversion success retroactively proves the source semantics.

Use the **direct net-derived BPMN** as the stakeholder visualization after it passes the declared audit. The accepting nets, tree if any, BPMN models, SVGs, reachability summaries, and simulation logs are generated artifacts linked to a specific source version. Mark an unvalidated or failing diagram as a candidate and keep it out of authoritative editing workflows.

For each derivative, retain its source identifier/hash, converter version, pipeline and parameters, marking definitions where applicable, label/identity mapping, diagnostic results, exception records, independent-oracle and round-trip trace comparisons with exact bounds and completeness flags, concurrency-step evidence, and any counterexamples. Include the visual review and the limits of each equivalence claim. A derivative with no executable semantics, such as an SVG, inherits evidence from its linked model rather than carrying independent behavioral proof.

Allow a process-tree intermediate only as an optional, separately audited derivative. Do not require tree conversion to succeed. Reject silent restructuring that serializes unordered work or duplicates activities merely to obtain a tree. If duplication is deliberately explored, document that event-structure fidelity is a separate obligation even if the bounded visible language matches. The direct route remains primary unless stronger evidence supports another choice.

Future semantic edits belong in POWL. Regenerate the net, BPMN, and evidence from the changed source; keep layout-only adjustments separate and recheck control-flow integrity after tooling rewrites. A stakeholder's BPMN edit is a proposed source change to review and implement in POWL, not an instruction to round-trip it into the canonical model. This prevents successive conversion and editing cycles from accumulating drift.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every proposed check passes:

- Three-iteration, fourteen-event equality does not establish equality for arbitrarily many iterations. A defect can appear only on a longer execution or require more reachable markings than were examined.
- Visible completed-trace equality and a few concurrent-step witnesses do not prove full event-structure equivalence, history-preserving equivalence, or preservation of every concurrency/choice distinction. They also do not observe when the silent optional branch completed.
- Completed traces hide silent divergence and internal routing differences. Deduplicating a silent cycle is correct for finite-trace existence, but does not prove absence of an infinite silent execution. Fairness and inevitability of termination need separate specifications and analysis; this source permits indefinite redo.
- Soundness establishes neither business correctness nor real-world termination. The source specifies no data guards, defect-resolution rule, risk thresholds, authorization roles, task durations, probabilities, or obligations to choose legal review.
- A passing BPMN-to-net round trip establishes properties of PM4Py's interpreted control flow, not conformance of every BPMN execution engine, interchange format, renderer, or human reading of the diagram.
- A successful artifact and audit for one PM4Py version do not guarantee the same behavior after a converter upgrade, serialization/reimport, manual edit, or label/identity remapping. Those changes require regeneration and renewed validation.
