## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Selection into completed cases.** Only closed cases are observed; if Orion’s harder cases more often stall or are abandoned, the within-stratum “faster” estimate is biased. *Most valuable addition:* a case-status field (open/abandoned/closed) plus censoring time, enabling survival analysis.

2. **Unobserved case risk beyond the coarse complexity label.** “Medium” cases may differ in claim amount, fraud risk, or sub-type across groups. *Most valuable addition:* a continuous risk/amount field recorded before routing.

3. **Queue vs compliance idle time confounded in `wait_before_review_h`.** The supplied wait variable mixes pure queueing and waiting around the compliance step. *Most valuable addition:* event-level timestamps with activity IDs so pre-review queue and compliance idle can be separated.

4. **Reviewer identity and skill unobserved.** “More certified reviewers” is asserted but not measured at case level, so resource_group proxies skill. *Most valuable addition:* reviewer_id and certification flag for within-reviewer paired comparisons.

5. **Routing rule internals unknown.** The rule co-varies perfectly with the group label, so any intervention on the label is confounded with the rule. *Most valuable addition:* routing_rule version/score and assignment reason, enabling rule-held-constant pilots.
