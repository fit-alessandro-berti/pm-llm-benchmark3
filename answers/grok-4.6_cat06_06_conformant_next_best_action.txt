## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning

The numbers do not support a single utility, but they do support dominance.

Unlawful or control-breaching execution sits in a band (€100k–€500k) that already exceeds the *sum* of P-884’s on-time value (€20k–€35k) and the top of the customer-harm band (€80k), and it carries non-monetary enforcement risk that is not commensurable with SLA cash. Any plan that executes without a valid approval path (A; B if treated as approval; G before both rule-10 facts exist) is therefore dominated, even if it drove SLA-breach probability to zero.

Deadline loss is the *smallest* monetized harm. Missing the 90-minute mark is not the same as rejecting or holding for a day. A late but conformant approval still captures most of the payment’s purpose and avoids the €15k–€80k false-reject/prolonged-hold band. That is why a plan that likely pays a bit late (D with S4) beats a plan that closes the case wrongly (F) or parks it for 6–24 hours (E) as a first move.

C buys only a *chance* of an on-time P-884 outcome, and a weak one: S2’s own 35–75 min review already spans “comfortable” to “deadline-expired,” and observational P(ultimate approve) is only 0.55–0.75. Interrupting P-901 therefore often spends another high-value case’s SLA to purchase a review that still ends in reject or hold. Transfer that cost only when S4 is confirmed late.

E is conformant information value after a *completed* evidence cycle. It is poorly timed as the next act: the fuzzy match is moderate (0.46), risk already fell 78 → 72, and a 6–24 h wait almost guarantees the worse customer-harm band. Keep E as a later branch if the screen *strengthens* the match and the file is still not rejectable.

F is an option only if the reject threshold is already met. Given predicted approval 0.55–0.75, the expected customer harm is first-order, not a rounding error.

Forecasts under “continue normal processing” (SLA breach 0.48–0.64) are observational and need not hold under interrupt, rescreen, reject, or override. They cannot be used as if they were interventional effects, and they cannot outrank a hard control.

Actions A and B cannot be recommended because a forecast looks good. Conformance here is a constraint, not a term that a €20k–€35k SLA save can buy out. The SLA-breach prediction is for *normal* continuation, not for “pay now” or “R17 signs twice.” Even a point-estimate EV that favored the shortcut would ignore asymmetric, partly non-monetary harm and would authorize a forbidden transition. A nonconformant shortcut is not acceptable merely because expected operational loss is lower.

**For F to become valid:** a recorded first-line recommendation to reject; residual evidence that meets the firm’s written reject-without-clearance standard (a 0.46 fuzzy hit on an established supplier after validated evidence is unlikely to suffice on its own); and either SOP confirmation that second-officer review is not required for reject, or an actual second-officer concurrence. Absent that file, F is a premature close.

**For G to become valid:** a real incident (not SLA anxiety), a recorded genuine incident identifier, and explicit Compliance Director authorization. All three must exist *before* any override execution. Creating an ID to decorate a late payment would be a separate integrity failure.

Filing a SAR, if reached, never authorizes Payment Executed and must not be disclosed in any customer evidence request.

## 5. Resource spillovers

**S2 versus S4.** S2 is free only by taking ~45 minutes from P-901, which has a 70-minute deadline and is also high-value; estimated breach lift 0.12–0.28. S4 costs 35–65 minutes of clock and nothing to P-901. S2’s review itself is 35–75 minutes, so “S2 now” is not a reliable in-SLA instrument. S4 plus a similar review is almost surely after P-884’s deadline. Prefer S4 when the alternative is spending P-901 to buy a modest, uncertain SLA increment on P-884.

**P-901.** Treat it as a real expected-cost object, not slack. Do not interrupt unless (i) S4’s realized ETA leaves no plausible conformant finish for P-884 inside a window that still matters operationally, (ii) S2 will perform a genuine independent review, and (iii) P-901 has some cover. A 0.12–0.28 breach increase on another high-value payment can erase P-884’s entire €20k–€35k on-time benefit.

**API quota.** Three expedited calls remain. Spending one on a €480k sanctioned-border payment with an unresolved 0.46 match and a stale score of 72 is justified. It reduces uncertainty; it does not retire rule 6. Hold the other two unless the first call is inconclusive.

**Exception time-burn.** Chasing G for 20–60 minutes without an incident consumes the same window S4 and the alternate screen need. Do not serialize G in front of D. A one-shot reachability check is enough unless a real incident appears.

**If S4 is available in 35 minutes:** keep D firmly first; launch the screen immediately so the pack is fresh when S4 sits down; do **not** touch P-901. C drops.  
**If S4 is available only in 65 minutes:** remaining P-884 time after S4 arrives is ~25 minutes, which is below S2’s 35-minute review floor. Raise C: start the alternate screen **and** call S2 now (or as soon as S4’s late ETA is confirmed), accepting the P-901 spillover in order to finish a *conformant* second review while any on-time tail still exists. D without interrupt remains the safer *late-approval* path if leadership is willing to spend the €20k–€35k SLA rather than tax P-901. The rank-1/rank-2 order of D and C therefore **flips only when S4 is confirmed late**; A, B, F, and G do not move up.

**Primary:** Execute **Action D as a parallel plan**. Start one expedited alternate screening immediately. Have R17 finish the post-evidence first-line review on the current pack now. Wait for **S4** for the independent second-officer review. Approve, reject, or SAR-hold only after that path. Execute only after a valid approval. Do not disclose a SAR. Do not self-certify. Do not pay.

**Fallback:** If S4’s realized wait is ~65 minutes, or S4 is still down when remaining time approaches S2’s 35-minute review floor, switch to **Action C**: interrupt P-901, assign S2, require an independent reading (ideally of the new screen). If the screen or the second officer strengthens the match, move to documented reject or SAR-hold rather than G. Open G only if a genuine incident and director authorization actually appear.

**Confidence:** **Medium.** The risk score is 12 minutes stale; the match is unresolved at only 0.46; approval and SLA probabilities are observational; S4’s arrival is a 30-minute range; P-901’s monetary exposure is unspecified.

**Rerank immediately on the first of:** S4’s realized availability (35 vs 65 minutes); alternate-screen output (cleared / still fuzzy / confirmed); a fresh risk score that crosses or stays above 70; a material change in P-901; or any genuine incident / director contact.
