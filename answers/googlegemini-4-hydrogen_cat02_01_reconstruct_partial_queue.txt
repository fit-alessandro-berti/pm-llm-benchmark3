# 1. Assumptions and notation

Let the observation window be $[T_0, T] = [\text{08:00}, \text{14:00}]$ on 2026-05-12 ($T = 360\text{ min}$), with stations $k \in \{S1, S2\}$ and single-task resources $\mathcal{R}_{S1} = \{I1, I2\}$ and $\mathcal{R}_{S2} = \{C1, C2\}$. For each case $i \in \{\text{P01},\dots,\text{P22}\}$, let $a_{i,1} = \text{arrival\_s1}$, $s_{i,k} = \text{s}k\text{\_start}$, $e_{i,k} = \text{s}k\text{\_end}$, and $a_{i,2} = e_{i,1}$ (queue entry to $S2$ occurs immediately upon $S1$ completion).

- **A1 (Event-order convention for simultaneous timestamps):** When multiple events share the same reported 5-minute timestamp $t$, events are ordered **Completions $\to$ Starts $\to$ Arrivals** at each station. Queue length $L_{q,k}(t)$, in-service count $L_{s,k}(t)$, and total WIP $L_k(t)$ are evaluated immediately after all events at $t$ (right-continuous step functions on $[t, t+5\text{ min})$). Under this convention, an $S1$ departure and an $S1$ arrival at the same timestamp $t$ (e.g., 09:25, 10:00, 11:30) do not create a momentary $+1$ WIP spike; reversing to Arrivals-before-Completions would raise peak WIP by $+1$ instantaneously at those points while leaving time-averaged WIP unchanged.
- **A2 (Timestamp rounding treatment):** Every reported timestamp $t$ represents an actual continuous time $\tilde{t} \in [t - 2.5, t + 2.5]\text{ min}$. Consequently, any single duration computed as $t_2 - t_1$ has rounding uncertainty up to $\pm 5.0\text{ min}$ (subject to physical non-negativity $\tilde{t}_2 \ge \tilde{t}_1$). Because consecutive jobs on the same resource form contiguous chains, endpoint rounding errors partially cancel over multi-job sums rather than accumulating additively.
- **A3 (Single-task non-preemptive capacity & availability):** Each resource processes at most one case at a time without preemption, and no case bypasses $S1$ or $S2$. $C2$ is unavailable for maintenance from 10:55 through 11:10 ($15\text{ min}$), reducing scheduled $S2$ capacity over $[08:00, 14:00]$ from $720$ to $705\text{ resource-minutes}$ (both denominators are reported).
- **A4 (Distinction among `MISSING`, `OPEN`, and blank statuses):**
  - `MISSING`: the activity executed on the named resource and completed at $e_{i,k}$, so $s_{i,k}$ is bounded by queue entry, prior resource completion, and activity completion.
  - `OPEN`: the activity started at $s_{i,k}$ and remained active at $T = 14:00$; only elapsed service $14:00 - s_{i,k}$ is observed, providing a lower bound on final service time.
  - Blank start and end: the activity had not started by $14:00$ (`WAITING_FOR_S1` or `WAITING_FOR_S2`). For P22, whose $S1$ arrival was 12:10 while $I2$ completed P20 at 13:45, `WAITING_FOR_S1` is treated at face value as unstarted through 14:00 (idle $I2$ for 15 min) rather than an unlogged start.
- **A5 (Imputation bounds and central rule for `MISSING` starts):** For each missing start $s_{i,k}$ on resource $r$, physical feasibility requires:
  $$s_{i,k}^{\min} = \max\!\left(a_{i,k},\, e_{\text{prev}(r)}\right), \qquad s_{i,k}^{\max} = e_{i,k} - s_{\min}$$
  where $s_{\min} = 0\text{ min}$ gives the strict mathematical bound and $s_{\min} = 5\text{ min}$ gives an operational positive-service bound. Across all 17 observed transitions on $I1$/$I2$ and all transitions on $C2$ and $C1$ (except $P07 \to P09$ at 11:00 on $C1$), resources exhibit a consistent 5-minute inter-job gap. We therefore compare:
  1. **Early-start / work-conserving (`S_early`):** $s_{i,k} = s_{i,k}^{\min}$ (zero changeover gap; minimizes wait, maximizes service/busy time).
  2. **Pattern-consistent central (`S_central`):** $s_{i,k} = \max(a_{i,k},\, e_{\text{prev}(r)} + 5\text{ min})$ when a resource predecessor exists and $s_{i,k} < e_{i,k}$ (reflecting the empirical 5-min inter-job gap).
  3. **Late-start / minimal-service (`S_late`):** $s_{i,k} = e_{i,k} - 5\text{ min}$ (operational lower bound on service) alongside the mathematical limit $s_{i,k} = e_{i,k}$ ($0\text{ min}$ service).

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

## Case-level quantities
For case $i$ and station $k \in \{S1, S2\}$:
- **Queue waiting time:** $W_{q,i,k} = s_{i,k} - a_{i,k}$ if started by $14:00$; if waiting at $14:00$, **elapsed wait** is $W_{q,i,k}^{\text{el}} = 14:00 - a_{i,k}$ (lower bound: $W_{q,i,k} \ge W_{q,i,k}^{\text{el}}$).
- **Active service time:** $S_{i,k} = e_{i,k} - s_{i,k}$ if completed by $14:00$; if `OPEN` at $14:00$, **elapsed service** is $S_{i,k}^{\text{el}} = 14:00 - s_{i,k}$ ($S_{i,k} \ge S_{i,k}^{\text{el}}$).
- **Station sojourn time:** $W_{i,k} = \min(e_{i,k}, 14:00) - a_{i,k}$ (for completed stations, $W_{i,k} = e_{i,k} - a_{i,k} = W_{q,i,k} + S_{i,k}$, which is **exact and invariant** to missing-start imputations because both $a_{i,k}$ and $e_{i,k}$ are observed).
- **Total system sojourn (completed cases P01–P14):** $W_i = e_{i,2} - a_{i,1} = W_{i,1} + W_{i,2}$.

## Station-level quantities over $[T_a, T_b]$ (duration $\Delta T = T_b - T_a$)
- **Arrival and departure rates:** $\lambda_k = N_{k}^{\text{arr}} / (\Delta T/60)$ and $\mu_k^{\text{eff}} = N_{k}^{\text{comp}} / (\Delta T/60)$ in cases/hour, using half-open intervals $[T_a, T_b)$ except $[13:00, 14:00]$ closed at the window boundary.
- **Busy time and utilization:** For resource $r$ in station $k$, busy time $B_r$ is the sum of active service durations inside $[T_a, T_b]$ (including `OPEN` elapsed time up to $14:00$ and imputed intervals for `MISSING` starts). Station busy time is $B_k = \sum_{r \in \mathcal{R}_k} B_r$, and utilization is $U_k = B_k / C_k$, where $C_{S1} = 2\Delta T$ and $C_{S2}$ is reported against both gross capacity ($2\Delta T$) and maintenance-adjusted available capacity ($705\text{ min}$ overall; $225\text{ min}$ in $[10:00, 12:00)$).
- **WIP and queue length:** By sample-path integration, time-averaged WIP is exact up to rounding and independent of missing-start imputations:
  $$\bar{L}_k = \frac{1}{\Delta T}\int_{T_a}^{T_b} L_k(t)\,dt = \frac{1}{\Delta T}\sum_{i} \text{overlap}\!\left([a_{i,k}, \min(e_{i,k}, T)],\, [T_a, T_b]\right)$$
  Similarly, $\bar{L}_{s,k} = B_k / \Delta T$ and $\bar{L}_{q,k} = \bar{L}_k - \bar{L}_{s,k}$. Peak WIP $\max_t L_k(t)$ and peak queue $\max_t L_{q,k}(t)$ are evaluated on the 5-minute event grid under A1.

## Little's Law consistency
We distinguish two formulations:
1. **Finite-horizon sample-path identity** over $[08:00, 14:00]$: $\bar{L}_k = \frac{N_{k,\text{arr}}}{T}\,\bar{W}_{k,\text{in-window}}$, which holds identically when censored cases contribute their in-window elapsed sojourn.
2. **Steady-state Little's Law** $L_k = \lambda_k W_k$: defensible only on sub-intervals where arrival rate roughly balances departure rate, WIP has no secular drift, and sojourn times are approximately stationary.

# 5. Bottleneck and temporal interpretation

## Observations vs. hypotheses
- **Observed capacity asymmetry:** With two servers each, $S1$'s observed mean service time is $28.1\text{ min}$ (implying gross capacity $\approx 4.28\text{ cases/hr}$, or $\approx 3.63\text{ cases/hr}$ when accounting for the observed 5-min inter-job gap). $S2$'s observed mean service time is $40.0\text{ min}$ (central incl. missing starts $37.5\text{ min}$, implying gross capacity $\approx 3.00\text{–}3.20\text{ cases/hr}$, or $\approx 2.67\text{–}2.82\text{ cases/hr}$ with a 5-min gap). Thus **$S2$ is the structural capacity bottleneck**.
- **Phase 1 — Upstream overload at $S1$ (08:00–10:30, observed):** External arrivals arrive at $6.5\text{ cases/hr}$ in 08:00–10:00 (7 cases in hour 1, 6 in hour 2), exceeding $S1$'s capacity ($\approx 3.6\text{–}4.3\text{/hr}$). Consequently, $S1$ absorbs the initial congestion: $S1$ WIP climbs to 7–8 cases and $S1$ waits grow from 5 to 80+ min, while $S1$ meters flow into $S2$ at only $\sim 3.0\text{–}3.5\text{ cases/hr}$. Early $S2$ waits remain modest (5–25 min).
- **Phase 2 — Maintenance shock and bottleneck shift to $S2$ (10:30–14:00, observed):** External arrivals fall to $4.0\text{/hr}$ (10:00–12:00) and $0.5\text{/hr}$ (12:00–14:00), allowing $S1$ to hold a plateau around 7–8 WIP until 12:15 and then drain to 2 WIP by 14:00. Meanwhile, $S1$ continues feeding $S2$ at $3.5\text{ cases/hr}$ (14 arrivals in 10:00–14:00), exceeding $S2$'s $\sim 2.7\text{–}3.0\text{/hr}$ effective capacity. Simultaneously, $C2$'s 15-min outage (10:55–11:10) cuts $S2$ to single-server capacity during a critical surge: $S2$ completes only 2 cases in `[11:00, 12:00)`, P08 waits 55 min, P11 waits 55–80 min, and $S2$ WIP climbs steadily to a peak of 7 cases at 13:45 (ending at 6 cases at 14:00).
- **Observed operational anomalies and hypotheses:**
  - *Consistent 5-min inter-job gaps:* Every consecutive transition on $I1$ (8/8), $I2$ (9/9), $C2$ (2/2 non-maintenance), and $C1$ (5/6) shows a 5-min gap despite queues waiting. **Hypothesis:** This reflects real setup, logging, or part-transfer time ($\sim 2.5\text{–}7.5\text{ min}$) rather than pure rounding noise, capping practical saturation below 100% ($\approx 85\%$ at $S1$, $\approx 89\%$ at $S2$—both nearly reached during peak hours).
  - *Non-FCFS dispatching at $S2$:* P09 (arrived S2 10:35) started $C1$ at 11:00 ahead of P08 (arrived 10:20); P17 (arrived S2 12:50) started $C1$ at 13:55 ahead of P16 (arrived 12:35). **Hypothesis:** Cases may have had resource-affinity, priority expediting, or physical staging order (notice all pre-10:55 cases followed odd$\to C1$, even$\to C2$, and P16 was an $I2$/even case while P17 was $I1$/odd).
  - *$I2$ idle gap (13:45–14:00) while P22 waits:* $I2$ finished P20 at 13:45 and remained idle for 15 min despite P22 waiting since 12:10. **Hypothesis:** End-of-window cutoff policy (not starting a $\sim 30$-min inspection 15 min before 14:00), an undocumented break/meeting, or delayed start logging not reflected in `state_at_14_00`.

## Little's Law consistency check
- **Global window $[08:00, 14:00]$:** Steady-state Little's Law ($L = \lambda W$) is **not defensible** globally because arrivals are strongly non-stationary ($6.5 \to 4.0 \to 0.5\text{/hr}$), the system ends far from empty (8 cases remaining), and $S2$ is unstable ($\lambda_{\text{in}} > \mu_2$). Indeed, using completed-case mean sojourn for $S2$ ($70.0\text{ min}$) with $\lambda_2 = 3.33\text{/hr}$ gives $3.89$ vs actual $\bar{L}_{S2} = 3.72$, and using departure rate $2.33\text{/hr}$ yields $2.72$ (a $-27\%$ bias). Only the **finite-horizon sample-path identity** holds exactly: $\bar{L}_{S1} = \frac{22}{6}\cdot\frac{94.77}{60} = 5.79$ and $\bar{L}_{S2} = \frac{20}{6}\cdot\frac{67.00}{60} = 3.72$.
- **Quasi-stationary sub-interval check ($S1$, 10:15–12:15):** During this 2-hr plateau, $S1$ WIP holds nearly constant at $\bar{L}_{S1} \approx 7.5\text{ cases}$ and throughput is $\lambda \approx 3.5\text{–}4.0\text{/hr}$. Steady-state Little's Law predicts $W_{S1} \approx 7.5 / 3.75 = 2.0\text{ hr} = 120\text{ min}$, which aligns closely with the observed $S1$ sojourn times of cases departing/arriving in that window ($100\text{–}130\text{ min}$, mean $\approx 115\text{–}125\text{ min}$).

# 2. Status-aware timestamp parsing (distinguishing MISSING, OPEN, and BLANK/NOT_STARTED)
def parse_ts_and_status(val: str):
    v = val.strip()
    if v == "":
        return pd.NaT, "BLANK"
    if v == "MISSING":
        return pd.NaT, "MISSING"
    if v == "OPEN":
        return pd.NaT, "OPEN"
    return pd.Timestamp(BASE_DATE + v), "OBSERVED"

df = df_raw.copy()
for col in ["arrival_s1", "s1_start", "s1_end", "s2_start", "s2_end"]:
    parsed = df[col].apply(parse_ts_and_status)
    df[col + "_ts"] = [p[0] for p in parsed]
    df[col + "_status"] = [p[1] for p in parsed]

# Note on requirement 7 (pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals):
# A direct station-specific pandas calculation is safer than pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals
# for the main reconstruction because: (a) PM4Py interval helpers operate on observed case traces
# and cannot retain right-censored OPEN/WAITING states at 14:00 or MISSING-start feasibility bounds,
# (b) station queue entry for S2 equals S1 completion rather than an external arrival event, and
# (c) resource-level outage adjustments (C2 maintenance 10:55-11:10 -> 705 avail min) are station-specific.

# 5. Direct station-specific pandas reconstruction and uncertainty scenarios
# Derive feasible imputation bounds for MISSING starts using queue entry and prior work on same resource
def build_scenario_df(scenario: str) -> pd.DataFrame:
    work = df.copy()
    # Collect known intervals per resource to find immediate predecessor end before each MISSING end
    all_obs = []
    for _, r in work.iterrows():
        for st, s_c, e_c, res_c in [("S1", "s1_start", "s1_end", "s1_resource"),
                                    ("S2", "s2_start", "s2_end", "s2_resource")]:
            if r[e_c + "_status"] == "OBSERVED":
                all_obs.append((r["case"], st, r[res_c], r[s_c + "_ts"], r[e_c + "_ts"]))
    obs_df = pd.DataFrame(all_obs, columns=["case", "st", "res", "s", "e"])

    for idx, r in work.iterrows():
        for st, q_col, s_col, e_col, res_col in [
            ("S1", "arrival_s1_ts", "s1_start", "s1_end", "s1_resource"),
            ("S2", "s1_end_ts", "s2_start", "s2_end", "s2_resource"),
        ]:
            if r[s_col + "_status"] == "MISSING":
                res = r[res_col]
                end_ts = r[e_col + "_ts"]
                q_ts = r[q_col]
                preds = obs_df[(obs_df["res"] == res) & (obs_df["e"] <= end_ts) & (obs_df["case"] != r["case"])]
                prev_end = preds["e"].max() if len(preds) else WIN_START
                s_min = max(q_ts, prev_end)
                if scenario == "early_work_conserving":
                    imp_s = s_min
                elif scenario == "pattern_central_5m":
                    imp_s = max(q_ts, prev_end + pd.Timedelta(minutes=5))
                elif scenario == "late_min_5m":
                    imp_s = end_ts - pd.Timedelta(minutes=5)
                elif scenario == "late_math_0m":
                    imp_s = end_ts
                else:
                    raise ValueError(scenario)
                work.at[idx, s_col + "_ts"] = imp_s
    return work

def compute_station_metrics(work: pd.DataFrame, station: str, avail_res_min: float):
    if station == "S1":
        q_ts_col, s_ts_col, e_ts_col = "arrival_s1_ts", "s1_start_ts", "s1_end_ts"
        s_st_col, e_st_col = "s1_start_status", "s1_end_status"
    else:
        q_ts_col, s_ts_col, e_ts_col = "s1_end_ts", "s2_start_ts", "s2_end_ts"
        s_st_col, e_st_col = "s2_start_status", "s2_end_status"

    entered = work[work[q_ts_col].notna()].copy()
    entered["eff_end"] = entered[e_ts_col].fillna(WIN_END).clip(upper=WIN_END)
    entered["soj_el"] = (entered["eff_end"] - entered[q_ts_col]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0

    started = entered[entered[s_ts_col].notna()].copy()
    started["wait_min"] = (started[s_ts_col] - started[q_ts_col]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0
    started["serv_el"] = (started["eff_end"] - started[s_ts_col]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0

    completed = entered[entered[e_st_col] == "OBSERVED"].copy()
    completed["serv_min"] = (completed[e_ts_col] - completed[s_ts_col]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0

    waiting_end = entered[entered[s_st_col] == "BLANK"].copy()
    waiting_end["wait_el"] = (WIN_END - waiting_end[q_ts_col]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0

    busy_min = started["serv_el"].sum()
    mean_wip = entered["soj_el"].sum() / 360.0
    mean_ls = busy_min / 360.0
    mean_lq = mean_wip - mean_ls

    # 5-min grid sweep with A1 order: Completions -> Starts -> Arrivals (right-continuous)
    grid = pd.date_range(WIN_START, WIN_END, freq="5min")
    wip_series, q_series = [], []
    for t in grid:
        in_sys = ((entered[q_ts_col] <= t) & (entered["eff_end"] > t)).sum()
        in_srv = ((started[s_ts_col] <= t) & (started["eff_end"] > t)).sum()
        wip_series.append(in_sys)
        q_series.append(in_sys - in_srv)

    return {
        "station": station,
        "arrivals": len(entered),
        "completed": len(completed),
        "mean_wait_started": round(started["wait_min"].mean(), 2),
        "mean_wait_all_lb": round((started["wait_min"].sum() + waiting_end["wait_el"].sum()) / len(entered), 2),
        "mean_serv_comp": round(completed["serv_min"].mean(), 2),
        "busy_min": round(busy_min, 1),
        "util_gross_pct": round(100.0 * busy_min / 720.0, 1),
        "util_avail_pct": round(100.0 * busy_min / avail_res_min, 1),
        "mean_wip": round(mean_wip, 2),
        "mean_queue": round(mean_lq, 2),
        "peak_wip": int(max(wip_series)),
        "peak_queue": int(max(q_series)),
    }

scenario_rows = []
for sc in ["early_work_conserving", "pattern_central_5m", "late_min_5m", "late_math_0m"]:
    w_df = build_scenario_df(sc)
    for st, cap in [("S1", 720.0), ("S2", 705.0)]:
        res = compute_station_metrics(w_df, st, cap)
