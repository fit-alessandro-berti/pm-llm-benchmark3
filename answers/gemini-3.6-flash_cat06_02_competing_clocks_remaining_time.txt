## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Chemistry Queue Discipline & Friday Execution (Rank 1)**
   - *Category*: Queue Uncertainty / Calendar Uncertainty.
   - *Impact*: Determines whether Chemistry starts Friday 17:00 or is forced into Monday morning. It controls the lower bound of the parallel join clock.

2. **Upstream Temperature Excursion Confirmation (Rank 2)**
   - *Category*: Dependence Uncertainty / Path Uncertainty.
   - *Impact*: Acts as a common-cause multiplier (prob 0.20–0.40). Confirmation simultaneously inflates Chem rerun prob, Micro contamination prob, and QA Investigation prob from <10% to 30–55%.

3. **QA Rework / Deviation Investigation Type (Rank 3)**
   - *Category*: Path Uncertainty / Service-Time Uncertainty.
   - *Impact*: Introduces a bimodal tail. A doc-only investigation adds 8–20 QA working hours (1–2 days), whereas a lab investigation adds 3–7 full calendar days.

4. **Microbiology Staffing & Readout Timing (Rank 4)**
   - *Category*: Calendar Uncertainty.
   - *Impact*: Dictates whether incubation completion on Sunday is processed immediately via weekend on-call (prob 0.25–0.45) or held until Monday 08:00.

5. **Future QA Queue Congestion at Join Time (Rank 5)**
   - *Category*: Queue Uncertainty.
   - *Impact*: Overdispersed batch arrivals (0.35/h) between Friday afternoon and Monday morning determine QA queue wait times upon parallel join completion.
