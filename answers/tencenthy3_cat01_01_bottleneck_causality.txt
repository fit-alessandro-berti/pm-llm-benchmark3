The operational facts that Orion has more certified reviewers and covers night work supply an identifiable process mechanism through which assignment to Orion can reduce queue wait and review service time for similar cases.

Strongest evidence for the verdict: stratified means, lower `wait_before_review_h` and slightly lower `review_service_h` inside strata, and the stated reviewer-certification advantage. Against: small stratum sizes (e.g., only 2 Nova high-complexity cases), unobserved case risk, shift/load not fully adjusted, and absence of open/abandoned cases limit identification. The proposed 50% reassignment will likely fail or backfire if complex, compliance-heavy night cases are moved to Nova without transferring certified capacity; Nova’s queues and appeal risk would rise. A controlled pilot and capacity targets are safer.

## 5. Competing causal explanations and falsification tests

## 6. Intervention and learning plan

| phase | intervention | target mechanism | assignment unit | safeguards | process-mining measurements | causal learning value | stop or rollback rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Retrospective stratification and queue-state reconstruction | Measure confounding, variant mix, bottleneck exposure | Historical cases | No operational change | DFG per group, `wait_before_review_h` and `review_service_h` per variant, overlap counts | Baseline and positivity diagnostics | N/A |
| 2 | Add night compliance-review capacity and queue pooling for Nova compliance-required cases without changing `resource_group` label | Reduce `queue_wait` and compliance bottleneck | Cases with `compliance_required` routed to Nova night | Cap at 20% volume, keep Orion backup, monitor quality | `wait_before_review_h`, compliance throughput, `rework_count`, `outcome_30d` | Tests whether wait is driven by capacity not group label | Stop if Nova appeal rate >2% or wait increases |
| 3 | Small randomized routing pilot for medium-complexity day/normal-load cases (10% randomly assigned to Orion vs Nova) | Estimate direct `resource_group` effect | Case after `Classify` | Exclude high complexity, limit n100, monitor | `cycle_time_h`, `review_service_h`, `rework_count`, `quality_outcome` | Unbiased direct contrast of Orion vs Nova | Roll back if Orion arm cycle >20% longer or quality worse |

Final recommendation: Management should not blindly reassign 50% of Orion cases to Nova. The crude 6.7h Orion penalty is largely a case-mix and routing artifact: Orion receives far more high-complexity, compliance-required, night cases. Within complexity strata Orion is actually 2–3.5h faster, mainly through lower queue wait. A blanket transfer would push the hardest, compliance-heavy cases onto Nova’s day-heavy, less-certified pool and likely increase waits, rework, and appeals. Instead, we recommend (1) complete the diagnostic stratification and queue-state reconstruction, (2) first augment Nova’s night/compliance capacity and queue routing so it can absorb complex variants without changing the group label, and (3) run a small randomized routing pilot on medium-complexity day cases to estimate the direct Orion effect. This sequence learns while limiting risk; expand only if the pilot shows Nova can match Orion’s wait/service without quality loss.

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Unobserved case risk within complexity levels** – Most valuable additional field: a detailed underwriting or clinical risk score recorded at `Register`/`Classify`.
2. **Selection bias from missing open or abandoned cases** – Most valuable additional data: a daily snapshot of in-progress and withdrawn cases with current stage and elapsed time.
3. **Reviewer skill and certification mix per case** – Most valuable additional field: `reviewer_id` linked to certification level, tenure, and training.
4. **Time-varying load and queue-state dynamics** – Most valuable design change: timestamped arrival, classification, queue-entry, and review-start events to reconstruct congestion.
5. **Compliance and rework drivers plus post-process quality** – Most valuable additional data: compliance start/end times, `Request Info` reasons, and 90-day appeal/outcome details.
