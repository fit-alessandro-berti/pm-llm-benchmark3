Mapping rationale: Site A’s nine-hour median reduction is not treated as an identified effect. The more transferable signals are waiting before Assessment (18  12 h) and overlap (8%  71%), but both are confounded by API, staffing, mix, huddles, and timestamp recording. Site B’s digital-only cases are only about 38% of volume; for them, the maximum plausible overlap saving is roughly min(identity 3.4 h, document 8.1 h), so median gains are likely 1–3 h overall, not 9 h. For originals-required cases, median originals delay is 18 h, so Document Check cannot start until after receipt. Legal review is 40% and legal capacity is only 2 reviewers, so saved verification time can shift into assessment/legal queues and worsen p90. The bottleneck-shift and legal/rework scenarios therefore intentionally allow average improvement with tail worsening.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Eligibility rules**
- All cases: after `Register`, create `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt` concurrently.
- No-originals/digital cases: after `Register`, create `Identity Check` and `Document Check` concurrently.
- Originals-required cases: do not create `Document Check` until `Originals Receipt` is complete. `Identity Check` may overlap with `Originals Receipt` and may still be open when `Document Check` starts.
- Legal cases: after `Assessment` is complete, create `Legal Review` if public-sector/high-value.

**Tasks that may be concurrent**
- `Identity Check` with `Originals Receipt` for all cases.
- `Identity Check` with `Document Check` for no-originals cases.
- For originals-required cases, `Identity Check` may overlap with `Document Check` only if `Identity Check` is still open after `Originals Receipt`.

**Mandatory statutory prerequisites**
- `Register` first.
- `Originals Receipt` before `Document Check` when originals are legally required.
- `Assessment` waits for both `Identity Check` and `Document Check` complete.
- `Legal Review` waits for `Assessment` when required.
- `Request Supplement` loops back to `Document Check` and then `Assessment`.

**Queue and WIP controls**
- Identity WIP cap: 8 open tasks; document WIP cap: 14; assessment WIP cap: 6; legal WIP cap: 4.
- Pause new verification starts when assessment or legal queue exceeds threshold.
- Daily queue huddles; prioritize digital cases for first wave, then originals-required cases with originals already received.

**Resource responsibilities**
- Identity reviewers do only identity; document reviewers handle originals receipt and document check; assessors do assessment; legal reviewers do legal review. If the legacy lock prevents simultaneous ownership, a verification coordinator may manage handoffs, but all work must be logged with true timestamps.

**Legal-review and supplement loops**
- Legal review may issue `Request Supplement`; then `Document Check` and `Assessment` are repeated as required, and `Legal Review` re-evaluates. Do not close the case until the supplement loop is resolved.

**Logging requirements**
- Task creation, assignment, start, complete, owner, lock workaround, originals receipt, document start, assessment start, legal review, supplement, rework, and queue length.

```text
Register < Identity Check
Register < Originals Receipt
Identity Check || Originals Receipt

If originals_required:
    Originals Receipt < Document Check
Else:
    Register < Document Check
    Identity Check || Document Check

Identity Check < Assessment
Document Check < Assessment

If legal_required:
    Assessment < Legal Review
    Legal Review < Request Supplement
    Request Supplement < Document Check
    Document Check < Assessment
    Assessment < Legal Review   # supplement loop
    Legal Review < (Approve xor Reject)
Else:
    Assessment < (Approve xor Reject)

(Approve xor Reject) < Notify
```

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

| design element | specification | causal purpose | operational safeguard | process-mining measurement |

| duration | 12–16 weeks plus washout | Captures learning and queue stabilization | Interim review at 6 and 10 weeks | Time-stamped period effects |
| stop rules | Stop if p90 worsens >15%, rework +3 pp, or legal backlog >20% for 2 weeks | Protects quality and tail performance | Independent review | p90, rework, legal backlog |

The pilot separates mechanism failure from implementation failure by measuring fidelity and effect separately. Implementation failure is indicated when intended tasks are not created concurrently, true overlap stays low, lock workarounds are frequent, or timestamps are back-entered. Mechanism failure is indicated when true overlap rises but cycle time does not improve because assessment or legal capacity absorbs the savings. If digital cases show overlap and shorter waiting but legal cases do not, that is effect modification, not failed implementation. The pilot should therefore report: intended start times, actual start times, true overlap, queue lengths, and stratum-specific cycle times. Expansion should require both fidelity and effect; otherwise the correct action is to fix the lock/process or abandon the mechanism for that subgroup.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, Transition, OperatorPOWL, SilentTransition

# ------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Site A before and after process trees
# ------------------------------------------------------------
site_a_before_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

site_a_after_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', "
    "X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_before_tree)
net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_after_tree)

# ------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Site B candidate POWL
# This models the originals-required path plus an optional legal-review
# branch with a simplified supplement loop. For digital/no-originals cases,
# remove Originals Receipt and allow Identity Check || Document Check.
# ------------------------------------------------------------
register = Transition("Register")
identity = Transition("Identity Check")
originals = Transition("Originals Receipt")
document = Transition("Document Check")
assessment = Transition("Assessment")
legal = Transition("Legal Review")
supplement = Transition("Request Supplement")
approve = Transition("Approve")
reject = Transition("Reject")
notify = Transition("Notify")
tau = SilentTransition()

# Main strict partial order: Assessment waits for both Identity and Document.
main = StrictPartialOrder([register, identity, originals, document, assessment])
main.add_edge(register, identity)
main.add_edge(register, originals)
main.add_edge(originals, document)   # statutory gate when originals are required
main.add_edge(identity, assessment)  # Assessment waits for Identity Check
main.add_edge(document, assessment)  # Assessment waits for Document Check

# Legal branch: after Assessment, either skip legal (tau) or enter legal review.
# Simplified loop: Legal Review -> Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment -> Legal Review.
legal_repeat = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.LOOP,
    [
        legal,
        OperatorPOWL(Operator.SEQUENCE, [supplement, document, assessment])
    ]
)
legal_choice = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [legal_repeat, tau])

# Final decision and notification.
decision = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [approve, reject])

# Top-level sequence: verification/assessment, optional legal loop, decision, notify.
site_b_candidate = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.SEQUENCE,
    [main, legal_choice, decision, notify]
)

# ------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Convert POWL candidate to accepting Petri net
# ------------------------------------------------------------
net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_b_candidate)

# ------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Check workflow-net / soundness properties
# ------------------------------------------------------------
for name, net, im, fm in [
    ("A_before", net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before),
    ("A_after", net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after),
    ("B_candidate", net_b, im_b, fm_b),
]:
    try:
        is_wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    except Exception as exc:
        is_wf = f"workflow check error: {exc}"

    try:
        is_sound = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)
    except Exception as exc:
        is_sound = f"soundness check error: {exc}"

    print(name, "workflow:", is_wf, "soundness:", is_sound)

# ------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. Optional playout
# ------------------------------------------------------------
try:
    traces = pm4py.play_out(net_b, im_b, fm_b, num_traces=5)
    print("Sample Site B traces:")
    for trace in traces:
        print(trace)
except Exception as exc:
    print("Playout skipped:", exc)

# ------------------------------------------------------------
# 6. What this model cannot encode
# ------------------------------------------------------------
# - Data-dependent legal flags: whether Legal Review is required depends on
#   public-sector/high-value case attributes, not control flow alone.
# - Data-dependent originals requirement: digital cases remove Originals Receipt
#   and allow Identity Check || Document Check.
# - Resource capacity: identity=4, document=7, assessment=3, legal=2.
# - Case locks: the legacy system may serialize active task ownership.
# - Durations, queueing, WIP limits, and service-time distributions.
# - Probabilistic rework and appeal behavior.
# - Timestamp reliability and back-entry workarounds.
```

## 9. Recommendation

`conditional go`

Transfer the mechanism, not the slogan. Site A’s useful transferable idea is that legally independent verification tasks should be allowed to overlap when prerequisites permit, with queue discipline and capacity controls. Site B should not copy “create Identity Check and Document Check simultaneously immediately after Register for every case.” That violates Site B’s statutory originals rule for 62% of cases and ignores 40% legal review, separate roles, the legacy case lock, and supplement loops.

The first subgroup should be digital/no-originals cases, especially non-legal, lower-complexity claims. For these cases, create Identity Check and Document Check concurrently after Register. For originals-required cases, only create Identity Check and Originals Receipt concurrently; Document Check must wait for Originals Receipt. Assessment must still wait for both Identity Check and Document Check. Legal Review and supplement loops must remain intact.

Expected benefits are modest: overall median cycle time may fall by roughly 1–3 hours if true overlap is achieved in the digital subgroup, with possible p90 improvement only if assessment and legal queues stay stable. Risks are bottleneck shift, IT-lock failure, false overlap from back-entered timestamps, and rework growth. The pilot should measure true overlap, actual task ownership, queue lengths, rework, p90, and legal backlog.

Expansion triggers: in digital/non-legal strata, true overlap >50%, median cycle time improves by at least 2 hours, p90 does not worsen by more than 5%, rework does not rise by more than 1 percentage point, and legal backlog remains stable. Modification triggers: benefits appear only in digital cases, originals cases show no gain, or assessment/legal queues grow. Abandonment triggers: true overlap remains low despite task creation, p90 worsens materially, rework rises, or legal backlog increases. Thus, proceed conditionally with a segmented pilot and staged expansion.

## 10. Assumption register

1. True overlap, not task-creation overlap, is the active mechanism — needed because Site A’s 71% overlap may be timestamp-biased — test with audit-log task start/end and lock-workaround counts — if false, the intervention is mostly symbolic and should be abandoned.
2. Site B’s digital/no-originals subgroup is approximately 38% — needed to estimate eligible share and pilot power — test against intake codes and document-requirement flags — if smaller, overall benefit is lower and the pilot must be longer.
3. Document Check cannot legally start before Originals Receipt for 62% of cases — needed to avoid illegal parallelization — test with legal review of intake rules — if false, more cases could enter the digital-style parallel treatment.
4. The legacy case lock can be bypassed or upgraded enough to allow simultaneous ownership in the pilot — needed for true concurrency — test with IT workflow tests and event-log audits — if false, implementation failure will dominate and the pilot should focus on IT.
5. Assessment and legal capacity are the main downstream constraints — needed to anticipate bottleneck shift — test by queue-length and WIP monitoring during pilot — if false, tail risk may be lower than expected.
6. Legal Review and supplement loops will remain statistically similar under faster verification — needed to separate verification gains from legal rework — test by comparing supplement rates by arm — if false, legal rework may offset cycle-time gains.
7. Daily queue huddles and WIP caps can be applied consistently across pilot clusters — needed to avoid queue-discipline confounding — test with adherence logs and queue dashboards — if false, treatment effect estimates will be biased.
8. The pilot has enough cases per stratum to detect a 2-hour median-cycle difference and monitor p90 — needed to distinguish signal from noise — test with accrual and minimum-support calculations — if false, expansion decisions should rely on fidelity and safety signals rather than small effect estimates.
