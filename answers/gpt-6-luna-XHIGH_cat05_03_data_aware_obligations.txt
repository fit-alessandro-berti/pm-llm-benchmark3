## 1. Temporal Data Semantics

Treat each case as an event-ordered stream. Sort by UTC timestamp, then by event_index within that timestamp. A nonblank attribute value updates the case state at that event’s timestamp; a blank means carry forward the last known value. Thus a release uses the last updates ordered before or at that release, and later values never flow backward. Under the stipulated same-time tie-break, an event with a lower event_index is earlier.

Use the event timestamp as the effective time of an update unless the log or other evidence establishes a different effective time. Keep distinct: event time (when the business event is said to occur), effective time (when a value or approval governs), record time (when a system stored it), and evidence time (for example, a qualified signature timestamp or a witness’s account). A later record can evidence an earlier approval, but the record’s displayed backdated fields alone do not prove it.

For these rules, treat a known risk, customer-type, or jurisdiction change as material for obligations that depend on that attribute. For amount-dependent reviews, apply R4: an increase greater than 20% after the latest Standard_Review or Enhanced_Review invalidates that review; a decrease alone does not. An increase from an unknown baseline leaves the review’s amount validity indeterminate. Final case attributes are not release-time evidence unless a reliable event-time record establishes that they already applied.

Missing decisive values stay unknown. In particular, do not infer a low amount or low risk from blank fields. Classify a trigger as indeterminate when the missing evidence prevents establishing or excluding it, unless other evidence resolves the uncertainty.

## 2. Normalized Rule Table

| Rule | Trigger event and condition | Required prior/subsequent event | Ordering constraint | State snapshot used | Exception | Missing-data treatment | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Release_Funds where current jurisdiction is not DE and amount is at least EUR 100,000 | Enhanced_Review | Enhanced_Review must precede release and follow the latest material change relevant to the obligation; amount validity is subject to R4 | Latest known release-time jurisdiction and amount; review-time state and intervening changes | Matching exemption valid under R5 may waive R1 | Indeterminate if amount or jurisdiction is missing and the other known values do not rule out the trigger | Ordered updates; review completion and reviewed state; exemption approval, scope, effective and expiry evidence |
| R2 | Release_Funds where current risk is HIGH or customer_type is PEP | Both Enhanced_Review and Compliance_Approve | Enhanced_Review < Compliance_Approve < Release_Funds; approval must follow the relevant enhanced review | Release-time risk and customer type; relevant review and approval sequence | No exception stated in the policy | A known HIGH or PEP value activates the rule even if the other field is missing; otherwise missing trigger fields may make activation indeterminate | Risk/customer updates and effective times; review type; approval event and ordering |
| R3 | Release_Funds when the release jurisdiction was added to the sanctions list after the latest Sanctions_Screen and before release | New Sanctions_Screen | List update < new screen < release | Jurisdiction at the list update and release; ordered screening and list events | No exception stated | Indeterminate if jurisdiction or relevant list/screen evidence is missing | Jurisdiction-specific list version/update and screening timestamps |
| R4 | Amount increase after the latest Standard_Review or Enhanced_Review | Review must be repeated for amount-dependent obligations if increase exceeds 20% | Compare each amount update after the review and before the obligation point; update is effective at its ordered event position | Amount at review and successive known amount updates | Decrease alone does not invalidate | Unknown starting amount makes an increase unclassifiable absent other evidence | Amount provenance, before/after values, event ordering, and the review’s amount basis |

| R6 | Any obligation whose trigger depends on amount, risk, jurisdiction, or customer type | Preserve unknown state; resolve only with reliable evidence | Apply the latest known value at the relevant event, not the eventual final value | Relevant event-time snapshot | No implicit low-risk or low-value default | Indeterminate when a decisive trigger cannot be established or excluded | Reliable event-time payloads and attribute provenance |
| R7 | Every obligation evaluation | Reconstruct ordered prefix through the relevant event | Compare (timestamp, event_index) lexicographically; lower index is earlier at equal timestamps | State after applying the ordered event prefix through the event under review | None | Uncertainty about ordering can make the result indeterminate if it changes the state or required sequence | Stable event index, timestamp source, and correction history |

Formal notation: e < f means (time(e), event_index(e)) is lexicographically earlier than (time(f), event_index(f)). state(c,e,a) is the value of attribute a after applying all nonblank updates for case c at events x ≤ e; latest(c,e,P) is the latest event satisfying predicate P with x < e. “After review” and “before release” below use this same strict ordering.

- **R1:** For release r, let J = state(c,r,jurisdiction) and A = state(c,r,amount). If J ≠ DE and A ≥ 100000, require an Enhanced_Review h with h < r, h after the latest relevant material change before r, and not invalidated for the amount obligation under R4, unless a matching R5-valid exemption waives R1 at r.
- **R2:** If state(c,r,risk_level) = HIGH or state(c,r,customer_type) = PEP, require an Enhanced_Review h and Compliance_Approve p such that h < p < r; h must be relevant to the trigger state and not invalidated for an amount-dependent obligation under R4.
- **R3:** For each applicable list-addition event u for the release jurisdiction, where the latest relevant Sanctions_Screen before r is earlier than u and u < r, require a Sanctions_Screen s with u < s < r.
- **R4:** For review v and amount update u with v < u < r, a known increase where amount(u) > 1.20 × amount immediately before u invalidates v for amount-dependent obligations. A decrease does not itself invalidate. If the prior amount is unknown, the invalidation result is unknown without additional evidence.
- **R5:** valid_exemption(x,r) only if approved_by(x)=Head_Compliance, approval is evidenced as existing at r, effective_from(x) ≤ time(r) < expiry(x), and trigger-time jurisdiction, customer type, and amount match scope. A database record time after r is compatible with validity only when other evidence establishes timely approval.
- **R6:** If a trigger predicate evaluates to unknown from the event-time state, return INDETERMINATE unless other reliable evidence resolves it. Never coerce missing values to a safe default.
- **R7:** Apply updates in ascending (timestamp,event_index) order. An update at the same timestamp with a greater event_index is later and cannot affect an earlier event at that timestamp.

## 4. Ambiguities and Alternative Readings

- **TX-305:** The release amount is unknown, so R1 cannot be activated or excluded. If the 150,000 settlement amount were independently tied to the immutable release payload, R1 would apply; with the logged Enhanced_Review before release and no intervening logged change, the event sequence could satisfy R1. That linkage is expressly absent here. If the release amount was below 100,000, R1 would not apply. Evidence that the Enhanced_Review was not actually completed, or that a material change occurred after it, would instead support NONCOMPLIANT. Settlement-time knowledge alone cannot be copied backward.
- **TX-310:** On the chosen reading, the evidence does not resolve whether approval existed at 10:00, so the case is INDETERMINATE. If an authenticated contemporaneous oral-approval record or reliable witness evidence established Head_Compliance approval at 08:45, the scope and effective window would support JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. If the qualified certificate’s 10:30 creation is treated as definitive proof that no prior approval existed, the absent Enhanced_Review makes the case NONCOMPLIANT. The displayed backdated PDF field by itself is insufficient.
- **TX-302:** EX-77 is supported as timely despite the late database insertion because the qualified signature timestamp is 09:05, before the trigger. The Enhanced_Review also independently satisfies R1. If the organization records cases that invoked a valid waiver as JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION even when the underlying review was performed, that label could be used; the substantive obligations are still met either way. If signature verification or the outage evidence were invalidated, the exemption would need reassessment, but the logged Enhanced_Review still satisfies R1 on this trace.
- **TX-311:** Event_index makes the Enhanced_Review earlier than the DE update at the shared 16:20 timestamp. The release snapshot is DE, so R1’s non-DE condition is false and the earlier review is immaterial to R1. If the source ordering were wrong and the DE update were actually effective only after release, release jurisdiction could be non-DE; at 115,000, R1 would then apply, and the review preceding that material jurisdiction update would not meet the stated after-change condition. A release payload proving DE at release would reinforce the current conclusion.
- **TX-309:** Given the stated event-effective updates, the 145,000 amount at release follows a 22.9% increase after Enhanced_Review, invalidating that review for the amount-dependent R1 obligation. If the 15:28 amount update were proven to take effect only after release or were a logging correction rather than a real change, the earlier Enhanced_Review could remain valid.
- **TX-307:** A different interpretation that any Compliance_Approve after any review suffices would change the approval analysis, but it conflicts with the supplied fact that this approval covered only a standard packet and with R2’s requirement for Enhanced_Review. Evidence of a separate enhanced review and a subsequent approval would change the result.

def missing(value):
    return pd.isna(value)

def amount_increase(before, after):
    if missing(before) or missing(after):
        return None
    return float(after) > 1.20 * float(before)

def exemption_check(release, exemption_id):
    if missing(exemption_id) or exemption_id not in exemption_by_id.index:
        return {"scope_match": False, "valid_r1_exemption": False,
                "record_time": pd.NaT, "signature_time": pd.NaT}
    ex = exemption_by_id.loc[exemption_id]
    scope_known = not missing(release["state_amount"])

        scope_known
        and release["state_jurisdiction"] == ex["jurisdiction"]
        and release["state_customer_type"] == ex["customer_type"]
        and float(release["state_amount"]) <= float(ex["max_amount"])
        and ex["approved_by"] == "Head_Compliance"
        and ex["waives"] == "R1"
    )
    in_window = ex["effective_from"] <= release["time:timestamp"] < ex["expires"]
    if not scope_match or not in_window:
        valid = False
    elif ex["evidence_status"] == "strong_timely" and ex["signature_time"] <= release["time:timestamp"]:
        valid = True
    else:
        valid = None  # conflicting evidence requires human judgment
    return {
        "scope_match": scope_match,
        "valid_r1_exemption": valid,
        "record_time": ex["record_time"],
        "signature_time": ex["signature_time"],
    }

def review_invalidated(g, review_pos, release_pos):

            increased = amount_increase(before, after)
            if increased is True:
                return "INVALIDATED"
            if increased is None:
                return "UNKNOWN_BASELINE"
    return "VALID"

            if "INVALIDATED" in statuses:
                r4_status = "INVALIDATED"
            elif "UNKNOWN_BASELINE" in statuses:
                r4_status = "UNKNOWN_BASELINE"
            else:
                r4_status = "NO_INVALIDATION_OBSERVED"

        def relevant_enhanced(p):
            after_material = latest_material_pos is None or p > latest_material_pos
            return (
                p < release_pos
                and after_material
                and review_invalidated(g, p, release_pos) == "VALID"
            )

        qualifying_enhanced = [p for p in enhanced_positions if relevant_enhanced(p)]
        ex_info = exemption_check(release, exemption_id)

        if jurisdiction == "DE":
            r1_trigger = False
        elif not missing(amount) and float(amount) < 100000:
            r1_trigger = False
        elif missing(jurisdiction) or missing(amount):
            r1_trigger = None
        else:
            r1_trigger = True

        if r1_trigger is False:
            r1_ok = True
        elif r1_trigger is None:
            r1_ok = None
        elif ex_info["valid_r1_exemption"] is True:
            r1_ok = True
        elif ex_info["valid_r1_exemption"] is None:
            r1_ok = None
        else:
            r1_ok = bool(qualifying_enhanced)

        if risk == "HIGH" or customer_type == "PEP":
            r2_trigger = True
        elif missing(risk) or missing(customer_type):
            r2_trigger = None
        else:
            r2_trigger = False

        qualifying_pairs = []
        for epos in qualifying_enhanced:
            for p in prior.index[prior["concept:name"].eq("Compliance_Approve")].tolist():
                if epos < p < release_pos:
                    qualifying_pairs.append((epos, p))
        if r2_trigger is False:
            r2_ok = True
        elif r2_trigger is None:
            r2_ok = None
        else:
            r2_ok = bool(qualifying_pairs)

        # R3: after the latest matching jurisdiction list update, there must
        # be a screen after that update and before release.
        list_positions = [
            p for p in prior.index
            if prior.loc[p, "concept:name"] == "Sanctions_List_Update"
            and g.iloc[p]["state_jurisdiction"] == jurisdiction
        ]
        screen_positions = prior.index[prior["concept:name"].eq("Sanctions_Screen")].tolist()
        if missing(jurisdiction):
            r3_trigger = None
            r3_ok = None
            latest_list_pos = None
        elif not list_positions:
            r3_trigger = False
            r3_ok = True
            latest_list_pos = None
        else:
            latest_list_pos = list_positions[-1]
            r3_trigger = not any(p > latest_list_pos for p in screen_positions)
            r3_ok = not r3_trigger

        unknown_flags = {
            "amount_unknown": missing(amount),
            "risk_unknown": missing(risk),
            "jurisdiction_unknown": missing(jurisdiction),
            "customer_type_unknown": missing(customer_type),
        }
        rows.append({
            "case": case_id,
            "release_time": release["time:timestamp"],
            "release_event_index": int(release["event_index"]),
            "amount_at_release": amount,
            "risk_at_release": risk,
            "jurisdiction_at_release": jurisdiction,
            "customer_type_at_release": customer_type,
            "exemption_id": exemption_id,
            "r1_trigger": r1_trigger,
            "r1_ok_coarse": r1_ok,
            "r2_trigger": r2_trigger,
            "r2_ok_coarse": r2_ok,
            "r3_trigger": r3_trigger,
            "r3_ok_coarse": r3_ok,
            "r4_status": r4_status,
            "exemption_scope_match": ex_info["scope_match"],
            "valid_r1_exemption": ex_info["valid_r1_exemption"],
            "exemption_record_time": ex_info["record_time"],
            "exemption_signature_time": ex_info["signature_time"],
            "last_standard_review_time": latest_time("Standard_Review"),
            "last_enhanced_review_time": latest_time("Enhanced_Review"),
            "last_compliance_approve_time": latest_time("Compliance_Approve"),
            "last_sanctions_screen_time": latest_time("Sanctions_Screen"),
            "last_sanctions_list_update_time": latest_time("Sanctions_List_Update"),
            "latest_material_change_time": latest_material_time,
            "unknown_amount": unknown_flags["amount_unknown"],
            "unknown_risk": unknown_flags["risk_unknown"],
            "unknown_jurisdiction": unknown_flags["jurisdiction_unknown"],
            "unknown_customer_type": unknown_flags["customer_type_unknown"],
        })

diagnostics = pd.DataFrame(rows)
print(diagnostics.to_string(index=False))
~~~

This implementation uses a deliberately coarse material-change definition and event-level review records. Production use should validate those against the organization’s exact definitions and capture what each review and approval actually covered.

## 6. Why Control-Flow-Only Conformance Is Insufficient

1. **Final-state leakage:** A case-level final value such as DE can be incorrectly assigned to an earlier release or review, even though it was updated afterward. Conversely, an earlier high-risk state may be hidden by a later low-risk value.
2. **Post-hoc exemptions:** A trace can contain Record_Exemption after Release_Funds and look as though the exemption existed in time. Control-flow order alone cannot distinguish a valid late database insert from a backdated field unsupported by timely evidence.
3. **Material changes after review:** The same sequence Enhanced_Review → Release_Funds can be compliant for an unchanged amount and invalid after a >20% increase between those events. Activity order alone does not compare values or review validity.
4. **Conditional activation:** Enhanced review is required only for specified release-time combinations, and sanctions re-screening depends on a list update for the release jurisdiction. A generic “review before release” constraint neither evaluates those predicates nor checks jurisdiction-specific list state.
5. **Missing data:** A trace with no amount update does not establish that amount was low or below threshold. Control-flow conformance treats absent data like no event, not like an unknown trigger condition.
6. **Same-timestamp ordering:** Timestamp-only sorting can put TX-311’s DE update before its review even though event_index says the review came first. That changes the inferred review context.
7. **Event semantics and scope:** A Standard_Review is not an Enhanced_Review, and an approval of a standard packet does not satisfy R2. Activity labels and event presence cannot establish review type, packet content, or exemption scope without payload semantics.
8. **Evidence strength and provenance:** Trace order cannot tell whether a signature existed at approval time, whether a witness is reliable, whether an amount came from the release or later settlement, or whether an update is a correction.

## 7. Recommended Logging and Control Improvements

- Store an immutable release payload containing amount, risk, jurisdiction, customer type, exemption reference, and the exact values used by the decision engine. Include a unique payload hash and link it to the release event.
- Record separate event, effective, record, and evidence timestamps with timezone and source. For approvals, retain the qualified signature timestamp, signer identity and authority, certificate validation result, and any later database insertion time.
- Version the policy and decision engine at each release. Log the triggered predicates, required controls, exemption decision, and the specific input snapshot used.
- Make review and approval records identify review type, covered amount and attributes, case-data version or hash, reviewer, completion time, and packet reference. Record whether Compliance_Approve followed a particular Enhanced_Review.
- Store exemption scope as structured jurisdiction, customer-type, amount range, effective_from, expiry, waivers, approver, and approval evidence. Preserve amendments and revocations as append-only events.
- For every attribute update, retain old and new values, source system, actor, reason, effective time, record time, and correction/supersession links. Distinguish an unknown value from an explicit null or deletion.
- Use a stable per-case sequence number or event_index generated transactionally, and preserve it through exports. Define deterministic ordering for events that share a timestamp.
- Require an explicit “data complete” or “amount unavailable” state before release. Route unresolved trigger fields and contradictory exemption evidence to a hold or documented human decision rather than silently defaulting them.
