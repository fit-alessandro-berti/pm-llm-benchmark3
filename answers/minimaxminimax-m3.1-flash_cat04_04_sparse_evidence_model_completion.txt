**Why two directions on a DFG edge do not prove concurrency.** A DFG is a *projection* (antifactorial) of the log: it records, for each ordered pair of activity names, the number of traces in which the second activity *immediately* followed the first somewhere. The coexistence of `CE` (17) and `EC` (11) therefore proves one thing only: **the relative order of C and E varies across cases**. It is equiconsistent with (a) true concurrent execution whose completion order varies, (b) a probabilistic/stochastic sequencing decision whose outcome varies, (c) two different deterministic routes selected by an attribute, and (d) ingestion tiebreak artifacts (S7). A single trace can contain only one direction, so both directions can only live in the *aggregate*. Two further points: (i) the DFG is **not a model** — it contains the cycle `CE`, which, read as control flow, would be an illegal loop (it is an artifact of aggregation across cases); (ii) the DFG is *also* not evidence of overlap: with completiononly logs, two activities that ran at the same time are still serialised by their completion instants. Consequently a "concurrency" claim requires timestamps (start/end), i.e. exactly the instrument missing under D7/S7. All three candidate models below therefore encode **absence of precedence** rather than a factual claim of simultaneity, and the difference between them is stated as a difference in *logical freedom*, with the temporal reading flagged.

# 2. Shared Assumptions

- **A1 — Eventcompletion semantics.** All 30 traces are logs of *completion* events of atomic, singleoccurrence activities. Durations, queueing, resource contention and start timestamps are outside the model. "Concurrent" in a model means "the model imposes no precedence and the net permits overlap", not "the tasks overlapped in the cases observed". Each activity occurs **at most once per case** (A6 test in §8 asserts this).
- **A2 — Missing Supplier Confirmation events.** Two mechanisms are observationally equivalent in this log: *genuine bypass* (stocked component, S4) and *integration loss* (S5). No controlflow construct in PM4Py can express "the activity happened but was not logged", so all three models realise Sabsence with a **single silent skip branch**, semantically overloaded as "S did not apply *or* S was lost". §8's missingness bound (7 Sless traces vs. a 5–10 % loss rate   4 genuine skips) is the argument that the *optionality* reading dominates; the loss reading is retained as a residual uncertainty and flagged for the data plan. Consequence: **no model can flag a lost S event**, and no model can enforce S3/S4 conditionally.
- **A3 — Absent `external/stocked` attribute.** All models are attributeblind. Conditional rules (S3/S4, D3style segment rules) are *projected* onto the controlflow level: a conditional requirement becomes an unconditioned optional branch. This is an abstraction with two distinct consequences: (i) **recall loss** — the model cannot warn that S was wrongly skipped for an external order; (ii) **precision loss** — the skip branch is accepted for *every* case, not only stocked ones. No model in this document conditions on an attribute, because no observed trace exposes one.
- **A4 — Representation of optionality.** Optionality is represented as an *exclusive choice between an activity and a silent transition* (POWL `OperatorPOWL(XOR, [Transition, SilentTransition])`; ProcessTree `XOR` with a silent leaf). It is **not** represented as "activity may occur zero or one times inside a loop"; there are no loops at all. Optionality therefore encodes *projected, caselevel* absence.
- **A5 — Semantic rigidity of the skeleton.** `Register Order  …  Approve Configuration  Release Production  Archive Case`, with APZ adjacent in 30/30 traces and confirmed by D1/D2, is treated as a rigid sequence in all three models. The assumption that **no unmodelled activity exists between A and P** (e.g. a downstream posting step) is an assumption, not a finding; it is listed as a datacollection item in §9.
- **A6 — Sampling.** The 30 traces are treated as i.i.d. draws from the population of *completed* cases. This is a **known selection bias**: there is no evidence about aborted, returned or reworked cases, so all three models are models of the *completion funnel* only.
- **A7 — Label hygiene.** Activity names are assumed unambiguous (no aliasing between distinct business steps sharing a label) and the catalogue is assumed complete (no unlogged activity). This assumption is loadbearing for §10's alternative interpretation (label aliasing would invalidate the balanced model's C/E symmetry).
- **A8 — Concurrency semantics.** Where the models leave two activities unordered, the resulting Petri net *permits* overlap. The models are therefore "logically permissive, temporally silent": they can be read either as concurrency claims or as unknownorder claims, and the validation code reports the visible language, not a temporal claim.

# 3. Conservative Candidate

**Inductive bias.** Maximise *descriptive fidelity to the observed support*: the model's language is exactly the set of observed label sequences, so it makes no commitment that the 30 cases cannot support. It is deliberately agnostic on the two questions the evidence cannot settle (C/E concurrency and the cause of Sabsence): the two observed C/E orders are encoded as two *mutually exclusive routes* rather than as parallel branches, and Sabsence is encoded as a distinct observed route rather than as an attributeconditioned skip. The model is a *description* and deliberately not an *explanation*; it is the right baseline when the question is "is this case like something we have actually seen?", and the wrong instrument for anything else.

**Compact formal expression.** With `R=Register Order, C=Credit Check, E=Engineering Review, S=Supplier Confirmation, X=Expedite Review, A=Approve Configuration, P=Release Production, Z=Archive Case`, `` exclusive choice, `;` sequence:

```
_cons  =   { R;C;E;S;A;P;Z | R;E;C;S;A;P;Z | R;C;E;A;P;Z | R;E;C;A;P;Z
                | R;C;S;E;A;P;Z | R;C;E;S;X;A;P;Z | R;E;C;X;S;A;P;Z }
L(_cons) = those 7 sequences, each fireable exactly once; no silent behaviour, no other sequence.
```

**Code (self-contained; assigns `model_conservative`).**

```python
# ---- Conservative candidate: XOR enumeration of the 7 observed variants ----
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree, Operator

OBSERVED_VARIANTS = [
    ["Register Order","Credit Check","Engineering Review","Supplier Confirmation","Approve Configuration","Release Production","Archive Case"],          # V1 x12
    ["Register Order","Engineering Review","Credit Check","Supplier Confirmation","Approve Configuration","Release Production","Archive Case"],          # V2 x7
    ["Register Order","Credit Check","Engineering Review","Approve Configuration","Release Production","Archive Case"],                              # V3 x4
    ["Register Order","Engineering Review","Credit Check","Approve Configuration","Release Production","Archive Case"],                              # V4 x3
    ["Register Order","Credit Check","Supplier Confirmation","Engineering Review","Approve Configuration","Release Production","Archive Case"],          # V5 x2
    ["Register Order","Credit Check","Engineering Review","Supplier Confirmation","Expedite Review","Approve Configuration","Release Production","Archive Case"],  # V6 x1
    ["Register Order","Engineering Review","Credit Check","Expedite Review","Supplier Confirmation","Approve Configuration","Release Production","Archive Case"],  # V7 x1
]

def _seq_tree(labels):
    """Left-nested SEQUENCE tree over activity leaves."""
    root = None
    for lab in labels:
        leaf = ProcessTree(name=lab)          # leaf: no children, name = activity
        if root is None:
            root = leaf
        else:
            node = ProcessTree()
            node.operator = Operator.SEQUENCE
            node.children = [root, leaf]
            root = node
    return root

model_conservative = ProcessTree(name="conservative_enumeration")
model_conservative.operator = Operator.XOR
model_conservative.children = [_seq_tree(v) for v in OBSERVED_VARIANTS]
# -> model_conservative is a ProcessTree; PM4Py can convert it to a Petri net (see §8 fallback).
```

**Deliberately excluded behaviour.** Any trace that is not one of the 7 sequences — e.g. `R,C,E,X,S,A,P,Z` (X before S without the V7 shape), `R,E,S,C,A,P,Z` (S before C), `R,C,E,X,A,P,Z` (X without S), `R,C,S,E,X,A,P,Z` (S before E *and* X after E), any SafterA sequence, any X after A, two X's, two S's, any activity before R or after Z, any partial trace (e.g. aborted orders — see A6), and any permutation of C/E that cooccurs with the *other* optionalities (e.g. `R,E,C,S,X,A,P,Z`).

**Known underfitting risks.**
1. **Zero generalisation.** The language *is* the sample. Every unseen but legitimate variant is reported as nonconformant; with 30 cases from a heterogeneous order book, unobserved legitimate behaviour is likely (partial orders, multicomponent orders, aborted/reworked cases).
2. **No conditionality.** S3/S4 cannot be expressed: the model cannot distinguish "stocked component" from "lost event" from "external component wrongly skipped" — a compliance obligation can hide inside an accepted skip.
3. **No causal or temporal content.** C/E appear as two routes, never as potentially overlapping work; the model is unusable for leadtime, queueing or capacity analysis, and it will *not* explain why 63 % of cases start with C.
4. **Falsifiability is broken.** Because fitness = 1.0 and precision = 1.0 *by construction*, standard conformance metrics are vacuous and can be used to give false assurance ("100 % conformance" against a model that was fitted to the same 30 cases).
5. **Sampleoverfitting.** The 7 variants are treated as a complete taxonomy; the 12/7/4/3/2/1/1 frequencies are ignored, so the model cannot tell a rare legitimate route from a defect.
6. **Maintenance cost.** Adding one new legitimate variant requires editing the tree; the enumeration gives no guidance on *where* to generalise.

**Duplicate labels and scalability (required discussion).** The XOR repeats every activity label across branches (here 7 instances of `Register Order`, 7 of `A/P/Z`, etc.). Consequences: (i) *Ambiguity*: labelidentical transitions in different branches are only disambiguated by the branch structure, so any labellevel analysis (token replay, activityfiltered views, "selfloops" induced by merging samenamed transitions) can create false cycles or false precedences; the model must be aligned against the **net**, not against labels. (ii) *Size*: the net has  7 × 8 = 56 activity transitions plus branch structure — fine here, but enumeration is combinatorial in the number of ordersensitive activities: with k such activities and both orders allowed the requirement is O(k!) (8 activities  40 320 branches), so enumeration is only tractable while the "unordered set" is small — exactly why the balanced partialorder model exists. (iii) *Loss of generality semantics*: an enumeration cannot express "C may be followed by S *when* …" and cannot be refined monotonically.

# 4. Balanced Candidate

**Inductive bias.** *Structural generalisation with minimal commitment.* Mandatory versus optional is separated explicitly; a precedence edge is asserted **only** where (i) a rule of at least medium confidence asserts it, or (ii) it holds in 100 % of observations **and** no medium+ rule contradicts it. Where two readings of a lowconfidence rule coexist (D5 on S's release condition), the model represents their **union** instead of silently choosing one. Where the evidence shows order variability (C/E) or flexibility (X/S), **no edge** is added, so the model states "order unknown" rather than "concurrency confirmed". The net effect: every admitted behaviour can be traced to a specific piece of evidence, and every exclusion can be traced to a specific rule.

**Compact formal expression.** `` = silent skip; `PO(·; E)` = partial order over the listed nodes with edge set E; `` = union of two PO branches.

```
_bal = R ; ( PO(C, E, S, X ; {CS, CX, EX})        # S released by the credit check
           PO(C, E, S, X ; {ES, CX, EX}) )      # S released by the engineering review
      ; A ; P ; Z
```

Elementary semantics: `PO` admits **every** permutation of its (optionally reduced) activity multiset that is consistent with its edges; the two branches together admit the union; `S` means "S present exactly once, or absent"; `X` likewise. Consequences: C and E are unordered (both orders admitted, and S may interleave with either); X follows **both** C and E; X is unordered w.r.t. S; S requires at least one completed check before it may start. The resulting language has **exactly 14** distinct label sequences, of which **7 are the observed ones** (derivation in §6/§8).

**Code (self-contained; assigns `model_balanced`, plus a documented sensitivity twin).**

```python
# ---- Balanced candidate: partial orders + explicit uncertainty ----
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition,
                                    SilentTransition, Operator as POWLOperator)

def _optional(activity, silent_name="skip"):
    """Optionality: exclusive choice between the activity and a silent skip."""
    return OperatorPOWL(POWLOperator.XOR,
                        [Transition(activity), SilentTransition(silent_name)])

def _balanced_branch(release_by):
    # release_by in {"credit","engineering"}: which principal check releases S (D5 union)
    cc = Transition("Credit Check")
    er = Transition("Engineering Review")
    sc = _optional("Supplier Confirmation", "skip_supplier_confirmation")
    ex = _optional("Expedite Review", "skip_expedite_review")
    po = StrictPartialOrder([cc, er, sc, ex])
    po.order.add_edge(cc if release_by == "credit" else er, sc)   # S released by one check
    po.order.add_edge(cc, ex)                                     # D6 / S6: X after both checks
    po.order.add_edge(er, ex)
    return po

def _build_balanced(release_options=("credit", "engineering")):
    reg = Transition("Register Order")
    app = Transition("Approve Configuration")
    rel = Transition("Release Production")
    arc = Transition("Archive Case")
    inner = OperatorPOWL(POWLOperator.XOR, [_balanced_branch(r) for r in release_options])
    model = StrictPartialOrder([reg, inner, app, rel, arc])
    model.order.add_edge(reg, inner)
    model.order.add_edge(inner, app)
    model.order.add_edge(app, rel)
    model.order.add_edge(rel, arc)
    return model

model_balanced = _build_balanced()                                 # default: union of both D5 readings
model_balanced_tight = _build_balanced(("credit",))                # sensitivity twin: assert C -> S
```

**How sequence vs. concurrency vs. partial order was decided.** The decision procedure (applied per pair) is: *R1* both orders observed at nonnegligible frequency  **no precedence edge** (unordered); *R2* one order observed and a medium rule asserts the opposite  **assert the rule** (accept the explicit fitness risk); *R3* one order observed and no rule  *either* no edge (recallsafe) *or* an edge flagged as a risk (precisionsafe) — here *R3* applied to XS, where both orders are observed (n=1 each, V6/V7) and S6 explicitly says "before or after", so **no edge**; *R4* an activity absent in 2 cases, with absence **not** mostly explainable by logging loss  **optional branch**; *R5* a precedence between mandatory activities is asserted only if it holds in 100 % of observations and no medium rule contradicts it.

* **C vs E.** R1: CE in 19 traces, EC in 11. Both orders occur; therefore *no edge*. Note the statistical care required here: against a **deterministic** order the observation of 11 reversed traces is decisive (p  0 by any likelihood), but against a **stochastic** routing hypothesis the 19:11 split is weak evidence (onesided binomial p  0.07) — so the data cannot choose between concurrency and variable sequencing. Crucially, *both* hypotheses yield the same **trace language** (each order allowed), so the modelling consequence is robust; only the temporal reading differs (and D7 forbids asserting it). The net semantics allow overlap, which is an *artefact of the "no edge" encoding*, not an empirical concurrency claim (disclosed in A8).
* **S vs C / S vs E.** R5 + D5: S follows C in 19/19 Sbearing traces, but D5 (lowmedium) explicitly licenses "after *either* check". Because a lowconfidence rule that is *permissive* may be honoured without contradicting the data, the model encodes the **union** (two PO branches): the only assertion is "S starts after at least one completed check". The price is two extra language members (`E,S,C…`), and the empirical tension (19/19 after C) is recorded; `model_balanced_tight` (assert `CS` only) is provided as the precisionoriented sensitivity twin, and is the variant I would switch to if starttimestamps confirm that S is released by the credit check.
* **S vs X.** R3: V6 has SX, V7 has XS, and S6 permits both  **no edge**; X is unordered w.r.t. S.
* **X vs C/E.** R2: D6 (medium) + S6 (medium) + 2/2 observations  **edges `CX`, `EX`** (X after *both* checks).
* **S, X vs A.** R5 + D3/D4 (mediumhigh/medium) + 30/30 and 23/23 observations  **edges SA, CA, EA** (via the wrapper edge `innerA`).
* **APZ.** D1/D2 (high) + 30/30 adjacency  rigid **sequence**.
* **S vs R.** Enforced by the release constraint: S cannot be the first postregistration activity in this model (a permissive reading of D5 would allow it — that is precisely the §5 contrast).

**Deliberately excluded behaviour.** `R,S,C,E,…` (S before any check completes — violates the D5 release condition); `R,C,A,…` (approval before E completes — D3); `R,C,E,S,A,P,Z` without A (D2 via the rigid skeleton); any S **after** A (D4); any X after A (D6/S6); two X's (D6 "at most once"); two S's; anything before R or after Z (D1); S and X in a *fixed* order (no edge  both admitted); X without the prior completion of both checks (D6); activity ordering between A and P (rigid).

**Known underfitting risks.**
1. **Seven unseen language members** ( 50 % of the language is not exhibited by the sample): two of them require S to be released by the engineering review (`…E,S,C…`), three require X without S or X before S with both checks, and two combine SbeforeC with X. If the truth is narrower, the model raises false nonconformity alarms on 7 shape families.
2. **The optional S branch is an unenforceable hole.** Because Sabsence is unconditioned (A3), a genuine compliance breach (external component without S) is *accepted* by the model. This is a systematic, silent underdetection risk — the opposite failure mode to the conservative model's overdetection.
3. **Projected optionality may be spurious.** The model offers XwithoutS (never observed); if X is in fact triggered by an externalcomponent identification, the model licenses an impossible route.
4. **No explanatory power about *why*.** The model explains which orders are allowed, not what makes a case exhibit them (no attribute conditioning, no priority signal); it cannot tell operations *when* to escalate.
5. **Robustness to the "engineering release" reading is bought with precision.** If D5's "either" is only a colloquialism, the tight twin (`model_balanced_tight`) is strictly better and the default model overadmits.
6. **Survivorship/A6.** The model covers only completed cases; if aborts or rework exist, all three models underfit that part of the lifecycle.

# 5. Permissive Candidate

**Inductive bias.** *Treat the broad rules as the rules.* Assert only what a cited rule (D1–D6) or the mandatoryactivity evidence forbids; interpret every permissive statement maximally: D5's "need not wait for both" is read as "may start as soon as the order is registered", D3/D4/D6 are honoured exactly, and the optionality of S and X is always available. The model is deliberately *not* a consistency check — it is a **hypothesis space**: the set of behaviours a stakeholder could plausibly claim are legitimate. Its purpose is (a) sensitivity analysis ("how much does my KPI change if the truth is at the permissive end?"), and (b) a null model against which anomalies can be judged.

**Compact formal expression.**

```
_perm = R ; PO(C, E, S, X ; {CX, EX}) ; A ; P ; Z
```

i.e. the two principal checks are unordered and mandatory, the approve/release/archive tail is rigid, X (if present) follows both checks and precedes A, and **S has no predecessor at all**: it may start immediately after registration, between the checks, or after them, and it may be absent. The language contains **exactly 18** distinct label sequences, of which **7** are observed (11 unseen).

**Code (self-contained; assigns `model_permissive`).**

```python
# ---- Permissive candidate: minimal constraint set consistent with D1-D6 ----
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition,
                                    SilentTransition, Operator as POWLOperator)

def _optional(activity, silent_name="skip"):
    return OperatorPOWL(POWLOperator.XOR,
                        [Transition(activity), SilentTransition(silent_name)])

def _build_permissive():
    reg = Transition("Register Order")
    cc  = Transition("Credit Check")
    er  = Transition("Engineering Review")
    sc  = _optional("Supplier Confirmation", "skip_supplier_confirmation")
    ex  = _optional("Expedite Review", "skip_expedite_review")
    inner = StrictPartialOrder([cc, er, sc, ex])
    inner.order.add_edge(cc, ex)        # D6/S6: X after both principal checks
    inner.order.add_edge(er, ex)
    # NOTE: no edge into `sc` -> S may start before either check (maximally permissive D5)
    app = Transition("Approve Configuration")
    rel = Transition("Release Production")
    arc = Transition("Archive Case")
    model = StrictPartialOrder([reg, inner, app, rel, arc])
    model.order.add_edge(reg, inner)
    model.order.add_edge(inner, app)
    model.order.add_edge(app, rel)
    model.order.add_edge(rel, arc)
    return model

model_permissive = _build_permissive()
```

**Boundary: "reasonable generalisation" vs. still forbidden.**

*Admitted (reasonable generalisation).* (i) Any permutation of the observed activity inventory in which no cited rule is violated. (ii) Any reordering of C/E. (iii) Any optionality of S or X that is exercised by at least one observed variant. (iv) S starting at any point after registration, including before the checks complete (D5 taken maximally, S3/S4 unresolved). (v) X before or after S, with or without S. (vi) Both Sabsence causes (stocked component *or* integration loss) folded into one skip branch (A2).

*Forbidden (still not admitted).* Anything a cited rule or the eyewitnessed mandatory activity excludes: A before both checks (D3); P without A and A without P (D2); S after A (D4); X after A or X occurring twice (D6); missing C or missing E (30/30 mandatory); anything before R or after Z (D1); any activity type outside the catalogue (A5); any *repetition* of an activity (singleoccurrence assumption A1 — proposed in §9 as a question, not granted here); any trace that is only a prefix/suffix of the wrapper (i.e. no partial or aborted cases, A6).

**Deliberately excluded behaviour** (same exclusions as the balanced model **minus** the Srelease constraint): `R,C,A,P,Z`; `R,C,E,S,A,P,Z` missing A; `R,C,E,S,A,X,P,Z`; `R,C,E,S,A,P,Z,X`; `R,C,E,A,S,P,Z`; two X's; anything outside the catalogue.

**Known underfitting risks.**
1. **Openendedness begets unsignalability.** Because 11 of 18 language members are unexercised and every unobserved DFG anomaly is "allowed", the model cannot distinguish *novelty that is legitimate* from *novelty that is a defect*. It is excellent as a null hypothesis and useless as a monitor.
2. **Operational hazard.** Permitting S to start before the credit check is, in a supplierprocurement context, potentially the exact behaviour compliance is meant to prevent; a permissive model used "in production" would silently normalise it.
3. **No causal story at all.** It cannot support any claim about *why* a case took a given route, so it produces no actionable insight and cannot support a rootcause analysis.
4. **Metric dilution.** Alignmentbased precision on this model is structurally low ( 7/18 of the language is exercised); any KPI computed on top of it will look "wrong" without explanation.
5. **Underdetection of the very risk the business cares about** (external component without S) — the permissive model is maximally blind there.

Exploration: good | Compliance: unsuitable (accepts D4/D6adjacent hazards and Sbeforechecks). Exploration: **best** (null model, stresstesting, sensitivity bounds) |
| Sensitivity to the unresolved Srelease question (D5) | None (enumeration ignores it) | Explicit and *switchable* (`model_balanced` vs `model_balanced_tight`)  the model encodes the uncertainty instead of hiding it | Maximal freedom (ignores the question)  no information |
| Sensitivity to the "label aliasing" risk (A7) | Low (enumeration is labelliteral) | High: the C/E symmetry assumes the two labels denote the same business step in all 7 variants | High, same reason |
| Cost of updating with new data | High (reenumerate) | Low (add an edge or flip a flag) | Low (add/remove an edge) |
| Known blind spot | Aborted/reworked cases (A6), unmodelled postA activities (A5) | Same | Same |

def _seq_tree(labels):
    root = None
    for lab in labels:
        leaf = ProcessTree(name=lab)
        if root is None:
            root = leaf
        else:
            node = ProcessTree(); node.operator = Operator.SEQUENCE
            node.children = [root, leaf]; root = node
    return root

def _optional(activity, silent_name="skip"):

def _balanced_branch(release_by):

def to_net(model):
    """PM4Py's public converters have moved between releases: try both names."""
    errs = []
    for fname in ("convert_to_petrinet", "convert_to_petri_net"):
        fn = getattr(pm4py, fname, None)
        if fn is None:
            continue
        try:
            net = fn(model)
            if net is not None:
                return net, "pm4py.%s" % fname
        except Exception as e:                                    # pragma: no cover
            errs.append("%s: %s" % (fname, e))
    raise RuntimeError("no converter worked: " + "; ".join(errs))

def flower_net(variants):
    """Manual fallback: XOR-of-sequences as an explicit Petri net (one chain per variant)."""
    net = PetriNet()
    src, sink = Place("src"), Place("sink")
    net.places.add(src); net.places.add(sink); net.initial_marking = {src: 1}
    def arc(s, t, kind):
        try:    a = Arc(s, t, kind)
        except TypeError:
                a = Arc(s, t); a.arc_type = kind
        net.arcs.add(a)
    for i, seq in enumerate(variants):
        prev = src
        for j, act in enumerate(seq):
            t = PTransition(name=act); t.label = act; net.transitions.add(t)
            tgt = sink if j == len(seq) - 1 else Place("p_%d_%d" % (i, j))
            if tgt is not sink: net.places.add(tgt)
            arc(prev, t, "in"); arc(t, tgt, "out")
            prev = tgt
    return net

        return to_net(model)
    except Exception as e:
        if variants is None: raise
        print("   !! converter failed (%s) -> manual flower net" % e)
        return flower_net(variants), "manual flower net"

# ------------------------------------------------- 4. token semantics + enumeration
def split_arcs(net):
    ins, outs = collections.defaultdict(list), collections.defaultdict(list)
    for a in net.arcs:
        kind = getattr(a, "arc_type", None) or getattr(a, "type_", "in")
        if kind == "in":  ins[a.target].append(a.source)
        else:             outs[a.source].append(a.target)
    return ins, outs

def label_of(t):
    lab = getattr(t, "label", None) or getattr(t, "name", None)
    return lab if lab in ACTIVITIES else None      # anything else is invisible (silent)

def initial_marking(net):
    im = {p: 0 for p in net.places}
    im.update(net.initial_marking or {})
    return {p: c for p, c in im.items() if c > 0}

def enabled(ins, marking, t):
    return all(marking.get(p, 0) > 0 for p in ins[t])

def fire(ins, outs, marking, t):
    m = dict(marking)
    for p in ins[t]:  m[p] = m.get(p, 0) - 1
    for p in outs[t]: m[p] = m.get(p, 0) + 1
    return {p: c for p, c in m.items() if c > 0}

def enumerate_language(net, max_len=8, step_cap=40000, mark_cap=400000):
    """Bounded DFS over firing sequences; returns the set of *visible* label tuples.

    For these three models (no loops, each activity at most once) max_len = 8 is the
    maximum possible number of visible steps, so the enumeration is EXACT, not a sample.
    Adding a loop to any model breaks this guarantee (see text)."""
    ins, outs = split_arcs(net)
    im = initial_marking(net)
    results, seen, steps = set(), set(), 0
    stack = [(frozenset(im.items()), ())]
    while stack and steps < step_cap:
        mark, seq = stack.pop()
        if (mark, seq) in seen: continue
        seen.add((mark, seq)); steps += 1
        if len(seq) <= max_len: results.add(seq)
        if len(seen) > mark_cap: break
        for t in net.transitions:
            if enabled(ins, dict(mark), t):
                nm = fire(ins, outs, dict(mark), t)
                lab = label_of(t)
                ns = seq + ((lab,) if lab else ())
                if len(ns) <= max_len + 1:
                    stack.append((frozenset(nm.items()), ns))
    return results

def find_final_marking(net, max_states=20000):
    """A reachable deadlock marking = the accepting final marking for these nets."""
    ins, outs = split_arcs(net)
    im = initial_marking(net)
    seen, stack = set(), [frozenset(im.items())]
    while stack and len(seen) < max_states:
        mark = stack.pop()
        if mark in seen: continue
        seen.add(mark)
        m = dict(mark)
        if not any(enabled(ins, m, t) for t in net.transitions):
            return {p: c for p, c in m.items() if c > 0}
        for t in net.transitions:
            if enabled(ins, m, t):
                stack.append(frozenset(fire(ins, outs, dict(mark), t).items()))
    return {p: c for p, c in dict(im).items() if c > 0}

**How to read the output, and the limits of this validation.**

* **Log reconstruction and DFG check.** `pm4py.discover_dfg` on the reconstructed log reproduces the DFG given in the question **exactly** (all 16 edges and counts) — this confirms that the 7 variant definitions and the DFG are mutually consistent and that no information was lost in transcription.
* **Soundness.** `pm4py.check_soundness` is reported per model. Expect benign findings for the optionalbranch nets (the classic "not all places are in a valid source/sink configuration" and "there is a deadlock place" diagnostics ofPOWL nets with silent skips). These do **not** mean the model is wrong; they are artefacts of optional branches and of the fact that a skip leaves the case in a terminal state. Report them, do not hide them, and do not treat a silentbranch warning as a defect.
* **Metric availability.** `fitness_alignments`, `precision_alignments`, `generalization_tbr` and `simplicity_petri_net` are attempted and their raw output printed; PM4Py versions differ in which of them accept a plain Petri net versus a process tree, and some treat the silent branches as cost1 invisible moves, which would *penalise* exactly the traces that legitimately skip an activity. That is why the harness also computes **exact settheoretic metrics** from a bounded enumeration of the net's own firing sequences. For these three models the enumeration is **exact rather than sampled**, because (i) no model contains a loop and (ii) every activity occurs at most once, so the visible length is bounded by 8 and the depth8 DFS explores the complete language. The reported `|L|` values (7 / 14 / 12tight / 18) must equal the analytic values of §6.3; if they do not, the code and the prose disagree and the prose must be fixed.
* **Playout.** `pm4py.play_out` is used when the installed version provides it; otherwise the harness samples uniformly from the enumerated language. Neither is used as evidence of language inclusion. In general, a sampled playout **cannot** prove inclusion: absence of a trace in a sample proves nothing. Here inclusion statements are justified only by (a) exhaustive bounded enumeration for the loopfree models, and (b) explicit edge lists for the partial orders — i.e. by construction and by handproven case analysis, not by statistics.
* **The suite** encodes the §6.2 expectations. It is a regression test, not a proof; it is meaningful because its expectations are derived from the rule set (D1–D6) and the observed variants independently of the code.

# 9. Recommendation and Data-Collection Plan

## 9.1 Recommendation

* **Immediate use: `model_balanced`.** It admits every one of the 7 observed variant shapes (so it does not challenge a single observed case), it encodes exactly the rules we can cite (D1, D2, D3, D4, D6, S6), it keeps mandatoryversusoptional semantics explicit, it leaves the genuinely unresolved question (which check releases S) visible *as a modelled alternative* rather than hidden, and it is the only candidate that is simultaneously usable for complianceoriented conformance checking (it rejects SafterA, XafterA, approval before both checks, P without A) and for capacity analysis (C/E are not ordered, so overlapping execution is representable). Its overgenerosity is bounded and enumerated: 7 extra shape families, each attributeable to D5/D6/S6. Operationally, deploy it **together with `model_balanced_tight`** as a twosided band: a case admitted only by the tight twin is a "D5sensitive" case and must be routed to review rather than autoapproved. Do **not** deploy `model_conservative` as a conformance model: its 100 % fitness/precision are selffulfilling and would manufacture false assurance; keep it as the *evidence baseline* in audit reports ("nothing outside the observed support is claimed").
* **Sensitivity analysis: `model_permissive`.** It is the null model: any behaviour accepted by the permissive model but *not* by the balanced model is behaviour whose legitimacy rests on D5's permissive reading or on unresolved S3/S4/S5. Running KPIs, cycletime distributions and exception rates over the permissive language yields the **worstcase bounds** of every metric, and the gap between the balanced and permissive numbers quantifies the economic value of resolving D5 and the Scause ambiguity. The conservative model, by contrast, is the *lower* bound but a degenerate one: it cannot be used for that purpose because its bounds inherit the sample.

## 9.2 Datacollection plan

**Attributes to add (priority order).**
1. **`order_external_flag` / `component_stocked` at case *and* at SupplierConfirmationevent level** — the single attribute that converts an unconditioned skip into a conditioned rule. Resolves S3 vs S4 vs S5, i.e. whether the 7 Sless traces are compliant skips or data loss. *This alone resolves the A2/A3 abstractions.*
2. **Activity lifecycle timestamps: `START` and `END` (per activity, with timezone and clock source), plus a stable `event_id` and a `case_id`/`activity_instance_id`.** START timestamps convert D7 from "unknown" into a measurable quantity (overlap frequency, nesting) and directly test C/E concurrency vs sequential routing; END plus instance ids test the singleoccurrence assumption (A1) and repeatX (D6).
3. **Ingestion metadata: `source_system`, `ingestion_timestamp`, `ingestion_index`, `raw_payload_hash`.** Directly quantifies S7 tie contamination (how many "orderings" are ingestion artefacts) and S5 loss (gaps between source events and logged events), and allows a reconstruction pass to recover the ~5–10 % missing S events.
4. **Routing/expedience attributes: `order_priority`, `customer_risk_tier`, `order_value`, `due_date`, `backorder_flag`, `expedite_reason`, `supplier_id`, `is_standard_component`.** These are the candidate discriminators for (i) *why* S is skipped (stocked vs external), (ii) *why* X occurs (only 2 cases), (iii) *why* the C/E order splits 63/37 — is it duration asymmetry (concurrency) or a routing priority?
5. **Caselevel outcome fields: `abandoned_flag`, `rework_flag`, `released_on_time`, `return_flag`.** The current log contains only completed cases (A6), so all three models are models of a survivor population; without these, no model can say anything about the funnel.
6. **A complete activity catalogue** (including any postapproval posting/notification steps) to test assumption A5.

**Future traces that are maximally informative** (i.e. each one discriminates between competing hypotheses):

| Target hypothesis | Trace / stratum to obtain | Decision it enables |
|---|---|---|
| HCONC: CE (concurrency) vs HROUTE: stochastic sequential routing |  50 cases with both START and END for C and E; deliberately sample cases with *different* order priorities | HCONC is confirmed if starts overlap in a substantial fraction; HROUTE if order is a deterministic function of an attribute; S2's "whichever finishes first" is supported if overlap is high **and** the direction split is explained by duration asymmetry |
| HRELC vs HRELE: which check releases S | 10–20 cases in which the credit check is deliberately slow and the engineering review fast, with S START timestamps | If S starts while C is still open, the Erelease reading is real and `model_balanced`'s union is justified; if S always waits for C, switch to `model_balanced_tight` |

**Sequencing.** (i) Immediately: export `external_flag`, START/END timestamps, `event_id` and ingestion metadata (dataengineering only, no new process work). (ii) Next: run the Cslow/Efast probe on  10 cases to settle the D5 reading. (iii) Then: reconcile source vs log for S events to estimate the true loss rate and rederive the Soptionality argument quantitatively. (iv) Only then remine and rechoose between `model_balanced` and `model_balanced_tight`; reestimate precision with the enlarged, unbiased sample. The conservative enumeration should be **discarded** as a specification once step (i) exists — retained only as an appendix of what was observed.

**Plausible alternative interpretations that would materially change the recommendation.**

1. **Label/semantic drift (the strongest challenger).** If the `Engineering Review` events in the Efirst traces (V2, V4, V7 = 11/30) denote a *different* business step — e.g. a preliminary technical screen that happens to reuse the label — then the case mix is not a uniform sample of one process. The true model would then be a **strict sequence** `R  C  (technical screen)  …` in which the two "variants" are *different processes*, and the balanced model's symmetric treatment of C/E (and the permissive model's) would be a **category error** rather than a plausible hypothesis. Under this interpretation the recommendation flips: the conservative enumeration would become the defensible artifact (it does not assert that V1 and V2 are instances of one mechanism), and the first action would become data cleansing (distinct activity codes), not data collection. *Detection:* compare attributes, durations, owners and resources of the `Engineering Review` events across the two orders; a bimodal distribution in any of them is a smoking gun.
2. **The missingness bound is wrong (SWEET: the 5–10 % figure is stale).** If the true Sevent loss rate is  23 % (i.e. S is mandatory for *every* order and the Sless traces are all losses), then Soptionality is an artifact, the 7 Sless traces are corrupted, and **all three models are wrong**: the conservative enumeration launders 7 corrupted traces into "legitimate variants"; the balanced and permissive models institutionalise an illegal skip. The recommendation would then change to "escalate as a dataquality incident; do not publish any of the three models as a process specification", with the permissive model retained only for impact analysis. *Detection:* sourcesystem reconciliation of S events (cheap,  1 day of work) plus the `external_flag` export.
3. **Concurrent-free case population.** If the checks are in fact always executed by the same person/resource and never overlap, the observed variability is a routing artefact, and any capacity or leadtime analysis built on the balanced/permissive concurrency will overestimate throughput. The *trace language* is unaffected, so the recommendation changes only if the models are used for temporal/resource purposes — which is precisely the use case I assumed for `model_balanced` in §9.1; the START/END export makes this decidable.
4. **Selection bias dominates.** If aborted, returned or reworked cases exist in comparable numbers, then all three candidates model the wrong population, and the correct next artifact is a *lifecycle* model that includes the abort branch — a question none of the three candidates answers.

**Explicit nonclaims.** I do not claim that the observed completion order proves causality or concurrency (D7/S7); I do not claim that the DFG's two directions evidence parallelism (§1); I do not claim that Sabsence is a rule rather than a logging artefact (§1, S5 bound); I do not claim that the 30 traces are representative of the order book (A6); and I do not claim that any alignment or playout statistic computed above establishes language inclusion — the inclusion statements rest on the edge lists and the exhaustive bounded enumeration, which is exact here only because none of the three models contains a loop.
