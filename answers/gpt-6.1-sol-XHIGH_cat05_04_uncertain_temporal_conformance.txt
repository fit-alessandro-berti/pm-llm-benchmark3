## 1. Normalization Assumptions

All intervals below are closed, conservative bounds on occurrence time. `Z` denotes UTC. Calculations retain milliseconds; a minute-floor upper endpoint is the specified `:59.999`. Rounding ties are included conservatively. Interval endpoints express feasibility, not probabilities or confidence intervals in the statistical sense.

If the displayed clock time has resolution interval [r_low, r_high] and `clock_offset = recorded_clock - true_clock` is in [a, b], the corrected interval is:

    [r_low - b, r_high - a].

Resolution uncertainty is applied before clock correction. Thus HUB contributes ±1.5 seconds overall; SENSOR contributes ±10 seconds; a minute-floor OPS-BER timestamp expands by −120 to +149.999 seconds; VENDOR-NY expands by −180 to +239.999 seconds; and each LEGACY-BER fold expands by −60 to +119.999 seconds. MOBILE-LON nearest-minute rounding and clock uncertainty together contribute ±330 seconds.

June Berlin times use CEST, UTC+02:00; New York uses EDT, UTC−04:00; London uses BST, UTC+01:00. On 2025-10-26, Berlin fold 0 uses UTC+02:00 and fold 1 uses UTC+01:00. Each legacy timestamp initially has two alternatives. Conversion is performed separately for each alternative; neither averaging the folds nor spanning the gap is valid.

For independently bounded occurrence intervals X=[x_low,x_high] and Y=[y_low,y_high], the duration interval is [y_low−x_high, y_high−x_low]. When constraints couple events, I project the joint feasible set instead. Alternative fold scenarios produce a union of duration intervals, preserving genuine gaps. Direct A→C durations are computed directly, rather than by adding independently maximized A→I and I→C durations.

Clock bounds are allowed to vary between events, even within one source. A shared, constant offset is not established by the metadata. This conservatively permits independent event offsets; verified common-offset behavior could narrow durations. Section 4 shows a material example.

The following assumptions affect particular cases:

- **T-404:** The signed 18–25 minute investigation duration is elapsed wall time between this investigation's start and completion. Start is inferred as completion minus [1080,1500] seconds, retaining completion uncertainty. If the record instead measures active work excluding pauses, this finite start interval is unjustified and start-dependent findings become INDETERMINATE.
- **T-405:** As the question indicates, I interpret the ingestion sequence as establishing occurrence order A≤I≤C. Jointly imposing that order leaves only alert in fold 0, investigation start in fold 1, and containment in fold 1. This is a semantic assumption about ingestion order, not a consequence of time-zone conversion. If arrival order does not establish occurrence order, retain the alternatives and obtain causal evidence before enforcing these conclusions.
- **T-406:** Primary analysis treats the displayed mobile time as the device clock reading at entry. If occurrence preceded entry by d∈[0,300] seconds, occurrence time equals corrected entry time minus d. This yields an asymmetric interval. The alternative interpretation is that the user accurately entered the historical occurrence time and only recorded it late; then entry delay affects recording/ingestion time, not occurrence time. Both are analyzed.
- **T-407:** The baseline treats the latency bound as conditional on successful delivery; it does not prove an application-level `Alert_Received`. Receipt-dependent results are therefore INDETERMINATE. A separate conditional calculation assumes receipt occurred within [0,1200] seconds of send and R≤I. If the supplied network evidence is intended to prove actual receipt, those conditional classifications can instead be adopted. `Alert_Sent` is never renamed to `Alert_Received` without this inference and provenance label.
- **T-409:** The ordinary OPS-BER diagnostic bound is not used for the unsupported hour. The supplied ±12 minute estimate produces a conditional interval, but I do not regard an explicitly weak estimate as an enforceable bound. Start-dependent baseline results are INDETERMINATE. Conditional calculations accept that bound and normal process order A≤I≤C. The order is also conditional here because occurrence order is not independently established by the weak start timestamp. Section 4 distinguishes accepting the estimate from assuming an enforced workflow order alone.

For the other cases, the bounded timestamps already establish the relevant forward order. Hard-rule acceptance is [0,L], including equality, for a following-event duration. Negative durations would require resolving occurrence/lifecycle order; they are not silently treated as passing an upper-only SLA or as valid eventually-following profile observations. Missing events imply neither compliance nor violation. No absence-completeness guarantee or observation cutoff is supplied.

Every material profile change follows from the same duration bounds:

- T-401 A→I becomes certain pass only at zeta=3; its upper bound exceeds the zeta=2 upper limit of 960 seconds.
- T-402 A→I is wholly above the zeta=1 band, partially overlaps at zeta=2, and still straddles the zeta=3 upper boundary of 1140 seconds. I→C changes PV→CP at zeta=2, and A→C changes CV→CP there.

- T-404 A→I changes to CP at zeta=3; I→C changes to CP at zeta=2. The narrow 2700-second boundary overlap is retained at zeta=1.
- T-405 A→I changes to CP at zeta=3 under the ordered-fold model. The other supplied, testable profile pairs already pass.
- T-406 I→C changes to CP at zeta=2. A→I remains possible at all three zeta values: at zeta=1 it extends below and above the band; at zeta=2 its lower bound is below 240 and its upper bound above 960; at zeta=3 its upper bound still exceeds 1140.

- Accepting T-409's conditional model changes I→C from PV at zeta=1 to CP at zeta=2 and 3. Its conditional A→I remains PV at all three settings because zero-duration starts remain feasible. Three additional semantic/correlation sensitivities matter:

1. **Mobile delay as recording-only:** T-406 A→I becomes [508.5,1171.5] seconds and I→C becomes [1948.5,2611.5] seconds. S2 and the zeta=1 I→C profile then become CP immediately; all their wider scenarios pass too. S1 retains PV/PV/CP, and A→I retains PV/PV/PV. A→C is unchanged. Delay should not be added a second time to the source offset or interpreted symmetrically.
2. **Receipt and causal semantics:** Without R≤I, but assuming successful receipt within the latency bound, T-407 R→I is [−183,1023] seconds and R→C is [2097,3303]. The negative portion does not satisfy eventually-following semantics; occurrence order needs evidence or a stated condition. Under R≤I the correct R→I interval is [0,1023], not [−3,1023] obtained by independently subtracting already-projected event intervals. R→C becomes [2277,3303] and passes all three profile bands. For T-409, without A≤I the weak-bound raw differences are A→I=[−481.5,1021.499] and I→C=[1078.501,2581.5]. With order they become [0,1021.499] and [1078.501,2103]. If an enforced workflow independently guarantees A≤I≤C, S2 is CP even without any trustworthy start clock: I→C≤A→C≤2103 seconds. Under order alone A→I and I→C can each range from 0 to 2103 seconds; their profile classifications remain PV, and S1 is PV for all three tolerances. That is different evidence from trusting the weak clock estimate.
3. **Legacy order and common offsets:** Before occurrence-order conditioning, T-405 raw I→C differences form [−1559.999,−1200.001] ∪ [2040.001,2399.999] ∪ [5640.001,5999.999] seconds. The negative branch is an order problem, and the two positive branches respectively pass and violate S2. Enforcing the full A≤I≤C chain removes all but the middle branch. S1 then passes at +2 minutes; A→I becomes CV at zeta=1 and CP at zeta=2. Stronger evidence can reveal a certain statistical deviation as well as establish a pass.

## 5. PM4Py Temporal-Conformance Code

The following block requires Python 3.9+, pandas, PM4Py, and an available IANA time-zone database. It embeds all observations and reads no external data files. Normalization uses pandas and `zoneinfo`; a difference-bound matrix preserves inference and causal dependencies, and separate feasible worlds retain DST alternatives. The main interval classifications do not use midpoints.

The separate PM4Py log is explicitly a **lossy, conditional midpoint approximation**, including provenance for the inferred start and hypothetical receipt. For these data, projected interval centers form a consistent selected world; this is checked for the relevant constraints. That property is not guaranteed for arbitrary constraint networks or multiple surviving folds.

The isolated diagnostic adapter assumes PM4Py's common event-log result shape: a list per trace, whose deviation tuples begin with source and target activity. It uses actual trace order and does not depend on duration/score column positions. Unrecognized shapes remain opaque; direct midpoint durations still provide the comparison, and raw diagnostics are printed for inspection. Case keys are given a non-date-like prefix to prevent identifier parsing from changing case identity. PM4Py analyzes the point log; the interval engine and evidential policy are implemented separately.

def merge_intervals(intervals):
    """Union, retaining genuine gaps rather than taking a convex hull."""
    result = []
    for lo, hi in sorted(intervals):
        if result and lo <= result[-1][1]:
            result[-1] = (result[-1][0], max(hi, result[-1][1]))
        else:
            result.append((lo, hi))
    return result

def normalize(row):
    zone, rlo, rhi, olo, ohi = META[row.source]
    if row.case_id == "T-409" and row.activity == I:
        olo, ohi = -720, 720  # weak estimate, not the missing NTP bound
    local = pd.Timestamp(row.recorded).to_pydatetime()
    alternatives = []
    for fold in (0, 1):
        # Apply displayed-resolution uncertainty in the local clock domain.
        low = local + timedelta(seconds=rlo)
        high = local + timedelta(seconds=rhi)
        low = pd.Timestamp(low.replace(tzinfo=ZoneInfo(zone), fold=fold)).tz_convert("UTC")
        high = pd.Timestamp(high.replace(tzinfo=ZoneInfo(zone), fold=fold)).tz_convert("UTC")
        low -= pd.Timedelta(seconds=ohi)
        high -= pd.Timedelta(seconds=olo)
        if row.source == "MOBILE-LON" and ENTRY_DELAY_AFFECTS_OCCURRENCE:
            low -= pd.Timedelta(minutes=5)  # subtract delay in [0, 300]
        alternatives.append((low, high))
    # Unambiguous fold=0 and fold=1 produce identical intervals.
    return merge_intervals(alternatives)

# Difference-bound matrix: d[x][y] bounds t_y - t_x from above.
# Integer nanoseconds avoid floating-point errors at acceptance boundaries.
def ns(seconds):
    return int(round(seconds * 1_000_000_000))

def close_world(bounds, constraints):
    names = [None] + sorted(bounds)  # None is the fixed zero-time origin
    index = {name: n for n, name in enumerate(names)}
    n = len(names)
    d = [[inf] * n for _ in range(n)]
    for q in range(n):
        d[q][q] = 0
    for activity, (lo, hi) in bounds.items():
        q = index[activity]
        d[0][q], d[q][0] = hi.value, -lo.value
    for x, y, lo, hi in constraints:
        ix, iy = index[x], index[y]
        if hi != inf:
            d[ix][iy] = min(d[ix][iy], ns(hi))
        if lo != -inf:
            d[iy][ix] = min(d[iy][ix], -ns(lo))
    for k in range(n):
        for i in range(n):
            if d[i][k] == inf:
                continue
            for j in range(n):
                if d[k][j] != inf:
                    d[i][j] = min(d[i][j], d[i][k] + d[k][j])
    if any(d[q][q] < 0 for q in range(n)):
        return None
    return index, d

worlds = {}
for case_id, group in observed.groupby("case_id", sort=True):
    activities = group.activity.tolist()
    worlds[case_id] = []
    choices = [raw_intervals[(case_id, activity)] for activity in activities]
    for selected in product(*choices):
        bounds = dict(zip(activities, selected))
        constraints = []
        if case_id == "T-404":

            bounds[I] = (lo - pd.Timedelta(seconds=1500),
                         hi - pd.Timedelta(seconds=1080))
            constraints.append((I, J, 1080, 1500))

            bounds[A] = (lo, hi + pd.Timedelta(seconds=1200))
            constraints.append((S, A, 0, 1200))
        if ASSUME_PROCESS_ORDER:
            for x, y in ((A, I), (I, C), (C, K)):
                if x in bounds and y in bounds:
                    constraints.append((x, y, 0, inf))
        closed = close_world(bounds, constraints)
        if closed is not None:
            worlds[case_id].append(closed)

# Exact projections for each convex constraint world, then union across folds.
def event_intervals(case_id, activity):
    result = []
    for index, d in worlds[case_id]:
        if activity in index:
            q = index[activity]
            result.append((pd.Timestamp(-d[q][0], unit="ns", tz="UTC"),
                           pd.Timestamp(d[0][q], unit="ns", tz="UTC")))
    return merge_intervals(result)

def durations(case_id, pair):
    x, y = pair
    result = []
    for index, d in worlds[case_id]:
        if x in index and y in index:
            ix, iy = index[x], index[y]
            result.append((-d[iy][ix] / 1e9, d[ix][iy] / 1e9))
    return merge_intervals(result)

def classify(intervals, region):
    if not intervals:
        return IND
    a, b = region
    if any(lo > hi for lo, hi in intervals):
        return IND
    if all(a <= lo and hi <= b for lo, hi in intervals):
        return CP
    if all(hi < a or lo > b for lo, hi in intervals):
        return CV  # permits one branch below and another above the band
    return PV

def operational_class(case_id, pair, conditional):
    if case_id == "T-407" and A in pair and not TRUST_T407_RECEIPT:
        return IND
    if case_id == "T-409" and I in pair and not TRUST_T409_CLOCK_BOUND:
        return IND
    return conditional

def diagnostic_pairs(diagnostics):

            comparison.append({"case_id": case_id, "pair": pair, "zeta": zeta,

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency

For a PV result, additional evidence must narrow the **joint feasible duration set** wholly into the acceptance region or wholly outside it. Merely reducing its width may leave a boundary straddle. For an IND result, first establish the missing occurrence, valid bound, or semantic premise; then check whether the remaining interval is still mixed. No finite extra evidence can be promised to resolve every boundary case in advance.

Stronger synchronization evidence resolves clock uncertainty; missing-activity evidence resolves occurrence/existence; duration evidence supports lifecycle inference; semantic evidence resolves entry delay, successful receipt, and whether ingestion order denotes occurrence order. These are distinct gaps and require different remedies.

## 7. Governance Recommendation

Store canonical occurrence timestamps in UTC at sufficient precision, alongside the unchanged raw timestamp, original IANA zone, observed UTC offset, DST fold, source identity, timestamp-resolution/rounding semantics, and conversion-rule version. Keep occurrence, clock capture, user entry, and ingestion times separately. Never overwrite an ambiguous timestamp with one guessed fold or an interval midpoint.

Collect source offset telemetry with calibration validity periods, drift bounds, synchronization status, and an explicit distinction between verified bounds and engineering estimates. Associate each event with the relevant calibration record. Record common-clock epochs where offset cancellation is justified; otherwise retain event-level uncertainty. External and mobile sources should carry provenance and evidence-strength labels rather than inherit central-system trust.

Use explicit lifecycle activity names and start/complete markers. Record missing starts when possible; tie signed duration evidence to the correct investigation instance and specify elapsed time versus active effort. Preserve device completion semantics. Use case, message, and causal identifiers to link send, receipt acknowledgment, investigation, containment, and closure, including retransmissions and clock resets. Record whether an ordering guarantee concerns occurrence or ingestion.

Adopt an explicit interval enforcement policy: all feasible durations within the legal maximum establish compliance; all feasible durations beyond it establish a breach; overlap triggers review and targeted evidence collection, and is insufficient by itself for a certain-breach sanction. INDETERMINATE triggers evidence remediation. Retain the exact interval/union and assumptions used in every decision. Apply operational tolerances only when authorized and documented; a larger statistical zeta does not relax the legal SLA.

Manage the temporal profile separately as a statistical monitoring tool. Document its training population, lifecycle semantics, zeta, and treatment of censored/missing events. Calibrate review thresholds on trustworthy timing data and label midpoint-only PM4Py diagnostics as approximations. Establish log-completeness and observation-cutoff controls so that an absent close can be distinguished from a proven open incident. Require review of weak bounds and semantic assumptions before turning conditional findings into enforceable decisions.
