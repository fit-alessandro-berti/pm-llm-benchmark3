# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The source POWL is authoritative. It is a `StrictPartialOrder` over seven top-level nodes, one of which (`optional_legal`) is an XOR operator hiding an optional silent branch, and one of which (`verification_loop`) is a binary `LOOP` operator whose "do" body is itself a strict partial order `configure_trial → inspect_trial` and whose "redo" child is `correct_defect`. The partial-order edges declare *causal precedence only*; the *absence* of an edge is a first-class semantic statement of concurrency/unorderedness, not an omission.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 (start) | `Register Change` occurs first; nothing precedes it | `Register, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release Change, Archive Change` | `Assess Risk, Register, ...` (Register not first) | Yes — Register is a visible label |
| I2 (fork order) | Both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` are causally after `Register` | any trace with Register before both | a trace where `Prepare Evidence` precedes `Register` | Yes |
| I3 (concurrency / absence of order) | `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` are *unordered*; both orderings are legal executions | `...Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence...` **and** `...Prepare Evidence, Assess Risk...` both accepted | a model that forces only one of the two orders (the other rejected) | Yes — both interleavings observable |
| I4 (optional legal, XOR) | `Legal Review` is optional: exactly one of {execute Legal Review, skip via tau} | trace containing `Legal Review`; trace omitting it | a trace containing `Legal Review` **twice**, or one where legal is mandatory | Partially — presence observable; the *skip* is a silent tau and observable only as *absence* of the label |
| I5 (legal placement) | `Legal Review`, when taken, is after `Assess Risk` and before `Release Change` | `Assess Risk ... Legal Review ... Release Change` | `Legal Review` before `Assess Risk`, or after `Release Change` | Yes when label present |
| I6 (legal ∥ verification) | There is **no** ordering edge between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`; they may overlap/interleave at the model level | trace interleaving legal with configure/inspect/correct | a model that forces all legal activity strictly before or strictly after the whole loop | Yes — interleavings observable when label present |
| I7 (sync before verification) | Verification cannot start until **both** `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` complete (AND-join into the loop) | `Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial...` | `Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial` with `Assess Risk` never done before Configure | Yes — Configure Trial is the first visible loop event; its enabledness gates on both |
| I8 (intra-iteration order) | Each iteration runs `Configure Trial` before `Inspect Trial` | `Configure Trial, Inspect Trial` | `Inspect Trial, Configure Trial` in the same iteration | Yes |
| I9 (loop separator) | A *repeated* iteration is separated by `Correct Defect` (redo child) | `Configure, Inspect, Correct Defect, Configure, Inspect` | two consecutive `Configure, Inspect` with no `Correct Defect` between | Yes |
| I10 (min iterations) | At least **one** iteration executes (loop "do" runs before any exit) | shortest accepted trace contains one `Configure, Inspect` | a trace reaching `Release Change` with zero `Configure Trial` | Yes |
| I11 (join before release) | `Release Change` waits for completion of **both** the optional-legal branch and the verification loop (AND-join) | `...(legal resolved)...(loop exited)... Release Change` | `Release Change` while loop still owes an iteration, or before legal branch resolved | Yes |
| I12 (finality) | `Archive Change` is the last activity, strictly after `Release Change`, and marks completion | `... Release Change, Archive Change` [end] | `Archive Change` before `Release Change`, or activity after `Archive Change` | Yes |

Key non-local invariants for the audit: **I3/I6 (true unorderedness), I7/I11 (AND-synchronization), I9/I10 (binary-loop shape), I4 (silent optionality).** These are exactly the properties most vulnerable to conversion loss.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 (primary): POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN** | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` → `(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | POWL has a defined, semantics-preserving translation to an accepting Petri net; the net carries true concurrency (AND-splits/joins) and the loop/XOR structurally; BPMN generated from a marked net keeps token semantics | BPMN layout may *visually* suggest sequencing that the net does not impose; silent transitions become unlabeled routing nodes; label-based comparison can be muddied by duplicated silent routers | `convert_to_petri_net` raising, or a null/None marking, or resulting net not a workflow net |
| **P2: POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN** | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` (in `try/except`); if it succeeds `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | If it *were* block-structured, a process tree is the cleanest canonical, block-structured artifact and yields unambiguous BPMN | The source is **not** guaranteed block-structured: the partial order has an AND-fork (`Register` → {Assess, Prepare}) that re-synchronizes into the loop, plus an unordered legal branch that re-joins at release. Net→tree conversion (region/inductive-miner-style folding) frequently **cannot** represent such non-block-structured concurrency without approximation and may raise | `convert_to_process_tree` raising an exception — **this is an acceptable, informative outcome**, evidencing that the behavior is not block-structured |
| **P3 (control/reference): direct POWL → BPMN** | *None defined for a faithful direct POWL→BPMN* | — | — | **Not attempted.** No direct POWL→BPMN API is assumed; a "direct" call would be undefined and must not be invented |

**Chosen primary: P1.** P2 is run as a diagnostic probe of block-structuredness and is *allowed to fail*. P3 is explicitly excluded because the task forbids inventing a direct POWL→BPMN converter.

# 3. Conversion Code

```python
import traceback
import pm4py

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Assumes `source_powl` is already built exactly as in the task file.
# ------------------------------------------------------------------

def _valid_marking(m):
    """A marking must be non-None and non-empty to be trusted."""
    return m is not None and len(m) > 0

artifacts = {}          # name -> (net, im, fm)
bpmn_models = {}        # name -> bpmn

# --- Step 1: POWL -> accepting Petri net ---------------------------
net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)

if not _valid_marking(im_from_powl):
    raise RuntimeError("POWL->Petri produced a null/empty initial marking; reject.")
if not _valid_marking(fm_from_powl):
    raise RuntimeError("POWL->Petri produced a null/empty final marking; reject.")

artifacts["from_powl"] = (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
print("[P1] POWL -> Petri net OK | places=%d transitions=%d arcs=%d"
      % (len(net_from_powl.places), len(net_from_powl.transitions), len(net_from_powl.arcs)))

# --- Step 2: Petri net -> BPMN (primary pipeline) ------------------
bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
bpmn_models["bpmn_from_net"] = bpmn_from_net
print("[P1] Petri -> BPMN OK")

# --- Step 3: try the process-tree intermediate (P2), allow failure -
tree_from_net = None
try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    print("[P2] Petri -> process tree SUCCEEDED:", tree_from_net)
except Exception as exc:                      # do NOT suppress; interpret it
    print("[P2] Petri -> process tree FAILED (expected if not block-structured):")
    print("     %s: %s" % (type(exc).__name__, exc))
    traceback.print_exc()

# --- Step 4: if the tree exists, build a BPMN from it --------------
bpmn_from_tree = None
if tree_from_net is not None:
    try:
        bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
        bpmn_models["bpmn_from_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
        print("[P2] process tree -> BPMN OK")
    except Exception as exc:
        print("[P2] process tree -> BPMN FAILED:", type(exc).__name__, exc)

# --- Step 5: convert each produced BPMN back to an accepting net ---
for name, bpmn in bpmn_models.items():
    try:
        rnet, rim, rfm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)
        if not (_valid_marking(rim) and _valid_marking(rfm)):
            print("[RT] %s -> Petri: NULL marking, quarantining" % name)
            continue
        artifacts["roundtrip_%s" % name] = (rnet, rim, rfm)
        print("[RT] %s -> Petri OK" % name)
    except Exception as exc:
        print("[RT] %s -> Petri FAILED: %s %s" % (name, type(exc).__name__, exc))

# --- Step 6: workflow-net + soundness diagnostics on every net ----
diagnostics = {}
for name, (n, i, f) in artifacts.items():
    is_wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(n)
    try:
        sound = pm4py.check_soundness(n, i, f, print_diagnostics=True)
        # pm4py may return a bool or a (bool, diagnostics) tuple depending on version
        is_sound = sound[0] if isinstance(sound, tuple) else sound
    except Exception as exc:
        is_sound = None
        print("[DIAG] soundness check raised for %s: %s" % (name, exc))
    diagnostics[name] = {"workflow_net": is_wf, "sound": is_sound}
    print("[DIAG] %-22s workflow_net=%s sound=%s" % (name, is_wf, is_sound))

# --- Step 7: save visualizations with clear filenames -------------
pm4py.save_vis_powl(source_powl, "vis_00_source.powl.png")
pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl,
                         "vis_01_powl_to_petri.png")
pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(bpmn_from_net, "vis_02_petri_to_bpmn.png")
if tree_from_net is not None:
    pm4py.save_vis_process_tree(tree_from_net, "vis_03_petri_to_tree.png")
if bpmn_from_tree is not None:
    pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(bpmn_from_tree, "vis_04_tree_to_bpmn.png")
for name, (n, i, f) in artifacts.items():
    if name.startswith("roundtrip_"):
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(n, i, f, "vis_05_%s.png" % name)

print("Artifacts produced:", list(artifacts.keys()))
print("BPMN models produced:", list(bpmn_models.keys()))
```

Notes:
- Markings are checked for non-null/non-empty before any net is trusted (`_valid_marking`).
- `convert_to_process_tree` is wrapped in `try/except`; the exception is printed and interpreted, never swallowed.
- No undefined converter (e.g., "direct POWL→BPMN") is called.
- `check_soundness` return shape is handled defensively across PM4Py versions.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

Assessment terms restricted to: `expected preserved`, `validated preserved`, `approximated`, `ambiguous`, `lost`, `conversion failed`, `not tested`.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 start (Register first) | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Register is the unique enabled visible transition from `im`; witnessed by enabled-transition set at `im`. |
| I2 fork after Register | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | expected preserved | AND-split place structure from POWL edges; visible in net topology. |
| I3 true concurrency (Assess ∥ Prepare) | validated preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | ambiguous | Net keeps a genuine AND-split so both interleavings are reachable (footprints show `||`); **BPMN layout** cannot itself prove concurrency — needs the parallel gateway to be present, else it is only *approximated visually*. |
| I4 silent optional legal (XOR + tau) | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | ambiguous | Tau/skip becomes an unlabeled routing node; after hiding `None`-labeled transitions, "skip" is observable only as *absence* of `Legal Review`. Duplicate silent routers can appear on round trip. |
| I5 legal placement | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | expected preserved | Ordering places around the XOR carry the constraint; witnessed when label present. |
| I6 legal ∥ verification (no edge) | validated preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | ambiguous | The **absence** of an ordering edge is the hardest property: the net must *not* introduce a place forcing legal before/after the loop. Footprint `||` between legal and loop activities is the evidence; BPMN gateway layout can falsely suggest sequencing. |
| I7 AND-sync before verification | validated preserved | approximated | conversion failed | approximated | Loop entry place requires tokens from both Assess and Prepare branches; verified by enabledness of Configure Trial only after both complete. BPMN join-gateway type must be AND, not XOR. |
| I8 intra-iteration order (Configure→Inspect) | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | expected preserved | Sequence place inside loop body. |
| I9 loop separator (Correct Defect on redo) | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | approximated | Binary LOOP → net has a redo path through Correct Defect; BPMN renders as a back-edge that can be misread. |
| I10 min one iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Shortest visible trace must contain one Configure/Inspect; checkable by bounded exploration. |
| I11 AND-join before Release | validated preserved | approximated | conversion failed | approximated | Release consumes tokens from both legal branch and loop-exit; join-gateway type is the risk in BPMN. |
| I12 finality (Archive last) | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Unique final marking with Archive as terminal visible event. |
| Activity-label uniqueness | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | ambiguous | All source labels are distinct; **silent** transitions/routers are label-`None` and can multiply on round trips, but no *visible* label is duplicated. |
| Initial/final markings | validated preserved | not tested (BPMN has no marking) | conversion failed | validated preserved | Checked non-null on POWL→Petri and on every round-tripped net; BPMN itself carries no marking, so markings are re-derived on BPMN→Petri. |
| Gateway/routing-node clarity | not tested | ambiguous | conversion failed | ambiguous | A layout-level, not behavioral, property; see §6. |

Discussion of the required points:
- **True concurrency vs arbitrary interleaving:** the accepting Petri net models I3/I6 as genuine token concurrency (marking with multiple tokens), which is stronger than an interleaving abstraction. Footprints and reachability confirm both orders are possible; but any *trace-language* comparison necessarily flattens concurrency to interleaving and cannot, by itself, distinguish "true parallel" from "either order." That distinction is `ambiguous` at the trace level and must be argued from net structure.
- **Missing edge between legal and verification (I6):** preserved only if the converter adds no spurious ordering place; verified via `||` in the footprint relation between `Legal Review` and loop activities.
- **Silent optional behavior (I4):** the XOR-with-tau collapses into a routing structure; after hiding `None` labels, only the *presence/absence* of `Legal Review` is observable, so "skip" is validated only indirectly.
- **Binary-loop semantics (I9/I10):** do-part mandatory once, redo through `Correct Defect`; preserved in the net, approximated in BPMN back-edge rendering.
- **Join before Release (I11):** correctness hinges on the join being an AND-join; an XOR-join would wrongly admit early release — a rejection trigger.
- **Activity-label uniqueness:** all visible labels distinct; only silent nodes proliferate.
- **Markings:** never assumed non-null; explicitly checked.
- **Gateway ambiguity:** a rendering concern only (§6).

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

```python
import pm4py

def soundness(name, n, i, f):
    try:
        r = pm4py.check_soundness(n, i, f, print_diagnostics=True)
        return r[0] if isinstance(r, tuple) else r
    except Exception as exc:
        print("[SND] %s raised %s" % (name, exc))
        return None

def footprint_compare(a, b):
    """Compare directly-follows / parallel / causal footprint relations."""
    try:
        fa = pm4py.discover_footprints(*a)
        fb = pm4py.discover_footprints(*b)
    except Exception as exc:
        return None, "footprint discovery failed: %s" % exc
    keys = ("dfg", "sequence", "parallel", "start_activities", "end_activities")
    diffs = {}
    for k in keys:
        va, vb = fa.get(k), fb.get(k)
        if va != vb:
            diffs[k] = (va, vb)
    return (len(diffs) == 0), diffs

# ---- bounded, tau-hiding visible-trace exploration ----------------
def bounded_visible_traces(net, im, fm, max_visible=20, max_loops=3):
    """
    DFS over markings. Transitions with label None are hidden (fired but
    not emitted). Requires EXACT final-marking equality to accept a trace.
    Guards against infinite silent cycling and bounds loop iterations.
    Returns (set_of_visible_traces, exhaustive_flag).
    """
    results = set()
    exhaustive = True
    # visited guards on (marking, iteration-signature) to stop silent cycles
    def marking_key(m):
        return frozenset((str(p), c) for p, c in m.items())

    stack = [(im, tuple(), 0, frozenset())]  # marking, visible seq, loop count, silent-visited
    while stack:
        m, seq, loops, silent_seen = stack.pop()
        if m == fm and len(seq) >= 0:
            results.add(seq)
            # continue: fm may not be a dead marking, but record acceptance
        try:
            enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m)
        except Exception as exc:
            print("[EXP] get_enabled_transitions failed:", exc)
            exhaustive = False
            continue
        if not enabled:
            continue
        for t in enabled:
            # fire t -> new marking
            new_m = dict(m)
            for a in t.in_arcs:
                new_m[a.source] = new_m.get(a.source, 0) - 1
            for a in t.out_arcs:
                new_m[a.target] = new_m.get(a.target, 0) + 1
            new_m = {p: c for p, c in new_m.items() if c > 0}

            label = t.label
            if label is None:  # silent: hide from trace, guard against cycling
                sig = marking_key(new_m)
                if sig in silent_seen:
                    continue  # silent cycle -> prune
                stack.append((new_m, seq, loops, silent_seen | {sig}))
            else:
                if len(seq) + 1 > max_visible:
                    exhaustive = False
                    continue
                nloops = loops + (1 if label == "Correct Defect" else 0)
                if nloops > max_loops:
                    exhaustive = False
                    continue
                stack.append((new_m, seq + (label,), nloops,
                              frozenset()))  # reset silent guard after a visible step
    return results, exhaustive

# ---- run comparisons ---------------------------------------------
base = (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
print("[SND] base sound:", soundness("base", *base))

base_traces, base_exhaustive = bounded_visible_traces(*base)
print("[EXP] base visible traces=%d exhaustive=%s" % (len(base_traces), base_exhaustive))

for name, art in artifacts.items():
    if not name.startswith("roundtrip_"):
        continue
    print("---- comparing base vs", name, "----")
    print("[SND]", name, "sound:", soundness(name, *art))
    eq, diffs = footprint_compare(base, art)
    print("[FP] footprints equal:", eq, "diffs:", diffs)
    rt_traces, rt_exhaustive = bounded_visible_traces(*art)
    print("[EXP] %s traces=%d exhaustive=%s" % (name, len(rt_traces), rt_exhaustive))
    print("[EXP] trace-set equal:", base_traces == rt_traces)
    only_base = base_traces - rt_traces
    only_rt = rt_traces - base_traces
    if only_base:
        print("[EXP] LOST from round trip (sample):", list(only_base)[:3])
    if only_rt:
        print("[EXP] EXTRA in round trip (sample):", list(only_rt)[:3])

# ---- positive / negative witness traces --------------------------
positive_witnesses = [
    ("Register", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register", "Prepare Evidence", "Assess Risk", "Legal Review",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
]
negative_witnesses = [
    ("Register", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),                 # zero iterations
    ("Register", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Inspect Trial",
     "Configure Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),         # wrong intra-order
    ("Assess Risk", "Register", "..."),                              # Register not first
]

def replay_ok(trace, net, im, fm):
    """Best-effort token replay of a visible trace over an accepting net."""
    from pm4py.algo.conformance.tokenreplay import algorithm as tr
    from pm4py.objects.log.obj import EventLog, Trace, Event
    log = EventLog()
    t = Trace()
    for a in trace:
        e = Event(); e["concept:name"] = a; t.append(e)
    log.append(t)
    try:
        rep = tr.apply(log, net, im, fm)
        return rep[0]["trace_is_fit"]
    except Exception as exc:
        return "replay-error: %s" % exc

for w in positive_witnesses:
    print("[POS] fit=%s :: %s" % (replay_ok(w, *base), w))
for w in negative_witnesses:
    if "..." in w:
        continue  # illustrative only
    print("[NEG] fit(should be False)=%s :: %s" % (replay_ok(w, *base), w))
```

**Exhaustiveness statement:** the comparison is exhaustive **only within the chosen bound** (`max_visible=20`, `max_loops=3` iterations of the verification loop, silent-cycle pruning). Because the source contains an unbounded loop (I10 guarantees ≥1 iteration but no upper bound), the visible-trace language is infinite; no finite exploration is truly exhaustive. Within the bound, if `base_traces == rt_traces` **and** footprints are equal **and** both nets are sound, the round trip is *bounded-equivalent*. Footprint equality additionally guards the concurrency relation (`parallel`) that pure interleaving traces would blur.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN diagram can be behaviorally acceptable yet visually misleading:

- **Parallel gateways (AND):** the AND-split after `Register` and the AND-join before `Release Change` encode I2/I3/I7/I11. If the renderer draws the two branches stacked with one visually "above," readers infer priority or ordering that does not exist. Mitigation: annotate the split/join explicitly as "AND — both branches concurrent, no ordering implied."
- **Exclusive gateways (XOR):** the optional-legal XOR with a tau branch renders as a diamond with one path leading to `Legal Review` and one empty/silent path. Readers may miss that the empty path is a legitimate *skip*. Mitigation: label the silent branch "skip legal (τ)".
- **Loop visualization:** the binary LOOP becomes a back-edge from the exit test through `Correct Defect` to `Configure Trial`. A back-arrow can be misread as "optional retry" rather than "mandatory once, repeatable, separated by Correct Defect (I9/I10)." Mitigation: annotate "loop body executes ≥1×; repeat only via Correct Defect."
- **Silent routing nodes:** taus become unlabeled tasks/gateways. Stakeholders may treat an unlabeled node as an undocumented activity. Mitigation: style silent nodes distinctly (e.g., filled black) and add "routing only — no work performed."
- **Apparent ordering of graph layout:** left-to-right / top-to-bottom placement implies temporal order even between unordered activities (I6: legal ∥ verification). The layout is *not* a semantic statement. Mitigation: add a legend "horizontal position ≠ execution order; only gateways and sequence flows constrain ordering," and consider a companion "concurrency map" listing unordered pairs.

Recommended annotations to ship with any BPMN artifact: (1) gateway-type legend, (2) explicit list of concurrent/unordered activity pairs, (3) loop bounds and separator, (4) tau legend, (5) a disclaimer that the BPMN is a *derivative view*, with the authoritative model referenced.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a conversion result if **any** of the following holds:

1. **Unsoundness** — `check_soundness` returns False (or the net is not a workflow net) for `net_from_powl` or any round-tripped net (deadlocks, dead transitions, improper completion).
2. **Null/empty markings** — a conversion returns `im` or `fm` that is None/empty; markings are never assumed valid.
3. **Release before verification / broken AND-join** — the join before `Release Change` behaves as XOR, so a bounded/replay test admits `Release Change` with zero completed iterations or before both Assess+Prepare (violates I7/I10/I11).
4. **Loss of a required interleaving** — bounded exploration or footprint comparison shows one of the mandatory orders of Assess/Prepare (I3) or of legal ∥ verification (I6) is no longer reachable, i.e., `parallel` footprint downgraded to `sequence`.
5. **Collapse of optional behavior** — `Legal Review` becomes mandatory or is dropped entirely (violates I4), detected when no accepted bounded trace either contains or omits it.
6. **Failed exact-final-marking test** — a "completed" trace reaches a marking ≠ `fm`, or `fm` is reachable with residual tokens (improper completion).
7. **Footprint divergence** — `discover_footprints` differs between base and round-trip on `parallel`, `sequence`, `start_activities`, or `end_activities`.
8. **Spurious ordering edge (I6)** — the converter introduces a place forcing legal strictly before/after the loop.
9. **Visible-label duplication or corruption** — a distinct source label appears duplicated or renamed among visible transitions (silent-node multiplication alone is not disqualifying but must be documented).

Any single trigger → quarantine the artifact; do not treat successful serialization/visualization as validation.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

- **Authoritative / canonical for storage:** the **source POWL model** (`source_powl`). It is the human-authored intent, and it natively expresses partial order (true concurrency), the XOR-with-tau optionality, and the binary loop *without* forcing a block structure or a marking-level encoding. Store it as the single source of truth (e.g., serialized POWL / the generating code).
- **Secondary canonical (machine-checkable):** the **accepting Petri net** `(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)` produced by `convert_to_petri_net`, retained as the *validated execution semantics* and the reference for all soundness/footprint/round-trip checks.
- **Derivative artifacts (never authoritative):** `bpmn_from_net`, `bpmn_from_tree` (if any), the process tree (if produced), all round-tripped nets, and every rendered PNG. Each is a *view* generated from the canonical POWL/net.
- **Validation evidence required with each derivative:** soundness result, workflow-net check, footprint equality vs the reference net, bounded-visible-trace equality (with the bound stated), positive/negative witness replay results, and the annotation set from §6 for any BPMN. A derivative without this evidence is quarantined.
- **Process-tree intermediate:** **allowed only as a diagnostic probe, not as a canonical or a required pipeline.** Given the AND-fork/re-synchronization and the unordered legal branch, `convert_to_process_tree` is expected to fail or approximate; if it succeeds, treat the tree as a lossy/approximate view and validate the tree-derived BPMN against the reference net before any use. Never let a process tree become the stored source, because block-structuring can silently serialize away I3/I6.
- **Avoiding round-trip drift on future edits:** always edit the **POWL source**, then regenerate the Petri net and derivatives from scratch; never edit a BPMN/tree and reverse-convert it back into the canonical. Every regeneration must re-run the §5 validation and the §7 rejection gate before publishing. Version the POWL and pin the PM4Py version so conversions are reproducible.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every test in §5 passes, the following cannot be established:

1. **Unbounded-loop equivalence.** The verification loop is unbounded (I10 gives a lower bound, none upper). Bounded exploration (`max_loops=3`) and finite trace/footprint checks cannot prove behavioral equivalence for all iteration counts; a divergence could exist beyond the bound.
2. **True concurrency vs interleaving semantics.** Trace-based and replay-based checks flatten concurrency to interleavings. Footprint `parallel` relations are strong evidence but not a proof that the converted net preserves *step/partial-order* semantics rather than merely admitting the same interleavings (e.g., it cannot distinguish genuine simultaneity from all interleavings if the underlying formalism differs).
3. **Silent-behavior intent.** After hiding `None` labels, the "skip legal" branch is observable only as absence of `Legal Review`. Tests cannot confirm that a silent routing node in a derivative encodes exactly the intended optionality versus some other tau path with equivalent visible projection but different internal state.
4. **Semantic correctness of labels/intent.** Validation checks structure and behavior, not meaning: it cannot verify that `Assess Risk`, `Legal Review`, etc., correspond to the real-world activities intended, nor that the modeled optionality/synchronization matches the organization's actual change-control policy.
5. **Renderer/layout fidelity.** Passing behavioral tests says nothing about whether a given BPMN rendering (gateway types, back-edges, silent nodes) will be *read* correctly by stakeholders; visual misinterpretation risk (§6) is outside what any conformance test can certify.
