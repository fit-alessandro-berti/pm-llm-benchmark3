# 1. Assumptions and notation

**Event-order convention (A1).** When two events share the same rounded timestamp, I treat the physically necessary order as: arrival  S1 start, S1 end  S2 start, S2 start  S2 end. A zero-minute reported gap between, say, P08-S2-end (12:00) and P11-S2-start (12:00) is permissible because rounding to ±2.5 min can collapse a real gap of up to 5 min.

**Rounding treatment (A2).** Every reported time *t* may represent an actual time in [*t*  2.5 min, *t* + 2.5 min]. Any single time difference therefore carries ±5 min rounding uncertainty; I do **not** propagate this into point estimates but note where it matters for conclusions.

**Resource capacity (A3).** Each of I1, I2, C1, C2 can serve exactly one case at a time. Observed overlaps (none found in this data set after imputation) would be attributed to rounding, logging delay, or undocumented assistance.

**Queue discipline (A4).** I assume FIFO at each station, consistent with the data: cases are assigned to resources in arrival order with no overtaking.

**Imputation convention (A5 – "earliest-feasible start").** For each MISSING start, the resource must be free and the case must be available. The earliest time both conditions hold is the central (point) estimate; the latest time that still yields positive service time is the upper bound. I also report a "5-min-gap" alternative (Scenario B) that respects the consistently observed inter-case gap pattern. See §6.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

**Queue waiting time.**
$$W_{S1} = t_{\text{s1\_start}} - t_{\text{arrival}}, \qquad W_{S2} = t_{\text{s2\_start}} - t_{\text{s1\_end}}$$

For imputed starts: $W \in [W_{\text{min}}, W_{\text{max}}]$ where $W_{\text{min}}$ uses the earliest feasible start and $W_{\text{max}}$ the latest.

**Active service time.**
$$S_{S1} = t_{\text{s1\_end}} - t_{\text{s1\_start}}, \qquad S_{S2} = t_{\text{s2\_end}} - t_{\text{s2\_start}}$$
For OPEN activities: $S_{\text{elapsed}} = 14{:}00 - t_{\text{start}}$; final $S > S_{\text{elapsed}}$.

**Station sojourn.** $T_{\text{station}} = W + S$.

**Case-level total sojourn.** $T_{\text{case}} = t_{\text{s2\_end}} - t_{\text{arrival}}$ (exact for COMPLETE cases, independent of imputation).

**WIP at time *t*.** Count of cases whose arrival  *t* and whose S2 completion > *t* (including those in S1 queue, S1 service, S2 queue, S2 service).

**Throughput.** Completed departures per unit time, computed over sub-periods.

**Utilization.** $\rho = \frac{\text{busy minutes}}{\text{available minutes}}$ per resource over [08:00, 14:00]. For C2, available minutes exclude the 15-min maintenance window.

**Little's Law.** $L = \lambda \cdot W$ where $L$ = time-average WIP, $\lambda$ = throughput rate, $W$ = mean sojourn. We check this only in intervals where queues are continuously non-empty and the system is not in a pure start-up or drain-down transient. The generalized integral form is:

$$\int_{t_1}^{t_2} L(t)\,dt = \sum_{\text{all cases}} \bigl(\min(t_2, \text{departure}_i) - \max(t_1, \text{arrival}_i)\bigr)^+$$

# 5. Bottleneck and temporal interpretation

## Evidence

1. **Service time comparison.** S2 average service  39 min vs. S1  28 min. With equal resource counts (2 each), S2's maximum sustainable throughput is  3.1 cases/hr vs. S1's  4.3 cases/hr.

2. **Queue dynamics.** The S2 queue grows from 0 at 08:00 to 4 cases at 14:00, while the S1 queue drains from its peak of 5–6 (at ~11:00–12:00) to 1 by 14:00. This is the classic signature of a downstream bottleneck: work piles up behind the slower station.

3. **WIP migration.** Early in the window S1 WIP dominates (rapid arrivals overwhelm both stations, but S1 fills first). By 13:00–14:00, S2 WIP dominates (6 vs. 2 for S1), confirming that S2 is the binding constraint during the drain-down phase.

4. **Throughput stability.** S1 completion rate is stable at 3–4/hr across all sub-periods. S2 completion rate is 2–3/hr, consistently lower, and dips to 2/hr in the 11:00–12:00 period (coinciding with the C2 maintenance aftermath).

## Hypothesis: the bottleneck is primarily S2 throughout, with a transient S1 contribution

- **08:00–10:00 (high arrival rate, 6.5/hr).** Both stations are overloaded. S1 fills quickly because it is the entry point; S2 fills as S1 completions arrive. Both are bottlenecks simultaneously under this arrival surge.
- **10:00–12:00 (moderate arrivals, 4/hr).** S1 begins to catch up (completion rate 3.5–4/hr  arrival rate 4/hr for part of the period). S2 remains overloaded (throughput 2–3/hr < 4/hr input from S1). The C2 maintenance window (10:55–11:10) amplifies this: P08 is delayed ~25 min beyond what it would have been without maintenance, and the delay cascades to P11 and subsequent C2 cases.
- **12:00–14:00 (low arrivals, 0.5/hr).** S1 drains its queue. S2 still has 4 cases waiting plus 2 in progress at 14:00. S2 is unambiguously the bottleneck.

## Effect of C2 maintenance

The 15-min maintenance window directly idles C2 from 10:55–11:10. However, the **effective delay** is approximately 25 min for P08 (which could have started at ~10:50 but instead starts at 11:15) and propagates to P11 (~12:00 instead of ~11:35). The cascading delay to the S2 queue adds roughly 50–75 case-minutes of cumulative extra waiting across affected cases.

# ================================================================
df["s1_start_missing"] = df["s1_start"].eq("MISSING")
df["s1_end_open"]      = df["s1_end"].eq("OPEN")
df["s2_start_missing"] = df["s2_start"].eq("MISSING")
df["s2_end_open"]      = df["s2_end"].eq("OPEN")
df["s2_blank"]         = df["s2_start"].isin(["", None])
df["s1_blank"]         = df["s1_start"].isin(["", None]) & ~df["s1_start_missing"]

def parse_ts(v):
    if v in ("", None, "MISSING", "OPEN"):
        return pd.NaT
    return pd.Timestamp(f"2026-05-12 {v}")

for c in ["arrival","s1_start","s1_end","s2_start","s2_end"]:
    df[c + "_dt"] = df[c].apply(parse_ts)

# ================================================================
# 3. DEFINE THREE IMPUTATION SCENARIOS
# ================================================================
scenarios = {
    "A_earliest": {  # resource immediately starts  max service, min wait
        "P07_s1": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 09:25"),
        "P15_s1": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 11:40"),
        "P05_s2": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 09:45"),
        "P11_s2": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 12:00"),
    },
    "B_5min_gap": {  # consistent 5-min inter-case gap on each resource
        "P07_s1": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 09:30"),
        "P15_s1": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 11:45"),
        "P05_s2": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 09:50"),
        "P11_s2": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 12:05"),
    },
    "C_latest": {  # latest practical start  min service, max wait
        "P07_s1": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 09:55"),
        "P15_s1": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 12:10"),
        "P05_s2": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 10:10"),
        "P11_s2": pd.Timestamp("2026-05-12 12:20"),
    },
}

def apply_scenario(base_df, imp):
    d = base_df.copy()
    d.loc[d["case"]=="P07","s1_start_dt"] = imp["P07_s1"]
    d.loc[d["case"]=="P15","s1_start_dt"] = imp["P15_s1"]
    d.loc[d["case"]=="P05","s2_start_dt"] = imp["P05_s2"]
    d.loc[d["case"]=="P11","s2_start_dt"] = imp["P11_s2"]
    # S1 service: use OBS_END for OPEN, else s1_end_dt
    s1_end_eff = d["s1_end_dt"].where(~d["s1_end_open"], OBS_END)
    d["s1_svc"]  = (s1_end_eff - d["s1_start_dt"]).dt.total_seconds()/60
    d["s1_wait"] = (d["s1_start_dt"] - d["arrival_dt"]).dt.total_seconds()/60
    # S2 service / wait (only where s2_start is known)
    has_s2 = d["s2_start_dt"].notna()
    s2_end_eff = d["s2_end_dt"].where(~d["s2_end_open"], OBS_END)
    d["s2_svc"]  = np.where(has_s2, (s2_end_eff - d["s2_start_dt"]).dt.total_seconds()/60, np.nan)
    d["s2_wait"] = np.where(has_s2, (d["s2_start_dt"] - d["s1_end_dt"]).dt.total_seconds()/60, np.nan)
    # Total sojourn (exact for COMPLETE cases)
    d["sojourn"] = np.where(d["state"]=="COMPLETE",
                            (d["s2_end_dt"]-d["arrival_dt"]).dt.total_seconds()/60, np.nan)
    return d

sc = {k: apply_scenario(df, v) for k, v in scenarios.items()}

# ================================================================
# 4. PM4Py-COMPATIBLE INTERVAL-EVENT DATAFRAME (Scenario A)
#    Only include activities with known start AND end (not OPEN).
# ================================================================
base = sc["A_earliest"]

# S1 interval events (exclude OPEN P21 and blank P22)
s1_mask = base["s1_start_dt"].notna() & base["s1_end_dt"].notna() & ~base["s1_end_open"]
s1_evs = base.loc[s1_mask].copy()
s1_log = pd.DataFrame({
    "case:concept:name": s1_evs["case"].values,
    "concept:name":      "S1_Incoming_Inspection",
    "start_timestamp":   s1_evs["s1_start_dt"].values,
    "time:timestamp":    s1_evs["s1_end_dt"].values,
    "org:resource":      s1_evs["s1_res"].values,
})

# S2 interval events (only completed S2 — exclude OPEN, blank, waiting)
s2_mask = base["s2_start_dt"].notna() & base["s2_end_dt"].notna() & ~base["s2_end_open"]
s2_evs = base.loc[s2_mask].copy()
s2_log = pd.DataFrame({

pm_log = pd.concat([s1_log, s2_log], ignore_index=True)
pm_log = pm_log[["case:concept:name","concept:name",
