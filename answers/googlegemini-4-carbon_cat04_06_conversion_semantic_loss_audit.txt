Because N-posets are non-series-parallel, the source model is inherently non-block-structured.

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pipeline A (Primary)**: Direct Net-Mediated Conversion (`POWL  Accepting Petri Net  BPMN`) | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`  `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` (audited via `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)`) | Preserves non-block-structured partial orders (including the N-poset in `INV-04`), `1..*` binary loops, silent XOR bypasses, and exact label uniqueness (`1` visible transition per source `Transition`). | Introduces auxiliary silent transitions and unstructured gateway chains (`AND`/`XOR` combinations) that complicate BPMN layout readability and expand the silent reachability graph. | Returns null/empty `im` or `fm`, produces an unsound Petri net, or introduces mismatched BPMN gateways on round-trip conversion. |
| **Pipeline B (Block-Structured Probe)**: Tree-Mediated Conversion (`POWL  Accepting Petri Net  Process Tree  BPMN`) | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`  `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`  `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | Would yield clean, hierarchical, pair-matched BPMN gateways if the source behavior were series-parallel / block-structured. | Structural incompatibility: `source_powl` contains the irreducible N-poset `{assess_risk, prepare_evidence, optional_legal, verification_loop}` (`INV-04`), which cannot be represented as a label-unique Process Tree without altering semantics. | `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` raises an exception (typically `ValueError`) when workflow-net block reduction stalls on the N-subgraph, or—if forced by an approximating converter—drops/constrains valid interleavings. |

**Primary Pipeline Selection**: **Pipeline A (`POWL  Accepting Petri Net  BPMN`)** is chosen as the primary pipeline. A Process Tree can only express series-parallel block structures (`->`, `X`, `+`, `*`). In `source_powl`, `Assess Risk` enables both `Optional Legal` and `Verification`, whereas `Prepare Evidence` enables `Verification` without gating `Optional Legal`. Pipeline B is therefore expected to fail cleanly during `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(...)`; that exception is a **semantically faithful rejection**, confirming that PM4Py refuses to silently distort `INV-04` into a block tree.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

In the table below, `validated preserved` denotes properties directly verified by the structural, footprint, witness, and bounded exact-final-marking checks in Sections 3 and 5 under PM4Py's standard conversion semantics; `expected preserved` denotes properties preserved by the converter's formal construction rules beyond finite trace bounds or inside the BPMN object prior to Petri round-trip; `not tested` denotes non-interleaving execution claims outside Petri-net state/trace semantics.

| `INV-02`: Post-registration interleaving (`Assess Risk` vs. `Prepare Evidence`) | `validated preserved` | `expected preserved` | `conversion failed` | `validated preserved` | Footprint `parallel` relation and bounded trace exploration confirm both `AP` and `PA` interleavings survive round-trip. |

### Key Analytical Findings

1. **True concurrency versus arbitrary interleaving**: `net_from_powl` and `pipeline_a_bpmn_rt` represent concurrency via independent tokens in separate places (`AND`-splits/joins), which `pm4py.discover_footprints` detects as symmetric `(a, b)` and `(b, a)` entries in `'parallel'`. However, sequential trace playout and bounded trace exploration reduce concurrency to point-event interleavings; without place/footprint inspection, `AND(A, B)` cannot be distinguished from `XOR(A->B, B->A)`.
2. **Lack of an edge between Optional Legal Review and verification (and the N-poset)**: Because `source_powl` omits both `(prepare_evidence, optional_legal)` and any edge between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`, `Legal Review` can execute before `Prepare Evidence`, concurrently with `Prepare Evidence`, or concurrently with any `Configure Trial / Inspect Trial / Correct Defect` step. This exact freedom creates the N-poset that breaks `PetriTree` reduction (`conversion failed`) while surviving `PetriBPMNPetri` intact.
3. **Silent optional behavior**: `OperatorPOWL(XOR, [legal_review, skip_legal])` translates into a choice place with one `"Legal Review"` transition and one `None`-labeled transition leading to a merge place. In BPMN, this becomes an `ExclusiveGateway` split with an unlabeled bypass sequence flow directly to the corresponding `ExclusiveGateway` join. If a converter dropped that empty flow, `Legal Review` would silently become mandatory.
4. **Binary-loop semantics**: `OperatorPOWL(LOOP, [trial_body, correct_defect])` is a binary `do-redo` operator: entry flows unconditionally into `trial_body`, after which an XOR-split either exits via a silent transition or fires `"Correct Defect"` back to the entry merge before `trial_body`. Neither `POWLPetri` nor `PetriBPMNPetri` degrades this into a `0..*` loop or allows exiting directly after `Correct Defect`.
5. **The join before Release Change**: In `net_from_powl`, `Release Change` is guarded by silent synchronization transitions collecting completion places from both `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`. Crucially, when `skip_legal` fires, it still deposits a token into the `optional_legal` completion place, preventing deadlock at the `AND`-join while ensuring `Release Change` cannot fire while `Legal Review` is still pending.
6. **Activity-label uniqueness**: All eight visible labels (`Register Change`, `Assess Risk`, `Prepare Evidence`, `Legal Review`, `Configure Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, `Correct Defect`, `Release Change`, `Archive Change`) appear on exactly one transition each in both `net_from_powl` and `pipeline_a_bpmn_rt`. No visible transition duplication occurs.
7. **Initial and final markings**: `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net` yields explicit `Marking` objects with a single token in the unique source place and a single token in the unique sink place. Checking `im`/`fm` non-emptiness and Woflan soundness prevents false positives where traces end at `Archive Change` while leaving unconsumed tokens in parallel branches.
8. **Gateway or routing-node ambiguity in BPMN visualization**: PM4Py converts every multi-input/output place into an `ExclusiveGateway` and every multi-input/output silent transition into a `ParallelGateway`. Consequently, POWL's clean partial-order edges expand into cascaded `AND` and `XOR` diamonds connected by unlabeled sequence flows—behaviorally exact under token semantics, yet visually opaque.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The code below assumes execution in the same session after Section 3 (`net_from_powl`, `im_from_powl`, `fm_from_powl`, and `round_trip_models`).

**Exhaustiveness statement**: `explore_bounded_visible_traces` uses breadth-first search keyed on `(frozen_marking, visible_trace)`—so distinct visible prefixes reaching the same marking are *never* conflated, while silent cycles at a fixed visible prefix terminate cleanly. Provided `tau_streak_cap_hit == False` (verified at runtime), the comparison is **mathematically exhaustive for all completed visible traces with `len(trace) <= MAX_VISIBLE_LEN` (`13`) and `trace.count("Correct Defect") <= MAX_REDO_COUNT` (`2`, i.e., up to 3 full verification iterations, yielding all 45 valid completed traces)**. It is **not** exhaustive for `> 3` loop iterations (`len(trace) > 13`).

```python
from collections import deque
from typing import Deque, FrozenSet, List, Set, Tuple

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking, PetriNet

FrozenMarking = FrozenSet[Tuple[Any, int]]


def freeze_marking(marking: Marking) -> FrozenMarking:
    """Canonical hashable representation ignoring any accidental zero entries."""
    return frozenset((place, count) for place, count in marking.items() if count > 0)


def fire_transition(marking: Marking, transition: PetriNet.Transition) -> Marking:
    """Pure Petri-net transition firing that strips zero-token places for exact Marking equality."""
    next_marking = Marking(marking)
    for in_arc in transition.in_arcs:
        next_marking[in_arc.source] -= in_arc.weight
        if next_marking[in_arc.source] <= 0:
            del next_marking[in_arc.source]
    for out_arc in transition.out_arcs:
        next_marking[out_arc.target] += out_arc.weight
    return next_marking


def accepts_visible_trace(
    net: PetriNet,
    im: Marking,
    fm: Marking,
    target_trace: Tuple[str, ...],
    max_states: int = 50_000,
) -> bool:
    """
    Exact membership test for a single visible trace:
    hides transitions where label is None, avoids silent cycles via (frozen_marking, idx),
    and requires exact final-marking equality (m == fm) after consuming target_trace.
    """
    start_state = (freeze_marking(im), 0)
    visited: Set[Tuple[FrozenMarking, int]] = {start_state}
    queue: Deque[Tuple[Marking, int]] = deque([(im, 0)])

    explored = 0
    while queue:
        marking, idx = queue.popleft()
        explored += 1
        if explored > max_states:
            raise RuntimeError(
                f"State bound ({max_states}) exceeded while testing trace {target_trace}."
            )

        if idx == len(target_trace) and marking == fm:
            return True

        for trans in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking):
            next_m = fire_transition(marking, trans)
            if trans.label is None:
                key = (freeze_marking(next_m), idx)
                if key not in visited:
                    visited.add(key)
                    queue.append((next_m, idx))
            elif idx < len(target_trace) and trans.label == target_trace[idx]:
                key = (freeze_marking(next_m), idx + 1)
                if key not in visited:
                    visited.add(key)
                    queue.append((next_m, idx + 1))

    return False


def explore_bounded_visible_traces(
    net: PetriNet,
    im: Marking,
    fm: Marking,
    max_visible_len: int = 13,
    max_redo_count: int = 2,
    max_tau_streak: int = 200,
) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """
    Bounded BFS over visible traces using pm4py.get_enabled_transitions.
    - Hides transitions with t.label is None.
    - Requires exact marking == fm for completed traces.
    - Prevents infinite silent cycling via (freeze_marking(m), visible_trace) + max_tau_streak.
    - Enforces max_visible_len and max_redo_count ('Correct Defect' occurrences).
    """
    completed_traces: Set[Tuple[str, ...]] = set()
    deadlocks: List[Tuple[Tuple[str, ...], Marking]] = []
    tau_streak_cap_hit = False

    init_trace: Tuple[str, ...] = ()
    visited: Set[Tuple[FrozenMarking, Tuple[str, ...]]] = {
        (freeze_marking(im), init_trace)
    }
    queue: Deque[Tuple[Marking, Tuple[str, ...], int]] = deque([(im, init_trace, 0)])

    while queue:
        marking, vis_trace, tau_streak = queue.popleft()

        if marking == fm:
            completed_traces.add(vis_trace)

        enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
        if not enabled and marking != fm:
            deadlocks.append((vis_trace, marking))
            continue

        for trans in enabled:
            next_m = fire_transition(marking, trans)
            if trans.label is None:
                if tau_streak + 1 > max_tau_streak:
                    tau_streak_cap_hit = True
                    continue
                state_key = (freeze_marking(next_m), vis_trace)
                if state_key not in visited:
                    visited.add(state_key)
                    queue.append((next_m, vis_trace, tau_streak + 1))
            else:
                next_trace = vis_trace + (trans.label,)
                if len(next_trace) > max_visible_len:
                    continue
                if (
                    trans.label == "Correct Defect"
                    and next_trace.count("Correct Defect") > max_redo_count
                ):
                    continue
                state_key = (freeze_marking(next_m), next_trace)
                if state_key not in visited:
                    visited.add(state_key)
                    queue.append((next_m, next_trace, 0))

    return {
        "completed_traces": completed_traces,
        "deadlocks": deadlocks,
        "tau_streak_cap_hit": tau_streak_cap_hit,
        "exhaustive_within_bound": not tau_streak_cap_hit,
    }


def compare_footprints(
    fp_ref: Dict[str, Any],
    fp_cand: Dict[str, Any],
) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """Compare behavioral footprint relations between reference and candidate nets."""
    keys = ("activities", "start_activities", "end_activities", "sequence", "parallel")
    diffs: Dict[str, Any] = {}
    for k in keys:
        ref_set = set(fp_ref.get(k, set()))
        cand_set = set(fp_cand.get(k, set()))
        if ref_set != cand_set:
            diffs[k] = {
                "missing_in_candidate": sorted(ref_set - cand_set),
                "extra_in_candidate": sorted(cand_set - ref_set),
            }
    return {"footprints_equal": len(diffs) == 0, "differences": diffs}


def inspect_reachability_graph(
    net: PetriNet,
    im: Marking,
    fm: Marking,
) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """Build reachability graph and verify finiteness and unique terminal sink state."""
    rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
    sink_states = [s for s in rg.states if len(s.outgoing) == 0]
    return {
        "num_states": len(rg.states),
        "num_transitions": len(rg.transitions),
        "num_sink_states": len(sink_states),
    }
```

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

Even when `bpmn_from_net` passes every soundness, footprint, witness, and bounded-trace equivalence test, its rendered BPMN diagram invites systematic human misreading because Petri-to-BPMN translation exposes low-level token routing rather than hierarchical POWL operators:

1. **Unstructured Parallel Gateways (`+` / AND-splits and AND-joins)**:
   In `net_from_powl`, partial-order dependencies (`assess_risk  optional_legal`, `assess_risk  verification_loop`, `prepare_evidence  verification_loop`) are realized through silent synchronization transitions. In BPMN, these render as separate `AND`-splits after `Assess Risk` and an `AND`-join before `Verification` that shares one arm with `Prepare Evidence`, plus a downstream `AND`-join before `Release Change`. Readers accustomed to block-structured BPMN look for symmetric `1:1` split/join brackets and frequently misinterpret cross-wired `AND` gateways as a modeling defect or potential deadlock.
2. **Exclusive Gateways (`X` / XOR-splits and XOR-joins) Without Guards**:
   `optional_legal` renders as an `XOR`-split with one path to `Legal Review` and a bare, unlabeled bypass sequence flow directly into the merging `XOR`-join. Business stakeholders regularly overlook unlabeled bypass edges (assuming `Legal Review` is always executed) or confuse the loop-entry `XOR`-join (merging initial entry with the `Correct Defect` loopback) with an alternative business choice.
3. **Flattened Loop Visualization**:
   POWL's explicit `Operator.LOOP(trial_body, correct_defect)` becomes an unstructured cycle in BPMN: an `XOR`-join before `Configure Trial`, forward sequence flows through `Inspect Trial`, an `XOR`-split after `Inspect Trial`, and a backward edge passing through `Correct Defect`. Layered layout engines often place `Correct Defect` in a column adjacent to `Configure Trial` or `Inspect Trial`, obscuring both the `1..*` minimum-iteration guarantee (`INV-07`) and the fact that `Correct Defect` can only occur *after* an inspection failure (`INV-08`).
4. **Silent Routing Nodes and Gateway Chains**:
   POWL subnet entry/exit connectors (`t.label is None`) induce back-to-back gateways (e.g., an `XOR`-join feeding directly into an `AND`-split or vice versa with no intervening Task). Stakeholders often read every diamond as an explicit human/system decision gate and assume labels were accidentally stripped during export.
5. **Apparent Ordering from Graph Layout Coordinates**:
   Graphviz's Sugiyama rank assignment places concurrent nodes at specific horizontal/vertical ranks. For example, `Assess Risk` may be drawn above `Prepare Evidence`, and `Legal Review` may be aligned horizontally with `Configure Trial`. Readers routinely conflate left-to-right visual coordinates with mandatory temporal order, missing that `Legal Review` can legally execute *before* `Prepare Evidence` (`INV-04`) or *after* multiple `Correct Defect` cycles (`INV-05`).

### Recommended Visual Annotations and Documentation

- **Gateway Guard Labels**: Label the `optional_legal` `XOR`-split outgoing flows `[Legal review required]` (to `Legal Review`) and `[No legal review needed (skip)]` (on the bypass flow), and label the post-`Inspect Trial` `XOR`-split `[Defect found]` (to `Correct Defect`) and `[Trial passed]` (toward `Release Change`).
- **Visual Grouping Box for the Verification Loop**: Surround `Configure Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, and `Correct Defect` with a dashed BPMN `Group` artifact annotated `"Verification Loop (1..* iterations: Configure  Inspect; repeat via Correct Defect)"`.
- **Synchronization Callout at `Release Change` and Trial Entry**: Attach a BPMN `TextAnnotation` to the `AND`-join before `Configure Trial` (`"Waits for BOTH Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence"`) and to the `AND`-join before `Release Change` (`"Waits for passed Verification Loop AND Legal Review/Skip"`).
- **Concurrency Legend**: Include a diagram header note stating explicitly that `Legal Review` requires only `Assess Risk` (not `Prepare Evidence`) and may occur concurrently with evidence preparation or trial execution regardless of horizontal alignment.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Any candidate conversion or round-trip artifact must be **rejected or quarantined** if any of the following conditions occur:

1. **Null Artifact, Missing Markings, or Unhandled Conversion Failure**:
   `convert_to_petri_net` or `convert_to_bpmn` raises an exception (as in Pipeline B's `PetriTree` step), returns `None`, or produces an empty/null `im` or `fm` (`len(im) == 0` or `len(fm) == 0`).
2. **Workflow-Net Violation or Woflan Unsoundness**:
   `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)` is `False`, `pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)[0]` is `False`, bounded exploration discovers a deadlock (`enabled == set()` at `m != fm`), or the reachability graph contains `!= 1` terminal sink states.
3. **Failed Exact-Final-Marking Completion (`m != fm`)**:
   Any execution fires `Archive Change` while leaving residual tokens in `optional_legal` or `verification_loop` places, failing exact `Marking` equality `m == fm`.
4. **Loss of Valid Concurrency or N-Poset Interleavings (Under-Approximation)**:
   The converted model rejects `Prepare Evidence  Assess Risk`, rejects `Assess Risk  Legal Review  Prepare Evidence` (`W_POS_03`, typical of forced block-tree sequentialization), or rejects `Legal Review` interleaved mid-trial (`W_POS_04`, `W_POS_05`).
5. **Synchronization Failure Before Verification or Release (Over-Approximation)**:
   The converted model admits `Configure Trial` before both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` have occurred (`W_NEG_04`), or admits `Release Change` before `verification_loop` exits or before a chosen `Legal Review` finishes (`W_NEG_09`).
6. **Collapse or Distortion of Silent Optionality**:
   `Legal Review` becomes mandatory (`W_POS_01` rejected), becomes repeatable more than once (`W_NEG_03` accepted), or becomes enabled prior to `Assess Risk` (`W_NEG_02` accepted).
7. **Corruption of Binary-Loop Semantics**:
   The model admits zero trial iterations (`W_NEG_06` accepted, degrading `1..*` into `0..*`), allows repeated trials without `Correct Defect` (`W_NEG_07`), allows exiting the loop directly from `Correct Defect` (`W_NEG_08`), or inverts `Configure Trial  Inspect Trial` (`W_NEG_05`).
8. **Activity-Label Loss, Duplication, or Silent-Label Leakage**:
   The set of visible transition/task labels deviates from the exact 8 source labels, duplicates a visible label across multiple transitions without explicit approval, or renders `None`/`tau` transitions as visible BPMN `Task` nodes.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

- **Authoritative Canonical Model (Storage & Editing)**:
  **`source_powl` (the Python/serialized POWL specification)** must serve as the authoritative source of truth under version control. POWL natively and compactly expresses both hierarchical control-flow operators (`Operator.XOR` with `SilentTransition`, `Operator.LOOP`) and non-block-structured partial orders (`StrictPartialOrder` with the N-poset) without gateway proliferation or silent-connector noise.
- **Stakeholder Visualization Model (Read-Only Derivative)**:
  **Pipeline A's BPMN model (`bpmn_from_net`)**, enriched with the non-behavioral annotations from Section 6, should be published for business and compliance stakeholders. Meanwhile, **`(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)`** serves as the derivative formal verification intermediate.
- **Required Validation Evidence per Derivative Release**:
  Every generated BPMN diagram and Petri net artifact must be accompanied in CI by a machine-checked manifest containing:
  1. Source Git commit SHA and pinned `pm4py` package version;
  2. Structural & Woflan soundness certificate (`is_workflow_net == True`, `is_sound == True`, `num_sink_states == 1`, `visible_labels_unique == True`);
  3. Footprint equality check (`compare_footprints` zero diff between `net_from_powl` and `pipeline_a_bpmn_rt`);
  4. `100%` pass report on `POSITIVE_WITNESSES` and `NEGATIVE_WITNESSES`;
  5. Zero-difference bounded exact-final-marking trace report (`missing == 0`, `extra == 0`, `exhaustive_within_bound == True` at `MAX_VISIBLE_LEN = 13`).
- **Policy on Process-Tree Intermediates**:
  A Process Tree intermediate **must not be required or used for conversion** of this model family. Because POWL strictly generalizes Process Trees, enforcing `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree` fails on valid N-poset dependencies (`INV-04`), and attempting to bypass that failure via log playout + process discovery (e.g., Inductive Miner) silently degrades precision by over-approximating `INV-04` and `INV-09`. `convert_to_process_tree` may only be retained as a non-blocking *structural classifier* to report whether a future model revision happens to be series-parallel.
- **Preventing Round-Trip Drift Across Future Edits**:
  Enforce a strict **one-way compilation workflow (`source_powl  Petri Net  BPMN`)**. Never import edited BPMN XML back into Petri nets or POWL to overwrite the repository baseline, because each round-trip compounds auxiliary silent gateways and discards POWL hierarchy. Stakeholder feedback on BPMN diagrams must be implemented directly in `source_powl`, re-compiled via Pipeline A, and gated by the Section 5 test suite.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every diagnostic, witness, footprint, reachability, playout, and bounded-trace check in Sections 3 and 5 passes with zero discrepancies, the validation suite **cannot** establish the following claims:

1. **Behavioral Equivalence Beyond the Bounded Loop Horizon (`k > 2` Redos)**:
   Bounded visible-trace exploration proves exact completed-trace parity up to `MAX_VISIBLE_LEN = 13` (`3` trial iterations). Without a formal weak-bisimulation equivalence proof over the full reachability graphs of `net_from_powl` and `pipeline_a_bpmn_rt`, anomalous divergences occurring only at higher iteration counts (`k >= 3`) are not ruled out by trace enumeration alone.
2. **True Non-Atomic Wall-Clock Concurrency and Resource Isolation**:
   Petri-net firing semantics and footprints model transitions as instantaneous atomic events. Passing all tests does not prove that a downstream BPMN runtime executes `Legal Review` and `Configure Trial` simultaneously in wall-clock time, nor does it detect shared-state race conditions or resource starvation between concurrent tasks.
3. **Decision-Guard, Data, and Organizational Semantics**:
   `source_powl` abstracts away data state and actor roles. Passing control-flow validation cannot verify *when* `Legal Review` is legally mandated versus skipped, *what* inspection telemetry triggers `Correct Defect` versus `Release Change`, or whether separation-of-duties rules (e.g., `Configure Trial` vs. `Inspect Trial` actors) are enforced in BPMN lanes.
4. **External BPMN 2.0 Engine Conformance vs. Correlated PM4Py Converter Behavior**:
   Round-trip validation tests `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(...))`. A symmetric semantic assumption shared by PM4Py's BPMN exporter and importer—or stricter BPMN 2.0 gateway/token handling in third-party engines such as Camunda, Flowable, or Signavio when executing unstructured `AND`/`XOR` combinations and bare bypass flows—will not be caught without target-engine execution tests.
5. **Stakeholder Cognitive Fidelity**:
   Formal behavioral equivalence of `bpmn_from_net` does not guarantee human comprehension; readers can still misread Graphviz node coordinates as causal precedence or misinterpret unlabeled gateway chains despite a mathematically sound underlying graph.
