| **Rework rate** | Number of rework loops per 100 cases | Lower | Reduction > 1 loop per 100 cases |
| **Backlog** | Number of cases awaiting any resource at month end | Lower | No specific threshold; monitor trend |
| **Robustness** | Change in p90 duration under stress scenarios vs. baseline stress | Smaller increase | If increase over 15% is prevented, intervention is robust |
| **Cost/resource shift** | Additional FTE months (e.g., temporary inspector) vs. duration gains | Qualitative tradeoff | Report cost per day saved |

The selection of interventions will be based on a multicriteria dashboard: an intervention is considered superior if it improves at least two key metrics (median, p90, SLA) without a statistically significant degradation in any other metric (except possibly resource utilisation). The combined D10 package will be assessed for synergistic or antagonistic effects.
