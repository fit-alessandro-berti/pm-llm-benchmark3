# Warranty Process Redesign Analysis

## 1. Current-process diagnosis

The warranty process exhibits a fundamental tension between efficiency and quality/compliance. The most frequent path (Intake  Auto eligibility screen  Fast approval  Dispatch replacement) is attractive for its speed (median 0.5+0.4+1.8+3.2=6.9 hours) and low cost (€0.20 auto-screen + minimal labor), but suffers from:
- **High rework rates**: 14.1% complaint/reopen rate for Fast approval cases vs. 3.3% for repair paths, with wrong replacements costing €95-€410
- **Compliance violations**: Frequent "Fast approval before Evidence completeness" deviations (violating R2) and "Dispatch replacement without risk review for high-value devices" (violating R1)
- **Long-tail failures**: Dispatch replacement  Customer complaint/reopen has median 70 hours (p90 180h), indicating severe downstream pain
- **Hidden costs**: Fast approval cases are only 9% cheaper *after* accounting for complaints/reshipments, despite being 31% cheaper before rework
- **Segment mismatch**: Premium high-value devices show 22% complaint rate on fast path vs. 5% on reviewed path, yet 14% of cases take this risky route

The process-mining artifacts reveal:
1. **Low conformance**: Log fitness 0.91 with only 68% fitting traces, indicating systematic deviations from normative rules (R1-R3)
2. **Precision issues**: Discovered model allows invalid combinations of auto-approval and risk review
3. **Temporal deviations**: Manual eligibility review  Repair quote is too slow for Premium cases (median 15h, p90 60h)
4. **Rework loops**: 26% of cases entering "Request missing evidence" do so at least twice, with median 26h per iteration
5. **Resource bottlenecks**: Fraud/risk review (2 specialists) and Manual eligibility review (7 reviewers at 82%+ utilization) are constrained

The contradiction is resolved by recognizing that the fast path's apparent efficiency is an artifact of:
- Outdated auto-screen rules (not segment-aware)
- Missing pre-dispatch quality gates
- Inadequate evidence completeness checks for high-risk segments
- Underinvestment in upstream quality preventing downstream fire-fighting

## 2. Redesign A: Segment-Aware Fast Lane with Pre-Dispatch Gate

**Process flow**:
1. Intake claim  **Segment/risk classifier** (new)
2. For **Premium low-risk accessories**:
   - Auto eligibility screen  Fast approval  **Lightweight quality gate** (new)  Dispatch replacement
3. For **Standard low-risk accessories**:
   - Auto eligibility screen  Fast approval  Dispatch replacement (unchanged)
4. For **all other segments**:
   - Auto eligibility screen  **Enhanced evidence check** (new)  Manual eligibility review or Fraud/risk review (risk-based)
   - Then proceed to Technical decision  Fulfillment

**Targets**:
- Premium low-risk accessories (18% of cases) where service credits are at risk if >24h
- Standard low-risk accessories (25%) where customers tolerate 72h with good visibility

**Mechanism**:
- The **segment/risk classifier** (deployable in 2 weeks) routes cases based on customer tier and defect type
- **Lightweight quality gate** (6-18h) catches wrong replacements for Premium accessories before dispatch
- **Enhanced evidence check** reduces repeated evidence requests by 30-45% (via new portal) for complex cases

**Sacrifices**:
- Adds 6-18h to Premium accessory path (but still under 24h threshold)
- Increases manual review inflow by 15-35% (from auto-screen rule updates)
- Does not address high-value device compliance risks as aggressively as other redesigns

## 3. Redesign B: Risk-Based Dual Track with Mandatory Review for High-Value

**Process flow**:
1. Intake claim  **Value/risk triage** (new)
2. **Low-value track** (<€400, low-risk):
   - Auto eligibility screen  Fast approval  Dispatch replacement
3. **High-value track** (€400 or high-risk):
   - **Mandatory Fraud/risk review** (new)  Technical decision 
     - If repair: Repair quote  Customer approval  Repair device  Quality check
     - If replace: **Pre-dispatch verification** (new)  Dispatch replacement
4. **Unclear defect track**:
   - Manual eligibility review  **Dedicated evidence concierge** (new)  Fraud/risk review if needed

**Targets**:
- High-value devices (Premium and Standard, 35% of cases) where compliance risk and wrong-replacement costs are highest
- Unclear defect cases (22%) with high abandonment rates

**Mechanism**:
- **Mandatory Fraud/risk review** for all €400 claims enforces R1
- **Pre-dispatch verification** (6-18h) prevents wrong replacements (€410 average cost for high-value)
- **Evidence concierge** reduces repeated requests by proactively guiding customers

**Sacrifices**:
- Increases cycle time for high-value cases (adds 12-16h median for Fraud/risk review)
- Requires adding 1-2 Fraud/risk specialists (current bottleneck)
- Standard low-risk cases see no improvement in their 8% complaint rate

## 4. Redesign C: Premium-Only Concierge with Standard Process Optimization

**Process flow**:
1. Intake claim  **Premium detection**
2. **Premium cases**:
   - **Dedicated Premium queue**  Manual eligibility review (prioritized) 
     - If accessory: Fast approval  Dispatch replacement
     - If device: Fraud/risk review  Technical decision  Fulfillment
   - **24/7 status visibility** and **proactive updates**
3. **Standard cases**:
   - Auto eligibility screen 
     - If evidence complete: Fast approval  Dispatch replacement
     - If missing evidence: **Automated evidence request** (new) with 48h deadline  Manual review if unresolved
   - **Self-service repair scheduling** for repair quotes

**Targets**:
- Premium customers (32% of cases) with strict service expectations
- Standard cases with missing evidence (22% of total)

**Mechanism**:
- **Dedicated Premium queue** reduces Manual eligibility review time for Premium cases (current median 10h  target 4h)
- **Automated evidence requests** with deadlines reduce repeated loops (26%  target 10%)
- **Self-service repair scheduling** reduces Repair quote  Customer approval time (current median 21h  target 8h)

**Sacrifices**:
- No direct improvement for Standard high-value device compliance (still 18% complaint rate on fast path)
- Requires adding 1-2 Manual eligibility reviewers for Premium queue
- Standard cases with complex defects may still hit bottlenecks

## 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

**Redesign A**:
- **Failure 1**: Segment classifier misclassifies high-value devices as low-risk  compliance violations persist
- **Failure 2**: Lightweight gate becomes a rubber stamp under time pressure  wrong replacements still shipped
- **Bottleneck migration**: Manual eligibility review queue grows 15-35%  utilization exceeds 100%

**Redesign B**:
- **Failure 1**: Fraud/risk review becomes a pro forma step without real scrutiny  compliance theater
- **Failure 2**: Pre-dispatch verification adds delay without catching errors  cycle time up, quality unchanged
- **Bottleneck migration**: Fraud/risk review becomes severe bottleneck (2 specialists can't handle 35% of cases)

**Redesign C**:
- **Failure 1**: Premium queue starves Standard cases of resources  Standard cycle times degrade
- **Failure 2**: Automated evidence requests alienate low-digital-literacy customers  abandonment increases
- **Bottleneck migration**: Manual eligibility review for Premium cases hits capacity (7 reviewers can't prioritize 32% of cases)

## 7. Recommended experiment sequence

**First Pilot**: Redesign A (Segment-Aware Fast Lane)
- **Scope**: Premium low-risk accessories (18% of cases) + Standard low-risk accessories (25%)
- **Metrics**:
  - Primary: Complaint/reopen rate (target: <4% for Premium, <5% for Standard)
  - Secondary: Cycle time (target: <24h for Premium, <72h for Standard), cost per case, Fraud/risk review utilization
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - Stop if: Complaint rate >6% for Premium or >7% for Standard after 4 weeks
  - Go if: Complaint rate <5% for both segments *and* cycle time targets met for 3 consecutive weeks
- **Sequencing Logic**:
  - If successful, expand to Standard mid-risk devices
  - If Fraud/risk review utilization >90%, implement Redesign B's mandatory review for high-value before scaling further
- **Downstream Validation**:
  - Track Dispatch replacement  Customer complaint/reopen rate for 90 days post-pilot
  - Compare wrong-replacement costs vs. pre-pilot baseline

**Second Pilot**: Redesign B (Risk-Based Dual Track)
- **Scope**: High-value devices only (35% of cases)
- **Prerequisite**: Must add 1 Fraud/risk specialist (total 3) to handle volume
- **Metrics**:
  - Primary: Compliance violations (R1/R2), wrong-replacement cost
  - Secondary: Cycle time for high-value (target: <48h median), Fraud/risk review queue time
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - Stop if: Fraud/risk review queue >48h median or compliance violations >5%
  - Go if: Wrong-replacement cost reduced by >50% *and* compliance violations <2% for 4 weeks

**Third Pilot**: Redesign C (Premium Concierge)
- **Scope**: Premium customers only (32% of cases)
- **Prerequisite**: Must add 1 Manual eligibility reviewer (total 8)
- **Metrics**:
  - Primary: Premium customer satisfaction (CSAT), cycle time (target: <24h for accessories, <48h for devices)
  - Secondary: Abandonment rate for Standard cases, Manual review queue time
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - Stop if: Standard case cycle time increases >10% or abandonment rate >25%
  - Go if: Premium CSAT improves >15 points *and* cycle time targets met for 3 weeks

**Learning Approach**:
- Use **control groups** (10% of cases in each segment continue on current process)
- Monitor **leading indicators**: Time in Fraud/risk review queue, evidence request loops
- Conduct **root-cause analysis** on any complaint/reopen cases in pilot groups
- **A/B test** the evidence portal in Redesign A to measure digital literacy impact before full rollout
