## 2. Competing explanations and timeline

**Jan–Mar: Stable baseline.**  
Variant mix ~45/35/20, label maturity 100%, median delay ~33 d, DFG part-delay 28–35 h, V3 mature breach ~0.30–0.32, AUROC ~0.81, ECE ~0.025. No overlap of deterioration; pipeline clean.

**Apr: Pipeline failure, not system deterioration.**  
Migration renames diagnosis event. The feature pipeline recognized only the old label, so prediction prefixes were built at wrong events or omitted. Unknown-activity share explodes to 17%, missing-queue 14%, and the derived “events before diagnosis” feature was cached wrong. The observed breach rate (0.19) is unchanged from baseline, but mean predicted risk jumps to 0.28, AUROC collapses to 0.67, Brier 0.184, ECE 0.110. Variant mapping fails (18% unmapped). **Do not interpret April as concept or process drift; it is measurement failure.**

**May: Partial repair with residual pipeline debt.**  
Mapping patch deployed 6 May; most April records backfilled, but cached feature not fully recomputed. Unknown activity falls to 2%, missing queue 3%, metrics recover partially (AUROC 0.78, Brier 0.146, ECE 0.050) but remain worse than Jan–Mar. Residual error must be cleaned before using April/May for training.

**Jun: Real process mix change, not necessarily degradation.**  
Remote-routing policy (1 Jun) drives V2 from ~34% to 52% and V1 to 30%. The remote-fix DFG edge stays stable (3.8–4.6 h); V2 breach rate stays ~0.09–0.11. Mean predicted risk 0.20 vs observed 0.15; AUROC 0.79 is acceptable. This is **covariate / process drift** (routing), not concept drift, because the conditional outcome for each variant is largely preserved.

**Jul: Emerging supplier-driven process/concept drift.**  
Supplier misses targets (10 Jul). Median `Order Part  Part Received` jumps to 65 h (from 38). V3 mature breach rises to 0.46. Label maturity 98%, so this is largely real. Model AUROC 0.76 begins to slide; mean predicted risk 0.18 vs observed 0.19 is close, but V3 subgroup is deteriorating.

**Aug: Overlap of process, concept, and early censoring.**  
Supplier severe: part delay 130 h. V3 breach 0.64; V3 share rises to 35% (pre-Z). Observed breach 0.28; predicted 0.19 (underprediction). AUROC 0.68; Brier 0.197; ECE 0.120. Label maturity 93% (7% immature, mostly long V3 paths). **August must not be treated as pure label delay; the supplier-driven V3 degradation is real, but some observed rate is inflated by unresolved cases that will breach later.**

**Sep: Triple overlap—supplier, population, label censoring.**  
Partner Z onboarded (1 Sep): 24% of cases, 58% V3 routing. Supplier delay 148 h. V3 breach 0.61; observed overall 0.31; predicted 0.21 (large gap). Label maturity 82%; median delay 42 d; Z labels mature more slowly. **This is the most confounded month:** process drift (supplier), population shift (Z), conceptual change (V3 conditional outcome high), and label delay all overlap.

**Oct: Label-delay and API backlog dominate.**  
Outcome API backlog; maturity 55%; delay 52 d. Observed breach 0.16 collapses—this is an **artifact of censoring**, because long V3 paths (now ~41% of cases) have not resolved or been labeled. Predicted risk 0.23 is consistent with high underlying risk. AUROC 0.64 and ECE 0.130 are **unreliable** if computed against immature, selectively censored labels. **October should be excluded from performance comparison until fixed-vintage mature cohorts are available.**

## 3. Diagnostic tests

### A. Label-maturity curves and fixed-vintage backtesting
*Method:* For each monthly cohort, plot maturity % by days since case start. For Oct, only 55% mature at reporting cutoff (vs 100% Jan–Jun). Backtest: train on Jan–Jun, predict Jul–Sep cohorts, and evaluate using labels matured at a fixed vintage (e.g., +60 d after case start) rather than at cutoff.

- **Under H1 (label delay / censoring explains Oct and partly Aug–Sep):** Fixed-vintage evaluation for Oct shows observed breach rate rebounds to ~0.30+ (similar to Sep) and AUROC improves toward ~0.75; censoring bias explains the 0.16 observed rate.
- **Under H2 (true performance collapse):** Even with fully mature Oct labels, observed rate stays near 0.16 and AUROC remains ~0.64; model is genuinely degraded by other drift.

### B. Recompute after activity-label normalization and duplicate cleaning
*Method:* Normalize all `Diagnostic Assessment Completed`  `Initial Diagnosis Completed`; apply global exact-duplicate removal (not just post-20 Apr); rebuild “events before diagnosis” feature from cleaned log; rerun feature pipeline for Apr–May.

- **Under H1 (pipeline failure):** Apr metrics restore to baseline (AUROC ~0.81, ECE ~0.02); unknown share <0.5%; mean predicted risk aligns with observed.
- **Under H2 (real Apr process/concept change):** Metrics stay degraded (AUROC <0.75) even with perfect labels and cleaned features; suggests a coincident operational change.

### C. Calibration and discrimination stratified by variant / source / month
*Method:* Compute AUROC, Brier, ECE separately for V1/V2/V3; for Partner Z vs non-Z; per month.

- **Under H1 (localized V3 concept/process drift):** V1/V2 AUROC remain ~0.80; only V3 degrades (to ~0.60); ECE high only in V3.
- **Under H2 (global pipeline / data issue):** All variants degrade in Apr, and all degrade uniformly in Aug–Oct; correlation with unknown-activity / missing-queue share explains performance.

### D. Process variants and DFG / performance-DFG edges
*Method:* Monthly `pm4py.discover_dfg` and `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg`; extract median elapsed times for `Order Part  Part Received` and `Initial Diagnosis Completed  Remote Fix`.

- **Under H1 (supplier-driven process drift):** Only V3 edge times lengthen (28155 h); remote-fix edge stable; variant share change explains mix but not per-case outcome change alone.
- **Under H2 (universal system slowdown / timestamp error):** All edges lengthen; remote fix also increases; indicates clock or logging issue rather than supplier failure.

### E. Outcome relationship conditional on same path
*Method:* Restrict to V3 cases with `Order Part` recorded; stratify by month (Jul vs Aug vs Sep) and by quartile of part-delay time; check if P(breach | early features, path = V3) changed.

- **Under H1 (concept drift / supplier effect):** Within the same part-delay quartile, Aug/Sep breach rate is still significantly higher than Jul; conditional relationship changed.
- **Under H2 (pure process-mix / population):** After controlling for part-delay time and source (Partner Z vs other), breach rate is stable; observed rise is driven by longer delays and more Z/V3 cases.

### F. Population reweighting / transport-style checks for Partner Z
*Method:* Estimate propensity of being Partner Z by observed features (excluding unavailable rural/device-age); reweight non-Z to Z distribution (or vice versa); compare predicted vs observed breach; train model on non-Z, test on Z.

- **Under H1 (population shift / missing features):** Reweighted metrics match baseline; model failure on Z is due to feature absence / distribution shift, not unfair behavior.
- **Under H2 (intrinsic outcome difference):** Even after reweighting to identical feature distributions, Z cases breach at higher rates; suggests unobserved process or geographic effect requiring new predictors.

### G. Feature missingness and lineage audits
*Method:* Audit pipeline job logs per feature; correlate missing-queue rate with renamed event source; check if duplicate removal was applied globally; compare derived feature distributions pre/post patch.

- **Under H1 (pipeline failure):** Missingness is localized to features dependent on diagnosis event label and pre-20 Apr duplicates; recomputation eliminates gaps.
- **Under H2 (systematic decay):** Missingness spreads randomly across unrelated features; no correlation with migration event.

## 4. Recalibration-versus-retraining decision

**Immediate containment (now)**
- **Quarantine October cohort:** Do not include Oct in any training, validation, or calibration set until labels mature (target: 90% mature, median delay <40 d). Use only mature cohorts (case start before Aug 1, evaluated at fixed +60 d) for current metrics.
- **Freeze model deployment for V3-heavy routing:** Until supplier-delay feature is added, apply conservative manual review for V3 predictions in Aug–Oct.
- **Data quality gate:** Block model updates if unknown-activity share >1% or missing-queue rate >2%.

**Short-term recalibration (2–4 weeks, after pipeline fix)**
- After feature pipeline is audited (normalize diagnosis, deduplicate globally, backfill cached feature), run **variant-specific calibration** (Platt/isotonic) on Jan–Jul mature data.
- **Do not globally retrain:** V1/V2 relationships are stable; global retraining is unnecessary and risky if Apr/May pipeline errors persist.
- Recalibrate V3 separately because its conditional outcome has shifted. Use July (pre-severe) and mature Aug/Sep cases with fixed vintage to learn new calibration.

**Feature repair (1–2 months)**
- Add `partner_source`, `rural_distance`, `device_age`, `part_order_to_receipt_hours` (from performance-DFG), and a `supplier_delay_flag` (triggered when median DFG edge >threshold).
- Implement duplicate removal at ingestion, not post-20 Apr.
- Build cohort-version tracking: every feature must specify source-event version.

**Full retraining (after 60–90 days, when Oct/Nov labels mature)**
- **Safe training months:** Jan–Mar (clean, mature, baseline); May–Jul (clean after patch, mature); Aug–Sep only after fixed-vintage maturity check (exclude cases <90 d old).
- **Unsafe months:** Apr (until fully recomputed and audited); Oct (until API backlog cleared and >90% mature); any month with unknown-activity >5% or missing-queue >5%.
- **Segment-specific model justified:** Yes. V2 is low-breach, stable, and remote; V1 is stable; V3 is high-breach, supplier-sensitive, and now partner-heavy. A shared model with variant indicators may work if feature richness improves, but **variant-specific calibration layers** (or separate sub-models for V3) reduce risk of masking localized concept drift.

**Decision logic:** Retrain only when (1) pipeline audit passes, (2) fixed-vintage label maturity >90% for target cohort, (3) process-DFG edge changes are captured as features, and (4) segment-specific validation shows improvement. Otherwise, recalibrate and repair.

## 5. Monitoring architecture

| Monitor | Metric / Method | Alert dependency / escalation |
|---|---|---|
| **Predictive** | Rolling 30-day AUROC, mean predicted risk; per-variant | If AUROC <0.75 **and** data-quality green  investigate concept drift; if data-quality red  do not trigger retraining |
| **Calibration** | Brier, ECE, reliability diagram; per-variant & per-source | If ECE >0.08 over 2 weeks  recalibration review; if tied to V3 only  supplier/concept check |
| **Label maturity** | % mature by cohort age; median label delay; maturity curves | **No direct retraining trigger.** If maturity <80% for month-1 cases  hold evaluation; if median delay >50 d  activate API backlog protocol |
| **Data quality** | Unknown-activity share; missing-queue rate; duplicate rate; pipeline job status | If unknown >2% or missing-queue >3%  **pipeline repair required**; freeze updates until resolved |
| **Process-variant** | Variant share %; `get_variants` counts | If V3 share changes >10pp in 1 month  process-change review; link to supplier/partner alerts |
| **Performance-DFG** | Median `Order Part  Part Received`; `Diagnosis  Remote Fix`; discovered via `discover_performance_dfg` | If part-delay >2× baseline for >7 days  supplier alert; if remote-fix >5h  routing anomaly |
| **Population** | Partner Z share; rural-distance mean; device-age mean; source mix | If Z share >20% or feature mean shifts >1 SD  population-reweighting check; add features before retraining |
| **Intervention / deployment** | Shadow mode for updated model; rollback on calibration degradation | Retraining allowed only if label-maturity gate passed, pipeline audit green, and shadow AUROC  current best segment |

**Alert dependency rule:**  
- `Label_delay_alarm`  suppresses `Retrain` alarm; activates `Fixed_vintage_backtest`.  
- `Pipeline_failure_alarm` (unknown/missing)  suppresses `Retrain`; activates `Feature_repair`.  
- `Process_drift_alarm` (DFG edge)  activates `Concept_check` and `Segment_recalibration`; retraining only if label-maturity also satisfied.

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.conversion.log import converter as log_converter
from pm4py.algo.discovery.dfg import algorithm as dfg_disc
from pm4py.algo.discovery.performance_dfg import algorithm as perf_dfg_disc

# 1. Read and normalize renamed diagnosis label
df = pd.read_csv("field_service_events.csv", parse_dates=["timestamp"])
df["activity"] = df["activity"].replace(
    {"Diagnostic Assessment Completed": "Initial Diagnosis Completed"}
)

# 2. Duplicate-handling strategy: exact duplicates removed globally;
#    audit count preserved; near-duplicates flagged for review
df_sorted = df.sort_values(["case_id", "timestamp", "activity"])
exact_dupes = df_sorted.duplicated(subset=["case_id", "timestamp", "activity"], keep="first")
df_clean = df_sorted[~exact_dupes].copy()
audit_dupes = int(exact_dupes.sum())

# 3. Format for pm4py
df_fmt = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    df_clean, case_id="case_id", activity_key="activity", timestamp_key="timestamp"
)
event_log = log_converter.apply(df_fmt)

# 4. Cohort definitions: different interpretations
#    Event-calendar cohort: events occurring in month (process dynamics)
#    Case-start cohort: cases whose first event falls in month (label maturity trajectory)
df_clean["month"] = df_clean["timestamp"].dt.to_period("M")
case_starts = df_clean.groupby("case_id")["timestamp"].min().reset_index()
case_starts["start_month"] = case_starts["timestamp"].dt.to_period("M")

monthly_summaries = {}
for m in sorted(df_clean["month"].astype(str).unique()):
    # Calendar cohort: filter log by calendar window
    cal_log = pm4py.filter_time_range(
        event_log,
        pd.Timestamp(m + "-01"),
        pd.Timestamp(m + "-01") + pd.DateOffset(months=1) - pd.Timedelta(seconds=1)
    )

    # Case-start cohort: subset cases and build sub-log
    case_ids = case_starts[case_starts["start_month"].astype(str) == m]["case_id"].tolist()
    df_start = df_clean[df_clean["case_id"].isin(case_ids)].copy()
    start_log = log_converter.apply(
        pm4py.format_dataframe(df_start, case_id="case_id", activity_key="activity", timestamp_key="timestamp")
    )

    # Process evidence (calendar cohort for dynamics; start cohort for aging)
    dfg_cal = dfg_disc.apply(cal_log)
    perf_dfg_cal = perf_dfg_disc.apply(cal_log)
    variants_cal = pm4py.get_variants(cal_log)
    # Temporal features (case-level) on calendar cohort; useful for prefix / length audits
    temporal_features = pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(cal_log)

    monthly_summaries[m] = {
        "calendar_events": len(cal_log),
        "calendar_cases": len(pm4py.get_cases(cal_log)),
        "start_cohort_cases": len(case_ids),
        "variants": variants_cal,
        "dfg_edges": len(dfg_cal),
        "performance_dfg_edges": len(perf_dfg_cal),
        "mean_events_before_diag_proxy": temporal_features.get("Event #1").mean() if not temporal_features.empty else None,
        "duplicate_rate_audited": audit_dupes / len(df),
        "unknown_activity_share": (df_clean["activity"] == "UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY").mean() if "UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY" in df_clean["activity"].values else 0.0
    }

# Return auditable summaries (no model fitting)
# Example interpretation: "2026-04" calendar cohort shows process-mapping failure;
# "2026-10" start cohort reveals long resolution paths likely censored.
print(monthly_summaries.get("2026-04"))
```

## 6. Risks of acting on the wrong diagnosis

| Failure mode | What goes wrong if wrong diagnosis chosen | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Retraining on immature October labels** | Model learns false low-breach baseline (observed 0.16 vs true ~0.30+) because long V3 cases are censored; future predictions underforecast risk; calibration collapses permanently | Enforce fixed-vintage backtest: only train on cohorts with 90% label maturity and median delay <40 d; exclude Oct and recent V3-heavy months until API backlog clears |
| **Calibrating around the April pipeline bug** | Isotonic/Platt calibration on corrupted feature prefixes masks root cause; April/May “recovery” is illusory; subsequent months inherit hidden feature errors | Mandate pipeline audit (label normalization, dedup, feature recomputation) **before** any recalibration; compare metrics on recomputed features vs original; do not calibrate on pre-patch April data |
| **Ignoring genuine supplier-driven concept drift (Aug–Sep V3)** | If attributed only to population shift or label delay, model fails to capture that part-delay now strongly predicts breach; V3 predictions remain underconfident; operational risk untracked | Monitor performance-DFG independently; if `Order Part  Part Received` median exceeds 2× baseline, add supplier-delay feature and perform stratified recalibration for V3; do not rely solely on global calibration |
| **Attributing Partner Z to unfair model behavior without stratified evidence** | Unfairness claim triggers unnecessary model removal/restriction; actual issue is missing rural/device features and higher V3 routing; removal harms service to rural customers | Conduct transport/reweighting checks; compare AUROC/Brier by source **and** variant; if performance gap disappears after adding features / controlling for V3, treat as population/feature shift, not bias; add features rather than disable predictions |
| **Treating June routing change as concept drift** | Overcorrecting for a healthy V2 increase could deflate predictions for low-risk remote cases, leading to over-alerts and wasted intervention resources | Separate process-change monitors from outcome monitors; verify V2 breach rate stayed ~0.10; if stable, update mix expectations but do not alter V2 model architecture |

**Cross-cutting safeguard:** Require **stratified evidence** (variant × source × maturity) for any drift label before acting. No global “retrain” decision is permitted until the pipeline audit is green, label maturity gates pass, and segment-specific validation confirms improvement over the best stable baseline (Jan–Mar).
