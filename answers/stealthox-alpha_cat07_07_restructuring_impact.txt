# 4. Risks invisible in average cycle time

1. **Tail latency.** Near >0.85, mean waits grow slowly while p95/p99 explode. A stable mean can coexist with a collapsing tail. Expose with per-edge and per-stratum p90/p99, and WIP trends (WIP rises weeks before the mean moves).
2. **High-complexity starvation.** Routing rules send exactly the hardest cases into the most loaded queue. Their *conditional* wait grows even if the fleet average improves; survivors of the old local path disappear from comparison (survivorship bias). Expose with complexity-stratified cycle-time survival curves.
3. **Local knowledge loss / context re-acquisition tax.** Missing regional context doesn't show up as more handovers — it shows up as *longer service times at the receiving end* and silent corrections. Expose with service-time inflation on TSS-touching activities and rework conditioned on `local_expertise_flag`.
4. **Fairness / Simpson's paradox.** The aggregate can improve while East improves, North/South worsen, and regional-language segments worsen most. Expose with stratified deltas and p90/p50 wait ratios per segment; never report a single blended number.
5. **Learning pipeline decay.** Juniors learn by watching local specialists handle hard cases; routing hard cases away removes the curriculum. Effects surface 3–9 months later as longer ramp-up, falling `local_expertise_flag`, and rising referral imprecision — and exhibit hysteresis (cheap to prevent, expensive to rebuild).
6. **Accountability diffusion.** Shared approval splits decision rights; work parks in gaps nobody owns. Invisible in averages as "waiting." Expose with orphan-waiting share, ping-pong edges, and `handover_reason`="clarification" frequency.
7. **Exception-handling degradation.** Standardized queues optimize the modal case; exceptions bounce between local and central actors, generating new rare variants with long waits. Expose with variant-frequency vs cycle-time correlation and new-variant detection.
8. **Common-mode resilience failure.** Today a local specialist's absence degrades one region; tomorrow a TSS outage, a faulty routing-rule deployment, or one key-person resignation degrades all regions simultaneously. Expose with absence-shock replay: drop top-k resources, recompute wait elasticity.
9. **Measurement confounding.** Pre/post raw comparisons are contaminated by mix shift (routing changes *which* cases go where), seasonality, and backlog flush. An "improvement" can be pure composition effect. Expose with segmented difference-in-differences, never raw averages.

# 5. Pilot design

**Preconditions before any cutover (non-negotiable):**
- Case-note schema standardized across locations (the documented cross-team failure cause).
- Priority policy codified and machine-checkable in the shared queues.
- TSS capacity sized so projected   0.80 at *peak*, including absorbed QC and legal/risk triage load.
- Carve-out list agreed: regional/language-accommodation cases, legal deadline-critical cases.
- RACI published for preparer / advisor / approver, including SLA ownership.
- Instrumentation live: all Section 3 leading indicators on dashboards.

**Design: shadow phase + stepped-wedge rollout (each team is its own control; teams awaiting cutover serve as concurrent controls).**

- **Phase 0 (weeks 8 to 0):** Baseline instrumentation; freeze all other process changes; capture 8–12 weeks of pre-data spanning month-end/seasonal spikes.
- **Phase 1 (weeks 0–4): Shadow mode.** TSS produces advice/approvals in parallel with no operational dependency. Measures: agreement rate with local decisions, latent wait times under simulated load, triage precision. Zero customer risk.
- **Phase 2 (weeks 4–12): Limited cutover.** High-complexity technical reviews only, for **Team East + Team South**. Rationale: East is the strongest hypothesized beneficiary (tests the upside honestly); South is mid-range; **North remains control**. Approval centralization deliberately *not* included — separating the two interventions preserves attributability.
- **Phase 3 (weeks 12–20):** Extend to North if guardrails green; introduce shared approval for medium/high-value in pilot teams only.
- **Phase 4:** Remaining scope only after 8 consecutive clean weeks.

**Comparison group & windows:** Difference-in-differences on case-level outcomes; each team's pre-period as self-control; North and non-cutover case types as concurrent controls; case-mix weighting for comparability. Checkpoints at weeks 2, 6, 12 after each cutover. **No lagging-indicator conclusions before week 12.**

**Mandatory segmentation of every report:** team × complexity (low/med/high) × required_specialty (legal/risk/security/technical/none) × customer_segment × priority.

**Guardrails and triggers:**

| Guardrail | Trigger | Action |
|---|---|---|
| TSS utilization  | >0.85 for 2 weeks | Add capacity / pause expansion |
| Edge wait median (any shared edge) | > plan +25% for 2 weeks | Triage/routing recalibration |
| Rework, any stratum | +2pp for 4 weeks | Carve-out review for that stratum |
| High-complexity p90 cycle time | +15% vs baseline | Pause expansion |
| SLA miss rate | +2pp any stratum | Pause expansion |
| `local_expertise_flag` | 20% relative | Launch rotation/mentorship countermeasure |
| CSAT any region | 0.3 | Regional carve-out |
| Senior attrition signal | Any TSS/senior-local exit citing queue | Executive review |

**Staff feedback:** weekly 5-item frontline pulse tagged by team; biweekly rotating focus groups; optional case-linked friction tag written into `handover_reason`; specialist workload-fairness survey; escalation-log review. Qualitative codes feed adjustment decisions alongside the log metrics.

**Rollback ladder (pre-agreed; reverting is not failure):** tune routing thresholds  temporary capacity add  reinstate part-time embedded liaison specialists (hybrid model)  revert the case-type slice to local handling  full revert. Each rung has a named owner and a 1-week decision SLA.

# 6. Recommendation

**Modify, then pilot first (stepped-wedge with shadow phase); do not approve full rollout now.**

The plan contains a real, evidence-backed upside — East is demonstrably overloaded (E_Tech 82%, worst p90, highest rework), and specialist workload leveling is a genuine fairness gain. But management's own routing table contradicts the headline claim: handover *events* rise (343359/100), managed-path waits rise (+2.2h), and the scarcest resource (Legal/Risk, 91–93% utilized) receives +33% volume. The plan is plausible only under specific modifications: capacity sized to 0.80, case-note standardization first, regional carve-outs, hybrid retention of part-time local liaisons, and sequencing approval centralization *last*.

**Conditional commitments:**
- **Proceed to next phase** only if: 6 consecutive weeks of green leading indicators (0.85, edge medians within plan+25%, no rework slope, stable `local_expertise_flag`) **and** 12-week lagging non-inferiority in every stratum **and** East improves without North/South breaching tolerance.
- **Modify (amber)** per the guardrail table: add capacity, carve out strata, reinstate liaisons, delay the approval phase, recalibrate triage.
- **Roll back (red)** on guardrail breaches; pre-agree this publicly so teams report problems early.

**What management must NOT infer from early averages:**
1. A better mean does not mean better complex-case outcomes — inspect strata and tails.
2. Stable early waits  steady state: WIP accumulates first (Little's law); judge at 8–12 weeks, not week 2.
3. Falling specialist utilization or referral rate may be *success* (unnecessary referrals eliminated), not idleness — verify quality before celebrating or condemning.
4. An early rework dip may be backlog flush or mix shift, not quality improvement.
5. East's early win can invert at pool saturation; North/South losses arrive late (expertise decay is slow-onset).
6. Complaint silence  health — escalation suppression is itself a warning sign; watch passive indicators.
7. Raw pre/post differences are confounded; only segmented, difference-in-differences reads count.
8. Hitting the utilization target  achieving the goal; utilization is an input, outcomes are the goal.

# 7. PM4Py analysis sketch

```python
import numpy as np
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

LOG_FILE = "event_log.csv"
PRE  = ("2024-01-01", "2024-03-31")   # baseline (match seasonality)
POST = ("2024-07-01", "2024-09-30")   # post-cutover
SHARED_ROLES = {"Technical_Shared_Service", "Shared_Approval_Queue",
                "Legal_Risk_Shared_Queue"}
SLA_DAYS = {"high": 3, "medium": 7, "low": 14}

# ---------- 0. Load & format ----------
df = pd.read_csv(LOG_FILE)
df["time:timestamp"] = pd.to_datetime(df["time:timestamp"])
df = pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id="case:concept:name",
                            activity_key="concept:name",
                            timestamp_key="time:timestamp")

def window(d, span):
    return d[(d["time:timestamp"] >= span[0]) & (d["time:timestamp"] < span[1])]

def subset(d, team=None, complexity=None, specialty=None, segment=None):
    """Case-level filtering via pm4py (keeps whole cases)."""
    if team:       d = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(d, "team", team, level="case", retain=True)
    if complexity: d = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(d, "case_complexity", complexity, level="case", retain=True)
    if specialty:  d = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(d, "required_specialty", specialty, level="case", retain=True)
    if segment:    d = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(d, "customer_segment", segment, level="case", retain=True)
    return d

pre_df, post_df = window(df, PRE), window(df, POST)

# ---------- 1. Consecutive-event pairs: waits, rework-after-handover ----------
def consecutive_pairs(d):
    d = d.sort_values(["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp"]).copy()
    nxt = d.groupby("case:concept:name").shift(-1)
    p = pd.DataFrame({
        "case": d["case:concept:name"],
        "t_from": d["time:timestamp"],
        "act_from": d["concept:name"], "act_to": nxt["concept:name"],
        "res_from": d["org:resource"], "res_to": nxt["org:resource"],
        "team_from": d["team"], "team_to": nxt["team"],
        "complexity": d["case_complexity"], "segment": d["customer_segment"],
        "priority": d["priority"],
        "wait_h": (nxt["time:timestamp"] - d["time:timestamp"]).dt.total_seconds() / 3600,
        "rework_next": nxt["rework_flag"],
    }).dropna(subset=["act_to"])
    return p

def edge_stats(pairs, by=("act_from", "act_to")):
    """Median/p90 wait + rework-after per handover edge -> leading indicator #3."""
    return (pairs.groupby(list(by))
                 .agg(n=("wait_h", "size"),
                      wait_p50=("wait_h", "median"),
                      wait_p90=("wait_h", lambda s: s.quantile(0.9)),
                      rework_after=("rework_next", "mean"))
                 .reset_index().sort_values("n", ascending=False))

pre_pairs, post_pairs = consecutive_pairs(pre_df), consecutive_pairs(post_df)
print(edge_stats(pre_pairs).head(12))    # baseline edge profile
print(edge_stats(post_pairs).head(12))   # compare vs plan table (41/4.0h, 49/3.0h, 12/10.5h...)

# ---------- 2. Handover-of-work network: pre/post edge diff ----------
def handover(d):
    log = pm4py.convert_to_event_log(d)
    return pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(log)   # (nodes, edges)

_, he_pre  = handover(pre_df)
_, he_post = handover(post_df)
# NOTE: adjust column names to your pm4py version ('source','target','weight' assumed)
diff = he_pre.merge(he_post, on=["source", "target"], how="outer",
                    suffixes=("_pre", "_post")).fillna(0)
diff["delta"] = diff["weight_post"] - diff["weight_pre"]
print(diff.reindex(diff["delta"].abs().sort_values(ascending=False).index).head(20))
# New/disappeared edges and hub formation around SHARED_ROLES = structural shift signal

# ---------- 3. Ping-pong (accountability diffusion) ----------
def pingpong_share(pairs, hours=72):
    a = pairs[["case", "res_from", "res_to", "t_from"]]
    b = a.rename(columns={"res_from": "r2", "res_to": "r1", "t_from": "t2"})
    m = a.merge(b, on="case")
    m = m[(m.res_from == m.r1) & (m.res_to == m.r2) &
          (m.t2 > m.t_from) & (m.t2 <= m.t_from + pd.Timedelta(hours=hours))]
    return m["case"].nunique() / max(pairs["case"].nunique(), 1)
print("ping-pong pre/post:", pingpong_share(pre_pairs), pingpong_share(post_pairs))

# ---------- 4. Working-together network: intra-team cohesion ----------
res_team = df.groupby("org:resource")["team"].agg(lambda s: s.mode().iat[0])
_, we = pm4py.discover_working_together_network(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(pre_df))
we["same_team"] = we["source"].map(res_team) == we["target"].map(res_team)
print("intra-team weight share:",
      we.loc[we["same_team"], "weight"].sum() / we["weight"].sum())  # track decline

# ---------- 5. Subcontracting network: shared queue as subcontractor ----------
sn, se = pm4py.discover_subcontracting_network(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df))
print(se[se["target"].isin(SHARED_ROLES)].sort_values("weight", ascending=False).head())

# ---------- 6. Variants, drift, performance DFG ----------
var_pre  = pm4py.get_variants(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(pre_df))
var_post = pm4py.get_variants(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df))
top = lambda v, n=20: set(sorted(v, key=v.get, reverse=True)[:n])
print("top-20 variant Jaccard:", len(top(var_pre) & top(var_post)) /
      len(top(var_pre) | top(var_post)))                       # variant drift

log_hi = subset(pre_df, complexity=["high"])
vh = pm4py.get_variants(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(log_hi))
kept = [v for v, c in vh.items() if c >= 10][:5]               # dominant high-complexity paths
log_hi_f = pm4py.filter_variants(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(log_hi), kept)

dfg, sa, ea = pm4py.discover_dfg(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df))          # frequency DFG
pdfg, _, _, _ = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df))
# pdfg[(a,b)]["mean"] -> mean transition time; compare against edge_stats percentiles

# ---------- 7. Service vs waiting time, case duration, SLA ----------
svc = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(df)   # adds per-case service/waiting columns
try:                                               # per toolchain; manual fallback below
    st = pm4py.get_service_time(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df))
except AttributeError:
    st = None

case = df.groupby("case:concept:name").agg(
    start=("time:timestamp", "min"), end=("time:timestamp", "max"),
    team=("team", "first"), complexity=("case_complexity", "first"),
    segment=("customer_segment", "first"), priority=("priority", "first"))
case["dur_d"] = (case["end"] - case["start"]).dt.total_seconds() / 86400
case["sla_miss"] = case["dur_d"] > case["priority"].map(SLA_DAYS).fillna(14)
print(case.groupby(["team", "complexity"])["dur_d"]
          .quantile([0.5, 0.9, 0.99]).unstack())           # tails by stratum, never means alone
print(case.groupby(["team", "complexity"])["sla_miss"].mean())

# ---------- 8. Resource similarity, roles, rework ----------
sim_pre  = pm4py.discover_activity_based_resource_similarity(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(pre_df))
sim_post = pm4py.discover_activity_based_resource_similarity(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df))
# Rising within-role similarity = homogenization/deskilling signal
for r in pm4py.discover_organizational_roles(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df)):
    print(len(r.resources), sorted(r.activities)[:5])      # role drift vs designed RACI
print("rework pre :", pm4py.get_rework_cases_per_activity(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(pre_df)))
print("rework post:", pm4py.get_rework_cases_per_activity(pm4py.convert_to_event_log(post_df)))

# ---------- 9. Queue load (rho approximation) & priority discipline ----------
def queue_rho(pairs, q_res, heads, staffed_h_day, mean_svc_h):
    into = pairs[pairs["res_to"].isin(q_res)]
    days = max((into["t_from"].max() - into["t_from"].min()).total_seconds() / 86400, 1)
    lam = len(into) / days                                # arrivals/day (crude)
    return lam * mean_svc_h / (heads * staffed_h_day)     # alert if > 0.85
print("rho TSS:", queue_rho(post_pairs, {"Technical_Shared_Service"},
                            heads=6, staffed_h_day=8, mean_svc_h=1.5))

def inversion_rate(pairs):
    ent = pairs[pairs["res_to"].isin(SHARED_ROLES)][["case", "t_from", "priority"]].copy()
    ent = ent.dropna(subset=["priority"]).sort_values("t_from")
    ent["arrival_rank"] = ent.groupby(ent["t_from"].dt.date).cumcount() + 1
    ent["prio_rank"] = ent["priority"].map({"high": 1, "medium": 2, "low": 3})
    return (ent["arrival_rank"] < ent.groupby(ent["t_from"].dt.date)["prio_rank"]
            .transform("max")).mean()                     # crude FIFO-vs-priority violation proxy
print("priority inversions:", inversion_rate(pre_pairs), "->", inversion_rate(post_pairs))

# ---------- 10. Fairness ratios & absence-shock resilience ----------
def fairness_ratio(pairs):
    q = pairs.groupby("segment")["wait_h"].quantile([0.5, 0.9]).unstack()
    return (q[0.9] / q[0.5]).rename("p90_over_p50")
print(fairness_ratio(pre_pairs), "\n", fairness_ratio(post_pairs))

def absence_shock(d, k=1):
    top_res = d["org:resource"].value_counts().head(k).index
    return edge_stats(consecutive_pairs(d[~d["org:resource"].isin(top_res)]))["wait_p90"].median()
print("p90 wait under top-1 absence shock:", absence_shock(post_df, k=1))
```
