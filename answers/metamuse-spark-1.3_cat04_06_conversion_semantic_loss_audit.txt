Notes on authority:

- Positive witnesses are visible traces with tau removed.
- Negative witnesses must be rejected by replay requiring exact final-marking equality.
- INV-02 and INV-05 are absence-of-order invariants. They cannot be validated by a single positive trace; they require **both** interleavings to be accepted and are the most likely to be lost by block-structuring.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines
One pipeline is mandatory. The second is an intentional stress test that is allowed to fail.

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pipeline A — primary: `POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN` | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`  `net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl`; `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)`  `bpmn_from_net`; then `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)`  round-trip net | Direct, supported path. Petri net preserves partial-order joins, silent skip, and LOOP redo without forcing block structure. BPMN derivation is documented for Petri nets. Best chance of semantic fidelity. | PetriBPMN introduces gateways and silent routing tasks. True concurrency becomes interleaving in analysis. Visualization suggests sequence where only layout is sequential. Initial/final markings must be carried explicitly or BPMN start/end events drift. | Reject/quarantine if `convert_to_bpmn` raises, returns unserializable BPMN, round-trip net is non-workflow or unsound, or witness replay diverges. Layout success is not acceptance. |
| Pipeline B — secondary / allowed to fail: `POWL  accepting Petri net  process tree  BPMN` | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` as above; `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)` in `try/except`; if success `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)` and `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_tree)` | If it succeeded it would give a clean block-structured tree and simple BPMN with nested XOR/LOOP blocks, easy for stakeholders and for inductive-miner-style tooling. | Source is not block-structured: diamond `Assess{Legal, Verification}` with asymmetric join `PrepareVerification only`, plus concurrent Legal vs loop, is not series-parallel. Tree discovery/conversion must either fail or impose extra order / duplicate labels / collapse concurrency. High risk of `lost` or `approximated` even if it returns an object. | **Expected failure:** `convert_to_process_tree` raises `Exception`. That is a correct outcome and must be caught and interpreted, not suppressed. If it unexpectedly succeeds, it must still pass all INV checks; otherwise quarantine and do not promote to canonical. |

Primary choice: **Pipeline A.** Pipeline B is diagnostic only.

- Do not call any direct POWL-to-BPMN function. It is not in the supported interface.
- Do not assume markings are non-null. Check them.
- Do not equate “returned an object” or “saved a PNG” with fidelity. Diagnostics and witness replay in §5 are required.

| Gateway/routing-node rendering | `not tested` | `ambiguous` | `conversion failed` | `ambiguous` | Petri net has no gateways. Every BPMN `+`/ `×` / cycle arrow and every silent BPMN task is converter-introduced routing. Appearance of parallelism/exclusivity/loop is visual suggestion, not proof. See §6. |

Discussion:

- **True concurrency vs interleaving:** POWL `StrictPartialOrder` with no edge states causal independence. Petri nets preserve this as concurrency. All PM4Py trace-based checks (`discover_footprints`, `get_enabled_transitions` playout, reachability graph linearized to sequences) observe only interleavings. Passing both `Assess,Prepare` and `Prepare,Assess` shows interleaving is preserved; it does not prove true concurrency is preserved under a step semantics. Hence `approximated`, never `validated preserved` for concurrency itself.
- **Missing edge Legal–verification:** Validators tend to test presence of order; here correctness is absence. A converter that “tidies” the model by adding an order still replays each single positive trace but fails the pair. Both orders must be in the test suite.
- **Silent optional behavior:** `skip_legal` is `label=None`. Bounded exploration must hide it, avoid infinite silent cycling, and treat absence as valid. In BPMN the skip may vanish (empty sequence flow) or materialize as an unlabeled task — both confusing.
- **Binary-loop semantics:** `Operator.LOOP` with two children is do-body-then-optional-redo-body. It is not an inclusive choice nor a self-loop on one task. Misreading Correct Defect as skippable-inside-iteration or as exit task breaks INV-08/INV-09.
- **Join before Release:** This is the soundness-critical synchronization. Loss admits the catastrophic trace `Release before verification` and must trigger rejection even if the diagram “looks right.”
- **Labels, markings, gateways:** Uniqueness holds in source and should hold downstream; any duplication is drift. Markings are semantic, not decoration. Gateways are the least trustworthy artifact: they are added by conversion and disambiguated only by replay, not by shape or layout.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code
## 5.1 Scope and exhaustiveness claim
- Compares `net_from_powl` against each available round-trip net (`net_from_bpmn`, and `net_from_tree_bpmn` if Pipeline B succeeded).
- Uses soundness, footprints, reachability-graph size sanity, bounded visible-trace enumeration via `get_enabled_transitions`, and positive/negative witness replay.
- Bounded enumeration is **bounded-exhaustive only**: exhaustive up to `MAX_VISIBLE` and `MAX_CORRECT` and silent-step cap. It is **not** unbounded-exhaustive because the loop language is infinite. Passing does not prove equivalence.
- Hides transitions with `label is None`, requires `marking == fm` for completion, avoids infinite tau cycling via visited `(marking_hash, visible_tuple)` plus per-path silent budget.

```python
import pm4py
from collections import deque

MAX_VISIBLE = 10      # visible-length bound (covers Register+Assess+Prepare+Legal+ up to 2x(C,I)+Corrects+Release+Archive)
MAX_CORRECT = 2       # loop-iteration bound expressed as max Correct Defect occurrences
MAX_SILENT_PER_STEP = 4
MAX_STATES = 10000

def marking_key(marking):
    # Marking is dict Place->int; hash by sorted place names + counts
    return tuple(sorted((p.name, c) for p, c in marking.items()))

def enabled_visible_labels(net, marking):
    # Wrapper around pm4py.get_enabled_transitions hiding tau in caller
    return pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)

def bounded_visible_language(net, im, fm, max_visible=MAX_VISIBLE, max_correct=MAX_CORRECT):
    """BFS over (marking, visible_trace). Tau transitions advance marking without growing trace."""
    if im is None or fm is None:
        return set(), {"error": "null marking"}
    fm_key = marking_key(fm)
    results = set()
    queue = deque()
    queue.append((dict(im), tuple()))  # (marking dict copy semantics via pm4py objects, visible trace)
    visited = set()  # (marking_key, visible_trace) to prevent infinite tau cycling
    states = 0
    while queue and states < MAX_STATES:
        marking, vis = queue.popleft()
        states += 1
        key = (marking_key(marking), vis)
        if key in visited:
            continue
        visited.add(key)
        # Completion requires exact final-marking equality
        if marking_key(marking) == fm_key:
            results.add(vis)
            # Do not extend past final marking (finality per INV-11)
            continue
        if len(vis) > max_visible:
            continue
        try:
            enabled = enabled_visible_labels(net, marking)
        except Exception as e:
            continue
        # Budget silent exploration to avoid infinite cycling
        silent_used = 0
        for t in enabled:
            # Fire transition: pm4py semantics via manual token play using get_enabled + firing?
            # Use pm4py firing helper if available; fallback: use petri net semantics inline
            from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils import petri_utils
            try:
                new_marking = petri_utils.add_markings(marking, {})  # copy helper may vary; use dict copy
            except Exception:
                new_marking = dict(marking)
            # Manual fire: consume + produce (robust without assuming helper API)
            # Build new marking dict
            nm = dict(marking)
            # consume
            for arc in t.in_arcs:
                p = arc.source
                nm[p] = nm.get(p, 0) - 1
                if nm[p] == 0:
                    del nm[p]
            # produce
            for arc in t.out_arcs:
                p = arc.target
                nm[p] = nm.get(p, 0) + 1
            lab = t.label  # None means silent
            if lab is None:
                # hide tau; do not grow visible trace; enforce silent budget via visited + length guard
                queue.append((nm, vis))
            else:
                if lab == "Correct Defect" and vis.count("Correct Defect") >= max_correct:
                    continue
                queue.append((nm, vis + (lab,)))
    return results, {"states": states, "bounded": True}

def replay_trace(net, im, fm, visible_trace):
    """Replay a visible trace allowing tau moves. Returns (accepted_bool, reason). Requires exact fm equality."""
    if im is None or fm is None:
        return False, "null marking"
    fm_key = marking_key(fm)
    # BFS over marking x position in visible trace, with tau closure
    queue = deque([(dict(im), 0)])
    seen = set()
    silent_cap = 20
    steps = 0
    while queue and steps < 5000:
        marking, pos = queue.popleft()
        steps += 1
        sk = (marking_key(marking), pos)
        if sk in seen:
            continue
        seen.add(sk)
        if pos == len(visible_trace) and marking_key(marking) == fm_key:
            return True, "accepted with exact final marking"
        if pos == len(visible_trace):
            # Trace consumed but not in fm: try tau moves toward fm
            pass
        try:
            enabled = enabled_visible_labels(net, marking)
        except Exception as e:
            continue
        for t in enabled:
            nm = dict(marking)
            for arc in t.in_arcs:
                p = arc.source
                nm[p] = nm.get(p, 0) - 1
                if nm.get(p, 0) == 0:
                    nm.pop(p, None)
            for arc in t.out_arcs:
                p = arc.target
                nm[p] = nm.get(p, 0) + 1
            if t.label is None:
                queue.append((nm, pos))
            else:
                if pos < len(visible_trace) and t.label == visible_trace[pos]:
                    queue.append((nm, pos + 1))
    return False, "rejected (no tau-aware path with exact final marking)"

# ---------- Positive and negative witnesses (visible labels only) ----------
POSITIVES = [
    ("P1 skip+single, Assess-Prepare order", ["Register Change","Assess Risk","Prepare Evidence","Configure Trial","Inspect Trial","Release Change","Archive Change"]),
```

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review
## 6.1 Why a correct net can still be misread
Serialization and PNG export prove nothing about behavior. The following misreadings persist even when the underlying round-trip net replays correctly:

- **Parallel gateways (`+`):** The converter emits a fork after Register and a join before verification and before Release. Viewers read `+`-split as “all branches run simultaneously” and `+`-join as “wait for all,” which is approximately right for INV-04/INV-10, but the diagram cannot show **which** join waits for **which** branches, nor that Prepare does not gate Legal (INV-03). Two `+` diamonds in sequence are easily conflated into one. Layout order (Legal lane above verification lane) is mistaken for temporal order, erasing INV-05.
- **Exclusive gateways (`×`):** The optional Legal XOR appears as a `×`-split with one empty outgoing flow. An empty flow is visually indistinguishable from a missing edge or a drawing shortcut. Stakeholders may read it as “Legal always happens” (ignoring the empty path) or as “Legal never happens” (ignoring the labeled path), collapsing INV-06. The gateway shape does not state 0/1 cardinality or that double-Legal is forbidden.
- **Loop visualization:** BPMN has no single LOOP operator matching POWL `LOOP(Body,Redo)`. Converters render either a cycle edge from Inspect back through Correct to Configure, or an XOR-join/XOR-split pair around the body. Both are ambiguous: is Correct Defect the redo, an exit, or an exception? Is zero iteration allowed (while-style) or is one required (do-while, INV-09)? Can Correct repeat without Configure/Inspect (violating INV-08)? The arrow alone does not answer. A cycle drawn below the body suggests “retry on defect” but hides that Correct is forbidden first/last.
- **Silent routing nodes:** Petri silent transitions become unlabeled BPMN tasks or gateway helpers. In the PNG they look like modeling noise and are deleted in “cleaned” hand edits, which silently converts an AND-join to an OR-join or removes the tau-skip, admitting `Release before verification` or forcing mandatory Legal. Because tau is hidden in traces, its deletion is invisible in happy-path demos.
- **Apparent ordering of graph layout:** Left-to-right / top-to-bottom placement is layout, not semantics. With Legal and verification unordered, any placement puts one above or before the other. Readers infer precedence from pixels. Similarly, ConfigureInspect arrow is semantic, but LegalRelease arrow placed parallel to verificationRelease arrow is misread as two alternative endings rather than a joint precondition.

## 6.2 Annotations and documentation to reduce ambiguity
- Title every BPMN export as **derivative, not authoritative**, with source hash: e.g. `BPMN derived from POWL src=<hash> via Pipeline A; do not edit; see validation report`.
- Annotate each gateway in text boxes: `+JOIN waits for Assess AND Prepare before Configure`, `+JOIN waits for (Legal OR skip) AND (verification loop) before Release`, `×SKIP means 0-or-1 Legal, never 2`.
- Annotate the loop: `do ConfigureInspect (>=1); redo via Correct Defect only between full iterations; Correct never first/last/consecutive`.
- Keep silent nodes visible but styled (dashed, stereotype `<<tau-routing, do not delete>>`) and explain that empty path = explicit skip.
- Publish the PNG together with the witness table (P1–P6 must pass, N1–N10 must be rejected) and the bound statement. State that layout proximity implies nothing.
- For stakeholder walkthroughs, show **both** interleavings (Legal-before-verification and verification-before-Legal) side by side to teach absence-of-order.

# 7. Rejection Criteria
Reject or quarantine the conversion result (do not store as canonical, do not show to stakeholders without warning) if **any** holds:

1. **Technical failure:** any required call raises or returns `None`/empty marking, BPMN fails to serialize, or round-trip `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` raises. A returned object without markings or without successful save is not acceptance.
2. **Unsound or non-workflow net:** `check_is_workflow_net` is `False` or `check_soundness` reports deadlock, livelock, dead transition, or improper completion. Unsoundness invalidates all downstream replay.
3. **Loss of required interleaving:** either `Register,Assess,Prepare` or `Register,Prepare,Assess` is rejected, or either Legal-before-verification or verification-before-Legal positive witness is rejected. This signals collapse of INV-02 or INV-05 into spurious sequence.
4. **Admission of release before verification or zero-iteration bypass:** negative witnesses N6 (`no Configure/Inspect`) or N7 (`Release inside/before verification`) are admitted, or bounded difference `candidate  base` contains a trace with Release but no Configure/Inspect. This is a safety violation of INV-09/INV-10.
5. **Collapse of optional behavior:** Legal-absent positive (P1/P2) is rejected (Legal became mandatory) or double-Legal negative (N9) is admitted, or Legal-present positive (P3/P4) is rejected (Legal became impossible). Either direction breaks INV-06.
6. **Loop-scope distortion:** N4 (Inspect before Configure), N5 (Correct first), or N10 (trailing Correct) is admitted, or P5 (two iterations via Correct) is rejected. Indicates redo/exit confusion in INV-07/INV-08.
7. **Failed exact-final-marking test:** a trace is counted as complete without `marking == fm` (e.g. token left on Legal branch, extra token after Archive, or Archive without Release). Near-completion is not completion; quarantine on any mismatch.
8. **Label/branch drift on round trip:** transition-label multiset differs (duplicated or dropped activity labels), silent-task count explodes, or footprint `sequence`/`parallel` relations for the pairs (Assess,Prepare) and (Legal,Configure/Inspect) flip. Visual similarity without footprint/witness agreement is rejected.

Any single criterion triggers quarantine. Pipeline B’s expected `conversion failed` is itself grounds to forbid the tree intermediate as canonical (see §8), not grounds to patch the source to fit the tree.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation
## 8.1 Authoritative vs derivative
- **Authoritative for storage: the source POWL (`source_powl`).** It alone expresses asymmetric partial order (`PrepareVerification` but not `PrepareLegal`), absence of order (Legal  verification), XOR-with-tau optionality, and binary LOOP redo without forcing block structure or introducing routing nodes. Store the POWL code/text plus version hash as the single source of truth.
- **Secondary analytic canonical (optional): the accepting Petri net `net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl` from Pipeline A.** It is the executable formalization used for soundness, footprints, reachability, and replay. Store it with its explicit `im/fm`, but mark it derived: any divergence between POWL and Petri net is resolved in favor of POWL after audit.
- **Derivative for stakeholder visualization only: `bpmn_from_net`.** BPMN is a rendering with converter-added gateways and silent tasks. It must never be edited as a model and never be used as input to further mining or code generation without re-derivation.
- **All PNGs and round-trip nets (`net_from_bpmn`, tree artifacts if any) are ephemeral evidence**, not releases.

## 8.2 Validation evidence that must accompany each derivative
- BPMN release bundle: source POWL hash, converter versions, `is_workflow_net` + `check_soundness` outputs for both original and round-trip nets, footprint diff summary, bounded-language diff (`basecandidate`, `candidatebase` under stated bound), full P1–P6/N1–N10 replay log with exact-fm requirement, and the exhaustiveness disclaimer from §5.
- If any rejection criterion fires, ship the bundle as `QUARANTINED` with the failing witness highlighted; do not silently substitute the diagram.

## 8.3 Whether a process-tree intermediate should be allowed
- **No for canonical or production use.** The source partial order is not block-structured; `convert_to_process_tree` is expected to raise, and any success would have to sequentialize, duplicate labels, or drop concurrency to fit `SEQUENCE/PARALLEL/XOR/LOOP` nesting. Allow Pipeline B only as a diagnostic that documents non-block-structure. If a future tree conversion ever succeeds, it enters quarantine until it passes the full §5 suite and a manual INV-02/INV-05 review — and even then it remains derivative, never authoritative.

## 8.4 Avoiding round-trip drift
- Edit only POWL; regenerate Petri net and BPMN forward. Never edit BPMN or Petri net and convert backward as a change workflow.
- Pin `pm4py` version, record hashes of `source_powl`, `net_from_powl`, and `bpmn_from_net` on every release; reject any artifact whose hash chain breaks.
- Require the §5 suite to pass on regeneration; treat gateway/layout changes as semantic changes until proven otherwise by replay.
- Keep silent transitions explicit and protected (`do not delete tau` lint); review any diff that adds/removes gateways, merges joins, or alters loop cardinality as a semantic diff, not a cosmetic one.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty
Even if every soundness check, footprint comparison, bounded enumeration, and P/N witness passes, the following remain unestablished:

1. **True concurrency and timing/overlap:** Sequential replay cannot prove that Legal Review and verification can truly overlap in time, share resources safely, or remain independent under data, role, or system constraints. It proves both interleavings are accepted, not that parallel execution is race-free or performant. Step semantics, resource conflicts, and duration are outside the tested formalism.
2. **Unbounded loop behavior:** Validation is bounded (`MAX_VISIBLE`, `MAX_CORRECT`, silent budget, finite reachability sample). It cannot establish equivalence for arbitrarily many iterations, fairness/livelock-freedom under infinite redo, or that an implementation will not admit a third Correct, a starved exit, or an overflow after the bound. The infinite language is never exhaustively compared.
3. **Converter generality and BPMN execution semantics:** Passing on this model does not prove the converter is correct in general, that a different BPMN engine will enforce the same join/exit semantics as the Petri replay, or that visual interpretation by stakeholders matches formal semantics. Gateway dialect differences, start/end-event handling, silent-task optimization, and data-based branching in deployment can all diverge from the audited net.
4. **Identity, data, and intent beyond labels:** Label equality is not instance identity. Tests cannot show that two `Configure Trial` occurrences refer to the same trial object, that `Legal Review` approval actually gates release in the real process, that hidden data guards do not add preconditions, or that the POWL source itself correctly captures the intended real-world policy — validation checks derivatives against POWL, not POWL against reality.
