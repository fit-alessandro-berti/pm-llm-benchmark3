## 8. Falsification and additional data

**Observations weakening top-ranked hypothesis (Batch synchronization):**
1. OVEN-1 maintenance log shows scheduled downtime 08:00–12:00, explaining idle period independently of batch policy.
2. J1/J2 readiness timestamps are artificially delayed by upstream ERP release rules, not actual component availability.
3. RACK-7 cleaning protocol requires 4 hours post-use; B2 delay is purely tool-driven, not batch-driven.
4. Batch thermal profile mandates minimum 3-job load for cure quality; partial batches are physically invalid.
5. Supervisor override for J2 was standard practice; lot quarantine did not actually block processing.
6. S1 dispatch time is fixed by carrier pickup schedule (19:00 daily), independent of job completion.

**Additional fields/event types to discriminate hypotheses:**
1. Explicit `WaitForResource` events with resource type and queue position.
2. Tool availability status timestamps (free/occupied/calibrating) separate from job events.
3. Lot quality test results and quarantine reason codes.
4. Shipment customer SLA constraints and carrier pickup windows.
5. Batch size policy rules and thermal efficiency thresholds.
6. Machine maintenance windows and tool cleaning/quarantine logs with start/end timestamps.
