## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method
- GPIP-4 is the highest-level normative source with the most explicit authority regarding procurement controls. However, it can be overridden by more specific, local or incident-specific directives if they are more narrowly tailored and explicitly referenced.
- Emergency directives (ECD) are presumed to be the most authoritative during their effective window, unless overridden by a later, more specific directive or a formal exception.
- The ERP timestamp is a secondary source of evidence; telephone recordings, signed PDFs, and access logs are considered more reliable.
- Lack of a formal written exception does not automatically nullify a compliance breach; the burden of proof rests on demonstrating that the exception existed and was properly applied.
- When multiple normative sources conflict, the most restrictive control (i.e., the one requiring the most approvals or approvals from higher authorities) should be applied.

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

### P-501
**Alternative Interpretation:** If the CFO email (D) were interpreted as granting the CFO authority to waive all Finance approvals for the duration of ECD-2, the primary classification could shift to JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.
**Reasoning:** The CFO's explicit authority to interpret Finance controls (D1) could override R8, allowing Finance_Approve to be bypassed. However, this interpretation is weak because D does not explicitly waive Finance controls and the CFO's role is not defined as a budget owner.

### P-502
**Alternative Interpretation:** If ECD-2 were interpreted to implicitly grant the authority to use new suppliers (C2) without requiring joint authorization, the primary classification could shift to JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.
**Reasoning:** The ambiguity in ECD-2's scope (C2) could be expanded to include new supplier authorization without joint authorization. However, this interpretation is credible only if the incident directive is considered broader than its explicit wording.

### P-503
**Alternative Interpretation:** If the CFO email (D) were interpreted as granting the CFO authority to waive the EUR 100,000 threshold for purchases during ECD-2, the primary classification could shift to JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.
**Reasoning:** D1 suggests that joint Incident Command authorization is sufficient for all operational approvals up to EUR 150,000. This interpretation is credible if D1 is applied broadly to override the EUR 100,000 threshold.

### P-504
**Alternative Interpretation:** If SHIFT-2's unilateral commitment (R2) were interpreted as permissible under ECD-2's C2, the primary classification could shift to JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.
**Reasoning:** ECD-2's C2 allows joint authorization by Incident Command members. If SHIFT-2 is considered an authorized member, then R2 could be justified. However, this interpretation is weak because SHIFT-2's authority is not explicitly defined in ECD-2.

### P-507
**Alternative Interpretation:** If the CFO email (D) were interpreted as granting the CFO authority to waive the EUR 100,000 threshold for unapproved suppliers during ECD-2, the primary classification could shift to JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.
**Reasoning:** D1 indicates that joint Incident Command authorization is sufficient for all operational approvals up to EUR 150,000. This interpretation is credible if D1 is applied broadly to include unapproved suppliers.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Proposed Norm Hierarchy:**
1. **Incident Directive (ECD)** - Highest authority during its effective window, overriding GPIP-4 and Aachen SOP unless explicitly superseded.
2. **CFO Email (D)** - Provides interpretation authority for Finance controls during ECD-2, but not waiver authority.
3. **GPIP-4 Board Policy** - Sets baseline procurement controls, with specific exceptions allowed by C1, C2, and D.
4. **Aachen SOP DE-AAC-PROC-17** - Local operating procedures, with precedence over GPIP-4 only when explicitly referenced.

**Proposed Exception Clause:**
- **Exception Clause:** During an active ECD-2, the following controls may be temporarily waived or overridden:
  - **Supplier Commitment (R5):** Joint Incident Command authorization may precede Create_PO, overriding GPIP-4 R2 and Aachen SOP R1.
  - **Supplier Registration (R3):** Joint Incident Command authorization may waive supplier registration with GPIP-4 R3.
  - **Finance Approval (R8):** CFO email (D1) may waive Finance segregation of duties for purchases up to EUR 150,000, and CFO + Legal clearance may waive for > EUR 150,000.
  - **Compliance Clearance (R3):** New suppliers not on the approved register may be used with joint Incident Command authorization.
  - **Emergency Waiver (A7):** The Chief Risk Officer’s exception may be issued retrospectively to justify any missed approvals.

**Safeguard Against Emergency Normalization:**
- **Audit Trail Requirement:** All waivers or overrides must be documented within 4 hours of the exception issuance and retained for 12 months. Any deviation from the standard approval process must be reviewed by the Chief Risk Officer within 30 days of the incident conclusion.
