## 1. Executive causal assessment

**Provisional verdict: `mainly confounded`; confidence: 70/100.** This confidence concerns the explanation of the crude gap, not a claim that reassignment will be beneficial. The strongest supporting evidence is a reversal: Orion is 6.71 hours slower overall, yet faster within every recorded complexity stratum. Weighting the stratum means by the pooled complexity distribution gives Orion 17.14 hours versus Nova 19.65. Orion also handles substantially more high-complexity, night, high-load, and compliance-required work. The strongest counterevidence is that the stratified estimates use tiny, poorly overlapping cells, do not resolve residual difficulty or operating conditions, and include only completed cases. Selection could therefore contribute to the reversal, while genuine group-specific queues or work practices could still cause harm.

For management, the relevant **total** effect is the effect of changing the routing decision, including induced changes in queueing, service, rework, and capacity. A **direct** effect would hold specified mechanisms fixed; **mediated** effects would operate through queue wait, review service, or rework. Conditioning on those post-routing components does not identify either effect.

The 50% transfer could succeed if comparable cases genuinely obtain shorter waits in Nova and Nova has spare capacity. It could fail because the crude means provide no transferable treatment effect. It could backfire by congesting Nova, diluting difficult-case or night expertise, delaying required checks, or increasing rework and tail delays. A small-pilot benefit also need not survive scale-up.

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Eligibility, positivity, and the total assignment effect:** The population genuinely eligible for either group is unknown, so current contrasts cannot identify the effect of a broad transfer. **Most valuable design change:** randomize routing among prospectively eligible cases, log assignment and deviations, and analyze all assignments by intention to treat.

2. **Residual difficulty within observed complexity:** The routing engine may use risk information not represented by Low/Medium/High, limiting both observational adjustment and extrapolation beyond a pilot. **Most valuable additional field:** the pre-assignment routing difficulty/eligibility score generated before group selection.

3. **Queue mechanisms and capacity feedback:** Aggregate durations cannot locate the decisive queue or predict whether a small-pilot result survives a 50% transfer and its congestion externalities. **Most valuable design change:** implement a timestamped operations log combining activity starts/ends with queue-state and available-capacity snapshots at each shift.

4. **Selection into completed cases:** Open or abandoned cases could change mean cycle, completion risk, and even the direction of group contrasts, with no assured direction of bias. **Most valuable design change:** establish an all-assigned-case follow-up cohort that retains open/abandoned cases and their censoring or abandonment status.

5. **Quality safety:** Two coarse 30-day outcomes cannot exclude meaningful harm, particularly under changed reviewer mix or increased rework, and appeals may reflect post-process factors. **Most valuable design change:** prespecify an independently adjudicated quality outcome at fixed follow-up for every pilot assignment, regardless of completion status.
