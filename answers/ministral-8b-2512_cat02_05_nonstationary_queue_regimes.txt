# Process-Mining Benchmark Analysis: Service Center Operating Regimes

## 1. Data Audit and Estimands

### Queueing Quantities and Units
- **Arrival intensity ()**: Cases per hour during 07:00–19:00 (counts/day ÷ 12 hours)
- **Completion intensity ()**: Cases completed per hour during 07:00–19:00 (counts/day ÷ 12 hours)
- **Service time**: Median (25th percentile) and p90 (90th percentile) in minutes, excluding waiting
- **WIP (mean)**: Mean number of cases in system from 15-minute snapshots (not directly convertible to queue length)
- **Capacity**: Full-time-equivalent (FTE) staff availability (6.0 FTE = 6 staff × 8 hours = 48 staff-hours/day)
- **Complexity**: Percentage of arrivals following multi-step variants (potential proxy for case-mix)

### Opening-Hours Convention
All metrics are aggregated over the 12-hour window 07:00–19:00. Overnight carry-over is possible but unsampled.

### Limitations of Aggregates
1. **No initial backlog**: Missing 07:00 queue lengths prevent accurate WIP/throughput linkage.
2. **Service-time  flow-time**: Median/p90 service times exclude waiting, violating Little’s Law assumptions.
3. **Staffing FTEs are averages**: Do not reflect intraday staffing patterns or overtime.
4. **Outage on 2026-04-15**: 180 minutes of unlogged downtime obscures true capacity.
5. **Case-mix ambiguity**: `complex_pct` may reflect real demand shifts or coding changes.

## 7. Operational Implications

### Staffing Recommendations
1. **Preventive Staffing**:
   - **Weekdays**: Maintain 6.0 FTEs during 08:00–12:00 (peak `peak_08_10_pct`).
   - **Weekends**: Reduce to 2.5 FTEs (aligned with current capacity).
   - *Risk*: Pooled averages suggest 5.2 FTEs suffice, but segmented view shows 6.0 FTEs are needed post-intervention.

2. **Backlog Recovery**:
   - **2026-04-15 Outage**: Add 2–3 temporary staff for 12 hours post-outage to clear backlog.
   - *Pooled mislead*: Average FTE=5.2 suggests no action; segmented view shows  < .

   - *Evidence*: 2026-04-21 shows 34 arrivals at 08:00–09:00; completions lag by 2 hours.

### Data Collection Recommendations

     - `pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` (intraday throughput).
