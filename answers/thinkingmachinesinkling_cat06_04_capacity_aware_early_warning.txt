## 1. Decision rule

**Hard eligibility gate**
- Stage must be `Triage` or `Diagnosis Started`. Once `Diagnosis Complete` is reached (I-03), the swarm is ineligible regardless of risk.
- Feasibility flag: if time-to-deadline < 1.5 h, evaluation proceeds but is down-weighted; if < 1.0 h, allocation is restricted to supervisor exception only.

**Calibration adjustment (segment-specific)**
- SME: `p_adj = min(1, p_now + 0.065)` (midpoint of 0.04–0.09 underprediction).
- Strategic Enterprise: `p_adj = max(0, p_now – 0.085)` (midpoint of 0.05–0.12 overprediction).
- Public Sector: `p_adj = p_now`.

**Alert persistence and material-change test**
- First alert in stage: evaluate immediately.
- Repeat alert within 90 min with `|p| < 0.10`, no stage change, no new document/upload, and no resolved imputation error  **suppress**; do not allocate.
- Material change (breaks suppression): new customer log, feature correction (e.g., I-06 queue data restored), stage transition, or documented root-cause shift.

**Net Expected Intervention Value (NEIV)**
```
NEIV_mid = [ (r_low + r_high)/2 × V – €450 – F ] × confidence_factor
```
- `confidence_factor`: 1.0 (complete data, calibrated segment), 0.85 (imputed feature), 0.75 (Non-Native or small sample).
- Rank by `NEIV_mid`. Allocate slots to Tier 1 (`NEIV_mid` > €2,500 and stage eligible). Tier 2 (€800–€2,500) is waitlisted. Tier 3 (`NEIV_upper < 0` or suppressed alert) is blocked.

**Spillover and queue constraint**
- Each used slot harms six untreated diagnosis-stage cases (`+0.01` to `+0.03` breach probability, base `~+0.02`).
- Cap active allocations at **3 of 4 slots** when the untreated diagnosis-stage pool is 3 cases, preserving one slot for regulatory/emergency arrival (management preference).
- If queue-delay index exceeds 20 min aggregate, freeze non-critical allocations.

**Non-intervention conditions**
- Ineligible stage; suppressed alert without material change; `NEIV_upper < €0` after false-alarm burden; unresolved critical data failure (unless corrected).
