2. **Missing parts / Customer not ready (Operational) - Rank 2:** Highly plausible for C01's "initial backlog" status. Alters diagnosis: Changes QC from a complex hybrid (initially looking like SPT/EDF) to a perfectly strict FIFO queue.
3. **Delayed arrival scans (Data quality) - Rank 3:** Plausible given the prompt's hint ("arrival scans can be delayed by up to two minutes"). B05 arrived at 09:46 but might not have been visible until just after the 09:48 decision. Alters diagnosis: Resolves the B03/B05 contradiction in QB, solidifying the Priority 1 > FIFO rule.

The dispatcher might have panicked about B03 waiting 28 minutes and manually intervened. Alters diagnosis: Suggests QB is not fully automated and relies on manual SLA management.
