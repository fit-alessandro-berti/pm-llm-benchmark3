One hidden Orion High case at 60 h gives (189.4+60)/7 = 35.6 vs 35.0, which reverses the High gap. | No direct evidence of truncation or abandonment. | Snapshot of open and abandoned cases with age. Censoring-aware analysis (Kaplan-Meier or cumulative incidence) by group and complexity. Tipping-point analysis. Audit of the wait definition. | If open cases concentrate in Orion night High, the survival curves cross and the stratified advantage disappears. If open cases are balanced, the contrasts hold. |
| 7 | **H7 Genuine harmful Orion effect** | Scarce certified reviewers centralise compliance and hard work, causing batching, night handoffs and thoroughness-driven Request Info loops. | Crude +6.7 h. Orion rework is crude-higher. High-stratum Orion wait is 19.5 h. The only Orion appeal had rework. | All strata negative. Service is not higher. Harm would need Nova's within-stratum cases to be harder, which is the opposite of stated routing. | Sensitivity analysis: Orion cases would have to be  2.5 h easier within strata to flip the sign. Randomized pilot in Medium cases with a pre-set harm margin. | The pilot Orion-minus-Nova difference is  +1 h with a CI excluding 0. Otherwise H7 is falsified for Low and Medium cases. |

## 6. Intervention and learning plan

| phase | intervention | target mechanism | assignment unit | safeguards | process-mining measurements | causal learning value | stop or rollback rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Instrument and reconstruct (weeks 0–4) | No routing change. Log routing inputs and reason codes, lifecycle timestamps (assigned, start, complete, compliance), reviewer_id, on-duty roster and open cases. Pre-register estimands. | Measurement: observe queue state, open cases and routing | None (observational, all cases) | Data governance. Pre-specified non-inferiority margins. | Queue length at routing, hour-block, activity-level waits, variant and DFG conformance, open-case ages | Fixes positivity and selection blind spots. Tests H1, H3 and H6 without operational risk. | If logging is not live by week 4, do not start the routing pilot. |
| 1. Queue and compliance capacity pilot (weeks 4–12) | Add targeted night or compliance-check capacity (flex reviewers or a dedicated compliance slot). The group label and routing rule stay fixed. | queue_wait and compliance waiting (about 58% of cycle time) | Shift-block (day × shift × week), stepped-wedge | Compliance step never skipped. Certified oversight on High cases. Routing rule frozen. | Wait by activity, utilisation, compliance duration, DFG loops, cycle by complexity | Separates queue and capacity effects from the Orion label. Informs H2, H3 and H5. | Stop if High-case median wait rises > 20% over baseline for 2 weeks, or any required compliance check is skipped. |
| 2. Randomized routing pilot (weeks 8–20) | Randomly divert 10–15% of eligible Orion-bound Low and Medium cases to Nova (1:1), blocked by complexity × shift × arrival_load. Randomly send some Nova-bound day cases to Orion. Keep High cases with Orion. | resource_group effect at equal case mix and time | Case, with blocks. Analyse on an intention-to-treat basis. | Nova utilisation cap. Logged overrides. Second check on Medium compliance cases. Blinded quality audit. | Wait, service, rework loops, DFG, reviewer_id, 30-day appeals, blinded audit score | Identifies total effect for Low and Medium cases, builds Orion-day and Nova-night overlap, and estimates queue externality. About 30 per arm per block detects 1.5 h if SD  2 h. | Pause if Nova median wait (diverted plus native) exceeds baseline by > 25% for 3 days, or Medium rework rises  10 pp. |
| 3. Gated dose escalation (weeks 20+) | Raise the diverted share stepwise (10%25%50%) in weekly switchback blocks, for Low and Medium cases only. Consider High only after a separate pilot. | Nonlinear queue response (H4) and routing policy effects | Week-long block, randomized sequence | Pre-declared scale-up criteria. Reversion to baseline routing at any time. | Nova wait against share, service, rework, appeals, audit | Shows whether the 50% proposal is viable and where congestion starts. | Roll back to the prior share if the CI for Nova's Medium cycle difference exceeds +0.5 h or quality audit scores worsen. |

**Final recommendation.** Do not reassign 50% of Orion's cases now. The evidence shows the Orion penalty is mostly case mix, and the only lever it points to is queue wait, not the group label. Start with two inexpensive moves. First, instrument routing and queue state (Phase 0). Second, add targeted night and compliance queue capacity under a stepped-wedge design (Phase 1), which can reduce waits whichever way the label question resolves. In parallel, run a blocked randomized diversion of Low and Medium cases to Nova (Phase 2), starting at 10–15% of eligible Orion-bound cases. Keep High-complexity work with Orion, because Nova has only two observed High cases. Escalate the share only if Nova's waits stay within the pre-set margin and Medium rework does not rise, and stop at the rollback triggers. This route either confirms that Orion's advantage is real and tied to capacity or skill, in which case keep the current allocation and strengthen night staffing. Or it shows that Nova can absorb the work, and then a larger shift rests on evidence rather than on the crude mean.

MIN_CELL = 3
ORDER = ["Low", "Medium", "High"]
GROUPS = ["Orion", "Nova"]


def warn(msg):
    print(f"  WARNING: {msg}")


# ---------------------------------------------------------------- 1. load
df = pd.read_csv(io.StringIO(CSV))
df["rework_flag"] = (df["rework_count"] > 0).astype(int)
# Derived residual (cycle - wait - service): NOT a measured duration.
df["other_h"] = (df["cycle_time_h"] - df["wait_before_review_h"]
                 - df["review_service_h"]).round(2)

# ------------------------------------------------ 2a. crude comparison
print("\n=== 2a. Crude comparison ===")
AGG = dict(
    n=("case_id", "size"),
    mean_cycle=("cycle_time_h", "mean"),
    median_cycle=("cycle_time_h", "median"),
    mean_wait=("wait_before_review_h", "mean"),
    mean_service=("review_service_h", "mean"),
    mean_other=("other_h", "mean"),
    rework_pct=("rework_flag", lambda s: 100 * s.mean()),
)
crude = df.groupby("group").agg(**AGG).round(2)
print(crude)
print("Crude Orion - Nova mean cycle (h):",
      round(crude.loc["Orion", "mean_cycle"] - crude.loc["Nova", "mean_cycle"], 2))

# ------------------------------------------ 2b. complexity-stratified
print("\n=== 2b. Complexity-stratified comparison ===")
idx = pd.MultiIndex.from_product([ORDER, GROUPS], names=["complexity", "group"])
strat = df.groupby(["complexity", "group"]).agg(**AGG).round(2).reindex(idx)
print(strat)
for (c, g), row in strat.iterrows():
    if pd.isna(row["n"]) or row["n"] < MIN_CELL:
        warn(f"complexity={c}, group={g}: n={0 if pd.isna(row['n']) else int(row['n'])} "
             f"(< {MIN_CELL}); stratum contrast is poorly supported.")

# ------------------------------------ 3. complexity-standardized contrast
print("\n=== 3. Complexity-standardized Orion - Nova contrast ===")
means = (df.groupby(["complexity", "group"])["cycle_time_h"].mean()
           .unstack("group").reindex(ORDER))
diff = means["Orion"] - means["Nova"]
print("Stratum contrasts (Orion - Nova):\n", diff.round(2))


def mix(mask):
    return (df.loc[mask, "complexity"].value_counts(normalize=True)
              .reindex(ORDER).fillna(0.0))


weights = {
    "pooled complexity mix, 12/10/8 of 30 (PRIMARY)": mix(pd.Series(True, index=df.index)),
    "Orion mix (ATT-like)": mix(df["group"] == "Orion"),
    "Nova mix (ATC-like)": mix(df["group"] == "Nova"),
}
for name, w in weights.items():
    print(f"{name}: weights={w.round(3).to_dict()} -> standardized diff = "
          f"{(w * diff).sum():.2f} h")

# Stratified bootstrap (within complexity x group cells), pooled weights
rng = np.random.default_rng(42)
w_pool = list(weights.values())[0]
cells = {k: v["cycle_time_h"].to_numpy()
         for k, v in df.groupby(["complexity", "group"])}
boot = []
for _ in range(5000):
    d = 0.0
    for c in ORDER:
        o, n_ = cells[(c, "Orion")], cells[(c, "Nova")]
        d += w_pool[c] * (rng.choice(o, len(o)).mean() - rng.choice(n_, len(n_)).mean())
    boot.append(d)
print("Bootstrap 2.5/50/97.5 pct of pooled-weight contrast:",
      np.percentile(boot, [2.5, 50, 97.5]).round(2))
warn("Bootstrap intervals are optimistic: cells with n=2 (Orion-Low, Nova-High) "
     "barely vary under resampling. Standardization adjusts only for observed "
     "complexity; it does not address shift, routing on unobserved risk, "
     "interference or completed-case selection.")

# ------------------------------------ 4. overlap counts (complexity x shift x group)
print("\n=== 4. Overlap counts: complexity x shift x group ===")
d2 = df.copy()
full = pd.MultiIndex.from_product([ORDER, ["Day", "Night"], GROUPS],
                                  names=["complexity", "shift", "group"])
counts = d2.groupby(["complexity", "shift", "group"]).size().reindex(full, fill_value=0)
ov = counts.unstack("group")[GROUPS]
ov["overlap"] = ov.min(axis=1) > 0
print(ov)
for (c, s), row in ov.iterrows():
    if row["Orion"] == 0 or row["Nova"] == 0:
        warn(f"complexity={c}, shift={s}: no overlap (Orion={row['Orion']}, Nova={row['Nova']}).")
    elif min(row["Orion"], row["Nova"]) < MIN_CELL:
        warn(f"complexity={c}, shift={s}: thin overlap "
             f"(Orion={row['Orion']}, Nova={row['Nova']}; < {MIN_CELL} in a group).")

# Shift-matched (exact complexity x shift) contrast, weighted by Orion counts
m = (d2.groupby(["complexity", "shift", "group"])["cycle_time_h"].mean()
       .unstack("group")).dropna()
n_o = ov.loc[m.index, "Orion"]
att = ((m["Orion"] - m["Nova"]) * n_o).sum() / n_o.sum()
print("\nExact-cell contrasts (Orion - Nova):\n", (m["Orion"] - m["Nova"]).round(2))
print(f"Orion-weighted shift-matched contrast: {att:.2f} h "
      f"({int(n_o.sum())} of {int(ov['Orion'].sum())} Orion cases have a counterpart)")
warn("Matched comparators are single cases in several cells; treat as descriptive.")

print("\nNova-only night - day within complexity (H3 check):")
nv = d2[d2["group"] == "Nova"].groupby(["complexity", "shift"])["cycle_time_h"].agg(["mean", "size"])
print(nv.round(2))

# ------------------------------------ 5. variants and directly-follows relations
print("\n=== 5a. Trace variants by group (counts) ===")
variants = df.groupby(["trace", "group"]).size().unstack("group", fill_value=0)[GROUPS]
variants["Orion_pct"] = (100 * variants["Orion"] / variants["Orion"].sum()).round(1)
variants["Nova_pct"] = (100 * variants["Nova"] / variants["Nova"].sum()).round(1)
print(variants.sort_values("Orion", ascending=False).to_string())


def dfg_edges_per_case(traces):
    """PM4Py DFG edge frequencies per case. parse_event_log_string creates
    synthetic timestamps only to build a log; they are never used for durations."""
    traces = list(traces)
    log = pm4py.parse_event_log_string(traces, sep=">")
    dfg, _starts, _ends = pm4py.discover_dfg(log)
    return pd.Series({k: v / len(traces) for k, v in dfg.items()}, dtype=float)


def compare_dfg(sub, label):
    cols = {}
    for g in GROUPS:
        t = sub.loc[sub["group"] == g, "trace"]
        if len(t) == 0:
            warn(f"{label}, group={g}: no cases; DFG not computed.")
            cols[g] = pd.Series(dtype=float)
            continue
        if len(t) < MIN_CELL:
            warn(f"{label}, group={g}: only {len(t)} cases; DFG rates are unstable.")
        cols[g] = dfg_edges_per_case(t)
    tab = pd.concat(cols, axis=1).reindex(columns=GROUPS).fillna(0.0)
    tab["Orion_minus_Nova"] = tab["Orion"] - tab["Nova"]
    print(f"\n--- Directly-follows edges per case: {label} ---")
    print(tab.round(2).to_string())


print("\n=== 5b. PM4Py directly-follows comparison ===")
compare_dfg(df, "all cases")
for c in ORDER:
    compare_dfg(df[df["complexity"] == c], f"complexity={c}")

print("\nDiagnostics only: none of these outputs establishes a causal effect of Orion.")

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Whether Orion receives systematically harder or easier cases within complexity strata (unobserved_case_risk and routing).**
   Most valuable addition: the routing engine's logged inputs, reason codes and any difficulty score at the moment of assignment.
2. **Whether the apparent Orion edge is a group effect or a shift, load and capacity effect (Orion day n=1, night equals High load).**
   Most valuable addition: the queue length and on-duty reviewer headcount per group at each routing decision.
3. **How much of `wait_before_review_h` is true queueing versus compliance waiting or handoff time.**
   Most valuable addition: activity-level lifecycle timestamps (assigned, started, completed), including for the compliance step.
4. **Whether completed-only sampling hides long or abandoned Orion or Nova cases, and in which direction.**
   Most valuable addition: a snapshot of all cases opened in the window, with status and current age.
5. **Whether any speed difference trades off against quality, given only two appeals in 30 cases.**
   Most valuable addition: a blinded per-case audit quality score, independent of the appeal outcome.
