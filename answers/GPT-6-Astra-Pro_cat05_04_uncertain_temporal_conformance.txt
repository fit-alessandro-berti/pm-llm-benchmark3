## 1. Normalization Assumptions

**At zero SLA tolerance, the evidence supports three certain SLA violations: T-402/S1, T-408/S2, and T-408/S3.** Nine additional SLA assessments are possible violations. T-409’s two start-dependent SLA assessments remain indeterminate because its clock bound is explicitly only a weak estimate. These conclusions use the assumptions below—not exact interpretations of displayed timestamps.   

### Interval construction

Let the displayed timestamp, after selecting a valid time-zone interpretation, be \(r\). Let its resolution window be \([q_\ell,q_u]\), and let its clock offset be \([a,b]\). Then

$$
T=[r+q_\ell-b,\ r+q_u-a].
$$

Thus, **a clock that runs ahead produces an earlier estimated true event time**. Resolution uncertainty is applied before clock correction, as required. I use closed intervals and retain the supplied floor-to-minute upper endpoint of `59.999` seconds. For nearest-rounding ties, including both endpoints is conservative. 

The resulting source-level windows, relative to the UTC interpretation of the displayed value, are:

| Source                                        | True-time window relative to displayed UTC value |
| --------------------------------------------- | -----------------------------------------------: |
| HUB                                           |                          \([-1.5,+1.5]\) seconds |
| OPS-BER, ordinary diagnostic bound            |                      \([-120,+149.999]\) seconds |
| SENSOR                                        |                            \([-10,+10]\) seconds |
| VENDOR-NY                                     |                      \([-180,+239.999]\) seconds |
| LEGACY-BER, separately for each fold          |                       \([-60,+119.999]\) seconds |
| MOBILE-LON, before entry-delay interpretation |                          \([-330,+330]\) seconds |

These are **feasibility bounds, not probability distributions**. No uniformity, Gaussian timestamp error, or probability proportional to interval length is assumed.

### Time zones and clock dependence

For the supplied June timestamps, I use CEST = UTC+02:00, EDT = UTC−04:00, and BST = UTC+01:00. For T-405, the earlier Berlin fold uses UTC+02:00 and the later fold UTC+01:00, consistent with the supplied DST ambiguity. 

The baseline permits each event’s clock offset to vary anywhere within its source bound. It does **not** assume that two events from the same source share an identical offset. This is conservative, but it is not a claim of statistical independence. A constant-offset alternative is examined in Section 4.

### Durations and classifications

For unconstrained event intervals \(U=[u_\ell,u_u]\) and \(V=[v_\ell,v_u]\),

$$
V-U=[v_\ell-u_u,\ v_u-u_\ell].
$$

Where events share constraints, durations are projected from the **joint feasible model**. For example, T-407’s inferred receipt cannot be independently placed after its investigation start. Likewise, independently maximized adjacent durations must not be added to obtain an end-to-end maximum.

SLA acceptance is inclusive: \(0\le d\le L+\tau\), where \(\tau\) is an explicitly selected tolerance. The lower bound represents the adopted occurrence-order semantics, not an additional contractual deadline. A negative candidate duration is not silently accepted as an exceptionally fast response.

For an acceptance region \(B\) and nonempty feasible duration set \(D\):

$$
\begin{aligned}
D\subseteq B &\Rightarrow \texttt{CERTAIN\_PASS},\\
D\cap B=\varnothing &\Rightarrow \texttt{CERTAIN\_VIOLATION},\\
D\cap B\ne\varnothing,\quad D\nsubseteq B
&\Rightarrow \texttt{POSSIBLE\_VIOLATION}.
\end{aligned}
$$

Insufficient evidence, missing required occurrences, unbounded uncertainty, inconsistent constraints, or unresolved ordering under the chosen semantics produces `INDETERMINATE`. These definitions also apply to unions; their gaps must not be filled by taking a convex hull.

T-409’s start-dependent profiles remain indeterminate at every \(\zeta\); changing a statistical threshold does not improve timestamp evidence.

### Material semantic alternatives

**T-406: entry delay affects recording only.**
Suppose the displayed value is a retrospective occurrence-time value, and the five-minute delay concerns only when the record was entered. Then do not subtract that delay:

$$
I=[\text{2026-06-01T12:08:30Z},\ \text{2026-06-01T12:19:30Z}].
$$

This gives:

$$
A\to I=[508.5,1171.5],\qquad I\to C=[1948.5,2611.5].
$$

S2 and profile \(I\to C\) at \(\zeta=1\) become **certain passes**. S1 and profile \(A\to I\) remain possible violations. This is a substantive change in occurrence semantics, not a different numerical rounding convention.

**T-407: successful receipt is not established.**
If the latency bound applies only conditionally to successful delivery, rather than establishing that delivery occurred, S1 and both receipt-dependent profile assessments become **indeterminate**. S2 and profile \(I\to C\) remain certain passes.

If receipt existence is accepted but `receipt ≤ start` is not, the source-only receipt interval extends to `2026-06-01T14:20:01.500Z`, and the signed response-duration interval is `[-183,1023]` seconds. Under the ordering-required semantics used here, S1 and profile \(A\to I\) become indeterminate. Under a purely algebraic upper-bound interpretation of S1, it would still be possible, but that interpretation would fail to address whether this was genuinely a response to the receipt.

**T-405: ingestion order does not establish occurrence order.**
Without that assumption, the source-only \(A\to I\) duration set is:

$$
[-6479.999,-6120.001]
\ \cup\
[-2879.999,-2520.001]
\ \cup\
[720.001,1079.999].
$$

Those gaps are real. Replacing the union with one interval would invent feasible durations. Under ordering-required semantics, the unresolved fold assignments make the affected ordered-pair assessments indeterminate.

**T-405: one constant LEGACY-BER clock offset.**
If independent evidence establishes one unchanged offset across the three events, the offset cancels from same-source durations. Then:

$$
A\to I=[840.001,959.999]\text{ seconds}.
$$

S1 remains possible at zero tolerance but becomes a certain pass at two minutes. Profile \(A\to I\) becomes a **certain violation at \(\zeta=1\)** and a **certain pass at \(\zeta=2\)**. Narrower uncertainty can reveal a definite anomaly rather than always making results more favorable.

**T-409: accept the weak envelope and add occurrence order.**
As a explicitly conditional scenario, accepting the ±12-minute estimate as a hard bound and imposing \(A\le I\le C\) yields:

$$
A\to I=[0,1021.499],\qquad I\to C=[1078.501,2103].
$$

S1 is possible at 0 and 2 minutes, and a certain pass at 5 minutes. S2 is a certain pass throughout. Profile \(A\to I\) is possible at all three \(\zeta\) values; profile \(I\to C\) is possible at \(\zeta=1\) and a certain pass at \(\zeta=2,3\). These conditional conclusions do not upgrade the engineering estimate’s evidential quality.

           (A, C): (2700, 720), (C, X): (86400, 21600)}

CP, CV, PV, ID = ('CERTAIN_PASS', 'CERTAIN_VIOLATION',
                  'POSSIBLE_VIOLATION', 'INDETERMINATE')


def union(intervals):
    """Merge overlapping components only; preserve genuine gaps."""
    out = []
    for lo, hi in sorted(set(intervals)):
        if lo > hi:
            raise ValueError('Reversed interval')
        if out and lo <= out[-1][1]:
            out[-1] = (out[-1][0], max(hi, out[-1][1]))
        else:
            out.append((lo, hi))
    return out


def epoch_ms(dt):
    return pd.Timestamp(dt).value // 1_000_000


def normalize(row, mobile_delay=True):
    zone, rlo, rhi, olo, ohi = META[row.system]
    if row.case == 'T-409' and row.activity == I:
        olo, ohi = -720000, 720000  # WEAK candidate, not certified

    naive = datetime.fromisoformat(row.recorded)
    tz = ZoneInfo(zone)
    result = []

    for fold in (0, 1):
        ends = []
        for rounding in (rlo, rhi):
            local = naive + timedelta(milliseconds=rounding)
            aware = local.replace(tzinfo=tz, fold=fold)
            utc = aware.astimezone(timezone.utc)
            back = utc.astimezone(tz)

            if back.replace(tzinfo=None) != local or back.fold != fold:
                break  # nonexistent local time or redundant fold
            ends.append(epoch_ms(utc))

        if len(ends) == 2:
            lo, hi = ends[0] - ohi, ends[1] - olo
            if row.system == 'MOBILE-LON' and mobile_delay:
                lo -= 300000  # occurrence = corrected entry - delay [0,5m]
            result.append((lo, hi))

    if not result:
        raise ValueError(f'Unsupported/nonexistent local timestamp: {row}')
    return union(result)


def close_constraints(names, boxes, edges):
    """D[u][v] bounds t[v]-t[u]. Floyd-Warshall gives exact projections
    for these difference constraints; None means an inconsistent branch.

    edges: (source, target, duration_lower, duration_upper), in ms.
    """
    names = ['ZERO'] + names
    index = {name: j for j, name in enumerate(names)}
    n = len(names)
    d = [[0 if u == v else inf for v in range(n)] for u in range(n)]

    def add(u, v, lo, hi):
        u, v = index[u], index[v]
        d[u][v] = min(d[u][v], hi)
        d[v][u] = min(d[v][u], -lo)

    for name, (lo, hi) in boxes.items():
        add('ZERO', name, lo, hi)
    for edge in edges:
        add(*edge)

    for k in range(n):
        for u in range(n):
            for v in range(n):
                d[u][v] = min(d[u][v], d[u][k] + d[k][v])

    if any(d[j][j] < 0 for j in range(n)):
        return None
    return index, d


def project(branch, source, target):
    idx, d = branch
    u, v = idx[source], idx[target]
    return -d[v][u], d[u][v]


def build(mobile_delay=True, trust_ingestion=True, infer_receipt=True,
          receipt_before_start=True, accept_weak=False):
    """accept_weak also assumes A <= I <= C for T-409.

    Main mode does neither: its I-dependent findings are evidence-gated.
    """
    source_intervals, scenarios, meta = {}, {}, {}

    for case, group in raw.groupby('case', sort=True):
        boxes, edges = {}, []
        for row in group.itertuples(index=False):
            if row.activity in boxes:
                raise ValueError('Use event IDs to handle repeated activities')
            boxes[row.activity] = normalize(row, mobile_delay)
            source_intervals[(case, row.activity)] = boxes[row.activity]

        if case == 'T-404':
            boxes[I] = [(lo - 1500000, hi - 1080000)
                        for lo, hi in boxes[E]]
            edges.append((I, E, 1080000, 1500000))

        if case == 'T-405' and trust_ingestion:
            edges += [(A, I, 0, inf), (I, C, 0, inf)]

        if case == 'T-407' and infer_receipt:
            boxes[A] = [(lo, hi + 1200000) for lo, hi in boxes[S]]
            edges.append((S, A, 0, 1200000))
            if receipt_before_start:
                edges.append((A, I, 0, inf))

        if case == 'T-409' and accept_weak:
            edges += [(A, I, 0, inf), (I, C, 0, inf)]

        names, branches = list(boxes), []
        for choice in product(*(boxes[name] for name in names)):
            branch = close_constraints(
                names, dict(zip(names, choice)), edges
            )
            if branch is not None:
                branches.append(branch)

        scenarios[case] = branches
        meta[case] = {
            'names': names, 'edges': edges, 'accept_weak': accept_weak
        }

    return source_intervals, scenarios, meta


def feasible(model, case, source, target):
    return union(
        project(b, source, target) for b in model[1][case]
        if source in b[0] and target in b[0]
    )


def classify(intervals, lower, upper, evidence_ok=True, ordered=True):
    if not evidence_ok or not intervals:
        return ID
    if any(not isfinite(x) for pair in intervals for x in pair):
        return ID
    if ordered and any(lo < 0 for lo, hi in intervals):
        return ID  # order uncertainty, not an exceptionally fast pass

    inside = all(lower <= lo and hi <= upper for lo, hi in intervals)
    overlap = any(
        max(lo, lower) <= min(hi, upper) for lo, hi in intervals
    )
    return CP if inside else (PV if overlap else CV)


def finding(model, case, source, target, lower, upper):
    ranges = feasible(model, case, source, target)
    weak = (
        case == 'T-409' and I in (source, target)
        and not model[2][case]['accept_weak']
    )
    return ranges, classify(
        ranges, lower, upper, evidence_ok=not weak
    )


def seconds(ranges):
    return [(lo / 1000, hi / 1000) for lo, hi in ranges]


def iso(ms):
    return (
        pd.Timestamp(ms, unit='ms', tz='UTC')
        .isoformat().replace('+00:00', 'Z')
    )


def tables(model):
    events, findings = [], []

    for case, details in model[2].items():
        for activity in details['names']:
            ranges = feasible(model, case, 'ZERO', activity)
            events.append({
                'case': case,
                'activity': activity,
                'UTC_intervals': [(iso(lo), iso(hi)) for lo, hi in ranges],
                'weak_candidate': case == 'T-409' and activity == I,
                'inferred': (case, activity) in {('T-404', I), ('T-407', A)}
            })

        for rule, (source, target, limit) in SLAS.items():
            for tolerance in (0, 120, 300):
                ranges, status = finding(
                    model, case, source, target,
                    0, (limit + tolerance) * 1000
                )
                findings.append(dict(
                    case=case, kind='SLA', rule=rule,
                    setting=tolerance / 60,
                    seconds=seconds(ranges), status=status
                ))

        for (source, target), (mean, sd) in PROFILE.items():
            for zeta in (1, 2, 3):
                ranges, status = finding(
                    model, case, source, target,
                    (mean - zeta * sd) * 1000,
                    (mean + zeta * sd) * 1000
                )
                findings.append(dict(
                    case=case, kind='PROFILE',
                    rule=f'{source} -> {target}', setting=zeta,
                    seconds=seconds(ranges), status=status
                ))

    return pd.DataFrame(events), pd.DataFrame(findings)


def midpoint_log(model):
    """LOSSY: choose one feasible joint branch per case, then midpoints.

    Never average across disjoint DST alternatives.
    Assert joint feasibility rather than assuming midpoint compatibility.
    """
    rows = []

    for case, branches in model[1].items():
        if not branches:
            continue
        b = branches[0]  # representative branch, not a probability
        values = {'ZERO': 0}

        for activity in model[2][case]['names']:
            lo, hi = project(b, 'ZERO', activity)
            values[activity] = (lo + hi) / 2
            rows.append(dict(
                case_id=case,
                activity=activity,
                timestamp=pd.Timestamp(
                    int((lo + hi) * 500000), unit='ns', tz='UTC'
                ),
                approximation='LOSSY_MIDPOINT',
                branch=0,
                inferred=(case, activity) in {('T-404', I), ('T-407', A)},
                weak_candidate=case == 'T-409' and activity == I
            ))

        idx, d = b
        for u in idx:
            for v in idx:
                assert values[v] - values[u] <= d[idx[u]][idx[v]]

    return pd.DataFrame(rows).sort_values(
        ['case_id', 'timestamp', 'activity']
    )


def diagnostic_keys(result, case):
    """Only version-specific adapter; called on ONE case at a time.

    Known DataFrame schema: source_activity/target_activity.
    Known list schema: [[(source,target,seconds,z_score), ...]].
    Unknown schemas fail loudly rather than silently implying a pass.
    """
    if isinstance(result, pd.DataFrame):
        if result.empty:
            return set()
        required = {'source_activity', 'target_activity'}
        if not required.issubset(result.columns):
            raise RuntimeError(
                f'Adapt diagnostic_keys for columns {result.columns.tolist()}'
            )
        return {
            (case, str(r.source_activity), str(r.target_activity))
            for r in result.itertuples(index=False)
        }

    if isinstance(result, (list, tuple)) and len(result) == 1:
        out = set()
        for deviation in result[0]:
            if not isinstance(deviation, (list, tuple)) or len(deviation) < 4:
                raise RuntimeError(f'Unexpected deviation: {deviation!r}')
            out.add((case, str(deviation[0]), str(deviation[1])))
        return out

    raise RuntimeError(
        f'Unexpected PM4Py diagnostics: {type(result)} {result!r}'
    )

        import pm4py

            'PM4Py comparison NOT RUN.'

    formatted = pm4py.format_dataframe(

    formatted.attrs['timestamp_semantics'] = 'LOSSY_MIDPOINT_APPROXIMATION'
    comparison = []
    parameters = inspect.signature(
        pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile

    for zeta in (1, 2, 3):
        keys = set()
        for case, g in formatted.groupby('case:concept:name', sort=True):
            options = {'zeta': zeta}

            keys |= diagnostic_keys(result, str(case))

        for case, g in midpoint.groupby('case_id', sort=True):

                elapsed = (points[target] - points[source]).total_seconds()
                if elapsed < 0:
                    continue  # not an eventually-following midpoint pair

                expected = not (
                    mean - zeta * sd <= elapsed <= mean + zeta * sd

                if expected != actual:

                        'PM4Py semantics/schema mismatch: '

                    model, case, source, target,
                    (mean - zeta * sd) * 1000,
                    (mean + zeta * sd) * 1000

                comparison.append(dict(
                    case=case, source=source, target=target, zeta=zeta,
                    midpoint_seconds=elapsed,
                    midpoint_deviation=actual,
                    interval_seconds=seconds(ranges),
                    interval_classification=status
                ))

    print('PM4Py version:', pm4py.__version__)
    return pd.DataFrame(comparison)

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency

The minimum requirement is generally **not an exact timestamp**. It is enough to obtain trustworthy additional evidence that moves the entire remaining feasible duration set inside the acceptance region, or entirely outside it.

For a maximum \(L\), narrower independent endpoint intervals establish a pass when

$$
v_u-u_\ell\le L,
$$

and establish a violation when

$$
v_\ell-u_u>L.
$$

For a two-sided profile, both boundaries must be considered. Evidence that only removes the “too slow” possibility may leave a “too fast” possibility.

| Case and unresolved assessments                                                                  | Minimum sufficient additional evidence                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **T-401:** S1 and profile \(A\to I\)                                                             | A tighter, trustworthy relative start-time bound, from contemporaneous OPS-BER synchronization and/or unrounded capture. ## 7. Governance Recommendation

**Store occurrence evidence without erasing uncertainty.** Use UTC for normalized time storage, but retain the raw timestamp string, original time-zone identifier, recorded UTC offset, DST fold where applicable, and conversion-rule version. Where occurrence time is uncertain, retain lower/upper bounds or alternative intervals rather than publishing a midpoint as an exact event time. Keep `occurred_at`, `recorded_at`, and `ingested_at` distinct.

**Make clock provenance auditable.** Retain source clock identifiers, synchronization observations, offset sign convention, validity periods, measurement error, clock jumps, and drift assumptions. Distinguish certified worst-case bounds from engineering estimates. Record whether multiple events share a calibration interval or demonstrably constant offset; this permits justified cancellation instead of either overconfident or unnecessarily wide duration estimates.

**Preserve resolution and lifecycle semantics.** Record whether a timestamp was exact, floored, or rounded, including its resolution. Treat `Investigation_Start`, `Investigation_Complete`, and `Containment_Complete` as different lifecycle occurrences. Link inferred starts to the signed duration evidence and record whether duration means elapsed time or active work. Never overwrite the original event with an inferred event.

**Capture causal identifiers and receipt evidence.** Link send attempts, deliveries, acknowledgments, investigation instances, and incident cases using stable identifiers. Document whether ingestion order preserves occurrence order. For mobile records, record entry time separately from the claimed occurrence time and explicitly encode retrospective entry.

**Adopt an asymmetric enforcement policy.** A `CERTAIN_VIOLATION` supported by acceptable evidence should trigger formal SLA review; `POSSIBLE_VIOLATION` should trigger investigation or evidence collection, not automatic adverse enforcement. Mere overlap with the violation region is insufficient for a finding of breach, and overlap with the pass region is insufficient to certify compliance. `INDETERMINATE` should remain a visible evidence-quality status rather than being counted as a pass.

Finally, report **hard-SLA conformance, statistical-profile conformance, and evidence quality separately**. Version the selected \(\zeta\), any authorized tolerance, and the assumptions behind inferred events. For this dataset, prioritize the established T-402/S1 and T-408/S2–S3 exceedances for operational review, while directing evidence-repair work toward T-409’s missing synchronization, T-407’s receipt semantics, and T-406’s occurrence-versus-entry distinction.
