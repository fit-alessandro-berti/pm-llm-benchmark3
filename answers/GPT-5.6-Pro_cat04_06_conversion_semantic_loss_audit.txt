# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

For compact witnesses, the following aliases are used:

* `R` = Register Change
* `A` = Assess Risk
* `P` = Prepare Evidence
* `L` = Legal Review
* `C` = Configure Trial
* `I` = Inspect Trial
* `D` = Correct Defect
* `X` = Release Change
* `H` = Archive Change
* `τ` = the silent legal-skip transition

A witness is a **complete visible trace** unless stated otherwise. Hiding `τ` means that choosing the legal-skip branch contributes no visible event.

| Invariant ID | Invariant                                                                                                                                                               | Positive witness                                                                 | Negative witness                                                                                                                                       | Observable after hiding tau?                                                              |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| I-01         | `R` is the unique first visible activity and precedes every other visible activity.                                                                                     | `R,A,P,C,I,X,H`                                                                  | `A,R,P,C,I,X,H`                                                                                                                                        | Yes                                                                                       |
| I-02         | After `R`, `A` and `P` are unordered: either may occur first. This is absence of an order constraint, not an XOR choice.                                                | Both `R,A,P,C,I,X,H` and `R,P,A,C,I,X,H`                                         | A candidate that accepts only one of those two linearizations violates the invariant.                                                                  | Partly: both interleavings are visible, but traces alone do not prove true concurrency.   |
| I-03         | `L`, when selected, requires completion of `A`; it does not require completion of `P`.                                                                                  | `R,A,L,P,C,I,X,H`                                                                | `R,L,A,P,C,I,X,H`                                                                                                                                      | Yes                                                                                       |
| I-04         | Verification cannot start until both `A` and `P` have completed.                                                                                                        | `R,P,A,C,I,X,H`                                                                  | `R,A,C,P,I,X,H`                                                                                                                                        | Yes                                                                                       |
| I-05         | The optional-legal node and the verification loop are unordered once enabled. `L` may occur before, during, or after the visible verification work, but before release. | `R,A,L,P,C,I,X,H`; `R,A,P,C,L,I,X,H`; `R,A,P,C,I,L,X,H`                          | Adding either a global `L < verification` or `verification < L` constraint would reject at least one positive witness.                                 | Partly: the interleavings are visible, but literal simultaneous execution is not.         |
| I-06         | Legal review is optional through `XOR(L, τ)`: one legal review or a silent skip, never both and never repeated.                                                         | With legal: `R,A,P,L,C,I,X,H`; without legal: `R,A,P,C,I,X,H`                    | A model that requires `L`, forbids `L`, or accepts two occurrences of `L` violates the invariant.                                                      | Yes, as presence or absence of `L`; the identity of the silent branch is hidden.          |
| I-07         | Every verification iteration executes `C` before `I`.                                                                                                                   | `R,A,P,C,I,X,H`                                                                  | `R,A,P,I,C,X,H`                                                                                                                                        | Yes                                                                                       |
| I-08         | Repetition follows binary-loop semantics: `C,I,(D,C,I)*`. Every repeated iteration is preceded by exactly one `D`.                                                      | `R,A,P,C,I,D,C,I,X,H`                                                            | `R,A,P,C,I,C,I,X,H`; `R,A,P,C,I,D,X,H`                                                                                                                 | Yes                                                                                       |
| I-09         | At least one verification iteration occurs. The loop body is not optional.                                                                                              | `R,A,P,C,I,X,H`                                                                  | `R,A,P,X,H`                                                                                                                                            | Yes                                                                                       |
| I-10         | `X` synchronizes completion of both the optional-legal branch and the verification loop.                                                                                | `R,A,P,C,I,L,X,H`; `R,A,P,C,I,X,H` when the skip branch is selected              | `R,A,P,C,X,H`; `R,A,P,C,I,X,L,H`                                                                                                                       | Yes                                                                                       |
| I-11         | `H` occurs after `X`, and a completed execution ends after `H`.                                                                                                         | `R,A,P,C,I,X,H`                                                                  | `R,A,P,C,I,H,X`; `R,A,P,C,I,X,H,L`                                                                                                                     | Yes, provided completion means exact final-marking equality.                              |
| I-12         | Each visible source label denotes one source transition. Routing behavior is silent rather than encoded through duplicate visible activities.                           | Exactly one occurrence-capable source transition for each of `R,A,P,L,C,I,D,X,H` | A converted model with two separately enabled visible transitions both labelled `Release Change` is structurally suspect even if some traces coincide. | Not fully: a label-only trace cannot distinguish two transitions carrying the same label. |

Two semantic levels must remain separate:

1. **Partial-order semantics:** `A` and `P`, and later legal and verification, are genuinely unordered in the source model.
2. **Visible trace semantics:** hiding `τ` yields linearizations of that partial order. Seeing all expected linearizations is necessary but not sufficient to prove preservation of true concurrency.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline                                                        | PM4Py calls                                                                                                        | Expected benefit                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Expected risk                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Failure condition                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| --------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary: POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN**                  | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` followed by `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`                         | Retains a token-game representation capable of expressing asymmetric synchronization, concurrency, silent routing, and the loop without first forcing block structure. The accepting Petri net provides explicit initial and final markings for executable validation. | BPMN conversion may introduce silent routing transitions, gateways, and visually block-like layouts that are not present as named constructs in the source. A later BPMN-to-Petri conversion may restructure the net while retaining only some behavioral properties.                                                                                                                                  | Missing or null markings; non-workflow or unsound net; conversion exception; missing positive witnesses; extra negative witnesses; or bounded visible-language mismatch after BPMN round trip.                                                                                                  |
| **Secondary: POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN** | `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` inside `try/except`, then `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)` if successful | May yield a compact, explicitly block-structured model and a cleaner BPMN rendering when the Petri-net behavior is process-tree representable.                                                                                                                         | The source has overlapping synchronization: `A` enables legal review and contributes to enabling verification, while `P` contributes only to verification. At the macro level this forms an N-like asymmetric partial order rather than a straightforward nested sequence/parallel block. A tree conversion may therefore fail, duplicate behavior, or impose an order between legal and verification. | An exception or null tree; visible-label multiplication; loss of either `A/P` order; loss of legal/verification interleavings; changed loop language; or any witness/language mismatch. A tree-conversion exception rejects this derivative pipeline, not the source POWL or primary Petri net. |

The first pipeline is primary because it does not make process-tree representability a prerequisite for obtaining a stakeholder BPMN. The second pipeline is an opportunistic structured projection only.

The current PM4Py conversion interface dispatches POWL to an accepting Petri net, accepts a marked Petri net as input to BPMN conversion, and permits Petri-net-to-process-tree conversion to raise when no suitable tree can be derived. ([GitHub][1])

# 3. Conversion Code

The code below:

* reconstructs the authoritative `source_powl`;
* creates the specifically requested variables;
* checks every returned marking for `None`;
* attempts the process-tree route without hiding exceptions;
* converts every successfully produced BPMN back to an accepting Petri net;
* diagnoses every accepting net that was successfully produced;
* records conversion and visualization failures separately;
* saves clearly named visualizations.

```python
from __future__ import annotations

from collections import Counter
from pathlib import Path
from typing import Any

import pm4py

from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Authoritative source POWL
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

register = Transition(label="Register Change")
assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()
optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
)

configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP,
    children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
)

release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        register,
        assess_risk,
        prepare_evidence,
        optional_legal,
        verification_loop,
        release_change,
        archive_change,
    ]
)

source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Audit bookkeeping
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

OUTPUT_DIR = Path("powl_conversion_audit")
OUTPUT_DIR.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)

# Values are (net, initial_marking, final_marking).
accepting_nets: dict[str, tuple[Any, Any, Any]] = {}

bpmn_models: dict[str, Any] = {}
conversion_failures: dict[str, dict[str, str]] = {}
visualization_failures: dict[str, dict[str, str]] = {}
diagnostics: dict[str, dict[str, Any]] = {}


def failure_record(exc: Exception, interpretation: str) -> dict[str, str]:
    """Keep the original exception visible and attach a semantic interpretation."""
    return {
        "exception_type": type(exc).__name__,
        "message": str(exc),
        "interpretation": interpretation,
    }


def require_accepting_net(
    conversion_name: str,
    result: Any,
) -> tuple[Any, Any, Any]:
    """
    Validate the structural return contract without assuming that all three
    returned values are non-null.
    """
    if not isinstance(result, (tuple, list)) or len(result) != 3:
        raise TypeError(
            f"{conversion_name} did not return a three-element "
            f"(net, initial_marking, final_marking) result: {type(result)!r}"
        )

    net, initial_marking, final_marking = result

    missing = [
        name
        for name, value in (
            ("net", net),
            ("initial_marking", initial_marking),
            ("final_marking", final_marking),
        )
        if value is None
    ]
    if missing:
        raise ValueError(
            f"{conversion_name} returned null component(s): {', '.join(missing)}"
        )

    return net, initial_marking, final_marking


def unpack_soundness_result(raw_result: Any) -> tuple[bool, Any]:
    """
    Be tolerant of PM4Py versions returning either a boolean or a tuple whose
    first element is the boolean result.
    """
    if isinstance(raw_result, tuple):
        if not raw_result:
            return False, {"error": "Empty soundness result tuple"}
        details = raw_result[1] if len(raw_result) == 2 else raw_result[1:]
        return bool(raw_result[0]), details

    return bool(raw_result), None


def run_net_diagnostics(
    name: str,
    net: Any,
    initial_marking: Any,
    final_marking: Any,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """
    Run both requested diagnostics on every accepting net, independently.
    A workflow-net check failure does not suppress the soundness call.
    """
    result: dict[str, Any] = {}

    try:
        result["is_workflow_net"] = bool(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    except Exception as exc:
        result["is_workflow_net"] = None
        result["workflow_net_error"] = failure_record(
            exc,
            "The workflow-net diagnostic itself could not be completed.",
        )

    try:
        raw_soundness = pm4py.check_soundness(
            net,
            initial_marking,
            final_marking,
            print_diagnostics=True,
        )
        is_sound, soundness_details = unpack_soundness_result(raw_soundness)
        result["is_sound"] = is_sound
        result["soundness_details"] = soundness_details
    except Exception as exc:
        result["is_sound"] = None
        result["soundness_error"] = failure_record(
            exc,
            "Soundness was not established; this accepting net must be quarantined.",
        )

    visible_label_counts = Counter(
        transition.label
        for transition in net.transitions
        if transition.label is not None
    )
    result["visible_label_counts"] = dict(sorted(visible_label_counts.items()))
    result["duplicate_visible_labels"] = {
        label: count
        for label, count in visible_label_counts.items()
        if count > 1
    }
    result["silent_transition_count"] = sum(
        transition.label is None for transition in net.transitions
    )
    result["initial_marking_is_non_null"] = initial_marking is not None
    result["final_marking_is_non_null"] = final_marking is not None

    print(f"\nDiagnostics for {name}:")
    print(result)
    return result


def safe_save(
    artifact_name: str,
    saver: Any,
    *args: Any,
) -> None:
    """
    Visualization failure is recorded, not confused with conversion failure.
    For example, a missing Graphviz installation does not by itself prove a
    semantic defect.
    """
    try:
        saver(*args)
    except Exception as exc:
        visualization_failures[artifact_name] = failure_record(
            exc,
            "The model may exist even though its visualization could not be rendered.",
        )
        print(f"Visualization failed for {artifact_name}: {exc!r}")


def try_bpmn_round_trip(
    result_name: str,
    bpmn_model: Any,
) -> tuple[Any, Any, Any] | None:
    """Convert a produced BPMN model back into an accepting Petri net."""
    try:
        triple = require_accepting_net(
            result_name,
            pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_model),
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        conversion_failures[result_name] = failure_record(
            exc,
            "The BPMN derivative could not be rematerialized as an accepting Petri net.",
        )
        print(f"{result_name} failed: {exc!r}")
        return None

    accepting_nets[result_name] = triple
    return triple


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Primary pipeline: POWL -> accepting Petri net -> BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = require_accepting_net(
    "POWL to accepting Petri net",
    pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl),
)
accepting_nets["net_from_powl"] = (
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
)

bpmn_from_net = None
try:
    bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
        net_from_powl,
        im_from_powl,
        fm_from_powl,
    )
    if bpmn_from_net is None:
        raise ValueError("Petri-net-to-BPMN conversion returned None")
    bpmn_models["bpmn_from_net"] = bpmn_from_net
except Exception as exc:
    conversion_failures["petri_to_bpmn"] = failure_record(
        exc,
        "The primary accepting Petri net exists, but its BPMN derivative was not produced.",
    )
    print(f"Primary Petri-to-BPMN conversion failed: {exc!r}")

net_from_bpmn = None
im_from_bpmn = None
fm_from_bpmn = None

if bpmn_from_net is not None:
    direct_bpmn_round_trip = try_bpmn_round_trip(
        "net_from_direct_bpmn_round_trip",
        bpmn_from_net,
    )
    if direct_bpmn_round_trip is not None:
        (
            net_from_bpmn,
            im_from_bpmn,
            fm_from_bpmn,
        ) = direct_bpmn_round_trip


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Secondary pipeline:
#    accepting Petri net -> process tree -> BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

tree_from_net = None
net_from_tree = None
im_from_tree = None
fm_from_tree = None

bpmn_from_tree = None
net_from_tree_bpmn = None
im_from_tree_bpmn = None
fm_from_tree_bpmn = None

try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl,
        im_from_powl,
        fm_from_powl,
    )
    if tree_from_net is None:
        raise ValueError("Petri-net-to-process-tree conversion returned None")
except Exception as exc:
    conversion_failures["petri_to_process_tree"] = failure_record(
        exc,
        (
            "The accepting Petri net was not converted into a process tree. "
            "This is compatible with the source having asymmetric, non-block-"
            "structured dependencies. It rejects the tree derivative, not "
            "the authoritative source or the primary Petri net."
        ),
    )
    print("Process-tree intermediate was not produced.")
    print(conversion_failures["petri_to_process_tree"])
else:
    # Diagnose the process tree's own accepting-net semantics as well.
    try:
        tree_net_result = require_accepting_net(
            "Process tree to accepting Petri net",
            pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net),
        )
        net_from_tree, im_from_tree, fm_from_tree = tree_net_result
        accepting_nets["net_from_process_tree"] = tree_net_result
    except Exception as exc:
        conversion_failures["process_tree_to_petri_net"] = failure_record(
            exc,
            "The derived process tree could not be materialized for semantic comparison.",
        )
        print(f"Process-tree-to-Petri conversion failed: {exc!r}")

    try:
        bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
        if bpmn_from_tree is None:
            raise ValueError("Process-tree-to-BPMN conversion returned None")
        bpmn_models["bpmn_from_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
    except Exception as exc:
        conversion_failures["process_tree_to_bpmn"] = failure_record(
            exc,
            "The process-tree intermediate exists, but its BPMN derivative was not produced.",
        )
        print(f"Process-tree-to-BPMN conversion failed: {exc!r}")

    if bpmn_from_tree is not None:
        tree_bpmn_round_trip = try_bpmn_round_trip(
            "net_from_tree_bpmn_round_trip",
            bpmn_from_tree,
        )
        if tree_bpmn_round_trip is not None:
            (
                net_from_tree_bpmn,
                im_from_tree_bpmn,
                fm_from_tree_bpmn,
            ) = tree_bpmn_round_trip


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. Workflow-net, soundness, and label diagnostics for every successful net
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

for net_name, (net, initial_marking, final_marking) in accepting_nets.items():
    diagnostics[net_name] = run_net_diagnostics(
        net_name,
        net,
        initial_marking,
        final_marking,
    )


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 6. Visualizations
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

safe_save(
    "00_source_powl",
    pm4py.save_vis_powl,
    source_powl,
    str(OUTPUT_DIR / "00_source_powl.svg"),
)

safe_save(
    "10_powl_to_accepting_petri_net",
    pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
    str(OUTPUT_DIR / "10_powl_to_accepting_petri_net.svg"),
)

if bpmn_from_net is not None:
    safe_save(
        "20_petri_to_bpmn",
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
        bpmn_from_net,
        str(OUTPUT_DIR / "20_petri_to_bpmn.svg"),
    )

if net_from_bpmn is not None:
    safe_save(
        "21_direct_bpmn_round_trip_petri_net",
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
        net_from_bpmn,
        im_from_bpmn,
        fm_from_bpmn,
        str(OUTPUT_DIR / "21_direct_bpmn_round_trip_petri_net.svg"),
    )

if tree_from_net is not None:
    safe_save(
        "30_petri_to_process_tree",
        pm4py.save_vis_process_tree,
        tree_from_net,
        str(OUTPUT_DIR / "30_petri_to_process_tree.svg"),
    )

if net_from_tree is not None:
    safe_save(
        "31_process_tree_to_petri_net",
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
        net_from_tree,
        im_from_tree,
        fm_from_tree,
        str(OUTPUT_DIR / "31_process_tree_to_petri_net.svg"),
    )

if bpmn_from_tree is not None:
    safe_save(
        "32_process_tree_to_bpmn",
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
        bpmn_from_tree,
        str(OUTPUT_DIR / "32_process_tree_to_bpmn.svg"),
    )

if net_from_tree_bpmn is not None:
    safe_save(
        "33_tree_bpmn_round_trip_petri_net",
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
        net_from_tree_bpmn,
        im_from_tree_bpmn,
        fm_from_tree_bpmn,
        str(OUTPUT_DIR / "33_tree_bpmn_round_trip_petri_net.svg"),
    )


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 7. Machine-readable summaries retained in memory
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

print("\nSuccessful accepting nets:")
print(sorted(accepting_nets))

print("\nProduced BPMN models:")
print(sorted(bpmn_models))

print("\nConversion failures:")
print(conversion_failures)

print("\nVisualization failures:")
print(visualization_failures)
```

The code deliberately distinguishes conversion failure, semantic-diagnostic failure, and visualization failure. PM4Py exposes separate workflow-net and soundness checks and separate save functions for Petri nets, BPMN, process trees, and POWL; rendering therefore remains a derivative operation rather than evidence of behavioral equivalence. ([GitHub][2])

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

This is a **design-time assessment**, not a claim that the code has already produced matching artifacts. Therefore no cell is marked `validated preserved`. That term should be assigned only after the relevant runtime checks pass.

| Source invariant                                                                       | POWL→Petri           | Petri→BPMN           | Petri→Tree           | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty                                                                                                                                                                             |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------- | -------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `A` and `P` are unordered after `R`.                                                   | `expected preserved` | `expected preserved` | `ambiguous`          | `not tested`    | The accepting net should expose both orders. A process tree may be unable to represent the overlapping dependency without restructuring. Bounded traces must include both linearizations.           |
| True concurrency rather than a choice between two fixed sequences.                     | `expected preserved` | `ambiguous`          | `ambiguous`          | `not tested`    | Petri-net causality can express concurrency, but ordinary visible-trace equality observes only interleavings. A BPMN drawing can suggest parallelism without proving the same concurrency relation. |
| No edge between optional legal and verification.                                       | `expected preserved` | `ambiguous`          | `ambiguous`          | `not tested`    | Required witnesses place `L` before, inside, and after verification. A visually convenient block decomposition could impose an unintended order.                                                    |
| `L` is optional through a silent XOR branch.                                           | `expected preserved` | `approximated`       | `ambiguous`          | `not tested`    | In the net, the skip should be represented by one or more `None`-labelled transitions. BPMN will normally express the skip as routing rather than as a visible task.                                |
| Verification requires both `A` and `P`.                                                | `expected preserved` | `expected preserved` | `ambiguous`          | `not tested`    | Reject any candidate admitting `C` before either prerequisite. The key issue is executable enabling, not the apparent placement of gateway symbols.                                                 |
| Binary-loop language `C,I,(D,C,I)*`.                                                   | `expected preserved` | `ambiguous`          | `expected preserved` | `not tested`    | A tree, if faithfully obtained, has a natural binary-loop construct. A generated BPMN may instead use a back-edge and silent routing whose scope is visually unclear.                               |
| At least one verification iteration.                                                   | `expected preserved` | `expected preserved` | `expected preserved` | `not tested`    | The negative witness `R,A,P,X,H` must remain impossible. A loop gateway must not accidentally provide a zero-iteration bypass.                                                                      |
| Synchronization of optional legal and verification before `X`.                         | `expected preserved` | `expected preserved` | `ambiguous`          | `not tested`    | Early-release witnesses must fail. A process tree may struggle because legal and verification have different prerequisites but later synchronize.                                                   |
| `H` follows `X` and exact completion occurs at the final marking.                      | `expected preserved` | `approximated`       | `expected preserved` | `not tested`    | BPMN uses start/end events rather than carrying the original marking objects. The round-trip net must be checked by exact marking equality, not containment.                                        |
| One source transition per visible activity label.                                      | `expected preserved` | `expected preserved` | `ambiguous`          | `not tested`    | Compare visible-label multisets, not just label sets. Duplicate transitions with the same label can hide structural drift from trace-level checks.                                                  |
| Initial and final markings remain non-null and operationally meaningful.               | `expected preserved` | `approximated`       | `approximated`       | `not tested`    | Marking object identity cannot survive a BPMN representation. What can be checked is non-null reconstruction and equivalent accepted completion behavior.                                           |
| BPMN gateways and silent routing nodes communicate the source semantics unambiguously. | `not tested`         | `ambiguous`          | `ambiguous`          | `ambiguous`     | Gateway type, layout, and connector placement are visual encodings generated from another formalism. They require a separate human-interpretation review.                                           |

Runtime update rules should be explicit:

* If `convert_to_process_tree(...)` raises, every applicable `Petri→Tree` cell becomes `conversion failed`; the primary pipeline remains independently assessable.
* A successful conversion changes no cell to `validated preserved` by itself.
* `validated preserved` is justified only for the precise tested property—for example, bounded visible-language equality up to three verification iterations. It does not imply unbounded or branching-time equivalence.
* If a positive witness is missing, the associated invariant is `lost`.
* If only a weaker structure or visual proxy remains, the status is `approximated` or `ambiguous`, not `validated preserved`.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The following code assumes that the conversion code in Section 3 has run in the same Python process and populated `accepting_nets`.

The custom exploration:

* hides every transition with `label is None`;
* accepts a trace only when the reached marking is **exactly equal** to the supplied final marking;
* caches marking/trace states and limits consecutive silent execution;
* bounds visible length and the number of `Configure Trial` occurrences;
* reports whether state or silent bounds prevented exhaustive exploration within the declared visible/iteration scope.

```python
from __future__ import annotations

from collections import Counter, deque
from typing import Any

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics as pn_semantics


if "net_from_powl" not in accepting_nets:
    raise RuntimeError(
        "Run the conversion code first; accepting_nets['net_from_powl'] is missing."
    )


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. General helpers
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def marking_key(marking: Any) -> tuple[tuple[int, int], ...]:
    """
    A stable key within one in-memory net exploration.

    Place object IDs are not compared across different nets; they are used only
    to detect repeated states inside a single exploration.
    """
    return tuple(
        sorted(
            (id(place), int(token_count))
            for place, token_count in marking.items()
            if token_count
        )
    )


def visible_label_multiset(net: Any) -> Counter:
    return Counter(
        transition.label
        for transition in net.transitions
        if transition.label is not None
    )


def normalize_soundness(raw_result: Any) -> dict[str, Any]:
    if isinstance(raw_result, tuple):
        if not raw_result:
            return {"sound": False, "details": "Empty result tuple"}
        return {
            "sound": bool(raw_result[0]),
            "details": raw_result[1] if len(raw_result) == 2 else raw_result[1:],
        }

    return {"sound": bool(raw_result), "details": None}


def freeze(value: Any) -> Any:
    """
    Convert nested footprint structures into deterministic, comparable values.
    This is diagnostic normalization, not a new semantic equivalence relation.
    """
    if value is None or isinstance(value, (str, int, float, bool)):
        return value

    if isinstance(value, dict):
        frozen_items = [
            (freeze(key), freeze(item))
            for key, item in value.items()
        ]
        return tuple(sorted(frozen_items, key=repr))

    if isinstance(value, (set, frozenset)):
        return tuple(sorted((freeze(item) for item in value), key=repr))

    if isinstance(value, (list, tuple)):
        return tuple(freeze(item) for item in value)

    if hasattr(value, "label"):
        return (
            "labelled_object",
            getattr(value, "label", None),
            getattr(value, "name", None),
        )

    return repr(value)

# 2. Exhaustive bounded visible-trace exploration
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def bounded_visible_language(
    net: Any,
    initial_marking: Any,
    final_marking: Any,
    *,
    max_visible_length: int = 14,
    max_verification_iterations: int = 3,
    max_consecutive_tau: int = 100,
    max_states: int = 500_000,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """
    Enumerate completed visible traces under explicit bounds.

    Completion requires:
        reached_marking == final_marking

    Silent transitions:
        transition.label is None

    Verification iteration count:
        number of visible "Configure Trial" occurrences

    The search is exhaustive under the visible/iteration bounds only when
    neither max_states nor max_consecutive_tau truncates it.
    """
    queue = deque(
        [
            (
                initial_marking,
                tuple(),  # visible trace
                0,        # Configure Trial count
                0,        # consecutive silent-transition count
            )
        ]
    )

    completed_traces: set[tuple[str, ...]] = set()

    # For a fixed marking, trace and iteration count, a lower tau streak
    # dominates a higher one.
    best_tau_streak: dict[
        tuple[tuple[tuple[int, int], ...], tuple[str, ...], int],
        int,
    ] = {}

    states_examined = 0
    state_cap_hit = False
    tau_cap_pruned = False
    visible_bound_pruned = False
    iteration_bound_pruned = False
    final_marking_had_enabled_transitions = False

    while queue:
        marking, trace, iteration_count, tau_streak = queue.popleft()

        semantic_key = (
            marking_key(marking),
            trace,
            iteration_count,
        )
        previous_best = best_tau_streak.get(semantic_key)
        if previous_best is not None and tau_streak >= previous_best:
            continue
        best_tau_streak[semantic_key] = tau_streak

        states_examined += 1
        if states_examined > max_states:
            state_cap_hit = True
            break

        enabled = list(pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking))

        if marking == final_marking:
            completed_traces.add(trace)
            if enabled:
                final_marking_had_enabled_transitions = True
            # Deliberately continue: a malformed model might permit behavior
            # after reaching an accepting marking.

        for transition in enabled:
            next_marking = pn_semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
            if next_marking is None:
                continue

            label = transition.label

            if label is None:
                if tau_streak >= max_consecutive_tau:
                    tau_cap_pruned = True
                    continue

                queue.append(
                    (
                        next_marking,
                        trace,
                        iteration_count,
                        tau_streak + 1,
                    )
                )
                continue

            if len(trace) >= max_visible_length:
                visible_bound_pruned = True
                continue

            next_iteration_count = iteration_count
            if label == "Configure Trial":
                next_iteration_count += 1

            if next_iteration_count > max_verification_iterations:
                iteration_bound_pruned = True
                continue

            queue.append(
                (
                    next_marking,
                    trace + (label,),
                    next_iteration_count,
                    0,
                )
            )

    return {
        "completed_traces": completed_traces,
        "completed_trace_count": len(completed_traces),
        "states_examined": states_examined,
        "max_visible_length": max_visible_length,
        "max_verification_iterations": max_verification_iterations,
        "max_consecutive_tau": max_consecutive_tau,
        "max_states": max_states,
        "state_cap_hit": state_cap_hit,
        "tau_cap_pruned": tau_cap_pruned,
        "visible_bound_pruned": visible_bound_pruned,
        "iteration_bound_pruned": iteration_bound_pruned,
        "final_marking_had_enabled_transitions": (
            final_marking_had_enabled_transitions
        ),
        "exhaustive_under_declared_bound": (
            not state_cap_hit and not tau_cap_pruned
        ),
    }

# 3. Exact witness acceptance
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

def accepts_visible_trace_exactly(
    net: Any,
    initial_marking: Any,
    final_marking: Any,
    visible_trace: tuple[str, ...],
    *,
    max_consecutive_tau: int = 100,
    max_states: int = 250_000,
) -> dict[str, Any]:
    """
    Determine whether a fixed visible trace can reach exactly final_marking.

    Other visible transitions are not followed. Silent routing is explored.
    None is returned as the acceptance result when a resource bound prevents
    a conclusive result.
    """
    queue = deque(
        [
            (
                initial_marking,
                0,  # next visible event index
                0,  # consecutive tau count
            )
        ]
    )

    best_tau_streak: dict[
        tuple[tuple[tuple[int, int], ...], int],
        int,
    ] = {}

    states_examined = 0
    tau_cap_pruned = False

    while queue:
        marking, event_index, tau_streak = queue.popleft()

        key = (marking_key(marking), event_index)
        previous_best = best_tau_streak.get(key)
        if previous_best is not None and tau_streak >= previous_best:
            continue
        best_tau_streak[key] = tau_streak

        states_examined += 1
        if states_examined > max_states:
            return {
                "accepted": None,
                "reason": "state cap reached",
                "states_examined": states_examined,
            }

        if marking == final_marking and event_index == len(visible_trace):
            return {
                "accepted": True,
                "reason": "exact final marking reached",
                "states_examined": states_examined,
            }

        for transition in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking):
            label = transition.label

            if label is None:
                if tau_streak >= max_consecutive_tau:
                    tau_cap_pruned = True
                    continue

                next_marking = pn_semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
                if next_marking is not None:
                    queue.append(
                        (
                            next_marking,
                            event_index,
                            tau_streak + 1,
                        )
                    )
                continue

            if (
                event_index < len(visible_trace)
                and label == visible_trace[event_index]
            ):
                next_marking = pn_semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
                if next_marking is not None:
                    queue.append(
                        (
                            next_marking,
                            event_index + 1,
                            0,
                        )
                    )

    if tau_cap_pruned:
        return {
            "accepted": None,
            "reason": "silent-transition bound pruned part of the search",
            "states_examined": states_examined,
        }

    return {
        "accepted": False,
        "reason": "no exact-final-marking execution found",
        "states_examined": states_examined,
    }
```

For the authoritative source, a completed trace with at most three verification iterations has at most 14 visible activities:

[
3\text{ prefix activities}

* 2k\text{ trial activities}
* (k-1)\text{ corrections}
* 2\text{ final activities}
* {0,1}\text{ legal activity}
  = 4 + 3k + {0,1}.
  ]

For (k=3), the maximum is 14. Therefore, subject to the reported state and silent-transition flags, the explorer is exhaustive for complete visible traces containing at most three `Configure Trial` occurrences. It is **not exhaustive for the unbounded loop**.

Footprints, soundness, and reachability are complementary diagnostics rather than interchangeable equivalence proofs. Reachability-state counts can differ solely because one conversion introduces extra silent routing states. Random playout is weaker still: it can expose a defect but cannot prove absence of an unsampled trace. PM4Py provides the footprint, reachability, enabled-transition, Petri execution, and model-playout facilities used by this audit. ([GitHub][1])

The validation layers establish different things:

* **Soundness:** whether the candidate has appropriate completion properties.
* **Footprints:** whether a coarse abstraction of order, parallelism, start, and end relations agrees.
* **Witnesses:** whether specifically important intended and prohibited behaviors are handled correctly.
* **Bounded language:** exact equality of completed visible traces under the declared bounds.
* **Reachability summary:** structural evidence and debugging support, not equivalence by state count.
* **Playout:** sampled corroboration only.
* **Exact final equality:** prevents a trace from being accepted merely because its marking contains the final marking plus residual tokens.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN rendering can be plausible and readable while still being semantically misleading.

**Parallel gateways.** The source is not simply “after registration, run two independent branches and join them.” `A` and `P` are unordered, but their downstream effects differ:

* `A` enables optional legal review.
* Both `A` and `P` are required for verification.
* Legal and verification then remain unordered until their common release synchronization.

A generated BPMN may need several gateways and silent routing paths to encode this asymmetric dependency. A viewer may incorrectly treat the nearest split and join as one well-nested parallel block.

**Exclusive gateways.** The XOR between `L` and `τ` contains one visible activity and one silent skip. In BPMN, the skip will ordinarily appear as an unlabeled sequence flow rather than a task. Common misreadings are:

* legal review is mandatory because only its task is visually salient;
* the empty route is a modeling accident;
* the XOR selects between legal and all verification work;
* taking the skip allows release before verification.

The diagram should explicitly annotate the gateway as **“Optional legal review: Legal Review or silent skip”**.

**Loop visualization.** The source loop has the precise language

[
(C,I)(D,C,I)^*.
]

A Petri-net-to-BPMN converter may render this as a backward sequence flow with gateways and silent routing. That can be mistaken for:

* a zero-or-more loop, allowing verification to be skipped;
* a self-loop around only `Inspect Trial`;
* an unconditional correction after every inspection;
* permission to execute `Correct Defect` and then leave without another trial;
* permission to repeat `Configure Trial` without `Correct Defect`.

The BPMN documentation should therefore state the loop formula directly rather than relying on the visual back-edge.

**Silent routing nodes.** Converter-introduced silent transitions or gateways are technical devices. They must not be renamed as business activities such as “Decision” or “Synchronization” unless those activities exist in the source. Doing so would change the observable alphabet and could create false audit events.

**Apparent layout order.** Left-to-right, top-to-bottom, vertical alignment, connector crossings, and proximity do not create sequence constraints. In particular:

* placing legal review above verification does not mean it happens first;
* drawing `P` farther left than `A` does not mean `P < A`;
* placing the release gateway next to `Inspect Trial` does not eliminate its dependency on the legal branch;
* a line that visually crosses another flow does not create synchronization.

**Atomic model events versus real-time overlap.** The partial-order model permits interleavings and represents absence of causal order. A static BPMN diagram does not establish that two real executions had overlapping start/end intervals. That requires lifecycle timestamps or interval data.

Recommended annotations for the stakeholder BPMN are:

1. A legend distinguishing business tasks, generated gateways, and silent routes.
2. Stable gateway identifiers such as `G-LEGAL-SKIP`, `G-VERIFY-LOOP`, and `G-RELEASE-JOIN`.
3. A callout at release: **“Enabled only after legal-or-skip and completed verification.”**
4. A bracket or note between legal and verification: **“No ordering constraint; interleaving permitted.”**
5. A loop note: **“One or more trials; correction is required between trials.”**
6. A footer containing the source POWL hash, PM4Py version, conversion path, and validation-manifest identifier.
7. A warning that graphical placement is non-normative; sequence flows and gateway semantics are normative.
8. A small witness panel showing one no-legal trace, one legal/verification interleaving, and one repeated verification trace.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

A result should be rejected or quarantined under any of the following conditions:

1. **Incomplete accepting-net result.** The converter returns no net, a null initial marking, a null final marking, or something other than a three-element accepting-net result.

2. **Workflow-net failure.** `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)` is false or cannot complete. A more general Petri net might still have behavior, but it should not be accepted as the intended workflow derivative without a separate justification.

3. **Soundness failure.** `pm4py.check_soundness(...)` is false, raises, or is inconclusive. Dead transitions, deadlocks, improper completion, or residual tokens are grounds for quarantine.

4. **Loss of a required `A/P` interleaving.** Either the `A`-before-`P` witness or the `P`-before-`A` witness is rejected.

5. **Imposed order between legal and verification.** The model rejects legal before verification, legal during verification, or legal after verification but before release.

6. **Verification starts too early.** The candidate admits `Configure Trial` before either `Assess Risk` or `Prepare Evidence`.

7. **Early release.** The candidate admits release before `Inspect Trial`, admits zero verification iterations, or permits release before a selected legal review completes.

8. **Collapsed optional behavior.** Legal review becomes mandatory, impossible, repeatable, or observationally conflated with another visible task.

9. **Changed loop scope.** The candidate permits `Inspect Trial` before `Configure Trial`, a second trial without `Correct Defect`, `Correct Defect` before the first trial, or exit immediately after `Correct Defect`.

10. **Failed exact-final-marking test.** A supposedly completed witness reaches only a marking that covers the final marking while retaining additional tokens, or the exact final marking still enables further visible or silent behavior.

11. **Visible-label drift.** A source label disappears, is renamed, is duplicated across separately enabled transitions, or a routing construct acquires a visible business label.

12. **Bounded-language mismatch.** Under an exhaustive bounded run, the derivative has any missing or extra completed visible trace relative to `net_from_powl`.

13. **Inconclusive bounded exploration.** The state cap or silent-transition cap is reached. This is not evidence of mismatch, but it prevents acceptance and requires quarantine or a stronger analysis.

14. **Silent livelock.** The derivative admits an indefinitely repeatable silent cycle or otherwise fails to guarantee progress to visible behavior or completion.

15. **Unexplained footprint disagreement.** A difference in start activities, end activities, sequence relations, or parallel relations is not automatically decisive, but it must be explained before acceptance.

16. **Process-tree conversion exception.** This rejects the process-tree derivative only. It must not trigger substitution of a hand-written approximate tree while preserving the label “converted faithfully.”

17. **Unreviewable BPMN routing.** The BPMN technically converts but its gateways, skip path, release join, or loop scope cannot be interpreted reliably without reverse-engineering the generated Petri net.

18. **Provenance failure.** The artifact lacks a source hash, PM4Py version, conversion path, validation parameters, or recorded exception details, making later reproduction impossible.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

The **canonical stored model should be the source POWL**, accompanied by a machine-readable invariant manifest. The POWL is authoritative because it directly represents the intended partial-order constraints, optional silent behavior, and nested loop without requiring a visually convenient block decomposition.

The canonical package should contain:

* the POWL source or stable serialization;
* the exact activity labels;
* the source-semantics invariant table;
* positive and negative witness traces;
* a source-content hash;
* the PM4Py and Python versions used for generation;
* explicit loop semantics: `trial_body (correct_defect trial_body)*`;
* the validation bounds and acceptance policy.

The **stakeholder visualization should be the BPMN generated through the primary accepting-Petri-net route**:

[
\text{POWL} \rightarrow \text{accepting Petri net} \rightarrow \text{BPMN}.
]

That BPMN should be treated as an annotated projection, not as the authoritative editable source.

The generated artifacts have the following status:

| Artifact                                                    | Status                                        | Required accompanying evidence                                                                                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accepting Petri net from POWL                               | Primary executable derivative                 | Non-null markings, workflow-net result, soundness result, visible-label multiset, source hash, witness audit, and bounded-language baseline                     |
| BPMN from primary Petri net                                 | Stakeholder derivative                        | Conversion provenance, annotated gateway semantics, successful BPMN-to-Petri round trip, witness results, bounded-language diff, and exact-final-marking checks |
| Process tree from Petri net                                 | Optional derivative                           | Recorded success or full exception; label audit; accepting-net materialization; witness and bounded-language comparison                                         |
| BPMN from process tree                                      | Optional derivative of an optional derivative | All process-tree evidence plus its own BPMN round-trip evidence                                                                                                 |
| Reachability graphs, footprints, playout logs, and diagrams | Diagnostic artifacts                          | Converter version, parameter values, source/derivative identifiers, and a statement of what the artifact does not prove                                         |

A process-tree intermediate should be allowed only under four conditions:

1. Conversion succeeds without manual repair.
2. The resulting tree can be converted to an accepting Petri net with non-null markings.
3. All positive and negative witnesses pass.
4. The bounded visible language matches the POWL-derived baseline exhaustively under the declared bound.

Even then, the tree remains derivative. Its cleaner block structure is not a reason to replace the source POWL.

If process-tree conversion raises, the exception should be retained as evidence. It may indicate that the source's asymmetric dependencies are not representable by the converter's process-tree class. It does not prove a defect in the POWL.

Future edits should follow a one-way regeneration policy:

[
\text{edit POWL}
\rightarrow
\text{regenerate Petri net}
\rightarrow
\text{regenerate BPMN/tree artifacts}
\rightarrow
\text{rerun validation}.
]

In particular:

* Do not edit the generated BPMN and convert it back to redefine the POWL.
* Do not treat a process-tree reconstruction as a normalized replacement for the source.
* Do not carry forward manually moved or reconnected BPMN gateways without semantic review.
* Keep generated files immutable or clearly marked as generated.
* Run the invariant and bounded-language audit in continuous integration after every source change.
* Treat a stakeholder BPMN edit as a **proposed semantic change**: update the invariant manifest and source POWL deliberately, then regenerate all derivatives.
* Pin converter versions for reproducible releases. A later PM4Py version may generate a structurally different but potentially equivalent derivative, which still requires revalidation.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every proposed test passes, the following claims remain unestablished:

1. **Unbounded loop equivalence.** Equality through three verification iterations does not prove equality for four, five, or arbitrarily many iterations.

2. **True-concurrency equivalence.** Equality of all bounded visible interleavings does not prove preservation of the same causal, conflict, and concurrency relations. Two models can have the same linear trace language while differing under step, pomset, history-preserving, or branching-time semantics.

3. **Branching structure after silent choices.** Hiding `τ` can make two models trace-equivalent while one resolves choices earlier than the other or introduces different silent branching. Footprints and visible traces may not expose that distinction.

4. **Absence of silent divergence beyond explored states.** State caching and silent bounds prevent the validator itself from looping forever, but an inconclusive or insufficiently large search cannot prove that all silent divergence is absent.

5. **Mathematical non-representability as a process tree.** A PM4Py conversion exception establishes that this converter invocation did not produce a tree. It does not by itself prove that no equivalent process tree exists under every possible encoding or equivalence notion.

6. **Human interpretation of BPMN.** A behaviorally acceptable round-trip net does not establish that legal, compliance, or operational stakeholders will read the rendered gateway structure correctly.

7. **Timing overlap.** The model-level absence of order does not establish that `Legal Review` and trial work overlap in wall-clock time. Duration, lifecycle, calendar, and resource data are absent.

8. **Resource, data, and decision semantics.** The audit says nothing about who may perform an activity, what data condition selects legal review, how defects are determined, or whether separation-of-duty rules exist.

9. **Label-to-business-concept identity.** Matching label multisets cannot prove that a converted task has the same business meaning, input data, responsibility, or completion criterion as the source transition.

10. **Robustness across PM4Py versions.** Passing evidence applies to the exact converter implementation and environment recorded in the validation manifest. It does not automatically transfer to later releases.

11. **Equivalence outside completed traces.** The bounded-language comparison focuses on completed visible executions. It does not fully compare all incomplete prefixes, refusal sets, deadlock points after arbitrary prefixes, or recovery behavior unless those are separately explored.

12. **Visual fidelity of saved files.** Successful SVG generation proves only that a renderer produced a file. It does not establish correct gateway labels, readable connector routing, accessibility, or absence of visual overlap.
