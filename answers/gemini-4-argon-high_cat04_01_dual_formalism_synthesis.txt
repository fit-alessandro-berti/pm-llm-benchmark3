# 1. Assumptions and Interpretation

1. **Representation of the low-risk / high-risk choice**: Because case attributes (such as risk level) are not encoded in an activity-only control-flow model, the routing decision after `Analyze Root Cause` is represented as a non-deterministic exclusive choice (`Operator.XOR`) between two branches: (a) a single `Supervisor Review` transition for low-risk cases, and (b) a concurrent execution of `Quality Review` and `Regulatory Review` for high-risk cases.
2. **Representation of optional supplier consultation**: The data-dependent option to consult the supplier is modeled as an exclusive choice (`Operator.XOR`) between the visible activity `Consult Supplier` and an unobservable skip transition (`SilentTransition` / $\tau$). Placing this `XOR` block outside any loop guarantees that `Consult Supplier` executes at most once per case.
3. **Abstraction of inspection outcomes and repeat-until loop semantics**: Because inspection outcomes ("pass" vs. "fail") are not distinct event labels, the trial cycle is modeled using a structured repeat-until loop (`Operator.LOOP(do, redo)`). The `do` body is the sequence `->('Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial')` and the `redo` body is `'Adjust Parameters'`. Under standard PM4Py `LOOP` semantics, the `do` branch executes first; upon completion of `Inspect Trial`, an unobservable control choice either exits the loop (representing a passing inspection) or triggers the `redo` branch `Adjust Parameters` followed immediately by another execution of the `do` branch (representing a failing inspection). This structurally enforces that every loop entry starts with `Execute Trial`, every `Adjust Parameters` is bracketed by a preceding failed `Inspect Trial` and a subsequent `Execute Trial`, and the loop can only exit immediately after `Inspect Trial` (never after `Adjust Parameters`).
4. **Prohibition of duplicate visible activity labels**: Each of the 14 business activities is instantiated as a single unique visible node (1-to-1 label injectivity). Duplicating visible labels or subtrees to simulate cross-branch dependencies in the Process Tree is explicitly rejected because it causes exponential subtree blow-up around the unbounded trial loop, breaks transition identity in Petri net conversions, and distorts activity-level frequency and bottleneck analytics.
5. **Process-Tree approximation policy (Conservative Under-Approximation)**: The narrative specifies a non-block-structured Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) of dependencies—specifically, an Asymmetric Synchronization (Wait-See / N-crown) pattern where `Execute Trial` depends on both `Plan Verification` (from the `Contain Material` branch) and `Design Corrective Action` (from the `Analyze Root Cause` branch), while `Approval Route` and `Consult Supplier` depend *only* on `Analyze Root Cause` and may overlap `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, and `Design Corrective Action`. Because a single-label Process Tree is restricted to series-parallel (cograph) hierarchies, it cannot represent this DAG exactly. We adopt a **conservative under-approximation policy**: we synchronize the two preparation chains (`Contain Material -> Plan Verification` and `Analyze Root Cause -> Design Corrective Action`) at a barrier before launching the parallel execution of the approval route, optional supplier consultation, and trial loop. This guarantees 100% precision (zero unsafe or prerequisite-violating traces are admitted) at the cost of recall (excluding valid early executions of `Approval Route` and `Consult Supplier` before `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, and `Design Corrective Action` complete).
6. **Interpretation of "may overlap" and completion-time logging**: Although the narrative states that activities "may overlap" in real-time duration, event logs record atomic completion timestamps (Narrative Rule 11). Consequently, at the model level, "may overlap" is formalized as **causal independence** (absence of a partial-order precedence edge in POWL, or placement in separate branches of `Operator.PARALLEL` in a Process Tree), which induces **interleaving semantics** over atomic completion events in the accepted trace language while preserving true concurrency in the underlying Petri net token game.

# 2. Intended Dependency Specification

Let the composite submodels be defined as:
- $S_{\text{high}} = \text{Concurrent}(\text{Quality Review}, \text{Regulatory Review})$
- $S_{\text{app}} = \text{XOR}(\text{Supervisor Review}, S_{\text{high}})$
- $S_{\text{sup}} = \text{XOR}(\text{Consult Supplier}, \tau)$
- $S_{\text{cycle}} = \text{Sequence}(\text{Execute Trial}, \text{Inspect Trial})$
- $S_{\text{trial}} = \text{Loop}(do=S_{\text{cycle}}, redo=\text{Adjust Parameters})$

| ID | Source activity or submodel | Target activity or submodel | Relation | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D1** | `Register Deviation` | `Contain Material`, `Analyze Root Cause` | Strict Precedence (Split) | Every case begins with `Register Deviation`, which enables both initial work streams (Rules 1, 2). |
| **D2** | `Contain Material` | `Analyze Root Cause` | Concurrency / Independence | Containment and root-cause analysis proceed independently and may complete in either order (Rule 2). |
| **D3** | `Contain Material` | `Plan Verification` | Strict Precedence | Verification planning requires material containment to be completed first (Rule 3). |
| **D4** | `Analyze Root Cause` | `Design Corrective Action` | Strict Precedence | Corrective action design requires root-cause analysis to be completed first (Rule 4). |
| **D5** | `Analyze Root Cause` | $S_{\text{app}}$ (`Approval Route`) | Strict Precedence | Risk routing and subsequent reviews can only be initiated after root-cause analysis completes (Rule 5). |
| **D6** | `Supervisor Review` | $S_{\text{high}}$ (`Quality Review` $\parallel$ `Regulatory Review`) | Exclusive Choice (`XOR`) | Cases follow either the low-risk route or the high-risk route, never both (Rule 5). |
| **D7** | `Quality Review` | `Regulatory Review` | Concurrency / Independence | Within the high-risk route, both reviews are required and may complete in either order (Rule 5). |
| **D8** | `Analyze Root Cause` | $S_{\text{sup}}$ (`Optional Supplier Consultation`) | Strict Precedence | Supplier consultation, when selected, may start only after root-cause analysis finishes (Rule 6). |
| **D9** | `Consult Supplier` | $\tau$ (Silent Skip) | Optionality (`XOR` with $\tau$, at most once) | Consulting the supplier is optional based on unmodeled case data and occurs at most once (Rule 6). |
| **D10** | `Plan Verification`, `Design Corrective Action` | $S_{\text{trial}}$ (`Trial Loop`) | And-Join Synchronization | The first `Execute Trial` may start only after *both* `Plan Verification` and `Design Corrective Action` are complete (Rule 7). |
| **D11** | `Execute Trial` | `Inspect Trial` | Strict Precedence (Per Iteration) | Within each trial cycle, trial execution must precede trial inspection (Rule 8). |
| **D12** | $S_{\text{cycle}}$ (`Execute Trial -> Inspect Trial`) | `Adjust Parameters` | Structured Repeat-Until Loop (`LOOP`) | Failed inspections trigger `Adjust Parameters` followed by a new trial cycle; passing inspection exits without trailing adjustment (Rule 8). |
| **D13** | $S_{\text{app}}$ | `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, `Design Corrective Action`, $S_{\text{sup}}$, $S_{\text{trial}}$ | Concurrency / Independence | Once `Analyze Root Cause` completes, approvals are independent of containment, planning, design, supplier consultation, and trials (Rules 5, 6, 7). |
| **D14** | $S_{\text{sup}}$ | `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, `Design Corrective Action`, $S_{\text{app}}$, $S_{\text{trial}}$ | Concurrency / Independence | Once `Analyze Root Cause` completes, supplier consultation may overlap design, verification planning, approvals, and trial work (Rule 6). |
| **D15** | $S_{\text{trial}}$, $S_{\text{app}}$, $S_{\text{sup}}$ | `Release Material` | And-Join Synchronization | Material release requires a passing trial inspection, completion of the chosen approval route, and completion of supplier consultation if selected (Rule 9). |
| **D16** | `Release Material` | `Archive Case` | Strict Precedence (Termination) | `Archive Case` occurs only after `Release Material` and terminates the case (Rules 1, 10). |

# 3. PM4Py Process-Tree Model

### 1. Compact Process-Tree Expression

```text
->(
  'Register Deviation',
  +(
    ->( 'Contain Material', 'Plan Verification' ),
    ->( 'Analyze Root Cause', 'Design Corrective Action' )
  ),
  +(
    X(
      'Supervisor Review',
      +( 'Quality Review', 'Regulatory Review' )
    ),
    X( 'Consult Supplier', tau ),
    *(
      ->( 'Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial' ),
      'Adjust Parameters'
    )
  ),
  'Release Material',
  'Archive Case'
)
```

### 2. Self-Contained Python Code Constructing `tree`

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree, Operator


def attach_child(parent: ProcessTree, child: ProcessTree) -> ProcessTree:
    """Explicitly wires parent-child pointers in both directions."""
    parent.children.append(child)
    child.parent = parent
    return child


# Root sequence: ->( Register Deviation, Prep_Phase, Exec_Phase, Release Material, Archive Case )
tree = ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE)

# 1. Start activity
n_reg_dev = attach_child(tree, ProcessTree(label="Register Deviation"))

# 2. Preparation Phase: +( ->(Contain Material, Plan Verification), ->(Analyze Root Cause, Design Corrective Action) )
n_prep_par = attach_child(tree, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.PARALLEL))

n_contain_seq = attach_child(n_prep_par, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE))
attach_child(n_contain_seq, ProcessTree(label="Contain Material"))
attach_child(n_contain_seq, ProcessTree(label="Plan Verification"))

n_rca_seq = attach_child(n_prep_par, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE))
attach_child(n_rca_seq, ProcessTree(label="Analyze Root Cause"))
attach_child(n_rca_seq, ProcessTree(label="Design Corrective Action"))

# 3. Execution & Review Phase: +( Approval_XOR, Supplier_XOR, Trial_LOOP )
n_exec_par = attach_child(tree, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.PARALLEL))

# 3a. Risk-based Approval Route: X( Supervisor Review, +(Quality Review, Regulatory Review) )
n_approval_xor = attach_child(n_exec_par, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.XOR))
attach_child(n_approval_xor, ProcessTree(label="Supervisor Review"))
n_high_risk_par = attach_child(n_approval_xor, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.PARALLEL))
attach_child(n_high_risk_par, ProcessTree(label="Quality Review"))
attach_child(n_high_risk_par, ProcessTree(label="Regulatory Review"))

# 3b. Optional Supplier Consultation: X( Consult Supplier, tau )
n_supplier_xor = attach_child(n_exec_par, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.XOR))
attach_child(n_supplier_xor, ProcessTree(label="Consult Supplier"))
attach_child(n_supplier_xor, ProcessTree(label=None))  # Silent transition (tau)

# 3c. Trial Loop: *( ->(Execute Trial, Inspect Trial), Adjust Parameters )
n_trial_loop = attach_child(n_exec_par, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.LOOP))
n_trial_do = attach_child(n_trial_loop, ProcessTree(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE))
attach_child(n_trial_do, ProcessTree(label="Execute Trial"))
attach_child(n_trial_do, ProcessTree(label="Inspect Trial"))
attach_child(n_trial_loop, ProcessTree(label="Adjust Parameters"))

# 4. Release and Archive
n_release = attach_child(tree, ProcessTree(label="Release Material"))
n_archive = attach_child(tree, ProcessTree(label="Archive Case"))
```

### 3. Explicit Parent/Child Handling and Mapping to Narrative
Every `ProcessTree` node above is instantiated via `ProcessTree(...)` and linked through `attach_child`, which appends `child` to `parent.children` and sets `child.parent = parent` (leaving `tree.parent is None` at the root). The outer `Operator.SEQUENCE` enforces that `Register Deviation` occurs first (Rule 1) and `Release Material -> Archive Case` occurs last (Rules 9, 10). The first `Operator.PARALLEL` block (`n_prep_par`) runs `Contain Material -> Plan Verification` (Rule 3) concurrently with `Analyze Root Cause -> Design Corrective Action` (Rules 2, 4). Once both preparation streams complete, the second `Operator.PARALLEL` block (`n_exec_par`) concurrently enables the risk-route `Operator.XOR` (Rule 5), the optional `Consult Supplier` `Operator.XOR` with a silent leaf `ProcessTree(label=None)` (Rule 6), and the `Operator.LOOP` trial cycle (Rules 7, 8).

### 4. Analysis of Representational Loss and Approximation in the Process Tree
Because Process Trees can only generate series-parallel (cograph) dependency orders, any partial order containing an induced "N"-shaped (fence) subposet cannot be represented exactly without label duplication. Here, `Plan Verification` requires `Contain Material`, whereas `Design Corrective Action`, `Approval Route`, and `Consult Supplier` require `Analyze Root Cause`; subsequently, `Trial Loop` requires both `Plan Verification` and `Design Corrective Action`, while `Approval Route` and `Consult Supplier` do *not* require `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, or `Design Corrective Action`. To ensure that `Trial Loop` waits for both `Plan Verification` and `Design Corrective Action` (preserving Rule 7) while still running concurrently with `Approval Route` and `Consult Supplier` (preserving parts of Rules 6 and 7), `tree` places `Approval Route` and `Consult Supplier` inside the second parallel block (`n_exec_par`) *after* the first parallel block (`n_prep_par`) finishes. Consequently, `tree` **overconstrains** (under-approximates) two intended requirements:
1. **Delayed Approval Route**: In the narrative (Rule 5), `Supervisor Review`, `Quality Review`, and `Regulatory Review` only require `Analyze Root Cause` and may overlap `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, and `Design Corrective Action`. In `tree`, approvals cannot complete until *all* of `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, and `Design Corrective Action` have completed.
2. **Delayed Supplier Consultation**: In the narrative (Rule 6), `Consult Supplier` only requires `Analyze Root Cause` and may overlap `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, and `Design Corrective Action`. In `tree`, `Consult Supplier` is likewise blocked until `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, and `Design Corrective Action` have all completed.

# 4. PM4Py POWL Model

### 1. Compact POWL Description

```text
Nodes:
  rd           = 'Register Deviation'
  cm           = 'Contain Material'
  arc          = 'Analyze Root Cause'
  pv           = 'Plan Verification'
  dca          = 'Design Corrective Action'
  approval_xor = X( 'Supervisor Review', PO(nodes={'Quality Review', 'Regulatory Review'}, order={}) )
  supplier_xor = X( 'Consult Supplier', tau )
  trial_loop   = *( PO(nodes={'Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial'}, order={'Execute Trial' --> 'Inspect Trial'}), 'Adjust Parameters' )
  rm           = 'Release Material'
  ac           = 'Archive Case'

Top-Level StrictPartialOrder:
  PO(
    nodes = { rd, cm, arc, pv, dca, approval_xor, supplier_xor, trial_loop, rm, ac },
    order = {
      rd --> cm,
      rd --> arc,
      cm --> pv,
      arc --> dca,
      arc --> approval_xor,
      arc --> supplier_xor,
      pv --> trial_loop,
      dca --> trial_loop,
      approval_xor --> rm,
      supplier_xor --> rm,
      trial_loop --> rm,
      rm --> ac
    }
  )
```

### 2. Self-Contained Python Code Constructing `powl`

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)

# 1. Atomic transitions for root-level activities
rd = Transition(label="Register Deviation")
cm = Transition(label="Contain Material")
arc = Transition(label="Analyze Root Cause")
pv = Transition(label="Plan Verification")
dca = Transition(label="Design Corrective Action")
rm = Transition(label="Release Material")
ac = Transition(label="Archive Case")

# 2. Risk-based Approval submodel: XOR( Supervisor Review, PO(Quality Review || Regulatory Review) )
sup_rev = Transition(label="Supervisor Review")
qual_rev = Transition(label="Quality Review")
reg_rev = Transition(label="Regulatory Review")
high_risk_po = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[qual_rev, reg_rev])  # Unconnected = concurrent
approval_route = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [sup_rev, high_risk_po])

# 3. Optional Supplier Consultation submodel: XOR( Consult Supplier, SilentTransition )
consult_sup = Transition(label="Consult Supplier")
skip_sup = SilentTransition()
supplier_opt = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [consult_sup, skip_sup])

# 4. Trial Loop submodel: LOOP( do=PO(Execute Trial --> Inspect Trial), redo=Adjust Parameters )
exec_trial = Transition(label="Execute Trial")
insp_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_cycle = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[exec_trial, insp_trial])
trial_cycle.order.add_edge(exec_trial, insp_trial)

adj_param = Transition(label="Adjust Parameters")
trial_loop = OperatorPOWL(Operator.LOOP, [trial_cycle, adj_param])

# 5. Root StrictPartialOrder combining all 10 top-level nodes with cross-branch DAG edges
powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        rd,
        cm,
        arc,
        pv,
        dca,
        approval_route,
        supplier_opt,
        trial_loop,
        rm,
        ac,
    ]
)

# Initial split after Register Deviation
powl.order.add_edge(rd, cm)
powl.order.add_edge(rd, arc)

# Containment branch
powl.order.add_edge(cm, pv)

# Root-cause branch fan-out (enables design, approvals, and supplier consultation independently)
powl.order.add_edge(arc, dca)
powl.order.add_edge(arc, approval_route)
powl.order.add_edge(arc, supplier_opt)

# Trial synchronization (waits for both Plan Verification and Design Corrective Action)
powl.order.add_edge(pv, trial_loop)
powl.order.add_edge(dca, trial_loop)

# Release synchronization (waits for approval route, optional supplier consultation, and passing trial loop)
powl.order.add_edge(approval_route, rm)
powl.order.add_edge(supplier_opt, rm)
powl.order.add_edge(trial_loop, rm)

# Final archival step
powl.order.add_edge(rm, ac)
```

### 3. Justification of Intentionally Unconnected Concurrent Node Pairs
In a `StrictPartialOrder`, two nodes $u$ and $v$ are concurrent if neither $(u, v)$ nor $(v, u)$ belongs to the transitive closure of `.order`. Every unconnected pair in `powl` directly reflects domain independence:
- **`Quality Review` and `Regulatory Review` (inside `high_risk_po`)**: Intentionally unconnected so high-risk reviews can execute concurrently and complete in either order (Rule 5).
- **`{cm, pv}` and `{arc, dca, approval_route, supplier_opt}`**: After `rd` completes, the containment chain (`cm -> pv`) has no causal dependency on the root-cause analysis tree (`arc` and its downstream nodes `dca`, `approval_route`, `supplier_opt`) until `pv` and `dca` synchronize at `trial_loop` (Rules 2, 3, 4, 5, 6). Thus `cm` and `pv` may freely interleave with `arc`, `dca`, `approval_route`, and `supplier_opt`.
- **`dca`, `approval_route`, and `supplier_opt`**: All three are direct successors of `arc`, with no edges between them. Once root-cause analysis finishes, corrective-action design (`dca`), risk reviews (`approval_route`), and supplier consultation (`supplier_opt`) proceed on independent tracks (Rules 4, 5, 6).
- **`trial_loop`, `approval_route`, and `supplier_opt`**: While `trial_loop` depends on `pv` and `dca`, neither `approval_route` nor `supplier_opt` has an edge to or from `trial_loop`. Therefore, approvals and supplier consultation can start before, during, or after `trial_loop`, synchronizing only at `rm` (`Release Material`) (Rules 6, 7, 9).

# 7. Preferred Representation and Validation Plan

### Canonical Representation Defense
**`powl` (the PM4Py POWL model) is selected as the canonical representation.**
The material-deviation process exhibits an asymmetric cross-branch synchronization DAG: `Analyze Root Cause` splits into three independent branches (`Design Corrective Action`, `Approval Route`, `Optional Supplier Consultation`), only one of which (`Design Corrective Action`) joins with the parallel `Contain Material -> Plan Verification` stream to enable `Trial Loop`, before all branches finally synchronize at `Release Material`.
- **POWL** is strictly more expressive than Process Trees while retaining hierarchical block guarantees: by replacing flat parallel/sequence operators with `StrictPartialOrder` DAGs over submodels (`OperatorPOWL`), `powl` captures every precedence, choice, loop, and concurrency relation in Section 2 **with 100% precision and 100% recall** and **zero duplicate labels**.
- **Process Trees** cannot express non-series-parallel posets without either overconstraining valid interleavings (as `tree` does on T4 and T5), underconstraining safety prerequisites (which would allow `Execute Trial` or `Consult Supplier` before their prerequisites), or duplicating labels across exponential XOR branches.

### Concrete Validation Plan
The self-contained Python validation suite below performs five verification layers on both converted models:
1. **Conversion to accepting Petri nets** via `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net`.
2. **Soundness checking (Woflan)** via `pm4py.check_soundness` to verify the workflow-net structure, coverage by S-components, absence of dead transitions, livelock freedom, and proper completion to the final marking.
3. **Bounded/sampled playout** via `pm4py.play_out` to sample executable traces and verify structural invariants (start/end activities, trial-loop alternation, mutual exclusion of risk routes, and prerequisite orderings).
4. **Explicit positive and negative trace tests (T1–T8)** via optimal alignment fitness (`pm4py.fitness_alignments`), confirming exact acceptance (`fitness == 1.0`) or rejection (`fitness < 1.0`) against the behavioral witnesses table.
5. **Structural audit of silent transitions ($\tau$) and duplicate visible labels**, ensuring 1-to-1 visible label injectivity (all 14 business labels appear exactly once) and verifying that silent transitions cannot bypass mandatory activities (no empty trace acceptance and no zero-trial traces).

```python
from collections import Counter
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.log.obj import EventLog, Trace, Event


def traces_from_dict(trace_dict: dict) -> dict:
    """Converts lists of activity names into single-trace PM4Py EventLogs."""
    logs = {}
    for tid, activities in trace_dict.items():
        trace = Trace([Event({"concept:name": act}) for act in activities])
        logs[tid] = EventLog([trace])
    return logs


def is_trace_accepted(log: EventLog, net, im, fm) -> bool:
    """Checks exact trace acceptance via alignment fitness == 1.0."""
    res = pm4py.fitness_alignments(log, net, im, fm)
    return abs(res["averageFitness"] - 1.0) < 1e-9


# 1. Convert both models to Accepting Petri Nets
net_pt, im_pt, fm_pt = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)
net_powl, im_powl, fm_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl)

# 2. Soundness Checking (Woflan)
is_sound_pt, diag_pt = pm4py.check_soundness(net_pt, im_pt, fm_pt)
is_sound_powl, diag_powl = pm4py.check_soundness(net_powl, im_powl, fm_powl)
assert is_sound_pt, f"Process Tree net failed soundness: {diag_pt}"
assert is_sound_powl, f"POWL net failed soundness: {diag_powl}"

# 3. Structural Check for Duplicate Visible Labels and Silent Behavior
expected_labels = {
    "Register Deviation", "Contain Material", "Analyze Root Cause",
    "Plan Verification", "Design Corrective Action", "Supervisor Review",
    "Quality Review", "Regulatory Review", "Consult Supplier",
    "Execute Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Adjust Parameters",
    "Release Material", "Archive Case",
}
for name, net in [("ProcessTree", net_pt), ("POWL", net_powl)]:
    vis_labels = [t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None]
    tau_transitions = [t for t in net.transitions if t.label is None]
    label_counts = Counter(vis_labels)
    assert set(vis_labels) == expected_labels, f"{name}: missing or extra labels"
    assert all(c == 1 for c in label_counts.values()), f"{name}: duplicate visible labels found: {label_counts}"
    assert len(tau_transitions) > 0, f"{name}: expected silent routing transitions"

# 4. Bounded / Sampled Playout Invariant Verification
playout_log = pm4py.play_out(net_powl, im_powl, fm_powl)
for trace in playout_log:
    acts = [e["concept:name"] for e in trace]
    assert acts[0] == "Register Deviation" and acts[-1] == "Archive Case"
    assert acts.count("Consult Supplier") <= 1
    # Verify silent transitions never bypass mandatory trial cycle or approval route
    assert acts.count("Execute Trial") >= 1
    assert acts.count("Execute Trial") == acts.count("Inspect Trial")
    assert acts.count("Adjust Parameters") == acts.count("Execute Trial") - 1
    has_low = "Supervisor Review" in acts
    has_high = ("Quality Review" in acts) and ("Regulatory Review" in acts)
    assert has_low ^ has_high

# 5. Explicit Positive and Negative Witness Tests (T1 - T8)
witness_traces = {
    "T1": ["Register Deviation", "Contain Material", "Analyze Root Cause", "Plan Verification", "Design Corrective Action", "Supervisor Review", "Execute Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Material", "Archive Case"],
    "T2": ["Register Deviation", "Analyze Root Cause", "Contain Material", "Design Corrective Action", "Plan Verification", "Execute Trial", "Regulatory Review", "Inspect Trial", "Adjust Parameters", "Execute Trial", "Quality Review", "Inspect Trial", "Release Material", "Archive Case"],
    "T3": ["Register Deviation", "Contain Material", "Analyze Root Cause", "Plan Verification", "Design Corrective Action", "Consult Supplier", "Execute Trial", "Supervisor Review", "Inspect Trial", "Release Material", "Archive Case"],
    "T4": ["Register Deviation", "Analyze Root Cause", "Consult Supplier", "Supervisor Review", "Contain Material", "Plan Verification", "Design Corrective Action", "Execute Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Material", "Archive Case"],
    "T5": ["Register Deviation", "Contain Material", "Analyze Root Cause", "Quality Review", "Plan Verification", "Consult Supplier", "Design Corrective Action", "Execute Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Adjust Parameters", "Regulatory Review", "Execute Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Material", "Archive Case"],
    "T6": ["Register Deviation", "Contain Material", "Analyze Root Cause", "Design Corrective Action", "Execute Trial", "Plan Verification", "Inspect Trial", "Supervisor Review", "Release Material", "Archive Case"],
    "T7": ["Register Deviation", "Contain Material", "Analyze Root Cause", "Plan Verification", "Design Corrective Action", "Supervisor Review", "Execute Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Adjust Parameters", "Release Material", "Archive Case"],
    "T8": ["Register Deviation", "Contain Material", "Analyze Root Cause", "Plan Verification", "Design Corrective Action", "Quality Review", "Execute Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Material", "Regulatory Review", "Archive Case"],
}
witness_logs = traces_from_dict(witness_traces)

expected_verdicts = {
    "T1": (True, True),
    "T2": (True, True),
    "T3": (True, True),
    "T4": (False, True),  # Exposes Process-Tree conservative approximation
    "T5": (False, True),  # Exposes Process-Tree conservative approximation
    "T6": (False, False), # Violates cross-branch Plan Verification -> Execute Trial
    "T7": (False, False), # Violates loop exit (Adjust Parameters after final Inspect Trial)
    "T8": (False, False), # Violates Release Material synchronization on Regulatory Review
}

for tid, (exp_pt, exp_powl) in expected_verdicts.items():
    act_pt = is_trace_accepted(witness_logs[tid], net_pt, im_pt, fm_pt)
    act_powl = is_trace_accepted(witness_logs[tid], net_powl, im_powl, fm_powl)
    assert act_pt == exp_pt, f"{tid} ProcessTree mismatch: got {act_pt}, expected {exp_pt}"
    assert act_powl == exp_powl, f"{tid} POWL mismatch: got {act_powl}, expected {exp_powl}"
```

### Limitations of the Validation Plan
1. **Unbounded state space of the trial loop**: Because `Operator.LOOP` can iterate arbitrarily many times, any finite sample from `pm4py.play_out` covers only a subset of possible loop unrollings and interleavings.
2. **Stochastic playout coverage**: Random playout (`pm4py.play_out`) chooses enabled transitions uniformly at random and may under-sample rare interleavings (e.g., `Consult Supplier` occurring between the 2nd and 3rd trial iterations while `Quality Review` and `Regulatory Review` straddle `Plan Verification`).
3. **Absence of data/guard validation**: Converting to a classical Place/Transition Petri net validates control-flow ordering and structural soundness, but cannot verify data-aware business rules (e.g., checking that a case flagged `risk_level == "HIGH"` in the database did not take the `Supervisor Review` path, or that `Adjust Parameters` was only skipped when `inspection_result == "PASS"`).
4. **Atomic event abstraction vs. interval overlap**: The validation plan checks completion-order interleavings rather than interval timestamps (`start_timestamp`, `complete_timestamp`), so it cannot detect if an activity *started* prematurely before its prerequisite completed unless it also *completed* prematurely.

# 8. Final Self-Critique

1. **Semantic Weakness — Atomic Completion Events vs. True Interval Concurrency**: Following Rule 11, both models treat activities as atomic completion events. In a real manufacturing environment where activities have non-zero durations, an activity $B$ that is only allowed to *start* after $A$ *completes* ($A \prec B$) requires interval or life-cycle events (`start`, `complete`) to enforce completely. Under completion-only semantics, if $B$ illegally starts *before* $A$ completes but happens to finish *after* $A$ completes, the completion trace $\langle \dots, A, B, \dots \rangle$ is still accepted by both `powl` and `tree`.
2. **Semantic Weakness — Data-Blind Non-Determinism in XOR and Loop Guards**: Because control-flow models abstract away case attributes and inspection outcomes, the risk choice (`Supervisor Review` vs. `Quality Review || Regulatory Review`), the supplier consultation choice (`Consult Supplier` vs. $\tau$), and the trial loop exit condition are modeled as purely non-deterministic choices. Consequently, the models cannot enforce data-level consistency—for instance, preventing a high-risk deviation from routing through `Supervisor Review`, or preventing a trial loop from re-executing `Execute Trial` without re-evaluating `Design Corrective Action` when field parameters alone are insufficient to fix a flawed corrective action design.
3. **Representational Trade-off in the Process-Tree Approximation**: By adopting a strict conservative under-approximation without label duplication for `tree`, we preserved 100% precision and 1-to-1 label identity, but accepted a recall penalty on valid traces where `Supervisor Review`, `Quality Review`, `Regulatory Review`, or `Consult Supplier` complete before `Contain Material`, `Plan Verification`, or `Design Corrective Action` (as demonstrated in Witnesses T4 and T5). If the downstream application were purely fitness-based conformance auditing rather than process simulation or discovery, an over-approximating Process Tree (placing `Contain Material -> Plan Verification` in parallel with everything after `Analyze Root Cause` except `Release Material`) would avoid false-negative fitness penalties on T4 and T5, albeit at the unacceptable safety cost of allowing `Execute Trial` to run before `Contain Material` and `Plan Verification` (admitting forbidden trace T6).
