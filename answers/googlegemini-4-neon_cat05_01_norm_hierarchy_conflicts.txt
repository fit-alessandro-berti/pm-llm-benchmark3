   - *Financial & Budget-Owner Controls* (A2, B2 Finance_Approve) occupy an intermediate tier. However, because D is an uncontrolled email and ECD-2 lacks CRO A7 ratification and formal budget-owner status for IC, transactions relying on C+D to omit pre-commitment Finance/budget-owner approvals are treated as **justified exceptions** rather than strict baseline compliance with unamended GPIP-4.
2. **Specificity (*Lex Specialis*) Bounded by Jurisdiction:** A more specific rule (e.g., ECD-2 or CFO email) overrides a general rule only within the issuing authority's competence, stated scope, and active time window.
3. **Strict Time-of-Act Evaluation:** Compliance is judged against the normative regime in force at the moment of the legally/operationally binding act—primarily `Commit_to_Supplier` (and pre-commitment prerequisites). Later-in-time directives (e.g., ECD-2 at `07-14 09:00` or CFO email at `07-15 07:20`) do not apply retroactively; expired directives (`07-18 18:00`) cease immediately absent a written, authorized extension.
4. **Purposive Scope of ECD-2:** Between Operations' broad view (all backup-line spares) and Internal Audit's narrow view (<24h only), the defensible test for "critical sterilization-line lots required to contain the current contamination event" is **direct functional necessity to restore or sustain active containment/sterilization capacity within the incident horizon**. Non-production items (cafeteria equipment) are categorically excluded; multi-day backup stock (e.g., 3 days' supply) falls outside immediate containment necessity absent documented imminent depletion.
5. **Evidential Hierarchy & Retrospective Proof:** Real-world act time controls over database ingestion time when credible, contemporaneous external evidence (supplier acknowledgment emails, telephone recordings, signed PDFs with creation metadata) contradicts an ERP timestamp affected by documented outages (ON-44, document-system queues) or clock skew. By contrast, **post-hoc approvals** executed after commitment do not cure a pre-commitment breach (A6), though they reduce severity.
6. **Burden of Proof:** Baseline rules (A and B) presumptively apply. The party invoking an exception (B4 emergency/delegation, ECD-2 coverage or extension, CFO overlay, or outage delay) bears the burden of producing verifiable, contemporaneous evidence. Uncorroborated assertions (such as an unrecorded verbal extension) fail.

### Decision Method & Classification Definitions
Each case is evaluated sequentially: (1) establish real-world event timeline from best evidence; (2) identify applicable normative regime at `Commit_to_Supplier`; (3) test scope and threshold triggers; (4) test core integrity controls (A1, A3), then financial controls (A2, B2, D1–D2), then operational/recording controls (B1, B3, B4, B6, C1–C3); (5) classify:
- **COMPLIANT:** Satisfies all applicable baseline norms (A and B) at the time of action without relying on unratified policy overlays.
- **JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION:** Departs from baseline GPIP-4/SOP mechanics (specifically relying on unratified ECD-2 and/or informal CFO Finance interpretation, or suffering a non-substantive system ingestion lag), *only where* (a) the act fell within the directive's active window and defensible scope, (b) all core integrity controls (A1, A3) and active emergency conditions (C3, D2) were satisfied pre-commitment, and (c) substantive control intent was preserved.
- **NONCOMPLIANT:** Breaches a non-derogable core integrity control (A1, A3), acts outside the temporal or substantive scope of applicable emergency exceptions, violates explicit conditions of the invoked exception (e.g., reachable Plant Manager under B4, missing joint IC auth under C3), or lacks pre-commitment approvals under baseline rules without an active, valid overlay.
- **INDETERMINATE:** Decisive facts necessary to determine whether a controlling rule was satisfied or breached cannot be established from the record, and neither side's burden of proof resolves the ambiguity.

## 2. Defensible Norm Hierarchy

Because no single total ordering validly resolves conflicts across board policy, local SOPs, executive incident directives, and functional Finance interpretations, precedence is structured **by control dimension**:
1. **Core Integrity & Compliance Controls (A1 SoD; A3 Compliance_Clearance):** `A (GPIP-4)` > all other sources unless `CRO A7` exception exists.
2. **Local Supplier Register vs. Global Vendor Compliance:** `C2` overrides `B3` (local register) during ECD-2, but **never** overrides `A3` (global new-supplier Compliance_Clearance).
3. **Financial & Budget-Owner Approvals (>= EUR 25k / 50k / 150k):** Baseline `A2` + `B2`; during `07-15 07:20` to `07-18 18:00` within ECD-2 scope, `D1–D2` (backed by CFO D3 authority) + `C3` act as a *defensible operational overlay* mitigating `A2`/`B2-Finance`, subject to CRO/Board governance gap.
4. **Operational Workflow Ordering & Local Emergency Substitution:** Within ECD-2 window/scope, `C1`/`C3` override `B1` and `B2-Plant` (via `B7` and precedent). Outside ECD-2, `B4` overrides `B1` and `B2-Plant` only when strict B4 conditions hold.

6. overrides_for(po_before_commit, Local_Emergency_6h, Any_Time) :- imminent_safety_or_stoppage_6h, recorded_within_24h(Emergency_Form) unless (Plant_Manager_Reachable and Substituted_By_ShiftLead).

8. justified_exception(Case) :- violates_baseline_only(A2_or_B1_or_B2_or_B3), overrides_for(Relevant_Issue, Scope, T_real_commit), not violates(R_A1), not violates(R_A3), supported_by(Contemporaneous_Records).

10. post_hoc_approval(Event) -> mitigates_severity(Case) and not cures_breach(Event).

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

1. **Case P-508 — Alternative Classification: `COMPLIANT` (instead of `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`)**
   - *Premise that must change:* Treat Northbridge's prior organizational practice (treating signed incident directives as temporary policy overlays) combined with the CFO's explicit Finance-control authority (D3) as creating an *ex ante* valid emergency norm set during CRO unavailability—meaning ECD-2 + CFO email legally satisfied A7 and A2 rather than merely excusing a departure from them.
   - *Strength evaluation:* **Highly credible.** Operationally, BUY-8 and Incident Command executed every control required by the active executive guidance plus board-level SoD and Compliance rules. The only reason `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` remains slightly stronger is textual discipline: A7 reserves formal exceptions to the CRO (who never ratified ECD-2), D was an uncontrolled email, and IC members are not formally designated as budget owners. *(Conversely, a strict formalist reading of A7—holding that zero Finance approvals can ever be waived without a CRO signature—would flip P-508, P-507, and P-505 to `NONCOMPLIANT`, though that view is weaker because it ignores the CFO's explicit D3 authority and organizational precedent.)*

2. **Case P-505 — Alternative Classification: `INDETERMINATE` (instead of `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`)**
   - *Premise that must change:* Apply a strict evidential-completeness rule requiring affirmative documentary proof of *every* mandatory step (`Create_PO`, actor identities for R-A1 SoD, and R-C3 4-hour recording timestamp) before granting `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`, rather than treating unrecorded metadata in the case extract as non-dispositive pending audit verification.
   - *Strength evaluation:* **Credible and disciplined.** Unlike P-507 and P-508, P-505's log omits `Create_PO` altogether (C1 permits *deferring*, not *omitting*, a PO), leaves SoD actors unnamed, and states only that the ERP clock "may" have been 9 minutes fast. However, because the `17:59:31` supplier acknowledgment email independently proves pre-`18:00` commitment and the team obtained both `Compliance_Clearance` (`17:40`) and `Finance_Approve` (`19:00`), `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` with `Medium` confidence better reflects the weight of known evidence while flagging missing logs for retrieval. *(A second alternative—classifying P-505 `NONCOMPLIANT` by treating the `18:06` ERP entry as the controlling commit timestamp—is **weak**, because it elevates a skewed database log over contemporaneous external contract formation.)*

3. **Case P-501 — Alternative Classification: `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` (instead of `NONCOMPLIANT`)**
   - *Premise that must change:* Interpret ERP outage ON-44 (`08:30–13:00`) plus the imminent 4-hour sterilizer stoppage as creating practical impossibility for formal pre-commitment approvals, and treat same-day ratification by both Plant Manager (`15:10`) and Finance (`15:12`)—alongside a timely 23-hour emergency form—as substantially curing the missing formal delegation of SHIFT-1.
   - *Strength evaluation:* **Moderately credible on risk grounds, but weak normatively.** Spend was under GPIP-4's EUR 50k threshold, the supplier was approved, and SoD held. However, the scenario explicitly establishes that the Plant Manager was *reachable* and *did not formally delegate SHIFT-1*. Because B4 explicitly conditions Shift Lead substitution on an *absent* Plant Manager and *active* delegation—and an ERP outage does not prevent a phone call—excusing P-501 would nullify clear local emergency boundaries.

4. **Case P-502 — Alternative Classification: `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` (instead of `NONCOMPLIANT`)**
   - *Premise that must change:* Adopt an expansive *lex specialis* reading of ECD-2 C2 and C4 ("takes precedence over ordinary purchasing timing and supplier-onboarding requirements"), treating global GPIP-4 `Compliance_Clearance` (A3) as a "supplier-onboarding requirement" temporarily deferred until post-commit (`18:30`) for urgent same-day sterile filters.
   - *Strength evaluation:* **Weak.** While operationally sympathetic (sterile filters were needed the same day and Compliance cleared the vendor 8 hours later), this reading contradicts four converging constraints: C5 (ECD-2 is silent on Compliance and GPIP-4), organizational precedent (incident directives have *never* overlaid new-supplier Compliance clearance), D3/D4 (integrity controls untouched), and A4/A6 (post-hoc clearance does not cure missing pre-commitment clearance).

5. **Case P-507 — Alternative Classification: `NONCOMPLIANT` (instead of `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`)**
   - *Premise that must change:* Either (a) adopt strict board-policy formalism holding that an uncontrolled CFO email (D1) cannot waive GPIP-4 A2's pre-commitment Finance Controller approval for a EUR 110,000 purchase without CRO A7 ratification, or (b) interpret C3's "recorded within four hours" strictly as *recorded in the ERP system of record* (`14:30 > 12:55/13:00`).
   - *Strength evaluation:* **Split credibility.** Premise (a) is genuinely credible from a strict board-governance audit standpoint (and would uniformly flip P-505, P-507, and P-508), though it underweights the CFO's explicit Finance framework authority (D3) during CRO unavailability. Premise (b) is **weak**: a signed PDF created at `12:40` (`3h45m` after authorization) satisfies substantive recording within 4 hours; penalizing an involuntary document-system outage queue confuses an IT ingestion defect with a compliance breach.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

### Draft Replacement Clause: GPIP-4 Clause A7 (Revised) — Norm Hierarchy, Emergency Exception Governance, and Anti-Normalization Safeguards

> **A7.1 Norm Hierarchy & Non-Derogable Controls.** GPIP-4 governs all Northbridge entities. Local SOPs may tighten, but never weaken, GPIP-4. Under **no** circumstances—including active emergencies—may any directive, SOP, or executive email waive: (a) **Segregation of Duties (A1)** (no requester or committer may act as an approver or co-authorizer of the same purchase), or (b) **Pre-Commitment Compliance Screening (A3)** for purchases `> EUR 100,000` or any **new global supplier**. Where a corporate incident directive waives a *local approved-supplier register*, Compliance shall provide an expedited **2-hour Emergency Compliance Fast-Track** rather than a waiver.
>
> **A7.2 Issuing Authority & CRO Succession.** A formal policy exception or Corporate Incident Procurement Directive (CIPD) must be written, scoped, time-limited, and entered into the Controlled Policy Repository prior to taking effect. Authority rests with the **Chief Risk Officer (CRO)**. If the CRO is documented unavailable for `> 2 hours` during a declared Category-1 incident, a temporary CIPD may be jointly signed by at least two of the **COO, CFO, and General Counsel**, subject to mandatory CRO (or Audit Committee Chair) written ratification within **72 hours**; unratified directives automatically lapse at hour 72. Ordinary emails (including from executive officers) are strictly interpretive and **cannot** create, modify, or waive policy thresholds unless codified in a signed CIPD.
>
> **A7.3 Objective Scope.** A CIPD applies solely to goods or services strictly required to prevent imminent physical safety harm, contain an active contamination/regulatory event, or avoid a production stoppage expected within **24 hours**. Non-production site amenities (e.g., administrative or cafeteria facilities) and multi-day buffer/backup inventory (`> 24 hours` on-hand stock) are categorically excluded unless individually itemized in the CIPD schedule.
>
> **A7.4 Harmonized Emergency Thresholds.** During an active CIPD, `Commit_to_Supplier` may precede `Create_PO` (PO required within 24 hours), and approvals follow a single tiered matrix (all pre-commitment, preserving A1 SoD):
> - `<= EUR 25,000`: One independent Incident Command (IC) member (acting as delegated budget owner).
> - `> EUR 25,000` to `EUR 150,000`: Two distinct independent IC members (explicitly empowered as temporary budget owners by the CIPD) **plus** an assigned Incident Finance Lead (designated in writing by the CFO).
> - `> EUR 150,000`: All controls above **plus** pre-commitment `Legal_Clearance` and `Compliance_Clearance`.
>
> **A7.5 Retrospective Records & Evidential Hierarchy.** Verbal pre-commitment authorizations under a CIPD must be recorded in a timestamped system or signed PDF within **4 hours**. During documented IT/ERP outages, contemporaneous external or cryptographically timestamped offline evidence (e.g., supplier acknowledgments, recorded lines, signed PDFs) governs real-world act time over delayed ERP ingestion logs. Post-hoc approvals executed *after* `Commit_to_Supplier` must be logged as **mitigation only** and never cure a pre-commitment breach.
>
> **A7.6 Hard Sunset & Prohibition of Verbal Extensions.** Every CIPD expires automatically at its stated UTC/local timestamp, at which point ERP workflows automatically revert to baseline controls. **Verbal extensions are strictly void.** Any extension requires a new written, signed CIPD published to the repository *before* the prior window closes.
>
> **A7.7 Safeguards Against Emergency Normalization.** (a) No CIPD may exceed **72 hours** per issuance; extensions beyond 7 cumulative days require Audit Committee approval. (b) Internal Audit shall perform a **100% post-incident review** of all CIPD-coded and out-of-register purchases within 10 business days of expiry. (c) If emergency-coded spend at any site exceeds **5% of monthly site procurement spend** or triggers **more than two directives within 90 days**, automatic escalation to the CRO and CFO is mandated to address root-cause operational or planning failures.
