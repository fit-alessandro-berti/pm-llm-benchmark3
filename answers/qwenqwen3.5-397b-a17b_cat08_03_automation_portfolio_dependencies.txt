# 7. Final recommendation

**Recommended Portfolio:** **A3 (KYC Pre-classifier), A4 (Entity Mismatch Assistant), A5 (Missing-doc Checklist), A6 (Financial Extraction).**

**Why this portfolio?**
I recommend **against** automating the highest-frequency activity (A1: Register Application) as the first priority, despite management's initial inclination. Process-mining evidence demonstrates that **frequency is a misleading proxy for value** in this context.

1.  **Synchronization Dominates Frequency:** While "Register Application" (A1) occurs 11,880 times with only 1.5h wait, "Resolve Entity Mismatch" (A4) occurs only 1,750 times but creates a **31.0h waiting time** and blocks the critical path in 82% of cases when present. Automating A1 without fixing A4 simply floods the bottleneck, increasing work-in-progress without improving end-to-end cycle time.
2.  **Control Prerequisites:** Compliance mandates a control-strengthening module. **A3 (KYC Pre-classifier)** satisfies this while also reducing the 4.0h KYC wait. Automating downstream tasks like Signing (A10) or Contracts (A9) before securing KYC (A3) risks amplifying the existing 6.5% KYC-violation rate.
3.  **Resource Network Balance:** Credit Analysts are central in the social network but at risk of overload. **A6 (Financial Extraction)** improves their efficiency directly, whereas A1 would increase their inflow without support.
4.  **Integration Constraints:** This portfolio fits the **two-deep-integration limit** (A3 and A6) in the first year while delivering high-impact process changes.

**Trade-off Argument:**
This portfolio sacrifices the immediate, visible labor savings of high-frequency tasks (A1, A9) for **structural process improvement**. By targeting the **31h Entity Mismatch wait** and the **52% Missing Document rework loop**, we reduce the end-to-end cycle time more effectively than by shaving minutes off the registration step. This approach respects the **Control First** mandate and prevents the "automation of chaos" where faster upstream intake overwhelms constrained downstream resources (Compliance Specialists and Credit Analysts).
