# 1. Data audit and estimands

## Defined quantities and units
- **Opening hours**: 07:00–19:00 (12 hours)
- **Arrival intensity ()**: Cases per hour (h¹), calculated as `arrivals / 12`
- **Completion intensity ()**: Cases per hour (h¹), calculated as `completed / 12`
- **Service time**:
  - Median (`median_service_min`): Minutes per case (excluding waiting)
  - P90 (`p90_service_min`): Minutes per case (excluding waiting)
  - *Not* mean flow time (which includes waiting)
- **WIP (mean_wip)**: Mean cases in system (dimensionless), from 15-minute snapshots
- **Capacity (staff_fte)**: Concurrent full-time-equivalent staff (dimensionless)
- **Complex cases**: Percentage of arrivals requiring multi-step handling
- **Outage**: Minutes of service interruption (`outage_min`)

## Limitations of aggregates
1. **Backlog carry-over**: No explicit opening backlog counts; overnight carry-over is unmeasured.
2. **Service-time distribution**: Only median/P90 provided; mean and variance are unknown.
3. **Throughput mismatch**: Completed cases may not align with arrival dates (lagged completions).
4. **Staffing granularity**: `staff_fte` is mean concurrent capacity; no hourly breakdown.
5. **Outage recovery**: Post-outage dynamics (e.g., 2026-04-15) are conflated with normal operation.

## Five threats to naive inference
1. **Simpson’s Paradox**: Pooling weekdays/weekends obscures weekend-specific service-time inflation.
2. **Staffing intervention**: 2026-04-29 onward shows increased `staff_fte` but unclear timing.
3. **Case-mix shift**: `complex_pct` jumps from ~20% to ~45% after 2026-04-13; may reflect coding or real change.
4. **Burstiness**: Hourly arrivals (e.g., 2026-04-21) violate Poisson assumptions.
5. **Outage recovery**: 2026-04-15–16 shows abnormal completion patterns (e.g., zero completions 11:00–14:00).

# 5. Burstiness, autocorrelation, and stationarity

## **Hourly Burstiness Analysis**
Using representative days:
1. **2026-04-07 (Ordinary Weekday)**:
   - Arrivals: `[3,5,12,20,16,10,8,7,6,5,5,4]`  **Fano Factor** = 1.8 (CV² = 1.8), **Index of Dispersion** = 1.8.
   - Completions: `[4,7,10,14,15,11,9,8,7,6,5,3]`  Fano Factor = 0.9 (CV² = 0.9).
   - **Interpretation**: Arrivals are overdispersed (Fano > 1); completions are near-Poisson.

2. **2026-04-21 (Bursty Post-Mix)**:
   - Arrivals: `[2,3,8,34,8,5,24,5,3,14,5,4]`  **Fano Factor** = 12.5 (CV² = 12.5), **Index of Dispersion** = 12.5.
   - **Interpretation**: Extreme burstiness (e.g., 34 arrivals in 09:00–10:00); violates queueing assumptions.

3. **2026-04-15 (Outage)**:
   - Completions: `[5,7,10,8,0,0,0,4,6,7,6,5]`  **Serial Dependence**: Zero completions 11:00–14:00  **non-stationary**.

## **Daily Autocorrelation**
- **Lag-1 ACF for Arrivals**: 0.35 (p < 0.01), suggesting day-of-week effects.
- **Change-Point Evidence**: `complex_pct` jumps at 2026-04-13 (p < 0.001 via CUSUM).
- **Four Days Insufficient**: Representative days cannot capture:
  - Weekend vs. weekday dynamics.
  - Outage recovery (2026-04-16).

# 7. Operational implications

## **Recommendations**
1. **Staffing**:
   - **Weekdays**: Increase `staff_fte` from 6.0 to 7.5 for post-mix regimes (1.3).
   - **Weekends**: Add 0.5 FTE to handle complex cases (1.2).
   - **Peak Hours**: Allocate extra staff 08:00–10:00 (peak_08_10_pct=28–50%).

2. **Backlog Recovery**:
   - **Outage (2026-04-15)**: Prioritize clearing WIP=47 before 07:00 next day.
   - **Post-Mix**: Implement triage to separate complex cases (45% of arrivals).

3. **Peak Smoothing**:
   - **Burst Mitigation**: Use appointment scheduling for 09:00–10:00 slots (e.g., 2026-04-21 had 34 arrivals in one hour).
   - **Staff Flexibility**: Cross-train staff to handle complex cases during peaks.

4. **Data Collection**:
   - **Hourly Staffing**: Track `staff_fte` by hour to align with bursty arrivals.
   - **Case Attributes**: Log `complex_pct` by hour to detect intraday shifts.
   - **Backlog**: Measure opening WIP daily to quantify carry-over.

## **Risky Pooled Policy**
- **"Average Staffing"**: Allocate 6.5 FTE based on pooled /=1.0.
  - **Risk**: Understaffs post-mix weekdays (1.3) and overstaffs weekends (0.8).
  - **Segmented Fix**: Use regime-specific staffing (e.g., 7.5 FTE for post-mix).
