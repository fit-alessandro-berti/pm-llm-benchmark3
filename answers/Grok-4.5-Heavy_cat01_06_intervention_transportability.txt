Shared causal mechanisms include the structural benefit of concurrent execution of independent verification tasks when both can start after registration: reducing the sum of sequential service times and associated waiting, thereby lowering waiting-before-assessment and overall cycle time. Independence of identity and document data sources supports this at both sites when documents are digital. The AND-join before Assessment is common.

Site-specific mechanisms dominate transport failure risk. At B, originals_required (62%) imposes a hard statutory prerequisite on Document Check that does not exist at A (90% digital). Role specialization and limited specialized capacities replace A’s cross-trained flexibility. The IT_case_lock prevents true simultaneous ownership that A’s system supports. Legal review for 40% of cases introduces capacity constraints and rework loops (Request Supplement) absent at A. Selection nodes S_* capture these differences in case mix, legislation, resources, and IT.

Effect modifiers include originals_required, digital_documents, case_complexity, role_specialization, IT_case_lock, legal capacity, and assessment capacity. These alter both the feasibility of true_overlap and the magnitude of downstream effects on cycle time, rework, and appeals.

A similar control-flow diagram (Register then parallel verifications then Assessment) does not guarantee transportability because the diagram abstracts away data-dependent prerequisites, resource contention, locking semantics, and selection on case attributes. Partial-order structure is necessary but insufficient without matching mechanisms for enabling concurrency and absorbing capacity shifts. Confounding at A further weakens direct effect transfer.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

Modified intervention (segmented, not a full copy of Site A):

**Eligibility rules:** Only cases where originals are NOT legally required (estimated ~38% of volume, digital claims available at registration). For originals-required cases, maintain the current flow (Identity Check and Originals Receipt may already run concurrently after Register; Document Check starts only after Originals Receipt).

**Concurrent tasks:** For eligible cases, after Register create Identity Check and Document Check tasks simultaneously. Both may execute in parallel under dual ownership where the IT system permits.

**Statutory prerequisites remaining mandatory:**  
- Originals Receipt before Document Check when required.  
- Both Identity and Document complete before Assessment.  
- Legal Review for public-sector/high-value cases (40%).  
- Any Request Supplement triggers re-execution of Document Check (and possibly Assessment).

**Queue and WIP controls:**  
- Enforce per-role WIP limits (identity reviewers ≤4–5 open, document ≤6).  
- Priority for eligible parallel cases but protect capacity for originals and legal streams.  
- Optional daily huddles; continuous monitoring for interference.

**Resource responsibilities:** Keep specialized roles. Identity pool handles only Identity; Document only Document. No forced cross-training during pilot. Assessors and Legal remain downstream.

**Handling of legal-review and supplement loops:** Unchanged. Parallelization does not alter Legal entry condition or the supplement path. Track whether earlier verification completion increases Legal queue pressure.

**Logging requirements:** Require explicit real-time start/end timestamps for every task; flag or disallow late back-entry for verification tasks; record eligibility flag and whether true concurrent ownership occurred. Improve event logging so that true_overlap can be measured reliably.

**POWL-style / partial-order description (eligible path):**  
Register ≺ {Identity Check, Document Check} (concurrent) ≺ Assessment ≺ (Legal Review optional with possible Supplement loop) ≺ X(Approve, Reject) ≺ Notify  

Strict partial-order edges for the core eligible fragment:  
- Register < Identity Check  
- Register < Document Check  
- Identity Check < Assessment  
- Document Check < Assessment  

For non-eligible cases the existing partial order is retained: Register ≺ (Identity Check ∥ Originals Receipt) ≺ Document Check ≺ Assessment ≺ \ldots  

This preserves every statutory dependency while enabling concurrency only where legally and operationally feasible.

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

| design element | specification | causal purpose | operational safeguard | process-mining measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| unit of assignment | individual case (or case batch by arrival day within team) | estimate case-level effect of parallelization | avoid team-level contamination where possible | case_id linked event log |
| randomization or phased assignment | stratified randomization within digital/no-originals stratum; phased rollout by team for others if needed | identify causal effect of treatment version vs control | ethics/ops: start with low-risk digital stratum | treatment flag in case attributes |
| eligible strata | primary: no-originals-required + digital documents at Register; secondary: low-complexity; exclude or separate high-value/legal initially | test mechanism where structural parallel is feasible | statutory compliance: never start Doc Check before originals when required | attribute: originals_required, digital_flag, complexity, legal_flag |
| treatment versions | T1: simultaneous creation of Identity Check + Document Check tasks after Register (eligible only); T0: status-quo sequential/as-is | isolate parallelization effect | WIP limit per reviewer; no change to legal path | task creation timestamps; ownership periods |
| primary and secondary outcomes | primary: median cycle time (eligible cases); secondary: p90 cycle, waiting-before-Assessment, true overlap %, rework %, 90-day appeal %, service times by role | measure transportable effect + quality | pre-specified | DFG/performance on filtered logs; alignment of start/end intervals for overlap |
| interference controls | randomize at case level within teams; monitor cross-case resource contention; optional team-level clusters | limit spillover via shared specialized capacity | capacity monitoring dashboards | resource utilization time-series; queue length by role |
| power or minimum-support logic | target ≥800–1000 eligible cases per arm for detecting 3–4 h median difference (based on baseline variance); or sequential analysis | ensure detectable effect size given B volume (~3400/6 mo, ~38% eligible ≈1300) | interim looks | sample size calc from baseline cycle distribution |
| duration | 3–4 months active + 90-day follow-up for appeals | capture steady-state + quality lag | seasonal adjustment | calendar-time event log |
| stop rules | harm: rework or appeal rise >2 pp or p90 deterioration >10 h in treated; fidelity: true overlap <30% despite assignment; capacity: any role utilization >90% sustained | protect operations & quality | real-time alerts | automated KPI monitoring from event log |
| transportability analysis | estimate effects by stratum (digital, complexity, legal); test interaction with modifiers (IT-lock proxy, capacity); compare realized vs predicted true_overlap; negative controls if available | distinguish mechanism transport vs implementation failure; update transport graph | pre-registered analysis plan | stratified process mining + subgroup ATEs |

The pilot separates mechanism failure from implementation failure by first restricting to the stratum where the structural prerequisite for concurrency holds (no originals required, digital documents available). Within that stratum, randomization identifies the effect of simultaneous task creation under B’s actual IT, roles, and capacities. True overlap is measured via overlapping ownership intervals and timestamps (improved logging required), not merely task creation. If assigned parallel cases show low true overlap, the failure is implementation (IT lock, workarounds, or discipline), not mechanism. If high true overlap occurs but cycle-time or quality gains fail to materialize, or bottlenecks shift (assessment/legal queues rise), the mechanism does not transport because of effect modifiers (capacity, specialization, downstream loops). Stratified analysis and pre-specified interactions with selection nodes quantify which modifiers bind. Stop rules and fidelity metrics prevent scaling a broken implementation. Secondary outcomes on rework/appeals protect against quality degradation. This design therefore tests both “can we create true concurrency here?” and “does true concurrency reduce cycle time without harming quality under B’s constraints?”, providing the evidence needed to update transportability claims before wider rollout.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator, ProcessTree
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.conversion.process_tree import converter as pt_converter
from pm4py.objects.conversion.powl import converter as powl_converter

# ---------- 1. Site A before (sequential) ----------
# -> (Register, Identity Check, Document Check, Assessment, X(Approve, Reject), Notify)
a_before_str = "->('Register','Identity Check','Document Check','Assessment',X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')"
tree_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(a_before_str)
net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_before)

# ---------- 2. Site A after (parallel Identity || Document) ----------
# -> (Register, + (Identity Check, Document Check), Assessment, X(Approve, Reject), Notify)
a_after_str = "->('Register',+('Identity Check','Document Check'),'Assessment',X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')"
tree_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(a_after_str)
net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_after)

# ---------- 3. Site B candidate POWL (eligible digital path + optional legal/supplement loop) ----------
# Transitions
reg = Transition(label="Register")
id_check = Transition(label="Identity Check")
doc_check = Transition(label="Document Check")
assess = Transition(label="Assessment")
legal = Transition(label="Legal Review")
supp = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
approve = Transition(label="Approve")
reject = Transition(label="Reject")
notify = Transition(label="Notify")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()  # for optional legal

# Parallel verification after Register (for eligible cases)
parallel_verif = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[id_check, doc_check])
# no order edges between id_check and doc_check => concurrent

# Core sequential after parallel
# Assessment waits for both (enforced by partial order)
core = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[reg, parallel_verif, assess])
core.order.add_edge(reg, parallel_verif)          # Register before parallel
core.order.add_edge(parallel_verif, assess)       # both verifications before Assessment
# (StrictPartialOrder with nested PO ensures Assessment after both children)

# Optional Legal Review with possible Supplement loop back to Document Check
# Loop body: Legal -> (Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment)* -> exit
legal_loop_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[legal, supp, doc_check, assess])
legal_loop_body.order.add_edge(legal, supp)
legal_loop_body.order.add_edge(supp, doc_check)
legal_loop_body.order.add_edge(doc_check, assess)
# Simplified: use OperatorPOWL LOOP for the rework possibility
# For illustration we construct an XOR of (skip legal) or (legal with possible loop)
xor_legal = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[skip_legal, legal])

# Decision after Assessment / Legal
decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[approve, reject])

# Top-level partial order for eligible path
# Register -> parallel ID||Doc -> Assessment -> (optional Legal) -> Decision -> Notify
top = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[core, xor_legal, decision, notify])
top.order.add_edge(core, xor_legal)
top.order.add_edge(xor_legal, decision)
top.order.add_edge(decision, notify)

# For a fuller model one would also add the non-eligible branch with Originals Receipt,
# but the above captures the candidate concurrent fragment and the legal loop.

# ---------- 4. Convert POWL to accepting Petri net ----------
net_b, im_b, fm_b = powl_converter.apply(top)

# ---------- 5. Soundness / workflow-net checks ----------
print("Site A before is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_before))
print("Site A after is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_after))
print("Site B candidate is workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_b))

# Soundness (may be expensive; use on simplified nets)
try:
    print("A-before soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before))
    print("A-after soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after))
    print("B candidate soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b))
except Exception as e:
    print("Soundness check limited by model size/complexity:", e)

# ---------- 6. Optional playout ----------
print("\nSample traces from A-after:")
for tr in pm4py.play_out(net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after, no_traces=3):
    print([e["concept:name"] for e in tr])

print("\nSample traces from B candidate (eligible path):")
for tr in pm4py.play_out(net_b, im_b, fm_b, no_traces=3):
    print([e["concept:name"] for e in tr])

# ---------- 7. What the model cannot encode ----------
# - Data-dependent legal requirements and originals_required predicates
#   (control-flow is static; eligibility is a case attribute guard not present in pure POWL)
# - Resource capacities, role specialization, and WIP limits
# - Case-locking / single-owner semantics of the legacy IT system
# - Durations, waiting times, and queueing dynamics
# - Stochastic choice of which cases need Legal Review or produce Supplement
# - Timestamp quality / back-entry artifacts
# Consequently the models demonstrate structural feasibility of the partial order
# but cannot predict cycle-time, bottleneck shift, or quality effects.
```

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go**

What should be transferred is the structural insight that independent verification tasks can run concurrently when both are enabled after registration, thereby replacing a sequential sum with a maximum and reducing waiting before Assessment. The supporting mechanisms—data independence of identity and document checks, an AND-join before Assessment, and the value of measuring true overlapping ownership intervals—are portable in principle. What must not be copied is the unsegmented “create both tasks for every case immediately after Register.” At Site B that instruction violates the statutory originals prerequisite for 62 % of cases, ignores specialized role pools, and collides with the legacy single-owner lock.

The first subgroup that should enter is the digital / no-originals-required stratum (approximately 38 % of volume). Within that stratum a randomized or carefully phased pilot can test whether true concurrent ownership is achievable under the existing IT system and whether the resulting reduction in verification wait translates into shorter cycle times without elevating rework or appeals. Expected benefits are modest: an overall median-cycle reduction of 2–4 h (larger inside the stratum) and a corresponding but smaller improvement in the lower part of the tail, provided assessment and legal capacity absorb the earlier arrivals. Risks include (a) illusory overlap caused by lock work-arounds and back-dated events, (b) bottleneck shift that improves the median while lengthening the p90, (c) legal-queue congestion for the 40 % high-value cases, and (d) quality degradation if reviewers are overloaded.

Expansion, modification, or abandonment should be governed by pre-specified evidence: expand if true overlap exceeds 50 %, the eligible-stratum median falls by at least 4–5 h, p90 does not deteriorate, and rework/appeal rates remain stable; modify (add limited cross-training, dual-ownership IT exception, or tighter WIP) if fidelity is low or capacity saturates; abandon or sharply restrict if true concurrency cannot be realized, if downstream queues grow, or if quality metrics worsen by more than the stop-rule thresholds. In short, transfer the partial-order idea and the measurement discipline; do not transfer the unadapted control-flow change or the confounded magnitude observed at Site A.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Assumption** — The share of cases with no legal originals requirement is approximately 38 % and remains stable. **Why needed** — Determines the maximum eligible population for pure Identity∥Document parallelization. **How to test** — Extract the attribute distribution from the current event log and confirm against statutory coding rules. **Consequence if false** — Eligible volume (and therefore overall impact) is smaller or larger than planned; pilot power and expected effect sizes change.

2. **Assumption** — Identity and document checks remain data-independent when both are digital. **Why needed** — Independence is the causal mechanism that makes concurrent execution valuable rather than merely simultaneous. **How to test** — Audit a sample of cases for cross-dependencies or shared external queries; measure service-time correlation. **Consequence if false** — Parallel starts produce contention or rework, eroding or reversing the cycle-time gain.

3. **Assumption** — The legacy case lock can be relaxed or reliably worked around for pilot cases so that true concurrent ownership is observable. **Why needed** — Without simultaneous ownership the treatment cannot produce true_overlap. **How to test** — Instrument dual-ownership intervals and late-entry flags during the pilot. **Consequence if false** — Measured overlap stays near baseline; the pilot detects implementation failure rather than a transportable effect.

4. **Assumption** — Specialized capacity (especially the 3 assessors and 2 legal reviewers) has sufficient headroom to absorb earlier arrivals from the parallel stratum. **Why needed** — Downstream bottlenecks determine whether verification speed-up improves overall cycle time or merely shifts the queue. **How to test** — Monitor role utilization, queue length, and waiting-before-Assessment in real time; compare treated vs control. **Consequence if false** — Median may improve while p90 and legal congestion worsen (bottleneck-shift scenario).

5. **Assumption** — Parallelization does not systematically degrade verification quality or increase supplement rates. **Why needed** — Quality neutrality is required for the intervention to be acceptable. **How to test** — Stratified rework and 90-day appeal rates; targeted quality audits on treated cases. **Consequence if false** — Stop rules trigger; the intervention is abandoned or restricted.

6. **Assumption** — Arrival rates and case-mix composition remain approximately constant during the pilot window. **Why needed** — Avoids confounding the treatment effect with demand or complexity shifts (as occurred at Site A). **How to test** — Track weekly arrivals, complexity flags, and legal flags; include them as covariates. **Consequence if false** — Observed cycle-time changes are partially compositional, weakening causal claims.

7. **Assumption** — Improved real-time timestamp logging can be enforced so that overlap metrics reflect actual concurrent work rather than back-entry. **Why needed** — Reliable measurement of the mediating variable true_overlap is essential for distinguishing mechanism from implementation failure. **How to test** — Compare system clocks with event timestamps; rate of flagged late entries. **Consequence if false** — Fidelity metrics become noisy; learning about transportability is compromised.

8. **Assumption** — Statutory ordering constraints (originals before Document Check, Legal Review for high-value cases) will not be relaxed during the evaluation period. **Why needed** — The intervention is designed around those hard constraints; any change would alter the transportability graph. **How to test** — Confirm with legal and compliance owners; monitor for policy updates. **Consequence if false** — Eligibility rules and the candidate process model must be redesigned; prior pilot evidence becomes less relevant.
