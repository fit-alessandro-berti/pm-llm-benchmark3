# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Demand and service assumptions

**Observation.** The process is `Intake → diagnostic → Validation` with five diagnostic types (M, E, S, ME, ES). All 42 expected base-day cases require Intake and Validation. Priority targets are elapsed-time SLAs: P1 120 min, P2 480 min, P3 1440 min.

**A1 (independence).** Given a two-hour block, case priority and type are independent multinomial draws from that block’s percentages. This is required because the prompt gives only separate margins.

**A2 (service law).** Each activity draw is a two-point mixture of uniforms: with the tabled complex-tail probability use the complex range, otherwise the ordinary range. Draws are independent across activities of a case. Ranges become distributions this way rather than midpoints-only, so tails can threaten P1.

**A3 (speed).** A resource with multiplier \(m\) occupies \(m\) times the base draw. Ineligible cells (`-`) are hard constraints.

**A4 (arrivals).** Arrivals are a piecewise-constant Poisson process with block mean equal to `expected_arrivals` (U1 multiplies 08:00–12:00 means by 1.7). Poisson is an assumption; it supplies clustering that deterministic spacing would hide.

**A5 (U1 mix).** The +8 pp P1 increase in 08:00–12:00 is taken from P2 and P3 in proportion to their original shares.

**A6 (U2 mix).** In 12:00–16:00, E% and ES% double and the five type shares are renormalized to 100%. Arrival counts and service laws are unchanged.

**A7 (U4).** Only diagnostic tail probabilities double (Mechanical, Electrical, Software). Intake and Validation tails stay at base.

**A8 (teams).** Mechanical team = {Ada, Ben}. Electrical/software team = {Chen, Emil}. Deepa and Farah are cross-skilled bridges. The extra +5 team-crossing delay applies when a case moves between those teams unless the same bridge resource retains the case.

**A9 (calendar).** Breaks pause an in-progress activity with no 10% restart penalty. Off-shift gaps are the same. The 10% penalty applies only to P1 preemption of a lower-priority *diagnostic* activity, at most once per case. Overtime is at most 30 min per resource and is used only to finish work already started or to start a P1 that can finish inside remaining OT.

**A10 (open day).** The roster repeats the next morning. Day-2 arrivals are not modeled. Unfinished day-1 work parks overnight (about 20:30→08:00) and is part of elapsed SLA time.

**A11 (utilization).** Planned utilization is busy minutes including OT and resource-occupying handover/setup, divided by shift minus break (not including unpaid night). Planned utilization above 90% for any person is treated as unfair and fragile, as specified.

## How ME/ES parallelism is decided

Decision is made when the first diagnostic branch is assigned, then held (A12).

- Sequential: one branch, then the other; no +8 coordination; critical path is the sum of branches plus waits.
- Parallel: both branches may be queued at once; +8 resource-free coordination before Validation if the branches overlap in time.

Elapsed-time lower bounds at speed 1.0, no waits (mid-mixture means): ME sequential 81.8 min vs parallel 63.5; ES sequential 94.0 vs parallel 69.2. Parallel is shorter on the clock and more expensive in resource-minutes.

## Decision variables

Eligibility (who may do which activity), priority score, P1 reservation, preemption rule, parallelization threshold, overflow wait thresholds, same-resource retention, and skill-family stickiness.

## Two objective formulations

**O1 — tail / SLA risk (primary service objective).**
\[
\Lambda = 4\,(0.90-\mathrm{P1\_SLA})_+ + 2\,(0.90-\mathrm{P2\_SLA})_+ + 0.5\,(0.90-\mathrm{P3\_SLA})_+ + 0.001\,\mathrm{p90\_sojourn} + 0.01\,\mathbb{E}[(\mathrm{sojourn}_{P1}-120)_+].
\]
This penalizes missing the 90% targets, especially P1, and still prices a long tail.

**O2 — fairness and robustness (minimax).**
Let \(s\) run over {base, U1, …, U5}. Minimize the worst-scenario loss
\[
R = \max_s \Big[ 0.40(1-\mathrm{P1\_SLA}_s) + 0.25(1-\mathrm{P2\_SLA}_s) + 0.20(\hat u_s-0.90)_+ + 0.15(\hat u_s-\check u_s) \Big],
\]
where \(\hat u,\check u\) are max and min individual planned utilizations. A policy is **robust** if it is near-optimal on \(R\), not if it wins mean delay on the base day.

A policy that only trims base-case mean delay is rejected if it fails U3/U5 P1 or parks P2 overnight.

## Bottlenecks by scenario

**Base.** Dual bottleneck: (i) evening Software/Validation on Emil+Deepa; (ii) structural daily deficit that parks P3 (and badly routed P2) overnight. Electrical is the largest diagnostic pile (798 min) but has two speed-1 owners (Chen, Deepa) until 16:00/18:00.

Bottleneck migrates to Intake/Mechanical/Electrical *before* lunch; Farah and Chen become the critical path. Afternoon inherits a large unfinished inventory.

**U2 Electrical/software incident.** 12:00–14:00 types renormalize to about M 15.7 / E 40.0 / S 12.9 / ME 14.3 / ES 17.1; 14:00–16:00 to M 14.1 / E 42.3 / S 12.7 / ME 14.1 / ES 16.9. Daily electrical cases rise to ~26.2 (work ~861). The shock is *timing*: E/ES hits 12:00–16:00 when Ada/Ben cannot do Software and Farah is gone. Bottleneck is Chen–Deepa–Emil, then Validation bunching.

**U3 Absence.** Lose Chen’s 450 min, including the only speed-1 electrician present at 08:00 and the only morning software alternative to slow Farah (\(m=1.40\)). Remaining software effective capacity is Emil 450 + Farah 171 = 621 vs 495 speed-1 demand — adequate on a daily sum, inadequate in the morning and whenever Emil is on Electrical/Validation. Bottleneck: morning E/S and all-day Electrical depth.

**U4 Long-tail day.** Diagnostic means become M 29.60, E 37.14, S 44.50 (+12.5%, +13.2%, +15.6%). Diagnostic work +242 min. P1 risk is not only queues: a complex Software draw of 80 min on Chen is 100 min of service before Intake/Validation/handover, which can miss 120 min with *no* queue.

**U5 Combined.** U1 inventory + no Chen + Intake freeze 09:30–10:00. **Estimate:** ~3 Intake requests land during the outage (11.9/4) and hit 10:00 together with the 15.3-case block. Bottleneck starts at Intake, migrates to every diagnostic skill by noon, then to Deepa/Emil after 16:00. This is the worst case for both O1 and O2.

**Hypothesis.** Specialization fails when the shock hits the thin skill (U2, U3). Naive pooling fails by feeding slow multipliers and handover tax on a day that already lacks ~230–500 min. The binding constraints are *calendar shape* and *penalty waste*, not a single daily utilization number.

# 3. Candidate policies

## P-SS — strict specialization, fixed queues

**Eligibility.** One home queue per diagnostic skill.

- Mechanical: Ada then Ben. Deepa/Farah Mechanical only for P1 if both homes are busy (still a specialist exception, not general overflow).
- Electrical: Chen then Deepa; Emil only if both are busy; Ada/Farah Electrical only for P1.
- Software: Farah (08:00–12:00) then Chen as backup; Emil sole primary after 12:00; nobody else.
- Intake: Farah while present; then Ben; then Deepa/Emil after 16:00.
- Validation: last diagnostic resource if qualified, else the home team’s fastest validator (Ben or Emil).

**Priority.** Inside a home queue: P1 by earliest SLA deadline, then P2, then P3, FCFS within class.

**Reservation.** None.

**Preemption.** A P1 may preempt a P2/P3 *on the home specialist of the needed skill* if no home resource is idle and remaining victim work ≥ 8 min. At most one preemption per victim.

**Parallelization.** ME/ES run in parallel only if both homes are idle at branch start; otherwise sequential in the order the homes free. Sequential is the default (avoids +8).

**Handover minimization.** Do not change resource inside a team if the home can continue. No cross-team overflow, so team-cross +5 is rare except on ME/ES sequential handoff.

**Overflow.** None. After 16:00 Mechanical is Deepa only; after 18:00 Mechanical waits until Ada the next day.

## P-FP — full pooling, transparent dispatch

**Eligibility.** Any resource with a finite multiplier.

**Priority score** (operator-auditable; lower is better). When a resource becomes free, score each ready activity
\[
Z = 1000\,\pi + 2.0\,\mathrm{slack} + 0.8\,m\cdot \mathbb{E}[\mathrm{remaining}] + H + F - 20\cdot\mathbf{1}_{\text{same case}} - 8\cdot\mathbf{1}_{\text{same team}},
\]
where \(\pi\in\{0,1,2\}\) for P1/P2/P3, slack is minutes to SLA (negative if late), \(H\) is 7 plus 5 if team-crossing and not a retaining bridge, \(F\) is 5 if this would change the resource’s last diagnostic family. Assign the minimum \(Z\) among eligible pairs. Idle resources do not sit while any eligible P1 exists.

**Reservation.** None.

**Preemption.** If a P1 diagnostic is ready, no eligible resource is idle, slack < 45 min, and a busy resource is on P2/P3 diagnostic, preempt the victim with the smallest remaining work (still ≥ 5 min). One preemption per case.

**Parallelization.** Start the second ME/ES branch as soon as any qualified resource is free (true parallel; pay +8).

**Handover minimization.** Same-case continuation beats a new case of equal priority if the continuation’s \(Z\) is within 15 points.

**Overflow.** Not applicable.

## P-HY — hybrid: reserved P1 capacity and controlled overflow

**Eligibility (home first).**

| resource | home | overflow |
|---|---|---|
| Ada | Mechanical, Intake | Electrical only via trigger |
| Ben | Mechanical, Validation, Intake | none |
| Chen | Electrical | Software via trigger; Intake/Val backup |
| Deepa | Electrical, Validation, Intake | Mechanical via trigger (bridge) |
| Emil | Software, Validation | Electrical via trigger |
| Farah | Intake 08:00–12:00 | any qualified skill via trigger |

**Priority.** Same deadline rule as P-SS inside the eligible set produced below.

**Reservation.** After 14:00, Emil and Deepa may start a P3 only if (a) no P1 is queued or in Intake that they can serve, and (b) the other of {Deepa, Emil} is idle or finishing within 10 min. Before 14:00, Ada may start a P3 only if Chen or Deepa is available for Electrical P1 and Farah or Ben can take Intake. This is the reserved P1 token.

**Overflow triggers.**

- P1: any qualified resource immediately.
- P2: overflow if time already waited at home > 40 min, or slack < 180 min.
- P3: overflow if time waited > 120 min **and** destination projected remaining-shift utilization < 85% **and** no P1/P2 is waiting there.

**Preemption.** P1 preempts P3 on a needed skill whenever no qualified resource is idle. P1 preempts P2 only if P1 slack < 30 min. Never preempt a case that has already been preempted.

**Parallelization.** P1 ME/ES: parallel if a second qualified resource is free or due within 10 min; else sequential on the fastest bridge (Deepa, else Farah, else Ada for ME). P2: parallel only if both homes are idle. P3: always sequential (save the second resource and skip +8).

**Handover minimization.** After Intake, retain the Intake resource for diagnostic if they are qualified and the home queue of that skill is empty. Validation prefers Ben / Deepa / Emil. Skill-family stickiness: do not change diagnostic family unless a P1 overflow requires it or the resource has finished two consecutive cases in the current family.

**Evening protocol (explicit).** 16:00–18:00 Deepa owns Mechanical/ME/Electrical/Validation; Emil owns Software/ES/Electrical/Validation; they parallelize P1 ME/ES; P3 Mechanical after 18:00 is parked for Ada at 08:00.

# 5. Robust recommendation

**Primary policy: P-HY**, scored on **O2** (worst-scenario SLA + fairness) and checked against **O1**.

It dominates P-SS on every shock because overflow and the P1 token exist exactly for the thin skills the roster creates (morning Software, afternoon Electrical, evening two-person shop, Chen’s dual role). It dominates naive pooling because it keeps long Software off Chen/Farah unless a trigger fires, keeps P3 off Emil/Deepa after 14:00, parallelizes only when the clock needs it, and avoids paying +8 and +7 on work that can wait. It is the only one of the three that can plausibly sit near the 90% P1 line on the base day without pushing max planned utilization through 90% as a *plan*.

It does **not** dominate on raw base-case mean delay; leftover volume is mostly structural. Claiming a mean-delay win in the base scenario would be the wrong reason to pick it.

**Fallback mode: P-EM (emergency pooling).** P-FP scoring, plus: freeze new P3 diagnostic starts; mandatory parallel P1 ME/ES; all remaining OT authorized; Farah OT 12:00–12:30; Ada/Ben/Chen OT 16:00–16:30; Intake is the only work allowed to use a free qualified person until the Intake queue is under 10 min.

**Switch triggers (any one fires P-EM for the rest of the shift, review hourly).**

- P1 ready-and-waiting ≥ 2, or any P1 slack < 40 min with no idle qualified resource.
- Intake wait of the oldest case > 15 min, or the 09:30–10:00 Intake channel is down (U5).
- A named resource is absent (U3) or morning arrivals in 08:00–10:00 exceed 11 (≈1.6× base 7).
- Electrical or Software queue (ready work) exceeds 90 min of expected remaining service.

**Revert triggers (all required).** P1 queue empty; oldest Intake wait < 8 min for 30 min; no absent named electrician/software owner; max projected remaining-shift utilization ≤ 90%.

**When another policy is preferable.**

- **P-SS** for a training/audit week, or if unmodeled rework from messy handovers is known to be large (the model prices time, not quality defects).
- **P-FP without reservation** for a multi-day absence or when the type mix is unknown and home lists would be wrong.
- **None of these** if the organization can change the roster. The robust *staffing* move, not just routing, is 90 min of Mechanical coverage after 18:00 and a Software-capable person before 12:00. Routing cannot create Mechanical eligibility after 18:00.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

**Gaming of estimates.** P-FP and P-HY both use expected remaining time. If people inflate estimates, they look like long jobs and (under SERPT-like terms) are delayed, or they attract extra parallelization. Mitigation: use the tabled mixture means by activity×complexity flag, not free-text ETAs. Complexity is a binary draw from historical tail frequency, not a negotiable label.

**P3 starvation.** Strict priority plus a P1 token will park P3 into the overnight tail. That is acceptable under a 24 h SLA **until** a second surge day, when yesterday’s P3 competes with today’s P1. Mitigation: a P3 age-out — if slack < 4 h, promote to P2 overflow eligibility.

**Skill erosion.** P-SS keeps Ada off Electrical and Emil off Mechanical. P-FP does the opposite and spends 1.35–1.40 multipliers. P-HY’s controlled overflow is also the practice mechanism; cap overflow share per person (e.g. Farah diagnostic ≤ 40% of her day) so Intake does not disappear and so Ada does not become a full-time slow electrician.

**Excessive preemption.** The 10% remaining-work restart makes preempting a fat-tail Electrical job expensive. A case may be preempted only once, so a badly timed P1 can freeze a half-finished P2 for the rest of the day. P-HY’s “preempt P2 only if slack < 30 min” is the brake. Log every preemption; if more than one in 15 cases, raise the slack threshold.

**Coordination overhead.** Parallel ME/ES plus two handovers into Validation can add 8+7+7 = 22 min around a P1 that only had ~50–70 min of slack. Sequential on Deepa (ME) or Emil (ES, if she/he is cross enough — Emil is not Mechanical) can be faster *in elapsed time* when the second resource is not actually free. Operators should see the +8 as a real activity, not a footnote.

**New Validation bottleneck.** Faster diagnostic completion, especially under P-FP parallelization, synchronizes 10–18 min Validation jobs into 14:00–17:00 onto Ben/Deepa/Emil. Ada at 1.15 and Farah at 1.20 are poor default validators. Watch Validation queue length as a first-class signal; it is not a residual chore.

**Fairness.** Emil is structurally the evening sink; Farah is structurally the morning generalist. A policy that equalizes utilization by handing Emil Mechanical work he cannot do, or by parking Farah on 1.40 Software all morning, is “fair” on the clock and unfair on the customer. O2 uses utilization spread *and* the 90% fragility cap, not equality for its own sake.

**Interpretability.** P-SS is easiest to audit and weakest under shocks. P-FP’s score \(Z\) is transparent if posted as a five-line card, but people will argue the coefficients. P-HY is a short list of homes, three numeric triggers, and an evening protocol — that is the implementable form. Do not ship an unweighted “AI dispatcher.”

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

## Event attributes (interval log)

Required PM4Py interval fields: `case:concept:name`, `concept:name`, `start_timestamp`, `time:timestamp`, `org:resource`.

Also write, on every interval:

| attribute | purpose |
|---|---|
| `queue_entry_ts` | when the case became ready for this stage |
| `assignment_ts` / `assignment_rule` | when and why this resource was chosen |
| `eligible_resource_set` | who could have taken it (audit of eligibility) |
| `skill_used` | Intake / Mechanical / Electrical / Software / Validation / Handover / Setup / Coordination |
| `planned_service_min` | mixture mean × multiplier at assignment |
| `actual_service_min` | occupied on-shift minutes |
| `pause_reason` | break / off-shift / preempt / outage / none |
| `resume_flag` | restart after pause |
| `preempt_count` | 0/1 |
| `handover_flag` / `team_cross_flag` / `setup_flag` / `parallel_flag` | penalty tags |
| `policy_version` | P-SS / P-FP / P-HY / P-EM |
| `scenario_or_shock_flag` | base / U1…U5 / observed-surge / absence / outage |
| `priority` / `case_type` | P1–P3, M–ES |
| `sla_deadline_ts` / `sla_clock_start` | arrival; immutable |
| `slack_at_assignment` | decision feature for drift |

Handover, Setup, and Coordination are **explicit activities** occupying the receiving resource (Coordination may use `org:resource = Coordinator`). Do not bury them inside diagnostic intervals.

## Deployment indicators (hourly)

P1/P2 SLA hit-rate and minutes-over; oldest wait by stage (Intake / each diagnostic skill / Validation); eligible-set size at assignment; penalty minutes per case; planned vs actual service by resource×skill; individual utilization vs 90%; preemption rate; overnight carry-out count and its priority mix; Farah diagnostic share; Emil after-16:00 mix.

**Important labeling.** PM4Py case-level service/waiting (from `insert_case_service_waiting_time`) is **sojourn split into sum of interval lengths vs the residual**. That residual includes nights, breaks, outages, and resource-free coordination if those are gaps rather than events. It is **not** Intake-queue wait, **not** Mechanical-queue wait, and **not** open-shop delay. Stage waits must be computed from `queue_entry_ts` to `start_timestamp` on the stage’s own intervals, excluding off-shift calendar gaps if the question is “were we slow while open?”

## Which PM4Py analyses show what

- `pm4py.get_service_time` on the interval log, split by `org:resource` and `scenario_or_shock_flag`: **service-time change** and multiplier drift (actual vs planned).
- `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network`: **handover burden**. Filter to `{Intake, Mechanical, Electrical, Software, Validation}` to see case flow between people; include Handover/Setup to see penalty concentration. A rise in Ada→Chen or Farah→Emil edge weight is expected under overflow; a rise in Farah→Farah diagnostic self-loop is skill erosion or dumping.
- Dotted chart / performance spectrum by `policy_version` and hour: **bottleneck migration** (Intake pile at 10:00 under U5; Software/Validation at 16:00 under base; Electrical at 14:00 under U2).
- `insert_case_service_waiting_time` plus a **separate** stage-wait table: if case-level waiting jumps but stage open-shop waits do not, the change is overnight parking, not dispatcher slowness.
- Temporal profile and social-network comparison week-over-week: **drift** in setup (family thrashing) vs genuine demand shift.
- Parallel ME/ES appears as overlapping Mechanical|Electrical intervals on the same case — a working-together pattern, not a handover. Do not interpret that overlap as SNA handover.

#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""
Incident-process policy comparison.
Mechanisms implemented explicitly: mixture-uniform service, speed multipliers,
shift/break/OT calendars, handover +7, team-cross +5, setup +5, ME/ES
parallel vs sequential (+8 coordination iff overlap), P1 preemption with
10% remaining-work restart (max once), U1-U5 demand transforms.

Omitted (not hidden): fatigue, rework/quality, estimate gaming, day-2 arrivals,
within-activity learning, travel, and any correlation between priority and type
beyond block margins.

from __future__ import annotations

import math
import random
from collections import defaultdict, deque
from copy import deepcopy
from datetime import datetime, timedelta
from heapq import heappush, heappop

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

ORIGIN = datetime(2026, 3, 16, 8, 0, 0)
HANDOVER_MIN = 7.0
TEAM_CROSS_MIN = 5.0
SETUP_MIN = 5.0
COORD_MIN = 8.0
PREEMPT_PENALTY = 0.10
OT_CAP = 30.0
DAY = 1440.0

SERVICE = {
    "Intake": {"ord": (5.0, 8.0), "tail": (10.0, 14.0), "p": 0.08, "diag": False},
    "Mechanical": {"ord": (18.0, 28.0), "tail": (35.0, 55.0), "p": 0.15, "diag": True},
    "Electrical": {"ord": (22.0, 35.0), "tail": (40.0, 65.0), "p": 0.18, "diag": True},
    "Software": {"ord": (25.0, 40.0), "tail": (45.0, 80.0), "p": 0.20, "diag": True},
    "Validation": {"ord": (10.0, 18.0), "tail": (22.0, 35.0), "p": 0.12, "diag": False},
}
FAMILY = {"Mechanical": "M", "Electrical": "E", "Software": "S"}

MULT = {
    "Ada": {"Intake": 1.00, "Mechanical": 1.00, "Electrical": 1.35, "Validation": 1.15},
    "Ben": {"Intake": 1.10, "Mechanical": 1.00, "Validation": 1.00},
    "Chen": {"Intake": 1.10, "Electrical": 1.00, "Software": 1.25, "Validation": 1.10},
    "Deepa": {"Intake": 1.00, "Mechanical": 1.25, "Electrical": 1.00, "Validation": 1.00},
    "Emil": {"Intake": 1.10, "Electrical": 1.30, "Software": 1.00, "Validation": 1.00},
    "Farah": {"Intake": 1.00, "Mechanical": 1.40, "Electrical": 1.40, "Software": 1.40, "Validation": 1.20},
}
SHIFT = {
    "Ada": (0.0, 480.0), "Ben": (0.0, 480.0), "Chen": (0.0, 480.0),
    "Deepa": (120.0, 600.0), "Emil": (240.0, 720.0), "Farah": (0.0, 240.0),
}
BREAK = {
    "Ada": (255.0, 285.0), "Ben": (240.0, 270.0), "Chen": (270.0, 300.0),
    "Deepa": (360.0, 390.0), "Emil": (480.0, 510.0), "Farah": None,
}
MECH_TEAM = {"Ada", "Ben"}
ES_TEAM = {"Chen", "Emil"}
BRIDGES = {"Deepa", "Farah"}
ALL_RES = list(MULT.keys())

# start, end, n, p1,p2,p3, m,e,s,me,es  (percents)
BLOCKS = [
    (0.0, 120.0, 7.0, 10, 55, 35, 30, 25, 15, 20, 10),
    (120.0, 240.0, 9.0, 15, 55, 30, 25, 25, 15, 25, 10),
    (240.0, 360.0, 7.0, 12, 58, 30, 22, 28, 18, 20, 12),
    (360.0, 480.0, 10.0, 18, 52, 30, 20, 30, 18, 20, 12),
    (480.0, 600.0, 6.0, 10, 50, 40, 20, 25, 25, 15, 15),
    (600.0, 720.0, 3.0, 5, 45, 50, 25, 20, 30, 10, 15),
]
SLA = {"P1": 120.0, "P2": 480.0, "P3": 1440.0}
TYPES = ["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"]
PRIOS = ["P1", "P2", "P3"]


def mixture_mean(spec, p_scale=1.0):
    p = min(1.0, spec["p"] * p_scale)
    o = 0.5 * (spec["ord"][0] + spec["ord"][1])
    t = 0.5 * (spec["tail"][0] + spec["tail"][1])
    return (1.0 - p) * o + p * t


def draw_base(activity, rng, tail_scale):
    spec = SERVICE[activity]
    p = min(1.0, spec["p"] * tail_scale)
    lo, hi = spec["tail"] if rng.random() < p else spec["ord"]
    return rng.uniform(lo, hi)


def team_of(resource):
    if resource in MECH_TEAM:
        return "mech"
    if resource in ES_TEAM:
        return "es"
    return "bridge"


def scenario_blocks(name):
    rows = [list(b) for b in BLOCKS]
    if name in ("U1", "U5"):
        for i in (0, 1):
            rows[i][2] *= 1.7
            p1, p2, p3 = rows[i][3], rows[i][4], rows[i][5]
            p1n = p1 + 8.0
            rest = 100.0 - p1n
            s = p2 + p3
            rows[i][3], rows[i][4], rows[i][5] = p1n, rest * p2 / s, rest * p3 / s
    if name == "U2":
        for i in (2, 3):
            m, e, s, me, es = rows[i][6:11]
            e, es = 2 * e, 2 * es
            tot = m + e + s + me + es
            rows[i][6:11] = [100 * x / tot for x in (m, e, s, me, es)]
    return rows


def generate_cases(scenario, rng):
    cases = []
    cid = 0
    for start, end, n, p1, p2, p3, m, e, s, me, es in scenario_blocks(scenario):
        k = rng.poisson(n)
        width = end - start
        pri = [p1, p2, p3]
        typ = [m, e, s, me, es]
        for _ in range(k):
            cid += 1
            cases.append({
                "id": cid,
                "arrival": start + rng.uniform(0.0, width),
                "priority": rng.choices(PRIOS, pri)[0],
                "ctype": rng.choices(TYPES, typ)[0],
            })
    cases.sort(key=lambda c: c["arrival"])
    return cases


def is_on_shift(res, t, allow_ot, ot_used):
    tod = t % DAY
    s, e = SHIFT[res]
    br = BREAK[res]
    if s <= tod < e:
        if br and br[0] <= tod < br[1]:
            return False
        return True
    if allow_ot and e <= tod < e + max(0.0, OT_CAP - ot_used):
        return True
    return False


def next_on_shift(res, t, allow_ot, ot_used):
    t0 = t
    # coarse jump over night / before shift
    for _ in range(4):
        tod = t % DAY
        day0 = t - tod
        s, e = SHIFT[res]
        br = BREAK[res]
        if tod < s:
            t = day0 + s
            continue
        if br and br[0] <= tod < br[1]:
            t = day0 + br[1]
            continue
        if s <= tod < e:
            return t
        if allow_ot and e <= tod < e + max(0.0, OT_CAP - ot_used):
            return t
        t = day0 + DAY + s
    return t0 + 3 * DAY


def advance_work(res, start, work, allow_ot, ot_used):
    """Consume `work` on-shift minutes. Returns finish, new_ot_used, segments."""
    if work <= 1e-9:
        return start, ot_used, []
    t = next_on_shift(res, start, allow_ot, ot_used)
    left = work
    segs = []
    seg_s = None
    ot = ot_used
    guard = 0
    while left > 1e-9 and guard < 20000:
        guard += 1
        if not is_on_shift(res, t, allow_ot, ot):
            if seg_s is not None:
                segs.append((seg_s, t))
                seg_s = None
            t = next_on_shift(res, t, allow_ot, ot)
            continue
        if seg_s is None:
            seg_s = t
        step = min(left, 1.0)
        tod = t % DAY
        s, e = SHIFT[res]
        br = BREAK[res]
        if br and s <= tod < e:
            step = min(step, br[0] - tod) if tod < br[0] else step
            if tod < e:
                step = min(step, e - tod)
        elif tod >= e:
            step = min(step, e + max(0.0, OT_CAP - ot) - tod)
            ot += step
        else:
            step = min(step, e - tod)
        if step <= 1e-12:
            t += 0.1
            continue
        t += step
        left -= step
    if seg_s is not None:
        segs.append((seg_s, t))
    return t, ot, segs


def branches_for(ctype):
    return {
        "M": ["Mechanical"],
        "E": ["Electrical"],
        "S": ["Software"],
        "ME": ["Mechanical", "Electrical"],
        "ES": ["Electrical", "Software"],
    }[ctype]


def intake_blocked(scenario, t):
    if scenario != "U5":
        return False
    tod = t % DAY
    return 90.0 <= tod < 120.0  # 09:30-10:00 day 0 (and same clock next days)


class Job:
    __slots__ = ("case", "activity", "work", "ready", "remaining",
                 "preempted_already", "queue_entry")

    def __init__(self, case, activity, work, ready):
        self.case = case
        self.activity = activity
        self.work = work
        self.remaining = work
        self.ready = ready
        self.queue_entry = ready
        self.preempted_already = False


def penalty_minutes(res, job, state):
    h = 0.0
    last = job.case.get("last_resource")
    if last and last != res and job.activity in ("Intake", "Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software", "Validation"):
        h += HANDOVER_MIN
        lt, nt = team_of(last), team_of(res)
        if lt != nt and lt != "bridge" and nt != "bridge" and res not in BRIDGES:
            h += TEAM_CROSS_MIN
        # same bridge retaining: waived (res == last already excluded)
        if last in BRIDGES and res == last:
            h = 0.0
    fam = FAMILY.get(job.activity)
    prev = state["last_family"][res]
    setup = 0.0
    if fam and prev and prev != fam:
        setup += SETUP_MIN
    return h, setup


def eligible(res, job, policy, state, now, absent):
    if res in absent:
        return False
    if job.activity not in MULT[res]:
        return False
    if job.activity == "Intake" and intake_blocked(state["scenario"], now):
        return False
    pri = job.case["priority"]
    waited = now - job.queue_entry
    slack = SLA[pri] - (now - job.case["arrival"])
    if policy == "P-SS":
        home = {
            "Mechanical": {"Ada", "Ben"},
            "Electrical": {"Chen", "Deepa"},
            "Software": {"Emil", "Chen", "Farah"},
            "Intake": {"Farah", "Ben", "Ada", "Deepa", "Emil", "Chen"},
            "Validation": set(ALL_RES),
        }
        extra_p1 = {
            "Mechanical": {"Deepa", "Farah"},
            "Electrical": {"Emil", "Ada", "Farah"},
            "Software": set(),
        }
        allow = set(home[job.activity])
        if pri == "P1":
            allow |= extra_p1.get(job.activity, set())
        # time-of-day software primary
        if job.activity == "Software" and (now % DAY) < 240:
            allow = {"Farah", "Chen"} | ({"Emil"} if pri == "P1" else set())
        if job.activity == "Software" and (now % DAY) >= 240:
            allow = {"Emil", "Chen"} if pri != "P1" else {"Emil", "Chen", "Farah"}
        return res in allow
    if policy == "P-FP":
        return True
    # P-HY
    if pri == "P1":
        return True
    homes = {
        "Mechanical": {"Ada", "Ben"},
        "Electrical": {"Chen", "Deepa"},
        "Software": {"Emil", "Chen", "Farah"},
        "Intake": {"Farah", "Ada", "Deepa", "Ben"},
        "Validation": {"Ben", "Deepa", "Emil", "Chen", "Ada"},
    }
    if res in homes[job.activity]:
        # reservation: Deepa/Emil after 14:00 should not take P3 if they are the token
        if pri == "P3" and (now % DAY) >= 360 and res in {"Deepa", "Emil"}:
            if state["p1_demand_token"]:
                return False
        return True
    # overflow triggers
    if pri == "P2" and (waited > 40.0 or slack < 180.0):
        return True
    if pri == "P3" and waited > 120.0 and not state["p1_demand_token"]:
        return True
    return False


def score(res, job, policy, state, now):
    pri = job.case["priority"]
    slack = SLA[pri] - (now - job.case["arrival"])
    m = MULT[res][job.activity]
    h, setup = penalty_minutes(res, job, state)
    pi = {"P1": 0, "P2": 1, "P3": 2}[pri]
    same = 20.0 if job.case.get("last_resource") == res else 0.0
    same_team = 8.0 if team_of(res) == team_of(job.case.get("last_resource") or res) else 0.0
    z = 1000 * pi + 2.0 * slack + 0.8 * m * job.remaining + h + setup - same - same_team
    if policy == "P-HY" and job.activity == "Validation" and res in {"Ben", "Deepa", "Emil"}:
        z -= 12.0
    if policy == "P-HY" and job.activity == "Software" and res == "Emil":
        z -= 15.0
    if policy == "P-SS":
        z = 1000 * pi + job.case["arrival"] + 0.01 * m * job.remaining
    return z


def decide_parallel(case, policy, idle_skill_ok):
    dual = case["ctype"] in ("ME", "ES")
    if not dual:
        return False
    if policy == "P-SS":
        return idle_skill_ok and case["priority"] == "P1"
    if policy == "P-FP":
        return idle_skill_ok
    # P-HY
    if case["priority"] == "P1":
        return True
    if case["priority"] == "P2":
        return idle_skill_ok
    return False


def run_once(policy, scenario, seed, collect_log=False):
    rng = random.Random(seed)
    absent = {"Chen"} if scenario in ("U3", "U5") else set()
    tail_scale = 2.0 if scenario == "U4" else 1.0
    cases = generate_cases(scenario, rng)
    # state
    waiting = []  # jobs
    active = {}  # res -> dict
    ot_used = {r: 0.0 for r in ALL_RES}
    last_family = {r: None for r in ALL_RES}
    busy_until = {r: 0.0 for r in ALL_RES}
    busy_min = {r: 0.0 for r in ALL_RES}
    events = []
    evq = []
    seq = 0
    for c in cases:
        c.update({
            "last_resource": None,
            "preempts": 0,
            "parallel": False,
            "need": set(branches_for(c["ctype"])),
            "done_diag": set(),
            "base_draws": {},
            "coord_pending": False,
            "finished": None,
            "started_intake": False,
            "val_ready": False,
        })
        for act in ["Intake"] + branches_for(c["ctype"]) + ["Validation"]:
            c["base_draws"][act] = draw_base(act, rng, tail_scale if SERVICE[act]["diag"] else 1.0)
        heappush(evq, (c["arrival"], seq, "arrival", c["id"]))
        seq += 1
    case_ix = {c["id"]: c for c in cases}
    state = {
        "scenario": scenario,
        "last_family": last_family,
        "p1_demand_token": False,
    }

    def log_interval(case, name, res, t0, t1, extra=None):
        if not collect_log or t1 <= t0:
            return
        rec = {
            "case:concept:name": f"{scenario}|{policy}|s{seed}|c{case['id']}",
            "concept:name": name,
            "start_timestamp": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(t0)),
            "time:timestamp": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(t1)),
            "org:resource": res,
            "priority": case["priority"],
            "case_type": case["ctype"],
            "policy_version": policy,
            "scenario_or_shock_flag": scenario,
            "sla_deadline_ts": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(case["arrival"] + SLA[case["priority"]])),
            "sla_clock_start": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(case["arrival"])),
            "skill_used": name,
        }
        if extra:
            rec.update(extra)
        events.append(rec)

    def enqueue_job(case, activity, ready):
        work = case["base_draws"][activity]  # multiplier applied at assignment
        job = Job(case, activity, work, ready)
        waiting.append(job)
        return job

    def refresh_token(now):
        p1_waiting = any(
            j.case["priority"] == "P1" for j in waiting
        )
        p1_active = any(
            info["job"].case["priority"] == "P1" for info in active.values()
        )
        # predicted P1: remaining expected in current block
        state["p1_demand_token"] = p1_waiting or p1_active

    def try_preempt(p1_job, now):
        if p1_job.case["priority"] != "P1":
            return False
        slack = SLA["P1"] - (now - p1_job.case["arrival"])
        if policy == "P-FP" and slack >= 45.0:
            return False
        if policy == "P-HY" and slack >= 30.0:
            # still allow vs P3
            pass
        victims = []
        for res, info in list(active.items()):
            vj = info["job"]
            if vj.activity not in MULT[res]:
                continue
            if p1_job.activity not in MULT[res]:
                continue
            if not SERVICE[vj.activity]["diag"]:
                continue
            if vj.case["priority"] == "P1":
                continue
            if vj.case["preempts"] >= 1 or vj.preempted_already:
                continue
            if policy == "P-HY" and vj.case["priority"] == "P2" and slack >= 30.0:
                continue
            if policy == "P-SS" and not eligible(res, p1_job, policy, state, now, absent):
                continue
            # remaining work: fraction of calendar still to consume
            fin = info["finish"]
            tot_span = max(1e-6, fin - info["start"])
            rem_frac = max(0.0, (fin - now) / tot_span)
            rem_work = rem_frac * info["assigned_work"]
            if rem_work < 5.0:
                continue
            victims.append((rem_work, res, vj, info))
        if not victims:
            return False
        victims.sort(key=lambda x: x[0])
        rem_work, res, vj, info = victims[0]
        # close victim segment
        log_interval(vj.case, vj.activity, res, info["start"], now, {
            "pause_reason": "preempt",
            "planned_service_min": info["assigned_work"],
            "queue_entry_ts": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(vj.queue_entry)),
            "eligible_resource_set": ",".join(sorted(r for r in ALL_RES if r not in absent and p1_job.activity in MULT[r])),
            "assignment_rule": policy,
        })
        vj.remaining = rem_work * (1.0 + PREEMPT_PENALTY)
        vj.preempted_already = True
        vj.case["preempts"] += 1
        vj.queue_entry = now
        waiting.append(vj)
        busy_min[res] += max(0.0, now - info["start"])
        del active[res]
        busy_until[res] = now
        return True

    def assign(res, job, now):
        m = MULT[res][job.activity]
        h, setup = penalty_minutes(res, job, state)
        allow_ot = job.case["priority"] == "P1" or res in active
        # occupy handover/setup on resource
        t = now
        if h > 0:
            fin, ot_used[res], segs = advance_work(res, t, h, allow_ot, ot_used[res])
            for a, b in segs:
                log_interval(job.case, "Handover", res, a, b, {
                    "pause_reason": "none",
                    "planned_service_min": h,
                    "queue_entry_ts": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(job.queue_entry)),
                    "assignment_rule": policy,
                    "handover_flag": True,
                })
                busy_min[res] += b - a
            t = fin
        if setup > 0:
            fin, ot_used[res], segs = advance_work(res, t, setup, allow_ot, ot_used[res])
            for a, b in segs:
                log_interval(job.case, "Setup", res, a, b, {
                    "pause_reason": "none",
                    "planned_service_min": setup,
                    "queue_entry_ts": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(job.queue_entry)),
                    "assignment_rule": policy,
                    "setup_flag": True,
                })
                busy_min[res] += b - a
            t = fin
        assigned = job.remaining * m
        fin, ot_used[res], segs = advance_work(res, t, assigned, job.case["priority"] == "P1", ot_used[res])
        if segs:
            log_interval(job.case, job.activity, res, segs[0][0], segs[-1][1], {
                "pause_reason": "none",
                "planned_service_min": assigned,
                "actual_service_min": assigned,
                "queue_entry_ts": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(job.queue_entry)),
                "eligible_resource_set": ",".join(sorted(
                    r for r in ALL_RES if r not in absent and job.activity in MULT[r]
                    and eligible(r, job, policy, state, now, absent)
                )),
                "assignment_rule": policy,
                "skill_used": job.activity,
                "slack_at_assignment": SLA[job.case["priority"]] - (now - job.case["arrival"]),
            })
            # count on-shift occupied
            busy_min[res] += assigned
        fam = FAMILY.get(job.activity)
        if fam:
            last_family[res] = fam
        job.case["last_resource"] = res
        active[res] = {
            "job": job, "finish": fin, "start": t,
            "assigned_work": assigned,
        }
        busy_until[res] = fin
        heappush(evq, (fin, next_seq(), "finish", res))

    def next_seq():
        nonlocal seq
        seq += 1
        return seq

    def dispatch(now):
        refresh_token(now)
        # P1 preemption attempt
        for job in list(waiting):
            if job.case["priority"] == "P1" and SERVICE.get(job.activity, {}).get("diag"):
                idle_ok = any(
                    r not in active and r not in absent
                    and job.activity in MULT[r]
                    and is_on_shift(r, now, True, ot_used[r])
                    and eligible(r, job, policy, state, now, absent)
                    for r in ALL_RES
                )
                if not idle_ok:
                    if try_preempt(job, now):
                        break
        progress = True
        while progress:
            progress = False
            refresh_token(now)
            idle = [
                r for r in ALL_RES
                if r not in active and r not in absent
                and is_on_shift(r, now, False, ot_used[r])
            ]
            # P1 may use OT start
            if not idle:
                idle = [
                    r for r in ALL_RES
                    if r not in active and r not in absent
                    and is_on_shift(r, now, True, ot_used[r])
                    and any(j.case["priority"] == "P1" and j.activity in MULT[r] for j in waiting)
                ]
            best = None
            for res in idle:
                for job in waiting:
                    if not eligible(res, job, policy, state, now, absent):
                        continue
                    z = score(res, job, policy, state, now)
                    if best is None or z < best[0]:
                        best = (z, res, job)
            if best is None:
                break
            _, res, job = best
            waiting.remove(job)
            assign(res, job, now)
            progress = True

    def on_finish(res, now):
        if res not in active:
            return
        info = active.pop(res)
        job = info["job"]
        case = job.case
        act = job.activity
        if act == "Intake":
            br = branches_for(case["ctype"])
            idle_skill_ok = True
            for b in br:
                idle_skill_ok = idle_skill_ok and any(
                    r not in active and r not in absent and b in MULT[r]
                    and is_on_shift(r, now, False, ot_used[r])
                    for r in ALL_RES
                )
            par = decide_parallel(case, policy, idle_skill_ok)
            case["parallel"] = par
            if par:
                for b in br:
                    enqueue_job(case, b, now)
                case["coord_pending"] = True
            else:
                # sequential: longest expected first on a capable home
                ordered = sorted(br, key=lambda a: case["base_draws"][a], reverse=True)
                case["seq_rest"] = ordered[1:]
                enqueue_job(case, ordered[0], now)
        elif act in FAMILY:
            case["done_diag"].add(act)
            rest = case.get("seq_rest") or []
            if rest:
                nxt = rest.pop(0)
                case["seq_rest"] = rest
                enqueue_job(case, nxt, now)
            elif case["need"] <= case["done_diag"]:
                t_val = now
                if case["coord_pending"]:
                    # resource-free coordination hold
                    log_interval(case, "Coordination", "Coordinator", now, now + COORD_MIN, {
                        "pause_reason": "none",
                        "planned_service_min": COORD_MIN,
                        "parallel_flag": True,
                        "queue_entry_ts": ORIGIN + timedelta(minutes=float(now)),
                        "assignment_rule": policy,
                    })
                    t_val = now + COORD_MIN
                    case["coord_pending"] = False
                    heappush(evq, (t_val, next_seq(), "coord_done", case["id"]))
                    case["val_hold"] = t_val
                else:
                    enqueue_job(case, "Validation", t_val)
        elif act == "Validation":
            case["finished"] = now

    # seed intake
    finished_events = 0
    t_end = 3 * DAY
    while evq:
        now, _, kind, payload = heappop(evq)
        if now > t_end:
            break
        if kind == "arrival":
            c = case_ix[payload]
            enqueue_job(c, "Intake", c["arrival"])
            dispatch(now)
        elif kind == "finish":
            # ignore stale finish events
            if payload in active and abs(active[payload]["finish"] - now) <= 1e-6:
                on_finish(payload, now)
                dispatch(now)
        elif kind == "coord_done":
            c = case_ix[payload]
            if c["finished"] is None and not any(j.case is c and j.activity == "Validation" for j in waiting):
                if not any(info["job"].case is c and info["job"].activity == "Validation" for info in active.values()):
                    enqueue_job(c, "Validation", now)
            dispatch(now)
        # wake dispatch at every block boundary-ish via next resource becoming on shift
        if all(c["finished"] is not None for c in cases):
            break
        # if idle waiting and no events soon, jump to next shift start
        if kind in ("arrival", "finish", "coord_done"):
            if waiting and not active:
                nxt = min(
                    (next_on_shift(r, now, cpri, ot_used[r])
                     for r in ALL_RES if r not in absent
                     for cpri in (False, True)),
                    default=now + 60,
                )
                if nxt > now + 1e-6 and (not evq or evq[0][0] > nxt + 1e-6):
                    heappush(evq, (nxt, next_seq(), "wake", None))
        if kind == "wake":
            dispatch(now)

    # force remaining unfinished as open-end at horizon (failed)
    sojourns = []
    rows = []
    paid = {}
    for r in ALL_RES:
        s, e = SHIFT[r]
        br = BREAK[r]
        paid[r] = (e - s) - (0.0 if not br else br[1] - br[0])
    for c in cases:
        fin = c["finished"] if c["finished"] is not None else t_end
        soj = fin - c["arrival"]
        sojourns.append(soj)
        rows.append({
            "priority": c["priority"],
            "ctype": c["ctype"],
            "sojourn": soj,
            "sla": SLA[c["priority"]],
            "hit": soj <= SLA[c["priority"]],
            "finished_flag": c["finished"] is not None,
        })
    df = pd.DataFrame(rows) if rows else pd.DataFrame(
        columns=["priority", "ctype", "sojourn", "sla", "hit", "finished_flag"]
    )
    utils = {r: busy_min[r] / paid[r] for r in ALL_RES if r not in absent and paid[r] > 0}
    handover_burden = 0.0
    if events:
        evdf = pd.DataFrame(events)
        pen = evdf[evdf["concept:name"].isin(["Handover", "Setup", "Coordination"])]
        if len(cases) > 0 and not pen.empty:
            dur = (pen["time:timestamp"] - pen["start_timestamp"]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0
            handover_burden = float(dur.sum() / max(1, len(cases)))
    metrics = {
        "n": len(cases),
        "mean_delay": float(df["sojourn"].mean()) if len(df) else math.nan,
        "p90_delay": float(df["sojourn"].quantile(0.90)) if len(df) else math.nan,
        "P1_SLA": float(df.loc[df.priority == "P1", "hit"].mean()) if (df.priority == "P1").any() else math.nan,
        "P2_SLA": float(df.loc[df.priority == "P2", "hit"].mean()) if (df.priority == "P2").any() else math.nan,
        "P3_SLA": float(df.loc[df.priority == "P3", "hit"].mean()) if (df.priority == "P3").any() else math.nan,
        "max_resource_utilization": float(max(utils.values())) if utils else math.nan,
        "min_resource_utilization": float(min(utils.values())) if utils else math.nan,
        "utilization_fairness": float(1.0 - (max(utils.values()) - min(utils.values()))) if utils else math.nan,
        "handover_or_setup_burden": handover_burden,
        "finish_rate": float(df["finished_flag"].mean()) if len(df) else 0.0,
    }
    return metrics, events, df

def summarize(reps):
    keys = ["mean_delay", "p90_delay", "P1_SLA", "P2_SLA", "P3_SLA",
            "max_resource_utilization", "utilization_fairness",
            "handover_or_setup_burden", "n", "finish_rate"]
    out = {}
    for k in keys:
        xs = np.array([r[k] for r in reps], dtype=float)
        out[k] = (float(np.nanmean(xs)), float(np.nanpercentile(xs, 10)), float(np.nanpercentile(xs, 90)))
    return out

# 9. Limits and validation plan

**What expected arrivals and service ranges cannot decide.**

- Realized SLA is a function of correlations the prompt does not give (P1 more often ES; complex tail more often at 15:00). Independence (A1–A2) may be wrong in either direction.
- Mid-range vs true distributional shape: a 80-minute software tail on Chen is a P1 failure mode that midpoints hide; the opposite (mass near the ordinary low end) would erase the structural deficit.
- Human behavior: holding a ticket, informal buddy help, declining a cross-skill job, and gaming complexity flags.
- Quality: handover might add defects, not just 7 minutes. Parallel ME might reduce *or* increase rework.
- Absolute SLA percentages in §4 are **bounds/estimates**, not measurements. The ranking P-HY ⪰ P-FP ≫ P-SS on robustness is more credible than any single cell.
- Preemption at most once and OT of 30 min are policy facts here, not empirical.

**Pilot / backtest that can distinguish policies without an unsafe rollout.**

1. **Shadow dispatch (week 1–2), all live work still under the current informal rule, with a hard P1 safety overlay equal to P-HY reservation.** For every assignment, write the eligible set and the action each of P-SS, P-FP, P-HY *would* have taken. Do not auto-execute P-FP/P-HY yet.
2. **Matched historical replay.** Build an interval log from the last 20–30 working days with the attributes in §7. Replay all three policies on the observed arrivals and the observed base-service draws (or residual bootstrap of actual/planned ratios). Hold out the three worst historical mornings as synthetic U1/U5. If the log lacks pause/resume, do not pretend to know preemption effects — mark them omitted.
3. **Restricted live trial (week 3–6).** Alternate P-SS and P-HY by day *within* morning vs afternoon strata, always keeping the P1 token and the P-EM triggers armed. Never trial a day without a Software-capable person after 12:00. Freeze the trial if two P1 cases in a day miss 120 min.
4. **Tabletop U3.** One planned Chen-off day announced in advance, P-HY overflow pre-authorized, P-EM armed. Success is P1_SLA ≥ 0.85 that day and no silent P2 overnight of cases that arrived before 14:00.
5. **Decision rule at the end of the pilot.** Adopt P-HY if it reduces P1 minutes-over-120 and P2 overnight carry-out versus P-SS without raising penalty minutes per case by more than 8, and if operators can explain the last 20 assignments from the five-line card. Keep P-SS as the documented weekend/training mode. Do not adopt P-FP unless the shadow log shows home lists systematically wrong.

The roster hole after 16:00 and the speed-1 deficit versus 2490 minutes will still be there after any routing change. A validation plan that only A/B-tests dispatch and never measures evening Mechanical coverage is asking a policy to create a skill that the shift table does not contain.
