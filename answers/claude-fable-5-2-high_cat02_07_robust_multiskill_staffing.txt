# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

**Reading guide.** Statements are tagged **[O]** observation taken from the prompt, **[E]** estimate (arithmetic or simulation), **[B]** bound, **[H]** hypothesis, **[R]** recommendation. All simulation figures come from the script in Section 8 (40 seeds, common random numbers across policies) and are estimates conditional on the assumptions below, not measurements.

**Where the analysis lands (details in Sections 2, 4 and 5).**
- [E] The supplied roster cannot carry the supplied base demand under any routing policy: 2,721 base minutes of work per day against 2,490 net rostered minutes (load 1.09 at multiplier 1.00, before any overhead), with the shortfall concentrated after 16:00 (load 1.87 and 1.60 in the last two blocks).
- [E] Routing therefore decides who is sacrificed, not whether somebody is. On the given roster a hybrid policy gives the best P1 attainment in every scenario and the lowest weighted SLA shortfall; its ordering rule must be chosen according to the objective (two defensible answers).
- [R] The robust recommendation is the hybrid dispatch rules plus a staffing change. No dispatch rule in the tested family reaches the P2 target on the given roster.

**From ranges to distributions and bounds.** [E] Mixture means in base minutes: Intake 6.94, Mechanical 26.30, Electrical 32.82, Software 38.50, Validation 15.74 (for example Mechanical = 0.85 x 23 + 0.15 x 45). Under U4 the diagnostic means become 29.60, 37.14 and 44.50. [B] If the true means sat at the lower or upper ends of every range, daily base work would be 2,065 or 3,376 minutes instead of 2,721, so the base load ratio is bounded by 0.83 and 1.36. Even the lower end leaves the 16:00-18:00 block at 1.41 and the 18:00-20:00 block at 1.21, so the evening overload does not depend on the uniform assumption.

**How ME/ES parallelism is decided.** [E] Planning arithmetic at mean service times, counted from Intake completion to "ready for Validation":

| case | parallel on two primary resources | sequential on one cross-skilled resource | consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| ME | max(26.3, 32.8) + 7 + 5 + 8 = 52.8 min elapsed; 59.1 resource-min | Deepa: 26.3 x 1.25 + 32.8 = 65.7; Ada: 26.3 + 32.8 x 1.35 = 70.6 | parallel is 13-18 min faster only if the second primary is free within 13-18 min; sequential costs 6.6-11.5 extra resource-min |
| ES | max(32.8, 38.5) + 7 + 8 = 53.5 min elapsed; 71.3 resource-min | Chen: 32.8 + 38.5 x 1.25 = 80.9; Emil: 32.8 x 1.30 + 38.5 = 81.2 | parallel is about 27 min faster if the second primary is free within about 27 min; sequential costs about 10 extra resource-min |

Decision rule used by the hybrid: a P1 case is split whenever the fast-lane rule finds a second resource; for P2/P3 the second branch is offered at once only to a primary resource (multiplier 1.00), is held for the resource working on the sibling branch if that resource is qualified at 1.30 or better, and is released to overflow resources only when slack falls below 60 minutes. Under fixed queues the split is forced; under full pooling it is whatever the free resources produce.

**Objectives.**
- **O1, SLA-risk objective (tail/SLA risk).** L1 = 10 x s(P1) + 3 x s(P2) + 1 x s(P3), where s(k) = max(0, 90% - attainment of class k) in percentage points for cases arriving on the scenario day. Because L1 is blind to how late a breached case is, p90 delay and the unfinished share are reported next to it as tail guards.
- **O2, lateness and fairness objective.** L2 = mean over cases of w x tardiness in hours (w = 10, 3, 1 for P1, P2, P3; tardiness = time beyond the target) + 0.10 x utilisation spread in percentage points (highest minus lowest individual utilisation).
- **Robust form of both.** For each objective the policy value is its maximum regret over {Base, U1, ..., U5}, regret being the loss minus the best loss of any policy on the same roster in that scenario. A policy is called robust if its maximum regret is small, its worst-scenario P1 attainment is the highest or tied, and it does not depend on a queue that becomes unstaffed when one person is absent. The weights 10/3/1 and 0.10 are my choices, not data.

**Decision variables.** Eligibility of each resource for each activity by time of day; the ranking key for waiting work; overflow thresholds (multiplier cap, waiting time, queue length); the form of P1 reservation (none, interruptible, idle); preemption conditions and victim choice; the parallelisation rule; the treatment of cases that can no longer meet their target; and staffing (shift timing and added shifts).

**What the table implies.**
- [E] Mean touch time is 64.8 base minutes per case (2,721 / 42). With all 180 minutes of permitted overtime the ratio is still 1.02 before multipliers, setups and idle fragments.
- [E] The simulated policies all finish about 34-35 cases per day against 42 arrivals, so open cases at close grow by 7-8 per day in Base. With individual utilisation at about 100% of rostered time, that corresponds to about 1.12 resource minutes per base minute (multipliers above 1.00 and setups). Sizing consequence: about 3,050 resource minutes are needed per day, 560 more than rostered at 100% utilisation (about 1.25 shifts) and about 900 more under the 90% rule (two shifts).
- [E] Skill-family balance: Mechanical needs 476 minutes against 900 primary minutes (Ada, Ben), all of them before 16:00. Electrical plus Software need 1,292 minutes against 1,350 primary minutes (Chen, Deepa, Emil), which leaves 58 minutes for the Intake and Validation of their own cases. Software has no primary resource before 12:00 (154 minutes of demand; Chen at 1.25 or Farah at 1.40) and a single one afterwards (Emil: 341 minutes of Software demand in 450 minutes that must also cover Intake, Validation, Electrical after 18:00 and every evening P1). The electrical/software family is the structural bottleneck; Mechanical slack exists but in the wrong hours.
- [O/E] Coverage gaps: no Mechanical-qualified resource after 18:00, only Deepa (1.25) from 16:00; Electrical after 18:00 only at 1.30; one person on site after 18:00.
- [E] Clock effect (A12): a P2 case arriving after about 12:30 has its deadline between 20:30 and 04:00, so it must finish the same evening. Counting from 12:00, that is 13.6 of 22.4 expected P2 cases per day (61%); 4.35 of them (19%) arrive after 16:00. [B] If none of the after-16:00 P2 cases can be finished, P2 attainment cannot exceed 81%.
- [O] The 90% individual-utilisation rule cannot be met by routing: 90% of 2,490 minutes is 2,241, below the 2,721 base minutes required.

# 3. Candidate policies

Three routing policies on the supplied roster (A, B, C), two variants of C used as ablations, and three staffing variants that run C's rules on other rosters. The staffing variants are decision-variable what-ifs, not prompt data; the two hypothetical hires copy Chen's and Ada's multipliers.

| dimension | A_fixed: strict specialisation, fixed queues | B_pool: full pooling | C_hybrid: ownership, P1 fast lane, controlled overflow |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eligibility | Static queue owners: Mechanical = Ben, Ada; Electrical = Chen, Deepa; Software = Farah (08-12), Emil (12-20). A deputy list applies only when no owner of the queue is on shift: Mechanical = Deepa; Electrical = Emil or Ada; Software = Chen. Intake is done by an owner of the case's first skill; Validation by the resource that finished the last branch (the first-skill queue if that person has left). | Any qualified resource may take any task. | Any qualified resource, subject to the overflow and reservation rules below. Intake prefers a resource that is primary (1.00) for one of the case's skills; others may take it after 5 minutes of waiting. |
| Priority score | Class P1, then P2, then P3; inside a class first-come-first-served by case arrival. | P1 first; then earliest SLA deadline (EDD); the least-loaded idle qualified resource takes the task (speed-blind). | Lexicographic bands per task-resource pair: (0) P1; (1) urgent: slack below 60 minutes, or 24 hours past the deadline (max-age guarantee); (2) own-case continuation; (3) primary skill; (4) controlled overflow; (5) lost cases (slack already negative). Inside a band: earliest deadline, then same resource, then lower multiplier. Slack = deadline - now - planned remaining touch time. |
| Reservation | None; each queue's owners are implicitly reserved for that queue. | None. | Interruptible reserve: the last fast resource (multiplier 1.30 or better) of a family that is idle or interruptible may not start long non-interruptible work for a non-urgent case, so one resource per family can always be seized by a P1. No idle capacity is held back. |
| Preemption | P1 interrupts immediately inside its queue; victim = lowest class, latest deadline. | P1 interrupts immediately; victim = lowest class, latest deadline among all qualified. | P1 interrupts only if no acceptable resource is idle and no fast qualified resource is planned to be free within 5 minutes; victim = lowest class, then largest slack, preferring a victim whose resource is fast for the P1 task. P1 fast lane: a diagnostic P1 task goes only to a resource whose planned finish is within 10 minutes of the best planned finish over all qualified resources, counting interruptible resources as available now. |
| Parallelisation | Always split across the two queues (8 minutes coordination, 7 or 12 minutes hand-over), except when a deputy owns both. | Emergent: whoever is free; usually two resources. | Threshold rule of Section 1: P1 split; P2/P3 split only onto a primary resource; otherwise held for the sibling-branch owner and continued sequentially; released to overflow when slack falls below 60 minutes. |
| Hand-over minimisation | One engineer keeps Intake, own branch and Validation of a single-skill case. | None beyond the fact that EDD often returns the same case to the same resource. | Own-case continuation outranks all non-urgent work, so single-skill cases normally stay with one resource from Intake to Validation. |
| Overflow triggers | None (deputies only when a queue has no owner on shift). | Not applicable: everything is always open. | A non-primary resource (multiplier above 1.00) may take a diagnostic only if the task is urgent, has waited 20 minutes, at least 2 tasks of that skill are waiting, or the resource is continuing its own case's second branch; multipliers above 1.30 (Ada on Electrical, Farah on diagnostics) additionally need urgency or at least 4 waiting tasks of that skill. |

Variants:
- **C_edd**: C without band 5. Cases that can no longer meet their target keep their deadline rank, so the oldest work is always served first.
- **C_idle_reserve**: C plus a classical idle reserve: in blocks with at least one expected P1 arrival, the last idle fast resource of a family may not start a P3 diagnostic.
- **D_hybrid_retimed**: C's rules, same head-count and paid hours, Ben's shift moved to 12:00-20:00 (break 16:30-17:00).
- **E_whatif_plus1**: D plus one hypothetical electrical/software shift 12:00-20:00. **F_whatif_plus2**: E plus one hypothetical mechanical/electrical shift 12:00-20:00.

**When another policy would be preferable.** [H] A-type fixed queues if cross-skill multipliers were far worse than supplied, if load were below roughly 70% so that queues never starve, or if single-owner accountability is a regulatory need. **Parameter caveat.** The thresholds (1.30 cap, 60-minute slack band, 20-minute and 2-task overflow triggers, 5-minute preemption wait, 24-hour max-age) are starting values. During development I varied the cap, the overflow triggers and the preemption wait on an earlier revision without SLA changes beyond noise, while the slack band visibly moved P3 attainment; those runs are not reproduced by the script and should be treated as hypotheses for the pilot.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates.** Slack and the fast lane depend on declared priority, declared case type and planned service times. Predictable distortions: upgrading tickets to P1 to obtain the fast lane and preemption rights; declaring a case complex to obtain planned time; letting a case drift into the "lost" band so that it stops competing; holding a task to trip the queue-length overflow trigger. Controls [R]: planned times taken from logged medians per activity and resource, never self-reported; sampled audit of P1 classification; planned-versus-actual ratio per resource on the dashboard; the lost flag set only by the system and visible to a supervisor; the 24-hour max-age guarantee.
- **Starvation of P3 work.** Fixed class priority (A) starves P3 outright: 0.16-0.37 attainment and 16-22% of the scenario day's cases still open three days later. EDD (B, C_edd) does not starve but lets P3 attainment fall to 0.04-0.67. The salvage ordering protects the P3 cases that can still be saved (0.66-0.92) and starves the lost ones of any class; that is why the max-age rule is not optional. Monitor the number and oldest age of lost cases.
- **Skill erosion.** Under C, secondary skills are exercised largely through overflow and urgent work (28 of 473 assignments were labelled controlled overflow in the logged U5 run), and after a staffing fix they would be used even less. Multipliers such as Ada's 1.35 or Chen's 1.25 would then drift upwards exactly where U3-type resilience depends on them. [R] A small scheduled quota of secondary-skill cases per person per week, and quarterly re-estimation of multipliers from the log.
- **Excessive preemption.** One P1 case can trigger up to four interruptions (Intake, each branch, Validation). Simulated preemptions per scenario day: B 7.3-11.6, C 6.3-9.7, A 4.1-7.4. The once-per-case rule means that in U1 and U5 the supply of interruptible work can run out, after which P1 cases queue behind each other. Monitor preemptions per P1 case, penalty minutes, and the share of non-P1 cases already interrupted.
- **Coordination overhead.** Splitting a two-branch case costs 15 minutes of latency (20 for ME) per case. Hand-over, crossing, setup and coordination add 12.6-13.8 minutes per case under C against 16-17 under B and 6-7 under A. The hybrid should not be tuned toward more splitting for P2/P3; the capacity analysis says splitting pays only for P1.
- **New bottleneck at Validation.** Validation is 661 base minutes per day (24% of touch time) and nobody is dedicated to it. Under ownership it queues behind its owner, and at shift ends (12:00, 16:00, 18:00) open validations are orphaned onto the remaining staff: in the logged U5 run (one run, illustrative) the mean Validation queue wait was 8-20 minutes in most blocks but 117 minutes for the 10:00 block and 263 minutes for the 16:00 block. [R] A "validate before you leave" sweep in the last 30-40 minutes of each shift and a dashboard tile for validation-ready cases; [H] Farah (Validation 1.20, Intake 1.00) and Ben are the natural absorbers.
- **Fairness.** Under B and C the most loaded person uses the full 30 minutes of overtime on a typical day (maximum utilisation 1.05-1.07) and nobody is below 96%. Deepa and Emil carry all evening P1 work and interruptions; Farah's diagnostic multiplier pushes her toward Intake and Validation, which may be perceived as de-skilling. A's apparent calm for Ada and Ben (81-82%) is unfairness in the other direction. Fairness needs a capacity change; rotating the evening shift and the interruptible role are the routing-side mitigations.
- **Human interpretability.** The hybrid is six ordered questions (P1? urgent? my own case? my primary skill? overflow trigger met? otherwise lost-case backfill) and every assignment records which one fired, the eligible set and the slack. The salvage ordering is the hardest part to defend to a requester whose case was given up, so the business owner must approve it explicitly, and the rule label must be shown on the case.

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

**Deployment indicators.**
- SLA attainment by priority and by arrival window (08-12, 12-16, 16-20), with the count of cases at risk (slack below 60 minutes) in real time.
- Projected same-day load ratio at the four checkpoints; open cases at close; number and oldest age of lost cases; max-age activations per day.
- Queue wait by stage and skill (assignment minus queue entry minus hand-over latency); wait from arrival to first diagnostic start by case type.
- Overflow share and mean multiplier by resource and skill; planned-versus-actual service ratio by activity and resource.
- Hand-overs per case, hand-over and team-crossing minutes, setup minutes per resource-day, coordination minutes, share of two-branch cases that were split, by priority.
- Preemptions per day and per P1 case, victims by class, restart-penalty minutes.
- Utilisation and overtime per resource, utilisation spread, evening load; Validation queue in the 40 minutes before each shift end.
- P1 specifics: time to first touch, share of P1 diagnostics served at multiplier 1.00, P1 touch time against 120 minutes.

**Event attributes.** One interval event per uninterrupted work segment, with the standard keys `case:concept:name`, `concept:name`, `start_timestamp`, `time:timestamp`, `org:resource`, plus:

| requirement | attribute(s) in the simulated log | note |
|---|---|---|
| SLA clock | `case_arrival`, `sla_deadline`, `priority`, `slack_at_assign_min` | the clock starts at arrival, not at the first start event |
| queue entry | `queue_entry` | time the task became available (predecessor end plus coordination) |
| assignment decision | `assign_time`, `decision_rule` | rule label that fired, including "P1 preemption" and "max-age guarantee" |
| eligible-resource set | `eligible_resources` | qualified resources on shift at assignment |
| skill used | `concept:name` with `skill_multiplier`, `case_type` | |
| planned / actual service | `planned_service_min`, `actual_service_min`, `tail_case` | planned = mean x multiplier, or remaining time at a pause |
| pause / resume | `paused_by_preemption`, `resumed_segment` | a paused activity appears as two segments |
| hand-over | `handover_min` | 7, 12, or 5 (crossing only) |
| setup | `setup_min` | |
| policy version | `policy_version` | |
| scenario / shock flag | `scenario_flag`, `arrival_day` | in production: absence, outage and surge flags per day |

In production I would add the previous resource, the identifier of the preempting case, case-level coordination minutes and an overtime flag; the simulator keeps these internally but does not write them.

**PM4Py analyses and what they reveal.**
- **Format and case-level times.** `pm4py.format_dataframe` followed by `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(..., start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")`. The returned columns must be relabelled: `@@service_time` is the sum of segment durations (overlapping parallel branches are counted twice), `@@sojourn_time` runs from the first start to the last completion, and `@@waiting_time` is their difference. None of them sees the queue before Intake. In the logged U5 run the PM4Py "waiting" of P2 cases averaged 536 minutes while the pre-Intake queue averaged a further 735 minutes and the SLA-clock elapsed time 1,337 minutes, so the case-level PM4Py figure captured well under half of the real delay. The SLA clock and all stage waits are therefore computed from `case_arrival`, `queue_entry` and `assign_time`.
- **Service-time changes.** `pm4py.get_service_time` on the interval log gives mean segment duration per activity; grouped by resource in pandas it re-estimates the multiplier matrix. Paused activities must first be summed per case and activity, otherwise preemption looks like faster service. A rising planned-versus-actual ratio for one resource and skill indicates erosion or gaming; a rising share of `tail_case` indicates a U4-type regime.
- **Hand-over burden.** `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network` per week and per policy version shows which pairs exchange work (largest arcs in the logged run: Ben to Deepa, Deepa to Emil, Deepa to Ada); arcs toward later-shift resources should grow when work is carried across shift ends [H]. It counts directly-follows relations by completion time, so interleaved parallel branches inflate it; the explicit `handover_min` attribute is the ground truth (166 of 473 segments in the logged run). `pm4py.discover_organizational_roles` shows whether the de facto roles still match the eligibility matrix.
- **Bottleneck migration.** Stage queue waits pivoted by stage, skill and two-hour block of queue entry (computed in the script) are the primary view; `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg` per window and scenario flag gives the same picture on arcs, with the caveat that it works on completion timestamps and so mixes waiting with the successor's service. Under ownership routing the waiting is recorded before Intake although it is caused by diagnostic capacity; it must be attributed by case type, as in Section 2.
- **Drift.** PM4Py's simplified interface has no turnkey drift test, so drift is detected by windowed comparison: `pm4py.filter_time_range` or a pandas split by `policy_version` and week, then variant frequencies (`pm4py.get_variants`), the directly-follows graph, service times and stage waits per window, with control limits on each indicator. `pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` shows when arrivals outpace completions.
- **Conformance.** Token-based replay or alignments against `Intake -> (one branch, or two branches in either order or in parallel) -> Validation`, after merging paused segments, detects skipped Validation, diagnostics before Intake and wrong branch sets.

MAX_OVERDUE = 1440.0                  # salvage ordering: a lost case regains top rank when 24 h past its deadline
#-- Policy A (fixed queues): static owners per skill queue, static deputies only if no owner is on shift.
OWN = {"Mechanical": ("Ben", "Ada"), "Electrical": ("Chen", "Deepa"), "Software": ("Farah", "Emil")}
DEPUTY = {"Mechanical": ("Deepa",), "Electrical": ("Emil", "Ada"), "Software": ("Chen",)}
POLICIES = {  # name: (dispatch rule set, roster)
    "A_fixed": ("A", ROSTER), "B_pool": ("B", ROSTER), "C_hybrid": ("C", ROSTER), "C_edd": ("C-edd", ROSTER),
    "C_idle_reserve": ("C+idle", ROSTER), "D_hybrid_retimed": ("C", ROSTER_RETIMED),
    "E_whatif_plus1": ("C", ROSTER_PLUS1), "F_whatif_plus2": ("C", ROSTER_PLUS2)}
GIVEN_ROSTER = ["A_fixed", "B_pool", "C_hybrid", "C_edd", "C_idle_reserve"]   # comparable on the supplied roster
WINDOWS = ("08-12", "12-16", "16-20")          # arrival windows used to localise SLA losses
W_CLASS = {"P1": 10.0, "P2": 3.0, "P3": 1.0}   # objective weights per priority class
FAIR_W = 0.10                                  # O2: hours of weighted tardiness per pp of utilisation spread
MEAN = {a: (1 - p) * (lo + hi) / 2 + p * (tl + th) / 2 for a, (lo, hi, tl, th, p) in SERVICE.items()}


#==== 3. DEMAND GENERATOR ====
def gen_cases(seed, scen, days=N_DAYS, shock_day=SHOCK_DAY):
    """Poisson arrivals per block/type (A1), uniform service inside ordinary/tail range (A2).
    Day 0 = base warm-up, day 1 = scenario day, later days = base recovery."""
    rng, cases = np.random.default_rng(seed), []
    for d in range(days):
        sc = SCENARIOS[scen] if d == shock_day else {}
        for b, (start, n, prio, mix) in enumerate(BLOCKS):
            p1 = prio[0] + sc.get("p1_add", {}).get(b, 0)
            rest = (100 - p1) / (prio[1] + prio[2])
            pp = np.array([p1, prio[1] * rest, prio[2] * rest]) / 100
            for ti, typ in enumerate(TYPES):
                lam = (n * sc.get("arr_mult", {}).get(b, 1) * mix[ti] / 100
                       * sc.get("type_mult", {}).get(b, {}).get(typ, 1))
                for _ in range(rng.poisson(lam)):
                    arr = d * DAY + start + rng.uniform(0, 120)
                    pr = str(rng.choice(["P1", "P2", "P3"], p=pp))
                    base, tail = {}, {}
                    for a in ("Intake",) + TYPES[typ] + ("Validation",):
                        lo, hi, tl, th, p = SERVICE[a]
                        p = min(1.0, p * sc.get("tail_mult", 1.0)) if a in DIAG else p
                        tail[a] = bool(rng.random() < p)
                        base[a] = float(rng.uniform(tl, th) if tail[a] else rng.uniform(lo, hi))
                    cases.append(dict(arr=arr, prio=pr, typ=typ, skills=TYPES[typ], base=base, tail=tail,
                                      day=d, deadline=arr + TARGET[pr], done=None, preempted=False,
                                      branch_res={}, act_end={}, segs=[], handovers=0, ho_min=0.0,
                                      setup_min=0.0, coord_min=0.0))
    cases.sort(key=lambda c: c["arr"])
    for i, c in enumerate(cases):
        c["id"] = f"C{i:04d}"
    return cases


#==== 4. DISCRETE-EVENT SIMULATION ====
class Sim:
    def __init__(self, cases, policy, scen, days=N_DAYS, shock_day=SHOCK_DAY):
        self.cases, self.pname, self.scen = cases, policy, scen
        self.rule, self.roster = POLICIES[policy]
        self.hybrid = self.rule.startswith("C")
        self.salvage = self.rule != "C-edd"
        self.sc, self.shock_day, self.H = SCENARIOS[scen], shock_day, days * DAY
        self.res = {r: dict(busy=None, fam=None, fam_case=None, fam_day=-1, break_until=-1.0,
                            breaks=set(), work=defaultdict(float)) for r in self.roster}
        self.waiting, self.ev, self.seq, self.log, self.npre = [], [], 0, [], defaultdict(int)
        for c in cases:
            self.push(c["arr"], "arrival", c)
        for d in range(days):
            for r, (s, e, bs, be) in self.roster.items():
                self.push(d * DAY + s, "wake", None)
                if bs is not None:
                    self.push(d * DAY + bs, "wake", None)
            if "intake_outage" in self.sc and d == shock_day:
                self.push(d * DAY + self.sc["intake_outage"][1], "wake", None)

    def push(self, t, kind, payload):
        self.seq += 1
        heapq.heappush(self.ev, (t, self.seq, kind, payload))

    # ---- availability -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    def absent(self, r, t):
        return int(t // DAY) == self.shock_day and r in self.sc.get("absent", ())

    def on_shift(self, r, t):
        s, e = self.roster[r][:2]
        return s <= t % DAY < e and not self.absent(r, t)

    def idle(self, r, t):
        st = self.res[r]
        return self.on_shift(r, t) and st["busy"] is None and st["break_until"] <= t

    def take_breaks(self, t):   # A8: floating break, starts at the first idle moment after its scheduled start
        d = int(t // DAY)
        for r, (s, e, bs, be) in self.roster.items():
            st = self.res[r]
            if bs is not None and self.idle(r, t) and t % DAY >= bs and d not in st["breaks"]:
                st["breaks"].add(d)
                st["break_until"] = t + (be - bs)
                self.push(st["break_until"], "wake", None)

    # ---- task release / readiness ---------------------------------------------------------------
    def release(self, c, act, t, prev, rem=None):
        task = dict(case=c, act=act, rem=c["base"][act] if rem is None else rem, entry=t, prev=prev,
                    plan=MEAN[act] if rem is None else rem)   # planned base minutes (mean, or remaining at pause)
        self.waiting.append(task)
        for lag in (HANDOVER, HANDOVER + TEAM_CROSS):   # re-dispatch when the hand-over latency expires
            self.push(t + lag, "wake", None)

    def crossing(self, task, r):
        """+5 min if an ME case ends up on two different resources in different skill families (A5)."""
        c, a = task["case"], task["act"]
        if a not in DIAG:
            return False
        return any(FAMILY[b] != FAMILY[a] and q != r for b, q in c["branch_res"].items() if b != a)

    def ho_delay(self, task, r):   # A4: hand-over is case latency, it does not consume resource time
        ho = HANDOVER if (task["prev"] is not None and task["prev"] != r) else 0.0
        return ho + (TEAM_CROSS if self.crossing(task, r) else 0.0)

    def setup(self, task, r, t):
        st, a = self.res[r], task["act"]
        return SETUP if (a in DIAG and st["fam_day"] == int(t // DAY) and st["fam"] != FAMILY[a]
                         and st["fam_case"] != task["case"]["id"]) else 0.0

    def feasible(self, task, r, t):
        a, m = task["act"], SKILL[r].get(task["act"])
        if m is None or task["entry"] + self.ho_delay(task, r) > t + 1e-9:
            return False
        if a == "Intake" and "intake_outage" in self.sc and int(t // DAY) == self.shock_day:
            o1, o2 = self.sc["intake_outage"]
            if o1 <= t % DAY < o2:
                return False
        shift_end = int(t // DAY) * DAY + self.roster[r][1]
        # A9 (oracle simplification): start only if the realised finish respects the 30-min overtime cap
        return t + self.setup(task, r, t) + task["rem"] * m <= shift_end + OVERTIME

    # ---- policy logic: returns a sortable key (lower = better) or None if the pair is not allowed ------
    def todo(self, c):
        return [a for a in ("Intake",) + c["skills"] + ("Validation",) if a not in c["act_end"]]

    def slack(self, c, t):
        return c["deadline"] - t - sum(MEAN[a] for a in self.todo(c))   # planned (mean) remaining touch time

    def interruptible(self, r, t):
        b = self.res[r]["busy"]
        return (b is not None and b["task"]["act"] in DIAG and b["task"]["case"]["prio"] != "P1"
                and not b["task"]["case"]["preempted"] and t >= b["svc_start"] and b["end"] - t >= 1)

    def eta(self, task, r, t):
        """Planned finish of `task` on r if r is used as early as the rules allow (P1 fast lane)."""
        a, st = task["act"], self.res[r]
        if a not in SKILL[r] or not self.on_shift(r, t):
            return np.inf
        free = t if (st["busy"] is None or self.interruptible(r, t)) else max(t + 1, st["busy"]["svc_start"] + st["busy"]["plan"])
        begin = max(free, st["break_until"], task["entry"] + self.ho_delay(task, r))
        fin = begin + task["plan"] * SKILL[r][a]
        return fin if fin <= int(t // DAY) * DAY + self.roster[r][1] + OVERTIME else np.inf

    def a_eligible(self, task, r, t):
        c, a = task["case"], task["act"]
        if a == "Validation" and task["prev"] is not None and self.on_shift(task["prev"], t):
            return r == task["prev"]                     # the engineer validates his/her own case
        q = a if a in DIAG else c["skills"][0]
        owners = [x for x in OWN[q] if self.on_shift(x, t)]
        return r in owners if owners else r in DEPUTY[q]

    def reserve_blocks(self, task, r, t):
        """Hybrid P1 reservation.
        (i) Interruptible reserve (always on): the last fast resource of a skill family that is idle or
            interruptible may not start long non-interruptible work (e.g. resuming an already-preempted
            diagnostic) for a non-urgent case, so a P1 can always seize somebody quickly.
        (ii) Idle reserve (variant C+idle only): in P1-peak blocks the last idle fast resource of a family
            may not start a P3 diagnostic."""
        c, a = task["case"], task["act"]
        fast = lambda x, f: any(FAMILY[k] == f and SKILL[x].get(k, 9) <= CAP for k in DIAG)
        seizable = lambda x: x != r and self.on_shift(x, t) and (self.idle(x, t) or self.interruptible(x, t))
        locks = not (a in DIAG and not c["preempted"]) and task["plan"] * SKILL[r][a] > LONG_LOCK
        if locks:
            for f in {FAMILY[k] for k in DIAG if SKILL[r].get(k, 9) <= CAP}:
                if not any(seizable(x) and fast(x, f) for x in self.roster):
                    return True
        if self.rule == "C+idle" and a in DIAG and c["prio"] == "P3":
            b, d = min(5, max(0, int((t % DAY - 480) // 120))), int(t // DAY)
            sc = self.sc if d == self.shock_day else {}
            exp_p1 = (BLOCKS[b][1] * sc.get("arr_mult", {}).get(b, 1)
                      * (BLOCKS[b][2][0] + sc.get("p1_add", {}).get(b, 0)) / 100)
            others_idle = any(x != r and self.idle(x, t) and fast(x, FAMILY[a]) for x in self.roster)
            if exp_p1 >= IDLE_RESERVE_P1_RATE and not others_idle:
                return True
        return False

    def pair_key(self, task, r, t):
        if not self.feasible(task, r, t):
            return None
        c, a, m = task["case"], task["act"], SKILL[r][task["act"]]
        load = self.res[r]["work"][int(t // DAY)]
        if self.rule == "A":      # class priority, then FIFO by case arrival, inside fixed queues
            return (PRANK[c["prio"]], c["arr"], load, "fixed queue: class, then FIFO") if self.a_eligible(task, r, t) else None
        if self.rule == "B":      # P1 first, then earliest SLA deadline; least-loaded qualified resource
            return (0 if c["prio"] == "P1" else 1, c["deadline"], c["arr"], load, "pool: P1, then EDD, least-loaded")
        # ---- C: hybrid ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
        own = task["prev"] == r
        if c["prio"] == "P1":     # fast lane: only resources close to the best planned finish (may be busy)
            if a in DIAG and self.eta(task, r, t) > min(self.eta(task, x, t) for x in self.roster) + P1_ETA_TOL:
                return None
            return (0, c["deadline"], 0 if own else 1, m, load, "P1 fast lane")
        sl = self.slack(c, t)
        aged = self.salvage and t - c["deadline"] >= MAX_OVERDUE
        lost = self.salvage and sl < 0 and not aged
        urgent = sl < CRIT and not lost
        qlen = sum(1 for x in self.waiting if x["act"] == a)
        if a in DIAG:
            primary = m <= 1.0
            if not primary:
                sib = [q for b, q in c["branch_res"].items() if b != a and b not in c["act_end"]]
                if sib and sib[0] != r and SKILL[sib[0]].get(a, 9) <= CAP and not urgent:
                    return None       # sibling-branch owner can continue sequentially: hold the branch
                seq_cont = any(q == r for b, q in c["branch_res"].items() if b != a)
                trig = urgent or seq_cont or t - task["entry"] >= W_OVER or qlen >= Q_OVER
                if not trig or (m > CAP and not (urgent or qlen >= SURGE_CAP_QUEUE)):
                    return None
        elif a == "Intake":
            primary = any(SKILL[r].get(k, 9) <= 1.0 for k in c["skills"])   # likely case owner
            if not primary and not (urgent or t - task["entry"] >= 5.0):
                return None
        else:
            primary = True
        if not urgent and self.reserve_blocks(task, r, t):
            return None
        band = 5 if lost else 1 if urgent else 2 if (own and m <= CAP) else 3 if primary else 4
        label = ("", "max-age guarantee" if aged else "urgent: slack < 60 min", "own-case continuation", "primary skill",
                 "controlled overflow", "lost-case backfill")[band]
        return (band, c["deadline"], 0 if own else 1, m, load, label)

    # ---- start / finish / preempt -----------------------------------------------------------------
    def start(self, task, r, t, rule):
        c, a, st, d = task["case"], task["act"], self.res[r], int(t // DAY)
        su, ho = self.setup(task, r, t), self.ho_delay(task, r)
        elig = [x for x in self.roster if a in SKILL[x] and self.on_shift(x, t)]
        if a in DIAG:
            st["fam"], st["fam_case"], st["fam_day"] = FAMILY[a], c["id"], d
            c["branch_res"][a] = r
        c["handovers"] += int(task["prev"] is not None and task["prev"] != r)
        c["ho_min"] += ho
        c["setup_min"] += su
        end = t + su + task["rem"] * SKILL[r][a]
        st["busy"] = dict(task=task, t0=t, svc_start=t + su, end=end, setup=su, ho=ho, elig=elig, tok=self.seq + 1,
                          plan=task["plan"] * SKILL[r][a], rule=rule, slack=self.slack(c, t))
        st["work"][d] += end - t
        self.waiting.remove(task)
        self.push(end, "finish", (r, self.seq + 1))

    def write_log(self, r, t_end, preempted):
        b = self.res[r]["busy"]
        task, c = b["task"], b["task"]["case"]
        c["segs"].append((b["svc_start"], t_end))
        self.log.append({
            "case:concept:name": c["id"], "concept:name": task["act"], "start_timestamp": b["svc_start"],
            "time:timestamp": t_end, "org:resource": r, "priority": c["prio"], "case_type": c["typ"],
            "case_arrival": c["arr"], "sla_deadline": c["deadline"], "queue_entry": task["entry"],
            "assign_time": b["t0"], "decision_rule": b["rule"], "slack_at_assign_min": b["slack"],
            "eligible_resources": "|".join(b["elig"]),
            "skill_multiplier": SKILL[r][task["act"]], "planned_service_min": b["plan"],
            "actual_service_min": t_end - b["svc_start"], "handover_min": b["ho"], "setup_min": b["setup"],
            "resumed_segment": task["plan"] != MEAN[task["act"]], "paused_by_preemption": preempted,
            "tail_case": c["tail"][task["act"]], "policy_version": self.pname, "scenario_flag": self.scen,
            "arrival_day": c["day"]})

    def finish(self, r, t):
        st = self.res[r]
        task, c, a = st["busy"]["task"], st["busy"]["task"]["case"], st["busy"]["task"]["act"]
        self.write_log(r, t, False)
        st["busy"] = None
        c["act_end"][a] = t
        if a == "Intake":
            for b in c["skills"]:
                self.release(c, b, t, r)
        elif a in DIAG and all(b in c["act_end"] for b in c["skills"]):
            parallel = len({c["branch_res"][b] for b in c["skills"]}) > 1   # two resources => coordination
            c["coord_min"] = COORD if parallel else 0.0
            self.push(t + c["coord_min"], "release_val", (c, r))
        elif a == "Validation":
            c["done"] = t

    def try_preempt(self, t):
        for task in sorted([x for x in self.waiting if x["case"]["prio"] == "P1"], key=lambda x: x["case"]["deadline"]):
            if any(self.idle(r, t) and self.pair_key(task, r, t) is not None for r in self.roster):
                continue
            cands = []
            for r, st in self.res.items():
                if not (self.on_shift(r, t) and self.interruptible(r, t)):
                    continue                       # only non-P1 diagnostics, at most once per case
                b, vc = st["busy"], st["busy"]["task"]["case"]
                st["busy"] = None                  # tentatively free the resource to test the pair
                ok = self.pair_key(task, r, t) is not None
                st["busy"] = b
                if ok:
                    m = SKILL[r][task["act"]]
                    key = ((m > CAP, -PRANK[vc["prio"]], -self.slack(vc, t), m) if self.hybrid
                           else (-PRANK[vc["prio"]], -vc["deadline"]))
                    cands.append((key, r))
            if not cands:
                continue
            if self.hybrid:                        # controlled preemption: wait if someone frees very soon
                if any(st["busy"] and SKILL[r].get(task["act"], 9) <= CAP
                       and 0 <= st["busy"]["svc_start"] + st["busy"]["plan"] - t <= PREEMPT_WAIT
                       for r, st in self.res.items()):
                    continue
            r = min(cands)[1]
            st = self.res[r]
            b = st["busy"]
            vt, vc = b["task"], b["task"]["case"]
            self.write_log(r, t, True)
            st["work"][int(b["t0"] // DAY)] -= b["end"] - t
            rem_base = (b["end"] - t) / SKILL[r][vt["act"]] * (1 + PREEMPT_PENALTY)   # restart penalty
            st["busy"], vc["preempted"] = None, True
            self.npre[int(t // DAY)] += 1
            self.release(vc, vt["act"], t, r, rem=rem_base)
            self.start(task, r, t, "P1 preemption")

    def dispatch(self, t):
        self.take_breaks(t)
        while True:
            idle = [r for r in self.roster if self.idle(r, t)]
            best = None
            for task in self.waiting:
                for r in idle:
                    k = self.pair_key(task, r, t)
                    if k is not None and (best is None or k < best[0]):
                        best = (k, task, r)
            if best is None:
                break
            self.start(best[1], best[2], t, best[0][-1])
        self.try_preempt(t)

    def run(self):
        while self.ev:
            t, _, kind, p = heapq.heappop(self.ev)
            if t >= self.H:
                break
            if kind == "arrival":
                self.release(p, "Intake", t, None)
            elif kind == "finish":
                b = self.res[p[0]]["busy"]
                if b is None or b["tok"] != p[1]:
                    continue                        # stale event of a preempted activity
                self.finish(p[0], t)
            elif kind == "release_val":
                self.release(p[0], "Validation", t, p[1])
            self.dispatch(t)
        return self


#==== 5. METRICS ====
def union_len(segs):
    tot, cur_s, cur_e = 0.0, None, None
    for s, e in sorted(segs):
        if cur_e is None or s > cur_e:
            tot += (cur_e - cur_s) if cur_e is not None else 0.0
            cur_s, cur_e = s, e
        else:
            cur_e = max(cur_e, e)
    return tot + ((cur_e - cur_s) if cur_e is not None else 0.0)


def run_metrics(sim, day=SHOCK_DAY):
    """Metrics for the cases that ARRIVE on the scenario day (SLA clock = arrival -> Validation end)."""
    cs = [c for c in sim.cases if c["day"] == day]
    first_diag = {}
    for row in sim.log:
        if row["concept:name"] in DIAG:
            cid = row["case:concept:name"]
            first_diag[cid] = min(first_diag.get(cid, np.inf), row["assign_time"])
    rows = []
    for c in cs:
        end = c["done"] if c["done"] is not None else sim.H           # censored at horizon => lower bound
        cyc = end - c["arr"]
        tod = c["arr"] % DAY
        rows.append(dict(prio=c["prio"], typ=c["typ"], cycle=cyc, delay=cyc - union_len(c["segs"]),
                         win="08-12" if tod < 720 else "12-16" if tod < 960 else "16-20",
                         met=c["done"] is not None and cyc <= TARGET[c["prio"]],
                         wtard_h=W_CLASS[c["prio"]] * max(0.0, cyc - TARGET[c["prio"]]) / 60,
                         to_diag=first_diag.get(c["id"], sim.H) - c["arr"],
                         ho=c["handovers"], overhead=c["ho_min"] + c["setup_min"] + c["coord_min"]))
    df = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    util = {}
    for r, (s, e, bs, be) in sim.roster.items():
        if not sim.absent(r, day * DAY + s):
            util[r] = sim.res[r]["work"][day] / ((e - s) - ((be - bs) if bs is not None else 0))
    u = np.array(list(util.values()))
    open_at = lambda d: sum(1 for c in sim.cases if c["arr"] < (d + 1) * DAY and (c["done"] is None or c["done"] > (d + 1) * DAY))
    out = dict(n=len(df), mean_delay=df.delay.mean(), p90_delay=df.delay.quantile(0.9), p95_delay=df.delay.quantile(0.95),
               wtard_h=df.wtard_h.mean(), max_util=u.max(), util_spread=u.max() - u.min(),
               ho_per_case=df.ho.mean(), overhead_min=df.overhead.mean(), preemptions=sim.npre[day],
               open_d1=open_at(day), open_d2=open_at(day + 1),
               unfinished=float(np.mean([c["done"] is None for c in cs])))
    for p in TARGET:
        out[f"n_{p}"], out[f"met_{p}"] = int((df.prio == p).sum()), int(df[df.prio == p].met.sum())
    for typ in TYPES:
        out[f"to_diag_{typ}"] = df[df.typ == typ].to_diag.mean()
    for p in ("P1", "P2"):
        for w in WINDOWS:
            sel = df[(df.prio == p) & (df.win == w)]
            out[f"n_{p}_{w}"], out[f"met_{p}_{w}"] = len(sel), int(sel.met.sum())
    return out, util


def experiment(n_seeds=40):
    recs, utils = [], []
    for scen in SCENARIOS:
        for seed in range(n_seeds):
            for pol in POLICIES:
                sim = Sim(gen_cases(seed, scen), pol, scen).run()   # common random numbers across policies
                m, u = run_metrics(sim)
                recs.append(dict(scenario=scen, policy=pol, seed=seed, **m))
                utils.append(dict(scenario=scen, policy=pol, seed=seed, **u))
    return pd.DataFrame(recs), pd.DataFrame(utils)


def summarise(res):
    g = res.groupby(["scenario", "policy"], sort=False)
    cols = ["mean_delay", "p90_delay", "p95_delay", "wtard_h", "max_util", "util_spread", "ho_per_case",
            "overhead_min", "preemptions", "open_d1", "open_d2", "unfinished"] + [f"to_diag_{t}" for t in TYPES]
    out = g[cols].mean()
    out["mean_delay_se"] = g["mean_delay"].sem()
    for p in TARGET:                                   # pooled attainment over all seeds
        out[f"{p}_SLA"] = g[f"met_{p}"].sum() / g[f"n_{p}"].sum()
        out[f"{p}_SLA_se"] = np.sqrt(out[f"{p}_SLA"] * (1 - out[f"{p}_SLA"]) / g[f"n_{p}"].sum())
    seed_p2 = res.assign(x=res.met_P2 / res.n_P2.clip(lower=1)).groupby(["scenario", "policy"], sort=False).x
    out["P2_SLA_p10"], out["P2_SLA_p90"] = seed_p2.quantile(0.1), seed_p2.quantile(0.9)
    # O1: weighted SLA shortfall (pp below 90%).  O2: weighted tardiness hours/case + fairness penalty.
    out["O1"] = sum(W_CLASS[p] * (0.90 - out[f"{p}_SLA"]).clip(lower=0) * 100 for p in TARGET)
    out["O2"] = out["wtard_h"] + FAIR_W * out["util_spread"] * 100
    return out


def sla_by_window(res, scen):
    """Pooled P1/P2 attainment by arrival window: shows where in the day the SLA is lost."""
    g = res[res.scenario == scen].groupby("policy", sort=False).sum(numeric_only=True)
    return pd.DataFrame({f"{p}_{w}": g[f"met_{p}_{w}"] / g[f"n_{p}_{w}"] for p in ("P1", "P2") for w in WINDOWS})


def robustness(summ):
    rows = []
    for obj in ("O1", "O2"):
        tab = summ[obj].unstack("scenario")[list(SCENARIOS)]
        regret = tab.loc[GIVEN_ROSTER] - tab.loc[GIVEN_ROSTER].min()      # regret vs best policy on the given roster
        for pol in tab.index:
            rows.append(dict(objective=obj, policy=pol, **tab.loc[pol].round(1).to_dict(), mean=tab.loc[pol].mean(),
                             worst=tab.loc[pol].max(), max_regret=regret.loc[pol].max() if pol in regret.index else np.nan))
    return pd.DataFrame(rows).set_index(["objective", "policy"])


#==== 6. CAPACITY APPROXIMATION (no simulation) ====
def offered_load_table():
    rows = []
    for scen, sc in SCENARIOS.items():
        backlog = 0.0
        for b, (start, n, prio, mix) in enumerate(BLOCKS):
            cnt = {typ: n * sc.get("arr_mult", {}).get(b, 1) * mix[i] / 100 * sc.get("type_mult", {}).get(b, {}).get(typ, 1)
                   for i, typ in enumerate(TYPES)}
            mean = dict(MEAN)
            if "tail_mult" in sc:
                for a in DIAG:
                    lo, hi, tl, th, p = SERVICE[a]
                    mean[a] = (1 - 2 * p) * (lo + hi) / 2 + 2 * p * (tl + th) / 2
            work = {a: sum(v for k, v in cnt.items() if a in TYPES[k]) * mean[a] for a in DIAG}
            work["Int+Val"] = sum(cnt.values()) * (mean["Intake"] + mean["Validation"])
            net = {}
            for r, (s, e, bs, be) in ROSTER.items():
                if r not in sc.get("absent", ()):
                    on = max(0, min(e, start + 120) - max(s, start))
                    net[r] = on - (max(0, min(be, start + 120) - max(bs, start)) if bs is not None else 0)
            cap = sum(net.values())
            best = {a: min([SKILL[r][a] for r in net if net[r] > 0 and a in SKILL[r]], default=np.nan) for a in DIAG}
            prim = {a: sum(v for r, v in net.items() if SKILL[r].get(a, 9) <= 1.0) for a in DIAG}
            backlog = max(0.0, backlog + sum(work.values()) - cap)      # fluid carry-over at multiplier 1.00
            rows.append(dict(scenario=scen, block=f"{start // 60:02d}-{start // 60 + 2:02d}", arrivals=sum(cnt.values()),
                             **{f"{a[:4]}_min": work[a] for a in DIAG}, IntVal_min=work["Int+Val"],
                             work_min=sum(work.values()), cap_min=cap, load=sum(work.values()) / cap,
                             **{f"{a[:4]}_primcap": prim[a] for a in DIAG},
                             **{f"{a[:4]}_bestmult": best[a] for a in DIAG}, fluid_backlog=backlog))
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)

# 9. Limits and validation plan

**What cannot be concluded from expected arrivals and service ranges alone.**
- Absolute SLA levels. They depend on the shape of the distributions inside the ranges (the load ratio alone is bounded only by 0.83-1.36), on arrival burstiness, on correlation of tails within a case and with priority, and on the carry-in backlog, none of which is supplied. Policy rankings that repeat across six scenarios are more trustworthy than any attainment figure.
- Steady-state behaviour. With load above capacity there is no steady state; every figure is for a four-day transient with one warm-up day, and longer horizons would look worse for all policies.
- P1 differences between policies. With 5-9 P1 cases per day, 40 simulated days give standard errors of 0.02-0.03; the C over B advantage (1-7 points per scenario, same sign in all six) is suggestive, not proven.
- The real size of hand-over, setup and coordination costs, whether hand-overs consume the receiver's time, whether case type is known before Intake, whether breaks can move, and how multipliers behave under fatigue or practice. Each could change the specialisation-pooling balance.
- Human responses: gaming, acceptance of interruptions and of the salvage ordering, and absence patterns correlated with overload.
- The hypothetical hires: their multipliers are copied from Chen and Ada, which is a planning convenience, not evidence.
- The thresholds: they were not optimised and their sensitivity is not established by the script.

**Validation plan.**
1. **Calibration backtest.** Extract three to six months of the real event log with the attributes of Section 7. Estimate arrival dispersion by block, service-time distributions per activity and resource, and realised hand-over latency. Replay the historical arrivals through the simulator under the current dispatch practice and require it to reproduce historical attainment within about 5 points and mean delay within about 10% before any policy comparison is trusted. Then replay the same arrivals under A, B, C_hybrid and C_edd (trace-driven comparison), including historical days that resemble U1-U5.
2. **Shadow mode, two weeks.** The dispatch tool computes the hybrid's recommendation for every real decision while operators keep working as today. Measure agreement, the reasons for disagreement and the counterfactual load ratio. No operational risk.
3. **Switchback pilot, six to eight weeks.** Alternate the policy by day (randomised within weekday pairs) rather than by case, because cases share resources and case-level randomisation would contaminate both arms. First contrast: C_hybrid against C_edd, which differ only in the ordering of non-P1 work. Second contrast: C against current practice. Primary endpoint: the O1 loss; secondary: p90 delay, weighted tardiness, overtime minutes, preemptions. Sizing: with a single-day P2 attainment spread of roughly 0.15 and an expected difference of 7 points, about 70 days per arm would be needed for P2 alone, whereas the expected P3 difference (about 25 points) should be detectable in roughly two weeks per arm; covariate adjustment for arrival block, case type and tail flag shortens this.
4. **P1 protection during the pilot.** P1 handling is identical in both arms (fast lane and preemption always on). P1 superiority is not tested, since it would need more than a thousand P1 cases; instead a non-inferiority guard runs continuously: stop the arm if P1 attainment over the last 20 P1 cases falls 10 points below the pre-pilot baseline, if any P1 case waits more than 15 minutes for its first touch, or if any P1 case exceeds 240 minutes. A duty supervisor can override any assignment, and overrides are logged as their own decision rule.
5. **Staffing trials.** Retime one mechanical/validation shift for three weeks on days not flagged as surge risk and compare P2 attainment for arrivals after 14:00 and open cases at close with matched weekdays. Trial one added electrical/software evening shift before committing to two. Success criteria: P2 attainment for after-16:00 arrivals above 50%, backlog at close not growing week over week, nobody above 100% utilisation.
6. **Shock rehearsal.** U1-U5 are too rare to wait for. Replay them in the calibrated simulator each quarter, flag natural occurrences (absence, outage, surge) in the log, and compare observed degradation with the simulated ranges in Section 4. A policy stays "robust" only while observed regret remains inside those ranges.
7. **Roll-out rule.** Adopt permanently if the O1 loss improves by a pre-agreed margin, P1 is non-inferior, p90 delay stays inside the tolerance agreed with the business owner, and the fairness indicators (overtime per person, utilisation spread) do not deteriorate. Otherwise fall back to C_edd ordering, which is the low-regret choice under the lateness objective.
