Now the resilience vs concealed noncompliance distinction. Key definitional frame:
- Resilience = the adaptive action preserves the *control objective* (not necessarily the control mechanism) and leaves reconstructable evidence, with compensating controls of equivalent assurance.
- Concealed noncompliance = the mechanism is dropped and the objective is assumed rather than verified, and the gap is only detectable later (or never), with evidence produced after the fact and presented as if contemporaneous.
- Ambiguous = objective partially preserved; depends on risk appetite, product class, and whether compensating control exists.

Good. Now section 3: definitions and the three buckets, plus tests.

Three tests:
1. Control-objective test: is the *objective* (authorized, verified condition, custody, evidence) still achieved, or merely assumed?
2. Contemporaneity test: is evidence created at the time of the act, or later? Is it flagged as reconstructed?
3. Compensating-control test: is there an alternative control of equivalent assurance, documented, and independent?
4. Burden test: who bears the residual risk — the customer, the public, or the company's own records? (Deferral shifts to customers; skip-auth shifts to regulatory/public; scan loss shifts to company records but also to public safety for recalls.)
5. Detectability test: would a deviation be visible in the metrics, or normalised as a mode of working? (T2: 58% dispatch-without-scan vs 9.5% flagged exceptions  detection ratio 16%.)

## 3. Resilience versus concealed noncompliance

**Resilience** is preserving the *control objective* under degraded means — authorization genuinely verified, product condition genuinely attested, custody genuinely witnessed — with evidence produced **at the time of the act**, by a documented compensating control of equivalent assurance.

**Concealed noncompliance** is dropping the mechanism while *assuming* the objective held, where the gap is detectable only later (or never), and where the evidence that does exist was created afterwards and can be mistaken for contemporaneous.

Five tests separate them in this case:

1. **Objective test** — was the objective *achieved* or merely *assumed*? B achieved it (2,500 staff-verified authorizations). C assumed it (1,600 shipped on "standing authorization").
2. **Contemporaneity test** — was evidence created at the time? A signed handoff log at dispatch is contemporaneous; a scan entered three days later from memory is not. **G produced no evidence at all; E and H produced evidence later.**
3. **Compensating-control test** — is there an alternative of *equivalent* assurance? F was equivalent for controlled substances (100% coverage) but **not equivalent for cold-chain**, because the substitute control never captured temperature.
4. **Burden test** — who carries the residual risk? I and J shifted it onto Pharmacy-standard customers (fill 61%, 30% backlog cancellation). C and G shifted it onto the regulatory record and, for a recall, onto patients.
5. **Detectability test** — would the deviation be visible in the metrics? T2's detection ratio was 16%: 532 flagged traceability exceptions against 3,250 structurally scan-less dispatches. **When a deviation becomes the *mode of working* rather than an *exception*, monitoring stops seeing it.**

**True resilience:** **B** (objective preserved, contemporaneous, independent human control); **F's structure** (a real release gate existed where the system gate did not); **I and J's *existence*** (deliberate, prioritised rationing is a rational response, not a defect); **A's prioritisation intent**; **K** (necessary restoration of the system of record).

**Risky shortcuts / concealed noncompliance:** **C** (authorization never verified — surfaced later as +86% compliance holds); **G** (no contemporaneous custody evidence — the single largest exposure, 58% of phase volume); **D beyond its TTL** (a Day-0 snapshot used on Day 2 — reconciled into 1,400 corrections and 300 cancellations); **H for controlled products** (attestation without custody); **E without a reconstruction flag** (misdated evidence is worse than absent evidence).

**Ambiguous — resolved by risk appetite, not by intuition:** **A** (legitimate only if the fast lane sequences rather than weakens controls); **D early** (hours-old snapshot = reasonable; 60h-old snapshot = not); **F's sampling** (defensible for ambient, indefensible as the *only* gate — 50% of packs got neither scan nor documented release); **I and J's fairness** (legitimate if explicit and capped, unfair if informal); **K and L** (correct and necessary, but they are *debt repayment*, not *resilience*).

**Two distinctions the data forces:**

- **Throughput preservation is not resilience when controls disappear.** T2 looks protective — 5,600 cases at 2,240/day and hospital fill at 91% — but 58% dispatched without scan, **no `Quality/release check` activity exists in the T2 model**, 4.8% of temperature documentation missing, 2.9% dual-control missing, fitness 0.71, and **only 45% of cases reached an end state**. What was preserved was *intake and critical supply*, bought with control coverage.
- **Deviation from the baseline model is not automatically noncompliance — and nonconformance is not automatically deviation.** T2's `Temporary release control` is a *model* deviation that faithfully serves the release objective; T2's `Skip system authorization check` and `Dispatch without scan` are *log* deviations that abandon it. Conformance must therefore be measured against **two models** — the normative model and an approved *continuity model* containing the sanctioned variants — or genuine violations (C, G) stay buried inside a single fitness number of 0.71.

**Finally, after-the-fact audit remediation is not real-time compliance.** L can attach evidence that exists elsewhere; it cannot manufacture a temperature excursion record, a second controlled-substance signature, or a custody scan. Where the physical event never occurred, L's correct output is a **deviation record and a product/regulatory disposition**, not a completed case. The tell that this distinction was being blurred is that T4's audit queue and reconciliation branches persisted long after Hospital-critical median cycle time returned to baseline 8h — **operational recovery was declared while compliance recovery was still open.**

## 4. Recovery process model

### 4.1 Notation legend (POWL / process-tree style)

- `` **sequence**: left element completes before right element.
- `×` **exclusive choice**: exactly one branch is taken.
- `` **concurrency**: branches interleave freely; *all* branches must complete before the enclosing sequence advances.
- `[name]` activity or sub-process.
- `` loop / re-entry.

### 4.2 The model

```
DisruptionResponse 

(
  [1 Trigger  Declare],
  [2 Triage: ×( FastLane , StandardManualLane , ControlledLane ,
                ColdChainLane , DeferLane , HoldLane )],
  ( [3 Fulfill: ( Authorize , Reserve , Pick , Pack , Release ,
                     Handoff , ProofOfDelivery , Invoice )  Backlog ],
     [4 Evidence: ( IssueForm , RecordActualTime , DualSignWhereRequired ,
                     PhotoUpload  ReconstructionQueue )] ),
  [5 BacklogPrioritise: ( Score , Sequence , Execute , CancelApproval )],
  [6 Reconcile: ( MatchExpectedVsObserved ,
                    ×( LinkExistingEvidence , RaiseDeviation ) )],
  [7 Remediate: ( WorkQueue , EvidenceClosure , CaseClose )  ],
  [8 Exit: ( OpsGate , ComplianceGate )]
)
```

*Read as:* trigger and declaration first; one triage route per order; **fulfilment and evidence capture run concurrently and both must finish** before the case can be reconciled; backlog scoring precedes any cancellation; reconciliation precedes remediation; and the incident ends only after **both** the operational gate and the compliance gate pass.

### 4.3 Explicit trigger conditions

| ID | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **G1** | WMS / order orchestration unavailable **>15 minutes**, or any confirmed cyber event | Incident declared; continuity mode armed; incident commander and continuity lead named with a written authority matrix |
| **G2a — Band 1 (<4h)** | Degraded mode | Full controls retained; offline pick lists permitted; scans buffered locally; snapshot valid |
| **G2b — Band 2 (4–24h)** | Manual continuity | Product-scope restrictions apply; continuity lead sign-off; **mandatory snapshot refresh every 4h**; numbered forms issued |
| **G2c — Band 3 (24–72h)** | Extended manual mode | Executive sign-off; legal/regulatory notification review; customer communication; **dispatch of Controlled-substance / Cold-chain blocked unless the offline control set is certified in use** |
| **G2d — Band 4 (>72h)** | Prolonged outage | Invoke DR / off-site operation, external support, reduced active catalogue |
| **G3 — Product gate** | No offline control available for a class (temperature logging, dual control, lot capture) | That class **auto-holds regardless of customer segment** |
| **G4 — Authorization gate** | No verifiable authorization record | **Hold. No ambient exemption.** (directly retires adaptation C) |
| **G5 — Evidence gate** | Numbered forms or photo capture unavailable | Dispatch blocked for Controlled and lot-tracked items; dual signature mandatory |

### 4.4 Triage rules (deterministic, time-stamped, published within 4 hours)

`P = 3×Hospital-critical + 2×Pharmacy-standard + 1×Wholesale-bulk` **plus** product adder `+4 Controlled, +3 Cold-chain/Recall-sensitive, +1 Ambient` **plus** age adder `+1 per 12h open`.

> **Governing design rule: product risk sets the *control level*; customer criticality sets only the *sequence*.** This is the precise defect in adaptation A — the fast lane changed sequencing and also, in 80 cases, suppressed controls.

- **FastLane** — Hospital-critical, any product: expedited *sequence*, full control set, documented manual authorization before dispatch.
- **StandardManualLane** — Pharmacy-standard / Wholesale-bulk on-system path with offline forms.
- **ControlledLane** — 100% dual sign-off, 100% release, lot/serial capture, witnessed handoff, signed POD. No sampling, no fast lane.
- **ColdChainLane** — logger start record before pick + receiver temperature acknowledgement; no logger  hold.
- **DeferLane** — capacity rationing under §4.7 rules; customer notified; deferral counter incremented.
- **HoldLane** — any failed gate; release only by named approver, logged as an exception with expiry.

### 4.5 Minimum controls by product / customer risk

| Product × customer | Authorization | Inventory | Pick evidence | Release | Dispatch evidence | POD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Any + Controlled-substance** | Dual manual verification (2 named people) | 4h snapshot + vault count spot-check | Numbered list + lot/serial, second signature | **100% pharmacist**, no sampling | Witnessed handoff log, tamper-seal number, 2 signatures | Signed note with lot/serial + actual time |
| **Any + Cold-chain** | Manual verification | 4h snapshot + location check | Numbered list + pack-out time | **100% condition verification** | Handoff log **+ logger record** | Signed note + receiver temp ack |
| **Any + Recall-sensitive** | Manual verification | 4h snapshot | **100% lot/expiry capture** | 100% lot verification at pack | Handoff log with lot | Signed note with lot |
| **Hospital-critical + Ambient** | Manual check (form + phone), B-pattern | 4h snapshot | Numbered list + photo upload | Published sample rule; 100% in Band 3+ | Two-person timed handoff log | Signed photo of delivery note |
| **Pharmacy-standard / Wholesale-bulk + Ambient** | Manual check; may enter DeferLane | 4h snapshot | Numbered list | Sample rule | Handoff log (carrier POD acceptable) | Carrier POD + reconstruction flag |

### 4.6 Manual evidence capture

- Pre-numbered **triplicate forms** (pick / release / handoff / POD), form ID bound to case ID; single-use, reconciled like controlled stationery.
- Mandatory fields: **actual event time (handwritten)**, recorder identity, second identity where dual control applies.
- Photograph and upload within **24h** into a reconstruction queue; every record carries `source=manual`, `actual_time`, `entry_time`, `reconstructed=true|false`.
- **No back-signing of dual control.** Missing dual control  deviation record, never "completed".
- Controlled products travel in a numbered tamper-evident envelope whose seal number is captured at handoff — this is the direct replacement for adaptation G's missing scan.

### 4.7 Backlog prioritisation

- Apply the score in §4.4; publish the rule set at the start of the backlog phase (retires the "possibly unfair if informal" ambiguity in J).
- **Cancellation preconditions (all three):** logged customer contact attempt, substitute/alternative offered, supervisor approval.
- **Deferral caps:** Pharmacy-standard max 48h without escalation; no customer more than 2 consecutive deferrals; allocated/shortage product never deferred without documented approval.
- Daily fairness dashboard: deferrals and cancellations per customer and per segment, plus the service-spread ratio.

### 4.8 Reconciliation and audit-remediation flow

- **R1 — Match:** automated comparison of *expected vs observed* events per case (authorization, reservation, scan, release, temperature, dual control, POD, invoice)  status  {complete, reconstructed, gap}.
- **R2 — Classify every gap:**
  - *Remediable* (evidence exists elsewhere)  link evidence, set `reconstructed=true`, close.
  - *Irreparable* (physical event never occurred)  **deviation record + disposition**: cold-chain without temperature evidence  quarantine and stability/release decision; controlled substance without dual control  deviation and regulatory-notification assessment; missing POD  billing hold until claim evidence or credit.
- **R3 — Work queue:** SLA, named staffing, burn-down chart, queue age reported daily. Cases in queue are **operationally closed but not compliantly closed** — two distinct states.
- **R4 — Two exit gates (both required to end incident mode):**
  - **Ops gate:** median cycle time within 1.25× baseline for all three segments; fill rate  baseline 2pp; backlog age p90 within segment SLA; daily throughput stable for 3 days.
  - **Compliance gate:** conformance fitness **0.95** and fitting traces **90%** *against the approved continuity model*; traceability exception rate 1.5× baseline **and absolute count  baseline**; cold-chain temperature and controlled dual-control completeness **99.5% of the at-risk population**; remediation queue age within SLA with **zero open irreparable gaps lacking a deviation record**; 100% of manual records flagged and retained.

## 7. Management trade-off statement

Management should accept that during a genuine system outage cycle times will lengthen, fill rates will dip, and some non-urgent orders will be deferred or cancelled — provided the rationing rules are written down, published within hours, time-capped, notified to customers, counter-tracked per customer, and applied by sequence rather than by silently reducing controls, and provided the critical hospital lane is protected (91% fill, 10h median) as it was here. Management should **not** accept shipments with no verifiable authorization (adaptation C's 1,600 cases), dispatch with no contemporaneous custody evidence (G's 3,250 cases — 58% of the phase), sampling that leaves half of packed orders with neither a scan nor a documented release (F's 1,800 cases), inventory validated against a snapshot beyond a 4-hour TTL (D, which reconciled into 1,400 corrections and 300 cancellations), rate-only reporting that showed risk halving in T3 while absolute exceptions rose 16% and rework rose 47%, informal fairness rules that shifted the outage's cost onto Pharmacy-standard customers (fill 61%) and produced 30% cancellation of prioritised backlog, or the presentation of after-the-fact audit remediation as restored compliance when fitness was still 0.90/76%, exceptions 2.75× baseline, and roughly 600 cases remained open in the remediation queue — remediation that can attach missing evidence but can never create a temperature record or a second signature that never existed. What would change this recommendation is evidence, in either direction: validated offline controls demonstrating contemporaneous, reproducible, equivalent evidence (authorization coverage 99%, release-gate coverage 100% for controlled and cold-chain, detection ratio 80%, conformance 0.95/90% against the approved continuity model, remediation queue closed within SLA) would justify widening Band 1–2 autonomy and relaxing holds; conversely, confirmation that a control gap is physically irreparable — irrecoverable temperature data or an unsigned controlled-substance dual check — should **narrow** autonomy, trigger a deviation record and product-level disposition (quarantine/stability assessment for cold chain, regulatory-notification assessment for controlled substances), extend incident mode rather than close it, and widen customer and, where required, regulator notification.
