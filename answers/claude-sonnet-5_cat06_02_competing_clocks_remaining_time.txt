## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

**A. Smooth path**
- *Chemistry wait+service:* 4 jobs ahead, ~2.75 analyst-hours of Friday capacity remain after finishing in-progress jobs  at most ~1 of the 4 clears today; B-417 usually does **not** start before 18:00 close. Work pauses, resumes Mon 08:00. Under risk-priority (45% of cases) B-417 may grab a slot ~16:40–17:30 Friday, run 1–1.3 h before pausing, then finish Monday within ~0.5 h of opening. Either way: chemistry usable result  **Mon 08:30–14:00** (queue + mean service 1.5 h wait + 2.5 h mode service, occasionally more).
- *Microbiology incubation+readout:* Ready Sun 12:00–20:00 (46–54 h). Without weekend authorization (majority case), read Monday from 08:00, +0.5–1.5 h  **Mon 08:30–09:30**. With authorization (25–45%), read Sun 13:00–23:00.
- *Parallel join:* Use **max**, not sum, of the two branch completion times. In the majority sub-case (no weekend authorization), both branches converge near Monday morning, and chemistry's longer service time makes it the binding branch  **join  Mon 10:00–14:00**. In the minority sub-case (chemistry finishes Friday + micro authorized weekend), join could be as early as **Sun 13:00–23:00**, but QA is closed Sunday, so nothing happens until Monday anyway.
- *QA wait+service:* QA is closed Sun; earliest QA action is Mon 08:00. Backlog: 5 current + ~4–5 new arrivals accumulated over ~10–14 open-hours of Fri evening/Sat/Mon-morning (0.35/h, overdispersed) worked down partly by Sat (1 reviewer, 4 h) and 2 Monday reviewers. Net wait 1–5 h; service median 1.5 h (p80 3.5 h). Total QA stage  **2–8 h**.
- *Rework:* none in this scenario by definition.
- *Release:* +0.25–0.75 h.
- **Terminal:** Mon 21 Sep ~15:00 to Tue 22 Sep ~10:00.

**B. Chemistry rerun**
- Chemistry primary run resolves borderline (10–20%, up to ~25% with excursion), triggering rerun (triangular 1.0/1.8/3.0 h) that gets priority but cannot cross a closing boundary. If the primary run finishes Monday afternoon near 17:00–18:00, the rerun cannot fit and pauses to **Tue 08:00**, adding a full overnight calendar gap for ~1–3 h of actual work.
- Microbiology proceeds as in A (Sun/Mon).
- *Join* = max(chemistry-with-rerun, microbiology)  chemistry now clearly dominant: **Mon 17:00–Tue 11:00**.
- QA stage similar to A but queue may be larger by Tuesday (+more overdispersed arrivals): **2–8 h**.
- **Terminal:** Mon 21 Sep evening  Wed 23 Sep morning.

**C. Delayed/complicated microbiology**
- Sub-case "no authorization" is already inside A's envelope (Mon 08:30 readout).
- Contamination sub-case: initial readout (Mon ~08:30–09:30, or Sun evening if authorized) flags contamination (5–12%, higher with excursion). Confirmatory incubation +20–30 h continuous  ready **Tue ~05:00–15:00**; readout needs a microbiologist, so if the ready-time falls outside 08–18 it waits to next opening  realistic confirmed-result time **Tue 09:00–16:00**.
- Chemistry (independent-ish branch) likely already resolved Monday, so **join = microbiology-confirmation time**, i.e., **Tue 09:00–16:00** (a clean use of "max," now with microbiology as the binding branch).
- QA stage: 2–8 h as before, but investigation probability is elevated (30–55%) *because* an anomaly exists — see scenario D/E coupling.
- **Terminal:** Tue evening  Thu/Fri if investigation stacks on top.

**D. QA query or investigation tail**
- After join (from A, B, or C), QA reviews and, with documentation completeness 0.82, raises a Documentation Query with prob. 0.15–0.30, adding 4–12 *working* hours (roughly 1–2 QA business days given Sat's short window and Sunday closure) before QA resumes.
- Alternatively, opens a Deviation Investigation: baseline 4–10% (clean branches) or 30–55% (any rerun/contamination/excursion). 60–80% of these are documentation-only (+8–20 working hours, ~1.5–3 business days); the remainder require lab work (+3–7 **calendar** days, running through weekends).
- These are not simply additive to A/B/C — they start the clock only *after* whichever join/QA-first-touch time applies, then stack their own duration on top.
- **Terminal:** ranges from same-day (short query) to +3–7 calendar days beyond first QA touch (lab investigation) — i.e., stretching into **Fri 25 Sep–Tue 29 Sep** or later in combination with B/C.

**E. Compound worst case**
- Temperature excursion confirmed (20–40%) raises P(borderline chemistry), P(contamination), and P(investigation) jointly — this is the dependence structure the scenario warns about; treat as a correlated shock rather than three independent low-probability draws.
- Effective path: chemistry rerun (B) **and** microbiology contamination confirmation (C) **and** an opened, possibly laboratory, investigation (D) — a near-worst intersection.
- Using max/then-add logic: join pushed to Tue–Wed by microbiology; QA investigation (lab-type) adds 3–7 calendar days afterward.
- **Terminal:** roughly **Fri 25 Sep – following Tue 29 Sep**, occasionally later.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers (ranked)

1. **QA rework path (Documentation Query / Deviation Investigation, especially laboratory investigation)** — *path uncertainty*. Single largest driver of tail length (adds days, not hours).
2. **Weekend microbiology staffing authorization** — *calendar / resource-authorization uncertainty*. Determines whether microbiology clears Sunday or waits to Monday, and interacts with whether it becomes the binding branch.
3. **Chemistry queue discipline realization (FIFO vs. risk-priority) and whether B-417 starts before Friday close** — *queue uncertainty*. Governs whether chemistry finishes Friday, Monday morning, or later, and is the most likely binding branch in the modal scenario.
4. **Temperature-excursion flag resolution** — *dependence uncertainty*. Not itself a delay, but it re-weights three downstream probabilities (borderline chemistry, contamination, investigation) simultaneously, making it the key correlator of tail risk.
5. **QA queue congestion at the actual join moment (overdispersed arrivals, reviewer roster)** — *queue uncertainty*. Adds a variable 1–8+ h wait on top of the branch-completion time, and is itself uncertain because the join time (and hence which reviewers are on shift) is uncertain.

(Service-time variance in chemistry/microbiology/QA is present but secondary — it perturbs each stage by ~1–3 h, dwarfed by the above structural/calendar/path effects.)

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

**Primary trigger:** The **first microbiology readout outcome and its timing** — i.e., (a) whether a weekend on-call microbiologist is actually authorized/dispatched, and (b) whether that readout indicates contamination.
- If authorized **and** clean: shifts the microbiology branch fully out of the critical path (chemistry becomes the sole binding branch), tightening the whole distribution toward the earliest/central estimates.
- If unauthorized: locks microbiology to Monday 08:00+, reinforcing the central estimate but ruling out the very-earliest tail.
- If contamination is indicated: immediately shifts the entire forecast toward Scenario C/E, adding ~1–2 days regardless of chemistry's status — the single biggest possible re-rating of the case.

**Secondary trigger:** The **outcome of B-417's chemistry service today** (does it start before 18:00, and is the result borderline/rerun-triggering?). This resolves the queue-discipline ambiguity and tells us whether chemistry will bind the join at Monday morning (smooth) or Monday afternoon/Tuesday (rerun), materially narrowing the central 70–95 h band in either direction.
