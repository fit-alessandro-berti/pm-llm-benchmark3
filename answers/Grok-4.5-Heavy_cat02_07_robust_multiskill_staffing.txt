# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

**Demand and service assumptions (labeled)**  
- A1 (service distributions). Only ranges and complex-tail probabilities are supplied. We therefore treat ordinary and complex service times as independent Uniform draws from the stated intervals; the mixture is formed with the given probability. A multiplier is applied after the draw. Uniform is the maximum-entropy continuous distribution consistent with the supplied bounds.  
- A2 (arrivals). Expected arrivals per two-hour block are taken as given. Within-block arrival process for simulation is Poisson with rate = expected / 120 min⁻¹; case type and priority are drawn multinomially and independently from the block percentages.  
- A3 (ME/ES work content). Mechanical + Electrical (respectively Electrical + Software) work content is additive for offered-load calculations. Parallel execution uses two distinct qualified resources and adds an 8-minute coordination delay before Validation; sequential execution incurs no coordination delay but places the sum on the critical path. Parallel-versus-sequential choice is a policy decision variable.  
- A4 (handover, team-switch, setup). Resource change on the same case adds 7 min elapsed. Crossing the Mech-family ↔ Elec/Soft-family boundary adds a further 5 min unless a single cross-skilled resource retains the case. A resource that switches skill family between consecutive distinct cases incurs a 5-minute setup (resource-busy).  
- A5 (preemption). Only a P1 case may preempt a lower-priority diagnostic activity, at most once per case. The interrupted activity restarts with a 10 % penalty on its remaining service time. Intake and Validation are never preempted.  
- A6 (availability). Shifts and breaks are deterministic. Overtime is capped at 30 min per resource and is used preferentially for in-progress or queued P1 work. Cases unfinished at 20:00 resume the next day (SLA clocks continue).  
- A7 (other). Single-case assignment only; Validation occurs only after all diagnostic work (plus coordination if parallel) is complete. No night shift or additional resources exist.

**How ranges become distributions or bounds**  
Fluid offered-load calculations use the mixture means:  
Intake 6.94 min, Mechanical 26.3 min, Electrical 32.82 min, Software 38.5 min, Validation 15.74 min.  
Simulation and tail metrics draw the full uniforms. Extreme values of the ranges supply hard service-time bounds; doubling of complex-tail probabilities under U4 yields the corresponding higher means (Mechanical 29.6, Electrical 37.14, Software 44.5).

**ME/ES parallelism decision**  
Policy-specific. A transparent threshold compares expected makespan (max of branches + 8) against sequential sum, subject to resource availability, projected Soft/Elec util, and presence of higher-priority work. Under Soft scarcity sequential is preferred.

**Decision variables**  
Eligibility sets and preference order per activity; priority/urgency score (class weight + remaining SLA slack + skill-match + handover continuity – util penalty); reservation flags (P1-preferential and Soft-preferential resources); overflow wait-time / projected-util triggers; parallelization threshold and conditions; preemption enablement and protection of reserved resources; mode-switching thresholds based on observed Soft backlog or scenario flags.

**Objective formulations**  
- Obj1 (tail / SLA risk). Maximize the scenario-weighted average of (0.5·P1_SLA + 0.3·P2_SLA + 0.2·P3_SLA) minus a linear penalty on p90/p95 case delay, with elevated weights on U3 and U5. Constraint: individual planned utilisation ≤ 0.90 under the base scenario.  
- Obj2 (fairness + robustness). Minimize the worst-case (over base + U1–U5) composite  
  (1 – average SLA attainment) + α·utilisation unfairness (max–min gap or Gini of busy fractions) + β·handover/setup burden (extra minutes per case).  
  A policy is declared robust if it attains the lowest value of this worst-case objective.

**Synthesis**. Soft skill scarcity combined with the 16–18 time mismatch is the structural fragility even in the base case. Chen is a single point of failure for Soft (and a major contributor to Electrical). Evening Mechanical capacity gap is secondary but material for late arrivals. Parallelism can shorten P1 critical paths yet risks locking the two scarcest resources. Specialisation therefore fails under Soft or Chen shocks; uncontrolled pooling improves mean delay but degrades fairness and handover burden; a hybrid that protects Soft capacity and P1 reservation addresses the observed bottleneck migration most robustly.

# 3. Candidate policies

All policies respect the common rules of A4–A6 (handover, setup, preemption at most once, OT ≤ 30 min). Priority class order is always P1 > P2 > P3; within class earliest-arrival or tightest remaining SLA slack is used.

**Policy SPEC – Strict specialisation with fixed queues**  
- Eligibility: Intake/Validation – any qualified, prefer mult ≤ 1.15. Mechanical queue – only Ada, Ben, Deepa, Farah (prefer mult = 1). Electrical – Ada, Chen, Deepa, Emil, Farah (prefer mult = 1). Software – Chen, Emil, Farah (prefer Emil).  
- Dispatch: FCFS within priority class on the primary queue. No cross-skill overflow unless the primary queue has been empty > 30 min and a qualified resource is idle.  
- Parallelisation: never for ME/ES (always sequential; prefer a cross-skilled resource to avoid the +5 team-switch).  
- Reservation / preemption: none beyond ordinary priority jump; preemption allowed on any lower-priority diagnostic.  
- Handover minimisation: prefer same-resource continuation when the resource is free within 15 min.  
- Goal: minimise handover/setup and keep behaviour predictable. Fragile to Soft-mix and Chen shocks.

**Policy POOL – Full pooling with transparent urgency dispatch**  
- Eligibility: any resource that possesses the required skill.  
- Priority score (transparent, auditable components):  
  1000·class_weight (P1 = 3, P2 = 2, P3 = 1)  
  + 100·SLA_urgency (max(0, target – elapsed – E[remaining])/target)  
  – 50 if mult > 1.2  
  + 30 if same resource as prior stage  
  – 20 if skill-family setup would be incurred  
  – 40 if current util > 0.85.  
- Dispatch: when a resource frees, assign the eligible waiting activity with highest score (or scan free resources for the global highest-score pair).  
- Parallelisation: if two distinct qualified resources are free within 5 min of each other and expected makespan reduction > 10 min, parallelise; otherwise sequential.  
- Reservation: none.  
- Goal: flexibility and mean-delay reduction at the cost of higher handover/setup and possible util imbalance.

**Policy HYBRID – Reserved P1 capacity + controlled Soft overflow + dynamic parallel**  
- Eligibility: same as POOL, augmented by soft reservations.  
- Reservations:  
  – At least one of {Ada, Deepa} is P1-preferential until 16:00.  
  – After 12:00 Emil is Soft-preferential (+150 score boost for Soft/ES or P1 Soft-related work when Soft backlog ≥ 1 or projected Soft ρ > 0.85). Farah receives the same Soft preference while on shift.  
  – Non-P1 work may claim a reserved resource only if no P1 backlog exists and the resource would otherwise idle.  
- Overflow triggers: primary-skill wait > 10 min (P1), 20 min (P2) or 40 min (P3), or projected block util > 0.88 → allow higher-mult qualified resources. Soft overflow is limited to the three Soft-capable resources; sequential ES is preferred when Soft is scarce.  
- Priority score: POOL score + 200 for P1 on a reserved resource; stronger util penalty above 0.90.  
- Parallelisation: only when (i) two qualified free, (ii) both branches > 20 min expected, (iii) no P1 waiting that could use them, (iv) critical-path reduction > 12–15 min. Otherwise sequential on the best single (preferably cross-skilled) resource.  
- Preemption: permitted, but prefer non-reserved resources.  
- Goal: protect P1 SLAs under surge/absence, keep Soft capacity from collapsing, limit handover burden, and enforce fairness (planned util ≤ 0.90).

# 5. Robust recommendation

**Primary policy: HYBRID.**  
Under Obj2 (worst-case composite of SLA shortfall + util unfairness + handover burden) HYBRID dominates. It keeps planned individual utilisation ≤ 0.90 in the base case, protects Soft capacity via Emil/Farah preferential scoring, shields P1 work with explicit reservations, and only parallelises when the expected critical-path gain justifies the coordination and contention risk. Consequently its degradation under U3 and U5 is materially smaller than that of SPEC (which cannot overflow Soft) or POOL (which overloads remaining Soft resources and incurs high handover). Under Obj1 it remains competitive: base P1_SLA is the highest of the three and remains the highest under the stress scenarios that matter most.

**Fallback mode.**  
If real-time Soft backlog exceeds 3 cases or projected Soft ρ > 1.05 for the next block, switch temporarily to a “Soft-protect” sub-mode: force sequential ES, raise Soft-preferential score further, and defer non-P1 Soft work for up to 20 min. Revert when Soft queue ≤ 1 and projected ρ < 0.85. Trigger thresholds are observable from the live event stream and therefore auditable.

**When another policy is preferable.**  
- Pure SPEC is acceptable only if the organisation values predictability and minimal coordination above all else and can guarantee Chen’s presence and no Soft-mix shocks.  
- POOL may be preferred for a short pure-efficiency campaign on a low-variance day when Soft capacity is known to be ample, provided fairness monitors are active.  
- In all other operating regimes, and especially whenever any of U1–U5 can materialise, HYBRID (with the Soft-protect fallback) is the robust choice.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates.** Operators may inflate reported remaining service times to avoid preemption or to influence urgency scores. Mitigate by using system-generated expected remaining times based on historical averages and by auditing large discrepancies.  
- **Starvation of P3.** Aggressive P1 reservation and Soft preferential scoring can push P3 Soft/Mechanical work into overnight. Soft time-limits (e.g., P3 wait > 4 h forces temporary suspension of non-critical reservations) are required.  
- **Skill erosion.** Specialists who rarely perform secondary skills lose proficiency. Periodic cross-training quotas (even under HYBRID) and monitoring of mult-effective performance prevent long-term degradation.  
- **Excessive preemption.** Restart penalties accumulate; a hard “at most one preemption per case” rule already exists, but a further soft limit (no more than two preemptions per resource per hour) reduces thrashing.  
- **Coordination overhead.** Parallel ME/ES adds the fixed 8 min plus potential dual handovers. The HYBRID threshold already guards against this; under Soft scarcity sequential is forced.  
- **New Validation bottleneck.** Parallel coordination and accumulated handover delays can bunch arrivals at Validation. Validation capacity is ample in the fluid analysis, yet real-time monitoring of Validation queue length is still needed.  
- **Fairness perception.** Even when max util ≤ 0.90, specialists may feel that Soft-preferential rules “steal” interesting work. Transparent score components and published utilisation dashboards reduce resistance.  
- **Human interpretability.** All three policies expose their decision logic (eligibility, score terms, reservation flags, parallel threshold). Operators can audit any assignment; the score breakdown is logged with every dispatch event.

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

**Required event attributes (in addition to the standard XES core)**  
`case:concept:name`, `concept:name` (Intake / Mechanical / Electrical / Software / Validation / Coordination / Handover / Setup / PreemptionPenalty), `start_timestamp`, `time:timestamp`, `org:resource`,  
plus: `lifecycle:transition` (start / complete / suspend / resume), `priority`, `case_type` (M/E/S/ME/ES), `eligible_resource_set` (list), `skill_used`, `planned_service_min`, `actual_service_min`, `remaining_at_preempt`, `handover_min`, `setup_min`, `coordination_min`, `policy_version`, `scenario_or_shock_flag`, `sla_clock_start`, `queue_entry_timestamp`, `assignment_decision_timestamp`, `reservation_flag`, `parallel_branch_id`.

**Deployment indicators**  
- Soft and Electrical block utilisations (rolling 30 min).  
- Soft backlog length and oldest Soft wait.  
- P1 remaining-SLA slack distribution.  
- Handover and setup counts per resource per hour.  
- Preemption frequency and restart-penalty minutes.  
- Realised versus planned service-time ratios (detect mult drift or complex-tail change).  
- Validation queue length and time-in-system quantiles by priority.

**PM4Py analyses that reveal drift and migration**  
- `pm4py.format_dataframe` followed by `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` yields case-level service and waiting; filter by `concept:name` or by priority to obtain stage-specific metrics.  
- `pm4py.get_service_time` (or the equivalent filtered statistics) tracks service-time distribution shift (U4-like or resource-speed change).  
- `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network` quantifies handover burden and identifies unexpected resource pairs.  
- Directly-follows graphs or inductive miner on the filtered Soft/Electrical sub-log expose bottleneck migration under each scenario flag.  
- Performance spectrum or dotted-chart views by resource and by time-of-day locate the Soft 16–18 and evening Mechanical gaps.  
- Case-level waiting-time distributions conditioned on `policy_version` and `scenario_or_shock_flag` measure resilience.

            if ctype in ("E", "ME", "ES"):
                services["Electrical"] = sample_service("Electrical", rng, tail_mult)
            if ctype in ("S", "ES"):
                services["Software"] = sample_service("Software", rng, tail_mult)
            services["Validation"] = sample_service("Validation", rng, 1.0)
            cases.append({
                "case_id": f"C{cid:03d}",
                "arr_time": t,
                "type": ctype,
                "prio": prio,
                "services": services,
                "chen_absent": chen_absent,
            })
            cid += 1
    cases.sort(key=lambda c: c["arr_time"])
    return cases

def assign_and_simulate(cases, policy, rng):
    """
    Approximate assignment: greedy by priority score at arrival or free epochs.
    Returns list of events and summary metrics.
    Explicitly adds handover/setup/coord/preempt penalties to elapsed time.
    """
    events = []
    resource_free = {r: 8.0 for r in RESOURCES}  # hours
    resource_last_family = {r: None for r in RESOURCES}
    resource_busy_min = defaultdict(float)
    handovers = 0
    setups = 0
    coords = 0
    preempts = 0
    case_complete = {}
    case_delay = {}

    for case in cases:
        cid = case["case_id"]
        t = case["arr_time"]
        prio = case["prio"]
        ctype = case["type"]
        services = case["services"]
        chen_abs = case["chen_absent"]
        elapsed = 0.0
        prev_res = None
        prev_family = None
        stages = ["Intake"]
        if ctype == "M":
            stages += ["Mechanical"]
        elif ctype == "E":
            stages += ["Electrical"]
        elif ctype == "S":
            stages += ["Software"]
        elif ctype == "ME":
            stages += ["Mechanical", "Electrical"]  # sequential default; parallel decided below
        elif ctype == "ES":
            stages += ["Electrical", "Software"]
        stages += ["Validation"]

        # Parallel decision (policy-specific)
        do_parallel = False
        if ctype in ("ME", "ES") and policy in ("POOL", "HYBRID"):
            # simplified: parallel if two resources free and Soft not scarce
            elig_m = eligible_resources("Mechanical" if ctype == "ME" else "Electrical", t, chen_abs)
            elig_s = eligible_resources("Electrical" if ctype == "ME" else "Software", t, chen_abs)
            if len(elig_m) >= 1 and len(elig_s) >= 1 and policy == "POOL":
                do_parallel = True
            elif policy == "HYBRID" and len(elig_m) >= 1 and len(elig_s) >= 1:
                # Soft-scarce check (Emil free?)
                soft_free = any(resource_free.get(r, 99) <= t + 0.1 for r, _ in eligible_resources("Software", t, chen_abs))
                if soft_free or ctype == "ME":
                    do_parallel = True

        if do_parallel and ctype == "ME":
            stages = ["Intake", "Mechanical+Electrical", "Validation"]
            coords += 1
        elif do_parallel and ctype == "ES":
            stages = ["Intake", "Electrical+Software", "Validation"]
            coords += 1

        current_t = t
        for stage in stages:
            if "+" in stage:
                # parallel: take max of two services + coord
                s1, s2 = stage.split("+")
                base1 = services[s1]
                base2 = services[s2]
                elig1 = eligible_resources(s1, current_t, chen_abs)
                elig2 = eligible_resources(s2, current_t, chen_abs)
                if not elig1 or not elig2:
                    # fall back sequential
                    do_parallel = False
                    stages = [st for st in stages if "+" not in st]
                    # re-process this stage later – simplified skip
                    continue
                # choose fastest available
                res1 = min(elig1, key=lambda x: (max(resource_free[x[0]], current_t), x[1]))[0]
                res2 = min(elig2, key=lambda x: (max(resource_free[x[0]], current_t), x[1]))[0]
                start1 = max(resource_free[res1], current_t)
                start2 = max(resource_free[res2], current_t)
                mult1 = RESOURCES[res1]["mult"][s1]
                mult2 = RESOURCES[res2]["mult"][s2]
                dur1 = base1 * mult1
                dur2 = base2 * mult2
                # preemption approx for P1
                if prio == "P1":
                    preempts += 0.5  # fractional for approx
                    dur1 *= (1 + PREEMPT_PENALTY_FRAC * 0.5)
                    dur2 *= (1 + PREEMPT_PENALTY_FRAC * 0.5)
                end1 = start1 + dur1 / 60.0
                end2 = start2 + dur2 / 60.0
                finish = max(end1, end2) + COORD_MIN / 60.0
                resource_free[res1] = end1
                resource_free[res2] = end2
                resource_busy_min[res1] += dur1
                resource_busy_min[res2] += dur2
                events.append({"case:concept:name": cid, "concept:name": s1, "start_timestamp": start1,
                               "time:timestamp": end1, "org:resource": res1, "prio": prio})
                events.append({"case:concept:name": cid, "concept:name": s2, "start_timestamp": start2,
                               "time:timestamp": end2, "org:resource": res2, "prio": prio})
                events.append({"case:concept:name": cid, "concept:name": "Coordination", "start_timestamp": max(end1, end2),
                               "time:timestamp": finish, "org:resource": "SYSTEM", "prio": prio})
                current_t = finish
                prev_res = None  # parallel ends continuity
                continue

            # sequential stage
            act = stage
            base = services[act]
            elig = eligible_resources(act, current_t, chen_abs)
            if not elig:
                # no resource – delay until next possible (approx +1 h)
                current_t += 1.0
                elig = eligible_resources(act, current_t, chen_abs)
                if not elig:
                    current_t += 2.0
                    elig = eligible_resources(act, current_t, chen_abs)
            # policy-specific choice
            if policy == "SPEC":
                # prefer lowest mult primary
                elig = sorted(elig, key=lambda x: x[1])
            elif policy == "POOL":
                # urgency + skill match + continuity
                def score(item):
                    res, mult = item
                    sc = 0
                    if prio == "P1": sc += 3000
                    elif prio == "P2": sc += 2000
                    else: sc += 1000
                    sc -= 50 * max(0, mult - 1.0)
                    if res == prev_res: sc += 30
                    return -sc  # lower better for min
                elig = sorted(elig, key=score)
            else:  # HYBRID
                def score(item):
                    res, mult = item
                    sc = 0
                    if prio == "P1": sc += 3200
                    elif prio == "P2": sc += 2000
                    else: sc += 1000
                    if act == "Software" and res in ("Emil", "Farah"):
                        sc += 150
                    if prio == "P1" and res in ("Ada", "Deepa", "Emil"):
                        sc += 200
                    sc -= 50 * max(0, mult - 1.0)
                    if res == prev_res: sc += 30
                    return -sc
                elig = sorted(elig, key=score)

            res, mult = elig[0]
            start = max(resource_free[res], current_t)
            # setup if family switch
            family = "Mech" if act == "Mechanical" else ("ElecSoft" if act in ("Electrical", "Software") else "Other")
            if resource_last_family[res] is not None and resource_last_family[res] != family and family != "Other":
                start += SETUP_MIN / 60.0
                setups += 1
            # handover
            if prev_res is not None and prev_res != res:
                start += HANDOVER_MIN / 60.0
                handovers += 1
                if prev_family and family and prev_family != family and family != "Other" and prev_family != "Other":
                    start += TEAM_SWITCH_MIN / 60.0
            # preemption approx
            dur = base * mult
            if prio == "P1" and act in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                preempts += 1
                dur *= (1 + PREEMPT_PENALTY_FRAC)
            end = start + dur / 60.0
            resource_free[res] = end
            resource_busy_min[res] += dur
            resource_last_family[res] = family
            events.append({"case:concept:name": cid, "concept:name": act,
                           "start_timestamp": start, "time:timestamp": end,
                           "org:resource": res, "prio": prio})
            current_t = end
            prev_res = res
            prev_family = family

        case_complete[cid] = current_t
        case_delay[cid] = (current_t - case["arr_time"]) * 60  # minutes

    # metrics
    delays = list(case_delay.values())
    mean_d = np.mean(delays) if delays else 0
    p90_d = np.percentile(delays, 90) if delays else 0
    p1_cases = [c for c in cases if c["prio"] == "P1"]
    p1_ok = sum(1 for c in p1_cases if case_delay.get(c["case_id"], 999) <= 120)
    p1_sla = 100 * p1_ok / max(1, len(p1_cases))
    p2_cases = [c for c in cases if c["prio"] == "P2"]
    p2_ok = sum(1 for c in p2_cases if case_delay.get(c["case_id"], 999) <= 480)
    p2_sla = 100 * p2_ok / max(1, len(p2_cases))
    max_util = 0.0
    for r, busy in resource_busy_min.items():
        avail = (RESOURCES[r]["shift"][1] - RESOURCES[r]["shift"][0]) * 60
        if RESOURCES[r]["break"]:
            avail -= 30
        util = busy / max(1, avail)
        max_util = max(max_util, util)
    return {
        "events": events,
        "mean_delay": mean_d,
        "p90_delay": p90_d,
        "P1_SLA": p1_sla,
        "P2_SLA": p2_sla,
        "max_util": max_util,
        "handovers": handovers,
        "setups": setups,
        "coords": coords,
        "preempts": preempts,
        "n_cases": len(cases),
    }

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Run base + U scenarios for three policies (few seeds for uncertainty)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
def run_comparison(n_seeds=3):
    scenarios = ["Base", "U1", "U2", "U3", "U4", "U5"]
    policies = ["SPEC", "POOL", "HYBRID"]
    results = []
    all_events = []
    for scen in scenarios:
        for pol in policies:
            metrics_list = []
            for s in range(n_seeds):
                cases = generate_cases(scen, np.random.default_rng(s), seed=100 * s + hash(scen) % 100)
                m = assign_and_simulate(cases, pol, np.random.default_rng(s + 7))
                metrics_list.append(m)
                if s == 0:  # keep one event log per policy-scenario
                    for e in m["events"]:
                        e["policy"] = pol
                        e["scenario"] = scen
                    all_events.extend(m["events"])
            # summary
            results.append({
                "scenario": scen,
                "policy": pol,
                "mean_delay": np.mean([x["mean_delay"] for x in metrics_list]),
                "p90_delay": np.mean([x["p90_delay"] for x in metrics_list]),
                "P1_SLA": np.mean([x["P1_SLA"] for x in metrics_list]),
                "P2_SLA": np.mean([x["P2_SLA"] for x in metrics_list]),
                "max_util": np.mean([x["max_util"] for x in metrics_list]),
                "handovers": np.mean([x["handovers"] for x in metrics_list]),
                "n_cases": metrics_list[0]["n_cases"],
            })
    return pd.DataFrame(results), all_events

df_metrics, event_list = run_comparison(n_seeds=2)

# 9. Limits and validation plan

**What cannot be concluded from expected arrivals and service ranges alone**  
- Exact queueing delays under high variance and preemption cascades require either a full discrete-event simulation with mid-service interrupts or empirical observation; fluid ρ only flags potential overload.  
- Real resource behaviour (fatigue, informal help, preference for certain case types) is invisible.  
- Correlation of complex tails across activities of the same case, or between arrival mix and time of day beyond the supplied blocks, is unknown.  
- Operator acceptance of score-based dispatch and the true frequency of skill-family switches can only be measured in situ.  
- Overnight spill-over and next-day interactions are outside the single-day scope.

**Pilot / backtest design**  
1. Historical or shadow-mode backtest: replay the last 4–6 weeks of real event logs under the three policy assignment rules (offline), protecting every historical P1 by forcing it onto a reserved resource if the policy would otherwise delay it beyond 90 min. Compare realised versus counterfactual SLA attainment, Soft util, and handover counts.  
2. Controlled pilot (2 weeks): run HYBRID on one geographic or product segment while SPEC remains the default elsewhere. Live Soft-backlog and P1-SLA monitors with automatic fall-back to Soft-protect mode if Soft queue > 3. Daily fairness and handover dashboards visible to operators.  
3. Safety gates: any P1 breach triggers immediate reversion to the previous policy for that case; cumulative P1_SLA < 85 % over any 4-hour window aborts the pilot.  
4. Success criteria: HYBRID shows (a) P1_SLA ≥ base SPEC, (b) Soft peak util ≤ 0.95, (c) handover burden not more than 30 % higher than SPEC, (d) no statistically significant rise in P3 overnight volume. Only then expand.

This design distinguishes the policies while never exposing the full organisation to an untested Soft-capacity risk.
