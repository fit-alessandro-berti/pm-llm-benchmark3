### Alias Normalization and Service-Account Attribution Methodology
1. **Deterministic Alias Normalization (Pre-SoD):** Before evaluating any segregation-of-duties (RR1, RR2) or social-network metrics, raw account identifiers (`org:resource`) are mapped to canonical human identifiers (`canonical_human`). Specifically, `u_bk` and `b.koch` both resolve to `Ben_Koch`. Evaluating RR1 on raw IDs would miss self-approval across aliases (CHG-508).
2. **Tiered Service-Account Attribution (`svc_qms`):** Under RR5, `svc_qms` is never treated as an authorized actor on its own. Instead, each `svc_qms` event is resolved at the **event-instance level** using auxiliary session forensics:
   - **High-confidence human binding:** Where multi-factor physical + cryptographic token evidence binds the session to a specific person immediately prior to the timestamp (CHG-509: `WS-IV-02` badge + personal FIDO token at `11:58Z` for a `12:00Z` action), the event is attributed to `Eva_Novak` (`attributed_confidence = HIGH`), while retaining a technical logging-hygiene flag for shared-account usage.
   - **Unresolved / Unattributed service session:** Where evidence shows only room entry and a shared terminal without individual re-authentication or CCTV (CHG-503: `WS-DM-04`, room badge by `Diego_Martins`), the actor is coded as `UNATTRIBUTED_SVC_QMS` (`attributed_confidence = LOW`), which automatically fails RR5 conformance. For RR2 risk sensitivity, we also test the adverse bound (`suspect_human = Diego_Martins`) without asserting proof.
3. **Contested Personal-Account Sessions:** Where a human account (`u_bk`, `u_as`) exhibits availability/location anomalies (CHG-506, CHG-505 retrospective review, CHG-504), normalization maps the account to the nominal human for baseline rule checks, while an orthogonal **Identity Integrity Flag** prevents naive "technical pass" conclusions when impersonation or credential sharing is indicated.

## 2. Rule Interpretation

### Formalization of Resource Rules (RR1–RR6)
Let $H(e)$ denote the resolved canonical human for event $e$ (returning $\bot$ if unattributed), $t(e)$ its UTC timestamp, $c(e)$ its case, $\text{risk}(c) \in \{\text{LOW}, \text{HIGH}\}$, $\text{scope}(c)$ its technical domain, and $\text{emerg}(c) \in \{\text{true}, \text{false}\}$.

* **RR1 (Drafter/Approver Separation):** For all cases $c$, if $e_d = \text{Draft\_Change\_Request}(c)$ and $e_a = \text{Approve\_Change}(c)$, conformance requires $H(e_d) \neq \bot$, $H(e_a) \neq \bot$, and $H(e_d) \neq H(e_a)$. RR1 applies **unconditionally across both LOW- and HIGH-risk cases**.
* **RR2 (Implementer/Verifier Separation):** For all cases $c$, if $e_i = \text{Implement\_Change}(c)$ and $e_v = \text{Verify\_Implementation}(c)$, conformance requires $H(e_i) \neq \bot$, $H(e_v) \neq \bot$, and $H(e_i) \neq H(e_v)$.
* **RR3 (High-Risk Approval Authority):** If $\text{risk}(c) = \text{HIGH}$ and $\neg\text{emerg}(c)$ (or non-emergency pathway), $H(e_a)$ must hold standing role $\text{QM}$ at $t(e_a)$ **or** hold a delegation $D$ from a standing $\text{QM}$ $P$ such that: (i) $t(e_a) \in [t_{\text{start}}(D), t_{\text{end}}(D)]$, (ii) $\text{scope}(c) \subseteq \text{scope}(D)$, (iii) if $D$ requires principal absence, $P$ is absent/unavailable at $t(e_a)$, (iv) $D$ is not derived from prohibited subdelegation, and (v) $D$ is supported by valid pre-event authorization evidence.
* **RR4 (Emergency Substitution & Retrospective QM Review):** If $\text{risk}(c) = \text{HIGH}$ and $\text{emerg}(c) = \text{true}$ with credible emergency/safety rationale:
  1. $H(e_a)$ prior to $e_i$ must hold standing $\text{IC}$ (or $\text{QM}$) at $t(e_a)$, or a valid $\text{IC}$ delegation satisfying interval, incident scope, and non-subdelegation constraints; **and**
  2. An event $e_r = \text{Retrospective\_QM\_Review}(c)$ must occur within **2 business days** of emergency execution, performed by a human $H(e_r)$ holding valid standing $\text{QM}$ or valid $\text{QM}$ delegation **covering $\text{scope}(c)$ at $t(e_r)$**. (Although RR1 explicitly names `Approve_Change`, sound control objectives also require $H(e_r) \neq H(e_d)$.)
* **RR5 (Identity Evidence):** Conformance requires $H(e) \neq \bot$ backed by trustworthy authentication. A service account (`svc_qms`) satisfies RR5 if and only if independent session telemetry (e.g., personal hardware token + proximate badge log on dedicated station) uniquely attributes the action to an authorized human. Furthermore, if credible physical/HR evidence refutes that the account owner performed the action (e.g., certified sick leave + denial + office IP), RR5 fails due to compromised identity integrity.
* **RR6 (Suspicion vs. Proof):** Statistical network skew (high pair frequency, reciprocity, shared rooms) raises an audit hypothesis (`SUSPICIOUS_PATTERN`) warranting targeted review, never standalone proof of `UNAUTHORIZED` collusion.

### Specific Doctrinal Determinations
1. **Counting Business Days for RR4:** Business days are Monday–Friday on the site calendar, excluding Saturday (`2026-08-15`), Sunday (`2026-08-16`), and site holidays (none in this period). For an emergency change approved/implemented on business day $D_0$, Day $D_0 + 1$ is the first business day and Day $D_0 + 2$ is the second business day (deadline `23:59:59Z` on $D_0 + 2$, or strictly $+48$ business-day hours from approval/implementation). Under **both** exact-timestamp and end-of-business-day rules:
   - `2026-08-11` (Tue) $\rightarrow$ Day 1 is `2026-08-12` (Wed), Day 2 is `2026-08-13` (Thu).
   - CHG-510 reviewed `2026-08-12 10:00Z` = **1 business day** (timely, though invalid on authority).
   - CHG-505 reviewed `2026-08-14 16:00Z` (Fri) = **3 business days** (untimely under RR4).
2. **Action by a Principal While on Leave:** Annual leave is an HR scheduling state, not a suspension or revocation of quality qualification or standing QM authority. Therefore, when Asha (`u_as`) logs in via VPN on `2026-08-13 09:22Z` during recorded leave to approve CHG-504, her **standing QM authority remains legally valid**, provided she actually performed the act (supported by her affirmative statement, though weakened by lack of MFA proof against credential sharing). However, a principal’s active intervention temporarily interrupts "while principal is absent" status during her active session (`09:15–09:40Z`): Ben could not validly claim Asha was "unavailable" at `09:22Z`, though absence resumed once she disconnected. Conversely, **weekend off-duty status** (`2026-08-15` in CHG-509) represents practical off-site unavailability for a date-specific Saturday delegation (`D-QM-02`), even though formal HR annual leave ended `2026-08-14`—a minor documentation gap rather than substantive usurpation.
3. **Can a Later Database Entry Prove an Earlier Delegation?** **No, not by itself—but a verifiable pre-existing signed instrument can.** A QMS database row created on `2026-08-12 09:15Z` cannot *prove* authority on `2026-08-11 09:00Z` (CHG-501), because retrospective database insertion without contemporaneous evidence violates ALCOA+ *contemporaneous* and *original* principles and could mask post-hoc backdating. However, `D-QM-01` is evidenced by a **signed PDF bearing a timestamp of `2026-08-09 18:00Z`** (prior to both `2026-08-10` and CHG-501). Assuming the PDF’s digital signature/timestamp is valid, substantive delegation authority existed at event time (`VALID_SUBSTITUTION`), while the delayed QMS database entry reflects a master-data migration lag (`DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM`) that disabled automated preventive QMS controls during Aug 10–12.

def detect_four_eyes_violations(

):

        pm4py_violations = returned_cases & cases_with_both
    else:
        pm4py_violations = cases_with_both - returned_cases
    return sorted(pm4py_violations)

def evaluate_approval_event(row, case_events):
    ts = row["time:timestamp"]
    actor = row["canonical_actor"]
    risk = row["risk"]
    scope = row["scope"]
    emerg = row["emergency"]
    act = row["concept:name"]
    flags = []

    # Availability / credential anomaly checks
    if actor == "Ben_Koch" and (BEN_SICK[0] <= ts <= BEN_SICK[1]):
        flags.append("ACTOR_ON_CERTIFIED_SICK_LEAVE_CREDENTIAL_ANOMALY")
    if actor == "Asha_Rao" and (ASHA_LEAVE[0] <= ts <= ASHA_LEAVE[1]):
        flags.append(
            "PRINCIPAL_ACTING_DURING_ANNUAL_LEAVE_VPN"
            if (ASHA_VPN[0] <= ts <= ASHA_VPN[1])
            else "PRINCIPAL_ON_LEAVE_NO_VPN"
        )
    if row["raw_resource"] != "u_bk" and actor == "Ben_Koch":
        flags.append(f"SECONDARY_ALIAS_USED({row['raw_resource']})")

    # Standing roles at event timestamp
    s_roles = set(
        STANDING_ROLES[
            (STANDING_ROLES["actor"] == actor)
            & (STANDING_ROLES["start"] <= ts)
            & (ts <= STANDING_ROLES["end"])
        ]["role"]
    )

    # Required role logic
    if act == "Approve_Change" and risk == "LOW":
        return "STANDING_LOW_RISK_OK", s_roles, flags

    target_roles = (
        {"QM", "IC"}
        if (act == "Approve_Change" and risk == "HIGH" and emerg)
        else {"QM"}
    )

    # Check timing for Retrospective_QM_Review (RR4: <= 2 business days)
    if act == "Retrospective_QM_Review":
        ref_ev = case_events[
            case_events["concept:name"].isin(
                ["Approve_Change", "Implement_Change"]
            )
        ]["time:timestamp"].min()
        b_days = int(
            np.busday_count(
                ref_ev.strftime("%Y-%m-%d"), ts.strftime("%Y-%m-%d")
            )
        )
        if b_days > 2:
            flags.append(f"RR4_LATE_RETROSPECTIVE_REVIEW({b_days}_BUSDAYS)")

    if s_roles & target_roles:
        return f"AUTHORIZED_STANDING({','.join(s_roles & target_roles)})", s_roles, flags

    # Check delegations at event timestamp
    cand_del = DELEGATIONS[
        (DELEGATIONS["delegate"] == actor)
        & (DELEGATIONS["del_role"].isin(target_roles))
        & (DELEGATIONS["start"] <= ts)
        & (ts <= DELEGATIONS["end"])
    ]

    if cand_del.empty:
        # Diagnose why delegation failed (scope/prohibited/time)
        any_del = DELEGATIONS[(DELEGATIONS["delegate"] == actor)]
        flags.append("RR3_RR4_NO_VALID_AUTHORITY_AT_TIMESTAMP")
        return "UNAUTHORIZED_NO_ROLE_OR_ACTIVE_DELEGATION", s_roles, flags

    for _, d in cand_del.iterrows():
        if d["prohibited_subdel"]:
            flags.append(f"INVALID_PROHIBITED_SUBDELEGATION({d['del_id']})")
            continue
        if d["scope"] != scope:
            flags.append(
                f"OUT_OF_DELEGATION_SCOPE({d['del_id']}:{d['scope']}!={scope})"
            )
            continue
        if d["req_principal_absent"]:
            absent_ok, abs_note = check_principal_absent(d["principal"], ts)
            if not absent_ok:
                flags.append(f"PRINCIPAL_NOT_ABSENT({d['del_id']})")
                continue
            if "Weekend" in abs_note:
                flags.append("WEEKEND_ABSENCE_INFERRED_ONLY")
        if ts < d["db_ts"] and ts >= d["doc_ts"]:
            flags.append(f"RETROSPECTIVE_DB_MIGRATION_PDF_PRECEDED({d['del_id']})")
        if d["del_id"] == "D-IC-03":
            flags.append("VERIFY_CI44_INCIDENT_LINK_MANUALLY")
        return f"VALID_DELEGATION({d['del_id']})", s_roles, flags

    return "UNAUTHORIZED_DELEGATION_CONSTRAINTS_FAILED", s_roles, flags

## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation Creation:** Eliminate ad-hoc PDF/email delegations (`D-QM-01` lag, `D-IC-04` email). Require all QM and IC delegations to be authored natively inside a validated QMS workflow with hard schema constraints enforcing allowed scopes (`production_equipment`, `cooling_system`, `training_document`), explicit `allow_subdelegation = FALSE` locks, and mandatory Part 11 / Annex 11 dual electronic signatures before activation.
2. **Effective-Time Recording:** Enforce immutable system-generated creation timestamps (`created_at_utc`) separate from `effective_from_utc` / `effective_to_utc`, blocking retroactive backdating (`effective_from_utc >= created_at_utc`). During QMS migrations, freeze high-risk electronic approvals until delegation tables are reconciled and verified.
3. **Principal Availability Integration:** Couple the QMS authorization engine bi-directionally with HR leave/timekeeping and an explicit QMS "Check-In / Out-of-Office" toggle.
   - If a principal (`u_as`) authenticates to perform an urgent override while on leave (CHG-504), require a recorded justification reason and automatically log temporary principal presence without leaving HR/QMS records contradictory.
   - If a delegate (`u_bk`) enters certified sick leave (CHG-506, CHG-505), automatically **suspend active delegations and revoke active session tokens** at the start of sick leave, triggering fallback escalation to backup QM/Site Quality leadership.
   - Explicitly define whether weekend/off-shift hours satisfy "principal unavailable" conditions (`D-QM-02` in CHG-509) via on-call roster integration.
4. **Alias Management & Identity Governance:** Enforce a strict `1 Human : 1 Unique Enterprise Person UUID` architecture federated via SSO/IdP. Decommission legacy IDs (`b.koch`) immediately upon cutover, link historical logs via an immutable alias-mapping table, and evaluate all QMS SoD rules (RR1, RR2, plus Approver $\neq$ Verifier on HIGH-risk changes) against the **canonical `person_uuid`** at runtime so self-approval attempts like CHG-502, CHG-508, and CHG-510 are preventively blocked.
5. **Individual Authentication & Service-Account Lockdown:** Prohibit interactive human logins to `svc_qms` (21 CFR §11.10(d),(g); EU GMP Annex 11 §12). Restrict `svc_qms` strictly to headless API integrations. Configure shared shop-floor (`WS-DM-04`) and verifier (`WS-IV-02`) terminals with fast user switching, 2-minute inactivity screen locks, and **step-up personal FIDO2/smart-card re-authentication** at every electronic signature (`Approve_Change`, `Verify_Implementation`, `Retrospective_QM_Review`), including hardware-bound MFA on all remote VPN connections (`u_as`).
6. **Emergency Review Controls:** Require structured selection of a registered Incident ID (e.g., `CI-44`) when `emergency = true` is toggled, auto-validating incident-scoped IC delegations (`D-IC-03`). Start an automated **business-day SLA timer** upon emergency approval/implementation: notify standing/delegate QMs with matching technical scope at $+24\text{h}$, escalate to Site Quality Head at $+36\text{h}$, and auto-trigger a Quality Deviation / CAPA if `Retrospective_QM_Review` is uncompleted at $+48$ business hours (preventing CHG-505).
7. **Pair Rotation & Multi-Layer Independence:** Introduce a QMS allocation rule for HIGH-risk changes that rotates qualified QM/delegate reviewers across production cells (capping any single Drafter–Approver pair at, e.g., $\le 50\%$ of rolling 30-day HIGH-risk approvals) or requires secondary QA spot-check when cell staffing forces repeated pairing. Additionally, prohibit the same person from acting as both `Approve_Change` and `Verify_Implementation` on HIGH-risk changes (addressing CHG-509).
8. **Network Monitoring:** Deploy periodic PM4Py/SNA analytics (`discover_handover_of_work_network`, `discover_working_together_network`, reciprocal dyad ratios, and approval dwell-time distributions) as **risk-based QA audit triggers**, explicitly calibrated under RR6 so high-frequency pairs generate targeted technical dossier reviews rather than automated disciplinary accusations.
9. **Evidential Retention:** Synchronize NTP clocks across QMS, IdP/MFA, VPN, badge-access, and workstation session logs, retaining immutable audit trails for the full GMP record-retention lifecycle. Add CCTV coverage or electronic dual-presence logging in shared critical terminal rooms (`WS-DM-04`), and cryptographically seal external transition artifacts (e.g., PKI-timestamped PDFs).

## 7. Executive Conclusion

Across the ten investigated change-control cases, **six cases (`60%`: CHG-502, CHG-505, CHG-506, CHG-507, CHG-508, CHG-510) involve genuine substantive authority, segregation-of-duties, or credential-integrity failures**, rather than mere clerical anomalies:
- **Genuine Authority & SoD Breakdowns (5 cases directly, 1 via impersonation):** Unblocked self-approvals occurred three times—plainly in CHG-502 (`u_cs`), disguised via dual accounts in CHG-508 (`b.koch` $\rightarrow$ `u_bk`), and compounded by an unlawful email subdelegation (`D-IC-04`) in CHG-510. - **Identity, Authentication & Master-Data Quality Defects (affecting 6 cases, primary driver in 1–3):** CHG-503 cannot demonstrate RR2/RR5 compliance because `svc_qms` was used unauthenticated on implementer Diego’s shared workstation (`WS-DM-04`). Conversely, CHG-501 and CHG-509 represent **valid substitutions** degraded by master-data lag (`D-QM-01` DB migration delay), `svc_qms` UI usage, inferred weekend unavailability, and Approver=Verifier role compression in CHG-509. CHG-504 represents valid standing QM action by Asha clouded by HR leave/VPN MFA ambiguity.
