## 2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map

**Upstream Data Quality Dependencies:**
- A1 (registration)  A2 (classification)  A3/A5/A6 (downstream activities)
- A5 (document checklist)  A6 (financial normalization)
- A3 (KYC pre-classifier)  A4 (entity mismatch)  A9 (contract generation)

**Legal/Compliance Controls:**
- KYC clearance (A3/A4) must precede A9 (contract generation) and A10 (e-signature)
- Committee decision must match contract terms (A8  A9)
- Four-eyes principle requires separation between credit analysis and committee packaging (A6  A8)

**Human Escalation Capacity:**
- A3 (KYC pre-classifier) requires sufficient compliance specialists for escalations
- A6 (financial normalization) may increase credit analyst workload if not properly scoped
- A4 (entity mismatch) requires senior compliance specialists

**External Valuation Dependency:**
- A7 (collateral triage) can reduce but not eliminate external wait time
- A8 (committee packaging) depends on external valuation completion

**Core-Banking Integration Constraints:**
- Deep integration required for: A1 (registration), A9 (contract generation)
- Limited to 2 deep integrations in first 6 months

## 3. Portfolio Alternatives

### Quick-Win Portfolio (A1, A5, A9, A10)
**Optimizes:** High-frequency activities with immediate labor savings
**Sacrifices:** Process-level synchronization benefits, compliance strengthening

- A1: Auto-registration (high frequency, simple)
- A5: Missing-document checklist (reduces rework loops)
- A9: Contract generation (high frequency, labor intensive)
- A10: E-signature adoption (reduces signing wait)

### Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (A4, A5, A6, A7)
**Optimizes:** Critical path waiting times and rework
**Sacrifices:** Immediate labor savings, political quick wins

- A4: Entity-mismatch resolution (critical path blocker)
- A5: Missing-document checklist (rework reduction)
- A6: Financial-statement normalization (enables credit analysis)
- A7: Collateral valuation triage (reduces external wait)

### Compliance-First Portfolio (A3, A4, A9, A12)
**Optimizes:** Control adherence and auditability
**Sacrifices:** Some labor savings, customer experience

- A3: KYC pre-classifier (with strong audit trail)
- A4: Entity-mismatch resolution (critical compliance blocker)
- A9: Contract generation with control checks (legal compliance)
- A12: Control remediation workbench (policy clarity)

## 4. Expected Process-Level Effects

### Quick-Win Portfolio
- **Cycle time:** Moderate reduction (5-10%) from reduced registration and signing times
- **Waiting time:** Reduced at registration (A1) and signing (A10), but potential increase at classification (A2 not automated) and document review (A5 may increase analyst workload)
- **Rework:** Reduced document loops (A5) and contract remediation (A9)
- **Handovers:** Fewer registration handovers, but potential increase in classification errors
- **Resource demand:** Reduced back office (A1), but potential increase for credit analysts (A5)
- **Control violations:** Potential increase in misclassification (A2 not addressed) and KYC issues (A3/A4 not addressed)

### Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio
- **Cycle time:** Significant reduction (20-30%) from addressing critical path blockers
- **Waiting time:** Dramatic reduction at entity mismatch (A4) and financial normalization (A6), moderate reduction at collateral valuation (A7)
- **Rework:** Significant reduction in document loops (A5) and financial normalization rework (A6)
- **Handovers:** Fewer compliance specialist handovers (A4), but potential increase in credit analyst workload (A6)
- **Resource demand:** Reduced compliance specialist queue (A4), but potential credit analyst overload (A6)
- **Control violations:** Reduced KYC remediation (A4), but potential increase if A6 produces poor quality data

### Compliance-First Portfolio
- **Cycle time:** Moderate reduction (10-15%) from reduced remediation loops
- **Waiting time:** Reduced at KYC (A3) and contract preparation (A9), but potential increase at entity mismatch (A4 may require more specialist time)
- **Rework:** Significant reduction in KYC remediation (A3/A4) and contract remediation (A9)
- **Handovers:** Fewer compliance handovers (A3/A4), but potential increase in legal ops workload (A9)
- **Resource demand:** Reduced compliance specialist queue (A3/A4), but potential legal ops overload (A9)
- **Control violations:** Significant reduction in KYC and contract violations

## 5. Risks and Compensating Controls

| Risk | Compensating Control |
|------|----------------------|
| False negatives in KYC pre-classifier (A3) | - Human-in-the-loop for all hits <90% confidence<br>- Daily audit sample of negative cases<br>- Escalation path for customer disputes |
| Misclassification in product/exposure (A2) | - Four-eyes review for High-exposure cases<br>- Monthly audit of classification accuracy<br>- Customer confirmation for critical paths |
| Poor financial statement extraction (A6) | - Quality threshold for auto-processing<br>- Analyst review for low-confidence extractions<br>- Regular model retraining with new data |
| Contract generation errors (A9) | - Rule library version control<br>- Legal review of all rule changes<br>- Post-generation sample audit |
| E-signature identity issues (A10) | - Multi-factor authentication<br>- Video verification for High-exposure cases<br>- Audit trail of all signature events |
| Increased analyst workload (A5/A6) | - Workload monitoring dashboard<br>- Temporary staffing during transition<br>- Prioritization rules for automated cases |
| Model drift in classifiers (A2/A3/A6) | - Monthly performance monitoring<br>- Retraining triggers based on accuracy thresholds<br>- Fallback to manual process for degraded models |
| Four-eyes principle violations | - System-enforced separation of duties<br>- Automated alerts for violations<br>- Monthly audit of High-exposure cases |

## 6. Recommended Phased Rollout

**Phase 1 (Months 1-3): Foundation and Quick Wins**
- **Modules:** A1 (Auto-registration), A5 (Missing-document checklist)
- **Rationale:** High feasibility, immediate labor savings, minimal risk
- **Deep integrations:** A1 (registration system)
- **Monitoring metrics:**
  - Registration time reduction
  - Document rework rate
  - Back office utilization
- **Gate criteria:** >90% auto-registration success, <5% increase in document rework

**Phase 2 (Months 4-6): Compliance and Critical Path**
- **Modules:** A4 (Entity-mismatch resolution), A9 (Contract generation)
- **Rationale:** Addresses critical path blocker (A4) and compliance requirement (A9)
- **Deep integrations:** A9 (contract management system)
- **Monitoring metrics:**
  - Entity mismatch resolution time
  - Contract remediation rate
  - Compliance specialist utilization
- **Gate criteria:** >70% reduction in entity mismatch resolution time, <10% contract remediation rate

**Phase 3 (Months 7-9): Process Optimization**
- **Modules:** A6 (Financial-statement normalization), A10 (E-signature adoption)
- **Rationale:** Enables credit analysis (A6) and reduces signing wait (A10)
- **Monitoring metrics:**
  - Financial normalization time
  - Credit analyst utilization
  - Signing completion time
- **Gate criteria:** >60% reduction in financial normalization time, >80% e-signature adoption

**Phase 4 (Months 10-12): Monitoring and Adjustment**
- **Focus:** Performance monitoring, exception handling refinement
- **Activities:**
  - Model performance audits (A1, A4, A6, A9)
  - Resource utilization analysis
  - Customer feedback collection
  - Policy refinement for edge cases

## 7. Final Recommendation

**Recommended Portfolio:** Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (A4, A5, A6, A7)

**Rationale:**
1. **Process-level thinking:** This portfolio directly addresses the most severe synchronization delays and rework loops identified in the process mining evidence. The entity mismatch resolution (A4) and financial statement normalization (A6) are critical path activities that create disproportionate waiting times despite their lower frequency.

2. **Dependency awareness:** The portfolio accounts for key dependencies:
   - A5 (document checklist) improves input quality for A6 (financial normalization)
   - A4 (entity mismatch) must precede contract generation (though not in this portfolio, it enables future compliance improvements)
   - A7 (collateral triage) works within the constraint of external valuation dependency

3. **Control considerations:** While not explicitly compliance-focused, this portfolio reduces control violations by:
   - Reducing document rework (A5) that can lead to incomplete files
   - Improving financial data quality (A6) for credit decisions
   - Addressing the critical KYC blocker (A4) that causes provisional signing

4. **Resource demand:** The portfolio carefully manages changed resource demand:
   - A4 reduces compliance specialist queue time
   - A6 may increase credit analyst workload, but this is mitigated by:
     - Prioritizing automated cases
     - Temporary staffing during transition
     - Monitoring dashboards

5. **Exception handling:** Each module includes robust exception handling:
   - A4: Human escalation for complex cases
   - A5: Customer-friendly document requests
   - A6: Quality thresholds for auto-processing
   - A7: Manual override for valuation routing

6. **Trade-off argument:** While senior management initially wanted to automate the highest-frequency activity (A1), the process mining evidence clearly shows that frequency alone is misleading. The entity mismatch resolution (A4) appears in only 1750 cases but creates 31-hour median waiting times and blocks 82% of critical paths when present. Similarly, financial document review (A5/A6) creates significant rework loops that dominate end-to-end performance.

The recommended portfolio sacrifices some immediate labor savings and political quick wins in favor of fundamental process improvements that will yield greater long-term benefits in cycle time, customer experience, and risk reduction. The phased rollout ensures that each automation builds on a solid foundation while respecting integration constraints and resource limitations.
