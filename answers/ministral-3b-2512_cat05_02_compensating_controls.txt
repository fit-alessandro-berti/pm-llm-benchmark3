### **2. Equivalence and Sufficiency Test**
A compensating-control package is **fully equivalent** if:
- **Preventive**: AV-2 blocks release *and* MG-4 is pre-settlement with no override. **Timing**: Manager approval must precede bank settlement (not just release).
- **Partially equivalent**: AV-2 + MG-4 with **audit within 24 hours** of release + **no override** (detective + corrective gap closed).
- **Mitigating**: ED-1 conditions met (e.g., emergency token, bank-change exclusion) *and* AV-2 passed *or* MG-4 is post-settlement with audit within 1 business day.
- **Ineffective**: Same-user handling with no compensating controls, late manager approval, or audit after recall window.

**Key criteria**:
- **Independence**: Manager must be L3+ and not in a conflict-of-interest relationship.
- **Recovery**: Audit must allow recall within 24 hours (bank-dependent).
- **Collusion resistance**: Shared workstations or repeated pairs require **additional evidence** (e.g., audit anomalies).
- **Overrideability**: Warning/block  approval; rubber-stamped MG-4 is weak.
- **Evidence quality**: AV-2 pass  goods receipt; audit must review *all* supporting docs.
