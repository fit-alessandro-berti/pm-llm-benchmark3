## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

Let:

- **C** = time when chemistry has a usable result.
- **M** = time when microbiology has a usable result.
- **J** = parallel join time = **max(C, M)**.  
  This is the key competition/max step. The branches run in parallel, so the join is governed by the slower usable branch, not by adding branch durations.
- QA can start only at or after **J**, then faces its own queue wait and service time.
- Terminal time = QA completion + any rework/investigation + electronic release or rejection recording.

I use approximate arithmetic and scenario envelopes rather than a single Monte Carlo point estimate.
