## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning

Compare changes in outcome probability. For example, a 10-percentage-point increase in the chance of *timely, valid execution* is worth €2k–€3.5k. A 10-point increase in false rejection or prolonged hold costs €1.5k–€8k. Use a common baseline and count overlapping delay losses once. The normal-processing breach forecast of 0.48–0.64 and ultimate-approval forecast of 0.55–0.75 describe different events. Neither their product nor the approval forecast alone estimates timely execution under C or D.

The €100k–€500k unlawful-execution exposure includes neither a probability estimate nor a price for sanctions-control failure, reputational damage, or loss of regulatory trust. Screen confidence of 0.46 is not a calibrated probability of unlawful payment. Mandatory controls determine feasibility before financial comparisons. Among feasible plans, D can improve the decision during otherwise idle time, C can buy time at P-901’s expense, and E already consumes the entire deadline. E becomes preferable if a material fact cannot be resolved through the faster reviews.
