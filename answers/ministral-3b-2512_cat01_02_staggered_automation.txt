X['is_adopter_month'] = X['is_adopter_month'].astype(int)
X['month_num'] = X['month_num'].astype(int)
X['month_num_sq'] = X['month_num']**2
X['is_adopter_month_x_month'] = X['is_adopter_month'] * X['month_num']
X['is_adopter_month_x_month_sq'] = X['is_adopter_month'] * X['month_num_sq']

model = ols('median_cycle_h ~ C(branch) + is_adopter_month + month_num + month_num_sq + is_adopter_month_x_month + is_adopter_month_x_month_sq + arrivals + fte + high_complexity_pct', data=df).fit()
print("\n--- Descriptive Panel Model (OLS) ---")
print(model.summary())

# Treatment-intensity model (AT effect)
df['intensity'] = df['automated_fast_path_pct']
X_intensity = df[['intensity', 'month_num', 'arrivals', 'fte', 'high_complexity_pct']]
X_intensity['month_num'] = X_intensity['month_num'].astype(int)
X_intensity['month_num_sq'] = X_intensity['month_num']**2
X_intensity['intensity_x_month'] = X_intensity['intensity'] * X_intensity['month_num']
X_intensity['intensity_x_month_sq'] = X_intensity['intensity'] * X_intensity['month_num_sq']

model_intensity = ols('median_cycle_h ~ C(branch) + intensity + month_num + month_num_sq + intensity_x_month + intensity_x_month_sq + arrivals + fte + high_complexity_pct', data=df).fit()
print("\n--- Treatment-Intensity Model (AT) ---")
print(model_intensity.summary())

## **10. Final claim ladder**
- **Observed**: Automation availability (`automation=1`) coincides with **improved median cycle times** in adopters (Alpha/Beta/Gamma) but **worsens tail performance** (`p90_cycle_h`) and **increases escalation rates** (confounded by routing/staffing).
- **Suggestive**: In Gamma, CSAT improves slightly (0.2 points) with automation, but response rates are unstable, limiting causal inference.
- **Conditionally causal**: **Automation may reduce median cycle time by ~10–20% in historically weak branches**, but **tail performance is uncertain** due to staffing/routing interference. Effects depend on **actual use intensity** (`automated_fast_path_pct`).
