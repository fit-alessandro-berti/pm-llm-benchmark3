# Conversion Audit: POWL Change-Control Model to BPMN

---

## 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-1 | Register Change is the initial activity; no activity can execute before it. | [Register] | [Assess Risk] executed without Register | Yes; Register is visible |
| INV-2 | Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence are unordered after Register; both orderings are valid. | [Register, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence] AND [Register, Prepare Evidence, Assess Risk] | [Assess Risk, Register] | Yes; both visible, no forcing order |
| INV-3 | Legal Review is optional; trace may skip it via silent transition. | [Register, ..., Assess Risk, Legal Review, Release] AND [Register, ..., Assess Risk, Release] | [Register, Legal Review] without Assess Risk | Partially; silent transition hides one path, but visible Assess Risk precedes both |
| INV-4 | Legal Review is ordered after Assess Risk. | [Register, Assess Risk, Legal Review, ...] | [Legal Review, Assess Risk, ...] | Yes; visible order |
| INV-5 | Verification cannot start until both Assess Risk AND Prepare Evidence are complete (synchronization join). | [Register, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial] | [Register, Assess Risk, Configure Trial] without Prepare Evidence | Yes; both preconditions are visible |
| INV-6 | Optional Legal Review and verification loop are unordered at the partial-order level; either may be first. | [Assess Risk, Legal Review, Configure Trial] AND [Assess Risk, Configure Trial, Legal Review] (both valid before Release) | Neither; the model semantics allow no edge between them | Partially; no edge enforces ordering, but both are ordered after Assess Risk |
| INV-7 | Configure Trial must precede Inspect Trial in every verification iteration. | [Configure Trial, Inspect Trial] within each iteration | [Inspect Trial, Configure Trial] in same iteration | Yes; visible order |
| INV-8 | At least one complete iteration of [Configure Trial, Inspect Trial] must occur; loop has minimum iteration of 1. | [Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release] (no Correct Defect between them) | [Release] without any iteration (loop is mandatory, not optional) | Yes; at least one Configure and one Inspect visible |
| INV-9 | Correct Defect separates iterations; after Correct Defect, a new iteration [Configure Trial, Inspect Trial] may follow. | [Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Correct Defect, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release] | [Configure Trial, Configure Trial] without Correct Defect between | Yes; visible separators |
| INV-10 | Release Change is ordered after both Optional Legal Review XOR branch AND verification loop complete. | [Assess Risk, Legal Review, Inspect Trial, Release] AND [Assess Risk, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release] (with or without Legal Review) | [Release, Inspect Trial] (Release before verification ends) | Yes; Release is visible and must follow both branches |
| INV-11 | Archive Change is the final activity; no activity executes after it. | [..., Release Change, Archive Change] | [..., Archive Change, Release Change] | Yes; Archive is last and visible |
| INV-12 | No trace can deadlock or livelock before reaching Archive Change in a sound model. | All enabled-transition paths lead to Archive Change eventually | Silent loop within optional_legal or trial_body traps execution | If soundness holds, yes |
| INV-13 | Silent transition label is `None`; optionality is a semantic property, not a visible activity. | Trace without Legal Review in execution | Trace with "Skip Legal" as visible label | No; silent transition is invisible |

## 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Pipeline A (Primary)** POWL  Petri net  BPMN | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`  `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Direct; preserves partial-order concurrency; Petri net is canonical form; BPMN is stakeholder-facing. | BPMN visualization may collapse concurrency into sequential gateways; loop in BPMN may appear overly strict; silent transitions appear as routing nodes. | Petri net is unsound; BPMN conversion fails or returns invalid BPMN; loop structure causes deadlock. |
| **Pipeline B (Secondary)** POWL  Petri net  Process Tree  BPMN | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`  `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`  `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | If process tree succeeds, offers block-structured intermediate that guarantees single-entry/single-exit blocks. | Model is not sufficiently block-structured; conversion to process tree fails (expected for partial orders with unordered branches). When it succeeds, it may impose an arbitrary total order on unordered concurrency. | `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree()` raises an exception; tree blocks do not correspond to POWL semantics. |
| **Pipeline C (Validation)** POWL  Petri net  round-trip BPMN  Petri net | Same as A, plus `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_net)` | Detects lossy conversions; validates that BPMN rendering is behaviorally equivalent to source. | Round-trip may introduce new behaviors (overapproximation) or lose edge cases. | Soundness differs; final marking differs; trace set is not equivalent. |

**Selected Primary Pipeline:** Pipeline A (POWL  Petri net  BPMN) with Pipeline C validation.

**Key Discussion Points:**

- **True concurrency vs. interleaving:** POWL partial order explicitly supports true concurrency. Petri net preserves this via place capacity and concurrent marking. BPMN AND gateway is semantically correct but visual layout does not always distinguish true concurrency from arbitrary sequential ordering post-hoc. Requires reachability graph inspection or trace enumeration to validate.

- **Unordered Optional Legal and Verification:** No edge in POWL order means they are truly unordered. Petri net conversion must allow all interleavings. BPMN visualization (two parallel branches converging at a join before Release) is correct, but layout may suggest a sequence. Validation: confirm traces like `[Register, Assess Risk, Configure Trial, Legal Review, Inspect Trial, Release]` are possible.

- **Silent optional behavior:** POWL XOR with SilentTransition is converted to two Petri net paths (one labeled, one with tau/empty label). BPMN may render this as an exclusive gateway with one visible-activity branch and one empty/router branch. Semantically correct; visually ambiguous. Trace filtering (hiding invisible transitions) should show exactly two equivalence classes: with and without Legal Review.

- **Binary-loop semantics:** POWL LOOP `[trial_body, correct_defect]` means trial_body executes, then either exit or Correct Defect re-enters loop. Minimum iteration is 1. Petri net must enforce at least one trial_body completion. Standard conversion may or may not make this explicit. BPMN while-loop or repeat-until notation should clarify. Validation: confirm traces do not exit loop without at least one Configure + Inspect pair.

- **Join before Release Change:** Critical synchronization. Both optional_legal and verification_loop must complete before Release fires. In Petri net, this is a confluence of two paths (an AND join). In BPMN, this is an AND merge gateway. Validation: confirm traces attempting Release before verification completes are rejected.

- **Activity-label uniqueness:** All visible activities in source POWL have distinct labels. No collision in conversion. Petri net and BPMN inherit this.  No ambiguity.

- **Initial and final markings:** Source POWL is a single-source partial order (Register is unique source). Petri net initial marking should have one token in the start place. Source is acyclic except for LOOP (which is bounded). Final marking should be a single token in the sink place after Archive. Validation: compare `im_from_powl == im_from_bpmn` and `fm_from_powl == fm_from_bpmn` after round-trip.

- **Gateway/routing-node ambiguity:** BPMN conversion introduces AND/XOR gateways for concurrency and choice. These do not exist in POWL or Petri net as first-class entities; they are routing abstractions. Layout may be misleading. Recommendation: annotate gateways with their role (e.g., "sync join: Assess Risk + Prepare Evidence", "choice: Legal Review or skip").

## 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

```python
"""
Round-trip validation: Compare source Petri net with round-tripped net.
Includes soundness checks, footprint comparison, reachability graph,
and bounded visible-trace exploration.
"""

import pm4py
from pm4py.util import constants
from collections import defaultdict, deque

print("\n" + "=" * 80)
print("ROUND-TRIP VALIDATION")
print("=" * 80)

# ============================================================================
# HELPER: Bounded Visible-Trace Exploration
# ============================================================================

def explore_visible_traces(net, im, fm, max_depth=20, max_traces=1000):
    """
    Explore all reachable visible traces from initial marking to final marking.
    Hidden transitions (label=None) are traversed without incrementing trace.
    Returns a set of (visible_trace) tuples and a count of complete traces.
    
    Args:
        net: Petri net
        im: initial marking
        fm: final marking
        max_depth: maximum recursion depth
        max_traces: maximum number of traces to collect before stopping
    
    Returns:
        complete_traces: set of tuples, each a sequence of visible activity labels
        deadlock_states: set of stuck markings (not final, no enabled transitions)
        visited_count: total number of states explored
    """
    
    complete_traces = set()
    deadlock_states = set()
    visited_markings = {}  # marking hash -> trace count
    
    def marking_to_str(m):
        """Convert marking to hashable string for visited set."""
        return str(sorted((p, m[p]) for p in m if m[p] > 0))
    
    def dfs(current_marking, visible_trace, depth, tau_depth):
        """
        Depth-first traversal.
        
        Args:
            current_marking: current marking dict
            visible_trace: list of visible activity labels so far
            depth: recursion depth counter
            tau_depth: consecutive tau transitions (prevent infinite silent loops)
        """
        if len(complete_traces) >= max_traces or depth >= max_depth:
            return
        
        marking_str = marking_to_str(current_marking)
        visited_markings[marking_str] = visited_markings.get(marking_str, 0) + 1
        
        # Check if final marking is reached
        if current_marking == fm:
            complete_traces.add(tuple(visible_trace))
            return
        
        # Get enabled transitions
        try:
            enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, current_marking)
        except Exception as e:
            print(f"    Warning: get_enabled_transitions failed: {e}")
            deadlock_states.add(marking_str)
            return
        
        if not enabled:
            # No enabled transitions and not at final marking -> deadlock
            if current_marking != fm:
                deadlock_states.add(marking_str)
            return
        
        # Explore each enabled transition
        for trans in enabled:
            new_marking = current_marking.copy()
            
            # Execute transition
            for arc in net.arcs:
                if arc.source == trans:
                    # Output arc (transition to place)
                    if arc.target in new_marking:
                        new_marking[arc.target] += 1
                    else:
                        new_marking[arc.target] = 1
                elif arc.target == trans:
                    # Input arc (place to transition)
                    if arc.source in new_marking:
                        new_marking[arc.source] -= 1
            
            # Decide whether to count this transition as visible
            is_visible = trans.label is not None and trans.label != ""
            
            new_trace = visible_trace + ([trans.label] if is_visible else [])
            new_tau_depth = (tau_depth + 1) if not is_visible else 0
            
            # Prune infinite silent loops (allow up to 5 consecutive taus)
            if new_tau_depth <= 5:
                dfs(new_marking, new_trace, depth + 1, new_tau_depth)
    
    dfs(im, [], 0, 0)
    
    return complete_traces, deadlock_states, len(visited_markings)
```

## 6. Visual-Semantics Review

### How BPMN Rendering Misreads Converted Models

1. **Parallel (AND) Gateways and True Concurrency:**
   - POWL partial order with unordered edges (Assess Risk  Prepare Evidence) is rendered in BPMN as a parallel gateway that splits and later joins.
   - **Ambiguity:** The layout may suggest a sequential execution order (left-to-right or top-to-bottom) even though the gateway semantics are concurrent. A stake-holder reading the diagram left-to-right might think Assess Risk must complete before Prepare Evidence, which is false.
   - **Mitigation:** Label the parallel gateway with a note: "Concurrent: Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence may execute in any order."

2. **Exclusive (XOR) Gateways and Optional Behavior:**
   - POWL XOR with SilentTransition (Legal Review or skip) is converted to an exclusive gateway with two branches.
   - **Ambiguity:** The skip branch may be rendered as an empty or unlabeled flow, or may be explicitly labeled "skip". Tools handle silent transitions differently. A stakeholder may think both branches are visible activities, or may not realize one path is truly invisible.
   - **Mitigation:** Explicitly label the silent branch: "Skip Legal Review (automated)" or use a dashed line and annotation.

3. **Loops (LOOP Operator):**
   - POWL LOOP(trial_body, correct_defect) is converted to a Petri net with a transition cycle. BPMN may render this as a repeat-until or while-do loop structure.
   - **Ambiguity:** The minimum iteration count (1 in this case) may not be obvious in the diagram. A stakeholder may misinterpret a while-loop (0+ iterations) as repeating zero times, entering Release before any trial.
   - **Mitigation:** Annotate the loop with "At least 1 iteration required: Configure Trial and Inspect Trial must complete before exit."

4. **Silent Routing Nodes:**
   - Petri net transitions with `label=None` (internal routing) are sometimes rendered in BPMN as invisible transitions or as empty nodes.
   - **Ambiguity:** A diagram with many invisible nodes may appear incomplete or confusing. Stakeholders may not realize that routing decisions are being made.
   - **Mitigation:** Render silent transitions as small diamond nodes with a tau symbol () and a note: "Internal routing (not visible in activity logs)."

5. **Join Synchronization Before Release:**
   - The join before Release Change (waiting for both optional_legal and verification_loop) is a confluence of two paths.
   - **Ambiguity:** In BPMN, this is an AND join gateway. A stakeholder might misread a join that comes after a parallel split as not truly synchronizing (i.e., one branch can proceed without waiting for the other).
   - **Mitigation:** Explicitly label the join gateway: "Synchronization: Both Legal Review (if applicable) and Trial Verification must be complete before Release."

6. **Graph Layout Suggesting Sequence:**
   - Even if BPMN semantics are correct, the physical layout (e.g., left-to-right or top-to-bottom) may suggest a sequence that does not exist.
   - **Example:** If Assess Risk is positioned left and Prepare Evidence right, readers may infer Assess Risk happens first.
   - **Mitigation:** Use layout annotations or explicit "can occur in any order" labels; consider using swimlanes or color-coding to indicate concurrency.

7. **Activity Boundary Ambiguity:**
   - Silent transitions in the Petri net may be converted to internal routing that is invisible in BPMN. The exact point where a decision or routing happens may be unclear.
   - **Ambiguity:** Is the XOR decision made explicitly (as a visible gateway) or implicitly (as a silent transition)?
   - **Mitigation:** Ensure silent routing is visually distinct (e.g., different color, smaller font) and documented.

### Recommended Annotations and Documentation

- **Title block:** "Change Control Process (Partial Order with Concurrency)"
- **Gateway annotations:**
  - Parallel gateway after Register: "Concurrent execution (any order)"
  - Exclusive gateway for Legal Review: "Choice with automatic skip option"
  - Loop boundary: "Repeat until Inspect Trial passes (minimum 1 iteration)"
  - Join before Release: "Synchronization point (all prior branches must complete)"
- **Activity labels:** Include timestamps or duration ranges for verification (Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Correct Defect) to clarify loop iterations.
- **Legend:** Define symbols for silent transitions, parallel/exclusive gateways, and loop structures.
- **Trace examples:** Provide 2-3 example traces (with and without Legal Review, with and without loop iterations) to clarify allowed behaviors.

## 7. Rejection Criteria

A conversion result must be **rejected or quarantined** if any of the following conditions hold:

1. **Soundness Failure:**
   - The resulting Petri net (from any pipeline) fails the soundness check (i.e., `pm4py.check_soundness()` returns False or raises an exception).
   - **Rationale:** Soundness is a prerequisite for a valid workflow model. Unsound models may deadlock, livelock, or reach unintended states.
   - **Action:** Reject the conversion result; return to POWL and debug.

2. **Lost Synchronization (Release Before Verification):**
   - A trace is discovered where Release Change is enabled and fires **before** both verification_loop and optional_legal have completed.
   - **Rationale:** INV-10 (Release waits for both optional_legal and verification_loop) is a critical invariant. Loss of this invariant breaks the process logic.
   - **Action:** Reject; verify Petri net join structure.
   - **Test:** Bounded trace exploration; negative witness trace `["Register", "Assess Risk", "Release Change"]` must NOT exist.

3. **Lost Minimum Iteration (Zero-Iteration Loop):**
   - A trace is discovered where Release Change is enabled **without** any Configure Trial or Inspect Trial in the trace.
   - **Rationale:** INV-8 requires at least one verification iteration. Loss of this invariant allows bypassing trial verification.
   - **Action:** Reject; verify LOOP operator conversion.
   - **Test:** Negative witness `["Register", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Release Change"]` must NOT exist.

4. **Collapsed Concurrency (False Total Ordering):**
   - The converted model enforces an order between Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence (or between optional_legal and verification_loop) where none should exist in POWL.
   - **Rationale:** INV-2 and INV-6 require true concurrency (unordered execution). Forced sequencing is a semantic loss.
   - **Action:** Quarantine (not reject outright); mark as "overly restrictive". May be acceptable if documented as a deployment constraint.
   - **Test:** Both positive witness traces `["Register", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", ...]` and `["Register", "Prepare Evidence", "Assess Risk", ...]` must exist.

5. **Silent Transition Visibility:**
   - The converted model includes a visible activity labeled with the skip-legal behavior (e.g., an activity "Skip Legal Review").
   - **Rationale:** INV-13 requires silent transitions to remain invisible. A visible "skip" activity misrepresents the semantics.
   - **Action:** Reject; re-configure XOR conversion to use true silent (empty label) transitions.
   - **Test:** Trace set analysis; no trace should contain a label derived from `SilentTransition()`.

6. **Marking Inequality After Round-Trip:**
   - `im_from_powl  im_from_bpmn` or `fm_from_powl  fm_from_bpmn` after BPMNPetri round-trip conversion.
   - **Rationale:** Markings define the initial and final states of the process. Inequality indicates loss of state information or incorrect conversion logic.
   - **Action:** Reject the round-tripped net; may accept original conversion if initial/final markings match source.
   - **Test:** Explicit equality check in code (Section 5, step 1).

7. **Deadlock or Livelock in Reachability Graph:**
   - The reachability graph or bounded trace exploration discovers a marking that is neither the final marking nor a deadlock-free state.
   - **Rationale:** INV-12 requires sound progression to Archive Change. Deadlocks are unacceptable.
   - **Action:** Reject; investigate and fix conversion.
   - **Test:** Trace exploration; deadlock_states set must be empty (or contain only the final marking).

8. **Footprint Mismatch (Overapproximation):**
   - Round-trip footprint includes directly-follows relations not present in the source.
   - **Rationale:** Overapproximation allows behaviors not in the original model. While sometimes acceptable (models are abstractions), large overapproximations indicate lossy conversion.
   - **Action:** Quarantine with explanation; requires manual review. If gained relations form a large set (>20% of source relations), recommend rejection.
   - **Test:** Footprint comparison (Section 5, step 3); check `gained_traces` set size.

9. **Trace Set Underapproximation:**
   - A positive witness trace that should be valid (e.g., one of the INV-* witness traces) is **not** discovered in the converted model.
   - **Rationale:** Underapproximation loses allowed behaviors, breaking the semantic contract.
   - **Action:** **Automatic rejection.** This is a critical invariant violation.
   - **Test:** Witness trace validation (Section 5, step 7); any failed positive witness is grounds for rejection.

10. **Process Tree Conversion Exception (Expected Failure):**
    - `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree()` raises an exception (e.g., `TransitionSystemToProcessTreeException`).
    - **Rationale:** POWL partial orders with unordered concurrency cannot be reduced to block-structured trees. This is **expected and acceptable** for POWL models.
    - **Action:** **Quarantine Pipeline B; document as expected failure.** Proceed with Pipeline A (POWLPetriBPMN).
    - **Mitigation:** Do not use process tree as intermediate; rely on Petri net as canonical form.

## 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

### Authoritative Representation: Petri Net (POWL Source)

**Choice:** The **source POWL model** is the authoritative specification. If a Petri net or BPMN must be chosen, the **Petri net (converted from POWL) is the canonical computation model**.

**Rationale:**
- POWL is a high-level, concise notation that explicitly separates concurrency, choice, and iteration.
- POWL can be edited by process experts without deep technical knowledge.
- The POWL model in the source code (Python) serves as the single source of truth.
- Petri net conversion is deterministic and reversible (traces are preserved).
- BPMN is a visualization/communication tool, not a computation model.

### Derivative Representations

1. **Petri Net (Primary Derivative):**
   - **Generated by:** `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`
   - **Purpose:** Computational substrate for analysis (soundness checking, reachability, trace exploration).
   - **Validation Required:**
     - Soundness check: `pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)` must pass.
     - Trace witness test: positive and negative witness traces (Section 5, step 7) must pass.
     - Marking equality: `im == source_im` and `fm == source_fm`.
   - **Storage:** Serialize as PNML or pm4py internal format with metadata (source_powl_hash, generation_date, soundness_status).
   - **Update Procedure:** Regenerate from POWL source whenever POWL is edited; do not edit Petri net directly.

2. **BPMN (Secondary Derivative, Stakeholder Visualization):**
   - **Generated by:** `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(petri_net, im, fm)`
   - **Purpose:** Visualization and communication with non-technical stakeholders.
   - **Validation Required:**
     - Round-trip conversion: convert back to Petri net; compare footprints (must have no lost relations).
     - Visual inspection: layout must not suggest false sequences; annotations must be present.
   - **Storage:** Serialize as BPMN XML with visual metadata (layout, colors, annotations).
   - **Update Procedure:** Regenerate from Petri net whenever Petri net changes. Do not edit BPMN directly (edits will be lost on next regeneration).
   - **Annotations:** Include embedded text boxes, labels, and comments (as described in Section 6) to reduce visual ambiguity.

3. **Process Tree (NOT RECOMMENDED):**
   - **Status:** Expected to fail for this model.
   - **Rationale:** POWL partial order with unordered concurrency (Assess Risk  Prepare Evidence) cannot be reduced to a block-structured tree.
   - **Decision:** Do not attempt process tree intermediate; document as unsupported.
   - **Fallback:** If stakeholders demand a tree-structured representation, this is a process redesign question, not a conversion issue.

### Future-Edit Protocol (Avoid Round-Trip Drift)

1. **Source-of-truth edits:**
   - All edits must occur in the POWL source code (Python).
   - Edits to Petri net or BPMN are **strictly forbidden** (they will be lost on regeneration).

2. **Change workflow:**
   - Edit POWL source.
   - Run conversion script (Section 3) to regenerate Petri net and BPMN.
   - Run validation suite (Section 5) to confirm soundness and witness tests.
   - If validation fails, revert POWL edit and investigate.
   - Commit POWL source and regenerated artifacts to version control.

3. **Version control:**
   - Track POWL source in Git (high priority; this is the canonical spec).
   - Track Petri net PNML as derivative artifact (informational; can be regenerated).
   - Track BPMN as visualization only (informational; can be regenerated).
   - Include a README documenting regeneration procedure.

4. **Regeneration script:** Ensure conversion script (Section 3) is idempotent and deterministic. Include a hash or checksum of source POWL in generated artifacts to detect stale regenerations.

## 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if all tests in Sections 5 and 7 pass, the following claims **cannot be established** by automated validation:

1. **Semantic Alignment with Real-World Process:**
   - Automated tests validate that the converted model is **internally consistent** (sound, traces are preserved, invariants hold).
   - **Cannot verify:** Whether the POWL model correctly represents the actual change-control process as it is executed in practice.
   - **Example:** The model requires "Legal Review" after "Assess Risk," but in reality, legal review is sometimes skipped informally. The model's XOR choice may not capture this.
   - **Implication:** Soundness and trace validation are necessary but not sufficient for real-world correctness. Domain experts must review the POWL specification independently.

2. **Stakeholder Interpretation of BPMN Visualization:**
   - Automated tests cannot measure whether a stakeholder (non-technical reviewer) correctly interprets the BPMN diagram.
   - **Example:** Even with annotations, a stakeholder may still misread parallel gateways as sequential due to visual layout or cultural expectations.
   - **Implication:** BPMN annotations (Section 6) reduce ambiguity but do not eliminate it. Walkthroughs with stakeholders are required to validate correct understanding.
   - **Remediation:** Conduct process review sessions with stakeholders using both the diagram and the automated trace examples.

3. **Completeness of Witness Trace Set:**
   - The witness traces in Section 5 are manually curated based on invariants. They do not exhaustively cover all possible valid combinations.
   - **Example:** A complex interleaving like `["Register", "Prepare Evidence", "Assess Risk", "Configure Trial", "Legal Review", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect", "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release", "Archive"]` may be valid but not included in the witness set.
   - **Implication:** Passing all witness tests does not prove the model is fully correct; only that key invariants hold. Additional domain-specific traces should be validated.

4. **Handling of Unbounded Loops:**
   - The bounded trace exploration (Section 5) limits loop iterations to prevent infinite recursion.
   - **Cannot verify:** Whether loop behavior is correct under unbounded iteration (e.g., after 100 Correct Defect cycles).
   - **Rationale:** POWL LOOP operator semantics are "at least 1 iteration, then repeat," but the BPMN and Petri net translations may not enforce this identity-preservingly at scale.
   - **Implication:** For change-control processes with bounded defect counts (e.g., max 5 tries), this is acceptable. For long-running or unbounded loops, additional analysis (e.g., liveness checking) is needed.

5. **Tool-Specific BPMN Rendering Differences:**
   - Automated tests verify that the BPMN XML is well-formed and round-trips correctly.
   - **Cannot verify:** How different BPMN tools (e.g., Camunda, Signavio, Bizagi) render the same BPMN file.
   - **Example:** One tool may collapse multiple gateways into a single node; another may render them separately. Visual ambiguity depends on the tool.
   - **Implication:** BPMN is a standard, but visualization is tool-dependent. Stakeholders should view the diagram in the intended tool. Provide BPMN XML and document the target rendering tool.

6. **Silent Transition Presence in Real Traces:**
   - Automated tests hide silent transitions (label=None) and validate visible trace sets.
   - **Cannot verify:** Whether real process execution logs actually contain the inferred silent transitions in the expected places.
   - **Example:** If the process is executed manually, a "skip legal review" decision might not be explicitly logged, making it impossible to distinguish from a "legal review was performed but not logged" scenario.
   - **Implication:** The model's silent transitions are an abstraction convenience. Alignment with real logs requires log-enrichment or domain verification beyond automated testing.
