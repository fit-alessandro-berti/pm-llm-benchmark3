## 1. Feasibility model

```text
Partial-Order Precedence Relations (Poset (A, <_P)):
1.  Register <_P Triage
2.  Triage <_P Identity Check
3.  Triage <_P Document Check (if Originals Required = False)
4.  Triage <_P Originals Receipt <_P Document Check (if Originals Required = True)
5.  Identity Check <_P Assessment
6.  Document Check <_P Assessment
7.  Assessment <_P Escalation Advice (optional / conditional on ambiguity)
8.  Assessment <_P Request More Info <_P Document Check <_P Assessment (iterative rework cycle)
9.  Assessment <_P Compliance Review (mandatory if High Risk = True ∨ High Value = True)
10. Escalation Advice <_P Compliance Review (when escalation precedes compliance)
11. Compliance Review <_P Request More Info (if compliance identifies evidentiary defects)
12. Compliance Review <_P Decision (for High Risk ∨ High Value cases)
13. Assessment <_P Decision (for Low Risk ∧ Low Value cases without Compliance Review)
14. Decision <_P Notify
15. Concurrency Relation: Identity Check || Document Check (iff Originals Required = False)
16. Concurrency Relation: Preliminary Escalation / Compliance Screen || (Identity Check, Document Check)
```

```text
Conditional and Declarative Constraints:
1. [Mandatory Compliance Review]: (HighRisk = True ∨ HighValue = True) ⇒ (∃ c ∈ ComplianceReview : latest(Assessment) < c < Decision)
2. [Physical Document Ingress]: OriginalsRequired = True ⇒ (OriginalsReceipt < DocumentCheck)
3. [Four-Eyes Separation]: ∀ a ∈ Assessment, ∀ c ∈ ComplianceReview : resource(a) ≠ resource(c) (specifically resource(final(Assessment)) ≠ resource(final(ComplianceReview)))
4. [Post-Rework Completeness]: ∀ r ∈ RequestMoreInfo ⇒ ∃ d ∈ DocumentCheck, ∃ a ∈ Assessment : (r < d < a < Decision) ∧ (HighRisk/Value ⇒ ∃ c ∈ ComplianceReview : a < c < Decision)
5. [Resource Single-Tasking]: ∀ r ∈ Resources, ∀ i_1, i_2 ∈ ServiceIntervals(r) : i_1 ≠ i_2 ⇒ (end(i_1) ≤ start(i_2) ∨ end(i_2) ≤ start(i_1))
6. [Decision Precedence]: Decision < Notify
7. [Compliance Invariance]: PreliminaryScreen(S1/C1) does NOT satisfy Mandatory Compliance Review condition unless reaffirmed post-final-Assessment.
8. [Decision Completeness]: Decision ∈ {Approve, ApproveWithConditions, Reject}; unresolved material discrepancies must be explicitly conditioned or rejected, never omitted.
```

### Intervention Classification
- **Timing only (K-411)**: Parallelizing Identity Check and Document Check removes an artificial software case-lock without altering task definitions, activity routing, resource allocations, or decision criteria.
- **Routing only / Resource substitution (K-219 & K-104)**: K-219 reroutes the document verification task from specialist D1 to generalist D2 upon originals arrival, preserving the normative activity sequence while altering queue selection and stochastic duration/risk. K-104 initiates an asynchronous preliminary advisory/compliance branch at Triage, shifting preparation earlier while maintaining the mandatory post-assessment compliance gate.
- **Decision policy change (K-337)**: Prohibiting the second `Request More Info` loop truncates the iterative evidentiary gathering cycle and mandates an immediate terminal adjudication (`Approve with Conditions` or `Reject`) under epistemic uncertainty, fundamentally altering risk exposure and legal defensibility.

### K-104

Total cycle time was 58h 30m.
- `Minimal intervention:` At Triage (Mon 08:50), dispatch a preliminary escalation brief to S1 and place a provisional reservation in C1's queue for Tuesday late afternoon, enabling concurrent preliminary compliance analysis alongside the core D1 document check.
- `Temporal propagation:` S1 reviews the sanctions ambiguity during Monday midday (Mon 10:00–12:00, utilizing S1's advisory overlap capability). S1 attaches guidance notes to the dossier before A1 picks it up. D1 completes the complex document check Tue 05:40. A1 begins Tue ~14:30; because the sanctions issue is pre-analyzed, A1's assessment is streamlined (Tue 14:30–17:30, 3h). Instead of waiting 16h and reading from scratch for 4.5h, C1 conducts an expedited final review in 1.5–2.0h (Tue 19:00–21:00 via reserved slot, or Wed 08:30–10:30). M1 signs the decision Wed 10:30–11:00.
- `Process-feasibility check:` Conforms strictly to Normative Rules 1, 2, 5, 9, 10. Preliminary compliance screening does not replace final review; C1 executes the mandatory final Compliance Review strictly after A1's final Assessment. ### System-Wide Analysis: 100-Case Weekly Rollout
When scaling these interventions to a 100-case weekly intake, localized single-case efficiencies trigger significant systemic interactions across queues, resource pools, and operational boundaries:

1. **Compliance capacity reservation (K-104)**: C1 is a rigid single-server bottleneck with a 16–20 hour baseline queue. Reserving speculative C1 slots for 100 cases consumes over 150 hours of capacity. If preliminary packets arrive incomplete or out of sequence, reserved slots sit idle or require duplicate review, inducing severe queue inflation (exceeding 35 hours) for unreserved urgent cases.

2. **D1/D2 resource substitution (K-219)**: Offloading 100 handwritten cases from D1 to D2 successfully drains D1's 13-hour backlog. However, because D2 requires 4–8 hours versus D1’s 3–6 hours, total document labor increases by ~150–200 hours. If D2's error rate causes even a 20% clarification rate, the resulting rework loops generate 20–30 new document checks, overwhelming D2 and reversing initial speed advantages.

3. **Avoided rework and external delays (K-337)**: Eliminating second rework loops across 100 complex cases recaptures over 600 hours of D1 and A1 capacity while avoiding brutal weekend dead-time (where Friday requests linger until Monday morning). However, shifting from exhaustive verification to conditional approvals transfers operational risk to credit monitoring and dispute handling, potentially overwhelming manager M1 with exception escalations.

4. **Assessment bottlenecks and arrival bunching (K-411)**: Parallelizing identity and document verification removes serialization delay across 100 cases, releasing cases to Assessment 3–5 hours earlier. However, synchronized parallel completions create bursty batch arrivals at the A1/A2 assessment queue on Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons. This shifts the primary system bottleneck downstream, temporarily inflating Assessment queues by 4–6 hours unless assessment staffing is dynamically leveled.

5. **Timestamp fidelity versus true concurrency**: Workarounds simulating concurrency without true parallel processing risk creating synthetic audit timestamps, distorting process-mining discovery models and masking operational handoff delays. Robust telemetry must capture true independent resource start and end timestamps.

## 7. Final synthesis

The operational analysis reveals sharp divergence between localized cycle-time gains and system-wide policy robustness:

1. **Most robust intervention**: **K-411 (Parallel Verification)** is the most robust and immediately deployable policy. It eliminates an artificial legacy software constraint without altering resource competencies, decision thresholds, or regulatory compliance. Its cycle-time reduction (3.5–18.5 hours) is achieved purely through genuine concurrency, posing zero risk to quality or four-eyes compliance.

2. **Most dangerous intervention**: **K-337 (Prohibiting Second Rework Loop)** is exceptionally dangerous. Although it yields the largest nominal time savings (84–92 hours) by skipping weekend supplier delays, it achieves speed by mutating the decision policy under epistemic uncertainty. Forcing conditional approval or outright rejection on contradictory records exposes the organization to severe credit default, regulatory non-compliance, and costly customer litigation.

3. **Least transportable counterfactual**: **K-104 (Preliminary Escalation / C1 Reservation)** is least transportable from a single case to a fleet-wide policy. In isolation, reserving C1 capacity accelerates a single high-value case. Scaled across 100 cases, reserving slots in a single-server queue (C1) with an existing 20-hour backlog creates severe blocking, artificial queue starvation, and catastrophic wait-time inflation for non-target urgent cases.

4. **Most valuable event and queue data**: The most critical data requirements include: (a) granular, non-synthetic timestamps capturing true task start, suspension, and completion to verify concurrency fidelity; (b) real-time queue state telemetry (work-in-process depth and server utilization) at the C1 and A1/A2 bottlenecks; (c) stratified error and clarification rates for generalist versus specialist handlers (D2 vs. D1); and (d) post-decision defect tracking (appeal rates, covenant breaches, and compliance audit penalties) to quantify the hidden costs of truncated rework.

## 8. Assumption audit

1. `[structural]` **Normative Concurrency Independence**: Identity verification and document authentication are functionally independent processes whose parallel execution creates no information deficits for either reviewer when no physical originals are required.
2. `[resource]` **Single-Server Compliance Incompressibility**: C1 is the sole certified compliance officer in the operational window, operating as a non-preemptible, single-server queue where reserved idle capacity cannot be reclaimed dynamically.
3. `[temporal]` **External Response Weekend Hysteresis**: External supplier and applicant inquiries issued after Friday 10:00 experience an administrative dead-band until Monday 08:00, multiplying calendar cycle time by 48–72 hours.
4. `[quality]` **Resource-Skill Specialization Gap**: Generalist resource D2 exhibits a non-zero probability (20–30%) of misinterpreting complex or handwritten documents, whereas specialist D1 achieves near-zero error rates on complex texts.
5. `[interference]` **Arrival Batching Downstream**: Upstream parallelization (K-411) releases completed verifications in synchronized clusters, transforming smooth arrival distributions into bursty arrivals at the downstream Assessment queue.
6. `[behavioral]` **Adjudication Risk-Aversion Trade-off**: When forced to decide on incomplete evidence under suppressed rework policies, managers and compliance officers exhibit risk-averse behavior, biasing outcomes toward restrictive conditions or outright rejections.
7. `[temporal]` **Preliminary Review Cognitive Depreciation**: Preliminary screening by S1 and C1 saves 1.0 to 2.5 hours of final review effort only if the underlying document evidence verified by D1 matches initial triage disclosures without material divergence.
8. `[quality]` **Four-Eyes Non-Substitutability**: Compliance review mandates an independent evaluation post-assessment; preliminary advisory screens cannot legally or procedurally waive the final four-eyes compliance sign-off on high-risk/high-value cases.
