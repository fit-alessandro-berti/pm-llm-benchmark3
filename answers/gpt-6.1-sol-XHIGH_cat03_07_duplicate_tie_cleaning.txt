## 1. Duplicate-handling decision tree

Start with immutable source observations and qualifier-aware event-to-object (E2O) relations. Equal harmonized activity and timestamp generate candidates; they do not establish identity. Assess occurrence identity before abstraction level or timestamp ordering. Never infer equivalence transitively from overlapping objects alone.

1. Same source and source event key, identical business payload, original timestamp, activity, and complete relations? **Exact technical duplicate:** drop redundant replay rows, retaining their identifiers and ingestion records in provenance. A repeated key with changed content is a revision or conflict, requiring review. No exact technical duplicate is established among these seventeen rows.
2. Reconciliation or an occurrence identifier establishes one physical/business event across systems? **Multiple observations:** merge attributes using field-level authority and merge compatible relations; retain every source observation. Different hashes can reflect enrichment.
3. One record derives from another but describes a status, balance, or different abstraction level? **Derived summary:** retain both with refined labels or qualifiers. Merge only in an explicitly justified projection where both represent the same transition.
4. Source meanings differ despite harmonization? **Harmonization collision:** retain both with refined labels or qualifiers.
5. Same label/time but different occurrence-defining objects or transactions? **Concurrent distinct events:** retain both unchanged; preserve their individual relations. Different objects are evidence of distinction, not necessarily proof of physical simultaneity.
6. Evidence remains inconclusive or contradictory? **Unresolved candidate:** quarantine for review, retaining it in the conservative view with an uncertainty flag. Quarantine is an annotation/review queue, not silent removal.

## 3. Timestamp-tie ordering rules

Construct a DAG of retained events, mapping source observations to canonical events. Record every precedence edge's reason and evidence. Domain constraints, such as e12→e13 for M1 and e09→e10, are semantic. Within-source sequence supplies local recorded precedence: after scan merging, scanner 551→552 gives e03→e05, WMS 100→110 gives e03→e06, and PSP 400→401→402 gives e12→e13→e14. Sequence numbers from different systems are incomparable. Local record order need not prove physical causality.

Lifecycle constraints require shared relevant objects and a validated lifecycle profile. Picks of items consumed by this packing transaction may precede packing; invoice issuance need not universally precede authorization. Source-specific semantics distinguish release, acceptance, handover, and summaries. Message-derived edges require identified inputs: “after payment messages” alone does not identify whether e15 follows both captures.

For each timestamp bucket, topologically sort using stable canonical/source IDs among available nodes. Original timestamps order different buckets. Detect cycles and precedence contradicting timestamps; quarantine the conflicting constraints rather than silently selecting a winner. Ingestion time is diagnostic; if ever used as a last display tie-breaker, it is merely deterministic. This policy does not need it.

Admissible displays include:

- **10:00, conservative:** e03, e05, e06, e07. e03 precedes e05 and e06 from source evidence. e05→e06 requires the packing lifecycle assumption; e06→e07 requires correlation to the packing message. Without those validations, additional interleavings are admissible. Under packing equivalence, e07 disappears into e06.
- **10:15:** e08, e09, e10. e09→e10 is established. e08→e09 is conditional on a validated release-before-acceptance rule; otherwise e09,e08,e10 and e09,e10,e08 remain admissible.
- **12:00, after invoice merging:** e11,e12,e13,e14,e15. Established constraints include e11→e15 from ERP sequence and e12→e13→e14 from PSP sequence. e11 can interleave with the PSP chain. If message lineage confirms both captures as e15 inputs, e15 follows both; otherwise its placement after e14 is only one admissible display.

Microseconds assigned before resolving constraints can encode a false story in directly-follows edges and durations. Assign them only after ordering, tag them synthetic, and retain the DAG. A total order is a reproducible linear extension, not proof of physical order.

## 4. Conservative cleaning pipeline

Archive the raw OCEL; validate IDs, objects, timestamps, and relation qualifiers. Generate candidate groups and classify them using source semantics. Deduplicate verified technical replays only; none are demonstrated here. Merge e03/e04 and e11/e16 with reversible attribute reconciliation. Retain packing and payment summaries separately, refine the identified harmonization collisions, and flag unresolved equivalence questions.

Preserve original IDs, harmonized labels, available source labels, UTC timestamps, source keys, sequence numbers, payloads, and ingestion metadata. Keep concurrency/uncertain-order annotations alongside the deterministic display. Propagate canonical IDs and timestamps into relation tables and validate integrity.

The result has **15 events and 39 E2O relations**, counting each displayed event-object link once. Raw has 17 events and 44 relations; the confirmed merges remove three and two repeated links respectively. All eight objects remain.

## 5. Aggressive alternatives

**Evidence-based semantic merging:** additionally merge e06/e07 only when message/transition reconciliation confirms one occurrence and the analytical view intentionally combines these abstraction levels. This conditional scenario has **14 events and 37 relations**. Its principal loss is visibility of ERP status propagation. With only the supplied evidence, that additional verification is unavailable: the executable default remains **15/39**. Do not merge e15 into either payment; a summary of several messages is not one capture.

**Mechanically aggressive PM4Py merging:** inspect same-label/time candidates using the original harmonized labels, protect e05 and e14 as standalone events, and apply the library only to the remaining candidate groups. Merging e03/e04, e06/e07, e08/e09, e11/e16, and e13/e15 yields **12 events and 32 relations**, assuming union semantics and the listed links. This diagnostic scenario falsely collapses release with acceptance and assigns an invoice summary to M1. It is unsuitable as the default analysis view.

An unrestricted equal-label/time merge would potentially produce **10 events/28 relations**, collapsing e05 and e14 as well. That is a damage illustration, not a permitted output pipeline. Protection must come from candidate classification and explicit exclusions.

```python
from copy import deepcopy
from collections import defaultdict
import heapq
import json
import warnings
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

RAW_ARCHIVE = deepcopy(ocel_raw)       # immutable reconstruction source
EID = ocel_raw.event_id_column
ACT = ocel_raw.event_activity
TS = ocel_raw.event_timestamp
OID = ocel_raw.object_id_column
OT = ocel_raw.object_type_column
QUAL = "ocel:qualifier"
AUDIT = []
ATTRIBUTE_AUTHORITY = {"location": "WMS"}  # add verified invoice field owners
PACK_EQUIVALENCE_VERIFIED = False
# Scenario assumption: I2 is picked before this packing transaction.
LIFECYCLE_EDGES = [("e05", "e06")]
# Populate only from matched message IDs, not ingestion timestamps.
CONFIRMED_MESSAGE_EDGES = []

def freeze(value):
    return json.dumps(value, sort_keys=True, default=str)

def missing(value):
    return value is None or (
        pd.api.types.is_scalar(value) and bool(pd.isna(value))
    )

def prepare(base):
    o = deepcopy(base)
    o.events = o.events.reset_index(drop=True)
    o.events[TS] = pd.to_datetime(o.events[TS], utc=True)
    o.events["original_timestamp"] = o.events[TS].copy()
    o.events["original_activity"] = o.events[ACT].copy()
    if "source_activity" in o.events:
        o.events["original_source_activity"] = o.events["source_activity"]
    else:
        o.events["original_source_activity"] = pd.NA
    # Only these source meanings are explicitly supplied by the question.
    for event_id, meaning in {
        "e08": "released from warehouse", "e09": "accepted by carrier"
    }.items():
        o.events.loc[o.events[EID] == event_id,
                     "original_source_activity"] = meaning
    observations = o.events.to_dict("records")
    o.events["_observations"] = [[r] for r in observations]
    o.events["_source_eids"] = [(r[EID],) for r in observations]
    o.events["_attribute_conflicts"] = [[] for _ in observations]
    return sync_validate(o)

def sync_validate(o):
    assert o.events[EID].is_unique and o.objects[OID].is_unique
    assert o.relations[EID].isin(o.events[EID]).all()
    assert o.relations[OID].isin(o.objects[OID]).all()
    ev = o.events.set_index(EID)
    for col in (ACT, TS):
        if col in o.relations:
            o.relations[col] = o.relations[EID].map(ev[col])
    if OT in o.relations:
        o.relations[OT] = o.relations[OID].map(o.objects.set_index(OID)[OT])
    for name, columns, valid in (
        ("e2e", (EID, EID + "_2"), set(o.events[EID])),
        ("o2o", (OID, OID + "_2"), set(o.objects[OID])),
        ("object_changes", (OID,), set(o.objects[OID])),
    ):
        table = getattr(o, name, None)
        if table is not None:
            for col in columns:
                if col in table:
                    assert table[col].isin(valid).all()
    return o

def relation_signature(o, event_id, complete=False):
    rows = o.relations[o.relations[EID] == event_id]
    cols = ([c for c in rows if c not in (EID, ACT, TS)] if complete
            else [OID] + ([QUAL] if QUAL in rows else []))
    return tuple(sorted(freeze(r) for r in rows[cols].to_dict("records")))

def candidates(o):
    result = []
    for (activity, timestamp), frame in o.events.groupby([ACT, TS]):
        ids = sorted(frame[EID].tolist())
        if len(ids) < 2:
            continue
        links = [set(o.relations.loc[o.relations[EID] == i, OID])
                 for i in ids]
        result.append({
            "activity": activity, "timestamp": timestamp, "ids": ids,
            "common_objects": set.intersection(*links),
            "identical_links": len({relation_signature(o, i) for i in ids}) == 1,
            "source_keys": frame[["source", "source_event_key"]].to_dict("records"),
            "hashes": frame["payload_hash"].tolist(),
        })
    return result

def scoped(o, ids):
    s = deepcopy(o)
    s.events = s.events[s.events[EID].isin(ids)].copy()
    s.relations = s.relations[s.relations[EID].isin(ids)].copy()
    if getattr(s, "e2e", None) is not None:
        for col in (EID, EID + "_2"):
            if col in s.e2e:
                s.e2e = s.e2e[s.e2e[col].isin(ids)].copy()
    return sync_validate(s)            # retain objects; no object pruning

def replace_group(o, ids, canonical, rule, confidence, supplied_relations=None):
    rows = o.events.set_index(EID).loc[ids]
    base = rows.loc[canonical].to_dict()
    base[EID] = canonical
    obs = [r for group in rows["_observations"] for r in group]
    conflicts = [r for group in rows["_attribute_conflicts"] for r in group]
    metadata = {EID, ACT, TS, "source", "source_event_key", "source_seq",
                "ingested_at", "payload_hash", "source_activity"}
    for col in rows.columns:
        if col in metadata or col.startswith("original_") or col.startswith("_"):
            continue
        values = [r for r in obs if col in r and not missing(r[col])]
        if not values:
            continue
        owner = ATTRIBUTE_AUTHORITY.get(col)
        preferred = [r for r in values if owner and r.get("source") == owner]
        if preferred:
            base[col] = preferred[0][col]
        elif missing(base.get(col)):
            base[col] = values[0][col]
        if len({freeze(r[col]) for r in values}) > 1:
            conflicts.append({"field": col, "values": [
                {"eid": r[EID], "source": r["source"], "value": r[col]}
                for r in values], "authority": owner})
    base["_observations"] = obs
    base["_source_eids"] = tuple(sorted({r[EID] for r in obs}))
    base["_attribute_conflicts"] = conflicts
    old_rel = o.relations[o.relations[EID].isin(ids)].copy()
    merged_rel = (old_rel if supplied_relations is None
                  else supplied_relations.copy())
    merged_rel[EID] = canonical
    keys = [EID, OID] + ([QUAL] if QUAL in merged_rel else [])
    # Preserve every relation variant in audit before selecting projected links.
    relation_variants = merged_rel.to_dict("records")
    merged_rel = merged_rel.drop_duplicates(subset=keys)
    o.events = pd.concat([
        o.events[~o.events[EID].isin(ids)], pd.DataFrame([base])
    ], ignore_index=True)
    o.relations = pd.concat([
        o.relations[~o.relations[EID].isin(ids)], merged_rel
    ], ignore_index=True)
    projected_self_links = []
    if getattr(o, "e2e", None) is not None:
        aux = o.e2e.copy()
        for col in (EID, EID + "_2"):
            if col in aux:
                aux[col] = aux[col].replace({i: canonical for i in ids})
        if EID in aux and EID + "_2" in aux:
            newly_self = ((aux[EID] == aux[EID + "_2"]) &
                          (o.e2e[EID] != o.e2e[EID + "_2"]))
            projected_self_links = o.e2e[newly_self].to_dict("records")
            aux = aux[~newly_self]
        o.e2e = aux.drop_duplicates()
    AUDIT.append({"canonical_eid": canonical, "source_eids": base["_source_eids"],
                  "rule": rule, "rule_version": "tie-cleaning-v1",
                  "confidence": confidence, "observations": deepcopy(obs),
                  "attribute_conflicts": deepcopy(conflicts),
                  "input_relations": old_rel.to_dict("records"),
                  "projected_relation_variants": relation_variants,
                  "projected_self_links": projected_self_links})
    return sync_validate(o)

def verified_pair(o, a, b, reason, allow_subset=False):
    rows = o.events.set_index(EID).loc[[a, b]]
    assert rows["original_activity"].nunique() == 1
    assert rows["original_timestamp"].nunique() == 1
    left, right = set(relation_signature(o, a)), set(relation_signature(o, b))
    assert (right <= left) if allow_subset else (left == right)
    return replace_group(o, [a, b], a, reason, "high")

def exact_replays(o):
    groups = defaultdict(list)
    ignored = {EID, "ingested_at"}
    for row in o.events.to_dict("records"):
        if missing(row["source_event_key"]):
            continue
        payload = {k: v for k, v in row.items()
                   if k not in ignored and not k.startswith("_")}
        signature = (row["source"], row["source_event_key"], freeze(payload),
                     relation_signature(o, row[EID], complete=True))
        groups[signature].append(row[EID])
    for ids in groups.values():
        if len(ids) < 2:
            continue
        ids = sorted(ids)
        # Justified library scope: verified full-content technical replays only.
        trial = pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates(scoped(o, ids))
        assert len(trial.events) == 1
        survivor = trial.events[EID].iloc[0]
        assert relation_signature(trial, survivor) == relation_signature(o, ids[0])
        o = replace_group(o, ids, ids[0], "exact technical replay", "high",
                          supplied_relations=trial.relations)
    return o

def refine(o):
    labels = {"e07": "Update ERP Packing Status",
              "e08": "Release from Warehouse", "e09": "Carrier Acceptance",
              "e15": "Update Invoice Payment Balance"}
    for event_id, label in labels.items():
        o.events.loc[o.events[EID] == event_id, ACT] = label
    return sync_validate(o)

raw = prepare(RAW_ARCHIVE)
precheck = candidates(raw)             # inspect semantic candidates BEFORE mutation
technical = exact_replays(deepcopy(raw))
conservative = deepcopy(technical)
conservative = verified_pair(conservative, "e03", "e04", "scan reconciliation")
conservative = verified_pair(conservative, "e11", "e16", "confirmed V1 issuance")
conservative = refine(conservative)
evidence = deepcopy(conservative)
if PACK_EQUIVALENCE_VERIFIED:
    evidence = verified_pair(evidence, "e06", "e07",
                             "confirmed identical packing transition",
                             allow_subset=True)

aggressive = deepcopy(raw)             # separate scenario; original harmonized labels
warnings.warn("Diagnostic merge can collapse release/acceptance and capture/summary; "
              "common objects do not establish occurrence identity.")
protected = {"e05", "e14"}
for candidate in candidates(aggressive):
    ids = [i for i in candidate["ids"] if i not in protected]
    if len(ids) < 2:
        continue
    subset = scoped(aggressive, ids)
    common = set.intersection(*[
        set(subset.relations.loc[subset.relations[EID] == i, OID]) for i in ids
    ])
    assert common
    trial = pm4py.ocel_merge_duplicates(subset, have_common_object=True)
    assert len(trial.events) == 1      # fail if installed version differs
    survivor = trial.events[EID].iloc[0]
    expected_links = set().union(*[
        set(relation_signature(subset, i)) for i in ids
    ])
    assert set(relation_signature(trial, survivor)) == expected_links
    aggressive = replace_group(aggressive, ids, min(ids),
                               "aggressive library projection", "low",
                               supplied_relations=trial.relations)

def ordered_display(o):
    o = deepcopy(o)
    ev = o.events.set_index(EID)
    alias = {original: canonical for canonical, row in ev.iterrows()
             for original in row["_source_eids"]}
    constraints = []
    def edge(a, b, reason):
        a, b = alias[a], alias[b]
        if a == b:
            return                    # internal to one projected occurrence
        ta, tb = ev.at[a, "original_timestamp"], ev.at[b, "original_timestamp"]
        if ta > tb:
            raise ValueError("Precedence contradicts timestamps; review required")
        constraints.append((a, b, reason))
    for a, b in [("e12", "e13"), ("e09", "e10")]:
        edge(a, b, "known domain constraint")
    for a, b in LIFECYCLE_EDGES:
        edge(a, b, "declared lifecycle assumption")
    for a, b in CONFIRMED_MESSAGE_EDGES:
        edge(a, b, "confirmed message lineage")
    observations = [r for group in o.events["_observations"] for r in group]
    by_source = defaultdict(list)
    for row in observations:
        if not missing(row["source_seq"]):
            by_source[row["source"]].append(row)
    for source, records in by_source.items():
        records.sort(key=lambda r: (r["source_seq"], r[EID]))
        levels = defaultdict(list)
        for row in records:
            levels[row["source_seq"]].append(row[EID])
        ordered_levels = sorted(levels)
        for left, right in zip(ordered_levels, ordered_levels[1:]):
            for a in levels[left]:
                for b in levels[right]:
                    edge(a, b, "within-source sequence: " + source)
    rank = {}
    for timestamp, frame in o.events.groupby("original_timestamp", sort=True):
        nodes = set(frame[EID])
        successors = {n: set() for n in nodes}
        indegree = dict.fromkeys(nodes, 0)
        for a, b, reason in constraints:
            if a in nodes and b in nodes and b not in successors[a]:
                successors[a].add(b)
                indegree[b] += 1
        available = [n for n in nodes if indegree[n] == 0]
        heapq.heapify(available)         # stable eid fallback, not ingestion order
        completed = 0
        while available:
            a = heapq.heappop(available)
            rank[a] = len(rank)
            completed += 1
            for b in sorted(successors[a]):
                indegree[b] -= 1
                if indegree[b] == 0:
                    heapq.heappush(available, b)
        if completed != len(nodes):
            raise ValueError("Tie DAG cycle; quarantine conflicting constraints")
    o.events["_tie_rank"] = o.events[EID].map(rank)
    o = pm4py.ocel_sort_by_additional_column(o, "_tie_rank")
    expected_order = sorted(rank, key=rank.get)
    assert o.events[EID].tolist() == expected_order
    o = pm4py.ocel_add_index_based_timedelta(o)  # only AFTER semantic/topological order
    o.events["adjusted_timestamp"] = o.events[TS]
    o.events["synthetic_timestamp"] = True
    assert o.events[TS].is_monotonic_increasing
    assert (o.events[TS] >= o.events["original_timestamp"]).all()
    buckets = o.events.groupby("original_timestamp")[TS].max()
    times = list(buckets.index)
    for left, right in zip(times, times[1:]):
        if buckets.loc[left] >= right:
            raise ValueError("Synthetic offsets cross a business timestamp; use rank only")
    AUDIT.append({"rule": "deterministic display order", "constraints": constraints,
                  "rank": rank, "timestamp_mapping": o.events[
                      [EID, "original_timestamp", "adjusted_timestamp"]].to_dict("records")})
    return sync_validate(o)

def business_view(display):
    o = deepcopy(display)
    o.events[TS] = o.events["original_timestamp"]
    return sync_validate(o)            # use for performance; retain tie-rank sidecar

scenarios = {"raw": raw, "conservative": conservative,
             "evidence_based": evidence, "aggressive_library": aggressive}
for name, o in scenarios.items():
    sync_validate(o)
    assert protected <= set(o.events[EID])
    for i in protected:
        assert o.events.set_index(EID).at[i, "_source_eids"] == (i,)
    assert {i for ids in o.events["_source_eids"] for i in ids} == set(RAW_ARCHIVE.events[EID])
counts = {name: (len(o.events), len(o.relations)) for name, o in scenarios.items()}
assert counts["raw"] == (17, 44)
assert counts["conservative"] == (15, 39)
assert counts["evidence_based"] == ((14, 37) if PACK_EQUIVALENCE_VERIFIED else (15, 39))
assert counts["aggressive_library"] == (12, 32)
ordered = {name: ordered_display(o) for name, o in scenarios.items()}
performance_inputs = {name: business_view(o) for name, o in ordered.items()}
```

## 7. Sensitivity analysis

Compare identical object populations and report both refined-label and harmonized-label projections. Otherwise relabeling alone can masquerade as changed behavior. Use shared admissible ordering policies; separately vary uncertain tie orders.

| Dataset | Events / E2O links | Principal interpretation change |
|---|---|---|
| Raw | 17 / 44 | Repeated observations, collisions, and summaries inflate apparent activity |
| Conservative | 15 / 39 | Verified observation duplicates disappear; meaningful layers remain |
| Evidence-based merge | 15 / 39 with available evidence; conditional 14 / 37 | Confirmed packing equivalence removes one status observation from the projection |
| Aggressive library merge, protected | 12 / 32 | Distinct logistics semantics and capture/summary granularity collapse |

**Order-flattened DFG:** raw Pick Item→Pick Item transitions include the repeated scan and the legitimate I2 pick. Conservative cleaning removes the former but retains the latter. These are at least six concrete changing patterns, not guaranteed physical paths.

**ET–OT weights:** state whether weights count E2O links, events, or distinct objects. Pick–Item link count falls from three to two; Invoice issuance–Invoice falls from two to one. Capture–Payment remains two because both payments survive. Refinement moves e15's Order/Invoice links into a balance-update event type. Packing projection and aggressive logistics merging reduce observation counts for their Order/Package links.

**OTG:** for an unweighted co-event object graph, topology stays unchanged in these permitted scenarios: merged relation sets are identical or nested. Co-occurrence support counts can decrease. The forbidden unrestricted payment merge would introduce M1–M2 co-occurrence, an invented relation. Keep explicit object-to-object relations distinct from inferred co-event relations.

**Lifecycle/performance:** conservative cleaning preserves the real release/acceptance/handover distinctions and ERP propagation observations. Aggressive merging removes observable boundaries. Original timestamps retain zero measured differences within ties, reflecting second-level resolution, not proven zero elapsed time. Ignore synthetic microseconds in durations, SLA checks, and throughput calculations. Report ordering and timestamp-resolution uncertainty.

## 8. Audit trail and reversibility

Use a versioned transformation ledger with scenario/run ID, canonical eid, source eids and keys, merge/drop/refinement rule, reviewer or rule version, confidence, and reason/evidence reference. Store each observation's original/refined activity, source semantics, original/adjusted UTC timestamps, ingestion time, sequence, payload hash, all attributes, retained authoritative values, rejected/projected conflicts, and authority decisions. Also store original and projected qualified relations, auxiliary-link changes, ordering constraints, fallback rank, and uncertainty flags.

Keep immutable raw event, relation, object, and auxiliary tables plus canonical-to-observation mappings. Reconstruction returns archived observations and their original links, including dropped replay rows; it does not split a unioned relation set heuristically. Synthetic timestamps and display labels belong to derived views. Validate reconstruction against archived row counts and content digests.

## 9. Failure modes and safeguards

1. **Collapsing partial payments:** protect occurrence-defining M1/M2 identities and e13/e14 individuality.
2. **False rework from abstraction updates:** retain and qualify status/balance events; compare abstraction-specific projections.
3. **Ordering by ingestion:** use established precedence and local sequence; keep ingestion diagnostic.
4. **Shared objects treated as duplicate proof:** require occurrence evidence beyond O1/V1/P1 overlap.
5. **Incompatible E2O unions:** inspect object identity, qualifiers, and relation attributes; quarantine contradictions and archive variants.
6. **Synthetic durations:** restore original timestamps for performance and prevent offsets crossing timestamp buckets.
7. **Lost authoritative attributes:** use field-level authority, namespaced observations, and conflict records.
8. **Whole-OCEL duplicate functions without inspection:** pre-check candidates, scope calls, protect distinct events, and assert results.
9. **Lost constraints after merging:** aggregate all sources' sequence evidence through alias mappings; detect cycles and projected self-links.
10. **Overconfident total-order interpretation:** retain the DAG and distinguish semantic edges from display choices.
11. **Hash equality or activity equality used as identity:** verify keys, payload coverage, occurrence context, and relations; enrichment can also change hashes.
12. **Broken references or irreversible provenance:** validate every endpoint after changes and retain immutable raw tables with complete lineage.
