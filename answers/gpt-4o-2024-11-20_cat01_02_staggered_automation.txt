## 1. What is observed

- **Availability of Automation**: Automation was introduced in staggered rollouts, starting with Alpha and Beta in April 2026 and Gamma in July 2026. Delta and Epsilon had not adopted by August 2026.
- **Actual Use or Treatment Intensity**: Automation did not fully replace manual triage even where available. Use of the automated fast path increased gradually post-adoption (e.g., Alpha went from 25% automated fast-path cases in April to 48% by August). Treatment intensity (automated_fast_path_pct) is a better indicator of actual use than binary automation availability.
- **Branch Selection into Rollout**: The rollout targeted historically weaker branches (Alpha, Beta, and later Gamma), with higher cycle times before adoption. Managers retained considerable discretion, including changes in work practices.
- **Contemporaneous Operational Changes**: Several co-occurring changes complicate isolation of automation effects:
  - Workloads were redistributed, sending higher volumes to automated branches after adoption.
  - Staffing levels fluctuated (e.g., Alpha lost two FTEs in May; Beta gained one in April).
  - A centralized fraud desk began operation in March, shifting borderline cases from branches.
  - A product change in May increased the proportion of high-complexity cases across branches.
- **Outcome Measurement Limitations**: Survey response counts for customer satisfaction (csat_1to5) are unavailable, creating potential nonresponse bias. Aggregated branch-month cycle times (median and p90_cycle_h) do not capture individual case performance.

No direct causal claim can be made based solely on these observations—careful identification strategies are needed.

## 3. Identification strategy

### Primary Identification Strategy

| strategy                     | treatment timing representation    | comparison group | adjustment variables                    | estimand targeted | identifying assumptions                                        | diagnostic checks       | main failure mode                  |
|------------------------------|------------------------------------|------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Staggered adoption/event study | Binary post-treatment indicator   | Not-yet-treated branches | Arrival mix, staffing, fraud desk usage | E1, E3, E5         | Parallel trends hold; no major spillovers                     | Pre-trends test; sensitivity to spillovers | Time-varying confounding       |

### Alternative Strategy 1

| strategy                     | treatment timing representation | comparison group   | adjustment variables                    | estimand targeted | identifying assumptions                                        | diagnostic checks       | main failure mode                  |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Matched differences-in-differences | Static treated/control match | Delta, Epsilon     | See above                               | E1, E3             | Proper match; no unmeasured confounding; parallel trends hold | Balance checks; sensitivity to violations | Poor match, sample size         |

### Alternative Strategy 2

| strategy                   | treatment timing representation      | comparison group | adjustment variables                    | estimand targeted | identifying assumptions                                        | diagnostic checks       | main failure mode                  |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------------|------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------|-------------------------|------------------------------------|
| Treatment-intensity regression | Continuous (automated_fast_path_pct) | Within automated branches | See above                               | E2, E4             | Valid instrument for treatment usage; exogeneity of intensity | Instrument validity tests| Omitted-variable bias              |

**Explanation**: Staggered adoption with comparison to not-yet-treated branches provides the clearest approach to estimate ITT effects while allowing pre-trends checks. Intensity regressions risk endogeneity from unobserved managerial choices, while matching heavily relies on parallel-trends validity and good matching quality.
