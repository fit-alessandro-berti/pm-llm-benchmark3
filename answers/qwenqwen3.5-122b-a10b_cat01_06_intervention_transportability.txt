## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

**Process Specification:**
1.  **Eligibility:** Intervention applies only to cases where `Originals Receipt` is not legally required (Digital-Only).
2.  **Concurrency:** `Identity Check` and `Document Check` tasks are created simultaneously at `Register`.
3.  **Prerequisites:** `Document Check` cannot proceed if `Originals Receipt` is pending (system check). `Assessment` requires completion of both checks.
4.  **Queue/WIP:** Each reviewer pool (Identity, Document) has a WIP limit of 6 active cases.
5.  **Resources:** Identity Reviewers (4), Document Reviewers (7).
6.  **Legal/Loops:** `Legal Review` remains mandatory for 40% of cases post-Assessment. `Request Supplement` triggers re-execution of `Document Check`.
7.  **Logging:** System timestamps must be recorded at task start/completion; back-entry prohibited.

**POWL Description:**
```text
Register -> {Identity Check, Document Check}
Identity Check -> Assessment
Document Check -> Assessment
Assessment -> {Legal Review, Approve, Reject}
Legal Review -> {Request Supplement, Approve, Reject}
Request Supplement -> Document Check
```
*Dependencies:* `Assessment` waits for `Identity Check` AND `Document Check`. `Document Check` waits for `Originals Receipt` if `Originals Required = True`.

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go**

Site A's improvement cannot be directly copied to Site B due to structural constraints. Parallelism is only transportable to the 38% of cases that do not require physical originals. For these digital cases, the mechanism of reducing `waiting_before_assessment` should hold, provided IT system locks are resolved. However, Site B's specialized roles and 40% legal review rate introduce a high risk of shifting the bottleneck to the Assessment or Legal Review stages, potentially negating cycle time gains or increasing tail times.

Do not copy the intervention to physical claims; statutory requirements make parallelism impossible. Do not copy the "six open tasks" WIP limit without verifying Site B's queue discipline, as role specialization may require different limits per pool. The Digital-Only subgroup should enter the pilot first.

Expected benefits include a 10-15% reduction in median cycle time for digital cases. Risks include increased rework if parallel verification reduces quality checks, and assessment queue congestion. Expansion should be triggered only if rework rates remain below 18% and p90 cycle times do not increase. If the pilot shows no cycle time improvement, the mechanism is likely blocked by downstream bottlenecks, and the intervention should be abandoned for Site B.
