## 1. Conformance semantics

I classify each observed deviation using seven mutually exclusive classes, applied **after** checking three filters: (a) does the norm's stated scope actually cover this observation? (b) is the summary's abstraction faithful to the norm's logic? (c) is there positive evidence of an extraction artifact?

- **Forbidden behavior** — a relation explicitly marked `forbidden` whose scope applies. These are the only relations under a closed-world assumption: any observed instance is a violation *as recorded*. If investigation later proves the instance is a logging artifact, the finding is reclassified as a data-quality error — but containment is still immediate, because the record as it stands asserts illegal behavior.
- **Missing mandatory behavior** — a `mandatory` relation absent where scope clearly applies *and* the abstraction faithfully expresses the rule. Scope failures (e.g., a package never intended for external shipment under N4) or abstraction failures (N1's "eventually" rendered as directly-follows) downgrade or void the finding.
- **Unexpected but unregulated behavior** — observed relations absent from the norm entirely. Because the norm is open-world everywhere except forbidden relations and in-scope mandatory columns, absence from the norm is *not* noncompliance. These are recorded informationally.
- **Typical-pattern absence** — a `typical` relation not observed. Absence is presumptively legitimate (credit terms, write-offs, reviews); it escalates only with corroborating signals such as aging or frequency anomalies.
- **Likely data-quality error** — evidence (join keys, reused identifiers, attributes that contradict the graph) indicates the *observation* is wrong, not the *process*. Kept strictly separate from behavioral noncompliance.
- **Abstraction mismatch** — the diagnostic lens (directly-follows, per-type projection, aggregation granularity) misrepresents the normative claim, producing apparent deviations that vanish under a faithful lens.
- **Insufficient evidence** — summaries cannot discriminate between competing classes; specific discriminating evidence is named.

**Open vs. closed world:** closed-world reasoning applies only to N3, T6, E10 (forbidden) and to mandatory columns inside their stated scopes (N1, N4, E1–E8). Everything else — N2, N5, N6, T3–T5, E9, all codeath relations, all unlisted event/object combinations — is open-world: unlisted  violated.

## 3. Detailed reasoning by model family

### OC-DFG

**Can establish:** per-object-type directly-follows frequencies, giving denominators (100 created Orders, 200 shipped Packages, 110 Invoices) and exposing sequence-level anomalies such as the N3 pair. **Cannot establish:** eventual reachability (so it cannot alone adjudicate N1), cross-object causality, or whether an absent edge means an absent event or an unlinked event. The N3 observation is instructive: "Cancel Order  Dispatch Package" under the *Order* perspective is only visible because dispatch events are also registered on Orders — a pure Package-perspective DFG would hide it entirely. **Counts and scopes:** with no tolerance bands supplied, shortfalls of 2/100 are triggers for case inspection, not verdicts. **Abstraction danger:** N1 states Orders should "*eventually* enter validation"; the summary approximates this with directly-follows. The 3 observed Create  Manual Compliance Review detours show exactly how a legitimate intermediate event breaks the DF pair while satisfying the business rule. Declaring the 2 uncovered Orders noncompliant on DF evidence alone would be a category error; they must be re-evaluated under eventual-reachability. Conversely, DF conformance says nothing about validation *timeliness*. A relation can therefore be conforming at the business-rule level and deviant at the DF abstraction (and vice versa).

### OTG

**Can establish:** which object-type pairs interact and lifecycle couplings (cobirth/codeath) — T6 is *only* visible here. **Cannot establish:** which activity produced an edge, per-case scope, or sequencing. This creates the sharpest abstraction contrast in the dataset: T2 is satisfied at OTG level (Package–Delivery interaction = 200) while E6 fails at ET-OT level (Dispatch–Delivery = 198). Same phenomenon, opposite verdicts, because the OTG edge may originate from a non-dispatch event or reflect counting drift (Finding 15). Meanwhile the Sensor Heartbeat–Invoice edge is invisible in any OC-DFG (it perturbs no activity sequence) yet blatant in ET-OT — conforming-looking at one abstraction, forbidden at another. Small weights (1–7) demand case-level inspection since no statistical floor is defined.

### ET–OT

**Can establish:** completeness of event-typeobject-type bindings with exact denominators per event type (e.g., 1450 Add Item events, 93 Record Payment events). **Cannot establish:** ordering, correctness of the binding's *semantics* (a heartbeat-invoice join passes syntactic discovery perfectly), or whether an exception label (E8's unapplied cash) applies. The E8 gap (92 vs 93) is undecidable without the subtype label — precisely the kind of question graph summaries cannot answer. Across families: mandatory-edge gaps must always be tested against scope (N4's "intended for external shipment") and abstraction (N1's "eventually") before being called violations; forbidden-edge hits must be tested against correlation integrity before being called behaviors.

## 5. PM4Py conformance code sketch

```python
import pm4py
from dataclasses import dataclass

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# NORM METADATA LAYER -- deliberately OUTSIDE the discovered graph structures.
# pm4py graphs carry only topology/frequencies. Status (mandatory/typical/
# forbidden/unspecified), scope predicates, and exception labels MUST be
# maintained here or in an external registry; nothing below infers them.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
OCDFG_NORM = {
    ("Order", "Create Order", "Validate Order"):
        {"id": "N1", "status": "mandatory",
         "caution": "rule is EVENTUAL; directly-follows is only an approximation"},
    ("Order", "Validate Order", "Close Order"): {"id": "N2", "status": "typical"},
    ("Order", "Cancel Order", "Dispatch Package"):
        {"id": "N3", "status": "forbidden"},          # closed-world
    ("Package", "Pack Package", "Dispatch Package"):
        {"id": "N4", "status": "mandatory",
         "scope": "package.external_shipment == True"},
    ("Invoice", "Issue Invoice", "Record Payment"): {"id": "N5", "status": "typical"},
    ("Order", "Reopen Order", "Close Order"):       {"id": "N6", "status": "unspecified"},
}
OTG_NORM = {
    ("Order", "object_interaction", "Item"):       {"id": "T1", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Package", "object_interaction", "Delivery"): {"id": "T2", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Invoice", "object_interaction", "Payment"):  {"id": "T3", "status": "typical"},
    ("Order", "object_interaction", "Customer"):   {"id": "T4", "status": "unspecified"},
    ("Order", "object_interaction", "RiskCase"):   {"id": "T5", "status": "unspecified"},
    ("Order", "object_cobirth", "Payment"):        {"id": "T6", "status": "forbidden"},
}
ETOT_NORM = {
    ("Create Order", "Order"): {"id": "E1", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Add Item", "Order"):     {"id": "E2", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Add Item", "Item"):      {"id": "E3", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Pack Package", "Package"):     {"id": "E4", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Dispatch Package", "Package"): {"id": "E5", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Dispatch Package", "Delivery"):{ "id": "E6", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Record Payment", "Payment"):   {"id": "E7", "status": "mandatory"},
    ("Record Payment", "Invoice"):   {"id": "E8", "status": "mandatory",
                                      "exception": "subtype == 'unapplied_cash'"},
    ("Manual Compliance Review", "RiskCase"): {"id": "E9", "status": "unspecified"},
    ("Sensor Heartbeat", "Invoice"):          {"id": "E10", "status": "forbidden"},
}

@dataclass
class Finding:
    norm_id: str; observed: str; deviation_class: str
    severity: str; confidence: str; note: str

def classify(status, present, scope_ok=True):
    """Semantic layer. Never mutates raw pm4py diagnostics."""
    if status == "forbidden":
        return ("forbidden_behavior", "critical") if (present and scope_ok) else ("conforming", "info")
    if status == "mandatory":
        if not present:
            return ("missing_mandatory_pending_scope_check", "high") if scope_ok else ("out_of_scope", "info")
        return ("conforming", "info")
    if status == "typical":
        return ("typical_absence_presumptively_legitimate", "low") if not present else ("conforming", "info")
    return ("unexpected_but_unregulated", "info")   # open-world default

# ------------------------------ discovery ----------------------------------
ocdfg = pm4py.discover_ocdfg(ocel)
otg   = pm4py.discover_otg(ocel)      # exact signature varies by pm4py version
etot  = pm4py.discover_etot(ocel)

# ------------- RAW diagnostics: preserve verbatim, read-only ---------------
raw_ocdfg = pm4py.conformance_ocdfg(ocdfg, ocel)
raw_otg   = pm4py.conformance_otg(otg, ocel)
raw_etot  = pm4py.conformance_etot(etot, ocel)
# WARNING: raw outputs are purely structural (deviations vs the DISCOVERED
# model). They encode no mandatory/forbidden/typical semantics, no scope
# predicates, no exceptions. They are inputs to interpretation, not verdicts.

findings = []
for key, meta in OCDFG_NORM.items():                       # OC-DFG pass
    present = key in ocdfg["edges"]["event_couples"]       # adapt accessor
    cls, sev = classify(meta["status"], present)
    findings.append(Finding(meta["id"], str(key), cls, sev, "structural",
                            meta.get("caution", "")))
for edge, meta in OTG_NORM.items():                        # OTG pass
    present = edge in getattr(otg, "edges", {})            # adapt accessor
    cls, sev = classify(meta["status"], present)
    findings.append(Finding(meta["id"], str(edge), cls, sev, "structural", ""))
for (act, ot), meta in ETOT_NORM.items():                  # ET-OT pass
    n_ev  = sum(1 for e in ocel.events if e["ocel:activity"] == act)
    n_bnd = etot.get((act, ot), 0)                         # adapt accessor
    gap   = n_ev - n_bnd
    if meta["status"] == "mandatory":
        cls = "conforming" if gap == 0 else "missing_mandatory_pending_scope_check"
        findings.append(Finding(meta["id"], f"{act}->{ot} gap={gap}", cls,
                                "high" if gap else "info", "quantitative",
                                meta.get("exception", "")))
    else:
        cls, sev = classify(meta["status"], n_bnd > 0)
        findings.append(Finding(meta["id"], f"{act}->{ot}", cls, sev, "", ""))

# Forbidden rules additionally require CASE-LEVEL verification over raw
# events: aggregate diagnostics can dilute single-instance violations.
# E.g., group events per Order to confirm each N3 pair's true ordering, and
# inspect birth events to confirm the T6 cobirth is not a logging artifact.
```

## 6. Norm-completeness assessment

1. **N1/N2 temporal logic unstated.** *Risk:* "eventually" evaluated as directly-follows yields false positives (the 2/100 gap) and false comfort about timeliness. *Clarification:* define N1 as eventual reachability within a bounded window (e.g., 48h). *Status:* mandatory.
2. **N4 scope predicate unoperationalized.** "Intended for external shipment" has no attribute. *Risk:* the 2-package gap is unadjudicable. *Clarification:* require an `external_shipment` flag set at pack time. *Status:* mandatory (conditional on flag).
3. **No norm for Write Off bindings.** *Risk:* a financial event type with zero governance drifts unmonitored. *Clarification:* bind Write Off  Invoice as typical, with approval attribute. *Status:* typical.
4. **Unapplied cash lacks a distinct event subtype** despite E8's carve-out. *Risk:* every unmatched payment is permanently ambiguous (Finding 8). *Clarification:* mandate explicit `unapplied_cash` typing at recording time. *Status:* mandatory (labeling duty).
5. **Lifecycle couplings other than T6 ungoverned.** *Risk:* codeath edges (7 observed) are silently unassessable; future cobirth anomalies lack a baseline. *Clarification:* declare Package–Delivery codeath typical (joint confirmation). *Status:* typical.
6. **Customer relations wholly unspecified, including MCR  Customer.** *Risk:* privacy/access policies operate with no process-mining hook. *Clarification:* mark T4/MCR-Customer unspecified-but-referenced to the external customer-data policy. *Status:* explicitly unspecified with pointer.
7. **No tolerance bands or minimum support.** *Risk:* severity becomes analyst whim; rare-but-benign events get escalated (or real single-instance forbidden hits get dismissed). *Clarification:* zero-tolerance for forbidden; defined review threshold (not auto-fail) for mandatory gaps. *Status:* typical (procedural).
8. **Identifier-reuse/join policy ungoverned.** *Risk:* E10-style contamination recurs. *Clarification:* forbid joining telemetry to financial objects on non-namespaced keys. *Status:* forbidden.

## 8. Uncertainty statement

**Robust:** the log *as recorded* contains instances of all three forbidden patterns (N3 ×2, T6 ×1, E10 ×1); the typical-pattern absences (N5, E8-shaped) and all unregulated observations are not violations under the current open-world norm; mandatory OTG edges T1/T2 are satisfied. **Dependent on norm incompleteness:** whether the 98/100 N1 result, the MCR/Customer/codeath/reopen behaviors, and write-offs are acceptable — the norm neither condemns nor blesses them. **Dependent on suspected data-quality issues:** the heartbeat–invoice edge (near-certain join artifact), the 2 missing Delivery links, the 2 missing Add Item  Order links, and possibly the cobirth edge. **Undecidable from graph summaries alone:** true per-case event ordering and causality, shipment-intent scope flags, existence of unrecorded intermediate events, whether the unapplied-cash exception applies, and the source of the OTG/ET-OT counter discrepancy — all require case-level OCEL inspection and source-system corroboration before any finding is converted from "suspected" to "confirmed."
