## 2. Rule Interpretation

**RR1 Drafter/approver separation**
Same human normalised for aliases may not perform both `Draft_Change_Request` and `Approve_Change` in the same case.

**RR2 Implementer/verifier separation**
Same human normalised for aliases may not perform both `Implement_Change` and `Verify_Implementation` in the same case. Service accounts are not humans; conformance requires reliable session evidence mapping to an authorised human.

**RR3 High-risk approval authority**
For `risk == HIGH`, `Approve_Change` must be performed by the QM in role at event time or a valid QM delegate. Validity requires:
* effective interval covers event timestamp,
* scope covers case `scope`,
* principal absence / unavailability when required by delegation,
* no prohibited subdelegation.
Current master role is ignored; role is evaluated at event timestamp.

**RR4 Emergency substitution**
For `risk == HIGH` and `emergency == true`:
* `Approve_Change` may be performed by Incident Commander or valid IC delegate.
* `Retrospective_QM_Review` must be performed by QM or valid QM delegate within two business days.
Business days = Mon-Fri per site calendar. 2026-08-15 and 2026-08-16 are weekend days. Counting starts on the business day after approval. A later QMS database entry cannot prove an earlier delegation unless corroborated by signed evidence timestamped before the event.

**RR5 Identity evidence**
Service accounts do not satisfy SoD. Alias normalisation must precede SoD checks. Principal acting while on leave is technically permitted for the principal but raises identity evidence concerns.

**RR6 Suspicion versus proof**
Repeated resource pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations and concentrated handovers are indicators only.

## 6. Control Improvements

* Delegation creation: require signed delegation with effective interval recorded at creation time in QMS, prohibit back-dated DB entries, enforce no subdelegation clauses in system.
* Effective-time recording: immutable timestamp of delegation approval, separate from migration records.
* Principal availability: formal unavailability declaration linked to leave system, VPN use during leave triggers identity verification.
* Alias management: central identity registry with canonical human IDs, prevent alias collisions.
* Individual authentication: eliminate shared service accounts for SoD-sensitive activities; require FIDO re-auth per activity, workstation binding.
* Emergency review: enforce two business day clock in workflow, scope check for retrospective QM review delegation.
* Pair rotation: formal rotation policy for drafter/approver pairs, alert on >60% concentration.
* Network monitoring: automated handover/working-together dashboards with thresholds, not automated collusion labels.
* Evidential retention: badge, FIDO, VPN device fingerprint retention for 12 months, CCTV for shared terminals.
