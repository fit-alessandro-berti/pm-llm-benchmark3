## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model

Rules used, classified as instructed. Domain semantics 1-10 are treated as defeasible unless stated.

**Hard invariants (violation = log invalid):**
* **H1 Referential and type integrity:** Every `ocel:eid` and `ocel:oid` in relations must exist, type must match qualifier domain. Directional O2O: `carries` only `Delivery -> Package`, `packs` only `Package -> Item`, `contains` only `Order -> Item`, `bills` only `Invoice -> Order`, `settles` only `Payment -> Invoice`.
* **H2 Temporal-lifecycle monotonicity:** An object cannot be used after it is logically closed without explanation. `Create Order < Add Item < Pack Item < Dispatch Package < Confirm Delivery < Issue Invoice < Record Payment` is the normal precedence, but overlaps are allowed. A strong violation is e.g. packing an item before its order is created.
* **H3 No invented entities:** Repair may only add relations between existing events and objects. No new EIDs/OIDs.
* **H4 Deduplication:** One E2O edge is at most once per event-object pair; one O2O edge at most once per ordered pair-qualifier.

**Strong defaults (presumed true, require positive evidence to override):**
* **S1 Cardinality defaults:** `Create Order` = 1 Order (D1); `Add Item` = 1 Order +1 Item (D2); `Pack Item` = 1 Item +1 Package (D3); `Dispatch Package` = 1 Package +1 Delivery (D4); `Record Payment` = 1 Payment +1 Invoice (D7). Violation is not impossible - bulk dispatch, scanner outage, unapplied cash are explicitly allowed - but needs corroboration.
* **S2 Lifecycle completeness expectation (weak completeness):** We expect each Order to have Create, Add, Pack, Dispatch, Delivery confirmation. Each Package to have Pack and Dispatch, each Delivery to have Dispatch and Confirm, each Invoice to have Issue and at least one Payment if settled. *Not* a hard invariant per Constraints: do not repair merely to complete a lifecycle.
* **S3 Customer/route coherence:** Events that share `customer=C17` or `route=NORTH` are more likely to relate than cross-customer/route links, but route_hint is not a proof of delivery assignment.
* **S4 Existing O2O is ground truth:** Observed O2O edges are assumed correct and not deleted. Inferred O2O must be compatible with them.

**Weak heuristics (informative, never decisive alone):**
* **W1 Identifier prefix/similarity:** `scanner_ref=PK8-2`, `external_ref=N-421?` vs `carrier_ref=N-421`, `N-42*`, `B-771/772`. Useful for ranking candidates, per D9 not decisive.
* **W2 Timestamp proximity:** ev08 (09:05) immediately follows ev07 (09:04) on PK-8; ev09 and ev10 share 10:00; ev11 at 14:00 four hours after dispatch - plausible delivery window. Synchronous timestamps suggest bulk operation.
* **W3 Amount arithmetic:** `ORD-A 130 + ORD-B 100 = INV-44 230`, `PAY-1 100` matches ORD-B/INV-44 remainder, `PAY-2 130` matches ORD-A remainder. Per Constraints and D6/D7: evidence, not proof. Fees, discounts, taxes, partial settlements can explain any sum.
* **W4 Order_hint /declared_value:** `IT-A1/A2 -> ORD-A`, `IT-B1 -> ORD-B` hints from source system, but not authoritative.

**Evidence against a repair (negative weights):**
* **N1 Cardinality stretch:** Creating 1 Package -> 2 Items from different Orders (PK-8 packs IT-A2 from ORD-A and IT-B1 from ORD-B) creates a many-to-one Order-to-Package fan-in that is allowed but unusual and should be flagged as many-to-many if also linked to multiple orders via invoice.
* **N2 Identifier conflict:** Assigning `ev10 external_ref=N-421?` to DEL-X (N-420) requires ignoring the `1` suffix.
* **N3 Orphan creation:** Linking ev10 to DEL-Y and DEL-Y to PK-8 leaves DEL-X with only PK-7 and PK-8 unassigned if alternative bulk hypothesis were true - creates ambiguity about which delivery is confirmed by ev11.
* **N4 Portal loss:** ev11 has `portal_customer=C17` but degraded `external_ref=N-42*`. Allowing it to map to 0 deliveries (customer/order only) is explicitly permitted by D5. Forcing it to a delivery overstates portal fidelity.
* **N5 Circular confirmation:** An O2O edge inferred solely because an E2O edge was just hypothesized, then cited as independent corroboration for that E2O edge, is circular per section 5 requirement.

**Evidence combination model:**
No single dimension decides. Confidence is abductive: `confidence ~ f(cardinality fit, lifecycle order, independent corroboration across E2O+O2O, customer/route consistency, identifier/time proximity)`. Highest confidence requires at least two independent dimensions, ideally one strong default + one weak heuristic + no hard violation, and corroboration across both an E2O and a compatible O2O that are not circularly derived. Amount equality alone caps at ~0.6-0.7; identifier prefix alone caps at ~0.6. Leaving `unresolved` is preferred to adding a 0.4-0.5 edge that would impose a false deterministic structure on downstream discovery.

## 3. Alternative repair bundles

### Bundle A: Conservative / minimum-change repair
**Philosophy:** Fix only structurally forced gaps where scanner outage and Dispatch cardinality are strongly evidenced, keep financial and confirmation ambiguity explicit. Prefer orphan or provisional over forced completion.

* **ev08 and PK-8:** Add PR-01 (ev08-PK-8 E2O, accept) and PR-02 (PK-8 packs IT-B1 O2O, provisional). Rationale: scanner_ref is the strongest independent hint; leaving IT-B1 without a package violates H2 lifecycle more than tolerating a multi-order package. Confidence 0.85/0.82 but flagged as provisional for O2O to avoid circular confirmation.
* **ev10 and DEL-X/DEL-Y:** Add PR-03 (ev10-DEL-Y E2O) and PR-05 (DEL-Y carries PK-8) as provisional. Keeps 1 Package:1 Delivery default (S1) without bulk assumption. Rejects PR-04/PR-06 bulk merge. Leaves DEL-X with PK-7 and DEL-Y with PK-8 cleanly separated.
* **ev11 and delivery objects:** Leave PR-07/PR-08/PR-09 **unresolved**. ev11 remains `Confirm Delivery` related only to ORD-A as observed, respecting D5 portal-loss mode. Reason: `N-42*` matches both deliveries equally (45% each); choosing one would violate Constraint "do not assign ev11 uniquely unless alternatives explained as weaker" - alternatives are equally weak. Mark candidate relations as unresolved with reason `ambiguous carrier prefix + portal customer-only acknowledgement permitted`.
* **INV-44 and ORD-B:** Keep PR-10 (INV-44 bills ORD-B O2O) and PR-11 (ev12-ORD-B E2O) as **provisional**, not accept. Include in Bundle A provisional set but note they rely on arithmetic (W3) + customer coherence only. Conservative stance would allow downstream analysis to toggle them. If strictest minimum, leave them unresolved; Bundle A chooses provisional to enable sensitivity analysis while preserving that amount is not proof. Lifecycle: ORD-B would then be billed, but we do not claim certainty.
* **PAY-2 and INV-44:** Add PR-12 (PAY-2 settles INV-44 O2O) and PR-13 (ev14-INV-44 E2O) as provisional (0.70/0.72). Gives PAY-2 a settlement without asserting exclusivity; acknowledge unapplied-cash alternative remains viable.

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects

**Unrepaired log:** OC-DFG will show Pack Item -> Dispatch Package edges fragmented: IT-B1 has Pack Item with no Package outgoing edge, package hub disconnected; DEL-Y isolated node with no incoming event, filtered by frequency thresholds; Confirm Delivery connects only to Order, not to Delivery, hiding logistics vs order convergence; Issue Invoice connects INV-44 only to ORD-A, leaving ORD-B without outgoing invoice edge; Record Payment shows PAY-1->INV-44 but PAY-2 isolated. Flattened logs will be misleading.

**Bundle A vs unrepaired:**
* **OC-DFG:** Appears new edge `Pack Item --PK-8--> Dispatch Package` for IT-B1 (previously dangling). New O2O-driven edge `DEL-Y --carries--> PK-8` introduces `Dispatch Package --DEL-Y--> Confirm Delivery` path if ev11 left unresolved it will still not appear, so OTG will show DEL-Y dispatched but never confirmed - revealing incompleteness rather than faking confirmation. Invoice sub-graph gains `INV-44 --bills--> ORD-B` (provisional) creating `Add Item --ORD-B--> Issue Invoice` transitive dependency.
* **OTG:** Unrepaired OTG shows disconnected DEL-Y component and disconnected PAY-2. Bundle A connects PAY-2 into INV-44 component, merging Invoice-Payment cluster; connects IT-B1 into Package-Delivery chain, merging north route components into one connected OTG? Still leaves Delivery-Confirm edge absent, highlighting portal loss.
* **ET–OT graph:** Without repair, ET-OT events ev08, ev10, ev14 each miss one object type (scanner/delivery/invoice). After repair, ET-OT density increases: ev08 now bridges Item-Package, ev10 Package-Delivery, ev14 Payment-Invoice. Object type co-occurrence counts rise, affecting pattern mining.
* **Flattenings:** 
  * *Order flattening:* Unrepaired: ORD-A trace `Create->Add->Add->Pack->Pack->Dispatch->Confirm->Issue->Record`; ORD-B trace `Create->Add` then truncated (no dispatch/confirm/invoice). Bundle A (with invoice provisional): ORD-B extends to `...Pack->Dispatch->Issue->Record` closing its trace, adding 3 events to its case length, changing average trace length and variant clustering.
  * *Package flattening:* Unrepaired: PK-8 case contains `Pack IT-A2` only, missing IT-B1 packing and dispatch linkage partially (ev10 lists PK-8 but no delivery). Repaired: PK-8 case gains `Pack IT-B1` and `Dispatch DEL-Y`, doubling its event count and making it multi-item.
  * *Delivery flattening:* Unrepaired: DEL-X `Dispatch->(no Confirm)`; DEL-Y empty. Repaired A: DEL-Y `Dispatch` appears; still no Confirm. Bundle B would collapse to single Delivery case `Dispatch PK-7, PK-8 ->Confirm`.
  * *Invoice/ Payment flattening:* Unrepaired: INV-44 `Issue->Record(PAY-1)`; PAY-2 isolated single-event case. Repaired: INV-44 gains second `Record(PAY-2)` event, changing invoice settlement lead time from one to two steps and merging payment cases.

## 8. Over-repair warnings

1. **Fabricating a multi-order package:** Adding `PK-8 packs IT-B1` + `ev08-PK-8` as accepted fact merges ORD-A and ORD-B via a shared package. If PK-8 actually held only IT-A2 and IT-B1 went in an unseen package, the repair creates a false convergence point that will generate a spurious OC-DFG edge `Order(ORD-A) <-> Order(ORD-B) via Package`, distort package flattening weight analysis, and inflate batch-packing performance metrics.

2. **Collapsing two northbound deliveries into one (bulk over-merge):** Under Bundle B, assigning both PK-7 and PK-8 to DEL-X and orphaning DEL-Y fabricates a `1 Delivery ->2 Packages` bulk pattern. Downstream, delivery lead time, on-time confirmation rate, and carrier N-420 utilization will be overstated, while DEL-Y ghost delivery disappears, hiding a potential data quality issue (duplicate carrier_ref).

3. **Forcing Confirm Delivery to a single delivery on wildcard `N-42*`:** Assigning ev11 uniquely to DEL-X (or DEL-Y) pretends degraded portal data is precise. This injects a false `Confirm Delivery --DEL-X--> Delivery` edge that did not exist, causing OTG to show complete delivery lifecycles where actually none is confirmed, biasing delivery SLA mining and obscuring that confirmation is ambiguous for both deliveries.

4. **Proving invoice consolidation by arithmetic:** Treating `230 =130+100` as proof that `INV-44 bills ORD-B` and `ev12-ORD-B` must exist creates false financial consolidation. If INV-44 includes fees/discounts/taxes or PAY-1 is partial settlement of a prior invoice, the repair merges two independent order-to-cash cases, duplicating Order flattening events, distorting equivalent-execution clustering around Order, and producing a fake many-to-one `Invoice->Orders` relation that violates the instruction to leave such linkage unresolved when uncertain.

5. **Forcing payment settlement by remainder amount:** Accepting `PAY-2 settles INV-44` solely because 130 matches the invoice remainder hides legitimate unapplied cash. Over-repair will link PAY-2 into INV-44's flattened trace, shortening Days Sales Outstanding incorrectly, creating a false `Invoice --settles--> Payment` OTG path and masking a cash-application exception that should be investigated.

6. **Circular E2O+O2O reinforcement:** Adding an E2O (ev10-DEL-Y) then immediately adding the symmetric O2O (DEL-Y carries PK-8) and citing each as independent evidence for the other circularly inflates confidence. Discovery will then show two corroborating edges where only one weak hypothesis existed, making the repair look validated by `pm4py.discover_*` graphs when it is self-referential.

Additional guard: Any repair that makes every object lifecycle complete (Packages with both pack and dispatch, Deliveries with dispatch+confirm, Orders with invoice+payment) may look aesthetically pleasing but explicitly violates the Constraint to preserve uncertainty; such completeness should be treated as a diagnostic warning, not a goal.
