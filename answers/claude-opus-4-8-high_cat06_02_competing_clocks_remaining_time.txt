# Remaining-Time Forecast for Sterile Batch B-417

**Anchor / "now":** Friday, 18 September 2026, 15:30.
**Terminal events considered:** Electronic Release (dominant) or Batch Rejected (rare tail).

**Orientation before the sections.** The two parallel clocks are chemistry and microbiology. Chemistry, even with queueing and a possible weekend spillover, can plausibly finish inside a few working hours to early next week. Microbiology cannot: primary incubation alone is 46–54 h from Friday 14:00, i.e. it does not become *technically ready* until roughly Sunday midday at the earliest, and readout normally requires a weekday microbiologist. Because QA joins on the **maximum** of the two branch clocks, microbiology (plus its staffing calendar) governs the smooth path. Chemistry only becomes the binding constraint in the low-probability world where it spawns a rerun that itself slips across the weekend, or where it triggers correlated QA rework. The temperature-excursion flag is a latent, correlation-inducing variable that widens the anomaly tail on *both* branches simultaneously, so it is treated as a common factor rather than an independent event.

**Shortcut disclosure.** I use scenario envelopes and probability *bands*, not a Monte-Carlo convolution. Triangular service times are summarized by their means (min+mode+max)/3 for central figures and by their max for tails. Calendar pauses are applied by hand at the Friday-18:00 chemistry close, the weekend microbiology staffing gap, and QA opening hours. "Working hours" additions are unfolded onto the calendar (e.g., 8 QA working hours starting Monday 08:00 ≈ Monday end-of-first-pass).

Probability bands are approximate and **overlap**: reruns, contamination, queries, and investigations can co-occur, and the temperature excursion correlates several of them. They are not meant to sum to 100%.

## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

Reference calendar for the incubation window: Fri 14:00 + 46 h = **Sun 12:00**; + 50 h = **Sun 16:00**; + 54 h = **Mon 20:00**. Central technically-ready ≈ **Sun evening to Mon early morning**.

### Scenario A — Smooth path
- **Chemistry waiting + service.** At 15:30 two analysts finish current work at ~16:00–16:30 (0.4–0.9 h) and ~16:45–17:30 (1.2–2.0 h). Under risk-priority (~45%) B-417 can grab the first freed analyst ~16:00–16:30; triangular service mean ≈ (1.5+2.5+4.5)/3 ≈ 2.83 h would run past the 18:00 close → **pauses, resumes Mon 08:00**, finishing ~Mon 09:30–11:00. Under FIFO (~55%) it starts even later and clearly spills to **Mon morning**. Net: chemistry-usable ≈ **Mon 09:00–11:00** in most sub-cases; occasionally late Friday only if a short service starts before ~15:30 equivalent (unlikely here). *Chemistry is not the binding clock.*
- **Microbiology incubation + readout.** Technically ready Sun evening–Mon early. Weekend readout only if on-call authorized (0.25–0.45). Most-likely: read **Mon from 08:00**, +0.5–1.5 h → microbiology-usable **Mon ~08:30–10:00** (or **Sun +1–3 h** if on-call, i.e. Sun evening).
- **Parallel join = max(chemistry, microbiology).** Both land Monday morning; join ≈ **Mon ~10:00–11:00**. This is a max, not a sum: the ~2–3 h chemistry service and the ~1 h microbiology readout are not added — the branches ran concurrently and both simply have to be *present*.
- **QA waiting + service.** Monday brings two reviewers; the Saturday reviewer may have partially drained the queue. Expect **0–3 h** wait, then service ~1.5 h (P80 ~3.5 h). QA done ≈ **Mon early–late afternoon**.
- **Rework/investigation.** None by definition of A.
- **Final release.** +0.25–0.75 h → **Electronic Release Mon 22 Sep, ~13:00–19:00**; with an early on-call microbiology readout the optimistic edge slides to **Mon midday** or, exceptionally, **Sun night**.

### Scenario B — Chemistry rerun
- Chemistry primary as in A, then a borderline result (p 0.10–0.20) triggers a **priority rerun** triangular(1.0,1.8,3.0), mean ≈ 1.93 h, **cannot cross the 18:00 close**. If the primary only finishes Monday, the rerun also runs Monday ~08:00–10:30 → chemistry-usable **Mon ~10:00–12:00**.
- Microbiology unchanged → still Monday morning. **Join still ≈ Mon late morning** (max), so the rerun frequently hides *behind* the microbiology clock and adds little to the terminal time.
- Consequence that *does* matter: the rerun lifts the **investigation** probability into the 0.30–0.55 band (link to D). Absent an investigation, terminal ≈ **Mon evening–Tue 23 Sep**.

### Scenario C — Delayed microbiology
- **C-staffing:** ready late-band (Mon ~18:00–20:00) or no weekend on-call → readout slips to **Mon 08:00** (if ready by then) or **Tue 08:00** (if it only becomes ready Monday evening after the microbiologist has left). Microbiology-usable **Mon morning → Tue morning**. Join and QA follow → terminal **Mon 22 – Tue 23 Sep**.
- **C-contamination:** contamination indication (p 0.05–0.12) requires **+20–30 h continuous re-incubation** plus **0.5–1.5 h** staffed readout. Starting from a Sun–Mon ready point, +20–30 h reaches **Mon evening–Wed**, then readout again bounded by weekday staffing → microbiology-usable **Wed 24 – Thu 25 Sep**. This is a genuine additive extension *within* the microbiology branch (sequential re-incubation), unlike the cross-branch join which is a max. Terminal **Wed–Fri 26 Sep**, and contamination usually co-opens an investigation (D).

### Scenario D — QA query / investigation tail
- Start from whichever branch max lands (Mon–Wed).
- **Documentation Query** (p 0.15–0.30): QA pauses, +4–12 **working** hours before resuming. On a Monday start that is roughly +0.5–1.5 QA working days → resume Tue, second QA pass ~1.5–3.5 h → terminal **Wed 24 – Fri 26 Sep**.
- **Deviation Investigation:** doc-only (60–80%) adds 8–20 working h → **Wed–Fri**; **laboratory investigation** (remaining) adds **3–7 calendar days** → **Mon 28 Sep – Thu 1 Oct**, and if stacked on a contamination/excursion cluster, **into early October**. A confirmed **critical** defect here routes to **Batch Rejected** (recording 0.5–1.0 h) instead of release — a low-probability terminal.

**Where max vs. sum is used.** Max is applied once, at the chemistry∥microbiology join (concurrent branches, only presence required). Sums are applied *within* a branch (incubation → readout; primary → rerun; re-incubation for contamination) and *after* the join (QA wait → QA service → optional rework → release), because those steps are strictly sequential. Working-hour rework adds are unfolded against QA/lab calendars, not added as raw clock hours.

## 4. Forecast summary

All figures are remaining time from **Fri 18 Sep 15:30**, expressed as ranges (rounded), with approximate clock dates.

- **Plausible earliest completion:** ~**30–46 h** → **Sun 20 Sep night to Mon 21 Sep morning**. Requires weekend on-call microbiology readout (authorized, dispatched fast) *and* a clean QA pass. Low probability but real.
- **Central / P50-like remaining time:** ~**70–100 h** → **Electronic Release Monday 21–Tuesday 22 Sep, roughly midday to evening.** Driven by Monday microbiology readout + a same-day or next-morning QA pass. (Correcting for calendar: "Monday" here is **Mon 21 Sep**; if the late-band incubation pushes readout to Tuesday, the P50 edges to **Tue 22 Sep**.)
- **P80-like remaining time:** ~**100–150 h** → **Tuesday 22 – Thursday 24 Sep.** Absorbs one moderate complication: no weekend on-call, a chemistry rerun, a Documentation Query, or a doc-only investigation.
- **P90-like / severe tail:** ~**170–330+ h (≈ 7–14 days)** → **Monday 28 Sep – early October**, dominated by a **laboratory Deviation Investigation** (3–7 calendar days) and/or **contamination confirmation** (+20–30 h re-incubation), especially if the **temperature excursion is confirmed** and correlates these events. A **Batch Rejected** terminal lives in this tail with small probability.
- **Most plausible terminal event(s):** **Electronic Release** in the great majority of worlds (~85–92%). **Batch Rejected** is the minority terminal (~a few percent, concentrated in the excursion/contamination/critical-defect cluster).

> Note on dates: with the given anchor (Fri 18 Sep), the "next Monday" is **21 September 2026**. The section-3 narrative labels the readout day as Monday; concretely that is Mon 21 Sep, with Tuesday = 22 Sep, etc. Ranges above use those calendar dates.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers (ranked)

1. **Weekend microbiology readout staffing (on-call authorization + late-band incubation).** *Calendar + observation uncertainty.* This single factor separates a Sunday-night finish from a Monday/Tuesday finish and sets the smooth-path P50.
2. **Deviation Investigation type (doc-only vs. laboratory).** *Path uncertainty.* The lab branch adds 3–7 calendar days and is the primary determinant of the P90 tail.
3. **Temperature-excursion confirmation.** *Dependence uncertainty.* Latent common factor that jointly inflates rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities; it is why the far tail is fatter than an independence assumption would suggest.
4. **QA queue and capacity at the join.** *Queue uncertainty.* Overdispersed arrivals plus the Saturday-single / Monday-double reviewer schedule swing the post-join wait by ~0 to ~1+ working day.
5. **Chemistry queue discipline and 18:00 spillover.** *Queue + calendar + service-time uncertainty.* Mostly hidden behind the microbiology max, but it governs whether chemistry contributes a rerun and therefore feeds the correlated investigation risk.

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

**Single most forecast-changing observation: whether an on-call microbiologist is authorized for the weekend (and, jointly, exactly when primary incubation crosses "technically ready").**
- *If authorized and incubation is ready by Sunday:* microbiology-usable jumps to **Sunday evening**, the whole distribution shifts left by roughly a working day, and a **Sunday-night / early-Monday Electronic Release** becomes the central case.
- *If not authorized (or the result matures only Monday evening after staffed hours):* readout is pinned to **Monday 08:00 (or Tuesday 08:00)**, locking the P50 into Mon 21–Tue 22 Sep and removing the optimistic edge entirely.

**Secondary update trigger: confirmation (or clearing) of the temperature-excursion flag.** Confirmation simultaneously raises borderline-chemistry, contamination, and investigation probabilities — pushing mass from the P80 band into the multi-day P90 tail and increasing the (still minority) chance of a **Batch Rejected** terminal. Clearing the flag collapses the correlated tail and tightens the forecast toward Scenario A.
