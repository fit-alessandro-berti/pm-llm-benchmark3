# 1. Executive Verdict

The net is syntactically a workflow net (single source and single sink, with every node on a source-to-sink path), but it fails to be sound. Its principal structural defect is a concurrency-choice mismatch at the split-join boundaries: the model splits into parallel paths (analysis and review) using an implicit OR/AND topology, but the review branch offers a race between a standard review and a waiver via a shared place `p_review`. 

Because `t_standard_review` and `t_waive_review` consume the same token from `p_review`, the model behaves as an exclusive choice between a standard review and a waiver. However, the completion of analysis (`t_join_normal`) and the waiver completion (`t_join_waived`) feed into two separate join transitions that both push tokens to `p_decision`. 

The business consequence is twofold: first, deadlocks occur if standard review is chosen (because the waiver join `t_join_waived` is starved); second, token duplication occurs if the waiver is chosen (because both analysis and waiver paths fire, sending two tokens to `p_decision`, which leaves a dangling token at `sink` while another token may remain trapped or cause unsound final markings).

# 2. Structural Diagnosis

| Element or subnet | Intended role | Actual structural effect | Potential symptom |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Initial/Final Markings** | Define start (`source`) and end (`sink`) of the workflow. | Exactly 1 token in `source`, 1 in `sink` at completion. | Structurally valid workflow net source/sink pair, but behavioral token accumulation violates proper completion. |
| **Split (`t_split`)** | Parallel split initiating analysis and review. | Places one token in `p_analysis` and one token in `p_review`. | Correct structural parallel split, but downstream choices misalign. |
| **Review Alternatives** | Exclusive choice between standard review and waiver. | `t_standard_review` and `t_waive_review` both consume from `p_review`. | Race condition; mutually exclusive execution paths correctly modeled at this micro-step. |
| **Joins (`t_join_normal`, `t_join_waived`)** | Synchronize analysis and review outcomes. | `t_join_normal` requires `p_analysis_done` AND `p_review_done`. `t_join_waived` requires only `p_waived`. | Deadlock if standard review is chosen (and analysis finishes, but review fails to feed `p_review_done` if waived instead). Deadlock or token leak depending on execution race. |
| **Path Connectivity** | Every node must lie on a source-to-sink path. | All places and transitions are connected from `source` through intermediate states to `sink`. | Satisfies structural workflow-net prerequisite, but hides behavioral unsoundness. |

# 3. Marking-Based Counterexample

Consider the legal firing sequence for the **waiver route**:
1. `t_register`
2. `t_split`
3. `t_waive_review`
4. `t_analyze`
5. `t_join_waived`
6. `t_join_normal`
7. `t_authorize`

### Marking Table (Waiver Route)

| Step | Fired transition | Resulting nonzero marking | Enabled transitions | Observation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0 | (Initial) | `{source: 1}` | `t_register` | Initial state. |
| 1 | `t_register` | `{p_ready: 1}` | `t_split` | Case registered. |
| 2 | `t_split` | `{p_analysis: 1, p_review: 1}` | `t_analyze, t_standard_review, t_waive_review` | Parallel branches created. |
| 3 | `t_waive_review` | `{p_analysis: 1, p_waived: 1}` | `t_analyze, t_join_waived` | Waiver recorded; review branch closed. |
| 4 | `t_analyze` | `{p_analysis_done: 1, p_waived: 1}` | `t_join_normal, t_join_waived` | Analysis completed. |
| 5 | `t_join_waived` | `{p_analysis_done: 1, p_decision: 1}` | `t_join_normal, t_authorize` | Waiver join fires, placing a token in `p_decision`. |
| 6 | `t_join_normal` | `{p_decision: 2}` | `t_authorize` | Normal join fires **even though the review was waived**, placing a second token in `p_decision`. |
| 7 | `t_authorize` | `{sink: 1, p_decision: 1}` | `t_authorize` (if re-enabled) | `t_authorize` fires once, placing a token in `sink`, but **a leftover token remains in `p_decision`**. |

**Explanation of leftover tokens:** The exact final marking requirement ($\text{fm} = \{\text{sink}: 1\}$) is violated because a stray token remains trapped in `p_decision`. If standard review is chosen instead, `t_join_waived` is never enabled, and `t_join_normal` cannot fire because `p_review_done` is never populated, resulting in a deadlock.

# 4. Minimal-Repair Alternatives

| Repair | Exact edits | Edit cost | Preserved intent | Changed behavior | New risk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Repair A (Waiver Join Sync)** | Remove arc `(p_waived, join_waived)`. Add arcs `(p_analysis_done, join_waived)` and `(p_waived, join_waived)`. Route `join_waived` to `p_decision`. | 3 arc changes (1 removed, 2 added) | Mandatory analysis on all paths; explicit waiver synchronization. | Waiver path now explicitly waits for analysis completion before proceeding to decision. | Deadlock if waiver path bypasses analysis conceptually (mitigated by policy). |
| **Repair B (Unified Join with Silent Transition)** | Remove `join_waived` and `p_waived`. Add silent transition `t_skip_review` between `p_review` and `p_decision` (or merge into `join_normal`). | 2 places/transitions removed, 3 arcs rewired | Flexible review outcome feeding standard decision node. | Waiver completely skips analysis and review, merging directly into decision. | Violates business intent that analysis is *always* mandatory. |

**Minimality Justification:** Under an edit-cost model counting atomic graph mutations (add/remove arcs, places, transitions), **Repair A** incurs a cost of 3 (1 arc removal, 2 arc additions) and preserves the core business policy that analysis is mandatory. **Repair B** incurs a higher structural transformation cost (removing a place and transition, rewiring multiple arcs) and alters the business rule by making analysis optional when waived. Thus, Repair A is the minimal structural fix that adheres strictly to the business intent.

# 5. Corrected PM4Py Code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet, Marking
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils import petri_utils

repaired_net = PetriNet("parallel_review_with_waiver_repaired")

source = PetriNet.Place("source")
p_ready = PetriNet.Place("p_ready")
p_analysis = PetriNet.Place("p_analysis")
p_review = PetriNet.Place("p_review")
p_analysis_done = PetriNet.Place("p_analysis_done")
p_review_done = PetriNet.Place("p_review_done")
p_waived = PetriNet.Place("p_waived")
p_decision = PetriNet.Place("p_decision")
sink = PetriNet.Place("sink")

for p in {
    source, p_ready, p_analysis, p_review, p_analysis_done,
    p_review_done, p_waived, p_decision, sink
}:
    repaired_net.places.add(p)

register = PetriNet.Transition("t_register", "Register Case")
split = PetriNet.Transition("t_split", None)
analyze = PetriNet.Transition("t_analyze", "Perform Analysis")
standard_review = PetriNet.Transition("t_standard_review", "Perform Standard Review")
waive_review = PetriNet.Transition("t_waive_review", "Record Review Waiver")
join_normal = PetriNet.Transition("t_join_normal", None)
join_waived = PetriNet.Transition("t_join_waived", None)
authorize = PetriNet.Transition("t_authorize", "Authorize Case")

for t in {
    register, split, analyze, standard_review, waive_review,
    join_normal, join_waived, authorize
}:
    repaired_net.transitions.add(t)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(source, register, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(register, p_ready, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_ready, split, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(split, p_analysis, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(split, p_review, repaired_net)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_analysis, analyze, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(analyze, p_analysis_done, repaired_net)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_review, standard_review, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(standard_review, p_review_done, repaired_net)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_review, waive_review, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(waive_review, p_waived, repaired_net)

# Normal join: requires analysis done AND standard review done
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_analysis_done, join_normal, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_review_done, join_normal, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(join_normal, p_decision, repaired_net)

# Repaired waiver join: requires analysis done AND waiver recorded (Repair A)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_analysis_done, join_waived, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_waived, join_waived, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(join_waived, p_decision, repaired_net)

petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(p_decision, authorize, repaired_net)
petri_utils.add_arc_from_to(authorize, sink, repaired_net)

repaired_im = Marking()
repaired_im[source] = 1
repaired_fm = Marking()
repaired_fm[sink] = 1
```

# Helper to safely fire a transition along a path
def safe_fire(net, marking, transition_name):
    enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
    target_t = next((t for t in enabled if t.name == transition_name), None)
    if not target_t:
        raise ValueError(f"Transition '{transition_name}' is not enabled in marking {marking}")
    return pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils.petri_utils.execute(target_t, marking)

**Expected Qualitative Difference:** The original net diagnostics will report unsoundness due to deadlocks (when standard review is chosen) and/or improper completion (stray tokens in `p_decision`). The repaired net diagnostics will confirm that the net is a sound workflow net, guaranteeing proper completion and absence of deadlocks.

# 7. Repair Trade-Offs and Residual Risks

- **Distinguishability:** Standard review and waiver remain fully distinguishable through the firing of `t_standard_review` versus `t_waive_review`.
- **Silent Transitions:** The repair introduces no new silent transitions, preserving clear event logs for process mining discovery.
- **Soundness vs. Business Rules:** Soundness proves absence of deadlocks and proper termination, but it does not intrinsically prove that business rules (e.g., "analysis cannot be skipped") are met unless modeled structurally.
- **Regression Testing:** Positive traces (Register $\rightarrow$ Analyze $\rightarrow$ Standard Review $\rightarrow$ Authorize) and (Register $\rightarrow$ Analyze $\rightarrow$ Waive $\rightarrow$ Authorize) must be tested. Negative traces (attempting to authorize without completing review or analysis) must be verified as blocked.
- **Bypass Detection:** Accidental bypasses of mandatory analysis are prevented structurally because both `join_normal` and `join_waived` explicitly depend on `p_analysis_done`.

# 8. Final Recommendation

**Deployment Choice:** **Repair A** (Waiver Join Sync) is selected for deployment. It requires minimal structural changes (modifying only incoming arcs to `join_waived`), preserves all original transition labels and semantics, and strictly enforces the business mandate that analysis must always precede authorization regardless of whether the review is performed or waived.

**Fallback Choice:** As a fallback, if business requirements evolve to allow parallel waiver processing *without* waiting for analysis, a redesigned exclusive choice gateway preceding both branches should be implemented, converting the process into a structured XOR-split over two distinct subprocess branches rather than a flawed concurrent split.
