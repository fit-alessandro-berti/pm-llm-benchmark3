# 3. Supporting and contradicting cases

**QA – primary FIFO + appointment:**
- Support: A02 over A04 at 08:30 (FIFO vs priority); A05 over A08 at 09:05 (FIFO vs priority, also vs SPT where A07 would win); A07 over A08 at 09:32 (FIFO vs priority); A04/A05/A06 durations equal estimates, so SPT had accurate data yet was ignored – strengthens FIFO claim.
- Challenge: A03 over A02 at 08:20 violates pure FIFO. Explained by known constraint: appointment 08:20 honored exactly. Does not require undocumented exception if rule is “appointment reserves capacity.” Alternative undocumented exception – dispatcher favored early-arriving appointment – would also fit but appointment rule is documented candidate, so constraint explanation suffices.
- Challenge: 08:30-08:32 2-min idle before A02. Explained by scan jitter / setup or calibration prep, not policy. If idle were voluntary to wait for A03? No, A03 already done. So minor data-quality gap, not violation.
- A06 “started late” 5 min after appt because A05 nonpreemptible. Supports nonpreemptive appointment (appointment does not preempt). Contradicts preemptive appointment.

**QB – primary preemptive priority:**
- Support: B02 preempts B01 (B01 segments prove stop/resume, reason “urgent arrival”); B05 selected at 10:00 ahead of older B01/B04 and shorter B04 – only priority predicts B05, rejecting FIFO and SPT/SRPT; B06 over B07 at 10:36 matches priority+ FIFO and rejects appointment (B07 overdue) and SPT (B07 shorter).
- Challenge: B01 resume at 09:31 ahead of B03 (P2) violates fresh priority selection. Explainable as preemptive-resume discipline (resume preempted job before re-ranking) – a known preemption-cost control, not necessarily policy change. Requires auxiliary assumption: resume has priority over new selection.
- Challenge: B01 resume at 10:14 ahead of B06 (P2) and B04/B07 cannot be resume-immediately-after-preemption because B01 had already run 09:31-09:48 and 10:14 is after intervening B03+B05. This needs stronger exception: either (i) dispatcher batches to avoid third preemption/setup, or (ii) queue switched to FIFO drain, or (iii) B06/B07 eligibility mis-timed. Scan delay cannot explain 9-min gap (10:0510:14). So undocumented exception or hybrid switch required. This is the weakest point.
- Challenge: B04 over B06 at 10:28 violates priority; supports FIFO. Could be explained if dispatcher deferred P2 job with est 20 to finish short P3 (est 8) before appointment B07? **QC – primary SPT:**

# 4. Counterfactual discrimination tests

**Test 1 – QB 09:48 (B01 second stop) – Priority vs SPT/SRPT.**
- Episode: B01 runs 09:31-09:48, stops, B03 starts 09:48-10:00. B04 (e8) and B05 (P1 e14, arr 09:46) plausibly waiting.
- Prediction under H1 preemptive priority with 2-min scan delay: if B05 scan delayed to 09:48, eligible set = {B01-residue P3, B03 P2, B04 P3}  highest priority B03  predicts B03 (observed). If B05 visible, predicts B05 (not observed).
- Prediction under H2 SPT/SRPT: among {B01-residue ~14, B03 12, B04 8, B05 14} shortest is B04  predicts B04 (not observed). Even excluding B05, predicts B04 over B03 (not observed).
- Thus observed B03 favors H1 + invisibility over H2, but only if B05 invisible. Discriminating observable: dispatch-decision log with queue-entry scan timestamp vs arrival-scan timestamp, plus eligibility snapshot at 09:47-09:49 and dispatcher reassignment ticket for “second stop.” If B05 scan time 09:46 and was displayed, H1 fails and reassignment was non-priority. Also current estimated remaining time for B01 at 09:48 would separate SRPT (if residue <8, SRPT would predict B01 continue, not B03).

**Test 2 – QC 10:32 (C04 vs C06) – SPT vs priority/EDD/feasibility-skip.**
- Episode: after C03, remaining C01 e36, C04 e22, C05 P1 e40 dl10:50, C06 e6 dl15:00. Observed C04.
- Prediction under H1 pure SPT: C06 (6)  not observed.
- Prediction under H2 SLA/priority override (SPT unless endangered): endangered is C05 (deadline 18 min away, duration 40)  predicts C05  not observed.
- Both strongest candidates fail, revealing third hypothesis H3: feasibility-based deferral + EDD batching (e.g., Moore-Hodgson: skip jobs that cannot meet deadlines, or skip far-deadline short job to keep deadline order among feasible?). H3 would need to explain why C04 (dl 14:30) before C06 (dl 15:00) and before C05 (infeasible). Counterfactual: H1 predicts C06 next, H2 predicts C05 next.
- Discriminating observable: contemporaneous `est_min` as seen at 10:32 (was C06 estimate really 6 then, or revised?), dispatch-decision time log, and policy-version / SLA-risk flag for C05 at 10:32 vs 10:54. If C05 flagged “infeasible – deferred” at 10:32 but “must-run – tardy” at 10:54, that supports SLA-state-dependent hybrid. If C06 estimate was temporarily larger or C06 not released (e.g., missing paperwork), eligibility-set log would show C06 ineligible, rescuing SPT without hybrid.

# 5. Operational exceptions and alternative explanations

Ranked by plausibility (most to least plausible given prompt allowances):

1. **Arrival-scan delay up to 2 min hides new arrivals at decision instants – HIGH.** Explains QB 09:48 (B05 arr 09:46 invisible at 09:48 choice of B03) and smooths 09:16 preemption lag (09:1409:16). Alters diagnosis: rescues preemptive priority at 09:48 without invoking SPT failure. Does not rescue QB 10:14/10:28 or QC 10:32 (gaps >2 min). Needed because timestamps accurate to 1 min but scans lagging is explicitly allowed.
2. **Preemption resume affinity / nonpreemptive resume to avoid waste/setup – HIGH.** Explains QB 09:31 (resume B01 over B03) as standard preemptive-resume, not FIFO violation. If resume cost is high, dispatcher rationally completes preempted job before re-ranking. Alters diagnosis: QB remains preemptive priority with resume, rather than FIFO hybrid, for early phase. Less able to explain 10:14 second resume after intervening jobs; would need to posit escalating resume priority or sunk-cost bias.
3. **SLA-endangered policy switch (SPT  priority/EDD) and starvation aging – MODERATE-HIGH.** Explains QC 10:54 (promote C05 when deadline missed/at risk) and QC 11:40 (force C01 after 100-min starvation) while preserving SPT as base. Prompt explicitly allows policy change when SLA endangered. Alters diagnosis: QC from “failed SPT” to “SPT + urgency override + aging” hybrid, raising confidence in SPT base. Cannot explain 10:32 without additional skip rule.
4. **Cross-server coordination / look-ahead idling and reservation (skill + meeting + appointment) – MODERATE.** Explains QD D-F idle 11:36-12:00 (reserve D-F for D05-only-qualified, avoid meeting overlap, leave D10/D06 to D-X) and D-X idle 11:20-11:30 (wait for appointment). Alters diagnosis: confirms skill-based routing is not myopic per-server FIFO but system-level routing with future-availability reasoning. Without this, D-F idle looks like violation of work-conservation.
5. **Noisy / strategically inaccurate `est_min` – LOW-MODERATE in-sample.** In-sample estimates equal realized durations exactly in all queues, so invoking noise to rescue QC 10:32 (C06 estimate larger then) or QB deviations is ad hoc. Plausible only if estimates revised after dispatch (not logged). If allowed, it could rescue SPT anywhere, making SPT unfalsifiable – hence dangerous. Ranked lower because no evidence of inaccuracy here, though prompt warns it is possible.
6. **Undocumented dispatcher reassignment / operational reason without logged interruption_reason – LOW but explicitly allowed.** QB second stop logged as “dispatcher reassignment” – could be exogenous (operator request, tooling, urgency not captured by priority). Could explain any residual (QB 10:14/10:28, QC 10:32) as one-off. Ranked lowest because it explains everything and therefore nothing; invoking it for every violation removes identifiability. Absence of reason does not prove no reason, but repeated use would downgrade all confidences.

Effect on diagnoses if 1-4 accepted: QA unchanged (FIFO+appointment solid); QB primary remains preemptive priority but secondary becomes resume+delay rather than policy switch (confidence would rise to ~65 if 10:14/10:28 also attributed to drain, else stays ~55); QC primary SPT strengthened with hybrid override (confidence rises); QD skill+FIFO+appointment strengthened.
