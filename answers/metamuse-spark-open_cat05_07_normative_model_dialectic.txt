## 1. Stipulated Facts, Disputed Propositions, and Burden of Proof

### 1.1 Stipulated facts

**Normative baseline:** CS-9 is controlled, versioned and effective 2025-01-01 onward. Content undisputed: (i) every onboarding in scope requires Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review before activation; (ii) every activation requires Manual_Procurement_Approval before Activate_Supplier; (iii) requester and manual approver must be different humans. M0 faithfully encodes (i)-(ii) in `StrictPartialOrder` form: `Register -> (Sanctions || Quality) -> XOR(Manual->Activate , Reject) -> Notify -> Close`. M0 does **not** encode the SoD resource rule, any loop, any amount/risk/time predicate, or any scope carve-out.

**Overlays:** PD-3 is documented only in steering-committee minutes + follow-up email, window 2026-06-01 to 2026-07-31 inclusive. Wording undisputed: LOW-risk DE supplier in approved corporate group may use Auto_Approve instead of Manual; Sanctions_Check + automated master-data validation before activation remain mandatory; Quality_Review may be deferred up to 2 business days. Compliance observer’s contemporaneous note: “pilot may proceed as monitored exception” followed later by denial that she approved weakening CS-9. FD-7 is documented, window 2026-07-10 00:00 to 2026-07-12 23:59, scope “critical relief suppliers needed to prevent shutdown”. Content undisputed: Sanctions_Check before activation mandatory; Quality_Review and manual approval may follow provisional activation; spend cap EUR 20,000 until retrospective approval; daily monitoring + Quality_Review 2 business days. It expired automatically by its own terms. N5: Operations routinely performs 1 Request_More_Info cycles before decision; 47 cases in period show this and none activated before evidence arrived; stakeholders agree behaviour is desirable.

**Data environment:** From 2026-07-01 Notify_Requester emits primarily from CRM; ~70% mirrored to onboarding log. Stable `notification_id` can link when present; older links are mutable text entered later. Missing-notify rate in onboarding log jumped 2%  29% post-migration. Migration duplication incident produced identical `source_transaction_id` for duplicated Register events (SUP-711). QA_Clearance was introduced as UI/procedure rename for Quality_Review; mapping approved one week after SUP-713, audit confirms content equivalence. Aggregate: 312 Auto_Approve cases June-July; 289 appear pilot-eligible by retained attributes, 14 clearly ineligible, 9 missing eligibility attributes; 6 additional Auto_Approve in first week of August after pilot expiry.

**Case traces as logged:** The order rows for SUP-701 to SUP-715 are stipulated as observed log order. Alias resolution for SUP-712 (same human via two aliases) and SUP-715 (spouses in same reporting line, undisclosed) and amounts/spend and monitoring assertions above are stipulated as investigatory findings, not as log-inherent facts.

### 1.2 Disputed propositions

1. **Authority hierarchy:** Can PD-3 or informal “monitored exception” language create a legally effective exception to a controlled standard without incorporation into CS-9/M0? Does factual persistence of an emergency extend FD-7 beyond its sunset clause without written extension?
2. **Scope of M0/CS-9:** Are rejected pre-screen leads (SUP-709) and reactivations relying on still-valid prior certification (SUP-710) governed cases? Are provisional activations with capped spend and retro-review qualitatively distinct cases?
3. **Identity and label resolution:** Does `QA_Clearance`  `Quality_Review` pro tunc? Does `Auto_Approve` + `Activate_Supplier` with deferred `Quality_Review` count as compliance when pilot conditions are met?
4. **Logging sufficiency:** Does a CRM record with stable `notification_id` prove `Notify_Requester` occurred for conformance purposes (SUP-702)? Does mutable text linking suffice (SUP-706)? Does absence in the onboarding log prove omission?
5. **Materiality:** Is trace-deviation synonymous with business misconduct? Does desirable-but-unmodelled behaviour (Request_More_Info) excuse deviation? Does “no transaction before clearance” mitigate a preventive-control bypass (SUP-708)? Is post-pilot “normalised” practice a good-faith expectation defence (SUP-714)?
6. **Model adequacy:** Is high deviation rate evidence of operational noncompliance or of M0 obsolescence/incompleteness (missing loop, missing concurrent deferred path, missing resource rule, missing scope rules, missing label-version mapping)?

All disputed propositions depend on **authority**, **scope**, **identity**, or **logging assumptions**, not on trace syntax alone. They cannot be resolved by conformance replay alone.

### 1.3 Burden of proof

**Presumption of validity:** CS-9/M0 remain binding as published until formally amended through the controlled governance path. Informal communications, anticipated approvals, or factual exigency do not amend them.

**Declaring noncompliance:** Requires (a) identification of a specific CS-9/M0 or incorporated overlay rule violated, (b) preponderance of evidence that the rule applied at the event timestamp and to that case type, and (c) trace + contextual evidence excluding reasonable data-quality or scope doubt. `NONCOMPLIANT` for preventive controls (Sanctions, SoD, spend caps) may rest on ex-ante risk, not ex-post loss. Severe/critical requires clear evidence of intentional bypass, high-risk context, or excess spend.

**Accepting an exception/justification:** Requires (a) competent, documented authority covering the timestamp, (b) entity and risk predicates actually satisfied via contemporaneous attribute snapshot, (c) any compensating controls (cap, monitoring, deadline) evidenced. “Pilot-eligible in retrospect” without snapshot, “emergency still existed”, or “CRM probably notified” invert the burden and are rejected as justification. Incomplete logging creates `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE`, not a presumption of compliance or violation – the party relying on the unlogged event bears the burden of producing stable, immutable evidence (`notification_id`, approval `version_id`, `eligibility_snapshot`).

**Scope and identity:** Ambiguity construed against the modeller, not the operator, but only where scope documents are genuinely ambiguous and operator handling is consistent and low-risk. Where operator exploits ambiguity to evade a core control for high-risk/high-value cases, violation is found and scope is treated as a defect to be repaired prospectively.

Application: This framework lets us vindicate low-risk pilot-conforming deferrals with full evidence, while rejecting ineligible high-risk uses, post-expiry extensions without written authority, and sanctions-after-activation.

## 2. Prosecution Brief

The operation is materially noncompliant and CS-9/M0 must be enforced until formally changed.

**Formal authority controls.** CS-9 is the only controlled standard; PD-3 never progressed beyond minutes/email. The Compliance observer explicitly qualified her remark as “monitored exception” and later repudiated any weakening of CS-9. Monitored exception means deviation is *tolerated for measurement and remains a deviation* – prosecution acknowledges this nuance rather than caricaturing it as covert approval. Likewise FD-7 self-terminates 2026-07-12 23:59; its drafters chose automatic expiry precisely to force active re-authorisation. Allowing “factual emergency still existed” (SUP-705) or “permanent approval was imminent” (SUP-714) as substitutes replaces legal certainty with operator self-certification. The aggregate drift proves the point: 14 clearly ineligible Auto_Approvals including a HIGH-risk foreign supplier at EUR 180,000 (SUP-703), 9 with missing attributes that cannot be retro-deemed eligible, and 6 post-pilot continuations in one week. Normalisation of deviance is not harmless variation – it is what pilot governance warned against.

**Desirable does not equal authorised.** Prosecution concedes M0 is incomplete: it forces `Notify` in the onboarding log, omits the `Request_More_Info` loop (47 beneficial cases, zero early activations), lacks label-version indirection for QA_Clearance, and duplicates Register on migration. Those defects inflate raw deviation rates and must not be weaponised to criminalise good practice – SUP-707, SUP-713 and SUP-711 (after dedup) are not misconduct. Nor is SUP-702, when viewed narrowly, abusive. But desirable outcomes do not retroactively amend policy. If “we all agree requesting more info is good” entailed compliance, any operator could invent any step and claim desirability. The correct inference is: M0 should be repaired; until then behaviour remains nonconforming-by-model but must be judged for material risk through overlays – not declared compliant.

### 5.2 System-level judgment

**Operational compliance:** *Conditionally noncompliant with isolated severe tail.* The bulk operation (approx. 289 pilot-eligible deferrals with timely retro-Quality, 47 info-request improvements, emergency activations within cap/monitoring) followed the *spirit* of controlled standards via disciplined use of pilots/emergencies where conditions held. Yet the tail is material and not attributable to model obsolescence: **SUP-703** (ineligible high-risk Auto_Approve, EUR 180k), **SUP-708** (sanctions inversion), **SUP-712** (SoD alias concealment) and the **post-expiry continuations** (SUP-705, SUP-714 + five siblings) together breach preventive, segregation, and governance controls. Two are flagged by Internal Audit as potentially severe. Together they demonstrate (i) eligibility gating and IAM alias resolution failures, (ii) sunset discipline failure enabling normalisation, and (iii) a resource-blind model that allowed SoD evasion to appear conformant.

Verdict: Operation requires **targeted enforcement/remediation** for the tail and for pilot/emergency sunset hygiene; wholesale condemnation (“high deviation rate”) is rejected because a third of deviations are instrumentation (29% Notify missing due to 70% mirroring) or positively beneficial (info-request loops) or syntactic (label rename, ETL dup).

**Model validity:** **Invalid as sole normative arbiter.** M0 remains valuable as the baseline Happy Path but is obsolete/incomplete as a compliance oracle:

- missing `Request_More_Info` LOOP (acknowledged desirable),
- single-path `Sanctions||Quality -> Manual->Activate` that forbids legitimate provisional/deferred patterns governed by risk, cap and deadlines,
- no resource/CoI perspective (SoD rule unenforceable),
- no label/version indirection,
- no scope rules for intake, reactivation, withdrawal,
- no overlay governance for time-boxed pilots/emergencies,
- no data-aware predicates (risk class, jurisdiction, group membership, amount, time).

Continued use of M0 without layered companions will simultaneously under-detect real control failures (712, 715) and over-flag good practice (702, 704, 707).

### 5.3 Where each side is rejected

*Prosecution rejected:* Claim that every Auto_Approve (including 289 eligibles with timely review) is ipso noncompliant; strict calendar fetishism that treats SUP-702 as equal to SUP-703; “any extra step proves deviance” reading of Request_More_Info; and reliance on onboarding-log Notify after the service was deprecated. These collapse monitored innovation into violation and ignore compensating controls.

*Defence rejected:* “M0 obsolete therefore everything compliant”; factual persistence as substitute for written emergency extension (SUP-705); “imminent approval” as pilot extension (SUP-714); retrospective eligibility claims without snapshots; and that Sanctions-after-Activate or Ineligible-high-risk pilot use is harmless if no loss ensued (SUP-708, SUP-703). Risk-based controls cannot be judged by outcome, and aliases/disclosure gaps cannot be waved as mere data issues.

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

Layered “baseline + constraints + data checks + organisational control + scope + governance overlay” – only part belongs in executable POWL.

#### Layer 1 – Control-flow (POWL) : *what ordering is possible*
Holds sequencing, concurrency, optionality and iteration. Belongs in POWL (`StrictPartialOrder`, `OperatorPOWL`). Enforces: `Register` precedes everything; `Notify` precedes `Close` in all terminating branches; provisional versus standard paths are distinct XOR alternatives; `Request_More_Info` is repeatable. POWL *cannot* enforce risk/amount/date/actor predicates – deliberately leaves those to lower layers; otherwise model pretends to guarantee what it cannot police.

Revised POWL (see §7) contains:
- Standard route: concurrent Sanctions & Quality  Manual  Activate.
- Low-risk auto route: Sanctions  Auto_Approve  Activate  Deferred Quality (eventual before Close).
- Emergency/provisional route: Sanctions  Provisional Activate  concurrent Quality + Manual retro.
- Intakereject route: Sanctions  Reject (no Quality).
- Loop: zero-or-more Request_More_Info before decision.

All converge on NotifyClose.

#### Layer 2 – Declarative/data-aware constraints (DECLARE / log-skeleton style): *what temporal dependencies must hold*

Implemented as DECLARE templates checked alongside POWL replay:
- `Precedence(Sanctions_Check, Activate_Supplier)` globally (hard).
- `Response(Activate_Supplier, Quality_Review)` for provisional/auto paths with deadline 2 business days (SLA, not POWL-blocked; violation creates time-bound finding).
- `Response(Activate_Supplier, Manual_Procurement_Approval)` for emergency path same deadline.
- `NotCoExistence(Auto_Approve, Manual_Procurement_Approval)` per case,
- `Precedence(Auto_Approve, Activate_Supplier)`, `Precedence(Manual_Procurement_Approval, Activate_Supplier)` within respective branches (POWL already orders, but double-checked across label variants).
- `Precedence(Notify_Requester, Close_Case)`. These catch ordering irrespective of label-alias remapping.

#### Layer 3 – Data-aware eligibility/amount/time rules (pandas / SQL / CEP): *which path is permissible for which case*

Not POWL-encodable. Executed as case-attribute predicates joining event stream to policy overlay table (see §7.2):
- `risk_class == LOW && jurisdiction == DE && corporate_group_status == APPROVED  auto-route allowed`
- `emergency_designation == CRITICAL_RELIEF && flood_flag && expiry_window  provisional route + spend_cap EUR 20,000 until retro-approval`
- `amount_first_purchase`, `initial_spend` checked against caps; breach creates hard stop regardless of trace.
- `case_type` drives scope routing (intake vs onboarding vs reactivation) – see Layer 5.
- Temporal predicates compare `event_timestamp_effective` against overlay effective window; sunset automatically disables path (no silent carry-over).

These are implemented as versioned pandas checks on a normalized event table, not as POWL guards, because POWL has no data-flow semantics and baking data into control-flow causes state explosion and unsafe approximation (e.g., duplicating Activate for every cap threshold).

#### Layer 4 – Resource and CoI rules (IAM + four-eyes engine): *who may approve whom*

Separate from control-flow:
- SoD: `actor(requester)  identity(manual_approver)` resolved via global `person_id` after alias graph closure;_alias multiplicity is mastered in IAM, not in POWL; checked as post-replay identity constraint over `person_id`, not alias string.
- Delegation: any `on-behalf` approval requires `delegation_token` with grantor, validity window, and scope (risk tier) – missing token  invalid approval even if label present.
- CoI: spouse/household/shared-reporting-line relationships trigger `disclosure_required = true`; approval blocked in workflow unless `coi_disclosure_id` is linked; policy defines recusal matrix (approver  reviewer when kinship degree 2 and cost centre overlap).
- Alias hygiene: nightly alias clustering; `actor_alias` retained only as display, `person_id` is normative. This moves 712 from “invisible” to detectable.

Implementation: HR master + IAM graph + workflow hard-blocks, audited offline; PowL remains oblivious.

#### Layer 5 – Scope rules (process architecture, not POWL branching): *which cases M0 governs*

Explicit document disambiguation:
- **Intake/pre-screen:** Cases created as `case_class=intake`; mandatory `Sanctions_PreScreen`; `Quality_Review` optional; `Reject` terminal. Transition to `case_class=onboarding` only upon `Accept_Lead` (creates new onboarding case id or retypes with lineage). SUP-709 thus conforms under intake scope.
- **Onboarding:** Governed by revised POWL + Layers 2-4. Requires `Register_Supplier`.
- **Reactivation:** `case_class=reactivation` with `linked_supplier_master_id` and `prior_certification_valid_until`; `Register_Supplier` optional if supplier identity unchanged; `Quality_Review` waivable only with still-valid certification attestation within validity window and no material-change flag. SUP-710 fits after linkage.
- **Withdrawal/cancellation** (anticipated): explicit `Withdraw` sink, not `Reject`.

This stops stretching one POWL over heterogeneous lifecycles.

#### Layer 6 – Policy-overlay governance for pilots/emergencies (governance lifecycle)

Controls meta-rules:
- Every time-boxed overlay is a registered `policy_version` row with `authority_doc_id`, `effective_from/to`, `scope_predicate`, `allowed_path_id`, `compensating_controls`, `sunset_action` (expireblock). Workflow loads *currently effective* versions by `event_effective_timestamp`. No `effective` row  path unavailable – prevents SUP-705/714 style self-extension.
- Pilot requires Compliance sign-off captured as `attestation_id`; “monitored exception” is a status flag meaning *deviation tolerated & tracked*, not *compliant*; dashboards report exception burn-rate separately from compliance rate.
- Emergency invocation requires `emergency_declaration_id` referencing dated directive with explicit scope (CRITICAL_RELIEF) and cap; prolongation needs signed extension before new activations.
- All events store `policy_version_id_checked` + `eligibility_snapshot_json` at decision time; retrospective ineligibility claims impossible without contemporaneous snapshot.

Organisational control: CAB for permanent adoption; Internal Audit samples ineligible (the 14) and post-sunset tail.

Together: POWL answers “was order possible?”, DECLARE answers “did mandatory precedence hold?”, data checks answer “was this option allowed for this supplier at this time and spend?”, resource layer answers “did the right *person* act with independence and disclosure?”, scope rules answer “was this even the right process?”, and overlay governance answers “was this path under valid authority?”.

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

> POWL limitations acknowledged: POWL cannot enforce amount 20k, risk==LOW, date windows, `personperson`, label alias `QA_Clearance  Quality_Review`, or sunset authority. It also cannot attach data predicates to arcs. Duplicating `Activate_Supplier` with same label across XOR branches is an intentional approximation – log labels are identical; distinguishing context is delegated to the policy-overlay snapshot (Layer 3) rather than to label invention. `Request_More_Info` LOOP is approximated as a Kleene star (zero-or-more) via `LOOP(Silent, Request_More_Info)` preceding the decision, matching the observed 0 cycles before approval. Emergency `Activate` semantically provisional but labelled identically; temporality resolved via overlay `emergency_flag`. These are *defensible approximations* – honest about what POWL checks versus what data checks enforce.

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# Robust import of SilentTransition (pm4py version variance)
try:
    from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import SilentTransition  # pm4py >=2.7
except ImportError:
    class SilentTransition(Transition):  # fallback invisible
        def __init__(self):
            super().__init__(label=None)
            self.invisible = True

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Base common transitions
register = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")
notify   = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")
close    = Transition(label="Close_Case")
request_info = Transition(label="Request_More_Info")

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Repeatable Request_More_Info mechanism – Kleene star: zero or more
# LOOP(Silent, Request_More_Info) produces language (Request_More_Info)*
star_silent = SilentTransition()
info_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[star_silent, request_info])
# Note: info_loop will be sequenced after Register, before decision.
# Observed pattern in SUP-707 (Request after Sanctions, before Quality) is 
# approximated as pre-decision star for tractability; stricter placement
# would require unfolding Sanctions into loop body and is avoided for readability.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Branch 1 – STANDARD manual route: concurrent Sanctions || Quality -> Manual -> Activate
s1 = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
q1 = Transition(label="Quality_Review")  # covers QA_Clearance via ingest mapping
checks_std = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s1, q1])  # no edge = concurrency
m1 = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
a1 = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
std_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[checks_std, m1, a1])
std_branch.order.add_edge(checks_std, m1)
std_branch.order.add_edge(m1, a1)

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Branch 2 – LOW-RISK AUTO-APPROVAL route (PD-3):
# Sanctions before Auto_Approve before Activate; Quality deferred after Activate
# but guaranteed before Close (enforced via edge Activate -> Quality here,
# plus global Notify->Close ordering). Master-data validation is a
# data-check layer, not a POWL activity, hence not a separate visible step.
s2 = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
auto2 = Transition(label="Auto_Approve")
a2 = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
q2 = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
auto_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s2, auto2, a2, q2])
auto_branch.order.add_edge(s2, auto2)
auto_branch.order.add_edge(auto2, a2)
auto_branch.order.add_edge(a2, q2)   # deferred Quality – still before Notify/Close
auto_branch.order.add_edge(s2, q2)   # Sanctions still precedes Quality

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Branch 3 – EMERGENCY / PROVISIONAL route (FD-7):
# Sanctions -> Provisional Activate -> concurrent (Quality + Manual retro)
s3 = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
a3 = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")  # provisional semantically, same label
q3 = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
m3 = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
post_em = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[q3, m3])  # concurrent retro-review
emergency_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s3, a3, post_em])
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(s3, a3)
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(a3, post_em)

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Branch 4 – EARLY REJECTION / pre-screen (scope-controlled intake)
# Sanctions -> Reject, no Quality required. Maps to CS-9 abbreviated path.
s4 = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
reject = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
reject_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[s4, reject])
reject_branch.order.add_edge(s4, reject)

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Main XOR between the four substantive routes
main_decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[
    std_branch,
    auto_branch,
    emergency_branch,
    reject_branch
])

# ------------------------------------------------------------------
# Top-level assembly: Register -> (Request_More_Info)* -> Decision -> Notify -> Close
M_revised = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, info_loop, main_decision, notify, close])
M_revised.order.add_edge(register, info_loop)
M_revised.order.add_edge(info_loop, main_decision)
M_revised.order.add_edge(main_decision, notify)
M_revised.order.add_edge(notify, close)

# M_revised now exhibits all required behaviours:
# - concurrent Sanctions||Quality in std_branch
# - standard manual-approval route
# - low-risk auto-approval route
# - emergency/provisional route
# - repeatable Request_More_Info via LOOP approximation
# - rejection and closure and Notify_before_Close in every path
```

### Event / data-collection proposal (immutable, linkable)

Every event must emit with these normalized columns in the onboarding topic (not derived later):

**Core identity & lineage:**
- `event_id` (UUID, immutable), `case_id` (canonical lifecycle id, separate from supplier master), `supplier_master_id`, `case_class  {intake,onboarding,reactivation}`, `linked_case_id` (for reactivation/intakeonboarding trace), `event_type` (original label), `concept_activity` (mapped to POWL concept: `Quality_Review` for both `Quality_Review` and `QA_Clearance`), `original_label_preserved`, `mapping_version_id`.

**Timestamps (dual):**
- `event_time_effective` (business occurrence, e.g., approval moment) – used for policy-window evaluation, `record_time_system` (immutable ingest time) – for sequencing audits, `notification_id` (stable UUID generated by CRM at send; primary notification key, copied to onboarding log instead of mutating text link). **No mutable text case links.**

**Actor & authority:**
- `actor_alias` (as typed), `person_id_resolved` (IAM-resolved global person, nullable until resolved, audited), `delegation_token_id` (if on-behalf), `policy_version_id_checked`, `eligibility_snapshot_json` (risk, jurisdiction, group status, sanctions result at decision instant), `authority_doc_id` (for emergency/pilot invocation), `emergency_flag`, `cap_amount_at_activation`.

**Evidence artifacts:**
- `master_data_validation_id`, `monitoring_log_id` (daily check), `coi_disclosure_id`, `certification_reference_id` + `cert_valid_until`, `spend_event_ids`.

**Governance controls:**
- Append-only store with hash-chain immutability; retroactive mapping (e.g., QA_Clearance rename) creates new `mapping_version` with `effective_from` but does not mutate prior event rows; conformance replay accepts mapping by concept lookup with version awareness.

This restores auditability: SUP-702’s notification becomes provable via `notification_id`; SUP-706’s weakness is exposed as missing stable id; SUP-711 deduplicates via `source_transaction_id`; SUP-712/715 resolve via `person_id` and `coi_disclosure_id`; pilot/emergency windows are system-enforced rather than operator-remembered; and scope is explicit so rejected-lead quality omission (SUP-709) and reactivation certificate reuse (SUP-710) no longer pollute deviation metrics.
