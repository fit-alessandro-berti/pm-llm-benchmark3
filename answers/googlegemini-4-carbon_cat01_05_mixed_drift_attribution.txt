## 2. Raw-versus-normalized interpretation

The divergence between `raw_model_fitness` (collapsing from 0.92–0.93 in Jan–Feb to 0.55 in March) and `mapped_model_fitness` (holding at 0.90–0.91 in Mar–Apr) demonstrates that the March conformance shock is overwhelmingly a semantic and provenance artifact rather than an operational breakdown. Mapping `Eligibility Screen` back to `Validate Eligibility` and deduplicating technical `Open Case` events restores ~90–95% of lost token/alignment fitness. However, this gap does *not* prove the business process remained completely unchanged: `mapped_model_fitness` still drops slightly (0.925 to 0.905), and label harmonization only establishes control-flow equivalence at a coarse activity level. It cannot rule out altered screen validation logic, changed exception thresholds, or new micro-steps inside the migrated system.

Meanwhile, technical event duplication and missing resource identifiers corrupt distinct downstream analyses. Duplicated `Open Case` events distort trace-length statistics, variant counts, start-activity distributions, and raw DFG edge frequencies (`Open Case -> Register` appearing 26 times in March). Non-random `missing_resource_pct` (jumping from ~1% to 4.8–6.5% on migrated steps) leaves unattributed control-flow models intact, but biases social-network discovery, workload-per-FTE calculations, handover-of-work metrics, and team-level queueing attribution.

Crucially, post-May patterns cannot be dismissed as logging drift. After the March migration stabilized, no new activity labels appeared (`distinct_activity_labels` stayed constant at 11 from March through December) and `duplicate_open_case_pct` remained flat (4–6%). Yet starting in May, operational tables and normalized DFGs simultaneously show `rework_variant_pct` doubling (12% to 24–29%), `Assess -> RequestInfo` jumping from 12 to 24–29 per 100 cases, `Assess -> SeniorAssessment` surging to 22 in October, `mapped_model_fitness` eroding to 0.86, and median/p90 cycle times expanding by >50% (+17.8h median, +53h p90 by November). End-to-end timestamps and rework loops reflect genuine execution delays and extra customer interactions, not naming conventions.

### 3. Cycle-time deterioration decomposition
Comparing baseline (`median_cycle_h` ~31.2h, `p90` ~68h) against the summer staffing/supplier trough (Jul–Aug: median ~44.6h [+13.4h], p90 ~107h [+39h]) and the autumn renewal peak (Oct–Nov: median ~48.4h [+17.2h], p90 ~118.5h [+50.5h]):
- **Service time (~5–10% of deterioration; ~0.5–1.5h median impact)**: Recorded median `Assess` service time grew only ~0.6h (4.8h to 5.4h) by Jul–Aug. Even allowing for modest slowdowns in `Collect Documents` or `Senior Assessment` active work, pure task execution duration explains very little of the +13h to +18h median shift.
- **Queueing (~45–60% in Jul–Aug; ~40–55% in Oct–Nov)**: Additional observations confirm reconstructed queue wait before `Assess` increased substantially. In June–August, rework stabilized (27–29%) while median cycle time rose another +4.2h (40.8h -> 45.0h) and `p90` surged +15h (95h -> 110h), directly reflecting the June 15 loss of 4 assessors and July 1 priority rule (which starves standard cases, inflating `p90`). Queueing is nonlinear: higher arrivals (Oct–Nov) + rework re-entries multiply utilization $\rho$, driving $\frac{\rho}{1-\rho}$ waiting explosions.
- **Control-flow / rework (~25–35% in May–Aug; ~20–30% in Oct–Nov)**: In May (before staffing cuts), a +12.5 pp jump in `rework_variant_pct` raised median cycle time by +4.5h (32.0h -> 36.5h) and `p90` by +13h. Rework adds extra `Request Info` + customer wait + `Collect Documents` + second `Assess` pass (and also feeds back into queueing by consuming assessor capacity).
- **Case mix / population (~5–15% in May–Aug; ~20–35% in Oct–Nov)**: Through August, `high_complexity_pct` rose modestly (+8.5 pp), though Supplier Z cases were also inherently more complex. In Oct–Nov, `high_complexity_pct` surged +20.5 to +24.5 pp, triggering `Senior Assessment` (+18 per 100 cases) and longer inherent handling paths—though priority queueing shielded high-complexity cases from part of the wait, shifting delay onto standard cases.

*(Note: Because medians and p90s are non-additive across heterogeneous mixtures and rework directly amplifies utilization-driven queues, exact additive shares cannot be uniquely identified from monthly aggregates; interaction effects account for significant shared variance.)*

### 4. Candidate change points by drift type
- **Logging / semantic drift**:
  1. **2026-03** (Primary): Sudden drop in `raw_model_fitness` (-0.38), jump in `distinct_activity_labels` (9 -> 11), `missing_resource_pct` (1.0% -> 4.8%), and `duplicate_open_case_pct` (0% -> 4%).
  2. **2026-10** (Secondary candidate): Sudden jump in `Assess -> SeniorAssessment` (7 -> 22 per 100 cases); while aligned with seasonal complexity, the prompt notes `Senior Assessment` was "historically recorded inconsistently," making partial logging-practice standardization a plausible secondary logging shift.
- **Behavioral / control-flow drift**:
  1. **2026-05**: Sudden doubling of `rework_variant_pct` (13% -> 24%) and `Assess -> RequestInfo` (12 -> 24), normalized DFG $L_1$ jumping from 3 to 39.
  2. **2026-10** (and partial reversal in **2026-09**): Rework recedes in September ($L_1$ drops 59 -> 26), followed by a new structural regime in October where `Assess -> SeniorAssessment` triples (7 -> 22) and rework rebounds (`rework_variant_pct` 20% -> 26–29%, $L_1 = 64$).
- **Performance drift**:
  1. **2026-05 / 2026-06**: Onset of sustained cycle-time expansion (`median_cycle_h` breaks out from 30.8–32.0h band to 36.5h in May and 40.8h in June; `p90` jumps 71h -> 95h).
  2. **2026-07 / 2026-10**: July marks accelerated tail divergence (`p90` jumps to 104–110h after staffing loss + priority queueing despite flat rework), and October marks a second step-up to the annual performance worst (`median` 47.8–49.0h, `p90` 116–121h).

### 5. Global vs. mechanism-specific change points
The data decisively **reject a single global change point** and instead support **staggered, mechanism-specific change points**. In March, logging metrics shift violently while operational cycle times and normalized DFGs remain stationary ($L_1 = 3$, median = 31.2h). In May, control-flow rework and Supplier Z share jump while label cardinality and duplicate-event rates remain flat. Between June and August, cycle-time tails (`p90` +26h from May to Aug) continue rising even after rework and Supplier Z shares plateau or begin declining, pointing to June/July capacity and queueing shifts. Finally, October introduces volume, complexity, and `Senior Assessment` spikes distinct from Supplier Z (`supplier_z_pct` stays 26–27% vs. 43–46% in summer). Fitting a single global change-point detector across multivariate features would blur March, May, June/July, and October into a meaningless composite boundary.

| **L3: Organizational / Resource Strata** | `Assessment Team` or `Assessor Cohort` (Tenured survivors vs. Leavers vs. Overtime pool) **restricted to `Assess` / `Senior Assessment` events** | Distinguish capacity queueing from individual behavioral adaptation and audit resource missingness. | $\ge 30$ `Assess` executions per team/month (and missing resource rate $<5\%$ on `Assess`). | Within-assessor service speed, `Request Info` propensity, and handover bottlenecks. | **Moderate-to-High**: Priority rules and complexity routing assign non-random cases to senior/specific assessors; migrated upstream tasks (`Eligibility Screen`) must be excluded due to non-random missing IDs. |

**Primary monitoring population and rationale**: **Level 1 (`Complexity Tier at Intake × Supplier Group`)**—with **Standard Complexity × Non-Supplier Z** serving as the cleanest "canary" reference cell (largest stable cell: ~300–390 cases/month in Jan–Apr, ~200–320 in May–Dec)—should be treated as the primary monitoring segmentation. Both variables are determined at case arrival (exogenous to internal processing), directly align with the known priority-queue split (High vs. Standard) and external bundle risk (Supplier Z vs. Others), avoid endogenous mediator conditioning (unlike Rework), avoid migrated-step resource missingness, and maintain sufficient monthly cell counts across almost the entire year (requiring 60-day pooling only for the `High Complexity × Supplier Z` cell during low-Supplier-Z winter months).

### Handling seasonality and multiple testing
1. **Seasonality**: Static year-round thresholds would fire relentlessly every October–November when volume (+25–30%) and `high_complexity_pct` (41–45%) naturally surge. We handle seasonality in two layers:
   - *Covariate conditioning (L1 stratification)*: Monitors M3–M7 and M11 run *inside* `Complexity × Supplier` strata, automatically stripping out first-order composition shifts (e.g., higher `Senior Assessment` simply because more cases are High Complexity).
   - *Seasonal baselines & load-adjusted expectations*: Intake monitor M8 uses a harmonic / calendar-month seasonal index (comparing Oct 2026 against historical Oct renewal profiles rather than Aug 2026). Queueing monitor M6 separates *expected seasonal congestion* (given seasonal arrivals and planned staffing) from *excess unexpected queue drift*, while still maintaining hard business SLA breach alerts so real seasonal understaffing is acted upon operationally rather than normalized away.
2. **Multiple testing**: Running 11 monitors across 4 L1 strata bi-weekly creates ~30–45 concurrent hypothesis tests per cycle, guaranteeing false alarms under naive $\alpha = 0.05$. We organize alerts hierarchically into three families—(A) *Data/Logging Integrity* (M1, M2, M9), (B) *Intake/Population* (M8), and (C) *Behavioral, Performance & Outcomes* (M3–M7, M10, M11)—applying **gatekeeping** (suppressing or flagging Family C alerts as suspect if Family A fails schema/timestamp checks), **Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control ($q \le 0.02$)** within Family C across L1 strata, **minimum practical effect-size floors** paired with p-values, and **two-consecutive-window persistence** rules for gradual performance shifts.

def run_part_a_aggregate_diagnostics() -> Tuple[pd.DataFrame, pd.DataFrame]:
    df_sum = pd.read_csv(io.StringIO(MONTHLY_CSV.strip()))
    df_dfg = pd.read_csv(io.StringIO(NORM_DFG_CSV.strip()))

    # 1. Validate embedded data
    assert len(df_sum) == 12, "Expected 12 monthly summary rows."
    assert not df_sum.isna().any().any(), "Unexpected NaNs in monthly summary."
    pct_cols = [c for c in df_sum.columns if c.endswith("_pct")]
    assert ((df_sum[pct_cols] >= 0) & (df_sum[pct_cols] <= 100)).all().all(), "Invalid percentages."
    assert ((df_sum[["raw_model_fitness", "mapped_model_fitness"]] >= 0) &
            (df_sum[["raw_model_fitness", "mapped_model_fitness"]] <= 1)).all().all(), "Fitness out of [0, 1]."
    assert (df_sum["p90_cycle_h"] >= df_sum["median_cycle_h"]).all(), "p90 must be >= median."
    assert set(df_dfg["month"]).issubset(set(df_sum["month"])), "DFG months must align with summary."

    # 2. Baseline-relative changes (Baseline = Jan-Feb 2026 mean)
    base_mask = df_sum["month"].isin(["2026-01", "2026-02"])
    baseline = df_sum.loc[base_mask].mean(numeric_only=True)

    metrics = [
        "cases", "high_complexity_pct", "supplier_z_pct", "rework_variant_pct",
        "median_cycle_h", "p90_cycle_h", "raw_model_fitness", "mapped_model_fitness",
        "missing_resource_pct", "duplicate_open_case_pct"
    ]
    deltas = df_sum[["month"]].copy()
    for m in metrics:
        deltas[f"delta_{m}"] = (df_sum[m] - baseline[m]).round(3)
    deltas["pct_change_median_h"] = ((df_sum["median_cycle_h"] / baseline["median_cycle_h"] - 1.0) * 100).round(1)
    deltas["pct_change_p90_h"] = ((df_sum["p90_cycle_h"] / baseline["p90_cycle_h"] - 1.0) * 100).round(1)

    # 3. Transparent distances between monthly normalized DFG fragments (L1, L2, Linf)
    edge_cols = [c for c in df_dfg.columns if c != "month"]
    base_vec = df_dfg.loc[df_dfg["month"] == "2026-01", edge_cols].iloc[0].to_numpy(dtype=float)

    dfg_dist = df_dfg[["month"]].copy()
    mat = df_dfg[edge_cols].to_numpy(dtype=float)
    diff_vs_jan = np.abs(mat - base_vec)
    dfg_dist["L1_vs_2026_01"] = diff_vs_jan.sum(axis=1)
    dfg_dist["L2_vs_2026_01"] = np.sqrt((diff_vs_jan ** 2).sum(axis=1)).round(2)
    dfg_dist["Linf_vs_2026_01"] = diff_vs_jan.max(axis=1)

    step_diff = np.vstack([np.zeros((1, len(edge_cols))), np.abs(np.diff(mat, axis=0))])
    dfg_dist["L1_vs_prev_supplied"] = step_diff.sum(axis=1)

    # 4. Raw-to-mapped fitness recovery
    df_sum["fitness_gap"] = (df_sum["mapped_model_fitness"] - df_sum["raw_model_fitness"]).round(3)
    raw_loss = baseline["raw_model_fitness"] - df_sum["raw_model_fitness"]
    df_sum["fitness_loss_recovered_pct"] = np.where(
        raw_loss > 0.05,
        ((df_sum["mapped_model_fitness"] - df_sum["raw_model_fitness"]) / raw_loss * 100).round(1),
        np.nan
    )

    # 5. Simple, explainable mechanism-specific change-point flags (first-difference thresholds)
    # Rule: flag month t if |x_t - x_{t-1}| exceeds transparent domain-specific step thresholds
    cp = pd.DataFrame({"month": df_sum["month"]})
    cp["d_raw_fit"] = df_sum["raw_model_fitness"].diff().round(3)
    cp["d_labels"] = df_sum["distinct_activity_labels"].diff()
    cp["d_rework_pp"] = df_sum["rework_variant_pct"].diff()
    cp["d_high_comp_pp"] = df_sum["high_complexity_pct"].diff()
    cp["d_median_h"] = df_sum["median_cycle_h"].diff().round(2)
    cp["d_p90_h"] = df_sum["p90_cycle_h"].diff().round(2)

    cp["flag_logging_cp"] = (cp["d_raw_fit"].abs() >= 0.15) | (cp["d_labels"].abs() >= 1)
    cp["flag_behavioral_cp"] = cp["d_rework_pp"].abs() >= 6.0
    cp["flag_population_cp"] = (cp["d_high_comp_pp"].abs() >= 10.0) | (df_sum["supplier_z_pct"].diff().abs() >= 12.0)
    cp["flag_performance_cp"] = (cp["d_median_h"].abs() >= 4.0) | (cp["d_p90_h"].abs() >= 10.0)

    print("=== BASELINE-RELATIVE DELTAS (VS JAN-FEB MEAN) ===")
    print(deltas[["month", "delta_supplier_z_pct", "delta_high_complexity_pct",
                  "delta_rework_variant_pct", "delta_median_cycle_h", "delta_p90_cycle_h",
                  "delta_raw_model_fitness", "delta_mapped_model_fitness"]].to_string(index=False))

    print("\n=== NORMALIZED DFG FRAGMENT DISTANCES ===")
    print(dfg_dist.to_string(index=False))

    print("\n=== RAW-TO-MAPPED FITNESS RECOVERY & CANDIDATE CHANGE POINTS ===")
    merged_report = df_sum[["month", "raw_model_fitness", "mapped_model_fitness",
                            "fitness_gap", "fitness_loss_recovered_pct"]].merge(
        cp[["month", "flag_logging_cp", "flag_behavioral_cp", "flag_population_cp", "flag_performance_cp"]],
        on="month"
    )
    print(merged_report.to_string(index=False))

    # 6. Caveats about omitted edges and aggregate data
    print("\n=== METHODOLOGICAL CAVEATS ===")
    print("1. Omitted DFG edges: Supplied normalized DFG rates exclude loop re-entry details, Decision->Close,")
    print("   early drop-offs/rejections, and non-conformant skips; computed L1 distances are strict LOWER BOUNDS.")
    print("2. Ecological / aggregation limits: Monthly medians and p90s are non-additive across Supplier Z and")
    print("   Priority strata; aggregate cycle times mask opposite-signed priority effects after 2026-07-01.")
    print("3. Operational-table mismatch: Variant shares came from operational tables, whereas mapped_model_fitness")
    print("   used normalized event logs; cohort assignment (arrival vs completion month) may smear step boundaries.")
    return deltas, dfg_dist

try:
    import pm4py
except ImportError:

def prepare_pm4py_dataframe(
    raw_df: pd.DataFrame,
    case_col: str = "case_id",
    activity_col: str = "activity",
    timestamp_col: str = "timestamp",
    timest_format: Optional[str] = None,
) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """1. Formats a copy of the raw dataframe to PM4Py standard columns without mutating input."""
    if pm4py is None:
        raise ImportError("pm4py is required for Part B event-level analysis.")
    df_copy = raw_df.copy(deep=True)
    return pm4py.format_dataframe(
        df_copy,
        case_id=case_col,
        activity_key=activity_col,
        timestamp_key=timestamp_col,
        timest_format=timest_format,
    )


def slice_event_window(
    formatted_df: pd.DataFrame,
    dt_start: str,
    dt_end: str,
    mode: str = "traces_contained",
) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """
    2. Slices time windows with pm4py.filter_time_range(...).
    Supported modes in PM4Py include 'events', 'traces_contained', and 'traces_intersecting'.
    Using 'traces_intersecting' or arrival-cohort filtering avoids right-censoring bias vs 'traces_contained'.
    """
    return pm4py.filter_time_range(
        formatted_df,
        dt1=dt_start,
        dt2=dt_end,
        mode=mode,
        case_id_key="case:concept:name",
        timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    )


def normalize_labels_and_deduplicate(
    formatted_df: pd.DataFrame,
    label_map: Optional[Dict[str, str]] = None,
    dedup_technical_activity: str = "Open Case",
) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """
    Helper for 7: Applies semantic label mapping and removes duplicate technical Open Case events
    per case on a deep copy without overwriting the original dataframe.
    """
    if label_map is None:
        label_map = {"Eligibility Screen": "Validate Eligibility"}

    norm_df = formatted_df.copy(deep=True)
    norm_df["concept:name"] = norm_df["concept:name"].replace(label_map)
    norm_df = norm_df.sort_values(["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp"], kind="mergesort")

    is_tech = norm_df["concept:name"] == dedup_technical_activity
    dup_tech = is_tech & norm_df.duplicated(subset=["case:concept:name", "concept:name"], keep="first")
    return norm_df.loc[~dup_tech].reset_index(drop=True)


def compute_monthly_process_artifacts(
    formatted_df: pd.DataFrame,
    month_windows: Dict[str, Tuple[str, str]],
    window_mode: str = "traces_intersecting",
    include_declarative: bool = False,
) -> Dict[str, Dict[str, Any]]:
    """
    3-6. Computes monthly DFGs, variants, temporal profiles, and optional log skeletons / DECLARE models.
    """
    monthly_results: Dict[str, Dict[str, Any]] = {}

    for month_label, (dt1, dt2) in month_windows.items():
        sub_df = slice_event_window(formatted_df, dt1, dt2, mode=window_mode)
        if sub_df.empty:
            continue

        # 3. Discover frequency DFG (returns (dfg_dict, start_activities, end_activities))
        dfg_freq, start_acts, end_acts = pm4py.discover_dfg(
            sub_df,
            activity_key="concept:name",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
            case_id_key="case:concept:name",
        )

        # 4. Compute trace variants
        variants = pm4py.get_variants(
            sub_df,
            activity_key="concept:name",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
            case_id_key="case:concept:name",
        )

        # 5. Discover pairwise temporal profile
        temporal_profile = pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(
            sub_df,
            activity_key="concept:name",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
            case_id_key="case:concept:name",
        )

        artifacts: Dict[str, Any] = {
            "n_cases": int(sub_df["case:concept:name"].nunique()),
            "dfg": dfg_freq,
            "start_activities": start_acts,
            "end_activities": end_acts,
            "variants": variants,
            "temporal_profile": temporal_profile,
        }

        # 6. Optional declarative / constraint discovery (Log Skeleton & DECLARE)
        if include_declarative:
            artifacts["log_skeleton"] = pm4py.discover_log_skeleton(
                sub_df,
                noise_threshold=0.05,
                activity_key="concept:name",
                timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
                case_id_key="case:concept:name",
            )
            if hasattr(pm4py, "discover_declare"):
                artifacts["declare_model"] = pm4py.discover_declare(
                    sub_df,
                    activity_key="concept:name",
                    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
                    case_id_key="case:concept:name",
                )

        monthly_results[month_label] = artifacts

    return monthly_results


def compare_raw_vs_normalized_logs(
    raw_formatted_df: pd.DataFrame,
    month_windows: Dict[str, Tuple[str, str]],
    label_map: Optional[Dict[str, str]] = None,
    normative_net_im_fm: Optional[Tuple[Any, Any, Any]] = None,
) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """
    7. Compares raw and normalized label mappings without overwriting raw_formatted_df.
    Optionally evaluates token-based replay / alignment fitness if a normative Petri net is supplied.
    """
    norm_formatted_df = normalize_labels_and_deduplicate(raw_formatted_df, label_map=label_map)

    raw_artifacts = compute_monthly_process_artifacts(raw_formatted_df, month_windows)
    norm_artifacts = compute_monthly_process_artifacts(norm_formatted_df, month_windows)

    rows = []
    for month_label in sorted(raw_artifacts.keys()):
        dt1, dt2 = month_windows[month_label]
        raw_sub = slice_event_window(raw_formatted_df, dt1, dt2, mode="traces_intersecting")
        norm_sub = slice_event_window(norm_formatted_df, dt1, dt2, mode="traces_intersecting")

        row: Dict[str, Any] = {
            "month": month_label,
            "n_cases": raw_artifacts[month_label]["n_cases"],
            "raw_distinct_labels": int(raw_sub["concept:name"].nunique()),
            "norm_distinct_labels": int(norm_sub["concept:name"].nunique()),
            "raw_variant_count": len(raw_artifacts[month_label]["variants"]),
            "norm_variant_count": len(norm_artifacts[month_label]["variants"]),
            "events_removed_dedup": len(raw_sub) - len(norm_sub),
        }

        if normative_net_im_fm is not None:
            net, im, fm = normative_net_im_fm
            row["raw_fitness"] = pm4py.fitness_alignments(raw_sub, net, im, fm)["averageFitness"]
            row["mapped_fitness"] = pm4py.fitness_alignments(norm_sub, net, im, fm)["averageFitness"]

        rows.append(row)

    return pd.DataFrame(rows)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    run_part_a_aggregate_diagnostics()
