# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Would increase concern**

- Authenticated vendor-master and payment-approval logs show the same human changed the effective beneficiary account and approved payment, or separate users coordinated to bypass independent verification. A released payment to an unauthorized account, confirmed against vendor records and settlement data, would make the exposure concrete.
- The account used at release differs from the version independently approved. Version history shows the bank record changed after approval and the payment proceeded without renewed approval.
- Original timestamps reproduce the short bank-change intervals and show required verification was skipped. Assignment logs reveal repeated discretionary rerouting to the same pair after accounting for eligibility, supplier assignments, and migration work. Communications coordinating an override of controls or verified links between a beneficiary and an involved party would strengthen concern about intent.
- Signed records confirm that an override issuer also justified the override, that the justification was first completed after the three-working-day deadline, or that activity logs show repeated retrospective edits to emergency flags without contemporaneous incident support. These findings would substantiate control failures.

**Would reduce concern**

- Migration tickets, upload manifests, independently confirmed vendor instructions, and bank settlement records reconcile the exact V-77x account changes and subsequent payments. Effective-dated access logs verify independent actors, valid approval authority, and approval of the version actually paid.
- Source records explain compressed intervals through batch posting, distinguish Bot_PO from the approver, or identify distinct humans behind shared-account events. The nine bank-change flags warrant resolution against each case's original audit trail.
- On-call schedules, incident records, delegation limits, and signed justifications support authorized emergency processing, independent PO approval, and independent retrospective review within three working days.
- Within comparable workloads and eligible staffing pools, route frequencies and timing are consistent with assignment rules, with documented corrections and fresh review where material details changed.

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

The procurement-to-payment log shows recurring buyer–approver pairs, rapid approvals, and apparent segregation-of-duties exceptions. The routine Aline/Elena and Boris/Felix patterns are consistent with stable team assignments. The concentration of urgent medical purchases and weekend repairs in the Carla/Gita and Dan/Hugo cohorts is consistent with specialization and emergency handling. The reported delays in retrospective justification warrant review because independent justification within three working days is a condition of the emergency override.

The V-77x bank-detail change and payment-approval cases are the highest priority. All nine apparent same-resource exceptions for those activities involve that vendor family. Separately, all 16 cases in the fast Lena/Kim subgroup were manually uploaded, with a median interval of 17 minutes. The vendor's banking-platform migration provides a plausible operational explanation, and the log lacks links to the relevant maintenance tickets. The overlap between the two flagged populations requires reconciliation.

Shared accounts, spreadsheet batch entry, and a legacy mapping error can distort the recorded actor or timestamp. A confirmed same-human match across a prohibited activity pair or an overdue required justification would establish a control exception. A finding of collusion would require corroborating evidence of coordinated circumvention.

Audit should reconcile the flagged V-77x transactions to approved maintenance tickets, independent vendor verification, authenticated decision records, and the beneficiary accounts actually paid. It should also verify individual PO approvers and obtain the original signed emergency justifications to test authorship and timeliness. The results should determine whether the cases require control remediation or further investigation.
