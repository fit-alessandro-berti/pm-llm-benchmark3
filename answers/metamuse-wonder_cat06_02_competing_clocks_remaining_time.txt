## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Calendar anchor

- Chemistry closes Friday at 18:00, only 2.5 h after the forecast time, and does not reopen until Monday 08:00.
- Microbiology incubation runs continuously: started Friday 14:00, so technical readiness is **Sunday 20 Sep 12:00-20:00**.
- Without weekend authorization, readout waits until Monday 08:00. With authorization, Sunday readout can be recorded roughly Sunday afternoon/evening.
- QA works Friday until 20:00, Saturday 09:00-13:00, and from Monday 08:00 with two reviewers. No Sunday QA work means even a Sunday microbiology result cannot produce a Sunday release.
- The join time is  
  **max(chemistry usable-result time, microbiology usable-result time)**,  
  not their sum. Subsequent QA and final steps are then added.

### S1 — Smooth path

- **Chemistry waiting and service:**  
  Under FIFO, B-417 is behind four samples and almost certainly receives little or no Friday service. Friday analyst time first clears work already in progress and then begins queued samples; unfinished queued work pauses over the weekend. On Monday, B-417 plausibly starts late morning to early afternoon and completes mid-afternoon to early evening.  
  Under risk-priority, it likely starts Friday around 16:40-17:40, completes 0.2-1.3 h of testing before closing, resumes Monday 08:00, and finishes roughly 09:00-12:00.  
  Combining the 55/45 discipline uncertainty gives a defensible Monday chemistry range of approximately **09:30-17:00**, with FIFO-heavy outcomes later.
- **Microbiology incubation and readout:** Technically ready Sunday 12:00-20:00. If on-call is authorized, usable by Sunday about 14:00-23:00. Otherwise, readout Monday 08:00-09:30, allowing for recording and modest Monday congestion.
- **Parallel join:** Usually Monday **10:00-17:00**. Microbiology sets a Sunday/Monday floor, while chemistry's Friday queue and weekend pause often determine the actual Monday join. If chemistry is priority-processed, microbiology readout and chemistry may finish close together.
- **QA waiting and service:** QA cannot begin at the join instantly unless a reviewer and queue position are available. Expected wait is 1-6 working hours. Service has median 1.5 h and an 80th percentile around 3.5 h. A Monday-afternoon start may be interrupted by the 20:00 close and resume Tuesday.
- **Optional rework/investigation:** None by definition.
- **Final release:** Add 0.25-0.75 h, continuously available. Central terminal time is therefore **Monday evening-Tuesday midday**, approximately **74-93 h remaining**. The absolute early edge is Monday late morning if chemistry received priority, QA is empty, and review is short.

### S2 — Chemistry rerun

- **Chemistry waiting and service:** Initial chemistry follows S1 until a borderline result is declared. That declaration itself may not occur until Monday, because the initial test is often incomplete before Friday closing.
- **Rerun:** Triangular 1.0-3.0 h, priority service. If the borderline is found Monday morning and the rerun begins promptly, completion can occur Monday afternoon. If found late Monday, only part can run before 18:00 and the remainder resumes Tuesday 08:00.
- **Microbiology:** Usually ready Monday 08:30-09:30 without weekend authorization, so it is already waiting at the revised join.
- **Parallel join:** `max(rerun completion, microbiology)` is normally the rerun time: Monday late afternoon to Tuesday late morning.
- **QA waiting and service:** Starts Tuesday in many cases. Allow 1-6 working hours of waiting plus 1.5-3.5 h for a typical-to-adverse review. A rerun also raises deviation probability, but that escalation belongs to S4.
- **Final step:** Release 0.25-0.75 h after acceptance. Central terminal time **Tuesday morning-Wednesday morning**, approximately **90-114 h remaining**. A documentation query or deviation moves this to S4 timing.

### S3 — Microbiology delay or confirmation

- **Chemistry:** Usually follows the Monday S1 completion profile. In a no-authorization weekend, chemistry and microbiology converge Monday; neither should simply be added to the other.
- **Primary readout delay:** Incubation ends Sunday, but without authorization the usable result is Monday 08:30-09:30. This delays the earliest possible join, although chemistry spillover often absorbs part or all of the delay.
- **Contamination confirmation:** If the first readout indicates contamination, add 20-30 continuous hours plus 0.5-1.5 h readout. A Sunday first readout could produce confirmation Monday afternoon-Tuesday. A Monday first readout produces technical readiness Tuesday morning-early Wednesday; if readiness falls outside staffing hours, readout slips to the next microbiology working period.
- **Parallel join:** For ordinary delay, Monday late morning-afternoon. For confirmation, usually **Tuesday-Wednesday**.
- **QA:** Add 1-6 working hours of waiting and median-to-P80 service of 1.5-3.5 working hours. Starting Wednesday can interact with the 20:00 close.
- **Final step:** Central contamination-path terminal time **Tuesday afternoon-Wednesday afternoon**, approximately **96-122 h remaining**, before any deviation escalation.

### S4 — QA query or investigation tail

- **Branch stages and join:** Any of the branch scenarios above can feed this path. The join itself is unchanged, but an abnormal branch result increases the investigation probability from the normal 0.04-0.10 range to roughly 0.30-0.55.
- **Initial QA waiting and service:** QA must first open and examine the batch: approximately 1-6 h waiting plus part or all of the 1.5-3.5 h typical review before a query/investigation is raised.
- **Documentation Query:** Add 4-12 **working** hours while information is supplied. If raised Tuesday afternoon, resumption may be Wednesday; if raised Friday afternoon in a slower branch path, the weekend intervenes. QA then needs residual review time.
- **Documentation-only deviation:** Add 8-20 working hours. At roughly 12 QA hours on a full weekday, this is about 1-2 working days, but weekend boundaries can turn it into 3-4 calendar days.
- **Laboratory deviation:** Add 3-7 calendar days, followed by QA reassessment. This creates the severe calendar tail and is also the main route to a confirmed critical defect and rejection.
- **Final step:** Release takes 0.25-0.75 h after acceptance. Rejection recording takes 0.5-1.0 h after a confirmed critical defect. This difference is negligible relative to investigation time.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **QA/investigation path after the join — mainly path uncertainty.**  
   A documentation query adds working hours, while a laboratory deviation adds days. Conditional investigation probability rises sharply after any branch anomaly.

2. **Temperature-excursion status and induced dependence — mainly dependence and observation uncertainty.**  
   The sensor gap leaves a 0.20-0.40 chance of confirmation. Confirmation makes chemistry rerun, microbiology contamination, and deviation investigation cluster together, fattening the joint tail.

3. **Chemistry queue discipline and Monday spillover — mainly queue uncertainty.**  
   FIFO versus risk-priority changes Friday starts and can move the join by several working hours. Because the laboratory closes Friday 18:00, small Friday differences become a weekend calendar difference.

4. **Microbiology staffing and confirmation — mainly calendar uncertainty, with a path-uncertainty component.**  
   Weekend authorization determines whether Sunday readiness becomes a Sunday result. Contamination adds another continuous incubation cycle and another staffed readout.

5. **QA queue at the future join — mainly queue uncertainty.**  
   The current five-batch queue is not the relevant queue. Arrivals during Friday evening, Saturday, and Monday, combined with overdispersion and a heavy-tailed review time, make waiting at the actual join difficult to predict.

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

The single most forecast-changing observation would be **definitive resolution of the upstream temperature-excursion flag**, ideally before or at the microbiology readout.

- **If the excursion is ruled out:** rerun and contamination probabilities fall toward their lower baseline ranges, the probability of a deviation investigation also falls, and the forecast should shift toward the smooth-path range—Monday evening to Tuesday midday—with a materially narrower P80/P90 tail.
- **If the excursion is confirmed:** the forecast should not merely add independent rerun and contamination allowances. It should shift probability mass toward joint chemistry/microbiology anomalies, contamination confirmation, and a deviation investigation. The P50 could move by roughly half a day to a day, while P80/P90 could move by several days.

A secondary update trigger is **whether B-417 actually starts chemistry service before Friday 18:00, and how much work is completed**. Starting under risk-priority supports a Monday-morning join; no Friday start strongly supports FIFO-like spillover and a Monday-afternoon or later join.
