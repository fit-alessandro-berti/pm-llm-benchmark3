# 4. Risks invisible in average cycle time

1. **Tail latency explosion**:
   - High-complexity cases may face **multi-day queues** in Technical Shared Service, masked by stable median cycle time.
   - *Detection*: 90th/95th percentile cycle time by `case_complexity` in `discover_performance_dfg`.

2. **Local knowledge loss**:
   - Early-stage informal advice (preventing rework) disappears, but rework only appears later in the process.
   - *Detection*: Correlation between `local_expertise_flag=False` and `rework_flag=True`.

3. **Fairness degradation**:
   - Regional teams (North/South) may see **disproportionate delays** for cases requiring local context, while East improves.
   - *Detection*: Cycle time variance by `team`/`location` in `extract_temporal_features_dataframe`.

4. **Learning atrophy**:
   - Local teams stop developing technical skills, reducing long-term resilience.
   - *Detection*: Decline in `activity_based_resource_similarity` between local caseworkers and specialists.

5. **Accountability dilution**:
   - Shared approval queue obscures ownership, leading to **stalled cases** with no clear resolver.
   - *Detection*: Increase in `reassignment_count` for cases in shared approval queue.

6. **Exception handling failures**:
   - Shared services may lack flexibility for **edge cases** (e.g., urgent customer segments), increasing hidden rework.
   - *Detection*: `handover_reason="exception"` frequency + `rework_flag` correlation.

# 5. Pilot design

### **Pilot Scope**
- **Teams**: East (highest current specialist utilization) + **50% of North’s cases** (control group: remaining North cases).
- **Process Steps**: Only **Technical Shared Service** and **Legal/Risk centralization** (keep local supervisors for now).
- **Customer Segments**: Exclude high-priority segments initially.

### **Comparison Group**
- **North (non-pilot)**: Continue with embedded specialists.
- **South/East (pre-pilot)**: Historical baseline.

### **Observation Window**
- **Pre-pilot**: 3 months of baseline data.
- **Pilot**: 4 months (allowing for queue stabilization).
- **Post-pilot**: 2 months of washout period.

### **Segmentation**
- By `team` (East vs. North-pilot vs. North-control).
- By `case_complexity` (low/medium/high).
- By `required_specialty` (technical/legal/risk/none).
- By `customer_segment` (regional vs. national).

### **Guardrail Metrics**
| **Metric**                          | **Threshold**                          | **Action if Breached**                     |
|-------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| 90th percentile cycle time          | >20% increase vs. baseline             | Pause pilot; investigate shared queues     |
| Rework rate for high-complexity     | >15%                                   | Roll back Technical Shared Service        |
| Shared queue wait time (median)     | >6h for Technical Shared Service       | Add temporary local specialist capacity    |
| SLA miss rate (North-pilot)         | >10%                                   | Revert North to embedded specialists       |
| `reassignment_count` >2 per case     | >10% of cases                          | Audit handover reasons                     |

### **Staff Feedback Collection**
- **Bi-weekly surveys**: Role clarity, perceived fairness, workload.
- **Focus groups**: Monthly with caseworkers and specialists to discuss pain points.
- **Shadowing**: Observe 5 cases/week in shared queues for qualitative insights.

### **Rollback/Adjustment Triggers**
- **Immediate rollback**: If 90th percentile cycle time **doubles** for any segment.
- **Adjustment**: If shared queue wait times exceed 8h, **add a local specialist buffer** for East.
- **Pilot expansion**: If East shows **>15% cycle time improvement** and North-pilot rework rate ** baseline +2%**, expand to South.

---

# 6. Recommendation

**Conditional Proceed with Phased Pilot**:
- **Proceed** with the **East + partial North pilot** as designed, but **do not scale to South** until:
  1. **Leading indicators** (shared queue backlog, reassignment counts) stabilize within 4 weeks.
  2. **Lagging indicators** (rework rate, 90th percentile cycle time) show **no degradation** vs. baseline for North-pilot.
  3. **Fairness metrics** (cycle time variance by team) remain **within 10% of baseline**.

- **Do not infer success from early averages**:
  - A stable **median cycle time** may hide **tail latency** (e.g., high-complexity cases stalling in shared queues).
  - **Specialist utilization 15%** does not guarantee **net efficiency** if rework or handovers increase elsewhere.

- **Modify before full rollout**:
  - If East benefits but North suffers, **retain embedded specialists for North/South** and only centralize East’s work.
  - If shared approval queue causes role ambiguity, **reintroduce local supervisors for low-complexity cases**.

- **Reject full centralization** if:
  - Rework rate for `local_expertise_flag=False` cases **exceeds 12%**.
  - 90th percentile cycle time for **high-complexity cases** increases by **>15%**.
