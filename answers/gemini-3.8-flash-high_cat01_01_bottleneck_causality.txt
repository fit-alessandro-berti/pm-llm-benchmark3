## 1. Executive causal assessment
Provisional verdict: `mainly confounded` (confidence level: 85/100).

Strongest evidence FOR: The crude aggregate association showing Orion with longer cycle times (mean 21.96h vs Nova's 15.24h, a +6.71h difference) completely reverses when stratifying by case complexity—a classic manifestation of Simpson's paradox. Within every single complexity stratum, Orion completes cases faster than Nova: Low complexity (7.50h vs 9.64h; -2.14h), Medium complexity (17.17h vs 19.38h; -2.21h), and High complexity (31.57h vs 35.00h; -3.43h). Standardizing to the overall case-mix distribution demonstrates that Orion is on average 2.51 hours faster than Nova. Orion's higher unadjusted duration is an artifact of severe confounding by indication and operational sorting: Orion handles 42.9% High-complexity cases (vs Nova's 12.5%), 64.3% compliance-mandated cases (vs 25.0%), and 92.9% night-shift cases under high arrival load (vs 25.0%).

Strongest evidence AGAINST: Residual within-stratum contrasts cannot be definitively interpreted as an isolated group efficiency effect due to structural positivity violations and bundled treatments. Orion is staffed with certified reviewers and handles night shifts, meaning group assignment is completely entangled with reviewer skill and nocturnal operating dynamics. Furthermore, conditioning on completed cases may induce survivorship bias if unfinished complex cases remain in Orion's queue.

Why the proposal may backfire: Management's proposal to reassign 50% of Orion's caseload to Nova is based on a naive interpretation of confounded aggregate metrics. Reassigning complex, compliance-heavy, night-shift claims to Nova will almost certainly backfire: Nova is slower on complex cases (+3.43h in High complexity) and possesses fewer certified reviewers. Shifting these demanding cases to Nova will increase overall cycle times, introduce daytime queue congestion, and elevate appeal risks, rather than generating the anticipated efficiency gains.

Management must immediately reject the blanket 50% reassignment proposal. The belief that Nova outperforms Orion is a statistical illusion produced by Simpson's paradox: Orion handles 42.9% high-complexity claims and 92.9% night shifts, yet delivers faster cycle times and lower queue wait within every observed complexity stratum. Shifting half of Orion's workload to Nova would transfer complex, compliance-heavy claims to a team with fewer certified reviewers and higher stratum-specific cycle times (+3.43 hours in High complexity), actively degrading organizational performance and worsening delays.

Instead, operations should address the true operational bottlenecks revealed by process decomposition. Over 60% of case duration is consumed by queue wait before review, largely driven by compliance check staging and night-shift arrival bursts. Management should first decouple and expedite compliance processing during night shifts to eliminate queue stagnation. Subsequently, implement the proposed 15% stratified routing pilot on Medium-complexity cases to establish rigorous, unconfounded causal benchmarks. Finally, transition routing logic from rigid team silos to dynamic dispatching based on reviewer certification and real-time backlog. This strategy eliminates operational risk while systematically optimizing throughput.

```python
        'Orion_freq_pct': f"{dfg_orion.get((src, tgt), 0) / len(orion_traces):.1%}",
        'Nova_count': dfg_nova.get((src, tgt), 0),
        'Nova_freq_pct': f"{dfg_nova.get((src, tgt), 0) / len(nova_traces):.1%}",
    }
    for src, tgt in all_edges
])
print("\nDirectly-Follows Graph (DFG) Edge Comparison:")
print(dfg_comparison.to_string(index=False))
print("\nDiagnostic complete: analysis confirms confounding by indication; not proof of isolated causation.")
```

## 8. Residual uncertainty
1. Unobserved within-stratum case severity (residual confounding): The categorical `complexity` rating (Low, Medium, High) is coarse, and Orion may receive the most challenging or high-dollar claims within each tier.
   - Most valuable additional data field or design change: Claim monetary amount / estimated damage value, or an objective pre-routing claim severity risk score captured during `Register`.

2. Structural non-overlap between work shift, arrival load, and resource group: Orion operates almost exclusively on night shifts under high arrival load (13 of 14 cases), whereas Nova operates primarily on daytime shifts (12 of 16 cases), making environmental shift effects structurally collinear with group assignment.
   - Most valuable additional data field or design change: A planned shift-rotation schedule or shift-balanced routing design where both Nova and Orion handle balanced allocations of day and night shifts under matched arrival loads.

3. Survivorship and truncation selection bias from conditioning exclusively on completed cases: The dataset omits open, stalled, or abandoned claims, meaning observed Orion durations may represent only the subset of complex claims that finished cleanly.
   - Most valuable additional data field or design change: A complete work-in-progress (WIP) census tracking all active, suspended, and abandoned cases, capturing case start timestamps and right-censored durations for survival analysis.

4. Unmeasured reviewer certification and individual skill heterogeneity: Orion's faster within-stratum performance is attributed to having more certified reviewers, but individual reviewer IDs and credential flags are absent from the event log.
   - Most valuable additional data field or design change: An anonymized reviewer identifier (`reviewer_id`) coupled with individual certification status (`is_certified`) recorded on every review activity instance.

5. Coarse and confounded 30-day quality outcome: The 30-day appeal indicator contains only two total events across the entire dataset (one in Nova, one in Orion) and is confounded by downstream policyholder litigation and external postal/customer response times.
   - Most valuable additional data field or design change: A standardized first-pass internal quality audit score (`quality_audit_score`) assigned by an independent review auditor immediately upon case closure prior to customer notification.
