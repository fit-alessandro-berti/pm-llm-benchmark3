## 1. System boundary and assumptions

**Environmental scope: gate-to-delivery + direct rework/return within the order process, not full cradle-to-grave.**

**Inside the boundary (measured or proxied in the enriched log):**
- Direct production energy: `energy_kwh` per event for `machine_group` = CNC_A, CNC_B, heat_treat, inspection_cell. Allocated per order / `batch_id` and `batch_size`.
- Direct material waste: `material_scrap_kg` per production/rework event + `package_material_kg` per packaging event.
- Outbound transport: `transport_kgco2e` per shipment event differentiated by `route_mode` = parcel_ground, parcel_air, dedicated_van, consolidated_truck. Includes partial shipment second leg and backorder loops: `Pick from stock -> Partial shipment -> Package standard / Backorder wait -> Pick from stock`.
- Rework and packaging rework loops: `rework_flag=true`, `Inspection -> Rework production -> CNC production`, `Package -> Rework packaging -> Package reinforced/standard`.
- Cancellation waste: cases where energy/material was consumed before `Availability check -> Cancel due to delay` (410) and `Backorder wait -> Cancel due to delay` (60) -> `END` (530 total) - work in process already emitted.

**Outside the boundary (not in log, material for displacement assessment):**
- Upstream Scope 3: raw material extraction, semi-finished parts, inbound supplier freight to depot.
- Facility baseline: warehouse HVAC, lighting, machine idling, IT. `energy_kwh=0.8-0.9` for Standard-stock is marginal picking/packaging only.
- Customer use phase and final end-of-life of spare part itself.
- Empty return trips of dedicated_van (evidence: often return empty) unlikely fully allocated in `transport_kgco2e`. Same for consolidated_truck last-mile de-consolidation.
- Obsolescence after Close: evidence 6% of batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days when forecast wrong - eventual scrapping/storage not in `material_scrap_kg`.
- Return freight after `Close -> END` for damage that passed inspection.

**Assumptions and uncertainty:**
- `transport_kgco2e` uses fixed emission factors per mode x weight x distance; does not capture real-time fill rate or congestion. Dedicated van 52.0 kgCO2e/order vs ground 3.6-5.8 kgCO2e/order is order-level average, not ton-km.

### S3. Selective shift MTO from CNC_B to CNC_A on slack
- **Target path/segment:** MTO -> CNC_B -> inspection -> rework path 5% (160h, 32 kWh, 1.8kg scrap, 18% rework, 32% late) vs CNC_A path 13% (118h, 16 kWh, 0.7kg scrap, 9% rework, 21% late).
- **Mechanism:** Routing rule at `Plan production / Emergency production -> CNC production`: if due-date slack > estimated delta (CNC_A queue time - CNC_B) and part not urgent, prefer CNC_A. Avoids 390 `Rework production -> CNC production` loops, saves second energy and scrap.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Net cycle time  despite slower machine (rework avoided dominates), late risk , quality , material . Risk: CNC_A bottleneck if shifted without cap; requires load balancing.
- **Evidence to monitor:** `machine_group` assignment rate, `rework_flag` by machine, `material_scrap_kg`, `energy_kwh`, avg rework loops per order, queue length before CNC, late % by routing.

### S4. Replace unjustified air for Standard-stock Service-contract with priority pick + ground
- **Target path/segment:** Stock -> standard pack -> air 5% (17h, 18.5 kgCO2e, 3% late) and SP1 violations 22% within Service-contract Standard-stock 16% (avg 7.8 vs Regular 4.2 kgCO2e despite same stock).
- **Mechanism:** Instead of upgrading `route_mode`, upgrade internal priority: expedited `Pick from stock` + `Package standard` SLA to gain ~12-20h, allowing ground to meet promise. Air only when `promised_lead_time_hours` < ground reliable time even with priority.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Transport 4-6x saving, cycle time +~6-12h but still within promise for most SP1 violations. Late risk +1pp if picking capacity constrained. Resilience neutral. Quality neutral.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Mode mix before/after, SP1 violation rate, `transport_kgco2e`, median Ship -> Confirm duration vs promised, late % for priority-pick-ground vs air cohort, expedite_flag trend.

### S5. Twice-daily consolidated truck for non-urgent Standard-stock
- **Target path/segment:** Standard-stock Regular 39% low late (7%) stock->standard->ground 42% (34h, 3.6 kgCO2e). Consolidate 850 existing + shift portion of 5100 ground where slack permits.
- **Mechanism:** Scheduled consolidation departures impose 12-24h wait to fill truck, 35-55% lower kgCO2e vs individual parcels per prompt. Only for orders where promised slack > consolidation wait + ground time with 85% confidence.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Transport , cycle time median +12h, p90 stable if schedule reliable. Late risk  if schedule missed or carrier disruption (R1 requires alternative). Packaging unchanged.
- **Evidence to monitor:** % shipped via consolidated_truck, `transport_kgco2e`/order vs fill rate, consolidation wait duration, late % by slack bucket, Cancel due to delay after consolidation wait.

### S6. Pre-shipment packaging-route compatibility check
- **Target path/segment:** Packaging rework loops 260+360=620 cases, Q1 violations (>8kg MTO must use reinforced unless certified) correlating with returns. MTO heavy parts.
- **Mechanism:** Hard gate before `Package -> Ship`: validate `package_material_kg`/type vs part geometry/weight and `route_mode` vibration/handling. Enforce reinforced for >8kg MTO via ground/van/air unless inspection certifies low risk. Prevents `Rework packaging -> Package reinforced (420) / standard (130)`.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Material  per correctly reinforced order (2x) but net  when avoiding double packaging and damage returns (40% damage reduction). Cycle time  (eliminate rework), late risk . Resilience neutral.
- **Evidence to monitor:** `Rework packaging` rate, Q1 violation rate, `package_material_kg` per order, `transport_kgco2e` wasted on re-ships, damage return rate, rework_flag downstream.

### S7. Inspection quality gate for high-rework combos
- **Target path/segment:** MTO via CNC_B + heat treatment + inspection queues where rushed inspections miss defects later causing packaging/customer returns. 470 `Inspection -> Rework production`, inspection_cell queue root cause.
- **Mechanism:** Risk score at `Inspection`: if `machine_group=CNC_B` or heat batch > threshold or queue rush detected, require full inspection cycle time and second signature, not skipped. Blocks premature pass.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Inspection time + small energy +h but avoids 32kWh rework loop and 1.8kg scrap path, net cycle time  (160h -> ~118h), rework , transport waste . If applied indiscriminately, inspection becomes bottleneck.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Inspection duration, queue length, `rework_flag` and `Inspection -> Rework` rate by risk tier, `material_scrap_kg`, `energy_kwh`, downstream packaging rework, late %.

### S8/S9 Combined. Shared emergency lanes + triage of Critical-downtime
- **Target path/segment:** Critical-downtime Service-contract 11% (15h, 52 kgCO2e, 8% rework) via `Emergency production (760) -> Dedicated van (610)` path 8% (14h, 52 kgCO2e, 3% late). SP2 missing justification 31%.
- **Mechanism:** S9: split Critical into `true downtime` (machine down) vs `precautionary urgent` (risk of down) via new case attribute at Receive order. S8: true keeps dedicated_van/air with justification event; precautionary uses shared emergency courier lane pooling multiple urgent orders on corridor (higher fill, still <12h) or stock/ground exception.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Transport  for ~40-60% of Critical that is precautionary (evidence: 3% stock/ground exception already exists at 23h/9% late, acceptable for precautionary but not true). Service protected for true. Resilience  via pooled lane availability vs single van dependency. Risk: misclassification -> late for true.
- **Evidence to monitor:** Share dedicated_van vs shared lane vs stock/ground by urgency tier, `transport_kgco2e`, median cycle by tier, late % and escalation rate, SP2 justification completeness, empty return rate proxy.

**Additional options for package coherence:**
- **S11 Reduce partial shipments:** Change `Availability check` rule: if <85% lines available and replenishment forecast <24h, hold vs 1010 partial shipments (7.2 kgCO2e, 1.4 kWh double shipment, 24% late). Hold only when customer not Critical.
- **S10 Carbon-aware promise negotiation:** For Made-to-order Regular 22% (18% late, 94h), offer +24-48h green promise option enabling batch+consolidated for lower fee. Uses S2+S5 capability without imposing delay.
- **S12 Sustainability exception log:** Mandatory event for any SP1/SP2/Q1 override capturing reason, slack, approver. Governance enabler for all above.

## 5. Rebound and displacement risks

1. **Batching energy vs transport rebound:** Larger `batch_size` cuts `energy_kwh`/item 2.8->1.3 kWh but median wait 4h->38h. For MTO Regular p90 180h and heat 1.7x late, waiting pushes orders over `promised_lead_time_hours` -> triggers `Inspection -> Package reinforced -> Ship air (800)` (23 kgCO2e) or `Emergency production (760)`. Energy saved displaced to 4-6x transport.

2. **Consolidated truck wait vs air/ground split displacement:** 12-24h consolidation wait saves 35-55% `transport_kgco2e` vs individual ground, but if promise was tight, next steps in DFG show shift to `Package -> Ship air (620)` for stock orders to recover. Service-contract customers with low tolerance may cancel: `Backorder wait -> Cancel due to delay`.

3. **Lightweight packaging vs damage return displacement:** Using `Package standard` for >8kg MTO saves ~50% `package_material_kg` now, but Q1 violations correlate with `Rework packaging` and downstream customer returns. Double packaging + second transport + scrap after inspection loop outweighs initial material saving. Reinforced everywhere reverse displacement: over-packaging of 39% Standard-stock Regular inflates material for 40% benefit that only applies to heavy MTO.

4. **Rework avoidance vs bottleneck displacement:** Moving all MTO to CNC_A (9% rework) or adding inspection gate avoids 470 `Rework production` loops and 32 kWh / 1.8kg path, but without capacity control creates new queue before `CNC production` and `Inspection_cell`. That queue drives same late->air displacement as batching, and low DFG precision indicates disruption-era combinatorial explosion.

5. **Obsolescence waste displacement:** Maximizing batch to reach heat >10 efficiency produces inventory not linked to firm order. Prompt: 6% of batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days when forecast wrong. This displaces production energy/material to warehouse obsolescence and eventual scrap not captured in `material_scrap_kg`/order, plus storage.

6. **Partial shipment suppression vs emergency production displacement:** Reducing 1010 partial shipments avoids second parcel 7.2 vs 3.6 kgCO2e and duplicate `energy_kwh`. If `Availability check` simply holds orders without segmentation, Critical-downtime orders waiting for backorder (220) may miss downtime window -> forced `Availability check -> Emergency production (760) -> Dedicated van (610)` at 52 kgCO2e, far worse. Regular customers may also trigger `Cancel due to delay` (410) leading to lost sale plus wasted picking.

7. **Dedicated van pooling vs service escalation displacement:** Sharing vans improves fill and cuts `transport_kgco2e` vs empty returns, but pooling adds handling/transshipment. If `true downtime` misclassified as precautionary (S9 failure), delay causes customer escalation: next time customer demands air and reinforced packaging even when not needed, increasing baseline expectations and Expedite_flag usage. Historical 31% SP2 unjustified vans suggests normalization of exception.

8. **Customer behavior rebound (green promise):** Offering S10 extended lead time for lower carbon can induce customers to order earlier but order larger safety stock, or to negotiate green date then expedite via phone, creating `expedite_flag` outside log logic. Within process, achieved savings offset by increased order frequency and inventory holding.

## 6. Balanced recommendation

**Coherent redesign package in 3 layers, phased by segment risk, not isolated green ideas:**

**Foundation (month 0-2, all segments): S12 + S9 + S1 + S6**
Implement sustainability exception log (S12) mandatory for any SP1 air/consolidated override, SP2 van without Critical justification, Q1 lightweight packaging. This fixes 22% SP1 and 31% SP2 non-conformance visibility and governance - prerequisite for measuring others.

Concurrently split Critical-downtime at `Receive order` into true vs precautionary (S9) with distinct SLA: true = 15h/30h target keeps `Emergency production -> Dedicated van (52 kgCO2e)` allowed; precautionary = 23h/48h allows `stock/ground exception` (4.8 kgCO2e). Deploy green shipping recommender (S1) that checks `promised_lead_time_hours` - 85% reliable transport time; integrates packaging compatibility check (S6, Q1 hard gate). This captures quick win on Standard-stock Service-contract: replace ~70-80% of unjustified air (22% violations within 16% segment ~ ~380-420 orders) with priority pick + ground, 4-6x saving, without touching true downtime service or imposing batch waits. Preserves R1: recommender must surface at least one alternative carrier for Service-contract.

**Production efficiency (month 2-5, Made-to-order focus): S2 + S3 + S7**
Enable dynamic batching (S2) with caps: Service-contract MTO 12h, Regular MTO 24h, heat 12h. Cap prevents 38h tail and 1.7x late risk while still harvesting 16 vs 24 kWh advantage for batches >=12. Add selective routing CNC_B->CNC_A when slack > queue delta (S3) coupled with inspection quality gate for high-risk combos (S7). Together they attack the 5% worst path (160h, 32kWh, 1.8kg, 32% late) and 470 rework loops: net energy -~40-50% per affected MTO order, scrap -~50-60%, cycle median -~30-40h despite extra inspection. Monitor CNC_A utilization to avoid bottleneck displacement; if queue > threshold, fallback to CNC_B with enhanced inspection.

**Logistics fulfilment (month 3-6): S5 + S11 + S8, enabler S10**
Twice-daily consolidated truck for Standard-stock Regular with slack (S5) where promised allows 12-24h wait - shift portion of 5100 ground parcels, 35-55% cut per order. Pair with partial shipment rule tightening (S11): hold if replenishment <24h and not Critical; otherwise allow split with customer consent to avoid backorder cancellation (60 cases) or emergency displacement. Replace remaining unjustified dedicated vans for precautionary Critical with shared emergency lane (S8). Pilot S10 carbon-aware promise extension for Regular MTO: offer +24h green option at order negotiation, unlocking S2/S5 without breaching promise.

**Why balanced:**
- Environmental: Attacks three largest intensive domains simultaneously - air 4-6x, dedicated van 52 kgCO2e, rework 32 kWh/1.8kg loops, and double shipments 7.2 kgCO2e - rather than maximizing one at expense of others. Avoids over-batching obsolescence 6% and over-packaging 2x material.
- Service: Differentiated by segment/slack. Critical true downtime service unchanged (4% late, 15h); precautionary explicitly re-promised slower. Standard-stock late risk held near 5-7% via priority pick compensation and 85% probability gate. MTO late risk falls from 18%/21-32% rework path to ~13-15% via less rework and capped waits.
- Quality: Q1 and packaging gate + inspection gate reduce `rework_flag` 11%/10% MTO and 8% Critical rework, preventing hidden scrap and returns that would erase transport gains.
- Resilience: R1 kept - no single mode for Service-contract, shared lanes add alternative vs single van carrier, exception log ensures emergency paths (760) remain available but justified. Low DFG precision during disruptions becomes monitor: if variant entropy spikes, caps automatically relax.
- Not lowest-emission everywhere: we do NOT force all MTO to max batch, all stock to consolidated, all Critical to ground - each would violate resilience/service and rebound to air via late escalation.

## 7. Validation and governance plan

**Event-log based validation design (before-after with control cohorts, not aggregates alone):**

1.  **Baseline & cohort definition:** Freeze 12,000-order baseline DFG, case-mix, and segment KPIs (table in prompt). For each segment (Standard-stock Regular/Service-contract, MTO Regular/Service-contract, Critical true/precautionary) define control chart limits for median/p90 cycle, late %, `rework_flag`, `energy_kwh`, `transport_kgco2e`, `material_scrap_kg`, `package_material_kg`, cancellation (530) and partial shipment (1010) rates.

2.  **Process mining validation pipeline after go-live:**
    - *Conformance checking (SP1/SP2/Q1):* Replay new log against normative model with constraints: SP1 ground/consolidated when >85% on-time feasible, SP2 van requires `justification_event` with urgency tier, Q1 >8kg requires reinforced. Measure violation rate trend weekly. Target: SP1 22%-><8%, SP2 missing 31%-><5%, Q1 violations -><3% within 3 months. Flag overrides via S12 exception log reason distribution.
    - *Variant & performance comparison:* Compare discovered DFG after vs before. Expect: `Receive -> Availability check -> Pick -> Package standard -> Ship ground` share rises from 42% toward 48-52%; `air` edges 620+800 down 30-50% for non-precautionary; `Batch wait -> CNC` median wait down from 38h tail to cap, batch size median up but variance down; `Inspection -> Rework` 470 down 20-40%; `Rework packaging` 620 down 40-60%; `Partial shipment` 1010 down. Monitor DFG precision - should improve as emergency combinatorics reduce.
    - *Segment-level service monitoring:* For each case_type x customer_type, track median/p90 cycle, late % vs promised, `Cancel due to delay` and `Backorder wait -> Cancel`. Alert if late % worsens >2pp vs baseline or p90 breach for Critical true tier. Use `promised_lead_time_hours` slack stratification: late should not increase in low-slack decile.
    - *Multi-criteria sustainability dashboard:* Per order: `energy_kwh`, `transport_kgco2e`, `material_scrap_kg` + `package_material_kg` + obsolescence % not shipped 14d. Report per segment and per path (Table 2 paths). Watch rebound: if `energy_kwh`/item  but `transport_kgco2e`  per same segment, trigger cap review. Attribute allocation: energy per batch_id apportioned by `batch_size`, consolidated_truck apportioned by weight.
    - *Rework/returns & obsolescence:* Track `rework_flag`, return events after Close (extend log beyond Close if needed), 390 `Rework -> CNC` loops, and 14-day obsolescence rate per batch. Success = rework MTO 11%->~7%, material scrap 0.90->~0.60kg MTO, obsolescence 6%-><3%.
    - *Resilience stress test:* Simulate carrier disruption period: verify R1 holds - % Service-contract orders with 1 alternative route event available stays >95%. Monitor share that correctly fell back to alternative vs breached SP1/R1. Review dedicated van fill rate (proxy for empty return) via shared lane pooling.

3.  **Governance:**
    - S12 exception log requires: `case_id`, policy (SP1/SP2/Q1), requested `route_mode`/`machine_group`/`package`, recommended mode, `promised_lead_time_hours` & slack, approver, customer impact. Weekly sustainability operations review owns violations, monthly steering owns target adjustment.
    - Owner per intervention: logistics owns S1/S4/S5/S8, production owns S2/S3/S7, quality owns S6/Q1, product owns S9 triage definition, sales owns S10 promise negotiation.
    - Guardrails: If median cycle for Critical true >18h or late >6%, auto-exempt from consolidation/shared lane. If CNC_A queue > threshold or inspection queue > threshold, temporarily disable S3/S7 shift to prevent bottleneck rebound.
    - Continuous improvement loop: Every 6-8 weeks, re-mine batch energy curve and route reliability tables to recalibrate recommender thresholds and wait caps; update emission factors for `transport_kgco2e` to reflect actual fill rates.

This closes lifecycle gaps: visible rework and obsolescence prevent burden shifting, service/resilience hard limits prevent lowest-emission-but-unacceptable choices, and conformance evidence ties every sustainability decision to an event-attribute trail.
