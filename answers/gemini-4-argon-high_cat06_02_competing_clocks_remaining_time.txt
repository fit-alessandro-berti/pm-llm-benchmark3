## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Competing-clocks formulation at the parallel join
Because QA Review requires usable results from **both** branches, the parallel join time $T_{\text{join}}$ is governed by the maximum operator rather than the sum of branch durations:
$$T_{\text{join}} = \max\left(T_{\text{Chem}},\; T_{\text{Micro}}\right)$$
Post-join stages (QA waiting, QA review, optional QA query/investigation rework, and final release/rejection) are sequential and therefore additive in calendar time (subject to QA working hours):
$$T_{\text{terminal}} = \max\left(T_{\text{Chem}},\; T_{\text{Micro}}\right) + W_{\text{QA}}\!\left(T_{\text{join}}\right) + S_{\text{QA}} + D_{\text{rework}} + \Delta_{\text{calendar}} + S_{\text{final}}$$
where $\Delta_{\text{calendar}}$ represents overnight and weekend shift closures.

### Scenario S1: Smooth path (Risk-Priority vs. FIFO Chemistry)

1. **Chemistry waiting and service ($T_{\text{Chem}}$)**:
   - *Active servers at Friday 15:30*: Analyst 1 has $1.2\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$ remaining (free at Fri $16:42\text{–}17:30$); Analyst 2 has $0.4\text{–}0.9\text{ h}$ remaining (free at Fri $15:54\text{–}16:24$). Four samples (1 urgent, 3 ordinary) are waiting ahead of B-417.
   - *Sub-regime S1a — Risk-Priority ($45\%$ chance)*: Analyst 2 takes the 1 urgent sample at $15:54\text{–}16:24$. B-417 bypasses the 3 ordinary samples and starts on Analyst 1 at **Friday 16:42–17:30** ($1.2\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$ wait). It receives $0.5\text{–}1.3\text{ h}$ of service before the **Friday 18:00 lab closure** ($T = 2.5\text{ h}$). Because B-417's total service time is $\text{Triangular}(1.5, 2.5, 4.5)\text{ h}$ (mean $2.83\text{ h}$), even the minimum $1.5\text{ h}$ service cannot complete on Friday ($1.2 + 1.5 = 2.7\text{ h} > 2.5\text{ h}$). Work pauses for **$62.0\text{ h}$ over the weekend** and resumes **Monday 08:00** ($T = 64.5\text{ h}$) with $0.2\text{–}4.0\text{ h}$ (central $1.0\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$) remaining. Chemistry completes on **Monday 08:12–12:00** ($T = 64.7\text{–}68.5\text{ h}$; central **Monday 09:00–10:00**, $T = 65.5\text{–}66.5\text{ h}$).
   - *Sub-regime S1b — FIFO ($55\%$ chance)*: B-417 waits behind all 4 queued samples. Total backlog ahead of B-417 is $(1.6\text{–}2.9)\text{ analyst-h}$ (current jobs) $+ 4 \times \text{Tri}(1.5, 2.5, 4.5)\text{ analyst-h} \approx 9.1\text{–}17.9\text{ analyst-h}$ (central $12.3\text{–}14.8\text{ analyst-h}$). Friday $15:30\text{–}18:00$ supplies $2 \times 2.5 = 5.0\text{ analyst-h}$, followed by the $62\text{ h}$ weekend pause. On Monday at 08:00, the remaining $4.1\text{–}12.9\text{ analyst-h}$ of backlog requires $2.1\text{–}6.5\text{ clock hours}$ across 2 analysts, so B-417 starts service at **Monday 10:05–14:30** (central **11:30–12:50**). Adding B-417's own service ($\text{Tri}(1.5, 2.5, 4.5)\text{ h}$) yields Chemistry completion on **Monday 11:35–18:00** ($T = 68.1\text{–}74.5\text{ h}$; central **Monday 13:45–16:00**, $T = 70.25\text{–}72.5\text{ h}$).
2. **Microbiology incubation and readout ($T_{\text{Micro}}$)**:
   - Automated primary incubation ($46\text{–}54\text{ h}$ from Friday 14:00) runs continuously through the weekend and becomes technically ready on **Sunday 12:00–20:00** ($T = 44.5\text{–}52.5\text{ h}$; central **Sunday 15:00–17:00**).
   - *With weekend on-call authorization ($p = 0.25\text{–}0.45$)*: Dispatch and readout ($1\text{–}3\text{ h}$) complete on **Sunday 13:00–23:00** ($T = 45.5\text{–}55.5\text{ h}$).
   - *Without weekend authorization ($p = 0.55\text{–}75\%$)*: The technically ready sample waits $12\text{–}20\text{ h}$ until routine staffing starts **Monday 08:00** ($T = 64.5\text{ h}$). Readout and recording take $0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$ (plus $0\text{–}0.5\text{ h}$ if queued behind another weekend batch), completing on **Monday 08:30–10:00** ($T = 65.0\text{–}66.5\text{ h}$).
3. **Parallel join time ($T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{Chem}}, T_{\text{Micro}})$)**:
   - Under **S1a (Risk-Priority Chem)**: If on-call microbiology ran Sunday, $T_{\text{join}} = \max(45.5\text{–}55.5\text{ h},\; 64.7\text{–}68.5\text{ h}) = T_{\text{Chem}}$ (**Monday 08:12–12:00**); thus, weekend on-call microbiology saves at most $0\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$ on the smooth path because Chemistry is frozen until Monday 08:00. Without on-call, both branches finish Monday morning and compete closely: $T_{\text{join}} = \max(\text{Mon } 08:12\text{–}12:00,\; \text{Mon } 08:30\text{–}10:00) =$ **Monday 08:30–12:00** ($T = 65.0\text{–}68.5\text{ h}$; central **Monday 09:00–10:15**, $T = 65.5\text{–}66.75\text{ h}$).
   - Under **S1b (FIFO Chem)**: Chemistry finishes hours after Microbiology: $T_{\text{join}} = \max(\text{Mon } 11:35\text{–}18:00,\; \text{Sun/Mon } \le 10:00) =$ **Monday 11:35–18:00** ($T = 68.1\text{–}74.5\text{ h}$; central **Monday 13:45–16:00**, $T = 70.25\text{–}72.5\text{ h}$).
4. **QA waiting and service ($W_{\text{QA}} + S_{\text{QA}}$)**:
   - At Monday 08:00, the QA queue holds $2\text{–}8$ batches (central $4\text{–}6$) served by **2 reviewers**.
   - *Earliest plausible (S1a join Mon 08:12–08:30)*: With priority placement or light queue ($W_{\text{QA}} = 0.2\text{–}0.5\text{ h}$) and fast QA service ($S_{\text{QA}} = 0.6\text{–}0.8\text{ h}$), QA review completes at **Monday 09:15–09:45** ($T = 65.75\text{–}66.25\text{ h}$).
   - *Central*: For S1a (join Mon 09:00–10:15), $W_{\text{QA}} = 2.0\text{–}4.5\text{ h}$ and $S_{\text{QA}} = 1.2\text{–}2.5\text{ h}$ yield QA completion on **Monday 13:30–17:00** ($T = 70.0\text{–}73.5\text{ h}$). For S1b (join Mon 13:45–16:00, after 2 reviewers have drained the morning queue for 6–8 hours), $W_{\text{QA}} = 1.0\text{–}3.5\text{ h}$ and $S_{\text{QA}} = 1.2\text{–}2.5\text{ h}$ yield QA completion at **Monday 16:30–20:00** ($T = 73.0\text{–}76.5\text{ h}$), or if total QA time exceeds remaining Monday shift hours, work pauses at **Monday 20:00** ($12\text{ h}$ overnight pause) and finishes **Tuesday 08:30–10:00** ($T = 89.0\text{–}90.5\text{ h}$).
   - *Tail (S1b late join + overdispersed QA arrivals + P80–P90 service $3.5\text{–}5.0\text{ h}$)*: Pauses at Monday 20:00 and finishes **Tuesday 09:45–14:30** ($T = 90.25\text{–}95.0\text{ h}$).
5. **Optional rework/investigation**: None ($0\text{ h}$).
6. **Final release**: Electronic Release adds $0.25\text{–}0.75\text{ h}$ (continuous 24/7), yielding terminal completion at **66.0–66.75 h** (earliest: **Mon 09:30–10:15**), **70.5–77.0 h** (central Monday: **Mon 14:00–20:30**, or **89.5–91.0 h / Tue 09:00–10:30** with overnight spillover), and **90.5–95.75 h** (tail: **Tue 10:00–15:15**).

### Scenario S2: Chemistry rerun path

1. **Chemistry waiting, primary service, and priority rerun ($T_{\text{Chem}}$)**:
   - Primary chemistry finishes at **Monday 08:12–12:00** (Risk-Priority) or **Monday 11:35–18:00** (FIFO).
   - A borderline result ($p = 0.10\text{–}0.20$) triggers a priority rerun requiring $\text{Triangular}(1.0, 1.8, 3.0)\text{ h}$ (mean $1.93\text{ h}$).
   - Under **Risk-Priority**, the rerun starts Monday morning/midday and finishes within Monday's shift at **Monday 09:15–14:30** ($T = 65.75\text{–}71.0\text{ h}$; central **Monday 11:00–13:00**, $T = 67.5\text{–}69.5\text{ h}$).
   - Under **FIFO**, primary chemistry finishes Monday afternoon ($13:45\text{–}16:00$ central). Adding a $1.0\text{–}3.0\text{ h}$ rerun either barely finishes before **Monday 18:00** (**Monday 15:45–18:00**, $T = 72.25\text{–}74.5\text{ h}$) or is truncated by the **Monday 18:00 lab closure** ($14\text{ h}$ overnight pause until Tuesday 08:00), completing on **Tuesday 08:30–11:30** ($T = 89.0\text{–}92.0\text{ h}$).
2. **Microbiology incubation and readout ($T_{\text{Micro}}$)**: Completes **Sunday 13:00–23:00** (on-call) or **Monday 08:30–10:00** (routine).
3. **Parallel join time ($T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{Chem}}, T_{\text{Micro}})$)**: Strictly dominated by Chemistry ($T_{\text{join}} = T_{\text{Chem}}$): **Monday 09:15–14:30** (Risk-Priority) or **Monday 15:45 to Tuesday 11:30** (FIFO).
4. **QA waiting and service**:
   - Joins occurring Monday late afternoon or Tuesday morning face $W_{\text{QA}} = 0.5\text{–}4.0\text{ h}$ and $S_{\text{QA}} = 1.2\text{–}3.5\text{ h}$. Monday afternoon joins frequently cross the **Monday 20:00 QA closure** ($12\text{ h}$ pause to Tuesday 08:00).
5. **Optional rework/investigation**: None in the pure S2 path (cases where the rerun also triggers a QA investigation are captured in the S4/S5 overlap, which occurs with $p = 0.30\text{–}0.55$).
6. **Final release**: Electronic Release ($+0.25\text{–}0.75\text{ h}$) yields **67.5–68.5 h** (earliest: **Mon 11:00–12:00**), **73.0–94.0 h** (central: **Mon 16:30 to Tue 13:30**), and **94.5–115.5 h** (tail: **Tue 14:00 to Wed 11:00**).

### Scenario S3: Delayed microbiology readout or contamination confirmation

1. **Chemistry waiting and service ($T_{\text{Chem}}$)**: Finishes **Monday 08:12–16:00** (or up to Tuesday morning if combined with a chemistry rerun).
2. **Microbiology incubation, confirmation, and readout ($T_{\text{Micro}}$)**:
   - Primary incubation is ready **Sunday 12:00–20:00**.
   - *Subcase S3a — Weekend on-call authorized ($25\%\text{–}45\%$)*: Primary readout completes **Sunday 13:00–23:00**. A contamination indication ($p = 0.05\text{–}0.12$) immediately initiates $20\text{–}30\text{ h}$ of continuous confirmation incubation on Sunday, which matures between **Monday 09:00** ($13:00 + 20\text{ h}$) and **Tuesday 05:00** ($23:00 + 30\text{ h}$). Secondary readout ($0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$ during weekday 08:00–18:00 staffing) completes **Monday 09:30–18:00** (if ready by Mon 16:30–17:30) or **Tuesday 08:30–09:30** (if maturing after Mon 17:00).
   - *Subcase S3b — No weekend authorization ($55\%\text{–}75\%$)*: Primary readout waits until Monday 08:00 and finishes **Monday 08:30–10:00** ($T = 65.0\text{–}66.5\text{ h}$). Continuous confirmation incubation ($20\text{–}30\text{ h}$) runs through Monday night and matures on **Tuesday 04:30–16:00** (central **Tuesday 08:30–14:00**). Secondary readout ($0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$) completes on **Tuesday 08:30–17:30** ($T = 89.0\text{–}98.0\text{ h}$; central **Tuesday 09:30–15:30**, $T = 90.0\text{–}96.0\text{ h}$).
3. **Parallel join time ($T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{Chem}}, T_{\text{Micro}})$)**:
   - Dominated by Microbiology in all confirmation cases without Sunday on-call ($T_{\text{join}} =$ **Tuesday 08:30–17:30**, $T = 89.0\text{–}98.0\text{ h}$), and in most Sunday on-call cases ($T_{\text{join}} =$ **Monday 11:30 to Tuesday 09:30**).
4. **QA waiting and service**:
   - By Tuesday midday, 2 QA reviewers have operated for $14\text{–}18\text{ h}$ since Monday 08:00, reducing the QA queue to a steady-state $1\text{–}3$ batches ($W_{\text{QA}} = 0.5\text{–}3.0\text{ h}$, $S_{\text{QA}} = 1.2\text{–}3.5\text{ h}$). Late Tuesday joins cross the **Tuesday 20:00 QA closure** into Wednesday morning.
5. **Optional rework/investigation**: In $45\%\text{–}70\%$ of confirmation cases where no formal investigation is opened (or where a brief documentation check suffices), QA completes within Tuesday afternoon or Wednesday morning. (The remaining $30\%\text{–}55\%$ trigger a Deviation Investigation, overlapping with S4/S5.)
6. **Final release or rejection**: Adding $0.25\text{–}0.75\text{ h}$ (Release) or $0.5\text{–}1.0\text{ h}$ (Rejection) yields **68.0–70.0 h** (earliest with Sunday on-call: **Mon 11:30–13:30**; **90.5–92.0 h / Tue 10:00–11:30** without on-call), **93.5–116.5 h** (central: **Tue 13:00 to Wed 12:00**), and **116.5–144.5 h** (tail: **Wed 12:00 to Thu 16:00**).

### Scenario S4: QA documentation query or documentation-only investigation tail

1. **Chemistry and Microbiology branches**: Complete per S1 (join **Monday 08:30–16:00**) or S2/S3 (join **Monday 16:00 to Tuesday 15:30**).
2. **Parallel join and initial QA review**: QA review begins after $W_{\text{QA}} = 1.0\text{–}4.5\text{ h}$ and identifies a documentation gap or anomaly after $0.5\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$ of initial review (typically **Monday 11:00–19:00** on clean lab paths, or **Tuesday 10:00–17:00** on lab-rework paths).
3. **Optional rework/investigation (Dominant clock)**:
   - *Documentation Query ($p = 0.15\text{–}0.30$ given completeness score $0.82$)*: Adds **$4\text{–}12\text{ working hours}$** to supply missing batch records. Because weekday operational shifts are $10\text{–}12\text{ h}$, a query raised Monday afternoon ($14:00\text{–}18:00$) pauses overnight and returns to QA on **Tuesday 09:00–16:00**, followed by $0.5\text{–}1.5\text{ h}$ of QA verification review.
   - *Documentation-Only Deviation Investigation ($60\%\text{–}80\%$ of opened investigations)*: Adds **$8\text{–}20\text{ working hours}$** ($0.8\text{–}2.0$ weekday shifts, i.e., $20\text{–}48\text{ calendar hours}$ including overnight pauses) plus $1.0\text{–}2.0\text{ h}$ of final QA review. An investigation opened Monday afternoon returns to QA between **Tuesday 15:00 and Wednesday 18:00**; if preceded by Tuesday lab rework or stacked with a documentation query, it finishes **Thursday 09:00–19:30**.
4. **Final release**: Electronic Release ($+0.25\text{–}0.75\text{ h}$) yields **72.0–74.0 h** (earliest: 4 h query resolved Monday evening, **Mon 15:30–17:30**), **91.5–122.5 h** (central: **Tue 11:00 to Wed 18:00**), and **122.5–148.5 h** (tail: **Wed 18:00 to Thu 20:00**).

### Scenario S5: Severe tail — Laboratory deviation investigation (Release or Rejection)

1. **Correlated trigger**: Most frequently initiated when the upstream temperature excursion is confirmed ($p = 0.20\text{–}0.40$), which elevates both chemistry rerun and microbiology contamination probabilities and directly raises the QA Deviation Investigation probability to $0.30\text{–}0.55$. In $20\%\text{–}40\%$ of opened investigations, laboratory investigation work is required.
2. **Parallel join and QA initiation**: Investigation is opened during QA review on **Monday 11:00–20:00** (if triggered by confirmed excursion or Monday chemistry rerun) or **Tuesday 11:00–19:00** (if triggered after Tuesday microbiology confirmation).
3. **Laboratory investigation and second-weekend calendar trap**:
   - Adds **$3\text{–}7\text{ calendar days}$** ($72\text{–}168\text{ calendar hours}$) of additional laboratory testing, followed by $1.0\text{–}2.5\text{ h}$ of QA review.
