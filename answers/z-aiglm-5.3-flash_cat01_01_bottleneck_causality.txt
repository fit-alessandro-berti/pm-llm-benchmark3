| 2 | Stratified **randomized/quasi-random routing pilot**: block-randomize arrival half-days; 10–15% of eligible Low/Medium cases routed to the opposite group; Day/Normal emphasis, with a guarantee provision (night float) if any Night case is randomized | Isolates the direct group effect net of shift/load in the overlap region | Arrival time-block (cluster) to protect queue integrity; case-level within block | Exclude High-complexity/uncovered shifts from eligibility; volume caps; backstop certified reviewers; appeal sentinel monitoring | ITT decomposition of cycle into wait/service; mediation share estimates; DFG-drift and conformance alerts per arm | Encouragement/IV or ITT estimate of total effect where positivity holds; validates or kills Hypotheses 1, 2, 5 | Pause if pilot-arm median cycle exceeds control by >10% (CI excluding 0, n50/arm) or any confirmed quality incident; auto-revert routing parameter |
| 3 | **Queueing & compliance capacity engineering** (label unchanged): pre-booked compliance slots for cases classified Medium+; evening/night float coverage for Nova; expedite lane when load crosses threshold | Attacks queue_wait formation and compliance staging directly | Site-week schedule / capacity allocation | Gradual ramp; SLA alerting; no forced migration of High cases | p50/p90 wait-by-hour; Little's-law utilization reconstruction; staging-slot conformance rate | Difference-in-differences vs control hours isolates the congestion channel predicted by Hypotheses 2–3 | Rollback if p90 wait deteriorates for 2 consecutive weeks vs baseline seasonality |
| 4 | Adaptive stepped-wedge expansion of the winning option, capped share growth, sunset clause | Scales verified mechanisms only | Weekly cohort wedges | Prespecified expansion thresholds; continuous quality watch | Same dashboard as Phases 2–3; overlap refresh each wedge | Sequential evidence with staged commitment | Freeze expansion and return to previous wedge if any phase gate trips |

**Final recommendation.** Do not execute the blanket 50% reassignment. The crude gap management cites is almost certainly composition: within every complexity stratum — and within both waiting and service components — Orion shows no deficit, and often a small surplus, though these remain confounded by shift, load, compliance burden, latent risk, and survivorship sampling. Instead: (1) begin Phase 1 instrumentation immediately, since today's data cannot distinguish a slow group from a completion-filtered sample; (2) run the small block-randomized routing pilot confined to regions with demonstrated overlap, sized to detect 2–3 h differences; (3) in parallel, relieve the mechanisms we can see — compliance slotting and night-float capacity — which help both groups regardless of the causal question; (4) expand only through the stepped-wedge if mechanism-level metrics (waits, service, rework) and quality sentinels cooperate. Communicate upfront that if the stratified pattern holds, migrating cases will yield approximately nothing, and force-moving night work to Nova plausibly *increases* waiting while hiding new backlog in uncompleted cases.

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Unmeasured case risk steering routing.** The most valuable addition: export of the routing engine's input features (score, attributes) per case — separating latent-risk confounding from any true group effect.
2. **Survivorship bias from missing withdrawals/still-open cases.** Most valuable: a full intake-versus-outcome status ledger (registered, in-flight, withdrawn, completed) reviewed weekly as a funnel by group×complexity.
3. **Timing opacity** (no activity timestamps; wait conflates queue and compliance staging; cycle  wait+service). Most valuable: a complete event log with activity start/end timestamps and reviewer IDs enabling queue-state reconstruction and compliance-segment attribution.
4. **Shift and arrival-load mechanics undefined** (coverage rosters, load definition, time-varying). Most valuable: hourly staffing roster and queue-length feed per group coverage window, converting shift/load into measurable utilization.
5. **Coarse, post-process 30-day outcome.** Most valuable: blinded adjudication of defects/appeal reason codes mapped to the responsible process stage, replacing the single binary endpoint.
