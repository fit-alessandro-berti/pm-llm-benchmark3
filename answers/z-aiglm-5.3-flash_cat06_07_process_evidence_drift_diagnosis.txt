**July — the hinge month.** Supplier problems start Jul 10; the parts edge nearly doubles (42h65h) and V3 conditional breach jumps to 0.46 — observed, operational, label-free evidence. Meanwhile censoring quietly begins: delay 36 d, weighted-aggregate deficit at the top of the noise band. Two mechanisms start co-moving and must be tracked separately thereafter.

**August–September — deliberate overlaps, not one cause.** Three things happen at once: (i) the parts edge blows out to 130–155h (process/performance drift); (ii) conditional-on-V3 breach doubles to ~0.6+ while path definition is unchanged (concept drift — the supplier shock is invisible to current X); (iii) Partner Z enters (population shift), skewing V3 further and carrying unmodeled covariates. Decomposition says the Aug jump splits roughly half mix / half within-path worsening; and the growing weighted-aggregate deficit (0.033, 0.031) shows censoring is beginning to distort the denominator differentially, because slow-maturing V3/Z cases are under-represented among labeled ones. Treating Aug–Sep as a single "drift blob" would hide that one third of the problem is Who-are-the-cases (population), one third Where-is-the-process (parts leg), one third What-does-Y-mean-given-X (concept).

**October — a measurement breakdown masquerading as improvement.** Observed 0.16 is *below* prediction and below every plausible continuation of the Aug–Sep trajectory; that is only explicable if the labeled 55% excludes the long-resolution paths that generate most breaches — exactly what median delay 52 d plus the API backlog predict. AUROC 0.64 is computed on a truncated, easier cohort and is not comparable to prior months. Any model action taken against October's headline numbers would optimize toward an artifact.

Why these are not one homogeneous failure: April shows predictions bad/outcomes fine; June shows outcomes improved/predictions stale; Aug–Sep shows outcomes worsened/rankings degraded; October shows the measuring instrument failing. Four different signatures, four different remedies (repair, recalibrate, relearn, wait).

## 3. Diagnostic tests

1. **Label-maturity curves & fixed-vintage backtesting.** Rebuild each monthly cohort at D+35 / D+60 / D+90 after month end and recompute all metrics. *Under censoring-only:* metrics converge with age — Oct's "improvement" vanishes, final Oct breach lands  0.28–0.31, Z's 0.45 shifts measurably. *Under real drift:* late-arriving labels confirm sustained elevation (Aug–Sep stay  0.60 on V3) rather than mean-reverting. Bonus discriminator: fixed vintage does *not* rescue April — distinguishing censoring (an aging problem) from corruption (an immutable content problem).
2. **Replay recomputation after label normalization + dedup.** Rebuild April scoring features from raw events with the alias map and uniform dedup, rescore with the unchanged model. *Under pipeline-failure:* AUROC  0.81, mean pred  0.19, ECE  0.03 restored for April; if metrics stay poor with verified-clean inputs, either inputs still aren't clean (check new-source timing semantics post-migration) or something else broke — escalate accordingly.
3. **Calibration/discrimination stratified by variant × source × month.** Reliability diagrams per stratum. *Under covariate-shift-only (June):* within-stratum calibration lines sit on the diagonal; pooled miscalibration is composition arithmetic. *Under concept drift (Aug–Sep):* underprediction concentrates in the V3 stratum (slope  1) while V1/V2 strata stay calibrated; a mixed picture = combined causes, which is what the aggregate evidence already suggests. For Z: if a non-Z-transported score underpredicts Z even after reweighting  unmodeled Z covariates; if reweighting closes the gap  pure composition.
4. **Variant and DFG / performance-DFG comparison.** Month-over-month total-variation distance on variant shares plus CUSUM on the parts-edge median. *Expected under process story:* the edge alarm fires mid-July, *preceding* any predictive-metric alarm — establishing operational causation with label-free evidence. *Under model-side-only story:* edges flat while metrics degrade — already falsified by 28h155h. Also confirm the remote leg's flatness holds, otherwise the "localized" reading dies.
5. **Did the outcome relation change conditional on the same path?** Fit breach ~ case covariates + month indicators within stratum V3; test Chow/interaction at the supplier breakpoint; repeat controlling for measured part-wait. *Under true concept drift in the deployed feature space:* post-period coefficients shift significantly. *Under hidden-covariate story:* adding live part latency makes pre/post coefficients statistically indistinguishable — i.e., Y|X was stable all along once richer X is used. Both outcomes justify updating X; they differ in whether relearning weights is needed versus plumbing a feature. Also run a sensitivity probe: score records with injected part-latency proxies to check the model even responds to that signal.
6. **Population reweighting / transport check for Partner Z.** IPW or entropy-balance September non-Z onto Z's covariate profile; predict and compare with observed Z. *Composition-only:* reweighted non-Z breach  Z's observed (after maturity correction). *Residual gap:* Z-intrinsic factors/missing features. Run inside matched maturity-age slices to prevent censoring from impersonating a population effect, since Z matures slowest.
7. **Feature missingness & lineage audit.** Diff old/new source schemas; unit-test the alias mapping against every deployment; timestamp caches; chart null-rate per feature with deployment markers. *Under the pipeline account:* fixes produce sharp step-changes in null rates aligned to May 6 (observed: 14%3% ) with zero movement in outcome statistics during those windows. *Under broader ingestion failure:* missingness uncorrelated with releases and drifting steadily — expand audit scope beyond April.

## 4. Recalibration-versus-retraining decision

**Stage 0 — Containment (this week).** Freeze acceptance of current-vintage comparisons for Sep–Oct; register all intervention dates on every dashboard; replay-and-recompute April; enforce dedup globally; disable any automated retraining trigger; add temporary business buffers (extra capacity/padded promises) for V3-dispatched work while scoring lags reality.

**Stage 1 — Short-term recalibration ( 2 weeks).** Refit per-segment (at minimum: V1/V2 pool and V3 separately) isotonic/Platt calibrators on the clean, post-policy, fully mature **June** vintage; validate on May and on July's matured portion with maturity-gated pairing (never compare a prediction to a not-yet-existing outcome). Expected: restores ECE/Brier for V1/V2; V3 calibrator will need weekly refresh during the active supplier regime — do not hard-code its correction.

**Stage 2 — Feature repair and augmentation (2–6 weeks).** Backfill/recompute the April cached feature; make dedup uniform; shorten the parts-queue feature's freshness cycle; add supplier ETA, observed part-leg latency percentile, rural distance, device age; expose routing intent (or a strong proxy) available at scoring time.

**Stage 3 — Full retraining (gated).** Ship only when: (a) Test 5 confirms persistent conditional change on matured labels, (b) new features are live, (c) a fixed-vintage D+60 challenge set shows challenger beats incumbent on AUROC *and* Brier for Aug–Sep, (d) human sign-off against the intervention registry. While the supplier regime is still moving, prefer incremental updates with drift-weighted sampling over one big retrain that fits a transient regime.

**Training-data safety.**
- *Safe now:* Jan–Mar, May, Jun (100% mature, clean pipelines; June is the essential post-policy anchor).
- *Conditional:* July (98% mature, but split pre/post Jul 10 and flag supplier era). April only after successful Stage-0 replay — never its original features.
- *Unsafe:* September (82%, Z immature — admit later with maturity/IPW weights only); **August** border-case (93% — usable with weights for retraining, exclude from calibration fitting); **October absolutely excluded** (55% + API backlog) until D+90.

**Segment-specific modeling.** Justified *now* for V3 as a separate calibration head (its conditional rate halved-or-doubled while others stood still — a pooled calibrator would underfit both sides). Justified *later and conditionally* for Z: only if Test 6 shows residual Z-specific behavior, and with a shared-backbone-plus-per-segment-calibrator architecture rather than fully separate models, because Z contributes only ~1.2–1.4k cases/month and fragmentation increases variance. Revisit once Z matures and the new features exist.

## 5. Monitoring architecture

Monitor layers (each writes status into a shared context consumed downstream):

1. **Predictive-quality monitors** — rolling AUROC/Brier per vintage; publish only with a maturity badge (e.g., metrics marked `provisional` until 90% mature or maturity-adjusted via censoring weights using predicted resolution time).
2. **Calibration monitors** — running reliability curves and ECE per segment (variant × source) with alert bands; catch June-type composition gaps via aggregate-vs-stratum divergence.
3. **Label-maturity monitors** — actual vs historical maturity curves and delay quantiles per cohort; deviations raise `metrics_provisional`, never model actions.
4. **Data-quality monitors** — schema contract (activity enum, required fields), unknown-label share, duplicate rate, per-feature null rates, cache-version/lineage freshness; breach sets `data_invalid` and quarantines scoring.
5. **Process-variant monitors** — monthly variant-share profiles (from Test 4) with TV-distance alarms and new-variant emergence detection (would have caught the June policy immediately and Partner Z's V3 skew).
6. **Performance-DFG monitors** — per-edge median/SLA control charts (`Order Part -> Part Received` CUSUM fires 11 July, ~3 weeks before any label-based alarm); label-independent, so immune to censoring games.
7. **Population monitors** — source mix, Z share, PSI/KL on key covariates; trip when a new source crosses a volume threshold and force stratified reporting.
8. **Intervention/deployment monitors** — registry of migrations, patches, policies, suppliers, partners, API incidents; auto-annotates all other panels and resets baselines one full matured cycle after each event.

**Alert dependencies (the anti-false-positive spine):**
- `data_invalid` (from 4)  suppress/downgrade all predictive & calibration alerts to informational; block retraining automation; page data engineering.
- `metrics_provisional` (from 3)  metric deltas cannot fire recalibration or retraining; route to a human review queue instead.
- `intervention_active` (from 8 overlapping window)  downgrade drift alarms; schedule baseline re-anchoring post-hoc.
- Only when all three flags are clear do standard thresholds escalate, and escalation follows a fixed ladder: *segment recalibration (cheap, reversible)  challenger evaluation (shadow)  retraining (requires fixed-vintage evidence + sign-off)*. A label-delay alarm therefore never reaches the retraining lane directly; it can only annotate dashboards and defer judgment.

```python
"""Monthly process-evidence extraction for the breach-monitor audit.

Auditable summaries only - no model fitting. Drift detection happens DOWNSTREAM
by comparing these snapshots month-over-month (TV-distance on variant shares,
CUSUM/EWMA on edge medians); PM4Py ships no off-the-shelf drift detector, so
none is invoked here. Assumes CSV columns: case_id, activity, time:timestamp.
"""
import pm4py
import pandas as pd

CSV_PATH = "field_service_events.csv"
WATCHED_EDGES = [("Order Part", "Part Received"),
                 ("Initial Diagnosis Completed", "Remote Fix")]

# -- 1) load -> NORMALISE the renamed diagnosis label FIRST -> deduplicate ---
raw = pd.read_csv(CSV_PATH)
raw["time:timestamp"] = pd.to_datetime(raw["time:timestamp"], errors="coerce")
raw["case:concept:name"] = raw["case_id"].astype(str)

# 2026-04-01 migration split one step into two spellings. Unify BEFORE any
# extraction, or prefixes fragment and V1/V3 variants artificially split.
raw["concept:name"] = (raw["activity"].astype(str).str.strip()
                       .replace({"Diagnostic Assessment Completed":
                                 "Initial Diagnosis Completed"}))

# Migration duplicated events; the 20-Apr fix covered only the NEW source, so
# clean uniformly here: collapse exact (case, activity, timestamp) repeats,
# keep earliest, and AUDIT the rate instead of discarding silently.
KEYS = ["case:concept:name", "concept:name", "time:timestamp"]
dup_rate = float(raw.duplicated(KEYS, keep="first").mean())
log_all = (raw.loc[~raw.duplicated(KEYS, keep="first")]
              .sort_values(KEYS).reset_index(drop=True))

log_all = pm4py.format_dataframe(log_all, case_id="case:concept:name",
                                 activity_key="concept:name",
                                 timestamp_key="time:timestamp")
case_start = log_all.groupby("case:concept:name")["time:timestamp"].min()

def variant_profile(lg):
    """Variant shares via pm4py.get_variants; mapped to the V1/V2/V3 taxonomy."""
    vd = pm4py.get_variants(lg)
    size = lambda v: len(v) if isinstance(v, (list, set, tuple)) else int(v)
    tot = sum(size(v) for v in vd.values())
    prof = dict(V1_standard=0.0, V2_remote=0.0, V3_parts=0.0, other=0.0)
    for vk, v in vd.items():
        s = str(vk)
        k = ("V2_remote"  if "Remote Fix" in s else
             "V3_parts"   if "Order Part" in s else
             "V1_standard" if "Repair Completed" in s else "other")
        prof[k] += (size(v) / tot) if tot else 0.0
    return {k: round(x, 3) for k, x in prof.items()}

def watched_edge_medians_h(lg):
    """Median hours on watched consecutive edges. Primary figure: transparent
    pandas computation; pm4py performance DFG called as a cross-check hook."""
    lg = lg.sort_values(["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp"], kind="mergesort")
    nxt_a = lg.groupby("case:concept:name")["concept:name"].shift(-1)
    nxt_t = lg.groupby("case:concept:name")["time:timestamp"].shift(-1)
    hrs = (nxt_t - lg["time:timestamp"]).dt.total_seconds() / 3600.0
    out = {}
    for a, b in WATCHED_EDGES:
        m = (lg["concept:name"].values == a) & (nxt_a.values == b)
        out[f"{a}->{b}"] = round(float(hrs[m].median()), 1) if m.any() else None
    try:                              # tolerated across pm4py versions;
        pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(lg)        # pandas stats above stand alone
        dfg, _, _ = pm4py.discover_dfg(lg)
        out["dfg_edges_n"] = len(dfg)             # structural fingerprint
    except Exception:
        pass
    return out

def temporal_bits(lg):
    try:
        tf = pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(lg)
        cols = [c for c in tf.columns if "duration" in c.lower()]
        if cols:
            return {"mean_case_duration_h":
                    round(float(tf[cols[0]].mean()) / 3600.0, 1)}
    except Exception:
        pass
    return {}

rows = []
for p in pd.period_range("2026-01", "2026-10", freq="M"):
    lo, hi = p.start_time, (p + 1).start_time

    # (a) EVENT-CALENDAR cohort: events falling inside the window; ongoing
    #     cases are truncated -> WORKLOAD view, sensitive to window borders.
    calendar = pm4py.filter_time_range(log_all, lo, hi, mode="events")

    # (b) CASE-START cohort: FULL traces of cases whose first event starts
    #     in-window -> INTAKE view; the one that matches the "Cases started"
    #     business table and the correct basis for outcome/label attachment.
    #     (Not equivalent to mode='traces_contained': those spill past borders.)
    cid = case_start.index[case_start.dt.to_period("M") == p]
    started = log_all[log_all["case:concept:name"].isin(cid)]

    for coh, lg in (("event_calendar", calendar), ("case_start", started)):
        r = dict(month=str(p), cohort=coh,
                 n_cases=lg["case:concept:name"].nunique(),
                 input_duplicate_rate_pct=round(dup_rate * 100, 2),
                 unknown_activity_pct=round(
                     lg["concept:name"].eq("UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY").mean() * 100, 2))
        r.update(watched_edge_medians_h(lg))
        r.update({f"share_{k}": v for k, v in variant_profile(lg).items()})
        r.update(temporal_bits(lg))
        rows.append(r)

monthly_process_evidence = (pd.DataFrame(rows)
                            .sort_values(["cohort", "month"])
                            .reset_index(drop=True))
monthly_process_evidence
```

Interpretation note: the two cohort views must agree in quiet months; a growing `event_calendar` vs `case_start` divergence is itself a censoring/backlog symptom (late-stage events accumulating), which feeds Monitor 3.

## 6. Risks of acting on the wrong diagnosis

1. **Retraining on immature labels (the October trap).** Mechanism: censored cohorts teach the model that long, drawn-out cases rarely breach — institutionalized survivorship bias that under-predicts exactly where money is lost. Mitigation: hard maturity gates for training eligibility, fixed-vintage (D+60/D+90) evaluation as a shipping criterion, and inverse-probability-of-censoring weights when later-life labels are admitted.
2. **Calibrating around the April bug.** Mechanism: a calibrator fitted on bugged-prefix scores memorizes an artifact; worse, it legitimizes skipping the root-cause fix and will recur at the next schema change. Mitigation: version/lineage stamps on every scoring record, mandatory replay verification before any recalibration window, schema contracts in CI, and an explicit quarantine list (Apr original features permanently ineligible).
3. **Dismissing genuine supplier-driven drift as "measurement noise."** Mechanism: the October deflation invites a complacent "metrics recovered" narrative while the parts edge sits at 155h; dispatch planning then starves V3 work, converting process pain into contractual breaches. Mitigation: keep the label-free performance-DFG monitors authoritative for operational claims — they cannot be fooled by label delay — and couple them to business KPIs (promised-date feasibility per variant).
4. **Attributing Partner Z's elevated breach to unfair model behavior (or unfairly to Z's operators) without stratified evidence.** Mechanism: with Z's labels the least mature and two predictive features absent, both accusations outrun the data; the wrong remedy (fairness patch, punitive ops review) damages trust and fixes nothing. Mitigation: mandate source × variant × maturity-age stratified reporting; require the Test 6 transport/reweighting result plus collection of distance/device-age features before any attribution; route conclusions through joint analytics–operations governance.
5. **Treating one knob as the cure — global recalibration for everything.** Mechanism: June's gap deserves recalibration, August's ranking damage does not; a global recalibrator flattens the legitimate V2 improvement while papering over the V3 signal, and delaying real feature/retrain work. Mitigation: bind each remediation action to a row of the diagnosis table, with the AUROC movement check as the fork (calibration-only when ranking survives; relearning when it does not), and cool-downs on automated triggers to prevent churn loops between the two.
