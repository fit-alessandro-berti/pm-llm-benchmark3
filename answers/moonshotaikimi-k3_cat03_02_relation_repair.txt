PK-8 packs two items *from two different orders* — the most unusual structure the repair creates; legitimate, but it is the main source of downstream entanglement. Each package has exactly one carrier in A/B; in C, DEL-X carries two (rule 4 bulk). No package orphans remain after A.

**Delivery.** DEL-X: dispatched, carrying PK-7, unconfirmed in A. DEL-Y is a *total orphan* in the raw log (zero events, zero O2O) — the strongest single argument for P1. After A it has a minimal dispatched-only lifecycle. In C it stays an orphan *deliberately*: an eventless object is tolerable as reference data; a fabricated lifecycle is not.

**Invoice.** INV-44: issued, bills ORD-A, settled by PAY-1 (100 of 230). Under A it remains partially settled — a legitimate open item, not a defect to be fixed.

**Payment.** PAY-1 complete. PAY-2: recorded, unapplied. ev14 is not an orphan event (it relates PAY-2); only its O2O neighborhood is empty, which rule 7 blesses.

**Orphan events.** ev08 (no package) — repaired in all bundles. ev10 (no delivery) — repaired in A/B, reinterpreted in C. ev11 (no delivery) — left as a legitimate order-level confirmation in A. ev14 (no invoice) — left as unapplied cash in A.

**Independence and circular reasoning.** Observed pairs (e.g., ev03's {ORD-A, IT-A1} alongside `ORD-A contains IT-A1`) are two recorded facts, though both likely stem from one scan, so even there independence is limited. Our repaired pairs (P1/P2, P3/P4) are one recorded fact plus a derived mirror: the O2O edge *cannot* corroborate the E2O edge, and confidences are capped accordingly rather than combined. We also reject the loop available in Bundle B — "ORD-B is on the invoice because it was delivered" plus "ORD-B was delivered because it is on the invoice"; P7/P8 rest on customer + amount + rule 6 only.

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects

**OC-DFG.** Bundle A is *topology-neutral*: no new activity nodes or edges per object type, only changed support counts — except one concrete new edge: in the Package fragment, PK-8 now yields `Pack Item  Pack Item` (ev07 09:04  ev08 09:05), a self-loop that a naive reader may misinterpret as rework. The Delivery fragment still shows only `Dispatch Package`, now supported by two deliveries instead of one. Bundle B adds two real edges: `Dispatch Package  Confirm Delivery` in the Delivery fragment, and `Add Item  Issue Invoice` in the Order fragment (via ORD-B); the Invoice fragment's `Record Payment` support goes from one payment to two. Bundle C gives DEL-X two same-timestamp dispatches, producing an ordering ambiguity or a `Dispatch  Dispatch` self-loop before `Confirm`.

**OTG.** Stable across the raw log and all bundles: Order–Item, Package–Item, Delivery–Package, Invoice–Order, Payment–Invoice all pre-exist. No repair introduces a new object-type pair. Schema-level robustness, instance-level change.

**ET–OT graph.** Unrepaired and Bundle A are identical at the type level (`Dispatch Package`–Delivery already exists via ev09). Bundle B (and C) add exactly one new edge: `Confirm Delivery`–Delivery. Nothing touches `Record Payment`–{Payment, Invoice} or `Create Order`–Order.

**Flattenings** (direct-E2O semantics; O2O-propagating variants noted below):
- *Order*: ORD-A = [Create, Add, Add, Confirm, Issue]; ORD-B = [Create, Add] — unchanged by A. Under B, ORD-B = [Create, Add, Issue], and ev12 is *duplicated* across both order cases.
- *Package*: PK-7 = [Pack, Dispatch]; PK-8 gains ev08 under A  [Pack, Pack, Dispatch].
- *Delivery*: DEL-Y is an *empty case* in the raw log and may vanish from the flattened log entirely; A gives it [Dispatch]; B gives both deliveries [Dispatch, Confirm] with ev11 duplicated across cases; C gives DEL-X [Dispatch, Dispatch, Confirm] and DEL-Y disappears.
- *Invoice*: [Issue, Record Payment]  under B/C [Issue, Record, Record].
- *Payment*: PAY-1 = [Record], PAY-2 = [Record] — **unchanged in every bundle**, because no repair touches the payment-side E2O of ev14. A useful stability check.

**Activity frequencies.** Event-based counts are invariant (14 events). Unique-object and total-incidence counts are not: `Dispatch Package` goes from 3 related objects to 4 (A); `Confirm Delivery` from 1 to 3 (B); `Record Payment`'s related invoices from 1 to 2 (B). Any dashboard computing "frequency by related objects" is silently inflated by repairs — a reason to keep the raw copy.

**Equivalent-execution clustering around Order.** Raw/A: two clearly separated variants ({C,A,A,Conf,Inv} vs {C,A}). Bundle B shrinks the distance (both orders now "invoiced"); if an O2O-propagating flattening is applied on top, the mixed package PK-8 and the shared ev11/ev12 pull pack/dispatch/confirm events into *both* order cases, and the two executions can collapse into one cluster — halving the apparent variant count and hiding the fact that ORD-B was never delivery-confirmed. Had P11 been accepted, the merge would be total. This sensitivity is the concrete reason provisionals stay out of the golden copy.

## 8. Over-repair warnings

1. **Arithmetic-only invoice repair (P7/P8).** Adding `INV-44 bills ORD-B` because 130+100=230 merges the two order variants; equivalent-execution clustering collapses them, and constraints like "every invoiced order was confirmed" begin passing spuriously for ORD-B.
2. **Unique assignment of ev11.** Forcing ev11 onto DEL-X (or onto both deliveries) manufactures carrier confirmations; the Delivery OC-DFG gains a `Dispatch  Confirm` edge and lead-time KPIs are computed from timestamps the portal never reliably recorded.
3. **Unflagged mixed package (P3/P4).** Under O2O-propagating flattening, ev07/ev08/ev10 duplicate into *both* order cases; handover and resource views attribute ORD-B's item to ORD-A's logistics flow, and the Package fragment's new `Pack  Pack` self-loop reads as rework that never happened.
4. **Settling PAY-2 to INV-44.** The invoice appears fully paid; dunning and accounts-receivable mining lose a genuine open item; cash-application rates are inflated.
5. **"Hedging" by adding both carriers.** Keeping `DEL-Y carries PK-8` *and* `DEL-X carries PK-8` gives PK-8 two carriers — a cardinality violation that then invites further fabricated repairs (split shipments that never occurred).
6. **Merging ev09/ev10 on timestamp equality.** Treating the two 10:00 events as one bulk dispatch erases the `N-421` evidence, changes the `Dispatch Package` event count from 2 to 1, and pre-decides Bundle C without admitting it.
7. **"Fixing" unusual-but-legitimate rows.** Deleting ev08 as noise orphans IT-B1 and understates `Pack Item`; forcing DEL-Y, PAY-2, or ORD-B into complete lifecycles converts missingness into fiction — the exact failure mode rule 10 and the task constraints prohibit.
8. **Symmetrizing O2O.** Adding reverse edges (e.g., `PK-7 carried-by DEL-X`) doubles OTG edge weights and makes some exporters emit both directions as independent facts.
