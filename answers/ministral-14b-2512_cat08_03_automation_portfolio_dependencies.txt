## **2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map**

### **Upstream Data Quality Dependencies**
- **A2 (Classification), A3 (KYC), A6 (Normalization), A8 (Committee Pack), A9 (Contract Gen)** all require clean input data.
  - Example: **A6** fails if financial statements are scanned poorly; **A2** misroutes if product fields are incomplete.
  - **Mitigation**: Prioritize **A5 (Missing-Document Nudging)** to improve data quality before automation.

### **Legal/Compliance Controls**
- **Four-eyes principle**: **A8 (Committee Pack)** and **A9 (Contract Gen)** must not bypass human review for High-exposure SMEs.
  - **Risk**: Automated packing (**A8**) could enable same-person approvals if not gated.
  - **Mitigation**: Enforce **human-in-the-loop** for High-exposure cases.
- **KYC clearance before signing**: **A10 (E-Sign)** must verify KYC status; **A4 (Entity Mismatch)** must block signing if unresolved.
- **Contract-remediation loops**: **A9** and **A12** must integrate to flag mismatches early.

### **Human Escalation Capacity**
- **Compliance specialists (3 FTE)** are a bottleneck for **A3 (KYC Pre-Classifier)** and **A4 (Entity Mismatch)**.
  - **Risk**: False positives in **A3** could overload specialists.
  - **Mitigation**: Start with **A4** (lower volume but higher impact) before **A3**.
- **Credit analysts (9 FTE)** are central; **A6 (Normalization)** must not overload them.
  - **Risk**: If normalization fails, analysts spend more time fixing.
  - **Mitigation**: Pilot **A6** with a small batch first.

### **External Valuation Dependency**
- **A7 (Collateral Triage)** depends on external valuers.
  - **Risk**: Partner delays could still block committee packaging.
  - **Mitigation**: Use **A7** to parallelize requests but not eliminate human oversight.

### **Core-Banking Integration Constraints**
- Only **2 modules** can integrate deeply in 6 months.
  - **Top priorities for integration**:
    1. **A4 (Entity Mismatch)** – Critical path for 82% of cases.
    2. **A6 (Normalization)** – Feeds credit analysis and committee packaging.
  - **Secondary candidates**: **A5 (Missing Docs)** or **A9 (Contract Gen)**.

## **3. Portfolio Alternatives**

### **A. Quick-Win Portfolio (Optimizes Labor Savings & Political Appeal)**
**Modules**: **A1 (Auto-Registration)**, **A5 (Missing-Doc Nudging)**, **A10 (E-Sign)**, **A2 (Classification Recommender)**
- **Optimizes**:
  - High-frequency, low-risk automations.
  - Visible back-office efficiency gains (12k + 3.3k + 7.3k cases).
- **Sacrifices**:
  - **No major bottleneck reduction** (e.g., KYC, collateral waits remain).
  - **No compliance strengthening** (only **A2** has minor control benefits).
- **Risk**: May expose **KYC/credit bottlenecks** without addressing them.

### **B. Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (Optimizes Cycle Time & Rework)**
**Modules**: **A4 (Entity Mismatch)**, **A6 (Normalization)**, **A7 (Collateral Triage)**, **A5 (Missing-Doc Nudging)**
- **Optimizes**:
  - **Critical path delays**:
    - **A4** reduces **Resolve Entity Mismatch** (31h wait  ?).
    - **A6** reduces **Normalize Financials** (20h wait  ?).
    - **A7** parallelizes **Wait for External Valuation** (58h  ?).
  - **Rework reduction**: **A5** cuts **Request Missing Docs** loops (52% repeat rate).
- **Sacrifices**:
  - **Lower direct labor savings** than Quick-Win (focuses on high-impact, not high-frequency).
  - **Higher integration complexity** (especially **A4** and **A6**).
- **Risk**: **A7** depends on external valuers; **A6** may fail on poor PDFs.

### **C. Compliance-First Portfolio (Optimizes Control Strengthening & Risk Reduction)**
**Modules**: **A3 (KYC Pre-Classifier)**, **A4 (Entity Mismatch)**, **A9 (Contract Gen)**, **A12 (Control Remediation)**
- **Optimizes**:
  - **Reduces control violations**:
    - **A3/A4** prevent KYC-related signing delays (6.5% provisional signing risk).
    - **A9** reduces contract-remediation loops (8.5%  ?).
    - **A12** improves remediation efficiency.
  - **Auditability**: All modules require clear trails (compliance mandate).
- **Sacrifices**:
  - **Lower immediate labor savings** (focuses on risk, not volume).
  - **Higher false-positive/negative risk** (e.g., **A3**’s KYC hits).
- **Risk**: **A3** could overload compliance specialists if not tuned carefully.

## **6. Recommended Phased Rollout**

### **Phase 1 (Months 1–6): Core-Banking Integration Focus**
**Modules**: **A4 (Entity Mismatch)**, **A6 (Normalization)**
- **Order**:
  1. **A4 (Entity Mismatch)** – Highest impact on critical path (82% of cases).
     - **Monitor**: Reduction in **Resolve Entity Mismatch** waiting time (target: >50%).
     - **Gate**: False-negative rate <1% (audit trail required).
  2. **A6 (Normalization)** – Feeds credit analysis; must integrate with core banking.
     - **Monitor**: Reduction in **Normalize Financials** time (target: >40%).
     - **Gate**: Manual override rate <5% (indicates model confidence).

**Supporting Module**: **A5 (Missing-Doc Nudging)** (non-integration, high feasibility)
- **Pilot**: 10% of cases to test customer friction.
- **Monitor**: Reduction in **Request Missing Docs** loops (target: >30%).

### **Phase 2 (Months 7–12): Compliance & Bottleneck Expansion**
**Modules**: **A3 (KYC Pre-Classifier)**, **A9 (Contract Gen)**
- **Order**:
  1. **A3 (KYC Pre-Classifier)** – Must be tuned carefully to avoid false negatives.
     - **Monitor**: Reduction in **KYC Check** time (target: >30%).
     - **Gate**: False-negative rate <0.5% (compliance approval required).
  2. **A9 (Contract Gen)** – Reduces remediation loops.
     - **Monitor**: Reduction in **Contract Remediation** (target: >50%).
     - **Gate**: Legal approval of rule library; zero contract-remediation escalations.

**Optional Add-On**: **A7 (Collateral Triage)** (if external valuer partnerships are stable)
- **Monitor**: Reduction in **Wait for External Valuation** time (target: >20%).

## **7. Final Recommendation: **Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (A4, A6, A7, A5)**

### **Why Not Automate High-Frequency First?**
- **Frequency  Impact**: **Register Application (A1)** is high-frequency but has **no downstream bottlenecks**. Automating it first would:
  - Free up back-office capacity but **not reduce cycle time** (which is dominated by KYC, credit, and collateral waits).
  - Risk **exposing hidden bottlenecks** (e.g., if KYC/credit are already overloaded).
- **Process Mining Insights**:
  - **Resolve Entity Mismatch (1.75k cases)** has a **31h wait time** and blocks **82% of cases** on the critical path.
  - **Wait for External Valuation (2.4k cases)** has a **58h wait** and is a **synchronization point** for committee packaging.
  - **Financial Document Review (9.2k cases)** has a **24% rework rate**, causing loops that delay credit analysis.

### **Why the Bottleneck Portfolio?**
1. **Targets the Longest Waits**:
   - **A4** reduces the **31h entity mismatch delay** (critical for signing).
   - **A6** cuts the **20h financial normalization wait** (feeds credit analysis).
   - **A7** parallelizes **58h external valuation waits** (uncontrollable but can be triaged).
2. **Reduces Rework Loops**:
   - **A5** cuts **52% repeated missing-docs requests**, improving financial review efficiency.
3. **Balances Risk & Reward**:
   - **A4** and **A6** have **medium-high feasibility** but **high impact**.
   - **A7** is **medium feasibility** but addresses an **external dependency**.
   - Avoids **compliance-first overkill** (unlike Portfolio C) while still improving controls indirectly.
4. **Aligns with Core-Banking Constraints**:
   - **A4** and **A6** are the **top two integration priorities** for Phase 1.
5. **Political Feasibility**:
   - While not the **highest-frequency** wins, it delivers **visible cycle-time reductions** (30–40%) and **reduces rework**, which is more compelling to senior management than incremental labor savings.

### **Trade-Off Justification**
- **Sacrifice**: Lower direct labor savings than **Quick-Win (A1, A5, A10, A2)**.
- **Gain**: **30–40% faster cycle times**, **reduced rework**, and **improved resource balance** (critical for compliance and credit teams).
- **Defensible Argument**:
  > *"Automating the highest-frequency activity (e.g., registration) would optimize for labor savings but ignore the process-mining evidence that **waiting times and rework** dominate end-to-end performance. The bottleneck portfolio targets the **longest delays** (KYC, credit, collateral) and **high-rework activities** (missing docs, entity mismatches), delivering **greater cycle-time improvements** while maintaining compliance. This aligns with the bank’s goal of **automating for performance**, not just efficiency."*
