## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

**Facts.** GPIP-4 applies throughout. The Aachen SOP predates it, but A5 preserves compatible local controls. ECD-2 was signed by the COO and Incident Commander for a specified incident, purpose and period. No Chief Risk Officer (CRO) exception or ratification is evidenced. The CFO can interpret Finance controls. Neither executive's status, the CRO's unavailability, nor past use of incident directives establishes a general power to dispense with board controls.

**Interpretive assumptions.** I use the first act that bound Northbridge to the supplier as the commitment. All times below are CEST. I test site, purpose, amount, supplier status and authority at that act. “Approved” describes register status and “new” describes the relationship. One does not settle the other. I apply the new-supplier trigger where newness is stated or evidenced. For ECD-2 I require an operational link to containment and a credible need to procure the lot during the incident. Need within 24 hours is strong evidence, but C6 supplies no binding 24-hour cutoff. Continued recovery after expiry does not extend the window.

I give a later, more specific instruction effect on the issue its issuer can govern. I treat C1–C4 as an authorized local change to PO sequence and register eligibility under B7, subject to C3. I treat D as prospective from receipt, presumed at issue time on July 15, and confined to purchases covered by C. Its authority is strongest on how Finance approval can be given or evidenced. On my reading it does not appoint every Incident Command (IC) member a Finance Controller or independent budget owner. A single joint authorization may discharge several duties if the signers actually hold the necessary functions and exercise them for this purchase. A requester and committer may be the same person. Under A1 neither may supply a final business approval, including as a necessary co-approver.

I read B2 as requiring both local approvals without independently specifying their deadline. A2 imposes an explicit earlier deadline where its threshold is met. B4 separately permits PO deferral and, under narrower conditions, substitution for the Plant Manager. I apply B6 when the local emergency procedure is invoked. For a purchase relying solely on ECD-2, I apply C3's incident record requirement. A cumulative reading of B6 would require additional proof of a local form or accepted equivalent. I measure C3's four hours from the authorization, the earlier and more protective starting point. A record can fall due after the directive expires for a commitment made within it.

**Decision and proof.** Ordinary compliance requires support for applicable controls. JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION denotes supported use of an authorized departure with the remaining material controls satisfied. The party invoking a departure bears the burden on its scope, time and conditions. If that showing fails, I test the ordinary rule. NONCOMPLIANT requires a material departure established on balance. INDETERMINATE means a live factual issue prevents deciding whether a material control operated. I give role-labelled events ordinary evidential credit absent a specific conflict, but an IC title alone does not establish either A2 capacity. A missing event prompts inquiry. A positively timed late approval is stronger adverse evidence. A6 allows mitigation without rewriting the sequence. Severity concerns the control exposure, and confidence concerns the classification on this record.

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency and Remediation

**Policy breach** concerns the real sequence, as with P-502's evidenced late clearances. **Late evidence of a timely act** includes a contemporaneous signed authorization uploaded later. A **logging defect** exists if a system misstates the action time or misses a required posting deadline. P-507's delayed visibility does not itself establish such a deadline was breached. **Inability to prove compliance** concerns a missing material fact, such as the budget mandates in P-507 and P-508. Log time, document creation, verbal decision, supplier acceptance and later ratification should each retain their own timestamp.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Proposed clause, prospective.** GPIP-4 governs all spend. Compatible site controls apply in addition. A COO and Incident Commander directive may temporarily change PO sequence and register eligibility for a named incident, site, line and set of lots. Record the start and expiry with time zone. A covered lot must be needed for containment within 24 hours, or Engineering must document before commitment why ordering a backup lot now is necessary given stock, lead time and the containment plan. Two distinct IC members must authorize before binding commitment.

At Aachen, purchases of EUR 25,000 or more require Plant and Finance approval before commitment. At EUR 50,000 or more, an independent budget owner and a distinct Finance Controller must both approve before commitment. A single instrument may carry all required approvals if it identifies each signer's active mandate and capacity. Neither requester nor committer may give final business approval, alone or jointly. Purchases above EUR 100,000 and purchases from any new supplier require prior Compliance clearance, including when off-register sourcing is permitted. Incident purchases above EUR 150,000 also require prior Legal clearance. The CFO may specify Finance evidence. Any relaxation of a substantive board control requires a written CRO exception naming the clause, lots, financial limit, compensating controls and term before commitment. Final-approval independence and new-supplier Compliance clearance are nonwaivable.

Record verbal IC decisions within four hours of authorization in a signed, time-stamped incident record. Complete the site emergency form within 24 hours of commitment. A single record may fulfill both duties if it contains the required information by each deadline. Preserve original evidence of decision, binding acceptance and creation time during outages and link it to the later upload. A later sign-off is recorded at its actual time. Authority expires at the stated instant for new commitments. Renewal requires a newly signed prospective instrument from the competent issuer. Complete documentation for covered commitments even if its deadline falls after expiry. On closure, independently review all exceptions and repeated emergency sourcing before authorizing any recurrence.
