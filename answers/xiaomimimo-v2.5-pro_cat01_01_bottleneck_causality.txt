## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Unobserved case difficulty within complexity strata.** Even after controlling for Low/Medium/High, the routing engine may use features (claim type, policy tier, customer history) that create within-stratum variation in difficulty correlated with group assignment. *Most valuable addition:* a continuous case-risk score from the routing engine or an independent underwriter assessment, recorded at the point of classification.

2. **Separation of resource-group, shift, and queue-state effects.** The near-perfect confounding of Orion with night shift for medium- and high-complexity cases means the current data cannot distinguish a reviewer-skill effect from a shift-queue-dynamics effect. *Most valuable addition:* timestamped queue-entry and queue-exit events for each case, enabling reconstruction of queue depth at arrival and separation of waiting time from service time.

3. **Selection bias from the completed-case-only sample.** If difficult cases assigned to Nova are disproportionately abandoned or left open, the completed-case comparison overstates Nova's performance or understates Orion's advantage within strata. *Most valuable addition:* a complete case registry including abandoned and still-open cases with their resource group, complexity, and abandonment/last-activity timestamp.

4. **Routing-engine decision logic.** We do not know the full set of inputs the routing engine considers beyond observed complexity. If the engine uses unobserved features correlated with cycle time, the propensity model is misspecified and all observational adjustments are biased. *Most valuable addition:* a routing decision log recording every input feature and the assignment rule applied to each case.

5. **Time-varying queue congestion and adaptive routing.** The static cross-sectional data cannot capture within-session queue dynamics (earlier cases affecting later cases' waits) or possible adaptive routing (engine adjusting assignments based on current queue depth). These dynamics could create feedback loops that bias any comparison based on aggregate statistics. *Most valuable addition:* high-frequency queue-length snapshots (e.g., every 15 minutes) for each resource group, enabling time-series reconstruction of queue state at each case's arrival and departure.
