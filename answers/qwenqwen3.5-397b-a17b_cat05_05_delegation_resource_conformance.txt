2.  **Principal Availability:** Integrate HR leave systems with QMS. If a Principal is on leave, system should lock their account or require explicit "Return from Leave" confirmation before allowing approvals.

6.  **Pair Rotation:** Implement mandatory rotation for Draft/Approve pairs in high-risk cells to reduce collusion risk (addressing Clara/Ben frequency).
7.  **Subdelegation Logic:** Systematically enforce "No Subdelegation" flags in delegation records. Prevent creation of D-IC-04 if D-IC-03 prohibits it.
8.  **Evidential Retention:** Store session metadata (IP, Workstation, Badge ID) alongside transaction logs for service account actions to enable forensic attribution.
