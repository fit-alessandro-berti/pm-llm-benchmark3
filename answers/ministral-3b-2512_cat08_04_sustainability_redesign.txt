# **Sustainable Process Redesign for Industrial Spare-Parts Distribution**

---

## **1. System Boundary and Assumptions**
### **Environmental Scope**
- **Included metrics**:
  - Direct energy consumption (kWh/order) from CNC/heat treatment machines.
  - Transport emissions (kgCOe/order) via parcel, ground, air, dedicated van, or consolidated truck.
  - Material waste (kg scrap/order) from rework, packaging rework, or obsolescence.
  - Service-related metrics: cycle time, late deliveries, rework rates, and resilience (alternative routes).
- **Excluded**:
  - Supply chain upstream (material sourcing, supplier emissions).
  - End-of-life recycling of spare parts (assumed negligible impact).
  - Customer behavior shifts (e.g., order volume changes) beyond process-level adjustments.
- **Assumptions**:
  - Batching and transport decisions are optimized per order segment (Standard-stock, Made-to-order, Critical-downtime) and customer type (Regular/Service-contract).
  - Rework and obsolescence are treated as process inefficiencies to be mitigated, not as inherent trade-offs.
  - Promised lead time is probabilistic (SP1 compliance requires 85% probability of meeting delivery).
  - Dedicated vans and express couriers are justified only for Critical-downtime or Service-contract cases with explicit risk mitigation.

---

## **2. Sustainability-Related Process Indicators**
| **Category**       | **Indicator**                          | **Formula/Definition**                                                                 | **Target Range**                          |
|--------------------|----------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Energy**         | Energy per item (kWh/item)             | `Total energy_kwh / batch_size` (for production batches)                           | 1.5 kWh/item (CNC_A), 2.0 kWh/item (CNC_B) |
|                    | Energy per order (kWh/order)           | `Total energy_kwh / order_count`                                                      | 10 kWh/order (MTO), 1.5 kWh/order (Standard-stock) |
| **Transport**      | Transport emissions per kg (kgCOe/kg)| `transport_kgCOe / (order_weight_kg)`                                          | 0.5 kgCOe/kg (consolidated truck)      |
|                    | Transport mode share (by segment)      | % of orders shipped by parcel_ground, parcel_air, dedicated_van, consolidated_truck | 80% ground/consolidated for Standard-stock Service-contract |
| **Materials**      | Material waste per order (kg)          | `material_scrap_kg + package_material_kg`                                          | 0.1 kg/order (Standard-stock)            |
|                    | Rework rate (%)                        | `rework_flag_count / total_orders`                                                 | 5% (Standard-stock), 8% (MTO)          |
| **Service**        | Cycle time (hours)                     | Median/90th percentile of `promised_lead_time_hours`                              | 24h (Critical-downtime), 72h (MTO)     |
|                    | Late delivery rate (%)                | `Late_delivery_count / total_orders`                                               | 10% (Standard-stock), 20% (MTO)        |
| **Resilience**     | Alternative route availability          | % of Service-contract orders with 1 viable backup route (e.g., stock + express)   | 90% coverage                          |
| **Quality**        | Defect rate (rework/returns)           | `Rework_flag_count + (returns_implied_from_package_rework) / total_orders`         | 12% (MTO)                               |

## **3. Intervention Options**
### **S1. Green Shipping Recommender (SP1/SP2 Compliance)**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Standard-stock Service-contract (70% of ground/air violations) and Made-to-order Regular (high transport emissions).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Dynamic routing**: Use a rule-based system to default to ground + priority picking if promised lead time has 85% probability of meeting (SP1).
  - **Consolidation for non-urgent stock**: Enforce S5 (twice-daily consolidated truck departures) for Standard-stock Service-contract orders with slack >24h.
  - **Air transport justification**: Only allow parcel_air for Critical-downtime or Service-contract cases with explicit risk mitigation (e.g., dedicated van fallback).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Energy**: Reduce air transport share from 5% to 2% (Standard-stock Service-contract).
  - **Late deliveries**: Minimal impact (SP1 compliance avoids delays; consolidation adds 12–24h wait but reduces emissions).
  - **Rework**: No direct effect; reduces packaging rework by improving route compatibility.
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - Event-log tracking of `expedite_flag` and `promised_lead_time_hours` violations.
  - Transport mode share by segment (compare pre/post consolidation).

---

### **S2. Dynamic Batching with Waiting-Time Caps**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Made-to-order (MTO) cases on CNC_B or heat treatment (high rework/late-delivery risk).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **CNC_A preference**: Shift MTO from CNC_B to CNC_A when queue slack >48h and batch_size 12 (reduces energy per item by 50%).
  - **Batch size limits**: Cap batch_size at 12–20 for CNC_A (reduces energy per item to 1.3 kWh/item) and enforce batch wait 38h (median).
  - **Heat treatment**: Batch size 10 to minimize energy per item, but enforce batch wait 72h to avoid late-delivery risk.
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Energy**: 30–50% reduction in MTO production energy (CNC_A vs. CNC_B).
  - **Late deliveries**: Risk of 1.7x increase if batch wait exceeds 72h (mitigate with alternative routes).
  - **Rework**: CNC_A rework rate drops from 18% to 9% (vs. CNC_B).
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - Batch_size distribution by machine_group and segment.
  - Rework_flag and `promised_lead_time_hours` violations tied to batch wait.

---

### **S3. Inspection Quality Gates for High-Risk Paths**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Made-to-order parts >8 kg (high rework risk) or CNC_B/heat treatment queues.
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Pre-inspection gate**: Add a "defect detection" step before packaging for MTO >8 kg, using inspection_cell data to flag high-risk batches.
  - **Automated rework routing**: If inspection_cell queue length >24h, reroute to CNC_A for immediate inspection (reduces rework loops).
  - **Packaging compatibility**: Enforce Q1 (reinforced packaging for MTO >8 kg) only after inspection certifies low damage risk.
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Rework**: Reduce from 18% to 12% (CNC_B  CNC_A + inspection gate).
  - **Cycle time**: Add 8–12h inspection wait but reduces rework-production loops.
  - **Material waste**: Minimize packaging rework by 30% (via compatibility checks).
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - Inspection_cell queue lengths and rework_flag correlation with batch_size/machine_group.

---

### **S4. Carbon-Aware Promise-Date Negotiation**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Made-to-order Regular (high energy/transport emissions, but flexible promised lead time).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Negotiated promises**: For MTO Regular, allow carbon-aware promise dates (e.g., extend lead time by 24–48h to switch from air to ground).
  - **Dynamic routing**: Use S1’s green shipping recommender to align transport mode with negotiated promises.
  - **Customer communication**: Transparent carbon footprint disclosure (e.g., "Switching to ground adds 24h but reduces emissions by 90%").
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Energy**: Reduce air transport share from 6% to 1% (MTO Regular).
  - **Cycle time**: Add 24–48h wait but avoids late deliveries (customer accepts).
  - **Resilience**: No direct impact; ensures Service-contract customers remain unaffected.
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - `promised_lead_time_hours` adjustments and carbon footprint tracking per order.

---

### **S5. Consolidated Truck Departures (S5 + S1)**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Standard-stock Service-contract (high transport emissions, low rework).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Twice-daily consolidation**: Group Standard-stock Service-contract orders into 2–3 consolidated truck departures (reduces transport kgCOe by 50%).
  - **Priority picking**: Enforce S1’s ground mode for orders with 85% probability of meeting promised lead time.
  - **Slack-based routing**: Ship consolidated trucks only if promised lead time has slack >24h.
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Transport**: Reduce from 7.8 kgCOe/order to 3.5 kgCOe/order.
  - **Cycle time**: Add 12–24h wait but avoids air transport delays.
  - **Resilience**: No impact; consolidation routes are pre-planned.
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - Transport mode share by consolidation window and segment.

---

### **S6. Packaging-Route Compatibility Checks**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Partial shipments or MTO orders with mixed packaging routes (e.g., standard vs. reinforced).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Pre-shipment validation**: Use event-log attributes (`package_material_kg`, `route_mode`) to flag incompatible combinations (e.g., reinforced packaging + parcel_air).
  - **Automatic rework**: If compatibility fails, reroute to rework packaging (reduces damage-related returns by 40%).
  - **Default to standard**: For Standard-stock, default to standard packaging unless customer requests reinforced (Q1 compliance).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Material waste**: Reduce packaging rework from 260 to 100 cases/year.
  - **Rework**: Minimize returns by 40% (MTO heavy parts).
  - **Cycle time**: Add 3–6h for compatibility checks.
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - `Rework_flag` tied to packaging_route_mode mismatches.

---

### **S7. Dedicated Van Optimization (S8)**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Critical-downtime Service-contract (high transport emissions, but justified by risk).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Shared lanes**: Replace 30% of dedicated vans with shared emergency courier lanes (e.g., DHL Express) for non-Critical-downtime cases.
  - **Justification logs**: Require explicit SP2 justification for remaining dedicated vans (e.g., "Downtime risk >10% per 24h").
  - **Route consolidation**: Group Critical-downtime vans into 2–3 daily departures (reduces empty returns by 30%).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Transport**: Reduce from 52 kgCOe/order to 25 kgCOe/order.
  - **Cycle time**: No impact; vans remain fast.
  - **Resilience**: Maintain Critical-downtime service levels (dedicated lanes for high-risk cases).
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - Transport mode share by Critical-downtime justification logs.

---

### **S8. Critical-Downtime Segregation (S9)**
- **Target path/segment**:
  Made-to-order Critical-downtime (high transport emissions, but low rework).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **Precautionary vs. true downtime**:
    - **True downtime**: Justified by SP2 (e.g., "Machine failure risk >50% per 24h").
    - **Precautionary**: Default to ground + priority picking unless true downtime applies.
  - **Energy/transport trade-off**: For precautionary cases, use ground + consolidation (38 kgCOe/order) instead of dedicated van (52 kgCOe/order).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience effects**:
  - **Transport**: Reduce from 38 kgCOe/order to 20 kgCOe/order (precautionary cases).
  - **Late deliveries**: No impact; true downtime cases remain fast.
  - **Resilience**: Maintain Critical-downtime service levels for true cases.
- **Monitoring evidence**:
  - `expedite_flag` and `Critical-downtime` justification logs.

## **5. Rebound and Displacement Risks**
1. **Batching rebound**:
   - **Risk**: Larger batches (S2) may increase obsolescence if demand forecasts are wrong (6% of batch-produced items become obsolete).
   - **Mitigation**: Enforce batch_size caps (12–20) and obsolescence tracking (e.g., "Not shipped within 14 days" flag).

2. **Transport displacement**:
   - **Risk**: Consolidation (S5) may reduce ground transport efficiency if orders are too small to consolidate (e.g., <50 kg).
   - **Mitigation**: Dynamic consolidation thresholds (e.g., 100 kg per truck).

3. **Packaging displacement**:
   - **Risk**: Reinforced packaging (Q1) may increase material waste if not needed (e.g., Standard-stock light parts).
   - **Mitigation**: Pre-inspection gate (S3) to avoid over-packaging.

4. **Rework displacement**:
   - **Risk**: Inspection gates (S3) may increase cycle time, causing Service-contract customers to demand expedited routes.
   - **Mitigation**: Resilience policy R1 ensures alternative routes (e.g., express courier) remain available.

5. **Customer behavior displacement**:
   - **Risk**: Carbon-aware promises (S4) may reduce order volume if customers perceive delays as unacceptable.
   - **Mitigation**: Transparent carbon footprint disclosure and flexible negotiation windows.

6. **Energy efficiency trade-offs**:
   - **Risk**: CNC_A (S2) has lower energy per item but higher batch wait, increasing waiting times for some segments.
   - **Mitigation**: Segment-specific batching rules (e.g., prioritize MTO Regular over Standard-stock).

## **6. Balanced Recommendation**
### **Redesign Package**
**Core Components**:
1. **S1 + S5 (Green Shipping + Consolidation)**:
   - Default to ground + priority picking for Standard-stock Service-contract (SP1 compliance).
   - Enforce twice-daily consolidated truck departures for non-urgent stock orders.
   - Justify air transport only for Critical-downtime or Service-contract cases with explicit risk mitigation.

2. **S2 + S3 (Dynamic Batching + Inspection Gates)**:
   - Shift Made-to-order from CNC_B to CNC_A when queue slack allows (reduces rework by 15–25%).
   - Add pre-inspection gates for MTO >8 kg to minimize packaging rework and returns.

3. **S4 (Carbon-Aware Promises)**:
   - Negotiate extended promised lead times for MTO Regular to switch from air to ground transport.
   - Transparent carbon footprint disclosure to align customer expectations.

4. **S6 + S8 (Packaging Checks + Dedicated Van Optimization)**:
   - Enforce packaging-route compatibility checks to reduce rework packaging.
   - Replace 30% of dedicated vans with shared emergency courier lanes for non-Critical-downtime cases.

### **Why This Balances Trade-offs**
| **Priority**       | **Environmental Gain**                          | **Service/Quality Trade-offs**                          | **Resilience Mitigations**                          |
|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| **Transport**      | 40–50% reduction in kgCOe/order (S1 + S5)   | Minimal: Consolidation adds 12–24h wait; SP1 compliance avoids late deliveries. | Backup routes for Service-contract (R1).           |
| **Production**      | 30–40% reduction in energy per item (S2)      | Rework rate drops from 18% to 9% (CNC_A preference).  | Dynamic batching respects promised lead time slack. |
| **Packaging**      | 30–40% reduction in material waste (S6)      | Cycle time increases by 3–12h; returns drop by 40%.    | Pre-inspection gates avoid over-packaging.         |
| **Critical-downtime** | 30–40% reduction in transport emissions (S8) | True downtime cases remain fast; precautionary cases shift to ground. | Dedicated lanes for high-risk cases.              |

### **Key Trade-off Management**
- **Batching vs. Obsolescence**: Enforce batch_size caps (12–20) and track obsolescence to avoid hidden waste.
- **Cycle Time vs. Rework**: Inspection gates (S3) add 8–12h but reduce rework by 15–25%.
- **Transport Efficiency vs. Customer Acceptance**: Carbon-aware promises (S4) extend lead times but avoid late deliveries.

## **7. Validation and Governance Plan**
### **Conformance Monitoring**
| **Policy** | **Monitoring Metric**                          | **Threshold**               | **Alert Trigger**                     |
|------------|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------|
| SP1        | % of Standard-stock Service-contract orders shipped by ground/consolidated truck | 85%                        | Violations >10% (auto-escalate).      |
| SP2        | Justification logs for dedicated vans         | All cases documented         | Missing logs >5% (audit).             |
| Q1         | Reinforced packaging for MTO >8 kg           | 100% compliance              | Violations >2% (inspection gate trigger). |
| R1         | Alternative routes for Service-contract        | 90% coverage                | Gaps >15% (backup route activation).   |

### **Segment-Level Service Monitoring**
- **Daily**:
  - Transport mode share by segment (compare to S1/S5 targets).
  - Rework_flag and `promised_lead_time_hours` violations.
- **Weekly**:
  - Batch_size distribution and obsolescence rates.
  - Inspection_cell queue lengths and inspection gate effectiveness.
- **Monthly**:
  - Carbon footprint per order segment.
  - Customer satisfaction (e.g., late-delivery complaints).

### **Exception Governance**
- **Sustainability Exception Log**:
  - Manual overrides for SP1/SP2/Q1 violations (e.g., "Critical-downtime case requires air transport").
  - Justification required for all exceptions (e.g., "Downtime risk >50% per 24h").
- **Rebound Risk Review**:
  - Quarterly review of displacement risks (e.g., obsolescence, customer behavior shifts).
  - Adjust batching/consolidation thresholds if trade-offs exceed acceptable limits.

### **Validation Framework**
1. **Process-Mining Validation**:
   - Compare pre/post-intervention event logs for changes in:
     - Transport mode share (S1/S5).
     - Batch_size distribution (S2).
     - Rework_flag and `package_material_kg` (S3/S6).
   - Use DFG to identify new paths (e.g., inspection gates creating new production routes).
2. **Performance Benchmarking**:
   - Compare sustainability indicators (energy/transport/material waste) against segment targets.
   - Validate resilience by checking alternative route coverage (R1).
3. **Customer Validation**:
   - Survey Service-contract customers on perceived delays (e.g., "Did you accept the extended promised lead time?").
   - Track return/rework rates for MTO heavy parts (S3/S6).

---
**Final Note**: This redesign prioritizes **segment-specific optimization** (e.g., Standard-stock vs. MTO) and **proactive risk mitigation** (e.g., inspection gates, justification logs). It avoids low-hanging fruit (e.g., eliminating all air transport) in favor of **balanced trade-offs** that align with resilience and quality policies.
