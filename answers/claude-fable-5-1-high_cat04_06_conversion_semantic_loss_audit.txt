# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The source of truth is the `source_powl` object, not the prose. Where the prose is silent, the table records what the object says and flags the gap. Abbreviations: R = Register Change, AR = Assess Risk, PE = Prepare Evidence, LR = Legal Review, CT = Configure Trial, IT = Inspect Trial, CD = Correct Defect, RC = Release Change, AC = Archive Change. "Observable after hiding tau?" asks whether the invariant can be decided from visible traces plus an exact final-marking check, i.e. whether a trace-based validator can ever establish it.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | R is the first visible event and occurs exactly once (edges R→AR, R→PE; every other node is reachable only through AR/PE). | `R AR PE CT IT RC AC` | `AR R PE CT IT RC AC` | Yes (position of R in every trace). |
| I2 | AR and PE are unordered: both `AR PE` and `PE AR` are admitted, each exactly once. | `R PE AR CT IT RC AC` | any trace with AR twice, or a model that admits only one order | Only as interleaving set: both orders must be accepted. Whether they are *concurrent* (independent) or merely *either-or ordered* is not observable from traces. |
| I3 | LR is optional (XOR with silent skip): 0 or 1 occurrence, never 2. | `R AR PE CT IT RC AC` (0) and `R AR LR PE CT IT RC AC` (1) | `R AR LR LR PE CT IT RC AC` | Yes for count; the skip itself is invisible, so absence of LR is the only evidence of the tau branch. |
| I4 | LR, if present, occurs after AR (edge AR→optional_legal). | `R AR LR PE CT IT RC AC` | `R LR AR PE CT IT RC AC` | Yes. |
| I5 | No edge between optional_legal and verification_loop, and none between optional_legal and PE: LR may occur before PE, between CT and IT, between iterations, or after the last IT. The prose only says "may occur after Assess Risk" and is silent about PE; the object says unordered. | `R AR PE CT LR IT RC AC`; `R AR LR PE CT IT RC AC` | a model that forces LR before CT or after IT (rejects one of the witnesses) | Only as interleaving set (same caveat as I2). |
| I6 | Verification cannot start before both AR and PE are complete (edges AR→loop, PE→loop). | `R PE AR CT IT RC AC` | `R AR CT IT PE RC AC` | Yes. |
| I7 | Every iteration is `CT` then `IT` (order inside `trial_body`); no `IT` without a preceding `CT`, no `CT CT`. | `R AR PE CT IT CD CT IT RC AC` | `R AR PE IT CT RC AC`; `R AR PE CT CT IT RC AC` | Yes. |
| I8 | Binary-loop semantics: CD occurs only between two iterations. Projected onto {CT, IT, CD} every trace matches `(CT IT)(CD CT IT)*`; CD is never the last loop event. | `R AR PE CT IT CD CT IT RC AC` | `R AR PE CT IT CD RC AC` | Yes. |
| I9 | At least one iteration (do-part is mandatory). | `R AR PE CT IT RC AC` | `R AR PE LR RC AC` | Yes. |
| I10 | RC waits for the optional-legal branch (LR done or skipped) and for the loop to have exited (join). | `R PE AR CT IT LR RC AC` | `R AR PE RC AC`; `R AR PE CT IT RC LR AC` | Yes for visible orderings. The *moment* the loop-exit decision is taken (before or after LR) is a silent choice and is not observable. |
| I11 | RC occurs exactly once, AC exactly once, AC directly after RC, AC is last (edge RC→AC; nothing is concurrent with RC/AC because RC joins everything). | `... IT RC AC` | `... IT AC RC`; `... IT RC` (no AC) | Yes. |
| I12 | Proper completion: the trace ends in exactly the final marking, no residual tokens, no further enabled visible activity. | every accepted trace above reaches `fm` exactly | a net that accepts `... RC AC` while leaving a token in a legal or loop place | Not from the visible trace alone: requires the exact final-marking test on the executable model. |
| I13 | Label uniqueness: nine distinct labels, each transition label used once in the model; every visible event in a trace maps to exactly one model element. | inspection of the label multiset | a tree/net that introduces duplicate `Legal Review` or `Configure Trial` transitions | Not from traces (a duplicated-label model can be trace-equivalent); observable only by structural inspection. |
| I14 | True concurrency: AR‖PE and LR‖(loop) are independent, not "a choice between orders". | independent tokens/branches in the executable model | a net that encodes all interleavings as an XOR over sequences | No. Trace-based validation is blind to this; it needs structural or partial-order semantics checks. |

Key consequences for the audit: I2, I5, I14 (independence), I10 (moment of choice), I12 (proper completion) and I13 (labels) are exactly the properties that "the conversion returned an object" or even "the round-trip traces match" cannot establish; they need soundness diagnostics, exact final-marking checks, and structural inspection respectively.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 (primary)** POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN, with BPMN → Petri net round trip | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | POWL→net is the construction the POWL semantics are defined by (sound WF-net by construction); the net→BPMN converter is structural (place → exclusive gateway, silent multi-arc transition / multi-arc task → parallel gateway) so nothing is re-discovered or approximated; the net can be executed and exhaustively compared. | pm4py applies a silent-transition reduction to the net, so the loop-exit tau and the partial-order split/join taus may be folded into visible transitions, changing branching-time structure (moment of choice) while keeping the language; BPMN gateways carry no conditions, no default flow, no loop marker; the BPMN has no marking concept, only start/end events. | `convert_to_petri_net` returns null/empty markings; net is not a WF-net or not sound; BPMN→net round trip produces a non-isomorphic or non-equivalent net; any witness fails. |
| **P2 (secondary, allowed to fail)** POWL → Petri net → process tree → BPMN → Petri net | `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` inside `try/except`; if it succeeds `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)`, `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_tree)`, `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)` | A tree would give a block-structured, layout-friendly BPMN with clean gateway nesting and guaranteed soundness. | The source partial order contains the N-pattern AR<LR, AR<loop, PE<loop with PE and LR unordered. A partial order is series-parallel iff it is N-free, so no process tree with unique labels has this language; a tree could only exist by duplicating labels (violating I13) or by adding/removing orderings (violating I2/I5). | `convert_to_process_tree` raises (observed: `ValueError: Parsing of WF-net Failed`). If it does *not* raise, the result must be quarantined and inspected for duplicate leaves or changed ordering. |
| **P3 (diagnostic)** POWL → Petri net → BPMN → process tree | `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(bpmn_from_net)` inside `try/except` | Independent second attempt at block-structuring, starting from the BPMN (pm4py internally converts BPMN → net → tree). Confirms whether the failure in P2 is intrinsic to the behaviour or an artefact of the specific net shape. | Same as P2. | Same as P2 (observed: same `ValueError`). |
| **P4 (stability probe)** BPMN → Petri net → BPMN → Petri net (second generation) | `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt)`; `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_gen2)` | Detects round-trip drift: if the converters are not idempotent, the second generation exposes it. | Extra silent transitions or gateway pairs accumulating with each generation. | Second-generation net differs in language from `net_from_powl`, or grows in size generation over generation. |

Primary pipeline: **P1**. It is the only pipeline whose every step is defined for this model, and its intermediate (the accepting Petri net) is the artefact on which soundness, exact-final-marking acceptance, and exhaustive language comparison can actually be run. P2 is kept because its *failure* is informative: it certifies that the model is not block-structured, which is a property of the source, not a defect of the converter.

# 3. Conversion Code

The code below was executed with pm4py 2.7.23.8 / Python 3.13. Every visualisation is wrapped so that a renderer problem (in this environment `save_vis_powl` needed `cairosvg` for PNG and fell back to SVG) cannot abort the semantic audit; every conversion result that is supposed to be an accepting net is checked for a real `PetriNet` and non-null, non-empty markings before use.

```python
"""Section 3: conversion pipelines starting from `source_powl`."""
import os
import json
import traceback

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet, Marking
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree

from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- source model
register = Transition(label="Register Change")
assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()
optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[legal_review, skip_legal])

configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[trial_body, correct_defect])

release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[register, assess_risk, prepare_evidence, optional_legal,
           verification_loop, release_change, archive_change]
)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- helpers
OUT_DIR = os.environ.get("POWL_AUDIT_OUT", "powl_conversion_audit")
os.makedirs(OUT_DIR, exist_ok=True)

nets = {}        # name -> (net, im, fm); every accepting net that exists
bpmns = {}       # name -> BPMN
trees = {}       # name -> ProcessTree
failures = {}    # step -> "ExceptionType: message"
diagnostics = {} # net name -> dict


def out(name):
    return os.path.join(OUT_DIR, name)


def safe_save(save_fn, *args, label):
    """Visualisation is evidence, not proof: a renderer failure must not abort the audit."""
    try:
        save_fn(*args)
        print(f"[VIS ] wrote {args[-1]}")
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        # Retry as SVG (the POWL visualiser needs cairosvg for PNG in some installs).
        alt = os.path.splitext(args[-1])[0] + ".svg"
        try:
            save_fn(*args[:-1], alt)
            print(f"[VIS ] wrote {alt} (PNG failed: {type(exc).__name__})")
        except Exception as exc2:  # noqa: BLE001
            failures[f"visualisation:{label}"] = f"{type(exc2).__name__}: {exc2}"
            print(f"[VIS ] FAILED {label}: {failures[f'visualisation:{label}']}")


def as_marked_net(result, step):
    """Normalise a conversion result to (net, im, fm); reject null/empty markings."""
    if not (isinstance(result, tuple) and len(result) == 3):
        raise TypeError(f"{step}: expected a (net, im, fm) triple, got {type(result).__name__}")
    net, im, fm = result
    if not isinstance(net, PetriNet):
        raise TypeError(f"{step}: first element is {type(net).__name__}, not PetriNet")
    if im is None or fm is None:
        raise ValueError(f"{step}: null marking returned (im={im!r}, fm={fm!r})")
    im, fm = Marking(im), Marking(fm)
    if sum(im.values()) == 0 or sum(fm.values()) == 0:
        raise ValueError(f"{step}: empty marking returned (im={im}, fm={fm})")
    for p in list(im) + list(fm):
        if p not in net.places:
            raise ValueError(f"{step}: marking refers to place {p} outside the net")
    return net, im, fm


def record_failure(step, exc):
    failures[step] = f"{type(exc).__name__}: {exc}"
    print(f"[FAIL] {step} -> {failures[step]}")
    traceback.print_exc(limit=2)


def tree_leaf_labels(tree):
    labels = []
    stack = [tree]
    while stack:
        node = stack.pop()
        if node.operator is None:
            if node.label is not None:
                labels.append(node.label)
        stack.extend(node.children)
    return labels


def diagnose(name, net, im, fm):
    """Workflow-net + soundness diagnostics (step 6). Never raises."""
    rep = {
        "places": len(net.places),
        "transitions": len(net.transitions),
        "silent_transitions": sum(1 for t in net.transitions if t.label is None),
        "arcs": len(net.arcs),
        "initial_marking": {p.name: c for p, c in im.items()},
        "final_marking": {p.name: c for p, c in fm.items()},
    }
    labels = [t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None]
    rep["visible_labels"] = sorted(labels)
    rep["duplicate_labels"] = sorted({l for l in labels if labels.count(l) > 1})
    try:
        rep["is_workflow_net"] = bool(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        rep["is_workflow_net"] = f"error: {exc!r}"
    try:
        # pm4py 2.7 returns (bool, diagnostics-dict); older versions returned bool.
        res = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=False)
        sound = res[0] if isinstance(res, tuple) else res
        rep["is_sound"] = bool(sound)
        if isinstance(res, tuple) and isinstance(res[1], dict):
            rep["soundness_keys"] = sorted(str(k) for k in res[1].keys())
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        rep["is_sound"] = f"error: {exc!r}"
    diagnostics[name] = rep
    print(f"[DIAG] {name}: " + json.dumps(rep, indent=None, default=str))
    return rep


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- step 0: source
safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_powl, source_powl, out("00_source_powl.png"), label="source_powl")

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- step 1: POWL -> Petri
net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = as_marked_net(
    pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl), "POWL->Petri")
nets["net_from_powl"] = (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl,
          out("10_net_from_powl.png"), label="net_from_powl")

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- step 2: Petri -> BPMN (primary)
bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
bpmns["bpmn_from_net"] = bpmn_from_net
safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, bpmn_from_net, out("20_bpmn_from_net.png"), label="bpmn_from_net")

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- step 3/4: Petri -> tree -> BPMN (secondary)
tree_from_net = None
bpmn_from_tree = None
try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
    if not isinstance(tree_from_net, ProcessTree):
        raise TypeError(f"convert_to_process_tree returned {type(tree_from_net).__name__}")
    trees["tree_from_net"] = tree_from_net
    leaves = tree_leaf_labels(tree_from_net)
    dups = sorted({l for l in leaves if leaves.count(l) > 1})
    print(f"[INFO] process tree obtained: {tree_from_net}")
    if dups:
        # The tree exists only by duplicating labels -> quarantine, do not treat as faithful.
        print(f"[WARN] tree duplicates labels {dups}; block-structure was bought with label duplication")
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_process_tree, tree_from_net, out("30_tree_from_net.png"), label="tree_from_net")
    bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
    bpmns["bpmn_from_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, bpmn_from_tree, out("31_bpmn_from_tree.png"), label="bpmn_from_tree")
except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001  (interpreted, not suppressed)
    record_failure("Petri->Tree (and dependent Tree->BPMN)", exc)

# A second, independent attempt: BPMN -> tree (pm4py routes it through a Petri net again).
try:
    tree_from_bpmn = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(bpmn_from_net)
    trees["tree_from_bpmn"] = tree_from_bpmn
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_process_tree, tree_from_bpmn, out("32_tree_from_bpmn.png"), label="tree_from_bpmn")
except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
    record_failure("BPMN->Tree", exc)

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- step 5: BPMN -> Petri (round trips)
for bpmn_name, bpmn_obj in list(bpmns.items()):
    step = f"{bpmn_name}->Petri"
    try:
        net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt = as_marked_net(pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_obj), step)
        rt_name = "net_rt_" + bpmn_name.replace("bpmn_", "")
        nets[rt_name] = (net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt)
        safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, net_rt, im_rt, fm_rt, out(f"40_{rt_name}.png"), label=rt_name)
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        record_failure(step, exc)

if tree_from_net is not None:
    try:
        net_t, im_t, fm_t = as_marked_net(pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net), "Tree->Petri")
        nets["net_from_tree"] = (net_t, im_t, fm_t)
        safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, net_t, im_t, fm_t, out("41_net_from_tree.png"), label="net_from_tree")
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        record_failure("Tree->Petri", exc)

# Second-generation BPMN (stability / drift probe): Petri(rt) -> BPMN again.
if "net_rt_from_net" in nets:
    try:
        n2, i2, f2 = nets["net_rt_from_net"]
        bpmn_gen2 = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(n2, i2, f2)
        bpmns["bpmn_gen2"] = bpmn_gen2
        safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, bpmn_gen2, out("50_bpmn_gen2.png"), label="bpmn_gen2")
        n3, i3, f3 = as_marked_net(pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_gen2), "bpmn_gen2->Petri")
        nets["net_rt_gen2"] = (n3, i3, f3)
        safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, n3, i3, f3, out("51_net_rt_gen2.png"), label="net_rt_gen2")
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        record_failure("second-generation round trip", exc)

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- step 6: diagnostics on every accepting net
for name, (n, i, f) in nets.items():
    diagnose(name, n, i, f)

for name, b in bpmns.items():
    kinds = {}
    for node in b.get_nodes():
        kinds[type(node).__name__] = kinds.get(type(node).__name__, 0) + 1
    print(f"[BPMN] {name}: nodes={kinds}, flows={len(b.get_flows())}")

print("\n[SUMMARY] nets produced:", sorted(nets))
print("[SUMMARY] bpmn produced:", sorted(bpmns))
print("[SUMMARY] trees produced:", sorted(trees))
print("[SUMMARY] failures:", json.dumps(failures, indent=2))
with open(out("diagnostics.json"), "w", encoding="utf-8") as fh:
    json.dump({"diagnostics": diagnostics, "failures": failures}, fh, indent=2, default=str)
```

Observed run (pm4py 2.7.23.8):

- `net_from_powl`: 12 places, 11 transitions (9 visible + 2 silent), 26 arcs, `im = {source: 1}`, `fm = {sink: 1}`, WF-net = True, sound = True, no duplicate labels. pm4py's post-conversion reduction removed the POWL split/join taus and the loop-entry/exit taus: `Register Change` itself has two output places (it *is* the AND-split), `Assess Risk` has two output places (legal branch and loop join), `Release Change` has two input places (it *is* the AND-join), one tau joins AR and PE into the loop entry place, and one tau is the legal skip. The place after `Inspect Trial` feeds both `Correct Defect` and `Release Change` while `Release Change` also needs the legal place, so the reduced net is **not free-choice**: the loop-exit decision is no longer a separate silent transition but is merged with the synchronisation on the legal branch.
- `bpmn_from_net`: 9 tasks, 4 parallel gateways, 4 exclusive gateways, 1 start, 1 end, 22 flows.
- `Petri->Tree`: `ValueError: Parsing of WF-net Failed`. `BPMN->Tree`: same `ValueError`. No tree, therefore no `bpmn_from_tree` and no `net_from_tree`. This is the expected outcome (Section 2, P2).
- `net_rt_from_net` and `net_rt_gen2`: both 12 places / 11 transitions / 2 silent / 26 arcs, single-token `source`/`sink` markings, WF-net and sound, structurally isomorphic to `net_from_powl` (only place/transition names differ). `bpmn_gen2` has the same node census as `bpmn_from_net`: no drift across generations.
- `check_soundness` is marked deprecated in pm4py 2.7 but still returns `(bool, diagnostics)`; the code tolerates both return shapes.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

Assessment vocabulary: `expected preserved`, `validated preserved`, `approximated`, `ambiguous`, `lost`, `conversion failed`, `not tested`. "Validated" means established by the Section 5 code (exact language equivalence for net-vs-net, bounded equality with the spec oracle for source-vs-intent, witness results, soundness). "Expected" means established by construction or structural inspection but not by an executed test.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 R first, once | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Start activity footprint = {R}; negative witness `AR R …` rejected; product automaton equivalence with both round-tripped nets. BPMN itself is not executable in pm4py; its fidelity is inferred from its round-tripped net. |
| I2 AR ‖ PE (both orders admitted) | validated preserved (interleavings) | expected preserved (parallel gateway after R) | conversion failed | validated preserved (interleavings) | Footprint `parallel` contains (AR, PE); both order witnesses accepted. The *interleaving set* is validated; see I14 for what is not. |
| I3 LR optional, ≤ 1 | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Tau `skip_13` survives in `net_from_powl`; reappears as tau `sfl_…` after round trip; `legal twice` rejected; both 0- and 1-occurrence witnesses accepted. |
| I4 LR after AR | validated preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Footprint sequence (AR, LR); `legal before assess` rejected. |

BPMN has no marking; a single `NormalEndEvent` is the only carrier of the final state, and BPMN engines' implicit-termination semantics are not the same as exact-marking equality. |
| I13 label uniqueness | validated preserved | validated preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | `duplicate_labels = []` on every net; BPMN has 9 tasks with 9 distinct names. A successful tree would have had to duplicate labels; the failure protected this invariant. |
| I14 true concurrency (independence, not enumerated interleavings) | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | expected preserved | Structural inspection: AR and PE sit on separate places fed by one split; LR branch and loop are separate token flows; 4 parallel gateways in BPMN. No executed test distinguishes this from an interleaving-enumerating model; footprints and language equivalence are blind to it. |
| Initial marking | validated preserved | approximated (start event) | conversion failed | validated preserved | `im = {source: 1}` before and after; BPMN start event carries no token count. |
| Gateway/routing-node semantics | n/a | ambiguous | conversion failed | validated preserved (as language) | Exclusive gateways carry no conditions and no default flow; the legal skip is a bare flow between two exclusive gateways; the loop-exit exclusive gateway feeds a parallel join. Structurally correct, semantically under-documented. |

Discussion points required by the task:

- **True concurrency versus arbitrary interleaving.** The POWL states *absence of order*. The Petri net encodes this as independent tokens, the BPMN as parallel gateways, and both are structurally faithful. But every test in Section 5 observes interleavings only. A net that replaced the AND-split with an XOR over `AR PE` and `PE AR` would pass footprints, witnesses, bounded language, and the product-automaton check. Hence I14 is `expected preserved`, never `validated preserved`, and the audit says so.
- **Lack of an edge between Optional Legal Review and verification.** Validated as a set of interleavings (LR before PE, between CT and IT, between iterations, after the last IT all accepted). The prose of the task under-specifies LR versus PE; the object allows LR before PE, the oracle was built to the object, and this is recorded as a specification ambiguity for stakeholders rather than a conversion loss.
- **Silent optional behaviour.** Preserved as a tau in every net and as an unlabelled flow in BPMN. What is lost in BPMN is *why* the branch is skipped: there is no condition expression, so "Legal Review" reads as an arbitrary choice.
- **Binary-loop semantics.** The `(CT IT)(CD CT IT)*` language is preserved exactly. The *representation* changed: the POWL's dedicated loop-exit tau was reduced away, so in the net the exit is decided at the same place as the synchronisation with the legal branch (non-free-choice). Language-preserving, branching-time-changing.
- **Join before Release Change.** Preserved: `Release Change` is itself the AND-join in the reduced net, and a parallel gateway in BPMN. Negative witnesses that release before verification or before legal completion are rejected by all nets.
- **Activity-label uniqueness.** Preserved everywhere that exists; the tree that would have broken it does not exist.
- **Initial and final markings.** Single-token `source`/`sink` before and after round trip; the BPMN carries them only as one start and one end event.
- **Gateway ambiguity in BPMN.** Discussed in Section 6; the converter is structurally deterministic (place → exclusive gateway; multi-arc silent transition or multi-arc task → parallel gateway; trivial gateways reduced), so the gateway *types* are trustworthy, the gateway *decision logic* is absent.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

Executed in the same session immediately after the Section 3 code. Design constraints from the task: transitions with `label is None` are hidden; a completed trace requires `Marking(fm)` to be exactly one of the tau-reachable markings (no "≥ fm" tolerance); silent closures are memoised (no infinite silent cycling) and capped (an unbounded net aborts instead of looping); the bounded exploration enforces a visible-length bound and a loop-iteration bound counted on `Correct Defect`; the spec oracle is built from the prose semantics, independently of pm4py, because comparing `net_from_powl` with its own round trips can never detect a defect in the first arrow.

```python
"""Section 5: round-trip validation. Runs in the same session as the Section 3 code
(needs: net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl, nets, failures)."""
import json
from collections import deque

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- firing / closure
STATE_CAP = 20000      # product states; reaching it makes the equivalence verdict inconclusive
CLOSURE_CAP = 5000     # markings inside one silent closure
TOKEN_CAP = 50         # more tokens than this under silent firing => unbounded => abort


def fire(t, marking):
    m = Marking(marking)
    for a in t.in_arcs:
        m[a.source] -= a.weight
        if m[a.source] <= 0:
            del m[a.source]          # canonical form so Marking.__eq__/__hash__ are reliable
    for a in t.out_arcs:
        m[a.target] += a.weight
    return m


def tau_closure(net, markings):
    """Markings reachable via label-None transitions only. Memoised, so silent cycles
    terminate; capped, so unbounded silent growth aborts instead of looping forever."""
    seen = set(markings)
    stack = list(markings)
    while stack:
        m = stack.pop()
        if sum(m.values()) > TOKEN_CAP:
            raise RuntimeError("silent closure produced an unbounded marking")
        for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m):
            if t.label is None:
                m2 = fire(t, m)
                if m2 not in seen:
                    if len(seen) >= CLOSURE_CAP:
                        raise RuntimeError("silent closure exceeded CLOSURE_CAP")
                    seen.add(m2)
                    stack.append(m2)
    return frozenset(seen)


def visible_labels(net, state):
    return {t.label for m in state for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m) if t.label is not None}


def visible_step(net, state, label):
    nxt = {fire(t, m) for m in state for t in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m) if t.label == label}
    return tau_closure(net, nxt) if nxt else None


def accepts(triple, trace):
    """Exact acceptance: after the whole trace some tau-reachable marking equals fm."""
    net, im, fm = triple
    state = tau_closure(net, [Marking(im)])
    for label in trace:
        state = visible_step(net, state, label)
        if state is None:
            return False
    return Marking(fm) in state


def bounded_language(triple, max_visible_len, loop_marker, max_loop_iter, prefix_cap=200000):
    """All complete visible traces (exact fm) and reachable prefixes within the bounds.
    Exhaustive for words of length <= max_visible_len with <= max_loop_iter loop markers."""
    net, im, fm = triple
    fm = Marking(fm)
    frontier = [((), tau_closure(net, [Marking(im)]))]
    complete, prefixes = set(), set()
    while frontier:
        nxt_frontier = []
        for word, state in frontier:
            prefixes.add(word)
            if len(prefixes) > prefix_cap:
                raise RuntimeError("prefix cap exceeded")
            if fm in state:
                complete.add(word)
            if len(word) >= max_visible_len:
                continue
            for lbl in sorted(visible_labels(net, state)):
                if lbl == loop_marker and word.count(lbl) >= max_loop_iter:
                    continue
                nxt_frontier.append((word + (lbl,), visible_step(net, state, lbl)))
        frontier = nxt_frontier
    return complete, prefixes


def language_equivalent(tA, tB):
    """Synchronous product of the two tau-closed visible automata.
    Exact (not bounded) whenever both nets are bounded and STATE_CAP is not hit."""
    nA, imA, fmA = tA
    nB, imB, fmB = tB
    fmA, fmB = Marking(fmA), Marking(fmB)
    a0, b0 = tau_closure(nA, [Marking(imA)]), tau_closure(nB, [Marking(imB)])
    seen = {(a0, b0)}
    queue = deque([(a0, b0, ())])
    while queue:
        sa, sb, word = queue.popleft()
        if (fmA in sa) != (fmB in sb):
            return False, {"reason": "acceptance mismatch", "word": word, "A_accepts": fmA in sa, "B_accepts": fmB in sb}
        la, lb = visible_labels(nA, sa), visible_labels(nB, sb)
        if la != lb:
            return False, {"reason": "enabled-label mismatch", "word": word, "A": sorted(la), "B": sorted(lb)}
        for lbl in sorted(la):
            pair = (visible_step(nA, sa, lbl), visible_step(nB, sb, lbl))
            if pair not in seen:
                if len(seen) >= STATE_CAP:
                    return None, {"reason": "STATE_CAP reached; verdict inconclusive", "states": len(seen)}
                seen.add(pair)
                queue.append((pair[0], pair[1], word + (lbl,)))
    return True, {"reason": "product fully explored", "product_states": len(seen)}


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- spec oracle (independent of pm4py)
R, AR, PE, LR, CT, IT, CD, RC, AC = ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Legal Review",
                                     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect", "Release Change",
                                     "Archive Change")


def intended_language(max_iterations, with_legal_options=(False, True)):
    """Linear extensions of the intended partial order, built from the prose semantics,
    NOT from the POWL object or any pm4py conversion."""
    words = set()
    for k in range(1, max_iterations + 1):
        for legal in with_legal_options:
            events = [R, AR, PE, RC, AC] + ([LR] if legal else [])
            loop = []
            for i in range(k):
                loop += [(CT, i), (IT, i)]
                if i < k - 1:
                    loop.append((CD, i))
            events = [(e, 0) for e in events] + loop
            before = {e: set() for e in events}
            def prec(a, b): before[b].add(a)
            prec((R, 0), (AR, 0)); prec((R, 0), (PE, 0))
            prec((AR, 0), (CT, 0)); prec((PE, 0), (CT, 0))
            if legal:
                prec((AR, 0), (LR, 0)); prec((LR, 0), (RC, 0))
            for i in range(len(loop) - 1):
                prec(loop[i], loop[i + 1])
            prec(loop[-1], (RC, 0)); prec((RC, 0), (AC, 0))

            def extend(done, remaining, word):
                if not remaining:
                    words.add(tuple(word)); return
                for e in list(remaining):
                    if before[e] <= done:
                        extend(done | {e}, remaining - {e}, word + [e[0]])
            extend(frozenset(), frozenset(events), [])
    return words


POSITIVE_WITNESSES = {
    "minimal, no legal":            [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC],
    "PE before AR":                 [R, PE, AR, CT, IT, RC, AC],
    "legal before evidence":        [R, AR, LR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC],
    "legal inside loop body":       [R, AR, PE, CT, LR, IT, RC, AC],
    "legal after loop":             [R, PE, AR, CT, IT, LR, RC, AC],
    "two iterations":               [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, CT, IT, RC, AC],
    "three iterations + legal mid": [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, LR, CT, IT, CD, CT, IT, RC, AC],
}
NEGATIVE_WITNESSES = {
    "release before verification":  [R, AR, PE, RC, AC],
    "verification before evidence": [R, AR, CT, IT, PE, RC, AC],
    "legal before assess":          [R, LR, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC],
    "inspect before configure":     [R, AR, PE, IT, CT, RC, AC],
    "ends on correct defect":       [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, RC, AC],
    "correct defect without redo":  [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, RC, AC],
    "zero iterations":              [R, AR, PE, LR, RC, AC],
    "legal twice":                  [R, AR, LR, LR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC],
    "archive missing":              [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC],
    "release after archive":        [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, AC, RC],
    "legal after release":          [R, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, LR, AC],
    "double configure":             [R, AR, PE, CT, CT, IT, RC, AC],
}

# --------------------------------------------------------------------------- run
results = {}
ref = nets["net_from_powl"]
MAX_LEN, MAX_ITER = 14, 3      # 14 visible events = 3 iterations + legal; exhaustive below that

for name, triple in nets.items():
    net, im, fm = triple
    r = {}
    # (a) soundness / WF-net (already in diagnostics, repeated here as the gate for the rest)
    res = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=False)
    r["sound"] = bool(res[0] if isinstance(res, tuple) else res)
    r["workflow_net"] = bool(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    # (b) footprints
    try:
        fp = pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm)
        r["footprints"] = {k: sorted(map(str, v)) if isinstance(v, (set, frozenset, list)) else v
                           for k, v in fp.items() if k in ("activities", "start_activities", "end_activities",
                                                            "sequence", "parallel", "min_trace_length")}
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        r["footprints"] = f"error: {exc!r}"
    # (c) reachability graph (raw, includes silent moves: size is descriptive only)
    try:
        rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
        r["reachability_graph"] = {"states": len(rg.states), "transitions": len(rg.transitions)}
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        r["reachability_graph"] = f"error: {exc!r}"
    # (d) bounded visible exploration
    try:
        complete, prefixes = bounded_language(triple, MAX_LEN, CD, MAX_ITER)
        r["bounded_complete_traces"] = len(complete)
        r["bounded_prefixes"] = len(prefixes)
        r["_complete"] = complete
        r["_prefixes"] = prefixes
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        r["bounded_exploration"] = f"error: {exc!r}"
    # (e) witnesses
    r["positive_witnesses_accepted"] = {k: accepts(triple, v) for k, v in POSITIVE_WITNESSES.items()}
    r["negative_witnesses_rejected"] = {k: (not accepts(triple, v)) for k, v in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES.items()}
    results[name] = r

# (f) compare each round-tripped net with net_from_powl
comparisons = {}
for name, triple in nets.items():
    if name == "net_from_powl":
        continue
    c = {}
    c["footprints_equal"] = results[name].get("footprints") == results["net_from_powl"].get("footprints")
    c["bounded_complete_equal"] = results[name].get("_complete") == results["net_from_powl"].get("_complete")
    c["bounded_prefixes_equal"] = results[name].get("_prefixes") == results["net_from_powl"].get("_prefixes")
    if not c["bounded_complete_equal"]:
        a, b = results["net_from_powl"]["_complete"], results[name]["_complete"]
        c["only_in_source"] = sorted(a - b)[:5]
        c["only_in_roundtrip"] = sorted(b - a)[:5]
    try:
        eq, info = language_equivalent(ref, triple)
        c["exact_language_equivalent"] = eq
        c["equivalence_info"] = info
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        c["exact_language_equivalent"] = None
        c["equivalence_info"] = f"error: {exc!r}"
    comparisons[name] = c

# (g) spec oracle vs converted net (this is what round-trip comparison alone cannot test)
oracle = {w for w in intended_language(MAX_ITER) if len(w) <= MAX_LEN}
src_complete = results["net_from_powl"]["_complete"]
oracle_check = {
    "oracle_traces": len(oracle),
    "net_traces": len(src_complete),
    "equal_within_bound": oracle == src_complete,
    "only_in_oracle": sorted(oracle - src_complete)[:5],
    "only_in_net": sorted(src_complete - oracle)[:5],
}

# (h) playout cross-check (stochastic; supplementary only)
playout_check = {}
try:
    log = pm4py.play_out(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
    try:
        import pandas as pd
        if isinstance(log, pd.DataFrame):
            log = pm4py.convert_to_event_log(log)
    except Exception:  # noqa: BLE001
        pass
    traces = [tuple(ev["concept:name"] for ev in tr) for tr in log]
    complete_traces = [t for t in traces if accepts(ref, t)]      # drop length-truncated playouts
    playout_check["played_out"] = len(traces)
    playout_check["complete_in_source"] = len(complete_traces)
    for name, triple in nets.items():
        if name != "net_from_powl":
            playout_check[f"accepted_by_{name}"] = sum(accepts(triple, t) for t in complete_traces)
    playout_check["satisfy_spec_oracle"] = sum(t in intended_language(max(1, max(t.count(CD) for t in complete_traces) + 1))
                                              for t in complete_traces) if complete_traces else 0
except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
    playout_check["error"] = repr(exc)


def strip(d):
    return {k: v for k, v in d.items() if not k.startswith("_")}

print("=== per-net results ===")
print(json.dumps({k: strip(v) for k, v in results.items()}, indent=1, default=str))
print("=== comparisons with net_from_powl ===")
print(json.dumps(comparisons, indent=1, default=str))
print("=== spec oracle ===")
print(json.dumps(oracle_check, indent=1, default=str))
print("=== playout ===")
print(json.dumps(playout_check, indent=1, default=str))
print("=== conversion failures carried over ===")
print(json.dumps(failures, indent=1))
```

- Footprints identical across the three nets. `sequence` = {(AR,CT), (AR,LR), (CT,IT), (CD,CT), (IT,CD), (IT,RC), (LR,RC), (PE,CT), (R,AR), (R,PE), (RC,AC)}; `parallel` = {(AR,PE), (LR,PE), (LR,CT), (LR,IT), (LR,CD)} and their mirrors; `start_activities` = {R}. This pm4py version's Petri footprints have no `end_activities` key, so footprints do not cover finality (I11); the exact-final-marking test does.
- Raw reachability graphs: 15 states / 26 transitions for all three nets (identical because the nets are isomorphic; in general the raw graphs of a net and its round trip are *not* expected to be isomorphic because silent structure differs, which is why the comparison is done on tau-closed visible automata).

Exhaustiveness statement. (i) The net-versus-net comparison via `language_equivalent` is exhaustive: it is a standard product construction over the determinised, tau-closed visible automata, and since all three nets are sound (hence bounded) the automata are finite; the run terminated with the product fully explored, so the visible trace languages of `net_from_powl`, `net_rt_from_net`, and `net_rt_gen2` are *exactly* equal, including all loop iteration counts. (ii) The `bounded_language` and spec-oracle comparisons are exhaustive only within the bound (visible length ≤ 14 and ≤ 3 `Correct Defect` occurrences); behaviour beyond three iterations is covered against the oracle only by the argument that the loop structure is regular, not by enumeration. (iii) Witnesses and playout are samples, not proofs. (iv) None of the tests observe independence (I14) or branching-time structure (moment of choice, I10); they observe interleavings and exact completion only.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

The rendered `bpmn_from_net` is behaviourally acceptable (its round-tripped net is exactly language-equivalent to the source net), yet it can be misread in the following ways.

- **Parallel gateways.** The converter inserts a parallel gateway wherever a task or silent transition has more than one input or output arc. The diagram therefore contains a split after `Register Change`, a split after `Assess Risk`, a join before `Configure Trial` (AR and PE), and a join before `Release Change`. The split after `Assess Risk` is easily misread as "Assess Risk is done twice" or as a decision; it is in fact the point where the legal branch becomes concurrent with the AR-side input to verification. Nothing in the diagram says that the join before `Release Change` synchronises two things of different nature (an optional single task and a loop).
- **Exclusive gateways.** Four exclusive gateways appear, none with a condition expression, none with a default flow, none labelled. The legal-review split and join are indistinguishable from the loop-entry join and loop-exit split except by topology. A reader cannot tell who decides whether Legal Review happens, or on what criterion the loop exits. pm4py places → exclusive gateways, so the gateway type is trustworthy; the decision semantics are simply absent.
- **Loop visualisation.** The loop is a back-edge `Inspect Trial → X → Correct Defect → X → Configure Trial`. There is no BPMN loop marker, no "at least once" annotation, and no visual cue that `Correct Defect` cannot be the last thing done. Readers frequently interpret such a back-edge as "optional rework" without noticing that rework always forces a full re-execution of `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial` (binary-loop semantics).
- **Silent routing nodes.** The skip branch of the XOR is a bare sequence flow from one exclusive gateway to the next. Some readers treat an empty branch as a diagram error or a "default" path; others do not notice it at all and read Legal Review as mandatory. The silent AR‖PE join before the loop has become a parallel gateway, which correctly conveys synchronisation but hides the fact that it was a POWL ordering edge, not a domain decision.
- **Apparent ordering of the layout.** The left-to-right layout puts `Legal Review` in the upper lane above `Configure Trial`/`Inspect Trial` and to the right of `Assess Risk`. It suggests (a) that Legal Review happens "while" verification runs, hiding that it may also occur before `Prepare Evidence` has even started, and (b) that `Prepare Evidence` "comes after" `Assess Risk` because it is drawn lower and slightly to the right; both are layout artefacts. Horizontal position in an auto-layouted BPMN encodes graph depth, not time.
- **Moment of choice.** The BPMN puts a committing exclusive gateway right after `Inspect Trial`, then a parallel join. The reduced Petri net, by contrast, keeps the exit decision open until `Release Change` is enabled. The picture therefore shows a different branching-time story from the executable artefact it was derived from, even though the traces agree.

Annotations and documentation that reduce the ambiguity:

1. Attach text annotations to the two exclusive gateways of the legal branch ("Legal Review optional; skip path is intentional") and to the loop-exit gateway ("exit only after a complete Configure→Inspect iteration; at least one iteration; Correct Defect always followed by a new iteration").
2. Name the parallel gateways by role ("fork: assess/evidence", "join: assess + evidence → verification", "join: legal branch + verification → release").
3. Publish the Section 1 invariant table and the positive/negative witness traces next to the diagram; the witnesses `R AR LR PE …` and `R AR PE CT LR IT …` are the fastest way to correct the layout-induced reading of Legal Review.
4. Publish the legend "horizontal position = graph depth, not time; unlabelled flows between gateways are intentional skips" with every generated BPMN.
5. Ship the diagram with the validation report (Section 5 output) and the pm4py version, so that a reader knows which claims about the picture are backed by an executed check and which are not.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

A conversion result must be rejected (or quarantined pending review) if any of the following holds:

1. **Null or empty markings.** `convert_to_petri_net` returns `None` for `im` or `fm`, an empty marking, or a marking referring to places outside the net (`as_marked_net` raises).
2. **Not a workflow net or not sound.** `check_is_workflow_net` is False or `check_soundness` is False on any produced accepting net (dead transitions, unbounded places, improper completion).
3. **Loss of a required interleaving.** Any positive witness is rejected, in particular `R PE AR …`, `R AR LR PE …`, or `R AR PE CT LR IT …`; or the footprint `parallel` relation loses (AR, PE) or any (LR, x) pair for x ∈ {PE, CT, IT, CD}.
4. **Admission of release before verification or before the legal branch.** `R AR PE RC AC` or `… RC LR AC` is accepted; or the footprint `sequence` relation loses (IT, RC) or (LR, RC).
5. **Collapse or inflation of optional behaviour.** Traces without `Legal Review` are rejected (optional became mandatory), or traces with two `Legal Review` events are accepted, or the tau skip disappears without a compensating structure.
6. **Broken loop semantics.** `… CT IT CD RC AC` is accepted (loop may end on `Correct Defect`), `… IT CT …` is accepted, or a trace with zero iterations is accepted.
7. **Failed exact-final-marking test.** A trace is "accepted" only because `fm` is *covered* rather than *equal*, or a trace ending in `Archive Change` leaves residual tokens (the `accepts` function returns False on a positive witness while a token-coverage check returns True).
8. **Language divergence.** `language_equivalent` returns False (with counterexample word) or None (state cap reached, unbounded behaviour suspected) between `net_from_powl` and any round-tripped net; or `bounded_complete_equal` / `bounded_prefixes_equal` is False.
9. **Spec-oracle divergence.** `oracle_check["equal_within_bound"]` is False: the very first conversion (POWL→Petri) disagrees with the stated semantics; round-trip stability cannot excuse this.
10. **Label duplication or renaming.** `duplicate_labels` non-empty on any net, a BPMN task name differing from the transition label, or a process tree whose leaf multiset differs from the nine source labels.
11. **Process-tree conversion that "succeeds" suspiciously.** For this model a process tree is not expected to exist; a tree returned without exception must be quarantined and checked for duplicate leaves and language equality before any downstream use.
12. **Round-trip drift.** The second-generation net differs from the first-generation one in size or language, or a BPMN gains or loses gateways between generations.
13. **Structural downgrade of concurrency.** Inspection shows AR/PE or LR/loop realised by an exclusive choice over enumerated orders rather than by independent flows (trace tests will not catch this; it must be a manual or structural gate).
14. **Toolchain exceptions.** Any `record_failure` entry on a step of the primary pipeline; failures in P2/P3 are expected and interpreted, failures in P1 are disqualifying.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

- **Authoritative representation: the POWL source definition**, stored as the version-controlled Python construction (the code in Section 3, or an equivalent serialised POWL) together with a content hash of `str(source_powl)`. It is the only artefact that states absence of order explicitly, keeps the loop-exit as a first-class silent choice, and has unique labels by construction. Every dispute about behaviour is settled by the POWL, never by a diagram.
- **Canonical stored executable: the accepting Petri net `net_from_powl` with its markings**, written as PNML (`pm4py.write_pnml(net, im, fm, path)`), regenerated from the POWL on every change and stored next to it with the pm4py version that produced it. It is the artefact against which soundness, exact-final-marking acceptance, and language equivalence are computed, so it is what conformance checking and simulation should run on. It is derivative of the POWL, not authoritative; if it is ever hand-edited it must be discarded and regenerated.
- **Stakeholder visualisation: the BPMN `bpmn_from_net`**, written as `.bpmn` XML (`pm4py.write_bpmn(bpmn, path)`) plus the rendered image, annotated as in Section 6. It is strictly derivative and must never be edited by hand as a way to change the process.
- **Derivative artefacts**: the PNML, the BPMN XML, all PNG/SVG renderings, the footprints, the reachability graph statistics, and the playout logs.
- **Validation evidence that must accompany each derivative**: for the PNML, the `diagnostics.json` (WF-net, soundness, label uniqueness, markings) and the spec-oracle result with its bound; for the BPMN, additionally the result of the BPMN→Petri round trip (`exact_language_equivalent = True`, product states explored, witness table all green), the gateway census (4 parallel, 4 exclusive) and the annotation legend; for every artefact, the pm4py version, the exception log (`failures`), and the hash of the POWL it was generated from.
- **Process-tree intermediate**: not allowed for this model. The partial order contains an N-pattern (AR<LR, AR<loop, PE<loop, PE‖LR), so no unique-label process tree has the same language; the observed `ValueError: Parsing of WF-net Failed` is the correct outcome. As a general policy, a tree intermediate may be admitted only when (a) `convert_to_process_tree` succeeds without exception, (b) the leaf multiset equals the source label set (no duplication), and (c) the tree's Petri net passes `language_equivalent` against `net_from_powl`. A tree obtained by relaxing or adding ordering edges to "make it block-structured" is a different process and must be treated as a change request, not a conversion.
- **Avoiding round-trip drift on future edits**: edit only the POWL; regenerate PNML and BPMN in a single scripted step; run the Section 5 suite as a gate and store its report with the artefacts; treat any hand edit of a BPMN as a proposal to be translated back into a POWL edit; add a CI check that reconverts the stored POWL and fails if the regenerated PNML is not language-equivalent to the stored one, and if the regenerated BPMN's gateway census changes; pin the pm4py version, because the reduction behaviour that folds taus into visible transitions is version-dependent and changes the branching structure of the stored net even when the language is unchanged.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even with every test in Section 5 passing, the following claims are not established:

1. **True concurrency / independence.** The tests observe interleavings. A model that realised AR‖PE or LR‖verification as an exclusive choice over enumerated orders would pass footprints, witnesses, bounded language, and exact language equivalence. Independence is asserted by structural inspection only.
2. **Branching-time equivalence (moment of choice).** The POWL commits to loop exit via a silent transition and then waits for the legal branch; the reduced net keeps the exit open until `Release Change` is enabled; the BPMN commits at an exclusive gateway before a parallel join. These three are trace-equivalent but not weakly bisimilar. Any stochastic, timing, or decision-mining interpretation that depends on *when* the choice is made is unsupported by the validation.
3. **Behaviour beyond the enumeration bound against the specification.** Net-versus-net equivalence is exact, but agreement with the independently built spec oracle was checked only up to 14 visible events and 3 `Correct Defect` occurrences. That the loop generalises correctly for larger iteration counts follows from the regular structure of the net, not from an executed comparison.
4. **That the POWL matches the domain intent.** The object allows Legal Review before Prepare Evidence, and the prose does not say whether that is intended. Validation confirms the converters preserved the POWL, not that the POWL is right. The same applies to whether `Correct Defect` must be followed by a *full* re-run of both trial activities, which the model enforces and stakeholders may or may not expect.
5. **Decision logic, data, resources, and time.** No test says who chooses Legal Review, on what condition the loop exits, whether `Release Change` waits for a legal *approval* or merely for the branch to have finished, or how long anything takes. BPMN conditions, defaults, and loop markers are absent, and the Petri net has no data.
6. **Exact-final-marking semantics in BPMN engines.** The round trip used pm4py's BPMN→Petri interpretation, in which the end event corresponds to `{sink: 1}`. A BPMN engine with implicit termination or multiple-token end events may accept or reject completions differently; the validation covers pm4py's reading of the diagram only.
7. **Stability across tool versions.** The size of `net_from_powl` (12 places, 11 transitions, 2 taus) and the non-free-choice merge of the loop exit are products of pm4py 2.7.23.8's reduction. A different version may return a larger or differently shaped net that is still language-equivalent; results, including gateway counts in the BPMN, are certified only for the pinned version.
8. **Label identity beyond string equality.** Tasks are matched by the string `concept:name`. Nothing establishes that `Legal Review` in the BPMN is the same organisational activity as `Legal Review` in the event data a future conformance check would use.
