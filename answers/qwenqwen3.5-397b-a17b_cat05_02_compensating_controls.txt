The nominal Four-Eyes Principle (FEP-1) aims to ensure separation of duties. Decomposing this into specific threat scenarios reveals where compensating controls (AV-2, MG-4, RA-7, ED-1) succeed or fail.

| Threat Scenario | Control Objective | AV-2 (Automated) | MG-4 (Manager) | RA-7 (Audit) | ED-1 (Emergency) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Fraudulent Initiation** (Self-approval for theft) | Prevent unauthorized transfer | **Prevent** (Sanctions/Ownership checks) | **Prevent** (If independent & pre-release) | **Detect** (Post-loss) | **Mitigate** (Strict conditions) |
| **2. Erroneous Initiation** (Mistake/Duplicate) | Prevent financial loss | **Prevent** (Duplicate/Amount checks) | **Prevent** (Review evidence) | **Detect** (Post-loss) | **Neutral** (Relies on AV-2) |

**Analysis:**
*   **AV-2** is strong on data integrity (duplicates, sanctions) but blind to commercial reasonableness or collusion.
*   **MG-4** is the primary compensating control for FEP-1 violations, but only if independent and performed *before* irrevocable settlement.
*   **RA-7** is purely detective unless action is taken within the bank recall window (approx. 24 hours).
*   **ED-1** is a risk acceptance framework, not a control itself; it relies on the other three to remain safe.

## 2. Equivalence and Sufficiency Test

A compensating control package is not sufficient merely because a control activity occurred. Sufficiency is judged by whether the residual risk matches the risk of the nominal compliant process.

| Classification | Criteria |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fully Equivalent** | Independent human review (MG-4) **before** `Release_Payment`; AV-2 pass without override; ED-1 pre-conditions met (if applicable). |
| **Partially Equivalent** | Independent human review **before** `Bank_Settlement` but after `Release_Payment`; or ED-1 pre-conditions slightly violated (e.g., token timing) but manager review intact. Prevents loss, but process integrity is compromised. |
| **Merely Mitigating** | Audit (RA-7) occurs after the bank recall window; or Manager review is rubber-stamped (no evidence of document check); or AV-2 overridden without secondary approval. Detects loss but does not prevent it. |
| **Ineffective** | Manager has conflict of interest (e.g., direct supervisor rating); AV-2 unavailable during release; Audit delayed beyond recovery window; Collusion evident. |

**Why "Same Expected Loss" is Insufficient:**
Control objectives include **integrity** and **deterrence**, not just loss prevention. A process that allows same-user payments regularly (even if losses are recovered) erodes the deterrence value of FEP-1, increasing the likelihood of future, unrecoverable fraud. Therefore, a case with zero financial loss but high process violation (e.g., AP-206 token timing) cannot be classified as fully equivalent to a compliant case.

**Semantics of Manager Action Before Settlement:**
A manager action occurring after `Release_Payment` but before `Bank_Settlement` is **Hybrid**.
*   **Detective:** It detects that the `Release_Payment` event occurred without prior approval (a process violation).
*   **Preventive:** It prevents the *financial loss* because the bank settlement has not yet finalized the transfer.
*   **Judgment:** For control objective classification, this is treated as **Partially Met**. The financial objective is satisfied, but the process integrity objective (separation of duties at the point of release) is violated. This creates a dependency on the settlement delay window, which is an external factor, not an internal control.

## 6. Evidence and Monitoring Recommendations

| **Override Rate** | >1% of AV-2 warnings overridden | Fraud Risk | Review all overrides weekly. |
| **Late Manager Approval** | Approval timestamp > Release timestamp | Control Weakness | Flag for "Hybrid" classification; track settlement timing. |
| **Audit-Before-Recall** | Audit timestamp > 24h after Settlement | Recoverability Risk | Escalate to Audit Committee. |

| **Shared Workstation** | Same host ID for distinct users | Credential Sharing | IT Security investigation. |
| **Bank-Change Proximity** | Bank change <7 days before payment | Fraud Risk | Mandatory secondary verification (MG-4). |
| **Control Outages** | AV-2 unavailable during payment | High Risk | Auto-block payments during outage. |

**Distinction:** An alert on "Repeated Resource Pairs" (AP-204/209) suggests *opportunity* for collusion, not proof. Proof requires evidence of fabricated documents (as found in AP-204) or financial linkage.

# Since pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle may not exist in all versions,

def check_four_eyes(df, prepare_act="Prepare_Payment", release_act="Release_Payment"):
    # Pivot to get resources per case per activity
    pivot = df.pivot_table(index='case_id', columns='activity', values='resource', aggfunc='first')
    violations = []
    for case_id, row in pivot.iterrows():
        prep_res = row.get(prepare_act)
        rel_res = row.get(release_act)
        if pd.notna(prep_res) and pd.notna(rel_res):
            if prep_res == rel_res:
                violations.append(case_id)
    return violations
