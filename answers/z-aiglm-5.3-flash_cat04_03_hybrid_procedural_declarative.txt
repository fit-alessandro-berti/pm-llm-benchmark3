# 1. Policy Interpretation and Conflict Resolution

## Material assumptions

| # | Assumption | Rationale |
|---|---|---|

| A2 | The three checks (`Capture Master Data`, `Run Sanctions Screening`, `Verify Bank Account`) occur exactly once each in the pre-classification phase. Repeat verification is possible **only** in the post-activation bank-change flow of R11. | Keeps `activ_freq` definable; disclosed as a restriction on amendment workflows. |
| A3 | An accepted case runs **one** route: low-risk  `Manager Approval`; high-risk  `Enhanced Due Diligence` then `Compliance Approval`. | A6 | `Sanctions Escalation` may be raised after screening and is governed by a shared "sanctions-state" token: while an escalation is *open*, outcome-approval and activation cannot fire anywhere in the case. | A7 | Event attributes relied on downstream: `concept:name`, `time:timestamp`, `org:resource`; case attributes: `case:concept:name` and externally stamped `emergency_authorized: bool`. | Needed by R9, R6 and the data layer. |
| A8 | Occurrence caps: `{Activate, Reject, Release, Manager Approval, Compliance Approval, EDD, Suspend}  1`; `Verify Bank Account  2`; exactly one `Receive Onboarding Request` and one `Close Onboarding` per case. | R10 plus backbone reading. |

## The R5 vs R6 conflict

- **R5 (legal):** after `Sanctions Escalation`, `Obtain Legal Opinion` must precede `Activate Vendor` — *"under all circumstances."*
- **R6 (operations):** emergency activation may occur *before* the opinion, contingent on joint authorization **outside the event log**, with the opinion arriving within 24 h. The log itself contains neither an emergency marker nor the authorization.

These are not reconcilable *inside* an activity-sequence formalism: the distinguishing fact is not visible in the trace. Mixing them silently (e.g., letting the discovery tool decide from data) would launder an undocumented policy into a discovered model.

### Resolution A — Absolute compliance gate
R5 is encoded unconditionally in **every** formal layer. No bypass exists in any artifact. Emergency cases are handled off-system: activation is paused, legal is expedited, and operations escalate to management rather than to a modeling exception.
- *Pros:* audit-proof, simple, no reliance on external integrity.
- *Cons:* operational friction creates incentives for workarounds (e.g., performing activation under a different label), which mining cannot see.

### Resolution B — Controlled deviation exception (**chosen**)
All activity-only formalisms encode the **strict** rule (A-style). The emergency exception is admitted **only** through an externally stamped, read-only case attribute `emergency_authorized == True` created jointly by Compliance and Operations heads in a system outside the mining stack. If and only if that attribute is present **and** `(t_Obtain Legal Opinion  t_Sanctions Escalation)  24 h`, the conformance engine downgrades the precedence breach from *fail* to *warning*, records it in an override registry, and routes it to human compliance review. Absent attribute, breached 24-hour budget, or tampered/unparseable attribute  **strict fail**.

**Compliance risk of the chosen Resolution B:** the strictness of R5 now depends on the governance quality of a system the process model does not control. Without watertight provisioning (immutable attribute, joint dual sign-off, weekly override auditing, aggregated reporting), R5 erodes into a routinely overridden checkbox, and frequent "normal" overrides will normalize the exception. Mitigation: the override path touches **no** control-flow artifact; the Petri net, DECLARE model, and log skeleton all remain maximally strict, so the exception is visible as a deviation in every conformance metric and can be revoked at any time by dropping the attribute.

Throughout this document the implemented posture is: **strict-by-default everywhere, exception adjudicated exclusively in the data-aware layer.**

# 3. Procedural Backbone

## 3.1 Compact formal description

An accepting Petri net *N = (P, T, F, , m_i, m_f)* with a **sanctions-state semaphore** `s_clear`/`s_open` and a **clarification gate** `q_clear`/`q_pending`:

- `RxOnboardReq` : p_start  p_checks, splits into three parallel checks whose results must all be present (multi-input join) for `ClassifyRisk`.
- `ClassifyRisk` : p_checks_joined × `q_clear`  p_decision. While a clarification is pending, classification is disabled; `ReqClar` (`q_clearq_pending`) / `RecvClar` (`q_pendingq_clear`) form an unbounded alternating loop.
- `QDecision` : p_decision  {p_reject_route | p_low_route | p_high_route}.
  - `RejectVendor`  p_closed (terminal path 1).
  - Low: `MgrApproval` ( consumes `s_clear`)  `ActivateVendor` (consumes `s_clear`)  p_active  [`PayBlock` rework cycle × n (CbBSusVBA)]  `RelFirstPmt`  p_paid (terminal via `CloseOnboarding`).
  - High: `EnhancedDueDiligence`  `ComplApproval` ( consumes `s_clear`)  p_high_approved, joining p_active upstream of activation.
  - `SanctionsEscalation` (enabled wherever `s_clear` + an in-flight branch exists): consumes `s_clear`, produces `s_open`; only `ObtainLegalOpinion` consumes `s_open` and restores `s_clear`.
- Accepting marking: one token in p_closed.

While `s_open`, **neither approval nor activation can fire anywhere** — this is the executable form of R5 under the strict default of Resolution B. The emergency bypass (R6) is deliberately *absent* from the net and adjudicated only in §7.

**Why warnings, not failures.** Requirement R12 explicitly frames these as "flag but not automatically reject" — a soft-normative instrument. Design consequences:

1. **Isolation of authority.** The temporal layer contributes at most `warning` verdicts, never `fail`, whatever the deviation magnitude (deviation = |  | / ; alarm if > zeta· i.e.  outside ±2).
2. **Gaussian skepticism.** One standard deviation covering broad reality (24 h s.d. on a 48 h mean) indicates the underlying stochastic process isn't tightly tuned; over-reacting to single-tail spikes would flood humans.

```python
    Policy: STRICT precedence (R5) is default; the ONLY admissible bypass requires
    (i) externally stamped case attribute emergency_authorized == True,
    (ii) Obtain Legal Opinion within 24h of Sanctions Escalation,
    (iii) opinion still eventually obtained (R4 never waived)."""
    results = []
    for cid, evs in traces.items():
        ts = {e["concept:name"]: e["time:timestamp"] for e in evs}
        esc, opl, act = ts.get("Sanctions Escalation"), \
                        ts.get("Obtain Legal Opinion"), ts.get("Activate Vendor")
        breach = esc and opl and act and (opl > act)          # activation preceded opinion
        if not breach:
            results.append((cid, "PASS", None)); continue
        if opl is None:
            results.append((cid, "FAIL_STRICT", "R4 violated: no opinion at all")); continue
        granted = case_attrs.get(cid, {}).get("emergency_authorized", None)
        within = (opl - esc).total_seconds() <= 24 * 3600
        if granted is True and within:
            results.append((cid, "WARNING_MANAGED_DEVIATION",
                            "override recorded to compliance registry; human sign-off required"))
        elif granted is None:
            results.append((cid, "FAIL_STRICT",
                            "breach but no authorization evidence in external register"))
        else:
            results.append((cid, "FAIL_STRICT",
                            f"authorization {'missing' if not granted else 'expired'} "
                            f"and/or opinion later than 24h ({(opl-esc).total_seconds()/3600:.1f}h)"))
    return results
```

Attribute-schema expectations and degradation: absence of `org:resource` blocks affirmative SoD clearance; absence of `emergency_authorized` for breaching cases equates to unauthorized (default-deny), preserving R5 verbatim under Resolution B.

# 9. Contradictions, Redundancies, and Blind Spots

1. **Duplicated enforcement:** R1/R8 rules appear in DECLARE, Log-Skeleton `never_together`, and the procedural XOR — triplicate. Redundancy aids forensic triangulation (which layer catches what depends on how the miner samples), but forces a triage policy to avoid alert fatigue; §10 deduplicates.
2. **Loops vs static pairs:** R11 breaks static readability — a naive `always_after(Verify Bank Account, Activate)` would break after the *second* Verify (post-activation); the skeleton sidesteps by pairing CBDSus instead, and never asserting Verify ordering absolutely (Verify flexes {1,2} in `activ_freq` precisely for this reason). Likewise `always_before` relations flip-flop under re-enactment; pairs were selected conservatively to survive iteration.
3. **Vacuity risks:** several obligations (`responded_existence`, several `always_after`s, etc.) are *silent* on healthy trajectories — the rules essentially never bite for smooth traces, granting false assurance during healthy-period audits. Deeper linkage into skeleton numeric keys mitigates latent hollow-rule depreciation. Support/confidence metadata, likewise, must never substitute for policy review: they score *how well data conforms historically*, not whether the encoded rule is *normatively correct*. A rule at confidence 0.95 can still be unlawful; conversely 100%-confirmed spurious correlations can smuggle in constraints regulators would reject.
4. **Missing case attributes:** e.g., authoritative *risk tier* from the classifier is stored in process data (not in activity payload), limiting derivation to proactive gating on the Declare/DotA side — masked under case identity correlations. Currently the best compensating control is surgical pairing: outcome routes reinforce each other's evidence.
5. **Timestamp-only limitations:** with second-granularity stamps, event ordering around concurrency is reconstructed, not witnessed — causal invocation versus simple simultaneity is unknowable, especially under millisecond coalescence boundaries (cf. request/response pairs landing adjacent ticks). Contested merging could flatten directive evidence; care is warranted before treating isolated temporal anomalies as control-flow proof.
6. **Resource/data blindness formally unavoidable:** declaring SoD in either formal-only artifact would be a false promise: activity traces alone never reveal performers; whenever the check drops (`org:resource` NaN), the architecture preserves ambiguity *publicly* rather than inventing a false pass.
