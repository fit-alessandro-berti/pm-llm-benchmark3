## 1. Control-Objective Decomposition

**FEP-1 Objectives:**
1. **Prevent Single-Actor Fraud:** Avoid one actor both preparing and releasing payments, minimizing the chance of malicious transfers.
   - **Preventive:** AV-2 (automated checks for anomalies or fraud indicators).
   - **Detective:** RA-7 (one-day audit).
   - **Corrective:** RA-7 can recover funds if issues are caught early.
   - **Fails to Address:** MG-4, ED-1 (these rely on humans handling fraud properly).

2. **Detect Collusion Risks:** Ensure collusion signals (e.g., shared resources or close relationships) are identified and mitigated.
   - **Preventive:** None (collusion is inherently a social issue).
   - **Detective:** RA-7 catches fabricated service receipts but may miss collusion outright.
   - **Corrective:** None; after-settlement actions have limited effect.
   - **Fails to Address:** AV-2, MG-4 do not evaluate organizational relationships or incentives.

3. **Validate Payment Legitimacy:** Ensure payments conform to business rules, contracts, and receipts.
   - **Preventive:** AV-2 validates against known patterns (e.g., duplicate invoice, limits, sanctions lists).
   - **Detective:** MG-4 manager review of evidence, RA-7 audit of receipt legitimacy.
   - **Corrective:** RA-7 if recall is possible.
   - **Fails to Address:** ED-1 bypasses receipt review in emergencies.

4. **Mitigate Process Errors:** Catch and respond to unintentional process failures or misconfigurations.
   - **Preventive:** AV-2 blocks obvious technical errors.
   - **Detective:** RA-7 audits process for oversight gaps.
   - **Corrective:** ED-1 ensures minimal emergency eligibility criteria, but control failure must still show up in audits.
   - **Fails to Address:** Post-settlement MG-4, as issues may no longer be correctable.

5. **Limit Catastrophic Exposure:** Ensure rapid detection and intervention when large amounts or high-risk conditions (e.g., new vendors, recent bank changes) are present.
   - **Preventive:** AV-2 warning or blocking is critical here.
   - **Detective:** RA-7 audits, though timing (e.g., 4 days in AP-208) may leave exposure.
   - **Corrective:** Manager intervention pre-settlement provides limited recovery opportunity.
   - **Fails to Address:** Late approvals and opaque processes (e.g., ED-1 misuses or override-sharing like AP-202).

## 2. Equivalence and Sufficiency Test

**Equivalence Thresholds:**
- **Full Equivalence:** Independent roles, a clean AV-2 pass, timely MG-4 review pre-settlement, strong RA-7 coverage (audits before recall window), and no detectable override sharing or collusion.
- **Partial Equivalence:** Automated validations and timely independent manager review address most objectives, but some objectives (e.g., validating service receipt) rely on detective steps post-settlement.
- **Merely Mitigating:** Automated checks and manual audits exist but lack either independence, speed, or comprehensive coverage.
- **Ineffective:** Compensating controls fail to reliably prevent or detect key risks (e.g., when AV-2 is out, managers are late, or audits miss recovery timing).

**Standards Beyond Expected Loss:**
- Timing and independence determine whether controls are preventative or reactive.
- Collusion resistance matters; repeated resource pairs are particularly concerning.
- Overridden warnings or weakly enforced eligibility rules erode trust in ex-post controls.
- Criminal/legal risks (e.g., fraud linked to employees' relatives) must also be weighed.

## 4. Preventive–Detective–Corrective Comparison

| Event                                   | Timing Relative to Risk | Classification | Rationale                                                                                                           |
|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------|----------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Manager review **before settlement**    | After release            | Hybrid         | Detects post-Release_Payment issues; prevents irrevocable settlement.                                               |
| Manager review **after settlement**     | After risk materializes  | Detective      | Detects issues post-mortem but cannot prevent exposure once funds settle.                                           |
| Audit review (timely)                   | Before recall window     | Detective + Corrective | Provides chance to recall funds and address process gaps.                                                          |
| Automated validation (real-time)        | Before Release_Payment   | Preventive     | Blocks or warns before funds exit, directly thwarting some anomalies.                                               |
| Emergency-detection conditions (ED-1)   | Before release           | Hybrid         | Removes precondition risks but depends critically on manager reliability and independent final audit safeguards.     |

## 5. Counterfactual and Residual-Risk Analysis

1. **AP-202:** Plausible loss from insider fraud; AV-2 warning could avoid it if enforceable. A second independent releaser would most likely block misuse of an override credential.
2. **AP-204:** Fraud via collusion possible. A second workstation or more agile audit would detect it, though it may evade RA-7.
3. **AP-208:** AV-2 unavailability left high vendor-bank-ownership risk. Independent releasers would question vendor legitimacy.
4. **AP-206:** Documentation/ED-1 timing inconsistency. Better timestamp integrity would clarify causation but may not affect residual risk much.
5. **AP-207:** Plausible single-use emergency; structural improvement hinges on whether FEP-1 justifies more conservative fallback layers (e.g., non-paired handlers).

## 6. Evidence and Monitoring Recommendations

**Monitoring Rules:**
1. Record **same-user instance rates** for Prepare/Release—alert above 5%.
2. Detect **AV-2 overrides** or warnings—mandatory escalation at the time of override.
3. Monitor **manager approvals** post-settlement—flag late approvals unless documented urgency exists.
4. Trigger alerts for **audit timelines exceeding the recall window**.
5. Raise warnings for **repeated resource pairs** acting jointly within 30 days.
6. Track **shared workstations** and investigate possible collusion.
7. Log **bank-change proximity** events—require dual releases for recent changes.
8. Log **validation outages** (e.g., AP-208)—investigate AV-2 failures immediately.
