## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning
• Regulatory/compliance losses dominate because they are orders of magnitude larger (€100-500 k) and include licence & reputation threats.  
• Actions A & B carry that risk with certainty; any operational saving (€20-35 k) is dwarfed and cannot compensate.  
• Among conformant options, expected monetary benefit = (probability of meeting deadline) × (€20-35 k).  
  – Composite 1 gives highest chance (0.70-0.85) without hurting P-901 badly, so €14-30 k upside against €5 k expected spillover.  
  – Action C yields similar upside but larger spillover cost, so weakly dominated.  
• False-rejection cost is higher-tail (€15-80 k) but only realised if we reject. Therefore Action F is inferior unless compliance proof threshold for rejection is already met—a fact not in evidence.  
• Non-monetary harms (regulator trust, press) are effectively infinite for the business case, so any non-conformant shortcut is ruled out.
