## 1. Conformance semantics
**Forbidden behavior** is explicitly disallowed by the norm (e.g., `N3`, `T6`, `E10`). **Missing mandatory behavior** occurs when a required relation (e.g., `N1`, `N4`, `E1–E8`) is absent within its stated scope. **Unexpected but unregulated behavior** involves relations not covered by the norm (e.g., `N6`, `T4`, `T5`), which are open-world by default. **Typical-pattern absence** (e.g., `N2`, `N5`, `T3`) indicates deviations from common but non-mandatory expectations. **Likely data-quality errors** (e.g., `Sensor Heartbeat`–Invoice) are artifacts like misjoined IDs. **Abstraction mismatch** arises when a normative "eventually" rule (e.g., `N1`) is checked as a directly-follows relation. **Insufficient evidence** applies when counts are low (e.g., 1 `object_cobirth` edge) or context is missing (e.g., unrecorded review events).

**Open-world vs. closed-world**: Only `forbidden` relations and `mandatory` relations within scope are closed-world. All else (e.g., `typical`, `unspecified`) permits unmodeled behavior unless explicitly prohibited.
