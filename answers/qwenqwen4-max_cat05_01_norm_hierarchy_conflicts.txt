- **I7 (evidence: substance over system latency).** A timestamp is a claim about an act, not the act. ON44's skew is applied only to 0714 recovery entries; P505's "nine minutes fast" (0718) and P507's documentqueue delay (0717) are **separate, unverified clock/latency claims** requiring their own corroboration.
- **I8 (burden of proof).** The party asserting an exception must prove its trigger, scope, window **and actor authority**. Unproven exception  the base rule applies. Conversely, an *absent record* of a required event is prima facie nonsatisfaction, not proof of a breach of an unstated rule.
- **I9 (necessity mitigates; it does not create authority).** Genuine urgency reduces culpability and supports ratification; it cannot supply a missing CRO signature.
- **I10 (no retrocure).** A6 applies to every postcommitment approval and to any later ratification.
- **I11 (cumulative application).** Stricter local rules (B1, B2 €25k, B3, B6) apply *alongside* GPIP4; C3's 4hour record and B6's 24hour form are different artifacts and both bind.

**Assumptions with defeaters** `[A]`
- **A1** `Finance_Approve` was executed by a Finance Controller/delegate (defeater: role log shows a noncontroller).
- **A2** `Plant_Approve` was by the Plant Manager as budget owner, independent of requester/committer (defeater: identity/independence check).
- **A3** "Unapproved supplier"  "new supplier" for A3 (defeater: evidence of prior vetting/delisting; then B3 breach remains but A3 falls away).
- **A4** No undisclosed written CRO exception exists (defeater: production of one flips several cases).
- **A5** The listed event set is materially complete per case (defeater: signed PDFs, phone logs, mail archives showing unrecorded timely acts).

**Method:** for each case, fix the **act time** (best evidence, not ERP), then test (1) temporal scope, (2) subjectmatter scope, (3) applicable base rules at that instant, (4) each claimed exception against trigger/scope/window/actor, (5) residual deviations, (6) severity and confidence separately. **Severity scale:** Critical = integrity gate defeated + material value + no valid authority; High = mandatory precommitment integrity/Compliance gate missed at material value; Medium = required approval absent/late or scope/sequencing breach without integrity defeat; Low = actor/sequencing/documentation defect with genuine urgency and substantive controls intact.

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

**P501  JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION or COMPLIANT (credible, strongest of all).** Premise change: read B4's two limbs as **independent** — "commitment may precede the PO" is actorneutral, and the delegation clause governs only *substituting for Plant_Approve*. Combined with I4 (B2 states no timing), every applicable requirement is met: genuine 6h emergency, approved supplier, both approvals obtained, form at 23h. Credible because it invents no obligation; weak only because it lets an undelegated shift lead commit while the designated manager was reachable, defeating B4's control purpose. **Reversal evidence:** a standing authority matrix entry permitting Shift Lead commitment, or proof Plant_Approve 15:10 was the PM's own independent approval.

**P502  JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION (weaktomoderate).** Requires **three** premise changes: (i) ECD2 counts as an A7 exception by custom/necessity; (ii) C4's "supplieronboarding requirements" includes newsupplier Compliance clearance; (iii) IC members hold budgetowner delegations **and** the CFO may substitute A2's Finance limb. Credible on (i) alone given documented overlay practice and CRO absence; **weak precisely here** because the practice fact excludes newsupplier clearance, and A5 forbids silent weakening. Note that even accepting (i) and (iii) leaves A3 breached — the classification only flips if (ii) also holds.

**P503  INDETERMINATE (weak).** Premise change: the ERP `Emergency_Authorize` entry misattributes PROC3 (e.g., entered on his behalf for another IC member), so A1 is not established. Weak because ON44's skew window is 0714 only and cannot explain a 0715 entry, and because A2/A3 breaches are independent of the actor question. **A scopeonly alternative fails**: adopting Operations' broad "critical contingency" reading rescues B1 but leaves A1/A2/A3 untouched — the classification is scoperobust.

**P505  JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION (moderate).** Premise change: commitment formed at ~17:57–17:59:31 (verified mail clock + acceptance content), **and** D1 validly reconfigures A2's Finance limb, **and** one IC authorizer holds a budget delegation. Then all controls precede commitment inside the window and the ERP 18:06 entry is a clock artifact. Credible on timing (two independent artifacts beat one ERP field); weak on authority, since D3/D4 and A7 do not support the CFO substitution. **Reversal evidence:** acceptancebearing acknowledgment + clock audit + delegation instrument.

**P507  JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION (moderate).** Premise change: A2's dual approval is satisfiable by two IC authorizers where the CFO's D1 interpretation is treated as binding on Finance **and** the COO's directive is treated as carrying executive budget delegation. Add verified PDF creation at 12:40 and every control (Compliance, joint authorization, recording, PO flexibility) is timely. Credible because Compliance clearance — the control that actually protects against unvettedsupplier risk — was obtained **before** commitment; weak because it requires reading budget ownership into a charter that is silent.

**P508  JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION (moderatestrong on the merits, weak on authority).** Premise change: recognise a **necessitybased, ratifiable** exception category where (a) all integrity controls were performed precommitment, (b) SoD was preserved, (c) Legal was added above €150k, and (d) the CRO was unavailable. This is the best candidate for a "substance over form" reading. It remains weak under the current text because A4 says other controls remain "unless" a CRO exception issues, and no ratification occurred. **Reversal evidence:** a CRO (or proven deputy) ratification with explicit noncure language, plus Finance Controller/budgetowner confirmations — which would make this the model case for the clarified clause in §7.

**Nonflipping alternative worth noting:** for P504, rebutting A3 (supplier previously vetted, merely delisted) removes the A3 breach but leaves B1, B3 and the false emergency characterization  classification unchanged. For P506, a written extension alone rescues B1 but not A2's late budgetowner limb  two discoveries needed to flip.

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency and Remediation

**Four distinct categories — do not conflate them**

1. **Established policy breach** (act proven, rule applicable, no valid exception): P503 A1/A2/A3; P502 A3 (clearance 8h15 late, commit time corroborated); P504 B1/B3 and outofscope emergency use; P506 B1 and late budgetowner approval; P502/P505/P507/P508 A2 (no precommit dual approval on any reading).
2. **Logging / records defect** (act apparently timely, record late or missing): P507 ERP authorization at 14:30; P505 ERP commit at 18:06; missing `Record_Emergency_Form` in P502/503/505/506/507/508 (B6); missing C3 written records in P503/505/508. These are **controlevidence failures**, not proof that the underlying act was late.
3. **Late evidence of a timely act**: P507 signed PDF created 12:40 (3h45) queued during a document outage; P502 verbal 10:10 recorded 12:55 (expressly permitted by C3); P505 acknowledgment 17:59:31. Accept **only** with verified creation time / queue logs / content — otherwise reclassify as category 4.
4. **Inability to prove compliance** (no affirmative breach proof, but control not evidenced): actor identities for A1 in P502/505/506/507; IC budgetowner status everywhere; Plant approval and PO existence in P505; P501 SHIFT1 authority; P506 claimed verbal extension. Under I8 these remain **unproven**, and unproven  compliant.
5. **Crosscutting: authoritydesign defect** — ECD2's missing A7 coverage. This is an organizational breach of A4/A5 by the directive's issuers, analytically prior to individual case faults.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Draft clause — "Hierarchy, Emergency Exceptions and Evidence (replaces GPIP4 A4–A7 and SOP B7)"**

1. **Hierarchy.** GPIP4 integrity controls — segregation of duties, dual financial authorization, Compliance clearance — rank above any incident directive, operational instruction or email, regardless of recency or wording. Local SOPs may add stricter controls and define operational evidence; they may never weaken GPIP4. Laterintime and specificity arguments operate only between instruments of equal rank.
2. **Exception authority.** Only the CRO, or a **standing prenamed deputy** activated automatically when the CRO is unavailable >2 hours, may except a GPIP4 control, in writing, scoped, valuecapped and timelimited, **before** the act. A COO/IncidentCommander directive is valid **without countersignature only** for an enumerated list: (a) PO/commitment sequencing, (b) competitive tendering, (c) supplierregister onboarding lead time. To touch financial authorization or Compliance clearance it requires CRO/deputy countersignature within 4 hours; absent that, those controls apply unmodified. **Segregation of duties is never waivable.**
3. **Scope.** "Critical lot" means a component with a recorded necessity link to sustaining or restoring the affected sterilization capacity, evidenced by lot ID plus engineering attestation that need arises within 24 hours or that buffer stock is <24 hours. Contingency/backup purchases and all nonproduction purposes (catering, administration, facilities) are excluded. The requesting party may not selfclassify scope.
4. **Thresholds and emergency quorum.** €25k: Plant + Finance approval. €50k: independent budget owner **and** Finance Controller **before commitment**; in a declared emergency the quorum may be two distinct Incident Command members **provided at least one holds a written budget delegation and one is a Finance Controller or delegate**. >€100k or any new supplier: expedited Compliance clearance before commitment (target 2h; verbal permitted, written 4h). >€150k: add Legal clearance. Percommitment emergency cap €250k; above it, CRO + CFO joint written authorization.
5. **Segregation of duties.** A requester or committer is never counted toward any authorizing quorum and never becomes final business approver; ERP hard block. Delegation must be written, active, timeboxed and recorded **before** the act; retroactive delegation is void.
6. **New suppliers.** Register status may be waived provisionally; **Compliance clearance may not**. Provisional registration expires after 30 days unless full vetting completes.
7. **Records and retrospective evidence.** Verbal authorization is permitted; written record within 4 hours; emergency form within 24 hours. A system entry timestamp is rebuttable by a contemporaneous artifact with a verifiable creation time and queue/audit log; **late evidence of a timely act is not a late act**, but an act with no qualifying evidence is **unproven**, and the burden lies on the party asserting compliance. Ratification must be requested within 72 hours and must state expressly that it mitigates consequences and **does not cure** the missing precommitment control.
8. **Expiry.** Directive authority ends at the stated instant. Extensions require writing by the issuing authority **plus** CRO/deputy countersignature **before** expiry; verbal extensions are void; systems revert automatically. Maximum cumulative duration 7 days, then Risk Committee approval.
9. **Antinormalization safeguards.** (a) Emergencydirective spend capped at 5% of quarterly site spend — exceeding it suspends directive authority automatically; (b) mandatory postincident review within 15 days, with the exception register published to the Risk Committee; (c) three or more directives in a rolling quarter triggers mandatory policy redesign rather than reissuance; (d) mislabeling a noncritical purchase as critical is a disciplinary integrity offence; (e) quarterly Audit testing of scope labels, offregister use and commitbeforePO frequency, reported as normalization indicators.
10. **Evidence infrastructure.** Clocksynchronisation monitoring for ERP, telephony, mail and document systems; outage notices attach automatically to affected records and adjust evidential weight only — never normative obligations.
