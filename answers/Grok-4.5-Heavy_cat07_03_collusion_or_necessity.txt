# 2. Benign alternative explanations

**Buyer_Carla ↔ Approver_Gita (fast approvals, four-eyes hits, subcontracting loops, emergency medical supplies)**  
- Alternative 1 – Formal/informal specialisation + emergency handling. Carla is the designated high-volume specialist for emergency medical supplies; Gita is the delegated urgent-case approver. Fast times and loops reflect a known rush channel. Supporting evidence: 88 % emergency_flag, low vendor concentration, presence of Emergency Override activity. Refuting evidence: systematic absence of medical-urgency documentation, justifications missing or late beyond the 3-working-day window by the same principal, or exclusive pairing that exceeds documented specialisation.  
- Alternative 2 – On-call / rota arrangement for medical emergencies rather than personal preference. Supporting evidence: cases distributed across shifts consistent with a rota; multiple resources appear in related activities. Refuting evidence: financial or performance linkage between the pair and specific vendors outside normal metrics.

**Buyer_Dan ↔ Manager_Hugo (facilities repairs, weekend cases, fast overrides)**  
- Alternative 1 – Emergency facilities protocol. Dan specialises in repairs; Hugo handles exception overrides. Supporting evidence: 79 % emergency_flag, weekend timestamps, low vendor concentration. Refuting evidence: overrides without subsequent independent justification or repair work-order documentation.  
- Alternative 2 – Legitimate managerial escalation for operational continuity. Supporting evidence: linkage to known facility-incident logs. Refuting evidence: repeated same-person justifications or missing Retrospective Justification within policy.

**VendorMaster_Lena → AP_Kim (bank-detail changes + payment approval, V-77x concentration, manual_upload, fast timing)**  
- Alternative 1 – Legitimate mass bank-platform migration for vendor family V-77x together with role adjacency. Supporting evidence: known platform change, many changes described as maintenance, missing ticket-to-case linkages explained by data-model limitations. Refuting evidence: changed bank accounts belonging to entities linked to the resources, or payments released without any dual-control evidence outside the event-log fields.  
- Alternative 2 – Data-quality / process-design artefact rather than intentional bypass. Supporting evidence: 83 % manual_upload share, source-system patterns, possible conflation of maintenance tickets. Refuting evidence: access logs showing coordinated activity without ticket reference or subsequent payment diversions.

**Same-resource Emergency Override + Retrospective Justification (17 cases)**  
- Alternative 1 – Quarter-close data-migration artefact. Supporting evidence: 12/17 identical timestamps from quarter_close_upload spreadsheet. Refuting evidence: original source timestamps showing real-time same-person entry without batch characteristics.  
- Alternative 2 – Shared-account / assistant entry under manager credentials for documentation catch-up. Supporting evidence: known shared “Approver_Gita” account with assistant entered_by values. Refuting evidence: detailed audit trail showing the principal themselves performing both actions without delegation record.

**Shared-account and mapping problems**

Supporting evidence: explicit notes on shared accounts and legacy_erp mapping-table errors. Refuting evidence: deliberate exploitation of shared credentials beyond the documented mapping issues.

# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Evidence that would increase concern**  
- Access logs or detailed application audit trails showing Lena and Kim performing bank-detail change and payment-approval actions under individual credentials within minutes, without intervening independent review or ticket reference, outside the known V-77x platform-migration window.  
- Discovery that changed bank accounts for V-77x cases belong to entities controlled by or linked to the resources (or close associates), or that payments were diverted relative to prior bank details.

- Financial or performance incentives linking the paired resources to specific vendors outside normal procurement metrics.  
- Evidence that shared-account usage or legacy_erp mapping was deliberately exploited to conceal repeated same-person control breaches beyond the documented mapping errors.  
- Pattern of high-amount cases repeatedly using the fast path without corresponding emergency justification.

**Evidence that would reduce concern**  
- Existence of migration-project tickets, change-management records or vendor notifications documenting the V-77x banking-platform switch, with case or vendor IDs matching the 44 Lena–Kim cases.  
- Detailed audit-trail fields (entered_by, approved_by, justification text, source ticket ID) showing that apparent four-eyes violations and fast actions were performed by different human assistants under shared accounts, by bots, or were post-facto data loads.  
- Confirmation that Carla is formally designated specialist for emergency medical supplies and Gita is the rostered urgent approver; analogous designation for Dan/Hugo on facilities; and that Emergency Override policy explicitly permits same-day approval with independent Retrospective Justification within 3 days.  
- Service-level or on-call logs showing the observed fast medians align with documented emergency response times for medical and facilities categories.  
- Independent review (e.g., by AP supervisor or external party) of a sample of V-77x payments confirming correct payee and dual control outside the event-log org:resource field.
