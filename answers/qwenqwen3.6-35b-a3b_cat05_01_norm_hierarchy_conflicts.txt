## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Replacement Hierarchy & Exception Clause:**
1. **Authority & Scope:** GPIP-4 is the foundational control framework. ECD-2 and local SOPs may suspend competitive tendering, PO sequencing, and approved-supplier lists during active contamination events. Only the Chief Risk Officer (CRO) may issue written, scoped, time-limited exceptions to GPIP-4 A1 (SoD), A2 (Finance/Budget), and A3 (Compliance). COO/IC directives or CFO guidance may substitute Plant_Approve with IC joint authorization for purchases €150k, but do not override Finance/Compliance pre-commitment or SoD without CRO ratification.
2. **Thresholds & Pre-Commitment:** Finance/Budget approval (€50k) and Compliance_Clearance (>€100k or new supplier) must occur before `Commit_to_Supplier`. Emergency continuity may justify post-commitment processing if documented as “Emergency Retro-Approval” with root cause, but this does not cure the breach for audit purposes.
3. **Segregation of Duties:** The requester, committer, and final business approver (including IC acting as approver) must be distinct individuals. Verbal or informal approvals do not satisfy A1.
4. **Retrospective Records & Expiry:** ERP timestamps are rebuttable by contemporaneous logs (phone recordings, signed PDFs, email timestamps). ECD-2 expires automatically at stated time; extensions require written IC/CRO notation. Verbal extensions are invalid.
5. **Safeguard Against Emergency Normalization:** All emergency purchases >€100k or involving new suppliers require mandatory CRO ratification within 48 hours post-incident. Failure to ratify reverts the purchase to standard procurement with mandatory management review and potential disciplinary action. Post-incident audits must verify that emergency waivers were strictly scoped to sterilization-line continuity and did not normalize bypassing core integrity controls.
