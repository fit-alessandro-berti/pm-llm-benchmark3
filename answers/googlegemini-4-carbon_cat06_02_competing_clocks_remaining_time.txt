### Calculation shortcuts and queueing approximations
1. **Elapsed incubation offset:** Microbiology started Friday at 14:00 (1.5 h before $t_0 = 15:30$), so 46–54 h total incubation equals **44.5–52.5 h remaining** from $t_0$.
2. **Triangular means:** Chemistry primary service $\text{Tri}(1.5, 2.5, 4.5)$ has mean $2.83\text{ h}$ (median $\approx 2.68\text{ h}$); chemistry rerun $\text{Tri}(1.0, 1.8, 3.0)$ has mean $1.93\text{ h}$.
3. **QA fluid/renewal backlog projection:** Fitting median $1.5\text{ h}$ and P80 $3.5\text{ h}$ to a lognormal service law yields mean $\approx 2.49\text{ h}$ (throughput $\approx 0.40\text{ batches/reviewer-hour}$). Between Friday 15:30 and Monday 08:00 there are $8.5$ single-reviewer QA hours (Fri 15:30–20:00 = 4.5 h; Sat 09:00–13:00 = 4.0 h). Expected completions ($\approx 3.4$ batches) nearly offset expected arrivals ($0.35 \times 8.5 \approx 3.0$ batches, overdispersed). Thus, the initial 5-batch QA queue does **not** vanish over the weekend: at Monday 08:00 it plausibly holds **2–9 batches** (central ~4.5–5), after which **two reviewers** ($\approx 0.80\text{ batches/h}$ combined capacity vs. $0.35\text{ /h}$ arrivals) drain the backlog at $\approx 0.45\text{ batches/h}$.
4. **Parallel synchronization (`max`, not `sum`):** QA can receive B-417 only at $T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{chem}}, T_{\text{micro}})$. Summing branch durations would falsely double-count the 64.5 h weekend span and Monday morning overlap.
