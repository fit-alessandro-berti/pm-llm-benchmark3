## 1. Executive causal assessment
**Verdict:** Mainly confounded. **Confidence:** 85/100.

The aggregate data suggests Orion is slower (21.96h vs 15.24h), but stratified analysis reveals a consistent reversal: within every complexity stratum (Low, Medium, High), Orion cases complete faster than Nova cases. For example, High-complexity Orion cases average 31.57h versus 35.00h for Nova. This indicates the observed delay is driven by case mix (Orion handles 43% High complexity vs Nova's 12.5%) and shift patterns (Orion is 93% Night vs Nova's 25%), rather than intrinsic group inefficiency.

The strongest evidence for confounding is the uniform reversal across complexity strata. The strongest evidence against a purely confounded verdict is the "completed cases only" selection; if Orion abandons or delays difficult cases that Nova completes, the sample is biased. Additionally, Night shifts (Orion) may inherently suffer from handover delays not captured in `wait_before_review_h`.

The proposed 50% reassignment will likely backfire. Transferring Orion's high-complexity volume to Nova will force Nova to process cases where it is demonstrably slower (35h vs 31.6h). Without adding capacity or adjusting routing logic to match complexity to group strengths, total system cycle time will increase, and Nova's queue will spike. Management is mistaking a selection effect for a performance deficit.

## 8. Residual uncertainty
1.  **Unobserved Reviewer Skill:** We know Orion has "more certified reviewers" but lack individual performance data. *Data needed:* `reviewer_id` linked to `cycle_time`.
2.  **Abandoned Cases:** Only completed cases are present; if Orion drops difficult cases, their cycle time is artificially low. *Data needed:* `case_status` (Open, Closed, Abandoned).
3.  **Routing Logic Transparency:** We assume `routing_rule` is based on complexity, but it might include hidden factors (e.g., customer tier). *Data needed:* `routing_decision_reason` or `routing_rule_version`.
4.  **Compliance Duration:** `wait_before_review_h` includes compliance wait, but we don't know if compliance *duration* varies by group. *Data needed:* `compliance_duration_h` (start to end of check).
5.  **Quality Measurement:** `outcome_30d` is coarse (Appeal vs No appeal) and may lag process completion. *Data needed:* `quality_score` (e.g., audit pass/fail) at case closure.
