## 10. Final claim ladder
* Observed: Automation availability is staggered and non-random, while actual automated fast-path use rises gradually and coincides with routing, staffing, and case-mix changes.
* Suggestive: Adopters show higher escalation and tail cycle times after availability, and never-treated branches show similar cycle-time rises from complexity, indicating confounding.
* Conditionally causal: Under parallel trends and no spillovers, Gamma’s post-adoption median reduction is consistent with a modest beneficial effect of actual use, but the estimate is not identified with current aggregates.
* Not supported: A single statement that automation reduces cycle time by X% for all branches, or that automation improves customer satisfaction, is not supported by the supplied branch-month data.
