## 1. Conformance semantics

The norm is a partial policy, not an exhaustive description of legitimate behavior. Classification must distinguish a recorded discrepancy from a verified business breach.

- **Forbidden behavior:** an explicitly prohibited relation occurs within scope. Even one occurrence is a conformance signal; provenance determines whether it reflects actual behavior or recording error.
- **Missing mandatory behavior:** a required relation is absent from an applicable event or history. Establish scope, observation completeness, and temporal semantics before concluding that the business obligation was missed.
- **Unexpected but unregulated behavior:** an unspecified or unlisted relation occurs. It is admissible under this norm unless another applicable obligation or prohibition is violated.
- **Typical-pattern absence:** a desirable pattern is missing. Contextual review is appropriate, not automatic noncompliance.
- **Likely data-quality error:** an association, identifier, timestamp, or event classification probably misrepresents reality. Preserve the recorded discrepancy while investigating its cause.
- **Abstraction mismatch:** a graph condition, such as immediate succession, differs from a business condition, such as eventual validation.
- **Insufficient evidence:** aggregation, unknown scope, incomplete histories, or undefined lifecycles prevent a defensible determination.

Closed-world reasoning applies to explicit prohibitions and mandatory requirements within scope. Other relations remain open-world: absence from a normative graph does not prohibit them. Extra event–object links are permissible unless forbidden. No frequency tolerance excuses a prohibition or converts typical behavior into mandatory behavior.

## 3. Detailed reasoning by model family

### OC-DFG

An OC-DFG describes successive activities along object histories. Counts identify observed transitions and gaps; they cannot establish complete histories, deadlines, event accuracy, or ultimate outcomes.

N1 requires eventual validation for newly created **standard** Orders. Its 98/100 directly-follows coverage is not necessarily a 98% business compliance rate: scope and completion remain unverified. An intervening event can remove adjacency while preserving eventual validation. A genuinely **unrecorded** review alone cannot interrupt observed adjacency if creation and validation are both present on the same Order. Therefore check observed intermediate events, omitted validation links, projection, and observation boundaries. Review substitutes for validation only if an authorized rule establishes equivalence.

N4's scope is supported by the 200 externally shipped Packages; its two missing transitions cannot be excused as internal shipments. Determine whether packing is absent, incorrectly ordered, or separated from dispatch, and whether N4 requires adjacency or precedence. E4/E5 participation does not prove packing preceded dispatch for each Package.

N3 prohibits dispatch after cancellation even with intervening events. The two observed transitions are positive signals, but checking only adjacent pairs would miss other breaches.

N2 and N5 are typical. The 83 closing and 72 invoicing observations need not describe disjoint Orders; repeated activity and alternative paths prevent simple subtraction. For 110 Invoices, 92 payments, four write-offs and 14 without successors account for reported outcomes, but do not establish delinquency. N6 remains unspecified.

### OTG

An OTG establishes observed relations between object types and their weights. Edge presence does not prove every scoped interaction has required participants. Weights may count distinct pairs, occurrences, or another aggregation; do not automatically equate them with event counts.

Thus 200 Package–Delivery interactions can coexist with two dispatches missing Delivery links: other events or multiple relationships may supply the aggregate edge. Likewise, 1450 Order–Item interactions cannot certify every Add Item event. T3's 92 Invoice–Payment interactions are typical, whereas E8 imposes an event-level obligation with an explicitly typed exception.

T6 signals a forbidden birth association, but first appearance need not be business creation. T4/T5 are unspecified. Codeath is outside coverage; the seven shared confirmations are benign on the supplied explanation.

### ET–OT

ET–OT exposes event-type/object-type participation, including E10's forbidden link. Universal requirements need event-level checks: totals can hide missing links, repeated relations, or multiple objects per event.

The Add Item weights differ by two, but this does **not** prove exactly two deficient events. Additional context does establish two missing Dispatch–Delivery links and one missing Record Payment–Invoice link. Carrier attributes do not satisfy E6 without a modeled Delivery relation. Informal explanations of unapplied cash do not activate E8's explicit-typing exception.

Equal counts across families do not establish identity-level agreement. Normal type-level interactions can coexist with event-level violations. E9 and extra Customer participation permit the three reviews; rarity has no independent normative significance.

## 4. Mandatory, forbidden, typical, and unregulated behavior

- **Mandatory:** E1 records 100 Create–Order links; E4/E5 record 200 Package links each, without proving universal coverage. N1's two and N4's two transition gaps need scope/history checks. E2/E3/T1 need individual Add Item checks. E6/T2 require dispatch participation despite OTG-edge presence. E7/E8 require payment participation; the unlinked payment qualifies for E8's exception only with justified explicit typing.
- **Forbidden:** two N3 sequences, one T6 cobirth edge, and one E10 association are recorded prohibition signals. Verified histories establish business breaches; proven recording errors change their causal classification to data-quality defects.
- **Typical:** N2's 83 closes and N5's 92 payments are positive observations, not universal guarantees. Four write-offs are permitted exceptions; 14 successor-free Invoices require maturity/window context.
- **Unregulated:** 72 Validate–Invoice paths, three Create–Review paths, one Reopen–Close path, seven codeaths, 100 Order–Customer links, three Order–RiskCase links, and the three reviews' RiskCase/Customer participation. Other applicable mandatory or forbidden rules still constrain surrounding histories.

## 5. PM4Py conformance code sketch

Preserve library diagnostics as structural evidence. Policy metadata and event-level checks form a separate interpretation. The adapter supports a first argument accepting either the observed OCEL or graph; the sketch assumes the reference model is second and a release exposing all six functions.

```python
import inspect
import pm4py

# Supplied: ocel, norm_ocdfg (dict), norm_otg (tuple), norm_etot (tuple).
observed_models = {
    "OC-DFG": pm4py.discover_ocdfg(ocel),
    "OTG": pm4py.discover_otg(ocel),
    "ET-OT": pm4py.discover_etot(ocel),
}


def observed_input(function, graph):
    # Check the installed API: some interfaces take an OCEL first.
    first = next(iter(inspect.signature(function).parameters))
    return ocel if first in {"ocel", "log", "observed_ocel"} else graph


# Preserve these outputs intact; do not rewrite them as business verdicts.
raw_diagnostics = {
    "OC-DFG": pm4py.conformance_ocdfg(
        observed_input(pm4py.conformance_ocdfg, observed_models["OC-DFG"]),
        norm_ocdfg,
    ),
    "OTG": pm4py.conformance_otg(
        observed_input(pm4py.conformance_otg, observed_models["OTG"]),
        norm_otg,
    ),
    "ET-OT": pm4py.conformance_etot(
        observed_input(pm4py.conformance_etot, observed_models["ET-OT"]),
        norm_etot,
    ),
}

# Maintain statuses/scopes OUTSIDE bare graph structures.
# Forbidden edges are negative constraints, not expected positive edges.
# Never assign a default "forbidden" status to an unlisted relation.
policy_rows = [
    ("N1", "OC-DFG", ("Order", "Create Order", "Validate Order"),
     "mandatory", "new standard Orders; eventual validation"),
    ("N2", "OC-DFG", ("Order", "Validate Order", "Close Order"),
     "typical", "intermediate activities permitted"),
    ("N3", "OC-DFG", ("Order", "Cancel Order", "Dispatch Package"),
     "forbidden", "any later dispatch for the same cancelled Order"),
    ("N4", "OC-DFG", ("Package", "Pack Package", "Dispatch Package"),
     "mandatory", "Packages intended for external shipment"),
    ("N5", "OC-DFG", ("Invoice", "Issue Invoice", "Record Payment"),
     "typical", "credit/write-off/open-term exceptions permitted"),
    ("N6", "OC-DFG", ("Order", "Reopen Order", "Close Order"),
     "unspecified", "outside coverage"),
    ("T1", "OTG", ("Order", "object_interaction", "Item"),
     "mandatory", "each Add Item interaction"),
    ("T2", "OTG", ("Package", "object_interaction", "Delivery"),
     "mandatory", "each Dispatch Package interaction"),
    ("T3", "OTG", ("Invoice", "object_interaction", "Payment"),
     "typical", "unapplied cash possible"),
    ("T4", "OTG", ("Order", "object_interaction", "Customer"),
     "unspecified", "separate policy"),
    ("T5", "OTG", ("Order", "object_interaction", "RiskCase"),
     "unspecified", "risk review permitted"),
    ("T6", "OTG", ("Order", "object_cobirth", "Payment"),
     "forbidden", "same business creation event"),
    ("E1", "ET-OT", ("Create Order", "Order"), "mandatory", "every event"),
    ("E2", "ET-OT", ("Add Item", "Order"), "mandatory", "every event"),
    ("E3", "ET-OT", ("Add Item", "Item"), "mandatory", "every event"),
    ("E4", "ET-OT", ("Pack Package", "Package"), "mandatory", "every event"),
    ("E5", "ET-OT", ("Dispatch Package", "Package"), "mandatory", "every event"),
    ("E6", "ET-OT", ("Dispatch Package", "Delivery"), "mandatory", "every event"),
    ("E7", "ET-OT", ("Record Payment", "Payment"), "mandatory", "every event"),
    ("E8", "ET-OT", ("Record Payment", "Invoice"),
     "mandatory", "except explicitly typed unapplied cash"),
    ("E9", "ET-OT", ("Manual Compliance Review", "RiskCase"),
     "unspecified", "legitimate review"),
    ("E10", "ET-OT", ("Sensor Heartbeat", "Invoice"),
     "forbidden", "every sensor event"),
]
policy = {
    (family, key): {"id": rid, "status": status, "scope": scope}
    for rid, family, key, status, scope in policy_rows
}

# Check individual events instead of subtracting aggregate edge weights.
eid = ocel.event_id_column
activity = ocel.event_activity
object_type = ocel.object_type_column
linked_types = (
    ocel.relations[[eid, object_type]]
    .drop_duplicates()
    .groupby(eid)[object_type]
    .agg(set)
    .to_dict()
)
# Populate from authoritative explicit typing only; empty in this snapshot.
explicit_unapplied_cash_event_ids = set()
event_assessments = []
for _, event in ocel.events.iterrows():
    event_id, event_type = event[eid], event[activity]
    present = linked_types.get(event_id, set())
    for (family, key), rule in policy.items():
        if family != "ET-OT" or key[0] != event_type:
            continue
        target_type = key[1]
        if rule["id"] == "E8" and event_id in explicit_unapplied_cash_event_ids:
            continue
        missing = rule["status"] == "mandatory" and target_type not in present
        prohibited = rule["status"] == "forbidden" and target_type in present
        if missing or prohibited:
            event_assessments.append({
                "event_id": event_id,
                "norm_id": rule["id"],
                "class": "missing_mandatory_link" if missing
                         else "recorded_forbidden_link",
                "business_breach": "requires provenance/context assessment",
            })
    for target_type in present:
        rule = policy.get(("ET-OT", (event_type, target_type)))
        if rule is None or rule["status"] == "unspecified":
            event_assessments.append({
                "event_id": event_id,
                "relation": (event_type, target_type),
                "class": "unregulated", "severity": "informational",
            })

# Contextual assessments from the supplied summary, separate from raw outputs.
# Resolve these against object histories before declaring business breaches.
context_assessments = [
    {"norm_id": "N1", "gap": 2, "class": "mandatory_df_gap",
     "needs": "standard scope, eventual validation, complete window"},
    {"norm_id": "N4", "gap": 2, "class": "mandatory_df_gap",
     "needs": "packing evidence, sequence and adjacency interpretation"},
    {"norm_id": "N3", "count": 2, "class": "forbidden_sequence_signal",
     "needs": "same-Order authoritative chronology, including nonadjacent events"},
    {"norm_id": "T6", "count": 1, "class": "forbidden_birth_signal",
     "needs": "true business births rather than first observed appearances"},
    {"norm_id": "N5", "absent": 18, "class": "typical_absence",
     "needs": "terms, four write-offs, fourteen open histories"},
    {"norm_id": "E10", "count": 1, "class": "likely_data_quality_error",
     "needs": "reused-identifier join lineage"},
]
semantic_diagnostics = {
    "event_assessments": event_assessments,
    "context_assessments": context_assessments,
    "policy": policy,
    "default_for_unlisted_relations": "unregulated",
    "limitations": [
        "Graph-edge presence cannot establish universally mandatory participation.",
        "Typical absence is advisory; no tolerance thresholds were supplied.",
        "Raw differences require scope, lifecycle and provenance interpretation.",
    ],
}
result = {"observed_models": observed_models,
          "raw_diagnostics": raw_diagnostics,
          "semantic_diagnostics": semantic_diagnostics}
```

## 6. Norm-completeness assessment

1. **Validation timing/scope:** “standard” and “eventually” lack operational definitions; indefinite waiting can conceal missed validation. Define eligibility, deadline, permitted intervening activities, and censored-case handling. **Mandatory**, with adjacency unnecessary.
2. **Packing semantics:** adjacency versus prior packing is unresolved. Legitimate scans could trigger false alarms; missing packing could pass aggregates. Specify packing before external dispatch, intermediate activities, and repacking after changes. **Mandatory**; decide adjacency explicitly.
3. **Cancellation/reopening:** N3/N6 leave reactivation consequences unresolved; teams might infer reopening authorizes dispatch. Preserve N3 and specify any future authorized-reactivation exception through an explicit revision. Cancellation restriction **forbidden**; reactivation safeguards **mandatory** if introduced.
4. **Interaction cardinality:** multiple Orders, Items, Packages or Deliveries per event are undefined; aggregates can hide orphan objects. Specify per-event minimum participation, per-Package delivery coverage, and permitted multiplicities. Required coverage **mandatory**; legitimate extras remain open-world.
5. **Unapplied cash:** practice and E8's typing requirement lack an operational alignment; staff might fabricate Invoice links to suppress diagnostics. Define an authoritative exception marker and reconciliation workflow. Correct classification **mandatory**; unapplied cash permitted with that exception.
6. **Invoice outcomes:** N5 lacks due-date/window treatment; normal credit may be escalated and overdue balances overlooked. Keep payment **typical**, document adjustments, and introduce a separate **mandatory** overdue-review obligation if desired.
7. **Lifecycle definitions:** first/last observations may imitate birth/death, causing false T6 allegations or codeath suppression. Define birth evidence and extraction-boundary handling. Genuine cobirth remains **forbidden**; codeath **explicitly unspecified** unless separately governed.
8. **Identifiers/provenance:** absent namespace and source requirements allow contamination across graph families. Require typed, source-qualified identifiers and auditable joins as **mandatory** data controls; retain E10 as **forbidden**.
9. **Review/customer governance:** absent ownership/substitution rules invite invented prohibitions or treating review as validation. Keep these relations **explicitly unspecified** here, identify the separate policy, and make any validation-equivalence conditions **mandatory**.

These are future clarifications, not retrospective grounds for additional violations.

## 7. Investigation and remediation plan

- **Immediate containment:** prioritize the two N3 histories: verify cancellation and carrier dispatch, block further affected shipments, and address shipments already moving. Prioritize T6 next: preserve creation evidence, establish genuine cobirth, and suspend an implicated creation route if substantiated. Quarantine the E10 link and affected join output; avoid broadly stopping legitimate reviews or confirmations.
- **Data repair:** preserve originals; repair joins/identifiers after provenance checks. Resolve the two carrier references into Delivery relations when supported. Reconcile the unlinked payment and add its correct Invoice link or justified unapplied-cash typing. Repair events, associations and timestamps from authoritative records, never by inventing compliant histories.
- **Process investigation:** trace N3/T6 causes and cases sharing those mechanisms. Examine the two packing histories, two validation gaps and individual Add Item events. Determine invoice maturity and write-off authorization under applicable policies. Separate business findings from recording defects.
- **Norm revision:** resolve Section 6 with process/data owners; version scopes, exceptions and effective dates. Express eventual validation as a history obligation and cancellation as an all-later-events prohibition. Retain unregulated relations unless justified policy governs them.
- **Monitoring:** check mandatory participation per event and temporal obligations per object. Alert on verified prohibitions regardless of rarity. Track open obligations using deadlines and extraction coverage; report typical rates descriptively. Rediscover graphs and rerun diagnostics after repairs, retaining original evidence and semantic decisions.

## 8. Uncertainty statement

N3/T6/E10 prohibition signals, two missing Delivery links, and the untyped payment's E8 recording gap are robust observations. Unlisted relations alone are not violations; typical absence has no breach threshold. Validation, packing, birth and open-Invoice conclusions depend on scope, completeness and timing. E10 likely reflects the suspected join error; other provenance problems remain possible. Summaries cannot establish exact Add Item failures, genuine births, authoritative chronology, eventual outcomes, or identity agreement across equal aggregate counts.
