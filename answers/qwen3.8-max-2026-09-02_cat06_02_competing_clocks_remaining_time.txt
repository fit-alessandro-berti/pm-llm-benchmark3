## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

A compact structural formula is used throughout:

\[
T_{\text{join}} = \max(T_{\text{chemistry usable}}, T_{\text{microbiology usable}})
\]

Then QA can begin only after the next QA opening and after queue waiting:

\[
T_{\text{QA done}} = T_{\text{join}} + \text{QA queue wait} + \text{QA service} + \text{rework}
\]

with all additions converted through the QA working calendar. Terminal time is:

\[
T_{\text{terminal}} = T_{\text{QA done}} + \text{Electronic Release or rejection recording}
\]

The key point is that the two test branches are **parallel**, so their join uses a **maximum**, not a sum. QA and rework are then added sequentially, subject to working-hour pauses.

### Scenario A: Smooth path

**Chemistry waiting and service**

- At Fri 15:30, two analysts are busy: one has 0.4–0.9 h remaining, the other 1.2–2.0 h. Both current jobs can finish before 18:00.
- Ahead of B-417 are one urgent and three ordinary samples. Under FIFO, B-417 is behind all four; under risk-priority, it may move ahead of ordinary samples but still behind the urgent sample.
- Only 2.5 h remain before chemistry closes. After current jobs, residual capacity is roughly 2.1–3.4 analyst-hours. A full chemistry service time is 1.5–4.5 h. Therefore, completing B-417 on Friday requires near-minimum service and favorable priority; I treat Friday completion as a low-probability earliest edge, perhaps <5–10%.
- More likely, B-417 either starts Friday and pauses at 18:00, or starts Monday. Resuming Monday 08:00, a reasonable usable chemistry result window is **Mon 21 Sep 10:00–16:00**, with a tail to **Mon 18:00 pause  Tue 22 Sep 08:00–11:00** under strict FIFO, long services, or new urgent work.

**Microbiology incubation and readout**

- Incubation started Fri 14:00. Technical readiness is 46–54 h later: **Sun 20 Sep 12:00–20:00**.
- If on-call authorization is granted, readout occurs Sunday with 1–3 h dispatch/readout: **Sun 13:00–23:00**.
- If not authorized, the result is read Monday morning: **Mon 08:30–10:00**.
- No contamination indication is assumed in this scenario.

**Parallel join**

- Micro is usable Sunday afternoon/evening or Monday morning.
- Chemistry is usually usable Monday late morning/afternoon.
- Therefore, the join is usually governed by chemistry: **Mon 10:00–16:00**.
- This is a maximum: if micro is ready Sunday and chemistry is ready Monday, the micro result simply waits. We do not add micro time to chemistry time.

**QA waiting and service**

- QA queue starts with five batches at Fri 15:30.
- Fri 15:30–20:00: one reviewer, 4.5 h, arrivals about 1.6 batches. Likely completions 1–3.
- Sat 09:00–13:00: one reviewer, 4 h, arrivals about 1.4 batches. Likely completions 1–3.
- By Monday morning, the backlog is uncertain but not necessarily empty. With two reviewers from Mon 08:00 and arrivals 0.35/h, a plausible queue wait for B-417 at a Monday join is **0.5–5 h** centrally, with a tail of **6–8+ h** if arrivals clustered or long services occurred.
- QA service time is approximated as **1–4 h**, reflecting median 1.5 h and 80th percentile 3.5 h.
- QA open Mon 08:00–20:00. If review crosses 20:00, remaining work pauses to Tue 08:00.

**Final release**

- Electronic Release takes 0.25–0.75 h and is continuous.
- Earliest plausible smooth terminal: if chemistry completes very early, micro is read Sunday, and QA queue is low, **Mon 21 Sep ~10:00**.
- Central smooth terminal: **Mon 16:00–22:00**, or **Tue 08:00–12:00** if QA cannot finish before 20:00.

### Scenario B: Chemistry rerun

- Borderline result probability is 0.10–0.20 baseline, higher if the temperature excursion is confirmed. I do not treat rerun probability as independent of excursion.

**Parallel join**

- Join becomes chemistry-dominated: **Mon 16:00–18:00** if rerun finishes before close, otherwise **Tue 08:00–14:00**.
- Again, the join is a maximum: micro readiness does not add time once it is earlier than chemistry.

**QA and terminal**

- If join is Mon 17:00–18:00, QA may still complete before 20:00 only if queue is very short and service is short. More often it spills to Tuesday morning.
- If join is Tuesday morning, QA wait plus service usually yields terminal **Tue afternoon/evening**.
- If QA queue is unfavorable or service is long, terminal slips to **Wed 23 Sep**.

### Scenario C: Delayed microbiology readout or confirmation

This scenario covers two different but related mechanisms: simple weekend staffing delay and contamination confirmation.

**C1. No weekend microbiologist authorization**

- Primary incubation is technically ready Sun 12:00–20:00.
- Without authorization, readout waits until Mon 08:00. With 0.5–1.5 h readout and a small queue, usable micro is **Mon 08:30–10:00**.
- If chemistry is not ready until Mon 10:00–16:00, this delay may not change the join much. If chemistry is early, it can shift QA start from Monday 08:00 to Monday late morning.

**C2. Contamination indication requiring confirmation**

- Contamination indication probability is 0.05–0.12 baseline, higher if temperature excursion is confirmed.
- At primary readout, a contamination indication adds 20–30 h continuous incubation plus another 0.5–1.5 h readout.

If primary readout occurs Sunday under on-call authorization:

- Primary usable: Sun 13:00–23:00.
- Add 20–30 h: technical second readiness roughly **Mon 09:00 – Tue 05:00**.
- Second readout during microbiology staffing: usable micro roughly **Mon 09:30 – Tue 10:00**, with some Monday evening cases pushed to Tuesday if readiness occurs after 18:00.

If primary readout occurs Monday because no weekend authorization:

- Primary usable: Mon 08:30–10:00.
- Add 20–30 h: technical second readiness **Tue 04:30–16:00**.
- Second readout during Tuesday staffing: usable micro roughly **Tue 08:30–17:30**.

**Chemistry**

- Chemistry is usually ready Mon 10:00–16:00 unless a rerun also occurs. In this scenario, unless chemistry rerun is also present, chemistry is often earlier than contaminated micro.

**Parallel join**

- For simple no-authorization delay, join is still often chemistry-dominated: **Mon 10:00–16:00**.
- For contamination confirmation, join is usually **Mon evening to Tuesday**, because the second micro readout is later.

**QA and terminal**

- If join is Monday evening, QA may complete Monday before 20:00 only with short queue/service; otherwise Tuesday.
- If join is Tuesday, terminal usually becomes **Tue evening** or **Wed morning/afternoon**.
- Calendar pauses matter: incubation continues continuously, but readout pauses outside microbiology staffing; QA pauses outside QA working hours.

### Scenario D: QA documentation query or documentation-only investigation tail

This scenario assumes the test branches have produced usable results, but QA review generates rework.

**QA initial review**

- QA wait after join is still governed by the future queue. For a Monday/Tuesday join, wait is commonly **0.5–5 h**, tail longer.
- QA service is commonly **1–4 h**.

**Documentation query**

- Probability 0.15–0.30. Documentation completeness 0.82 suggests the middle/lower part of that range is plausible, but not negligible.
- Query adds 4–12 QA working hours before QA can resume.
- Example: query raised Mon 14:00. Four working hours could be resolved by Mon 18:00; twelve working hours could push to Tue 10:00–11:00, because Sunday is not QA working time and Saturday has only 4 h.
- After information is supplied, B-417 returns to QA. I approximate a small re-queue/re-review allowance within the QA service/rework envelope.

**Deviation investigation**

- If both branches are normal, investigation probability is 0.04–0.10.
- If rerun, contamination indication, or confirmed excursion exists, investigation probability rises to 0.30–0.55.
- Documentation-only investigation: about 60–80% of opened investigations, adding 8–20 QA working hours. In QA calendar terms, this is roughly **0.7–1.7 working days**, with Saturday contributing only 4 h.
- If opened Monday, documentation-only investigation often finishes Tue/Wed. If opened Tuesday, it often finishes Wed/Thu.

**Final release**

- After QA acceptance, electronic release adds 0.25–0.75 h and can occur continuously.
- Central documentation-only tail therefore lands around **Wed 23 Sep**, with a tail into **Thu 24 Sep**.

### Scenario E: Correlated excursion with laboratory investigation severe tail

**Dependence structure**

- The temperature-excursion flag is unknown. I treat confirmation as a common-cause event that increases both chemistry rerun probability and microbiology contamination probability, and also increases QA investigation probability.
- This avoids assuming independent anomalies. A confirmed excursion makes simultaneous branch problems more plausible than a simple product of baseline probabilities.

**Branch effects**

- Chemistry may require rerun, pushing usable chemistry from Monday to Tuesday.
- Microbiology may require contamination confirmation, pushing usable micro from Sunday/Monday to Tuesday.
- The join may therefore be **Tue 22 Sep**, rather than Mon 21 Sep.

**Laboratory investigation**

- If an investigation is opened and it is not documentation-only, the prompt gives 3–7 calendar days.
- If opened Mon 21 Sep, 3 calendar days gives Thu 24 Sep; 7 calendar days gives Mon 28 Sep.
- Add QA re-review and electronic release: roughly 0.5–1 additional working day, depending on QA queue and working calendar.

**Terminal**

- Earliest severe tail: **Thu 24 Sep**.
- Central severe tail: **Fri 25 Sep – Mon 28 Sep**.
- Extreme severe tail: **Tue 29 Sep – Wed 30 Sep**, especially if the investigation takes near 7 days and QA re-review encounters queue delay.
