## 8. Residual uncertainty

1.  **Unobserved Case Risk:** Complexity categories (Low/Med/High) may not capture all risk factors driving service time.
    *   *Reduction:* Add `estimated_claim_value` or `risk_score` from the classification step to the dataset.
2.  **Selection Bias (Abandonment):** Data includes only completed cases; Orion might abandon difficult cases more often.
    *   *Reduction:* Include `case_status` (Completed/Abandoned/Transferred) and `transfer_reason` in the data extract.
3.  **Reviewer Skill Heterogeneity:** "Orion" and "Nova" are groups; individual reviewer skill variance is hidden.
    *   *Reduction:* Add `reviewer_id` to enable within-reviewer or fixed-effects analysis.
4.  **Queue State Dynamics:** `arrival_load` is coarse (Normal/High); real-time queue length is unknown.
    *   *Reduction:* Capture `queue_length_at_arrival` and `staff_on_duty` timestamps for each case.
5.  **Night Shift Context:** "Night" may imply different supervisory support or system latency not captured.
    *   *Reduction:* Conduct a time-and-motion study or add `system_latency_ms` logs during night shifts.
