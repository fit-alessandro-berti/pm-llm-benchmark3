# 1. Assumptions and Interpretation

1. **Case scope and termination.** The comparison concerns finite, completed, single-case visible traces. Register Deviation occurs exactly once, first; Archive Case occurs exactly once, last. Mandatory activities outside the trial loop occur once. The model offers completion after any inspection, but an unbounded nondeterministic loop can also retry forever. Eventual passage and completion are assumptions about case execution, not universal termination guarantees established by these models.
2. **Risk.** A single exclusive choice after Analyze Root Cause selects either Supervisor Review or the high-risk submodel containing both Quality Review and Regulatory Review. The high-risk reviews have no mutual ordering. Risk remains fixed throughout the case. The control-flow choice abstracts the omitted data guard; it cannot verify that the selected route matches the actual risk value.
3. **Supplier.** An exclusive choice between a silent skip and Consult Supplier represents optional participation. I place this choice after Analyze Root Cause; an earlier data decision would have the same visible behavior provided consultation still waits for analysis. The consultation branch executes exactly once if selected, and Release Material waits for the chosen branch to finish. Selection is fixed for the case, rather than reconsidered during retries.
4. **Inspection outcomes.** The trial body is Execute Trial followed by Inspect Trial. Choosing the redo branch represents failure and requires Adjust Parameters followed by another complete body. Choosing loop exit represents passage. Thus the trial projection of an accepted completed trace is `ET IT (AP ET IT)*`. Exit is implicit and silent; no artificial visible “pass” event is introduced. Once the trial component exits, it cannot execute Adjust Parameters. Without outcome data, the model cannot distinguish an actual passing inspection from an inspection interpreted as failed by its control-flow choice.
5. **Visible labels and identity.** Neither implementation duplicates visible activity nodes: each of the fourteen labels occurs at one structural location. Repeated trial events arise by revisiting loop nodes. Duplicate visible nodes are technically possible, but are deliberately excluded from these constructions. Distinct model nodes are never represented by reusing the same object in two parents.
6. **Process-tree approximation.** I use a conservative under-approximation of the intended completed visible language. Both initial activities must finish before any planning, design, approval, or supplier work begins. This adds an early synchronization barrier while retaining approval–trial and supplier–trial independence.
7. **Concurrency and observation.** “May overlap” means independently enabled work has no imposed order and may execute concurrently. Parallel and unordered partial-order children admit the corresponding interleavings; neither simultaneous execution nor both completion orders in every individual case are required. Precedence represents finish-to-start enabling. Completion-only traces cannot establish actual overlap or detect every premature start that subsequently completes in a permissible order.
8. **Unspecified constraints.** There are no additional resource, duration, fairness, cancellation, or cross-case constraints. Adjust Parameters does not invalidate planning, design, completed approvals, or consultation; the narrative does not require those activities to be repeated.

The following abbreviations are used in expressions, dependencies, and witnesses. Python uses the full activity labels.

# 2. Intended Dependency Specification

`APPROVAL` denotes the selected risk submodel; `SUPPLIER` denotes the optional consultation submodel; `TRIAL` denotes the complete repeat-until component. A precedence edge to a submodel enables its entry only after the predecessor completes. An edge from a submodel waits for its completion. Subscripts identify trial iterations, not additional activity labels.

| ID | Source activity or submodel | Target activity or submodel | Relation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | Case entry | RD | Mandatory first event | Registration starts every case. |
| D02 | RD | CM and AR | Precedence and independent enabling | Both initial activities become available after registration. |
| D03 | CM | AR | Concurrency; neither precedes the other | Containment and analysis may overlap and finish in either order. |
| D04 | CM | PV | Precedence | Verification planning requires completed containment. |
| D05 | AR | DC | Precedence | Corrective-action design requires completed analysis. |
| D06 | AR | APPROVAL | Precedence | Risk-route selection and the selected reviews follow analysis. |
| D07 | APPROVAL entry | SR, or the submodel containing QR and RR | Exclusive choice | Exactly one risk route is selected; the two routes cannot be combined. |
| D08 | QR | RR | Concurrency within a mandatory pair | Both high-risk reviews are required, with either completion order. |
| D09 | AR | SUPPLIER | Precedence | Any selected consultation must wait for analysis. |
| D10 | SUPPLIER entry | Silent skip or CS | Exclusive optionality | Consultation occurs zero or one time. |
| D11 | PV and DC | TRIAL entry / first ET | AND synchronization | Both work products are required before trial execution. |
| D12 | APPROVAL | PV, DC, and TRIAL | No additional precedence | Approval work can overlap preparation and the trial component. |
| D13 | Selected CS | CM, PV, DC, APPROVAL, and TRIAL | No additional precedence beyond D09 | Consultation may overlap these activities whenever their own prerequisites permit. |
| D14 | ET_i | IT_i | Precedence within every iteration | Each execution is inspected before any retry decision. |
| D15 | Failed IT_i | AP_i, then ET_(i+1) and IT_(i+1) | Conditional redo sequence | Failure requires adjustment and another complete trial; retries may repeat. |
| D16 | Passing IT_k | TRIAL completion | Exclusive loop exit | Passage ends the component; it allows no subsequent AP or ET in that case. |
| D17 | Completed TRIAL, APPROVAL, and SUPPLIER | RM | AND synchronization | Release requires passage, all selected reviews, and consultation completion if selected. A skipped supplier branch is satisfied silently. |
| D18 | RM | AC and case completion | Precedence; AC is last | Archiving follows release and closes the case. |

Only the listed precedences and their transitive consequences apply. In particular, analysis need not precede planning, and containment need not precede design, reviews, or consultation.

More formally, an intended completed trace has exactly one occurrence each of RD, CM, AR, PV, DC, RM, and AC; either one SR and no QR/RR, or one QR and one RR and no SR; and zero or one CS. For some integer `k >= 1`, its projection onto `{ET, IT, AP}` is `ET IT (AP ET IT)^(k-1)`. It also satisfies the boundary and precedence conditions above. This defines the intended visible language, denoted `L_int`, without reference to either implementation.

# 3. PM4Py Process-Tree Model

`SEQ` means sequence, `AND` means parallel composition, `XOR` means exclusive choice, and `tau` is silent. The binary `LOOP(B, R)` has language `B (R B)*`, with implicit exit after a body execution.

```text
BODY     = SEQ(ET, IT)
TRIAL    = LOOP(BODY, AP)
APPROVAL = XOR(SR, AND(QR, RR))
SUPPLIER = XOR(tau, CS)
WORK     = SEQ(AND(PV, DC), TRIAL)

tree = SEQ(
    RD,
    AND(CM, AR),
    AND(WORK, APPROVAL, SUPPLIER),
    RM,
    AC
)
```

The first parallel block introduces the chosen barrier. In the second parallel block, planning and design jointly enable the trial, while approval and supplier submodels progress independently. Completing that whole block enables release.

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree, Operator


def activity(label):
    return ProcessTree(label=label)


def branch(operator, *children):
    parent = ProcessTree(operator=operator)
    for child in children:
        if child.parent is not None:
            raise ValueError("A process-tree node cannot have two parents")
        parent.children.append(child)
        child.parent = parent
    return parent


trial = branch(
    Operator.LOOP,  # Binary loop: body (redo body)*.
    branch(
        Operator.SEQUENCE,
        activity("Execute Trial"),
        activity("Inspect Trial"),
    ),
    activity("Adjust Parameters"),
)

approval = branch(
    Operator.XOR,
    activity("Supervisor Review"),
    branch(
        Operator.PARALLEL,
        activity("Quality Review"),
        activity("Regulatory Review"),
    ),
)

supplier = branch(
    Operator.XOR,
    ProcessTree(),  # Silent omission; label and operator are both None.
    activity("Consult Supplier"),
)

work = branch(
    Operator.SEQUENCE,
    branch(
        Operator.PARALLEL,
        activity("Plan Verification"),
        activity("Design Corrective Action"),
    ),
    trial,
)

tree = branch(
    Operator.SEQUENCE,
    activity("Register Deviation"),
    branch(
        Operator.PARALLEL,
        activity("Contain Material"),
        activity("Analyze Root Cause"),
    ),
    branch(Operator.PARALLEL, work, approval, supplier),
    activity("Release Material"),
    activity("Archive Case"),
)
```

Every child is explicitly appended to its parent's children and receives that parent reference. Every constructed object is attached once; the root retains `parent=None`.

**Exact extent of the approximation.** Relative to `L_int`, the tree additionally requires `AR < PV`, `CM < DC`, `CM < each selected review`, and `CM < CS` when consultation is present. Therefore planning cannot proceed while analysis is unfinished, and design, reviews, and consultation cannot proceed while containment is unfinished. The approval and supplier choices also wait for this barrier. These are all the added visible precedences: the two initial activities remain unordered, high-risk reviews remain unordered, and approval and consultation can interleave with planning, design, and every trial iteration after the barrier. Trial entry, mandatory adjustment on redo, optionality, exclusivity, and release synchronization otherwise have the intended abstract semantics. Hidden data and physical timing have the shared limitations stated in Section 1.

Consequently, `L_tree` is exactly `L_int` restricted by those extra precedences, so `L_tree` is a strict subset of `L_int`. A5 below demonstrates strictness. This policy preserves release prerequisites while potentially increasing lead time.

The obstacle to a compact exact tree with unique visible nodes is structural. Fix the low-risk route, omit consultation, and consider one successful trial. Project the intended precedence relation onto AR, SR, PV, and ET. It contains `AR < SR`, `AR < ET`, and `PV < ET`, while AR/PV, SR/PV, and SR/ET are incomparable. This is an induced N-shaped partial order, which cannot be composed solely by nesting sequence and parallel blocks over those unique occurrences. An exact visible-language encoding using duplicated alternatives or a much larger regular-expression construction is a different possibility; this answer does not claim impossibility under unrestricted duplication. Such a construction would obscure the independent obligations and make label-based analysis harder to interpret.

# 4. PM4Py POWL Model

`PO(nodes, edges)` denotes a partial order with arbitrary interleaving of incomparable children. `TC` means transitive closure. Binary `LOOP` has the same body–redo semantics as above.

```text
APPROVAL = XOR(SR, PO({QR, RR}, {}))
SUPPLIER = XOR(tau, CS)
BODY     = PO({ET, IT}, {ET < IT})
TRIAL    = LOOP(BODY, AP)

powl = PO(
    {RD, CM, AR, PV, DC, APPROVAL, SUPPLIER, TRIAL, RM, AC},
    TC({
        RD < CM, RD < AR,
        CM < PV,
        AR < DC, AR < APPROVAL, AR < SUPPLIER,
        PV < TRIAL, DC < TRIAL,
        TRIAL < RM, APPROVAL < RM, SUPPLIER < RM,
        RM < AC
    })
)
```

The code explicitly installs both the listed dependencies and their transitive consequences using `.order.add_edge(source_node, target_node)`. Computing closure avoids relying on the converter to complete an initially nontransitive relation. Closure adds no precedence beyond that already implied by the listed dependencies.

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)


def add_closed_edges(po, nodes, dependencies):
    # Work by object identity, not visible labels.
    index = {id(node): i for i, node in enumerate(nodes)}
    count = len(nodes)
    reachable = [[False] * count for _ in nodes]

    for source_node, target_node in dependencies:
        i = index[id(source_node)]
        j = index[id(target_node)]
        reachable[i][j] = True

    for k in range(count):
        for i in range(count):
            for j in range(count):
                reachable[i][j] = reachable[i][j] or (
                    reachable[i][k] and reachable[k][j]
                )

    if any(reachable[i][i] for i in range(count)):
        raise ValueError("Cyclic precedence relation")

    for i, source_node in enumerate(nodes):
        for j, target_node in enumerate(nodes):
            if reachable[i][j]:
                po.order.add_edge(source_node, target_node)


rd = Transition(label="Register Deviation")
cm = Transition(label="Contain Material")
ar = Transition(label="Analyze Root Cause")
pv = Transition(label="Plan Verification")
dc = Transition(label="Design Corrective Action")
sr = Transition(label="Supervisor Review")
qr = Transition(label="Quality Review")
rr = Transition(label="Regulatory Review")
cs = Transition(label="Consult Supplier")
et = Transition(label="Execute Trial")
it = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
ap = Transition(label="Adjust Parameters")
rm = Transition(label="Release Material")
ac = Transition(label="Archive Case")

# Both high-risk reviews occur, with no edge between them.
high = StrictPartialOrder([qr, rr])
approval = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [sr, high])
supplier = OperatorPOWL(
    Operator.XOR, [SilentTransition(), cs]
)

body_nodes = [et, it]
body = StrictPartialOrder(body_nodes)
add_closed_edges(body, body_nodes, [(et, it)])

# Exit after body is implicit; every redo executes Adjust Parameters.
trial = OperatorPOWL(Operator.LOOP, [body, ap])

root_nodes = [rd, cm, ar, pv, dc, approval, supplier, trial, rm, ac]
powl = StrictPartialOrder(root_nodes)
add_closed_edges(
    powl,
    root_nodes,
    [
        (rd, cm),
        (rd, ar),
        (cm, pv),
        (ar, dc),
        (ar, approval),
        (ar, supplier),
        (pv, trial),
        (dc, trial),
        (trial, rm),
        (approval, rm),
        (supplier, rm),
        (rm, ac),
    ],
)
```

**Why the unordered pairs are intentional.** After transitive closure, the following list exhausts the incomparable pairs among the root's children:

- CM with each of AR, DC, APPROVAL, and SUPPLIER: analysis and its downstream obligations can proceed while containment is unfinished.
- AR with PV: planning can proceed once containment finishes, even while analysis is unfinished.
- Every pair among PV, DC, APPROVAL, and SUPPLIER: each has its own prerequisite, with no additional ordering among these four obligations.
- TRIAL with APPROVAL, and TRIAL with SUPPLIER: these components synchronize at release, allowing arbitrary interleaving before then.

Inside the high-risk submodel, QR and RR are the only pair and intentionally have no edge. The trial body orders ET before IT. XOR alternatives are mutually exclusive, not concurrent, and loop semantics order successive body and redo executions. Every other pair of root children is ordered through reachability; merely omitting a direct edge would not make such a pair concurrent.

**Fidelity argument.** Any completed POWL execution selects exactly one risk route, selects or skips consultation once, executes a trial projection of the specified form, and respects every dependency in Section 2. Conversely, any trace in `L_int` determines those branch choices and a finite number of trial iterations. Its projections fit the child submodels, and its ordering respects the root's dependencies, so the root can interleave those projections to realize the trace. Thus `L_powl = L_int` under the stated completion-trace and hidden-data abstraction. Ordering the whole TRIAL after PV and DC is exact because neither work product is repeated or invalidated during a retry.

# 5. Behavioral Witnesses

These are complete visible traces. “Allowed” means an accepting execution exists after allowing silent model moves. An inspection followed locally by redo is interpreted as failed; the inspection at loop exit is interpreted as passing. Other branches' events may intervene in either case.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | `RD CM AR PV DC SR ET IT RM AC` | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed | Low risk, supplier omitted, one successful trial; all prerequisites are complete before release. |
| A2 | `RD AR CM DC PV ET QR IT RR RM AC` | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed | High risk, QR before RR. Trial starts before either review completes, and RR finishes after the passing inspection. |
| A3 | `RD CM AR RR PV DC ET QR IT CS RM AC` | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed | High risk with the reverse review order and selected consultation. Supplier completion follows the passing inspection and still precedes release. |
| A4 | `RD AR CM DC PV ET IT AP SR ET CS IT RM AC` | Allowed | Allowed | Allowed | Low risk with consultation and two trial iterations. The first inspection fails, adjustment occurs, and the second inspection passes. Approval and consultation interleave with the trial component. |

# 7. Preferred Representation and Validation Plan

**POWL is the canonical representation.** Its root edges expose the separate obligations and their release join directly, while its local choices and loop contain exactly the behavior needing those operators. It preserves early downstream progress without duplicating visible labels. The process tree is a documented conservative alternative for consumers requiring that representation; its rejection of A5 is an expected limitation.

The two code blocks were constructed and converted in the local PM4Py 2.7.23.8 environment. Both converted nets returned a positive soundness result, and accepting-net alignment checks agreed with all sixteen model/verdict combinations in the witness table. Additional loop checks verified that retries require AP and that a trace ending its trial work immediately after AP cannot complete. These checks support the implementation; the language claims also rely on the compositional arguments above.

A concrete continuing validation plan is:

1. **Check structure and convert.** Execute each construction independently and record the PM4Py version. Check process-tree parent references, unique node identity, and one structural occurrence of each visible label. Check each partial order for irreflexivity and transitivity. Convert each supported model using `net, im, fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(model)`, retaining the initial and final markings as an accepting net. A conversion exception is an implementation failure to investigate, not a fidelity result.
2. **Check soundness.** Run `pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)` on both nets and inspect its result and diagnostics. Check option to complete, proper completion, absence of dead transitions, and boundedness where available. Proper completion must leave precisely the final marking, with no outstanding approval or supplier tokens. Soundness supplies an option to finish; it does not force eventual passage on every execution.
3. **Perform bounded and sampled playout.** Enumerate accepting executions with one, two, and three trial iterations, covering both risk routes, both supplier choices, and both high-risk review orders. Use a marking explorer with silent closure and a per-path trial counter, or equivalent bounded playout. Also obtain sampled accepting runs through `pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm)` with reproducible random seeds and recorded settings. Treat runs cut off by bounds as inconclusive prefixes, never as completed cases. Deduplicate visible traces after silent projection; do not interpret raw sampling frequencies as business probabilities.
4. **Use an independent specification monitor.** For every generated trace, check the Section 2 activity counts, route exclusivity, supplier bound, trial projection, boundary conditions, and predecessor obligations. Check that every generated tree trace satisfies `L_int`. Compare bounded trace sets against the POWL set; every tree trace should occur in it, while intended traces violating the tree's extra initial barrier should be absent only from the tree. The monitor must be derived from the dependency specification rather than copied from either model's structure.
5. **Run explicit positive and negative tests.** Retain all eight witnesses as regression cases with their separate expected verdicts. Add systematic mutations that delete either high-risk review, combine both routes, repeat CS, move CS before AR or after RM, move ET before either work product, omit AP between iterations, or finish immediately after AP. Test membership against the final marking. An accepted alignment may contain silent model moves, but must contain no skipped visible log event or unmatched visible model event; a rounded fitness score alone is insufficient.
6. **Audit silent behavior and labels.** Compute epsilon closures when testing acceptance. Confirm that supplier omission works through its silent branch, while silent paths cannot bypass the first trial body, a required adjustment on redo, or either selected high-risk review. Inspect the reachable silent-transition subgraph for silent divergence. In particular, keep the loop binary and check the no-adjustment retry mutation: adding a silent child to a loop must not accidentally create an empty redo alternative. Compare visible-label multiplicities before and after conversion and trace any generated copies back to their original node identities.
7. **State the limits of the evidence.** Enumeration grows rapidly with concurrency, and any iteration bound excludes longer executions. Sampling cannot prove completeness or expose every unintended trace. Soundness does not establish equality with the narrative, data-guard correctness, physical overlap, resource feasibility, or termination on every possible run. For stronger unbounded visible-language evidence, construct a separate finite-state specification monitor and check language inclusion/equivalence against the accepting nets' reachable-state automata, provided boundedness and feasible state-space size are established. Even that comparison would retain the stated data and timing abstraction.

# 8. Final Self-Critique

1. **Hidden outcomes and case data remain a semantic gap.** The POWL equality claim is with the abstract visible language. A case taking the wrong risk route, skipping a supplier known to be required, or retrying after a real passing result can appear control-flow compliant when its data is omitted.
2. **The process-tree compromise can materially affect operations.** The initial barrier is simple and safe relative to the stated prerequisites, but it suppresses valid early planning, design, reviews, and consultation. Its lead-time or concurrency statistics should not be treated as estimates of the unrestricted intended process.
3. **Execution evidence is narrower than full operational correctness.** Construction, soundness, and the tested traces do not independently prove every language claim or inevitable completion. Completion-only observations also cannot verify actual start times or resource-feasible overlap; those require a richer event and execution model.
