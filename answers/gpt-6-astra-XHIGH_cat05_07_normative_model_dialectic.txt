## 1. Stipulated Facts, Disputed Propositions, and Burden of Proof

The record establishes material operational noncompliance and material defects in the assessment framework. Neither finding cancels the other. M0 deviation is evidence about a trace and a particular model; business noncompliance additionally requires an applicable obligation, reliable evidence of its violation, and consideration of valid exceptions.

**What M0 actually requires.** Register_Supplier precedes completion of both checks. Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review are unordered relative to each other: either observed order is permitted, and the activities may overlap. Concurrency does not require simultaneous timestamps. Both checks must finish before either decision branch begins. The approved branch requires manual approval before activation; the alternative is rejection, not approval followed by rejection. Notification follows the selected decision, and closure follows notification. Every required activity occurs once on its selected branch. M0 contains neither information-request repetition nor auto-approval, and it requires quality review even for rejected cases. It does not constrain risk, amounts, authority, deadlines, check outcomes, human identity, or conflicts of interest.

**Established propositions.**

- N1 is a controlled, approved standard effective throughout the cases. It requires preactivation sanctions and quality checks, preactivation manual approval, and different humans as requester and manual approver. The last obligation exceeds M0's control-flow semantics.
- PD-3 exists and describes a bounded pilot, but its power to override CS-9 is disputed. Eligibility alone does not establish a valid exception. Automated master-data validation is also required; it is absent from the supplied pilot traces and not separately evidenced for SUP-702.
- FD-7 expressly permits the early activation at issue during its stated emergency window, while retaining sanctions, spend, monitoring, and retrospective-quality conditions. Its expiry is explicit. Continued flooding is evidence of need, not evidence of an extension.
- SUP-703 was ineligible for PD-3; SUP-708 activated before sanctions without an applicable exception; SUP-712's two actor aliases resolve to one human. These are substantive adverse facts, not merely unexpected labels.
- N4 establishes incomplete notification collection and a possible external evidence source. SUP-711's duplicate registration has an identified migration cause and common source transaction ID. SUP-713 has affirmative evidence of equivalent review content.
- Information requests secured evidence before approval in SUP-707; the 47 aggregate cases did not activate before their requested evidence arrived. Neither fact establishes a permanent auto-approval authorization.

**Disputed or unproved propositions.** These include the steering committee's delegated exception authority; whether the observer's assent bound Compliance; complete fulfillment of PD-3's retained controls; M0's boundary around rejected leads and reactivations; reliable attribution and preclosure timing of SUP-706's CRM message; and whether SUP-715's required disclosure actually occurred. The record proves no disclosure was recorded, not necessarily that no disclosure was made. FD-7's stated scope and issuance are accepted for this review because no competing authority claim is supplied; this is a different evidential position from the expressly contested PD-3. SUP-704's initial EUR 18,000 spend does not alone establish total spend before retrospective approval.

**Burden and standard.** For a substantive noncompliance finding, the reviewer must establish, on the balance of reliable evidence, an applicable effective rule and a breach attributable to this case. A complete authoritative record can support an omission finding; an event missing from a demonstrably incomplete log ordinarily cannot. Severity concerns exposure and control importance, not whether a violation has been proved. Allegations of dishonesty or intentional circumvention require evidence beyond a deviation.

The party relying on an exception must show competent authority, applicable scope and time, and the required compensating controls. A branch label, favorable outcome, or common practice does not discharge that burden. Failure to establish an exception means it cannot be certified or used as prospective permission; where a concrete authority conflict remains unresolved, it does not automatically prove intentional misconduct. The ordinary standard remains the operational default pending resolution. Reversal requires contemporaneously applicable authority or better evidence of historical facts, not merely a later policy change.

Findings distinguish raw-log conformance, substantiated business behavior, and unresolved audit evidence. COMPLIANT means no breach is established on the applicable evidence assessed here; it is not a claim that every unspecified control has been independently audited. JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION identifies an established authorization for the exceptional ordering, with any outstanding retained-control assurance stated separately. The one MIXED classification below identifies a specific unresolved authority/control combination, not a compromise score.

## 2. Prosecution Brief

An organization cannot replace approved activation controls through operator selection, committee optimism, or a flow becoming familiar. PD-3's minutes and email are real evidence, but the record supplies no settled delegation allowing that committee to displace CS-9. The observer's contemporaneous “monitored exception” supports the defense; her later qualification and the absence of a controlled amendment make its legal-operational effect contestable. ## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

**Control flow and scope.** Keep a versioned standard route with registration, concurrent sanctions and quality checks, a decision, notification, and closure. Approval requires manual procurement approval before activation; rejection is exclusive of activation. Add zero or more Request_More_Info occurrences that may interleave with predecision checks, with all required responses resolved before the decision. The repeated requests in SUP-707 must not force sanctions and quality into a fixed sequence. Material new information must trigger reassessment of any invalidated check under a separate rule.

Represent pilot and emergency routes as separately selectable structural variants. The pilot variant retains sanctions and automated master-data validation before activation, replaces manual approval with auto-approval only when authorized, and allows quality before or after activation within its deadline. The emergency variant retains sanctions before activation while allowing quality and manual approval on either side of provisional activation. All unresolved completion duties must finish before ordinary successful case closure in the proposed design. Notification can occur after activation but before deferred quality or approval; forcing it after all retrospective work would unnecessarily reject prompt communications such as SUP-702's likely sequence. Exact CRM timestamps, rather than the visual position of an inserted event, determine its reconstructed order.

The proposed scope boundary is explicit: intake leads enter the onboarding family at recorded intake registration. A failed sanctions pre-screen can lead directly to rejection and notification without quality. Accepted leads continue on a full route. Distinguish these lifecycle states and assign one case/episode identifier; do not exclude failed leads after seeing deviations. This is a prospective scope resolution, not proof that either competing interpretation already governed SUP-709.

Reactivation is a new linked lifecycle episode, with a distinct opening event, a current sanctions check, approval, and either fresh quality review or an explicitly authorized validation of reusable certification. Until evidence reuse is approved, an old certificate cannot be silently relabeled as a new review. The policy must state acceptable validity, unchanged supplier/legal-entity scope, and changes that trigger fresh quality assessment. SUP-710 remains historically unresolved until the then-applicable boundary is established.

Withdrawal is a separate nonactivation outcome with an authorized requester action, notification, closure reason, and retained history. It must not erase earlier work or violations. Early withdrawal is straightforward to encode; withdrawal during concurrent work needs cancellation semantics or separate prefix variants. Postactivation exit is deactivation/termination with surviving obligations, not a retroactive withdrawal that removes sanctions, approval, or retrospective-review duties.

**Eligibility, money, and time.** A trusted policy evaluator selects the allowed variant from authoritative case attributes and the effective activation instant. Assess LOW risk, DE jurisdiction, and approved-group membership using an immutable decision-time snapshot, not subsequently edited master data. Automated validation must have an attributable result and completion time; absence of its label is not proof it never ran, but auto-approval is not proof that it did. Emergency eligibility requires evidence of critical relief and shutdown-prevention need.

Retain sanctions completion/clearance before effective activation across all activating routes. Check results and unresolved information requests require data rules beyond mere event occurrence. Apply the EUR 20,000 cap to cumulative relevant spend until retrospective approval, not separately to each purchase. The owner must define precisely which spend states and exchange-rate treatment count; those definitions cannot be invented as historical requirements. Monitor daily coverage and calculate quality deadlines using a governed business calendar and timezone. N3 supplies no fixed retrospective-manual-approval deadline; adding one is a prospective policy decision. An authorized activation's outstanding duties survive overlay expiry; expiry prevents new admissions unless an express, authorized grandfathering rule exists.

**Humans and conflicts.** Resolve accounts, service identities, and aliases to effective-dated human identities. Enforce requester-human != manual-approver-human, including where one person acts through delegated or shared accounts. Retain the actual acting human and valid delegation; a delegation document does not create independent judgment if the requester still performs the approval. Assess the pilot's absence of a manual approver only under a valid manual-approval waiver, rather than treating a bot as a second human. Maintain a disclosure register for relevant relationships and evidence of required acknowledgment/disposition. The spouses in SUP-715 are not automatically disqualified under the supplied policy; a recusal or independent-review rule would require its own authority.

**Overlay governance.** Separate the structural model from authority to use a route. Each pilot/emergency version needs an issuing authority, evidence of delegation, scope predicate, effective interval, explicit rules overridden, retained duties, expiration behavior, and monitoring owner. Store the disputed status of PD-3 rather than manufacturing an approval. Treat FD-7's stated authority as operative for the supplied historical review while preserving its provenance. Exceptions never weaken unmentioned controls by implication. If two overlays could apply, require an explicit selected overlay and authorized precedence/combination rules; operators cannot combine whichever fragments are easiest.

Expired permissions must automatically stop new route admissions. Extensions and permanent automation require competent approval, versioned rules, and an effective date before use. Emergency issuance needs a practical authorized escalation path so that urgent need does not depend on informal operator improvisation. Historical review preserves both the rule effective at the action and the evidence known at each later review; later amendments normally govern future behavior.

**Division of implementation responsibility.**

- POWL represents partial order, concurrency, exclusive routes, repetition, and completion structure. It supplies a structural language, not eligibility or exception authority.
- DECLARE-style constraints express precedence, response, mutual exclusion, and outstanding obligations: sanctions before activation; manual approval before activation unless validly replaced/deferred; notification before closure; no activation and rejection in one episode; deferred quality eventually completed. Metric deadlines need timed/data-aware monitoring. Log-skeleton-style occurrence, equivalence, and before/after constraints provide secondary checks against missing or duplicated events, with profile-specific rules rather than one global skeleton.
- pandas or comparable data checks join event evidence to effective policy versions, test risk/country/group predicates, validate canonical identities, compute calendar deadlines and cumulative spend, detect daily-monitoring gaps, and report missing values as unknown rather than false or compliant. These are audit calculations; transaction and activation services must enforce critical gates in real time.
- Organizational controls establish authority, resolve scope, manage policy amendments and conflicts, and hold named owners accountable. Software cannot infer delegated power from observed frequency. Separate model owners, data owners, policy owners, and exception approvers, with a controlled route for adjudicating disagreements.

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

The following code constructs a structural envelope and narrower candidate models. It requires PM4Py but reads no files or remote resources. The construction was executed with local PM4Py 2.7.23.8. All candidate models converted to Petri nets, and 13 trace-acceptance checks confirmed, among other properties, either standard check order, repeated requests, early notification with deferred quality, and rejection of late sanctions or missing required notification in the appropriate variants.

The full envelope is not the effective normative model for every case. Its auto-approval and emergency branches are usable only after an independent evaluator establishes an applicable authorized overlay. Neither a convenient alignment to the envelope nor an operator-supplied route name is authorization. For historical audit, replay against the justified route or report the unresolved route binding; do not choose the cheapest alignment as the governing policy.

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    Transition, SilentTransition, StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def event(label):
    # Each call creates a distinct node, even for repeated activity labels.
    return Transition(label=label)


def partial_order(nodes, edges=()):
    """Build an acyclic, transitively closed strict partial order.

    Edges are pairs of zero-based node indexes.
    """
    nodes = list(nodes)
    n = len(nodes)
    reach = [[False] * n for _ in range(n)]
    for i, j in edges:
        if not (0 <= i < n and 0 <= j < n):
            raise ValueError("Edge index out of bounds")
        reach[i][j] = True
    for k in range(n):
        for i in range(n):
            for j in range(n):
                reach[i][j] = reach[i][j] or (reach[i][k] and reach[k][j])
    if any(reach[i][i] for i in range(n)):
        raise ValueError("Strict partial orders cannot contain cycles")
    result = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)
    for i in range(n):
        for j in range(n):
            if reach[i][j]:
                result.order.add_edge(nodes[i], nodes[j])
    return result


def sequence(*nodes):
    return partial_order(nodes, [(i, i + 1) for i in range(len(nodes) - 1)])


def parallel(*nodes):
    return partial_order(nodes)


def exclusive_choice(*nodes):
    return OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=list(nodes))


def requests():
    # POWL LOOP(do, redo) means do (redo do)*.
    # With silent do, the visible language is Request_More_Info*.
    return OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[SilentTransition(), event("Request_More_Info")],
    )


def standard_route():
    preparation = parallel(
        event("Sanctions_Check"), event("Quality_Review"), requests()
    )
    decision = exclusive_choice(
        sequence(event("Manual_Procurement_Approval"), event("Activate_Supplier")),
        event("Reject_Supplier"),
    )
    return sequence(preparation, decision, event("Notify_Requester"))


def pilot_route():
    # Quality may precede or follow activation, and may follow notification.
    # Sanctions and validation precede Auto_Approve: a conservative design choice.
    nodes = [
        event("Sanctions_Check"),                    # 0
        event("Automated_Master_Data_Validation"),   # 1
        requests(),                                 # 2
        event("Auto_Approve"),                      # 3
        event("Quality_Review"),                    # 4
        event("Activate_Supplier"),                 # 5
        event("Notify_Requester"),                  # 6
    ]
    return partial_order(nodes, [(0, 3), (1, 3), (2, 3), (3, 5), (5, 6)])


def emergency_route():
    # Quality and manual approval can be before or after provisional activation.
    # All nodes must finish before the enclosing branch permits Close_Case.
    nodes = [
        event("Sanctions_Check"),                   # 0
        requests(),                                 # 1
        event("Activate_Supplier"),                 # 2
        event("Quality_Review"),                    # 3
        event("Manual_Procurement_Approval"),       # 4
        event("Notify_Requester"),                  # 5
    ]
    return partial_order(nodes, [(0, 2), (1, 2), (2, 5)])


def prescreen_rejection_route():
    # Gate: an intake lead eligible for the ratified abbreviated procedure.
    return sequence(
        parallel(event("Sanctions_Check"), requests()),
        event("Reject_Supplier"),
        event("Notify_Requester"),
    )


def early_withdrawal_route():
    # Approximation: withdrawal before substantive processing begins.
    return sequence(event("Withdraw_Request"), event("Notify_Requester"))


def build_onboarding(allowed_routes):
    factories = {
        "standard": standard_route,
        "pilot": pilot_route,
        "emergency": emergency_route,
        "prescreen_reject": prescreen_rejection_route,
        "early_withdrawal": early_withdrawal_route,
    }
    selected = tuple(allowed_routes)
    if not selected or len(set(selected)) != len(selected):
        raise ValueError("Choose at least one route, without duplicates")
    if any(name not in factories for name in selected):
        raise ValueError("Unknown route")
    branches = [factories[name]() for name in selected]
    body = branches[0] if len(branches) == 1 else exclusive_choice(*branches)
    return sequence(event("Register_Supplier"), body, event("Close_Case"))


def build_reactivation_candidate():
    # Requires a prospective policy amendment; old certification is not a new review.
    quality_evidence = exclusive_choice(
        event("Quality_Review"), event("Validate_Existing_Quality_Certification")
    )
    preparation = parallel(event("Sanctions_Check"), quality_evidence, requests())
    decision = exclusive_choice(
        sequence(event("Manual_Procurement_Approval"), event("Activate_Supplier")),
        event("Reject_Supplier"),
    )
    return sequence(
        event("Open_Reactivation_Case"), preparation, decision,
        event("Notify_Requester"), event("Close_Case"),
    )


# This envelope displays proposed and historical routes; it grants no permission.
M1_envelope = build_onboarding(
    ("standard", "pilot", "emergency", "prescreen_reject", "early_withdrawal")
)

# Ratification is still needed for the new loop and explicit intake scope.
# No permanent auto-approval or emergency permission is enabled here.
M1_baseline_candidate = build_onboarding(
    ("standard", "prescreen_reject", "early_withdrawal")
)
M1_reactivation_candidate = build_reactivation_candidate()

# A trusted external policy evaluator must supply the allowed route for a case.
# Example: replay a standard case against the standard route, not the full envelope.
M1_standard_for_replay = build_onboarding(("standard",))

if __name__ == "__main__":
    print("Constructed the envelope, baseline, reactivation, and standard models.")
```

**Semantics and limits of the construction.** Each branch contains notification, and the enclosing partial order places closure after the entire selected branch. Thus deferred quality and retrospective approval cannot be bypassed by closing immediately after notification. The pilot partial order allows quality before or after activation and notification; the emergency partial order allows quality and manual approval before or after activation. Neither branch relaxes sanctions-before-activation. Rejection after full checks and abbreviated pre-screen rejection are distinct choices requiring external scope guards.

The LOOP is an exact encoding of zero or more visible Request_More_Info events, not an assertion that a response arrived. Requests can interleave with standard checks, and the preparation block must finish before the decision. Request/response linkage and evidence completeness remain data obligations. Rechecking after material changes, arbitrary late withdrawal, suspension, and cancellation of concurrent tasks are not fully represented. The code implements early withdrawal and a separate prospective reactivation candidate rather than granting unconstrained cancellation or silently accepting stale evidence. Those additional variants require explicit design and ratification.

The pilot code conservatively places sanctions and automated validation before Auto_Approve, whereas PD-3 explicitly requires them before activation. That is a proposed operational strengthening; a historical pilot with valid auto-decision-before-validation but validation-before-activation must be assessed against PD-3's actual rule, not retrospectively condemned by this stronger candidate. Likewise, requiring all retrospective duties before Close_Case, formal response gates, reusable-certification validation, and explicit withdrawal behavior are proposed controls where the materials do not already specify them. New validation events must come from evidence; they must not be fabricated to improve replay fitness.

CS_S remains applicable under both exception overlays; repeating it as if waived and recreated is unnecessary. CS_H remains applicable whenever a manual approval occurs, including retrospective emergency approval. A valid PD_AUTO replacement removes that manual activity's prerequisite for that route; it does not create a general identity-rule waiver for any additional manual action. PD3's authority dispute attaches to every purported override, not just its eligibility row. No perpetual auto-approval version is present.

The two-business-day rules require a separately versioned BusinessCalendar and defined provisional-activation timestamp. The monitoring period and what counts as daily coverage must be made explicit by the authorized owner; a model should not invent a narrower end condition from silence. For admissions, use the effective activation instant unless an authoritative rule specifies another trigger. Starting a case during a pilot is not, without such a rule, permission to activate after its expiry. Duties incurred by an authorized activation keep their own deadlines after the version stops admitting new cases.

Store normalized PolicyAuthority records linking each policy version to issuer, approving body, authority/delegation instrument, signed decision evidence, recorded_at, and approval status history. Store RuleBinding records linking case episode, rule_id, policy_version_id, eligibility_snapshot_id, authority_evidence_id, evaluated_at, evaluator_version, and outcome PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN. An explicit override relation records which base rule is displaced; otherwise base duties remain. Conflicting or unknown authority blocks new exception admission and remains an unresolved finding in historical audit. Approval status changes are append-only, not edits that rewrite the original decision.

**Event and data collection.**

1. **Separate source evidence from canonical events.** Retain immutable raw rows. A canonical event has event_id, source_system, source_transaction_id, case_episode_id, supplier_legal_entity_id, raw_activity, canonical_activity, lifecycle, occurred_at, recorded_at, ingested_at, timezone/offset, source sequence, and evidence reference/hash. Record effective activation separately from a UI click. Capture check completion and result, not merely task start. Preserve corrections through superseding records with both effective and recorded timestamps; do not overwrite the past.
2. **Make notification evidence joinable.** CRM emits a stable notification_id and immutable message metadata, including recipient, channel, send/accepted/delivered states, source timestamp, and case correlation. The owner must define which state fulfills Notify_Requester; sending, receipt, and reading must not be conflated. Use an append-only CaseNotificationLink relation with notification_id, case_episode_id, asserted_at, source evidence, and supersession history. Reconcile CRM to onboarding independently of the mirror. SUP-702's stable identifier is strong evidence; SUP-706's late mutable text link requires corroboration. Check actual notification time against closure rather than inserting an event at a convenient position. Missing links remain UNKNOWN.
3. **Preserve eligibility and authority at decision time.** Store risk assessment/version, country basis, approved-group identity and membership evidence, master-data-validation result/run ID, activation state (provisional or fully approved), selected policy/rule versions, pilot/emergency case authorization, delegated authority, exception reason, and immutable eligibility snapshot. Record what was effective when the decision happened separately from when evidence was entered. A subsequently edited LOW-risk field cannot validate an originally HIGH-risk pilot admission.
4. **Resolve humans and delegation explicitly.** Maintain effective-dated ActorAlias records mapping account/source identifiers to a stable human_id, with resolution provenance and confidence. Approval records identify requester, actual actor, role, represented principal if any, and delegation instrument with scope and validity. Unresolved aliases produce UNKNOWN, not automatic distinctness. Conflict records link disclosure, relationship category, acknowledgment/disposition, applicable rule, and both occurrence and recording times, with access restricted appropriately.
5. **Capture retained duties and lifecycle links.** Link quality evidence to legal entity, review scope, validity, issuer, and changes. Link reactivation episodes to prior cases without importing old events as newly performed work. Record request_id and corresponding evidence/response receipts, daily monitoring attestations and exceptions, transaction amounts/currency/time and applicable spend state, retrospective approvals, withdrawal/deactivation reasons, and all outstanding obligations. Certification reuse must have its own validation event and policy binding.
6. **Normalize reversibly and report uncertainty.** Deduplicate SUP-711 using the proven source transaction/lifecycle identity while preserving both raw row IDs and the normalization decision. Map SUP-713 through an activity-equivalence relation carrying source vocabulary/procedure version, semantic effective interval, approved_at, and audit evidence. A mapping accepted after the event can improve today's interpretation of an unchanged historical activity; it does not backdate authority for a substantively different act. Reconstruct event order from reliable effective timestamps and lifecycle evidence, retaining clock uncertainty and genuine concurrency.

Report three distinct outputs per case: raw M0 deviations; canonical, route-specific structural deviations; and effective-policy results with PASS/FAIL/UNKNOWN plus severity. Maintain a separate evidence-quality status so that a weak CRM link is not confused with proof of nonnotification. Report aggregate denominators by scope, effective policy version, and evidence completeness. This preserves the serious findings while making model corrections and improved data visible without rewriting historical obligations.
