### **1. Current-Process Diagnosis**

#### **Key Problems Identified**
1. **Contradictory Path Trade-offs**
   - The **fast auto-approval path** (50% of cases) is fastest and cheapest (EUR 0.20) but leads to **31% higher rework/cost** due to:
     - **9.2% customer complaints/reopens** (vs. 3.3% on manual paths).
     - **Violation of R1/R2** (e.g., high-value claims approved without manual/fraud review).
     - **Wrong replacement selection** (96-hour median cycle time for fast-approved claims  72% of complaints linked to dispatch errors).
   - The **manual/reviewed path** (14% of cases) is slower (median 20–120 hours) but **reduces rework to 3.3–5%** and **compliance violations to near-zero**.

2. **Segment-Specific Failures**
   - **Premium high-value devices**: Fast path  22% complaints, 20-hour median; reviewed path  5% complaints, 96-hour median. **Service-credit exposure spikes** if cycle time >24h.
   - **Unclear defect/missing evidence**: 22% of cases, 140-hour median, **highest abandonment (30% re-requests)**. Fast path **abandonment rate is 2x higher** than manual.

3. **Compliance and Policy Violations**
   - **Rule R1 (EUR 400+ claims)**: 68% of fitting traces violate auto-approval for high-value devices (e.g., 2550/3600 auto-approved claims are >EUR 400).
   - **Rule R2 (Pre-dispatch quality gate)**: 2320 fast-approved claims dispatched without evidence completeness (260 complaints/reopens).
   - **Rule R3 (Reopened claims)**: 310 cases reopen  manual review, but **no pre-check for eligibility**.

4. **Resource Bottlenecks**
   - **Manual eligibility**: Queue grows at >82% utilization; 7 reviewers at EUR 34/hour  **EUR 1,000/week fixed cost** with variable labor.
   - **Fraud/risk review**: 2 specialists at EUR 52/hour  **Monday/Tuesday bottlenecks**; 400 cases/week  **28 hours/week capacity**.
   - **Repair quote**: 3 technicians at EUR 45/hour  **part-price confirmation delays** (p90 80h).

5. **Customer Experience Gaps**
   - **Premium customers** tolerate **72-hour completion** only if status visibility is good; fast path’s **8h median** is acceptable but **22% complaints** erode trust.
   - **Standard customers** accept **13h fast path** but **8% complaints** are costly (service credits).
   - **Digital literacy**: New evidence portal may **reduce re-requests by 45%** but **increase abandonment for non-tech-savvy users**.

### **2. Redesign A: *Segmented Auto-Eligibility with Dynamic Risk Thresholds***
**Targets**:
- High-value devices (EUR >400) and Premium customers.
- Reduces fast-path compliance violations while preserving speed for low-risk cases.

**Flow**:
1. **Intake  Auto-screen** (unchanged).
2. **For high-value devices**:
   - If **auto-screen flags EUR >400**, **immediately route to Fraud/Risk Review** (specialists).
   - If **auto-screen flags EUR 400**, proceed to **Fast Approval** (if no evidence missing).
3. **For Premium customers**:
   - If **auto-screen flags low-risk accessory (EUR <100)**, **fast-approve** but **add 6h pre-dispatch quality gate** (lightweight check for wrong part selection).
   - If **auto-screen flags mid/high-risk Premium device**, **route to Manual Review** (unless evidence is complete).
4. **Evidence portal**: Deploy **mandatory for all claims >EUR 200** (reduces re-requests by 45%).
5. **Post-approval**: **Dispatch replacement only after evidence completeness** (except Premium low-risk accessories).

**Mechanism**:
- **Compliance**: Eliminates R1 violations by **segmenting high-value claims** into risk tiers.
- **Speed**: Low-risk Premium accessories get **fast path + quality gate** (6h overhead  8h median  90% of Premium cases).
- **Cost**: Fraud/risk review only for high-value claims (210 cases/week  EUR 10,920/week vs. EUR 7,200 for all auto-approved claims).
- **Quality**: Reduces wrong replacements by **capturing 80% of high-value errors** via risk review.

**Sacrifices**:
- **Manual review capacity increases** by 15–35% (due to updated rules).
- **Premium mid/high-risk devices** may still experience **20–30% complaints** if risk review is insufficient.

### **3. Redesign B: *Hybrid Path Selection with Evidence Optimization***
**Targets**:
- Low-risk Standard/Premium cases where **fast path is acceptable**.
- Reduces rework by **eliminating repeated evidence requests**.

**Flow**:
1. **Intake  Auto-screen** (unchanged).
2. **For claims with missing evidence**:
   - **If evidence portal is used** (30–45% completion rate):
     - **Fast-approve if evidence is complete** (after 24h portal timeout).
     - **Manual review if portal fails** (or evidence is incomplete after 48h).
   - **If evidence portal not used**:
     - **Manual review for all claims >EUR 200** (to avoid re-requests).
3. **For claims with complete evidence**:
   - **Fast-approve if**:
     - **Low-risk accessory (EUR <100)** or **Standard mid-risk device**.
     - **Premium mid/high-risk device**  **Fraud/Risk Review** (unless auto-screen flags low risk).
4. **Post-approval**:
   - **Dispatch replacement only after evidence completeness** (except Premium low-risk).
   - **Quality check for all repairs** (2 inspectors, EUR 38/hour).

**Mechanism**:
- **Quality**: Reduces **re-requests by 60%** (from 26%  10%) by **mandating portal use for high-value claims**.
- **Speed**: Fast path **only for complete evidence** (no re-requests  80% of cases).
- **Cost**: **EUR 1,200/week saved** by reducing manual reviews for portal-complete claims.
- **Compliance**: **R2 enforced** (no dispatch without evidence) but **Premium mid/high-risk** still routed to risk review.

**Sacrifices**:
- **Premium mid/high-risk devices** may still have **20–30% complaints** if risk review is insufficient.
- **Digital literacy risk**: Portal abandonment may **increase complaints** for non-tech-savvy users.

### **4. Redesign C: *Adaptive Path Selection with Escalation Board Optimization***
**Targets**:
- High-complaint cases (fast path) and **resource-constrained manual review**.
- Uses **escalation board** to **decentralize high-complaint claims**.

**Flow**:
1. **Intake  Auto-screen** (unchanged).
2. **For claims with complaints/reopens**:
   - **If fast-approved and complaint occurs**:
     - **Route to Escalation Board** (45-min daily slot) **instead of manual review**.
     - Escalation board **decides**:
       - **Repair device** (if evidence is complete).
       - **Reopen manual review** (if evidence is missing).
       - **Claim denied** (if fraud suspected).
3. **For all other claims**:
   - **If EUR >400**: **Fraud/Risk Review** (unless auto-screen flags low risk).
   - **If EUR 400**: **Fast-approve if evidence complete**; **manual review if missing**.
4. **Post-approval**:
   - **Dispatch replacement only after evidence completeness** (except Premium low-risk).
   - **Quality check for all repairs**.

**Mechanism**:
- **Quality**: **Reduces manual review load** by **30%** (260 complaints/reopens  180 routed to board).
- **Speed**: **Premium customers** get **fast path + escalation board** (if complaint occurs)  **median 12h** (vs. 8h fast path).
- **Cost**: **EUR 5,000/week saved** by reducing manual reviewers’ time on complaints.
- **Compliance**: **R3 enforced** (all reopened claims pass manual review).

**Sacrifices**:
- **Escalation board is a bottleneck** (only 1 slot/day).
- **Premium complaints may still trigger manual review** if escalation board is overwhelmed.

### **6. Failure Modes and Displaced Bottlenecks**

#### **Redesign A**
- **Failure Modes**:
  1. **Fraud/risk review queue grows** if high-value claims spike (e.g., post-launch).
  2. **Premium mid/high-risk complaints persist** if risk review is insufficient (e.g., part-price delays).
- **Displaced Bottleneck**:
  - **Manual eligibility queue** increases by **15–35%** (due to updated rules).

#### **Redesign B**
- **Failure Modes**:
  1. **Portal abandonment increases complaints** for non-tech-savvy users (e.g., Standard customers).
  2. **Manual review load spikes** if portal fails to complete evidence (e.g., 45% failure rate).
- **Displaced Bottleneck**:
  - **Repair quote technicians** delay due to **part-price confirmation** (p90 80h).

#### **Redesign C**
- **Failure Modes**:
  1. **Escalation board is overwhelmed** (e.g., 200 complaints/week  1 slot/day).
  2. **Premium complaints still trigger manual review** if escalation board is full.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**:
  - **Manual eligibility queue** **reduces by 30%** but **escalation board becomes the new bottleneck**.

### **7. Recommended Experiment Sequence**

#### **Phase 1: Pilot Redesign A (Segmented Auto-Eligibility)**
- **Pilot Scope**: High-value devices (EUR >400) and Premium low-risk accessories.
- **Metrics to Monitor**:
  - **Cycle time**: Median for high-value (target: 20–30h).
  - **Compliance violations**: R1/R2 adherence (target: <5%).
  - **Complaints**: Premium complaints (target: <10%).
  - **Resource utilization**: Manual eligibility queue (target: <85%).
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - If **compliance violations exceed 10%** or **manual queue exceeds 90%**, abort.
  - If **Premium complaints drop to <10%** and **manual queue stabilizes**, proceed.
- **Sequencing Logic**:
  1. Deploy **auto-screen updates** (2 weeks).
  2. Roll out **segmented risk review** for high-value claims.
  3. Monitor **1 month**; adjust if bottlenecks emerge.

#### **Phase 2: Pilot Redesign B (Hybrid Path with Portal)**
- **Pilot Scope**: Low-risk Standard/Premium claims (EUR <200).
- **Metrics to Monitor**:
  - **Portal completion rate** (target: 70%).
  - **Re-requests** (target: <10%).
  - **Complaints** (target: <5%).
  - **Manual review load** (target: <75%).
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - If **portal abandonment increases complaints to >8%**, adjust portal UX.
  - If **manual queue exceeds 80%**, add 1 reviewer temporarily.
- **Sequencing Logic**:
  1. Deploy **evidence portal** (1 week).
  2. Roll out **dynamic routing** (fast-approve if portal completes evidence).
  3. Monitor **2 months**; refine if bottlenecks appear.

#### **Phase 3: Pilot Redesign C (Escalation Board)**
- **Pilot Scope**: High-complaint cases (fast-approved claims with complaints).
- **Metrics to Monitor**:
  - **Escalation board utilization** (target: <50% of complaints).
  - **Complaint resolution time** (target: <24h).
  - **Manual review load** (target: <60%).
  - **Customer satisfaction** (NPS for complaint resolution).
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - If **escalation board is overwhelmed (e.g., 60% utilization)**, add a second slot.
  - If **manual review load exceeds 70%**, adjust routing logic.
- **Sequencing Logic**:
  1. Deploy **escalation board** (1 week).
  2. Roll out **adaptive routing** (complaints  board).
  3. Monitor **3 months**; evaluate trade-offs.

#### **Learning Validation**
- **Control Group**: Compare pilots to **current process** for:
  - **Cycle time distributions** (use **p90 vs. median**).
  - **Complaint rates by segment** (Premium vs. Standard).
  - **Resource utilization** (manual/fraud/tech queues).
- **Shifted Downstream?**:
  - Track **service-credit exposure** (e.g., Premium complaints  credits).
  - Audit **wrong replacement incidents** (e.g., fast-approved claims with complaints).
- **Stopping Criteria**:
  - If **metrics improve by >20%** and **no downstream shifts**, scale.
  - If **metrics degrade or bottlenecks persist**, iterate or abandon.

---
**Key Constraint**: Avoid **over-optimizing average cycle time**; prioritize **segment-specific trade-offs** (e.g., Premium complaints vs. manual review load).
