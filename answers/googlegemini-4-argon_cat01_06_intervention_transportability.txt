### Causal Mechanisms and Transportability
The core **shared causal mechanism** is the partial-order synchronization structure: `treatment_parallelization` increases `true_overlap` between `Identity Check` and `Document Check`, replacing the sum of their sequential waiting and service times with the maximum of the two branches (`waiting_before_assessment`), thereby reducing `cycle_time`. It also shares a potential negative mechanism: losing sequential information flow can slightly increase `rework` and `appeal`.

However, critical mechanisms are **site-specific**. At Site A, reductions in `waiting_before_assessment` and `cycle_time` were co-produced by `API_latency` improvements, `queue_huddles`, expanded `document_capacity` (+2 FTE), and a marketing shift toward simpler `digital_documents` (`case_complexity`). At Site B, statutory `originals_required` (62%), downstream `legal_capacity` constraints, and `Request Supplement` `rework` loops govern the critical path.

Key **effect modifiers** on the path from `treatment_parallelization` to `cycle_time` include:
- `IT_case_lock`: directly throttles `true_overlap` by preventing simultaneous task ownership.
- `originals_required` / `digital_documents`: legally blocks `Document Check` from starting at registration, rendering early task creation an empty queue-inflation exercise.
- `assessment_capacity` and `legal_capacity`: determine whether faster upstream verification actually lowers `cycle_time` or merely shifts waiting to downstream bottlenecks.
- `role_specialization`: changes how capacity is pooled across parallel branches.

A similar control-flow diagram (`+ ('Identity Check', 'Document Check')`) does **not** guarantee transportability because Petri nets and process trees abstract away data-dependent statutory guards, resource contention, IT locking mechanics, and arrival distributions. When selection nodes ($S_*$) point into both the mediator (`true_overlap`) and downstream outcomes (`waiting_before_assessment`, `cycle_time`), the causal effect cannot be transported by structural analogy alone; it must be re-weighted and structurally adapted to target constraints.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

### Quantitative Derivation from Site A and Site B Data
1. **Baseline Decomposition (Site B)**:
   - Volume = $3,400$ cases / 26 weeks $\approx 130.8$ cases/week ($\lambda \approx 3.27$ cases/hour assuming a 40h workweek).
   - **Workload & Utilization ($\rho$)** over 6 months ($\approx 1,040$ productive hours/FTE):
     - `Identity Check` (4 FTE = 4,160h): $\sim 3,400 \text{ cases} \times 3.4\text{h} = 11,560\text{h}$ total span if 3.4h were pure work, which exceeds 4,160h. Thus, the reported "service h" (3.4h Identity, 8.1h Document) is **task elapsed time** (active handling + intra-task pauses/queries), while actual hands-on work is ~1–3h, or reviewers handle multiple open tasks concurrently (consistent with Site A's 6-task WIP limit).
     - `Document Check` handles $3,400 + 345 \text{ (supplement loops)} = 3,745$ checks across 7 FTEs.
   - **DFG Structural Baseline**: Currently, `Register -> Identity Check` (2,050) and `Register -> Originals Receipt` (1,860) split initial flow, and `Identity Check -> Originals Receipt` happens 1,020 times! This means in $1,020 / 2,108 \approx 48\%$ of originals-required cases, Site B **waits for `Identity Check` to finish before even starting `Originals Receipt`**, and in $1,160$ digital cases, `Identity Check -> Document Check` is strictly sequential.
2. **Maximum Theoretical Savings per Branch**:
   - **Digital cohort (38% = 1,292 cases)**: Removing sequential `Identity Check` (3.4h service + ~3.5h queue wait $\approx 6.9\text{h}$) from the critical path yields $\Delta T = \min(T_{\text{ID}}, T_{\text{Doc}})$. Since $T_{\text{Doc}}$ (8.1h service + wait) > $T_{\text{ID}}$, expected savings on digital cases is **4h to 6.5h** at the median.
   - **Originals cohort (62% = 2,108 cases)**: `Document Check` is gated by `Originals Receipt` (median 18h). If `Identity Check` (3.4h + wait) runs in parallel with `Originals Receipt` (fixing the 1,020 cases where `Identity Check -> Originals Receipt` was sequential), we save ~3.4h–6h on those 1,020 cases, and 0h on cases where `Identity Check` already finished during `Originals Receipt`.
   - **Downstream Bottleneck**: `Assessment` (3 FTEs) and `Legal Review` (2 FTEs, 1,360 cases + 360 supplement loops adding ~20h–35h to the upper tail) cap p90 reductions and can inflate p90 if burstiness increases.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

### Modified Process Specification
We **reject** management's naive proposal to instantiate `Document Check` immediately after `Register` for all cases. Instead, we specify a **State-Gated, Lock-Decoupled Parallel Verification Process**:

1. **Eligibility & Triage Rules (at `Register`)**:
   - Every case completes `Register` and is classified by two binary attributes: `originals_required` $\in \{\text{True (62\%)}, \text{False (38\%)}\}$ and `legal_review_required` $\in \{\text{True (40\%)}, \text{False (60\%)}\}$.
2. **Concurrent Task Creation & Statutory Prerequisites**:
   - **Branch 1 (Identity)**: `Identity Check` is created immediately after `Register` for **100% of cases**.
   - **Branch 2a (Digital-Eligible, 38%)**: If `originals_required == False`, `Document Check` is created immediately after `Register` in parallel with `Identity Check`.
   - **Branch 2b (Originals-Required, 62%)**: If `originals_required == True`, `Originals Receipt` (tracking/intake task) is triggered immediately after `Register` in parallel with `Identity Check` (eliminating the legacy pattern where 1,020 cases waited for `Identity Check` before starting `Originals Receipt`). `Document Check` is **strictly gated** and only instantiated after `Originals Receipt` completes.
   - **Synchronization Gate**: `Assessment` cannot be instantiated until **both** `Identity Check` and `Document Check` reach `Complete` and pass an automated cross-check rule (matching claimant name/ID across both checks).
3. **Queue Discipline and WIP Controls**:
   - **IT Lock Modification**: Replace whole-case locking with **sub-task locking** (`CaseID_IDCheck` and `CaseID_DocCheck`) so an Identity reviewer and a Document reviewer can work simultaneously without blocking each other.
   - **WIP Cap**: Each Identity reviewer (4 FTE) and Document reviewer (7 FTE) is capped at **4 active open tasks** (lower than Site A's 6 due to Site B's higher complexity).
   - **Sibling-Priority Pull Rule**: In the unassigned queue, tasks whose sibling branch is already marked `Complete` (e.g., `Document Check` where `Identity Check` is already done, or vice versa) receive **Priority 1** pull status to minimize join-point waiting before `Assessment`.
   - **Daily 10-Minute Cross-Role Huddle**: Team leads from Identity, Document, Assessment, and Legal review queue balance and throttle intake if pre-`Assessment` waiting exceeds 15 hours.
4. **Resource Responsibilities**:
   - Identity (4 FTE), Document (7 FTE), Assessment (3 FTE), and Legal (2 FTE) remain specialized roles as required by governance, coordinated via the Sibling-Priority Pull queue.
5. **Handling of Legal Review and Supplement Loops**:
   - After `Assessment`, if `legal_review_required == False`, the assessor executes `X('Approve', 'Reject')` $\rightarrow$ `Notify`.
   - If `legal_review_required == True` (40%), the case routes to `Legal Review`.
   - If `Legal Review` issues `Request Supplement`, the loop routes **back to `Document Check` and then `Assessment`** (`Request Supplement` $\rightarrow$ `Document Check` $\rightarrow$ `Assessment` $\rightarrow$ `Legal Review`) without re-running `Identity Check` (unless an explicit identity defect flag is raised). Loop cases receive Priority 1 queue placement so they do not inflate the p90 tail.
6. **Logging Requirements**:
   - System must automatically log four lifecycle timestamps per task (`created_at`, `assigned_at`, `started_at`, `completed_at`) at the sub-task level. Manual retroactive timestamp editing is disabled.

Phase 2 (Weeks 5–10): Add Stratum 2 (Originals-required claims, 50% T2 / 50% C) if Phase 1 passes safety gates. | Isolates pure verification parallelization (Phase 1) before testing parallel `Identity Check` $\parallel$ `Originals Receipt` (Phase 2). | Phased entry avoids shocking the legacy IT system and `Assessment` pool simultaneously. | Check covariate balance across arms on complexity, claim value, and arrival hour. |
| **Eligible strata** | Four mutually exclusive strata ($2 \times 2$):<br>S1: Digital + No Legal (22.8%)<br>S2: Digital + Legal (15.2%)<br>S3: Originals + No Legal (37.2%)<br>S4: Originals + Legal (24.8%). | Enables estimation of conditional average treatment effects ($\text{CATE}_{s}$) across the key effect modifiers in the transportability graph. | High-fraud-risk or VIP statutory exception claims (<2%) excluded a priori. | Stratified process cubes comparing DFG edge durations and waiting times per stratum. |
| **Treatment versions** | **Control (C)**: Legacy workflow + upgraded timestamp logging.<br>**T1 (Digital Parallel)**: Task-level lock + immediate `Identity Check` $\parallel$ `Document Check` + Sibling-Priority queue + WIP cap (4).<br>**T2 (Originals Parallel)**: Task-level lock + immediate `Identity Check` $\parallel$ `Originals Receipt` $\rightarrow$ `Document Check` + Sibling-Priority queue + WIP cap (4). | Separates the effect of task-level parallelization in digital claims (T1) from parallelizing intake delay with identity checking (T2). | Automated join-gate cross-check rule active in both T1 and T2 before `Assessment`. | Conformance checking against T1 and T2 POWL models; compliance rate with Sibling-Priority pull order. |
| **Primary and secondary outcomes** | **Primary**: (1) Median and p90 `waiting_before_assessment`; (2) Median and p90 total `cycle_time`.<br>**Secondary**: True verification interval overlap %; `Request Supplement` loop rate; overall rework %; post-join waiting time for `Assessment` and `Legal Review`. | Tests both the proximal mediator (`waiting_before_assessment`) and distal outcome (`cycle_time`) plus quality/bottleneck-shift risks. | Quality floor: any spike in `Request Supplement` or rework is flagged weekly. | Interval overlap ratio $\frac{\max(0, \min(E_{\text{ID}}, E_{\text{Doc}}) - \max(S_{\text{ID}}, S_{\text{Doc}}))}{\min(D_{\text{ID}}, D_{\text{Doc}})}$ using lifecycle start/complete timestamps. |
| **Interference controls** | Partition verification reviewers into isolated Treatment and Control queues; downstream `Assessment` (3 FTE) and `Legal Review` (2 FTE) process cases via strict **scheduled-release slots** (or FIFO based on theoretical baseline arrival time) during Phase 1 so Treatment cases do not cannibalize Control assessment slots. | Eliminates violation of SUTVA (Stable Unit Treatment Value Assumption) where fast Treatment verification delays Control cases at `Assessment`. | Cap daily Treatment release into `Assessment` at 50% of daily assessor capacity. | Monitor Control group's post-verification waiting time before `Assessment` vs. historical 6-month baseline (20h). |
| **Power / minimum-support logic** | Site B processes ~131 cases/week (~50 digital, ~81 originals). Over 10 weeks: ~500 digital cases (250 T1, 250 C) and over 6 weeks of Phase 2: ~486 originals cases (243 T2, 243 C). At $\alpha = 0.05$ (two-sided), $N=250$/arm provides >85% power to detect a $\ge 3.0\text{h}$ median reduction (assuming IQR $\approx 18\text{h}$) and a $\ge 15\%$ shift in overlap. | Prevents underpowered false negatives or noisy tail estimates in subgroup analyses. | Minimum 200 completed cases per stratum-arm before making expansion decisions. | Bootstrap confidence intervals (1,000 resamples) on median and p90 quantile treatment effects. |
| **Duration** | **10 weeks active enrollment** (4 weeks Phase 1 + 6 weeks Phase 2) + **4 weeks tail follow-up** to ensure >95% of enrolled cases (including 110h+ p90 cases and supplement loops) reach `Notify` without right-censoring bias. | Prevents right-censoring from artificially truncating p90 cycle time and missing late `Request Supplement` rework loops. | Freeze workflow rules during the 10+4 week window; no concurrent marketing or staffing shifts. | Cohort completion ratio tracking; Kaplan-Meier / survival analysis for any censored in-flight cases at week 14. |
| **Stop rules** | **Immediate Halt / Rollback if**: (1) IT task-lock error rate exceeds 2% of cases; (2) `Request Supplement` rate or rework increases by >4 percentage points over Control; (3) p90 cycle time or pre-`Assessment` queue depth increases by >15% for two consecutive weeks. | Protects operational SLA compliance and statutory quality during experimentation. | Automated daily dashboard alerting process owners if thresholds are breached. | Real-time token-replay bottleneck monitoring on active cases in `Assessment` and `Legal Review` queues. |
| **Transportability analysis** | Re-weight stratum-specific treatment effects ($\widehat{\text{CATE}}_{s}$) by Site B's target population shares ($P_B(S=s)$) using the g-formula / inverse odds of sampling weights, and decompose total effect into direct synchronization gain vs. downstream queue congestion offset. | Validates the transportability graph and produces an unconfounded predictor for full-scale Site B rollout. | Compare predicted vs. realized fleet throughput before removing sub-pool ring-fencing. | Structural equation / mediation analysis: `Treatment` $\rightarrow$ `Overlap %` $\rightarrow$ `Wait before Assessment` $\rightarrow$ `Cycle Time`. |

### Separating Mechanism Failure from Implementation Failure
The pilot disentangles **implementation failure** (the intervention failed to produce true parallel execution) from **mechanism failure** (true parallel execution occurred, but failed to improve cycle time or degraded quality at Site B) by tracking a three-stage causal chain in the event log:

1. **Implementation Fidelity Check (`Treatment` $\rightarrow$ `True Overlap`)**: Using automated sub-task lifecycle timestamps (`started_at`, `completed_at`), we verify whether T1 (digital) achieves $>60\%$ overlapping verification intervals without retroactive timestamp back-entry, and whether T2 starts `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt` within 15 minutes of `Register` (eliminating the legacy `Identity Check -> Originals Receipt` delay). If overlap remains low (<20%) or lock errors occur, the failure is **implementation** (IT locking, queue pull non-compliance, or reviewer habit).
2. **Proximal Mechanism Check (`True Overlap` $\rightarrow$ `Pre-Join Waiting`)**: If overlap exceeds 60% in T1, we measure the time from `Register` to the completion of $\max(t_{\text{ID}}, t_{\text{Doc}})$. If this duration does not drop by $\ge 3\text{h}$ relative to Control, the mechanism failed locally (e.g., complex `Document Check` variance completely swallows `Identity Check`).
3. **Systemic Mechanism Check (`Pre-Join Completion` $\rightarrow$ `Cycle Time` & `Rework`)**: If verification completes faster, but time from verification completion to `Assessment` start or `Legal Review` completion increases—or `Request Supplement` loops rise—then the core synchronization mechanism works, but fails systemically due to **downstream bottleneck shifting** or **lost sequential error discovery**.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def build_and_verify_models():
    # =========================================================================
    # 1. Construct Site A's BEFORE and AFTER process trees
    # =========================================================================
    site_a_before_str = """-> (
        'Register',
        'Identity Check',
        'Document Check',
        'Assessment',
        X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ),
        'Notify'
    )"""

    site_a_after_str = """-> (
        'Register',
        + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ),
        'Assessment',
        X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ),
        'Notify'
    )"""

    pt_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(site_a_before_str)
    pt_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(site_a_after_str)

    # =========================================================================
    # 2. Convert both Site A trees to accepting Petri nets & check soundness
    # =========================================================================
    net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(pt_a_before)
    net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(pt_a_after)

    print("=== SITE A MODELS ===")
    print(
        f"Site A Before - Workflow Net: {pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_before)}, "
        f"Sound: {pm4py.check_soundness(net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before)[0]}"
    )
    print(
        f"Site A After  - Workflow Net: {pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_after)}, "
        f"Sound: {pm4py.check_soundness(net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after)[0]}"
    )

    # =========================================================================
    # 3. Construct Site B Candidate Model using POWL classes
    # =========================================================================
    # Atomic transitions (Note: each node instance in POWL must be distinct)
    t_register = Transition(label="Register")
    t_id_check = Transition(label="Identity Check")

    # Document verification branch: either immediate 'Document Check' (38% digital)
    # or 'Originals Receipt' -> 'Document Check' (62% originals required)
    t_doc_digital = Transition(label="Document Check")
    t_originals = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
    t_doc_after_orig = Transition(label="Document Check")

    po_originals_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[t_originals, t_doc_after_orig])
    po_originals_branch.order.add_edge(t_originals, t_doc_after_orig)

    xor_doc_branch = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR, children=[t_doc_digital, po_originals_branch]
    )

    # Assessment + Optional Legal Review + Request Supplement Loop
    # Loop semantics in OperatorPOWL(Operator.LOOP, [body, redo]):
    # Execute 'body', then either exit loop or execute 'redo' and repeat 'body'.
    # Body: 'Assessment' -> XOR(SilentTransition, 'Legal Review')
    # Redo: 'Request Supplement' -> 'Document Check' (repeat doc check before re-assessment)
    t_assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")
    t_legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    tau_skip_legal = SilentTransition()

    xor_optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR, children=[tau_skip_legal, t_legal_review]
    )

    po_assess_and_legal = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[t_assessment, xor_optional_legal]
    )
    po_assess_and_legal.order.add_edge(t_assessment, xor_optional_legal)

    t_req_supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
    t_doc_recheck = Transition(label="Document Check")
    po_supplement_redo = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[t_req_supplement, t_doc_recheck])
    po_supplement_redo.order.add_edge(t_req_supplement, t_doc_recheck)

    loop_assessment_legal = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[po_assess_and_legal, po_supplement_redo]
    )

    # Final decision and notification
    t_approve = Transition(label="Approve")
    t_reject = Transition(label="Reject")
    xor_decision = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR, children=[t_approve, t_reject]
    )
    t_notify = Transition(label="Notify")

    # =========================================================================
    # 4. Explicitly set top-level POWL partial order edges
    #    Ensuring Assessment (inside loop_assessment_legal) waits for BOTH
    #    Identity Check AND Document verification branch to complete.
    # =========================================================================
    powl_site_b = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            t_register,
            t_id_check,
            xor_doc_branch,
            loop_assessment_legal,
            xor_decision,
            t_notify,
        ]
    )

    # Register precedes both parallel branches
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(t_register, t_id_check)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(t_register, xor_doc_branch)

    # Both Identity Check and Document branch must finish before Assessment starts
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(t_id_check, loop_assessment_legal)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(xor_doc_branch, loop_assessment_legal)

    # Decision and Notify follow Assessment/Legal Review loop completion
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(loop_assessment_legal, xor_decision)
    powl_site_b.order.add_edge(xor_decision, t_notify)

    # =========================================================================
    # 5 & 6. Convert Site B POWL model to Accepting Petri Net & verify soundness
    # =========================================================================
    net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl_site_b)
    is_wf_b = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_b)
    is_sound_b, sound_diagnostics = pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b)

    print("\n=== SITE B CANDIDATE POWL MODEL ===")
    print(f"Site B POWL   - Workflow Net: {is_wf_b}, Sound: {is_sound_b}")
    print(
        f"Petri Net Size: {len(net_b.places)} places, {len(net_b.transitions)} transitions"
    )

    # =========================================================================
    # 7. Perform playout to generate and inspect sample traces
    # =========================================================================
    sim_log = pm4py.play_out(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    print(f"\nGenerated {len(sim_log)} traces via play_out. Sample traces:")
    seen_variants = set()
    for trace in sim_log:
        variant = tuple(event["concept:name"] for event in trace)
        if variant not in seen_variants:
            seen_variants.add(variant)
            print("  ->", " -> ".join(variant))
        if len(seen_variants) >= 6:
            break

    # =========================================================================
    # 8. Modeling Limitations (What POWL / Workflow Nets Cannot Encode)
    # =========================================================================
    # NOTE ON MODEL LIMITATIONS:
    # 1. Data-dependent statutory guards: The XOR split for 'Originals Receipt' (62%)
    #    and 'Legal Review' (40%) is modeled as non-deterministic choice. Standard
    #    POWL / sound WF-nets do not encode case attributes (e.g., claim_value,
    #    public_sector, originals_required) or guard preventing 'Request Supplement'
    #    from occurring when 'Legal Review' was skipped (tau_skip_legal).
    # 2. Resource capacity & role specialization: The Petri net has no notion of
    #    the finite pools (4 Identity, 7 Document, 3 Assessment, 2 Legal FTEs) or
    #    cross-case queueing contention.
    # 3. IT Case Locking & WIP Caps: The net allows true token concurrency between
    #    'Identity Check' and 'Document Check', abstracting away legacy whole-case
    #    locks and the per-reviewer WIP cap of 4 tasks.
    # 4. Stochastic durations & tail delays: The net is untimed; it cannot represent
    #    the 18h median delay (and heavy tail) of 'Originals Receipt' vs. the 3.4h
    #    duration of 'Identity Check'.


if __name__ == "__main__":
    build_and_verify_models()
```

## 9. Recommendation

**Decision: `conditional go`**

### Rationale and Transfer Strategy
Site B should **not** copy Site A's intervention unconditionally, nor should it accept Site A's 9-hour median cycle time reduction as a realistic expectation. Site A's headline gain was heavily confounded by an 11% verification staffing increase (+2 FTE), a marketing-driven drop in case complexity (48% to 40%), an API latency upgrade, and a 90% digital document share. However, the core partial-order synchronization mechanism—removing `Identity Check` from the sequential critical path of document verification—is valid and worth transferring under strict operational and technical conditions.

**What should be transferred:**
1. **Concurrent execution of `Identity Check` and `Document Check`** for the **38% of Site B claims that are digitally complete at registration**.
2. **Immediate parallel initiation of `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt`** at `Register` for the **62% originals-required cohort** (fixing the current dysfunction where 1,020 cases wait for `Identity Check` to finish before `Originals Receipt` even starts).
3. **Operational co-interventions from Site A**: daily cross-role queue huddles and strict per-reviewer WIP caps (set at 4 tasks per reviewer at Site B), augmented by a **Sibling-Priority Pull Rule** so one completed verification branch does not wait long at the `Assessment` join gate.

**What must NOT be copied:**
- Do **not** create active `Document Check` tasks immediately after `Register` for the 62% of cases requiring physical originals; doing so violates statutory prerequisites and floods queues with unworkable WIP.
- Do **not** launch concurrency on top of a whole-case IT lock; forcing staff to use off-system workarounds will increase rework and corrupt timestamp logging.

**Sequencing, Benefits, and Risks:**
Enter **Phase 1 with the 38% digital-only subgroup** after patching the IT system to support sub-task locking and automated start/end timestamps. Expected benefit is a **4.0h to 6.0h median reduction** within the digital cohort and a **3.5h to 5.5h fleet-wide median reduction** once Phase 2 (parallel `Identity Check` $\parallel$ `Originals Receipt`) rolls out. Key risks are **bottleneck shifting** into the 3-FTE `Assessment` and 2-FTE `Legal Review` pools (which can worsen p90 cycle time even as the median drops) and **higher rework/supplement loops** from losing sequential error discovery.

**Decision Triggers:**
- **Expand to Phase 2 (Originals cohort)** if Phase 1 achieves $>60\%$ true verification overlap, reduces pre-`Assessment` waiting by $\ge 3\text{h}$, and keeps rework/supplement changes within $+1.0\%$.
- **Modify** (tighten release rate into `Assessment` or strengthen automated pre-join validation) if post-join `Assessment` queue time or `Request Supplement` loops rise by $>2\%$.
- **Abandon / Rollback** if IT task-lock errors exceed $2\%$, rework jumps by $>4$ percentage points, or p90 cycle time degrades by $>15\%$ for two consecutive weeks.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Task-Level IT Lock Feasibility** — *Why needed*: Without decoupling `Identity Check` and `Document Check` task ownership from the parent case lock, concurrent execution is impossible without shadow workarounds. — *How to test*: Conduct a pre-pilot technical sandbox test with concurrent saves by two role profiles on the same claim ID. — *Consequence if false*: High lock contention, shadow back-entry of timestamps, and zero true concurrency in Phase 1 (requires postponing digital parallelization until IT patch is delivered and only parallelizing `Identity Check` with unlocked `Originals Receipt`).
2. **Independence of `Document Check` from `Identity Check` Outputs** — *Why needed*: Site A's checks used independent data; if Site B's Document reviewers rely on artifacts or corrections produced during `Identity Check`, running them in parallel will cause defects or rework. — *How to test*: Audit 100 historical rework and `Request Supplement` cases at Site B to check how often `Document Check` caught or depended on an `Identity Check` discrepancy. — *Consequence if false*: Parallelization will spike rework (>20%) and `Request Supplement` loops; requires adding an automated cross-check gate before `Assessment` or keeping high-risk cases sequential.
3. **Accurate Classification of `originals_required` at `Register`** — *Why needed*: Our segmented intervention routes the 38% digital-eligible cases to immediate `Document Check` and holds the 62% originals-required cases in `Originals Receipt`. — *How to test*: Measure the historical false-negative rate where cases initially flagged as digital-ready at `Register` later required physical originals during `Document Check`. — *Consequence if false*: Misclassified cases will stall mid-`Document Check`, wasting Document reviewer capacity and creating an unmodeled rework loop.
4. **Elapsed vs. Hands-On Service Time Slack** — *Why needed*: Reported median service times (3.4h Identity, 8.1h Document) exceed available FTE hours if treated as continuous hands-on labor, implying they include intra-task waiting/queries that can be interleaved under a WIP cap of 4. — *How to test*: Instrument active window focus or lifecycle state (`started_at`, `paused_at`, `completed_at`) during a 2-week baseline observation. — *Consequence if false*: If 8.1h is pure continuous labor, the 7 Document FTEs are heavily over-utilized and any increase in rework loops will immediately collapse queue stability.
5. **Non-Saturation of Downstream `Assessment` (3 FTE) and `Legal Review` (2 FTE) Pools** — *Why needed*: For verification speedups to translate into end-to-end `cycle_time` reductions, `Assessment` and `Legal Review` must have sufficient slack or smoothed arrival pacing to absorb faster upstream completions. — *How to test*: Calculate current traffic intensity ($\rho = \lambda / (c \mu)$) and pre-service queue waiting time specifically for `Assessment` and `Legal Review`. — *Consequence if false*: Bottleneck-Shift Scenario occurs—median cycle time improves slightly while p90 tail worsens due to downstream queue buildup.
6. **Avoidability of the Legacy `Identity Check -> Originals Receipt` Delay** — *Why needed*: DFG evidence shows 1,020 cases where `Originals Receipt` occurred *after* `Identity Check`; Phase 2 gains assume `Originals Receipt` can be triggered immediately at `Register` alongside `Identity Check`. — *How to test*: Verify with intake staff whether `Originals Receipt` represents waiting for the claimant's mail (which starts at `Register`) or an internal mailroom request that was needlessly held until `Identity Check` finished. — *Consequence if false*: If `Identity Check` must legally clear before requesting originals in those 1,020 cases, Phase 2 cannot parallelize them and fleet median savings drop by ~1.5h–2.5h.
7. **Effectiveness of Sub-Pool Ring-Fencing and Scheduled Release (SUTVA)** — *Why needed*: In the pilot, Treatment and Control claims share the same physical department; queue spillovers would bias causal estimates. — *How to test*: Track Control group queue wait times before `Identity Check`, `Document Check`, and `Assessment` during the pilot against the 6-month historical baseline (20h). — *Consequence if false*: Pilot treatment effect estimates will be upwardly biased (if Treatment crowds out Control) or attenuated, requiring switchback (time-clustered) randomization instead of claim-level randomization.
8. **Identity Invariance Across `Request Supplement` Loops** — *Why needed*: Our candidate model routes `Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment` without repeating `Identity Check` (matching the DFG count of 345 `Request Supplement -> Document Check` transitions). — *How to test*: Check the full DFG for `Request Supplement -> Identity Check` frequency (at most $360 - 345 = 15$ cases). — *Consequence if false*: Negligible operational impact (~4% of loops), handled as an exception branch.
