Opposite DFG edges do not prove concurrency. `CC→ER` and `ER→CC` mean only that both *completion* orders were recorded as immediately-follows relations. That pattern is produced by at least four different mechanisms: (1) an XOR of two sequential procedures, (2) an unordered pair with no overlap requirement, (3) truly overlapping executions whose completions interleave, and (4) equal completion timestamps ordered by ingestion index (S7). An AND-split asserts enablement of both activities and, in the usual Petri-net reading, overlapping enablement of all linear extensions. Moving from a bidirectional DFG to that construct is an inductive leap, which is why D7 is treated as binding epistemology rather than as a license to insert `+(Credit Check, Engineering Review)` without comment.

# 2. Shared Assumptions

All three candidates share the following commitments.

**Completion-event semantics.** Every event is a completion record. A sequence `X, Y` means “X completed, then Y completed.” It does not mean Y started after X finished. Same-timestamp events are ordered by ingestion index (S7), so a recorded order can be an artefact.

**Mandatory skeleton.** Register Order is the unique start and Archive Case the unique end (D1). Approve Configuration, Release Production, and Archive Case form a fixed suffix, and Release Production is not enabled without approval (D2). Credit Check and Engineering Review both occur on every case and both complete before approval (D3). No candidate allows activity repetition: nothing in the log or the domain rules supports a loop.

**Supplier Confirmation absence is a mixed abstraction.** The log has no external/stocked attribute (S5). Seven of thirty cases lack SC (23%). An integration loss of 5–10% cannot explain all of those absences, so genuine stocked skips (S4) and dropped SC events (S5) are both in play. Every model represents this mixture as a control-flow alternative `XOR(Supplier Confirmation, τ)`. That is not a claim that every skip is a legitimate stocked case, and it is not a claim that SC is optional in the business rule for external components (S3). It is a disclosure that the missing attribute has been compiled into control flow.

**Expedite Review.** Optional, at most once, after both principal checks, before approval (D6, S6). Position versus SC is not fixed.

**Frequencies are not encoded.** Variant counts inform inductive bias and the evidence ledger; they do not become weights, guards, or stochastic choices in the control-flow models.

**No causality from order alone.** Where the candidates differ is exactly where D5, D7, S1–S5 underdetermine the mechanism: the relationship among Credit Check, Engineering Review, Supplier Confirmation, and Expedite Review.

# 3. Conservative Candidate

**Inductive bias.** Prefer the smallest language that still replays every observed variant and obeys D1–D2 and the non-repeating reading of D6. Do not insert concurrency, “after at least one check” enablement, or unobserved Exp placements. The model is an explicit XOR of the seven recorded sequences. This overfits the sample on purpose: it is a hypothesis that the log’s support *is* the process, and that every other interleaving is unsupported.

**Formal expression.**

\[
L_{\mathrm{cons}} = \{v_1,\ldots,v_7\}
\]

\[
\texttt{X}(\sigma(v_1),\sigma(v_2),\sigma(v_3),\sigma(v_4),\sigma(v_5),\sigma(v_6),\sigma(v_7))
\]

where each \(\sigma(v_i)\) is the sequential process tree of variant \(i\). Duplicate labels are essential: each activity name appears once per branch that uses it.

**PM4Py code.**

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator, ProcessTree

def pt_leaf(label):
    return ProcessTree(label=label)

def pt_op(operator, children):
    node = ProcessTree(operator=operator, children=list(children))
    for child in node.children:
        child.parent = node
    return node

def pt_seq(labels):
    return pt_op(Operator.SEQUENCE, [pt_leaf(label) for label in labels])

_OBS = [
    ["Register Order", "Credit Check", "Engineering Review", "Supplier Confirmation",
     "Approve Configuration", "Release Production", "Archive Case"],
    ["Register Order", "Engineering Review", "Credit Check", "Supplier Confirmation",
     "Approve Configuration", "Release Production", "Archive Case"],
    ["Register Order", "Credit Check", "Engineering Review",
     "Approve Configuration", "Release Production", "Archive Case"],
    ["Register Order", "Engineering Review", "Credit Check",
     "Approve Configuration", "Release Production", "Archive Case"],
    ["Register Order", "Credit Check", "Supplier Confirmation", "Engineering Review",
     "Approve Configuration", "Release Production", "Archive Case"],
    ["Register Order", "Credit Check", "Engineering Review", "Supplier Confirmation",
     "Expedite Review", "Approve Configuration", "Release Production", "Archive Case"],
    ["Register Order", "Engineering Review", "Credit Check", "Expedite Review",
     "Supplier Confirmation", "Approve Configuration", "Release Production", "Archive Case"],
]

model_conservative = pt_op(Operator.XOR, [pt_seq(v) for v in _OBS])
```

**Duplicate labels and scalability.** The tree contains seven copies of `Register Order`, `Approve Configuration`, `Release Production`, and `Archive Case`, six or fewer copies of the checks and SC, and two of Exp. Conversion to a Petri net yields distinct transitions that share labels. Alignments and token-based replay still work, but the net is not unique-label, place invariants are duplicated, and the structure grows linearly with the number of variants. Seven variants are acceptable. A log with hundreds of variants would make this encoding unreadable and would hide any shared suffix. The XOR-enumeration is a deliberate finite-language hypothesis, not a scalable discovery pattern.

**Behavior deliberately excluded.**
- Any interleaving not among the seven variants, including `RO, ER, SC, CC, …` (D5-style, unobserved).
- Expedite Review without Supplier Confirmation.
- Expedite Review on the V1-shaped path after both checks but in a different SC/Exp order than V6, other than the exact V6 and V7 strings.
- SC before both checks.
- Skipping Credit Check or Engineering Review.
- Repeating any activity; inserting work after Archive Case; starting without Register Order.
- A true AND-split of the two checks (that would add unobserved linear extensions).

**Known underfitting risks.**
- If S2/D5 are right, tomorrow’s `ER, SC, CC` case is legal and will be marked deviant.
- If Exp is a rare but general optional step (D6), Exp-without-SC and the missing Exp/SC interleavings are falsely forbidden.
- If 5–10% of SC events are dropped onto *new* check orders, those repaired-looking traces will not fit.
- The model cannot absorb a new but legitimate stocked-component path that differs from V3/V4 only in check interleaving with something else.
- Used as a runtime constraint, it will over-flag normal variation and train stakeholders to treat the sample as the law.

# 4. Balanced Candidate

**Inductive bias.** Replay all 30 cases, keep every high-confidence rule, and generalize only where the log and the medium-confidence rules point the same way. Represent uncertainty with partial order (“no evidence of a forced order”) rather than with an AND-split story, and keep SC enablement as “after at least one principal check” (D5) rather than “after both” or “after Register Order.” Optionality of SC and Exp is explicit. Unobserved combinations are admitted only when a named rule licenses them.

**How sequence, concurrency, and partial order are chosen.**

| Pair | Choice | Why this and not the others |
|---|---|---|
| Credit Check vs Engineering Review | **Partial order, no edge** (unordered pair) | Both completion orders are common (19 vs 11). S1 is contradicted as an as-is rule. S2 explains the two orders. D7 forbids reading that pair as proven overlap. A *sequence* CC≺ER is refuted by V2/V4/V7. An XOR of the two sequences would replay V1–V4, V6–V7 but would still force a total order and would not by itself place SC between the checks (V5). A Petri-net AND-split has the same *language* as an unordered pair on two activities, but it *suggests* overlapping enablement we cannot see. POWL’s missing edge is the weaker claim: “no forced order.” |
| Supplier Confirmation vs the checks | **XOR of two partial orders plus a skip branch**: SC after CC (ER ∥ SC), or SC after ER (CC ∥ SC), or no SC | “After both” (AND-join then SC) kills V5 and fights D5. “Fully concurrent from RO” admits SC-first, which is unobserved and stronger than D5. “After at least one” is exactly D5 and covers V1, V2, V5, V6, V7. The unobserved sibling `ER, SC, CC` is admitted on purpose (symmetric reading of D5). Skip vs SC compiles S4+S5 into control flow, as disclosed in §2. |
| Expedite Review vs the checks | **Sequence after both checks** | D6 and S6, and both observed Exp traces. Not concurrent with the checks: that would allow Exp before a check. |
| Expedite Review vs Supplier Confirmation | **No order edge** | V6 has SC≺Exp; V7 has Exp≺SC; S6 permits both. A sequence either way loses fitness. |
| Everything vs the suffix | **Sequence** | D1, D2, 30/30 observations. |

**Formal expression.**

\[
\mathrm{RO}
\;\prec\;
\mathrm{XOR}\big(
  \mathrm{PO}\{\mathrm{CC},\mathrm{ER}\}\prec\mathrm{XOR}(\mathrm{Exp},\tau),\;
  \mathrm{PO}\{\mathrm{CC}\prec\mathrm{SC},\;\mathrm{CC}\prec\mathrm{Exp},\;\mathrm{ER}\prec\mathrm{Exp}\},\;
  \mathrm{PO}\{\mathrm{ER}\prec\mathrm{SC},\;\mathrm{CC}\prec\mathrm{Exp},\;\mathrm{ER}\prec\mathrm{Exp}\}
\big)
\;\prec\;
\mathrm{AC}\prec\mathrm{RP}\prec\mathrm{Arc}
\]

In the two SC branches, ER∥SC or CC∥SC respectively, and Exp∥SC. The first XOR child is the genuine-skip / missing-SC abstraction.

**PM4Py code.**

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import OperatorPOWL, SilentTransition, StrictPartialOrder, Transition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

def act(label):
    return Transition(label=label)

def optional(label):
    return OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[act(label), SilentTransition()])

def balanced_middle(sc_after):
    """sc_after is None, 'Credit Check', or 'Engineering Review'."""
    cc = act("Credit Check")
    er = act("Engineering Review")
    exp = optional("Expedite Review")
    nodes = [cc, er, exp]
    edges = [(cc, exp), (er, exp)]
    if sc_after is not None:
        sc = act("Supplier Confirmation")
        nodes.append(sc)
        predecessor = cc if sc_after == "Credit Check" else er
        edges.append((predecessor, sc))
        # no edge between SC and Exp: either order (V6, V7, S6)
        # no edge between SC and the other check: V5 and D5
    po = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)
    for src, tgt in edges:
        po.order.add_edge(src, tgt)
    return po

_ro = act("Register Order")
_mid = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[
        balanced_middle(None),
        balanced_middle("Credit Check"),
        balanced_middle("Engineering Review"),
    ],
)
_ac = act("Approve Configuration")
_rp = act("Release Production")
_arc = act("Archive Case")

model_balanced = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[_ro, _mid, _ac, _rp, _arc])
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_ro, _mid)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_mid, _ac)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_ac, _rp)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_rp, _arc)
```

**Behavior deliberately excluded.**
- SC completing before both checks (`RO, SC, …`).
- Exp completing before both checks (`RO, CC, Exp, ER, …`).
- Missing Credit Check or Engineering Review.
- SC after Approve Configuration; any work after Archive Case.
- A second Expedite Review or any other repeated label.
- S1 as a hard sequence CC≺ER.
- SC treated as mandatory (S3 without S4/S5).

**Known underfitting risks.**
- If procurement may legally start as soon as the order exists (a strong reading of S3), SC-first cases will be false negatives.
- If the two “SC after CC only” cases (V5) are logging accidents and the real rule is “SC after both,” the model over-admits mid-check SC, including the unobserved ER-SC-CC family.
- If almost all SC skips are missing events and SC is mandatory for this product mix, optional SC underfits the *norm* even while it fits the *log*.
- The XOR-of-enablement-sites encoding is a stand-in for an inclusive OR-join (“after at least one check”), which POWL cannot write as a single partial order. Some equivalent interleavings are represented twice (SC after both checks sits in two children).

# 5. Permissive Candidate

**Inductive bias.** Keep the hard skeleton (D1–D3, D6, AC≺RP≺Arc) and admit every interleaving of the two checks with optional SC that a completion log could show if procurement is allowed to run from order registration. D5 is read as permission to start SC after one check, *not* as a ban on starting earlier. S3 (SC is a normal, early procurement action) is allowed to dominate the low-to-medium “wait for a check” reading. Exp remains compliance-bound: after both checks, at most once, before approval, unordered with SC. No loops, no skipped principal checks, no post-approval procurement.

**Formal expression.** Linear extensions of

\[
\begin{align*}
\mathrm{RO} &\prec \mathrm{CC},\;
\mathrm{RO} \prec \mathrm{ER},\;
\mathrm{RO} \prec \mathrm{XOR}(\mathrm{SC},\tau),\\
\mathrm{CC} &\prec \mathrm{XOR}(\mathrm{Exp},\tau),\;
\mathrm{ER} \prec \mathrm{XOR}(\mathrm{Exp},\tau),\\
\{\mathrm{CC},\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{XOR}(\mathrm{SC},\tau),\mathrm{XOR}(\mathrm{Exp},\tau)\} &\prec \mathrm{AC} \prec \mathrm{RP} \prec \mathrm{Arc}.
\end{align*}
\]

**Boundary: reasonable generalization vs still forbidden.**

| Admitted (reasonable but partly unobserved) | Still forbidden |
|---|---|
| SC immediately after RO, interleaved arbitrarily with CC and ER | Exp before both checks have completed (D6, S6) |
| `ER, SC, CC` and every other shuffle of {CC, ER, SC} | Missing CC or ER (D3) |
| Exp without SC; SC without Exp; both; neither | SC after Approve Configuration (D4) |
| Exp before leftover SC or after SC, once both checks are done | Any start other than RO; any end other than Arc (D1) |
| All two-check orders, without claiming they overlap | Repeated activities / loops |
| Optional SC as the S4+S5 abstraction | Skipping AC, RP, or Arc; RP before AC (D2) |

The ProcessTree `→(RO, +(CC, ER, X(τ,SC), X(τ,Exp)), AC, RP, Arc)` would also admit Exp-before-checks. That crosses the forbidden boundary and is **not** used.

**PM4Py code.**

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import OperatorPOWL, SilentTransition, StrictPartialOrder, Transition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

def act(label):
    return Transition(label=label)

def optional(label):
    return OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[act(label), SilentTransition()])

ro = act("Register Order")
cc = act("Credit Check")
er = act("Engineering Review")
sc = optional("Supplier Confirmation")
exp = optional("Expedite Review")
ac = act("Approve Configuration")
rp = act("Release Production")
arc = act("Archive Case")

model_permissive = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[ro, cc, er, sc, exp, ac, rp, arc])
model_permissive.order.add_edge(ro, cc)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(ro, er)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(ro, sc)      # SC may begin at order registration
model_permissive.order.add_edge(cc, exp)     # D6 / S6
model_permissive.order.add_edge(er, exp)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(sc, ac)      # D4 when SC is present; τ still precedes AC
model_permissive.order.add_edge(exp, ac)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(ac, rp)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(rp, arc)
```

**Behavior deliberately excluded.** Listed in the “still forbidden” column above. In particular the model does *not* allow a finance-only path that skips Engineering Review, a late SC after approval, a double expedite, or Exp as a peer of the principal checks.

**Known underfitting / over-approximation risks.**
- Over-approximation: SC-first and many SC/check shuffles were never seen; if D5 is a hard wait-for-information rule, those traces are undesirable.
- Over-approximation: optional SC still mixes genuine skips with missing events; if S3 is strictly true for this log’s population, skips should be deviations, not language.
- Underfitting relative to an even wilder exploration model: we still reject Exp-before-checks and missing principal checks. That is intentional.
- Structural simplicity hides a large finite language. Precision will look worse than the conservative XOR even if every extra trace is “reasonable.”

# 6. Unobserved Behavior Audit

The conservative language is exactly the seven observed variants (finite, no silent loops, bound = sequences over the eight labels with each label used at most once). It therefore admits **no** unobserved complete trace inside that bound.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| Balanced | RO, ER, SC, CC, AC, RP, Arc | SC-after-ER branch; CC ∥ SC | Plausible | D5, S2, symmetry with V5 |
| Balanced | RO, CC, ER, Exp, AC, RP, Arc | Skip-SC branch; Exp after both checks | Plausible | D6, S4, S6; V3+V6 composition |
| Balanced | RO, ER, CC, SC, Exp, AC, RP, Arc | SC-after-ER (also after both); Exp ∥ SC | Plausible | V2+V6, S6 |

| Permissive | RO, CC, ER, Exp, AC, RP, Arc | Optional SC skipped; Exp after checks | Plausible | D6, S4 |

| Model | Excluded trace | Earliest violated constraint |
|---|---|---|

# 8. Validation Code

The script reconstructs the 30-case log, builds all three candidates, converts each to an accepting Petri net, checks soundness, computes the available quality metrics, runs a bounded playout, and scores a hand-written positive/negative suite.

**Limitation.** None of these models has a loop, so each language is finite. The conservative language is tiny and can be compared exactly to the log support. The balanced and permissive languages are larger finite sets of linear extensions. `play_out` samples or enumerates up to a bound; a sample that happens to look like a subset of the log does **not** prove language inclusion, and a sample that happens to contain an extra trace does **not** enumerate the extra language. Alignment fitness on the 30 cases does not prove that unobserved legal traces fit. Exhaustive language comparison would require enumerating all linear extensions (feasible here by a dedicated enumerator, not by sampled playout).

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import OperatorPOWL, SilentTransition, StrictPartialOrder, Transition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator, ProcessTree

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. Reconstruct the 30-case log
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
VARIANT_COUNTS = [
    (12, "Register Order,Credit Check,Engineering Review,Supplier Confirmation,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case"),
    (7,  "Register Order,Engineering Review,Credit Check,Supplier Confirmation,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case"),
    (4,  "Register Order,Credit Check,Engineering Review,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case"),
    (3,  "Register Order,Engineering Review,Credit Check,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case"),
    (2,  "Register Order,Credit Check,Supplier Confirmation,Engineering Review,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case"),
    (1,  "Register Order,Credit Check,Engineering Review,Supplier Confirmation,Expedite Review,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case"),
    (1,  "Register Order,Engineering Review,Credit Check,Expedite Review,Supplier Confirmation,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case"),
]
trace_strings = []
for count, variant in VARIANT_COUNTS:
    trace_strings.extend([variant] * count)
assert len(trace_strings) == 30
log = pm4py.parse_event_log_string(trace_strings)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Models (same constructions as §§3–5)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
def pt_leaf(label):
    return ProcessTree(label=label)

def pt_op(operator, children):
    node = ProcessTree(operator=operator, children=list(children))
    for child in node.children:
        child.parent = node
    return node

def pt_seq(labels):
    return pt_op(Operator.SEQUENCE, [pt_leaf(label) for label in labels])

_OBS = [v.split(",") for _, v in VARIANT_COUNTS]
model_conservative = pt_op(Operator.XOR, [pt_seq(v) for v in _OBS])

def act(label):
    return Transition(label=label)

def optional(label):
    return OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[act(label), SilentTransition()])

def balanced_middle(sc_after):
    cc = act("Credit Check")
    er = act("Engineering Review")
    exp = optional("Expedite Review")
    nodes = [cc, er, exp]
    edges = [(cc, exp), (er, exp)]
    if sc_after is not None:
        sc = act("Supplier Confirmation")
        nodes.append(sc)
        predecessor = cc if sc_after == "Credit Check" else er
        edges.append((predecessor, sc))
    po = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)
    for src, tgt in edges:
        po.order.add_edge(src, tgt)
    return po

_ro = act("Register Order")
_mid = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[
        balanced_middle(None),
        balanced_middle("Credit Check"),
        balanced_middle("Engineering Review"),
    ],
)
_ac = act("Approve Configuration")
_rp = act("Release Production")
_arc = act("Archive Case")
model_balanced = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[_ro, _mid, _ac, _rp, _arc])
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_ro, _mid)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_mid, _ac)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_ac, _rp)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_rp, _arc)

ro, cc, er = act("Register Order"), act("Credit Check"), act("Engineering Review")
sc, exp = optional("Supplier Confirmation"), optional("Expedite Review")
ac, rp, arc = act("Approve Configuration"), act("Release Production"), act("Archive Case")
model_permissive = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[ro, cc, er, sc, exp, ac, rp, arc])
for src, tgt in [
    (ro, cc), (ro, er), (ro, sc),
    (cc, exp), (er, exp),
    (sc, ac), (exp, ac),
    (ac, rp), (rp, arc),
]:
    model_permissive.order.add_edge(src, tgt)

MODELS = {
    "conservative": model_conservative,
    "balanced": model_balanced,
    "permissive": model_permissive,
}

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2–5. Nets, soundness, metrics, bounded playout
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
def convert(model):
    return pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(model)

def try_call(fn, *args, **kwargs):
    try:
        return fn(*args, **kwargs)
    except Exception as exc:
        return f"unavailable ({type(exc).__name__}: {exc})"

nets = {}
for name, model in MODELS.items():
    net, im, fm = convert(model)
    nets[name] = (net, im, fm)
    print(f"\n=== {name} ===")
    print("soundness:", try_call(pm4py.check_soundness, net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True))
    print("fitness_alignments:", try_call(pm4py.fitness_alignments, log, net, im, fm))
    print("precision_alignments:", try_call(pm4py.precision_alignments, log, net, im, fm))
    print("generalization_tbr:", try_call(pm4py.generalization_tbr, log, net, im, fm))
    print("simplicity_petri_net:", try_call(pm4py.simplicity_petri_net, net, im, fm))
    played = try_call(pm4py.play_out, net, im, fm)
    if isinstance(played, str):
        print("play_out:", played)
    else:
        n_played = len(played) if played is not None else 0
        print(f"play_out: got {n_played} traces (sampled/bounded; not a language proof)")

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 6. Manual positive / negative suite
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE = [variant for _, variant in VARIANT_COUNTS]  # the 7 observed shapes
NEGATIVE = {
    "sc_first":
        "Register Order,Supplier Confirmation,Credit Check,Engineering Review,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case",
    "exp_before_second_check":
        "Register Order,Credit Check,Expedite Review,Engineering Review,Supplier Confirmation,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case",
    "missing_engineering":
        "Register Order,Credit Check,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case",
    "sc_after_approve":
        "Register Order,Credit Check,Engineering Review,Approve Configuration,Supplier Confirmation,Release Production,Archive Case",
    "er_sc_cc_no_exp":
        "Register Order,Engineering Review,Supplier Confirmation,Credit Check,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case",
    "exp_without_sc":
        "Register Order,Credit Check,Engineering Review,Expedite Review,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case",
}

def one_trace_log(trace_csv):
    return pm4py.parse_event_log_string([trace_csv])

def is_fitting(trace_csv, net, im, fm):
    result = pm4py.fitness_alignments(one_trace_log(trace_csv), net, im, fm)
    if not isinstance(result, dict):
        return None
    for key in ("perc_fit_traces", "percentage_of_fitting_traces"):
        if key in result:
            return result[key] >= 100.0 - 1e-9
    if "average_trace_fitness" in result:
        return result["average_trace_fitness"] >= 1.0 - 1e-9
    return None

print("\n=== positive suite (must fit all three models) ===")
for name, (net, im, fm) in nets.items():
    for trace in POSITIVE:
        print(f"{name:13} FIT={is_fitting(trace, net, im, fm)}  {trace}")

print("\n=== negative / discriminator suite ===")
print("expected: conservative rejects all of these;")
print("          balanced accepts er_sc_cc_no_exp and exp_without_sc, rejects sc_first;")
print("          permissive additionally accepts sc_first;")
print("          all reject exp_before_second_check, missing_engineering, sc_after_approve.")
for name, (net, im, fm) in nets.items():
    for tag, trace in NEGATIVE.items():
        print(f"{name:13} {tag:28} FIT={is_fitting(trace, net, im, fm)}")
```

If a metric API is missing in the installed PM4Py, `try_call` records that fact instead of inventing a number. Alignment fitness on the 30-case log is a replay check, not a language-equality check.

# 9. Recommendation and Data-Collection Plan

**Immediate use: `model_balanced`.** It replays the log, obeys D1–D4 and D6/S6, treats S1 as a refuted as-is rule, and generalizes only along D5 (SC after either check) and optional Exp. That is the right default for operations and for compliance monitoring of expedite and dual-check rules. `model_conservative` is too tight for a living process (the next D5-consistent case becomes an incident). `model_permissive` is too willing to treat early procurement as already authorized.

**Sensitivity analysis: `model_permissive`, with `model_conservative` as a language oracle.** Run the same future cases through all three. Disagreements are the diagnostic: a case that fits permissive only is an SC-early (or novel shuffle) event; a case that fits balanced and permissive but not conservative is a predicted generalization firing; a case that fits none is a true skeleton break (missing check, late SC, late Exp, wrong terminals). Keep the conservative XOR as a regression snapshot of “what the first 30 cases actually were.”

**Data-collection plan (maximally informative next measurements).**

1. **Lifecycle timestamps, not only completion.** Record `start` and `complete` (and, if the system has them, `schedule`/`assign`) for Credit Check, Engineering Review, Supplier Confirmation, and Expedite Review. Overlapping intervals vs disjoint intervals is the only observation that can separate S2’s true concurrency from an XOR of two sequences and from a mere unordered pair (D7).
2. **Export the external vs stocked attribute** on every order line, and a case-level flag “any external component.” This dissolves the S3/S4/S5 mixture that is currently compiled into `XOR(SC, τ)`.
3. **Repair the SC integration.** Until drop-rate is measured on cases that are independently known to be external, optional SC remains an abstraction. Log a technical “SC expected / SC received” heartbeat.
4. **Do not break ties by ingestion index alone.** If two completions share a timestamp, keep them as a recorded concurrency `{X, Y}` rather than forcing an order (S7).
5. **Resource and case attributes** that S1/S2 may actually be about: whether finance and engineering are the same person, whether a rush flag was set, whether configuration was standard.

**Future traces that discriminate the hypotheses** (one new case of any of these is worth more than another dozen V1 copies):

| New trace (plus the fixed suffix AC–RP–Arc) | If it appears | Favours |
|---|---|---|
| RO, ER, SC, CC, … | D5 is symmetric | Balanced over conservative |
| RO, SC, CC, ER, … or RO, SC, ER, CC, … | Procurement starts with the order | Permissive; weaken D5 to a permission |
| RO, CC, ER, Exp, … (no SC) on a *stocked* case | Exp is independent of SC | Balanced/permissive optionality of both |
| RO, CC, ER, Exp, … (no SC) on an *external* case | Missing SC event, not a skip | Repair the log; consider mandatory SC |
| Start(CC) < Start(ER) < Complete(CC) < Complete(ER) | Genuine overlap | Drop XOR-of-sequences; keep the unordered pair / AND-split |
| Complete(CC) < Start(ER) in (almost) every case, including those logged ER-first today | Logging artefact; S1 may be true | Reorder by start time; conservative/balanced both change |
| A second Exp on one case | D6’s “at most once” is wrong | All three models are too tight |
| SC after Approve Configuration | D4 is not operational | None of the candidates; escalate as a control failure |

# 10. Uncertainty Statement

| Modeling decision | Calibrated confidence | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| RO start, Arc end, AC≺RP≺Arc | Very high (~0.95) | D1, D2, 30/30, no competing story |
| Both checks mandatory and before approval | High (~0.85) | D3, 30/30; residual doubt only if the export can drop a whole check the way it may drop SC |
| Exp optional, ≤1, after both checks, before AC | High (~0.8) | D6+S6+V6+V7; only two observations, so the *optional* part is more certain than any finer placement |
| Do not enforce S1 (CC before ER) as as-is | High (~0.8) | 11 contradicting completion sequences; could still be a to-be rule |
| CC and ER as an unordered pair rather than proven concurrency | Medium (~0.6) | Right *language* given D7; the *mechanism* is unidentified without start times |
| SC optional in control flow | Medium (~0.55) | 23% absence vs 5–10% stated drop rate plus S4, but S3 and the missing attribute cut the other way |
| SC enabled after *at least one* check (balanced) | Medium-low (~0.5) | D5 is low-to-medium; only V5 (n=2) shows after-one-only, and only on the CC side |
| Admit ER-SC-CC by symmetry | Medium-low (~0.45) | Clean rule, zero observations |
| Reject SC-first (balanced) / admit SC-first (permissive) | Low-to-medium split (~0.4 / ~0.35) | This is the main live disagreement; evidence is absence, which is weak after n=30 |
| No loops | High (~0.85) | No repeats observed; no domain redo rule; still possible for rework after a failed approval that this extract never saw |

**Alternative interpretation that would change the recommendation.** Two alternatives, either of which is enough.

*A. Normative finance rule.* Treat S1 as a to-be control, not an as-is description. The 11 ER-before-CC cases become deviations. The recommended model would be a sequential `CC ≺ ER` skeleton (conservative or a tightened balanced), and the current balanced model would be demoted to “as-is diagnosis only.” Resource planning and compliance dashboards would flip.

*B. Mandatory SC plus missingness.* Treat S3 as true for this population, S4 as applying to a class that is rare or absent here, and the 7 skips as the S5 integration defect (perhaps understated). SC becomes mandatory, `XOR(SC, τ)` is removed, and the log is repaired or those seven cases are labeled incomplete. Immediate-use recommendation would move toward a mandatory-SC balanced model, and the current optional-SC balanced/permissive pair would be reserved for stocked-product lines once that attribute exists.

Until start timestamps and the external/stocked flag exist, those two alternatives remain open. The three candidates are competing hypotheses under that incompleteness, not three renderings of one known process.
