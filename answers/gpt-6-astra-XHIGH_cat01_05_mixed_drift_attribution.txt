## 2. Raw-versus-normalized interpretation

March’s raw fitness of 0.55 versus mapped fitness of 0.90 gives a 0.35-point recovery. Relative to the January–February reference of 0.925, this recovers about 93% of the raw decline. The March normalized DFG is also close to January, and cycle times barely change. Together, these strongly support a migration-related semantic/provenance explanation for the initial collapse. The gap measures sensitivity to the specified correction, not a causal fraction of all drift or the percentage of compliant cases.

Mapping labels can make distinct recorded traces equivalent to the model while concealing differences in eligibility criteria, automation, decision rules, or work content. Fitness also depends on model expressiveness, alignment settings and observation completeness. A historically under-recorded senior step may expose model inadequacy. Recovery therefore does not prove unchanged business behavior; inspect alignment deviations and process semantics separately.

Duplicated technical events can create spurious edges, variants, repetitions and alignment penalties. Remove only provenance-verified extras, retaining an audit trail. Missing resources chiefly undermine handover networks, workload attribution and organizational comparisons; they need not change activity-only conformance. Their concentration by migrated activity and team makes complete-case resource analysis selective.

After May, normalized information-request rates double from January, return-to-collection rates rise similarly, and cycle-time tails expand alongside reconstructed assessment queues. These coordinated patterns survive the stated corrections and fit operational disruptions. Logging drift alone is consequently insufficient, although timestamps, cohort boundaries and operational variant definitions still require validation. Only the supplied fitness variant normalization is guaranteed; recompute operational fast/rework categories consistently.

For a future **mean-cycle** accounting, define entry strata (x), their shares (p_t(x)), and mean durations (m_t(x)). An exact descriptive standardization is

\[
\Delta\mu=\sum_x[p_t(x)-p_0(x)]m_0(x)+\sum_xp_t(x)[m_t(x)-m_0(x)].
\]

The first term is a specified case-mix component; the second is within-stratum change. Use a sequential accounting within strata with visit counts (n_{t,a,x}), per-visit service (s_{t,a,x}), queue waiting (q_{t,a,x}), and other elapsed time (o_{t,x}). Define visit-level averages consistently so mean totals equal visit counts times those averages. For nonoverlapping elapsed intervals, replace baseline factors in this order:

1. Mix: change (p_0) to (p_t), holding conditional durations fixed.
2. Flow: change visit counts (n_0) to (n_t), holding per-visit service and waiting fixed.
3. Service: change (s_0) to (s_t), using current mix and visits.
4. Queueing: change (q_0) to (q_t), using current mix and visits.
5. Other time: change (o_0) to (o_t).

This telescopes for means under that accounting convention. Handle concurrent activities through elapsed-time/critical-path accounting to prevent double counting. Reversing replacement order reallocates interactions; report that sensitivity or use Shapley averaging. Supplier-generated visits can themselves cause queues, so accounting components are not independent causal effects. For medians and p90s, standardize complete duration distributions and recompute quantiles, with censoring handled explicitly; do not add differences of component medians.

The **primary monitoring population is all registered cases in a stable ingestion scope, indexed by registration cohort**, including cases still open. Maintain both observed-mix results, which capture actual customer impact, and results standardized to a fixed entry population, restricted to common support. Do not make Z cases, completed cases or cases with known resources the primary denominator. Registration cohorts preserve entry composition and avoid duration-dependent completion selection. Calendar-time arrivals, work-in-progress and ready-to-start assessment visits form a complementary operational view. Early 2026 supplier strata may fail the proposed support rules; that limits inference rather than justifying precision from tiny cells.

def candidate_changes(summary, fragment):
    """Exploratory material-change flags, not significance tests or fitted breaks."""
    records = []

    def add(previous, current, dimension, reason):
        records.append({'bracket': f'{previous:%Y-%m} -> {current:%Y-%m}',
                        'dimension': dimension, 'reason': reason})

    gains = summary['mapped_model_fitness'] - summary['raw_model_fitness']
    for i in range(1, len(summary)):
        previous, current = summary.index[i-1:i+1]
        change = summary.loc[current] - summary.loc[previous]
        if (abs(gains.loc[current] - gains.loc[previous]) >= 0.10
                or abs(change['missing_resource_pct']) >= 2
                or abs(change['distinct_activity_labels']) >= 1):
            add(previous, current, 'logging', 'fitness gap/schema/completeness shift')
        if abs(change['rework_variant_pct']) >= 5:
            add(previous, current, 'behavioral candidate',
                'operational rework share changed by >=5 percentage points')
        if abs(change['median_cycle_h']) >= 3 or abs(change['p90_cycle_h']) >= 10:
            add(previous, current, 'performance', 'median >=3 h or p90 >=10 h change')
    for i in range(1, len(fragment)):
        previous, current = fragment.index[i-1:i+1]
        change = fragment.loc[current] - fragment.loc[previous]
        if change.abs().sum() >= 25:
            add(previous, current, 'normalized-flow candidate',
                'reported-fragment L1 >=25 occurrences per 100 cases')
        if abs(change['Assess->SeniorAssessment']) >= 10:
            add(previous, current, 'logging OR behavioral candidate',
                'senior edge >=10/100 change: audit recording and actual routing')
    return pd.DataFrame(records)

def format_event_log(df, case_col='case_id', activity_col='activity',
                     timestamp_col='timestamp', sequence_col=None):
    import pm4py
    required = [case_col, activity_col, timestamp_col]
    if sequence_col is not None:
        required.append(sequence_col)
    missing = set(required) - set(df.columns)
    if missing:
        raise ValueError(f'Missing columns: {sorted(missing)}')
    result = df.copy(deep=True)
    if result[required].isna().any().any():
        raise ValueError('Case, activity, timestamp and any chosen sequence must be complete')
    # Naive input timestamps must already represent UTC; localize local wall time first.
    result[timestamp_col] = pd.to_datetime(result[timestamp_col], errors='raise', utc=True)
    if result[activity_col].astype(str).str.strip().eq('').any():
        raise ValueError('Empty activity label')
    result['_original_activity'] = result[activity_col].copy()
    result['_input_row'] = np.arange(len(result))
    if sequence_col is None and result.duplicated([case_col, timestamp_col]).any():
        warnings.warn('Tied timestamps: input order is only a technical tie-breaker; audit DFG sensitivity')
    order = [case_col, timestamp_col]
    if sequence_col is not None:
        order.append(sequence_col)
    order.append('_input_row')
    result = result.sort_values(order, kind='mergesort').reset_index(drop=True)
    return pm4py.format_dataframe(result, case_id=case_col,
                                  activity_key=activity_col, timestamp_key=timestamp_col)


def normalize_copy(df, activity_col='activity', verified_duplicate_col=None):
    """Copy before mapping; remove ONLY provenance-verified extra Open Case rows."""
    result = df.copy(deep=True)
    result['_unmapped_activity'] = result[activity_col].copy()
    result[activity_col] = result[activity_col].replace(
        {'Eligibility Screen': 'Validate Eligibility'})
    if verified_duplicate_col is None:
        warnings.warn('No verified-duplicate flag: technical duplicates retained')
    else:
        flag = result[verified_duplicate_col]
        if flag.isna().any() or not pd.api.types.is_bool_dtype(flag.dtype):
            raise ValueError('Verified-duplicate flag must be complete and boolean')
        remove = result[activity_col].eq('Open Case') & flag
        result = result.loc[~remove].copy()
    return result


def utc_bound(value):
    value = pd.Timestamp(value)
    return value.tz_localize('UTC') if value.tzinfo is None else value.tz_convert('UTC')


def slice_window(log, start, end, mode='events'):
    """Use PM4Py filtering; event mode enforces a half-open [start, end) window."""
    import pm4py
    if mode not in {'events', 'traces_contained', 'traces_intersecting'}:
        raise ValueError('Unsupported PM4Py time-window mode')
    left, right = utc_bound(start), utc_bound(end)
    if left >= right:
        raise ValueError('start must precede end')
    window = pm4py.filter_time_range(log.copy(deep=True), left.isoformat(),
                                     right.isoformat(), mode=mode)
    if mode == 'events':
        stamp = window['time:timestamp']
        window = window.loc[(stamp >= left) & (stamp < right)].copy()
    # Trace modes preserve PM4Py semantics. Containment excludes long spanning cases.
    return window


def registration_cohort(log, start, end):
    """Select by Register date, retain every available event for those case IDs."""
    registration = log.loc[log['concept:name'].eq('Register')].copy()
    counts = registration.groupby('case:concept:name').size()
    if (counts > 1).any():
        warnings.warn('Repeated Register events: investigate before interpreting cohorts')
    all_ids = set(log['case:concept:name'])
    if set(counts.index) != all_ids:
        raise ValueError('Some cases lack Register; define an audited entry rule')
    first_registration = (registration.sort_values('time:timestamp', kind='mergesort')
                          .drop_duplicates('case:concept:name', keep='first'))
    selected = slice_window(first_registration, start, end, mode='events')
    ids = selected['case:concept:name'].unique()
    return log.loc[log['case:concept:name'].isin(ids)].copy()


def process_artifacts(log, include_skeleton=False, include_declare=False):
    import pm4py
    if log.empty:
        return {'n_cases': 0, 'status': 'empty window'}
    dfg, starts, ends = pm4py.discover_dfg(log)
    n_cases = log['case:concept:name'].nunique()
    result = {
        'n_cases': n_cases,
        'dfg_counts': dfg,
        'dfg_occurrences_per_100_cases': {edge: 100 * count / n_cases
                                          for edge, count in dfg.items()},
        'start_activities': starts,
        'end_activities': ends,
        'variants': pm4py.get_variants(log),
        # Default temporal-profile values are elapsed-time statistics in seconds.
        # They do not isolate queue waiting or active service.
        'temporal_profile': pm4py.discover_temporal_profile(log),
    }
    if include_skeleton:
        result['log_skeleton'] = pm4py.discover_log_skeleton(log, noise_threshold=0.05)
    if include_declare:
        result['declare'] = pm4py.discover_declare(log)
    return result


def monthly_artifacts(log, months, population='registration', min_cases=100,
                      include_skeleton=False, include_declare=False):
    """months: iterable such as ['2026-01', '2026-02']; log is formatted already."""
    results = {}
    for month in months:
        period = pd.Period(month, freq='M')
        left = period.start_time.tz_localize('UTC')
        right = (period + 1).start_time.tz_localize('UTC')
        if population == 'registration':
            window = registration_cohort(log, left, right)
        elif population == 'events':
            # Calendar-event DFGs can truncate paths; keep distinct from cohort DFGs.
            window = slice_window(log, left, right, mode='events')
        else:
            raise ValueError('Choose registration or events population')
        n_cases = window['case:concept:name'].nunique()
        if n_cases < min_cases:
            results[str(period)] = {'n_cases': n_cases, 'status': 'insufficient support'}
            continue
        result = process_artifacts(window, include_skeleton, include_declare)
        result['population'] = population
        result['observed_close_fraction'] = (
            window.loc[window['concept:name'].eq('Close'), 'case:concept:name'].nunique()
            / n_cases)
        # This is a censoring diagnostic, not a conversion estimate. Retain open cases.
        results[str(period)] = result
    return results


def compare_raw_and_normalized(df, months, verified_duplicate_col=None,
                               case_col='case_id', activity_col='activity',
                               timestamp_col='timestamp', sequence_col=None,
                               population='registration', min_cases=100):
    """The caller's original dataframe is never overwritten."""
    raw = format_event_log(df, case_col, activity_col, timestamp_col, sequence_col)
    mapped_source = normalize_copy(df, activity_col, verified_duplicate_col)
    normalized = format_event_log(mapped_source, case_col, activity_col,
                                  timestamp_col, sequence_col)
    months = list(months)
    raw_results = monthly_artifacts(raw, months, population, min_cases)
    normalized_results = monthly_artifacts(normalized, months, population, min_cases)
    # raw/normalized artifacts remain separate for audit and label-version checks.
    return {'raw_log': raw, 'normalized_log': normalized,
            'raw_monthly': raw_results, 'normalized_monthly': normalized_results}

## 9. Provisional attribution

The strongest conclusion is that the March raw-fitness collapse is predominantly a logging-semantic/provenance discontinuity, with high confidence. Mapping and technical-event correction restore fitness from 0.55 to 0.90, normalized adjacency barely changes, and cycle time remains near baseline. Missing identifiers constitute a separate, well-supported measurement problem for organizational analysis. Neither result establishes unchanged eligibility semantics or perfect event coverage.

There is also strong evidence of an observed rework-frequency shift after May: normalized requests and returns to document collection increase together. Supplier disruption is the leading mechanism with moderately high confidence, supported by timing and September’s partial reversal. Attribution remains conditional because Z’s exposure rises sharply and its customers were already different. The aggregates cannot distinguish more problematic Z cases from worsening document quality within comparable Z cases, or quantify either contribution.

Capacity loss is a moderately well-supported explanation for additional queueing after mid-June. Small changes in recorded assessment service contrast with much larger waiting and tail increases. However, supplier-generated revisits consume the same reduced capacity, so supplier and staffing effects interact and cannot be cleanly added. Priority-induced redistribution is plausible and the policy change is known, but its effect on each class’s waiting remains unidentified.

October–November clearly exhibit population and performance drift. Renewal season is a credible contributor; actual annual recurrence has only contextual support because earlier-year observations are absent. Increased senior routing may combine greater complexity, changed referral practice and more consistent recording. Persistent behavioral adaptation is possible but currently has low confidence: December still differs in capacity, mix and backlog.

December’s improvement supports partial recovery, with moderate confidence in a combined load, quality and capacity explanation. Separate effects of overtime and the quality campaign are not identifiable. I would revise this account if comparable Z cases show no defect/rework increase, queues fail to track effective capacity, class-specific waits contradict priority redistribution, or timestamp/coverage repairs erase apparent operational changes. Stable-mix fresh cohorts after backlog clearance, verified specialist work records, and comparable prior renewal seasons would most directly resolve the remaining attribution disputes.

## 10. Monitoring decision rules

1. IF raw fitness falls while normalized artifacts and verified durations remain stable, or identifier completeness deteriorates without corroborating staffing changes, THEN audit label versions, verified duplicates and resource capture before attributing operational failure, BECAUSE semantic and provenance changes can distort conformance and organizational measures independently of business execution.
2. IF comparable Z cases show increased bundle defects and normalized information-request loops after May, with weaker changes among overlapping non-Z cases, THEN prioritize supplier-quality remediation and estimate conditional pre/post contrasts with pre-trend and spillover checks, BECAUSE defective documents plausibly generate repeated collection and assessment while supplier selection confounds naive comparisons.
3. IF assessment-ready arrivals exceed completions, backlog age grows and waiting rises while per-visit service remains broadly stable after capacity loss, THEN reconcile staffed hours and revisit demand and implement a capacity/backlog response, BECAUSE reduced effective capacity and rework demand jointly increase queueing.
4. IF post-July high-complexity waiting improves relative to standard waiting while overtaking and standard-case aging increase, THEN review priority allocation and introduce an aging safeguard subject to service objectives, BECAUSE queue priority can redistribute delay and leave an acceptable aggregate concealing excessive standard-case waits.
5. IF aggregate autumn routing and duration changes largely disappear after entry-mix standardization and align with validated prior renewal phases, THEN classify the explainable component as recurring seasonal population pressure and activate seasonal capacity planning, BECAUSE predictable composition and demand shifts can reproduce aggregate drift without changed within-class routing.
6. IF normalized variants, senior referrals or precedence constraints remain changed within comparable fresh cohorts after source validation, supplier-quality recovery and backlog clearance, THEN investigate persistent operating practice and reconcile the normative model with verified business policy, BECAUSE conditional persistence would weaken explanations based solely on temporary mix, backlog or logging artifacts.
7. IF December-style improvements coincide with lower load and multiple interventions, THEN track fresh and backlog cohorts, actual staffed hours, defect rates and decision outcomes separately and retain the previous reference until recovery is sustained, BECAUSE coincident changes prevent isolated intervention attribution and faster completion alone does not establish restored process quality.
