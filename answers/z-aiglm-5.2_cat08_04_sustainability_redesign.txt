### 3. Intervention options

1. **S4. Replace air for Standard-stock Service-contract with priority pick + ground**
- **Target path:** Standard-stock Service-contract -> standard pack -> air.
- **Mechanism:** Uses `promised_lead_time_hours` to calculate slack. If ground transit meets the deadline with >85% probability, switch `route_mode` to `parcel_ground` and expedite the picking phase. Cuts `transport_kgco2e` by 4-6x.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Maintains service levels by saving time in the upstream pick/pack phases. Preserves resilience by keeping air as a fallback for tight-slack cases.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** `route_mode` vs. `promised_lead_time_hours`, `Late delivery` rate, SP1 conformance.

2. **S9. Split Critical-downtime into true downtime and precautionary urgent categories**
- **Target path:** Critical-downtime Service-contract -> emergency production -> dedicated van.
- **Mechanism:** Differentiates genuinely line-down machines from precautionary urgent orders. Routes precautionary orders via `parcel_air` or `consolidated_truck` instead of `dedicated_van` and `Emergency production`.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Protects extreme service for true downtime while moderately relaxing cycle time (e.g., from 14h to 23h) for precautionary parts. Reduces empty-return van emissions.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** `case_type` reclassification, `transport_kgco2e`, `Late delivery`, SP2 override logs.

3. **S2. Dynamic batching for CNC and heat treatment with maximum waiting-time caps**
- **Target path:** MTO -> Plan production -> Batch wait -> CNC -> heat treat.
- **Mechanism:** Balances the `batch_size` energy efficiency curve (1.3 kWh at size 12-20 vs 2.8 kWh at 1-3) with waiting time. Sets strict caps on `Batch wait` based on `promised_lead_time_hours` to prevent the 1.7x late risk.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Reduces energy consumption without inflating p90 cycle time or obsolescence risk.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** `batch_size`, `energy_kwh`, `Batch wait` duration, p90 cycle time.

4. **S3. Shift Made-to-order production from CNC_B to CNC_A when due-date slack allows**
- **Target path:** MTO -> CNC_B -> inspection -> rework.
- **Mechanism:** CNC_B has a higher rework rate (18% vs 9%). Shifting to CNC_A reduces `material_scrap_kg` and rework loop energy.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Increases quality but may increase cycle time if CNC_A queues are long. Requires a queue-check rule.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** `machine_group`, `rework_flag`, `material_scrap_kg`, queue times.

5. **S7. Add inspection quality gate for high-rework machine/part combinations**
- **Target path:** CNC production -> Inspection -> Rework production.
- **Mechanism:** Rushed inspections miss defects, causing downstream packaging/customer returns. Adding a strict gate for high-risk parts prevents scrap-heavy rework loops.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Adds minor cycle time at inspection but drastically reduces `material_scrap_kg` and overall p90 cycle time by avoiding rework.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** `rework_flag` after Inspection, `material_scrap_kg`, Q1 violation rates.

6. **S6. Improve packaging-route compatibility checks before shipment**
- **Target path:** Package standard/reinforced -> Rework packaging.
- **Mechanism:** Incompatible packaging choice for `route_mode` or part geometry causes rework loops. A pre-shipment check prevents this, saving `package_material_kg` and handling energy.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Prevents delays caused by repacking. Neutral to positive for resilience.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** Frequency of `Rework packaging` event, `route_mode` combinations.

7. **S5. Consolidate truck departures twice daily for non-urgent stock orders**
- **Target path:** Stock -> standard pack -> Ship ground.
- **Mechanism:** Shifts individual ground parcels to `consolidated_truck`, reducing `transport_kgco2e` by 35-55%.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Adds a 12-24h consolidation wait. Only applicable for Standard-stock Regular orders with adequate slack.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** `route_mode` usage, median cycle time shift, `promised_lead_time_hours`.

8. **S11. Reduce partial shipments by changing availability-check rules**
- **Target path:** Pick from stock -> Partial shipment -> Backorder wait.
- **Mechanism:** Partial shipments double `transport_kgco2e` and extend cycle times. Holding orders briefly until full stock is available (or substituting production) prevents the second shipment.
- **Service/quality/resilience effect:** Delays initial confirmation but improves full-order delivery time and reduces customer contact.
- **Process-mining evidence to monitor:** `Partial shipment` frequency, `transport_kgco2e`, `Late delivery`.

### 5. Rebound and displacement risks
1. **Batching Obsolescence Rebound:** Increasing `batch_size` to 12-20 saves `energy_kwh` but creates a 6% obsolescence rate. The emissions saved in production are displaced by the material waste and embedded emissions of unshipped, obsolete parts.
2. **Transport Displacement:** Switching Standard-stock Service-contract orders to `consolidated_truck` (S5) adds a 12-24h wait. If this pushes an order past `promised_lead_time_hours`, it may trigger a last-minute `parcel_air` expedite or `Emergency production`, multiplying the emissions.
3. **Packaging Material Rebound:** Reinforced packaging reduces damage-related returns by 40%, but it doubles `package_material_kg`. If Q1 is applied too broadly to low-risk parts, the packaging material waste outweighs the emissions saved from avoided returns.
4. **Machine Routing Displacement:** Moving production from CNC_B to CNC_A (S3) reduces `rework_flag` rates but CNC_A is slower. If CNC_A queues grow, `Batch wait` increases, forcing air transport to meet lead times—trading material scrap for transport emissions.
5. **Customer Behavior Rebound:** If "green" shipping routes result in inconsistent delivery times, Regular Made-to-order customers may begin purchasing Service-contract status or defaulting to demanding `parcel_air` for all future orders, increasing overall emissions.
6. **Partial Shipment Displacement:** Reducing partial shipments by holding orders for full availability reduces transport emissions but may increase inventory holding times. If holding parts causes them to become obsolete or if it requires emergency expediting later, emissions shift from transport to material waste or air freight.

### 6. Balanced recommendation
I recommend a phased redesign package that integrates **S4**, **S9**, **S2**, **S7**, and **S6**. 
- **Phase 1 (Transport Optimization):** Implement S4 for Standard-stock Service-contract orders and S9 for Critical-downtime cases. This segments transport based on true urgency. By using `promised_lead_time_hours` to calculate slack, we shift high-emission air and dedicated van routes to ground/consolidated only where service levels remain protected (>85% probability). This directly addresses SP1 and SP2 violations without sacrificing resilience (keeping air as a fallback).
- **Phase 2 (Production & Quality Alignment):** Implement S2 (dynamic batching) and S7 (inspection gate). By capping `Batch wait` based on due-date slack, we capture CNC_A energy efficiencies without triggering the 1.7x late-delivery risk or the 6% obsolescence rate. Adding a strict inspection gate catches defects before they enter the costly rework loop, dramatically reducing `material_scrap_kg` and `energy_kwh`.
- **Phase 3 (Packaging Compatibility):** Implement S6 to eliminate the Rework packaging loops. This ensures that `route_mode` and part geometry dictate `package_material_kg` upfront, avoiding double material usage and rehandling.

This package balances environmental impact by targeting the highest polluting paths (air, vans, rework) while explicitly using slack variables to protect p90 cycle times and `Late delivery` rates for Service-contract and Critical-downtime customers.
