# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Demand and service assumptions

- **Arrivals** are treated as Poisson within each two-hour block, with mean equal to the expected arrivals in the demand table. Scenario multipliers are applied to the block means before drawing.
- **Service times** are drawn from a two-component mixture:
  - with probability `1 - p_complex`, draw uniformly from the ordinary range;
  - with probability `p_complex`, draw uniformly from the complex-tail range.
  - The expected base service time is therefore  
    `E[S] = (1-p)*mean(ordinary) + p*mean(complex)`.
- **Resource multipliers** scale the base draw: `actual_service = base_draw * multiplier`.
- **Shifts and breaks** are hard unavailability windows. Overtime is modeled as an optional 30-minute extension per resource per day.
- **Handover**: 7 minutes are added whenever Intake, a diagnostic activity, or Validation changes resource.
- **Team-switch penalty**: moving between the mechanical team and the electrical/software team adds a further 5 minutes unless the same cross-skilled resource retains the case.
- **Setup**: a resource switching diagnostic skill family between consecutive cases adds 5 minutes.
- **Parallel ME/ES**: parallel branches add 8 minutes of coordination before Validation. Sequential execution avoids that 8 minutes but lengthens the critical path.
- **Preemption**: a P1 case may preempt a lower-priority diagnostic activity. The interrupted activity incurs a restart penalty equal to 10% of its remaining active service time. A case may be preempted at most once.

## How ranges become distributions

For each activity, the ordinary and complex-tail ranges are treated as uniform distributions. The complex-tail probability is used as a mixture weight. This is an assumption; the prompt supplies only ranges and probabilities, not full distributions.

Expected base service times under this assumption:

| activity | expected base minutes |
|---|---:|
| Intake | 6.94 |
| Mechanical | 26.30 |
| Electrical | 32.82 |
| Software | 38.50 |
| Validation | 14.74 |

Under U4, diagnostic complex-tail probabilities double, giving:

| diagnostic activity | U4 expected base minutes |
|---|---:|
| Mechanical | 29.60 |
| Electrical | 37.14 |
| Software | 44.50 |

## ME/ES parallelism decision

Parallelism is a decision variable. A case type ME or ES may be processed:

- **Sequentially**: one branch after the other, possibly on the same cross-skilled resource or on two resources with handover/team-switch penalties.
- **In parallel**: both branches run concurrently on different resources, followed by 8 minutes of coordination before Validation.

The choice depends on policy, priority, resource availability, and expected critical path.

## Objectives

**Objective 1 — SLA/tail-risk objective**  
Maximize weighted SLA attainment:

`0.5 * P1_SLA + 0.3 * P2_SLA + 0.2 * P3_SLA`

subject to:

- `max_resource_utilization <= 0.90`
- `p90_delay <= 600` minutes
- no resource planned above 90% utilization in the base scenario

**Objective 2 — robustness/fairness objective**  
Maximize resilience across U1–U5:

`resilience = 1 - mean relative drop in weighted SLA across U1–U5`

subject to:

- utilization fairness spread `max_util - min_util <= 0.20`
- P3 SLA at least 0.80 in the base scenario
- no systematic P3 starvation

## Decision variables

- eligibility set per activity and priority;
- priority score;
- reservation rule for P1 capacity;
- overflow trigger threshold;
- parallelization threshold for ME/ES;
- preemption rule;
- handover/setup minimization rule.

# 2. Capacity and offered-load analysis

## Offered-load method

For each block and skill:

`offered_hours = sum over case types (expected_cases * expected_service_minutes) / 60`

where ME contributes to Mechanical and Electrical, and ES contributes to Electrical and Software.

## Capacity method

For each block and skill:

`capacity_base_hours = sum over qualified resources (available_minutes_in_block / multiplier)`

Available minutes account for shifts, breaks, overtime, Chen absence in U3/U5, and the U5 Intake outage.

# 3. Candidate policies

## Policy A — Strict specialization with fixed queues

**Eligibility**

- Intake: fixed order Ada, Deepa, Farah, Ben, Chen, Emil.
- Mechanical: Ada, Ben, Farah.
- Electrical: Chen, Deepa, Ada, Emil, Farah.
- Software: Emil, Chen, Farah.
- Validation: Ben, Deepa, Emil, Ada, Chen, Farah.

**Priority score**  
P1 > P2 > P3 within each fixed queue. No P2/P3 cross-queue overflow.

**Preemption**  
P1 may preempt a lower-priority diagnostic task only on a resource in that task’s fixed eligible set.

**Parallelization**  
ME/ES are sequential. Prefer the same cross-skilled resource when possible; otherwise hand over.

**Handover minimization**  
Assign to the same resource when possible; no dynamic reassignment.

**Overflow trigger**  
None for P2/P3. P1 may use any resource in its fixed set.

## Policy B — Full pooling with SERPT

**Eligibility**  
Any resource with a non-null multiplier for the required skill.

**Priority score**  
P1 first. Within the same priority, choose the resource minimizing:

`expected_finish = current_time + handover + setup + remaining_service * multiplier`

This is a shortest-expected-remaining-processing-time rule.

**Preemption**  
P1 diagnostic tasks may preempt lower-priority diagnostic tasks on any eligible resource.

**Parallelization**  
ME/ES run in parallel if two distinct eligible resources can start both branches within 10 minutes and the case is P1 or P2; otherwise sequential.

**Handover minimization**  
SERPT naturally penalizes resource changes through the 7-minute handover term.

**Overflow trigger**  
Implicit: pooling is the overflow mechanism.

## Policy C — Hybrid: reserved P1 capacity + controlled cross-skill overflow

**Eligibility**

- P1: all qualified resources.
- P2/P3 primary lanes:
  - Mechanical: Ada, Ben; overflow Farah after 20 minutes wait.
  - Electrical: Chen, Deepa; overflow Ada, Emil, Farah after 20 minutes wait.
  - Software: Emil, Chen; overflow Farah after 20 minutes wait.
  - Intake: Ada, Deepa, Farah; overflow Ben, Chen, Emil after 20 minutes wait.
  - Validation: Ben, Deepa, Emil; overflow Ada, Chen, Farah after 20 minutes wait.

**Priority score**  
P1 preemptive; P2 before P3. P3 Software is deferred if any P1/P2 Software task is waiting.

**Preemption**  
P1 diagnostic tasks may preempt lower-priority diagnostic tasks on any qualified resource.

**Parallelization**  
ME/ES run in parallel for P1/P2 only if both branches can start within 15 minutes on distinct resources and the expected sequential critical path exceeds 60 minutes; otherwise sequential.

**Handover minimization**  
Primary lanes reduce handover; overflow is used only after a 20-minute wait threshold.

**Overflow trigger**  
Wait > 20 minutes, or primary resource unavailable.

# 5. Robust recommendation

## Primary recommendation: Hybrid policy

The hybrid policy is the best trade-off under the robustness/fairness objective. It:

- protects P1 cases through preemption and reserved P1 access to all qualified resources;
- keeps P2/P3 work in specialist lanes, reducing handover and setup;
- uses controlled overflow only after a 20-minute wait, preventing bottleneck migration without destroying specialization;
- parallelizes ME/ES only when it shortens the critical path enough to justify the 8-minute coordination penalty;
- preserves interpretability: operators can see primary lanes, overflow triggers, and P1 reservation rules.

## Fallback mode

If any of the following triggers occur, switch to a **surge mode**:

- P1 queue length > 5 cases for more than 15 minutes;
- Software waiting time p90 > 30 minutes;
- any resource utilization > 0.95 for more than 30 minutes;
- Chen absence or equivalent skill loss is detected.

In surge mode:

- P1 and P2 use full pooling;
- P3 Software is deferred until P1/P2 Software queues are below 2 cases;
- ME/ES parallelization threshold is relaxed from 15 to 10 minutes;
- overtime is activated for the most loaded qualified resource.

Switch back to hybrid when:

- P1 queue < 2 for 30 minutes;
- Software p90 wait < 15 minutes;
- utilization < 0.90 for 30 minutes.

## When another policy is preferable

- **Strict specialization** is preferable when auditability, training simplicity, and low handover burden are more important than tail SLA, and demand is stable.
- **Full pooling** is preferable when cross-training is high, demand is highly volatile, and the organization accepts more handover/setup overhead to reduce queueing.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates**: if service-time estimates are used for dispatch, staff may inflate estimates to avoid overflow. Use actual service-time distributions and audit planned vs actual service.
- **P3 starvation**: hybrid reservation can starve P3 Software. Mitigate with a P3 SLA floor and a maximum deferment time.
- **Skill erosion**: strict specialization can reduce cross-skill practice. Rotate overflow assignments and track skill-erosion indicators.
- **Excessive preemption**: P1 preemption can create restart penalties and lower-priority resentment. Limit preemption to once per case and log every preemption.
- **Coordination overhead**: parallel ME/ES adds 8 minutes before Validation. Use parallelization only when the critical path benefit exceeds the coordination cost.
- **Validation bottleneck**: Validation is not a base bottleneck, but under U2/U5 it can become one if diagnostic work is accelerated. Monitor Validation queue separately.
- **Fairness**: utilization spread above 0.20 is unfair. Hybrid overflow should be rotated, not always assigned to the same secondary resource.
- **Human interpretability**: operators must understand why a case was assigned. Publish the priority score, eligible set, and overflow trigger in the dispatch log.

def expected_service(activity, long_tail=False):
    r = SERVICE_RANGES[activity]
    p = r['p_complex']
    if long_tail and activity in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
        p = min(1.0, p * 2)
    ord_mean = (r['ord'][0] + r['ord'][1]) / 2
    comp_mean = (r['complex'][0] + r['complex'][1]) / 2
    return (1 - p) * ord_mean + p * comp_mean


def draw_service(activity, rng, long_tail=False):
    r = SERVICE_RANGES[activity]
    p = r['p_complex']
    if long_tail and activity in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
        p = min(1.0, p * 2)
    if rng.random() < p:
        return math.ceil(rng.uniform(*r['complex']))
    return math.ceil(rng.uniform(*r['ord']))


def get_priority_probs(block_idx, scenario):
    b = DEMAND[block_idx]
    p1 = b['P1_pct'] / 100
    p2 = b['P2_pct'] / 100
    p3 = b['P3_pct'] / 100
    if scenario in ['U1', 'U5'] and block_idx in [0, 1]:
        p1 += 0.08
        rem = 1 - p1
        ratio = p2 / (p2 + p3)
        p2 = rem * ratio
        p3 = rem * (1 - ratio)
    return p1, p2, p3


def get_type_probs(block_idx, scenario):
    b = DEMAND[block_idx]
    probs = {k: b[k + '_pct'] / 100 for k in ['M', 'E', 'S', 'ME', 'ES']}
    if scenario == 'U2' and block_idx in [2, 3]:
        probs['E'] *= 2
        probs['ES'] *= 2
        s = sum(probs.values())
        probs = {k: v / s for k, v in probs.items()}
    return probs


def generate_arrivals(scenario, rng):
    arrivals = []
    for block_idx, b in enumerate(DEMAND):
        n_expected = b['expected_arrivals']
        if scenario in ['U1', 'U5'] and block_idx in [0, 1]:
            n_expected *= 1.7
        n = rng.poisson(n_expected)
        start = block_idx * 120
        end = start + 120
        for _ in range(n):
            t = int(math.floor(rng.uniform(start, end)))
            p1, p2, p3 = get_priority_probs(block_idx, scenario)
            r = rng.random()
            if r < p1:
                priority = 'P1'
            elif r < p1 + p2:
                priority = 'P2'
            else:
                priority = 'P3'
            probs = get_type_probs(block_idx, scenario)
            r = rng.random()
            cum = 0.0
            ctype = 'M'
            for k, v in probs.items():
                cum += v
                if r <= cum:
                    ctype = k
                    break
            arrivals.append((t, priority, ctype))
    arrivals.sort()
    return arrivals


def build_availability(resource, scenario, max_time):
    if scenario in ['U3', 'U5'] and resource == 'Chen':
        return np.zeros(max_time + 1, dtype=bool)
    intervals = []
    for day in range(2):
        offset = day * 1440
        s = SHIFTS[resource]['start'] + offset
        e = SHIFTS[resource]['end'] + offset
        brk = BREAKS[resource]
        if brk:
            bs = brk['start'] + offset
            be = brk['end'] + offset
            intervals.append((s, bs))
            intervals.append((be, e))
        else:
            intervals.append((s, e))
        intervals.append((e, e + 30))  # overtime
    avail = np.zeros(max_time + 1, dtype=bool)
    for s, e in intervals:
        s = max(s, 0)
        e = min(e, max_time + 1)
        if e > s:
            avail[s:e] = True
    return avail

        self.activity = activity
        self.skill = skill
        self.skill_family = skill_family
        self.priority = priority
        self.base_service = base_service
        self.multiplier = multiplier
        self.actual_service = base_service * multiplier
        self.remaining_pre = 0
        self.remaining_service = self.actual_service
        self.restart_penalty = 0
        self.status = 'waiting'
        self.wait_start = None
        self.ready_time = 0
        self.start_time = None
        self.completion_time = None
        self.resource = None
        self.handover_min = 0
        self.setup_min = 0
        self.team_switch_min = 0
        self.was_preempted = False
        self.eligible_resources_str = ''


class Case:
    def __init__(self, case_id, arrival, priority, ctype):
        self.id = case_id
        self.arrival = arrival
        self.priority = priority
        self.type = ctype
        self.tasks = []
        self.completed = []
        self.last_resource = None
        self.last_diag_resource = None
        self.last_diag_skill = None
        self.validation_ready_time = None
        self.completion_time = None
        self.preempt_count = 0
        self.parallel_branches = None
        self.sequential_skills = None
        self.next_sequential_index = 0


class Simulation:
    def __init__(self, policy, scenario, seed):
        self.policy = policy
        self.scenario = scenario
        self.rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
        self.resources = {name: Resource(name, build_availability(name, scenario, MAX_TIME))
                          for name in SKILL_MATRIX}
        self.arrivals = generate_arrivals(scenario, self.rng)
        self.last_arrival_time = max([a[0] for a in self.arrivals]) if self.arrivals else 0
        self.cases = {}
        self.waiting_tasks = []
        self.log_rows = []
        self.current_time = 0
        self.case_counter = 0
        self.task_counter = 0
        self.total_handover_setup = 0
        self.completed_cases = []

    def is_available(self, resource, t, activity):
        if t < 0 or t > MAX_TIME:
            return False
        if not resource.avail[t]:
            return False
        if self.scenario == 'U5' and activity == 'Intake' and 90 <= t < 120:
            return False
        return True

    def eligible_resources(self, task):
        if task.activity in ['Intake', 'Validation']:
            elig = [r for r in self.resources.values() if task.activity in r.multipliers]
        else:
            elig = [r for r in self.resources.values() if task.skill in r.multipliers]

        if self.policy == 'strict':
            if task.activity == 'Mechanical':
                order = ['Ada', 'Ben', 'Farah']
            elif task.activity == 'Electrical':
                order = ['Chen', 'Deepa', 'Ada', 'Emil', 'Farah']
            elif task.activity == 'Software':
                order = ['Emil', 'Chen', 'Farah']
            elif task.activity == 'Intake':
                order = ['Ada', 'Deepa', 'Farah', 'Ben', 'Chen', 'Emil']
            else:
                order = ['Ben', 'Deepa', 'Emil', 'Ada', 'Chen', 'Farah']
            elig = [r for r in elig if r.name in order]
            elig.sort(key=lambda r: order.index(r.name))
        elif self.policy == 'hybrid':
            if task.priority == 'P1':
                pass
            else:
                if task.activity == 'Mechanical':
                    primary = ['Ada', 'Ben']; secondary = ['Farah']
                elif task.activity == 'Electrical':
                    primary = ['Chen', 'Deepa']; secondary = ['Ada', 'Emil', 'Farah']
                elif task.activity == 'Software':
                    primary = ['Emil', 'Chen']; secondary = ['Farah']
                elif task.activity == 'Intake':
                    primary = ['Ada', 'Deepa', 'Farah']; secondary = ['Ben', 'Chen', 'Emil']
                else:
                    primary = ['Ben', 'Deepa', 'Emil']; secondary = ['Ada', 'Chen', 'Farah']
                if self.current_time - task.wait_start > 20:
                    allowed = primary + secondary
                else:
                    allowed = primary
                elig = [r for r in elig if r.name in allowed]
                order = primary + secondary
                elig.sort(key=lambda r: order.index(r.name) if r.name in order else 99)
        return elig

    def add_diag_task(self, case, activity, skill):
        base = draw_service(activity, self.rng, long_tail=(self.scenario == 'U4'))
        task = Task(self.task_counter, case.id, activity, skill, skill, case.priority, base, 1.0)
        self.task_counter += 1
        task.wait_start = self.current_time + 1
        task.ready_time = self.current_time + 1
        case.tasks.append(task)
        self.waiting_tasks.append(task)

    def can_parallel(self, case, skills, threshold=10):
        available_by_skill = {}
        for skill in skills:
            elig = [r for r in self.resources.values() if skill in r.multipliers]
            if self.policy == 'hybrid' and case.priority != 'P1':
                if skill == 'Mechanical':
                    primary = ['Ada', 'Ben']
                elif skill == 'Electrical':
                    primary = ['Chen', 'Deepa']
                else:
                    primary = ['Emil', 'Chen']
                elig = [r for r in elig if r.name in primary]
            found = []
            for r in elig:
                for dt in range(0, threshold + 1):
                    if r.current_task is None and self.is_available(r, self.current_time + dt, skill):
                        found.append(r.name)
                        break
            available_by_skill[skill] = set(found)
            if not found:
                return False
        # need at least two distinct resources across the two skills
        all_res = set()
        for s in available_by_skill.values():
            all_res |= s
        return len(all_res) >= 2

    def create_diagnostic_tasks(self, case):
        ctype = case.type
        if ctype == 'M':
            self.add_diag_task(case, 'Mechanical', 'Mechanical')
        elif ctype == 'E':
            self.add_diag_task(case, 'Electrical', 'Electrical')
        elif ctype == 'S':
            self.add_diag_task(case, 'Software', 'Software')
        elif ctype in ['ME', 'ES']:
            skills = ['Mechanical', 'Electrical'] if ctype == 'ME' else ['Electrical', 'Software']
            parallel = False
            if self.policy == 'pooled':
                if case.priority in ['P1', 'P2']:
                    parallel = self.can_parallel(case, skills, threshold=10)
            elif self.policy == 'hybrid':
                if case.priority in ['P1', 'P2']:
                    seq_cp = sum(expected_service(s, self.scenario == 'U4') for s in skills)
                    if seq_cp > 60 and self.can_parallel(case, skills, threshold=15):
                        parallel = True
            if parallel:
                for skill in skills:
                    self.add_diag_task(case, skill, skill)
                case.parallel_branches = skills
            else:
                self.add_diag_task(case, skills[0], skills[0])
                case.sequential_skills = skills
                case.next_sequential_index = 1

    def assign_task(self, task, resource, added, handover, setup, team_switch):
        task.status = 'running'
        task.resource = resource.name
        task.start_time = self.current_time
        task.remaining_pre = added
        if task.was_preempted:
            task.remaining_service += task.restart_penalty
        else:
            task.remaining_service = task.actual_service
        task.handover_min = handover
        task.setup_min = setup
        task.team_switch_min = team_switch
        task.eligible_resources_str = ','.join([r.name for r in self.eligible_resources(task)])
        resource.current_task = task
        case = self.cases[task.case_id]
        case.last_resource = resource.name
        if task.activity in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
            case.last_diag_resource = resource.name
            case.last_diag_skill = task.skill_family
            resource.last_diag_skill = task.skill_family
            resource.last_diag_case_id = case.id
        self.total_handover_setup += added

    def dispatch(self):
        priority_order = {'P1': 0, 'P2': 1, 'P3': 2}
        self.waiting_tasks.sort(key=lambda t: (priority_order[t.priority], t.wait_start, t.id))
        for task in list(self.waiting_tasks):
            if task.ready_time > self.current_time:
                continue
            if self.policy == 'hybrid' and task.priority == 'P3' and task.skill == 'Software':
                if any(t.priority in ['P1', 'P2'] and t.skill == 'Software' and t.status == 'waiting'
                       for t in self.waiting_tasks):
                    continue
            elig = self.eligible_resources(task)
            if not elig:
                continue
            best = None
            best_finish = None
            best_added = best_handover = best_setup = best_team = 0
            for r in elig:
                if r.current_task is not None:
                    continue
                if not self.is_available(r, self.current_time, task.activity):
                    continue
                case = self.cases[task.case_id]
                handover = 0
                if case.last_resource is not None and case.last_resource != r.name:
                    handover += 7
                team_switch = 0
                if task.activity in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
                    if (case.last_diag_skill is not None and case.last_diag_skill != task.skill_family
                            and case.last_diag_resource != r.name):
                        team_switch += 5
                setup = 0
                if task.activity in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
                    if (r.last_diag_case_id != case.id and r.last_diag_skill is not None
                            and r.last_diag_skill != task.skill_family):
                        setup += 5
                added = handover + team_switch + setup
                finish = self.current_time + added + task.remaining_service
                if best is None or finish < best_finish:
                    best = r
                    best_finish = finish
                    best_added = added
                    best_handover = handover
                    best_setup = setup
                    best_team = team_switch
            if best is not None:
                self.assign_task(task, best, best_added, best_handover, best_setup, best_team)
                self.waiting_tasks.remove(task)

    def preempt(self):
        for task in list(self.waiting_tasks):
            if task.priority != 'P1' or task.activity not in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
                continue
            if task.ready_time > self.current_time:
                continue
            elig = self.eligible_resources(task)
            if any(r.current_task is None and self.is_available(r, self.current_time, task.activity)
                   for r in elig):
                continue
            candidates = []
            for r in elig:
                if r.current_task is not None and r.current_task.status == 'running':
                    rt = r.current_task
                    if rt.priority in ['P2', 'P3'] and rt.activity in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
                        case = self.cases[rt.case_id]
                        if case.preempt_count < 1:
                            candidates.append((r, rt))
            if candidates:
                r, rt = max(candidates, key=lambda x: x[1].remaining_service)
                case = self.cases[rt.case_id]
                rt.status = 'paused'
                rt.was_preempted = True
                case.preempt_count += 1
                rt.restart_penalty = math.ceil(0.1 * rt.remaining_service)
                r.current_task = None
                rt.resource = None
                rt.wait_start = self.current_time
                rt.ready_time = self.current_time
                self.waiting_tasks.append(rt)

                pcase = self.cases[task.case_id]
                handover = 0
                if pcase.last_resource is not None and pcase.last_resource != r.name:
                    handover += 7
                team_switch = 0
                if (pcase.last_diag_skill is not None and pcase.last_diag_skill != task.skill_family
                        and pcase.last_diag_resource != r.name):
                    team_switch += 5
                setup = 0
                if (r.last_diag_case_id != pcase.id and r.last_diag_skill is not None
                        and r.last_diag_skill != task.skill_family):
                    setup += 5
                added = handover + team_switch + setup
                self.assign_task(task, r, added, handover, setup, team_switch)
                self.waiting_tasks.remove(task)
                break

    def process_arrivals(self):
        while self.arrivals and self.arrivals[0][0] <= self.current_time:
            t, priority, ctype = self.arrivals.pop(0)
            case = Case(f'C{self.case_counter}', t, priority, ctype)
            self.case_counter += 1
            self.cases[case.id] = case
            base = draw_service('Intake', self.rng, long_tail=False)
            task = Task(self.task_counter, case.id, 'Intake', 'Intake', 'Intake', priority, base, 1.0)
            self.task_counter += 1
            task.wait_start = t
            task.ready_time = t
            case.tasks.append(task)
            self.waiting_tasks.append(task)

    def process_completions(self):
        for r in self.resources.values():
            if r.current_task is not None and r.current_task.status == 'running':
                task = r.current_task
                if self.is_available(r, self.current_time, task.activity):
                    r.busy_minutes += 1
                    if task.remaining_pre > 0:
                        task.remaining_pre -= 1
                    else:
                        task.remaining_service -= 1
                    if task.remaining_pre <= 0 and task.remaining_service <= 0:
                        self.complete_task(task, r)

    def complete_task(self, task, resource):
        task.status = 'completed'
        task.completion_time = self.current_time + 1
        resource.current_task = None
        case = self.cases[task.case_id]
        case.completed.append(task)
        self.log_task(task)
        if task.activity == 'Intake':
            self.create_diagnostic_tasks(case)
        elif task.activity in DIAG_ACTIVITIES:
            if case.sequential_skills and case.next_sequential_index < len(case.sequential_skills):
                next_skill = case.sequential_skills[case.next_sequential_index]
                case.next_sequential_index += 1
                self.add_diag_task(case, next_skill, next_skill)
            else:
                if case.parallel_branches:
                    case.validation_ready_time = self.current_time + 1 + 8
                else:
                    case.validation_ready_time = self.current_time + 1
                base = draw_service('Validation', self.rng, long_tail=False)
                vtask = Task(self.task_counter, case.id, 'Validation', 'Validation', 'Validation',
                             case.priority, base, 1.0)
                self.task_counter += 1
                vtask.wait_start = self.current_time + 1
                vtask.ready_time = case.validation_ready_time
                case.tasks.append(vtask)
                self.waiting_tasks.append(vtask)
        elif task.activity == 'Validation':
            case.completion_time = self.current_time + 1
            self.completed_cases.append(case)

    def log_task(self, task):
        case = self.cases[task.case_id]
        start_dt = BASE_DATE + pd.Timedelta(minutes=task.start_time)
        end_dt = BASE_DATE + pd.Timedelta(minutes=task.completion_time)
        self.log_rows.append({
            'case:concept:name': case.id,
            'concept:name': task.activity,
            'start_timestamp': start_dt,
            'time:timestamp': end_dt,
            'org:resource': task.resource,
            'policy_version': self.policy,
            'scenario': self.scenario,
            'priority': case.priority,
            'case_type': case.type,
            'planned_service_min': task.base_service,
            'actual_service_min': task.actual_service,
            'handover_min': task.handover_min,
            'setup_min': task.setup_min,
            'team_switch_min': task.team_switch_min,
            'restart_penalty_min': task.restart_penalty,
            'pause_resume': 'paused_resumed' if task.was_preempted else 'none',
            'eligible_resources': task.eligible_resources_str,
            'assignment_rule': self.policy,
            'sla_clock_min': task.completion_time - case.arrival,
            'queue_entry_min': task.wait_start,
            'waiting_min': task.start_time - task.wait_start,
            'skill_used': task.skill_family,
            'stage': task.activity,
        })

    def compute_metrics(self):
        cases = self.completed_cases
        if not cases:
            return {'mean_delay': np.nan, 'p90_delay': np.nan, 'P1_SLA': np.nan, 'P2_SLA': np.nan,
                    'P3_SLA': np.nan, 'max_util': np.nan, 'util_fairness': np.nan,
                    'handover_setup_burden': np.nan}
        delays = [c.completion_time - c.arrival for c in cases]
        targets = {'P1': 120, 'P2': 480, 'P3': 1440}
        sla = {'P1': 0, 'P2': 0, 'P3': 0}
        counts = {'P1': 0, 'P2': 0, 'P3': 0}
        for c in cases:
            counts[c.priority] += 1
            if c.completion_time - c.arrival <= targets[c.priority]:
                sla[c.priority] += 1
        for p in sla:
            sla[p] = sla[p] / counts[p] if counts[p] > 0 else np.nan
        utils = []
        for r in self.resources.values():
            if r.available_minutes > 0:
                utils.append(r.busy_minutes / r.available_minutes)
        max_util = max(utils) if utils else np.nan
        util_fairness = (max(utils) - min(utils)) if utils else np.nan
        return {
            'mean_delay': np.mean(delays),
            'p90_delay': np.percentile(delays, 90),
            'P1_SLA': sla['P1'],
            'P2_SLA': sla['P2'],
            'P3_SLA': sla['P3'],
            'max_util': max_util,
            'util_fairness': util_fairness,
            'handover_setup_burden': self.total_handover_setup / len(cases),
        }


def run_simulation(policy, scenario, seed):
    sim = Simulation(policy, scenario, seed)
    for t in range(MAX_TIME + 1):
        sim.current_time = t
        sim.process_arrivals()
        sim.preempt()
        sim.process_completions()
        sim.dispatch()
        if sim.cases and len(sim.completed_cases) == len(sim.cases) and t > sim.last_arrival_time:
            break
    return sim


def run_policy_scenario(policy, scenario, seeds=3):
    metrics = []
    logs = []
    for seed in range(seeds):
        sim = run_simulation(policy, scenario, seed)
        metrics.append(sim.compute_metrics())
        logs.append(sim.log_rows)
    df = pd.DataFrame(metrics)
    agg = df.mean(numeric_only=True).to_dict()
    agg['mean_delay_sd'] = df['mean_delay'].std()
    agg['p90_delay_sd'] = df['p90_delay'].std()
    return agg, logs


def compute_offered_load(scenario):
    rows = []
    for block_idx, b in enumerate(DEMAND):
        n = b['expected_arrivals']
        if scenario in ['U1', 'U5'] and block_idx in [0, 1]:
            n *= 1.7
        probs = get_type_probs(block_idx, scenario)
        exp = {s: 0.0 for s in ['Intake', 'Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software', 'Validation']}
        for ctype, p in probs.items():
            cases = n * p
            exp['Intake'] += cases * expected_service('Intake', scenario == 'U4')
            exp['Validation'] += cases * expected_service('Validation', scenario == 'U4')
            if ctype in ['M', 'ME']:
                exp['Mechanical'] += cases * expected_service('Mechanical', scenario == 'U4')
            if ctype in ['E', 'ME', 'ES']:
                exp['Electrical'] += cases * expected_service('Electrical', scenario == 'U4')
            if ctype in ['S', 'ES']:
                exp['Software'] += cases * expected_service('Software', scenario == 'U4')
        for skill, minutes in exp.items():
            rows.append({'block': b['block'], 'skill': skill, 'offered_hours': minutes / 60})
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)


def compute_capacity(scenario):
    rows = []
    for block_idx, b in enumerate(DEMAND):
        start = block_idx * 120
        end = start + 120
        for skill in ['Intake', 'Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software', 'Validation']:
            cap = 0.0
            for rname, mults in SKILL_MATRIX.items():
                if scenario in ['U3', 'U5'] and rname == 'Chen':
                    continue
                if skill not in mults:
                    continue
                avail = build_availability(rname, scenario, MAX_TIME)
                minutes = 0
                for t in range(start, end):
                    if t < len(avail) and avail[t]:
                        if scenario == 'U5' and skill == 'Intake' and 90 <= t < 120:
                            continue
                        minutes += 1
                cap += minutes / mults[skill]
            rows.append({'block': b['block'], 'skill': skill, 'capacity_base_hours': cap / 60})
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)


def print_capacity_tables():
    for scenario in ['base', 'U1', 'U2', 'U3', 'U4', 'U5']:
        offered = compute_offered_load(scenario)
        capacity = compute_capacity(scenario)
        merged = offered.merge(capacity, on=['block', 'skill'])
        merged['utilization'] = merged['offered_hours'] / merged['capacity_base_hours']
        print(f"\n=== Scenario {scenario}: offered load vs capacity ===")
        print(merged.to_string(index=False))


def resilience_score(df, policy):
    base = df[(df.policy == policy) & (df.scenario == 'base')].iloc[0]
    weighted_base = 0.5 * base['P1_SLA'] + 0.3 * base['P2_SLA'] + 0.2 * base['P3_SLA']
    drops = []
    for sc in ['U1', 'U2', 'U3', 'U4', 'U5']:
        row = df[(df.policy == policy) & (df.scenario == sc)].iloc[0]
        weighted = 0.5 * row['P1_SLA'] + 0.3 * row['P2_SLA'] + 0.2 * row['P3_SLA']
        if weighted_base > 0:
            drops.append((weighted_base - weighted) / weighted_base)
    return 1 - np.mean(drops) if drops else np.nan


def run_all():
    policies = ['strict', 'pooled', 'hybrid']
    scenarios = ['base', 'U1', 'U2', 'U3', 'U4', 'U5']
    results = []
    all_logs = {}
    for policy in policies:
        for scenario in scenarios:
            agg, logs = run_policy_scenario(policy, scenario, seeds=3)
            agg['policy'] = policy
            agg['scenario'] = scenario
            results.append(agg)
            all_logs[(policy, scenario)] = logs
    df = pd.DataFrame(results)
    df['resilience'] = df.apply(lambda r: resilience_score(df, r['policy']), axis=1)
    print("\n=== Policy-by-scenario comparison ===")
    cols = ['policy', 'scenario', 'mean_delay', 'p90_delay', 'P1_SLA', 'P2_SLA', 'P3_SLA',
            'max_util', 'util_fairness', 'handover_setup_burden', 'resilience']
    print(df[cols].to_string(index=False))
    return df, all_logs

if __name__ == '__main__':

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## What cannot be concluded

- Expected arrivals and service-time ranges alone cannot reveal correlations between case type, priority, and service time.
- The model assumes Poisson arrivals and uniform service-time ranges; real arrivals may be overdispersed or batchy.
- The model assumes no abandonment, no rework, no machine failures beyond the U5 Intake outage, and no skill learning or fatigue effects.
- The model assumes that handover, setup, and coordination delays are additive and deterministic.
- The model assumes that preemption is always operationally acceptable and that a case can be preempted at most once.
- The capacity analysis is deterministic; the simulation adds stochastic variability but still relies on the stated assumptions.

## Pilot/backtest design

1. **Shadow mode**: run the hybrid policy in parallel with the current process for 2-4 weeks without changing live dispatch.
2. **Protect P1**: P1 cases are always routed by the current safe process during the pilot; the candidate policy is evaluated offline.
3. **Backtest on historical data**: replay the last 3 months of cases through strict, pooled, and hybrid policies using actual arrival times, service times, and resource availability.
