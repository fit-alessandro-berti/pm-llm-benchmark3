## 2. Competing explanations and timeline

**Jan–Mar — stable reference.** Observed: AUROC 0.81–0.82, ECE ~0.023, breach 0.18–0.19, V3 breach ~0.31, parts edge 28–35 h. Everything later is judged against this.

**April — a schema/pipeline incident, not model decay.** Observed: predicted risk 0.28 vs observed 0.19, AUROC 0.67, ECE 0.110, unknown-activity 17%, missing queue 14%, 18% unmapped variants — with 100% label maturity and a normal 34 d delay. *Inference:* the outcome channel was untouched by the migration, so labels are fine; the feature/prefix construction broke (wrong diagnosis anchor, duplicates, high-risk default imputation). The inflated predictions are consistent with spurious prefix features (hypothesis about mechanism; the DQ counters are the observed evidence). *Falsifier:* full recomputation from the cleaned raw log fails to restore ~0.80 AUROC / ~0.03 ECE.

**May — recovery with residue.** Observed: AUROC 0.78, ECE 0.050, missing 3%, unknown 2%. *Inference:* patch worked for the label mapping, but the cached event-count feature was not backfilled and dedup only covers events from 20 Apr — residual contamination, not new drift.

**June — a real routing-mix change that need not imply concept drift.** Observed: V2 3452%, observed breach drops to 0.15 (maturity 100%, so this is real — V2 breaches at 0.09), prediction stuck at 0.20, ECE 0.060, AUROC 0.79. *Inference:* covariate drift only; within-variant outcomes are unchanged, so recalibration/mix-awareness suffices. *Falsifier:* stratified curves show V2 or V1 reliability degrading in June.

**July — aggregate metrics hide two offsetting errors.** Observed: predicted 0.18 vs observed 0.19 looks "calibrated", but V2-heavy mix pulls the observed rate down while V3 breach jumps to 0.46 and the parts edge hits 65 h. Maturity 98%, delay 36 d — mild censoring begins. *Inference:* early supplier-driven concept/process drift masked by mix offset. *Falsifier:* V3 matured breach, covariate-matched, shows no elevation vs Jan–Jun.

**August — unambiguous genuine drift.** Observed: V3 matured breach 0.64, edge 130 h, observed 0.28 vs predicted 0.19, AUROC 0.68, ECE 0.120 — **with zero Partner Z**, so population shift is ruled out as the driver. Maturity 93% means the true rate is, if anything, *understated* (the missing 7% are slow, long-path cases). *Inference:* p(y|V3-path) changed because supplier lead times tripled; the model has no feature that sees this.

**September — overlapping layers.** Observed: V3 42%, edge 148 h, observed 0.31 vs predicted 0.21, AUROC 0.66, maturity 82%, delay 42 d, Partner Z at 24% with 58% V3 routing, observed Z breach 0.45 vs 0.27, and Z's predictive features (rural distance, device age) missing. *Inference:* four things at once — continuing supplier concept/process drift, V3 covariate drift, a new population with feature gaps, and growing censoring. The Z-vs-non-Z gap is itself maturity-confounded and must not be read at face value.

**October — measurement failure dominates.** Observed: maturity 55%, delay 52 d, observed breach *falls* to 0.16 while predicted rises to 0.23, AUROC 0.64. *Inference:* the API backlog selectively withholds long-path (V3, breaching) labels, so the evaluated cohort is enriched with fast remote fixes; the "recovery" and the "overprediction" are largely artifacts. Real performance is indeterminate until maturity ~90%.

**Why this is not one homogeneous failure:** the mechanisms differ (schema bug  policy mix  supplier physics  new population  censoring backlog), the evidence types differ (DQ counters vs within-variant outcomes vs DFG latencies vs maturity curves), and the remediations differ (recompute  recalibrate  feature+retrain  transportability handling  wait). Critically, aggregate metrics actively mislead here: April's labels were fine while features broke; July's aggregate looked calibrated while two strata erred in opposite directions; October's labels broke while the process genuinely worsened.

## 3. Diagnostic tests

**T1. Label-maturity curves & fixed-vintage backtesting.** Plot P(label matured) vs days-since-diagnosis per variant per cohort; recompute breach rate/AUROC only at fixed vintages (e.g., cases 45/60 d past diagnosis; re-score Oct at 90% maturity).
- *Under censoring:* Oct breach estimate climbs monotonically with maturity toward ~0.28–0.35; V3 maturity curves are much flatter; matched-vintage Oct AUROC recovers materially.
- *Under genuine change:* Oct matured breach stays 0.16 and curves overlap prior months  censoring explanation falsified.
- Bonus: if maturity-adjusted Aug–Sep breach > observed, deterioration is understated, not overstated.

**T2. Recomputation after label normalization + duplicate cleaning (Apr–May replay).** Rebuild prefixes/features from raw events (normalized labels, dedup, backfilled event-count), score with the frozen model.
- *Under pipeline failure:* Apr returns to ~0.80 AUROC / ~0.03 ECE / ~0.19 mean prediction; May returns to baseline.
- *Under additional real drift:* Apr recovers but Jul–Sep stay degraded  pipeline explains only Apr–May. If Apr does *not* recover, the patch was incomplete or another cause exists.

**T3. Calibration/discrimination stratified by variant × source × month.** Reliability curves and AUROC per stratum; decompose aggregate ECE into mix vs within-stratum components.
- *Under covariate-only (Jun):* within-stratum curves stable; aggregate shift fully reproduced by reweighting to the new mix.
- *Under concept drift (Aug–Sep):* V3 curve degrades (systematic underprediction of breach); V1/V2 stable.
- *Under population shift:* Partner Z deviates even relative to non-Z V3 after maturity matching; if Z  non-Z V3, mix + supplier explain everything.

**T4. Variant inventory + DFG / performance-DFG comparison.** Monthly `get_variants`, edge-set diffs, and performance medians for key edges; clean-room replay for April.
- *Under process/performance drift:* parts-edge median climbs 42155 h while diagnosisRemote Fix stays ~4 h; possibly new edges (e.g., re-order loops) appear from July.
- *Under pipeline artifact:* April shows phantom structure (UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY self-edges, duplicated edges, renamed diagnosis node) that vanishes after cleaning; June shows the *same edge set* with only frequency changes and stable durations  routing mix, not structural change.

**T5. Did the outcome relationship conditional on the same path change?** Restrict to identical paths (e.g., non-Partner-Z V3 cases with a part ordered); fit breach ~ month + covariates; likelihood-ratio test on month indicators; compare Aug–Sep vs Jan–Jun within stratum.
- *Under concept drift:* significant month effect within non-Z V3 (Aug–Sep odds  1), absent for V2.
- *Under population shift:* effect concentrated in the Z stratum; non-Z V3 effect small.
- *Under a feature gap ("representable" drift):* adding a trailing supplier lead-time feature to the frozen model eliminates the month effect  missing feature, not irreducible concept change.

**T6. Population reweighting / transportability check for Partner Z.** Reweight Jan–Jun legacy cases toward Z's covariate profile (propensity/overlap weights; inspect overlap), score with the frozen model, compare against maturity-matched Z outcomes.
- *Under covariate shift only:* reweighted legacy breach  Z observed breach; error concentrated in low-overlap regions.
- *Under concept shift:* Z breach exceeds the reweighted prediction even after adding distance/device-age features  separate segment treatment justified.

**T7. Feature missingness & lineage audit.** Per-feature monthly missingness joined to the deployment log; verify backfill completeness of the cached event-count feature and dedup coverage by date; October label-arrival latency by path vs API queue depth.
- *Under pipeline failure:* April's missingness spike is co-located with the rename; per-case error magnitude correlates with the affected features and shrinks post-fix.
- *Under random missingness:* no correlation  pipeline hypothesis weakened for any residual gap; October label latency should track path length even absent the backlog (mechanical delay) vs jump discontinuously at the backlog onset (API failure).

**T8. Standardized (mix-fixed) aggregate calibration, e.g., July.** Recompute July's calibration holding variant mix at Jan–Mar weights.
- *Under offsetting errors:* standardized July exposes V3 underprediction that the V2-heavy mix masked (aggregate looked fine at 0.18 vs 0.19).
- *Under uniform mild drift:* all strata shift slightly together.

## 4. Recalibration-versus-retraining decision

**Stage 0 — Immediate containment (now, 0–2 weeks).**
- Quarantine April (and pre-patch May records) from training and benchmarking; mark Apr–May metrics "pipeline-invalid" on dashboards.
- Switch all evaluation to maturity-gated cohorts (90% maturity or fixed 45–60 d vintage); publish maturity next to every metric; flag October as non-comparable.
- Interim triage, not a silent score change: apply a logged V3 risk uplift (from the matured V3 hazard ratio, Aug–Sep V3 observed ~0.6 vs predicted ~0.2) or route high-latency V3 cases to manual review.
- Freeze auto-retraining triggers while DQ/maturity alarms are active.

**Stage 1 — Short-term recalibration (2–6 weeks), no retraining yet.**
- Fit segment-stratified calibrators (V1 / V2 / V3 / Partner Z) on **matured labels only**: Jan–Jul fully, Aug–Sep matured subsets (censoring-robust, e.g., IPCW or elapsed-time restriction). **Never pool October; never use April.**
- Acceptance gate: stratified ECE  ~0.05 and AUROC  ~0.78 on matured Aug–Sep  defer full retraining; otherwise proceed to Stage 3.

**Stage 2 — Feature repair (concurrent, 2–8 weeks).**
- Backfill the cached event-count feature (idempotently), dedup pre-20 Apr, add lineage tests.
- New features: trailing 7/28-day supplier lead-time (rolling median of `Order Part  Part Received`), parts-queue depth, routing-policy flag, rural travel distance, device age (closes the Partner Z gap).
- Champion–challenger: retrain with new features on Jan–Jul + weighted matured Aug–Sep; validate on fixed-vintage slices.

**Stage 3 — Full retrain (after labels mature, ~6–10 weeks).** Retrain on Jan–Mar, recomputed May, Jun, Jul, and maturity-weighted Aug–Sep; add Partner Z once its features exist and its labels are 90% matured. Validate per segment, per vintage, with rollback.

| Window | Training | Evaluation |
|---|---|---|
| Jan–Mar | Safe | Safe (baseline) |
| Apr | **Unsafe** (features corrupt) even though labels are mature | Invalid |
| May | Only after recomputation | Conditional |
| Jun | Safe (mature, clean; include to learn the routing-policy regime) | Safe |
| Jul | Safe (98% mature; supplier onset is *real signal*, not corruption) | Safe with maturity note |
| Aug–Sep | Matured subsets / censoring-weighted only; V1–V2 fine, V3 with care | Conditional |
| Oct (incl. Partner Z) | **Unsafe** until 90% maturity and backlog cleared | Invalid for now |

**Segment-specific model?** Start with one model + stratified calibration + the new features: V1/V2 are stable, drift is V3-localized, and the V3 shift appears feature-explainable (supplier latency is observable). Escalate to variant-specific heads or a separate Partner Z model only if (a) T6 shows residual concept shift for Z after reweighting and feature addition, or (b) V3 miscalibration persists after the supplier feature. A shared-trunk/segment-head hierarchical model is the middle option; full data partitioning would starve V1/V2 and complicate governance.

## 5. Monitoring architecture

**Monitor layers**

- **M0 Intervention/deployment registry:** every migration, patch, policy, onboarding, and model promotion logged with timestamps; all alerts auto-join this log.
- **M1 Data-quality (ingest):** schema/label-set diff, unknown-activity share, unmapped-variant share, per-feature missingness, duplicate rate  sets `data_quality_ok=False`; freezes training-data refresh and marks predictive metrics invalid (the April guard).
- **M2 Label-maturity:** maturity %, median delay, maturity curves by variant/path, outcome-API queue depth  sets `maturity_ok=False` for affected cohorts; schedules automatic re-evaluation at the next maturity threshold.
- **M3 Predictive performance:** AUROC/Brier on maturity-gated, fixed-vintage cohorts with trailing control limits, plus segment-stratified views.
- **M4 Calibration:** ECE/reliability per segment × month; alerts even when aggregates look fine (the July guard).
- **M5 Process-variant monitor:** variant-mix PSI vs trailing 90 days; new/rare variant emergence; unmapped share.
- **M6 Performance-DFG monitor:** rolling medians/p90 of key edges vs trailing baselines; label-independent early warning (edge latency degrades weeks before breach labels mature).
- **M7 Population monitor:** source mix, per-feature PSI/KS, covariate-overlap index for new segments.
- **M8 Retraining gate:** opens a (human-approved) retraining ticket **only** when `data_quality_ok` and `maturity_ok` are true, M3/M4 breach limits for k consecutive windows, and M0 shows no explanatory change; otherwise routes to triage with the drift taxonomy attached. Promotion requires champion–challenger validation on fixed vintages; rollback hooks mandatory.

**Alert dependencies (the key design rule):** every published metric carries `data_quality_ok` and `maturity_ok` flags for its cohort. An M2 label-delay alarm sets `maturity_ok=False`, which **suppresses/downgrades M3/M4 alerts for that cohort to informational** and blocks M8 — so a label-delay alarm triggers re-evaluation later, never immediate retraining. An M1 alarm invalidates metrics outright (April pattern). M6/M7 alarms are label-independent: they open feature-engineering or transportability tickets and may justify interim triage rules, but never auto-retraining.

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

CSV = "field_service_events.csv"
NEW_DIAG, OLD_DIAG = "Diagnostic Assessment Completed", "Initial Diagnosis Completed"
KEY_EDGES = [("Order Part", "Part Received"), ("Initial Diagnosis Completed", "Remote Fix")]

def load_normalized(path=CSV):
    """Read, format, normalize the renamed diagnosis label, and audit duplicates."""
    df = pd.read_csv(path)
    df["timestamp"] = pd.to_datetime(df["timestamp"])
    df = pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id="case_id", activity_key="activity",
                                timestamp_key="timestamp")  # -> case:concept:name / concept:name / time:timestamp
    # (1) Normalize the migrated label BEFORE any feature, variant, or DFG extraction
    #     so pre-/post-migration traces are comparable.
    df["concept:name"] = df["concept:name"].replace({NEW_DIAG: OLD_DIAG})
    # (2) Duplicate handling: audit share per calendar month, then drop exact
    #     re-inserted events (same case+activity+timestamp), keeping the first.
    df["cal_month"] = df["time:timestamp"].dt.to_period("M")
    dup_share = (df.assign(is_dup=df.duplicated(
                      subset=["case:concept:name", "concept:name", "time:timestamp"], keep="first"))
                   .groupby("cal_month")["is_dup"].mean().rename("duplicate_share"))
    df = (df.sort_values(["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp"])
            .drop_duplicates(subset=["case:concept:name", "concept:name", "time:timestamp"], keep="first"))
    # (3) Flag UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY rather than silently dropping it: it is audit evidence.
    df["is_unknown"] = df["concept:name"].eq("UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY")
    return df, dup_share

def _median_hours(perf_dfg, edge):
    rec = perf_dfg.get(edge, {}) or {}
    for k in ("median", "mean", "pm4py:performance"):   # pm4py stores seconds
        if k in rec:
            return rec[k] / 3600.0
    return float("nan")

def monthly_process_audit(df, dup_share):
    """Auditable monthly summaries. No model is fitted and no PM4Py 'drift detector'
    is invoked: month-over-month deltas are computed transparently in pandas."""
    case_start = df.groupby("case:concept:name")["time:timestamp"].min()
    months = pd.period_range(df["cal_month"].min(), df["cal_month"].max(), freq="M")
    rows = []
    for m in months:
        t0, t1 = m.start_time, (m + 1).start_time
        # (a) EVENT-CALENDAR cohort: all events occurring in month m regardless of case
        #     start. Interpretation: "what the process did that month" (load, observed
        #     edge latencies). Mixes intake cohorts -- not a model-evaluation cohort.
        cal = pm4py.filter_time_range(df, t0, t1, mode="events", case_id_key="case:concept:name")
        cal_dfg, _, _ = pm4py.discover_dfg(cal)
        # (b) CASE-START cohort: cases opened in month m, full (possibly right-censored)
        #     traces. Interpretation: "the intake cohort the predictor is scored on".
        #     Recent months are censored -> closed_share is a maturity proxy, and
        #     performance medians on unfinished paths are biased low.
        cases_m = case_start[case_start.dt.to_period("M") == m].index
        cs = df[df["case:concept:name"].isin(cases_m)]
        variants = pm4py.get_variants(cs)                    # control-flow inventory
        v3_cases = cs.loc[cs["concept:name"].eq("Order Part"), "case:concept:name"].nunique()
        closed = cs.loc[cs["concept:name"].eq("Case Closed"), "case:concept:name"].nunique()
        perf_dfg, _, _ = pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(cs)
        tf = pm4py.extract_temporal_features_dataframe(cs)   # per-case temporal features
        dur_col = next((c for c in ("caseDuration", "case:duration", "duration") if c in tf.columns), None)
        row = {"month": str(m), "cases_started": len(cases_m),
               "events_in_calendar_month": len(cal),
               "dfg_edges_calendar_month": len(cal_dfg),     # structural-change hint
               "n_variants": len(variants),
               "v3_share_case_start": v3_cases / max(len(cases_m), 1),
               "closed_share_case_start": closed / max(len(cases_m), 1),
               "unknown_activity_share": float(cs["is_unknown"].mean()),
               "duplicate_share": float(dup_share.get(m, float("nan"))),
               "median_case_duration_d": (tf[dur_col].median() / 86400.0) if dur_col else float("nan")}
        for e in KEY_EDGES:
            row[f"{e[0][:12]}->{e[1][:12]}_median_h"] = _median_hours(perf_dfg, e)
        rows.append(row)
    out = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    out["parts_edge_pct_change"] = out.filter(like="Order Part->").pct_change().iloc[:, 0]
    out["v3_share_delta"] = out["v3_share_case_start"].diff()
    return out

if __name__ == "__main__":
    df, dup_share = load_normalized()
    audit = monthly_process_audit(df, dup_share)
    print(audit.round(3).to_string(index=False))
    audit.to_csv("monthly_process_audit.csv", index=False)
```

## 6. Risks of acting on the wrong diagnosis

1. **Retraining on immature labels (Sep–Oct).** *Failure:* the model learns the censoring artifact — long paths appear not to breach — so it systematically underpredicts exactly the riskiest cases; damage surfaces months later when labels mature. *Mitigation:* maturity gates and fixed-vintage training snapshots; censoring-aware losses (IPCW) or elapsed-time restrictions; exclude October entirely; validate on matured holdouts only.
2. **Calibrating around a pipeline bug (using Apr–May in the calibration set).** *Failure:* the calibrator absorbs corrupted-feature behavior (April's inflated scores bias it high) and breaks silently once the pipeline is fixed, creating a second incident on top of the first. *Mitigation:* lineage/DQ gates on calibration data; "recompute-before-calibrate" policy; exclude quarantined windows; version features with the pipeline build ID.
3. **Dismissing Aug–Sep as a label artifact (over-correcting for censoring).** *Failure:* waiting for "clean labels" while a genuine supplier-driven concept drift doubles V3 breach rates; avoidable business impact accumulates. *Mitigation:* label-independent early warnings (performance-DFG edge latency), V3-stratified matured-breach tracking, interim logged uplift/triage, expedited supplier-lead-time feature.
4. **Misreading June as concept drift — or ignoring it.** *Failure:* unnecessary retraining on a mix artifact causes model churn and overfitting to one month's routing policy; conversely, ignoring the mix shift leaves persistent overprediction in V2-heavy months. *Mitigation:* within-stratum stability test plus mix-decomposition of ECE (T3/T8); recalibrate rather than retrain; add the routing-policy feature.
5. **Attributing Partner Z's gap to unfair model behavior — or dismissing a real disparity — without stratified evidence.** *Failure:* the raw 0.45-vs-0.27 gap is maturity-confounded and confounded by Z's V3 share and missing features; a premature fairness verdict triggers wrong governance action, while dismissing it could leave rural customers genuinely underserved. *Mitigation:* maturity-matched, variant-matched comparisons; transportability/reweighting tests (T6); add distance/device-age features; document the evidence chain before any fairness determination; monitor post-remediation disparity.
6. **Non-idempotent backfills creating new duplicates.** *Failure:* a second "April" appears weeks later as phantom drift, eroding trust in every metric. *Mitigation:* idempotent, uniquely keyed backfills with reconciliation counts; automated DQ regression tests on ingest; the M1 gate blocks training refresh until counts reconcile.
