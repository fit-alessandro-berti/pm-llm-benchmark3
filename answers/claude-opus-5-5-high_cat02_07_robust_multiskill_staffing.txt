# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

**Evidence labels used throughout:** **[O]** observation taken directly from the prompt; **[A]** assumption I introduce, with the reason; **[E]** estimate (calculation or simulation); **[B]** bound; **[H]** hypothesis; **[R]** recommendation. All simulation numbers come from the script in Section 8 (40 seeds per policy-scenario cell, common random numbers, fixed seed base 1000).

## 1.1 Demand and service assumptions

| # | Assumption | Why it is needed |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | Arrivals per 2-hour block are Poisson with the given mean. Arrival times are uniform within the block. Priority and case type are drawn independently from the block percentages. | The prompt gives only expected counts. Poisson is the least-informative count model and makes day-to-day variability explicit. |
| A2 | Each activity is "complex-tail" with the stated probability, independently per activity. Otherwise it is "ordinary". | A4 | Handover (7 min), team-change (+5 min) and parallel coordination (8 min) are **case delays, not resource time**. At about 15 handover-minutes per case × 42 cases, receivers who actually spent these minutes briefing would lose roughly 20–25% of capacity. | A6 | Setup (5 min, resource time) applies when a resource's diagnostic family (M, E or S) differs from its previous diagnostic case. There is no setup between the two branches of one sequential case. | The rule says "between consecutive cases". The within-case switch is a flag in the code. |
| A7 | Preemption: only a diagnostic activity of a lower-priority case can be interrupted, at most once per case. The remaining active time is increased by 10% and the rest is re-queued; any eligible resource may resume it, with handover if it is a different person. | This is the prompt's rule, made executable. |

| A9 | **The SLA clock is elapsed calendar time, including nights.** | The prompt says "elapsed". This is decisive: a P2 case arriving after about 12:30 must be finished the same evening, and a P1 arriving after about 18:30 has almost no staff. |
| A10 | The horizon has four days. Day 0 is a base-load warm-up, day 1 is the measured scenario day, day 2 has base arrivals (so carry-over competes with new work), and day 3 only drains. ## 1.2 How ME/ES parallelism is decided

There is a cost asymmetry. **Parallel** execution costs *elapsed time*: 8 minutes of coordination plus 7–12 minutes of handover. It costs no extra capacity. **Sequential** execution on one cross-skilled person costs *capacity*, because the secondary skill runs slower:

- ME on Deepa: +6.6 min (26.3 × 0.25).
- ES on Chen: +9.6 min (38.5 × 0.25).
- ES on Emil: +9.8 min (32.8 × 0.30).
- Ada (E ×1.35) and Farah (×1.40) are excluded as sequential owners by a 1.30 cap.

A zero-queue Monte Carlo check (included in the script) gives the elapsed minutes for a P1 case with no waiting at all:

| P1 path | mean | p90 | P(>120 min) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| M (Ada Intake+M, then Ben Validation) | 56 | 72 | 0.000 |
| E (Chen, then Deepa Validation) | 63 | 82 | 0.000 |
| S, 12–20 (Emil end-to-end) | 62 | 86 | 0.000 |
| S, 08–12 (Chen with S ×1.25) | 73 | 103 | 0.023 |
| ME parallel | 88 | 106 | 0.013 |
| ME sequential (Deepa) | 88 | 112 | 0.043 |
| ES parallel | 80 | 103 | 0.009 |
| ES sequential (Chen) | 106 | 137 | **0.224** |

Decision rule:
- **P1 ME/ES cases always run in parallel.** A P1 two-branch case has only about 15 (at p90) to 40 (at the mean) minutes of total slack for queues.
- Non-P1 cases run sequentially only if the estimated sequential completion is at least 10 minutes earlier than the parallel estimate. The parallel estimate is the maximum over branches of (queue work ÷ on-shift home servers + mean duration + handover), plus 8. The sequential owner must have both multipliers ≤ 1.30.
- A sequential job not started within 60 minutes is split back into parallel branches.

## 1.3 Objectives (two or more, deliberately different)

Let short(x) = max(0, 90 − 100·x), the SLA shortfall in percentage points.

1. **J_mean** (for contrast only): mean delay in the base scenario. Delay = lead time − ideal no-wait critical path, where the ideal uses base durations at multiplier 1.0 and the maximum of the branches for two-branch cases.
2. **J_tail** (tail and SLA risk): 3·short(P1) + 1·short(P2) + 0.5·short(P3) + 0.02·p90_delay[min].
3. **J_fair** (fairness): J_tail + 0.5·(percentage points by which the maximum individual utilization exceeds 90) + 0.2·(utilization range, max − min, in percentage points).
4. **Robustness criteria over {BASE, U1, …, U5}:** the primary criterion is **minimax regret of J_fair** (the worst-case gap to the best policy in each scenario). The secondary criteria are worst-case J_fair, the mean of J_tail, and the worst-case P1 and P2 SLA.
5. An **operational lexicographic reading**: protect P1 first, then P2 same-day completion, then P3 age, then fairness. This is used to set the dispatch rules and the mode triggers.

The weights are value judgments. Section 5 notes where a different weighting would change the recommendation.

## 1.4 Decision variables

- The eligibility matrix by (resource, skill, time of day).
- The priority score: class, due date, and aging (P3 is promoted when less than 4 hours remain).
- Reservation: which guard serves which window, and the queue-length threshold that releases the guard.
- Preemption: the wait W before a P1 may preempt, and the lookahead L (is anyone eligible about to become free?), both by time of day.
- The parallel/sequential rule and its thresholds.
- Overflow waits by priority.
- **Roster**: Ben's start time, and Farah's hours and their timing.
- Overtime authorization.

## 2.3 What this implies (base)

1. **Structural deficit [B].** Under the central assumption the day needs 2,721 base-minutes against 2,490 scheduled minutes. That is 109% before any multiplier penalty and 102% even with every person's 30 minutes of overtime.
   - The unavoidable multipliers (morning Software on Chen at ×1.25, evening Mechanical on Deepa at ×1.25, evening Electrical on Emil at ×1.30) add roughly 65 more minutes.
   - A **fluid LP** (perfect pooling that ignores precedence, handovers and randomness) needs a maximum individual utilization of **1.09**. If work cannot be served before it arrives, it still leaves at least **183 base-minutes** for the next morning with overtime, or **508** with utilization capped at 90% and no overtime.
   - **The 90%-utilization fairness rule is therefore infeasible for every routing policy [B].** Meeting it would need about 3,023 scheduled minutes, i.e. roughly +530 minutes, or about 1.2 extra shifts.
   - Only if true durations sit near the range minimums (2,065, ρ = 0.83) is the base comfortably feasible. The right-skewed triangular case (2,502) is still at about 100%.
2. **Morning bottleneck: Chen [E].** No home Software resource exists before 12:00. Chen is also the only ×1.00 Electrical person in 08–10.
   - In 08–10, Chen's potential demand is 126 minutes of Electrical plus 67 × 1.25 = 84 minutes of Software, i.e. 210 minutes in a 120-minute block.
   - Farah (Software ×1.40) is the only relief valve.
   - Morning Software work either waits for Emil at 12:00 or burns 25–40% extra minutes.
3. **Electrical is the largest skill load** (797 base-min, 29%). Only Chen and Deepa are ×1.00 (900 minutes combined). Covering Electrical alone takes 89% of their time, yet both are also home validators.
4. **The evening cliff [E].** Work arriving 16–20 is 584 base-minutes against 330 scheduled minutes (ρ ≈ 1.8).
   - After 18:00, *nobody* is qualified for Mechanical (only Deepa's overtime to 18:30), and Emil handles everything else alone (Electrical ×1.30, Intake ×1.10).
   - Because the SLA clock runs overnight, P2 cases arriving after about 14:00 must be finished by 20:30 or they fail. That is about 9.5 P2 cases per day, 43% of all P2.
   - Among P1 cases, those that need Mechanical after 18:00 cannot be served at all. The others, if they arrive after about 19:30, cannot be completed within the overtime cap even when Emil is free (about 66 minutes of work, with the last finish at 20:30).
5. **Validation is the latent bottleneck.** It is 661 base-minutes (24%), and its home servers (Ben, Chen, Deepa, Emil) are the same people who run the diagnostic bottlenecks. When diagnostics speed up, Validation queues appear; the single-run log in Section 8 shows a 302-minute mean Validation wait for work entering the queue at 14:00.

**Roster levers change the fluid bound [E].**
- Moving Ben to 10:00–18:00 cuts the base carry-over from 183 to **88** minutes, because morning work has due-date slack and evening work does not. It does not help U1 (778), because U1 is a morning shock.
- Adding Farah 16:00–20:00 (+240 minutes) brings the base carry-over to **0**, U4 to 95 and U3 to 332.
- Under U5, no lever tested here gets the carry-over below about 1,000 minutes.

**Bottom line of the capacity analysis.** Routing decides *who waits*; it cannot remove a 9–70% capacity deficit. The routing policies should therefore be judged on:
- protection of P1;
- the size of the P2 same-day window;
- P3 starvation;
- wasted capacity through slow multipliers and setups;
- fairness.

## 3.2 HYB as an operator "rule card" (auditable in one page)

1. **When you become free**, look only at tasks you may start *now*. Your home skills are always allowed. Another skill you hold is allowed if one of these applies:
   - (a) the task is P1;
   - (b) nobody with that home skill is on shift (Software before 12:00; Electrical 08–10 when Chen is absent);
   - (c) a P2 has waited 45 minutes or a P3 has waited 120 minutes, *and* nothing in your own home queue is waiting.
2. **Order of work:**
   - P1.
   - Then P2, together with any P3 that has less than 4 hours to its due time, by earliest due time.
   - Then other P3 by due time.
   - Among equals, prefer home skill over another skill, then the person who would finish first. That includes 7–12 minutes of handover and 5 minutes of setup, so "keep the case" and "stay in your family" win ties.
3. **P1 guard:** 08–12 Farah and 12–16 Deepa start P1 work first. They do not start routine P3 diagnostics unless their own home queue exceeds 2 hours of planned work.
4. **Interrupting for P1:**
   - Before 16:00: if a P1 has waited 10 minutes and nobody eligible frees up within 15 minutes, interrupt the eligible person working on the lowest-priority diagnostic with the most time remaining.
   - After 16:00: interrupt immediately.
   - A case can be interrupted only once; the interrupted work gets +10% of its remaining time.
5. **Two-branch cases:** P1 always in parallel. Others in parallel unless a cross-skilled owner (Deepa for ME; Chen or Emil for ES) would finish at least 10 minutes earlier. Revert to parallel if the owner has not started within 60 minutes.
6. **Near shift end:** start a task only if its planned finish is within 30 minutes after your shift end.

Every assignment logs a reason code (`home`, `overflow-P1`, `overflow-gap`, `overflow-wait`, `seq-owner`, `preempt`) and the set of eligible idle people, so any decision can be replayed.

**Why HYB_R moves Ben.** Morning arrivals have P2 due times of 16:00–20:00 and can absorb queueing. Arrivals after 14:00 cannot. Ben's skills (Mechanical ×1.00, Validation ×1.00) are exactly what is missing from 16:00 to 18:00.

**Why Farah's evening block (HYB_RF).** She is the only person who can cover Mechanical after 18:00 (at ×1.40). She does Intake at ×1.00 and is a generic E/S/Validation reserve. This is a *staffing* decision: it adds 9.6% paid capacity and needs a contract change. It is evaluated so that the value of capacity can be compared with the value of routing.

# 4. Scenario comparison

**Scale and uncertainty.**
- `mean_delay` is in minutes: lead time minus the ideal no-wait critical path. It includes queues, handovers, coordination, setups, slow-multiplier excess and **overnight waiting**. The brackets give the 10th–90th percentile of the per-seed mean across 40 seeds.
- `p90_or_p95_delay` is pooled over cases.
- SLA values are pooled proportions, and P1 carries a ±95% binomial half-width (212–340 P1 cases per cell).
- `max_resource_utilization` is the mean over seeds of the busiest person's utilization; values above 1.0 mean overtime was used.
- `utilization_fairness` is the max−min utilization range in percentage points, plus the number of people above 90%.
- `handover_or_setup_burden` is handover + coordination + setup minutes per case, with the setup part in parentheses.
- `resilience` is the base-minutes of unfinished work at 20:30 on the shock day, and the change in P2 SLA versus the same policy's BASE.

**Reading the evidence.**

- **The tension between specialization and pooling is visible.**
  - SPEC wastes Farah (52%) and starves P3 (0.05–0.29). It fails P1 in the morning, where Software has no home server (0.80–0.83), and at 14:00 (0.69).

  - As a result, POOL's P1 attainment degrades most under the morning surge (0.74) and combined stress (0.56).
  - HYB keeps POOL's base P1 (0.88), holds 0.74–0.82 P1 under shocks, cuts setups by about 65% and preemptions by about 80%, and has fewer inherited backlog minutes.

- **Parallel versus sequential (sensitivity, HYB_SEQ).** Forcing sequential ME/ES (about 21% of cases) lowers handover burden from 19.7 to 15.5 minutes per case (1.76 → 1.39 handovers). But it *worsens* P2 (0.66 → 0.56 base; 0.43 → 0.36 in U3), P3 (0.69 → 0.57), and backlog (+225 base-min). The cross-skilled owners are themselves the bottleneck, and the ×1.25/×1.30 penalties consume capacity. The threshold rule almost never fires (about 1% of cases), which is the correct behaviour at ρ ≈ 1.
- **Inherited backlog matters (sensitivity with an empty start).** Starting the measured day empty lowers base mean delay by 33–43% for every policy. The ranking is unchanged: HYB_RF has P1 0.91, P2 0.87, P3 0.97; HYB_R 0.89 / 0.82 / 0.89; POOL 0.87 / 0.79 / 0.94; HYB 0.85 / 0.77 / 0.90; SPEC 0.81 / 0.75 / 0.60. On an empty start POOL has a slightly better P3 than HYB, because pooling helps when the backlog is small.
- **Nobody meets the targets.** Every policy exceeds 90% utilization for 4–6 people, and no policy reaches P2 ≥ 0.90 in any scenario. P1 ≥ 0.90 is reached only by HYB_RF with an empty start. This is the capacity finding of Section 2 showing up in the simulation, not a routing failure.

# 5. Robust recommendation

**[R] Primary policy: HYB_RF.** This means hybrid dispatch (home first, P1 guards, timed controlled overflow, threshold parallelization with P1 always parallel, immediate P1 preemption after 16:00), Ben moved to 10:00–18:00, and Farah's contract extended to a split shift of 08:00–12:00 plus 16:00–20:00.

**Why it is the robust choice under the stated objectives.**
- It has the lowest J_fair in **every** scenario (maximum regret 0).
- It has the best worst-case P1 (0.77) and P2 (0.41), and the lowest mean and p90 delay in every scenario.
- It lowers the fluid carry-over bound to 0 in the base case.
- The capacity is placed where the elapsed-time SLA binds: the 16:00–20:00 window, and specifically Mechanical after 18:00.

It does **not** dominate on every metric:
- Its base P1 (0.86 ±0.05) is statistically indistinguishable from HYB and POOL.
- POOL has a slightly narrower utilization range in some scenarios.
- It costs 240 additional paid minutes per day.

**[R] If extra hours are not available: HYB_R** (zero cost, maximum regret 34.8, better than HYB in P2 in all six scenarios). **If rosters cannot change at all: HYB.** In either case, management should be told explicitly that the 90% SLA targets and the 90% utilization cap are **not achievable** with current capacity under the central service assumption. Closing the gap needs about +530 scheduled minutes per day, mostly after 14:00, or evidence that true service times are near the lower end of the ranges.

## 5.2 When another policy is preferable

- **Utilization equality as the dominant objective:** POOL has a slightly narrower range (8–11 pp against 8–16 pp for the hybrids), at the price of P1 resilience under surges and much more interruption.
- **True service times near the range minimums (ρ ≈ 0.83):** the policies converge. Prefer HYB without guards (fewer rules), or even SPEC with overflow at thresholds, which preserves skills and cuts setups.
- **Handover delays much larger than stated, or consuming the receiver's time** (worth roughly 20–25% of capacity, see A4): move towards sequential case ownership for non-P1 ME via Deepa, and let one person carry Intake → diagnostic → Validation where qualified. HYB_SEQ's lower burden (15.5 against 19.7 min per case, 1.39 against 1.76 handovers) would then outweigh its multiplier cost. This must be re-simulated with `handover_consumes_resource=True` before acting.
- **Only base mean delay matters:** the ordering is the same (HYB_RF < HYB_R < HYB < POOL < SPEC), but this should not be the decision basis. POOL's base mean is only 8% worse than HYB's while its U5 P1 is 18 percentage points worse.
- **U1-type shocks becoming routine:** the 10:00 start for Ben hurts in the morning. Keep Ben at 08:00 and look for evening capacity elsewhere.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates.** Planned durations drive three decisions: whether a task may start near shift end, the sequential-versus-parallel choice, and guard release (queued planned minutes > 120).
  - Staff could inflate plans to avoid late starts, or to trigger the guard's release onto P3 work.
  - Intake staff could inflate priority, because P1 buys preemption rights.
  - Mitigations: set plans from historical category and resource medians, not self-reports. Publish plan/actual ratios by person and skill. Monitor the P1 share against the planned 10–18% per block and audit a sample of P1 classifications after the fact.
- **Starvation of P3.**
  - SPEC reaches P3 SLA of 0.05–0.29. The hybrids reach 0.23–0.85 because of aging promotion, but under U5 even HYB_RF only reaches 0.31.
  - Add a hard rule: no P3 older than 20 hours, and one "P3 sweep" slot in the 12:00–14:00 block, which is the only base block with ρ < 0.9.
- **Skill erosion.** The hybrid keeps cross-skill work low: overflow happens only on gaps or waits, and sequential cases are about 1%. Farah's and Ada's Electrical/Software skills, and Chen's Software, may therefore decay, and they are exactly the skills needed in U3 and U5.
  - Schedule deliberate cross-skill P3 work (for example 2 cases per week each) in low-load windows.
  - Track per-person multipliers from the log; a rising multiplier is a skill-erosion signal.
- **Excessive preemption.**
  - POOL interrupts 7–11 times per day; each interruption costs 10% of the remaining time plus a handover if resumed by someone else.
  - The hybrids interrupt 1.2–2.3 times per day. Cap it at 2 interruptions per person per day and never interrupt within 5 minutes of completion.
  - Real diagnostics may have non-interruptible steps (safety, test rigs). A `non_preemptible` flag per step should be added.
- **Coordination overhead.** Every policy averages about 20 minutes per case of handover, coordination and setup (1.5–2.1 handovers per case).
  - Parallelism is still right at this load, because the alternative costs capacity.
  - But the 8-minute coordination and the 12-minute cross-team handover should be reduced by process means: shared case notes, and joining the branches at the Validation queue.
- **New bottleneck at Validation.** Validation is 24% of the load, and its home servers are the diagnostic bottleneck people. Speeding up diagnostics moves queues to Validation (the 14:00 queue-entry block in the log).
  - Mitigations: allow Ada (×1.15) and Farah (×1.20) to validate on overflow. Give P1 Validation the same guard priority as P1 diagnostics. Watch the Validation waiting-time share.
- **Fairness.**
  - Under POOL and the hybrids everyone is at about 97–106%, so fairness is poor by construction; the 90% rule is breached for 4–6 people in every policy.
  - SPEC is unfair *in both directions*: Farah at 52% while the others are above 100%.
  - The roster changes also shift personal burdens (Ben's later shift, Farah's split shift). Rotate the late shift, and make split shifts voluntary and compensated.
  - Overtime-cap breaches of 0.15–0.9 per day show that the "planned finish" start rule is not safe enough near shift end; use the p80–p90 plan in the last hour.
- **Human interpretability.** The rule card fits on one page, and each assignment carries a reason code and the eligible idle set.
  - Risks: people bypassing rules (for example keeping favourite cases) and silent mode switches.
  - Show the current mode on the queue board, log every mode change with its trigger, and keep the score lexicographic, not a weighted opaque number.

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

## 7.1 Event attributes (minimum schema; all emitted by the simulator)

| attribute | purpose |
|---|---|
| `case:concept:name`, `concept:name`, `start_timestamp`, `time:timestamp`, `org:resource` | interval log (service time = complete − start) |
| `lifecycle` (complete / interrupted), `pause_resume` (pause / resume) | preemption fragments; restart penalty |
| `queue_entry` | waiting = start − queue entry, per stage (not derivable from complete-only logs) |
| `assign_reason` (home, overflow-P1, overflow-gap, overflow-wait, seq-owner, preempt) and decision time | audit of the dispatch rule |
| `eligible_idle_set` | who *could* have taken the task, needed for counterfactual and fairness analysis |
| `skill_used`, `home_skill`, `skill_mult` | cross-skill share; multiplier drift and skill erosion |
| `planned_min`, `actual_min` | estimate quality and gaming; long-tail detection |
| `handover_min` (from resource/team), `setup_min`, `branch_mode` (parallel / sequential), coordination flag | handover and setup burden |
| `policy_version`, `scenario_flag` / mode (N, S, A, L, O), `arrival_day` | change-point attribution; separates shock days |
| `priority`, `case_type`, `sla_due`, `sla_clock_at_start_min` | SLA clock per step; slack-at-start analysis |

## 7.2 Deployment indicators (daily, with alarm thresholds)

- **SLA:** P1, P2 and P3 attainment by *arrival block*. P1 wait per step (alarm above 15 min). P2 same-day completion for arrivals from 14:00. Maximum P3 age (alarm above 20 h).
- **Load and backlog:** backlog at 20:30 in base-minutes (alarm above 400). Queue wait by stage and block.
- **Utilization and fairness:** utilization per person and its range (alarm if anyone is above 95% for 3 consecutive days). Overtime minutes and breaches.
- **Churn:** preemptions per day (alarm above 3). Handovers and setup minutes per case.
- **Routing mix:** cross-skill share by person. Sequential share. Frequency of guard releases and of each mode.
- **Estimate quality:** plan/actual ratio by skill and person.

## 7.3 PM4Py analyses

- **Format:** `pm4py.format_dataframe(df, case_id=..., activity_key=..., timestamp_key="time:timestamp", start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")`.
  - Several PM4Py functions default `start_timestamp_key` to `time:timestamp`. It must be passed explicitly or service times collapse to 0.
  - Add an instantaneous `Arrival` event so that case durations include the Intake queue.
- **Service-time drift (U4, multipliers, skill erosion):**
  - `pm4py.get_service_time(..., start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp", aggregation_measure="mean"/"median")` per week. Also per resource, using `pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values` on `org:resource` or `skill_used`.
  - Compare with `planned_min`. A sustained shift in the ratio, or in the tail share, triggers mode L or a re-plan.
  - Interrupted fragments must be merged (sum per case and activity) before interpreting per-activity service times.
- **Case-level service and waiting:** `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(..., start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")`. Label the outputs honestly:
  - PM4Py's "service" is the **sum of activity durations**, which double-counts parallel branches.
  - "Waiting" = sojourn − sum is therefore **not queue time**; it can even be negative when branches overlap.
  - Stage-specific waiting must come from `queue_entry` (done with pandas in the script).
- **Handover burden:** `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network`. Self-loops (for example Ben → Ben) are *continuations, not handovers* and must be excluded.
  - Compare the Mechanical → Electrical/Software team edges (the +5 minutes) before and after deployment.
  - `pm4py.discover_working_together_network` shows the co-working structure; `pm4py.discover_activity_based_resource_similarity` and `pm4py.discover_organizational_roles` show role drift, for example Farah turning into a de facto Electrical resource.
- **Bottleneck migration:** slice by time window (`pm4py.filter_time_range`) and by `scenario_flag`/mode. Then:
  - compare `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg` edge times (Intake → diagnostic, diagnostic → Validation);
  - pivot queue wait by stage × queue-entry block, as in the script. The single-run table shows the base peaks: Software queue entering 08:00, Electrical 10:00, Validation 14:00, and all stages 16:00+. Under U5, Electrical and Intake peak in 08–12 and remain high all day.
- **Conformance and rework:** discover a reference model on a stable period with `pm4py.discover_petri_net_inductive`. Then check new periods with `pm4py.fitness_alignments` or `pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_token_based_replay` to catch skipped Validation, repeated diagnostics, or ad-hoc reroutes. Use `pm4py.get_rework_cases_per_activity` for rework loops, which are not in the simulator.
- **Drift detection:** PM4Py has no built-in statistical drift test suited to this. Compute weekly KPIs (service time, waiting per stage, cross-skill share, handovers per case) with pandas and apply CUSUM / change-point tests, stratified by `policy_version` and mode.

# 8. Reproducible Python

The script is self-contained. It needs pandas and numpy, pm4py for the post-analysis, and optionally scipy for the fluid LP bound (it returns NaN without scipy). It runs in about 2 minutes on 16 processes; set `REPS` lower for a quick run.

Mechanisms deliberately **not** modelled (the scaffold identifies them):
- rework or failed Validation;
- fatigue and learning effects on multipliers;
- correlated complexity across the branches of one case;
- non-preemptible steps;
- staff deviating from the rules;
- weekend or holiday patterns.

```python
def hm(s):
    h, m = map(int, s.split(":")); return 60 * h + m


def mix_mean(svc, a):
    r = svc.loc[a]
    return (1 - r.p_tail) * (r.ord_lo + r.ord_hi) / 2 + r.p_tail * (r.tail_lo + r.tail_hi) / 2


def scenario(name):
    d, svc = DEMAND.copy().astype({c: float for c in DEMAND.columns if c != "block"}), SERVICE.copy()
    absent, outage = set(), []
    if name in ("U1", "U5"):
        m = d.block.isin(["08:00", "10:00"])
        rest = d.loc[m, "P2"] + d.loc[m, "P3"]
        d.loc[m, "P2"] *= (rest - 8) / rest          # ASSUMPTION: +8pp P1 taken pro rata from P2/P3
        d.loc[m, "P3"] *= (rest - 8) / rest
        d.loc[m, "P1"] += 8
        d.loc[m, "n"] *= 1.7
    if name == "U2":                                  # E and ES counts double -> total rises, renormalize
        m = d.block.isin(["12:00", "14:00"])
        old = d.loc[m, TYPES].sum(axis=1)
        d.loc[m, ["E", "ES"]] *= 2
        new = d.loc[m, TYPES].sum(axis=1)
        d.loc[m, "n"] *= new / old
        d.loc[m, TYPES] = d.loc[m, TYPES].div(new, axis=0) * 100
    if name in ("U3", "U5"):
        absent = {"Chen"}
    if name == "U4":
        svc.loc[DIAG, "p_tail"] *= 2
    if name == "U5":
        outage = [(hm("09:30"), hm("10:00"))]         # no Intake may start in this window
    return dict(demand=d, service=svc, absent=absent, outage=outage)


MEAN_BASE = {a: mix_mean(SERVICE, a) for a in ACTS}   # planning estimates (dispatcher does not know the shock)


def roster(pol):
    ros = {}
    for r in RES:
        s, e, b = SHIFTS.loc[r, ["start", "end", "break_start"]]
        if r in POLICIES[pol]["roster"]:
            s, e, b, *blen = POLICIES[pol]["roster"][r]
        else:
            blen = []
        ros[r] = (hm(s), hm(e), hm(b) if isinstance(b, str) else None, blen[0] if blen else RULES["break_min"])
    return ros


# ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- 3. capacity approximation
def avail_minutes(ros, r, b0, b1, absent=()):
    if r in absent:
        return 0.0
    s, e, b, bl = ros[r]
    tot = max(0, min(e, b1) - max(s, b0))
    if b is not None:
        tot -= max(0, min(b + bl, b1) - max(b, b0))
    return float(tot)


def offered_load(scn):
    S = scenario(scn); d, svc = S["demand"], S["service"]
    rows = []
    for _, row in d.iterrows():
        n = row.n; cnt = {t: n * row[t] / 100 for t in TYPES}
        tasks = {a: 0.0 for a in ACTS}
        tasks["Intake"] = tasks["Validation"] = n
        for t in TYPES:
            for a in BRANCHES[t]:
                tasks[a] += cnt[t]
        load = {a: tasks[a] * mix_mean(svc, a) for a in ACTS}
        rows.append(dict(block=row.block, n=round(n, 1), n_P1=round(n * row.P1 / 100, 2),
                         **{f"L_{a[:4]}": round(load[a]) for a in ACTS}, L_tot=round(sum(load.values()))))
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)


def capacity_table(scn, pol="HYB"):
    S, ros = scenario(scn), roster(pol)
    rows = []
    for blk in DEMAND.block:
        b0 = hm(blk); b1 = b0 + 120
        am = {r: avail_minutes(ros, r, b0, b1, S["absent"]) for r in RES}
        row = dict(block=blk, head_min=sum(am.values()))
        for a in ACTS:   # base-minute ceiling if every present qualified resource did only this skill
            row[f"home_{a[:4]}"] = round(sum(am[r] / SKILL.at[r, a] for r in RES
                                             if SKILL.at[r, a] <= RULES["home_mult_max"]))
            row[f"qual_{a[:4]}"] = round(sum(am[r] / SKILL.at[r, a] for r in RES if not np.isnan(SKILL.at[r, a])))
        rows.append(row)
    return pd.DataFrame(rows)


def fluid_lp(scn, pol="HYB", timed=True, u_cap=None):
    """Fluid bound (ignores precedence, handover, setup, discreteness, randomness).
    timed=False: minimal max individual utilization needed to clear the day's work (whole-day pooling).
    timed=True : with utilization capped at u_cap (1.0 incl. 30-min overtime, or 0.9 excl. overtime), the
                 minimal base-minutes that must carry over to the next morning; work cannot be served before
                 its arrival block. Returns (carry_total, carry_by_skill)."""
    try:
        from scipy.optimize import linprog
    except ImportError:
        return np.nan
    S, ros = scenario(scn), roster(pol)
    L = offered_load(scn); blocks = [hm(b) for b in DEMAND.block]; nb = len(blocks)
    ot = RULES["overtime_cap_min"]

    def cap(r, b):
        if not timed:
            return avail_minutes(ros, r, 0, 1440, S["absent"])
        c = avail_minutes(ros, r, blocks[b], blocks[b] + 120, S["absent"])
        if u_cap >= 1.0 and r not in S["absent"]:          # overtime lands in the block containing shift end
            e = ros[r][1]
            if blocks[b] <= e < blocks[b] + 120 or (b == nb - 1 and e >= blocks[b] + 120):
                c += ot
        return c
    if not timed:
        nb = 1
    idx = {(r, a, b): None for r in RES for a in ACTS for b in range(nb) if not np.isnan(SKILL.at[r, a])}
    for i, k in enumerate(idx):
        idx[k] = i
    nx = len(idx); ncar = len(ACTS) if timed else 0
    nv = nx + ncar + (0 if timed else 1)
    A_ub, b_ub, A_eq, b_eq = [], [], [], []
    for r in RES:
        for b in range(nb):
            row = np.zeros(nv)
            for a in ACTS:
                if (r, a, b) in idx:
                    row[idx[(r, a, b)]] = SKILL.at[r, a]
            if timed:
                A_ub.append(row); b_ub.append(u_cap * cap(r, b))
            else:
                row[nv - 1] = -cap(r, b); A_ub.append(row); b_ub.append(0.0)
    for j, a in enumerate(ACTS):
        col = f"L_{a[:4]}"
        arr = L[col].values if timed else np.array([L[col].sum()])
        for b in range(nb):
            row = np.zeros(nv)
            for r in RES:
                for bb in range(b + 1):
                    if (r, a, bb) in idx:
                        row[idx[(r, a, bb)]] = 1
            if b == nb - 1:
                if timed:
                    row[nx + j] = 1
                A_eq.append(row); b_eq.append(arr.sum())
            else:
                A_ub.append(row); b_ub.append(arr[:b + 1].sum())
    c = np.zeros(nv)
    if timed:
        c[nx:nx + ncar] = 1
    else:
        c[nv - 1] = 1
    res = linprog(c, A_ub=np.array(A_ub), b_ub=b_ub, A_eq=np.array(A_eq), b_eq=b_eq, bounds=[(0, None)] * nv,
                  method="highs")
    if not res.success:
        return np.nan
    if timed:
        return res.fun, {a[:4]: round(res.x[nx + j]) for j, a in enumerate(ACTS) if res.x[nx + j] > 0.5}
    return res.x[nv - 1]


def p1_critical_path_check(n=200_000, seed=1):
    """Zero-queue elapsed minutes of a P1 case (no waiting at all) under the handover/coordination rules."""
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)

    def draw(a):
        r = SERVICE.loc[a]; tail = rng.random(n) < r.p_tail
        return np.where(tail, rng.uniform(r.tail_lo, r.tail_hi, n), rng.uniform(r.ord_lo, r.ord_hi, n))
    I, M, E, S, V = (draw(a) for a in ACTS)
    h, hx, co = RULES["handover_min"], RULES["handover_min"] + RULES["team_change_extra_min"], RULES["parallel_coord_min"]
    paths = {
        "M : Ada intake+M -> Ben validation": I + M + h + V,
        "E : Chen intake+E -> Deepa validation": 1.10 * I + E + h + V,
        "S : Emil end-to-end (12-20)": 1.10 * I + S + V,
        "S : Chen end-to-end, S x1.25 (08-12)": 1.10 * I + 1.25 * S + 1.10 * V,
        "ME parallel: Ada I+M | Chen E -> Ben V": I + np.maximum(M + h, hx + E + hx) + co + V,
        "ME sequential: Deepa end-to-end": I + 1.25 * M + E + V,
        "ES parallel: Chen I+E | Emil S -> Emil V": 1.10 * I + np.maximum(E + h, h + S) + co + V,
        "ES sequential: Chen end-to-end": 1.10 * I + E + 1.25 * S + 1.10 * V}
    return pd.DataFrame({k: dict(mean=v.mean(), p90=np.quantile(v, .9), P_over_120=(v > 120).mean())
                         for k, v in paths.items()}).T.round(3)

class Task:
    __slots__ = ("uid", "case", "acts", "base", "plan", "stage", "prio", "preds", "coord", "qentry", "due",
                 "combined", "resumed", "assigned")
    _ids = itertools.count()

    def __init__(self, case, acts, base, stage, preds, coord, qentry, combined=False, resumed=False, plan=None):
        self.uid = next(Task._ids); self.case = case; self.acts = list(acts); self.base = list(base)
        self.plan = list(plan) if plan is not None else [MEAN_BASE[a] for a in acts]
        self.stage = stage; self.prio = case.prio; self.preds = list(preds); self.coord = coord
        self.qentry = qentry; self.due = case.due; self.combined = combined; self.resumed = resumed
        self.assigned = False


class Case:
    __slots__ = ("cid", "day", "arr", "prio", "ctype", "base", "due", "preempted", "pending", "branch_ends",
                 "mode", "done", "act_done", "ho_min", "n_ho", "setup_min", "coord_min", "offskill_min")

    def __init__(self, cid, day, arr, prio, ctype, base):
        self.cid, self.day, self.arr, self.prio, self.ctype, self.base = cid, day, arr, prio, ctype, base
        self.due = arr + RULES["sla_min"][prio]; self.preempted = 0; self.pending = 0
        self.branch_ends = []; self.mode = "single" if len(BRANCHES[ctype]) == 1 else None
        self.done = None; self.act_done = {}; self.ho_min = 0.0; self.n_ho = 0; self.setup_min = 0.0
        self.coord_min = 0.0; self.offskill_min = 0.0


def team_of(r, act):
    if act == "Mechanical":
        return "MECH"
    if act in ("Electrical", "Software"):
        return "ES"
    return TEAM_HOME[r]


def handover(pr, pteam, r, rteam):
    if pr is None or pr == r:
        return 0.0
    extra = RULES["team_change_extra_min"] if (pteam and rteam and pteam != rteam) else 0.0
    return RULES["handover_min"] + extra


class Sim:
    def __init__(self, pol, scn, seed, keep_log=False, meas_day=MEAS_DAY):
        self.pol, self.scn, self.P = pol, scn, POLICIES[pol]
        self.md = meas_day; self.nd = meas_day + 3
        self.rng = np.random.default_rng(seed); self.keep_log = keep_log
        self.S = {d: scenario(scn if d == self.md else "BASE") for d in range(self.nd)}
        self.ros = roster(pol)
        self.heap, self.seq = [], itertools.count()
        self.cases, self.queue, self.log, self.busy_iv, self.preempt_events = [], [], [], [], []
        self.st = {r: dict(task=None, until=0.0, segs=None, brk_until=-1.0, brk_taken=set(), last_fam=None,
                           last_case=None, ver=0, t0=0.0, why="") for r in RES}
        self.horizon = self.nd * 1440.0
        self._qw = None
        for d in range(self.nd):
            for r in RES:
                s, e, b, _ = self.ros[r]
                for x in (s, e, b):
                    if x is not None:
                        self.push(d * 1440 + x, "wake")
            for g, (gs, ge) in self.P["guard"].items():
                self.push(d * 1440 + hm(gs), "wake"); self.push(d * 1440 + hm(ge), "wake")
            for (o0, o1) in self.S[d]["outage"]:
                self.push(d * 1440 + o1, "wake")
        self.gen_arrivals()

    # ---- helpers
    def push(self, t, kind, payload=None):
        heapq.heappush(self.heap, (float(t), next(self.seq), kind, payload))

    def gen_arrivals(self):
        cid = 0
        for d in range(self.nd - 1):               # last day: drain only
            S = self.S[d]
            for _, row in S["demand"].iterrows():
                b0 = d * 1440 + hm(row.block)
                n = self.rng.poisson(row.n)       # ASSUMPTION: Poisson counts, uniform times inside block
                pp = np.array([row[p] for p in PRIOS], float); pp /= pp.sum()
                tp = np.array([row[t] for t in TYPES], float); tp /= tp.sum()
                for tt in np.sort(self.rng.uniform(0, 120, n)):
                    prio = PRIOS[self.rng.choice(3, p=pp)]; ctype = TYPES[self.rng.choice(5, p=tp)]
                    base = {}
                    for a in ["Intake"] + BRANCHES[ctype] + ["Validation"]:
                        s = S["service"].loc[a]
                        tail = self.rng.random() < s.p_tail   # ASSUMPTION: uniform inside each range
                        base[a] = self.rng.uniform(s.tail_lo, s.tail_hi) if tail else self.rng.uniform(s.ord_lo, s.ord_hi)
                    c = Case(f"d{d}_c{cid:03d}", d, b0 + tt, prio, ctype, base); cid += 1
                    self.cases.append(c); self.push(c.arr, "arrive", c)

    def mult(self, r, a):
        return SKILL.at[r, a]

    def present(self, r, t):
        d = int(t // 1440)
        return not (r in self.S[min(d, self.nd - 1)]["absent"])

    def shift_bounds(self, r, t):
        d = int(t // 1440); s, e, b, _ = self.ros[r]
        return d, d * 1440 + s, d * 1440 + e, (d * 1440 + b if b is not None else None)

    def in_shift(self, r, t):
        d, s, e, _ = self.shift_bounds(r, t)
        return self.present(r, t) and s <= t < e

    def in_outage(self, t):
        d = int(t // 1440); tod = t - d * 1440
        return any(o0 <= tod < o1 for (o0, o1) in self.S[min(d, self.nd - 1)]["outage"])

    def home(self, r, a):
        m = self.mult(r, a); return (not np.isnan(m)) and m <= RULES["home_mult_max"]

    def earliest(self, task, r):
        rteam = team_of(r, task.acts[0])
        if RULES["handover_consumes_resource"]:
            return max(p[0] for p in task.preds) + task.coord
        return max(p[0] + handover(p[1], p[2], r, rteam) for p in task.preds) + task.coord

    def setup_needed(self, r, task):
        st, a0 = self.st[r], task.acts[0]
        switch = a0 in DIAG and st["last_fam"] not in (None, a0) and st["last_case"] is not task.case
        return RULES["setup_switch_min"] if switch else 0.0

    def exp_dur(self, task, r):
        return sum(p * self.mult(r, a) for p, a in zip(task.plan, task.acts)) + self.setup_needed(r, task)

    def is_guard(self, r, t):
        g = self.P["guard"].get(r)
        if not g:
            return False
        tod = t % 1440
        return hm(g[0]) <= tod < hm(g[1])

    def queued_work(self, a):
        if self._qw is not None:
            return self._qw[a]
        return sum(p for q in self.queue if not q.assigned for p, aa in zip(q.plan, q.acts) if aa == a)

    def guard_released(self, r, t):
        return sum(self.queued_work(a) for a in DIAG if self.home(r, a)) > self.P["guard_release_min"]

    def gap(self, a, t):
        return not any(self.home(r, a) and self.in_shift(r, t) for r in RES)

    # ---- eligibility (returns reason string or None)
    def eligible(self, task, r, t):
        ms = [self.mult(r, a) for a in task.acts]
        if any(np.isnan(m) for m in ms):
            return None
        if task.stage == "intake" and self.in_outage(t):
            return None
        if task.combined:
            return "seq-owner" if all(m <= RULES["seq_cross_mult_max"] for m in ms) else None
        home = all(m <= RULES["home_mult_max"] for m in ms)
        e = self.P["elig"]
        if e == "home":
            return "home" if home else None
        if e == "any":
            return "home" if home else "pool-cross"
        if (self.is_guard(r, t) and task.prio == "P3" and task.stage == "diag"
                and task.due - t >= self.P["p3_promote_slack"] and not self.guard_released(r, t)):
            return None                            # P1 reserve: guard does not start un-promoted P3 diagnostics
        if home:
            return "home"
        if task.prio == "P1":
            return "overflow-P1"
        if self.gap(task.acts[0], t):
            return "overflow-gap"
        if t - task.qentry >= self.P["overflow_wait"][task.prio]:
            return "overflow-wait"
        return None

    def score(self, task, r, t, why):
        m = self.exp_dur(task, r)
        pr = PRIOS.index(task.prio)
        if self.P["score"] == "spec":
            qrank = {"diag": 0, "val": 1, "intake": 2}[task.stage]
            return (pr, qrank, task.qentry, m)
        if self.P["score"] == "edd":
            return (task.due, m)
        band = pr
        if task.prio == "P3" and task.due - t < self.P["p3_promote_slack"]:
            band = 1                               # anti-starvation promotion
        guard_bonus = 0 if (self.is_guard(r, t) and task.prio == "P1") else 1
        off = 0 if why in ("home", "seq-owner") else 1
        return (band, guard_bonus, off, task.due, self.earliest(task, r) + m)

    def can_start(self, r, task, t):
        _, s, e, _ = self.shift_bounds(r, t)
        return t + self.exp_dur(task, r) <= e + RULES["overtime_cap_min"]

    # ---- assignment / execution
    def assign(self, task, r, t, why, cands):
        st, c = self.st[r], task.case
        setup, a0 = self.setup_needed(r, task), task.acts[0]
        rteam = team_of(r, a0)
        hos = [handover(p[1], p[2], r, rteam) for p in task.preds]
        c.ho_min += sum(hos); c.n_ho += sum(h > 0 for h in hos); c.setup_min += setup
        cur = t + setup + (max(hos) if RULES["handover_consumes_resource"] else 0.0)
        segs = []
        for i, a in enumerate(task.acts):
            if i > 0 and RULES["setup_within_case"] and a != task.acts[i - 1]:
                cur += RULES["setup_switch_min"]; c.setup_min += RULES["setup_switch_min"]
            dur = task.base[i] * self.mult(r, a)
            segs.append((a, cur, cur + dur, self.mult(r, a), task.plan[i] * self.mult(r, a)))
            if not self.home(r, a) and a in DIAG:
                c.offskill_min += dur
            cur += dur
        task.assigned = True
        st.update(task=task, until=cur, segs=segs, t0=t, why=why, ver=st["ver"] + 1,
                  meta=dict(setup=setup, ho=sum(hos), cands=",".join(sorted(cands)), qentry=task.qentry,
                            resumed=task.resumed))
        if task.acts[-1] in DIAG:
            st["last_fam"] = task.acts[-1]; st["last_case"] = c
        self.push(cur, "finish", (r, st["ver"]))

    def logrow(self, c, a, t0, t1, r, life, mult, planned, meta, why):
        if not self.keep_log:
            return
        self.log.append({"case:concept:name": c.cid, "concept:name": a,
                         "start_timestamp": BASE_DATE + pd.to_timedelta(t0, "m"),
                         "time:timestamp": BASE_DATE + pd.to_timedelta(t1, "m"), "org:resource": r,
                         "lifecycle": life, "priority": c.prio, "case_type": c.ctype, "branch_mode": c.mode,
                         "policy_version": f"{self.pol}-v1",
                         "scenario_flag": self.scn if c.day == self.md else "BASE", "arrival_day": c.day,
                         "queue_entry": BASE_DATE + pd.to_timedelta(meta.get("qentry", t0), "m") if meta else pd.NaT,
                         "eligible_idle_set": meta.get("cands", "") if meta else "", "assign_reason": why,
                         "pause_resume": "pause" if life == "interrupted" else
                         ("resume" if meta and meta.get("resumed") else ""),
                         "skill_used": a if a in DIAG else "generic", "home_skill": mult <= RULES["home_mult_max"],
                         "skill_mult": mult, "planned_min": planned, "actual_min": t1 - t0,
                         "sla_clock_at_start_min": t0 - c.arr,
                         "setup_min": meta.get("setup", 0.0) if meta else 0.0,
                         "handover_min": meta.get("ho", 0.0) if meta else 0.0,
                         "sla_due": BASE_DATE + pd.to_timedelta(c.due, "m")})

    def finish(self, r, t):
        st = self.st[r]; task = st["task"]; c = task.case
        first = True
        for (a, s0, s1, m, pl) in st["segs"]:
            self.logrow(c, a, s0, s1, r, "complete", m, pl, st["meta"] if first else {"qentry": s0}, st["why"])
            c.act_done[a] = s1; first = False
        self.busy_iv.append((r, st["t0"], t))
        st["task"] = None; st["segs"] = None
        self.progress(task, r, t)

    def progress(self, task, r, t):
        c = task.case
        if task.stage == "intake":
            self.start_diag(c, r, t)
        elif task.stage == "diag":
            c.pending -= 1
            c.branch_ends.append((t, r, team_of(r, task.acts[-1])))
            if c.pending == 0:
                coord = RULES["parallel_coord_min"] if c.mode == "parallel" else 0.0
                c.coord_min += coord
                self.new_task(Task(c, ["Validation"], [c.base["Validation"]], "val", c.branch_ends, coord, t))
        else:
            c.done = t

    def new_task(self, task):
        self.queue.append(task)
        tmax = max(p[0] for p in task.preds) + task.coord
        for dt in (0, 7, 12):
            self.push(tmax + dt, "wake")
        if "overflow_wait" in self.P:
            for w in self.P["overflow_wait"].values():
                self.push(task.qentry + w, "wake")
            if "seq_timeout" in self.P and task.combined:
                self.push(task.qentry + self.P["seq_timeout"], "wake")
        if task.prio == "P1" and self.P["preempt_wait"] is not None:
            self.push(task.qentry + self.P["preempt_wait"], "wake")

    def est_wait(self, a, t):
        homes = [r for r in RES if self.home(r, a) and self.in_shift(r, t)]
        if not homes:
            return None
        return self.queued_work(a) / len(homes)

    def start_diag(self, c, r_in, t):
        br = BRANCHES[c.ctype]
        pred = [(t, r_in, team_of(r_in, "Intake"))]
        if len(br) == 1:
            c.pending = 1
            self.new_task(Task(c, br, [c.base[br[0]]], "diag", pred, 0.0, t)); return
        seq_owner = None
        if self.P["parallel"] == "seq_if_owner" and c.prio != "P1":
            owners = [o for o in RES if self.in_shift(o, t) and all(
                not np.isnan(self.mult(o, a)) and self.mult(o, a) <= RULES["seq_cross_mult_max"] for a in br)]
            if owners:
                seq_owner = owners[0]
        if self.P["parallel"] == "threshold" and c.prio != "P1":
            ws = {}
            for a in br:
                w = self.est_wait(a, t)
                ws[a] = self.P["overflow_wait"][c.prio] if w is None else w
            in_team = team_of(r_in, "Intake")
            h_in = {a: RULES["handover_min"] + (RULES["team_change_extra_min"]
                                                if in_team and in_team != team_of(r_in, a) else 0.0) for a in br}
            par = max(ws[a] + MEAN_BASE[a] + h_in[a] for a in br) + RULES["parallel_coord_min"]
            best = None
            for o in RES:
                ms = [self.mult(o, a) for a in br]
                if any(np.isnan(m) or m > RULES["seq_cross_mult_max"] for m in ms) or not self.in_shift(o, t):
                    continue
                dur = sum(MEAN_BASE[a] * self.mult(o, a) for a in br)
                if t + dur > self.shift_bounds(o, t)[2]:
                    continue
                w = min([ws[a] for a in br if self.home(o, a)] or [max(ws.values())])
                est = w + dur + (0 if o == r_in else RULES["handover_min"])
                if best is None or est < best[0]:
                    best = (est, o)
            if best is not None and best[0] <= par - self.P["seq_gain_min"]:
                seq_owner = best[1]
        if seq_owner is not None:
            c.mode = "sequential"; c.pending = 1
            self.new_task(Task(c, br, [c.base[a] for a in br], "diag", pred, 0.0, t, combined=True))
        else:
            c.mode = "parallel"; c.pending = 2
            for a in br:
                self.new_task(Task(c, [a], [c.base[a]], "diag", pred, 0.0, t))

    # ---- dispatch
    def idle(self, r, t):
        st = self.st[r]
        if st["task"] is not None or t < st["brk_until"] - 1e-9 or not self.in_shift(r, t):
            return False
        d, s, e, b = self.shift_bounds(r, t)
        if b is not None and d not in st["brk_taken"] and t >= b:
            st["brk_taken"].add(d); st["brk_until"] = t + self.ros[r][3]
            self.push(st["brk_until"], "wake"); return False
        return True

    def split_stale_sequential(self, t):
        if "seq_timeout" not in self.P:
            return
        for q in list(self.queue):
            if q.combined and not q.assigned and not q.resumed and t - q.qentry >= self.P["seq_timeout"]:
                self.queue.remove(q); c = q.case; c.mode = "parallel"; c.pending = 2
                for a, b in zip(q.acts, q.base):
                    self.new_task(Task(c, [a], [b], "diag", q.preds, 0.0, q.qentry))

    def dispatch(self, t):
        self.split_stale_sequential(t)
        while True:
            idle = [r for r in RES if self.idle(r, t)]
            if not idle:
                return
            ready = [q for q in self.queue if not q.assigned]
            self._qw = None
            self._qw = {a: self.queued_work(a) for a in ACTS}
            pairs, has_home = [], {r: False for r in idle}
            for q in ready:
                for r in idle:
                    if self.earliest(q, r) > t + 1e-9:
                        continue
                    why = self.eligible(q, r, t)
                    if why is None or not self.can_start(r, q, t):
                        continue
                    pairs.append((q, r, why)); has_home[r] |= why in ("home", "seq-owner")
            best = None; cand = {}
            for q, r, why in pairs:
                if self.P["elig"] == "hybrid" and why in ("overflow-gap", "overflow-wait") and has_home[r]:
                    continue                          # cross-skill overflow only when own home queue is empty
                cand.setdefault(q.uid, set()).add(r)
                sc = self.score(q, r, t, why)
                if best is None or sc < best[0]:
                    best = (sc, q, r, why)
            self._qw = None
            if best is None:
                return
            _, q, r, why = best
            self.queue.remove(q)
            self.assign(q, r, t, why, cand.get(q.uid, {r}))

    def try_preempt(self, t):
        W, L = self.P["preempt_wait"], self.P["preempt_lookahead"]
        if self.P.get("p1_aggressive_after") and t % 1440 >= hm(self.P["p1_aggressive_after"]):
            W, L = 0, 0
        for q in sorted([q for q in self.queue if not q.assigned and q.prio == "P1"], key=lambda x: x.qentry):
            if t - q.qentry < W - 1e-9:
                continue
            elig_busy, victims = [], []
            for r in RES:
                st = self.st[r]
                if st["task"] is None or not self.in_shift(r, t):
                    continue
                v = st["task"]
                if self.eligible(q, r, t) is None:
                    continue
                elig_busy.append(st["until"])
                if (v.stage != "diag" or v.prio == "P1" or v.case.preempted >= RULES["max_preempt_per_case"]
                        or st["until"] - t < RULES["preempt_min_remaining"] or self.earliest(q, r) > t + 1e-9
                        or not self.can_start(r, q, t)):
                    continue
                victims.append((-PRIOS.index(v.prio), self.exp_dur(q, r), -(st["until"] - t), r))
            if not victims or (L > 0 and any(u <= t + L for u in elig_busy)):
                continue
            r = sorted(victims)[0][3]
            self.preempt(r, t)
            self.assign(q, r, t, "preempt", {r})
            self.queue.remove(q)

    def preempt(self, r, t):
        st = self.st[r]; v = st["task"]; c = v.case
        acts, base, plan = [], [], []
        for i, (a, s0, s1, m, pl) in enumerate(st["segs"]):
            if s1 <= t:
                self.logrow(c, a, s0, s1, r, "complete", m, pl, st["meta"], st["why"]); c.act_done[a] = s1
            elif s0 < t:
                self.logrow(c, a, s0, t, r, "interrupted", m, pl, st["meta"], st["why"])
                rem = (s1 - t) / m * (1 + RULES["preempt_restart_frac"])
                acts.append(a); base.append(rem); plan.append(v.plan[i] * rem / v.base[i])
            else:                                 # not started (incl. interrupted during setup): no penalty
                acts.append(a); base.append(v.base[i]); plan.append(v.plan[i])
        self.busy_iv.append((r, st["t0"], t))
        c.preempted += 1
        self.preempt_events.append((t, r, c.cid, v.prio))
        nt = Task(c, acts, base, "diag", [(t, r, team_of(r, acts[0]))], 0.0, t,
                  combined=len(acts) > 1, resumed=True, plan=plan)
        st["task"] = None; st["segs"] = None; st["ver"] += 1
        self.new_task(nt)

    # ---- main loop
    def run(self):
        while self.heap:
            t, _, kind, p = heapq.heappop(self.heap)
            if t > self.horizon:
                break
            self.handle(t, kind, p)
            while self.heap and self.heap[0][0] <= t + 1e-9:
                _, _, k2, p2 = heapq.heappop(self.heap); self.handle(t, k2, p2)
            self.dispatch(t)
            if self.P["preempt_wait"] is not None:
                self.try_preempt(t)
        return self

    def handle(self, t, kind, p):
        if kind == "arrive":
            c = p
            self.new_task(Task(c, ["Intake"], [c.base["Intake"]], "intake", [(t, None, None)], 0.0, t))
        elif kind == "finish":
            r, ver = p
            if self.st[r]["ver"] == ver and self.st[r]["task"] is not None:
                self.finish(r, t)

    # ---- metrics for cases arriving on the measured day
    def metrics(self):
        d0, d1 = self.md * 1440, (self.md + 1) * 1440
        cs = [c for c in self.cases if c.day == self.md]
        rows = []
        for c in cs:
            done = c.done if c.done is not None else self.horizon
            br = BRANCHES[c.ctype]
            ideal = c.base["Intake"] + max(c.base[a] for a in br) + c.base["Validation"]
            rows.append(dict(prio=c.prio, ctype=c.ctype, block=f"{int((c.arr - d0) // 120) * 2:02d}:00",
                             lead=done - c.arr, delay=done - c.arr - ideal,
                             met=(c.done is not None and done - c.arr <= RULES["sla_min"][c.prio]),
                             burden=c.ho_min + c.setup_min + c.coord_min, nho=c.n_ho, setup=c.setup_min,
                             off=c.offskill_min, seq=(c.mode == "sequential")))
        cdf = pd.DataFrame(rows)
        util = {}
        for r in RES:
            if r in self.S[self.md]["absent"]:
                continue
            s, e, b, bl = self.ros[r]
            net = (e - s) - (bl if b is not None else 0)
            busy = sum(max(0, min(t1, d1) - max(t0, d0)) for (rr, t0, t1) in self.busy_iv if rr == r and t0 >= 0)
            util[r] = busy / net
        ot = {}
        for r in util:
            e = d0 + self.ros[r][1]
            ot[r] = sum(max(0, t1 - max(t0, e)) for (rr, t0, t1) in self.busy_iv if rr == r and d0 <= t0 < d1)
        snap = d0 + hm("20:30")
        backlog = sum(c.base[a] for c in self.cases if c.arr <= snap
                      for a in c.base if not (a in c.act_done and c.act_done[a] <= snap))
        u = pd.Series(util)
        return dict(cases=cdf, util=u, max_util=u.max(), util_range=u.max() - u.min(), backlog=backlog,
                    ot_breach=sum(v > RULES["overtime_cap_min"] + 1e-6 for v in ot.values()),
                    preempt=sum(1 for (t, *_rest) in self.preempt_events if d0 <= t < d1))


def run_one(job):
    pol, scn, seed, md = job
    return job[:3], Sim(pol, scn, seed, meas_day=md).run().metrics()


def evaluate(pols, scns, reps, seed0=1000, workers=16, meas_day=MEAS_DAY, return_cases=False):
    jobs = [(p, s, seed0 + k, meas_day) for p in pols for s in scns for k in range(reps)]   # common random numbers
    try:
        import multiprocessing as mp
        with mp.get_context("fork").Pool(workers) as pool:
            done = dict(pool.map(run_one, jobs, chunksize=4))
    except Exception:                                                          # portable fallback
        done = dict(map(run_one, jobs))
    out, allc = [], []
    for pol in pols:
        for scn in scns:
            cases, reprow = [], []
            for k in range(reps):
                m = done[(pol, scn, seed0 + k)]
                cases.append(m["cases"])
                reprow.append(dict(mean_delay=m["cases"].delay.mean(), max_util=m["max_util"],
                                   util_range=m["util_range"], backlog=m["backlog"], preempt=m["preempt"],
                                   ot_breach=m["ot_breach"], util=m["util"]))
            C = pd.concat(cases); R = pd.DataFrame(reprow)
            allc.append(C.assign(policy=pol, scenario=scn))
            sla = C.groupby("prio").met.mean()
            nP1 = int((C.prio == "P1").sum())
            U = pd.DataFrame(list(R.util)).mean()
            out.append(dict(policy=pol, scenario=scn,
                            mean_delay=C.delay.mean(),
                            md_lo=R.mean_delay.quantile(0.1), md_hi=R.mean_delay.quantile(0.9),
                            p90_delay=C.delay.quantile(0.9), p95_delay=C.delay.quantile(0.95),
                            P1_SLA=sla.get("P1", np.nan), P2_SLA=sla.get("P2", np.nan), P3_SLA=sla.get("P3", np.nan),
                            n_P1=nP1, P1_ci=1.96 * math.sqrt(max(sla.get("P1", 0) * (1 - sla.get("P1", 0)), 1e-9) / max(nP1, 1)),
                            max_util=R.max_util.mean(), util_range=R.util_range.mean(),
                            over90=int((U > RULES["fair_util_cap"]).sum()),
                            burden=C.burden.mean(), ho_per_case=C.nho.mean(), setup_per_case=C.setup.mean(),
                            offskill=C.off.mean(), seq_share=C.seq.mean(),
                            preempt=R.preempt.mean(), backlog_2030=R.backlog.mean(), ot_breach=R.ot_breach.mean(),
                            util_by_res="/".join(f"{r[0]}{100*U[r]:.0f}" for r in U.index)))
    return (pd.DataFrame(out), pd.concat(allc)) if return_cases else pd.DataFrame(out)


def robust_scores(res):
    sh = lambda x: np.maximum(0, 100 * RULES["sla_target"] - 100 * x)   # SLA shortfall in pp
    res = res.copy()
    res["J_tail"] = 3 * sh(res.P1_SLA) + 1 * sh(res.P2_SLA) + 0.5 * sh(res.P3_SLA) + 0.02 * res.p90_delay
    res["J_fair"] = res.J_tail + 0.5 * 100 * np.maximum(0, res.max_util - RULES["fair_util_cap"]) \
        + 0.2 * 100 * res.util_range
    res["regret"] = res.J_fair - res.groupby("scenario").J_fair.transform("min")
    base = res[res.scenario == "BASE"].set_index("policy")
    agg = res.groupby("policy").agg(J_tail_mean=("J_tail", "mean"),
                                    J_fair_mean=("J_fair", "mean"), J_fair_worst=("J_fair", "max"),
                                    max_regret=("regret", "max"),
                                    worst_P1=("P1_SLA", "min"), worst_P2=("P2_SLA", "min"))
    agg.insert(0, "J_tail_base", base.J_tail); agg.insert(1, "base_mean_delay", base.mean_delay)
    return res, agg.sort_values("max_regret")

        print(f"  {a:>6} -> {c:<6} {v:.3f}")
    print(f"\nruntime {time.time() - t_start:.0f}s")
```

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## 9.1 What cannot be concluded from expected arrivals and service ranges alone

- **Whether the base is actually over capacity.** The deficit rests on range midpoints. The admissible load spans 2,065 to 3,376 base-minutes (ρ 0.83–1.36). The *shape* inside each range, the complexity-tail probabilities, and the reliability of the multipliers all have to be estimated from real interval logs.
- **Multi-day dynamics.** If ρ > 1 persists, backlog grows without bound and every delay figure depends on the horizon. The one-day warm-up makes the numbers conditional, "a typical day after one base day". Real operations absorb the deficit through informal overtime, deferred P3 work or triage, none of which is in the data.
- **Behavioural and organisational effects.** Compliance with the rules, fatigue near shift end, learning, setup costs that depend on context, and the real cost of interruption (possibly far above 10%) are all unknown.
- **The mechanism behind the handover delays** (case delay or receiver effort), and whether coordination can overlap with handover.
- **Correlated complexity** within a case, and burstiness beyond Poisson. Both would fatten the tails for ME/ES and P1.
- **P1 SLA differences of a few percentage points.** There are only about 5.4 P1 cases per day. Detecting 0.85 against 0.90 with 80% power needs about 680 P1 cases per arm, i.e. about 125 working days per arm. P1 comparisons in any pilot must therefore rely on proxies such as P1 queue wait and slack at each step, plus simulation.

## 9.2 Validation plan that protects P1

1. **Calibration backtest (weeks 0–4, offline).**
   - Extract a historical interval log with queue entry, start and complete events.
   - Fit service distributions per activity × resource: check the ranges, the tail probabilities, the multipliers, and the correlation of complexity between branches.
   - Replay the historical arrival streams through the simulator under the *current* policy and check that it reproduces the observed KPIs (for example waits per stage within ±15%).
   - Only then compare SPEC, POOL, HYB, HYB_R and HYB_RF by trace-driven simulation.
   - Re-estimate the Section 2 load table; if ρ < 0.9, the recommendation shifts towards the simpler HYB.
2. **Shadow mode (2–4 weeks).** The dispatcher computes HYB assignments in parallel with current practice, without acting.
   - Log recommendation against actual decision, with reasons.
   - Measure agreement and the counterfactual effect (simulated), and test whether operators understand the rule card.
   - No operational risk.
3. **Staged live pilot with a P1 safety envelope.**
   - The **P1 fast lane** is identical in every arm: guards, P1 always parallel, preemption rules. Pilot arms differ only in P2/P3 overflow, aging and parallelization thresholds.
   - Use alternating weeks (ABAB) rather than a split team, because the resources are shared.
   - **Stop rules:**
     - any P1 waiting > 20 min for a step;
     - rolling-20 P1 SLA < 0.80;
     - more than 2 overtime-cap breaches per week;
     - backlog at 20:30 above 600 base-min on 3 consecutive days.
   - **Primary endpoints, measurable in weeks:**
     - P2 same-day completion for arrivals from 14:00 (about 9.5 per day, so ±15 percentage points is detectable in about 3–4 weeks per arm);
     - backlog at 20:30;
     - maximum P3 age;
     - preemptions per day;
     - setup minutes per case.
   - Analyse with regression on daily arrivals, the P1 share and absences, so that load is not mistaken for policy.
4. **Roster trial.** Ben at 10:00–18:00, and a fixed-term 4-hour evening block for Farah, for 4–6 weeks each.
   - Evaluate with an interrupted time series on 14:00+ P2 SLA and evening backlog, with the mode flags recorded.
   - Keep the late-P1 escalation path active throughout.
5. **Shock validation.** When a real surge, absence or outage occurs, compare the observed behaviour (mode trigger timing, queue migration, P1 waits) with the matching U-scenario prediction.
   - A mismatch is a model-error signal, and the thresholds are re-tuned out-of-sample.
   - The evening aggressive-P1 rule was itself derived from the block-level diagnostic in Section 4, so it must be confirmed on new data.
6. **Rollback.** Every change is policy-versioned in the log. If any stop rule fires, revert to the last version that met the envelope, and investigate with the process-mining queries in §7.3.
