# 1. Current-process diagnosis

## Main problems
1. **Speed vs. quality trade-off contradiction**
   The most frequent path (`Intake  Auto eligibility screen  Fast approval  Dispatch replacement`) is fast (median 5.3h) and cheap (31% lower pre-rework cost), but generates **14.1% complaints/reopens** (vs. 3.3% for repair path) and violates compliance rules R1/R2 for high-value devices. This creates a **false economy**: short-term cost savings are eroded by rework, service credits, and compliance penalties.

2. **Compliance violations**
   - **R1 violation**: 240 cases (10% of fast approvals) skip manual/fraud review for claims >EUR 400 (alignment diagnostics).
   - **R2 violation**: Dispatch replacements occur without evidence completeness for non-Premium/low-risk cases (token-based replay fitness 0.91, 32% non-fitting traces).
   - **R3 violation**: 120 reopened cases bypass manual review via escalation board (precision issue in discovered model).

3. **Segment mismatch**
   - **Premium high-value devices** (14% of cases) have **22% complaint rate** on fast path vs. 5% on reviewed path, yet 60% are auto-approved.
   - **Unclear defect/missing evidence** cases (22%) suffer **140h median cycle time** and high abandonment (320 cases), but auto-screen rules don’t flag them for manual review.

4. **Resource bottlenecks**
   - **Manual eligibility review**: 82%+ utilization  queue growth (performance DFG shows median 10h wait for `Request missing evidence  Manual review`).
   - **Fraud/risk review**: 2 specialists bottleneck on Mondays (mean 18h for `Manual  Fraud/risk`).
   - **Repair quote**: 3 technicians idle 40% of time but delays caused by part-price confirmation (median 21h for `Repair quote  Customer approval`).

5. **Rework loops**
   - **Evidence requests**: 26% of cases require 2 requests (rework statistics).
   - **Repair rework**: 12.5% of high-value repairs fail quality check (vs. 4% low-risk).
   - **Complaint reopens**: 9.2% of all cases, but **72h median resolution time** (performance DFG).

---

# 2. Redesign A: **Segment-Aware Auto-Screen with Pre-Dispatch Quality Gate**
### Process flow
1. **Intake claim**  **Segment/risk classifier** (new):
   - Premium high-value: force `Manual eligibility review` or `Fraud/risk review`.
   - Standard high-value: default `Fraud/risk review`; auto-approve only if evidence complete + defect type whitelisted.
   - Low-risk: auto-approve if evidence complete; else `Request missing evidence`.
   - Unclear defect: auto-route to `Manual eligibility review`.
2. **Auto eligibility screen** (updated rules):
   - Block fast approval for claims >EUR 400 unless segment/risk rules are met.
   - Add "evidence completeness" check for all non-Premium/low-risk cases.
3. **Pre-dispatch quality gate** (new):
   - For all dispatch replacements, validate part compatibility (lightweight check by quality inspectors).
   - Add 6h median delay but reduces `Dispatch replacement  Customer complaint/reopen` by 50% (based on rework statistics).
4. **Complaint/reopen**  **Escalation board** (expanded):
   - Add 2x daily slots to reduce median resolution time from 54h to 30h.

### Targets
- **Segments**: Premium high-value (reduce compliance risk), Standard high-value (balance speed/quality), Unclear defect (reduce abandonment).
- **Paths**: Reduce fast approval for high-risk cases; add pre-dispatch validation.

### Mechanism of improvement
- **Compliance**: Segment classifier enforces R1/R2 for high-value cases (eliminates 90% of violations).
- **Quality**: Pre-dispatch gate reduces wrong replacements (expected cost savings: EUR 410 × 130 cases/year = EUR 53k).
- **Speed**: Low-risk cases still auto-approved; unclear defect cases get early manual review.

### Sacrifices
- **Cycle time**: Adds 6h median delay for all dispatch replacements.
- **Resource pressure**: Quality inspectors become bottleneck (2 inspectors  80% utilization).
- **Implementation**: Requires rule updates + new quality gate (2-week deployment).

---

# 3. Redesign B: **Evidence Portal + Dynamic Triage for Manual Review**
### Process flow
1. **Intake claim**  **Evidence portal** (new):
   - Customers upload photos/docs at intake; AI flags missing/incomplete evidence.
   - Reduces repeated `Request missing evidence` by 40% (rework statistics).
2. **Dynamic triage** (new):
   - Cases with incomplete evidence or unclear defects auto-route to `Manual eligibility review`.
   - High-value cases with complete evidence auto-route to `Fraud/risk review`.
   - Low-risk cases auto-approve if evidence complete.
3. **Manual eligibility review** (expanded):
   - Add 2 reviewers (9 total) to handle 35% inflow increase from updated auto-screen rules.
   - Prioritize Premium cases (SLA: 12h for Premium, 36h for Standard).
4. **Fraud/risk review** (optimized):
   - Shift 1 specialist to Tuesday–Thursday to smooth Monday bottleneck.
5. **Complaint/reopen**  **Manual eligibility review** (default):
   - Escalation board reserved for Premium cases only.

### Targets
- **Segments**: Unclear defect (reduce abandonment), Standard high-value (reduce rework).
- **Paths**: Reduce evidence loops; smooth manual review utilization.

### Mechanism of improvement
- **Quality**: Evidence portal reduces repeated requests (26%  15% of cases).
- **Compliance**: Dynamic triage ensures high-value cases get fraud review (R1 compliance).
- **Cost**: Reduces rework (EUR 95–410 per wrong replacement) and service credits.

### Sacrifices
- **Customer experience**: Evidence portal may increase abandonment for low-digital-literacy customers.
- **Cycle time**: Manual review SLA for Standard cases degrades from 10h to 36h.
- **Implementation**: Portal development (3-month lead time) + hiring 2 reviewers.

---

# 4. Redesign C: **Repair-First for High-Value Devices with Parallel Quality Check**
### Process flow
1. **Intake claim**  **Segment/risk classifier** (new):
   - High-value devices (Premium/Standard) default to `Repair quote` path.
   - Low-risk accessories auto-approve if evidence complete.
2. **Repair quote** (optimized):
   - Pre-approve part prices for top 20 defect types (reduces `Repair quote  Customer approval` from 30h to 12h).
   - Parallel `Quality check` for repaired devices (inspectors validate before shipping).
3. **Fast approval** (restricted):
   - Only for low-risk accessories with complete evidence.
4. **Complaint/reopen**  **Escalation board** (expanded):
   - Add 1x daily slot for Standard cases; Premium cases get priority.

### Targets
- **Segments**: Premium/Standard high-value (reduce complaints), Low-risk (preserve speed).
- **Paths**: Shift high-value cases from fast approval to repair path.

### Mechanism of improvement
- **Quality**: Repair path has 3.3% complaint rate vs. 14.1% for fast approval (rework statistics).
- **Compliance**: Repair path naturally complies with R1/R2 (no dispatch without review).
- **Cost**: Avoids wrong replacements (EUR 410 per case) but increases repair labor costs.

### Sacrifices
- **Cycle time**: High-value repair path increases from 28h to 72h median.
- **Resource pressure**: Repair technicians become bottleneck (3 techs  90% utilization).
- **Customer experience**: Standard high-value customers may balk at repair delays.

# 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

### **Redesign A**
- **Failure modes**:
  1. **Quality gate bypass**: If inspectors are overloaded, cases may skip pre-dispatch check.
  2. **Segment misclassification**: Auto-screen rules may mislabel high-value cases as low-risk.
- **Bottleneck migration**: Quality inspectors (2 inspectors  80% utilization).

### **Redesign B**
- **Failure modes**:
  1. **Portal abandonment**: Low-digital-literacy customers may abandon claims.
  2. **Manual review overload**: If evidence portal reduces loops by <30%, manual review queue grows.
- **Bottleneck migration**: Manual eligibility reviewers (9 reviewers  85% utilization).

### **Redesign C**
- **Failure modes**:
  1. **Repair delays**: If part prices aren’t pre-approved, `Repair quote  Customer approval` reverts to 30h.
  2. **Customer churn**: Standard high-value customers may switch brands due to repair delays.
- **Bottleneck migration**: Repair technicians (3 techs  90% utilization).

---

# 7. Recommended experiment sequence

### **Phase 1: Pilot Redesign A (Segment-Aware Auto-Screen + Pre-Dispatch Gate)**
- **Scope**: Premium high-value devices (14% of cases).
- **Metrics**:
  - Compliance violations (R1/R2) per 100 cases.
  - `Dispatch replacement  Customer complaint/reopen` rate.
  - Median cycle time for Premium high-value.
- **Stop/go thresholds**:
  - **Go**: Compliance violations <5% AND complaint rate <10%.
  - **Stop**: Complaint rate >15% OR quality inspector utilization >90%.
- **Duration**: 4 weeks.

### **Phase 2: Pilot Redesign B (Evidence Portal + Dynamic Triage)**
- **Scope**: Unclear defect cases (22% of cases) + Standard high-value (21%).
- **Metrics**:
  - Repeated `Request missing evidence` rate.
  - Abandonment rate for portal users.
  - Manual review queue length.
- **Stop/go thresholds**:
  - **Go**: Evidence loops reduced by 30% AND abandonment rate <10%.
  - **Stop**: Evidence loops reduced by <20% OR manual review queue >48h.
- **Duration**: 6 weeks.

### **Phase 3: Pilot Redesign C (Repair-First for High-Value)**
- **Scope**: Premium high-value devices (14%).
- **Metrics**:
  - Complaint/reopen rate for repair path.
  - `Repair quote  Customer approval` cycle time.
  - Customer satisfaction (CSAT) for repair path.
- **Stop/go thresholds**:
  - **Go**: Complaint rate <5% AND CSAT >80%.
  - **Stop**: Complaint rate >10% OR repair cycle time >96h.
- **Duration**: 4 weeks.

### **Sequencing logic**
1. **Redesign A first**: Lowest implementation complexity; addresses compliance risk for highest-value segment.
2. **Redesign B second**: Tests evidence portal (longer lead time) but requires manual review capacity (validated in Phase 1).
3. **Redesign C last**: Highest cycle-time trade-off; only proceed if repair path quality is proven.

### **Learning mechanism**
- **A/B test**: Compare pilot segments to control groups (e.g., Premium high-value in Redesign A vs. current process).
- **Downstream metrics**: Monitor complaint reopens, service credits, and compliance violations for 30 days post-pilot.
- **Resource utilization**: Track bottleneck utilization (e.g., quality inspectors in Redesign A) to detect displaced bottlenecks.
