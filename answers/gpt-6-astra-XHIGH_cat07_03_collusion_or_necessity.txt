# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Evidence increasing concern:**

- Authenticated, action-specific audit entries establish the same human changed bank details and approved the affected payment, or created and approved the same PO, after correcting shared-account and bot mappings. This confirms a control exception; evidence of deliberate cooperation is still a separate question.
- A payment was released to a bank-account version changed immediately beforehand, without required independent verification or renewed authorization. Inspect bank-account history, verification time, approval time, release time, payment reference, beneficiary, and actual amount.
- V-77x maintenance records fail to cover the flagged vendors, authorized beneficiary details conflict with payment instructions, or unrelated vendors unexpectedly share an account without a documented explanation. Verify legal entities and legitimate shared treasury arrangements before drawing conclusions.
- Access logs, delegation records, and document histories corroborate intentional bypasses—for example, authenticated approvals before supporting documents existed, unauthorized use of another person's account, or deliberate alteration of approval evidence. Shared IP addresses or a shared account alone are insufficient.
- Original justification records confirm late or same-author review, emergency designations were added only after processing, or incident records contradict claimed urgency. These findings increase concern about control operation without alone proving collusion.
- Excess pair concentration and unusually rapid approval persist after credible adjustment for role assignments, emergency mix, source systems, calendar coverage, and event-time quality, and coincide with unsupported payments or repeated override abuse.

**Evidence reducing concern:**

- Original authenticated approvals identify different authorized humans, and a reproducible legacy_erp mapping repair explains the false same-resource results across the affected population.
- Versioned source records establish that independently authored justifications occurred within the applicable deadline and that quarter_close_upload timestamps describe migration rather than review. This reduces substantive exception counts while leaving a logging weakness to remediate.
- Authorized V-77x migration tickets map completely to vendors, bank-account changes, and payments; verified vendor notices, independent bank checks, and payment reconciliation confirm the intended beneficiaries and sequencing.
- Emergency incident records, weekend duty rosters, delegations, and contemporaneous procurement review explain expedited approvals; valid independent retrospective review satisfies the required safeguard.
- Assignment rules and suitably matched comparisons explain pair frequencies and loop rates; comments and version histories show substantive clarification or correction within those loops.
- Original business timestamps explain apparent speed, lifecycle/job records explain repeated bot events, and a reconciled control sample shows no unexplained missing or altered transactions.

An isolated clean document or corrected row should not clear an entire cluster. Conversely, a confirmed documentation or segregation failure should be reported accurately without treating it as evidence of improper intent that has not been established.

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Our review identified reported procurement and payment control exceptions that warrant targeted follow-up. The available evidence is consistent with a combination of established team assignments, emergency processing, and deficiencies in event attribution and timestamp quality. It does not establish fraud, collusion, or improper benefit.

The most concerning subgroup involves VendorMaster_Lena and AP_Kim. Its combination of bank-detail changes, payment approvals, high-value transactions, vendor-family concentration, and manual-source records creates a stronger basis for review than repeated collaboration alone. Nine reported bank-change/payment segregation exceptions and sixteen unusually fast manual-upload cases require reconciliation to determine their overlap and actual actors. The recent V-77x banking-platform migration offers a plausible explanation, but maintenance authorizations are not linked to procurement cases in the supplied log.

Rapid approvals involving Buyer_Carla/Approver_Gita and Buyer_Dan/Manager_Hugo are compatible with emergency medical procurement and weekend repairs. Nonetheless, emergency status does not itself demonstrate compliance with segregation requirements or timely, independent retrospective justification. Frequent routine buyer/approver pairings and resource-return loops are not sufficient evidence of misconduct.

We recommend validating the highest-exposure bank-change/payment cases against vendor-maintenance tickets, authenticated approval records, beneficiary details, and payment-release histories. Emergency exceptions should be checked against delegation records, incident evidence, and original justification authorship and dates.

Reported exception counts remain provisional because shared accounts, bot-to-human mapping errors, and quarter-close uploads may distort identity, order, and duration. Management should reconcile these records and preserve source evidence. Confirmed control failures should be distinguished from unresolved logging issues, with further investigation guided by corroborated transaction evidence.
