

# 1. Model scope and state variables

**Boundary:** The digital twin simulates the internal municipal workflow from submission to permit issuance, excluding external construction phases. It captures case interactions and resource constraints.

**Entities:**
*   **Case:** Unique building-permit application (e.g., P001). Contains attributes: case_type, district, architect_id, application_value, requires reviews.
*   **Resource:** Human roles (clerk, planner, inspector) and system.
*   **Queue:** Activity-specific queues (e.g., Planning review queue).
*   **Bundle:** Group of cases sharing architect/district scheduled for joint inspection.

**State Variables:**
*   **Case Status:** Current activity, elapsed time, completed activities.
*   **Resource State:** Busy/Idle, assigned activity, location (for inspectors), availability calendar.
*   **Queue Lengths:** Number of cases waiting per activity.
*   **Bundle State:** Accumulating cases pending geographically efficient grouping.
*   **System Time:** Current simulation clock.

**Resources & Calendars:**
*   Resources have fixed shifts (e.g., Fire reviewers Tue-Thu).
*   Legal officers have blackout periods for council meetings.
*   Inspectors have district-dependent travel time multipliers.

**Cross-case Dependencies:**
*   **Architect Bundling:** Cases by same architect in same district within window $W$ can group for inspection.
*   **District Travel:** Inspectors grouped by district to reduce travel.
*   **Rework Loops:** Decision rejections trigger return to specific activities based on variant.

# 2. Parameter-estimation plan

1.  **Event Log Analysis:** Use PM4Py to compute process variants, variants of F, and performance data. Calculate wait times per activity.
2.  **Arrival Processes:** Fit time-dependent Poisson rates by month and weekday. Apply seasonality factor (1.7x peak vs baseline) and daily multiplier (2.2x Mon).
3.  **Service Times:**
    *   **Human activities:** Fit parametric distributions (e.g., Weibull) to median/p90 data if start logs are missing, infer from completion minus average processing time.
    *   **System tasks:** Fixed or short uniform distributions.
4.  **Routing:** Estimate branching probabilities from the event-log variant summary (e.g., 18% of cases require fire + notice).
5.  **Resource Calendars:** Map HR schedules to simulation calendars. Deduct council meetings explicitly for Legal check activity.
6.  **Dependencies:** Use log clustering (architect_id, district) to estimate bundle size probabilities. Assume bundling window based on submission proximity.
7.  **Hidden Variables:** Model objection impact as a distribution over Public-notice duration, rather than explicit events, given data gaps.

# 3. Assumptions

*   **Arrivals:** Non-stationary Poisson process. Monday spikes and seasonal peaks follow provided factors.
*   **Service Times:** Service and waiting are distinct. Waiting is queue-dependent; service is resource-dependent. If start timestamps missing, infer waiting as (Completion - Median Service - Previous Completion).
*   **Routing:** Branching is static based on case_type and district rules, unless policy changes intervene.
*   **Resources:** Resource skills are binary (competent to do task). No multitasking within an activity.
*   **Rework:** Rework probabilities derived from observed loops in the log. Unstructured text reasons are treated as stochastic triggers.
*   **Applicant Responses:** Modeled as a separate agent with heavy-tailed distribution (e.g., Lognormal) for missing document returns.
*   **Cross-case:** Bundling occurs only for site inspection. Other reviews remain independent.
*   **Non-stationary:** Demand seasonality is known and applied as multipliers to base rates.
*   **Missing Data:** Objection events are unobserved; their impact is absorbed into Public-notice duration variability.

# 5. Warm-up, replications, and uncertainty treatment

*   **Warm-up:** 3 months simulation time (to reach steady state under seasonal demand).
*   **Run Length:** 18 months (matches historical log duration).
*   **Replications:** 30 independent replications per configuration to ensure statistical power.
*   **Random Seeds:** Fixed seeds per replication for reproducibility.
*   **Uncertainty:** 95% Confidence Intervals (CIs) on key metrics (total duration, throughput).
*   **Sensitivity:** Vary service time distributions (p5/p95 vs median/p90) to test robustness.
*   **Heavy Tails:** Model applicant response and long-duration variants using Pareto or Lognormal distributions to capture extreme outliers (p90, p99).
*   **Validation of Tails:** Ensure simulated p90 service times match empirical p90 within 10% tolerance.

# 6. Validation strategy

1.  **Face Validation:** Review process flow and logic with process experts to confirm calendar and rework rules match reality.
2.  **Event-Log Replay:** Simulate the provided 18-month log input and compare generated statistics against the provided summary (case counts, median durations).
3.  **Distributional Validation:** Compare empirical histograms of waiting times and durations against simulation outputs.
4.  **Resource Utilization:** Verify utilization rates match empirical expectations (e.g., Planners at 70-85%, Legal at lower rates due to meetings).
5.  **Bottleneck Validation:** Ensure simulation bottlenecks align with observed bottlenecks (e.g., Legal check, Inspection scheduling).
6.  **Out-of-Sample:** Validate with separate recent months data if available, or hold out specific districts for testing.

# 7. Decision criteria

*   **Median Duration:** Primary metric for efficiency. Target: <50% reduction in tail duration for Commercial cases.
*   **Tail Duration (p90):** Risk mitigation. Target: <260 days for Complex variance.
*   **SLA Compliance:** Percentage of cases processed within statutory limit. Threshold: 90% compliance.
*   **Fairness:** Gini coefficient or max disparity in mean duration across Districts D3/D5 vs others.
*   **Resource Utilization:** Avoid overutilization (>90%). Target 70-85% for key roles.
*   **Backlog:** Average queue length at peak periods.
*   **Robustness:** Performance variance under Stress scenarios. Lower variance preferred.
