# 3. Risk assessment

### Bottleneck Risk
* **Nature & Mechanics**: Mira is not an in-service processing bottleneck (her active handling time is 4.2 minutes), but she represents a single-channel transit bottleneck. Because her betweenness centrality is 0.77 and out-degree centrality is 0.82, exception workflows must pass sequentially through her workstation.
* **Calibrated Assessment**: The risk is highly localized to tail latency. Routine claims (78% of volume) bypass her completely with minimal cycle-time impact. However, for non-standard claims, any disruption at her desk inflates waiting times and variance. The 6-day absence confirmed this: 90th percentile cycle time for exception claims escalated from 8.4 to 13.9 days (+65.5%), demonstrating that unserviced queues form rapidly when her clearing capacity is unavailable.

### Coordination Risk
* **Nature & Mechanics**: A structural disconnect exists between centralized exception dispatching and decentralized regional execution.
* **Calibrated Assessment**: Mira's low co-occurrence with regional team leads (`Alice`: 5%, `Bruno`: 4%) and adjusters (6–8%) indicates that she reallocates work without participating in operational team planning. When Mira routes complex rework or reopened claims to adjusters (`Carla`: 350, `Diego`: 330, `Elise`: 290, `Farid`: 280), regional leads lack immediate visibility into these incoming demands, risking mismatched capacity planning, fragmented ownership, and conflicting priorities between routine adjusting and exception rework.

### Key-Person Risk
* **Nature & Mechanics**: Severe organizational vulnerability stemming from reliance on undocumented, individual routing heuristics without backup redundancy.
* **Calibrated Assessment**: Qualitative evidence indicates that adjusters rely on Mira because she "knows where hard cases should go." There is no secondary coordinator, deputy, or documented routing manual in the log. If Mira leaves the organization, takes extended leave, or becomes incapacitated, the exception resolution pipeline will experience immediate structural failure, as demonstrated by the accumulation of backlogged cases during her 6-day absence.

### Compliance and Control Risk
* **Nature & Mechanics**: Concentration of discretionary authority across multiple critical stages in a regulated claims environment without dual control (Segregation of Duties / Four-Eyes principle).
* **Calibrated Assessment**: The compliance reviewer observes that Mira is "both detecting problems and selecting the next specialist, which may create uncontrolled discretion." Subcontracting networks show she initiates specialist assignments (126 medical, 98 legal) and handles post-review actions.
* *Calibration Note*: There is no evidence of fraudulent steering, external kickbacks, or bad-faith routing. The risk is strictly structural and regulatory: an individual operator exercises unmonitored discretion over specialist procurement, complaint escalations, and claim reopenings without mandatory supervisory sign-off or transparent rule-based audit trails, exposing the organization to regulatory compliance audit failures.

### Workload Fairness Risk
* **Nature & Mechanics**: Suboptimal or inequitable distribution of high-friction exception claims across adjuster experience tiers and regional operating units.
* **Calibrated Assessment**: Mira distributes handoffs roughly equally across senior adjusters (`Carla`: 350, `Diego`: 330) and junior adjusters (`Elise`: 290, `Farid`: 280). However, junior adjusters have shorter baseline service times (17–18 min vs. 22–24 min for seniors) and lack experience with complex disputes. Pushing high proportions of complex rework and missing-information escalations to junior adjusters risks cognitive overload, errors, or delays, while diverting senior adjusters from high-value settlement negotiations.

### Data-Quality and Telemetry Risk
* **Nature & Mechanics**: Coarse activity semantics, missing lifecycle transitions, and aggregated performance indicators that distort operational measurement.
* **Calibrated Assessment**:
  * Activity aggregation: *Request Missing Information* is executed by Mira (median service 4.2 min, 48% exception rate), junior adjusters (17–18 min, 35–36% exception rate), and temporary staff (8–9 min, 13–14% exception rate). Lumping these together under identical activity labels obscures that temporary staff handle routine intake while Mira handles contentious escalations.
  * Metric misinterpretation: Elapsed transit times (42 min to Omar, 51 min to Priya) can easily be misinterpreted as execution delay by Mira, whereas they reflect downstream queueing.
  * Absence of explicit lifecycle states (`start`, `complete`, `suspend`) prevents precise mathematical separation of active touch time from passive queue wait time across the exception pipeline.

  * Interview Mira and document her tacit routing logic into explicit Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
  * Establish clear, objective criteria for when a claim requires medical review (`Omar`), legal review (`Priya`), or assignment to senior vs. junior adjusters. This addresses the compliance reviewer's concern regarding uncontrolled discretion without introducing procedural friction.

  * Maintain Mira's operational desk. Removing her without a validated replacement mechanism would immediately collapse exception throughput, overload medical/legal specialists with unvetted inquiries, and drive up customer complaints.

  * Track passive waiting times between `Bot_Rules` and coordinator pick-up, and between specialist completion and adjuster reassignment.
  * Establish an automated operational threshold alert if the exception queue exceeds 15 pending cases or if queue dwell time exceeds 4 hours.
* **Specialist Referral Quality and Conversion**:
  * Continuously monitor the downstream outcome of referrals to `Omar_Medical` and `Priya_Legal`.
  * Track whether specialist reviews result in substantive settlement adjustments or are returned without impact, ensuring scarce specialist capacity (37–51 min service times) is not wasted on frivolous referrals.

  * Ensure junior staff are not disproportionately burdened with contested or complex claims that lack senior supervisory support.

  * Enforce secondary supervisory sign-off from regional leads (`Alice` or `Bruno`) on high-value exception settlements (`amount_band` above threshold).

### 3. What Should NOT Be Changed Until More Evidence Is Available
* **Do Not Prematurely Automate Triage Rules in `Bot_Rules`**:
