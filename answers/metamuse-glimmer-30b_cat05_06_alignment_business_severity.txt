* 1 Negligible / technical noise. No control impact, reversible, no policy breach.
* 2 Minor evidence / logging gap. Control likely performed, evidence weak but recoverable.
* 3 Moderate weakness. Potential customer/regulatory harm or evidence fragility, recoverable.
* 4 Serious policy breach / financial exposure. Data-aware rule violated or missing key control evidence, payment already made.
* 5 Critical control failure. Pay before approval, irreversible loss, severe regulatory/customer harm.

Confidence is independent of severity and reflects how well the alignment + business evidence supports the interpretation: High = timestamped system logs + corroborating documents; Medium = plausible explanation but missing corroboration; Low = reliance on mutable fields, verbal claims, or post-hoc mapping.

Raw alignment cost is sum of uniform visible moves. It does not encode amount, risk, data conditions, reversibility or scope.
