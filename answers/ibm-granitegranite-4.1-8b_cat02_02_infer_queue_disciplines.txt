# 3. Supporting and contradicting cases  

## Queue QA  
- **Supporting**:  
  - **A04** (priority1) departs before **A02** (priority3) despite later arrival  priority overtakes FIFO.  
  - **A03** (appointment) starts exactly at 08:20 despite being a laterarrival lowpriority job  appointment overrides FIFO.  
  - **A06** (appointment) begins at 09:20 exactly as scheduled, pushing aside a lowerpriority job  appointment rule.  

- **Contradicting**:  
  - **A02** (prio3, arrival08:04) is served after **A01** (prio3, arrival08:00) even though FIFO would predict the opposite. However, the arrivalscan delay (max2min) could make A02’s true arrival slightly later, preserving FIFO.  

## Queue QB  
- **Supporting**:  
  - **B02** (prio1) departs immediately after B01’s first interruption, indicating higherpriority jobs jump ahead  priority emerges.  
  - **B05** (prio1) is served before **B06** (prio2), consistent with priority ordering.  

- **Contradicting**:  
  - **B01** has multiple segments with unrelated interruptions; the longest job (45min) is still processed first, which could be interpreted as pure FIFO, but the interruptions suggest capacitylevel preemption rather than strict FIFO.  

## Queue QC  
- **Supporting**:  
  - **C05** (prio1, deadline10:50) is selected before **C03** (deadline12:30) despite arriving later  deadline urgency overrides arrival order.  
  - **C07** (deadline12:00) is processed immediately after **C06** finishes, matching EDF.  

- **Contradicting**:  
  - **C02** (deadline14:00) is processed before **C03** (deadline12:30) despite having a later deadline; this could be explained by a tiebreaker priority (prio3 vs prio2) but is ambiguous without explicit priority column.  

## Queue QD  
- **Supporting**:  
  - **D05** is the only job that can be served by **DF** (firmware+electrical)  strict skillbased routing.  
  - **D06** appointment starts at its booked time on **DX**, showing appointment scheduling works when skill is satisfied.  

- **Contradicting**:  
  - **D06** (electrical appointment) begins after **D07** (mechanical+electrical) even though **D07** arrived earlier; this suggests that **appointment** may override skillfirst routing for the specific appointment resource, potentially indicating a hybrid policy.

- **Episode**: **A03** (appointment at 08:20, priority3).  
- **Predictions**:

If the dispatch decision occurs **before** the appointment time (08:20) with a margin <1min, it would favor **appointmentdominant**; if the decision is logged **after** the appointment time, it favors **prioritydominant**.

- **Episode**: **C02** (deadline14:00, priority3) vs **C03** (deadline12:30, priority2).  
- **Predictions**:

If a **resource availability log** shows C03 was blocked, it supports **prioritydominant**; otherwise, it supports **EDF**.  

# 5. Operational exceptions and alternative explanations  

1. **Arrivalscan delay (±2min)**  
   - *Impact*: Can invert apparent FIFO order, making lowpriority jobs appear higher in the queue.  
   - *Plausibility*: Medium – timestamps are precise, but delays are systematic.  
   - *Effect on diagnosis*: Reduces confidence in FIFOonly explanations for QA and QB.  

2. **Resourcelevel preemption due to capacity shifts**  
   - *Impact*: Interrupts jobs (e.g., B01 in QB) to serve higherpriority or appointment jobs, blurring pure priority/SPT rules.  
   - *Plausibility*: High – documented in QB notes.  
   - *Effect*: Increases strength of “secondary preemption” component for QB.  

3. **Appointment tolerance windows (±2min)**  
   - *Impact*: Allows slight flexibility; an appointment listed at 08:20 could be dispatched at 08:1808:22 without violating policy.  
   - *Plausibility*: High – arrival scans already allow delay.  
   - *Effect*: Makes appointment rule appear more robust for QA and QD.  

4. **Skilloverlap ambiguity**  
   - *Impact*: Overlapping skills (e.g., DF vs DX for electrical) create conflict when multiple resources are free.  
   - *Plausibility*: Medium – seen in D06 and D07 decisions.  
   - *Effect*: Weakens strict skillfirst routing for QD; suggests hybrid scheduling based on resourcelevel availability.  

*Ranking*: 1 Resourcelevel preemption (high), 2 Appointment tolerance (high), 3 Arrivalscan delay (medium), 4 Skilloverlap ambiguity (medium).

# 7. Final synthesis  

- **Most identifiable queue**: **QC** – the combination of explicit deadlines and a clear “earliestdeadlinefirst” pattern, coupled with minimal competing constraints, yields the highest confidence (80%).  
- **Least identifiable queue**: **QB** – interruptions, resource reassignments, and ambiguous appointment vs priority interactions keep confidence at 70% and introduce the greatest analytical uncertainty.  
- **Most dangerous to infer a single universal policy**: Assuming **priorityonly** dispatch across all queues (as management claimed) would be misleading, especially for **QB** (where FIFO with preemption is more plausible) and **QD** (where skillbased routing dominates). The evidence strongly suggests **queuespecific disciplines** rather than a onesizefitsall rule.
