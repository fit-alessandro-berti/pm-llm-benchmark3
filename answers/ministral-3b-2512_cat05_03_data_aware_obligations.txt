### **6. Why Control-Flow-Only Conformance Is Insufficient**
1. **Final-state leakage**: Rules depend on state at `Release_Funds`, but control-flow traces only capture events up to that point. A post-release exemption (e.g., TX-310) cannot be detected.
2. **Post-hoc exemptions**: Exemptions may be recorded after release (e.g., TX-310’s `Record_Exemption` at 11:00). Control-flow traces ignore this.
3. **Material changes after review**: TX-309’s amount increase invalidates the Enhanced_Review, but control-flow traces only see the release event.
4. **Conditional activation**: Rules like R1 depend on exemption validity, which requires querying an external register. Control-flow traces cannot represent this.
5. **Missing data**: TX-305’s unknown amount at Enhanced_Review cannot be resolved by control-flow alone.
6. **Same-timestamp ordering**: TX-311’s jurisdiction update after Enhanced_Review requires event-index resolution, which control-flow traces cannot enforce.
