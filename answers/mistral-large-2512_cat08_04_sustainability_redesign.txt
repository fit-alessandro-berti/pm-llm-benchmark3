# Process Redesign for Sustainable Spare-Parts Distribution

## 1. System Boundary and Assumptions

**Environmental scope:**
- **Inside boundary:** Energy consumption (production machines), transport emissions (kgCO2e), material waste (scrap and packaging), rework loops, and batching effects (waiting time vs. energy efficiency)
- **Outside boundary:** Upstream mining/manufacturing of raw materials, end-of-life disposal/recycling, and capital equipment embodied carbon (though these could be considered in future LCA)
- **Uncertainty remains:** Exact carbon intensity of energy mix, detailed lifecycle impacts of different packaging materials, and long-term customer behavior changes in response to lead time adjustments

**Key assumptions:**
- The distributor has control over production batching, transport mode selection, packaging choices, and order promising rules
- Service-contract customers have higher service expectations but may accept negotiated lead times
- Regular customers may tolerate slightly longer lead times if reliability improves
- Critical-downtime orders maintain absolute priority for service levels
- The current event log represents typical operational conditions (no extreme disruptions)

## 3. Intervention Options

### **S1: Green Shipping Recommender**
- **Target path:** All ship events, especially Standard-stock Service-contract (22% SP1 violations)
- **Mechanism:** AI-based route selection using:
  - Promised lead time slack (current time to deadline)
  - Historical carrier reliability by route_mode
  - Customer segment (Service-contract vs Regular)
  - Real-time consolidation opportunities
- **Environmental improvement:** Reduces air transport by 30-50% for eligible orders
- **Service effect:** Maintains or improves on-time delivery through better reliability prediction
- **Quality/resilience:** No direct impact, but may reduce packaging rework from rushed shipments
- **Monitoring evidence:** % SP1 violations, air transport share, late delivery rate by route_mode

### **S2: Dynamic Batching with Segment-Specific Caps**
- **Target path:** Made-to-order production (CNC_A, CNC_B, heat treatment)
- **Mechanism:**
  - CNC_A: Batch size 8-12 with max 24h wait for Regular, 12h for Service-contract
  - CNC_B: Only for urgent orders (max 8h wait) due to rework risk
  - Heat treatment: Batch size 10 with max 36h wait (balances energy efficiency and late risk)
- **Environmental improvement:** Reduces energy per item by 20-40% while controlling cycle time
- **Service effect:** Reduces late delivery risk from batch waiting by 15-25%
- **Quality/resilience:** Lower rework rates from CNC_A vs CNC_B
- **Monitoring evidence:** Batch size distribution, energy per item, batch wait time, late delivery rate

### **S4: Priority Pick + Ground for Standard-Stock Service-Contract**
- **Target path:** Standard-stock Service-contract orders currently shipped by air
- **Mechanism:**
  - Front-load picking for these orders
  - Use ground transport unless slack < 12h
  - Add "priority ground" service level with guaranteed 24h delivery
- **Environmental improvement:** Reduces air transport emissions by 60-80% for this segment
- **Service effect:** Maintains 95%+ on-time delivery through priority handling
- **Quality/resilience:** No impact on quality; may improve resilience by reducing air dependency
- **Monitoring evidence:** Air transport share for this segment, late delivery rate, SP1 violations

### **S5: Consolidated Truck Departures**
- **Target path:** Standard-stock Regular orders with ground transport
- **Mechanism:**
  - Fixed daily departures at 10:00 and 16:00
  - Minimum 8 orders or 80% capacity utilization
  - 12h consolidation window for non-urgent orders
- **Environmental improvement:** Reduces transport emissions by 35-55% for consolidated orders
- **Service effect:** Adds 6-12h to cycle time but maintains 90%+ on-time delivery
- **Quality/resilience:** No impact on quality; improves resilience through route diversity
- **Monitoring evidence:** Consolidation rate, transport emissions per order, late delivery rate

### **S6: Packaging-Route Compatibility Check**
- **Target path:** All package events, especially reinforced packaging with air transport
- **Mechanism:**
  - Pre-shipment validation of packaging vs route_mode
  - Automated compatibility matrix (e.g., reinforced packaging not needed for ground)
  - Exception workflow for urgent cases
- **Environmental improvement:** Reduces packaging material by 20-30% and rework loops by 40%
- **Service effect:** Minimal impact on cycle time; may prevent some delays from rework
- **Quality/resilience:** Reduces damage-related returns by 30-40%
- **Monitoring evidence:** Rework packaging rate, package material per order, return rate

### **S7: Inspection Quality Gate for High-Rework Combinations**
- **Target path:** Made-to-order production with CNC_B or heat treatment
- **Mechanism:**
  - Mandatory secondary inspection for parts from high-rework machine/part combinations
  - Automated flagging of combinations with >15% historical rework rate
  - Additional documentation for exceptions
- **Environmental improvement:** Reduces rework loops by 30-50%, cutting energy and material waste
- **Service effect:** Adds 2-4h to cycle time but prevents longer rework delays
- **Quality/resilience:** Reduces return rate by 25-40%
- **Monitoring evidence:** Rework rate by machine/part combination, scrap per order, return rate

### **S8: Shared Emergency Courier Lanes**
- **Target path:** Critical-downtime dedicated van shipments
- **Mechanism:**
  - Replace 50% of dedicated vans with shared emergency courier network
  - Dynamic routing to combine multiple urgent shipments
  - Priority lanes at carriers for these shipments
- **Environmental improvement:** Reduces transport emissions by 30-40% for critical orders
- **Service effect:** Maintains 98%+ on-time delivery for true downtime cases
  - Adds 1-2h to cycle time for precautionary urgent cases
- **Quality/resilience:** Improves resilience through carrier diversity
- **Monitoring evidence:** Transport emissions for critical orders, on-time delivery rate, SP2 violations

### **S10: Carbon-Aware Promise-Date Negotiation**
- **Target path:** Made-to-order Regular orders
- **Mechanism:**
  - Customer portal showing carbon impact of different lead times
  - Default to "green lead time" (20% longer than standard) with carbon savings estimate
  - Incentives for choosing longer lead times (discounts, priority for future orders)
- **Environmental improvement:** Enables better batching and transport consolidation
  - Estimated 15-25% reduction in energy and transport emissions
- **Service effect:** Customers may accept longer lead times with proper incentives
- **Quality/resilience:** No direct impact
- **Monitoring evidence:** Average promised lead time, energy/transport emissions, customer acceptance rate

## 5. Rebound and Displacement Risks

1. **Batching Obsolescence Risk:**
   - Larger batches improve energy efficiency but increase obsolescence risk (6% of batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days)
   - *Mitigation:* Dynamic batching with demand forecasting integration and maximum age limits

2. **Transport Mode Shifting:**
   - Reducing air transport may increase ground transport volume, potentially increasing congestion and emissions in ground networks
   - *Mitigation:* Monitor ground transport emissions and implement consolidation to offset volume increases

3. **Packaging Material Trade-off:**
   - Reduced packaging to save materials may increase damage-related returns and rework
   - *Mitigation:* Maintain reinforced packaging for high-risk parts and routes, with clear compatibility rules

4. **Rework Displacement:**
   - Stricter inspection may catch more defects before shipping, increasing internal rework but reducing customer returns
   - *Mitigation:* Track both internal rework and customer returns to ensure net improvement

5. **Customer Behavior Rebound:**
   - Customers may game the system by always choosing "urgent" to get faster service, undermining green shipping recommendations
   - *Mitigation:* Enforce strict justification for expedited shipping and monitor abuse patterns

6. **Resilience vs. Efficiency:**
   - Consolidation and batching improve efficiency but may reduce flexibility during disruptions
   - *Mitigation:* Maintain minimum route diversity and monitor alternative route activation time

7. **Energy vs. Time Trade-off:**
   - Faster production (CNC_B) may reduce cycle time but increase energy and rework
   - *Mitigation:* Use CNC_B only for truly urgent orders with clear justification

8. **Obsolescence vs. Batching:**
   - Larger batches reduce energy per item but increase inventory holding time and obsolescence risk
   - *Mitigation:* Implement first-in-first-out (FIFO) for batch-produced items and monitor age at shipment

## 6. Balanced Recommendation

**Recommended Package:**
1. **Core Interventions (Immediate Implementation):**
   - **S1: Green Shipping Recommender** (reduces air transport with minimal service impact)
   - **S2: Dynamic Batching with Segment-Specific Caps** (balances energy efficiency and service)
   - **S4: Priority Pick + Ground for Standard-Stock Service-Contract** (high-impact, low-risk)
   - **S6: Packaging-Route Compatibility Check** (reduces rework and material waste)

2. **Supporting Interventions (Phased Rollout):**
   - **S5: Consolidated Truck Departures** (after monitoring S1/S4 impacts)
   - **S7: Inspection Quality Gate for High-Rework Combinations** (after establishing baseline rework rates)
   - **S10: Carbon-Aware Promise-Date Negotiation** (pilot with Regular Made-to-order customers)

3. **Critical Downtime Protection:**
   - **S8: Shared Emergency Courier Lanes** (maintains service while reducing emissions)
   - **S9: Split Critical-Downtime Categories** (better justification for dedicated resources)

**Rationale:**
- **Environmental Impact:** This package targets the highest-emission paths (air transport, rework, inefficient batching) while maintaining service levels. The combination of S1, S2, and S4 addresses 60-70% of transport emissions and 30-40% of energy consumption.
- **Service Preservation:** Dynamic batching caps and priority picking maintain or improve on-time delivery rates. The green shipping recommender uses reliability data to prevent service deterioration.
- **Quality Improvement:** Packaging compatibility checks and inspection quality gates directly reduce rework and returns, improving quality without adding significant cycle time.
- **Resilience Enhancement:** Consolidated trucks and shared emergency couriers improve route diversity, while the green shipping recommender reduces dependency on air transport.
- **Balanced Trade-offs:** The package avoids extreme batching that would increase obsolescence or extreme consolidation that would harm service. It focuses on high-impact, low-risk changes first.

## 7. Validation and Governance Plan

**Implementation Monitoring:**
1. **Conformance Monitoring:**
   - **SP1 (Ground/consolidated when slack allows):**
     - Weekly dashboard showing % violations by segment
     - Automated alerts for repeated violations
   - **SP2 (Dedicated van justification):**
     - Monthly audit of dedicated van shipments without justification events
     - Exception workflow for missing justifications
   - **Q1 (Reinforced packaging for >8kg):**
     - Daily report of packaging violations by part weight
     - Root cause analysis for repeated violations

2. **Segment-Level Service Monitoring:**
   - **Critical-downtime:**
     - On-time delivery rate (target: 98%+)
     - Median cycle time (target: 16h)
     - Alternative route activation time during disruptions
   - **Service-contract:**
     - On-time delivery rate (target: 95%+)
     - Air transport share (target: 15%)
     - Late delivery rate by route_mode
   - **Regular:**
     - On-time delivery rate (target: 90%+)
     - Customer acceptance rate for green lead times
     - Partial shipment rate (target: 5%)

3. **Sustainability Impact Tracking:**
   - **Energy:**
     - Energy per order by segment (weekly)
     - Energy per item by batch size (monthly)
   - **Transport:**
     - Transport emissions per order by route_mode (weekly)
     - Consolidation rate (monthly)
   - **Materials:**
     - Material scrap per order (weekly)
     - Packaging material per order (weekly)
     - Rework rate by machine/part combination (monthly)

**Governance Process:**
1. **Monthly Sustainability Review Board:**
   - Representatives from operations, customer service, logistics, and sustainability
   - Reviews conformance reports, service metrics, and environmental impact
   - Approves adjustments to batching caps, shipping rules, and packaging standards

2. **Exception Governance:**
   - **Sustainability Exception Log (S12):**
     - All SP1/SP2/Q1 violations require documented justification
     - Monthly review of exceptions to identify systemic issues
   - **Carbon Budget Tracking:**
     - Quarterly carbon budget allocation by segment
     - Exceptions require approval from sustainability board

3. **Continuous Improvement:**
   - **Process Mining Refresh:**
     - Quarterly update of process models with new event log data
     - Identification of new high-impact paths or emerging risks
   - **Customer Feedback Loop:**
     - Quarterly surveys on lead time flexibility and service expectations
     - Adjustment of green shipping recommender parameters based on feedback
   - **Carrier Performance Integration:**
     - Monthly update of carrier reliability data in green shipping recommender
     - Dynamic adjustment of route_mode recommendations based on performance

**Validation Approach:**
1. **Before/After Comparison:**
   - 3-month baseline period before implementation
   - 3-month measurement period after full implementation
   - Statistical testing of changes in key indicators (energy, transport, service levels)

2. **A/B Testing:**
   - Pilot S1 (Green Shipping Recommender) with 50% of Standard-stock Service-contract orders
   - Compare air transport share, late delivery rate, and customer satisfaction between groups

3. **Simulation Modeling:**
   - Use process mining tools to simulate the impact of dynamic batching (S2) on cycle time and energy
   - Validate against historical data before full implementation

4. **Customer Acceptance Testing:**
   - Pilot S10 (Carbon-Aware Promise-Date Negotiation) with 10% of Regular Made-to-order customers
   - Measure acceptance rate and impact on promised lead times

This governance plan ensures that the redesign remains aligned with business objectives while continuously improving environmental performance. The combination of conformance monitoring, segment-level service tracking, and exception governance provides the necessary feedback loops to balance sustainability with service, quality, and resilience.
