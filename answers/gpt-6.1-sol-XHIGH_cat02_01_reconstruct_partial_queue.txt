# 1. Assumptions and notation

The evidence supports an early accumulation at S1 followed by a growing downstream backlog at S2. This describes where congestion appears; it does not, by itself, prove which resource capacity limits the entire process.

- **A1 — Event order and window.** The nominal reconstruction uses the reported times. Queue intervals are `[entry, start)`, service intervals `[start, completion)`, and station residence intervals `[entry, completion)`. At identical reported times, process completions and transfers, external arrivals, and then starts as one batch; report the state after the entire batch. Do not count temporary within-batch peaks. Thus C1 can finish P07 and start P09 at 11:00 without nominal overlap. Flow counts use `[left, right)`; time averages integrate over `[08:00, 14:00)`. The 14:00 snapshot retains all unfinished cases.
- **A2 — Population coverage.** Treat the supplied 22 cases as the population being reconstructed, with no unlisted carry-in cases. The nominal system is empty immediately before the first 08:00 arrival. This assumption is necessary to identify total queue lengths from this extract. Under rounding, P01 could actually arrive just before 08:00; include its in-window exposure as a boundary case. Additional unlisted work would invalidate absolute occupancy and utilization estimates.
- **A3 — Capacity and activity execution.** For the main reconstruction, each named resource executes at most one case at a time, assignments are accurate, and an activity occupies its resource continuously from start to completion. Service therefore means occupied activity time; finer distinctions between hands-on work and undocumented pauses are unavailable. Activities are consecutive within a case, with S2 queue entry exactly equal to S1 completion. C2's stated maintenance interval is treated as a fixed unavailability interval, with no service during it. There is no bypass or preemption. These assumptions make resource sequencing a usable constraint; an assistance or logging-error alternative is discussed later.
- **A4 — Rounding.** Each recorded operational timestamp, including an external arrival, has a possible error of ±2.5 minutes. No distribution, independence, or clock-offset estimate is imposed. A difference between two recorded timestamps consequently has an outer uncertainty of ±5 minutes. Errors must jointly respect case precedence, resource sequencing, and maintenance. The administrative horizon 14:00 and the supplied statuses are taken as reliable.
- **A5 — Missing starts.** MISSING is an executed, completed activity with an unknown start. Its start lies between queue/resource availability and its completion, also respecting the next job. No positive minimum duration is supplied, so zero service is admitted as a conservative limiting bound, not a realistic operational estimate. Three nominal scenarios use the earliest feasible, central, and latest feasible starts. The central scenario back-calculates from the median fully observed completed service duration—30 minutes at S1 and 40 at S2—and clips the result to the feasible interval. Missingness is not assumed random.
- **A6 — Dispatching.** Do not impose FIFO or unrestricted interchangeability beyond the stated resource assignments. The supplied sequence includes overtaking; reconstruct it rather than silently correcting it.
- **A7 — Censoring.** OPEN activities contribute elapsed service and residence up to 14:00, but have no completion event. Cases waiting at 14:00 contribute elapsed waiting, with their final wait right-censored. No service duration is assigned to an activity that has not started. S2 measures are inapplicable to cases that have not completed S1. No distribution is assumed for future waits or service, so their final durations have no finite upper bound from this extract.
- **A8 — Interpretation of averages.** Time averages describe this six-hour sample. Started-case, completed-case, and all-entered-case summaries are kept separate. No stationary operating regime is assumed merely because arrival and departure counts happen to balance in one short interval.

All durations below are in minutes unless stated otherwise. A plain number is a nominal calculation, not an exact physical duration. `c` denotes the central imputation; `E` denotes elapsed service at the horizon; `N/E` means the station has not been entered.

# 2. Reconstruction method and formulas

For case (i) at station (j), let (a_{ij}), (b_{ij}), and (d_{ij}) be queue entry, activity start, and completion. Here (a_{i1}) is external arrival and (a_{i2}=d_{i1}), when S1 has completed.

For a completed station visit:

\[
w_{ij}=b_{ij}-a_{ij},\qquad
v_{ij}=d_{ij}-b_{ij},\qquad
z_{ij}=d_{ij}-a_{ij}=w_{ij}+v_{ij}.
\]

These are queue waiting, active service, and station sojourn, respectively. For a fully completed case:

\[
T_i=d_{i2}-a_{i1}=z_{i1}+z_{i2}.
\]

For an open activity, elapsed service is (H-b_{ij}), whereas final service is at least that amount. For an unstarted visit, elapsed waiting is (H-a_{ij}); its final waiting time is at least that amount. Every unfinished entered visit has elapsed station sojourn (H-a_{ij}). These are lower bounds on final durations, not completed observations.

Use an unknown future start/completion as (+\infty) in occupancy indicators:

\[
Q_j(t)=\sum_i\mathbf1\{a_{ij}\le t<b_{ij}\},\qquad
B_j(t)=\sum_i\mathbf1\{b_{ij}\le t<d_{ij}\},
\]
\[
W_j(t)=Q_j(t)+B_j(t),\qquad W_{\mathrm{system}}(t)=W_1(t)+W_2(t).
\]

An internal transfer removes a case from S1 and adds it to S2, so it does not change system WIP. Mean queue length and WIP are their integrated case-minute areas divided by the interval length. Queue-area calculations include ongoing waits, and busy-time calculations include open service.

For a subperiod of (Delta) hours, the arrival rate is entries/(Delta), and throughput is completed services/(Delta). Starts are not departures. Rates are realized flow, rather than estimates of sustainable capacity.

Resource busy time is the length of the union of its service intervals intersected with the analysis window. Since the main scenarios satisfy non-overlap, this also equals the sum of their clipped durations. Resource utilization is busy minutes/available minutes; station utilization pools busy minutes and availability across its two resources. Full-window availability is 720 resource-minutes at S1 and (360+345=705) at S2. Calendar-capacity utilization at S2 instead uses 720; both denominators must be identified.

For a missing start, the nominal bounds are

\[
b^- = \max(a,\text{previous completion},\text{applicable availability}),\qquad
b^+ = \min(d,\text{next known start}).
\]

The central choice is (b^c=\operatorname{clip}(d-m_j,b^-,b^+)), where (m_j) is the observed station median service duration. Earliest starts minimize missing-case waiting and maximize service; latest starts reverse that allocation. Missing-case waiting and service bounds are coupled: their sum remains the station sojourn. Rounding bounds expand endpoint boxes and enforce the constraints jointly. In particular, a shared resource boundary cannot simultaneously take two incompatible values to maximize both adjacent jobs.

Little's Law would relate (ar W_j=\lambda_j E[z_j]) and (ar Q_j=\lambda_j E[w_j]) for consistent populations in an adequately stable regime or an appropriately closed finite cohort. For the complete operational population here, use the exact exposure identity instead:

\[
\int_O^H W_j(t)\,dt
=\sum_i |[a_{ij},d_{ij})\cap[O,H)|,
\]

with future completions clipped to (H) for exposure only. Section 4 checks the closed-cohort version and explicitly accounts for unfinished visits.

# 5. Bottleneck and temporal interpretation

**Observed pattern.** The external surge is initially larger than S1's realized output: 13 nominal arrivals but six S1 completions before 10:00. S1's queue is consequently much larger than S2's early on. Its mean queue rises from about 2.1 in the first hour to about 5.9 during 11:00–12:00. Individual S1 waits rise from five minutes to roughly 100 minutes. This establishes substantial upstream congestion during the demand surge.

S2's backlog grows as it receives S1 output. It receives seven cases and completes five during 10:00–12:00, then receives seven and completes six during 12:00–14:00. Its mean queue increases throughout the hourly summaries, ending with four waiting and two active. External arrivals stop after 12:10, and S1 drains to one waiting and one active, while S2 remains congested. This is stronger evidence of a downstream constraint later in the day.

**Capacity interpretation.** Fully observed completed service has median 30 minutes at S1 and 40 at S2. With two continuously available resources and no other losses, those medians suggest rough service capacities of four and three cases/hour, respectively. This is a heuristic, not a fitted capacity model; there are variable durations, gaps, missing starts, and uncompleted jobs. The evidence is most consistent with S1 being heavily overloaded by the initial arrival burst and S2 becoming the persistent downstream throughput constraint. S2 may be the lower-capacity station structurally throughout, even while the largest visible queue is upstream. Central utilization percentages alone cannot establish the ranking, particularly given their sensitivity ranges.

**Maintenance.** C2's 10:50 completion and 11:15 next start bracket the 10:55–11:10 outage. The nominal 25-minute resource gap contains 15 minutes of known maintenance and ten minutes outside it; rounding makes the total gap about 20–30 minutes. The outage reduces six-hour S2 availability by 15 resource-minutes, equivalent to 0.375 of a 40-minute service slot as a capacity comparison. That is not an observed loss of 0.375 completed cases.

At 10:55, P08, P09, and P10 are waiting for S2. P08 has already waited 35 minutes before maintenance begins, so its entire 55-minute wait cannot be attributed to the outage. C1 starts P09 at 11:00; C2 starts P08 at 11:15. The outage contributes a local capacity restriction, but the extract cannot identify its causal effect on final throughput or each waiting time without a dispatching counterfactual. S2 queues continue growing after maintenance ends, so maintenance alone does not explain the downstream backlog.

**Dispatching hypotheses.** P09 overtakes P08, and P17 starts S2 while the earlier-entering P16 is still waiting. These orderings survive ±2.5-minute rounding and rule out a simple pooled FIFO interpretation. Possible explanations include priorities, resource eligibility, delayed logging, or other dispatch rules; none is established. Short gaps with waiting work also suggest either operational losses or scheduling constraints. Many reported five-minute gaps could disappear under rounding, so they should not all be labeled avoidable idleness.

At fixed reported timestamps, the earliest and latest scenarios bound the entire queue trajectory pointwise. S1 queue length is uncertain only during 09:25–10:00 and 11:40–12:15; S2 only during 09:45–10:15 and 12:00–12:25. Use half-open intervals for these bands. Within each band, the station's upper and lower queue trajectories differ by exactly one case; outside them, they coincide. Active-service counts change in the opposite direction, leaving station WIP unchanged. This gives queue bounds between the hourly summaries as well as at their snapshots.

The latest-start scenario uses zero-duration limiting activities and is a conservative envelope. The central S2 imputations coincide with earliest feasible starts because a 40-minute duration cannot fit between the previous job and recorded completion. P05/S2 can last at most 30 nominal minutes, or 35 allowing rounding; P11/S2 at most 25 nominal, or 30 allowing rounding. This does not prove a defect: shorter calibration jobs are possible, and P10 has an observed 25-minute duration. It does show why blindly assigning the median to every missing activity would violate the data constraints.

If assistance or incorrect resource logging is allowed instead of A3, an earlier missing start might overlap previous work. The much weaker nominal queue-entry/completion-only service bounds become 75 for P07/S1, 125 for P15/S1, 50 for P05/S2, and 80 for P11/S2. Those are alternative-model upper bounds, not evidence of such durations. A 40-minute S2 imputation for P05 or P11 would require nominal overlap of ten or fifteen minutes with the previous assigned job; rounding can absorb only five minutes of such overlap. Under an overlap hypothesis, resource busy time must use interval unions, and the capacity interpretation needs additional evidence.

The corresponding resource busy envelopes are I1 202.5–357.5, I2 240–340, C1 232.5–332.5, and C2 217.5–297.5 minutes. The calculation enforces the shared 11:00 C1 boundary rather than counting simultaneous conflicting endpoint extrema as real concurrent work.

Conservative endpoint envelopes put mean WIP at roughly 5.5–6.1 for S1, 3.5–4.0 for S2, and 9.3–9.8 for the system; the system range is calculated directly because internal transfer errors cancel. A rounded arrival before a same-time S1 departure can briefly raise the physical S1 peak from eight to nine. S2's nominal peak of seven could shrink to six if the 13:45 transfer and 13:50 departure coincide at the touching rounding endpoints. The system peak of 13 survives the rounding envelope: the relevant external arrivals and system departures remain sufficiently separated.

**Censoring and selection.** The five waiting cases and three open activities remain in exposure and occupancy calculations. They are excluded only from summaries requiring final service or final waiting. In the central scenario, S2's completed-case mean wait is 31.8 minutes, its started-case mean is 36.6, and its already-accrued waiting across all 20 entered cases averages 38.5. The eventual mean wait for those 20 is at least 38.5 under that imputation. Dropping P16 and the other waiting cases would conceal the oldest unresolved wait.

The nominal sum of completed and unfinished end-to-end exposure is 3,425 minutes. Consequently, the eventual mean total sojourn of all 22 cases is at least 155.7 minutes at nominal timestamps, already greater than the completed-only mean of 146.4. There is no finite upper bound on the eventual mean from these data. A survival estimate would need additional assumptions about censoring, changing congestion, and service heterogeneity; none is imposed here.

**Stable conclusions:** substantial S1 delay during the early surge; increasing S2 congestion into the afternoon; eight unfinished cases at the horizon; robust overtaking inconsistent with pooled FIFO; and a localized loss of C2 availability. **Sensitive conclusions:** exact service allocations for the four missing starts, utilization ranking, brief physical peaks, hourly boundary counts, and all future completion/waiting estimates. The most useful additional records would be the four missing starts, actual completion times after 14:00, dispatch/eligibility rules, and higher-resolution resource and maintenance timestamps.

# 7. Reproducible Python

The following self-contained program requires pandas and PM4Py. It embeds the supplied table, retains distinct timestamp statuses, checks feasible schedules, and prints station, resource, temporal, case, and uncertainty results. The observed PM4Py interval table contains 18 S1 and 12 S2 completed intervals; open and waiting visits remain in the pandas exposure calculations.

The PM4Py demonstrations use a separate projection of the 14 fully completed cases, with central imputations explicitly marked. A zero-duration external-arrival event makes case sojourn span external arrival through S2 completion; case service sums both station services, and case waiting includes both queues. These are **case-level** outputs. In the demonstrated API, the columns named arrival/finish “rate” contain inter-arrival/inter-finish gaps in seconds, with an initial zero sentinel; they are not the station flow counts per hour computed above. The projection omits unfinished cases and must not be used to infer full-population arrival rates or utilization.

```python
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

DATE = "2026-05-12 "
T = lambda clock: pd.Timestamp(DATE + clock)
O, H = T("08:00"), T("14:00")
EPS = pd.Timedelta(minutes=2.5)
DOWN0, DOWN1 = T("10:55"), T("11:10")
MIN = pd.Timedelta(minutes=1)

DATA = """P01|08:00|08:05|08:25|I1|08:30|09:00|C1|COMPLETE
P02|08:05|08:10|08:35|I2|08:40|09:20|C2|COMPLETE
P03|08:10|08:30|08:55|I1|09:05|09:45|C1|COMPLETE
P04|08:20|08:40|09:10|I2|09:25|10:05|C2|COMPLETE
P05|08:25|09:00|09:25|I1|MISSING|10:15|C1|COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P06|08:35|09:15|09:45|I2|10:10|10:50|C2|COMPLETE
P07|08:45|MISSING|10:00|I1|10:20|11:00|C1|COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P08|09:00|09:50|10:20|I2|11:15|12:00|C2|COMPLETE
P09|09:05|10:05|10:35|I1|11:00|11:40|C1|COMPLETE
P10|09:15|10:25|10:55|I2|11:45|12:10|C1|COMPLETE
P11|09:25|10:40|11:05|I1|MISSING|12:25|C2|COMPLETE_WITH_MISSING_START
P12|09:35|11:00|11:30|I2|12:15|13:00|C1|COMPLETE
P13|09:50|11:10|11:40|I1|12:30|13:20|C2|COMPLETE
P14|10:00|11:35|12:00|I2|13:05|13:50|C1|COMPLETE
P15|10:10|MISSING|12:15|I1|13:30|OPEN|C2|OPEN_IN_S2
P16|10:25|12:05|12:35|I2||||WAITING_FOR_S2
P17|10:45|12:20|12:50|I1|13:55|OPEN|C1|OPEN_IN_S2
P18|11:00|12:40|13:10|I2||||WAITING_FOR_S2
P19|11:15|12:55|13:25|I1||||WAITING_FOR_S2
P20|11:30|13:15|13:45|I2||||WAITING_FOR_S2
P21|11:50|13:30|OPEN|I1||||OPEN_IN_S1
P22|12:10|||||||WAITING_FOR_S1"""

COLS = ["case", "arrival_s1", "s1_start", "s1_end", "s1_resource",
        "s2_start", "s2_end", "s2_resource", "state_at_14_00"]
df = pd.DataFrame([line.split("|") for line in DATA.splitlines()], columns=COLS)
TIME_COLS = ["arrival_s1", "s1_start", "s1_end", "s2_start", "s2_end"]
for col in TIME_COLS:
    raw = df[col].str.strip()
    df[col + "_raw"] = raw
    df[col + "_kind"] = raw.map(
        lambda v: "BLANK" if v == "" else v if v in {"MISSING", "OPEN"}
        else "OBSERVED")
    df[col] = raw.map(lambda v: pd.NaT if v in {"", "MISSING", "OPEN"}
                     else pd.Timestamp(DATE + v))
for col in ["s1_resource", "s2_resource"]:
    df[col] = df[col].replace("", pd.NA)


def station_frame(z, st):
    entry = z["arrival_s1"] if st == "s1" else z["s1_end"]
    f = pd.DataFrame({"case": z.case, "entry": entry,
                      "start": z[st + "_start"], "end": z[st + "_end"],
                      "resource": z[st + "_resource"],
                      "start_kind": z[st + "_start_kind"],
                      "end_kind": z[st + "_end_kind"]})
    return f.loc[f.entry.notna()].copy()


def area(begin, finish, left=O, right=H):
    # NaT begins contribute zero; artificial H is used for exposure only.
    duration = (finish.clip(upper=right) - begin.clip(lower=left)) / MIN
    return float(duration.clip(lower=0).fillna(0).sum())


def occupancy(begin, finish, at):
    # A missing finish means still present; OPEN is never a departure at H.
    return int(((begin <= at) & (finish.isna() | (finish > at))).sum())


def peak(begin, finish, left=O, right=H):
    events = pd.concat([begin, finish]).dropna()
    times = sorted(set([left] + events.loc[(events >= left) &
                                             (events < right)].tolist()))
    return max(occupancy(begin, finish, at) for at in times)


# Same-resource order follows known completions, with OPEN last.
bounds = []
for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
    known = df[st + "_start"].notna() & df[st + "_end"].notna()
    median_service = ((df.loc[known, st + "_end"] -
                       df.loc[known, st + "_start"]) / MIN).median()
    for resource, jobs in df.loc[df[st + "_resource"].notna()].groupby(st + "_resource"):
        ids = jobs.assign(order_end=jobs[st + "_end"].fillna(H)).sort_values("order_end").index.tolist()
        for pos, idx in enumerate(ids):
            if df.at[idx, st + "_start_kind"] != "MISSING":
                continue
            entry = df.at[idx, "arrival_s1" if st == "s1" else "s1_end"]
            finish = df.at[idx, st + "_end"]
            prev_end = df.at[ids[pos - 1], st + "_end"] if pos else O
            next_start = df.at[ids[pos + 1], st + "_start"] if pos + 1 < len(ids) else H
            lo, hi = max(entry, prev_end), min(finish, next_start)
            rlo, rhi = max(entry - EPS, prev_end - EPS), min(finish + EPS, next_start + EPS)
            if resource == "C2" and finish > DOWN0 and lo < DOWN1:
                lo, rlo = max(lo, DOWN1), max(rlo, DOWN1)
            central = min(hi, max(lo, finish - pd.Timedelta(minutes=float(median_service))))
            assert lo <= central <= hi
            bounds.append(dict(case=df.at[idx, "case"], station=st, resource=resource,
                               lo=lo, central=central, hi=hi,
                               rounded_lo=rlo, rounded_hi=rhi))
bounds = pd.DataFrame(bounds)


def nominal_scenario(choice):
    z = df.copy()
    for _, b in bounds.iterrows():
        z.loc[z.case.eq(b.case), b.station + "_start"] = b[choice]
    return z


def validate(z):
    for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
        f = station_frame(z, st)
        started = f.loc[f.start.notna()].copy()
        assert (started.start >= started.entry).all()
        assert (started.end.fillna(H) >= started.start).all()
        for resource, g in started.groupby("resource"):
            g = g.sort_values("start")
            previous_finish = None
            for _, r in g.iterrows():
                finish = r.end if pd.notna(r.end) else H
                if previous_finish is not None:
                    assert previous_finish <= r.start
                if resource == "C2":
                    assert not (r.start < DOWN1 and finish > DOWN0)
                previous_finish = finish


def rounded_busy_scenario(maximize):
    # Joint feasible endpoint schedules, rather than adding incompatible
    # per-case duration extrema. Arrivals stay nominal here: in this data
    # they do not bind the service-time extrema.
    z = df.copy()
    for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
        for part in ["start", "end"]:
            col = st + "_" + part
            mask = z[col + "_kind"].eq("OBSERVED")
            direction = (-1 if maximize else 1) if part == "start" else (1 if maximize else -1)
            z.loc[mask, col] = df.loc[mask, col] + direction * EPS
    for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
        for resource, jobs in df.loc[df[st + "_resource"].notna()].groupby(st + "_resource"):
            ids = jobs.assign(order_end=jobs[st + "_end"].fillna(H)).sort_values("order_end").index.tolist()
            previous_finish = O
            for idx in ids:
                entry = z.at[idx, "arrival_s1" if st == "s1" else "s1_end"]
                finish = z.at[idx, st + "_end"]
                finish = finish if pd.notna(finish) else H
                missing = df.at[idx, st + "_start_kind"] == "MISSING"
                if missing:
                    start = max(previous_finish, entry) if maximize else finish
                else:
                    start = max(z.at[idx, st + "_start"], previous_finish, entry)
                    assert df.at[idx, st + "_start"] - EPS <= start <= df.at[idx, st + "_start"] + EPS
                if resource == "C2" and start < DOWN1 and finish > DOWN0:
                    start = DOWN1
                z.at[idx, st + "_start"] = start
                previous_finish = finish
    validate(z)
    return z


scenarios = {"early": nominal_scenario("lo"),
             "central": nominal_scenario("central"),
             "late": nominal_scenario("hi"),
             "round_min_busy": rounded_busy_scenario(False),
             "round_max_busy": rounded_busy_scenario(True)}
metrics, resource_metrics = [], []
for name, z in scenarios.items():
    validate(z)
    for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
        f = station_frame(z, st)
        wait = (f.start - f.entry) / MIN
        service = (f.end - f.start) / MIN
        q_area = area(f.entry, f.start.fillna(H))
        busy = area(f.start, f.end.fillna(H))
        w_area = area(f.entry, f.end.fillna(H))
        assert abs(q_area + busy - w_area) < 1e-8
        available = 720 if st == "s1" else 705
        metrics.append(dict(scenario=name, station=st,
                            mean_started_wait=wait.mean(),
                            mean_completed_service=service.mean(),
                            queue_area=q_area, busy_minutes=busy, wip_area=w_area,
                            mean_Q=q_area / 360, peak_Q=peak(f.entry, f.start),
                            mean_WIP=w_area / 360, peak_WIP=peak(f.entry, f.end),
                            utilization_pct=100 * busy / available))
        for resource, g in f.loc[f.start.notna()].groupby("resource"):
            available_resource = 345 if resource == "C2" else 360
            rb = area(g.start, g.end.fillna(H))
            resource_metrics.append(dict(scenario=name, resource=resource,
                                         busy_minutes=rb,
                                         utilization_pct=100 * rb / available_resource))

central = scenarios["central"]
print("\nMissing-start bounds; times on 2026-05-12")
print(bounds.to_string(index=False))
print("\nStation sensitivity (minutes, counts, percentages)")
print(pd.DataFrame(metrics).round(2).to_string(index=False))
print("\nResource busy-time sensitivity")
print(pd.DataFrame(resource_metrics).round(2).to_string(index=False))

hourly = []
for left in pd.date_range(O, H, freq="h")[:-1]:
    right = left + pd.Timedelta(hours=1)
    row = {"interval": left.strftime("%H:%M") + "-" + right.strftime("%H:%M")}
    for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
        f = station_frame(central, st)
        row[st + "_arrivals_per_h"] = int(((f.entry >= left) & (f.entry < right)).sum())
        row[st + "_departures_per_h"] = int(((f.end >= left) & (f.end < right)).sum())
        row[st + "_mean_Q"] = area(f.entry, f.start.fillna(H), left, right) / 60
        row[st + "_mean_WIP"] = area(f.entry, f.end.fillna(H), left, right) / 60
    hourly.append(row)
print("\nHourly station rates and time-weighted occupancy (central)")
print(pd.DataFrame(hourly).round(2).to_string(index=False))

snapshots = []
for at in pd.date_range(O, H, freq="h"):
    row = {"time_after_batch": at.strftime("%H:%M")}
    for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
        f = station_frame(central, st)
        row[st + "_Q"] = occupancy(f.entry, f.start, at)
        row[st + "_active"] = occupancy(f.start, f.end, at)
        row[st + "_WIP"] = occupancy(f.entry, f.end, at)
    row["total_WIP"] = row["s1_WIP"] + row["s2_WIP"]
    snapshots.append(row)
print("\nHourly queue/active/WIP snapshots (central)")
print(pd.DataFrame(snapshots).to_string(index=False))

# All changes, including off-hour peaks, can be reconstructed from these
# unique entry/start/completion times with the same occupancy function.
event_times = sorted(set(pd.concat([central[c] for c in TIME_COLS]).dropna().tolist() + [O, H]))
total_peak = max(sum(occupancy(station_frame(central, st).entry,
                               station_frame(central, st).end, at)
                     for st in ["s1", "s2"]) for at in event_times)
case_metrics = central[["case", "state_at_14_00"]].copy()
for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
    f = station_frame(central, st).set_index("case")
    for label, values in {
        "wait_if_started": (f.start - f.entry) / MIN,
        "wait_exposure": (f.start.fillna(H) - f.entry) / MIN,
        "service_if_complete": (f.end - f.start) / MIN,
        "service_exposure": (f.end.fillna(H) - f.start) / MIN,
        "sojourn_exposure": (f.end.fillna(H) - f.entry) / MIN
    }.items():
        case_metrics[st + "_" + label] = case_metrics.case.map(values)
case_metrics["total_sojourn_exposure"] = (central.s2_end.fillna(H) - central.arrival_s1) / MIN
print("\nCase metrics: exposure is final only when the relevant status is complete")
print(case_metrics.round(2).to_string(index=False))
print("System peak WIP:", total_peak,
      "; mean WIP:", round(case_metrics.total_sojourn_exposure.sum() / 360, 2))

check_rows = []
for st in ["s1", "s2"]:
    f = station_frame(central, st)
    completed = f.end.notna()
    residence = (f.end - f.entry) / MIN
    complete_area = residence.loc[completed].sum()
    residual_area = area(f.loc[~completed, "entry"], pd.Series(H, index=f.index[~completed]))
    check_rows.append(dict(station=st, completed_cohort= int(completed.sum()),
                           completed_mean_sojourn=residence.loc[completed].mean(),
                           cohort_mean_WIP=complete_area / 360,
                           cohort_rate_times_sojourn=(completed.sum() / 6) * residence.loc[completed].mean() / 60,
                           residual_WIP_area=residual_area))
print("\nFinite-cohort Little's-Law identity; NOT a steady-state station estimate")
print(pd.DataFrame(check_rows).round(2).to_string(index=False))


def interval_events(z, external_arrival=False):
    records = []
    for _, r in z.iterrows():
        if external_arrival:
            records.append({"case:concept:name": r.case, "concept:name": "External arrival",
                            "start_timestamp": r.arrival_s1, "time:timestamp": r.arrival_s1,
                            "org:resource": "external_system", "start_was_imputed": False})
        for st, activity in [("s1", "Incoming Inspection"), ("s2", "Calibration")]:
            if pd.notna(r[st + "_start"]) and pd.notna(r[st + "_end"]):
                records.append({"case:concept:name": r.case, "concept:name": activity,
                                "start_timestamp": r[st + "_start"],
                                "time:timestamp": r[st + "_end"],
                                "org:resource": r[st + "_resource"],
                                "start_was_imputed": r[st + "_start_kind"] == "MISSING"})
    events = pd.DataFrame(records)
    return pm4py.format_dataframe(events, case_id="case:concept:name",
                                  activity_key="concept:name", timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
                                  start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")


# Observed intervals only: 18 S1 and 12 S2. Open activities are kept in
# station_frame, not fabricated as completed PM4Py interval events.
observed_intervals = interval_events(df)
print("\nPM4Py-compatible observed intervals (count by station)")
print(observed_intervals.groupby("concept:name").size().to_string())

# A completed-case projection with a zero-duration external-arrival marker
# makes case span = end-to-end sojourn and case waiting = S1 wait + S2 wait.
# Explicitly central-imputed starts are marked. No censored case is included.
complete_cases = central.loc[central.s2_end.notna()].copy()
projection = interval_events(complete_cases, external_arrival=True)
before = set(projection.columns)
case_level = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(
    projection.copy(), start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp")
case_level = pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate(case_level)
added = [c for c in case_level.columns if c not in before and c != "@@diff_start_end"]
manual = complete_cases.set_index("case")
expected_service = ((manual.s1_end - manual.s1_start) +
                    (manual.s2_end - manual.s2_start)).dt.total_seconds()
expected_sojourn = (manual.s2_end - manual.arrival_s1).dt.total_seconds()
case_summary = case_level.groupby("case:concept:name", sort=True)[added].first()
assert (case_summary["@@service_time"].reindex(manual.index) - expected_service).abs().max() < 1e-8
assert (case_summary["@@sojourn_time"].reindex(manual.index) - expected_sojourn).abs().max() < 1e-8
assert (case_summary["@@waiting_time"].reindex(manual.index) -
        (expected_sojourn - expected_service)).abs().max() < 1e-8
print("\nPM4Py CASE-LEVEL columns, completed-case projection only; seconds")
print(case_level.groupby("case:concept:name", sort=True)[added].first().round(2).to_string())
print("Projection excludes eight unfinished cases; its arrival/finish columns",
      "are inter-arrival/inter-finish gaps in seconds (first-case zero sentinel),",
      "not station flow rates in cases/hour.")
```
