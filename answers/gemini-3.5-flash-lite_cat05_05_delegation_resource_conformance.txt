## 2. Rule Interpretation

- **RR1 (Drafter/Approver Separation):** The human performing `Draft_Change_Request` must not perform `Approve_Change` for the same case. Normalized resource IDs are checked.
- **RR2 (Implementer/Verifier Separation):** The human performing `Implement_Change` must not perform `Verify_Implementation` for the same case. Normalized resource IDs are checked.
- **RR3 (High-Risk Approval Authority):** HIGH-risk changes require approval by the QM (Asha Rao) or a valid QM delegate. A delegation is valid only if:
  1. Effective interval covers the event timestamp.
  2. Scope covers the change category.
  3. Principal is absent or formally unavailable.
  4. No prohibited subdelegation occurred.
- **RR4 (Emergency Substitution):** High-risk emergency changes may be implemented after approval by the Incident Commander (IC) or valid IC delegate. A QM or valid QM delegate must perform `Retrospective_QM_Review` within **two business days** (calculated as 48 hours / 2 calendar business days excluding weekends, counting from the completion of `Verify_Implementation` or implementation time).
- **RR5 (Identity Evidence):** Service accounts (`svc_qms`) require reliable session evidence (FIDO token, badge logs) to map to an authorized human. Aliases (`u_bk` and `b.koch`) must be merged before SoD checks.
- **RR6 (Suspicion versus Proof):** Repeated pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, and concentrated handovers are heuristic indicators of risk, *not* definitive proof of collusion without corroborating evidence (e.g., communication intercepts, concurrent logins).
- **Principal on Leave Handling:** An action performed by a principal (e.g., Asha Rao on annual leave) while audit logs show active VPN sessions is evaluated as technically valid under standing role authority, though flagged for administrative review due to policy conflicts with leave status.
- **Delegation Database Recording:** A later database entry (e.g., QMS database record created 2026-08-12 for D-QM-01) does *not* retroactively validate an action if the contemporaneous signed PDF timestamp (2026-08-09) and effective interval cover the event. However, unrecorded delegations rely entirely on contemporaneous physical or digital audit trails.

1. **Repeated Pairs (Clara Stein & Ben Koch):**

   - *Benign Explanation:* Operational specialization; Clara is the primary process engineer for that specific production cell, and Ben is the designated QE assigned to support it.
   - *Discriminator:* Detailed technical review logs showing substantive feedback vs. instant rubber-stamping; process yield metrics.
2. **Reciprocal Behavior (Ben drafting low-risk training changes approved by Clara):**
   - *Adverse Explanation:* Quid pro quo arrangement trading approvals.
   - *Benign Explanation:* Cross-functional training task coverage during resource shortages.
   - *Discriminator:* Interviews regarding task allocation and peer review records.
3. **Shared Workstations & Service Accounts (`svc_qms` on WS-DM-04):**
   - *Adverse Explanation:* Intentional masquerading to hide the true executor of verification.
   - *Benign Explanation:* Convenience terminal left logged in by Maintenance Lead (Diego) while automated QMS integration script ran.

   - *Benign Explanation:* Principal logging in remotely from vacation for a critical decision; colleague utilizing shared desk terminal while legitimate user is away.

## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation Creation:** Require all delegations to be created and approved within the QMS database with automated validation rules preventing conflicting intervals or scope mismatches, eliminating reliance on retrospective PDF records.
2. **Effective-Time Recording:** Implement strict timestamp boundaries on delegation activation tokens that automatically revoke permissions at the exact expiration minute.
3. **Principal Availability:** Integrate HR leave and medical sick-leave calendars directly into the QMS authorization engine so that standing roles are automatically suspended or transferred upon leave commencement.
4. **Alias Management:** Enforce single, immutable user identifiers across all sub-systems (VPN, QMS, FIDO tokens, badge logs) to prevent manual alias aliasing (`b.koch` vs `u_bk`).
5. **Individual Authentication:** Prohibit shared workstation service-account actions (`svc_qms`) without mandatory FIDO2 hardware token re-authentication at the terminal.
6. **Emergency Review:** Configure automated calendar alerts and workflow locks for HIGH-risk emergency changes requiring Retrospective QM Review within the strict 48-hour window.
7. **Pair Rotation:** Establish mandatory rotation policies for Quality Engineers and Process Engineers to prevent concentration of approvals (e.g., the Clara/Ben pairing frequency).
8. **Network Monitoring:** Deploy real-time session tracking to flag concurrent logins (e.g., Asha logging in via VPN while officially marked on annual leave).
9. **Evidential Retention:** Mandate automated audit trail archiving for CCTV logs, badge swipes, and workstation terminal assignments linked to critical QMS events.
