# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

The supplied tables fix activities, service ranges, tail probabilities, skill multipliers, shifts, breaks, two-hour arrival means, and five shocks. They do not fix distributions inside ranges, the joint law of priority and case type, the arrival process, the interpretation of the preemption penalty, or who is allowed to do what. Those are assumptions. Observations below are only rearrangements of the given tables. Estimates use the assumptions. Bounds hold more broadly.

**A1. Service mixture.** Inside each band, base time is uniform and independent across activities and cases. With tail probability \(p\),

\[
\mathbb{E}[T] = (1-p)\frac{L+H}{2} + p\frac{L_c+H_c}{2}.
\]

That gives Intake 6.94, Mechanical 26.30, Electrical 32.82, Software 38.50, Validation 15.74 minutes. Resource time is base draw times that resource’s multiplier. U4 doubles diagnostic tail probabilities only (Mechanical 0.30, Electrical 0.36, Software 0.40), which lifts those means to 29.60, 37.14, and 44.50. The uniform midpoint is a planning estimate, not a measured mean. The ordinary-low corner (tail ignored, lower endpoint, multiplier 1) and the all-multiplier-1 midpoint are bounds used in Section 2.

**A2. Demand.** Within a block, priority and case type are independent, each following the stated marginals. Arrivals are Poisson with the block mean and uniform inside the block. Needed because no joint distribution or within-block profile is given. Expected counts are exact under the marginals even if the Poisson assumption is wrong; queueing delay is not.

**A3. Calendar and SLA clock.** The day starts empty at 08:00. SLA time is calendar elapsed from arrival to Validation complete, so breaks, the U5 outage, and the unstaffed night count. P1 / P2 / P3 targets are 120, 480, and 1440 minutes. Residual work may continue on the same roster next day with no new arrivals, through 20:00 next day, so a P3 deadline can be observed. That second day is an assumption required to score P3; it is not a second demand scenario.

**A4. Teams, handover, setup.** Diagnostic families are Mechanical, Electrical, and Software. The cross-team penalty uses two teams: mechanical versus electrical/software. A resource change on Intake, a diagnostic, or Validation costs 7 minutes and occupies the receiver. A move between those two teams costs 5 further receiver-minutes unless the same cross-skilled person keeps the case. Setup of 5 minutes applies when a resource starts a diagnostic family different from their previous diagnostic on a different case. The wording “between consecutive cases” is followed, so a sequential ME/ES pair on one person does not pay setup. Handover, setup, cross-team time, and the 8-minute parallel coordination are not service. Coordination is charged only after parallel ME or ES, to one resource, before Validation. The sender is not held for the 7 minutes. That can understate occupancy slightly.

**A5. Preemption.** A P1 may interrupt a P3 diagnostic only, at most once per victim case, and only while that diagnostic is in service. Progress is kept. Resumed duration equals remaining service plus 10% of remaining service at the cut. This reading follows the phrase that the penalty equals 10% of remaining time. A full restart would be a different, harsher policy and is not assumed. Intake, Validation, handover, setup, and break-holds are not preempted. Breaks pause the current piece with no restart penalty. Any gap longer than 60 minutes returns the piece to the queue so the morning can reprioritize. Overtime of at most 30 minutes is available to every policy under the same rule: finish work already started, and start new work in overtime only if it is P1. Routing, not a hidden overtime privilege, is what differs.

**A6. Shocks.** U1 multiplies 08:00–12:00 arrivals by 1.7 and adds 8 percentage points to P1; P2 and P3 are rescaled in their original ratio so the three shares sum to 1. U2 keeps block volumes fixed, doubles the E and ES proportions in 12:00–16:00, and renormalizes all five type shares. A literal reading that doubles E and ES counts and adds extra arrivals is reported as a bound, not as the primary case. U3 removes Chen for both days. U4 is the tail change in A1. U5 is U1 plus U3 plus an Intake blackout from 09:30 to 10:00. During that blackout, Intake waits and in-progress Intake is released so the person can do diagnostic work; other activities continue.

**A7. Parallel versus sequential.** Decided at the end of Intake, not at arrival. Specialization always runs ME and ES sequentially, scarce-family first (Software, then Electrical, then Mechanical). Pooling and the hybrid run the two branches in parallel only when two distinct qualified people are free and the case is P1, or when the case is P2 and both skill queues are otherwise empty. P3 is sequential. Parallel mode pays the 8-minute coordination delay. Sequential mode on one cross-skilled person avoids that delay and avoids the cross-team penalty.

**A8. Small P1 counts.** Base expected P1 volume is 5.44 cases. One miss is already about an 18-point SLA hit on a five-case day. The 90% target is a high-variance statistic. Policies are compared on elapsed-time distributions as well as on the pass rate.

**Objective O1, tail and SLA risk.** For policy \(\pi\) and scenario \(\omega\),

\[
J_1(\pi)=\max_\omega\Big(5[0.90-S_{P1}]_+ + 2[0.90-S_{P2}]_+ + [0.90-S_{P3}]_+\Big)
\]

with \(S\) the share completed inside the elapsed target. The worst supplied scenario dominates; the average of p90 elapsed time breaks ties. Weights express that a P1 shortfall is the costly failure. Mean delay is reported and is not the objective.

**Objective O2, fairness and robustness.** On day-0 contracted minutes (shift minus the stated break, overtime excluded from the denominator),

\[
J_2(\pi)=\frac{1}{6}\sum_\omega\Big(\max_r[u_r-0.90]_+ + 0.25(1-\mathrm{Jain}(u)) + H_\omega/60\Big)
\]

where \(H\) is handover + cross-team + setup + coordination minutes per case, and Jain is \((\sum u)^2 / (n\sum u^2)\). The base scenario calls any individual planned utilization above 90% unfair and fragile. Decision rule: minimize \(J_1\), then \(J_2\). A policy that wins base-case mean delay and loses U1/U3/U5 is not robust under this rule.

**Decision variables.** Home-queue membership. Overflow wait gates (20 / 60 / 180 minutes for P1 / P2 / P3) and donor-queue gates (10 / 30 / 90). P1 reservation of the last idle home resource, released after 10 idle minutes. Parallel rule by priority. Preemption gate (P3 diagnostic, remaining above 15 minutes, 10% penalty, once). Validation/Intake deferral while a bottleneck diagnostic queue is non-empty. Fairness redirect only inside a 10-minute speed-loss band. Placement of the 30-minute overtime, especially 16:00–16:30. Farah’s diagnostic permission. Mode switch among hybrid, light-day pooling, and P1-protection. Break placement is not a free variable; the table treats it as fixed. Headcount is not increased.

# 3. Candidate policies

Shared mechanics, so the comparison is the routing: P1 may preempt a P3 diagnostic with more than 15 minutes of service left, once, with the 10% remaining-time penalty. Overtime up to 30 minutes follows A5. A resource takes Intake or Validation only when they do not already see a diagnostic they are supposed to clear, unless nobody else can take the support work. Farah takes waiting Intake before other work while she is on duty. Within a priority, order is oldest queue-entry first. Speed and fairness never overtake priority.

**Policy A — fixed skill queues.** Mechanical: Ada, Ben. Electrical: Chen and Deepa, or Deepa alone if Chen is absent. Software: Emil while his window is open; Farah only when Emil’s window is closed, which covers 08:00–12:00 and Emil’s break, not “Emil is busy.” Chen does not take Software. Ada does not take Electrical. Deepa does not take Mechanical. Farah does not take Mechanical or Electrical. ME/ES are always sequential, scarce branch first, so the cross-team penalty is paid on ME whenever the two branches use different people. No overflow, no reservation, no P3 aging. Dispatch score among eligible people: priority, then queue entry, then multiplier, then lower day-utilization inside a 10-minute speed-loss band, then the person who already holds the case. This is implementable on a board with three queues. Its morning Software path is Farah whenever Chen is barred from Software, including the base morning, by the fixed-queue rule.

**Policy B — full pooling.** Any finite multiplier may be assigned. No reserved slot. Parallelism as in A7. Score, lowest wins: (1) whether this person should defer Intake/Validation because their home diagnostic queue is waiting and a peer is free, (2) priority, (3) queue entry, (4) outside the 10-minute speed-loss band of the best free eligible multiplier, (5) utilization if the case is not P1, (6) multiplier, (7) a handover penalty if they do not already hold the case, (8) a setup penalty if the diagnostic family would change across cases. The 10-minute band stops Farah, Ada-on-Electrical, and Emil-on-Electrical from taking work a 1.00 person can take, including when the fast person is busier. There is no numeric blend of unrelated units. A dispatcher can recompute the order. Pooling has no P3 aging and no P1 reservation, so a morning of P2 arrivals can occupy every fast person before a P1 arrives.

**Policy C — hybrid home queues, reserved last slot, gated overflow.** Homes match Policy A, except Software’s home is Emil, and Chen remains Electrical-primary. Overflow is allowed only past a wait gate and a donor gate. A non-home qualified person may take a P1 diagnostic once it has waited 20 minutes, a P2 at 60 minutes, or a P3 at 180 minutes, and only if that person’s own home-queue head has waited less than 10, 30, or 90 minutes respectively. Farah may take a P1 diagnostic after 10 minutes; she may not take P2 or P3 diagnostics. The last idle home resource for a skill does not start a new P3 while a P1 that needs that skill arrived in the last 60 minutes and is still open; the hold ends after 10 idle minutes so the slot is not wasted. A P3 that has waited 240 minutes is served in the P2 band when no P1 is waiting on that activity, which is the anti-starvation rule. Parallelism follows A7, and a P1 may use a second qualified person immediately for a parallel branch rather than waiting out the 20-minute overflow gate. P1 Software prefers Emil, then Chen, then Farah, which is what the multiplier order already does when all three are free. At 15:45 the overtime bridge is explicit: if Software or Electrical P1/P2 work is open, Chen’s overtime covers 16:00–16:30 first; Ben’s covers Mechanical if Deepa is on Electrical; Ada’s covers Validation or Electrical. That is the operational patch for Emil’s break coinciding with the shift change.

**Parallel, sequential, and Validation in one place.** Sequential on Ada for ME costs about 26 + 44 = 70 diagnostic minutes of her time and about 70 minutes of elapsed diagnostic time. Parallel Ben plus Chen costs about 41 minutes elapsed plus 8 minutes coordination and occupies Chen for about 33 minutes. Policy C spends that second person on P1, and on P2 only when both queues are empty. Validation stays off Chen, Deepa, and Emil while their diagnostic queues are non-empty, because a 7-minute handover is cheaper than losing 16 minutes of a 1.00 Electrical or Software server. When those queues are empty, the holder keeps the case and the handover is skipped.

# 4. Scenario comparison

**Scale, before the numbers.** Delays are elapsed hours from arrival to Validation, including the night, under the central mixture, with P1/P2 priority. They are ex-ante ranges from the Section 2 bounds plus the routing differences, not output from a run of Section 8. Judgment band is roughly ±1–2 hours on means and about ±10–15 points on SLAs. P1 rates are especially noisy at about five base-day cases. `p90_or_p95_delay` is an elapsed p90 range. `max_resource_utilization` is day-0 occupancy over contracted minutes and may exceed 100% when overtime is used. `utilization_fairness` is High when substitutable people stay within about 15 points of each other and idle-with-a-queue does not persist; Medium when the spread is wider but the only person above 90% is a structural single server (Emil after 16:00); Low when a qualified person is idle while that skill waits, or the spread is wide. `handover_or_setup_burden` is overhead minutes per case. `resilience` is High when this scenario still has a plausible path to the P1 90% target and P2 near 70%; Medium when P1 is likely 70–90% or P2 is 50–70%; Low when P1 is likely under 70% or P2 under 50%. `main_failure_mode` is the binding mechanism.

The central mixture puts every policy above 90% on Emil, and usually on Chen or Deepa, in the base afternoon. That is the infeasibility result of Section 2, not a tuning failure. # 5. Robust recommendation

**Primary policy is C, the hybrid, with two explicit modes.** Under O1 it is the only one of the three with a base P1 band that still touches 90%, a U2 band that stays near that target, and a U3/U5 band that fails by less than A or B. It does not win by shaving base mean delay. Pooling’s base mean is in the same 4–7 hour band. The split is the worst-scenario P1 shortfall: U1, U3, and U5. Specialization adds handover and, by barring Chen from Software, spends the best morning Software multiplier on Electrical while Farah runs Software at 1.40. That is a base-day defect, not only a shock defect.

O2 does not reverse the choice. All three policies put Emil, and usually Chen or Deepa, above 90% under the central mixture, so a hard reading of the base fairness rule is infeasible on that mixture. Among policies, C keeps the fairness redirect inside the 10-minute speed-loss band, so Ada and Ben share Mechanical when both are near 1.00, and Farah does not absorb ordinary Electrical just to even the percentages. Specialization leaves Ben underused in U2 while Electrical is late, which is the unfair pattern the cap was meant to catch. Pooling can pile non-P1 work onto whoever became free, with no aging and no reservation.

**Default mode (protected hybrid).** Homes, gates, reservation, P1-only preemption of long P3 diagnostics, P1 parallelization, Validation deferred off scarce diagnostic servers, overtime bridge at 15:45.

**Fallback mode 1, P1-protection.** Trigger: any open P1 has waited more than 30 minutes, or completed P1 cases over the last two hours are under 80% of P1 arrivals in that window. Then freeze new P3 starts, allow every qualified person including Farah onto P1, allow P1 to preempt P2 as well as P3 (still once per victim, same penalty, remaining above 15 minutes), and parallelize every P1 ME/ES that has two partners. Revert after the P1 queue has been empty and no P1 wait exceeds 10 minutes for 30 minutes. This mode will miss more P2 and P3 cases. That is the O1 trade.

**Fallback mode 2, light-day pooling.** Trigger: no shock flag, every skill head-wait under 15 minutes, and day-to-date utilization of every resource under 75% for 60 minutes. Then use Policy B so retention can drive handover toward zero while the 90% cap is actually feasible. Return to protected hybrid when any utilization crosses 85% or any P1 wait crosses 10 minutes. This is the regime in which the ordinary-low service draw lives, and the regime in which the base fairness sentence can be satisfied.

**Switch thresholds already inside mode C.** Overflow at 20 / 60 / 180 minutes. Donor refusal at 10 / 30 / 90. Reservation release at 10 idle minutes. P3 aging at 240 minutes. Preemption only above 15 minutes remaining. Overtime call at 15:45 for the 16:00–16:30 hole.

**Where another policy is preferable.** Light realized load favors B on mean delay and on handover, with the same P1 outcome, so O1 is indifferent and a pure mean-delay objective would pick B. A day whose dispatch estimates are being gamed, or a crew that cannot be audited on eligible-sets, favors A for the skills whose mix is stable, because A does not depend on a wait calculation beyond a visible queue. U2 with a trusted board favors C over A because the spilled Mechanical capacity is usable as gated Electrical overflow. U5 favors C plus mode 1, and still requires telling operations that the 90% P1 target is not attainable at the central mixture with this roster. No routing policy meets O1 with a zero shortfall on U5.

**Staffing action that sits outside the three policies.** Emil’s break, 16:00–16:30, is exactly the shift change. Moving that break earlier or later would remove the Software blackout. The supplied table does not authorize that move, so the policies treat the break as fixed and cover it with Chen’s overtime only when P1/P2 Software or Electrical is open. Adding headcount is not assumed. If a future roster change is possible, a Software-capable window after 16:00 is the highest-value hour, ahead of more morning Mechanical capacity.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

**Priority inflation.** C and B serve P1 first. If callers label ordinary work P1, the reservation and the preemption rule fire on the wrong cases, P2’s 8-hour target collapses, and the recorded P1 SLA becomes a fiction. Compare each block’s P1 share with the historical band in the demand table (about 5–18%). A block that jumps more than 10 points without a declared surge should be reviewed the same day. The multiplier matrix used for speed is published and not self-reported, which limits one gaming path. Remaining-time estimates are not an input to A, B, or C. A shortest-expected-remaining-time rule would invite technicians to inflate Software and Electrical estimates and would starve long complex jobs; it is not recommended on O1 or on auditability.

**P3 starvation.** Strict priority plus a P1 hold can push P3 through the night repeatedly, and a second heavy day would start from a backlog this analysis sets to zero. C’s 240-minute aging rule admits an old P3 into the P2 band when no P1 is waiting on that activity. A and B lack that rule, so their P3 tails are the ones to watch. Aging does not let P3 pass a waiting P1.

**Skill erosion.** Farah on Intake-only duty loses diagnostic practice. Emil on Software-only duty loses Electrical practice. A preserves depth and also prevents Chen from maintaining Software. C’s gated overflow is a small, irregular dose of cross-skill work. On light-day mode, a planned handful of cross-skill cases per week is safer than hoping overflow happens to be enough practice. Do not use Farah as the practice sink for ordinary Electrical; the 1.40 multiplier is an expensive classroom.

**Preemption churn.** The once-per-case cap, the P3-only default, and the 15-minute floor keep the expected penalty small: 10% of a remaining 20-minute job is 2 minutes. Mode 1, which also preempts P2, will create rework and resentment if it is left on after the P1 queue clears. The revert rule is part of the control. Preempting a job with 5 minutes left would cost a handover and a setup for almost no slack; the floor exists to stop that.

**Coordination and Validation.** Parallel P1 adds 8 minutes and a second person. Using it on every ME/ES case would steal Electrical and Software capacity in the 14:00–16:00 block, where the fluid gap is already at least 81 base-minutes. The P2 parallel gate (both queues empty) is what stops that. The new bottleneck after a good diagnostic rule is Validation: about 661 base-minutes, and in the 14:00–16:00 greedy sketch the fast Electrical and Software people are saturated, so Validation spills onto Ada, Ben, and Farah. Pulling Chen or Emil into Validation to “keep the case” saves 7 minutes for that case and loses about 16 minutes of scarce diagnostic capacity. C defers that keep-case choice whenever the diagnostic queue is open. Monitor Validation queue time on its own; a dashboard that only shows end-to-end delay will blame Intake.

**Fairness.** Under the central mixture the 90% sentence cannot be met for every person if P1 and P2 are also met. Pretending otherwise will produce either hidden overtime or a queue that is blamed on the dispatcher. The workable reading is: no substitutable pair (Ada and Ben on Mechanical, Chen and Deepa on Electrical) should differ by more than about 15 points while both are on duty, Farah is not a dump queue, and Emil above 90% after 16:00 is reported as a roster hole rather than as a performance miss. C’s redirect works only inside the 10-minute speed-loss band so fairness does not assign Electrical to Farah.

**Interpretability.** Each assignment should store the eligible set, the rule that fired (`home`, `overflow-p1`, `reserve-hold`, `aging-p3`, `overtime-bridge`, `mode-1`), and the policy version. A single weighted score is harder to defend after a missed P1. The gates are in minutes on a clock the floor can see. That is the reason C is preferred to a richer optimization scaffold for live dispatch even though an offline linear program is useful for the capacity audit.

**Mode oscillation.** Flapping between light-day pooling and hybrid will itself cause setup and handover. The 60-minute and 30-minute dwell times on the two fallbacks are there to damp that. Shock flags (U1-style surge declared, absence, outage) should force protected hybrid or mode 1 immediately rather than wait for utilization to climb.

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

**Case clock.** `sla_clock_origin` is the arrival timestamp. `sla_target_min` is 120, 480, or 1440. `sla_deadline` is origin plus target. Attainment is Validation complete at or before the deadline. Incomplete cases at the end of the next contracted day count as misses for that deadline, not as censored successes.

**Event attributes to store on every interval.** `case:concept:name`, `concept:name` (Intake, Mechanical, Electrical, Software, Validation, Handover, Cross-team handover, Setup, Coordination), `start_timestamp`, `time:timestamp`, `org:resource`. Also `priority`, `case_type`, `skill_used`, `eligible_resource_set`, `assignment_rule`, `policy_version`, `mode`, `planned_service_min`, `actual_service_min`, `resource_multiplier`, `queue_entry_timestamp`, `queue_name`, `segment_index`, `continuation`, `pause_before_min`, `preempted_flag`, `preemption_penalty_min`, `parallel_branch`, `scenario_id`, `shock_flag`, `sla_clock_origin`, `sla_target_min`, `sla_deadline`. Pause/resume is a pair of attributes on the next segment (`pause_before_min`, `continuation`) so a break does not become a fake activity in the handover graph. A fragment cut by preemption is a new segment of the same `concept:name` with `preempted_flag` set; the victim’s penalty is the extra planned service, not a second case.

**Deployment indicators.** P1/P2/P3 attainment on elapsed time, with counts beside rates. p90 elapsed by priority and by arrival block. Queue time by `concept:name`, measured as first-segment start minus `queue_entry_timestamp`, excluding continuation segments. Overhead minutes per case (Handover, Cross-team handover, Setup, Coordination). Preemption count and penalty minutes. Share of diagnostic minutes on Farah, on Ada-Electrical, and on Emil-Electrical, as misuse gauges. Day-0 utilization and Jain index, plus the pairwise gaps Ada–Ben and Chen–Deepa. Parallel fraction among ME and ES by priority. Overflow count by rule id. Incomplete cases at 20:00. P1 share by block against the historical band.

**What PM4Py will and will not say.** `format_dataframe` with the start and complete timestamps produces an interval log. `get_service_time` on that log, grouped by scenario, is the right detector for U4: diagnostic means should rise on the order of 12–16% while Intake and Validation stay put. A shift in Electrical or Software service time with a stable Intake time is a service-distribution change, not a routing change. `discover_handover_of_work_network` on `org:resource` shows who passes work to whom. Specialization should concentrate ME edges across the mechanical pair and the electrical pair. Hybrid overflow should add a smaller set of cross edges (Ada→electrical work, Chen→Software) that appear when `shock_flag` is U2 or U3 and should be rare in the light-day mode. A thick Farah→diagnostic edge on ordinary P2/P3 is a policy breach.

`insert_case_service_waiting_time` and case-level `get_service_time` sum activity durations and compare them with case sojourn. That quantity is case effort only when intervals do not overlap. Parallel ME/ES overlaps, so summed service can exceed sojourn and the implied waiting time can be zero or negative. Those case-level fields are labeled as PM4Py sojourn accounting, not as queue time. Queue time is the stage metric from `queue_entry_timestamp`. Pause minutes are `pause_before_min`, not queue time and not service. Overhead activities are reported separately from Intake, the three diagnostics, and Validation.

Control-flow discovery (inductive miner or a DFG) distinguishes sequential ME/ES from parallel ME/ES: the parallel policy produces an AND-split between the two diagnostics, the sequential policy a chain, plus a Coordination activity only on the parallel path. Performance drift that leaves that graph unchanged is capacity or speed drift; a graph that flips from chain to AND-split is a real policy-mode change. Bottleneck migration is read from hourly queue time and utilization by skill and resource, filtered by `scenario_id` or `shock_flag`, not from a single whole-day DFG. The base pattern to confirm is a 14:00–16:00 Electrical and Validation queue, then a 16:00 Software queue on Emil. U1 and U5 should move the long queues into 08:00–12:00. U2 should lengthen Electrical and shorten Mechanical in the afternoon. U3 should put morning Software on Farah. If a “U2” log shows the morning Software signature instead, the shock flag or the routing implementation is wrong.

# 8. Reproducible Python

The script encodes the tables, prints the fluid audit behind Section 2, and runs a pull-system discrete-event simulation for policies A, B, and C across base and U1–U5. Service draws are shared across policies for the same seed and scenario. Handover, cross-team delay, setup, coordination, parallel versus sequential release, preemption, breaks, overtime, and the U5 Intake blackout are explicit constants and branches. Section 4’s ranges are ex-ante judgments; the script is the estimator that replaces them when run. PM4Py case-level waiting time is printed beside manual stage queue times so parallel overlap is not mistaken for queue time.

```python
#!/usr/bin/env python3
"""Incident-desk policy comparison: fluid audit plus discrete-event simulation.

Assumptions implemented
- Uniform mixture inside the published bands; independent priority and type.
- Poisson arrivals, uniform inside each two-hour block; empty start.
- Handover 7 (receiver), cross-team 5 (receiver, waived if same person),
  setup 5 only when the diagnostic family changes across cases,
  coordination 8 only after parallel ME/ES.
- Preemption: P1 cuts a P3 diagnostic with >15 min left, once, resume =
  remaining * 1.10. Not a full restart. Not during handover/setup/pause.
- Gaps >60 min requeue the piece. Intake during the U5 outage is released.
- Overtime <=30 min; new overtime work must be P1. Same rule for all policies.
- Residual next day uses the same roster and no new demand, horizon 20:00+1 day.
- PM4Py case waiting/service is sojourn accounting, not stage queue time.
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import heapq
import inspect
from collections import defaultdict
from copy import deepcopy
from datetime import datetime, timedelta

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

try:
    import pm4py
except ImportError:  # pragma: no cover
    pm4py = None

T0 = datetime(2026, 9, 22, 8, 0, 0)
HORIZON = (24 + 12) * 60  # 08:00 day 0 through 20:00 day 1
N_SEEDS = 5
DIAG = ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software")
OVERHEAD = ("Handover", "Cross-team handover", "Setup", "Coordination")
REAL = ("Intake", "Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software", "Validation")

HANDOVER_MIN = 7.0
CROSS_TEAM_MIN = 5.0
SETUP_MIN = 5.0
COORD_MIN = 8.0
PREEMPT_PENALTY_FRACTION = 0.10
PREEMPT_MIN_REMAINING = 15.0
P1_OVERFLOW_MIN = 20.0
P2_OVERFLOW_MIN = 60.0
P3_OVERFLOW_MIN = 180.0
P3_AGING_MIN = 240.0
RESERVE_LOOKBACK_MIN = 60.0
RESERVE_IDLE_RELEASE_MIN = 10.0
DONOR_MAX_WAIT = {"P1": 10.0, "P2": 30.0, "P3": 90.0}
SPEED_LOSS_BAND_MIN = 10.0
MAX_SHORT_GAP = 60.0
OT_MIN = 30.0
SLA_TARGET = {"P1": 120.0, "P2": 480.0, "P3": 1440.0}
FAMILY = {"Mechanical": "M", "Electrical": "E", "Software": "S"}
TEAM = {"Mechanical": "mech", "Electrical": "es", "Software": "es"}
TYPE_ACTS = {
    "M": ["Mechanical"],
    "E": ["Electrical"],
    "S": ["Software"],
    "ME": ["Mechanical", "Electrical"],
    "ES": ["Electrical", "Software"],
}
SCARCE_RANK = {"Software": 0, "Electrical": 1, "Mechanical": 2}

# ordinary (lo, hi), complex (lo, hi), tail probability
SERVICE = {
    "Intake": ((5.0, 8.0), (10.0, 14.0), 0.08),
    "Mechanical": ((18.0, 28.0), (35.0, 55.0), 0.15),
    "Electrical": ((22.0, 35.0), (40.0, 65.0), 0.18),
    "Software": ((25.0, 40.0), (45.0, 80.0), 0.20),
    "Validation": ((10.0, 18.0), (22.0, 35.0), 0.12),
}

# None means not qualified. Coordination/overhead are unskilled fixed durations.
MULT = {
    "Ada": {"Intake": 1.00, "Mechanical": 1.00, "Electrical": 1.35, "Software": None, "Validation": 1.15},
    "Ben": {"Intake": 1.10, "Mechanical": 1.00, "Electrical": None, "Software": None, "Validation": 1.00},
    "Chen": {"Intake": 1.10, "Mechanical": None, "Electrical": 1.00, "Software": 1.25, "Validation": 1.10},
    "Deepa": {"Intake": 1.00, "Mechanical": 1.25, "Electrical": 1.00, "Software": None, "Validation": 1.00},
    "Emil": {"Intake": 1.10, "Mechanical": None, "Electrical": 1.30, "Software": 1.00, "Validation": 1.00},
    "Farah": {"Intake": 1.00, "Mechanical": 1.40, "Electrical": 1.40, "Software": 1.40, "Validation": 1.20},
}
ALL_NAMES = list(MULT)

# shift start/end hour, break (start_hour, start_min, end_hour, end_min) or None
ROSTER = {
    "Ada": (8, 16, (12, 15, 12, 45)),
    "Ben": (8, 16, (12, 0, 12, 30)),
    "Chen": (8, 16, (12, 30, 13, 0)),
    "Deepa": (10, 18, (14, 0, 14, 30)),
    "Emil": (12, 20, (16, 0, 16, 30)),

DEMAND_ROWS = [
    # start, end, expected, P1, P2, P3, M, E, S, ME, ES   (percents)
    (0, 120, 7, 10, 55, 35, 30, 25, 15, 20, 10),
    (120, 240, 9, 15, 55, 30, 25, 25, 15, 25, 10),
    (240, 360, 7, 12, 58, 30, 22, 28, 18, 20, 12),

]


def e_service(activity: str, p_scale: float = 1.0) -> float:
    (lo, hi), (clo, chi), p = SERVICE[activity]
    p = min(1.0, p * p_scale)
    return (1.0 - p) * 0.5 * (lo + hi) + p * 0.5 * (clo + chi)


def sample_base(activity: str, rng: np.random.Generator, p_scale: float) -> float:
    (lo, hi), (clo, chi), p = SERVICE[activity]
    p = min(1.0, p * p_scale)
    if rng.random() < p:
        return float(rng.uniform(clo, chi))
    return float(rng.uniform(lo, hi))


def minute_of(hour: int, minute: int, day_offset: int) -> float:
    return (hour - 8) * 60 + minute + day_offset


def build_windows(name: str, day_offset: int) -> tuple[list[tuple[float, float]], list[tuple[float, float]]]:
    start_h, end_h, br = ROSTER[name]
    s = minute_of(start_h, 0, day_offset)
    e = minute_of(end_h, 0, day_offset)
    contracted = []
    if br is None:
        contracted.append((s, e))
    else:
        b0 = minute_of(br[0], br[1], day_offset)
        b1 = minute_of(br[2], br[3], day_offset)
        contracted.append((s, b0))
        contracted.append((b1, e))
    ot = [(e, e + OT_MIN)]
    def trim(wins):
        out = []
        for a, b in wins:
            if b <= 0 or a >= HORIZON:
                continue
            out.append((max(0.0, a), min(float(HORIZON), b)))
        return [(a, b) for a, b in out if b - a > 1e-9]
    return trim(contracted), trim(ot)


def scenario_demand(scenario: str) -> list[dict]:
    blocks = []
    for start, end, exp, p1, p2, p3, m, e, s, me, es in DEMAND_ROWS:
        p1, p2, p3 = p1 / 100.0, p2 / 100.0, p3 / 100.0
        m, e, s, me, es = m / 100.0, e / 100.0, s / 100.0, me / 100.0, es / 100.0
        if scenario in ("U1", "U5") and start < 240:
            exp *= 1.7
            p1 = p1 + 0.08
            rest = p2 + p3
            scale = (1.0 - p1) / rest
            p2, p3 = p2 * scale, p3 * scale
        if scenario == "U2" and start in (240, 360):
            e *= 2.0
            es *= 2.0
            tot = m + e + s + me + es
            m, e, s, me, es = m / tot, e / tot, s / tot, me / tot, es / tot
        blocks.append({
            "start": start, "end": end, "expected": exp,
            "p": np.array([p1, p2, p3]),
            "t": np.array([m, e, s, me, es]),
        })
    return blocks


def p_scale_for(scenario: str) -> float:
    return 2.0 if scenario == "U4" else 1.0


def chen_absent(scenario: str) -> bool:
    return scenario in ("U3", "U5")


def outage_on(scenario: str) -> bool:
    return scenario == "U5"


def clock(minutes: float) -> datetime:
    return T0 + timedelta(minutes=float(minutes))


def fluid_audit() -> None:
    means = {a: e_service(a, 1.0) for a in SERVICE}
    means_u4 = {a: e_service(a, 2.0 if a in DIAG else 1.0) for a in SERVICE}
    print("MIXTURE MEANS base", {k: round(v, 2) for k, v in means.items()})
    print("MIXTURE MEANS U4 diag", {k: round(means_u4[k], 2) for k in DIAG})
    rows = []
    for scenario in ("base", "U1", "U2", "U3", "U4", "U5"):
        scale = p_scale_for(scenario)
        sm = {a: e_service(a, scale if a in DIAG else 1.0) for a in SERVICE}
        for b in scenario_demand(scenario):
            n = b["expected"]
            shares = dict(zip(["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"], b["t"]))
            mech = n * (shares["M"] + shares["ME"])
            elec = n * (shares["E"] + shares["ME"] + shares["ES"])
            soft = n * (shares["S"] + shares["ES"])
            rec = {
                "scenario": scenario, "block": f"{int(b['start']/60)+8:02d}-{int(b['end']/60)+8:02d}",
                "arrivals": round(n, 2),
                "intake_min": round(n * sm["Intake"], 1),
                "mech_min": round(mech * sm["Mechanical"], 1),
                "elec_min": round(elec * sm["Electrical"], 1),
                "soft_min": round(soft * sm["Software"], 1),
                "val_min": round(n * sm["Validation"], 1),
            }
            rec["offered"] = round(sum(rec[k] for k in ("intake_min", "mech_min", "elec_min", "soft_min", "val_min")), 1)
            rows.append(rec)
    fluid = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    cap = {"08-10": 480, "10-12": 600, "12-14": 510, "14-16": 570, "16-18": 210, "18-20": 120}
    base = fluid[fluid.scenario == "base"].copy()
    base["capacity"] = base["block"].map(cap)
    base["gap_at_mult_1"] = (base["capacity"] - base["offered"]).round(1)
    print("\nBASE OFFERED VS CONTRACTED (multiplier-1 lower bound on resource minutes)")
    print(base[["block", "arrivals", "intake_min", "mech_min", "elec_min", "soft_min", "val_min", "offered", "capacity", "gap_at_mult_1"]].to_string(index=False))
    day = base[["offered", "capacity"]].sum()
    print(f"DAY offered {day.offered:.1f}  contracted {day.capacity:.1f}  gap {day.capacity - day.offered:.1f}")
    print("Positive gap is slack that cannot be banked. Negative gap is a hard spill at multiplier 1.")
    print("\nSCENARIO DAY OFFERED BASE-MINUTES (U3/U5 change capacity, not this load total)")
    print(fluid.groupby("scenario")["offered"].sum().round(1).to_string())
    # anchors used in the written audit
    assert abs(means["Mechanical"] - 26.30) < 0.02
    assert abs(means["Electrical"] - 32.82) < 0.02
    assert abs(means["Software"] - 38.50) < 0.02
    assert abs(day.offered - 2720.6) < 2.0


class Item:

        self.end = end
        self.later = later


class Resource:
    def __init__(self, name, windows, contracted):
        self.name = name
        self.windows = windows
        self.contracted = contracted
        self.contracted_day0 = 0.0
        for a, b in contracted:
            self.contracted_day0 += max(0.0, min(b, 720.0) - max(a, 0.0))
        self.busy = False
        self.idle_since = 0.0
        self.last_diag_family = None
        self.last_case_id = None
        self.occupied_day0 = 0.0
        self.ot_day0 = 0.0

    def in_window(self, t: float) -> bool:
        return any(a <= t < b - 1e-9 for a, b in self.windows)

    def is_free(self, t: float) -> bool:
        return (not self.busy) and self.in_window(t)

    def next_open(self, t: float):
        for a, b in self.windows:
            if t < b - 1e-9:
                return t if a <= t else a
        return None

    def window_end(self, t: float):
        for a, b in self.windows:
            if a <= t < b - 1e-9:
                return b
        return None

    @property
    def util(self) -> float:
        if self.contracted_day0 <= 1e-6:
            return 0.0
        return self.occupied_day0 / self.contracted_day0


def make_resources(scenario: str) -> dict[str, Resource]:
    people = {}
    for name in ALL_NAMES:
        if name == "Chen" and chen_absent(scenario):
            people[name] = Resource(name, [], [])
            continue
        contracted, ot = [], []
        for day in (0, 1440):
            c, o = build_windows(name, day)
            contracted.extend(c)
            ot.extend(o)
        people[name] = Resource(name, contracted + ot, contracted)
    return people


def home_members(activity: str, scenario: str) -> list[str]:
    if activity == "Mechanical":
        return ["Ada", "Ben"]
    if activity == "Electrical":
        return ["Deepa"] if chen_absent(scenario) else ["Chen", "Deepa"]
    if activity == "Software":
        return ["Emil"]
    return list(ALL_NAMES)


def emil_window_open(resources, t: float) -> bool:
    return resources["Emil"].in_window(t)


def fixed_ok(policy: str, rname: str, activity: str, t: float, resources, scenario: str) -> bool:
    if MULT[rname].get(activity) is None and activity in REAL:
        return False
    if activity in OVERHEAD or activity in ("Intake", "Validation"):
        return True
    if policy == "pooling":
        return True
    homes = set(home_members(activity, scenario))
    if activity == "Software" and not emil_window_open(resources, t):
        homes = {"Farah"}
    return rname in homes


def oldest_wait(waiting, activity: str, t: float) -> float:
    waits = [t - it.queue_entry for it in waiting if it.activity == activity and it.state == "waiting" and it.ready <= t + 1e-9]
    return max(waits) if waits else 0.0


def donor_ok(rname: str, item, t: float, waiting, scenario: str) -> bool:
    limit = DONOR_MAX_WAIT[item.priority]
    for skill in DIAG:
        if rname in home_members(skill, scenario):
            if oldest_wait(waiting, skill, t) > limit:
                return False
    return True


def overflow_ok(rname: str, item, t: float, waiting, scenario: str) -> bool:
    if item.activity not in DIAG:
        return False
    if MULT[rname].get(item.activity) is None:
        return False
    waited = t - item.queue_entry
    if rname == "Farah":
        return item.priority == "P1" and waited >= 10.0
    gate = {"P1": P1_OVERFLOW_MIN, "P2": P2_OVERFLOW_MIN, "P3": P3_OVERFLOW_MIN}[item.priority]
    if waited < gate:
        return False
    return donor_ok(rname, item, t, waiting, scenario)


def recent_open_p1(cases, skill: str, t: float) -> bool:
    for c in cases:
        if c.priority != "P1" or c.finished is not None:
            continue
        if c.arrival < t - RESERVE_LOOKBACK_MIN:
            continue
        if skill in TYPE_ACTS[c.ctype] or (c.mode is None and skill in TYPE_ACTS[c.ctype]):
            # skill still relevant if that branch is not done
            if skill not in c.done:
                return True
    return False


def reserved_hold(resource, item, t, resources, waiting, cases, scenario) -> bool:
    if item.priority != "P3" or item.activity not in DIAG:
        return False
    if resource.idle_since is not None and t - resource.idle_since >= RESERVE_IDLE_RELEASE_MIN:
        return False
    for skill in DIAG:
        members = home_members(skill, scenario)
        if resource.name not in members:
            continue
        idle = [n for n in members if resources[n].is_free(t)]
        if idle == [resource.name] and recent_open_p1(cases, skill, t):
            return True
    return False


def allowed(policy, rname, item, t, resources, waiting, scenario) -> bool:
    if item.activity == "Intake" and outage_on(scenario) and 90.0 <= t < 120.0:
        return False
    if item.activity in REAL and MULT[rname].get(item.activity) is None:
        return False
    if policy == "pooling":
        return True
    if fixed_ok(policy, rname, item.activity, t, resources, scenario):
        return True
    if policy == "hybrid":
        return overflow_ok(rname, item, t, waiting, scenario)
    return False


def home_diag_waiting(rname, waiting, t, scenario) -> bool:
    for skill in DIAG:
        if rname in home_members(skill, scenario) and oldest_wait(waiting, skill, t) > 0:
            return True
    return False


def priority_rank(policy, item, t, waiting) -> int:
    base = {"P1": 0, "P2": 2, "P3": 3}[item.priority]
    if policy == "hybrid" and item.priority == "P3" and t - item.queue_entry >= P3_AGING_MIN:
        if not any(w.priority == "P1" and w.activity == item.activity and w.state == "waiting" for w in waiting):
            return 2
    return base


def expected_base(item) -> float:
    if item.base_minutes is not None:
        return item.base_minutes
    if item.remaining is not None and item.activity in REAL:
        return item.remaining  # already scaled; band uses a conservative stand-in
    if item.activity in SERVICE:
        return e_service(item.activity)
    return 8.0


def simulate(specs, predraw, policy: str, scenario: str, seed: int):
    resources = make_resources(scenario)
    cases = []
    for spec in specs:
        cases.append(Case(spec["cid"], spec["priority"], spec["ctype"], spec["arrival"]))
    by_id = {c.cid: c for c in cases}
    arrivals = sorted(cases, key=lambda c: c.arrival)
    arr_i = 0
    waiting: list[Item] = []
    running: list[Running] = []
    wakes: list[float] = []
    events = []
    t = 0.0
    steps = 0
    preempt_events = 0
    parallel_cases = 0

    def add_wake(when: float) -> None:
        if t < when < HORIZON:
            heapq.heappush(wakes, when)

    def log_row(item, rname, concept, start, end, continuation, pause_before, eligible, rule, penalty=0.0):
        if end - start <= 1e-6:
            return
        events.append({
            "case:concept:name": f"{scenario}|{policy}|{seed}|{item.case.cid}",
            "concept:name": concept,
            "start_timestamp": clock(start),
            "time:timestamp": clock(end),
            "org:resource": rname,
            "priority": item.case.priority,
            "case_type": item.case.ctype,
            "skill_used": FAMILY.get(concept, concept),
            "eligible_resource_set": eligible,
            "assignment_rule": rule,
            "policy_version": {"specialization": "spec-fixed-v1", "pooling": "pool-fastest-v1", "hybrid": "hybrid-reserve-v1"}[policy],
            "planned_service_min": None if item.base_minutes is None else round(item.base_minutes, 3),
            "actual_service_min": round(end - start, 3),
            "resource_multiplier": MULT[rname].get(concept),
            "queue_entry_timestamp": clock(item.queue_entry),
            "queue_name": item.activity if item.activity in REAL else "overhead",
            "segment_index": 0 if not continuation else 1,
            "continuation": int(continuation or pause_before > 0),
            "pause_before_min": round(pause_before, 3),
            "preempted_flag": int(penalty > 0 or item.case.preempt_count > 0 and concept in DIAG),
            "preemption_penalty_min": round(penalty, 3),
            "parallel_branch": int(item.case.mode == "parallel" and concept in DIAG),
            "scenario_id": scenario,
            "shock_flag": scenario if scenario != "base" else "none",
            "sla_clock_origin": clock(item.case.arrival),
            "sla_target_min": SLA_TARGET[item.case.priority],
            "sla_deadline": clock(item.case.deadline),
            "seed": seed,
        })
        # occupancy on day 0, including overtime so util can exceed 1
        if concept != "Pause":
            overlap = max(0.0, min(end, 720.0) - max(start, 0.0))
            resources[rname].occupied_day0 += overlap
            # OT portion: inside an OT-only region roughly after shift end; accounted in util via numerator
            resources[rname].ot_day0 += 0.0

    def enqueue(case, activity, ready, prev_resource, prev_team):
        it = Item(case, activity, ready, prev_resource, prev_team)
        # predrawn base; multiplier applied at start
        if activity in predraw[case.cid]:
            it.base_minutes = predraw[case.cid][activity]
        waiting.append(it)
        for delta in (10, 20, 60, 180, 240):
            add_wake(ready + delta)
        return it

    def complete_item(resource, item, tnow):
        item.state = "done"
        case = item.case
        if item.activity in DIAG or item.activity in ("Intake", "Validation"):
            case.last_resource = resource.name
            case.last_team = TEAM.get(item.activity)
        if item.activity in DIAG:
            resource.last_diag_family = FAMILY[item.activity]
            resource.last_case_id = case.cid
            case.done.add(item.activity)
            if case.pending:
                nxt = case.pending.pop(0)
                enqueue(case, nxt, tnow, resource.name, TEAM.get(item.activity))
                return
            needed = set(TYPE_ACTS[case.ctype])
            if needed <= case.done:
                if case.mode == "parallel" and case.ctype in ("ME", "ES") and not case.coord_queued:
                    case.coord_queued = True
                    enqueue(case, "Coordination", tnow, resource.name, TEAM.get(item.activity))
                    return
                enqueue(case, "Validation", tnow, resource.name, TEAM.get(item.activity))
        elif item.activity == "Coordination":
            enqueue(case, "Validation", tnow, resource.name, case.last_team)
        elif item.activity == "Validation":
            case.finished = tnow
        elif item.activity == "Intake":
            release_diagnostics(case, resource.name, tnow)

    def release_diagnostics(case, intake_resource, tnow):
        acts = TYPE_ACTS[case.ctype]
        if len(acts) == 1:
            case.mode = "single"
            enqueue(case, acts[0], tnow, intake_resource, None)
            return
        ordered = sorted(acts, key=lambda a: SCARCE_RANK[a])
        mode = "sequential"
        if policy in ("pooling", "hybrid"):
            def partners(act):
                found = []
                for n, r in resources.items():
                    m = MULT[n].get(act)
                    if m is None or not r.is_free(tnow):
                        continue
                    if n == "Farah":
                        continue
                    if policy == "hybrid" and n not in home_members(act, scenario) and case.priority != "P1":
                        continue
                    found.append(n)
                return found
            pa, pb = partners(acts[0]), partners(acts[1])
            distinct = any(x != y for x in pa for y in pb)
            queues_empty = all(oldest_wait(waiting, a, tnow) <= 0 for a in acts)
            if distinct and case.priority == "P1":
                mode = "parallel"
            elif distinct and case.priority == "P2" and queues_empty and policy in ("pooling", "hybrid"):
                mode = "parallel"
        case.mode = mode
        if mode == "parallel":
            nonlocal parallel_cases
            parallel_cases += 1
            for a in acts:
                enqueue(case, a, tnow, intake_resource, None)
        else:
            case.pending = ordered[1:]
            enqueue(case, ordered[0], tnow, intake_resource, None)

    def phases_for_new(resource, item):
        phases = []
        if item.saved_phases and resource.name == item.resume_on:
            return list(item.saved_phases)
        # dropping a saved partial onto a new person: keep service remaining only
        handover = 0.0
        cross = 0.0
        setup = 0.0
        prev = item.prev_resource
        if prev is not None and prev != resource.name and item.activity in ("Intake", "Validation") + DIAG:
            handover = HANDOVER_MIN
            if item.prev_team in ("mech", "es") and TEAM.get(item.activity) in ("mech", "es"):
                if item.prev_team != TEAM.get(item.activity):
                    cross = CROSS_TEAM_MIN
        if item.activity in DIAG and resource.last_diag_family not in (None, FAMILY[item.activity]):
            if resource.last_case_id not in (None, item.case.cid):
                setup = SETUP_MIN
        if handover:
            phases.append(("Handover", handover))
        if cross:
            phases.append(("Cross-team handover", cross))
        if setup:
            phases.append(("Setup", setup))
        if item.activity == "Coordination":
            phases.append(("Coordination", COORD_MIN))
            return phases
        if item.remaining is None:
            base = item.base_minutes if item.base_minutes is not None else e_service(item.activity)
            item.base_minutes = base
            mult = MULT[resource.name].get(item.activity) or 1.0
            item.remaining = base * mult
        phases.append((item.activity, item.remaining))
        return phases

    def suspend(resource, item, phases, tnow):
        item.saved_phases = list(phases)
        item.resume_on = resource.name
        item.remaining = sum(d for n, d in phases if n == item.activity) or item.remaining
        item.ready = tnow
        item.queue_entry = tnow
        item.continuation = True
        item.state = "waiting"
        item.prev_resource = resource.name
        waiting.append(item)
        resource.busy = False
        resource.idle_since = tnow
        add_wake(tnow + 1.0)

    def schedule_work(resource, item, phases, tnow, rule, eligible, pause_before=0.0):
        phases = [(n, d) for n, d in phases if d > 1e-6]
        if not phases:
            resource.busy = False
            complete_item(resource, item, tnow)
            resource.idle_since = tnow
            return
        # Intake blackout: do not hold the person across 09:30-10:00
        if item.activity == "Intake" and outage_on(scenario):
            if 90.0 <= tnow < 120.0:
                suspend(resource, item, phases, tnow)
                return
        nxt = resource.next_open(tnow)
        if nxt is None:
            suspend(resource, item, phases, tnow)
            return
        gap = nxt - tnow
        outage_gap = item.activity == "Intake" and outage_on(scenario) and tnow < 90.0 <= nxt
        if gap > MAX_SHORT_GAP or outage_gap:
            suspend(resource, item, phases, tnow)
            return
        if gap > 1e-6:
            running.append(Running(resource, item, "PAUSE", tnow, nxt, phases))
            resource.busy = True
            return
        w_end = resource.window_end(tnow)
        if w_end is None:
            suspend(resource, item, phases, tnow)
            return
        # stop Intake at the outage boundary
        if item.activity == "Intake" and outage_on(scenario) and tnow < 90.0 < w_end:
            w_end = 90.0
        name, dur = phases[0]
        take = min(dur, w_end - tnow)
        if take <= 1e-6:
            suspend(resource, item, phases, tnow)
            return
        left = dur - take
        later = ([(name, left)] if left > 1e-6 else []) + phases[1:]
        running.append(Running(resource, item, name, tnow, tnow + take, later))
        resource.busy = True
        item.state = "running"
        item._rule = rule
        item._eligible = eligible
        item._pause_before = pause_before

    def start_item(resource, item, tnow, rule, eligible):
        if resource.in_window(tnow) and tnow >= resource.contracted_day0:
            pass
        # new overtime work only for P1; continuations of saved phases are allowed
        shift_end_regions = []
        if item.saved_phases is None and not _inside_contract(resource, tnow):
            if item.case.priority != "P1":
                return False
        phases = phases_for_new(resource, item)
        item.saved_phases = None
        waiting.remove(item) if item in waiting else None
        schedule_work(resource, item, phases, tnow, rule, eligible)
        return True

    def _inside_contract(resource, tnow) -> bool:
        return any(a <= tnow < b - 1e-9 for a, b in resource.contracted)

    def on_running_end(job: Running, tnow: float):
        resource, item = job.resource, job.item
        if job.phase != "PAUSE" and tnow > job.start + 1e-8:
            penalty = 0.0
            log_row(
                item, resource.name, job.phase, job.start, tnow,
                item.continuation, getattr(item, "_pause_before", 0.0),
                getattr(item, "_eligible", ""), getattr(item, "_rule", ""), penalty,
            )
            item.continuation = False
            item._pause_before = 0.0
            if job.phase == item.activity and item.remaining is not None:
                item.remaining = max(0.0, item.remaining - (tnow - job.start))
        schedule_work(
            resource, item, job.later, tnow,
            getattr(item, "_rule", "continue"),
            getattr(item, "_eligible", ""),
            pause_before=(tnow - job.start) if job.phase == "PAUSE" else 0.0,
        )

    def service_left(job: Running, tnow: float) -> float:
        if job.phase != job.item.activity:
            rem = 0.0
        else:
            rem = max(0.0, job.end - tnow)
        rem += sum(d for n, d in job.later if n == job.item.activity)
        return rem

    def try_preempt(tnow: float) -> bool:
        nonlocal preempt_events
        p1s = [it for it in waiting if it.priority == "P1" and it.activity in DIAG and it.ready <= tnow + 1e-9 and it.state == "waiting"]
        if not p1s:
            return False
        did = False
        for p1 in list(p1s):
            if p1 not in waiting:
                continue
            candidates = []
            for job in running:
                if job.phase != job.item.activity or job.item.activity not in DIAG:
                    continue
                vic = job.item
                if vic.case.priority != "P3":
                    continue
                if policy == "hybrid" and False:
                    pass
                if vic.case.preempt_count >= 1:
                    continue
                if not allowed(policy, job.resource.name, p1, tnow, resources, waiting, scenario):
                    # home/overflow permission required so specialization does not steal across queues
                    if not (policy == "hybrid" and fixed_ok("specialization", job.resource.name, p1.activity, tnow, resources, scenario)):
                        if not fixed_ok(policy if policy != "hybrid" else "specialization", job.resource.name, p1.activity, tnow, resources, scenario):
                            if not (policy == "pooling" and MULT[job.resource.name].get(p1.activity) is not None):
                                continue
                rem = service_left(job, tnow)
                if rem < PREEMPT_MIN_REMAINING:
                    continue
                candidates.append((rem, job))
            if not candidates:
                continue
            _, job = max(candidates, key=lambda x: x[0])
            rem = service_left(job, tnow)
            # cut the current segment
            if tnow > job.start + 1e-6 and job.phase == job.item.activity:
                log_row(job.item, job.resource.name, job.phase, job.start, tnow, job.item.continuation, 0.0, "", "preempted", PREEMPT_PENALTY_FRACTION * rem)
            vic = job.item
            vic.remaining = rem * (1.0 + PREEMPT_PENALTY_FRACTION)
            vic.case.preempt_count += 1
            vic.saved_phases = None
            vic.resume_on = None
            vic.ready = tnow
            vic.queue_entry = tnow
            vic.continuation = False
            vic.state = "waiting"
            vic.prev_resource = job.resource.name
            running.remove(job)
            job.resource.busy = False
            job.resource.idle_since = tnow
            if vic not in waiting:
                waiting.append(vic)
            preempt_events += 1
            # P1 takes the freed resource immediately
            if start_assign(job.resource, p1, tnow, "preempt-p1"):
                did = True
        return did

    def start_assign(resource, item, tnow, rule) -> bool:
        if item not in waiting:
            return False
        if not resource.is_free(tnow):
            return False
        if not _inside_contract(resource, tnow) and item.case.priority != "P1" and not item.saved_phases:
            return False
        eligible_names = [n for n in ALL_NAMES if allowed(policy, n, item, tnow, resources, waiting, scenario) and resources[n].is_free(tnow)]
        phases_preview = True
        item.state = "waiting"
        waiting_still = item in waiting
        if not waiting_still:
            return False
        ok = start_item(resource, item, tnow, rule, "|".join(eligible_names))
        return ok and resource.busy

    def best_pair(tnow):
        free = [r for r in resources.values() if r.is_free(tnow)]
        if not free:
            return None
        # cache best multiplier per item among free allowed people
        best_mult = {}
        live = [it for it in waiting if it.state == "waiting" and it.ready <= tnow + 1e-9]
        for it in live:
            ms = []
            for r in free:
                if allowed(policy, r.name, it, tnow, resources, waiting, scenario):
                    if it.activity in REAL:
                        m = MULT[r.name].get(it.activity)
                        if m is not None:
                            ms.append(m)
                    else:
                        ms.append(1.0)
            if ms:
                best_mult[it.seq] = min(ms)
        pairs = []
        for r in free:
            for it in live:
                if not allowed(policy, r.name, it, tnow, resources, waiting, scenario):
                    continue
                if policy == "hybrid" and reserved_hold(r, it, tnow, resources, waiting, cases, scenario):
                    continue
                if r.name == "Farah" and it.activity != "Intake" and any(w.activity == "Intake" and w.state == "waiting" and w.ready <= tnow for w in live):
                    continue
                if not _inside_contract(r, tnow) and it.case.priority != "P1" and not it.saved_phases:
                    continue
                defer = 0
                if it.activity in ("Intake", "Validation") and home_diag_waiting(r.name, live, tnow, scenario):
                    if any(o.is_free(tnow) and o.name != r.name for o in resources.values()):
                        defer = 1
                if it.activity == "Coordination" and home_diag_waiting(r.name, live, tnow, scenario):
                    if any(o.is_free(tnow) and o.name != r.name for o in resources.values()):
                        defer = 1
                pri = priority_rank(policy, it, tnow, live)
                if it.activity in REAL:
                    mult = MULT[r.name].get(it.activity) or 9.0
                else:
                    mult = 1.0
                ebase = expected_base(it)
                if it.activity in DIAG and it.remaining is not None and mult > 0:
                    ebase = it.remaining / mult
                bm = best_mult.get(it.seq, mult)
                near = 0 if (mult - bm) * ebase <= SPEED_LOSS_BAND_MIN else 1
                util_key = r.util if it.case.priority != "P1" else 0.0
                keep = 0 if it.prev_resource == r.name else 1
                setup_flag = 0
                if it.activity in DIAG and r.last_diag_family not in (None, FAMILY[it.activity]) and r.last_case_id not in (None, it.case.cid):
                    setup_flag = 1
                intake_pref = 0 if it.activity == "Intake" and r.name == "Farah" else 1
                score = (defer, pri, it.queue_entry, near, util_key, intake_pref, mult, keep, setup_flag, r.name)
                pairs.append((score, r, it))
        if not pairs:
            return None
        pairs.sort(key=lambda x: x[0])
        return pairs[0]

    def dispatch(tnow: float) -> None:
        try_preempt(tnow)
        guard = 0
        while guard < 50:
            guard += 1
            picked = best_pair(tnow)
            if picked is None:
                return
            _, r, it = picked
            rule = "home"
            if policy == "hybrid" and it.activity in DIAG and not fixed_ok("specialization", r.name, it.activity, tnow, resources, scenario):
                rule = f"overflow-{it.case.priority.lower()}"
            if policy == "pooling":
                rule = "pool"
            if not start_assign(r, it, tnow, rule):
                return
            try_preempt(tnow)

    while t < HORIZON and steps < 200000:
        steps += 1
        while wakes and wakes[0] <= t + 1e-9:
            heapq.heappop(wakes)
        # arrivals
        while arr_i < len(arrivals) and arrivals[arr_i].arrival <= t + 1e-9:
            c = arrivals[arr_i]
            arr_i += 1
            enqueue(c, "Intake", c.arrival, None, None)
        # completions
        done_jobs = [j for j in running if j.end <= t + 1e-9]
        for job in done_jobs:
            running.remove(job)
            on_running_end(job, t)
        dispatch(t)
        # next time
        cands = []
        if arr_i < len(arrivals):
            cands.append(arrivals[arr_i].arrival)
        for job in running:
            cands.append(job.end)
        if wakes:
            cands.append(wakes[0])
        for r in resources.values():
            if not r.busy:
                nxt = r.next_open(t + 1e-6)
                if nxt is not None and nxt > t:
                    cands.append(nxt)
        future = [c for c in cands if c > t + 1e-9]
        if not future:
            break
        t = min(future)
        if t > HORIZON:
            break

    # metrics
    elapsed = []
    sla = {k: [] for k in SLA_TARGET}
    incomplete = 0
    for c in cases:
        if c.finished is None:
            incomplete += 1
            el = HORIZON - c.arrival
            hit = False
        else:
            el = c.finished - c.arrival
            hit = el <= SLA_TARGET[c.priority] + 1e-6
        elapsed.append(el)
        sla[c.priority].append(hit)
    utils = {n: r.util for n, r in resources.items() if r.contracted_day0 > 0}
    u = np.array(list(utils.values())) if utils else np.array([0.0])
    jain = float((u.sum() ** 2) / (len(u) * np.sum(u ** 2))) if np.sum(u ** 2) > 0 else 1.0
    ev = pd.DataFrame(events)
    if len(ev):
        overhead_min = ev.loc[ev["concept:name"].isin(OVERHEAD), "actual_service_min"].sum()
    else:
        overhead_min = 0.0
    n = max(1, len(cases))
    arr = np.array(elapsed) if elapsed else np.array([0.0])
    def rate(key):
        return float(np.mean(sla[key])) if sla[key] else float("nan")
    metrics = {
        "policy": policy,
        "scenario": scenario,
        "seed": seed,
        "n_cases": len(cases),
        "incomplete": incomplete,
        "mean_delay": float(arr.mean()),
        "p90_delay": float(np.quantile(arr, 0.90)),
        "p95_delay": float(np.quantile(arr, 0.95)),
        "P1_SLA": rate("P1"),
        "P2_SLA": rate("P2"),
        "P3_SLA": rate("P3"),
        "max_resource_utilization": float(u.max()) if len(u) else 0.0,
        "utilization_fairness": jain,
        "n_resources_over_90": int(np.sum(u > 0.90)),
        "handover_or_setup_burden": float(overhead_min) / n,
        "preemptions": preempt_events,
        "parallel_cases": parallel_cases,
        "steps": steps,
    }
    return metrics, ev


def generate(scenario: str, seed: int):
    rng = np.random.default_rng(1000 + seed + 17 * (abs(hash(scenario)) % 1000))
    p_scale = p_scale_for(scenario)
    specs = []
    predraw = {}
    k = 0
    pri_labels = np.array(["P1", "P2", "P3"])
    type_labels = np.array(["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"])
    for b in scenario_demand(scenario):
        n = int(rng.poisson(b["expected"]))
        for _ in range(n):
            k += 1
            cid = f"c{k:03d}"
            arrival = float(rng.uniform(b["start"], b["end"]))
            pr = str(rng.choice(pri_labels, p=b["p"]))
            ct = str(rng.choice(type_labels, p=b["t"]))
            specs.append({"cid": cid, "priority": pr, "ctype": ct, "arrival": arrival})
            predraw[cid] = {}
            for act in ["Intake", "Validation", *TYPE_ACTS[ct]]:
                predraw[cid][act] = sample_base(act, rng, p_scale if act in DIAG else 1.0)
    specs.sort(key=lambda s: s["arrival"])
    return specs, predraw


def stage_queue_table(ev: pd.DataFrame) -> pd.DataFrame:
    """Queue time is first start minus queue entry. It excludes continuation segments.
    Service is the sum of real-activity durations. Overhead is separate.
    PM4Py case-level waiting time is not this quantity when branches overlap.
    """
    d = ev.copy()
    d["dur"] = (d["time:timestamp"] - d["start_timestamp"]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0
    d["queue_wait_min"] = (d["start_timestamp"] - d["queue_entry_timestamp"]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0
    first = d[(d["continuation"] == 0) & (d["concept:name"].isin(REAL))]
    q = first.groupby("concept:name")["queue_wait_min"].mean()
    s = d[d["concept:name"].isin(REAL)].groupby("concept:name")["dur"].mean()
    o = d[d["concept:name"].isin(OVERHEAD)].groupby("concept:name")["dur"].sum()
    out = pd.concat([q.rename("mean_queue_wait_min"), s.rename("mean_segment_service_min")], axis=1)
    print("\nSTAGE QUEUE AND SERVICE (authoritative; not PM4Py case waiting)")
    print(out.round(2).to_string())
    print("OVERHEAD MINUTES (sum over the filtered log)")
    print(o.round(1).to_string() if len(o) else "none")
    return out


def invoke(fn, frame, named: dict):
    sig = inspect.signature(fn)
    accepted = {k: v for k, v in named.items() if k in sig.parameters}
    try:
        return fn(frame, **accepted)
    except TypeError:
        return fn(frame)
```

# 9. Limits and validation plan

Expected arrivals and service ranges cannot identify the SLA rates in Section 4. The same ranges support a feasible day (ordinary-low corner, about 1811 base-minutes) and an infeasible day (central mixture, about 2721 base-minutes before multipliers and overhead). Which one the desk lives in is an empirical question. The ranges also cannot settle the dependence between priority and case type, clustering inside a block, whether the preemption penalty is 10% of remaining time or a full restart, how often a “cross-skilled retain” actually happens, or whether breaks are movable. The evening Mechanical hole and the 16:00 Software blackout are consequences of the roster table; their size still depends on the service draw. P1 attainment at five cases a day will swing wildly. A single simulated seed, or a single live day, cannot rank the policies.

**Pilot.** For two weeks, run protected hybrid in shadow mode: the dispatcher records the eligible set and the rule C would have fired, and the crew follows today’s practice. That produces the event log in Section 7 without putting P1 at the mercy of an untried board. Then randomize by day, not by case, between C and the current rule, for long enough to collect on the order of 150–200 P1 cases (several weeks at the base rate; shorter if a surge period is included after the procedure is stable). Primary endpoint is elapsed time by priority, which has more power than the 90% indicator. The pass rate is secondary. Each day starts with a declared shock flag so U1-like mornings are labeled rather than discovered after the fact.

**P1 protection during the test.** If any P1 wait crosses 20 minutes, the floor may break randomization and move that case to the fastest qualified free person, including overtime. Those cases stay in the log with `assignment_rule = manual-override` and are not dropped. If a test day’s P1 attainment falls below 70%, the next day reverts to mode 1 or to the current rule for the rest of the week. That abort rule is the barrier against an unsafe full rollout. Do not pilot mode 1’s “preempt P2” behavior on day one.

**What would change the recommendation.** Adopt B as the default if shadow and randomized days show utilization staying under 75% and C’s reservation slots sitting idle while P2 elapsed time is worse than B’s. Keep A for a skill if override and overflow logs show systematic mis-use of Farah or Ada-Electrical, or if priority labels prove inflated. Revisit the roster, not the dispatch score, if the log confirms Software queue time concentrated in 16:00–20:00 and Mechanical queue time concentrated after 18:00: those are the holes no eligible-set rule can fill. Re-estimate the mixture means from `planned_service_min` against `actual_service_min` before treating the central-mixture infeasibility result as a fact about this desk rather than a fact about the midpoint assumption.
