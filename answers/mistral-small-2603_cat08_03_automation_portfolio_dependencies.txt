## 2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map

### **Upstream Data Quality Dependencies**
- **A2 (Classification)** depends on **A1 (Auto-registration)** for clean data.
- **A3 (KYC pre-classifier)** depends on **A1/A2** for correct product/exposure classification.
- **A5 (Missing-document checklist)** depends on **A2** to route cases correctly.
- **A6 (Financial normalization)** depends on **A5** to ensure complete documents.
- **A7 (Collateral triage)** depends on **A2** to identify Equipment finance cases.
- **A8 (Committee pack)** depends on **A3, A6, A7** for clean artifacts.
- **A9 (Contract generation)** depends on **A8** for reconciled artifacts.
- **A10 (E-signature)** depends on **A9** for clean contracts.
- **A11 (Loan activation)** depends on **A10** for signed contracts.

### **Legal/Compliance Controls**
- **Four-eyes principle** (A6  A8 must be separate roles for High-exposure cases).
- **KYC clearance before signing** (A3 must precede A10).
- **Contract terms must match Committee decision** (A9 must enforce this).
- **Audit trail** required for all automated suggestions and overrides (A1–A12).

### **Human Escalation Capacity**
- **Compliance specialists** (A3, A4) are a bottleneck; automation must not overload them.
- **Credit analysts** (A5, A6) must not be overwhelmed by increased clean inflow.
- **Relationship managers** (A5, A10) may resist changes increasing customer friction.

### **External Valuation Dependency**
- **A7 (Collateral triage)** cannot eliminate the 58h wait for external valuers but can parallelize requests.

### **Core-Banking Integration Constraints**
- **A1, A2, A9, A10, A11** require deep integration (limited to 2 in first 6 months).
- **A3, A4, A5, A6, A7, A8, A12** can be lightweight integrations (APIs, portals, workbenches).

## 7. Final Recommendation

**Recommended Portfolio**: **Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio (A3, A5, A6, A8)**

### **Why This Portfolio?**
1. **Process-Level Impact**: Addresses the most critical synchronization delays (KYC, document loops, financial normalization, Committee packaging) that dominate cycle time.
2. **Compliance Alignment**: A3 improves KYC clearance (a control risk), and A8 reduces Committee packaging errors (indirectly improving control adherence).
3. **Resource Balance**: Reduces credit analyst workload (A6) while managing inflow (A5), avoiding overload.
4. **Scalability**: Lightweight integrations for A5/A6/A8 allow rapid deployment; A3 requires deeper integration but is justified by its impact.
5. **Political Feasibility**: Avoids high-risk compliance-only modules (A4, A12) that offer lower direct benefits.

### **Why Not Automate High-Frequency Activities First?**
- **Register application (A1)** is already 72% digital; further automation offers diminishing returns and risks exposing downstream bottlenecks (KYC, Credit analysis).
- **Contract preparation (A9)** and **E-signature (A10)** are politically attractive but do not address the root causes of delay (synchronization, rework).
- **Compliance-first modules (A4, A12)** are critical but offer lower process-level benefits compared to bottleneck mitigation.

### **Trade-Off Argument**
The highest-frequency activities (e.g., Register, Contract prep) are not the highest-impact targets. Instead, the portfolio focuses on **synchronization points** (KYC, Committee packaging) and **rework loops** (missing documents, financial normalization) that create the longest waits. This aligns with process-mining evidence showing that **median waiting times** (e.g., 58h for external valuation, 28h for Credit analysis) dominate cycle time more than frequency alone.

By prioritizing **A3 (KYC pre-classifier)** and **A6 (Financial normalization)**, the portfolio directly reduces the two largest waiting times in the process. **A5 (Missing-document checklist)** and **A8 (Committee pack)** further address rework and synchronization, creating a compounding effect on cycle time. This approach balances labor savings, compliance, and process efficiency without overloading constrained resources.
