**Why bidirectional DFG  concurrency:** A DFG edge records that some completion of A was immediately followed by some completion of B in at least one trace. Edges AB and BA can arise from (i) exclusive sequential variants, (ii) true interleaving/concurrency, or (iii) overlapping executions serialized by completion time and ingestion index (S7, D7). Without start events or explicit parallel instrumentation, models must treat concurrency as a hypothesis, not a fact.

# 2. Shared Assumptions

1. **Event semantics:** Each activity label is a **completion** event. Models constrain completion order, not start/enablement overlap.
2. **Fixed spine:** All models enforce `Register Order`  …  `Approve Configuration`  `Release Production`  `Archive Case` (D1, D2).
3. **Both principal checks required:** `Credit Check` and `Engineering Review` each occur exactly once before approval (D3; supported by all 30 traces).
4. **Expedite Review:** Optional, at most once, enabled only after both principal checks, always before approval; may sit before or after SC (D6, S6). Represented as `XOR(Expedite Review, )`.
5. **Supplier Confirmation optionality vs missingness:** The log shows SC absent in 7/30 cases. S3–S5 conflict. All models use control-flow optionality `XOR(SC, )` as an **abstraction that collapses** (a) legitimate stocked-part skip and (b) possible missing SC events. This is disclosed technical debt, not a claim that skips are all legitimate.
6. **Absent attribute:** No external/stocked flag is available; models cannot route on it.
7. **No duplicate concurrent instances:** Each labeled activity is executed at most once per case (no loops in the candidates below).
8. **Silent transitions:** `` only encodes optionality, never unobserved business work.
9. **Language view:** Fitness is judged on completion traces; “concurrency” means partial-order / parallel operator permission to interleave completions.

# 3. Conservative Candidate

**Inductive bias.** Prefer the smallest language that still replays every observed variant exactly, using only high-confidence spine rules (D1–D3, D6 as seen). Do not invent interleavings, SC-before-CC, or Exp placements that never appear. Bias is toward precision and compliance auditability at the cost of brittleness and duplicate labels.

**Formal expression.**
\[
\begin{align*}
&\mathrm{RO} \cdot
\big(
  (\mathrm{CC}\cdot\mathrm{ER}\cdot\mathrm{SC})
  + (\mathrm{ER}\cdot\mathrm{CC}\cdot\mathrm{SC})
  + (\mathrm{CC}\cdot\mathrm{ER})
  + (\mathrm{ER}\cdot\mathrm{CC})
  + (\mathrm{CC}\cdot\mathrm{SC}\cdot\mathrm{ER})
  + (\mathrm{CC}\cdot\mathrm{ER}\cdot\mathrm{SC}\cdot\mathrm{Exp})
  + (\mathrm{ER}\cdot\mathrm{CC}\cdot\mathrm{Exp}\cdot\mathrm{SC})
\big)
\cdot \mathrm{AC} \cdot \mathrm{RP} \cdot \mathrm{Arch}
\end{align*}
\]
(Exact XOR enumeration of the seven observed middle-blocks.)

**PM4Py code.**

```python
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,

# --- model_conservative: ProcessTree XOR enumeration of observed middles ---
model_conservative = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register Order',"
    " X("
    "  ->('Credit Check','Engineering Review','Supplier Confirmation'),"
    "  ->('Engineering Review','Credit Check','Supplier Confirmation'),"
    "  ->('Credit Check','Engineering Review'),"
    "  ->('Engineering Review','Credit Check'),"
    "  ->('Credit Check','Supplier Confirmation','Engineering Review'),"
    "  ->('Credit Check','Engineering Review','Supplier Confirmation','Expedite Review'),"
    "  ->('Engineering Review','Credit Check','Expedite Review','Supplier Confirmation')"
    " ),"
    " 'Approve Configuration','Release Production','Archive Case')"
)
```

**Behavior deliberately excluded.**
- Any SC before Credit Check (e.g., ROERSCCC…).
- Exp before both checks, after AC, or more than once.
- SC after ER without CC first when ERSCCC.
- Unobserved Exp/SC schedules such as ROCCERExpSC… (not in the seven blocks).
- True parallel enablement beyond the listed sequences.
- Skipping CC or ER; reordering AC/RP/Arch.

**Known underfitting risks.**
- Duplicate labels across XOR branches: poor scalability, harder subprocess reuse, some algorithms treat copies as distinct.
- Brittle to the next legitimate interleaving (e.g., ERCCSCExp).
- Treats rare paths as closed sets; cannot absorb minor logging permutations (S7).
- Collapses missing SC vs stocked skip without a data attribute (S5).
- Cannot represent Finance-only sequential policy as a soft preference—only as hard exclusion of ER-first (which would drop fitness).

# 4. Balanced Candidate

**Inductive bias.** Encode the strongest regularities that fit all 30 cases without enumerating them: both checks once; SC never precedes CC in the evidence (so require CC  SC when SC occurs); ER may finish before or after SC; Exp optional after both checks and unordered w.r.t. SC; optionality for SC as explicit XOR. Use a partial order (POWL) so unobserved but structurally similar interleavings are admitted once. Manage uncertainty by not assuming full SCchecks from RO and not forcing CCER (rejects S1 as hard law, accepts S2 as completion-order explanation).

**Decision on sequence vs concurrency vs partial order.**
- **CC vs ER:** Bidirectional DFG + S2 + D7  **unordered (concurrent completions)** in POWL (no edge). Not a forced sequence either way (rejects S1 as hard constraint). Not an XOR of two long sequences (that is the conservative model).
- **SC:** Evidence always has CC before SC; V5 shows SC before ER  **edge CC  SC**, **no edge ER  SC** (partial order). Optional via XOR. This is stricter than D5 (“after either”) but better supported by data; D5’s ER-only trigger remains a permissive hypothesis.
- **Exp:** D6/S6  edges from both CC and ER to Exp; Exp  AC; **no order vs SC** (allows V6 and V7 generalizations).
- **Join:** CC, ER, opt_SC, opt_Exp all precede AC.

**Formal expression (POWL).**
\[
\begin{align*}
&\mathrm{RO} \prec \mathrm{CC},\ \mathrm{RO} \prec \mathrm{ER},\\
&\mathrm{CC} \prec \mathrm{optSC},\quad
\mathrm{CC} \prec \mathrm{optExp},\ \mathrm{ER} \prec \mathrm{optExp},\\
&\{\mathrm{CC},\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{optSC},\mathrm{optExp}\} \prec \mathrm{AC} \prec \mathrm{RP} \prec \mathrm{Arch},\\
&\mathrm{optSC}=\mathrm{SC}+\tau,\quad \mathrm{optExp}=\mathrm{Exp}+\tau,\\
&\text{no order between CC and ER;\ no order between ER and optSC;\ no order between optSC and optExp.}
\end{align*}
\]

**PM4Py code.**

```python
# --- model_balanced: POWL partial order with SC after CC, Exp after both ---
_RO = Transition(label="Register Order")
_CC = Transition(label="Credit Check")
_ER = Transition(label="Engineering Review")
_SC = Transition(label="Supplier Confirmation")
_EX = Transition(label="Expedite Review")
_AC = Transition(label="Approve Configuration")
_RP = Transition(label="Release Production")
_AR = Transition(label="Archive Case")
_t_sc = SilentTransition()
_t_ex = SilentTransition()

opt_sc_b = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[_SC, _t_sc])
opt_ex_b = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[_EX, _t_ex])

model_balanced = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[_RO, _CC, _ER, opt_sc_b, opt_ex_b, _AC, _RP, _AR]
)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_RO, _CC)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_RO, _ER)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_CC, opt_sc_b)   # SC only after Credit Check
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_CC, opt_ex_b)   # Exp after both checks
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_ER, opt_ex_b)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_CC, _AC)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_ER, _AC)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(opt_sc_b, _AC)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(opt_ex_b, _AC)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_AC, _RP)
model_balanced.order.add_edge(_RP, _AR)
```

**Behavior deliberately excluded.**
- SC before CC (including ROSC… and ROERSCCC…).
- Exp before either check; Exp after AC; second Exp.
- Skipping CC or ER; AC before checks; RP before AC.
- SC after AC.

**Known underfitting risks.**
- Still underfits a true “SC after ER-only” policy if D5/S3 operate that way (unobserved ERSCCC).
- May over-attach to “CC before SC” if that pattern is sampling accident (only 23 SC cases).
- Optional SC still confounds missing events (S5).
- If Finance policy (S1) is enforced operationally, balanced allows ER-first completions that compliance might flag even when enabled concurrently.

# 5. Permissive Candidate

**Inductive bias.** Admit all completion orders consistent with high/medium domain rules (D1–D4, D6) and the weaker D5 reading: SC may begin after RO once the case exists, interleaving freely with the two checks, still optional, still before AC. Keep Exp after both checks. Purpose is exploration, sensitivity to missing SC, and “reasonable generalization,” not deployment as a compliance fence.

**Boundary: reasonable vs forbidden.**
- **Reasonable (admitted):** any interleaving of CC, ER, and optional SC after RO; optional Exp after both checks, unordered w.r.t. SC; SC skipped; Exp skipped; both check orders.
- **Still forbidden:** start  RO; end  Arch; RP before AC; AC before CC or ER; Exp before both checks complete; Exp after AC; multiple Exp/CC/ER/SC; SC after AC; activities outside the label set.

**Formal expression (POWL).**
\[
\begin{align*}
&\mathrm{RO} \prec \mathrm{CC},\ \mathrm{RO} \prec \mathrm{ER},\ \mathrm{RO} \prec \mathrm{optSC},\\
&\mathrm{CC} \prec \mathrm{optExp},\ \mathrm{ER} \prec \mathrm{optExp},\\
&\{\mathrm{CC},\mathrm{ER},\mathrm{optSC},\mathrm{optExp}\} \prec \mathrm{AC} \prec \mathrm{RP} \prec \mathrm{Arch},\\
&\mathrm{optSC}=\mathrm{SC}+\tau,\ \mathrm{optExp}=\mathrm{Exp}+\tau.
\end{align*}
\]

**PM4Py code.**

```python
    nodes=[p_RO, p_CC, p_ER, opt_sc_p, opt_ex_p, p_AC, p_RP, p_AR]

model_permissive.order.add_edge(p_CC, p_AC)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(p_ER, p_AC)

model_permissive.order.add_edge(opt_ex_p, p_AC)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(p_AC, p_RP)
model_permissive.order.add_edge(p_RP, p_AR)
```

**Behavior deliberately excluded.**
- Violations of D1/D2/D3/D6 as listed in the boundary above.
- Loops, extra activity copies, Exp without both checks.

**Known underfitting risks.**
- Low precision: many SC-first and ERSCCC traces never seen; may be operationally invalid.
- Weak for compliance (S6 is kept, but SC discipline is thin).
- Increases confusion between missing SC and intentional skip by enlarging SC schedules.
- Overfitting is unlikely; **underfitting of hidden constraints** is the main risk (false confidence in exploration).

```python
p_RO = Transition(label="Register Order")

for name, model in candidates.items():

    nets[name] = (net, im, fm)
    print(f"\n=== {name} soundness ===")
    try:
        sound = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
        print("soundness:", sound)

        print("soundness check error:", e)

    try:
        fit = pm4py.fitness_alignments(log, net, im, fm)
        print("fitness:", fit)
    except Exception as e:
        print("fitness error:", e)

    try:
        prec = pm4py.precision_alignments(log, net, im, fm)
        print("precision:", prec)
    except Exception as e:
        print("precision error:", e)

    try:
        gen = pm4py.generalization_tbr(log, net, im, fm)
        print("generalization:", gen)
    except Exception as e:
        print("generalization error:", e)

    try:
        simp = pm4py.simplicity_petri_net(net, im, fm)
        print("simplicity:", simp)
    except Exception as e:
        print("simplicity error:", e)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

        print(f"playout {name}: got {len(played)} traces (sample/bound only)")

    "Register Order,Engineering Review,Credit Check,Supplier Confirmation,Expedite Review,Approve Configuration,Release Production,Archive Case",

negative = [
```

# 9. Recommendation and Data-Collection Plan

**Immediate use:** `model_balanced`.  
It replays all 30 cases, avoids duplicate-label XOR blow-up, encodes D1–D4/D6 cleanly, respects the empirical invariant **CC  SC**, and leaves CC/ER unordered in line with S2/D7 without embracing SC-first behavior unsupported by the log.

**Sensitivity analysis:** `model_permissive` (and, if Finance locks policy, a sequential specialization of the conservative XOR that drops ER-first branches). Use permissive to quantify precision loss and to stress-test automation rules before enforcing them. Keep `model_conservative` as a regression oracle for “have we left the observed envelope?”

**Data-collection plan (maximally informative).**
1. **Lifecycle timestamps:** export `start` and `complete` for CC, ER, SC, Exp. Distinguishes true overlap (concurrency) from sequential finish-order serialization (D7/S7).
2. **Case attributes:** `component_source  {external, stocked, mixed}`, `expedite_flag`, `credit_risk_tier`. Separates S3 vs S4 and legitimate SC skip from S5 missingness.
3. **Integration repair:** end-to-end SC acknowledgment ID joined to the case; measure actual SC missing rate vs 5–10% claim (S5).
4. **Explicit enablement logging:** when SC is offered in the UI/API relative to CC/ER completion—tests D5 (“after either”) vs “after CC only” vs “after both.”
5. **Informative future traces to capture deliberately (shadow mode, not production force):**
   - ER completes  SC completes  CC completes (tests ER-only SC enablement).
   - CCER with overlapping start/end (tests S1 vs S2).
   - Exp with no SC on documented stocked cases.
   - Exp between the two checks (should remain forbidden under S6—if it appears, compliance rule is wrong or logging is wrong).
   - Near-simultaneous CC/ER completions with ingestion swap (quantifies S7 artifacts).
6. **Decision rule after collection:**  
   - If start times show systematic non-overlap and CC always starts first  move balanced toward sequential CCER.  
   - If SC starts after ER-only with external parts  relax CCSC (shift toward permissive/D5).  
   - If stocked flag explains all SC absences  replace XOR() with attribute-guarded choice; if missingness remains  keep  but add quality metric.  
   - If Exp ever starts before both completes  escalate to compliance (S6) before changing the model.

**Plausible alternative that would change the recommendation.**  
If new lifecycle data show that Credit Check **always starts and finishes before Engineering Review is enabled** (hard S1 workflow configuration), and ER-first log rows are only ingestion artifacts, then the recommendation would switch to a **sequential** model `RO  CC  ER  (SC  Exp?)  AC  RP  Arch` (ProcessTree), demoting balanced concurrency to a rejected hypothesis. Equally, if external-component cases show SC systematically starting after ER without CC, the CCSC edge should be dropped and a D5-style “after either check” choice model (or guarded OR-join) should replace balanced—moving the production recommendation toward a structured variant of permissive, not the current balanced POWL.
