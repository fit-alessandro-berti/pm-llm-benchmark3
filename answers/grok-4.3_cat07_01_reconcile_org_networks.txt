# 1. Network-by-network interpretation
- **handover-of-work network**: High out-degree and betweenness indicate Mira_Coordinator frequently passes work onward (especially exceptions) and sits on many shortest paths. It does not imply she performs substantial work on those cases or remains involved after the handover.
- **working-together network**: Low weighted degree shows she rarely co-occurs with the same adjusters or specialists across full case lifetimes. This is consistent with short routing or assignment events rather than sustained collaboration.
- **subcontracting network**: High betweenness reflects repeated patterns in which she assigns a specialist and later re-enters the case. It does not prove she controls outcomes or that the subcontracting is discretionary.
- **performance/time statistics**: High variance in cycle-time contribution and elevated exception_flag share suggest involvement concentrates on difficult cases. Median service times are moderate, indicating her events are typically brief.
- **role-discovery or activity-profile similarity**: Her activity profile (Request Missing Information, Reopen Claim, Escalate Complaint, Assign Specialist) clusters separately from both team leads and adjusters, pointing to an exception-oriented rather than production-oriented role.

# 3. Risk assessment
Bottleneck risk is present for exception_flag=True cases but limited to that subpopulation. Coordination risk arises if her routing knowledge is tacit and undocumented. Key-person risk is moderate: absence already lengthens tail cycle times, yet she handles under one-quarter of total volume. Compliance/control risk exists because she both detects exceptions and selects the next actor, creating potential for uncontrolled discretion; however, no evidence of improper selection is present. Workload fairness risk is low because her events are short and concentrated on a minority of cases. Data-quality risk is modest: many of her events may be recorded as single “assign” or “reopen” activities, understating true coordination effort.
