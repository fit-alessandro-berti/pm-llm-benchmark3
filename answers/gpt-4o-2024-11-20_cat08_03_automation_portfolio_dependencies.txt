# 2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map

### **Upstream Data Quality Dependencies**
- Modules like **A2 (Product/Exposure Classification)** and most downstream automations (e.g., **A9 Contract Generation**) require reliable and accurate registration data (**A1 Auto-registration**).
- **A6 Financial Normalization** depends on correctly collected and complete financial statements, requiring either cleaner upstream practices (**A5 Missing Document Checklist**) or automation-supported document extraction.

### **Legal/Compliance Controls**
- **KYC-related modules (A3, A4)** must ensure strict compliance with anti-money laundering (AML) laws and maintain auditable trails.
- **A9 Contract Generation** is dependent on accurate KYC clearance (**A3**) and validated committee decisions to avoid legal risks.

### **Human Escalation Capacity**
- Modules such as **A3 (KYC Pre-Classifier)** and **A4 (Entity Mismatch Assistant)** rely on human specialists for escalations. Overloading specialist teams would bottleneck even an automated process.

### **External Valuation Dependency**
- **A7 Collateral Valuation Triage and Routing** depends on the performance and reliability of external valuers, limiting the benefits of automation at this stage.

### **Core-Banking Integration Constraints**
- Deep core banking integration is required for critical modules like **A1 Registration Automation**, **A9 Contract Generation**, and **A11 Loan Activation Bot**. IT constraints limit full integration to two modules in the first six months.

# 3. Portfolio Alternatives

### **Portfolio 1: Quick-Win Portfolio**
- **Modules:** A1 (Auto-registration), A2 (Classification), A5 (Missing-document checklist), A10 (E-signature automation)
- **Optimizes:** Immediate labor savings, high-frequency benefits, visible improvements to customer-facing processes.
- **Sacrifices:** Control risk not significantly mitigated; limited long-term resolution of bottlenecks.

### **Portfolio 2: Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio**
- **Modules:** A1 (Auto-registration), A4 (Entity mismatch assistant), A5 (Missing-document checklist), A6 (Financial-statement normalization)
- **Optimizes:** Tackling synchronization points like missing documents and KYC backlog, reducing rework loops, improving process flow.
- **Sacrifices:** Customer friction may temporarily increase; less focus on downstream steps like contract generation or activation.

### **Portfolio 3: Compliance-First Portfolio**
- **Modules:** A3 (KYC hit pre-classifier), A4 (Entity mismatch assistant), A9 (Contract generation), A12 (Control remediation workbench)
- **Optimizes:** Resolution of compliance/control violations (e.g., KYC issues, contract loops), audit trail improvements.
- **Sacrifices:** Covers lower-frequency activities, delaying benefits to cycle time and customer-facing improvements.

# 4. Expected Process-Level Effects

### **Portfolio 1: Quick-Win Portfolio**
- **Cycle Time:** Moderate reduction for high-frequency activities.
- **Waiting Time:** Shorter application registration and customer nudging timelines.
- **Rework:** Minimal reduction; downstream errors unresolved.
- **Handovers:** Minor improvement during document handling.
- **Resource Demand:** Reduced effort for back office clerks and minor credit analyst relief.
- **Control Violations:** No major compliance risks mitigated.

### **Portfolio 2: Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio**
- **Cycle Time:** Significant reduction in KYC, document-handling, and financial-statement normalization phases.
- **Waiting Time:** Lower delays in synchronization across KYC specialists and credit analysts.
- **Rework:** Reduced rework for incomplete documents.
- **Handovers:** Major reduction across KYC, document normalization flow.
- **Resource Demand:** Expanded capacity for compliance specialists and credit analysts.
- **Control Violations:** Some indirect gains but no direct focus.

### **Portfolio 3: Compliance-First Portfolio**
- **Cycle Time:** No immediate reduction; some delays during stricter compliance checks.
- **Waiting Time:** Lengthens short-term as manual remediation is supplemented by stricter processes.
- **Rework:** Downstream rework reduced from stronger KYC and control-stage improvements.
- **Handovers:** Slight improvement from streamlined KYC escalation.
- **Resource Demand:** Higher short-term demand for compliance specialists.
- **Control Violations:** Direct reduction of KYC and contract-related violations.

# 5. Risks and Compensating Controls

### **Risks Introduced**
- **Auditability Gaps:** Automated decisions (e.g., classification and KYC) may lack visible override information.
- **False Positives/Negatives:** Misclassification risks from **A2** or KYC false negatives in **A3**.
- **Exception Handling:** Strain on specialist teams if escalation volumes rise suddenly.
- **Four-Eyes Compliance:** Automation without safeguards may increase compliance errors during peak periods.

### **Compensating Controls**
- **Auditable Trail:** Automation must log model input/output, user overrides, and final decisions.
- **Escalation Guardrails:** Clearly define triggers for human overrides; allocate escalation capacity proactively.
- **Policy Updates:** Regular audits and updates to rule libraries (e.g., contract rules in **A9**).
- **Exception Reporting:** Track and measure exception frequency to detect systemic patterns.

# 6. Recommended Phased Rollout

### **Phase 1 (Months 1–6)**
1. Implement **A1 Auto-registration** (deep integration).
   - **Metrics:** Reduction in registration time, error rate.
   - **Gate:** 95% successful integration with core banking.
2. Implement **A5 Missing-document checklist** (light integration).
   - **Metrics:** Loop reduction rate, customer notification response times.

### **Phase 2 (Months 7–12)**
3. Implement **A4 Entity mismatch resolution assistant**.
   - **Metrics:** Wait time for KYC escalations.
   - **Gate:** Verification that KYC-related escalations are reduced.
4. Implement **A6 Financial-statement normalization** (deep integration).
   - **Metrics:** Analyst throughput, reduction in normalization time.

---

# 7. Final Recommendation

### **Recommended Portfolio: Bottleneck/Synchronization Portfolio**
- **Why?** While high-frequency automation (**A1**) is included for quick wins, this portfolio focuses most effort on addressing critical synchronization delays (e.g., missing documents, KYC escalations) and analyst bottlenecks. These factors dominate the process’s end-to-end performance, making them higher leverage than raw frequency.
- **Trade-Off Rationale:** A compliance-first portfolio delays visible cycle-time improvements, while the quick-win portfolio risks exacerbating bottlenecks without addressing root causes. This balance addresses both systemic flow issues and stakeholder priorities.
