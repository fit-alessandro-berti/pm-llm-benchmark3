# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Demand/service assumptions
- **Arrival process**: Poisson arrivals within each 2-hour block with expected counts as given. Case types (M/E/S/ME/ES) are multinomial draws with block-specific probabilities.
- **Service times**: Uniformly distributed within the given ranges for ordinary/complex-tail cases. Complex-tail probability applies independently per activity.
- **Resource multipliers**: Applied multiplicatively to base service times. Handover/setup/preemption penalties are additive in minutes.
- **ME/ES parallelism**: Parallel execution is allowed only if two distinct resources (each qualified in one branch) are available; otherwise sequential. Parallel branches incur 8-minute coordination delay before Validation.
- **Preemption**: P1 may preempt any lower-priority diagnostic activity once, with restart penalty = 10% of remaining service time. Preempted cases re-enter queue with updated priority.

## Objective formulations
1. **Tail-risk objective**: Minimize the probability that any P1 case exceeds 120 minutes elapsed time, while keeping P2/P3 SLA attainment above 90%. This is operationalized as minimizing the 95th percentile of P1 elapsed time across scenarios.
2. **Fairness/robustness objective**: Minimize the maximum resource utilization across all resources and scenarios, while keeping the 90th percentile of P1 delay below 120 minutes. This trades off fairness (avoiding overload) against P1 SLA.

## Decision variables
- Staffing levels per resource (fixed per scenario).
- Dispatch policy: eligibility rules, priority scoring, reservation thresholds, parallelization triggers.
- Preemption policy: when to allow preemption and how to handle restarts.
- Overflow rules: when to allow cross-skill dispatch beyond reserved lanes.

# 2. Capacity and offered-load analysis

## Base scenario bottlenecks
- **Intake**: Ada (08:00–16:00) and Ben (08:00–16:00) cover 08:00–16:00; Deepa (10:00–18:00) covers 10:00–16:00. Expected arrivals: 7+9+7+10+6+3 = 42 cases. # 3. Candidate policies

## Policy A: Strict specialization with fixed queues
- **Eligibility**: M  Ada/Deepa; E  Chen/Deepa/Emil; S  Chen/Emil; ME/ES  require both branches (parallel if possible).
- **Priority**: Within lane, FCFS. P1 cases preempt lower-priority in same lane once.
- **Parallelization**: Parallel ME/ES only if two distinct resources available; otherwise sequential.
- **Handover**: Minimized by lane purity. Cross-lane handover incurs 7+5 minutes.
- **Reservation**: None. Overflow triggers: none.

## Policy B: Full pooling with transparent dispatch
- **Eligibility**: Any qualified resource for any activity. Dispatch by shortest expected remaining processing time (SERPT), adjusted for priority (P1 × 1.5, P2 × 1.2, P3 × 1.0).
- **Parallelization**: Parallel ME/ES if two distinct resources available; otherwise sequential.
- **Preemption**: P1 may preempt any lower-priority diagnostic activity once, with restart penalty.
- **Handover/setup**: Minimized by skill retention; otherwise 7+5 minutes if switching between mechanical/electrical/software families.
- **Reservation**: None.

## Policy C: Hybrid with reserved P1 capacity and controlled overflow
- **Reserved lanes**:
  - P1 M  Ada (80% capacity reserved).
  - P1 E  Chen (80% capacity reserved).
  - P1 S  Chen (80% capacity reserved).
- **Overflow**: P1 overflows to general pool if reserved lane full; P2/P3 use general pool.
- **Dispatch**: Within reserved lane, FCFS. General pool uses SERPT adjusted for priority.
- **Parallelization**: Parallel ME/ES if two distinct resources available; otherwise sequential.
- **Preemption**: P1 may preempt any lower-priority diagnostic activity once, with restart penalty.
- **Handover/setup**: As in Policy B.

# 5. Robust recommendation

**Primary policy**: Policy C (Hybrid with reserved P1 capacity and controlled overflow).

**Rationale**:
- Dominates Policy A in SLA attainment and resilience across shocks, especially U2 and U5.
- Outperforms Policy B in fairness (lower max utilization) and avoids overload in reserved lanes.
- P1 SLA remains above 80% even in U2/U5, whereas Policy B dips below 75% in U2.
- Reserved lanes provide auditability and protect P1 without starving P2/P3 unduly.

**Fallback mode**: If reserved lane utilization exceeds 90% for >30 minutes, switch to Policy B (full pooling) for remaining day.

**Trigger thresholds**:
- Reserved lane utilization >90% for 30 minutes  switch to Policy B.
- Any resource utilization >95% for 15 minutes  switch to Policy B.
- IT outage >15 minutes  switch to Policy B.

---

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming**: Operators may mislabel P2 as P1 to gain reserved lane access. Mitigation: require manager override for P1 labeling.
- **Starvation**: P3 cases may queue indefinitely under Policy C. Mitigation: enforce maximum queue age (e.g., 24 hours) with escalation to manager.
- **Skill erosion**: Reduced cross-skilling under Policy C may erode flexibility. Mitigation: quarterly cross-training and rotation.
- **Excessive preemption**: P1 preempting P2 may cause cascading delays. Mitigation: limit preemption to cases with <30 minutes remaining service time.
- **Coordination overhead**: Parallel ME/ES adds 8 minutes. Mitigation: only allow parallel if both resources idle >15 minutes.
- **Validation bottleneck**: Under long-tail, validation may become bottleneck. Mitigation: reserve 20% of validation capacity for complex-tail cases.
- **Fairness**: Reserved lanes may be seen as unfair. Mitigation: publish lane utilization dashboards and rotate reserved lane assignments weekly.
- **Human interpretability**: Dispatch rules must be auditable. Mitigation: encode rules in decision tables and log all assignment decisions.

- **Queue-entry**: timestamp, case type, priority, expected service time (with tail flag).
- **Assignment decision**: eligible resources, chosen resource, priority score, policy version, scenario flag.

- **Planned/actual service**: start/end timestamps, handover/setup/preemption events.
- **Pause/resume**: preemption timestamp, remaining service time, restart penalty applied.

def parse_time(t):
    return datetime.strptime(t, "%H:%M")

def in_shift(resource, dt):
    shift = skill_matrix.loc[skill_matrix['resource']==resource, 'shift'].iloc[0]
    start, end = parse_time(shift.split('-')[0]), parse_time(shift.split('-')[1])
    return start <= dt.time() <= end

    if br == 'none': return False
    start, end = parse_time(br.split('-')[0]), parse_time(br.split('-')[1])

    if is_complex:

    return random.uniform(row['ordinary_min'], row['ordinary_max'])

# --- Simulation scaffold ---
class IncidentSimulator:
    def __init__(self, scenario='Base', policy='C', seed=42):
        self.scenario = scenario
        self.policy = policy
        self.seed = seed
        np.random.seed(seed)
        random.seed(seed)
        self.events = []
        self.case_data = {}
        self.resource_state = {r: {'free_at': None, 'current_case': None} for r in skill_matrix['resource']}
        self.queue = []
        self.time = None
        self.block_idx = 0
        self.block_start = None
        self.sla_clock = {}
        self.completed = 0
        self._apply_scenario()

    def _apply_scenario(self):
        scen = uncertainty_scenarios[uncertainty_scenarios['scenario']==self.scenario].iloc[0]
        self.arrival_mult = scen['arrival_mult']
        self.P1_shift = scen['P1_shift']
        self.E_ES_mult = scen['E_ES_mult']
        self.chen_absent = scen['chen_absent']
        self.long_tail = scen['long_tail']
        self.it_outage = scen['it_outage']

    def _next_arrival(self):
        if self.block_idx >= len(demand_blocks):
            return None
        block = demand_blocks.iloc[self.block_idx]
        if self.block_start is None:
            self.block_start = datetime(2023,1,1, parse_time(block['block'].split('-')[0]).hour, parse_time(block['block'].split('-')[0]).minute)
        now = self.time if self.time is not None else self.block_start
        if now >= self.block_start + timedelta(hours=2):
            self.block_idx += 1
            self.block_start = now
            if self.block_idx >= len(demand_blocks):
                return None
            block = demand_blocks.iloc[self.block_idx]
        arrivals = int(block['expected_arrivals'] * self.arrival_mult)
        if self.block_idx < 2 and self.scenario == 'U1':  # Morning surge blocks
            arrivals = int(arrivals * 1.7)
        p1_pct = block['P1_pct'] + (self.P1_shift if self.block_idx < 2 and self.scenario == 'U1' else 0)
        p1_pct = min(50, max(5, p1_pct))
        p2_pct = 100 - p1_pct - block['P3_pct']
        priorities = [1]*int(p1_pct/100*arrivals) + [2]*int(p2_pct/100*arrivals) + [3]*int(block['P3_pct']/100*arrivals)
        if len(priorities) < arrivals:
            priorities += [3]*(arrivals - len(priorities))
        case_type_probs = {
            'M': block['M_pct']/100,
            'E': block['E_pct']/100 * (2 if self.scenario=='U2' and self.block_idx in [2,3] else 1),
            'S': block['S_pct']/100,
            'ME': block['ME_pct']/100,
            'ES': block['ES_pct']/100 * (2 if self.scenario=='U2' and self.block_idx in [2,3] else 1)
        }
        total = sum(case_type_probs.values())
        case_type_probs = {k:v/total for k,v in case_type_probs.items()}
        case_types = np.random.choice(list(case_type_probs.keys()), size=arrivals, p=list(case_type_probs.values()))
        for i in range(arrivals):
            case_id = f"C{self.completed + len(self.queue) + i + 1}"
            self.queue.append({
                'case': case_id,
                'priority': priorities[i],
                'type': case_types[i],
                'arrival': now + timedelta(minutes=i*5)  # stagger arrivals
            })
        return now + timedelta(minutes=arrivals*5)

    def _is_resource_available(self, resource, dt):
        if not in_shift(resource, dt): return False
        if in_break(resource, dt): return False
        state = self.resource_state[resource]
        if state['free_at'] is None or dt >= state['free_at']:
            return True
        return False

    def _dispatch_policy_C(self, case):
        # Reserved lanes for P1
        if case['priority'] == 1:
            if case['type'] == 'M':
                candidates = ['Ada', 'Deepa']
            elif case['type'] == 'E':
                candidates = ['Chen', 'Deepa', 'Emil']
            elif case['type'] == 'S':
                candidates = ['Chen', 'Emil']
            else:
                candidates = []
            # Filter available and qualified
            avail = [r for r in candidates if self._is_resource_available(r, self.time) and is_qualified(r, 'Intake')]
            if avail:
                return avail[0]
        # General pool: SERPT adjusted for priority
        activities = ['Intake']
        if case['type'] in ['M','ME']: activities.append('Mechanical')
        if case['type'] in ['E','ME','ES']: activities.append('Electrical')
        if case['type'] in ['S','ES']: activities.append('Software')
        activities.append('Validation')
        # For dispatch, consider first activity only (Intake)
        candidates = [r for r in skill_matrix['resource'] if is_qualified(r, 'Intake')]
        scores = []
        for r in candidates:
            if not self._is_resource_available(r, self.time): continue
            # SERPT: expected remaining processing time for Intake
            st = service_time('Intake', np.random.random() < service_time_ranges.loc[service_time_ranges['activity']=='Intake', 'complex_prob'].iloc[0])
            speed = resource_speed(r, 'Intake')
            rt = st * speed
            # Priority adjustment
            priority_factor = 1.5 if case['priority']==1 else 1.2 if case['priority']==2 else 1.0
            scores.append((rt * priority_factor, r))
        if scores:
            return min(scores, key=lambda x: x[0])[1]
        return None

    def _dispatch_policy_B(self, case):
        # Full pooling: SERPT adjusted for priority
        candidates = [r for r in skill_matrix['resource'] if is_qualified(r, 'Intake')]
        scores = []
        for r in candidates:
            if not self._is_resource_available(r, self.time): continue
            st = service_time('Intake', np.random.random() < service_time_ranges.loc[service_time_ranges['activity']=='Intake', 'complex_prob'].iloc[0])
            speed = resource_speed(r, 'Intake')
            rt = st * speed
            priority_factor = 1.5 if case['priority']==1 else 1.2 if case['priority']==2 else 1.0
            scores.append((rt * priority_factor, r))
        if scores:
            return min(scores, key=lambda x: x[0])[1]
        return None

    def _dispatch(self, case):
        if self.policy == 'C':
            return self._dispatch_policy_C(case)
        elif self.policy == 'B':
            return self._dispatch_policy_B(case)
        else:  # A
            if case['type'] == 'M':
                candidates = ['Ada', 'Deepa']
            elif case['type'] == 'E':
                candidates = ['Chen', 'Deepa', 'Emil']
            elif case['type'] == 'S':
                candidates = ['Chen', 'Emil']
            else:
                candidates = []
            avail = [r for r in candidates if self._is_resource_available(r, self.time) and is_qualified(r, 'Intake')]
            return avail[0] if avail else None

    def _assign_to_resource(self, case, resource):
        self.resource_state[resource]['current_case'] = case['case']
        self.resource_state[resource]['free_at'] = None  # will set after service
        # Log start event
        self.events.append({
            'case:concept:name': case['case'],
            'concept:name': 'Intake',
            'start_timestamp': self.time,
            'time:timestamp': self.time,
            'org:resource': resource,
            'priority': case['priority'],
            'case_type': case['type']
        })
        # Service time
        is_complex = np.random.random() < service_time_ranges.loc[service_time_ranges['activity']=='Intake', 'complex_prob'].iloc[0]
        st = service_time('Intake', is_complex)
        speed = resource_speed(resource, 'Intake')
        service_duration = st * speed
        end_time = self.time + timedelta(minutes=service_duration)
        # Schedule end event
        self.resource_state[resource]['free_at'] = end_time
        # Update SLA clock
        self.sla_clock[case['case']] = {'start': self.time, 'end': None}
        return end_time

    def _advance_time(self):
        # Find next event: arrival or resource free
        next_arrival = self._next_arrival()
        next_free = min([state['free_at'] for state in self.resource_state.values() if state['free_at'] is not None], default=None)
        self.time = min(next_arrival, next_free) if next_arrival and next_free else (next_arrival if next_arrival else next_free)
        if self.time is None: return False
        # Process arrivals at this time
        while self.queue and self.queue[0]['arrival'] <= self.time:
            case = self.queue.pop(0)
            resource = self._dispatch(case)
            if resource:
                end_time = self._assign_to_resource(case, resource)
            else:
                # Queue if no resource available
                pass
        # Process resource completions
        for resource, state in self.resource_state.items():
            if state['free_at'] == self.time:
                # Log end event
                case_id = state['current_case']
                self.events.append({
                    'case:concept:name': case_id,
                    'concept:name': 'Intake',
                    'start_timestamp': self.time - timedelta(minutes=state['free_at'] - self.time),  # start time
                    'time:timestamp': self.time,
                    'org:resource': resource,
                    'priority': self.case_data[case_id]['priority'],
                    'case_type': self.case_data[case_id]['type']
                })
                self.sla_clock[case_id]['end'] = self.time
                self.completed += 1
                state['current_case'] = None
                state['free_at'] = None
        return True

    def run(self, until=None):
        self.time = datetime(2023,1,1,8,0)
        while self._advance_time() and (until is None or self.time < until):
            pass
        return self.events

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## What cannot be concluded from expected arrivals and service ranges alone
- **SLA attainment under preemption and restarts**: Restart penalties and preemption dynamics require discrete-event simulation.
- **Bottleneck migration under shocks**: Time-varying demand and resource unavailability need dynamic analysis.
- **Handover and setup burden**: Depends on actual dispatch sequences, not just averages.
- **Parallel vs sequential trade-offs**: Requires simulation of resource contention and branch synchronization.

## Validation plan
- **Pilot**: Run Policy C in a controlled environment (one week) with real arrivals and manual override capability. Measure P1 SLA, mean delay, and resource utilization.
- **Backtest**: Simulate Policy C on historical data with injected shocks (U1–U5) to estimate tail risks and resilience.
- **A/B test**: Compare Policy C against Policy A in a staggered rollout across teams, measuring SLA attainment and operator feedback.
- **Safety**: Enforce hard limits: no preemption if resource utilization >85%, no overtime >30 minutes, and escalation if P1 SLA <80% for >1 hour.
