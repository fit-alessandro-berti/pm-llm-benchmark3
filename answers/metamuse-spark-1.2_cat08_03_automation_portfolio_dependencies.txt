# 2. Dependency and prerequisite map

**Upstream data quality dependencies (sequential illusion):**
Information system logs `KYC check -> Credit analysis` (4200) and `Financial document review -> Normalize (5050) -> Credit analysis (5000)` sequentially, but mining notes they are often concurrent. `Committee packaging (3900)` is a *synchronization* activity waiting for *all* of: KYC status, normalized financials, valuation if required, and analysis notes. Automating A8 (pack assembly) before A5/A6/A4/A7 yields empty or inconsistent packs. Similarly, A9 (contract) depends on clean `Committee decision -> Contract (3650)` data match; A6 depends on complete documents (A5) else extraction fails on low-quality PDFs.

**Legal / compliance controls:**
- KYC clearance must precede `Customer signing` (DFG 7300). 6.5% provisional signing violations prove system currently allows bypass. Any A10 must enforce hard gate from A3/A4/A12.
- Four-eyes: `Credit analysis` and `Committee packaging` by same person in 5.8% of High-exposure SME at month-end. A2 (correct exposure tagging) + A12 (workbench enforcement) are prerequisites for A8.
- Contract-decision match: 8.5% remediation loops, 14% for Credit-line renewal (`Contract -> Remediate 650 -> Contract 510`). A9 without maintained rule library + A12 increases legal risk.
- Auditability: Compliance requires every model suggestion + human override logged. Applies to A2, A3, A4, A6, A9.

**Human escalation capacity:**
`Resolve entity mismatch` (1750) handover network concentrates on 2 senior specialists among 3 FTE. A3 (pre-classifier) without A4 assistant and explicit escalation SLA *increases* false-positive load on same bottleneck. A6 without analyst capacity planning overloads 9 FTE analysts who are central in working-together network; automation that "increases clean inflow" without parallel analyst efficiency gain worsens 28h credit wait. RM behavior (incomplete instructions) must be changed for A5 to work.

**External valuation dependency:**
`Collateral valuation request (2400) -> Wait (2350) -> Committee packaging (1980)` is outside direct control (19% late) and not automatable internally (A7 low internal automation). Leverage is triage, early parallelization, and routing to best valuer, not bot speed. Requires A2 to start valuation immediately after `Credit analysis -> Valuation (2400)` rather than sequential.

**Core-banking integration constraints:**
Only two deep integrations in first 6 months. Deep = needs core decision tables, KYC/compliance engine, contract ledger: A3, A6, A9, A11, A12. Light = portal/RPA/API overlay: A1, A2, A5, A7, A10. A8 needs integration to four artifact stores - effectively deep. Phasing must defer at least 2-3 deep modules to H2.

# 3. Portfolio alternatives

**Portfolio Q – Quick-Win (Politically attractive, management's initial impulse)**
*Composition: A1 Auto-registration + A5 Missing-doc portal + A10 E-signature + A11 Loan activation bot*
- **Optimizes:** Visible direct labor saving on highest frequencies (11880 + 7300 + 7100), fast demoable wins, end-to-end "paperless" narrative, low technical risk.
- **Sacrifices:** Process-level throughput and control. A1 shifts bottleneck downstream (analyst/compliance overload), A10/A11 violate control findings by automating `Signing` and `Activation` before KYC/decision integrity is assured (6.5% provisional signing, 4-eyes violations). Does not touch 31h/58h synchronization delays. Fails compliance mandate unless A5 is counted as control (weak). Expected rework unchanged or worse. Resource demand: back-office idle, analysts/compliance flooded.

**Portfolio B – Bottleneck / Synchronization (Cycle-time focus)**
*Composition: A4 Entity-mismatch assistant + A5 Missing-doc checklist + A6 Statement extraction + A7 Valuation triage*
- **Optimizes:** Mean end-to-end cycle time and waiting time by attacking critical-path blockers: 31h entity mismatch (82% critical when present), 52% repeated missing-doc loops (3300), 20h+75m normalization queue, 58h external valuation sync. Targets prerequisites of `Committee packaging` sync point.
- **Sacrifices:** Defers visible high-frequency win (A1) and downstream automation (A9/A11). Requires most change management (analysts, RMs, external valuers) and human-in-loop design. Control improvement is indirect; needs companion A12 in next phase to satisfy compliance gate fully (A4 partly qualifies but A12 stronger). Limits deep integrations to A6 only in H1, respecting IT cap.

**Portfolio C – Compliance-First (Control & risk focus)**
*Composition: A12 Control remediation workbench + A3 KYC pre-classifier + A2 Classification recommender + A9 Contract generation with rule checks*
- **Optimizes:** Control violations, auditability, and rework prevention. A2 prevents misrouting to wrong control path; A3+A12 enforce KYC-before-signing and four-eyes with auditable trail; A9 enforces `Decision  Contract` match (cuts 14% Credit-line renewal remediation). Directly satisfies Compliance insistence.
- **Sacrifices:** Slower cycle-time impact in first year; A3/A9/A12 are all deep integrations (exceeds 2-deep-in-6-months limit if parallel). Requires mature rule library maintenance and heavy model governance for KYC false negatives. Leaves 58h valuation wait and 31h mismatch wait (unless A4 added) largely untouched, so waiting time improvement modest.

**Portfolio D – Balanced Hybrid (Recommended basis, within 4-module limit)**
*Composition: A5 + A4 + A6 + A12* — combines B + control element; discussed in Section 7 as recommended evolution of B.

# 5. Risks and compensating controls

| Automation risk | Manifestation in this process | Compensating control |
|---|---|---|
| **False negatives (missed risk)** | A3 misses KYC hit; A2 misclassifies High-exposure SME as Micro  wrong control path (no collateral, no committee) | Human escalation mandatory for High-exposure, model confidence threshold + senior specialist review, audit trail of suggestion/override/decision, periodic back-testing against adjudicated hits |
| **False positives (overload)** | A3 flags too many hits  3 FTE specialists flooded, 31h wait worsens; A6 flags poor PDFs as errors  analyst queue | Tuning with precision/recall monitoring, tiered auto-clear vs. review queues, capacity buffer, daily drift dashboard |
| **Model/Rule drift & garbage-in** | A6 extraction drifts on low-quality scans; A9 rule library outdated  contract terms diverge from `Committee decision` | Versioned rule library owned by Legal/Compliance with monthly review, extraction confidence score + human verification below threshold, reconciliation check contract vs. decision artifact before generation |
| **Four-eyes & segregation breach** | Auto-assembly (A8) or A3/A9 allows same analyst to do `Credit analysis` and `Committee packaging` for High-exposure at month-end | System-enforced segregation by exposure tag (A2), A12 workbench hard block + violation alert, month-end staffing guardrail |
| **Exception handling erosion** | Bots handle happy path; 11% KYC exceptions, 38% mismatch rework, 24% doc review exceptions fall through cracks | Explicit exception queues with SLA, retained human capacity (do not reduce FTE before stable), KB for `Resolve entity mismatch`, customer fallback channel for A5/A10 friction |
| **Auditability loss** | Automated decisions without trail violate compliance finding | Immutable log: applicant data, model version, score, suggested action, human override reason, final decision; linked to case ID for KYC-before-signing proof |
| **External dependency amplification** | A7 triage creates quality disputes with valuers, gaming | SLA & quality scorecard per valuer, triage logic transparent, parallel valuation for High-exposure to reduce 19% late tail |
| **Shifted bottleneck & overload** | A1/A5 increase clean cases  9 FTE analysts central bottleneck collapses (`Credit analysis` 110m + 28h wait) | Coupling: do not deploy upstream throughput bots without downstream capacity (A6) or WIP limits/Kanban before `Credit analysis`; monitor analyst utilization & waiting time before analysis |
| **Customer friction** | A5 rigid checklist, A10 e-sign consent issues | Co-design checklist with RMs, pilot with SME segment, plain-language nudging, opt-out human assist, consent/ID verification step |

# 6. Recommended phased rollout

**Principles:** No downstream bot (A8/A9/A11) before upstream controls (A2/A3/A4/A12) stable; respect 2 deep integrations in first 6 months; keep humans for exceptions; measure waiting time, not just service time.

**Phase 0 – Foundation (Months 0-2) No module, governance**
- Fix logging of concurrency (`KYC` || `Financial review`) for true process mining, baseline metrics.
- Define monitoring: median wait per activity (focus 31h, 58h, 44h, 28h), rework rates, % provisional signing, four-eyes violations, valuer lateness, analyst utilization, handover counts.
- Appoint rule owners (Legal for A9, Compliance for A3/A12) and exception capacity plan (retain 3 specialists, 9 analysts).

**Phase 1 – Unblock & hygiene (Months 2-6) Light + 1 deep**
- **Deploy A5 Missing-doc checklist (light)**  Gate: pilot on Working-capital (lower collateral), checklist accuracy >90%, RM adoption >70%, `Request missing docs` rate down 25% without NPS drop.
- **Deploy A2 Classification recommender (light)**  Gate: accuracy vs. senior back-office >92%, High-exposure recall >98%, auditable override log in place.
- **Deploy A4 Entity-mismatch assistant (light/medium, counts as light integration)**  Gate: median wait for `Resolve` down 30%, specialist handover concentration (Gini) reduced, 82% critical-path cases tracked.
- *Deep integration slot 1 used:* start A6 Financial-statement extraction *development* in parallel (deep), not yet production.

*Metrics after Phase 1:* `Financial review` wait , `Normalize` queue stable, provisional signing unchanged (expected).

**Phase 2 – Analyst relief & control hard gate (Months 6-9) Deep 1 + Light 1**
- **Go-live A6 Extraction & normalization (deep #1)**  Human verification queue for <85% confidence. Gate: analyst prep time  30%, extraction error rate <5% on pilot PDFs, no increase in `Credit analysis` wait (WIP limit enforced).
- **Deploy A12 Control remediation workbench (deep #2)**  This satisfies compliance mandate: enforces KYC-before-signing hard gate (`Signing` blocked without clearance), four-eyes block for High-exposure, contract-decision match check. Gate: provisional signing 6.5%<1%, four-eyes violations logged/blocked, `Contract -> Remediate` trending down.
- **Start A7 Valuation triage (light)** routing logic (no deep core needed). Gate: external wait median down, late 19% .

*IT constraint respected:* Only A6 and A12 are deep in H1/H2 transition (A6 started month 2, live month 6; A12 live month 8). A3/A9 deferred to respect 2-deep limit.

**Phase 3 – Scale & selective downstream (Months 9-12)**
- **Deploy A3 KYC pre-classifier (deep)** only after A4/A12 stable and escalation SLA proven. Pilot on low-risk Micro SME. Gate: false-negative rate <0.5% on back-test, audit trail complete.
- **Pilot A9 Contract generation (deep, next cycle)** for low-complexity Working-capital only, with rule library version control. Gate: remediation loops 8.5%<4%, zero contracts generated without KYC+decision artifacts.
- **Expand A7** to predictive late-valuation flagging; **pilot A10 E-signature** for cleaned KYC cases only.

*Defer to Year 2:* A1 Auto-registration (intentionally last), A8 Pack auto-assembly (needs all inputs clean), A11 Activation bot (only when A12 proves controls 100% reliable). This avoids shifted-demand failure.

**Monitoring & gate criteria across all phases:**
Cycle time median/90th percentile per product/exposure, waiting time distribution per activity, rework rate per DFG edge (`ResolveKYC`, `RequestReview`, `ContractRemediate`), handover network density, resource utilization (Back-office vs Analyst vs Specialist), control violation rates, model confidence/drift, customer NPS/friction.

Kill-switch: if analyst wait before `Credit analysis` rises >10% after A5/A6, introduce WIP cap and accelerate A6 tuning before further upstream automation.

# 7. Final recommendation

**Recommend Portfolio B evolved into Hybrid D for Year 1: A5 + A4 + A6 + A12** (Missing-doc portal, Entity-mismatch assistant, Statement extraction, Control workbench).

**Why this portfolio, not highest-frequency A1:**

1. **Frequency  bottleneck.** Process mining shows `Register application` (11880) costs 8m service and 1.5h wait, but downstream `Resolve entity mismatch` (31h median, 82% critical), `Wait for external valuation` (58h), `Financial document review` (18h wait + 24% rework with 52% repeated loops), and `Credit analysis` (28h wait + 110m judgment) dominate end-to-end time. Automating A1 saves visible clerk time but by Little's Law simply pushes WIP to the true constraints (9 analysts, 3 specialists), *increasing* cycle time for customers – the classic "high-frequency trap" confirmed by back-office utilization <65% after 15:00 and DFG concurrency.

2. **Synchronization, not service time, drives SLA.** `Committee packaging` waits for four parallel prerequisites. Portfolio B attacks the prerequisites themselves (A5A6 chain 50505000 Committee, A4 unlocks KYC, A7 would be next) rather than automating the sync activity A8 prematurely.

3. **Exception handling and resource reality:** 38% exception on `Resolve`, 52% repeated missing docs, 16% on `Normalize`, and RM-triggered loops require human-in-loop design. A4/A6 keep specialists/analysts in the loop for exceptions while automating preparation. This avoids the failure mode where throughput bots flood the working-together core (analysts).

4. **Control is non-negotiable:** Compliance requires at least one control-strengthening module. Portfolio Q fails this; Portfolio B alone is control-light. Adding A12 (instead of A7 in first year) satisfies the mandate, hard-blocks the observed 6.5% provisional signing and 5.8% four-eyes violations, and makes downstream A9/A11 safe later. It also compensates for auditability required for A2/A3/A6. Budget of 4 modules is respected; deep integrations are sequenced (A6, A12) within IT limit, deferring A1 (light but low value) and A11 (unsafe).

5. **Phased risk containment:** By starting with light prerequisites (A5,A4) and one deep (A6), we prove data quality before scaling KYC/contract automation (A3,A9). Political need for a "quick win" is met with A5 (high visibility to RMs/customers, measurable loop reduction in 2 months) rather than the misleading A1 win.

**Trade-off made explicit:** We sacrifice immediate labor savings on 11880 registrations and a demoable end-to-end bot story (A1/A11) to achieve 20-35% cycle-time reduction on the tail, cut rework loops by ~40%, and close control gaps that expose the bank to regulatory remediation. Frequency-based ROI looks higher for A1 on paper; process-level ROI – incorporating waiting time, rework, handovers, resource shifts, and violation cost (1.1% remediation on provisionals, 14% Credit-line renewal rework) – is higher for the bottleneck/control hybrid.

Year 2, once A12 proves KYC-before-signing gates and A6 stabilizes analyst load, we can safely add A7, A3, and then A9/A10, and only then reconsider A1/A11 – when upstream is capable of absorbing the freed inflow.
