## 1. Normalization Assumptions
All bounds are UTC and durations are seconds. For a displayed source-clock time, first apply its resolution uncertainty, convert each valid local-time interpretation to UTC, and subtract the offset interval: `[recorded_low − offset_high, recorded_high − offset_low]`. HUB has ±0.5 s measurement uncertainty and ±1 s clock error; SENSOR has ±5 s rounding and ±5 s clock error. Minute floors have an exclusive upper endpoint; nearest-rounding tie conventions are unspecified. For readability and computation I use closed outer envelopes with a 60-second floor. This can include a limiting endpoint that is not actually attainable; it is conservative at a boundary. Here it does not change any reported category.
Offsets and measurement errors are allowed to vary independently between events, even on the same system; the metadata does not establish a common constant offset. T-409 uses the explicitly weaker hourly estimate in place of the normal OPS-BER bound. All inferred bounds are conditional on the stated evidence being reliable. No probability distribution or midpoint is used in the classifications.
Pairs are eventually-following pairs and I impose their chronological order. In T-405 the explicit ingestion sequence is assumed to reflect true event order; otherwise fold assignments remain ambiguous. In T-409, whose weak bound permits a nominal start before the alert, ordering is a substantive assumption. In T-407 I assume network delivery constitutes an actual receipt and receipt precedes investigation start; both are conditional assumptions, since neither is acknowledged. Without those assumptions, receipt-dependent findings are INDETERMINATE. For T-404 the signed investigation duration is treated as true elapsed time, and its complete-event uncertainty is propagated when subtracting 18–25 minutes.
For T-406 I interpret the additional 0–5-minute late-entry delay as a delay between actual occurrence and the source-clock time entered, so I subtract it in addition to clock and rounding error. If it describes only when the record was submitted while the displayed timestamp refers to occurrence, do not subtract it. Time-zone conversions use CEST, EDT and BST as applicable. The Berlin fold is handled as a union, then combinations incompatible with the stated event order are removed.
Severity is descriptive, not a legal finding: high = certain SLA breach; moderate = possible SLA breach; statistical = profile deviation; none = certain pass. Confidence describes the evidence and depends on the assumptions, not a numeric probability. Baseline profile uses zeta 2; baseline SLA tolerance is 0.

        if all(a <= b for a,b in zip(lo,hi)):
            alternatives.append(dict(zip(order,zip(lo,hi))))
    chains[case] = alternatives

def merge(intervals):
    merged = []
    for lo,hi in sorted(intervals):
        if merged and lo <= merged[-1][1]:
            merged[-1] = (merged[-1][0], max(merged[-1][1],hi))
        else:
            merged.append((lo,hi))
    return merged

def durations(case, a, b):
    intervals = []
    for chain in chains[case]:
        if a in chain and b in chain:
            al,ah = chain[a]; bl,bh = chain[b]
            intervals.append((max(0.0,(bl-ah).total_seconds()), (bh-al).total_seconds()))
    return merge(intervals)

def classify(intervals, low, high):
    if not intervals:
        return 'INDETERMINATE'
    if all(low <= a and b <= high for a,b in intervals):
        return 'CERTAIN_PASS'
    if all(b < low or a > high for a,b in intervals):
        return 'CERTAIN_VIOLATION'
    return 'POSSIBLE_VIOLATION'

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency
For any possible result, the necessary information must narrow or constrain the relevant joint duration so that all feasible durations fall wholly inside or wholly outside the specific acceptance region. An additional record only helps if its uncertainty and semantics are sufficiently strong; exact timestamps are not always required. For an indeterminate result, first establish that the relevant event exists, occurs in the relevant lifecycle, and is ordered as required.
- **T-401 S1; profile AI:** obtain finer start-time resolution and event-specific clock offset, or a reliable synchronized elapsed-time measurement of alert to start. These resolve the overlapping 15-minute and zeta-2 boundaries.
- **T-402 S2; profile AI:** obtain a more precise OPS start time and offset, or a trusted start-to-containment duration; for profile AI, a sufficiently precise alert-to-start duration resolves the statistical boundary. S1 is already certain at zero tolerance, though its outcome at added tolerances needs similar precision.
- **T-403 S1; profile AI:** obtain a receipt acknowledgment with synchronized time or a tight event-specific vendor offset and finer timestamp resolution. At zeta 1, profile AC also needs a tighter receipt bound.
- **T-404 S1 and S2; profile AI:** obtain the actual investigation-start event or a narrower, independently corroborated investigation-duration bound and clock corrections. For S2, the start-to-containment elapsed duration could resolve the 3-second boundary overlap directly.
- **T-405 S1 and profile AI:** obtain better synchronized timing or reliable elapsed-time evidence for alert-to-start. Confirm that ingestion order reflects occurrence order and record the fold for each event; fold metadata alone removes the assumption but minute rounding and clock uncertainty still leave the boundary crossed.
- **T-406 S1, S2 and profile AI:** establish whether late entry shifts the displayed occurrence timestamp, establish the actual entry delay, and obtain a synchronized start timestamp or tighter clock-offset and rounding evidence. A reliable start-to-containment elapsed duration can resolve S2 directly.
- **T-407 S1 and profile AI:** first obtain a receipt acknowledgment or delivery record that establishes receipt as the SLA event, and evidence that receipt preceded start. Then bound the receipt time or alert-to-start elapsed duration tightly enough to resolve the relevant boundary. Without receipt or order evidence, receipt-dependent AI and AC are indeterminate, including at zeta 2.
- **T-409 S1 and profile AI:** verify that investigation followed receipt and obtain event-specific NTP telemetry or a synchronized start/elapsed-time record within a tighter bound. Without reliable order, the corresponding AI and other order-dependent results are indeterminate. At zeta 1, IC likewise needs a tighter start time.
- **T-408 profile IC at zeta 2:** obtain synchronized timing or an elapsed-duration measurement precise enough to decide whether IC is at most 3,300 s. T-408 S2 and S3 already have certain breaches at all requested tolerances. For any missing-close S3 or containment-to-close profile, record a trustworthy close event; absence of the event does not establish either a breach or compliance.

## 7. Governance Recommendation
Store event instants in UTC while retaining the original local timestamp, zone identifier, UTC offset, DST fold, source, timestamp resolution and rounding policy. Capture clock-offset telemetry with uncertainty and its validity period, preferably with event-level synchronization or monotonic elapsed-time measurements. Preserve whether a timestamp was measured, user-entered, inferred, corrected or late, together with provenance and the transformation history.
Record explicit lifecycle start and completion events, delivery and receipt acknowledgments, causal and incident identifiers, and signed evidence for inferred durations. Keep source-clock and event-order assumptions auditable. Alert on missing, contradictory, ambiguous or poorly synchronized evidence and retain the feasible interval or union instead of overwriting it with a point estimate.
Adopt an explicit enforcement policy: an interval merely overlapping the compliant region is not proof of compliance or breach. Treat a certain violation under reliable evidence as an enforcement candidate; route possible violations and indeterminate cases for evidence collection or review. Keep legal SLA thresholds and separately configurable statistical-profile thresholds distinct, document tolerance and zeta, and rerun decisions when stronger evidence arrives.
