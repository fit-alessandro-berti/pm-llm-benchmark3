# 2. Competing role hypotheses

- **Mechanism:** Dedicated resource for routing exceptions identified by automation (Bot_Rules) to the correct specialist or adjuster.
- **Evidence:** 64% of reopened claims, high exception_flag share, direct handovers from Bot_Rules.
- **Contradiction:** Owner description ("queue cleaner") may downplay her strategic triage role.
- **Prediction:** Without her, exception_flag cases will route inefficiently, increasing average service time.

**Hypothesis 2: Hidden Bottleneck / Key-Person Dependency**
- **Mechanism:** High-value knowledge or informal authority makes her the only person capable of correctly routing complex cases.
- **Evidence:** Absence causes 90th percentile exception cycle time to jump 66% (8.4 to 13.9 days).
- **Contradiction:** Ordinary claims do not slow down, suggesting the bottleneck is specific to complexity.
- **Prediction:** If she takes extended leave, exception queues will fill rapidly regardless of adjuster capacity.

**Hypothesis 3: Informal Quality Gatekeeper**
- **Mechanism:** She validates information before escalating, ensuring specialists receive complete packages.
- **Evidence:** Subcontracting patterns (Request Info  Assign Specialist).
- **Contradiction:** Compliance worries about her discretion in selecting specialists.
- **Prediction:** Replacing her with rigid rules might increase specialist rework (due to missing info).

# 3. Risk assessment

- **Bottleneck risk:** Confirmed for exception cases. Her absence creates queueing delays, but standard claims flow freely. This is a localized bottleneck.
- **Coordination risk:** High. She connects multiple teams (North, South, Medical, Legal). Removing her without a replacement could decouple these teams.
- **Key-person risk:** Moderate to High. Knowledge of routing logic appears implicit rather than codified.
- **Compliance/control risk:** Moderate. Her discretion in assigning specialists (subcontracting betweenness) could lead to inconsistent handling if not audited.
- **Workload fairness risk:** Moderate. Adjusters rely on her assignments, but she carries high variance.
- **Data-quality risk:** Low. Her events are short and frequent; logs seem reliable for network analysis.
