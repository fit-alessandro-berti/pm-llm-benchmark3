3. **Asymmetric Harm:**
   - **Regulatory exposure** (A/B) is **plausibly 10–50x worse** than deadline loss.
   - **False rejection (F)** is defensible only if evidence supports it (e.g., fuzzy match confidence + risk score justify rejection).

4. **Spillover Effects:**
   - **Interrupting P-901 (C)** trades P-884’s deadline for P-901’s breach risk (0.12–0.28).
   - **Waiting for S4 (35–65 min)** avoids spillover but risks deadline miss.

The ranking prioritizes **conformance-aware paths** that **minimize spillover** while **respecting the process’s guardrails**.
