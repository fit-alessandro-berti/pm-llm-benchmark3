## 1. Executive causal assessment

**Provisional verdict**: partly causal through mechanisms  
**Confidence level**: 70%  

**Strongest evidence for verdict**:  
The stratified cycle-time data by complexity reveal that while Orion's overall mean cycle time is higher (21.96 hours vs. Nova's 15.24), this reverses in low-complexity cases and narrows substantially in medium and high complexity. This suggests part of Orion's higher overall times comes from its case mix (e.g., more high-complexity, compliance-required cases). However, Orion still exhibits slightly longer median wait times across strata and operates predominantly at night under high arrival loads, potentially introducing causal queueing effects independent of case mix.  

**Strongest evidence against verdict**:  
Reviewer skill, unobserved case difficulty, and routing policies are not fully captured in the data, creating ambiguity about whether observed differences in cycle time are due to intrinsic effects from Orion's assignment or systematic selection into Orion.

**Proposed 50% reassignment**:  
Reassigning 50% of Orion’s cases to Nova may suffer from poorly understood causal drivers. If Orion’s delays are due to operational mechanisms (e.g., night shift, higher loads), the reassignment might alleviate queues and distribute workloads more evenly. However, if Orion is better suited for complex, compliance-heavy cases due to skill mix, assigning such cases to Nova may lead to bottlenecks or quality issues there, backfiring overall.

**Final recommendation**:  
The 50% reassignment proposal risks ignoring operational mechanisms like night-shift and compliance queues that are heavily concentrated in Orion. Instead, perform a small randomized pilot with 20% reassignment for low/medium complexity cases to test for queue impacts, coupled with compliance capacity augmentation to test mediation effects. Pair this with improved queue data collection and routing-weight transparency. Only after validated learning should broader reassignments occur.

## 7. Reproducible Python diagnostics

```python
import pandas as pd
from pm4py.objects.log.util.xes import factory as xes_factory
from pm4py.algo.discovery.dfg import algorithm as dfg_discovery
from pm4py.objects.conversion.log import factory as log_conversion

# Embedded CSV data
data = """
case_id,trace,complexity,group,shift,arrival_load,compliance_required,wait_before_review_h,review_service_h,rework_count,cycle_time_h,outcome_30d
C01,Register>Classify>Review>Close,Low,Orion,Night,High,No,3.3,3.1,0,7.2,No appeal
...
"""

# Load data
df = pd.read_csv(pd.compat.StringIO(data))

# Crude vs. stratified cycle times
crude_cycle_mean = df.groupby("group")["cycle_time_h"].mean()
stratified_means = df.groupby(["complexity", "group"])["cycle_time_h"].mean()

# Standardized contrast computation
weights = df.groupby("complexity").size() / len(df)
orion_mean = (df[df.group == "Orion"]
              .groupby("complexity")["cycle_time_h"].mean().values @ weights.values)
nova_mean = (df[df.group == "Nova"].groupby("complexity")["cycle_time_h"].mean().values @ weights.values)

print(f"Crude mean cycle time: {crude_cycle_mean}")
print(f"Stratified means: {stratified_means}")
print(f"Standardized contrast (Orion - Nova): {orion_mean - nova_mean}")

# Overlap diagnostics
overlap_counts = df.groupby(["complexity", "shift", "group"]).size()
print(f"Overlap counts: {overlap_counts}")

# Process-mining (trace variants and DFGs)
log = xes_factory.parse_log_string("\n".join(f"{row.trace};" for _, row in df.iterrows()))
dfg_orion = dfg_discovery.apply(log_conversion.apply(log[log.data["group"] == "Orion"]))
dfg_nova = dfg_discovery.apply(log_conversion.apply(log[log.data["group"] == "Nova"]))

print(f"Orion DFG: {dfg_orion}")
print(f"Nova DFG: {dfg_nova}")
```

## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Unobserved risk**: Obtain finer-grained complexity scores or manually scored case risks.  
2. **Dynamic queues**: Collect real-time or periodic queue-state data to clarify queue wait dynamics.  
3. **Reviewer skill**: Capture reviewer qualifications and historical quality outcomes.  
4. **Policy weights**: Document routing decision rules and their complexity/shift weighting explicitly.  
5. **Abandoned cases**: Include data on cases abandoned before completion to clarify selection biases.
