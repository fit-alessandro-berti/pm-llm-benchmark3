# 1. Assumptions and Interpretation

1. **Event semantics.** Events are recorded at activity completion only (narrative point 11). Therefore every requirement of the form "X may start only after Y is complete" is enforced at the trace level as "the (relevant) occurrence of Y precedes the occurrence of X." Start events are not modeled; "overlap" of two activities is observable only as freedom of interleaving of their completion events.
2. **Low-risk/high-risk choice.** The risk classification is case data and is not an event label. It is represented as a free exclusive choice in the control flow: `XOR( Supervisor Review , AND/PO( Quality Review , Regulatory Review ) )`. The model cannot check that a case that *is* high risk actually takes the high-risk branch; it only guarantees that exactly one of the two review routes occurs, that it starts after Analyze Root Cause, and that it completes before Release Material.
3. **Optional supplier consultation.** Consult Supplier is represented as `XOR( Consult Supplier , tau )`, i.e., an exclusive choice against a silent transition. Because this XOR block occurs exactly once and is not inside any loop, "at most once" is guaranteed structurally. The data condition that triggers it is deliberately unmodeled (assumption analogous to item 2).
4. **Passing inspection.** Pass/fail is not an event label. Abstraction: the *last* Inspect Trial in a trace is the passing one; every non-final Inspect Trial is a failed one and is followed by Adjust Parameters and a new Execute Trial–Inspect Trial cycle. This is exactly the semantics of `LOOP( body = Execute Trial → Inspect Trial , redo = Adjust Parameters )`: after each body execution the loop either exits (pass) or executes Adjust Parameters and repeats (fail). Adjust Parameters can therefore never occur after the final (passing) inspection. The models cannot distinguish "inspection passed but the team adjusted anyway and re-ran"; that trace is control-flow-identical to a legitimate failure iteration and is accepted (see Section 6).
5. **Duplicate visible labels.** Not permitted and not used: each of the 14 visible labels appears exactly once as a leaf/transition in each model. This keeps alignments and token replay unambiguous and avoids the label-duplication pitfalls discussed in Section 6. The only "invisible" nodes are silent (tau) transitions.
6. **Process-tree approximation policy: conservative under-approximation.** A block-structured tree cannot express that Plan Verification depends *only* on Contain Material while Design Corrective Action, the approval route, and Consult Supplier depend *only* on Analyze Root Cause, with a later cross-branch join at Execute Trial and at Release Material. I therefore insert a full synchronization point after `AND( Contain Material , Analyze Root Cause )`. Every trace accepted by the tree is intended behavior (no unsafe behavior is admitted), but some intended traces — e.g., Plan Verification completing before Analyze Root Cause completes — are excluded. I explicitly rejected (a) the permissive alternative (placing the trial after a big parallel block would either lose the trial's synchronization on Plan Verification/Design Corrective Action or force approvals to finish before the trial, an over-constraint elsewhere) and (b) duplication-based repair (duplicating the trial subtree under multiple branches breaks assumption 5 and distorts conformance diagnostics).
7. **"May overlap" interpretation.** Interpreted as *absence of an ordering constraint* between the corresponding nodes, i.e., interleaving freedom of completion events. Neither formalism's trace semantics distinguishes true concurrency from arbitrary interleaving once only completion events are logged, so this is the strongest faithful reading. In the POWL model, "may overlap" is realized precisely by *not* adding a partial-order edge between the two nodes.
8. **Loop-operator convention.** PM4Py's `Operator.LOOP` is used with two children `[do, redo]`: execute `do`; then either exit or execute `redo` followed by `do` again. Exit is implicit (no third tau child is added).

# 2. Intended Dependency Specification

Abbreviations used throughout: RD = Register Deviation, CM = Contain Material, ARC = Analyze Root Cause, PV = Plan Verification, DCA = Design Corrective Action, SR = Supervisor Review, QR = Quality Review, RR = Regulatory Review, CS = Consult Supplier, ET = Execute Trial, IT = Inspect Trial, AP = Adjust Parameters, RM = Release Material, AC = Archive Case. RISK = the selected approval route submodel; SUPP = the optional supplier submodel; TRIAL = the trial-cycle submodel.

| ID | Source activity or submodel | Target activity or submodel | Relation | Rationale |
|----|-----------------------------|-----------------------------|----------|-----------|
| D01 | RD | every other activity | precedence (unique start) | Narrative 1: every case starts with RD. |
| D02 | CM | ARC | concurrency (no mutual order) | Narrative 2: the two initial work streams proceed independently and may overlap. |
| D03 | CM | PV | precedence | Narrative 3: PV starts only after CM; note PV does **not** depend on ARC. |
| D04 | ARC | DCA | precedence | Narrative 4: DCA starts only after ARC; DCA does **not** depend on CM. |
| D05 | ARC | RISK | precedence | Narrative 5: route selected/executed after ARC. |
| D06 | SR | AND(QR, RR) | exclusive choice | Narrative 5: low risk → SR; high risk → both QR and RR. |
| D07 | QR | RR | concurrency (no mutual order) | Narrative 5: QR and RR may complete in either order. |
| D08 | ARC | CS | precedence, with CS optional (0..1) | Narrative 6: CS at most once, only after ARC. |
| D09 | CS | RM | precedence (conditional on selection) | Narrative 6: CS must complete before RM. |
| D10 | {PV, DCA} | first ET | synchronization (join of two branches) | Narrative 7: trial starts only when both work products exist. |
| D11 | RISK | TRIAL | **no order** (overlap allowed) | Narrative 7: approvals need not precede the trial and may overlap it. |
| D12 | ET | IT | precedence inside one cycle | Narrative 8: a cycle is ET then IT. |

# 3. PM4Py Process-Tree Model

**1. Compact expression** (this string is `pm4py.parse_process_tree`-compatible; `->` = sequence, `+` = parallel, `X` = exclusive choice, `*` = loop with children `[do, redo]`, `tau` = silent step):

```text
->( 'Register Deviation',
    +( 'Contain Material', 'Analyze Root Cause' ),
    +( ->( +( 'Plan Verification', 'Design Corrective Action' ),
           *( ->( 'Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial' ),
              'Adjust Parameters' ) ),
       X( 'Supervisor Review', +( 'Quality Review', 'Regulatory Review' ) ),
       X( 'Consult Supplier', tau ) ),
    'Release Material',
    'Archive Case' )
```

Mapping to the narrative: the outer sequence enforces the start/end rules (D01, D16) and the release gate (D15, realized as the join of the middle parallel block). `+( CM , ARC )` is the initial concurrent block (D02). The middle `+` runs three streams: (i) `->( +( PV , DCA ), *( ->( ET , IT ), AP ) )` — the trial waits for both work products (D10) and then loops ET→IT with AP only on the redo path (D12–D14); (ii) the risk route `X( SR , +( QR , RR ) )` (D06, D07), free to interleave with the trial stream (D11); (iii) the optional supplier `X( CS , tau )` (D08, D09, D17). Both approximated dependencies (D03, D04, D05, D08 in their *fine-grained* form) are coarsened: everything in the middle block waits for *both* CM and ARC.

**2–3. Self-contained construction code with explicit parent/child handling:**

```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree, Operator

def make_node(operator=None, label=None, parent=None):
    """Create a ProcessTree node, set its parent, and register it in parent.children."""
    node = ProcessTree(operator=operator, label=label, parent=parent)
    if parent is not None:
        parent.children.append(node)
    return node

tree = make_node(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE)

make_node(label="Register Deviation", parent=tree)

initial = make_node(operator=Operator.PARALLEL, parent=tree)
make_node(label="Contain Material", parent=initial)
make_node(label="Analyze Root Cause", parent=initial)

middle = make_node(operator=Operator.PARALLEL, parent=tree)

# Stream 1: trial prerequisites and repeat-until-pass trial cycle
trial_stream = make_node(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE, parent=middle)
prereq = make_node(operator=Operator.PARALLEL, parent=trial_stream)
make_node(label="Plan Verification", parent=prereq)
make_node(label="Design Corrective Action", parent=prereq)
trial_loop = make_node(operator=Operator.LOOP, parent=trial_stream)
cycle = make_node(operator=Operator.SEQUENCE, parent=trial_loop)   # child 0: do
make_node(label="Execute Trial", parent=cycle)
make_node(label="Inspect Trial", parent=cycle)
make_node(label="Adjust Parameters", parent=trial_loop)            # child 1: redo

# Stream 2: risk-dependent approval route
risk = make_node(operator=Operator.XOR, parent=middle)
make_node(label="Supervisor Review", parent=risk)
high = make_node(operator=Operator.PARALLEL, parent=risk)
make_node(label="Quality Review", parent=high)
make_node(label="Regulatory Review", parent=high)

# Stream 3: optional supplier consultation
supplier = make_node(operator=Operator.XOR, parent=middle)
make_node(label="Consult Supplier", parent=supplier)
make_node(parent=supplier)   # operator=None, label=None -> silent (tau) leaf

make_node(label="Release Material", parent=tree)
make_node(label="Archive Case", parent=tree)
```

The `ProcessTree` constructor stores the `parent` reference but does **not** append the node to `parent.children`; `make_node` performs that append explicitly, so every parent/child link is set on both sides. The loop node has exactly two children in the order `[do, redo]` per assumption 8.

**4. What the tree cannot represent exactly.** The tree coarsens the four fine-grained entry dependencies D03/D04/D05/D08 into one global synchronization: because Plan Verification sits in the same parallel block as Design Corrective Action, the approvals, and the supplier option, and that whole block is *sequenced after* `+( CM , ARC )`, the tree demands that **both** Contain Material and Analyze Root Cause are complete before Plan Verification, Design Corrective Action, either review route, or Consult Supplier can occur. The intended process only requires CM before PV, and only ARC before the other three. Consequently intended traces such as ⟨RD, CM, **PV**, ARC, …⟩ (verification planned while root-cause analysis is still running) or ⟨RD, ARC, **DCA**, CM, …⟩ are rejected by the tree. This is the announced conservative under-approximation: the tree's language is a strict subset of the intended language, and every excluded trace differs from an accepted one only in moving PV before ARC or moving DCA/reviews/CS before CM. Everything else — the release gate, the approval/trial overlap, the supplier window, and the repeat-until-pass loop with the AP prohibition — is represented exactly.

# 4. PM4Py POWL Model

**1. Compact description** (nodes of one strict partial order; `->` are partial-order edges; unlisted pairs are unordered):

```text
PO(
  nodes = {
    RD, CM, ARC, PV, DCA, RM, AC,
    RISK  = XOR( SR , PO(nodes={QR, RR}, edges={}) ),
    SUPP  = XOR( CS , tau ),
    TRIAL = LOOP( do = PO(nodes={ET, IT}, edges={ET->IT}) , redo = AP )
  },
  edges = {
    RD -> CM,  RD -> ARC,
    CM -> PV,
    ARC -> DCA,  ARC -> RISK,  ARC -> SUPP,
    PV -> TRIAL, DCA -> TRIAL,
    TRIAL -> RM, RISK -> RM,  SUPP -> RM,
    RM -> AC
  }
)
```

**2–4. Self-contained construction code:**

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# Visible activities (each label used exactly once)
reg      = Transition(label="Register Deviation")
contain  = Transition(label="Contain Material")
analyze  = Transition(label="Analyze Root Cause")
plan     = Transition(label="Plan Verification")
design   = Transition(label="Design Corrective Action")
sup_rev  = Transition(label="Supervisor Review")
qual_rev = Transition(label="Quality Review")
reg_rev  = Transition(label="Regulatory Review")
consult  = Transition(label="Consult Supplier")
execute  = Transition(label="Execute Trial")
inspect  = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
adjust   = Transition(label="Adjust Parameters")
release  = Transition(label="Release Material")
archive  = Transition(label="Archive Case")

# Risk route: XOR( Supervisor Review , PO{Quality Review, Regulatory Review} )
high_risk = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[qual_rev, reg_rev])   # no edges: concurrent
risk_route = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[sup_rev, high_risk])

# Optional supplier consultation: XOR( Consult Supplier , tau )
supplier_opt = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR,
                            children=[consult, SilentTransition()])

# Trial cycle: LOOP( do = ET -> IT , redo = AP )
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[execute, inspect])
trial_body.order.add_edge(execute, inspect)
trial_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[trial_body, adjust])

# Root partial order over all top-level obligations
powl = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[reg, contain, analyze, plan, design,
                                 risk_route, supplier_opt, trial_loop,
                                 release, archive])

powl.order.add_edge(reg, contain)            # D01 (start, via transitivity)
powl.order.add_edge(reg, analyze)            # D01
powl.order.add_edge(contain, plan)           # D03: PV needs only CM
powl.order.add_edge(analyze, design)         # D04: DCA needs only ARC
powl.order.add_edge(analyze, risk_route)     # D05
powl.order.add_edge(analyze, supplier_opt)   # D08
powl.order.add_edge(plan, trial_loop)        # D10 (cross-branch join, part 1)
powl.order.add_edge(design, trial_loop)      # D10 (cross-branch join, part 2)
powl.order.add_edge(trial_loop, release)     # D15: passing inspection before release
powl.order.add_edge(risk_route, release)     # D15: approval route before release
powl.order.add_edge(supplier_opt, release)   # D15/D09: CS (if chosen) before release
powl.order.add_edge(release, archive)        # D16
```

Every ordering requirement of Section 2 is one explicit `order.add_edge(...)` call (D01 holds by transitive closure of the strict partial order: RD precedes CM and ARC, and every other node is transitively behind one of those; AC is behind RM, behind everything else). `StrictPartialOrder` is used for the root, the high-risk pair, and the loop body; `OperatorPOWL` provides the two XORs and the LOOP; `Transition` provides all visible activities; `SilentTransition` provides the skip branch of the supplier XOR.

**5. Why every intentionally unconnected pair may remain concurrent.**
- **CM — ARC:** narrative 2 explicitly allows the two initial streams to overlap (D02).
- **PV — {ARC, DCA, RISK, SUPP}:** PV depends only on CM (D03); the narrative imposes no order between verification planning and root-cause work, design, approvals, or supplier contact, so omitting these edges realizes exactly the intended cross-branch independence.
- **DCA — {CM, PV, RISK, SUPP}:** DCA depends only on ARC (D04); the same reasoning applies symmetrically.
- **RISK — TRIAL and RISK — {PV, DCA, SUPP}:** narrative 7 states the approval route may overlap the trial cycle (D11); no order with planning/design/supplier is stated, so none is imposed.
- **SUPP — {PV, DCA, RISK, TRIAL}:** narrative 6 explicitly allows overlap with design, planning, approvals, and trial work (D17); the only obligations are ARC before CS and CS before RM, both of which are edges.
- **QR — RR (inside the high-risk PO):** narrative 5 allows either completion order (D07).

In POWL, absence of an edge is a deliberate statement of interleaving freedom, and under assumption 7 that is precisely what "may overlap" means at completion-event granularity. The POWL model therefore represents the intended language **exactly** (up to the data abstractions of assumptions 2–4, which apply to any pure control-flow model).

# 7. Preferred Representation and Validation Plan

**Choice: the POWL model is canonical.** The narrative's defining feature is a non-block-structured dependency pattern (D03/D04/D10/D15 cross branch boundaries), and POWL expresses each of these as one explicit partial-order edge with no loss, no duplication, and no silent scaffolding beyond a single skip-tau. The tree should be kept only as a derived, deliberately conservative artifact (useful where tree-only algorithms — e.g., inductive-miner-style comparison or tree-based simulation — are required), with A5-style traces documented as known false negatives.

**Validation plan** (all steps are concrete pm4py operations on the `tree` and `powl` variables from Sections 3–4):

1. **Conversion to accepting Petri nets.** `net_t, im_t, fm_t = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)` and `net_p, im_p, fm_p = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl)`. Treat successful conversion as *input to inspection*, not as evidence of fidelity: render both nets (`pm4py.view_petri_net`) and manually verify that the POWL net contains the two cross-branch joins (a transition enabled only by tokens from the PV-place and the DCA-place; the RM transition consuming from trial-, risk-, and supplier-places).
2. **Soundness checking.** `pm4py.check_soundness(net_t, im_t, fm_t, print_diagnostics=True)` and the same for the POWL net. Expect easy soundness (both formalisms are sound by construction); a failure would indicate a construction or conversion bug, not a modeling decision.
3. **Bounded/sampled playout.** `log = pm4py.play_out(model)` for both models (bounded by the playout's default trace cap; loops sampled, not enumerated). Programmatically assert invariants over every sampled trace: first event RD, last AC; RM present exactly once; index(CM) < index(PV); index(ARC) < index(DCA); max(index over selected reviews) < index(RM); every AP is preceded by an IT and followed by an ET; the last IT precedes RM; CS occurs at most once and, if present, after ARC and before RM. For the POWL playout additionally search for a witness of the fine-grained entry (some trace with PV before ARC) — it must exist; for the tree playout it must not.
4. **Explicit positive and negative trace tests.** Build an event log containing exactly the eight witnesses of Section 5 (as a pandas DataFrame with `case:concept:name`, `concept:name`, and a dummy timestamp) and compute `pm4py.fitness_alignments(log, net, im, fm)` per net. Required outcome: per-trace fitness 1.0 for A1–A4 on both nets, for A5 on the POWL net only; fitness < 1.0 for F1–F3 on both nets and for A5 on the tree net. Any other outcome falsifies either the model or my Section 5 verdicts.
5. **Silent-behavior check (targets assumption 3/5).** From the playouts, verify both a trace containing Consult Supplier and a trace not containing it exist (the tau branch is actually reachable and actually silent — no empty-labeled event leaks into the visible trace), and that no trace contains Consult Supplier twice. Also verify each of the 14 labels maps to exactly one labeled transition in each converted net (`len({t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label}) == 14` and no label repeated) — the duplicated-label check, expected to pass trivially because duplication was excluded by design.
6. **Limitations.** (a) Sampled playout cannot prove language equality: the loop makes the intended language infinite, so step 3 verifies invariants on a sample, not the full language. (b) Alignment fitness confirms membership for the eight witnesses only; the intended language is characterized declaratively (Section 2), and no finite test suite certifies that *no other* discrepancy exists. (c) Soundness of a converted net says nothing about fidelity to the narrative — a sound net for the wrong process passes step 2. (d) The POWL→Petri-net converter is trusted; a converter bug would corrupt every downstream check, which is why step 1 includes manual net inspection. (e) All checks operate at completion-event granularity; genuine start/complete overlap claims are untestable in this framework.

# 8. Final Self-Critique

1. **Semantic weakness — data-abstraction leakage.** Three requirements are stated as data-driven ("low risk", "inspection fails", "supplier involved") and my models replace all three with free choices. The resulting models accept behavior the organization would consider wrong (a high-risk case routed only through Supervisor Review; an Adjust Parameters after an inspection that actually passed). I declared this in the assumptions, but the declaration does not mitigate it: any conformance report based on these models is blind to exactly the violations that matter most for compliance, and the answer offers no data-aware (e.g., DPN/guard-based) companion model.
2. **Semantic weakness — the under-approximation is asserted, not measured.** I claim the tree's language is a strict subset of the POWL language differing only in the coarsened entry synchronization, and I support this with one witness family (A5-type traces) plus informal reasoning. I did not perform (and the validation plan only samples toward) a systematic language-containment argument, e.g., via a simulation relation between the two converted nets, so a second, unnoticed divergence could exist.
3. **Robustness weakness — version-sensitive API surface.** The code follows the two-child `Operator.LOOP` convention, pm4py's `ProcessTree` constructor behavior (parent stored but child registration manual), and `OperatorPOWL`/`StrictPartialOrder` constructor signatures of pm4py 2.7.x. Older or future pm4py versions differ (e.g., three-child loops, changed POWL parsing helpers), and the answer does not gate on `pm4py.__version__` or provide fallbacks, so the "self-contained" code is self-contained only relative to one library generation.
