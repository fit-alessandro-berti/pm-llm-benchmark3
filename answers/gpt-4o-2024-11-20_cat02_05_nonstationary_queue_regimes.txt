# 1. Data audit and estimands

### Queueing quantities and units
1. **Arrivals (`arrivals`)**: Number of customer requests entering the system during opening hours (07:00-19:00).
2. **Completions (`completed`)**: Number of requests resolved during the same window.
3. **Service times (`median_service_min`, `p90_service_min`)**: Median and 90th percentile of active handling time in minutes (excluding waiting).
4. **Mean WIP (`mean_wip`)**: Average number of active cases in progress across 15-minute snapshots.
5. **Staffing (`staff_fte`)**: Concurrent full-time-equivalent (FTE) availability of staff.
6. **Complexity (`complex_pct`)**: Percentage of arrivals following a multi-step process.
7. **Peak period arrivals (`peak_08_10_pct`)**: Fraction of daily arrivals between 08:00 and 10:00.

### Threats to naive inference
1. **Time aggregation mismatch**: Daily aggregates obscure variability within the 12-hour window and across days.
2. **Backlog carry-over**: Completed cases might originate from prior days, decoupling arrivals and completions.
3. **Non-stationarity**: Changes in staffing, complexity, or demand over time violate steady-state assumptions.
4. **Outages and recovery**: Disruptions like the 2026-04-15 outage skew WIP and completion rates.
5. **Overdispersion**: Burstiness in arrivals (e.g., peak 08:00-10:00) and completions complicate uniform modeling for service intensity and capacity.
