## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning

No single monetized utility is given and none would be credible because harms are asymmetric and non-monetary. Comparison uses range dominance and lexical compliance priority.

* **Deadline loss vs regulatory loss:** Avoided operational loss for meeting deadline is €20k-35k (bounded, symmetric). Regulatory/compliance harm from unlawful execution is €100k-500k *plus* non-monetary consequences (license, personal liability, criminal exposure) that are not commensurable. €20k-35k does not dominate €100k-500k even at extremes. Any action that trades a certain €100k+ exposure for a possible €20k gain is dominated unless the exposure is controlled via conformant approval.

* **False rejection/hold harm:** €15k-80k customer harm. This is also bounded but larger variance than deadline loss. It dominates deadline loss at high end (80k > 35k) but is still dominated by regulatory harm (80k < 500k). Therefore actions that *increase* false-rejection risk without reducing regulatory risk (F premature rejection) are not preferred over conformant review that preserves option value (C/D).

* **Prediction transportability:** Given predictions are observational (P breach 0.48-0.64, P approve 0.55-0.75 under normal processing), they do not transport to interventional shortcuts A/B/G. The 0.55-0.75 approval forecast assumes a conformant review; using it to justify A/B is a causal error. Only C/D/E preserve the data-generating process for that forecast.

* **Option value:** C and D* preserve ability to Approve *or* Reject after independent review, keeping expected harm at min(customer harm, regulatory harm) rather than committing to one tail. A/B commit to execution tail and its regulatory harm; F commits to rejection tail and its customer harm. Under uncertainty, preserving the branch is dominant.

* **Dominance ordering:** CONFORMANT NOW options with moderate time cost strictly dominate CURRENTLY NONCONFORMANT options regardless of apparent deadline benefit, because regulatory harm range strictly exceeds operational benefit range and is triggered with probability ~1 if audited. Among conformant options, ranking follows expected deadline success vs spillover: D* (low spillover, information value)  C (higher spillover but faster)  E (certain deadline loss)  conditional F.

Confidence is expressed as range, not point estimate, to reflect stale risk score (12m old), unresolved fuzzy match, and S4 variance.
