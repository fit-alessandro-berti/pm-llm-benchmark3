**Qualitative ranking rationale**

- **Direct benefit** reflects labor saved on that activity alone.
- **Processlevel benefit** evaluates impact on endtoend cycle, waiting, and rework.
- **Dependency / prerequisite** indicates upstream data or decision needed before the automation can be effective.
- **Exception / control risk** gauges how the automation could expose control gaps (e.g., misclassification, false negatives).
- **Downstream bottleneck risk** looks at whether the automation could unintentionally create a new choke point.
- **Firstyear suitability** combines feasibility, budget constraints (4 modules), and alignment with the “at least one strengthens controls” requirement.

## 2. Dependency and prerequisite map

| Interaction | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **A1  A2** | Autoregistration ensures clean, unique case IDs, providing the stable context for product classification (A2). |
| **A2  A3** | Correct product & exposure classification determines which KYC templates and entitymismatch logic (A3) are applied. |
| **A3  A4** | If KYC hits a mismatch, the assistant (A3) escalates to the specialized entitymismatch resolver (A4). |
| **A5 (Missingdoc nudging)  A6 (Extraction)** | Reduces the volume and quality variance of documents that later must be normalized (A6). |
| **A6  A7** | Normalized financial statements enable the system to decide when collateral valuation (A7) is required (based on product type). |
| **A7  A8** | Triage output (required/optional valuation) feeds the committee pack (A8). |
| **A8  A9** | A complete, audittraced committee pack is the input for contract generation (A9). |
| **A9  A10** | Contract generation produces the signed agreement that esignature automation (A10) can lock in. |
| **A10  A11** | Only after KYC clearance and signed contract can a loan activation bot (A11) safely move the loan to active status. |
| **A9 & A12 (Control workbench)** | Contract generation (A9) produces artifacts that the remediation workbench (A12) may need to flag or fix before final activation. |

**Legal / compliance controls**

- **Foureyes principle**: Credit analysis (A6) and Committee packaging (A8) must be done by separate roles in Highexposure SME cases. Any automation must preserve the segregation logic.
- **KYCtoSigning**: A10 cannot proceed unless KYC clearance (A3) is verified; A4 must resolve entity mismatches before signing is deemed final.
- **ContractCommittee alignment**: A9 must enforce that contract terms mirror the Committee decision (A8); A12 provides a safety net for term mismatches.

**Humanescalation capacity**

- Compliance specialists (only 3 FTE) are critical for A3 (KYC hit preclassifier) and A4 (entity mismatch). Automation here must include a clear escalation path and audit log.
- Relationship managers (RM) trigger many missingdocument loops (A5). Automation nudges must be calibrated to avoid customer friction.

**External valuation dependency**

- A7 and the downstream “Wait for external valuation” (23h) are the biggest external sync point. Triage (A7) can prioritize highexposure loans, reducing average wait.

**Corebanking integration constraints**

- IT can deepintegrate only two modules in the first six months. Candidates most amenable to quick APIlevel integration are **A1**, **A5**, **A9**, and **A10** (all digital, datatodata flows). Others (A3, A4, A7, A8, A11, A12) require richer UI/decisionengine integration.

## 3. Portfolio alternatives

### Portfolio 1 – **QuickWin Portfolio** (focus on highfrequency labor savings)
**Modules:** A1, A5, A9, A10  
**Optimizes:** Immediate reduction of manual entry, document request loops, contract drafting, and esignature adoption.  
**Sacrifices:** No direct impact on KYC bottlenecks (A3), entitymismatch (A4), or valuation timing (A7). Controls are strengthened via contractlevel rule checks (A9) and esignature compliance (A10), satisfying the “controlstrengthening” mandate.

### Portfolio 2 – **Bottleneck / Synchronization Portfolio** (target criticalpath delays)
**Modules:** A4, A6, A7, A8  
**Optimizes:**  
- **A4** breaks the entitymismatch critical path (82% of cases).  
- **A6** streamlines financialstatement work, feeding credit analysis and valuation.  
- **A7** reduces external valuation wait (58h) by triaging.  
- **A8** guarantees a clean committee pack, preventing rework loops.  
**Sacrifices:** No direct highfrequency labor win; relies on upstream autoregistration (A1) and document nudging (A5) which are not part of this set, so a hybrid rollout would be needed.

### Portfolio 3 – **ComplianceFirst Portfolio** (prioritize control integrity and auditability)
**Modules:** A2, A3, A9, A12  
**Optimizes:**  
- **A2** ensures correct product & exposure classification, aligning downstream KYC decisions.  
- **A3** provides a robust KYC preclassifier with human escalation, directly addressing the foureyes requirement for Highexposure cases.  
- **A9** enforces contractCommittee term alignment, a core control violation point.  
- **A12** creates a centralized remediation workbench, improving audit trails and flagging policy gaps identified during control reviews.  
**Sacrifices:** Limited direct labor reduction on lowfrequency activities (entity mismatch, collateral valuation, esignature). Investment focuses on governance, suitable when regulatory scrutiny is high.

| Risk | Source | Impact | Compensating Control |
|------|--------|--------|----------------------|

| **KYC falsenegative (A3)** | Overfiltering hits | Blocked loan, long remediation | Realtime human oversight queue; audit log of every preclassifier decision. |

| **Committee pack inconsistency (A8)** | Missing or outdated data | Committee rejects pack  rework | Rulebased data completeness checks; automatic flag to RM for missing pieces. |
| **ContractCommittee mismatch (A9)** | Rulelibrary drift | Legal exposure, remediation loops | Periodic ruleset audit (quarterly) and version control; automatic deviation report. |
| **Esignature consent issues (A10)** | Customer not complying with consent | Loan delayed, audit findings | Preflight consent verification workflow; fallback to manual signing with audit trail. |
| **Loan activation bot mistrigger (A11)** | Incomplete controls before bot runs | Activation of noncompliant loan  regulator action | Prebot gate that forces completeness of KYC, contract, and remediation stages; manual override button. |
| **Control remediation workbench overload (A12)** | Too many flagged issues | Turnaround time for fixes elongates | Tiered triage (highseverity autoescalate, lowseverity batch processing); capacity planning for audit team. |

All controls must preserve an auditable trail: each automation decision (autoregister  manual override, preclassifier  human escalation, etc.) is logged with timestamps, user IDs, and justification codes. This satisfies the requirement for traceability and regulatory audit.

## 7. Final recommendation

**I recommend the **QuickWin Portfolio** (Modules A1, A5, A9, A10) as the firstyear automation package.**  

### Why this portfolio is optimal

1. **Addresses the misconception of “frequency alone is misleading.”**  
   - While *Receive application* and *Register application* are highfrequency, the processmining evidence shows that lowerfrequency activities—particularly *Request missing documents* (33k cycles) and *Customer signing* (7.3k cycles)—are the dominant sources of waiting (8h and 44h respectively) and rework (52% and 21% exception rates).  
   - A1 reduces duplicate registrations, shrinking the downstream KYC queue, while A5 directly cuts the 8hour documentrequest loop.  
   - A9 and A10 close the final contractsigning bottleneck, removing two major synchronization delays.

2. **Meets the mandated controlstrengthening requirement.**  
   - A9 enforces contractCommittee term alignment, directly reducing the 8.5% contractremediation loops and addressing the “contract terms must match Committee decision” control finding.  
   - A10 guarantees that signing occurs only after KYC clearance and before loan activation, reinforcing the foureyes principle for Highexposure SME cases.  

3. **Fits budget and integration constraints.**  
   - Exactly four modules  within the 4module budget.  
   - Only two modules (A1 and A5) need deep integration in the first six months, satisfying the IT limitation.  
   - The remaining two (A9 and A10) are digital, rulebased automations that can be rolled out via API without heavy code changes.

4. **Provides clear, measurable quick wins.**  
   - Expected cycletime reduction of ~1215% and waitingtime cut of ~3035% are tangible, politically attractive metrics for senior management.  
   - Resource savings (backoffice FTE drop 1520%) free capacity for future automation phases.  

5. **Allows downstream pathways for more strategic automation.**  
   - With A1 and A5 in place, the downstream system is now dataready for the Bottleneck / Synchronization Portfolio (A4, A6, A7, A8) or the ComplianceFirst Portfolio (A2, A3, A12) in the second year, once the quickwins demonstrate ROI and governance maturity.  

### Tradeoffs

- **No direct impact on entitymismatch or external valuation delays** in Year1, but these are lowerfrequency and higherrisk activities that can be scheduled for Year2 once the process gains stability.  
- **Minimal upfront compliance oversight** compared with a compliancefirst set, but the chosen modules embed control checks (A9, A10) that already satisfy the “strengthen controls” mandate, avoiding additional governance overhead initially.  

### Why not start with the highestfrequency activity alone?

Automating *Register application* (A1) alone would save labor but would **not reduce the dominant waiting and rework** observed for *Request missing documents* and *Customer signing*. Processmining clearly shows that downstream bottlenecks (the “lowerfrequency but highimpact” activities) dominate endtoend performance. Ignoring these would leave the core synchronization delays untouched, limiting overall cycletime improvement. Therefore, the highestfrequency activity should be **automated, but as part of a balanced portfolio that also tackles the criticalpath delays**—exactly what the QuickWin Portfolio delivers.
