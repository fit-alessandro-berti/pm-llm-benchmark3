## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Temperature-excursion flag (dependence uncertainty, also observation uncertainty).** It is unknown because of a sensor gap. Confirmed, it moves P(smooth) from ~0.55–0.60 to ~0.25–0.30, raises P(lab investigation) from ~0.05 to ~0.13, and shifts the median by about one day.
2. **QA query and investigation path (path uncertainty).** Query, documentation-only investigation and laboratory investigation differ by hours, about a day, and 3–7 days. The laboratory investigation proportion sets P90 and the severe tail.
3. **Chemistry queue discipline and the 18:00 cliff (queue uncertainty combined with calendar uncertainty).** FIFO or priority moves the join by ~4.5 h (Mon 10:00 vs 14:30). A rerun near the end of the day can add a full day.
4. **QA backlog at the future join time (queue uncertainty).** Overdispersed arrivals and heavy-tailed service (median 1.5 h, P80 3.5 h) give a wait of <1 h to 8+ h. Whether review starts before or after 20:00 decides Monday versus Tuesday.
5. **Microbiology contamination and weekend readout authorization (path uncertainty plus calendar uncertainty).** Authorization barely helps the smooth path, because QA and chemistry are closed. It matters for contamination confirmation timing (~½ day). The 0.05–0.12 contamination probability itself drives rejection and Tuesday-or-later completion.
