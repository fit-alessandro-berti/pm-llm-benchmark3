## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

Common clock definitions used below:

- Chemistry usable result time: `T_C`.
- Microbiology usable result time: `T_M`.
- Parallel join time: `T_J = max(T_C, T_M)`.  
  This is a maximum, not a sum, because QA can start only when both branches have usable results.
- If `T_J` falls outside QA opening hours, QA waiting begins at the next QA opening: Friday 15:30–20:00, Saturday 09:00–13:00, or Monday 08:00.
- QA backlog approximation: initial 5 batches + expected arrivals during QA opening hours  expected completions. Effective QA service mean is taken as about 2.0–2.5 h, with median 1.5 h and P80-like service 3.5 h.

### Smooth / normal path

- **Chemistry waiting and service**
  - Friday 15:30–18:00 gives 5 analyst-hours of capacity. Current in-service residual work is 1.6–2.9 h, leaving 2.1–3.4 h of free analyst capacity before close.
  - Under FIFO, B-417 is unlikely to start before 18:00 because several ahead jobs remain. It is more likely to start Monday after remaining ahead jobs clear, giving `T_C` around Monday 13:00–17:00.
  - Under risk-priority, B-417 may start Friday 16:24–17:30 if the urgent job takes the first free slot and B-417 is placed before ordinary jobs. If service is short enough, it could finish Friday 17:30–18:00; more commonly it pauses at 18:00 and resumes Monday 08:00. This yields `T_C` around Monday 09:00–13:00.
  - Weighting FIFO and risk-priority, central smooth `T_C` is about Monday 11:00–15:00.

- **Microbiology incubation and readout**
  - Primary incubation is technically ready Sunday 12:00–20:00.
  - Without weekend authorization, readout starts Monday 08:00 and takes 0.5–1.5 h, so `T_M`  Monday 08:30–09:30.
  - With weekend authorization, `T_M` can be Sunday 13:00–23:00, but QA is closed Sunday. Therefore authorization saves only about 0.5–1.5 h in the smooth path unless chemistry is already complete Friday.

- **Parallel join time**
  - `T_J = max(T_C, T_M)`.
  - In the smooth central case, `T_C` Monday 11:00–15:00 is later than `T_M`, so chemistry dominates the join.
  - If chemistry finishes Friday and micro is authorized Sunday, `T_J` could be Sunday, but QA cannot start until Monday 08:00.

- **QA waiting and service**
  - By Monday 12:00, the QA backlog is roughly 2–4 batches ahead of B-417 under the fluid approximation.
  - With two reviewers from Monday 08:00, expected wait is about 1.5–4 h.
  - If `T_J` is Monday 11:00–15:00, QA start is roughly Monday 13:30–17:00.
  - QA service median 1.5 h gives completion Monday 15:00–18:30; P80-like service 3.5 h gives completion Monday 17:00–20:30. If service would finish after 20:00, it pauses and resumes Tuesday 08:00.

- **Optional rework/investigation**
  - Smooth path assumes no documentation query and no deviation investigation.

- **Final release or rejection**
  - Electronic Release takes 0.25–0.75 h after QA acceptance.
  - Central smooth terminal event: Electronic Release Monday 16:00–20:00, or Tuesday 08:00–12:00 if QA service tail crosses Monday close.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Chemistry queue discipline and whether B-417 starts before Friday 18:00**  
   - **Type:** mainly queue uncertainty, with service-time and calendar interaction.  
   - This determines whether chemistry result is Monday morning, Monday afternoon, or Tuesday after an overnight pause. It is the dominant near-term clock in the smooth path because micro primary result is usually available by Monday morning.

2. **QA queue and service tail at the future join time**  
   - **Type:** queue uncertainty plus service-time uncertainty.  
   - The current QA queue is known, but future arrivals, overdispersion, and reviewer capacity are uncertain. A Monday join can still produce a Tuesday release if QA service tail crosses the 20:00 close.

3. **Microbiology contamination confirmation and staffing calendar**  
   - **Type:** path uncertainty plus calendar uncertainty.  
   - Primary incubation is not highly binding in the smooth path, but contamination confirmation adds 20–30 h continuous incubation plus staffing-dependent readout. This can move the join from Monday to Tuesday or later.

4. **Temperature-excursion flag dependence**  
   - **Type:** dependence uncertainty, with observation uncertainty due to the sensor gap.  
   - A confirmed excursion increases both chemistry rerun and micro contamination probabilities and raises QA investigation probability. The tails are therefore correlated, not independent.

5. **QA investigation type and probability**  
   - **Type:** path uncertainty, with calendar uncertainty for lab investigations.  
   - Doc-only investigation adds 8–20 working hours, usually a 1–2 day delay. Lab investigation adds 3–7 calendar days and is the main driver of the severe tail.
