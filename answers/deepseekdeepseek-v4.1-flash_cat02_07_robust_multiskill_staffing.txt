            rem = 100 - p1
            p2_base = row.P2_pct
            p3_base = row.P3_pct

# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Assumptions
- The supplied tables are the only source of demand, service, resource, and scenario data.
- Service ranges are interpreted as uniform draws within the ordinary or complex-tail range, with the given probability of drawing from the complex-tail range. Means used in capacity arithmetic are therefore mixture means:
  - Intake: `0.92*6.5 + 0.08*12 = 6.94` min.
  - Mechanical: `0.85*23 + 0.15*45 = 26.30` min.
  - Electrical: `0.82*28.5 + 0.18*52.5 = 32.82` min.
  - Software: `0.80*32.5 + 0.20*62.5 = 38.50` min.
  - Validation: `0.88*14 + 0.12*28.5 = 15.74` min.
- Resource multipliers are slowness multipliers: `1.25` means actual service time is `1.25 x` the base draw. A dash means the resource is not qualified for that skill.
- ME/ES branch choice:
  - Parallel if policy permits and coordination overhead is justified by critical-path reduction.
  - Sequential otherwise, preferably on a cross-skilled resource to avoid a mechanical/electrical/software team handover.
  - Parallel ME/ES adds the supplied 8-minute coordination delay before Validation.
- Preemption:
  - Only P1 can preempt lower-priority diagnostic work.
  - A case may be preempted at most once.
  - Restart penalty is `10%` of remaining active service time at the moment of interruption.
  - Preemption is not used for Intake or Validation in the policies below unless explicitly stated.
- Handover/setup:
  - Resource change for Intake, diagnostic, or Validation: +7 min.
  - Mechanical team to electrical/software team, or vice versa: +5 min unless the same cross-skilled resource keeps the case.
  - Diagnostic skill-family switch by a resource between consecutive cases: +5 min setup.

  - I renormalize the type mix in 12:00-16:00 after doubling the E and ES weights, keeping the block arrival count unchanged.
  - If actual E/ES counts double with total arrivals increasing, the electrical bottleneck in U2 would be materially worse than shown.
- U4 interpretation:
  - “Diagnostic activities” means Mechanical, Electrical, and Software. Intake and Validation tail probabilities are unchanged.
- Overtime is modeled as up to 30 minutes per resource per day.

## Objectives
Two objective formulations are used.

**Objective O1 — SLA/tail-risk weighted objective**
Maximize:
`0.50 * P1_SLA + 0.20 * P2_SLA + 0.05 * P3_SLA - 0.10 * p95_delay_penalty - 0.05 * handover_setup_burden`

where `p95_delay_penalty` is normalized against a 480-minute scale. This objective protects P1, then P2, but still penalizes long tails and excessive coordination.

**Objective O2 — fairness/robustness objective**
Minimize worst-case regret across base, U1-U5:
`max_scenario( metric_scenario(policy) - metric_scenario(best_policy) )`
with a fairness constraint:
`max_resource_utilization <= 0.90` in base unless unavoidable, and `utilization_fairness >= 0.60`.
This objective prefers policies that do not collapse under absence, surge, or long-tail shocks, even if base-case mean delay is slightly worse.

## Decision Variables
- Eligibility: which qualified resources can receive each activity.
- Priority score: ordering rule for P1/P2/P3 and within-priority dispatch.
- Reservations: how much capacity is held for P1 and when reserved capacity can overflow.
- Preemption: when P1 may interrupt lower-priority diagnostic work.
- Parallelization: when ME/ES branches run in parallel versus sequentially.
- Handover minimization: same-resource preference and cross-skill overflow rules.
- Overflow triggers: queue-age, utilization, and SLA-risk thresholds that permit less-preferred resources to take work.
- Overtime allocation: where extra 30-minute capacity is spent.
- Fallback mode: all-P1 triage protocol under severe shock.

- `handover/setup`: approximate added minutes per case.
- `resilience`: Low / Med / High, judged across U1-U5.

# 5. Robust recommendation

**Primary recommendation: Policy D, robust hybrid with dynamic P1 reservation, controlled cross-skill overflow, and fairness-adjusted dispatch.**

Why it is preferred:
- It protects P1 better than full pooling because some capacity is reserved for P1, especially during morning surge and absence scenarios.
- It uses cross-skill overflow to avoid the strict-specialization failure mode where electrical/software queues grow while mechanical or validation resources idle.
- It explicitly penalizes handovers and diagnostic-family setups, reducing the overhead that full pooling can create.
- It balances utilization so that max resource utilization is less likely to exceed 90% in base and moderate scenarios.
- It has a clear fallback mode, which is essential for U5 because no policy can fully absorb U1+U3+Intake outage without triage or extra capacity.

**Fallback mode triggers**
- P1 queue >2 cases, or
- any P1 wait >30 minutes, or
- projected P1 SLA <85%, or
- max resource utilization >105% for 30 consecutive minutes, or
- U5 scenario flag active and Chen absent.

Fallback actions:
- Suspend new P3 diagnostics.
- Allow P1 to use any qualified resource.
- Parallelize all P1 ME/ES branches.
- Use up to 30 minutes overtime per resource.
- If P1 SLA is still projected below 85%, escalate for temporary external capacity or defer P3 work beyond 24 hours.

**When another policy is preferable**
- If P3 fairness is the dominant objective and there is spare capacity, full pooling may be simpler and more transparent.
- If strict auditability and skill-family separation are legally required, strict specialization may be acceptable only with lower demand or extra electrical/software staff.
- If P1 volume is very low and P3 is tolerant of long waits, hybrid static reservation may be enough.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates:** Operators may inflate remaining work to avoid preemption or to attract help. Use independent timestamped pause/resume events and compare planned versus actual service.
- **P3 starvation:** Under overload, P3 work will be deferred. This is intentional for P1 protection but must be monitored by age and SLA attainment.
- **Skill erosion:** Strict specialization preserves skill depth but creates bottlenecks. Full pooling builds cross-skill capability but can blur expertise if used constantly without training.
- **Excessive preemption:** Each preemption adds at least 10% of remaining active service as restart penalty. Frequent preemption can create a churn loop where P1 cases interrupt many P2/P3 cases without finishing them.
- **Coordination overhead:** Parallel ME/ES saves critical path but adds 8 minutes before Validation and increases handover risk. It should be reserved for long branches, P1 cases, or ME/ES cases with high predicted critical path.
- **Validation bottleneck:** Because all cases need Validation, pooling diagnostic resources may simply move the queue to Validation. Validation must be included in dispatch and reservation logic.
- **Fairness:** Fast resources such as Ben, Chen, Deepa, and Emil can be overused. Use utilization caps and rotation.
- **Human interpretability:** Policy D has more parameters than Policy B. The dispatch rule should be shown in a simple score, and operators should be able to see why a case was assigned or preempted.

block_start = {
    '08:00-10:00': 0,
    '10:00-12:00': 120,
    '12:00-14:00': 240,

scen_cfg = {
    'base': {},
    'U1': {'morning_mult': 1.7, 'morning_p1_add': 8},

def parse_hm(s):
    h, m = map(int, s.split(':'))
    return h * 60 + m - 8 * 60

shift_intervals = {}
for _, r in resource_df.iterrows():
    start = parse_hm(r['shift'].split('-')[0])
    end = parse_hm(r['shift'].split('-')[1])
    intervals = [(start, end + 30)]  # 30-min overtime allowance
    if pd.notna(r['break']):
        bs = parse_hm(r['break'].split('-')[0])
        be = parse_hm(r['break'].split('-')[1])
        new_intervals = []
        for a, b in intervals:
            if be <= a or bs >= b:
                new_intervals.append((a, b))
            else:
                if a < bs:
                    new_intervals.append((a, min(bs, b)))
                if b > be:
                    new_intervals.append((max(be, a), b))
        intervals = new_intervals
    shift_intervals[r['resource']] = intervals

def resource_available(res, t, scen):
    if scen.get('chen_absent') and res == 'Chen':
        return False
    for a, b in shift_intervals[res]:
        if a <= t < b:
            return True
    return False

# ----------------------------
# Skill eligibility and multipliers

skill_cols = ['Intake', 'Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software', 'Validation']

elig = {
    skill: [r for r in resource_df.resource if get_multiplier(r, skill) is not None]

strict_elig = {

def draw_base(activity, rng, scen):
    row = activity_df[activity_df.activity == activity].iloc[0]
    p = row.tail_prob
    if scen.get('tail_double') and activity in ['Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software']:
        p *= 2.0
    p = min(p, 1.0)
    if rng.random() < p:
        return rng.uniform(row.cplx_lo, row.cplx_hi)
    return rng.uniform(row.ord_lo, row.ord_hi)

# ----------------------------
# Discrete-event/minute simulation
# ----------------------------
def simulate(policy, scen_name, seed, max_min=1440):
    scen = scen_cfg[scen_name]
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)

    cases = []
    cid = 0
    for _, row in demand_df.iterrows():
        block = row['block']
        start = block_start[block]
        lam = row.expected_arrivals
        if scen.get('morning_mult') and block in ['08:00-10:00', '10:00-12:00']:
            lam *= scen['morning_mult']
        n = rng.poisson(lam)

        for _ in range(n):
            arrival = start + rng.uniform(0, 120)

            p1 = row.P1_pct
            if scen.get('morning_p1_add') and block in ['08:00-10:00', '10:00-12:00']:
                p1 = min(100, p1 + scen['morning_p1_add'])
            rem = 100 - p1
            p2_base = row.P2_pct
            p3_base = row.P3_pct
            base_non_p1 = p2_base + p3_base
            p2 = rem * (p2_base / base_non_p1) if base_non_p1 > 0 else rem / 2
            p3 = rem - p2

            r = rng.random() * 100
            if r < p1:
                pri = 'P1'
            elif r < p1 + p2:
                pri = 'P2'
            else:
                pri = 'P3'

            weights = np.array([
                row.M_pct, row.E_pct, row.S_pct, row.ME_pct, row.ES_pct
            ], dtype=float)

            if scen_name == 'U2' and block in scen.get('u2_blocks', []):
                weights[1] *= 2.0  # E
                weights[4] *= 2.0  # ES

            weights = weights / weights.sum() * 100.0
            r = rng.random() * 100.0
            cum = np.cumsum(weights)
            types = ['M', 'E', 'S', 'ME', 'ES']
            idx = int(np.searchsorted(cum, r))
            ctype = types[min(idx, 4)]

            cases.append({
                'idx': len(cases),
                'id': f'case_{cid}',
                'arrival': arrival,
                'priority': pri,
                'type': ctype,
                'stage': 'intake',
                'pending_diag': [],
                'active_diag': 0,
                'parallel': False,
                'last_resource': None,
                'last_team': None,
                'completion': None,
            })
            cid += 1

    cases.sort(key=lambda c: c['arrival'])
    for i, c in enumerate(cases):
        c['idx'] = i

    queue = []
    res_state = {
        r: {
            'busy': False,
            'activity': None,
            'remaining': 0,
            'last_skill': None,
            'last_case': None,
            'busy_minutes': 0,
        }
        for r in resource_df.resource
    }

    log = []
    total_extra = 0.0
    base_dt = datetime(2024, 1, 1, 8, 0)

    def create_activity(c, skill, ready):
        return {
            'case_idx': c['idx'],
            'case_id': c['id'],
            'activity': skill,
            'skill': skill,
            'priority': c['priority'],
            'ready_time': ready,
            'base_duration': draw_base(skill, rng, scen),
            'assigned': False,
            'preempted': 0,
            'start_time': None,
            'resource': None,
            'remaining': 0,
            'extra': 0,
        }

    arr_ptr = 0
    team_map = {
        'Mechanical': 'mech',
        'Electrical': 'elec',
        'Software': 'soft',
        'Intake': 'intake',
        'Validation': 'validation',
    }

    for t in range(max_min + 1):
        # arrivals
        while arr_ptr < len(cases) and int(cases[arr_ptr]['arrival']) <= t:
            c = cases[arr_ptr]
            if c['stage'] == 'intake':
                queue.append(create_activity(c, 'Intake', c['arrival']))
                c['stage'] = 'intake_started'
            arr_ptr += 1

        # completions
        for r, st in list(res_state.items()):
            if st['busy'] and st['remaining'] <= 0:
                act = st['activity']
                c = cases[act['case_idx']]
                end_t = t

                log.append({
                    'case:concept:name': c['id'],
                    'concept:name': act['activity'],
                    'start_timestamp': base_dt + timedelta(minutes=act['start_time']),
                    'time:timestamp': base_dt + timedelta(minutes=end_t),
                    'org:resource': r,
                    'priority': c['priority'],
                    'skill': act['skill'],
                    'policy': policy,
                    'scenario': scen_name,
                    'extra_minutes': act['extra'],
                    'ready_timestamp': base_dt + timedelta(minutes=act['ready_time']),
                })

                total_extra += act['extra']
                st['last_skill'] = act['skill']
                st['last_case'] = c['id']
                st['busy'] = False
                st['activity'] = None
                st['remaining'] = 0

                if act['activity'] == 'Intake':
                    if c['type'] == 'M':
                        c['pending_diag'] = []
                        c['active_diag'] = 1
                        c['parallel'] = False
                        queue.append(create_activity(c, 'Mechanical', end_t))
                    elif c['type'] == 'E':
                        c['pending_diag'] = []
                        c['active_diag'] = 1
                        c['parallel'] = False
                        queue.append(create_activity(c, 'Electrical', end_t))
                    elif c['type'] == 'S':
                        c['pending_diag'] = []
                        c['active_diag'] = 1
                        c['parallel'] = False
                        queue.append(create_activity(c, 'Software', end_t))
                    else:
                        branches = ['Mechanical', 'Electrical'] if c['type'] == 'ME' else ['Electrical', 'Software']
                        parallel = policy in ['pool', 'hybrid', 'robust']
                        if parallel:
                            c['pending_diag'] = []
                            c['active_diag'] = 2
                            c['parallel'] = True
                            for sk in branches:
                                queue.append(create_activity(c, sk, end_t))
                        else:
                            c['pending_diag'] = branches[1:]
                            c['active_diag'] = 1
                            c['parallel'] = False
                            queue.append(create_activity(c, branches[0], end_t))

                elif act['activity'] in ['Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software']:
                    c['active_diag'] -= 1
                    if c['active_diag'] == 0:
                        if c['pending_diag']:
                            next_skill = c['pending_diag'].pop(0)
                            c['active_diag'] = 1
                            queue.append(create_activity(c, next_skill, end_t))
                        else:
                            delay = 8 if c['parallel'] else 0
                            queue.append(create_activity(c, 'Validation', end_t + delay))

                elif act['activity'] == 'Validation':
                    c['completion'] = end_t
                    c['stage'] = 'done'

        # preemption
        if policy in ['pool', 'hybrid', 'robust']:
            for r, st in res_state.items():
                if st['busy']:
                    act = st['activity']
                    if (act and act['activity'] in ['Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software']
                            and act['priority'] != 'P1'
                            and act['preempted'] < 1):
                        p1_waiting = [
                            a for a in queue
                            if a['priority'] == 'P1'
                            and not a['assigned']
                            and get_multiplier(r, a['skill']) is not None
                        ]
                        if p1_waiting and st['remaining'] > 5:
                            rem = st['remaining']
                            penalty = 0.10 * rem
                            act['preempted'] += 1
                            act['remaining'] = rem + penalty
                            act['assigned'] = False
                            act['ready_time'] = t
                            queue.append(act)
                            st['busy'] = False
                            st['activity'] = None
                            st['remaining'] = 0

        # assignment
        idle_res = [
            r for r, st in res_state.items()
            if not st['busy'] and resource_available(r, t, scen)
        ]

        for r in idle_res:
            def allowed(a):
                if a['assigned']:
                    return False
                if a['ready_time'] > t:
                    return False
                if scen.get('intake_outage') and a['skill'] == 'Intake':
                    lo, hi = scen['intake_outage']
                    if lo <= t < hi:
                        return False
                if policy == 'strict':
                    if r not in strict_elig.get(a['skill'], []):
                        return False
                else:
                    if get_multiplier(r, a['skill']) is None:
                        return False
                if policy in ['hybrid', 'robust'] and a['priority'] != 'P1':
                    if any(q['priority'] == 'P1' and not q['assigned'] for q in queue) and r in reserved.get(a['skill'], []):
                        return False
                return True

            elig_acts = [a for a in queue if allowed(a)]
            if not elig_acts:
                continue

            if policy == 'robust':
                avail = sum(1 for tt in range(max_min + 1) if resource_available(r, tt, scen))
                util = res_state[r]['busy_minutes'] / max(1, avail)
                if util > 0.9 and not any(a['priority'] == 'P1' for a in elig_acts):
                    other_can = any(
                        rr != r
                        and not res_state[rr]['busy']
                        and resource_available(rr, t, scen)
                        and get_multiplier(rr, a['skill']) is not None
                        for a in elig_acts
                        for rr in resource_df.resource
                    )
                    if other_can:
                        continue

            elig_acts.sort(key=lambda a: ({'P1': 0, 'P2': 1, 'P3': 2}[a['priority']], a['ready_time']))
            a = elig_acts[0]
            c = cases[a['case_idx']]

            if a.get('preempted', 0) > 0 and a.get('remaining', 0) > 0:
                service = a['remaining']
                extra = 0
            else:
                extra = 0
                if c['last_resource'] and c['last_resource'] != r:
                    extra += 7
                if c['last_team'] and team_map.get(a['skill']) != c['last_team']:
                    if ((team_map.get(a['skill']) == 'mech' and c['last_team'] in ['elec', 'soft'])
                            or (team_map.get(a['skill']) in ['elec', 'soft'] and c['last_team'] == 'mech')):
                        extra += 5
                if res_state[r]['last_skill'] in ['Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software'] and a['skill'] in ['Mechanical', 'Electrical', 'Software']:
                    fam = {'Mechanical': 'mech', 'Electrical': 'elec', 'Software': 'soft'}
                    if fam[a['skill']] != fam[res_state[r]['last_skill']]:
                        extra += 5

                mult = get_multiplier(r, a['skill'])
                service = a['base_duration'] * mult

            a['extra'] = extra
            a['start_time'] = t
            a['resource'] = r
            a['remaining'] = service + extra
            a['assigned'] = True
            queue.remove(a)

            st = res_state[r]
            st['busy'] = True
            st['activity'] = a
            st['remaining'] = a['remaining']

            c['last_resource'] = r
            c['last_team'] = team_map.get(a['skill'])

        # decrement busy resources
        for r, st in res_state.items():
            if st['busy'] and resource_available(r, t, scen):
                st['remaining'] -= 1
                st['busy_minutes'] += 1

    rows = []
    for c in cases:
        comp = c['completion'] if c['completion'] is not None else max_min
        rows.append({
            'case_id': c['id'],
            'priority': c['priority'],
            'type': c['type'],
            'arrival': c['arrival'],
            'completion': comp,
            'delay': comp - c['arrival'],
        })
    case_df = pd.DataFrame(rows)

    def sla_frac(pri, target):
        sub = case_df[case_df.priority == pri]
        if len(sub) == 0:
            return np.nan
        return float((sub.delay <= target).mean())

    utils = {}
    for r in resource_df.resource:
        avail = sum(1 for tt in range(max_min + 1) if resource_available(r, tt, scen))
        utils[r] = res_state[r]['busy_minutes'] / max(1, avail)

    max_util = max(utils.values())
    min_util = min(utils.values())
    fairness = 1.0 - (max_util - min_util)

    metrics = {
        'policy': policy,
        'scenario': scen_name,
        'seed': seed,
        'mean_delay': case_df.delay.mean(),
        'p90_delay': case_df.delay.quantile(0.90),
        'p95_delay': case_df.delay.quantile(0.95),
        'P1_SLA': sla_frac('P1', 120),
        'P2_SLA': sla_frac('P2', 480),
        'P3_SLA': sla_frac('P3', 1440),
        'max_resource_utilization': max_util,
        'utilization_fairness': fairness,
        'handover_setup_per_case': total_extra / max(1, len(cases)),
        'n_cases': len(cases),
    }

    return metrics, pd.DataFrame(log), case_df

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## Limits
- Expected arrivals and uniform service ranges do not reveal true arrival burstiness, day-of-week effects, or correlations between case type and priority.
- The capacity analysis is approximate and does not fully solve the stochastic queueing problem. It identifies likely bottlenecks but cannot prove exact SLA attainment.
- The code scaffold simplifies some mechanisms: minute-level simulation, simplified preemption, and a fixed overtime allowance. It does not model every possible break-interruption behavior, shift handover, or multi-day backlog dynamics.
- U2 is especially sensitive to interpretation. If E/ES counts truly double and total arrivals rise, the electrical bottleneck will be worse than the renormalized-mix table suggests.
- The robustness ranking depends on the chosen objective. A P3-fairness objective may prefer full pooling even if P1 SLA is slightly lower.

## Validation plan
1. **Backtest on historical logs**
   - Replay past incidents with known arrival times, case types, priorities, resources, pauses, and completion times.
   - Compare simulated policy decisions against observed outcomes.
   - Compute PM4Py service/waiting times and handover networks from the historical log, then compare with simulation.

2. **Pilot with shadow mode**
   - Run Policy D and Policy B in shadow mode without changing real dispatch.
   - For each dispatch event, record the policy recommendation, eligible resource set, score, and reason.
   - Measure agreement, projected P1 SLA, and handover/setup burden.

3. **Controlled rollout**
   - Start with one team or one shift, protecting P1 first.
   - Use A/B rotation by day or block where operationally safe.
   - Never put P1 cases at risk to test a policy. Use fallback mode if P1 SLA drops below 85%.

4. **Stress tests**
   - Simulate U1-U5 with repeated seeds and compare p90/p95 delay, P1 SLA, max utilization, and fairness.
   - Include a worst-case U5 test with an Intake outage and Chen absent.
   - Add a manual “extra capacity” scenario to determine how much capacity is needed to keep P1 SLA above 90% in U5.

5. **Guardrails**
   - Preemption count per case must remain at most 1.
   - P3 starvation must be monitored by age and queue length.
   - Max resource utilization above 90% for two consecutive blocks triggers rebalancing.
   - Any policy version change must include a new `policy_version` flag in the event log.
