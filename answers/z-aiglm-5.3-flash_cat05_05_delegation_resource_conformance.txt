## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation creation:** central registry with unique IDs; cryptographically signed instrument hashed into the QMS **before** the effective start (minimum lead time, e.g., 24 h); explicit scope lists (activity × change category × risk); explicit "no subdelegation" enforcement at workflow level; delegate acknowledgment countersignature.
2. **Effective-time recording:** append-only, WORM audit store; distinguish `instrument_signed_at`, `registered_at`, `valid_from/to`; migrations carry provenance flags and are never the sole proof source; NTP-synced UTC everywhere.
3. **Principal availability:** live availability calendar consumed by the approval engine; at click-time the system evaluates the absence condition automatically; bounded reaction-window SLA for interruptibility; break-glass path (like CHG504) forces a same-day leave-variance record and dual notification.
4. **Alias management:** single golden identity per human; legacy aliases become authentication-only and are invisible to authorization decisions; automated quarterly reconciliation + alert on any new alias appearing in logs (CHG508 type traps vanish).
5. **Individual authentication:** ban shared/kiosk sessions for GxP-significant actions; per-action step-up authentication (person-bound FIDO2); force re-auth at commit; auto-logoff and screen-lock; associate badge presence with the acting session.
6. **Emergency review:** auto-generate the Retrospective_QM_Review task with a **business-calendar-aware** due date; escalation to a designated alternate-QM pool instead of letting scope-mismatched engineers fill gaps; 80% SLA timer alerts.
7. **Pair rotation:** minimum-distance quotas (no dyad above X% of a risk-class's approvals per quarter); mandatory occasional routing to the QM; periodic blind sampling of pair-approved changes.
8. **Network monitoring:** monthly baselines of handover/working-together graphs; alerts on *new* edges, concentration spikes, and bypass motifs (e.g., DraftClose same resource, ApproveVerify reciprocity); always human-reviewed.
9. **Evidential retention:** hash-chained session logs capturing method/MFA/badge/IP per action; retain delegation artifacts with their full validity story; CCTV/e-discovery retention aligned to GxP investigation horizons.
