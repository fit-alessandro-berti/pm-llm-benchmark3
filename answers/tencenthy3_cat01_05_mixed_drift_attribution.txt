## 9. Provisional attribution

Provisional attribution of the observed drift is partly confident and partly speculative. The strongest conclusion is that the March conformance collapse (raw fitness 0.920.55) is primarily logging and semantic drift from the system migration: label renaming, duplicated `Open Case`, and missing resources. Mapped fitness stays 0.86, confirming the control flow was largely preserved. This attribution is high confidence (95%).

The second high-confidence attribution is the May–September rework and cycle-time surge to Supplier Z’s disruption. Supplier Z share jumps to >40%, rework variant doubles, and `AssessRequest Info` rises to 24–29 per 100 even after label normalization. Because Z serves more complex regions, case-mix mediates part of the effect, but the abrupt onset on 5 May aligns with the API/staffing event.

Staffing cuts (15 June) and the July priority rule together explain most remaining queueing deterioration. The modest rise in Assess service time (4.85.4h) rules out service as main driver; therefore the extra 6–8h median cycle is queue waiting. Priority redistribution is plausible but only moderate confidence (60%) because monthly aggregates hide class-specific waits.

October–November high-complexity and Senior Assessment spikes are clearly seasonal renewal (confidence 90%). Some persistent rework in December suggests a behavioral adaptation or backlog, plausible but not proven.

Interaction among supplier, staffing, and priority prevents clean additive attribution for mid-year cycle time. I would revise the account if stratified logs show rework concentrated outside Supplier Z (weakening supplier hypothesis) or if standard-case waits do not increase after July (weakening priority effect).

## 10. Monitoring decision rules

1. IF raw_model_fitness <0.60 but mapped_model_fitness >0.85 in a month, THEN trigger log-schema review and label-mapping validation, BECAUSE the primary cause is logging/semantic drift rather than control-flow change.
2. IF rework_variant_pct >20% and supplier_z_pct >35%, THEN audit Supplier Z bundle completeness and initiate supplier remediation, BECAUSE supplier disruption drives rework.
3. IF median queue waiting before `Assess` increases >50% over pre-June baseline while `Assess` service time is unchanged, THEN review staffing capacity and activate overtime, BECAUSE staffing loss causes queueing performance drift.
4. IF high_complexity_pct >40% and Senior Assessment rate per 100 cases >20 during Oct–Nov, THEN confirm seasonal renewal case mix and adjust capacity plan, BECAUSE seasonal population drift is expected and not a defect.
5. IF after 2026-07 standard-case waiting exceeds high-complexity waiting by >20%, THEN evaluate priority-rule side effects, BECAUSE priority-induced redistribution may harm standard SLA.
6. IF missing_resource_pct >5% concentrated in migrated activities, THEN enforce resource logging at ingestion and backfill, BECAUSE measurement missingness biases performance attribution.
7. IF mapped_model_fitness drops >5pp from baseline AND rework rate rises concurrently in the non-supplier, non-seasonal segment, THEN launch behavioral-drift investigation on assessor variants, BECAUSE genuine control-flow adaptation is more likely than logging or case-mix alone.
