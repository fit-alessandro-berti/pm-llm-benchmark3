**Key Links:**
- **Nominal handover reduction**: Local specialist handovers drop from 36/100 to 0 (replaced by 41/100 to shared service). Cross-team handovers drop from 6/100 to 3/100.
- **Central queue formation**: Technical Shared Service (41/100 cases) + Legal/Risk (12/100) + Approval (49/100) create 3 major queues, with median waits of 4.0h, 10.5h, and 3.0h respectively.
- **Specialist utilization**: East’s E_Tech (82%  ?) may improve, while Legal/Risk (93%/91%) risks overload. Local specialists (68–71%) may drop to ~50%.
- **Rework**: Local expertise loss (Q) could offset reduced handover rework (L). Legal/Risk rework (14%) may persist or worsen.
- **Local expertise loss**: East’s high utilization suggests limited redundancy; North/South may lose tacit knowledge.
- **Role clarity**: Shared approval (F) replaces 1 supervisor but creates ambiguity (P). Local caseworkers lose direct specialist access.
- **Resilience**: Cross-team handovers (S) increase wait time from 8.9h to 6.0h, worsening absence coverage.
- **Fairness**: Standardized approvals (O) may reduce bias but could disadvantage regional/customer-specific cases.

# 4. Risks Invisible in Average Cycle Time

1. **Tail Latency**:
   - Average cycle time may hide **90th percentile delays** (e.g., 25.0d vs. 8.0d median).
   - *Process-Mining Check*: `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg()` to visualize tail delays.

2. **High-Complexity Cases**:
   - Complex cases may see **30% longer waits** in shared queues, but averages mask this.
   - *Process-Mining Check*: Filter by `case_complexity` and analyze `pm4py.get_service_time()`.

3. **Local Knowledge Loss**:
   - Reduced local expertise may increase **informal advice gaps**, raising rework later.
   - *Process-Mining Check*: Track `local_expertise_flag` usage pre/post.

4. **Fairness Across Teams**:
   - East may benefit, but North/South could see **slower local cases** due to lost expertise.
   - *Process-Mining Check*: Compare cycle times by `team` and `customer_segment`.

5. **Learning Decay**:
   - Local caseworkers may **stop developing technical skills**, leading to future rework.
   - *Process-Mining Check*: Monitor `rework_flag` by `org:resource` over time.

6. **Accountability Gaps**:
   - Shared approvals may reduce **ownership clarity**, increasing rework in ambiguous cases.
   - *Process-Mining Check*: Analyze `reassignment_count` and `handover_reason` for "approval" cases.

7. **Exception Handling**:
   - Rare but critical cases (e.g., legal emergencies) may face **longer waits** in shared queues.
   - *Process-Mining Check*: Filter by `priority` and `required_specialty`.

8. **Resilience to Absence**:
   - Cross-team handovers may **increase wait times** during staff shortages.
   - *Process-Mining Check*: Correlate `queue length` with `absence periods` (via `time:timestamp`).

# 5. Pilot Design

**Pilot Scope**:
- **Teams**: North and South (East excluded due to high baseline utilization).
- **Cases**: 20% of high-complexity cases (`case_complexity > 5`) and 10% of legal/risk cases.
- **Duration**: 8 weeks (4 weeks pre, 4 weeks post).
- **Comparison Group**: East team (no changes) + historical North/South data.

**Pre/Post Observation Window**:
- **Pre**: 4 weeks of baseline data (event logs + surveys).
- **Post**: 4 weeks of pilot data, with weekly snapshots.

**Segmentation**:
- By **team** (North vs. South vs. East).
- By **complexity** (`case_complexity` quartiles).
- By **specialty** (`required_specialty`).
- By **customer segment** (e.g., regional vs. national).

**Guardrail Metrics**:
| Metric                          | Threshold (Trigger Rollback) |
|---------------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Technical Shared Service queue length | >15 cases for >3h            |
| Legal/Risk queue wait time      | >12h median                 |
| Rework rate (North/South)      | >9%                         |
| Cross-team handover wait time   | >7h                         |
| Customer satisfaction drop      | >0.5 points (vs. baseline)  |
| Specialist utilization         | >95% for >2 weeks           |

**Staff Feedback Collection**:
- **Weekly**: Short surveys on role clarity, queue frustration, and local expertise access.
- **Biweekly**: Focus groups with local caseworkers and shared-service staff.
- **End of Pilot**: Structured interviews on accountability and learning.

**Rollback/Adjustment Triggers**:
1. **Immediate Rollback**:
   - Rework rate >10% in North/South.
   - Queue lengths >20 cases for >4h in any shared service.
2. **Partial Adjustment**:
   - Customer satisfaction drop >0.3 points.
   - Specialist utilization >90% for >1 week.
3. **Full Proceed**:
   - All guardrails met for 2+ weeks.
   - No significant fairness gaps detected.

# 6. Recommendation

**Conditional Proceed with Phased Rollout**:
Proceed with the restructuring **only if**:
1. **Pilot results** show:
   - No rework increase >5% in North/South.
   - Shared service queue waits <6h median for Technical, <12h for Legal/Risk.
   - East’s cycle time improves without North/South degradation.
2. **Monitoring** confirms:
   - Tail delays (90th percentile) do not exceed +15% vs. baseline.
   - Fairness metrics (cycle time by segment) remain stable.
   - Local expertise loss is mitigated via training or hybrid models.

**Management Must Not Infer**:
- **Early averages** (e.g., median cycle time) imply success without checking tails or fairness.
- **Reduced handovers** mean improved quality (rework may rise in shared queues).
- **Specialist utilization** >90% is sustainable (bottlenecks will emerge).
- **Local teams** will adapt quickly (skill decay is a lagging risk).

**Alternative**: If pilot fails, **modify** by:
- Keeping 1 local specialist per team for high-complexity cases.
- Introducing a **hybrid model** where shared service handles only >80% complexity cases.
- Adding **local triage** to reduce shared queue overload.
