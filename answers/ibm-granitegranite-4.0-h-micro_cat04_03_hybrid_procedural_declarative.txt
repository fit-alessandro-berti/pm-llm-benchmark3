# 1. Policy Interpretation and Conflict Resolution

## Assumptions
1. R5 (Legal opinion must occur after Sanctions Escalation) and R6 (Legal opinion may precede activation under emergency conditions) represent conflicting but necessary policies.
2. The emergency activation (R6) is a special case that should be allowed but should be flagged for compliance review.
3. The chosen resolution prioritizes R5, as legal compliance is a critical regulatory requirement.

## Resolutions of R5/R6 Conflict
### Resolution 1: Prioritize R5
- **Model Choice**: Enforce R5 strictly; R6 is allowed only as a conservative approximation, flagged as a warning.
- **Compliance Risk**: Reduces the risk of regulatory non-compliance but may lead to operational inefficiencies during emergencies.

### Resolution 2: Allow R6 with Flagged Exception
- **Model Choice**: Permit R6 but require an explicit flag in the DECLARE model indicating an exception.
- **Compliance Risk**: Introduces a control mechanism to monitor and audit emergency activations, ensuring accountability.

### Chosen Resolution
**Prioritize R5** – This choice ensures adherence to legal requirements, which are non-negotiable in vendor onboarding processes. The risk is mitigated by incorporating an explicit flag mechanism for emergency activations, ensuring traceability and auditability.

# 3. Procedural Backbone

## Compact Formal Description (ProcessTree)
The onboarding process is modeled as a directed acyclic graph (DAG) with the following structure:

1. **Start**: `Receive Onboarding Request`
2. **Concurrent Paths**:
   - `Capture Master Data`
   - `Run Sanctions Screening`
   - `Verify Bank Account`
3. **Risk Classification**:
   - `Classify Risk` follows successful completion of all three checks.
4. **Outcome Paths**:
   - **Reject Vendor**: `Reject Vendor`  `Close Onboarding`
   - **Low-Risk Route**: `Manager Approval`  `Activate Vendor`  `Release First Payment`  `Close Onboarding`
   - **High-Risk Route**: `Enhanced Due Diligence`  `Compliance Approval`  `Activate Vendor`  `Release First Payment`  `Close Onboarding`
5. **Special Cases**:
   - **Sanctions Escalation**: Leads to `Obtain Legal Opinion` (R4) and conditional `Activate Vendor` (R5).
   - **Emergency Activation**: May occur pre-legal opinion but flagged as an exception (R6).


## Concurrency and Clarification Loops
- **Concurrency**: `Capture Master Data`, `Run Sanctions Screening`, and `Verify Bank Account` are modeled as concurrent activities, reflecting parallel processing capabilities.
- **Clarification Loop**: The `Request Clarification` activity is modeled as a loop with variable delay, reflecting real-world communication delays.

## Choices and Terminal Outcomes
- The model explicitly models the two primary outcomes (`Reject Vendor` or `Activate Vendor`) and their respective approval processes.
- The emergency activation is modeled as a special path that overrides the standard legal opinion requirement (R6).

## Post-Activation Behavior
- The model includes post-activation behavior such as `Release First Payment` and `Close Onboarding` only after the appropriate approvals or activations.

# 7. Resource- and Data-Aware Checks

## Python Code for Resource/Data Checks
```python
import pandas as pd
from pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle import filter_four_eyes_principle

def verify_resource_duties(log):
    # Check segregation of duties
    resource_activities = log.groupby(['Resource']).apply(lambda x: list(x['Activity'].unique()))
    for activity1, activity2 in resource_activities:
        if activity1 == "Verify Bank Account" and activity2 == "Activate Vendor":
            raise ValueError("Violation of R9: Verify Bank Account and Activate Vendor performed by the same resource")
        if activity1 in ["Manager Approval", "Compliance Approval"] and activity2 in ["Activate Vendor", "Reject Vendor"]:
            raise ValueError("Violation of R8: Manager Approval and Compliance Approval co-occur")
    
def check_emergency_policy(log):
    # Check emergency activation conditions
    emergency_cases = log[log['Resource'] == 'Emergency Team']
    for case in emergency_cases:
        if 'Obtain Legal Opinion' not in case:
            raise ValueError("Violation of R6: Emergency activation without legal opinion")
        if 'Obtain Legal Opinion' not in case['FollowUp']:
            raise ValueError("Violation of R6: Legal opinion not received within 24 hours")
```

## Handling Missing Attributes
- **Resource Attribute**: If the resource attribute is missing, the system should default to an error flag indicating the need for resource assignment.
- **Time Attribute**: If time attributes are missing, the system should use the mean values from the temporal profile as defaults for analysis.
