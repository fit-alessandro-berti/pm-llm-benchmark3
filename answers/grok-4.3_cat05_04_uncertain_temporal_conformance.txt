## 1. Normalization Assumptions
True time = recorded time  offset, with offset bounds applied after resolution uncertainty. Resolution uncertainty is applied first: HUB exact second receives ±0.5 s; OPS-BER floor-to-minute yields [MM:00, MM:59.999]; SENSOR rounds to nearest 10 s yielding ±5 s; VENDOR-NY and LEGACY-BER floor-to-minute; MOBILE-LON rounds to nearest minute. Clock-offset bounds are subtracted directly from the already-resolved interval. All times converted to UTC; CEST = UTC+2, BST = UTC+1, EDT = UTC4. LEGACY-BER DST fold produces two candidate UTC intervals for ambiguous local times; ingestion order is used only to discard impossible orderings, not to collapse intervals. Mobile entry delay on T-406 is modeled as an additional [+0, +300 s] uncertainty added to the recorded time before clock correction. T-404 Investigation_Start is inferred as Containment_Complete minus the stated 18–25 min duration interval, preserving the full uncertainty. T-407 Alert_Sent is treated as a distinct activity from Alert_Received; the 0–20 min latency bound is applied only as a causal constraint on feasible durations, not as an identity. No midpoint substitution is performed.
