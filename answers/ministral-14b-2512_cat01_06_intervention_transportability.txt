By **stratifying by case type** and **monitoring overlap %, rework, and queue lengths**, we can distinguish:
- **Mechanism failure**: If overlap % is high but cycle time does not improve (e.g., due to originals delay).

- **Contextual failure**: If p90 worsens due to bottleneck shifts (e.g., legal review).

## 10. Assumption Register

1. **Assumption**: IT locks can be disabled for the pilot.
   - **Why needed**: True parallelism requires concurrent task ownership.
   - **How to test**: Measure overlap % in pilot; if <30%, locks are the issue.
   - **Consequence if false**: Parallelization will fail entirely.

2. **Assumption**: Reviewers can be cross-trained to handle both `Identity Check` and `Document Check`.
   - **Why needed**: Role specialization prevents overlap.
   - **How to test**: Track task assignment in pilot; if roles remain siloed, overlap will be low.
   - **Consequence if false**: Parallelization will be ineffective.

3. **Assumption**: `Originals Receipt` delay is 12h for 38% of cases (digital claims).
   - **Why needed**: Parallelization only helps if `Document Check` can start immediately.
   - **How to test**: Measure `Originals Receipt` delay distribution in pilot.
   - **Consequence if false**: No benefit for cases requiring originals.

4. **Assumption**: Legal review is not the bottleneck post-assessment.
   - **Why needed**: Parallelization may increase pressure on legal review.
   - **How to test**: Monitor legal-review queue lengths in pilot.
   - **Consequence if false**: Cycle time may worsen for legal cases.

5. **Assumption**: WIP limits (6 tasks per reviewer) will prevent queue saturation.
   - **Why needed**: Parallelization increases WIP risk.
   - **How to test**: Track queue lengths and cycle time under WIP limits.
   - **Consequence if false**: Cycle time may increase due to congestion.

6. **Assumption**: Timestamp integrity is maintained in the pilot.
   - **Why needed**: Overlap % must reflect true parallelism.
   - **How to test**: Audit timestamps for back-entry in pilot.
   - **Consequence if false**: Measured overlap may be artificially high.

7. **Assumption**: Rework rate will not increase by >5% due to parallelization.
   - **Why needed**: Overlapping checks may introduce conflicts.
   - **How to test**: Compare rework rates in control vs. treatment arms.
   - **Consequence if false**: Quality may degrade despite cycle time gains.

8. **Assumption**: Digital claims have similar complexity to Site A’s post-intervention cases.
   - **Why needed**: Site A’s improvement was driven by simpler cases.
   - **How to test**: Compare complexity scores between sites.
   - **Consequence if false**: Expected cycle time reduction may be lower.
