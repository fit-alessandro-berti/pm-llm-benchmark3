## 2. Rule Interpretation

### Formalization of Resource Rules (RR1–RR6)

* **RR1 (Drafter/Approver Separation):** For any case $c$, $\text{Identity}(\text{Draft\_Change\_Request}) \neq \text{Identity}(\text{Approve\_Change})$. Normalization applies: aliases owned by the same natural person yield an identity match and trigger a violation.
* **RR2 (Implementer/Verifier Separation):** For any case $c$, $\text{Identity}(\text{Implement\_Change}) \neq \text{Identity}(\text{Verify\_Implementation})$. Unattributed service accounts cannot satisfy this rule.
* **RR3 (High-Risk Approval Authority):** If $\text{Risk}(c) = \text{HIGH}$, $\text{Approve\_Change}$ must be executed by an actor holding QM authority at $t_{\text{event}}$. 
  * A delegation $D = (P \to Dg, [t_{start}, t_{end}], \text{Scope}, \text{Cond})$ is valid at $t_{\text{event}}$ if and only if:
    1. $t_{\text{start}} \le t_{\text{event}} \le t_{\text{end}}$;
    2. $\text{Scope}(c) \subseteq \text{Scope}(D)$;
    3. Principal $P$ is absent/unavailable as mandated by $\text{Cond}$;
    4. $D$ was validly executed without unauthorized subdelegation.
* **RR4 (Emergency Substitution & Retrospective Review):** If $\text{Risk}(c) = \text{HIGH}$ and $\text{Emergency}(c) = \text{true}$:
  1. $\text{Approve\_Change}$ may be performed by an IC or valid IC delegate at $t_{\text{event}}$.
  2. $\text{Retrospective\_QM\_Review}$ must be completed by a qualified QM or valid QM delegate within **two business days** of emergency approval ($t_{\text{retro}} \le t_{\text{approve}} + 2 \text{ Business Days}$).
* **RR5 (Identity Evidence & Attribution):** Automated or non-human accounts (`svc_qms`) provide technical execution capability but lack legal accountability. An action signed by `svc_qms` is non-compliant unless tied to a specific human via immutable multi-factor telemetry (e.g., FIDO token + workstation lock state).
* **RR6 (Suspicion vs. Proof):** Statistical anomalies (e.g., high pair co-occurrence, reciprocal approvals) generate procedural flags requiring audit, but do not constitute technical non-conformance without evidence of shared credentials, falsified records, or intentional rule bypass.

### Operational Definitions & Boundary Conditions

1. **Business Day Calculation (RR4):** Business days are Monday through Friday, excluding official site holidays. Weekend days (Saturday and Sunday) are excluded from the 48-hour clock. 
   * *Example:* An emergency approval on Tuesday, Aug 11 at 13:00 UTC requires retrospective review by Thursday, Aug 13 at 23:59 UTC (Day 1 = Wed Aug 12; Day 2 = Thu Aug 13). A review on Friday, Aug 14 at 16:00 UTC exceeds the two business day threshold.
2. **Principal Acting While on Leave:** If a principal (Asha Rao) logs in via secure remote access while recorded on administrative leave, the principal's standing authority remains active for actions personally performed under valid MFA. However, performing direct actions while on leave suspends the "principal absence" condition for delegates during that specific session window, invalidating parallel delegate actions taken under the assumption of absolute principal absence.
3. **Temporal Validity of Delegation Data Records:** A delegation documented by a signed paper/PDF instrument created prior to the effective interval (e.g., PDF timestamp 2026-08-09 18:00) is legally valid at $t_{\text{event}}$, even if the database entry was backfilled later (e.g., DB migration on 2026-08-12 09:15). Conversely, an email assertion attempting subdelegation contrary to parent terms (D-IC-04) is void regardless of database status.

## 4. Collusion and Alternative-Explanation Analysis

### Pattern Analysis & Hypothesis Testing

```
  [ Clara Stein (Process Eng) ]  <=== Reciprocal Low-Risk (4 Cases) ===>  [ Ben Koch (Quality Eng) ]
              |                                                                     |
              | High-Risk Drafts                                                    | High-Risk Approvals
              | (18 of 23 Cases)                                                    | (D-QM-01 Delegate)
              +---------------------> [ HIGH-RISK PROD CHANGES ] <------------------+
                                                |
                                                v
                                  [ Concentrated Cell Pair ]
```

#### 1. High Pair Co-Occurrence (Clara Stein & Ben Koch)
* **Observed Pattern:** Over 60 days, Clara drafted and Ben approved 18 of 23 HIGH-risk production changes.
* **Benign Explanation:** Both individuals are assigned to the same operational production cell where Clara is the sole Process Engineer and Ben is the dedicated Quality Lead. The volume reflects operational duty allocation rather than illicit collusion.
* **Adverse Explanation:** Clara and Ben colluded to push non-compliant process modifications through the change control gate without independent Quality review, capitalizing on Asha's absence.
* **Discriminating Evidence Required:** Shift rosters, engineering change request ticket details, and operational cell scope documentation proving sole-assignment mandates.

#### 2. Reciprocal Approval Pattern
* **Observed Pattern:** Ben drafted four LOW-risk training document changes that Clara approved (e.g., CHG-508 context).
* **Benign Explanation:** Mutual assistance on low-risk administrative/training documentation during high-workload periods to maintain plant compliance deadlines.
* **Adverse Explanation:** Quid-pro-quo arrangement ("scratch my back, I'll scratch yours") designed to evade engineering oversight and circumvent formal review protocols.
* **Discriminating Evidence Required:** Review notes, document diff histories, and timestamp logs proving whether substantive evaluation occurred prior to sign-off.

#### 3. Shared Workstation & Service Account Abuse (`svc_qms` on WS-DM-04)
* **Observed Pattern:** Case CHG-503 implementation (Diego Martins) and verification (`svc_qms`) executed on Diego's workstation WS-DM-04 without individual re-authentication.
* **Benign Explanation:** Maintenance personnel utilized a dedicated terminal in the shop-floor control room where an automated system verification script reported results via `svc_qms`.
* **Adverse Explanation:** Diego Martins signed off on his own maintenance work (violating RR2) by triggering the `svc_qms` verification endpoint using an unauthenticated batch script on his workstation.
* **Discriminating Evidence Required:** Process control system (PCS) log events, automated verification test suite execution records, and session lock logs for WS-DM-04.

#### 4. Credential Compromise / Invalid Activity During Sick Leave (CHG-506)
* **Observed Pattern:** Account `u_bk` approved CHG-506 from an internal office workstation at 13:00 UTC while Ben Koch was officially on certified sick leave (starting 12:00 UTC).
* **Benign Explanation:** Ben remained at his desk past 12:00 UTC to finish pressing pending approvals before departing the facility, leaving his sick leave start time slightly inaccurate.
* **Adverse Explanation:** A colleague (e.g., Clara Stein) utilized Ben’s unlocked workstation or shared credentials to self-approve CHG-506 in his absence.
* **Discriminating Evidence Required:** Turnstile badge-out timestamp for Ben Koch, Active Directory domain controller authentication logs, and IP MAC address mapping for the initiating terminal.
