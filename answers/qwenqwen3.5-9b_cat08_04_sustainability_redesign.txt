# 1. System boundary and assumptions

**Environmental Scope**
The system boundary encompasses the operational lifecycle from **Receive order** to **Close**, utilizing the flattened event log attributes.
*   **Inside Boundary:**
    *   **Energy:** Machine usage (`energy_kwh`), specifically associated with `CNC_A`, `CNC_B`, `heat_treat`, and `inspection_cell`.
    *   **Transport:** Logistics emissions (`transport_kgco2e`), including `parcel_air`, `parcel_ground`, `dedicated_van`, and `consolidated_truck`.
    *   **Materials:** Waste generation (`material_scrap_kg`), packaging (`package_material_kg`), and rework-related waste (`rework_flag` loops).
    *   **Process Time:** Cycle time affecting obsolescence risk (inventory holding).
*   **Outside Boundary:**
    *   Upstream raw material extraction and manufacturing of the parts themselves (beyond machine energy).
    *   Downstream customer usage of the spare part.
    *   Carbon factors for transport modes (assumed based on `transport_kgco2e` log data, exact grid mix unknown).
*   **Uncertainty:**
    *   **Attribution:** The log is flattened by order, making it difficult to attribute specific `machine_group` energy consumption to specific line items within a batch.
    *   **Demand Accuracy:** Forecast accuracy for Made-to-order (MTO) is unknown, creating uncertainty regarding the "obsolescence" metric (6% of batch-produced items not shipped within 14 days).
    *   **Carrier Reliability:** Historical disruption data is not explicit in the log, requiring assumption of `R1` (Resilience) risk based on route diversity.

**Assumptions**
*   **Service Definition:** "Unacceptable deterioration" is defined as a >5% increase in late delivery rate for Service-contract segments or failure to meet Critical-downtime SLA.
*   **Quality Definition:** Quality loss is measured by rework rates (`rework_flag`) and return loops (inferred from `Package reinforced` -> `Ship air` patterns).
*   **Resilience:** Defined as having at least two viable route options (`R1`) and avoiding single points of failure in production (`CNC_A` vs `CNC_B`).

# 3. Intervention options

The following interventions are selected from the candidate list (S1-S12) to balance environmental goals with service and quality constraints.

**S4. Route Selection for Standard-stock Service-contract**
*   **Target Path:** `Standard-stock` -> `Service-contract` -> `Availability check` -> `Ship`.
*   **Mechanism:** Shift default from `parcel_air` to `parcel_ground` or `consolidated_truck` when `promised_lead_time_hours` allows.
*   **Effect:** Reduces transport emissions by 4-6x per order (based on evidence).
*   **Risk:** Latency increases. Mitigation: Use priority picking logic to compensate for slower ground transport.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `Late delivery` rate for this segment post-change.

**S2. Dynamic Batching with Waiting Caps**
*   **Target Path:** `Plan production` -> `Batch wait` -> `CNC production`.
*   **Mechanism:** Group items to reduce `energy_kwh` per item (e.g., batch 12+ for CNC_A), but enforce a maximum `Batch wait` cap (e.g., 24h) to prevent obsolescence.
*   **Effect:** Decreases energy intensity by up to 50% for CNC/Heat-treat; limits inventory holding time.
*   **Risk:** Waiting time increases. Mitigation: Cap prevents obsolescence of 6% of batch items.
*   **Evidence:** Track `energy_kwh` vs `batch_size` vs `Late delivery`.

**S3. Machine Group Selection (CNC_A vs CNC_B)**
*   **Target Path:** `Plan production` -> `CNC production`.
*   **Mechanism:** Assign Made-to-order cases to `CNC_A` (lower rework, 9% vs 18%) unless queue time exceeds a threshold.
*   **Effect:** Reduces `material_scrap_kg` and rework loops (`rework_flag`).
*   **Risk:** `CNC_A` may be slower. Mitigation: Use only when `due-date slack` is sufficient.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `rework_flag` rate per `machine_group`.

**S6. Packaging-Route Compatibility Check**
*   **Target Path:** `Package standard` / `Package reinforced` -> `Ship`.
*   **Mechanism:** Logic gate prevents shipping heavy MTO parts via `parcel_air` without reinforced packaging, and prevents standard packaging on fragile MTO parts.
*   **Effect:** Reduces returns and secondary packaging waste (rework packaging loops).
*   **Risk:** Increased packaging material weight if forced to reinforced.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `Package reinforced` -> `Ship air` frequency and return rates.

**S5. Consolidated Truck Departures**
*   **Target Path:** `Package standard` -> `Ship ground`.
*   **Mechanism:** Delay non-urgent `Standard-stock` orders to `consolidated_truck` (reduces CO2 by 35-55%).
*   **Effect:** Significant reduction in `transport_kgco2e`.
*   **Risk:** Cycle time increases by 12-24h.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `consolidated_truck` share vs `Late delivery` for Regular customers.

**S7. Inspection Quality Gate**
*   **Target Path:** `Inspection` -> `Package`.
*   **Mechanism:** Introduce a pause for high-rework combinations to verify defect detection before releasing to production or packaging.
*   **Effect:** Reduces downstream `rework_flag` and packaging rework loops.
*   **Risk:** Short-term bottleneck.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `Inspection_cell` queue length vs `rework_flag` count.

**S9. Critical-downtime Segmentation**
*   **Target Path:** `Availability check` -> `Emergency production`.
*   **Mechanism:** Split "Critical-downtime" into true "Downtime" (requires speed) and "Precautionary" (can wait).
*   **Effect:** Reduces unnecessary `dedicated_van` usage and high-emission `emergency production` for non-critical cases.
*   **Risk:** Service degradation if misclassified.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `Late delivery` for "Precautionary" cases.

# 5. Rebound and displacement risks

Improving one metric may degrade another. The following risks must be managed:

1.  **Batching Obsolescence:** Increasing batch size to reduce `energy_kwh` increases the risk of 6% of items becoming obsolete if demand forecasts are wrong. *Mitigation:* Enforce `Batch wait` caps (S2).
2.  **Ground Shipping Latency:** Switching `Standard-stock Service-contract` from Air to Ground (S4) increases cycle time by 24h. If promised lead time is tight, this causes late delivery. *Mitigation:* Only apply when `promised_lead_time_hours` has sufficient slack.
3.  **Consolidation Capacity:** Using `consolidated_truck` (S5) reduces CO2 but requires 12-24h wait. This displaces capacity to urgent orders, potentially increasing lateness for `Service-contract` customers who need speed. *Mitigation:* Segment by urgency.
4.  **Inspection Bottlenecks:** Adding a quality gate (S7) reduces rework but increases `Inspection_cell` queues. If queues grow, `CNC production` waits longer, increasing total cycle time and late delivery risk. *Mitigation:* Parallelize inspection or add capacity.
5.  **Packaging Material Trade-off:** Using reinforced packaging (S6) reduces returns but doubles `package_material_kg` initially. If the return rate drops by >50%, the net material impact is positive, but up-front waste increases. *Mitigation:* Track total lifecycle material (pack + scrap).
6.  **Customer Behavior (Resilience):** If `Critical-downtime` is re-classified to reduce emissions (S9), customers may lose trust in emergency response. This creates a displacement risk where customers switch to competitors with higher resilience. *Mitigation:* Strict policy enforcement on "True Downtime" vs "Precautionary".

# 6. Balanced recommendation

**Redesign Package: "Lean-Resilient Flow"**

We recommend implementing a **coherent package** rather than isolated tweaks to ensure sustainability does not compromise the core business of spare parts (availability and speed).

1.  **Smart Routing (S4 + S5):** Implement a **Green Shipping Recommender** (S1) that defaults to `consolidated_truck` or `ground` for `Standard-stock` and `Regular` cases, strictly reserving `parcel_air` and `dedicated_van` for cases where `promised_lead_time_hours` would be violated otherwise. This addresses the 22% SP1 violation rate and reduces transport emissions significantly without hurting service.
2.  **Dynamic Batching with Caps (S2):** Enforce batching for `CNC_A` and `heat_treat` to lower energy intensity, but hard-code a maximum `Batch wait` (e.g., 24h). This captures the energy savings of large batches while mitigating the 6% obsolescence risk.
3.  **Quality Gate for MTO (S3 + S7):** For `Made-to-order` cases, prioritize `CNC_A` over `CNC_B` to reduce the 18% rework rate. Add a quality gate at `Inspection` for heavy parts (>8kg) before packaging to prevent the `Package reinforced` -> `Ship air` -> Return loop.
4.  **Governance (S12):** Create a **Sustainability Exception Log**. Any deviation from SP1 (Ground policy), SP2 (Van justification), or Q1 (Packaging) must be logged with a reason. This ensures conformance is visible and exceptions are reviewed, preventing policy drift.

**Why this balances:**
*   **Environment:** Reduces transport CO2 by 30-40% (via routing/batching) and energy by 20% (via batching/machine selection).
*   **Service:** Protects `Critical-downtime` and `Service-contract` SLAs by maintaining air/van options for urgent cases (S9/S4 logic).
*   **Quality:** Reduces rework and returns through better machine selection and inspection gates.
*   **Resilience:** Maintains route diversity (R1) by not banning air entirely, only shifting it to justified cases.
