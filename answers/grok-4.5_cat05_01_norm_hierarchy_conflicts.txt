## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

**Facts (given):** GPIP-4 is board-approved and globally scoped from 2026-01-01. Local SOP DE-AAC-PROC-17 is a controlled site procedure effective 2025-03-01 and may tighten, not silently weaken, GPIP-4. ECD-2 is a jointly signed COO/Incident Commander directive with a stated effective window (2026-07-14 09:00–2026-07-18 18:00 CEST) and stated subject-matter scope (“critical sterilization-line lots”). The CFO email is an ordinary uncontrolled message. The CRO did not issue or later ratify an A7 exception. ERP outage ON-44 affects timestamp weight only. Prior organizational practice has treated signed incident directives as temporary overlays, but not in a documented new-supplier Compliance-clearance precedent.

**Assumptions (explicit, revisable):**
1. Authority is multi-dimensional: formal rank (board policy > controlled procedure > incident directive > uncontrolled email), functional ownership (CFO over Finance interpretation; CRO over GPIP-4 written exceptions; Compliance over A3 clearance), specificity (narrow emergency overlay vs general rule), and time (rules applied at the moment of the governed act, especially commitment).
2. ECD-2 is a powerful operational overlay for timing and supplier-onboarding inside its window and subject scope, but it is not automatically an A7 CRO exception. Therefore it does not, by its own silence, extinguish GPIP-4 segregation of duties (A1), pre-commitment Finance/budget-owner control (A2), or pre-commitment Compliance clearance (A3), unless a separate defeater is proven.
3. “Emergency necessity” is evaluated under the definition actually invoked: B4 for pure local emergency (imminent safety harm or stoppage 6 hours); C-scope for ECD-2 lots. Necessity does not equal universal waiver.
4. Later approvals mitigate operational risk and may reduce severity; under A6 they do not automatically cure missing pre-commitment controls.
5. Retrospective records may prove a timely act if stronger contemporaneous evidence (call recordings, signed PDFs, supplier acks, access logs) supports the real-world sequence; ERP timestamps are rebuttable during ON-44 and similar skew.
6. Burden of proof: the organization must be able to demonstrate satisfaction of pre-commitment controls or a valid exception. Ambiguity on scope, delegation, or IC membership is resolved against a clean COMPLIANT finding when the control is integrity-critical (SoD, Compliance on new/high-value suppliers, Finance pre-commitment 50k).
7. CFO D1–D2 can narrow or interpret Finance operational approval mechanics up to the stated amounts during the ECD-2 window, but cannot by email waive Compliance or A1 SoD (D3–D4 acknowledge residual integrity controls).
8. Incident Command authorization is treated as joint operational authorization under ECD-2; it is not automatically “independent budget owner” approval under A2 unless facts establish budget ownership.

**Decision method:** For each case, (i) fix the governing clock time of Commit_to_Supplier (using best evidence), (ii) determine whether ECD-2 window and subject scope apply, (iii) apply GPIP-4 controls that ECD-2 does not clearly defeat, (iv) apply local SOP rules not superseded, (v) test SoD and actor identity, (vi) classify with severity and confidence, separating policy breach from logging defect.

- **Supplier onboarding / PO timing inside ECD-2 + critical lots:** ECD-2 C1/C2/C4 > B1/B3 > ordinary practice.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Replacement hierarchy and exception clause (draft)**

1. **Rank:** Board GPIP controls (segregation of duties; pre-commitment Finance + independent budget owner 50k; pre-commitment Compliance for new suppliers or >100k) outrank local SOPs and incident directives unless a valid exception under §2 exists. Local SOPs may add stricter thresholds and evidence rules.
2. **Incident overlay:** A directive jointly signed by COO and Incident Commander may, for a stated window 120 hours and a defined SKU/lot/purpose list, override (a) PO-before-commit sequencing and (b) approved-supplier register onboarding timing. It does **not** override GPIP SoD, pre-commitment Compliance, or the requirement of a Finance Controller affirmation before commitment unless §2(b) is met.
3. **§2 Exceptions:** (a) CRO may issue written, amount-capped, SKU-scoped, time-limited exceptions to any GPIP control. (b) If CRO is unavailable, COO+CFO+Incident Commander may issue a joint bridge exception 72 hours, automatically expiring unless CRO ratifies; bridge exceptions must restate each waived control by clause ID. (c) CFO alone may interpret Finance *routing* during a bridge/overlay but cannot waive Compliance or SoD.
4. **Emergency dual path:** Local 6h stoppage/safety emergencies allow commit-before-PO and delegated Shift Lead Plant substitution only with recorded active delegation; Finance and Compliance rules remain per §§1–3.
5. **Authorization content:** Dual IC authorization must name amount, supplier, purpose, and GPIP clause impacts; verbal allowed if two named IC members and a durable record within 4 hours.
6. **Evidence:** System timestamps are rebuttable; supplier acknowledgments, signed PDFs, and recorded calls control when clocks disagree. Late recording of a timely act is a logging defect, not compliance.
7. **No automatic cure:** Post-commitment approvals mitigate and must be obtained promptly but do not cure missing pre-commitment SoD/Compliance/Finance controls; breaches require logged risk acceptance by CRO or delegate within 10 business days.
8. **Safeguard against emergency normalization:** Any control waived under §§2–4 more than twice in 90 days for the same site/category triggers mandatory board Risk Committee review and automatic reinstatement of full GPIP pathways until review completes. Expiry of an overlay restores full controls without further notice; verbal extensions are void.

---

**Summary stance:** ECD-2 validly relaxed sequencing and register use inside its window and sterilization scope; it did not cleanly extinguish GPIP integrity pre-commitment controls. Most cases are NONCOMPLIANT with differentiated severity; P-505 is the clearest JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION on evidential timing plus pre-commit Compliance/IC; P-508 is nearest operationally but still fails documented Finance pre-commitment under the disciplined reading.
