## 1. Consistency rules and evidence model

**Hard invariants** (a violating repair is invalid, not merely unlikely):

- **H1 – Referential integrity:** every proposed E2O/O2O endpoint must exist in the given tables; no new events or objects may be invented.
- **H2 – Duplicate suppression:** at most one E2O triple (event, object, qualifier) and one directed O2O pair (source, target, qualifier). Re-adding an existing edge is a no-op, not a repair.
- **H3 – Qualifier directionality (rule 8):** `carries` runs DeliveryPackage, `packs` PackageItem, `contains` OrderItem, `bills` InvoiceOrder, `settles` PaymentInvoice. Reversed spellings are different facts, not synonyms.
- **H4 – Table independence:** `ocel.relations` (E2O) and the O2O store are separate; an edge in one never *logically entails* an edge in the other. Correlation between them is evidence, never entailment.
- **H5 – Additivity:** repairs here only add rows; observed rows are treated as correct recordings of *something*, so a repair must explain them, not overwrite them.

**Strong defaults** (rebuttable; from domain rules 1–7):

- **S1/S2:** Create Order  1 Order; Add Item  1 Order + 1 Item — already satisfied; they anchor the lifecycle prefix.
- **S3:** Pack Item  1 Item + 1 Package, with a *licensed* scanner-outage exception. ev08 has a scanner ref but no package, so the default reading is "a package relation was lost," not "no package existed."
- **S4:** Dispatch  1 Package + 1 Delivery, with a licensed bulk exception.
- **S5:** Record Payment  1 Payment + normally 1 Invoice, with licensed unapplied cash.
- **S6:** Issue Invoice  1 Invoice + 1 Order.
- **S7:** Cardinality defaults: PackageItem and DeliveryPackage are 1-many; PaymentInvoice many-to-one; InvoiceOrder may exceed 1. Repairs forcing many-to-many where 1-many is expected need extraordinary evidence.

**Weak heuristics:**

- **W1 Identifier similarity:** `scanner_ref=PK8-2` vs PK-8; `"N-421?"` vs N-421; wildcard `N-42*`. Prefixes anchor candidates; typos and wildcards cap their weight.
- **W2 Temporal adjacency:** ev07 (09:04, PK8-1) precedes ev08 (09:05, PK8-2); the dispatches share 10:00.
- **W3 Arithmetic identity:** 130+100=230 and 100+130=230. Strictly corroboration; **never proof** for invoice/payment links.
- **W4 Attribute agreement:** customer C17 on both orders, invoice, portal; route NORTH throughout; SKU types hint at like-with-like consolidation (glass alone in PK-7, cables together in PK-8).
- **W5 Orphan pressure:** DEL-Y and PAY-2 touch nothing; IT-B1's pack lacks a package. Orphanhood raises P(missing edge) but does not identify the partner.
- **W6 Stage order:** Create < Add < Pack < Dispatch < Confirm < Invoice < Pay. Feasibility filter only.

**Evidence against specific repairs:**

- **E1:** PK-8 gross_weight 2.9 looks tight for two items (weak; tare/weighing time unknown).
- **E2:** PK-8 would mix ORD-A and ORD-B items — operationally unusual, so "scan belongs to an unlogged package" is a real alternative.
- **E3:** Source data flags itself (`"N-421?"`, `"N-42*"`).
- **E4:** Observed O2O says INV-44 bills ORD-A only; absence of ORD-B is mild counter-evidence.
- **E5:** ev11 already names ORD-A; each added endpoint multiplies claimed scope beyond the record.

**Combination procedure:** anchor on the strongest identifier signal  corroborate with time, stage order, attributes  block on cardinality/occupancy (DEL-X is already fully paired, so it is a poor partner for PK-8)  use arithmetic only as tie-breaker  thresholds: 0.75 with a dominant candidate  *accept*; 0.55–0.74  *provisional*; overlapping candidates or a licensed incomplete state (rules 3, 5, 7, 10)  *unresolved*; direct contradiction  *reject*. Derived O2O projections inherit E2O confidence minus a derivation penalty and are **never** counted as independent confirmation (see §5).

## 3. Alternative repair bundles

**Bundle A — conservative / minimum change (selected).**

- **ev08 & PK-8:** add ev08–PK-8 and project `PK-8 packs IT-B1`. Direct scanner evidence; the strongest signals in the log.
- **ev10 & DEL-X/DEL-Y:** add ev10–DEL-Y and `DEL-Y carries PK-8`. Best identifier match; DEL-X excluded by occupancy; symmetric dispatch pattern. The "?" keeps this below accept.
- **ev11:** **no additions.** The wildcard fits both deliveries, but rule 5 explicitly licenses order-only confirmations, and the recorded row (ORD-A only) is a complete, legal fact. Left unresolved.
- **INV-44 & ORD-B:** unresolved. 230=130+100 with shared customer C17 is suggestive, but fees/taxes could coincide; leaving it alone contradicts nothing.
- **PAY-2 & INV-44:** unresolved. Unapplied cash is licensed; forcing settlement manufactures a closed receivable.

*Coherence:* lifecycles stay deliberately partial — DEL-Y ends at Dispatch, INV-44 is half-settled (100/230), PAY-2 stands alone, ORD-B truncates after Add Item in the Order view. Nothing is forced shut; every addition rests on direct identifier evidence.

**Bundle B — consolidated customer cycle.**

- **ev08/PK-8, ev10/DEL-Y:** as in A.
- **ev11:** add ev11–DEL-X **and** ev11–DEL-Y (joint acknowledgment — explicitly not a unique assignment). Reasoning: the portal recorded a *corrupted carrier reference* (`N-42*`), meaning it had delivery context and lost precision; that fits "acknowledged both shipments, ref truncated" better than "portal lost the identifier entirely." Optional extension ev11–ORD-B (0.35) only if P1/P2 hold, since PK-8 then physically carries ORD-B's item; flagged as the most speculative edge in the dataset.
- **INV-44 & ORD-B:** add ev12–ORD-B and `INV-44 bills ORD-B`. Consolidated billing for C17: arithmetic + customer + a single invoice event converge. Still not proven — if 230 embeds fees, ORD-B was never invoiced and this pair corrupts revenue attribution.
- **PAY-2 & INV-44:** add ev14–INV-44 and `PAY-2 settles INV-44`. Staged settlement completing 230; alternatives (unapplied cash, out-of-extract invoice) judged weaker because PAY-1 already targets INV-44 and both payments follow issue the same day.

*Coherence:* every object reaches a natural closing stage — both deliveries dispatched+confirmed, invoice fully settled, both orders invoiced. Internally consistent but "too tidy": the financial half stands or falls with the arithmetic reading, and errors correlate (wrong invoice scope  wrong settlement interpretation).

**Bundle C — bulk dispatch, phantom delivery, partial payment.**

- **ev08 & PK-8:** as in A (scanner evidence survives every bundle).
- **ev10:** read ev09+ev10 as one bulk dispatch onto DEL-X (truck N-420): add ev10–DEL-X (0.5). `"N-421?"` is a data-entry artifact; DEL-Y is a phantom/cancelled record and stays orphaned.
- **ev11:** add ev11–DEL-X only (one physical shipment confirmed; wildcard matched N-420).
- **INV-44 & ORD-B:** add ev12–ORD-B + `bills` (consolidated reading retained).
- **PAY-2 & INV-44:** leave unlinked — unapplied/prepayment; INV-44 remains 130 open.

*Coherence:* operationally plausible (one truck, one confirmation, part-payment) and explains DEL-Y's orphanhood as a data-quality artifact rather than a lost edge. Cost: it strains against the two distinct carrier refs (why mint N-421?), and the Delivery flatten then shows a repeated Dispatch on DEL-X, easily misread as a re-dispatch loop.

## 5. Lifecycle and cardinality audit

- **Order.** ORD-A: full legal lifecycle (create, add×2, pack via items, dispatch via PK-7, confirm, invoice, part-payment). ORD-B: create, add, then — under A — nothing further at Order level; its goods continue inside Item/Package/Delivery views. A truncated-but-valid prefix is not a defect. Mutually attested: `ORD-A contains IT-A1/IT-A2` (ev03/ev04 **and** observed O2O) — genuine two-channel support.
- **Item.** All three items have Add + Pack. IT-B1's pack gains its package only via P1; without it, ev08 remains a licensed exception row. No orphans.
- **Package.** PK-7: pack + dispatch + carry, each attested on both channels (ev06+O2O; ev09+O2O) — the only fully corroborated fragments in the log. PK-8: under A, packs two items (1-many, legal) drawn from two orders (semantically suspicious, E2), dispatches on DEL-Y.
- **Delivery.** Baseline: DEL-Y is a true orphan (zero events). A: gains Dispatch + carry, still unconfirmed — partial, acceptable. B: gains Confirm. C: stays orphan by design. DEL-X: dispatch + carry double-attested; confirm only in B/C.
- **Invoice.** INV-44: issue + bills ORD-A + settled by PAY-1, all double-attested. Under A it remains 130 open — a legitimate open receivable, not a bug. Under B it bills 2 orders and takes 2 payments (both licensed cardinalities).
- **Payment.** PAY-1 complete. PAY-2: recorded, unapplied under A/C — orphan *by rule 7's license*; I decline to "fix" it. Under B it settles.

**Orphan events:** none (every event keeps 1 object); ev08/ev10 are half-orphaned in the baseline and healed in A. **Suspicious cardinalities to watch:** PK-8{IT-A2, IT-B1} cross-order packing; any variant placing PK-8 on two deliveries (rejected); ev11 fan-out beyond three endpoints (B with P11) — legal but inflates confirmation semantics.

**Circularity disclosure.** P2 is justified only by P1 plus S3; P4 by P3; P6 by P5; P8 by P7. Those O2O rows are the *same assumption written into the other table*; they must not be cited as corroboration for their E2O parents, nor chained (P1P2"PK-8 is mixed"P11) as if each hop were independent — confidence compounds downward (0.80·0.75·…  ~0.35 for P11). By contrast, PK-7/IT-A1, DEL-X/PK-7, INV-44/ORD-A, and PAY-1/INV-44 enjoy genuine two-channel support, which is why the audit trusts them more than anything this repair adds.

## 6. Downstream process-mining effects

**Unrepaired baseline.** The OC-DFG learns "Pack Item" touching only Items and "Dispatch Package" touching only Packages — the licensed exceptions become the model's *normal* fragments. DEL-Y and PAY-2 have no interactions and vanish from OTG/ETOT. Package-flattened PK-8 = [Dispatch Package] alone (dispatch without pack); Delivery flattening has no DEL-Y case; Payment-flattened PAY-2 is a one-event case.

**Bundle A (purely additive; no edge disappears).**
- OC-DFG: new fragment on PK-8, *Pack Item  Dispatch Package*; new start fragment on DEL-Y, * Dispatch Package*.
- OTG: (Pack Item · Package–Item) +1 observation; (Dispatch Package · Package–Delivery) +1.
- ET–OT: (Pack Item, Package) and (Dispatch Package, Delivery) each gain one interacting event.
- Flattenings: PK-8 becomes [Pack Item, Dispatch Package]; DEL-Y = [Dispatch Package]; **Order/Invoice/Payment flattens are identical to baseline** — A is invisible at Order level, so the mixed-package anomaly stays hidden from Order-based views.
- Frequencies: event-count metrics unchanged (no new events). Unique-object metrics: Pack Item×Package 23; Dispatch Package×Delivery 12. Total-objects-per-event: ev08 12, ev10 12.
- Equivalent-execution clustering on Order: unchanged; {ORD-A: long trace} vs {ORD-B: Create, Add} still split sharply.

**Bundle B (additional deltas).**
- OC-DFG: *Dispatch Package  Confirm Delivery* appears on both DEL-X and DEL-Y; *Issue Invoice* joins ORD-B's Order-view trace; INV-44 gains *Issue Invoice  Record Payment  Record Payment*.
- OTG: a new **Delivery–Delivery** edge via ev11 (two deliveries in one event — a distinctive artifact of joint acknowledgment); (Issue Invoice · Invoice–Order) +1; (Record Payment · Payment–Invoice) +1.
- ET–OT: new pair (Confirm Delivery, Delivery); counts rise for (Issue Invoice, Order) and (Record Payment, Invoice).
- Flattenings: Delivery cases close with Confirm Delivery; the Invoice case shows two payments; ORD-B's Order trace lengthens (plus Confirm Delivery if P11 is taken).
- Frequencies: unique-object Confirm Delivery×Delivery 02; Issue Invoice×Order 12; Record Payment×Invoice 12; total objects ev11 13 (4 with P11), ev12 23, ev14 12.
- Clustering on Order: ORD-B's trace now resembles ORD-A's; clusters merge, coarsening granularity and masking the difference between a portal-keyed confirmation (ORD-A only) and a goods-keyed one. If ev11 is wrongly fanned out to 2 deliveries × 2 orders, one physical acknowledgment is counted in up to four flattened cases — duplicated lifecycle fragments.

**Bundle C.** DEL-X flatten shows *Dispatch  Dispatch* (self-loop) — false re-dispatch behavior; DEL-Y absent; PAY-2 isolated; otherwise A's effects plus the ev12–ORD-B changes. Combining A's P3 with C's P12 (both deliveries for ev10) would duplicate PK-8's dispatch across two Delivery cases and insert a many-to-many Delivery–Package pair — the classic double-counting failure.

## 8. Over-repair warnings

1. **Fan-out on ev11.** Linking it to both deliveries *and* both orders turns one portal acknowledgment into up to four flattened-case occurrences; Confirm Delivery frequencies inflate, and the real "portal lost the carrier id" behavior disappears from the model.
2. **Dual-delivery dispatch.** Adding both DEL-X and DEL-Y to ev10 (P3+P12) puts PK-8 on two trucks, duplicates its Dispatch fragment across two Delivery cases, and inserts a many-to-many Delivery–Package relation that S7 says should not exist.
3. **Arithmetic settlement.** Wiring PAY-2 to INV-44 purely because 100+130=230 manufactures a closed receivable if the cash is actually unapplied; downstream dunning/AR simulations then never fire. Equality is a hint, not a ledger entry.
4. **Circular O2O bootstrap.** Deriving `packs`/`carries`/`bills`/`settles` from assumed E2O edges and then citing those O2O rows as corroboration inflates confidence with zero new evidence — and the error propagates into both graphs simultaneously, looking doubly confirmed.
5. **Chained inference.** ev08PK-8"mixed package"ev11–ORD-B is a three-hop argument built on a 0.80 edge; if ev08 actually belonged to an unlogged package, ORD-B falsely gains a delivery confirmation and Order-level clusters are contaminated.
6. **Load-balancing repairs.** Moving ev08 to PK-7 to "even out" package contents contradicts the scanner ref and corrupts weight analytics (PK-7 at 3.1 kg holding glass *plus* a cable is implausible), creating fake multi-pack sequences.
7. **Completing DEL-Y because it exists.** If DEL-Y is a cancelled or mis-created record, attaching ev10/ev11 fabricates a shipment that never ran; on-time-delivery KPIs are then computed over a ghost. Orphanhood motivates a search, not an edge.
