# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Internal audit reviewed the procurement-to-payment event log to assess control exceptions. The evidence does not establish fraud or collusion. It is most consistent with a mixture of emergency handling, informal specialization, and data-quality limitations, with some exceptions requiring targeted follow-up. Routine high-volume pairs such as Buyer_Aline–Approver_Elena and Buyer_Boris–Approver_Felix show little beyond expected workflow. A second priority is the same-person Emergency Override/Retrospective Justification pattern, especially the batch quarter-close entries, because it may indicate control failure or data migration rather than misconduct.

Data-quality limitations are material: shared Approver_Gita accounts, legacy_erp bot/human mapping errors, missing vendor maintenance links, and spreadsheet uploads can distort actor attribution and timing. We recommend cleaning resource mappings, re-running four-eyes and network analyses on true actors, and reviewing the highest-risk V-77x and retrospective-justification cases before drawing conclusions.
