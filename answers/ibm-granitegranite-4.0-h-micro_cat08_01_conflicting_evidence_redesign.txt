1. Current-process diagnosis

The current warranty process suffers from several issues that affect speed, cost, quality, compliance, and customer experience. The most frequent path, which is fast and cheap, results in a high number of downstream complaints and compliance exceptions. On the other hand, slower paths are more expensive but lead to better quality and customer outcomes. This contradiction makes it challenging to find a balance.

Key problems include:
- **Fast path**: High rework rates (rework repair in 12.5% of high-value repairs) and compliance violations (fast approval without proper risk review for high-value devices).
- **Manual eligibility review**: Long wait times (mean 7.5 hours, p90 18 hours) leading to customer dissatisfaction, especially for Premium customers.
- **Customer complaints/reopens**: High rates (9.2% of all cases, but 14.1% using Fast approval) and rework (rework repair in 12.5% of repaired high-value devices).
- **Quality/compliance close**: Long delays (mean 3.2 hours, p90 14 hours) for high-value devices, particularly when wrong replacement parts are selected.
- **Resource pressure**: Manual eligibility review queue grows sharply when utilization exceeds 82%, and Fraud/risk review is a bottleneck, especially on Mondays and after marketing campaigns.

2. Redesign A: Risk-aware Fast Approval

**Process Flow**:
- Intake claim -> Auto eligibility screen -> Risk assessment -> Fast approval (if risk low) or Manual eligibility review (if risk high)
- Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote -> Customer approval -> Repair device

**Targeted Segments**:
- Standard low-risk accessory and Premium low-risk accessory cases
- Fast approval path targeted at Standard low-risk accessory cases with low risk

**Improvement Mechanism**:
- Introduces a risk assessment step before Fast approval to ensure compliance with Normative Rule R1.
- Reduces unnecessary manual reviews for low-risk cases, improving speed and cost.
- Maintains quality and compliance by ensuring high-risk cases go through Manual eligibility review.

**Deliberate Sacrifices**:
- May increase complexity for Premium low-risk accessory cases due to the additional risk assessment step.

3. Redesign B: Segmented Review Path

**Process Flow**:
- Intake claim -> Eligibility assessment -> Evidence completion (if needed) -> Segmented review path
  - Low-risk device: Direct Repair quote
  - Mid-risk device: Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote
  - High-value device: Manual eligibility review -> Fraud/risk review -> Repair quote

**Targeted Segments**:
- Low-risk and mid-risk devices
- High-value devices

**Improvement Mechanism**:
- Tailors the review process to the risk level of the device, ensuring that high-value devices go through thorough risk assessment.
- Reduces unnecessary steps for low-risk devices, improving speed and cost.
- Ensures better compliance and quality for high-value devices by mandating a Fraud/risk review.

**Deliberate Sacrifices**:
- May increase complexity for mid-risk devices due to the segmented review path.

4. Redesign C: Evidence Portal and Quality Gate

**Process Flow**:
- Intake claim -> Eligibility assessment -> Evidence submission portal
  - If evidence submitted within 24 hours: Direct Repair quote
  - If evidence not submitted: Manual eligibility review -> Fraud/risk review -> Repair quote
- Quality gate before dispatch replacement -> Quality/compliance close

**Targeted Segments**:
- All customer segments, especially Standard high-value devices

**Improvement Mechanism**:
- Introduces an evidence portal to reduce repeated evidence requests by 30-45%.
- Adds a lightweight pre-dispatch quality gate to catch wrong replacement selection, improving quality and reducing rework.
- Ensures compliance by mandating a quality gate before dispatch replacement.

**Deliberate Sacrifices**:
- May increase abandonment for customers with low digital literacy due to the evidence portal.

6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

**Redesign A**:
- **Failure Mode 1**: Increased complexity for Premium low-risk accessory cases due to the additional risk assessment step.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Fraud/risk review could become a bottleneck if the number of high-risk Standard low-risk cases increases.
- **Failure Mode 2**: May not address the root cause of long wait times for Manual eligibility review.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Manual eligibility review queue could still grow if the risk assessment step takes too long.

**Redesign B**:
- **Failure Mode 1**: Increased complexity for mid-risk devices due to the segmented review path.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Manual eligibility review queue could still grow if the number of mid-risk cases increases.
- **Failure Mode 2**: May not fully address the compliance risks for high-value devices.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Fraud/risk review could become a bottleneck if the number of high-value cases increases.

**Redesign C**:
- **Failure Mode 1**: Increased abandonment for customers with low digital literacy due to the evidence portal.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Quality gate could become a bottleneck if the number of cases requiring a quality gate increases.
- **Failure Mode 2**: May not fully address the root cause of long wait times for Manual eligibility review.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Evidence submission could become a bottleneck if the portal experiences high traffic.

7. Recommended experiment sequence

**First Pilot**:
- Select a subset of Standard low-risk accessory cases for Redesign A.
- Select a subset of Standard low-risk accessory cases for Redesign C.
- Monitor metrics such as cycle time, cost, quality/rework, compliance risk, and customer experience.

**Metrics to Monitor**:
- Cycle time (mean, median, p90)
- Cost (EUR)
- Quality/rework rate
- Compliance risk
- Customer experience (complaint/reopen rate)

**Stop/Go Thresholds**:
- Go: If improvements in cycle time, cost, quality/rework, and compliance risk are observed, and customer experience improves.
- Go: If improvements in cycle time, cost, quality/rework, and compliance risk are observed, and customer experience improves.
- Go: If improvements in cycle time, cost, quality/rework, compliance risk, and customer experience are observed.
- Stop: If improvements in any metric are not observed or if new issues arise.

**Sequencing Logic**:
- Implement Redesign A first to address immediate speed and cost concerns.
- Implement Redesign C to address evidence-related issues and improve customer experience.
- Implement Redesign B to address risk-based segmentation and ensure compliance.

**Learning and Validation**:
- Compare the performance metrics of each redesign against the current process to determine if improvements are real or shifted downstream.
- Use statistical analysis to validate the significance of the improvements.
- Continuously monitor and adjust the processes based on real-world data and customer feedback.
