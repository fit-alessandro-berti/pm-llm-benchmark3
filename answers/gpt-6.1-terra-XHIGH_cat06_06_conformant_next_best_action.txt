## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning

The €20,000–€35,000 is the stated value of timely execution, conditional on a valid approval and execution before the deadline. A breach does not by itself establish that the whole range is lost in every scenario. The €15,000–€80,000 customer-harm range applies to false rejection or prolonged hold; its probability and overlap with deadline loss are unknown. Ultimate approval after conformant review is estimated at 0.55–0.75, but it does not establish approval by the deadline. The normal-processing breach estimate of 0.48–0.64 is a baseline, not the effect of D or C. These observational estimates may change under intervention and cannot be multiplied into a reliable action-specific utility without further assumptions.

Unlawful execution carries a plausible €100,000–€500,000 regulatory/compliance exposure and non-monetary consequences, including supervisory, legal and reputational effects. These figures are uncertain and cannot be treated as a certain fine, but the asymmetry is material. More fundamentally, the mandatory review and approval rules are constraints: even a forecast in which immediate execution has a lower numerical expected cost does not make it permissible. Within the conformant choices, D initially preserves information and avoids an immediate spillover, while C can be preferable when the incremental chance of an on-time valid decision outweighs the harm imposed on P-901. Neither dominates in every scenario. E is appropriate when missing evidence is material; F is appropriate when the rejection threshold and required reviews are satisfied, not merely because holding is inconvenient.

- **E:** An evidence request is permitted in the analyst cycle. Received evidence must be validated and returned to analyst review; the second review remains required while its triggering conditions apply.
- **F:** A valid rejection requires documented facts meeting the applicable rejection threshold, an assessment against that threshold, and completion of the required reviews. The prompt establishes neither sufficient grounds nor completion of the post-evidence and independent reviews. Obtain those records and assessments before rejecting.
- **G:** A valid exception requires actual Compliance Director authorization and a valid, recorded and justified incident identifier. Establish that a genuine incident exists and that the authorization covers the proposed action; neither prerequisite is currently satisfied, and the prompt does not establish that an override would dispense with every other applicable obligation.

A Suspicious Activity Report, if warranted, is not an approval and must not be disclosed to the customer. Customer communications about a hold or evidence request must respect that prohibition.

Immediately rerank on a confirmed change in S4's availability, a material screening or refreshed-risk result, or a change in P-901's urgency. In particular, a confirmed 65-minute S4 wait favors assessing S2 now rather than waiting for S4.
