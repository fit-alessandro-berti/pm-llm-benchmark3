The collapse is conjunctive, not competing. Each layer is artefactual: it changes `recorded_labels` or `recorded_order` while `true_process` continues within policy, so median cycle and approval rates stay flat and fitness recovers stepwise 32588189% as each provenance error is repaired. In August a second, distinct mechanism starts: policy_change genuinely extends `true_process` with `Supervisor Sign-off` for >50k cases, but `normative_model` was not versioned, creating a new class of moves-on-log that persist at 66% even after full cleaning and explain the remaining DFG arcs Compliance Check/Standard ReviewSign-off. ## 7. Provisional conclusion

The measured conformance collapse 93%32% fitting June-July is primarily an artefactual measurement failure, not a behavioral breakdown. Confidence in this as the driver of the *metric* is high (~85-90%). The stepwise recovery to 89% after three provenance repairs - mapping `Risk Screen``Classify` (+26pp), removing the technical `Queue Routed` (+23pp), and ordering by source sequence (+8pp) - isolates lexical, extraction and temporal layers that explain the DFG explosion and the surge in moves on log. The UI release, gateway event and ETL ingestion-time change align chronologically, affect all traces rather than reviewer clusters, and leave business outcomes - median cycle time, approval and appeal rates - essentially flat, which is expected if the true process continued unchanged while recording changed.

A separate, real process change remains material and is the explanation for the August-September residual: the EUR 50k Supervisor Sign-off policy added a legitimate optional step before `Decision`, but the normative model was not versioned. After identical cleaning, August-September still fits at only 66% versus 89% in June-July, while a candidate model allowing optional sign-off restores fitness to 86%. Confidence that model obsolescence is the main cause of the second-period residual is moderate-high (~75%). This is a governance failure, not an logging error, and requires a data-aware model extension.

Whether genuine non-compliance persists is not answerable from fitness alone and confidence is low (~35% for systematic bypass). The 25+30 `Enhanced Review``Decision` traces without `Compliance Check` and 20-35 `Manual Override` traces survive all repairs and could be true bypasses, missing events, or authorized corrections; incident detection is acknowledged incomplete and the high-value attribute was omitted from reporting. Stable aggregate outcomes do not exonerate, because appeals lag and bypass risk may be concentrated.

Immediate actions: IT must freeze the current conformance job, restore source-sequence ordering, implement an event-semantic contract that maps `Risk Screen`= `Classify` and flags `Queue Routed` as technical excluded by default, and preserve source timestamps. Process owners must version the normative model with a guarded optional sign-off and require high-value stratification before release. Compliance must conduct the Phase 4 blinded audit of enhanced-review cases without checks and reviewer-level analysis of overrides rather than citing moves-on-log. What must not be claimed yet is that employees are systematically bypassing compliance or that the log is now “fixed” - no claim about bypass or about full conformance restoration is warranted until the provenance repairs, data-aware model comparison with precision, and manual audit are completed.

## 8. Model-governance recommendation

1. **Versioned normative model with change linkage:** Every policy, UI or workflow release that can affect control-flow, activity set, or gating conditions creates a new model version with effective date, owner sign-off and migration rules; conformance is always computed against the version effective at case start, with diff documentation.

2. **Event-semantic contract and registry:** Each logged `concept:name` must have a contract defining business meaning, lifecycle (start/complete), cardinality per case, resource type, and predecessor/successor expectations; renames like `Classify``Risk Screen` require explicit mapping entry and dual logging during transition, never silent relabeling.

3. **Technical-event policy and provenance flag:** System/gateway events (`Queue Routed`, retries, routing) carry `event_source=technical` and `is_conformance_relevant=false` by default; they are excluded from fitness unless whitelisted, and extraction jobs must preserve this flag and resource nullability metrics.

4. **Timestamp provenance with ground-truth ordering:** Retain both `event_time` (source system), `source_sequence_no`, and `ingestion_time`; ordering for conformance uses source sequence, with automated drift monitoring (Kendall-tau between ingestion and source order) and blocking alerts on ETL changes.

5. **Data-aware constraints separate from control-flow:** Monetary thresholds, authorizations and segregation-of-duties (e.g., EUR 50k sign-off, compliance-check assignment) are modeled as guarded transitions or DMN rules, not optional control-flow; fitness is reported both overall and stratified by case attributes to prevent overpermissive gains in precision.

6. **Post-release conformance validation gate:** Within 5 business days of any UI/ETL/interface release, an automated job recomputes DFGs, variant deltas, events-per-case, alignment cost distribution and fitness/precision versus prior period; any >10pp fitness drop or new activity DFG >5% triggers hold on KPI reporting and requires Data+Process+Compliance joint sign-off before publication.
