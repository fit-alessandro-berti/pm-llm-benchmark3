If we automate registration (I8) without smoothing arrivals (I7), we simply flood Pre-analytical check faster, turning it from a near-saturated node into a hard stop, and increase downstream Batch-wait and Assay fragmentation when Urgent bypass rules (74%58%) collide with batched Routine prep.

# 3. Robust redesign package

**Base package (always on, not scenario-specific):**
- **I7 (Smooth courier pickup slots)** combined with **I8 (Automate registration for complete digital metadata)** and **I6 (Early pre-analytical defect triage within 2h)**.
- **Flexible clerical reallocation:** Use Registration clerks freed by I8 to assist Accession correction under supervision (instead of cross-training Technical validators per I4, which would worsen the 101% Tech-val overload).

**Why robust:**
- I7 attacks arrival-variance that drives Pre-analytical and Courier queues in both S1 and S4.
- I6 reduces recollection-loop rate (which is ~2.7× higher per week in peak than normal) and therefore protects Assay and Technical validation from invalid WIP.
- I8 reduces Registration median queue (0.72.8h) and frees humans for exception handling, not just volume.
- By avoiding strict priority rules, no single case type is starved; the package works under mix shift (S1), machine stress (S2), and specialist absence (S3) because it lowers total invalid/WIP entry rather than reshuffling it.

**Assumptions:**
- Couriers can tolerate rescheduled slots (I7).
- Order metadata quality is sufficient for I8 to fire on a meaningful subset.
- Triage in I6 has high specificity (avoids false rejects).

**Where they may fail:**
- If weather forces exogenous delay (S4 extreme), I7 cannot smooth what is uncontrollable.
- If I6 triage is placed inside the already 96%-utilized Pre-analytical station without staging, it creates a new local bottleneck there (see Section 6).

# 4. Scenario-specific policies

- **Policy P1 – Tiered dispatch & dynamic Routine batching (I1+I2 variant):**  
  *Trigger:* Urgent+Complex share > 45% (peak is 49%) AND Assay queue < 15h.  
  *Action:* Urgent bypasses batch; Complex-panel gets secondary priority; Routine batch size raised 1224 only when Prep queue > 8h.  
  *Off-trigger:* Mix returns below 35% or Assay queue > 18h (then hold batch expansion to avoid flooding Tech val).
- **Policy P2 – Machine dedication with outsourced overflow (I3+I10 fallback):**  
  *Trigger:* M2 repeat-rate > 8% or M1 utilization > 95% with queue > 20h.  
  *Action:* Dedicate M1 to Urgent/Complex; route Routine to M2 if healthy, else activate I10 for Complex-panel overflow to partner (accepting 12h transport delay) to protect Specialist review.
- **Policy P3 – Evening Medical-validation shift (I5, constrained):**  
  *Trigger:* Specialist-review queue > 24h or calendar = second Friday (known absence).  
  *Action:* Evening shift pulls **only** cases already cleared by Technical validation, with Complex-panel and Urgent first, to avoid pulling premature work.
- **Policy P4 – Arrival compression protocol (I7+I6 intensified):**  
  *Trigger:* Courier arrival coefficient of variation > 0.8 or Courier queue > 6h.  
  *Action:* Enforce staggered intake and expand I6 triage staffing from floating clerks to prevent Pre-analytical saturation.

# 6. Residual vulnerabilities

- **Technical validation hard constraint:** Peak utilization is 101%; even after base redesign, this station has no slack and remains the ultimate limiter for releasing volume in all scenarios.
- **Repeat-after-failure clustering:** If Policy P2 pushes Routine entirely to M2 during stress, repeat assays (already 1,260 peak cases, clustered after M2/night use) will consume double Assay and Technical-validation capacity.
- **Knowledge concentration:** Specialist review relies on one specialist (absent every second Friday). Outsourcing (I10) mitigates queue but not internal knowledge loss for rare Complex-panel holds.
- **Vulnerability caused by the redesign itself:** Implementing I8 (automation) and I6 (early triage) without adding net staffing will **flood the Pre-analytical check station**. Historically it buffered intake via manual registration delay; removing that delay while adding a triage step at the same node (96% utilized in peak) converts Pre-analytical check into the new systemic bottleneck and may simply move the recollection-loop problem upstream rather than eliminating it.

# 7. Minimum monitoring dashboard

- **Courier intake queue median** – *Leading:* >5h signals imminent Pre-analytical spike within 2–4h.
