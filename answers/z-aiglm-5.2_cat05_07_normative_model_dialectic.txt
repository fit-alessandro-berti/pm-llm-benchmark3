**Disputed propositions**

**Burden of proof**

- To declare **noncompliance**, the proponent must show either (a) a control-flow deviation from the applicable norm at the relevant time, or (b) a breach of a CS-9 non-control-flow rule with evidentiary support, and must exclude justified exception, scope exclusion, logging defect, and label-mapping explanations.
- To accept a **justified exception**, the proponent must show a valid authority in force at the event time, eligibility satisfied at the event time, and any collateral conditions (spend cap, monitoring, retrospective checks) met.
- Where evidence is incomplete because of known data-collection defects, the conclusion should be **indeterminate**, not noncompliant, unless independent corroboration proves the omission.

## 6. Revised Normative Architecture

A layered architecture is required because POWL alone cannot enforce amount, date, risk, identity, and authority conditions.

**Layer 1 — Revised control-flow model (POWL)**
- Register_Supplier  optional repeatable Request_More_Info  concurrent Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review for the standard route  choice among:
  - standard manual route: Manual_Procurement_Approval  Activate_Supplier;
  - low-risk auto route: Auto_Approve  Activate_Supplier;
  - emergency provisional route: Activate_Supplier_Provisional  concurrent retrospective Quality_Review and Manual_Procurement_Approval  Confirm_Activation;
  - Reject_Supplier;
- then Notify_Requester  Close_Case.

**Layer 2 — Data-aware eligibility/amount/time rules (pandas/data checks)**
- PD-3 eligibility snapshot: risk class, country, corporate-group membership, timestamp inside effective window.
- FD-7 authority: critical-relief flag, timestamp inside window, spend cap  EUR 20,000, daily monitoring presence, retrospective checks within two business days.
- Label-mapping registry: QA_Clearance  Quality_Review with effective dates.
- Deduplication rule: identical source transaction ID collapses repeated events.

**Layer 3 — Resource and conflict-of-interest rules (DECLARE/log-skeleton + org controls)**
- `not same_person(requester, manual_approver)` across resolved aliases.
- `not same_reporting_line_or_relation(reviewer, approver)` unless disclosed and approved.
- Separation enforced by identity resolution, not by control flow.

**Layer 4 — Policy-overlay governance**
- Each pilot/emergency carries a rule_id, version, effective_from, effective_to, authority, and scope predicate. Expired rules cannot match events outside their window.
- A permanent amendment must be recorded before it is operational; “imminent” is not authority.

**Layer 5 — Explicit scope rules**
- Rejected pre-screen leads are intake cases outside the main POWL until pre-screen acceptance; model their abbreviated flow separately.
- Reactivation is a distinct variant: Register_Reactivation, validate existing certification, skip or reuse Quality_Review under a certification-validity rule.
- Withdrawal is an explicit terminal branch.

**Where each belongs**
- POWL: control flow, concurrency, loop, choices.
- DECLARE/log-skeleton: resource separation, COI, “activation not before sanctions,” “spend cap until retrospective approval.”
- Pandas/data checks: eligibility snapshots, spend caps, effective-date matching, label mapping, deduplication.
- Organizational controls: alias registry, COI disclosure, delegation evidence, emergency authority issuance.

## 7. PM4Py Model and Data Proposal

Limitation: POWL cannot enforce risk class, country, spend cap, timestamps, identity separation, or pilot/emergency eligibility. The model below encodes the defensible control-flow approximation; all such conditions are externalized to the overlay table and data checks.

```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# --- Activities ---
register = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")
rmi = Transition(label="Request_More_Info")
sanctions = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
quality = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
manual = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
auto = Transition(label="Auto_Approve")
activate = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
activate_prov = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier_Provisional")
retro_quality = Transition(label="Retrospective_Quality_Review")
retro_manual = Transition(label="Retrospective_Manual_Approval")
confirm = Transition(label="Confirm_Activation")
reject = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
notify = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")
close = Transition(label="Close_Case")
skip = SilentTransition()

# --- Optional repeatable Request_More_Info (approximation) ---
# LOOP(do=rmi, redo=skip) allows rmi one or more times; XOR with skip makes it optional.
rmi_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[rmi, skip])
rmi_optional = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[rmi_loop, skip])

# --- Standard checks: Sanctions and Quality concurrent ---
checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, quality])

# --- Standard manual approval route ---
standard_route = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[manual, activate])
standard_route.order.add_edge(manual, activate)

# --- Low-risk auto-approval route ---
auto_route = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[auto, activate])
auto_route.order.add_edge(auto, activate)

# --- Emergency/provisional route: provisional activation, then concurrent retrospective checks, then confirm ---
retro_checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[retro_quality, retro_manual])
emergency_route = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[activate_prov, retro_checks, confirm]
)
emergency_route.order.add_edge(activate_prov, retro_checks)
emergency_route.order.add_edge(retro_checks, confirm)

# --- Decision ---
decision = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[standard_route, auto_route, emergency_route, reject]
)

# --- Top-level model ---
M1 = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, rmi_optional, checks, decision, notify, close])
M1.order.add_edge(register, rmi_optional)
M1.order.add_edge(rmi_optional, checks)
M1.order.add_edge(checks, decision)
M1.order.add_edge(decision, notify)
M1.order.add_edge(notify, close)
```
