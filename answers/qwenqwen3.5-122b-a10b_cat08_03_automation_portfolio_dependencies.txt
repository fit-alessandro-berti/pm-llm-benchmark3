# 2. Dependency and Prerequisite Map

The automation landscape is defined by sequential data dependencies and resource constraints. Automating downstream activities without stabilizing upstream inputs creates "speed-to-failure" scenarios.

*   **Upstream Data Quality Dependencies:**
    *   **A6 (Normalization)** relies on **A5 (Missing-doc Checklist)**. If customers submit poor-quality docs due to lack of guidance, A6 fails to extract data, increasing analyst workload rather than reducing it.
    *   **A8 (Committee Pack)** and **A9 (Contract Gen)** rely on **A4 (Entity Mismatch)** and **A6**. If entity mismatches remain unresolved or financials are unnormalized, the committee pack contains inconsistent data, triggering the very remediation loops (14% for Credit-line renewal) A9 aims to prevent.
*   **Legal/Compliance Controls:**
    *   **A3 (KYC Pre-classifier)** cannot proceed without strict audit trails. False negatives here bypass mandatory checks, creating regulatory exposure.
    *   **A12 (Control Remediation WB)** is a prerequisite for safe operation of **A9 (Contract Gen)**. Without a way to manage exceptions (8.5% remediation rate), automated contract generation will either fail frequently or bypass necessary manual overrides.
*   **Human Escalation Capacity:**
    *   **A1 (Auto-registration)** increases case inflow to **Compliance Specialists** (only 3 FTE). Without **A4 (Mismatch Assistant)**, this inflow clogs the 31-hour wait queue, violating SLAs.
    *   **A6 (Normalization)** reduces manual effort for **Credit Analysts** (9 FTE), preventing the overload observed when clean inflow increases without normalization.
*   **External Valuation Dependency:**
    *   **A7 (Collateral Triage)** cannot resolve the 58-hour wait caused by external valuers. It can only optimize routing. Automation here offers limited cycle-time improvement compared to internal bottlenecks.
*   **Core-Banking Integration Constraints:**
    *   **A1** and **A9** typically require deep integration with the Core Banking System (CBS) for account creation and legal commitment recording. With only **two deep-integration slots available in the first six months**, prioritizing A1 or A9 consumes significant IT capacity that could be used for API-based workflow tools (A4, A5, A6, A12).
