```text

```

The sudden conformance collapse from 93% (April–May) to 32% (June–July) and 66% (August–September, partially normalized) is an **epistemic illusion masking a smaller, genuine operational drift**. It arises from four interacting mechanisms:

1. **Telemetry Pollution (H3, H6, H4):** Three rapid IT changes between May 31 and June 15 corrupted the mapping between business reality and the event log. Renaming `Classify` to `Risk Screen` created a synchronous penalty of one log move and one model move per trace. Injecting the automated `Queue Routed` step added another log move in 81.5% of cases, while switching `Decision` and `Notify` to warehouse-ingestion timestamps inverted their order in ~11% of cases. Together, these data defects generated 85% of the excess alignment cost in June–July without altering the underlying claims process.
2. **Governance Lag (H1, H2):** On August 1, a legitimate policy change mandated `Supervisor Sign-off` for claims over EUR 50,000. Because the normative process tree remained static and control-flow-only, compliant execution of the new policy registered as non-conformance (log moves on `Supervisor Sign-off`) in 210 traces.
3. **Compensatory Behavior (H1, H3):** The June 3 routing gateway likely introduced subtle routing errors, prompting staff to use `Manual Override` (20 cases in Jun–Jul, rising to 35 after the Aug 12 logging patch).
4. **Potential Localized Noncompliance (H5):** Across June–September, 55 `Enhanced Review` cases bypassed `Compliance Check`, and the July 10 memo suggests management concern.

**Why unchanged outcomes neither prove data error nor rule out noncompliance:** Global approval (~71.8%) and 30-day appeal (~3.7%) rates are insensitive to a 4% localized bypass rate (55/1,270 cases). Furthermore, skipping a preventative `Compliance Check` creates regulatory/fraud tail risk rather than customer appeals (customers rarely appeal approved claims), while data-logging errors naturally leave real-world outcomes unchanged.

# ==============================================================================
# 4. Normalization pipeline (Label mapping, Tech-event removal, Sequence repair)
# ==============================================================================
def normalize_log(
    event_log,
    map_risk_screen=False,
    remove_queue_routed=False,
    fix_notify_decision_order=False,
):
    """Applies transparent step-by-step normalization to an EventLog."""
    new_log = copy.deepcopy(event_log)
    for trace in new_log:
        # Step A: Map 'Risk Screen' -> 'Classify'
        if map_risk_screen:
            for event in trace:
                if event["concept:name"] == "Risk Screen":
                    event["concept:name"] = "Classify"

        # Step B: Filter technical event 'Queue Routed'
        if remove_queue_routed:
            kept_events = [
                e for e in trace if e["concept:name"] != "Queue Routed"
            ]
            trace._list = kept_events

        # Step C: Simulate ordering by source sequence number for 'Notify,Decision'
        if fix_notify_decision_order and len(trace) >= 2:
            if (
                trace[-2]["concept:name"] == "Notify"
                and trace[-1]["concept:name"] == "Decision"
            ):
                trace[-2]["concept:name"] = "Decision"
                trace[-1]["concept:name"] = "Notify"
    return new_log


# ==============================================================================
# 5. Alignment diagnostics & fitness evaluation helper

def evaluate_conformance(label, log_obj, net, im, fm, compute_prec=False):
    alignments = pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_alignments(log_obj, net, im, fm)
    fitness_res = pm4py.fitness_alignments(log_obj, net, im, fm)

    # Summarize dominant log and model moves from alignment diagnostics
    log_moves = {}
    model_moves = {}
    for al in alignments:
        for log_move, mod_move in al["alignment"]:
            if mod_move == ">>" and log_move != ">>":
                log_moves[log_move] = log_moves.get(log_move, 0) + 1
            elif (
                log_move == ">>" and mod_move is not None and mod_move != ">>"
            ):
                model_moves[mod_move] = model_moves.get(mod_move, 0) + 1

    prec_str = ""
    if compute_prec:
        prec = pm4py.precision_alignments(log_obj, net, im, fm)
        prec_str = f" | Precision: {prec:.3f}"

    print(
        f"[{label:48s}] "

## 7. Provisional conclusion

### Primary explanation for the measured conformance collapse
**Confidence Level: High (95%)**  
The headline collapse in fitting traces from 93% (April–May) to 32% (June–July) and 66% (August–September preprocessed) was overwhelmingly caused by **uncoordinated IT logging changes and normative model obsolescence**, not a sudden rebellion by operations staff. Specifically, three technical changes between May 31 and June 15—renaming `Classify` to `Risk Screen` in UI 4.2, injecting the automated `Queue Routed` event into the event stream, and overwriting event timestamps for `Decision` and `Notify` with warehouse-ingestion times—account for 57 percentage points of the 61-point fitness drop in June–July (recovering fitness from 32% to 89% once normalized). In August–September, the further drop to 66% was primarily driven by the August 1 policy mandate requiring `Supervisor Sign-off` for claims exceeding EUR 50,000 without updating or stratifying the normative process tree (which accounts for 20 percentage points of fitness in August–September).

### Separate assessment of whether a real compliance problem remains
**Confidence Level: Moderate (65% for localized noncompliance; 90% that an audit is required)**  
Stripping away logging noise and authorized sign-offs leaves a persistent, non-trivial residual of ~11–14% non-conforming traces across June–September:
1. **55 Enhanced Review cases (25 in Jun–Jul, 30 in Aug–Sep) with no recorded `Compliance Check`:** Because this anomaly emerged alongside the June gateway changes and July 10 supervisor memo, it is either genuine employee bypassing of mandatory controls or silent dropping of `Compliance Check` events in the logging pipeline.
2. **55 cases with `Manual Override` (20 in Jun–Jul, 35 in Aug–Sep):** Staff admit using overrides to correct routing mistakes, but unmonitored overrides could be used to downgrade high-risk claims into `Standard Review`.
3. **Unverified High-Value Sign-offs:** Because case attributes were omitted, we have not yet proved that all > EUR 50,000 claims received `Supervisor Sign-off`.

### Immediate actions
- **IT:** (1) Configure the conformance pipeline to order events by `(source_sequence_num, timestamp)`; (2) maintain a canonical activity mapping table (`Risk Screen` -> `Classify`); (3) tag `Queue Routed` as a technical routing event and exclude it from business-process conformance views; (4) check message-broker logs for dropped `Compliance Check` events and missing `org:resource` IDs.
- **Process Owners (Operations):** (1) Audit the 55 `Manual Override` cases to diagnose and fix the underlying `Queue Routed` routing rules; (2) update the normative model into two value-stratified branches (`<= EUR 50k` and `> EUR 50k`).
- **Compliance:** Perform a 100% provenance audit of the 55 `Enhanced Review -> Decision` cases against source database records and verify 100% sign-off coverage on claims > EUR 50,000.

### What must not be claimed yet
- **Do not claim** that employees bypassed 55 compliance checks until source database audit trails confirm the checks were never performed.
- **Do not claim** that flat approval (~71.8%) and appeal (~3.7%) rates prove full compliance; a 4% bypass rate or improper payouts will not register in customer appeals.
- **Do not claim** that the 86% fitness achieved by making `Supervisor Sign-off` optional validates August–September compliance, as an optional control-flow node masks missing sign-offs on > EUR 50,000 claims.

## 8. Model-governance recommendation

1. **Synchronized Normative Model Versioning & Policy Change Control:** No regulatory or operational policy change (such as the August 1 `Supervisor Sign-off` threshold) may take effect in production without a co-versioned update to the normative process model repository, tagged with effective start/end dates so historical traces are evaluated against the model version active at case inception.
2. **Immutable Event-Semantic Contracts:** Decouple front-end UI presentation labels from canonical process-mining activity identifiers (`concept:name`). Any UI release (such as Release 4.2 renaming `Classify` to `Risk Screen`) or logging patch (such as the August 12 `Manual Override` patch) must map to an immutable canonical ontology ID or trigger a formal schema deprecation and mapping review.
3. **Explicit Technical-Event Taxonomy & Layer Separation:** Require all emitted events to carry an explicit `event_layer` attribute (`business_activity` vs. `system_routing` vs. `ui_telemetry`) and a populated `org:resource` identifier (`SYSTEM` for automated gateways). Conformance checking against business policy models must automatically filter out `system_routing` events like `Queue Routed`.
4. **Source-Timestamp & Sequence Provenance Preservation:** Prohibit ETL pipelines from overwriting business execution timestamps (`time:timestamp`) with data-warehouse ingestion timestamps. Event schemas must separately persist `business_completion_time`, `source_sequence_num`, and `warehouse_ingestion_time`, with conformance engines strictly ordered by `(business_completion_time, source_sequence_num)`.
5. **Data-Aware Conformance Constraints (Multi-Perspective Checking):** Mandate that all policy rules conditioned on case attributes (e.g., `claim_amount > 50000`, risk scores, or Segregation of Duties on `org:resource`) be included in the conformance extract and evaluated via either stratified sub-models or data-aware Petri nets—explicitly banning silent `tau` optionality as a shortcut for conditional policy rules.
6. **Automated Post-Release Canary Conformance Gate:** Implement an automated CI/CD telemetry gate after every UI, gateway, or ETL deployment that compares DFG edge frequencies, event-per-case ratios, missing-resource rates, and alignment costs on a 24-hour canary sample, halting conformance dashboard publication and alerting IT if fitness drops by >5% without a corresponding normative model update.
