- **Immediate action:** Implement coordinator ownership and the merged address-validation/scheduling task **only for simple low-risk houses** (segment with low building risk, identified by available attributes).  
- **What to pause:** The field-survey skip rule for medium/high-risk addresses; any “Billing start before successful Activation test” flow; and permit-check bypass for older apartment buildings. Reinstate pre-billing activation and mandatory permit checks for all non-simple segments.  
- **What to scale:** Coordinator-owned front-end and reduced handover pattern for the simple-house segment; monitor coordinator utilization to keep <85% with backup.  
- **What to measure next (priority order):** M6 (rule validation), M4 (60-day downstream cost), M7 (conformance monitoring), M3 (reason codes), then M1/M2/M5/M8 as supporting.  
- **Threshold / evidence for broader rollout:**  
  1. M6 shows skip-rule accuracy 90% for medium-risk stratum; **or** skip remains paused.  
  2. M4 confirms total cost per case  EUR 198 (baseline estimated).  
  3. p90/p95 for apartments/old buildings returns to  baseline (31/45 days).  
  4. First-time-right 80% overall; complaint-within-30-days 6.1%; NPS recovers toward +21.  
  5. M7 shows 100% permit-check compliance and zero billing-before-activation violations.  

Only if these are met should management consider expanding Redesign R beyond simple low-risk houses.
