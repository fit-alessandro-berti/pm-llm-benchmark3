## 2. Dependency and prerequisite map

* **Upstream data quality dependencies.** A6 normalization is only effective after missing documents are reduced by A5. A8 committee pack auto-assembly is garbage-in/garbage-out without A6, A3 KYC status and A7 triage. A9 contract generation requires reconciled KYC clearance, Committee decision and normalized financials; DFG shows Contract preparation -> Customer signing 7300 and Contract preparation -> Remediate control issue 650.
* **Legal/compliance controls.** Customer signing cannot legally occur before KYC clearance. Provisional signing occurs in 6.5% of cases with 1.1% remediation. A9 and A11 must enforce KYC clearance, four-eyes for High-exposure SME and contract-decision match. Audit trail of model suggestion, human override and final decision is mandatory.
* **Human escalation capacity.** A3 pre-classifier and A4 assistant depend on 3 FTE Compliance specialists with high handover dependency from KYC ops. Resolve entity mismatch is low frequency 1750 but median waiting 31.0h and is on critical path in 82% of cases when present. Over-automation will increase specialist queue.
* **External valuation dependency.** Wait for external valuation is 2350 cases with median waiting 58.0h. A7 can triage and parallelize but cannot reduce external lead time. Committee packaging waits for valuation in 71% of Equipment finance and 18% of Working-capital cases.
* **Core-banking integration constraints.** A1, A9 and A11 need deep core integration. IT can integrate only two modules deeply in first six months. Back office is 12 FTE with utilization <65% after 15:00, so labor saving there has limited system impact.

## 3. Portfolio alternatives

**Quick-win portfolio: A1, A5, A9, A10**
Optimizes visible labor saving and political throughput. A1 removes Register application 8 min work at 11880 frequency, A5 reduces repeated Request missing documents loops, A9 cuts Legal ops work, A10 pushes Customer signing. Sacrifices synchronization: it increases clean inflow into KYC and Credit analysts without relieving entity-mismatch waiting 31h or financial statement normalization 20h waiting. Risk of exposing downstream bottlenecks.

**Bottleneck/synchronization portfolio: A4, A6, A5, A7**

Sacrifices immediate high-frequency labor saving and direct control strengthening; A1 remains manual.

**Compliance-first portfolio: A3, A2, A9, A12**

Sacrifices cycle time reduction on document loops and entity mismatch waiting; may increase waiting upstream.

## 4. Expected process-level effects

**Quick-win**
* Cycle time: modest reduction in registration and contract prep segments, no change in long waits.
* Waiting time: median waiting before Register application falls, median waiting before KYC check 4.0h and Normalize financial statements 20.0h unchanged or worsen due to increased arrival.
* Rework: missing-doc repeated rate may fall with A5, contract remediation may fall with A9.
* Handovers: fewer back office handovers, more handovers to Credit analysts.
* Resource demand: Back office utilization drops further after 15:00; Credit analysts 9 FTE face increased clean inflow; Legal ops month-end overload persists.
* Control violations: improved contract-decision match, KYC-before-signing risk unchanged.

**Bottleneck/synchronization**
* Cycle time: reduction on critical path where Resolve entity mismatch is present. Median waiting 31h before Resolve entity mismatch and 58h Wait for external valuation become primary levers.
* Waiting time: median waiting before Normalize financial statements and Financial document review 18.0h fall with A6 and A5. Request missing documents customer-response delay dominates and is shortened by nudging.
* Rework: Financial document review exception rate 24% and Request missing documents repeated 52% decline. Entity mismatch rework 38% declines with assistant.
* Handovers: fewer loops between Financial document review and Request missing documents, fewer KYC <-> Resolve entity mismatch cycles.
* Resource demand: Compliance specialists load more concentrated but shorter; Credit analysts receive cleaner statements, reducing 110 min analysis time and month-end four-eyes violations 5.8%.
* Control violations: indirect improvement via faster KYC clearance before signing.

**Compliance-first**
* Cycle time: limited direct reduction; may increase slightly due to additional checks.
* Waiting time: KYC check median waiting 4.0h may improve with pre-classifier prioritisation.
* Rework: classification errors 8% and contract remediation loops fall.
* Handovers: more structured escalation to specialists; four-eyes compliance improves.
* Resource demand: Compliance specialists 3 FTE become bottleneck if false positive rate high.
* Control violations: provisional signing 6.5% and contract-decision mismatch fall; auditable trail strengthened.

## 5. Risks and compensating controls

* False negatives in A3 KYC pre-classifier are severe. Compensate with mandatory human escalation, audit trail of suggestion/override/decision, and sampling.
* Model drift in A2 classification and A6 extraction. Compensate with monthly performance monitoring on misrouting rate and extraction accuracy by document quality, and human-in-the-loop thresholds.
* Exception handling overload. A4 and A3 increase specialist work. Compensate with capacity buffer, SLA for escalation <4h, and dashboard of queue age.
* Four-eyes compliance. Automation must block same person completing Credit analysis and Committee packaging in High-exposure SME. Compensate with system rule enforcement and monthly violation reporting.
* Customer friction from A5 rigid nudging. Compensate with relationship manager override and variant-aware checklists.
* Downstream bottleneck shift from A1. Compensate by not automating A1 in first wave and monitoring arrival rate to Credit analysts.

**Phase 1 Months 3-6 Deep integration 1**

## 7. Final recommendation

Recommend a Bottleneck/synchronization portfolio with compliance overlay: A4 Entity-mismatch resolution assistant, A6 Financial-statement extraction and normalization, A5 Missing-document checklist and customer portal nudging, A9 Contract generation with control-rule checks.

Highest-frequency activity Register application should not be automated first. Frequency 11880 masks process leverage. Register application is position 2 in 98% of cases with median waiting 1.5h and back office is already underutilized after 15:00. Automating it would increase arrival pressure on KYC check 4.0h waiting, Resolve entity mismatch 31.0h waiting, and Credit analysts who are central in working-together network. Process mining shows synchronization delays and rework dominate end-to-end performance: Resolve entity mismatch is critical path in 82% of cases when present, Financial document review -> Request missing documents loops are repeated 52%, and Committee packaging waits for multiple reconciled artifacts.

Targeting the synchronization points reduces waiting time, rework and control violations while preserving auditable human responsibility for KYC and four-eyes. A9 ensures compliance strengthening required by management, and A4/A6 relieve the specialist and analyst constraints that would otherwise be exposed by a naive high-frequency automation.
