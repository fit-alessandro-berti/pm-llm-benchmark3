## 1. Conformance semantics

Object-centric conformance against an incomplete norm separates structural differences into seven diagnostic classes:

- **Forbidden behavior**: Sequences, lifecycle couplings, or event-object links matching a `forbidden` rule (`N3`, `T6`, `E10`) that reflect real business execution rather than extraction errors.
- **Missing mandatory behavior**: Absence of a `mandatory` relation (`N1`, `N4`, `T1`, `T2`, `E1`–`E8`) when its scope applies and the gap is neither an abstraction artifact nor a logging defect.
- **Unexpected but unregulated behavior**: Edges marked `unspecified` (`N6`, `T4`, `T5`, `E9`) or omitted from the norm (`Validate Order -> Issue Invoice`, `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review`, `Manual Compliance Review -> Customer`, `(Package, object_codeath, Delivery)`), which are benign under open-world semantics.
- **Typical-pattern absence**: Non-occurrence of a `typical` relation (`N2`, `N5`, `T3`). Because `typical` paths allow business exceptions (write-offs, open credit terms, unapplied cash, intermediate events), departures are operational ratios, not violations.
- **Likely data-quality error**: Apparent breaches of `mandatory` or `forbidden` rules caused by ETL key collisions (`E10`), unmaterialized attributes (`E6`), missing subtype labels (`E8`), or clock skew.
- **Abstraction mismatch**: Divergence between a business rule and its graph projection—approximating an *eventually-follows* rule (`N1`, `N4`) with a *directly-follows* OC-DFG edge broken by valid intermediate events, or checking event-specific rules (`T1`, `T2`) on log-wide OTG counts.
- **Insufficient evidence**: Summary counts that cannot separate compliance from violation without per-event cardinalities, full traces, or aging windows (whether 14 open invoices in `N5` are overdue, or whether `E2` vs. `E3` reflects orphaned items or multi-item events).

**Open-world vs. closed-world assumptions**: Classical conformance assumes a **closed world** where any unmodeled edge is a violation. This norm is **closed-world only for `forbidden` rules (`N3`, `T6`, `E10`) and in-scope `mandatory` obligations (`N1`, `N4`, `T1`, `T2`, `E1`–`E8`)**, and **open-world everywhere else**: `unspecified` rules, `typical` exceptions, and unmodeled edges are valid unless they breach an external policy.

## 3. Detailed reasoning by model family

### OC-DFG

- **Capabilities and limits**: An OC-DFG projects event sequences onto individual object types, showing whether activity $B$ immediately followed activity $A$ in an object's trace. It catches forbidden sequential orderings (`N3`: `Cancel Order -> Dispatch Package` on 2 Orders) and measures direct transition counts (`N1`, `N2`, `N4`, `N5`). It cannot establish multi-hop *eventually-follows* reachability ($A \implies \Diamond B$), cannot tell whether a missing direct edge stems from a benign intermediate step or total omission of $B$, and cannot separate physical sequence errors from clock skew.
- **Danger of interpreting an "eventually expected" rule as directly-follows**: Norm `N1` requires that every newly created standard Order *eventually* enter validation, but approximates this with direct `Create Order -> Validate Order` (98/100). When a valid intermediate event attaches to `Order`—such as `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review` (3 Orders) or an unrecorded review—the trace becomes `Create Order -> Review -> Validate Order`. Eventual validation is satisfied, yet the direct edge disappears, creating a false violation. The same distortion affects `N2`: because `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice` (72 Orders) or fulfillment events intervene before closure, direct `Validate Order -> Close Order` drops to 83 Orders without any process failure. Likewise, in `N4`, `Pack Package -> Dispatch Package` appears on 198/200 externally shipped Packages even though `ET–OT` (`E4`=200, `E5`=200) proves all 200 executed both events; the 2 missing direct edges reflect an intervening step or inverted scan timestamps.
- **Counts, scopes, and cross-abstraction tension**: In `N4`, the observed population ("200 externally shipped Packages") matches the mandatory scope, making the 2-package gap actionable. In `N5` (`typical`), the 110 Invoices split into 92 paid, 4 written off, and 14 open ($92+4+14=110$). Thus, `N4` is conforming in `ET–OT` (`E4`, `E5`) yet suspicious in `OC-DFG` (198/200), while `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review` (3) looks unmodeled in `OC-DFG` but aligns with `OTG` (`T5`=3) and `ET–OT` (`E9`=3).

### OTG

- **Capabilities and limits**: An OTG captures log-wide lifecycle couplings between object types (`object_interaction`, `object_cobirth`, `object_codeath`). It catches lifecycle-boundary violations like `T6` (`(Order, object_cobirth, Payment)` = 1) and reveals unmodeled lifecycle endings like `(Package, object_codeath, Delivery)` = 7. Because OTG collapses the event dimension, it cannot establish *which event type* generated an interaction or whether it occurred at the mandatory process step.
- **Counts, scopes, and cross-abstraction tension**: Norms `T1` and `T2` specify event-level scopes ("Required for an Add Item interaction"; "Required for a Dispatch Package interaction"), whereas OTG weights (`T1`=1450, `T2`=200) aggregate across all events. Consequently, `(Package, object_interaction, Delivery)` appears 100% conforming in `OTG` (**200**), masking the suspicious `ET–OT` deficit in `E6` (`Dispatch Package -> Delivery` = **198**), because those 2 `Package`–`Delivery` pairs interacted in *another* event (e.g., confirmation) rather than at dispatch. Similarly, `T1` (**1450**) looks conforming in `OTG` while masking the 2-unit deficit in `ET–OT` `E2` (`Add Item -> Order` = **1448**). Edge `T3` (92) aligns with `N5` (92 paid invoices) and `E7`/`E8` (1 unapplied cash payment), while `T4` (100), `T5` (3), and codeath (7) are benign open-world patterns.

### ET–OT

- **Capabilities and limits**: An ET–OT graph measures participation counts between event types and object types, checking mandatory bindings (`E1`–`E8`) and exposing forbidden cross-domain links like `E10` (`Sensor Heartbeat -> Invoice` = 1). It cannot establish temporal ordering (missing `N3` and `N4`), cannot separate missing foreign keys from $1:N$ object-per-event cardinality without instance distributions, and cannot see when a missing object link resides in an event attribute (`E6`) or stems from a missing subtype label (`E8`).
- **Counts, scopes, and cross-abstraction tension**: Comparing companion mandatory rows exposes three discrepancies: (1) `Add Item`: `E3` (`Item`=1450) vs. `E2` (`Order`=1448), meaning either 2 single-item events omitted an `Order` link or 2 of 1,448 valid events attached 2 `Item` objects; (2) `Dispatch Package`: `E5` (`Package`=200) vs. `E6` (`Delivery`=198), where 2 events lack a `Delivery` object link despite storing a carrier reference attribute (suspicious in `ET–OT`, masked in `OTG` `T2`=200); and (3) `Record Payment`: `E7` (`Payment`=93) vs. `E8` (`Invoice`=92), where 1 unapplied-cash payment (`T3`) violates `E8` due to missing explicit typing. Meanwhile, `Manual Compliance Review -> Customer` (3) is benign under open-world rules (`E9`, `T4`).

## 4. Mandatory, forbidden, typical, and unregulated behavior

- **Mandatory**: Satisfied on `E1` (100), `E3` (1450), `E4` (200), `E5` (200), `E7` (93), and OTG `T1` (1450), `T2` (200). `N1` (`Create Order -> Validate Order`: 98/100) is **abstraction mismatch (compliant)** *if* the 2 Orders validated after review or were non-standard, and **missing mandatory** *if* standard orders never validated. `N4` (`Pack Package -> Dispatch Package`: 198/200) is **abstraction mismatch** *if* a package step intervened, **data-quality error** *if* timestamps inverted, and **missing mandatory** *if* dispatch preceded packing. `E2` (`Add Item -> Order`: 1448 vs. `E3`=1450) is **missing mandatory** *if* 2 events lacked an `Order`, and **conforming** *if* 2 of 1,448 events attached 2 `Item` objects. `E6` (`Dispatch Package -> Delivery`: 198/200) is formally **missing mandatory**, operationally a **data-quality error** (unmaterialized carrier attribute). `E8` (`Record Payment -> Invoice`: 92/93) is formally **missing mandatory** under `E8`, shifting to **typical-pattern absence (`T3`) + labeling omission** *if* confirmed as untyped unapplied cash.
- **Forbidden**: `N3` (`Cancel Order -> Dispatch Package`: 2) is **forbidden behavior** *if* shipment followed cancellation, and **data-quality error** *if* caused by clock skew/batch lag. `T6` (`(Order, object_cobirth, Payment)`: 1) is **forbidden behavior** *if* creation and settlement were merged in execution, and **data-quality artifact** *if* ETL bundled transactions or log slicing hid an earlier birth. `E10` (`Sensor Heartbeat -> Invoice`: 1) is syntactically **forbidden**, substantively a **data-quality error** (numeric ID collision).
- **Typical (and typical-pattern absence)**: `N2` (`Validate Order -> Close Order`: 83) shows **typical-pattern absence / abstraction mismatch** due to intermediate `Issue Invoice` (72), fulfillment, or review events. `N5` & `T3` (`Issue Invoice -> Record Payment`: 92/110; `(Invoice, object_interaction, Payment)`: 92) show **typical-pattern absence** for 18 unpaid invoices: 4 write-offs (`Issue Invoice -> Write Off`) and 14 open (`Issue Invoice with no observed successor`), shifting from **compliant credit terms** to **delinquency** *if* payment terms expired.
- **Unregulated**: Explicitly `unspecified` edges `N6` (`Reopen Order -> Close Order`: 1), `T4` (`(Order, object_interaction, Customer)`: 100), `T5` (`(Order, object_interaction, RiskCase)`: 3), `E9` (`Manual Compliance Review -> RiskCase`: 3), and unmodeled edges `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review` (3), `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice` (72), `Issue Invoice -> Write Off` (4), `(Package, object_codeath, Delivery)` (7), and `Manual Compliance Review -> Customer` (3) are benign unless an external policy (`T4`) is breached.

## 5. PM4Py conformance code sketch

```python
from copy import deepcopy
import pm4py


def run_normative_oc_conformance(ocel, norm_ocdfg, norm_otg, norm_etot):
    """
    Discover object-centric abstractions, run PM4Py structural conformance,
    preserve raw PM4Py outputs intact, and attach a separate semantic
    classification layer aware of open/closed-world normative statuses.
    """
    # 1. Discover observed graph abstractions from the OCEL
    obs_ocdfg = pm4py.discover_ocdfg(ocel)
    obs_otg = pm4py.discover_otg(ocel)
    obs_etot = pm4py.discover_etot(ocel)

    # 2. Run raw PM4Py conformance checks against bare normative structures
    raw_conf_ocdfg = pm4py.conformance_ocdfg(obs_ocdfg, norm_ocdfg)
    raw_conf_otg = pm4py.conformance_otg(obs_otg, norm_otg)
    raw_conf_etot = pm4py.conformance_etot(obs_etot, norm_etot)

    # Preserve pristine copies of PM4Py diagnostic results (never overwrite)
    raw_diagnostics = {
        "discovered": {
            "ocdfg": obs_ocdfg,
            "otg": obs_otg,
            "etot": obs_etot,
        },
        "conformance": {
            "ocdfg": deepcopy(raw_conf_ocdfg),
            "otg": deepcopy(raw_conf_otg),
            "etot": deepcopy(raw_conf_etot),
        },
    }

    # NOTE: Bare PM4Py graph structures (norm_ocdfg dict, norm_otg tuple,
    # norm_etot tuple) only store structural edges/weights. They CANNOT store
    # deontic status (mandatory, forbidden, typical, unspecified), conditional
    # scopes, or abstraction semantics (directly-follows vs. eventually-follows).
    # Therefore, normative metadata MUST be maintained in an external registry
    # outside the bare graph structures.
    norm_metadata_registry = {
        "ocdfg": {
            ("Order", ("Create Order", "Validate Order")): {
                "id": "N1", "status": "mandatory", "semantics": "eventually_approximated_as_df",
                "scope": "standard newly created orders", "denom": 100,
            },
            ("Order", ("Validate Order", "Close Order")): {
                "id": "N2", "status": "typical", "semantics": "df_with_unmodeled_intermediates",
                "scope": "validated orders", "denom": 100,
            },
            ("Order", ("Cancel Order", "Dispatch Package")): {
                "id": "N3", "status": "forbidden", "semantics": "strict_negative",
                "scope": "cancelled orders",
            },
            ("Package", ("Pack Package", "Dispatch Package")): {
                "id": "N4", "status": "mandatory", "semantics": "df_external_shipment",
                "scope": "externally shipped packages", "denom": 200,
            },
            ("Invoice", ("Issue Invoice", "Record Payment")): {
                "id": "N5", "status": "typical", "semantics": "exceptions_allowed",
                "scope": "issued invoices (excl. write-offs and open credit terms)", "denom": 110,
            },
            ("Order", ("Reopen Order", "Close Order")): {
                "id": "N6", "status": "unspecified", "semantics": "open_world",
                "scope": "outside norm coverage",
            },
        },
        "otg": {
            ("Order", "object_interaction", "Item"): {
                "id": "T1", "status": "mandatory", "scope": "Add Item interaction",
            },
            ("Package", "object_interaction", "Delivery"): {
                "id": "T2", "status": "mandatory", "scope": "Dispatch Package interaction",
            },
            ("Invoice", "object_interaction", "Payment"): {
                "id": "T3", "status": "typical", "scope": "unapplied cash allowed",
            },
            ("Order", "object_interaction", "Customer"): {
                "id": "T4", "status": "unspecified", "scope": "external customer policy",
            },
            ("Order", "object_interaction", "RiskCase"): {
                "id": "T5", "status": "unspecified", "scope": "risk review allowed",
            },
            ("Order", "object_cobirth", "Payment"): {
                "id": "T6", "status": "forbidden", "scope": "all business events",
            },
        },
        "etot": {
            ("Create Order", "Order"): {"id": "E1", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 100},
            ("Add Item", "Order"): {"id": "E2", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 1450},
            ("Add Item", "Item"): {"id": "E3", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 1450},
            ("Pack Package", "Package"): {"id": "E4", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 200},
            ("Dispatch Package", "Package"): {"id": "E5", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 200},
            ("Dispatch Package", "Delivery"): {
                "id": "E6", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 200,
                "dq_note": "check carrier attribute if Delivery object missing",
            },
            ("Record Payment", "Payment"): {"id": "E7", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 93},
            ("Record Payment", "Invoice"): {
                "id": "E8", "status": "mandatory", "expected": 93,
                "dq_note": "check unapplied cash event typing omission",
            },
            ("Manual Compliance Review", "RiskCase"): {"id": "E9", "status": "unspecified"},
            ("Sensor Heartbeat", "Invoice"): {
                "id": "E10", "status": "forbidden",
                "dq_note": "suspected numeric ID join collision",
            },
        },
    }

    def classify_relation(family, key, observed_count):
        meta = norm_metadata_registry[family].get(key)
        # Open-world default for edges absent from the normative specification
        if meta is None:
            return {
                "norm_id": None,
                "status": "unspecified_unmodeled",
                "classification": "unexpected_but_unregulated_behavior",
                "actionable_violation": False,
                "observed_count": observed_count,
            }

        status = meta["status"]
        norm_id = meta["id"]

        if status == "forbidden":
            classification = (
                "likely_data_quality_error_or_forbidden_behavior"
                if "dq_note" in meta and observed_count > 0
                else ("forbidden_behavior" if observed_count > 0 else "conforming_absence")
            )
            return {
                "norm_id": norm_id,
                "status": status,
                "classification": classification,
                "actionable_violation": observed_count > 0,
                "observed_count": observed_count,
                "note": meta.get("dq_note", meta.get("scope")),
            }

        if status == "mandatory":
            expected = meta.get("expected", meta.get("denom", observed_count))
            has_gap = observed_count < expected
            if not has_gap:
                classification = "conforming_mandatory"
            elif meta.get("semantics") == "eventually_approximated_as_df":
                classification = "abstraction_mismatch_or_missing_mandatory"
            elif "dq_note" in meta:
                classification = "likely_data_quality_error_or_missing_mandatory"
            else:
                classification = "missing_mandatory_behavior_or_abstraction_mismatch"
            return {
                "norm_id": norm_id,
                "status": status,
                "classification": classification,
                "actionable_violation": has_gap,
                "observed_count": observed_count,
                "expected_in_scope": expected,
                "note": meta.get("dq_note", meta.get("scope")),
            }

        if status == "typical":
            return {
                "norm_id": norm_id,
                "status": status,
                "classification": "typical_pattern_or_legitimate_exception",
                "actionable_violation": False,
                "observed_count": observed_count,
                "note": meta.get("scope"),
            }

        # Explicitly unspecified
        return {
            "norm_id": norm_id,
            "status": status,
            "classification": "unexpected_but_unregulated_behavior",
            "actionable_violation": False,
            "observed_count": observed_count,
            "note": meta.get("scope"),
        }

    # 3. Build semantic interpretation layer alongside raw PM4Py outputs
    semantic_layer = {"ocdfg": {}, "otg": {}, "etot": {}}
    for family in ("ocdfg", "otg", "etot"):
        for key, meta in norm_metadata_registry[family].items():
            semantic_layer[family][key] = classify_relation(family, key, observed_count=0)

    return {
        "raw_pm4py": raw_diagnostics,
        "semantic_interpretation": semantic_layer,
    }
```

## 6. Norm-completeness assessment

1. **Directly-follows vs. eventually-follows (`N1`, `N2`, `N4`)**: Direct-edge approximations trigger false alarms when valid intermediate events (`Manual Compliance Review`, `Issue Invoice`, package QA) occur. *Clarification*: Make `N1` and `N4` eventually-follows (**mandatory**) and add `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice` (**typical**).
2. **Unapplied-cash typing mismatch (`T3` vs. `E8`)**: `T3` permits unapplied cash (`typical`), but `E8` requires `Record Payment -> Invoice` unless typed as unapplied cash without defining the schema. *Clarification*: Add a `Record Unapplied Cash` event type (**mandatory** `Payment` link, **forbidden** `Invoice` link at receipt).
3. **Unregulated joint termination (`(Package, object_codeath, Delivery)`)**: Seven pairs end in a shared confirmation event while 193 end separately, skewing duration metrics. *Clarification*: Define shared confirmation codeath as **typical** (or **explicitly unspecified** by carrier).
4. **Incomplete `Manual Compliance Review` signature (`E9`, `T4`, `T5`)**: `E9` lists only `RiskCase` (`unspecified`), omitting observed `Customer` (3) and `Order` (3) links. *Clarification*: Keep review trigger **explicitly unspecified**, but make `RiskCase`, `Order`, and `Customer` links **mandatory** when executed.
5. **Missing per-event cardinality bounds (`T1`, `T2`, `E2`, `E3`, `E6`)**: Log-wide OTG counts (`T1`=1450, `T2`=200) mask event-level gaps (`E2`=1448, `E6`=198) and $1:N$ item ambiguity. *Clarification*: Make per-event cardinalities ($\text{Order}=1, \text{Item}\ge 1$ on `Add Item`; materialized $\text{Package}=1, \text{Delivery}=1$ on `Dispatch Package`) **mandatory**.
6. **Unbounded invoice aging and omitted write-off edge (`N5`)**: `N5` omits `Issue Invoice -> Write Off` (4) from the table and sets no aging limit for 14 open invoices. *Clarification*: Add `Issue Invoice -> Write Off` (**typical**) and bounded SLA resolution (**mandatory**).
7. **Missing key-isolation gates and tolerance bands (`E10`, `T6`)**: ETL numeric ID collisions (`E10`=1) mix with operational breaches (`N3`=2). *Clarification*: Make type-prefixed IDs **mandatory** and keep `E10`/`T6` **forbidden**.

## 7. Investigation and remediation plan

- **Immediate containment**: Prioritize **Rank 1 (`N3`: 2 Orders with `Cancel Order -> Dispatch Package`)** by identifying both cancelled Orders and shipments immediately, intercepting in-transit packages with the carrier, or initiating return/recovery and freezing billing. Prioritize **Rank 2 (`T6`: 1 `(Order, object_cobirth, Payment)` and `E2`: 2 missing `Add Item -> Order` links)** by quarantining the cobirth Order/Payment transaction to verify authorization and checking whether 2 `Item` objects lacking an `Order` link (`E2` vs. `E3`) are queued for unbilled picking. Do **not** escalate benign rare behavior (`Reopen Order -> Close Order`=1, `Manual Compliance Review`=3, `object_codeath`=7, `Write Off`=4).
- **Data repair**: Prefix object IDs by type before ETL joins to eliminate the `Sensor Heartbeat -> Invoice` (1, `E10`) collision; materialize and link `Delivery` objects from carrier attributes on the 2 `Dispatch Package` events (`E6`, `T2`); and after Finance confirmation, tag the 93rd `Record Payment` event as unapplied cash (`E8`, `T3`).
