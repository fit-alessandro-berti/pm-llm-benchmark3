5. Rebound and displacement risks  
1. **Batching rebound:** Dynamic batching caps (I2) avoid excessive wait times but may cause smaller batch releases, increasing energy per item compared with large uncapped batches. If caps are set too tight, the energy benefit is lost and scrap may rise because underfilled batches produce fewer “good” items per run.  
2. **Transport mode substitution:** Replacing air with ground (I4) or using consolidation (I5) may increase late deliveries in borderline cases; customers who experience delays could escalate and demand dedicated vans or emergency air for subsequent orders, shifting emissions to higherintensity modes.  
3. **Capacity congestion from CNC_A shift:** Shifting MTO volume to CNC_A (I3) can overload that machine, lengthening queues. To maintain service, more orders may need to be expedited via CNC_B or air freight, eroding the energy and rework savings and increasing transport.  
4. **Packaging material upsurge:** If packagingroute compatibility checks (I6) strictly enforce reinforced packaging rules (Q1) that were previously ignored, the total package material weight may rise, offsetting avoided rework packaging waste. The net material footprint must be monitored.  
5. **Obsolescence from promisedate extension:** Carbonaware promise negotiation (I9) delays order shipment by several days. If demand forecasts are wrong, more batchproduced items could become obsolete before shipment, increasing material scrap and wasted energy already embedded in those items.  
6. **Customer behavioral displacement:** When customers see longer promised lead times for Regular MTO, they may reclassify themselves as Servicecontract (if possible) or declare orders as “precautionary urgent,” undermining the Criticaldowntime split (I8) and increasing dedicatedvan usage. Governance must prevent such misclassification.

A coherent multilever redesign package, rather than isolated green ideas, is necessary to reduce environmental impact while safeguarding service, quality, and resilience. The recommended package is:

**D. Demandside adjustments**

**E. Governance and exception handling**
