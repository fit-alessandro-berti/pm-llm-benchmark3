# 1. Fairness dimensions

Raw approval rates differ significantly (Group A: 78% vs. Group D: 58%), but these gaps narrow after adjusting for case mix (e.g., Group D’s adjusted approval is -3.1pp). Outcome parity alone is insufficient to assess fairness, as disparities may stem from legitimate complexity or procedural burdens.

- **Approval**: Group B (-5.5pp), Group C (-2.2pp), Group D (-3.1pp).

- **Supervisor Review**: Group B (+8.0pp), Group C (+5.5pp).

- **Support steps**: Language Support Contact (Group B) and Accommodation Review (Group C) add mandatory steps that are **rights-preserving** but introduce delays (e.g., 3.8d wait for Language Support, 4.7d for Accommodation Review).

### **Resource-allocation fairness**

- **Specialized queues**: Accommodation Review and Fraud Review queues may **deprioritize** these cases due to limited staff.
- **Regional disparities**: Two regions have backlogs in Supervisor Review, disproportionately affecting Group B and C.

# 2. Adjusted comparison strategy

### **Matching/stratification variables**
- **Case complexity**: Stratify by `risk_score_band`, `documentation_completeness`, `household_size_band`, `prior_case_count`.
- **Channel/region**: Control for `channel` (in-person vs. online) and `region` (to account for regional backlogs).
- **Support needs**: Stratify by `language_preference` and `disability_accommodation_requested` to isolate the impact of rights-preserving steps.

### **Path/variant comparison**
- Compare **cycle time and approval rates** across variants (V1–V6) **within risk/documentation strata**.
  - Example: For low-risk, complete-documentation cases, does Group B still take longer in V4 vs. Group A in V1?
- Identify variants with **disproportionate handovers** (e.g., V4, V5) and test if they correlate with worse outcomes.

### **Resource-assignment comparison**
- **Workload analysis**: Use `pm4py.discover_working_together_network` to check if bilingual/accommodation specialists are **overloaded**.
- **Handover analysis**: Use `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network` to quantify **extra transitions** for Groups B/C.
- **Resource similarity**: Use `pm4py.discover_activity_based_resource_similarity` to see if certain caseworkers are **systematically assigned** to slower paths.

### **Waiting-time decomposition**
- Use `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` to split **service time** (caseworker action) vs. **waiting time** (queue delays).
- Compare **waiting times at each step** (e.g., `Submit  Identity Verification`) across groups, controlling for channel.
- Test if **rights-preserving steps** (Language Support, Accommodation Review) have **longer waits** than other steps.

### **Sensitivity analysis for biased covariates**
- **Risk score bias**: Re-run adjusted models **excluding `risk_score_band`** to test if it drives disparities.
- **Documentation completeness**: Check if `documentation_completeness` is **endogenous** (e.g., Group B may struggle with forms due to language barriers).
- **Historical bias**: Simulate **counterfactual risk scores** (e.g., assume Group D’s risk is overestimated by 10%) to test robustness.

### **Subgroup checks by region and channel**
- **Region-channel interaction**: Test if in-person applicants in **specific regions** face worse delays (e.g., Group B in regions with bilingual caseworker shortages).
- **Channel fairness**: Compare online vs. in-person **within groups** to isolate channel effects.
