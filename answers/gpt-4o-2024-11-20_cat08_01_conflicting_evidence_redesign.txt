## 1. Current-process diagnosis

The warranty process currently shows a fundamental trade-off between speed and cost versus quality, compliance, and customer satisfaction. The process-mining evidence reveals several problematic dynamics:

### **Main problems:**
1. **Speed/cost advantages of the fast path create downstream risks**: The most frequent path, **START -> Intake claim -> Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement -> Quality/compliance close**, is fast (median ~5 hours) and relatively cheap (EUR 0.20 for auto-screening versus manual labor). However, it directly leads to:
   - Customer complaints/reopens (9.2% overall; **14.1% for Fast approval cases**).
   - High compliance risks due to frequent violations of **R1 (missing manual or risk review for high-value cases)** and **R2 (dispatch before evidence completion)**, especially dangerous for high-value device claims. Alignment diagnostics show frequent **move-on-log deviations** at these points.
   - Costlier downstream resolutions, with rework eroding its initial cost savings (only **9% cheaper after rework**, despite being **31% cheaper upfront**).

2. **Reviewed paths improve quality but are too slow and resource-intensive**:
   - Manual eligibility reviews (median 5–26 hours initial  up to **100 hours after repeated evidence requests**) are bottlenecked due to labor capacity constraints, with queues exceeding tolerable limits at higher utilization rates.
   - Frequent loops such as **Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review** (26% of repeated use) further delay these paths.

3. **Inadequate handling of specific customer and claim segments**:
   - **Premium customers** (e.g., Premium low-risk accessories) demand strict service-level agreements (SLAs), and their claims are heavily penalized by delays.
   - High-value device claims, though better suited for manual review, frequently bypass compliance requirements (violations of **R1** and **R2**) on the Fast approval route, producing customer complaints and costlier corrections.

4. **Rework linked to poor part selection and missing evidence**:
   - **Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen** is frequently observed in the temporal profile (96 hours mean, **p90 = 180 hours**).
   - **Rework repair** is disproportionately common for **high-value devices (12.5%)**.

5. **Lack of cohesiveness in evidence handling**:
   - Requests for missing evidence are repeated inappropriately in many cases, leading to a high abandonment rate for unclear-defect or low-digital-literacy segments. This creates churn instead of moving cases to resolution efficiently (**26% repeated evidence requests**).

### **Contradiction Explanation**
The fast path is appealing on speed and cost (median intake-to-approval 2–3 hours), but **alignment diagnostics** and **complaint/reopen data** show it compromises downstream quality, compliance, and customer satisfaction (e.g., Premium high-value devices: **22% complaints on fast path vs. 5% on review/repair path**). In contrast, the slower reviewed paths improve outcomes but induce significant delays, bottleneck pressures, and abandonment for some customers (e.g., **Unclear defect/missing evidence segment with 140-hour median cycle time and highest abandonment rates**).

## 2. Redesign A: **Segmented Risk-Aware Process with Improved Auto-Screening**

### **Description**
- **Targets**: Low-risk accessory segments and unresolved compliance/rework risks for high-value segments.
- **Process flow changes**:
  1. **Upgraded auto-screen**: Deploy new, segment-aware rules prioritizing fast approval for **low-risk cases** while routing **high-value or unclear-defect cases** to enhanced Manual eligibility review or Fraud/risk review.
  2. **Lightweight pre-dispatch quality gate**: Add a validation checkpoint for replacement dispatch, focusing on high-value devices to prevent mis-shipments.
  3. **Evidence portal**: Introduce a new digital portal to consolidate evidence submissions upfront.
- **Mechanism**:
  - Improved auto-screen ensures compliance adherence (no more R1/R2 violations for high-value cases) while not overwhelming Manual review unnecessarily.
  - Quality gate stops wrong replacements early, reducing complaints and rework.
  - Evidence portal minimizes repeated evidence requests (30–45% reduction).

### **Deliberate sacrifices or unresolved issues**
- Partial increase in Manual eligibility review volumes (+15–35%).
- Abandonment may increase for low-digital-literacy customers who struggle with the evidence portal.

## 3. Redesign B: **Split Processes for Premium vs. Standard Segments**

### **Description**
- **Targets**: Premium customers (both low-risk and high-value) and Standard customers differently to resolve SLA vs. resource bottlenecks.
- **Process flow changes**:
  1. **Dedicated Premium path**: Premium claims bypass Manual eligibility review for faster SLAs. Instead:
     - **Pre-screen via fraud/risk specialists** to directly route low-risk Premium cases for Fast approval.
     - High-value Premium cases transition directly to Repair quote.
  2. **Batch scheduling for Standard claims**: Standard claims are queued for resource-efficient, slower Manual reviews or Repair quotes, optimizing throughput.
  3. No changes to evidence handling; focus is strictly on segment differentiation.
- **Mechanism**:
  - Premium cases receive tailored fast-track routing for SLA compliance.
  - Standard claims proceed through lower-cost parallel reviews.

### **Deliberate sacrifices or unresolved issues**
- Higher compliance risks linked to Premium fast-track (Premium high-value devices may bypass manual reviews).
- Repair-tech queue delays persist for Standard high-value cases.

---

## 4. Redesign C: **Dynamic Resource Pool and Priority Rules**

### **Description**
- **Targets**: Resource bottlenecks (reviewer/utilization pressure) and rework-related quality issues.
- **Process flow changes**:
  1. **Dynamic reviewer allocations**:
     - Merge Manual eligibility and Fraud/risk review labor pools, dynamically reallocating resources based on case priority (e.g., Premium, high-value).
  2. **First-touch repair inspections**: Technicians pre-inspect all high-value devices before Repair quote to reduce rework repair.
  3. **Simplified escalations**: Employ triage to reroute reopening cases to faster resolutions; fewer Escalation board referrals.
- **Mechanism**:
  - Flexibly redeployed staff reduce bottlenecks in review queues.
  - Pre-inspections minimize downstream repair rework by catching issues earlier.

### **Deliberate sacrifices or unresolved issues**
- High implementation complexity from pooled resource design.
- Risk of under-serving low-priority Standard cases during peak Premium demand.

## 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

### Redesign A
- **Failure mode 1**: Evidence portal increases abandonment in low-digital-literacy Standard segments.
- **Failure mode 2**: Pre-dispatch gate delays Premium replacements.
- **New bottleneck**: Manual eligibility queue may grow faster due to increased routing.

### Redesign B
- **Failure mode 1**: Premium fast-track increases mis-approvals for high-value devices.
- **Failure mode 2**: SLA adherence fails during fraud specialist capacity shortages.
- **New bottleneck**: Repair-tech bottleneck for Standard backlog cases.

### Redesign C
- **Failure mode 1**: Staffing pool coordination causes delays during high case inflow.
- **Failure mode 2**: Pre-inspections make Premium repairs slower instead of faster.
- **New bottleneck**: Technician capacity stretched thin for pre-inspections.

## 7. Recommended experiment sequence

1. **Pilot Redesign A (Evidence portal + new auto-screen rules)**:
   - **Metrics**: Time to Fast approval, manual-review inflow, evidence-request rate, abandonment rate, reopened cases.
   - **Stop/go thresholds**:
     - Abandonment not to rise >5% overall.
     - Reduce complaints/reopened cases by 10%+ for high-value claims.
   - **Sequence**: Test first with Standard low-risk accessory cases (largest share).

2. **Pilot Redesign B**:
   - Sequential Premium-only fast path for low-risk claims (verify SLA adherence).
   - Monitor escalations and mis-replacement rates.

3. **Pilot Redesign C**:
   - Dynamic staffing model tested only during peak demand windows.

Each pilot builds on earlier ones, with downstream complaint/rework tracking validating real vs. shifted improvements.
