### Rank 2: Wait for S4 -> Perform second-officer review

   - **Key uncertainty:** S2 availability and interruptions to P-901; low predictability for SLA breach.

### Rank 4: Reject now
1. **Immediate rejection (~10–20m):** Avoids compliance risk but incurs near-total customer harm for P-884, including potential business retention concerns.
   - **Key uncertainty:** Whether all mandatory rejection thresholds (validation of unresolved fuzzy match etc.) are met.

### Rank 5: Request more evidence

## 3. Benefit and harm reasoning

- **Operational losses (P-884):** Resolving within 90 minutes captures €20,000–€35,000 value. Delays risk severe SLA breach (~€15,000–€80,000 harm).
- **Compliance/regulatory harm:** Unlawful execution is highly asymmetric (€100,000–€500,000 and reputational/legal harm). A shortcut like Actions A or B cannot be justified for monetary loss avoidance due to these enormities.
- **Spillover to P-901:** Interrupting S2 creates additional SLA breach risks (€5,000–€20,000 downstream harm). Waiting for S4 or alternate-screening strategies mitigate this risk.

### Dominance arguments:
- Conformance prioritizes maintaining second-officer independence.
- Shortcuts offer faster resolution but are nonconformant and disproportionally risky.
- Customer harm weighs heavily but must not outweigh critical regulatory obligations.
