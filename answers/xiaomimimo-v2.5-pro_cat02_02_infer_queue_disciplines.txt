## QA

**Supporting (FIFO):**
- A02 selected at 08:30 over A04 (P1, est 7): FIFO , priority , SPT . *Most discriminating single decision in QA.*
- A05 selected at 09:05 over A08 (P1, est 25) and A07 (est 9): FIFO .
- A06 selected at 09:20 over A08 (P1) and A07 (est 9): FIFO  (also appointment-overdue ).
- A07 selected at 09:32 over A08 (P1): FIFO .

**Supporting (Appointment):**
- A03 starts exactly at 08:20 appointment time despite arriving at 08:08. A03 jumps ahead of A02 (earlier arrival).

**Contradicting (FIFO):**
- A03 selected over A02 at 08:20. **Resolution:** Appointment scheduling explains this as an override. Alternatively, SPT (est 10 vs 20) explains it. Since SPT is rejected elsewhere, appointment is the preferred explanation.

**Contradicting (Appointment):**
- A06's appointment (09:15) starts late at 09:20. **Resolution:** A1 was busy with A05 (09:05–09:20). Appointment was intended but could not be honoured exactly. This is consistent with "honour appointment if resource is free, else first-available."

## QB

**Supporting (FIFO + P1 override):**
- B04 selected at 10:28 over B06 (P2) and B07 (shorter est, overdue appointment): FIFO .
- B05 (P1) jumps queue at 10:00 over B01 and B04: P1 override .
- B02 (P1) preempts B01 at 09:16: P1 preemptive .
- B01 resumes at 09:31 and 10:14: resume-preempted-before-new-dispatch .

**Contradicting (pure FIFO):**
- B05 (arr 09:46) selected at 10:00 over B01 (arr 09:00) and B04 (arr 09:35). FIFO would pick B01. **Resolution:** P1 override.

**Contradicting (P1-only preemption):**
- B01 preempted at 09:48 for B03 (P2), labelled "dispatcher reassignment." No P1 trigger. **Resolution:** Likely manual dispatcher intervention. B05 (P1) arrived at 09:46; its arrival scan may have triggered a preemption flag, with B03 dispatched as a timing artefact. Alternatively, the dispatcher judged B03 had waited long enough (28 min).

**Contradicting (appointment scheduling):**
- B07's appointment (10:30) was not honoured; B07 starts at 10:56. **Resolution:** QB's primary discipline is FIFO (not appointment-aware). B07 was behind B04 (arr 09:35) and B06 (arr 10:05) in FIFO order.
