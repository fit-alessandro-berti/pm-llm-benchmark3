## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method
**Assumptions:**
- **Authority Hierarchy:** Board-approved policies (GPIP-4) outrank local SOPs (DE-AAC-PROC-17) unless the latter impose *stricter* controls (GPIP-4 A5). Incident directives (ECD-2) are temporary overlays with *issue-specific* precedence (ECD-2 C4) but do not implicitly override segregation of duties (GPIP-4 A1) or Compliance_Clearance (GPIP-4 A3) unless explicitly stated.
- **Temporal Priority:** Later-in-time rules (ECD-2, CFO email) apply *only* within their effective windows and stated scopes. Post-expiry actions revert to prior norms.
- **Emergency Necessity:** "Critical" in ECD-2 is interpreted as *imminent production stoppage or safety harm* (aligned with DE-AAC-PROC-17 B4). Backup-line components (P-503) are *not* presumptively critical unless evidence shows 6-hour risk.
- **Evidence:** Contemporaneous records (e.g., telephone logs, signed PDFs) override ERP timestamps during System Outage ON-44. Late database entries are treated as *logging defects*, not compliance failures, if stronger evidence exists.
- **Burden of Proof:** The organization bears the burden to prove compliance. Ambiguities (e.g., ECD-2’s silence on GPIP-4 A1/A3) are resolved against waiver of core controls.

**Facts (not assumptions):**
- ECD-2 was *not* ratified by the Chief Risk Officer (per organizational facts).
- The CFO’s email (D) has authority over *Finance* controls but not Compliance or segregation of duties (D3).
- DE-AAC-PROC-17 B6 requires emergency forms within 24 hours of *commitment*, not authorization.

**Method:**
1. For each case, identify the *earliest controlling act* (e.g., Commit_to_Supplier) and its timestamp.
2. Apply norms effective at that time, prioritizing:
   - Issue-specific directives (ECD-2 for supplier/timing)  Local SOP (DE-AAC-PROC-17)  Global policy (GPIP-4).
   - Explicit overrides (ECD-2 C4) only for stated issues (supplier onboarding, PO timing).
3. Classify deviations as:
   - **Policy breach**: Missing a required control with no valid exception.
   - **Logging defect**: Control performed but not recorded timely.
   - **Justified exception**: Valid override (e.g., ECD-2 C2 for new suppliers) *and* all override conditions met.

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency and Remediation

**Policy Breaches (Requiring Corrective Action):**
- **P-503**: Segregation of duties breach (PROC-3 as requester/committer/authorizer). *Remediation*: Retrospective independent review; disciplinary action if intentional.
- **P-504**: Unapproved supplier + no Incident Command auth. *Remediation*: Supplier onboarding; training on emergency scope.
- **P-506**: Missing budget owner approval post-ECD-2 expiry. *Remediation*: Obtain retroactive approval or document as a control failure.

**Logging Defects (Process Improvements):**
- **P-501**: Missing delegation record for SHIFT-1. *Remediation*: Formalize emergency delegation protocols.
- **P-505**: ERP timestamp discrepancy. *Remediation*: Calibrate ERP clocks; prioritize external evidence (e.g., supplier acks).
- **P-507**: Authorization recorded late (14:30 vs. 09:00 commit). *Remediation*: Enforce 4-hour recording rule (ECD-2 C3).

**Indeterminate/Weak Evidence:**
- **P-506**: Verbal ECD-2 extension. *Remediation*: Require written extensions for all directives.

**Monitoring Actions:**
1. Audit all ECD-2 purchases for segregation of duties (GPIP-4 A1).
2. Validate Compliance_Clearance timing for new suppliers (P-502, P-507, P-508).
3. Review Incident Command’s authority to waive Finance controls (conflict between CFO Email D1 and GPIP-4 A2).

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Proposed Hierarchy/Exception Clause:**
> **Emergency Procurement Override Protocol (EPOP-1)**
> 1. **Authority**:
>    - Incident directives (e.g., ECD-2) issued by COO + Incident Commander may override *timing* (PO before commitment) and *supplier onboarding* (approved register) for purchases scoped to *imminent production stoppage or safety harm* (6 hours).
>    - **Explicit Exclusions**: Segregation of duties (GPIP-4 A1), Compliance_Clearance for new suppliers >EUR 100k (GPIP-4 A3), and Finance_Approve for >EUR 50k (GPIP-4 A2) *cannot* be waived without written Chief Risk Officer exception.
> 2. **Thresholds**:
>    - For purchases EUR 150k during an incident, joint Incident Command authorization replaces Plant_Approve and Finance_Approve *only if* the CFO (or delegate) explicitly confirms in writing.
>    - Purchases >EUR 150k require Legal_Clearance *and* Compliance_Clearance, regardless of incident status.
> 3. **Evidence**:
>    - Verbal authorizations must be recorded in a controlled system within 4 hours.
>    - Retrospective approvals do not cure missing pre-commitment controls but may mitigate penalties if documented within 24 hours.
> 4. **Safeguards**:
>    - All emergency overrides expire automatically at the directive’s end time unless extended *in writing* by the original signatories.
>    - Quarterly audit of 100% of emergency purchases for segregation of duties and Compliance_Clearance.
>    - **Anti-Normalization**: Emergency directives must include a sunset clause (max 72 hours) and a post-incident review to assess control gaps.

**Key Improvements:**
- Resolves ambiguity on ECD-2’s scope (aligns with DE-AAC-PROC-17 B4’s 6-hour definition).
- Explicitly protects GPIP-4 A1/A3 from override.
- Adds time-bound extensions and audit requirements.
