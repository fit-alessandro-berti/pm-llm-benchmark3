## **3. Assumptions**

### **Arrivals**
- **Non-stationary demand**:
  - Monthly multipliers are fixed but can be updated via **rolling-window estimation**.

### **Service Times**
- **Applicant-driven delays**:
  - `Receive missing documents` follows a **log-normal distribution** (heavy-tailed).

### **Routing**
- **Fire/Environmental reviews**:
  - Required if `requires_fire/environment=True` **or** district is sensitive (incomplete data  assume 10% false negatives).

  - Always required for `Complex variance`; 30% of `New residential` cases (modeled as random).

### **Resources/Calendars**
- **Clerk portal support**:
  - 1 clerk dedicated to `Submit application` and `Request missing documents` on Mondays.
- **Legal officers**:
  - No work during council-meeting weeks (calendar defined externally).

### **Rework**
- **Rework reasons**:
  - Modeled as **exogenous events** (e.g., 5% chance after Planning review).
- **Applicant appeals**:
  - Only for `Complex variance` (10% chance after Legal check).

### **Applicant/Third-Party Response**
- **Missing documents**:
  - Applicant response time follows **Pareto distribution** (median=5d, p90=14d).
- **Objections**:
  - Modeled as a **binary latent variable** (5% probability for `New residential`).

### **Cross-Case Dependencies**
- **Inspection bundling**:
  - First case in a bundle incurs full travel time; subsequent cases share the inspector.
- **District batching**:
  - Inspections are grouped by `district` (D3 intervention).

### **Non-Stationary Demand**
- **Seasonality**:
  - March-May and Sept-Nov have 1.7× arrivals; modeled via **time-dependent Poisson process**.

  - Interventions (D1-D10) modify routing/calendars but do not affect base demand.

### **Missing Data**
- **Objection events**:
  - Modeled as a **hidden state** (5% probability of extension).
- **Start timestamps**:
  - Service times are split into **waiting + service** using empirical ratios (e.g., 80% waiting for `Planning review`).
