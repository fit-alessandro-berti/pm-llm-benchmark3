# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The source POWL model given in the task is authoritative. No conversion result redefines its semantics. In particular, absence of an order edge is itself semantic information: it permits either order and, at model level, overlap/interleaving. A converter returning an object, a file, or a picture does not prove that these invariants were preserved.

Visible labels are abbreviated in witnesses as: Register = Register Change, Assess = Assess Risk, Prepare = Prepare Evidence, Legal = Legal Review, Configure = Configure Trial, Inspect = Inspect Trial, Correct = Correct Defect, Release = Release Change, Archive = Archive Change. `tau` denotes the silent skip transition.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 | Register is first and precedes Assess and Prepare. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Assess, Register, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Yes |
| INV-02 | Assess and Prepare are unordered after Register; both orders are allowed. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive; and Register, Prepare, Assess, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | A model permitting only Register, Assess, Prepare and never Register, Prepare, Assess | Yes, as permitted interleavings, not as proof of true concurrency |
| INV-03 | Legal is optional via XOR(Legal, tau), and Legal may occur only after Assess. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive, with Legal skipped; and Register, Assess, Legal, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Legal, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive; and a trace in which Legal is mandatory | Partially: presence/absence of Legal is observable, the tau skip itself is not |
| INV-04 | Verification cannot start until both Assess and Prepare are complete. | Register, Prepare, Assess, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Configure, Inspect, Prepare, Release, Archive | Yes |
| INV-05 | Optional-legal branch and verification loop are otherwise unordered; neither precedes the other by necessity. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Legal, Inspect, Release, Archive; and Register, Assess, Legal, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | A model requiring all Legal completion before Configure, or requiring verification completion before Legal may start | Yes, as absence of a forced order in interleavings |
| INV-06 | Every verification iteration executes Configure before Inspect. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Prepare, Inspect, Configure, Release, Archive | Yes |
| INV-07 | A repeated iteration is separated by Correct: after Inspect, either exit the loop or execute Correct and then a new Configure/Inspect iteration. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Correct, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Yes |
| INV-08 | At least one verification iteration occurs before the loop can finish. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Prepare, Release, Archive | Yes |
| INV-09 | The loop body is Configure then Inspect; Correct is the redo/separator branch, not part of the first body execution and not a substitute for Inspect. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Correct, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Prepare, Correct, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive; and Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Correct, Release, Archive | Yes |
| INV-10 | Release waits for completion of both the optional-legal branch, including its silent completion, and the verification loop. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Release, Archive, with Inspect omitted; and Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive where Legal, if started after that point, could still follow Release | Partially: Release ordering is observable, completion of the silent alternative is inferred |
| INV-11 | Archive occurs after Release and is the final visible activity. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Archive, Release; and any visible activity after Archive | Yes |
| INV-12 | Completion is exact: a complete execution ends after Archive with no required visible work remaining and no extra visible activity inserted. | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive | Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive, Legal; and a trace ending at Release presented as complete | Yes for visible completion; exact token completion must still be checked on the net |

Two cautions follow from the table. First, interleaving witnesses can show that two orders are permitted, but a finite set of sequential traces does not by itself prove true concurrency in a Petri net or BPMN model. Second, optionality is only partly observable after hiding `tau`: absence of Legal is compatible with a successful skip, but also, in a broken conversion, with Legal having been deleted. Distinguishing those cases requires structural inspection or additional negative tests.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

No direct POWL-to-BPMN call is assumed, because none is provided in the API reminders. Both pipelines therefore pass through an accepting Petri net, which is the documented conversion supported from POWL.

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN, primary pipeline | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; then `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)`; validation via `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)`, `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net`, `pm4py.check_soundness`, `pm4py.discover_footprints`, `pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph` | Shortest documented path; Petri net retains partial-order concurrency, silent optionality, loop structure, and synchronization more directly than a block-structured tree; BPMN is produced only as a derivative view | BPMN conversion may introduce routing nodes, split/join gateways, or layout that suggests order not present in the net; round trip may change structure while retaining or losing behavior | Any null/empty marking, non-workflow net, unsoundness, exception, missing witness, admitted negative witness, or unexplained footprint difference |
| B. POWL  accepting Petri net  process tree  BPMN, exploratory only | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; then `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)` inside `try/except`; if successful, `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)` and Petri-net round trip | A process tree, if faithfully obtained, is compact, block-structured, and easy to render and explain | The source contains a non-local synchronization: Assess and Prepare both gate verification, while Legal depends only on Assess and joins only at Release. That cross-branch partial order is not naturally block-structured, so tree conversion may fail or approximate concurrency/synchronization | Exception from `convert_to_process_tree`, or a returned tree that serializes Legal relative to verification, duplicates activities, weakens the Release join, changes loop minimum, or cannot reproduce required interleavings |

**Primary pipeline: A.** Pipeline B is a diagnostic probe, not a required path. Its failure is informative and must be reported, not repaired by silently substituting Pipeline A results under Pipeline B's name. A process-tree intermediate should not be used merely because it is prettier or because BPMN-from-tree looks cleaner.

# 3. Conversion Code

The code assumes `source_powl` has already been created exactly as in the task. It checks returned markings instead of assuming they are usable, catches the process-tree failure, round-trips every BPMN model that is actually produced, runs diagnostics on every accepting net obtained, and saves visualizations only for objects that exist.

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking


def require_marking(marking, name):
    if marking is None:
        raise ValueError(f"{name} is None")
    try:
        total = sum(marking.values())
    except Exception as exc:
        raise ValueError(f"{name} is not a usable marking: {exc}") from exc
    if total <= 0:
        raise ValueError(f"{name} is empty")
    return marking


def diagnose_net(name, net, im, fm):
    result = {"name": name, "net": net, "im": im, "fm": fm}
    require_marking(im, f"{name}.initial_marking")
    require_marking(fm, f"{name}.final_marking")

    try:
        result["is_workflow_net"] = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    except Exception as exc:
        result["is_workflow_net"] = None
        result["workflow_net_error"] = repr(exc)

    try:
        soundness = pm4py.check_soundness(
            net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True
        )
        result["soundness_raw"] = soundness
        if isinstance(soundness, tuple):
            result["is_sound"] = bool(soundness[0])
        else:
            result["is_sound"] = bool(soundness)
    except Exception as exc:
        result["is_sound"] = None
        result["soundness_error"] = repr(exc)

    places = getattr(net, "places", [])
    transitions = getattr(net, "transitions", [])
    result["places"] = len(list(places))
    result["transitions"] = len(list(transitions))
    result["visible_labels"] = sorted(
        {t.label for t in transitions if getattr(t, "label", None) is not None}
    )
    result["silent_transitions"] = sum(
        1 for t in transitions if getattr(t, "label", None) is None
    )
    return result


diagnostics = {}

net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(
    source_powl
)
require_marking(im_from_powl, "im_from_powl")
require_marking(fm_from_powl, "fm_from_powl")
diagnostics["net_from_powl"] = diagnose_net(
    "net_from_powl", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
)

pm4py.save_vis_powl(source_powl, "source_powl.png")
pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(
    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl, "net_from_powl.png"
)

bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
)
if bpmn_from_net is None:
    raise ValueError("pm4py.convert_to_bpmn returned None for bpmn_from_net")
pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(bpmn_from_net, "bpmn_from_net.png")

bpmn_models = {"bpmn_from_net": bpmn_from_net}
round_tripped_nets = {}

tree_from_net = None
tree_error = None
bpmn_from_tree = None

try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    if tree_from_net is None:
        raise ValueError("convert_to_process_tree returned None")
    pm4py.save_vis_process_tree(tree_from_net, "tree_from_net.png")

    bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
    if bpmn_from_tree is None:
        raise ValueError("convert_to_bpmn returned None for the process tree")
    bpmn_models["bpmn_from_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
    pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(bpmn_from_tree, "bpmn_from_tree.png")

except Exception as exc:
    tree_from_net = None
    bpmn_from_tree = None
    tree_error = exc
    print(f"Process-tree pipeline failed and was quarantined: {exc!r}")

for bpmn_name, bpmn_model in bpmn_models.items():
    try:
        rt_net, rt_im, rt_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_model)
        require_marking(rt_im, f"{bpmn_name}.roundtrip_initial_marking")
        require_marking(rt_fm, f"{bpmn_name}.roundtrip_final_marking")
        round_tripped_nets[bpmn_name] = (rt_net, rt_im, rt_fm)
        diagnostics[f"{bpmn_name}_roundtrip_net"] = diagnose_net(
            f"{bpmn_name}_roundtrip_net", rt_net, rt_im, rt_fm
        )
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(
            rt_net, rt_im, rt_fm, f"{bpmn_name}_roundtrip_net.png"
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        diagnostics[f"{bpmn_name}_roundtrip_net"] = {
            "name": f"{bpmn_name}_roundtrip_net",
            "roundtrip_error": repr(exc),
        }
        print(f"BPMN round trip failed for {bpmn_name}: {exc!r}")

for entry_name, entry in diagnostics.items():
    print(entry_name, entry)
if tree_error is not None:
    print("tree_from_net_status: conversion failed:", repr(tree_error))
```

The important discipline is that `bpmn_from_tree` exists only if both the tree conversion and the subsequent BPMN conversion succeed. If the tree step raises, the correct result is a recorded failure, not an empty diagram, a manually invented tree, or a quietly reused `bpmn_from_net`.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

Assessments below are expectations before execution, upgraded only when the validation in section 5 actually passes. A successful function call alone does not change `expected preserved` into `validated preserved`.

| Source invariant | POWLPetri | PetriBPMN | PetriTree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 Register first | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Check first visible label in bounded exploration and footprints; tree cell must be replaced by the recorded outcome if conversion unexpectedly succeeds |
| INV-02 Assess/Prepare unordered | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | Petri net can retain concurrency; BPMN may show a parallel split/join or an interleaving layout. Sequential traces show permitted orders, not true concurrency itself |
| INV-03 Legal optional after Assess | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | XOR with a silent transition may become an exclusive gateway, a silent routing node, or may disappear visually. Absence of Legal alone does not prove the skip branch survived |
| INV-04 Verification gated by Assess and Prepare | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Test negative witnesses with Configure before Prepare or before Assess completes |
| INV-05 Legal branch and verification unordered | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | This non-local absence of order is the main block-structure risk. A tree or BPMN layout may serialize Legal before, inside, or after verification |
| INV-06 Configure before Inspect in each iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Footprints and bounded traces should show Configure causally before Inspect and forbid Inspect first |
| INV-07 Repeat separated by Correct | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | BPMN loop rendering may look like an unstructured cycle; verify Configure, Inspect, Configure without intervening Correct is rejected |
| INV-08 At least one iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Negative witness ending at Release without Configure/Inspect must fail exact-final-marking completion |
| INV-09 Correct is redo separator only | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Check Correct cannot fire before the first Inspect and cannot replace Inspect |
| INV-10 Release joins legal branch and verification | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | BPMN join semantics and gateway pairing must be inspected; a visual merge is not necessarily a synchronizing join |
| INV-11 Archive after Release and final | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Check no visible successor after Archive in completed traces |
| INV-12 Exact completion | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Depends on initial/final markings surviving BPMN conversion; require exact final-marking equality, not merely reaching Archive |

Discussion of the required risk points:

- **True concurrency versus arbitrary interleaving.** The POWL and a Petri net can represent Assess/Prepare concurrency directly through absence of causal dependency. BPMN can represent it with a parallel gateway, but many renderings still arrange activities left-to-right, inviting a sequential reading. Conversely, observing both sequential orders in traces proves interleaving equivalence under the test bound, not partial-order or true-concurrency equivalence. Footprints and reachability structure give stronger evidence than trace lists alone, but still depend on the converted net's correctness.

- **Lack of an edge between Optional Legal Review and verification.** This is the invariant most likely to be damaged by a process tree. In the source, Legal depends on Assess, verification depends on Assess and Prepare, and both join only at Release. A block-structured tree tends to nest one construct inside another or place them in sequence/parallel as whole blocks. Because Legal and the loop have different entry dependencies and a shared late join, forcing them into a tree can create an artificial order, duplicate a dependency activity, or move the join. Failure of `convert_to_process_tree` is therefore semantically plausible and preferable to a clean-looking approximation.

- **Silent optional behavior.** The XOR(Legal, tau) branch means Legal may be skipped without a visible event. In Petri-net form a silent transition may remain; in BPMN it may become an exclusive gateway with a default/empty path, a silent task-like node, or a routing element whose label is blank. If the BPMN renderer hides the silent path, stakeholders may read Legal as mandatory. If it deletes the Legal alternative, absence of Legal in traces will look correct while the optional opportunity has been lost.

- **Binary-loop semantics.** POWL `LOOP` with children `[trial_body, correct_defect]` means: execute the body at least once; after the body, either exit or execute the redo child and repeat the body. Here the body is itself sequential ConfigureInspect. A converter must not turn Correct into an alternative first step, an optional post-step after exit, or an activity required before Release after the final Inspect. The required pattern is `Configure, Inspect, (Correct, Configure, Inspect)*` within the verification scope.

- **Join before Release Change.** Release has two distinct predecessors in dependency terms: completion of optional-legal scope and exit from verification. A BPMN merge gateway drawn before Release may be only a visual merge, an exclusive merge, or a true parallel join depending on conversion semantics. The audit must test that Release cannot occur while a started iteration is incomplete and that the model does not deadlock when Legal is skipped.

- **Activity-label uniqueness.** Each visible label in the source is unique as a transition identity, although Configure, Inspect, and Correct recur across loop iterations by re-executing the same transitions. Conversion should not duplicate transitions with the same label in a way that makes trace comparison ambiguous, nor merge distinct activities. If BPMN round trip creates duplicate labels, label-only traces remain useful for witnesses but structural claims become weaker.

- **Initial and final markings.** POWL-to-Petri conversion must return a non-empty initial marking and a final marking representing exact completion. BPMN conversion may introduce start/end events whose round-tripped markings differ in place structure even if behavior is similar. Therefore exact final-marking equality is applied within each net's own completion test, while cross-net comparison is behavioral, not place-identity comparison.

- **Gateway and routing-node ambiguity in BPMN visualization.** BPMN gateways may represent behavior from the net, artifacts introduced to route tokens, or drawing conveniences. A diamond in the picture is not automatically a source-level XOR or AND. Conversely, a silent Petri-net transition may become visible layout machinery. Section 6 separates these visual questions from behavioral acceptance.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

The comparison is intentionally multi-level: workflow-net structure, soundness, footprints, reachability-graph size/final reachability where available, bounded visible-trace comparison, witness checks, and playout only inside `try/except`. Footprint equality is evidence, not a complete equivalence proof, especially with silent transitions and loops.

The bounded explorer hides transitions whose label is `None`, requires exact equality with the net's own final marking for a completed trace, limits visible length, limits consecutive silent steps and total silent steps, and tracks silent configurations on the current path to avoid infinite silent cycling. It is exhaustive only for visible traces up to the chosen visible bound and silent bound, under the assumption that any silent cycle relevant within those bounds is cut by the per-path repeated silent-configuration rule. It is not exhaustive for unbounded loop iterations, unbounded silent behavior, true-concurrency equivalence, or traces longer than the bound.

```python
import pm4py
from collections import deque
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking


def frozen_marking(marking):
    return tuple(sorted((place.name, count) for place, count in marking.items() if count > 0))


def marking_from_frozen(net, frozen):
    marking = Marking()
    places_by_name = {place.name: place for place in net.places}
    for place_name, count in frozen:
        marking[places_by_name[place_name]] = count
    return marking


def fire_manually(net, marking, transition):
    new_marking = Marking()
    for place, count in marking.items():
        new_marking[place] = count
    for arc in transition.in_arcs:
        new_marking[arc.source] = new_marking[arc.source] - arc.weight
        if new_marking[arc.source] <= 0:
            del new_marking[arc.source]
    for arc in transition.out_arcs:
        new_marking[arc.target] = new_marking[arc.target] + arc.weight
    return new_marking


def bounded_visible_traces(net, im, fm, max_visible=11, max_consecutive_tau=8, max_total_tau=24):
    require_marking(im, "bounded exploration initial marking")
    require_marking(fm, "bounded exploration final marking")

    completed = set()
    reachable_visible_sequences = set()
    start = frozen_marking(im)
    final = frozen_marking(fm)

    queue = deque()
    queue.append((start, tuple(), 0, 0, frozenset({start})))
    reachable_visible_sequences.add(tuple())

    if start == final:
        completed.add(tuple())

    while queue:
        frozen, visible_trace, consecutive_tau, total_tau, silent_path = queue.popleft()
        marking = marking_from_frozen(net, frozen)

        try:
            enabled = list(pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking))
        except Exception:
            enabled = []
            for transition in net.transitions:
                if all(marking.get(arc.source, 0) >= arc.weight for arc in transition.in_arcs):
                    enabled.append(transition)

        for transition in enabled:
            label = getattr(transition, "label", None)
            next_marking = fire_manually(net, marking, transition)
            next_frozen = frozen_marking(next_marking)

            if label is None:
                if consecutive_tau >= max_consecutive_tau:
                    continue
                if total_tau >= max_total_tau:
                    continue
                if next_frozen in silent_path:
                    continue
                queue.append((
                    next_frozen,
                    visible_trace,
                    consecutive_tau + 1,
                    total_tau + 1,
                    silent_path | {next_frozen},
                ))
            else:
                if len(visible_trace) >= max_visible:
                    continue
                next_visible = visible_trace + (label,)
                reachable_visible_sequences.add(next_visible)
                if next_frozen == final:
                    completed.add(next_visible)
                queue.append((
                    next_frozen,
                    next_visible,
                    0,
                    total_tau,
                    frozenset({next_frozen}),
                ))

    return completed, reachable_visible_sequences


def footprint_summary(net, im, fm):
    try:
        footprints = pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm)
        return repr(footprints)
    except Exception as exc:
        return f"footprints failed: {exc!r}"


def reachability_summary(net, im, fm):
    try:
        reachability_graph = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
        return {
            "type": type(reachability_graph).__name__,
            "repr_prefix": repr(reachability_graph)[:500],
        }
    except Exception as exc:
        return {"reachability_error": repr(exc)}


def optional_playout(net, im, fm):
    try:
        playout_fn = getattr(pm4py, "play_out", None)
        if playout_fn is None:
            return "play_out not available in this pm4py interface"
        log = playout_fn(net, im, fm)
        return f"play_out produced: {type(log).__name__}"
    except Exception as exc:
        return f"play_out failed or unsupported: {exc!r}"


POSITIVE_WITNESSES = [
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Prepare Evidence", "Assess Risk", "Legal Review",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Legal Review", "Inspect Trial",
     "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
]

NEGATIVE_WITNESSES = [
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial",
     "Prepare Evidence", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Legal Review", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Inspect Trial", "Configure Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial",
     "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Correct Defect", "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial",
     "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"),
    ("Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
     "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Archive Change", "Release Change"),
]

MAX_VISIBLE = 11

source_completed, source_reachable = bounded_visible_traces(
    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl, max_visible=MAX_VISIBLE
)

comparison_targets = {"net_from_powl": (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)}
for bpmn_name, triple in round_tripped_nets.items():
    comparison_targets[f"{bpmn_name}_roundtrip"] = triple

validation_report = {}

for target_name, (target_net, target_im, target_fm) in comparison_targets.items():
    entry = {}

    try:
        entry["is_workflow_net"] = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(target_net)
    except Exception as exc:
        entry["is_workflow_net"] = None
        entry["workflow_net_error"] = repr(exc)

    try:
        soundness = pm4py.check_soundness(
            target_net, target_im, target_fm, print_diagnostics=True
        )
        entry["soundness_raw"] = soundness
        entry["is_sound"] = bool(soundness[0]) if isinstance(soundness, tuple) else bool(soundness)
    except Exception as exc:
        entry["is_sound"] = None
        entry["soundness_error"] = repr(exc)

    entry["footprints"] = footprint_summary(target_net, target_im, target_fm)
    entry["reachability"] = reachability_summary(target_net, target_im, target_fm)
    entry["playout"] = optional_playout(target_net, target_im, target_fm)

    target_completed, target_reachable = bounded_visible_traces(
        target_net, target_im, target_fm, max_visible=MAX_VISIBLE
    )
    entry["completed_trace_count_bounded"] = len(target_completed)
    entry["completed_equal_to_source_under_bound"] = (
        target_completed == source_completed
    )
    entry["missing_completed_traces_sample"] = sorted(
        source_completed - target_completed
    )[:10]
    entry["extra_completed_traces_sample"] = sorted(
        target_completed - source_completed
    )[:10]

    entry["positive_witness_failures"] = [
        list(trace) for trace in POSITIVE_WITNESSES if trace not in target_completed
    ]
    entry["negative_witness_failures"] = [
        list(trace) for trace in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES if trace in target_completed
    ]
    entry["source_footprints"] = footprint_summary(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    entry["footprints_equal_as_repr"] = (
        entry["footprints"] == entry["source_footprints"]
    )

    validation_report[target_name] = entry
    print(target_name, entry)
```

Interpretation rules: a round-tripped net is provisionally behaviorally acceptable under the bound only if it is a workflow net, soundness does not fail, all positive witnesses are completed traces, no negative witness is a completed trace, and bounded completed-trace sets match the source. Footprint disagreement requires investigation even if witnesses pass, because witnesses sample only part of the behavior. Footprint agreement plus witness agreement is still not a proof of unbounded equivalence.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN picture can be behaviorally derived from an acceptable net and still mislead a reader. BPMN layout, gateway symbols, and routing artifacts must therefore be reviewed as communication, not treated as the semantics itself.

- **Parallel gateways.** A parallel split/join pair before Assess/Prepare or before Release may correctly suggest concurrency and synchronization. But readers may also interpret horizontal placement as sequence, or assume that two activities drawn on separate lanes are performed by different actors. If the converter uses a parallel gateway only for part of the model, for example splitting Assess/Prepare but using a simple merge before Release, the picture can falsely suggest that Release needs only one predecessor. The Release join must be explicitly annotated as requiring both verification completion and optional-legal completion.

- **Exclusive gateways.** The Legal option is an XOR with silence. An exclusive gateway with one path labeled Legal Review and another unlabeled path may be read as an error, an unfinished diagram, or a default path that is somehow less legitimate. If the silent path is not drawn, Legal looks mandatory. If both paths are drawn but the gateway is not labeled, readers may think there is a data-based decision with missing conditions. Annotate: “Legal Review optional; empty path = skip Legal Review; available only after Assess Risk.”

- **Loop visualization.** BPMN renderers often draw loops as back-edges crossing the diagram. A back-edge from Inspect toward Configure can be misread as allowing InspectConfigure directly, hiding the required Correct Defect separator. It can also make Correct look like a normal step after every Inspect, including the final one before Release. Annotate the loop region: “Repeat only via Correct Defect, then Configure Trial  Inspect Trial. Minimum one Configure/Inspect iteration. Correct is not required after the final Inspect.”

- **Silent routing nodes.** Blank tasks, blank gateways, or small unlabeled nodes inherited from silent Petri-net transitions may look like missing labels or system faults. Deleting them for cosmetic reasons can change semantics. The stakeholder view should either preserve them with a legend such as “blank node = silent routing / no business activity” or replace them only in a clearly marked presentation copy whose underlying model is unchanged.

- **Apparent ordering from graph layout.** Left-to-right drawing is necessary for readability but is not an order relation. Configure drawn above Legal, or Legal drawn before the loop, does not mean it must happen first. This matters most for INV-05: the picture will almost inevitably place the legal branch and verification loop in some spatial order. Add a note directly on the diagram: “Layout order is not execution order. Legal Review, after Assess Risk, is unordered relative to the verification loop; both must be complete before Release Change.”

Recommended annotations for the stakeholder BPMN: title stating “Derivative visualization — authoritative model is POWL/Petri net”; a legend for silent nodes and gateway types; text annotations for optionality, loop minimum and separator, and the Release synchronization; and a validation stamp naming the source model version, conversion pipeline, diagnostics date, and bounded-validation limit. Any hand-cleaned BPMN must be labeled as edited and must be re-round-tripped and revalidated before use.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

A conversion result should be rejected, or quarantined as an unapproved derivative, when any of the following holds:

1. **Conversion or marking failure.** `convert_to_petri_net`, `convert_to_bpmn`, or a round trip raises, returns `None`, or returns a null/empty initial or final marking; or the process-tree pipeline is represented as successful when `convert_to_process_tree` actually raised.
2. **Not a workflow net or unsound.** `check_is_workflow_net` is false, or `check_soundness` reports unsoundness, possible deadlock, dead transitions relevant to required activities, or tokens left after final marking. Soundness diagnostics are indecisive because the check itself errors.
3. **Required interleaving lost.** The converted model permits Register, Assess, Prepare but not Register, Prepare, Assess; or it forces Legal to precede verification, verification to precede Legal, Legal to precede Assess, or another order absent from the source.
4. **Forbidden behavior admitted.** A negative witness completes with exact final marking, especially Release before a complete Configure/Inspect iteration, Release after zero iterations, Archive before Release, Configure repeated without Correct, Correct before the first Inspect, or any visible activity after Archive.
5. **Release synchronization weakened.** Release can fire when verification has started but not finished, when only one of Assess/Prepare has completed before verification, or when the optional-legal branch has neither executed Legal nor taken its completing silent alternative.
6. **Optional behavior collapsed.** Legal becomes mandatory, Legal becomes impossible, the silent skip deadlocks the model, or absence of Legal in traces is achieved by deleting the Legal activity rather than preserving an optional branch.
7. **Loop semantics changed.** Zero iterations are allowed, Correct becomes an entry activity, Correct is required after the final Inspect before Release, Inspect can precede Configure, or the loop can exit from the middle of Configure/Inspect.
8. **Exact-final-marking test failed.** A trace reaches Archive but does not end in the net's exact final marking, reaches the final marking without Archive as the last visible activity, or leaves residual tokens/silent behavior that can continue after claimed completion.
9. **Label or identity corruption.** Required labels are missing, renamed inconsistently, duplicated so that witness traces are ambiguous, or distinct source activities are merged into one transition/task.
10. **Validation disagreement remains unexplained.** Bounded completed-trace sets differ from the source, footprints differ in causality/parallel relations for required activities, reachability analysis shows the final marking unreachable, or playout generates traces inconsistent with witnesses.
11. **Visualization-only acceptance.** The only evidence offered is that BPMN renders, exports, or “looks right,” without net diagnostics and witness validation. Such an artifact may be kept for discussion but must not be used for execution, conformance, or requirements sign-off.

Quarantine means the artifact is stored with its error, source version, and diagnostics, but is blocked from downstream use until the failure is explained and a corrected conversion passes the full audit.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

**Authoritative storage: the source POWL model.** POWL is the representation that directly states the intended partial order, the XOR-with-silence optionality, and the binary loop with a sequential body. It should be version-controlled as code or in a faithful POWL serialization, together with the invariant table in section 1. Changes to requirements should be made in POWL first.

**Canonical behavioral interchange/validation form: the accepting Petri net generated from POWL.** `net_from_powl` with its checked initial and final markings is the most useful derivative for analysis because concurrency, silence, synchronization, soundness, footprints, and reachability can be examined directly. It is still derivative: if it disagrees with POWL semantics, POWL wins and the conversion is quarantined.

**Stakeholder visualization: BPMN generated by Pipeline A.** BPMN is recommended for communication because stakeholders commonly read tasks and gateways more easily than POWL or Petri nets. It is not authoritative. The BPMN file should carry annotations from section 6 and a reference to the exact POWL/Petri-net version and validation report from which it was generated.

Derivative rules:

- Every BPMN or process-tree artifact must be accompanied by: source POWL version/hash, conversion calls used, PM4Py version if available, workflow-net and soundness results, marking checks, positive/negative witness results, bounded-trace comparison bound, footprint comparison outcome, and any exceptions.
- A process-tree intermediate should **not** be allowed for this model as a canonical or required step. It may be produced only as an experiment. If conversion fails, record the failure. If it succeeds, it must pass the same invariant and round-trip audit; a successful tree that serializes Legal relative to verification must be rejected despite being well-formed.
- Future edits must not be made by editing BPMN and converting back as the new source. That invites round-trip drift: silent nodes may be dropped, gateways reinterpreted, loop back-edges redrawn into different behavior, and layout changes mistaken for semantic changes. Instead, edit POWL, regenerate the Petri net, regenerate BPMN, rerun diagnostics and witnesses, and diff the new validation report against the previous one.
- If stakeholders request a BPMN-only change, treat it as a change request against the POWL source. Implement it in POWL, regenerate all derivatives, and show stakeholders the regenerated BPMN difference.

This separation keeps one semantic source of truth, one analyzable behavioral form, and one readable presentation form, without confusing their roles.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every diagnostic and witness test passes, the proposed validation cannot establish all of the following:

1. **Unbounded behavioral equivalence.** Bounded exploration with a visible-length limit cannot prove equality for arbitrarily many loop iterations, arbitrarily long silent runs, or traces exceeding the bound. Soundness and footprints strengthen confidence but are not, in this audit, a complete bisimulation or language-equivalence proof between POWL, Petri net, and BPMN round trip.
2. **True concurrency rather than interleaving equivalence.** Sequential witnesses and footprints can show that required orders are permitted and forbidden orders are excluded, but they do not fully prove that the BPMN implementation preserves partial-order/causal independence in the same way as POWL, especially after BPMN gateways and routing nodes are introduced.
3. **Stakeholder interpretation.** Tests cannot prove that readers will not misread layout order as sequence, treat a silent skip path as an omission, or interpret a merge gateway as synchronization. Annotations reduce this risk; they do not eliminate it.
4. **Converter correctness for all inputs.** Passing this model does not prove PM4Py conversion is correct for other POWL models, other BPMN round trips, different label patterns, data conditions, resources, time, or organizational assignments, none of which are represented in the source.
5. **Meaning of silent behavior beyond skip.** Hiding `tau` validates that Legal may be absent, but validation cannot by itself prove that a particular silent BPMN routing node means “deliberate skip” rather than converter machinery, nor that no business-relevant decision has been hidden.
6. **Operational adequacy.** The source says nothing about who performs Legal Review, what evidence is sufficient, defect severity, data objects, deadlines, or failure handling outside Correct Defect. Behavioral preservation of this control-flow skeleton does not validate those omitted requirements.

The defensible claim after full success is therefore narrow: under the stated bounds and diagnostics, the generated Petri net and BPMN round trip show no detected violation of the listed POWL invariants. That is evidence for using the BPMN as a validated derivative view, not proof that it is interchangeable with the authoritative POWL model.
