# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

**The source POWL is authoritative.** Successful conversion, serialization, rendering, or round-trip conversion is not, by itself, evidence of semantic equivalence.

I use the following abbreviations in witnesses:

`R` = Register Change; `A` = Assess Risk; `E` = Prepare Evidence; `L` = Legal Review; `C` = Configure Trial; `I` = Inspect Trial; `D` = Correct Defect; `U` = Release Change; `H` = Archive Change.

Witnesses are visible activity sequences unless a marking condition is explicitly stated. All precedence constraints include their transitive consequences. For an **absence-of-order invariant**, a failure witness is typically the **rejection of an admissible ordering**, rather than the acceptance of a forbidden trace.

| Invariant ID | Invariant                                                                                                                                                            | Positive witness                                                              | Negative witness                                                                                 | Observable after hiding tau?                                                                                                   |

The verification-loop language is

$$
(C\,I)(D\,C\,I)^*,
$$

not `(C I D)*`, not a zero-or-more loop, and not a loop around the complete change process. Legal review can be interleaved with this language without changing its projection.

The source specifies **permitted concurrency**, not measured overlap, durations, resource availability, probabilities, or conditions for choosing legal review or another correction.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

**Primary pipeline: POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN.** It avoids forcing the source into a block-structured intermediate. The documented interfaces support POWL-to-Petri conversion and BPMN conversion from a marked Petri net or process tree; markings must still be checked rather than assumed. ([Process Intelligence Solutions][1])

| Pipeline                                                                                      | PM4Py calls                                                                                                            | Expected benefit                                                                                                                                    | Expected risk                                                                                                                                                                     | Failure condition                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **P1 — Primary:** POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN → accepting Petri net                     | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)`                      | Preserves a general partial-order structure without requiring a process tree. Provides an executable intermediate and a stakeholder-facing diagram. | Routing transformations, gateway interpretation, silent bypasses, or endpoint reconstruction may change behavior. A readable diagram can conceal those changes.                   | Missing/invalid markings, conversion exception, unsoundness, source-oracle mismatch, lost joint-step witnesses, or incorrect final acceptance.           |
| **P2 — Experimental:** POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN → accepting Petri net | P1’s initial conversion; `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_bpmn(tree)`; `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | If faithfully representable, exposes a compact operator hierarchy and potentially simpler visualization.                                            | The source’s overlapping synchronization requirements may resist block decomposition. A superficially simpler tree may introduce order, lose a prerequisite, or duplicate labels. | A structural-conversion exception is an acceptable outcome. A returned tree is rejected if its accepting-net realization changes the required semantics. |

The structural difficulty is visible in the four-node dependency pattern

$$
A<O,\qquad A<V,\qquad E<V,
$$

where \(O\) is optional legal review and \(V\) is verification, while \(A\parallel E\), \(E\parallel O\), and \(O\parallel V\).

For example, putting `Parallel(A, E)` before `Parallel(O, V)` incorrectly adds `E < O`, excluding `R A L E C I U H`. Putting `Parallel(Sequence(A, O), E)` before verification incorrectly adds `O < V`, excluding interleaved or late legal review.

This is a reason to expect difficulty for a structural block-reduction algorithm—not an observed conversion failure, and not a claim that no larger, duplicate-labeled, trace-language-equivalent tree could ever be constructed. PM4Py’s inspected net-to-tree implementation attempts block reductions and raises an exception when reduction does not yield a tree. The corresponding research result concerns language preservation under its assumptions, not unrestricted equivalence of every successful library call. ([GitHub][2])

```python
def attempt(stage: str, operation):
    """Retain the exception; failure never becomes an empty valid model."""
    try:
        result = operation()
    except Exception as exc:
        failure(stage, exc)
        return None
    REPORT["stages"][stage] = {"outcome": "returned"}

def checked_net(result) -> NetTriple:
    if not isinstance(result, (tuple, list)) or len(result) != 3:
        raise TypeError("Expected (net, initial_marking, final_marking)")
    net, im, fm = result

    if type(net) is not PetriNet:
        raise TypeError(
            "This audit supports ordinary P/T nets, not net extensions"
        )

    for name, marking in (("initial", im), ("final", fm)):
        if marking is None:
            raise ValueError(
                f"Missing {name} marking; do not infer one silently"
            )
        if not isinstance(marking, Marking):
            raise TypeError(f"{name} marking is not a PM4Py Marking")
        for place, count in marking.items():
            if (
                place not in net.places
                or not isinstance(count, Integral)
                or count < 0

                raise ValueError(
                    f"Invalid place/token count in {name} marking"
                )

    if any(
        not isinstance(a.weight, Integral) or a.weight <= 0
        for a in net.arcs
    ):
        raise ValueError("Expected positive integer arc weights")
    if any(
        t.label is not None and not isinstance(t.label, str)
        for t in net.transitions
    ):
        raise TypeError("Visible labels must be strings")

    # Removing explicit zero entries does not invent tokens or endpoints.
    return (
        net,
        Marking({p: int(n) for p, n in im.items() if n}),
        Marking({p: int(n) for p, n in fm.items() if n}),
    )
```

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

The following is a **pre-execution assessment**, not a report of successful conversions. Accordingly, no cell claims `validated preserved`, and the anticipated tree difficulty is not mislabeled `conversion failed`.

The inspected POWL converter explicitly handles XOR alternatives, a binary loop with a silent exit after the body, and dependency routing for partial orders. This supports preservation **expectations**, but does not substitute for testing the concrete returned net. ([GitHub][5])

Several distinctions are important:

**Concurrency versus interleaving.** A model choosing between `A E` and `E A` can reproduce both visible orders without allowing a joint execution step. The validation therefore supplements visible-language comparison with token-based joint-step witnesses.

**No edge versus no order.** Missing a direct edge is not enough: an indirect path can still impose precedence. Here, neither optional legal review nor verification reaches the other through the source order. Their being listed consecutively in `nodes=[...]` creates no sequencing requirement.

**Silent optional behavior.** A no-legal trace demonstrates that review can be omitted. It does not establish that a particular skip transition fired, or that the same internal decision timing survived conversion. Adding a visible “Skip Legal Review” task would change the visible language.

**Markings and finality.** Markings from different nets should not be compared by place-object equality across conversions. Each model must instead satisfy its own endpoint contract and accept traces only at its own exact final marking. A final-place token plus leftover tokens is not completion.

**Label uniqueness.** The conservative audit expects one visible transition per source activity label. Intentional duplication is not automatically behavioral loss, but it breaks the simple activity-to-node identity contract and requires an explicit mapping and stronger review.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

Append this block to Section 3.

The validation has three independent reference points: the original converted net, a **source-derived language oracle**, and selected **joint-step concurrency requirements**. The oracle prevents two converters with the same defect from validating each other.

The search domain is

$$
B=\{w: |w|\leq14,\ \#_C(w)\leq3\}.
$$

For the intended source, one, two, and three verification iterations produce respectively **9, 15, and 21** completed visible words: **45 total**. These are independently derived source-oracle counts, not measured PM4Py conversion results.

The exploration is exhaustive within \(B\) **only when it returns normally**. It records `(marking, visible prefix)` states, hides exactly transitions labeled `None`, and collapses repeated silent-cycle states without imposing an arbitrary silent-depth cutoff. Unbounded silent token growth or excessive state expansion triggers an exception and an inconclusive result.

The firing primitive uses PM4Py’s enabled-transition interface and ordinary Petri-net execution semantics. ([GitHub][3])

```python
VISIBLE_BOUND = 14
ITERATION_BOUND = 3
MAX_PRODUCT_STATES = 200_000
MAX_MARKINGS = 20_000


class ExplorationLimit(RuntimeError):
    """An incomplete search is inconclusive, never a passing comparison."""


def signature(marking: Marking):
    # Place identities avoid collisions between duplicated place names.
    return frozenset((p, int(n)) for p, n in marking.items() if n)


def fire(net: PetriNet, marking: Marking, transition) -> Marking:
    result = semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
    if result is None:
        raise RuntimeError("An enabled transition could not be fired")
    return Marking({p: int(n) for p, n in result.items() if n})


def in_bound(trace: tuple[str, ...]) -> bool:
    return (
        len(trace) <= VISIBLE_BOUND
        and trace.count(LABELS["C"]) <= ITERATION_BOUND
    )


def explore(apn: NetTriple):
    net, im, fm = apn
    queue = deque([(im, ())])
    seen = {(signature(im), ())}
    completed, prefixes, steps = set(), set(), set()
    dirty_finals, deadlocks = set(), set()
    step_examples = {}
    enabled_at_final = False

    while queue:
        marking, trace = queue.popleft()
        prefixes.add(trace)
        enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)

        if marking == fm:       # EXACT equality, not fm <= marking.
            completed.add(trace)
            enabled_at_final |= bool(enabled)
        elif fm and all(marking[p] >= n for p, n in fm.items()):
            dirty_finals.add(trace)

        # A bound-pruned continuation is not called a deadlock.
        if not enabled and marking != fm:
            deadlocks.add(trace)

        # Joint-step enabling is stronger than observing both serial orders.
        # For ordinary P/T nets, the marking must cover combined input demand.
        visible = [t for t in enabled if t.label is not None]
        for t, u in combinations(visible, 2):
            demand = Counter()
            for arc in list(t.in_arcs) + list(u.in_arcs):
                demand[arc.source] += arc.weight
            if all(marking[p] >= n for p, n in demand.items()):
                pair = tuple(sorted((t.label, u.label)))
                steps.add(pair)
                step_examples.setdefault(pair, {
                    "activities": pair,
                    "prefix": trace,
                    "marking": repr(marking),
                })

        # Continue even at fm to expose behavior after nominal completion.
        for transition in enabled:
            nxt = (
                trace if transition.label is None
                else trace + (transition.label,)
            )
            if not in_bound(nxt):
                continue
            next_marking = fire(net, marking, transition)
            key = (signature(next_marking), nxt)

            if key not in seen:
                if len(seen) >= MAX_PRODUCT_STATES:
                    raise ExplorationLimit(
                        "Product-state cap reached; not exhaustive"
                    )
                seen.add(key)
                queue.append((next_marking, nxt))

    return {
        "completed": completed,
        "prefixes": prefixes,
        "steps": steps,
        "step_examples": list(step_examples.values()),
        "dirty_finals": dirty_finals,
        "deadlocks": deadlocks,
        "enabled_at_final": enabled_at_final,
        "product_states": len(seen),
        "exhaustive_under_bound": True,
    }


def source_language(max_iterations: int) -> set[tuple[str, ...]]:
    """Independent oracle: topological sorts of unrolled source orders."""
    language = set()

    for k in range(1, max_iterations + 1):
        for use_legal in (False, True):
            pred = {"r": set(), "a": {"r"}, "e": {"r"}}
            activity = {"r": "R", "a": "A", "e": "E"}

            if use_legal:
                pred["l"], activity["l"] = {"a"}, "L"

            for i in range(k):
                c, inspection = f"c{i}", f"i{i}"
                pred[c] = {"a", "e"} if i == 0 else {f"d{i-1}"}
                pred[inspection] = {c}
                activity[c], activity[inspection] = "C", "I"

                if i < k - 1:
                    pred[f"d{i}"] = {inspection}
                    activity[f"d{i}"] = "D"

            pred["u"] = {f"i{k-1}"} | ({"l"} if use_legal else set())
            pred["h"] = {"u"}
            activity["u"], activity["h"] = "U", "H"

            def visit(done: set[str], trace: tuple[str, ...]) -> None:
                if len(done) == len(pred):
                    language.add(trace)
                    return
                for node in sorted(pred):
                    if node not in done and pred[node] <= done:
                        visit(
                            done | {node},
                            trace + (LABELS[activity[node]],),
                        )

            visit(set(), ())

    return language


def finite_state_preflight(apn: NetTriple) -> tuple[int, int]:
    """Explore markings WITHOUT a loop bound before unrestricted APIs."""
    net, im, _ = apn
    queue, seen, edges = deque([im]), {signature(im)}, 0

    while queue:
        marking = queue.popleft()
        for transition in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking):
            edges += 1
            nxt = fire(net, marking, transition)
            key = signature(nxt)

            if key not in seen:
                if len(seen) >= MAX_MARKINGS:
                    raise ExplorationLimit(
                        "Marking cap reached; finiteness not established"
                    )
                seen.add(key)
                queue.append(nxt)

    return len(seen), edges
```

**How to interpret the evidence.** A successful bounded comparison establishes equality of the compared completed visible languages within the stated domain, not unlimited equivalence. Checking reachable prefixes additionally detects an early release that leads to a deadlock and therefore never appears in an accepted trace.

The finite-state preflight explores markings without a loop-count bound. If it finishes, unrestricted reachability and footprint diagnostics are attempted. **Different state counts across converted nets are not a semantic failure by themselves**, because additional silent routing can introduce states.

Footprints are supporting summaries, not acceptance-language certificates. In the inspected Petri-net footprint implementation, the result summarizes activity relations from reachability and does not itself enforce the supplied final marking. Consequently, footprint equality cannot replace exact-final-marking exploration. ([GitHub][6])

The bounded explorer is exhaustive model playout within its domain. Random trace playout is deliberately not an acceptance criterion: samples can illustrate behavior but cannot establish that required interleavings remain possible or that forbidden behavior is absent.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

**Parallel gateways.** A gateway marking should distinguish “enable both branches” from “wait for all required branch outcomes.” In this process, verification waits for `A` and `E`, while release waits for verification and the completed-or-skipped legal branch. These are different joins. A diagram with recognizable parallel gateways can still connect them incorrectly.

**Exclusive gateways.** The legal-review alternative is exclusive: execute legal review or take the silent bypass. But not every generated exclusive gateway represents a business decision. The inspected Petri-to-BPMN converter also introduces gateways while translating places and silent routing. Some are structural artifacts rather than decisions needing a user-facing question. ([GitHub][7])

**Loop visualization.** A back edge must be read as “after inspection, either exit verification or correct and configure again.” It must not suggest that correction is mandatory in the final iteration, that the first iteration can be skipped, or that risk assessment and legal review repeat. Annotate the verification region with:

> Verification: `Configure → Inspect`, followed by zero or more `Correct → Configure → Inspect` repetitions.

**Silent routing.** A bypass around legal review is not a logged waiver activity. Label it as an explanatory annotation—“legal review omitted; unobserved routing”—rather than introducing a visible task that changes the model’s language.

**Layout.** Left-to-right placement, vertical alignment, proximity, and crossing edges do not define additional order. Legal review drawn to the left of verification must not be interpreted as a prerequisite. Include the annotation:

> Legal review and verification are unordered after their respective prerequisites; legal review may occur before, during, or after verification, but before release.

**Completion.** A BPMN end-event symbol is not evidence that the round-tripped net has no residual tokens. The exact-final-marking result belongs in the accompanying audit, not in a visual inference.

A stakeholder package should therefore contain the BPMN rendering, a small legend explaining generated routing nodes, the verification-loop annotation, the two join conditions, and representative early/interleaved/late legal-review traces. It should also state that routing conditions, resource assignments, durations, and business justification for skipping review are unspecified by the source.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject observed violations; quarantine artifacts with missing or inconclusive evidence.

1. **Invalid conversion output.** A conversion raises an exception, returns the wrong object type, omits a marking, references places outside the returned net, or requires unsupported net semantics. A failed tree conversion stops that route; it does not justify inventing an ordering.

2. **Failed workflow or soundness requirements.** A required workflow-net property or soundness check is false. A diagnostic exception or unavailable result is inconclusive, not a pass.

3. **Loss of required behavior.** Either prerequisite ordering disappears, or early/interleaved/late legal review is excluded. Missing joint-step witnesses also require quarantine when the preservation requirement includes concurrency rather than only trace language.

4. **Admission of forbidden behavior.** Verification becomes reachable before both prerequisites, release becomes reachable before inspection, legal review can occur after release, or archive can precede release. Invalid reachable prefixes count even when they cannot complete.

5. **Changed optionality or loop semantics.** Legal review becomes mandatory or impossible; legal review repeats; verification becomes optional; consecutive iterations lack correction; correction permits immediate exit; or rework repeats outer activities.

6. **Incorrect final acceptance.** A model is treated as complete because it covers the final marking while retaining extra tokens, cannot reach its exact final marking after a required witness, or permits further work after nominal completion.

7. **Unexplained activity-identity changes.** Labels are lost, renamed, merged, or duplicated without an audited mapping. Intentional duplication requires a separately justified equivalence target.

8. **Incomplete validation presented as success.** A search hits its state cap, an unrestricted diagnostic cannot be completed, or only sampled traces were examined while the artifact is advertised as equivalent.

9. **Uncontrolled derivative or deployment drift.** The diagram lacks source/version provenance, has been manually edited without revalidation, or behaves differently in the target BPMN engine than in the tested conversion environment.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

**Store the POWL specification as the canonical model.** Use a version-controlled builder or an explicit declarative representation retaining stable node identities, operator types, loop-child order, and precedence edges. Preserve the supplied source rather than reconstructing authority from a generated drawing.

**Use the primary pipeline’s BPMN as the stakeholder visualization.** The accepting Petri net is its executable verification companion. Both nets, the BPMN, any process tree, renderings, and audit reports are derivatives.

Each derivative should carry its source revision or hash, PM4Py/Python versions, conversion route and parameters, exception records, activity-identity checks, initial/final marking information, workflow/soundness results, bounded comparison domain, counterexamples or passing witness results, and visual-review status. An unexplained “conversion succeeded” flag is inadequate provenance.

**Allow the process-tree intermediate only as a gated experiment.** It is unnecessary for the primary route and has an identifiable structural risk here. If conversion fails, retain the exception as a legitimate pipeline outcome. If it succeeds, validate its accepting-net realization separately before attributing any later mismatch to BPMN conversion. Do not replace failed structural conversion with discovery from bounded synthetic traces and call the result equivalent.

**Make future edits in the canonical source.** Regenerate and re-audit derivatives after every source or converter-version change. A proposed BPMN edit should be treated as a change request: express the intended change in POWL, review its invariant consequences, update the witnesses where policy genuinely changes, and regenerate. Repeatedly importing an edited derivative and exporting another representation invites round-trip drift.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every proposed test passes, the following claims remain unestablished:

1. **“All unbounded visible behavior is equivalent.”** The completed-language comparison is bounded. Full finite reachability graphs could support stronger automata-based equivalence checking, but merely constructing them and comparing summaries does not perform that proof.

2. **“All causal, branching, and silent behavior is preserved.”** Visible-language equality and selected joint-step witnesses do not establish full partial-order equivalence, branching bisimulation, identical choice commitment, or equivalent silent divergence.

3. **“Every execution eventually releases.”** The source permits arbitrarily many corrections. Soundness concerns the availability of proper completion; it does not force an execution to stop choosing another iteration.

4. **“Every BPMN engine will execute the derivative identically.”** The audit examines these conversions and ordinary Petri-net semantics. Engine-specific gateway handling, execution settings, import behavior, and deployment extensions require separate validation.

5. **“The process is operationally or policy-complete.”** The source does not define risk thresholds for legal review, correction criteria, resource constraints, durations, probabilities, or evidence that a skipped review was appropriately authorized. Conversion fidelity cannot establish facts that the authoritative model never stated.
