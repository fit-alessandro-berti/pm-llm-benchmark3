## 7. Provisional conclusion

A candidate model with optional sign-off lifts fitness to 86%. Confidence in the *measurement-defect + model-obsolescence* explanation: **high ( 85%)**.

**Real compliance problem.** A genuine operational concern may persist and is *not* refuted by the measurement fixes. Two specific signals remain: (i) 25 enhanced-review traces in Jun–Jul and 30 in Aug–Sep that end without a recorded `Compliance Check`, and (ii) the 2026-07-10 supervisor memo reminding reviewers not to bypass checks. Neither audit nor reviewer-level conformance has yet distinguished true bypasses from missing logging events. A control-flow-only optional sign-off model *would mask* this concern by accepting both compliant and noncompliant traces. Confidence that a real compliance issue exists: **moderate ( 40–50%)**—plausible but unproven.

**Immediate actions.**

## 8. Model-governance recommendation

1. **Version every normative model** with a semantic version, an effective date, and a linked policy reference; forbid conformance runs against an unversioned or pre-policy tree. Any policy change must trigger a model-review ticket before the policy goes live.
2. **Publish an event-semantic contract** mapping each event label to its business meaning, provenance class (UI action, system action, gateway event), and authoritative source; label changes require contract update and downstream notification before release.
3. **Adopt a technical-event policy** that classifies events as control-flow-relevant or telemetry-only; telemetry events (`Queue Routed`) must be flagged and excluded from conformance logs by default, with explicit opt-in.
4. **Mandate timestamp provenance**: every event must carry both source-system time and a monotonic source sequence number; warehouse-ingestion time must never be used as the ordering key for conformance checking.
5. **Require data-aware constraints** for value- or attribute-gated steps; the normative artefact must be a data Petri net (or equivalent) with guards, not a control-flow-only tree, whenever the policy references case attributes.
6. **Run post-release conformance validation** within five business days of any UI, ETL, gateway, or policy change, comparing pre- and post-change fitness stratified by event class, case attributes, and reviewer; any unexplained fitness delta blocks release sign-off until diagnosed.
