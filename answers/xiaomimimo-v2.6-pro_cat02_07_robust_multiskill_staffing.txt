### Policy P-B: Full pooling + transparent dispatch (SERPT)
- **Eligibility**: any resource qualified for the activity (skill matrix non-"").
- **Dispatch rule**: minimize expected remaining processing time for the case (SERPT) = sum over remaining activities of expected service × the multiplier of the *cheapest eligible resource now available*, minus a case-affinity bonus. Concretely: **score = earliest predicted case completion (EPC)** — assign the next activity to the eligible resource that minimizes the case's predicted completion time, with ties broken by longest-idle.
  Simpler and auditable: **"Case Owner First, else SERPT"**: (1) if a resource is already qualified for the next activity and is idle or will be free within 5 min and is the case's current owner, keep the owner (no handover); (2) otherwise pick the eligible resource minimizing `available_at + multiplier × expected_service` (i.e., earliest finish time, EFT). Ties  longest idle  lowest multiplier.
  This is a classic, transparent, auditable rule.
- **Reservation**: none; P1 gets priority in the dispatch order (a P1 case's next activity is always scheduled before a P2/P3's whenever the resource is free).
- **Parallelization**: for ME/ES, run in parallel iff both branches can start within 5 min of each other (both resources free) and case priority is P1, or the predicted sequential completion would breach the SLA. Otherwise sequential on the best cross-skilled resource (Ada/Deepa for ME; Chen/Emil for ES).
- **Handover minimization**: built into the rule (case-affinity preference).
- **Overflow**: unlimited (pooling).

Risk: gaming, unfairness (fast resources get more work or slow resources get dumped on), skill erosion.
