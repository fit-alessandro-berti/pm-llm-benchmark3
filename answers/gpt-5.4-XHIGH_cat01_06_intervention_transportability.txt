The shared mechanism is narrow but real: when identity and document evidence are largely independent and both tasks are legally executable, making them concurrent can increase `true_overlap`, reduce `waiting_before_assessment`, and lower `cycle_time`. Site A is consistent with that mechanism because overlap rose sharply and waiting before Assessment fell. But the full 9-hour median reduction at A is not transportable as a treatment effect; part of it is clearly mixed with staffing, API, mix, and queue-management changes.

The site-specific mechanisms at B are substantial. `originals_required` blocks Document Check for 62% of cases, so A-style immediate verification parallelism is structurally impossible for the majority. `role_specialization`, `IT_case_lock`, and smaller downstream pools mean that even if tasks are nominally created together, they may not execute together. The mandatory legal branch and supplement loop also create downstream delay that was not present in A.

The main effect modifiers are `originals_required`, `digital_documents`, `case_complexity`, `role_specialization`, `IT_case_lock`, `assessment_capacity`, and `legal_capacity`. They govern both whether treatment becomes actual overlap and whether any saved time survives later queues. A similar control-flow sketch therefore does not guarantee transportability: partial-order feasibility, data prerequisites, resource topology, and event quality can differ enough that the same-looking intervention has a different causal meaning.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention
Eligibility rules:
- Stage 1: include only cases with no statutory originals requirement, digital documents present at registration, no public-sector/high-value flag, low-to-medium complexity, and a pilot lane that supports separate live child work items.
- Stage 2 shadow lane: originals-required cases may enter only for `Identity Check` plus `Originals Receipt` concurrency; `Document Check` is created in blocked state and cannot execute until receipt is complete.
- Stage 3 expansion: legal-review cases enter only after Stage 1 shows stable assessor/legal queues and no increase in rework or supplements.

Concurrent execution policy:
- At `Register`, create child work items for `Identity Check` and the applicable document path.
- For Stage 1 cases, `Identity Check` and `Document Check` may be executed concurrently by different roles.
- For Stage 2 shadow cases, `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt` may proceed concurrently; `Document Check` auto-releases only when `Originals Receipt` completes.

Statutory prerequisites that remain mandatory:
- `Document Check` may not start before `Originals Receipt` when originals are legally required.
- `Assessment` may start only after both identity verification and the relevant document path are complete.
- `Legal Review` may start only after `Assessment` for flagged cases.

Queue and WIP controls:
- Run daily queue huddles in pilot lanes.
- Cap active open work at roughly 4 per identity reviewer and 4 per document reviewer.
- Cap the ready queue for `Assessment` at 9 cases and for `Legal Review` at 6 cases; pause new treated releases if caps are breached.

Resource responsibilities:
- Identity reviewers own only `Identity Check`.
- The document pool owns `Originals Receipt` intake and `Document Check`.
- Assessors pull only fully joined-ready cases.
- Legal reviewers own the legal branch and any supplement re-entry decision.

Handling of legal-review and supplement loops:
- If `Legal Review` issues `Request Supplement`, reopen `Document Check`, then `Assessment`, then return to `Legal Review`.
- Prior completion of Document or Assessment does not waive the repeat path after a supplement.

Logging requirements:
- Record `case_id`, `work_item_id`, treatment version, eligibility stratum, originals-required flag, legal-review flag, supplement count, blocked reason, owner changes, and create/release/start/complete timestamps.
- Record whether any event was manually back-entered.
- Compute overlap from actual start/complete times, not task creation alone.

Compact POWL-style description:
`DocPath = XOR( 'Document Check', ->( 'Originals Receipt', 'Document Check' ) )`

`LegalGate = XOR( tau, LOOP( 'Legal Review', ->( 'Request Supplement', 'Document Check', 'Assessment' ) ) )`

`Decision = XOR( 'Approve', 'Reject' )`

`PO = { Register < Identity Check, Register < DocPath, Identity Check < Assessment, DocPath < Assessment, Assessment < LegalGate, LegalGate < Decision, Decision < Notify }`

The pilot separates implementation failure from mechanism failure by measuring both assignment and executed overlap. If treated eligible cases do not show materially higher auto-timestamped overlap, earlier Document starts, or shorter ready-to-Assessment delay, the intervention failed to implement: the case lock, staffing practice, or noncompliance prevented treatment from becoming real concurrency. If overlap rises as intended but cycle time barely improves, `p90` worsens, or assessor/legal queues lengthen, the mechanism is being neutralized downstream: Site B's active bottleneck is assessment, legal review, originals delay, or supplement loops rather than verification order itself. The shadow lane for originals-required cases further distinguishes genuine verification concurrency from mere administrative staging. Strata by originals, legal flag, and complexity show whether limits are structural or just attenuating. Randomized or stepped team-week assignment supplies a contemporaneous control against seasonality, mix shift, and management attention. Conformance checking against the candidate POWL then verifies whether behavior matched the intended design rather than only appearing better in headline cycle time.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code
```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    Sequence,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Operator,
)

# 1) Site A process trees
site_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)
site_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "-> ( 'Register', + ( 'Identity Check', 'Document Check' ), 'Assessment', X ( 'Approve', 'Reject' ), 'Notify' )"
)

# 2) Convert Site A trees to accepting Petri nets
net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_before)
net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_a_after)

# 3) Construct a reasonable Site B candidate with POWL
# Document path: either documents are already available, or originals must arrive first.
register = Transition("Register")
identity = Transition("Identity Check")
doc_direct = Transition("Document Check")
originals_then_doc = Sequence([
    Transition("Originals Receipt"),
    Transition("Document Check"),
])
document_path = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [doc_direct, originals_then_doc])
assessment = Transition("Assessment")

# Verification stage as a strict partial order.
# Assessment must wait for both identity verification and the chosen document path.
verification = StrictPartialOrder([register, identity, document_path, assessment])
verification.add_edge(register, identity)
verification.add_edge(register, document_path)
verification.add_edge(identity, assessment)
verification.add_edge(document_path, assessment)

# Optional legal branch with a supplement loop.
# LOOP(do, redo) means: perform Legal Review, then either exit,
# or execute Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment and return to Legal Review.
legal_redo = Sequence([
    Transition("Request Supplement"),
    Transition("Document Check"),
    Transition("Assessment"),
])
legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(Operator.LOOP, [Transition("Legal Review"), legal_redo])
legal_optional = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [SilentTransition(), legal_loop])

# Final decision and notification.
decision = OperatorPOWL(Operator.XOR, [Transition("Approve"), Transition("Reject")])
site_b_candidate = Sequence([verification, legal_optional, decision, Transition("Notify")])

# 5) Convert the POWL model to an accepting Petri net
net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_b_candidate)

# 6) Workflow-net / soundness checks
for name, net, im, fm in [
    ("Site A before", net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before),
    ("Site A after", net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after),
    ("Site B candidate", net_b, im_b, fm_b),
]:
    wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    soundness = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)
    sound_flag = soundness[0] if isinstance(soundness, tuple) else soundness
    print(f"{name}: workflow_net={wf}, sound={sound_flag}")

# 7) Optional playout to inspect generated traces
log = pm4py.play_out(net_b, im_b, fm_b, parameters={"no_traces": 5})
for i, trace in enumerate(list(log)[:5], start=1):
    activities = [event["concept:name"] for event in trace]
    print(f"sample_trace_{i}: {activities}")

# 8) Modeling limitations
# This structural model does NOT encode:
# - data-dependent legal requirements (for example, deciding at runtime which cases require originals or legal review)
# - resource capacities, utilization, queue priorities, or WIP caps
# - the one-owner case lock or manual back-entry behavior
# - service-time or waiting-time distributions
# - reviewer skill differences or cross-training effects
# Those issues matter for transportability even when the workflow net is sound.
```

## 9. Recommendation
`conditional go`

Transfer the mechanism, not Site A's headline effect. What is portable is the idea that when identity and document evidence are independent and both tasks are legally executable, concurrency can reduce waiting before `Assessment`. What should not be copied is the blanket rule "create Identity Check and Document Check simultaneously immediately after Register for every case." At Site B that would violate statutory ordering for the 62% originals-required cases, ignore the 40% legal-review branch, and overstate feasibility in a system that still behaves like a one-owner workflow.

The first subgroup should be no-original, digitally submitted, low-to-medium complexity cases in pilot lanes where separate child tasks can truly be owned concurrently and automatically timestamped. If management wants a broader modified design, originals-required cases can join only through `Identity Check || Originals Receipt`, with `Document Check` remaining blocked until receipt. Legal-review cases should wait until the first wave shows that assessor and legal queues stay stable and that supplement loops do not rise.

Expected benefits are modest overall but meaningful in the eligible subgroup: lower waiting before `Assessment`, some cycle-time reduction, and better visibility of blocked work. The main risks are fake overlap from back-entered timestamps, bunching into the 3-person assessment pool, and a worse `p90` if earlier verification completion only feeds the legal/supplement loop faster. Expand only if treated eligible cases show real overlap, shorter ready-to-Assessment delay, stable or lower rework, and no material deterioration in assessor/legal queue age or `p90`. Modify the design if overlap rises but end-to-end time does not. Abandon the rollout if true overlap cannot be executed under the case-lock regime or if statutory and downstream bottlenecks dominate the observed effect.

## 10. Assumption register
1. A sizable subset of Site B cases truly does not require originals and can legally start `Document Check` immediately after `Register` - why needed: otherwise the treated subgroup is too small for meaningful transport - how to test: audit statutory flags against sampled cases and logs - consequence if false: restrict to `Identity Check || Originals Receipt` staging or treat A-style overlap as non-viable.
2. Identity and document evidence are sufficiently independent in eligible Site B cases - why needed: overlap only helps if one task need not wait on findings from the other - how to test: reviewer interviews plus dependency coding on a case sample - consequence if false: parallelization creates rework more than speed.
3.
