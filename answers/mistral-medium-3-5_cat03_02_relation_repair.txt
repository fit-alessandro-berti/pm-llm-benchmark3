## **8. Over-repair warnings**

1. **Forcing `ev08  PK-8`**:
   - *Risk*: Assumes `IT-B1` was packed in `PK-8` without scanner evidence. Could create a false `Pack Item  Dispatch Package` path in the OC-DFG.
   - *Effect*: Overestimates packaging efficiency; may hide real scanner outages.

2. **Linking `INV-44` to `ORD-B` based on arithmetic**:
   - *Risk*: Ignores possible fees/discounts (R6). Could merge unrelated orders in the OTG.
   - *Effect*: Distorts invoice-order cardinality; may falsely suggest multi-order invoicing is the norm.

3. **Assuming `ev11` confirms both `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y`**:
   - *Risk*: Wildcard `N-42*` may not include `DEL-Y`. Could duplicate `Confirm Delivery` edges in the OC-DFG.
   - *Effect*: Overstates delivery confirmation rates.

4. **Inferring `PAY-2 settles INV-44` because `PAY-1` does**:
   - *Risk*: Circular reasoning (R10). Could create a false `Payment  Invoice` edge in the OTG.
   - *Effect*: Hides unapplied payments; skews payment settlement metrics.

5. **Adding `PK-8 packs IT-B1` without E2O evidence**:
   - *Risk*: O2O edge lacks independent confirmation. Could create a spurious `Item  Package` link in the OTG.
   - *Effect*: Over-represents packaging completeness; may mask operational issues.

6. **Linking `ev10` to `DEL-X` (instead of `DEL-Y`)**:
   - *Risk*: Misinterprets `external_ref=N-421?` as `N-420`. Could swap `Delivery` objects in the ET–OT graph.
   - *Effect*: Incorrect route assignments; distorts delivery performance analysis.

7. **Forcing all `Payment` objects to settle `INV-44`**:
   - *Risk*: Ignores unapplied cash (R7). Could create a many-to-one `Payment  Invoice` relation where none exists.
   - *Effect*: Understates payment complexity; may hide financial discrepancies.
