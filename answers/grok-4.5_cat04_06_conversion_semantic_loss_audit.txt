# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

Restated from the authoritative `source_powl` definition and the stated intended semantics. Positive/negative witnesses are visible activity sequences (tau hidden where noted). “Observable after hiding tau?” asks whether the invariant remains meaningful on the visible projection.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 | **Start:** `Register Change` is the unique initial visible activity; nothing precedes it. | `Register Change, Assess Risk, …` | `Assess Risk, Register Change, …` | Yes |
| I2 | **Fork after register:** `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` are both enabled after registration and are unordered (true concurrency / interleaving both allowed). | `…Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence…` and `…Register Change, Prepare Evidence, Assess Risk…` | Forcing a fixed order as the only legal order (e.g. model that rejects one interleaving) | Yes |
| I3 | **No edge AssessPrepare:** neither activity causally depends on the other. | Both interleavings of I2 | Trace set that admits only one order | Yes |
| I4 | **Optional Legal:** after `Assess Risk`, either `Legal Review` occurs once or it is skipped (XOR with silent). | `…Assess Risk, Legal Review, …Release…` and `…Assess Risk, …Release…` with no Legal Review | Mandatory Legal Review on every trace, or multiple Legal Reviews in one optional branch instance | Partially (skip is tau; absence of Legal Review is the visible skip) |
| I5 | **Legal placement:** Legal Review is only on the optional-legal branch after Assess Risk; it is not required to finish before verification starts. | `…Assess Risk, Configure Trial, Legal Review, Inspect Trial…` (overlap) | Model that places a strict causal edge Legalverification start or verificationLegal as mandatory | Yes for label presence/order constraints |
| I6 | **Verification guard:** verification (`Configure Trial` first visible of the loop body) cannot start until **both** `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` completed. | `…Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Configure Trial…` (any order of the first two) | `…Register Change, Configure Trial…` before both predecessors | Yes |
| I7 | **Concurrency optional-legal  verification:** at the POWL level there is **no** order edge between `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`; they may overlap. | Legal Review interleaved with Configure/Inspect/Correct | Artificial total order Legal before all verification or after all verification as the only behavior | Yes |
| I8 | **Loop body order:** every verification iteration runs `Configure Trial` **before** `Inspect Trial` (strict partial order inside `trial_body`). | `…Configure Trial, Inspect Trial…` | `…Inspect Trial, Configure Trial…` inside one iteration | Yes |
| I9 | **Binary LOOP / redo:** a repeated iteration is separated by `Correct Defect` (redo child); structure is do=`trial_body`, redo=`Correct Defect`. | `Configure, Inspect, Correct Defect, Configure, Inspect` | `Configure, Inspect, Configure, Inspect` without Correct Defect between iterations | Yes |
| I10 | **Minimum iteration count  1:** at least one `trial_body` execution occurs; empty verification is forbidden. | traces with 1 Configure+Inspect | trace that reaches Release with zero Configure/Inspect | Yes |
| I11 | **Exit loop then join:** `Release Change` is enabled only after **both** (a) optional-legal branch finished and (b) verification loop exited. | both branches done then Release | `Release Change` while Inspect still outstanding, or while loop not exited | Yes |
| I12 | **Finality:** `Archive Change` occurs after `Release Change` and is the last visible activity of a completed run. | `…Release Change, Archive Change` (end) | `Archive` before `Release`; or activity after Archive in a completing trace | Yes |
| I13 | **Silent only for skip-legal:** the only intended silent transition in the source is `skip_legal`; loop/control silents may appear after Petri conversion but are not source-labeled activities. | visible labels  declared transitions | extra visible labels invented by conversion | N/A (meta) |
| I14 | **Label uniqueness:** each business activity label appears as one logical transition type in the source (no duplicate source nodes with same label). | single node per label | duplicate conflicting labels for distinct nodes | Yes |

**Structural summary (not a substitute for the table):**

```text
Register
    Assess Risk  optional_legal = XOR(Legal Review, )
                         
    Prepare Evidence 
                                       
              LOOP( do: ConfigureInspect , redo: Correct Defect )
                                       
                    join(optional_legal, verification_loop)
                                       
                               Release Change  Archive Change
```

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 (primary):** POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`  `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Stays closest to POWL operational semantics; Petri net can express concurrency, XOR-skip, and LOOP without forcing block structure; BPMN derived from marked net is the supported path | BPMN layout/gateways may **look** more sequential or nested than the net; silent transitions become routing noise; round-trip BPMNnet may not be identical | `im`/`fm` null; unsound net; BPMN converter drops markings or invents gateways that change join semantics |
| **P2 (secondary, allowed to fail):** POWL  accepting Petri net  process tree  BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net`  `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` in try/except  on success `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | If the net is free-choice / block-structured enough, tree gives cleaner hierarchical BPMN and easier stakeholder reading | Source has **non-block** overlap: optional-legal  verification with a later sync join, plus concurrent AssessPrepare feeding different successors — classic obstruction for faithful process trees | `convert_to_process_tree` **raises**; or tree succeeds but **loses** interleaving/overlap (over-sequentializes); BPMN from tree  BPMN from net |
| **P3 (audit-only derivative):** BPMN  accepting Petri net (round trip) | `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_net)` and, if tree path worked, `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_from_tree)` | Enables soundness, footprints, reachability, and bounded trace compare against `net_from_powl` | Marking incompleteness; gateway reverse-mapping ambiguity; tau proliferation | Conversion returns net without usable im/fm; soundness fails; footprints diverge |
| **P4 (not chosen as primary):** Direct mental “POWLBPMN” without net | No first-class PM4Py POWLBPMN API assumed | None justified | Inventing gateways by hand confuses layout with semantics | **Rejected a priori** — no authoritative direct API in the given interface |

**Primary pipeline:** **P1** (`POWL  accepting Petri net  BPMN`).  
**Secondary:** **P2**, with failure treated as a **valid diagnostic** when block structure is insufficient—not as something to suppress.

```python
"""
POWL conversion pipelines with diagnostics.
Authoritative source: source_powl (see model definition).
Primary: POWL  Petri net  BPMN
Secondary: POWL  Petri net  process tree  BPMN (may fail)
"""

from __future__ import annotations

import os
import traceback
from copy import deepcopy

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 0. Authoritative source model
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
register = Transition(label="Register Change")
assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()
optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
)

configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP,
    children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
)

release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        register,
        assess_risk,
        prepare_evidence,
        optional_legal,
        verification_loop,
        release_change,
        archive_change,
    ]
)

source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)

OUT_DIR = "conversion_artifacts"
os.makedirs(OUT_DIR, exist_ok=True)


def _safe_markings(result):
    """Normalize convert_to_petri_net return value; do not assume non-null markings."""
    if result is None:
        return None, None, None
    if isinstance(result, tuple):
        if len(result) == 3:
            net, im, fm = result
            return net, im, fm
        if len(result) == 1:
            return result[0], None, None
    return result, None, None


def diagnose_accepting_net(name, net, im, fm):
    """Workflow-net + soundness diagnostics; never assume markings exist."""
    print(f"\n===== DIAGNOSTICS: {name} =====")
    if net is None:
        print("NET IS None — skip diagnostics")
        return {"workflow": None, "sound": None}

    try:
        is_wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
        print(f"check_is_workflow_net: {is_wf}")
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"check_is_workflow_net raised: {ex}")
        is_wf = None

    is_sound = None
    if im is None or fm is None:
        print("WARNING: im or fm is None — soundness check skipped/partial")
    else:
        try:
            is_sound = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
            print(f"check_soundness: {is_sound}")
        except Exception as ex:
            print(f"check_soundness raised: {ex}")
            traceback.print_exc()
            is_sound = None
    return {"workflow": is_wf, "sound": is_sound}


def try_save_petri(net, im, fm, path):
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        print(f"skip save_vis_petri_net (null component): {path}")
        return
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_petri_net(net, im, fm, path)
        print(f"saved {path}")
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"save_vis_petri_net failed for {path}: {ex}")


def try_save_bpmn(bpmn, path):
    if bpmn is None:
        print(f"skip save_vis_bpmn: {path}")
        return
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_bpmn(bpmn, path)
        print(f"saved {path}")
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"save_vis_bpmn failed for {path}: {ex}")


def try_save_tree(tree, path):
    if tree is None:
        return
    try:
        pm4py.save_vis_process_tree(tree, path)
        print(f"saved {path}")
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"save_vis_process_tree failed: {ex}")

# 1. POWL  accepting Petri net
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
print("\n### P1 step: POWL  Petri net")
try:
    pm4py.save_vis_powl(source_powl, os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "00_source_powl.png"))
except Exception as ex:
    print(f"save_vis_powl: {ex}")

_net_result = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)
net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = _safe_markings(_net_result)

if net_from_powl is None:
    raise RuntimeError("POWLPetri conversion returned no net; cannot continue primary pipeline")

diag_powl_net = diagnose_accepting_net(
    "net_from_powl", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl

try_save_petri(
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
    os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "01_net_from_powl.png"),
)

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Petri net  BPMN (primary pipeline artifact)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
print("\n### P1 step: Petri net  BPMN")
bpmn_from_net = None
if im_from_powl is None or fm_from_powl is None:
    print("ERROR: cannot call convert_to_bpmn without im/fm")
else:
    try:
        bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
        try_save_bpmn(bpmn_from_net, os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "02_bpmn_from_net.png"))
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"convert_to_bpmn(net) failed: {ex}")
        traceback.print_exc()

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Petri net  process tree (secondary; expected possible failure)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
print("\n### P2 step: Petri net  process tree (may fail)")
process_tree_from_net = None
tree_conversion_error = None
bpmn_from_tree = None

if im_from_powl is None or fm_from_powl is None:
    tree_conversion_error = "missing im/fm"
    print("SKIP process tree: missing markings")
else:
    try:
        process_tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
            net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
        )
        print("process-tree conversion SUCCEEDED")
        try_save_tree(
            process_tree_from_net,
            os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "03_process_tree_from_net.png"),
        )
    except Exception as ex:
        tree_conversion_error = ex
        process_tree_from_net = None
        print(f"process-tree conversion FAILED (interpreted, not suppressed): {ex}")
        traceback.print_exc()

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. If tree exists  BPMN from tree
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
if process_tree_from_net is not None:
    print("\n### P2 step: process tree  BPMN")
    try:
        # BPMN from process tree: convert_to_bpmn accepts tree in PM4Py
        bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(process_tree_from_net)

        traceback.print_exc()

# Tree itself as accepting net (if tree exists), for completeness

if process_tree_from_net is not None:
    try:
```

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

Assessment vocabulary only: `expected preserved`, `validated preserved`, `approximated`, `ambiguous`, `lost`, `conversion failed`, `not tested`.

| Source invariant | POWLPetri | PetriBPMN | PetriTree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| I1 Register first | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | POWL start maps to source place; BPMN start event usually OK; tree may insert  |
| I2–I3 Assess  Prepare (true concurrency) | expected preserved | approximated | lost / conversion failed | ambiguous | Net can keep concurrent places; BPMN parallel gateway **if** discovered; tree often forces sequence or fails on non-block joins |
| I4 Optional Legal (XOR + ) | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | ambiguous | Skip is silent: BPMN may show empty branch or XOR that looks mandatory; reverse map may add extra  |
| I5 Legal  verification (no order edge) | expected preserved | approximated | lost / conversion failed | ambiguous | **Critical:** partial order has no Legalloop edge; block tree usually cannot express “XOR branch concurrent with LOOP then sync” without distortion |
| I6 Verification after both Assess & Prepare | expected preserved | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | AND-join into loop entry in net; tree may encode as strict nest |
| I8 Configure  Inspect each iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | Local sequence is block-structured; least at risk |
| I9 Redo = Correct Defect between iterations | expected preserved | approximated | expected preserved if tree OK | ambiguous | LOOP operator  Petri loop; BPMN while/loop markers vary by exporter |
| I10 1 verification iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved if tree OK | not tested | Classic do-redo LOOP is execute-do-at-least-once |
| I11 Join before Release (legal branch  loop) | expected preserved | approximated | lost / conversion failed | ambiguous | Petri synchronizing places; BPMN may draw misleading sequence flow; tree may sequentialize Legal before/after whole loop |
| I12 Archive after Release final | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | Linear tail |
| Silent optional behavior | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | ambiguous |  skip vs omitted flow vs conditional event — visual  semantic |
| Activity-label uniqueness | expected preserved | expected preserved | expected preserved | approximated | Round trip should not duplicate business labels; routing nodes unlabeled |
| Initial/final markings | expected preserved | ambiguous | ambiguous | ambiguous | Must **check** im/fm non-null after every conversion; BPMN start/end  automatic correct fm equality |
| Workflow-net + soundness | not tested until diagnostics | not tested | not tested | not tested | Code runs `check_is_workflow_net` / `check_soundness`; pass is necessary not sufficient for I5/I2 |

### Discussion (required topics)

**True concurrency vs arbitrary interleaving.**  
POWL `StrictPartialOrder` without an edge states **absence of causal order**, not merely “scheduler may interleave.” An accepting Petri net with two concurrent places preserves that. A process tree that encodes `AND(Assess, Prepare)` preserves interleavings of those two; but when successors differ (`optional_legal` only after Assess, loop after both), the **remainder** is not a pure AND of equal subtrees. Petritree often **fails** or **approximates** by total-ordering Assess before Prepare (or vice versa). BPMN parallel gateways preserve interleaving only if the split/join pair matches the net; diagram layout alone never proves it.

**No edge between Optional Legal Review and verification.**  
This is the main non-block pattern: after Assess, Legal’s XOR is concurrent with the loop that also waits on Prepare. Faithful models allow Legal during Configure/Inspect/Correct. A process-tree intermediate that only allows nested XOR/LOOP/SEQ/AND cannot place “optional fragment concurrent with loop, both joining before Release” without extra  or by **losing** overlap (e.g. `SEQ(XOR(Legal,), LOOP(...))` or the reverse). **P2 failure or sequential approximation is the expected honest outcome**, not a bug to hide.

**Silent optional behavior.**  
Source skip is an explicit `SilentTransition` in XOR. After Petri conversion, additional structural  transitions appear. After BPMN, skips may look like missing tasks, default flows, or blank gateway arms. Hiding  in trace projection is required for witness checks; treating “no Legal Review node drawn” as “legal path deleted” is a visual false negative.

**Binary-loop semantics.**  
POWL `Operator.LOOP` with `[trial_body, correct_defect]` is do/redo: at least one do; redo then do again. Petri encoding should not allow zero iterations or allow Configure without going through loop entry. BPMN loop markers or cyclic sequence flows can be misread as “zero or more” BPMN standard loops—documentation must state **at least once**, redo label = Correct Defect.

**Join before Release Change.**  
Release is a synchronization point for `optional_legal` and `verification_loop`. Petri nets implement this with preset places of a transition (or -join). BPMN must use a **parallel join** (or equivalent converging AND), not an exclusive merge. If the diagram shows a single sequence flow into Release, readers may think one branch suffices—**semantic mismatch even if engine net is correct**.

**Activity-label uniqueness.**  
Source labels are unique. Converters must not split one label into two conflicting visible tasks or merge Correct Defect with Inspect. Routing gateways should remain unlabeled (or ).

**Initial and final markings.**  
Code refuses to assume im/fm non-null. Soundness and round-trip final-marking equality are meaningless without them. BPMNnet may yield different fm shape (extra sink places).

**Gateway / routing ambiguity in BPMN visualization.**  
Exclusive vs parallel gateways are easy to swap visually; silent routing becomes empty diamonds; graph auto-layout may place Legal **above** the loop and suggest false precedence. **Serialization success  behavioral equivalence.**

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

```python
"""
Round-trip validation: compare net_from_powl to every round-tripped accepting net.
Not claimed exhaustive beyond the explicit bound.
"""

from collections import deque
from typing import Any, Dict, List, Optional, Set, Tuple

import pm4py

# Assumes net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl and round-trip nets
# from the conversion script are in scope.


def footprints_safe(net, im, fm, name):
    print(f"\n--- footprints: {name} ---")
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        print("missing net/im/fm")
        return None
    try:
        fp = pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm)
        print(fp)
        return fp
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"discover_footprints failed: {ex}")
        return None


def reachability_safe(net, im, fm, name):
    print(f"\n--- reachability graph: {name} ---")
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        print("missing net/im/fm")
        return None
    try:
        rg = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
        print(f"reachability graph object: {type(rg)}")
        return rg
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"convert_to_reachability_graph failed: {ex}")
        return None


def _tr_label(t) -> Optional[str]:
    return t.label if hasattr(t, "label") else None


def _marking_key(marking) -> Tuple:
    """Hashable marking snapshot for visited set."""
    if marking is None:
        return ()
    try:
        items = tuple(sorted((getattr(p, "name", str(p)), int(marking[p])) for p in marking))
        return items
    except Exception:
        return (str(marking),)


def bounded_visible_traces(
    net,
    im,
    fm,
    *,
    max_visible_len: int = 16,
    max_loop_iterations_hint: int = 3,
    max_silent_burst: int = 32,
    max_states: int = 20000,
) -> Tuple[Set[Tuple[str, ...]], Set[Tuple[str, ...]], Dict[str, Any]]:
    """
    Bounded DFS/BFS style exploration of visible traces.
    - Hides transitions with label None (tau)
    - Completed traces require exact equality with fm
    - Avoids infinite silent cycling via (marking, silent_burst, visible_trace) state
    - Enforces visible-length bound and a soft bound on Correct Defect count
      (proxy for loop iterations)
    Comparison is NOT exhaustive for unbounded loops; only under these bounds.
    """
    completed: Set[Tuple[str, ...]] = set()
    prefix_seen: Set[Tuple[str, ...]] = set()
    stats = {
        "expanded": 0,
        "pruned_silent_cycle": 0,
        "pruned_bound": 0,
        "truncated": False,
        "exhaustive_under_bound": True,
    }

    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        stats["exhaustive_under_bound"] = False
        return completed, prefix_seen, stats

    # state: marking, visible tuple, silent_burst, defect_count
    Start = (im, tuple(), 0, 0)
    dq = deque([Start])
    visited = set()
    visited.add((_marking_key(im), tuple(), 0, 0))

    while dq:
        marking, vis, silent_burst, defects = dq.popleft()
        stats["expanded"] += 1
        if stats["expanded"] > max_states:
            stats["truncated"] = True
            stats["exhaustive_under_bound"] = False
            break

        prefix_seen.add(vis)

        # exact final marking equality => completed accepting trace
        if marking == fm:
            completed.add(vis)
            # still may continue if other enabled transitions exist (extra tokens paths)
            # but for sound WF-nets typically terminal

        try:
            enabled = list(pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking))
        except Exception:
            # fallback: some versions need (net, marking) from objects.petri_net
            try:
                from pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics import enabled_transitions as _en

                enabled = list(_en(net, marking))
            except Exception as ex:
                print(f"get_enabled_transitions failed: {ex}")
                stats["exhaustive_under_bound"] = False
                break

        if not enabled and marking != fm:
            # deadlock non-final
            pass

        for t in enabled:
            lab = _tr_label(t)
            # fire
            try:
                from pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics import PetriNetSemantics

                new_marking = PetriNetSemantics.fire(net, t, marking)
            except Exception:
                try:
                    from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics as petri_semantics

                    new_marking = petri_semantics.execute(t, net, marking)
                except Exception as ex:
                    print(f"fire failed for {t}: {ex}")
                    stats["exhaustive_under_bound"] = False
                    continue

            if lab is None:
                new_silent = silent_burst + 1
                new_vis = vis
                new_def = defects
                if new_silent > max_silent_burst:
                    stats["pruned_silent_cycle"] += 1
                    continue
            else:
                new_silent = 0
                new_vis = vis + (lab,)
                new_def = defects + (1 if lab == "Correct Defect" else 0)
                if len(new_vis) > max_visible_len:
                    stats["pruned_bound"] += 1
                    stats["exhaustive_under_bound"] = False
                    continue
                if new_def > max_loop_iterations_hint:
                    stats["pruned_bound"] += 1
                    stats["exhaustive_under_bound"] = False
                    continue

            st_key = (_marking_key(new_marking), new_vis, new_silent, new_def)
            # For tau, also guard pure marking+vis cycles
            mk = (_marking_key(new_marking), new_vis)
            if lab is None and mk in visited and new_silent > 0:
                # allow until max_silent_burst via st_key
                pass
            if st_key in visited:
                stats["pruned_silent_cycle"] += 1
                continue
            visited.add(st_key)
            if lab is None:
                visited.add(mk)
            dq.append((new_marking, new_vis, new_silent, new_def))

    return completed, prefix_seen, stats

def compare_nets(name_a, net_a, im_a, fm_a, name_b, net_b, im_b, fm_b):
    print(f"\n########## COMPARE {name_a} vs {name_b} ##########")
    if any(x is None for x in (net_a, im_a, fm_a, net_b, im_b, fm_b)):
        print("UNCOMPARABLE: null net or marking")
        return {"comparable": False}

    # Soundness both sides
    try:
        s_a = pm4py.check_soundness(net_a, im_a, fm_a, print_diagnostics=False)
    except Exception as ex:
        s_a = f"error:{ex}"
    try:
        s_b = pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b, print_diagnostics=False)
    except Exception as ex:
        s_b = f"error:{ex}"
    print(f"soundness {name_a}={s_a} {name_b}={s_b}")

    fp_a = footprints_safe(net_a, im_a, fm_a, name_a)
    fp_b = footprints_safe(net_b, im_b, fm_b, name_b)
    fp_equal = None
    if fp_a is not None and fp_b is not None:
        try:
            fp_equal = fp_a == fp_b
        except Exception:
            fp_equal = "uncomparable_struct"
        print(f"footprints_equal: {fp_equal}")

    reachability_safe(net_a, im_a, fm_a, name_a)
    reachability_safe(net_b, im_b, fm_b, name_b)

    comp_a, pref_a, st_a = bounded_visible_traces(net_a, im_a, fm_a)
    comp_b, pref_b, st_b = bounded_visible_traces(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    print(f"completed_count {name_a}={len(comp_a)} stats={st_a}")
    print(f"completed_count {name_b}={len(comp_b)} stats={st_b}")

    only_a = comp_a - comp_b
    only_b = comp_b - comp_a
    print(f"symmetric difference size: only_a={len(only_a)} only_b={len(only_b)}")
    if only_a:
        print("example only in A:", next(iter(only_a)))
    if only_b:
        print("example only in B:", next(iter(only_b)))

    pos_a = {w: witness_in_set(w, comp_a) for w in POSITIVE_WITNESSES}
    pos_b = {w: witness_in_set(w, comp_b) for w in POSITIVE_WITNESSES}
    neg_a = {w: witness_in_set(w, comp_a) for w in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES}
    neg_b = {w: witness_in_set(w, comp_b) for w in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES}
    print("POSITIVE accepted in A?", pos_a)
    print("POSITIVE accepted in B?", pos_b)
    print("NEGATIVE wrongly accepted in A? (want all False)", neg_a)
    print("NEGATIVE wrongly accepted in B? (want all False)", neg_b)

    # Optional playout if API present
    try:
        # model playout may exist as pm4py.play_out
        pl = getattr(pm4py, "play_out", None)
        if pl is not None:
            log_a = pl(net_a, im_a, fm_a)
            log_b = pl(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
            print(f"play_out sizes: A={len(log_a)} B={len(log_b)}")
    except Exception as ex:
        print(f"play_out not used: {ex}")

    return {
        "comparable": True,
        "sound_a": s_a,
        "sound_b": s_b,
        "footprints_equal": fp_equal,
        "completed_equal_under_bound": comp_a == comp_b,
        "stats_a": st_a,
        "stats_b": st_b,
        "pos_a": pos_a,
        "pos_b": pos_b,
        "neg_a": neg_a,
        "neg_b": neg_b,
        "exhaustive_under_bound": st_a.get("exhaustive_under_bound")
        and st_b.get("exhaustive_under_bound")
        and comp_a == comp_b,
    }
```

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

Even when `net_from_powl` is sound and `bpmn_from_net` round-trips acceptably, **BPMN rendering can be misread**:

1. **Parallel gateways**  
   Assess  Prepare may be drawn as a parallel split after Register. Readers often assume a matching parallel join **immediately** after those two tasks. In the source, joins are **asymmetric**: Prepare joins only into verification; Assess also feeds optional Legal. A “box” around Assess+Prepare is a layout fiction.

2. **Exclusive gateways**  
   Optional Legal is XOR(Legal, ). Diagrams may show a lone Legal task on a side path without an explicit empty arm, so stakeholders think Legal is mandatory—or think the gateway is an OR. Conversely, an explicit blank arm may be read as “error path.”

3. **Loop visualization**  
   BPMN may use a looping subprocess, sequence-flow cycles, or duplicated tasks. “Standard loop is zero-or-more” mental models conflict with **1** POWL LOOP. Correct Defect may appear as a second outgoing path rather than redo, suggesting optional repair after release.

4. **Silent routing nodes**  
    from skip and from Petri expansions become gateways or invisible tasks. Crowded diamonds suggest complex business decisions that do not exist in the source (only skip-legal is a business-silent choice).

5. **Apparent ordering from graph layout**  
   Auto-layout often stacks Optional Legal **above** the loop and draws Release to the right, implying `Legal  Loop  Release` even when edges allow overlap. **Top-to-bottom reading order is not a causal order.**

6. **Join before Release**  
   If the tool draws two sequence flows into Release without a parallel join icon, readers may interpret exclusive arrival (“either legal done or verification done”). That would admit **release before verification**—a critical semantic error relative to I11.

**Annotations / documentation to reduce ambiguity**

- Attach an **invariant card** (I1–I12) to every exported PNG/SVG.  
- Stereotype gateways: `AND-split post-Register`, `XOR optional Legal (empty=skip)`, `AND-join pre-Release`.  
- Label the loop: `LOOP do=ConfigureInspect; redo=Correct Defect; minIterations=1`.  
- Explicit note: “No causal order between Legal Review and verification activities; interleaving allowed until pre-Release join.”  
- Publish **positive/negative witness traces** beside the diagram.  
- Prefer Petri-net viz for auditors; BPMN only with “derivative / non-authoritative” watermark.  
- If P2 tree fails, document failure reason on the BPMN stub rather than forcing a sequential tree drawing.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine a conversion result if **any** of the following hold:

1. **Technical:** `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` yields `net is None` or missing `im`/`fm` when required for downstream checks.  
2. **Unsoundness / non-workflow:** `check_is_workflow_net` is false or `check_soundness` is false (or raises) on `net_from_powl` or on a round-tripped net presented as equivalent.  
3. **Lost required interleaving:** bounded exploration or footprints show that one of `{AssessPrepare, PrepareAssess}` is impossible while the other is required—collapse of I2/I3.  
4. **Admission of release before verification:** any completed accepting trace contains `Release Change` with no prior full `Configure Trial``Inspect Trial` iteration (violates I10/I11), or negative witness #1 is accepted.  
5. **Optional behavior collapsed:** every completed trace forces `Legal Review`, or the skip path is impossible; or Legal can occur more than once per instance without model support.  
6. **Failed exact-final-marking test:** traces that should complete do not reach `marking == fm`, or “completed” is defined only by sink absence of enabled transitions without fm equality.  
7. **Loop mutilation:** zero-iteration verification accepted; or second iteration without `Correct Defect`; or Configure/Inspect order reversed inside an iteration.  
8. **False synchronization:** model requires Legal Review to finish before loop start (or after loop end) as the **only** behavior, eliminating I5/I7 overlap—especially common if a process-tree path “succeeds” via over-sequentialization.  
9. **Silent/label corruption:** visible labels appear that are not in the source alphabet; duplicate conflicting business tasks; tau projected into a labeled dummy activity shown to stakeholders as real work.  
10. **Secondary pipeline misuse:** process-tree conversion raises (or succeeds with language drift) but the tree/BPMN is still stored as authoritative without quarantine flag.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

| Role | Representation | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Authoritative (storage)** | **Source POWL** (`source_powl` serialized code or POWL object), optionally paired with the **accepting Petri net** `(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)` as the operational semantic snapshot | POWL is what the designer stated (partial order, XOR skip, LOOP). The Petri net is the executable expansion PM4Py defines—best machine-checkable form for soundness and witnesses |
| **Stakeholder visualization** | **BPMN derived via P1** (`bpmn_from_net`), watermarked derivative | Familiar notation; must not be edited as source of truth |
| **Derivative artifacts** | process tree (if any), BPMN-from-tree, PNGs, footprints dumps, reachability graphs, round-trip nets | All regenerable; never primary |

**Validation evidence that must accompany each derivative**

- For Petri snapshot: workflow + soundness report; export of im/fm; bounded witness pass/fail table for I1–I12.  
- For BPMN-from-net: round-trip net soundness; footprint compare / bounded trace diff vs `net_from_powl`; explicit list of invariants only `approximated` in the diagram.  
- For any tree-based artifact: either conversion exception log **or** full language-diff quarantine report; default policy = **do not ship** tree BPMN unless `completed_equal_under_bound` and all positive/negative witnesses match.

**Process-tree intermediate:** **Not allowed as an unattended stage** in the canonical pipeline. Allowed only as an optional optimization when automated equivalence evidence passes. Given optional-legal  verification + asymmetric predecessors, **expect P2 to fail or drift**—failure is correct engineering.

**Avoiding round-trip drift on future edits**

1. Edit **only** POWL (or agreed POWL generator); never “fix” BPMN and write back.  
2. CI gate: recompute Petri + soundness + witness suite on each change.  
3. Store hashes of `(powl, net, im, fm)` and regenerate BPMN; fail if BPMN hand-edited.  
4. Forbid promoting tree/BPMN round-trip nets to replace `net_from_powl` without invariant audit.  
5. Version the invariant table with the model; diagrams without invariant IDs are non-compliant deliverables.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every test in §§3–5 passes, at least these claims remain unestablished:

1. **Full language equivalence for unbounded loops**  
   Bounded exploration (visible length / Correct Defect cap / state cap) cannot prove that arbitrarily many verification iterations match across nets; only that no counterexample exists inside the bound.

2. **True concurrency vs. interleaving equivalence under all observers**  
   Trace (interleaving) equivalence does not prove preservation of **step** or **partial-order** semantics (e.g. whether Assess and Prepare are simultaneously fireable as a multi-step). Footprints are a partial proxy, not a complete concurrent-history equivalence.

3. **BPMN execution-semantics alignment across tools**  
   Passing PM4Py’s BPMNPetri conversion does not prove that Camunda/Engine-X/token games interpret gateways, default flows, and loops identically to `net_from_powl`. Vendor-specific none-interrupting paths and subprocess loop characteristics remain untested.

4. **Completeness of the negative witness set**  
   Hand-crafted negative traces do not cover all illegal behaviors (e.g. subtle double-join token games, Legal after Release, duplicate Archive). Absence of failures on the listed witnesses is not a universal safety proof.

5. **Hidden  side effects on branching probability and monitoring**  
   Tests hide `label is None`. They do not establish that added silent routes preserve intended choice fairness, observability for conformance checking against real logs, or that skip-legal remains the only **business** silent decision.

6. **Stability under PM4Py version skew**  
   Successful conversion today does not prove the same POWL yields marking-identical nets or the same BPMN gateway topology under future PM4Py releases—hence the need for frozen canonical POWL + re-validation, not trust in stored BPMN alone.
