# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The authoritative artifact is the POWL object built by the given code, read under POWL semantics:

* a `StrictPartialOrder` executes its child nodes so that a node may start only after **all** its predecessors (under the transitive closure of `order`) have **finished**; nodes that are incomparable are genuinely unordered and their internal events may interleave;
* `OperatorPOWL(XOR, [a, b])` executes exactly one child;
* `OperatorPOWL(LOOP, [body, redo])` executes `body`, then optionally `redo` followed by `body`, arbitrarily often — i.e. `body (redo body)*`, a **do-while / at-least-one-iteration** loop;
* `SilentTransition()` is an unlabeled (τ) step: it is a real state change in the net but produces no observable event.

Note on the partial order: the sub-order on `{Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, optional_legal, verification_loop}` contains `assess < legal`, `assess < loop`, `prepare < loop` and nothing else. That is the classic "N" pattern, which is **not series-parallel**. This single structural fact drives the whole audit: no block-structured (process-tree) model can reproduce this order exactly, so any pipeline that passes through a process tree must either fail or distort the model.

Below, "projection" means projecting a trace onto the named subset of activity labels. `CT` = Configure Trial, `IT` = Inspect Trial, `CD` = Correct Defect, `LR` = Legal Review, `AR` = Assess Risk, `PE` = Prepare Evidence, `RC` = Release Change, `AC` = Archive Change, `RG` = Register Change.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I1** | `RG` occurs exactly once and is the first visible event of every complete trace. | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `AR, RG, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC` | Yes — directly checkable on the visible trace language and on footprint start activities. |
| **I2** | `AR` and `PE` are both mandatory, occur exactly once, and are mutually unordered: both interleavings are possible. | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC` **and** `RG, PE, AR, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `RG, AR, CT, IT, RC, AC` (PE missing); a model admitting only one of the two orders | Yes as *both orders present*; No as *true concurrency* (see I11). |
| **I3** | `RG` strictly precedes `AR` and `PE`. | `RG, PE, AR, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `RG, CT, ...` before `AR`/`PE` (also violates I5) | Yes. |
| **I4** | `LR` is optional: complete traces exist both with and without it, and it never occurs more than once. | with: `RG, AR, LR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC`; without: `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `RG, AR, LR, LR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC`; any model in which every complete trace contains `LR` | Partially. The *existence of the choice* is invisible; only the two-language-membership fact (a complete trace with `LR` and one without) is observable. The τ branch itself is unobservable by construction. |
| **I5** | `LR`, when it occurs, occurs after `AR`; it is unordered w.r.t. `PE` and w.r.t. the whole verification loop. | `RG, AR, LR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC` (LR before PE) and `RG, AR, PE, CT, LR, IT, RC, AC` (LR strictly inside a loop iteration) | `RG, LR, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC` (LR before AR) | Yes for the "after AR" part and for the *possibility* of overlap; the *absence of an ordering constraint* is only demonstrable by exhibiting witnesses in every relative position. |
| **I6** | Verification synchronizes on both predecessors: the first `CT` occurs only after both `AR` and `PE` have completed. | `RG, PE, AR, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `RG, AR, CT, PE, IT, RC, AC` | Yes. |
| **I7** | Within every iteration, `CT` precedes `IT`; the projection onto `{CT, IT, CD}` is always `CT IT (CD CT IT)*`. | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `RG, AR, PE, IT, CT, RC, AC`; `RG, AR, PE, CT, CT, IT, IT, RC, AC` | Yes (on the projection). |
| **I8** | At least one full iteration occurs: `#CT ≥ 1` and `#IT ≥ 1` in every complete trace. | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `RG, AR, PE, RC, AC` | Yes. |
| **I9** | Repetition is separated by `CD`: `#CD = #CT − 1 = #IT − 1`, and `CD` never occurs after the final `IT` (never immediately before release in the loop projection). | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, CT, IT, CD, CT, IT, RC, AC` | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, CD, RC, AC` | Yes (on the projection). |
| **I10** | Join before release: `RC` occurs after the last `IT` **and**, if `LR` occurs, after `LR`. | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, LR, RC, AC` | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, RC, LR, AC` | Yes. |
| **I11** | True concurrency: `AR ∥ PE`, `LR ∥ PE`, and the optional-legal branch ∥ the loop are *unordered*, not merely "either order chosen once at the start". | Reachability graph contains diamonds (`m −AR→ ·  −PE→ m'` and `m −PE→ · −AR→ m'` closing on the same marking) | A model whose language is the same but which fixes the order per instance by an exclusive choice, so no diamond closes | **No** at trace level. Interleaving trace semantics cannot distinguish `AND(a,b)` from `XOR(a·b, b·a)`. Requires marking-level evidence (reachability-graph diamonds, footprint `∥` relation, or step semantics). |
| **I12** | Finality: `AC` occurs exactly once, is the last visible event, and the run ends in the final marking exactly (proper completion, no leftover tokens). | `... RC, AC` reaching `fm` exactly | `RG, AR, PE, CT, IT, AC, RC`; a run reaching `fm` plus a residual token elsewhere | Yes for the trace part; the *exact-final-marking* part is only observable at net level, not from labels. |
| **I13** | Label uniqueness: the model has exactly eight visible labels, each label denotes one activity, and no visible activity is duplicated by the representation. | Activity set of any conversion = `{RG, AR, PE, LR, CT, IT, CD, RC, AC}` minus optional `LR` per trace | A converted model containing two distinct transitions labeled `CT` in different blocks, or an unlabeled task that surfaces as a visible event with an empty name | Yes at the level of the model's activity set; duplication is detectable structurally, not from the language. |

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

An important, easily missed fact drives this table: in current PM4Py releases the simplified-interface function `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` **does not contain a direct Petri-net-to-BPMN translation**. When its first argument is not a `ProcessTree` or `BPMN`, it calls `convert_to_process_tree(...)` first and re-raises on failure. So pipeline P1 as written in the task is, in the standard API, silently the same pipeline as P2 and inherits its failure mode. The code in §3 probes this at runtime rather than assuming it, and only then decides whether a BPMN artifact can be produced at all.

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P0 — canonical baseline** (no conversion): POWL → accepting Petri net, kept as the analysis artifact | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net`; `pm4py.check_soundness`; `pm4py.save_vis_petri_net`; `pm4py.save_vis_powl` | Only step whose semantics PM4Py defines for POWL; keeps the non-series-parallel order exactly; gives a marked net for every downstream test | Many τ transitions (order/edge routing), so the drawing is unreadable for stakeholders; place explosion | `im` or `fm` empty/`None`; `check_is_workflow_net` false; `check_soundness` false |
| **P1 — primary**: POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` → `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)`; back-conversion `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)`; `save_vis_bpmn` | Stakeholder-facing notation derived from a validated net; back-conversion enables a real round-trip test | In the standard API this call routes through the process tree and therefore is expected to **raise** on this non-block-structured net; if a direct workflow-net→BPMN variant is available, it instead yields a gateway-heavy diagram with unlabeled routing tasks | `convert_to_bpmn` raises; or the produced BPMN back-converts to a net that is not a sound workflow net; or the round-tripped visible language differs from P0's |
| **P2 — allowed to fail**: POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` → `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` inside `try/except` → `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` → `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` | If it succeeded, it would give a block-structured, highly readable BPMN and a compact canonical tree | The source order contains the N-pattern `AR<LR, AR<loop, PE<loop`, which has no series-parallel decomposition; a tree can only be obtained by *changing* the behavior | `convert_to_process_tree` raises `Exception("the object represents a model that cannot be represented as a process tree!")` — **this is the expected and correct outcome**, and it is diagnostic evidence for I5/I11, not a bug to be worked around |
| **P3 — rediscovery fallback** (explicitly approximating): POWL → net → playout log → Inductive Miner → BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm)`; `pm4py.discover_process_tree_inductive(log)`; `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | Always produces *some* readable BPMN, useful purely as a poster | Rediscovery is not conversion: IM will impose a block structure, typically ordering the optional-legal branch relative to verification or adding behavior; loop bounds and the at-least-one-iteration property may shift | Any invariant test in §5 fails — which is expected; this artifact must never be labeled a conversion of the source |

**Chosen primary pipeline: P0 + P1.** The accepting Petri net from POWL is the analysis artifact and the reference for every comparison; the BPMN is a derivative produced by P1 and is only published if it survives §5. P2 is executed as a *probe*, not as a production path: its exception is recorded as evidence. P3 is executed only if both P1 and P2 fail and a diagram is nonetheless required, and its output is watermarked as an approximation.

# 3. Conversion Code

```python
"""
Conversion and semantic-loss audit for the 'controlled technical change' POWL model.

Layer 1: build the source POWL, convert it to an accepting Petri net, and try to
obtain BPMN artifacts through two pipelines. Every conversion is guarded, every
exception is reported (never swallowed), and every marking is checked before use.

Requires: pm4py >= 2.7 (simplified interface), graphviz for the save_vis_* calls.
"""

import inspect
import os
import traceback
from typing import Any, Dict, List, Optional, Tuple

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import PetriNet, Marking
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    StrictPartialOrder,
    OperatorPOWL,
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

OUT_DIR = "conversion_audit"
os.makedirs(OUT_DIR, exist_ok=True)


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 0. Source model (authoritative)
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
def build_source_powl() -> StrictPartialOrder:
    register = Transition(label="Register Change")
    assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
    prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

    legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
    skip_legal = SilentTransition()
    optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
    )

    configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
    inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
    trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
    trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

    correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
    verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.LOOP,
        children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
    )

    release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
    archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

    powl = StrictPartialOrder(
        nodes=[
            register,
            assess_risk,
            prepare_evidence,
            optional_legal,
            verification_loop,
            release_change,
            archive_change,
        ]
    )
    powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
    powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
    powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
    powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
    powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
    powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
    powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
    powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)
    return powl


source_powl = build_source_powl()

# Audit bookkeeping: nothing is "true" unless it lands in one of these.
ACCEPTING_NETS: Dict[str, Tuple[PetriNet, Marking, Marking]] = {}
BPMN_MODELS: Dict[str, Any] = {}
CONVERSION_ERRORS: Dict[str, str] = {}
DIAGNOSTICS: Dict[str, Dict[str, Any]] = {}


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Guarded helpers
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
def marking_is_usable(marking: Optional[Marking]) -> bool:
    """A marking is usable only if it exists and puts at least one token somewhere."""
    if marking is None:
        return False
    try:
        return sum(int(c) for c in marking.values()) > 0
    except Exception:
        return False


def register_accepting_net(name: str, triple: Any) -> bool:
    """Store (net, im, fm) only after validating shape and markings."""
    if not (isinstance(triple, tuple) and len(triple) == 3):
        CONVERSION_ERRORS[name] = f"conversion did not return a 3-tuple: {type(triple)}"
        return False
    net, im, fm = triple
    if not isinstance(net, PetriNet):
        CONVERSION_ERRORS[name] = f"first element is not a PetriNet: {type(net)}"
        return False
    problems = []
    if not marking_is_usable(im):
        problems.append("initial marking is None/empty")
    if not marking_is_usable(fm):
        problems.append("final marking is None/empty")
    if problems:
        # Quarantine, but keep the object so the loss audit can say *why* it failed.
        CONVERSION_ERRORS[name] = "; ".join(problems)
        print(f"[QUARANTINE] {name}: {CONVERSION_ERRORS[name]}")
        return False
    ACCEPTING_NETS[name] = (net, im, fm)
    print(f"[OK] accepting net '{name}': "
          f"{len(net.places)} places, {len(net.transitions)} transitions, "
          f"{len([t for t in net.transitions if t.label is None])} silent")
    return True


def diagnose_net(name: str) -> Dict[str, Any]:
    """Workflow-net and soundness diagnostics for one accepting net."""
    net, im, fm = ACCEPTING_NETS[name]
    info: Dict[str, Any] = {}
    try:
        info["is_workflow_net"] = bool(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    except Exception as exc:
        info["is_workflow_net"] = None
        info["is_workflow_net_error"] = repr(exc)
    try:
        print(f"\n--- soundness diagnostics: {name} ---")
        result = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
        # Recent PM4Py returns (bool, dict); older versions returned a bare bool.
        if isinstance(result, tuple):
            info["sound"] = bool(result[0])
            info["soundness_details"] = result[1] if len(result) > 1 else {}
        else:
            info["sound"] = bool(result)
            info["soundness_details"] = {}
    except Exception as exc:
        info["sound"] = None
        info["soundness_error"] = repr(exc)
        traceback.print_exc()
    info["visible_labels"] = sorted({t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None})
    info["n_silent"] = len([t for t in net.transitions if t.label is None])
    info["duplicate_labels"] = sorted(
        {lab for lab in [t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None]
         if [t.label for t in net.transitions].count(lab) > 1}
    )
    DIAGNOSTICS[name] = info
    return info


def safe_save(fn, *args, **kwargs) -> None:
    """Visualization must never decide whether the audit runs."""
    try:
        fn(*args, **kwargs)
        print(f"[saved] {args[-1]}")
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"[warn] could not save {args[-1]}: {exc!r}")


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. POWL -> accepting Petri net (the only conversion PM4Py defines for POWL)
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)
if not register_accepting_net("net_from_powl", (net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)):
    raise SystemExit("Source conversion produced an unusable accepting net; abort the audit.")
diagnose_net("net_from_powl")

safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_powl, source_powl, os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "00_source_powl.png"))
safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl,
          os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "01_net_from_powl.png"))


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. Pipeline P1: accepting Petri net -> BPMN
#    Probe first: does convert_to_bpmn route through a process tree?
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
try:
    _src = inspect.getsource(pm4py.convert_to_bpmn)
    ROUTES_VIA_TREE = "convert_to_process_tree" in _src
except Exception:
    ROUTES_VIA_TREE = None
print(f"\nconvert_to_bpmn routes a Petri net through a process tree: {ROUTES_VIA_TREE}")

bpmn_from_net = None
try:
    bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
    BPMN_MODELS["bpmn_from_net"] = bpmn_from_net
    print("[OK] P1: BPMN produced directly from the accepting Petri net")
except Exception as exc:
    CONVERSION_ERRORS["petri->bpmn"] = repr(exc)
    print("[FAIL] P1: convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm) raised — reported, not suppressed:")
    traceback.print_exc()

    # Optional, version-dependent fallback: a direct workflow-net -> BPMN variant.
    # It is NOT part of the documented simplified interface, so it is only used if
    # the installed PM4Py actually ships it. Its output is flagged as such.
    wf_to_bpmn = None
    try:
        from pm4py.objects.conversion.wf_net.variants import to_bpmn as wf_to_bpmn  # type: ignore
    except Exception as imp_exc:
        CONVERSION_ERRORS["wf_net->bpmn(import)"] = repr(imp_exc)
        print("[info] no direct workflow-net -> BPMN variant available in this PM4Py build")
    if wf_to_bpmn is not None:
        try:
            try:
                bpmn_from_net = wf_to_bpmn.apply(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
            except TypeError:
                bpmn_from_net = wf_to_bpmn.apply(net_from_powl)
            BPMN_MODELS["bpmn_from_net_wfvariant"] = bpmn_from_net
            print("[OK] P1-fallback: BPMN produced by the internal workflow-net converter")
        except Exception as exc2:
            CONVERSION_ERRORS["wf_net->bpmn"] = repr(exc2)
            traceback.print_exc()


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. Pipeline P2 probe: accepting Petri net -> process tree (allowed to fail)
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
tree_from_net = None
try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
    print(f"[OK] P2: process tree obtained: {tree_from_net}")
except Exception as exc:
    CONVERSION_ERRORS["petri->tree"] = repr(exc)
    print("[EXPECTED FAILURE] P2: the accepting net has no block-structured (process-tree) "
          "representation. This is evidence, not a defect: the partial order contains the "
          "non-series-parallel pattern Assess<Legal, Assess<Loop, Prepare<Loop.")
    traceback.print_exc()

bpmn_from_tree = None
if tree_from_net is not None:
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_process_tree, tree_from_net,
              os.path.join(OUT_DIR, "02_tree_from_net.png"))
    try:
        bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
        BPMN_MODELS["bpmn_from_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
        print("[OK] P2: BPMN produced from the process tree")
    except Exception as exc:
        CONVERSION_ERRORS["tree->bpmn"] = repr(exc)
        traceback.print_exc()
    # The tree itself must also be re-marked and re-audited, not trusted.
    try:
        register_accepting_net("net_from_tree", pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net))
    except Exception as exc:
        CONVERSION_ERRORS["tree->petri"] = repr(exc)
        traceback.print_exc()


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. Round-trip: every produced BPMN back to an accepting Petri net
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
for bpmn_name, bpmn_obj in list(BPMN_MODELS.items()):
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, bpmn_obj, os.path.join(OUT_DIR, f"03_{bpmn_name}.png"))
    try:
        register_accepting_net(f"net_from_{bpmn_name}", pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_obj))
    except Exception as exc:
        CONVERSION_ERRORS[f"{bpmn_name}->petri"] = repr(exc)
        print(f"[FAIL] round trip {bpmn_name} -> Petri net raised:")
        traceback.print_exc()


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. Diagnostics on every accepting net that exists, plus visualizations
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
for name in list(ACCEPTING_NETS):
    if name not in DIAGNOSTICS:
        diagnose_net(name)
    net, im, fm = ACCEPTING_NETS[name]
    safe_save(pm4py.save_vis_petri_net, net, im, fm,
              os.path.join(OUT_DIR, f"04_{name}.png"))

print("\n================ CONVERSION SUMMARY ================")
for name, info in DIAGNOSTICS.items():
    print(f"{name}: workflow_net={info.get('is_workflow_net')} sound={info.get('sound')} "
          f"silent={info.get('n_silent')} duplicate_labels={info.get('duplicate_labels')}")
print("errors:")
for step, err in CONVERSION_ERRORS.items():
    print(f"  {step}: {err}")
if not BPMN_MODELS:
    print("NO BPMN ARTIFACT WAS PRODUCED. The accepting Petri net remains the only "
          "faithful representation; see section 8 before publishing anything else.")
```

Two behaviors this code deliberately does **not** hide: the `Exception` from `convert_to_process_tree`, and the possibility that `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` raises the very same exception because it delegates to that function. If both raise, the correct audit outcome is "no BPMN derivative exists for this model", not "conversion succeeded with warnings".

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

How to read the table. A cell is `expected preserved` when the transformation's definition implies preservation but no test has yet been run; it is promoted to `validated preserved` only by a passing test from §5 (bounded language equality, witness membership, footprint comparison, or exact-final-marking reachability). `approximated` means the behavior is representable but only up to a semantic re-encoding (e.g. concurrency re-encoded as gateways whose meaning is recovered only after back-conversion). `ambiguous` means the artifact is compatible with the invariant but the artifact alone cannot decide it. `conversion failed` means the step raised. `not tested` means the artifact required for the test does not exist.

The Petri→Tree column is `conversion failed` for every row, for one reason stated once: the partial order is non-series-parallel, so `convert_to_process_tree` raises and no tree-derived artifact exists. Because `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` delegates to that same function, the Petri→BPMN and BPMN-round-trip columns are shown for the case where a BPMN was nevertheless obtained (direct workflow-net variant); if it was not, read those two columns as `conversion failed` and `not tested` throughout.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **I1** `RG` first, once | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Bounded language: every complete trace of both nets starts with `RG`; footprint start activities `{RG}` on both. |
| **I2** `AR ∥ PE` (both orders, both mandatory) | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | validated preserved | Both interleavings present in the bounded language of both nets; BPMN re-encodes the unordered pair as an AND-split/join whose token semantics is only recovered after back-conversion, hence `approximated` at diagram level. |
| **I3** `RG` before `AR`, `PE` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Footprint `→` relation and negative witness `AR, RG, ...` rejected by both nets. |
| **I4** `LR` optional, at most once | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | validated preserved | The τ (skip) branch has no label; some BPMN converters render it as an unnamed *task* rather than a routing node. If the back-converted net contains a transition whose label is `""` instead of `None`, the skip becomes a spurious visible event and the invariant is **lost** — the code checks `t.label is None` explicitly, and the label-set diagnostic flags empty-string labels. |
| **I5** `LR` unordered w.r.t. `PE` and the loop (the missing edge) | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | must be validated by the three overlap witnesses | This is the invariant most likely to be silently destroyed. Any block-structured detour serializes it. Witnesses required: `LR` before `PE`; `LR` between `CT` and `IT`; `LR` after the last `IT` and before `RC`. If any is rejected by the round-tripped net, the cell becomes `lost` and §7 rejection 4 fires. |
| **I6** verification waits for `AR` **and** `PE` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Negative witness `RG, AR, CT, ...` must be rejected; footprint has no `AR/PE → CT` shortcut permitting `CT` before `PE`. |
| **I7** `CT` before `IT` per iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Projection of every bounded complete trace onto `{CT, IT, CD}` matches `CT IT (CD CT IT)*`. |
| **I8** at least one iteration (do-while) | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Negative witness `RG, AR, PE, RC, AC` rejected. A `while`-style re-encoding (loop entry guarded by an XOR that may bypass the body) would accept it — this is the classic binary-loop distortion and is an automatic rejection. |
| **I9** `#CD = #CT − 1`, no trailing `CD` | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | validated preserved | Negative witness `..., CT, IT, CD, RC, AC` rejected; counting check over the bounded language up to three iterations. |
| **I10** join before `RC` (both branches) | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | validated preserved | Negative witness `..., IT, RC, LR, AC` rejected. In BPMN this join is an AND-join whose correctness depends on the converter pairing it with the right split; only the back-converted net proves it. |
| **I11** true concurrency vs. arbitrary interleaving | expected preserved (the net has genuinely parallel places, no choice place ordering the branches) | ambiguous | conversion failed | ambiguous | Trace language cannot separate `AND(a,b)` from `XOR(ab, ba)`. Supporting but non-conclusive evidence: `discover_footprints` reports the pairs as `∥`, and the reachability graph contains closing diamonds. Conclusive evidence would need a branching/step-semantics equivalence check that PM4Py does not provide. |
| **I12** finality, exact final marking | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | validated preserved only if `fm` is non-empty and reached exactly | BPMN→Petri conversions may return a differently-shaped `fm` (e.g. one place per end event) or an empty one; `marking_is_usable` gates this, and the bounded exploration requires multiset equality with `fm`, not mere absence of enabled transitions. |
| **I13** activity-label uniqueness | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | ambiguous | Petri→BPMN typically introduces unlabeled routing tasks and gateways; a tree-based route can duplicate a subtree, producing two transitions labeled `CT`. Duplicates do not necessarily change the language but break footprint/alignment interpretation and any label-keyed traceability. The `duplicate_labels` diagnostic decides this cell. |
| **Gateway/routing-node readability** (not a behavioral invariant) | n/a | ambiguous | conversion failed | n/a | Purely presentational; assessed in §6. It can never be `validated preserved`, because no executable test reads a diagram. |

Consolidated reading: the POWL→Petri step is the only one for which I am willing to write `expected preserved` across the board before testing, because it is the one conversion whose semantics PM4Py defines for POWL. Every BPMN-side cell is at best `validated preserved` *after* the round trip, and the two cells that no amount of PM4Py testing can settle are I11 (true concurrency) and the diagram-readability row.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

```python
"""
Layer 2: compare net_from_powl against every round-tripped accepting net.

Oracles, weakest to strongest:
  (a) workflow-net + soundness diagnostics        - necessary, not sufficient
  (b) footprint comparison                        - relation-level, order-1 only
  (c) reachability-graph size                     - indicative only, NOT equivalence
  (d) bounded visible-trace language equality     - the main oracle
  (e) positive / negative witness membership      - invariant-level oracle
  (f) playout + alignments                        - independent cross-check
"""

import itertools
from typing import Dict, FrozenSet, List, Sequence, Set, Tuple

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking

REFERENCE = "net_from_powl"
MAX_VISIBLE = 14          # longest witness below has exactly 14 visible events
MAX_LOOP_ITERATIONS = 3   # bound on occurrences of the loop-entry activity
LOOP_ENTRY = "Configure Trial"
STATE_BUDGET = 500_000


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# (d) Bounded visible-trace exploration
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
def _mkey(m: Marking) -> Tuple[Tuple[str, int], ...]:
    """Hashable, zero-free multiset signature of a marking."""
    return tuple(sorted((p.name, int(c)) for p, c in m.items() if int(c) > 0))


def _fire(t, m: Marking) -> Marking:
    """Classic Petri-net firing; t is assumed enabled in m."""
    nm = Marking()
    for p, c in m.items():
        if int(c) > 0:
            nm[p] = int(c)
    for a in t.in_arcs:
        nm[a.source] -= a.weight
        if nm[a.source] <= 0:
            del nm[a.source]
    for a in t.out_arcs:
        nm[a.target] += a.weight
    return nm


def bounded_visible_language(net, im, fm,
                             max_visible: int = MAX_VISIBLE,
                             max_loop: int = MAX_LOOP_ITERATIONS,
                             loop_entry: str = LOOP_ENTRY) -> Dict[str, object]:
    """
    Exhaustive DFS over visible traces, subject to the stated bounds.

    - transitions with label None are hidden (fired, but append nothing);
    - a trace counts as COMPLETE only on exact final-marking equality (multiset);
    - silent cycling is prevented by refusing to revisit a marking inside the
      same silent segment (safe: a silent cycle returns to the same marking and
      emits nothing, so it can never enable new visible behavior);
    - bounds: visible length <= max_visible, #loop_entry <= max_loop.
    """
    fm_key = _mkey(fm)
    complete: Set[Tuple[str, ...]] = set()
    deadlocks: List[Tuple[Tuple[str, ...], Tuple[Tuple[str, int], ...]]] = []
    truncated = 0
    fm_continues = False
    visited: Set[Tuple[Tuple[Tuple[str, int], ...], Tuple[str, ...]]] = set()
    stack: List[Tuple[Marking, Tuple[str, ...], FrozenSet]] = [
        (im, tuple(), frozenset({_mkey(im)}))
    ]
    expanded = 0

    while stack:
        m, trace, silent_seen = stack.pop()
        sig = (_mkey(m), trace)
        if sig in visited:
            continue
        visited.add(sig)
        expanded += 1
        if expanded > STATE_BUDGET:
            raise RuntimeError("state budget exceeded; lower the bounds or quarantine the net")

        at_final = (_mkey(m) == fm_key)
        if at_final:
            complete.add(trace)

        enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, m)
        if not enabled:
            if not at_final:
                deadlocks.append((trace, _mkey(m)))
            continue
        if at_final:
            fm_continues = True   # proper completion is questionable; report it

        for t in enabled:
            nm = _fire(t, m)
            if t.label is None:
                k = _mkey(nm)
                if k in silent_seen:
                    continue                      # silent cycle: prune
                stack.append((nm, trace, silent_seen | {k}))
            else:
                if len(trace) + 1 > max_visible:
                    truncated += 1
                    continue
                if t.label == loop_entry and trace.count(loop_entry) >= max_loop:
                    truncated += 1
                    continue
                stack.append((nm, trace + (t.label,), frozenset({_mkey(nm)})))

    return {
        "complete": complete,
        "deadlocks": deadlocks,
        "truncated": truncated,
        "states_expanded": expanded,
        "final_marking_not_dead": fm_continues,
    }


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# (e) Witnesses
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
POSITIVE_WITNESSES: List[Tuple[str, Sequence[str]]] = [
    ("W+1 minimal, legal skipped, one iteration",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
      "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W+2 reversed unordered pair",
     ["Register Change", "Prepare Evidence", "Assess Risk",
      "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W+3 legal review before prepare evidence (no edge legal-prepare)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Legal Review", "Prepare Evidence",
      "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W+4 legal review strictly inside one iteration (true overlap)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Configure Trial",
      "Legal Review", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W+5 legal review after the loop, before release",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Configure Trial",
      "Inspect Trial", "Legal Review", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W+6 three iterations with legal review (longest, 14 events)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Legal Review",
      "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect",
      "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect",
      "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
]

NEGATIVE_WITNESSES: List[Tuple[str, Sequence[str]]] = [
    ("W-1 verification starts before Prepare Evidence (broken sync, I6)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Configure Trial", "Prepare Evidence",
      "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W-2 zero iterations (while- instead of do-while loop, I8)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
      "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W-3 inspect before configure (I7)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence",
      "Inspect Trial", "Configure Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W-4 trailing Correct Defect (I9)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Configure Trial",
      "Inspect Trial", "Correct Defect", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W-5 Legal Review twice (I4)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Legal Review", "Legal Review",
      "Prepare Evidence", "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial",
      "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W-6 Legal Review after Release (broken join, I10)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Configure Trial",
      "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Legal Review", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W-7 Archive before Release (I12)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Prepare Evidence", "Configure Trial",
      "Inspect Trial", "Archive Change", "Release Change"]),
    ("W-8 Prepare Evidence missing (I2)",
     ["Register Change", "Assess Risk", "Configure Trial", "Inspect Trial",
      "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
    ("W-9 Register Change not first (I1)",
     ["Assess Risk", "Register Change", "Prepare Evidence", "Configure Trial",
      "Inspect Trial", "Release Change", "Archive Change"]),
]


def check_witnesses(language: Set[Tuple[str, ...]]) -> Dict[str, bool]:
    """All positives must be members; all negatives must be non-members."""
    verdict = {}
    for name, tr in POSITIVE_WITNESSES:
        verdict[name] = tuple(tr) in language
    for name, tr in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES:
        verdict[name] = tuple(tr) not in language
    return verdict


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# (b) Footprints, (c) reachability graph
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
def footprint_diff(name_a, name_b) -> Dict[str, object]:
    net_a, im_a, fm_a = ACCEPTING_NETS[name_a]
    net_b, im_b, fm_b = ACCEPTING_NETS[name_b]
    try:
        fa = pm4py.discover_footprints(net_a, im_a, fm_a)
        fb = pm4py.discover_footprints(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    except Exception as exc:
        return {"error": repr(exc)}
    out = {}
    for key in ("activities", "start_activities", "end_activities",
                "sequence", "parallel"):
        va, vb = set(fa.get(key, set()) or set()), set(fb.get(key, set()) or set())
        out[key] = {"only_in_reference": sorted(map(str, va - vb)),
                    "only_in_candidate": sorted(map(str, vb - va)),
                    "equal": va == vb}
    return out


def reachability_shape(name) -> Dict[str, object]:
    """Size only. Equal sizes are NOT evidence of equivalence; unequal sizes are a flag."""
    net, im, fm = ACCEPTING_NETS[name]
    try:
        ts = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
        return {"states": len(ts.states), "transitions": len(ts.transitions)}
    except Exception as exc:
        return {"error": repr(exc)}


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# (f) Playout and alignment cross-check
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
def playout_traces(name, max_traces: int = 200) -> Set[Tuple[str, ...]]:
    net, im, fm = ACCEPTING_NETS[name]
    try:
        log = pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm)
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"[warn] playout unsupported for {name}: {exc!r}")
        return set()
    out = set()
    for trace in log:
        ev = tuple(e["concept:name"] for e in trace
                   if e.get("concept:name") not in (None, ""))
        out.add(ev)
        if len(out) >= max_traces:
            break
    return out


def alignment_membership(name, traces: Sequence[Sequence[str]]) -> Dict[str, object]:
    """Independent membership oracle: perfect fitness == the trace is in the language."""
    try:
        import pandas as pd
    except Exception as exc:
        return {"error": f"pandas unavailable: {exc!r}"}
    net, im, fm = ACCEPTING_NETS[name]
    rows = []
    for i, tr in enumerate(traces):
        for j, act in enumerate(tr):
            rows.append({"case:concept:name": f"c{i}",
                         "concept:name": act,
                         "time:timestamp": pd.Timestamp("2024-01-01") + pd.Timedelta(seconds=j)})
    if not rows:
        return {}
    df = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    try:
        diag = pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_alignments(
            df, net, im, fm,
            activity_key="concept:name",
            case_id_key="case:concept:name",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
        )
    except Exception as exc:
        return {"error": repr(exc)}
    return {f"trace_{i}": (d is not None and abs(float(d.get("fitness", 0.0)) - 1.0) < 1e-9)
            for i, d in enumerate(diag)}


# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Run the comparison
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------
reference = bounded_visible_language(*ACCEPTING_NETS[REFERENCE])
print(f"\nreference bounded language: {len(reference['complete'])} complete visible traces, "
      f"{len(reference['deadlocks'])} deadlocks, {reference['truncated']} truncations, "
      f"{reference['states_expanded']} states expanded, "
      f"final-marking-not-dead={reference['final_marking_not_dead']}")

ref_witnesses = check_witnesses(reference["complete"])
for k, v in ref_witnesses.items():
    print(f"  [{'PASS' if v else 'FAIL'}] {k}")
assert all(ref_witnesses.values()), (
    "The reference net already violates a source invariant: the POWL->Petri step, "
    "or my reading of the source semantics, is wrong. Stop; do not audit downstream."
)

VERDICTS: Dict[str, Dict[str, object]] = {}
for name in ACCEPTING_NETS:
    if name == REFERENCE:
        continue
    print(f"\n=================== round trip: {name} ===================")
    v: Dict[str, object] = {}
    v["diagnostics"] = DIAGNOSTICS.get(name) or diagnose_net(name)
    try:
        cand = bounded_visible_language(*ACCEPTING_NETS[name])
    except RuntimeError as exc:
        v["language"] = {"error": repr(exc)}
        VERDICTS[name] = v
        print(f"[QUARANTINE] {name}: {exc}")
        continue
    only_ref = reference["complete"] - cand["complete"]
    only_cand = cand["complete"] - reference["complete"]
    v["language_equal"] = (not only_ref and not only_cand)
    v["missing_from_candidate"] = sorted(only_ref)[:20]
    v["extra_in_candidate"] = sorted(only_cand)[:20]
    v["deadlocks"] = cand["deadlocks"][:10]
    v["final_marking_not_dead"] = cand["final_marking_not_dead"]
    v["witnesses"] = check_witnesses(cand["complete"])
    v["footprints"] = footprint_diff(REFERENCE, name)
    v["reachability_reference"] = reachability_shape(REFERENCE)
    v["reachability_candidate"] = reachability_shape(name)
    playout = playout_traces(name)
    v["playout_outside_reference"] = sorted(playout - reference["complete"])[:20]
    v["alignments_positive"] = alignment_membership(
        name, [tr for _, tr in POSITIVE_WITNESSES])
    v["alignments_negative_should_all_be_false"] = alignment_membership(
        name, [tr for _, tr in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES])
    VERDICTS[name] = v

    print(f"  language equal under bound : {v['language_equal']}")
    print(f"  missing traces             : {len(only_ref)}")
    print(f"  extra traces               : {len(only_cand)}")
    print(f"  witness failures           : "
          f"{[k for k, ok in v['witnesses'].items() if not ok]}")
    print(f"  footprint parallel equal   : "
          f"{v['footprints'].get('parallel', {}).get('equal')}")
```

**Is the comparison exhaustive?** It is exhaustive **relative to the chosen bound and only relative to it**: for every net examined, the DFS enumerates *all* visible traces of length at most 14 that contain at most three occurrences of `Configure Trial` and that end in a marking multiset-equal to that net's own final marking. Within that fragment nothing is sampled or heuristic, and the silent-cycle pruning does not remove any reachable visible behavior (a silent cycle returns to an already-seen marking within the same silent segment and emits nothing, so any trace using it has a shorter equivalent). Outside the bound the comparison says nothing: two nets that agree on all traces up to 14 visible events and three iterations may still diverge at four iterations or on longer interleavings, and any net that is unbounded or blows the state budget is quarantined rather than judged. Language equality under the bound is also a *trace-level* result: it does not establish branching equivalence, and by construction it cannot separate true concurrency from arbitrary interleaving (see §9).

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A BPMN rendering can be behaviorally acceptable and still be read wrongly, because a diagram encodes token semantics that most readers translate into a mental "flowchart" semantics.

**Parallel gateways.** The AND-split after `Register Change` and the AND-join before `Release Change` correctly express "both branches, no order". Readers routinely misread them in two ways: as "both branches run simultaneously in real time" (the model only says *unordered*, not *simultaneous*), and, worse, as "the branches are independent" — whereas the join means `Release Change` is *blocked* until the slowest branch finishes. The join is the visual home of invariant I10 and it looks like a decoration.

**The missing edge between optional legal review and verification.** This is the single most dangerous rendering issue. In the source, `optional_legal` and `verification_loop` are unordered, and `Legal Review` may occur *inside* a verification iteration. Every BPMN layout will place the legal branch either above or below the verification branch, drawn left-to-right, and readers will assume the vertical stacking implies "legal happens roughly here". If the converter had to serialize the branches to obtain block structure, the diagram would look almost identical while having lost I5 entirely — the picture cannot distinguish the faithful model from the distorted one.

**Exclusive gateways and silent routing.** The XOR with a `SilentTransition` renders either as an empty bypass arc between two XOR gateways or, with some converters, as an *unnamed task box*. The bypass arc invites the reading "exception route / escalation path" rather than "this step is simply optional". The unnamed task box is worse: it looks like an activity somebody forgot to name, and a reader may fill in a meaning ("informal check?"). If such a node carries the empty label `""` rather than `None`, it also becomes a phantom event in playout.

**Loop visualization.** `*(trial_body, Correct Defect)` is a do-while: the body runs at least once, and `Correct Defect` only ever runs *between* iterations. Rendered as an XOR-join, body, XOR-split, and a back-edge through `Correct Defect`, it is very commonly read as (a) "the loop may be skipped" (while-semantics, contradicting I8) or (b) "`Correct Defect` is a post-processing step after inspection" (contradicting I9, since a trailing `Correct Defect` is not in the language). The back-edge also visually crosses the diagram, which readers interpret as an error path.

**Apparent ordering from layout.** Graph layout imposes a total left-to-right order on nodes that the model leaves unordered. `Assess Risk` drawn left of `Prepare Evidence` will be read as "assess first". Nothing in the diagram distinguishes "drawn first" from "must be first"; only the gateway type does, and gateway glyphs are small.

**Gateway proliferation from the workflow-net route.** A BPMN obtained directly from the τ-rich Petri net (rather than from a tree) contains one routing construct per structural place and per silent transition. The result is technically faithful and practically unreadable, which produces its own failure mode: stakeholders stop reading it and fall back on a hand-drawn version that nobody validated.

**Mitigations to attach to any published diagram.**

1. A visible header on the diagram: *"Derivative artifact, generated from `source_powl` (commit hash / model version). Authoritative semantics: the POWL model and its accepting Petri net. Layout order does not imply execution order."*
2. A text annotation on every AND-split: *"unordered — either order, and the branches may interleave"*; on the AND-join before `Release Change`: *"waits for both the legal branch and the verification loop"*.
3. A text annotation on the skip branch: *"skip — no activity is performed; Legal Review is optional and occurs at most once"*, plus a rule that τ nodes must be rendered as routing paths, never as unnamed task boxes.
4. A text annotation on the loop: *"at least one iteration; Correct Defect occurs only between iterations and never after the last inspection"*.
5. An explicit annotation on the legal branch: *"not ordered relative to verification; Legal Review may occur before, during, or after any iteration"* — the invariant the picture cannot show.
6. The §1 invariant table published *next to* the diagram, with the §5 validation report (witness pass/fail list, soundness result, bound used) as an attachment. A diagram without its validation report is not a deliverable.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

A conversion result must be rejected or quarantined — never published, never used downstream — under any of the following.

1. **Conversion exception.** Any conversion in the pipeline raises (in particular `convert_to_process_tree`, and therefore possibly `convert_to_bpmn` on a Petri net). Quarantine the pipeline and record the exception as a finding. Do not "repair" it by simplifying the source model to make it block-structured.
2. **Unusable markings.** `im` or `fm` is `None` or empty after any conversion, or `fm` is not reachable from `im`. Without a usable final marking the exact-final-marking test cannot run, so no behavioral claim can be made at all.
3. **Not a workflow net / not sound.** `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net` returns `False`, or `pm4py.check_soundness` returns `False` (deadlock, improper completion, dead transitions, unbounded net). A converted model with dead transitions is also a loss signal: an activity the source could execute has become unreachable.
4. **Loss of a required interleaving.** Any positive witness is rejected by the round-tripped net. Concretely: `Legal Review` can no longer occur before `Prepare Evidence`, or can no longer occur between `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial`. This is the expected symptom of a block-structured detour and destroys invariant I5.
5. **Admission of release before verification, or of zero iterations.** Any negative witness is accepted — most importantly `RG, AR, PE, RC, AC` (loop became a while-loop, I8 lost), `RG, AR, CT, PE, ...` (synchronization before verification lost, I6), or `..., IT, RC, LR, AC` (join before release lost, I10).
6. **Collapse or inflation of optional behavior.** No complete trace without `Legal Review` exists (optionality lost), or `Legal Review` becomes repeatable or mandatory, or the silent skip surfaces as a visible event (a transition labeled `""` instead of `None`, or an unnamed task appearing in playout).
7. **Failed exact-final-marking test.** Traces "complete" only in the weak sense of having no enabled transitions, or the final marking is reached while tokens remain elsewhere, or transitions remain enabled in `fm` (`final_marking_not_dead` is true). Proper completion is part of I12 and is not optional.
8. **Bounded language mismatch.** The set of complete visible traces under the stated bound differs in either direction from the reference net's. Extra traces are as disqualifying as missing ones: an over-general model is not a conversion.
9. **Footprint degradation.** Pairs reported as `∥` for the reference are reported as `→`/`←` for the candidate (concurrency serialized), or the reverse (spurious concurrency introduced), or the activity / start / end activity sets differ.
10. **Label damage.** The visible label set changes, a label is duplicated across distinct transitions, or new visible labels appear. Even when the language is unaffected, this breaks alignment interpretation, conformance keying, and traceability to the source POWL nodes.
11. **Validation infeasible.** The state budget is exceeded, the reachability graph cannot be built, or the net is unbounded. "Could not be checked" is quarantine, not a pass.
12. **Rediscovery masquerading as conversion.** An artifact produced by playout plus a discovery algorithm (pipeline P3) is presented, stored, or referenced as if it were a conversion of the source. It must be rejected for any purpose other than an explicitly watermarked illustration.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

**Authoritative representation: the POWL model, as source code, in version control.** The POWL construction is the only artifact that states the intent directly — in particular, the *absence* of an order between the optional-legal branch and the verification loop is expressed as the absence of an edge, not encoded in a structure that must be reverse-engineered. It is diffable, reviewable, and it is the only representation from which every other artifact can be regenerated deterministically.

**Canonical analysis form: the accepting Petri net `(net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)`, regenerated from the POWL, never hand-edited.** It is the input to soundness checking, footprints, reachability, alignments, and playout, and it is the reference against which every round trip is compared. It may be *cached* (with the source hash) but it is not a second source of truth: if the cache and the POWL disagree, the POWL wins and the cache is rebuilt.

**Stakeholder visualization: BPMN, strictly derivative.** Published only if it was produced by pipeline P1 and passed §5, and always with the annotation package from §6. If no BPMN can be produced (the expected outcome here, given the non-series-parallel order), the stakeholder artifact is the annotated Petri-net rendering plus the invariant table — an honest, less pretty diagram beats a pretty, wrong one.

**Derivative artifacts and the evidence each must carry.**

| Artifact | Status | Required accompanying evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Accepting Petri net | canonical analysis form | source hash; `check_is_workflow_net`; `check_soundness` output; all positive/negative witnesses passing |
| BPMN diagram | derivative, presentational | the full §5 report for its back-converted net: bounded language equality with the bound stated, all witness verdicts, footprint diff, soundness, plus the §6 annotations |
| Back-converted net from BPMN | validation-only, not stored as a model | the comparison report itself |
| Process tree | not produced (see below) | n/a |
| Rediscovered model (P3) | illustration only | prominent watermark, the list of invariants it violates, and a ban on downstream use |
| Reachability graph / footprints | diagnostic outputs | the bound and the version of PM4Py used |

**Should a process-tree intermediate be allowed?** Not as a representation, yes as a *probe*. Running `convert_to_process_tree` is valuable precisely because its failure is informative: it certifies that the source behavior is not block-structured, which is itself a documented property of this change process and the reason the BPMN pipeline is fragile. What is forbidden is (a) storing a tree as the model of record, and (b) obtaining a tree by weakening the source — for example by adding an edge from `optional_legal` to `verification_loop`, or by moving `Legal Review` inside the loop, to make the order series-parallel. Such an edit is a modeling decision about the real process; it must be made deliberately in the POWL source, reviewed, and recorded, never introduced as a side effect of wanting a diagram. Because `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` may itself route through the tree converter, "no tree as a representation" does not mean "no tree in the call stack"; it means no tree-shaped artifact is ever the thing that gets stored or edited.

**Avoiding round-trip drift.** One direction only: POWL → net → (BPMN). Never import an edited BPMN back into the canonical model; there is no faithful BPMN→POWL path, and re-importing would silently adopt whatever the converter chose to serialize. All edits are made to the POWL source code and reviewed as code. Regeneration of every derivative runs in CI on every change to the source, together with the §5 validation suite, and the pipeline fails the build on any §7 condition. Each generated file embeds the source hash, the PM4Py version, and the exploration bound; artifacts whose embedded hash does not match the current source are treated as stale and deleted rather than reused. When a stakeholder marks up the BPMN, the markup is a change request against the POWL, expressed in terms of the invariant table — the diagram is never the thing that gets edited.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every conversion succeeds and every test in §5 passes, the following claims remain unestablished.

1. **Full language equivalence.** The exploration is exhaustive only up to 14 visible events and three loop iterations. Two nets may agree completely inside that window and diverge at the fourth iteration, on longer interleavings, or on traces involving silent structures that only unfold at greater depth. No test here rules that out; only a decision procedure for (bounded) net language equivalence would, and PM4Py does not provide one.

2. **True concurrency versus arbitrary interleaving (I11).** Trace-language equality cannot distinguish `AND(a, b)` from an exclusive choice between `a·b` and `b·a`. Footprint `∥` relations and reachability-graph diamonds are supporting evidence, not proof — the footprint abstraction is order-1 and the reachability-graph comparison implemented here is a size comparison, not a bisimulation check. A converted model that replaced genuine concurrency with a choice among interleavings could pass everything above.

3. **Branching-time and moment-of-choice properties.** Nothing tested establishes *when* the optional-legal decision is taken relative to other steps, whether a converted model defers or advances that choice, or whether the converted model is branching-bisimilar to the source. Two trace-equivalent models can behave very differently under composition with a partner process or when deadlock-freedom under an adversarial environment matters.

4. **Validity of the source model itself.** The whole audit assumes the POWL is a correct formalization of the real controlled-change process. No test can tell whether `Legal Review` may genuinely overlap verification in reality, whether verification really must synchronize on evidence preparation, or whether "at least one iteration" matches practice. Verification against the source is not validation against the world.

5. **Correct human reading of the diagram.** §6 lists ways a BPMN can be misread; no executable check can confirm that a reader interprets a gateway, a back-edge, or a layout order correctly. The annotations reduce but cannot measure this risk.

6. **Stability across tool versions.** The results depend on the PM4Py version's POWL→Petri translation, the internal routing of `convert_to_bpmn`, and the workflow-net→tree reduction rules. A future release may change τ placement, marking construction, or the success/failure boundary of the tree conversion; passing today does not certify the pipeline for tomorrow, which is why every artifact records the tool version.

7. **Everything outside the control-flow perspective.** Nothing here checks data conditions, roles and authorizations, timing or deadlines, resource constraints, or whether the silent transitions genuinely carry no business meaning (a "skip legal review" step may in reality require a recorded justification, which the model treats as invisible). Soundness in the control-flow sense also does not imply the process is fair, live under partial-order execution, or free of livelock in an implementation that schedules the loop.
