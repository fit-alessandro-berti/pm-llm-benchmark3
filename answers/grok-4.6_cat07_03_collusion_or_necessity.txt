# 2. Benign alternative explanations

**Create PO + Approve PO violations and 8–10 min Carla/Gita and Dan/Hugo approvals**

1. *Emergency handling / informal specialization.* Carla is the medical-supply specialist; Gita is the delegated urgent approver. Dan covers facilities repairs; Hugo handles emergency overrides, including weekends. Support: `emergency_flag=True` in 88% / 79% of those pairs, low vendor concentration, purchase_category alignment, weekend timestamps for Dan–Hugo, presence of Emergency Override. Refute: emergency_flag set after the fact, category mismatch, no clinical/facilities ticket, approval comments that are empty or identical across cases.
2. *Shared account or `legacy_erp` resource mapping.* Assistants posting as Approver_Gita, or Bot_PO and a human collapsed to one `org:resource`, will look like same-person create/approve. Support: `entered_by` ≠ `org:resource`, `approved_by` names a different human, `source_system="legacy_erp"`. Refute: `entered_by`, `approved_by`, and access logs all show the same natural person performing both activities.

**Gita→Carla→Gita and Hugo→Dan→Hugo subcontracting loops**

1. *Clarification / escalation loops on incomplete emergency packs.* Approver returns the case; buyer amends; approver completes. Support: activity text or comments showing queries, amount/vendor changes between the two approval events, goods later received and matched. Refute: second approval with zero attribute change and no comment, repeating on the same vendors.
2. *Staffing design (single on-call pair).* Thin weekend/on-call rotas naturally recycle the same two people. Support: roster, on-call calendar, `region`/shift alignment. Refute: loops occur in ordinary weekday non-emergency cases with other approvers available.

**Lena→Kim bank-change + payment path (fast, manual, V-77x, 11-cycle loop)**

1. *Mass vendor-master maintenance after V-77x banking-platform change*, with missing ticket-to-case linkage. Support: change tickets, bank-letter/IBAN evidence, same new account across many V-77x entities, AP confirmation that payments went to the successor platform. Refute: heterogeneous new accounts, payments to accounts not on the vendor’s letter, no ticket, or changes only on invoices that Kim then released.
2. *Work-queue design: Kim owns higher-risk suppliers; Lena owns bank changes; `manual_upload` is the prescribed channel for platform migrations.* Support: procedure docs, SoD matrix allowing this pair if a third control exists, similar timing on non-V-77x manuals. Refute: no compensating control, only this pair is fast, payment released before bank verification completed.

**Same-person Emergency Override + Retrospective Justification (17; 12 batched)**

1. *Quarter-close backfill from a spreadsheet* (`source_system="quarter_close_upload"`), one clerk posting many justifications under the manager’s name. Support: identical timestamps to the minute, upload job logs, `entered_by=Temp_Nina` or a finance admin, original paper/email justifications dated within 3 days by someone else. Refute: interactive UI sessions by Hugo/Gita at those timestamps, no source documents, justifications created only after audit inquiry.
2. *Policy misunderstanding: override owner thought they must also “close” the justification.* Support: training materials, other managers doing the same, subsequent process change. Refute: repeated pattern after coaching, justifications that merely copy the override text.

**Vendor Check + bank change by Lena/Marc**

1. *Single vendor-master role covering both activities by design.* Support: job descriptions, SoD matrix, dual control at a later step (e.g., bank file dual-authorisation). Refute: written rule requiring two vendor-master staff.
2. *Small team covering leave.* Support: calendar, only 31 cases, mixed vendors. Refute: systematic pairing with subsequent same-person payment approval.

**Aline/Elena and Boris/Felix high co-working; Bot_Match→Kim**

1. *Formal team lanes (Team A / Team B) and automated exception handover to the higher-risk AP officer.* Support: org chart, stable 2.4–2.6 h medians, low emergency/manual rates. These are not suspicious by frequency alone.
2. *Bot exception design.* Support: match-fail reason codes, 6 min is near the bot’s handoff SLA. Refute: Kim overriding match failures without inspecting the fail code.

# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Would increase concern**

- Access or application logs showing the same natural person (not a shared queue id) performed both bank-detail change and payment approval, minutes apart, on V-77x cases, with no maintenance ticket.
- New bank accounts that do **not** match the V-77x platform-migration letters, or that match accounts of unrelated payees / employees.
- Payments released before any independent confirmation of the new account; or goods receipt / invoice match absent or created after payment.
- `entered_by` = `approved_by` on Create PO + Approve PO after correcting the Gita shared-account and `legacy_erp` mapping (violations remain).
- Retrospective justifications with no pre-existing document, authored by the override resource in an interactive session, outside `quarter_close_upload`.
- Identical approval comments, or Gita/Hugo approvals with zero dwell time and no field change on non-emergency, high-amount, non-medical / non-repair categories.
- Lena–Kim loop that *introduces* a new account after Kim has already approved payment.
- Kim overrides of Bot_Match “matched” invoices (not only exceptions) on the same V-77x cases.

**Would reduce concern**

- A complete V-77x migration file: same successor bank details on all 44 cases, tickets dated before the P2P events, payments confirmed to the new platform.
- After remapping `org:resource` → `entered_by` / `approved_by`, four-eyes PO violations fall to near zero except flagged emergencies with a timely third-person justification.
- On-call rotas and category assignment that explain Carla–Gita and Dan–Hugo; clinical/facilities tickets matching `purchase_category` and `emergency_flag`.
- Upload job logs proving 12 justifications were a single spreadsheet backfill; original signed justifications by a different human within 3 working days.
- SoD matrix that explicitly allows one vendor-master officer to check and change bank data, with dual control in the payment file instead.
- Stable, documented Team A / Team B routing for Aline–Elena and Boris–Felix; no excess high-amount or new-vendor rate versus other lanes.
- Independent confirmation that Bot_PO/`legacy_erp` collisions are a known mapping defect, with corrected identities showing distinct creator and approver.

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Internal Audit examined the procurement-to-payment event log for control exceptions: four-eyes breaches, short approval intervals, repeated resource loops, vendor concentration, and source-system anomalies. The review does not establish misconduct. Frequent co-working on routine buyer–approver lanes is consistent with team assignment and informal specialization and is not, by itself, a red flag.

Most Create-PO / Approve-PO exceptions and sub-15-minute approvals sit in two known rush channels: emergency medical supplies (Buyer_Carla with Approver_Gita) and weekend facilities repairs (Buyer_Dan with Manager_Hugo). Those subgroups show high emergency_flag rates, low vendor concentration, and documented exception activities. Residual gaps remain—late retrospective justifications and same-person override/justification pairs—but twelve of seventeen same-person justifications share identical minute-level timestamps after a quarter-close spreadsheet upload, which is more consistent with recording practice than with contemporaneous dual-role execution.

The cluster that most warrants targeted follow-up is VendorMaster_Lena with AP_Kim on vendor family V-77x: nine bank-change / payment-approval four-eyes exceptions, sixteen unusually fast approvals entirely on source_system=manual_upload, a high amount-band share, and a tight subcontracting loop. Even here, a recent vendor banking-platform change and missing ticket-to-case linkage are plausible data-quality explanations and must be tested before any conduct inference.

Shared-account mapping for Approver_Gita and legacy_erp bot/human resource collisions further limit org:resource-based four-eyes tests. We recommend a focused file review of the V-77x and non-batched justification cases, reconstruction of entered_by / approved_by and access logs, and remediation of resource-mapping and exception-documentation controls. Routine Aline/Elena and Boris/Felix pairs should remain on monitoring only.
