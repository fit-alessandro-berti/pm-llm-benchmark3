The transferable structural analogy is a fork followed by a join: Assessment must wait for both verification results, but the checks need not precede one another when legally and informationally independent. At B, the document branch is conditional receipt followed by verification. Starting receipt alongside identity can be useful even when the two verification services never overlap.

The DFG supplies evidence of routes, not a unique partial order, resource policy, or concurrency model. The 2,108 receipt-to-document relations and 1,360 assessment-to-legal relations match the reported 62% and 40% marginals, but do not establish subgroup intersections. Supplement relations support an explicit loop. The two listed registration out-edges total 3,910, exceeding 3,400 cases: with exactly one Register event per case and an ordinary total-order DFG, there can be only one immediate successor per registration. Clarify repeated events, tie handling, extraction semantics, or counting errors. Selected edges also omit routes and may count repeat visits; do not normalize them into branch probabilities or infer missing cases directly.

This is a qualitative selection diagram, not an identified transport formula. Selection nodes mark mechanisms or input distributions that may differ across sites; arrows express possible influence, not its sign. Baseline queues precede the intervention's realized overlap, while repeat workload and downstream bursts describe later consequences, avoiding a literal cycle for the operational rework loop.

Shared mechanisms include the verification join, the possibility of replacing serial elapsed time with the maximum of two independent branch times, and the effect of capacity and repeat work on waiting. Those mechanisms transfer only when branches are ready and can receive real resources. Queueing can amplify or erase a service-time saving.

Site-specific mechanisms include statutory receipt and legal-review gates, delivery variability, the IT lock, specialized staffing, API performance, and recording practices. Originals requirements, digital readiness, complexity, legal eligibility, role specialization, relative branch duration, and utilization are effect modifiers. Capacity can modify gains even when its nominal headcount remains unchanged: parallel releases alter arrival timing at the assessment join.

At A, calendar period connects treatment to API, staffing, marketing, huddles, and recording changes. Consequently, observed outcome differences do not identify parallelization's total effect. Measured overlap is also affected by recording, so it is not a sufficient mediator measurement.

Similar control-flow diagrams do not guarantee similar available inputs, qualified resources, transition guards, queue dynamics, or outcome measurement. Nor does A establish effects in B's legally constrained strata. Transport therefore requires local treatment fidelity, covariate support, and conditional effect estimates, followed by standardization to B's intended rollout population.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

Let changes be intervention minus baseline; negative hours mean faster processing. The principal table entries concern the whole-site cycle-time distribution, whose baseline median is 44 h and p90 is 110 h. These are conditional planning ranges, not identified causal estimates, additive decompositions, or confidence intervals. Scores express scenario plausibility under its assumptions.

For a genuinely serial, jointly ready case with fixed branch elapsed times, the maximum overlap saving is

`T_identity + T_document - max(T_identity, T_document) = min(T_identity, T_document)`.

Using service medians as illustrative fixed durations, A's original checks give `3.5 + 7.2 - 7.2 = 3.5 h`. Holding A's later service durations fixed gives 3.1 h. Neither calculation explains the observed nine-hour median change. At B, a ready, original-free case gives `3.4 + 8.1 - 8.1 = 3.4 h`. Additional benefits require less waiting or earlier release; actual elapsed branch durations include queues and can differ substantially from service medians.

For an originals case using the illustrative 18-hour receipt delay, the document path takes `18 + 8.1 = 26.1 h`. Delaying receipt until after identity would produce `3.4 + 26.1 = 29.5 h`; concurrent initiation could reduce that to 26.1 h. If receipt already starts at registration and identity finishes during that wait, this particular saving is zero. Parallelization cannot remove the delivery delay or allow premature document verification.

The optimistic median range combines approximately 0–3.4 h of illustrated service overlap with several hours of locally removable waiting. Its tail range assumes receipt and legal queues do not become limiting; A's −18 h supplies an optimistic reference, not a bound. There is no basis for assigning A's entire six-hour waiting improvement to concurrency at B.

The digital-only range assumes modest service overlap plus some release improvement within the treated stratum. The whole-site quantile ranges are deliberately smaller and uncertain: medians and p90s must be computed from mixed conditional distributions, not obtained by multiplying a subgroup median saving by its share. With marginal originals eligibility alone, the originals-not-required/no-legal intersection can be anywhere from 0% to 38%; digital readiness and lower complexity narrow it further. The assumed 20–30% planning share must be measured.

The adverse scenarios allow downstream or coordination delays to outweigh several hours saved upstream. For a concrete distributional illustration, suppose the faster 80% of cases each save four hours while the slowest 20% each gain twelve hours of queueing. The mean change is `0.8 × (−4) + 0.2 × 12 = −0.8 h`; if case ranks remain stable, the median improves by four hours while p90 worsens by twelve. Average improvement therefore does not justify accepting a deteriorating tail.

Calibrate these ranges with arrival rates, active effort distributions, joint stratum frequencies, actual receipt delays, resource calendars, and repeat-visit rates. FTE counts and service medians alone do not identify utilization; elapsed task intervals must not be assumed to be hands-on labor hours. A resource-aware simulation can explore workload effects, but should reproduce B's baseline queues, quantiles, and rework before informing rollout.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

Use readiness-based parallel verification with a mandatory join and guarded legal loop.

Eligibility begins with cases whose documents are digitally available and complete, originals are not legally required, no legal review is mandatory, complexity is within the pilot's agreed lower range, and neither check needs the other's result. Record these conditions before assignment. This is an unknown subset of the 38% without originals requirements. Cases outside this first stratum retain their compliant route until their own pilot stage passes.

After Register, create Identity Check and a document-path record. In the first stratum, both verification tasks may be released concurrently when their qualified resource pools and WIP budgets permit. Task creation does not authorize execution. For a later originals-required stratum, Identity Check and Originals Receipt may proceed concurrently; Document Check remains disabled until verified receipt of the required originals. Original-free but incomplete cases remain blocked for completeness. Assessment starts only when both current, valid verification results are complete. Do not insert an identity-to-document precedence unless a real information dependency requires it.

Assign identity work to the identity pool, document work to the document pool, assessment to assessors, and legal work to legal reviewers. A case coordinator monitors readiness and reconciles conflicting evidence; the coordinator does not bypass professional permissions. Enable safe task-level ownership and immutable audit events. If the legacy lock cannot support this, first complete the enabling IT work and validate it outside live decision processing.

Use unassigned ready queues ordered by age within statutory priority classes. Initially permit one executing and at most one additional ready assigned task per verification reviewer; use conservative one-task assignment caps for assessors and legal reviewers. Tune these provisional limits against measured workload, rather than copying A's six-task rule. Keep receipt-blocked placeholders out of active reviewer WIP, while counting them in total open-case inventory. Pace new verification releases against downstream completions and ready-queue age. Apply the same queue discipline and capacity budget to pilot treatment and control. No reviewer holds another role's resource while waiting for a handoff.

After Assessment, route every public-sector/high-value case to Legal Review. A material supplement invalidates the affected document conclusion and assessment: record Request Supplement, obtain any newly required originals, repeat Document Check and Assessment, then return to Legal Review. Previously verified identity may be retained only while valid. Identity-invalidating changes exit to a documented exception workflow that repeats identity and restores the join. A completed legal review permits a decision only when its requirements are resolved. Other cases skip legal review under the eligibility guard. Choose exactly one final Approve or Reject, followed by Notify.

Log case and task identifiers, assignment/eligibility/guard decisions, evidence versions, resource and role, readiness, release, actual start, pauses and reasons, actual finish, event-entry time, receipt validation, join-enabled time, legal outcomes, supplement iteration, final decision, and notification. Record overlaps separately for active work and open task intervals. Preserve original occurrence timestamps when events are entered later; flag reconstructed times and audit a sample against system telemetry.

A compact POWL-style specification is:

```text
D = SEQ(XOR(tau, Originals Receipt), Document Check)
  guard: choose receipt exactly when the document version needs required originals;
         Document Check also requires package completeness.

Redo = SEQ(Request Supplement, D_new, Assessment_new)
L = XOR(tau, LOOP(Legal Review, Redo))
  guard: tau only when legal review is not mandatory;
         LOOP(body, redo) means body (redo body)*;
         exit only when review permits a final decision.

Decision = XOR(Approve, Reject)
Candidate = PO{Register, Identity Check, D, Assessment, L, Decision, Notify}
Required precedence generators:
  Register < Identity Check
  Register < D
  Identity Check < Assessment
  D < Assessment
  Assessment < L
  L < Decision
  Decision < Notify
Take the transitive closure of these relations.
No order between Identity Check and D is imposed.
```

This executable control-flow abstraction is a superset of guarded business behavior. Its XOR choices require external data guards; its concurrency permits, rather than guarantees, simultaneous execution. Identity-invalidating exceptions are outside this initial candidate.

Monitor nonpilot queues. | Prevent one arm's gains from preferential capacity or another arm's congestion. | Keep all statutory priority rules; reserve capacity for protected cases; cap rollout saturation. | Arm-specific resource time, shared-resource contention, borrowing, and nonpilot queue age. |
| Power and minimum support | Use B's audited duration distribution to simulate power for a practically meaningful three-hour median reduction, with cluster/serial dependence and p90 precision. Provisional floor: 500 analyzable eligible cases per arm, approximately 50 observations in each upper decile. | Avoid unsupported quantile claims and underpowered subgroup extrapolation. | This floor is not a power guarantee; increase sample if intervals remain wide. At roughly 131 cases/week and assumed 20–30% eligibility, 1,000 eligible cases require about 26–39 weeks, then complete cycle and 90-day appeal follow-up. | Capture normal operational variation and mature outcomes. | Recalculate using actual arrivals and eligibility; predefine interim looks without optional stopping. | Cohort maturity, weekly load, seasonal variation, and 90-day follow-up completeness. |

Mechanism failure means genuine, lawful overlap occurs but the critical path or total cycle time does not improve, or downstream congestion offsets the gain. Evidence would include verified concurrent execution, unchanged receipt-dominated paths, longer assessment/legal queues, or increased repeat visits. Modify readiness rules, release pacing, or the eligible population before considering additional capacity as a separately evaluated change.

Implementation failure means assignment rarely produces the intended execution: locks serialize work, task readiness is misclassified, owners cannot act concurrently, or recorded overlap reflects back-entry. Diagnose this with ownership telemetry, occurrence-versus-entry timestamps, independent audits, and partial-order conformance. Repair implementation before inferring that the scheduling mechanism is ineffective.

The primary comparison remains intention-to-treat. Do not discard noncompliers or adjust the total-effect estimate for realized overlap, a post-assignment mediator. Fidelity analyses explain results but do not automatically identify mediation effects. Common logging and enabling IT in both arms isolate the policy where feasible; otherwise the estimate applies to the named enablement package. Monitoring shared and nonpilot queues reveals whether apparent local benefit is purchased through displacement rather than a site-wide gain.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

The model below retains concurrent identity and document paths, mandatory joins, optional receipt, and an optional legal-review loop. Its control-flow soundness does not establish legal compliance: actual case attributes must guard optional branches, and operational constraints require additional implementation or simulation.

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    Transition,
    SilentTransition,
    OperatorPOWL,
    StrictPartialOrder,
)


# A: each parse creates an independent tree with consistent parent/child links.
a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
    "X('Approve', 'Reject'), 'Notify')"
)
a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register', +('Identity Check', 'Document Check'), 'Assessment', "
    "X('Approve', 'Reject'), 'Notify')"
)
a_before_net, a_before_im, a_before_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(a_before)
a_after_net, a_after_im, a_after_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(a_after)


def strict_po(nodes, precedence_edges):
    # Explicitly construct a strict, transitively closed relation.
    # Match nodes by object identity: repeated activity labels are allowed,
    # but each structural occurrence below has a fresh POWL node.
    nodes = list(nodes)
    indices = {id(node): index for index, node in enumerate(nodes)}
    if len(indices) != len(nodes):
        raise ValueError("A POWL node cannot be reused as two children")
    count = len(nodes)
    reachable = [[False] * count for _ in range(count)]
    for source, target in precedence_edges:
        reachable[indices[id(source)]][indices[id(target)]] = True
    for intermediate in range(count):
        for source in range(count):
            for target in range(count):
                if (reachable[source][intermediate]
                        and reachable[intermediate][target]):
                    reachable[source][target] = True
    if any(reachable[index][index] for index in range(count)):
        raise ValueError("Precedence relation contains a cycle")
    result = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)
    for source in range(count):
        for target in range(count):
            if reachable[source][target]:
                result.order.add_edge(nodes[source], nodes[target])
    return result


def fresh_document_path():
    # The silent alternative is permitted only when this document version
    # has no outstanding statutory originals requirement. POWL itself does
    # not enforce that data guard or document-package completeness.
    receipt_choice = OperatorPOWL(
        operator=Operator.XOR,
        children=[SilentTransition(), Transition(label="Originals Receipt")],
    )
    document_check = Transition(label="Document Check")
    return strict_po(
        [receipt_choice, document_check],
        [(receipt_choice, document_check)],
    )


register = Transition(label="Register")
identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")
document_path = fresh_document_path()
assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")

# Every legal supplement redo uses new structural nodes, even though
# its visible activity labels match the first-pass labels.
request_supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
repeat_document_path = fresh_document_path()
repeat_assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")
redo = strict_po(
    [request_supplement, repeat_document_path, repeat_assessment],
    [
        (request_supplement, repeat_document_path),
        (repeat_document_path, repeat_assessment),
    ],
)

# LOOP(body, redo) executes body (redo body)*:
# Legal Review; optionally Request Supplement, documents, Assessment,
# then Legal Review again. Exit requires a resolved review in production.
legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP,
    children=[legal_review, redo],
)
legal_choice = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[SilentTransition(), legal_loop],
)
# The silent legal branch is forbidden for public-sector/high-value cases.

decision = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[Transition(label="Approve"), Transition(label="Reject")],
)
notify = Transition(label="Notify")

b_candidate = strict_po(
    [register, identity, document_path, assessment,
     legal_choice, decision, notify],
    [
        (register, identity),
        (register, document_path),
        (identity, assessment),       # Assessment waits for identity.
        (document_path, assessment),  # Assessment also waits for documents.
        (assessment, legal_choice),
        (legal_choice, decision),
        (decision, notify),
    ],
)
# strict_po calls order.add_edge for these edges and their transitive
# consequences. There is no identity -> document_path edge or reverse edge.
# Thus identity can overlap receipt/document work, with a mandatory join.

b_net, b_im, b_fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(b_candidate)

models = {
    "A before": (a_before_net, a_before_im, a_before_fm),
    "A after": (a_after_net, a_after_im, a_after_fm),
    "B candidate": (b_net, b_im, b_fm),
}
for name, (net, initial_marking, final_marking) in models.items():
    workflow_net = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    soundness_result = pm4py.check_soundness(
        net, initial_marking, final_marking
    )
    # The public soundness API commonly returns (bool, diagnostics).
    sound = (soundness_result[0] if isinstance(soundness_result, tuple)
             else bool(soundness_result))
    print(name, "workflow_net:", workflow_net, "sound:", sound)
    if not workflow_net or not sound:
        raise RuntimeError("Model failed workflow-net/soundness validation: " + name)

# Limitations:
# - XOR choices lack data guards: originals/legal obligations and final
#   approval criteria must be enforced by the surrounding application.
# - Resources, role qualifications, capacities, queue policies, WIP limits,
#   case locks, evidence versions, and durations are not encoded here.
# - Concurrency permits independent execution; it does not ensure temporal
#   overlap, shorter queues, lower cycle time, or independent check data.
# - Identity is assumed to remain valid through supplement loops. Cases
#   invalidating identity need an expanded exception model and a fresh join.
# - Structural soundness means proper control-flow completion remains
#   possible, not that every operational policy or loop will terminate.
# - Untimed playout would yield interleavings, not service/queue predictions;
#   performance forecasts require calibrated timed, resource-aware models.
```

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go**

Transfer the scheduling principle that independent, ready verification branches can run without an artificial identity-before-document dependency, followed by a mandatory assessment join. Treat Site A as evidence of plausibility and implementation conditions. Its nine-hour median reduction accompanied more staff, a faster API, queue huddles, better recording, and simpler claims; it does not identify the benefit of parallelization alone.

Do not copy immediate document execution for every case. Site B must preserve originals receipt, mandatory legal review, and supplement-dependent reassessment. Creating a blocked task is different from authorizing work. Also avoid copying A's six-task WIP rule or assuming specialized reviewers can substitute for one another. Audited concurrent ownership is a prerequisite for testing the policy reliably.

Begin with digitally complete, originals-not-required, lower-complexity claims without mandatory legal review or a genuine information dependency. Their actual share is unknown and cannot exceed 38%. This subgroup has the closest structural match to A and avoids the longest statutory branches. Protect other queues from capacity diversion. Eligible cases might save approximately three to six hours under favorable local queue conditions; the whole-site median benefit could be much smaller, and no tail improvement is assured.

Expand only after a concurrent pilot demonstrates real lawful overlap, a practically meaningful median benefit, and adequate evidence against material quality or tail harm. Prespecify a three-hour median target and require uncertainty compatible with a real benefit; use a five-hour p90 deterioration margin, two percentage points for rework, and one for appeals as provisional guardrails requiring agreement. Wide intervals warrant continued limited observation. Add originals and legal strata separately, checking spillovers and saturation as volume increases.

Modify release pacing or eligibility when verification improves but assessment/legal queues worsen. Repair IT and logging when assigned concurrency is not delivered. Abandon the policy in a tested stratum if faithful execution brings no useful cycle benefit, sustained tail deterioration, or unacceptable quality loss. Any statutory breach requires immediate suspension. This decision supports a guarded learning intervention, with expansion earned through local evidence rather than resemblance of process diagrams.

## 10. Assumption register

1. **Check independence can be established for each eligible case** — why needed: removing a genuine information dependency risks invalid results — how to test: map input lineage, audit disputed cases, and review which conclusions need verified identity — consequence if false: retain the needed precedence and exclude those cases from the initial concurrent policy.
2. **Legal and originals eligibility can be classified before assignment** — why needed: lawful treatment selection and mandatory gates require reliable attributes — how to test: compare flags against statutes and independently reviewed case files, including borderline cases — consequence if false: pause the affected stratum and use conservative mandatory receipt/review until classification is repaired.
3. **B has enough digitally ready, originals-not-required, lower-complexity, no-legal cases** — why needed: the first pilot needs support and the planning share is currently unobserved — how to test: measure the joint eligibility distribution prospectively at registration — consequence if false: extend accrual or redesign the supported stratum; do not substitute marginal percentages for joint eligibility.
4. **Audited concurrent task ownership can be delivered safely** — why needed: simultaneous task creation alone does not realize the treatment — how to test: exercise concurrent reads/writes, lock telemetry, failure recovery, and reviewer workflows before live deployment — consequence if false: postpone concurrent execution and evaluate IT enablement first.
5. **Durations, waiting, overlap, rework, and appeals can be measured consistently** — why needed: back-entry and elapsed-versus-active service ambiguity can create false mechanism and outcome signals — how to test: reconcile occurrence/entry timestamps with telemetry, validate definitions in both arms, and mature appeal follow-up — consequence if false: repair measurement, revise workload calculations, and withhold causal performance claims.
6. **Matched resource budgets and controlled interference are feasible** — why needed: apparent gains may otherwise reflect preferential capacity or displaced waiting — how to test: audit productive hours, borrowing, API/huddle changes, and nonpilot queue trajectories by calendar block — consequence if false: estimate a clearly defined package or saturation effect with an appropriate cluster design and report spillovers.
7. **Identity remains valid through the modeled supplement loop** — why needed: the candidate repeats documents and assessment without automatically repeating identity — how to test: audit supplement contents, identity expiry, and evidence-version invalidation rules — consequence if false: route those cases to an expanded exception workflow with identity rechecking and a renewed assessment join.
8. **Conditional effects remain informative at the intended rollout load** — why needed: low-saturation pilot benefits may not survive downstream congestion or unsupported case mix — how to test: stage saturation increases, check utilization and tail queues, and estimate outcomes in newly supported strata — consequence if false: restrict rollout, revise release/capacity arrangements, and re-estimate population effects rather than extrapolating the initial pilot.
