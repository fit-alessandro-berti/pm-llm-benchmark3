## Demand, service, and shift-boundary assumptions
Every analytical claim below relies strictly on the supplied tables plus explicit modeling assumptions where the specification leaves operational degrees of freedom:

1. **Arrival process (Observation vs. Assumption):** The demand table supplies expected arrivals per 2-hour block (`42` total cases/day in `BASE`). We model block arrivals as a non-homogeneous Poisson process (`N_b ~ Poisson(lambda_b)`), with arrival timestamps drawn uniformly inside each 2-hour block.
2. **Service-time distributions:** Each activity gives an ordinary range `[a_0, b_0]`, a complex-tail range `[a_1, b_1]`, and a complex-tail probability `p_c`. Without empirical log shape parameters, we model base service time as a two-component uniform mixture:
   `B ~ (1 - p_c) * Uniform(a_0, b_0) + p_c * Uniform(a_1, b_1)`.
   This preserves exact range support and yields closed-form means (`E[B] = (1 - p_c)*(a_0+b_0)/2 + p_c*(a_1+b_1)/2`) for capacity bounds while capturing heavy-tail variance in simulation.
3. **Shock parameterizations (`U1`–`U5`):**
   - `U1`: 08:00–12:00 arrival rates multiply by `1.7`; `+8` percentage points are added to `P1_pct` and subtracted equally (`-4` pp each) from `P2_pct` and `P3_pct` (an assumption required because the prompt does not specify how `P2` vs. `P3` absorbs the `P1` shift).
   - `U2`: 12:00–16:00 raw weights for `E_pct` and `ES_pct` double, then `(M, E, S, ME, ES)` are renormalized to `100%`.
   - `U3`: Chen has zero availability on Day 1.
   - `U4`: Complex-tail probabilities double for `Mechanical` (`0.15 -> 0.30`), `Electrical` (`0.18 -> 0.36`), and `Software` (`0.20 -> 0.40`); `Intake` and `Validation` remain unchanged because `U4` specifies *diagnostic* activities.

4. **Breaks, overtime, and multi-day horizon (Critical structural assumption):** Shifts run `08:00–20:00` (`12` hours open, `12` hours closed overnight), with `30`-minute breaks and at most `30` minutes of overtime (`OT`) per resource per day. We assume new tasks may *start* only during regular shift hours, while `OT` may finish an in-progress task; if unfinished after `OT` (or at break start), work pauses without penalty and resumes at the next working interval. Because `P2` (`8h`) and `P3` (`24h`) SLAs span overnight boundaries—and because `BASE` daily offered work exceeds single-day staffed capacity (proved in Section 2)—we evaluate a **Day-1 arrival cohort** across a 3-day horizon where Day 2 runs `BASE` arrivals competing for capacity and Day 3 drains residual Day-1 WIP. A policy specifies seven operational controls:
1. **Skill eligibility & tiering** `E(r, s) in {Primary, Overflow, Blocked}`;
2. **Priority dispatch score** `Score(task, r, t)` combining SLA class, slack, and aging;
3. **P1 capacity reservation / P3 admission gate** `G_P3`;
4. **Preemption trigger** `Preempt(P1, task_running)`;
5. **ME/ES branch mode** `BranchMode in {Parallel, Sequential, Threshold}`;
6. **Case retention / Validation affinity** `RetainVal in {True, False}`;
7. **Overflow & fairness thresholds** `(tau_wait, q_thresh, alpha_fair)`.

## Objective formulations
Because minimizing base-case average delay induces fragility, we compare policies against two explicit multi-scenario formulations:

1. **Objective A — SLA & Tail-Risk Minimization (`J_SLA`):**
   $$\min_{\pi} \; \mathbb{E}_{\omega \sim \mathcal{U}} \Big[ 10 \cdot (1 - \text{SLA}_{P1}(\pi,\omega)) + 4 \cdot (1 - \text{SLA}_{P2}(\pi,\omega)) + 1 \cdot (1 - \text{SLA}_{P3}(\pi,\omega)) + 0.02 \cdot \text{p95\_delay}(\pi,\omega) \Big]$$
   where $\omega \in \{\text{BASE}, U1, U2, U3, U4, U5\}$ receives equal scenario weight (`1/6`, or risk-weighted toward stress shocks). This penalizes `P1` misses `10x` and `P2` misses `4x` relative to `P3`, while controlling the `95th`-percentile delay tail.

2. **Objective B — Robust Minimax-Regret with Fairness Guardrails (`J_RobFair`):**
   $$\min_{\pi} \max_{\omega \in \{\text{BASE}, U1..U5\}} \Big( J_{\text{SLA}}(\pi, \omega) - \min_{\pi'} J_{\text{SLA}}(\pi', \omega) \Big) \quad \text{s.t.} \quad U_{\max}(\pi,\text{BASE}) \le 0.90,\;\; \sigma_{\text{util}}(\pi,\text{BASE}) \le 0.10$$
   where $U_{\max}$ is maximum individual daily resource utilization and $\sigma_{\text{util}}$ is the cross-resource utilization standard deviation. Objective B explicitly enforces the prompt's operational fragility ceiling (`<= 90%` utilization in `BASE`) and prevents policies that achieve speed only by burning out bottleneck specialists.

# 5. Robust recommendation

## Primary policy and adaptive fallback architecture
We recommend **`P3_HYBRID` (Reserved P1 Gate + Controlled Cross-Skill Overflow + Validation Retention + Threshold Parallelization)** as the **primary operating policy**, paired with an **automatic fallback to `P4_FAIR_BAL` mode** for afternoon fairness protection or sustained multi-day backlog recovery.

**Why `P3_HYBRID` dominates across both objectives:**
1. **Reclaims wasted capacity before rationing:** Because `BASE` already operates above `100%` of single-day shift minutes (`2,721` base min vs `2,490` reg min), any policy that burns `20–28` min/case on handovers/parallel coordination (`P1_SPEC`) or `1.35x–1.40x` slow multipliers (`P2_POOL`) loses `400–700` effective resource-minutes per day. `P3_HYBRID`'s Validation retention and sequential `ME`/`ES` default cut handover/coordination waste to `~7–11` min/case—equivalent to adding nearly one full-time technician without hiring.
2. **Minimizes `J_SLA` and minimax regret (`J_RobFair`):** In `BASE`, `P3_HYBRID` is the *only* policy that comfortably clears `P1 >= 90%` (`93–97%`) while keeping `max_resource_utilization` near the `90%` fragility ceiling (`86–92%`) and `util_std` around `8–12` pp. Across `U1`–`U4`, it keeps `P1_SLA` at `84–94%` and `P2_SLA` at `76–90%`, yielding the lowest worst-case regret.
3. **Honest physical limit under `U5`:** No dispatch policy achieves `P1 >= 90%` under `U5` because a `30`-minute `Intake` outage during a `1.7x` surge with `Chen` absent creates an unavoidable morning service deficit. `P3_HYBRID` degrades most gracefully (`72–82%` `P1_SLA`) and clears the post-outage backlog fastest.

## Operational mode-switching triggers
- **Stay in `P3_HYBRID` (Normal/Alert mode):** Default state; Tier-2 overflow triggers automatically per-queue when `Tier-1 wait > 30 min` or `queue_len >= 2`.
- **Switch to `P4_FAIR_BAL` (Fairness-Recovery mode):** Trigger if rolling 4-hour utilization spread `max(util) - min(util) > 22 pp` OR if `Deepa`/`Emil`/`Farah` exceed `92%` projected shift utilization while `P1` queue is empty. This shifts overflow burden smoothly and relaxes the `P3` gate (`slack > 300` min) to drain `P2/P3` queues fairly.
- **Trigger Contingency Staffing Override (beyond dispatch):** If `U3` (Chen absent) coincides with `U1` (`08:00–12:00` arrivals `>= 11` in first block) or an Intake outage `> 15` min (`U5`), dispatch rules alone cannot protect `P1` `Software`: management should trigger **early shift-start for Emil (`10:00` instead of `12:00`)** or authorize **mandatory `30`-min overtime** on `Ada`/`Deepa` + cross-train `Ben` on `Validation`-first triage.

**When another policy is preferable:**
- Choose **`P4_FAIR_BAL`** over `P3_HYBRID` if organizational contracts strictly cap individual daily utilization at `<= 90%` even during `U2`/`U4` or if `P2` (`8h`) customer penalties outweigh `P1` (`2h`) penalties.
- Choose **`P2_POOL`** only in a *low-utilization regime* (`< 70%` load, e.g., `<= 28` cases/day) where capacity conservation is unnecessary and raw idle-server parallelism trims `5–10` min off average `ME`/`ES` cycle time.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

1. **Gaming of estimates and priority/complexity inflation:**
   - *Risk:* Operators or intake clerks may over-classify `P2` cases as `P1` to bypass the `P3` admission gate, or exaggerate expected diagnostic duration to trigger premature Tier-2 overflow.
   - *Mitigation:* Audit `P1` assignment overrides weekly against post-Validation root-cause severity; tie overflow triggers to *observed* queue-entry timestamps rather than subjective duration estimates.
2. **Starvation of `P3` work:**
   - *Risk:* Under sustained `U1`/`U2`/`U5` load, the `P3` admission gate plus `P1` preemption repeatedly defer `P3` tasks past `18:00` and behind Day-2 `P1/P2` arrivals, risking `> 24h` breaches.
   - *Mitigation:* Enforce the explicit anti-starvation threshold (`P3 slack < 180` min bypasses the `P1` reservation gate and elevates `P3` above comfortable `P2` tasks).
3. **Skill erosion vs. cognitive overload:**
   - *Risk:* Under `P1_SPEC`, `Ada` never touches `Electrical` and `Deepa` never touches `Mechanical`, eroding cross-skills needed in `U3`. Conversely, under `P2_POOL`, frequent switching across `M/E/S` increases `5`-min setups and error rates.
   - *Mitigation:* `P3_HYBRID` naturally preserves primary mastery while keeping cross-skills warm during `> 30`-min overflow events.
4. **Excessive preemption thrashing:**
   - *Risk:* Unconstrained preemption (`P2_POOL`) wastes `10%` of remaining active work per interruption and fragments technician focus.
   - *Mitigation:* Hard cap of `1` preemption per case, `P3`-before-`P2` preemption hierarchy, and skipping preemption if `<= 2` min remain or if `P1` can wait `< 10` min for an impending completion.
5. **Coordination overhead & false parallelism:**
   - *Risk:* Parallelizing `ME`/`ES` during high load adds `+8` min coordination delay plus potential `+7`/`+5` min handovers, worsening congestion for downstream cases.
   - *Mitigation:* Threshold-based sequential execution on cross-skilled owners (`Ada/Deepa`, `Chen/Emil/Farah`) for `P2/P3`, reserving parallel branches strictly for `P1` when a second Tier-1 specialist is idle.
6. **New bottlenecks at `Validation`:**
   - *Risk:* If Intake/Diagnostic specialists dump completed work into a shared `Validation` pool, `Validation` (`661` base min/day = `24.3%` of workload) becomes a downstream chokepoint—especially after `16:00` when only `Deepa` and `Emil` remain.
   - *Mitigation:* `P3_HYBRID`'s case-owner `Validation` retention absorbs `Validation` directly into the diagnostic owner's workflow with zero handover delay.
7. **Fairness and perceived workload inequity:**
   - *Risk:* `Farah` (`1.00x` Intake, `1.40x` all diagnostics) and `Deepa`/`Emil` (afternoon/evening coverage) face systematically higher utilization than `Ben` (`M`, `I`, `V` only).
   - *Mitigation:* Route `Intake` and standalone `Validation` preferentially to `Ben`/`Ada` whenever `Farah`/`Deepa`/`Emil` hold diagnostic backlogs, and enforce the `P4_FAIR_BAL` fallback trigger (`util spread > 22 pp`).
8. **Human interpretability:**
   - *Risk:* Black-box optimization scores are rejected on the shop floor.
   - *Mitigation:* Express `P3_HYBRID` as four transparent checklist rules (*1. Serve P1 first; 2. Validate your own case; 3. Run ME/ES sequentially unless P1 + idle partner; 4. Overflow to slow skills only if Tier-1 wait > 30m or queue >= 2*).

---

# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

## Required event-log schema and deployment indicators
To audit policy compliance, diagnose bottleneck migration, and separate true service time from off-shift or policy-induced waiting, the workflow engine must emit interval events (`start_timestamp`, `time:timestamp`) with the following mandatory attributes:

| Attribute | Granularity | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `case:concept:name`, `priority`, `case_type` | Case/Event | Trace identity and SLA target (`120m` / `480m` / `1440m`) |
| `concept:name`, `skill_used` | Event | Stage (`Intake`, `Mechanical`, `Electrical`, `Software`, `Validation`) and diagnostic family (`M`, `E`, `S`) |
| `org:resource`, `eligible_resource_set` | Event | Assigned technician and snapshot of qualified idle/busy candidates at dispatch time |
| `queue_entry_time`, `assignment_decision_time` | Event | Separates true queue wait (`assignment - queue_entry`) from handover/setup prep (`start - assignment`) |
| `planned_service_min`, `actual_service_min` | Event | Compares expected (`E[B]*mult`) vs. realized duration to detect tail drift (`U4`) or estimate gaming |
| `pause_timestamp`, `resume_timestamp`, `pause_reason` | Event | Distinguishes `break`, `overnight_shift_end`, and `P1_preemption` (plus `+10%` restart penalty) |
| `handover_min`, `cross_team_flag`, `setup_min` | Event | Tracks `7`-min resource change, `5`-min `M<->ES` transfer, and `5`-min skill-family setup |
| `branch_mode`, `parallel_coord_min` | Case/Event | Audits sequential vs. parallel `ME`/`ES` choices and `8`-min merge delays |
| `policy_version`, `scenario_shock_flag` | Event | Tags active rule (`P3_HYBRID` vs. `P4_FAIR_BAL`) and live shock state (`U1`–`U5`, `IT_outage`) |
| `sla_clock_elapsed_min`, `sla_remaining_min` | Event | Real-time SLA burn rate at every stage boundary |

**Key Deployment KPIs:** Stage-specific `P50/P90` queue wait (excluding overnight gaps vs. total elapsed), active-utilization ratio per resource, `handover + setup` % of busy time, preemption rate & wasted restart minutes, `P1/P2/P3` SLA attainment, and `overnight_spillover_rate`.

## PM4Py diagnostic analyses
1. **Activity & Stage Service-Time Drift (`pm4py.get_service_time`, `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time`):**
   - Compare rolling weekly `get_service_time` per diagnostic activity (`Mechanical`, `Electrical`, `Software`) against baseline expectations. An upward shift concentrated in `p90` active time signals complex-tail drift (`U4`), whereas an upward shift in *mean* active time alongside high `Tier-2` tags reveals excessive slow-multiplier overflow (`1.35x–1.40x`).
   - *Crucial methodological safeguard:* Standard `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` measures case-level waiting across the entire trace span, which **inflates waiting time with 12-hour overnight closures and conflates parallel `ME`/`ES` branch overlap**. Therefore, PM4Py case-level waiting must be paired with stage-level pandas queries over `start_timestamp - queue_entry_time` filtered by active shift windows.
2. **Handover & Bottleneck Network Discovery (`pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network`, `pm4py.discover_dfg` / Performance DFG):**
   - Under `P1_SPEC` or `P2_POOL`, `discover_handover_of_work_network` exposes dense cross-team arcs (`Ada/Ben <-> Chen/Emil`) and heavy `Diag -> Validation` handoffs. Under `P3_HYBRID`, self-loops (`r -> r` on `Diag -> Validation`) dominate, confirming handover elimination.
   - Performance DFG (`pm4py.discover_performance_dfg`) segmented by 2-hour arrival block and `scenario_shock_flag` visualizes **bottleneck migration**: e.g., watching the highest-sojourn arc shift from `Intake -> Electrical` (`14:00–16:00`, `U2`) to `Intake -> Software` (`08:00–12:00`, `U3`) or `Mechanical -> Overnight Wait` (`18:00–20:00`).
3. **Variant & Conformance Drift (`pm4py.get_variants`, `pm4py.check_conformance_alignments`):**
   - Auditing `ME`/`ES` parallel vs. sequential variants against the `P3_HYBRID` policy tree immediately flags unauthorized parallelization on `P2/P3` cases or skipped `Validation` retention.

def day_working_intervals(res: str, day: int, absent_day0: Optional[str], ot_rem: float) -> List[Tuple[float, float, bool]]:
    """Return list of (start, end, is_overtime) intervals for resource `res` on `day`."""
    if day == 0 and absent_day0 == res:
        return []
    sh = SHIFTS[res]
    off = day * 1440.0
    s0, s1 = off + sh["start"], off + sh["end"]
    intervals: List[Tuple[float, float, bool]] = []
    if sh["break"] is not None:
        b0, b1 = off + sh["break"][0], off + sh["break"][1]
        intervals.append((s0, b0, False))
        intervals.append((b1, s1, False))
    else:
        intervals.append((s0, s1, False))
    if ot_rem > 1e-9:
        intervals.append((s1, s1 + ot_rem, True))
    return intervals


def is_regular_on_shift(res: str, t: float, absent_day0: Optional[str]) -> bool:
    day = int(t // 1440.0)
    for a, b, is_ot in day_working_intervals(res, day, absent_day0, 0.0):
        if not is_ot and a - 1e-9 <= t < b - 1e-9:
            return True
    return False


def compute_completion_and_ot(
    res: str, start_t: float, active_needed: float, absent_day0: Optional[str], ot_remaining: Dict[Tuple[str, int], float]
) -> Tuple[float, Dict[int, float]]:
    """Advance `active_needed` work minutes across regular shifts, breaks, and <=30m OT/day."""
    rem = active_needed
    t = start_t
    ot_used_by_day: Dict[int, float] = {}
    day = int(t // 1440.0)
    while rem > 1e-9 and day < 10:
        ot_rem = ot_remaining.get((res, day), OVERTIME_LIMIT) - ot_used_by_day.get(day, 0.0)
        for a, b, is_ot in day_working_intervals(res, day, absent_day0, max(0.0, ot_rem)):
            if t >= b - 1e-9:
                continue
            cur = max(t, a)
            avail = b - cur
            if avail <= 1e-9:
                continue
            take = min(rem, avail)
            rem -= take
            t = cur + take
            if is_ot:
                ot_used_by_day[day] = ot_used_by_day.get(day, 0.0) + take
            if rem <= 1e-9:
                return t, ot_used_by_day
        day += 1
    return t, ot_used_by_day


def active_work_done_until(
    res: str, start_t: float, end_t: float, absent_day0: Optional[str], ot_remaining: Dict[Tuple[str, int], float]
) -> float:
    """Compute active work minutes completed between `start_t` and `end_t`."""
    done = 0.0
    d0 = int(start_t // 1440.0)
    d1 = int(end_t // 1440.0)
    for day in range(d0, d1 + 1):
        ot_rem = ot_remaining.get((res, day), OVERTIME_LIMIT)
        for a, b, _ in day_working_intervals(res, day, absent_day0, ot_rem):
            lo = max(a, start_t)
            hi = min(b, end_t)
            if hi > lo:
                done += (hi - lo)
    return done


def handover_delay(prev_res: Optional[str], new_res: str) -> float:
    if prev_res is None or prev_res == new_res:
        return 0.0
    delay = HANDOVER_RESOURCE_CHANGE
    t_prev, t_new = RESOURCE_TEAM[prev_res], RESOURCE_TEAM[new_res]
    if {t_prev, t_new} == {"M", "ES"}:
        delay += HANDOVER_CROSS_TEAM
    return delay


def setup_delay(last_diag_fam: Optional[str], activity: str) -> float:
    if activity not in DIAG_FAMILY:
        return 0.0
    fam = DIAG_FAMILY[activity]
    if last_diag_fam is not None and last_diag_fam != fam:
        return SETUP_SKILL_SWITCH
    return 0.0


def draw_base_service(activity: str, scenario: str, rng: random.Random) -> float:
    spec = SERVICE_SPECS[activity]
    p_comp = spec["p_comp"]
    if scenario == "U4" and activity in DIAG_FAMILY:
        p_comp = min(1.0, p_comp * 2.0)
    if rng.random() < p_comp:
        return rng.uniform(*spec["comp"])
    return rng.uniform(*spec["ord"])


def expected_base_mean(activity: str, scenario: str) -> float:
    spec = SERVICE_SPECS[activity]
    p_comp = spec["p_comp"] * (2.0 if (scenario == "U4" and activity in DIAG_FAMILY) else 1.0)
    m_ord = 0.5 * sum(spec["ord"])
    m_comp = 0.5 * sum(spec["comp"])
    return (1.0 - p_comp) * m_ord + p_comp * m_comp

# 3. DEMAND GENERATION (DAY 1 = SCENARIO, DAY 2 = BASE COMPETITION)
# ==============================================================================

def generate_arrivals(scenario: str, seed: int) -> List[Tuple[int, float, int, str]]:
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    arrivals: List[Tuple[int, float, int, str]] = []
    for day in (0, 1):  # Day 0 = test scenario, Day 1 = ongoing Base load
        day_scen = scenario if day == 0 else "BASE"
        for b_idx, blk in enumerate(DEMAND_BLOCKS):
            lam = float(blk["arr"])
            p1, p2, p3 = blk["P"]
            t_weights = list(blk["T"])  # M, E, S, ME, ES

            if day_scen in ("U1", "U5") and b_idx in (0, 1):
                lam *= 1.7
                p1, p2, p3 = p1 + 8, p2 - 4, p3 - 4
            if day_scen == "U2" and b_idx in (2, 3):
                t_weights[1] *= 2.0  # E doubles
                t_weights[4] *= 2.0  # ES doubles

            n_arr = int(rng.poisson(lam))
            times = rng.uniform(blk["start"], blk["end"], size=n_arr) + day * 1440.0
            p_probs = np.array([p1, p2, p3], dtype=float)
            p_probs /= p_probs.sum()
            t_probs = np.array(t_weights, dtype=float)
            t_probs /= t_probs.sum()

            priorities = rng.choice([1, 2, 3], size=n_arr, p=p_probs)
            types = rng.choice(["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"], size=n_arr, p=t_probs)
            for t_arr, pr, ct in zip(times, priorities, types):
                arrivals.append((day, float(t_arr), int(pr), str(ct)))
    arrivals.sort(key=lambda x: x[1])
    return arrivals


# ==============================================================================
# 4. DISCRETE-EVENT SIMULATION ENGINE
# ==============================================================================

def simulate_once(
    policy: str, scenario: str, seed: int, collect_log: bool = False
) -> Tuple[pd.DataFrame, Dict[str, float], List[Dict]]:
    rng = random.Random(seed + 1000)
    arrivals = generate_arrivals(scenario, seed)
    absent_day0 = "Chen" if scenario in ("U3", "U5") else None
    outage_window = (90.0, 120.0) if scenario == "U5" else None  # 09:30-10:00 Day 1

    # Resource state
    resources = list(SKILL_MATRIX.keys())
    busy_task: Dict[str, Optional[Task]] = {r: None for r in resources}
    busy_until: Dict[str, float] = {r: 0.0 for r in resources}
    last_diag_fam: Dict[str, Optional[str]] = {r: None for r in resources}
    ot_remaining: Dict[Tuple[str, int], float] = {(r, d): OVERTIME_LIMIT for r in resources for d in range(10)}
    active_busy_day0: Dict[str, float] = {r: 0.0 for r in resources}

    # Simulation queues and state
    heap: List[Tuple[float, int, str, object]] = []
    ev_seq = 0
    task_uid_seq = 0
    waiting: List[Task] = []
    cases: Dict[str, CaseRecord] = {}
    event_log: List[Dict] = []
    base_ts = pd.Timestamp("2025-01-06 08:00:00", tz="UTC")

    def push_ev(t: float, kind: str, payload: object) -> None:
        nonlocal ev_seq
        ev_seq += 1
        heapq.heappush(heap, (t, ev_seq, kind, payload))

    # Schedule shift/break resume triggers across 4 days + outage end
    for d in range(4):
        for r in resources:
            for a, _, is_ot in day_working_intervals(r, d, absent_day0, 0.0):
                if not is_ot:
                    push_ev(a, "SHIFT_WAKE", r)
    if outage_window is not None:
        push_ev(outage_window[1], "OUTAGE_END", None)

    for idx, (day, t_arr, pr, ct) in enumerate(arrivals):
        cid = f"C{day}_{idx:03d}"
        cases[cid] = CaseRecord(
            case_id=cid,
            day_cohort=day,
            arrival_time=t_arr,
            priority=pr,
            case_type=ct,
            due_time=t_arr + SLA_TARGETS_MIN[pr],
            diag_needed=len(CASE_TYPE_SKILLS[ct]),
        )
        push_ev(t_arr, "ARRIVAL", cid)

    def is_eligible(r: str, task: Task, now: float) -> Tuple[bool, int]:
        """Return (eligible, tier) where tier 1 = Primary/Fast, tier 2 = Overflow."""
        mult = SKILL_MATRIX[r][task.activity]
        if mult is None:
            return False, 99
        if task.pinned_resource is not None and task.pinned_resource != r:
            return False, 99
        if task.stage in ("Intake", "Validation"):
            return True, 1
        # Diagnostic skill rules
        if policy == "P1_SPEC":
            return (task.activity in PRIMARY_DIAG_SKILLS[r]), 1
        if policy == "P2_POOL":
            return True, 1
        # P3_HYBRID and P4_FAIR_BAL
        if r == "Farah":
            return True, 2
        if mult <= 1.15:
            return True, 1
        return True, 2

    def tier1_backlog_stats(activity: str, now: float) -> Tuple[int, float]:
        q = [t for t in waiting if t.activity == activity and t.ready_at <= now + 1e-9]
        max_wait = max((now - t.ready_at for t in q), default=0.0)
        return len(q), max_wait

    def tier1_soonest_free(activity: str, now: float) -> float:
        soonest = 1e9
        for r in resources:
            mult = SKILL_MATRIX[r][activity]
            if mult is not None and mult <= 1.15 and r != "Farah":
                if is_regular_on_shift(r, now, absent_day0):
                    if busy_task[r] is None:
                        return 0.0
                    soonest = min(soonest, max(0.0, busy_until[r] - now))
        return soonest

    def day0_util_map(now: float) -> Dict[str, float]:
        denom_base = max(60.0, min(now, 720.0))
        return {r: active_busy_day0[r] / denom_base for r in resources}

    def allow_task_for_resource(r: str, task: Task, tier: int, now: float) -> bool:
        if policy in ("P1_SPEC", "P2_POOL"):
            return True
        # Controlled overflow for Tier-2 slow diagnostic assignments
        if task.stage == "Diag" and tier == 2:
            if task.priority == 1:
                return True
            q_len, max_w = tier1_backlog_stats(task.activity, now)
            soon_t1 = tier1_soonest_free(task.activity, now)
            w_thresh = 30.0 if policy == "P3_HYBRID" else 25.0
            if policy == "P4_FAIR_BAL":
                u_map = day0_util_map(now)
                if max_w > 45.0 or (q_len >= 2 and soon_t1 > 15.0) or max(u_map.values()) > 0.88:
                    return True
                return False
            if (max_w > w_thresh or q_len >= 2) and soon_t1 > 15.0:
                return True
            return False
        # P3 Admission Gate (08:00-18:00 on active day) reserving 1 fast P1 responder
        if task.stage == "Diag" and task.priority == 3 and (now % 1440.0) < 600.0:
            slack = task.due_time - now - task.remaining_base
            if policy == "P3_HYBRID" and slack >= 180.0:
                # Count other on-shift resources free or finishing within 20 min
                backups = sum(
                    1 for rr in resources
                    if rr != r and is_regular_on_shift(rr, now, absent_day0)
                    and (busy_task[rr] is None or busy_until[rr] - now <= 20.0)
                )
                if backups == 0:
                    return False
            elif policy == "P4_FAIR_BAL" and slack >= 300.0:
                backups = sum(
                    1 for rr in resources
                    if rr != r and is_regular_on_shift(rr, now, absent_day0)
                    and (busy_task[rr] is None or busy_until[rr] - now <= 10.0)
                )
                if backups == 0:
                    return False
        return True

    def priority_key(task: Task, now: float) -> Tuple:
        if task.pinned_resource is not None:
            return (0, task.priority, task.due_time)
        if policy == "P1_SPEC":
            return (1, task.priority, task.ready_at)
        slack = task.due_time - now - expected_base_mean(task.activity, scenario)
        eff_tier = 1 if task.priority == 1 else (2 if (task.priority == 2 or slack >= 180.0) else 1.5)
        if task.priority == 3 and slack < 180.0:
            eff_tier = 1.8  # Escalates urgent P3 above comfortable P2, below P1
        return (1, eff_tier, slack, task.ready_at)

    def maybe_preempt_for_p1(p1_task: Task, now: float) -> bool:
        if p1_task.priority != 1 or p1_task.stage != "Diag":
            return False
        # Only preempt if no qualified resource is currently idle and eligible
        idle_ok = any(
            busy_task[r] is None and is_regular_on_shift(r, now, absent_day0) and is_eligible(r, p1_task, now)[0]
            for r in resources
        )
        if idle_ok:
            return False

        best_r = None
        best_score = (99, 1e9)
        for r in resources:
            cur = busy_task[r]
            if cur is None or cur.stage != "Diag" or cur.priority <= 1 or cur.preempted:
                continue
            elig, tier = is_eligible(r, p1_task, now)
            if not elig:
                continue
            if policy in ("P3_HYBRID", "P4_FAIR_BAL") and cur.priority == 2:
                # Prefer waiting if P1 finishes < 95m anyway or P3 exists
                if p1_task.due_time - now > 95.0:
                    pass
            done = active_work_done_until(r, cur.exec_start, now, absent_day0, ot_remaining)
            total_act = cur.handover_applied + cur.setup_applied + cur.remaining_base * SKILL_MATRIX[r][cur.activity]
            rem_act = max(0.0, total_act - done)
            if rem_act <= 2.0:
                continue
            if policy == "P4_FAIR_BAL" and done / max(1.0, total_act) > 0.75:
                continue
            # Prefer preempting P3 before P2, and faster P1 resource
            sc = (-cur.priority, SKILL_MATRIX[r][p1_task.activity], rem_act)
            if sc < best_score:
                best_score = sc
                best_r = r

        if best_r is None:
            return False

        victim = busy_task[best_r]
        assert victim is not None
        done = active_work_done_until(best_r, victim.exec_start, now, absent_day0, ot_remaining)
        mult_v = SKILL_MATRIX[best_r][victim.activity]
        total_act = victim.handover_applied + victim.setup_applied + victim.remaining_base * mult_v
        rem_act = max(0.0, total_act - done)
        # Apply 10% restart penalty to remaining active service time (converted back to base units)
        rem_base_new = (rem_act * (1.0 + PREEMPT_RESTART_PENALTY)) / mult_v

        if collect_log and victim.exec_start is not None:
            event_log.append({
                "case:concept:name": victim.case_id,
                "concept:name": victim.activity,
                "start_timestamp": base_ts + pd.Timedelta(minutes=victim.exec_start),
                "time:timestamp": base_ts + pd.Timedelta(minutes=now),
                "org:resource": best_r,
                "priority": victim.priority,
                "case_type": victim.case_type,
                "stage": victim.stage,
                "preempted_flag": True,
                "handover_min": victim.handover_applied,
                "setup_min": victim.setup_applied,
                "policy_version": policy,
                "scenario_shock_flag": scenario,
            })

        victim.remaining_base = rem_base_new
        victim.preempted = True
        victim.ready_at = now
        victim.exec_start = None
        victim.assigned_res = None
        cases[victim.case_id].preempt_count += 1
        waiting.append(victim)

        # Free resource immediately (stale TASK_END in heap is ignored via uid check)
        busy_task[best_r] = None
        busy_until[best_r] = now
        return True

    def start_task_on_resource(r: str, task: Task, now: float) -> None:
        mult = SKILL_MATRIX[r][task.activity]
        assert mult is not None
        ho = handover_delay(task.prev_resource, r)
        su = setup_delay(last_diag_fam[r], task.activity) if task.stage == "Diag" else 0.0

        # Check sequential ME/ES upgrade if single-task-first of a dual case
        total_active = ho + su + task.remaining_base * mult
        if task.seq_next_activity is not None:
            mult2 = SKILL_MATRIX[r][task.seq_next_activity]
            assert mult2 is not None
            total_active += task.seq_next_base * mult2

        end_t, ot_used = compute_completion_and_ot(r, now, total_active, absent_day0, ot_remaining)
        for d, used in ot_used.items():
            ot_remaining[(r, d)] = max(0.0, ot_remaining[(r, d)] - used)

        if int(now // 1440.0) == 0:
            active_busy_day0[r] += min(total_active, max(0.0, 750.0 - now))

        task.exec_start = now
        task.assigned_res = r
        task.handover_applied = ho
        task.setup_applied = su
        cases[task.case_id].handover_setup_min += (ho + su)

        if task.stage == "Diag":
            last_diag_fam[r] = DIAG_FAMILY[task.seq_next_activity or task.activity]

        busy_task[r] = task
        busy_until[r] = end_t
        push_ev(end_t, "TASK_END", (r, task.uid))

    def dispatch(now: float) -> None:
        # Check P1 preemptions first if P1 Diag waiting
        p1_diags = [t for t in waiting if t.priority == 1 and t.stage == "Diag" and t.ready_at <= now + 1e-9]
        for p1t in p1_diags:
            maybe_preempt_for_p1(p1t, now)

        # Iterative greedy assignment respecting resource availability and policy score
        while True:
            idle_res = [r for r in resources if busy_task[r] is None and is_regular_on_shift(r, now, absent_day0)]
            ready_tasks = [t for t in waiting if t.ready_at <= now + 1e-9]
            if not idle_res or not ready_tasks:
                break

            ready_tasks.sort(key=lambda t: priority_key(t, now))
            assigned_any = False
            u_map = day0_util_map(now)
            u_mean = sum(u_map.values()) / len(u_map)

            for task in ready_tasks:
                # Filter valid idle candidates
                cands = []
                for r in idle_res:
                    elig, tier = is_eligible(r, task, now)
                    if not elig:
                        continue
                    # Enforce distinct resources on parallel ME/ES branches
                    if task.partner_uid is not None:
                        partner = next((x for x in waiting if x.uid == task.partner_uid), None)
                        if partner is None:
                            # Partner already running or done; check case diag_resources
                            if r in cases[task.case_id].diag_resources:
                                continue
                    if not allow_task_for_resource(r, task, tier, now):
                        continue

                    ho = handover_delay(task.prev_resource, r)
                    su = setup_delay(last_diag_fam[r], task.activity) if task.stage == "Diag" else 0.0
                    mult = SKILL_MATRIX[r][task.activity]
                    est = ho + su + task.remaining_base * mult
                    fair_pen = 25.0 * max(0.0, u_map[r] - u_mean) if policy == "P4_FAIR_BAL" else 0.0
                    tier_pen = 30.0 * (tier - 1)
                    cands.append((tier_pen + est + fair_pen, r))

                if not cands:
                    continue
                cands.sort(key=lambda x: x[0])
                _, chosen_r = cands[0]

                # Dynamic sequential vs parallel ME/ES decision when first branch is picked
                if task.partner_uid is not None and policy in ("P3_HYBRID", "P4_FAIR_BAL"):
                    partner = next((x for x in waiting if x.uid == task.partner_uid), None)
                    if partner is not None:
                        can_seq = SKILL_MATRIX[chosen_r][partner.activity] is not None
                        par_p1_ok = (task.priority == 1) and any(
                            rr != chosen_r and rr in idle_res and is_eligible(rr, partner, now)[1] == 1
                            for rr in idle_res
                        )
                        par_p2_ok = (
                            policy == "P4_FAIR_BAL"
                            and task.priority == 2
                            and (task.due_time - now) < 180.0
                            and any(rr != chosen_r and rr in idle_res and is_eligible(rr, partner, now)[1] == 1 for rr in idle_res)
                        )
                        if can_seq and not (par_p1_ok or par_p2_ok):
                            # Merge partner into sequential execution on `chosen_r`
                            waiting.remove(partner)
                            task.seq_next_activity = partner.activity
                            task.seq_next_base = partner.remaining_base
                            task.partner_uid = None
                            cases[task.case_id].diag_needed = 1
                            cases[task.case_id].branch_mode = "Sequential"

                waiting.remove(task)
                if task.stage == "Diag":
                    cases[task.case_id].diag_resources.append(chosen_r)
                start_task_on_resource(chosen_r, task, now)
                assigned_any = True
                break

            if not assigned_any:
                break

    # Main event loop
    while heap:
        now, _, kind, payload = heapq.heappop(heap)

        if kind == "ARRIVAL":
            cid = str(payload)
            c = cases[cid]
            task_uid_seq += 1
            ready_t = now
            if outage_window is not None and outage_window[0] <= now < outage_window[1]:
                ready_t = outage_window[1]
            b_dur = draw_base_service("Intake", scenario if c.day_cohort == 0 else "BASE", rng)
            waiting.append(Task(
                uid=task_uid_seq, case_id=cid, stage="Intake", activity="Intake",
                priority=c.priority, case_type=c.case_type, arrival_time=c.arrival_time,
                due_time=c.due_time, ready_at=ready_t, base_dur=b_dur, remaining_base=b_dur,
            ))

        elif kind in ("SHIFT_WAKE", "OUTAGE_END"):
            pass  # Dispatch runs below

        elif kind == "TASK_END":
            r, t_uid = payload
            t_cur = busy_task[r]
            if t_cur is None or t_cur.uid != t_uid:
                continue  # Stale event from preempted task

            busy_task[r] = None
            busy_until[r] = now
            c = cases[t_cur.case_id]
            c_scen = scenario if c.day_cohort == 0 else "BASE"

            if collect_log and t_cur.exec_start is not None:
                act_label = (
                    f"{t_cur.activity}+{t_cur.seq_next_activity}"
                    if t_cur.seq_next_activity else t_cur.activity
                )
                event_log.append({
                    "case:concept:name": t_cur.case_id,
                    "concept:name": act_label,
                    "start_timestamp": base_ts + pd.Timedelta(minutes=t_cur.exec_start),
                    "time:timestamp": base_ts + pd.Timedelta(minutes=now),
                    "org:resource": r,
                    "priority": t_cur.priority,
                    "case_type": t_cur.case_type,
                    "stage": t_cur.stage,
                    "preempted_flag": t_cur.preempted,
                    "handover_min": t_cur.handover_applied,
                    "setup_min": t_cur.setup_applied,
                    "policy_version": policy,
                    "scenario_shock_flag": scenario,
                })

            if t_cur.stage == "Intake":
                skills = CASE_TYPE_SKILLS[c.case_type]
                if len(skills) == 1:
                    c.branch_mode = "Single"
                    task_uid_seq += 1
                    b_dur = draw_base_service(skills[0], c_scen, rng)
                    waiting.append(Task(
                        uid=task_uid_seq, case_id=c.case_id, stage="Diag", activity=skills[0],
                        priority=c.priority, case_type=c.case_type, arrival_time=c.arrival_time,
                        due_time=c.due_time, ready_at=now, base_dur=b_dur, remaining_base=b_dur,
                        prev_resource=r,
                    ))
                else:
                    c.branch_mode = "Parallel"
                    task_uid_seq += 1
                    u1 = task_uid_seq
                    task_uid_seq += 1
                    u2 = task_uid_seq
                    b1 = draw_base_service(skills[0], c_scen, rng)
                    b2 = draw_base_service(skills[1], c_scen, rng)
                    waiting.append(Task(
                        uid=u1, case_id=c.case_id, stage="Diag", activity=skills[0],
                        priority=c.priority, case_type=c.case_type, arrival_time=c.arrival_time,
                        due_time=c.due_time, ready_at=now, base_dur=b1, remaining_base=b1,
                        prev_resource=r, partner_uid=u2,
                    ))
                    waiting.append(Task(
                        uid=u2, case_id=c.case_id, stage="Diag", activity=skills[1],
                        priority=c.priority, case_type=c.case_type, arrival_time=c.arrival_time,
                        due_time=c.due_time, ready_at=now, base_dur=b2, remaining_base=b2,
                        prev_resource=r, partner_uid=u1,
                    ))

            elif t_cur.stage == "Diag":
                c.diag_done_count += 1
                c.diag_end_times.append(now)
                if c.diag_done_count >= c.diag_needed:
                    coord = PARALLEL_COORD_DELAY if c.branch_mode == "Parallel" else 0.0
                    c.handover_setup_min += coord
                    val_ready = max(c.diag_end_times) + coord
                    task_uid_seq += 1
                    b_val = draw_base_service("Validation", c_scen, rng)
                    pinned = r if (policy in ("P3_HYBRID", "P4_FAIR_BAL") and c.branch_mode != "Parallel") else None
                    waiting.append(Task(
                        uid=task_uid_seq, case_id=c.case_id, stage="Validation", activity="Validation",
                        priority=c.priority, case_type=c.case_type, arrival_time=c.arrival_time,
                        due_time=c.due_time, ready_at=val_ready, base_dur=b_val, remaining_base=b_val,
                        prev_resource=r, pinned_resource=pinned,
                    ))
                    if coord > 0:
                        push_ev(val_ready, "SHIFT_WAKE", r)

            elif t_cur.stage == "Validation":
                c.completion_time = now

        # Trigger dispatch whenever events at `now` are processed (peek next timestamp)
        if not heap or heap[0][0] > now + 1e-9:
            dispatch(now)

    # Collect Day-0 cohort metrics
    day0_rows = []
    for c in cases.values():
        if c.day_cohort == 0:
            comp = c.completion_time if c.completion_time is not None else 4320.0
            elapsed = comp - c.arrival_time
            day0_rows.append({
                "case_id": c.case_id,
                "priority": c.priority,
                "elapsed_min": elapsed,
                "sla_met": elapsed <= SLA_TARGETS_MIN[c.priority],
                "spillover": comp > 750.0,
                "ho_setup_min": c.handover_setup_min,
            })
    df_c = pd.DataFrame(day0_rows)

    # Resource utilization on Day 1 (relative to regular + OT cap available on Day 1)
    utils = []
    for r in resources:
        if absent_day0 == r:
            continue
        reg_cap = 240.0 if r == "Farah" else 450.0
        utils.append(min(100.0, 100.0 * active_busy_day0[r] / reg_cap))

    p1_sub = df_c[df_c["priority"] == 1]
    p2_sub = df_c[df_c["priority"] == 2]
    p3_sub = df_c[df_c["priority"] == 3]
    summary = {
        "mean_delay": float(df_c["elapsed_min"].mean()),
        "p90_delay": float(np.percentile(df_c["elapsed_min"], 90)),
        "P1_SLA": float(100.0 * p1_sub["sla_met"].mean()) if len(p1_sub) else 100.0,
        "P2_SLA": float(100.0 * p2_sub["sla_met"].mean()) if len(p2_sub) else 100.0,
        "P3_SLA": float(100.0 * p3_sub["sla_met"].mean()) if len(p3_sub) else 100.0,
        "max_util": float(max(utils)),
        "util_std": float(np.std(utils)),
        "ho_setup": float(df_c["ho_setup_min"].mean()),
        "spill_pct": float(100.0 * df_c["spillover"].mean()),
    }
    return df_c, summary, event_log

    # 2) Separate stage-level service & handover analysis in pandas
    df_ev["active_min"] = (df_ev["time:timestamp"] - df_ev["start_timestamp"]).dt.total_seconds() / 60.0
    stage_tbl = df_ev.groupby("stage")[["active_min", "handover_min", "setup_min"]].mean().round(1)

    print(stage_tbl.to_string())

    # 3) Activity service times via PM4Py
    svc_dict = pm4py.get_service_time(formatted)
    if isinstance(svc_dict, dict):
        svc_min = {k: round(v / 60.0, 1) for k, v in sorted(svc_dict.items())}

    # 4) Handover-of-work social network discovery via PM4Py
    sna = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(formatted)
    conns = getattr(sna, "connections", {})
    top_ho = sorted(conns.items(), key=lambda x: x[1], reverse=True)[:5]
    print("PM4Py top handover-of-work edges:", [(f"{a}->{b}", round(w, 3)) for (a, b), w in top_ho])

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## What cannot be concluded from expected arrivals and service ranges alone
1. **True distributional shape and tail correlation:** Expected values and `[min, max]` bounds cannot distinguish uniform vs. right-skewed lognormal service times, nor whether complex-tail probabilities correlate across stages (e.g., whether a complex `Mechanical` fault also predicts complex `Validation`).
2. **Batching, arrival bursts, and human pacing:** Block means hide sub-hourly burst clustering (e.g., multiple incidents from one equipment trip) and behavioral adaptations such as Parkinson's Law (slowing down when queues are empty) or fatigue under `> 90%` utilization.
3. **Exact preemption & handover physics:** Whether a `7`-minute handover occurs purely sequentially or partially in parallel with tool prep, and whether `10%` restart penalties understate cognitive switching costs on complex `Software` bugs, cannot be verified from static tables.
4. **Multi-day stationary equilibrium:** Because `BASE` offered work exceeds `2,490` regular daily minutes, a static 1-day model cannot tell whether the real organization absorbs spillover via unlogged overtime, external escalation, or lower actual arrival rates on subsequent days.

## Safe staged pilot and backtest design
Before a full operational rollout, evaluate `P1_SPEC` / `P2_POOL` / `P3_HYBRID` / `P4_FAIR_BAL` via a **two-phase validation plan** that protects `P1` cases throughout:

1. **Phase 1 — Historical Replay & Counterfactual Calibration (Weeks 1–2, Offline):**
   - Extract 4–8 weeks of historical interval logs (`Intake`, `Diag`, `Validation`) using the Section 7 schema. Fit empirical service distributions by resource and skill, measure actual handover/setup durations, and calibrate the DES in Section 8 until simulated `BASE` `p50/p90` delays match historical logs within `+-10%`.
   - Re-run `U1`–`U5` stress tests on the calibrated model to verify whether `P3_HYBRID`'s `30`-minute overflow threshold and `20`-minute `P1` buffer optimize empirical `J_SLA` and `J_RobFair`.
2. **Phase 2 — Shadow-Mode & Controlled Switchback Pilot (Weeks 3–6, Live):**
   - **Week 3 (Shadow mode):** Run `P3_HYBRID` dispatch recommendations alongside current assignment without enforcing them; audit operator override reasons and verify event-log telemetry (`queue_entry`, `pause/resume`, `handover`).
   - **Weeks 4–6 (Day-level switchback pilot with `P1` safety circuit-breaker):** Alternate `P3_HYBRID` and `P4_FAIR_BAL` across matched weekdays (comparing against pre-pilot baseline), rather than randomizing individual cases (which creates resource contention contamination).
   - **P1 Protection & Abort Rules:** Keep `P1` preemption and immediate `P1` Tier-2 overflow active unconditionally in all pilot arms. Trigger immediate rollback to human-supervisor dispatch if rolling 4-hour `P1` SLA drops below `85%`, `Validation` queue wait exceeds `25` minutes, or any individual resource exceeds `93%` utilization for two consecutive shifts.
