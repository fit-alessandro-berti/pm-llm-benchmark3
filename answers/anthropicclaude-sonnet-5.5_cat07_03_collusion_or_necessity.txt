### 4.1 Four-eyes violations

```python
def four_eyes(df, a1, a2, res_col=RES):
    """Cases containing both activities where pm4py says four-eyes is NOT satisfied,
    cross-checked with an independent pandas computation."""
    both = (df[df[ACT].isin([a1, a2])].groupby(CASE)[ACT].nunique() == 2)
    both_cases = set(both[both].index)

    # pm4py: returns cases that SATISFY the principle (keep_violations=True in newer
    # versions returns the violators directly; check your version).
    ok = pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(
        df, a1, a2, activity_key=ACT, resource_key=res_col,
        timestamp_key=TS, case_id_key=CASE)
    viol_pm4py = both_cases - set(ok[CASE])

    # independent check
    g = (df[df[ACT].isin([a1, a2])]
         .groupby([CASE, ACT])[res_col].apply(set).unstack())
    g = g.dropna()
    viol_pd = set(g[g.apply(lambda r: len(r[a1] & r[a2]) > 0, axis=1)].index)
    return viol_pm4py, viol_pd

rules = [("Create Purchase Order", "Approve Purchase Order"),
         ("Change Vendor Bank Details", "Payment Approval"),
         ("Emergency Override", "Retrospective Justification"),
         ("Vendor Check", "Change Vendor Bank Details")]

for a1, a2 in rules:
    for col in ["org:resource", "entered_by", "approved_by"]:   # sensitivity to attribution
        vp, vd = four_eyes(human, a1, a2, col)
        print(a1, a2, col, len(vp), len(vd), len(vp ^ vd))
```

# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Evidence that would increase concern**
- **Bank-detail changes (V-77x):**
  - No maintenance ticket, or tickets dated *after* the change.
  - Missing callback verification to a known vendor contact.
  - A new beneficiary name or country that does not match the vendor master.
  - A bank change shortly before a high-value payment, followed by a reversion.
  - Change and payment approval on the same device or IP, or the same user account under another name.
  - Payments released before goods receipt or invoice match.
- **Identity and attribution:** `entered_by` or `approved_by` showing the same human in both roles of a four-eyes pair, for example a Lena or Kim credential used for both steps. Access logs showing shared credentials.
- **Emergency handling:**
  - Violations in cases with emergency_flag=False, or flagged emergency with no Emergency Override event.
  - No retrospective justification, or one authored by the override approver.
  - Justifications copy-pasted or dated after payment release or after audit notification.
  - Approvals above the delegated limit.
  - A cluster of emergency cases on a single vendor with low competition.
- **Timing:** approvals within a minute of creation outside business hours, repeatedly, for the same vendor. Fast-approval share by the same pair rising *after* threshold changes. Amounts just under approval limits.
- **Vendor and payment data:** bank accounts shared across vendors or with employee master data, vendor family V-77x split across regions in amounts below thresholds, and duplicate or sequential invoices.
- **Data quality resolved adversely:** source spreadsheet metadata showing a same-person author. Mapping tables confirming the human actually created the PO.
- **Behavior around audit:** batch backdating after audit inquiry, or deleted or altered upload logs.

**Evidence that would reduce concern**
- **Bank-detail changes:**
  - Maintenance tickets with batch IDs mapping each case to the platform migration.
  - Documented callback verification.
  - Changes that preceded the payment cycle by days, with original timestamps lost only through the manual_upload process.
  - A maker-checker approval by a third party on each bank change.
  - The bank change and the payment approval being unrelated (different vendors within the family).
- **Attribution:** `entered_by` and `approved_by` showing different actual humans behind shared accounts. A mapping-table fix turning the legacy_erp violations into bot/human pairs. Proof that the violating resource named in the table is an artefact.
- **Emergency handling:** a valid Emergency Override event before approval in all flagged cases. A delegation memo covering Gita and Hugo. Justifications by a different person within 3 working days, apart from quarter-close batches that can be tied to the original authors. Weekend rosters matching Dan/Hugo activity. Amounts within delegated limits, and clinical or facility incident tickets.
- **Timing:** the fast subgroup confined to business-appropriate emergency contexts, with normal goods receipt and invoice match timing afterwards.
- **Controls:** Kim's role description showing she handles high-risk suppliers by design. A role matrix permitting Vendor Check and Bank Change by the same VendorMaster user, with compensating review.
- **Statistical:** after adjusting for manual_upload, vendor family, amount and emergency status, the pair effect disappears or is similar to peer pairs.

Internal Audit analysed the procurement-to-payment event log for segregation-of-duties exceptions, approval timing, resource handovers and source-system characteristics.
