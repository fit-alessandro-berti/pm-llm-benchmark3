# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The code defining `source_powl` is authoritative. In the table, `R`, `A`, `P`, `L`, `C`, `I`, `D`, `U`, and `Z` abbreviate Register Change, Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence, Legal Review, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Correct Defect, Release Change, and Archive Change. A witness is a complete visible trace; juxtaposition denotes sequence. `τ` is a silent transition. Negative witnesses are prohibited visible traces, except where a stated loss is the rejection of an allowed trace.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | Registration is first; assessment and preparation follow it. | `R A P C I U Z` | `A R P C I U Z` | Yes, by visible ordering. |
| S2 | Assessment and preparation have no order between them. | Both `R A P C I U Z` and `R P A C I U Z` | A conversion that rejects either allowed trace imposes a false order. | Their interleavings are observable; true concurrency itself is not established by traces alone. |
| S3 | Legal review is optional, at most once, and requires assessment. | Both `R A P C I U Z` and `R A L P C I U Z` | `R L A P C I U Z`, or `R A L L P C I U Z` | Yes, in visible traces; the identity of the silent skip is hidden. |
| S4 | Verification starts only after assessment and preparation. | `R P A C I U Z` | `R A C I P U Z` | Yes. |
| S5 | There is no ordering edge between the optional legal branch and verification. Legal review can precede, follow, or interleave with an iteration, subject to its other dependencies. | `R A L P C I U Z`, `R A P C L I U Z`, and `R A P C I L U Z` | A conversion rejecting any of these allowed traces introduces a dependency; `R A P C I U L Z` violates the separate release join. | Visible interleavings are observable; a concurrency relation is not proven from them. |
| S6 | Each iteration has `C` followed by `I` within the verification branch. An independent legal review may occur between them. | `R A P C L I U Z` | `R A P I C U Z` | Yes, after projecting onto loop activities. |
| S7 | The binary loop executes its body once or more; each redo `D` separates two complete body executions. | `R A P C I D C I U Z` | `R A P U Z`, `R A P D C I U Z`, or `R A P C I C I U Z` | Yes for the visible body and redo. |
| S8 | Release waits for the legal branch, including its silent skip if selected, and for the entire verification loop. | Both `R A P L C I U Z` and `R A P C I L U Z` | `R A P L U C I Z` or `R A P C I U L Z` | Visible violations are observable; completion of the silent skip is inferred from the model. |
| S9 | Archive follows release and is the last visible activity of a completed case; completion requires the final marking. | `R A P C I U Z` | `R A P C I Z U`, `R A P C I U`, or `R A P C I U Z L` | Visible order and missing archive are observable. Exact final-marking equality requires state inspection. |

These are partial-order constraints, not a claim that simultaneous execution must be physically observed. Legal review and verification are independent only once their respective prerequisites have been met. The loop's standard binary process-tree interpretation is do-body, then either exit or execute redo and repeat the body.

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary:** POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | Uses the accepting net to represent synchronization and overlapping dependencies without requiring a tree decomposition. | BPMN routing, silent behavior, markings, or independent paths could be altered or misread; a returned object proves nothing about equivalence. | An exception, missing marking, failed diagnostic, or semantic mismatch makes the affected artifact unusable or subject to quarantine. |
| POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN | `convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)`; `convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)`; `convert_to_bpmn(tree)` | A successful tree may provide a compact structured view. | The overlapping dependencies need not admit a faithful block-structured tree; a converter may fail or produce a semantically different decomposition. | Tree conversion raises, or any downstream validation detects loss. An exception is a conversion failure, not evidence that the source is invalid. |

Each generated BPMN is separately converted back with `convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` for diagnostic comparison. A round trip can expose loss, but agreement with the returned net is not by itself a proof about BPMN execution in every engine.

# 3. Conversion Code

Run this in the same Python session after executing the source definition from the question. It catches and reports exceptions rather than substituting an artifact. A failed visualization does not establish conversion failure. The dictionary `nets` contains every net actually returned, including any with missing markings, so the next section can distinguish a skipped check from a pass.

```python
from pathlib import Path
from collections import Counter
import traceback
import pm4py

out_dir = Path("conversion_audit_artifacts")
out_dir.mkdir(parents=True, exist_ok=True)

nets = {}
bpmns = {}
tree = None
net_from_powl = im_from_powl = fm_from_powl = None
bpmn_from_net = bpmn_from_tree = None


def report_exception(stage, exc):
    print(f"{stage}: FAILED: {type(exc).__name__}: {exc}")
    traceback.print_exc()


def save(stage, save_function, *args):
    try:
        save_function(*args)
        print(f"{stage}: saved")
    except Exception as exc:
        report_exception(stage, exc)


def register_net(name, result):
    net, im, fm = result
    nets[name] = (net, im, fm)
    print(f"{name}: net present={net is not None}; "
          f"initial marking present={im is not None}; "
          f"final marking present={fm is not None}")
    if net is not None:
        counts = Counter(t.label for t in net.transitions if t.label is not None)
        duplicates = {label: n for label, n in counts.items() if n > 1}
        print(f"{name}: duplicate visible transition labels: {duplicates}")
    return net, im, fm


save("POWL visualization", pm4py.save_vis_powl,
     source_powl, str(out_dir / "source_powl.png"))

try:
    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = register_net(
        "powl_net", pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl))
except Exception as exc:
    report_exception("POWL to Petri net", exc)

if net_from_powl is not None and im_from_powl is not None and fm_from_powl is not None:
    try:
        bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
            net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
        if bpmn_from_net is None:
            raise ValueError("converter returned no BPMN model")
        bpmns["bpmn_from_net"] = bpmn_from_net
    except Exception as exc:
        report_exception("Petri net to BPMN", exc)

    try:
        tree = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
            net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
        if tree is None:
            raise ValueError("converter returned no process tree")
        save("process-tree visualization", pm4py.save_vis_process_tree,
             tree, str(out_dir / "tree_from_net.png"))
    except Exception as exc:
        tree = None
        report_exception("Petri net to process tree", exc)

    if tree is not None:
        try:
            bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)
            if bpmn_from_tree is None:
                raise ValueError("converter returned no BPMN model")
            bpmns["bpmn_from_tree"] = bpmn_from_tree
        except Exception as exc:
            report_exception("process tree to BPMN", exc)
else:
    print("Net-to-BPMN and net-to-tree conversions skipped: net or marking missing")

for name, bpmn in bpmns.items():
    save(f"{name} visualization", pm4py.save_vis_bpmn,
         bpmn, str(out_dir / f"{name}.png"))
    try:
        register_net(f"{name}_roundtrip", pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn))
    except Exception as exc:
        report_exception(f"{name} to Petri net", exc)

# Run diagnostics on all returned nets, and preserve the actual outcomes.
diagnostics = {}
for name, (net, im, fm) in nets.items():
    result = {"workflow_net": None, "sound": None, "details": None}
    diagnostics[name] = result
    if net is None:
        print(f"{name}: diagnostics skipped: net missing")
        continue
    try:
        result["workflow_net"] = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
        print(f"{name}: workflow net = {result['workflow_net']}")
    except Exception as exc:
        report_exception(f"{name} workflow-net diagnostic", exc)
    if im is None or fm is None:
        print(f"{name}: soundness and Petri visualization skipped: marking missing")
        continue
    try:
        result["sound"], result["details"] = pm4py.check_soundness(
            net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True)
        print(f"{name}: sound = {result['sound']}; details = {result['details']}")
    except Exception as exc:
        report_exception(f"{name} soundness diagnostic", exc)
    save(f"{name} visualization", pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
         net, im, fm, str(out_dir / f"{name}.png"))
```

The existence of the net, tree, BPMN, or PNG is not a pass. In particular, missing markings are not replaced with guessed ones. Treat an exception, an indeterminate diagnostic, and a false result as distinct outcomes in the audit record.

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

This is a **prospective** audit, not a report of measured conversion results. No execution or equivalence result is assumed. The last two columns describe the evidence needed; update the statuses only when that evidence is obtained. The assessment cells use only the specified terms.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Registration first; assessment and preparation unordered | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Check both visible orders and forbidden early actions; traces alone do not establish causal independence. |
| Optional legal review after assessment, at most once | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Check traces with and without review, early review, and duplicates. A BPMN gateway or silent route may implement the skip without a visible task. |
| Verification waits for both prerequisites | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Test early `C` after only one prerequisite, plus allowed orders. |
| Legal branch and verification have no edge between them | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Check legal before `P`, between `C` and `I`, and after `I`; inspect causality as well as interleavings. A structured tree may reject or serialize this overlap. |
| Loop body `C` then `I`, with `D` as redo, at least once | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Check one and two iterations and invalid redo/body orders; bounded tests cannot cover all iterations. |
| Release joins both branches, archive follows, exact completion | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Check early release and exact final marking, including residual tokens. An end event in a drawing is not sufficient. |
| Visible activity identities and labels | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Source labels are unique, but generated nets may duplicate labeled transitions. Compare multiplicities and mapping, not just names; equal visible languages can hide identity changes. |
| Initial and final markings | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Inspect both markings for each net; BPMN does not expose markings in the same form, and conversion can infer different ones. Never assume non-null. |
| Concurrency versus interleaving and gateway routing | expected preserved | ambiguous | not tested | not tested | Trace equality is compatible with both concurrency and nondeterministic serialization. Inspect net causality and BPMN split/join semantics; layout and gateway shapes alone are not proof. |

A successful tree conversion should initially be marked `ambiguous`, not automatically `validated preserved`; an exception is `conversion failed`. Even complete equality of bounded trace sets supports only bounded visible-language preservation, not the full concurrency invariant. Silent optional behavior may be implemented differently without changing visible traces, so preservation of the original silent node itself is not required; preservation of its observable effect and synchronization is.

# 5. Round-Trip Validation Code

Run this after section 3. It explores reachable markings paired with visible prefixes. It hides only labels equal to `None`, records a trace only when the marking equals the complete final marking, and continues exploring from final markings to detect any additional behavior. The visited set prevents repeated silent cycles at the same marking and prefix. A visible-length bound also bounds source loop iterations because each iteration has visible actions; no separate `Correct Defect` cutoff is applied, which would hide malformed redo behavior. The state cap prevents nontermination on unbounded nets and is reported explicitly.

```python
from collections import deque
from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics

MAX_VISIBLE = 11  # includes both one- and two-iteration legal/skip cases
MAX_STATES = 200_000

R = "Register Change"
A = "Assess Risk"
P = "Prepare Evidence"
L = "Legal Review"
C = "Configure Trial"
I = "Inspect Trial"
D = "Correct Defect"
U = "Release Change"
Z = "Archive Change"

positive = {
    "skip legal, assessment first": (R, A, P, C, I, U, Z),
    "skip legal, preparation first": (R, P, A, C, I, U, Z),
    "legal before preparation": (R, A, L, P, C, I, U, Z),
    "legal between trial activities": (R, A, P, C, L, I, U, Z),
    "legal after verification": (R, A, P, C, I, L, U, Z),
    "two iterations": (R, A, P, C, I, D, C, I, U, Z),
    "two iterations with legal": (R, A, P, C, I, L, D, C, I, U, Z),
}
negative = {
    "assessment before registration": (A, R, P, C, I, U, Z),
    "legal before assessment": (R, L, A, P, C, I, U, Z),
    "duplicate legal": (R, A, L, L, P, C, I, U, Z),
    "verification before preparation": (R, A, C, I, P, U, Z),
    "verification before assessment": (R, P, C, I, A, U, Z),
    "inspect before configure": (R, A, P, I, C, U, Z),
    "no iteration": (R, A, P, U, Z),
    "redo before first iteration": (R, A, P, D, C, I, U, Z),
    "repeat without redo": (R, A, P, C, I, C, I, U, Z),
    "release before verification": (R, A, P, L, U, C, I, Z),
    "release before legal": (R, A, P, C, I, U, L, Z),
    "archive before release": (R, A, P, C, I, Z, U),
    "missing archive": (R, A, P, C, I, U),
    "activity after archive": (R, A, P, C, I, U, Z, L),
}


def marking_key(marking):
    # Place objects retain identity within this net; zero entries are immaterial.
    return frozenset((place, count) for place, count in marking.items() if count != 0)


def bounded_completed_traces(net, im, fm, max_visible, max_states):
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        raise ValueError("net and both markings are required")
    start = (im, ())
    queue = deque([start])
    seen = {(marking_key(im), ())}
    completed = set()
    truncated = False

    while queue:
        marking, trace = queue.popleft()
        if marking == fm:  # exact marking equality, not just inclusion of final tokens
            completed.add(trace)
        for transition in pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking):
            label = transition.label
            if label is not None and len(trace) >= max_visible:
                continue
            next_marking = semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)
            if next_marking is None:
                raise RuntimeError("enabled transition could not be fired")
            next_trace = trace if label is None else trace + (label,)
            key = (marking_key(next_marking), next_trace)
            if key in seen:
                continue
            if len(seen) >= max_states:
                truncated = True
                continue
            seen.add(key)
            queue.append((next_marking, next_trace))
    return completed, truncated, len(seen)


def check_witnesses(name, traces, truncated):
    for kind, witnesses in (("positive", positive), ("negative", negative)):
        for description, witness in witnesses.items():
            if len(witness) > MAX_VISIBLE:
                outcome = "NOT TESTED: witness exceeds bound"
            elif witness in traces:
                outcome = "PASS" if kind == "positive" else "FAIL"
            elif truncated:
                outcome = "UNKNOWN: exploration truncated"
            else:
                outcome = "FAIL" if kind == "positive" else "PASS"
            print(f"{name}: {kind} {description}: {outcome}")


def summarize_footprints(net, im, fm):
    fp = pm4py.discover_footprints(net, im, fm)
    if not isinstance(fp, dict):
        raise TypeError(f"unexpected footprint result: {type(fp).__name__}")
    # Compare the fields when provided; absence is not an empty relation.
    return {key: set(fp[key]) for key in ("activities", "sequence", "parallel")
            if key in fp}


results = {}
footprints = {}
for name, (net, im, fm) in nets.items():
    if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
        print(f"{name}: trace/footprint checks NOT TESTED: missing net or marking")
        continue
    try:
        traces, truncated, states = bounded_completed_traces(
            net, im, fm, MAX_VISIBLE, MAX_STATES)
        results[name] = (traces, truncated)
        print(f"{name}: {len(traces)} completed traces, {states} states, "
              f"truncated={truncated}, max visible length={MAX_VISIBLE}")
        check_witnesses(name, traces, truncated)
    except Exception as exc:
        report_exception(f"{name} bounded exploration", exc)
    try:
        footprints[name] = summarize_footprints(net, im, fm)
        print(f"{name}: footprint fields={sorted(footprints[name])}")
    except Exception as exc:
        report_exception(f"{name} footprint discovery", exc)

reference = results.get("powl_net")
for name in sorted(n for n in nets if n.endswith("_roundtrip")):
    candidate = results.get(name)
    if reference is None or candidate is None:
        print(f"{name}: bounded comparison NOT TESTED")
    else:
        source_traces, source_truncated = reference
        candidate_traces, candidate_truncated = candidate
        if source_truncated or candidate_truncated:
            print(f"{name}: bounded comparison INCONCLUSIVE: exploration truncated")
        else:
            missing = source_traces - candidate_traces
            added = candidate_traces - source_traces
            print(f"{name}: missing source traces={len(missing)}, "
                  f"added traces={len(added)}")
            for trace in sorted(missing):
                print("  missing:", trace)
            for trace in sorted(added):
                print("  added:", trace)
            print("  bounded visible languages equal:", not missing and not added)

    if "powl_net" in footprints and name in footprints:
        left, right = footprints["powl_net"], footprints[name]
        for field in ("activities", "sequence", "parallel"):
            if field in left and field in right:
                print(f"{name}: footprint {field} equal = {left[field] == right[field]}")
            else:
                print(f"{name}: footprint {field} NOT TESTED: field unavailable")

# Optional: reachability graphs can be very large. Enable deliberately, after
# inspecting soundness results and resource limits; no graph isomorphism is claimed.
RUN_REACHABILITY = False
if RUN_REACHABILITY:
    for name, (net, im, fm) in nets.items():
        if net is None or im is None or fm is None:
            print(f"{name}: reachability graph NOT TESTED: missing net or marking")
            continue
        if diagnostics.get(name, {}).get("sound") is not True:
            print(f"{name}: reachability graph NOT TESTED: soundness not established")
            continue
        try:
            graph = pm4py.convert_to_reachability_graph(net, im, fm)
            print(f"{name}: reachability graph: {len(graph.states)} states, "
                  f"{len(graph.transitions)} transitions")
        except Exception as exc:
            report_exception(f"{name} reachability graph", exc)
```

When neither exploration is truncated and no exception occurs, the comparison is exhaustive for **completed visible traces of length at most 11** in each explored net, including arbitrary finite silent paths that return to already visited states. It is not exhaustive over longer traces or infinite-state spaces; a state-cap hit makes absence and equality claims inconclusive. Positive traces found and negative traces found remain evidence even under truncation. Equality of footprint fields or graph sizes is weaker than behavioral equivalence. Soundness is checked in section 3; soundness of both nets does not imply they have the same behavior. The source net itself must pass the independent witnesses and structural review: equality with an incorrect source conversion would merely reproduce its error. Playout can supply further examples but random samples cannot establish absence or equivalence.

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A parallel gateway can look like permission for unrestricted parallel execution, while joins and prerequisites elsewhere constrain when each path is enabled. Conversely, a layout placing one task to the left of another may suggest an order that no flow or dependency imposes. Check the actual flow graph and execution semantics, especially the independent legal and verification branches.

Exclusive gateways can depict the legal decision, but the skip may be shown as an unlabeled connector or routing node. The drawing may therefore make review look mandatory or hide when the optional branch has completed. Loop arrows can obscure the required initial `C`–`I` body and the placement of `D` only between iterations. Silent Petri transitions may become gateways or invisible routing steps, whose visual shapes and placement do not prove that they synchronize correctly. In particular, verify that the release join waits for both branches, including the skip route.

Accompany the rendering with a short legend for XOR and parallel gateways, an explicit note that legal review can occur before, during, or after verification when enabled, the do/redo loop rule, and the release-join condition. Include representative permitted and forbidden traces and identify the source model and conversion version. These annotations explain the diagram; they do not repair an incorrect underlying model.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Reject or quarantine an artifact if any of the following occurs:

1. A required conversion raises an exception, returns no model, or yields missing initial or final markings for a net that must be tested.
2. Workflow-net or soundness diagnostics fail; quarantine inconclusive checks until investigated.
3. Either assessment/preparation order is excluded, or an additional dependency forbids an allowed legal/verification interleaving.
4. Verification can start before assessment and preparation both complete.
5. Release can occur before verification finishes or before the selected legal branch completes.
6. Legal review becomes mandatory, disappears, is allowed before assessment, or can occur more than once.
7. The loop permits zero iterations, reverses its body, omits or misplaces the redo between iterations, or excludes required repetitions.
8. Archive can precede release, be omitted from a completed trace, or be followed by another visible activity.
9. A purported completed run fails exact final-marking equality, including residual tokens, or a required completed trace cannot reach that marking.
10. Labels or activity identities are lost or conflated, or an added transition creates unauthorized visible behavior.
11. A supposedly validated comparison is truncated or fails to run and there is no other sufficient evidence; quarantine pending validation rather than treating it as a pass.
12. The BPMN rendering has unresolved gateway or routing ambiguity that prevents reviewers from verifying the intended behavior, or its actual execution semantics differ from the accepted model.

A failed tree conversion disqualifies the tree pipeline; it does not by itself disqualify the separate direct net-to-BPMN pipeline.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

Keep the source POWL as the authoritative stored model, including stable activity identifiers, labels, dependencies, optional branch, loop structure, and version metadata. Use a generated BPMN diagram for stakeholder visualization. The accepting Petri net, any process tree, BPMN, round-tripped nets, and images are derivative artifacts tied to a particular POWL revision, PM4Py version, and conversion configuration.

Each derivative should carry its conversion outcome, marking availability where applicable, workflow-net and soundness results for associated nets, witness outcomes, bounded trace comparison and its bounds or truncation, label/identity mapping, and a review of concurrency, gateways, and the release join. Record exceptions and untested checks explicitly. A passing bounded test is evidence with a stated scope, not a certificate of full equivalence.

Allow a process-tree intermediate only when conversion succeeds and the resulting behavior and relevant concurrency properties have sufficient independent validation. Do not require it as a canonical form or assume every POWL partial order is representable faithfully by a tree. Make future semantic edits to the POWL, regenerate derivatives, and rerun the audit; treat edits made in a BPMN tool as proposals to reconcile against the POWL rather than silently importing a round trip as the new authority.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every proposed check passes, it cannot establish:

- Equality of the complete, unbounded visible languages or correctness for arbitrarily many loop iterations.
- Preservation of true concurrency, causal independence, or branching structure merely from equality of finite interleavings or footprint summaries.
- That the net-to-BPMN conversion and the reverse converter do not share compensating errors, or that a particular BPMN engine executes the diagram with the same semantics.
- That silent routing, divergence, and intermediate deadlocks are identical in every execution merely because completed traces match within a bound.
- Preservation of information outside this source definition, such as timing, resources, data conditions, business rules, and activity identity beyond labels.
- That a stakeholder will interpret the layout and gateway notation as intended, or that the authoritative source itself captures the real-world process correctly.
