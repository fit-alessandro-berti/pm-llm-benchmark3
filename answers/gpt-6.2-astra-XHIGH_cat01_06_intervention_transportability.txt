The shared mechanism is a join. Holding service effort and competing work fixed, advancing an independently executable branch brings verification completion forward only to the extent that it shortens the critical path. Spare identity and document capacity, evidence ready at release, and a system that permits two owners all affect how a change in routing translates into real overlap. Receipt delay can absorb an entire identity check even if no document reviewer starts earlier. The graph uses verification finish for the time both checks are complete and waiting before assessment for subsequent queuing. Historical waiting metrics need their clock origins checked.

Selection nodes mark mechanisms whose inputs or operating rules may differ across sites. B's originals rule changes readiness even for scanned files. Its specialist pools, ownership lock, assessment capacity, statutory legal route and repeat workload can amplify or reverse the end-to-end gain. Case complexity modifies work content and the likelihood of repeats. The arrival profile and queue delays represent effects across cases sharing a pool, including untreated cases. Back-entry modifies observation of overlap.

At A, the rollout period also predicts staffing, API latency, dispatch, intake and recording. Conditioning on observed overlap would select on readiness and load while conditioning on a treatment mediator. A similar control-flow diagram identifies a plausible ordering change. It supplies neither an unconfounded A contrast nor evidence for B's legally constrained strata or its response to higher throughput.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

All changes are in hours, intervention minus usual B practice, for **all B arrivals**. Negative means faster. As a service-only example, using B's two median services as representative durations gives 3.4 + 8.1 = 11.5 h in sequence and 8.1 h concurrently, a 3.4 h saving. These figures describe one representative case. A population median requires the joint distribution of times. For a case already awaiting originals from registration, identity at 3.4 h and receipt at 18 h give the same earliest lawful document start at 18 h under either policy if identity was already complete. Starting a previously delayed receipt request alongside identity could instead save that case 3.4 h. Handoff savings or queue penalties can change either result. A's six-hour fall in reported waiting motivates testing shifts of several hours, without attributing that fall to parallelization.

For a reproducible sensitivity calculation, assume the faster 80% of B cases have baseline times uniform on [20, 58.4] h and the slower 20% uniform on [90, 130] h. This illustrative distribution gives median 44 and p90 110. Assume no-original shares of 45% and 10% in the two bands (38% overall), and first-pilot eligibility of 35% and 10% (30% overall). Neither the pilot share nor readiness is known. Within each band, assume subgroup membership independent of exact baseline time. Under the shifts below, the median stays inside the faster band and p90 inside the slower one, within the linear part of every contributing subgroup CDF. Thus shifting the subgroup distributions and inverting the mixture gives the subgroup-share-weighted shift in the corresponding band. This shortcut depends on these particular distributions. It would be invalid to average observed subgroup medians or p90s in general.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

At Register, record the applicable originals rule, file availability, identity-dependent document exceptions, complexity, and public-sector/high-value status. The first release cohort consists of lower-complexity cases with a complete digital file, no statutory originals, no legal referral and independently executable checks. Keep usual practice for other cases. Uncertain classifications go to an authorized reviewer. Re-evaluate legal triggers before decision if facts change.

For the first cohort, make Identity Check and Document Check independently claimable after registration. The identity reviewer verifies identity, the document reviewer checks the current evidence version, and the assessor accepts a case only after both signed results are available. Use approved child work items that permit concurrent access, protect field ownership and commit results against the current version. A single writer can consolidate the signed results. If supported ownership or version reconciliation fails, record the exception and use serial handling. Creating two placeholders or recording two completions later confers no entitlement to start either check.

In a subsequent originals cohort, open Identity Check and the request/tracking item for Originals Receipt after Register. Record physical receipt and match it to the claim. Document Check stays disabled until receipt satisfies the rule. If identity is still active at receipt, document work may then overlap it, subject to data readiness. The reviewer handling delivery records receipt, and the document reviewer signs the verification result. For non-original cases with missing evidence, wait until a usable file is available.

Dispatch each role's oldest ready task within existing statutory priority and deadline classes. Retain ordinary slots for aged work and supplement returns. Set each pool's active-task cap from measured productive hours, service demand and observed queue age during run-in. Admit an extra concurrent task only with an available role slot and when assessment and legal queue ages are inside their agreed limits. Count blocked receipt waits separately from active work. Throttle elective forks before delaying mandatory processing. Record cap values, releases, holds and overrides.

After Assessment, route every public-sector or high-value case to a legal reviewer. A material supplement invalidates the previous document result and assessment. Record Request Supplement, wait for adequate evidence (including any required originals), repeat Document Check and Assessment on the new version, and return to Legal. An administrative clarification can return directly to Legal when the prior findings remain valid and the reason is recorded. Legal can request further supplements until it has enough evidence to clear the case for final decision. For the other cases Assessment can feed the decision directly. Exactly one of Approve and Reject precedes Notify.

Record case, task and visit IDs, arm and policy version, baseline flags, evidence version, request and receipt, readiness, release, claim, actual start and finish, owner/role, lock conflict, join, queue entry and exit, supplement reason, decision and notification. Store occurrence and entry times separately on synchronized clocks with an audit trail for corrections. Link appeals to their decisions. Compute identity/document overlap from actual active intervals, excluding pauses, and distinguish it from identity work during receipt transit.

```text
R = Register, I = Identity Check, D = Document Check, A = Assessment
O = Originals Receipt if required, otherwise skip
R ≺ I, R ≺ O, O ≺ D, I ≺ A, D ≺ A
I and O are unordered. I and D are unordered once D's readiness guard holds.
A ≺ L ≺ (Approve XOR Reject) ≺ Notify
L = skip if exempt, else LOOP(Legal Review, redo)
redo = Request Supplement → Supplement Received →
       (skip if prior checks remain valid, else Document Check → Assessment)
LOOP executes Legal Review first and again after every redo.
```

The relation permits serial schedules as well as overlaps. Eligibility, staffing, evidence guards and admission limits determine which schedule is available on a given case.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    OperatorPOWL, SilentTransition, StrictPartialOrder, Transition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


def ordered(nodes, edges):
    """Set the strict (irreflexive, transitively closed) precedence relation."""
    order = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)
    for earlier, later in edges:
        order.order.add_edge(earlier, later)
    order.order.add_transitive_edges()
    return order


def sequence(*nodes):
    return ordered(list(nodes), zip(nodes, nodes[1:]))


def xor(*nodes):
    return OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=list(nodes))


a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register','Identity Check','Document Check','Assessment',"
    "X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')"
)
a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register',+('Identity Check','Document Check'),'Assessment',"
    "X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')"
)
net_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(a_before)
net_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(a_after)

register = Transition(label="Register")
identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")
receipt = xor(SilentTransition(), Transition(label="Originals Receipt"))
document = Transition(label="Document Check")
assess = Transition(label="Assessment")

# Separate transition instances with repeated labels identify later visits.
revalidate = sequence(Transition(label="Document Check"), Transition(label="Assessment"))
redo = sequence(
    Transition(label="Request Supplement"),
    Transition(label="Supplement Received"),
    xor(SilentTransition(), revalidate),
)
# LOOP(do, redo) executes do at least once and returns to do after each redo.
legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[Transition(label="Legal Review"), redo]
)
legal_route = xor(SilentTransition(), legal_loop)
decision = xor(Transition(label="Approve"), Transition(label="Reject"))
notify = Transition(label="Notify")

b_candidate = ordered(
    [register, identity, receipt, document, assess, legal_route, decision, notify],
    [(register, identity), (register, receipt), (receipt, document),
     (identity, assess), (document, assess), (assess, legal_route),
     (legal_route, decision), (decision, notify)],
)
net_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(b_candidate)

for name, (net, initial, final) in [
    ("A before", net_before), ("A after", net_after), ("B candidate", net_b)
]:
    wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    sound, _ = pm4py.check_soundness(net, initial, final)
    print(f"{name}: workflow net={wf}, sound={sound}")
    assert wf and sound, name

# Illustrative label sequences. Log order alone does not establish concurrency.
sample = pm4py.play_out(*net_b, parameters={"noTraces": 3})
for trace in sample[:3]:
    print([event["concept:name"] for event in trace])

# XOR choices need external guards for originals, legal referral and revalidation.
# An unordered pair permits concurrent execution. Staffing, WIP, actual start
# times, owner locks, receipt delays and service durations need execution data
# and operational controls beyond the accepting net.
```

## 9. Recommendation

conditional go

Pilot the lawful removal of an unnecessary predecessor. Start with lower-complexity claims whose complete digital evidence is sufficient at registration, whose document check is independent of the identity result, and whose initial classification requires no Legal Review. These cases give B the clearest opportunity to learn whether two specialists can actually work concurrently. Use one accountable owner for each verification result and require the assessor to see both current, signed versions. Preserve the statutory receipt gate and any legal clearance required at decision.

A's nine-hour median improvement occurred alongside added staffing, faster queries, queue huddles and simpler intake. It gives management no defensible hours-saved target for B. On a ready B case with representative service times, hiding identity work could save about three hours. The whole-site median gain can be much smaller. A burst of assessment arrivals or repeated legal referrals can make slow cases take longer even as the mean falls. Simultaneously opening executable document tasks for every claim would also expose legally gated cases to premature work.

Expand one stratum at a time if audited starts confirm execution, the eligible median improves by a practically useful amount (proposed target 2 h), and uncertainty intervals support improvement while excluding prespecified unacceptable tail and quality deterioration. Set harm margins before enrollment, for example +10 h in p90, +2 percentage points in rework and +1 point in mature appeals. If rare harms remain too imprecise, continue follow-up or enrollment. Check the backlog of ineligible cases before scaling. Test identity alongside originals collection next, with document release tied to actual receipt. Add legal cases only when measured legal capacity can absorb both first referrals and returns.

Modify dispatch or reduce admission when verification finishes earlier but assessment queues consume the saving. Repair ownership or logging when there is no credible change in real overlap. Suspend immediately for a statutory bypass or lost finding. Abandon the affected version if safe concurrent ownership proves infeasible, or if a sufficiently powered, faithfully executed comparison rules out a worthwhile gain or shows persistent tail or quality harm.

## 10. Assumption register

1. Intake flags accurately identify legal and document prerequisites — eligibility and lawful release depend on the facts known at registration — independently audit flags against the case file and recheck at decision — route ambiguous cases to manual review and narrow the cohort if classification fails.
2. The initial digital subgroup has complete evidence and informationally independent checks — either reviewer must be able to finish without the other's result — audit field dependencies, blocked starts and corrections in the run-in — add the missing predecessor for affected cases and revise the eligible share.
3. Approved work items support independent owners and consistent result versions — nominal release must permit safe work in parallel — exercise simultaneous claims, stale writes and result consolidation, then audit production exceptions — fall back to serial handling and suspend that version if writes are unsafe.
4. Occurrence times measure real activity comparably in both arms — the first-stage and queue decomposition need credible clocks — compare instrumented events with timed source actions and sampled observation — repair instrumentation and withhold mechanism attribution until validated.
5. There is enough role-specific headroom under tested admission limits — earlier joins help only if verification, assessment and legal pools can serve the changed flow — measure arrival/service demand including returns, active WIP and queue age across loads — throttle or redesign dispatch and re-estimate the policy effect.
6. The synthetic time bands, subgroup weights and time shifts span plausible planning cases — numerical scenarios need a joint timing distribution absent from the summaries — fit empirical distributions to B intake cohorts, including open claims, and repeat the mixture calculations — replace the scenario estimates and resize the pilot.
7. Paired randomization at the chosen period length gives a meaningful policy contrast — lingering prior-arm work or arm-linked staffing changes could distort the estimate — audit allocation, co-interventions, queue carryover and sensitivity to lagged exposure — lengthen periods or redesign the comparison before extrapolation.
8. Pilot cohorts have sufficient follow-up and overlap with intended rollout strata and loads — quality comparisons and standardization require mature outcomes and supported populations — check unresolved cases, appeal linkage, joint stratum sizes and saturation against the rollout target — extend follow-up or enroll missing strata and restrict conclusions to observed support.
