# 1. Equivalence Notions

All comparisons below treat a **complete visible trace** as a finite sequence of activity labels from a run that starts in the initial state and ends in the unique final state. Silent `tau` (and any silent routing transitions introduced by Petri-net conversion) is hidden: it does not appear in the sequence and does not consume the visible-length budget. Duplicate labels are **distinct occurrences distinguished by position in the sequence**, not by object identity and not by bag equality. `⟨Analyze, Check, Analyze⟩` is different from `⟨Analyze, Analyze, Check⟩`; both have the same label multiset.

**Visible trace equivalence after hiding `tau`.**
Let \(L_v(M)\) be the set of complete sequences of non-silent labels generated by model \(M\). Models are equivalent iff \(L_v(A)=L_v(B)\). Order, repetition count, and position of every visible label matter. Optional `Approve Revision` is observed only by its presence or absence; the silent alternative is invisible.

**Bounded visible-language equivalence under P3.**
Let \(L_v^{\le}(M)\) be the subset of \(L_v(M)\) in which the sequence has length at most 12 and contains at most three `Analyze Revision` events. P3-equivalence is \(L_v^{\le}(A)=L_v^{\le}(B)\). Agreement on this finite set is **evidence**, not a proof that \(L_v(A)=L_v(B)\). Disagreement on this set **is** a proof of unbounded inequivalence.

**Business-relevant equivalence under P1.**
A complete visible trace is P1-compliant iff all of the following hold:

1. it starts with `Open Case` and ends with `Close Case`;
2. analyses come in **attempts** separated by `Rework Case`: if there are \(n\ge 1\) occurrences of `Analyze Revision`, there are exactly \(n-1\) occurrences of `Rework Case`, and the \(i\)-th rework lies after attempt \(i\) and before attempt \(i+1\);
3. **each** analysis attempt contains exactly one `Check Revision`, and that check occurs **before** the next `Rework Case` (or before `Close Case` on the last attempt);
4. `Approve Revision` may occur zero or one time per attempt, and remains a visible, countable event.

Models are P1-equivalent iff they generate the same set of P1-compliant traces (and no non-compliant ones). Concurrency of `Analyze Revision` and `Check Revision` **inside** an attempt is allowed by P1 but not required by the bullets; pairing and placement of the check **are** required.

**Abstracted dashboard equivalence under P2.**
The P2 observation of a complete trace \(\sigma\) is the tuple

\[
\Phi(\sigma)=\bigl(\text{first}=\texttt{Open Case},\;\text{last}=\texttt{Close Case},\;\#\texttt{Analyze Revision},\;\mathbf{1}[\#\texttt{Check Revision}\ge 1],\;\mathbf{1}[\#\texttt{Rework Case}\ge 1]\bigr).
\]

`Approve Revision`, `tau`, check multiplicity beyond “at least one”, and every interleaving of `Analyze Revision` with `Check Revision` are discarded. Models are P2-equivalent iff \(\Phi(L_v(A))=\Phi(L_v(B))\). This is equivalence of **abstractions**, not of languages.

# 2. Structural Comparison

Loop semantics used throughout: `*(D, R)` executes `D` once; then either exits, or executes `R` then `D` again; repeat.

| Feature | Model A | Model B | Semantic consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Loop scope | Loop **contains** the AND of `Analyze Revision` and `Check Revision`, then optional `Approve Revision`. Redo is `Rework Case`. | Loop contains only `Analyze Revision` then optional `Approve Revision`. Redo is `Rework Case`. `Check Revision` sits **outside** the loop, in parallel with the whole loop. | A pairs a fresh check with every analysis attempt. B performs exactly one check against the entire revision history. |
| Min / max `Check Revision` | Minimum 1 (first `D` is mandatory). Maximum unbounded: one check per loop iteration. | Minimum 1, maximum 1. | Any complete A-trace with two or more analyses has two or more checks and cannot be a B-trace. |
| Placement of `Check Revision` | Concurrent only with the `Analyze Revision` of the **same** iteration. Both finish before that iteration’s optional approve. A check cannot move across `Rework Case` into another attempt, and cannot occur after its attempt’s approve or after `Close Case`. | Concurrent with **every** loop event. Legal positions include before the first analyze, between analyze and approve, after approve, between rework and the next analyze, and after the last loop event. | B admits `Approve` then `Check`, and admits a single check anywhere along a multi-attempt history. A forbids both. |
| Optional approval | One XOR(`Approve Revision`, `tau`) **per iteration**, independently. At most one approve per attempt; total \(\#\texttt{Approve}\le\#\texttt{Analyze}\). | Same XOR **per iteration** of the analyze-loop. Same cardinality bound. | Optional approve is **not** the source of inequivalence. Even if approve were forced, pairing and check cardinality would still differ. |
| Concurrency | Only `Analyze Revision ∥ Check Revision` inside each attempt. Attempts are sequential. Approve is sequential after the AND-join of that attempt. | `Check Revision ∥` the entire analyze/approve/rework loop. Inside the loop, analyze, optional approve, and rework are totally ordered. | B can insert its only check into any sequential slot of the loop. A cannot export a check out of its attempt. |
| Join before `Close Case` | Sequential: the loop must exit (last `D` finished, redo not taken). Then `Close Case`. | AND-join: the single `Check Revision` **and** the loop must both complete. Then `Close Case`. | B can delay its check until after the last approve and still close. A must have finished the last check **before** that last approve (if present) and before close. |
| Check vs analyze cardinality | \(\#\texttt{Check}=\#\texttt{Analyze}\ge 1\), \(\#\texttt{Rework}=\#\texttt{Analyze}-1\). | \(\#\texttt{Check}=1\), \(\#\texttt{Analyze}\ge 1\), \(\#\texttt{Rework}=\#\texttt{Analyze}-1\). | Cardinality alone separates the languages as soon as a second analysis appears. |

# 3. Verdicts

| View | Verdict | Confidence | Decisive reason | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 (operational compliance) | `not equivalent` | High | P1 requires a dedicated `Check Revision` inside every analysis attempt, before the next `Rework Case` or `Close Case`. A enforces that pairing. B produces exactly one check and can leave later attempts unchecked. | If a reader quietly drops the “own check per analysis” rule, the verdict would collapse toward P2. That is not the stated P1 rule. |
| P2 (executive dashboard) | `equivalent only under stated abstraction` | High | \(\Phi(L_v(A))=\Phi(L_v(B))=\{(\texttt{Open},\texttt{Close},n,\top,n\ge 2)\mid n\ge 1\}\). Both models always check at least once, can realize every analyze count \(n\ge 1\), and rework occurs iff \(n\ge 2\). | If the dashboard later counts checks, shows approve counts, or shows analyze/check order, equivalence disappears immediately. |
| P3 (bounded testing) | `not equivalent` | High | Already inside the bound one finds A-only traces with two checks and B-only traces with one check after approve or across two analyses. So \(L_v^{\le}(A)\neq L_v^{\le}(B)\). | The bound **excludes** A’s 13-event traces (three analyses, three approves). That exclusion is not needed for the negative verdict. Bounded agreement would not have proved unbounded equality. |
| Visible traces after hiding `tau` (unbounded) | `not equivalent` | High | Neither language includes the other. See §4. | Standard process-tree loop and AND semantics are assumed, as specified. |

A single simplification cannot be trace-equivalent to both originals. It can preserve P1, or preserve P2, not both originals under P1.

# 4. Counterexample Traces

Abbreviations used only in the “First decisive semantic difference” column: `O` = Open Case, `A` = Analyze Revision, `C` = Check Revision, `P` = Approve Revision, `R` = Rework Case, `K` = Close Case. Traces in the table are written with full labels.

Neither inclusion holds, so witnesses are given in both directions. T1 and T2 have two analysis attempts.

| Trace ID | Trace | Model A | Model B | First decisive semantic difference | Relevant view |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Close Case | yes | no | Two checks, one per attempt, as P1 requires. B cannot emit a second `Check Revision`. | P1, P3, visible traces |
| T2 | Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Approve Revision, Rework Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Approve Revision, Close Case | yes | no | Second attempt again has its own check. Approves sit after each attempt’s AND-join, never after a foreign check. | P1, P3 |
| T3 | Open Case, Analyze Revision, Approve Revision, Check Revision, Close Case | no | yes | In A the check is joined **before** the optional approve, so `P` cannot precede `C` in the same (and only) attempt. | P1, P3 |
| T4 | Open Case, Analyze Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Close Case | no | yes | Two analyses, one check, and the second attempt is unchecked until after rework — forbidden by P1 and by A’s loop body. | P1, P3 |
| T5 | Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Close Case | no | yes | First attempt looks A-like, but the second analysis has no check before close. | P1, P3 |
| T6 | Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Close Case | yes | yes | Shared one-attempt, no-approve trace. Not a separator. | P2 (both map to the same dashboard tuple) |

**Inclusion claim (formal).**
Write \(D_A = \mathrm{shuffle}(\texttt{Analyze},\texttt{Check})\cdot(\texttt{Approve}\mid\varepsilon)\). Then

\[
L_v(A)=\{\texttt{Open}\}\cdot D_A\cdot(\{\texttt{Rework}\}\cdot D_A)^*\cdot\{\texttt{Close}\}.
\]

Write \(D_B=(\texttt{Analyze}\cdot(\texttt{Approve}\mid\varepsilon))\cdot(\texttt{Rework}\cdot\texttt{Analyze}\cdot(\texttt{Approve}\mid\varepsilon))^*\). Then

\[
L_v(B)=\{\texttt{Open}\}\cdot\mathrm{shuffle}(\{\texttt{Check}\},\,D_B)\cdot\{\texttt{Close}\}.
\]

Hence every A-trace satisfies \(\#C=\#A\), every B-trace satisfies \(\#C=1\). For \(\#A\ge 2\) the two sets are disjoint. For \(\#A=1\), \(L_v(A)\) is the four traces \(\{OACK, OCAK, OACPK, OCAPK\}\) while \(L_v(B)\) is those four plus \(OAPCK\). Therefore \(L_v(A)\not\subseteq L_v(B)\) and \(L_v(B)\not\subseteq L_v(A)\). The same four-plus-one picture already lives inside the P3 bound.

**How optional `Approve Revision` and hidden `tau` affect the verdict.**
`X('Approve Revision', tau)` only makes approve skippable **per iteration**. After hiding `tau`, that is exactly the presence or absence of the label `Approve Revision`. It does **not** create extra checks, and it does not move a check across A’s AND-join. The models already disagree on traces that contain no approve at all (T1 vs T4). Forcing an approve on every iteration would still leave T2 vs T3-style separators. Silent `tau` is irrelevant to visible-trace, P1, and P3 comparison once it is hidden; it matters only in that testers must not treat a missing approve as an incomplete run.

Under P2, T1 and T4 **collapse to the same dashboard record** `(Open, Close, 2 analyses, check occurred, rework occurred)`. That is why P2 can call the models equivalent while P1 and P3 cannot.

# 5. Bounded Comparison Code

The script below is exhaustive inside the P3 bound: it breadth-first explores the reachability graph, fires silent transitions without charging visible length, blocks silent cycles by the pair `(marking, visible trace)`, and accepts only when the marking equals the final marking. It is **not** `play_out`. A `play_out` branch is included only to state why that API is non-exhaustive.

```python
"""P3 bounded visible-language comparison of Models A and B.

Exhaustive BFS over markings. Not pm4py.play_out.
"""
from collections import deque

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics as pn_semantics


TREE_A = (
    "->('Open Case', "
    "*(->(+('Analyze Revision', 'Check Revision'), "
    "X('Approve Revision', tau)), 'Rework Case'), "
    "'Close Case')"
)
TREE_B = (
    "->('Open Case', "
    "+('Check Revision', "
    "*(->('Analyze Revision', X('Approve Revision', tau)), "
    "'Rework Case')), "
    "'Close Case')"
)

MAX_VISIBLE = 12
MAX_ANALYZE = 3
ANALYZE = "Analyze Revision"


def marking_key(marking):
    return tuple(sorted((p.name, marking[p]) for p in marking))


def fire(transition, net, marking):
    return pn_semantics.execute(transition, net, marking)


def bounded_language(net, im, fm, max_visible=MAX_VISIBLE, max_analyze=MAX_ANALYZE):
    """Exhaustive complete visible traces under P3 bounds.

    State = (marking, visible-trace). Silent cycles are cut by revisiting
    the same marking with the same visible trace. Visible length and
    Analyze Revision count are hard prunes.
    """
    accepted = set()
    seen = set()
    start = (marking_key(im), tuple())
    seen.add(start)
    queue = deque([(im, tuple())])

    while queue:
        marking, trace = queue.popleft()
        n_analyze = trace.count(ANALYZE)
        if marking == fm:
            if len(trace) <= max_visible and n_analyze <= max_analyze:
                accepted.add(trace)
            continue

        enabled = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
        for transition in enabled:
            label = transition.label
            silent = label is None or label == "tau"
            if silent:
                new_trace = trace
                new_n = n_analyze
            else:
                new_trace = trace + (label,)
                new_n = n_analyze + (1 if label == ANALYZE else 0)
            if len(new_trace) > max_visible or new_n > max_analyze:
                continue
            new_marking = fire(transition, net, marking)
            key = (marking_key(new_marking), new_trace)
            if key in seen:
                continue
            seen.add(key)
            queue.append((new_marking, new_trace))
    return accepted


def fmt(trace):
    return " → ".join(trace) if trace else "⟨ε⟩"


def main():
    tree_a = pm4py.parse_process_tree(TREE_A)
    tree_b = pm4py.parse_process_tree(TREE_B)
    net_a, im_a, fm_a = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a)
    net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_b)

    print("=== soundness ===")
    sound_a = pm4py.check_soundness(net_a, im_a, fm_a, print_diagnostics=True)
    sound_b = pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b, print_diagnostics=True)
    print("A sound:", sound_a)
    print("B sound:", sound_b)

    print("\n=== exhaustive P3 exploration (BFS over markings) ===")
    print("This enumeration is exhaustive for complete traces with")
    print(f"visible length ≤ {MAX_VISIBLE} and #{ANALYZE} ≤ {MAX_ANALYZE}.")
    print("It is NOT random playout and NOT pm4py.play_out.")

    lang_a = bounded_language(net_a, im_a, fm_a)
    lang_b = bounded_language(net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    only_a = sorted(lang_a - lang_b)
    only_b = sorted(lang_b - lang_a)
    both = sorted(lang_a & lang_b)

    print(f"|L_v^≤(A)| = {len(lang_a)}")
    print(f"|L_v^≤(B)| = {len(lang_b)}")
    print(f"|A − B|    = {len(only_a)}")
    print(f"|B − A|    = {len(only_b)}")
    print(f"|A ∩ B|    = {len(both)}")
    print("P3 equivalent:", lang_a == lang_b)

    print("\n--- traces unique to A (first 20) ---")
    for tr in only_a[:20]:
        print(fmt(tr))
    print("\n--- traces unique to B (first 20) ---")
    for tr in only_b[:20]:
        print(fmt(tr))
    print("\n--- traces in both (all) ---")
    for tr in both:
        print(fmt(tr))

    print("\n=== non-exhaustive contrast: pm4py.play_out ===")
    print("play_out samples or unfolds with an implicit cap. It can miss")
    print("legal traces and cannot prove equality. It is reported only")
    print("as a negative control, not as the P3 decision procedure.")
    try:
        log_a = pm4py.play_out(tree_a)
        log_b = pm4py.play_out(tree_b)
        pl_a = {tuple(e["concept:name"] for e in c) for c in log_a}
        pl_b = {tuple(e["concept:name"] for e in c) for c in log_b}
        print("play_out |A|, |B|, equal:", len(pl_a), len(pl_b), pl_a == pl_b)
    except Exception as exc:
        print("play_out skipped:", type(exc).__name__, exc)


if __name__ == "__main__":
    main()
```

**What the exhaustive analysis predicts (derived by unfolding the trees, not by sampling).**
Shared P3 traces are exactly the four one-attempt traces `{OACK, OCAK, OACPK, OCAPK}`. Every other P3-legal A-trace has \(\#C=\#A\in\{2,3\}\) and is A-only. Every other P3-legal B-trace has \(\#C=1\) (including `OAPCK` and every two- and three-analyze interleaving of the single check) and is B-only. A’s three-analyze, three-approve traces have 13 visible events and are excluded by P3; B’s corresponding traces have 11 visible events and remain inside the bound. That asymmetry is another bounded separator, not a reason to believe the unbound languages agree.

# 6. Stochastic and Footprint Comparison

These three APIs can add **circumstantial** evidence. None of them can promote P2-style agreement into visible-trace equivalence.

- `pm4py.discover_footprints(...)` builds a relation matrix (sequence, XOR, parallel, or no direct relation) over activity pairs. It will almost certainly **differ** on pairs involving `Check Revision`: in A, `Approve Revision` is never directly followed by `Check Revision` (after an approve the next visible event is `Rework Case` or `Close Case`); in B that directly-follows pair is legal. Parallelism of `Check Revision` with `Rework Case` is present in B and absent in A. Equal footprints would still be insufficient: footprints forget counts, pairing, and long-range “one check per attempt” constraints. Many non-equivalent languages share a footprint.
- `pm4py.get_stochastic_language(...)` needs a probability on traces. The models as given are **not** stochastic. Any EMD number therefore depends on a declared extra assumption.
- `pm4py.compute_emd(...)` then measures Earth Mover’s Distance between those assumed distributions. A strictly positive EMD supports “not the same distribution.” A low or even zero EMD on a finite support does **not** prove \(L_v(A)=L_v(B)\): mass can concentrate on the four shared traces, rare separators can be under-sampled, and two different languages can be close in move distance.

Declared assumption used in the code: **uniform probability over each model’s P3-bounded complete visible traces**. That is a testing measure, not an operational duration or frequency model.

```python
import pm4py


def uniform_language(traces):
    traces = list(traces)
    if not traces:
        return {}
    p = 1.0 / len(traces)
    return {tr: p for tr in traces}


def footprint_and_emd(tree_a, tree_b, lang_a, lang_b):
    print("=== footprints from the process trees ===")
    try:
        fp_a = pm4py.discover_footprints(tree_a)
        fp_b = pm4py.discover_footprints(tree_b)
        print("A footprints type:", type(fp_a))
        print("B footprints type:", type(fp_b))
        print("tree-footprint objects equal:", fp_a == fp_b)
    except Exception as exc:
        print("tree footprints unavailable:", type(exc).__name__, exc)

    def traces_to_log(traces):
        return [
            [{"concept:name": a} for a in tr]
            for tr in traces
        ]

    print("=== footprints from P3 languages (as logs) ===")
    fp_la = pm4py.discover_footprints(traces_to_log(lang_a))
    fp_lb = pm4py.discover_footprints(traces_to_log(lang_b))
    print("log-footprint objects equal:", fp_la == fp_lb)

    print("=== EMD under declared uniform-P3 measure ===")
    print("Assumption: each P3-complete trace of a model has equal mass.")
    print("This is NOT a fitted sojourn or frequency model.")
    sla = uniform_language(lang_a)
    slb = uniform_language(lang_b)
    try:
        sl_from_a = pm4py.get_stochastic_language(traces_to_log(lang_a))
        sl_from_b = pm4py.get_stochastic_language(traces_to_log(lang_b))
        print("pm4py stochastic-language keys A/B:", len(sl_from_a), len(sl_from_b))
        emd_api = pm4py.compute_emd(sl_from_a, sl_from_b)
        print("EMD(pm4py languages) =", emd_api)
    except Exception as exc:
        print("pm4py stochastic/EMD path:", type(exc).__name__, exc)
    try:
        emd_uniform = pm4py.compute_emd(sla, slb)
        print("EMD(uniform P3) =", emd_uniform)
    except Exception as exc:
        print("uniform EMD path:", type(exc).__name__, exc)
```

Equal footprints cannot see that A pairs the \(i\)-th check with the \(i\)-th analyze. A small EMD can be produced by giving almost all mass to `{OACK, OCAK, OACPK, OCAPK}` while the separators that decide P1 still exist. Stochastic comparison is therefore subordinate to the explicit counterexamples in §4.

# 7. Complexity Comparison

Tree metrics below are counted directly from the given expressions. Petri-net simplicity is **not** invented; it must be read off `pm4py.simplicity_petri_net` after conversion.

**Model A — 12 nodes, depth 5.**
Root `->` (3 children) → `Open Case`, `*`, `Close Case`. Under `*`: inner `->` and `Rework Case`. Under inner `->`: `+` and `X`. Leaves under those: `Analyze Revision`, `Check Revision`, `Approve Revision`, `tau`. Visible leaves = 6. Silent leaves = 1. Duplicate visible labels = 0.

**Model B — 12 nodes, depth 6.**
Root `->` → `Open Case`, `+`, `Close Case`. Under `+`: `Check Revision` and `*`. Under `*`: inner `->` and `Rework Case`. Under inner `->`: `Analyze Revision` and `X`. Under `X`: `Approve Revision`, `tau`. Visible leaves = 6. Silent leaves = 1. Duplicate visible labels = 0. Deeper than A because the check was lifted next to the loop, adding one operator level above the analyze/approve spine.

Both converted nets are block-structured and should be sound (the §5 script prints the checker). A uses a small AND (two activities) **inside** the loop; B uses a large AND (one activity ∥ an unbounded loop) **outside** the loop. B’s AND-split therefore stays open for the whole revision history, which is the semantic cost of the extra depth.

```python
from collections import Counter


def walk(tree, depth=1):
    yield tree, depth
    for child in getattr(tree, "children", None) or []:
        yield from walk(child, depth + 1)


def tree_metrics(tree, name):
    nodes = list(walk(tree))
    leaves = [(n, d) for n, d in nodes if not n.children]
    visible = [n.label for n, _ in leaves if n.label not in (None, "tau")]
    silent = [n for n, _ in leaves if n.label in (None, "tau")]
    ops = [n.operator for n, _ in nodes if n.operator is not None]
    counts = Counter(visible)
    print(f"=== {name} ===")
    print("nodes", len(nodes))
    print("depth", max(d for _, d in nodes))
    print("visible leaves", len(visible), visible)
    print("silent leaves", len(silent))
    print("duplicate-label count", sum(c - 1 for c in counts.values() if c > 1))
    print("operators", Counter(str(o) for o in ops))


def petri_metrics(net, im, fm, name):
    print(f"=== {name} Petri net ===")
    print("places", len(net.places))
    print("transitions", len(net.transitions))
    print("silent transitions", sum(1 for t in net.transitions if t.label in (None, "tau")))
    print("visible transitions", sum(1 for t in net.transitions if t.label not in (None, "tau")))
    print("arcs", len(net.arcs))
    print("simplicity_petri_net", pm4py.simplicity_petri_net(net, im, fm))


# after parsing / converting in §5:

# tree_metrics(tree_b, "B")
# petri_metrics(net_a, im_a, fm_a, "A")
# petri_metrics(net_b, im_b, fm_b, "B")
```

Exact values derived in this answer: **A = 12 nodes, depth 5, 6 visible leaves, 1 silent leaf, 0 duplicate labels**. **B = 12 nodes, depth 6, 6 visible leaves, 1 silent leaf, 0 duplicate labels**. Petri-net place/transition/arc counts and `simplicity_petri_net` are environment-dependent (silent routing transitions differ by converter version) and are left to the snippet.

# 8. Simplified Model

**Target semantics: P1**, with a deliberate extra sequentialization. P2 is too coarse to justify a control-flow model (it already calls A and B the same). P3 is a test bound, not a business purpose. P1 is the view in which the original design decision — check **inside** the revision loop vs check **beside** it — is actually a compliance question.

A single compact tree cannot preserve P1 for both originals, because the originals are not P1-equivalent. The simplification therefore preserves **A’s pairing rule**, which is the P1 rule, and drops the AND that P1 never required.

**Compact process tree (11 nodes, depth 5, no parallel operator):**

```text
->(
  'Open Case',
  *(
    ->(
      'Analyze Revision',
      'Check Revision',
      X('Approve Revision', tau)
    ),
    'Rework Case'
  ),
  'Close Case'
)
```

Compared with A (12 nodes, one AND) this removes the parallel operator and one node. Compared with B (12 nodes, depth 6, AND around the whole loop) it is both smaller and semantically aligned with per-attempt checking. It is simpler than both originals on operator set and at least as simple on node count.

```python
import pm4py

simplified_model = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Open Case', "
    "*(->('Analyze Revision', 'Check Revision', "
    "X('Approve Revision', tau)), 'Rework Case'), "
    "'Close Case')"
)
```

| Property | Status vs A | Status vs B | Status vs P1 / P2 / P3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open first, Close last | exact | exact | exact for all three views |
| Rework separates analysis attempts; \(\#R=\#A-1\) | exact | exact | exact for P1 |
| One `Check Revision` per analysis, before next rework or close | exact | **lost** (B has a single floating check) | exact for P1 |
| `Analyze ∥ Check` inside an attempt | **lost** (now `Analyze` then `Check`) | not a B property | P1 allows this weakening |
| Optional per-attempt `Approve Revision` after the check | exact (and now also after analyze, by sequence) | weakened: approve can no longer be concurrent with a late check | exact for P1’s “optional, still visible” clause |
| Check after approve on the same attempt (`OAPCK`) | already illegal in A; still illegal | **lost** as a legal B behavior | correctly rejected by P1 |
| Dashboard tuple \(\Phi\) | same image as A | same image as B | **exact** under P2 abstraction |
| Full visible language | **weakened** to a strict subset of \(L_v(A)\) | not comparable by inclusion with \(L_v(B)\) | P3 languages differ from both originals |
| Unbounded check count | kept (grows with analyses) | **lost** relative to B’s “check once” reading | required by P1, hidden by P2 |

**Regression traces** (must be accepted by `simplified_model`):

1. Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Close Case
2. Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Approve Revision, Close Case
3. Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Approve Revision, Close Case

**Negative traces** (must be rejected):

1. Open Case, Analyze Revision, Rework Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, Close Case — second attempt unchecked (legal in B, illegal under P1).
2. Open Case, Analyze Revision, Close Case — analysis without its check.
3. (extra witness of the deliberate weakening) Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Close Case — legal in A, illegal in the sequential simplification.

If a stakeholder insists that analyze and check **must** run concurrently, then Model A is already the near-minimal P1 model and should not be sequentialized. In that case A is the simplification of B, not the other way around.

# 9. Final Recommendation

- **P1 (operational compliance):** use **Model A**, or the §8 sequential tree if concurrency of analyze and check is not an operational requirement. **Model B is not suitable**: it cannot attach a check to each revision and can close after an unchecked later attempt. The simplified model may replace A only under that concurrency caveat; it must not replace B if B was intended as “check the case once.”
- **P2 (executive dashboard):** A, B, and the simplified model are interchangeable **after** \(\Phi\). Prefer the simplified model on structural grounds (11 nodes, no AND). Do not publish B as “the” process under a P1-looking picture just because the dashboard cannot see the difference.
- **P3 (bounded testing):** treat A and B as different systems. Any test suite that only plays one-attempt, no-approve traces (`OACK`, `OCAK`) will **false-pass** equivalence. Include at least one two-attempt trace and the `Analyze → Approve → Check` witness.
- **Do not replace both originals with one tree** if P1 and P2 must both remain faithful to their native observation boundaries. P1 distinguishes the models; P2 erases the distinction.

**Warning about bounded comparison.** P3 only inspects complete traces with at most 12 visible events and at most three analyses. That bound already contains decisive separators, so the inequivalence verdict does not rest on unexamined traces. The converse is false: had the bounded sets coincided, traces with four or more analyses, or A’s 13-event fully-approved triple revision, would still have been untested. Bounded equality is evidence. Unbounded counterexamples, or a structural invariant such as \(\#C=\#A\) versus \(\#C=1\), are proofs.
