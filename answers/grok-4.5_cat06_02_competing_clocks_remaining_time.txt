## 3. Stage-by-stage decomposition

### Shared calendar anchors
- Now: **Fri 18 Sep 2026 15:30**
- Chemistry open: until Fri 18:00, then Mon 08:00–
- Micro incubation free-runs; readout staff Mon–Fri 08–18; optional weekend on-call
- QA: Fri to 20:00; Sat 09–13; Sun closed; Mon+ 08–20 (two reviewers Mon)

Join time always uses **max(chem usable, micro usable)**, not sum.

---

### Scenario A — Smooth path
**Chemistry waiting + service**  
From 15:30: ~2.5 h lab left. Residual work clears one analyst ~16:10–16:24 and the other ~16:42–17:30. With mixed discipline, B-417 often starts late afternoon if priority helps, or may not start if FIFO + long heads.  
- Optimistic: start ~16:30–17:00, service ~2–3 h  **cannot finish by 18:00** if start after ~15:30–16:00 with mode/mean service; only very short service + early start finishes Friday.  
- Baseline smooth: unfinished or unstarted work **pauses Friday 18:00  resumes Mon 08:00**. Monday service 1.5–4.5 h  chemistry usable **Mon ~09:30–13:00** typical (earlier if partial Friday progress).  
Friday-complete chemistry is a thin left tail only.

**Microbiology incubation + readout**  
Ready Sun 12:00–Mon 20:00 (central Sun ~16:00).  
P(on-call) 0.25–0.45: if authorized and ready Sunday, readout Sun afternoon/evening (still QA-poor on Sunday).  
Without auth (more likely): hold until **Mon 08:00**, readout 0.5–1.5 h  usable **Mon 08:30–09:30**.  
No contamination in this scenario.

**Parallel join**  
max(chem Mon morning, micro Mon ~08:30–09:30)  **Mon 09:30–13:00** (micro or short chem wait). Chemistry rarely later than micro on the smooth path if Monday start is prompt; if chemistry still in queue Monday, join shifts to late morning/afternoon.

**QA waiting + service**  
Join Mon morning. Overnight/weekend arrivals and incomplete drain of the old “5 ahead”  uncertain 2–8 ahead plausible. Two reviewers from 08:00.  
Wait ~0.5–4 h open time + service median 1.5 h (p80 ~3.5)  QA accept often **Mon 13:00–19:00**, spill to **Tue morning** if queue heavy or service long.

**Rework**  
None in pure smooth.

**Final**  
Electronic release +0.25–0.75 h  terminal **Mon afternoon–Tue midday**.

---

### Scenario B — Chemistry rerun / spill emphasis
**Chemistry**  
Either (i) late Friday start  full service Monday, or (ii) first result borderline (P 0.10–0.20 baseline; higher if temp later confirmed), priority rerun Triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0) but only in open hours.  
Usable chemistry often **Mon 12:00–18:00** (rerun same day if morning first-pass) or **Tue 10:00–15:00** if rerun cannot finish Monday.

**Micro**  
Same as smooth unless dependence lifts contam — baseline still Mon AM readout.

**Join**  
max(later chem, micro Mon AM)  **Mon afternoon or Tue morning**. Chemistry becomes the binding clock more often.

**QA + final**  
Later join faces a rebuilt queue; accept **Mon eve–Tue**, release shortly after. Central remaining ~48–60 h; tail into Wednesday with congestion.

---

### Scenario C — Delayed micro / confirmation
**Chemistry**  
Usually ready by Mon (as A/B); does not bind once micro stretches.

**Micro**  
- Late readiness (toward 54 h  Mon evening) without on-call  readout **Tue 08:00–10:00**.  
- Contamination indication P 0.05–0.12 (higher if temp confirmed): +20–30 h continuous incubation from first readout moment, then another 0.5–1.5 h staffed readout. Example: first read Mon 09:00  confirm ready Tue 05:00–15:00  staffed read Tue/Wed open hours.  
Join **Tue–Thu** depending on confirmation.

**QA + final**  
Starts only after delayed join; more arrivals accumulate. Terminal often **Tue–Thu**; confirmation tail **Fri or into following week**.

Weekend on-call only partially helps first readout; it does not remove a 20–30 h confirmation incubation.

---

### Scenario D — QA query / investigation tail
**Labs**  
Join as in A–C (Mon–Tue typical if no micro confirmation).

**QA first pass**  
After wait+service, with P 0.15–0.30 documentation query  +4–12 **working** hours (QA calendar: evenings/Sat partial/Sun off). Example: query opened Mon PM  info back Tue–Wed  resume QA.  
Deviation: base P 0.04–0.10 if clean labs; **0.30–0.55** if rerun/contam/temp.  
- Doc-only (~60–80% of inv): +8–20 working hours  +1–4 calendar days.  
- Lab inv (residual): **+3–7 calendar days** (binds hardest).

**Final**  
Release after accept, or reject recording if critical defect confirmed (less common than accept-after-inv).  
Central doc-path terminals **Wed–Fri**; lab-inv tails **following week**.

---

### Scenario E — Temp confirmed (dependence booster)
Engineering confirm P 0.20–0.40 raises joint probability of chemistry borderline, micro contamination, and QA deviation.  
Join = max(extended chem, extended micro). Then high investigation rate.  
Use for **upper uncertainty envelope**, not as independent multiply of marginal probabilities.  
Dominant severe clock: lab investigation calendar days and/or micro re-incubation.

## 5. Main uncertainty drivers

1. **Microbiology usable-result time (incubation end + weekend on-call vs Monday gate + optional contamination re-incubation)** — calendar uncertainty + path uncertainty + service-time uncertainty.  
2. **Temperature-excursion confirmation and induced dependence** (joint lift of rerun, contamination, deviation) — dependence uncertainty + observation uncertainty.  
3. **QA path after join** (documentation query vs deviation; doc-only hours vs lab investigation days) — path uncertainty.  
4. **Chemistry queue discipline and Friday capacity / rerun** (whether chemistry binds Monday join) — queue uncertainty + service-time uncertainty + calendar uncertainty.  
5. **QA queue length at the (random) future join epoch** — queue uncertainty (arrivals, overdispersion, weekend partial drain).

## 6. Most forecast-changing next event

**Most valuable next observation:** **Microbiology first readout outcome and timestamp** (including whether weekend on-call was authorized, readiness time in the 46–54 h window, and pass vs contamination indication).

- If readout **clear Mon morning (or rare Sunday on-call)**  collapse join to max(that time, chemistry), shift mass toward Mon–Tue release; down-weight multi-day micro tail.  
- If **contamination indicated**  add 20–30 h incubation + staffed readout and sharply raise deviation probability  move central forecast to mid/late week and fatten lab-inv tail.  
- If **readiness only late Monday without on-call**  push join to Tuesday and slide all QA logic one day.

**Secondary update trigger:** **Resolution of the temperature-excursion flag** (cleared vs confirmed). Cleared  keep baseline anomaly rates; confirmed  immediately raise rerun/contam/investigation probabilities and widen severe-tail remaining time even before lab results finish.
