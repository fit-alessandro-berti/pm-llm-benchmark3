## 2. Rule Interpretation

**RR1 — Drafter/approver separation.** After alias normalization, the human behind `Draft_Change_Request` must  human behind `Approve_Change` on the same case. Violation is technical SoD failure regardless of risk.

**RR2 — Implementer/verifier separation.** After normalization and service-account attribution, human behind `Implement_Change` must  human behind `Verify_Implementation`. Unattributable service actions neither pass nor fail RR2 on identity grounds; they fail the control objective until mapped.

**RR3 — High-risk approval authority.** If `risk=HIGH` and path is non-emergency (or emergency approval is not under IC path), `Approve_Change` must be QM standing role or delegate under a delegation whose: (a) effective interval covers event timestamp; (b) scope covers case `scope`; (c) principal-absence/unavailability condition is met when the instrument requires it; (d) no prohibited subdelegation chain. Event-time roles only—not current HR master.

**RR4 — Emergency substitution.** If `emergency=true` and HIGH, implementation may follow approval by IC or valid IC delegate; then QM or valid QM delegate must perform `Retrospective_QM_Review` within two business days of the emergency approval (or first emergency implement, conservatively the approval timestamp). Exception holds only if emergency flag and safety rationale are credible. Scope of QM delegation still binds the retrospective.

**Business days (RR4):** Site calendar: 2026-08-15–16 weekend; no holidays in range. Business days = Mon–Fri excluding holidays. “Within two business days” = review timestamp  end of the second business day after the calendar date of the triggering emergency approval. Example: approval Tue 2026-08-11  BD1=Wed 12, BD2=Thu 13  deadline 2026-08-13 23:59:59 UTC. Fri 2026-08-14 is late.

**RR5 — Identity evidence.** `svc_qms` is never a human. Conformance credit requires reliable session mapping (badge+token+dedicated station stronger than shared WS+room badge). Aliases of one human merged before SoD.

**RR6 — Suspicion vs proof.** Repeated pairs, reciprocity, shared WS, concentrated handovers = indicators only; not collusion proof without independent corroboration.

**Principal acting while on leave:** Leave does not strip standing QM authority. Asha may validly approve while on leave if it is actually Asha. Leave is evidence for delegate “principal absent” conditions and for risk of credential sharing when remote/VPN use appears. Delegate on certified sick leave is treated as unavailable; actions on their account during sick leave are adverse identity indicators, not automatic proof of their personal act.

**Later database entry vs earlier delegation:** A QMS row created after the start of a claimed interval does not by itself prove the delegation existed at earlier timestamps. Contemporaneous signed PDF (D-QM-01, 2026-08-09 18:00) can prove earlier effectiveness; the delayed DB insert is a data-quality defect, not automatic invalidation, but weakens system-of-record reliance. Controlled DB record created before interval (D-QM-02) is strong.

    """End of nth business day after start_ts.date (weekends off; no holidays)."""

        d += pd.Timedelta(days=1)
    return d + pd.Timedelta(hours=23, minutes=59, seconds=59)

            qm_ok = True
    # IC path for emergency approve

    ic_del = []
    for _, d in delegations[delegations.kind == "IC"].iterrows():

        ic_del.append(dict(id=d.id, void=void, scope_ok=scope_ok, valid=ok))
        if ok:
            ic_ok = True
    auth = None
    if e["concept:name"] == "Approve_Change":
        if emerg and risk == "HIGH":
            auth = "IC_path_candidate" if ic_ok else ("QM_path" if qm_ok else "NO_AUTHORITY")

            auth = "QM_OK" if qm_ok else "NO_QM_AUTHORITY"

                     standing=standing, risk=risk, scope=scope, emergency=emerg,
