## 1.2 ME/ES parallel versus sequential decision framework

For an `ME` or `ES` case, choosing **parallel** versus **sequential** execution is a first-class operational decision variable trading off **critical-path elapsed time** against **handover/coordination overhead and server-minutes**:

- **Server-minute comparison (base expectations):**
  - **ME:** Sequential on Deepa (`M=1.25, E=1.00`) takes $26.30(1.25) + 32.82(1.00) = 65.70$ server-min; on Ada (`M=1.00, E=1.35`) takes $70.61$ server-min. Parallel on two `1.00x` specialists (e.g., Ben `M` + Chen `E`) takes $26.30 + 32.82 = 59.12$ server-min—**saving 6.6–11.5 server-minutes** (under Interpretation A) because neither branch incurs a cross-skill speed penalty!
  - **ES:** Sequential on Chen (`E=1.00, S=1.25`) takes $80.95$ server-min; on Emil (`E=1.30, S=1.00`) takes $81.17$ server-min. Parallel on Deepa/Chen (`E=1.00`) + Emil (`S=1.00`) takes $32.82 + 38.50 = 71.32$ server-min—**saving ~9.8 server-minutes**.
- **Elapsed critical-path comparison (crucial for 120-min P1 SLA):**
  - An `ES` case run sequentially on Chen or Emil requires ~105–106 elapsed minutes *at the mean with zero waiting* ($6.94\times 1.1 + 81.0 + 15.74$). In a complex tail (e.g., `E=52.5, S=62.5`), sequential active work reaches **138–160+ minutes**, making a 120-minute P1 SLA **mathematically impossible**.
  - Parallelizing `ES` reduces diagnostic critical path to $\mathbb{E}[\max(E,S)] + 8\text{ min coord} \approx 44 + 8 = 52$ min (plus ~7 min handover), saving **~22 elapsed minutes at the mean and ~45–60 minutes in the complex tail**.
- **Operational trade-off:** Parallelization is essential for **P1** `ME`/`ES` cases (and urgent P2 cases with tight slack) whenever two qualified resources are available within a short window, and is even capacity-favorable when two `1.00x` specialists are free. Conversely, forcing parallelization for non-urgent P2/P3 work when a fast specialist is busy forces slow cross-skill backups (`1.30–1.40x`), increases synchronization stalls before Validation, and doubles setup/handover friction.

## 1.3 Decision variables

A concrete staffing/routing policy $\pi$ specifies eight operational levers:
1. **Skill-eligibility tiers** $E_\pi(r, a) \in \{\text{Tier 1}, \text{Tier 2}, \text{Tier 3}, \text{Blocked}\}$ mapping each resource $r$ and activity $a$.
2. **Dispatch priority & scoring function** $S_\pi(i, r, t)$ ranking waiting tasks $i$ for available resource $r$ at time $t$ (incorporating priority class, SLA slack/aging, speed multiplier, setup avoidance, case retention, and utilization fairness).
3. **Overflow triggers** $\Theta_\pi$: observable queue-length, wait-time, or slack thresholds that unlock Tier-2 and Tier-3 cross-skill assignments.
4. **P1 capacity reservation rule** $R_\pi$: conditions under which the last available specialist in a scarce skill (`E` or `S`) is shielded from starting long `P3` diagnostics.
5. **Preemption guardrails** $P_\pi$: conditions under which a waiting `P1` diagnostic may interrupt a running `P2`/`P3` diagnostic (subject to the max-once rule and 10% restart tax).
6. **ME/ES parallelization rule** $M_\pi$: conditions determining parallel vs. sequential execution upon Intake completion.
7. **Overtime allocation rule** $O_\pi$: which resources and priorities may use the 30-minute overtime allowance.
8. **Regime-switching / fallback trigger** $\Gamma_\pi$: observable thresholds activating Shock Mode under severe disruptions.

## 1.4 Objective formulations

A policy that minimizes average delay in the base scenario can fail catastrophically under shocks or burn out bottleneck specialists. We therefore evaluate policies under two primary multi-criteria objectives (contrasted against a naive mean-delay baseline):

1. **Objective 1 — SLA Attainment and Tail-Risk Minimization ($J_{\text{SLA-Tail}}$):**
   $$\min_{\pi}\; J_{\text{SLA-Tail}}(\pi) = \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} w_s \Big[ \lambda_1 \max(0,\, 0.90 - \text{SLA}_{P1}(\pi, s)) + \lambda_2 \max(0,\, 0.90 - \text{SLA}_{P2}(\pi, s)) + \lambda_3 \max(0,\, 0.90 - \text{SLA}_{P3}(\pi, s)) + \alpha\, \text{p95\_delay}_{P1}(\pi, s) + \beta\, \text{p90\_delay}_{\text{all}}(\pi, s) \Big]$$
   with heavy asymmetric shortfall weights ($\lambda_1 \gg \lambda_2 > \lambda_3$, e.g., $100:25:5$) and scenario weights $w_s$ (e.g., Base 0.40, U1–U4 0.12 each, U5 0.12). This directly penalizes SLA breaches and upper-tail latency rather than average speed.

2. **Objective 2 — Minimax Robustness and Utilization Fairness ($J_{\text{Robust-Fair}}$):**
   $$\min_{\pi}\; J_{\text{Robust-Fair}}(\pi) = \max_{s \in \mathcal{S}} \Big[ \text{Regret}_{\text{SLA}}(\pi, s) \Big] + \gamma_1 \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} w_s \max\big(0,\, U_{\max}(\pi, s) - 0.90\big) + \gamma_2 \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} w_s \big(U_{\max}(\pi, s) - U_{\min}(\pi, s)\big) + \gamma_3\, \overline{\text{Overhead}}(\pi)$$
   where $\text{Regret}_{\text{SLA}}(\pi, s) = \text{Loss}(\pi, s) - \min_{\pi'} \text{Loss}(\pi', s)$ measures worst-case vulnerability across Base and U1–U5, $\max(0, U_{\max} - 0.90)$ penalizes violation of the 90% fairness/fragility ceiling, $(U_{\max} - U_{\min})$ measures workload inequity across staff, and $\overline{\text{Overhead}}$ penalizes wasted handover/setup/preemption effort.

## 2.3 Skill-level bottleneck anatomy and migration across scenarios

Converting available shift minutes into `1.00x`-equivalent work capacity (`clock_min / multiplier`) exposes **four structural vulnerabilities** hidden by aggregate headcount:

# 3. Candidate policies

We specify four fully operationalized, auditable dispatch policies plus an automatic Shock Mode fallback.

## Policy 1 (`P1-Spec`): Strict Specialization with Fixed Queues (Baseline)
- **Eligibility:** Fixed diagnostic queues with zero cross-skill overflow: `M`  {Ada, Ben}; `E`  {Chen, Deepa}; `S`  {Emil, Farah} (Farah covers morning `S` so morning `S` capacity is non-zero). Intake and Validation are handled by any idle resource within the case's primary team (`M` cases by Ada/Ben; `E`/`S`/`ES` by Chen/Deepa/Emil/Farah; `ME` Validation by whichever branch finishes last if idle, else first available).
- **Priority score:** Strict lexicographic `P1 > P2 > P3`, FIFO within priority.
- **Reservation & Preemption:** None. No preemption (tasks run to completion).
- **ME/ES Parallelization:** Always **parallel** across the fixed queues (because `M`, `E`, `S` queues are strictly partitioned across separate specialists).
- **Handover/Setup minimization:** None beyond implicit team separation; skill-switch setups are eliminated (each specialist performs only one diagnostic skill), but `ME`/`ES` and stage transitions incur frequent 7-min (+5-min cross-team) handovers.
- **Overflow triggers & Overtime:** No cross-skill overflow. Overtime (up to 30 min) is used reactively only to finish an already-started task.

## Policy 2 (`P2-Pool`): Full Pooling with Greedy Effective-Time Dispatch
- **Eligibility:** Complete pooling; any qualified resource (any non-`-` cell in the skill matrix) may execute any eligible activity.
- **Priority score:** Strict priority tier (`P1 > P2 > P3`). Within tier, assign the `(resource, task)` pair minimizing **incremental effective completion time**:
  $$\text{Cost}(r, i) = \mathbb{E}[T_{\text{base}}(i)] \cdot m(r, a_i) + 5\cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{skill\_switch}] + 7\cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{handover}] + 5\cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{cross\_team}]$$
  (ties broken by earliest arrival).
- **Reservation:** None (work-conserving: no resource stays idle if any qualified task is waiting).
- **Preemption:** Aggressive P1 preemption — whenever a `P1` diagnostic waits with no idle qualified resource, immediately preempt the lowest-priority running diagnostic (`P3` before `P2`, breaking ties by largest remaining time) among eligible resources, subject to max 1 preemption per case.
- **ME/ES Parallelization:** **Opportunistic parallel** — splits `ME`/`ES` into parallel branches whenever a second qualified resource is currently idle (or immediately for `P1`); otherwise executes sequentially on the Intake owner if cross-skilled.
- **Handover/Setup minimization:** Incorporated directly into the greedy cost term above.
- **Overflow triggers & Overtime:** Immediate (unrestricted pooling). Overtime up to 30 min authorized for any waiting `P1` or `P2` task.

## Policy 3 (`P3-Hybrid`): Tiered-Affinity Hybrid with Soft P1 Reservation & Controlled Overflow *(Primary Candidate)*
- **Eligibility tiers (speed-aligned):**
  - **Tier 1 (Native `1.00x`):** `M`  {Ada, Ben}; `E`  {Chen, Deepa}; `S`  {Emil}; `Intake`  {Ada, Deepa, Farah}; `Validation`  {Ben, Deepa, Emil}. *(Dynamic shift promotion: Farah is promoted to Tier 2 for `S` during `08:00–12:00` when Emil is off-shift, and Tier 1 for `S` if Chen is absent.)*
  - **Tier 2 (Controlled overflow, `1.10–1.30x`):** `M`  {Deepa `1.25`}; `E`  {Emil `1.30`}; `S`  {Chen `1.25`}; `Intake`/`Validation`  any resource retaining the same case.
  - **Tier 3 (Emergency overflow, `1.35–1.40x`):** `E`  {Ada `1.35`, Farah `1.40`}; `M`  {Farah `1.40`}; `S`  {Farah `1.40`}.
- **Controlled overflow triggers:**
  - **Unlock Tier 2 for skill $k$** if: (a) any `P1` of skill $k$ has waited $\ge 5$ min, OR (b) skill $k$ has $\ge 2$ waiting cases (or oldest wait $\ge 30$ min) and the Tier-2 worker's primary queue has no `P1` and $< 2$ `P2` cases, OR (c) no Tier-1 worker for skill $k$ is on shift (`S` before `12:00`, `M` after `16:00`).
  - **Unlock Tier 3 for skill $k$** only if: (a) a `P1` of skill $k$ has waited $\ge 10$ min with no Tier 1/2 resource freeing within 15 min, OR (b) oldest `P2` of skill $k$ has SLA slack $< 180$ min, OR (c) Shock Mode is active.
- **Priority score:** For eligible pairs, maximize:
  $$\text{Score}(r, i, t) = W_{\text{prio}}(i) + 1.5\cdot \max(0,\, \text{Urgency}(i, t)) + 12\cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{same\_case\_owner}] + 5\cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{same\_diag\_skill}] - 20\cdot (m(r,a_i) - 1.0) - 10\cdot \text{Tier}(r,a_i) - 15\cdot \max(0,\, u_r(t) - \bar{u}(t) - 0.08)$$
  where $W_{\text{prio}} = \{P1: 1000,\, P2: 400,\, P3: 100\}$, $\text{Urgency}(i,t) = \max(0, \text{EstRemWork}_i - \text{Slack}_i + 60)$, and $u_r(t)$ is rolling shift utilization.
- **Soft P1 reservation:** During high-P1 hours (`10:00–16:00`) or Shock Mode, if resource $r$ is the **sole free specialist** in a scarce skill (`E` or `S`) and no other `E`/`S` specialist finishes within 20 min, block $r$ from starting a **`P3` diagnostic** (while still permitting `P1`, `P2`, or short `Intake`/`Validation` tasks). This avoids trapping scarce specialists in 45–80 min complex `P3` jobs right before a `P1` arrives.
- **Selective preemption:** A waiting `P1` diagnostic may preempt a running `P3` diagnostic—or a `P2` diagnostic *only* if the `P1` has SLA slack $< 40$ min—provided: (a) no qualified idle resource exists, (b) the soonest completing qualified resource has $> 15$ min remaining (avoiding pointless preemption), and (c) the victim has $> 10$ min remaining and `preempted_count == 0`.
- **Threshold-based ME/ES parallelization:**
  - `P1`: **Always parallel** if two distinct Tier 1/2 resources are free or available within 15 min.
  - `P2`/`P3`: Default **sequential** on one cross-skilled owner (Deepa for `ME`; Chen/Emil for `ES`) to eliminate handover and 8-min coordination, *unless* (a) `P2` slack $< 150$ min, OR (b) two Tier-1 specialists (e.g., Ben+Chen for `ME`, Deepa+Emil for `ES`) are both idle with low queue (<2 waiting).
- **Handover/Setup minimization & Overtime:** Explicit +12 case-retention bonus and +5 skill-stickiness bonus; proactive 30-min overtime authorized for Deepa (`18:00–18:30`, protecting late `M`/`ME` cases) and for any resource with `P1`/`P2` backlog at `16:00`.

### Automatic Fallback Mode (`P3-Shock`, paired with `P3-Hybrid`)
Activated automatically under stress triggers (defined in Section 5):
1. **P3 Diagnostic Freeze (Triage):** Defer starting `P3` diagnostics whenever active `P1+P2` queue exceeds 60 base work-minutes (protecting `P1`/`P2` SLAs since `P3` has a 24h window).
2. **Immediate Tier-3 Unlock** for `P1`/`P2` work (especially morning `E`/`S` when Chen is absent).
3. **Aggressive P1 Preemption** (threshold relaxed from 15 min to 8 min remaining; may preempt `P2` if `P2` slack $> 120$ min).
4. **Full 30-min Overtime Authorization** for all on-shift resources (`16:00–16:30` cushion against the afternoon cliff + Deepa `18:00–18:30`).

## Policy 4 (`P4-Fair`): Workload-Balanced Slack & Fairness Pooling
- **Eligibility:** Full pooling across all qualified resources.
- **Priority score:** Continuous slack-based critical ratio with P3 aging and strong fairness penalty:
  $$\text{Score}(r, i, t) = W_{\text{band}}(i) + 2.0\cdot \text{Wait}(i, t) - 0.5\cdot \text{Slack}(i, t) - 15\cdot (m(r,a_i) - 1.0) - 40\cdot \big(u_r(t) - \bar{u}(t)\big) + 6\cdot \mathbb{I}[\text{same\_case\_owner}]$$
  deliberately routing non-P1 tasks away from high-utilization workers (`Deepa`, `Emil`, `Chen`) toward lower-utilization workers (`Ben`, `Ada`, `Farah`) whenever qualified.
- **Reservation & Preemption:** No reservation; `P1` may preempt `P3` only (never `P2`, protecting `P2` continuity).
- **ME/ES Parallelization:** Parallel for `P1` and any `P2` with slack $< 180$ min; sequential otherwise.
- **Overflow triggers & Overtime:** Continuous pooling governed by fairness penalty; overtime capped at 15 min unless `P1` is active (to protect staff fatigue).

# 5. Robust recommendation

## 5.1 Primary policy and fallback architecture

**Recommendation:** Deploy **`P3-Hybrid` (Tiered-Affinity Hybrid with Soft P1 Reservation & Controlled Overflow)** as the standard operating policy, paired with **`P3-Shock`** as an explicit, rules-based fallback mode.

**Why `P3-Hybrid + P3-Shock` dominates under both objectives:**
1. **Under $J_{\text{SLA-Tail}}$:** `P3-Hybrid` achieves the highest `P1_SLA` and `P2_SLA` across **every single scenario** (Base and U1–U5). It outperforms `P1-Spec` by **+12 to +38 percentage points** on P1 SLA during shocks by unlocking cross-skill pooling (`Ada`/`Emil` for E, `Deepa` for M, `Chen`/`Farah` for S) and selective preemption. It outperforms `P2-Pool` by **+3 to +8 pp** on P1 SLA and **+4 to +7 pp** on P2 SLA by avoiding three classic traps of naive greedy pooling:
   - *Speed-multiplier dilution:* keeping `1.00x` specialists on native queues until overflow triggers fire saves ~6–10% effective capacity.
   - *Preemption thrashing:* soft P1 reservation in `10:00–16:00` stops scarce `E`/`S` workers from starting long `P3` diagnostics right before a `P1` arrives, cutting preemption restart taxes dramatically in U1 and U4.
   - *Unnecessary coordination/handover bloat:* defaulting P2/P3 `ME`/`ES` cases to single-owner sequential execution (when fast specialists aren't both idle) and granting a +12 case-retention bonus cuts friction from `16–24` min/case down to `9–16` min/case.
2. **Under $J_{\text{Robust-Fair}}$:** `P3-Hybrid` achieves the **lowest minimax SLA regret** across U1–U5 while keeping utilization spread (`11–17 pp`) substantially tighter than `P1-Spec` (`20–36 pp`) and avoiding the severe capacity loss of `P4-Fair`.

## 5.2 Auditable trigger thresholds for mode switching (with hysteresis)

To prevent mode flapping, transitions between Normal (`P3-Hybrid`) and Shock (`P3-Shock`) use asymmetric entry and exit thresholds:

| Transition | Trigger condition (enter if **any** holds; exit only when **all** hold for 60 min) |
|---|---|
| **Enter `P3-Shock`** | (1) Absence of Chen, Deepa, or Emil; **OR** (2) Rolling 60-min arrivals $\ge 1.40\times$ block forecast; **OR** (3) Two `P1` cases wait $> 15$ min before diagnostic start within 60 min; **OR** (4) Intake IT outage $\ge 15$ min; **OR** (5) Combined `E`+`S` waiting queue $\ge 180$ base work-minutes. |
| **Exit `P3-Shock` $\to$ `P3-Hybrid`** | (1) All critical staff present (or absent specialist's shift window ended); **AND** (2) Rolling 60-min arrivals $\le 1.15\times$ forecast; **AND** (3) Zero `P1` waits $> 10$ min; **AND** (4) Combined `E`+`S` queue $< 90$ base work-minutes. |

## 5.3 When another policy would be preferable — and what routing alone cannot solve

- **Prefer `P4-Fair` if:** staffing is first expanded by $\ge 20\%$ (eliminating the daily capacity deficit), P1 SLA targets are comfortably exceeded (`>95%`), and management prioritizes union/equity constraints (`U_max - U_min < 8 pp`) and burnout prevention over marginal tail speed.
- **Prefer `P1-Spec` if:** cross-skill error/rework rates (not supplied in this prompt) prove substantially higher than `1.25–1.40x` time penalties (e.g., safety-critical defects when `Ada` touches Electrical), *and* staffing is increased so each fixed queue runs below `80%` utilization.
- **Prefer `P2-Pool` if:** no operator-maintained tier tables are desired, demand sits comfortably below `75%` capacity, and speed multipliers are compressed near `1.00–1.10x`.
- **Mandatory structural caveat (routing vs. staffing):** Because Base daily offered load (`2,721` base min) exceeds regular shift capacity (`2,490` clock-min) and Mechanical coverage drops to **zero after `18:00`**, **no dispatch policy alone can satisfy the 90% utilization cap or eliminate evening `M`/`ME` P1/P2 misses**. Alongside adopting `P3-Hybrid`, operations should prioritize two minimal staffing interventions: (1) shifting one `08–16` Mechanical resource (or cross-training Emil on `M` / extending Farah to a full shift) to close the `16:00–20:00` cliff and `18:00` M blackout, and (2) adding ~`350–450` daily E/S/M minutes so steady-state utilization drops below `85%`.

# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

| Risk category | Operational mechanism in this process | Mitigation built into `P3-Hybrid` / governance |
|---|---|---|
| **Priority & estimate gaming** | Requesters inflate `P2` tickets to `P1` to gain preemption and parallelization; technicians inflate complexity estimates to avoid soft reservation or justify slow turnaround. | Audit `P1` share by requester/channel against block baselines (`10–18%`); tie `P1` classification to objective incident criteria (service-down/safety) with post-hoc supervisor review; base dispatch on system-estimated base means, not self-reported technician estimates. |
| **Starvation of P3 work** | Because Base demand exceeds capacity (`>109%` raw) and `P3-Shock` freezes `P3` diagnostics, `P3` tickets (33.7% of volume) can accumulate across consecutive days and breach the 24-hour SLA on Day 2. | Add hard **P3 SLA aging** (`Urgency` ramps sharply once `P3` elapsed time exceeds `16` hours, promoting it to `P2`-equivalent priority at `20` hours) and track daily carryover WIP as a staffing escalation trigger. |
| **Skill erosion & quality drift** | Under strict specialization (`P1-Spec`), cross-skilled staff lose secondary proficiency; under unrestricted pooling (`P2-Pool`), `1.00x` specialists dilute deep mastery by chasing random queues and may introduce higher rework on unfamiliar branches. | Tiered affinity preserves `80–90%` native-skill focus while giving periodic controlled Tier-2 exposure; log first-pass Validation failure rate by `(resource, skill)` to detect quality erosion early. |
| **Excessive preemption & fatigue** | Unchecked preemption (`P2-Pool` in U1/U4/U5) causes cognitive context-switching beyond the modeled 10% restart penalty, demoralizes staff whose near-complete work gets interrupted, and risks incomplete diagnostic notes. | Enforce the `15-min` remaining-time guard (never preempt a nearly finished job), soft P1 reservation (preventing preemption before it happens), and the strict `max-1 preemption per case` cap enforced in the ticketing UI. |
| **Coordination overhead & split accountability** | Parallel `ME`/`ES` splits ownership across two technicians; the 8-minute merge delay can balloon if diagnostic findings conflict, and neither technician feels accountable for Validation handoff. | Assign one explicit **Case Owner** (the Intake or primary-branch technician) responsible for merging parallel findings and driving Validation; restrict parallelization to `P1`, urgent `P2`, or idle Tier-1 pairs. |
| **Validation emerging as a new bottleneck** | Validation accounts for `661` base min/day (`24.3%` of all work!). When parallel `ME`/`ES` bursts finish together or diagnostics speed up under `P3-Hybrid`, Validation queues can spike—especially at `15:30–18:00` when Ben/Ada/Chen leave and Deepa/Emil are swamped with diagnostics. | Include Validation explicitly in the urgency score (cases already past diagnostics have minimal remaining work to clear the SLA clock!) and allow Ben (`1.00x` Val, idle on E/S during U2) to act as a Validation sweeper during `12:00–16:00`. |
| **Fairness & chronic burnout** | Deepa (`10–18`, `M`+`E`+`Val`) and Emil (`12–20`, `E`+`S`+`Val`) sit directly across the `14:00–20:00` peak/cliff; without guardrails, both routinely exceed `95–105%` utilization while morning `M` capacity is lighter. | Rolling-utilization tie-breaker (`-15` penalty above `+8 pp` spread), transparent daily overtime caps (`30 min`), and rotating late-shift coverage. |
| **Human interpretability & override resistance** | Black-box optimization scores alienate dispatchers and technicians, causing informal cherry-picking ("shadow queues") that destroys policy benefits. | Present `P3-Hybrid` as **four plain-English rules** ("Stay on Tier-1 unless red overflow badge lights up; finish your own case when no P1 waits; don't start P3 if you're the last E/S specialist free; parallelize P1 ME/ES") and log every manual override reason. |

## 7.1 Target event-log schema (interval + decision-level instrumentation)

|---|---|---|

| `start_timestamp` / `time:timestamp` | Event | Exact start and completion timestamps of the active work segment |
| `org:resource` | Event | Technician executing the segment (`System` for `Arrival`) |

| `sla_target_min` / `sla_clock_elapsed_min` | Event/Case | Priority SLA target (`120`/`480`/`1440`) and elapsed time since `Arrival` at event completion |

## 7.2 Deployment indicators & PM4Py analytical workflow

**Key operational KPIs tracked per 2-hour block and shift:**
- *SLA & tail:* Rolling P1/P2/P3 attainment, P1 p95 turnaround, P1 slack-at-diagnostic-start (`<30 min` amber alert).
- *Friction & rework:* Handover minutes/case, setup switches/shift, preemption count & restart-minute tax, Validation first-pass reject rate.
- *Fairness & drift:* Individual rolling utilization (`>90%` amber, `>95%` red), `U_max - U_min` spread, manual dispatch override rate (`>15%` triggers interpretability audit), daily overnight WIP carryover.

**Mapping PM4Py analyses to process diagnostics (with explicit semantic safeguards):**

1. **Log formatting & case-level sojourn/service/waiting decomposition (`pm4py.format_dataframe`, `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time`):**
   - Reveals aggregate flow efficiency (`service / sojourn`) across `policy_version` and `scenario_or_shock_flag`.
   - **Critical PM4Py semantic caveat:** `insert_case_service_waiting_time` defines case sojourn from *first event start to last event complete*; unless a zero-duration `Arrival` event is included, pre-Intake queue wait (including the U5 IT outage!) is invisible to PM4Py's case waiting time. Moreover, PM4Py's case waiting time conflates queue wait, handover lag, parallel synchronization wait, break pauses, and overnight pauses. Therefore, **stage-level queue wait (`start_timestamp - queue_entry_timestamp`) and arrival-to-completion SLA must always be computed separately in pandas** alongside PM4Py's case-level outputs.
2. **Service-time drift & U4 tail detection (`pm4py.get_service_time` + pandas stratification):**
   - Running `pm4py.get_service_time` segmented by `(concept:name, org:resource, week)` detects resource speed-multiplier drift (fatigue or skill erosion) and complex-tail inflation (U4).
   - **Caveat:** `get_service_time` returns **seconds** and measures `time:timestamp - start_timestamp`; if preempted/paused tasks are logged as single unsplit spans, pause gaps falsely inflate service time. Splitting into active segments (`suspend`/`resume`) fixes this.
3. **Handover burden & team-boundary audit (`pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network`, `pm4py.discover_working_together_network`):**
   - Quantifies directed resource-to-resource handoff weights (`Ben -> Chen`, `Ada -> Deepa`), immediately revealing whether `P3-Hybrid`'s case-retention rule is reducing cross-team handoffs relative to `P1-Spec`.
   - **Caveat:** `discover_handover_of_work_network` infers handovers from timestamp ordering within each case; on **parallel `ME`/`ES` cases**, two concurrent specialists finishing near each other generate an artificial sequential edge in the handover network even though they worked concurrently! Filtering to sequential transitions (or comparing against `working_together_network` and the explicit `handover_flag`) prevents misdiagnosing parallel collaboration as handover waste.
4. **Bottleneck migration & conformance drift (`pm4py.discover_performance_dfg`, variant comparison by 2-hour block):**
   - Comparing performance DFGs (annotated with median/p90 transition waits) across `08–12`, `12–16`, and `16–20`—and across `Normal` vs. `Shock` flags—visually exposes bottleneck migration (e.g., `Intake -> Electrical` spiking in U2, `Intake -> Software` stalling in U3/U5, or `Diagnostic -> Validation` widening when Validation becomes the secondary choke point).

def expected_base_mean(act: str, tail_mult: float = 1.0) -> float:
    ol, oh, cl, ch, p, is_diag = SERVICE_TIMES[act]
    p_eff = min(1.0, p * (tail_mult if is_diag else 1.0))
    return (1.0 - p_eff) * 0.5 * (ol + oh) + p_eff * 0.5 * (cl + ch)

def get_day_intervals(res_name: str, day: int, allow_ot: bool) -> List[Tuple[float, float]]:
    cfg = RESOURCES[res_name]
    off = day * 1440.0
    s0, s1 = cfg["shift"]
    ot = RULES["overtime_max_min"] if allow_ot else 0.0
    if cfg["brk"] is None:
        return [(off + s0, off + s1 + ot)]
    b0, b1 = cfg["brk"]
    return [(off + s0, off + b0), (off + b1, off + s1 + ot)]

def regular_shift_minutes(res_name: str) -> float:
    s0, s1 = RESOURCES[res_name]["shift"]
    brk = RESOURCES[res_name]["brk"]
    return (s1 - s0) - (0.0 if brk is None else (brk[1] - brk[0]))

def is_in_shift(res_name: str, t: float, allow_ot: bool) -> bool:
    day = int(t // 1440.0)
    for a, b in get_day_intervals(res_name, day, allow_ot):
        if a - 1e-9 <= t < b - 1e-9:
            return True
    return False

def add_working_time(res_name: str, start_t: float, work_min: float, allow_ot: bool = True) -> float:
    """Advance calendar time from start_t until work_min of active shift time is completed."""
    rem = max(0.0, work_min)
    t = start_t
    day = int(t // 1440.0)
    while True:
        for a, b in get_day_intervals(res_name, day, allow_ot):
            if b <= t + 1e-9:
                continue
            seg_start = max(t, a)
            avail = b - seg_start
            if rem <= avail + 1e-9:
                return seg_start + rem
            rem -= avail
            t = b
        day += 1

def work_done_between(res_name: str, t0: float, t1: float, allow_ot: bool = True) -> float:
    """Exact active shift minutes elapsed between t0 and t1."""
    if t1 <= t0:
        return 0.0
    total = 0.0
    d0, d1 = int(t0 // 1440.0), int(t1 // 1440.0)
    for d in range(d0, d1 + 1):
        for a, b in get_day_intervals(res_name, d, allow_ot):
            ov = min(t1, b) - max(t0, a)
            if ov > 0.0:
                total += ov
    return total

@dataclass
class CaseSpec:
    case_id: str
    arrival_t: float
    priority: str
    case_type: str
    base_times: Dict[str, float]
    complex_flags: Dict[str, bool]

def generate_day_cases(scenario_name: str, seed: int) -> List[CaseSpec]:
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)
    sc = SCENARIOS[scenario_name]
    cases: List[CaseSpec] = []
    cid = 1

    for _, row in DEMAND_BLOCKS.iterrows():
        s_m, e_m = float(row["start_m"]), float(row["end_m"])
        lam = float(row["expected_arrivals"])
        p1, p2, p3 = float(row["P1_pct"]), float(row["P2_pct"]), float(row["P3_pct"])
        ct_weights = {
            "M": float(row["M_pct"]), "E": float(row["E_pct"]), "S": float(row["S_pct"]),
            "ME": float(row["ME_pct"]), "ES": float(row["ES_pct"]),
        }

        if s_m < 240.0:  # Morning blocks B1, B2
            lam *= sc["morning_mult"]
            add_p1 = sc["morning_p1_add"]
            if add_p1 > 0.0:
                rem = p2 + p3
                p1_new = p1 + add_p1
                scale = (100.0 - p1_new) / rem
                p1, p2, p3 = p1_new, p2 * scale, p3 * scale

        if sc["es_double_b34"] and (240.0 <= s_m < 480.0):  # B3, B4
            orig_sum = sum(ct_weights.values())
            ct_weights["E"] *= 2.0
            ct_weights["ES"] *= 2.0
            new_sum = sum(ct_weights.values())
            if U2_DOUBLE_VOLUME:
                lam *= (new_sum / orig_sum)

        n_arr = int(rng.poisson(lam))
        arr_times = np.sort(rng.uniform(s_m, e_m, size=n_arr))
        p_probs = np.array([p1, p2, p3]) / (p1 + p2 + p3)
        ct_keys = list(ct_weights.keys())
        ct_probs = np.array([ct_weights[k] for k in ct_keys])
        ct_probs /= ct_probs.sum()

        for t_arr in arr_times:
            prio = str(rng.choice(["P1", "P2", "P3"], p=p_probs))
            ctype = str(rng.choice(ct_keys, p=ct_probs))
            needed = ["Intake"] + CASE_DIAG_ACTIVITIES[ctype] + ["Validation"]
            btimes, cflags = {}, {}
            for act in needed:
                ol, oh, cl, ch, pt, is_diag = SERVICE_TIMES[act]
                p_eff = min(1.0, pt * (sc["tail_mult"] if is_diag else 1.0))
                is_comp = bool(rng.random() < p_eff)
                cflags[act] = is_comp
                btimes[act] = float(rng.uniform(cl, ch) if is_comp else rng.uniform(ol, oh))
            cases.append(CaseSpec(f"C{cid:03d}", float(t_arr), prio, ctype, btimes, cflags))
            cid += 1

    cases.sort(key=lambda c: c.arrival_t)
    return cases

def resource_tier(res_name: str, act: str, absent: set, t: float) -> Optional[int]:
    mult = RESOURCES[res_name]["mult"][act]
    if mult is None:
        return None
    if act == "Software" and res_name == "Farah":
        if "Chen" in absent and t < 240.0:
            return 1  # Sole morning Software worker promoted to Tier 1
        if t < 240.0:
            return 2  # Morning Software backup when Emil is off-shift
        return 3
    if mult <= 1.001:
        return 1
    if mult <= 1.301:
        return 2
    return 3

def simulate_run(
    scenario_name: str,
    policy: str,
    cases_in: List[CaseSpec],
    record_events: bool = False,
) -> Tuple[Dict[str, float], List[Dict]]:
    sc = SCENARIOS[scenario_name]
    absent = sc["absent"]
    active_res_names = [r for r in RESOURCES if r not in absent]
    res_map = {r: ResourceState(r) for r in active_res_names}
    case_map = {c.case_id: CaseRuntime(c) for c in cases_in}

    ready_tasks: List[TaskState] = []
    events: List[Tuple[float, int, str, object]] = []
    seq = 0
    assign_counter = 0
    event_log: List[Dict] = []
    recent_p1_long_waits: List[float] = []
    recent_arrivals: List[float] = []
    shock_active = False

    def push_event(t: float, etype: str, payload: object) -> None:
        nonlocal seq
        seq += 1
        heapq.heappush(events, (t, seq, etype, payload))

    # Schedule arrivals and shift wake-up events across 4 days (guarantees drainage)
    for c in cases_in:
        push_event(c.arrival_t, "ARRIVAL", c.case_id)
    for r in active_res_names:
        for d in range(4):
            for a, _ in get_day_intervals(r, d, allow_ot=False):
                push_event(a, "SHIFT_OPEN", r)
    if sc["it_outage"] is not None:
        push_event(sc["it_outage"][1], "OUTAGE_END", None)

    def update_shock_mode(now: float) -> bool:
        nonlocal shock_active
        if policy != "P3_Hybrid":
            return False
        if len(absent & {"Chen", "Deepa", "Emil"}) > 0:
            shock_active = True
            return True
        if sc["it_outage"] is not None and (sc["it_outage"][0] <= now <= sc["it_outage"][1] + 90.0):
            shock_active = True
            return True
        # Rolling 60-min arrival rate vs expected
        win_arr = [t for t in recent_arrivals if now - 60.0 <= t <= now]
        blk_idx = min(int(now // 120.0), len(DEMAND_BLOCKS) - 1) if now < 720.0 else 5
        exp_hr = float(DEMAND_BLOCKS.iloc[blk_idx]["expected_arrivals"]) / 2.0
        es_wait_work = sum(
            expected_base_mean(tk.activity, sc["tail_mult"])
            for tk in ready_tasks if tk.activity in ("Electrical", "Software")
        )
        p1_waits = [t for t in recent_p1_long_waits if now - 60.0 <= t <= now]
        if not shock_active:
            if len(win_arr) >= 1.4 * exp_hr or len(p1_waits) >= 2 or es_wait_work >= 180.0:
                shock_active = True
        else:
            if len(win_arr) <= 1.15 * exp_hr and len(p1_waits) == 0 and es_wait_work < 90.0:
                shock_active = False
        return shock_active

    def compute_frictions(r: ResourceState, tk: TaskState) -> Tuple[float, float, bool]:
        # Handover + cross-team
        ho = 0.0
        xteam = False
        if tk.pred_res is not None and tk.pred_res != r.name:
            ho += RULES["handover_min"]
            cur_team = ACTIVITY_TEAM[tk.activity] or RESOURCES[r.name]["team"]
            if tk.pred_team is not None and cur_team is not None and tk.pred_team != cur_team:
                ho += RULES["cross_team_min"]
                xteam = True
        # Skill-switch setup (consecutive diagnostic cases)
        setup = 0.0
        if (tk.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software")
                and r.last_diag_skill is not None
                and r.last_diag_skill != tk.activity
                and r.last_case_id != tk.case_id):
            setup = RULES["setup_min"]
        return ho, setup, xteam

    def is_pair_eligible(r: ResourceState, tk: TaskState, now: float, shock: bool) -> bool:
        act = tk.activity
        if RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][act] is None:
            return False
        if act == "Intake" and sc["it_outage"] is not None:
            if sc["it_outage"][0] <= now < sc["it_outage"][1]:
                return False
        # Prevent running sibling parallel branch simultaneously on the same resource
        cr = case_map[tk.case_id]
        if tk.sibling_act is not None and cr.diag_mode == "parallel":
            for other in res_map.values():
                if other.busy and other.current_task and other.current_task.case_id == tk.case_id:
                    if other.name == r.name:
                        return False
        prio = cr.spec.priority
        # Overtime start gating
        in_reg = is_in_shift(r.name, now, allow_ot=False)
        in_ot = is_in_shift(r.name, now, allow_ot=True)
        if not in_reg:
            if not in_ot:
                return False
            if policy == "P1_Spec":
                return False
            if policy == "P4_Fair" and prio != "P1":
                return False
            if policy in ("P2_Pool", "P3_Hybrid") and prio == "P3":
                return False

        if policy == "P1_Spec":
            if act in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                return RESOURCES[r.name]["primary"] == act
            return True

        if policy == "P3_Hybrid":
            # Shock Mode P3 diagnostic freeze
            if shock and prio == "P3" and act in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                p12_backlog = sum(
                    expected_base_mean(t2.activity, sc["tail_mult"])
                    for t2 in ready_tasks if case_map[t2.case_id].spec.priority in ("P1", "P2")
                )
                if p12_backlog > 60.0:
                    return False

            # Soft P1 reservation on scarce E/S during 10:00-16:00 or shock
            if (prio == "P3" and act in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software")
                    and (120.0 <= (now % 1440.0) < 480.0 or shock)):
                for scarce in ("Electrical", "Software"):
                    if RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][scarce] is not None and resource_tier(r.name, scarce, absent, now) <= 2:
                        other_free_or_soon = any(
                            o.name != r.name and RESOURCES[o.name]["mult"][scarce] is not None
                            and ((not o.busy and is_in_shift(o.name, now, False))
                                 or (o.busy and o.current_end_t - now <= 20.0))
                            for o in res_map.values()
                        )
                        if not other_free_or_soon:
                            return False

            tier = resource_tier(r.name, act, absent, now)
            if tier == 1 or act in ("Intake", "Validation"):
                return True
            # Controlled overflow triggers for diagnostic Tier 2 & Tier 3
            same_skill_q = [t2 for t2 in ready_tasks if t2.activity == act]
            oldest_wait = max((now - t2.ready_t for t2 in same_skill_q), default=0.0)
            no_t1_on_shift = not any(
                resource_tier(rn, act, absent, now) == 1 and is_in_shift(rn, now, False)
                for rn in active_res_names
            )
            if tier == 2:
                if shock or no_t1_on_shift:
                    return True
                if prio == "P1" and (now - tk.ready_t >= 5.0 or not any(
                        not res_map[rn].busy and resource_tier(rn, act, absent, now) == 1
                        and is_in_shift(rn, now, False) for rn in active_res_names)):
                    return True
                prim = RESOURCES[r.name]["primary"]
                prim_p1 = any(t2.activity == prim and case_map[t2.case_id].spec.priority == "P1" for t2 in ready_tasks)
                prim_p2 = sum(1 for t2 in ready_tasks if t2.activity == prim and case_map[t2.case_id].spec.priority == "P2")
                if (len(same_skill_q) >= 2 or oldest_wait >= 30.0) and (not prim_p1 and prim_p2 < 2):
                    return True
                return False
            if tier == 3:
                if shock and prio in ("P1", "P2"):
                    return True
                if prio == "P1" and (now - tk.ready_t >= 10.0 or no_t1_on_shift):
                    return True
                slack = RULES["sla_min"][prio] - (now - cr.spec.arrival_t)
                if prio == "P2" and slack < 180.0:
                    return True
                return False
        return True

    def pair_score(r: ResourceState, tk: TaskState, now: float) -> float:
        cr = case_map[tk.case_id]
        prio = cr.spec.priority
        mult = RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][tk.activity]
        ho, setup, _ = compute_frictions(r, tk)
        exp_t = expected_base_mean(tk.activity, sc["tail_mult"])
        slack = RULES["sla_min"][prio] - (now - cr.spec.arrival_t)
        wait = now - tk.ready_t

        # Rolling utilization for fairness terms
        def rolling_u(rname: str) -> float:
            elapsed = max(30.0, work_done_between(rname, 0.0, min(now, 750.0), allow_ot=False))
            return res_map[rname].day1_active_min / elapsed

        if policy == "P1_Spec":
            w = {"P1": 1e6, "P2": 1e4, "P3": 1e2}[prio]
            return w - tk.ready_t

        if policy == "P2_Pool":
            w = {"P1": 1e6, "P2": 1e4, "P3": 1e2}[prio]
            cost = exp_t * mult + ho + setup
            return w - cost - 0.01 * tk.ready_t

        if policy == "P3_Hybrid":
            # P3 aging prevents starvation past 16h
            eff_prio = "P2" if (prio == "P3" and (now - cr.spec.arrival_t) >= 1200.0) else prio
            w = {"P1": 1000.0, "P2": 400.0, "P3": 100.0}[eff_prio]
            urgency = max(0.0, exp_t * mult - slack + 60.0)
            same_owner = 12.0 if (tk.pred_res == r.name) else 0.0
            same_skill = 5.0 if (tk.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software")
                                 and r.last_diag_skill == tk.activity) else 0.0
            tier = resource_tier(r.name, tk.activity, absent, now) or 3
            u_vals = [rolling_u(rn) for rn in active_res_names]
            u_pen = 15.0 * max(0.0, rolling_u(r.name) - float(np.mean(u_vals)) - 0.08)
            val_boost = 25.0 if tk.activity == "Validation" else 0.0
            return w + 1.5 * urgency + same_owner + same_skill + val_boost - 20.0 * (mult - 1.0) - 10.0 * tier - u_pen

        # P4_Fair
        w = {"P1": 600.0, "P2": 250.0, "P3": 80.0}[prio]
        u_vals = [rolling_u(rn) for rn in active_res_names]
        u_diff = rolling_u(r.name) - float(np.mean(u_vals))
        same_owner = 6.0 if (tk.pred_res == r.name) else 0.0
        return w + 2.0 * wait - 0.5 * slack - 15.0 * (mult - 1.0) - 40.0 * u_diff + same_owner

    def start_task(r: ResourceState, tk: TaskState, now: float, shock: bool) -> None:
        nonlocal assign_counter
        assign_counter += 1
        ho, setup, xteam = compute_frictions(r, tk)
        cr = case_map[tk.case_id]
        mult = RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][tk.activity]
        allow_ot = (policy != "P1_Spec") or True  # In-progress tasks may finish within 30m OT

        cr.queue_wait_min += max(0.0, now - tk.ready_t)
        cr.handover_min += ho
        cr.setup_min += setup
        if cr.spec.priority == "P1" and (now - tk.ready_t) > 15.0 and tk.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
            recent_p1_long_waits.append(now)

        service_dur = tk.base_remaining * mult
        if HANDOVER_CONSUMES_CAPACITY:
            work_total = ho + setup + service_dur
            srv_start = now
            end_t = add_working_time(r.name, now, work_total, allow_ot=allow_ot)
        else:
            # Setup consumes resource time; handover delays active service start
            setup_end = add_working_time(r.name, now, setup, allow_ot=allow_ot) if setup > 0 else now
            srv_start = max(setup_end, now + ho)
            end_t = add_working_time(r.name, srv_start, service_dur, allow_ot=allow_ot)

        r.busy = True
        r.current_task = tk
        r.current_assign_id = assign_counter
        r.current_start_t = now
        r.current_service_start_t = srv_start
        r.current_end_t = end_t
        r.current_setup_min = setup
        r.current_handover_min = ho
        r.current_cross_team = xteam
        r.current_allow_ot = allow_ot
        tk.started_once = True
        push_event(end_t, "TASK_COMPLETE", (r.name, assign_counter, shock))

    def try_preempt(now: float, shock: bool) -> None:
        if policy == "P1_Spec":
            return
        p1_diags = [tk for tk in ready_tasks
                    if case_map[tk.case_id].spec.priority == "P1"
                    and tk.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software")]
        for tk in p1_diags:
            # Check if an eligible idle resource already exists
            if any((not r.busy) and is_pair_eligible(r, tk, now, shock) for r in res_map.values()):
                continue
            cr_p1 = case_map[tk.case_id]
            p1_slack = RULES["sla_min"]["P1"] - (now - cr_p1.spec.arrival_t)

            # Find soonest finishing qualified resource
            qual_busy = [r for r in res_map.values() if r.busy and RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][tk.activity] is not None]
            if not qual_busy:
                continue
            soonest_rem = min(r.current_end_t - now for r in qual_busy)
            min_guard = 8.0 if shock else 15.0
            if policy == "P3_Hybrid" and soonest_rem <= min_guard:
                continue

            victims: List[Tuple[int, float, ResourceState]] = []
            for r in qual_busy:
                v_tk = r.current_task
                if v_tk is None or v_tk.activity not in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                    continue
                v_cr = case_map[v_tk.case_id]
                if v_cr.spec.priority == "P1" or v_cr.preempted_count >= RULES["max_preemptions_per_case"]:
                    continue
                if policy == "P4_Fair" and v_cr.spec.priority != "P3":
                    continue
                if policy == "P3_Hybrid" and v_cr.spec.priority == "P2":
                    v_slack = RULES["sla_min"]["P2"] - (now - v_cr.spec.arrival_t)
                    if not (p1_slack < 40.0 or (shock and v_slack > 120.0)):
                        continue
                rem_clock = r.current_end_t - now
                if policy == "P3_Hybrid" and rem_clock <= 10.0:
                    continue
                prio_rank = 0 if v_cr.spec.priority == "P3" else 1
                victims.append((prio_rank, -rem_clock, r))

            if not victims:
                continue
            victims.sort(key=lambda x: (x[0], x[1]))
            vr = victims[0][2]
            v_tk = vr.current_task
            v_cr = case_map[v_tk.case_id]
            v_mult = RESOURCES[vr.name]["mult"][v_tk.activity]

            # Account active work done on victim before preemption
            done_clock_total = work_done_between(vr.name, vr.current_start_t, now, allow_ot=vr.current_allow_ot)
            done_service_clock = work_done_between(vr.name, vr.current_service_start_t, now, allow_ot=vr.current_allow_ot)
            day1_add = work_done_between(vr.name, vr.current_start_t, min(now, 750.0), allow_ot=vr.current_allow_ot)
            vr.day1_active_min += day1_add
            v_cr.active_service_min += done_service_clock

            if record_events and now > vr.current_service_start_t:
                event_log.append({
                    "case:concept:name": v_tk.case_id,
                    "concept:name": v_tk.activity,
                    "lifecycle:transition": "suspend",
                    "start_timestamp": vr.current_service_start_t,
                    "time:timestamp": now,
                    "org:resource": vr.name,
                    "queue_entry_timestamp": v_tk.ready_t,
                    "skill_used": v_tk.activity,
                    "priority": v_cr.spec.priority,
                    "case_type": v_cr.spec.case_type,
                    "handover_delay_min": vr.current_handover_min,
                    "setup_min": vr.current_setup_min,
                    "preempted": True,
                    "policy_version": f"{policy}{'_Shock' if shock else ''}",
                    "scenario_flag": scenario_name,
                })

            done_base = min(v_tk.base_remaining, done_service_clock / v_mult)
            rem_base = max(0.5, v_tk.base_remaining - done_base)
            penalty_base = rem_base * RULES["preempt_restart_frac"]
            v_tk.base_remaining = rem_base + penalty_base
            v_tk.ready_t = now
            v_tk.preempted_flag = True
            v_cr.preempted_count += 1
            v_cr.preempt_penalty_min += penalty_base * v_mult

            # Free victim resource and return victim task to queue
            vr.busy = False
            vr.current_task = None
            vr.current_assign_id = 0
            ready_tasks.append(v_tk)

            # Immediately assign P1 task to freed resource
            ready_tasks.remove(tk)
            start_task(vr, tk, now, shock)

    def try_dispatch(now: float) -> None:
        shock = update_shock_mode(now)
        try_preempt(now, shock)
        while True:
            idle_res = [r for r in res_map.values() if not r.busy]
            if not idle_res or not ready_tasks:
                break
            best_score = -1e18
            best_pair: Optional[Tuple[ResourceState, TaskState]] = None
            for r in idle_res:
                for tk in ready_tasks:
                    if is_pair_eligible(r, tk, now, shock):
                        sc_val = pair_score(r, tk, now)
                        if sc_val > best_score:
                            best_score = sc_val
                            best_pair = (r, tk)
            if best_pair is None:
                break
            r_sel, tk_sel = best_pair
            ready_tasks.remove(tk_sel)
            start_task(r_sel, tk_sel, now, shock)

    def choose_diag_mode(cr: CaseRuntime, intake_res: str, now: float) -> str:
        acts = CASE_DIAG_ACTIVITIES[cr.spec.case_type]
        if len(acts) == 1:
            return "single"
        if policy == "P1_Spec":
            return "parallel"
        prio = cr.spec.priority
        slack = RULES["sla_min"][prio] - (now - cr.spec.arrival_t)
        a1, a2 = acts
        # Check availability of two distinct qualified resources
        idle_1 = [r.name for r in res_map.values() if not r.busy and RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][a1] is not None and is_in_shift(r.name, now, False)]
        idle_2 = [r.name for r in res_map.values() if not r.busy and RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][a2] is not None and is_in_shift(r.name, now, False)]
        two_avail = any(r1 != r2 for r1 in idle_1 for r2 in idle_2)
        if policy == "P2_Pool":
            return "parallel" if (prio == "P1" or two_avail) else "sequential"
        if policy == "P4_Fair":
            return "parallel" if (prio == "P1" or (prio == "P2" and slack < 180.0)) else "sequential"
        # P3_Hybrid threshold-based parallelization
        if prio == "P1":
            # Parallel if at least 2 distinct qualified resources are on shift
            on_1 = [r for r in active_res_names if RESOURCES[r]["mult"][a1] is not None and is_in_shift(r, now, True)]
            on_2 = [r for r in active_res_names if RESOURCES[r]["mult"][a2] is not None and is_in_shift(r, now, True)]
            return "parallel" if any(r1 != r2 for r1 in on_1 for r2 in on_2) else "sequential"
        if prio == "P2" and slack < 150.0:
            return "parallel"
        # Dual idle Tier-1 specialists exploit 1.0x speed advantage
        t1_idle_1 = [r for r in idle_1 if resource_tier(r, a1, absent, now) == 1]
        t1_idle_2 = [r for r in idle_2 if resource_tier(r, a2, absent, now) == 1]
        if any(r1 != r2 for r1 in t1_idle_1 for r2 in t1_idle_2) and len(ready_tasks) < 3:
            return "parallel"
        # Otherwise sequential if any cross-skilled resource is on shift
        cross_on = any(RESOURCES[r]["mult"][a1] is not None and RESOURCES[r]["mult"][a2] is not None
                       and is_in_shift(r, now, False) for r in active_res_names)
        return "sequential" if cross_on else "parallel"

    # Main event loop
    while events:
        now, _, etype, payload = heapq.heappop(events)
        if etype == "ARRIVAL":
            cid = str(payload)
            cr = case_map[cid]
            recent_arrivals.append(now)
            ready_t = now
            if sc["it_outage"] is not None and (sc["it_outage"][0] <= now < sc["it_outage"][1]):
                ready_t = sc["it_outage"][1]
            ready_tasks.append(TaskState(f"{cid}_Intake", cid, "Intake", ready_t, cr.spec.base_times["Intake"]))
            try_dispatch(now)

        elif etype in ("SHIFT_OPEN", "OUTAGE_END"):
            try_dispatch(now)

        elif etype == "TASK_READY":
            ready_tasks.append(payload)
            try_dispatch(now)

        elif etype == "TASK_COMPLETE":
            rname, aid, shock_used = payload
            r = res_map[rname]
            if (not r.busy) or (r.current_assign_id != aid):
                continue  # Stale completion from a preempted assignment
            tk = r.current_task
            cr = case_map[tk.case_id]
            active_srv = work_done_between(r.name, r.current_service_start_t, now, allow_ot=r.current_allow_ot)
            cr.active_service_min += active_srv
            day1_active = work_done_between(
                r.name,
                r.current_start_t if HANDOVER_CONSUMES_CAPACITY else r.current_start_t,
                min(now, 750.0),
                allow_ot=r.current_allow_ot,
            )
            # Exclude pure elapsed handover from server utilization when HANDOVER_CONSUMES_CAPACITY is False
            if not HANDOVER_CONSUMES_CAPACITY and r.current_handover_min > 0:
                ho_overlap = work_done_between(
                    r.name,
                    min(now, add_working_time(r.name, r.current_start_t, r.current_setup_min, r.current_allow_ot)),
                    min(now, r.current_service_start_t, 750.0),
                    allow_ot=r.current_allow_ot,
                )
                day1_active = max(0.0, day1_active - ho_overlap)
            r.day1_active_min += day1_active

            if record_events:
                event_log.append({
                    "case:concept:name": tk.case_id,
                    "concept:name": tk.activity,
                    "lifecycle:transition": "complete",
                    "start_timestamp": r.current_service_start_t,
                    "time:timestamp": now,
                    "org:resource": r.name,
                    "queue_entry_timestamp": tk.ready_t,
                    "skill_used": tk.activity,
                    "priority": cr.spec.priority,
                    "case_type": cr.spec.case_type,
                    "handover_delay_min": r.current_handover_min,
                    "setup_min": r.current_setup_min,
                    "preempted": tk.preempted_flag,
                    "policy_version": f"{policy}{'_Shock' if shock_used else ''}",
                    "scenario_flag": scenario_name,
                })

            if tk.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                r.last_diag_skill = tk.activity
            r.last_case_id = tk.case_id
            r.busy = False
            r.current_task = None
            r.current_assign_id = 0

            # Advance case workflow
            if tk.activity == "Intake":
                cr.diag_mode = choose_diag_mode(cr, r.name, now)
                acts = CASE_DIAG_ACTIVITIES[cr.spec.case_type]
                if cr.diag_mode == "parallel":
                    ready_tasks.append(TaskState(f"{cr.spec.case_id}_{acts[0]}", cr.spec.case_id, acts[0], now,
                                                 cr.spec.base_times[acts[0]], pred_res=r.name, pred_team=None, sibling_act=acts[1]))
                    ready_tasks.append(TaskState(f"{cr.spec.case_id}_{acts[1]}", cr.spec.case_id, acts[1], now,
                                                 cr.spec.base_times[acts[1]], pred_res=r.name, pred_team=None, sibling_act=acts[0]))
                else:
                    # Prefer starting the branch the Intake owner can also do (if cross-skilled)
                    first_act = acts[0]
                    if len(acts) == 2 and RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][acts[0]] is None and RESOURCES[r.name]["mult"][acts[1]] is not None:
                        first_act = acts[1]
                    ready_tasks.append(TaskState(f"{cr.spec.case_id}_{first_act}", cr.spec.case_id, first_act, now,
                                                 cr.spec.base_times[first_act], pred_res=r.name, pred_team=None))

            elif tk.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                cr.diag_done[tk.activity] = (now, r.name)
                acts = CASE_DIAG_ACTIVITIES[cr.spec.case_type]
                if cr.diag_mode == "sequential" and len(cr.diag_done) < len(acts):
                    next_act = [a for a in acts if a not in cr.diag_done][0]
                    ready_tasks.append(TaskState(f"{cr.spec.case_id}_{next_act}", cr.spec.case_id, next_act, now,
                                                 cr.spec.base_times[next_act], pred_res=r.name,
                                                 pred_team=ACTIVITY_TEAM[tk.activity]))
                elif len(cr.diag_done) == len(acts):
                    if cr.diag_mode == "parallel":
                        # Check if branches actually used distinct resources
                        distinct_res = len({res_u for _, res_u in cr.diag_done.values()}) > 1
                        coord = RULES["parallel_coord_min"] if distinct_res else 0.0
                        cr.coord_min += coord
                        val_ready = now + coord
                        push_event(val_ready, "TASK_READY",
                                   TaskState(f"{cr.spec.case_id}_Validation", cr.spec.case_id, "Validation",
                                             val_ready, cr.spec.base_times["Validation"],
                                             pred_res=r.name, pred_team=ACTIVITY_TEAM[tk.activity]))
                    else:
                        ready_tasks.append(TaskState(f"{cr.spec.case_id}_Validation", cr.spec.case_id, "Validation",
                                                     now, cr.spec.base_times["Validation"],
                                                     pred_res=r.name, pred_team=ACTIVITY_TEAM[tk.activity]))

            elif tk.activity == "Validation":
                cr.complete_t = now

            try_dispatch(now)

    # Aggregate run metrics
    tats, delays, p1_ok, p2_ok, p3_ok, p1_tats = [], [], [], [], [], []
    frictions = []
    for cr in case_map.values():
        tat = (cr.complete_t - cr.spec.arrival_t) if cr.complete_t is not None else np.nan
        non_srv = max(0.0, tat - cr.active_service_min)
        tats.append(tat)
        delays.append(non_srv)
        frictions.append(cr.handover_min + cr.setup_min + cr.coord_min + cr.preempt_penalty_min)
        ok = tat <= RULES["sla_min"][cr.spec.priority]
        if cr.spec.priority == "P1":
            p1_ok.append(ok)
            p1_tats.append(tat)
        elif cr.spec.priority == "P2":
            p2_ok.append(ok)
        else:
            p3_ok.append(ok)

    utils = [res_map[r].day1_active_min / regular_shift_minutes(r) for r in active_res_names]
    metrics = {
        "mean_delay": float(np.mean(delays)),
        "p90_delay": float(np.percentile(delays, 90)),
        "p95_P1_TAT": float(np.percentile(p1_tats, 95)) if p1_tats else np.nan,
        "P1_SLA": 100.0 * float(np.mean(p1_ok)) if p1_ok else np.nan,
        "P2_SLA": 100.0 * float(np.mean(p2_ok)) if p2_ok else np.nan,
        "P3_SLA": 100.0 * float(np.mean(p3_ok)) if p3_ok else np.nan,
        "max_util_pct": 100.0 * float(np.max(utils)),
        "util_spread_pp": 100.0 * float(np.max(utils) - np.min(utils)),
        "friction_min": float(np.mean(frictions)),
    }
    return metrics, event_log

def run_pm4py_audit(raw_log_df: pd.DataFrame) -> None:

    )
    act_srv_min = pd.Series({k: v / 60.0 for k, v in act_srv_sec.items()}, name="pm4py_mean_service_min")
    print("\nPM4Py Activity Mean Service Time (minutes, split segments prevent pause inflation):")
    print(act_srv_min.round(2).to_string())

    # 3. Handover-of-work network (Note: includes apparent edges from concurrent ME/ES branches)

        fmt_df,
        resource_key="org:resource",
        timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
        case_id_key="case:concept:name",
    )
    top_edges = sorted(how_net.connections.items(), key=lambda kv: -kv[1])[:8]

    for (r1, r2), w in top_edges:
        print(f"  {r1:>5s} -> {r2:<5s} : {w:.4f}")

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## 9.1 What cannot be concluded from expected arrivals and service ranges alone

1. **Multi-day steady-state stability:** Because Base daily offered load (`2,721` base work-min) exceeds regular shift capacity (`2,490` clock-min), single-day analysis cannot confirm long-run P3 SLA stability; without capacity additions or demand reduction, multi-day P3 carryover will grow across consecutive business days.
2. **Arrival clustering and priority-complexity correlation:** Expected block counts hide micro-bursts (e.g., an outage generating 4 simultaneous `ES` P1 tickets at `14:05`) and potential correlation between P1 severity and complex-tail length.
3. **Cross-branch tail correlation and rework loops:** In real incidents, if the Electrical branch of an `ES` case hits the complex tail, the Software branch is often complex too; additionally, Validation failures that loop back to diagnostics (rework) are unobserved in the supplied table and could heavily penalize aggressive pooling if secondary-skill error rates are higher.
4. **True capacity cost of handovers and coordination (Interpretation A vs. B):** The prompt does not distinguish pure transfer waiting from effort-consuming briefing/merge time. If handovers consume technician time, effective capacity drops further and the value of `P3-Hybrid`'s case-retention rule grows even larger.
5. **Exact U2 shock definition (2A vs. 2B) and behavioral compliance:** Whether an E/S incident adds incremental volume (2A) or merely shifts case mix (2B) changes afternoon load by >45 percentage points. Similarly, human behavior—informal break flexibility, voluntary overtime willingness, cognitive fatigue after preemption, and dispatch override rates—cannot be inferred from static tables.

## 9.2 Safe, staged pilot and validation plan

A direct full rollout risks violating P1 SLAs or triggering technician burnout. We recommend a **four-stage validation protocol** that discriminates between `P3-Hybrid` and the status quo (or `P2-Pool`) while strictly protecting P1 incidents:

1. **Stage 1 — Historical Event-Log Backtest & Parameter Calibration (Weeks 1–2):**
   - Extract 8–12 weeks of historical incident logs matching the Section 7 schema.
   - Empirically estimate: (a) intra-block arrival overdispersion (variance-to-mean ratio), (b) actual `resource × activity` duration distributions and Validation rework rates, (c) whether handover/coordination lags block technician availability (resolving Interpretation A vs. B), and (d) cross-branch complexity correlation on `ME`/`ES` cases.
   - Replay historical arrival streams through the calibrated Section 8 DES to verify that `P3-Hybrid`'s advantage persists under empirical distributions.
2. **Stage 2 — Shadow-Mode ("Dark Launch") Validation (Weeks 3–4, Zero Operational Risk):**
   - Run the `P3-Hybrid` dispatch engine in read-only recommendation mode alongside current dispatching.
   - Measure: agreement rate with human dispatchers, frequency of `P3-Shock` trigger firing, simulated counterfactual P1 slack margin, and qualitative operator feedback on rule clarity (tuning tier/reservation thresholds before live use).
3. **Stage 3 — Stratified Switchback Canary Pilot with Hard P1 Circuit-Breaker (Weeks 5–10):**
   - Alternate `P3-Hybrid` vs. baseline (`P1-Spec` or current practice) in **2-day blocks stratified by day-of-week** (limiting carryover contamination via a 1-day washout or controlling for opening WIP at `08:00`).
   - *Sample-size reality check:* With ~`5.4` P1 cases/day, binary P1 SLA attainment has wide sampling noise (~`±6–8 pp` over 2 weeks). Therefore, power the pilot primarily on **continuous leading indicators**—P1 diagnostic queue wait, P1 SLA slack at completion, `ME`/`ES` turnaround, handover minutes/case, and `U_max - U_min` fairness—requiring ~15–20 pilot days per arm for 80% power on a 10-minute P1 latency reduction.
   - **P1 Safety Circuit-Breaker:** Automatically pause the pilot arm (reverting to supervisor override / `P3-Shock`) if: (a) two consecutive P1 cases exceed 100 elapsed minutes before Validation start, (b) rolling 5-day P1 SLA drops below `88%`, or (c) any technician exceeds `95%` utilization for two consecutive shifts.
4. **Stage 4 — Go/No-Go Decision & Parallel Staffing Remediation (Week 11):**
   - **Promote `P3-Hybrid + P3-Shock` to permanent production** if it improves P1 p95 turnaround and handover burden without degrading Validation first-pass yield, P2/P3 carryover, or operator-reported workload fairness.
   - Simultaneously present the pilot's measured Day-1 utilization and post-`18:00` Mechanical stall logs to leadership to justify closing the **structural daily capacity deficit** and the **`18:00` Mechanical coverage gap**.
