import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

register = Transition(label="Register Change")
assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()
optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[legal_review, skip_legal])

configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[trial_body, correct_defect])

release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[
    register, assess_risk, prepare_evidence, optional_legal,
    verification_loop, release_change, archive_change
])
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)

def get_petri(source):

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

BPMN rendering can mislead even with a behaviorally correct net:

* Parallel gateways for Assess Risk / Prepare Evidence may be drawn sequentially due to layout heuristics.
* The absence of an edge between Optional Legal Review and Verification Loop is often rendered as a sequential flow, suggesting ordering.
* Silent XOR for Legal Review becomes an exclusive gateway with an invisible path; stakeholders may assume Legal Review is mandatory.
* The LOOP over `trial_body` + `correct_defect` is rendered as a standard loop marker; the minimum-one-iteration requirement and the exact scope of the redo are not visually explicit.
* Routing nodes are collapsed, hiding the join before Release Change; the diagram may appear to allow Release after only one predecessor.

Mitigations: annotate gateways with optionality, add documentation of partial order, label silent branches, and provide a textual invariant table alongside the diagram.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

* Net is not a workflow net.
* Soundness check fails or diagnostics report dead transitions/places.
* Final marking test fails for a positive witness trace with exact marking equality.
* Negative witness trace is enabled within the bound.
* Optional Legal Review becomes mandatory or is lost after BPMN round-trip.
* Release Change is enabled before both Verification Loop completion and Optional Legal branch completion.
* Process tree conversion succeeds but footprint differs from POWLPetri baseline.
* BPMN round-tripped net has different visible language up to the chosen bound.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

* Authoritative storage: POWL model as source of truth, with the POWLPetri net as the canonical executable semantics.
* Stakeholder visualization: BPMN derived from the Petri net, not from an intermediate process tree.
* Derivative artifacts must be accompanied by soundness report, footprint comparison, and bounded witness trace results.
* Process-tree intermediate should be disallowed for this model; failure is informative of non-block-structured behavior.
* Future edits must be made in POWL and re-validated through the POWLPetriBPMN pipeline; BPMN edits must not be round-tripped back without re-baselining against POWL.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

* Whether PM4Py’s LOOP operator implements the intended “body then redo” semantics with at least one iteration and the exact placement of Correct Defect cannot be established without formal proof.
* Equivalence of true concurrency in the source partial order vs interleaving in the Petri net is undecidable in general and only approximated by bounded exploration.
* Visual interpretation of BPMN gateways by humans cannot be validated by automated checks; layout-induced ordering bias remains.
