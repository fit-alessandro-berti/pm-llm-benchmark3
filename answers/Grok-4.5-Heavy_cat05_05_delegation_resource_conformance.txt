## 2. Rule Interpretation

**RR1 — Drafter/approver separation.** After alias normalization to canonical human identity, the human who performs Draft_Change_Request must not perform Approve_Change on the same case. Technical SoD nonconformance is independent of risk level.

**RR2 — Implementer/verifier separation.** After alias normalization, the human who performs Implement_Change must not perform Verify_Implementation on the same case. Service-account actions are excluded from automatic SoD until reliable session evidence maps them to a specific human; only then is the check applied. Unmapped or weakly attributed service actions remain DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM.

**RR3 — High-risk approval authority.** For HIGH-risk cases, Approve_Change must be performed by the standing Quality Manager or a valid QM delegate. A delegation is valid only when (1) its effective interval fully covers the event timestamp, (2) its scope matches the change, (3) the principal-absence condition (where stated) is met by contemporaneous evidence, and (4) no prohibited subdelegation occurred. Standing roles are taken at event time. A later database migration entry cannot prove earlier existence of a delegation; a contemporaneous signed PDF timestamped before the interval can. Migration lag is an audit-trail weakness but does not automatically invalidate signed pre-dated evidence.

**RR4 — Emergency substitution.** A HIGH-risk emergency change (flag true + credible safety rationale) may be implemented after approval by the Incident Commander or a valid IC delegate. A QM or valid QM delegate must perform Retrospective_QM_Review within two business days. Business days are Monday–Friday excluding site holidays (none present). “Within two business days” is interpreted as completion by end-of-day of the second subsequent business day after the calendar day of Implement_Change (e.g., Implement on Tuesday → deadline end of Thursday). Failure of either the IC-path authority or the timely QM retrospective means the justified exception is incomplete and the case is nonconforming for the control objective.

**Principal acting while on leave.** A principal retains standing authority for his or her own acts; leave does not suspend the role. Supporting session evidence (VPN) strengthens identity attribution; residual credential-sharing risk is recorded as a secondary identity caveat only.

**Later database entry.** Cannot prove earlier creation of a delegation. Contemporaneous signed evidence can. Migration lag creates an audit weakness but does not retroactively invalidate a signed, pre-dated instrument.

**RR5 — Identity evidence.** A service account is not a human identity. It can support conformance only when reliable session evidence maps the action to an authorized human. Known aliases of the same human must be normalized before SoD checks.

**RR6 — Suspicion versus proof.** Repeated resource pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, concentrated handovers and absence/credential conflicts are indicators only. They do not constitute proof of collusion without supporting evidence.

## 4. Collusion and Alternative-Explanation Analysis

Repeated Clara-draft / Ben-approve pairs (18 of 23 prior HIGH production changes) are an organizational-pattern indicator under RR6. Benign explanation: both assigned to the same production cell, informal efficiency, absence of a formal pair-rotation policy. Adverse explanation: rubber-stamping or collusion. Discriminating evidence would include independent re-review of a sample of the 18 cases for quality of technical content, financial or access-privilege motive analysis, and comparison of approval dwell times against independent QM baselines.

Reciprocal Ben-draft / Clara-approve on LOW training changes is a weaker indicator. Benign: mutual convenience on low-risk documents. Adverse: reciprocal favor trading. Discriminate by sampling the four cases for content quality and checking whether either party gained non-work benefit.

Shared workstation WS-DM-04 for CHG-503 svc_qms: benign multi-user terminal in a maintenance area; adverse Diego verifying his own implementation. Discriminate with CCTV (unavailable), keystroke or session-binding logs, and badge timestamps relative to the exact 12:30 action.

Absence/credential conflicts (Asha VPN while on leave; Ben account activity during certified sick leave with denial and office IP): benign residual personal use by the named individual; adverse credential sharing or compromise. Discriminate by deeper forensic examination of the accounts (login source, concurrent sessions, device certificates) and corroborating statements under controlled conditions.

No direct financial benefit is known for any pair. Per RR6, repeated collaboration alone never proves collusion.

## 6. Control Improvements

- **Delegation creation:** Require contemporaneous controlled-database entry plus digital signature before the effective interval begins; reject email-only instruments; enforce no-subdelegation systemically; auto-expire at interval end.
- **Effective-time recording:** Immutable creation timestamps; prohibit migration lag without an auditable compensating control; link signed PDF hash to the QMS record at creation.
- **Principal availability:** Real-time integration of leave/calendar systems into the QMS approval gate; alert when a principal acts while marked absent or when a required absence condition is not satisfied.
- **Alias management:** Enforce a single canonical identity at the identity-provider level; prevent secondary aliases from appearing in process logs.
- **Individual authentication:** Mandate FIDO or equivalent re-authentication for every Approve_Change, Verify_Implementation and Retrospective_QM_Review; bind service-account sessions to badge + dedicated station; eliminate shared terminals for high-risk actions.
- **Emergency review:** System-enforced two-business-day timer with escalating notifications; require the safety rationale attachment before the emergency path can be closed.
- **Pair rotation:** Formal policy plus automated monitoring of repeated resource pairs above a threshold; force an alternate approver after N consecutive HIGH cases from the same pair.
- **Network monitoring:** Continuous extraction of handover and working-together metrics with anomaly alerts (self-SoD attempts, account activity on certified leave, concentrated handovers).
- **Evidential retention:** Retain badge, FIDO, IP, session and CCTV records for at least 90 days in forensic-ready form; bind signed delegation artefacts immutably to each case record.
