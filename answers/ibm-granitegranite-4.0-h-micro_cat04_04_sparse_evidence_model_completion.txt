# 2. Shared Assumptions

1. **Event-completion semantics**: Each trace represents a complete production order completion.
2. **Missing Supplier Confirmation events**: Around 5–10% of Supplier Confirmation events may be missing due to an integration problem (S5). Models will account for optional Supplier Confirmation.
3. **External/stocked attribute**: Not available in the data; assumed as missing (S4). Models will handle this abstraction.
4. **Optionality representation**: Expedite Review is treated as optional, occurring at most once (D6). Models will reflect this in their structure.
5. **Timestamp ordering**: Only completion timestamps are available; actual execution order remains unknown (D7). Models will avoid assuming concurrency based on timestamps alone.

# 3. Conservative Candidate

## Inductive Bias
The conservative model prioritizes adherence to high-confidence domain rules and observed variants, avoiding any behavior not explicitly supported by the data.

## Formal Expression (ProcessTree)
```python
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator, OperatorPOWL, ProcessTree

# Define the process tree structure based on observed variants and high-confidence rules
model_conservative = Operator("Register Order")
model_conservative.add_transition(Operator("Credit Check"))
model_conservative.add_transition(Operator("Engineering Review"))
model_conservative.add_transition(Operator("Supplier Confirmation"))
model_conservative.add_transition(Operator("Approve Configuration"))
model_conservative.add_transition(Operator("Release Production"))
model_conservative.add_transition(Operator("Archive Case"))

# Enforce high-confidence rules
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Register Order", "Credit Check"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Register Order", "Engineering Review"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Credit Check", "Engineering Review"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Credit Check", "Supplier Confirmation"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Engineering Review", "Approve Configuration"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Supplier Confirmation", "Approve Configuration"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Approve Configuration", "Release Production"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Release Production", "Archive Case"))
```

## Behaviors Excluded
- Any trace that includes Expedite Review not preceded by both Credit Check and Engineering Review.
- Traces where Supplier Confirmation occurs after Approve Configuration.
- Models do not admit any concurrency between Credit Check and Engineering Review.

## Known Underfitting Risks
- The model may underfit scenarios where Expedite Review is necessary but not observed.
- It may not capture cases where Supplier Confirmation is missing or occurs after Approve Configuration.

# 4. Balanced Candidate

## Inductive Bias
The balanced model aims to generalize the observed behavior while explicitly managing uncertainty, encoding the most plausible generalizations.

## Formal Expression (POWL)
```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree

# Define the process as a strict partial order to allow concurrency
model_balanced = StrictPartialOrder()

# Add edges based on observed variants and domain rules
model_balanced.order.add_edge(("Register Order", "Credit Check"))
model_balanced.order.add_edge(("Register Order", "Engineering Review"))
model_balanced.order.add_edge(("Credit Check", "Engineering Review"))
model_balanced.order.add_edge(("Credit Check", "Supplier Confirmation"))
model_balanced.order.add_edge(("Engineering Review", "Approve Configuration"))
model_balanced.order.add_edge(("Supplier Confirmation", "Approve Configuration"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Approve Configuration", "Release Production"))
model_conservative.add_constraint(("Release Production", "Archive Case"))
```

## Handling Credit Check, Engineering Review, Supplier Confirmation, and Expedite Review
- **Credit Check and Engineering Review**: Must be completed before Approve Configuration (D3).
- **Supplier Confirmation**: Can occur concurrently with Credit Check or Engineering Review (D5). Models allow this concurrency.
- **Expedite Review**: Optional and must follow both Credit Check and Engineering Review (D6). Models represent this as an optional transition.

## Decision Process
- **Sequence**: Enforced for D1, D2, D4, and D5 (high-confidence rules).
- **Concurrency**: Allowed for D5 (low-to-medium confidence).
- **Partial order**: Managed by allowing optional transitions for Expedite Review.

# 5. Permissive Candidate

## Inductive Bias
The permissive model admits reasonable but unobserved combinations, broadening the scope to include plausible but not strictly observed behaviors.

## Formal Expression (POWL)
```python
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import ProcessTree

# Define the process as a strict partial order with additional permissive rules
model_permissive = StrictPartialOrder()

# Add edges based on observed variants and domain rules
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Register Order", "Credit Check"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Register Order", "Engineering Review"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Credit Check", "Engineering Review"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Credit Check", "Supplier Confirmation"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Engineering Review", "Approve Configuration"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Supplier Confirmation", "Approve Configuration"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Approve Configuration", "Release Production"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Release Production", "Archive Case"))

# Allow additional permissive behaviors
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Credit Check", "Expedite Review"))
model_permissive.order.add_edge(("Engineering Review", "Expedite Review"))
```

## Boundary Between Reasonable Generalization and Forbidden Behavior
- **Reasonable**: Enables Expedite Review as an optional activity and allows concurrency between Credit Check and Engineering Review.
- **Forbidden**: Does not admit behaviors contradicting high-confidence rules (e.g., Supplier Confirmation after Approve Configuration).

# 9. Recommendation and Data-Collection Plan

## Recommended Model
- **Balanced Candidate (`model_balanced`)**: This model offers a balanced approach, generalizing the observed behavior while managing uncertainty effectively. It provides a middle ground between strict compliance and broad permissiveness, making it suitable for both compliance and exploration purposes.

## Sensitivity Analysis Model
- **Permissive Candidate (`model_permissive`)**: Use this model for sensitivity analysis to understand the impact of admitting optional behaviors like Expedite Review and concurrent Credit Check/Engineering Review.

## Data Collection Plan
1. **Collect timestamps** for each activity (if available) to better understand the actual execution order.
2. **Add event logs** that include:
   - Detailed timestamps for each activity.
   - Information on whether Supplier Confirmation is missing.
   - Additional interleavings that could reveal concurrency patterns.
3. **Focus on**:
   - Cases where Expedite Review is triggered.
   - Scenarios where Supplier Confirmation is missing.
   - Interleavings that could clarify the concurrency between Credit Check and Engineering Review.

## Unobserved Behavior Audit
- **Admitted Behaviors**:
  - **Permissive model** admits traces where Expedite Review is included without the prerequisite Credit Check and Engineering Review.
  - **Balanced model** admits concurrency between Credit Check and Engineering Review.

## Alternative Interpretations
- **Conservative**: May miss opportunities to optimize production flow due to strict adherence.
- **Permissive**: Might lead to unnecessary complexity and compliance risk due to broad generalizations.

# 10. Uncertainty Statement

- **Model Bias**: The conservative model may be overly conservative, potentially missing out on optimizing production processes due to its rigidity.
- **Permissive Model**: While more permissive, it may introduce unnecessary compliance risk if not properly validated against operational constraints.
- **Balanced Model**: Represents a pragmatic compromise but still carries uncertainty regarding the exact nature of concurrency and optionality.

A calibrated confidence assessment for each major modeling decision would be necessary to highlight that alternative interpretations could materially alter the recommended approach, especially concerning the handling of Expedite Review and concurrency between Credit Check and Engineering Review.
