## 3. Resilience versus concealed noncompliance

In regulated pharmaceutical distribution, **true resilience** is the capacity to sustain critical patient supply *while preserving the minimum non-negotiable safety, security, and traceability invariants* required by law and clinical safety. **Concealed noncompliance** occurs when teams preserve throughput and surface-level cycle-time metrics by silently dropping preventive controls or deferring evidence capture into an invisible post-hoc backlog—creating the illusion of recovery while accumulating regulatory, recall, and patient-safety liability.

### Categorization of Observed Adaptations

1. **True Resilience (Mission-Aligned, Control-Conscious Adaptations)**
   - **B (Manual authorization check)**, **F (Temporary release control)**, **J (Backlog prioritization)**, and **K (Reconcile manual reservation)** reflect genuine resilience intent: teams deliberately triaged scarce human expertise toward high-consequence risks (controlled substances, critical hospitals, stock integrity).
   - *Limitation:* Even resilient adaptations degraded when under-resourced—**B** saturated compliance staff, **F** left cold-chain orders exposed to sampling gaps, and **K** occurred too late (T4 instead of T3).

2. **Risky Shortcuts & Concealed Noncompliance**
   - **C (Skip system authorization check)** and **G (Dispatch without scan)** are clear shortcuts that traded mandatory regulatory controls for dock velocity. Dropping authorization checks and outbound scans caused T2 traceability exceptions to jump 12× (**0.8%  9.5%**) and left **650** T2 dispatches without matched `Phone proof of delivery`.
   - **L (Audit remediation)** exemplifies **concealed noncompliance**. By T4, Hospital-critical median cycle time returned to **8h** and model fitness rose to **0.90**, making operations look healthy. Yet **2,300 cases** entered `Audit remediation` *after* `Invoice close` (with **200** still open at Day 21). Reconstructing temperature or lot records weeks after delivery cannot mitigate a spoiled cold-chain biologic or enable rapid Class I recall execution; it merely papers over an already incurred risk.

3. **Ambiguous Adaptations (Dependent on Risk Appetite and Guardrails)**
   - **A (Hospital-critical fast lane)**: High clinical value (**91%** hospital fill rate), but ambiguous/unacceptable when it bypassed authorization checks for 1,000 orders. Acceptable *only* if paired with a lightweight offline authorization check and post-pick controlled/cold-chain verification.
   - **D (Manual inventory snapshot)** and **E (Manual pick list)**: Necessary physical bridges during WMS downtime, but **D** turns into operational self-sabotage once morning stock counts go stale, and **E** becomes concealed traceability failure unless lot/expiry data are captured contemporaneously at the bin/pack station.
   - **H (Phone proof of delivery)**: Acceptable risk-based fallback for low-risk **Ambient** deliveries to known pharmacies, but noncompliant for **Controlled-substance** chain-of-custody.
   - **I (Defer noncritical order)**: Strategically sound under severe capacity loss (protecting hospitals over bulk stock-ups), but ethically and commercially damaging when Pharmacy-standard fill rates dropped to **61%** and **33%** of queued backlog orders cancelled. Risk appetite determines the maximum tolerable deferral window before community pharmacies experience patient-access harm.

## 4. Recovery process model

To prevent improvised shortcuts in future cyber or WMS outages, the recovery workflow should operate as a **pre-authorized, risk-tiered Degradation Mode** with hard minimum control gates.

### Formal Control-Flow Notation (POWL / Process-Tree Style)

*Notation legend:* `(X, Y)` = strict sequence; `×(X, Y)` = exclusive choice (XOR); `(X, Y)` = parallel execution (AND); `(X, R)` = structured loop (execute `X`, optionally repeat via `R`); `[G]` = mandatory blocking gate before progression.

```text
RecoveryWorkflow :=
  (
    TriggerDegradationMode,
    TriageAndSegment(Order),
    ×(
      // Path 1: Deferred Bulk / Low-Urgency
      ( DeferWithSLANotice, EscalateOnAgeCap, ReEnterIntake ),
      // Path 2: Active Fulfillment (Hospital-Critical & Protected Pharmacy)
      (
        ×( OfflineWhitelistAuth, RapidComplianceCallAuth ),      // Mandatory Auth Gate [G1]
        RollingShiftStockCheck,                                  // 4h-refreshed ledger
        ( ManualPickWithLotStickerOrPhoto, OfflinePackVerify ), // Mandatory Lot Gate [G2]
        RiskBasedReleaseGate,                                    // [G3]: 100% CS/CC/Recall; sample Ambient
        ( OfflineScanOrManifestPhotoDispatch, CarrierDualSignOff ), // [G4]: No unlogged dispatch
        ×( SignedPaperOrPhotoPoD, PhonePoD_AmbientOnly ),        // [G5]: Segment-specific PoD
        PreCloseCompletenessGate                                 // [G6]: Blocks Invoice Close if G1-G5 missing
      )
    ),
    ( RollingInventoryAndScanReconciliation, PartialSystemRestore ), // Runs continuously from T3
    ScopedAdminRemediation                                       // Only minor billing/non-safety gaps
  )
```

### Detailed Model Components

1. **Explicit Trigger Conditions**
   - **Enter Degradation Mode (T1  T2):** WMS, ERP authorization, or RF scanning unavailable for **>30 minutes**, OR queue latency exceeds **2× baseline p90** without restoration ETA **<2 hours**. Formally declared by Incident Commander + Responsible Pharmacist (RP)/QA Lead.
   - **Transition to Hybrid Recovery (T3):** Order intake restored, but inventory sync error rate **>1%** or scanner drop rate **>5%**. Activate rolling reconciliation loop.
   - **Exit to Normal Operations (T4  T0):** Full system availability **>24 hours**, inventory variance **<0.5%**, scanner sync **>99%**, and **0** unverified controlled-substance/cold-chain dispatches in queue.

2. **Triage Rules (Intake & Capacity Allocation)**
   - **Tier 1 (Immediate Fast Lane):** **Hospital-critical** orders and emergency **Controlled-substance / Cold-chain** clinical requests  reserved **50–60%** of manual pick/pack capacity.
   - **Tier 2 (Protected Continuity):** **Pharmacy-standard** essential/chronic prescriptions  reserved **30–40%** capacity; never starved below **80%** fill-rate floor.
   - **Tier 3 (Controlled Deferral):** **Wholesale-bulk** and routine **Ambient** restocks  deferred into timestamped backlog with automated customer ETA notification.

3. **Minimum Non-Negotiable Controls by Product & Customer Risk**
   - **Controlled-substance (Any Customer):** Offline license/DEA verification (`[G1]`) + dual-control manual sign-off at pick/pack + 100% pharmacist release (`[G3]`) + dual-signature dispatch manifest (`[G4]`) + signed physical/photo PoD with receiver ID (`[G5]`). *Phone PoD and skipped auth strictly prohibited.*
   - **Cold-chain (Any Customer):** Validated pack-out verification + physical/digital temperature-logger serial recording and activation check by QA (`[G3]`) + lot/expiry capture (`[G2]`) + courier transit-window lock (`[G4]`).
   - **Recall-sensitive (Any Product/Customer):** Mandatory lot number and expiry capture at pick/pack via peel-off barcode sheet or handheld offline scanner/photo (`[G2]`) + outbound manifest link (`[G4]`).
   - **Ambient (Hospital/Pharmacy/Bulk):** Pre-cached offline customer eligibility check (`[G1]`); **10%** random QA sampling (`[G3]`); manifest photo at dispatch (`[G4]`); Phone PoD permitted temporarily (`[G5]`) with 48h follow-up.

4. **Manual Evidence Capture**
   - Deploy pre-staged **Offline Continuity Kits** per zone: daily encrypted CSV snapshots of authorized accounts/licenses (refreshed every night at 02:00), pre-printed lot/serial sticker sheets, offline battery barcode scanners (batch-mode USB upload), and dedicated dock tablets/cameras for manifest and signed-PoD photography.
   - Replace static morning stock printouts with **Shift Stock Tally Boards / Shared Offline Spreadsheets** decremented every **4 hours** at pick-wave close.

5. **Backlog Prioritization**
   - Use a deterministic weighted score during T3–T4: `Priority = (Customer_Tier_Weight × Product_Clinical_Risk) + Aging_Escalation`.
   - **Hard Anti-Starvation Cap:** Any **Pharmacy-standard** order aged **>36 hours** automatically elevates to Tier 1 priority; any **Wholesale-bulk** order aged **>72 hours** receives dedicated overnight recovery waves before new bulk intake.

6. **Reconciliation and Audit-Remediation Flow**
   - **Pre-Close Gate (`[G6]`):** `Invoice close` is blocked for any order missing `[G1]`–`[G5]` evidence, forcing immediate exception resolution while physical memories/couriers are accessible (within 24h), rather than hiding in a post-close T4 queue.
   - **Rolling Reconciliation (T3 onward):** Every 6 hours during partial recovery, batch-upload offline scanner logs, reconcile 4-hour stock tallies against WMS via cycle counts of fast-moving/high-value bins, and trigger immediate customer re-confirmation before cancelling aged orders.
   - **Scoped Remediation:** Post-close `Audit remediation` is restricted strictly to commercial/billing anomalies (e.g., PO number mismatches), never missing temperature, lot, or controlled-substance custody records.

## 7. Management trade-off statement

Management should explicitly **accept** modest, bounded throughput degradation during an outage—such as **10–12h** Hospital-critical median cycle times, temporary **Wholesale-bulk** deferrals, and delayed commercial invoicing—when required to sustain **100%** real-time authorization, cold-chain temperature verification, lot traceability, and controlled-substance dual control; conversely, management must **not accept** unlogged dispatches, skipped eligibility checks, verbal-only controlled-substance PoDs, severe community-pharmacy starvation (**61%** fill rate), or declaring operational recovery while **2,300** cases sit in post-close `Audit remediation`. This policy posture should only be relaxed if written regulatory emergency waivers (e.g., from national health or drug-enforcement authorities during a declared public-health crisis) explicitly authorize temporary recordkeeping exemptions for life-saving hospital shipments, or if verified offline telemetry proves that automated backup capture achieves **<1%** traceability and cold-chain exception rates without slowing critical hospital dispatch.
