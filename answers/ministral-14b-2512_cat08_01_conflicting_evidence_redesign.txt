# **1. Current-Process Diagnosis**

### **Core Problems**
The process exhibits a **fundamental trade-off between speed/cost and quality/compliance**, with no single "best" path. The most frequent path (Auto eligibility  Fast approval  Dispatch) is **fast (median 0.5 + 1.0 + 3.0 = 4.5 hours) and cheap (EUR 0.20 auto-screen cost)**, but suffers from:
1. **High rework and compliance violations**:
   - **14.1% complaint/reopen rate** for Fast approval cases (vs. 3.3% for Repair  Quality check).
   - **Frequent move-on-log deviations** (e.g., Fast approval before evidence completeness, violating **R2** for high-value devices).
   - **Low log fitness (68%)** and precision, indicating many traces violate normative rules.
2. **Segment mismatches**:
   - **Premium high-value devices** on the Fast path have **22% complaint/reopen** (vs. 5% for reviewed/repair), with **high compliance risk** (R1/R2 violations).
   - **Unclear defect/missing evidence cases (22% of volume)** have **median 140-hour cycle time**, high abandonment, and repeated evidence requests.
3. **Bottleneck misalignment**:
   - **Manual eligibility review** is underutilized for low-risk cases but overloaded for high-value ones (queue grows at >82% utilization).
   - **Fraud/risk review** is a bottleneck on Mondays and post-campaigns, yet **only 400 cases/week** use it (vs. 3,600 auto-screened).
4. **Downstream cost hidden in "fast" paths**:
   - Fast approval is **31% cheaper before rework** but only **9% cheaper after** accounting for complaints, reshipments (EUR 95–410 per error), and service credits.
5. **Customer experience fragmentation**:
   - **Premium customers** expect <24-hour completion but get **46-hour median** on manual paths.
   - **Standard customers** tolerate 72 hours if status visibility is good, but **Fast-path errors** (e.g., wrong replacements) erode trust.

### **Key Process-Mining Artifacts Highlighting Contradictions**
1. **Frequency DFG**:
   - **Auto eligibility  Fast approval (2,550 cases)** dominates, but **Fast approval  Customer complaint/reopen (140 cases)** and **Dispatch  Customer complaint/reopen (260 cases)** reveal hidden costs.
2. **Performance DFG (p90 times)**:
   - **Fast approval  Dispatch (p90 = 8 hours)** vs. **Manual  Repair quote (p90 = 60 hours)** shows speed, but **Dispatch  Customer complaint/reopen (p90 = 180 hours)** exposes long-tail pain.
3. **Conformance deviations**:
   - **Fast approval before evidence completeness** (violating **R2**) occurs frequently, especially for high-value devices.
4. **Rework statistics**:
   - **26% of cases** loop through **Request missing evidence 2 times**, with **12.5% rework for high-value repairs**.
5. **Customer segment outcomes**:
   - **Premium high-value devices** on Fast path have **22% complaint/reopen** vs. **5% on reviewed/repair**, despite slower median (20h vs. 96h).

# **2. Redesign A: "Segmented Fast-Track with Pre-Dispatch Guardrails"**
### **Targeted Changes**
- **Segments**: Premium low-risk accessories, Standard low-risk accessories, **and high-value devices with clear evidence**.
- **Paths Modified**:
  - **Auto eligibility  Fast approval  Dispatch** (for low-risk, evidence-complete cases).
  - **New "Express Review" path** for high-value devices with auto-approved evidence (bypassing Fraud/risk but adding a **lightweight pre-dispatch quality gate**).
- **Activities Added**:
  - **Evidence completeness pre-check** (auto-screen enhanced with segment-aware rules).
  - **6-hour pre-dispatch quality gate** (for high-value devices) to catch wrong replacements (cost: EUR 0.50–1.50 per case).

### **Mechanism of Improvement**
1. **Reduce Fast-path rework**:
   - **Update auto-screen rules** to flag high-value devices needing **Express Review** (not Fraud/risk, but with pre-dispatch check).
   - **Block Fast approval for high-value devices without evidence** (enforcing **R2**).
2. **Shift compliance risk upstream**:
   - **Pre-dispatch quality gate** (staffed by underused Quality check inspectors) catches **wrong replacements** before dispatch (reducing **EUR 410 errors**).
3. **Preserve speed for low-risk**:
   - **Premium low-risk accessories** remain on Fast path (median 8h  **unchanged**).
   - **Standard low-risk** sees **reduced complaints** (8%  **~5%**).

### **Sacrifices/Unresolved Issues**
- **Higher cycle time for unclear evidence cases** (still **140h median**).
- **Pre-dispatch gate adds 6–18 hours** to high-value devices (but reduces downstream complaints).
- **No change to manual-review bottlenecks** (still queues at >82% utilization).

# **3. Redesign B: "Dynamic Evidence Portal + Manual Review Tiering"**
### **Targeted Changes**
- **Segments**: All cases with **unclear defects/missing evidence (22% of volume)** and **high-value devices**.
- **Paths Modified**:
  - **New "Evidence Portal" path** for unclear defects (reduces repeated requests by **30–45%**).
  - **Tiered manual review**: High-value devices get **dedicated reviewers** (reducing **Manual  Repair quote p90 from 60h  40h**).
- **Activities Added**:
  - **Evidence portal** (with guided prompts, digital upload, and status visibility).
  - **Priority queues** for high-value manual reviews (reducing **Fraud/risk review bottlenecks**).

### **Mechanism of Improvement**
1. **Reduce evidence loops**:
   - **Portal reduces repeated requests** (26%  **~15%**), cutting **Request missing evidence  Manual eligibility review** cycles.
   - **Status visibility** improves Standard customer tolerance (72h  **acceptable with updates**).
2. **Tier manual review by risk**:
   - **High-value devices** get **dedicated reviewers** (reducing **Manual  Repair quote p90 from 60h  40h**).
   - **Low-risk cases** routed to **auto-screen or simplified manual review**.
3. **Offload Fraud/risk bottlenecks**:
   - **Auto-screen rules updated** to flag **low-risk fraud patterns** (e.g., duplicate claims), reducing **Fraud/risk review volume by 20%**.

### **Sacrifices/Unresolved Issues**
- **Evidence portal may increase abandonment** for low-digital-literacy customers (**~5–10% uplift**).
- **No change to Fast-path compliance violations** (still **R2 violations for high-value devices**).
- **Premium high-value devices** still have **20h median** (vs. 96h reviewed), but **compliance risk remains**.

---
# **4. Redesign C: "Compliance-First Hybrid Path with Escalation Automation"**
### **Targeted Changes**
- **Segments**: **All high-value devices and Premium customers**.
- **Paths Modified**:
  - **Mandatory Fraud/risk review for high-value devices** (even if auto-approved).
  - **Automated escalation triggers** for Fast-path errors (e.g., wrong replacement detected via **pre-dispatch check**).
- **Activities Added**:
  - **Fraud/risk review for all high-value devices** (bypassing Fast approval).
  - **AI-assisted escalation** (e.g., auto-flag wrong replacements to Escalation board).

### **Mechanism of Improvement**
1. **Eliminate Fast-path compliance violations**:
   - **Enforce R1/R2 for high-value devices** by **mandating Fraud/risk review** (even if auto-approved).
   - **Reduce Dispatch  Customer complaint/reopen** by **10–15%** (via pre-dispatch checks + escalation).
2. **Automate escalation**:
   - **AI flags wrong replacements** (using part-number databases) and routes to **Escalation board** (currently underused).
   - **Reduces manual review backlog** by **15%** (fewer complaints  fewer Manual eligibility reopens).
3. **Improve Premium experience**:
   - **High-value Premium devices** now follow **Fraud/risk  Repair quote** (median **21h** vs. Fast path’s **20h but 22% complaints**).

### **Sacrifices/Unresolved Issues**
- **High-value device cycle time increases** (from **20h Fast  ~30h reviewed**).
- **Auto-screen utilization drops** (since high-value cases bypass it).
- **Escalation board becomes a bottleneck** if AI flags too many cases (currently **1 daily slot**).

# **6. Failure Modes and Displaced Bottlenecks**

### **Redesign A: "Segmented Fast-Track"**
- **Failure Modes**:
  1. **Pre-dispatch gate becomes a bottleneck** if not staffed properly (currently **Quality check inspectors underused**).
  2. **Auto-screen rule updates misclassify high-value cases** as low-risk, leading to **R2 violations**.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**:
  - **From Fraud/risk review  To pre-dispatch quality gate** (if gate is slow).

### **Redesign B: "Dynamic Evidence Portal"**
- **Failure Modes**:
  1. **Portal abandonment rate exceeds 10%** due to low digital literacy (especially for **Standard customers**).
  2. **Tiered manual review doesn’t reduce queues** if high-value cases still flood the system.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**:
  - **From evidence loops  To manual review backlog** (if portal doesn’t reduce requests enough).

### **Redesign C: "Compliance-First Hybrid"**
- **Failure Modes**:
  1. **Fraud/risk review becomes a bottleneck** (currently **2 specialists**, but volume increases by **50%**).
  2. **AI escalation flags too many false positives**, overwhelming the **Escalation board**.
- **Displaced Bottleneck**:
  - **From Dispatch  Customer complaint  To Fraud/risk review and Escalation board**.

# **7. Recommended Experiment Sequence**

### **Phase 1: Pilot Redesign A ("Segmented Fast-Track")**
- **Target**: **Premium low-risk accessories + high-value devices with clear evidence** (32% of cases).
- **Metrics to Monitor**:
  - **Compliance violations** (R2 violations for high-value devices).
  - **Customer complaint/reopen rate** (target: **<15%** for Fast path).
  - **Pre-dispatch gate cycle time** (target: **<6 hours**).
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - **Go if**: Compliance violations **<5%** and complaint/reopen **<15%**.
  - **Stop if**: Pre-dispatch gate **>8 hours** or **>10% of high-value cases** bypassed incorrectly.
- **Sequencing Logic**:
  - If successful, **expand to Standard low-risk accessories**.
  - If pre-dispatch gate is slow, **increase Quality check inspector capacity**.

### **Phase 2: Pilot Redesign B ("Evidence Portal")**
- **Target**: **Unclear defect cases (22% of volume)**.
- **Metrics to Monitor**:
  - **Evidence request loops** (target: **<15%**).
  - **Abandonment rate** (target: **<10%**).
  - **Manual review cycle time** (target: **<40 hours p90**).
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - **Go if**: Loops **<20%** and abandonment **<10%**.
  - **Stop if**: Abandonment **>15%** or manual review **>50 hours p90**.
- **Sequencing Logic**:
  - If successful, **roll out portal to all unclear evidence cases**.
  - If abandonment is high, **add phone-based evidence support**.

### **Phase 3: Pilot Redesign C ("Compliance-First Hybrid")**
- **Target**: **High-value devices (35% of volume)**.
- **Metrics to Monitor**:
  - **Fraud/risk review cycle time** (target: **<24 hours p90**).
  - **Customer complaint/reopen rate** (target: **<10%**).
  - **Escalation board volume** (target: **<50 cases/week**).
- **Stop/Go Thresholds**:
  - **Go if**: Complaints **<10%** and Fraud/risk review **<24 hours p90**.
  - **Stop if**: Escalation board **>60 cases/week** or Fraud/risk review **>30 hours p90**.
- **Sequencing Logic**:
  - If successful, **mandate for all high-value devices**.
  - If Escalation board is overwhelmed, **add shift coverage**.

### **Learning Checks for Downstream Shifts**
- **For Redesign A**: Monitor **Dispatch  Customer complaint/reopen** to ensure pre-dispatch gate isn’t just shifting complaints upstream.
- **For Redesign B**: Track **Manual eligibility review volume** to confirm evidence portal isn’t just moving work elsewhere.
- **For Redesign C**: Measure **Fast approval volume** to ensure high-value cases aren’t still slipping through.

### **Final Validation**
- **Compare rework costs** (service credits, reshipments) **before/after** each pilot.
- **Survey Premium customers** on **perceived speed vs. accuracy** trade-offs.
- **Audit compliance violations** to ensure **R1/R2 adherence** in high-value paths.

This approach ensures **materially different redesigns**, each addressing distinct contradictions in the data while explicitly handling trade-offs. The experiment sequence is **staged by risk** (low-risk first, then high-value) and **validates downstream impacts**.
