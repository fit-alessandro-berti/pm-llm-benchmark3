## 1. Materiality Framework

**Severity Framework (1-5 Scale):**
1. **Negligible** – No control failure, no financial/regulatory/customer impact, reversible, no recurrence risk.
2. **Minor** – Technical deviation with no policy violation, minimal exposure, reversible, low recurrence risk.
3. **Moderate** – Policy violation with limited exposure, reversible but requires correction, moderate recurrence risk.
4. **Major** – Significant control failure, high exposure, irreversible or costly to reverse, high recurrence risk, potential regulatory/customer harm.
5. **Critical** – Severe control failure, material financial/regulatory/customer harm, irreversible, systemic recurrence risk.

**Dimensions:**
- **Control Objective:** Adherence to P1-P6 (e.g., approval hierarchy, fraud screening, notification timeliness).
- **Financial Exposure:** Claim amount, reversibility of payment, fraud risk.
- **Customer/Regulatory Harm:** Delayed notification, incorrect payment, lack of transparency.
- **Recurrence:** Frequency of deviation in full dataset.
- **Evidence Reliability:** Confidence in logs, external documents, or manual attestations.
- **Model Scope:** Whether the deviation stems from out-of-scope cases (e.g., denied/withdrawn claims).

**Confidence vs. Severity:**
- **Confidence** reflects the reliability of evidence (e.g., high for core system logs, low for mutable CRM fields or verbal attestations).
- **Severity** reflects the business impact of the deviation, regardless of evidence quality. A high-severity deviation with low confidence (e.g., CL-602) still warrants investigation.

## 6. Why Alignment Cost Is Not Business Risk

1. **Cost-function design**: The alignment cost function assigns uniform weights (1) to all visible moves, ignoring business context. For example:
   - CL-602 (missing fraud screening) has raw cost 1, but the business risk is critical due to high exposure and irreversibility.
   - CL-601 (UI telemetry) has raw cost 3, but the business risk is negligible.

2. **Scope**: The model scope excludes denied/withdrawn claims (P6), so deviations like CL-607 (raw cost 4) are irrelevant to business risk.

3. **Hidden moves**: Silent `tau` transitions (e.g., CL-605) have zero cost but may hide model design flaws that could cause future deviations.

4. **Data conditions**: The cost function ignores data conditions (e.g., claim amount, risk level, bank changes). CL-610 (raw cost 0) violates P1 due to a recent bank change, but the alignment cost does not reflect this.

5. **Activity granularity**: Label mismatches (e.g., CL-611) incur cost but may not affect business outcomes if the activity is substantively equivalent.

6. **Duplicated telemetry**: UI events (e.g., CL-601) and retry duplicates (e.g., CL-603) inflate raw cost without business impact.

7. **Missing-but-external evidence**: CL-606 (missing `Assess Loss`) has raw cost 1, but the external adjuster report reduces business risk. The cost function cannot account for external evidence.

8. **Irreversibility**: CL-604 (payment before approval) has raw cost 3, but the irreversibility of payment makes the business risk critical. The cost function does not distinguish reversible vs. irreversible deviations.

## 7. PM4Py Reproduction and Aggregation Code

```python
import pm4py
import pandas as pd
from pm4py.objects.log.util import dataframe_utils
from pm4py.objects.conversion.log import converter as log_converter

# 1. Parse process tree
process_tree_str = "->( 'Register Claim', 'Validate Coverage', 'Assess Loss', X( ->( 'Fraud Screen', 'Manual Approve' ), 'Auto Approve' ), 'Pay Claim', 'Notify Claimant' )"
process_tree = pm4py.parse_process_tree(process_tree_str)

# 2. Convert to Petri net
net, im, fm = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(process_tree)

# 3. Construct event log from cases
data = [
    # CL-601
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Register Claim", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:00:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Validate Coverage", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:05:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Open Workbench", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:10:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Assess Loss", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:15:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Autosave", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:20:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Auto Approve", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:25:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Pay Claim", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:30:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Notify Claimant", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:35:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-601", "concept:name": "Close Workbench", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 09:40:00"},
    # CL-602
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-602", "concept:name": "Register Claim", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 10:00:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-602", "concept:name": "Validate Coverage", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 10:05:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-602", "concept:name": "Assess Loss", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 10:10:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-602", "concept:name": "Manual Approve", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 10:15:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-602", "concept:name": "Pay Claim", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 10:20:00"},
    {"case:concept:name": "CL-602", "concept:name": "Notify Claimant", "time:timestamp": "2023-01-01 10:25:00"},
    # Add other cases similarly...
]

df = pd.DataFrame(data)
df = df.sort_values(by=["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp"])
log = log_converter.apply(df, variant=log_converter.Variants.TO_EVENT_LOG)

# 4. Compute alignments
alignments = pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_alignments(log, net, im, fm)

# 5. Construct move summaries and aggregate
move_summaries = []
for case in alignments:
    case_id = case["case_id"]
    for move in case["alignment"]:
        move_summaries.append({
            "Case": case_id,
            "Move": move[0][1] if move[0][0] == "log" else move[1][1],
            "Move_Type": move[0][0] if move[0][0] in ["log", "model"] else "sync",
            "Cost": move[2],
            "Raw_Cost": case["cost"]
        })

move_df = pd.DataFrame(move_summaries)

# Add business context
business_context = {
    "CL-601": {"Amount": 1200, "Risk": "LOW", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": False},
    "CL-602": {"Amount": 480000, "Risk": "HIGH", "Bank_Change": "Yes", "Policy_Violation": True},
    "CL-603": {"Amount": 3200, "Risk": "LOW", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": False},
    "CL-604": {"Amount": 90000, "Risk": "MEDIUM", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": True},
    "CL-605": {"Amount": 800, "Risk": "LOW", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": False},
    "CL-606": {"Amount": 75000, "Risk": "HIGH", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": True},
    "CL-607": {"Amount": 0, "Risk": "N/A", "Bank_Change": "N/A", "Policy_Violation": False},
    "CL-608": {"Amount": 250000, "Risk": "HIGH", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": False},
    "CL-609": {"Amount": 18000, "Risk": "MEDIUM", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": False},
    "CL-610": {"Amount": 4500, "Risk": "LOW", "Bank_Change": "Yes", "Policy_Violation": True},
    "CL-611": {"Amount": 62000, "Risk": "HIGH", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": False},
    "CL-612": {"Amount": 2000, "Risk": "LOW", "Bank_Change": "No", "Policy_Violation": False}
}

for case in business_context:
    move_df.loc[move_df["Case"] == case, "Amount"] = business_context[case]["Amount"]
    move_df.loc[move_df["Case"] == case, "Risk"] = business_context[case]["Risk"]
    move_df.loc[move_df["Case"] == case, "Bank_Change"] = business_context[case]["Bank_Change"]
    move_df.loc[move_df["Case"] == case, "Policy_Violation"] = business_context[case]["Policy_Violation"]

# 6. Aggregate patterns
pattern_df = move_df.groupby(["Move", "Move_Type"]).agg(
    Frequency=("Case", "nunique"),
    Total_Raw_Cost=("Raw_Cost", "sum"),
    Max_Amount=("Amount", "max"),
    High_Risk_Count=("Risk", lambda x: (x == "HIGH").sum())
).reset_index()

# 7. Risk-prioritization evidence table
risk_df = move_df.groupby("Case").agg(
    Raw_Cost=("Raw_Cost", "first"),
    Amount=("Amount", "first"),
    Risk=("Risk", "first"),
    Policy_Violation=("Policy_Violation", "first"),
    Move_Count=("Move", "count"),
    Sync_Moves=("Move_Type", lambda x: (x == "sync").sum()),
    Log_Moves=("Move_Type", lambda x: (x == "log").sum()),
    Model_Moves=("Move_Type", lambda x: (x == "model").sum())
).reset_index()

risk_df["Severity"] = [1, 5, 1, 5, 1, 4, 2, 3, 1, 4, 2, 2]  # From Section 2
risk_df["Confidence"] = ["High", "Low", "High", "High", "High", "Medium", "High", "Low", "High", "High", "High", "High"]

print("Move Patterns:")
print(pattern_df.sort_values("Frequency", ascending=False))
print("\nRisk-Prioritization Evidence Table:")
print(risk_df.sort_values("Severity", ascending=False))
```
