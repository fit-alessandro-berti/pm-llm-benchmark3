# 2. Benign alternative explanations

### Pattern A: `VendorMaster_Lena` + `AP_Kim` (`V-77x` concentration, 9 SoD violations, 17-min median approval, 83% `manual_upload`, 11 subcontracting loops)
*   **Benign Explanation 1 — Coordinated banking-platform migration & release of held invoices:** Vendor family `V-77x` recently changed banking platforms across multiple regional entities. AP may have placed high-value invoices on payment hold pending bank updates; once `Lena` uploaded verified mass-maintenance files, she alerted `AP_Kim` (who is formally assigned to higher-risk suppliers) to immediately release the queued backlog, and corrected minor regional formatting rejections (`Lena``Kim``Lena`).
*   **Benign Explanation 2 — `manual_upload` batch-ingestion attribution artifact:** When multi-step maintenance and payment-release templates are ingested via `source_system="manual_upload"`, the ingestion script may populate `org:resource` with the uploader's user ID across multiple events in the case, creating apparent four-eyes violations when `entered_by` and `approved_by` were actually distinct individuals.
*   **Supporting vs. Refuting Evidence:** *Supported* if IT change/master-data tickets link the `V-77x` platform migration to the new IBANs, external vendor call-back logs exist, and `entered_by != approved_by`. *Refuted* if bank accounts reverted to different accounts shortly after payment (`Lena``Kim``Lena`), no corporate banking migration notice covers the specific accounts used, or one user authenticated both steps in raw access logs.

### Pattern B: `Buyer_Carla` + `Approver_Gita` & `Buyer_Dan` + `Manager_Hugo` (8–14 min approvals, 42 PO four-eyes violations, tight subcontracting loops)
*   **Benign Explanation 1 — Dedicated emergency response channels:** `Carla` (emergency medical supplies) and `Dan` (weekend facilities repairs) handle time-critical operational disruptions (`emergency_flag=True` in 88% and 79% of cases) alongside designated urgent/exception approvers (`Gita` and `Hugo`). Verbal/chat alignment before ERP entry naturally produces 8–14 minute approval times and rapid clarification loops (`Gita``Carla``Gita`).
*   **Benign Explanation 2 — `legacy_erp` bot-mapping collisions and shared delegation accounts:** Mapping errors in `legacy_erp` overwrite `Bot_PO` with the human approver's ID in `org:resource`, generating synthetic `Create PO` + `Approve PO` self-approvals. Similarly, assistants using the shared `Approver_Gita` credential inflate Gita's apparent co-working and self-review counts.
*   **Supporting vs. Refuting Evidence:** *Supported* if `legacy_erp` cases show `Bot_PO` in raw system logs (`entered_by`), `Approver_Gita` events trace to distinct assistants in `entered_by`, and receiving records (`Goods Receipt` / work orders) confirm genuine medical or facility emergencies. *Refuted* if non-emergency purchases were misflagged `emergency_flag=True` to bypass competitive quoting (`Request Quote`) or if the same human bypassed `Bot_PO` in clean source systems.

### Pattern C: Same-person `Emergency Override` + `Retrospective Justification` (17 cases, 9-min median, `Hugo` & `Gita`)
*   **Benign Explanation 1 — Quarter-close spreadsheet migration artifact:** Twelve of the 17 cases were uploaded together via `source_system="quarter_close_upload"` with identical minute-level timestamps. A clerical uploader or manager importing offline sign-offs likely had their ID mapped to `org:resource` or `entered_by` for the entire batch.
*   **Benign Explanation 2 — Shared account usage or administrative transcription:** An assistant logged into `Approver_Gita` entered a justification reviewed by someone else, or `Manager_Hugo` transcribed an email justification from an operational director into the system to close open audit items at quarter-end.
*   **Supporting vs. Refuting Evidence:** *Supported* if the original pre-upload spreadsheet or archived emails contain independent reviewer signatures dated within 3 working days. *Refuted* if no underlying offline documentation exists or if the overriding manager substantively signed off on their own override in non-uploaded cases (specifically the 5 cases outside the batch of 12).

### Pattern D: High-frequency buyer–approver pairs (`Aline``Elena`: 510; `Boris``Felix`: 495) & `Vendor Check` + `Change Bank Details` (31 cases)
*   **Benign Explanation 1 — Formal organizational specialization:** `Elena` (Team A) and `Felix` (Team B) are structurally paired with high-volume buyers `Aline` and `Boris` for low/medium-value routine purchasing (2.4–2.6h median approval, 4–5% emergencies). Similarly, `Lena` and `Marc` performing both `Vendor Check` and `Change Vendor Bank Details` reflects standard consolidation within the Vendor Master team rather than a breach of the stated four-eyes rules.
*   **Benign Explanation 2 — Automated ERP workflow routing:** Workflow rules deterministically route requisitions by purchasing group, region, or commodity code to default approvers.
*   **Supporting vs. Refuting Evidence:** *Supported* by HR org charts, ERP routing tables, low vendor concentration, and normal 2+ hour review distributions. *Refuted* only if specific vendors are manually reassigned across teams to avoid scrutiny.

# 4. Network and conformance analyses

The following Python/PM4Py workflow operationalizes the audit tests, separates true human exceptions from `org:resource` mapping artifacts, and evaluates timing and declarative conformance.

```python
import pandas as pd
import numpy as np
import pm4py

# 0. Load and format event log (preserving raw audit columns alongside org:resource)
df_raw = pd.read_csv("p2p_event_log.csv")
df = pm4py.format_dataframe(
    df_raw,
    case_id="case:concept:name",
    activity_key="concept:name",
    timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
    timest_format="ISO8601"
)

# Normalize true actor column to control for legacy_erp Bot_PO & shared accounts
df["effective_actor"] = np.where(
    df["concept:name"].isin(["Approve Purchase Order", "Payment Approval"]),
    df["approved_by"].fillna(df["org:resource"]),
    df["entered_by"].fillna(df["org:resource"])
)

# 1. Four-eyes violations: compare raw org:resource vs. reconciled effective_actor
sod_pairs = [
    ("Create Purchase Order", "Approve Purchase Order"),
    ("Change Vendor Bank Details", "Payment Approval"),
    ("Emergency Override", "Retrospective Justification"),
]

for act1, act2 in sod_pairs:
    viol_raw = pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(
        df, act1, act2, activity_key="concept:name", resource_key="org:resource"
    )
    viol_clean = pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(
        df, act1, act2, activity_key="concept:name", resource_key="effective_actor"
    )
    print(f"{act1} + {act2} | Raw cases: {viol_raw['case:concept:name'].nunique()} "
          f"| Reconciled cases: {viol_clean['case:concept:name'].nunique()}")

# 2. Organizational & Social Network Analysis (Handover, Working Together, Subcontracting)
how_net = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(df, resource_key="effective_actor")
wt_net = pm4py.discover_working_together_network(df, resource_key="effective_actor")
sub_net = pm4py.discover_subcontracting_network(df, n=2, resource_key="effective_actor")

# Detailed multi-dimensional network profiling (e.g., handover grouped by source_system)
net_analysis = pm4py.discover_network_analysis(
    df,
    out_column="effective_actor",
    in_column="effective_actor",
    node_column_source="effective_actor",
    node_column_target="effective_actor",
    edge_column="concept:name",
    performance=True
)

# 3. Compare Emergency vs. Non-Emergency Variants and Control Paths
df_emerg = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(
    df, "emergency_flag", [True], level="case", retain=True
)
df_non_emerg = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(
    df, "emergency_flag", [False], level="case", retain=True
)
emerg_variants = pm4py.get_variants(df_emerg)
non_emerg_variants = pm4py.get_variants(df_non_emerg)

# 4. Timing Analysis: Path-Performance Filters & Case Service/Waiting Time
# Isolate unusually fast (< 30 min = 1800 sec) Bank Change -> Payment Approval paths
fast_bank_to_pay = pm4py.filter_paths_performance(
    df,
    path=("Change Vendor Bank Details", "Payment Approval"),
    min_performance=0,
    max_performance=1800
)

# Extract exact sub-traces between Bank Change and Case Close to inspect Lena->Kim->Lena loops
bank_subtraces = pm4py.filter_between(
    df, "Change Vendor Bank Details", "Close Case"
)

# Compute activity service times (using lifecycle:transition start/complete if present) and case waiting times
service_times = pm4py.get_service_time(df)
df_with_timings = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(df)

# 5. Declarative & Log-Skeleton Conformance for Emergency Override -> Retrospective Justification
# Train reference models on clean compliant emergency cases (excluding quarter_close_upload)
df_clean_emerg = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(
    df_emerg, "source_system", ["quarter_close_upload"], level="event", retain=False
)
declare_model = pm4py.discover_declare(df_clean_emerg)
declare_conf = pm4py.conformance_declare(df_emerg, declare_model)

skeleton_model = pm4py.discover_log_skeleton(df_clean_emerg)
skeleton_conf = pm4py.conformance_log_skeleton(df_emerg, skeleton_model)

# Explicit business-day SLA (<= 3 working days) and distinct-human check
overrides = df[df["concept:name"] == "Emergency Override"][
    ["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp", "effective_actor"]
].rename(columns={"time:timestamp": "ov_time", "effective_actor": "ov_actor"})

justifs = df[df["concept:name"] == "Retrospective Justification"][
    ["case:concept:name", "time:timestamp", "effective_actor", "source_system"]
].rename(columns={"time:timestamp": "just_time", "effective_actor": "just_actor"})

ov_check = overrides.merge(justifs, on="case:concept:name", how="left")
ov_check["bus_days"] = np.busday_count(
    ov_check["ov_time"].values.astype("datetime64[D]"),
    ov_check["just_time"].fillna(pd.Timestamp.now()).values.astype("datetime64[D]")
)
ov_check["missing_or_late"] = ov_check["just_time"].isna() | (ov_check["bus_days"] > 3)
ov_check["same_actor"] = ov_check["ov_actor"] == ov_check["just_actor"]

# 6. Isolate Source-System Effects (manual_upload & legacy_erp)
df_no_manual = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(
    df, "source_system", ["manual_upload", "quarter_close_upload"], level="event", retain=False
)
df_legacy_only = pm4py.filter_event_attribute_values(
    df, "source_system", ["legacy_erp"], level="event", retain=True
)
```
