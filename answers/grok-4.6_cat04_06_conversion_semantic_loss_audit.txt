# 1. Source-Semantics Baseline

The source POWL is the authoritative model. Edges are the only stated causal constraints. Missing edges are missing constraints, not drawing accidents. Silent skip of Legal Review is real optionality, not a layout gap. The loop is the binary process-tree/POWL loop `trial_body (correct_defect trial_body)*`.

| Invariant ID | Invariant | Positive witness | Negative witness | Observable after hiding tau? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 | `Register Change` is first. No visible activity precedes it. | `Register Change → Assess Risk → Prepare Evidence → Configure Trial → Inspect Trial → Release Change → Archive Change` | `Assess Risk → Register Change → …` | Yes. |
| INV-02 | After registration, `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` are unordered. Both interleavings are allowed. | `…Register Change → Assess Risk → Prepare Evidence…` and `…Register Change → Prepare Evidence → Assess Risk…` | A conversion that admits only one of those two orders | Yes. Absence of order is seen as both interleavings. |
| INV-03 | `Legal Review` is optional: XOR of the labeled activity and a silent skip. At most one occurrence. | Same prefix with `Legal Review` present, and the same prefix with it absent | Two `Legal Review` events; or making `Legal Review` mandatory | The skip itself is not visible. Presence/absence of `Legal Review` is visible. |
| INV-04 | If `Legal Review` occurs, it is after `Assess Risk`. | `Register Change → Assess Risk → Legal Review → Prepare Evidence → …` | `Register Change → Legal Review → Assess Risk → …` | Yes. |
| INV-05 | `Legal Review` does not wait for `Prepare Evidence`. | `Register Change → Assess Risk → Legal Review → Prepare Evidence → Configure Trial → …` | Forcing `Prepare Evidence` before every `Legal Review` | Yes. |
| INV-06 | The verification loop cannot start until both `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` have completed. | `Register Change → Assess Risk → Prepare Evidence → Configure Trial…` and `Register Change → Prepare Evidence → Assess Risk → Configure Trial…` | `Register Change → Assess Risk → Configure Trial → … → Prepare Evidence…`; or `Configure Trial` after only `Prepare Evidence` | Yes. |
| INV-07 | After its own predecessors, optional legal and the verification loop are unordered and may overlap. There is no source edge between those nodes. | `Legal Review` before, during, or after `Configure Trial` / `Inspect Trial` / `Correct Defect`, including after the last inspect and before release | Forcing `Legal Review` to finish before the first `Configure Trial`, or forcing the loop to finish before `Legal Review` | Yes, as interleavings. True overlap is not a trace property. |
| INV-08 | Every verification iteration executes `Configure Trial` before `Inspect Trial`. | `…Configure Trial → Inspect Trial…` and `…Configure Trial → Inspect Trial → Correct Defect → Configure Trial → Inspect Trial…` | `…Inspect Trial → Configure Trial…`; `…Configure Trial → Correct Defect → Inspect Trial…` | Yes. |
| INV-09 | A further iteration is separated by exactly one `Correct Defect` (the loop redo child). | `…Inspect Trial → Correct Defect → Configure Trial → Inspect Trial…` | `…Inspect Trial → Configure Trial…` (missing redo); `…Inspect Trial → Correct Defect → Correct Defect → Configure Trial…` | Yes. |
| INV-10 | At least one iteration of the trial body occurs. The loop is `A(BA)*`, not `(AB)*` and not zero-or-more `A`. | `…Configure Trial → Inspect Trial → Release Change…` | `…Prepare Evidence → Release Change…` with no trial; `…Correct Defect → Configure Trial…` as the first verification act | Yes. |
| INV-11 | `Release Change` waits for completion of both the optional-legal XOR and the verification loop. If legal was taken, release cannot precede it. Release cannot precede the last `Inspect Trial`. | `…Inspect Trial → Legal Review → Release Change…` and `…Legal Review → Inspect Trial → Release Change…` and skip-legal `…Inspect Trial → Release Change…` | `…Assess Risk → Legal Review → Release Change → Prepare Evidence → Configure Trial…`; `…Inspect Trial → Release Change → Legal Review…` when legal is taken | Yes for visible ordering. The silent skip path is visible only as release without `Legal Review`. |
| INV-12 | `Archive Change` occurs after `Release Change` and is the unique last visible activity. | `…Release Change → Archive Change` as the suffix | `…Archive Change → Release Change`; any visible event after archive | Yes. |
| INV-13 | Visible labels are unique and play one structural role each. Silent nodes carry label `None`. | Each labeled activity appears according to the invariants above | Duplicate labels, renamed silents, extra visible routing tasks | Labels are visible; invented visible routing would be a new observable. |

# 2. Candidate Conversion Pipelines

There is no supported direct POWL-to-BPMN call in the stated interface. Returning a BPMN object is not a fidelity proof.

| Pipeline | PM4Py calls | Expected benefit | Expected risk | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1 (primary). POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN** | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)` then `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` | The Petri net is the operational image of POWL (partial order, XOR-skip, binary loop, AND-join before release). BPMN is the stakeholder view of that net. | Petri-to-BPMN gateway folding; silent skip absorbed into an undocumented default flow; layout that looks sequential; markings on a later reverse conversion may be `None`. | `convert_to_bpmn` raises; produced BPMN cannot be diagnosed; reverse net is unsound or has null markings so soundness cannot be checked. |
| **P2. POWL → accepting Petri net → process tree → BPMN** | Same Petri conversion, then `pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(net, im, fm)` inside `try/except`, then `pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree)` only if the tree exists | If the net were series-parallel / SESE-block-structured, the tree would give a canonical block BPMN with cleaner gateways. | The source is not a process tree. `Assess Risk` fans out to optional legal and to verification, while `Prepare Evidence` joins only verification, and both branches AND-join at release. That crossing is not block-structured. A successful tree would almost certainly sequentialize INV-05 or INV-07. | **Expected:** `convert_to_process_tree` raises. Treat that as a correct refusal, not a defect to suppress. If it returns a tree, reject the tree unless footprints and bounded traces still satisfy INV-02, INV-05, INV-07, and INV-11. |
| P3 (validation only, not a design path). BPMN → accepting Petri net | `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn)` on each BPMN object | Enables workflow-net, soundness, footprint, and trace comparison against `net_from_powl`. | Reverse translation can add silent routing, drop markings, or change the join type. Success of the call is not equivalence. | Null initial/final marking; not a workflow net; unsound; language drift under the bounded explorer. |

**Primary pipeline: P1.** Keep the accepting Petri net as the semantic carrier. Use BPMN only as a derived picture of that net. Do not route authoring through a process tree.

```python
"""POWL conversion audit: Petri-net carrier, BPMN views, optional process tree."""

from __future__ import annotations

import traceback
from collections import deque
from copy import copy
from pathlib import Path

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.petri_net.obj import Marking
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import (
    OperatorPOWL,
    SilentTransition,
    StrictPartialOrder,
    Transition,
)
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Authoritative source (same object the audit talks about)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
register = Transition(label="Register Change")
assess_risk = Transition(label="Assess Risk")
prepare_evidence = Transition(label="Prepare Evidence")

legal_review = Transition(label="Legal Review")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()
optional_legal = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.XOR,
    children=[legal_review, skip_legal],
)

configure_trial = Transition(label="Configure Trial")
inspect_trial = Transition(label="Inspect Trial")
trial_body = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[configure_trial, inspect_trial])
trial_body.order.add_edge(configure_trial, inspect_trial)

correct_defect = Transition(label="Correct Defect")
verification_loop = OperatorPOWL(
    operator=Operator.LOOP,
    children=[trial_body, correct_defect],
)

release_change = Transition(label="Release Change")
archive_change = Transition(label="Archive Change")

source_powl = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[
        register,
        assess_risk,
        prepare_evidence,
        optional_legal,
        verification_loop,
        release_change,
        archive_change,
    ]
)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, assess_risk)
source_powl.order.add_edge(register, prepare_evidence)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, optional_legal)
source_powl.order.add_edge(assess_risk, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(prepare_evidence, verification_loop)
source_powl.order.add_edge(optional_legal, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(verification_loop, release_change)
source_powl.order.add_edge(release_change, archive_change)

OUT = Path("conversion_artifacts")
OUT.mkdir(exist_ok=True)

report: dict = {"pipelines": {}, "diagnostics": {}, "exceptions": {}}


def unpack_net(result, origin: str):
    """Accept only an explicit (net, im, fm) triple. Do not invent markings."""
    if result is None:
        raise RuntimeError(f"{origin}: conversion returned None")
    if not (isinstance(result, tuple) and len(result) >= 3):
        raise RuntimeError(
            f"{origin}: expected (net, im, fm), got {type(result)!r}"
        )
    net, im, fm = result[0], result[1], result[2]
    return net, im, fm


def markings_ok(im, fm) -> bool:
    return im is not None and fm is not None


def diagnose_net(name: str, net, im, fm) -> dict:
    info = {
        "name": name,
        "net_is_none": net is None,
        "im_is_none": im is None,
        "fm_is_none": fm is None,
        "is_workflow_net": None,
        "soundness": None,
        "soundness_error": None,
        "workflow_error": None,
    }
    if net is None:
        return info
    try:
        info["is_workflow_net"] = bool(pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net))
    except TypeError:
        try:
            info["is_workflow_net"] = bool(
                pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net, im, fm)
            )
        except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
            info["workflow_error"] = repr(exc)
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        info["workflow_error"] = repr(exc)

    if markings_ok(im, fm):
        try:
            info["soundness"] = bool(
                pm4py.check_soundness(
                    net, im, fm, print_diagnostics=True
                )
            )
        except TypeError:
            try:
                info["soundness"] = bool(pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm))
            except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
                info["soundness_error"] = repr(exc)
        except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
            info["soundness_error"] = repr(exc)
    else:
        info["soundness_error"] = "skipped: initial or final marking is None"
    report["diagnostics"][name] = info
    print(f"[diagnose] {name}: {info}")
    return info


def try_save(fn, path: Path, *args):
    try:
        fn(*args, str(path))
        print(f"[saved] {path}")
        return True
    except TypeError:
        try:
            fn(*args, file_path=str(path))
            print(f"[saved] {path}")
            return True
        except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
            print(f"[save failed] {path}: {exc}")
            return False
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        print(f"[save failed] {path}: {exc}")
        return False


# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# P1: POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl = unpack_net(
    pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(source_powl),
    "convert_to_petri_net(source_powl)",
)
diagnose_net("net_from_powl", net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl)
try_save(pm4py.save_vis_powl, OUT / "source_powl.png", source_powl)
try_save(
    pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
    OUT / "net_from_powl.png",
    net_from_powl,
    im_from_powl,
    fm_from_powl,
)

if not markings_ok(im_from_powl, fm_from_powl):
    raise RuntimeError(
        "POWL→Petri produced a null marking; refuse BPMN conversion on an "
        "unmarked net rather than guessing i/o places."
    )

bpmn_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(
    net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
)
try_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, OUT / "bpmn_from_net.png", bpmn_from_net)
report["pipelines"]["P1"] = "completed"

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# P2: Petri → process tree → BPMN  (allowed / expected to fail)
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
tree_from_net = None
bpmn_from_tree = None
try:
    tree_from_net = pm4py.convert_to_process_tree(
        net_from_powl, im_from_powl, fm_from_powl
    )
    print("[P2] convert_to_process_tree succeeded; tree must still be audited.")
    try_save(
        pm4py.save_vis_process_tree,
        OUT / "tree_from_net.png",
        tree_from_net,
    )
    bpmn_from_tree = pm4py.convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)
    try_save(pm4py.save_vis_bpmn, OUT / "bpmn_from_tree.png", bpmn_from_tree)
    report["pipelines"]["P2"] = "completed"
except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
    report["pipelines"]["P2"] = "failed"
    report["exceptions"]["convert_to_process_tree"] = {
        "type": type(exc).__name__,
        "message": str(exc),
        "traceback": traceback.format_exc(),
    }
    print(
        "[P2] process-tree conversion failed. This is the expected outcome "
        "if the net is not block-structured enough for a faithful tree."
    )
    print(traceback.format_exc())

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# Reverse each BPMN to an accepting net where the API actually returns one
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
roundtrip_nets = {}


def reverse_bpmn(tag: str, bpmn_model):
    if bpmn_model is None:
        return
    try:
        net_b, im_b, fm_b = unpack_net(
            pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(bpmn_model),
            f"convert_to_petri_net({tag})",
        )
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        report["exceptions"][f"reverse_{tag}"] = repr(exc)
        print(f"[reverse failed] {tag}: {exc}")
        return
    diagnose_net(f"net_from_{tag}", net_b, im_b, fm_b)
    if markings_ok(im_b, fm_b):
        try_save(
            pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
            OUT / f"net_from_{tag}.png",
            net_b,
            im_b,
            fm_b,
        )
    else:
        print(
            f"[reverse] {tag}: net exists but im/fm is None; "
            "skip Petri visualization and later marked analyses."
        )
    roundtrip_nets[tag] = (net_b, im_b, fm_b)


reverse_bpmn("bpmn_from_net", bpmn_from_net)
reverse_bpmn("bpmn_from_tree", bpmn_from_tree)

if tree_from_net is not None:
    try:
        net_t, im_t, fm_t = unpack_net(
            pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net),
            "convert_to_petri_net(tree_from_net)",
        )
        diagnose_net("net_from_tree", net_t, im_t, fm_t)
        if markings_ok(im_t, fm_t):
            try_save(
                pm4py.save_vis_petri_net,
                OUT / "net_from_tree.png",
                net_t,
                im_t,
                fm_t,
            )
            roundtrip_nets["tree_from_net"] = (net_t, im_t, fm_t)
    except Exception as exc:  # noqa: BLE001
        report["exceptions"]["reverse_tree"] = repr(exc)

print("Pipeline summary:", report["pipelines"])
print("Exceptions:", {k: v if isinstance(v, str) else v.get("message", v)
                      for k, v in report["exceptions"].items()})
```

# 4. Preservation and Loss Audit

Assessments below are structural. Runtime cells stay `not tested` until Section 5 is executed against a live PM4Py install. P2 is judged `conversion failed` on block-structure grounds; if a tree object appears anyway, treat the tree columns as `lost` / `approximated` until witnesses pass.

| Source invariant | POWL→Petri | Petri→BPMN | Petri→Tree | BPMN round trip | Evidence or uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| INV-01 Register first | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | POWL source place feeds only `Register Change`. BPMN start event should do the same; a leading silent routing node is still hidden after tau-hiding. |
| INV-02 Assess ∥ Prepare | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Petri can enable both after register (true concurrency). BPMN needs a parallel split. A sequential layout or exclusive split would look ordered. Footprints can confirm interleaving, not simultaneity. |
| INV-03 optional Legal Review | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | Petri XOR + `label is None` skip. BPMN often folds the tau into a default/empty flow. Optionality can remain, but the skip is easy to miss in the picture. |
| INV-04 Legal after Assess | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Sole POWL edge into the XOR is from `Assess Risk`. Loss would mean a gateway that enables legal from start or from prepare-only. |
| INV-05 Legal does not wait for Prepare | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | No POWL edge `Prepare Evidence → optional_legal`. BPMN auto-layout often places legal after both predecessors of the later AND-join and invites a false control-flow reading. A tree, if forced, typically inserts that edge. |
| INV-06 verification waits for Assess and Prepare | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Two incoming POWL edges into the loop. Petri AND-join before the loop subnet. BPMN must use a parallel join, not exclusive. |
| INV-07 no edge legal ↔ verification | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Concurrent subnets in the net. BPMN swim of tasks in one column reads as sequence. A successful tree almost always adds a sequence between the XOR and the loop. |
| INV-08 Configure ≺ Inspect per iteration | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | `trial_body` is a two-node PO with one edge. Should survive as a sequence inside the loop subnet / loop activity. |
| INV-09 redo is `Correct Defect` | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | Binary LOOP child encoding. BPMN back-edge may be drawn without a task, or `Correct Defect` may look like an optional side path rather than the redo body. |
| INV-10 ≥1 iteration, `A(BA)*` | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | LOOP enters the body first. An unsound BPMN loop or a while-`B`-then-`A` rewrite would admit the negative witnesses. |
| INV-11 AND-join before Release | expected preserved | ambiguous | conversion failed | not tested | Two POWL edges into `Release Change`. If BPMN uses XOR-join, release can fire after only one branch (admission of release before verification, or release while legal is still required). |
| INV-12 Archive after Release, final | expected preserved | expected preserved | conversion failed | not tested | Linear suffix. Risk is an extra XOR after release or a missing end event. |
| INV-13 unique labels / silent `None` | expected preserved | approximated | conversion failed | not tested | Visible labels should copy. Converter-invented visible gateways or empty tasks create new observables. Silent routing usually stays `None`. |
| Initial and final markings | expected preserved | not tested | conversion failed | ambiguous | POWL→Petri should yield a marked accepting net. Reverse BPMN→Petri is the call most likely to return `None` markings; soundness must then be skipped, not assumed. |

**True concurrency versus arbitrary interleaving.** The source states absence of order, which in POWL/Petri is simultaneous enablement, not a hidden scheduler. Trace exploration, footprints, and playout only see interleavings. A BPMN parallel gateway is the right *symbol* for INV-02 and INV-07; passing an interleaving test does not prove an engine will run the branches truly concurrently, and failing to draw a parallel gateway can still leave the Petri language intact.

**No edge between optional legal and verification.** That missing edge is content. P1’s net should keep two concurrent regions after `Assess Risk` (legal) and after `{Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence}` (loop). P2 cannot say the same thing in a process tree without adding order: either legal is boxed after both predecessors (breaks INV-05 and often INV-07), or the loop is boxed under prepare alone (breaks INV-06), or legal is sequenced before/after the loop (breaks INV-07). A raised exception here is fidelity, not an incomplete script.

**Silent optional behavior.** After hiding `None`, skip-legal is the empty visible choice. Diagnostics that look only at labeled nodes can mark legal as mandatory if the tau is dropped, or mark it as a second invisible path that never reaches the AND-join. INV-03 and INV-11 must be tested together: skip-legal still has to *complete* so release can fire.

**Binary-loop semantics.** Children are `(trial_body, correct_defect)` ⇒ `Configure-Inspect (Correct-Defect Configure-Inspect)*`. That is not “zero trials”, not “defect first”, and not an unguarded self-loop on inspect. BPMN loop markers and subprocess recycle buttons do not record which child is body versus redo.

**Join before Release Change.** Release is a synchronization, not an exclusive merge. The net should require a token from the XOR exit and a token from the loop exit. Visual XOR-join, implicit unlabelled merge, or an OR-join would be a semantic defect even if the diagram still “looks like a change process”.

**Activity-label uniqueness.** Source visible labels are unique, which makes witness replay well-defined. After conversion, do not treat gateway names, `None`, or generated `"tau"` strings as activities. If the BPMN exporter materializes the skip as a visible empty task, INV-03’s negative witness set changes.

**Initial and final markings.** Every later API used here (`convert_to_bpmn`, `convert_to_process_tree`, `check_soundness`, reachability, enablement) needs both markings. The script refuses to invent them. A reverse net without `im`/`fm` is an incomplete artifact, not a sound workflow net you forgot to print.

**Gateway and routing-node ambiguity.** Petri-to-BPMN is a drawing of places and transitions, not a second specification. Parallel vs exclusive vs inclusive, default flows on the legal skip, extra silent tasks, and the top-to-bottom order of shapes can all be misread while the underlying net still satisfies the invariants. Section 6 is about that gap.

def marking_key(marking) -> Tuple[Tuple[str, int], ...]:
    if marking is None:
        return tuple()
    items = []
    for place, tokens in marking.items():
        name = getattr(place, "name", None) or str(place)
        items.append((str(name), int(tokens)))
    return tuple(sorted(items))


def markings_equal(a, b) -> bool:
    if a is None or b is None:
        return False
    return marking_key(a) == marking_key(b)


def fire(net, marking, transition):
    """Execute one transition if the PM4Py marking API allows it."""
    if hasattr(pm4py, "execute_enabled_transition"):
        new_m = copy(marking)
        pm4py.execute_enabled_transition(net, new_m, transition)
        return new_m
    # Fallback used by pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics
    from pm4py.objects.petri_net import semantics as pn_sem

    if not pn_sem.is_enabled(transition, net, marking):
        return None
    return pn_sem.execute(transition, net, marking)


def enabled(net, marking):
    trans = pm4py.get_enabled_transitions(net, marking)
    return list(trans) if trans is not None else []


def is_silent(transition) -> bool:
    return getattr(transition, "label", None) is None


def explore_visible_language(
    net,
    im,
    fm,
    *,
    visible_bound: int = VISIBLE_BOUND,
    loop_bound: int = LOOP_BOUND,
    tau_run_bound: int = TAU_RUN_BOUND,
    state_bound: int = SEARCH_STATE_BOUND,
) -> dict:
    """
    Bounded visible-trace explorer.

    - hides transitions whose label is None
    - completed traces require exact equality with fm
    - tau-run bound plus per-branch visited markings block silent cycles
    - visible length and Correct Defect count bound the loop
    """
    if not markings_ok(im, fm):
        return {
            "completed": set(),
            "truncated": True,
            "reason": "null marking",
            "states": 0,
            "exhaustive_under_bound": False,
        }

    completed: Set[Tuple[str, ...]] = set()
    # state key: marking, visible length, defect count  (trace stored aside)
    q = deque()
    q.append((im, tuple(), 0))  # marking, visible trace, defect count
    seen: Set[Tuple] = set()
    seen.add((marking_key(im), 0, 0, tuple()))
    states = 0
    truncated = False

    while q:
        marking, trace, defects = q.popleft()
        states += 1
        if states > state_bound:
            truncated = True
            break

        if markings_equal(marking, fm):
            completed.add(trace)

        en = enabled(net, marking)
        if not en:
            continue

        # Silent closure step-by-step (not precomputed) so enablement of
        # visible transitions at intermediate tau markings is not lost.
        silent_here = [t for t in en if is_silent(t)]
        visible_here = [t for t in en if not is_silent(t)]

        # Fire at most one silent transition per node; the queue carries the
        # tau budget in a side map keyed by the current search node.
        # We encode remaining tau budget in the seen-set via a local DFS
        # from this marking.
        def push_state(new_marking, new_trace, new_defects):
            nonlocal truncated
            key = (
                marking_key(new_marking),
                len(new_trace),
                new_defects,
                new_trace,
            )
            if key in seen:
                return
            if len(seen) > state_bound:
                truncated = True
                return
            seen.add(key)
            q.append((new_marking, new_trace, new_defects))

        # Bounded silent exits from *this* marking, remembering visited
        # markings so a tau cycle cannot spin.
        tau_visited = {marking_key(marking)}
        tau_q = deque([(marking, 0)])
        reachable_for_visible = [(marking, 0)]
        while tau_q:
            t_mark, t_used = tau_q.popleft()
            if t_used >= tau_run_bound:
                truncated = True
                continue
            for t in enabled(net, t_mark):
                if not is_silent(t):
                    continue
                nxt = fire(net, t_mark, t)
                if nxt is None:
                    continue
                mk = marking_key(nxt)
                if mk in tau_visited:
                    # silent cycle: stop this branch, do not follow
                    continue
                tau_visited.add(mk)
                tau_q.append((nxt, t_used + 1))
                reachable_for_visible.append((nxt, t_used + 1))
                # A tau path may land in the final marking.
                if markings_equal(nxt, fm):
                    completed.add(trace)

        # Visible successors from the marking and from every tau-successor
        # discovered above (tau* then one visible).
        fired_visible_from: Set[Tuple] = set()
        for base_mark, _tau_used in reachable_for_visible:
            if len(trace) >= visible_bound:
                truncated = True
                continue
            for t in enabled(net, base_mark):
                if is_silent(t):
                    continue
                label = t.label
                new_defects = defects + (1 if label == "Correct Defect" else 0)
                if new_defects > loop_bound:
                    truncated = True
                    continue
                sig = (marking_key(base_mark), label, new_defects)
                if sig in fired_visible_from:
                    continue
                fired_visible_from.add(sig)
                nxt = fire(net, base_mark, t)
                if nxt is None:
                    continue
                push_state(nxt, trace + (label,), new_defects)

    return {
        "completed": completed,
        "truncated": truncated,
        "states": states,
        "exhaustive_under_bound": (not truncated),
        "bounds": {
            "visible_bound": visible_bound,
            "loop_bound": loop_bound,
            "tau_run_bound": tau_run_bound,
            "state_bound": state_bound,
        },
    }


def replay_visible(net, im, fm, trace: Sequence[str]) -> bool:
    """
    Accept iff there exist silent runs such that the labeled sequence fires
    and the marking afterwards equals fm exactly.
    """
    if not markings_ok(im, fm):
        return False

    def tau_successors(marking) -> List:
        found = [marking]
        visited = {marking_key(marking)}
        dq = deque([(marking, 0)])
        while dq:
            cur, used = dq.popleft()
            if used >= TAU_RUN_BOUND:
                continue
            for t in enabled(net, cur):
                if not is_silent(t):
                    continue
                nxt = fire(net, cur, t)
                if nxt is None:
                    continue
                mk = marking_key(nxt)
                if mk in visited:
                    continue
                visited.add(mk)
                found.append(nxt)
                dq.append((nxt, used + 1))
        return found

    fronts = [im]
    for label in trace:
        advanced = []
        seen_m = set()
        for mark in fronts:
            for base in tau_successors(mark):
                for t in enabled(net, base):
                    if t.label != label:
                        continue
                    nxt = fire(net, base, t)
                    if nxt is None:
                        continue
                    mk = marking_key(nxt)
                    if mk in seen_m:
                        continue
                    seen_m.add(mk)
                    advanced.append(nxt)
        if not advanced:
            return False
        fronts = advanced

    for mark in fronts:
        for terminal in tau_successors(mark):
            if markings_equal(terminal, fm):
                return True
    return False


def compare_witnesses(name: str, net, im, fm) -> dict:
    pos = {tr: replay_visible(net, im, fm, tr) for tr in POSITIVE_WITNESSES}
    neg = {tr: replay_visible(net, im, fm, tr) for tr in NEGATIVE_WITNESSES}
    return {
        "name": name,
        "positive_accepted": pos,
        "negative_accepted": neg,  # should all be False
        "positive_ok": all(pos.values()),
        "negative_ok": not any(neg.values()),
        "markings_present": markings_ok(im, fm),
    }


def safe_footprints(name: str, net, im, fm):

def footprint_pairs(fp) -> dict:
    """Best-effort extraction; PM4Py footprint containers vary by version."""
    if fp is None:
        return {}
    obj = fp[0] if isinstance(fp, (list, tuple)) and fp else fp
    out = {}
    for attr in (
        "dfg",
        "sequence",
        "parallel",
        "skip",
        "activities",
        "start_activities",
        "end_activities",
    ):
        if hasattr(obj, attr):
            out[attr] = getattr(obj, attr)
        elif isinstance(obj, dict) and attr in obj:
            out[attr] = obj[attr]
    return out

# 6. Visual-Semantics Review

A behaviorally acceptable net can still be drawn as the wrong process.

**Parallel gateways.** INV-02 needs a parallel split after `Register Change`. INV-06 needs a parallel join before the verification region. INV-11 needs a parallel join before `Release Change`. The same diagram often uses one pair of gateways for all three facts. Readers then infer that `Legal Review` also waits for `Prepare Evidence`, or that verification waits for legal. Neither inference is in the source.

**Exclusive gateways.** The legal XOR is a real exclusive choice. The loop exit after `Inspect Trial` is another. If the exporter draws only one diamond, skip-legal and “exit the loop” collapse into a single decision. If it draws no diamond and uses two outgoing sequence flows from a task, BPMN’s implicit exclusive semantics are easy to confuse with uncontrolled flow.

**Loop visualization.** Standard BPMN loop markers do not name the redo child. A back-edge that returns to `Configure Trial` without passing through `Correct Defect` looks like INV-09 failed even when the net still has the redo transition. Conversely, placing `Correct Defect` on a forward optional branch looks like a skippable side task, not the only way to iterate.

**Silent routing nodes.** Skip-legal and any converter-introduced tau become unlabeled events, empty tasks, or default sequence flows. Stakeholders either ignore them (and think legal is mandatory or that the join is exclusive) or treat them as extra work. Neither reading is the XOR+tau in the source.

**Graph layout is not order.** Force-directed or layered BPMN layout will often put `Legal Review` in a column between assess and configure because that is a common “review then test” story. INV-07 says that story is not required. Left-to-right order of shapes is not a POWL edge.

**Annotations that reduce the ambiguity**

- Caption every derived BPMN with: “Picture of accepting net `net_from_powl`. Not authoritative. Concurrent regions are unordered, not sequenced by this layout.”
- Tag the legal pair: `XOR(Legal Review, τ)` and “τ is a skip, 0-or-1, after Assess Risk only.”
- Tag the loop: `LOOP(Configure≺Inspect, Correct Defect) = A(BA)*`, “at least one iteration.”
- Draw or document three facts separately: (1) Assess ∥ Prepare, (2) Legal enabled by Assess only, (3) AND-join of {legal-XOR, loop} before release.
- Print the invariant table and the positive/negative witnesses on the diagram or in an attached note.
- Never use the rendered top-to-bottom order as an implementation spec.

# 7. Rejection Criteria

Quarantine a conversion result if any of the following hold.

1. **Technical failure of P1.** `convert_to_petri_net` or `convert_to_bpmn` raises, returns `None`, or returns a net without both an initial and a final marking.
2. **Not a workflow net, or unsound.** `check_is_workflow_net` is false, or `check_soundness` is false or cannot be evaluated because markings are missing. Deadlocks at the legal/verification join and improper completion after archive are included.
3. **Process-tree path used as a faithful carrier.** `convert_to_process_tree` raises (expected) and a caller substitutes a hand-built sequential tree; or the call succeeds and the tree language fails INV-05 or INV-07.
4. **Loss of a required interleaving.** Bounded replay rejects a positive witness, especially `Prepare Evidence` before `Assess Risk`, `Legal Review` before `Prepare Evidence`, or `Legal Review` between `Configure Trial` and `Inspect Trial`.
5. **Admission of release before verification, or release while a taken legal review is unfinished.** Any negative witness that puts `Release Change` before the last `Inspect Trial`, or before `Legal Review` when that activity is in the trace, is accepted.
6. **Collapse of optional behavior.** Every completed trace contains `Legal Review` (skip lost), or `Legal Review` can occur twice, or skip-legal no longer tokens the join (release deadlocks unless legal is taken).
7. **Broken binary loop.** Zero-iteration traces complete; `Correct Defect` can start the loop; two trial bodies occur without a redo; `Inspect Trial` can precede `Configure Trial` inside an iteration.
8. **Failed exact-final-marking test.** A labeled witness fires but the resulting marking is not equal to `fm` (tokens left on the legal branch, leftover loop tokens, missing archive token).
9. **Visible label drift.** Duplicate activity labels, skip materialised as a named task, or extra visible routing activities that change the observable alphabet.
10. **Round-trip language drift under the stated bound.** When both explorations report `exhaustive_under_bound=True`, the completed visible sets differ; or reverse BPMN→Petri invents traces in the negative-witness list.

# 8. Canonical Representation Recommendation

**Authoritative storage: the source POWL**, serialized as the constructing code or PM4Py’s POWL object, together with the invariant table in Section 1. POWL is the only representation that states *and* withholds order in the same formalism used to define the process.

**Operational semantic image: `net_from_powl` plus `im_from_powl` plus `fm_from_powl`.** Store the triple next to the POWL. It is the reference for enablement, soundness, and witness replay. It is still derivative: if it disagrees with the POWL constructor, the net is wrong.

**Stakeholder visualization: BPMN from P1 (`bpmn_from_net`), not from a process tree.** Ship it only with the annotations in Section 6 and with the witness/soundness report from Section 5.

**Derivative artifacts and required evidence**

| Artifact | Status | Must accompany |
|---|---|---|
| `source_powl` | canonical | Invariant table INV-01–INV-13 |
| `net_from_powl`, `im`, `fm` | operational derivative | Workflow-net check, soundness, positive/negative witness replay, bounded language snapshot |
| `bpmn_from_net` | visual derivative | The net it came from, gateway reading notes, statement that layout is not order |
| `tree_from_net` / `bpmn_from_tree` | not a lossless derivative | If present at all: quarantine label plus the exception or the failed INV-05/INV-07 witnesses |
| Reverse nets from BPMN | diagnostic only | Same checks as the operational net; never write-back as source |

**Process-tree intermediate: not allowed for this model.** The crossing dependencies (Assess fans out; Prepare does not; both join at release with a concurrent optional XOR) are exactly what process trees cannot say without adding edges. A failed `convert_to_process_tree` is the correct engineering result. Do not “repair” it with `SEQ`/`AND` nesting.

**Avoiding future round-trip drift**

- Edit only the POWL (or a documented POWL patch). Regenerate the net and the BPMN. Never edit the BPMN and convert back to become the new source.
- Refuse unmarked reverse nets.
- Re-run soundness, the witness sets, and the bounded explorer on every regeneration; treat any newly accepted negative witness as a breaking change.
- Keep activity labels stable and unique; do not rename silents into visible tasks to make the diagram prettier.
- If a future change becomes block-structured, a process tree may be added as an *extra* view after the same audit passes — it still does not replace POWL.

# 9. Residual Uncertainty

Even if every test in Section 5 passes, the following remain unestablished.

1. **True concurrency, not just interleaving.** Witnesses and footprints cannot distinguish “Assess and Prepare are simultaneously enabled” from “the scheduler may pick either next.” Engine-level parallel performance, interruption, and multi-instance semantics are out of scope of the trace tests.
2. **BPMN execution semantics of a specific engine.** Default sequence flows, implicit exclusive splits, OR-joins, event-based gateways, and subprocess instance lifecycle can diverge from the accepting Petri net even when the drawing was produced from that net.
3. **Observational equivalence under silent behaviour.** Bounded tau-run cutoffs, ignored silent livelocks that never reach `fm`, and branching vs linear time (when a choice is made versus which traces exist) are not settled by completed-trace set equality.
4. **Independence of legal review from mid-iteration trial states.** Tests show some interleavings; they do not prove that every reachable mid-loop marking still enables `Legal Review` exactly when the source POWL would, after exporter-added silent places.
5. **Fairness and compulsory loop exit.** The language includes arbitrarily many iterations. No test here shows that an implementation cannot livelock in verification, or conversely that it cannot force an exit after one iteration.
6. **Identity of markings and places across reverse conversion.** Exact `fm` equality is internal to one net. Two nets can have isomorphic visible languages and non-comparable place names; passing reverse-net replay does not mean the BPMN round-trip recovered the same places, invariants as typed objects, or the same token counts on silent regions.
