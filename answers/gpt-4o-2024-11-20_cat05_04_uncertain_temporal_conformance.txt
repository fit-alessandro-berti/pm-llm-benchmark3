## 1. Normalization Assumptions

1. **Interval Construction:** All timestamps are converted to feasible UTC intervals, taking into account recorded time, clock-offset bounds, rounding semantics, measurement uncertainty, and time-zone conversions. For rounding, upper and lower bounds are derived from the source system's semantics.
2. **Clock-Offset Direction:** True time = recorded clock time - clock offset. Clock-offset bounds are taken from metadata or assumptions (e.g., weak estimates).
3. **Time-Zone Conversion:** Standard time-zone conversions are applied, respecting DST transitions where explicitly specified. For Europe/Berlin (OPS-BER, LEGACY-BER), CEST applies for this data except for T-405, where DST folds create ambiguities.
4. **DST Fold Handling (T-405):** In DST folds (e.g., 2025-10-26 at 02:00–02:59 in LEGACY-BER), all possible hour-pairings consistent with additional metadata (e.g., ingestion sequence) are retained as disjoint intervals.
5. **Mobile Entry Delay (T-406):** User-entered delay of up to 5 minutes is added to the recorded time interval but treated as extending only the record time uncertainty, rather than the actual occurrence time.
6. **Inference (T-404):** Investigation Start is inferred as Containment_Complete minus the feasible duration interval (18–25 min) with retained full uncertainty.
7. **Causal Constraints (T-407):** Alert_Received is inferred to occur at or before Investigation_Start given causal assumptions and delivery latency bounds of [0, 20] minutes.
8. **Timestamp Confidence Level:** Intervals with known ambiguities or weak evidence enter classifications as POSSIBLE where overlapping time windows create uncertainty about compliance.
