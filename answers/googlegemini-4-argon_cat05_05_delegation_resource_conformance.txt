1. **Alias Normalization:** Before running any Segregation of Duties (SoD) or authority checks, all user identifiers are mapped to a canonical human identity (`human_id`). In analytical checks, we test both **Unresolved (`svc_qms_unresolved`)** and **Suspected (`Diego Martins`)** states to expose the latent RR2 self-verification risk without overstating proof.

## 2. Rule Interpretation

- **RR1 (Drafter/Approver Separation):** $\forall c \in \text{Cases}, \text{Human}(\text{Draft\_Change\_Request}_c) \neq \text{Human}(\text{Approve\_Change}_c)$. Applies universally across all risk levels (`LOW` and `HIGH`) and emergency states. Evaluated strictly post-alias normalization.
- **RR2 (Implementer/Verifier Separation):** $\forall c \in \text{Cases}, \text{Human}(\text{Implement\_Change}_c) \neq \text{Human}(\text{Verify\_Implementation}_c)$. If either activity is executed by an unattributed service account, RR2 conformance cannot be positively demonstrated, and any circumstantial link to the implementer raises a critical SoD flag.
- **RR3 (High-Risk Approval Authority):** If $\text{risk}_c = \text{HIGH}$ and $\text{emergency}_c = \text{false}$, `Approve_Change` must be executed by a principal QM (`u_as`) or a delegate holding a valid QM delegation covering event timestamp $t$, case scope $s$, and satisfying principal-absence and non-subdelegation constraints. (Note: `LOW`-risk changes do not require QM authority, though RR1 and RR2 still govern).
- **RR4 (Emergency Substitution):** If $\text{risk}_c = \text{HIGH}$ and $\text{emergency}_c = \text{true}$, `Approve_Change` may be performed by the standing IC (`u_dm`) or a valid IC delegate (`u_fl` under `D-IC-03`), provided the safety rationale is credible. Furthermore, `Retrospective_QM_Review` must be completed within **two business days** by a QM or a valid QM delegate *whose delegation covers the scope of the reviewed change*.
  - **Business-Day Counting:** We apply standard pharmaceutical QA day-counting (and cross-check against exact 48-hour elapsed business time). For an emergency approval/implementation on Tuesday `2026-08-11`, Business Day 1 ends Wednesday `2026-08-12 23:59 UTC`, and Business Day 2 ends Thursday `2026-08-13 23:59 UTC`. If counted by exact elapsed hours, `2026-08-11 13:00` plus 48 hours expires `2026-08-13 13:00 UTC`. Under both methods, a review on Friday `2026-08-14 16:00 UTC` (`CHG-505`) is **late** (3 business days / 75 elapsed hours).
- **Principal Action While on Leave:** Recorded annual leave satisfies the "principal is absent" condition for a delegate acting in good faith (`CHG-501`, `CHG-503`), and weekend absence outside normal working hours (`2026-08-15`) satisfies `D-QM-02` (`CHG-509`). When Asha (`u_as`) logs in via VPN during her leave on `2026-08-13 09:15–09:40` (`CHG-504`), a principal retains inherent standing role authority (`QM`) unless formally suspended; however, acting while recorded absent creates a governance anomaly and, because security cannot exclude credential sharing, introduces an identity assurance failure. Conversely, when a *delegate* (Ben) is on certified sick leave (`2026-08-13 12:00` onward) and denies performing an action logged from an office IP (`CHG-506`), the event is treated as unauthorized credential misuse.
- **Later Database Entry for Earlier Delegation (`D-QM-01`):** In GxP environments, contemporaneous controlled documentation governs substantive authority. Because `D-QM-01` was executed via a cryptographically signed PDF on `2026-08-09 18:00 UTC` (prior to the effective window starting `2026-08-10 00:00`), substantive delegation existed at the time of `CHG-501` (`2026-08-11`) and `CHG-503` (`2026-08-12 11:30`). The delayed QMS database migration record (`2026-08-12 09:15`) represents a **master-data synchronization lag**, not a retroactive fabrication of authority.
- **RR5 & RR6 (Identity Evidence and Suspicion vs. Proof):** Technical identity anomalies (`svc_qms`, alias splits) must be resolved before evaluating control objectives. High-frequency pairings (`Clara -> Ben`) and reciprocal approvals (`Ben <-> Clara`) are treated as behavioral risk indicators requiring corroboration, not standalone proof of collusion.

def evaluate_approval_event(row):
    ts = row["time:timestamp"]
    actor = row["human_strict"]
    risk = row["risk"]
    scope = row["scope"]
    emerg = str(row["emergency"]).lower() == "true"
    act = row["concept:name"]
    flags = []

    # Check if actor is on recorded leave at event timestamp
    actor_leave = absences[
        (absences["human"] == actor)
        & (absences["start"] <= ts)
        & (ts <= absences["end"])
    ]
    if not actor_leave.empty:
        flags.append(f"ACTOR_ON_{actor_leave.iloc[0]['type'].upper()}")

    if act == "Approve_Change":
        if risk == "LOW":
            flags.append("LOW_RISK_STANDING_OK")
        elif risk == "HIGH" and not emerg:
            if actor == "Asha Rao":
                flags.append("STANDING_QM_OK")
            else:
                d = delegations[
                    (delegations["delegate"] == actor)
                    & (delegations["role"] == "QM")
                    & (delegations["start"] <= ts)
                    & (ts <= delegations["end"])
                ]
                if d.empty:
                    flags.append("NO_ACTIVE_QM_DELEGATION")
                else:
                    del_row = d.iloc[0]
                    if del_row["scope"] != scope:
                        flags.append(
                            f"SCOPE_MISMATCH({del_row['scope']}!={scope})"
                        )
                    else:
                        flags.append(f"VALID_DELEGATION({del_row['id']})")
                        if pd.notna(del_row["db_recorded"]) and ts < del_row[
                            "db_recorded"
                        ]:
                            flags.append("PRE_DB_MIGRATION_PDF_RELIANCE")
        elif risk == "HIGH" and emerg:
            if actor == "Diego Martins":
                flags.append("STANDING_IC_OK")
            else:
                d = delegations[
                    (delegations["delegate"] == actor)
                    & (delegations["role"] == "IC")
                    & (delegations["start"] <= ts)
                    & (ts <= delegations["end"])
                ]
                if d.empty:
                    flags.append("NO_ACTIVE_IC_DELEGATION")
                else:
                    del_row = d.iloc[0]
                    if not del_row["valid_instrument"]:
                        flags.append(
                            f"INVALID_SUBDELEGATION({del_row['id']})"
                        )
                    elif del_row["scope"] != scope:
                        flags.append("IC_SCOPE_MISMATCH")
                    else:
                        flags.append(f"VALID_IC_DELEGATION({del_row['id']})")

    elif act == "Retrospective_QM_Review":
        if actor == "Asha Rao":
            flags.append("STANDING_QM_RETRO_OK")
        else:
            d = delegations[
                (delegations["delegate"] == actor)
                & (delegations["role"] == "QM")
                & (delegations["start"] <= ts)
                & (ts <= delegations["end"])
            ]
            if d.empty:
                flags.append("NO_ACTIVE_QM_DELEGATION_FOR_RETRO")
            else:
                del_row = d.iloc[0]
                if del_row["scope"] != scope:
                    flags.append(
                        f"RETRO_SCOPE_MISMATCH({del_row['scope']}!={scope})"
                    )
                else:
                    flags.append(f"VALID_RETRO_DELEGATION({del_row['id']})")
    return ", ".join(flags)

## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation Creation & Scope Enforcement:** Eliminate ad-hoc email delegations (`D-IC-04`). Require all delegations to be created as structured objects inside the QMS with hard-coded constraints: `start_time`, `end_time`, `allowed_scopes` (enforced via system drop-down matching case scope attributes), and a boolean `allow_subdelegation = False` flag enforced by the workflow engine.
2. **Effective-Time Recording & Synchronization:** Decouple QMS availability from manual database migrations. Prohibit paper/PDF delegations (`D-QM-01`) except during certified QMS downtime; if an offline PDF is used, lock HIGH-risk case closure until the delegation record and its trusted timestamp are ingested into the QMS audit trail.
3. **Principal Availability Integration:** Integrate the HR Time & Attendance system with the Identity Provider (IdP) and QMS.
   - When an employee is on **certified sick leave** (`Ben Koch` on `08-13 12:00`), automatically suspend approval privileges or trigger step-up biometric/FIDO re-authentication and security alerts.
   - When a principal (`Asha Rao`) is on **annual leave**, require an explicit "Temporary Return / Override" attestation before granting VPN/QMS approval access, temporarily suspending active absentee delegations (`D-QM-01`) for that window.
4. **Alias Management & Canonical Identity:** Consolidate multi-account records (`u_bk` and `b.koch`) under a single immutable Employee UUID in the IdP. Enforce SoD checks (`RR1`, `RR2`) against the canonical Employee UUID rather than the login string.
5. **Individual Authentication & Service Account Deprecation:** Prohibit `svc_qms` from executing human workflow transitions (`Verify_Implementation`). On shared shop-floor (`WS-DM-04`) and verifier (`WS-IV-02`) terminals, require session-level FIDO2/badge-tap re-authentication for every electronic signature (21 CFR Part 11 / EU GMP Annex 11 compliance) and pass the human principal directly to the QMS event log.
6. **Emergency Review SLA & Scope Guardrails:** Configure automated timers for `emergency = true` cases that escalate to the Principal QM and Site Quality Head at +24 hours and +36 hours, and block `Retrospective_QM_Review` sign-off by delegates whose delegation scope (`production_equipment`) does not match the emergency system scope (`cooling_system`).
7. **Pair Rotation & Network Monitoring:** Establish a policy requiring secondary QM review or pair rotation when a single Drafter–Approver pair (`Clara -> Ben`) exceeds a threshold (e.g., >60% of cell HIGH-risk changes over 30 days), and run automated monthly PM4Py handover/working-together scripts as supervisory QA inputs.
8. **Evidential Retention:** Mandate 90-day synchronized retention of workstation active-session logs, FIDO/MFA logs, VPN source telemetry, and room badge-in/badge-out logs, alongside CCTV coverage of shared GMP terminals.

## 7. Executive Conclusion

### Systemic Judgment
Across the 10 investigated change-control cases, apparent nonconformance splits into three distinct root-cause categories:
1. **Genuine Authority & Segregation Failures (60% — `CHG-502`, `CHG-505`, `CHG-506`, `CHG-507`, `CHG-508`, `CHG-510`):** Six cases represent substantive control failures. Three involve direct **RR1 self-approval** (`CHG-502` by Clara, `CHG-508` by Ben via alias `b.koch`, and `CHG-510` by Clara via void subdelegation `D-IC-04`). Two involve **scope/authority violations** (`CHG-507` approved by an Independent Verifier without QM authority; `CHG-505` and `CHG-510` retrospectively reviewed by Ben outside his `production_equipment` delegation scope, with `CHG-505` also violating the 2-business-day SLA). Most critically, `CHG-506` (and potentially `CHG-505`'s retrospective review) involves **unauthorized account usage while Ben was on certified sick leave**.
2. **Valid Substitutions Masked by Data/Technical Debt (20% — `CHG-501`, `CHG-509`):** Two cases met the underlying control objective through valid delegations (`D-QM-01` and `D-QM-02`) and proper human separation, but generated audit exceptions due to a 2-day QMS database migration lag (`CHG-501`) and a terminal wrapper logging `svc_qms` despite strong FIDO-token attribution to Eva (`CHG-509`).
3. **Indeterminate Identity & Shared-Terminal Exposure (20% — `CHG-503`, `CHG-504`):** `CHG-503` presents a high risk of **RR2 self-verification** by Diego Martins masked by `svc_qms` on his assigned workstation (`WS-DM-04`), while `CHG-504` is technically authorized if Asha acted personally via VPN during her leave, but remains indeterminate until credential sharing is ruled out.

### Uncertainty & Highest-Priority Investigations
While the `Clara <-> Ben` pair concentration (18/23 HIGH-risk approvals) and reciprocal LOW-risk approvals are partly explainable by shared production-cell assignment, the combination of alias self-approval (`CHG-508`), direct self-approval (`CHG-502`, `CHG-510`), and office activity under Ben's account during his certified sick leave (`CHG-506`) elevates collusion/credential-sharing risk from theoretical to acute.

**Three Highest-Priority Investigations:**
1. **Forensic Investigation of `CHG-506` (and `CHG-505` Review) Sick-Leave Activity:** Cross-reference the exact office workstation IP used by `u_bk` on `2026-08-13 13:00` and `2026-08-14 16:00` with physical room badge logs and Clara Stein's workstation/badge activity to determine if Clara (or another actor) used Ben's credentials.
2. **Retrospective QA Re-Verification of `CHG-503`, `CHG-505`, `CHG-507`, and `CHG-510`:** Require principal QM (`Asha Rao`) and an independent verifier to perform physical and technical re-verification of the cooling-system (`CI-44`) and production-equipment changes that lacked valid QM approval or independent verification (`WS-DM-04`).
