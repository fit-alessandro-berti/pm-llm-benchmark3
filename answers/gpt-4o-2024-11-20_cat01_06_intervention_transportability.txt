- **Shared mechanisms:** Concurrency plausibly increases overlap, shortens waiting before `Assessment`, and reduces overall cycle time under non-bottlenecked scenarios. Shared API latency, workload of verification reviewers, and rework mechanisms also apply across both sites.
- **Site-specific mechanisms:** Site B faces stricter legal constraints (e.g., originals required, legal reviews), IT-lock-induced bottlenecks, and more fragmented roles, all of which limit transferability. Site B's heavy-tailed delays disproportionately impact complex cases.
- **Effect modifiers:** Originals, legal review, case locking, and role specialization are key modifiers. Increased concurrency at Site B without addressing legal/originals-specific delays risks amplifying bottlenecks and rework.
- **Control similarity issue:** Identical process flow does not imply transportability due to differences in IT locks, legal workflows, high-complexity case volume, and delays caused by originals. Overlap at Site B will not scale uniformly without structural adjustments.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

- **Eligibility rules:** Digital-only cases initially; legal-review and originals-required excluded in first phase.  
- **Concurrent tasks:** Trigger both `Identity Check` and `Document Check` immediately post-`Register` for eligible cases only where originals are not required.  
- **Statutory prerequisites:** Originals channel fully respected; cases requiring supplement or legal consent must finish those tasks sequentially.  
- **Queue and WIP controls:** Enforce daily queue huddles for coordinators; limit reviewers to manageable concurrency.  
- **Resource responsibilities:** Digital-only subgroup focused within existing team capacity. Role specialization respected; cross-segment support preemptively scoped.  
- **Legal-review handling:** All legal-review cycles remain; routing logs explicitly code re-checks via `Supplement`.  
- **Logging:** Mandate concurrent overlap timestamps enter within SLA.

**Partial-order relation (POWL):**

```text
Register -> (Identity Check || Document Check)
Identity Check -> (Assessment)
Document Check -> Assessment
Assessment -> (Legal Review || Approve || Reject)
Legal Review -> (Approve || Reject || Request Supplement)
Request Supplement -> Document Check
```
