The only clearly shared mechanism is a critical-path reduction on verification. If Identity Check and Document Check are both enabled, use independent data, and can be owned at the same time, raising `true_overlap` can cut `waiting_before_assessment` and thereby `cycle_time`. Site A’s digital share, independent external queries, cross-training, and simultaneous-ownership CMS make that path plausible there.

Most of what moved at Site A is site-specific or a co-intervention. The registry API, two extra document reviewers, daily huddles, and a marketing-driven drop in high-complexity share all open back-doors from calendar time into waiting and cycle time. Site B adds mechanisms that do not exist in A’s trees: statutory `originals_required`, a 40% legal branch with supplement loops, specialized single-skill pools, and an IT case lock. Those are not nuisances around the same AND-split; they change which transitions are enabled and who can fire them.

Effect modifiers are `originals_required`, `digital_documents`, `role_specialization`, `IT_case_lock`, the four capacity nodes, `case_complexity`, and `legal_capacity`. Selection nodes `S_*` mark that these differ by site, so A’s before/after contrast does not identify a transport effect for B. Parallelization can raise `rework` and `appeal` if Document Check is created before a complete packet, or if faster simple cases saturate assessment and legal queues.

A similar control-flow diagram therefore does not guarantee transportability. A’s “after” tree omits Originals Receipt, Legal Review, Request Supplement, role assignment, locks, clocks, and capacities. Two models can share an AND of two labels and still differ in enabled partial order, interference, and outcome laws. Transport needs the same active partial order on startable work and comparable modifiers, not a visually similar happy path.

## 5. Quantitative transport scenarios

Site A’s median fell 9 h (41 → 32) and p90 fell 18 h, but that contrast is a bundle: mix 48% → 40% high-complexity, +2 FTE, API, huddles, and messier timestamps. A defensible reading is that true overlap contributed only part of the −6 h wait change, on the order of identity service (≈3–4 h) plus some queue time in a digital, unlocked pool. Site B’s pre-assessment wait (20 h) is dominated by originals (median 18 h in 62%) and a longer document service (8.1 h). Identity is already often sequenced with originals (DFG: Register→Identity 2,050, Register→Originals 1,860, Identity→Originals 1,020). Therefore any copied AND is diluted by eligibility, and downstream pools (3 assessors, 2 legal) can recapture the time.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|
| Optimistic structural transfer | Lock waived; both checks startable only when legal; ID ∥ Doc on digital cases; ID ∥ Originals on the rest; no extra FTE/API/huddles; assessment/legal slack | ~38% full AND; ~62% partial (ID with originals); 100% exposed to a modified rule | −2 to −5 h overall; digital cell −4 to −8 h | −1 to −6 h (tail still mostly originals + legal) | Rework 17% → 16–18%; appeals flat | 25 |
| Digital-claim-only transfer | Treat only no-originals cases; originals path unchanged; lock solved in that cell; capacities held fixed | ~38% (about 1,290 / 3,400) | Population −1 to −3 h; treated stratum −4 to −8 h | 0 to −3 h (p90 cases rarely digital-simple) | Quality stable if Document Check starts on a complete packet | 50 |

The legal/rework and bottleneck-shift scenarios are the required “median/average better, tail worse” pattern: simple digital cases leave the verification wait, while originals/legal cases age behind a busier scarce pool. None of these ranges is an identified transport functional of A’s −9 h. They are scenario maps from A’s proximal wait change, B’s 62% originals gate, 18 h originals delay, 40% legal share, and tiny downstream pools.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

Do not copy “create Identity Check and Document Check simultaneously after every Register.” Replace it with **startable-set parallelization**.

Eligibility
- After Register, compute a startable set from statutory flags known at intake.
- **Cell D (digital, originals not required):** startable = {Identity Check, Document Check}.
- **Cell O (originals required):** startable = {Identity Check, Originals Receipt}; Document Check is created only when Originals Receipt completes.
- **Cell L (public-sector or high-value):** same verification rule as D or O; Legal Review remains mandatory after Assessment and is never put in the verification AND.
- Exclude a case from any new concurrency if the CMS cannot grant two active verification owners.

Concurrency and statutory gates
- Register ≺ every other task.
- Identity Check may run concurrently with Originals Receipt and, in Cell D only, with Document Check.
- Originals Receipt ≺ Document Check whenever originals are required.
- {Identity Check, Document Check} ≺ Assessment.
- Assessment ≺ Legal Review when required.
- Legal Review may issue Request Supplement; then Document Check and Assessment may repeat; Identity Check is not automatically repeated unless the supplement reason names identity.
- XOR(Approve, Reject) ≺ Notify.
- Never start Document Check, Assessment, or a decision before their predecessors.

Queue and WIP
- Count only *startable* tasks toward WIP.
- Cap open startable verification tasks per identity reviewer and per document reviewer (pilot default: 4).
- Cap cases waiting for Assessment and for Legal Review; if either cap binds, stop pulling new Cell D cases into the AND.
- Keep any huddle in both pilot and control.

Resource responsibilities
- Identity pool owns Identity Check only.
- Document pool owns Document Check and receipt-complete triggering.
- Assessors own Assessment; legal reviewers own Legal Review and Request Supplement.
- No silent cross-role pickup except a pre-declared backup list.

Legal and supplement
- Legal Review stays after Assessment.
- Supplement re-opens Document Check (and Assessment) under the same originals rule; do not re-create a parallel Identity Check by default.

Logging
- For every verification task: create_ts, lock_owner, first_action_ts, last_action_ts, enabled_ts (when statutory predecessors completed).
- Flag `true_overlap` only when first-action intervals overlap and both tasks were enabled.
- Record eligibility cell, lock-bypass, and supplement reason.

POWL-style specification (partial order + choice + loop)

```text
Register
  → PO{ Identity Check  ∥  XOR(
           Originals Receipt → Document Check_init,   # Cell O
           Document Check_init                        # Cell D
         ) }
  → Assessment_init
  → XOR( τ ,  *( Legal Review , Request Supplement → Document Check_redo → Assessment_redo ) )
  → XOR( Approve , Reject )
  → Notify

Edges that must hold:
  Register ≺ Identity Check
  Register ≺ Originals Receipt
  Register ≺ Document Check_init          # only if originals not required
  Originals Receipt ≺ Document Check_init # if originals required
  Identity Check ≺ Assessment_init
  Document Check_init ≺ Assessment_init
  Assessment_init ≺ Legal Review          # if required
  Assessment_init ≺ {Approve, Reject}     # if not required
  Legal Review ≺ {Approve, Reject}        # if no supplement
  Request Supplement ≺ Document Check_redo ≺ Assessment_redo ≺ Legal Review
  {Approve, Reject} ≺ Notify
```

The pilot separates mechanism failure from implementation failure by measuring fidelity and outcomes in the same cells. Implementation failure is low `true_overlap`, Document Check created before it is enabled, lock-serialized owners, or first-action times that do not match create times. Mechanism failure is high fidelity with no wait reduction, or with wait reduction that reappears as Assessment/Legal delay, rework, supplements, appeals, or a worse p90. If fidelity is poor and outcomes are null, fix unlock/WIP/task creation and re-estimate. If fidelity is high and outcomes are null or harmful, the A mechanism does not transport—do not scale. Negative-control steps that the AND does not touch should stay flat; movement there signals mix, huddles, or measurement, the same confounders that make A’s before/after non-transportable.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
"""Site A trees and a Site B startable-set POWL (not a sequential copy)."""

import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator


# --- 1) Site A process trees -------------------------------------------------
tree_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register', 'Identity Check', 'Document Check', 'Assessment', "
    "X('Approve', 'Reject'), 'Notify')"
)
tree_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register', +('Identity Check', 'Document Check'), 'Assessment', "
    "X('Approve', 'Reject'), 'Notify')"
)

# --- 2) Accepting Petri nets for Site A --------------------------------------
net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_before)
net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_after)

# --- 3–4) Site B candidate POWL: partial order + optional legal loop ---------
# Unique node instances (same label may appear on redo).
register = Transition(label="Register")
identity = Transition(label="Identity Check")
originals = Transition(label="Originals Receipt")
document_o = Transition(label="Document Check")      # after originals
document_d = Transition(label="Document Check")      # digital / no originals
document_r = Transition(label="Document Check")      # supplement redo
assessment = Transition(label="Assessment")
assessment_r = Transition(label="Assessment")        # supplement redo
legal = Transition(label="Legal Review")
supplement = Transition(label="Request Supplement")
approve = Transition(label="Approve")
reject = Transition(label="Reject")
notify = Transition(label="Notify")
skip_legal = SilentTransition()

# Document-side XOR: originals gate vs immediately startable Document Check.
doc_after_originals = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[originals, document_o])
doc_after_originals.order.add_edge(originals, document_o)
document_choice = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[doc_after_originals, document_d])

# Identity || document-side work. No order edge => concurrent once Register fired.
verification = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[identity, document_choice])

# Assessment waits for BOTH branches of verification (the whole PO).
# Supplement loop: *(Legal Review, Request Supplement -> Document Check -> Assessment)
supplement_redo = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[supplement, document_r, assessment_r])
supplement_redo.order.add_edge(supplement, document_r)
supplement_redo.order.add_edge(document_r, assessment_r)
legal_loop = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.LOOP, children=[legal, supplement_redo])
legal_choice = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[skip_legal, legal_loop])
decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[approve, reject])

# Root strict partial order: Register < verification < Assessment < legal? < decision < Notify
root = StrictPartialOrder(
    nodes=[register, verification, assessment, legal_choice, decision, notify]
)
root.order.add_edge(register, verification)
root.order.add_edge(verification, assessment)   # Assessment after identity AND document path
root.order.add_edge(assessment, legal_choice)
root.order.add_edge(legal_choice, decision)
root.order.add_edge(decision, notify)

site_b_powl = root

# --- 5) POWL -> accepting Petri net ------------------------------------------
net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(site_b_powl)

# --- 6) Workflow-net / soundness checks --------------------------------------
def report(name, net, im, fm):
    wf = pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net)
    sound = pm4py.check_soundness(net, im, fm)
    print(f"{name}: workflow_net={wf} soundness={sound}")

report("Site A before", net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before)
report("Site A after", net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after)
report("Site B candidate", net_b, im_b, fm_b)

# --- 7) Optional playout ------------------------------------------------------
for name, net, im, fm in [
    ("A-before", net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before),
    ("A-after", net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after),
    ("B-candidate", net_b, im_b, fm_b),
]:
    try:
        log = pm4py.play_out(net, im, fm)
        print(f"--- sample traces ({name}) ---")
        for i, trace in enumerate(log):
            if i >= 5:
                break
            labels = [e.get("concept:name") for e in trace]
            print(labels)
    except Exception as exc:
        print(f"play_out skipped for {name}: {exc}")

# --- 8) What these models cannot encode --------------------------------------
# Data-dependent legal / originals flags: XOR/LOOP is not the intake predicate
#   "originals required" or "public-sector/high-value"; both branches are
#   offered as control-flow choices, not as case-attribute guards.
# Resource capacity and specialization: no 4/7/3/2 pools, no six-task WIP cap,
#   no huddles, no pairing of two specialists.
# Case locks and simultaneous ownership: the AND/PO says both verification
#   nodes are enabled; it cannot say the CMS allows only one owner.
```

## 9. Recommendation

**conditional go**

Transfer the *mechanism*, not the *model screenshot* and not Site A’s nine-hour median. The transferable object is startable-set parallelization: after Register, run Identity Check beside whatever document-side work is already legally enabled. In the digital no-originals cell that is Document Check. In the originals cell that is Originals Receipt only. Assessment still waits for both checks. Legal Review, Request Supplement, and the document–assessment redo stay mandatory. WIP is counted on startable tasks, and true overlap is defined from first-action times.

Do not copy universal dual-create after Register, Site A’s cross-trained six-task pool, the unadjusted −9 h / −18 h before/after numbers, or any relaxation of originals-before-document. Those pieces are either illegal at B, unidentified at A (API, +2 FTE, huddles, mix, timestamps), or actively harmful under a one-owner lock. Creating a Document Check token for the 62% originals cases is the failure mode to refuse: the task cannot start, may lock the case, inflates recorded overlap, and can delay Identity Check.

Enter first with Cell D0: digital, originals not required, not public-sector/high-value, on teams that can dual-own verification. That cell is the only place B’s enabled partial order resembles A’s AND. Expected benefit is a modest cut in wait before Assessment—roughly identity service plus some queue time in that cell (population median likely about 1–3 h if the rest of the mix is untouched), not a site-wide 9 h. Quality should stay flat. Risks are lock theater, assessor/legal congestion, and a better median with a worse p90 if simple cases jump the queue.

Expand into other digital cases only if D0 shows high true overlap, shorter wait, stable rework/supplements/appeals, and no p90 deterioration in untreated or legal cases. Modify if fidelity is poor (locks, back-entry, unstartable tasks) or if Assessment/Legal queues absorb the gain. Abandon the Site A copy if Document Check starts before required originals, if rework or appeals rise, or if the only “success” is recorded overlap without first-action concurrency. A’s evidence never identified a unique transport effect; B should not pretend otherwise.

## 10. Assumption register

1. Digital no-originals cases at B have information-independent identity and document work, as claimed for A — needed to justify an AND rather than a forced sequence — test by conflict/rework reason codes and whether assessors wait on mid-flight identity updates — if false, even Cell D0 should stay sequential.

2. Originals-required and legal-review flags are known at or immediately after Register — needed for startable-set eligibility — test by intake-field completeness and later reversals — if flags arrive late, dual-create will violate statute or under-treat.

3. Site A’s 41 → 32 h median is a bundled co-intervention effect, not the isolated AND-split effect — needed to stop transporting −9 h as a target — test by decomposing A-style contrasts only inside a controlled B pilot — if one instead treats −9 h as causal, B will over-promise and over-rollout.

4. Median originals delay (~18 h) does not fall because a Document Check task exists — needed to reject naive dual-create on Cell O — test by comparing originals arrival times T0 vs T1 — if false (staff chase originals harder), Cell O might still gain, but that is a reminder intervention, not parallelization.

5. Dual ownership can be implemented for Cell D0 without destroying event order — needed for true_overlap — test lock logs vs first_action_ts — if false, the IT-lock failure scenario applies and T1 must wait on a CMS change.

6. Legal-review and supplement probabilities do not rise merely because verification finishes earlier — needed to treat legal load as a modifier, not an outcome of AND — test supplement rates in D1 vs T0 with fidelity held high — if false, faster verification is quality-negative and the legal/rework scenario dominates.

7. Intake mix and verification FTE stay stable over the pilot, unlike A’s marketing shift and +2 reviewers — needed so B’s contrast is not another confounded before/after — test weekly complexity share and roster — if mix or headcount moves, freeze expansion and re-analyze as a co-intervention.

8. Rework, supplements, and 90-day appeals are sensitive enough sentinels for harm — needed for stop rules — test against complaint/quality-audit samples — if they miss silent defect substitution, add an audit sample before any scale-up.
