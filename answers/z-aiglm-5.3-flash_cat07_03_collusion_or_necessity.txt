def edges(net):
    g = net if isinstance(net, (nx.MultiDiGraph, nx.DiGraph)) else nx.MultiDiGraph(net)

# 5. Evidence that would materially change the assessment

**Would increase concern:**
- V-77x bank changes with **no maintenance ticket**, or tickets created *after* the change/payment; ticket requester outside vendor master.
- New beneficiary accounts **not on the migration list**; cross-bank/cross-country changes; one beneficiary account across multiple vendor IDs; account-name mismatch vs vendor legal name; repeated re-changes of the same vendor; changes clustered immediately before payment runs or quarter-end.
- `manual_upload` events performed with one person's credentials from another person's terminal/time window, or `entered_by` values matching no roster.
- Shared-account approvals that access logs attribute to a person **without delegation authority** or outside their shift.
- Non-emergency four-eyes PO violations **surviving mapping cleanup**; approvals preceding quote receipt; amount bands sitting just below approval thresholds across the same pair.
- The 11-case loop corrections **changing the beneficiary account after Payment Approval**.
- Non-batch self-justifications with copy-pasted boilerplate, edited after upload, or absent for high-amount overrides.
- ERP audit trail showing vendor-master records modified/deleted without corresponding change documents.

**Would reduce concern:**
- 90% of V-77x changes matched to migration work orders with dual sign-off; the remainder covered by callback-verification logs.
- Documented mapping-table defect (with change history predating the review period) after which the residual four-eyes count collapses to a handful of emergency cases.
- A named-assistant roster with delegation-of-authority and approval limits, and SSO logs consistent with `entered_by` per timestamp.
- A quarter_close_upload manifest showing original justifications were timely and written by a different person, merely uploaded late.
- Framework contracts and quotes on file for Carla+Gita high-amount cases; timely, independent justifications.
- Facilities tickets matching Dan+Hugo weekend overrides; loop corrections limited to formatting with bank-rejection codes.
- A written policy combining Vendor Check + bank-change duties with a compensating payment-level dual control.

# 6. Cautious audit-report wording

Scope and limitation. This review covered a quarter of procurement-to-payment event data. The analysis identified a limited number of segregation-of-duties exceptions and several data-quality deficiencies. At this stage, the evidence does not establish misconduct, fraud, or collusion, and no such conclusion should be drawn or implied. Most indicators — rapid approvals in the emergency medical and weekend repair channels, and repeated handover loops between the same buyer and approver — are consistent with documented emergency-handling practices, provided delegation records and retrospective justifications prove to be in order. Control exceptions are not, in themselves, evidence of misconduct; they are control weaknesses requiring remediation and follow-up. The most concerning subgroup is the set of nine cases in which VendorMaster_Lena recorded Change Vendor Bank Details and AP_Kim approved the related payment, concentrated in vendor family V-77x, predominantly high-value, entered through a manual source system, and executed within minutes. This pattern does not fit the emergency profile that plausibly explains the other clusters, and it warrants priority review. Even here, a benign explanation is plausible: the recent banking-platform migration required legitimate mass maintenance of bank details, although ticket linkage is currently missing from the event log. Data limitations are material: a shared approval account, a bot-to-human resource-mapping error in legacy_erp, and a quarter-close spreadsheet upload mean that several apparent violations may be recording artifacts. We recommend targeted follow-up: validate V-77x bank changes against maintenance tickets and verification logs; reconcile shared-account approvals to named, authorized individuals; correct the resource mapping and re-run conformance checks; review residual self-justified overrides; and strengthen retrospective-justification timeliness. Conclusions should be updated once these steps are complete.
