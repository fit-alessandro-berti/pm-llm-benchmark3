## 1. Temporal Data Semantics

I treat the log as an **event-time update stream**, not as a table of final case attributes.

- **State reconstruction**
  - For each case, events are ordered by `time:timestamp`, then by `event_index` where timestamps are equal.
  - A nonblank attribute cell is an update effective at that event’s timestamp/order.
  - A blank cell means “no update at this event,” not “set to null.”
  - The state at any event is the latest known value for each attribute from all prior events in the ordered stream, including same-timestamp events with a lower `event_index`.
  - The state at `Release_Funds` is therefore the state after all events strictly before the release, plus any same-timestamp events with a lower `event_index`, plus any attribute values carried by the release event itself. In this log, release events do not carry substantive attribute updates.

- **Forward-fill discipline**
  - Values are forward-filled only from past to future within a case.
  - Future values are never copied backward. For example, TX-305’s settlement amount on 2026-04-07 is not treated as the release-time amount on 2026-04-06 unless separate evidence establishes that the release-time amount was already fixed and known.

- **Same-timestamp ordering**
  - When two events share a timestamp, `event_index` is decisive.
  - Example: TX-311 `Enhanced_Review` at index 3 occurs before `Update_Jurisdiction` at index 4, even though both are at `2026-04-06T16:20:00Z`. Therefore the release at `16:30` sees jurisdiction `DE`, but the enhanced review occurred before the jurisdiction update.

- **Material changes**
  - For amount-dependent obligations, an amount increase of more than 20 percent after a review is treated as a material change that invalidates that review for amount-dependent obligations.
  - A decrease does not by itself invalidate a review.
  - An increase from an unknown prior amount is not safely classifiable as material or nonmaterial without additional evidence.
  - Other attribute changes are material when they change the activation of a rule, such as risk becoming `HIGH`, customer type becoming `PEP`, or jurisdiction changing in a way that affects a rule trigger.

- **Final attributes are not retroactive**
  - The final case state is not copied back to earlier events.
  - For example, TX-305’s later settlement amount is final/settlement-time evidence, not automatically release-time state.
  - Similarly, a later exemption record is record-time evidence; it may prove earlier approval, but it does not by itself make the exemption effective earlier.

- **Four time dimensions**
  - **Event time:** the timestamp in the log.
  - **Effective time:** when the business change is effective. Unless evidence says otherwise, I default effective time to event time.
  - **Record time:** when a database record was inserted. Example: TX-302 and TX-310 `Record_Exemption` events occur after release.
  - **Evidence time:** when proof such as a qualified signature, hash, certificate, witness statement, or outage record was created or became available.

- **Late evidence**
  - A late database record can be evidence of earlier approval, but it is not automatically conclusive.
  - A backdated displayed field alone is insufficient.
  - Independent or strong technical evidence, such as a qualified digital signature with a reliable timestamp, can support earlier effectiveness.
  - Self-interested oral evidence, especially where technical metadata contradicts the claimed time, is weak.

**Formal pseudo-logic**

Let:

```text
e1  e2  iff  ts(e1) < ts(e2)
              or (ts(e1) = ts(e2) and index(e1) < index(e2))

e1  e2  iff  e1  e2 or e1 = e2
```

State:

```text
state(c, attr, r) = v
  iff there is an update u in case c with u  r,
      update(u, attr, v),
      and no other update u' of attr in c satisfies u  u'  r.
```

Material amount increase:

```text
material_amount_increase(m)
  iff Update_Amount(m),
      prior_amount = state_before(m, amount),
      new_amount = update_value(m, amount),
      prior_amount is known,
      new_amount > 1.2 * prior_amount.
```

R1:

```text
R1_required(c, r)
  iff Release_Funds(r),
      state(c, jurisdiction, r) = J,
      state(c, amount, r) = A,
      J  DE,
      A  100000.

R1_satisfied(c, r)
  iff R1_required(c, r)
      and (
            valid_exemption(c, r, R1)
         or (
                e Enhanced_Review(e)
               such that e  r
               and ¬ m material_change(c, m)
                    such that e  m  r
            )
          ).

R1_indeterminate(c, r)
  iff Release_Funds(r)
      and (state(c, amount, r) unknown or state(c, jurisdiction, r) unknown)
      and trigger cannot be established or excluded.
```

R2:

```text
R2_required(c, r)
  iff Release_Funds(r)
      and (state(c, risk_level, r) = HIGH
           or state(c, customer_type, r) = PEP).

relevant_R2_trigger(c, r) =
  latest event t  r that set risk_level to HIGH or customer_type to PEP,
  according to the triggered attribute(s) at release.

R2_satisfied(c, r)
  iff R2_required(c, r)
      and  e Enhanced_Review(e), a Compliance_Approve(a)
      such that relevant_R2_trigger(c, r)  e  a  r.
```

R3:

```text
R3_required(c, r)
  iff Release_Funds(r),
      state(c, jurisdiction, r) = J,
       l Sanctions_List_Update(l)
      such that l adds J to sanctions list,
      l  r,
      and latest Sanctions_Screen before r is absent or precedes l.

R3_satisfied(c, r)
  iff R3_required(c, r)
      and  s Sanctions_Screen(s)
      such that l  s  r.
```

R4:

```text
review_invalidated_by_amount(c, review, r)
  iff review is Standard_Review or Enhanced_Review,
      review  r,
       m material_amount_increase(m)
      such that review  m  r.

increase_from_unknown(c, m)
  iff Update_Amount(m)
      and prior_amount unknown.

increase_from_unknown does not safely classify invalidation.
```

R5, defeasible exemption logic:

```text
valid_exemption(c, r, rule)
  iff  x exemption referenced or known for c,
      approved_by(x, Head_Compliance),
      effective_from(x)  r,
      expiry(x)  r,
      scope_matches(x, state(c, r)),
      evidence_supports_pre_release_approval(x, r).

defeater(x, r)
  if certificate_created(x)  r
     or only_backdated_displayed_field(x)
     or only_interested_witness(x)
     or no reliable approval evidence.

late_record(x, r) does not by itself defeat validity,
but late_record + backdate + weak evidence is insufficient.
```

R6:

```text
if decisive_attribute_unknown(c, r)
   and no other evidence resolves it,
then classification = INDETERMINATE.
```

R7:

```text
For all evaluations:
  use state(c, attr, r) as of release event r,
  order same-timestamp events by event_index,
  do not substitute final case state for earlier event-time state.
```

## 4. Ambiguities and Alternative Readings

**TX-302**

- Current result: JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION because EX-77 is supported by strong evidence and, independently, an Enhanced_Review occurred before release.
- The result is robust in one important way: even if a strict auditor rejected the late `Record_Exemption` entry, the case would still have no R1 gap because the Enhanced_Review at 09:20 precedes release and no later material change occurred.
- The result could change to NONCOMPLIANT only if additional evidence showed that the 09:20 Enhanced_Review did not actually review the current transaction state, and if EX-77 were also rejected.
- If one treats the exemption as the sole basis for release and requires the database record to exist before release, one might classify the exemption as unproven. Under the given evidence, however, the qualified signature at 09:05 and documented outage support pre-release validity.

**TX-305**

- Current result: INDETERMINATE because the release-time amount is unknown.
- Evidence that would change the result:
  - If an immutable release payload or signed release message showed amount < EUR 100,000, R1 would not trigger and the case could be COMPLIANT.
  - If an immutable release payload showed amount  EUR 100,000 and the Enhanced_Review artifact proved it reviewed that amount before release, the case could be COMPLIANT.
  - If the amount  EUR 100,000 was only established after the Enhanced_Review, or if the review artifact showed no amount was considered, the case could become NONCOMPLIANT.
- The settlement amount of 150,000 is reliable at settlement time but does not by itself prove the release-time amount. The note “settlement amount may equal release amount” is explicitly nonconclusive.

**TX-310**

- Current result: NONCOMPLIANT because R1 is triggered, there is no Enhanced_Review, and EX-88 is not supported by reliable pre-release evidence.
- Evidence that would change the result:
  - Independent Head_Compliance confirmation, system metadata, or a qualified signature proving approval before release could make EX-88 valid and produce JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION.
  - If the certificate creation at 10:30 were shown to be merely administrative and there were strong independent evidence of approval at 08:45, the result could move toward JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION or at least INDETERMINATE.
  - If one treats the requester’s oral statement as insufficient, especially because the witness is self-interested and the certificate postdates release, the current NONCOMPLIANT result is the stronger reading.

**TX-311**

- Current result: COMPLIANT because `event_index` establishes that the jurisdiction became DE before release, so R1’s foreign-jurisdiction condition is not met at release.
- Same-timestamp consequence: `Enhanced_Review` index 3 precedes `Update_Jurisdiction` index 4. Therefore the review occurred while jurisdiction was still US, but the release occurred after the DE update.
- Interpretations that could change the result:
  - If `event_index` were unreliable and the jurisdiction update were actually effective after release, the release would be evaluated under US. However, the Enhanced_Review still occurred after the >20% amount increase and before release, so R1 would likely still be satisfied unless evidence showed the review did not cover the 115,000 amount.
  - If the DE update were backdated or ineffective, and if some additional foreign-jurisdiction obligation applied that required a review after a later material change, the case could become NONCOMPLIANT or INDETERMINATE.
  - If one interpreted R1 as requiring a new Enhanced_Review after every jurisdiction update even when the release state is DE, TX-311 could be flagged, but that reading is not supported by the rule’s release-time trigger.

**TX-306**

- Current result: COMPLIANT because the amount increase from 220,000 to 230,000 is below 20% and does not invalidate the enhanced review for amount-dependent obligations.
- Alternative reading: if the policy were interpreted to treat every amount increase as material regardless of the 20% threshold, a new Enhanced_Review after 12:30 would be required and TX-306 would become NONCOMPLIANT.

**TX-308**

- Current result: COMPLIANT because the amount decreased from 140,000 to 100,000 and R4 says decreases do not by themselves invalidate a review.
- Alternative reading: if the threshold were “greater than 100,000” rather than “at least 100,000,” R1 would not trigger at all. The outcome would still be compliant, but the reasoning would differ.
- If decreases were treated as material changes requiring fresh review, TX-308 would become NONCOMPLIANT, but that contradicts R4.

            amount = state_after["amount"]
            risk = state_after["risk_level"]
            jurisdiction = state_after["jurisdiction"]
            customer_type = state_after["customer_type"]
            exemption_ref = state_after["exemption_id"]

            amount_unknown = is_missing(amount)
            risk_unknown = is_missing(risk)
            jurisdiction_unknown = is_missing(jurisdiction)
            customer_type_unknown = is_missing(customer_type)

            # R1 trigger and coarse check.
            if amount_unknown or jurisdiction_unknown:
                r1_trigger = None
                r1_status_pre = "REVIEW_REQUIRED"
            else:
                r1_trigger = (jurisdiction != "DE") and (float(amount) >= 100000)
                if not r1_trigger:
                    r1_status_pre = "NOT_TRIGGERED"

                    mat = trackers["last_gt20_increase"]
                    unk = trackers["last_unknown_amount_change"]

                        enh is None or unk > enh
                    )
                    enhanced_fresh = enh is not None and (mat is None or enh > mat)

                    if unknown_material_after_review:
                        r1_status_pre = "REVIEW_REQUIRED"
                    elif enhanced_fresh:
                        r1_status_pre = "PASS"
                    else:
                        r1_status_pre = "FAIL"

            # R2 trigger and coarse check.

                r2_trigger = None
                r2_status = "REVIEW_REQUIRED"
            else:
                r2_trigger = (risk == "HIGH") or (customer_type == "PEP")
                if not r2_trigger:
                    r2_status = "NOT_TRIGGERED"
                else:
                    relevant_orders = []
                    if risk == "HIGH" and trackers["last_high_trigger"] is not None:
                        relevant_orders.append(trackers["last_high_trigger"])
                    if customer_type == "PEP" and trackers["last_pep_trigger"] is not None:
                        relevant_orders.append(trackers["last_pep_trigger"])

                    relevant_trigger = max(relevant_orders) if relevant_orders else None
                    enh = trackers["last_enhanced"]
                    comp = trackers["last_compliance"]

                    if relevant_trigger is None:
                        r2_status = "REVIEW_REQUIRED"
                    else:
                        enhanced_fresh_r2 = enh is not None and enh > relevant_trigger
                        approval_after_review = (
                            comp is not None and enh is not None and comp > enh
                        )
                        if enhanced_fresh_r2 and approval_after_review:
                            r2_status = "PASS"
                        else:
                            r2_status = "FAIL"

            # R3 sanctions-list coarse check.
            current_list_order = None
            if jurisdiction_unknown:
                r3_status = "REVIEW_REQUIRED"
            else:
                current_list_order = trackers["sanctions_list"].get(jurisdiction)
                if current_list_order is None:
                    r3_status = "NOT_TRIGGERED"
                else:
                    screen = trackers["last_screen"]
                    if screen is not None and screen > current_list_order:
                        r3_status = "PASS"
                    else:
                        r3_status = "FAIL"

            # R4 coarse invalidation check.
            std = trackers["last_standard"]
            enh = trackers["last_enhanced"]
            mat = trackers["last_gt20_increase"]
            unk = trackers["last_unknown_amount_change"]

            latest_review = None
            if std is not None and enh is not None:
                latest_review = max(std, enh)
            elif std is not None:
                latest_review = std
            elif enh is not None:
                latest_review = enh

            r4_material_increase_after_review = (
                mat is not None and latest_review is not None and mat > latest_review
            )
            r4_unknown_increase_after_review = (
                unk is not None and latest_review is not None and unk > latest_review
            )

            diagnostics_rows.append(
                {
                    "case": case_id,
                    "release_time": row[TIME],
                    "release_index": int(row["event_index"]),
                    "release_amount": amount,
                    "release_risk": risk,
                    "release_jurisdiction": jurisdiction,
                    "release_customer_type": customer_type,
                    "amount_unknown": amount_unknown,
                    "risk_unknown": risk_unknown,
                    "jurisdiction_unknown": jurisdiction_unknown,
                    "customer_type_unknown": customer_type_unknown,
                    "exemption_id": exemption_ref,
                    "last_standard_time": order_ts(trackers["last_standard"]),
                    "last_standard_index": order_idx(trackers["last_standard"]),
                    "last_enhanced_time": order_ts(trackers["last_enhanced"]),
                    "last_enhanced_index": order_idx(trackers["last_enhanced"]),
                    "last_compliance_time": order_ts(trackers["last_compliance"]),
                    "last_compliance_index": order_idx(trackers["last_compliance"]),
                    "last_sanctions_screen_time": order_ts(trackers["last_screen"]),
                    "current_sanctions_list_update_time": order_ts(current_list_order),
                    "last_gt20_amount_increase_time": order_ts(trackers["last_gt20_increase"]),
                    "last_unknown_amount_change_time": order_ts(trackers["last_unknown_amount_change"]),
                    "r1_trigger": r1_trigger,
                    "r1_status_pre": r1_status_pre,
                    "r2_trigger": r2_trigger,
                    "r2_status": r2_status,
                    "r3_status": r3_status,
                    "r4_material_increase_after_review": r4_material_increase_after_review,
                    "r4_unknown_increase_after_review": r4_unknown_increase_after_review,
                }
            )

            # Release events are not treated as reviews, approvals, screens, or list updates.
            state = state_after
            continue

        # Amount-change detection for non-release events.
        if not is_missing(row["upd_amount"]):
            new_amount = float(row["upd_amount"])
            prior_amount = state.get("amount")

            if activity != "Create_Transaction":
                if prior_amount is None:
                    trackers["last_unknown_amount_change"] = order
                elif new_amount > 1.2 * float(prior_amount):
                    trackers["last_gt20_increase"] = order

            state["amount"] = new_amount

        # Apply non-amount state updates.
        for attr in ["risk_level", "jurisdiction", "customer_type", "exemption_id"]:
            value = row["upd_" + attr]
            if not is_missing(value):
                state[attr] = value

        # Track control events.
        if activity == "Standard_Review":
            trackers["last_standard"] = order
        elif activity == "Enhanced_Review":
            trackers["last_enhanced"] = order
        elif activity == "Compliance_Approve":
            trackers["last_compliance"] = order
        elif activity == "Sanctions_Screen":
            trackers["last_screen"] = order
        elif activity == "Sanctions_List_Update":
            listed_jurisdiction = (
                row["upd_jurisdiction"]
                if not is_missing(row["upd_jurisdiction"])
                else state.get("jurisdiction")
            )
            if not is_missing(listed_jurisdiction):
                trackers["sanctions_list"][listed_jurisdiction] = order

        # Track HIGH/PEP trigger times.
        if not is_missing(row["upd_risk_level"]):
            if row["upd_risk_level"] == "HIGH":
                trackers["last_high_trigger"] = order
            else:
                trackers["last_high_trigger"] = None

        if not is_missing(row["upd_customer_type"]):
            if row["upd_customer_type"] == "PEP":
                trackers["last_pep_trigger"] = order
            else:
                trackers["last_pep_trigger"] = None

    if is_missing(row.get("exemption_id")) or is_missing(row.get("scope_jurisdiction")):
        return False
    if is_missing(row.get("release_jurisdiction")) or is_missing(row.get("release_customer_type")):

        row["scope_jurisdiction"] == row["release_jurisdiction"]
        and row["scope_customer_type"] == row["release_customer_type"]

## 6. Why Control-Flow-Only Conformance Is Insufficient

1. **Final-state leakage**
   - Control-flow conformance often works on the trace or final case attributes.
   - That can incorrectly project final values backward.
   - Example: TX-305’s later settlement amount of 150,000 must not automatically become the release-time amount. A control-flow or final-state check could falsely conclude R1 was triggered and either pass or fail based on the wrong snapshot.

2. **Post-hoc exemptions and record-time confusion**
   - A control-flow check may see `Record_Exemption` in the trace and treat it as valid regardless of when approval actually occurred.
   - TX-302 and TX-310 both have exemption records inserted after release.
   - The compliance question is not whether the record exists eventually, but whether the exemption was valid and evidenced at release time. That requires evidence-time semantics, not mere trace presence.

3. **Material changes after review**
   - Control-flow conformance can verify that `Enhanced_Review` occurred before `Release_Funds`.
   - It cannot determine whether the review remained valid after a later amount increase.
   - TX-301 and TX-309 would look superficially acceptable if one only checked “review before release,” but R4 invalidates the review due to later >20% amount increases.

4. **Conditional activation**
   - Obligations depend on data values: amount thresholds, jurisdiction, risk level, PEP status, sanctions-list status.
   - A pure control-flow rule such as “Enhanced_Review before Release” is overinclusive and underinclusive.
   - Example: TX-307 requires Enhanced_Review because customer type became PEP, even though jurisdiction is DE and amount is low.
   - Example: TX-304 requires a new sanctions screen because the jurisdiction was added to the sanctions list after the first screen.

5. **Missing data and unknown triggers**
   - Control-flow conformance usually assumes complete cases or silently treats missing values as nontriggering.
   - R6 forbids defaulting missing amount, risk, jurisdiction, or customer type to low-risk values.
   - TX-305 is indeterminate precisely because the release amount is unknown; a control-flow checker would either falsely pass because an Enhanced_Review exists or falsely fail because it cannot inspect the missing payload.

6. **Same-timestamp ordering**
   - Many process-mining control-flow checks abstract away exact ordering or treat same-timestamp events as concurrent.
   - Here, `event_index` is decisive.
   - TX-311 depends on knowing that `Enhanced_Review` occurred before `Update_Jurisdiction` at the same timestamp, while the release later sees the updated DE jurisdiction.
   - Ignoring `event_index` can produce incorrect state snapshots and incorrect rule activation.

## 7. Recommended Logging and Control Improvements

1. **Immutable release payload**
   - At `Release_Funds`, log a signed or hashed payload containing:
     - release amount;
     - jurisdiction;
     - risk level;
     - customer type;
     - exemption references;
     - sanctions-list version used;
     - rule version applied;
     - release decision identifier.
   - This would resolve cases like TX-305 where the release amount is later disputed or unknown.

2. **Separate event time, effective time, record time, and evidence time**
   - Add explicit fields:
     - `event_time`;
     - `effective_from`;
     - `effective_to`;
     - `record_time`;
     - `signature_time`;
     - `evidence_time`.
   - This would distinguish late database inserts from earlier business effectiveness, directly addressing TX-302 and TX-310.

3. **Stable and monotonic event ordering**
   - Add a monotonic case sequence number in addition to timestamp.
   - Preserve subsecond precision where possible.
   - Require `event_index` to be immutable and system-generated.
   - This prevents same-timestamp ambiguity such as TX-311.

4. **Change provenance for attribute updates**
   - For every amount, risk, jurisdiction, or customer-type update, log:
     - old value;
     - new value;
     - reason code;
     - actor;
     - source system;
     - correlation identifier;
     - whether the change is effective immediately or retroactively.
   - This would make material-change analysis under R4 more deterministic.

5. **Review artifacts linked to state snapshots**
   - Each `Standard_Review` and `Enhanced_Review` should reference the exact data snapshot reviewed:
     - amount;
     - risk;
     - jurisdiction;
     - customer type;
     - document hash;
     - reviewer;
     - review scope.
   - This would prevent disputes about whether a review covered a later amount change.

6. **Exemption register with full validity metadata**
   - Exemptions should include:
     - unique exemption ID;
     - approver;
     - approval timestamp;
     - effective_from;
     - expires;
     - jurisdiction scope;
     - customer-type scope;
     - amount scope;
     - rules waived;
     - digital signature;
     - signature certificate metadata;
     - outage or late-insertion explanation;
     - evidence hash.
   - This would make TX-302-style late inserts easier to accept and TX-310-style backdating easier to reject.

7. **Sanctions-list versioning**
   - Log sanctions-list version and jurisdiction additions/removals.
   - Each `Sanctions_Screen` should record the list version screened.
   - This would make R3 checks deterministic and avoid ambiguity about whether a list update applied to the transaction’s jurisdiction.

8. **Rule versioning and decision logging**
   - Store the policy/rule version used at release.
   - Store the automated or manual decision outcome and the inputs used.
   - This supports auditability when rules change or when cases are reviewed later.

9. **Explicit unknown-data markers**
   - Instead of blank cells, use explicit markers such as `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_AVAILABLE`, or `PENDING_FEED`, with reason codes.
   - This would distinguish “no update” from “value unknown” and improve R6 handling.

10. **Approval semantics**
   - `Compliance_Approve` should state what was approved:
     - enhanced review packet;
     - standard review packet;
     - release payload hash;
     - exemption request;
     - exception rationale.
   - This would avoid TX-307-style ambiguity where approval of a standard packet cannot cure a missing enhanced review.
