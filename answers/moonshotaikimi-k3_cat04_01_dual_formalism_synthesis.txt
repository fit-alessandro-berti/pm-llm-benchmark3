# 1. Assumptions and Interpretation

1. **Completion-event abstraction.** Each activity is a single atomic event recorded at completion (narrative item 11). No start/end event pairs; durations are not modeled. A constraint “X may start only after Y is complete” is therefore enforced as “the completion event of X occurs after the completion event of Y.”
2. **“May overlap” = absence of an order constraint.** I interpret concurrency as *all interleavings permitted* (partial-order semantics), not as a claim of simultaneous execution. With completion-only records these are indistinguishable, so nothing is lost observationally.
3. **Risk route.** The low/high choice is an exclusive control-flow choice (`XOR`) placed after Analyze Root Cause: low  Supervisor Review; high  the unordered pair {Quality Review, Regulatory Review} (both mandatory, either completion order). The case data that *drives* the choice is deliberately not modeled (item 11); consequently the model accepts either route for any case, and consistency between data and route must be checked out of band.
4. **Supplier consultation.** Modeled as `XOR(Consult Supplier, )` (silent skip). “At most once” is guaranteed structurally: the node occurs once in the model and is not inside any loop. Selection is data-driven and unmodeled.
5. **Passing inspection.** The inspection outcome is not an event label. A `LOOP` with body `Execute Trial  Inspect Trial` and redo `Adjust Parameters` is used: loop *exit*  “inspection passed”; an Inspect event followed by Adjust  “failed.” Hence every trace ends its trial region with Inspect (the passing one), and Adjust can never follow the final Inspect.
6. **Duplicate visible labels.** Not permitted in the two delivered models: each of the 14 labels occurs exactly once in each model. A duplication-based tree refinement was considered and rejected (§3.4, §6).
7. **Process-tree approximation policy.** *Conservative under-approximation*: the tree must admit no intended-forbidden behavior, even at the price of excluding some intended behavior. The excluded family is characterized exactly in §3.4. Over-approximating variants that would admit unsafe trials (e.g., Execute Trial before Plan Verification) were rejected.
8. **POWL target.** Under assumptions 1–5, the POWL model is intended to be *language-exact* (no over- or under-approximation).
9. **Single case, multiplicity.** One trace = one case. Register/Archive occur exactly once, first/last. Execute/Inspect occur an equal number of times  1; Adjust occurs exactly (#Execute  1) times and only between cycles.
10. **Hierarchical interleaving.** Concurrent composite nodes (in the tree’s `` and in the POWL partial order) interleave their *internal* events freely; e.g., Quality Review and Regulatory Review may straddle trial events.

    return ProcessTree(label=label)

        t.children.append(c)   # insert into parent.children

**4. Exactness assessment (what the tree cannot say).** The tree is a *strict conservative under-approximation*: every trace it admits satisfies the narrative, but it excludes a precisely characterizable family of intended traces. Because a process tree is block-structured, the trial subtree must sit on one side of a join, and the risk route and supplier consult must sit either (a) inside the `A`-branch — forcing them to complete *before* the trial (serializing away the approval–trial overlap), or (b) after the join of `{CP, AD}` — the choice made here. Consequence of (b): **the risk route and Consult Supplier cannot complete before both D and P complete.** Requirements 5 and 6 give them the window *(Analyze Root Cause, Release Material)*; intended traces such as `R C A SR D P E I M Z` or `R C A CS D P SR E I M Z` (early sign-off / early supplier answer) are therefore wrongly rejected. All other requirements are exact: the initial streams (item 2), the split dependencies (items 3–4), the XOR route with unordered QR/RR (item 5), supplier optionality and at-most-once (item 6), approval–trial overlap *within the post-join window* (item 7), the repeat-until loop with no post-pass Adjust (item 8), release synchronization (item 9), and archive-last (item 10). Alternatives considered and rejected: (i) an over-approximating tree placing the trial as an unsynchronized sibling in a parallel (admits Execute Trial before Plan Verification — unsafe); (ii) a duplication-based union `XOR(T1, T2)` of “approvals-before-trial” and “approvals-during-trial” copies — it recovers early-completion traces but still cannot split one route across the join or interleave unbounded loop iterations with early approvals, while duplicating every visible label (double-counting in model statistics, ambiguous alignment mappings). The simple single-copy under-approximation was judged the best faithfulness/clarity trade-off.

# 7. Preferred Representation and Validation Plan

**Canonical representation: the POWL model.** It is language-exact under the assumptions of §1: the cross-branch dependencies (D8, D11–D13) are stated directly as partial-order edges, approvals and supplier work retain their full overlap windows, and choice/loop/optionality are isolated in composite nodes. The process tree is retained as a *derived, conservative* view for toolchains that only consume trees — safe because its language is a strict subset of the intended one, with the exclusion family documented in §3.4.

**Validation plan.**

1. **Conversion to accepting Petri nets (where supported).** `net_t, im_t, fm_t = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree)`; `net_p, im_p, fm_p = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl)` (recent PM4Py releases ship a POWLnet conversion; if unavailable in the installed version, fall back to converting the XOR/LOOP submodels via their tree encodings and hand-wiring the partial-order places, treating successful conversion as evidence to inspect, not proof).
2. **Soundness checking.** `pm4py.check_soundness(net_t, im_t, fm_t, print_diagnostics=True)` and likewise for `net_p`. Expect soundness (option to complete, proper completion, no dead transitions). Specifically inspect that the -skip cannot deadlock the release join and that the LOOP net cannot trap a case after a failed inspection.
3. **Bounded/sampled playout.** `pm4py.play_out(net_t, ...)` / `pm4py.play_out(net_p, ...)` with a few thousand traces; additionally *exhaustively* enumerate all topological sorts of the 12-edge partial order expanded over 2 risk variants × 2 supplier variants × 0–2 loop redos. Run every generated trace through a predicate encoding Table-2, e.g. checking precedence by index comparison and the trial region with the regex `^(EI(JEI)*)$` on the E/I/J subsequence. Acceptance criterion: 100 % spec-conformant for both models; additionally report the fraction of spec-valid traces rejected by the tree (quantifying the approximation gap; expect exactly the W3-family).
4. **Explicit trace tests.** Positive: W1–W5 must be perfectly fitting on `net_p` (token-based replay / alignments); W1, W2, W4, W5 must fit `net_t`; W3 must *fail* on `net_t` (documented approximation). Negative: F1–F3 plus extras — supplier consult after Release; supplier consult twice; Supervisor Review and Quality Review in the same case (route mixing); Execute before Design completes; Release before any Execute; Quality Review without Regulatory Review before Release; Archive before Release. All must be non-fitting on both nets.
5. **Silent-behavior / duplicate-label check.** Assert no played-out or replayed trace contains `None`/`tau` visibly; assert the leaf-label multiset of each model has 14 unique labels; assert both W1 (skip) and W4 (take) fit `net_p`, proving the  branch is a genuine silent alternative rather than a phantom or mandatory step; assert the tree and POWL label sets are identical.
6. **Limitations.** Sampled playout is not exhaustive for the unbounded loop (bounded to 2 redos); soundness certifies net well-formedness, not fidelity to the narrative; data-consistency of choices (risk level  route, inspection result  redo, supplier flag  CS) is outside the control-flow model and needs data-aware checks PM4Py does not provide; completion-time semantics cannot validate start-time claims; POWLnet conversion behavior varies across PM4Py versions and must be manually inspected when used.

# 8. Final Self-Critique

1. **The tree knowingly false-rejects legitimate cases (semantic).** Any real case where the supervisor signs off, or the supplier answers, before both Design Corrective Action and Plan Verification finish (W3-family) is intended behavior but non-conformant under the tree. No finite, duplication-free process tree can avoid this, but a deployment using the tree for conformance checking will produce systematic false positives on exactly the cases with fast governance.
2. **Completion-event abstraction weakens several “may start” clauses (semantic).** Constraints phrased as “may start only after …” (items 3, 4, 6) are enforced as completion-ordering. If start events are ever recorded, both models admit behaviors (e.g., supplier consultation *starting* before root-cause completion) that the narrative excludes, and the POWL edges would need re-interpretation as start-constraints rather than completion-constraints — a different concurrency reading, not a syntactic fix.
3. **Data-driven choices are modeled as free choices (semantic + tooling).** Nothing ties the XOR route to actual risk, the loop exit to actual pass/fail, or the -skip to actual supplier involvement; a low-risk case taking the high route is indistinguishable in the model. This is deliberate (item 11) but means conformance validation is only control-flow-deep, and part of the validation plan depends on POWLPetri-net conversion support that is version-sensitive in PM4Py and was assumed, not demonstrated.
