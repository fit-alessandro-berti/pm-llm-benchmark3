# 1. Objectives and Constraints

### **Optimization Objectives**
1. **Maximize throughput**: Complete cases efficiently while adhering to other objectives.
2. **Minimize SLA risk**: Prioritize urgent and high-priority cases to avoid violations.
3. **Develop skills**: Assign cases to support documented training priorities without high SLA or quality risks.
4. **Ensure workload fairness**: Prevent overloading any single resource while maintaining productivity.
5. **Preserve continuity**: Avoid unnecessary reassignments, especially for complex cases or those with high handover cost.
6. **Improve resilience**: Balance workloads across teams to reduce the risk of bottlenecks.

### **Hard/Compliance Constraints**
1. **Resource eligibility filtering**:
   - Do not assign cases to resources with skill 0 for the required activity.
   - For **Security Review**, use skill level 3, or skill 1–2 with supervision.
   - For **Final Approval**, use skill level 2–3; skill 1 allowed to prepare decisions only under supervision.
   - **Quality Check** can only be batched for normal/low-priority cases with SLA >24h.
2. **Safety considerations**:
   - Assign high-priority cases to skill level 2–3, unless supervised.
   - Avoid assigning work to resources with high overtime risk unless urgent and no safer alternative exists.
3. **Fairness constraints**:
   - Do not assign more than 4 new tasks per resource in the current batch.

### **Preference Trade-Offs**
1. **Continuity vs. skill-mismatch risk**:
   - Prefer continuity for high-complexity cases with high handover costs, unless the current owner's skill is insufficient.
2. **Skill development vs. SLA risk**:
   - Favor training assignments for normal/low-priority cases where SLA risk is acceptable.
3. **Immediate vs. long-term resilience**:
   - Avoid bottlenecks by balancing assignments across skill levels and teams.
4. **Error risk vs. throughput**:
   - Use specialists for urgent/high-value cases to reduce errors, accepting potential longer queues in bottlenecked activities.

# 2. Assignment Policy

### **Algorithm/Pseudocode**
For each case in the queue:
1. **Eligibility Filtering**:
   - Exclude resources with skill 0 for the required activity.
   - Apply case-specific constraints, e.g., supervision rules for high-priority or skill-limited resources.
   - Exclude resources violating the fairness constraint (max 4 new cases).

2. **SLA Urgency Scoring**:
   - Calculate urgency score:  
     `urgency = (1 / SLA_time_remaining) * case_priority * (1.0 if urgent else 0.75 if high else 0.5)`
   - Rank cases by urgency score (lower SLA time = higher score).

3. **Skill-Match Scoring**:
   - Assign skill-matching score:  
     `skill_score = max(0, resource_skill - required_skill) + (supervised_training_score if case supports training)`
   - Favor supervised training opportunities where SLA risk is low.

4. **Continuity Scoring**:
   - Assign penalty for reassignment:  
     - `continuity_score = -handover_cost (if owner changes)`  
     - Add priority continuity bonus for high-complexity cases.

5. **Workload and Overtime Fairness Adjustment**:
   - Penalize assignments to resources with high overtime risk unless SLA is urgent.
   - Prefer resources with fewer current open cases and more hours remaining.

6. **Bottleneck Avoidance/Resilience**:
   - Avoid overloading resources in currently overburdened teams or those handling bottlenecked activities like **Security Review** or **Final Approval**.

7. **Tie-Breaking**:
   - Rank based on: lowest current open cases  highest skill level  highest training priority  lowest overtime risk.

8. **Exception Handling**:
   - If no compliant resource can meet SLA for urgent/high cases, escalate for reallocation across non-standard teams or overtime approval.

# 5. Explanation to Workers

This policy helps match cases to your skills or growth priorities while respecting fair workloads. High-priority work is prioritized to prevent SLA breaches. Training is encouraged where it's safe, and continuity is maintained for high-complexity cases to reduce errors. If assigned supervised training tasks, it's because the activity supports your development while safeguarding quality. Workloads are designed to avoid overload and bottlenecks, ensuring fairness across the team.

# 6. Process-Mining Audit Indicators
1. **Handover frequency**: Measure total reassignments and their impact on SLA times.
2. **Reassignment count**: Track cases with >=2 reassignments.
3. **Skill mismatch**: Identify tasks assigned to under-skilled resources.
4. **Workload distribution**: Analyze variance in open cases and assigned hours.
5. **Overtime risk exposure**: Measure utilization of high overtime-risk resources.
6. **SLA misses**: Count cases breaching SLA deadlines.
7. **Rework/appeals rate**: Measure instances of rework or customer escalations.
8. **Training assignments**: Monitor % of assignments fulfilling training priorities.
9. **Specialist bottlenecks**: Analyze queues and wait times for critical roles like Security.
10. **Policy deviation rate**: Identify cases violating dynamic assignment rules.
