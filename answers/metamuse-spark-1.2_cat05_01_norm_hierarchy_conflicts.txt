## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method

**Facts (non-interpretive):**
- GPIP-4 is board-approved, repository-controlled, effective 2026-01-01, stated scope all entities/purchases.
- DE-AAC-PROC-17 Rev.6 is controlled local SOP, effective 2025-03-01, scope Aachen maintenance/production-support purchases.
- ECD-2 is jointly signed COO + Incident Commander, controlled as incident directive, stated effective window 2026-07-14 09:00 - 2026-07-18 18:00 CEST, stated scope "critical sterilization-line lots required to contain the current contamination event."
- CFO email 2026-07-15 07:20 is ordinary email, not in policy repository, sent during ECD-2 window.
- ERP outage ON-44: 2026-07-14 08:30-13:00 CEST, recovery timestamps may be up to 12 min late. Document-system outage queued PDF for P-507 until 12:40 creation.
- No CRO written A7 exception was issued or ratified. CRO unavailable.
- Organizational facts incomplete: IC charter does not explicitly designate IC members as budget owners; CFO owns Finance control framework; COO can issue operational directives; GPIP-4 reserves formal exceptions to CRO.

**Interpretive Assumptions (defeasible, must be stated):**
1.  **Authority is dimension-specific, not monolithic.** Board policy > controlled local SOP > time-limited incident directive > non-controlled email for purposes of formal norm conflict. However, within its *own* control domain (e.g., CFO for Finance), a lower-level source may have delegated interpretive authority.
2.  **Specificity and scope limit overrides.** A later or more specific norm only overrides an earlier/general norm *for the issue and scope it expressly addresses* and only *within its effective time*. Silence is not waiver. ECD-2 `overrides_for(timing, supplier_register, scope=critical_lots, time=window)` but does not expressly override GPIP-4 integrity controls (A1 segregation, A2 Finance+budget-owner, A3 Compliance).
3.  **Emergency necessity does not create implied exceptions.** GPIP-4 A4 explicitly limits emergency to competitive tendering unless CRO A7 exception. SOP B4/B5 define emergency narrowly and do not waive Finance or supplier-register by implication. Evidential outage (ON-44) changes weight, not normative validity.
4.  **Lex posterior does not automatically cure lex superior.** A newer directive or email cannot silently weaken a higher policy per GPIP-4 A5. A7 exception requires written, scoped, time-limited CRO issuance. Whether a COO+IC directive *counts* as A7 is uncertain (GPIP-4 silent) – we treat it as presumptively **not** an A7 exception absent CRO ratification or explicit GPIP-4 reference, but as a defensible operational overlay for *ordinary local workflow steps* per SOP B7.
5.  **Time of act governs.** Compliance assessed at `time(Commit_to_Supplier)` and `time(Create_PO)`, not case completion. A6: later approval mitigates but does not cure missing pre-commitment approval.
6.  **Evidence hierarchy:** Contemporaneous telephone recordings, signed PDFs, supplier acknowledgments, access logs > ERP timestamps during outage/recovery. Burden of proof on claimant of exception: exception must be `supported_by(evidence)` of timely joint authorization and scope. Ambiguity resolved against exception if no record within required window.
7.  **Segregation of duties (A1) and Compliance clearance for new supplier/>100k (A3) are integrity controls** that presumptively survive any ECD/CFO overlay. Finance approval (A2/B2) is a budget-control where CFO interpretive authority has weight during incident window.

**Decision Method:**
- Normalize rules to Trigger + Deontic + Temporal relation + Actor constraint + Scope + Defeater + Evidence.
- Apply defeasible reasoning: `Rule normally applies unless Defeater successfully invoked and supported_by(evidence) within scope/time and defeater has authority for that issue`.
- Classify: COMPLIANT = all applicable mandatory rules satisfied at relevant time or valid defeater invoked. JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION = deviation from ordinary rule but covered by a defensible, timely, scoped, authorized exception with evidence (even if hierarchy debatable). NONCOMPLIANT = material rule violated at relevant time without valid defeater. INDETERMINATE = outcome turns on unresolved scope/evidence where both interpretations reasonable and missing evidence would reverse.

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

**P-502 – Alternative: JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION (or COMPLIANT) for Compliance:**
*Premise change:* Treat ECD-2 jointly signed by COO+IC as an implied CRO A7 exception that *does* waive GPIP-4 A3 for new-supplier Compliance when the supplier is operationally necessary to contain contamination, especially given board practice of treating signed incident directives as temporary overlays.
*Why credible:* C4 "takes precedence over ordinary ... supplier-onboarding" could be read to include Compliance onboarding; urgency (same-day filters) makes Compliance delay impracticable; Compliance was obtained same day 18:30, showing no intent to evade. *Why weak:* GPIP-4 A3 is explicitly integrity, Compliance waiver not mentioned in ECD, CFO has no authority to waive Compliance (D3), and organization has *not* previously used directives to waive new-supplier Compliance; A7 requires CRO writing – not met; precedent risk of normalizing new-supplier bypass.

**P-503 – Alternative: JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION if scope + authorization recharacterized:**
*Premise change:* (a) Define "critical sterilization-line lots" broadly to include backup-line contingency deemed critical by Incident Command, and (b) treat PROC-3's role as *operational* authorizer not final approver, with IC-1 as independent authorizer satisfying distinctness, plus read D1 as waiving SoD for emergency.
*Why credible:* IC labeling has operational weight; D1 says "sufficient for all operational approvals" arguably includes Plant/Finance. *Why weak:* SoD (A1) is board-level integrity, not operational; PROC-3 self-authorizing own request/commit is textbook self-dealing prohibited by A1/A6 purpose; no evidence of distinct record within 4h; 140k without Compliance/Finance is high-risk; D3 explicitly excludes SoD/Compliance from CFO waiver.

**P-505 – Alternative: NONCOMPLIANT (post-expiry breach):**
*Premise change:* Treat ERP timestamp 18:06 as conclusive commit time (reject supplier ack as insufficient proof of commitment) and apply strict lex superior = ECD cannot extend Finance waiver beyond 18:00.
*Why credible:* ERP is system of record; "may be 9 min fast" is possibility not proof; commitment legally occurs at supplier acknowledgment? If ack 17:59 not = commitment but ERP 18:06 is, then commit after expiry loses ECD/CFO cover, violating B1 (PO before commit), B2, and A2. *Why weak:* Evidence principle from ON-44 and acknowledgment email is stronger contemporaneous evidence than batch-entered ERP timestamp; telephone log 17:57 start corroborates earlier real act; hierarchy requires applying rules at time of real act.

**P-507 – Alternative: NONCOMPLIANT (Finance integrity breach):**
*Premise change:* Reject CFO interpretive waiver for GPIP-4 A2; hold that Finance Controller + independent budget owner before commit is a GPIP-4 (board) requirement that *only* CRO A7 can waive; CFO email is non-controlled and D4 ("not meant to remove essential integrity controls") confirms Finance is essential.
*Why credible:* Preserves board supremacy and A7 exclusivity; IC not budget owner per incomplete facts; 110k is material. *Why weak:* Makes CFO's explicit Finance ownership and contemporaneous D1 guidance meaningless during incident; B7/C4 indicates incident directive can supersede ordinary workflow, and Finance operational approval could be considered ordinary; forces every ECD purchase 50k into noncompliance despite COO/CFO joint intent.

**P-508 – Alternative: NONCOMPLIANT (budget-owner + strict 150k cap):**
*Premise change:* (a) Read D1 as hard cap: joint IC auth sufficient *only* up to 150k, and above 150k requires *both* Legal *and* separate Finance Controller + budget owner (D2 does not waive Finance), and (b) enforce that IC members are not budget owners.
*Why credible:* Literal reading of D1 "up to EUR 150,000" and GPIP-4 A2 independent budget owner language; 155k exceeds threshold; no Finance event at all. *Why weak:* D2 says "obtain Legal clearance *as well*" – natural reading is Legal *in addition to* IC sufficiency, not in addition to full pre-incident approvals, which would defeat emergency purpose for the most critical (and most expensive) lot; still leaves SoD and Compliance intact, preserving key integrity.

**Additional reversal note for P-501:** Could be JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION if one treats Shift Lead substitution as impliedly delegated in emergency plus 4h stoppage, despite no formal delegation – weak because B4 requires active delegation and Plant was reachable.

## 6. Evidential Sufficiency and Remediation

Distinguish four defect types:
- **Policy breach** – missing substantive control before commit (SoD, Compliance, Finance/budget-owner, supplier legitimacy).
- **Logging defect** – act was timely but recorded late due to outage/document queue (ERP timestamp late).
- **Late evidence of timely act** – later timestamp proves earlier real act (supplier ack, phone recording).
- **Inability to prove compliance** – no evidence within required window (no joint auth record, no delegation).

|---|---|---|---|
| P-501 | Policy breach (Finance before commit, delegation) + late evidence not relevant | Obtain retrospective Plant Manager attestation on delegation impossibility and Finance Manager ratification memo acknowledging breach; File breach log; do not backdate approvals. **General remediation principles:**
- Do not treat post-hoc approval as cure (A6). Ratifications must be labeled as mitigation, not retroactive compliance.
- Preserve contemporaneous evidence (recordings, PDFs, acks) as primary; annotate ERP with correction reason, not overwrite timestamp.
- Distinguish breach severity: P-503 (SoD + 140k) and P-504 (misuse of emergency) require training/discipline review; P-502/P-507/P-508 require process fix for new-supplier Compliance fast path.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification

**Draft Replacement Clause – GPIP-4 §7 Emergency & Hierarchy:**

> **7.1 Norm hierarchy (issue-specific).** Board-approved GPIP-4 prevails over local SOPs and incident directives for integrity controls (segregation of duties, Compliance clearance, Finance/budget-owner thresholds). Local SOPs may impose stricter thresholds and define evidential forms but may not weaken GPIP-4. Incident directives may only supersede *ordinary operational workflow* (Create_PO timing, approved-supplier register, Plant delegation) within their expressly stated scope and time window, and only when the directive explicitly references the GPIP-4 clause it seeks to suspend.
>
> **7.2 Exception authority.** Only the Chief Risk Officer may grant a written, scoped, time-limited exception to GPIP-4 controls per A7. An incident directive jointly signed by the COO and Incident Commander is *not* an A7 exception unless it (a) cites GPIP-4 A7, (b) is countersigned or ratified by the CRO within 72h (or by CFO for Finance-only exceptions within delegated limits), and (c) states amount/scope limits and expiry with hard system enforcement.
>
> **7.3 Emergency definition and scope.** Emergency = imminent safety harm or production stoppage expected 6 hours, *or* during a declared ECD window, only those sterilization-line lots for which a delay >24 hours would halt containment, as tagged in the incident system. Backup-line contingency, catering, or general site continuity are out of scope unless explicitly named.
>
> **7.4 Thresholds and segregation.** (a) All purchases €25k (Aachen)/€50k (group) require independent budget owner *and* Finance Controller approval *before* Commit_to_Supplier; Incident Command members are not budget owners unless separately designated. (b) >€100k or any new supplier requires Compliance clearance *before* commit – no Finance or operational waiver can substitute. (c) Requester/Committer may never be final authorizer; joint Incident Command authorization requires two *distinct* members, neither being the requester/committer. Verbal authorization permitted only if recorded in the incident log within 4 hours, with contemporaneous evidence (phone recording/supplier ack) prevailing over ERP timestamp. Record_Emergency_Form within 24h.
>
> **7.5 Finance during incidents.** During an ECD window, the CFO may issue a controlled addendum (repository-entered) deeming joint Incident Command authorization as satisfying the *Finance operational* approval up to €150k; above €150k Legal clearance is additionally required *before* commit. This does not waive GPIP-4 budget-owner or Compliance requirements.
>
> **7.6 Retrospective evidence and expiry.** System outage does not extend normative windows. ERP timestamps are rebuttable by stronger contemporaneous evidence, which must be filed within the same 4h/24h windows. ECD expiry is hard-coded; any extension requires new written, time-stamped directive before expiry. Later approval mitigates consequence but does not retroactively confer compliance.
>
> **7.7 Safeguard against emergency normalization.** Every ECD must include (i) automatic expiry and spend cap, (ii) daily log review by Compliance/Finance, (iii) post-incident audit of all ECD purchases within 10 business days, and (iv) prohibition on using an ECD as precedent for the next incident until audit closure and CRO lessons-learned sign-off.

This clause resolves authority (board > CRO > CFO Finance interpretation > COO/IC operational), scope (narrow critical definition, system-enforced), thresholds (local 25k stricter but nested), SoD (non-waivable), new suppliers (Compliance hard gate), retrospective records (evidence hierarchy, non-curing), and expiry (hard stop + ratification), with an explicit anti-normalization control.
