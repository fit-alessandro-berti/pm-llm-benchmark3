## 2. Rule Interpretation

*   **RR1 (Draft/Approve Separation):** `Draft_Change_Request` resource $\neq$ `Approve_Change` resource (normalized). Applies to all risk levels unless explicitly exempted (no exemption found).
*   **RR2 (Implement/Verify Separation):** `Implement_Change` resource $\neq$ `Verify_Implementation` resource (normalized). Applies to all risk levels.
*   **RR3 (High-Risk Approval):** `Approve_Change` for `HIGH` risk must be performed by a user with `QM` standing role OR a `QM Delegate` whose `Effective interval` covers the event timestamp AND whose `Scope` covers the case scope AND whose `Principal-absence condition` is met (Principal is on leave/unavailable).
*   **RR4 (Emergency Substitution):**
    *   **Approval:** `Approve_Change` must be by `IC` or `IC Delegate` (valid interval/scope).
    *   **Review:** `Retrospective_QM_Review` must be performed by `QM` or `QM Delegate`.
    *   **Timing:** "Within two business days" is calculated as the event date (Day 0) + 2 business days (excluding weekends/holidays). E.g., Mon + 2 days = Wed. Thu is late.
*   **RR5 (Identity Evidence):** Service account actions are valid only if session logs map the session to a specific human identity at the event time. If the mapped human is the Implementer, RR2 is violated.
*   **RR6 (Suspicion vs Proof):** Patterns (e.g., repeated pairs) trigger investigation flags but do not constitute a violation finding without evidence of intent or policy breach.
*   **Leave/Availability:** If a Principal is on certified leave, they are considered "Absent". If they perform an action, it is a policy breach (unauthorized use of credentials) unless a specific emergency override exists. A delegation is valid only if the Principal is actually absent; if the Principal acts, the delegation is not required but the leave policy applies.
*   **Database Latency:** A delegation record created *after* the event (e.g., 08-12 record for 08-10 event) is technically retroactive. For audit purposes, the signed PDF (08-09) provides the legal basis, but the system control (DB record) was not present at event time. This is a Data Quality issue.

## 6. Control Improvements

1.  **Delegation Creation:** Move from PDF-based/retroactive DB records to real-time system updates. Delegation must be active in the QMS *before* the event timestamp to be valid.
2.  **Principal Availability:** Integrate HR Leave Management with QMS. If a Principal is on leave, the system should automatically trigger delegation activation and block the Principal from approving (preventing leave breaches).
3.  **Alias Management:** Centralize identity mapping. `b.koch` should resolve to `u_bk` at the authentication layer, not the audit layer.
4.  **Individual Authentication:** Service accounts (`svc_qms`) should be deprecated for critical actions. If used, require MFA or a "human-in-the-loop" re-authentication step before Verify_Implementation.
5.  **Emergency Review Timer:** Automated alert for `Retrospective_QM_Review` if not completed within 48 business hours. Block case closure if missing.
6.  **Pair Rotation:** Implement a mandatory rotation policy for Draft/Approve pairs to prevent long-term collusion patterns.
7.  **Network Monitoring:** Deploy PM4Py-based anomaly detection on the Working Together Network to flag unusual density between specific resource pairs (e.g., Clara/Ben) exceeding a threshold (e.g., >80% of cases).
8.  **Evidential Retention:** Store session logs (workstation ID, badge ID) linked to the case ID, not just the resource ID.
