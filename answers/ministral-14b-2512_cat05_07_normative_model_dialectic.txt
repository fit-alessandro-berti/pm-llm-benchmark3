# **1. Stipulated Facts, Disputed Propositions, and Burden of Proof**

### **Stipulated Facts (Established by Evidence)**
1. **M0’s Control-Flow Structure**:
   - `Sanctions_Check` and `Quality_Review` are concurrent in `checks`.
   - `Manual_Procurement_Approval` must precede `Activate_Supplier` in the approved branch.
   - `Reject_Supplier` is an XOR choice with the approved branch.
   - `Notify_Requester` follows the decision, and `Close_Case` follows notification.

2. **CS-9 (2025-01-01) Requirements**:
   - Sanctions and Quality checks are mandatory before activation.
   - Manual approval is required before activation.
   - Requester and approver must be distinct humans (not modeled in M0).

3. **Pilot PD-3 (2026-06-01–2026-07-31)**:
   - LOW-risk DE suppliers in approved corporate groups may use `Auto_Approve`.
   - Sanctions and automated validation must precede activation.
   - Quality review may occur up to 2 days post-activation.

4. **Emergency Directive FD-7 (2026-07-10–2026-07-12)**:
   - Provisional activation allowed before Quality review and manual approval.
   - Sanctions check still required.
   - Spend capped at €20,000 until retrospective approval.

5. **Data Collection Changes (N4)**:
   - `Notify_Requester` moved to CRM; 70% mirrored to logs.
   - Older notification links are mutable text fields.

6. **Stakeholder Practice (N5)**:
   - `Request_More_Info` loops are common and desirable but not modeled.
   - Operations wants permanent `Auto_Approve` (no formal policy).

7. **Observed Cases (SUP-701–SUP-715)**:
   - Some cases fit M0 but violate non-control-flow rules (e.g., same approver/requester).
   - Some cases use `Auto_Approve` outside pilot window (SUP-714).
   - Some cases lack notifications (SUP-706) or have missing eligibility data (SUP-703).

### **Burden of Proof**
- **Noncompliance**: Must show a case violates **formal policy (CS-9)**, **active directive (PD-3/FD-7)**, or **data integrity (e.g., missing notifications)**. Exceptions require **documented authority** (e.g., pilot eligibility, emergency scope).
- **Model/Data Defect**: Must show M0 **fails to represent real-world behavior** (e.g., loops, concurrency, eligibility rules) or **data is unreliable** (e.g., mutable links, missing attributes). Defects do not excuse violations but may explain deviations.

# **2. Prosecution Brief**

# **3. Defense Brief**
**Core Argument**: *M0 is obsolete/incomplete because:*
1. **Real-World Behavior Exceeds M0’s Scope**
   - **Concurrency Assumptions Are Flawed**:
     - M0 models `Sanctions_Check` and `Quality_Review` as **strictly concurrent**, but **SUP-704/SUP-705** (FD-7) allow **provisional activation before Quality_Review**.
     - **SUP-708** shows **Quality_Review after activation**—M0 cannot represent this.
   - **Missing Loops**:
     - **SUP-707** has **multiple `Request_More_Info` cycles**—M0 has **no loop mechanism**.
     - **Operations regards this as desirable**, but M0 **cannot express it**.

2. **Pilots and Emergencies Require Overlay Rules**
   - **PD-3 and FD-7 introduce valid deviations** that M0 **cannot represent**:
     - `Auto_Approve` for LOW-risk suppliers (SUP-702, SUP-714).
     - Provisional activation under FD-7 (SUP-704, SUP-705).
   - **M0 is a "base model"**—it **cannot encode time-bound exceptions** without external governance.

3. **Data Migration and Logging Defects Are Structural**
   - **Notification missingness (29%)** is **not a process flaw** but a **data collection issue** (CRM migration).
   - **Mutable text links (SUP-706)** make **retrospective auditing unreliable**.
   - **SUP-711’s duplicate `Register_Supplier`** is a **logging artifact**, not a process violation.

4. **Rejected Leads and Reactivations Are Out of Scope**
   - **SUP-709**: Process owner says rejected leads are **intake cases**; model owner says M0 starts **post-pre-screen**.
   - **SUP-710**: Reactivation lacks `Register_Supplier` and `Quality_Review`—**CS-9 is silent on reactivation**.
   - **M0’s scope is ambiguous**; **some cases are simply not governed by it**.

5. **Stakeholder Desires Should Inform Model Revision**
   - **Operations wants permanent `Auto_Approve`**—if justified, **CS-9 should be updated**, not ignored.
   - **Request_More_Info loops are a gap**—M0’s **lack of loops is a design flaw**.
   - **Label equivalence (SUP-713: `Quality_Review`  `QA_Clearance`)** shows **real-world flexibility** that M0 **cannot accommodate**.

6. **Emergency Authority May Have Extended Factually**
   - **FD-7’s "flood still existed" claim (SUP-705)** may justify **continued provisional activation**.
   - **No written extension  no authority**—**operational necessity** can override rigid models.

   - **~30% of cases are noncompliant or indeterminate** (SUP-703, SUP-708, SUP-712, SUP-714, SUP-715).

     - **No loops** (SUP-707).

     - **No resource rules** (same approver/requester).

   - **Conclusion**: **M0 is obsolete and requires revision**, but **some deviations are genuine noncompliance**.

3. **Rejected Arguments**:
   - **Prosecution’s claim that all deviations are noncompliance is false**—many are **model/data defects** (e.g., SUP-707, SUP-711).
   - **Defense’s claim that all deviations are justified is false**—**SUP-703, SUP-708, SUP-715 are clear violations**.
   - **Neither side adequately addresses the need for a layered governance model** (POWL + DECLARE + data checks).

# **6. Revised Normative Architecture**

### **Layered Architecture**
| Layer | Component | Purpose | Example Rules |
|-------|-----------|---------|---------------|
| **1. Control-Flow (POWL)** | Revised process tree | Core sequence and branching | `Sanctions_Check`  `Quality_Review` (concurrent)  `Decision` (XOR). |
| **2. Declarative Constraints (DECLARE)** | Temporal/logical rules | Eligibility, time windows, loops | `Auto_Approve` only if `LOW_RISK` AND `CORPORATE_GROUP_APPROVED`. |
| **3. Data-Aware Rules (pandas/data checks)** | Eligibility, amounts, actors | Dynamic conditions | `Manual_Approval` required unless `EMERGENCY_DIRECTIVE_ACTIVE`. |
| **4. Resource Rules (organizational controls)** | Actor separation, conflicts | Prevent same-person approvals | `Requester != Approver` (enforced via HR system). |
| **5. Policy Overlay (governance table)** | Pilots, emergencies, exceptions | Time-bound deviations | `FD-7` allows provisional activation until `2026-07-12`. |
| **6. Scope Rules (documentation)** | Rejected leads, reactivations | Clarify boundaries | `M0 does not govern intake cases (SUP-709)`. |

---

### **Key Components**
1. **POWL Model**:
   - Standard route: `Sanctions_Check`  `Quality_Review`  `Manual_Approval`  `Activate`.
   - Auto-approval route: `Sanctions_Check`  `Auto_Approve` (if eligible)  `Activate`  `Quality_Review` (within 2 days).
   - Emergency route: `Sanctions_Check`  `Provisional_Activate`  `Quality_Review` (within 2 days)  `Manual_Approval`.
   - Loop approximation: **Silent transitions** for `Request_More_Info` (with max retries).

2. **DECLARE Constraints**:
   - `Auto_Approve` only if:
     - `Risk_Level == "LOW"` AND
     - `Corporate_Group_Approved == True` AND
     - `Date in [2026-06-01, 2026-07-31]` (PD-3 window).
   - `Provisional_Activate` only if:
     - `Emergency_Directive == "FD-7"` AND
     - `Date in [2026-07-10, 2026-07-12]` (or extended period).

3. **Data Rules**:
   - **Notification**: Must have `notification_id` (immutable) and `timestamp`.
   - **Eligibility**: Snapshot `Risk_Level`, `Corporate_Group`, `Emergency_Status` at activation.
   - **Actors**: `Requester_Alias`  `HR_ID`, `Approver_Alias`  `HR_ID` (prevent same-person approvals).

4. **Governance Overlay**:
   - **Pilots**: Recorded in a **versioned policy table** with expiry dates.
   - **Emergencies**: Require **written extension** for continued authority.
   - **Exceptions**: Logged with `Exception_Code`, `Authority_Source`, `Expiry_Date`.

5. **Scope Clarifications**:
   - **Rejected leads**: Explicitly **not governed by M0** (intake process).
   - **Reactivations**: Require **full `Quality_Review`** unless prior certification is valid.

from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, OperatorPOWL, Transition, SilentTransition
from pm4py.objects.process_tree.obj import Operator
from pm4py.objects.log.util import dataframe_utils

# --- Transitions ---
register = Transition(label="Register_Supplier")
sanctions = Transition(label="Sanctions_Check")
quality = Transition(label="Quality_Review")
manual_approval = Transition(label="Manual_Procurement_Approval")
auto_approve = Transition(label="Auto_Approve")
provisional_activate = Transition(label="Provisional_Activate")
activate = Transition(label="Activate_Supplier")
reject = Transition(label="Reject_Supplier")
request_more_info = SilentTransition(label="Request_More_Info")  # Approximation for loops
notify = Transition(label="Notify_Requester")
close = Transition(label="Close_Case")

# --- Standard Route: Sanctions  Quality  Manual Approval  Activate ---
checks = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[sanctions, quality])
approved_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[manual_approval, activate])
approved_branch.order.add_edge(manual_approval, activate)

# --- Auto-Approval Route (PD-3) ---
auto_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[auto_approve, activate])
auto_branch.order.add_edge(auto_approve, activate)
quality_post_activate = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[quality])  # Quality review after activation

# --- Emergency Route (FD-7) ---
emergency_branch = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[provisional_activate, quality, manual_approval, activate])
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(provisional_activate, quality)
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(quality, manual_approval)
emergency_branch.order.add_edge(manual_approval, activate)

# --- Decision Points ---
decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[approved_branch, reject])
auto_decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[auto_branch, approved_branch, reject])
emergency_decision = OperatorPOWL(operator=Operator.XOR, children=[emergency_branch, reject])

# --- Request_More_Info Approximation (Silent Loop) ---
# Note: POWL cannot natively model loops, so we approximate with a silent transition.
# In practice, this would require DECLARE or a separate subprocess.
request_loop = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[request_more_info])
request_loop.order.add_edge(request_more_info, request_more_info)  # Self-loop (max 3 retries in data)

# --- Main Model Construction ---
# Standard path: Register  Checks  Decision  Notify  Close
M_revised = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, checks, decision, notify, close])
M_revised.order.add_edge(register, checks)
M_revised.order.add_edge(checks, decision)
M_revised.order.add_edge(decision, notify)
M_revised.order.add_edge(notify, close)

auto_path = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, sanctions, auto_decision, quality_post_activate, notify, close])
auto_path.order.add_edge(register, sanctions)
auto_path.order.add_edge(sanctions, auto_decision)
auto_path.order.add_edge(auto_decision, quality_post_activate)  # Quality review after activation
auto_path.order.add_edge(quality_post_activate, notify)
auto_path.order.add_edge(notify, close)

# --- Emergency Path (FD-7) ---
emergency_path = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[register, sanctions, emergency_decision, notify, close])
emergency_path.order.add_edge(register, sanctions)
emergency_path.order.add_edge(sanctions, emergency_decision)
emergency_path.order.add_edge(emergency_decision, notify)
emergency_path.order.add_edge(notify, close)

# --- Combined Model (Union of All Paths) ---
# Note: POWL cannot natively combine disjoint paths, so we represent them separately.
# In practice, a DECLARE model or a choice between submodels would be needed.
print("Revised POWL model components:")
print("- Standard path (M_revised):", M_revised.nodes)
print("- Auto-approval path (auto_path):", auto_path.nodes)

---
### **Key Improvements Over M0**
1. **Explicit Routes**:
   - Standard, auto-approval, and emergency paths are **separately modeled**.
2. **Loop Approximation**:
   - Silent transitions for `Request_More_Info` (with max retries enforced in data).
3. **Policy Overlay**:
   - Time-bound exceptions are **documented separately** (not hardcoded in POWL).
4. **Data Integrity**:
   - Immutable `notification_id` and `timestamp` prevent logging defects.
5. **Resource Rules**:
   - HR_ID resolution prevents same-person approvals.

### **Remaining Gaps**
- **True loops** require DECLARE or a subprocess.
- **Dynamic eligibility** (e.g., risk reassessment) needs data checks.
- **Emergency extensions** require governance approvals outside POWL.

This architecture **balances compliance rigor with operational flexibility** while **explicitly addressing the flaws in M0**.
