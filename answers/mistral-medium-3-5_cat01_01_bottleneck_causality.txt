## 8. Residual uncertainty

1. **Unobserved case difficulty**: Orion’s cases may have higher unmeasured risk (e.g., legal complexity). *Solution*: Add a pre-classification risk score.
2. **Abandoned cases**: Orion may abandon slow cases, biasing `cycle_time_h` downward. *Solution*: Track all cases, including abandoned ones, with timestamps.
3. **Reviewer skill heterogeneity**: Unobserved skill differences within groups may drive `review_service_h`. *Solution*: Include reviewer IDs and tenure.
4. **Time-varying queue dynamics**: `arrival_load` may fluctuate intra-shift. *Solution*: Log queue length at each step.
5. **Post-process quality factors**: `outcome_30d` may reflect external factors (e.g., customer behavior). *Solution*: Measure intermediate quality metrics (e.g., review accuracy).
