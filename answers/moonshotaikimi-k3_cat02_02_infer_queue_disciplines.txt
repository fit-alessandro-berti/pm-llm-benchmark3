# 3. Supporting and contradicting cases

**QA — support:** A03 (appointment chosen over older A02 and over P1/shortest A04); A02overA04 at 08:32 (rejects priority & SPT); A05overA08(P1) and over shorter A07 at 09:05; A07overA08(P1) at 09:32. **Challenge:** A04 (P1) and A08 (P1) are never expedited — this is *not* a violation of FIFO+appointment, it is the diagnosis (it only contradicts a hypothetical "QA is priority" claim). A06 starting 5 after its 09:15 appointment is fully explained by a *known* constraint (nonpreemption of A05 + no lookahead), not an undocumented exception. The 08:3008:32 idle before A02 is within the 2 scan/transition tolerance (data quality), not a policy exception. No undocumented behavioral exception is required for QA.

**QB — support:** B04 (P3, est 8) delayed to 10:28 behind longer/later jobs (kills SPT); B05 (P1) served at 10:00 ahead of older B04 and ahead of resuming B01 (a genuine P1 expedite); B01 (oldest) resumed at 10:14 over B06 (P2) and over shorter B04/B07 (rejects a *priority baseline*, supports FIFO baseline). **Challenge:** (i) B01's second stop at 09:48 launching B03 while B05 (P1) waited — the recorded reason is "dispatcher reassignment," i.e. an **offpolicy manual act (undocumented mechanism)**, not derivable from FIFO/priority/SPT; treating it as policy would falsely strengthen "nopriority." (ii) B02's preemption is **observationally SRPT** (B02 est15 < B01 rem31), so it cannot by itself prove preemptive priority. (iii) B05 (P1, arrived 09:46) did **not** preempt B01 (rem14) whereas B02 (P1) did preempt B01 (rem31) — reconciled only by a remainingwork/preemptioncost threshold (labeled **Athresh**) or by the concurrent reassignment. B07's missed 10:30 appointment is explained by a known feature (appointments not protected in QB) and is consistent with the diagnosis.

**QC — support:** C02C03C04C01 in strictly ascending estimate (8<14<22<36) is a textbook SPT signature on the 10:10 backlog; C06 (est6) inserted at 11:34 (shortest) ahead of long C01. **Challenge:** C04 chosen at 10:32 over C06 (est6, arrived 10:25) — estimatenoise is **not** available as a rescue (C06 realized = 6 exactly), so this *requires* the committedsnapshot/noreplan assumption (**Asnap**); without it, SPT is genuinely violated. C05's selection at 10:54 is explained by an SLA/priority expedite but is prioritydeadline confounded (a known ambiguity, not necessarily an exception). C01 (est36) over C07 (est18, P2, dl12:00) at 12:16 is explainable only by an aging/antistarvation flush (labeled **Aage**) or pairwise FIFO — and it *causes* C07 to miss 12:00, so if management had claimed EDF this is a hard contradiction (no known constraint rescues it).

**QD — support:** D04 over D08 on DM (D08 is P1 *and* shortest — skipped); D05DF only, D07DX only, D02DF only (skill pinning exactly as dictated); D06 started at its 11:30 appointment on the only free eligible resource (DX; DF busy until 11:36); D10 forced to DX at 12:16 by DF's meeting (known availability). **Challenge:** DF idles 11:36–12:00 while D10 (P1, est12) waited although D10 could have finished by 11:48 before the meeting — explainable only by perresource FIFO headofline blocking behind the older, meetingblocked D05, or by routing D10 exclusively to DX (labeled **Ablock/Aroute**); without one of these, the idle is a real keepbusy/priority violation. D10 (P1) sitting behind D07 (P3) on DX is consistent with arrival order, so it contradicts only a priority claim, not the FIFO reading. No undocumented *behavioral* exception is needed once blocking/routing is accepted.

# 4. Counterfactual discrimination tests

**Episode QB09:16 (B02 preempts B01).** Explanation A = preemptive priority (P1 preempts P3). Explanation B = SRPT (preempt because B02 est15 < B01 remaining31). They already diverge on the *next* free instant (09:31): pure priority (no victimresume) predicts B03 (P2); SRPTwithresume / insertandresume predicts B01 — and B01 is observed, favoring B/resume over A. The decisive additional observable: an arrival that is **P1 but *longer* than the current job's remaining work**. SRPT would then *not* preempt (victim finishes sooner) while priority still would. Field to capture: at the preemption instant, the victim's current *remaining* estimate, the preemptor's estimate, and a resumevictim flag. (In these data B02 est15 < rem31, so the two rules are tied; a single case with preemptorest > victimremaining separates them.)

**Episode QC10:54 (C05 selected).** Explanation A = priority override (serve P1). Explanation B = earliestdeadline/SLA override (serve deadline 10:50, already blown). They coincide for C05 (P1 **and** earliest deadline **and** already late). They diverge on a job that is P1 with a *late* deadline while a lowerpriority job has an *earlier tight* deadline: a priority rule picks the P1; an EDF rule picks the earlierdeadline job. Discriminating observable: log, at selection time, both the priority class **and** the computed slack (dl  now  est_remaining) plus the trigger that fired (prioritythreshold vs slack0). Note the data already contain a partial separator at 12:16 (C01 P3/dl13:00 vs C07 P2/dl12:00): EDF *and* priority both demand C07 (it could still meet 12:00 if started by 11:42–12:00), yet C01 ran — so the *tail* rejects EDF and priority and supports aging/FIFO; what remains open is only whether the *C05* override was priority or deadlinetriggered.

**Episode QA09:05 (A05 launched though A06's 09:15 appointment is near).** Explanation A = releasetime appointment + FIFO (no lookahead)  run A05 (est15), A06 slips to 09:20. Explanation B = protected appointment window  at 09:05 either idle to 09:15 or run A07 (est9, done 09:14) to keep A06 on time. Observed = run A05 (A06 slips)  supports A. Discriminating observable: whether the dispatcher *ever* inserts idle time or a short filler specifically to protect an upcoming appointment. Fields: perappointment earliest/latest start (tolerance) and a holdforappointment flag with the plannedvsactual start.

# 5. Operational exceptions and alternative explanations

Ranked most  least plausible; "effect" states how the diagnosis moves if the exception holds.

1. **Offpolicy manual dispatcher reassignment (QB, B01's 2nd stop 09:48) — high plausibility (explicitly recorded).** Effect: removes the B03 selection from policy inference; neutral for the FIFObaseline reading. If instead treated as policy, QB would look *even less* prioritydriven (B03 P2 over B05 P1), reinforcing the FIFO baseline. It is the one clear undocumented mechanism.
2. **Committedsnapshot / noreplan sequencing of the 10:10 backlog (QC) — highmedium (needed for C04 before C06 at 10:32).** Effect: preserves SPT for the backlog (C02C03C04). If false (dispatcher did replan on arrivals), C04overC06 is a real SPT violation and QC shifts to "SPT within a fixed batch only." Reinforced by the fact that estimates were exact (so noise cannot explain skipping C06).
3. **Perresource FIFO headofline blocking / fixed routing (QD) — medium.** Effect: explains DF's 11:36–12:00 idle (D05, older and DFonly, is meetingblocked and heads the queue) and D10's lateness without any priority override. If routing is instead free, that idle is a genuine keepbusy/priority violation and weakens QD's clean FIFO reading.
4. **Preemptioncost / remainingwork threshold for the asymmetric P1 handling (QB: B02 preempts B01 at rem31; B05 does not preempt B01 at rem14) — medium (assumption Athresh).** Effect: reconciles the two P1 arrivals under "expedite P1, preempt only when worthwhile." If no such threshold exists, the asymmetry is an undocumented inconsistency and QB's confidence drops further.
5. **Arrivalscan delay (2) and 1 rounding — high as a dataquality fact, low explanatory power.** Effect: does **not** flip any decision (A04/A08 present long before QA choices; C06 visible by 10:25 for the 10:32 choice; B05 visible by 09:46 for the 09:48 choice). It only softens borderline timing (e.g. the QA 08:3008:32 gap) and the B05at09:48 margin by ±2.
6. **Noisy or strategically inaccurate `est_min` — low, and largely refuted here.** Realized duration equals `est_min` for **every** job in all four queues (verified in code). Effect: this exception cannot justify any skippedshortjob (C06 at 10:32; D08 at 11:25; A04/A07 in QA). Those skips are therefore genuine policy choices, which *strengthens* the FIFO readings (QA, QD) and forces the snapshot assumption (QC) rather than letting "bad estimates" absorb the violations.

# 6. Additional event data needed

Priorityordered collection plan, tailored to the live ambiguities:

1. **Dispatchdecision timestamp + full eligibility set at the instant of choice (all queues).** Separates "not chosen" from "not eligible/known." Directly tests: QB09:48 (was B05 in the eligibility set?), QC10:32 (was C06 in the committed snapshot?), QD11:36 (was D10 eligible for DF or prerouted to DX?). Fields: decision_ts, resource_id, eligible_job_ids, and for each the arrival/queueentry time.

Separates preemptivepriority from SRPT (B02 case) and from the admitted reassignment (B01 2nd stop); also logs any preemption/setup cost to test Athresh.

Explains why A03/A06 (QA) are honored at release while B07 (QB, 10:30) is ignored, and whether any queue ever inserts idle/filler to protect a window (the QA09:05 test).

Deprioritized: auditing estimate accuracy — realized already equals estimated for every job, so estimateprovenance logging is only worth keeping if strategicallyinflated estimates are suspected in *other* periods.

# 7. Final synthesis

Most identifiable: **QA** — it offers replicated, independent discriminative decisions that all point one way (appointmentoverFIFO/priority at 08:20; FIFOoverpriority/SPT at 08:32, 09:05 and 09:32), and the estimates are exact, so the rejections of priority and SPT are clean.
