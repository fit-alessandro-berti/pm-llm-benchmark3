## 1. Duplicate-handling decision tree

Start with immutable source observations and a candidate report. Equal label/time, shared objects, similar payloads, and proximity in ingestion are candidate signals, not identity proofs. The supplied table contains 17 distinct event IDs and no demonstrated exact technical duplicate.

Apply this tree once per candidate, choosing a scenario before changing data:

- Same source transaction identity, identical business attributes and qualified relations, with no correction/version distinction? **Exact technical duplicate: drop** redundant observations from the analytical view; retain their raw rows and map them to the survivor. Different ingestion times alone need not distinguish retries.
- Explicit reconciliation or business identity establishes one occurrence, and event granularity agrees? **Multiple observations of one physical event: merge attributes and compatible relations.** Choose a stable canonical ID and preserve every observation.
- One record is generated from another transaction or represents an aggregate? **Derived summary: retain both with refined labels or qualifiers** by default. Merge only in an explicitly selected abstraction, with confirmed correspondence and compatible relations. A summary of several payments cannot be assigned arbitrarily to one payment.
- Original source meanings differ despite a harmonized label? **Harmonization collision: retain both with refined labels or qualifiers.** Shared objects do not repair a semantic collision.
- Same-label events concern different occurrence-defining objects? **Concurrent/distinct events: retain both unchanged**, retaining their object associations. This does not necessarily prove simultaneous execution.
- Evidence remains insufficient or conflicts? **Unresolved candidate: retain both unchanged**, flagged for review. **Quarantine for review** when contradictions prevent a coherent representation; keep the records in the immutable raw view and report their exclusion from any analytical view.

Check occurrence identity before payload equality: enrichment explains e03/e04's different hashes. Conversely, matching hashes alone would not prove identical occurrences.

“Full lineage” below means original event and E2O rows, source keys, sequences, hashes, timestamps, labels, and the decision record. Canonical scalar attributes never silently overwrite conflicting source values.

## 3. Timestamp-tie ordering rules

Build a precedence DAG within each original timestamp bucket, projecting original IDs onto canonical IDs after merging. Preserve edge reason and confidence. Use these distinct evidence classes:

1. **Domain constraints:** supplied occurrence-specific facts, notably e12→e13 and e09→e10, are semantic constraints.
2. **Within-source sequence:** increasing source_seq orders that source's records only. This is recorded-order evidence, not proof of cross-system physical causality. Merged observations contribute sequences from all their sources.
3. **Object lifecycle constraints:** add only validated rules at the relevant object and occurrence scope. Sharing O1 does not establish invoice-before-authorization or pick-before-pack prerequisites.
4. **Source-specific semantics:** message derivation can establish cause-before-update. The orders below assume the named packing transaction generated e07, and the listed captures generated e15's messages. Because message IDs are absent, also evaluate the variant without these particular edges.
5. **Ingestion time:** retain for auditing latency. It may optionally break otherwise unconstrained ties, explicitly as a technical convention; the default here does not use it.
6. **Stable fallback:** deterministic topological sort, selecting the lexicographically smallest canonical eid among ready nodes. This choice is purely representational.

A cycle blocks linearization pending review; do not silently discard a constraint. A source constraint contradicting timestamps also needs review rather than rewriting business time.

At 10:00, after e04→e03, supported source edges include e03→e05 (SCANNER) and e03→e06 (WMS); assumed message lineage adds e06→e07. There is no supplied hard e05→e06 prerequisite. The default extension is **e03, e05, e06, e07**; **e03, e06, e07, e05** is also admissible. A validated package lifecycle could narrow this set.

At 10:15, **e09→e10** is hard. Warehouse release preceding acceptance is plausible but not explicitly established by a cross-system link. Default: **e08, e09, e10**; **e09, e08, e10** also satisfies the supplied constraints.

At 12:00, e16→e11. ERP gives e11→e15; PSP gives e12→e13→e14. The latter does not make M2 dependent on M1's lifecycle. Assumed message lineage adds e13/e14→e15. Default: **e11, e12, e13, e14, e15**; **e12, e13, e14, e11, e15** is admissible. Do not invent invoice-issuance-before-authorization. In the evidence scenario, the merged packing event replaces e06/e07 elsewhere.

Only after choosing an extension may synthetic offsets encode it for software requiring strict timestamps. Adding microseconds first can turn row order into purported causality, change durations, and hide concurrency. Retain the DAG and tie membership alongside the extension.

## 4. Conservative cleaning pipeline

Snapshot raw tables; validate IDs, object types, timestamp parsing, and relation references. Generate candidates, inspect source identity and granularity, and record decisions. Merge only e03/e04 and e11/e16. Keep both summary events, refining e07/e15 as above, and restore e08/e09's different meanings. Preserve original identifiers, activities, sources, keys, and business timestamps in columns and an observation sidecar.

Merge attributes by documented per-field authority; otherwise retain named alternatives and flag conflicts. Union only compatible qualified relations. Do not fabricate missing Payment links for e15. Construct the DAG and deterministic extension, retaining original time in the principal analytical OCEL and offering a separately identified synthetic-time export.

Expected size: **15 events and 39 distinct E2O links**, from raw **17/44**. Removing duplicate observations subtracts three links for e04 and two for e16. No supplied record requires technical-duplicate removal or quarantine. The object inventory remains eight objects.

## 5. Aggressive alternatives

**Evidence-based semantic merge:** starting from the conservative view, additionally merge e06/e07 into e06 if message lineage and the selected transition-level abstraction justify it. Preserve e15 as an invoice-balance update and both captures. Expected: **14 events, 37 distinct E2O links**. Main damage: loss of a separately observable ERP update and its latency; ERP's observation may appear to cover items that only WMS recorded. Without confirmation, this remains a sensitivity scenario.

**Mechanically aggressive PM4Py merge:** use an independent raw-label copy, inspect its candidate groups, then merge equal activity/time events with common objects. Groups are {e03,e04,e05}, {e06,e07}, {e08,e09}, {e11,e16}, and {e13,e14,e15}. Expected, under that grouping contract: **10 events, 28 distinct E2O links**. The code verifies the installed implementation actually produces these groups.

`have_common_object=True` does not protect e13/e14: both share O1 and V1. The merged capture would connect M1 and M2, absorb an invoice summary, and lose capture multiplicity. Likewise, the merged pick links I1 and I2, and merged dispatch erases the release/acceptance distinction. These are intentional stress-test errors, never the production recommendation. Applying this operation after label refinement would yield different groups and counts; declare the starting view explicitly.

## 6. PM4Py-oriented Python implementation

The example uses OCEL-configured column names and the supplied source columns. It assumes unique event IDs, as in the table. Exact-duplicate removal is deliberately conditional: no such group is demonstrated here. PM4Py calls are guarded by structural postconditions rather than assumed to implement business identity. Relation conflicts stop processing. Attribute alternatives remain in lossless source records until a documented authority policy selects a value.

```python
from copy import deepcopy
import heapq
import json
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

E = ocel_raw.event_id_column
A = ocel_raw.event_activity
T = ocel_raw.event_timestamp
O = ocel_raw.object_id_column
OT = ocel_raw.object_type_column
RAW = deepcopy(ocel_raw)                  # Never mutate the caller's OCEL.
OBS = RAW.events.set_index(E, drop=False)
OBS.index.name = None  # Avoid index/column ambiguity in source-sequence sorting.
assert OBS.index.is_unique
assert {"source", "source_event_key", "source_seq", "ingested_at",
        "payload_hash"}.issubset(OBS.columns)
assert pd.to_datetime(OBS[T], utc=True, errors="raise").notna().all()
# Store RAW as a durable, access-controlled snapshot in a real deployment.
manifest = {"rule_version": "duplicate-tie-v1",
            "pm4py_version": getattr(pm4py, "__version__", "unknown")}
audit = []

def encode(value):
    return json.dumps(value, default=str, sort_keys=True)

def relation_keys(x):
    q = getattr(x, "qualifier", "ocel:qualifier")
    return [E, O] + ([q] if q in x.relations.columns else [])

def links(x, ids):
    rows = x.relations[x.relations[E].isin(ids)]
    return set(rows[O])

def lineage(ids):
    return encode([OBS.loc[i].to_dict() for i in sorted(ids)])

def origins(row):
    return json.loads(row["source_event_ids"])

def validate(x):
    assert x.events[E].notna().all() and x.events[E].is_unique
    assert x.objects[O].notna().all() and x.objects[O].is_unique
    assert x.relations[E].isin(x.events[E]).all()
    assert x.relations[O].isin(x.objects[O]).all()
    ev = x.events.set_index(E)
    assert x.relations[A].eq(x.relations[E].map(ev[A])).all()
    assert x.relations[T].eq(x.relations[E].map(ev[T])).all()
    if OT in x.relations:
        types = x.objects.set_index(O)[OT]
        assert x.relations[OT].eq(x.relations[O].map(types)).all()
    for name, cols, valid_ids in [
        ("e2e", [E, E + "_2"], set(x.events[E])),
        ("o2o", [O, O + "_2"], set(x.objects[O])),
        ("object_changes", [O], set(x.objects[O])),
    ]:
        table = getattr(x, name, None)
        if table is not None:
            for col in cols:
                if col in table:
                    assert table[col].isin(valid_ids).all()

def sync(x):
    ev = x.events.set_index(E)
    for col in [A, T, "original_timestamp", "original_activity"]:
        if col in ev:
            x.relations[col] = x.relations[E].map(ev[col])
    return x

def normalize_relations(x):
    sync(x)
    keys = relation_keys(x)
    # Duplicate keys may be collapsed only if all other relation values agree.
    for _, group in x.relations.groupby(keys, dropna=False, sort=False):
        for col in set(x.relations.columns) - set(keys):
            if group[col].map(repr).nunique(dropna=False) > 1:
                raise ValueError(f"Conflicting relation attribute: {col}")
    x.relations = x.relations.drop_duplicates(keys).reset_index(drop=True)
    return x

def remap_event_references(x, mapping):
    x.relations[E] = x.relations[E].replace(mapping)
    table = getattr(x, "e2e", None)
    if table is not None:
        for col in [E, E + "_2"]:
            if col in table:
                table[col] = table[col].replace(mapping)
        if E in table and E + "_2" in table:
            # Internalized edges remain recoverable from RAW and the ID map.
            x.e2e = table[table[E] != table[E + "_2"]].copy()

base = deepcopy(RAW)
base.events[T] = pd.to_datetime(base.events[T], utc=True)
base.events["original_timestamp"] = base.events[T]
base.events["original_activity"] = base.events[A]
base.events["source_event_ids"] = base.events[E].map(lambda i: encode([i]))
base.events["attributes_by_source"] = base.events[E].map(lambda i: lineage([i]))
sync(base)
validate(base)

# Candidate pre-check: inspect both identities and object scopes before acting.
candidate_report = []
for (activity, timestamp), group in base.events.groupby([A, T], sort=True):
    if len(group) > 1:
        candidate_report.append({
            "activity": activity, "timestamp": timestamp,
            "observations": [
                {"eid": row[E], "source": row["source"],
                 "key": row["source_event_key"], "hash": row["payload_hash"],
                 "objects": sorted(links(base, [row[E]]))}
                for _, row in group.iterrows()]
        })

def merge_verified(x, ids, canonical, rule, expected_objects, confidence):
    ids = list(ids)
    assert canonical in ids and set(ids).issubset(set(x.events[E]))
    selected = x.events[x.events[E].isin(ids)]
    assert selected[T].nunique() == 1  # This example merges only tied records.
    assert links(x, ids) == set(expected_objects)
    source_ids = sorted({i for _, row in selected.iterrows() for i in origins(row)})
    canonical_row = selected[selected[E] == canonical].copy()
    canonical_row["source_event_ids"] = encode(source_ids)
    canonical_row["attributes_by_source"] = lineage(source_ids)
    # Canonical scalar source/key remain the canonical observation's values.
    # Unknown field authority never overwrites these source-qualified records.
    audit.append({"canonical_eid": canonical, "source_event_ids": source_ids,
                  "merge_rule": rule, "confidence": confidence, **manifest,
                  "event_rows": selected.to_dict("records"),
                  "relation_rows": x.relations[x.relations[E].isin(ids)]
                                           .to_dict("records")})
    x.events = pd.concat([x.events[~x.events[E].isin(ids)], canonical_row],
                         ignore_index=True)
    remap_event_references(x, {i: canonical for i in ids})
    normalize_relations(x)
    validate(x)
    return x

def subset(x, ids):
    y = deepcopy(x)
    y.events = y.events[y.events[E].isin(ids)].copy()
    y.relations = y.relations[y.relations[E].isin(ids)].copy()
    table = getattr(y, "e2e", None)
    if table is not None and E in table and E + "_2" in table:
        y.e2e = table[table[E].isin(ids) & table[E + "_2"].isin(ids)].copy()
    return y

def exact_signature(x, row):
    # All source business fields agree; ingestion metadata may differ on retry.
    original = OBS.loc[row[E]]
    fields = [(c, repr(original[c])) for c in sorted(OBS.columns)
              if c not in {E, "ingested_at"}]
    rel = x.relations[x.relations[E] == row[E]].drop(columns=[E])
    relation_rows = sorted(encode(r) for r in rel.to_dict("records"))
    return encode([fields, relation_rows])

def technical_dedup(x):
    groups = {}
    for _, row in x.events.iterrows():
        if pd.isna(row["source_event_key"]):
            continue
        groups.setdefault(exact_signature(x, row), []).append(row[E])
    for ids in groups.values():
        if len(ids) < 2:
            continue                         # No call on unverified candidates.
        trial = pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates(deepcopy(subset(x, ids)))
        validate(trial)
        assert len(trial.events) == 1, "Installed drop contract differs; review"
        canonical = trial.events.iloc[0][E]
        assert canonical in ids
        assert links(trial, [canonical]) == links(x, ids)
        # Rebuild the validated survivor with full lineage and attributes.
        x = merge_verified(x, ids, canonical, "exact-source-retry",
                           links(x, ids), "high")
    return x

conservative = technical_dedup(deepcopy(base))
conservative = merge_verified(conservative, ["e03", "e04"], "e03",
    "reconciled-physical-scan", {"O1", "I1", "P1"}, "high")
conservative = merge_verified(conservative, ["e11", "e16"], "e11",
    "verified-same-invoice-issuance", {"O1", "V1"}, "high")
refinements = {"e07": "Update ERP Packing Status", "e08": "Release from Warehouse",
               "e09": "Accept by Carrier", "e15": "Update Invoice Balance"}
for eid, label in refinements.items():
    conservative.events.loc[conservative.events[E] == eid, A] = label
sync(conservative)
validate(conservative)

evidence = merge_verified(deepcopy(conservative), ["e06", "e07"], "e06",
    "conditional-one-packing-transition", {"O1", "I1", "I2", "P1"},
    "scenario assumption; require message/abstraction confirmation")

# WARNING: destructive sensitivity experiment on RAW labels.
# Every inspected candidate group shares O1, including the distinct payments.
plan = {(a, t): sorted(g[E])
        for (a, t), g in base.events.groupby([A, T], sort=True)}
assert all("O1" in links(base, [i]) for ids in plan.values() for i in ids)
aggressive = pm4py.ocel_merge_duplicates(deepcopy(base), have_common_object=True)
assert len(aggressive.events) == len(plan), "Unexpected installed merge contract"
assert not aggressive.events.duplicated([A, T]).any()
assert set(zip(aggressive.events[A], aggressive.events[T])) == set(plan)
new_ids = {}
for index, row in aggressive.events.iterrows():
    ids = plan[(row[A], row[T])]
    assert links(aggressive, [row[E]]) == links(base, ids)
    canonical = min(ids)                       # Replace generated IDs stably.
    new_ids[row[E]] = canonical
    aggressive.events.at[index, E] = canonical
    for col in ["source", "source_event_key", "source_seq", "ingested_at",
                "payload_hash"]:
        aggressive.events.at[index, col] = OBS.loc[canonical, col]
    aggressive.events.at[index, "source_event_ids"] = encode(ids)
    aggressive.events.at[index, "attributes_by_source"] = lineage(ids)
    aggressive.events.at[index, "original_timestamp"] = row[T]
    aggressive.events.at[index, "original_activity"] = row[A]
    audit.append({"canonical_eid": canonical, "source_event_ids": ids,
                  "merge_rule": "mechanical-common-object", "confidence": "unsafe",
                  **manifest})
remap_event_references(aggressive, new_ids)
normalize_relations(aggressive)
validate(aggressive)

# These particular message links are scenario assumptions, not identity proofs.
MESSAGE_EDGES = [("e06", "e07"), ("e13", "e15"), ("e14", "e15")]
DOMAIN_EDGES = [("e09", "e10"), ("e12", "e13")]

def ordered_views(x, message_edges=MESSAGE_EDGES):
    x = deepcopy(x)
    mapping = {i: row[E] for _, row in x.events.iterrows() for i in origins(row)}
    stamp = x.events.set_index(E)[T].to_dict()
    constraints = [(a, b, "domain") for a, b in DOMAIN_EDGES]
    constraints += [(a, b, "assumed-message-lineage") for a, b in message_edges]
    for _, group in OBS.groupby("source", sort=True):
        group = group.dropna(subset=["source_seq"]).sort_values(["source_seq", E])
        assert not group["source_seq"].duplicated().any(), "Review equal source sequences"
        seq = group[E].tolist()
        constraints += [(a, b, "source-record-sequence") for a, b in zip(seq, seq[1:])]
    adjacency = {i: set() for i in stamp}
    indegree = {i: 0 for i in stamp}
    edges = []
    for a0, b0, reason in constraints:
        a, b = mapping[a0], mapping[b0]
        edges.append((a0, b0, a, b, reason, a == b))
        if a == b:                             # Internalized, but still audited.
            continue
        if stamp[a] > stamp[b]:
            raise ValueError("Source/domain constraint conflicts with timestamp")
        if stamp[a] == stamp[b] and b not in adjacency[a]:
            adjacency[a].add(b)
            indegree[b] += 1
    order = []
    for _, bucket in x.events.groupby(T, sort=True):
        ids = set(bucket[E])
        ready = [i for i in ids if indegree[i] == 0]
        heapq.heapify(ready)
        extension = []
        while ready:
            i = heapq.heappop(ready)
            extension.append(i)
            for j in sorted(adjacency[i]):
                indegree[j] -= 1
                if indegree[j] == 0:
                    heapq.heappush(ready, j)
        if len(extension) != len(ids):
            raise ValueError("Precedence cycle: quarantine decision for review")
        order.extend(extension)
    rank = {i: n for n, i in enumerate(order)}
    x.events["tie_order"] = x.events[E].map(rank)
    x.relations["tie_order"] = x.relations[E].map(rank)
    x = pm4py.ocel_sort_by_additional_column(x, "tie_order")
    assert x.events[E].tolist() == order
    validate(x)
    # Original-time OCEL is the principal view; adjusted OCEL is a separate export.
    adjusted = pm4py.ocel_add_index_based_timedelta(deepcopy(x))
    adjusted.events["adjusted_timestamp"] = adjusted.events[T]
    sync(adjusted)
    adjusted.relations["adjusted_timestamp"] = adjusted.relations[T]
    assert adjusted.events["original_timestamp"].equals(x.events["original_timestamp"])
    times = adjusted.events.set_index(E).loc[order, T]
    assert times.diff().iloc[1:].gt(pd.Timedelta(0)).all()
    original = sorted(x.events[T].unique())
    next_time = dict(zip(original, original[1:]))
    for _, row in adjusted.events.iterrows():
        start = row["original_timestamp"]
        assert row[T] >= start
        if start in next_time:
            assert row[T] < next_time[start], "Offset crosses a business-time boundary"
    validate(adjusted)
    return x, adjusted, edges

scenarios = {"raw": deepcopy(base), "conservative": deepcopy(conservative),
             "evidence": deepcopy(evidence), "aggressive": deepcopy(aggressive)}
expected = {"raw": (17, 44), "conservative": (15, 39),

ordered, linearized, precedence = {}, {}, {}
for name, x in scenarios.items():

    assert counts == expected[name], (name, counts)
    ordered[name], linearized[name], precedence[name] = ordered_views(x)
```

## 7. Sensitivity analysis

Counts below use distinct (event, object) links; qualifiers would require a separate qualified-link count. All scenarios keep eight objects. Compare each cleaning scenario across admissible tie orders, keeping that uncertainty separate from deduplication effects.

| Dataset | Events / E2O | Order-flattened DFG | Item-flattened DFG | Payment-flattened DFG |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Raw | 17 / 44 | O1 contains every event; duplicate-looking loops and summary interleavings depend on ties | I1 has two scan observations; I2 has one pick; both reach packing | M1: Authorize→Capture; M2: one capture; e15 appears in neither |
| Conservative | 15 / 39 | Removes verified observation repetitions; labels distinguish logistics and summaries | I1 duplicate pick disappears; each item has Pick→Pack | Same object traces and capture multiplicity as raw |
| Evidence merge | 14 / 37 | Removes Pack→ERP Packing Status adjacency when that order was chosen | Same per-item traces as conservative: e07 had no Item links | Unchanged from conservative |
| Aggressive library merge | 10 / 28 | Removes several loops by collapsing real work and semantic distinctions | Each item still has Pick→Pack, concealing that one merged pick now covers both | Same activity-level trace shapes can survive, while a single capture eid is shared across M1/M2 |

Concrete changed patterns include: I1's **Pick→Pick** disappearing appropriately; O1's remaining **Pick→Pick** representing two items; raw **Pack→Pack** becoming **Pack→Update ERP Packing Status**; **Dispatch→Dispatch** becoming **Release→Accept**; **Accept→Handover** replacing Dispatch→Handover; repeated/interleaved **Issue Invoice** observations disappearing; and O1's **Capture→Capture→Capture** becoming two captures followed by **Update Invoice Balance** under the default order. Aggressive cleaning suppresses real pick/capture multiplicity. Alternative tie orders can replace adjacencies without changing any occurrence.

For OC-DFGs, report event-pair, unique-event, total object-occurrence, and unique-object frequencies separately, with the installed metric definitions recorded. The conservative Pick→Pack edge has two item occurrences and two different source pick events; the aggressive version can have two item occurrences but only one event pair. Summing flattened frequencies confuses these units. Report edges forced across all admissible extensions versus order-dependent edges; incomparability means unknown order, not proven physical concurrency.

ET–OT weights depend on definition. With event-object incidence weights, raw Pick–Item is 3, conservative/evidence 2, and aggressive still 2, although distinct Pick events incident to Item become 3/2/1. Capture–Payment incidence remains 2 in every scenario, masking the aggressive loss of a distinct capture. Capture–Order incidence changes 3→2→2→1 with label refinement included. Separate label-change effects from deletion effects.

For an OTG defined by objects co-occurring in an event, conservative/evidence merges introduce no new object pairs. Aggressive capture merging introduces M1–M2 co-occurrence. I1–I2 already co-occurred at packing, so merging picks changes its event support but not necessarily the binary OTG. For type-level OTGs, the Payment–Payment edge appears only if same-type edges are represented. A stored explicit O2O graph remains unchanged; specify which graph is measured.

Apparent loops are not automatically rework: distinguish repeated observations, repeated work on different objects, and abstraction updates. Compute lifecycle counts and durations on original timestamps. Tied steps have zero recorded separation and unresolved subsecond duration. Synthetic offsets must not become service times, waiting times, SLA violations, or measured concurrency. Evidence merging removes visibility into ERP propagation; aggressive merging may make overlapping payment lifecycles look like one transaction. Evaluate median/tail durations, start/end choices, and edge frequencies across ordering variants, reporting ranges without assigning unsupported probabilities.

## 8. Audit trail and reversibility

Use an append-only decision table keyed by scenario, decision ID, and canonical eid. Record source event IDs and immutable row locators; merge/drop/refinement rule; reviewer or rule version; original and refined activity; original, ingestion, and adjusted timestamps; ordering rank and constraint IDs; attributes retained from each source; unresolved or discarded-from-scalar conflicts; confidence and reason; source authority mapping; and original/canonical qualified E2O relations. Include library version, parameters, snapshot checksum, and every internalized precedence edge.

Store complete source records and relation rows separately, not merely concatenated IDs or hashes. Source/key scalar columns identify the canonical observation; the sidecar preserves all contributors. Reconstruct raw by selecting the immutable snapshot or replaying original rows and relations by row locator. Never reconstruct by splitting unioned relations heuristically. Scenario transformations and synthetic time are reversible views, not destructive changes to source evidence.

## 9. Failure modes and safeguards

- **Collapsing partial payments:** require occurrence identity and preserve M1/M2 captures separately.
- **False rework from summaries:** distinguish ERP updates from operational transactions and compare abstraction views.
- **Ordering by ingestion:** use source/domain evidence; retain ingestion only for technical analysis.
- **Shared objects treated as duplicate proof:** use shared scope only to generate candidates.
- **Incompatible E2O unions:** validate types, qualifiers, occurrence scope, and relation attributes; stop on conflicts.
- **Synthetic durations:** keep business time immutable and exclude offsets from performance calculations.
- **Lost authoritative attributes:** retain source-qualified alternatives and explicit per-field authority decisions.
- **Whole-OCEL duplicate functions without inspection:** preview groups, isolate technical duplicates, and validate mechanical scenario membership/counts.
- **Cross-source sequence comparison:** partition sequence evidence by source and retain all merged-source observations.
- **Harmonization collisions:** retain original labels and version the mapping; restore release versus acceptance.
- **Cycles hidden by arbitrary sorting:** expose contradictory edges and quarantine the ordering decision.
- **Equal DFGs mistaken for equal semantics:** compare event identity, E2O structure, object-specific multiplicity, and metric definitions alongside graphs.
