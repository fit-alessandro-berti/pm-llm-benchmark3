# 4. Statistical analysis plan

Kaplan-Meier for time-to-resolution treating competing events as censoring: simple but biased if withdrawals/rejections/transfers are informative.

Aalen-Johansen / cumulative incidence function: primary for Estimand 1. Estimates probability of resolution by 72 h in presence of competing Withdraw/Reject/Transfer. Stratify by cohort and priority. Handles right censored opens.

Cause-specific hazards: model hazard of resolution vs each competing cause. Useful for understanding drivers of escalation and withdrawal.

Subdistribution hazards Fine-Gray: models effect on cumulative incidence directly, useful for operational ranking.

Restricted mean time: summarizes average time in system up to 72 h.

Landmarking: handles time-varying process features without leakage. Landmark at e.g., 24 h and 48 h using features known up to landmark.

Multi-state model: recommended secondary approach. States: Submitted  Screening  Review/AutoCheck  Escalated/Rework  Resolve/Withdraw/Reject/Transfer. Allows stage-specific hazards and estimation of probability of being pending at 72 h.

Recommendation: Primary = Aalen-Johansen cumulative incidence of resolution with competing risks, cohort/priority stratified, with transfer sensitivity T0/T1/T2. Secondary = landmark multi-state model with time-varying stage, rework count, escalation timing, and inactivity gap.

# 2. Encode estimands and transfer scenarios
def encode(df, transfer_mode):
    out = df.copy()
    out["time"] = out["duration_or_age_h"]
    out["resolve_event"] = (out["observed_status"]=="resolved").astype(int)
    # competing codes
    out["competing"] = 0
    out.loc[out["observed_status"]=="withdrawn","competing"]=1
    out.loc[out["observed_status"]=="rejected","competing"]=2
    if transfer_mode=="competing":
        out.loc[out["observed_status"]=="transferred","competing"]=3
    elif transfer_mode=="censor":
        out.loc[out["observed_status"]=="transferred","resolve_event"]=0
    elif transfer_mode=="worst":
        # treat as resolve after horizon for estimand 1
        out.loc[out["observed_status"]=="transferred","time"]=72+1
        out.loc[out["observed_status"]=="transferred","resolve_event"]=1
    # censoring for open
    out["censored"] = (out["observed_status"]=="open").astype(int)
    return out

# 9. Limitations and additional data

Priority missing data:
* Exact event timestamps per activity to avoid fabricating order-only timestamps.
* External outcomes after Transfer to test informative censoring assumptions.
* Case start dates and cutoff date to model left truncation and calendar effects.
* Time-varying workload and queue length at each stage.
* Assignment mechanism to Cohort B and policy rollout compliance per case.
* Reasons for Withdraw/Reject to distinguish customer-driven vs process-driven.
* True censoring indicator for opens and whether last_event_gap_h reflects inactivity.

These are needed for unbiased multi-state survival analysis and causal interpretation of policy change.
