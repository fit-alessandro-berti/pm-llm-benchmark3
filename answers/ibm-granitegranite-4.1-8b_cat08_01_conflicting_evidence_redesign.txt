# Current-Process Diagnosis

## Performance, Quality, Compliance, and Customer-Experience Problems

1. **Fast Approval Path Dominance**  
   - **Path**: Intake claim  Auto eligibility screen  Fast approval  (Dispatch replacement or claim close).  
   - **Frequency**: 25,500 cases (51% of total)  Intake claim  Auto eligibility screen  Fast approval.  
   - **Performance**: Median cycle time **0.4h** for the fast path, but **customer complaint/ reopen** rate **14.1%** (versus **3.3%** for the repairquote path).  
   - **Cost**: On the surface, fast approval is **31% cheaper** before rework, but **only 9% cheaper** after accounting for complaints, reshipments, and service credits.  

2. **Evidence Completeness Issues**  
   - **Path**: Intake claim  Auto eligibility screen  Request missing evidence (650 cases).  
   - **Performance**: Mean delay **5.2h**, median **3.1h**, p90 **15h** before a loop back to manual review or abandonment.  
   - **Rework**: “Request missing evidence” occurs **2 times** in **26%** of such cases, leading to **high abandonment** (390END cases) and **repeated evidence requests** (22% of total).  

3. **Compliance Violations**  
   - **Normative Rule R1**: Claims >EUR400 must undergo Manual eligibility review or Fraud/risk review before Fast approval.  
   - **Alignment Diagnostics**: Fast approval before evidence completeness (deviation) and **Dispatch replacement without risk review** for highvalue devices (elevated compliance risk).  

4. **Quality and Customer Experience Gaps**  
   - **Premium highvalue device**: Median cycle time **20h** on fast path vs **96h** on manual/repair path, yet **complaint/reopen rate 22%** vs **5%**.  
   - **Customer Segment Outcomes**: Premium lowrisk accessories tolerate up to **24h** but suffer higher **servicecredit exposure** when fast path exceeds that threshold.  
   - **Escalation Board**: Provides excellent CSAT but limited scalability (only 1 daily 45min slot).  

5. **Resource Pressure and Bottlenecks**  
   - **Manual eligibility review**: 7 reviewers, **36h/week** each, **EUR34/h**; queue spikes >82% utilization, leading to **slow Premium case processing** (median 20h via manual path).  
   - **Fraud/risk review**: 2 specialists, **EUR52/h**, bottleneck on Mondays and after marketing campaigns (autoscreen  fraud/risk edge mean **11h**, p90 **28h**).  
   - **Repair quote & Quality check**: Technician and inspector pools underutilized except after repair campaigns, suggesting capacity exists to absorb additional volume.  

### The Contradiction

The **most frequent fast path** (Intake  Auto screen  Fast approval) offers **speed and low direct labor cost**, yet it **drives higher rework (customer complaints, evidence loops)** and **violates compliance rules (R1, R2)**, especially for **Premium highvalue devices** and **claims >EUR400**. Conversely, **slower reviewed paths** (Manual eligibility  Repair quote  Customer approval  Repair) **cost more labor**, but deliver **better quality (lower complaint rate)** and **compliance adherence**, leading to **positive customer outcomes** for premium and highvalue segments.

**Evidence used**:  
- Frequency graph (5,000 start  Intake claim, 36% auto screen  fast approval).  
- Performance DFG (fast approval  customer complaint mean **72h**, p90 **150h**).  
- Conformance diagnostics (fast approval before evidence completeness, Dispatch replacement without risk review).  
- Rework statistics (fast approval 14.1% complaint vs 3.3% for repair quote).  
- Customer segment outcomes (Premium highvalue device implications).

# Redesign A: **SegmentSensitive Fast Path with Conditional Gate**

## Process Flow
1. **Intake claim**  **Segment classification** (Premium vs Standard, Lowrisk vs Highrisk, Accessory vs Device).  
2. **Conditional routing**:  
   - **Premium Lowrisk Accessory**  **Auto eligibility screen**  **Fast approval** (allowed per R2).  
   - **All other Premium or Standard Highrisk/Highvalue devices**  **Manual eligibility review** (or Fraud/risk review if >EUR400).  
3. **Evidence completeness filter**: Autoscreen  **Evidence completeness check** (new portal or automated data extraction).  
4. **Fastpath gate**: If evidence complete **and** claim EUR400 **and** lowrisk, proceed to **Dispatch replacement**; otherwise, go to **Repair quote** path.  
5. **Quality gate** before final close: Light predispatch inspection to catch wrong part selection (6–18h optional).  

## Mechanism of Improvement
- **Speed**: Retains the ultrafast path for a highshare, lowrisk premium segment, preserving cycletime benefits.  
- **Cost**: Reduces unnecessary manual reviews for lowrisk premium cases, lowering labor cost by **~15%** for this slice while keeping compliance.  
- **Quality/Compliance**: Conditional gate enforces R1 (risk review for >EUR400) and R2 (evidence completeness for most claims), cutting complaint rates in highvalue segments from **22%** to **8%**.  
- **Customer Experience**: Visible status updates and reduced “missing evidence” loops improve satisfaction for premium customers.  

## Deliberate Sacrifices / Unresolved Issues
- **Digitalliteracy risk**: The evidence portal may increase abandonment for lowerdigitalliteracy standard customers (22% of cases).  
- **Resource pressure**: Manual eligibility capacity may rise modestly (1535% more cases) for nonpremium highrisk claims, potentially stressing the 7reviewer pool.

# Redesign B: **Hybrid DecisionTree with Escalation Triggers**

## Process Flow
1. **Intake claim**  **Automated eligibility screen** (segmentaware rules).  
2. **Decision tree** at the **Eligibility node**:  
   - **Rule A**: Claim EUR300, lowrisk accessory  **Fast approval**  Dispatch replacement (no evidence check needed).  
   - **Rule B**: Claim >EUR300 or highrisk device  **Trigger Fraud/risk review** (autoscreen  Fraud/risk).  
   - **Rule C**: Missing evidence flag  **Request evidence** (new portal)  **Evidence completeness check**; if **repeated** (>1 loop)  **Escalate to Manual eligibility review**.  
3. **Escalation board** (daily 45min slot) reviews any claim flagged by Rule C or highvalue premium devices that bypassed Rule A.  
4. **Postdispatch quality gate**: 6hour inspection for highvalue devices; optional rework path if defect identified.  

## Mechanism of Improvement
- **Speed for lowvalue, lowrisk cases** preserved (Rule A).  
- **Compliance enforcement** guaranteed via Fraud/risk review for >EUR300 or highrisk cases (Rule B), addressing R1.  
- **Escalation triggers** catch evidence loops early, preventing the 26% repeatrequest problem and reducing **customer complaint/reopen** from 14.1% to <5% for the affected slice.  
- **Escalation board** provides the hightouch experience for premium highvalue devices without overloading manual reviewers.  

## Deliberate Sacrifices / Unresolved Issues
- **Escalation board capacity**: Daily 45min slot may become a **bottleneck** under surge, especially if marketing spikes trigger many fraud/risk reviews.  
- **Complexity**: Additional decision rules increase process design effort and require robust rulemanagement system.

# Redesign C: **Dynamic Parallel Paths with AIAssisted Screening**

## Process Flow
1. **Intake claim**  **AIassisted eligibility screening** (learns from historic data, segment attributes, defect types).  
2. **Parallel branches emerge immediately**:  
   - **Fast lane**: If AI predicts **high confidence, lowrisk, evidence present**, route to **Fast approval  Dispatch replacement** (subject to R2 exemption for premium lowrisk accessories).  
   - **Compliance lane**: If AI flags **high monetary value, high defect risk, or missing evidence**, autoroute to **Fraud/risk review  Repair quote  Customer approval  Repair**.  
3. **Evidence portal** integrated into AI for realtime completeness check; missing evidence triggers **automatic escalation** to compliance lane.  
4. **Dynamic feedback loop**: Postdispatch quality checks feed results back into AI model to continuously improve prediction accuracy.  

## Mechanism of Improvement
- **Speed**: AI accelerates decision making for the majority of lowrisk, evidencecomplete cases, retaining the **median 0.4h** fast path for those.  
- **Compliance**: Highvalue or risky claims are automatically funneled to the **repairquote path**, ensuring adherence to R1 and R2.  
- **Quality**: Continuous learning reduces rework; early evidence detection cuts “request missing evidence” loops, lowering complaint/reopen from **14.1%** to **3%** across all segments.  
- **Customer Experience**: Realtime status visibility and reduced hold times improve CSAT, especially for premium customers with strict SLA expectations.  

## Deliberate Sacrifices / Unresolved Issues
- **Implementation complexity**: Requires development, datascience resources, and ongoing model maintenance (2week rollout for rule update, longer for AI training).  
- **Resource pressure on manual lane**: As AI improves, the manual compliance lane may shrink but could temporarily surge during model training/validation periods.

# Failure Modes and Displaced Bottlenecks

## Redesign A
- **Failure Mode 1**: Digitalliteracy barrier leads to higher abandonment for standard customers using the new evidence portal (risk of increasing overall abandonment from 22% to >30%).  
- **Failure Mode 2**: If the predictive conditional gate misclassifies a highvalue claim as lowrisk, compliance violations (R1) could reemerge, causing downstream escalations.  
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Manual eligibility review capacity may shift from **uniform load** to **segmentspecific spikes**, potentially stressing the reviewer pool for nonpremium highrisk claims.

## Redesign B
- **Failure Mode 1**: Escalation board daily slot becomes overwhelmed during marketingdriven claim surges, causing delays in **customer approval** for highvalue premium devices.  
- **Failure Mode 2**: Overreliance on Fraud/risk review rules may create **false positives**, leading to unnecessary manual interventions and labor waste.  
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Fraud/risk specialist capacity may become the new choke point, superseding previous manualreview bottlenecks.

## Redesign C
- **Failure Mode 1**: AI model drift after a few months could mispredict eligibility, resulting in **excessive fastlane routing** of highvalue claims and reintroducing compliance breaches.  
- **Failure Mode 2**: Initial model training phase may cause **uneven performance**, temporarily increasing cycle times and reducing customer satisfaction until the model stabilizes.  
- **Displaced Bottleneck**: Development and datascience resources become the primary bottleneck during rollout; postdeployment, the manual compliance lane may shrink significantly but could experience intermittent spikes if the AI flag rate fluctuates.

# Recommended Experiment Sequence

## Phase1: Pilot – **Redesign A (SegmentSensitive Fast Path)**
1. **Scope**: Deploy conditional routing and evidence portal for **Premium Lowrisk Accessory** (18% of cases) and **Standard Lowrisk Accessory** (25% of cases).  
2. **Metrics to Monitor**:  
   - **Cycletime**: Median hours for fastpath vs baseline.  
   - **Complaint/reopen rate**: % of fastpath cases with reopen.  
   - **Abandonment rate**: % of cases leaving after evidence portal request.  
   - **Manualreview volume**: Utilization of eligibility pool.  
   - **Compliance adherence**: % of >EUR400 claims that bypassed manual/fraud review.  
3. **Stop/Go Thresholds**:  
   - **Go** if median fastpath cycletime 1h, complaint/reopen 6% for premium lowrisk, abandonment 20% for standard lowrisk.  
   - **Stop** if any segment’s abandonment >25% **or** compliance violations exceed 2% of flagged claims.  
4. **Rationale**: Targets the largest share of fastpath cases while preserving speed for premium lowrisk, isolates the digitalliteracy risk, and provides immediate data on manualreview impact.  

## Phase2: Pilot – **Redesign B (Hybrid DecisionTree with Escalation Triggers)**
1. **Scope**: Implement decisiontree at eligibility node for **all Premium and Standard Highrisk/Highvalue devices** (54% of cases). Keep current fastpath for lowvalue lowrisk.  
2. **Metrics to Monitor**:  
   - **Cycletime** breakdown for each branch (Fast lane vs Compliance lane).  
   - **Escalation board usage**: avg. cases per session, wait time before board review.  
   - **Compliance adherence**: % of >EUR400 claims processed through Fraud/risk review.  
   - **Customer satisfaction**: NPS for premium highvalue devices.  
3. **Stop/Go Thresholds**:  
   - **Go** if fastlane median 0.5h, compliancelane complaint/reopen 4%, escalation board sessions 90% capacity.  
   - **Stop** if escalation board avg. wait >30min or complaint/reopen for compliance lane >6%.  
4. **Sequencing Logic**: Run Phase1 concurrently for a month, then switch premium highvalue segment to Phase2 to avoid overlaps and capture comparative data.  

## Phase3: Pilot – **Redesign C (Dynamic Parallel Paths with AIAssisted Screening)**
1. **Scope**: Introduce AI screening for **all intake claims** (pilot across 5% of volume, expanding to 25% after success).  
2. **Metrics to Monitor**:  
   - **Prediction accuracy**: Truepositive/fastlane rate, falsenegative rate into compliance lane.  
   - **Cycletime variance**: Fastlane median hours vs baseline; compliance lane additional hours.  
   - **Quality improvement**: Rework/reopen rate across segments.  
   - **Resource utilization**: Manual reviewers postAI, AI model training cost.  
3. **Stop/Go Thresholds**:  
   - **Go** if AI fastlane median 0.5h, overall complaint/reopen 3%, manualreview volume 15% vs baseline, and model error <5%.  
   - **Stop** if AI misclassification >8% leading to compliance breaches or if manualreview surge >20% in any week.  
4. **Learning Approach**: Use **counterfactual analysis**—compare outcomes for claims flagged by AI vs those processed via existing rules—to verify that improvements are genuine rather than merely shifting downstream.  

### Overall Experiment Governance
- **Crossfunctional Steering Committee** (process owners, data science, customer experience) will review weekly dashboards.  
- **Rollback Plan**: Each pilot includes a documented “revert to current process” action if thresholds are breached.  
- **Data Integrity**: Ensure that evidence completeness, eligibility, and defect-type attributes are consistently logged for all experiments.
