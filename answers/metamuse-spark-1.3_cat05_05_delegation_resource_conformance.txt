## 2. Rule Interpretation

**RR1 Drafter/approver separation:** For same `case:concept:name`, `human(Draft_Change_Request) != human(Approve_Change)` after alias normalization. Applies to all risks. Technical check via four-eyes principle. Control objective: prevent self-approval.

**RR2 Implementer/verifier separation:** For same case, `human(Implement_Change) != human(Verify_Implementation)` after alias normalization and only after valid svc attribution. If verifier is `svc_qms` without reliable session-to-human map, RR2 is **indeterminate**, not passed. Control objective: independent verification.

**RR3 High-risk approval authority:** If `risk==HIGH`, `Approve_Change` human at event timestamp must be (a) QM standing role holder, or (b) delegate whose interval covers event timestamp (inclusive, UTC), whose scope covers case `scope`, whose principal-absence condition is satisfied, and with no prohibited subdelegation chain. Event-time role evaluated from role intervals, not current master. E.g., Eva on 2026-08-09 is not QM; Ben is never QM except via D-QM-01.

**RR4 Emergency substitution:** If `risk==HIGH` and `emergency==true` with credible flag/rationale, `Approve_Change` may instead be by IC or valid IC delegate (same interval/scope/no-subdelegation test). Then `Retrospective_QM_Review` by QM or valid QM delegate is mandatory **within two business days**. Counting: business days = Mon-Fri excluding site holidays; 2026-08-15/16 are weekend, no holiday in period. Approval day = Day 0. Example: approval Tue 2026-08-11  Day 1 Wed 08-12, Day 2 Thu 08-13. Review on Fri 08-14 is Day 3 = late. Review must also satisfy RR3 authority (scope + absence). Failure of timeliness or authority = RR4 failure; original IC approval does not cure it.

**Principal acting while on leave:** Leave does not remove standing authority. Asha on annual leave 08-10 to 08-14 remains QM; a proven personal act (e.g., CHG-504) is AUTHORIZED. However brief personal activity does not terminate leave status for others: D-QM-01 requires "while Asha is absent." A single VPN session handling "one urgent case" does not automatically invalidate all other D-QM-01 uses that day, but creates ambiguity that must be documented. If principal were formally recalled, delegation would suspend.

**Later database entry:** A later QMS database timestamp does **not** by itself prove or disprove earlier delegation. D-QM-01 signed PDF 2026-08-09 18:00 predates interval  valid despite DB creation 2026-08-12 09:15 during migration. Evidential weight follows signed, pre-registered record, not migration timestamp. Conversely, email-only claim (D-IC-04) with explicit prohibition carries no weight.

**RR5 Identity evidence:** Service account, shared workstation, badge alone, or IP alone are not proof of human identity. FIDO + badge + station together is stronger but still reviewed manually. Aliases must be normalized first.

**RR6 Suspicion vs proof:** 18/23 ClaraBen pairs, reciprocal LOW-risk approvals, shared WS-DM-04/WS-IV-02, concentrated handovers, sick-leave/leave conflicts are indicators only. No collusion finding without intent/benefit/coordination evidence.

## 4. Collusion and Alternative-Explanation Analysis

**Repeated pair Clara draft  Ben approve (18/23 HIGH prod in 60 days, plus CHG-501/503/506):**
- Benign: same production cell assignment, competence, shift overlap, no rotation policy; efficiency, not intent.
- Adverse: tacit quid-pro-quo, rubber-stamping, Ben approving without independent review.
- Discriminate: review quality (comments, rejections, review duration), rejection rate vs other approvers, whether drafts assigned randomly, time-to-approve distribution, independent technical re-review of sample.

**Reciprocal LOW-risk training approvals (Ben drafted 4, Clara approved):**
- Benign: small team cross-covering LOW admin work, no benefit.
- Adverse: logrolling — you approve mine, I approve yours — normalizing SoD laxity that spills to HIGH.
- Discriminate: whether reciprocal approvals were truly independent, timing proximity, whether HIGH approvals followed LOW reciprocity, financial/benefit trail (none known), interviews.

**Shared workstations (WS-DM-04 for CHG-503, WS-IV-02 for CHG-509, office workstation for CHG-506/508):**
- Benign: shared terminals in cleanroom/ops areas, generic svc_qms for equipment integration, forgotten logoff.
- Adverse: deliberate use of shared/generic sessions to obscure actor, allow implementer to verify or non-delegate to approve.
- Discriminate: per-event Windows/session logs, badge-to-login correlation, FIDO/MFA logs (present for 509, absent for 503), workstation-to-user binding policy, re-authentication enforcement test.

**Absence conflicts (Asha leave + VPN 08-13; Ben sick 08-13 12:00 onward but u_bk used 08-13 13:00 and 08-14 16:00):**
- Benign: Asha brief good-faith urgent handling; Ben briefly worked while sick or colleague used unlocked session without malice.
- Adverse: credential sharing/borrowing to maintain throughput while principals unavailable; false QM coverage.
- Discriminate: VPN device fingerprint + MFA challenge, office badge logs for Ben on 08-13/14, workstation CCTV, HR sick/leave records, helpdesk tickets.

**Credential compromise / aliasing (u_bk vs b.koch; u_as VPN; u_bk denial):**
- Benign: legacy dual IDs, migration artifacts, user confusion.
- Adverse: alias to evade four-eyes filters; shared password to fabricate authority.
- Discriminate: IAM alias registry history, password vault logs, concurrent sessions, IP geolocation (office vs home for Ben), user interviews under just culture.

No collusion declared: networks show concentration but do not prove agreement or benefit. All patterns require the discriminating evidence above.

## 6. Control Improvements

- **Delegation creation:** Require pre-registered signed record in controlled QMS before effective start; prohibit email-only delegations; system-block subdelegation where prohibited (as in D-IC-03); enforce scope picklist (production_equipment vs cooling_system vs training_document).
- **Effective-time recording:** Separate `signed_time`, `registered_time`, `effective_start/end` (UTC); migration must preserve original timestamps with audit trail; late DB creation (as 08-12 for D-QM-01) flagged but not treated as creation.
- **Principal availability:** Integrate HR leave/sick + on-call roster with QMS; delegation valid only if principal status = absent/unavailable at event timestamp; brief VPN activity requires formal recall workflow — otherwise remains absent but logged as exception; delegate sick leave suspends delegate authority and triggers re-assignment.
- **Alias management:** Single IAM identity per human; link legacy IDs (`b.koch``u_bk`) in master; SoD engine must run on normalized human, not login string; periodic alias audit.
- **Individual authentication:** Prohibit generic `svc_qms` for Verify/Approve without FIDO2 re-authentication; enforce unique login on shared stations (WS-DM-04, WS-IV-02) with auto-logoff; retain badge+FIDO+session binding; CCTV or witness where shared terminal unavoidable.
- **Emergency review:** Automated 2-business-day clock (Mon-Fri, site holidays) from IC approval; scope-aware reviewer assignment (cooling  prod-equip); escalation on miss; require safety rationale attachment for emergency flag credibility.
- **Pair rotation:** Cap consecutive same draftapprove pairs; rotate HIGH-risk approvers across cells; require review comments/duration; monitor 18/23-type concentration via handover/working-together dashboards as early warning, not accusation.
- **Network monitoring:** Routine handover/working-together analysis with thresholds for reciprocal approvals and concentrated handovers; human review of outliers with quality sampling.
- **Evidential retention:** Immutable logs for VPN/MFA/IP/badge/session/CCTV, delegation PDFs, absence records; retain  GxP period; make absence, attribution, and scope evidence retrievable per case for inspection.

## 7. Executive Conclusion

About ten cases show a mix, not a single failure mode: **~30% genuine authority failure** (CHG-507 non-QM approval, CHG-510 prohibited subdelegation + self-approval, CHG-502/508 SoD self-approvals, CHG-505/510 out-of-scope retrospectives + late review), **~30% identity/data quality** (CHG-503 unattributable svc verification, CHG-506 sick-leave denial, CHG-508 alias masking, CHG-504 leave-vs-VPN attribution), and **~40% organizational-pattern risk** (ClaraBen concentration, reciprocal LOW approvals, shared stations, leave coverage gaps) that is suspicious but not proof of collusion. Valid substitutions exist (CHG-501, and conditionally CHG-509) where interval/scope/absence align.

Uncertainty is highest where human-to-account mapping is missing (503, 506) and where absence on weekend/leave-boundary determines validity (509, and spillover to 506). Technical SoD pass does not equal control effectiveness when approver = verifier (509) or scope is wrong (505/510).
