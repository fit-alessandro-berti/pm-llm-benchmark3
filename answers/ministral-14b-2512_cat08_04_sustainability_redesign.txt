# **1. System Boundary and Assumptions**
### **Scope Inclusion (Inside Boundary)**
- **Process scope**: All events from *Receive order* to *Close* (or *Cancel*), including:
  - Order segmentation (Standard-stock, Made-to-order, Critical-downtime; Regular/Service-contract).
  - Production paths (CNC_A/B, heat treatment, inspection, rework loops).
  - Transport modes (ground, air, dedicated van, consolidated truck) and packaging choices.
  - Batching decisions (batch size, wait times, obsolescence).
  - Rework and scrap generation (material, energy, and transport impacts).
  - Partial shipments, backorders, and cancellations.
- **Environmental metrics**:
  - Energy use (`energy_kwh`) per order/line item.
  - Transport emissions (`transport_kgco2e`) per order.
  - Material waste (`material_scrap_kg`, `package_material_kg`).
  - Indirect impacts: waiting times (obsolescence risk), rework loops (hidden material/energy), and packaging rework.
- **Service/quality/resilience metrics**:
  - Cycle time (median/p90), late delivery rates, rework/return rates.
  - Resilience: availability of alternative routes (e.g., ground vs. air for Service-contract).

### **Scope Exclusion (Outside Boundary)**
- **Upstream**: Raw material extraction, supplier logistics (beyond *Receive order*).
- **Downstream**: Customer use phase, end-of-life disposal (assumed negligible for spare parts).
- **Indirect rebound effects**: Customer behavior changes (e.g., ordering more due to "greener" service) not explicitly modeled in logs.
- **Macro-level displacement**: Shift in demand to other distributors or regions (not observable in event logs).

### **Key Assumptions**
1. **Data fidelity**: Event logs accurately reflect process paths and attributes (e.g., `batch_size` is recorded correctly).
2. **Trade-off transparency**: All environmental impacts (e.g., energy vs. transport) are observable via attributes.
3. **Policy adherence**: Current violations of SP1/SP2/Q1 are correctable via process changes (not systemic).
4. **No external shocks**: Carrier disruptions or supply chain crises are managed via existing resilience policies (R1).
5. **Customer segmentation stability**: Regular vs. Service-contract priorities are fixed (no behavioral shifts post-intervention).

# **3. Intervention Options**
### **S1. Green Shipping Recommender**
- **Target**: Standard-stock Service-contract orders (16% share) violating SP1 (22% of cases).
- **Mechanism**:
  - Replace air shipments with **priority pick + ground** when:
    - Promised lead time slack  24h (based on `promised_lead_time_hours`).
    - Carrier reliability data shows ground p90  promised lead time (85% confidence).
  - Exceptions: Critical-downtime or when customer explicitly requests air.
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - Reduce `transport_kgco2e` by ~90% (air  ground: 18.5  3.6 kgCO2e).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: +12h median (air: 17h  ground: 29h), but p90 may exceed promised time if slack <24h.
  - **Late delivery**: Risk of +3–5% if slack miscalculated (currently 5% late).
  - **Resilience**: Ground routes are less vulnerable to air carrier disruptions.
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - Track `route_mode` vs. `promised_lead_time_hours` compliance with SP1.
  - Late delivery rate by `customer_type` post-intervention.

### **S2. Dynamic Batching with Waiting-Time Caps**
- **Target**: Made-to-order orders on CNC_A/B and heat treatment (37% share).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **CNC_A**: Batch size capped at 12 items; max wait = 36h (current median wait at size 12–20 is 38h).
  - **CNC_B**: Shift 30% of MTO volume to CNC_A if queue <24h and due-date slack 48h (CNC_B has 18% rework vs. 9%).
  - **Heat treatment**: Batch size 10 only if batch wait <24h (current 1.7x late-delivery risk).
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - Energy per item: CNC_A (1.3 kWh) vs. CNC_B (higher rework  hidden energy).
  - Scrap: Reduce by 20% via CNC_A shift (rework drops from 18% to 12%).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: +4h median for CNC_A (batch wait cap), but faster for CNC_B  CNC_A shifts.
  - **Late delivery**: Risk of +2–4% if heat-treatment batches exceed wait cap.
  - **Resilience**: CNC_A is less prone to rework loops  fewer hidden delays.
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - `batch_size` vs. `batch_wait` distribution by `machine_group`.
  - Rework rate (`rework_flag`) for CNC_B vs. CNC_A post-shift.

### **S3. Packaging-Route Compatibility Checks**
- **Target**: All orders with `rework_packaging` flag (260 + 360 = 620 cases, 5% share).
- **Mechanism**:
  - Pre-shipment validation: Ensure `package_type` (standard/reinforced) matches:
    - `route_mode` (e.g., reinforced for air/dedicated van to prevent damage).
    - `part_weight` (Q1: >8 kg  reinforced unless inspected).
  - Automate checks in *Package* step; flag exceptions for manual review.
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - Reduce `package_material_kg` by 30% (eliminate mismatched reinforced packages).
  - Cut rework loops by 40% (current 260/620 = 42% reworked packaging).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: -2h (no rework loops).
  - **Late delivery**: -1% (fewer backorders from damaged goods).
  - **Resilience**: Fewer returns  lower risk of stockouts for replacements.
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - `rework_packaging` rate by `package_type`/`route_mode` combo.
  - Return rate for heavy parts (>8 kg) post-intervention.

### **S4. Consolidated Truck for Non-Urgent Stock**
- **Target**: Standard-stock Regular orders (39% share) shipped via ground/air.
- **Mechanism**:
  - Route 60% of Standard-stock Regular orders to **consolidated truck** (current 850/12k = 7%).
  - Departures: Twice daily (morning/evening) with 12–24h consolidation wait.
  - Exclude Service-contract and Critical-downtime.
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - `transport_kgco2e`: 4.2  2.5 kgCO2e (35% reduction).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: +18h median (ground: 31h  consolidated: 49h).
  - **Late delivery**: Risk of +5% if consolidation delays exceed slack.
  - **Resilience**: Less vulnerable to air/ground carrier strikes.
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - `route_mode` adoption rate for Standard-stock Regular.
  - Late delivery rate by `customer_type` post-consolidation.

### **S5. Inspection Quality Gate for High-Rework Combinations**
- **Target**: Made-to-order orders on CNC_B (11% rework rate) and inspection_cell loops.
- **Mechanism**:
  - Add **mandatory secondary inspection** for:
    - CNC_B-produced parts.
    - Parts with >3% historical rework rate.
  - Route to dedicated inspection lane with 2h wait (vs. current 1h rushed inspection).
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - Scrap: -30% (rework drops from 18% to 12%).
  - Energy: -5% (fewer rework loops).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: +2h (inspection wait).
  - **Late delivery**: -3% (fewer rework-induced delays).
  - **Resilience**: Higher quality  fewer customer returns.
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - Rework rate by `machine_group` post-gate.
  - Inspection_cell queue times.

### **S6. Carbon-Aware Promise-Date Negotiation**
- **Target**: Made-to-order Regular customers (22% share).
- **Mechanism**:
  - Offer **extended lead times** (e.g., +24h) for ground transport in exchange for:
    - 10% discount on transport emissions (e.g., ground vs. air).
    - Dynamic `promised_lead_time_hours` based on:
      - Current `route_mode` emissions.
      - Carrier reliability data.
  - Exclude Service-contract (resilience policy R1).
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - `transport_kgco2e`: -20% (air  ground for 30% of MTO Regular).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: +12h for 30% of orders.
  - **Late delivery**: Risk of +2% if promises are too tight.
  - **Resilience**: No impact (Regular customers have no SLAs).
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - Adoption rate of extended lead times.
  - Late delivery rate for Regular MTO.

### **S7. Split Critical-Downtime Segments**
- **Target**: Critical-downtime Service-contract orders (11% share).
- **Mechanism**:
  - **True downtime**: Machine failure with <4h window (current 8% share).
    - Use dedicated van (52 kgCO2e) only if no alternative.
  - **Precautionary urgent**: Forecasted downtime with >12h slack.
    - Default to consolidated truck (4.8 kgCO2e) unless customer insists.
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - `transport_kgco2e`: -40% (reduce dedicated van use by 50%).
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: +6h for precautionary urgent (truck vs. van).
  - **Late delivery**: -1% (fewer rushed shipments).
  - **Resilience**: Dedicated vans reserved for true emergencies.
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - `route_mode` split by downtime type.
  - Customer complaints for precautionary urgent delays.

### **S8. Shared Emergency Courier Lanes**
- **Target**: Critical-downtime orders (8% share) and high-priority MTO.
- **Mechanism**:
  - Replace 30% of dedicated vans with **shared courier lanes** (e.g., UPS/FedEx priority lanes).
  - Require explicit justification for dedicated vans (enforce SP2).
  - Shared lanes: 30 kgCO2e (vs. 52 kgCO2e for dedicated van).
- **Environmental Improvement**:
  - `transport_kgco2e`: -15% for Critical-downtime.
- **Service/Quality/Resilience Effects**:
  - **Cycle time**: +3h (shared lanes may have delays).
  - **Late delivery**: Risk of +2% if shared lanes are delayed.
  - **Resilience**: More flexible than dedicated vans.
- **Monitoring Evidence**:
  - SP2 compliance (justification events).
  - On-time delivery rate for shared vs. dedicated lanes.

# **5. Rebound and Displacement Risks**
1. **Batching  Obsolescence Trade-off**
   - *Risk*: Dynamic batching (S2) reduces energy but increases batch wait times, raising obsolescence for Made-to-order (current 6% for batches >12).
   - *Mitigation*: Cap batch size at 12 with 36h wait cap; monitor `batch_size` vs. `promised_lead_time_hours`.

2. **Consolidated Truck  Service Deterioration**
   - *Risk*: S4 increases cycle time by 18h for Standard-stock Regular, risking late deliveries if slack is miscalculated.
   - *Mitigation*: Restrict to orders with 24h slack; track late delivery rate by `customer_type`.

3. **Green Shipping  Customer Behavior Shift**
   - *Risk*: S1 may push Service-contract customers to upgrade to "express" contracts to avoid delays.
   - *Mitigation*: Offer priority pick + ground as default; monitor `customer_type` shifts.

4. **Inspection Gate  Bottlenecks**
   - *Risk*: S5 adds 2h to inspection, potentially delaying all Made-to-order orders.
   - *Mitigation*: Dedicate a secondary inspection lane; monitor `inspection_cell` queue times.

5. **Packaging Compatibility  Hidden Costs**
   - *Risk*: S3 may increase `package_material_kg` if reinforced packaging is overused due to misclassified parts.
   - *Mitigation*: Automate compatibility checks with `part_weight`/`route_mode` rules.

6. **Shared Courier Lanes  Resilience Gaps**
   - *Risk*: S8 reduces dedicated van use but may create single points of failure if shared lanes are disrupted.
   - *Mitigation*: Maintain 20% dedicated van capacity for true emergencies.

7. **Carbon-Aware Promises  Order Inflation**
   - *Risk*: S6 may incentivize Regular customers to order more to meet extended lead times.
   - *Mitigation*: Limit to orders with existing slack; monitor order volume trends.

8. **Critical-Downtime Splitting  Misclassification**
   - *Risk*: S7 may misclassify "precautionary urgent" as non-urgent, causing actual downtime delays.
   - *Mitigation*: Require explicit justification for precautionary urgent; audit `route_mode` choices.

# **6. Balanced Recommendation**
### **Redesign Package: "Precision Sustainability for Spare Parts"**
**Core Principles**:
1. **Segmentation**: Tailor interventions to `case_type`/`customer_type` to avoid one-size-fits-all.
2. **Trade-off Acceptance**: Allow minor service trade-offs (e.g., +5% late delivery) if environmental gains are >20% and resilience is preserved.
3. **Hidden Impact Focus**: Prioritize rework, obsolescence, and packaging waste over direct energy/transport.

**Recommended Interventions** (Prioritized):
| **Intervention**               | **Why Included**                                                                 |
|--------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **S1. Green Shipping Recommender** | Highest transport impact (airground) with minimal service risk for Regular customers. |
| **S3. Packaging Compatibility**  | Eliminates rework loops and material waste with no cycle-time penalty.          |
| **S2. Dynamic Batching**        | Reduces energy and scrap but requires careful wait-time management.             |
| **S5. Inspection Quality Gate**  | Lowers rework and scrap without significant cycle-time impact.                   |
| **S7. Split Critical-Downtime**  | Targets high-emission dedicated vans while protecting true emergencies.         |
| **S4. Consolidated Truck**      | Limited to Standard-stock Regular with slack; monitored for late-delivery risk. |

**Excluded Interventions**:
- **S6/S8**: Higher resilience risks or limited environmental gains.
- **S10/S12**: Complex to implement without clear segment-specific benefits.

**Implementation Sequence**:
1. **Short-term (0–3 months)**:
   - Deploy S1 (green shipping recommender) and S3 (packaging checks).
   - Audit SP1/SP2 compliance; enforce justification for dedicated vans.
2. **Medium-term (3–6 months)**:
   - Roll out S2 (dynamic batching) with wait-time caps.
   - Pilot S5 (inspection gate) on CNC_B.
3. **Long-term (6–12 months)**:
   - Expand S4 (consolidated truck) to more Standard-stock Regular orders.
   - Refine S7 with customer feedback on downtime segmentation.

**Expected Outcomes**:
- **Transport**: -30% kgCO2e (airground shift + consolidated truck).
- **Energy**: -10% (dynamic batching + CNC_A shift).
- **Materials**: -25% (scrap reduction from rework and packaging fixes).
- **Service**: +3–5% late delivery (acceptable for Regular customers; Service-contract protected).
- **Resilience**: Improved via packaging fixes and dedicated van discipline.
