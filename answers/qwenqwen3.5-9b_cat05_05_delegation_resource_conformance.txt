## 2. Rule Interpretation

**RR1 (Drafter/Approver):** Violation occurs if `Draft_Change_Request` and `Approve_Change` are performed by the same human identity for the same case. Applies universally (risk-agnostic).

**RR2 (Implementer/Verifier):** Violation occurs if `Implement_Change` and `Verify_Implementation` are performed by the same human identity. Applies universally.

**RR3 (High-Risk Approval):** Requires QM or valid QM delegate. Valid delegation requires:
1.  Effective interval covers event timestamp.
2.  Scope covers change type (e.g., production equipment vs. training).
3.  Principal (Asha) is absent/unavailable.
4.  No prohibited subdelegation.
*Counting Business Days:* Standard calendar (Mon-Fri). Weekends (Aug 15-16) are non-working days for "within two business days" calculations.
*Principal on Leave:* If Principal is on leave, delegation is valid. If Principal logs in (VPN session), delegation is superseded by standing authority.
*Database Entry:* A delegation record created *after* an event (e.g., D-QM-01 DB record Aug 12 for Aug 10-14) does not prove validity if the signed evidence (PDF) dates prior to the event. Validity relies on evidence at event time.

**RR4 (Emergency):**
*   **Approval:** IC or valid IC delegate may approve.
*   **Review:** QM or valid QM delegate must perform `Retrospective_QM_Review` within 2 business days.
*   **Condition:** Emergency flag must be credible.
*   **Availability:** Delegate must be available to perform review.

**RR5 (Identity):** Service accounts cannot satisfy role requirements unless session evidence maps to an authorized human.

**RR6 (Suspicion):** Patterns (e.g., Clara/Ben pairing) are indicators, not proof.
