# 1. Delay-decomposition definitions
We decompose each case’s flow time into non-overlapping components using the following definitions (for branch \(i \in \{A,B,C\}\) and case \(c\)):

- **Upstream elapsed**: \( \text{upstream\_elapsed}_c = \text{fork\_release}_c - \text{arrival}_c \)
  *Measures time from case entry to fork release.*

- **Branch queue delay**: \( \text{queue}_c^i = \max(0, \text{start}_c^i - \text{fork\_release}_c) \)
  *Waits in the branch queue after fork release.*

- **Branch service time**: \( \text{service}_c^i = \text{end}_c^i - \text{start}_c^i \)
  *Active work duration for branch \(i\).*

- **Branch synchronization wait**: \( \text{sync\_wait}_c^i = \text{join\_ready}_c - \text{end}_c^i \)
  *Time spent waiting for the final join after branch completion. Note that summing all \( \text{sync\_wait}_c^i \) would double-count the overlap with downstream queueing, as \( \text{join\_ready}_c \) is the latest branch completion time.*

- **Fork-join stage time**: \( \text{join\_ready}_c - \text{fork\_release}_c \)
  *Time from fork to when all branches are ready to join.*

- **Downstream queueing**: \( \text{final\_start}_c - \text{join\_ready}_c \)
  *Waiting at the final station before resource assignment.*

- **Final service**: \( \text{final\_end}_c - \text{final\_start}_c \)
  *Resource utilization at the final station.*

- **Total flow time**: \( \text{final\_end}_c - \text{arrival}_c \)
  *Sum of all decomposed components.*

# 7. Limits of inference
**Unidentified factors**:
- **Causality**: Cannot infer direct cause-effect between branch delays and final outcomes.
- **Branch resource capacity**: Service times may reflect capacity constraints not captured in timestamps.
- **Work preemption**: Branches may have been interrupted or reprioritized.
- **Final-station waiting**: Queueing may be due to release policy (e.g., F1/F2 assignment rules) rather than pure queueing.
- **Parallelism assumptions**: Branches may have overlapping service times not logged as events.
