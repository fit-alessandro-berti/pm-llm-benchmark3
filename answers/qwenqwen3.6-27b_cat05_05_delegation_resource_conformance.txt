- **RR4 (Emergency substitution & retrospective review):** IC or valid IC delegate may approve/implement emergency HIGH-risk changes. Retrospective_QM_Review must occur within **two business days** of the emergency action. Business days exclude weekends (Sat/Sun) and site holidays. The deadline is end-of-day on the second weekday.

- **RR6 (Suspicion vs proof):** Repeated pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, and concentration of handovers are treated as risk indicators, not violations. Classification requires corroborating evidence (e.g., credential logs, rotation policy breaches, financial/operational impact).

**Principal acting while on leave:** Permitted if the principal retains standing authority and the action is documented. However, it triggers identity/availability scrutiny and invalidates any delegation condition that requires the principal to be absent.

## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation Creation & Effective-Time Recording:** Enforce system-generated timestamps that match the signing event. Block backdating or late database ingestion. Require dual attestation (principal + delegate) for delegation activation.
2. **Principal Availability Integration:** Sync HR/leave systems with the QMS in real-time. Auto-flag or block delegation activation if principal absence condition is unmet. Require explicit "unavailable" status for emergency overrides.
3. **Alias & Identity Management:** Implement a centralized identity provider (IdP) with single canonical IDs. Deprecate legacy aliases. Enforce strict mapping before any SoD or authority evaluation.
4. **Individual Authentication & Session Binding:** Mandate MFA and device binding for all QM/IC actions. Prohibit shared service accounts for critical steps. Enforce session timeout and re-authentication on workstation handovers.
5. **Emergency Review SLA Tracking:** Integrate a business-day calendar into the QMS. Auto-alert when Retrospective_QM_Review exceeds 2 business days. Block case closure until review is logged by authorized QM/delegate.
6. **Pair Rotation & Concentration Limits:** Define maximum consecutive approvals per drafter/approver pair. System should trigger mandatory rotation or escalation when thresholds are breached.
7. **Network Monitoring & Anomaly Detection:** Deploy continuous handover/working-together network analysis. Flag edges exceeding statistical baselines for manual review. Integrate with SIEM for credential/session correlation.
8. **Evidential Retention:** Retain immutable audit trails, session recordings, FIDO/badge logs, and CCTV for a minimum of 12 months. Ensure logs are tamper-evident and accessible for forensic reconstruction.
