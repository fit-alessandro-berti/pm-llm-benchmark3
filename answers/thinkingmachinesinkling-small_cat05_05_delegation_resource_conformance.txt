## 2. Rule Interpretation

**RR1 — Drafter/approver separation:** For each `case:concept:name`, after alias normalization, `resource(Draft_Change_Request) != resource(Approve_Change)`. Applies to all risk levels; no exception.

**RR2 — Implementer/verifier separation:** `resource(Implement_Change) != resource(Verify_Implementation)` for the same case.

**RR3 — High-risk approval authority:** If `risk == HIGH`, the resource performing `Approve_Change` must hold the QM role or a valid QM delegation at the event timestamp. A delegation is **valid** only if cumulatively:
1. `delegation.start <= t <= delegation.end`;
2. `delegation.scope` covers `scope` of the case;
3. The delegation conditions require the principal to be absent/formally unavailable, and that condition is satisfied by formal evidence at `t`;
4. No prohibited subdelegation occurred (the delegate acts directly, not via a further chain).

A database entry created after the interval (e.g., D-QM-01 QMS record at 2026-08-12 09:15) **cannot prove** the delegation existed earlier; it is evidence of record migration, not of the signed authorization (PDF 2026-08-09 18:00).

**RR4 — Emergency substitution:** If `risk == HIGH` and `emergency == true`:
- Implementation may follow approval by the Incident Commander or a valid IC delegate (not necessarily QM).
- A QM or valid QM delegate **must** perform `Retrospective_QM_Review`.
- **Business-day count:** Exclude weekends (Sat/Sun) and site holidays. The site calendar shows no holiday; therefore 2026-08-15 (Sat) and 2026-08-16 (Sun) are not business days. If the implementation event is at business-day `D`, the two business days are `D+1` and `D+2`; the review must occur by `23:59` on `D+2`. For CHG-505 (implemented 08-11 Thu? Wait: 08-11 is Tue in 2026? Actually 2026-08-11 is a Tuesday; 08-12 Wed; 08-13 Thu; 08-14 Fri). Thus implementation Tuesday 08-11  deadline Thursday 08-13 end-of-day. Review at Friday 08-14 16:00 is **late**.
- Justified only when `emergency == true` and the safety rationale is credible; absence of documented rationale weakens justification.

**RR5 — Identity evidence:** 
- Alias normalization is mandatory before SoD.
- `svc_qms` is not a human; its use for approval/verification/retrospective is nonconforming unless reliable session evidence maps to an authorized human who satisfies role requirements at `t`. The mapping is **manual**, not automatic.

**RR6 — Suspicion versus proof:** Repeated resource pairs, unusual reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, or concentrated handovers are **indicators** only. They are not proof of collusion without corroborating evidence (financial gain, content analysis, admission, physical co-location during the event).

**Principal on leave / credential use:** If the principal is recorded on leave and a delegation requires absence, the absence condition is satisfied for the interval of leave. If the principal’s account is used during that leave (Asha VPN 08-13; Ben account 08-13 13:00+), this creates an **identity/availability conflict**: either the principal acted (potentially undermining the need for delegation, but not necessarily invalidating their own QM authority) or the credential was shared/compromised (potential unauthorized access). It does **not** automatically invalidate all actions taken by delegates, but it requires investigation.

## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation creation and recording:** Require *signed PDF timestamp before effective start*, not post-migration DB records. Store delegation metadata in an immutable audit ledger, not only a relational DB vulnerable to migration errors.
2. **Effective-time recording:** Enforce system-time stamps (NTP-synced) for delegation creation, and prohibit backdated activation without dual-authorization.
3. **Principal availability:** Integrate HR leave/calendar APIs with QMS access control; automatically suspend delegation activation (or require secondary approval) when the principal’s account is active during recorded leave.
4. **Alias management:** Enforce a master identity registry (`u_bk` only); reject `b.koch` at data entry or map automatically; conduct periodic alias audits.
5. **Individual authentication:** Prohibit approval/retrospective actions from shared workstations without individual FIDO/token re-authentication; disable `svc_qms` for approval/verification unless explicitly mapped to a named human in the session trail.
6. **Emergency review:** Implement an automated 2-business-day calendar reminder for `Retrospective_QM_Review` tied to `emergency == true`; block case closure until retrospective is completed.
7. **Pair rotation:** Introduce a formal policy that no drafter/approver pair may exceed a defined frequency (e.g., 3 consecutive HIGH-risk changes) without rotation; alert when Clara/Ben pairing exceeds threshold.
8. **Network monitoring:** Export `discover_handover_of_work_network` and `discover_working_together_network` weekly; flag edges whose weight exceeds organizational baselines; do not interpret high frequency as proof, but trigger content audit.
9. **Evidence retention:** Retain CCTV, session video, device fingerprinting, and IP logs for all approval/retrospective events; make workstation assignment and shared-terminal policies explicit.
10. **Role master synchronization:** Ensure event-time roles (e.g., Eva as Independent Verifier until 08-11 23:59) are not overwritten by later master updates; use temporal database practices (`valid_from`/`valid_to`) for role and delegation records.
