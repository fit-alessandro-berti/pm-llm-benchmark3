## 1. Interpretive Assumptions and Decision Method
**Facts taken as given:** Effective dates, windows, thresholds, amounts, supplier status, timestamps and evidence notes, authority statements, and organizational facts in the prompt. No additional facts assumed.

**Assumptions – separated from facts:**
A. Authority is issue-specific, not monolithic. Board-approved GPIP-4 is presumptively supreme on integrity controls, but does not automatically defeat a more specific operational rule on a point it expressly leaves to local definition.
B. Later-in-time does not automatically win. A later source wins only within its delegated competence, scope, and formal validity.
C. Specificity wins within competence: e.g., SOP on PO timing, ECD on supplier/timing within its window/scope, CFO on meaning of Finance controls.
D. Emergency necessity is not a free-standing waiver. It excuses only what its authorizing source expressly or by necessary implication allows. A4, B4-B5, C1-C4, D1-D2 are read narrowly against the drafter.
E. Timing is judged at the time of the relevant act – especially Commit_to_Supplier. Later cures mitigate but do not retroactively create pre-commitment compliance per A6.
F. Evidential hierarchy: contemporaneous primary evidence – signed PDF, telephone recording, supplier acknowledgment, access log – outweighs ERP timestamps when outage ON-44 or clock-skew is shown. ERP delay up to 12 min on 07-14 08:30-13:00, and 9-min fast clock on P-505, are facts affecting weight, not normative exceptions.
G. Burden of proof: party invoking exception/scope/authority must prove its elements – valid delegation, IC membership, critical-lot scope, written CRO exception, timely recording, pre-commitment approval. Ambiguity benefits neither side; unresolved decisive element yields INDETERMINATE or failed exception.
H. Classifications:
- COMPLIANT = no material breach of applicable rules at time of act, including through a valid override that authorized the conduct in advance.
- JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION = material deviation from baseline occurred, but a competent, scoped, timely emergency authority credibly covers it and core integrity purpose was preserved; or technical/logging defect without integrity risk.
- NONCOMPLIANT = material breach without valid advance authority; late approval alone does not cure.
- INDETERMINATE = decisive fact cannot be resolved on available evidence even under best interpretation.
I. Proportionality and purpose: segregation, Compliance for new/large suppliers, and dual finance control protect against fraud/conflict; PO sequencing and forms protect auditability. Failures in the former weigh heavier than the latter.

## 5. Strongest Alternative Interpretations

Credible because safety/production purpose favored action and 38k/approved-supplier is low integrity risk. Weak because B4 expressly conditions substitution on absence + active delegation, manager was reachable, and B5 preserves Finance; A6 rejects auto-cure.

**P-502  JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION (moderate).** Premise change: treat ECD C4 “precedence over ordinary timing and supplier-onboarding” as including timing of Finance and new-supplier Compliance when dual IC auth + same-day Finance/Compliance obtained. Credible because filters were same-day critical and onboarding delay would defeat containment; C2 expressly contemplates unapproved use. Weak because C5 silence, A4 tendering-only rule, A7 CRO exclusivity, and organizational fact that directives were never accepted for Compliance clearance point the other way; missing Plant remains uncovered and D postdates act.

**P-505  NONCOMPLIANT (moderate-strong).** Premise change: hold ERP 18:06 conclusive or hold clock-skew unproven, so commitment fell after 18:00 expiry; then ECD/CFO cover evaporates and Finance 19:00 + missing Plant are uncured breaches. Alternatively, even if pre-expiry, strictly construe CFO email as unable to waive Plant and unable to deem Finance satisfied without a separate Finance actor. Credible because expiry must be strict to prevent normalization and D1 “all operational approvals” exceeds CFO’s stated competence. Weak only because primary evidence (ack 17:59:31 + 9-min fast clock) strongly favors pre-expiry commit; would require disbelieving telecom/ack.

**P-506  INDETERMINATE (weak).** Premise change: shift burden to accept buyer’s verbal-extension claim as possibly true pending investigation, making ECD applicability undecided rather than failed. Credible only as interim investigatory stance – verbal IC decisions occurred elsewhere. Weak as compliance conclusion because exceptions require writing, scope, and timely dual-auth record; none exists, so even if conversation occurred it cannot authorize 51k PO-after-commit + late Plant.

**P-503  IN SCOPE + JUSTIFIED (weak).** Premise change: accept Operations reading that backup-line contingency = critical, and treat PROC-3 as legitimate IC member so dual-auth technically satisfied, with D1 covering Finance. **P-507  NONCOMPLIANT (moderate).** Premise change: insist Plant_Approve is substantive budget control not “ordinary workflow” and CFO cannot waive Plant; plus treat ERP 14:30 as the record, making C3 recording late. **P-508  NONCOMPLIANT (moderate).** Premise change: strictly require separate Finance + Plant even with IC+Legal, since ECD never mentions Finance and D is non-controlled email, and IC members are not budget owners. ## 6. Evidential Sufficiency and Remediation
Distinguish four states:
- **Policy breach:** missing advance authority/scope – P-503 (self-auth + no Compliance), P-504 (no scope/auth), P-502 (late Compliance + no Plant), P-506 (expired authority), P-501 (undelegated commit + late approvals).
- **Logging defect:** act timely but system timestamp late – P-507 ERP 14:30 vs PDF 12:40; P-505 ERP 18:06 vs ack 17:59.
- **Late evidence of timely act:** PDF queued in outage (P-507), telephone/ack proving pre-expiry commit (P-505), verbal auth recorded 12:55 within 4h (P-502) – timely act proved despite later system entry.
- **Inability to prove compliance:** P-506 verbal extension, P-503 IC membership/budget-owner status, P-505 missing PO time, all cases missing explicit budget-owner mapping and B6 forms where noted.

**Immediate case-level remediation:**
- P-501: file delegation gap review; coaching on B4; retain form; no re-approval needed but log as noncompliant timing for training. Confirm approver independence.
- P-502: retain late Compliance/Finance as mitigation; obtain retrospective Plant/budget-owner review noting non-curative status; add new supplier to register + enhanced monitoring; close gap on Plant accountability.
- P-503: escalate for independent review – self-authorization + 140k without Compliance; consider voidability/secondary review of pump need/pricing; require retrospective Compliance + Finance + Plant assessments; restrict PROC-3 from authorizing own requests; do not normalize backup-stock labeling.
- P-504: reverse or regularize cafeteria purchase if possible; require Compliance assessment if supplier retained; counsel SHIFT-2; flag misuse of “site continuity.”
- P-505: preserve call recording, ack email, clock-skew analysis, Compliance and IC auth records; file missing PO; obtain confirmatory Plant/budget-owner note for completeness.
- P-506: treat as unauthorized until written evidence produced; obtain full retrospective Plant/budget-owner + Compliance-equivalent review; quarantine further “extended ECD” claims without writing; supplier monitoring.
- P-507: file PDF 12:40 as official C3 record with outage ticket; file B6 form if missing; obtain Plant/budget-owner confirmatory note; close as justified with monitoring of seal supplier qualification.
- P-508: file IC+Legal as Finance-equivalent per CFO with explicit rationale; file B6 form; qualify or exit unapproved pump supplier; enhanced performance monitoring given sole-source.

**Monitoring:** 100% pre-commit Compliance check for >50k/new suppliers for 90 days; daily IC-auth 4h-record audit during any future ECD; ERP vs primary-evidence reconciliation; segregation exception report (requester=committer=authorizer); expiry-boundary alert 60 min; delegation-log completeness.

## 7. Recommended Policy Clarification
**Draft replacement hierarchy/exception clause for GPIP-4 + SOP + directives:**

1. **Order by issue:** (a) Integrity – segregation, Compliance for new/>100k – GPIP-4 board only, waivable only by written, scoped, time-limited CRO exception; (b) Finance substance/meaning – GPIP-4, interpretable only by CFO in controlled repository; (c) Operational sequence/supplier register/Plant workflow – local SOP, temporarily overlayable by incident directive.
2. **Incident directive validity:** must be written, numbered, time-windowed (start/end to minute + timezone), scope-defined by objective criteria (e.g., line ID + need-within-X-hours), jointly signed at or above COO + Incident Commander, published in controlled repository before reliance; verbal extensions invalid.
3. **Thresholds preserved:** ECD/CFO never raise EUR 50k/100k/25k thresholds and never waive segregation or pre-commit Compliance; CFO may deem joint IC (+Legal >150k) as satisfying Finance operational approval only, documented pre-commit, with essential integrity controls expressly reserved.
4. **Segregation & new suppliers:** dual IC authorization must be by members independent of requester/committer; self-authorization void; new/unapproved use requires pre-commit Compliance unless CRO exception states otherwise; post-commit Compliance mitigates only.
5. **Retrospective records:** verbal IC auth and outage-delayed entries valid only if recorded with primary evidence (recording/PDF/ack/log) within 4h (IC) / 24h (emergency form); ERP timestamp alone not conclusive during declared outage/clock incident; late approval noted as non-curative.
6. **Expiry:** controls revert automatically at expiry minute; acts straddling expiry judged by proven commitment moment via primary evidence; post-expiry Finance/Plant are new approvals, not cures.
7. **Safeguard against normalization:** every ECD use reported to Risk/Compliance within 48h; repeat or backup-stock use triggers CRO review; any directive purporting to waive segregation/Compliance without CRO signature is ultra vires and must be escalated, not followed; audit to report normalization patterns quarterly.
