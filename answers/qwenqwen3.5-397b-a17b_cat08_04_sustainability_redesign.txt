# 1. System boundary and assumptions

**Environmental Scope**
The assessment adopts a **gate-to-customer** boundary extended slightly upstream to include **internal production energy and material waste**.
*   **Inside Boundary:** Energy consumption (`energy_kwh`) during picking, production (CNC, heat treat), and inspection; Material waste (`material_scrap_kg`) generated during production and rework; Packaging material (`package_material_kg`); Transport emissions (`transport_kgco2e`) from shipment to customer confirmation.
*   **Outside Boundary:** Raw material extraction upstream of the facility (embodied carbon of raw stock is excluded unless scrapped internally); Customer use phase and end-of-life disposal; Administrative overhead emissions (office energy).

**Assumptions**
*   **Energy-Carbon Correlation:** `energy_kwh` is assumed to correlate linearly with Scope 2 emissions, assuming a static grid carbon intensity.
*   **Scrap Value:** `material_scrap_kg` represents total loss of embodied energy and material value; rework loops multiply this impact.
*   **Transport Data:** `transport_kgco2e` attributes are assumed accurate per shipment mode (air vs. ground vs. van).
*   **Service Equivalence:** "Service level" is defined primarily by `Late delivery` % and `promised_lead_time_hours` adherence.

**Uncertainty**
*   **Grid Intensity:** Variations in energy carbon intensity over time are not captured in static `energy_kwh`.
*   **Return Rates:** While `rework_flag` is logged, actual customer returns due to damage are inferred from packaging evidence rather than explicit return events in the log.
*   **Obsolescence:** The 6% obsolescence rate for batch-produced items is an estimate; actual write-off carbon cost is not fully quantified in the log.

# 3. Intervention options

The following eight interventions are analyzed based on the process mining evidence, targeting specific paths and segments.

**1. S4: Replace air with priority pick + ground (Standard-stock Service-contract)**
*   **Target:** Standard-stock Service-contract (16% of cases). Currently 22% violate SP1 (ground policy).
*   **Mechanism:** Use `promised_lead_time_hours` to calculate slack. If slack > ground transit time + priority pick buffer, force `Ship ground`.
*   **Effect:** Reduces `transport_kgco2e` (Air is 4-6x higher). Service maintained via priority picking (reduces `Pick from stock` time).
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `route_mode` shift from `parcel_air` to `parcel_ground`; track `Late delivery` % for this segment.

**2. S2: Dynamic batching with wait caps (Made-to-order)**
*   **Target:** MTO paths involving `Batch wait` -> `CNC production`.
*   **Mechanism:** Set maximum `batch_wait` time (e.g., 24h) based on `case_type`. If cap reached, release batch even if sub-optimal size.
*   **Effect:** Balances `energy_kwh` reduction (larger batches) against `Late delivery` risk (waiting increases risk 1.7x). Prevents obsolescence.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `batch_size` distribution vs. `Late delivery` %; track obsolescence rate (items >14 days).

**3. S3: Shift MTO production from CNC_B to CNC_A**
*   **Target:** MTO cases currently routed to `CNC_B`.
*   **Mechanism:** Route to `CNC_A` when `queue` time allows. CNC_B has 18% rework vs. 9% on CNC_A.
*   **Effect:** Reduces `material_scrap_kg` and `energy_kwh` (less rework loops). May increase `cycle_time` slightly if CNC_A is slower/busier.
*   **Evidence:** Compare `rework_flag` rates by `machine_group`; monitor `Inspection` -> `Rework production` loop frequency.

**4. S5: Consolidate truck departures (Standard-stock Regular)**
*   **Target:** Standard-stock Regular (39% of cases), specifically `Ship ground` paths.
*   **Mechanism:** Hold orders for up to 12-24h to fill `consolidated_truck`.
*   **Effect:** Reduces `transport_kgco2e` by 35-55%. Increases `cycle_time` by consolidation wait.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `route_mode` shift to `consolidated_truck`; track `Late delivery` % (must stay <7% baseline).

**5. S6: Packaging-route compatibility check**
*   **Target:** Paths showing `Rework packaging` (260 standard, 360 reinforced).
*   **Mechanism:** Add validation before `Package` event. Check `package_material_kg` type against `route_mode` and part geometry.
*   **Effect:** Eliminates `Rework packaging` loops (waste of material + energy). Prevents Q1 violations.
*   **Evidence:** Track `Rework packaging` event frequency; monitor `package_material_kg` efficiency.

**6. S7: Inspection quality gate for high-rework combinations**
*   **Target:** `CNC_B` and `Heat treatment` paths leading to `Inspection`.
*   **Mechanism:** Mandatory extended inspection for parts >8kg or from CNC_B before `Package`.
*   **Effect:** Reduces downstream returns/rework (hidden waste). Increases `cycle_time` at inspection.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `rework_flag` post-implementation; track `Inspection` -> `Rework production` loops.

**7. S9: Split Critical-downtime categories**
*   **Target:** Critical-downtime Service-contract (11% of cases).
*   **Mechanism:** Require explicit justification for `dedicated_van` vs. `parcel_air`. Differentiate true downtime vs. precautionary.
*   **Effect:** Reduces unnecessary `dedicated_van` use (high CO2e, often empty return). Protects resilience for true emergencies.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `SP2` conformance (justification event); track `transport_kgco2e` per Critical case.

**8. S1: Green shipping recommender (General)**
*   **Target:** All shipping decision points (`Ship ground/air/van`).
*   **Mechanism:** Algorithm suggests lowest carbon mode meeting `promised_lead_time_hours` with 85% probability.
*   **Effect:** Systematic reduction in `transport_kgco2e`. Supports SP1 compliance.
*   **Evidence:** Monitor `SP1` violation rate (target <5%); track `expedite_flag` usage.

# 5. Rebound and displacement risks

1.  **Batching Wait -> Express Transport:** Imposing batch waits (S2) to save energy may cause delays that trigger `expedite_flag` or `parcel_air` usage later to meet deadlines, negating transport savings.
2.  **Packaging Reduction -> Damage Returns:** Reducing `package_material_kg` without compatibility checks (S6) may increase damage rates, leading to returns that double the transport footprint per successful delivery.
3.  **CNC_A Queue -> Emergency Production:** Shifting load to CNC_A (S3) may create bottlenecks. If queues exceed thresholds, cases may route to `Emergency production` (760 cases), which has higher energy intensity and disrupts planning.
4.  **Consolidation -> Obsolescence:** Holding stock for `consolidated_truck` (S5) increases inventory age. If demand forecasts are wrong, the 6% obsolescence rate may rise, wasting embodied carbon of unsold parts.
5.  **Lead Time Negotiation -> Order Splitting:** If customers negotiate longer lead times (S10 implication), they may split orders to manage risk, increasing total shipment count and `transport_kgco2e` per part.
6.  **Van Restriction -> Resilience Loss:** Strictly enforcing `SP2` (S9) without adequate backup carriers may leave `Service-contract` customers vulnerable during carrier disruptions, violating `R1` (alternative route availability).

# 6. Balanced recommendation

To achieve environmental reduction without unacceptable service deterioration, I recommend a **Segmented Flow Redesign Package** combining interventions S1, S2, S3, S4, S6, and S9. This package avoids isolated "green" cuts that harm resilience.

**Core Logic: Segmentation-Based Routing**
*   **Critical-downtime (11%):** Maintain high service. Implement **S9** to distinguish true emergencies from precautionary urgents. Only true emergencies get `dedicated_van`. Precautionary urgents use `parcel_air` or `Ship ground` with priority. This reduces high-emission van usage while protecting resilience for true crises.
*   **Standard-stock Service-contract (16%):** Implement **S4** and **S1**. These cases currently violate SP1 (22% air usage). Use priority picking to compensate for slower ground transport. This captures low-hanging fruit (air emits 4-6x more) without impacting the tight SLAs (median 18h) because stock is available.
*   **Made-to-order (34%):** Implement **S2** (Dynamic Batching) and **S3** (CNC_A Shift). Do not maximize batch size blindly. Cap batch wait to prevent late delivery spikes (currently 18% late for MTO Regular). Prefer CNC_A to reduce the 18% rework rate on CNC_B, lowering hidden scrap and energy waste.
*   **Standard-stock Regular (39%):** Implement **S5** (Consolidation) and **S6** (Packaging Check). This segment has the most slack (median 31h). Consolidation saves 35-55% transport CO2. Packaging checks prevent rework loops.

**Why this balances the criteria:**
*   **Environment:** Targets high-emission air/van usage in Stock/Critical and high-energy rework in MTO.
*   **Service:** Protects Critical-downtime van access; uses priority picking to maintain Stock Service-contract SLAs; caps batching wait to limit MTO delays.
*   **Quality:** Reduces rework via CNC selection and packaging checks (addressing Q1 violations).
*   **Resilience:** Maintains alternative routes (R1) by not banning air/van entirely, but governing them via justification (SP2).

*   **SP1 (Ground Shipping):** Automate checks on `Standard-stock Service-contract` cases. Alert if `route_mode` = `parcel_air` when `promised_lead_time_hours` - `actual_cycle_time` > ground transit buffer. Target violation rate <5% (down from 22%).
*   **SP2 (Van Justification):** Monitor `Critical-downtime` cases. Ensure `dedicated_van` events are preceded by a specific justification attribute. Target missing justification <5% (down from 31%).
*   **Q1 (Packaging):** Flag cases where part weight >8kg and `package_material_kg` indicates standard packaging without inspection certification.

*   **Rebound Check:** Quarterly review of `material_scrap_kg` and `obsolescence` rates. If scrap increases while energy decreases, investigate CNC batching logic (S2/S3).
