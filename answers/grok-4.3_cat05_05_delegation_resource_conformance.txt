**## 2. Rule Interpretation**

RR1: Same human (after alias normalization) may not execute both Draft_Change_Request and Approve_Change on one case.  
RR2: Same human may not execute both Implement_Change and Verify_Implementation on one case.  
RR3: HIGH-risk Approve_Change requires QM standing or valid delegation at event timestamp; delegation must cover interval, scope, principal absence, and prohibit sub-delegation.  
RR4: HIGH-risk emergency change may be approved by IC/delegate; Retrospective_QM_Review must occur within two business days (Mon–Fri, excluding weekends).  
RR5: Service accounts require explicit human session attribution; aliases belonging to one person are collapsed before SoD evaluation.  
RR6: Repeated pairs, reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, and concentrated handovers are indicators only; they never constitute proof of collusion.

A principal acting while on recorded leave is treated as an identity anomaly requiring further evidence. A database record created after the effective interval cannot retroactively validate a delegation.
