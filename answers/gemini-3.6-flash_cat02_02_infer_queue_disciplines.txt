## Queue QA
- **Supporting Cases**:
  - **A03 (08:20 start)**: Directly supports appointment scheduling rule; started exactly at appointment time despite early arrival.
  - **A02, A05, A07**: Non-appointment jobs are processed in exact arrival sequence (08:04 -> 08:21 -> 08:35), proving a FIFO fallback.
- **Contradicting Cases**:
  - **A04 (P1) and A08 (P1)**: Priority 1 jobs were bypassed in favor of FIFO-older Priority 3 jobs (e.g., A02 chosen over A04 at 08:30; A05 chosen over A08 at 09:05).
  - **A06 (appt 09:15)**: Started 5 minutes late (09:20).
- **Explanation**: Apparent priority violations are explained because QA does *not* operate under a priority policy. The late start of A06 is an operational violation caused by non-preemptive execution of A05 (dispatched at 09:05 for 15 min without dynamic appointment buffer checking).

## Queue QB
- **Supporting Cases**:
  - **B01 & B02**: B01 (P3) is preempted at 09:16 when B02 (P1) arrives, directly confirming preemptive priority logic.
  - **B05**: Dispatched at 10:00 as P1 over lower-priority jobs.
- **Contradicting Cases**:
  - **B03 (P2) dispatched at 09:48 over B05 (P1)**: B05 scanned at 09:46.
  - **B07 (appt 10:30)**: Dispatched at 10:56, 26 minutes late.
- **Explanation**: B05's delayed dispatch is explained by a 2-minute arrival scan lag (actual physical readiness at 09:48:01 after B03 was already dispatched at 09:48:00). B07's delay indicates QB treats appointments as non-preemptive soft targets secondary to active preemptive priority queues.

## Queue QC
- **Supporting Cases**:
  - **C02, C03, C04**: Processed in strict ascending order of `est_min` (8m -> 14m -> 22m), bypassing older job C01 (36m) and P1 job C05 (40m).
  - **C06**: Dispatched at 11:34 (est_min=6m) immediately following C05's completion.
- **Contradicting Cases**:
  - **C05**: Dispatched at 10:54 despite having the largest estimated processing time (40m) among waiting jobs.
- **Explanation**: C05's selection is explained by an explicit operational SLA threshold rule: at 10:54, C05's deadline (10:50) was already breached/at risk. The dispatcher executed an emergency SLA override, temporarily suspending SPT.

## Queue QD
- **Supporting Cases**:
  - **D01, D04, D08, D09 on D-M**: D-M only processes `mechanical` jobs; within skill compatibility, jobs follow FIFO (D04 before D08).
  - **D05 on D-F**: D05 requires `firmware+electrical`. D-F is the only resource with both skills.
  - **D07 on D-X**: D07 requires `mechanical+electrical`, matching D-X.
- **Contradicting Cases**:
  - **D08 (P1) delayed until 11:35 on D-M**: Passed over at 11:25 for D04 (P3).
- **Explanation**: D08's delay is fully explained by skill-filtered FIFO dispatch (D04 arrived at 11:05, D08 arrived at 11:16). Priority is not the primary routing signal in QD.

# 4. Counterfactual discrimination tests

## Episode 1: Queue QC at 10:10 (Initial Backlog Dispatch)
- **Context**: Resource C1 becomes available at 10:10. Queue contains C01 (arr 10:00, est 36, P3, ddl 13:00), C02 (arr 10:03, est 8, P3, ddl 14:00), C03 (arr 10:05, est 14, P2, ddl 12:30), C04 (arr 10:07, est 22, P3, ddl 14:30).
- **Competing Hypotheses**:
  1. *Hypothesis A*: Shortest Expected Processing Time (SPT).
  2. *Hypothesis B*: Earliest Deadline First (EDF) / Priority.
- **Next-Job Predictions**:
  - Under **Hypothesis A (SPT)**: Predicts **C02** (min `est_min` = 8).
  - Under **Hypothesis B (EDF/Priority)**: Predicts **C03** (earliest deadline = 12:30, highest priority = P2).
- **Observed Result**: **C02** was dispatched at 10:10.
- **Discriminative Observable Needed**: A synthetic test job injected with high priority / tight deadline but long duration (e.g., est=50, ddl=11:30, P1) vs short low-priority job (est=5, ddl=16:00, P3). If the short job is chosen, SPT is conclusively isolated from EDF/Priority.

## Episode 2: Queue QB at 09:48 (Interruption of B01)
- **Context**: B01 (P3) is interrupted for the second time at 09:48. Waiting jobs are B03 (arr 09:20, P2, est 12) and B05 (arr 09:46, P1, est 14).
- **Competing Hypotheses**:
  1. *Hypothesis A*: Strict Preemptive Priority with Delayed Arrival Scan (B05 not visible until 09:48:05, so P2 job B03 dispatched at 09:48:00).
  2. *Hypothesis B*: Shortest Remaining Processing Time (SRPT) / Hybrid Dispatch (B01 remaining est = 14m, B03 est = 12m; dispatcher picked shortest duration B03).
- **Next-Job Predictions**:
  - Under **Hypothesis A**: If P1 B05 were fully visible at 09:48:00, it would preemptively start over B03.
  - Under **Hypothesis B**: B03 (12m) is selected because 12m < 14m (B05) < 14m (B01 remaining).
- **Observed Result**: **B03** was dispatched at 09:48.
- **Discriminative Observable Needed**: Millisecond-level timestamping of the dispatch system's event-bus trigger (`job_eligibility_perceived_at`) vs physical arrival scan timestamp.

# 7. Final synthesis

### Cross-Queue Identifiability Conclusion:
- **Most Identifiable Queue**: **Queue QD (Skill-Based Routing)**.
  - *Reason*: Rigid skill matrix constraints (mechanical, firmware, electrical combinations) combined with resource calendar exceptions leave zero structural ambiguity. Job allocations to resources (D-M, D-F, D-X) are deterministically bound by capability before secondary FIFO rules apply.
- **Second Most Identifiable Queue**: **Queue QC (Shortest Expected Processing Time - SPT)**.
  - *Reason*: Processing the initial backlog strictly by ascending duration (`est_min` 8 -> 14 -> 22) provides unambiguous empirical proof of SPT over FIFO and static priority, with clear evidence of SLA emergency override for C05.
- **Least Identifiable Queue**: **Queue QB (Preemptive Priority)**.
  - *Reason*: Observational noise introduced by arrival scan delays (up to 2 minutes), multiple preemption stops on job B01, delayed appointment servicing for B07, and missing preemption cost records create multiple observationally equivalent explanations (e.g., scan lag vs manual dispatcher reassignment).

### Most Dangerous Claimed Policy to Infer:
- **Inferring Pure Static Priority for Queue QA or Queue QC**.
  - *Risk*: Claiming QA or QC operate on Priority (P1 > P2 > P3) would lead to severe misconfigurations. In QA, Priority 1 jobs (A04, A08) are routinely passed over in favor of FIFO-older Priority 3 jobs. In QC, Priority 1 job C05 is bypassed twice for shorter Priority 3 jobs. Assuming priority enforcement in these queues would corrupt throughput capacity models and operational simulation digital twins.
