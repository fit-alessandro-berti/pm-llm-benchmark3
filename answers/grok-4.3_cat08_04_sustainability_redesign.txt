1. System boundary and assumptions
The environmental scope covers direct process-attributable impacts captured in the event log: production energy (energy_kwh from CNC_A, CNC_B, heat_treat), transport emissions (transport_kgco2e by route_mode), material scrap and packaging (material_scrap_kg, package_material_kg), and rework loops. Inside the boundary are paths from Availability check through Package/Ship to Confirm delivery, including batching, inspection, and partial shipments. Outside the boundary are upstream raw-material extraction, customer-side use or disposal of spare parts, and carrier fleet operations beyond the chosen route_mode. Uncertainty remains around actual empty-return rates for dedicated_van, real-time batch sizes not recorded per case, and downstream obsolescence once items leave the recorded process.

2. Sustainability-related process indicators
- Energy intensity: median energy_kwh per order by case_type and machine_group (CNC_A vs CNC_B).
- Transport intensity: median transport_kgco2e per order by route_mode and customer_type.
- Material efficiency: material_scrap_kg and package_material_kg per order, stratified by rework_flag and batch_size.
- Batching efficiency: batch_size versus batch_wait time and late-delivery rate for Made-to-order paths.
- Rework and return loop rate: frequency of Rework production and Rework packaging events by machine_group and route_mode.
- Service conformance: share of orders meeting promised_lead_time_hours by segment, plus SP1/SP2 violation rate.
- Resilience buffer: availability of at least one non-air/non-van alternative for Service-contract cases during disruption.

3. Intervention options
S1. Green shipping recommender using promised lead time, route reliability, and customer segment.  
Target: Standard-stock Service-contract air shipments (5 % path). Mechanism: route_mode switch to ground when slack permits. Service effect: preserves p90 cycle time for contract customers via priority pick. Monitoring: route_mode distribution and SP1 conformance by customer_type.

S2. Dynamic batching for CNC and heat treatment with maximum waiting-time caps by segment.  
Target: Made-to-order batch_wait paths (13 % and 5 %). Mechanism: enforce minimum batch_size 8–12 while capping wait at segment-specific thresholds. Service effect: reduces energy per item while limiting late-delivery increase. Monitoring: batch_size distribution, batch_wait time, and late-delivery rate by machine_group.

S3. Shift Made-to-order production from CNC_B to CNC_A when queue and due-date slack allow it.  
Target: 5 % MTO CNC_B rework path. Mechanism: route cases to lower-rework machine. Quality effect: lowers rework_flag and material_scrap_kg. Monitoring: machine_group assignment and rework rate by case_type.

S4. Replace air for Standard-stock Service-contract orders with priority pick + ground unless slack is insufficient.  
Target: Standard-stock Service-contract air (SP1 violation 22 %). Mechanism: enforce ground unless promised_lead_time_hours risk exceeds 85 % probability. Service effect: cuts transport_kgco2e while protecting contract SLAs. Monitoring: route_mode and late-delivery % by customer_type.

S5. Consolidate truck departures twice daily for non-urgent stock orders.  
Target: Standard-stock Regular ground and partial-shipment paths. Mechanism: scheduled consolidation windows. Environmental effect: lowers transport_kgco2e 35–55 %. Service effect: adds 12–24 h wait only for Regular segment. Monitoring: consolidated_truck share and cycle-time distribution.

S6. Improve packaging-route compatibility checks before shipment.  
Target: Rework packaging loops (260 + 360 events). Mechanism: pre-check route_mode against part geometry. Quality effect: reduces rework_flag and package_material_kg. Monitoring: Rework packaging frequency and package_material_kg.

S7. Add inspection quality gate for high-rework machine/part combinations.  
Target: Inspection  Rework production (470 events) and CNC_B paths. Mechanism: extra gate on high-risk combinations. Quality effect: lowers material_scrap_kg and late-delivery from returns. Monitoring: rework loop rate and inspection_cell queue time.

S8. Replace some dedicated vans with shared emergency courier lanes.  
Target: Critical-downtime dedicated_van (8 % path). Mechanism: pool capacity for verified downtime cases only. Resilience effect: maintains 3 % late-delivery while cutting empty runs. Monitoring: dedicated_van justification events and SP2 conformance.

5. Rebound and displacement risks
- Larger CNC batches cut energy_kwh but raise batch_wait, increasing late-delivery risk and obsolescence (6 % of batch-produced items).
- Switching Service-contract orders from air to ground reduces transport_kgco2e yet may trigger more partial shipments and second deliveries if availability checks remain unchanged.
- Reinforced packaging lowers damage returns but doubles package_material_kg; without route compatibility checks this simply displaces emissions into material waste.
- Shifting volume to CNC_A lowers rework but can lengthen inspection_cell queues, pushing more cases into Rework production loops.
- Twice-daily consolidated trucks cut transport emissions yet may encourage earlier ordering, increasing inventory holding and eventual obsolescence for slow-moving Standard-stock items.
- Stricter SP1 enforcement on Regular customers may displace demand into Service-contract channels, raising overall air and van usage and undermining the intended transport reduction.

6. Balanced recommendation
Implement S1 (green shipping recommender), S4 (priority-pick ground default for Service-contract), S2 (dynamic batching with segment caps), S3 (CNC_A preference), S7 (inspection gate), S6 (packaging compatibility), and S12 (sustainability exception log). This package targets the highest-volume Standard-stock and Made-to-order paths while protecting Critical-downtime service levels. It reduces energy and transport intensity through batching and route switches only where slack exists, limits rework via machine and inspection changes, and maintains resilience by retaining justified dedicated_van and air options. The exception log plus segment-level monitoring prevents policy drift that would otherwise erode service or create rebound loops.

7. Validation and governance plan
Replay the enriched event log monthly after rollout. Measure SP1/SP2/Q1 conformance rates and segment-level late-delivery %, rework %, and cycle-time p90 against pre-intervention baselines. Track exception-log entries for overrides and correlate them with energy_kwh, transport_kgco2e, and material_scrap_kg. Run segment-stratified dashboards for Service-contract versus Regular customers and flag any rise in partial-shipment or cancellation events. Governance requires quarterly review of exception patterns and automatic rollback triggers if any segment exceeds agreed late-delivery or rework thresholds.
