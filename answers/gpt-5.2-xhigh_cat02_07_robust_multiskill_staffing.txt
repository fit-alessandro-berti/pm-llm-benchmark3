# 1. Assumptions, objectives, and decision variables

## Observations (given)

- Control flow: Intake → diagnostic work (type-dependent; possible ME/ES fork/join) → Validation.
- Priority: P1 can preempt a lower-priority diagnostic once per victim case; restart penalty 10% of remaining active service time.
- Coordination/overhead: 7 min handover when resource changes; +5 min extra if moving between mechanical team and electrical/software team (unless the same cross-skilled resource retains the case); +5 min setup when a resource switches diagnostic family between consecutive cases; parallel ME/ES adds 8 min coordination before Validation.
- Shifts/breaks are fixed; overtime ≤30 min per resource; fairness: planned utilization >90% per individual is unfair/fragile.

## Distributional assumptions (explicit)

**A1 Service-time sampling**: For each activity, draw from a two-component mixture:
- with probability \(p\) (complex-tail probability) draw Uniform(tail_min, tail_max),
- else draw Uniform(ordinary_min, ordinary_max).
This matches the “ordinary range / complex-tail range / tail probability” table.

**A2 Independence**: service-time draws are independent across activities and cases; no correlation between case type and tail beyond the provided per-activity tail probability.

**A3 Arrivals inside each two-hour block**: cases arrive according to a Poisson count with mean = expected_arrivals for the block; arrival times are uniform within the block.

**A4 Working-time calendars**: resources are unavailable outside shift windows and during breaks; overtime extends shift end by 30 minutes. Activities are non-preemptive with respect to breaks/shift boundaries (i.e., an activity starts only if it can finish within the current availability window). This is conservative near breaks but transparent.

**A5 Overheads as time consumption**:
- Handover (7 min) is modeled as additional work time attached to the receiving activity when the resource differs from the predecessor activity’s resource.
- Cross-team handover (+5 min) is modeled as additional work time when an ME case moves between Mechanical and Electrical on different resources.
- Setup (5 min) is modeled as additional work time when a resource’s last diagnostic family differs from the next diagnostic family it executes.
- Parallel coordination (8 min) is modeled as additional work time attached to Validation when ME/ES was executed in “parallel mode”.

**A6 Preemption**: only diagnostic activities (Mechanical/Electrical/Software) may be preempted. When preempted at time \(t\), remaining processing time is multiplied by \(1.10\) (adds 10% of remaining). A case can be preempted at most once (victim side), as specified.

## ME/ES parallelism decision (decision variable + assumption)

For each ME/ES case, the policy chooses one of:
- **Parallel mode**: release both branch tasks after Intake; add 8 minutes coordination before Validation.
- **Sequential mode**: enforce a strict order (branch 1 then branch 2); no 8-minute coordination. Sequential mode may be constrained to cross-skilled resources to avoid team-switch/handover.

## Objectives (at least two)

**O1 Tail/SLA risk objective (priority-weighted)**:
- Maximize SLA attainment at 90th percentile targets, with heavy weight on P1, and penalize tail delay:
  - minimize \(J_1 = \sum_{p} w_p \max(0, 0.90 - \\widehat{SLA}_p) + \\lambda \\cdot \\widehat{p95}(\text{end-to-end delay})\),
  where \(w_{P1} \gg w_{P2} > w_{P3}\).

**O2 Robust-fair objective (scenario-robust with fairness)**:
- Minimize worst-case across uncertainty scenarios \(s \in \{\text{base}, U1,\dots,U5\}\):
  - \(J_2 = \max_s \\left[ \\alpha \\cdot (0.90-\\widehat{SLA}_{P1,s})_+ + \\beta \\cdot \\widehat{p95}_s + \\gamma \\cdot (\\max_r u_{r,s}-0.90)_+ + \\eta \\cdot \\text{Gini}(u_{r,s}) \\right]\).
This explicitly encodes robustness and the “>90% individual utilization is unfair/fragile” rule.

## Decision variables (what we can change)

- Routing/dispatch rule at each activity: which eligible resource gets the task next.
- Queue discipline: FIFO vs priority vs slack-based rules; preemption thresholds.
- “Home skill” assignments (specialization) vs pooling degree (overflow conditions).
- ME/ES parallelization threshold: when to parallelize vs enforce sequential.
- Reservation/guardrails: reserved P1 capacity, and caps on diagnostic family switching.

# 3. Candidate policies

Below are three *implementable* staffing/routing policies with explicit dispatch, preemption, and ME/ES parallelization rules.

## Policy A — Strict specialization with fixed queues (“SILO”)

Goal: minimize setup switching, keep rules simple; accept higher handovers and lower surge resilience.

- **Eligibility / queues**:
  - Separate queues for Intake, Mechanical, Electrical, Software, Validation.
  - Diagnostic resources are siloed:
    - Mechanical: Ada, Ben (Farah assists before 12:00 if needed).
    - Electrical: Chen, Deepa (Emil assists only if idle and SW queue is empty).
    - Software: Emil (primary after 12:00), Chen (secondary), Farah (pre-12:00 support).

- **Dispatch rule (per queue)**:
  - Priority order: P1 first, then P2, then P3.
  - Within priority: FIFO (arrival order into that activity queue).

- **Preemption**:
  - P1 diagnostic may preempt ongoing P2/P3 diagnostic within the same diagnostic silo.
  - Choose victim with largest remaining time; apply 10% remaining-time penalty; victim case preempted at most once.

- **ME/ES parallelization**:
  - Always “parallel mode”: enqueue both branches immediately after Intake; add coordination (8 min) before Validation.

- **Handover minimization**:
  - Not optimized; accept 7-min handovers when activities shift resources.

Expected behavior: good predictability and low setup, but poor in U1/U5 (silos saturate), and in U3 (SW pre-12 becomes very constrained).

## Policy B — Full pooling with transparent scoring (“POOL”)

Goal: maximize throughput and balance utilization; accept higher switching overhead.

- **Eligibility**:
  - Any qualified resource can take any ready task.
  - Single shared ready list, but resource choice is constrained by skills and calendars.

- **Dispatch score (interpretable)**:
  - Compute per-task “urgency” based on SLA slack \(= \text{due} - \text{now}\).
  - Score for a resource-task pairing:
    - prioritize P1 > P2 > P3,
    - then minimize negative slack (lateness risk),
    - then prefer lower expected processing time on that resource (range mean × multiplier),
    - add small penalties for diagnostic family switch (setup) and for likely handover.
  - Resource pulls the highest-score task it can execute without crossing a break/shift boundary.

- **Preemption**:
  - If a P1 diagnostic is waiting and no eligible resource is idle, preempt a running P2/P3 diagnostic on an eligible resource (subject to the “preempt once per victim case” rule), choosing the victim with largest remaining time.

- **ME/ES parallelization**:
  - Parallelize when system is not heavily loaded (e.g., estimated load < 0.85); otherwise enforce sequential to reduce coordination and concurrency pressure.

Expected behavior: best average delay and fairness in base/U2, but can thrash in U4 tails and U5 (switching overhead and E/S hard bottlenecks).

## Policy C — Hybrid with reserved P1 capacity + controlled overflow (“HYBRID-RESERVE”)

Goal: robustness across U1–U5, especially P1 SLA, while avoiding fragile >90% utilization and limiting switching overhead.

- **Home skills (default for P2/P3 diagnostics)**:
  - Ada + Ben: Mechanical home.
  - Deepa: Electrical home (also cross-skilled for ME sequential).
  - Emil: Software home (avoid pulling him into Electrical except in emergency).
  - Chen (if present): Electrical + Software home.
  - Farah: preferred Intake/Validation support and early Software relief (08:00–12:00).

- **P1 fast lane (reservation)**:
  - When any P1 diagnostic exists (or predicted within next 30 min), relax home restrictions: any qualified resource can take P1 tasks.
  - Keep “critical skill reserves”:
    - Preserve Emil for Software (especially P1 Software / ES branch).
    - Preserve Deepa for Electrical (P1 E / ME branch), avoiding frequent switches.
    - In Chen-absence scenarios, preserve Farah (08:00–12:00) for Software/Electrical rather than consuming her on Mechanical.

- **Controlled overflow for P2/P3**:
  - Allow cross-skill overflow only when the backlog of a diagnostic family exceeds a threshold (e.g., >180 “base minutes” waiting, roughly 4–6 jobs depending on type).
  - Cap diagnostic family switches per hour (operationally: “do not switch families unless backlog trigger is active”).

- **ME/ES parallelization**:
  - P1: parallelize unless the system is extremely loaded; if the second branch would wait “too long”, enforce sequential on a cross-skilled resource to avoid coordination and extra handovers.
  - P2/P3: prefer sequential under moderate/high load.

- **Preemption**:
  - Same as Policy B, but recommend an extra guardrail: only preempt if P1 slack < 30 min or if P1 has waited >10 min, to avoid excessive churn on long-tail days.

Expected behavior: slightly worse mean delay than full pooling in the base case, but better worst-case P1 SLA and lower max utilization under U1/U3/U5.

# 5. Robust recommendation

## Primary recommendation: HYBRID-RESERVE (Policy C)

Under the robust-fair objective \(J_2\) (worst-case across U1–U5, plus fairness penalty), HYBRID-RESERVE dominates because it:
- **Protects P1 SLAs under U3/U5** by reserving scarce cross-skilled E/S capacity and using preemption in a controlled way.
- **Avoids pooling thrash on U4** by keeping home skills for P2/P3 and preferring sequential ME/ES under load, which reduces coordination/setup amplification.
- **Keeps max individual utilization closer to the 90% fragility threshold** by avoiding over-concentrating all work on the “fastest” resource during shocks.

## Recommended fallback mode: POOL (Policy B) in calm periods

When the system is not under a known shock and no key absences are present, POOL tends to reduce average delay and improve fairness by using otherwise-idle capacity.

## Concrete triggers for switching modes (auditable)

Switch **into HYBRID-RESERVE** when any of the following holds:
- **Load trigger**: forecasted arrivals for the next 2-hour block exceed \(1.2\\times\) base expectations, or observed queue growth indicates base-work/staff-min > 1.
- **P1 trigger**: predicted P1 90th-percentile completion time exceeds 100 minutes, or any P1 has waited >10 minutes for diagnostics.
- **Capacity trigger**: Chen absent (U3/U5) or similar E/S capacity loss; or any outage blocks Intake for ≥15 minutes.

Switch **back to POOL** when:
- no absences/outages,
- trailing-hour max utilization < 0.85, and
- diagnostic family switching overhead (setup minutes) stays below an agreed threshold (e.g., <10% of busy time).

## Parallelization thresholds (ME/ES)

- P1: parallelize if both branches can start within ~10 minutes on distinct resources; otherwise sequentialize on a cross-skilled resource to reduce handover + coordination overhead.
- P2/P3: default to sequential when system load is high (e.g., base-work/staff-min > 1 for the next block), to maximize throughput.

## When another policy is preferable

- If the organization values **simplicity and minimal switching** over SLA robustness, SILO is easiest to explain and may be preferred—accepting worse U1/U5 behavior.
- If the primary KPI is **mean delay in stable demand** and training/setup costs are negligible, POOL can be preferred (with careful monitoring for U4 tail amplification).


# 6. Secondary effects and implementation risks

- **Gaming of estimates**:
  - If dispatch uses “expected remaining time” or slack, operators may up-code complexity to get earlier service. Mitigation: lock estimates post-Intake; audit estimate drift.

- **Starvation of P3**:
  - Preemption and P1 reservation can starve low priority cases, especially in U1/U5. Mitigation: aging/guaranteed minimum P3 throughput windows (e.g., 30 min per 2h block reserved for P3 if backlog exceeds threshold).

- **Skill erosion**:
  - Strong home-skill policies can reduce cross-skill practice; full pooling can overload staff with constant switching. Mitigation: planned cross-training slots off-peak; explicit “training” workload not counted against fairness.

- **Excessive preemption**:
  - Restart penalties compound on U4 long-tail days. Mitigation: preempt only when P1 slack is truly tight and avoid preempting tasks with very small remaining time.

- **Coordination overhead and hidden work**:
  - Parallel branches add explicit 8 min coordination, but also increase “implicit coordination” not modeled. If parallelization is overused, Validation becomes a bottleneck.

- **New bottleneck at Validation**:
  - Validation is non-trivial (10–18, tail 22–35). If diagnostics are accelerated but validation staffing isn’t protected, queue migrates to Validation.

- **Fairness perception**:
  - Even if utilization is balanced, reserving “fast lane” capacity can feel unfair if not transparent. Mitigation: publish rule set, rotate reserve duty, and expose per-person utilization metrics.

- **Human interpretability**:
  - Complex scoring rules can become “black box”. Mitigation: keep score features few (priority tier, slack bucket, expected duration bucket, switching penalty) and log all decisions for audit.


# 7. Monitoring and process-mining design

## Event log design (what to record)

Beyond the required interval fields, add attributes so routing policies are auditable and bottleneck migration is detectable:

- **Core interval fields**:
  - `case:concept:name`, `concept:name`, `start_timestamp`, `time:timestamp`, `org:resource`

- **Case attributes**:
  - `case:priority` (P1/P2/P3), `case:type` (M/E/S/ME/ES), `case:arrival_timestamp`
  - `case:sla_target_minutes`, `case:sla_deadline_timestamp`

- **Queueing and assignment**:
  - `queue:enter_timestamp` per stage (Intake queue, each diagnostic queue, Validation queue)
  - `decision:timestamp`, `decision:policy_version`, `decision:mode` (SILO/POOL/HYBRID)
  - `decision:eligible_resources` (serialized list), `decision:chosen_resource`
  - `decision:score_components` (priority tier, slack, expected duration, switching penalty, handover penalty)

- **Overheads and control**:
  - `handover:applied` (bool), `handover:minutes`
  - `setup:applied` (bool), `setup:minutes`, `setup:from_family`, `setup:to_family`
  - `coordination:parallel_mode` (bool), `coordination:minutes`
  - `preemption:occurred` (bool), `preemption:victim_case`, `preemption:remaining_minutes`, `preemption:restart_penalty_minutes`
  - `pause/resume`: if an activity is paused (break/off-shift), log `lifecycle:transition` events or split intervals into segments.

- **Shock flags**:
  - `scenario:flag` (U1..U5), `outage:flag`, `absence:flag`

## PM4Py analyses to run routinely

- **Performance**:
  - `pm4py.get_service_time` and activity/resource-level service distributions to detect tail growth (U4-like days).
  - `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` for **case-level totals** (labelled correctly as totals), then compare distributions across weeks/policies.

- **Handover and coordination**:
  - `pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network` on `org:resource` to quantify handover burden; track top edges over time.
  - Compare handover density and centrality between POOL vs HYBRID.

- **Bottleneck migration**:
  - Compute stage-specific waiting times using logged `queue:enter_timestamp` (Intake vs diagnostics vs Validation).
  - Detect time-of-day shifts in bottleneck by grouping by arrival block and comparing waiting/service times.

- **Drift and policy compliance**:
  - Track conformance to intended parallelization decisions (ME/ES parallel vs sequential) by checking overlap patterns and coordination flags.
  - Detect rule drift when observed decisions diverge from logged eligible sets or score ordering.

    brk_start: Optional[int]
    brk_end: Optional[int]

    def windows_for_day(self, day: int) -> List[Tuple[int, int]]:
        base = day * 1440
        s = base + self.shift_start
        e = base + self.shift_end + PARAMS["overtime_min"]
        if self.brk_start is None:
            return [(s, e)]
        b0 = base + self.brk_start
        b1 = base + self.brk_end
        return [(s, b0), (b1, e)]

    def current_window(self, t: float) -> Optional[Tuple[int, int]]:
        day = int(t // 1440)
        for d in (day - 1, day, day + 1):
            if d < 0:
                continue
            for w in self.windows_for_day(d):
                if w[0] <= t < w[1]:
                    return w
        return None

def scenario_blocks(scenario: str):
    out = []
    for start, end, lam, pr_mix, ty_mix in DEMAND_BLOCKS:
        lam2 = float(lam)
        pr2 = dict(pr_mix)
        ty2 = dict(ty_mix)

        if scenario in ("U1", "U5") and start in ("08:00", "10:00") or (scenario in ("U1", "U5") and start in ("10:00", "12:00")):
            lam2 *= 1.7
            pr2["P1"] += 8
            take = 8
            if pr2["P3"] >= take:
                pr2["P3"] -= take
            else:
                take2 = take - pr2["P3"]
                pr2["P3"] = 0
                pr2["P2"] = max(0, pr2["P2"] - take2)
            tot = pr2["P1"] + pr2["P2"] + pr2["P3"]
            for k in pr2:
                pr2[k] = 100 * pr2[k] / tot

        if scenario == "U2" and start in ("12:00", "14:00") or (scenario == "U2" and start in ("14:00", "16:00")):
            ty2["E"] *= 2
            ty2["ES"] *= 2
            tot = sum(ty2.values())
            for k in ty2:
                ty2[k] = 100 * ty2[k] / tot

        out.append((start, end, lam2, pr2, ty2))
    return out

def active_resources(scenario: str) -> List[str]:
    names = list(RESOURCE_TABLE)
    if scenario in ("U3", "U5"):
        names = [n for n in names if n != "Chen"]
    return names

def tail_mult(scenario: str) -> float:
    return 2.0 if scenario == "U4" else 1.0

def intake_outage_window(scenario: str) -> Optional[Tuple[int, int]]:
    return (hhmm_to_min("09:30"), hhmm_to_min("10:00")) if scenario == "U5" else None

class Policy:
    name = "base"

    def decide_parallel(self, priority: str, now: float, load_hint: float) -> bool:
        return True

    def allow_task(self, res: str, diag_family: Optional[str], priority: str, backlog_by_family: Dict[str, float]) -> bool:
        return True

    def score(self, task_priority: str, slack_min: float, exp_proc_min: float, switch_pen: float, handover_pen: float) -> float:
        # Transparent, few-feature score:
        # - priority tier dominates
        # - then lateness risk (negative slack)
        # - then short processing time
        # - then switching/handover penalties
        pr_w = {"P1": 3.0, "P2": 2.0, "P3": 1.0}[task_priority]
        return pr_w * 1e6 - 10.0 * max(0.0, -slack_min) - 0.5 * exp_proc_min - 0.2 * switch_pen - 0.2 * handover_pen

class SpecializedFixed(Policy):
    name = "specialized_fixed"
    HOME = {
        "Ada": {"Mechanical"},
        "Ben": {"Mechanical"},
        "Deepa": {"Electrical"},
        "Chen": {"Electrical", "Software"},
        "Emil": {"Software"},
        "Farah": set(),
    }

    def decide_parallel(self, priority: str, now: float, load_hint: float) -> bool:
        return True

    def allow_task(self, res: str, diag_family: Optional[str], priority: str, backlog_by_family: Dict[str, float]) -> bool:
        if diag_family is None:
            return True
        return diag_family in self.HOME.get(res, set())

class FullPooling(Policy):
    name = "full_pooling"
    def decide_parallel(self, priority: str, now: float, load_hint: float) -> bool:
        return load_hint < 0.85

class HybridReserved(Policy):
    name = "hybrid_reserved"
    HOME = {
        "Ada": {"Mechanical"},
        "Ben": {"Mechanical"},
        "Deepa": {"Electrical"},
        "Chen": {"Electrical", "Software"},
        "Emil": {"Software"},
        "Farah": set(),
    }

    def decide_parallel(self, priority: str, now: float, load_hint: float) -> bool:

            return load_hint < 0.95

    def allow_task(self, res: str, diag_family: Optional[str], priority: str, backlog_by_family: Dict[str, float]) -> bool:
        if priority == "P1":
            return True
        if diag_family is None:
            return True
        if diag_family in self.HOME.get(res, set()):
            return True
        return backlog_by_family.get(diag_family, 0.0) > 180.0

@dataclass
class Task:

    case_id: str
    activity: str
    priority: str
    due: float
    base_min: float
    eligible: List[str]
    diag_family: Optional[str]
    prev_resource: Optional[str]
    prev_activity: Optional[str]

@dataclass
class Case:
    case_id: str
    arrival: float
    priority: str
    ctype: str
    due: float
    preemptions: int = 0
    parallel_mode: bool = False
    pending_branches: int = 0
    completion: Optional[float] = None

@dataclass
class ResState:
    name: str
    cal: Calendar
    busy_task: Optional[Task] = None
    task_end: float = 0.0
    last_diag_family: Optional[str] = None
    busy_min: float = 0.0

def sample_service(rng: np.random.Generator, activity: str, tail_m: float) -> float:
    (o0, o1), (t0, t1), p = SERVICE_TABLE[activity]
    p = min(1.0, p * tail_m)
    return float(rng.uniform(t0, t1)) if rng.random() < p else float(rng.uniform(o0, o1))

def simulate(policy: Policy, scenario: str, seed: int, days: int = 2):
    rng = np.random.default_rng(seed)

    # Build calendars
    calendars: Dict[str, Calendar] = {}
    for r, (mult, shift, brk) in RESOURCE_TABLE.items():
        s, e = shift.split("-")
        if brk is None:
            b0 = b1 = None
        else:
            b0s, b1s = brk.split("-")
            b0, b1 = hhmm_to_min(b0s), hhmm_to_min(b1s)
        calendars[r] = Calendar(mult=mult, shift_start=hhmm_to_min(s), shift_end=hhmm_to_min(e), brk_start=b0, brk_end=b1)

    resources = active_resources(scenario)
    res: Dict[str, ResState] = {r: ResState(name=r, cal=calendars[r]) for r in resources}

    def eligible(activity: str) -> List[str]:
        return [r for r in resources if activity in calendars[r].mult]

    # Arrivals on day 0 only (08:00–20:00)
    arrivals = []
    for start, end, lam, pr_mix, ty_mix in scenario_blocks(scenario):
        a, b = hhmm_to_min(start), hhmm_to_min(end)
        n = int(rng.poisson(lam))
        for _ in range(n):
            t = float(rng.uniform(a, b))
            arrivals.append((t, pr_mix, ty_mix))
    arrivals.sort(key=lambda x: x[0])

    outage = intake_outage_window(scenario)

    # Events: (time, id, type, payload)
    q = []
    ev_id = 0
    def push(t: float, typ: str, payload: dict):
        nonlocal ev_id
        heapq.heappush(q, (t, ev_id, typ, payload))
        ev_id += 1

    for i, (t, pr_mix, ty_mix) in enumerate(arrivals):
        push(t, "arrival", {"idx": i, "pr_mix": pr_mix, "ty_mix": ty_mix})

    # Calendar events: window starts for each resource
    horizon = days * 1440
    for r in resources:
        for d in range(days):
            for ws, _ in calendars[r].windows_for_day(d):
                push(ws, "cal", {"r": r})

    cases: Dict[str, Case] = {}
    ready: List[Task] = []
    next_task_id = 0
    log_rows = []

    def load_hint(now: float) -> float:
        base_work = sum(t.base_min for t in ready)
        cap = max(1.0, 60.0 * len(resources))
        return float(min(2.5, 1.1 * base_work / cap))

    def backlog_by_family() -> Dict[str, float]:
        b: Dict[str, float] = {}
        for t in ready:
            if t.diag_family:
                b[t.diag_family] = b.get(t.diag_family, 0.0) + t.base_min
        return b

    def duration(rs: ResState, task: Task) -> float:
        mult = rs.cal.mult[task.activity]
        dur = task.base_min * mult

        # setup time (resource-level)
        if task.diag_family and rs.last_diag_family and rs.last_diag_family != task.diag_family:
            dur += PARAMS["setup_overhead_min"]

        # handover time (receiver overhead)
        if task.prev_resource is not None and task.prev_resource != rs.name:
            dur += PARAMS["handover_overhead_min"]

        # cross-team time for ME moving between Mechanical and Electrical on different resources
        if task.prev_resource is not None and task.prev_resource != rs.name:
            if {task.prev_activity, task.activity} == {"Mechanical", "Electrical"}:
                dur += PARAMS["team_switch_overhead_min"]

        # coordination time for parallel ME/ES
        if task.activity == "Validation" and cases[task.case_id].parallel_mode:
            dur += PARAMS["parallel_coord_overhead_min"]

        return float(dur)

    def can_start_now(rs: ResState, task: Task, now: float) -> bool:
        w = rs.cal.current_window(now)
        if w is None:
            return False
        if outage is not None and task.activity == "Intake" and outage[0] <= now < outage[1]:
            return False
        return now + duration(rs, task) <= w[1]

    def start(rs: ResState, task: Task, now: float):
        d = duration(rs, task)
        rs.busy_task = task
        rs.task_end = now + d
        rs.busy_min += d
        if task.diag_family:
            rs.last_diag_family = task.diag_family

        log_rows.append({
            "case:concept:name": task.case_id,
            "concept:name": task.activity,
            "start_min": now,
            "end_min": now + d,
            "org:resource": rs.name,
            "priority": task.priority,
            "case_type": cases[task.case_id].ctype,
            "policy": policy.name,
            "scenario": scenario,
        })

        push(now + d, "done", {"r": rs.name, "task_id": task.task_id})

    def enqueue(case_id: str, activity: str, priority: str, due: float, prev_r: Optional[str], prev_a: Optional[str], eligible_override: Optional[List[str]] = None):
        nonlocal next_task_id
        next_task_id += 1
        fam = activity if activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software") else None
        ready.append(Task(
            task_id=next_task_id,
            case_id=case_id,
            activity=activity,
            priority=priority,
            due=due,
            base_min=sample_service(rng, activity, tail_mult(scenario)),
            eligible=eligible_override if eligible_override is not None else eligible(activity),
            diag_family=fam,
            prev_resource=prev_r,
            prev_activity=prev_a,
        ))

    def dispatch(now: float):
        # 1) P1 diagnostic preemption (if needed)
        p1_diags = [t for t in ready if t.priority == "P1" and t.diag_family]
        p1_diags.sort(key=lambda t: t.due)
        for t in p1_diags:
            # try idle eligible resources
            for rs in res.values():
                if rs.busy_task is not None:
                    continue
                if rs.name not in t.eligible:
                    continue
                if not policy.allow_task(rs.name, t.diag_family, t.priority, backlog_by_family()):
                    continue
                if can_start_now(rs, t, now):
                    ready.remove(t)
                    start(rs, t, now)
                    return

            # try preemption of a running P2/P3 diagnostic
            for rs in res.values():
                if rs.busy_task is None:
                    continue
                cur = rs.busy_task
                if cur.diag_family is None or cur.priority == "P1":
                    continue
                if rs.name not in t.eligible:
                    continue
                victim = cases[cur.case_id]
                if victim.preemptions >= PARAMS["max_preemptions_per_case"]:
                    continue
                if not can_start_now(rs, t, now):
                    continue

                remaining = rs.task_end - now
                if remaining <= 0:
                    continue

                victim.preemptions += 1
                base_remaining = (remaining / rs.cal.mult[cur.activity]) * (1.0 + PARAMS["preempt_restart_fraction"])

                # cancel running task, re-enqueue remaining pinned to same resource
                rs.busy_task = None
                rs.task_end = now

                enqueue(cur.case_id, cur.activity, cur.priority, cur.due, cur.prev_resource, cur.prev_activity, eligible_override=[rs.name])
                # overwrite base duration with remaining
                ready[-1].base_min = base_remaining

                ready.remove(t)
                start(rs, t, now)
                return

        # 2) Normal assignment: each idle resource greedily picks best feasible task
        b = backlog_by_family()
        for rs in res.values():
            if rs.busy_task is not None:
                continue
            if rs.cal.current_window(now) is None:
                continue

            best = None
            best_score = -1e18
            for t in ready:
                if rs.name not in t.eligible:
                    continue
                if not policy.allow_task(rs.name, t.diag_family, t.priority, b):
                    continue
                if not can_start_now(rs, t, now):
                    continue

                exp_proc = t.base_min * rs.cal.mult[t.activity]
                slack = t.due - now
                switch_pen = PARAMS["setup_overhead_min"] if (t.diag_family and rs.last_diag_family and rs.last_diag_family != t.diag_family) else 0.0
                hand_pen = PARAMS["handover_overhead_min"] if (t.prev_resource and t.prev_resource != rs.name) else 0.0
                sc = policy.score(t.priority, slack, exp_proc, switch_pen, hand_pen)
                if sc > best_score:
                    best_score = sc
                    best = t

            if best is not None:
                ready.remove(best)
                start(rs, best, now)

    # Main event loop
    while q:
        now, _, typ, payload = heapq.heappop(q)
        if now > horizon:
            break

        if typ == "arrival":
            idx = payload["idx"]
            pr_mix = payload["pr_mix"]
            ty_mix = payload["ty_mix"]

            pr = rng.choice(["P1", "P2", "P3"], p=[pr_mix["P1"] / 100, pr_mix["P2"] / 100, pr_mix["P3"] / 100])
            ct = rng.choice(["M", "E", "S", "ME", "ES"], p=[ty_mix["M"] / 100, ty_mix["E"] / 100, ty_mix["S"] / 100, ty_mix["ME"] / 100, ty_mix["ES"] / 100])
            cid = f"C{idx:04d}"
            due = now + SLA_MIN[pr]
            cases[cid] = Case(case_id=cid, arrival=now, priority=pr, ctype=ct, due=due)
            enqueue(cid, "Intake", pr, due, prev_r=None, prev_a=None)

        elif typ == "done":
            r = payload["r"]
            tid = payload["task_id"]
            rs = res.get(r)
            if rs is None or rs.busy_task is None or rs.busy_task.task_id != tid:
                continue

            task = rs.busy_task
            rs.busy_task = None
            c = cases[task.case_id]

            if task.activity == "Intake":
                if c.ctype in ("M", "E", "S"):
                    enqueue(c.case_id, {"M": "Mechanical", "E": "Electrical", "S": "Software"}[c.ctype], c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a="Intake")
                else:
                    hint = load_hint(now)
                    c.parallel_mode = policy.decide_parallel(c.priority, now, hint)
                    if c.ctype == "ME":
                        b1, b2 = "Mechanical", "Electrical"
                        cross = [rr for rr in resources if ("Mechanical" in calendars[rr].mult and "Electrical" in calendars[rr].mult)]
                    else:
                        b1, b2 = "Electrical", "Software"
                        cross = [rr for rr in resources if ("Electrical" in calendars[rr].mult and "Software" in calendars[rr].mult)]

                    if c.parallel_mode:
                        c.pending_branches = 2
                        enqueue(c.case_id, b1, c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a="Intake")
                        enqueue(c.case_id, b2, c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a="Intake")
                    else:
                        c.pending_branches = 1
                        c._seq_second = b2
                        enqueue(c.case_id, b1, c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a="Intake", eligible_override=cross if cross else eligible(b1))

            elif task.activity in ("Mechanical", "Electrical", "Software"):
                if c.ctype in ("ME", "ES") and c.parallel_mode:
                    c.pending_branches -= 1
                    if c.pending_branches == 0:
                        enqueue(c.case_id, "Validation", c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a=task.activity)

                elif c.ctype in ("ME", "ES") and not c.parallel_mode:
                    if hasattr(c, "_seq_second"):
                        b2 = c._seq_second
                        delattr(c, "_seq_second")
                        if c.ctype == "ME":
                            cross = [rr for rr in resources if ("Mechanical" in calendars[rr].mult and "Electrical" in calendars[rr].mult)]
                        else:
                            cross = [rr for rr in resources if ("Electrical" in calendars[rr].mult and "Software" in calendars[rr].mult)]
                        enqueue(c.case_id, b2, c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a=task.activity, eligible_override=cross if cross else eligible(b2))
                    else:
                        enqueue(c.case_id, "Validation", c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a=task.activity)

                else:
                    enqueue(c.case_id, "Validation", c.priority, c.due, prev_r=r, prev_a=task.activity)

            elif task.activity == "Validation":
                c.completion = now

        # dispatch on every event time
        dispatch(now)

    completed = [c for c in cases.values() if c.completion is not None]
    if not completed:
        return None

    delays = np.array([c.completion - c.arrival for c in completed], dtype=float)

    # Utilization against total availability over the horizon
    utils = []
    for rr, rs in res.items():
        avail = 0.0
        for d in range(days):
            for a, b in rs.cal.windows_for_day(d):
                avail += (b - a)
        utils.append(rs.busy_min / max(1.0, avail))

    def gini(x):
        xs = sorted(x)
        s = sum(xs)
        if s <= 0:
            return 0.0
        cum = 0.0
        for i, xi in enumerate(xs, 1):
            cum += i * xi
        n = len(xs)
        return 2 * cum / (n * s) - (n + 1) / n

    sla = {}
    for pr, lim in SLA_MIN.items():
        arr = np.array([c.completion - c.arrival for c in completed if c.priority == pr], dtype=float)
        sla[pr] = float(np.mean(arr <= lim)) if len(arr) else float("nan")

    metrics = dict(
        mean_delay=float(np.mean(delays)),
        p95_delay=float(np.quantile(delays, 0.95)),
        P1_SLA=sla["P1"],
        P2_SLA=sla["P2"],
        P3_SLA=sla["P3"],
        max_util=float(np.max(utils)),
        gini_util=float(gini(utils)),
        n_cases=int(len(completed)),
    )

    # Build interval log with required fields (plus extra attributes)
    log_df = pd.DataFrame(log_rows)
    base_ts = pd.Timestamp("2026-08-20 08:00:00")
    log_df["start_timestamp"] = base_ts + pd.to_timedelta(log_df["start_min"], unit="m")
    log_df["time:timestamp"] = base_ts + pd.to_timedelta(log_df["end_min"], unit="m")
    log_df = log_df[[
        "case:concept:name", "concept:name", "start_timestamp", "time:timestamp", "org:resource",
        "priority", "case_type", "policy", "scenario"
    ]]

    return metrics, log_df

# ============================================================
# Experiment runner + PM4Py demo

def run_experiment(reps: int = 10, seed0: int = 42):
    policies = [SpecializedFixed(), FullPooling(), HybridReserved()]
    scenarios = ["base", "U1", "U2", "U3", "U4", "U5"]

    rows = []
    sample_log = None

    for pol in policies:
        for sc in scenarios:
            for i in range(reps):
                out = simulate(pol, sc, seed=seed0 + i)
                if out is None:
                    continue
                m, log_df = out
                rows.append({"policy": pol.name, "scenario": sc, **m})
                if sample_log is None and pol.name == "hybrid_reserved" and sc == "base" and i == 0:
                    sample_log = log_df

    res = pd.DataFrame(rows)
    summary = res.groupby(["policy", "scenario"])[
        ["mean_delay", "p95_delay", "P1_SLA", "P2_SLA", "P3_SLA", "max_util", "gini_util"]
    ].agg(["mean", "std"]).round(3)

    print("\n=== Policy x scenario metrics (minutes; mean±std over seeds) ===")
    print(summary)

    # PM4Py demo on one interval log
    if sample_log is not None and not sample_log.empty:
        print("\n=== PM4Py demo on one simulated log (hybrid_reserved/base) ===")

        elog = pm4py.format_dataframe(
            sample_log,
            case_id="case:concept:name",
            activity_key="concept:name",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
            start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp",
        )

        # Case-level total service/waiting/sojourn times (replicated per event row)
        elog2 = pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time(
            elog,
            service_time_column="@@service_time",
            waiting_time_column="@@waiting_time",
            sojourn_time_column="@@sojourn_time",
            case_id_key="case:concept:name",
            activity_key="concept:name",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
            start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp",
        )

        # Correct labeling: these are CASE-level totals (seconds)
        case_totals = elog2.groupby("case:concept:name")[["@@service_time", "@@waiting_time", "@@sojourn_time"]].first()
        case_totals_min = (case_totals / 60.0).describe(percentiles=[0.5, 0.9, 0.95]).round(2)
        print("\nCase-level totals (minutes) summary:")
        print(case_totals_min)

        # Activity-level mean service time (seconds) -> minutes
        svc = pm4py.get_service_time(
            elog2,
            aggregation_measure="mean",
            activity_key="concept:name",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
            start_timestamp_key="start_timestamp",
            case_id_key="case:concept:name",
        )
        svc_min = {k: v / 60.0 for k, v in svc.items()}
        print("\nMean service time by activity (minutes):", {k: round(v, 2) for k, v in svc_min.items()})

        # Handover-of-work network
        sna = pm4py.discover_handover_of_work_network(
            elog2,
            resource_key="org:resource",
            timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
            case_id_key="case:concept:name",
        )
        top = sorted(sna.connections.items(), key=lambda kv: kv[1], reverse=True)[:8]
        print("\nTop handovers (from->to, weight):", [(f"{a}->{b}", round(w, 3)) for (a, b), w in top])

run_experiment(reps=10)

# 9. Limits and validation plan

## What cannot be concluded from this prompt alone

- The true arrival process may be non-Poisson, correlated with incident bursts, or have within-block seasonality (not provided).
- Service times may be correlated across activities within a case (e.g., complex software implies complex validation), which changes tail risk materially.
- The real meaning of “handover delay” and “setup time” could be non-resource time, bilateral time, or partially parallelizable; different interpretations change utilization and queueing.
- Preemption behavior in practice might be constrained by tooling (e.g., cannot safely pause diagnostics), and restart penalty might be larger than 10% of remaining if context is lost.
- Night/weekend staffing is not described; P2/P3 SLA feasibility is sensitive to off-hours coverage.

## Validation / pilot design (safe, decision-informative)

- **Shadow evaluation first**:
  - Run HYBRID-RESERVE’s decision logic in “recommendation only” mode for 2–4 weeks.
  - Log eligible sets, recommended assignments, and predicted slack; compare to actual outcomes.

- **Controlled pilot**:
  - A/B by day-of-week or by alternating half-days, with strict guardrail:
    - never reduce P1 handling capacity,
    - cap preemptions/day and measure restart penalty minutes.
  - Keep SILO as immediate rollback mode if preemption or switching becomes unsafe.

- **Backtesting with process mining**:
  - Reconstruct activity-level waiting/service times per stage (Intake queue, each diagnostic queue, Validation queue).
  - Compare handover networks and bottleneck migration before/after; look for:
    - reduced P1 tail,
    - controlled setup/handovers,
    - no explosion in P3 lateness beyond accepted miss rate.

- **Decision criterion for rollout**:
  - Achieve ≥90% P1 within 120 minutes on both base-like weeks and at least one stress week (surge or absence), while keeping max utilization ≤0.90 on average weeks and not increasing handover/setup burden above a chosen threshold.
