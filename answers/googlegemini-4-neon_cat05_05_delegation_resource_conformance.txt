## 2. Rule Interpretation

### Formalization of RR1–RR6
- **RR1 — Drafter/Approver Separation:** For every case $c$ (regardless of risk level or emergency status), letting $H(e)$ be the canonical human performing event $e$, $H(\text{Draft\_Change\_Request}_c) \neq H(\text{Approve\_Change}_c)$. Applies strictly to `LOW`-risk and `HIGH`-risk cases alike and is not waived by emergencies.
- **RR2 — Implementer/Verifier Separation:** For every case $c$, $H(\text{Implement\_Change}_c) \neq H(\text{Verify\_Implementation}_c)$. If either event uses an unattributed service account, conformance cannot be established. *(Control-objective nuance: RR2 enforces dual-person independence, not a specific verifier title; however, verification by a non-verifier or by the same human who approved the change weakens defense-in-depth.)*
- **RR3 — High-Risk Approval Authority:** For every case $c$ with `risk = HIGH` (and `emergency = false`), `Approve_Change` at timestamp $t$ must be executed by a human $h$ who, at timestamp $t$, either:
  1. holds the standing `QM` role ($t \in [\text{role\_start}, \text{role\_end}]$), **or**
  2. holds a valid QM delegation $d$ where: (a) $t \in [d.\text{start}, d.\text{end}]$, (b) $c.\text{scope} \in d.\text{scopes}$, (c) if $d$ requires principal absence, the principal is absent/formally unavailable at $t$, (d) $d$ is not a prohibited subdelegation, and (e) contemporaneous pre-event evidence establishes $d$ existed prior to $t$.
- **RR4 — Emergency Substitution:** For `risk = HIGH` and `emergency = true`, `Approve_Change` before `Implement_Change` may be performed by a standing `IC` or a valid `IC` delegate (evaluated under the same interval, scope, condition, and non-subdelegation criteria as RR3), **provided all three post-conditions hold**:
  1. the emergency flag and safety rationale are credible (substantive validity, not merely a flag set to `true`),
  2. a standing `QM` or valid `QM` delegate (whose scope covers the change!) executes `Retrospective_QM_Review`, and
  3. that review occurs within **two business days**.
- **RR5 — Identity Evidence:** Service accounts (`svc_qms`) lack human authority and fail conformance unless independent, reliable session evidence binds the specific transaction to an authorized human. All known human aliases must be normalized prior to RR1/RR2 evaluation.
- **RR6 — Suspicion vs. Proof:** Statistical concentration (repeated drafter–approver pairs, reciprocal low-risk approvals, shared workstations, handover centrality) serves strictly as a **risk indicator** for targeted audit/forensic inquiry, never as standalone proof of collusion or misconduct.

### Specific Edge-Case Rules
1. **Business-Day Counting for RR4:**
   - Business days are Monday–Friday excluding site holidays (none in this window). Saturday `2026-08-15` and Sunday `2026-08-16` are excluded.
   - Anchor date is the date of emergency approval/implementation (Day 0; both occurred on Tuesday `2026-08-11` for `CHG-505` and `CHG-510`).
   - Day 1 = Wednesday `2026-08-12`; Day 2 = Thursday `2026-08-13` (deadline either `48` business-day hours or `2026-08-13 23:59` UTC). Under **both** strict hour-based and end-of-day interpretations, `CHG-510` (`2026-08-12 10:00`, Day 1) is compliant on timeliness, whereas `CHG-505` (`2026-08-14 16:00`, Day 3) breaches the two-business-day limit.
2. **Action by a Principal While on Recorded Leave:**
   - **Standing authority:** HR-recorded annual leave does not automatically revoke a principal's underlying legal/standing role unless corporate policy explicitly suspends system authority during leave. Thus, if Asha personally authenticated and approved `CHG-504`, she possessed standing QM authority.
   - **Identity & governance impact:** Acting while on leave creates two immediate defects: (a) **elevated credential-compromise risk** requiring affirmative MFA/endpoint verification (because security cannot exclude credential sharing, conformance is `INDETERMINATE`), and (b) **delegation-condition ambiguity**—brief remote intervention by a principal on leave does not necessarily revoke an HR-documented absence for the entire leave window unless policy mandates automatic delegation suspension, but it blurs accountability by creating concurrent principal and delegate activity on the same day.
3. **Can a Later Database Entry Prove an Earlier Delegation?**
   - **No.** A database record created on `2026-08-12 09:15` proves only when the record was ingested into the QMS database; by itself it cannot prove authority existed on `2026-08-11 09:00` and could otherwise mask retroactive backdating.
   - However, a late DB entry does **not** invalidate an earlier action if independent, contemporaneous, pre-event documentary evidence exists. Here, `D-QM-01` is supported by a signed PDF timestamped `2026-08-09 18:00` (before the interval) and a documented system migration context. Evidentiary validity therefore turns on verifying the cryptographic/forensic authenticity of the `2026-08-09` PDF and migration audit trail—not on the `2026-08-12` DB timestamp.

def check_four_eyes(df: pd.DataFrame, act1: str, act2: str) -> dict:
    """Calls pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle and cross-checks pairs explicitly
    so violating cases are reported unambiguously across PM4Py versions."""
    fe_df = pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(
        df,
        act1,
        act2,
        activity_key="concept:name",
        timestamp_key="time:timestamp",
        case_id_key="case:concept:name",
        resource_key="org:resource",
    )
    fe_cases = set(fe_df["case:concept:name"].unique())

    a1 = df[df["concept:name"] == act1][["case:concept:name", "org:resource"]].rename(
        columns={"org:resource": "r1"}
    )
    a2 = df[df["concept:name"] == act2][["case:concept:name", "org:resource"]].rename(
        columns={"org:resource": "r2"}
    )
    pairs = a1.merge(a2, on="case:concept:name")
    same_res = set(pairs.loc[pairs["r1"] == pairs["r2"], "case:concept:name"])
    diff_res = set(pairs.loc[pairs["r1"] != pairs["r2"], "case:concept:name"])

    if fe_cases == same_res:
        violations = fe_cases
    elif fe_cases == diff_res:
        violations = set(pairs["case:concept:name"]) - fe_cases
    else:
        violations = same_res

        pairs.loc[
            (pairs["r1"] == "UNATTRIBUTED_SERVICE_ACCOUNT")
            | (pairs["r2"] == "UNATTRIBUTED_SERVICE_ACCOUNT"),
            "case:concept:name",
        ]
    )
    return {
        "pm4py_returned_cases": sorted(fe_cases),
        "violating_cases": sorted(violations),
        "unattributed_cases": sorted(unattr),
    }

## 6. Control Improvements

1. **Delegation Creation:** Replace ad-hoc PDFs and emails with a single electronic delegation workflow requiring principal cryptographic signature, explicit scope enumeration from controlled taxonomy values (clearly defining whether `cooling_system` is distinct from `production_equipment`), hard system-enforced `no_subdelegation` flags (blocking `D-IC-04`), and mandatory pre-activation approval before any delegated permission is provisioned.
2. **Effective-Time Recording:** Enforce bi-temporal data modeling in the QMS (`effective_from`/`effective_to` vs. `recorded_at`/`migrated_from_source_ref`). Require migration imports to attach immutable cryptographic hashes of source documents (e.g., `D-QM-01` PDF) so auditors can distinguish genuine migration lag from retroactive backdating.
3. **Principal Availability:** Integrate HR leave/sick-leave feeds directly with QMS IAM. Automatically (a) block or require step-up justification + dual acknowledgement when a principal on leave logs in (`CHG-504`), (b) suspend active delegate permissions when the delegate enters certified sick leave (`CHG-505`, `CHG-506`), and (c) define formal "weekend/off-duty unavailability" states so planned weekend delegations (`D-QM-02`) do not rely on ambiguous absence records.
4. **Alias Management:** Establish an authoritative HR-driven Identity Provider (IdP) with one unique immutable human Person-ID mapped to all usernames. Immediately decommission legacy aliases (`b.koch`) during system migrations, and enforce SoD rules (`RR1`/`RR2`) on the canonical Person-ID rather than raw username strings (preventing `CHG-508`).
5. **Individual Authentication:** Eliminate interactive human use of shared service accounts (`svc_qms`) on shop-floor kiosks (`WS-DM-04`, `WS-IV-02`). Require per-transaction re-authentication via badge-tap + FIDO2/PIN (21 CFR Part 11 / Annex 11 compliant e-signatures) so the QMS captures the actual human identity directly, eliminating reliance on indirect workstation/badge correlation (`CHG-503`, `CHG-509`).
6. **Emergency Review:** Hard-code RR4 guardrails into the QMS: (a) enforce RR1 self-approval blocking even during emergencies (`CHG-510`), (b) require mandatory Incident ID linkage (e.g., CI-44) and structured safety-rationale fields, (c) automatically verify that the retrospective reviewer holds standing QM or scope-matched QM delegation, and (d) trigger automated escalations at 24h and 36h business hours before the 2-business-day SLA expires (`CHG-505`).
7. **Pair Rotation:** Implement risk-based reviewer rotation or periodic random sampling by an independent QM/QA peer when any single drafter–approver pair exceeds a concentration threshold (e.g., $>60\%$ of HIGH-risk changes in a rolling 60-day window) or exhibits reciprocal approval patterns across risk tiers. Also restrict Approver = Verifier overlap on HIGH-risk changes (`CHG-509`).
8. **Network Monitoring:** Deploy scheduled automated PM4Py/pandas surveillance computing normalized self-loops, out-of-window authority checks, leave-vs-login collisions, and handover/working-together drift. Treat network concentration strictly as a triage input for quality-review sampling (RR6), never as an automated disciplinary trigger.
9. **Evidential Retention:** Synchronize retention policies across QMS logs, VPN/MFA telemetry, workstation Windows/AD security logs, physical badge logs, and CCTV (minimum 90–180 days hot retention for GMP change-control areas, immutable WORM storage for e-signature/delegation artifacts) so forensic gaps like missing CCTV in `CHG-503` or unverified VPN device telemetry in `CHG-504` do not render high-risk cases indeterminate.

## 7. Executive Conclusion

### Systemic Assessment
Across the 10 investigated change-control cases, apparent resource nonconformance is **not** a single monolithic failure—it reflects three distinct root causes:

1. **Genuine Authority and SoD Control Failures (Primary Risk — 5 confirmed cases, 1 highly probable):**
   - Clear non-compliance occurred in **`CHG-502`** (LOW-risk self-approval), **`CHG-505`** (late and out-of-scope emergency retrospective QM review during delegate sick leave), **`CHG-507`** (HIGH-risk approval by an unauthorized Independent Verifier), **`CHG-508`** (alias-masked self-approval), and **`CHG-510`** (HIGH-risk self-approval via prohibited email subdelegation + out-of-scope retrospective review), with **`CHG-506`** strongly indicative of unauthorized credential use during certified sick leave.
   - Systemically, the QMS lacks **preventive technical controls**: it permitted self-approval, out-of-role approval, active legacy aliases, and account use during certified medical leave.
2. **Identity and Data-Quality Deficiencies (Material Contributor — 4 cases affected):**
   - **`CHG-501`** appears to be a valid substitution obscured by a QMS migration ingestion delay (`D-QM-01` DB lag).
   - **`CHG-503`** and **`CHG-509`** expose shared service-account (`svc_qms`) logging defects, though external telemetry sharply distinguishes them (`CHG-509` has strong attribution to Eva; `CHG-503` cannot rule out self-verification by Diego).
   - **`CHG-508`** was directly enabled by unmerged IAM aliases (`b.koch`/`u_bk`), and **`CHG-504`** cannot be resolved without MFA/VPN endpoint forensics.
3. **Organizational-Pattern Risk (Secondary Governance Weakness):**
   - Heavy Clara–Ben pairing (18/23 HIGH-risk production changes), reciprocal LOW-risk approvals, and Eva continuing verifications after leaving the Independent Verifier role (and both approving and verifying `CHG-509`) erode defense-in-depth, even where formal RR1–RR6 rules are technically met. Under RR6, however, these patterns remain **unproven risk indicators**, not evidence of collusion.

### Key Uncertainties
Final adjudication remains open on (i) whether `u_bk` activity during Ben's sick leave (`CHG-506`, `CHG-505`) and `u_as` VPN activity during Asha's leave (`CHG-504`) reflect credential compromise versus unrecorded personal work, (ii) who physically operated `svc_qms` at `WS-DM-04` in `CHG-503`, (iii) the cryptographic authenticity of the `D-QM-01` signed PDF (`CHG-501`), and (iv) whether site SOPs classify `cooling_system` under `production_equipment` and weekend off-duty status as "principal absent."

### Three Highest-Priority Investigations
1. **Forensic Investigation of Ben Koch's Sick-Leave Approvals (`CHG-506` and `CHG-505`):** Highest immediate integrity risk—an office workstation approved a HIGH-risk production change (`CHG-506`) under `u_bk` during certified sick leave despite Ben's explicit denial, and `u_bk` also closed an overdue emergency review (`CHG-505`) the next day. Pull workstation IDs, AD/MFA logs, badge/CCTV records, and evaluate potential credential misuse (including whether a drafter covertly bypassed RR1).
2. **Unattributed `svc_qms` Verification and Potential Self-Verification (`CHG-503`):** High GMP product-quality risk on a HIGH-risk production equipment change where circumstantial evidence points toward implementer Diego (`WS-DM-04` + badge entry). Audit full room access logs, offline verification test records, and perform an independent re-verification of the implemented equipment change if attribution cannot be established.
3. **Emergency Governance & Preventive QMS RBAC/SoD Audit (`CHG-510`, `CHG-507`, `CHG-505`, `CHG-508`):** Address the systemic control breakdown that allowed unauthorized HIGH-risk approvals, prohibited subdelegation (`D-IC-04`), unblocked self-approvals, and legacy alias bypasses (`b.koch`/`u_bk`), alongside retrospective QA impact assessment of `CHG-507` and `CHG-510` and verification of Asha's `CHG-504` VPN session and the `D-QM-01` PDF timestamp.
