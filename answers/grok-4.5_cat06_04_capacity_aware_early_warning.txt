## 1. Decision rule

**Eligibility (hard gates)**  
1. Stage gate: intervene only if current stage  {Triage, Diagnosis Started}. `Diagnosis Complete` and later  ineligible (effect  0).  
2. Repeat-swarm gate: no second swarm on the same case unless a material new event is logged (new customer artifact, stage regression, priority re-class, or 0.10 calibrated probability jump not explained by refresh alone).  
3. Capacity gate: at most 4 slots/day; policy default is hold 1 slot for unlisted regulatory/priority arrivals after 12:00 unless the marginal robust net benefit of a 4th case clearly exceeds the option value of reserve.  
4. Data-quality gate: if a pivotal live feature is imputed, cap the case at “watch / manual review” unless time-to-SLA < 2 h and calibrated risk remains high after a downward uncertainty discount.

**Probability robustification (before scoring)**  
- SME: add +0.06 to `p_now` (midpoint of 0.04–0.09 under-prediction).  
- Strategic Enterprise: subtract 0.08 from `p_now` (midpoint of 0.05–0.12 over-prediction).  
- Public Sector: no mean shift; keep ±0.03 band.  
- Non-Native-Language Support (NNLS): no mean shift; apply an explicit confidence discount (multiply expected effect by 0.6–0.7) and flag for human double-check.  
- Treat `p_now` as observational only; never equate it to P(breach | swarm). Use the supplied absolute risk-reduction (ARR) ranges as uncertain effect estimates, not point causal effects.  
- For sharp jumps with imputation (I-06), haircut ARR by ~30% until the missing feature is repaired or a human confirms the signal.

**Alert persistence / minimum evidence change**  
- Same-case re-alert within 90 min with |p| < 0.05 and no new event  suppress (do not consume analyst attention or slot).  
- Re-alert allowed if: new event, stage change, or calibrated p  0.10, or time-to-SLA crosses an urgency band (< 2 h).  

**Prioritization score (deployable)**  
For each eligible case compute a **robust expected net benefit (RENB)**:

RENB  (ARR_low × Value)  swarm cost  false-alarm burden  
then adjust:  
RENB_adj = RENB × urgency_weight × segment_confidence  spillover_share  

where  
- ARR_low = lower bound of supplied range (pessimistic direct effect),  
- urgency_weight = 1.0 if time-to-SLA  3 h and stage = Diagnosis Started; 0.8 if  2 h Triage; 0.6 if 3–6 h; 0.4 if > 6 h still in Triage,  
- segment_confidence  {1.0 Public, 0.85 SME, 0.75 Strategic after calibration, 0.6 NNLS},  
- spillover_share  (0.02 × 6 × median untreated diagnosis-case value × 0.5) / n_selected, charged to each selected case under base congestion.  

Rank by RENB_adj. Intervene top cases while slots (minus reserve) remain and RENB_adj > 0 under the adverse band. Ties broken by shorter time-to-SLA, then by diagnosis-stage over triage.  

**Portfolio constraint**: re-evaluate spillover after tentative selection; if 3 diagnosis-stage untreated cases would absorb stacked spillovers, drop the lowest RENB_adj case or keep the reserve.

## 4. Repeated alerts and temporal policy

**Cool-down:** 90 minutes after an alert that did not produce a swarm, or 6 hours after a swarm, whichever applies.  
**Suppression:** automatic if refresh-only (|p| < 0.05, same stage, no new artifacts). Suppressed alerts are logged for model monitoring but do not page the duty manager.  
**Escalation:** if time-to-SLA enters < 2 h and calibrated p > 0.65, or if a new high-value segment marker appears, break cool-down and re-score.  
**Re-entry after swarm:** only on material new event (defined in §1) AND revised RENB_adj > 0 using a fresh ARR estimate (default: shrink prior ARR by 50% unless evidence shows the first swarm failed for a remediable reason).  
**Batch timing:** recompute portfolio scores at 12:00, 14:00, and on any regulatory arrival; do not thrash slots more often.

**I-05 vs I-07:**  
- I-05: third alert, p  +0.02, explicitly “no new event”  pure refresh noise. Apply suppression/cool-down; do not swarm. Count toward false-alarm and stability metrics.  
- I-07: third alert but new customer diagnostic log = material evidence change; time-to-SLA 1.5 h; SME upward calibration. Allow re-entry, score, and select. The slight drop in raw p (0.750.72) is dominated by new information and urgency, not treated as all-clear.

## 5. Fairness and feedback-loop risks

**Value-based starvation:** High-€ Strategic cases (I-04, I-08) can crowd out SME/Public cases (I-01, I-07, I-10).  
Safeguard: enforce a daily minimum service floor — at least one non-Strategic eligible case in the selected set when such cases have RENB_adj > 0 (satisfied today by I-07). Track intervention rate by segment weekly; if SME/Public rate < 40% of their share of high-risk eligible volume for 2 consecutive weeks, temporarily boost segment_confidence or add a diversity slack in the knapsack.

**NNLS uncertainty (I-09):** Sparse calibration risks both neglect and over-confident intervention.  
Safeguard: NNLS cases require human confirmation before slot use; effect priors widened; separate hold-out audit every month; do not use NNLS outcomes alone to update global ARR until n  30 intervened.

**Differential calibration:** SME under-predicted, Strategic over-predicted  raw `p_now` ranking would systematically over-serve Strategic and under-serve SME.  
Safeguard: mandatory segment offsets (§1) before scoring; publish calibrated vs raw leaderboards; recalibrate offsets monthly on recent closed cases.

**Selective intervention  label bias:** Swarmed cases that avoid breach look “easy” in observational logs; untreated spillovers look riskier; future models may learn “high value + swarm” rather than true risk.  
Safeguards: (i) log intervention as a feature and never drop it; (ii) maintain a small randomized uplift set (when capacity surplus  1 and ethics/contract allow) or quasi-experimental discontinuity around RENB threshold; (iii) train breach models with inverse-propensity or doubly robust adjustment using recorded RENB scores as propensity inputs; (iv) shadow-predict “what p would have been without policy.”

**Spillover incidence:** Diagnosis-queue delays fall on non-intervened cases, often lower-value or lower-alert cases, potentially amplifying disparity.  
Safeguards: measure post-slot breach and cycle-time deltas for untreated diagnosis-stage cases by segment; cap simultaneous swarms at 3 when diagnosis congestion > threshold; rotate “spillover protection” (temporary priority credit) to segments that absorbed spillovers in the prior week.

## 6. Monitoring and recalibration plan

**Operational (daily/real-time)**  
- Slot utilization, reserve hit rate, time from alert  swarm start, specialist interruption minutes.  
- Cool-down/suppression counts (I-05-type vs I-07-type).  
- Immediate process: % of swarms executed before Diagnosis Complete (target > 95%).

**Predictive (weekly)**  
- Segment-wise calibration plots (expected vs observed breach) on non-intervened and overall; update SME (+)/Strategic () offsets if drift > 0.03.  
- Alert precision at the RENB > 0 threshold; stability of `p_now` under refresh-only.

**Causal-effect (monthly or per 40 swarms)**  
- Primary: difference-in-differences on time-to-Diagnosis-Complete and breach, comparing swarmed vs matched eligible-unswarmed (match on stage, time-to-SLA, segment, calibrated p).  
- Secondary: interrupted time series on diagnosis queue delay per slot (validate 8–20 min and 0.01–0.03 spillover bands).  
- If randomized uplift slots exist, analyze as Bernoulli trial on breach.  
- Shrink ARR priors toward new estimates; widen ranges if posterior variance high (especially NNLS).

**Capacity & spillover (per shift)**  
- Congestion dashboard: diagnosis WIP, stacked spillover probability, aggregate delay minutes.  
- Trigger: if realized delay > 25 min/slot or untreated breach uplift > 0.03, reduce max daily swarms from 4 to 3 or raise RENB bar.

**Fairness (weekly)**  
- Intervention rate, suppression rate, and spillover-exposed rate by segment and language support.  
- Value vs volume Pareto; floor compliance (§5).  
- Escalation if any eligible segment receives 0 interventions while contributing > 15% of high-risk volume.

**Policy-induced drift & rule change**  
- Monitor propensity of intervention as function of value and segment; if model re-train without intervention adjustment, block deployment.  
- Change thresholds/allocation when: (i) calibration offset drifts > 0.05, (ii) estimated ARR midpoints move > 25% relative, (iii) net-impact under base scenario negative for 3 consecutive weeks, or (iv) fairness floor breaches twice in 4 weeks.  
- Any rule change requires a recorded A/B or shadowed week before full cutover.  
- Quarterly review of reserve policy vs actual regulatory arrival rate.

This policy is deliberately conservative on I-03 (stage), I-05 (no new evidence), and I-08 (weak effect + high FA), uses lower-bound effects for ranking, holds one slot, and makes calibration, spillover, and feedback-loop risks first-class operational controls rather than afterthoughts.
