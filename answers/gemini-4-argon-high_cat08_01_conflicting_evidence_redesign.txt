# 1. Current-process diagnosis

### The Core Contradiction: Local Speed and Unit-Cost Illusion vs. End-to-End Quality and Compliance Failure
The executive dilemma stems from an aggregation fallacy in how the current process is measured. At the local activity level, the dominant automated path (`Intake claim -> Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement -> Quality/compliance close`) looks highly attractive: it handles the majority of volume (`3,600` of `5,000` claims enter `Auto eligibility screen`; `2,550` flow directly to `Fast approval`), costs only **EUR 0.20 per screen** versus **EUR 34–52/hour** for specialist reviews, and achieves median transition times of **0.5h** (`Intake -> Auto screen`), **0.4h** (`Auto screen -> Fast approval`), and **1.8h** (`Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement`).

However, synthesizing the process-mining artifacts reveals that this speed and cost advantage is largely an illusion created by **outdated, segment-blind auto-screen rules** that push inappropriate claims onto the fast path:
1. **Eroded Cost Advantage:** While `Fast approval` cases are **31% cheaper before rework**, that advantage collapses to **only 9% after accounting for complaints, reshipments, and service credits**. A single wrong replacement costs **EUR 410** for high-value devices and **EUR 95** for low-risk accessories—wiping out the labor savings of dozens of automated screens.
2. **Severe Segment Mismatch:** The fast path works well for **Premium low-risk accessories** (`18%` of volume, **8h** median cycle time, **6%** complaint rate, avoiding high >24h service-credit exposure) and **Standard low-risk accessories** (`25%` of volume, **13h** median, **8%** complaint rate). Conversely, when **Premium high-value devices** (`14%` share) and **Standard high-value devices** (`21%` share) leak onto the fast path, complaint/reopen rates spike to **22%** and **18%** respectively, compared to just **5%** and **6%** on the reviewed/repair path.
3. **Chronic Upstream Rework and Starvation on the Slow Path:** The reviewed/repair path delivers superior technical outcomes (**3.3%** complaint rate on `Repair quote -> Customer approval -> Repair device` vs. **14.1%** on `Fast approval`), but suffers from extreme queuing delays (**96h** median for Premium high-value devices; **120h** median for Standard high-value devices; **140h** median for the `22%` `Unclear defect / missing evidence` segment).

### Synthesis Across Process-Mining Artifacts

1. **Frequency Directly-Follows Graph (DFG) — Volume Leakage, Ping-Pong Loops, and Abandonment:**
   - Of `5,000` cases, `Auto eligibility screen` processes `3,600` (`72%`) and routes `2,550` (`51%` of all claims) straight to `Fast approval`, even though low-risk accessories represent only `43%` (`18% + 25%`) of the claim mix. At least `8%+` of total volume (and significantly more given evidence/risk splits) consists of high-value or unclear-defect claims improperly fast-tracked.
   - `Manual eligibility review` is a massive convergence hub: beyond its `1,400` direct inflows from `Intake claim`, it absorbs `890` rework inflows from `Request missing evidence` and `310` reopened claims from `Customer complaint/reopen` (`2,600` total inflows).
   - A severe ping-pong loop exists between `Manual eligibility review -> Request missing evidence` (`780` edges) and `Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` (`890` edges).
   - Customer attrition is concentrated at friction points: `Request missing evidence -> Claim abandoned` (`320` cases) and `Customer approval -> Claim abandoned` (`70` cases), totaling `390` abandoned claims (`7.8%` of all cases).
   - `Customer complaint/reopen` receives `510` total transitions (`260` from `Dispatch replacement`, `140` from `Fast approval`, `80` from `Repair quote`, `30` from `Repair device`), feeding `310` back to `Manual eligibility review` and `120` to `Escalation board`.

2. **Performance DFG — Heavy Right-Skew (Mean vs. Median/p90) and Post-Delivery Failure Tails:**
   - Every manual transition exhibits heavy right-skew where `p90` is `3x–4x` the median, signaling severe capacity queuing rather than complex processing work: `Intake -> Manual eligibility review` (mean **7.5h**, median **5.0h**, p90 **18.0h**), `Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote` (mean **22.0h**, median **15.0h**, p90 **60.0h**), and `Fraud/risk review -> Repair quote` (mean **21.0h**, median **16.0h**, p90 **50.0h**).
   - The evidence-collection loop is the single largest time sink: `Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` takes a mean of **34.0h** (median **26.0h**, p90 **88.0h**). When combined with `Manual eligibility review -> Request missing evidence` (mean **14.0h**, p90 **36.0h**), a single evidence cycle adds **48h mean** (**124h at p90**).
   - Downstream failures surface days after fulfillment: `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` shows a mean of **96.0h** (median **70.0h**, p90 **180.0h**), and `Fast approval -> Customer complaint/reopen` shows a mean of **72.0h** (median **50.0h**, p90 **150.0h**). Temporal profile diagnostics confirm the `96.0h` tail is driven by customers receiving an **incorrect replacement part** and reopening the claim after delivery.

3. **Conformance Checking (Token-Based Replay, Alignments, Precision, and Temporal Profiles):**
   - **Deceptive Token-Replay Fitness vs. Trace Fitness:** Token-based replay reports a seemingly healthy **0.91 log fitness**, yet **only 68% of traces are fully fitting** (`32%` or `1,600` cases violate normative policy). Token replay masks serious single-transition bypasses across multi-step traces.
   - **Normative Rule Violations & Alignment Diagnostics:**
     - **Rule R1** (*Claims > EUR 400 require Manual eligibility or Fraud/risk review before Fast approval*) is systematically violated by move-on-log deviations where high-value devices pass through `Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` without risk review.
     - **Rule R2** (*Replacement dispatch requires confirmed evidence completeness, except Premium low-risk accessories*) is violated by move-on-log deviations of `Fast approval` before evidence completeness on Standard and high-value claims.
     - **Rule R3** (*Reopened claims must pass Manual eligibility review before final close or denial*) is violated by the `120` cases routed `Customer complaint/reopen -> Escalation board`, which flow directly to `Dispatch replacement` (`65`), `Repair device` (`45`), or `Claim denied` (`10`) without returning through `Manual eligibility review`.
   - **Low Model Precision:** Unauthorized spaghetti combinations of auto-approval, risk review, and repair degrade model precision and predictability.

4. **Rework Statistics — Multi-Touch Loops and Post-Repair Failures:**
   - **26%** of cases entering `Request missing evidence` loop through it **at least twice**, explaining the **140h** median cycle time for the `Unclear defect / missing evidence` cohort.
   - `Rework repair` (`Quality check -> Rework repair: 45`, mean **14.0h**, p90 **36.0h**; `Rework repair -> Repair device: 42`) hits **12.5%** of repaired high-value devices versus **4.0%** of low-risk devices, showing technical diagnostic gaps in high-value repairs.

5. **Resource Utilization & Cost Economics — The 82% Utilization Cliff and Misallocated Capacity:**
   - `Manual eligibility review` (`7` reviewers $\times$ `36` productive hours/week = **252 hours/week** at **EUR 34/h**) operates near or above its **82% utilization tipping point** (~**206.6 hours/week**), only because `1,200` (`890 + 310`) of its `2,600` inflows are avoidable rework and reopenings.
   - `Fraud/risk review` (`2` specialists at **EUR 52/h**) bottlenecks sharply on Mondays and post-marketing campaigns (`Auto screen -> Fraud/risk` p90 **28.0h**; `Manual review -> Fraud/risk` p90 **44.0h**).
   - `Repair quote` (`3` technicians at **EUR 45/h**) suffers artificial delays (mean **21–22h**, p90 **50–60h**) not from technician labor, but from **waiting on external/back-office part-price confirmation**.
   - `Quality check` (`2` inspectors at **EUR 38/h**) is **chronically underused** (handling only `360` repair checks), while `2,365` replacement dispatches exit with zero part-compatibility verification.

# 2. Redesign A: Segment- & Risk-Stratified Triage with Pre-Dispatch Technical Gate

### Redesigned Process Flow
1. **Context-Aware Auto Eligibility Screen (Deployable in 2 Weeks):** Reconfigure `Auto eligibility screen` decision tables to ingest claim segment, asset value (EUR 400 threshold), defect clarity, and initial evidence metadata at `Intake claim`.
2. **Three Deterministic Routing Tracks:**
   - **Track A1 — Zero-Touch Express Replacement (Premium Low-Risk Accessories, 18% share):** Immediately routed `Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` without waiting for secondary evidence confirmation (explicitly permitted under **R2** exception).
   - **Track A2 — Automated Evidence-Verified Fast Track (Standard Low-Risk Accessories, 25% share):** Routed through automated digital evidence completeness check at `Auto eligibility screen`; once complete, proceeds to `Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` (satisfying **R2** while keeping cycle time near ~13–20h, well below the **72h** customer tolerance).
   - **Track A3 — Mandatory Risk/Eligibility Gate (All Claims > EUR 400 / High-Value Devices, 35% share):** Blocked from direct `Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval`. Low-anomaly high-value claims route to `Manual eligibility review`; high-anomaly claims route to `Fraud/risk review`, enforcing **100% R1 and R2 conformance**.
3. **Repurposed Pre-Dispatch Technical Gate:** Reallocate idle capacity from the **2 underused `Quality check` inspectors** (EUR 38/h) to perform a lightweight **Pre-Dispatch Part-Selection Check** between `Fast approval` (or `Escalation board`) and `Dispatch replacement` for all high-value replacement approvals and ambiguous accessory SKUs.
4. **R3 Compliance Hard-Stop on Reopens:** Modify workflow routing so `Customer complaint/reopen` must pass through a prioritized `Manual eligibility review` compliance sign-off before or immediately after `Escalation board` prior to `Dispatch replacement`, `Repair device`, or `Claim denied`.

### Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
- **Segments:** Premium high-value devices (`14%`), Standard high-value devices (`21%`), and Premium/Standard low-risk accessories (`43%`).
- **Paths & Activities:** `Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval` (`2,550` edges), `Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` (`2,300` edges), `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` (`260` edges), `Quality check` resource pool, and `Customer complaint/reopen -> Escalation board` (`120` edges).

### Mechanism of Improvement
- **Eliminates High-Cost Wrong Replacements:** Catching incorrect replacement part selections before shipment via underused `Quality check` inspectors adds **6–18 hours** to replacement dispatch for inspected items, but prevents the **96.0h mean (180.0h p90)** post-delivery complaint loop and avoids the **EUR 410** (high-value) / **EUR 95** (accessory) wrong-replacement penalty.
- **Closes R1, R2, and R3 Conformance Gaps:** Hard-coding the EUR 400 threshold and evidence check into `Auto eligibility screen` eliminates move-on-log alignment deviations and pushes fitting traces from **68% toward >92%**.
- **Protects Premium Accessory SLAs:** Keeping Premium low-risk accessories on an unencumbered fast path preserves their **8h median** cycle time and avoids the **>24h** service-credit cliff.

### Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
- **Sacrifices Fast-Path Speed for High-Value Claims & Adds Manual Review Load:** Updating auto-screen rules increases inflow to `Manual eligibility review` by **15–35%** (+210 to +490 cases). High-value claims previously slipping through in **20–28h** will now take longer.
- **Leaves Upstream Evidence Ping-Pong and Repair Quote Delays Unresolved:** Does not fix the `Request missing evidence` multi-touch loop (`26%` repeat rate) or the part-price confirmation delay in `Repair quote`.

# 3. Redesign B: Guided Front-End Evidence Architecture & Decoupled Repair-Quote Pipeline

### Redesigned Process Flow
1. **Interactive Guided Evidence Portal at Intake:** Embed a structured, device-specific diagnostic and evidence-upload portal into `Intake claim` and `Request missing evidence`. The portal enforces mandatory fields (serial number, symptom tree, photo validation) before submission and replaces generic "missing evidence" emails with single-pass guided checklists.
2. **Auto-Routing of Complete Evidence Back to Auto-Screen:** When a claim in `Request missing evidence` submits validated artifacts via the portal, low-risk claims return to `Auto eligibility screen` (EUR 0.20) rather than defaulting `890` cases into `Manual eligibility review` (EUR 34/h).
3. **Decoupled & Pre-Indexed Part-Price Confirmation for `Repair quote`:** Eliminate the passive waiting time in `Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote` (mean **22.0h**, p90 **60.0h**) and `Fraud/risk review -> Repair quote` (mean **21.0h**, p90 **50.0h**) by:
   - Pre-indexing standard part prices for top device SKUs so `Repair quote` technicians (`3` at EUR 45/h) can generate instant quotes.
   - Triggering asynchronous vendor part-price requests *in parallel* the moment a high-value claim enters `Manual eligibility review` or `Fraud/risk review`, so pricing is already confirmed when the technician opens the case.
4. **Proactive Status-Visibility Tracker & Automated Quote Approval Nudges:** Provide real-time milestone tracking to Standard customers (unlocking their **72-hour tolerance** without complaints) and automated SMS/one-click approval for `Repair quote -> Customer approval` (currently mean **30.0h**, p90 **80.0h**, with `70` abandonments).

### Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
- **Segments:** `Unclear defect / missing evidence` (`22%` share, **140h** median cycle time) and repaired `Premium/Standard high-value devices` (`35%` share, **96h–120h** median on reviewed/repair path).
- **Paths & Activities:** `Auto screen / Manual review -> Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` (`1,430` inflows, `890` return edges, mean **34.0h**), `Manual/Fraud review -> Repair quote -> Customer approval -> Repair device`, and `Rework repair` (`45` edges).

### Mechanism of Improvement
- **Breaks the 82% Utilization Cliff in Manual Review:** Reducing repeated evidence requests by **30–45%** cuts hundreds of ping-pong loops (`780` out / `890` back). Combined with routing validated low-risk re-submissions to `Auto eligibility screen`, `Manual eligibility review` inflow drops significantly, pulling utilization well below the **82% queueing threshold** and collapsing the **18.0h–36.0h p90** waits.
- **Compresses Reviewed/Repair Cycle Time by 35–50% Without Sacrificing Quality:** By removing part-price wait states in `Repair quote` and speeding up `Customer approval`, the reviewed/repair path (which already boasts a **3.3%** complaint rate vs. **14.1%** on `Fast approval`) becomes fast enough to serve High-Value devices without tempting managers to use risky `Fast approval` shortcuts.
- **Reduces High-Value Repair Rework (12.5%):** Better upfront structured symptom/photo capture at intake equips `Repair device` technicians with accurate failure data, reducing internal `Quality check -> Rework repair` loops.

### Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
- **Increases Digital Abandonment Risk:** The new evidence portal may **increase abandonment (`Claim abandoned`, currently `390` cases) among customers with low digital literacy** who struggle with structured self-service uploads.
- **Leaves Outdated Auto-Screen Rules and Wrong-Replacement Dispatches Intact:** Does not directly stop high-value claims from leaking through `Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval` or add a pre-dispatch part check.

# 4. Redesign C: Value-Tiered Dual-Network Operating Model & Cross-Functional Pod Pooling

### Redesigned Process Flow
Instead of running all five segments through one monolithic workflow with sequential handoffs across five siloed departments, Redesign C bifurcates the process at `Intake claim` into **two distinct end-to-end operating networks** based on customer tier and economic risk:

1. **Stream 1 — Premium & High-Value Integrated Diagnostic Pod (Covers Premium Low-Risk 18%, Premium High-Value 14%, Standard High-Value 21%):**
   - **Premium Low-Risk Accessories (`18%`):** Instant `Auto eligibility screen -> Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` (**8h** median preserved; R2-compliant).
   - **High-Value Devices (`35%`):** Routed to an **Integrated High-Value Pod** combining `Manual eligibility review`, `Fraud/risk review`, `Repair quote` technicians, and `Quality check` inspectors into a single cross-functional cell.
   - Instead of sequential queues (`Intake -> Manual review [7.5h] -> Fraud/risk [18.0h] -> Repair quote [22.0h]`, totaling **47.5h mean** in handoff waits alone), the pod performs **single-touch triage + technical scoping**: confirming eligibility/risk (satisfying **R1/R2**), selecting the exact replacement SKU or pre-approved repair scope (eliminating the **EUR 410** wrong-replacement error), and Reserve-Capacity SLA slotting for Premium claims.
2. **Stream 2 — Standard & Unclear-Defect Asynchronous Lane with Load-Smoothing & Assisted Fallback (Covers Standard Low-Risk 25% & Unclear Defect 22%):**
   - **Demand Load-Smoothing for Fraud/Risk:** Standard claims requiring `Fraud/risk review` on Mondays or post-marketing campaigns are held in an asynchronous buffer with **proactive 72-hour SLA status visibility** (leveraging Standard customers' stated tolerance for **72h** completion when status visibility is good), smoothing workload across Tuesday–Friday for the `2` Fraud/risk specialists.
   - **Assisted Evidence Concierge Fallback:** If a customer stalls in `Request missing evidence` for >12 hours or fails a digital upload once, the case triggers a 5-minute assisted phone/chat capture by a cross-trained reviewer rather than re-entering the passive email ping-pong loop (`26%` repeat rate) or abandoning.
   - **Escalation Board De-Bottlenecking:** Replace the single **45-minute daily `Escalation board` slot** for routine reopens with empowered pod leads who hold `Manual eligibility review` authority (satisfying **R3** immediately), reserving the daily `Escalation board` strictly for disputed denials (`Claim denied`).

### Target Segments, Paths, and Activities
- **Segments:** Explicitly differentiates all 5 segments by SLA sensitivity (Premium 24h vs. Standard 72h tolerance) and asset risk (EUR 95 vs. EUR 410 error cost).
- **Paths & Activities:** Sequential handoffs between `Manual eligibility review`, `Fraud/risk review`, and `Repair quote`; Monday/campaign peaks in `Fraud/risk review`; the 45-minute `Escalation board` bottleneck (`Customer complaint/reopen -> Escalation board: 54.0h mean, 130.0h p90`); and `Request missing evidence -> Claim abandoned` (`320` edges).

### Mechanism of Improvement
- **Eliminates Inter-Departmental Queue Multiplication:** Collapsing sequential handoffs (`Manual -> Fraud -> Quote`) into a single pod review eliminates two massive queuing stages, bringing high-value reviewed cycle times down dramatically while maintaining the **5–6%** low complaint rate of reviewed paths.
- **Protects Monday/Campaign Bottlenecks via SLA Arbitrage:** Exploiting the **72-hour tolerance** of Standard customers (via status visibility) protects `Fraud/risk review` (`2` specialists) and `Manual eligibility review` (`7` reviewers) from exceeding **82% utilization** during Monday/campaign surges, preserving instant capacity for Premium claims.
- **Prevents Both Digital Abandonment and Escalation Gridlock:** Assisted fallback prevents the digital-literacy abandonment penalty of pure self-service, while delegating routine reopen authority to pod leads eliminates the **54.0h mean / 130.0h p90** wait for the 45-minute daily `Escalation board`.

### Deliberate Sacrifices and Unresolved Issues
- **Deliberately Slows Standard Claims During Peak Volumes:** Standard low-risk and high-value claims submitted during Monday/campaign spikes are intentionally buffered toward **48–72 hours** rather than processed FIFO, sacrificing average speed for Standard segments to guarantee Premium SLAs and specialist stability.
- **High Organizational & Change-Management Complexity:** Requires re-skilling, workflow re-architecting, and breaking departmental silos across Manual Reviewers (EUR 34/h), Fraud Specialists (EUR 52/h), Quote Technicians (EUR 45/h), and Quality Inspectors (EUR 38/h).

# 6. Failure modes and displaced bottlenecks

### Redesign A: Stratified Triage & Pre-Dispatch Gate
- **Failure Mode 1 — The 82% Utilization Tipping-Point Collapse in `Manual eligibility review`:** Updating `Auto eligibility screen` rules increases manual-review inflow by **15–35%** (+210 to +490 claims on top of `1,400` direct intake + `890` evidence returns + `310` reopens). Because the `7` reviewers (`252` productive hours/week) already operate near the **82% utilization cliff**, this added volume will trigger non-linear queue growth, inflating `Intake -> Manual eligibility review` p90 well beyond **18.0h** and causing widespread **>24h service-credit penalties** on Premium claims.
- **Failure Mode 2 — Rubber-Stamping or Over-Inspection at the Pre-Dispatch Gate:** If the pre-dispatch gate (adding **6–18 hours**) is applied indiscriminately to low-risk accessories or lacks a clear part-compatibility lookup tool, the `2` `Quality check` inspectors will either rubber-stamp approvals (failing to prevent the **EUR 410** wrong replacement) or slow Premium claims past the 24-hour service-credit threshold.
- **Displaced Bottleneck Migration:** The bottleneck migrates from **downstream post-delivery complaint handling (`Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen`)** upstream to **`Manual eligibility review` and `Fraud/risk review`** (especially on Mondays and after marketing campaigns), as well as creating a new micro-queue ahead of `Dispatch replacement` when repair campaigns simultaneously consume the `2` `Quality check` inspectors.

### Redesign B: Guided Evidence Portal & Decoupled Quote Pipeline
- **Failure Mode 1 — Silent Attrition via Digital-Literacy Abandonment:** Replacing human-initiated evidence requests with a rigid self-service portal can cause less tech-savvy customers to drop out when photo validation or diagnostic trees reject their input. `Request missing evidence -> Claim abandoned` (currently `320` cases) could surge, artificially lowering cycle times and rework metrics by driving away legitimate claimants (**survivorship bias**).
- **Failure Mode 2 — Stale Pre-Indexed Part Prices Driving Quote Disputes or Margin Loss:** If pre-indexed catalog part prices are outdated or parallel vendor confirmations arrive late for non-standard defects, `Repair quote` technicians will either issue inaccurate quotes (increasing `Repair quote -> Customer complaint/reopen`, currently `80` cases, or `Customer approval -> Claim abandoned`, currently `70` cases) or revert to manual waiting.
- **Displaced Bottleneck Migration:** As `Manual eligibility review` and `Repair quote` release cases 35–50% faster and fewer claims abandon in evidence collection, the bottleneck migrates downstream to **`Repair device` bench capacity and `Quality check` (`2` inspectors)**, whichpreviously relied on upstream quoting delays to meter their inflow.

### Redesign C: Value-Tiered Dual-Network & Cross-Functional Pods
- **Failure Mode 1 — Specialist Dilution and Pod Starvation:** Splitting only **`2` `Fraud/risk review` specialists**, **`3` `Repair quote` technicians**, and **`2` `Quality check` inspectors** across dedicated Premium/High-Value pods and Standard lanes reduces statistical pooling efficiency. Absence, illness, or a repair campaign affecting even one specialist can starve a pod.
- **Failure Mode 2 — SLA Buffer Breach on Standard Claims During Multi-Day Campaign Surges:** Load-smoothing Monday/campaign peaks into a 48–72h buffer works for single-day spikes, but after major multi-day marketing campaigns, buffered Standard claims may breach the **72-hour customer tolerance threshold**, triggering a wave of status inquiries and `Customer complaint/reopen` escalations even with good status visibility.
- **Displaced Bottleneck Migration:** The bottleneck shifts from **`Manual eligibility review` and the 45-minute `Escalation board`** to **Cross-Functional Pod Scheduling / Specialist Availability** and the **Assisted Evidence Fallback concierge queue** during peak campaign weeks.

# 7. Recommended experiment sequence

Rather than deploying a high-risk big-bang transformation, the organization should execute a **three-stage sequenced experiment plan** designed so that **Stage 1 creates the resource headroom required by Stage 2**, and **Stages 1–2 validate the routing and pod mechanics required by Stage 3**.

### Why Sequencing Order Matters (Capacity-First Logic)
Deploying Redesign A first (updating auto-screen rules immediately) is a classic operational trap: adding **+15–35% inflow** to `Manual eligibility review` *before* draining its `890` evidence-loop rework inflows will push the `7` reviewers past the **82% utilization cliff**, causing catastrophic queuing delays. Therefore, **capacity-releasing interventions must precede or pair tightly with compliance-tightening interventions**.

---

### Stage 1 Pilot (Weeks 1–4): Capacity Relief & Pre-Dispatch Containment
**Scope:**
1. **Pilot 1A (Pre-Dispatch Gate on High-Value Replacements):** Route 100% of `Fast approval -> Dispatch replacement` claims for **high-value devices (>EUR 400)** through the underused `Quality check` inspectors (`2` inspectors) for a 10-minute SKU/symptom compatibility check before shipping.
2. **Pilot 1B (Guided Evidence Portal with Assisted Fallback on 50% of Intake):** A/B test the new evidence portal on 50% of `Unclear defect / missing evidence` claims, coupled with a rapid human-assisted callback if a user fails upload once, plus pre-indexed part pricing for the top 20 high-value device SKUs in `Repair quote`.
3. **Pilot 1C (R3 Quick-Fix):** Require one `Manual eligibility review` reviewer to attend or counter-sign the daily 45-minute `Escalation board` slot (`120` cases) to achieve immediate **R3 conformance**.

**Metrics to Monitor:**
- **Upstream Rework & Utilization:** Repeat `Request missing evidence` rate (baseline: **26%**); `Request missing evidence -> Manual eligibility review` edge volume (baseline: `890`) and transition time (baseline: median **26.0h**, p90 **88.0h**); `Manual eligibility review` weekly utilization % (threshold: **<82%**).
- **Quality & Cost Error:** Wrong-part catch rate at the Pre-Dispatch Gate; `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` rate for high-value devices (baseline: **22% Premium / 18% Standard**).
- **Speed & Abandonment:** `Manual eligibility review -> Repair quote` transition time (baseline: median **15.0h**, p90 **60.0h**); `Claim abandoned` rate in the portal cohort vs. control (baseline: `320 / 1,430` = **22.4%** of evidence requests).

**Stop / Go Thresholds:**
- **GO to Stage 2 if:**
  - Repeated evidence requests in the portal cohort drop by **$\ge$ 25%** (toward the 30–45% target), bringing `Manual eligibility review` utilization stably down to **$\le$ 72–75%** (creating a >7–10% buffer below the 82% cliff).
  - Pre-dispatch inspection adds **$\le$ 12 hours median** while intercepting wrong replacement selections and reducing high-value fast-path reopens by **$\ge$ 40%**.
  - Portal cohort abandonment increases by **< 2 percentage points** relative to control.
- **STOP / ADJUST if:**
  - Portal abandonment rises by **$\ge$ 3–5 percentage points** (trigger immediate mandatory assisted-concierge fallback for low-digital-literacy segments).
  - Pre-dispatch gate delay exceeds **16 hours median** or causes Premium claims to breach the **24-hour service-credit threshold**.

### How to Verify Improvements Are Real Rather Than Shifted Downstream
To prevent local metric gaming or hidden bottleneck displacement, the executive sponsor must enforce four diagnostic controls:
1. **14-Day Lagged Cohort Accounting (Addressing the 96h / 180h p90 Complaint Tail):** Because `Dispatch replacement -> Customer complaint/reopen` has a **mean of 96.0h (4 days) and p90 of 180.0h (7.5 days)**, cycle time and cost per claim must never be evaluated at `Quality/compliance close` alone. Every experimental cohort must be locked and tracked for **14 full days post-close** to capture delayed reopenings, reshipments, and service credits.
2. **Survivorship-Bias Audit on Abandonments:** Track `(Completed Claims + Abandoned Claims)` as the denominator. If median cycle time drops from **140h to 80h** in the `Unclear defect` segment, verify via survival analysis that the improvement comes from faster `Request missing evidence -> Manual review` transitions and **not** from slow cases dropping out early into `Claim abandoned`.
3. **End-to-End Conservation of Flow Across Queue Buffers:** Monitor work-in-progress (WIP) and p90 waiting times simultaneously across *all* downstream nodes (`Fraud/risk review`, `Repair quote`, `Repair device`, `Quality check`, and `Escalation board`). A real improvement reduces **total system WIP and end-to-end p90 lead time** (`START -> END` including reopen loops), whereas a shifted bottleneck reduces upstream wait time while inflating downstream WIP or `Quality check -> Rework repair` (`12.5%` baseline).
4. **Full Cost-of-Quality (CoQ) Ledger per Claim:** Evaluate cohorts using a unified ledger:
   $$\text{Total Claim Cost} = \text{Screening/Review/Quote/Repair Labor} + \text{Wrong-Replacement Cost (EUR 95 / EUR 410)} + \text{SLA Service Credits} + \text{Reopen/Escalation Labor}$$
   An improvement is validated as real only when **Total Claim Cost** and **30-day Unreopened Resolution Rate** improve simultaneously across both Premium and Standard segments.
