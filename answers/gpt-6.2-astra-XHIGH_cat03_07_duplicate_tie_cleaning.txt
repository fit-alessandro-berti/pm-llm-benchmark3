## 1. Duplicate-handling decision tree

Decide what one event represents before matching rows. For the conservative view it can be a physical operation, a business transaction or a separately recorded status update. Keep the raw OCEL immutable. Apply the following tests to the source observations, before changing labels or breaking timestamp ties.

1. **Exact technical duplicate?** Require a replay-safe `(source, source_event_key)`, equal business fields, equal timestamp and identical typed, qualified object links. Allow the ingestion receipt and assigned eid to differ. **Drop** the redundant copy from the analytical view and record its eid and receipt against the survivor. A reused key with changed content needs review. No two listed rows demonstrate an exact replay.
2. **Independent evidence of one occurrence?** A reconciliation match or a unique document issuance with matching lifecycle meaning can establish identity across systems. **Merge attributes and relations** under source-authority and role-compatibility rules. Equal payload hashes are unnecessary for enriched observations. Equal hashes alone would also be insufficient.
3. **A derived message or summary?** **Retain both with refined labels or qualifiers** and record the derivation and its granularity. A transition-level view can merge a documented one-to-one projection. A balance update summarizing multiple payments has no unique capture to absorb it.
4. **Different source meanings hidden by harmonization?** **Retain both with refined labels or qualifiers** taken from the source semantics. Object equality is compatible with successive custody milestones.
5. **Distinct occurrences?** Different operative items or payment IDs, or evidence of distinct lifecycle actions, support **retaining both unchanged** in identity and incidence. The events can occupy the same timestamp bin. An unrelated label repair can still apply to either event.
6. **Still unresolved?** **Quarantine the proposed match for review** and retain both observations in the conservative view. Record evidence for and against the match. An event with no candidate passes through unchanged.

## 3. Timestamp-tie ordering rules

Sort different business timestamps chronologically. For each tie, build a directed acyclic graph on the original observations. Add documented domain dependencies such as a generating transaction before its status update, object-specific lifecycle edges such as authorization before capture **of M1**, and source-specific facts such as carrier acceptance before handover. Add increasing `source_seq` edges within each source where it documents local order. These are evidence about business dependencies or local recording order, respectively. A local sequence across two payments establishes no financial dependency. Never compare sequence numbers across systems.

Contract verified identities, retaining the external edges of every member. Remove internal edges, preserve the reasons for the others, and send a cycle for review. Use a topological sort that chooses the lexically smallest canonical eid among currently eligible nodes. Ingestion time could serve as a documented technical fallback among eligible nodes, followed by eid. Here eid alone suffices. Neither fallback supplies a semantic edge.

- **10:00** gives `e03[e04], e05, e06, e07`. The scanner places e04 before e05, WMS places e03 before e06, and the packing message places e06 before e07. Picking I2 before packing it is a further edge if the package lifecycle contract requires it. Without that contract the e05/e06 order here comes from the fallback.
- **10:15** gives `e08, e09, e10`. Only e09 before e10 is required by the supplied carrier evidence. For example, `e09, e08, e10` is also admissible unless a cross-system custody rule establishes release before acceptance.
- **12:00** gives `e11[e16], e12, e13, e14, e15`. PSP supplies e12 before e13 before e14, and ERP places e11 before e15. For this example assume both listed capture messages feed the balance update, giving e13 and e14 before e15. In production bind these edges to message IDs and omit unconfirmed contributors. No supplied rule puts invoice issuance before authorization. Moving e11 between e14 and e15 also satisfies these constraints.

Keep the graph, timestamp bin and chosen rank. Adding microseconds first would turn an arbitrary input row order into apparent evidence, hide equal-time duplicate candidates and manufacture positive durations. Adjusted times belong only in an order-compatible export for consumers that require unique times.

## 4. Conservative cleaning pipeline

Snapshot and validate events, objects and qualified relations. Preserve original IDs, timestamps, source keys, source sequences, ingestion times, harmonized labels and available source labels. Profile repeated source keys and equal-label/equal-time groups with their object sets. Check the business identity of each candidate before editing anything.

Merge e04 into e03 and e16 into e11. Preserve both sources’ attributes in lineage, promote agreed or authoritative values, and collapse identical compatible relations. Apply the four label refinements for e07, e08, e09 and e15. Keep the status events visible to systems analysts who may need to measure message propagation. Rank the resulting events as above. The **15 events** are the 17 input observations less two redundant observations. The **39 E2O links** are the original 44 less three repeated scan links and two repeated invoice links, counting one row per listed event/object pair.

## 5. Aggressive alternatives

**Evidence-based business-transition view — 14 events, 37 links.** Also absorb e07 into e06 under the scenario assumption that this status is the one-to-one ERP projection of this WMS pack for P1. Retain e06’s four links, the ERP observation in lineage and its message dependency in the audit record. This costs visibility into ERP acknowledgement and propagation latency. Reject that merge for studies of system interactions or if a single status can summarize several packs. Keep e15 separate in either case.

**Mechanically aggressive library view — 13 events, 34 links.** Starting independently from the harmonized input, run the library merge in the scoped blocks {e03,e04}, {e06,e07}, {e08,e09} and {e11,e16}. Select these blocks by equal activity/time, protecting distinct operative item IDs and the entire ambiguous capture group. The subtraction from 44 is 3 + 2 + 3 + 2 links. Leave the merged dispatch labelled `Dispatch Package` to expose the effect of the collision. It erases one of two distinct custody observations even though all their object IDs agree.

`have_common_object=True` would admit e13 and e14 through O1 or V1. The ERP summary also overlaps both. Unrestricted merging of all overlapping rows in these label/time groups would, under union semantics, yield 10 events and 28 links, including one capture connected to both M1 and M2. That is a counterfactual damage estimate. The four datasets below all preserve e05 and e14 as independent events.

## 6. PM4Py-oriented Python implementation

The code uses native OCEL columns and keeps source observations in side tables keyed by their original eid. A library call decides whether an isolated block actually coalesces. The common materialization step gives the result a stable eid and restores field and relation provenance. `authority` can be populated from the field ownership contract. The invoice field names and their owners are unspecified here.

```python
from copy import deepcopy
from itertools import pairwise
import networkx as nx
import pandas as pd
import pm4py

base = deepcopy(ocel_raw)
E, A, T = base.event_id_column, base.event_activity, base.event_timestamp
O, OT = base.object_id_column, base.object_type_column
Q = getattr(base, 'qualifier', 'ocel:qualifier')
base.events['original_timestamp'] = base.events[T].copy()
base.events['original_activity'] = base.events[A].copy()
base.events[T] = pd.to_datetime(base.events[T], utc=True, errors='raise')
obs = base.events.set_index(E, drop=False, verify_integrity=True)
links = base.relations.copy(deep=True)
# The supplied log has only E2O event references. For richer OCELs, also
# translate qualified E2E endpoints and audit internal edges before proceeding.
if len(getattr(base, 'e2e', [])):
    raise ValueError('E2E endpoint remapping required for this input')

objects_of = links.groupby(E)[O].agg(frozenset).to_dict()
candidates = [g.assign(object_ids=g[E].map(objects_of))
              for _, g in obs.groupby([A, T], sort=True) if len(g) > 1]
# candidates retain the keys, hashes and sequences for inspection.

def check(z):
    ev = z.events.set_index(E, verify_integrity=True)
    ob = z.objects.set_index(O, verify_integrity=True)
    r = z.relations
    assert not ev.index.hasnans and not ob.index.hasnans
    assert ev[[A, T]].notna().all().all()
    assert r[E].isin(ev.index).all() and r[O].isin(ob.index).all()
    assert set(r[E]) == set(ev.index)  # every supplied event has objects
    assert r[A].eq(r[E].map(ev[A])).all()
    assert pd.to_datetime(r[T], utc=True).eq(pd.to_datetime(r[E].map(ev[T]), utc=True)).all()
    assert r[OT].eq(r[O].map(ob[OT])).all()

check(base)

def coalesces(ids, operation):
    # Process just this block. Library-generated eids are local to the probe.
    z = deepcopy(base)
    z.events = z.events[z.events[E].isin(ids)].copy()
    z.relations = z.relations[z.relations[E].isin(ids)].copy()
    expected = set(z.relations[O])
    z.objects = z.objects[z.objects[O].isin(expected)].copy()
    merged = operation(z)
    return (len(merged.events) == 1 and set(merged.relations[O]) == expected
            and set(merged.relations[E]) == set(merged.events[E]))

def technical_replays():
    groups, review = [], []
    for _, g in obs.dropna(subset=['source', 'source_event_key']).groupby(
            ['source', 'source_event_key']):
        ids = sorted(g[E])
        if len(ids) < 2:
            continue
        def content(e):
            fields = obs.loc[e].drop([E, 'ingested_at'])
            rel = links.loc[links[E].eq(e)].drop(columns=E)
            keys = [O, Q] if Q in rel else [O]
            return fields, rel.sort_values(keys).reset_index(drop=True)
        first, rel0 = content(ids[0])
        exact = all(first.equals(f) and rel0.equals(r)
                    for f, r in map(content, ids[1:]))
        # Safe only for verified replay keys in this feed. No such group here.
        if exact and coalesces(ids, pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates):
            groups.append(ids)
        else:
            review.append(ids)
    return groups, review

replays, review_queue = technical_replays()
verified = [('e03', 'e04'), ('e11', 'e16')]
mechanical = verified + [('e06', 'e07'), ('e08', 'e09')]
labels = {'e07': 'Record Packing Status', 'e08': 'Release from Warehouse',
          'e09': 'Accept by Carrier', 'e15': 'Update Invoice Balance'}
authority = {('e03', 'location'): 'WMS'}  # add documented invoice field owners here
representative_fields = {E, A, T, 'original_activity', 'original_timestamp',
                         'source', 'source_event_key', 'source_seq', 'ingested_at'}

def sync_relations(z):
    index = z.events.set_index(E)
    for col in (A, T, 'tie_rank'):
        if col in index:
            z.relations[col] = z.relations[E].map(index[col])

# Causal assumptions for this run, including both contributors to e15.
causes = [('e06', 'e07'), ('e09', 'e10'), ('e12', 'e13'),
          ('e13', 'e15'), ('e14', 'e15')]

def establish_order(z, canonical):
    ranks, order_edges = {}, []
    for stamp, g in obs.groupby(T):
        graph = nx.DiGraph()
        graph.add_nodes_from(canonical[e] for e in g[E])
        def add(u, v, why):
            a, b = canonical[u], canonical[v]
            order_edges.append((stamp, u, v, a, b, why))
            if a != b:
                graph.add_edge(a, b)
        for u, v in causes:
            if u in g.index and v in g.index:
                add(u, v, 'domain or message')
        for source, stream in g.dropna(subset=['source_seq']).groupby('source'):
            layers = [rows[E].tolist() for _, rows in stream.groupby('source_seq', sort=True)]
            for earlier, later in pairwise(layers):
                for u in earlier:
                    for v in later:
                        add(u, v, ('source_seq', source))
        if not nx.is_directed_acyclic_graph(graph):
            raise ValueError(('review tied precedence cycle', stamp, nx.find_cycle(graph)))
        ranks.update({e: i for i, e in enumerate(nx.lexicographical_topological_sort(graph))})
    z.events['tie_rank'] = z.events[E].map(ranks)
    sync_relations(z)
    z = pm4py.ocel_sort_by_additional_column(z, 'tie_rank')
    z.events = z.events.sort_values([T, 'tie_rank'], kind='stable').reset_index(drop=True)
    sync_relations(z)
    z.relations = z.relations.sort_values([T, 'tie_rank', O], kind='stable').reset_index(drop=True)
    return z, order_edges

def build(scenario):
    z = deepcopy(base)
    group = nx.utils.UnionFind(obs.index)
    decisions = []
    def join(ids, rule):
        group.union(*ids)
        decisions.append((tuple(ids), rule))
    if scenario != 'raw':
        for ids in replays:
            join(ids, 'verified technical replay')
    if scenario in ('conservative', 'evidence'):
        for ids in verified:
            join(ids, 'scan reconciliation' if ids[0] == 'e03' else 'confirmed issuance')
        if scenario == 'evidence':
            join(('e06', 'e07'), 'one packing transition, assumed 1:1 projection')
    if scenario == 'library':
        for ids in mechanical:
            assert obs.loc[list(ids), [A, T]].drop_duplicates().shape[0] == 1
            # WARNING: even a common object permits release/acceptance to collapse.
            # Never pass the entire pick or capture group to this operation.
            if not coalesces(ids, lambda part: pm4py.ocel_merge_duplicates(
                    part, have_common_object=True)):
                raise ValueError(('inspect library behavior for block', ids))
            join(ids, 'scoped library merge, common object')

    canonical, rows = {}, []
    for members in group.to_sets():
        ids = sorted(members)
        cid = ids[0]  # fixed representatives e03, e06, e08 and e11 for these groups
        canonical.update(dict.fromkeys(ids, cid))
        row = obs.loc[cid].copy()
        if len(ids) > 1:
            for col in obs.columns.difference(list(representative_fields)):
                present = obs.loc[ids].dropna(subset=[col])
                owner = authority.get((cid, col))
                if owner is not None:
                    present = present[present['source'].eq(owner)]
                values = present[col].drop_duplicates()  # scalar metadata in this log
                row[col] = values.iloc[0] if len(values) == 1 else pd.NA
        if scenario != 'raw' and not (scenario == 'library' and cid == 'e08'):
            row[A] = labels.get(cid, row[A])
        rows.append(row)
    z.events = pd.DataFrame(rows).reset_index(drop=True)
    z.relations[E] = z.relations[E].map(canonical)
    sync_relations(z)
    z.relations = z.relations.drop_duplicates().reset_index(drop=True)
    role_key = [E, O] + ([Q] if Q in z.relations else [])
    if z.relations.duplicated(role_key).any():
        raise ValueError('review conflicting relation attributes')
    # Only the groups above are approved for role-compatible union in this fixture.
    for cid, g in obs.groupby(obs[E].map(canonical)):
        assert set(z.relations.loc[z.relations[E].eq(cid), O]) == set().union(
            *(objects_of[e] for e in g[E]))
    z, edges = establish_order(z, canonical)
    check(z)
    history = {'members': obs.assign(canonical_eid=obs[E].map(canonical)).copy(),
               'assertions': links.assign(canonical_eid=links[E].map(canonical)).copy(),
               'merge_decisions': decisions, 'order_evidence': edges}
    return z, history

counts = {'raw': (17, 44), 'conservative': (15, 39),
          'evidence': (14, 37), 'library': (13, 34)}
views, audit, ordered_exports = {}, {}, {}
for name, expected in counts.items():
    z, audit[name] = build(name)
    assert (len(z.events), len(z.relations)) == expected
    assert {'e05', 'e14'}.issubset(z.events[E])
    views[name] = z  # original time scale for all performance calculations
    export = pm4py.ocel_add_index_based_timedelta(deepcopy(z))
    sync_relations(export)
    adjusted = export.events.set_index(E).loc[z.events[E], T].reset_index(drop=True)
    assert adjusted.is_monotonic_increasing and adjusted.is_unique
    # Reject offsets crossing the next distinct business time in a denser log.
    next_bin = {a: b for a, b in pairwise(z.events[T].drop_duplicates())}
    limit = z.events[T].map(next_bin)
    assert (limit.isna() | adjusted.lt(limit)).all()
    assert export.events.set_index(E)['original_timestamp'].equals(
        z.events.set_index(E)['original_timestamp'].reindex(export.events[E]))
    check(export)
    ordered_exports[name] = export
```

## 7. Sensitivity analysis

Use the same object populations, tie policy and frequency definition in each run. Flatten once per **object instance** of the selected type. Sort each trace by original timestamp and chosen rank, including after a PM4Py flattening if the consumer re-sorts ties. For an OC-DFG, report the object type, activity edge, number of object-trace transitions, number of distinct event pairs and number of contributing objects separately. Two objects can witness the same event pair. For ET–OT (event type–object type) weights below, each listed E2O link contributes one unit. A count of distinct events touching a type is a different statistic.

| View | Events / E2O | Order-flattened DFG on O1 | Item-flattened DFG | Payment-flattened DFG |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Raw | 17 / 44 | 16 transitions. Three consecutive picks, two packs, two dispatches and three captures. ERP and billing issuance appear at different positions under eid fallback. | I1 has `Pick Item → Pick Item → Pack Package`. I2 has one pick then pack. | M1 has `Authorize Payment → Capture Payment`. M2 has a single capture. |
| Conservative | 15 / 39 | 14 transitions. Two picks remain. Packing leads to packing status, release to acceptance, and the second capture to the balance update. | Each item has one `Pick Item → Pack Package` transition. | Same edge on M1 and singleton on M2. e15 contributes to neither trace. |
| Evidence-based | 14 / 37 | 13 transitions. `Pack Package → Record Packing Status → Release from Warehouse` becomes `Pack Package → Release from Warehouse`. | Same two item transitions. ERP status never had item links. | Same M1 and M2 traces. |
| Library, scoped | 13 / 34 | 12 transitions. `Dispatch Package → Handover to Carrier` replaces two distinguishable custody steps. | Same two item transitions because e05 survives independently. | Same M1 and M2 traces because the capture group was protected. |

Several patterns explain the differences in the metrics.

- On O1 the raw `Pick Item → Pick Item` frequency falls from two to one in every cleaned view. The surviving loop crosses I1 and I2. On the Item OC-DFG the loop falls from one to zero. The Pick–Item ET–OT weight falls from three links to two, while two distinct items participate throughout.
- On O1 and P1 the raw `Pack Package → Pack Package` edge becomes detail followed by status in the conservative view. The evidence and library views lose that intermediate event. Pack–Item still has weight two, both links from the **same** detailed pack. Counting distinct packs touching Item gives one.
- On O1, P1 and D1 the raw `Dispatch Package → Dispatch Package` is one event pair witnessed by three objects. The conservative and evidence views give `Release from Warehouse → Accept by Carrier → Handover to Carrier` in the chosen order. In the library view only one dispatch touches D1 instead of two. Across admissible orders the D1 release-to-acceptance direct-edge frequency ranges from zero to one. Even the required acceptance-before-handover precedence yields no direct edge if release falls between them.
- On O1 and V1 the raw `Capture Payment → Capture Payment` frequency falls from two to one when e15 becomes `Update Invoice Balance`. The remaining loop represents two distinct payments. In the raw eid-based order, `Capture Payment → Issue Invoice` ends the noon group with e16. After invoice reconciliation the balance update ends the group and precedes `Confirm Delivery` on O1. Issue–Invoice link weight falls from two to one.
- For the object-type graph (OTG), join types whose instances co-participate in an event. Track the underlying object pairs as well. Binary adjacency is unchanged across the four views. For instance I1 and I2 already co-occur in the pack, and M1 and M2 never co-occur. With one unit per witnessing event, the Order–Package weight (here entirely O1–P1) falls from 9 to 8, 7 and 6 respectively. An unrestricted capture union would fabricate M1–M2 adjacency even if the two Payment traces still looked individually plausible.

Measure object lifetimes, waiting times and throughput on original timestamps. O1 spans 24 hours in all four views. M1’s authorization and capture occupy the same recorded second. Removing duplicated observations reduces the number of zero-recorded-lag edges and changes denominators for mean waiting times. The evidence view loses the ERP status checkpoint, and the library view loses the release-to-acceptance interval entirely. Repeat each DFG with alternate admissible tie orders and, separately, with optional lifecycle/message edges removed. Report frequency ranges for order-sensitive edges and the fraction of lags within timestamp resolution. This distinguishes changes caused by event identity from changes caused by linearization.

## 8. Audit trail and reversibility

Version each view against an immutable raw snapshot. Store a membership row per `(view_version, canonical_eid, raw_eid)` with source, source key, sequence, ingestion receipt, original source label when known, harmonized label, original timestamp and payload reference. Record the disposition, match or derivation rule, rule version, reviewer or automated decision ID, confidence, reason and evidence reference. Keep the refined activity, canonical attributes, rank, adjusted timestamp and export offset unit on the canonical record.

Store a field-decision row per candidate value with source eid, field name, value, authority, selected value and disposition. Preserve conflicts discarded from display. Store each qualified E2O assertion with its original eid, object ID, type, qualifier and resulting canonical relation or exclusion reason. Keep precedence edges with their evidence and distinguish optional edges from the chosen rank. Version the label dictionary, software and scenario assumptions alongside the results.

Reconstruct by selecting the raw snapshot or emitting one event per raw eid from membership and source rows, then restoring each raw eid’s own assertions and the original object tables. Restore original labels and timestamps, including receipts of dropped replays. Compare sorted records and counts with snapshot checksums. Splitting a canonical event and giving every member its union of links would fail this check.

## 9. Failure modes and safeguards

| Failure | Safeguard |
|---|---|
| Collapse partial payments on a shared invoice | Require operative Payment identity and preserve e13/M1 and e14/M2 separately. |
| Read an ERP status as physical rework | Give the status its source meaning and record its generating transaction. |
| Infer business order from ingestion delays | Use evidenced precedence, source-local sequence and an explicit tie rank. |
| Treat shared objects as proof of duplication | Check the source action and identity evidence even when the entire object set matches. |
| Union incompatible E2O roles or spread summary links across payments | Check typed, qualified assertions individually and keep their source lineage. |
| Manufacture durations with synthetic microseconds | Keep original time for metrics and validate export offsets against the next bin. |
| Lose source-authoritative attributes | Apply field-level authority, retain conflicting values and prohibit silent last-write-wins. |
| Run duplicate functions across the whole OCEL | Inspect candidate blocks, isolate approved inputs and verify cardinality and incidence afterward. |
| Discard a noncanonical observer’s sequence constraints | Build precedence from all raw members and test the contracted graph for cycles. |
| Mistake a deterministic DFG edge for a causal dependency | Retain tie groups and compare admissible linearizations, especially across source systems. |
