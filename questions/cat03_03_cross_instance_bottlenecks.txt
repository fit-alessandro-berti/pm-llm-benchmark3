### Batch B2 on OVEN-1 using RACK-7

J4 is ready at 13:10, J5 is ready at 14:40, and B2 starts at 14:50. OVEN-1 is idle from 12:55 to 14:50, but RACK-7 is recorded as “cleaning/quarantine” until 14:35.

### Fixture contention

- J3 acquires FIX-A at 09:00 and releases it at 11:40.
- J4 reaches CNC-2 at 09:50.
- CNC-2 is idle from 10:05 to 10:47.
- J4’s `Machine Process` starts at 11:45 and relates to J4, C4, CNC-2, and FIX-A.
- The system has no explicit `Wait for Tool` activity.

### Shared component-lot release

- C2 for J2 and C5 for J5 both belong to L7.
- A `Quarantine Lot` event at 10:20 relates to L7, C2, C5, J2, and J5.
- A `Release Lot` event at 13:50 relates to the same objects.
- The Job flattening contains neither event because the extraction used only events directly related to the Job at source; an alternative integration pipeline would relate these events to J2 and J5.
- J2 had already entered B1 before the release event because a supervisor override was recorded in a separate system.
- J5 did not proceed until after release.

### Calibration tool CAL-3

- TEST-1 can test any job, but every `Final Test` event also requires CAL-3.
- J5 and J6 are ready for final test at 16:00 and 16:05.
- J5 tests from 16:10 to 16:35.
- CAL-3 is then recalibrated from 16:35 to 17:20.
- J6 tests from 17:25 to 17:50.
- TEST-1 is otherwise idle from 16:35 to 17:25.

### Shipment consolidation

- J1 is packed at 13:30.
- J4 is packed at 19:10.
- S1, containing J1 and J4, dispatches at 19:25.
- S2 dispatches 25 minutes after its last job is packed.
- S3 dispatches 35 minutes after its last job is packed.
- The shipment-flattened log measures only `Create Shipment -> Load -> Dispatch`; it does not represent how long an already packed job waits for its shipment partners.
