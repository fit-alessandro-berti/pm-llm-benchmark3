You are assessing transaction-review compliance from an event log in which amount, risk, jurisdiction, customer type, and exemption data can change during a case. A final case attribute is not necessarily the value that governed an earlier event. There is deliberately no single canonical answer for cases with missing or contradictory evidence. An LLM judge will evaluate temporal semantics, formalization quality, case reasoning, and the usefulness of the PM4Py/pandas code.

Do not ask questions. Blank attribute cells mean “no update at this event,” not “set the value to null.” Treat an attribute update as effective at its event timestamp unless the evidence explicitly establishes another effective time.

POLICY RULES

R1 — Foreign high-value enhanced review
At Release_Funds, if the current jurisdiction is not DE and the current amount is at least EUR 100,000, an Enhanced_Review must have completed before release and after the latest material data change, unless a matching exemption was already valid.

R2 — High-risk or PEP approval
At Release_Funds, if current risk_level is HIGH or current customer_type is PEP, both Enhanced_Review and Compliance_Approve are required. Compliance_Approve must occur after the relevant Enhanced_Review and before release.

R3 — Sanctions-list update
If the current jurisdiction is added to the sanctions list after the latest Sanctions_Screen but before Release_Funds, a new Sanctions_Screen must occur after the list update and before release.

R4 — Material amount change
An amount increase of more than 20 percent after the latest Standard_Review or Enhanced_Review invalidates that review for amount-dependent obligations. A decrease does not by itself invalidate a review. An increase from an unknown amount is not safely classifiable without additional evidence.

R5 — Exemption validity
An exemption is effective only if:
- approved by Head_Compliance;
- effective_from is no later than the obligation trigger;
- its jurisdiction, customer-type, and amount scope match;
- it has not expired; and
- the evidence supports that approval existed at the relevant time.
A late database record can be evidence of earlier approval, but a displayed backdated field alone is not conclusive.

R6 — Missing data
Do not silently default missing amount, risk, jurisdiction, or customer type to a low-risk value. Classify as INDETERMINATE when a decisive trigger cannot be established or excluded, unless other evidence resolves it.

R7 — Event-time semantics
Evaluate each obligation using the latest known value at the relevant event. Do not copy the final case state backward in time. When two events share a timestamp, use `event_index` as the tie-breaker and state the consequence.
