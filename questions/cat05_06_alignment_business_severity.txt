NORMATIVE CONTROL-FLOW MODEL
The process tree is:

-> ( 'Register Claim', 'Validate Coverage', 'Assess Loss', X ( -> ( 'Fraud Screen', 'Manual Approve' ), 'Auto Approve' ), 'Pay Claim', 'Notify Claimant' )

BUSINESS POLICY OVERLAY NOT FULLY ENCODED IN THE PROCESS TREE
P1. Auto Approve is allowed only when amount <= EUR 5,000, fraud risk is LOW, and no beneficiary-bank change occurred during the previous seven days.
P2. A claim above EUR 50,000, a HIGH-risk claim, or any recent beneficiary-bank change requires Fraud Screen and Manual Approve before Pay Claim.
P3. Pay Claim before the applicable approval is a severe control failure even if the model alignment can repair it cheaply.
P4. Notify Claimant is required within 24 hours, but notification may occur in the core system, secure portal, or CRM if linked evidence is retained.
P5. UI telemetry, autosave, retry, and transport events are not business activities unless they reveal a meaningful control issue.
P6. The process tree was designed for payable claims; whether denied pre-screen leads and re-opened claims are in scope is disputed.

ALIGNMENT NOTATION
- `S(A)` = synchronous move on log event A and model transition A; technical cost 0.
- `L(A,c=n)` = move on log: A occurred in the log but is not mimicked by the model; technical cost n.
- `M(A,c=n)` = visible move on model: model requires A but no matching log event exists; technical cost n.
- `T(tau,c=0)` = hidden/silent model move; technically fitting.
- The listed `raw_cost` is the sum under the supplied cost function.
