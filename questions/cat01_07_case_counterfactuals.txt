ROLE
You are a process-mining and operations expert reviewing four difficult completed cases. For each case, construct a plausible counterfactual execution under a specified intervention.

OBJECTIVE
Generate counterfactual process paths that remain compatible with control-flow, timing, capacity, resource, and compliance constraints. Explain how a local intervention propagates through queues and may create adverse effects elsewhere.

There is no hidden correct counterfactual duration. The cases are not generated from a known structural model, and several futures are plausible. Your answer will be evaluated by another LLM for minimal-change counterfactual reasoning, temporal consistency, process feasibility, uncertainty calibration, and recognition of interference.

NORMATIVE PROCESS RULES
1. `Register` must be first, followed by `Triage`.
2. `Identity Check` and `Document Check` both must complete before `Assessment`.
3. They may be executed concurrently when no original document is required.
4. When originals are required, `Originals Receipt` must precede `Document Check`; `Identity Check` may still proceed earlier.
5. A high-risk or high-value case requires `Compliance Review` after the latest relevant `Assessment` and before `Decision`.
6. `Assessment` or `Compliance Review` may issue `Request More Info`.
7. After `Request More Info`, a new `Document Check` and a new `Assessment` are required before a decision; high-risk/high-value cases then need a new or explicitly reaffirmed compliance review.
8. `Decision` must precede `Notify`.
9. Four-eyes rule: the same person may not perform both the final `Assessment` and final `Compliance Review`.
10. A preliminary compliance screen may occur earlier, but it does not replace the final compliance review.
11. Resources cannot perform two service intervals at the same time.
12. Queue estimates are forecasts, not guarantees.
