SCENARIO
A medical-device manufacturer handles post-market product complaints. The event log records task-completion events, not task starts. Therefore, a task may already be in progress even when its completion event is not yet visible.

Normative state abstraction
- S0 REGISTERED: Complaint Registered has occurred, but triage has not been completed.
- S1 TRIAGED: Initial Triage is complete. A low-severity, single-device, clearly duplicate complaint may be fast-closed.
- S2 EVIDENCE_PENDING: Customer Evidence Requested has occurred, but no corresponding Evidence Received event has yet been logged.
- S3 EVIDENCE_REVIEW: Evidence has been received. Evidence Review may either request more evidence, open investigations, or support a non-investigation closure.
- S4 INVESTIGATION_FORK_OPEN: one or more investigation obligations are open. Technical Investigation and Regulatory Assessment may be executed in either order or concurrently. Supplier Analysis is additionally required when a common lot, supplier, or component is implicated.
- S5 DISPOSITION_READY: every mandatory investigation obligation is complete and a disposition can be selected.
- Terminal outcomes include Fast Close, Close — No Safety Issue, Corrective Action Opened, Authority Notification followed by Close, and Product Hold/Recall Escalation.

Control-flow and compliance rules
1. Complaint Registered must precede Initial Triage.
2. Fast Close is permitted only when severity is Low, there is no injury or safety signal, and the complaint is not linked to multiple devices or a suspicious common lot.
3. An evidence cycle is Customer Evidence Requested -> Evidence Received -> Evidence Review. Evidence Review may create another evidence cycle.
4. High-severity complaints and complaints with a possible reportability signal require both Technical Investigation and Regulatory Assessment before final disposition.
5. Supplier Analysis is mandatory when two or more apparently related complaints concern the same lot or supplier component. Disposition must wait for an opened Supplier Analysis.
6. If Regulatory Assessment concludes “reportable,” Authority Notification must occur before closure. Notifications are transmitted in a scheduled batch.
7. The operational log may omit or delay events during system migration. A missing event is not automatically evidence that the underlying task did not occur.

TASK
For each live case:
1. Infer the most plausible latent process state, while retaining at least one alternative state when the evidence supports it.
2. Predict exactly three plausible next logged activities or termination outcomes.
3. Give an honest probability interval for each prediction. Do not invent false precision. The midpoint probabilities should be broadly coherent as a top-three distribution, but intervals need not sum exactly to 1 because they express uncertainty.
4. Separate evidence that supports the prediction from evidence that contradicts it.
5. Identify unknowns that could materially change the ranking.
6. Distinguish a process-enabled action from the event most likely to be observed next in the log. For example, a task can be in progress while its completion event is not yet visible.
