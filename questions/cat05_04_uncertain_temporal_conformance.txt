TEMPORAL PROFILE
Durations are in seconds and describe eventually-following activity pairs:

{
  ('Alert_Received', 'Investigation_Start'): (600, 180),
  ('Investigation_Start', 'Containment_Complete'): (2100, 600),
  ('Alert_Received', 'Containment_Complete'): (2700, 720),
  ('Containment_Complete', 'Close_Incident'): (86400, 21600)
}

For a chosen positive `zeta`, a point duration is accepted by the temporal profile when it lies within average +/- zeta * standard deviation. Your interval analysis must distinguish:
- CERTAIN_PASS: every feasible duration satisfies the rule;
- CERTAIN_VIOLATION: every feasible duration violates the rule;
- POSSIBLE_VIOLATION: feasible durations include both pass and violation outcomes;
- INDETERMINATE: evidence is too weak, unbounded, internally inconsistent, or not testable under your stated semantics.

For a two-sided temporal-profile band, an interval wholly below or wholly above the band is a certain violation. An interval wholly inside is a certain pass. Partial overlap is a possible violation. If feasible time is a union of disjoint intervals, preserve that union when it matters rather than collapsing it blindly to one large interval.
