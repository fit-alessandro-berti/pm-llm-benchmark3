RESOURCE RULES

RR1 — Drafter/approver separation
The human who performs Draft_Change_Request must not perform Approve_Change for the same case.

RR2 — Implementer/verifier separation
The human who performs Implement_Change must not perform Verify_Implementation for the same case.

RR3 — High-risk approval authority
For HIGH-risk cases, Approve_Change must be performed by the Quality Manager (QM) or a valid QM delegate. A delegation is valid only if its effective interval covers the event, its scope covers the change, the principal is absent or formally unavailable when required by the delegation, and no prohibited subdelegation occurred.

RR4 — Emergency substitution
A HIGH-risk emergency change may be implemented after approval by the Incident Commander (IC) or valid IC delegate, but a QM or valid QM delegate must perform Retrospective_QM_Review within two business days. This is a justified exception only when the emergency flag and safety rationale are credible.

RR5 — Identity evidence
A service account is not a human identity. It can support conformance only if reliable session evidence maps the action to an authorized human. Aliases belonging to the same human must be normalized before segregation-of-duties checks.

RR6 — Suspicion versus proof
Repeated resource pairs, unusual reciprocal approvals, shared workstations, and concentrated handovers are indicators. They are not proof of collusion without supporting evidence.
