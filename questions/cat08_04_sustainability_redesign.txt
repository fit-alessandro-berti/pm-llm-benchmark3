Batching evidence:
- CNC_A energy per item decreases from 2.8 kWh at batch size 1-3 to 1.3 kWh at batch size 12-20, but median Batch wait increases from 4h to 38h.
- CNC_B is faster but has a higher rework rate: 18% vs 9% on CNC_A for Made-to-order cases.
- Heat treatment energy per item is lowest at batch size above 10, but cases waiting for a heat-treatment batch have a 1.7x higher late-delivery risk.
- Large production batches increase obsolescence: 6% of batch-produced items are not shipped within 14 days when demand forecasts are wrong.
- Inspection_cell queues are responsible for many rework-production loops because rushed inspections miss defects that later cause packaging or customer returns.

Transport and packaging evidence:
- Parcel air cuts median transport time by 24h versus ground but emits approximately 4-6x more kgCO2e per order.
- Dedicated vans protect Critical-downtime service levels but often return empty.
- Consolidated trucks reduce transport kgCO2e by about 35-55% versus individual ground parcels, but require a 12-24h consolidation wait.
- Reinforced packaging reduces damage-related returns by 40% for Made-to-order heavy parts, but doubles package material.
- Packaging rework occurs when packaging choice is incompatible with route_mode or part geometry.
- Standard-stock Service-contract orders are often shipped by air even when promised lead time still allows ground plus priority picking.

Conformance and risk findings:
- Sustainability policy SP1: use ground or consolidated truck when promised lead time can still be met with probability above 85%.
- Sustainability policy SP2: dedicated vans require explicit Critical-downtime justification.
- Quality policy Q1: Made-to-order parts over 8 kg must use reinforced packaging unless inspection certifies low damage risk.
- Resilience policy R1: at least one alternative route must remain available for Service-contract customers during carrier disruption.
- Conformance findings: SP1 is violated in 22% of Standard-stock Service-contract cases; SP2 is missing a justification event in 31% of dedicated-van cases; Q1 violations correlate with return/rework loops.
- Precision of the discovered model is low because emergency paths and partial shipments combine in many ways during disruptions.

S1. Green shipping recommender using promised lead time, route reliability, and customer segment.

S4. Replace air for Standard-stock Service-contract orders with priority pick + ground unless slack is insufficient.
S5. Consolidate truck departures twice daily for non-urgent stock orders.
S6. Improve packaging-route compatibility checks before shipment.
S7. Add inspection quality gate for high-rework machine/part combinations.
S8. Replace some dedicated vans with shared emergency courier lanes.
S9. Split Critical-downtime into true downtime and precautionary urgent categories.
