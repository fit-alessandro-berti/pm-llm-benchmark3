DOMAIN SEMANTICS

Treat these as useful but defeasible rules rather than infallible axioms.

1. `Create Order` normally relates to exactly one Order.
2. `Add Item` normally relates to one Order and one Item.
3. `Pack Item` normally relates to one Item and one Package. A scanner outage can create an item-only row that is reconciled later.
4. `Dispatch Package` normally relates to one Package and one Delivery. A bulk dispatch event may cover multiple packages.
5. `Confirm Delivery` may refer to one delivery, several deliveries acknowledged together, or only a customer/order if the portal loses the carrier identifier.
6. `Issue Invoice` relates to one Invoice and one or more Orders. Fees, discounts, and taxes mean that arithmetic equality is evidence, not proof.
7. `Record Payment` relates to exactly one Payment and normally one Invoice. Unapplied cash is possible.
8. O2O qualifiers are directional. For example, `DEL-X carries PK-7` is not stored as `PK-7 carries DEL-X`.
9. Matching timestamps and identifier prefixes are informative but not decisive.
10. It is acceptable to leave a relation unresolved.
