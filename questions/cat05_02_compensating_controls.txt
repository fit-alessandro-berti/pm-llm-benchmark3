You are reviewing payment-process conformance for an industrial group. The nominal four-eyes principle is sometimes violated, but preventive, detective, and corrective controls differ across cases. There is no single canonical answer. An LLM judge will evaluate whether you reason from control objectives, timing, coverage, independence, recoverability, and case risk rather than merely counting activities.

Do not ask questions. Do not equate “a control occurred” with “the control was sufficient.” Do not classify solely from payment amount or raw four-eyes conformance.

NOMINAL PROCESS RULE FEP-1
For every completed payment, the human resource performing Prepare_Payment must differ from the human resource performing Release_Payment. Shared service accounts are not independent human actors. The purpose is to prevent one person from both initiating and authorizing an erroneous or fraudulent transfer.

COMPENSATING CONTROLS

AV-2 — Automated validation before release
- Checks duplicate invoice, sanctions-list status, bank-account ownership, bank-account changes during the previous seven days, amount against operator limit, and basic purchase-order match.
- Can block release or emit a warning that an authorized manager may override.
- Does not establish that goods/services were received, detect collusion, validate commercial reasonableness, or establish who controlled a compromised account.
- A clean pass is strong evidence only for the checks actually covered.

MG-4 — Independent manager approval
- Strongest when performed before release or before irrevocable bank settlement.
- The manager must be level L3 or above, not the preparer/releaser, and not in a relationship that creates an unresolved conflict of interest.
- A rubber-stamped approval without reviewing invoice, bank details, and supporting receipt is weak.

RA-7 — Retrospective audit and recall
- Emergency same-user payments should receive 100% audit within one business day.
- The bank can usually recall a transfer within 24 hours; after that, recovery is uncertain.
- Audit is detective. It becomes partly corrective only when a defect can still be stopped, recalled, or recovered.

ED-1 — Emergency single-operator permission
A single operator may prepare and release only when all conditions hold:
1. an emergency token was active before Prepare_Payment;
2. amount is below EUR 100,000;
3. vendor has been active for at least 180 days;
4. no bank-account change occurred in the previous seven days;
5. AV-2 was fully operational and passed without override;
6. an independent MG-4 manager approved before bank settlement; and
7. RA-7 audit completed within one business day.
ED-1 is a permission with conditions, not a statement that the four-eyes objective disappears.
