NORM INTERPRETATION

Each normative relation has one of four statuses:

- mandatory: expected whenever its stated scope applies;
- forbidden: explicitly disallowed;
- typical: common and desirable, but exceptions may be legitimate;
- unspecified: intentionally outside the norm’s coverage.

The norm is closed-world only for relations explicitly marked forbidden and for the mandatory columns within the stated scope. It is otherwise open-world.
