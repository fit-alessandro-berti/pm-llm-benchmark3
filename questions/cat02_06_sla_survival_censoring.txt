ANALYTICAL TASK
Design a process-aware survival analysis of SLA tail risk. The phrase “probability of breaching the SLA” is intentionally ambiguous. Define at least two useful estimands, for example:
- probability of not being resolved by 72 hours;
- cumulative incidence of resolution after 72 hours;
- probability of still having no terminal disposition at 72 hours;
- cause-specific risk under competing withdrawal/rejection/transfer outcomes.

Explain which estimand best matches which operational decision. Distinguish right censoring from competing events and from already-observed breaches. Do not analyze only completed resolved cases. Address the possibility that transfers and long event gaps make censoring informative.

Use the prefixes to propose process-aware covariates or a multi-state representation. Discuss immortal-time and leakage risks: an activity such as `Escalate` occurs after case start and cannot safely be treated as a baseline covariate without a time-aware design.
