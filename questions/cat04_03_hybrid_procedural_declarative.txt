SCENARIO: CONTROLLED VENDOR ONBOARDING
Use the following visible activity labels exactly as written:

- Receive Onboarding Request
- Capture Master Data
- Run Sanctions Screening
- Verify Bank Account
- Classify Risk
- Request Clarification
- Receive Clarification
- Sanctions Escalation
- Obtain Legal Opinion
- Enhanced Due Diligence
- Manager Approval
- Compliance Approval
- Reject Vendor
- Activate Vendor
- Change Bank Details
- Suspend Vendor
- Release First Payment
- Close Onboarding

The stable procedural backbone is:

1. Receive Onboarding Request starts every case.
2. Capture Master Data, Run Sanctions Screening, and Verify Bank Account may proceed concurrently after receipt.
3. Classify Risk requires completion of all three checks.
4. After classification, the case follows one primary outcome:
   - reject the vendor; or
   - use the low-risk route, requiring Manager Approval; or
   - use the high-risk route, requiring Enhanced Due Diligence followed by Compliance Approval.
5. Activation follows the applicable approval. Release First Payment follows activation. Close Onboarding follows either rejection or first payment.

Cross-cutting requirements and stakeholder statements are:

R1. Reject Vendor and Activate Vendor must never occur in the same case.
R2. Exactly one of Manager Approval and Compliance Approval should occur in an accepted case. Neither should occur in a rejected case.
R3. Every Request Clarification must eventually receive a Receive Clarification before the next clarification request and before any final outcome. Several request/response cycles are possible.
R4. If Sanctions Escalation occurs, Obtain Legal Opinion must eventually occur.
R5. Legal department: after Sanctions Escalation, Obtain Legal Opinion must precede Activate Vendor under all circumstances.
R6. Operations department: emergency activation may occur before Obtain Legal Opinion, but only when jointly authorized outside the event log; the opinion must then arrive within 24 hours. The event log does not contain an “emergency” attribute or the joint authorization.
R7. Sanctions Escalation may occur only after Run Sanctions Screening.
R8. Enhanced Due Diligence and Manager Approval should not co-occur. Compliance Approval and Manager Approval should not co-occur.
R9. The resource performing Verify Bank Account must differ from the resource performing the applicable approval. The resource performing Capture Master Data must differ from the resource performing Activate Vendor.
R10. Activate Vendor and Reject Vendor each occur at most once. Release First Payment occurs at most once.
R11. If Change Bank Details occurs after activation, Suspend Vendor must eventually follow it; another Verify Bank Account must occur before any later Release First Payment.
R12. Compliance considers the following temporal expectations normative enough to flag but not automatically reject a case:

| Activity pair | Mean elapsed time | Standard deviation |
|---|---:|---:|
| Request Clarification → Receive Clarification | 48 hours | 24 hours |
| Sanctions Escalation → Obtain Legal Opinion | 12 hours | 6 hours |
| Activate Vendor → Release First Payment | 72 hours | 36 hours |

Use `zeta = 2.0` for temporal-profile deviations.

PM4PY DICTIONARY AND API REMINDERS

A Log Skeleton uses the keys:
`equivalence`, `always_before`, `always_after`, `never_together`, `activ_freq`, and `directly_follows`. Pair-valued entries are sets of activity pairs. `activ_freq` maps activities to allowed occurrence-count sets.

A DECLARE model may use:
`existence`, `absence`, `exactly_one`, `init`, `responded_existence`, `coexistence`, `response`, `precedence`, `succession`, `altresponse`, `altprecedence`, `altsuccession`, `chainresponse`, `chainprecedence`, `chainsuccession`, `noncoexistence`, `nonsuccession`, and `nonchainsuccession`.

A temporal profile has the form:

```python
{("A", "B"): (average_seconds, standard_deviation_seconds)}
```

Relevant calls include:

```python
pm4py.conformance_declare(log, declare_model)
pm4py.conformance_log_skeleton(log, log_skeleton)
pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile(log, temporal_profile, zeta=2.0)
pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(log, activity1, activity2)
```
