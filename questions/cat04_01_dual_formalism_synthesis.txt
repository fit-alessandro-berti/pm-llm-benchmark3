PROCESS NARRATIVE
A manufacturer handles material-deviation cases using the following activity labels:

- Register Deviation
- Contain Material
- Analyze Root Cause
- Plan Verification
- Design Corrective Action
- Supervisor Review
- Quality Review
- Regulatory Review
- Consult Supplier
- Execute Trial
- Inspect Trial
- Adjust Parameters
- Release Material
- Archive Case

The intended behavior is described below.

1. Every case starts with Register Deviation and ends with Archive Case.
2. After Register Deviation, Contain Material and Analyze Root Cause may proceed independently and overlap.
3. Plan Verification may start only after Contain Material is complete.
4. Design Corrective Action may start only after Analyze Root Cause is complete.
5. The risk route is selected after Analyze Root Cause:
   - low-risk cases require Supervisor Review;
   - high-risk cases require both Quality Review and Regulatory Review, which may occur concurrently and in either completion order.
6. Consult Supplier is optional and occurs at most once. When selected, it may start only after Analyze Root Cause. It may overlap corrective-action design, verification planning, approvals, and trial work, but it must be complete before Release Material. Whether it is selected depends on case data that is not represented in the control-flow model.
7. Execute Trial may start as soon as both Design Corrective Action and Plan Verification are complete. The selected approval route does not have to be complete before the trial starts and may overlap the trial cycle.
8. A trial cycle consists of Execute Trial followed by Inspect Trial. If inspection fails, Adjust Parameters occurs and a new Execute Trial–Inspect Trial cycle is performed. This repeats until an inspection passes. Adjust Parameters must never occur after the passing inspection.
9. Release Material requires all of the following: a passing Inspect Trial, completion of the selected approval route, and completion of Consult Supplier if that optional activity was selected.
10. Archive Case occurs after Release Material.
11. Activities are recorded at completion time. The model is activity-based and need not encode data conditions such as “low risk,” “high risk,” “supplier involved,” or the inspection result. Those conditions must instead be represented through control-flow alternatives and documented assumptions.

The dependencies deliberately form a non-block-structured pattern: Plan Verification depends on containment, Design Corrective Action and approvals depend on root-cause analysis, the trial waits for the first two work products, approvals may overlap the trial, and release synchronizes these independently progressing obligations.
