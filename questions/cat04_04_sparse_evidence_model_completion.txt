DOMAIN RULES WITH DIFFERENT CONFIDENCE LEVELS

D1 — high confidence: Register Order is the intended start; Archive Case is the intended end.
D2 — high confidence: Release Production requires Approve Configuration.
D3 — medium-to-high confidence: both Credit Check and Engineering Review should be completed before Approve Configuration.
D4 — medium confidence: Supplier Confirmation, when required, must occur before Approve Configuration.
D5 — low-to-medium confidence: Supplier Confirmation may begin after either Credit Check or Engineering Review has produced enough information; it need not wait for both.
D6 — medium confidence: Expedite Review is optional, occurs at most once, requires completion of both Credit Check and Engineering Review, and precedes Approve Configuration.
D7 — unknown: completion-order timestamps do not reveal whether Credit Check and Engineering Review were actually concurrent, sequential, or overlapping.

STAKEHOLDER AND DATA-QUALITY STATEMENTS

S1 — Finance manager: “Credit Check must finish before Engineering Review starts.”
S2 — Engineering lead: “The checks normally run in parallel; whichever finishes first appears first in the log.”
S3 — Procurement lead: “Supplier Confirmation is mandatory for every external component.”
S4 — Production planner: “Stocked components legitimately skip Supplier Confirmation.”
S5 — Data engineer: “The external/stocked attribute was not exported. Around 5–10% of Supplier Confirmation events may also be missing because of an integration problem.”
S6 — Compliance officer: “Expedite Review is permitted only after both principal checks. It may occur before or after Supplier Confirmation, but before approval.”
S7 — Logging note: only completion timestamps are available. Activities with the same timestamp are ordered by ingestion index.

YOUR TASK
Construct three models:

1. `model_conservative`: prioritize not admitting behavior unsupported by the observed variants or high-confidence rules;
2. `model_balanced`: encode the most plausible generalization while explicitly managing uncertainty;
3. `model_permissive`: admit reasonable but unobserved combinations consistent with the broad domain rules.

Use PM4Py ProcessTree and/or POWL code. At least one candidate must be a ProcessTree and at least one must be POWL. You may use an XOR enumeration for the conservative model, but discuss duplicate labels and scalability. You may represent missing data conditions through control-flow alternatives, but must disclose the abstraction.
