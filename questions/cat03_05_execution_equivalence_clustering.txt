You are given eight compact object-centric executions centered on a lead object of type Order. Define useful equivalence criteria, cluster the executions, and explain the business semantics of the clusters.

There is intentionally no single correct clustering. Exact structural equivalence, typed isomorphism, behavioral equivalence, and business-semantic equivalence may produce different answers. Your answer will be evaluated on explicit definitions, reproducibility, and meaningful treatment of borderline cases.

NOTATION

An event is written as:

Activity{ObjectType:object_ids; ObjectType:object_ids; ...}

Events are listed in timestamp order. Object identifiers are local unless an identifier begins with `EXT`, which denotes an object whose lifecycle is centered on another Order execution.

O2O notes follow each sequence.

Add Item{Order:O1; Item:I11}
Add Item{Order:O1; Item:I12}
