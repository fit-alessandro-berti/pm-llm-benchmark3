3.2

The submission has the required heading skeleton and a few correct fully observed durations, but it is not a reliable reconstruction. Numerical errors, inconsistent uncertainty handling, a broken WIP definition, and a Python section that misses most mandated PM4Py/pandas requirements keep it far from the quality bar.

**Case-level arithmetic is frequently wrong.** Aggregating P01–P04 as S1 wait 5–10, S1 service 20–25, S2 wait 5, S2 service 30 is false (P03/P04 S1 waits are 20 min; S2 waits are 5/5/10/15; S2 services are 30/40/40/40). P15 S2 wait is 13:30−12:15 = **75 min**, not 15. P17 is labeled `OPEN_S1, OPEN_S2` even though S1 finished at 12:50. P18–P20 are omitted. Case-level total sojourn for completed cases is never reported.

**Missing starts are not reconstructed under the constraints the write-up itself states.** A4 claims resource adjacency, but the table uses near-vacuous windows: P07 S1 wait [15, 75] and service [20, 80] ignore that I1 is on P05 until 09:25 (feasible start ≈[09:25, 10:00], wait ≈[40, 75], service ≈[0, 35]). P15 S1 [0, 115]/[5, 120] ignores I1 on P13 until 11:40. P05/P11 S2 lower waits of 5 min ignore C1 busy until 09:45 and C2 busy until 12:00. Wait is given as a range while service is a single number (15 min), which cannot be true for a missing start: wait and service are complements of a fixed end−ready interval.

**Open and waiting cases are treated as finished or discarded.** OPEN service is written as 30 and 5 min, not elapsed lower bounds. P16/P18–P20 S2 wait is “—”, not ≥85/≥50/≥35/≥15 at 14:00. P21 service “30 min” is only elapsed time. That directly violates the quality bar: do not confuse active/elapsed service with final service, and do not drop right-censored cases.

**Station metrics do not match the log.** S1 08:00–10:00 arrivals are not 7 (P01–P13 arrive before 10:00). S1 12:00–14:00 arrivals are not 7 (only P22). S2 08:00–10:00 arrivals are S1 completions (~7), not 4. S1 waiting “5–95, median 50” understates a sequence that climbs through 100–105 min. S1 busy 500–550 min is roughly the **completed-only** sum and omits P07, P15, and P21 elapsed time; feasible S1 busy is ~600+ min on 720 min of inspector time (~80–85%+). S2 busy 560–600 on 720 min is ~78–83%, not the stated 65–75%. Utilization is therefore both miscomputed and inconsistent with the busy-time column.

**WIP/queue accounting is conceptually wrong.** Section 2 defines WIP as arrivals minus completions (reasonable). The code does arrival +1, start −1, **and** end −1, so a case leaves the count at start and is subtracted again at end (path to negative “WIP”). That series is then reported as mean/peak WIP. Queue vs in-service is never separated. Simultaneous rounded events are named in A1 but never turned into a time-in-state convention (who occupies the resource at a shared end/start instant).

**Bottleneck narrative fights its own numbers.** Mid-morning is called an S2 bottleneck while the same table gives S1 utilization *higher* than S2. Late-window “S1 re-emerges” with S1 WIP 6–8 vs S2 4–6 is not what the 14:00 snapshot shows: S2 has four waiters plus two open jobs; S1 has P21 open and P22 waiting. The C2 outage is mentioned, but not the 10:55–11:00 interval when C1 is still on P07 (S2 capacity briefly 0). Arrival and departure *rates* are not actually estimated.

**Sensitivity is theatrical.** Three scenarios are asserted as single utilization points with no recomputed waits, WIP paths, or resource-feasible imputations. Rounding (±2.5 min per stamp, up to ±5 min on a duration) is never propagated. Little’s Law is not carried in the prose; the script mixes all arrivals with completed-only sojourn and a broken WIP average, then declares an approximate hold.

**Section 7 fails the spec.** `MISSING`, `OPEN`, and blank are all parsed to `None`. Only fully observed closed intervals enter the event log (no OPEN-to-14:00 intervals, no waiting cases). The code never calls `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` or `pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`, never calls `pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals`, and has no comment explaining why the latter is skipped. Uncertainty scenarios are not implemented. `capacity` uses a leftover loop variable `time`. Notes below the fence claim comments and compact tables that are not there.

**Presentation.** The document is wrapped in a `markdown` fence with a preface, so it is not “exactly these top-level headings.” Symbols for ≥, λ, μ, ρ are dropped, leaving broken formulas (“Final service time elapsed”, “L = W”).

What remains is a correctly titled outline, several accurate mid-table point durations (P06, P08–P14 S1, some S2), and a recognition that missingness and the C2 outage matter. That is not enough for a defensible queue reconstruction under the stated bar.
