**4.5**

The document follows the required nine-heading skeleton and the later governance sections (visual misread, rejection, canonical storage) are competent. That is not enough. This is a semantic-invariant task, and the invariant table — the foundation everything else depends on — systematically reverses concurrency and optionality.

**Fatal witness errors.** `I-CONC-ASSESS-PREP` lists `Register → Prepare Evidence → Assess Risk` as a *negative* witness. That interleaving is licensed by the source. The same mistake is repeated for `I-UNORDER-LEGAL-VERIF` (Legal-before-loop vs loop-before-Legal are both valid) and `I-OPT-LEGAL` (the path *with* Legal Review is treated as negative). A negative witness of a permission is not “the other allowed branch.” These three rows are exactly the properties the prompt told the author to protect: absence of order, optionality, overlap of legal review with verification. Used as written, the audit would reject a *correct* conversion.

**“Validated” is unearned.** Section 4 marks ordering, join-before-release, finality, and BPMN round-trip as `validated preserved`. Section 5 never checks a witness: firing is a comment (`# assume a helper fire_transition exists`), positive/negative loops are `pass`, and reachability is compared by state count. On this rubric those cells must be `expected preserved` or `not tested`. Calling them validated equates conversion success with behavioral fidelity — the failure mode the prompt forbids.

**The explorer is semantically wrong, not just incomplete.** `hide_tau` *drops* enabled silent transitions instead of firing them without appending a label. The skip-legal τ must fire for the optional branch to complete; never firing it cannot reach final marking on the skip path and will false-fail `I-OPT-LEGAL`. There is also no silent-cycle bound on τ-firing (only a visible-length bound), and `max_loop_iter` is threaded but never incremented. The required exact-final-marking test is therefore not implemented.

**Smaller but real gaps.** `bpmn_from_tree` is never converted back to an accepting net, so “each produced BPMN” is not honored. Process-tree failure is asserted in the audit table without an executed exception. `convert_to_bpmn(tree_from_net)` is plausible but not the documented `(net, im, fm)` form; a defensive tree→net→BPMN path is missing. Residual claim 2 calls language/concurrency equivalence “undecidable in general”; 1-safe language equivalence is decidable — the real residue is pomset/true-concurrency and LOOP-operator interpretation, which they do mention elsewhere.

Sections 2, 6, 7, and 8 are the only parts that would survive a strict review: two pipelines with a justified primary, no invented POWL→BPMN API, layout vs semantics, and POWL/Petri as authority with BPMN as derivative. They cannot offset an invariant table that would quarantine correct models.
