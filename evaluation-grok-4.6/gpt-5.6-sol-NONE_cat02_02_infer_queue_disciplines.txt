## Decision-set errors

The task asks the analyst to identify, at each start or preemption, which jobs were plausibly waiting and eligible. That is not done reliably.

**QA at 08:20.** A04 arrived at 08:13. The eligible set is A02, A03, **and A04**, not just A02 and A03. The observed choice is still A03, which actually *strengthens* an appointment reading: A03 jumps both older A02 and higher-priority, shorter A04. The answer instead reports a priority tie among p3 jobs and an SPT prediction of A03 (10 vs 20). Both predictions are wrong. SPT and non-preemptive priority both predict A04 (`est_min` 7, priority 1). This is not a rounding quibble; it is the first discriminating decision in QA.

**QA at 08:30–08:32.** A05 arrived at 08:21 and A06 at 08:29. The answer lists only A02 and A04. A06 can perhaps be withheld for a 09:15 appointment; A05 cannot. The FIFO conclusion survives, but the reconstructed set is incomplete.

**QD at 11:21 on D-F.** D06 is electrical and D-F has electrical skill. Unless an appointment hold is asserted explicitly, D06 belongs in that set.

**QD at 11:25 on D-M.** D09 arrived at 11:20. The note “D09 arrives later” is false.

It undercuts a clean “skill constraints plus local FIFO” story and needed an explicit exception (advance assignment of D10 to D-X, a pre-meeting freeze, or an unrecorded hold).

Section 2 and Section 3 correctly treat A04 as the oldest remaining job at 08:58. The diagnosis table therefore contradicts the body and invents a waiting job. The QB contradiction is also misstated: B04 is selected “before older, higher-priority B06.” B04 arrived at 09:35; B06 arrived at 10:05. B06 is higher priority, not older.
