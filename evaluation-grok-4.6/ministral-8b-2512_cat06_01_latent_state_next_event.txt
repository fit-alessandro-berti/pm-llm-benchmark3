The answer looks structured and process-flavored, but it fails the core scoring dimensions: latent-state identification, next-event prediction, and disciplined use of the given abstraction. Several errors are not stylistic. They invert the process position of the live cases.

**State inference is systematically wrong.** S3 `EVIDENCE_REVIEW` is defined as: evidence has been received and review may request more evidence, open investigations, or support closure. The write-up then contradicts itself. The C6-A object says `EVIDENCE_PENDING`; `same_prefix_different_state` says C6-A is `INVESTIGATION_FORK_OPEN`. Both cannot be the intended primary state.
