**6.5**

The design is coherent and hits the required eight-section shape, but it is not close to flawless. Several explicit deliverables are missing, several methods are named rather than specified, and a few decision numbers fight the data.

**What is solid.** The twin is not a set of independent cases. Arrivals are non-stationary, resources have calendars, and architect bursts, district batching, and latent objections are first-class. Service and waiting are kept separate in the narrative, and objections are treated as a mixture/proxy rather than as observed events. The experiment table covers baseline, D1–D10, D10 as a bundle, and more than three stress cases. Validation and uncertainty sections list the right families of checks (replay, distributional, utilization, bottleneck, time-split OOS, CRN, bootstrap tails, sensitivity). The code correctly avoids `pm4py.read_csv`, uses `format_dataframe` with the required keys, and keeps PM4Py discovery away from the DES engine.

**Required output gaps (costly).** The skeleton never builds service/waiting columns, even as NaNs with a comment that starts are missing. That was an explicit compute requirement. There is also no experiment-execution placeholder (no loop over BASE/D1–D10/stress, no replication/seed/policy config, no metric roll-up)—only a single `SimulationEngine.run` that raises `NotImplementedError`. The inline log is a two-row stub, not the provided excerpt. `start_timestamp_key=None` is a plausible PM4Py footgun; the argument should be omitted.

**Method and grounding problems.** Parameter estimation name-drops Hawkes, hierarchical Bayes, and Sobol without identification steps, and it barely uses the given mix, variant, and timing tables as calibration targets. Travel time (D3/D5), no-shows, consultant delays, fire-area thresholds, prior violations, and the Decision-drafting join on all applicable reviews are under-specified in the state vector. Starting from a “representative WIP snapshot” *and* discarding 180 days is not justified. Averaging 24 non-stationary months hides seasonality. Fifty replications are thin for p90/p95 of heavy tails. KS tests on cycle times ignore shared-queue dependence. SLA cutoffs (30/60/90/180 days) are loose or misaligned with the empirical medians/p90s. Using “statistically significant median improvement” as the selection rule is a weak decision criterion. Effect sizes (50% fewer missing-doc loops, 20% faster responses) are invented. Minus signs disappeared in the service/wait formulas, which makes those sentences ambiguous.

**Bottom line.** This is a competent consulting outline with the right architecture and honest data-gap treatment, not a near-complete, internally consistent digital-twin design. The missed code requirements and the poorly grounded decision/estimation details keep it well below the top of the scale.
