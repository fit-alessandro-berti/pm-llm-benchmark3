**8.2**

This is a strong, well-structured investigation: event-time roles, alias normalization, three-valued authority logic, SoD, RR4, and collusion hygiene are mostly handled with care. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading, several judgment, modeling, and verification gaps are material.

## What is actually right

Case-level SoD is correct after alias resolution: **CHG-502**, **CHG-508**, and **CHG-510** fail RR1; **CHG-508** is a real same-human failure, not a data glitch; LOW risk and emergency status do not waive RR1. **CHG-507** is a clean RR3 miss (Eva is Independent Verifier on 9 Aug, D-QM-02 is later and wrong scope). D-IC-04 is void because D-IC-03 forbids subdelegation. Service-account handling is event-specific: **CHG-503** stays unresolved; **CHG-509** is only provisionally Eva. The 18/23 pair statistic is kept historical and is not treated as collusion. Event-time roles are not read from a current QE/QM master. RR2 is not invented into a job-title rule for Grace or post-11-Aug Eva.

The 43-event / 33-handover arithmetic, the six Diego→Eva adjacencies, and the Clara–Diego / Clara–Ben co-case counts are consistent with the stated attribution policy.

## Material defects

**1. Section 5 is not empirically verified.** The writeup admits PM4Py was not executed. That is a direct miss on a scored requirement, not a footnote. `keep_violations=True` on `filter_four_eyes_principle` and especially on `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is version-dependent; if those kwargs are rejected, the “library checks” path crashes. Hardcoded `assert`s that encode the narrative findings also make the script a self-consistency check, not an independent reconstruction.

**2. `absence_at` treats “not on the absence list” as UNKNOWN, not present.** That is the wrong default for a condition that must be affirmatively true. It happens to matter mainly on **CHG-509** (Saturday, 15 Aug, after leave ends), which is why INDETERMINATE is defensible as a *classification*, but the function would also refuse to say Asha was present on an ordinary weekday. Weekend unavailability is a hypothesis; it is not evidence.

**3. CHG-504 as AUTHORIZED is too firm.** The only `u_as` event sits inside the VPN window, and Asha claims one personal intervention, but security cannot exclude credential sharing. “Provisional” and medium actor confidence belong in the label itself; primary AUTHORIZED overstates what the session evidence can carry. Asha calling it “urgent” while `emergency=false` is also unexamined.

**4. CHG-501 should carry a data-quality secondary.** The signed 9 Aug PDF can support VALID_SUBSTITUTION; the QMS row created 12 Aug still cannot prove 11 Aug authority by itself. The apparent deviation *is* stale/migrated master data. Omitting DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM as a secondary flattens that distinction.

**5. Delegation-scope conservatism is uneven.** Cooling vs production-equipment and “QM approval” vs retrospective review are correctly left unproven, but the CHG-510 note that Farid “was a valid IC delegate at the time” and that Clara relied on D-IC-04 (CI-44 onward) is underused. CI-44 membership should stay unproven; it should not be written as if there were no directional evidence.

**6. RR4 deadline code is opaque.**  
`normalize() + 2 * CustomBusinessDay + Timedelta(days=1)` happens to match “exclusive Friday 00:00 = Thursday EOD” on this snapshot, and the two emergency dates are robust to several nearby conventions. That does not make the offset a clear implementation of “two business days,” and the plant timezone is assumed away with UTC calendar dates.

**7. Presentation residue.** The `sandbox:/mnt/data/change_control_audit.py` link is an artifact. GitHub raw citations do not replace a live PM4Py run.

## Smaller logical nits

- Standing IC power is modeled only as a role set; subdelegation, if ever permitted, would not see Farid’s IC as delegated rather than standing.
- `purpose = True if APPROVE else None` is a global activity hack, not a per-instrument parse of D-QM-01’s wording.
- Network commentary is careful not to allege collusion, but PM4Py working-together on `UNRESOLVED:<event_id>` nodes will still emit junk edges unless readers stay on the pandas known-human table.
- Control-improvement recommendations are relevant but generic relative to the sharpness of sections 1–4.

## Bottom line

The resource story is mostly the right story: mixed genuine authority failures (502, 507, 508, 510, late 505), identity/attribution holes (503, 506, residual 504), one conditional delegation (509), and one supported substitution (501). The analysis earns that conclusion. It loses a high score because PM4Py was not actually run, absence is modeled as unknown-by-default, CHG-504 is over-authorized, and a few evidence edges are either over- or under-weighted.
