## Material analytical errors

**1. The L7 override story is backwards.** Narrative 1 (and Motif 2) claims J2’s supervisor override “forced J5 to bear the full duration of L7’s quality hold, directly pushing B2’s execution into the late afternoon.” J5 is held by L7 regardless of the override. The override lets J2 join B1 during the 10:20–13:50 quarantine, so B1 can start at 12:05. That *advances* RACK-7’s cleaning window (cleaning is done by 14:35). Without the override, B1 would likely wait past 13:50 and B2 would start *later*. The decoupling of J2 and J5 is real; the claimed direction of the B2 spillover is not. In a section whose job is causal hygiene, this is a serious miss.

**2. FIX-A is treated as if it explains J4’s day and S1.** J4’s process starts at 11:45, five minutes after FIX-A is released — that local association is fair. Claiming a 10:05 start and a 100-minute saving is not. CNC-2 is idle only from 10:05 to 10:47; the machine-level “longest gap” is 48 minutes, so something else is recorded at 10:47. The answer never asks what that event is. Narrative 2 then “speculates” that the fixture delay “directly shifted J4’s completion to 19:10” and thereby locked J1 until 19:25. J4 still has B2 (13:10 ready, 14:50 start), inspect, possible rework (2/6 jobs), and pack. A morning fixture wait cannot be promoted to the cause of a 19:10 pack time.
