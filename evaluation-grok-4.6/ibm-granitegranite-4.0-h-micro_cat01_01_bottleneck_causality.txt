## Fatal empirical error

The central pattern in the supplied tables is a **crude-vs-stratified reversal**. Orion is slower in the aggregate (21.96 vs 15.24) and **faster in every complexity stratum** (High 31.57 vs 35.00; Medium 17.17 vs 19.38; Low 7.50 vs 9.64). The evaluation brief says a strong answer must detect that reversal and not treat it as automatic identification. This answer never states it and repeatedly inverts it:

- High-complexity row: numbers say Orion is faster, then the text says “Orion worse for high complexity.”
- Executive summary treats Orion’s higher mean as an efficiency problem that reassignment might fix, and even claims high-complexity cases “benefit from Nova’s reviewer pool.” The process facts say the opposite: Orion has more certified reviewers and is selected for difficult cases.
- Night comparison invents **9.50h vs 5.06h**. Those are not night cycle times (they look like Low-complexity waits). Night Orion cases are mostly medium/high and much longer.

Once the reversal is missed, the 50% reassignment discussion is backwards. The main risk is **backfire**: moving Orion’s hard/night mix onto Nova, which is slower in every observed stratum and has almost no High/Night capacity.

## Causal discipline

The chosen verdict, `partly causal through mechanisms` at 55%, does not match the write-up. The text lists mix factors (complexity, shift, load, compliance) as if they were **unobserved** confounders — they are observed — then never separates **total vs direct vs mediated** Orion effects. Complexity is pre-routing case mix, not a mediator of group. That error is repeated in §3 and as rank-1 in §5.

The graph is not an adjacency list (required). It is a thin mermaid diagram with a reversed arrow **`quality_outcome → cycle_time`**. The 30-day appeal is downstream and post-process. Missing, despite being in the allowed set: `complexity → routing_rule`, `complexity → compliance_required`, `shift`/`arrival_load`/`compliance` → `queue_wait`, group → wait/service/rework, `case_completed` selection, `unobserved_case_risk`. “Supported arrows” is a variable list, not arrows. The “feedback” example (`rework → cycle_time`) is just a forward path.

Role table mislabels almost everything: wait and rework as confounders of a group effect; review service as the estimand rather than a mediator; no collider; no `case_completed` selection; proxy mentioned only in passing.
