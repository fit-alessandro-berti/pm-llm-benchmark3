The final write-up (sections 1–7) is a strong, internally consistent business reading of the alignments. It is not near-flawless.

**Policy logic.** Flagging CL-610 as a P3 “pay before required approval” failure is wrong. P3 is a sequencing control (Pay occurs before the applicable approval). CL-610 is Auto Approve then Pay: the failure is using the wrong approval path under P1/P2, not paying before an approval event. That also slightly blurs the otherwise clean distinction between P3 (CL-604) and data-aware policy bypass (CL-609/CL-610).

- Heavy `try/except` would hide those failures rather than produce a faithful reproduction.
