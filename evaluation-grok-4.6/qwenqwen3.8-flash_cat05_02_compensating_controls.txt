The answer is strong on the dimensions the task actually grades: it reasons from control objectives, timing, independence, coverage, overrideability, recoverability, and case risk rather than counting events or mapping four-eyes form onto the verdict. Headings, classification vocabulary, and the required tables are all present. Same-user cases are not mechanically failed (AP-203/AP-207 are `OBJECTIVE_MET`; AP-201 is only partial; AP-206 is indeterminate), and distinct-user cases are not mechanically passed (AP-204 fails; AP-209 is indeterminate). That is the central intellectual requirement, and it is done well.

What keeps it from a top score is a set of real, if mostly small, defects.

**Classification nits.** AP-204 as `OBJECTIVE_FAILED` is defensible because a fabricated receipt cleared prepare/release, but the log has no `Bank_Settlement`, and the facts never say funds left or that recall failed. The row notes missing settlement evidence and uses only medium confidence, yet still states failure as the objective result. A stricter reading is that preventive four-eyes failed while detective capacity is untimed, which is closer to a hedged partial/failed call than a clean fail. AP-201 as `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET` tracks the prompt’s “technically nonconforming but tolerable” idea, but the rationale leads with “low-risk profile.” Amount is not the sole basis, yet it is more prominent than the prompt’s warning comfortably allows; the unauthorized (no-token) character of the same-user path could have been weighted more heavily. AP-203 is stated as ED-1 conditions *are* met; the source text only says they *appear mostly* satisfied. That is a small overclaim.

**Section 5 is thinner than claimed.** The counterfactual table covers six cases and avoids fake probabilities, but it does not actually use qualitative ranges in a consistent way (high/medium/low residual, high/medium/low chance an independent releaser would have stopped the loss). Several cells are narrative restatements of Section 3 rather than a causal-chain analysis with an exposed remainder.

**AP-208 package is slightly under-specified in the case row.** Amount, bank-change, AV outage, post-settlement manager, and late audit are captured. The unsigned manual checklist—the only purported substitute for AV-2 at release time—is pushed to missing evidence instead of being treated as a weak, non-equivalent control in the preventive column.

**Code is careful but not clean.** It meets every listed mechanical requirement: embedded CSV, UTC parse, `format_dataframe`, `filter_four_eyes_principle`, blocked-attempt handling, joined facts, evidence flags, and an explicit “not a verdict” label. Version hedging is appropriate. The defects are in the facts construction: `manager_independent=False` for m21 (independence is unknown), `manager_independent=False` on cases with no manager at all, and `vendor_active_days=30` invented for a “new vendor.” Those flags can mislead a later reviewer even if the prose does not rely on them.

**No serious reasoning failure.** Compensating controls are not treated as sufficient merely because they occurred; ED-1 is treated as a conditional permission; lucky legitimacy (AP-208) is not treated as control success; collusion/shared-workstation risk is separated from nominal four-eyes form. Those are the easy ways to fail this task, and the answer avoids them.
