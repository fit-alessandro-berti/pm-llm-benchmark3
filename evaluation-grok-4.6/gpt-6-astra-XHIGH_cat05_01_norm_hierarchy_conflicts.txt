8.3

This is a strong, process-aware opinion: issue-specific hierarchy, time-of-act application, contemporaneous evidence over ERP stamps, no automatic post-hoc cure, and a clean split among JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION, NONCOMPLIANT, and INDETERMINATE. It does not treat ECD-2, GPIP-4, or the CFO email as a total ranking. Facts, interpretive choices, and recommendations are separated with unusual discipline. The remaining defects are real.

**Logical tension on burden of proof.** Section 1 requires positive support for an exception, then treats an identified Incident Command authorization with unproven A2 capacities as INDETERMINATE rather than a failed exception. That choice is explicit, but it is not reconciled with the stated burden. It drives P-505, P-507, and P-508. For P-505 the tension is sharpest: Finance_Approve at 19:00 is after every candidate commitment time. If D/IC substitution is not established, A2 has already failed and ECD-2 expiry is a second, independent issue. The write-up overweightsthe window question relative to that A2 problem.

**P-501’s controlling reading is the weakest primary premise.** Refusing to imply a B2 pre-commitment deadline is textually available (A2 and B1 have temporal language; B2 does not). It sits poorly with B5 (emergency does not waive Finance_Approve) and with the point of an approval control. The alternative in §5 is the better process-purpose reading; using the gap as the main rule is a substantive, not merely stylistic, choice. Labeling the B4 PO reversal JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION rather than compliance with a built-in emergency pathway is defensible but under-argued.

**Inconsistent operationalization.** Severity is not a scale: P-501 gets a sentence, P-506 a range, P-505/P-507/P-508 “potentially high if…”. Confidence is handled more carefully. Secondary classifications are allowed and would have made the dual character of P-505/P-507/P-508 visible; they are omitted.

**Small evidential and hierarchy gaps.** Create_PO at 12:20 and 12:40 on 14 July falls inside ON-44 unavailability and is not examined. C4’s “supplier-onboarding” phrase is the best textual competitor to A3 and belongs in the hierarchy table, not only as a rejected alternative in §5. “Cannot move Finance 13:05 before it” is unclear.

**What remains intact.** No invented facts; new vs unregistered suppliers are kept distinct; requester-equals-committer is not misread as an A1 breach; B4’s PO permission is not collapsed into Shift Lead substitution; P-501 cannot be pulled into ECD-2 by rolling a stamp backward; P-504 and P-506 are not rescued by labels or a verbal extension; P-507’s PDF is treated as recording evidence, not as an outage waiver; A6 is applied; the rule catalogue and defeasible logic match the prose; §6 separates breach, late upload, and unproven compliance; §7 does not back-write July 2026 authority.

The case outcomes are all arguable under the stated method. The method itself has one unstable joint (exception burden vs INDETERMINATE) and one weak primary reading (B2 timing). That is why this is not near-flawless.
