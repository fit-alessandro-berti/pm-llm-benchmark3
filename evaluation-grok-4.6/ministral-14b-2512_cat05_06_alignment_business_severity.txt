## Section 7 is not executable and violates the spec

Required: parse the given tree, convert to an accepting net, build a small log of the case traces, call `pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_alignments`, warn that PM4Py’s optimal alignments may differ from the *authoritative* supplied moves, build a dataframe of those supplied summaries, aggregate, and produce an evidence table **without replacing narrative judgment with a formula**.

The code does not do that:

- Unused and dubious imports (`xes_importer`, `pnml_importer`, `petri_net_to_df`); alignments imported from `pm4py.algo.conformance_diagnostics.alignments.algorithm`, which is not the public API requested.
- `convert_to_petri_net` returns `(net, im, fm)`; the tuple is never unpacked, so any `apply(log, pn)` call is malformed.
- The log is one case. `case:concept:name` has length 1 and `concept:name` has length 6, so the dataframe construction is invalid.
- `dataframe_utils.convert_to_dataframe` is the wrong entry point; the prompt allowed `parse_event_log_string` or a properly row-per-event frame.
- `variant=alignments.Variants.ALPHA` is a discovery algorithm, not an alignment variant.
- The supplied authoritative alignments are never loaded. Requirement 5 (PM4Py may differ; do not overwrite the given moves) is not stated in the code path.
- Severity is then computed with a lambda on `policy_violation` and `risk`. That *is* replacing narrative judgment with a formula.

A non-running appendix that contradicts item 7 would cap an otherwise clean answer. Here it lands on top of the interpretation errors.
