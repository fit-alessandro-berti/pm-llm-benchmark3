That is real process-mining competence — on a partial worksheet.

## Defects in the text that *was* submitted

**Broken mathematics.** Operators are systematically missing. As written:

- A1: `[t2.5 min, t+2.5 min]` is not the rounding interval (must be `[t − 2.5, t + 2.5]`).
- A5–A8 and all §2 definitions: `s1s  a`, `14:00  start`, `L = ·W`, `WIP(t) = #arrivals  t  #departures  t`. A reader cannot parse these without reconstructing the author’s intent. Under a strict reading the quantities are **not defined**.
- P07 note `(I1 free 09:2510:00)` and P05 `(09:2510:15)` have the same dash-stripping problem.

**Rounding is announced and then ignored.** A1 correctly notes that a duration from two rounded stamps has a ±5 min noise floor, then says this “is not re-derived for every number.” The case table still reports bare integers (`5`, `20`, `25`) as if exact. That is a milder form of the “silent point estimate” the prompt forbids.

**Imputation bounds are slightly looser than the log allows, and slightly tighter than A4 admits.**

- A4 says FIFO is *not* a hard constraint, but P11’s wait floor of 55 min **assumes** C2 cannot start P11 before P08 ends. That is defensible as a no-preemption / no-dual-occupancy constraint (A3), not as FIFO — the write-up never makes that distinction.
- A 5-minute C2 gap exists after the outage (11:10–11:15). Starting P11 there would collide with P08 at 11:15, so excluding it is right; the collision argument is never stated.
- Apparent same-resource adjacencies (P07 end 11:00 / P09 start 11:00 on C1) are left to A2. Fine, but A3’s “5 min overlap as rounding artifact” is never applied to any concrete pair.

**Central estimates are unjustified midpoints.** A5 says the midpoint is used “unless stated otherwise.” For P05, a start of 09:45 (C1 free) is much more plausible than a start immediately at 09:25 (C1 still on P03) or a 0-minute service ending at 10:15. Treating 15 min service as the “central” value is a convenience, not a likelihood. The prompt allowed this if **justified**; here it is only labeled.

**Censoring semantics are defined and then not used.** A6–A8 correctly separate elapsed service, elapsed wait, and final times. Because P15/P16/P17/P21/P22 never appear, those definitions do no work. The table also never reports station sojourn as a first-class column, only an occasional total-sojourn note for completed cases.

**Little’s Law is only a promissory note.** The “WIP returns to the same level” criterion is a reasonable proxy, but no interval is checked, no λ or L is computed, and “approximately the same level” is left unspecified. Announcing a method is not a consistency check.

**Small wording slips.**

- A9: “we do not assume C2 was still finishing a case at 10:55” is correct (P06 ended 10:50), but they never mention the idle 10:50–10:55 gap, which matters for utilization and for whether the outage was the first moment of lost C2 capacity.
- P08 is not uniquely “the first case queued through the C2 outage”: P09 is also in the S2 wait at 10:55 (entered 10:35). P08 is the first case **on C2** delayed across the outage. The note overstates uniqueness.
- “No resource is assumed to process two cases at once except where rounding could produce an apparent (5 min) overlap” — no such overlap is exhibited, so the hedge is unused.

## What cannot be credited because it is missing

The quality bar is explicit: do not confuse active service with elapsed station time, do not drop open cases, do not infer utilization from completed cases only, and show which conclusions survive timestamp/imputation choices.

This answer **never computes** queue-length paths, time-weighted WIP, subperiod arrival/departure rates, resource busy time, utilization ranges, or a bottleneck argument. It never compares optimistic / central / pessimistic scenarios. It never produces a PM4Py interval log, never calls `format_dataframe`, `insert_case_service_waiting_time`, or `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`, and never explains when those case-level tools are invalid for station-level claims.

A strong §1–§2 cannot substitute for that work. The unfinished table even withholds the cases that would have tested whether the author actually distinguishes elapsed vs final service (P15, P17, P21) and censored waits (P16–P20, P22).
