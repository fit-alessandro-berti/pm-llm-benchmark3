**5.4**

The write-up is well organized, hits the required headings, and gets the *strategic* call right: do not pick a winner by averaging scores; treat S as exploratory-only; treat C as an overfit enum; demand a third design plus a negative suite. That is not enough. The task is a language-and-policy audit. The behavioral reasoning is wrong in several places that the rubric treats as central, and the third model reintroduces a constraint the policy statement explicitly forbids.

**Process-tree semantics for S are incorrect.**  
`->(Register, +(X(CC,τ), X(TR,τ), X(SR,τ), X(ER,τ)), X(MA, AA), Release)` is a sequence. The AND-block sits *between* register and approval. Each child fires at most once. There is no loop and no `Correct Dossier`. S therefore cannot emit checks after approval, cannot emit both approvals, cannot emit a repeated check, and cannot emit a correction. Section 3 still claims the parallel block can put checks after approval “if parsing is loose,” lists a double-approval trace, and lists `…, Credit, Technical, Credit, …` as admitted. Those traces are not in the language. Calling this “loose parsing” is not the precise uncertainty the prompt asked for; it is a misread of sequential composition.

**The probe matrix has material errors.**  
B2 (`Technical Review` then `Credit Check` then `Auto Approval`) is *not* in C. C’s only Auto branch is `Credit, Technical, Auto`. Saying “Yes (Trace 2/8)” ORs two different traces. B6 and B8 are *not* in S (foreign label / extra occurrence). The matrix marks both as accepted. That is three wrong cells on the required 8-probe table, plus no note that “accept” was being used in some alignment-cost sense rather than language membership.

**The five “admitted” S traces do not meet the spec.**  
After discarding the impossible ones, the list collapses to skip-checks / all-τ and Expedite-before-checks — really one pathology (optional unordered block) stated twice. S *does* admit sharp, distinctive violations (skip only Technical Review; Expedite with no checks; Supplier/Expedite interleaved before both mandatories). Those are not used.

**The third model violates P4.**  
P4 says Expedite may occur before *or after* Supplier Review. The backbone is `Sequence(Parallel(CC, TR), XOR(SR, τ), XOR(ER, τ))`, which forces Supplier-or-skip *then* Expedite. `StrictPartialOrder` is named in the API reminder and is the obvious fix; it is never used. B6 is also rejected (loop redo requires *both* checks), which is acknowledged as an over-approximation but then not carried into the §8 matrix. §8 does not actually cover B1–B8. The three extra “adversarial” traces are sloppy (`T1` omits approval) and do not jointly separate Third from both S and C.

**`model_third` is not runnable PM4Py.**  
`pm4py.objects.powl.model.POWLModel`, `OperatorPOWL.PARALLEL` as a constructor tag, and wrapping a tree in `POWLModel(...)` are invented. Standard construction is `OperatorPOWL(Operator.PARALLEL, children)` / `StrictPartialOrder`. No DECLARE dict or executable external-check object is given, only a one-line `IF External` comment.

**§6 is incomplete relative to the required pathology list.**  
Missing-event bias, silent-transition effects, and alignment-cost sensitivity are not treated as first-class items (the last two appear only later as experiment ideas). Those are exactly the mechanisms that make S look “robust” and make the two teams’ numbers non-comparable.

**Smaller defects.**  
Dissenting rebuttal is ~140 words (below 150) before the evidence paragraph; still a real argument, not a token one. §2 comparability cells are uneven (training fitness’s “concern” is not a comparability concern). Duplicate-approval / flower-like readings of S recur instead of being withdrawn.

What remains strong is the metric skepticism (holdout collapse, positive-only precision, fitness-via-optionality, generalization-via-permissiveness), the use-case split, the validation program, and the governance stance. Those cannot offset a required behavior audit that misstates the languages of both submitted models and a “safer” third model that hard-codes an illegal Expedite/Supplier order.
