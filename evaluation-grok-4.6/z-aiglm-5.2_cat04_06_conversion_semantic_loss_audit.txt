**6.2**

The write-up is complete, well structured, and conceptually aligned with the task: POWL is treated as authoritative, there is no invented POWL→BPMN API, P2 is allowed to fail, and serialization/visualization is not treated as equivalence. That is the main reason it is not a low score. Under a strict reading it is still far from nearly flawless.

**What holds up.** Sections 1–2 and 6–9 are the strongest parts. The invariant set covers start, unordered Assess/Prepare, optional legal, the verification join, loop body order, redo via Correct Defect, ≥1 iteration, the release join, and finality. P1 is the right primary pipeline. The visual-semantics discussion (empty XOR edge, layout-as-order, anonymous gateways, flattened LOOP) and the storage-vs-visualization split are accurate. Residual uncertainty is unusually honest, especially on bounded traces, silent-path collapse, and pomset vs interleaving.

**Material defects.**

1. **Validation code is not faithful to the stated method.** `LOOP_ITER_BOUND` is declared and cited in the disclaimer, then never used. The required loop-iteration bound is therefore not implemented. Firing uses `fire_transition(t, marking)` and a fallback `pm4py.play_out_transition(...)`, neither of which is in the allowed PM4Py surface; the task forbids undefined conversion helpers. If both fail, enabled transitions are skipped, so a correct net can reject a positive witness.

2. **Net comparison is incomplete.** Footprints and reachability graphs are computed per net and not compared. That does not satisfy “compare `net_from_powl` with every round-tripped net.”

3. **Audit overclaims unobserved outcomes.** Petri→Tree is scored `conversion failed` for every invariant without a run. The non-SESE cross-dependency (Legal after Assess only; verification after Assess *and* Prepare) makes failure *likely*, but the rubric terms distinguish `conversion failed` from `not tested`. Using the former is an epistemic error. Section 4 is also almost only a table; the required discussion of true concurrency vs interleaving is deferred to §9.

4. **Witness quality is uneven, and §1 contradicts §9.** INV-02/INV-05 are marked observable after hiding τ, yet §9 correctly says interleaving traces cannot establish true concurrency. INV-02’s negative witness is a release-too-early trace, not a concurrency violation. INV-05’s negative is “Legal Review repeats,” which tests cardinality, not missing order. Several “positive” prefixes omit Prepare Evidence before Configure Trial. In the executable suite, Legal-during-verification is missing, and witness 4 is mislabeled as overlapping verification.

5. **Smaller inconsistencies.** INV-12’s “witnesses” are structural claims, not traces. `expected preserved` is used everywhere on POWL→Petri even though the same document never executes that path—acceptable caution, but then the tree column should have been equally cautious.

The conceptual frame is good enough that a cleaned API, an implemented loop bound, inter-net footprint comparison, and disciplined use of `not tested` vs `conversion failed` would move this a lot. As written, the conversion/validation core does not meet the same standard as the surrounding audit.
