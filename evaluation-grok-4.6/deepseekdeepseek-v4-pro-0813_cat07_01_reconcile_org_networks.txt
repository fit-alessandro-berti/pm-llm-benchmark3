**7.4**

The write-up hits the required headings, stays multi-hypothesis, and correctly treats high centrality as neither virtue nor vice. The handover vs. working-together contradiction is explained in the right causal terms: Mira is a high-throughput transfer node (~960 events / 905 cases, short 4.2‑minute touches), so directed flow and A–B–A subcontracting light up while undirected co-case rates stay low. Automation and vendors are not ignored. Removal is not recommended without a replacement function. Risks are calibrated; no misconduct is invented.

It is not near-flawless.

**Hypothesis set is softer than it looks.** Five labels are given, but A (exception desk), D (queue cleaner), and E (specialist scheduler) share almost the same mechanism and the same edges. B is mostly a risk restated as a role, which the prompt invited, but the write-up does not separate “what she does” from “what happens if she is the only one who does it.” Supporting and limiting evidence is recycled across hypotheses (Bot_Rules→Mira, absence shock, short service time), so the hypotheses are not independently pinned down.

**Missed structure that was sitting in the tables.** Team North (Alice–Carla–Elise) and Team South (Bruno–Diego–Farid) are stable working-together blocs. Mira’s low co-case rates with both leads are the signature of a *cross-team* broker, not just a “lightweight coordinator.” Alice/Bruno subcontracting is approval ping-pong; Mira’s is specialist consultation. That contrast is barely used, so role-discovery comments stay speculative (“would likely group with coordinators or Bot_Rules”) instead of predicting that activity vectors will *not* collapse Mira onto the team leads.

**Predictions and the executive summary leak a preferred story.** Several “future log” tests are circular (formalize the role, then observe formalization). The summary opens with “exception triage point” and only then lists alternatives, which undercuts the no-single-cause constraint in the one section a process owner will read.

**Analyses are valid but thin.** Named PM4Py calls match the brief. They mostly re-slice the same SNA already computed. Little is said about using `lifecycle:transition` to stop counting incomplete events, elapsed-time on specific arcs, or `discover_network_analysis` as a distinct object. Immediate recommendations are an unprioritized bundle.

Technically sound, complete, and usable. Not tight enough in causal separation or unused table structure to score higher under a strict bar.
