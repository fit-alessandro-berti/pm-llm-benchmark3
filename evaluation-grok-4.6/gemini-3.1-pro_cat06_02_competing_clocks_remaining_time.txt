**5.5**

The writeup has the right skeleton and avoids the classic failure modes (summing stage means, ignoring the weekend, one false-precise clock time). It is not close to a high score. Material calendar and queue arithmetic is wrong, internally inconsistent, or too tight, and several required uncertainty treatments are only named, not used.

**Dominant-clock error.** The smooth path is not “Chemistry, full stop.” Both branches are weekend-gated: incubation is technically ready Sunday 12:00–20:00, chemistry cannot finish Friday under any defensible discipline (urgent still outranks B-417; the second analyst frees only 16:42–17:30 and cannot complete a 1.5 h minimum service). Monday morning is a race. Priority leftover work can put chemistry at ~08:15–11:30, overlapping micro readout 08:30–09:30. Declaring chemistry the join clock and assigning **Low** sensitivity to weekend microbiology authorization treats a close race as a settled bottleneck. The sensitivity column is also misused: *if* that assumption is wrong, weekend authorization becomes first-order (possible Saturday join), so impact is high, not low.

**FIFO is not carried through.** Four jobs ahead, two servers, Friday residual only 2.5 h: under FIFO, B-417 typically does **not** start Friday. Monday it sits behind two in-progress residuals plus two full ordinary samples. Completion Monday 09:30–14:00 is essentially the priority case. The ledger itself says strict FIFO finishes “late Monday,” which already contradicts the smooth-path window; FIFO can spill to Tuesday. The given analyst residuals (0.4–0.9 h, 1.2–2.0 h) are never converted into free-slot times. “Waits behind 1–4 samples” is a shrug, not a queue model. A 50/50 blend is imposed on a stated 55/45 split and then not actually mixed.

**“Earliest plausible” is not earliest.** Monday 14:00 uses a mid/late chemistry time and ordinary QA, not optimistic tails. Even on the writeup’s own numbers, join 09:30 + short QA + 0.25 h release is Monday late morning, not 14:00. True optimistic envelope is closer to Monday ~10:30–12:00 (priority start Friday, short remaining service, weekend QA clearing much of the current five, short review). Using the slow end of chemistry as the early end of the terminal event is backwards.

**Internal contradictions.** Smooth decomp: terminal Monday 14:15–Tuesday 10:45. Table: earliest Monday 14:00, central Monday 16:00–Tuesday 10:00. Rerun “Monday 11:30–17:00” is not min+min or max+max on 09:30–14:00 plus triangular(1.0, 1.8, 3.0). First-result window is copied from the no-rerun completion window, so long first tests plus a same-day rerun are excluded. Rerun pause at 18:00 is ignored despite the problem stating it cannot continue after close.

**Micro confirmation clock is sloppy.** 20–30 h is timed from Monday 08:00, not from end of the 0.5–1.5 h readout that *detects* contamination. The Tuesday 08:30–15:30 completion band drops the late-Tuesday / Wednesday-08:00 readout spillover when 30 h lands after 18:00. The “delayed micro” terminal is then pushed to Wednesday as if investigation were already included, blurring two required scenario types.

**QA join-time queue is inconsistent.** 14.5 reviewer-hours vs ~5×1.5 h plus ~4 arrivals (~13.5 h) is a near wash, i.e. ~0–2 waiting in the median, not “~2–4.” Overdispersion can justify a wide band, but not a central 2–4 that ignores the same arithmetic. Join time is fixed at Monday 11:00 while chemistry is 09:30–14:00; leftover queue is not re-solved at those endpoints. Median 1.5 h / P80 3.5 h is treated as a hard service interval; the 20% tail beyond 3.5 h disappears.

**Dependence is labeled, not forecast.** Temperature excursion is correctly called a risk multiplier, then never used to shift rerun/contamination/investigation rates or to add a joint-anomaly row. The four scenarios remain roughly single-fault. Overlap is a parenthetical, not a partition (smooth ∩ query, rerun ∩ investigation, contamination ∩ lab investigation). Shortcuts (50/50 blend, P80-as-max, unstated triangular mean 2.83 h) are not declared.

**Mixture quantiles are poorly anchored.** Unconditional P50 as Monday 16:00–Tuesday 12:00 is only defensible if most query/doc-investigation mass still finishes by Tuesday midday; that is not shown. P80 as Wednesday noon–Thursday evening is fat without a CDF. “P90-like or severe-tail” at 28–30 September is lab-investigation territory (~20–40% of a ~0.15–0.20 investigation base ⇒ a few percent): that is a P95–P99 envelope sold as P90. Rejection is waived as “highly unlikely” with no link from contamination/lab path to “confirmed critical defect.”

**Section 3 is too thin for the non-smooth paths.** Weekend pause is mentioned once; Saturday QA is only in the ledger; electronic release’s continuous calendar is not used to explain why a Monday 19:40 accept can still release the same evening. Batch rejection is absent from the decomposition. Calculation is interval endpoints, not calendar traces.

What remains creditworthy: required six-section form; max-not-sum at the join; weekend lab closure; scenario envelope instead of one mean; qualitative dependence; investigation as tail driver; imminent Friday assignment as a secondary update. That is a competent sketch with broken numbers, not a defensible forecast.
