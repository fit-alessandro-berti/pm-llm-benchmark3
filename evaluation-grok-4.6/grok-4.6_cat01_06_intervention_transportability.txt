**7.3**

The answer is structurally complete, causally literate, and correctly refuses a naive copy of Site A. It still has enough modeling impurities, quantitative tension, and operational overconfidence that it is not close to flawless.

**What is actually strong.** The core analogical move is right: transport depends on the *enabled* partial order, not on sharing an AND of labels. Site A’s −9 h / −18 h contrast is treated as a confounded bundle (mix, +2 FTE, API, huddles, timestamps), not as an identified treatment effect. Statutory `Originals Receipt ≺ Document Check` is preserved. Bottleneck shift and “median up, tail down” are explicit. The candidate intervention (startable-set parallelization, cells D/O/L, startable WIP, first-action overlap) matches B’s constraints. The POWL sketch and PM4Py nesting (`Identity ∥ XOR(Originals→Doc, Doc)` then `verification ≺ Assessment`, optional `*(Legal, Supplement→Doc→Assessment)`) encode the stated dependencies rather than collapsing B into a sequence. The pilot’s fidelity-vs-mechanism split is the right learning target.

**Process table and transport graph.** Section 1 covers the required features and the extra ones that matter (data independence, exogenous delay, FTE). Minor wording slip: Site A after is said to have “same legal gates” after a before-state of “no originals gate.” Section 2 includes every required node and a within-band 265-word gloss, but the DAG is not a clean selection diagram. `digital_documents → originals_required` is definitional/inverse, not a mechanism. `S_queue_huddles` and `S_API_latency` treat A’s co-interventions as baseline selection variables; huddles and the API upgrade are time-varying treatments at A, not site-level covariates in the Bareinboim–Pearl sense. `originals_required → rework` is under-motivated. Modifiers on the `treatment_parallelization → true_overlap` arrow are drawn as direct causes of the mediator, which is usable but sloppy. `legal_capacity` is listed both inside “four capacity nodes” and again separately.

**Evidence and modifiers.** Section 3 is the best part: conservative confidences, no identification of the nine-hour drop, co-interventions given their own rows. Section 4 hits all mandated failure modes. Two weak cells: digital claims get “none specific if WIP capped,” which ignores assessment congestion and quality even in the A-like stratum; the 38% “complete digital packet” share is inferred as `1−0.62` (and happens to match `3400−2108`) but is then used as if it were a measured eligibility flag.

**Quantitative scenarios.** The pre-table reasoning is better than the table. Identity (~3.4 h) is dominated by originals delay (~18 h) on the 62% path, and B already allows `Register` to open both Identity and Originals (DFG: 2,050 and 1,860). Cell O is therefore close to current policy, so it cannot contribute much cycle-time change. Optimistic overall −2 to −5 h nonetheless credits “~62% partial,” which is inconsistent with the digital-only row (−1 to −3 h population) and with the critical-path arithmetic. Digital-cell −4 to −8 h also overruns a pure service-overlap ceiling of ~3.4 h unless a large unidentified queue effect is smuggled in. The bottleneck-shift and legal/rework rows correctly give a better median and a worse p90; ranges are scenario maps, as required, but they are not tightly derived.

**Intervention vs model.** Prose says Cell O is Identity ∥ Originals only. The POWL makes Identity concurrent with the whole document-side XOR, so Identity may overlap Document Check after originals arrive. That is legally finer than the prose and slightly different from the eligibility rules. `Register ≺ Document Check_init` is listed as an edge that “must hold” and then immediately qualified. Not fatal, but the specification is not unique.

**Pilot.** Design elements are all present and the ≤220-word mechanism/implementation note is clear. The operating picture is not. Productive pools are 4 / 7 / 3 / 2. A cluster or stepped-wedge by team cannot isolate 3 assessors and 2 legal reviewers; SUTVA through shared downstream queues is the central interference problem and is waved at rather than solved. “Detect a 4 h median wait shift” with ~300 D0 cases is asserted without a scale or variance. D0 ≈ 23% assumes weak independence of digital and legal; if legal cases are concentrated in the originals stratum, D0 is closer to 38% (or the reverse). Refusing T2 on Cell O is legally correct and costs a useful contrast.

**Code.** Constructors and parent/child use are consistent with the requested PM4Py/POWL style; Site A trees match the prompt; Assessment waits on the whole verification PO; comments on what the net cannot encode are accurate. Hierarchical `verification → assessment` satisfies the wait condition but is not two explicit leaf edges. Soundness/playout APIs are used as specified, not demonstrated. Duplicate labels on redo transitions are appropriate.

**Recommendation and assumptions.** `conditional go` is the only defensible call. The 250–400-word rationale is aligned with the rest. The parenthetical “population median likely about 1–3 h” is ambiguous (level vs change; sign omitted) after they otherwise write signed deltas. The eight assumptions follow the required template; 1, 3, 5, and 7 are load-bearing. Assumption 6 (legal/supplement rates invariant to faster verification) is hoped for more than warranted by A’s small rework/appeal upticks.

**Net.** This is a high-quality transport memo with the right refusal, the right first cell, and the right confounders. Strictly, it is not nearly flawless: the causal graph mixes selection and co-interventions, the optimistic numbers fight the critical path, and the pilot understates interference in a two-lawyer pool. Those are not cosmetic.
