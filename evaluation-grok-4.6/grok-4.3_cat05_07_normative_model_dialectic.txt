Neither brief does that work.

## §7 model — fails mandatory semantics

This is the largest single defect. The prompt required a working approximation of standard concurrent checks, manual route, low-risk auto route, emergency/provisional route, repeatable more-info, reject/close, and notify-before-close — and an explicit statement where POWL cannot carry data/authority rules.

What the code actually builds:

`register → (Sanctions ‖ Quality) → LOOP(τ, Request_More_Info) → XOR(Manual→Activate, Auto_Approve, Provisional_Activate, Reject) → Notify → Close`

Failures:

1. **Auto route has no `Activate_Supplier`.** Observed auto traces are Auto_Approve → Activate → Quality. `Auto_Approve` is treated as a terminal decision child.
2. **Quality is compulsory before every decision.** PD-3 and FD-7 exist precisely to allow quality *after* provisional activation. 702/703/704/705/714 cannot replay.
3. **Emergency is a relabeled leaf, not a route.** Observed emergency behavior is `Activate_Supplier` (same label) before quality and manual, then retrospective approval. `Provisional_Activate` will not align to the log, and post-activation quality/manual/monitoring are absent.
4. **Reject still requires `Quality_Review`.** That re-encodes the SUP-709 scope bug the same answer classified as MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT.
5. **`Request_More_Info` is after both checks.** SUP-707 is Sanctions → RMI → RMI → Quality. The loop cannot fire between the concurrent checks. The LOOP(τ, more_info) encoding of zero-or-more is fine; its **placement** is not. The required “clearly explained approximation” is missing.
6. **No limitation note** where the structure is awkward. The prompt forbade hiding that. Auto-without-activate and emergency-without-late-quality are not defensible approximations; they are different processes.

The “policy-overlay table” is a column list, not a table: no rows for CS-9, PD-3 (2026-06-01–07-31, LOW/DE/group, quality ≤2 business days after activate), or FD-7 (2026-07-10–07-12, sanctions-before-activate, EUR 20k, daily monitoring). The data-collection sentence lists the right field names and stops. No snapshot-at-decision rule, no mutability policy for `notification_id`, no actor-alias identity proof, no recantation handling.

The snippet is at least self-contained and uses the requested PM4Py types. That is the only reason this section is not a failing mark on its own.

Under a standard that treats minor gaps as costly and reserves high scores for near-flawless dialectic *and* model semantics, this is a mid/low artifact: right skeleton, unfinished argument, broken revision.
