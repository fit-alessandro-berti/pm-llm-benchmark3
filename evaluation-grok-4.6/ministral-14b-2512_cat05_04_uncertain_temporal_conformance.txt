**1. Time-zone conversion is the central failure.**  
Section 1 says every timestamp is converted to UTC. Section 2’s table is titled “UTC Event-Time Intervals,” yet OPS-BER, VENDOR-NY, LEGACY-BER, and MOBILE-LON times are left in local wall time (or treated as if the offset *were* the zone). **4. DST fold (T-405) is inverted.**  
26 Oct 2025 in Europe/Berlin is a *fold* (03:00 CEST → 02:00 CET); 02:00–02:59 occurs twice, it is not skipped. UTC is local *minus* the offset, not plus. Ingestion order uniquely selects Alert = first 02:50 CEST ≈ 00:50 UTC, Start = second 02:05 CET ≈ 01:05 UTC, Containment = second 02:42 CET ≈ 01:42 UTC. Clock error is ±60 *seconds*, not hour-wide blobs `[00:50, 01:50] ∪ [02:50, 03:50]`. The author’s S1/S2 unions with negative durations and CERTAIN_VIOLATION labels do not survive the fold.
