4.6

The write-up looks complete and hits every required heading, but it is not a trustworthy digital-twin design. Breadth is high; process-mining grounding, process semantics, and experimental honesty are substantially weaker than the polished structure suggests.

**Process semantics and twin scope.** Section 1 never commits to a simulation paradigm or to the state needed for the given tree. The `+` block (planning with optional fire and environmental review) requires AND-split/join tokens and a barrier before decision drafting; that state is absent. Cross-case objects (architect bursts, geographic inspection bundles, neighborhood objections) are named, then left as labels. Applicant/third-party delay is not a first-class entity with its own clock. Council weeks, holidays, reserved environmental capacity, Monday portal clerks, and simple-renovation-only planners appear as prose, not as state. Shared-resource contention is not the same thing as the required inter-case dependence.

**Parameter estimation is internally inconsistent and PM4Py-weak.** Waiting time cannot be both (a) sampled from an empirical/M/G/c model and (b) produced by calendars and queues. That double-counts delay. The completion-only split “service = min(sojourn, p95 of similar activities)” is not a method; it invents a wait/service cut the log cannot support, which violates the instruction not to collapse those times without a real limitation statement. `pm4py.get_case_arrival_average` is an inter-arrival statistic, later treated as a weekly volume. `pm4py.create_resource_calendar()` is not a real top-level API. Architect same-week clustering is reduced to a 31/69 mixture, not a clustered arrival process. Objection is handled by the rule “duration > 30d ⇒ objection occurred,” which treats a missing event as observed.

**Assumptions fight the interventions and the log.** Constant rework across types contradicts the variant table. “Architects do not change submission behavior” contradicts D7. “Case mix is stable” is doubtful under D1. “Neighborhood objections hit every public-notice case in the district” is too strong. Legal routing later restricted to Commercial/Complex contradicts P001 (New residential with a legal check) and the 26% “simple clean path without legal check” figure.

**Experiments and uncertainty.** The matrix lists baseline, D1–D10, and three stresses, which satisfies the outline, but cells are On/Off flags with no operational definition, no CRN/paired design, and no input-uncertainty factor (start-time imputation, objection proxy, area/sensitivity missingness). Welch is named, then replaced by a flat 12-month warmup. Seeds as `replication_id` and one-at-a-time ±10–20% sweeps do not separate aleatory noise from epistemic uncertainty. Heavy-tail discussion is limited to “bootstrap the p90.”

**Validation is partly the wrong theory.** Token-replay fitness/precision/generalization validate a *model against a log*, not a *simulator against a log*. Needed are distributional comparisons (cycle time, variant mix, queue trajectories), calendar-conditioned checks, and tests of bundle/burst structure. A final-3-month holdout on an 18-month seasonal series is a weak out-of-sample plan; KS tests on ~31k cases will reject almost any residual error.

**Decision criteria are arbitrary and incomplete.** Adopt-if-median −15% and p90 does not rise, plus utilization <90% and district spread <10%, is ungrounded and likely rejects every useful policy (D3/D5 travel already breaks the fairness rule). D2 has no compliance/quality risk metric. There is no Pareto or costed comparison for a temporary inspector. No optimality claim is made, which is one of the few constraint hits.

**Python skeleton (largest hard failures).**
- The prompt asked to use `pm4py.parse_process_tree` or explain a *real* limitation of the *simplified string*. The answer asserts PM4Py cannot parse this format and returns `None`. That is false; the string is essentially PM4Py’s tree syntax.
- Required DFG is never computed. Service/waiting *columns* are never added; pandas sojourn helpers are aimed at an `EventLog`.
- Imports such as `from pm4py.discovery import discover_variants` are not faithful public PM4Py usage.
- `ArrivalGenerator` emits deterministic `int(daily_demand)` counts; day-of-week factors are not normalized; arrival “rate” units are wrong; architect bursts are not generated as clustered submissions.
- `RoutingEngine` serializes the AND-block, sends planning rework to completeness, and mishandles legal/public-notice. Decision drafting does not wait on open reviews.
- Only clerks and planners are instantiated. Public-notice is not a timed calendar activity. Interventions are a unused constructor list, so D1 equals baseline. Cases are never marked completed, so reported cycle times would be empty. Utilization is increment/minutes-since-midnight. Applicant remaining time is redrawn every check.
- Closing notes claim the engine *includes* architect bundles, district bundles, and neighborhood effects. The code does not. That is a factual error, not a stub.

**Net.** The document is a competent outline of topics a twin should touch, not a nearly correct design. False PM4Py claims, a non-operational wait/service story, objections treated as data, AND-join and inter-case dependence left unimplemented, and a skeleton that would not exercise D1–D10 or the stated calendars keep this well below a high score.
