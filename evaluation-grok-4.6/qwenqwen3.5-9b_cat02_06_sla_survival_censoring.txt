**3.7**

The write-up has the required nine headings and some of the right vocabulary (competing risks vs right-censoring, informative transfer, immortal time on `Escalate`, Aalen–Johansen as a CIF estimator). It is not a competent survival or process-mining analysis of *this* extract. Multiple required distinctions are wrong, the case table is miscounted, and the code would not implement the estimands it claims.

**Estimands and case contributions (major).** Estimand A is defined as \(S(72)=P(T_{\text{terminal}}>72)\), then S03 (resolved at 76) is called a “Failure (Survival < 1)”. Under that definition S03 is still non-terminal at 72 and must count as surviving the horizon, not as a failure. Estimand B mixes “non-resolution exits,” “unresolved at 72,” and “breach” into one ill-posed \(F(72)\). The four required worked examples are therefore internally inconsistent: S09 (open at 92) is *observed* still open at 72, not “censored at 72”; S03 is an already-observed late resolution / still-open-at-72 case, not a vague KM failure. Already-observed breaches (resolve or still open after 72) are never cleanly separated from right-censoring.

**Censoring encoding (major).** Every `open` row is coded “right-censored at 72 (assumed open).” That is incorrect for ages below 72 (S10 47h, S15 35h, S24 67h, S29 51h, S31 23h): the cutoff age is the censoring time; one cannot assume they remain open until 72. Cases already older than 72 and still open (S09, S11, S25, S26) are *not* censored at 72 for a 72-hour functional—they are observed past the horizon. S32 (transferred at 96) is past the SLA *before* transfer; the table does not say so. The encoding table also collapses “breach” and “success” into one scheme, and it never shows three transfer encodings per row. The three prose assumptions for transfer are only a sketch; treating transfer as immediate success is listed and then unused.

**Misuse of the supplied table (major).** “Cohort A has 3 transfers (0%)” is self-contradictory and false (A has S08 only; B has S21 and S32). Rework is 2 in A (S07, S13) and 1 in B (S27), not “4 vs 1.” The P1 list in §7 includes S01 (P2). These are not rounding issues; they are evidence-handling failures on a 32-row extract.

**Naive-analysis section is incomplete.** Survivor bias is gestured at, but the prompt-required contrast—open cases *below* vs *above* 72h—is missing or inverted. Informative censoring from long `last_event_gap_h` (S11 55h, S25 48h, S26 72h) is not analyzed.

**SAP.** Landmarking and cause-specific hazards are essentially skipped; multi-state is dismissed in one sentence. Recommending “KM with competing-risk labeling” is confused. The secondary plan (KM on open vs resolved only) *reintroduces* the completed-case bias §3 warned about. AJ as primary is defensible; the justification is generic and not tied to the actual event counts.

**Process features.** Treating `has_autocheck` as a baseline covariate is exactly the post-start / leakage pattern the task forbids; AutoCheck is an intermediate activity (and in B often *before* Screen). The state diagram omits observed edges: `Submit→AutoCheck`, `Screen/AutoCheck→Escalate`, `Rework→Resolve` (S27).

**Sensitivity.** Withdrawal/rejection treatment is not actually varied. No analysis is computed, so “which qualitative conclusions could reverse” is an empty hypothetical. Transfer-as-censor vs competing is described, not shown.

**Code (fails the spec).** The priority vector is wrong from S18 onward (S18 P2 vs P1, S25 P3 vs P1, S26 P1 vs P3, S27 P3 vs P2, S28 P1 vs P2, S29 P2 vs P1, S30 P1 vs P3), so the “stratified” results are on corrupted data. Only one event indicator (resolved vs not) is built; the second transfer flag is never used, so there are not two estimands or three transfer scenarios. Open cases are not turned into a proper 72h risk-set (no `min(T,72)`, no separate coding for already-breached vs still-at-risk). Step indices 0,1,2,… are fabricated timestamps, which the task forbids. `KaplanMeierFitter.survival_function_72` does not exist. \(S(72)\) is printed as “P(Resolved by 72h)”: that is \(P(T>72)\) for time-to-resolution, i.e. the *complement* of what the label says (and 1−KM is the wrong CIF under competing events). The fallback “resolved-by-72 / n” is the naive proportion §3 rejects. `get_variants` is treated as a list. The block is not a working demonstration.

**What is acceptable.** Time origin at `Submit`; transfer not blindly labeled non-informative; immortal-time note on escalation; limitations list matches the requested missing data; format is followed.

Those positives do not offset inverted survival interpretation, illegal censoring times, a corrupted case table, and non-compliant code. Under a strict bar this is a structured but technically unreliable answer.
