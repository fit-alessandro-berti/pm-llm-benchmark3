8.6

The audit is conceptually strong: it catches both obvious leakage (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`) and subtler lineage defects (extraction-date SQL, nightly snapshot, unhistoricalized `final_handler_team`, mutable `risk_score_current`, employee-experience join, forward ±5 min snapshot tolerance). It correctly keeps `queue_length_at_t0`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, and `initial_damage_complexity`, and it treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as process-legitimate but post-`t0`, unlike the footprint-impossible same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` pattern. Predictive vs causal vs actionable vs fairness standards are separated, and the Case X/Y/Z JSON mostly stays inside the `t0` prefix.

Deductions are for real, if local, flaws:

- **V5 rule is wrong as written.** “Explanations may use enabled-now activities as observed features” conflates enablement with observability. At `t0` the last observed event is `Coverage Check Completed`; `Documents Requested` / `Fraud Investigation Opened` may be *enabled next*, but they have not occurred and cannot be observed inputs. Section 1 handles this correctly; Section 2’s rule does not.
- **Severity is inconsistent.** `num_document_requests_total` (complete-trace, importance 0.22) is only High while the weaker `full_trace_variant_id` is Critical; `last_resource_role` is High while the same closure-time defect on `final_handler_team` is Critical. Parentheticals also violate the specified `critical/high/medium/low` column.
- **Case Y double-counts `prior_claim_count`** as a credible “increase” driver and as a repaired/unknown value. **`initial_reserve` is listed as credible** despite unverified freeze-time lineage.
- **Category stretch:** `total_case_duration_hours` is not “unusable until lineage repaired”; it is unusable at this prediction point by definition. `calendar_week` in Case Z is a confound, not a data ambiguity.
- **Clock-start for the 10-day target is assumed**, not evidenced, when calling elapsed time “arithmetic of the target.”

None of these undo the audit, but they are enough to keep the score well below near-flawless.
