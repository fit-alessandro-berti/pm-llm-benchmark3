**Comparison is only qualitative.** First-available and specialist-only are described in general terms. The question asked for a comparison on seven dimensions; there is no worked assignment set for either baseline, so claims about throughput, SLA risk, and fairness are not checkable against this queue.

**PM4Py sketch is incomplete and partly incorrect.** Required pieces that are missing or unused: `discover_working_together_network`, `discover_activity_based_resource_similarity`, `discover_batches`, `get_service_time`, `get_variants`. This audits a few signals; it does not learn a policy.

Those strengths do not offset factual mistakes, self-contradictory assignments, and an algorithm that cannot implement the rules it announces. Under a near-flawless standard, this is a mid-range answer.
