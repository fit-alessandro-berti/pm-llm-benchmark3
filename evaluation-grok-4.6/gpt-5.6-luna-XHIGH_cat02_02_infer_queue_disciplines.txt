**6.4**

The write-up looks like a competent process-mining memo and follows the required skeleton, but it fails the task’s actual test: reconstructing eligibility sets at decision epochs. Several of those sets are wrong, and the wrong sets are then used to justify the headline diagnoses.

## Material errors in decision sets

**QA at 08:20 omits A04.** A04 arrived at 08:13, so it is waiting when A03 starts. The table lists only A02 and A03. That single omission collapses the central identification argument. With A04 present:

- SPT/SEPT predicts **A04** (7 min), not A03 (10)
- priority predicts **A04**
- FIFO predicts A02
- appointment predicts A03

The observed choice is therefore *not* observationally equivalent to SPT. The answer repeats the false equivalence in §1 (“A03 is also the shorter job”), §3 (“its 10-minute estimate is half A02’s”), and the “strongest contradicting episode.” The later FIFO run after 08:32 *does* kill pure SPT; the 08:20 instant already would have, had A04 been in the set. QA confidence of 78 and the stated identifiability problem are artifacts of this miss.

**QB at 10:14 omits B06 and B07.** Both have been waiting since 10:05 and 10:08. The table gives only unfinished B01 and B04, then claims a priority tie broken by FIFO. That is false: B06 is priority 2. Strict priority predicts B06, not a B01/B04 resume. SPT predicts B07 (6) or B04 (8), not B01’s remaining ~14. The omitted jobs are exactly the ones that discriminate “preemptive priority” from “P1-only / resume-then-FIFO.”

**QD idle epochs are not treated as decisions.** D-X becomes available at 11:20 and sits idle until D06’s 11:30 appointment, while D03 (electrical, older, shorter) is waiting and D-F is still on D02 until 11:21. FIFO, priority, and SPT all start work at 11:20; only an appointment hold explains the idle. The answer instead evaluates 11:30, notes that D06 is then the oldest X-compatible job, and concludes the appointment is not identified. The identifying observation is the unused 11:20–11:30 window.

At 11:36 D-F frees with D10 waiting (electrical, priority 1, 12 min, finishes before the 12:00 meeting) and does not take it. Shared-queue skill eligibility does not explain that. The answer never builds this set. “Skill-based routing, FIFO among compatible jobs” is therefore under-tested, and 86 is too high.

## Diagnosis problems that follow from those errors

**QB is over-labeled.** After B02’s explicit P1 preemption, the trace is mostly resume-of-incumbent and then FIFO: B01 over B03 at 09:31; B04 (P3) over B06 (P2) at 10:28; B07’s appointment ignored. That is closer to **preempt only for urgent/P1, otherwise resume/FIFO** than to “preemptive priority, with exceptions.” B04≻B06 is treated as a stray exception rather than as evidence that P2 is not a dispatch key. B05 at 09:48 sits exactly on the stated two-minute scan bound (arrived 09:46); that is mentioned as a generic possibility and not applied to the strongest alleged priority violation.

**QA’s strongest support and strongest contradiction are the same episode**, and both readings depend on the incomplete 08:20 set. There is no clean contradicting episode for FIFO+soft appointment once A04 is restored; A06’s five-minute lateness is explained by non-preemption of A05.

**QD’s “strongest contradicting episode”** (D06 beating D10’s priority) does not contradict skill-based *routing*. It contradicts a priority tie-break inside a feasible set. The real threats to the QD story — D-X held idle, D-F ignoring D10 — are absent.

## Smaller factual and logical nits

- §4 Test 1 lists A07 and A08 among SPT alternatives after A03 ends at 08:30. A07 arrives 08:35; A08 arrives 08:40.
- Remaining-time arithmetic for B01 is handled carefully and labeled; that part is fine. The 09:48 SPT line is still conditional on an unused remaining estimate, which the answer admits, then still leans on it.

## What is actually strong

Format compliance is exact. Competing rules are scored against sets, not against raw start order. QC is correctly left as a low-confidence hybrid; C03≻C05 at 10:18 is a real anti-priority observation; C01≻C07 at 11:40 is a real anti-EDD/anti-SPT observation. §5’s ranked exceptions and §6’s event-sourced collection plan match the genuine ambiguities (decision time ≠ start time, eligibility snapshot, appointment semantics, policy version, remaining estimate). §7’s ranking — QD most identifiable, QC least, strict preemptive priority the most dangerous over-claim — is directionally right even though the supporting reconstructions are incomplete.

Those strengths do not offset wrong candidate sets at the epochs the prompt asked to reconstruct. The method is announced (“eligibility sets are necessarily ranges”) and then not applied at the three most informative times. Under a strict reading of this rubric, that is a large, not a cosmetic, failure.
