**7.4**

The final write-up is a strong, constraint-aware design: it does not mass-merge on activity+timestamp, does not drop e05/e14, does not treat ingestion as business order, keeps original timestamps, and does not claim that a total order is physical truth. The decision tree, pair table, dual pipelines, `have_common_object` argument, and required failure modes are mostly right. It is not nearly flawless.

**What holds up**

The tree cleanly separates exact technical clones, verified multi-system observations, detail/summary pairs, harmonization collisions, concurrent same-label events with different objects, and quarantine. Pair decisions match the source facts: e03/e04 and e11/e16 merge with union+prefix; e03/e05 and e13/e14 stay distinct; e08/e09 are relabeled rather than collapsed; e06/e07 and e13/e15/e14/e15 stay at two abstraction levels in the conservative path. Event/E2O counts (17/44 → 15/39 → 14/37 → 10/28) are internally consistent. Ordering policy is the right shape: domain/lifecycle/source semantics first, `source_seq` only within source, `eid` as a non-semantic fallback, microseconds only after semantics. The six-plus edge list in §7 is concrete and useful.

**Where it loses points**

1. **Implementation does not implement the policy.** §3 describes a rule engine; `assign_order_key` is a hardcoded `tie_rank` map for this toy log. That is demonstration, not a pipeline. After merging e04 into e03, e03 < e05 is justified by *scanner* `source_seq` on a row that no longer exists—this is never computed, only baked in.

2. **Provenance claims overreach the helper.** `payload_hash`, `source_seq`, and `ingested_at` of the absorbed event sit in `structural` and are skipped. The text promises both payload hashes and full source traces; the merge function keeps lists only for `source` / `source_event_key`. That is a direct design/code contradiction.

3. **Sensitivity misses a required axis.** The prompt asked for ET–OT weight changes on all four datasets. §7 never names ET–OT. Item-flattened DFG is only implied (I2 pick “disappears”), not analyzed as a flattening.

4. **PM4Py usage is specified, not robust.** Required calls are present and the drop-after-relabel justification is sound, but `duplicates_type="symbolic"` is undefined, there is no fallback, timestamps are never parsed before `astype("int64")`, and the evidence scenario is cloned *after* synthetic timedeltas. Hard-wiring keep-ids `e03`/`e11` also never justifies source authority.

5. **Small overclaims.** Invoice-before-payment is treated as a hard domain rule though all 12:00 events share one second; PSP `source_seq` is first called semantic, then correctly walked back for e13/e14. Default conservative relabeling of every Pick/Capture with object qualifiers is more than “minimum-change” and will split order-level DFGs—the risk appears only late in §9.

Net: conceptually well above average, operationally and spec-completely short of a high score under a strict rubric.
