## Material factual and logical errors

**August is not a population shift.** Partner Z is 0% through August and starts 1 September. Section 2’s heading “August–September: Compounding Concept Drift and Population Shift” is therefore wrong on the timeline. August is supplier/process-performance plus V3 mix (22% → 35%) and a V3 matured-breach jump (0.46 → 0.64). Population shift is September–October only. This is not a wording quibble; it is a mis-attribution of a dated intervention.

**The model scores immediately after diagnosis, before the variant exists.** V2 vs V3 is realized *after* `Initial Diagnosis Completed` (Remote Fix / Order Part / Dispatch). Variant-specific production models, “dynamic V3 thresholds,” and an immediate override “when part delivery delay exceeds 96 hours” are not available at the stated decision point unless you first predict the path or inject an *external* supplier-state feature. The answer never confronts this. For a process-monitoring audit that is a central conceptual failure, not a refinement.

**October is listed as concept drift with High confidence.** You cannot sustain that claim on a 55% mature cohort whose outcome API systematically drops long paths. Process evidence (155 h parts edge, 41% V3, 27% Partner Z) can still be observed; the *outcome relationship* cannot be trusted. Putting October in the concept-drift row as if it were like August is exactly the maturity mistake the prompt warns against.

**April is not an unrecoverable training month.** The May patch backfilled most April records; the residual issue is stale derived features and post-20-April-only dedup. Calling April unsafe “without deep synthetic imputation” overstates the damage and contradicts the answer’s own replay test, which expects AUROC to return to ~0.80 after normalization.

**Several table claims are simply false or sloppy.** V1 is not “relatively stable” (45% → 23%). All six rows are “High” confidence, which erases the difference between a dated migration log and an immature October label. April missingness is filed as covariate drift when it is pipeline-induced *apparent* shift in X; true P(X) change is later (mix, Partner Z, unmeasured rural/device-age). July at 98% maturity does not belong in the same label-delay bin as October. May is described as if stale features continued after the patch; the audit says April caches were not fully recomputed, not that May onward stayed broken.
