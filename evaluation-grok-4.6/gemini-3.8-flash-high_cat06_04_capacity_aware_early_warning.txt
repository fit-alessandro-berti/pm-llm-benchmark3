**6.2**

The answer is structurally complete and hits the main quality bars (I-03 ineligible, I-05 ≠ I-07, spillovers, calibration, feedback loops). It is not close to flawless. Decision-theoretic coherence and operational realism—the stated grading axes—are repeatedly broken by internal contradictions, unauditable arithmetic, invented parameters treated as binding, and a slot assignment that its own rule cannot justify.

**Decision rule vs. what is actually used.** Section 1 defines ranking by NPEV with linear value and a uniform spillover charge. The 12:00 allocation does not follow that rule. Midpoint direct NPEV is about €8,950 for I-04 and about €4,550 for I-08, versus €2,700 for I-02, €3,550 for I-09, and €800 for I-07 (€−1,000 after the same spillover charge). I-07 is nevertheless Tier 1 and I-04/I-08 are waited. Option value of delay for long-runway triage is a defensible extra criterion, but it is not in the NPEV formula. The “> 8.0 h defer” clause is not the rule either: I-01 (7.0 h, triage) is waited, and I-08 sits exactly on the cutoff. The SME capacity floor, concave \(U(V)=V^{0.5}\), and Enterprise \(p_{\text{adj}}\ge 0.65\) screen appear only in §5; none are in §1, and the 0.65 screen would **reject I-02** (\(p_{\text{adj}}\approx 0.54\)–\(0.61\)), the case given Slot 1. NNLS is minimax in §1 (widen effects 25%), UCB/optimistic in §5, and midpoint-unadjusted in the actual table. That is three incompatible rules.

**I-09 factual error.** “Highest net direct benefit in the portfolio” is false. I-04’s midpoint direct NPEV is more than double I-09’s.

**I-07 is a weak use of a scarce slot.** The answer’s own feasibility gate says a 45-minute swarm cannot avert breach if downstream diagnosis-complete, resolution, and validation need more time than remains. I-07 is still in Triage with 1.5 h left; after a 45-minute swarm, ~45 minutes remain for the rest of the path. Net NPEV after spillover is negative. In the adverse scenario the authors themselves set I-07’s effect to 0.02 because it “fails SLA.” Spending Slot 3 on a case that is both low-value and likely unsalvageable is not rescued by calling it a “fairness override” that was never written into the deployable rule.

**Sequencing is ignored.** Two seniors deliver the swarm, so slots are sequential, not concurrent. Three 45-minute swarms occupy 12:00–14:15. Ordering I-02, then I-09, then I-07 puts the 13:30-deadline case last—i.e., the swarm starts when the SLA expires. Even reversing the order leaves I-09/I-02 with little post-swarm process time. A deployable policy had to state order, parallelism, and who is pulled off the diagnosis queue.

**Reserve logic is fabricated, then treated as strict.** Management’s reserve is a preference. The answer invents \(P(\text{arrival})=0.40\) and \(\mathbb{E}[V]=€35{,}000\), gets a €14,000 hurdle, and “strictly” holds a slot because no case clears it. Optimistic I-04 is ~€14,350 and would. Release-to-I-06 at 14:00 conflicts with hold-until-14:30; a 45-minute swarm cannot be started at 14:30 for an incident that may arrive until 15:00. \(\overline{V}_{\text{diag}}=€15{,}000\) is also invented and then drives every spillover euro figure.

**Section 3 does not audit.** Optimistic spillover is labeled “3 slots × 6 × 0.010–0.015 × €15k” but reported as €1,620–€2,700; that product is €2,700–€4,050. Base is labeled 0.015–0.025 and reported as €3,600–€7,200; the product is €4,050–€6,750. Adverse ranges pair max benefit with max spillover, unlike optimistic. I-09’s promised 25% effect widening is never applied. Base “High” confidence is unjustified given imputed \(V\), non-causal effect ranges, and linear stacking of three slots’ spillovers (which likely overstates harm if swarms are sequential on the same two people). False-alarm burden is charged in full as if certain, which is conservative but then double-counted rhetorically as both “intervention cost” and customer harm.

**Calibration is only half-used.** Segment offsets on \(p\) are reasonable, as is \(\gamma_{\text{DQ}}=0.50\) on I-06. They barely affect allocation because value uses supplied \(\Delta p\), not \(p_{\text{adj}}\). The §4 state machine then reintroduces an undefined “Alert Threshold” on \(p_{\text{adj}}\), contradicting the claim that probability thresholds are invalid. Effects are still treated as known causal bounds (midpoint = \(\mathbb{E}[\Delta p]\)), against the task instruction.

**What works, and why it does not save the score.** Stage-gating I-03 is correct and well argued. I-05 is suppressed as a clock-only refresh; I-07 re-enters on a new log—this distinction is required and is done well. I-06 is not dispatched on an imputed spike. Spillovers, performative label bias, IPTW/logging, SME starvation, and NNLS exclusion are all named with concrete (if inconsistent) safeguards. Monitoring dimensions match the prompt. Those are the bones of a strong answer; the flesh is over-formalized, internally inconsistent, and operationally incomplete. Under a strict reading of coherence, realism, and uncertainty, that caps the grade well below the top band.
