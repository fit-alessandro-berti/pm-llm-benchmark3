**7.4**

The later six-section draft is the only part that counts. It follows the required headings, respects every hard constraint, uses the scenario numbers accurately, and resists a single-cause story. That is a strong answer, not a near-flawless one.

**What it gets right.** Section 1 separates the seven mechanisms with signatures, supporting evidence, limits, and tests. Early complexity, late queue pressure, and the U-shaped error pattern are used correctly. Section 2’s three-regime account is the right causal story: selected hard work at the start, a mid-shift sweet spot, then a speed–quality tradeoff under high WIP, with fatigue and clock time left unidentified. Interventions 6 and 8 are handled correctly: no QC removal for throughput, no raw late-shift punishment. The dashboard covers the required indicators and blocks worker leaderboards.

**Material flaws.** The flagship logit still treats `error_flag` as a property of the same event that carries service time, position, and WIP. Given delayed Quality Check, that model will rediscover the end-of-shift detection burst the narrative warns about. Origin mapping is discussed later, not built into the primary specification. The OLS/logit kitchen sink (`position × WIP` + hour + date + shift + resource FE) is weakly identified: within a shift, position and clock hour move together. Overlapping shifts are mentioned, not shown to identify the contrast. `filter_paths_performance(Quality Check → Rework Correction)` assumes a directly-follows path that the process likely does not have. The main service model does not drop `Bot_Risk`, so zero-duration bot events can distort estimates. `COMPLEXITY_CUT = 0.65` and the WIP=70 cut are invented. `cluster_log(..., KMeans(...))` is not a reliable PM4Py call.

**Smaller issues.** Sequence-bin tables have no denominators, and that gap is underplayed. `resource_experience_months` is nearly collinear with resource FE. A few API details and one dashboard typo remain. None of these are fatal, but they are enough that this is a very good analysis with several real methodological holes, not a 9–10.
