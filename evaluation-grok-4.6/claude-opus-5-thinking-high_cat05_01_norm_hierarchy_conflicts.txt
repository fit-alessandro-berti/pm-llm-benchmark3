**7.3**

The assessment is unusually well constructed: it states a method, keeps facts distinct from assumptions, refuses a single total ordering, applies controls at act-time, privileges contemporaneous artefacts over ERP stamps, and does not treat emergency status or later sign-off as a cure. Most case results follow from that method. It is not nearly flawless. Several load-bearing moves are internally inconsistent, one classification is poorly grounded in the author’s own rules, and a few generous outcomes hang on a single contestable premise.

**What is strong**

Section 1 is the right kind of method statement. M1–M4 correctly refuse “newest / strongest wording wins,” limit ECD-2 to the issues it claims (timing and onboarding *use*), and keep GPIP-4 competence (A7 / CRO) separate from operational overlay. M7–M9 on hard expiry, late evidence versus late authority, and ON-44 as weight rather than exception are exactly what the file requires. The issue-specific hierarchy in §2 is the operative contribution, not the weight column. The catalogue is mostly well normalized; the pseudo-logic uses `normally` / `unless` / `overrides_for` and does not smuggle data-conditional rules into an unconditional declare, except where SoD is correctly marked `always`.

Time and scope are handled with real discipline. P-501 is kept outside ECD-2 because 08:50 precedes 09:00 and ON-44’s lag runs against the buyer. P-502 cannot use the CFO email because the email did not yet exist. P-506 is assessed under the ordinary regime after hard expiry. P-505 is dated from the 17:59:31 acknowledgment and the 17:57 call, not from an 18:06 ERP stamp on a possibly fast clock. P-507’s C3 clock is taken from the 12:40 signed PDF, not the 14:30 ERP row. P-503 is correctly treated as over-determined so the C6 scope fight does not decide it. P-504 is correctly treated as out of every emergency definition. Section 5 is better than the minimum: the M5-rejection alternative, the D1-ceiling reading of P-508, and the “C4 sweeps in clearance” theory for P-502 are the actual reversing premises, and the author says which findings survive them.

**Material defects**

1. *P-501 is classified against the author’s own catalogue.* R6 states that B2 is silent on order and that R2’s pre-commitment rule starts at EUR 50,000. P-501 is EUR 38,000. A2 and A3 are not triggered; B4’s genuine four-hour stoppage excuses commit-before-PO; B6 is timely; SoD holds. The remaining theory — that SHIFT-1 “exercised de facto plant authority” — conflates *who may commit* with B4’s narrower rule about who may *substitute for an absent Plant Manager on Plant_Approve*. Plant_Approve later occurred. On the author’s stated rules this is at most a delegation-hygiene or documentation issue, not a clean NONCOMPLIANT. Section 5 admits this is the closest call; the primary row does not give that admission enough effect.

2. *M10 is announced strictly and then not applied.* Section 1 reserves INDETERMINATE for balanced primary facts and sends unproven exceptions to NONCOMPLIANT. No case is allowed to be INDETERMINATE. P-505 and P-507 are then classed JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION while independence of the IC authorizers — an express R9 condition the author says would flip the result — is unknown, and P-505 also lacks a C3 write-up and any Create_PO record. Section 6.1(d) quietly invents a softer rule (“open fact would only confirm an otherwise coherent justification”) that §1 never authorized. That is a method breach, not a nuance.

3. *Three JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION results are a single-point interpretation.* P-505, P-507, and P-508 all require M5 (an uncontrolled CFO email can functionally replace A2). The hierarchy table simultaneously says ECD-2 cannot override A2. Substitution is then smuggled in through R9, which fuses C3 (a real ECD-2 duty) with D1/D2 (a competence-limited, uncontrolled interpretation). That fusion hides the contested step inside a “rule.” P-508 is the weakest of the three: D1’s sufficiency statement is capped at EUR 150,000; D2 adds Legal “as well” and does not clearly revive sufficiency above the cap. Choosing the buyer-friendly reading for the largest off-register commitment, while being harsh on P-501, is an uneven strictness gradient. Severity “Low” / “Documentation-only” on 505/507 is too light once independence is unproven and Create_PO is missing entirely (C1 reorders PO and commitment; it does not waive PO).

4. *New supplier is silently equated with off-register supplier.* A3 is triggered by a *new* supplier or by amount. B3 is the local register. R3 writes “new / off-register” as if they were one trigger. That leap is load-bearing for P-504’s A3 theory at EUR 24,000. P-504 is still noncompliant on B3, B1, and the absence of any authorization, so the case result survives; the “crucial” A3 point does not. The same conflation is harmless for P-507/P-508 because the amount limb of A3 already applies.

5. *Smaller but real slips.* B2 is described as “the local implementation of A2’s budget-owner limb,” which is an assumption, not a file fact, and it is then used to downgrade missing Plant_Approve to documentation whenever R9 is in play — in tension with A5’s permission for stricter local controls. A1’s text is about *the* final business approver; P-503’s joint PROC-3/IC-1 authorization is treated as an automatic SoD collapse without working through the singular wording (the purpose argument is still credible). P-504’s emergency-form timing is not checked against B6. “No case is INDETERMINATE” plus “nothing would rescue P-503” overstates evidential closure relative to the instruction to keep uncertainty visible in the classification itself. Section 7 is a capable overlay clause with a real anti-normalization safeguard, but it is not concise.

**Net**

The piece would be in the high 8s or 9s if P-501 tracked R6, if M10 were either relaxed in §1 or enforced in §4, if R9 did not bury M5, and if P-508 were NONCOMPLIANT or INDETERMINATE on the literal D1 ceiling. Those are not cosmetic nits; they are the difference between a disciplined opinion and a method that sometimes follows its own burden-of-proof and trigger rules. The remainder — especially act-time evaluation, ON-44, A6, C6 non-decisiveness for P-503, and the reversing-premise work in §5 — is well above ordinary quality and is why the score is not lower.
