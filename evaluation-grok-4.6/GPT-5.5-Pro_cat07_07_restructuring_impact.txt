**Section 7 is the largest technical deduction.** The sketch looks production-like but has real process-mining errors:

- Period is assigned **per event**. Splitting DFGs, variants, and org networks on that cut fragments in-flight traces and invents spurious starts/ends. Case-level metrics then label a case by its first event while using full-span cycle time. In-flight / cohort handling is never discussed.
- Several PM4Py calls are shaky (`insert_case_service_waiting_time` and `get_service_time` are used as if they take an EventLog; `filter_event_attribute_values` / `filter_variants` are listed in the prompt and barely used).

- Handover summaries are mostly activity-to-activity, while the management tables are organizational/resource loops.
- `future_rework` via `groupby.transform` plus `iloc`/`shift` is fragile; discovered roles and resource similarity are computed but not compared.
- Calendar waits ignore working-time / weekends.

**Net.** This is expert-adjacent consulting analysis with the right recommendation and unusually broad PM coverage. It is not a 9+: the “map” is a table, scenarios underuse the numbers, learning is uneven, the pilot is only half-identified, and the PM4Py section has a first-order pre/post design flaw. Those are more than nits.
