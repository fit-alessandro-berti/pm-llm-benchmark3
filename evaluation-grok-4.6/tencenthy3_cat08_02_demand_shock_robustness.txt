**6.7**

The final write-up hits every required section and does the core job: scenarios vary by mix and disruption, not just volume; bottleneck migration is traced with DFG frequencies, queue/utilization figures, and variant behavior; the standing package is process-control rather than “add staff everywhere”; Urgent, Routine, and Complex-panel are not treated as simultaneously first-priority; and the dashboard separates leading queues from lagging SLAs/rework.

It is not close to flawless.

**Quantitative and presentation defects.** Normal-to-peak ranges are systematically smashed together (`18%29%`, `0.72.8h`, `1.07.5h`, `60%88%`, `1224`). In an operations note those figures are the evidence; as written they are unreadable without the source table. The daily recollection warning of **>100/day** is also wrong against the log: peak recollection is about 780 / 21 days ≈ **37/day**. That threshold would fire only after a collapse, so it is not a leading indicator.

**Conceptual slips.** I6 is sold as reducing the **recollection-loop rate** (cited as ~2.7× higher in peak). The log shows more defective/recollectable work in the wave; early triage shortens the 42h/60h loop and keeps junk off prep/assay. It does not, by itself, cut how many tubes fail. That is the wrong mechanism. In S2, I10 is briefly treated as a way to push **Routine** to a partner lab; I10 is Complex-panel overflow with a 12h haul. Section 4 later uses I10 correctly, but the table row still implies Routine delay from outsourcing.

**Weaker reasoning in places.** “Starving Technical validation of balanced case mix” is fuzzy: peak tech-val is already at **101%** utilization; the real I1/I2 risk is WIP aging and a later flood into an already saturated validator pool. Moving freed registration clerks onto accession is asserted without the data’s own warning (error rate after long shifts; accession is a two-person specialty). Courier **CV > 0.8** is invented. p90s (Urgent 33h vs 18h contract, Complex 210h, Repeat 240h) are barely used as design constraints.

**What still works.** S1–S4 match the required disruption types. Migration paths (pre-analytical → accession → tech-val; I5 idling behind 101% tech-val; I8 without I7 flooding pre-analytical; M2 repeats doubling assay/tech-val load) are grounded. Refusing full I4 is the right read of the utilization table. Residual risk includes a redesign-caused failure (I6+I8 saturating the 96% pre-analytical node). The table columns match the spec.

Strong structure and mostly evidence-based process logic; too many broken numbers, one bad threshold, and a few mechanism errors for anything higher under a strict bar.
