**5.5**

The submission is a complete, on-template design with real simulation literacy (NHPP arrivals, architect bursts, calendars, CRN, Morris, tail KPIs). It is not a near-miss expert twin. Under a strict reading it has multiple methodological errors, PM4Py violations, and places where it talks past the stated constraints.

**What holds up.** The boundary, entity list, queues, inspection batch buffer, and architect-burst arrivals are the right *kind* of model: not independent i.i.d. cases. Calendars are first-class. Objections are treated as latent and proxied by public-notice duration rather than invented events. D1–D10 plus three stresses appear. No optimality claim. Log load goes through pandas + `format_dataframe`, not a fake `pm4py.read_csv`. PM4Py is at least *intended* as a mining layer separate from the engine.

**Simulation design (serious).** A 90-day warm-up is too short when Complex-variance p90 is ~260 days and inspection/legal queues are seasonal. Worse, the text hunts for a WIP “steady state.” Under the given 1.7× seasonal demand there is no classical steady state; you need a long transient (on the order of a year), then periodic/season-matched comparisons. Mixing 30 independent replications with “batch-means CIs” is unspecified. Common random numbers are claimed, then broken: `seed+exp_id` assigns a different stream to every policy, which is the opposite of CRN. Heavy-tail handling via “KS vs lognormal, else empirical” is naive: with large *n* KS almost always rejects. Input uncertainty (imputed starts, objection proxy, reconstructed calendars) is mostly collapsed into a Morris screen rather than treated as a first-class uncertainty layer.

**Process-mining / timing (constraint violations).** Waiting is defined as start(*i*) − end(*i*−1) even though many activities have only completion stamps. That quantity is an inter-completion sojourn, not waiting. The skeleton then sets `service_time = waiting * 0.1`, which is exactly the service/wait collapse the prompt forbids; a one-line “placeholder” comment does not explain the limitation or a defensible imputation design (multi-timestamp subset, resource-busy inference, bounds, sensitivity). Decision-drafting synchronization (AND-join over planning / fire / environment / notice / inspection) is barely a state variable. Planning/legal/appeal rework appears in the prose but not in the given tree; the twin never says whether it will *extend* the tree or stay loyal to it. Neighborhood objections that hit several related cases are explicitly aggregated away.

**PM4Py conventions (code would not do what it claims).** The prompt asked for `pm4py.parse_process_tree` or a clear account of why the string is unusable. The string is standard PM4Py tree notation; `parse_process_tree` is the right API. The skeleton uses `process_tree.importer` on a raw string (that importer expects PTML/a path), swallows the exception, and sets `tree = None`. `pm4py.resources.calendar` is not a real estimation object. `pm4py.objects.dfg.factory` is leftover 1.x and unused. `pm4py.statistics.traces.log.case_statistics` is a stale path. Alignments are proposed to validate start/end *timing*; alignments check control-flow, not duration distributions. Mean daily arrivals are computed as a single average after a long lecture on non-stationarity.

**Experiments and decisions (thin where the prompt was specific).** The matrix is a policy on/off list, not a factor–level design. D3 batch window, D6 age threshold, D7 slot capacity, D8 triage rule, and D4 peak definition never get levels. The Notes column is corrupted (“Expect  missing-doc loops”), so stated trade-offs are unreadable. Stresses are applied mainly to baseline/D10, not to the interventions that should be robust. Hold-out of the *last three months* is a poor OOS plan on an 18-month seasonal log. Decision rules omit architect fairness, D2 residual risk / missed-defect quality, D1 applicant effort, and implementation cost. Utilization 60–85% is a blunt band for Tue–Thu fire reviewers and council-dark legal. District gap &lt; 10 days fights the stated geography of D3/D5.

**Assumptions that are too convenient.** Imputed start times are waved through as “bias negligible after calibration.” Eighteen-month trend is assumed flat without a test. Rework loops are independent given case type. “No sick leave” in the base model sits next to a sick-inspector stress with no absenteeism process. Prior-violation data required by D2 is never marked as missing.

Useful draft. Not a trustworthy digital-twin specification, and not faithful enough to the PM4Py/timing constraints to score higher.
