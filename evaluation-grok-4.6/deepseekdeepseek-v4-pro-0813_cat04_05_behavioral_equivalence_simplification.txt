**7.4**

The final write-up is substantively strong: loop/`+`/`X`/`tau` semantics are handled correctly, the A/B language split is real, and T1–T5 are valid complete-trace witnesses (including two-analysis cases and the `A P C` approval-placement split). Headings, verdict labels, P3-vs-proof caution, and the sequential P1 simplification are all in line with the task.

It is not near-flawless. The P2 map bakes in these models’ invariants (`CheckOccurred=True`, `ReworkOccurred=n>1`) instead of defining a generic projection (`#C>0`, `#R>0`) and then proving the images coincide. P1 is formalized as *exactly* one Analyze and one Check per segment, which is stronger than “must have its own check.” Observation policy and compliance predicate are mixed in that definition; §1 also promises a report on whether *all* generated traces are P1-valid, and §3 never states that cleanly for A. The BFS is the right idea, but silent-only marking growth and PM4Py `Marking`/API edge cases are unaddressed, and the code is unevaluated. “footprins” is a sloppy typo. The simplified tree is only one node smaller than A; calling it simpler is fair mainly because `+` is removed, not because of a large structural cut.
