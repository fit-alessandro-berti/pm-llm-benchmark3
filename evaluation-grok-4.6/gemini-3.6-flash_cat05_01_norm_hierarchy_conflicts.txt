Allowed primary labels include JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION and INDETERMINATE. The answer uses neither, anywhere, and never a secondary label. Severity is also loose. P-504 (cafeteria freezer, EUR 24k) is Critical, same band as P-503’s EUR 140k SoD/scope/A3 stack. P-507 is COMPLIANT with severity “Low.” Those labels are doing atmosphere, not work.

## Hierarchy and catalogue problems

§1 never states a **burden of proof**, though the prompt requires it. The working rule is visible in the cases (exceptions must be evidenced), but it is not owned as a method.

R-A1 is declared “None (Unwaivable integrity control).” That contradicts A7, which the same paper ranks as able to override A1–A6. GPIP-4 is said to override everything and to be overridden by nothing. A5 is the obvious counter: local procedures may be **stricter**. B2’s EUR 25,000 line is not a GPIP-4 victory; it is an A5 overlay the global policy is not allowed to weaken.

The formalized catalogue is thin. B2, B3, B5, B6, A4, A5, and A6 are not first-class rules, even though the cases turn on them. Baking D1 into R-A2 as if it were the black-letter norm smuggles an interpretation into the rule table.

Pseudo-logic line 8 is malformed: `requires_legal_clearance` is defined by already having a timely Legal_Clearance. That is satisfaction, not a trigger. Line 9 is not executable even as sketch. Line 5’s `unless ... overrides_for` is not a well-formed defeater. The prompt asked not to force data-conditional rules into unconditional DECLARE form; the spirit is right, the craft is not.

Minor but real: “Emergency Emergency Rationale”; D4 overread as an “explicit prohibition”; §6’s broken ASCII figure; quarantine of “received goods” for a cafeteria freezer repair; disciplinary referral as if that were an audit finding rather than an HR decision.
