**7.3**

The final write-up follows the required seven-section format, reconstructs decision sets rather than start-order folklore, and is factually careful on timestamps, remaining times, skill eligibility, and the calibration/meeting/prior-job constraints. Assumptions are labeled, confidence tracks ambiguity rather than rhetoric, and the cross-queue synthesis (QD most identifiable, QC least, preemptive priority the most dangerous overfit) is defensible. That is a strong process-mining answer. It is not nearly flawless.

**The section-1 “contradicting episode” cells are internally inconsistent with the diagnoses they sit under.** For QB the stated primary is FIFO plus urgent P1 exceptions, yet the “strongest contradicting” cell is 10:28 (B04 before B06/B07) and 09:31 (B01 resumed before B03). Those episodes *support* FIFO-with-retained-age and *refute rejected alternatives* (priority, appointments). The real contradictions to *that* primary are B02’s preemption of B01, B05 jumping older B04 at 10:00, and the 09:48 dispatcher stop of B01. The same slip appears on QD: D-F idling 11:36–12:00 while D05 waits is evidence *for* availability-aware routing (their own A5), not against it. Section 3 later admits both points, so the table is not just terse—it is misaligned with the analysis.

**“FIFO” in QB is three rules sharing a name.** Sometimes the suspended job keeps arrival age (09:31, 10:14), sometimes only never-started jobs compete (09:48 after a manual stop), and P1 jumps are carved out as exceptions. The text flags A4, but still sells a single “FIFO base.” That is the exact kind of observational-equivalence blur the task asked to keep explicit.

**QC is over-labeled as SPT-leaning.** The decision table itself shows SPT winning cleanly only at 10:10, 10:18, and 11:34. 10:32 (C04 over C06) and 11:40 (C01 over shorter, higher-priority, earlier-deadline C07) are hard SPT failures; 10:54 is priority/deadline, not SPT. Confidence 30 is honest; calling the primary discipline “SPT/SEPT-leaning” is not. “No stable identifiable rule / undocumented hybrid” would have matched the evidence. The feasible-deadline screen (skip C05 once 40 minutes cannot meet 10:50) is an obvious competing predictor and never appears as a column, only as later commentary.

**The 09:48 B05 visibility hedge leans on the weaker reading of scan delay.** A recorded arrival of 09:46 means the scan has already occurred unless one assumes the arrival column is physical time and dispatch lags. Using that to keep 09:48 compatible with priority is possible, but it is presented more evenly than the data warrant.

**Small reconstruction gaps.** D-X becoming available at 11:20 and waiting ten minutes for D06’s appointment—rather than starting D07 (would overrun) or D08 (mechanical, P1, short)—is one of the cleanest appointment-protection decisions in the log and is not tabled as a decision opportunity. QA’s 08:32 two-minute gap is handled, but the 11:20 wait is more informative and is underweighted.

No invented observations, no systematic arithmetic errors, and the counterfactual tests (QA 09:05, QC 10:32, QB 10:28) are well chosen. The deductions are for logical alignment and over-labeling, not for missing the main structure of the queues.
