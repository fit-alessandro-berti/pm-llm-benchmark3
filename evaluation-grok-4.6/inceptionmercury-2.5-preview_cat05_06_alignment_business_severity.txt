## Required structure vs. required content

The required deviation taxonomy was process-model mismatch, event-label mismatch, missing evidence, actual operational deviation, data-aware policy failure, and harmless technical noise. ## Case-level errors and overclaims

CL-608 overstates P4. Notification in CRM/secure portal is allowed if linked evidence is retained; +3 hours is inside 24 hours. The live issue is evidence reliability (mutable text field), not demonstrated non-notification. “P4 Compliance Risk” at severity 3 is too strong relative to that overlay.
