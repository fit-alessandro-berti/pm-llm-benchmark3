**Quantitative errors (not cosmetic).** In §3.1 the largest month-to-month jumps include “median +4.5pp MayJun”. **Material gap: Senior Assessment.** The baseline states that Senior Assessment was **historically recorded inconsistently**. January already has ~21% high-complexity cases but only 4 Senior edges per 100 (ratio ~0.19). October has 41% high-complexity and 22 Senior edges per 100 (ratio ~0.54). That is not “more of the same mix.” It is higher routing or more complete recording of Senior Assessment, on top of the seasonal mix shift. The answer treats the October L1 jump as largely population/seasonal, mentions priority only as an untestable waiting-time story, and never decomposes Senior rate into mix vs routing-given-mix vs logging. That is the discrimination the prompt asked for, and it is missing at the exact activity the prompt warned about.
