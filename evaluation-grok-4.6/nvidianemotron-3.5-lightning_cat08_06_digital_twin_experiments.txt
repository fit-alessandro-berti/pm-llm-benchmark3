5.1

The eight-section shell is complete and the high-level twin is not a naive independent-case model: calendars, non-stationary arrivals, rework, and architect/district coupling are all in scope, objections are treated as unobserved, and service vs waiting is not silently collapsed. That is the ceiling. Under a strict reading the answer is far from nearly flawless.

**Process-mining / PM4Py (largest failure).** The prompt required conventions-faithful PM4Py. The skeleton imports non-existent modules (`pm4py.algo.mining.dfg`, `pm4py.algo.mining.variants`, `pm4py.algo.performance.dfg`, `pm4py.mining.petri_net`) instead of discovery/statistics APIs (`discover_dfg`, `discover_performance_dfg`, `get_variants`, `get_case_arrival_average`). Performance DFG is imported and never computed. “Case arrival average” is implemented as mean case duration, which is the wrong quantity; PM4Py’s own arrival helper is unused. `pm4py.parse_process_tree` is mischaracterized as expecting a Petri net/BPMN; the given tree string is close to PM4Py syntax and should have been attempted with a precise limitation note. Token-based replay is described as if it validated a digital twin against simulated traces. These are not cosmetic naming slips; they violate an explicit constraint.

**Code correctness.** Raw timestamps stay strings on `df`, so `end_time - submit_time` is not a reliable datetime subtraction. The `join(..., on='case:concept:name')` after a groupby that already used that key is a pandas foot-gun. Formatting/mining is duplicated. There is no routing placeholder despite the required split (arrivals / routing / calendars / experiments). Cross-case bundling never appears in the engine interface. `ResourceCalendar` omits council weeks, fire Tue–Thu, and the 40% environmental reservation already stated in the design.

**Simulation methodology.** Complex-variance p90 is 260 days, yet warmup is “5% of six months” or a 3% queue-length rule after a zero-backlog start. That cannot flush a process whose tails exceed the warmup. Thirty replications and a 3-year horizon are asserted, not justified against tail estimation error. Mixture/Pareto/Gamma parameters and Sobol are named but not tied to a calibration protocol.

**Unlabeled invention.** 40% objection incidence, 30% travel savings, 2–5 day bundle delay, 5% fire-label noise, SLA cutoffs 30/60/90/180, and many “~15% / ~5d” trade-offs are written as if measured. Some are later called assumptions; many are not. Decision thresholds (5% median, 10% p90, 3 pp rework) are arbitrary policy numbers presented as selection science.

**Clarity.** Increase/decrease symbols and Greek parameters were stripped, so Section 4 trade-offs and several estimation formulas are unreadable (“ rework loops”, `_t = _base`). That is a presentation defect in a required table.

**What still works.** Boundary and state variables are usable; data-gap handling is honest; D1–D10 plus three relevant stresses exist; no optimality claim; applicant delay is separated from municipal service. Those keep the score out of the failure band, not near the top.
