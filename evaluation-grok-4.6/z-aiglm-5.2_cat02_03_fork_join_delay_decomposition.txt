**5.6**

The write-up has the right fork-join skeleton and the right qualitative conclusion (B dominates via completion time, not raw service; non-critical speedups can be worthless; F can mask branch gains). It is not close to flawless. Required numbers, internal consistency, and the F02 worked example are wrong in ways that then leak into aggregates and counterfactuals.

**What is solid**
- Additive identity is correct: \(T = U + \max_i D_i + DQ + FS\). Sync waits are treated as explanatory, not as a third copy of the fork–join interval.
- Critical-branch IDs are right (A: F01/F11; C: F04/F08; B: 8/12). Ranking B ≫ A > C is justified by frequency, tail, and “A is a floor if B improves,” not by mean service.
- Assumptions around F simulation are at least labeled. Section 7 is the strongest part: capacity vs policy, preemption, shared resources, n=12, the unexplained F01 8-minute wait.

**Numerical / consistency failures (these are not cosmetic)**
- Required table and the **required F02 walkthrough** split B incorrectly: 08:45−08:25 is 20 min, not 40; 09:25−08:45 is 40, not 20. F09 B is likewise 25+40, not 45+20. F01 `C_service` is 18, not 38 (08:38−08:20).
- Those errors are offsetting on `join_ready`, so the critical path survives — which makes the slips easy to miss and does not make them acceptable.
- Aggregates fight each other: B queue+service is written as 54.2 and as 56.7; listed C services sum to 262 but are said to sum to 242; B “sync wait when non-critical” 5.2 is 52/10 instead of 52/4 = 13.
- Section 4 says F01 CF1 goes `09:00 → 08:38` with \(\Delta=2\) “because C_end=08:38 becomes binding.” That sentence cannot be true: 09:00→08:38 is 22 min, A only shrinks 10 min (to 08:50), and the binder would be B at 08:55 (\(\Delta=5\)).

**Counterfactuals**
- The three required designs are present, and “zero benefit when another branch remains last” is understood.
- CF2 F09 JR cut is 12.5 min, not 15, because the B queue/service split is wrong. CF1 F01 is 5 min, not 2.
- The G/G/2 resimulation is not identified: it does not recover observed `final_start` (including F01). Reporting 105 / 2 / 5 min as *the* flow-time savings, then also saying observed DQ totals 176 min, is an unresolved contradiction. Bounds (flow-time cut \(\in [0,\Delta JR]\) if F availability is held fixed) would have been the competent uncertainty treatment.

**Section 6**
- The script’s `to_min` and raw clock times are correct, so a run would **contradict** §§2–4. That is a reproducibility defect, not a feature.
- Per-case CF savings are computed and then not printed; only aggregates are.
- PM4Py scaffolding and the DFG-vs-sync comment meet the letter of the spec.

**Format**
- Headings match. One code block. Double-counting is explained, if a bit loosely (the clean point is \(D_i+W_i=FJ\) for every \(i\), so summing sync waits replicates the same interval).

Conceptually this is a mid/high answer. Under a strict reading — wrong required F02 arithmetic, a corrupted case table, inconsistent summaries, CF figures that do not follow from the stated clock times, and a simulation that is over-interpreted — it cannot sit in the 8–10 band.
