**6.5**

The final write-up is complete, well structured, and mostly aligned with the required headings and tables. It correctly refuses a unique transport effect, refuses illegal Document Check start-before-receipt, treats Site A’s 9-hour median drop as confounded association, and proposes a segmented intervention plus a learning pilot. Those are the right analogical moves. It is not close to flawless.

**What holds up.** Sections 1–4 and 6–7, 9–10 show real process-structural reasoning. Concurrency, originals prerequisite, role specialization, case lock, mix shift, timestamp artifact, and co-interventions (API, extra FTE, marketing, huddles) are used as transport blockers rather than decoration. The selection-node graph is in the right family. T1/T2 eligibility, statutory partial order, WIP caps, and “mechanism failure vs implementation failure” (overlap high / outcome flat vs overlap low) are the correct design. `conditional go` with digital/no-original first and explicit expand/stop rules is the right recommendation. The eight-assumption register is usable.

**Material defects.**

1. **Quantitative inconsistency (Section 5).** The mapping paragraph says 60–75% of Site B’s 20 h pre-assessment wait can be removed, “bounded by 4–7 h.” Those numbers do not agree (0.6×20 = 12 h). Service-overlap arithmetic is never shown (`min(3.4, 8.1)` ≈ 3.4 h serial-service saving, plus queue). The bottleneck scenario is supposed to improve the average while worsening the tail; the median range is `+1 to -3 h`, which includes average deterioration. Eligible-share 20% silently assumes independence of “no originals” (38%) and “no legal” (60%). 16 weeks is treated as 4 months in the power note (≈860 vs ≈790). For a section whose job is quantitative transport, this is not a rounding issue.

2. **Model fidelity (Section 8).** Activities are encoded as one-child XOR operators. Real PM4Py POWL uses `Transition` / `OperatorPOWL` / `Operator` and `StrictPartialOrder.order.add_edge`, not `Operator(OperatorType.XOR|SEQUENCE|LOOP)` plus guessed `add_node`/`add_order`. The legal/supplement fragment is also the wrong control flow: `*(Document+Assessment, RequestSupplement)` after Legal Review **always** re-executes document and assessment, whereas Site B repeats those steps only after a supplement. The question asked for that loop specifically. Comments on what the net cannot encode are fine; the constructed net is not.

3. **Smaller structural slips.** Section 1 never gives **loops** their own row (Legal → Supplement → Document → Assessment), despite the required feature list. `digital_documents -> originals_required` is not a causal edge. Case-level randomization on shared specialized pools leaves interference only partly controlled. None of these would sink a good answer by themselves; stacked on (1) and (2) they keep the score well below the “nearly flawless” band.

Format compliance is high; analogical and causal content is strong; the two pillars the rubric weights hardest after reasoning—**numbers and the executable process model**—are the weakest parts. That caps a hypercritical grade in the mid-6s.
