- Non-block-structured / non-series-parallel structure is conflated with “non-free-choice.” Those are different properties; the POWL DAG here need not yield a non-free-choice net.

## Bottom line

This is a competent audit *outline* with the right architecture (POWL canonical, Petri for checking, BPMN for people, tree disallowed, unidirectional compile). It is not a reliable audit.
