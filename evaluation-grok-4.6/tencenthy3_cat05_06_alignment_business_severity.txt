CL-610 is P1 **and** P2 (same-day bank change). Rating both 4, and ranking 609 above CL-608 (EUR 250k HIGH, weak notification proof), flattens the overlay the framework claims to use.

Recurrence can justify that, but it is not argued tightly against “pay-before-approval is a severe control failure.”
