**5.2**

The JSON is complete, well-structured, and clearly above a DFG-copy baseline: primary states for C6-A/C/E are mostly right, C6-D treats the migration feed as an observation model rather than as negative proof, lot and reportability obligations are recognized, and the A-vs-B discussion is directionally correct. That is not enough. Under a strict process-aware reading the answer has several material errors, not nits.

**The central failure is that ranks 2–3 are usually later obligations, not competing next log events.** The task asks for a top-three *next* distribution and an explicit split between what is process-enabled now and what will be observed next. Marking `Supplier Analysis Completed` or `Technical Investigation Completed` as the next logged activity while Evidence Review has not finished (C6-A, C6-E), or `Disposition Recorded` / `Authority Notification` while Regulatory Assessment is still open (C6-C, C6-D), is sequential storytelling dressed up as a forecast. Midpoints such as ~0.65 / ~0.30 / ~0.20 are therefore not a coherent next-event distribution: if review is the successor, those later completions cannot also be next at those rates. `process_enabled: true` is applied to activities that are not yet enabled.

**C6-B is the worst single case.** After `Customer Evidence Requested → Evidence Received`, the case has left the S1 fast-close gate. The DFG only supports Fast Close from Initial Triage; the matching terminal after review is Close — No Safety Issue. Ranking Fast Close at 0.35–0.55, setting the alternative state back to S1, and writing “None significant” under contradicting evidence ignores the strongest counter-evidence in the file: someone already chose an evidence cycle, and the case was assigned to R-T4, who “usually handles potentially reportable cases.” Completeness 0.98 makes another request unlikely; it does not revive an unused triage shortcut.

**Other concrete mistakes**

- **C6-D alternative `S0 REGISTERED`** is incoherent. Registered, triage, and technical investigation are already in the prefix. A serious alternative is delayed evidence (source attachment, migration feed) or Reg already finished but unlogged.
- **C6-C rank 3 `Authority Notification`** is not enabled. Rule 6 and the DFG both put notification after a reportable regulatory conclusion (and, in the log, after Disposition). Batches go out at 16:00; this is not a live next event at 14:00 with Reg still missing.
- **C6-E calibration is poor.** Second evidence arrived at 13:20; review has not occurred; Supplier Analysis typically takes 6–36 h. Putting `Supplier Analysis Completed` at 0.25–0.45 as the next log event is not an honest interval. Completeness 0.72 plus an existing second cycle made `Customer Evidence Requested` the obvious omitted rival.
- **C6-A** correctly opens Tech / Reg / Supplier obligations, then forecasts two long-running completions (median Tech ~9 h, Supplier 6–36 h) instead of the live rival `Customer Evidence Requested` (completeness 0.86; DFG review→request = 101). Regulatory Assessment is required by Rule 4 and then disappears from the top three.
- **Queue and clock are under-used.** Engineer downtime and high-severity barge-in matter for A/D and against E; Reg’s 12-deep reportability queue and the 16:00 notification batch are mentioned more than they constrain ranks.

State sketches and caveats are the strongest parts. The forecasts systematically confuse “this must happen sometime” with “this is what the log will show next,” and C6-B plus the C6-D alternative show the semantics are not under control. That is a mid-pack answer, not a near-flawless one.
