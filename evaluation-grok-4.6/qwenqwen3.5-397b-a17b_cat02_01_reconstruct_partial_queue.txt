**3.8**

The write-up has the required heading skeleton and some sound instincts (labeled assumptions, resource-precedence bounds in principle, OPEN as elapsed not final, C2 outage as a capacity shock). It is not a defensible reconstruction. Arithmetic, definitions, and the required quantitative program all fail under a strict reading.

**Material calculation and consistency errors**

- **P12:** S2 wait is \(12{:}15-11{:}30=45\) min, not 70. A simple subtraction error on a fully observed case.
- **P17:** S2 wait is \(13{:}55-12{:}50=65\) min, not 5. The 5 min figure is elapsed S2 service at 14:00. Active service and residual time are confused on a case the prompt required to be treated explicitly.
- **P07:** Wait \([40,75]\) implies start \(\in[09{:}25,10{:}00]\) after I1 finishes P05, so service is \([0,35]\) (plus rounding), not \([25,60]\). The two columns contradict each other.
- **P15:** After I1’s P13 (\(11{:}10\)–\(11{:}40\)), a single-task start lies in \([11{:}40,12{:}15]\). Wait is then \([90,125]\) min and service \([0,35]\) min. The reported \([50,85]\) / \([30,65]\) is neither resource-feasible nor internally consistent (that wait interval would imply service \(40\)–\(75\)).
- **P11:** Bounds \([5,80]\) / \([0,75]\) only use the outage floor \(11{:}10\). C2 is on P08 from \(11{:}15\)–\(12{:}00\). A start in that window overlaps P08 by far more than \(\pm5\) min rounding, so the non-overlap interval is essentially \([12{:}00,12{:}25]\) (wait \(\approx 55\)–\(80\), service \(\approx 0\)–\(25\)), unless undocumented sharing is argued. It is not argued.
- **Utilization:** Body text gives S1 \(\approx 77\%\) from \(20\times 28/720\); the station table says \(85\)–\(95\%\). Those cannot both be the same estimate.

**Required analyses that are missing or only gestured at**

- No case-level **total sojourn** for completed cases.
- No **arrival/departure rates by subperiod** (counts or \(\lambda,\mu\)).
- No **WIP/queue-length path** and no convention for simultaneous rounded events (who is present when a reported end and the next start share a 5-minute stamp, e.g. P07 C1 end \(11{:}00\) and P09 C1 start \(11{:}00\)).
- **Little’s Law** is defined in §2 and never computed. The prompt asked for a check only where assumptions are defensible; silence is not a check.
- Sensitivity names optimistic/central/pessimistic but does not recompute waits, busy time, or utilization under those imputations for P05/P07/P11/P15.

**Process-mining / bottleneck errors**

- S1 waits are already \(20\to70\) min by P10, **before** the C2 outage. That is an S1 saturation from the early arrival burst (roughly 8 arrivals in the first hour vs two servers at \(\sim30\) min service). Calling the morning “balanced / no bottleneck” is not supported by the wait trajectory.
- Afternoon S1 waits are explained as downstream blocking / “queue full.” The log shows S1 **continuing** to start P16–P21 while S2 is backed up. There is no evidence of a finite buffer or blocking policy. S1 wait is time-to-I1/I2, not time in the S2 queue.
- End-of-window state (4 waiting for S2, 2 in S2, 1 in S1, 1 waiting for S1) does favor S2 as the later constraint; a time-varying **combination** is the reading the data actually support. The S2-only afternoon story overfits the maintenance interval.

**Section 4 is too coarse to meet the spec**

One row per station, a single 08:00–14:00 window, a single mean wait, and a vague WIP band do not separate transient morning flow from the post-10:55 regime. Coverage labels (“High”/“Medium”) are decorative. Peak WIP is not derived from a timeline. Busy time uses completed-case averages and drops MISSING/OPEN service, which the quality bar explicitly forbids.

**Python does not implement the paper analysis**

- `MISSING` and blank both become `None`; `OPEN` becomes 14:00. That **does** treat the three statuses as interchangeable at parse time, against the spec. P17 then has a non-null `s2_end` and can enter the “clean” PM4Py projection as a finished case.
- Resource-feasible imputation is deferred (“in a full implementation…”). No optimistic/central/pessimistic columns, no WIP series, no Little’s Law.
- `insert_case_*` is attempted only on a complete-complete subset (reasonable) but outputs are not clearly isolated as case-level cycle metrics, and the call is wrapped so API failure is silent.
- Nominal `sum` of service minutes **omits** NaN missing starts, so printed utilization undercounts busy time and cannot back §4.

**What remains acceptable**

A1–A7 are mostly the right kinds of assumptions; P05’s C1 bound after P03 is correct; OPEN vs final service is stated in prose; P16/P18–P22 censoring floors are right; the incomplete-case **count** as a lower bound on lag is valid; PM4Py vs pandas division of labor is conceptually acknowledged.

Those do not offset wrong highlighted-case numbers, an unused Little’s Law requirement, no rate or WIP path, a blocking story the log contradicts, and code that neither distinguishes statuses nor computes the uncertainty scenarios the narrative claims. Under a nearly-flawless bar this is a mid/low answer, not a near miss.
