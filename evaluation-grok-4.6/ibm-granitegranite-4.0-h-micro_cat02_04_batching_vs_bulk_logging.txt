**Cluster diagnoses are mostly wrong.** G1’s top label (genuine simultaneous batch) is the only clearly correct primary call, and even there the justification is inverted: identical 09:00–09:03 PLC timestamps plus a four-item fixture are the evidence, not “back-to-back” work or 1-second “batched logging.” The second label, end-of-shift data entry at 09:03, is absurd. **Section 2 is the wrong matrix and factually false.** The task asked for a *hypothesis-by-signature* matrix (how each competing explanation should look). The answer produced a cluster-by-signature table and then misstated the data: G1 end-equality is “No” when all four completes are 09:03; G3 start-equality is “Yes” when starts are 11:02/11:10/11:17/11:24; G4/G8 “overlap = Yes” when intervals are adjacent and non-overlapping; G6 start-equality is “No” when every start is 17:00; G7 start and end equality are both “No” when every interval is 14:00–14:15. Utilization consequences are asserted, not derived from resource-time vs case-time.
