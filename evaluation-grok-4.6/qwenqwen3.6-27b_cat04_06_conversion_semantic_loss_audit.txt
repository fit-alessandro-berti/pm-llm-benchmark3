**6.0**

The write-up is complete in outline and mostly aimed at the right problems (POWL as source of truth, process-tree failure as a legitimate outcome, layout ≠ semantics, serialization ≠ equivalence). Under a strict reading it is not close to a high score: the invariant table is sloppy, the audit overclaims, required diagnostics are missing, and the validation program is not a sound check of the stated semantics.

**What holds up.** Headings and table schemas match the spec. The source reading is largely correct: register first; assess/prepare unordered; legal optional via XOR+tau; verification gated on both predecessors; legal and the loop unordered; do-body is configure≺inspect; redo is correct-defect; at least one iteration; release joins both branches; archive is last. Choosing POWL→net→BPMN as primary, and treating POWL→net→tree→BPMN as likely to throw, is the right split. Sections 6–9 are the strongest part: gateway/layout/silent-routing ambiguity, quarantine rules, POWL-as-canonical vs derivative artifacts, and residual limits (untimed models, human misread, unbounded equivalence, engine variance) are on target.

**Invariant table (material defects).** INV-02 and INV-07 use model properties, not traces, as “negative witnesses.” INV-03’s positive witness inserts `skip_legal` as if it were a visible event, then marks the invariant unobservable after hiding tau. That last claim is wrong: optionality of *Legal Review* is exactly a fact about the visible language (traces with vs without the label). What hiding removes is the silent skip as an event, not the invariant. INV-10 (single start/end, exact final marking) is a net/conversion property, not a source-POWL semantic invariant. Loop *scope* (correct-defect is redo, not in the do-body) is only implicit.

**Conversion code (incomplete vs the checklist).** The happy path (`convert_to_petri_net` → `convert_to_bpmn`, tree in `try/except`, BPMN back to a net, marking null-checks, `check_soundness`, `save_vis_*`) is in the right shape and does not invent a POWL→BPMN API. It still misses an explicit requirement: workflow-net diagnostics (`check_is_workflow_net`) on every accepting net. Source POWL is never visualized. Tree-success goes tree→net→BPMN rather than BPMN-from-tree; that is allowed, but markings on that intermediate net are not checked as carefully. Tree failure is *assumed* in the audit rather than treated as an observed exception.

**Audit table (methodological error).** Allowed labels are used, but “validated preserved” appears for Petri→BPMN and the BPMN round-trip with no execution and no witness/footprint evidence. That term is then doing rhetorical work the rest of the document says it must not do. Petri→Tree is blanket “conversion failed” for every row without a caught exception. Several cells should have been `not tested` or `expected preserved`.

**Round-trip validation (the largest failure).** “Hide tau” is implemented as `if t.label is None: continue`, i.e. **silent transitions are never fired**. Hiding is a *projection* of the visible word, not deletion of silent moves from the token game. The designated positive witness is the *skip-legal* complete trace. On a faithful net that skip is a tau; this explorer cannot take it, so a correct conversion would fail the author’s own witness. Infinite silent cycling is “avoided” by refusing tau rather than bounding silent depth or detecting silent cycles. Negative witnesses are a placeholder `("Assess Risk", "Register Change", ...)` and are never checked. `check_trace` is dead. Playout is omitted despite being requested. Firing uses non-PM4Py attributes (`input_arcs` / `output_arcs`); reachability comparison uses a non-existent `.markings` on the transition system. Even if those names were fixed, marking-set equality is not behavioral equivalence. The exhaustiveness caveat is stated, which is one of the few things this section gets right.

**Net of the grade.** This is a competent exam-shaped design note with a broken checker and several places where language outruns evidence. It is well above a shallow or off-format answer, and well below something you could trust to accept or reject a conversion. Minor polish would not fix that; the tau-hiding mistake and the unearned “validated preserved” cells are enough to keep it far from the top of the scale.
