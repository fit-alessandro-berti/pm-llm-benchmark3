**Code sketch (Section 5).** The required PM4Py calls, frozen raw diagnostics, and external `NORM_STATUS` metadata are present and the comments are in the right spirit. The classifier is not. `classify()` only marks missing mandatory when `expected_weight is not None and observed_weight < expected_weight`, but the OC-DFG loop never passes `expected_weight`. N1 (98) and N4 (198) would therefore be labeled `conforming`, which is the main thing the semantic layer needed to catch. OTG is the same. The zero-observation backfill only helps edges that are entirely absent. `if expected_weight and ...` uses truthiness, so an expected weight of 0 would be skipped. Forbidden+`dq_suspect` collapses to `likely_data_quality` and drops the dual “formally forbidden” status used in the table. Conformance outputs are stored and then ignored; classification is rebuilt from discovery only.

The sketch is “executable-looking” as requested, not a claim of real PM4Py signatures, so that is not penalized.

The normative reading and investigation plan are well above average; the internal contradictions, the OC-DFG classifier bug, and the E6 robustness overclaim are enough to block anything in the high 8s–9s.
