### Model-semantics errors
M0 is mostly described correctly (concurrent checks, XOR of approved vs reject, notify then close, no resources). Calling the both-checks-before-decision requirement “sequential completion” is wrong: in M0 the checks are a partial order with no edge between them. The real constraint is *joint completion before the XOR*, not sequence.

The revised POWL has a real encoding defect. In the auto-approve route, `rmi_auto` is a LOOP node in the same `StrictPartialOrder` with **no edges**. In POWL that is a shuffle of `(Request_More_Info)*` with the entire sanctions→auto-approve→activate→quality path, so RMI may occur after `Quality_Review`. The prompt allowed an approximation only if the limitation is stated. It is stated for the standard-route concurrency comment; it is not stated for the auto route. That is hiding an unsafe over-approximation.

A second semantic slip: CS-9’s four-eyes rule is **requester ≠ manual approver**. The architecture and DECLARE layer repeatedly encode `actor(Register_Supplier) ≠ actor(Manual_Procurement_Approval)`. Registering clerk and requester are not shown to be the same role; SUP-712 is an identity-resolution problem on requester/approver aliases, not a proof that Register is the right left-hand side.

Reactivation vs skipped-register fraud is indistinguishable in the XOR (`de_novo_workflow` vs `reactivation_route`). `parent_case_id` in the JSON schema is the right mitigation; the POWL section does not say that the control-flow layer cannot tell them apart.

`timestamp(Quality_Review) - timestamp(Activate_Supplier) <= 48h` is not “two business days.” Weekends and holidays are exactly why the sources use business days.

The 12-month reactivation cutoff is invented policy. That is allowed in a revision, but it is presented as if it were a recovered CS-9 rule.

Most labels are defensible once overlay validity inside a window is accepted (702, 704) and sunset is treated as hard (705, 714). Control flow and CS-9 four-eyes (distinct humans) are satisfied. Dominant label should have been framed as COI/disclosure failure with control-flow compliance, or `MIXED` with that dominant reason—not process noncompliance in the same bucket as SUP-708.

SUP-711 as `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` with **high** confidence is taxonomically strained. Identical source transaction IDs from migration make the log defect determinate and the business trace otherwise M0-like. Indeterminate is the residual bucket, not a finding of high certainty.
