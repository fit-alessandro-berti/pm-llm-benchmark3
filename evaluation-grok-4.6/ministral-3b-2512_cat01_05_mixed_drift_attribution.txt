That is not enough. Under a strict reading of the spec, the quantitative work, causal restraint, and several hard requirements fail.

**Format and completeness.** Headings are `###` plus bold, not the required `##` titles. Section 2 is ~140 words against a 180–280 requirement and omits most of the mandated argument. **Section 1 is temporally sloppy.** Onsets are repeatedly misclassified. Supplier Z on 2026-05-05 is a step change (`supplier_z_pct` 18→43, `rework_variant_pct` 13→24), not gradual. The May–June cycle-time story uses March’s 31.2 h as the start point and assigns the 31.2→40.8 h rise to the 15 June staffing cut; the first large jump is April→May, *before* that cut. **Section 2 gets the central point backwards.** A label map that lifts fitness from ~0.55 to ~0.90 shows that *a large share of the raw non-conformance is a naming/duplication artifact*. It does **not** prove the business process was unchanged. That is exactly the distinction the prompt required.
