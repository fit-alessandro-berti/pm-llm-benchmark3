**6.1**

The analysis in §§1–5 is substantially better than the model revision in §§6–7. Dialectical structure, burden-of-proof framing, and case-level traceability are strong; the required POWL revision is not. Under a near-flawless standard, that gap is decisive.

**What holds up.** Stipulated vs. disputed propositions are separated cleanly, and authority is treated as the real issue (CS-9 vs. PD-3 vs. FD-7; expiry vs. “factual persistence”; desirability vs. amendment). Both briefs are actually argued, not caricatured. The prosecution correctly treats SUP-703, SUP-708, and post-expiry Auto_Approve as real failures and does not let a “desirable” Request_More_Info practice rewrite CS-9. The defense correctly treats M0 as an incomplete oracle and isolates CRM missingness, rejected-lead scope, reactivation, and label drift as non-equivalent to misconduct. The matrix covers all 15 cases, including M0-fitting resource problems (SUP-712, SUP-715). System-level judgment refuses a bland split: exception creep is real, and M0’s deviation rate is also inflated.

**Classification errors, not nits.** SUP-713 is not a justified exception. A post-hoc activity rename with equivalent content is vocabulary/model drift, not an authorized overlay like PD-3 or FD-7. Calling it `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` blurs the taxonomy the prompt requires. SUP-715 is entered as `MIXED` in the table and then walked to `NONCOMPLIANT` in prose, which is exactly the hedge the prompt forbids. SUP-705 as `NONCOMPLIANT` is defensible, but confidence and severity are not carried consistently against the conceded good-faith/emergency facts. SUP-702 is treated as a clean justified pilot case even though Notify is absent from the onboarding trace; the CRM `notification_id` is good evidence, but the write-up never states whether notification-before-close is found, waived by overlay, or merely assumed.

**Model semantics are the largest failure.** M0 is described correctly. M1 is not a defensible encoding of the required routes. `checks = {Sanctions_Check, Quality_Review}` is placed *before* a single XOR that includes auto-approve, emergency, and reject. That forces Quality_Review before every decision, which directly contradicts PD-3, FD-7, SUP-702/703/704/705/714, and the answer’s own Layer-2 story. Inventing `Provisional_Activate`, `Retrospective_Approval`, and `Retrospective_Quality_Review` does not repair this: those labels are not in the log, and the observed emergency traces are `Activate_Supplier` then `Quality_Review` then `Manual_Procurement_Approval`. The auto-approve traces are likewise `Auto_Approve → Activate_Supplier → Quality_Review`, which M1 cannot replay. Saying “Layer 2 overrides POWL” or “use route-specific templates” is not an approximation; it abandons a single normative POWL for the routes the prompt required it to represent.

Request_More_Info is also misplaced. SUP-707 (and the “desirable” practice) puts the loop *after* Sanctions_Check and *before* Quality_Review. M1 and constraint C8 put all RMI before both checks. That is the wrong control-flow claim. Reusing one `activate` Transition inside three XOR children is a POWL identity error. `loop_body` is dead. The print of `M1.order.graph.edges()` is not reliable PM4Py (`order` is a `BinaryRelation`). The prompt asked for **one** executable POWL block; the answer emits three scripts and a model that would not accept the very exception traces it classifies as justified.

**Data/governance layer is better than the POWL, still uneven.** The overlay registry, eligibility snapshots, canonical actor IDs, immutable notification IDs, and scope table for rejected leads/reactivations are the right artifacts. Several Layer-2 rules, however, restate the same incorrect sequencing (quality before all routes; RMI before sanctions). Resource/COI rules are discussed well in prose and only loosely bound to enforcement.

**Net.** This is a capable legal-process memo attached to a control-flow model that does not implement the routes it argues for. Fairness and evidence handling are well above average; model semantics and the revision artifact are not close to flawless.
