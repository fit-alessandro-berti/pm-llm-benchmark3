**7.0**

The write-up follows the required heading structure, respects the hard constraints (raw gaps ≠ discrimination; case mix ≠ automatic justification; risk scores not treated as neutral; support steps treated as possible burden; no proposal to remove them), and the bottom line is appropriately calibrated.

It is not close to flawless.

**Material flaws**
- Section 2 lists `applicant_group` as a matching variable. Matching on the grouping factor makes cross-group comparison impossible. The intended design is match/stratify on covariates and compare *across* groups. That is a basic identification error.
- Variant median cycle times (V4 19.2d, V5 21.6d) are treated as group-specific path times. The table gives one median per variant, not by group. Within-path treatment differences are therefore asserted, not shown.
- The PM4Py plan does not implement the strategy in §2. Required pieces are missing or wrong: `values` to `filter_event_attribute_values` should be a collection, not a scalar; no `filter_variants`, `filter_case_performance`, `get_all_case_durations`, or plain `discover_dfg`; `filter_paths_performance` is an empty stub; no region/channel filters, no matching/stratification, no sensitivity runs.

**Notable omissions / unclarities**
- SLA design (no separate timeliness targets for rights-preserving steps) is in the scenario and never used as a mechanism.
- Positivity is ignored: Group A cells for language support (2%) and accommodation (4%) are too thin for the matching design as written.
- Residual supervisor-review and request-docs gaps are discussed as “consistency” without separating composition (different legitimate paths) from same-path differential treatment.
- Provided signals are underused: two-region supervisor backlog, V6 appeal path, 90th-percentile tails, Group B 1.6d vs 0.4d wait on Completeness → Request Docs.

**What is solid**
Dimensions, limitations, mitigations-with-tradeoffs, and the indeterminate-but-concerning conclusion are substantively right and do not overclaim. Those strengths are why this is not lower; the identification slip, variant-time misread, and incomplete PM4Py plan are why it cannot be high.
