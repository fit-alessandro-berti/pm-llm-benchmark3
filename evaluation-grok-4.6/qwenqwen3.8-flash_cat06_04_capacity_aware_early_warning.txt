**7.4**

The answer is complete, correctly structured, and decision-theoretically serious: it refuses I-03, separates I-05 from I-07, treats `p_now` as observational rather than causal, applies segment calibration, and prices spillover into both selection and the impact table. Those are the main quality bars. It is not nearly flawless.

The largest defect is an internal contradiction on alert persistence. Section 1 says a repeated alert with no material new event is suppressed for swarm allocation if calibrated Δp < 0.10. I-02 moves 0.58 → 0.66 (0.08) and I-09 moves 0.62 → 0.69 (0.07); neither has a stated new event. Both are then selected, justified mainly as “second alert, no prior swarm.” That conflates the repeated-*swarm* rule with the repeated-*alert* rule. Section 4’s time-critical exception could have licensed I-02/I-09, but the allocation table never invokes it. I-05 is suppressed on the same persistence logic, so the rule is not applied evenly.

The ranking apparatus is weaker than it looks. The “risk-adjusted score” is missing operators, so it is not deployable as written. Thresholds (p_adj_low ≥ 0.35, ARR_med ≥ 0.10, Δp ≥ 0.10, 1.0 h, 4 h cool-down, escalation at 0.70) are not derived from the given economics; the 0.35 floor in particular looks reverse-engineered to keep I-04 and drop I-08. That drop is not reconciled with expected value: I-08’s median net after spillover (~€2.2k) beats I-02 (~€0.3k). Robust exclusion of I-08 is defensible, but then the policy is maximin/robust, not the expected-net ranking advertised in Section 1.

Capacity logic is slightly inconsistent. The fourth slot is withheld because I-01’s median net after base spillover is negative, which is the right call. Section 2 then names I-01 as the release candidate if the reserve is unused, which violates the same “only spend if net after spillover is positive” rule unless congestion is assumed to be low—and that condition is not stated.

Smaller issues: no option value of waiting on I-04 (10 h to deadline, still pre-diagnosis); no sequencing constraint from two specialists delivering 45-minute swarms; confidence multipliers are used in selection but not carried into the impact arithmetic except via the Adverse row; minus signs are dropped in the score formula and in one net-impact cell, which undercuts the claim that the table is auditable. The €20k untreated-value proxy is disclosed and acceptable.

The six required sections, the 10-row allocation table, the three-scenario arithmetic (the base 0.58 / €16.55k / €7.2k / ~€8k path is correct), the I-05 vs I-07 comparison, and the fairness/feedback/monitoring safeguards are all present and mostly operational. Those strengths keep the grade well above a weak p-ranking answer; the persistence contradiction, the non-operational score, and the I-08/I-01 inconsistencies keep it well below a high score under a strict rubric.
