**7.4**

The final write-up is a strong process-mining survival analysis: estimands are defined before estimators, competing events are not treated as ordinary censoring, open cases are retained, immortal time and prefix leakage are handled, and the required nine-section format is followed with one substantial code block. It is not near-flawless.

**What is solid.** Time origin (`Submit`), horizon (72 h), and the distinction among right censoring, competing events, and already-observed breaches are clear. E1–E3 are operationally mapped. Concrete IDs are used well (`S03`/`S09`/`S06`/`S08`; transfers `S08`/`S21`/`S32` all P1 and escalated; long-gap opens `S11`/`S25`/`S26`). Known-case arithmetic is correct (18/27, bounds 18/32 and 23/32, P1 5/7). The feature table, derived last-event times for `S10`/`S11`/`S25`, and observed-only transition list are process-aware without invented timestamps. The SAP recommendation (Aalen–Johansen primary, landmarking secondary, multi-state only descriptively at n=32) fits the data. The code encodes multiple estimands and transfer scenarios, keeps open rows, stratifies, and degrades if PM4Py fails.

**Material defects.**

1. **Broken mathematics.** Core formulas are unreadable: `P(T_R  72)`, `P(T_R > 72 and T_R < )`, `lim_{dt0} P(t  T_R < t+dt | T_any  t)`. For a survival estimand document this is a real unclarity, not a typesetting nit.

2. **Sensitivity claims that the evidence does not support.** Section 6 says “P1 has the highest breach risk” could reverse if transfers are censored, and “Cohort B is worse” could reverse if transfers count as success. On the same known-case accounting the write-up uses, P3 is higher than P1 (7/9 vs 5/7), and B is not worse than A (9/14 vs 9/13). Those reversals are asserted, not shown.

3. **Prose–code mismatch on late transfers.** The encoding table correctly treats `S32` (transfer at 96 h) as a known internal non-resolution by 72 for E2. Under `transfer_censor`, the code censors at `min(dur, 72)`, discarding that the case was still unresolved at the horizon. That is the wrong information set for the no-resolve estimand.

4. **Smaller slips.** E2 event time is left as “duration or 72 depending encoding” in the table; Fine–Gray is stretched toward a composite 72-hour endpoint; `pm4py.get_variants` is called on a timestamp-free DataFrame (fallback saves the requirement, but the PM4Py path is speculative).

The analysis would be in the high 8s/low 9s if the formulas were intact, sensitivity conclusions matched the case counts, and transfer-after-72 censoring matched the stated estimand. Under a strict bar those gaps keep it well below “nearly flawless.”
