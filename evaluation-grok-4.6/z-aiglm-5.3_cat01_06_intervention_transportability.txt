**7.6**

The answer is a strong, well-structured transportability assessment that correctly refuses a naive copy of Site A, segments by legal and channel constraints, and treats the 9-hour median drop as a confounded composite rather than an identified treatment effect. Format is largely followed. The main losses are a quantitative double-count, over-reading of DFG frequencies, a likely-wrong POWL constructor, and a few graph/measurement slips. Under a strict bar those are enough to keep it well short of near-flawless.

**What holds up**

Process structure is handled carefully. Rule 4 is treated as a hard gate, not a sequencing preference. The DFG identity `Originals Receipt → Document Check = 2,108` is correctly aligned with 62% of 3,400 cases. Digital cases are identified as the true analog of Site A’s sequential identity-then-document flow. Legal review, the supplement loop, specialized pools, the case lock, and timestamp back-entry are all treated as effect modifiers rather than noise.

Causal transport is explicit: S-nodes, a shared AND-join at Assessment, site-specific originals delay and legal loop, and a clear statement that matching control-flow syntax does not transport effects. Co-interventions (API, +2 reviewers, mix shift, huddles, no negative control, overlap-as-recording-artifact) are carried through the evidence table instead of being mentioned once and dropped.

The intervention and pilot are the best parts. Executable document tasks are not created for originals-required cases; “blocked-ready” plus event release is the right modification. The bottleneck-shift scenario has median down and p90 up, as required. The pilot separates dose (validated overlap, creation, AND-join release) from outcome, uses ineligible strata as negative controls, and includes a switchback for pool interference.

**Material flaws**

The 9-hour decomposition double-counts service-time change. Serial 3.5+7.2 vs parallel max(3.1, 6.5) already folds in the 0.4 h identity and 0.7 h document reductions, which are then added again as API/staffing/huddles. The “structural share 35–55%” and the Site B ranges that inherit it are therefore too clean. That is a direct hit on quantitative transport.

DFG counts are treated as exclusive case-level precedence. `Identity Check → Originals Receipt = 1,020` is not “identity already precedes originals in 1,020 of 2,108 originals cases.” Directly-follows is not always-precedes, and the 1,020 is not shown to be a subset of the 2,108. The 90% sequential claim for digital cases (`1,160/1,292`) is similarly a bit too precise.

Tier 1 ≈23% and Tier 2 ≈15% assume independence of “digital” and “legal review.” Public-sector/high-value work is likely to correlate with originals. The 38% “digital” share is also just the complement of “originals legally required,” not evidence that those cases have complete files at registration.

In the graph, `timestamp_reliability → true_overlap` points at the latent dose rather than at observed overlap. `role_specialization → true_overlap` has mixed sign (separate pools help concurrency, prevent pooling) but is drawn as a single unlabeled arrow.

**Model and format**

Section 8 almost certainly does not match current PM4Py POWL. The usual objects are `OperatorPOWL` plus the process-tree `Operator` enum, and edges often go on `po.order.add_edge`, not `Operator(Operator.XOR, [...])` / `po.add_edge`. Try/except around soundness and playout hides API mismatch rather than constructing the model as specified. `redo.add_edge(supp, assess2)` is redundant given `supp → doc2 → assess2`. The comments on what the net cannot encode (guards, capacity, locks, durations) are good; the constructors are not.

Section 2’s prose is slightly over the 180–280 cap; Section 9 is at or slightly over 400. The “no negative-control” row in the evidence table is a design lesson stuffed into an evidence-transfer confidence score.

**Net**

The analogical core is right: copy the partial order only where document data exist at registration; do not copy the 9-hour number, uniform task creation, or unvalidated overlap. That, plus a segmented pilot that can distinguish mechanism failure from implementation failure, is most of the assignment. The grade is held down because the quantitative mapping, DFG interpretation, and POWL construction—the pieces the rubric names as model fidelity and scenario analysis—are not as clean as the qualitative argument.
