### Critical failures

**1. Microbiology readiness is mis-timed by about a day.**  
Primary incubation is 46–54 h after Friday 14:00, i.e. **Sunday 12:00–20:00**, not Saturday. Saturday 13:00–15:00 on-call readout is impossible. The assumption-ledger sensitivity (“if 100% on-call, readout occurs Saturday”) and the delayed-micro scenario are therefore built on a false clock. Weekend on-call can at best pull readout to Sunday afternoon/evening; the default is Monday 08:00 plus 0.5–1.5 h. That is the central calendar fact of the micro branch, and it is missed.

**2. Section 4’s hours and clock times contradict each other, and the P50 band is calendar-impossible.**  
Friday 15:30 → Monday 10:00 is **~66.5 h**, not 18.5 h. The 18.5 h figure is the time-of-day difference after dropping Saturday and Sunday. The same break appears throughout the table:

| Stated remaining | Implied clock from Fri 15:30 | Stated clock |
|---|---|---|
| 18.5 h | Saturday 10:00 | Monday 10:00 |
| 40–60 h | Sunday 07:30–Monday 03:30 | Monday 18:00–Tuesday 12:00 |
| 70–90 h | Monday 13:30–Tuesday 09:30 | Wednesday 10:00–14:00 |
| 120–168 h | Wednesday 15:30–next Friday 15:30 | Friday–following Monday |

A central/P50 remaining time of 40–60 h lands on Sunday or the Monday pre-open window, when chemistry is paused, micro is only just becoming technically ready, and QA is closed. That is not a defensible P50. This is a failure of the required deliverable, not a rounding issue.

**3. The “earliest” terminal time contradicts the author’s own decomposition.**  
Smooth-path join is written as Monday 10:30, then 4–6 h QA wait, 1.5–3.5 h service, and release — i.e. Monday afternoon/evening. Earliest completion is still listed as Monday 10:00. That would require skipping QA. An optimistic earliest near Monday 10:00–12:00 *can* be argued (Friday chemistry finish + Sunday on-call + thin Monday QA queue), but that path is never constructed; the numbers on the page are inconsistent.

**4. Chemistry is not a one-server sum, and Friday finish is almost ruled out.**  
Two analysts, residual work 0.4–0.9 h and 1.2–2.0 h, **four** jobs ahead (one urgent, three ordinary), 2.5 h until close, and \(\mathrm{tri}(1.5,2.5,4.5)\) service. Adding “1.2–2.0 + 0.4–0.9 + 2.5” is the wrong model. Under FIFO, B-417 essentially cannot start on Friday. Under risk-priority the urgent sample still has a claim on the first free analyst; the second analyst frees ~16:42–17:30, which is too late to finish a \(\ge 1.5\) h test by 18:00. Friday completion is only a thin exception (first free analyst takes B-417 *and* a short draw). The write-up’s “starts 16:30–17:00, finishes 19:00–19:30, therefore Monday 08:00 + 2.5 h = 10:30” is internally incoherent: either Friday work reduces Monday residual, or service does not start Friday. FIFO vs priority is not a 0.5–2 h effect; it is the difference between a Monday-morning residual and a Monday queue behind several unfinished jobs.

**5. The QA queue at the future join is modelled backwards.**  
Arrivals are **only during QA opening hours** (Fri 08:00–20:00, Sat 09:00–13:00, not Sunday, Mon 08:00–20:00). Friday 15:30–Monday 10:30 contains ~11 QA hours, not a 0.35/h stream over calendar time. More importantly, the current five batches can be worked Friday 15:30–20:00 *and* Saturday 09:00–13:00 (one reviewer, median 1.5 h). Median capacity over that window is on the order of the incumbent queue. The join-time queue is residual plus overdispersed arrivals, **not** “5 + 6 = 11 still sitting there Monday.” Saturday QA hours exist in the prompt specifically to prevent this mistake. The 4–6 h Monday wait is therefore unearned.

### Structural and probabilistic problems

**Scenarios are mis-partitioned.** “Delayed microbiology readout” at 25–35% is mostly the **modal** no-on-call path (authorization only 0.25–0.45). The distinctive micro delay is contamination confirmation (+20–30 h continuous + readout), which is buried. Smooth-path 30–40% already assumed a 65% Monday readout, so the first and third rows overlap by construction. Chemistry-rerun 10–20% is just the rerun marginal, not a terminal-path probability. QA query/investigation 20–30% is low once query (0.15–0.30) and investigation (0.04–0.10 normal, 0.30–0.55 after anomaly) can co-occur.

**\(\max\) is asserted, then ignored.** In the author’s own numbers chemistry joins Monday ~10:30 and routine micro is Monday ~08:00–10:00. Sunday on-call then **does not move the join**. The ledger and §6 still treat denied on-call as +16–24 h. That is adding clocks, not competing them. A careful answer would say: on-call only binds if chemistry has already finished on Friday (unlikely); otherwise Monday chemistry and Monday micro readout are a close race.

**Dominant-clock language is unstable.** Smooth path is labelled “microbiology incubation,” then join is taken from chemistry. Incubation *duration* is longer in raw hours; after the chemistry weekend pause the two branches **synchronize on Monday morning**. That synchronization is the interesting structure and is never stated.

**Dependence is named, not used.** Midpoints are plugged in everywhere (35% on-call, 30% excursion, 22.5% query, 15% rerun). The invented uplifts “rerun ~30%, contamination ~20% if flag confirmed” are not labelled as judgement. Conditional independence given the flag is reasonable; treating those midpoints as if they were data is not. Rejection at 5–10% has no critical-defect mechanism in the prompt.

**Rerun timing is wrong even on the author’s clock.** If first-pass chemistry finishes Monday 10:30, a priority rerun does not “start Monday 08:00.” Lab investigation 3–7 **calendar** days is the real P90/P95 engine; the tail row “following Monday 10:00” is near the *optimistic* end of that range if investigation starts Monday/Tuesday, not the severe end.

### What is actually defensible (and is only partly present)

A strict forecast looks roughly like this:

- Chemistry almost surely pauses at 18:00. Optimistic residual finishes Monday ~09:00–12:00; FIFO can push start into Monday afternoon.
- Micro is technically ready Sunday 12:00–20:00; readout is Sunday afternoon only with on-call, otherwise Monday ~08:30–09:30. Contamination adds a further ~1 day.
- Join is \(\max(\text{chem},\text{micro})\), typically **Monday morning–early afternoon**, not a Saturday event and not 18.5 h from “now.”
- QA can burn down the incumbent queue Friday evening and Saturday; Monday congestion is uncertain, not “11 batches.”
- Smooth electronic release: **Monday afternoon–Tuesday**, ~66–96 calendar hours.
- Query / first-pass investigation: **Tuesday–Wednesday** (working-hour, not calendar, additions).
- Lab investigation / correlated excursion–rerun–contamination: **~3–7+ calendar days**, P90-class, not a 40–60 h centre.

The submitted answer gestures at that shape in weekday labels while the arithmetic, the Saturday readout, the QA inventory, and the remaining-hour column all say something else.
