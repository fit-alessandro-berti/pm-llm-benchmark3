### Material causal-graph errors
The DAG cannot represent the paper’s own confounding story.

- `quality_outcome → case_completed` is temporally impossible. `outcome_30d` is a post-close appeal flag; it cannot select cases into the completed sample.
- Residual difficulty is drawn as `complexity → unobserved_case_risk` with **no** arrow from that risk into `routing_rule` or `resource_group`. After conditioning on complexity, residual risk then does not confound assignment. The text’s central threat (“difficulty beyond coded complexity drives Orion routing”) is not in the graph.
- `unobserved_reviewer_skill` is disconnected from `resource_group`. As drawn it is neither a group mechanism nor a confounder of group contrasts. The prose nonetheless treats skill as “inside the Orion bundle” and claims assumed arrows “from group to … quality” that are absent from the adjacency list.
- `shift → arrival_load` reverses the more plausible time-of-arrival structure (clock time jointly sets shift mix and system load).

Those are not notation nits. They are identification mistakes in the section whose job is to encode identification.
