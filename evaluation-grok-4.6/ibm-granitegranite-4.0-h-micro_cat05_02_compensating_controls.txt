The response copies the required heading skeleton but fails the actual control-review task: it misstates case facts, collapses the FEP-1 vs. objective distinction, and delivers non-compliant, non-runnable checking code.

**Section 1 is not a decomposition of FEP-1.** The five items mostly rename the compensating controls (AV-2, ED-1, RA-7) or restate the rule itself. Missing threat scenarios include honest single-operator error, self-dealing, credential compromise, bank-account substitution, collusion, goods-not-received, and emergency-process abuse.
