**6.6**

The write-up is structurally complete and methodologically on the right side of the rubric: it uses `max` rather than a sum, keeps queue wait separate from service, respects the weekend calendars, treats FIFO vs risk-priority as a real fork, and treats the temperature flag as a common cause rather than an independent coin-flip. It also follows the required six-section format and does not collapse the problem into a single fake point estimate. That is a competent forecast skeleton, not a weak one. It is not nearly flawless.

The most damaging defect is **calibration dressed up as precision**. The P50 band is given as **75–93 h = Mon 18:30–Tue 10:30**. Tuesday 10:30 is **91 h** from Friday 15:30 (96 h to Tuesday 15:30, minus 5 h), not 93 h. The parenthetical “96−3” is simply wrong. A remaining-time forecast that advertises clock-to-hour conversions cannot miss a 2-hour mapping in its headline interval.

The **P90 / severe-tail band is worse**. Lab investigation is only about 4–12% of paths. The 90th percentile should *enter* that family, not run to its extreme. **136–260 h (Thu 24 Sep through Tue 29 Sep)** is essentially the full support of a 3–7 day laboratory investigation, i.e. something closer to P95–P99, not P90. Merging “P90-like” and “severe tail” into one 124-hour window makes the upper forecast look more likely than the path probabilities in §2 allow.

There is also an **internal contradiction on microbiology confirmation**. §3 correctly notes that a Sunday authorized readout plus a 20 h confirmation incubation can still produce a usable Monday result. The scenario table then sets the earliest terminal for that path at **Tue 08:30**. Those two statements cannot both be the operational earliest time.

Several smaller issues compound the impression of careful prose over careful arithmetic:

- The **smooth-path earliest of Mon 09:30** is optimistic. QA service has median 1.5 h and no stated minimum; a near-empty Monday queue plus sub-median review plus chemistry already done is a thin slice of the joint distribution, not a “plausible earliest” band of only two hours.
- The P50 interval **walks across Monday night**, when QA is closed. Mass is bimodal (late Monday *or* Tuesday morning), not spread through 20:00–08:00. That is not disclosed in §4.
- **Monday inbound chemistry work** is ignored. The chemistry clock is built only from Friday’s residual queue. New Monday samples would push FIFO completions later and widen the join.
- Clock stamps such as **13:30 / 18:30 / 19:30** are finer than the triangular and queue inputs support, after the text has warned against false precision.
- The required “delayed microbiology *readout* or confirmation” scenario is reduced to contamination. Weekend non-authorization is correctly judged as often non-terminal, but it is then dropped from the scenario table instead of being carried as a distinct calendar branch with its own (narrower) effect.

What remains good should still be counted. Incubation arithmetic (Sun 12:00–20:00) is right. Friday chemistry completion is correctly treated as rare. QA Saturday is used to burn the *current* queue, not to review B-417 before both results exist. Dependence, overlaps, and the temperature flag as the highest-leverage next observation are the right calls. Those strengths keep the grade out of the lower half. The mapping error, the mislabeled tail, and the scenario-table inconsistency keep it well short of a high score under a strict reading.
