**5.5**

The final JSON is well-formed, case-specific, and not a DFG-copying answer. Primary states, obligation lists, feed/queue awareness, and the A-vs-B discussion are the strongest parts. It still fails several core requirements of the task.

**Next-event task is systematically misread.** Ranks 2–3 are mostly later events on one path, not competing next observations. That clashes with the required top-three *distribution*. C6-E rank 3 even calls Supplier Analysis a “later event.” C6-A/B/C likewise put Tech Inv, Reg Assessment, Close, and Authority Notification after an as-yet-unlogged Evidence Review. Midpoints then cannot be a coherent next-event distribution.

**Normative alternatives are often the wrong state.** C6-A is not S2: Evidence Received is already logged. C6-B is not S1 after an evidence cycle. C6-C is not S3 after Review and Tech Inv have completed. The right alternatives (parallel S4 already open for A/E; delayed/unlogged Reg Assessment or S5 for C/D) are missed or poorly motivated.

**`process_enabled` is unused.** All 15 predictions are `true`, so the required split between an enabled action and the next *logged* completion is never encoded. C6-C Authority Notification is not enabled (Reg Assessment has not concluded reportable; DFG and rule 6 put it after disposition, in a 16:00 batch). C6-B Close and C6-E another evidence request are enabled only after the current review.

**Control-flow errors.** High+injury C6-D cannot go to Disposition as a compliant next event unless Reg Assessment already finished and is missing from the feed; that caveat is too weak. C6-C Authority Notification as next skips both Reg Assessment completion and Disposition. C6-D’s missing evidence is more consistent with delayed *Received/Review* (attachment already in the source system) than a fresh Request.

**Weaker process use of the live snapshot.** A, B, and E all contend for the same 2 reviewers and the “4 waiting” queue; A/B may *be* those items. C and D contend for Regulatory Assessment. High-severity A could already have Tech Inv running since ~09:08 (DFG triage→tech inv exists; R-T4 is a reportable resource), which is the only clean reason Tech Inv Completed could beat Review to the log—and that argument is not made.

**What is actually good.** Primaries S3/S3/S4/S4/S3 are right. Lot/supplier and high-severity/reportability obligations are mostly correct. Migration is applied only to C6-D. Intervals are broad rather than fake-precise. DFG is treated as observational. Cross-case text correctly refuses identical A/B forecasts.

Those strengths keep it out of the lower band. The next-vs-subsequent error, wrong state IDs, unused `process_enabled`, and a few illegal next events are enough, under a strict bar, to block anything near “nearly flawless.”
