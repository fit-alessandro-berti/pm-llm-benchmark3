**2.0**

The draft never becomes the required nine-section document, and the conclusions it does settle on rest on a fatal misreading of the operators.

## What the draft finally claims

After a long internal debate it locks in these positions:

- `+` is exclusive choice, so both models are sequential and have “no concurrency.”
- Model B cannot put `Check Revision` and `Analyze Revision` in the same trace.
- \(L_B \subseteq L_A\) for visible traces after hiding `tau`.
- The two models are **not equivalent** under visible-trace equivalence, P1, P2, or P3.
- Witnesses such as `Open, Analyze, Rework, Check, Close` are in A but not B; there are no B-only traces.
- A simpler tree with sequential `Analyze` then `Check` inside the loop is sketched and then cut off.

Those are the “final” claims. They are almost all wrong.

## The operator error

The task states Model B as “one check runs **concurrently** with the entire revision loop,” writes that check under `+(...)`, and asks for `pm4py.parse_process_tree`. In PM4Py, `+` is **AND / parallel** and `X` is XOR. Model A’s `+('Analyze Revision', 'Check Revision')` is a parallel pair **inside** each loop iteration. Model B’s `+('Check Revision', *(...))` is **one** check in parallel with the **whole** loop.

The draft treats `+` as XOR throughout. That inverts the languages:

| Fact under the actual operators | Draft’s settled claim |
|---|---|
| Every complete A-trace has **both** Analyze and Check **per iteration** (interleaved) | A may take Analyze **or** Check |
| Every complete B-trace has **exactly one** Check, interleaved anywhere through the loop, **and** at least one Analyze | B’s check-branch has **no** Analyze |
| B can (and must, on the loop branch) mix Check with Analyze/Rework | B cannot mix them |
| Inclusion \(L_B \subseteq L_A\) is false | Inclusion is asserted |
| Both models have real concurrency; the join before `Close Case` is a parallel join | “No parallel constructs” |

Once that parse is wrong, the counterexamples, the inclusion “proof,” the P1/P2 abstractions, and the structural table are all unsound. Being eventually confident about a false language is worse than remaining uncertain.

## Verdicts (even the accidentally right ones)

- **Visible traces / P3.** “Not equivalent” happens to be correct, but for the opposite reason: A pairs a check with **every** iteration; B has **one** check concurrent with the entire loop. The proposed A-only traces (`Analyze` then `Rework` then `Check` with no second analysis in that iteration, or traces with Check and no Analyze) are **not** A-traces under parallel `+`. The claim that B has no unique traces is false: B admits a single Check overlapping several Analyze/Rework cycles; A does not.
- **P1.** “Not equivalent” is again accidentally right, but the draft’s reason (B never checks after an analysis) is the reverse of P1. P1 requires **each** analysis to have its **own** check before the next Rework or Close. A’s per-iteration `Analyze ∥ Check` is the design that can meet that; B’s single concurrent check is exactly what P1 rejects when there are two or more analyses.
- **P2.** The draft says not equivalent because B cannot have `check_occurred ∧ analyze_count ≥ 1`. Under the real operators **every** complete trace of **both** models has at least one Analyze and at least one Check. After P2’s collapse (checks → Boolean, hide Approve/`tau`, keep Analyze count and “any Rework”), A and B can look the same. Calling them unequivalent under P2 for a reason that is factually inverted is a serious error, not a near miss.

Optional `X('Approve Revision', tau)` and hidden `tau` are discussed, but never used to repair the operator mistake. Distinguishing labels by position is stated and then wasted on the wrong language.

## Missing required work

The prompt demands a single Markdown artifact with **exactly** nine top-level headings, a structural table, a verdicts table, ≥4 traces (or a real inclusion proof), self-contained PM4Py code, footprint/EMD discussion **with** code, structural counts **or** exact derived numbers, a finished simplified model with preservation table and regression/negative traces, and a recommendation with a bounded-comparison warning.

None of that is delivered. There is no runnable parser/net/soundness/BFS comparison, no `simplicity_petri_net` figures, no assigned `simplified_model`, no preservation table, no regression/negative traces. Planning to write them is not the answer.

## What is actually sound

Binary-loop operational semantics (“do the first child once; exit or do the redo child and the first child again”) is correct. The idea that equivalence depends on an observation boundary is the right *shape* of answer. Footprints / stochastic language / EMD cannot prove trace equivalence — that point is true, but it is only outlined.

Those fragments do not outweigh a language that is the dual of the models, an inclusion claim that does not hold, P2 reasoning that is backwards, and a missing deliverable.

A 2 is already generous: it credits the loop semantics and the multi-view framing. Under the stated bar (hypercritical, even minor flaws sink the score), inverted concurrency plus no required artifact cannot go higher.
