The final write-up is a competent station-level reconstruction with the right structure, but it is not close to flawless. Strong case arithmetic and imputation constraints are undercut by a broken Little’s-Law argument, an incomplete required code section, and a few claims that do not survive the author’s own scenarios.

**What holds up.** Most completed-case waits, services, and sojourns match the table. Resource timelines are used properly for the four missing starts (I1 after P05/P13; C1 after P03; C2 after P08, not after maintenance). Total sojourns for P05/P07/P11 are correctly treated as exact. OPEN vs WAITING vs MISSING are not collapsed. Utilization includes in-progress work (P15, P17, P21), not completed cases only. The time-varying bottleneck story is defensible: early overload at both stations, then WIP shifting onto S2; C2’s outage as a ~25-minute cascade rather than a 15-minute local hole is reasoned. The three-scenario frame and the list of stable vs unstable conclusions are the right shape.

**Serious defects.**

1. **Little’s Law is circular, not a check.** In [11:00, 13:00] the author sets \(\int L=825\), then “mean sojourn \(=825/7=117.9\)”, then \(L=\lambda W\) recovers 6.88. That identity is automatic and does **not** use the actual S1 sojourns of the seven leavers (all of which entered S1 before 11:00). Calling this “exact consistency” and using it to “confirm” the P15 start is a core queueing error. The interval is also a poor LL window (startup/drain, time-varying \(\lambda\)). The all-window 3425/360 vs a 7-point snapshot mean is only a rough accounting check.

2. **Section 7 does not meet the spec.** The single Python block dies inside `pm4py.format_dataframe`. It never calls `insert_case_service_waiting_time` or `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`, never uses or comments `convert_log_to_time_intervals`, and never prints the case/station/WIP/scenario tables the narrative relies on. Reproducibility is asserted, not delivered.

3. **Unstable claims marked stable.** Scenario C cuts I1 busy time by ~60 minutes (\(\rho\approx71\%\)), so “utilization \(>78\%\) for all resources” and “small imputation effects” are false on the author’s own bounds. Station “utilization range” was required; only point estimates appear. S1 mean wait “67 min” does not match the case table (~63–65 depending on whether P21 is included).

**Smaller issues.** Service-time means mix fully observed and imputed samples without a clear rule. Peak/mean WIP are unweighted hourly snapshots, not time-integrals (except the unverifiable 825). Rounding is stated (±5 min on differences) but not carried into metric ranges. Scenario C’s 5-minute service floor is arbitrary (labeled, but it then pollutes “stable” utilization). The PM4Py log treats imputed intervals as observed.

Reconstruction and uncertainty framing are clearly above a mid-pack answer; the LL fallacy plus a truncated mandatory code section, under a strict rubric, cap the grade at 6.0.
