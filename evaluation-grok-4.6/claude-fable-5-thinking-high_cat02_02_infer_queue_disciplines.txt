2. **SRPT timing is internally inconsistent.** The QB table correctly has SRPT picking B03 at 09:31 (resume of B01 is observed). The diagnosis text still says SRPT “mimics the B02 preemption until refuted at 10:36.” 10:36 refutes non-preemptive SPT. SRPT is already dead at 09:31, and again at 10:00 and 10:14.
