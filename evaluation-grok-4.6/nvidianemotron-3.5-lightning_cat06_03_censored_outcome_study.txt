**5.3**

The write-up avoids the usual failure modes (treating the 21,600-row export as the population, coding rejects as non-default, a random split, and using post-outcome fields as features). The nine-section structure is complete, the 48,000-case census adds up, and the leakage table is mostly directionally right. That is not enough for a high score: the statistical target, the time scale of the proposed model, the validation windows, and the PM4Py skeleton all contain material errors.

**Target and estimand.** The primary target is the right *family* of questions (default only after activation). The estimand is not. The text defines the population as eventually activated accounts, then writes a probability “conditional on the observed prefix” without conditioning on activation. Those are different objects. \(P(\text{default}_{90}\mid\text{prefix})\) is not identified from these data; \(P(\text{default}_{90}\mid\text{prefix},\text{activated})\) is a selected-population parameter and cannot be applied to “every credit application” at the first few events without an explicit selection or two-stage model. Prediction at Credit Review is also a different operational problem from a short prefix after start; both are offered as if interchangeable. Horizon wording collides “prediction cutoff,” prefix end, activation+90d, and extract date.

**Competing risks on the wrong clock.** Section 3 puts rejected, withdrawn, transferred, and still-open cases into a cause-specific / Fine–Gray model whose risk set is activated accounts and whose time origin is activation. Cases that never activate do not sit on a time-since-activation clock. That is not a wording slip; it is the central design choice, and it is incoherent. Selection into activation and competing events after activation needed to be separated. “Censor at activation” for partner accounts with no feed is equivalent to dropping them. Calling the 3,600 immature activated cases left-truncated is the wrong missing-data language (delayed ascertainment / late label, not left truncation).

**Cohort table vs labels.** The 21,600 rows are defined in the prompt as matured labeled outcomes. The table still marks them as possibly right-censored at extract. The 3,600 immature activated cases are the censored/delayed group. Still-open cases are called both a competing outcome and right-censored. Transfers are not split into the stated 60% linked vs 40% unlinkable follow-up.

**Validation.** Temporal splitting is the correct idea, and the argument against a random split is fine. The dates are not. Training is described as covering early post-policy data while ending on 30 June 2025, the day before the policy break. Test fold 1 (starts Oct–Dec 2025) will often lack a matured 90-day label plus 7–35 day feed by 15 February 2026. Test fold 2 uses application starts in January–February 2026; the event window ends 31 December 2025, so those cases are not in the snapshot. Subgroup performance (channel, region, partner-origin, missing-feed) is barely specified despite being requested.

**Features and leakage.** Dropping `final_limit`, `decision_reason`, `collection_status`, and post-prefix events is correct. The resource “repair” is not: `extraction_time - event_time` is recency, not experience at the event. `first_activation_activity` and a prefix-neighborhood default rate are leak-prone or post-treatment. Same-day `documents_total` is handled only as a slogan.

**Section 9.** The skeleton does not implement the pipeline the prompt asked for. `pm4py.get_trace_statistics_log`, `pm4py.get_case_attributes` as used, and `pm4py.filter_event_log(..., lambda)` are not appropriate public APIs for this job. Activation time is the case’s last timestamp, not the `Account Activated` event. Prefix objects are not queried as `pref[0].attributes["time:timestamp"]`. `label_available_at` is never parsed. Terminal states for the 48,000-case flow are not built. Post-prefix *columns* are not stripped from a feature table. Printed “cohort counts” are not the required census. `get_variants` is first used as if it found activations. Comments about later survival modeling do not offset a non-runnable, conceptually wrong risk-set construction.

Net: honest about selection and leakage, unusable as a statistical analysis plan or implementation sketch without rework.
