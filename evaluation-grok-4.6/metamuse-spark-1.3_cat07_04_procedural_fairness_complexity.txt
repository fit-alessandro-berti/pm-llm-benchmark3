**Core logical flaw: over-reading the preliminary adjusted model.** The given controls are `risk_score_band`, `documentation_completeness`, `channel`, `household_size_band`, `prior_case_count`, `region`, and `month`. They do **not** include `language_preference` or `disability_accommodation_requested`. **Related misattribution of Supervisor Review for Group C.** V5 already includes Supervisor Review and is 31% of C versus 2% of A. Mechanism 6 still offers “discretionary caution” for C’s +5.5pp supervisor residual. **Missed accessibility finding.** Group B has 57% language need but only 24% V4; Group C has 62% accommodation requested but only 31% V5. Top-path shares for B and C sum to ~98–99%, so this is either under-provision of required support or a warning that “path summaries” omit activities. The answer never flags it, and the Declare/skeleton plan checks fraud response rather than “need ⇒ support activity.” That is a material omission on a dimension the prompt required.

**Approval versus time residuals are bundled.** Language support can explain extra time; it should not, by itself, explain a −5.5pp approval gap. Appeal (+4.4pp B, +5.7pp D) and approval residuals are more informative for decision quality than cycle-time residuals, which are partly mechanical. The bottom line lists them as one “persistent adjusted burden” package.
