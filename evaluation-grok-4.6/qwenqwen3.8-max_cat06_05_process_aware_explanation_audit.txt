**8.3**

The final audit is substantively strong: it covers every required item, keeps legitimate `t0` signals, and makes the hard process distinctions the rubric cares about. It is not near-flawless.

**What is solid.** Section 1 hits all 12 global features, all five sequence patterns, and the local-only fields (`claimant_channel`, `final_decision`, `initial_reserve`, `risk_score_current`, `last_resource_role`). Obvious leakage (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`) is treated as critical. Subtler lineage bugs are caught: extraction-date SQL on prior claims, nightly same-day document snapshot, closure-time `final_handler_team` without valid time, untimestamped `risk_score_current`, last-role-over-complete-case, and employee experience as-of extraction. `queue_length_at_t0` and `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` are correctly preserved. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated as process-legitimate but post-`t0`, unlike the same-timestamp, normatively impossible `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` pattern. Predictive vs causal vs actionable vs fairness standards are kept separate; correlated attributes are not bulk-deleted. Case X/Y statuses as `recompute` and Case Z as invalid pending repair are the right calls. The JSON has the required keys and does not replay leaked outcomes as drivers.

**What keeps it off a high score.** Remaining SHAP values on non-leaked features are treated too much like they can be kept after “removing” leaky terms. A model trained on duration, appeal, full variant, and final decision has contaminated attributions; that point is only implied by later retrain language, not stated as a finding. Proxy features are put in `credible_drivers` while still “pending fairness review,” which blurs the paper’s own taxonomy. `calendar_week` is known at `t0`; listing it among Case Z’s *ambiguities* conflates missing timestamps with drift/proxy interpretation. Some complete-trace items (`num_document_requests_total`, the appeal pattern) are only “high” even though they are inadmissible at this prediction time. `claimant_channel` is assumed registration-time without lineage. Case Z’s status string is not the specified `retain / recompute / invalidate` enum. Case Y’s narrative still explains the old 0.71 via the leaked variant, which is meta-leakage even if the variant string is omitted.

These are real but not structural failures. The audit would be usable; it is not tight enough for 9+.
