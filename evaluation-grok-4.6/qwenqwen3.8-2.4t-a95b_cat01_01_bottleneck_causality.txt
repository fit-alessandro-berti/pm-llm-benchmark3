The final write-up is strong on the things the rubric cares about most: it catches the crude-versus-stratified reversal, does not treat stratification as identification, separates queueing from service, treats `resource_group` as a routing bundle, and refuses the 50% reassignment. It is not close to flawless.

**What is solid.** The verdict `mainly confounded` (62) is aimed at the right estimand—the crude Orion penalty—not at a claim that no Orion mechanism exists. Within-stratum means, the pooled-weight standardized contrast (−2.51h), and the wait/service split are mostly auditable and point the same way. Positivity is handled honestly (High–Nova n=2, Low–Orion n=2, empty Medium/Day/Orion). The DAG-plus-table apparatus covers confounder, mediator, collider/selection, and proxy. The six hypotheses include the five required types, and each test has a predicted observation. The three-phase plan (instrument → night/compliance capacity → small RCT on an overlap subset) is safer and more informative than moving half of Orion’s work.

**Causal-graph errors.** `quality_outcome → case_completed` is temporally impossible: the 30-day appeal is post-close and cannot select the completed-case sample. The prose also does not match the graph: it says complexity, load, shift, and compliance point *to* `resource_group`, but those arrows go to `routing_rule` only. The table allows `compliance_required` as a mediator “if routing affects check flow,” which is the opposite of `compliance_required → routing_rule`. `resource_group → unobserved_reviewer_skill` treats skill as a consequence of the label; that is a coherent exposure story, but it is then also called a proxy/confounder-like quantity, so the role is muddled.

**Inaccuracies and overclaim.** Medium Orion compliance-required mean is **16.93h**, not 16.87h. The claim that night is “not clearly slower” within medium/high leans on High–Day n=1 and should have been labeled unsupported, not used as contradictory evidence. The executive presents the −2.5h standardized gap with more precision than those cells deserve, even though later text walks it back. Rollback rules say “exceeds threshold” without a threshold.

**Section 7.** The script is complete and diagnostic, with stated weights, a full overlap grid, variant counts, and n<3 warnings. The PM4Py path is still shaky: `from pm4py import DFG_VARIANT` is not the usual public API, and feeding hand-built XES into `parse_event_log_string` may not be what that helper expects. The broad `except` then silently falls back to a manual DFG, so the required PM4Py comparison may never run.

Format, word-band, and “do not invent facts” discipline are otherwise met. The analysis would be in the high 8s if the DAG, the graph/prose consistency, the 16.87 slip, and the PM4Py call were clean. Under a strict reading they are not.
