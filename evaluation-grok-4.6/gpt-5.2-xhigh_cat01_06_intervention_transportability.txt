## Material defects

**1. Section 8 would very likely crash.** `Sequence` is imported from `pm4py.objects.powl.obj`. Public PM4Py exposes `Transition`, `SilentTransition`, `StrictPartialOrder`, and `OperatorPOWL`; sequences are `OperatorPOWL(Operator.SEQUENCE, …)` or a linear `StrictPartialOrder`. That import sits at the top, so the whole script dies before Site A trees are built. A second likely runtime error is `sound, diagnostics = pm4py.check_soundness(...)` — the high-level helper returns a `bool`, not a pair. The *intended* POWL (Identity ∥ Document, Assessment after both, XOR legal path, LOOP on supplement) is the right shape, so this is implementation infidelity rather than conceptual failure, but the task explicitly scored model construction against the PM4Py constructors.

**2. Optimistic scenario arithmetic does not add up.** Eligible share is “~100% attempted,” yet “eligible” median change is −6 to −10h and “overall” is −3 to −6h. Those two columns cannot differ if eligibility is universal unless “eligible” silently means the 38% digital stratum — which is not what the cell says. The prose also stacks “mechanical savings ≤ ~3.4h identity service” with A’s −6h wait change, which double-counts: wait-before-assessment already includes time until both checks finish. For originals cases, Identity is *already* startable after Register in Site B’s rules and DFG (`Register → Identity Check` 2,050; `Register → Originals Receipt` 1,860). Incremental effect there is mostly trigger-start and intake, not a second copy of A’s wait reduction. Confidence 30 does not repair a malformed range.

**3. Pilot power contradicts pilot duration.** Stage 1 is 6–8 weeks on a ~20–25% stratum of 3,400/6 months ≈ 567 cases/month, randomized 50/50, with a rule of ≥400 treated cases before declaring an effect. That stratum yields on the order of 60–70 treated cases per month, i.e. five to seven months, not six to eight weeks. The “extend if support is low / treat as feasibility only” clause acknowledges the tension but leaves the specified design incoherent. Causal forests on that sample are ornament.

**4. Site A throughput is misread.** Completions 4,800 → 5,200 with FTE 18 → 20 is *lower* output per productive FTE (267 → 260), during a shift toward simpler digital claims. The transfer table still treats volume as a positive, merely confounded, signal (confidence 30). After staffing, it is weakly negative. That is exactly the kind of co-intervention arithmetic the section exists to do.

## Smaller but real issues

- **S-node semantics are mixed.** `S_queue_huddles` and `S_API_latency` are co-interventions in A’s treatment package, not baseline selection variables in Pearl’s sense. The prose gets this right; the graph does not. `legal_capacity → rework` is claimed in the write-up and absent from the adjacency list.
- **DFG frequencies are almost unused.** Supplement probability is sitting in the data (`Legal Review → Request Supplement` 360/1,360 ≈ 26.5%) and never enters the legal/rework scenario. Identity already concurrent with originals is likewise in the DFG and underweighted in the optimistic case.
- **“Strictly sequential” at A before is false in the log** (8% overlapping intervals). The tree is sequential; execution was not.
- **Section 9’s “practical start condition”** undersells a statutory gate that Section 6 correctly treats as mandatory.
- **Identity-capacity row in Section 4** describes “starting identity earlier” as the parallelization effect; at B that start is already legal. The new start is Document Check on digital cases.
- **Eligible-share column in Section 5** changes meaning across rows (share treated vs share driving the scenario).
- **Independence of originals (62%) and legal (40%)** is used to get 20–25% Stage-1 volume and never flagged as an assumption (high-value/public-sector cases may be more likely to require originals).
- **Code model omits Document Intake/Triage**, the one new activity the intervention text relies on, while comments correctly say resources and data guards cannot be encoded.
