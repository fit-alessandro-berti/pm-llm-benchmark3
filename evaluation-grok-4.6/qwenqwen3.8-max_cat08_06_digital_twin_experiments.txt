**6.8**

The write-up is complete, well structured, and mostly faithful to the required digital-twin brief. It is not close to flawless under a strict reading.

**What is solid.** The eight required sections are present and used for their intended purpose. The twin is not an independent-case replay: non-stationary demand, resource calendars, rework, applicant delays, and cross-case dependence (architect bursts, district objection shocks, inspection batching, shared pools) are first-class. Service and waiting are not collapsed; missing start times and unobserved objections are treated as inference/proxy problems, not as observed events. The experiment matrix covers baseline, D1–D10, combined packages, and more than three stress overlays, with trade-offs. Uncertainty treatment correctly rejects a stationary warm-up. The skeleton loads the excerpt with pandas, calls `pm4py.format_dataframe` with the specified keys, separates PM4Py diagnostics from the engine, and stubs arrivals, routing, calendars, dependencies, and experiments.

**Material defects.**

1. **Demand intensity is internally inconsistent.** `base_cases_per_day = 58` is the *overall* 18-month average (~31,200 / ~547 calendar days). Multiplying that by Jan–Feb-referenced seasonal factors (1.7) *and* Wednesday-referenced weekday factors (Monday 2.2) without renormalizing overstates arrivals—peak Mondays become ~217/day. λ₀ should be a reference-cell rate, or the multipliers should be mean-one. As written, every experiment would be quantitatively wrong.

2. **PM4Py API misuse, against an explicit constraint.** `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(log, variant="performance")` is not the high-level signature (`business_hours`, calendar args). `variant=` belongs to the low-level DFG algorithm. `pm4py.get_variants` is treated as variant→count; in current PM4Py it is variant→list of cases, so the demo print is wrong. Fallback imports (`process_tree.utils.parse`, `process_tree.parser.parse`) are version-fiction; try/except hides that rather than documenting the real entry point (`utils.generic.parse` / `pm4py.parse_process_tree`).

3. **Decision criteria fight the empirical summary.** Proposed medians (New residential 35d, Commercial 50d, Complex 90d) sit *below* observed medians (48d / 68d / 140d). Baseline SLA would be near zero for those types, so “+3–5 pp SLA” and “do not worsen another type by >2 pp” are not operational until targets are anchored to the log or labeled as stretch goals.

4. **Quantile/uncertainty design is under-specified.** Thirty to 100 replications is thin for p95/p99 of the stated heavy tails (objections, complex variance, missing-doc response). There is no sequential sampling, no variance model for quantile CIs beyond a generic bootstrap mention, and no plan for how CRN streams are actually split in code.

5. **Estimation is not tied to the experiment knobs.** D1’s 30/50/70% missing-doc cuts, D3 travel savings, D8 low-risk share, etc. are free parameters, not quantities derived from the 18-month log, variants, or performance DFG. Weekday factors other than Monday/Wednesday are invented (Fri 0.9, weekend 0.1).

6. **Validation wording is sloppy.** Token-based replay is a log-vs-model check; using it as sim-vs-log fitness conflates conformance with predictive validation. KS-on-large-N is waved at but not replaced with a pre-specified practical-significance rule (quantile error, CRPS thresholds).

7. **Skeleton overclaims.** `ExperimentRunner` “collects results” from `SimulationEngine.run`, which only raises `NotImplementedError`. `DemandScenario` bakes in the broken intensity model. Resource calendars are generic fields; Monday portal-clerk diversion, fire Tue–Thu, inspector Tue–Fri, and 40% environmental non-permit duty never become executable rules. That is acceptable for a stub only if it is not presented as an experiment runner.

**Lesser issues.** SLA/utilization cutoffs (85–90%, 5–10% fairness) are generic. “Completeness rework” as a loop redo label is acknowledged but never mapped to observed activity names. Decision-drafting join semantics are described well in prose and absent from the tree/code. Rare-variant (7%) and unstructured rework text are parked rather than given a concrete residual process.

The design would be a good first draft for a municipality workshop. It would not survive implementation or a methods review without fixing demand calibration, PM4Py calls, and SLA anchors. That caps the score well below the “nearly flawless” band.
