**3.8**

The answer has the right skeleton and some of the right vocabulary, but it fails the central empirical test of this prompt and then builds a causal story on that failure. That is not a cosmetic defect.

**The decisive error.** Orion is *faster* in every complexity stratum (Low 7.50 vs 9.64, Medium 17.17 vs 19.38, High 31.57 vs 35.00). Section 1 states the opposite for Medium — “Orion’s Medium cases are also slower (17.17h vs. 19.38h)” — and uses that inversion as the main reason to treat the effect as “partly causal through mechanisms.” Section 4 then correctly says Orion is faster in Medium. A reader cannot tell which claim the author believes. Missing a uniform Simpson reversal, then citing the reversed comparison as evidence of a residual group effect, is a core failure of causal discipline, not a wording slip.

**Verdict is therefore unearned.** `partly causal through mechanisms` at 65% is not defensible from the supplied tables. After mix adjustment the descriptive residual points the other way, and night/load/compliance are almost perfectly entangled with the Orion label. `mainly confounded` or `not identifiable from the supplied evidence` would have been serious options. The chosen label is never defined in the required terms: there is no explicit split of a *total* effect of assignment, a *direct* effect holding shift/compliance/rework/queue fixed, and *mediated* paths through those mechanisms. That distinction was mandatory.

**Arithmetic in §4 is mostly unauditable.** Copied prompt aggregates are fine. Almost every number the author computed is wrong: Orion wait is ~12.8h not 13.1; Nova wait ~8.8h not 7.8; review service ~7.0 vs ~5.0, not 7.8 vs 6.1; night vs day cycle ~21.7 vs ~14.0, not 22.8 vs 15.6; compliance-required vs not ~26.8 vs ~12.0, not 28.8 vs 13.2; Orion appeal rate is 1/14 = 7.1%, not 14.3%. “All Orion cases have High/Night” is false (C02 is Day/Normal). Hypothesis 3’s “contradicting” claim that Nova-night waits are *lower* than Orion-day wait is also false (Orion day wait is 3.6; every Nova-night wait is higher). Approximate calculation was allowed; inventing a trail of incorrect means was not.

**Graph and roles are incomplete and partly misspecified.** Supported process facts are mixed with convenient arrows. `resource_group → shift` and `shift → arrival_load` treat a joint operating bundle as a clean chain. There is no `resource_group → review_service` / `rework` / `queue_wait`, so the graph cannot represent a direct group effect or a capacity effect. `quality_outcome` is absent. `case_completed` is in the DAG but not in the role table, so the required collider/selection discussion is only a gesture. `compliance_required` is labeled a mediator of *group* when the data show it tracks complexity/policy, not Orion vs Nova within stratum. Conditioning on `queue_wait` is misdescribed as an `arrival_load` collider problem. Standardization (Nova-weighted Orion mean ≈ 12.9h vs Nova 15.2h) is computed in code and never used in the argument.

**Hypotheses and tests are thinner than they look.** The five required families are nominally present, which is the strongest part of the memo. Several tests are still sloppy: front-door via `resource_group → compliance_required` is not identified from these facts; H3’s predicted contrast is missing an operator and compares the wrong cells; H5 never states a real truncation check or a design that recovers incomplete cases. Predicted observations exist in form more than in operational detail.

**Intervention plan is directionally safer, then contradicts itself.** Rejecting a 50% dump onto Nova is correct. Phase 1 (small randomized reroute of Orion-bound Low/Medium) and a non-label intervention on queue/compliance capacity meet the spec. Then the recommendation says: exclude High from the pilot, and “if the pilot shows Orion’s advantage for High cases…” That is incoherent. Rollback rules are local and do not protect quality or High-complexity overflow.

**§7 does not do the process-mining job.** Crude/stratified means and a stated Nova-weighted standardization are fine. Overlap counts are fine. Warnings fire only on the overlap table, not on every thin comparison. Trace *variants* are never compared. The DFG helper does not call `pm4py.parse_event_log_string`, collapses each trace into a single fake activity (and drops `Close`), likely omits a timestamp that `format_dataframe` needs, remaps case ids, and prints two uncompared objects. It would not recover group-specific directly-follows relations. The prompt gave a valid approach; this code ignores it.

**Format and length.** Headings and table schemas match. Section 1 is under the 180-word floor (~150) and the final recommendation is under 120 words. Neither section does the conceptual work those word budgets were there to force.

**What remains salvageable:** crude-vs-High reversal is noticed; small cells are flagged; group is briefly treated as a routing-policy proxy/bundle; completed-case selection is at least drawn; the learning plan is less reckless than management’s proposal; residual-uncertainty items are reasonable. That is a mid-structured draft with a broken empirical spine. Under the instructed standard it cannot score much above the high 3s.
