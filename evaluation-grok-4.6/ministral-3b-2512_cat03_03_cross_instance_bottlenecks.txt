**Correct.** J1 ready 08:10, B1 at 12:05 → 3 h 55 min. J2 ready 08:25 → 3 h 40 min. J1 packed 13:30, S1 dispatch 19:25 → 5 h 55 min elapsed.

The answer writes “J4 acquires FIX-A at 09:00, releases at 11:40.” That single swap destroys the contention story. The 15 min from 11:40 to 11:45 is start lag after release, not the tool wait.
