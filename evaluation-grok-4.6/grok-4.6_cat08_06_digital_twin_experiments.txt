7.4

The answer is a strong, constraint-compliant digital-twin design: it treats the process tree as a control-flow skeleton, keeps PM4Py out of the simulator, refuses to invent start/complete splits, treats objections as latent, and makes calendars and cross-case coupling first-class. It is not nearly flawless. Several numerical, identification, and specification gaps are material under a strict reading.

**What is actually good.** Scope and state are the right grain: cases, architects, districts, bundles, latent objection clusters, and policy switches. Arrivals are non-homogeneous and architect-clustered rather than i.i.d. Service is working time; sojourn is the observed quantity; wait/service is only split when starts exist. Routing is attribute-conditional. D9 is not allowed to pretend the log contains objection events. The experiment matrix has a real baseline, one-way D1–D9, a non-additive D10, extra packages, and identification stress (S4), not just demand shocks. Warm-up-from-snapshot, CRN on differences, heavy-tail reporting (median/p90/p95, not the max), and a staged validation ladder are the correct statistical posture. The code path is faithful to the stated PM4Py conventions: `pandas` load, `format_dataframe`, `parse_process_tree`, variants/DFG/performance DFG/arrival average, and an honest sojourn column.

**Process-mining and calibration errors.** The skeleton’s missing-document probabilities are inconsistent with the variant table. “Simple with missing docs” is 19% of all cases and simple renovation is 45%, so P(missing docs | simple) is about 42%, not 0.28. That is not a rounding issue; it would mis-size D1. Architect bursts are specified as Hawkes/cluster processes, but the log is flattened and the estimation plan never says how same-week multi-submit structure is recovered from case-level traces. Fire/environment extra probability for unobserved area/sensitivity is left as “latent threshold or extra probability” with no identification target. `get_case_arrival_average` is computed and then ignored for generation—correct scientifically, but the skeleton never shows how the NHPP is fitted to first-event timestamps by type/weekday/month.

**Simulation-specification holes.** Queue discipline is unspecified except for D6, so the aging policy cannot be implemented unambiguously (FCFS vs priority vs reserved token). Inspection scheduling exists in the log (`scheduler_1`, 3d median wait) and is never a resource; only field inspectors appear. Monday portal-clerk reservation and the two simple-only planners are in the prose and absent from `ResourceCalendar`/`ResourceManager`. Environmental `reserved_fraction` is stored and unused. Public notice is both an unlimited system clock and subject to holiday/council interruption; those two statements are not reconciled. Warm-up of 90 days is shorter than Complex p90 (~260d). Snapshot initialization mitigates this; it does not remove the need to say how residual age bias is checked for the long-tail types that drive D5/D6/D9. No event list (arrival, calendar open/close, start/end service, batch release, latent cluster) is given, so the DES remains a set of dataclasses plus `NotImplementedError`.

**Experiment and uncertainty overclaim.** Factor levels in the table and hardcoded policy effects in code disagree (D1 remaining missing-doc rate 0.4 is not in {−40%, −70%}; D7 Monday multiplier 1.15 is not a fitted compliance parameter). `run_experiments` drops C2 and builds seeds as `hash(exp.exp_id)`, which is not stable across Python processes, undermining the CRN story the text sells. R = 30 is thin for p90 of a 260-day tail; the text never shows a precision calculation. Sobol on eight weakly identified parameters is named, not designed (sample size, inner/outer loop, what ranking-flip means operationally). Optional “credible intervals” are mentioned without a likelihood or prior. SLA cutoffs (30/90/120/180) and the +5/−3 pp / +10% fairness rules are invented; labeling them as proposals is honest, but they are still ungrounded in the 18-month summary.

**Validation and decision rule.** Face/replay/distributional/utilization/bottleneck/OOS is the right checklist. Missing are numeric acceptance bands (e.g., holdout Wasserstein or p90 relative error that blocks ranking). Replay-as-diagnostic is correct; “alignments will fail on appeal” is correct; there is still no plan for what fitness/precision gap is tolerable before the tree is abandoned as a routing prior. Transfer validation for D1/D2/D7/D9 is mostly “ask staff,” which is appropriate and also means those cells are exploratory—this should have been a hard label in the decision table, not only a caveat for D9.

**Code quality vs the brief.** Modular and directionally right; not a coherent executable experiment layer. Parallel AND-join state is only `pending_results`. Legal probability, appeal, planning/legal rework, and no-shows are discussed and not represented. Calendars are hour-granular and ignore holidays as data. That would be acceptable if the text and skeleton agreed; they do not.

Net: expert-level structure and the right scientific refusals (no replay twin, no fake objections, no collapsed wait/service, no optimality). Under a hypercritical bar, the variant-table inconsistency, unreproducible seeds, missing scheduler/calendar mechanisms, warmup/tail mismatch, and text–code drift keep it well below near-flawless.
