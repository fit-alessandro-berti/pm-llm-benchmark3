**6.3**

The final write-up follows the required skeleton and is clearly above a generic ops-essay: it names the fast-path contradiction, cites multiple mining artifacts, keeps a fast lane for some low-risk work, and states sacrifices. It is not nearly flawless. Several evidence-use and design errors are material.

**Diagnosis (strongest section, still not clean).**  
The core contradiction is stated well: Auto→Fast approval (2,550) is frequent and locally cheap/fast, yet Fast-approval cases have a 14.1% complaint rate versus 3.3% on the quote→approval→repair path, and the 31% pre-rework cost edge shrinks to 9% after complaints, reships, and credits. Fitness 0.91 / 68% fitting traces, the three named alignment deviations, R1/R2, segment medians (8h vs 46h; 20h/22% vs 96h/5%; unclear-defect 140h), 26% repeat evidence requests, reviewer utilization >82%, and EUR 95/410 wrong-replacement costs are all used. That meets the “five artifacts” bar.

It also misreads the DFG. “Request missing evidence is entered by 890+650 cases” confuses an *outflow* (Request missing evidence → Manual eligibility review: 890) with inflows (Auto 650 + Manual 780 = 1,430; plus 220 from that activity into fraud). R3 is almost absent from the *final* diagnosis despite 120 complaint→Escalation board cases and 10 escalation denials. Spare QC capacity, 12.5% vs 4% rework-repair, Fast approval→Close 350 (no dispatch), and model precision are unused. END counts (3,015+220+390=3,625 of 5,000) are never interrogated.

**Redesigns (philosophically different, mechanistically leaky).**  
A (segmented triage + gate + portal), B (capacity + loop-kill + hard blocks), and C (promise/CX + parallel risk + concierge) are not mere parameter tweaks. Trade-offs are explicit. But A is largely a bundle of the *prompt’s own* three levers (rule update, portal, 6–18h gate), not a distinctive mined redesign. B then “shares the gate function with Redesign A,” so the trio is not a clean set of independent alternatives.

B’s headline cycle-time claim is a process-mining error. *Repair quote → Customer approval* (median 21h) is time-to-customer-acceptance. Part-price wait belongs in the quote *creation* edges (Manual/Fraud → Repair quote, 15–16h median). Cutting that edge to “4h” with a price DB is the wrong activity. The “96h → 50–60h” reviewed-path estimate is therefore unsupported; customer think-time is still in the path. Incomplete-evidence approval is also left in tension with R2 for high-value devices.

C overgeneralizes the **Premium low-risk accessory** 24h credit trigger into a **all-Premium** guarantee. On the given data, Premium high-value reviewed work sits at 96h median; a 24h credit rule either forces illegal/fast high-value paths or pays credits on almost every compliant case. A 2-FTE concierge for unclear-defect (22%) plus Premium high-value (14%) is unsized against any volume/time window. “AI risk scoring” is only loosely grounded; the existing auto-screen is the artifact that should have been redesigned first.

**Table (constraint breach).**  
All required columns appear, and “dominated?” is used cautiously. The instruction was qualitative signs/ranges and no fake precision. Cells such as “+10% / –20%,” “–25%,” “–15% / –5%,” and “factor 1.5” savings are invented. That is a direct violation, not a rounding issue.

**Failure modes and experiments (good, then stacked).**  
Two failures plus one migrated bottleneck per design are specific and use the prompt (gate vs 24h credits, 15–35% manual inflow, portal abandonment, ignored confirmation). The first pilot (gate on high-value only) matches the EUR 410 / 22% complaint evidence. Displacement checks (total cost of claim, complaint→manual and escalation edges) are the right learning logic.

The sequence then *stacks* A→portal→B→C instead of discriminating among three competing designs—the Pareto table’s point. Six weeks / 30% sample is arbitrary because case throughput over time is unknown. Stop/go thresholds are usable but sit on the same unsupported percentages.

**Net.** Competent sponsor-facing structure and real use of conflicting artifacts, undermined by DFG misreads, a segment-illegal Premium SLA, prompt-lever recycling, non-independent designs, and forbidden numeric theater. Too many concrete faults for anything in the 8–10 band under a strict bar.
