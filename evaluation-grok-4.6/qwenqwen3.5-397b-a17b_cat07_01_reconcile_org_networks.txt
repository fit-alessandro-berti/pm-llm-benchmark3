**5.4**

The write-up is complete, on-format, multi-hypothesis, and appropriately refuses to fire Mira. That is not enough. The central question is a metric-semantics problem, and the answer gets the main metric wrong.

**Process-mining validity.** Working-together is defined as simultaneous time overlap. In PM4Py / standard SNA it is case co-occurrence (here: “percentage of cases in which the pair co-occurs”). That error is fatal to the required reconciliation. High handover/betweenness plus low WT is the classic star-vs-clique pattern: Mira fans out to many targets, so no pair is dense, while Alice–Carla and Bruno–Diego form teams. The “she hands off and does not overlap in time” story is operationally plausible and still the wrong explanation of the given WT table. Section 4 then proposes a “time-overlap threshold” on `discover_working_together_network`, which that function does not compute.

**Factual mistakes.** Mira’s exception share (0.48) is called the highest among humans; Omar is 0.62 and Priya 0.71. The executive summary then says she “handles 48% exception cases,” which collapses her *mix* with her *coverage*. The strong coverage numbers—22% of claims, 64% of reopens, 71% of complaints—are underused. Hypothesis 1 predicts Mira in “90%+” of exception variants with no denominator that supports it.

**Ignored structure.** Automated and vendor resources are a stated constraint. Bot_Rules is the real volume router (~4040 ingest edges; ~1200 to team leads vs ~920 to Mira). Vendor_Pay is the approval/payment sink for Alice/Bruno; Mira is absent from that path, so she is not a shadow team lead and payment SoD is actually informative. Vendor_Verify’s long service time and the Temp_1 loop are unused. 960 events / 905 cases (~1.06) sits awkwardly next to large A–B–A counts and is never flagged.

**Thin analysis.** Only the three mandatory hypotheses; the suggested data-artifact, queue-cleaner, and gatekeeper readings are dropped, and the stakeholder quotes barely appear. Role-discovery is asserted as if clustered. Risks are listed but fairness is reduced to burnout, not routing into North/South or junior/senior benches. The absence window is the best identification evidence in the file; Section 4 never proposes a time-range contrast around those six days.

**What still works.** Headings match; each hypothesis has mechanism / support / limit / prediction; centrality is not treated as automatically good; removal is not recommended; reason-codes plus backup monitoring is a defensible management split; length of the summary is in range. Competent consulting structure, incorrect core SNA reading, and several number errors. Not near-flawless.
