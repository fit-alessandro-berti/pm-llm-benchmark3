**4.4**

The memo is directionally right to reject a naïve full-site copy and to condition on digital eligibility, but it fails several hard specification requirements, invents facts, and mishandles the partial-order structure that the prompt treats as the core of transport. Under a strict rubric this is not a high-quality answer.

**Process structure.** The comparison table is the strongest section, but it fabricates a Site A legal-review rate of 10%. Site A’s brief states that high-value cases do **not** require separate legal review. “Loops” is a required feature and is missing as a structural row, even though Site B’s `Legal Review → Request Supplement → Document Check/Assessment` loop is the main non-transportable control-flow object. The largest analogical miss is elsewhere but already implicit here: Site B already permits `Identity Check ∥ Originals Receipt` after `Register`. Parallelizing only digital `Identity ∥ Document` leaves the majority path’s feasible concurrency unused.

**Transport graph.** The adjacency list is not a transportability graph. Required nodes `digital_documents`, `originals_required`, and `API_latency` are absent as causal variables or are sinks. `true_overlap` has in-edges and no out-edge, so the mediator never reaches `waiting_before_assessment` or `cycle_time`. Selection nodes are incomplete (`S_role_specialization`, `S_IT_case_lock`, `S_legal_capacity`, `S_case_complexity` are missing). Co-interventions (`queue_huddles`, `API_latency`, staffing) are not drawn as confounders of Site A’s observed Δ. The prose is below the 180-word floor and asserts shared benefit rather than identifying a shared mechanism under stated constraints.

**Evidence transfer.** The prompt forbids treating the 9-hour median drop as an identified treatment effect. The table still interprets it as “parallel verification reduces waiting.” Confounders are listed as non-transferable add-ons, which is not the same as identification. Several major before/after series are omitted (completions, wait-before-assessment, identity/document service times, appeals). Overlap 8%→71% is given 70 confidence despite the prompt’s warning that timestamps improved. Complexity mix and +2 FTE are Site A co-interventions, not weakly transferable “components.” Confidence numbers are systematically high relative to an uncontrolled two-week rollout.

**Modifiers.** Coverage of the ten required rows is adequate, but several signs are wrong. Role specialization can *enable* true overlap if two pools work at once; Site A’s cross-training can *prevent* it if one person serializes both checks. Originals-required cases are given “beneficial signature: none,” which ignores legal identity/originals concurrency. Legal-review cases can still gain on the pre-assessment critical path. Digital share is taken as 1−0.62 = 38% with no caveat that “originals not legally required” ≠ Site A’s “90% digital at registration.”

**Scenarios.** This section fails the spec. There is no scenario in which the median improves and the p90 worsens; bottleneck and legal/rework rows still improve both. There is no mapping from Site A’s confounded deltas and Site B’s service times (3.4h, 8.1h, 18h originals wait) to the quoted ranges. Perfect overlap of identity and document saves about `min(3.4, 8.1) ≈ 3.4h` of service, plus some queueing; 8–12h median cuts for digital cases treat Site A’s 9h as nearly portable. The legal/rework row uses “10% of legal cases get supplements”; the DFG is 360/1,360 ≈ 26.5%. Optimistic vs digital-only effects are ordered backwards. Sign characters are stripped, so several cells are unreadable.

**Intervention.** Segmenting to digital is a reasonable rejection of management’s proposal, but the design is too conservative and internally incomplete. It does not create `Identity Check ∥ Originals Receipt` for the 62% statutory path. Assessment is written as depending only on document check. Supplement loops omit the required re-assessment. Huddles are folded into the treatment, confounding later learning. The POWL sketch is not a well-formed partial order and does not match the eligibility rules.

**Pilot.** The table hits many requested elements, then contradicts itself: strata are “digital only” while randomization is stratified on digital/physical, and the write-up invokes a physical control arm that eligibility excluded. Case-level randomization on shared identity, document, assessment, and legal queues is interference-dominated; a 20% volume cap does not identify a case-level effect. Assessor masking is implausible. Stop rules (“rework >5%”) are undefined against a 17% baseline. Huddles remain in the bundle, so mechanism and co-intervention stay entangled. The mechanism-vs-implementation paragraph is short and does not use overlap, lock-workaround, and queue-length fidelity metrics as the discriminator.

**PM4Py.** This is a specification failure, not a style issue. The prompt requires `pm4py.parse_process_tree(...)` on the two given trees. The code never calls it. `ProcessTree(operator="")` and ad-hoc child rewiring do not encode `->` or `+`. The POWL API (`POWL`, `POWLNode`, `OrderingRelation`, `POWLLoop`, `POWLXor`) is invented; PM4Py uses `Transition`, `StrictPartialOrder`, `OperatorPOWL`, and `order.add_edge`. The Site B net *forces* `Identity → Document`, i.e. the opposite of the recommended intervention, and the XOR/loop wiring does not implement optional legal review plus supplement-back-to-document-and-assessment. Comments on unencodable resources and data guards are the only solid part.

**Recommendation and assumptions.** `conditional go` is the right class, but expected rework reduction contradicts Site A (rework 11.8%→12.4%, appeals up). Expansion trigger “rework 15%” is incoherent with Site B’s 17% baseline. Assumptions 3 and 8 encode the same role-specialization and huddle confusions. The register never states the central identifying assumption: that Site A’s Δ is not mostly mix, FTE, API, huddles, and timestamp artifact.

**Net.** Competent outline, wrong legal fact, broken transport graph, unidentified 9-hour effect, unused DFG, failed tail scenario, missed `Identity ∥ Originals`, and non-executable required models. That is well below a near-flawless transport memo.
