**6.4**

The answer’s causal core is competent: it refuses a blanket copy of Site A, treats the 9-hour median drop as unidentified, names the co-interventions, and redesigns around statutory gates. That is not enough for a high score. Under a strict reading of the rubric, model fidelity, quantitative mapping, and pilot identifiability have material failures.

**What holds.** Section 1 covers the required features and correctly ties concurrency to the originals gate, role silos, and the case lock. Section 3 is the strongest part: every headline Site A metric is decomposed, and the API upgrade, added FTEs, mix shift, huddles, and timestamp artifact are not laundered into a treatment effect. Sections 4, 6, 9, and 10 are directionally right. `conditional go`, digital/non-legal first entry, WIP caps, logging discipline, and explicit expand/modify/abandon triggers match the process structure. The bottleneck-shift scenario does produce the required median-up / tail-down pattern.

**Process and transport gaps.** The adjacency list includes the mandated nodes, but several edges are conceptually wrong. `digital_documents -> originals_required` is not a causal mechanism; the two are complementary selection variables. `true_overlap -> rework` is asserted without support (Site A rework barely moved and is more plausibly mix- or congestion-driven). Site-selection nodes are incomplete relative to the capacity story the rest of the memo tells. The 180–280-word commentary is thin: shared mechanism is reduced to “wait before assessment,” and the DFG frequencies (1,160 Identity→Document, 2,108 Originals→Document, 360 supplements) are almost unused as quantitative constraints. A similar control-flow diagram is said not to guarantee transportability, but the graph never encodes that `treatment_parallelization` is blocked by `originals_required` and `IT_case_lock` rather than merely moderated after the fact.

**Quantitative and design errors.** Eligible share is unstable: 38% digital, then ~25%, then 20–25%, then “25% digital, 15% non-legal digital.” Dependence between the 62% originals rule and the 40% legal rule is never stated, so the feasible set could be near 0% or near 38%. After correctly rejecting Site A’s 9-hour figure, section 5 still assigns the digital stratum a 5–8 hour median gain without showing the mapping from Site A’s wait decomposition (18→12), service-time changes, and added capacity. Per-scenario reasoning is mostly a single paragraph. The pilot’s “200 cases per stratum × arm” across three binary factors is infeasible: Site B finishes 3,400 cases in six months, so eight weeks cannot populate eight strata × two arms. “Eight weeks per arm” also contradicts case-level randomization, which should run arms concurrently. T2 (no WIP cap) is a good idea that the power and duration design cannot support.

**Section 8 is a hard miss on model fidelity.** The code will not run as PM4Py POWL. `Operator` is not a factory on `pm4py.objects.powl.obj`; XOR/SEQUENCE/PARALLEL are not called that way; the usual objects are `OperatorPOWL` plus `Operator` from the process tree module. `check_soundness(net)` and `play_out(net)` omit markings. A `PARALLEL` node is built and never inserted. `doc_check` is reused inside the XOR and as a top-level POWL node. The supplement “loop” is a one-shot sequence, not a `LOOP`. `Decision` sits downstream of an XOR that already contains Approve/Reject, adding a spurious activity. Most damaging: `orig_recept -> doc_check` is unconditional, so the net encodes current Site B (document work waits on originals), not the candidate intervention that parallelizes identity and document check on digital cases. Comments admit data/resources/locks cannot be encoded, but that does not excuse building the wrong control-flow candidate. Sections 6 and 8 therefore disagree on the same process.

**Smaller defects.** Comparison operators and the euro threshold are missing or garbled. ID reviewers are later allowed to pick up document work, quietly reintroducing the cross-training Site B does not have. Negative-control and huddle rows in the transfer table are incomplete. These would be minor in isolation; on top of the code, power, and share inconsistencies they confirm the memo is not tight enough for a high band.

The recommendation is usable as strategy and weak as a specified, identified, and executable transport design. That is a mid-6, not an 8.
