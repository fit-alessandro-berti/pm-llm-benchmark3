**6.4**

The fairness write-up is structured correctly and avoids the main forbidden errors (raw-rate = discrimination; case-mix excuses everything; risk scores treated as neutral; support steps ignored or deleted). Sections 1–6 are substantively competent. The answer is not close to flawless.

**What works.** All eight required headings are present. The dimensional analysis uses the tables rather than slogans: residual gaps after controls (B −5.5 pp / +2.6 d, C −2.2 pp / +4.1 d, D −3.1 pp / +0.9 d), V4/V5 as rights-preserving but slow paths, bilingual/accommodation queues (3.8 d / 4.7 d), handovers, and the 7- and 5-person bottlenecks. The strategy section correctly asks for within-variant contrasts, wait-vs-service decomposition, sensitivity on biased covariates, and region/channel cuts. Mechanisms and mitigations include both legitimate routing and capacity/SLA/handover problems, with trade-offs.

**Material analytical flaws.** The bottom line overreaches the evidence. Residual 2–5 pp approval gaps and support-queue delay are concerning, but they do not license “the process appears procedurally unfair” / “fails to provide equitable procedural fairness.” A calibrated verdict is **indeterminate with substantial evidence of inequitable burden**, especially because risk and documentation are admitted to be contaminated, support routing is policy-required, and D’s cycle-time gap largely disappears after adjustment. Section 8 then lumps D with B/C as facing extra delays, contradicting Section 3’s own +0.9 d finding. The +9.5 pp “Request Missing Documents” residual is treated as likely stricter screening; that is one hypothesis, not a warranted inference. There is no uncertainty language (CIs, residual confounding, mediator vs. confounder). The cleanest contrast—same support need, different group—is missing.

**Section 7 is not reliable, even as “PM4Py-style” pseudocode.** Several calls are wrong, not merely abbreviated: `get_variants` is not a Series and has no `.value_counts()`; `get_all_case_durations` returns a list, so `.median()` fails; `filter_variants` expects a list of variants (tuples), not a flat activity list; `discover_performance_dfg` returns a tuple and is indexed as a dict; `pm4py.constants.VariableConstant` is invented; `filter_event_attribute_values(..., 'Group A')` passes a string as a character collection. `variants_C` / `variants_D` are never defined. `nsmallest` is the opposite of concentration. Listed tools are skipped or unused (`format_dataframe`, frequency DFG, `get_service_time`, `filter_paths_performance`, DECLARE). Log-skeleton is a stub comment, not a constraint check.

**Net.** Stronger than a generic fairness essay, far from a nearly flawless assessment. The process-mining plan would mislead a practitioner, and the headline verdict is stronger than the paper’s own caveats allow.
