7.6

The interval analysis is unusually careful and mostly correct. Clock-offset direction, resolution-before-correction, zone conversion, the T-405 fold filter, inferred T-404/T-407 intervals, and the SLA/profile classifications I recomputed all match. Sensitivity around the tight boundaries (T-402 S1 at +2/+5, T-404/T-407/T-409 S1 at +2, T-403 AI at ζ=3, T-408 IC at ζ=2) is handled honestly. Assumptions are explicit, midpoints are quarantined, and the seven required headings and table schemas are present.

It is not near-flawless.

**Code is not reliably executable.** `duration_union` yields `pd.Timedelta` pairs, then `classify_duration_union` compares them to raw integers/floats (`900`, `mean ± zeta*std`). In current pandas that is a `TypeError` (or, historically, a nanosecond comparison). The required classification path would crash before printing SLA or profile matrices. Midpoint durations correctly use `.total_seconds()`; interval durations do not. `timestamp_utc.dt.tz_localize(None)` on an aware series is also version-fragile (`tz_convert(None)` is the safe removal). For a mandated “one executable Python code block,” this is a real defect, not a nit.

**Interval notation is sloppy in the tables.** HUB intervals are closed; OPS/SENSOR/VENDOR/LEGACY/MOBILE uppers are half-open. Section 3 then mixes `[718.5, 991.5)`, `[2488.5, 2761.5]`, `[-183, 1023]`, and `[1078.5, 2581.5]` without a consistent convention. No classification turns on an endpoint, but the writeup claims more rigor than it delivers.

**T-407 is classified too cleanly as POSSIBLE_VIOLATION.** The prompt flags missing receipt, no ack, and a causal assumption that is “plausible but not directly logged.” Treating `Alert_Sent + [0,20] min` as a testable `Alert_Received` interval is a defensible *secondary* semantics; as the *primary* S1/AI result it underuses INDETERMINATE. The caveat is buried in §6 instead of owning the classification.

**A few smaller issues.** Same-source HUB offset correlation is acknowledged but never quantified in the duration intervals that actually sit on boundaries (T-404 S2’s 3 s, T-408 IC’s 3 s). Severity/confidence labels are invented and only loosely tied to evidence. The PM4Py comparison layer is a large speculative parser; that is allowed, but it does not compensate for the Timedelta crash above. Bracket/ISO formatting in §2 is good; §3’s `d  900`-style acceptance cells look like damaged `≤` glyphs.

The reasoning quality of §§1–4 and §6–7 is high. The required code and a handful of presentation/classification choices keep it well short of 9+.
