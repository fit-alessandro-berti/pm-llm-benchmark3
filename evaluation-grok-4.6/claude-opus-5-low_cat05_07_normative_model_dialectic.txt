**7.2**

The review is unusually well structured and, in §§1–5, often excellent: it separates stipulated facts from authority/scope/identity disputes, sets an explicit burden of proof, argues both sides at genuine strength, and then refuses a bland split. The M0 reading (concurrency inside `checks`, XOR decision, missing loop/alphabet/overlays) is correct, and the residual prosecution case after concessions (703, 708, 705, 714, ineligible/post-expiry auto-approval, 712, 715, CRM trail) is the right residual. That is not enough for a high score under a strict standard, because the piece then makes several material errors in the exact places the rubric weights: model semantics, evidence hygiene, and the proposed revision.

**Material model errors.** M1 is described as the union of historically permitted routes, with acceptance *necessary* but not sufficient. The 7.3 table then asserts that 702, 704, 707, and 710 fit M1. They do not.

- The auto route makes `Master_Data_Validation` mandatory. No observed auto-approval trace contains it, so 702/703/714 fail M1 on the alphabet, not only on D-1/D-2.
- The emergency route makes `Emergency_Provisional_Authorization` and `Daily_Transaction_Monitoring` mandatory logged activities. 704/705 have neither. The brief treats monitoring as contextual evidence, then silently promotes it to a required POWL node.
- `Request_More_Info` is a pre-decision block (`intake → rmi_loop → decision`). SUP-707 is `Sanctions_Check`, then two RMIs, then `Quality_Review`. M1 cannot produce that interleaving. The code comment that the loop “legalizes SUP-707” is false. A concurrent `rmi_loop` inside the check partial order, or an explicit limitation, was the honest approximation; this hides the limitation.
- Reactivation is said to skip `Quality_Review`, but no POWL route does. 710 still cannot execute. Layer 0 and Layer 1 contradict each other.

Notify is correctly made optional-in-log because the event left the system. Authorization, master-data validation, and daily monitoring — which are *also* absent from the log — are not. That inconsistency undercuts the claim that M1 is a careful approximation rather than a TO-BE wishlist.

**Smaller but real POWL issues.** Auto-route quality is forced *after* activate; PD-3 allows post-activation review, it does not forbid a prior one. Daily monitoring is a single transition, not a repeating obligation; that approximation is barely flagged. `M1.get_children()` is a dubious PM4Py API choice in the smoke-print (the object is a `StrictPartialOrder` whose public handle is `.nodes`).

**Evidence and burden-of-proof slips.** Aggregate N5 is “six post-pilot cases” in the first week of August; SUP-714 (2 Aug) is almost certainly one of them. The brief repeatedly writes “SUP-714 **and** the six August cases” / “six plus SUP-714,” inflating the cluster. “Missingness tripled-plus (2% → 29%)” is numerically wrong (≈14×). SUP-711 is a *resolved* ETL duplicate, not `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE`. U2 leaves 712’s alias identity unproved, and the reviewer’s own bar for `NONCOMPLIANT` is clear-and-convincing evidence that is not an identity artefact — yet 712 is still called `NONCOMPLIANT`. SUP-702 is granted `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` while PD-3 is simultaneously `UNRATIFIED_DEROGATION` and while criterion (4) (contemporaneous exception record) is not shown. The operator-vs-institution distinction is coherent, but it bends the documentary floor announced in §1.4 and is the closest the piece comes to splitting the difference on the central authority question.

**What remains strong.** §§2–3 concessions are real, not decorative. 703 and 708 are correctly indefensible on both briefs. 704 is correctly a justified overlay, 705/714 correctly fail on expiry, 706 is correctly indeterminate, 707/709/710/713 are correctly instrument defects rather than misconduct. Invisible SoD/COI cases (712, 715) are used properly to show M0 under-detects. The layered architecture (POWL vs DECLARE vs pandas vs org controls), G-1–G-5, dual timestamps, immutable `notification_id`, and eligibility snapshots are the right design. The Python is self-contained and uses the requested classes.

Those strengths are why this is not a middling answer. They do not cancel false acceptance claims in the required model, an internally inconsistent reactivation/RMI design, or sloppy counts in a review that otherwise advertises forensic precision. Under a hypercritical bar, that is a clear step below “nearly flawless.”
