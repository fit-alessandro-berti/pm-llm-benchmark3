**4.3**

The answer is complete in outline and shows some process-mining and DES literacy, but it is not close to a trustworthy digital-twin design. Under a strict reading of the constraints, several failures are structural rather than cosmetic.

**Constraint violations.** Cross-case dependence is mostly decorative. Related-case lists, inspection batches, and neighborhood effects are declared, then unused; arrivals sample architects independently, so the documented burst structure never appears. The running model is independent cases plus resource contention. Service and waiting are collapsed: completion-only logs are treated as if PM4Py can emit service/waiting columns, then inter-event time is reused as service. Objection events are not observed, yet duration \(>14\)d is treated as a label and D9 assumes a 50% detection rate that cannot be identified from the log. Invented PM4Py calls (`get_branching_factors`, `get_rework_frequency`, `discover_resource_profiles`, `add_service_and_waiting_times`, `get_arrival_rate` on sojourn-time, DFG objects with `.edges`) violate the “plausible PM4Py conventions” requirement.

**Simulation methodology.** Seasonal, calendar-driven demand is not a steady-state system. A 6-month Welch warm-up plus “queue lengths stabilize” is the wrong framework; batch-means on a seasonal trajectory is also wrong. “Fixed seed per experiment” contradicts independent replications. Input uncertainty (start-time split, latent objections, incomplete sensitivity/area fields) is not separated from run-to-run noise. Common random numbers for policy comparison are absent. The AND of planning/fire/environment is specified in the tree and then implemented as a sequential `if` chain, so D5 and review-join dynamics cannot be studied. D3/D5 longer travel is illicitly used as environmental sensitivity. Rework rates (15/20/10%) are not implied by variant shares (inspection-rework variants are 9% of all cases). Monday 2.2× arrivals plus one clerk reserved for portal support is named and then not operationalized. D2’s “no prior violations” attribute is missing from the log and is replaced by a planning-rework proxy without saying the policy is unidentified.

**Skeleton quality.** The excerpt DataFrame is internally inconsistent (`P001` is repeated 12 times against 13 P001 events). `pm4py.parse_process_tree` is not used; the fallback tree is malformed (loop arity, public-notice no longer optional). Service times are deterministic medians. `datetime.now()` is used for aging. D3/D4/D6–D9 and all stress cells are stubs. NHPP intensity is not normalized for weekday multipliers, so the long-run volume is not 31,200/18 months. This is not a small implementation gap; it means the required artifact does not actually encode the design.

**What remains acceptable.** Section structure matches the spec. Calendars, applicant delays, and non-stationarity are at least named. `format_dataframe` is used correctly and `read_csv` is avoided. The experiment list includes baseline, D1–D10, and stress cases. Decision criteria cover tails, SLA by type, fairness, utilization, and robustness, and the text does not claim calibrated optimality. That is enough for a mediocre design memo, not for a high score.
