The final write-up is well-structured and largely aligned with the task’s intent: open-world vs closed-world is defined correctly, the four relations that must not be treated as violations solely by omission are not so treated, typical-pattern absences (N2/N5/T3) are not inflated into defects, and forbidden cells are not excused by rarity. That is the right skeleton.

It is not nearly flawless. Several mistakes are material under a strict reading.

**Logical errors.** Section 3 treats N3 as “almost isomorphic” to a directly-follows fact. N3 is an *after-cancel* prohibition: `Cancel → X → Dispatch` would still be forbidden and would be invisible in the OC-DFG. Directly-follows is sufficient for the two observed cases, not necessary. That is the same eventual-vs-DF hazard the prompt flags, applied only to N1 and inverted for the highest-severity finding. Rank 5 / Section 4 also misuse N4’s scope as an escape hatch: the count is already “198 of 200 *externally shipped*” packages, so those two gaps are in-scope. Remaining explanations are abstraction, unrecorded pack events, or true unpacked dispatch—not “maybe not external.”

**Table and count hygiene.** Rank 4 and Rank 6 list T1 and T2 as if they were implicated in the gaps; OTG weights 1450 and 200 satisfy those mandatory interactions. The defects are E2 (1448 vs 1450) and E6 (198 vs 200). Validate→Close is grouped as “expected interposition”; it is the typical path itself, not an interposed activity. Create→Validate (98/100) plus Create→Manual Compliance Review (3) is never reconciled with a 100-order base; 98+3 exceeds 100 if both are exclusive DF successors from Create, and that inconsistency is left unremarked. Order–RiskCase (T5, weight 3) is not a distinct table row despite the instruction to include every unusual observation, including benign ones.

**Code sketch.** This fails a stated assumption. The prompt requires `norm_ocdfg`, `norm_otg`, and `norm_etot` as the normative structures. The sketch never uses those names and calls `conformance_*` on models *discovered from the same log*, which is tautological. The semantic layer against `NORM_*_META` is the right idea, but `_expected` and `_iter_etot_observed` are undefined, OC-DFG/OTG loops are comments only, and the block is not “executable-looking” in the sense requested.

**Lesser issues.** Word count is likely above the 1,700–2,600 target once the table is counted. “Mandatory-with-scoping” is not one of the four allowed statuses for Section 6, though the underlying gap analysis is otherwise strong.

The piece would sit in the high 8s if N3/N4, T1/T2, the 98+3 inconsistency, and the conformance-to-self sketch were fixed. As written, those are not nits; they cut into normative reasoning, the DF/eventual lesson, and the only required code artifact.
