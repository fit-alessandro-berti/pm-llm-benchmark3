7.5

The answer meets the required six-section structure and correctly separates April (pipeline), June (routing mix), August–September (overlapping process/concept/population), and October (label maturity). That is the core of the task, and the remediation staging, alert gating, and most of the diagnostic design are proportionate. It is not nearly flawless. Several claims that the piece treats as load-bearing arithmetic or identification results do not hold.

**Methodological error in the “mix-implied” device.** The opening identity is stated as: same-month variant shares × same-month matured variant rates, compared with the pooled observed rate, “separates mix from conditional outcome change.” It does not. That product is constructed to recover the pooled rate on labelled cases, up to rounding, unmapped residual, and labelled-versus-full mix. The Jan–Jul “gaps” of ~0 are near-tautologies, not findings. The one valid use of this particular gap is Aug–Oct, where maturity can make the labelled mix unrepresentative. The correct mix-versus-concept decomposition is current shares × **baseline** rates (or baseline shares × current rates). The prose later claims June is “fully reproduced … with per-variant rates held at their baseline values,” but the table does not show that calculation. June happens to give ~0.158 either way because a V2 decline and a V3 rise cancel; that coincidence is not a method. Presenting the device as the audit’s anchor overstates what the numbers identify.

**Over-precise or wrong numerical claims.** Feb mix-implied is ~0.172, not 0.174; Mar is ~0.166, not 0.168. April per-variant rates are 2–4 points above the Jan–Mar band, not “within roughly one point.” June is not “exactly” reproduced (0.158 vs 0.15). Test 3.3’s V3 predicted level “near 0.22” and O/E “approaches 3” are invented; a historically calibrated V3 rate near 0.32 would imply O/E nearer 2. July is not a “nearly quiet” pooled dashboard (AUROC 0.76, Brier 0.151, ECE 0.042).

**Weaker causal stories.** The June AUROC dip is attributed to mass moving into a homogeneous low-risk segment and “reducing between-segment variance that AUROC feeds on.” That mechanism is at best speculative and often runs the other way: easily ranked low-risk cases can raise AUROC. The honest statement, which appears later, is that per-variant AUROC is unavailable. Unmeasured Partner Z attributes (rural distance, device age) that “were not in the original model” are classified as covariate drift; that is population shift / omitted variables, not a change in \(P(X)\) for the fitted \(X\).

**Format and overclaim.** The prompt asked for exactly six sections. A long preamble plus a seventh implicit “reading conventions / mix table” is extra. “Observed / inferred / hypothesis” is a good discipline and is mostly kept; it is not kept when tautological gaps are sold as triangulation, or when April’s label channel is called clean beyond what the variant table shows.

**Tests, decision, monitoring, code.** Tests 3.1–3.7 match the required battery and usually give two hypotheses plus a falsifier. Test 3.1’s falsifier is loose: leaving Jan–Mar unchanged after 55% truncation would not, by itself, refute informative censoring in October. Stage 1–4 containment → variant-stratified calibration → feature repair → retraining is the right order; quarantining April and October and refusing a Partner Z model are correct. The V3-specific model discussion correctly notes that path is only partly known at diagnosis. Monitoring layers and hard DQ/maturity gates are sound. The Python sketch uses the required PM4Py surface, normalizes the rename before extraction, deduplicates uniformly, and distinguishes event-calendar vs case-start cohorts. Defects: performance edges use **mean** while the audit evidence is **median**; event-calendar “variants” on truncated month fragments are not flagged as fragments; timezone-naive `filter_time_range` strings vs UTC timestamps are a likely runtime footgun; `source_system` / label columns are assumed without a schema guard.

**Section 6.** The six failure modes are the right ones and the mitigations match the diagnosis. This section is stronger than the opening arithmetic.

Net: the operational reading of the ten months is largely right and would not prescribe the worst wrong actions. The piece still asserts an identification result its own statistic cannot support, misstates several magnitudes, and mislabels at least one drift channel. Under a strict bar that is well short of a top score.
