**6.4**

This is a competent, well-structured response that hits the required format and the main empirical pattern, but it is not nearly flawless. Several causal mistakes, a misleading positivity claim, DAG errors, and a learning-plan/estimand mismatch keep it well below a high score.

**What it gets right.** It detects the crude-versus-stratified reversal (Orion slower overall, faster in Low/Medium/High), treats that reversal as evidence of mix rather than identification, separates wait from service, treats `resource_group` as a routing bundle rather than a clean treatment, and refuses to bless the 50% reassignment. Arithmetic on the supplied aggregates is mostly auditable and correct. The five hypotheses match the required types, tests have predicted observations, and the three-phase plan includes both a capacity intervention and a small routing pilot. Residual uncertainties are specific.

**Serious causal errors.** The executive summary equates the *total effect* with the crude gap. That is association, not a total effect; it is a basic identification mistake in the one place the answer is supposed to be most careful. The same paragraph then defines a direct effect as “what remains after holding queue_wait, review_service and rework fixed” without noting post-treatment confounding of those mediators. `mainly confounded` is defensible, but the confidence (62) is higher than the design can support: High-Nova has two cases, Orion has no day-medium or day-high observations, and completed-case selection is uncheckable.

**Misleading overlap claim.** “Almost no overlap for Orion in day, medium, or high strata” is false as written. Orion’s mass *is* medium and high (6 and 6). The positivity failures are Orion-day-medium, Orion-day-high, Orion-low (n=2), and several Nova night/high cells. That sentence would mislead a committee about where the data actually live.

**DAG problems.** `complexity -> unobserved_case_risk` is backwards or incoherent: unobserved risk should typically cause, or share causes with, classified complexity. `unobserved_reviewer_skill -> resource_group` fights the stated mechanism that Orion *contains* more certified reviewers; under a bundle interpretation the arrow should run group → skill, or skill should not cause assignment. `arrival_load` is called a pre-routing confounder in the prose but has no arrow into `routing_rule`/`resource_group`. Direct `resource_group -> cycle_time` and `resource_group -> case_completed` arrows are stacked on top of the mediated paths without a clear justification.

**Intervention/estimand mismatch.** The recommended RCT randomizes low/medium, non-compliance, similar-shift cases. That is the *wrong* population for the decision under consideration, which would move future Orion work (mostly night, high-complexity, compliance-required). The pilot can learn a well-identified effect that does not answer whether reassigning typical Orion cases would help or backfire. That limitation is not stated clearly enough.

**Process mining is bolted on.** Trace variants and DFGs appear only in the Python block. The written assessment never uses variant or directly-follows differences (compliance and request-info loops concentrated in Orion because of mix). PM4Py is not used consistently: parse/discover sit inside a fallback to a hand-rolled DFG, and `parse_event_log_string` is called on a two-column CSV with `timestamp_attr=None`, which is a doubtful match to the usual PM4Py API.

**Smaller issues.** Standardized Nova service (~6.09) looks slightly off relative to the case-level service times (Low Nova service is 3.64, not 3.68). Several table cells rest on n=1 comparisons (night-medium Nova, high-load high Nova) while still talking as if they are stratified patterns. The graph explanation treats shift as a pre-routing condition and a routing consequence without resolving the fork. Quality conclusions are appropriately sparse, but the executive still leans on “mainly confounded” as if mix closed the question.

The answer would be a solid 8 if the total-effect slip, the overlap sentence, the unobserved-variable arrows, and the pilot estimand had been right. Under a strict reading those are not nits.
