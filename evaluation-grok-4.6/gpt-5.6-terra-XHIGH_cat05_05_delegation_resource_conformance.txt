The write-up is unusually complete: it follows the required headings, reasons at event time rather than from the current role master, normalizes aliases before SoD, refuses to treat `svc_qms` as a person, keeps collusion as an indicator rather than a finding, and separates recorded-rule conformance from control-objective strength. **Material factual error.** Section 2 states that both emergencies were implemented on “Monday 2026-08-11,” with a Wednesday 13 August deadline and a “Thursday” 14 August review. The prompt says 15–16 August were weekend days, so 11 August is Tuesday, 13 August is Thursday, and 14 August is Friday.
