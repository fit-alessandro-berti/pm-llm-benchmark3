The answer is well-structured, multi-causal, and mostly well-calibrated, but it is not nearly flawless. Several numerical claims are overprecise, one quantitative reconciliation is internally inconsistent with the rest of the same table, and a few causal readings are stronger than the evidence.

**Section 1.** The qualitative metric literacy is strong: handover is adjacency, not effort; working-together is set membership, not duration-weighted collaboration; subcontracting is a sequential loop, not hierarchy; betweenness can be a designed fan-out point rather than personal importance. The quantitative scaffolding is weaker. Interpreting Mira–Carla 8% as \(350/4100 \approx 8.5\%\) looks neat, but that global-denominator reading cannot be applied to the rest of the working-together table. Alice–Carla at 27% of all ~4,100 cases would require Alice in at least ~1,107 cases, yet she has only 665. Omar–Priya at 4% *is* feasible as a global rate. The table therefore cannot be a single coherent co-case metric, so Alice–Carla 27% vs Mira–Carla 8% cannot be used as an apples-to-apples contrast between a “fixed dyad” and a split broker. The answer spent real effort on handover/subcontracting arithmetic inconsistencies and missed this larger incomparability.

Related overclaim: 4–5% co-occurrence with team leads does **not** show that Mira and the leads “sit on alternative branches.” Under the answer’s own global denominator, 5% of 4,100 is ~205 cases, i.e. ~23% of Mira’s 905 cases also contain Alice. That is substantial overlap, not branch exclusivity.

Other section-1 issues: out-degree 0.82 on a “normalized SNA graph” is not safely translated into “11–12 of 14 resources” (weighted/normalized degree is not neighbor count); the fan-out is described as “remarkably flat” despite a clear senior > junior > specialist ranking; “high variance” cycle-time contribution is treated as *therefore* waiting time in front of her events, which is one reading among others (heterogeneous exception mix is at least as likely); the working-together note is attributed to “the organisation” when it is scenario commentary; subcontracting “must have been” a different log slice is too strong, given that start/complete lifecycle pairs can produce A–B–A without 224 surplus activity instances; 43% of Bot_Rules human handovers is overprecise on an incomplete top-edge list.
