7.5

The reconstruction is unusually complete and mostly careful: labeled assumptions, interval (not point) imputation for every `MISSING` start, explicit elapsed-vs-final treatment of `OPEN` and unstarted cases, utilization that includes incomplete work, a closed-cohort Little’s identity that is not oversold as stationarity, and a time-varying bottleneck reading that is qualitatively right. The Python block also matches the requested contract. It is not nearly flawless.

**Material error.** The 12:00–14:00 S2 entry count is wrong. There are 20 S2 entries in total and 7 S1 completions after 12:00, so S2 entries in that bin are 7, not 8. The table’s `6+7+8=21` already contradicts the author’s own “20 entries from S1.” The same false `6 vs 8` comparison is then reused as bottleneck *evidence* in §5. The imbalance still exists (`6 vs 7`), but this is an internal inconsistency, not a rounding quibble.

**Convention vs implementation.** A4 says an S1 completion / S2 entry is processed with completions. The event sweep assigns entries priority 2 (after starts). Post-timestamp snapshots often net out; intra-timestamp peaks and the advertised tie rule do not. The claim that an alternate tie order can produce S1 WIP of 9 is not supported by any actual simultaneous arrival/completion on the reported grid while WIP is already 8.

**Smaller nits.** Midpoint starts such as 09:42:30 leave the five-minute grid without a stated clock-alignment rule. Station sojourn is defined and used in the cohort identity but is not summarized at station level with the same care as wait and service. The “usable” PM4Py log drops `MISSING`/`OPEN` intervals entirely; that is defensible, but then `insert_case_service_waiting_time` / `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` are demonstrated only on an 11-case complete-pair projection whose waiting semantics are activity-gap waiting, not external S1 wait. The comment declining `convert_log_to_time_intervals` is appropriate.

**What remains strong.** Case times for P01–P22, the four missing-start feasible intervals, S1/S2 residence areas (2085 and 1340), busy-time bounds (535–605 / 515–570), resource-level ranges, the 30-minute WIP path under the midpoint scenario, and the three-way sensitivity table all check out. Open cases are retained as censored, not dropped. Utilization is not inferred from completed cases alone. Stable vs fragile conclusions are separated, and the C2 outage is correctly treated as locally relevant but too small to explain the late S2 backlog by itself.

The piece is high-competence process-mining work with one conspicuous counting contradiction and a few convention gaps. That is well short of a near-flawless 9–10 under a strict rubric.
