**6.7**

The final six-section writeup has the right forecast architecture: parallel join as a **max**, not a sum; a Monday QA floor from incubation plus calendars; queue vs service split; weekend pause/resume; path bands with stated overlaps; and temperature-excursion dependence treated as a coupler rather than an independent coin-flip. That is the core of what the rubric asks for. It is not close to flawless.

**Material errors**

1. **Hours and dates in the tail do not match.** P90 is given as ~120–165 h and “Fri 25 Sept – Mon 28 Sept.” From Fri 18 Sept 15:30, 120 h is about **Thu 24 Sept 15:30** and 165 h is about **Fri 25 Sept 12:30**. Mon 28 Sept 15:30 is **~240 h**. Section 2 then assigns the same date span ~140–240 h in one row and ~120–165 h in another. The severe tail is internally inconsistent.

2. **Uncertainty ranking contradicts the calendar analysis.** Weekend micro authorization is ranked the #1 driver, yet Section 6 correctly says approval moves readout to Sunday and **does not change release time** because QA is closed Sunday. Unconditionally that driver is second-order (it mainly matters on the ~5–12% contamination path). Investigation/lab-vs-doc outcome and the temperature flag move the terminal event by **days**; authorization usually does not. That is a serious logic error, not a wording quibble.

3. **FIFO chemistry is under-modeled.** “Pure FIFO ⇒ start Monday 08:00, +~14 h” is wrong on two counts. Under FIFO, B-417 is fifth; Friday’s two servers take the urgent/ordinary work, leave leftover service into Monday, then still have two ordinary samples before B-417. A plausible FIFO start is **late Monday morning**, not 08:00. The “+14 h” looks like a single overnight (18:00→08:00) and **drops Saturday and Sunday**. Elsewhere the weekend is handled correctly; the sensitivity cell is not.

4. **Scenario B’s “central” range is the investigation tail, not the rerun.** A priority rerun is ~1–3 h on Monday. Even with rerun, investigation is only 0.30–0.55. Bundling rerun with a Tue–Wed central band overstates the typical rerun effect by a day or more.

**Smaller defects**

- Section 4 calls Sunday 12:00 a “**48 h** minimum”; the spec is **46–54 h**, and 48 h is Sunday 14:00. Section 3 had this right.
- Friday start is treated as the central chemistry path despite FIFO ~55%. Priority start Friday is a **scenario**, not the default.
- Smooth-path hours are slightly sloppy (Mon 12:00 is ~68.5 h, not ~70 h; earliest ~66 h, not ~65 h).
- QA queue of 4–7 at Monday join is pessimistic if Friday/Saturday processing is near the median; that nudges P50 toward Tuesday without a clear justification.
- Rejection after a confirmed critical defect is mentioned only as “&lt;5%” and never decomposed.

**What still works**

Assumption ledger structure, overlap-aware scenario table, max-join reasoning, incubation window (Sun 12:00–20:00), on-call vs Monday readout, chemistry close-at-18:00 with preserved partial work, QA Saturday being moot, release vs reject as terminal events, and choosing the temperature flag as the highest-value next observation are all defensible. The forecast is in the right family: Monday–Tuesday center, multi-day investigation tail.

Those strengths keep it well above a “sum the means / ignore the weekend” answer. The date–hour contradictions, the #1-driver inconsistency, and the FIFO/weekend arithmetic keep it well below a high score under a strict reading.
