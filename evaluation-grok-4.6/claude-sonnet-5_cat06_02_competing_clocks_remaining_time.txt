**5.5**

The write-up looks like a strong forecast: correct sectioning, max-not-sum parallelism, weekend calendars, overlapping path envelopes, and an explicit dependence story for the temperature flag. Under a strict reading it is not close to flawless. Several numbers contradict each other, one impossible path is treated as real, and that error then distorts the uncertainty ranking and the “next event” claim.

**Internal incoherence (most damaging).** Scenario A’s earliest terminal is Mon 21 Sep ~10:00–11:00; §3 then puts the same scenario at Mon 15:00–Tue 10:00 after a 2–8 h QA stage; §4’s “plausible earliest” is 66–70 h / Mon 09:30–13:00. Those three pictures cannot be true together. A Monday 09:30–11:00 terminal requires join near 08:30 and roughly 1–2 h of QA+release, which is below the 2–8 h QA range the same answer just defended. The scenario table’s “earliest terminal” column repeatedly reads like a join time (A ~10:00–11:00, B ~12:00, C Mon 09:30), not a terminal event. A remaining-time forecast that cannot keep earliest / central / QA-stage arithmetic aligned should not be trusted at the level the prose implies.

**Chemistry Friday completion is essentially impossible, but it is used anyway.** Two analysts are already busy; one urgent plus three ordinary samples are waiting; chemistry service is at least 1.5 h; the lab closes at 18:00. Under FIFO, B-417 does not start Friday. Under risk-priority, the first free analyst takes the urgent job (~15:54–16:24); B-417 can start only when the second analyst frees (~16:42–17:30), leaving 0.5–1.3 h before close. That cannot finish a 1.5–4.5 h assay. Even the illegal story “skip the urgent sample at 15:54” still finishes Friday only in the fast tail of the service distribution (roughly P(service ≤ remaining window) ≈ 0.1). §3’s “minority sub-case (chemistry finishes Friday + micro authorized weekend)” and the Sunday 13:00–23:00 join are therefore not a real left tail. The same slip appears as “finish Monday within ~0.5 h of opening” after 1–1.3 h of Friday work: remaining work at the mode is about 1.2–2.0 h, not 0.5 h. “Run 1–1.3 h Friday” also ignores the 17:30 start.

**That false path then mis-ranks the live clocks.** Once chemistry cannot finish Friday, weekend on-call authorization barely moves the *smooth* terminal time: QA is closed Sunday, and Monday micro readout is 0.5–1.5 h while chemistry still has leftover queue/service. Authorization mainly matters on the contamination path (Sunday first read starts the 20–30 h confirmation clock one day earlier). Ranking weekend micro staffing as driver #2, and making “authorized and clean ⇒ micro leaves the critical path” the headline update in §6, overstates a lever that is nearly redundant with chemistry and the Sunday QA closure. Chemistry discipline (FIFO vs risk-priority) and the Monday QA backlog are larger for the center; excursion-driven rework is larger for the tail.

**FIFO chemistry is too fast.** “Mon 08:30–14:00” with “1.5 h wait + 2.5 h mode” is a priority/light-queue picture. The mode is not the mean: triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) has mean 8/3 h. Under 55% FIFO, two leftover Friday jobs plus two untouched ordinary samples sit in front of B-417 on two servers. Elapsed Monday time is often late morning to mid-afternoon, and a long-service draw can spill past 18:00 to Tuesday *without* a rerun. Calling that “occasionally more” understates the modal FIFO branch. The join is therefore later, and more often chemistry-binding, than the smooth-path window admits.

**Scenario C is mis-specified.** “No weekend authorization *and/or* contamination” cannot be a 10–18% band. Non-authorization is 0.55–0.75 and is the *usual* micro path; the text even says that sub-case already sits inside A. The 10–18% figure is only coherent as a contamination band, so the row’s title, probability, earliest time (Mon 09:30), and tail label (“weekend micro gap stacking”) pull in different directions. After a Monday first readout, confirmation is a weekday 20–30 h clock; a second weekend does not “stack” unless some other delay pushes first read to Friday. Thu–Fri in that tail is QA/investigation time, not a micro-calendar effect.

**Working-hour arithmetic is sloppy.** The ledger adopts QA’s own calendar (Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 = 12 h, Sat 4 h). Then 4–12 working hours become “1–2 QA business days.” On a 12 h QA day a 4–12 h documentation query is often same day or next morning if first touch is Monday morning. Twenty working hours is ~1.5–2 QA days, not automatically a Thu 24–Tue 29 object. The long tail is real only for the laboratory-investigation minority (3–7 calendar days), which the table partly conflates with ordinary queries.

**§4 bands are internally loose.** P80 is 90–125 h but glossed as “Tue evening – Wed evening”; 90 h after Fri 15:30 is Tue ~09:30. P90-like / severe tail is 150–330 h (6–14 days) but the clock column only runs Thu 24–Tue 29 (~150–220 h). 330 h is ~2 Oct, i.e. beyond a 7-day lab investigation started mid-week unless extra unstated holds are piled on. The P50 window (Mon 15:00–Tue 15:00) is also a bit left-shifted once a 15–30% query rate and a non-trivial FIFO chemistry delay are mixed in; a more honest center reaches further into Tuesday–Wednesday.

**Smaller but real defects.** Smooth-path 45–55% is a narrow band given unspecified excursion lifts and a 15–30% query that can occur on an otherwise clean batch; with fixed marginals, positive correlation among bad events actually *raises* P(all clean) relative to independence, which is not discussed. Rerun 10–20% is the *base* rate, not the excursion-mixed unconditional. Rejection 3–8% / release 85–92% is invented (flagged, but still presented as if it were a computed mix). QA wait 1–5 h is a plausible sketch, not a derivation from 5 incumbents, 0.35/h overdispersed arrivals, Friday/Saturday drain, and a join time that itself depends on chemistry. Several table cells are missing range punctuation, which makes already-inconsistent windows harder to read.

**What remains creditworthy.** The assumption ledger is the best part: usable result = posted readout, four waiters ≠ two in-service jobs, risk-priority as Urgent > High > Ordinary, pause/resume vs full restart, overdispersed QA arrivals, and explicit non-independence of excursion, rerun, and contamination. Overlaps are declared rather than forced to 100%. Stage notes generally use max(chemistry, micro) and then add QA/rework, and they do not naively sum unconditional means. Path set A–E matches the required coverage. Driver #1 (QA/lab investigation) and driver #4 (excursion as a common shock) are the right tail story. Electronic release as a short continuous last step is handled.

**Net.** This is a competent scenario envelope with the right qualitative machinery, not a reliable quantitative forecast. The Friday-chemistry fiction, the colliding earliest/central/QA numbers, the broken Scenario C probability, and the overweighted weekend-micro lever are enough, under a harsh bar, to keep the score well below the “nearly flawless” band.
