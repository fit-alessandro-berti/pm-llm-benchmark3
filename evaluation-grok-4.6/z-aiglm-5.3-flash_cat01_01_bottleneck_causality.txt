**7.3**

Strong, committee-usable analysis that hits the required structure and the main causal story (crude Orion penalty reverses within complexity; group is a routing/staffing bundle; 50% reassignment is a bad experiment). It is not nearly flawless. Under strict scoring the DAG omissions, one misread aggregate, one bad number, and a reversed prediction are enough to keep it well below the top band.

**What is actually good.** Sign reversal is computed correctly (crude +6.72 h vs within-stratum Orion advantages of about 2.1–3.4 h). Standardization arithmetic is right (pooled 40/33/27 weights → 17.14 vs 19.65, Δ −2.51 h) even though the displayed formula drops the Medium weight. Wait vs service split of the crude gap (+4.01 / +2.00 / residual +0.71) is auditable and correct. Overlap/positivity is taken seriously. Completed-case selection, capacity feedback, and “label vs bundle vs routing proxy” are handled as causal issues, not boilerplate. The five hypothesis types are present; Phases 2 and 3 match the required pilot and non-label capacity intervention. Code covers the six diagnostic requirements and refuses to treat PM4Py timestamps as performance data.

**Material flaws.**

1. **DAG is missing the main process arrows from complexity.** Recorded complexity clearly drives `review_service`, `rework`, and `queue_wait` (service ~3 / 6 / 9 h across Low/Med/High). Those paths are omitted; complexity only reaches outcomes via routing, compliance, and `unobserved_case_risk`. That understates a dominant mechanism and mis-states what conditioning on complexity does. `quality_outcome` is almost disconnected from the process (only `unobserved_case_risk`). `unobserved_reviewer_skill` is never tied to `resource_group` despite the bundle story.

2. **Shift is drawn as a mediator (`resource_group → shift`) and treated as a supported fact.** Night is at least as plausibly a *cause* of assignment (routing after Classify; Orion covers nights). That swaps confounder vs mediator and changes whether the total effect should condition on shift. The table flags temporal ambiguity; the graph does not.

3. **Factual slip in the executive summary.** `night_shift_pct = 92.9` is the share of *Orion cases* that are night, not Orion’s share of night work (13/17 ≈ 76%). “Carries 92.9% of night work” is wrong. “Two strata rest on n=2 Nova cells” is also wrong: High–Nova is n=2, Low–Orion is n=2.

4. **Numeric error.** Stratified service gaps are given as 0.38 / 0.38 / 0.13. Medium is ~0.23 (6.17 vs 6.40), not 0.38. Low like-for-like Day/Normal silently drops C23.

5. **H3 “predicted observation” is the prediction if mediation is *false*** (CDE ≈ total; compliance ~1 h). Under the stated hypothesis the gap should shrink when the compliance/rework path is blocked.

Smaller issues: Section 1 is slightly over the 180–300 word cap; unused `g` in the overlap loop; `parse_event_log_string` on a Series is API-fragile (mitigated by fallback). None of these would matter on a generous rubric; they compound under the instructed one.

**Net.** Causal discipline and use of the tables are well above average; process-mining competence is real but the DAG does not fully encode the process. Not a 9+ unless the graph, the 92.9% claim, the 0.38, and the H3 prediction are fixed.
