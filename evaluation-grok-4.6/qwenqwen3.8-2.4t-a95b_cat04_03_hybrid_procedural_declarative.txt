**7.3**

The final write-up is a strong hybrid design: it keeps control-flow, declarative, skeleton, temporal, and resource concerns in the right layers, states the R5/R6 conflict instead of papering over it, and the Petri net is mostly faithful to T1–T8. It is not close to flawless.

**Material errors**

1. **T5 temporal cell is wrong.** T5 still contains `Activate Vendor@9` → `Release First Payment@80` (71 hours), which is inside `72 ± 2×36`. The temporal layer should be `pass`. Marking the whole column `not evaluated` because the clarification pair is incomplete ignores a pair the profile actually measures.

2. **Log-skeleton `always_before` is misread.** Omitting `(Verify Bank Account, Classify Risk)` because a later re-verification can follow classification is incorrect. `always_before(A, B)` only requires that when `B` occurs, some `A` already occurred. A second `Verify Bank Account` after `Classify Risk` does not falsify that constraint. That is a real formalism mistake, not just a conservative choice.

3. **Temporal cells are inconsistent.** T3 has no monitored pairs and is scored `pass`; T8 has no complete monitored pair and is `not evaluated`; T5 has one complete pair and is also `not evaluated`. Same situation, three different conventions.

**Smaller but real issues**

- Under the strict policy, the “resource/data” column for T6 is just a second copy of R5. That blurs the layer they elsewhere correctly treat as resource- and attribute-sensitive.
- `Change Bank Details` is encoded as a forced `CBD → Suspend → Verify` chain, which is stronger than R11’s eventual/“before later payment” wording. Acceptable as a procedural encoding, but it is an approximation and is not labeled as such as clearly as the R5 DECLARE gap.
- `pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle` keeps compliant cases; the comment treats it as a drop-in helper without stating that polarity.

**What is solid**

R1–R12 are mapped with an authoritative layer and honest DECLARE/skeleton limits (especially that `precedence(Obtain Legal Opinion, Activate Vendor)` would wreck no-escalation accepts). The net’s clarification token, `legal_clear`/`legal_open` debt, exclusive post-classify branches, and pre-payment bank loop match the intended semantics. DECLARE templates are used with the right directionality (`altresponse` vs `chainresponse`, `noncoexistence` vs `nonsuccession`). Seconds, `zeta = 2.0`, T7’s 34-hour legal warning, and the T6 dual-resolution table are correct. Resource missing-attribute policy and the vacuity/support-confidence discussion are appropriate.

The answer is coherent and mostly executable-looking. The T5 matrix error plus the `always_before` confusion keep it well below a top score under a strict reading.
