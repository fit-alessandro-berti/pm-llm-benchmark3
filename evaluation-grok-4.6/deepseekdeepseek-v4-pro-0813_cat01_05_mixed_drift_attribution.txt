The final write-up is format-compliant and, on the substance that matters most (March as logging, later real drift, multiple mechanism-specific onsets, supplier/rework vs staffing/queueing vs season vs priority), it is the right account. **Material defects.**

Priority evidence strength 55 is fair; ranking “interaction” first is fine, but it is not a standalone identifying mechanism.

Strong process-mining structure and correct arithmetic keep this well above a middling answer. The garbled required tables, loose median decomposition, and October logging under-weighting keep it well short of a high score under a hypercritical bar.
