### Material flaws

**1. The required scenario table does not contain four materially different paths.**  
Row 3 puts “no weekend readout alone: about 55–75%” next to contamination at 5–18%. Monday readout *is* the modal smooth path (authorization is only 0.25–0.45). The smooth central window (Mon 15:00–Tue 11:00) and the “clean delayed readout” window (Mon afternoon–Tue morning) are the same forecast. Overlap is mentioned for rerun/investigation, but this collapse is not. That violates the point of the scenario table.

**2. Dependence is named, not used.**  
The temperature flag is correctly treated as a common cause and ranked first, but no conditional bands are given (e.g. rerun 0.10–0.20 → ? if confirmed). Without that, “do not multiply as independent” is a disclaimer, not an analysis. The 3–10% severe band is therefore only loosely anchored.

**3. QA-at-join is the weakest quantitative piece.**  
The prompt makes future QA congestion a first-class object. The answer gives a residual-queue story and then a loose “1–6 working hours.” Overdispersion, two Monday reviewers, and arrivals during the wait are not turned into even a rough queue envelope (e.g. leftover 0–6 plus Monday arrivals). Same-day vs next-day release is asserted more than computed.

**4. “Microbiology dominates the smooth path” is over-sold.**  
Weekend staffing explains why nothing finishes before Monday. Conditional on weekday staffing, FIFO is the *majority* discipline (55%). Under FIFO, chemistry is often the later branch (Mon 12:00–17:30 vs micro ~08:30–09:30). The dominant *clock for Monday AM vs PM* is chemistry queueing, not incubation. The answer notes spillover but still labels micro the dominant smooth-path clock.

**5. P90 and the disaster tail are fused.**  
“P90-like or severe-tail: 136–267 h (Thu–next Tue)” spans roughly a documentation-heavy week and a 7-day lab investigation. That is an envelope, not a P90. A stricter split would put P80/P90 around Tue night–Thu/Fri and reserve late-the-following-week for a thin tail.
