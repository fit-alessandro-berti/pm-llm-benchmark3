## Section 1 — incomplete and too thin
The verdict and backfire logic are the strongest part of the write-up. The crude +6.72 h Orion gap and the within-complexity sign flip are correctly identified, and it is right that moving labels does not move mix, night coverage, or compliance load.

It still fails the assigned brief. The executive note is ~130–140 words against a required 180–300. It never distinguishes **total vs direct vs mediated** Orion effects, which the prompt listed as a minimum. “The gap disappears once complexity (and its correlates night shift and compliance) is held fixed” is overstated: only complexity was stratified; shift and compliance remain badly imbalanced inside those strata. “Equal or lower” is also sloppy — every stratum is strictly lower. Confidence 65 is reasonable; the identification claim is not.

## Section 2 — not a usable DAG
The block is not a clean directed adjacency list. `case_completed -> (selection)` is not an edge. `quality_outcome <- cycle_time, unobserved_case_risk` reverses notation. Supported arrows are smashed into unreadable tokens (`complexityrouting_rule`).

Fact-supported structure is missing. Wait is defined to include compliance queueing, so `compliance_performed → queue_wait` should be present. Review service includes post–Request Info review, so `rework → review_service` should be present. Complexity almost certainly points at `review_service`, `rework`, and `cycle_time`. `arrival_load` is a parent of wait and then floats. Treating `resource_group → shift` as a fact is one reading of “Orion covers most night work”; the equally plausible `night arrival → routing_rule → resource_group` is ignored. Selection is named but not generated by `cycle_time` / `unobserved_case_risk` / `queue_wait`.

Confounder / mediator / selection / proxy are all named, which satisfies the letter of the request. The collider reasoning is not. Arrival load, review service, routing rule, and quality are absent.

The rest is not. Day/night, compliance, wait vs service, rework, and quality lack the required arithmetic. Crude Orion wait is about 12.8 h vs Nova 8.8 h and crude service about 7.0 h vs 5.0 h; within strata Orion is slightly faster on **both**.
