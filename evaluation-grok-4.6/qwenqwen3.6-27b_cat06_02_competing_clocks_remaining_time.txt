**4.5**

The write-up has the right *shape* of a remaining-time forecast (six required sections, `max` of parallel branches rather than a sum, scenario envelopes, calendar pauses, overlapping path bands). That is not enough. The dominant clock is computed incorrectly, queueing is mishandled, and the severe tail is too short. Under a strict reading those are not cosmetic defects.

**Fatal calendar error on the microbiology branch.** Incubation starts Friday 14:00 and is ready 46–54 h later: **Sunday 12:00–20:00**, not Monday 10:00–18:00. Friday 14:00 + 46 h = Sunday 12:00; +54 h = Sunday 20:00. The assumption ledger then states that weekend on-call “is irrelevant for primary readout.” That is the opposite of the scenario design. The problem states a 0.25–0.45 weekend-authorization probability *because* the result becomes technically ready on Sunday. Dismissing that mechanism collapses staffing-calendar uncertainty into a Monday afternoon service window that does not exist.

The error cascades. Join is placed at Monday ~14:00–16:00 with micro as the clear slower branch. Correctly, a no-on-call readout starts Monday 08:00 and finishes about 08:30–09:30; chemistry remainder also resumes Monday 08:00. The smooth-path join is a **Monday-morning race**, often chemistry-limited or close, after a weekend that QA cannot use (Sunday closed; Saturday already past). Weekend authorization only buys a few Monday-morning hours, because QA is closed Sunday anyway — a real subtlety — but that is not the analysis given. The answer’s “micro dominates Monday afternoon” story is an artifact of adding roughly a day to incubation.

**Chemistry queueing is not modeled.** At 15:30 there are **one urgent and three ordinary samples waiting**, plus two busy analysts. The answer treats wait as the residual in-service work (0.4–2.0 h) and has service start ~16:15–16:45. That is only plausible if B-417 jumps the entire waiting line. Under FIFO (~55%) it is fifth. Two servers cannot clear four queued triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) jobs in the 2.5 h to 18:00; B-417 often **does not start Friday** and can finish Monday afternoon or later. Calling that “jumps ahead of ordinary samples under FIFO” is a contradiction. Risk-priority (~45%) still leaves the urgent job and unknown extra service ahead. The “if wrong, chemistry spills to Tuesday” sensitivity note is in the right direction but does not repair the central path.

**QA queue at the future join is not a net queue.** Five batches are already in QA *now*. Friday 15:30–20:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00 are about 8.5 reviewer-hours *before* any Monday join; Monday 08:00 also has two reviewers. Arrivals at 0.35/h must be offset by that drain. Using “5 + ~10 arrivals → 8–12 at join” overstates congestion and is why QA is pushed into Tuesday in the central case. Overdispersion is acknowledged; inventory balance is not.

**Laboratory-investigation tail is compressed.** A lab investigation adds **3–7 calendar days**, not a same-week Friday finish. From a Wednesday contamination join that is the following weekend through the next Wednesday, i.e. well beyond ~162 h. The P90/severe band (Thu–Fri) is therefore not a P90 for the stated path. Contamination-only extra incubation (20–30 h) is mixed with investigation delay; those are different clocks.

**Stage arithmetic is internally sloppy even on its own (wrong) timeline.** Monday 15:00 → Tuesday 09:00 is 18 calendar hours, but the breakdown “5 h Mon + 3 h weekend pause + 10 h Tue morning” is false: the Monday 20:00–Tuesday 08:00 close is **12 h**, not 3 h. Earliest Monday 16:00 as “~71 h” is off (Fri 15:30 → Mon 16:00 ≈ 72.5 h). Small, but it matches a pattern of hour-counting that is not reliable.

**Next-event choice is second-best and incomplete.** Temperature-excursion confirmation is a real dependence update for the *tail*. The observations that most split the *near* forecast are whether chemistry starts/finishes before 18:00 **today** (2.5 h away) and whether weekend micro readout is authorized. Those are barely used. Monday 08:00 QA depth is a fair secondary trigger.

**What is actually defensible.** Format and columns match the spec. Parallel join is a maximum. Rerun cannot run past 18:00. Query/investigation are separate optional clocks, not added into every path. Temperature excursion is treated as a common shock rather than independent Bernoulli draws. Scenario bands are allowed to overlap. Point estimates are avoided. Those are necessary conditions for a competent answer, not evidence the forecast is right.

**Net.** This is a well-structured envelope forecast built on a one-day error in the main remaining-work clock, a non-FIFO “FIFO” wait, an un-netted QA queue, and a truncated lab-investigation tail. Remaining-time *order of magnitude* (through the weekend into Monday/Tuesday, with multi-day rework tails) survives only because the weekend barrier is so large that a 6–8 h Monday bias still lands in the same days. That is luck, not a correct temporal decomposition. A strict grade cannot sit in the high band.
