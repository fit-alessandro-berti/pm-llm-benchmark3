**2.8**

The write-up follows the required seven-section skeleton and gets a few local S2 facts right (P08 starting just after the C2 outage; P05’s C1 start after P03; P11’s C2 start after P08). Almost everything that depends on those facts is still unreliable.

**Case-level reconstruction is systematically wrong.** S1 waiting time is `s1_start − arrival`. The table repeatedly reports 0–20 minutes where the log shows 35–105 minutes (P05 35 not 0; P06 40 not 20; P08 50 not 55; P09 60 not 10; P10 70 not 10; P11 75 not 15; P12 85 not 10; P13 80 not 15; P14 95 not 15). For P16–P21 the S1 start is observed, yet the answer prints wide “0–…” ranges as if it were missing. P17 is flagged `S1_start MISSING` though `s1_start = 12:20`. P05’s S2 wait is written as a point 50 minutes even while the note says the start lies in `[09:45, 10:15]` (wait should be `[20, 50]`, service `[0, 30]`, not wait 50 and service `0–50`). P11’s wait cell `55-25 min` is not a valid interval. P15’s previous I1 job is P13 (ends 11:40), not P14 (I2); the imputed S1 service bound `0–15` is therefore false. P22’s 110 minutes is inconsistent with the parenthetical `12:10–13:20`.

That is not rounding noise. It is a confusion of case wait with resource idle/gap time, plus misread fields. Under the stated grading bar this alone precludes a high score.

**Station metrics inherit the same errors and add new ones.** S1 has 20 completed services (P01–P20), not 14; 14 is the count of cases that finished *both* stations. I2 has every start observed: busy time is 290 min in a 360 min window (about 81%), not “5h 15m / 87.5%”. C1’s “4h 50m / 81.7%” does not even divide correctly (290/360 ≈ 80.6%). Waiting-time commentary (“S1 mostly 0–20 min”) is the opposite of the log: S1 waits grow through the morning and P22 is still waiting at 14:00 after arriving 12:10. Peak/mean WIP figures are asserted without a stated tie-breaking rule for simultaneous rounded events. Arrival and departure rates by subperiod are missing.

**Bottleneck and Little’s Law.** Declaring S2 the sole bottleneck and S1 “not limiting” is not supported once S1 waits are computed correctly. Both stations congest; S1 is already overloaded in the first two hours (arrivals well above two ~30-minute servers). The C2 outage discussion for P08 is plausible, but it is not the main story. Case sojourn is defined as `S1_end − arrival`, which is not total sojourn for completed cases (`S2_end − arrival`). Little’s Law is then applied to the whole 08:00–14:00 window with completed-only throughput and a WIP series that, in the code, drops WAITING_FOR_S2 / OPEN_IN_S2 cases after `s1_end`. That interval is not stable, and the identity is not a consistency check.

**Missingness / sensitivity.** Three scenario names appear, but no numeric optimistic/central/pessimistic tables are produced. Several “stable” conclusions (S1 not the bottleneck; I1/I2 both 87.5%) are artifacts of the wait and utilization mistakes. Rounding is acknowledged (±2.5 min) and then almost never propagated.

**Section 7 does not meet the spec and would not reproduce the tables.** P05’s S1 start is coded as `MISSING` (it is 09:00). `start_timestamp` values are concatenations of two clock times, not datetimes. The code never calls `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` or `pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`. `format_dataframe` is invoked with a nonstandard argument. `df_complete` is printed with `s2_wait`/`s2_service` that were only attached to `df`. The WIP helper ignores arrival and misses post-S1 queueing. Requirement 7 is at best a vague comment, not a demonstration.

**What remains defensible:** heading structure; A1–A16 labeling; some C1/C2 adjacency constraints; distinguishing elapsed vs final service for OPEN cases in prose; P08 vs the 10:55–11:10 outage.

Those fragments do not offset inverted queues, wrong utilizations, an invalid Little’s Law check, an unexecuted sensitivity analysis, and non-compliant Python. A nearly flawless answer is not in view.
