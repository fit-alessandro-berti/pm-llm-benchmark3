That structural compliance is not enough for a high score. Several classification conditions contradict the snapshot itself, so the normative reasoning is not nearly flawless.

A second concrete error is the E2 escape clause: “conforming only if those 2 are non-Add-Item interactions.” The missing bindings are on the `Add Item` event type (`Add Item–Item` 1450 vs `Add Item–Order` 1448). The answer never reconciles type-level edge presence with per-event obligation.
