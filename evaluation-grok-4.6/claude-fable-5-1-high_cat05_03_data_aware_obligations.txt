The table’s “after the latest material data change” is not the same predicate as `¬invalidated ∧ ¬changed(jurisdiction)` in `validER1`. Risk/customer changes are material for R2 and, on a literal R1 reading, could be material there too; the write-up elsewhere says materiality is rule-dependent, so the formalization is not a single theory.
3. R4 in code is not the R4 in prose. Section 1 correctly takes the review-time amount as baseline (so two +10% steps after a review can still invalidate). `last_material_ts` is computed from stepwise `amount_ratio_vs_prev > 20%`. Those can diverge. Again, this log does not expose it.
