Operators are stripped throughout: `32%58%81%89%`, `Classify  Risk Screen`, `26.026.5h`, `Enhanced ReviewDecision`. Several tables become ambiguous without arrows. That is not cosmetic in a diagnostic write-up whose claims are quantitative deltas.

## Section 2 — ranking

The footnote breaks the table. It says posteriors measure **cause of the measured collapse**, then gives obsolete-model **80** while admitting sign-off cannot explain June. ## Section 3 — abduction

The staged 32→58→81→89 pattern is used well: different defects produce different move types, so repairs should be approximately additive. Calling the label clash a **“synchronous move mismatch”** is wrong. A synchronous move is a matching pair. `Risk Screen` vs `Classify` is a **log move plus a model move**. That is a process-mining vocabulary error in the core causal paragraph.

The adjacency list required `gateway`/`Queue Routed` as recording, not as process. `gateway_change -> true_process` treats a technical insert as behavioral. `alignment_moves -> DFG_inversions` is backwards: inversions are in `recorded_order` / the log, and alignments read them.

The outcomes paragraph is the strongest part of the essay: stable appeals do not prove clean data **or** clean conduct when detection is incomplete and 55 enhanced-without-check traces are unaudited.

## Section 6 — code

Intent matches the brief: tree → net, variant expansion, DFG, alignments, remap, optional `Queue Routed` drop, candidate optional sign-off, precision, and the required data-condition caveat.

Defects:
- Log construction is not robust. `parse_event_log_string` in current PM4Py takes a **collection of trace strings**, not `"<A,B> 380"` blocks. If a string is passed, it can be iterated as characters. Angle brackets and trailing counts would become activity names (`"<Receive"`, `"Notify> 380"`). Even if a course manual defined that syntax, **frequencies must be expanded** or fitness is an unweighted 7-variant statistic, not a 650-case one.
- Alignment “diagnostics” loop is `for d in diags[:5]: pass`. Dominant moves are never extracted.
- `discover_dfg(...)[0]` is version-fragile.
- Sequence repair swaps a terminal `Notify,Decision` pair instead of sorting on a `sequence_number` attribute. The comment admits this, but then the script does not demonstrate the method it claims to test.
- `deepcopy` on a PM4Py log is a known footgun.

The caveat printout about control-flow-only optional sign-off is exactly what the prompt asked for and is the best part of the listing.

## Section 7 — conclusion

Primary story (June = logging/provenance; August remainder = stale model) is right. Confidence bands are roughly in range. Actions for IT / owners / compliance are specific and do not over-claim.

Sloppiness: “55–60” Manual Override cases (20+35=55); garbled deltas; no mention that Request Info is gone or that the enhanced share jumped. “~40% plausibility of concentrated noncompliance” is a reasonable residual, not a finding.
