**Process-mining and causal gaps**

The prompt states Senior Assessment was “historically recorded inconsistently.” The October `Assess → Senior Assessment` jump (4–9 per 100 cases → 22) is treated almost entirely as seasonal population drift. A logging/semantic alternative—more complete recording of a path that always existed—is underplayed. That is exactly the discrimination the task asks for.

Cycle-time shares (queueing 50–60%, rework 20–30%) are directionally plausible given the 4.8 → 5.4 h Assess service-time note, but they are not tied tightly enough to the rework-rate change (12% → 29%) or to priority-induced redistribution, which the same answer correctly says aggregates hide.
