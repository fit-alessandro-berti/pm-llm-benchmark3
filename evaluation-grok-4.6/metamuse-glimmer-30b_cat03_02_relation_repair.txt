**5.7**

The final write-up follows the required eight-section skeleton, respects the main constraints (no unique `ev11` assignment, arithmetic treated as evidence not proof, no invented objects/events, no completeness-for-its-own-sake repair), and the Bundle A / Bundle B split is coherent rather than a pile of isolated edges. That is the ceiling. Under a strict reading the answer is not close to flawless.

**What is actually good.** Section 1’s rule taxonomy is usable. The table includes explicit non-additions (`R01`, `R02`), which the prompt asked for. Bundle A is genuinely conservative: `ev08` left as a scanner-outage gap, `ev11` left order-only, invoice and `PAY-2` left open. **Technical errors that matter.**

1. **Order lifecycle / OC-DFG / flattening are wrong.** Observed E2O for `ORD-A` is `ev01`, `ev03`, `ev04`, `ev11`, `ev12`. `Pack Item` and `Dispatch Package` are on items/packages, not on the order. Standard OC-DFG and `ocel_flattening` follow E2O, not O2O inheritance. Claiming that the unrepaired Order OC-DFG contains Pack → Dispatch, and that flattening on Order “duplicates Pack Item and Dispatch Package,” is a process-mining mistake, not a wording slip.

2. **OTG is misdefined.** “OTG now connects PK-8 to DEL-Y via E2O” is false. An object-type graph is induced by O2O. Bundle A adds no `carries` edge, so that OTG fragment should stay missing. The answer confuses ET–OT / E2O with OTG.

3. **`R01` vs `P04` is sloppy.** The same edge `ev11`–`DEL-X` is both provisional (0.55) and rejected (0.20). What they mean is “reject *exclusive* assignment,” but the table schema cannot express that, and they never make the distinction operational.

**Calibration and missed abduction.** `P03` at 0.85 is too high for an identifier that literally contains `?`. `P07` at 0.70 is too high next to their own “arithmetic is not proof” rule. The most natural payment split — `PAY-1=100` with `ORD-B`, `PAY-2=130` with `ORD-A` — is barely used; they only note that 130 matches `ORD-A`. Mixed-order packing (`PK-8` holding `IT-A2` and `IT-B1`) is mentioned, not analyzed as a cardinality/flattening hazard.

**Spec misses.** Prose is well under the 1,700–2,700-word band; §6 and the audit are thin because of that. The PM4Py block copies tables and checks keys, then comments out constructor, `discover_ocdfg` / `discover_otg` / `discover_etot`, and both flattenings. Unused `deepcopy`. No O2O referential check. As an outline it is a sketch, not the executable validation the prompt described.

**Net.** Right genre of answer, right conservatism, several load-bearing PM claims false, length and code short of spec. That is mid-pack, not high.
