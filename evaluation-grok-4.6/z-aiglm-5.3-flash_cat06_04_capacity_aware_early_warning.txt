**6.3**

The six-section write-up is a genuinely strong policy memo on the dimensions the prompt cares about most: it refuses I-03, splits I-05 from I-07, treats `p` as observational rather than causal, applies segment calibration, and prices untreated-case harm. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading, several internal contradictions and operational errors are large enough to keep it well below the top of the scale.

**What is solid.** The eligibility gate, fingerprint/cool-down logic, three-scenario impact table, and fairness/monitoring sections are the right shape. Arithmetic for `N = r·V − 450 − (1−p)F` is auditable and mostly correct. I-03 is excluded on stage, not rank. I-05 is suppressed as a stale refresh; I-07 is re-opened because of the new log. Spillover is not an afterthought: `Δ ≈ 6 × 0.02 × €20k = €2,400` per slot is stated, used in Section 3, and tied to a stop-loss in the adverse case. Safeguards in Section 5 are concrete (equity budget, silent-cohort dashboard, holdout/ITT, dwell-time split by segment). Section 6 has pre-registered change triggers rather than slogans.

**Decision-theoretic incoherence (largest flaw).** R6 says stop filling when marginal `N − Δ ≤ 0`. I-07 has `N ≈ +€0.8k` and therefore `N − Δ ≈ −€1.6k` even at the *low* end of spillover (`Δ ≈ €1.2k` still leaves I-07 negative). The memo still spends Slot 1 on I-07. I-02, with `N ≈ +€3.0k` and `N − Δ ≈ +€0.6k`, is deferred *because* the residual is thin. That is the same test, applied in opposite directions. “The option expires in 90 minutes” does not repair this: expiry makes waiting worthless, it does not make a negative-net action worth taking. The write-up never adds an explicit non-monetary overlay (SME duty, civic floor) that would make I-07’s net positive; it just promotes it to Tier 1. Rule and allocation disagree.

**Operational timing errors.** I-02 has 3.0 h left at 12:00, so the SLA is 15:00. Putting I-02 on a “15:00 rebid” is intervening at (or after) the deadline. That is not a wait-and-see option; it is a missed window. I-07 is Triage with 1.5 h to a *resolution* SLA: a 45-minute swarm ending ~12:55 leaves ~35 minutes for Diagnosis Complete → Resolution → Validation → Closed. The supplied `r` is used as if that path were unconstrained. That is an operational hole the memo does not discuss.

**Allocation list does not match the scoring rule.** I-08 is second by `N` (`≈ +€6.3k`; lower bound still positive before `Δ`) and still has 8 h. The 15:00 rebid pool is `{I-02, I-05, I-06, I-10, I-01}` — I-08 is simply omitted. That is not a documented deprioritization; it is a missing row in the policy’s own continuation rule. I-04 as a diagnosis-start trigger is reasonable, but the impact table then treats I-04 as “likely executed.” If diagnosis has not started, Section 3’s base net of ~+€7.4k is an upper case of the *plan*, not the plan as of 12:00. That overclaim is not flagged in the table.

**Uncertainty is uneven.** NNL/I-09: calibration is widened (`p ∈ [0.59, 0.79]`) and the memo claims the choice “survives the entire widened interval,” but the effect range is *not* widened even though the prompt says intervention-effect estimates are uncertain in that segment. If `r` is haircut toward 0.10, I-09’s net after `Δ` is no longer robust. SE downward adjustment of `p` is applied cleanly; SME upward adjustment is applied cleanly; I-06’s imputation haircut is the right instinct. Adverse spillover then stacks a higher `p` increment *and* a jump in `V_dx` from €20k to €30k without saying which untreated cases justify the new average.

**Smaller defects that still count.** “NLL” vs “NNL”; `P(reg) ≈ 30%` is invented without being labeled as a sensitivity; repeat-*swarm* (one swarm per case unless a new event) is thinner than repeat-*alert* policy; a 10–20% randomized holdout on four slots/day is not a realistic identification strategy without a multi-week sample-size note; the illustrative “`p ≳ 0.5`” line is easy to misread as the probability threshold the prompt forbids, even with the disclaimer.

**Net.** This is well above a weak `p_now` ranking, and it would pass a “did they hit the quality criteria” checklist. It would not pass a consistency or operations review: the written stop rule is violated by the first named intervention, the I-02 wait is timed to the SLA, and the second-best EV case falls off the continuation set. Those are not nits. Hence 6.3 rather than the 8–9 the structure might suggest at a glance.
