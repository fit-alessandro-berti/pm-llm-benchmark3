**6.3**

The final write-up is a complete six-section policy and it clears the main traps: I-03 is ineligible, I-05 is suppressed while I-07 is re-opened on new evidence, calibration is adjusted by segment, and spillovers enter the objective. It is not close to flawless. Several decision-theoretic and operational errors are large enough that a strict reading cannot go much above the mid-6s.

**Internal arithmetic and rule contradictions.** Section 1 sets today’s spillover cost at €2,400 (`0.02 × €120k`), then Section 2 ranks every case with scores that only reproduce if `SP = €2,000` (I-04 `+€5030`, I-09 `+€1550`, I-01 `−€315`). Those point scores are also unauditable in the final text: the ENB components are not shown, while later sections warn against false precision. The same document then states an emergency override (`p_adj > 0.80` and/or SLA `< 2h`) that is never applied to I-07 (`1.5h`, SME-adjusted `p` about `0.76–0.81`). A rule that is announced and then ignored on the only time-critical live case is a serious coherence failure.

**“When to intervene” is weaker than “whom to rank.”** I-04 is first choice solely because mid-point ENB is large, despite `10h` of slack and a still-open triage window. With four slots, a preferred reserve, and a possible regulatory arrival before 15:00, the option value of waiting is first-order. Parking I-02 (`Diagnosis Started`, `3h`) as “wait” while locking a slot on a long-horizon triage case gets the process constraint backwards: the swarm dies at `Diagnosis Complete`, so diagnosis-stage cases are the ones that expire. Leaving a fourth slot unused is defensible conservatism; not pricing that unused slot against I-02’s closing window is not.

**Uncertainty is acknowledged, then overridden by a mechanical point estimate.** I-09 is committed as the second slot on a mid-point ENB that uses the *unscaled* `0.18–0.30` band, even though the brief flags both calibration and effect estimates in Non-Native-Language Support as sparse. Preferring I-09 to better-calibrated Public Sector I-01 is numerically tidy if those ranges are treated as known causal effects; the brief says they are not. Proportional rescaling of Strategic Enterprise effects by `p_adj / p_now` is an extra identifying assumption presented as if it were a correction. The Adverse row then uses lower-bound effects (`0.075` and `0.18`) rather than the required “at least one selected case receives little or no benefit because the model’s reason for alerting was wrong.” I-09 still “works” in Adverse. That is not the scenario that was asked for.

**Spillover accounting is only half-closed.** Subtracting a constant `SP` from every case ignores that a swarm on a diagnosis-stage case removes that case from the victim set and that two specialists are pulled off ordinary diagnosis. Intervening on I-09 therefore has a structural side-effect on I-02/I-06 that is discussed in Section 3’s victim list and omitted from Section 2’s ranking. False-alarm burden is treated as a sure cost, which is fine; queue delay of `8–20` minutes (Adverse `15–30`) is collapsed into a single euro spillover and never connected back to the two named untreated diagnosis cases’ own SLA clocks.

**Fairness and monitoring are concrete but partly ornamental.** The safeguards (segment bias dashboard, intervention flags, spillover-exposure cap, quarterly shadow policy) are the strongest part of the memo. The `2–5%` random holdout is not reconciled with four scarce slots and critical incidents, so it reads as a research design pasted onto an operations desk. Value-based starvation of SMEs is named, then I-07 is discarded on ENB with no live application of the safety-net rule designed for exactly that case.

**What remains creditworthy.** Stage eligibility, repeated-alert logic, I-05 vs I-07, refusal to rank on `p_now` alone, explicit reserve, and a three-scenario impact table with visible (if brittle) arithmetic are all present. That is a competent operational memo with several real flaws, not a near-miss on an excellent one. Under a hypercritical bar, those flaws—inconsistent SP, unused emergency rule, mistimed I-04, fragile I-09 commitment, and a non-compliant Adverse case—cap the score in the low-to-mid 6s.
