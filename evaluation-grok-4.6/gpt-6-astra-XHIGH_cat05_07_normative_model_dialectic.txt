**8.3**

This is a strong, process-aware review: both briefs are real arguments, M0’s partial-order/XOR semantics are stated correctly, cases are traced to sources, and the layered revision is mostly implementable. It is not near-flawless. Under a strict reading, several analytical and modeling defects keep it well below a top score.

**Burden of proof vs. SUP-702.** The largest flaw is internal. Section 1 says an unproven exception does not get certified and that the ordinary CS-9 default remains. That standard yields **NONCOMPLIANT** (or at best uncertified noncompliance with mitigated culpability) for SUP-702: Auto_Approve and post-activation Quality_Review violate CS-9, PD-3’s power to override is unproved, and Automated_Master_Data_Validation is unevidenced. Section 5 instead uses **MIXED**. The write-up of the dispute is careful, and MIXED is an allowed label, but this is still a hedge against the reviewer’s own rule. “Do not use mixed as a substitute for analysis” is only partly met: the reason is specified, yet the classification avoids the consequence the burden section announced.

**Section 1 smuggles the verdict.** “The record establishes material operational noncompliance and material defects” is a conclusion, not a stipulated fact. The later fact/dispute split is good; the opening is not.

**A few classifications are softer than the evidence.** SUP-713 is substantively a compliant Quality_Review under an approved-equivalent label; **MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT** overweights mapping lag. SUP-711 as **COMPLIANT** is defensible after dedup, but the raw log still fails M0; the matrix should have kept the data defect as a first-class status rather than folding it into compliance. SUP-704 as **JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION** is right for ordering, but cumulative-cap and full monitoring remain unproved; calling residual risk “limited” slightly outruns the record.

**Pilot POWL is not PD-3.** Edges force Sanctions and Automated_Master_Data_Validation *before Auto_Approve*, not merely before activation. Quality has **no** edges, so the 2-business-day rule is entirely outside the model (acceptable only as a data check, but then the POWL is not a faithful overlay). Historical SUP-702/703/714 traces lack the validation event and would fail this candidate unless events are invented—the text warns against fabrication, then still presents this as the pilot route. Emergency/pilot loops cannot fire after activation; that limitation is under-specified relative to provisional-activation practice.

**Unverifiable code claims.** “Executed with local PM4Py 2.7.23.8,” Petri-net conversion, and “13 trace-acceptance checks” are not in the snippet (`__main__` only prints). Awkward “either … and” wording. The helpers are otherwise sound: LOOP(τ, Request_More_Info) = Request_More_Info*, XOR routes, close after the whole branch, cycle check. Envelope-as-permission is correctly denied in prose, but `M1_envelope` will still align ineligible auto-approve and expired emergency traces if anyone replays against it.

**Smaller nits.** DECLARE is described, not specified. Withdrawal-during-concurrency is only named. Prospective rules (RMI resolved before the gate, reuse validation, close-after-retrospective-duties) are sometimes written as if they were already normative. None of this wrecks the architecture, which is the best part of the answer: override table, half-open windows, CS_S retained, CS_H on retrospective manual approval, snapshots, aliases, notification_id vs. mutable CRM text.

**What is actually excellent (and why the score is not lower).** Prosecution does not treat every M0 deviation as guilt; defense does not treat frequency as authority. June-15 SUP-702 is not blamed on the July CRM cutover. FD-7’s cap is not imported into SUP-703. Requester≠approver is not confused with the SUP-715 spouse/COI issue. Expiry cases (705, 714) are not saved by continuing need or “imminent” amendment. System-level dual finding—real noncompliance **and** an obsolete oracle—is the right outcome, with a refusal to invent a violation rate.

**Net:** Dialectical structure, evidence hygiene, and the policy/data design are high-end. The MIXED/burden inconsistency, a pilot model that quietly tightens PD-3, and test claims that cannot be checked are enough, on a harsh rubric, to deny anything in the 9s.
