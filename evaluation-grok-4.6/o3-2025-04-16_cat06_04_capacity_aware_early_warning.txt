**5.8**

The answer clears the main traps (I-03 is ineligible, I-05 ≠ I-07, effects are not set equal to `p_now`, spillovers are priced), and the six-section scaffold is complete. Under a strict rubric that still fails it well short of “nearly flawless,” because the rule, the ranking, and the arithmetic do not hang together, and several operational and fairness claims are either unused, contradicted, or invented.

**Decision rule is only partly deployable.** Eligibility and the no-repeat-swarm constraint are right. The rest is incoherent. Segment offsets to `p_now` (+0.06 SME, −0.08 Strategic) are defined and then never enter the score; ranking is by midpoint ARR × value. That severs calibration from choice: an over-predicted Strategic alert can still buy a large “effect” that the same model may have mis-attributed. Midpoints of the supplied ranges are treated as decision-grade causal effects, contrary to the brief; the 0.7 haircut is an ad hoc fudge, not a robust or interval decision rule. The €1,800 spillover levy assumes an undocumented €15k average loss on six unnamed cases. “10% relative weight” is not an operational feature-change test. Time-to-SLA, remaining process path, and the option value of waiting never appear in the rule, despite 45-minute swarms and a closing diagnosis window.

**Capacity table contradicts the stated rule.** After the author’s own formula (mid effect, FA, €450, €1,800 spillover, 0.7 on I-06/I-09), approximate nets are I-04 ≈ +7.2k, I-08 ≈ +2.8k, I-02 ≈ +0.9k, I-09 ≈ +0.3k, and I-01/I-05/I-06/I-07/I-10 ≤ 0. The table nevertheless ranks I-02 above I-08, gives I-07 and I-08 the same tier, and parks I-09 as “Wait.” Priority scores are not shown, so the ranking is unauditable. I-03 is correctly dropped. I-05 is correctly suppressed. The selected book (I-04, I-08, I-02 + reserve) is an EV-plausible high-value slice, but it spends scarce slots immediately on I-04 (10 h, Triage) and I-08 (8 h, smallest ARR, €5k FA, Strategic over-prediction). I-09 is left waiting in Diagnosis Started with 2.5 h left—the window where the intervention actually dies. I-07’s 1.5 h remaining path (triage → diagnosis → resolution → validation) is never checked for salvageability. I-01’s “main uncertainty” is listed as under-prediction even though Public Sector is the segment they chose *not* to shift. “Wait” vs “do not intervene” is undefined for cases that will not get a slot today. No release rule for the reserved 15:00 slot.

**Impact table is only partly auditable.** Base-case arithmetic (0.19+0.10+0.15 = 0.44 breaches; €25.15k − €8.95k − €5.4k ≈ +€10.8k) checks out. Optimistic does not: euro lines use upper-half midpoints (0.24 / 0.125 / 0.19 → 0.555 avoided, ~€31k) while “0.70 breaches” does not match those effects or the upper bounds (0.65). The +€19–22k band is unexplained. Adverse I-08-as-dud arithmetic is fine (~€8k − €8.95k − €9k), but the loss is written “9.9 k (loss)” without a sign, and €3k/slot is not derived from the stated 0.01–0.03 or 15–30 min delay. FA is charged in every world as if it were a sure intervention tax; that needed a sentence. Non-monetary / public-sector obligations are absent. No alternative book (e.g., swap I-09 for I-08) is stress-tested, even though Adverse already treats I-08 as the dud.

**Temporal policy is the strongest section, with leftover overclaim.** Cool-down, suppression, human escalation, and the I-05 vs I-07 split are the right ideas and match the brief. Claiming I-07’s new log is a “>10% feature-weight shift” invents a quantity the case file does not give. Automatic re-entry at 8 h with no new evidence recreates the I-05 refresh loop the cool-down was meant to stop.

**Fairness names the right risks and then weakens the fixes.** Value starvation, NNL uncertainty, segment calibration, label shift from treated survivors, and spillover incidence are all on the required list. The 20–40% SME/Public quota is voided by “unless net gain < 0”: after the €1,800 levy those segments are almost all negative, so the quota never binds. The 0.7 NNL penalty itself can systematically deprioritize that slice; that circularity is not faced. “Untreated diagnosis-stage cases disproportionately belong to SME” is asserted with no evidence (the six spillover cases are unlisted). Weekly Platt on small/moderate segment samples is a recipe for noisy recalibration, not a safeguard.

**Monitoring is a checklist, not a design.** Dashboards, segment Brier, queue-delay tracking, an intervention flag, and a review trigger are directionally right. A standing 10% randomized withhold among eligible high-stakes incidents is proposed with no ethics screen, no power calculation, and no handling of I-04-class €60k exposures; quarterly HTE from that stream will be noise. “Last 100 interventions, net < 0” is too coarse to retune thresholds. There is no champion–challenger for the allocation rule, no monitor on the +0.06/−0.08 offsets, and no explicit test for policy-induced drift beyond a training flag.

Net: a competent policy memo that avoids the dumb ranking-by-`p_now` failure mode, then loses a large share of the marks to an unused calibration layer, a ranking that ignores its own score, one clear optimistic-row inconsistency, a fragile I-08-now / I-09-wait allocation, and fairness/monitoring language that looks concrete until the escape clauses and invented facts are checked.
