**3.2**

The write-up has the required skeleton and a few correct high-level instincts (QA uses appointments; QB has real preemption; QD is skill-constrained). As process-mining inference it is unreliable. Decision sets are repeatedly wrong, several job orders are inverted, and the primary labels for QA and QC do not survive the event log.

**Factual inversions.** These are not rounding issues; they reverse the evidence.
- QA: A02 finishes at 08:52; A04 starts at 08:58. The “strongest supporting episode” claims A04 departs *before* A02. A03 runs 08:20–08:30; A02 runs 08:32–08:52. The “strongest contradicting episode” claims A02 is served *before* A03.
- QA at 09:20: FIFO’s next job is A06 (08:29), not A07. A06’s appointment is 09:15, not 09:20; the log says it started late.
- At 08:02, A02 (08:04) is not waiting.
- QC: C03 is 10:18–10:32; C05 is 10:54–11:34. The diagnosis claims C05 was selected *before* C03. After C02 the next start is C03, not C04. After C05 the next start is C06, then C01, then C07 — not C07.
- C01 arrives at 10:00, not C02. QC has an explicit `priority` column.
- QD: D05 is not “served immediately.” It waits from 11:07 until 12:20. Section 1 has D06 before D07; §3 says D06 begins *after* D07.
- QB at 10:14: the observed start is B01’s third segment, not B07 at 10:56.

**Priority scale is unstable.** The prompt states priority 1 is highest. QA calls A02 (prio 3) “higher priority” than A04 (prio 1). The QC counterfactual calls C02 (prio 3) higher priority than C03 (prio 2). Elsewhere C05 prio 1 is treated as urgent. That is not a wording slip; it breaks every priority prediction.

**Wrong primary disciplines.**
- **QA is not non-preemptive priority.** After A03’s slot, A02 (prio 3) starts while A04 (prio 1) waits; A05 (prio 3) starts while A08 (prio 1) waits; A07 (prio 3) starts while A08 still waits. The walk-in sequence is arrival order, with A03/A06 as reserved appointments and A1’s 08:52–08:58 calibration unused. Appointments are not a “secondary” add-on; they are the only systematic FIFO break. Confidence 85 is indefensible.
- **QC is not EDF.** At 10:10 EDF picks C03, log picks C02 (shortest estimate). At 10:18 EDF/priority pick C05, log picks C03. At 10:32 EDF picks C05, log picks C04. At 11:34 EDF picks C07, log picks C06. At 11:40 EDF/SPT/priority pick C07, log picks C01. SPT explains the opening choices; C05 is the SLA-risk exception the prompt asked for. EDF fails almost every choice set.
- **QB as “FIFO with preemption” is underspecified and internally at war with §3**, which treats B02/B05 as priority evidence. B02’s preemption is labeled “urgent arrival” (prio 1). After B02 they *resume* B01 (prio 3) over B03 (prio 2). At 09:48 they start B03, not waiting B05 (prio 1). At 10:14 they resume B01 over B06 (prio 2). At 10:28 they take B04 over B06. B07’s 10:30 appointment is missed. This is a hybrid (urgent preemption + resume + residual FIFO/priority), not FIFO.
- **QD is skill constraints + availability + appointment hold + FIFO inside a match, not SPT-within-skill.** D-M: D04 (est 10, prio 3) before D08 (est 8, prio 1). D-X: D07 (28) before D10 (12, prio 1). D-X idles 11:20–11:30 for D06. D-F does not start D05 at 11:36 because a 30-minute job collides with the 12:00 meeting. No QD job is preempted; the meeting is unavailability, not preemptive priority.

**§2 does not do the assigned work.** The task is: at each start/preemption, list eligible waiters and compare FIFO, priority, SPT, appointment, *and* skill-based predictions. Skill is omitted for QA–QC. Several rows attach the wrong observed job to the decision epoch. Resource constraints that change eligibility are missing (A1 calibration, C1 free only at 10:10, D-X from 11:20, D-F meeting). Three (sometimes four) times per queue is the minimum; the times chosen are often the ones that were misread.

**§4 tests do not discriminate.** Test 1’s timestamp rule does not separate appointment-dominant from priority-dominant policy. Test 2 assumes the C02-vs-C03 choice is priority vs EDF, but non-inverted priority also picks C03; the actual fork is SPT (C02) vs EDF/priority (C03). The “if C03 was blocked, that supports priority” clause is backwards.

**§5–§7.** Arrival-scan delay, appointment windows, and idle-before-meeting are real, but the ranking does not map onto the actual ambiguities (QA priority vs FIFO+appointments; QC SPT vs SLA switch; QB resume-vs-priority; QD FIFO vs SPT inside a skill class). Inventing a management claim of “priority-only everywhere” is unsupported. Calling QC the *most* identifiable queue is the reverse of the log: QA’s appointment+FIFO pattern and QD’s skill/availability pattern are sharper; QC is the least identified and the most dangerous single-policy inference (EDF, SPT, and priority are observationally entangled, and the answer picked the worst of the three).

**Format nits (secondary).** Column names do not match the spec; `confidence_0_to_100` appears as “(0100)”; several cells argue with themselves in the same row.

The document looks like a complete PM memo and occasionally names the right family of mechanisms. It cannot be trusted on timestamps, choice sets, or the priority scale, so it cannot be trusted on the diagnoses those facts were meant to support.
