## Material errors

**R3 is unsound as specified.** The table claims exact `fm` and soundness. The edits do not deliver that. Adding `p_analysis → t_join_waived` only cancels a *pending* analysis. The new silent transition is specified with preset `{p_waived}` only. After `t_analyze` then `t_waive_review`, the marking is `{p_analysis_done:1, p_waived:1}`: the cancelled-pending join is disabled, the new transition fires, and `p_analysis_done` is stranded — the original defect, reintroduced. From `{p_analysis:1, p_waived:1}` that same silent transition can also fire without cancelling analysis at all. Cost 4 is internally consistent with the omitted `p_analysis_done` arc, so this is not a wording slip; the cost model encodes the mistake. “Completed analysis discarded” in the same row contradicts the listed edits. A valid member of this family needs the post-analysis join to consume `p_analysis_done` as well (cost at least 5), and must not leave a `p_waived`-only path.

**Section 6’s helper is not PM4Py.** `Transition.fire(marking)` is not the public API. Firing is `PetriNetSemantics.fire(t, net, m)` / `semantics.execute(...)`. Enabledness is checked, which is good; the actual token move would raise `AttributeError`. That is a required artifact, not a comment.

## Smaller but real faults

- “It is a workflow net; it should **therefore** not be expected to be sound” is a non sequitur. WF-net structure does not imply unsoundness. The three soundness clauses are never separated: the original net has **no dead transitions**; it fails option-to-complete and proper completion only.
- R2 is a valid, behaviorally equivalent structural alternative, but “12 is minimal in that family” is false. A silent merge `p_waived → p_review_done` plus deletion of the dead waiver join is the same idea at cost 6; redirecting `t_waive_review` onto `p_review_done` is cheaper still. The tempting-invalid-edit paragraph is good; the minimality claim after it is not.
- R1’s “Changed behavior: None” confuses policy with the Petri-net language. The repair **must** remove traces such as `Register → … → Record Review Waiver → Authorize` without analysis. That is the point.
- Section 1 overruns the 150-word cap. Counterexample 2 does not start from `im` in the table. Comparison operators (`≠`, `≥`) are missing in several sentences, which makes those lines ungrammatical as written.
