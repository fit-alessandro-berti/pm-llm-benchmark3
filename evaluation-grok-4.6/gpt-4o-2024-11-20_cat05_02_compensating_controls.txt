**Section 1 is conceptually inverted in places.** FEP-1 exists so one person cannot both initiate and authorize an erroneous or fraudulent transfer. Independent MG-4 is the main human compensating control for that threat. Calling MG-4 and ED-1 controls that “fail to address” single-actor fraud, because they “rely on humans,” is a category error. Collusion is not “inherently” beyond prevention: independence rules, conflict-of-interest bars, no shared service accounts, and ED-1’s manager/audit conditions are preventive design, not merely social residual.
