**6.5**

The final six-section writeup is structurally strong: it uses `max` rather than summed means, keeps queue wait separate from service, respects weekday/weekend calendars, treats FIFO vs risk-priority, and does not pretend anomalies are independent. Those are the right bones for this problem. The grade is held down because the *required numeric forecast* is internally inconsistent, and a remaining-time answer that disagrees with itself on clock time versus hours is not close to flawless.

**What is done well**

- Parallel join is consistently `max(T_C, T_M)`, then snapped to the next QA opening. Sunday authorization is correctly treated as almost useless for a *smooth* path because QA is closed Sunday; it is correctly treated as material if contamination is indicated on a Sunday readout.
- Chemistry pause/resume at 18:00 → Monday 08:00 is handled, including the narrow Friday-finish path (second analyst free ~16:24, min service 1.5 h).
- QA is not frozen at “five batches now.” The fluid backlog (opening-hour arrivals vs reviewer-hours) is the right shortcut, and overdispersion is used to widen bands rather than ignored.
- Path table includes the four required families, bands are not forced to 100%, and overlaps (rerun ∩ investigation, temperature ∩ both labs) are named.
- Temperature is used as a dependence device, not a third independent coin flip. Investigation probability is switched from the normal-branch band to the anomaly band when any of rerun / contamination / confirmed excursion is present.
- Uncertainty drivers are typed (queue, path, calendar, dependence, observation). The next-event section gives directional updates, not a slogan.

**Defects that actually matter**

1. **Hour ↔ clock-time contradictions in the scored outputs.**  
   Remaining hours and dates are both required; they do not match.
   - Thu 24 Sep 12:30 is ~141 h from Fri 15:30, not 130 h. 130 h is ~Thu 01:30.
   - Mon 28 Sep 12:00 is ~236.5 h, not 212 h. Tue 29 Sep 12:00 is ~260.5 h, not 236 h.
   - Fri 25 Sep 08:00–Mon 28 Sep 12:00 is written as 160–212 h; the left end is right (~160.5 h) and the right end is a full day short (off-by-one from Fri 18 to Mon 28).
   That error sits in the scenario table *and* the P90/severe band. For this task that is not a rounding nit.

2. **Triangular-mean slip, then used as if it were a computed mean.**  
   Chemistry `tri(1.5, 2.5, 4.5)` has mean `(1.5+2.5+4.5)/3 = 2.83` h, not 2.67. Rerun `tri(1.0, 1.8, 3.0)` has mean ~1.93 h; 1.8 h is the mode. Queue drain times built from the wrong mean are only “approximate” in the advertised sense if the error is small and labeled; here it is a silent arithmetic mistake.

3. **“Plausible earliest” is optimistic relative to the same QA model.**  
   Monday 08:00 backlog is on the order of four jobs and two reviewers. A release at 11:00–13:00 needs both a very early join *and* a thin leftover queue *and* a short review. That belongs in an exception envelope, not the headline earliest band, unless the queue-luck assumption is stated as extreme.

4. **Smooth-path bottleneck is argued one way, the scoring rubric another.**  
   The writeup makes chemistry the typical join clock (Mon 11:00–15:00) and micro a Monday-morning floor. That is defensible *if* six jobs are ahead and FIFO is common. It is not the first-order story the scenario is built to teach: incubation always lands on the weekend, readout is staffing-gated to Monday 08:00+, and chemistry only *spills past* that floor. The floor is present, but it is under-weighted in the narrative of the smooth path.

5. **Smaller hygiene issues.**  
   Section 3 adds extra `###` headings despite “exactly six sections.” Rejection probability 2–8% is invented (disclosed, but still a free parameter). Smooth earliest hours 66–69 h vs Mon 11:00–13:00 (actually ~67.5–69.5 h) is another conversion miss, smaller than (1).

**Net**

Conceptually this is a good expert-style envelope forecast, not a weak add-the-means answer. Under a strict bar it is not near-flawless: the severe-tail and P90 clock times are wrong by about a day, the chemistry mean is wrong, and the earliest band is happier than the QA math. That combination is a 6.5, not an 8+.
