5.2

The write-up is well structured and conceptually above average, but it fails several checks that this task is explicitly graded on: calendar arithmetic, remaining-time consistency, and whether “earliest / central / tail” times are physically reachable. Those are not cosmetic nits.

**Material calendar and arithmetic errors**

The 46–54 h incubation window is mishandled. Incubation starts Friday 14:00, so readiness is **Sunday 12:00–Sunday 20:00**, an 8-hour band. The ledger maps 46 h → Sun 12:00 and 50 h → Sun 16:00 (correct) and then 54 h → **Mon ~20:00** (wrong by 24 h). Sun 12:00 to Mon 20:00 is 32 hours, which cannot be a 46–54 h interval. That contradiction should have been caught and it contaminates Scenario C: “late Monday readiness → Tuesday first readout” is not a primary-incubation path.

Remaining hours and clock times do not match. Friday 15:30 → Monday 11:00–15:00 is **~67.5–71.5 h**, not “~43–48 h”. Friday 15:30 → Monday late afternoon is ~72–78 h, not the low 40s. The same ~24 h undercount appears in Scenario A (“42–55 h” for Mon afternoon–Tue midday) and in Section 4’s P50/P80 hour bands. The dates are in a plausible neighborhood; the required hour ranges are not internally consistent with those dates.

**Impossible left tail**

Saturday afternoon as “plausible earliest” (~19–28 h) is structurally impossible. Microbiology is not technically ready until Sunday 12:00; Saturday QA ends 13:00; QA has no Sunday hours. Even Sunday on-call readout cannot start QA before Monday 08:00. Section 3 correctly notes Sunday is QA-poor, then Section 4 advertises a Saturday terminal anyway.

Friday chemistry completion is also treated as a thin left tail. It is not. One urgent sample is explicitly ahead; the first free analyst is therefore not available to B-417. The second analyst frees ~16:42–17:30, leaving 0.5–1.3 h before 18:00, below the 1.5 h service minimum. Without overtime (which the answer says it will not assume), **B-417 cannot finish chemistry on Friday**. The optimistic “start ~16:30–17:00” is already too early, and “only very short service + early start finishes Friday” is not a real baseline path.

**Scenario design problems**

Scenario B conflates Friday spill with rerun. Friday non-completion is the default, not a 0.10–0.20 event. Scenario C treats “no/limited weekend authorization” as a delay scenario; that is the majority case (0.55–0.75), and given Sunday-only readiness it produces the ordinary Monday 08:00–09:30 readout that already belongs in the smooth path. Weekend on-call is oversold as a left-tail engine: it can finish readout on Sunday, but the join still waits on QA’s Monday open (and usually on leftover chemistry). The incremental effect is ~0.5–1.5 h plus any unmodeled Monday micro queue, not a Saturday/Sunday release.

**What is actually strong**

The assumption ledger, explicit `max` join, refusal to add unconditional means, dependence treatment (temperature flag as a common cause, Scenario E as a booster rather than an independent product), QA-at-future-join uncertainty, and the driver ranking are all in the right spirit. The *shape* of the date-based forecast—Monday–Tuesday mass, mid-week P80 from query/congestion/light investigation, week-plus tail from confirmation or lab investigation, Electronic Release as the modal terminal—is defensible. Section 6’s primary/secondary triggers are reasonable.

Those strengths do not offset wrong incubation bounds, inconsistent remaining-time units, an impossible earliest completion, and scenarios that relabel the baseline as special cases. Under a strict reading of “correct temporal decomposition” and “use of calendar constraints,” this is not near-flawless.
