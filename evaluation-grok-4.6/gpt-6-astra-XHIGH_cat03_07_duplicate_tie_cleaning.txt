The answer is strong, structured, and mostly faithful to the required conservative stance. It is not near-flawless. Under strict grading the defects below are enough to keep it well short of the 9–10 band.

**What holds up.** The six-way classification is the right one: exact technical duplicate, multi-system observation of one occurrence, derived summary, harmonization collision, concurrent same-label work on different occurrence-defining objects, and unresolved/quarantine.
