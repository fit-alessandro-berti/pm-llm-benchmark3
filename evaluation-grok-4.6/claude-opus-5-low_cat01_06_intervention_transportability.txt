**7.3**

The answer is unusually complete and, on the main evaluative axes, mostly right: it refuses to treat Site A’s −9 h as an identified effect, rejects the proposed copy, ties transport to partial-order structure rather than a similar CFD, keeps statutory `Originals Receipt → Document Check`, anticipates bottleneck shift and fake overlap, and designs a segmented fidelity-aware pilot. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading there are several substantive reasoning and quantitative errors, plus format and model-fidelity defects that keep it well below the top band.

**What is strong.** Section 1 covers the required features and the right transport breaks (originals, legal capacity, role specialization, lock, mix shift, timestamp artefact). The selection-diagram adjacency list includes every required node and the explanation correctly separates a shared critical-path mechanism from site-specific co-interventions and modifiers. Section 3 does not launder the 9 h reduction; the per-FTE check (4,800/18 vs 5,200/20) is correct; huddles and the API upgrade are treated as transferable *levers*, not as evidence for parallelization. S3 is a genuine “median down, p90 up” case with a plausible mechanism. The candidate intervention is the right object: receipt-triggered document work, not universal dual-task creation. The four-cell overlap/waiting diagnostic in §7 is the best part of the pilot. `conditional go` is the only defensible recommendation.

**Quantitative and DFG errors (costly).** The power row is wrong in three stacked ways. (i) 1,292 is the no-originals share (38% of 3,400), not the wave-1 “digital non-legal” stratum; after the legal and complexity cuts the answer itself later calls ~20–25%. (ii) 1,292 per six months is ~50/week, not 108/week — six months were treated as twelve weeks. (iii) 108 × 6 ≈ 648 is a *wave total*, not “~650 per arm.” Actual wave-1 n is on the order of ~70–90 per arm before V1/V2/V3 splits; the claimed 80% power for a 3–4 h median shift is then unsupported, especially with a heavy tail. Three treatment versions inside already thin strata, with a ≥30-case reporting floor, overcrowds the design the table pretends is powered.

The originals-stratum DFG gloss is confused. `Identity Check → Originals Receipt` (1,020) is identity *completing before* originals arrive — already-hidden work — not evidence about cases that start identity *after* receipt. The sentence claims the incremental saving “comes only from” the latter and then cites the former. The unused signal is the complementary one: `Register → Identity Check` is only 2,050/3,400, so a large block currently delays identity for no statutory reason; starting identity at registration could be more valuable in the originals stratum than the 0–2 h the text allows. Correlation between originals-required and legal-required is never discussed, so the 20–25% wave-1 share is an independence assumption dressed as a fact.

**Model fidelity.** Section 6’s supplement cycle is `Legal Review → Request Supplement → Document Check → Assessment → Legal Review`. The PM4Py `LOOP` is `do=Legal Review`, `redo=Request Supplement → Document Check (supplement)`. Re-assessment is omitted, so the net does not encode Site B rule 7. XOR gates are unguarded (acknowledged), but that does not excuse dropping a control-flow dependency the prose already stated. Unused `POWL` import and unguarded `" -> ".join(variant)` on playout traces that may contain silent transitions are smaller robustness nits. Site A trees and the explicit `identity → assessment`, `document_block → assessment` edges with no `identity → document` edge are correct.

**Overclaim and inconsistency.** “8% overlap is mostly measurement noise” overreads a note that applies mainly to the *post* period. “Almost certainly the API upgrade” for the 0.4 h identity-service drop ignores the mix shift. Site A pre-intervention queue discipline is labeled “ad hoc” with no evidence. Cross-training is “2–3 staff” in §4 and “2 identity and 2 document” in §6. `Legal Screening` is loaded onto the same two-person legal pool identified as the binding constraint. Section 9 overshoots the 250–400 word cap (~500). Appeal follow-up is 12 weeks for a 90-day endpoint.

**Net.** The causal architecture, the refusal to copy, and the mechanism-vs-implementation pilot logic are well above a typical answer. The grade is held down because the task explicitly scores quantitative scenario analysis, DFG-grounded analogical reasoning, and model fidelity — exactly where the mistakes sit — and because the rubric treats even smaller defects as disqualifying for the 8–10 range.
