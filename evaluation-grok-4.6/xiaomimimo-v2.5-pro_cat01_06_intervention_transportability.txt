**6.8**

The final write-up is a strong transportability memo: it refuses to treat Site A’s 9-hour median drop as an identified treatment effect, segments around the originals prerequisite, and designs a pilot that can distinguish mechanism failure from implementation failure. It is not nearly flawless. Explicit length caps are broken, the quantitative table does not match its own reasoning, and the PM4Py fragment would likely fail on a standard install.

**What holds up.** The process table correctly treats concurrency, statutory order, the supplement loop, legal review, document channel, specialized pools, case lock, mix, tail, rework, and timestamp quality as transport-relevant, not as cosmetic control-flow differences. The proposed intervention does not start Document Check before originals when the law forbids it. The DAG’s shared path (`treatment_parallelization → true_overlap → waiting_before_assessment → cycle_time`) is the right core mechanism, and the selection nodes for originals, specialization, lock, complexity, and channel are the right kind of site difference. Co-interventions at A (API, +2 FTE, mix shift, huddles, timestamp cleanup, no negative control) are carried through the evidence table with appropriately low transfer confidence. Scenarios include the required case where the median can improve while p90 worsens (assessment/legal congestion). The recommendation `conditional go` on the no-originals, no-legal-review slice is consistent with that analysis.

**Material defects.** Section 2’s narrative overshoots the 180–280 word cap (about 330 words). Section 9 overshoots 250–400 by a wide margin. Section 7’s “no more than 220 words” note is at or over the line. Those are stated constraints, not style nits.

S1’s table claims a 2–4 hour median improvement (44 → 40–42), but the accompanying decomposition gives a weighted effect near 1.6 hours. S1/S2/S5 also mix unsigned “reductions” with signed “+” worsenings, so the required “expected median-cycle change” column is ambiguous. The 3-hour “pure parallelization” slice of A’s 9 hours is asserted more firmly than the uncontrolled before/after design supports.

The transport graph omits a selection node for Site B’s legal-review mandate and does not give originals delay a direct path into cycle time. Those are first-order Site B constraints; folding them only through `true_overlap` or `legal_capacity` understates how much of B’s cycle time is legally unreachable by A’s intervention.

**Model fidelity.** The POWL *shape* is right: Register before verification, Identity Check concurrent with a document XOR, Assessment after the whole verification order, legal path as LOOP plus decision. Limitations comments (data-dependent gates, capacity, locks, time, back-entry) match the brief. The code is still not faithful to the stated PM4Py API: `OperatorPOWL.XOR` / `OperatorPOWL.LOOP` are not the usual constructors (`Operator.XOR` / `Operator.LOOP` from the process-tree `Operator` enum). `SilentTransition` is unused. That is enough to fail the “construct and check” requirement if executed as written.

**Pilot.** Unit, strata, T1/T2 split, interference, stop rules, and the mechanism-vs-implementation logic are the strongest part of the memo. The 4 × 2 cell design with 150 cases per cell is tight against B’s volume and the smaller strata; power claims are optimistic.

Net: analogical and causal reasoning are well above average; format, internal numbers, DAG completeness, and executable model construction keep it well short of a high score under a strict rubric.
