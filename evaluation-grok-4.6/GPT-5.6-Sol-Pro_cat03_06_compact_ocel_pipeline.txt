## What is genuinely strong

The mandatory artifact is complete: four in-memory frames, 17 events, 12 objects, timezone-aware timestamps, denormalized E2O activity/timestamp copies, required O2O predicates, isolated `OCEL(...)` construction, the required prints, flatten-then-DFG/skeleton loop, OC-DFG / OTG / ET–OT / OCPN / interactions calls, and commented visualization. The source-vs-`derived:o2o` split is a coherent answer to the required richness trade-off. Spot-checks of the advertised cardinalities match the code: 53 E2O rows, 34 source / 19 derived, 12 O2O rows. The high-leverage events are identified correctly (e06, e07, e11, P1 vs P2, V1/M1/M2). Flattened Order traces and the Order DFG edge multiset are right, including I2 skipping reservation and the two-branch self-loops on Pack/Dispatch/Confirm. Payment-as-attribute, non-allocation of the 100/150, and the weakness of Payment flattening are handled well. The OCPN write-up correctly refuses a unique net.

## Defects that block a high score

**Overstated PM4Py internals.** The 158-row interactions figure and the ET–OT weights are derived from assumed contracts (“every ordered pair,” “every E2O row”) and then stated as what the current functions emit. Those APIs may aggregate unique pairs, undirected pairs, or per-event type incidences (`Record Payment`–`Order` would then be 2, not 4). Section 5 presents deductions as implementation facts.

**Version isolation is incomplete.** Only the constructor is isolated. The task-mandated `pm4py.discover_otg` and `pm4py.discover_etot` names are the brittle surface and are called with no fallback. `discover_log_skeleton` on a flattening DataFrame is asserted, not guarded.

**Order DFG interpretation is incomplete.** Self-loops and payment double-counting are flagged, but the more serious distortion is not: finance and logistics are interleaved on the same case, so `Dispatch Package → Issue Invoice` and `Record Payment → Confirm Delivery` look like control flow. That is the main way this flattening misleads.

**E2O qualifier vocabulary is only loosely consistent.** `created_order` / `closed_order` are event-like; `order_context` is a catch-all across Add/Reserve/Pack/Dispatch/Confirm; `carried_package` is an O2O predicate reused as an E2O role on Confirm. Meaningful enough, but not a clean role grammar.

**Provenance is advertised more than used.** `source_relations` / `derived_relations` are built and counted; discovery still runs only on the enriched 53-row table. Assumptions also say logistics events are “directly related” to Orders/Items, which blurs the source/derived distinction the rest of the answer is careful about.

**Minor extra-assumption and extra-column risk.** `completes_settlement` on M2 is flagged, but it still hard-wires a 250-total that the narrative does not state. `relation_origin` and `source_sequence` are reasonable, yet they are unstated extras in the OCEL payload with no note that a strict constructor/schema might drop or reject them.

## Section-level

Assumptions, audit tables, alternatives, failure modes, and semantic checks are complete and mostly accurate. The pipeline is executable-looking and not a pile of separate classic logs. The remaining gap is not missing homework; it is overclaiming library behavior and under-explaining the worst flattening artifact. That is why this is high-7s, not 9+.
