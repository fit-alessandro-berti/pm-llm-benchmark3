## Fatal calendar / earliest-time error

Section 4’s “plausible earliest” of **Sunday 09:30–15:30 (42–48 h)** is impossible.

- Primary incubation is not technically ready until **Sunday 12:00–20:00** (Friday 14:00 + 46–54 h). Sunday 09:30 is before the incubation window even opens.
- Chemistry is closed all weekend. B-417 almost certainly cannot finish Friday (minimum service 1.5 h, close 18:00, one urgent sample still ahead under risk-priority, first free analyst often after 16:30). A Sunday join therefore has no chemistry result.
- QA is closed Sunday. Electronic release cannot precede QA acceptance.

The earliest *coherent* terminal is **Monday morning**, after both weekend calendars reopen. Section 2 already puts the smooth earliest on Monday; section 3’s join is Monday 10:30–11:30; section 4 then invents a Sunday finish. That is not a rounding issue. It is a broken clock.

## Hours and clock times do not match

## Chemistry pause semantics are wrong

The smooth chemistry story is: start Friday 16:00–17:00, service ~2.5 h, “finish” Friday 18:30–19:30, **pause**, resume Monday, finish Monday 10:30–11:30. That double-counts the same work. Pause-and-resume means only the residual after 18:00 carries to Monday. A Friday 16:00 start with 2.5 h of work leaves ~0.5 h on Monday (finish ~08:30), not another 2.5 h.

The same error poisons the rerun path. A rerun is *after* a first result, not a substitute for leftover first-run work. In the write-up, chemistry under rerun finishes **Monday 09:30–11:30**, which is *earlier* than the no-rerun finish of Monday 10:30–11:30. That is incoherent.

FIFO vs risk-priority is named but not used. With two servers, 2.5 h to close, one urgent plus three ordinary waiting, and service triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5), B-417 is unlikely to start at 16:00–17:00 under FIFO and cannot finish Friday under any honest priority rule. The ledger’s “~1–2 h” sensitivity badly understates that.

## Parallelism is asserted, then contradicted

Quality criterion: the **slower branch** governs the join. The write-up says `max`, then:

- Section 2: smooth dominant clock is **microbiology / weekend pause**; earliest is Monday 08:00 + readout + QA.

Those three claims cannot be true together. If chemistry is still open Monday morning, Sunday on-call readout does not move the join. If microbiology is truly dominant, the stage table is wrong.
