## Material analytical flaw

The most important logical slip is in how residual **Request Missing Documents** is interpreted.

The preliminary model already controls for `documentation_completeness`. The remaining **+9.5pp** for Group B is therefore *extra requesting among applicants who look similarly complete on the recorded measure*. That is a different claim from “language barriers produce incompleteness, which then produces requests.” Mechanism 4 in §4 is a plausible *raw* pathway, and it is a reason not to treat completeness as a neutral covariate, but it does not explain the *adjusted* document-request gap. After that control, the live hypotheses are discretionary requesting, worse request quality that induces extra loops not captured by the completeness flag, or a misspecified/post-treatment completeness variable. The answer notes that completeness can be process-contaminated and proposes include/exclude sensitivity, but it still presents +9.5pp and mechanism 4 as if they were the same finding. That blunts the sharpest procedural-fairness signal in the adjusted table.

A smaller version of the same issue applies to supervisor review (+8.0pp for B after controls): the text calls for an audit, but does not stress that this is extra escalation *net of documented risk, completeness, channel, region, and prior-case count*.

## PM4Py plan: conceptually strong, operationally uneven

The surrounding prose is better than the code. Correct and non-trivial points: case-level vs event-level filtering; do not repair handover nets by dropping isolated events; completion-to-completion is not queueing; defaulting service-time start to the completion timestamp is invalid; a direct-edge filter misses Language Support → Eligibility when V4 has intervening activities; discovered skeletons are not fairness obligations; PM4Py does not estimate adjusted disparity.

Defects:

- `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg(..., perf_aggregation_key="all")` is not a documented public signature. `perf_aggregation_key` exists internally on DFG statistics helpers, typically with `mean`/`median`/etc. `"all"` looks borrowed from `get_service_time`. As written, this call is likely to `TypeError`.
- `filter_variants(done, [traces.iloc[0]])` is a token use of the API. The substantively required filters are the support and fraud paths (V3–V6 analogues), not “whatever variant happens to sort first.”
- `conformance_log_skeleton(..., return_diagnostics_dataframe=True)` is at best version-specific; it is not shown as a verified call.
- `level="case"` on `filter_event_attribute_values` is older tutorial style; current public signatures are not consistently that. This is survivable, but it is not “nearly flawless” engineering.
- The function is a per-group profiler plus a later “join to weights” instruction. It never shows the actual fairness contrast: within-stratum or weighted DFGs, paired stage clocks for support queues, handover counts per case, or obligation checks (language need → Language Support Contact) joined to group and region. Those ideas appear in comments and in §2, not in executable form.

None of this makes the plan unusable, but §7 over-claims precision relative to what the snippet would actually compute.
