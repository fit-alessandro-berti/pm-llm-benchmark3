**4.6**

The memo looks complete and is directionally right to reject a naive copy, condition on originals, and treat Site A’s before/after as confounded. Under a strict reading of the rubric it is not close to a high score. Several required artifacts are malformed, and one structural fact about Site B is wrong in enough places to contaminate the causal story, the POWL, the pilot, and the assumptions.

**Process structure.** Site B’s DFG and rules put `Legal Review` *after* `Assessment` (`Assessment -> Legal Review` 1,360; then `Legal Review -> Approve/Reject` or `Request Supplement -> Document Check`). The answer repeatedly treats legal review as a gate on first-pass assessment or verification (“assessment blocked,” “Assessment waits for Legal Review,” legal cases excluded because legal work would block the parallel phase). That inverts the loop. The real transport issue is: first-pass ID/Doc parallelism cannot start `Document Check` before originals when originals are required, and a later supplement re-enters document check *and* assessment. Getting this wrong is not a wording slip; it is the distinctive Site B mechanism.

**Transport graph.** The fenced block is not a directed adjacency list over the required variables. Edges such as `treatment_parallelization -> Site A after` and `digital_documents -> Site A (90%)` are site captions, not a DAG. Required nodes (`true_overlap`, capacities, `waiting_before_assessment`, `cycle_time`) are not wired as treatment → mediator → outcome with `S_*` selection nodes into effect modifiers. The prose (shared independent checks vs originals/legal/lock modifiers) is better than the graph it was supposed to formalize.

**Evidence transfer.** The table omits major metrics the prompt asked to include: completions 4,800→5,200, high-complexity 48→40, p90 96→78, wait-before-assessment 18→12, identity/document service times, appeals, and the added-reviewer co-intervention as its own row. Worse, productive FTE 18→20 is interpreted as “better utilization from parallelism.” The prompt lists two added document reviewers as a co-occurring change. That is a causal misattribution, not a cautious reading of a confounded design. The refusal to treat the 9-hour median drop as an identified effect is correct and should have been applied to FTE as well.

**Quantification.** Digital-only = 38% treats “originals not required” as disjoint from the 40% legal-review share; those are overlapping, not nested, so eligible share and scenario ranges are not identified. Optimistic eligibility of ~100% contradicts the 62% originals constraint. Mapping from Site A’s confounded 9-hour movement to Site B ranges is mostly assertion. DFG counts (e.g. 360/1,360 supplements among legal reviews; existing `Register -> Originals` and `Identity -> Document` paths) are underused. Confidence footnotes disagree with the table.

**Pilot.** Randomizing only “originals not required” cases is reasonable; making the control wait for `Originals Receipt` on cases that do not require originals is not. For that stratum the contrast should be current sequential verification versus concurrent ID/Doc, not a fabricated originals gate. Case-level randomization in pools of 4 / 7 / 3 / 2 with a six-task WIP cap is interference-heavy; “no huddles” does not address SUTVA. Power (“80 per arm, 4h”) is hand-waved. Four weeks plus extra filters (no legal, below-median complexity) may not deliver the stated support.

**PM4Py (required section, largely failed).** The prompt required `pm4py.parse_process_tree(...)` on the given trees, `convert_to_petri_net`, and POWL classes from `pm4py.objects.powl.obj` (`Transition`, `StrictPartialOrder`, `OperatorPOWL`, `order.add_edge`). The code invents `ProcessTree(events=...)`, `POHLModel`/`POHLNode`/`POHLEdge` (wrong name), `convert_to_workflow_net`, and a `play_out(..., events=[...])` API that does not exist. Site A trees never use `parse_process_tree`. `Legal Review` has no predecessor from `Assessment`, so the net does not encode the optional legal branch plus supplement loop as specified. Limitation comments are the only salvageable part. This would not run and does not demonstrate model fidelity.

**What still works.** Originals as a hard start constraint; timestamp/lock confounding of the 8%→71% overlap; bottleneck-shift and IT-lock scenarios; a median-up / p90-down case; WIP and logging; `conditional go` rather than copy-A; eight testable assumptions in the requested shape. Those are why this is not a 2–3. They do not offset inverted legal topology, a non-graph “graph,” a missing/misread evidence table, and fabricated PM4Py.

A near-flawless answer would have used the DFG to place legal review after assessment, built a real POWL partial order with an XOR/loop around legal/supplement, parsed the Site A trees with the supplied syntax, and transported only the feasible overlap of identity with *eligible* document work. This answer gestures at that and then breaks it.
