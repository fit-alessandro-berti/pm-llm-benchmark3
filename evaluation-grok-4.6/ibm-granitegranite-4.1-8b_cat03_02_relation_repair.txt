## Constraint and data-reading failures

The domain statement and the task constraints are not treated as binding.

1. **Arithmetic is treated as proof.** Hard invariant 2 requires that an invoice bill orders whose `declared_value` aggregates to `total`. R11/R14/R16 then “accept” invoice and payment links because \(130+100=230\) and \(100+130=230\). The prompt forbids this twice: fees/taxes make equality evidence, not proof, and “Do not state that arithmetic matching proves the invoice or payment relation.” Elevating the equality to a hard invariant is a core methodological error, not a wording slip.

2. **New objects are invented.** Bundle A “creates” `DELY` to complete the dispatch invariant. `DEL-Y` is already in the object table (`carrier_ref=N-421`, `route=NORTH`). Inventing a delivery violates “Do not invent new events or objects” and also “Do not repair the log merely to make every object lifecycle complete.” The audit then calls `DELY` “inferred,” which conceals that the real object is currently an **orphan** (zero E2O rows).

3. **ev11 is assigned uniquely.** R10 is `accept` at 0.85 for a DEL-X reading; Bundle A follows. Domain rule 5 explicitly allows one delivery, several deliveries, or order-only confirmation when the portal drops the carrier id. `external_ref=N-42*` matches **both** `N-420` and `N-421`. The constraint “Do not assign ev11 uniquely unless you explain why alternatives are weaker” is not met; “customer = C17” does not discriminate DEL-X vs DEL-Y vs both vs none.

4. **`ev10` is misread.** `external_ref=N-421?` is not a missing carrier reference. It is a noisy but pointed identifier that aligns with existing `DEL-Y.carrier_ref=N-421`. Calling the carrier “absent” and then either inventing a delivery or attaching PK-8 to DEL-X because the timestamps are equal inverts the evidence ranking.
