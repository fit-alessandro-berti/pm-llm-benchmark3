**Critical flaws**

1. **Self-contradictory dependency map vs recommended portfolio.** Section 2 states A5 “cannot dynamically enforce document completeness without” A2, and that document packs are product/exposure-specific. The recommended set is `{A4, A5, A6, A9}` — A5 without A2. A2 is also called an “essential routing foundation” for KYC, valuation, and four-eyes, then dropped. That is a core dependency failure, not a nit.

2. **Four-eyes is mapped to the wrong control.** Policy is: credit analysis and committee packaging must not be the same person on High-exposure SME (5.8% month-end). A9 is contract generation (terms vs committee; KYC-before-sign). The write-up repeatedly credits A9 (and sometimes A2) with driving four-eyes from 5.8% to 0%. A8 is the natural SoD surface; it is omitted. Phase-3 gates and the final recommendation therefore claim a control effect the chosen module cannot produce.

3. **Waiting times are added as if they were universal and sequential.** “Saves ~115h” / 40–50% E2E for Portfolio 2 sums 31h + 20h + 58h (and similar additive stories elsewhere). Entity mismatch is ~15% of cases, valuation ~20% (71% of equipment, 18% of working capital), normalization ~43%, and KYC/financial review are concurrent. Median waits on different variants are not a case-level clock. The 35–45% Year-1 target is then reused for the hybrid *without* A7, which is inconsistent with their own Portfolio 2 vs 3 comparison.

4. **Resource math is started well and then abandoned.** 29,700h vs ~15,750h for nine analysts is a real gap. A6 removes ~6,500h and still leaves ~23,000h (document review + judgment-heavy credit analysis), plus committee packaging (3,900 × 50 min) which was left out of the 29,700. Claiming A6 “crushes” the analyst constraint and stops month-end panic is false on the answer’s own numbers. The problem already warned that cleaner inflow can overload analysts; residual credit-analysis demand is the new bottleneck and is not treated as such.

5. **Interim demand shift is ignored.** Legal is idle when KYC is slow and overloaded at month-end. Phases 1–2 accelerate KYC/docs/spreading; A9 does not land until months 7–9. That is exactly the pattern that should spike Legal and the contract-remediate loop before the control gate exists. Changed resource demand was a stated requirement.
