**`~struct` is two relations.** The numbered definition preserves the timestamp total/weak order. The concurrency paragraph silently replaces that with a lifecycle poset. Those are not the same equivalence. Reflexivity/symmetry/transitivity are shown for one; clustering later pretends the other.

**`~topo` is not a definition.** “Quotient graphs of object-type interaction cardinalities and lifecycle phase DAGs are isomorphic” never specifies the quotient, the phase cut, or the DAG edges. Calling this RST because “feature graphs are equal” assumes the missing map. The relation is then unused in the table.

**Preferred `~sem` is a hand-built 5-tuple, not an extracted equivalence.** Categories such as `CoPackedDelivery` vs `SinglePackageDirect` are not defined on the data until the code, and the code overfits this sample: any external object on Pack/Dispatch becomes `COPACK`, independent of actual co-packing. On X1–X8 the semantic partition is *identical* to the typed-structural partition (only X1=X2 merge). The prompt warned that the finest cut is not automatically the best, and that semantic equivalence may differ from typed isomorphism. Here “business-semantic” does no extra grouping work; it only relabels the structural cut.

A signature scheme that does not reproduce the claimed signatures fails reproducibility, which the prompt said would be graded.
