8.2

The answer is estimand-first, process-aware, and internally consistent on almost every required point. Counts, case IDs, and the 72-hour identification arithmetic check against the table. Transfers are not casually treated as ordinary censoring, open cases are retained, and `Escalate` / full prefixes / `last_event_gap_h` are correctly flagged as leakage or immortal-time problems. That is the floor for a high score, not the ceiling. Under a strict reading it is not nearly flawless.

**Notation and estimand definitions are sloppy in places that matter.** \(T_R\) is introduced as “time to internal `Resolve`” without the convention that \(T_R=\infty\) (or is undefined) when a competing terminal occurs. The identity \(P(T_R>72)=1-F_R(72)\) is then used as if it were obvious. In the same block, \(T_J\) is reject time while \(J=R\) is a cause indicator, and \(J\) is never defined as a cause. That collision is avoidable and makes the main display formula harder to audit than it should be.

**The state-transition “diagram” is incomplete.** Successors are listed, but the arrows are missing, so the object is a set of adjacency notes rather than a readable multi-state graph. The prompt allowed text, not a broken diagram. Several transitions are observationally supported; the presentation still fails the clarity bar.

**A few estimand interpretations are slightly mixed.** Censoring at transfer is described both as a selected “non-transferred population” parameter and as a hypothetical independent-censoring world. Those are not the same target. The writeup also never states explicitly that Aalen–Johansen CIFs are identified from independent *censoring* alone and do not require independent competing events — a distinction this answer is otherwise careful enough to owe the reader.

**The code meets the checklist but is not tight.** It builds the table, keeps all nine open cases, encodes two estimands and three transfer scenarios, prints cohort×priority strata and sensitivity bounds, and parses prefixes without inventing timestamps. Residual issues: `event_unsuccessful_terminal` is dead; full-prefix features (`rare_variant`, raw `review_visits`) are emitted without restating the leakage warning from §5; the PM4Py `AttributeError` fallback is narrower than the version surface it claims to cover; and the narrative never reports the AJ point estimates the code would produce, so §§1–6 remain a plan plus Manski-style sample bounds rather than a completed survival analysis.

**No major process-mining error.** Survivor bias (S09/S11/S25/S26), competing risks (S05/S06), informative transfer (all P1 and escalated), open-below vs open-above 72, incomplete AutoCheck compliance (2/15 vs 13/17), and the possibility that cohort order reverses under different imputations are all handled with the right cases. Primary AJ + secondary KM/RMST + deferred landmarking is the correct hierarchy for *these* data. The remaining deductions are for unclarity, incomplete definitions, and small execution gaps, not for a wrong analysis.
