5.7

The writeup looks disciplined and follows the required skeleton, but it is not audit-grade. Several headline classifications survive a check; the hard case and the required sensitivity matrices do not.

**What is actually strong.** Clock-offset direction is stated and applied correctly (`true = recorded − offset` after resolution). HUB, OPS-BER, SENSOR, VENDOR-NY, MOBILE-LON, T-404 inference, and T-407 “sent ≠ received” are handled in the right spirit. Midpoints are not used as primary evidence. T-406’s entry-delay ambiguity is treated as a real semantic choice. T-407 without the unlogged causal rule is correctly left indeterminate. Sections 6–7 are the best part of the answer.

**T-405 is the featured case and is wrong.** LEGACY-BER offset is \([-60,+60]\) s on a minute floor, so 02:50 CEST/CET should be roughly `[00:49:00Z, 00:51:59.999Z]` and `[01:49:00Z, 01:51:59.999Z]`, not `00:48` / `01:48`. The table is inconsistent with both the stated formula and the Python (`-60, 60`). Worse, ingestion order plus ±60 s does **not** leave a menu of fold pairings. Same-fold 02:05 cannot follow 02:50, and a second-fold alert cannot precede a first- or second-fold start. The only coherent world is first-fold alert → second-fold start → second-fold containment. That makes S2 about 34–40 min (**CERTAIN_PASS**), not a 33–41 ∪ 93–101 union, and it makes Alert→Containment about 49–55 min (**CERTAIN_PASS**), not `[0,0] ∪ [48m,56m]`. The `[0,0]` component is physically meaningless (first-fold containment is *before* first-fold alert) and would not even survive the code’s `hi >= lo` filter. Pairwise cartesian products plus clipping are not a substitute for one consistent fold assignment.

**Section 3 is mostly right elsewhere, with that exception.** T-401 S1 possible, T-402 S1 certain violation, T-408 S2/S3 certain violations, T-404 inferred-start straddles, T-409 weak offset, and zeta=2 profile calls for the HUB-heavy cases are in the right bucket. That is not enough once T-405 S2 and T-405 Alert–Containment are mislabeled.

**The sensitivity section, which the prompt required, is unreliable.**

SLA tolerances:
- T-402 S1 at +5 min is still **possible** (max ≈ 20m31s > 20m), not a pass.
- T-407 S1 at +2 min is still **possible** (max ≈ 17m03s > 17m), not a pass.
- T-408 S2 at +5 min is still a **certain violation** (min ≈ 54m57s > 50m). Saying a “55-minute interval straddles” a 50-minute limit is a conceptual error, not a rounding quibble.
- The T-408 S3 self-correction shows the author noticed this class of mistake and then left the same class in the table.

Profile zeta:
- zeta=1 Start→Containment band is `[25m,45m]`. T-401 is wholly inside (should be pass, marked possible). T-404, T-406, and T-409 are not wholly inside (marked pass).
- T-402 Alert→Start at zeta=1 is wholly *above* `[7m,13m]` (certain violation, marked possible); at zeta=3 it still exceeds 19m (possible, marked pass).
- T-402 Alert→Containment and T-408 Alert→Containment at zeta=1 are wholly above the band (certain violations, marked possible).
- T-408 Start→Containment at zeta=1 is wholly above 45m (certain violation, marked possible).
- T-403 and T-406 Alert→Start at zeta=3 still poke through 19m (possible, marked pass).

Those are not edge-second disputes. They are repeated mis-comparisons of interval vs band.

**Smaller defects that still matter.** “EDT is UTC4” is false (the arithmetic used UTC−4). Section 2 lists both T-405 folds as live event times after claiming ingestion order was enforced. The PM4Py log keeps `Investigation_Complete` and `Alert_Sent` and collapses T-405’s union to an envelope midpoint that can invert order; the lossy label is fair, the reconstruction is still careless. The code implements pairwise fold products, so it cannot reproduce a coherent T-405 chain and does not match the written T-405 bounds.

**Net.** This is a competent-looking interval report with the right vocabulary and the wrong answers on the DST case and on the sensitivity matrices the prompt asked to be taken seriously. Under a strict standard that treats even small classification errors as expensive, it is a mid-score draft, not a near-flawless analysis.
