The answer gets the central descriptive fact right (crude Orion penalty reverses inside every complexity stratum) and correctly rejects a blind 50% reassignment. That is not enough. Under a strict reading of the rubric it fails several *explicit* requirements, overclaims identification, and contains concrete causal and arithmetic errors.

**Section 1.** `mainly confounded` is a permitted and defensible label, but confidence **85** is not. With \(n=30\), High–Nova \(n=2\), almost no Orion daytime Medium/High support, and only completed cases, the task is underdetermined by design. Total vs direct vs mediated Orion effects are never cleanly separated (ATE of the group label vs effects via compliance, queue wait, rework, night coverage). Low-complexity rework “0% vs 10%” is \(0/2\) vs \(1/10\) and should not be used as supporting evidence.

**Section 2.** Two arrows are wrong or poorly justified. `resource_group → shift` treats night as a consequence of the label; the operating facts are about coverage and routing after Classify, so `shift`/`arrival` → `routing_rule` → `resource_group` is the more defensible path (or at least bidirectional). `quality_outcome → case_completed` is temporally backwards: the 30-day outcome is post-Close. `cycle_time → case_completed` plus a dummy `[Selection]` node is not a well-specified selection diagram.
