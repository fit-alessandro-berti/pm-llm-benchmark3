**Architecture.** The layering idea (POWL / DECLARE / pandas / org controls) is the right shape. **PM4Py model — fails the required minimums.** This is the largest technical failure.

- There is no `Operator.LOOP`. The “loop” is a `StrictPartialOrder` that **forces `Request_More_Info` on every case**, then fires sanctions/quality, then `checks` fires them **again**. That is not a repeatable optional redo; it changes the language of the entire process.
- Claiming true loops need “Petri nets with reset arcs” is false. POWL’s `Operator.LOOP` is the intended construct.
- Because `checks` precedes `decision`, **both the auto and emergency XOR children still require `Quality_Review` before the approval/activation choice.** The auto branch then requires quality *again* after activate. The model therefore does not implement a low-risk route with deferred quality, nor an emergency route with provisional activation before quality — two of the explicit requirements.
- The same `Transition` objects (`sanctions`, `quality`) are reused in `loop`, `checks`, and `auto_branch`. That is an unsafe PM4Py construction and produces duplicated/shared nodes rather than alternative routes.
- `SilentTransition(label="tau_loop")` is an awkward API use and does not create a back-edge.
- Time, amount, eligibility, and identity are correctly declared out of POWL, and the overlay/data proposal (stable `notification_id`, rule/version IDs, eligibility snapshots, alias table, effective vs record time) is the strongest part of §7.

**Net.** A structured, on-format review with several correct case calls, undermined by a fact error on FD-7/SUP-704, a self-contradiction on SUP-705, a defense that will not admit misconduct, a CS-9 scope error on rejection, and a revised POWL that does not mean what the prose claims. Under a hypercritical bar that reserves high scores for near-flawless work, this is mid-pack, not high.
