**6.0**

The write-up is complete, well structured, and hits the hard constraints (non-stationary demand, calendars, rework, cross-case dependence, objections as unobserved). It is not close to flawless. Several technical errors and methodological shortcuts are serious enough to keep it well below a high score.

**What works.** The eight required sections are present and on-topic. The twin is framed as a resource-constrained DES rather than independent case replay. Architect bursts, district batching, shared pools, and calendar blocks are first-class. Objections are treated as a missing process and proxied through the public-notice tail, not as logged events. Service and waiting are not silently collapsed. The experiment table includes baseline, D1–D10, D10 combined, and three stress cases. The code loads the log with pandas, uses `pm4py.format_dataframe` with the requested keys, and keeps PM4Py analysis separate from engine stubs.

**Process-mining and PM4Py errors (largest single deduction).** The process-tree handling is wrong. PM4Py’s `parse_process_tree` already uses `->`, `+`, `X`, `*`, and `tau`. The given string is already in that family. Replacing those operators with `SEQUENCE`/`AND`/`XOR`/`LOOP` invents a false limitation and would break a working parse. The real limitations of the string—loop semantics vs. appeal/rework reasons, XOR legal check vs. council-gated work, AND of reviews vs. “draft waits for every applicable result,” missing fire-area and environmental-sensitivity predicates—are not explained. The return type `pm4py.objects.petri.tree` does not exist; `from pm4py.objects.petri.net import PetriNet` is the old path. `pm4py.discover_variants` is not the stable public API (`get_variants` is). Unused and incorrect imports make the skeleton look more faithful than it is.

**Estimation and service/wait split.** “Idle pool ⇒ duration is service, busy pool ⇒ duration is wait+service” is not identified from completion-only stamps without a circular busy-state model. The plan never says how idle/busy would be inferred, how interval-censored or multi-server time would be handled, or how median/p90 tables would be turned into fitted distributions (MLE vs. quantile matching). Fire-area thresholds and incomplete environmental sensitivity are named in the brief and then dropped. Effect sizes later treated as known (40% fewer missing-doc loops, 30–50% travel cut) are not estimated, elicited, or put on a prior.

**Simulation methodology.** A 60-day warm-up is stated without reconciling it to seasonal non-stationarity: discard window, start month, and peak vs. trough occupancy are not specified. Truncating durations at p99 while optimizing p90/SLA tails is inconsistent and can hide the Complex-variance/council tail that the log summary flags. There is no common-random-numbers or paired-replication design for D1–D10, which is the standard way to compare policies under shared demand. Fifty replications is asserted, not justified for heavy-tailed p90s. Sensitivity stays OAT/Morris; intervention effect sizes are not themselves factors.

**Validation and decisions.** “Replay with fixed seed, case-duration correlation > 0.85” confuses trace-driven replay with a stochastic generative twin; individual cases are not pairable once service draws are random. KS > 0.15 and MAPE < 15% are arbitrary cutoffs. Cross-case mechanisms cannot be validated from a flattened log; that limit is underplayed. Decision thresholds (Gini ≤ 0.25, 10% duration gain, 70–85% utilization) are invented. Applicant effort (D1, D7) and intervention cost/FTE (D4, D10) are missing even though those policies exist to trade municipal time for applicant or budget cost. The no-optimality caveat is correct and should have been matched by treating D1–D10 magnitudes as uncertain.

**Code skeleton.** Modular placeholders are acceptable. Empty `pass` methods, a `SimulationRunner` that does not take an experiment configuration, and no wiring of D1–D10 into routing/calendars/batching leave the “experiment execution” requirement thin. Duration is computed as inter-event time only; service/waiting columns are not actually produced, which is defensible given missing starts but should have been explicit column-level output with a quality flag.

Net: a competent digital-twin outline with the right scope, and enough PM4Py, queueing, and uncertainty mistakes that it cannot be scored as strong.
