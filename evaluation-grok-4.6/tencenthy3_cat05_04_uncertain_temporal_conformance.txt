Rounding endpoints (`59.999` vs half-open minute, nearest-10s ties) are not specified tightly. `duration_interval` is documented as `B−A` while computing `A−B`. The analytical core would sit near the high end if Section 5 were complete and INDETERMINATE were applied where evidence, not just the derived interval, is the issue. As written, it is a strong interval memo with a missing implementation and a softer-than-required evidential taxonomy.
