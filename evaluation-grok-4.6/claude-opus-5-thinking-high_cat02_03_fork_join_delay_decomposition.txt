7.4

The required seven-section skeleton is followed, the additive identity is the right one, and the case table plus the four worked examples are numerically correct. Fork-join logic is handled as a maximum, synchronization waiting is correctly treated as slack rather than elapsed time, and the factor-of-three double-counting argument is stated cleanly. Influence is ranked by last-completing frequency, cycle time, slack, attributable margin, and tail behavior rather than mean service, and the A-as-floor versus B-as-current-constraint distinction is the right structural reading of this log.

Those strengths do not make the write-up nearly flawless. Several claims are wrong or internally inconsistent.

The F10 pooling example is false. F1 is free from 10:40 to 11:10, but F10 is not join-ready until 11:20, after which F1 is occupied by F07 and then F09 for the entire F10 wait. F10 never had a free alternate server. The companion F11 claim is overstated in the same way: F2 is idle for only ten of F11’s twenty downstream minutes, not the whole wait. In §4, CF3a is described as “9 cases unaffected, and two cases get worse,” which cannot be true at once; seven cases are unchanged, two are harmed, three improve. The CF1+CF2 interaction paragraph then misattributes F11’s extra join movement to unlocking B, and “recovers 122 of a theoretical 120-minute join headroom” mixes realized flow-time spillover with unique join margin.

The downstream simulator is fitted to reproduce the baseline by construction (`Lag_c` is a residual). That is disclosed, but the prose still treats 122, 204, and similar figures as if they were identified effects rather than outputs of an unidentified handover model. Utilization and server-count remarks lean on peak concurrency as if it were capacity. A few table cells are incomplete or loosely worded (A’s instantaneous-join column; “buying a third of the final station’s capacity”).

The Python block matches the spec, including the PM4Py interval log and the correct comment that a directly-follows performance view cannot recover AND-join slack. Section 7 is strong on what the timestamps cannot identify. The remaining defects are avoidable factual and bookkeeping errors in an otherwise careful decomposition, which is why the score is well below the near-flawless band.
