**6.4**

The write-up is well structured, generally respects event-time roles, does not treat repeated pairing as proof of collusion, and gets several hard calls right (Eva is not QM on CHG-507; `b.koch`/`u_bk` is one human on CHG-508; D-IC-04 is void; CHG-503 is identity opacity, not a proven RR2 breach). It is not close to flawless. Material factual misses, an internal contradiction, and checking code that is unlikely to run as written keep the score well below the top band.

**Factual miss that changes the identity picture.** Ben’s certified sick leave is 2026-08-13 12:00 through 2026-08-14 23:59. CHG-506 is flagged. CHG-505 `Retrospective_QM_Review` at 2026-08-14 16:00 by `u_bk` is not. That is a second use of the same account inside the same absence window, on a HIGH-risk emergency QM review that is already late and likely out of D-QM-01 scope. Treating sick-leave conflict as a one-case anomaly understates the credential/session problem and weakens §3, §4, and the investigation ranking in §7.

**Delegation logic is inconsistent.** RR3’s absence condition applies *when required by the delegation*. D-QM-01 and D-QM-02 state it; D-IC-03 does not. The matrix invents “principal must be unavailable for CI-44,” the code sets `principal_abs_required=True` for D-IC-03 (and `principal_absent(Diego)` is false on a weekday while Diego is implementing minutes later), yet the case table still treats Farid’s CHG-505 approval as a valid IC substitution. Narrative and executable logic do not agree. CI-44 linkage for CHG-505/510 is assumed more firmly than the log supports.

**CHG-504 is over-certified.** Asha’s standing QM authority can survive leave, but the prompt states security cannot exclude credential sharing, and the VPN window is short. Primary AUTHORIZED is defensible; “High” confidence on the actor is not. Identity should have been a secondary INDETERMINATE/DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM.

**Narrower authority defects are underplayed.** D-QM-01 is “QM *approval* for *production-equipment* change controls.” Using it for `Retrospective_QM_Review` on `cooling_system` (CHG-505, CHG-510) is a stretch on both activity and asset. Scope is caveated; the approval-versus-review mismatch is not. Weekend-as-absence for D-QM-02 and “two business days” from implementation date are reasonable but presented with more certainty than the policy text warrants. CHG-501 as high-confidence AUTHORIZED also glosses that the QMS row for D-QM-01 did not exist until 12 Aug (the PDF argument belongs in the case row, not only in §2).

**PM4Py/pandas block does not match the claim of executable, high-quality checks.**

- `filter_four_eyes_principle` takes two activity strings, not a list; `resource_key="human"` is not the documented interface. Alias normalization therefore may never reach the four-eyes filter; CHG-508 is exactly the case that would then be missed.
- `format_dataframe` is not reliably specified with `resource_key`; `org:resource` is never overwritten with the normalized human.
- Naive vs timezone-aware timestamps, `ts.tz_convert` on possibly naive values, and unparsed `"true"`/`"false"` strings can throw or treat every case as emergency (`"false"` is truthy).
- The promised second SoD pass on `svc_attributed_human` is described and not implemented. Absence/credential flags, invalid subdelegation, and “QM *or* emergency IC” approval are computed incompletely or omitted from `flags`. Emergency HIGH approvals would be marked `high_risk_missing_qm_or_delegate` even when RR4 is the intended path.
- `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is called and unused. Networks are printed, not interpreted.

That is below the bar the prompt set for PM4Py/pandas quality.

**What still holds.** Alias policy, service-account non-identity, later-DB-entry vs signed PDF, RR1 on CHG-502/508/510, RR3 on CHG-507, void subdelegation on CHG-510, CHG-505 review treated as late under a standard business-day count, and §4’s paired benign/adverse tests are the strongest parts. §6 maps onto the required control themes. §7’s three-way split (authority failure vs identity/data vs pattern risk) is directionally right but incomplete once CHG-505’s sick-leave review is included.

Highest-priority investigations should have been CHG-510, **both** Ben-account uses in the sick-leave window (506 and 505 retro), and `svc_qms` attribution—not 506 alone.
