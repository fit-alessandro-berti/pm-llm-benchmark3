That is not enough to carry the piece. The core deliverable is case judgment from **timing, coverage, and independence**. That is exactly where the writeup is sloppy.

These are not nits. They are the facts the rubric says to reason from.

- **AP-201.** The table says AV-2 “passed after release (cannot prevent).” The log is Prepare 08:00 → Automated_Validate 08:03 → Release 08:06. AV-2 ran **before** release. Calling preventive controls “None” is therefore false. AV-2 did not restore four-eyes, but it was a live preventive control.
- **AP-203.** The answer says audit at 15:00 occurred **before** settlement at 12:00, and even “25 hours post-release but before settlement.” Settlement is 12:00; audit is 15:00 **the same day**, ~5 hours after release and **after** settlement. Corrective capacity is overstated. The OBJECTIVE_MET label can still be defended on ED-1 (manager before settlement; audit within one business day), but the timing story used to justify it is backwards.
- **AP-204.** Audit is described as “eight days after release.” Release is 2026-05-04 11:05; audit is 2026-05-08 09:00 — about **four** days, not eight.
- **AP-202.** The log has no `Bank_Settlement`. “Settled approximately 2.5 hours later” is invented. Post-settlement manager approval is a fair inference from the case facts; the 2.5-hour figure is not.

The answer first says it fails, then says it “may be borderline satisfied,” in a broken clause (“8 months  240 days”). - **AP-209.** “39 payments together in 30 days (up from 37)” is fabricated. The source gives 37 for the pair in AP-204 and does not increment it for AP-209.

### Classification problems
The prompt forbids mechanically mapping distinct users to “met” and same-user to “failed.” The answer mostly avoids that, then overcorrects on **AP-209**. Formal four-eyes holds, AV-2 passes, audit is timely, **no defect is found**, and the prompt explicitly says the u12/u13 pattern may be a dedicated cell with **missing design documents**. That is INDETERMINATE or at most OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET. Labeling it OBJECTIVE_FAILED treats a realized fabricated-receipt failure (AP-204) as the same class as an unresolved independence concern. The text even says the failure is only “probable,” then emits a binary fail.

AP-206 as PARTIALLY_MET is defensible; INDETERMINATE would have been cleaner given the token-before-prepare ambiguity the answer itself flags.

Section 5 invents precise fraud probabilities and expected losses (40%, EUR 192,000, etc.) without labeling them illustrative, against the prompt. Mixing ex-ante “~40%” with a case that already lost the money is confused.
