That is a four-object-type cascade (Job, Tool, Batch, Shipment), with CNC-2 and OVEN-1 as further participants. The 42 min idle *while FIX-A is held* is good evidence that capacity was not binding in that window. Inflating that to 1 h 50 min of fixture delay is conjecture presented with too much precision. The 5 min 11:40→11:45 handoff is the only tight fixture-adjacent fact.
