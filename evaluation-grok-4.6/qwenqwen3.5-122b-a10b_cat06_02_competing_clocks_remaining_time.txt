**5.3**

The write-up has the right *shape* (six required sections, ranges rather than a fake point forecast, a join `max`, weekend pauses, some path split, some dependence talk). Under a strict reading it is still not a reliable forecast. Several of the quantities that matter most are internally inconsistent or calendar-wrong.

**Dominant-clock error.** The quality bar is that microbiology incubation plus the staffing calendar should dominate the *smooth* path, with chemistry treated as a competing spillover. The answer concludes the opposite (“Chemistry is the dominant driver… not Microbiology”) and then makes S3 identical to S1. That is not a defensible reduction. Primary incubation is ready only Sunday 12:00–20:00; Saturday QA (09:00–13:00) is already closed by then; Sunday has no QA. The mass of remaining time is weekend dead time, after which the two branches *race* on Monday morning. Calling chemistry the long pole, and treating “no weekend readout” as a distinct scenario with the same clock, inverts the temporal decomposition.

**Queueing mistakes (chemistry).** Remaining work on *two* analysts (roughly 1.6–2.9 h of work content) is added as if it were elapsed time, then added again to an urgent job to get “5 h → 20:30 Friday.” That ignores parallelism. Elapsed time to first free analyst is 0.4–0.9 h; to both free, 1.2–2.0 h. Under risk-priority the first free server should take the *urgent* sample, not B-417; B-417 can start only when the second analyst frees (~16:42–17:30). Service is at least 1.5 h, so B-417 **cannot finish Friday** (this is correct as a conclusion, wrong as a derivation). The FIFO sensitivity note (“+5–8 h, Tuesday”) is also hand-waved: two servers on Monday still start B-417 the same calendar morning, only later that morning.

**Queueing mistakes (QA) — the largest numerical error.** The live QA queue of five is frozen until Monday. That discards Friday 15:30–20:00 (QA, not chemistry, closes at 20:00) and Saturday 09:00–13:00: about 8.5 reviewer-hours that will drain a large part of the current queue and some arrivals. The answer also uses a 2.5 h Friday window (chemistry close) for arrivals, under-counts openings, and ignores that two reviewers already work Monday 08:00 until the *future* join. The “7 ahead → 5.25 h wait → start 15:45” path is therefore not a central estimate; it is a one-sided congestion scenario. Overdispersion is named and then replaced by `0.35 × hours`.

**Required microbiology-confirmation path is missing.** The brief asked for a delayed-readout *or confirmation* scenario. S3 only restates “no on-call, Monday 08:00 readout.” The 0.05–0.12 contamination branch (+20–30 h continuous incubation + second readout) is never staged. That path is where weekend authorization actually moves the join by about a day (Sunday indication vs Monday indication). Declaring S3’s tail “same as S1” is therefore false for the scenario the table claims to cover.

**Calendar illegalities.** Section 6 says authorization can move release to “Sunday night.” Electronic release follows QA acceptance; QA is closed Sunday. Saturday QA is also unusable because incubation is not ready until Sunday. The assumption ledger never records this, and the earliest envelope still sits at Monday 14:00 with a heavy Monday queue — not a true earliest (short leftover chemistry Monday ~08:30, short QA after Fri/Sat drain, release late Monday morning).

**Forecast table does not add up.** Remaining hours and clock stamps disagree.

| Stated band | Stated clock | Actual hours from Fri 15:30 |
|---|---|---|
| 74–86 h central | Mon 18:00 – Tue 10:00 | 74.5 h – **90.5 h** |
| 96–110 h P80 | Wed 10:00 – Thu 10:00 | **114.5 h – 138.5 h** |
| 168–240 h P90 | “Following Friday” | 168 h is next Friday 15:30; 240 h is the following Monday |

P80 hours describe roughly Tuesday afternoon to Wednesday pre-dawn, not Wednesday–Thursday. That is not “approximate arithmetic”; it is an inconsistent deliverable.

**Scenario algebra is confused.** S3 is labeled 25–35% (the *authorization* band) while the prose says no-auth probability 0.65. No-auth is 0.55–0.75. S1 (35–45%) plus S3 (25–35%) double-counts essentially the same Monday join. S4’s 10–20% does not sit cleanly on the given query (0.15–0.30) and investigation (0.04–0.10 clean / 0.30–0.55 dirty) rates. Overlap is mentioned, not used.

**Dependence is asserted, not propagated.** The ledger’s jump to “rerun 25%, contamination 20%” is a point guess with no stated uplift from 0.10–0.20 and 0.05–0.12. The prompt’s investigation lift to 0.30–0.55 when rerun / contamination / confirmed excursion exists is almost unused. There is no joint “excursion confirmed → clustered lab failure → lab investigation” tail, which is the mechanism that should populate the 3–7 calendar-day P90, not a generic S4 blob.

**Internal contradictions.** Weekend authorization is ranked the #1 uncertainty driver after the stage model says it does not change the terminal time. Section 6 then correctly treats excursion confirmation as more forecast-changing than authorization — so sections 5 and 6 disagree. “Log-normal QA service” is listed and never used (only the 1.5 h median appears). Clock dates (21–25 Sep 2026) are omitted despite an explicit request for dates/times.

**What is actually sound.** Parallel join as a `max`; no-overtime default; electronic release as a short continuous tail; rejection kept rare absent a confirmed critical defect; FIFO vs priority as a named policy uncertainty; documentation that probabilities need not sum to 100%; recognition that a later observation (excursion flag) induces cross-branch dependence. Those keep the answer well above a mean-adding fail. They do not offset the queue drain error, the missing confirmation path, the Sunday-release claim, or the broken hour/clock table.

Net: structured and directionally process-aware, but not defensible as a queueing-aware, calendar-correct remaining-time forecast. **5.3.**
