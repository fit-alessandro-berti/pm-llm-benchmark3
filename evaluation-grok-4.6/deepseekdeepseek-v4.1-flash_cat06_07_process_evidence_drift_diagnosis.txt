**7.6**

The answer is a strong, correctly structured audit: it uses the six required sections, keeps April / June / Aug–Sep / October as distinct failure modes, and does not treat October’s 0.16 observed breach rate as comparable to mature months. The main diagnoses match the evidence. It is not nearly flawless.

**What is right.** April is correctly treated as a schema/pipeline break (unknown 17%, missing queue 14%, unmapped 18%, AUROC 0.67, ECE 0.11, mean predicted 0.28 vs observed 0.19) with mature labels, so the drop is not label delay. June is correctly a routing/mix shift (V2 34%→52%) with stable within-variant rates, not automatic concept drift. August is supplier/V3 performance; September adds Partner Z. October is dominated by 55% maturity, 52-day delay, and long-path outcome-API backlog. Predictive metrics are read against DFG edges, variant mix, and maturity rather than as a single drift label. Falsifiers and staged remediation (freeze Apr/Oct, repair then recalibrate, retrain only on mature clean cohorts) are proportionate. Alert dependencies correctly stop a label-delay alarm from triggering retraining.

**Material conceptual slip.** July/August “concept drift” is over-claimed. A jump in V3 breach as `Order Part → Part Received` goes 42h→65h→130h is first process-performance / omitted-covariate change. True concept drift is a change in \(P(y \mid \text{path}, \text{delay})\). The table later asks for that test and marks confidence medium, but Section 2 still states August as “process/performance plus concept drift” as if both were established. That blurs the taxonomy the task is grading.

**Missing triangulation.** There is no mix decomposition. August \(0.25\cdot0.18 + 0.40\cdot0.11 + 0.35\cdot0.64 \approx 0.31\) vs observed 0.28, and September \(\approx 0.34\) vs observed 0.31, would show that V3 rate + V3 share explain the headline rates and that residual immaturity biases observed breach *down*. The answer never says Aug–Sep observed rates are lower bounds. October’s 52-day median delay is also among the 55% already labeled (fast paths); that selection is not called out.

**Other defects.** October is filed under both data-pipeline failure and label delay; the outcome-API backlog is a labeling problem, and double-counting muddies the table. “No major outcome-definition change” is unused as a negative finding. Contradicting-evidence cells often qualify rather than contradict. Recalibration is underspecified (no method, no maturity threshold). The code sketch hits the required PM4Py calls and the two cohorts, but `filter_time_range` has no `mode` (event-calendar vs case-contained traces), a bare `except Exception` swallows the temporal-features call, diagnosis is remapped to underscored names while other activities keep spaces, and `is_unknown` may not survive `format_dataframe`.

Those are not fatal, but they are enough that this is a good audit with several real gaps, not a near-flawless one.
