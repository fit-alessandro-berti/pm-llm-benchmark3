**6.2**

The write-up is structured correctly, avoids mechanical four-eyes scoring, and gets the hardest case distinctions mostly right. It is not close to flawless. Several analytical slips, one internal contradiction, and a Section 7 program that would not do what the prompt required keep the score well below the top band.

**What holds up.** Same-user cases are not treated as a single class. AP-203 and AP-207 are rightly treated as successful ED-1 uses (token before prepare, amount under EUR 100,000, AV-2 clean, independent manager before settlement, timely audit). AP-202 and AP-208 are correctly failed. AP-205 is correctly `LOSS_PREVENTED_BEFORE_COMPLETION` rather than a completed-payment verdict. AP-201 is reasonably `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET`: unjustified convenience, but AV-2 passed, m04 is independent, and ratification is before the stated 10:00 settlement. Sections 1–2 cover threat scenarios, control limits (especially AV-2’s coverage), and why equal expected loss is not equivalence. Section 6 separates alerts from proof and hits the required monitoring topics.

**Case-analysis defects.** AP-208’s narrative omits the clearest ED-1 breach: EUR 900,000 is far above the EUR 100,000 cap. Bank-change, AV-2 outage, post-settlement ratification, and late audit are listed; the amount limit is not. AP-204 as only `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET` understates a completed payment with a fabricated receipt; the four-eyes *form* held, the prevention objective did not. “Corrective action taken externally” is not in the facts. AP-209 is scored the same as AP-204 despite no defect and a timely audit; a pattern flag is not the same as a failed package on that case. AP-206 is defensible as partial, but the facts explicitly leave token timing as logging delay versus precondition failure, which is the one place `INDETERMINATE` is the more honest label. Section 4 contradicts itself: MG-4 after release and before settlement is first called detective, then the same act is declared preventive “by chosen semantics.”

**Section 7 fails the spec.** `pm4py.format_dataframe` is called with `activity_id` and `activity_resource_key`; the real parameter is `activity_key`, and there is no resource argument of that name. `format_dataframe` returns a DataFrame, which is then iterated as if it were an event log (`for trace in log` / `trace[0][...]`). `filter_four_eyes_principle` is described as returning *violators*; in PM4Py it keeps traces that *satisfy* four-eyes. Violating and blocked case sets are never joined to the facts table or printed, so requirements 3–5 are not actually delivered. The facts frame codes AP-201 AV-2 as not a full pass. `emergency_eligible` hard-codes vendor tenure as `True` and would mark AP-206 eligible even though the token is after prepare. `av_coverage` maps every non-pass to “Warning/Override,” including the block and the outage.

Strong control reasoning with several avoidable errors and a non-executable, polarity-wrong checking script. That is a mid-6, not an 8–10.
