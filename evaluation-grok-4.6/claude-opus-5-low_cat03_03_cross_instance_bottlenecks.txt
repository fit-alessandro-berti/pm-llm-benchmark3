**Calculation and wording errors (Section 2).** The required FIX-A calculation disagrees with itself. The author first treats 1 h 50 min (09:50–11:40) as fixture-attributable and offers a second-fixture start at ≈09:55. Immediately afterward, CNC-2 is idle only 10:05–10:47 (42 min), leaving ~73 min “unexplained by both machine and tool records.” Those cannot both be right. Fixture occupancy already covers almost the entire wait; the 73 min is the complement of *recorded machine idle*, not a residual after jointly applying machine and tool evidence. The 09:55 counterfactual also ignores that CNC-2 is not known to be free before 10:05. This is the most serious flaw in a section that otherwise does the arithmetic correctly (B1 3 h 55 / 3 h 40 / 10 min; J6 16:05→17:25 = 1 h 20; S1 13:30→19:25 = 5 h 55; S2/S3 25/35 min).
