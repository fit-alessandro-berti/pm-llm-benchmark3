`E[Benefit] = (p_adj × Value) − swarm cost − false-alarm burden`.  
That is expected loss *as if the swarm removed the entire breach probability*. The prompt’s quantity is `ARR × Value − cost − false-alarm burden`, and it explicitly warns that `p` is observational and is not the intervention effect. Using `p_adj` in place of ARR is the central error the rubric flags. Calibration offsets are then applied to the wrong object: shrinking Strategic `p` does not identify a smaller causal effect, and inflating SME `p` does not identify a larger one.
