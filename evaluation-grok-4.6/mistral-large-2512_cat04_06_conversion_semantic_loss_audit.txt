**4.0**

The submission has the right skeleton: nine required headings, an invariant table, two pipelines, try/except around the tree path, and a visual-vs-semantic distinction. That is the only reason it is not lower. Under a strict reading it is not close to a working audit. It invents APIs the prompt forbade, reports “validated preserved” without executing anything, and gets the source language wrong in the witnesses that are supposed to ground the rest.

**Section 1 is already semantically sloppy.** I11 treats a Petri marking as “contains only Archive Change.” Finality is a marking (and proper completion), not a last activity name. I3 marks optionality as unobservable after hiding τ; the silent skip is unobservable, but presence vs. absence of Legal Review is exactly what remains visible. I2’s negative witness is a repeated Assess Risk, which tests occurrence count, not absence of order. I9’s “positive” fragments never show the AND-join: one side can be missing and the row still looks satisfied. The interesting source fact — Legal Review may start after Assess Risk *while Prepare Evidence is still running*, because there is no `prepare_evidence → optional_legal` edge — is never stated as its own invariant.

**Section 2 names the required pipelines and then stops.** “May fail due to non-block-structured behavior” is a slogan, not an analysis. This POWL is not `seq(Register, AND(Assess, Prepare), AND(XOR(Legal, τ), LOOP(...)), Release, Archive)`. Legal is enabled by Assess alone; verification is enabled by Assess *and* Prepare; Release waits for both branches. That overlapping partial order is why a faithful process tree should fail or over-constrain. The table never says that, so a successful tree conversion would not be recognized as the more dangerous outcome.

**Section 3 violates explicit constraints.** Markings from BPMN→net are unpacked and used with no `None` checks. Workflow-net is run only on `net_from_powl`, not on every accepting net. `save_vis_powl` is omitted. Unused object/visualizer imports pad the listing. The code is a conversion sketch, not the required diagnostic pipeline.

**Section 4 is methodologically false.** Almost every POWL→Petri and BPMN-round-trip cell is `validated preserved`. Nothing was run. Tree cells are `conversion failed` by assumption. BPMN round-trip does not preserve place identities or exact `Marking` objects; claiming exact initial/final markings as validated is almost certainly wrong even if behavior is close. Petri→BPMN is `validated preserved` for the join and for I4, while I5 is only `ambiguous`. That misses the real loss mode: a block-structuring BPMN encoding can delay Legal Review until after Prepare Evidence and still look “sound.” True concurrency vs. interleaving is mentioned, then immediately over-credited as validated on the net side.

**Section 5 is the decisive failure.** `pm4py.get_marking_after_transition` and `pm4py.check_is_possible_trace` are not in the allowed API; the prompt said not to invent them. `convert_to_reachability_graph` is called without `fm`. Playout is imported from a dubious path and never used. Footprints appear only in §3 and are printed, not compared.

The explorer is not the specified algorithm:
- `loop_count` increments on *every* τ, not on verification iterations. POWL→Petri encodings insert many routing silents; `max_loop_iterations=3` can abort before a single complete visible trace.
- Visible redo (`Correct Defect`) does not count as a loop iteration.
- Bound `max_visible_length=10` excludes the authors’ own 11-event second witness (Legal + one defect cycle).
- `fm1 == fm2` across independently constructed nets is not a semantic test.

Worst: a listed *positive* witness is
`[Register Change, Assess Risk, skip_legal, Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, Release Change, Archive Change]`.
`skip_legal` is not a visible label, and Prepare Evidence is missing, so this is an I4 violation sold as a legal trace. Any checker that accepts it is wrong; any checker that rejects it will be read as a conversion bug. That single row collapses the witness discipline.

The closing claim of exhaustiveness under the bound is therefore false on its own terms.

**Section 6 is the strongest part and still contains a hard error.** Parallel-gateway, XOR, loop, silent-route, and layout caveats are the right topics. Then the Release join is described as “completion of either branch.” That is XOR-join language for an AND-join. A stakeholder doc that repeated this sentence would teach the opposite of I9.

**Section 7 mixes real reject rules with a contradiction of the task.** Unsoundness, lost interleaving, premature release, collapsed optionality, zero-iteration loop, and silent livelocks belong. Criterion 12 (quarantine because process-tree conversion failed) is exactly what the prompt said is an acceptable, even correct, secondary outcome. Exact-final-marking rejection, as they implemented it, will fire on every honest BPMN round trip. The actual high-value reject — Legal Review no longer concurrent with Prepare Evidence — is absent.

**Section 8 is directionally right (POWL stored, derivatives regenerated) but incomplete.** It never cleanly names BPMN as the stakeholder visualization artifact or states what evidence must travel with it. Petri net as the executable semantic shadow of POWL is not considered.

**Section 9 is padded.** Bounded non-exhaustiveness, layout misread, and tool-specific BPMN rendering are real residual claims. Performance, “future PM4Py versions,” and generic silent-visualization worry do not substitute for the claims their tests cannot settle: language equivalence beyond the bound, true-concurrency vs. interleaving, τ-structure differences with the same visible language, and whether a “successful” tree/BPMN encoding over-serialized the Assess/Prepare/Legal diamond.

The piece looks complete and is not a serious conversion audit. It fails the prompt’s two non-negotiables: do not invent APIs, and do not treat a returned object (or an unrun table of “validated preserved”) as fidelity.
