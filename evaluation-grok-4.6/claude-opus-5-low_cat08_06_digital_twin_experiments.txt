**7.3**

This is a strong, professionally structured design that hits every required section and respects the hard constraints (cross-case coupling, calendars, no silent wait/service collapse, latent objections, no optimality claim, no `pm4py.read_csv`). Under a strict rubric it is still well short of near-flawless: several methodological and implementation errors would matter in a real twin.

**What holds up.** Scope and state are the right grain: activity-level DES with explicit `(enqueue, start, complete)`, concurrent `+` marking, applicant delays as infinite servers, and five real couplings (architect clusters, inspection bundles, neighborhood shocks, calendar freezes, shared pools). The Monday clerk withdrawal against the Monday arrival spike is correctly treated as a first-order interaction, as are the fire Tue–Thu sawtooth and legal council-week outages. Service vs waiting is handled honestly: waiting is an output; gaps are not dumped into service; calendar conversion before fitting is specified. Objections stay latent and D9 is framed as value-of-information. The experiment matrix is the right shape (baseline, D1–D10, combinations, stress), with sub-additivity of D10 and error-cost frontiers for D2/D8. Validation is bidirectional and effect-size-based rather than p-value theater. The skeleton’s Part A/B split, process-tree limitations, and per-component RNG streams are the right architecture.

**Material flaws.**

1. **The runner does not execute the matrix it defines.** `Experiment` carries `demand_multiplier` and `resource_overrides`, but `ExperimentRunner.run` constructs `PermitSimulator(params, exp.policy, ss)` and never applies those fields. S1/S2 in `main()` are therefore inert. That is a coherence failure, not a missing `raise NotImplementedError`.

2. **Parameter uncertainty is nested incorrectly.** `params = self.params_posterior[rep % len(...)]` confounds Monte Carlo noise with posterior draws. It does not produce the credible intervals promised in §5.4. A nested or properly crossed design is required; this shortcut would misstate ranking stability.

3. **Arrival estimation can double-count bursts.** A negative-binomial GLM is fit to *aggregate* daily counts *and* an additive Neyman–Scott top-20 process is layered on. Over-dispersion would be absorbed twice unless the GLM is estimated on the residual (non-burst) stream. That is not specified.

4. **The given tree is a poor conformance instrument as written.** `'Completeness rework'` and `'Inspection rework'` do not appear in the log. Alignments against this tree will systematically penalize every rework case. The write-up notes redo-as-activity semantics but never says the redo labels should be `tau` (or mapped) before `conformance_diagnostics_alignments`. Planning-review rework, legal rework, and applicant appeal—explicitly in the brief—remain underspecified in the executable model.

5. **Stated computational design is not credible.** 3.5-year runs × 100–300 replications for tails × ~200 posterior draws × a large intervention/stress set is not budgeted. Sequential precision and a 2⁴ fraction are mentioned, then contradicted by S6 + heavy tail replication advice. Without a staged budget (screen → refine → posterior on a shortlist), the uncertainty program is aspirational.

6. **PM4Py surface is slightly sloppy.** §2 uses `get_case_arrival_avg` and `get_case_duration`; the code uses `get_case_arrival_average` and `get_all_case_durations`. Only the latter pair matches current public PM4Py. Claiming Part B has “no PM4Py import dependency” is false in a single module that `import pm4py` at the top.

**Smaller but real issues.** Snapshot WIP *plus* a 6-month Welch truncation fights itself: a real open-case snapshot already removes most initialization bias; discarding half a year after that wastes the snapshot. Public-notice is sequential in the tree but treated as a join obligation in `pending_reviews`. B1 assumes implicit batching already exists, which blurs the as-is baseline against D3 (an intervention). “2 FTE equivalent, Tuesday–Thursday only” is read as two people on three days without defending that reading. “Weibull shape < 1” is not a clean model of a heavy *right* tail. Invented SLA/utilization cutoffs (Simple ≤ 15d, 70–85% utilization, district p90 ratio ≤ 1.25) are presented as decision thresholds without statutory or empirical anchoring; D3/D5 travel may already violate 1.25. Extra top-level “Closing caveat” violates the required exact heading set. Environmental reservation is called “equivalently 1.8 FTE” and then correctly denied as equivalent—contradictory wording.

**Net.** Simulation reasoning, PM grounding, experiment structure, and uncertainty *intent* are well above a typical answer. Strictly, the unused stress knobs, broken posterior nesting, burst double-counting, unusable rework labels for replay, and an uncosted uncertainty stack keep it out of the 8–10 band.
