**4.0**

The write-up has the right skeleton and some real process-mining instincts (status semantics, resource-feasible bounds, censoring vs.\ elapsed service, three-way sensitivity). Under a strict reading it is not close to competent execution. Core quantities are wrong often enough that the “reconstruction” cannot be trusted.

**Data integrity (severe).** P05’s S1 start is recorded as **09:00**; P11’s S1 start is **10:40**. Only S2 starts are missing on those cases. The answer treats both S1 starts as missing, invents a P05 S1 window of 08:55–09:00, and then encodes P05 in Python as `s1_start = 08:25` (wait 0). That is not uncertainty handling; it is misreading the table and then contradicting itself. Every downstream P05/P11 S1 range, and the claim that four S1 starts must be imputed, is invalid.

**Case-level waits are systematically confused with resource idle gaps.** Reported S2 wait is `s2_start − s1_end` (or `14:00 − s1_end` if still waiting). The answer often substitutes the gap after the *previous job on the same calibrator*:

| case | correct S2 wait | stated |
|---|---:|---:|
| P02 | 5 | 20 |
| P06 | 25 | 5 |
| P08 | 55 | 75 |
| P10 | 50 | 35 |
| P12 | 45 | 15 |
| P13 | 50 | 10 |
| P14 | 65 | 5 |
| P18 | 50 (censored) | 85 |
| P19 | 35 (censored) | 85 |
| P20 | 15 (censored) | 85 |

P18–P20 are a copy-paste of P16’s 85 min. That is not a rounding issue. It is the difference between queue wait and setup/idle time — a basic competence failure.

**Station metrics do not use timestamps.** Arrivals 08:00–10:00 are P01–P13/P14 (13–14 cases), not “10 (P01–P10)”. Only **P22** arrives after 12:00, not “P17–P22”. S1 completions 12:00–14:00 include P15 and P16 (six, not three). Arrival/departure rates by subperiod are therefore not estimated.

**Busy time / utilization are biased low and then over-interpreted.** Feasible S1 busy time in-window is about **575–605 / 720 ≈ 80–84%** (counting P21’s 30 min elapsed, not a fictional finish). S2 is about **555–570 / 705 ≈ 79–81%** after the 15 min C2 outage. The answer’s 70–78% vs 65–70% is undercounted by roughly an hour of work on S2 and is not a real contrast. Utilization was also not built from a resource timeline; it is a rounded guess.

**Required analyses are missing or only promised.** Little’s Law is defined, then never computed in any interval. There is no event-driven queue/WIP path, only a few peak/mean remarks, so the mandatory simultaneity convention is unused. Case-level **total sojourn** for completed cases is never tabulated (`s2_end − arrival` runs 60 → 230 min and is the cleanest congestion signal in the file).

**Bottleneck claim is too sharp for the evidence, and partly an artifact of the wait errors.** Morning S1 queue growth is real. By 14:00 the snapshot is 2 cases still in S1 vs **6 in S2**. Correct afternoon S2 waits are 45–75 min on completed/open work, plus a long P16 wait. That is a time-varying mix, not “S1 dominant, S2 capacity-adequate.” The C2 outage effect is also understated: P08 has been ready since 10:20; if C2 were free after P06 at 10:50, start would move by ~25 min, not “10–15.”

**Sensitivity section is theatre.** Optimistic/central/pessimistic labels are fine in principle, but they rest on invented S1 missingness and on the same wrong waits. The Python “scenarios” subtract the case *number* from a dictionary, then leave `# not real; replaced below` in place. Nothing in the code reconstructs resource windows, WIP, or the printed tables.

**Python fails the spec in substance.** The in-memory table is not the supplied table (P05). Status parsing is only half-done; usable-interval filtering is reasonable, but there is no real station-level pandas calculation of queue/WIP/uncertainty. Required PM4Py insertions sit in `try/except` and may print nothing. `insert_case_service_waiting_time` / `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` are not demonstrated on a clearly valid complete-case projection with labeled numeric output.

**What is actually good (why this is not a 1–2).** Headings match. A1–A8 state rounding, single-task resources, no bypass, and OPEN vs MISSING vs blank correctly in words. P07/P15 S1 bounds are in the right neighborhood *if* one accepts a 20 min `s_min`. Open work is not dropped. The C2 gap after P06 is correctly tied to maintenance. The three-scenario *idea* and the warning that late Little’s Law is indefensible are sound.

Those are framing virtues. The numerical reconstruction, the S2 wait definition, the rate table, utilization, bottleneck timing, and the reproducible code are not reliable. Under the stated rubric that is a low-4, not a mid-range pass.
