**3.0**

The write-up has the required six headings and some of the right vocabulary (parallel join, calendar pause, overlapping scenarios, dependence). Substantively it is a failed forecast. The central clock is wrong by about a day, several paths schedule work when the relevant resource is closed, and the four “materially different” scenarios collapse into the same invented weekend window.

**Fatal clock error.** Primary incubation is 46–54 h after Friday 14:00. That is **Sunday 12:00–Sunday 20:00**, not Saturday. Friday 14:00 + 46 h is Sunday noon; Saturday 18:00 is only 28 h later. Every smooth-path, on-call, join, and P50 number is built on that arithmetic mistake. Weekend on-call cannot fire on Saturday because the result is not technically ready. Without authorization, readout starts Monday 08:00. With authorization, readout is Sunday afternoon/evening; **QA is closed Sunday**, so a usable join for review is still Monday 08:00. Saturday evening release is not an early tail. It is process-illegal on the given calendars.

**Chemistry queue and Friday close are mishandled.** At 15:30 there are two jobs in service (0.4–2.0 h left) and **four waiting samples**, with triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) h service and 2.5 h until 18:00. The answer adds the two in-service remainders, drops the four queued jobs, invents a 1.0 h wait, and declares chemistry done Friday 18:00. Its own 1.0 + 2.5 = 3.5 h from 15:30 is **19:00**, after close; unfinished work must pause until Monday 08:00. Finishing Friday requires an aggressive priority start *and* a near-minimum service time in a ~1.5–2 h residual window. That is a thin exception, not the base case. The rerun scenario then has chemistry finishing **Saturday 12:00** in a lab that does not work weekends.

**Scenario table is not a scenario table.** All four rows share essentially the same central band (Fri 15:30–Sun 18:00) and tail (Mon 12:00–Tue 08:00). A range that starts at “now” is not a terminal-time range. Earliest times are incoherent with the scenario names (a “delayed micro” earliest of Sat 18:00; a query that “starts Fri 15:30” before either branch has a result). Bands of 20–30% are assigned to a Saturday smooth path whose probability is ~0. Overlaps are mentioned but not identified. Temperature-excursion dependence is named in the ledger and then unused: no joint uplift of rerun, contamination, and investigation.

**Stage decomposition violates the process.** QA review cannot start Friday 15:30 or Saturday 18:00. Saturday QA exists only 09:00–13:00 and is useless because micro is not ready until Sunday. “Sat 26:00” is not a time. Treating QA service as triangular(1.5, 1.5, 3.5) is invented. Future QA queue uses `0.35/h × 2 days ≈ 13` as if arrivals run around the clock; arrivals are only during QA hours, and Friday evening plus Saturday capacity can also drain the current five. FIFO vs risk-priority is never turned into a wait-time envelope. Contamination (+20–30 h continuous) and laboratory investigations (3–7 calendar days) are absent from the paths. Rejection is never considered.

**Forecast summary is internally inconsistent and about two days too early.** “P50: 18–24 h (Sat 18:00–Sun 18:00)” does not add up (18–24 h from Fri 15:30 is Saturday morning to Sunday afternoon; Sat 18:00–Sun 18:00 is ~26–50 h). A defensible envelope is roughly: earliest **Monday morning** (~64–68 h) if chemistry finished or is short Monday and QA is free; central **Monday afternoon to Tuesday** once chemistry spillover, Monday readout, and QA congestion are taken as the max, not the sum; P80 into mid-week with query/rerun/contamination; P90/severe into a multi-day laboratory investigation, especially if the temperature flag confirms and the anomalies are treated as dependent. Most plausible terminal event is **Electronic Release on Monday–Tuesday**, not Saturday 23:00.

**Uncertainty ranking and next-event section are only half-right.** Staffing and dependence belong on the list; chemistry *starting* Friday, weekend authorization, and the temperature flag are more important than “incubation completion before Sat 18:00,” which the 46 h minimum forbids. Secondary trigger (queue discipline) is reasonable but secondary to the excursion flag or an on-call decision.

**What little credit is due:** section skeleton; refusal to publish a single point estimate; note that probabilities need not sum to 100%; qualitative sense that microbiology/staffing matter and that a max, not a sum, governs the join. That is format and vocabulary, not a defensible remaining-time argument. Under the stated bar (hypercritical; high scores only if nearly flawless), this is a structured miss of the dominant calendar constraint.
