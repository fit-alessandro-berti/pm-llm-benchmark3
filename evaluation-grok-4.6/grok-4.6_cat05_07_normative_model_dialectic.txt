**7.3**

The brief is unusually strong on dialectic, authority, and case-level discipline. It is not nearly flawless. The required executable model is not executable, and it fails the very pilot trace the opinion treats as the clean justified exception.

**What is actually excellent.** Sections 1–3 do the job the prompt asked for. Stipulated facts, disputed propositions, and unproved claims are separated with unusual care. The burden rule is the right one: in-scope, time-effective, written overlay or demonstrated artifact; informal practice does not mint authority; missing linkage is indeterminate unless the missing field *is* the control. Prosecution and defense are both argued at full strength, and both sides are forced to concede what they must. The prosecutor does not pretend SUP-707, CRM missingness, rejects, reactivations, or label drift are crimes. The defense does not launder SUP-703, SUP-708, alias four-eyes, or post-expiry continuation into “the model is old.” That is real adversarial work, not a split-the-difference average.

The matrix is complete, and the classifications are mostly the ones a careful reviewer should reach: 701 compliant; 703/708 severe; 704 justified; 705/714 unauthorized continuation; 706 indeterminate; 707/709/710/713 model or scope; 712 a resource-rule fail that M0 cannot see. System-level judgment is also right: operations are not globally collapsed, M0 is not a valid exclusive KPI, and CS-9’s residual core still has to be enforced.

**Fatal problem in the required artifact.** `standard_route.nodes[0]` will not run. In PM4Py, `StrictPartialOrder.nodes` is a set. Sets are not subscriptable. The intended edge is obvious (`rmi_star() → decision_std`), so this is carelessness, but the prompt asked for one executable block. A one-line crash in the only code deliverable is not a cosmetic miss.

**Material POWL error relative to the opinion’s own best case.** M1 puts `Quality_Review` *inside* `auto_route` and then orders `routes → notify → close`. That forces `Activate_Supplier ≺ Quality_Review ≺ Notify_Requester`. SUP-702 is classified JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION at high confidence on the basis of a CRM notify at +2 hours and quality the next business day — i.e., `Activate ≺ Notify ≺ Quality`. Once that evidence is admitted, 702 still does not fit M1. The model therefore fails the flagship eligible-pilot case it was rewritten to accept. The same mistake is theoretically wrong: on the auto/emergency paths, notification follows the *decision*, while delayed quality is a residual control. Those should not be serialized in POWL. Two-business-day quality already belongs in the pandas/DECLARE layer the brief itself defines. This is a process-aware modeling error, not a taste difference.

**Burden-of-proof inconsistency on SUP-702.** Section 1 lists automated master-data validation as unproved and says a justified exception requires evidence that residual controls actually ran. PD-3 requires that validation before activation. 702 still gets JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION / High. The matrix notes the missing control and then does not let it reduce confidence or threaten the class. That is the brief violating its own standard. Medium-high, or justified-but-incomplete-evidence, would have been coherent. High is not.

**Smaller but real slips.** The nine June–July auto-approvals with missing eligibility attributes are exactly the case Section 1 says should not default to innocence on an exception path; the system judgment then talks mainly about the 14 clear ineligibles and under-decides the nine. `Master_Data_Validation` is never even introduced as a labeled activity on the auto arm, despite being a PD-3 residual. Withdrawals are scoped in prose and then omitted from M1. SUP-715 as MIXED is analytically honest, but the disclosure duty failed on the record; the class still reads like a hedge. The EUR 180k / FD-7 cap comparison for a non-FD-7 case is rhetoric, not evidence.

**What does not get a pass under a strict reading.** The architecture essay in §6 is the right layered design (POWL for shape, DECLARE for the sanctions-before-activate backbone, pandas for predicates/windows/amounts, identity graph for SoD/COI, an overlay register for pilots and emergencies). The data proposal — stable `notification_id`, eligibility snapshots, alias/delegation, overlay_id, immutable effective vs record time — is what this log actually needs. None of that rescues an un-runnable snippet or a revised model that still cannot replay 702 under the facts the author accepted.

Net: an A-minus legal/process opinion attached to a B-minus model. Under a hypercritical rubric that is a 7.3, not a high 8 or 9.
