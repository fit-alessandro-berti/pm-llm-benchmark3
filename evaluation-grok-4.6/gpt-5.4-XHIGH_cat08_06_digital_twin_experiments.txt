**7.2**

A strong, professionally structured digital-twin design that meets the required section contract and is clearly not a naïve independent-case replay. It still has enough API, routing, identification, and design–code coherence problems that it is not near-flawless under a strict reading.

**What holds up.** The boundary is right: municipal DES plus exogenous applicant/notice timers, not a flattened log replay. State is specified at case, pool, and cross-case levels (architect bursts, notice cohorts, inspection bundles, council/holiday flags). Calendars are first-class. Missing starts are not silently turned into service times. Objections are treated as a latent state proxied by notice duration, not as observed events. Arrivals are non-homogeneous and clustered. Decision drafting is correctly framed as an AND-join on applicable predecessors. The experiment matrix has baseline, D1–D10, extra packages, and three stresses with real trade-offs. Warm-up is seasonal rather than fake steady-state; CRN, stream splitting, percentile CIs, and heavy-tail handling are appropriate. Validation covers face, replay, distributions, utilization ranges, bottlenecks, and a holdout. The write-up refuses optimality and uses a Pareto/guardrail rule. The skeleton loads with pandas, uses `format_dataframe` on the required keys, and keeps PM4Py upstream of a separate engine.

**Process-mining / PM4Py faults.** `pm4py.get_variants_as_tuples` is not a reliable public entry point; `pm4py.get_variants` is. That call is unguarded, so a required artifact can crash. `pm4py.get_case_arrival_average` is stored as `case_arrival_average_hours` without conversion, while the fallback *does* convert to hours — a unit inconsistency. The process-tree string is a label-mismatched prior (`Completeness rework` / `Inspection rework` are not log activities); the text says so, but routing never reconciles loop semantics with observed repeats. Performance DFG is computed (good) after a correct warning not to treat edge times as service; the code still surfaces it as if it were a timing estimate.

**Logical gaps in the twin.** `RoutingModel.initial_plan` is a linear checklist, not the AND/XOR/loop structure argued in §1, so parallelism and the completeness loop are not actually represented. Legal check is forced only for Commercial/Complex (or a flag); the excerpt and tree make legal optional for *all* types, and P001 (New residential) has a legal check. D2 is implemented with `prior_violations=False` by default, so every eligible simple case would skip inspection — the opposite of the 5/10/20% prevalence the matrix treats as material. Appeal is in scope in prose and absent as an activity, probability, or calendar interaction. `estimate_external_delays` inner-merges request/receive without pairing instances; multi-loop cases explode into a Cartesian product.

**Calendars and resources are underspecified in the artifact that would run.** Monday clerk portal diversion, the two Simple-only planners, council-week legal blocking, and environmental 60% availability are stated in §1 and then mostly omitted or inert in code: `effective_capacity` is stored and never applied in `is_open`. Inspectors own both scheduling and site work despite a distinct `scheduler_1` in the log. Public-notice’s 14-day statutory minimum is never encoded.

**Estimation and experiments are good, not tight.** The missing-start identification problem is acknowledged via sensitivity, but there is no real strategy for separating queueing, calendar off-shift, and service when starts are MNAR-by-activity. Objection length is a confounded proxy (other delays also stretch notice). “Low/base/high” could be OFAT, a 3³ grid, or three composite settings — the compute budget is undefined. No ranking-and-selection or multiplicity control for 10+ policies. SLA cutoffs (20/60/90/180 days) and the 10%/15%/5pp/2pp gates are invented; the caveat is present, but those numbers then drive selection as if they were policy.

**Skeleton quality.** Modular and closer to PM4Py conventions than a typical draft, but not internally faithful to the design it accompanies. Placeholders are allowed; contradictions and unofficial APIs are not.

Net: high-quality simulation reasoning with several avoidable inaccuracies. Not a 8–10 under a hypercritical bar.
