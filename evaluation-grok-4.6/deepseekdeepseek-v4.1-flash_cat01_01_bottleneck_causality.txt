**7.2**

The answer is strong on the core causal and process-mining requirements: it detects the crude-versus-stratified reversal, refuses to treat stratification as identification, separates queue wait from review service, treats `resource_group` as a routing bundle rather than a clean treatment, and recommends a learning-safe alternative to a 50% reassignment. Format compliance is mostly complete. It is not nearly flawless.

The most important gap is that Section 1 never explicitly distinguishes **total, direct, and mediated** interpretations of an Orion effect, even though the prompt listed that as a minimum requirement. Case mix, wait, and compliance appear descriptively, but the executive verdict does not say what would count as a total effect of assignment, a direct group effect after holding mechanisms fixed, or a mediated effect through queueing, compliance, or rework. Later tables imply this, which is not the same as stating it.

Section 4 has two clarity problems. The standardized contrast is given as “2.51 h” with no signed estimand, after a crude contrast that was Orion-minus-Nova and positive. The “causal interpretation allowed” column often restates prohibitions rather than naming the claim that *is* licensed. Day versus night is only a crude split; there is no group-by-shift comparison despite Nova having four night cases. That is the natural next check for the shift/load story the rest of the memo emphasizes.

The DAG is usable but conceptually muddy: `case_completed -> cycle_time` is selection/observation, not a process mechanism, and several group-to-outcome arrows are assumed without being marked in the list itself. The Python block is diagnostic and reproduces the required contrasts, overlap cells, variant counts, and DFG comparison, but it fabricates timestamps and wraps PM4Py in a fallback instead of parsing traces in the form the prompt described. Those are not fatal, but they are real defects under a strict reading.

Hypotheses, falsification tests, and the phased intervention plan are the best parts: they include a harmful Orion effect, a beneficial effect masked by mix, shift/load, compliance/rework mediation, and selection/measurement, each with a predicted observation, plus both a restricted routing pilot and a queue/compliance-capacity intervention. Residual uncertainties are specific and actionable.

Net: a competent, well-structured assessment with solid arithmetic and the right operational recommendation, pulled down by the missing total/direct/mediated statement in the executive section, several table-level ambiguities, a thin shift decomposition, and a slightly loose DAG/code implementation.
