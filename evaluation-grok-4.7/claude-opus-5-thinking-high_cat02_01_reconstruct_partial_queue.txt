The reconstruction is technically serious and most of its audited arithmetic holds, but it is not nearly flawless. Several headline figures are wrong, one causal story is contradicted by the same log, and two reported uncertainty ranges disagree with the answer’s own scenarios.

What holds up. Missing-start windows are the right ones: P07 on I1 is [09:25, 10:00] with central 09:30; P15 on I1 is [11:40, 12:15] with central 11:45; P05 on C1 is [09:45, 10:15] with central 09:50; P11 on C2 is [12:00, 12:25] with central 12:05. Central waits and services for the fully observed cases match the timestamps. Busy time is I1 305, I2 290, C1 295, C2 265, so full-window utilization is about 83% and 79% on 720 and 705 available server-minutes. Queue integrals match the case sums: 1490 S1 wait-minutes (mean 4.14) and 780 S2 wait-minutes (mean 2.17). System WIP mean 9.51 and peak 13 are correct, as are the 14:00 stock (8) and the core means (S1 queue 5.53, S1 WIP 7.22, S2 queue 2.53, S2 WIP 4.22, system WIP 11.44). The S2 queue is empty for 50 minutes, all before 09:10. The Little’s Law rows that were checked are arithmetically right, including the 10:00–12:00 identity mean Lq = 5.875 and L/λ = 100.7. Open cases are kept as elapsed service, censored waits are kept as lower bounds, and the drain after 12:10 is read correctly: S1 queue 6→1 with I2 idle from 13:45, S2 queue 3→4 with both calibrators busy. That is the right bottleneck evidence. Early/central/late imputation totals in §6 also match the feasible endpoints.

Errors that should not be in a result this careful.

- S1 median wait is 75 among the 21 uncensored waits (77.5 if P22’s censored 110 is included). §4 states 85.
- Fully observed S2 service is 25–50, not 30–50. P10’s 25 is in the case table and then omitted from the station summary and from bottleneck evidence point 1. The same paragraph halves service into “14.1 and 20.0 server-minutes” per case. A case occupies one server for the whole service, so the consumption is about 28 and 40 server-minutes. The 1.4× ratio survives; the unit does not.
- P08’s S2 wait of 55 is not the largest fully observed S2 wait. P14 is 65, P17 is 65, and P15 is 75. P10 is not the only I2→C1 case; P12 and P14 are too. The I1→C1 / I2→C2 map does not flip permanently at P10: P17 is I1→C1. P09 starting before P08 is one inversion; P17 starting at 13:55 while P16, queued since 12:35, is still waiting is a second. “The only apparent FIFO violation” is false, and the paired-subqueue hypothesis does not cover the log.
- §4’s S1 “imputation-hard band 68–88%” is I1’s band (245/360 to 315/360). The station band in §6 is 74.3–84.0%. The reported imputation uncertainty is about twice the scenario spread. S2’s 73–81% does match the station.
- The 08:00–12:10 and 12:10–14:00 rate rows are not a partition. 5.3/h is 22 arrivals over 250 minutes, which includes P22 at 12:10; 0.5/h is that same arrival counted again, in a row labeled “no arrivals.” Inclusive of 12:10, the drain departure rate is not 2.7/h unless P10 is counted twice as well. Overload above roughly 3.6 and 2.7 cases/h still stands at about 5.0–5.3/h; the printed split does not.
- Smaller false notes: P05’s service cap of 30 is not “strictly shorter than C1’s other jobs (30–45)” (P01 is 30 and P10 is 25); during 10:55–11:10 the S2 queue is 2 or 3, not three throughout; P12–P14 queue shares are about 63%, 62%, and 70%, not 63–70%. “10/10” and “5/6” handover counts are not a clean description of the gaps. Calling a sojourn-versus-arrival slope above 1 “proof” overstates a rough 1.4 fit; growth of sojourn is the overload signal.

Stable conclusions that survive those corrections: both stations are saturated after 09:10, S2 service is longer than S1 service in every non-degenerate imputation, the C2 outage is visible and not recovered inside the window, and after arrivals stop S2 is the station that keeps filling. The unstable list in §6 is otherwise well drawn, especially P11’s 0–25 minute cap, the ±3 to ±5 point utilization band, and the censoring gap between an S2 mean wait of 39 elapsed and about 48 projected.
