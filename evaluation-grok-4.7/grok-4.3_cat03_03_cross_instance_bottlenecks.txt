4.8

The write-up has the required eight-part scaffold, concrete object IDs, and the right instinct not to crown machine capacity. The core quantitative and causal claims are nevertheless unreliable, and several of those errors sit inside the calculations and mechanisms the task made mandatory.

The B1 figures 3 h 55 min, 3 h 40 min, and 10 min match the table, and 19:25 − 13:30 = 5 h 55 min for J1 in S1 is arithmetically right. The counterfactuals are not. Starting B1 at 08:10 is infeasible: J2 is ready only at 08:25 and J3 at 11:55. That counterfactual cannot drive both J1 and J2 to zero, and it never splits partner-wait (until 11:55) from the 10 min launch lag. The FIX-A claim is worse. CNC-2 is documented idle only from 10:05 to 10:47 (42 min). J4 arrived at 09:50, while the machine was still busy, and nothing says the machine stayed free from 10:47 until the 11:45 start. Treating 11:45 − 10:05 = 1 h 40 min as the fixture delay drops the availability assumption and overstates the evidenced hold. The CAL-3 counterfactual is simply false: J6 cannot start at 16:10, because J5 holds TEST-1 and CAL-3 until 16:35. Recalibration-plus-handover is 16:35–17:25 (50 min), not the 1 h 15 min obtained by erasing J5’s test. Section 6 then cites 42 min and 50 min, contradicting Section 2.

RACK-7 is mishandled in the same way. Cleaning ends at 14:35; J5 is ready at 14:40; B2 starts at 14:50. The rack is free before the last batch member is ready, so it does not bind B2’s start, and 14:35–14:50 is after cleaning, not the cleaning delay. That mistaken block is used as support for the top-ranked mechanism and as the benefit of intervention 5. The largest measured delay, J1’s 5 h 55 min shipment wait, is ranked fourth with no comparison to the smaller batch and tool numbers and no argument that it is mostly downstream of J4. J3’s own path is ignored as a mediator: J3 releases FIX-A at 11:40 and is batch-ready at 11:55, so much of J1/J2’s “batch” wait may be propagated fixture time. J4’s 13:10–14:50 B2 wait and the unexplained 14:50–19:10 stretch before the 19:10 pack are never calculated, so the S1 delay is treated as pure consolidation policy.

The coupling the question asks for is therefore missing. The live chain is L7 holding J5, J5 holding B2, B2 holding J4, S1 holding the already packed J1, with FIX-A on J3/J4 and CAL-3 on J5/J6 as further ties, plus structural but untimed links through L8 and S2/S3. The three narratives stay local timelines. Narrative 3 does not clearly span four object types, FIFO is stated as policy rather than speculation, and the four required epistemic layers are blended into single sentences. Motif 2 is only Job–ComponentLot, so the “three motifs of more than two types” requirement is not met. Rank 6 shifts columns, and “no machine exceeds 1.8” misstates a job-duration statistic. Falsifier 6 (overrides remove the waits) would support batch synchronization, not weaken it. The logging and flattening remarks do note the lot-link inconsistency and the median Load→Dispatch illusion, but “omits” overclaims a Pack→Dispatch edge that exists for all six jobs, and low utilization is explained only as “non-machine objects,” not as off-machine queue time plus one cure event covering three jobs. The PM4Py block names the required functions, yet the interaction-count comparison is exactly the frequency-versus-impact mistake the interaction table warns against.
