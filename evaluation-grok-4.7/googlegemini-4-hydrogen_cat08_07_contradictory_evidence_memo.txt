7.5

The memo reaches the right decision—segmented hybrid deployment, no national scale-up, no full rollback—and it covers every required section with usable trade-offs, a real evidence split, and decision-linked measurement priorities. It is not nearly flawless.

The clearest error is in the fitness/precision/generalization explanation. Low precision is correctly tied to an overly permissive model. Low generalization is then blamed on that same permissiveness. Those are opposite model faults: a flower-like model is permissive (low precision, usually high generalization); low generalization means overfitting. The evidence-table assumption that generalization 0.56 measures “regional instability” or an “unrepresentative sample” repeats the misunderstanding. Baseline 0.87 to pilot 0.94 is also treated as if the miner loosened the model in order to hit 0.94.

Epistemic control slips in the narrative. Segment deltas are observational, attributes are missing before week 3, regions differ from baseline, and costs after week 4 are missing. The memo still says the pilot “proved” a bimodal effect and that within-stratum deterioration “proves” it is not mix. Later text calls simple-house gains “verified” and the merged path “validated.” The evidence table is more careful than the recommendation that rests on it.

Smaller logic and attribution gaps: 93% utilization is generalized beyond the two regions stated; a queueing-theory cliff at 93% is asserted without arrival or service evidence; rural harm is paused as a survey-skip problem when the note points to rescheduling; VOI rank mixes decision value with implementation sequence, leaving the randomized skip test too low relative to the decision it would change; rollout gates such as precision 0.72, billing-before-activation under 0.5%, and overall FTR at 82% are arbitrary, and overall FTR is a weak expansion test while most volume remains on the old process. “Doubled” for 7% to 15% manual activation is slightly loose. Pod backup is adopted as if it were already shown to preserve the handover gain.

Facts, segment math, DFG edges, alignments, and the A–E risk framing are otherwise accurate and the action list matches the constraint set. The grade is held down by the process-mining mistake and by treating confounded associations as proof.
