7.5

The stage arithmetic is unusually careful and mostly exact: flag-specific medians, the 16.51 vs 8.17 means, the +8.34 gap, and the occurrence-versus-duration split all recompute from the timestamps. The causal framing is also the right one. The flag is treated as a bundle, natural direct and indirect effects are rejected for cross-world dependence, exposure-induced confounding, interference, and no positivity, and P0–P4 are separated as different component interventions.

Those strengths do not make the answer nearly flawless.

The senior-queue claim is a timestamp error. R13 is ready at 07:36 and starts escalation at 16:36; R20 is ready at 09:12 and starts at 15:12. R20 overtakes R13. R06 likewise starts at 00:00 ahead of R01, who was ready at 18:42. Calling that “roughly FIFO,” and using the R20–R13 pair as the evidence, reverses what the clocks show.

The required PM4Py code misstates its own performance DFG. `discover_performance_dfg` is called on completion timestamps only, with no start-timestamp key. On the usual API those edge values are floats, so `perf.get("median")` fails, and even a successful run would be completion-to-completion time, including the next activity’s service. The comment says the opposite: completion of one activity to the start of the next. Absent stages are correctly omitted, and the explicit intervals are the reliable part.

The pilot does not respect the durations just measured. Review and escalation waits are about 7 hours and flagged cycle time is about 16 hours, but the design alternates 6-hour blocks with a 2-hour washout. Downstream work will routinely spill into the next regime, so the block contrast is not a sustained P2 effect. The primary endpoint, mean cycle time of all arrivals, is also diluted by a narrow waiver (near-cutoff, medium complexity only). The non-inferiority margin is unnamed.

Smaller identification problems remain. Senior service is compared only among escalated cases, so that negative control is conditioned on a post-treatment mediator. The encouragement “exclusion” check conditions on being reviewed anyway. E3 writes a discretionary mediator draw as `Y(P0, …)`, and E6 calls an individual controlled direct effect well-defined despite the shared queues emphasized elsewhere. Section 9’s “case mix explains part of the gap: within-stratum differences are +4.6 to +7 h” points at the residual within-complexity gaps, not at the portion complexity accounts for. “100% of unflagged high-complexity cases are reviewed” is 3/3, not a policy law, and “the triage gain is real” is stronger than the T1/T2 confounding allows.
