8.3

The models are largely faithful, the approximation is explicit, and the eight verdicts are right. The score is held down by witness-level inaccuracies and a few places where the prose overclaims precision.

The POWL model correctly encodes the non-block structure: containment and analysis are independent after registration; planning depends only on containment; design, the risk XOR, and the supplier XOR depend only on analysis; the trial loop waits only for planning and design; risk and supplier stay unordered with the trial and join only at release; archive follows release. The loop shape `body (redo body)*` with `Execute → Inspect` and redo `Adjust Parameters` matches the repeat-until reading, including “at least one trial” and “no adjustment after the exiting inspection.” The process tree is a real conservative under-approximation of that partial order, not a relabeling of it: the pretrial parallel join forces the risk route and the supplier choice to finish before `Execute Trial`. Parents are set, labels are not statically duplicated, and the lost overlap is stated and then shown.

The witness verdicts hold. A1 and A5 fit both models. A2–A4 are intended-allowed and POWL-allowed, and they are exactly the traces the tree must reject. F1 breaks `Contain Material → Plan Verification`. F2 releases before the low-risk review. F3 exits the loop on the redo. Low risk, high risk, supplier consultation, a two-iteration trial, and the tree approximation are all present.

Deductions:

- F1 is not a cross-branch violation. `Plan Verification` before `Contain Material` is the containment-stream precedence. The cross-stream release join is F2, but the write-up attaches the cross-branch claim to the wrong row. No forbidden trace isolates the other cross-branch join, `Execute Trial` before design or before planning.
- A3’s reason is false on the event order. `Consult Supplier` falls between `Execute Trial` and `Inspect Trial`; `Supervisor Review` falls after `Inspect Trial`. The supervisor event is after the trial cycle, so it does not “complete during the trial interval.” The verdicts survive; the justification does not.
- The concurrency argument does not cover every incomparable pair it claims to cover. `Contain Material` is unordered with design, the risk route, and supplier consultation, and those pairs are never named.
- A4 confounds two facts. It is the only two-iteration trace, and the tree rejects it because the supervisor review sits inside the second cycle. Nothing in the table shows that the tree itself accepts `Execute, Inspect, Adjust, Execute, Inspect` when the approval route is already finished.
- Assumption 7 treats completion precedence as a local modeling compromise. The problem already fixes an activity-based, completion-time semantics, so this is a shared limit of both models, not an extra tree defect.

The validation plan is appropriately non-circular: conversion, soundness, bounded playout, the eight traces, silent-skip and duplicate-label checks, and an explicit statement that soundness is not fidelity. Residual uncertainty is the untested constructor/parent behavior of `OperatorPOWL` and `StrictPartialOrder` across PM4Py versions, which the self-critique names but does not close.
