7.5

The conservative design is largely right: only e03/e04 and e11/e16 are merged, e05 and e14 are kept, abstraction and harmonization collisions stay separate, and the 17→15 event and 44→39 E2O counts follow. Ordering policy, provenance fields, and the eight required failure modes are all present and mostly sound.

The score is held down by implementation and analysis gaps. The Python block mixes prompt columns (`eid`, `timestamp`) with PM4Py names (`ocel:eid`, `oid`), so the `pm4py.ocel_*` calls are not actually wired to a PM4Py OCEL. `ocel_drop_duplicates` is invoked with no exact-duplicate criterion, and `ocel_merge_duplicates` is the unguarded whole-log call, which conflicts with the guarded “about 12 / 32” scenario. OTG is treated like E2O; the M1–M2 co-occurrence created by merging e13 with e14 is missed. OC-DFG, ET–OT weights, and lifecycle/performance effects are named more than analyzed, and invoice-before-authorization plus release-before-acceptance are stated as domain constraints the facts do not give.
