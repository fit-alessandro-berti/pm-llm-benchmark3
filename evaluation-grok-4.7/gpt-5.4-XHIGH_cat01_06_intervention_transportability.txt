7.3

The response meets the required structure and reaches the right decision: conditional adoption, limited to cases where document checking is legally free to start, with Site A’s 9-hour median treated as confounded. The comparison, staged partial order, bottleneck-shift scenario, and stop rules are substantively strong. The score is held down by internal inconsistencies and loose quantitative claims.

The transport graph omits any path from `treatment_parallelization` or `true_overlap` to `rework`, while Sections 4, 5, and 9 treat extra rework and supplement loops as effects of parallelization. `cycle_time -> appeal` is asserted without a mechanism. Those gaps matter because the graph is supposed to be the causal object that later sections use.

In the evidence table, “transport confidence” changes meaning by row. Outcome rows use low scores for unidentified effects (median cycle time at 25). Co-intervention rows use high scores (added reviewers 95, marketing mix 95, API upgrade 90) even where the text says the Site A delta does not carry. A reader using the column as specified will misread confounders as highly transportable.

The scenario table does not keep “eligible share” as a share: the IT-lock row reports realized overlap, and the legal/rework row mixes 38% and 40%. The only explicit critical-path bound is `min(3.4h, 8.1h) = 3.4h`, but the digital-only scenario then assigns eligible cases a 3–6 hour gain, importing Site A’s confounded 6-hour waiting drop. Overall p90 improvement of up to 3 hours from treating the easiest 20–25% is weakly supported, because Site B’s heavy tail is tied to originals, legal review, and loops. The 20–25% share is also unreconciled with the joint rate of no-originals, no-legal, and lower complexity. The directly-follows counts, including Originals Receipt → Document Check = 2,108 = 62% of 3,400, are unused.

The pilot rebundles queue huddles and WIP caps into the treated package. Control is ordinary processing, so the design repeats the Site A confounding it diagnoses. Team-week assignment is paired with a case-count target of 150 per arm and no cluster adjustment. At roughly 12% joint eligibility, 10–12 weeks does not reliably produce that support. Section 6 holds legal-review cases for Stage 3; Section 7 treats “no originals plus legal flag” as an initial treatment stratum.

Smaller faults: the six-task cap is placed in Site A “after,” although the source lists it as standing context and lists only huddles as the new queue practice. The playout call uses `no_traces`; PM4Py’s parameter is `noTraces`, so the limit is ignored. `Sequence` and `Operator` are imported from `pm4py.objects.powl.obj`, and edges are set with `add_edge`, which will fail on versions where the order graph is `order.add_edge` and `Operator` lives in `process_tree.obj`. The POWL dependencies themselves are the right ones: assessment is a join, originals stay in front of document checking, and the legal supplement loop returns through document checking and assessment.
