7.5

The write-up is strong on structure, capacity arithmetic, and the specialization–pooling trade-off, but it is not nearly flawless. Section 5 overclaims relative to Section 4, and the simulator contradicts several rules it claims to implement.

The load model is mostly auditable and correct. Activity means (Intake 6.94, Mechanical 26.30, Electrical 32.82, Software 38.50, Validation 15.74), 42 arrivals, 2,720.6 base work-minutes, 2,490 regular minutes, and 2,670 minutes with full overtime are right, so the proof that base load is at least 109.3% of regular capacity and 101.9% even with overtime holds. Block capacities, U1’s 1.7 morning scaling, U2 interpretation 2A (+40% / +42%, about 642.5 and 930.3 minutes), Chen’s absence, and the post-18:00 Mechanical blackout are also right. Parallel versus sequential server-minutes for ME and ES are computed correctly, and mean complex-tail ES on Chen or Emil really does exceed 120 minutes before waiting.

Deductions:

- U4 block totals are sloppy (about 483.2, 638.0, 495.2, 709.0, 427.6, and 209.3, not 484.3, 638.9, 495.2, 708.8, 427.1, and 208.1). The daily total 2,962.4 is right only because the errors cancel.
- Section 4’s SLA bands overlap, but Section 5 still says hybrid wins every scenario by +3 to +8 P1 points and +12 to +38 versus specialization. Base P1 is 92–96% versus 88–93% for full pooling, and base P2 is 84–90% versus 80–87%. Those ranges do not support a strict ranking. The quoted gaps do not match the table’s own endpoints.
- Those bands are not produced by the code. They are hand ranges, then treated as results. Uncertainty is labeled, but the dominance claim is stronger than the evidence.
- Assumption A3 says handover does not consume server time. `start_task` still sets `busy = True` at assignment, so the resource is blocked through the 7- and 5-minute delays. Utilization math later tries to subtract that overlap. Interpretation A is not what the DES does.
- A preempted task keeps `pred_res`, and `started_once` is never used, so resuming the same resource charges handover again.
- Stated rules missing or different in code: P1-Spec team-restricted Intake/Validation (code pools them), the 60-minute shock hysteresis, P4’s 15-minute overtime cap, Deepa’s targeted 18:00–18:30 overtime, and Intake/Validation tiers. `allow_ot = (policy != "P1_Spec") or True` is always true.
- `discover_handover_of_work_network(...).connections` is version-fragile; a NetworkX return crashes the audit after the comparison print.

What keeps this above a mid score is real process-mining substance: infeasible same-day 90% utilization, bottleneck migration from morning E/S to the 16:00 cliff and the Mechanical blackout, P1 parallelization versus P2/P3 sequential retention, two explicit objectives, auditable dispatch rules, PM4Py caveats on pre-Intake wait, conflated waiting time, and false handover edges from parallel branches, plus a P1 circuit-breaker in the pilot.
