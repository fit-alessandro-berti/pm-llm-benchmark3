8.2

The POWL model is exact and the process tree is a sound, clearly labeled conservative under-approximation. The witnesses, dependency table, and comparison are internally consistent, and the forbidden traces correctly name the earliest broken condition. This is not nearly flawless because the approximation defense is overstated.

The POWL order matches the narrative: `CM` only before `PV`, `ARC` before `DCA`, the risk route, and optional supplier; the trial joins `PV` and `DCA`; approvals and supplier stay unordered with the loop and with each other; release joins the loop, the selected route, and the supplier choice. Nested `XOR(SR, unordered QR‖RR)`, `XOR(CS, τ)`, and `LOOP(ET→IT, AP)` are the right control-flow stand-ins for the hidden data. The duplicate-free tree’s barrier after both preparation branches preserves safety and trial/approval overlap, and it does exclude the early-approval behavior shown in T3 and T4. T1–T8 have the right intended, tree, and POWL verdicts, including the cross-branch failure in T6.

The main semantic error is the claim that duplicated labels still could not express an unbounded trial loop overlapping the cross-branch activities, and the later claim that avoiding duplication is “precisely why” the overconstraint was required. Each approval and the supplier occur at most once. An XOR of early versus late copies, with each late copy in parallel with one shared loop, can cover the trace language, including a review before planning and the other review inside a later iteration. What duplication cannot preserve is one N-shaped pomset. Given that the answer itself treats observable behavior as interleavings, calling duplication unfaithful is too strong. Complexity and ambiguous alignments would have been a valid reason to reject it.

Smaller issues: “100% precision” is informal; assumption 7’s exception wording is easy to misread; section 4 justifies concurrency of operator nodes but does not explicitly extend that argument to inner events such as `SR` between `ET` and `IT`, even though T1 depends on that shuffle. The construction was not executed, as the answer admits, so library-level parent or conversion quirks remain unchecked.
