4.5

The document has the required nine sections, a usable pipeline split, and a sound storage choice (POWL authoritative, BPMN derivative). Those merits do not survive the semantic and validation errors below. Several of them would manufacture false equivalence.

**Baseline witnesses are mislabeled.** INV-02 puts a legal order, `Register → Prepare Evidence → Assess Risk`, in the negative-witness cell and then says it is valid. INV-03’s negative witness is the allowed silent skip, written as if `skip_legal` were a visible label, while the same row claims the invariant is invisible after hiding tau. INV-10’s positive trace places Legal Review before Prepare Evidence, so the verification body has not started; it does not witness overlap of the legal branch with the loop. The binary-loop exit is missing: `Inspect Trial` may be followed directly by `Release Change`, with no `Correct Defect`. A converter that makes the redo mandatory is not ruled out by the table.

**“Validated preserved” is asserted with no run.** The audit uses that term for almost every POWL→Petri cell on the strength of unexecuted claims, including “the initial marking enables only Register.” POWL→Petri encodings ordinarily enable a silent source transition first; Register is first only in the visible language. Petri→BPMN for INV-10 is marked `lost` because a layout might look sequential. Layout is not the round-tripped language. That collapses the distinction the task requires.

**The process-tree diagnosis is wrong.** The loop body is already a sequence, and `Operator.LOOP` is block-structured. `Operator.PARALLEL` does express concurrency. What is not a process tree is the asymmetric partial order: Assess Risk enables the legal XOR and, together with Prepare Evidence, the loop, while Release joins both. Section 2 blames “non-block-structured loops,” and section 8 says process trees cannot represent true concurrency.

**Section 3 misses an explicit safety requirement and can crash after a caught failure.** Markings are never tested for `None` before soundness or replay. If BPMN conversion throws, `net_from_bpmn` is still referenced in the soundness loop and raises `NameError`, so the Petri net that did convert is never diagnosed. Exceptions are printed, not interpreted. The source POWL is not visualized.

**The bounded explorer does not implement the required comparison.**
- Shortest accepting visible word has length 7 without Legal Review (`Register`, `Assess Risk`, `Prepare Evidence`, `Configure Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, `Release Change`, `Archive Change`) and 8 with it. `max_visible_length=5` cannot reach any exact final marking, so both trace sets stay empty and look equal.
- `visited` is keyed only by marking, so the second interleaving and every later loop iteration are dropped.
- Stored traces append `t.label`, including `None`, so tau is not hidden.
- Firing rebuilds a plain `dict` of places with a positive token count. The next `get_enabled_transitions` / `marking[place]` step raises `KeyError` on absent zero places.
- Silent limiting counts every `None` in the whole trace, not a cycle.
- Declared positive and negative witnesses are never replayed. Model playout is absent. Footprints and reachability graphs are printed, not compared. The note that the search is non-exhaustive does not say it is vacuous.

**Further false or muddled claims.** Section 9 treats further loop iterations as behavior beyond the source; the source has a minimum of one iteration and no maximum. Rejection criterion 5 equates a final marking with “Archive is the last activity.” Section 6 is the strongest part, but it still says BPMN cannot represent this loop; the loop itself is structured, and the visual hazard is the unstructured top-level partial order and silent XOR.

Taken together, the write-up would accept an empty trace diff, a picture, and a soundness flag as fidelity evidence. That is the failure mode the task is about.
