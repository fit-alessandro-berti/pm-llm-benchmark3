The audit is strong and largely reliable: it covers every global feature, sequence pattern, and local-only driver, catches the planted lineage traps, and keeps the right distinctions between leakage, process impossibility, proxies, and legitimate prefix signals. It is not nearly flawless.

The best material is the separation of `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` (later-stage legal, unavailable at coverage) from `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` (normatively suspicious, same-timestamp, and post-`t0`). The 65% team coincidence, `claim_created_at <= extraction_date`, the 23:00 document snapshot, mutable `risk_score_current`, extraction-dated employee experience, and missing sequence index are all handled correctly. Queue length and elapsed time are preserved. Predictive, causal, actionable, and fairness standards are separated, including the point that dropping region or channel can hide disparity. Label-versus-feature leakage is explicit in the retrain plan. Case narratives do not restate the leaked appeal, partial decision, or full variant.

These defects keep it below a near-perfect score:

- Credible drivers still inherit signs from a model whose top features are leaked. `recompute` and the discarded base value acknowledge contamination, but Case X’s narrative still presents complexity plus congestion as the admissible explanation. Those signs are not identified effects until a prefix-only refit.
- Case Y’s narrative calls the reserve “verified,” while the same answer correctly treats verification as an unmet condition.
- The proxy paragraph assigns repair-network, language, and broker mechanisms jointly to region, channel, prior-claim count, and reserve. Prior-claim count and reserve do not proxy language or repair-network coverage.
- The conformance rule says evidence cycles may run “alongside” damage assessment. In the stated process, document cycles precede damage assessment; parallelism is specified for fraud investigation.
- `claimant_channel` is a credible driver without an availability timestamp, which is looser than the standard applied to reserve, risk score, and prior-claim count.
- Severity leaves the required scale with `Medium/High` and `High/Critical`. Alignment and footprint checks are named, but only the DFG frequency limit is actually explained.
- Permanently post-outcome fields are placed under “unusable until lineage is repaired,” then correctly described as not repairable. The heading and the prose disagree.
- The prior-claim finding says the SQL can include claims created after `t0`, but the current claim itself also satisfies `claim_created_at <= extraction_date`. Exclusion of the current claim appears only in the remediation.
