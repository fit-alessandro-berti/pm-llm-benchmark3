The final forecast is a serious calendar-aware analysis: parallel branches are joined by a maximum, Friday chemistry cannot finish before the 18:00 close, weekend staffing and the Sunday QA closure are applied, queue discipline is treated as a real fork, and rework is carried as overlapping scenarios rather than added means. The assumption ledger, driver ranking, and excursion-versus-chemistry-start update are usable.

The headline probabilities are still mis-centered. The fully clean path is only about 0.40–0.55 and is the early half of the mixture, yet its Monday 13:00–17:00 center is reported as the unconditional P50. With queries, reruns, and investigations sitting later, a mixture median belongs nearer Monday evening to Tuesday morning, and the stated P80 is early by a similar step. Monday 09:30 as a plausible earliest release also stacks a minimum chemistry service, a cleared QA queue, and a sub-median QA service.

The dependence arithmetic does not hold up. The joint 0.024 is compared with a 0.013 base-rate product and called roughly double, while the marginals implied by those same conditionals multiply to about 0.02. The uplift values (0.30, 0.20, 0.13, 0.07) are not in the assumption ledger. Several hour-to-clock labels slip: 69 h is not 11:00, 78 h is not Monday 20:00, and 95 h is Tuesday afternoon rather than Wednesday. The on-call contamination window is also late: a Sunday 13:00 readout plus 20 h can be ready Monday morning, not 13:30.
