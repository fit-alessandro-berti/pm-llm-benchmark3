The behavioral comparison is strong: the visible languages, loop and join scope, both directions of non-inclusion, the P3 counts 76/85/4, and the P2 image argument all check out, and the counterexamples are genuine complete words. The simplified model really does preserve the stated dashboard abstraction and is structurally smaller as a tree.

The converted-net comparison does not. Under the same place-bordered translation and silent-transition reduction that yields the reported sizes for A (9 places, 9 transitions, 20 arcs) and for S (5, 5, 10), B is the same size as A, not 7/7/16. A and B have the same operator and leaf multiset, and the loop-exit silent is the only reducible transition in both. The claim that B is a smaller net with a higher mean degree and simplicity 7/9 is therefore false, even though it is presented as a PM4Py 2.7.23.4 measurement. The Earth Mover’s Distance figure is similarly asserted as a run result without a derivation that could be checked. Those unsupported metrics are a real accuracy failure in an otherwise careful argument.
