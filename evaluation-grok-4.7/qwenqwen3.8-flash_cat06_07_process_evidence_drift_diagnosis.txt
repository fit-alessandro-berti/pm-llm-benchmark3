8.3

The diagnosis is strong and correctly refuses a single drift label. April is treated as a schema/pipeline incident, June as a routing-mix change that does not imply concept drift, August–September as overlapping process, concept, and population effects with different timing, and October as label-censoring rather than a real drop to a 0.16 breach rate. Falsifiers, maturity gates, training-month exclusions, and alert dependencies are proportionate and evidence-tied.

Deductions are for precision and the required code. May’s residual gap is tied too directly to the April feature-cache backfill. Unobserved Partner Z attributes (rural distance, device age) are partly filed as covariate drift even though they were never in the model. April’s variant breach rates are called stable despite small increases (V2 0.10–0.11 to 0.13). The PM4Py sketch would not run: `format_dataframe` is called with `activity_id`/`timestamp` instead of `activity_key`/`timestamp_key`, later code expects `case:case_id` rather than `case:concept:name`, and `filter_time_range` is given a column name plus a nonexistent `timeframe` argument. `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` is also called with kwargs that do not match the function.
