The case-level control readings are mostly right—especially the zero-cost P1/P2 breaches (CL-609, CL-610), the cost-4 scope artifacts (CL-607, CL-612), telemetry/retry/label noise, silent τ as a fit, and P3 on CL-604—but the write-up is not consistent or uncertainty-safe enough to score higher.

The materiality scale is usable, yet severity 1 mixes fully conforming execution (CL-605) with noise, and “Critical” is applied to CL-610 while the score is 4. Section 2 states mechanisms as fact (rubber-stamping, a disbursement daemon, STP misconfiguration, settled payable-only scope) that Section 4 then treats as open hypotheses. That split matters: P6 is explicitly disputed, and a missing event is not proof the activity did not occur. CL-602 is still scored as a high-confidence control failure and paired with an immediate fraud clawback, while the verbal-check and dropped-interface alternatives remain live. CL-608 is called substantively P4-compliant even though the only link is a mutable text field, which is exactly the condition P4 requires to be reliable.

Pattern synthesis overreaches. “Four cases per month in the dataset sample” is false: the sample contains two synchronous auto-approve breaches; the note of four cases is not a monthly rate. UI telemetry and CRM linkage are generalized to “systemic” and “common” from single examples. Pattern 6 bundles CL-602 with CL-604 as out-of-sequence disbursement and cites bank timestamps as confirmation; CL-602 was approved before payment, so that evidence does not apply. “Up to EUR 570k” recovery treats a 17-minute approval lag on a possibly legitimate EUR 90k claim as the same loss as the unscreened EUR 480k payment.

Remediation covers model repair, correlation, investigation, and a data guard, but the summary table omits the required caveat column, several items claim 100% risk reduction, and a future payment-token build is ranked above containing the live EUR 480k exposure. The STP predicate is also ambiguous for a never-changed bank account. The PM4Py block hits the required calls and keeps judgment severity separate from raw cost, but positional alignment indexing and dividing by 10,000 are version-fragile, and it never actually aggregates move patterns, risk, or policy flags—only severity.
