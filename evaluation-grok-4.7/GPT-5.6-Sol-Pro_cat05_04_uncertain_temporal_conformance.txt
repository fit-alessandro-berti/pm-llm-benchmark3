The interval arithmetic, DST-fold filtering, SLA/profile classifications, and tolerance/zeta matrices are largely exact, including the delicate boundary cases (T-402/S1 still late at its earliest feasible time, T-404/S2 and T-408 investigation→containment overshooting by about 3 seconds, T-403 still outside the zeta=3 band by 1.5 seconds, T-409 still possible at +2 minutes). Assumptions are stated, midpoints are kept secondary, and T-407 is correctly left indeterminate rather than equated with `Alert_Sent`. That is not enough for a near-flawless score.

The required PM4Py call is not executable against the real API. `conformance_temporal_profile` takes zeta inside `parameters={"zeta": ...}`; passing `zeta=` raises `TypeError`. The adapter also assumes a 4-field deviation tuple whose fourth element is a standardized distance. Current PM4Py returns directly-follows deviations as a longer tuple `(source, target, elapsed, mean, std)` and does not score non-consecutive profile pairs such as Alert→Containment. The comparison then treats “absent from the deviation list” as `POINT_PASS`, so an unobserved pair is silently counted as conforming.

T-409 is internally inconsistent. The write-up conditions Alert→Investigation on nonnegative duration, but the reported investigation→containment interval `[17m58.5s, 43m01.5s]` still uses start times before the alert. Under that same constraint the latest feasible start-to-containment duration is about 35m03s. Classifications do not flip, but the published feasible interval does not match the stated semantics. Truncating negative durations to zero also fights the literal S1 reading: a start before receipt still occurs “no later than 15 minutes after” receipt. The raw negative span is disclosed, yet the baseline interval is the clamped one.

Lesser issues: mobile entry delay is modeled primarily as occurrence uncertainty, which is the less natural reading of “entered the displayed time late” (the record-only alternative is at least analyzed); HUB common-offset cancellation is not tested on the 3-second T-404/T-408 boundaries; and the fold check allows equality despite an “after” constraint. None of those change the baseline verdicts.
