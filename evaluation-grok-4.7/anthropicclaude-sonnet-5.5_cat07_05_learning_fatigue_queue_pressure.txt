7.5

The memo is complete, constraint-compliant, and substantively strong: it refuses a single-cause story, treats early complexity and late queue pressure as first-order, rejects punishment and quality-check removal, and covers every required mechanism, analysis, intervention, caution, and dashboard item. The score is held down by several concrete errors and overclaims.

The early-bin comparison is not valid as stated. Bins 1–5 mix complexities (mean 0.72) at medium queue (46). Their 3.4% error rate cannot be read against the medium-WIP, high-complexity cell of 5.6% as if that cell were the mix-adjusted prediction. The gap is asserted twice and then used to infer unusually careful early handling.

Seniors are described, in adjacent sentences, as doubling errors under high WIP (2.2% to 4.9%) and as having “little quality loss.” Those claims conflict. “Only fast workers reach positions 76+” is also too strong: sequence position depends on shift length, concurrency, and case mix, not speed alone. Cumulative service time in that bin cannot fit inside 459 elapsed minutes if work were sequential, and that concurrency/timestamp tension is never examined even though it bears directly on service time, batching, and selection into late bins.

The code would mislead if implemented. `C(shift_id.str.split('_').str[0])` sits inside a formula string, so it is not valid patsy and assumes an undocumented `shift_id` format. The Kitagawa sketch drops non-overlapping complexity×WIP cells without renormalizing, so composition plus within-stratum change does not recover the early–late gap. The rework merge is a case-level Cartesian product and would bias the proposed survival model. `pm4py.discover_batches` is called with keyword arguments rather than its parameters dict, and `filter_event_attribute_values` is suggested for a numeric WIP split it does not do. `exp_group` is parsed from the resource name, so Night is treated as an experience level in the interaction model.

The rest of the mechanism table, the speed–accuracy account, the intervention risks, the ethics section, and the dashboard specification are careful and usable. The deductions are for those specific logical and technical failures, not for the overall structure.
