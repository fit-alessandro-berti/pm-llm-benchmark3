2.5

The submission matches the required outline and names the right PM4Py types, but it does not construct either model, and its control-flow reading contradicts the narrative in several places that the witnesses then repeat.

Section 1 names every required ambiguity and then resolves the important ones incorrectly or not at all. Low-risk versus high-risk and optional supplier consultation are both ordinary control-flow choices (`XOR` of Supervisor Review against concurrent Quality Review and Regulatory Review; `XOR` of Consult Supplier against a skip). The text says those choices “cannot be explicitly modeled.” Passing inspection is described as a labelless transition and, in the same paragraph, as a successful outcome, with no loop exit. Duplicate labels are declared permitted and then never used. “May overlap” is treated as true concurrency, but in both formalisms the trace semantics are interleavings from absent order; only the process tree must encode that with an explicit parallel block. The stated policy is conservative under-approximation, which section 7 later calls under-constraint.

Section 2 inverts several relations. Plan Verification before Execute Trial is precedence, not concurrency. Adjust Parameters is the redo between a failed Inspect Trial and the next Execute Trial, not concurrent with either. Release is an AND-join, not a concurrency edge. “Immediately after Register Deviation” is too strong. Supervisor Review, Quality Review, Regulatory Review, Consult Supplier, the risk choice, supplier optionality, approval–trial overlap, and the release synchronization are missing. The table therefore does not specify the intended language.

Section 3 is not a process tree. There is no tree expression. `create_process_tree` is not part of the given API, and activity–activity pairs are not parent/child operator structure. No child is inserted and no `.parent` is set. Four of the fourteen labels are absent. The approximation paragraph claims the tree cannot express the risk route or optional supplier consultation, which are the block-structured parts, and never mentions the actual obstacle: Plan Verification depends only on containment, design and approvals depend only on root-cause analysis, the trial joins the first two, and release joins the trial, the selected approval route, and the optional consultation.

Section 4 is not a POWL model. `Transition` and `SilentTransition` are called with activity pairs; `StrictPartialOrder()` is empty; `OperatorPOWL` is unused; `.order.add_edge` never appears. Silent transitions are described as the mechanism for concurrency. Consult Supplier is cited as an unconnected concurrent node although it is not in the node set. There is no separate POWL description and no justification for each intentionally unordered pair.

Section 5 does not meet the witness contract. The split is four allowed and four forbidden, not five and three. No allowed trace contains a low-risk review, a high-risk pair, supplier consultation, or Archive Case. No row has a process-tree verdict that differs from the POWL verdict, so the approximation is never exposed. Trace 3 skips Contain Material and Plan Verification and is marked allowed. Trace 5 calls a supervisor-only path forbidden for missing high-risk reviews. Trace 6 treats an optional consultation as mandatory. Trace 7’s visible word sequence does not contain Consult Supplier, while the reason says it occurs after release. Trace 8 has the same legal multi-cycle shape as trace 4 (adjust, then another execute–inspect, then release) and is marked forbidden for an adjustment after the passing inspection that the sequence does not show. Forbidden rows do not identify the earliest violated condition.

Section 6 asserts coverage the code does not have: `Operator.PARALLEL` and `Operator.LOOP` are not built, approvals are not nodes, and a final transition is not a cross-branch join. Duplicate-label risk is omitted. “No business impact” is attached to requirements the models drop. Supplier omission is called only an under-constraint; dropping the activity also deletes every trace that legally contains it.

Section 7 prefers the process tree, which cannot express the stated partial order without duplication or a changed language. POWL is the representation that can carry those edges directly. Soundness is described as narrative compliance rather than option to complete, proper completion, and no dead parts. The silent-label and duplicate-label check is a sentence, not a check. The limitation statement contradicts the section 1 policy.

Section 8 stays at the level of “could be clearer” and does not notice that the artifacts would not run, that trace 3 is illegal, or that trace 8 matches an allowed pattern.
