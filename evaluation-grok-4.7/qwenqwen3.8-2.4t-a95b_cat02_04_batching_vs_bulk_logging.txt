7.5

The diagnoses for G1–G8 are mostly well supported, numerically consistent with the table, and careful not to treat identical timestamps as physical simultaneity. Waiting, service, utilization, and the G6 zero-service/inflated-wait mechanism are explained in the right directions, and the required sections and columns are present.

The score is held down by ranking and method gaps. G1’s second explanation (start batching) fits poorly with identical ends; the stronger alternative—cycle-level PLC timestamp assignment—is only developed later. G4’s second label, queueing after capacity loss, is not evidenced; the trace only shows a short backlog and a 7-minute lead-in. G6’s two explanations largely collapse into one logging story. The code calls `format_dataframe` and `discover_batches` and builds the requested pandas checks, but `min_batch_size` 2 vs 4 does not split any cluster (all have at least four events), and the write-up never states which PM4Py temporal class each cluster would receive or where that label would mislead—especially G6 and G7 looking simultaneous while G1 might actually be a fixture cycle. Interval bounds are discussed qualitatively, including an overly tight “under one minute” service bound for G6, but not computed.
