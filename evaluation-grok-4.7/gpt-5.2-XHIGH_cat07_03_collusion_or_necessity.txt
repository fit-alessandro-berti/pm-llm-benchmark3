7.5

The audit judgment is careful and well differentiated, but the recommended PM4Py procedure contains material logic errors, and a few claims are stronger than the evidence.

The prioritization is the strongest part. V-77x bank-detail changes plus payment approval are correctly treated as the residual high-impact cluster; Carla→Gita and Dan→Hugo are kept in the emergency/specialization lane; Aline→Elena and Boris→Felix are monitoring baselines rather than suspicion. Severity is not collapsed across pairs. Benign alternatives generally include both supporting and refuting evidence, and the report wording stays within 200–300 words, names V-77x / Lena–Kim, separates control exceptions from misconduct, and records shared accounts, missing ticket linkage, and migration artifacts. Constraints on accusations and on co-working frequency are respected.

Section 4 would mislead an auditor who ran it. `filter_four_eyes_principle` has long kept cases that satisfy four-eyes (no shared resource), including cases in which one activity never occurs. Naming that output `viol_*` selects the complementary population. `format_dataframe(..., resource_key="actor")` is not a real parameter; the resource must already be `org:resource` or be passed into the filter. Building `actor` as `entered_by`, then `approved_by`, then `org:resource` can hide the approver on approval events and will not reproduce the stated 42 and 9 violations; the code never also runs the `org:resource` baseline. `retro_justification_ok` uses only the last override, accepts a justification timestamped before that override, and treats a missing actor as a different person because `NaN != r0` is true. Slicing on event-level `source_system` drops sibling events and breaks the case before handover or four-eyes analysis. The log skeleton is discovered from the same log and then checked against itself, so it does not encode “different person within three working days,” despite the prose saying it enforces existence and order. Path-performance filtering is directly-follows only; intervening events would drop real fast segments. Bots are left in the SNA logs, where Bot_PO and Bot_Match will dominate.

Smaller calibration problems: 12 of 17 same-person justifications are already described as a quarter-close spreadsheet batch, yet that set is still a high-priority cluster rather than split into likely upload artifact versus unexplained remainder. “Documented” emergency handling is not established; the evidence supports a known rush channel. The quarter-close / Temp_Nina explanation is tied too loosely to the Lena→Kim→Lena loop. “Diversion-impact” is inherent-risk language, but it is sharper than the rest of an otherwise cautious answer. There is no single explicit conclusion that the log fits a mixture and that collusion is not the best-supported reading.
