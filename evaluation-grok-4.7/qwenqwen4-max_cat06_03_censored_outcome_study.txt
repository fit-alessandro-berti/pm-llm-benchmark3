7.5

The written design is strong: all 48,000 cases are accounted for, rejected/withdrawn/open cases are not treated as non-defaults, activation is handled as selection rather than ordinary censoring, and the leakage table, temporal split, and decision-use limits match the business question. The score is not higher because the primary estimand and the skeleton do not fully implement that design.

The 180-day CIF of state 2 is equated with “activated within 90 days of τ and default within 90 days of activation.” Those are not the same event: an activation after day 90 can still produce a default inside 180 days. Cases already activated inside the 72-hour window stay in the risk set, so post-decision activities can enter an “early warning” prefix. Seventy-two hours is also unmotivated relative to KYC and credit review.

Known defaults are mishandled. `state = default_90d` requires `activation + 90 + 35 days ≤ cutoff` even when `default_90d = 1` and `label_available_at` is already before the cutoff, so observed defaults are censored until the worst-case feed lag. That biases the CIF downward. The lag rule is appropriate only for unconfirmed non-defaults.

`build_cases` does not replay history as of the cutoff: events and labels are not truncated, despite the docstring. `obs_days` sets mature cases to the administrative date, stops immature activations at activation, and censors non-default causes at extraction−35 days, so the survival hook cannot support Aalen–Johansen. The train assert requires every training `label_available_at ≤ 2025-03-31`, which fails for late training starts instead of censoring them. Variant frequencies use post-`TRAIN_END` prefix events, and resource experience is frozen at `TRAIN_END`, leaking later activity into earlier training cases. Only the event timestamp is localized, so as-of comparisons with the other datetime columns are inconsistent.

Section 7 still presents the July–September block as the main test of the 180-day target, while Section 2 shows that only about 12 post-policy start days have complete follow-up. Section 8 withholds a post-migration claim but not a post-policy 180-day claim.
