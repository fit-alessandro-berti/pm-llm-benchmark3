The audit is organized and catches the obvious post-cutoff fields (duration, full-trace variant, appeal flag, closure team, document totals, nightly document snapshot, the prior-claim SQL, mutable risk score, and last role). It also correctly keeps queue length, registration age, and initial complexity, and it does not treat region or SHAP as causal. That is not enough for a high score.

The prediction point is immediately after coverage check, and every illustrated prefix is only registration then coverage. Appeal, settlement, damage assessment, and document cycles are not merely unknown; they are not enabled yet. `appeal_seen_anywhere = 1` and `final_decision = Partial` on Case X are process-impossible, not just future leakage. The same error appears in the claim that damage assessment “may have happened” before settlement at `t0`, and in the validation rule that treats a missing predecessor as impossible rather than future-possible. Because conforming prefixes are identical here, “recompute the variant from the prefix” would be constant and non-predictive.

`Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is a legal later reconsideration path and still inadmissible at `t0`. `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is different: it reverses the lifecycle and, in Case Z, shares a timestamp with no sequence index. The audit gives both the same leakage label and removal remedy. `total_case_duration_hours` is also under-specified: it is post-closure and highly collinear with a 10-day breach label, not a generic duration feature that can be swapped for elapsed time.

Case X and Case Y are marked retain even though leaked drivers dominate the stated probabilities (+0.26 appeal, +0.31 full variant, plus a large unaudited residual). Section 5’s recalibration advice contradicts that. The 65% team overlap is structural, not coincidental, and is exactly where future reassignment can hide. Same-day document count is a lineage defect, not a fairness proxy. Case Z’s recomputation list imports unrelated variant and prior-claim fixes.
