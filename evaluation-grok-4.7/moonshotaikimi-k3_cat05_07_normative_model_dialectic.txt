The final judgment is strong, case-complete, and genuinely dialectical: both briefs concede real points, 704/705 and 702/714/703 are separated by window and eligibility rather than by a compromise, and 706 vs 702 correctly tracks immutable `notification_id` versus a late mutable link. M0 concurrency, the checks-before-reject over-specification, SoD (712), and COI disclosure (715) are read accurately, and the POWL loop encoding (XOR of τ and LOOP(Request_More_Info, τ)) is the right zero-or-more approximation.

It is not nearly flawless. The stated burden says any unproved overlay safeguard defeats the exception, yet SUP-702 is classified JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION while PD-3’s mandatory pre-activation master-data validation is still unproved. The system totals double-count: SUP-703 sits inside the 14 ineligible June–July cases, and SUP-714 sits inside the six August Auto_Approve cases, but both are added on top (“703 + 14”, “714 + 6”). The executable model leaves Register → Sanctions → Reject non-conformant, despite that being the proposed resolution of SUP-709, and no sibling pre-screen/reactivation POWL is actually built. The auto route silently forces Quality_Review after Activate, so a stricter quality-before-activation order does not fit, and that over-constraint is not disclosed. Smaller nits: “deliberate” misuse is not established for 703, 712 is both stipulated and missing provenance, and the sanctions-before-activation helper is false for rejects that never activate.
