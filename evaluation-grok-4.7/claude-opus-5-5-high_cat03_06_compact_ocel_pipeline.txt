The data model is internally consistent and the required pipeline structure is complete. Object types, all 17 events, the 12 O2O edges, timezone-aware timestamps, payment amounts as event attributes, and the source-versus-derived split all match the narrative. Hand checks confirm 35 source E2O rows, the Order traces (O100: 8 events, O200: 6; 14 rows from 11 events), the flattened DFGs, the 18 derived rows, and the OC-DFG distinction between one Reserve Stock→Pack Package event couple and two Orders/Items. Qualifiers, validation, and the flattening caveats (copied e06/e07/e11, I2 never reserved, empty Payment DFG, double arcs) are strong.

Points are lost on interpretive accuracy. The OC-DFG is not two disconnected regions: Order arcs already run through Issue Invoice to Close Order, and the union of typed edges also reaches Dispatch/Confirm and Record Payment. The claim that `ocel_objects_interactions_summary` has 58 rows treats an unaggregated ordered co-occurrence expansion as the summary; the partner sets are right, but that row count is the ungrouped size, not a pair summary. OTG interaction weights are never defined, and 5 for Item–Order is distinct pairs rather than the 6 shared events, while descendants/inheritance/codeath are described without tying them cleanly to the tuple shape claimed earlier. The Petri-net remark that one Pack firing consumes O100, O200, I1, and I3 states an observed binding as if it were net structure, despite the later caveat.
