8.5

The fork-join decomposition is internally consistent and arithmetically reliable. Upstream, queue, service, `join_ready = max` completion, synchronization, downstream wait, final service, and total flow time reconcile case by case and in total (`235 + 732 + 176 + 240 = 1383`). Critical labels, margins, and the no-double-counting identity are right, including the F07 demonstration that summed sync waits (77) exceed stage time (75) and that a naive sum (280) counts the stage three times. Worked cases F02, F04, F07, and F11 check out. Ranking B over A, despite A’s longer service, is supported by critical frequency (8/12), the long-case tail, margin sums (120 of 147), and the constant 50-minute A floor. Near-ties at 5 minutes are reported as ranges rather than forced ranks. The M1/M2 counterfactuals, including knock-on effects (CF2 M2 = 122, CF2x M2 = 160, CF5 M2 = 129.5, CF6 = 268.5), match a resource replay. Assumptions A1–A6 and the limits section correctly refuse to treat derived waits as causes.

The score is held down by a real error in the required PM4Py section. Both `discover_performance_dfg` calls order on completion `time:timestamp`, so they are the same completion-ordered DFG and cannot emit negative arc times. The write-up still says that running the code produces an interval DFG with negative durations and no Laboratory Assay → Release Decision arc. That describes a start-ordered log this script never builds. Service times and the completion-arc check (mean 34.67 = 14.67 + 20) are fine; the interval contrast is not.

CF3a is also softer than the table implies. “Extra final capacity, gap kept → 73” is infinite capacity on the pinned resource, or “delete same-resource blocking,” not the effect of one added server. The pinned schedule already has a three-way overlap under `start = join + gap` (F03, F04, F05 around 10:10–10:20), so two pooled servers cannot realize all 73 minutes, while a third server with pre-assignment would realize almost none. The 25 / 73 / 176 bracket is discussed later, but the summary row over-identifies 73 with capacity.

Smaller nits: “three cases are ready between 11:30 and 11:35” for the CF3b residual (two are already in service; F11 waits 5, which is correct); ±1 minute is stated per duration, whereas a difference of two timestamps moves by up to about 2 minutes (still not enough to flip a 5-minute margin).
