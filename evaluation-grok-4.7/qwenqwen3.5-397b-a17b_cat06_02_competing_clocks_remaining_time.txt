The write-up has the required six-part shape and a few sound anchors: incubation at Friday 14:00 plus 50 h is Sunday 16:00, the join is written as a maximum, weekend closures are acknowledged, and scenario overlap is declared. Those points do not rescue the forecast. The headline durations do not match the headline clocks, and the scenario table does not match the later timelines.

Elapsed time from Friday 18 September 15:30 to Monday 21 September 15:30 is 72 h, not the stated ~48 h. The central band of 47–68 h is about one day short of Monday 15:00–Tuesday 12:00. The P80 upper figure, 98 h, is not Wednesday 17:00 (~121 h). The severe tail’s 165 h is Friday 25 September around midday, not Monday 28 September (~236 h). Tuesday plus five calendar days is Sunday 27 September, not Monday 28 September. A 12 h QA working-time addend from Tuesday 14:30 lands near Wednesday 14:30, not Wednesday 10:30.

The same paths are given two different endings. Chemistry rerun is Tuesday in the scenario table and Monday 18:30 in the decomposition. The smooth-path table calls microbiology the dominant clock, while the arithmetic sets the join at chemistry’s Monday 10:30 versus microbiology’s Monday 09:00.

Chemistry cannot finish on Friday if the urgent sample keeps its place. The first analyst is free around 15:54–16:24 and the second around 16:42–17:30; minimum service is 1.5 h and the lab closes at 18:00. Strict priority makes a Friday finish less likely, not more likely. The claim that a Friday start pulls the terminal event forward by ~24 h is also untenable: primary readiness is Sunday 12:00–20:00, readout still needs a microbiologist, and QA is closed Sunday, so Saturday’s QA window cannot be used. The baseline also skips partial Friday service resumed Monday at 08:00, and it collapses FIFO versus priority into one Monday 08:00 start despite four waiting samples plus two already in process.

Contamination is timed as if the indication exists when incubation becomes technically ready. It is observed at readout. Without weekend authorization, confirmation incubation starts Monday morning, not Sunday 16:00, so a Monday 17:00 usable result is too early. Monday QA is treated as one reviewer facing a static queue of two to four batches. Two reviewers are on from 08:00 and can drain much of that queue before a late-morning join. The smooth path’s “N/A” tail drops ordinary service spread, including chemistry out to 4.5 h and QA’s 3.5 h upper percentile.

The temperature-excursion dependence is named and then overstated. An investigation probability of 0.30–0.55, of which only about 20–40% is laboratory work, does not turn the severe tail into a majority outcome or move the P80 to 28 September. P(electronic release) > 0.85 and P(rejection) < 0.05 are not identified by the evidence; queries and investigations delay release, they do not define the rejection rate.
