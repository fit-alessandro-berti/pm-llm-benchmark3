8.2

The final model is strong and mostly faithful. The process tree is a real conservative under-approximation, the POWL partial order encodes the cross-branch joins directly, and the eight witnesses actually separate intended behavior, tree loss, and forbidden traces. The approximation is disclosed rather than hidden, and the loop, risk XOR, and optional supplier are represented in the required control-flow way.

Deductions are for precision, not for a broken model. The validation plan treats A1–A5 as accepted witnesses without limiting that claim to the POWL model, even though A2, A3, and A5 are exactly the traces the tree must reject. Section 6 also hedges the repeat-until row as both overconstraint and underconstraint, and several POWL rows are called exact while the hidden pass/fail condition remains nondeterministic. The concurrency explanation gives the right rule and examples, but it does not account for every intentionally unordered pair. Those are local slips in an otherwise consistent answer.
