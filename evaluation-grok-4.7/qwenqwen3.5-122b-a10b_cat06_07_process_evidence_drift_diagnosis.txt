6.5

The audit correctly separates the period into distinct regimes: April is treated as a schema and pipeline failure, June as a routing-mix change, August–September as overlapping supplier performance, concept, and population effects, and October as label censoring. The six-section structure, the drift table, the maturity gate on retraining, and the four risk cases all match the task. The score stays well below the top of the scale because several required checks are incomplete or logically mismatched, and the remediation plan contradicts itself.

The table understates timing and blurs mechanisms. Concept drift is dated to August–September, while the matured V3 breach rate already jumps in July (0.35 to 0.46) as the part-wait edge moves from 42 h to 65 h. Process drift stops at August even though that edge keeps worsening in September (148 h) and October (155 h). May is left out of the pipeline row despite residual unknown-activity share, missing queue features, and the unrecomputed cached count feature. Duplicate removal, limited to the new source after 20 April, is never used. June’s stable AUROC is offered as evidence against process drift, but a mix shift can be real while discrimination stays intact. September calls Partner Z’s 0.45 rate “inherent risk” after the table itself says those labels mature more slowly.

The narrative never checks the variant mix against the matured rates. June’s mix (30/52/18 at 0.16/0.09/0.35) lands near the observed 0.15, which is the clean proof that June is composition. August’s mix implies about 0.31 against an observed 0.28 at 93% maturity, and September’s implies about 0.34 against 0.31 at 82% maturity. Those gaps are the censoring signal, and they are not computed. October’s mean risk of 0.23 is described as the model compensating for unseen risk; the score movement is equally consistent with a V3 and Partner Z feature shift against downward-biased labels.

The test list misses pieces the task required. Normalization is not paired with duplicate cleaning. Calibration is not also discrimination, and it is not stratified by source and month. The lineage row is an omitted-variable check, not a missingness or feature-lineage audit of the April queue feature and the uncached diagnosis count. The Partner Z reweighting hypotheses are crossed: removing Partner Z’s mass would still leave non-Partner-Z September breach at 0.27, so a remaining gap supports the supplier shift. It does not, by itself, show that rural distance and device age are the cause.

The training decision does not line up with the diagnosis. Short-term isotonic recalibration is fit on July–August V1/V2, the segments whose matured breach rates barely moved, and V3, the segment that moved from about 0.35 to 0.64, is excluded. Full retraining is delayed until February 2027, then restricted to January–March plus July, so the wait for mature labels does not result in using the post-supplier or Partner Z regimes. May, June, August, and September are never marked safe or unsafe. A variant-specific model is reasonably justified; a V3-only level correction versus a retrain, depending on within-V3 discrimination, is not.

Monitoring covers data quality, maturity, variant mix, and the part-wait edge, and the maturity gate correctly blocks a label-delay alarm from forcing a retrain. Calibration is folded into a mean-versus-rate comparison. There is no population monitor and no deployment monitor for the April migration, May patch, June policy, and September onboarding. The code sketch normalizes the renamed diagnosis label, drops exact duplicates, and calls the requested PM4Py helpers without inventing a drift detector. It does not build both an event-calendar cohort and a case-start cohort or state their interpretations. `format_dataframe` is called with `activity` and `timestamp` rather than `activity_key` and `timestamp_key`, `filter_time_range` is called with `min_time` and `max_time` before formatting, `len(log)` counts events but is labeled as cases, and the performance DFG is computed and then ignored. Those are concrete failures in a required deliverable, not cosmetic sketch looseness.
