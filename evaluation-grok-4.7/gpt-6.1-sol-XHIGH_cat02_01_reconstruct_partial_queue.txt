8.8

The reconstruction is unusually rigorous: station sojourn is separated from active service, open and still-waiting cases stay in exposure and WIP, missing starts are bounded rather than filled with one silent value, and the bottleneck claim is tied to flow and backlog timing rather than to a single utilization percentage. Core case arithmetic, resource non-overlap, C2’s 15-minute loss, the central/earliest/latest allocations, queue and busy areas (1490/595 at S1 and 770/570 at S2), the hourly central occupancies, the closed-cohort Little’s-Law identities (1845 and 980 case-minutes), and the finished-case total of 2050 minutes all check out. PM4Py is confined to a completed-case projection, with case sojourn/service/waiting checked against the two stations and the arrival/finish columns explicitly labeled as inter-event gaps.

The remaining defects are small but real. In the hourly table, independently rounded totals do not match the displayed addends (6.2+2.8 versus 8.9, and 3.3+5.8 versus 9.2). The S1 busy envelope is summarized as 443–698 resource-minutes while the resource rows sum to 442.5–697.5. Several hourly means are shown to one decimal even though the missing-start bands move queue length by one case for up to 35 minutes, about 0.6 of an hourly mean, and those bands are not carried back into the hourly figures. The rounding-aware start intervals are marginal outer boxes; joint feasibility is explained later, but the constraint table can still be read as a rectangle. None of that overturns the stable conclusions.
