The policy is decision-theoretically strong, operationally usable, and arithmetically sound. It refuses I-03 on stage grounds, separates I-05’s stale refresh from I-07’s new log, adjusts segment calibration without treating observational `p` as a causal effect, and prices queue spillovers in all three scenarios.
