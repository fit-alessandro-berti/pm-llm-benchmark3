7.5

The brief is a strong, case-complete conformance review, but it is not nearly flawless. The control-flow proposal rests on a false limitation, and several classifications conflict with the answer’s own burden rules.

The required sections, matrix, and case coverage are all present. Authority, expiry, scope, CRM linkage, label drift, and segregation of duties are handled with real evidence, and both briefs concede the points they must concede. SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-705, SUP-708, SUP-712, and SUP-714 are classified on defensible dominant reasons.

The PM4Py proposal is the largest failure. `OperatorPOWL` supports `Operator.LOOP`; POWL has always included loop. The claim that only a zero-or-one XOR is possible is false. That approximation cannot replay SUP-707, which contains two `Request_More_Info` events, and it places the only request before sanctions. The observed and desired pattern is one or more requests after sanctions have started and before the decision. Distinct per-route transitions, standard-route concurrency, and the manual, auto, emergency, and reject skeletons are otherwise structurally sound.

The auto route orders `Quality_Review` before `Notify_Requester`. SUP-702, which the answer calls a justified exception, has CRM notification at +2 hours and quality review on the next business day, so notification precedes quality. The revised model rejects the pilot pattern it blesses.

SUP-702 is also internally inconsistent. The stated burden for `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` requires every derogation predicate to be proved. PD-3 requires automated master-data validation before activation, and that result is absent. Moderate confidence does not repair a classification the burden rule itself forbids.

SUP-711 is mislabeled. The answer says the shared source transaction ID proves a migration duplicate and that the deduplicated process is compliant, with high confidence and no severity, then assigns `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE`. Under the answer’s own definition, that label means a reliable finding cannot be made.

Smaller defects: the overlay table extends FD-7’s two-business-day deadline to manual approval, where the directive clearly imposes that deadline on monitoring and `Quality_Review` and only caps spend until retrospective approval; six early-August auto-approvals are treated as proof that the pilot “became the default”; SUP-715 is first listed as a disputed disclosure trigger and later as established noncompliance.
