7.5

The JSON is complete, case-ordered, and genuinely process-aware: it separates C6-A from C6-B, overrides the more frequent Technical Investigation → Disposition path in C6-C because of the reportability rule, treats the migration-affected log in C6-D as incomplete, and uses queues, lot structure, and completion-only logging instead of raw DFG frequencies.

The main defects are in the next-event model. `process_enabled` is often false for activities the same answer treats as open under a stated alternative state, so the required distinction between an enabled in-progress task and the next visible completion is applied inconsistently. In C6-C, Authority Notification is ranked as a next logged event even though Regulatory Assessment and Disposition are still unlogged and the batch follows disposition. In C6-A, Technical Investigation and Regulatory Assessment completions receive too much mass as the immediate next event only about two hours after evidence receipt, while another evidence cycle is left out of the top three. C6-D calls a regulatory completion around 14:00 plausible although the case was opened at 12:40 and the stated window is 2–14 hours after assignment. Midpoints for C6-A, C6-B, and C6-E also leave almost no residual mass despite the calibration note saying the candidate sets are not exhaustive.
