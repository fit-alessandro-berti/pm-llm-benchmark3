6.5

The design covers every required section and respects the main constraints: non-stationary arrivals, calendars, separate applicant delays, latent objections, cross-case coupling, and no claim of optimality. It is still far from a sound twin specification. Several methodological choices are wrong or internally inconsistent, and the code does not follow PM4Py’s actual conventions.

**Process-mining grounding is the weakest part.** A performance DFG does not report waiting time and service time. On completion-timestamp logs its edge statistic is the elapsed time between successive events, which mixes the target activity’s wait and service and is further distorted by calendars. The skeleton then computes that interval incorrectly: `elapsed_h` is the gap from the previous event, but it is stored on the edge from the current activity to the next one, and end-of-case rows that hold the real outgoing gaps are dropped. `pm4py.discovery.discover_dfg` and `pm4py.discovery.discover_performance_dfg` are not the public API; `pm4py.discover_dfg` and `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg` return `(graph, starts, ends)`, not a single dict. `format_dataframe` returns a dataframe, so `to_event_log` is misnamed. `pm4py.get_variants` is the wrong current entry point. The `AttributeError` fallbacks hide these mismatches only when the attribute is missing; a successful call would propagate the wrong object. DFG frequencies are also the wrong estimator for routing once reviews interleave. Presence of `Fire-safety review` or `Legal check` at case level, conditional on attributes, is the identified quantity. Two-parameter fits “per activity × type × district × pool” from median and p90 ignore sparse cells, business-time conversion, and the likely bias that only some resources record start timestamps. There is no inverse procedure that chooses service distributions so simulated flow times match the log. “Imputation” of the unobserved fire/environment threshold adds a quantity the log does not contain; occurrence probabilities given `case_type`, value, and district do not require that threshold. Excluding rare variants does not stress the emergency cases already removed from the log.

**Control flow is unresolved.** The tree’s `+` joins planning, fire, and environment before public notice; the business rule says decision drafting waits for those reviews plus notice and inspection; the excerpt shows notice starting immediately after fire, then a long gap before scheduling. The design never chooses an enablement and join rule. `RoutingEngine.next_activity` returns one activity, so the skeleton cannot express a parallel split, an optional branch, or an AND-join. Public notice and `Receive missing documents` are timers or external delays, but the only execution primitive is a capacity pool. Appeal and planning rework have no return point. Prior violations, required for D2, are absent from case state. Environmental “40% reserved” and “2 FTE equivalent” fire staff are copied from the brief without a capacity representation (`fte` is an `int`, and `is_available` always returns true). The same planner often both reviews and drafts; that continuity is ignored. Council-week “urgent” legal work is undefined.

**The experiment and decision layers do not match the uncertainty the text itself describes.** D1’s 40% loop reduction, D7’s Monday factor of 1.2, D8’s 20% speed-up, and D9’s 50% detection rate are treated as factor levels even though they are unidentified causal effects. D3’s batch size and D6’s 20-day aging cutoff are single arbitrary settings; the aging cutoff is also poorly matched to case-type SLAs that range from about a month to half a year. S4 changes missing timestamps and calendar error together, so it cannot be attributed. A fixed 90-day discard plus a classical steady-state reading is the wrong warm-up for seasonal demand: after warm-up the run is in a different season, and a preloaded WIP census is valid only for the start date’s season. “30–100” replications is not tied to the rarest estimand (complex-variance or objection tails). Truncating heavy tails moves the same p90/p95 figures used for decisions. CRN is named without stream-to-source mapping, which matters once D1 shifts arrivals and D2 deletes activities. The selection rule then ignores interval estimates: a point 10% gain can be noise. The 70–90% utilization band rejects policies whose benefit is lower utilization. D2’s missed-defect risk, inspector cost, and applicant burden never become metrics. Fairness “worsen by 10%” does not define the district ratio. Annual SLA compliance can conceal peak-season failure, and case-mix is implicitly stationary. Conformance checking of random simulated traces is not replay validation. A single last-six-months holdout confounds error with season, and nothing validates architect clustering, batching, or objection correlation.

The skeleton is modular and the excerpt path is real, including an explicit combined flow-time column when starts are absent. Those merits do not offset the incorrect performance calculation, the non-parallel routing API, or an experiment that would rank interventions on assumed effect sizes.
