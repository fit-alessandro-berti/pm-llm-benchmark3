8.3

The two models are the right constructions under a clearly stated completion-event semantics. The process tree is a genuine conservative under-approximation: `Register Deviation`, then the two streams `(Contain Material → Plan Verification) ∥ (Analyze Root Cause → Design Corrective Action)`, then `(trial loop ∥ risk XOR ∥ supplier XOR)`, then `Release Material → Archive Case`. That keeps the trial join and release synchronisation, and the lost language is exactly the early route and `Consult Supplier` completions characterised in §3.4. The POWL root order is the right partial order: twelve covering edges, high-risk as an unordered pair inside an XOR, supplier as XOR-with-skip, and `*(Execute Trial → Inspect Trial, Adjust Parameters)`. Loop child order, at-most-once consultation, and exclusive routes are right. A5 is a real separator, and the fitness figures are internally consistent with pm4py’s empty-trace denominator (shortest visible length 10): A5 is `1−3/21`, F1 is `1−2/20`, F2 is `1−1/21`, F3 is `1−2/21`.

The write-up still overclaims and mis-locates a few facts.

The duplication argument is too strong. Independent skippable copies do yield zero or two `Supervisor Review`s, but that is one encoding. An XOR of alternative contexts can fire exactly one copy. With an unbounded trial loop, finite duplication still cannot recover every interleaving; that is the actual limit, and it is not the argument given.

F2’s “earliest” breach is pinned on the 8th event, `Adjust Parameters`. The prefix through `Adjust Parameters` and through the following `Supervisor Review` is still extendable by `Execute Trial, Inspect Trial, Release Material, Archive Case`, because the route is concurrent with the loop and may sit between the redo and the next body. The first non-extendable event is `Release Material`. The cost-1 alignment (delete `Adjust Parameters`) explains the fitness, but that is a minimal edit, not the first divergent prefix. D19 is only settled once the loop exits.

§8.1 says the witnesses place a review between `Execute Trial` and `Inspect Trial`. None of A1–A5 do. A3 puts `Quality Review` before the cycle and `Regulatory Review` after it, which a block-atomic trial would also allow. The corner the critique itself calls delicate is untested in the published traces, and “topological orders of the dependency graph” reads as an order on the coarse nodes, which never drops a review inside `ET IT (AP ET IT)*`.

“All tasks are live” is the wrong property. Soundness here is option-to-complete plus no dead transitions. After the low-risk XOR branch, `Quality Review` is not live. “Easy-sound (can and must)” mixes those notions as well.

§6 states `L(POWL) = L(intended)` as a language result. §8 correctly reduces that to a biased sample. The equality is plausible for this partial order, but the text asserts it before the only evidence that could fail it — route or supplier events inside the loop body, including between `Adjust Parameters` and the next `Execute Trial` — has been exhibited.
