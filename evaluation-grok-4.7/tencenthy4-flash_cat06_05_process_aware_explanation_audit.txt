8.3

The audit is strong and usable: it covers every required feature and pattern, separates obvious post-outcome leakage from subtler lineage defects, keeps queue length and elapsed time, treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as later-legitimate but inadmissible at `t0`, and keeps prediction, causation, actionability, and fairness apart. The revised JSON is structurally valid and does not retell leaked futures.

Deductions:

- Enabled, merely future-possible, and impossible are not cleanly separated. Immediately after coverage, damage assessment and document request are enabled next activities. Document receipt and settlement are only future-possible. Appeal is impossible until a Partial or Deny decision. The write-up labels enabled activities “future-possible” and lumps settlement, appeal, and receipt together as “not usable.”
- `total_case_duration_hours` is called a tautology. Time-to-close is post-outcome and near the 10-day settlement label, especially with appeal and closure after the decision, but it is not the label itself.
- Mean absolute importance has no sign. The team’s directional stories are therefore unsupported by the ranking metric, and that point is missed.
- The large local residual (“base value and other features”) is unaudited, so hidden leakage can remain inside the retained probabilities.
- Irreparable future fields sit in “unusable until lineage is repaired,” which the prose partly corrects but the class name still overstates repairability.
- `claimant_channel` moves from “likely” known at registration in the table to an asserted credible driver in the JSON, with no lineage proof.
