The answer covers every required section, keeps most arithmetic tied to the logs, and builds a usable recovery model with product-by-customer gates, workaround codes, separate operational and compliance closure, and indicators for throughput, protection, traceability, control completeness, backlog age, rework, and fairness. Several claims are still wrong or tighter than the evidence.

The temporal reading misstates volume. In every period that has a DFG, “Cases” equals `START → Receive order` (42,000, 5,600, and 35,000). T4 backlog prioritization is only 1,500 of 35,000 receipts. Those case counts are new intake, so they are not inflated by backlog drain, and a higher daily receipt rate does not show recovered capacity being overstated by queue flushing. The T2 rate of ~2,240/day also assumes a 2.5-day denominator that the window does not fix, and it is set against a 21-business-day baseline.

The resilience-versus-noncompliance split contradicts the answer’s own test. `Skip system authorization check` and `Dispatch without scan` are named move-on-log deviations, so they are visible waivers. The concealed gaps are the move-on-model absences: no quality/release check anywhere in the manual DFG, missing temperature records, and dispatch with no proof of delivery. Calling C the clearest concealed noncompliance reverses that distinction. Fast lane (1,000) is the path that omits authorization without a skip label.

F is graded too cleanly. Temporary release still flows to dispatch without scan in 1,450 of 1,650 cases, and T2 controlled-substance dual-control misses are 2.9% versus a 0.2% baseline. That is an outcome failure of the compensating control during the period it was used, not only a cold-chain scope gap. I is treated as already-explicit resilience while J is treated as ambiguous, although the log shows the same lack of governance evidence for both.

Smaller quantitative overreads remain. “About 200 still open” is the 2,300−2,100 audit-remediation residual on a DFG the answer elsewhere says does not balance. “About 3,500 cases” adds the 2,300 and 1,200 remediation arcs and then calls them cases. “4,500 cases passed” the snapshot includes 580 deferrals. T4 traceability is 2.75× baseline, outside the stated “2 to 2.5×” band. Hospital median time returned; p90 is 16h versus 15h. T4’s stated 3.0% cancellation rate is not the same as `Cancel order → END` 750/35,000. Spreadsheet invoice hold, a named move-on-log deviation and the recorded end state for about 2,100 manual cases, is barely interpreted. The sequence symbol in the notation key is missing, and the fairness baseline “ratio ≈ 1.0” is undefined against deliberately different segment cycle times.
