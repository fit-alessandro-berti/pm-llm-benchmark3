5.4

The case table is mostly right, but the required formalization and checking code are not usable, and several classifications and examples are loose.

Temporal reconstruction is largely sound: forward-fill, blanks as non-updates, no backward projection of final or settlement values, and `event_index` as the same-timestamp tie-break. “Material data change” is narrowed to an increase above 20 percent, so later changes in risk, jurisdiction, or customer type are not defined as ordering events for R1.

The rule table has the requested columns and the right triggers for R1–R3, including that exemptions waive only R1 and that a standard-packet approval does not satisfy R2. The pseudo-logic does not. Quantifiers and connectives are missing, comparisons are collapsed (`jurisdictionDE`, `amount100000`, `effective_fromt_triggerexpiry`), `max_material_change` and `prev_amount` are undefined, and R7 reads as both `time <` and `time =`. R4 is not composed back into what counts as a still-valid review.

Case outcomes that hold up are TX-301, TX-303, TX-304, TX-305, TX-306, TX-307, TX-308, TX-309, and TX-311. TX-302 is the clear miss: the 09:20 Enhanced_Review already meets R1, with no later material change, so the valid EX-77 signature makes this COMPLIANT; JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION implies a deviation that is not there. TX-310 is only barely defensible as INDETERMINATE. The certificate is created at 10:30, after release, the displayed 08:50 time is the non-conclusive backdate R5 warns about, and the witness is the requester, which is closer to an unproven exemption and therefore NONCOMPLIANT. Section 4 never states the TX-302 review-versus-exemption fork, and its TX-311 counterfactual is empty: release is at 16:30, so jurisdiction is DE at release either way.

The Python block is not executable. The log stops at TX-301 and literally contains `...`. It forward-fills and calls `format_dataframe`, but it never tracks `Sanctions_List_Update` or the latest material amount change, never flags R3 or R4, never joins the exemption register, and does not show a DECLARE or trace-order check. Section 6 names the six required failure modes, then wrongly says EX-77 fails the evidence-time test; the qualified signature at 09:05 is why it does not. Section 7 does cover the requested logging fixes.
