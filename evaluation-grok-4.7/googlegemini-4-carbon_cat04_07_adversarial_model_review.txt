8.2

The review is strong and, on the central modeling questions, mostly accurate. It separates exploration from enforcement, refuses a composite score, reads both process trees correctly on B1–B8, and proposes a real third design rather than a cosmetic middle ground. The POWL backbone is the right shape: both initial checks are mandatory and unordered, expedite is ordered after both, supplier stays concurrent with expedite, approval is a true XOR, and `LOOP(τ, Correct → XOR(credit, tech, both))` gives zero-or-more targeted rework without a silent empty correction. The attribute layer’s `VALID` / `VIOLATION` / `INDETERMINATE` split respects the missing `sourcing_type` and `affected_checks` fields instead of optionalizing those obligations. Probe and adversarial traces A1–A4 follow from those semantics. The validation program and the dissent are concrete.

The metric section still misstates the main discriminating number. Holdout alignment fitness 0.744 means average normalized alignment cost is about 0.256. It does not mean Model C fails on ~25% of holdout alignments or “flags 25% of holdout behavior.” That slip is repeated in the governance recommendation and is exactly the kind of fitness reading this review is supposed to police. The same table undercounts Model S as 7 labeled nodes plus 4 silent nodes; the tree has 8 labeled leaves (Register, Credit, Technical, Supplier, Expedite, Manager, Auto, Release) and 4 `τ`s. The ~0.008 training-fitness gap is also assigned to the missing `Correct Dossier` as if that were established, and the 0.229 holdout drop is attributed wholly to legitimate unseen combinations when F4 also allows missing supplier events and ordinary noncompliance.

Smaller defects: the prose cost schedule is 10/2/1 while the skeleton uses 10000/2000/1000 with no scaling note; the text promises a stratified split and the code shuffles; the set notation `$R_i = \alpha.affected\_checks[i] \subseteq \ldots$` is not well-formed. The forced full initial pass before any correction is a real extra constraint and is only partly disclosed. None of that breaks the third model, but it keeps the paper short of nearly flawless.
