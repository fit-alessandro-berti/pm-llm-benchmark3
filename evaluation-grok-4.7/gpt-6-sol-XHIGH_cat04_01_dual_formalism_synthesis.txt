The POWL model is the right canonical object: the partial order, the XOR risk and supplier choices, the ordered execute–inspect loop body, and the three-way release join match the activity-level specification, and the eight witness verdicts are internally consistent with that model. The process-tree code, parent links, loop orientation, and declared under-approximation are also coherent, and the answer does not pretend conversion or soundness would prove fidelity.

The main semantic shortfall is the tree’s approximation, not its syntax. Forcing the whole analysis-side parallel (design, the selected reviews, and consultation) to finish before the trial deletes requirement 7 entirely, including reviews that finish only after the last inspection and before release. That is a legal conservative under-approximation, and it is disclosed, but it is not shown to be the most faithful block-structured subset. Another series-parallel under-approximation can keep approval and consultation concurrent with the trial by giving up some preparation-phase interleaving. The write-up never compares those lost languages, so “as faithful as possible” is asserted rather than argued. The later sentence that the tree excludes overlap “during any trial cycle” is also narrower than the constraint actually imposed.

F1’s earliest violation is identified correctly, but calling containment-before-planning a cross-branch dependency is wrong. That edge is local to one stream. The cross-stream join is F2, where release precedes the low-risk review; the reason column notes the tree dies earlier, at Execute Trial, yet never states that the POWL prefix through Inspect Trial is still legal. “Causal concurrency” is an unclear name for an absence of order, though the following sentence repairs it.

In the comparison table, missing risk, supplier, and pass/fail data guards are scored as underconstraints. The task places those guards outside the control-flow model and asks for exactly the XOR and loop encoding used here. Those rows blur a mandated abstraction with a modeling defect. The self-critique repeats that data gap and the completion-time gap, then only vaguely suggests that some other tree policy might be better, without naming which intended traces it would recover or which unsafe ones it might admit.
