7.5

The transportability judgment is sound: conditional go, no universal copy of Site A, no Document Check before originals, and no treatment of the 9-hour median drop as an identified effect. Sections 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, and 10 hit the required structure and the right mechanisms (true overlap vs. recorded overlap, co-interventions, bottleneck shift, segmented pilot).

Deductions are for fidelity and quantitative looseness, not for the recommendation.

The POWL code does not match PM4Py’s object model. `Activity`, `StrictPartialOrder()` with no nodes, and `add_node` / `add_edge` are not the constructors; activities are `Transition`s, order is `po.order.add_edge(...)`, and an optional legal branch plus supplement loop belongs in `OperatorPOWL` (`Operator.XOR`, `Operator.LOOP`). The legal model is a separate, always-on, once-unrolled path that omits Reject, so it is neither optional nor a loop. Site A nets are built and never checked. Defensive `try/except` does not repair that.

The causal graph sends `API_latency` to `identity_capacity` (capacity is headcount; latency changes service time). Scenario ranges are not derived from the DFG counts or from service times: identity is only 3.4h and originals delay is 18h for 62% of cases, so an overall median of −4h to −7h is not reconciled with a 38% full-parallel share, and mixture medians are not weighted averages of subgroup medians. The DFG (2,108 originals→document, 360 supplements, 1,360 legal) is barely used. Assumption 8’s “if false” consequence does not follow, and the pilot’s “400 cases or posterior >0.9” is not a power calculation.
