7.5

The design is strong and mostly faithful to the problem: the primary estimand is an observed-policy activated-default cumulative incidence, rejected/withdrawn/open cases are not coded as non-defaults, the 48,000-case flow is complete, censoring mechanisms are separated, and the leakage table and temporal split are the right instruments.

It is not nearly flawless. The 90-day clock starts at activation while the CIF is taken from `T0`, and the optional 180-day cap is never operationalized, so follow-up length still depends on the extraction date. Permanently missing partner feeds are not located inside the 48,000 partition, so the 18,900 “mature non-defaults” may be contaminated. The +35-day maturity rule and the `label_available_at` rule are not reconciled, and IPCW is loosely offered for “informative” missingness. The primary train window is entirely pre-policy.

The skeleton is the weakest part. It is far longer than the requested 80–160 lines. Cutoffs are timezone-aware while `read_csv` timestamps are not, so the maturity filter and the temporal split would raise on comparison. `get_prefixes_from_log`, the DFG, and variants are built on the full future-inclusive log and are not the feature table. `eligible_for_early_warning` is computed and then ignored, and capping `T0` at activation keeps the activation event (`timestamp <= T0`).
