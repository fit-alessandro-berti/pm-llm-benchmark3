3.5

The audit has the right five-part shape and correctly flags several obvious leaks (`total_case_duration_hours`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_handler_team`, the prior-claim SQL predicate, the nightly document snapshot, mutable `risk_score_current`, and end-of-case `last_resource_role`). Those hits are outweighed by misclassification of the decision-time problem and by revised explanations that put future outcomes back in.

At `t0`, immediately after the first Coverage Check, a conforming prefix contains only registration and coverage. Appeal, document cycles, damage assessment, settlement, and closure are all still in the future. `appeal_seen_anywhere = 1` on that prefix is critical leakage, and an appeal is process-impossible before a Partial or Deny. The table rates it only High and proposes `appeal_seen_before_t0`. Case X then treats that invented flag as a credible driver and narrates “prior appeal history.” That restates the leaked appeal.

The same error is repeated for patterns. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is a legitimate later sequence after Partial or Deny, and it is still unavailable at `t0`; calling it Low and “not leakage,” and saying to retain it, confuses process legality with temporal admissibility. `Documents Requested -> Documents Received` and `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` are likewise future-trace patterns, not prefix signals. `Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is a normal post-`t0` edge; the same-timestamp defect belongs only to `Claim Closed` and `Damage Assessment Completed`, whose order is normatively implausible and unresolvable because the sequence index is missing. Labeling closure-before-assessment “future-possible” reverses that distinction.

`queue_length_at_t0` is a real congestion state with a ±5-minute tolerance, not a future-queue feature. `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` is a valid slack signal. Case X files the elapsed-time decrease under removed drivers while saying it was retained, and drops it from the narrative. Case Y drops the risk-reducing queue of 4. Both legitimate signals the audit was supposed to preserve are lost in the case write-ups.

Coverage is incomplete. `final_decision = Partial` never appears; it is a post-`t0` outcome. `claimant_channel = Broker` never appears. `initial_reserve` is dropped because it is “not in top-12,” which is not a lineage finding; if the reserve is frozen at registration it can be a severity association, and if it is later revised it is mutable. Employee experience joined as of extraction date is never addressed. Case X is marked “retain” and Case Y “recompute” even though both published scores come from a leaked feature set. Case Y’s “prefix path” retells `DOC-DOC-DMG-DEC-APP-DEC-CLOSE`, which is the full future variant. Case Z’s note omits the same-day document snapshot and the week-52 policy/holiday confound.

Section 2 calls a DFG a declarative model and uses it to enforce rules, despite frequent edges not being normative. The stated rules are wrong: appeal does not gate damage assessment, and closure does not precede damage assessment. Enabled, future-possible, and impossible are illustrated with incorrect examples. Missing operators leave several sentences unreadable.

Section 3 states causal claims (“take longer due to inherent uncertainty,” “weekend/holiday periods may slow processing,” “systemic biases”) inside “what may be claimed,” then says causal claims are disallowed. `calendar_week` is week-of-year confounding with a policy change and holidays, not a weekend effect. `prior_claim_count` is a lineage defect, not by itself a region-style fairness proxy. Predictive association, causal effect, operational actionability, and fairness review are never separated. Unusable-until-repaired omits the main leaked globals.

Section 5 names the requested topics, but conformance is described as a 10% outcome holdout, counterfactuals are offered as a way to isolate causation, and schema/process drift is ranked Low. The plan does not require as-of joins, prefix-only refits, or invalidating probabilities until those refits exist.
