6.3

The recommendation is the right one — conditional go, with blanket copying rejected — and Site A’s before/after change is correctly treated as confounded. The write-up still fails several checks that the task makes central: partial-order fidelity, transport math, and a pilot that could actually identify the mechanism.

Process structure is only partly right. Site B is not a pure sequence. Identity Check and Originals Receipt may both start after Register; Document Check is blocked only when originals are legally required (62%). Overlap is already 6%. Equating that 38% non-originals share with “digital claims” is unsupported and is repeated in eligibility, scenarios, and the recommendation. The directly-follows counts are unused. Originals Receipt → Document Check is exactly 0.62 × 3,400 = 2,108, and Identity Check → Document Check (1,160) is the real sequential opportunity; that evidence never enters the design.

The transport graph includes the required nodes and selection nodes, but several edges are the wrong causal claims: `digital_documents → originals_required`, `originals_required → true_overlap` (originals block document start), `legal_capacity → rework`, and `S_legal_strictness → legal_capacity` (strictness is not staffing). The prose is about 150 words, short of the required 180–280, and it treats pre-existing WIP limits and Site A huddles as if they were the parallelization mechanism.

The evidence table is the strongest part: mix shift, added document FTEs, API latency, huddles, and timestamp changes are separated from the treatment, and the 9-hour median drop is not taken as an effect. The empty confidence cell on the negative-control row breaks the 0–100 column.

Scenarios name the right failure modes, including a case where the median improves and the p90 worsens, but the hour ranges are not derived from service times, the 18-hour originals delay, or pool sizes. A 5–8 hour stratum effect reprints Site A’s confounded wait drop. Portfolio medians are computed as share × stratum median, which is not how medians combine. Eligible share is internally inconsistent: 38% in the intervention, ~25% in one scenario, “25% digital, 15% non-legal digital” in another, and 20–25% in the recommendation. Parallel identity and document checks are not illegal for legal-review cases; the statutory bar is originals before Document Check, and Assessment (and Legal Review where required) still wait.

The pilot’s T0/T1/T2 contrast is a real attempt to separate queue discipline, logging artifacts, and parallelization. It is not executable as specified. Case-level randomization on shared identity, document, and assessor pools does not hold congestion constant. Eight strata × three arms × 200 cases is thousands of cases; two teams over 8–16 weeks cannot supply that, especially inside a ~25% slice. A +1 pp stop on 90-day appeals cannot be observed in two weeks. Interactions with originals and legal flags are promised for strata excluded from treatment. “Eight teams” is not in the evidence.

The PM4Py block builds the two Site A trees with the requested calls, then the Site B model breaks. `Operator.XOR`, `SEQUENCE`, and `PARALLEL` are not constructors in `pm4py.objects.powl.obj`; POWL concurrency is a strict partial order without an edge, and a loop is `OperatorPOWL(Operator.LOOP, [do, redo])`. The `parallel` node is unused. `doc_check` is placed both in the partial order and inside the choice, which is illegal parentage. Originals always precede Document Check, so the digital path is not in the net. The supplement path is a one-shot sequence, not a loop, and it does not repeat Assessment. `decision_lr` is dead. `play_out(net)` does not return one trace, and soundness checks are invoked without the markings those functions require. The limitation comment is accurate and does not repair the model.

Two of the eight assumptions fail their own tests. If originals delay is not the binding constraint, eligibility does not automatically expand past 38%. An arm without a WIP cap does not test whether huddles, rather than parallelization, produced Site A’s change.

Conditional go, a narrow first stratum, preserved statutory order, and explicit confounders are the parts that hold. The transport graph, the numeric scenarios, the pilot’s sample size and timing, and the Petri/POWL model do not.
