The write-up follows the requested nine-part structure and reaches several sound case judgments: keep e05 and e14 in the recommended pipelines, treat e08/e09 as a harmonization collision, keep pack and payment summaries in the conservative path, order e09 before e10 from the carrier sequence, and refuse ingestion time as business order. That core is not enough to carry the submission. Required mechanics, counts, and several local claims are wrong or mutually inconsistent.

The implementation misses the contract. The block never calls `pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates`, `pm4py.ocel_merge_duplicates`, `pm4py.ocel_sort_by_additional_column`, or `pm4py.ocel_add_index_based_timedelta`. The library path only prints a warning and deletes a hardcoded list. The verified-pair helper does not union attributes: it copies a keeper row and fills only nulls, so the stated location/line-item merge never happens. Provenance written by the code is a thin action log, not the section 8 schema. Referential integrity is a single eid assertion. `source_seq` is compared across sources, and the topological queue is seeded from a set, so the sort is not the deterministic within-source policy described in prose. After the stated constraints, e05 can land after e06.

Event facts are mishandled. Fact 1 puts the location enrichment on WMS e03; sections 2 and 4 take it from scanner e04. The e06/e07 row says retain both and also merge payloads. Section 2 adds a microsecond tie-break for e09/e10, while sections 3 and 4 forbid encoding order in the timestamp. The admissible order e11, e12, e13, e15, e14 places the invoice-level summary before M2 despite fact 8. Attribute authority for invoice amount and billing line items is invented.

The aggressive library account is inconsistent. Same activity is the merge key, so Authorize Payment e12 is not in a group with Capture Payment. The drop list actually keeps e12 and e13 and drops e14 and e15, but the prose says those payment events collapse into e12 and become one node. `have_common_object=True` is never analyzed. That flag still merges e13 with e14 because they share O1 and V1 while referring to M1 and M2.

Sensitivity figures are not derived from the log. Raw E2O size is 44, not about 32; conservative retention of duplicate object links is 39, not 30. The evidence-based column says item picks were merged even though that scenario keeps e05, and it says M1/M2 weights merge even though e14 remains. Raw order-flattened behavior is described as having no rework, although repeated labels already produce Pack→Pack, Dispatch→Dispatch, Invoice→Invoice, and Capture→Capture. One of the six “changed” patterns is explicitly unchanged.

Failure-mode text reverses two of its own examples. e03 is ingested at 10:00:01 and e04 at 10:00:03, so ingestion order does not put e04 first. Union of E2O links keeps the WMS object set; it does not drop that enrichment. The reconstruction clones the cleaned row, nulls discarded fields, and restores no relations, so it cannot rebuild the raw view.
