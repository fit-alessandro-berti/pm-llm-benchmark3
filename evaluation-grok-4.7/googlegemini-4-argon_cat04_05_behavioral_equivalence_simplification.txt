6.5

The qualitative verdicts are right: A and B are not visibly trace-equivalent, not P1-equivalent, not P3-equivalent, and they match only after the P2 abstraction. The four counterexamples are valid, the loop reading is correct (body at least once; exactly \(k\) checks in A and exactly one in B), and the P2 simplification really does preserve \(\alpha_{P2}\) while dropping approval, concurrency, and per-iteration checks. That is the core of the task, and it is handled well.

The reported bounded cardinalities are not. Under length \(\le 12\) and at most three `Analyze Revision` events, the visible languages have 76 traces (A) and 85 traces (B), not 62 and 68. Intersection 4 is the one correct count: only the four \(k=1\) traces in which `Approve Revision` does not precede `Check Revision`. For A, each of \(k\) iterations has 4 visible patterns (both orders of analyze/check, with or without approval), giving \(4+16+64\); the 8 traces with three approvals have length 13 and fall outside the bound, leaving 76. For B, every \(k\le 3\) trace has length \(\le 11\), and inserting the single check into each approval-skeleton gives \(5+20+60=85\). Stating 62 and 68 as exhaustive state-space results, with “high” confidence, is a false measurement. A correct run of the BFS would not print those figures.

The footprint write-up is also wrong on its own directly-follows definition. In A, `Rework Case` can be immediately followed by `Check Revision` and the converse also occurs, so that pair is parallel in the footprint of both models. Only pairs such as `Check Revision` with `Approve Revision` actually separate them. Equal footprints still would not give trace equivalence; that caveat is fine, but the claimed difference is not.

Other problems keep the score down. `simplicity_petri_net` is called identical and then given an underived range \(\approx 0.67\text{–}0.73\); same operator multiset does not imply the same net degrees when the parallel sits in different scopes. Soundness handling is inconsistent (`sound_a[0]` versus `is_sound, _ = ...`) and will crash if `check_soundness` returns a bool or a diagnostics dict, so the script never reaches the comparison. P1 equivalence is defined as full language equality, which collapses it into visible-trace equivalence despite the extra invariant \(\Phi_{P1}\). “Strictly simpler in every dimension” is false for duplicate labels, which stay 0, and “Model A is already minimal” is asserted rather than shown. The BFS idea itself (marking plus visible trace, \(\tau\) left invisible, bounds before enqueue, final-marking acceptance, playout labeled non-exhaustive) is sound; the published numbers and the footprint contrast are not.
