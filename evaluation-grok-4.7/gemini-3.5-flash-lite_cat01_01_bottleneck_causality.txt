5.2

The crude-versus-stratified reversal is real and correctly read (Orion 21.96 h vs Nova 15.24 h overall; Orion lower in every supplied complexity row: 7.50 vs 9.64, 17.17 vs 19.38, 31.57 vs 35.00), and rejecting a blind 50% move is the right operational direction. The write-up then overclaims that pattern, mislabels estimands, and gets several process facts wrong.

Section 1 calls the crude gap “entirely” an artifact and says stratification “completely reverses” it, while the only common-support cells are tiny (Low Orion n=2, High Nova n=2; no complexity×shift cell has three or more cases in both groups). “Minor negative mediation through shift/compliance” is the wrong role and the wrong sign language: night share and compliance are largely co-assigned with difficulty, so they confound the crude contrast. Total, direct, and mediated effects are never separated. Confidence 75 is high for that identification. “Will fail,” higher appeals, and Nova “lacking” night coverage are stronger than the data (one appeal in each group; Nova already has night cases).

The DAG does not match the prose. `shift` and `arrival_load` affect `queue_wait` but do not point into routing or `resource_group`, so the graph does not encode the confounding story. `complexity` does not affect `review_service` or `queue_wait` except through compliance. `unobserved_reviewer_skill` never meets `resource_group`, so it is neither confounder nor part of the treatment bundle. `case_completed → cycle_time` is the wrong direction, and the node is not a collider in the adjacency list even though the text calls it one. Compliance is not deterministic: every Low case is No, every High case is Yes, and Medium is split. `queue_wait + review_service` is also not cycle time (residuals cluster near 0.8 h, about 2.2 h with compliance, about 3.7 h with compliance and rework).

Section 3 repeats the estimand error: complexity stratification is described as a “direct effect,” and the queue path is called a “direct path.” `compliance_required` is treated as a mediator of group although policy, not the group label, determines it. Rework is called a collider without a collider structure. “Truncation-by-zero” is the wrong selection term.

Section 4 copies the complexity table and then stops being auditable. Within Low, night cycle time is shorter than day, so “night shifts aggregate longer queues” is the confounded aggregate. Within Medium, compliance-required cases are not slower (about 17.8 h and 9.3 h wait versus about 18.3 h and 10.7 h wait when not required). The group gap inside complexity is mostly wait (about 1.6–3.3 h), while review service differs by only a few tenths of an hour; that comparison is asserted, not shown. “Rework adds ~7–8 hours” is the crude, complexity-confounded gap. Within stratum the increment is about 1–2 h, and rework cases do not have higher `review_service_h`. The allowed reading that the within-complexity “direct effect” is unconfounded if no unmeasured confounders remain is the mistake the task forbids: shift and load remain badly imbalanced inside those strata.

The five hypothesis types are present, and direct standardization is a valid check of H1. H2’s mechanism is queueing, but the predicted observation is a service-time tie, which does not test it. H4’s positive Group×Complexity interaction is not what a uniform harmful Orion effect would show, and the same row already reports Orion faster in every stratum. H5 treats “no dropped cases in the file” as evidence against selection; that absence is what the selection story predicts. “Performance gap widens” does not say which gap.

The pilot and the staffing phase match the required intervention types, and limiting the pilot to Medium cases is safer than moving High night work. Phase 1 is an analysis, not an intervention, and with this overlap it will not “establish” a controlled effect. The pilot excludes the compliance and High cases that drive the decision, does not say what happens to shift when the group is randomized, and its 20% stop rule has no baseline. The closing recommendation is about 96 words against a 120–200 requirement and still states that reassignment will raise rework and harm quality.

The Python loads the CSV, reproduces the crude and complexity means, standardizes with the pooled complexity distribution, and warns when a complexity×shift×group cell has n<3. It does not use `parse_event_log_string`. It fabricates hourly timestamps after being told not to fabricate timestamps, and the DFG “comparison” is an edge count. Both groups already contain the same four variants, so that count does not test a mechanism. The diagnostics are not a causal proof, and the code does not claim they are.

Section 8’s five uncertainties are the right kind (residual risk, reviewer identity, completion, queue logs, a finer outcome). They do not repair the estimand errors above.
