**5.0**

The final policy is complete in form and gets the prompt’s explicit traps right: I-03 is ineligible once diagnosis is complete, I-05 is suppressed as an unchanged refresh, I-07 can re-enter because of the new log, one slot is reserved, and effects are not treated as equal to `p_now`. Those points are not enough to carry the allocation or the impact accounting.

The audited base-case spillover is wrong by 10×. With the answer’s own inputs, \(3 \times 6 \times 40{,}000 \times 0.015 = 10{,}800\), not \(1{,}080\); the optimistic and adverse figures are \(7{,}200\) and \(21{,}600\), not \(720\) and \(2{,}160\). Under those assumptions the base and adverse nets are negative, so “moderate net positive” is the wrong conclusion. “2–3 breaches plausibly avoided” is also inconsistent with the ARR sums (about \(0.75\) in the base case, under \(1\) even at the upper bounds). False-alarm burdens are omitted from the netting.

Case selection does not follow expected loss reduction. I-07’s direct value is roughly \(0.26 \times 5{,}000 - 450 - 50 \approx 800\), which does not clear a per-slot spillover on six diagnosis-queue cases, yet it is Tier 1. I-01 is both “Wait” in the table and treated as intervened in the portfolio and impact table. I-04 (\(0.19 \times 60{,}000\) before costs and calibration) is deferred mainly because the deadline is 10 hours away. I-02 and I-09 are dropped as if a second alert with no prior swarm were the same as I-05’s documented no-new-event refresh; the repeated-swarm ban does not say that. I-06’s imputed queue jump is noted and then still given a slot.

Calibration error in breach probability is applied as an arbitrary \(\pm 0.02\) shift to the ARR midpoint, then not actually used in the scenario arithmetic. The stated bias ranges are about probability, not effect size, and an ARR cannot exceed true risk. The monitoring plan treats a later change in predicted `p` as an “actual” effect; that is not a causal estimate, and the spillover check is confounded by intervening when the queue is already congested. Non-monetary duties are thin, triage cases are folded into the diagnosis-queue externality, and the NLLS adjustment in section 5 contradicts the unadjusted midpoint rule in section 1.
