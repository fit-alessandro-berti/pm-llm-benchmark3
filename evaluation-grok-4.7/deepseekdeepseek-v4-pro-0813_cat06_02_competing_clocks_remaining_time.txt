The final six-section forecast is structurally complete and gets the main process logic right: the join is the slower of the two branches, microbiology plus the weekend staffing calendar governs the clean path, chemistry can spill past Friday 18:00, and the temperature-excursion flag is treated as a dependence that fattens the joint rework tail. The scenario set, calendar pauses, and release-versus-rejection distinction are all present, and the headline ranges are wide enough to be usable.

The QA-queue conclusion is not. “Likely 0–2 batches ahead, wait 0–2 h” by a Monday join treats median service (1.5 h) as if it were throughput. With median 1.5 h and an 80th percentile of 3.5 h, mean service is about 2–2.5 h, so one reviewer from Friday 15:30 through Saturday barely drains a queue of five at 0.35 arrivals per open hour. A Monday-morning join still sees a multi-batch backlog; only a late-afternoon join has a real chance of finding QA clear. That interaction — fast chemistry meets a still-full QA queue, slow chemistry meets a draining one — is missing, so the smooth-path terminal window of Monday 12:00–20:00 is early, and QA congestion is ranked too low.

The summary table also disagrees with itself. Monday 21 Sep 11:00–12:00 is 67.5–68.5 h after Friday 15:30, not 68–70 h. The P80 and severe-tail hour labels are similarly rounded past the clocks they are paired with (Thursday 18:00 is about 146 h, not 150; Tuesday 29 Sep 18:00 is about 266 h, not 270). The P80 band out to Thursday is loose relative to a laboratory-investigation probability of only several percent, while the severe upper end stops on Tuesday 29 Sep and does not carry a late contamination or chemistry spill plus a 7-calendar-day laboratory investigation through the following QA resumption.

Smaller gaps: the chemistry-rerun and contamination rows reuse the smooth earliest clock without showing the slack that would make that possible; marginal rerun and contamination bands stay near the unconditional baselines even though a confirmed excursion is supposed to lift both; and “rare” rejection is asserted with no defect rate in the evidence.
