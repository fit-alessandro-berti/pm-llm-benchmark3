4.5

The write-up has the required headings, all 15 cases, and several defensible final labels (SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-706, SUP-708, SUP-712, SUP-714). It is not reliable as a conformance judgment. It misreads evidence, misstates authority, violates its own burden standard, and delivers a POWL that does not mean what the prose claims.

Stipulated facts are incomplete and partly wrong. SUP-703 is described as missing eligibility data; the record says the supplier was a known high-risk foreign supplier and the operator chose “pilot” anyway. The distinct-human rule is both stipulated and then listed as disputed. There is no separate unproved category, although linkage of SUP-706, master-data validation, scope of rejected leads, and any post-expiry emergency extension are unproved rather than merely contested. The burden test treats “data integrity” as noncompliance and then is not applied to SUP-705.

The prosecution does not acknowledge genuine model or logging defects. It calls the late mutable CRM link in SUP-706 “evidence tampering,” which the facts do not establish. It applies CS-9’s requester/approver rule to SUP-715, where the stated duty is an undisclosed conflict of interest and no rule forbids the relationship itself. It barely uses the strongest authority facts: PD-3 was never incorporated into CS-9, and the compliance observer later denied approving a weakening of CS-9. The matrix then says the prosecution view of SUP-702 is that “the pilot expired,” which is false: 2026-06-15 is inside 2026-06-01 through 2026-07-31.

The defense does not acknowledge genuine risk or the limit of informal practice. It claims operational necessity can override an expired directive. It misreads SUP-708: Quality_Review is before activation; Sanctions_Check is after it. That case is not evidence that concurrency was modeled wrongly. It treats SUP-714 as the same kind of pilot behavior as SUP-702. “Strictly concurrent” is also the wrong reading of an unordered pair in a strict partial order.

The matrix repeats those errors and adds logical ones. SUP-704 is “no manual approval” even though approval occurred the next business day, and the stated reverser concerns expiry for a case inside the window. SUP-710 is called noncompliant because CS-9 is silent, which inverts the burden. SUP-711’s reverser asks for the same-source transaction ID that is already present and is what shows a duplicate. SUP-712’s reverser, delegation, would not cure a same-human prohibition. Abbreviated pre-screen, the 47 information requests that all waited for evidence, automated master-data validation, and the stable `notification_id` versus mutable-link contrast are largely unused.

SUP-705 is classified MIXED because it would be justified if an unwritten extension existed and noncompliant otherwise. That is the forbidden use of MIXED. On the answer’s own burden rule, the dominant result is NONCOMPLIANT. SUP-702 is JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION at high confidence despite contested authority and no validation event. The system totals are invented: the cited buckets are roughly 5, 2, and 6 of 15 cases, not 30/20/50, and SUP-705 is counted as a justified exception while also being mixed. “Partially excusable” blurs violations with model gaps.

The architecture omits withdrawals, under-specifies conflict-of-interest disclosure, and resolves rejected leads by fiat. The standard route is described as sequential after being defined as concurrent. The emergency path needs a second activation after retrospective review, so it does not replay SUP-704 or SUP-705. Historical pilot dates are hardcoded into what is presented as the revised normative model, leaving a dead branch rather than a versioned overlay.

The code does not implement one revised POWL. It builds three disconnected models, reuses the same transition objects across them, and falsely claims POWL cannot combine alternatives. The information-request “loop” is an unattached self-edge on a strict partial order, which is not a partial order, and a silent transition would not match observed `Request_More_Info` events. The eligibility function omits the DE requirement and master-data validation, and calls `pd.to_datetime` with pandas never imported. Aliases are made mutable, delegation evidence is absent, and effective time is not separated from record time. The cap, two-business-day deadline, daily monitoring, and bitemporal notification identity are not enforced anywhere executable.
