The policy is coherent in outline and gets the mandatory calls right: I-03 is stage-ineligible, I-05 is suppressed as a no-event refresh, I-07 re-enters on the new log but is not given a swarm, calibration is segment-adjusted, and queue harm is priced rather than ignored. The case-level EDB arithmetic checks out, and sections 5–6 are concrete. It is not a reliable deployable rule, because the stated rule does not generate the stated portfolio.

The core break is Gate 4 versus section 2. Deferral is waived when EDB exceeds both €2,000 and €3,500. I-08’s EDB is about €5,275, so the rule says intervene; the allocation defers it. The reserve may be released before 15:00 only if net value exceeds €4,500 and the deadline is under 2 hours. Confirmed I-06 (EDB about €2,600, 4 hours left) and deferred I-08 (net about €3,300, 8 hours left) both fail that test, yet the text still promises them Slot 4. “Lower-bound net benefit near zero” for I-08 is also wrong: 0.05 × €100,000 − €450 − 0.655 × €5,000 − €2,000 is about −€725.

I-02 is not robust to the spillover model used to justify it. The hurdle uses the midpoint, 6 × 0.02 × €16,700 ≈ €2,000, so €2,695 clears. At the top of the stated base band, 6 × 0.03 × €16,700 ≈ €3,000, I-02 fails. Its own lower-bound payoff is negative after spillover (about −€700). Ambiguity aversion is applied to treatment effects and not to externalities, congestion, or the full payoff, so the third slot is exactly the one a pessimistic rule would drop. The €16,700 untreated-case value is an untested assumption; the portfolio was not re-solved at the ends of that range.

Two specialists deliver one swarm. Three 45-minute slots are therefore about 2.25 hours of serial work, but Slot 1 is given to I-04 (10 hours, still in triage) ahead of I-09 (2.5 hours). No sequence is specified. That can consume I-09’s remaining window before the swarm starts.

Section 3 is mostly auditable and the optimistic and base nets follow from the stated inputs. The adverse upper gross of €8,600 does not follow from zeroing I-02 and applying the other lower bounds (that gross is about €9,600, or about €8,000 if I-09 is the zero instead). The €1,400–€5,500 benefit range mixes those stories. The adverse column is also net destructive (−€4.5k to −€17.8k), and there is no scenario weight explaining why a Hurwicz policy with w_low = 0.60 should still run the portfolio. False-alarm cost drifts from (1 − p_cal) × B_FA to round scenario totals of €1.0–1.5k and €2.5–3.1k.

Smaller policy holes: repeated-alert suppression treats I-05 as ineligible rather than as one quiet decision on a case that may never have been dispositioned (harmless today only because it also fails the hurdle); the weekly SME floor never says whether it can override a negative spillover hurdle; ECE < 0.03 is stricter than the 0.04 recalibration trigger and is unrealistic given the reported biases; non-monetary duty is collapsed into a €4,500 shadow price. The monitoring design itself is strong, including untreated-only calibration, tie-breaker randomization, and spillover triggers.
