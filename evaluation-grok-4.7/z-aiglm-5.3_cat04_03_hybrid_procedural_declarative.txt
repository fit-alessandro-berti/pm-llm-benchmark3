The graded text is only the final document, not the planning notes. That document is a strong opening that then stops being an answer.

Sections 1–2 do the hard part well. R5 and R6 are treated as incompatible policies, not blended. Resolution A (legal-strict) and Resolution B (gated exception) are both stated, B is chosen, and the compliance risk is real: the 24-hour check is detectable, the joint authorization is not, so those cases can only be `policy-dependent`. The R1–R12 table assigns each rule to a formalism that can actually express it, names an authoritative layer when several apply, and correctly refuses unconditional `precedence(Obtain Legal Opinion, Activate Vendor)` because ordinary activations have no opinion. `altresponse` for clarification cycles, `precedence(Run Sanctions Screening, Sanctions Escalation)`, skeleton frequencies for “at most once,” and a policy layer for four-eyes, emergency activation, and post-activation bank changes are the right splits. The AND of the three checks matches “all three must finish before classification.”

The submission then fails the required document. Headings 4–10 are absent: no `declare_model`, no `log_skeleton`, no `temporal_profile`, no resource checks, no T1–T8 matrix, no T6 verdict under both resolutions, and no deployment rule for conflicting layers. Section 3’s required explanation of concurrency, choice, the loop, terminals, and excluded post-activation behavior is also missing.

The process-tree code that does appear is not valid PM4Py. `Operator.LOOP` has two children, `do (redo do)*`. The code builds three (`τ`, `Request Clarification`→`Receive Clarification`, `τ`). `convert_to_petri_net` uses only `children[0]` and `children[1]`, so the third node is a dangling child, and tree checks that require exactly two loop children reject the model. The intended language `(Request; Receive)*` would be `LOOP(τ, SEQ(Request Clarification, Receive Clarification))` alone. The table also switches to undefined abbreviations (`RV`, `MA`, `RC`, …) while the task requires the visible labels in the encodings.

What exists of the policy design is about an 8. The missing seven sections and the malformed procedural model make the deliverable incomplete.
