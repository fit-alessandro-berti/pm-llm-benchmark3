5.5

The final answer has the required eight-part structure and the right high-level story: raw approval gaps are not discrimination, language-support and accommodation steps are legitimate but burdensome, risk scores are not neutral, and support steps should not be removed. Those points are grounded in the given variant shares, waits (3.8 and 4.7 days), and staffing notes.

It then mishandles the evidence the question is built on. Section 3 repeatedly treats raw variant shares and raw median waits as if they were already within-stratum, complexity-controlled estimates. It misses the residuals that remain after the stated controls: about +9.5 points on missing-document requests and +8 points on supervisor review for Group B, +4.1 days for Group C, and +5.7 points on appeals for Group D. It misreads V2 as a language-support path and calls the request-document gap “consistent with complexity,” which softens a disparity the adjusted table says survives documentation control. Fraud routing for Group D is treated as fully justified even though appeal and approval gaps remain after risk is controlled. V6 and the regional supervisor backlog are barely used.

The conclusion does not pick a calibrated verdict. It says the process is not procedurally fair, is procedurally fair for some groups, is indeterminate, and is not unfair because intent is unproven. Procedural unfairness does not require intent, so that last move is a category error.

Mitigations cover capacity, SLAs, handovers, explanations, and a risk-score check, but they omit a real audit of documentation completeness and ongoing monitoring of adjusted disparities. The accommodation SLA is set at 5 days against a 4.7-day median and is then described as reducing 4.7 days to 5. Staffing and wait-reduction figures (1–2 FTE, 2 days, 30%) are invented.

The PM4Py section claims realistic API use but would not run and does not do the requested analysis: imports and `pd.Median` are invalid, variant keys are activity tuples rather than “V4”, event filters are used as case strata, Group C is wrongly required to have language support, and `insert_case_service_waiting_time`, performance DFGs, and log-skeleton/DECLARE checks are imported or named and then not used. Mechanisms 1 and 3 overlap, and mechanism 4 adds an unsupported stress pathway.

Coverage is broad, but the adjusted-inference errors, contradictory verdict, and nonfunctional analysis plan are central failures under a strict reading.
