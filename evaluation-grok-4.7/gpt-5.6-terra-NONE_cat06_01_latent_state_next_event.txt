The state readings are generally right, and C6-A versus C6-B is differentiated for the right reasons: severity, injury, lot linkage, and duplicate similarity, rather than the shared prefix. Obligations, concurrency, queue pressure, and the completion-only log are used with real care. The write-up is still not nearly flawless, because several “next event” rankings conflict with the supplied control flow, clocks, and observation model.

C6-D is the clearest miss. The case is migration-affected, a customer attachment exists in the source system, and no evidence-cycle completion is in the feed. A late Customer Evidence Requested, Evidence Received, or Evidence Review Completed event is one of the most plausible next log lines. It is absent from the top three. Authority Notification is ranked instead, at 0.08–0.22. That cannot be the immediate next event unless Regulatory Assessment and, on the observed DFG, Disposition Recorded are both missing. R-R2 only opened the case at 12:40; assessment typically takes 2–14 hours, disposition follows, and the batch is at 16:00. Same-day notification is a tail, not a third mode near 0.15.

The same next-versus-downstream confusion recurs. C6-B gives Close — No Safety Issue 0.16–0.33 while marking it not process-enabled; on a normal feed the unlogged review completion should almost always appear first, and the historical review-to-close lag is about 3 hours. C6-E gives another Customer Evidence Requested 0.18–0.36 only 40 minutes after receipt, below the stated 1–5 hour review time, and again marks it not enabled. C6-C’s Authority Notification range has the same prerequisite problem, though with a slightly longer parallel-assessment window.

C6-D’s Disposition Recorded midpoint near 0.27 is also too high relative to the stated 8–15% delayed-event rate. Skipped regulatory completion plus a recorded disposition is a joint feed failure, not a main outcome. C6-A’s technical and regulatory completion mass is aggressive against 7- and 12-item queues and multi-hour medians, and its midpoint sum is essentially 1, leaving no residual for supplier analysis or another evidence cycle.

Smaller defects: C6-B’s S5 alternative treats an imminent future as a current latent state; some “contradicting” bullets merely restate that the predicted event has not happened yet; C6-A calls supplier analysis likely from three unconfirmed similars while the same-lot fact is two devices in one complaint; A, B, and E are not treated as contending for the same four-item review queue.
