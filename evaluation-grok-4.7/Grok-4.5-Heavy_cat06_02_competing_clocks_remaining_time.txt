6.4

The forecast has the right skeleton: parallel join as a maximum, weekend calendars, queue-versus-service split, conditional dependence on the temperature flag, overlapping scenario bands, and ranges rather than a single timestamp. That keeps it well above an “add the means” answer. The numerical decomposition is not reliable enough for a high score.

Friday chemistry capacity is misstated. From 15:30 to 18:00 there are 2.5 h left, so free analyst-time is about 2.1–3.4 h (0.5–1.3 h plus 1.6–2.1 h), not 1.5–2.5 h. One analyst never has a 1.5 h minimum service window, so “clears 0–2 samples” is infeasible; full Friday completions are 0–1. The smooth-path claim that B-417 starts Monday 09:00–13:00 and finishes 11:00–15:00 then treats that optimistic window as the main case, even though FIFO is the 55% regime and the ledger itself says FIFO adds 2–6 h. Partial Friday starts, which the pause rule allows, are also omitted, so the near-tie with Monday microbiology readout is missed and microbiology is dismissed too cleanly.

The contamination clock is off by about a day. A Monday ~09:00 readout plus 20–30 h is Tuesday morning to Tuesday afternoon, with a staffed reread still on Tuesday. “Technical ready … Wednesday 15:00” and a Wednesday-afternoon central case do not follow. That scenario also bundles ordinary weekend staffing lag, which does not move the terminal time once chemistry is later, with true confirmation delay, and then assigns the long timestamps to the whole band.

Section 4 does not match its own hours. 100 h from Friday 15:30 is Tuesday ~19:30, not early Wednesday; 180 h is about Saturday 26 September 03:30, and 350 h is about 3 October, not “28 September–6 October.” QA “8–10 reviewer-hours vs 5 + ≈4 arrivals” compares hours with batch counts; Friday–Saturday arrivals are about 3, not 4. The lower scenario probabilities sum to roughly 0.65, and overlap does not explain missing mass. The overall rejection band below 0.05–0.10 is asserted without a critical-defect rate.

The temperature-flag update and the chemistry-start secondary trigger are directionally sound, and the assumption ledger is doing real work. The headline shape (Monday floor, early-week center, multi-day QA tail, rare next-week laboratory tail, release as the usual terminal event) is defensible, but several clocks and the reported hour-to-date mapping are concretely wrong.
