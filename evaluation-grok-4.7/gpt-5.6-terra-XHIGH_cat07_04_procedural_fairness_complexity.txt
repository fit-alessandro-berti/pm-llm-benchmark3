The assessment is substantively strong: all required dimensions, estimands, mechanisms, limitations, and mitigations are covered; the reported figures match the tables; raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination; case mix is not used to dismiss residual burden; risk scores are not treated as neutral; and support steps are preserved rather than removed to shorten cycle time. The bottom line is appropriately limited to a credible fairness risk.

The score is held down by concrete defects in the analysis plan and a few loose inferences. The worked regression is described as a document-request model but estimates entry to language support, and it omits language need, so the coefficient mostly rediscovers legitimate routing. Variant code calls `len` on `get_variants` values, which for a dataframe are counts, not case-id lists. Direct-successor DFG, path-performance, and `shift(-1)` elapsed-time logic do not measure the reported Language Support → Eligibility interval, because V4 places completeness and document request between those activities. Performance DFGs are left on the default mean despite median evidence; DECLARE discovery has no support or confidence threshold; `"non_majority_language"` is an invented attribute value; and matching weights are attached and then unused. “Especially in the upper tail” is attached to the adjusted cycle-time gaps, while the tail figures are unadjusted.
