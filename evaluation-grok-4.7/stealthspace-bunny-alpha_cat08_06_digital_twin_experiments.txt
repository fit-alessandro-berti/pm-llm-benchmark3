7.6

The design is a serious digital-twin plan: open population, calendars, separate service, queue, off-shift, applicant, statutory, and bundle time, latent objections, and validation that does not treat replay as a counterfactual proof. The score stays well below excellent because of process-tree errors, a few design contradictions, and a skeleton that does not faithfully build the excerpt, call PM4Py, or advance a non-stationary arrival clock.

**Process-tree and measurement errors.** `*(->('Inspection scheduling','Site inspection'), 'Inspection rework')` is a loop back to scheduling and inspection after the redo activity. Calling that “sequential, not a loop” misstates process-tree semantics. The sequence after the parallel review block is already a join; the real defect is that the tree over-orders public notice and inspection and omits planning, legal, and appeal loops. Consecutive inter-event gaps are also used as time columns even though planning, fire, and environmental review can overlap, so those gaps are not waits. With no start timestamps, the full gap is stored in `waiting_time_days`. The quality flag only partly repairs that. Performance-DFG sojourns have the same confounding and are not caveated where they are produced. No-shows, which the log summary flags for site inspection, never appear as a state or a parameter.

**Design contradictions.** The estimation text correctly refuses to multiply the 1.7 seasonal factor by the Monday factor, then `HawkesArrivalSource.intensity` does exactly that, and `mean_daily_arrivals` is not renormalized for peak months, so the configured mean is not the realized mean. S1 stacks “at least 1.7×” on a baseline that already contains 1.7× peaks, plus another multiplier up to 1.5, without saying which reference period is being scaled. Warm-up mixes a historical backlog snapshot, an extra peak-and-trough warm-up, a six-month measurement window, a 365-day horizon, and a drain. Complex-variance p90 is about 260 days, so a six-month window censors the tail the decision rule cares about; the cohort/drain idea is the right remedy, but it is not tied to one run definition. The illustrative SLAs put Simple renovation at 80% within 30 days and Commercial at 95% within 90 days, against medians of 12–22 days and 68 days, with no reason for the reversed reliability targets. Neighborhood objections that hit several related cases stay optional until a relationship file appears, so one of the three stated cross-case mechanisms has no default latent shock. D1’s volume and timing response is named as a trade-off and then excluded by the common-demand rule, with no adoption level in the factor table.

**Skeleton vs PM4Py and the simulation clock.** `zip(CASE_ATTRIBUTES, EXCERPT_EVENTS)` keeps two rows, drops every later event, and writes the case id into `org:resource`. Resources from the excerpt are never stored. `pm4py.format_dataframe` is called with `timestamp_format` (the parameter is `timest_format`). `DataFrame.to_event_log`, `discover_variants`, `discover_perf_dfg`, `discover_traces`, `get_event_name`, and `get_siblings` are not the PM4Py API (`convert_to_event_log`, `get_variants`, `discover_performance_dfg`; tree nodes expose `.label` and `.children`). Walking only the root’s children would still miss nested activities. `execute_experiment` never yields until `next_time`, and `next_arrival` is always called at the unmoved `env.now`, so arrivals are independent draws from the start instant while every `env.process` starts at time zero. The Hawkes term uses only the last event, and `hawkes_beta` (per day) is multiplied by a difference in seconds, so clustering dies within a second. There is no Ogata thinning. `Policy` treats `D10` as an atomic flag, so the combined policy does not turn on D2. `CalendarResource` always queues by `case.submitted`, which puts aging priority into the baseline and blurs D6. `run_case` raises before it can return a generator, so the experiment entry point cannot start. Closures are ignored inside `next_boundary`.

Sections 1–7 still cover scope, estimation, assumptions, the D1–D10 matrix, stresses, CRN, censoring, and decision metrics at a level that is usable as a design memo. The deduction is for incorrect tree semantics, contradictory arrival assumptions, and a required code section that is not a coherent PM4Py-plus-simulation skeleton.
