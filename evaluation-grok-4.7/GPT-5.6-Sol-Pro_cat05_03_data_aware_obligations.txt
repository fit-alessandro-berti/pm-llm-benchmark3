8.4

The case conclusions are substantively right, but the answer is not nearly flawless: the checker diverges from the stated semantics, a few formal predicates are broader than the rules, and several diagnostics are coarser or more hand-labeled than the prose admits.

Temporal reconstruction is strong. Blanks are non-updates, state is forward-only, equal timestamps are ordered by `event_index`, and event, effective, record, and evidence time are kept apart. TX-305’s 7 April settlement amount is correctly kept out of the release snapshot. TX-311 is correctly read as US at the review and DE at release. Release states, thresholds, and the stated percentages (56.25%, about 4.55%, about 22.88%) check out. R1 includes EUR 100,000. Decreases and sub-20% increases do not invalidate a review. EX-77 is timely on the qualified signature despite the 11:00 insert; EX-88 is not established by a displayed 08:50 field, a post-release certificate, and the requester’s oral claim. COMPLIANT versus JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION versus INDETERMINATE is defined and applied consistently, including the right refusal to let a standard-packet approval satisfy R2.

The rule table and pseudo-logic cover R1–R7, Kleene behavior for unknown triggers, and vacuous R3 success. Sections 4, 6, and 7 hit the required cases and failure modes. All eleven coarse outcomes line up with the narrative labels, and the code really does reconstruct the log, call `pm4py.format_dataframe`, forward-fill inside the case, and avoid a case-id lookup.

The deductions are concrete. The prose defines R4 materiality against the amount at the latest review, including cumulative growth, but `_is_material_change` uses a consecutive-step ratio and treats any risk, jurisdiction, or customer-type edit as material. R1 validity happens to be rescued by `amount_invalidation_after`, so these rows survive; the latest-material-change diagnostic would not. R2’s “latest relevant change before the enhanced review” is stricter than R2’s text and would stale a still-valid PEP review after an unrelated risk edit. The sanctions check compares the forward-filled case jurisdiction with the release jurisdiction, so a list-update payload in that shared column would overwrite customer state. `enhanced_scope_unknown` is false when no enhanced review exists. R2’s packet test is a negative search for the phrase “standard review packet,” and it is not even what fails TX-307. `evidence_status` is a manual PROVEN/DISPUTED label; stored evidence time is not tested against release, despite the surrounding discussion of evidence time. Severity is an undefined scale, and indeterminate “confidence” switches meaning from confidence in the label to confidence that the file is insufficient. The optional trace check is real, but it is not DECLARE, and the PM4Py citation carries a ChatGPT campaign parameter.

So the legal reading of this log is expert; the implementation is a faithful sketch with undisclosed semantic drift, not a fully reliable encoding of the formalism.
