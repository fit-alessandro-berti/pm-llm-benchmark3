The brief is dialectically strong, case-complete, and usually careful about authority, scope, and what M0 actually accepts. It is not nearly flawless: the revised model mis-states a permissive rule, the SUP-705 mitigation is ahead of the record, and a second control breach is dropped.

The best work is the separation of stipulated facts from D1–D7, the three-part burdens, and classifications that name a dominant reason instead of hiding in MIXED. M0 is read correctly: an unordered `checks` partial order still requires both activities before the XOR, including before `Reject_Supplier`, while a sequential sanctions-then-quality trace such as SUP-701 remains allowed. Prosecution and defense each concede the other side’s real points. SUP-703, SUP-708, SUP-712, SUP-714, and in-window SUP-702/SUP-704 are handled with the right kind of distinction between control-flow fit, CS-9, overlays, and logging. The matrix, rejection of specific arguments, and the split across POWL, data checks, and overlay governance match the task.

The defects are concrete. PD-3 says quality review may follow provisional activation by up to two business days. M1’s auto route orders `Quality_Review` strictly after `Activate_Supplier`, so a stricter pilot trace (quality before auto-activation) fits neither the auto route nor the manual route. `Master_Data_Validation` is a mandatory visible transition that appears in no trace and is absent from the collection schema and the limitations; SUP-702, called a justified exception, does not align to the proposed auto route. `Record_Emergency_Authority` and `Retrospective_Manual_Approval` likewise make SUP-704 unalignable, and that label break is not disclosed. The code comment assigns the two-business-day deadline to retrospective approval; N3 attaches that deadline to quality review and daily monitoring. Section 6’s R-EMG-2 never appears in the overlay table.

SUP-705’s record gives the same activity sequence, the expiry, the operations claim, and EUR 12,000. It does not restate daily monitoring or next-business-day quality and approval. The defense and the judgment treat “every substantive FD-7 control” as established mitigation. That overruns the evidence standard set in section 1. SUP-708 is also incompletely specified: activation precedes `Manual_Procurement_Approval`, a separate CS-9 breach, not only the sanctions inversion. “Verified executable against PM4Py 2.7.x” is an unsupported claim, and JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION for SUP-702 still relaxes the answer’s own requirement that derogating authority be shown to be competent.
