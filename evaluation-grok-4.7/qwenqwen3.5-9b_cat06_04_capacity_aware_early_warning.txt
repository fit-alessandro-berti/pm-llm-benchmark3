4.5

The six-section structure is present, I-03 is correctly rejected as past the effective stage, I-05 is suppressed for an unchanged repeat, spillover is included, and the portfolio is not ranked by `p_now` alone. Those points do not rescue the decision math.

The impact table treats “breaches plausibly avoided” as 3, then 2, then 1 cases. Absolute risk reductions on the chosen cases sum to roughly 0.6 expected breaches in the base case and well under 1 even in the optimistic case. Three avoided breaches would be worth about €94k, not the stated €22–25k, so the breaches column contradicts the benefit column.

Costs are not auditable. The table charges €1,350 in every scenario, while the notes charge €4,600 by adding false-alarm burden. The implied average burden of €1,083 is wrong: I-04, I-02, and I-01 average €950, so labor plus burden is €4,200. The base net of €7,580 mixes those incompatible cost definitions. Spillover of €5,400 also depends on an unexplained €15k average value for six diagnosis-queue cases that are not identified.

Calibration is misstated and then not applied. Public Sector calibration is described as acceptable, but I-01 is repeatedly treated as underpredicted and, in the fairness section, as an SME case. SME underprediction belongs to I-05, I-07, and I-10. Section 1 says Strategic effects should be cut by 0.05–0.10 and that Non-Native cases need a lower bound above 0.15; the allocation uses unadjusted midpoints and still excludes I-09, whose lower bound is 0.18 and whose base direct value is higher than I-01 and I-02. I-08 is rejected because the €5k burden “outweighs” benefit at an ARR of 0.10, but \(0.10 \times €100{,}000 - €5{,}450\) is still about €4,550 before spillover.

The stated rules conflict with the actions. The 40% Strategic cap is violated by giving two of three slots to I-04 and I-02. The probability-delta screen is skipped for I-02. I-06 is both “eligible” and “suspended.” I-07 is called escalated because of the new log, then left without a slot, and the reserve sentence has the logic backwards: if the regulatory case uses the reserve, I-07 does not become eligible for a fourth slot. Propensity-score matching cannot identify effects if this same observable rule assigns treatment, and the recalibration trigger treats the planning “base scenario” as if it were an observed monthly result.
