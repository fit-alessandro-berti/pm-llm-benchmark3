7.5

The answer is strong on the central pattern and on causal caution, but it is not nearly flawless. The crude-versus-stratified reversal is correctly detected, the arithmetic that was checked holds, stratification is not treated as identification, queue time is separated from review service, and the 50% reassignment is refused in favor of a staged pilot plus a capacity intervention.

The main defects are causal-graph consistency and one invalid contrast. `unobserved_case_risk` is called a confounder of cycle time, but the DAG never sends it to `cycle_time` or `quality_outcome`. `shift` is likewise treated as a confounder of cycle time, yet it only points at routing and group, so it is not a backdoor factor. Section 5 then uses “night is not higher within complexity” as evidence against a shift effect, even though those night cells are Orion-heavy, so the contrast is still group-confounded. Section 1 frames evidence for and against a harmful effect rather than for and against the stated verdict, and it never says when the reassignment could succeed. The unsigned “2.51 h” contrast, the “OrionNova” wording, a vague rework-as-collider claim, underspecified front-door assumptions, a non-identified capacity phase, and residual cycle time outside wait plus service are smaller but real gaps.
