7.5

The causal core is strong: the crude Orion gap is correctly reversed within complexity and under direct standardization, stratification is not treated as identification, queueing is separated from service, and the 50% reassignment is rejected in favor of a bounded pilot plus a capacity test. Arithmetic for the main contrasts checks out, and the required sections, roles, and hypothesis types are present.

It is not nearly flawless. The diagnostic script groups on `resource_group`, which is not a column (`group` is), so overlap counts, warnings, and the PM4Py comparison never run. `quality_outcome → case_completed` reverses time: the 30-day outcome is defined only after completion. The graph prose concatenates arrow endpoints, and the standardization cell drops the equals sign (`31.5717.14`, `35.0019.65`), so those results are not cleanly auditable. Rework is loosely called collider-like. Total, direct, and mediated effects are implied across the table rather than specified as estimands. The routing pilot is confined to historical common support, which is almost empty and is not the medium/high night workload the proposal would move.
