6.4

The write-up is complete, process-aware, and usually careful about identification, but it is not reliable as a committee analysis because its headline adjusted estimate is arithmetically impossible and its causal graph assigns the wrong role to key operating variables.

The complexity-standardized contrast is wrong in sign and magnitude. With the stated pooled weights (12/30, 10/30, 8/30) and the stratum means given in the same table, Orion standardizes to about 17.14h and Nova to about 19.65h, a contrast of about −2.51h. The reported 18.67h versus 17.09h (+1.58h) cannot be produced by any positive weighting of stratum gaps that are all negative (about −2.14h, −2.21h, and −3.43h). That cell contradicts the within-stratum rows, the claim that mix explains the crude +6.72h gap, and the Python block, which implements the correct pooled standardization. A reader who trusts the adjusted number would think Orion remains slower after case-mix adjustment.

Two other figures do not match the cases they cite. Orion compliance-required cycle time is 240.2/9 = 26.69h, not “about 25.8h”; 25.84h is the mean of the eight non-appeal required cases, so the stated count and the mean disagree. Overall Orion review service is 98.5/14 = 7.04h, not about 6.9h. Nova’s non-required mean is about 11.33h, not 11.2h.

The graph conflicts with the variable-role table. `routing_rule -> shift` and `routing_rule -> arrival_load` make shift and load descendants of the assignment policy, but the table treats both as confounders of the group effect. Case arrival time and arrival load precede routing; Orion’s night coverage is a staffing constraint on who can receive night work. `quality_outcome -> case_completed` reverses time: the 30-day appeal is defined after closure and cannot cause completion. `unobserved_reviewer_skill -> resource_group` only encodes skill as a confounder, while the intervention discussion treats certification as part of the manipulable Orion bundle. The later prose does identify queue feedback that a static DAG drops, and it correctly labels group-to-wait/service arrows as hypotheses.

Elsewhere the causal reasoning holds. The crude-versus-stratum reversal is real and is not treated as identification. Queue wait is separated from review service, completed-case selection is explicit, and resource group is handled as a policy bundle rather than a clean treatment. The seven hypotheses have distinct mechanisms and predicted results. The phased plan correctly refuses a 50% transfer, includes a small stratified routing pilot, and includes a capacity change that does not relabel the group. Section word counts and the required columns are met.

The code would reproduce the crude and stratified comparisons, warn on cells under three cases, and fall back to a `>` split if DFG unpacking fails. It never passes the trace separator into `parse_event_log_string`, and it only prints group DFGs rather than contrasting edge frequencies. Those are secondary to the false +1.58h result.
