5.5

The interval analysis in §§1–4 is mostly careful and internally consistent, but the answer is not close to complete or exact.

The largest failure is structural: the required output stops during §4. Sections 5–7 are absent, so there is no executable interval/PM4Py program, no evidential-sufficiency mapping, and no governance recommendation. The sensitivity write-up also does not discuss every classification change; T-408 containment→close (CV/CV/CP) and T-409 investigation→containment (PV/CP/CP) are in the matrix and then omitted.

There is a concrete interval error. Under the answer’s own floor-plus-offset rule, LEGACY-BER 02:42 with offset ±60 s is true-local [02:41:00, 02:44:00], hence second-fold UTC [01:41:00Z, 01:44:00Z]. The table gives [01:41:00Z, 01:43:00Z], one minute short, unlike the correctly expanded 02:50 and 02:05 intervals. That makes T-405 S2 [2040, 2340] and alert→containment [2940, 3240] wrong; they should be [2040, 2400] and [2940, 3300]. Classifications happen not to flip.

S3 tolerance thresholds are also wrong: +2 and +5 minutes are 129720 s and 129900 s, not 130800 s and 131400 s. S1/S2 thresholds are right, and these bad S3 numbers do not change the reported labels.

What remains is strong: HUB ±1.5 s, OPS/vendor expansions, DST-fold elimination to one ordered triple, T-404’s retained duration band, the 3-second SLA/profile edge cases, and the zeta 1/2/3 matrices I checked are otherwise correct, with assumptions stated rather than collapsed to midpoints. That quality cannot offset a missing code deliverable, two missing analytical sections, a wrong published interval, and incorrect S3 thresholds.
