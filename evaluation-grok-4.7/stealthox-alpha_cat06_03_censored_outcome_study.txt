6.2

The design correctly refuses to code rejects, withdrawals, or open cases as non-defaults, separates structural non-activation from censoring and missing feeds, and the leakage table is mostly the right keep/repair/drop policy. The study as specified is still not internally identifiable or implementable.

The primary estimand is not one estimand. Section 1 defines an as-run absolute risk with transfer as an absorbing competing state, and also a fixed-horizon cumulative incidence \(F_D(t,\tau)\) after the \(k\)-th event. Default is only defined inside 90 days after activation, so those clocks are not the same, and \(\tau\) is never fixed. Section 3 then censors unlinked transfers in the primary analysis. Censoring transfer estimates a hazard under an independent-censoring assumption the process description makes implausible; it is not the as-run competing-risk risk from Section 1. Inverse-probability weighting of missing feeds is also installed as primary while the same section says partner feeds are plausibly MNAR and untestable. That weighting identifies the target only under MAR, which is not the stated belief.

Cohort accounting does sum to 48,000, and the maturity bound activation on or before 13 October 2025 for a 90+35 day lag before 15 February 2026 is arithmetically right. The validation design then ignores it. The prose test window is starts from 15 October through 31 December 2025. Activation cannot precede start, so none of those cases can be fully mature on the paper’s own rule; “reduced power” is the wrong conclusion. Any fast-feed slice that remains is selected on short time-to-activation. April–June 2025 starts are also absent from the prose split. The skeleton uses different cutpoints (validation from 1 April 2025, test from 1 August 2025) and a 210-day embargo that drops training back to about September 2024. Random-split criticism and the metric list are directionally fine, but Uno-style AUC and a single IPCW Brier score are not, without adaptation, competing-risk scoring rules.

Prefixing conditions on reaching \(k=3\) events while the declared population remains all 48,000 applications. Event 3 is entry into Credit Review on the stated process, not “shortly after” it. Short cases are deferred to a 48-hour prefix that the code never builds.

The skeleton does not run. `status` reads Rejected, Withdrawn, and Transferred from `cases` after those columns were left on `first`. `risk_score_t0` is indexed by case and assigned into an event-level frame, so it does not align. `status`, which contains `labeled:default`, is joined into `feat_tbl`, and the audit only looks for a column named `default_90d` on an already filtered frame. `followup_end` is the last observed event, not the 15 February 2026 administrative censor time, yet the survival hook says to censor there. Dedup runs before renaming, so migration duplicates that differ only by the new activity name survive. `discover_dfg`, variants, temporal features, and waiting times are fit on full training traces, including post-prefix and post-outcome activities, and are never restricted to the prediction-time prefix. `get_prefixes_from_log` is computed and then unused.
