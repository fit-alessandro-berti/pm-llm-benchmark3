3.5

The submission understands the narrative and correctly prefers a partial order for the cross-branch join, but both models are behaviorally wrong on the requirements that matter, and the write-up asserts that several of those errors are exact.

The trial cycle is inverted in both models. PM4Py `LOOP(do, redo)` means `do (redo do)*`, with exit as the choice not to take `redo`. The code puts `Adjust Parameters` inside `do` and a silent transition in `redo`, so the visible language is `(Execute Trial, Inspect Trial, Adjust Parameters)+`. Every path, including a first-pass inspection, ends the trial with `Adjust Parameters`. That is exactly the behavior requirement 8 forbids, and it excludes the intended happy path and the two-iteration witness `Execute, Inspect, Adjust, Execute, Inspect`. Section 6 still marks repeat-until as exact for both models. The comment above the tree describes a different control flow from the constructors.

The process tree also misses the split dependencies it claims to encode. `Plan Verification` is after the join of `Contain Material` and `Analyze Root Cause`, so planning waits for root-cause analysis. `Execute Trial` is parallel with `Design Corrective Action`, so the trial can start before design finishes. Approvals are sequenced only after that whole block, so they cannot overlap the trial. `Consult Supplier` cannot start until design finishes and cannot overlap approvals. Section 3 states the opposite for the trial join and for approval overlap. The approximation policy is therefore mislabeled: the tree both drops required interleavings and admits unsafe ones. Leaf parents for `Execute Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, and `Adjust Parameters` are unset in the Section 3 constructors. The compact expression has empty operator symbols and is not a PM4Py tree string.

The POWL model does not construct. `OperatorPOWL` accepts only `XOR` and `LOOP`; `Operator.SEQUENCE` and `Operator.PARALLEL` are rejected, so the high-risk branch and the trial body fail at initialization. The same transitions are then placed both inside those operators and in the top-level `StrictPartialOrder`. Nodes with no incoming edge, including a second `Execute Trial`, can fire before `Register Deviation`. The partial-order edges on `trial_loop`, `approval_xor`, and `supplier_xor` do not repair that. The unconnected-pair explanation describes the intended concurrency, not the graph that was built. Calling this model about 95% faithful is unsupported.

The witness section does not meet its own table. Process-tree and POWL verdicts are blank, then T8 is revised in place until the intended verdict is `FORBID*` for an interleaving the narrative allows (`Design` before `Plan`). T5 (`Analyze` before `Contain`) is allowed by the tree’s initial parallel and does not expose the over-sequencing. T1–T3 and T5 omit `Adjust Parameters`, so they are outside the constructed loop; T7, marked forbidden, is the loop’s one-iteration word. No forbidden row cleanly names the earliest violated cross-branch condition. T1 is also impossible for the tree for a second reason: `Supervisor Review` sits after the trial block, not between design and execution.

The validation plan names the right checks and then cannot perform them. Imports and call shapes do not match PM4Py (`pm4py.convert`, `pm4py.analysis`, `pm4py.simulation`, `Event(key=..., value=...)`, `trace.index(Event(...))`). The duplicate-label assertion is `adjust == execute - 1 or adjust <= execute`, which accepts `adjust == execute`, i.e. the loop bug. The closing “sound / fitness ≥ 0.95 / forbidden fitness < 0.5” report is presented as an outcome without a run. The self-critique discusses silent-loop replay and net size while leaving the inverted loop, the trial-before-design edge, and the illegal POWL operators unmentioned.
