The three candidates are genuinely different hypotheses, and their control-flow claims match the log and the rules. The conservative tree is exactly the seven observed variants. The balanced POWL keeps both check orders, requires Credit Check before Supplier Confirmation, places optional Expedite Review after both checks and only on the supplier branch, and therefore adds exactly `R C E X S A P Z`, `R C S E X A P Z`, and `R E C S X A P Z` (10 traces total). The permissive tree treats either completed check as a supplier gate and makes Supplier Confirmation and Expedite Review independent skips, adding exactly `R E S C A P Z`, `R E S C X A P Z`, `R C E X A P Z`, and `R E C X A P Z` (14 traces). The ledger covers V1–V7, D1–D7, and S1–S7, and the DFG paragraph correctly treats reciprocal arcs as compatible with separate sequential routes, finish-order variation under overlap, or ingestion tie-breaks. Exclusion points, the matrix, the monitoring-versus-sensitivity recommendation, and the confidence table line up with those languages.

The required validation program does not establish any of that. `pm4py.check_soundness` returns one boolean, so `sound, diagnostics = pm4py.check_soundness(...)` raises before metrics or playout. `bounded_playout` also puts `(Marking, trace)` into a set, while `Marking` subclasses `collections.Counter` and is unhashable, so execution raises `TypeError` at `visited = {start}`. The statement that a local run passed soundness, accepted all 30 cases, passed 17 suite checks, and measured languages of 7, 10, and 14 is not a result of this script. Those sizes are the right reading of the models, but the script never performs the required soundness, metric, playout, or suite checks, and a bounded search is not shown by execution to be exhaustive.

Smaller faults sit in the write-up of otherwise careful models. The conservative “underfitting” list mixes duplicate-label replay ambiguity and variant-count scalability into underfitting. “Sequence optional X” describes a partial order in which Expedite Review stays unordered relative to Supplier Confirmation. The balanced net also duplicates Credit Check and Engineering Review across the XOR branches, and that cost is left unstated where duplicate labels are discussed for the trees.
