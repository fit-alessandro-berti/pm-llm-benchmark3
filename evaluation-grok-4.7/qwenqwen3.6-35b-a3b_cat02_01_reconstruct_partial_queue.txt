3.5

The write-up matches the required seven headings and recognizes rounding, censoring, the C2 outage, and missing starts as distinct issues. Those merits do not survive the calculations.

P05 is parsed backwards: S1 is fully observed (09:00–09:25 on I1) and the missing timestamp is the S2 start, ending at 10:15 on C1. The answer treats S1 as missing, invents a zero-length S2 at 10:15, and then builds I1/C1 bounds and the sensitivity list on that error. P21’s S1 start is observed at 13:30, not missing; P15’s S1 is finished at 12:15, yet its service is reported as about 105–120 minutes still running at 14:00.

S1 waits are systematically wrong from P06 onward. Reported waits stay near 20–30 minutes while arrival-to-start is about 40 minutes for P06, about 75 for P11, and about 95–110 for P16–P21. P16’s censored S2 wait is given as 120–130 minutes; 14:00 minus 12:35 is 85. P21’s stated start window does not produce the wait range in the same row. Several later S2 services are also shifted (P10 is 25 minutes, not about 40; P13 is 50, not about 40).

Station counts are not a defensible binning. External arrivals from 08:00 through 10:00 are P01–P14 (14), not 7; the four “10:00–12:00 arrivals” are P08–P11, all of whom arrived before 10:00. The claim that 11 arrivals versus 9 S1 completions in the last two hours makes S1 the bottleneck uses the wrong population. Utilization drops real intervals (I2’s P06 and P08 in the morning; C2’s P08 at midday) and places P13’s afternoon work in the 10:00–12:00 window, so the percentages are not reproducible from the log.

Definitions and checks are incomplete. “S1 sojourn” is defined as S1 service plus S2 wait plus S2 service, which is not station sojourn. Queue length is not separated from WIP, completed-case flow times are not reported, and Little’s Law is named but never applied. Global FIFO is assumed and is contradicted by P09 starting S2 before P08. The three scenarios are described, not computed, and the claim that rounding moves means by less than 3 percent is unsupported: a difference of two rounded timestamps is about ±5 minutes.

The Python block encodes the same P05 swap, gives `s2_start` 23 values for 22 cases, and will not build the frame. Missing/OPEN strings are then subtracted as if they were times. The PM4Py service/waiting and arrival/finish calls are applied to one collapsed row per case, which does not preserve case-level activity semantics, and the printed station summary references columns the frame does not have.
