5.5

The primary labels for G1, G2, G3, G5, G6, G7, and G8 are mostly defensible, and the required seven-part structure is present. The answer is not reliable enough to score higher: several rankings contradict the timestamps, the performance section misstates bias, and the required PM4Py comparison is not actually carried out.

The diagnosis table overstates certainty and picks impossible runners-up. G3’s second explanation, genuine simultaneity, is incompatible with starts at 11:02, 11:10, 11:17, and 11:24. G4’s second explanation, concurrency, is incompatible with exact 1-second adjacency and no overlap. G1’s runner-up, coarsening, fights a 1-second PLC source. G6’s two explanations are the same logging event. “None” as counterevidence for G4, G6, and G7 ignores live alternatives. Two numeric contradictions are missed: G5 has four-way overlap while `analyst_pool` has at most three analysts, and G7 yields negative waits because M02–M05 arrive after the rounded 14:00 start (about −1, −4, −7, and −11 minutes).

Section 3 does not cover waiting, service, utilization, and bottleneck detection for every cluster. “No bias” for G1 is wrong: four shared intervals equal 12 item-minutes of service in 3 fixture-minutes, so capacity-1 utilization is inflated. G4 does not have zero waiting; arrival-to-start waits are about 7, 10, 13, 16, and 19 minutes. Inter-item idle time was substituted for queue time. G7 is described only as quantization noise, omitting negative waits and the utilization distortion from five stacked 15-minute intervals.

The signature matrix is too vague, and one mechanism is reversed. Identical coarsened timestamps stay equal under a smaller tolerance; a smaller tolerance cannot reveal the pre-rounded sequence. Interval bounds do that. Nested overlap is also mislabeled as partial for start and end batches.

The code transcribes the rows and attempts both pandas diagnostics and `discover_batches`, but it does not meet the experiment requirement. `min_batch_size` is never varied. Batch categories are not printed or joined back to clusters; a comment guesses them. `merge_distance` is passed as a bare keyword despite PM4Py taking a parameters dictionary, so the calls are likely to fail. Equality of all starts is not an equality-group diagnostic. Seconds equal to zero marks G1 as coarse. Pairwise `complete > start` gives G6 overlap 0 even though all seven instants coincide. Mean start-minus-arrival is not queue build-up.

Section 5 only really stress-tests G5 and G7, then overclaims that censoring shows G7 was sequential. Section 6 omits workstation capacity. Section 7 clears G1 and G4 for direct performance use, leaves the strongest logging call (G6) out of the high-confidence list, and treats clock-skewed G8 as needing no correction. Assumptions are not labeled, and observations are not kept separate from hypotheses.
