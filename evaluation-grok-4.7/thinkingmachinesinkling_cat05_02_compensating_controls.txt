7.5

The final analysis is strong on control objectives, timing, independence, and non-mechanical case judgment, but it is not nearly flawless. Several internal inconsistencies and code defects are material under a strict reading.

The best work is the control decomposition, the equivalence dimensions, and the hybrid reading of a manager action after `Release_Payment` but before settlement: detective as to the authorization split, and only late-preventive as to irrevocable settlement. Case labels are mostly well supported. AP-205 is correctly `LOSS_PREVENTED_BEFORE_COMPLETION`. AP-202 and AP-208 are correctly `OBJECTIVE_FAILED` because overrides, conflicts, outages, and post-settlement review do not become sufficient merely by occurring. AP-203 and AP-207 are reasonably `OBJECTIVE_MET` under ED-1, including AP-207’s pre-settlement ratification. AP-201 and AP-206 are reasonably partial rather than automatic failures. Monitoring separates alerts from proof and covers the requested signals.

The main flaws are in the final conclusions themselves. For AP-204 the label is `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET` while the rationale says the preventive objective “likely failed,” after a fabricated receipt and an audit outside the recall window. That is an unresolved contradiction, not a nuance. Section 2 defines full equivalence as independent authorization before release, then AP-207 is treated as the objective fully met with approval only before settlement, without reconciling the two tests. Recall capacity for AP-201 is also slightly optimistic: the audit starts near the edge of a 24-hour window, and settlement is already treated as normally irrevocable.

The code has the right skeleton, but it is not reliable evidence. `format_dataframe` is called with `resource_key`, which many PM4Py versions reject, and that call is outside the fallback, so the script can die before the four-eyes check. There is no actual join of log results to the case-facts frame. AP-206 is coded as `emergency_token_before_prepare: True` even though the prose correctly says the token is after prepare. AP-205 is flagged as audited inside the recall window although no audit or settlement occurred. Repeated-pair and manager-timing flags are hardcoded rather than derived. Those errors would mislead the judgment the narrative otherwise gets right.
