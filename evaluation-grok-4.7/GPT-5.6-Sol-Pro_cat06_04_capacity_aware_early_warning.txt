The answer is decision-theoretically serious and arithmetically careful, but it is not nearly flawless. Several of its own rules are applied loosely, and a few operational comparisons are left unresolved.

What holds up: I-03 is correctly barred for stage, not rank. I-05 is suppressed as one unchanged episode, while I-07 is reopened because of the new log despite a lower score. Calibration is handled as segment bands rather than as a fake causal probability. Lower-bound nets check out (I-01 €920, I-02 €950, I-04 €3,550, I-05 €1,200, I-06 €1,500, I-07 €500, I-08 −€450, I-09 €2,350, I-10 €720), as do midpoint effects, the three-case optimistic sum 0.65–0.84, base €8,400, cost €1,350, false-alarm total €1,450, and the break-evens near €13k–€39k and €26k–€57k. Unknown untreated value is left as a break-even instead of invented. Fairness and monitoring are concrete: lanes, value cap, propensities, interference clusters, and a stop rule when the adverse case binds.

The main logical break is the robust gate. Money must clear the lower effect, but breach-count margin uses the midpoint. I-02’s lower effect is 0.08, against about 0.12 expected induced breaches per slot at the midpoint spillover and 0.18 at the high end. It fails a lower-bound breach test and still sits in the selected portfolio. “Subject to a queue gate” hedges a selection the stated rule would not routinely allow. I-09 is the same problem in milder form: its published floor of 0.18 merely ties high-end per-slot spillover, and that floor is not a real floor in a sparse Non-Native-Language segment. Calling it A1, tied with the best-evidenced urgent case, treats an unreliable interval as identified.

Spillover is partly double-counted. Delay of 8–20 minutes (15–30 in the adverse case) is the congestion mechanism behind the 0.01–0.03 probability shift, but the harm cells add expected induced breaches plus delay minutes. Linearity of expectation across the six cases is fine; adding both descriptions of one externality is not.

Operational gaps remain. The last-responsible-moment rule subtracts the 45-minute swarm and downstream resolution plus validation, yet I-07, with only 1.5 hours left and still in triage, is dispatched first without asking whether that window is already inside the post-cutoff region. Two specialists are treated as a delay factor, not as a serial constraint: three swarms cannot overlap if both people are required, and I-09 and I-02’s clocks shrink while I-07 runs. The 0.03 calibration trigger is already breached by the stated SME and Strategic Enterprise biases, but the monitoring plan does not say that today’s band rule is the interim control. Public-sector duty is only a generic override, so I-01 is ranked as a €9,000 case. Tiers such as B1-static are not defined, and the impact table evaluates three swarms as a bundle after the text has already said the third should often not be used.

Section 4’s I-05 versus I-07 contrast is the right policy. The portfolio that includes I-02 and co-ranks I-09 is less robust than the rule claims.
