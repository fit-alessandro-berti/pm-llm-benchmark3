7.5

The final assessment is strong and usable, but it is not nearly flawless. The Simpson reversal is correctly identified and is not treated as causal identification. The verdict is scoped to the crude association, case mix, queueing versus service, selection, and the unsafe 50% reassignment are all handled, and the required sections, hypothesis types, pilot, and capacity phase are present. Most arithmetic checks out.

These defects keep it well below a top score:

- Evidence for and against the stated verdict is misaligned. Lack of overlap is positivity failure, and completed-case selection is selection bias; neither is “residual confounding.” Within-stratum faster Orion cases are also compatible with dropping Orion’s longest open cases, so they do not contradict selection bias.
- `resource_group -> shift` is labeled as supported. Night coverage only supports association. Arrival time can cause both routing and delay, so shift may confound rather than mediate. `arrival_load -> shift` is an unexplained assumption.
- The pilot is described as estimating a direct effect. Randomizing the group estimates a total effect inside a narrow stratum.
- `cycle_time` is not `wait + review service`. The residual is systematically larger with compliance and rework, and that process gap is missed.
- The Nova load comparison used against the load hypothesis is itself unstratified.
- “Front-door” is named without the assumptions that would make it valid.
- The PM4Py path is unlikely to work: trace identity is not standard XES `concept:name`, and `discover_dfg` commonly returns a three-tuple that this code does not unpack, so it falls through to a manual DFG.
