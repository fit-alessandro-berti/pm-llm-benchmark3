7.2

The case rulings and the two briefs are strong, traceable, and genuinely dialectical: authority, expiry, scope ambiguity, hidden resource breaches, and data defects are separated rather than averaged, and both sides make the required concessions. Severe cases (703, 708, 705, 714) and flow-conformant rule breaches (712, 715) are handled correctly.

The result is still short of a tight conformance opinion. SUP-702 is called a justified exception at medium-high confidence even though PD-3’s master-data-validation precondition is unevidenced and the defense’s claim that every stated PD-3 condition was met is false. SUP-713 is marked compliant on a mapping approved after the fact, which contradicts the stated rule that retroactive ratification cannot reclassify history. The “2%→29%” fact and many formal operators in the matrix and rule table are corrupted into unreadable gaps, including the segregation rule.

The POWL revision covers the required route types and honestly parks amounts, identity, and windows outside the net, but it is not the most defensible approximation. The information-request loop sits only before routing, so it cannot generate SUP-707. Auto and emergency edges turn “may follow activation” into “must follow activation.” Master-data validation is absent from both the auto path and the rule table. The reactivation route promised in the architecture is not in the model. DECLARE `response` is used for a precedence/data condition it does not mean.
