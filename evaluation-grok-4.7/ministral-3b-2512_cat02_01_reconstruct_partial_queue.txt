2.5

The write-up keeps the required seven headings and a few early, fully observed durations (P01–P03, several 30-minute S1 services). Almost everything that the task asks to be reconstructed is wrong, internally inconsistent, or missing.

Case arithmetic does not match the log. P04’s S1 wait is 20 minutes and its service is 30, not 0 and 35. Later waits are routinely off by 20–40 minutes (P06, P08, P10, P12–P14, P16–P21). P05 and P11 are given an S2 wait of 90 and a service of 60 even though those starts would fall after the known completion times. P07’s stated wait of 60 and service of 30 cannot both fit an arrival at 08:45 and a completion at 10:00. P15 and P21 borrow another case’s timestamp (13:05, 12:55). Right-censored waits for P16 and P18–P20, and P22’s S1 wait, are reported as 0; at 14:00 those elapsed waits are about 85, 50, 35, 15, and 110 minutes. Open service is inverted: final service is at least the elapsed time, not strictly less than it, and P17’s elapsed S2 service is 5 minutes, not 0.

Missing starts are replaced by single values. The notes’ bounds are not resource-feasible: P05/P11 “upper” starts sit after completion, and P07’s lower and upper bounds are the same instant. Predecessor and successor occupancy on I1, C1, and C2, and the 10:55–11:10 C2 gap, are never used.

Station figures contradict both the source log and the case table: 22 S1 arrivals and 20 S1 completions become 19 and 13; S2 completions are 14, not 10; an average S1 wait of 30 does not follow from the case column (that column itself averages near 50). WIP is labeled in minutes, utilization is “100% (2h)” inside a 6-hour window, and coverage is called 100% despite missing starts and censoring. Subperiod arrival and departure rates, a queue-length path with a tie convention, completed-case sojourn, and any Little’s Law check are absent. Section 5’s 1.5/h versus 1/h comparison is invented. Section 6’s three scenarios move “queue length” by 5–15 minutes, swap early and late starts, and do not recompute a single metric.

The Python block cannot run: `s2_start` and `s2_resource` have 23 entries against 22 cases, and the embedded S1 times are shifted relative to the prompt (P05’s known 09:00 start is stored as MISSING; P14’s end becomes 12:35; P17’s S2 start is moved onto P18). Blank, MISSING, and OPEN all collapse to `None`. The event builder reads nonexistent `*_resource` columns, sets `concept:name` to S1–S4 from column index, and never builds intervals. `format_dataframe` is called with an invalid argument and without `case:concept:name`. `insert_case_service_waiting_time`, `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`, and `convert_log_to_time_intervals` are never used, and no comment justifies the omission. Assumptions A3 and A4 contradict themselves (unlimited capacity and single-task resources; open service both “< elapsed” and “≥ elapsed”).
