6.5

The final write-up is a real decision-set analysis with the required structure, and the broad stories for QA (FIFO, with one appointment override) and QD (skill filters, then FIFO) are in the right neighborhood. It is not close to exact: several comparison tables are built on the wrong eligible set or the wrong prediction, and the closing synthesis states a false cross-queue fact.

The QA 08:58 row says A06 had not yet arrived. A06 arrived at 08:29, and A07 and A08 were also waiting; the 09:05 row in the same table lists A06’s 08:29 arrival. Priority and SPT still pick A04, so the call is unchanged, but the eligible set is wrong. The QA “contradicting” cell is also misused: A02 over A04, and A08 last, support FIFO and refute priority. The actual pressure on pure FIFO is A03 at 08:20, which is filed as support for the hybrid.

QB is the weakest diagnosis. At 09:48, B05 (priority 1, scanned at 09:46) is waiting, so priority selects B05, not B03. The table only gets B03 by assuming B05 was not registered, then treats B03 as support for preemptive priority. The recorded reason is “dispatcher reassignment.” At 10:14, B07 (arrived 10:08, est 6) is omitted, and SPT is given as B04; the next row includes B07 and correctly gives SPT as B07. After the first urgent preemption, 09:31, 09:48, 10:14, and 10:28 all violate priority. “Preemptive priority” as the primary rule, at confidence 65, is too strong.

QC correctly flags C01 over C07 at 11:40 as a joint failure of SPT, priority, and earliest deadline. The EDF column is still wrong: at 10:18 and 10:32 the earliest deadline is C05 (10:50), not C03 or C01. C05 was already impossible at 10:18 and was not chosen, so “promote once the deadline is missed” does not explain the timing. Section 5 then cites C07 at 12:16 as deadline promotion, while Section 2 correctly calls that start trivial. Those two claims conflict.

QD’s 11:25 (D04 over D08) and 11:48 (D07 over D10) comparisons are the right anti-priority, anti-SPT evidence. The D-F/D10 argument is not. D-F is free from 11:36 to 12:00, and D10 needs 12 minutes; “D05 needs 12:20” does not make that window unusable, nor does it reconcile the job note with the meeting interval. The 11:20 moment, when D-X is free and idles until D06’s appointment while other qualified jobs wait, is missing. D05 could also have started at 11:21 and finished before the meeting, so “correctly delayed” overstates a constraint that the earlier choice created. Confidence 85 is high for that residue.

Section 7 says FIFO is contradicted only in QB. QC’s early order is a direct FIFO failure (C02, C03, and C04 before C01). That sentence is false, and it is load-bearing for the claim that priority is the most dangerous inference. Priority is still a reasonable danger, but not for the reason given. Test A’s delayed-scan alternative for A04 is also impossible: A04 was scanned at 08:13, seven minutes before 08:20, against a two-minute scan lag.
