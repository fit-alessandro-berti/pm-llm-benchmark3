The review is a real governance argument: use cases are separated, every reported number is disaggregated, S and C are judged by language membership, and the third model is an actual control-flow design rather than a compromise slogan. The probe calls for S and C match the expressions. That is not enough for a near-flawless mark. Several claims that the board would rely on are imprecise or internally inconsistent.

The behavior matrix is the strongest part. Under ordinary PM4Py tree semantics (`->` sequence, `+` full interleaving, `X` exclusive choice, `tau` a free silent step), S really does admit every subset and every order of the four reviews, so B3 and B4 are perfect members of the language, not near-misses. C really is exactly those eight sequences: B2, B5, and B6 are out, B1 and B7 are in, and B8 is out only because that movie was never stored. The split between intentional noise tolerance (optional Supplier Review, optional Expedite, exactly one approval) and accidental policy deletion (optional Credit Check and Technical Review, Expedite as their peer, Correct Dossier missing) is the right split. P1–P7 are covered, and A1–A3 actually separate the third language from both submissions.

The third backbone matches the regex it claims. Initial `StrictPartialOrder` of the two checks makes both mandatory and unordered. `LOOP(tau, SEQUENCE(Correct Dossier, XOR(CC, TR, CC∥TR)))` is zero or more repairs under PM4Py’s do-then-(redo-do)* reading. Expedite is no longer a peer of the mandatory checks. Exactly one approval is a real XOR. Optional Supplier Review is confined to one stage and is labeled a data gap. That is safer than S and strictly more general than C, and it does not optionalize the mandatory checks.

The defects are local and concrete.

The P6 checker does not implement the rule it states. `declare_hooks` and §7.1 say that once `affected_checks` is present, the next recheck set must equal it and a mismatch is a deviation. `posthoc_policy_flags` reads that attribute and then flags only the missing-attribute case. A present but wrong arm produces no flag. There is also no window definition for “next recheck set” across repeated corrections, and a single case-level attribute cannot describe per-event repairs. `last_index` is unused. Shipping this function after the attributes arrive would silently pass the false negative the dissent correctly worries about.

Experiment G tests the wrong cost. Skipping Credit Check on S is a cost-0 tau model move; the log contains no Credit Check event, so raising the log-move cost of Credit Check does not touch B3-like traces and cannot make S’s fitness advantage disappear. §6 already distinguishes model moves from log moves, so §9G contradicts the review’s own mechanics. The skeleton accepts `costs` and never passes them. The negative-suite language check is the test that would actually catch pathology 2; the cost experiment as written would not.

A few interpretations are softer than the prose admits. A trace in L(S), including B3, aligns at cost 0. “Near-perfect” understates that. The training gap of 0.008 is an average alignment-fitness residual, not a shown 0.8% of cases or events, and it is not specifically identified as Correct Dossier. Token-based generalization is described with “unused outgoing arcs,” which is escaping-edge precision, not the TBR rarity penalty. Who produced the 1–5 comprehension scores is not in the evidence.

A3 is a sequencing choice, then promoted to a compliance reject in the minimum negative suite. P3 does not place Supplier Review after both checks. P4 forces that order on Expedite only, and explicitly allows Expedite before Supplier Review, which is compatible with Supplier Review overlapping the checks. The interpretation cell admits this is revisable; §9C still treats A3 as a ship blocker. Correction interleaved between the two initial checks is likewise excluded without being listed as a limitation.

The dissent is long enough and not token, but one of its main charges is false. C does not close the external-without-Supplier-Review hole: branches 1, 2, 5, and 8 reach Release Order with no Supplier Review. Optional Supplier Review on the third model widens the combinations, which is a fair objection; it does not reopen a prohibition C encoded. “Silent loop bodies” is also the wrong child: the silent child is the do/exit anchor, and the redo body is visible.

The recommendation that follows from the probes still stands: S is an exploration map that must not be a control, C is a frequent-variant catalog that must not be the SOP, and hard-blocking on P3/P6 has to wait for attributes. The grade is held at 8.0 because those conclusions were reached by the right method, while the checker, the cost experiment, and the dissent each misstate a mechanism the rest of the document depends on.
