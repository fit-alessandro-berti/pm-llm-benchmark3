6.2

The core UTC conversions, clock-offset direction, and most zeta=1 classifications are numerically right, including the single surviving T-405 fold combination (Alert in CEST, investigation and containment in CET). That is not enough for a high score. Several required judgments are wrong, inconsistent, or not actually carried through.

The SLA acceptance region is defined as `[0, limit]`, but “no later than” is one-sided. That inconsistency shows up in §4: T-409 S1 is called `CERTAIN_PASS` at +5 minutes even though the duration interval still includes negative values, which lie outside `[0, 1200]`. T-409’s ±12 minute bound is explicitly a weak engineering estimate, yet S2 is a high-confidence `CERTAIN_PASS` with only about two minutes of slack. `INDETERMINATE` is never used.

Arithmetic and bookkeeping slip in places that the write-up still treats as exact. T-401 S3/P4 is `[86917, 86943]` rather than `[86918.5, 86941.5]`. The +5 minute S3 limit is written as 130100 s instead of 129900 s. T-409’s upper endpoint is `16:17:00`, while every other floored minute is kept at `.999`. §2 omits `Investigation_Complete` and `Alert_Sent`, and the T-407 note calls the receipt upper bound unconstrained when the table caps it at 14:20:01.500Z.

§4’s matrix is mostly faithful, including the tight T-408 P2 result at zeta=2 (`[3297, 3303]` vs `[900, 3300]`). The discussion is not. It groups T-407 S2, already a pass at +0, and T-406 S2, still a possible violation at +2 minutes, with the minor breaches that clear at +2 minutes. Many classification changes are never discussed. The mobile entry-delay assumption is also material and untested: dropping the extra 300 s moves T-406 S2 and P2 from possible violation to certain pass.

§6 does not cover every possible violation (T-402 S2/P2 are absent) and mislabels T-409 S2 as a possible violation. Several “minimum” evidence claims do not actually force a certain result. Narrowing OPS-BER to ±2 s still leaves T-401 S1 across 900 s, because the minute floor alone straddles the limit. The same is true of T-403 at ±15 s and of T-405 even after the fold is fixed.

§5 does not do what it claims. The script never computes duration intervals or interval classifications. `conformance_temporal_profile` is called without the supplied temporal profile, so the call is not valid. DST folds are hardcoded rather than represented as alternatives. The midpoint comparison is a fixed print that misnames profile P1 as SLA S1.
