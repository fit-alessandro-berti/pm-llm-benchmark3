The case judgments are mostly careful, but the write-up is not internally consistent, and several concrete errors keep it well short of a near-flawless review.

The equivalence test in section 2 says a package is fully equivalent only when timing, independence, coverage, and evidence are strong, and that missing decisive evidence should block a clean pass. Section 3 then labels AP-203 and AP-207 `OBJECTIVE_MET`. Both lack documented L3 status, which MG-4 explicitly requires. AP-207 also depends on an unverified settlement hold, which section 4 itself says must not be assumed, and the label is hedged with “pending verification,” outside the allowed classification set. The code agrees with the stricter reading: both come out `ELIGIBLE_PENDING_EVIDENCE`. AP-206 is scored `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET` even though the emergency-token timestamp is an unresolved precondition and the manager approved before AV-2. Under the stated rule, that case is `INDETERMINATE`.

The AP-208 audit interval is wrong. Release is 2026-05-04 15:04Z and audit is 2026-05-09 10:00Z, about 115 hours, not “about 91 h.” The embedded log and the code both have May 9; the prose used a different day. Severity is also uncalibrated: the unrecovered EUR 480,000 case is High, while the legitimate EUR 900,000 case is Critical, with no scale tying realized loss to exposure.

The corrective column often describes preventive gates. AP-201, AP-203, and AP-206 put pre-settlement or pre-release stops in that column. For AP-202, “no bank-ownership check was made” is inaccurate: AV-2 warned on the bank change and the warning was overridden.

The code is substantive and correctly keeps the pandas oracle ahead of an uncertain PM4Py filter, but `ed1_eval` returns condition 6 as `UNVERIFIED` whenever manager independence is unknown, before it tests approval against settlement. AP-208’s post-settlement ratification is therefore not recorded as a failure. `shared_workstation=False` for AP-201 invents a negative fact. The late-manager boolean also omits after-release, before-settlement approvals that section 6 tells monitors to alert. The version note ends mid-sentence at “and flags.”

The decomposition, the rejection of expected-loss as the only standard, the hybrid reading of pre-settlement ratification, and the hard calls on AP-204, AP-205, AP-208, and AP-209 are sound. Those strengths support a good score. The mismatched pass standard, the bad elapsed time, and the evidence-flag bug do not support a higher one.
