The survival arithmetic is internally consistent and reproduces to the reported rounding. With time origin at `Submit`, right censoring at open age, and first-exit codes R/W/J/X, the product-limit Aalen–Johansen values at 72 hours are F_R = 30.4%, F_W = 14.0%, F_J = 10.1%, F_X = 7.5%, and S(72) = 38.0%, so the internal miss is 69.6%. The restricted mean of that event-free curve is 50.4 hours, and F_R(96) − F_R(72) = 14.3%. Cohort curves (A 70.8% miss and S = 33.9%; B 68.4% and 41.6%), priority-standardized misses (68.8% and 67.5%), P1 stratum reversal (66.7% vs 80.0%), transfer sensitivities (68.7% censored, 62.1% handoff), last-event censoring (65.8%), and the finite-population bounds 18/32–23/32 and 9/32–14/32 all match the case table. The risk set just before 72 is the stated nine cases. Competing exits are kept separate from censoring, already-observed breaches are separated from unresolved cases under 72, escalation is treated as immortal time, and final prefix, rework, and gap are correctly marked as leakage. Assumptions, non-identification of the cohort-policy effect, and the n = 9 horizon are stated explicitly.

The required control-flow block does not reliably compute the feature it prints. `pm4py.get_variants` returns either a comma-separated variant string mapped to traces, in which `sequence[:2]` keeps two characters, or a variant mapped to a count, in which `len(traces)` throws. The directly-follows list in section 5 is consistent with the prefixes, but the code does not produce that summary on either common API.

Censoring transfers and reporting 1 − F_R as any-provider resolution overstates the functional. That quantity extrapolates the retained internal resolution hazard. It is not the external resolution law. The separate 16/32–23/32 bound is the defensible any-provider statement.

The last-event stress test is coherent as a recording assumption, and 65.8% matches its tie convention, but discarding observed open status past 72 also widens the identification bound to 14/32–23/32. Leading with 65.8% leaves that reversal range out. “3 transfers” in the 18-miss decomposition also pools S32 at 96 with the pre-deadline handoffs, which matters only for timing, not for the miss count. Standardized contrasts to 0.1 percentage point on cells of size 3–7 are sharper than the risk set supports, although the overlapping cohort bounds are reported beside them.
