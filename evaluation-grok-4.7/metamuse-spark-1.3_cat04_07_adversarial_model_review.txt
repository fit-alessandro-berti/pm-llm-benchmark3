7.7

The review is strong on skepticism, use-case separation, and concrete S/C behavior, but it is not nearly flawless: the third model’s central correction semantics are internally contradictory, and that error is written into the audit as if it were settled.

Sections 1–6 and the S/C probe matrix are the best part of the document. Use cases correctly invert the fitness/precision trade-off. Every reported measure is interpreted without a fake aggregate. Model S is shown to buy fitness by optionalizing mandatory work (`tau` skips plus parallel interleaving), with explicit forbidden traces for B3, B4, skip-one, and expedite-without-checks, and the noise-tolerance versus semantic-flaw distinction is drawn correctly. Model C is correctly treated as an eight-trace allowlist: B2 and B5 are real false negatives, the correction branch only repeats both checks credit-first, and training precision is mostly memorization on a positive log. Pathologies are ranked by decision impact and cover the required mechanisms.

The third alternative’s backbone is a real improvement: both checks required and unordered, supplier/expedite optional only after them, exactly one approval, a correction loop, no duplicate labels, and missing attributes left as `UNCERTAIN` rather than silent optionality. That design is safer than S and more maintainable than C.

It does not, however, do what sections 7–8 claim for P6/B6/A1. `LOOP(checks, Correct)` with `checks` requiring both credit and technical means every iteration, including every recheck, must contain both. B6 (`… Correct, Technical …`) and A1 (`… Correct, Credit …`) are therefore not in the language and would not have alignment fitness 1. The audit nonetheless says the third model accepts them when `affected` is attested. The post-hoc function does not repair this: the affected-check branch is `pass`. Three incompatible stories sit side by side — procedural “both every time,” declarative “at least one,” and the table’s “accept a single recheck.” That is the requirement the task made hardest, and the board would be misled.

Smaller defects stack. The duplicate-label reconstruction (Credit ×7, Technical ×7, Manager ×6) does not match the eight branches (Credit and Technical occur 9 times each, Manager 7; 48 visible occurrences, not ~33). High precision is primarily a restricted language, not “state fragmentation.” `check_trace` never checks approval position or selective recheck, and the POWL-versus-policy conjunction rule is undefined. The dissenting rebuttal is about 135 words, short of the required 150–250, and it does not attack the recommendation at its strongest point (S’s silent false accepts, and the third model’s own recheck hole).

Validation, governance conditions, and the refusal to invent metrics are solid. The grade stops below 8 because a decision-relevant cell in the behavior audit is false relative to the model the answer itself defines.
