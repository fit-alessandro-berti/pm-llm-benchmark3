6.5

The forecast is structurally serious: parallel completion is treated as a maximum, weekend closure is applied, reruns and investigations are separated from the smooth path, and the temperature-excursion dependence is not ignored. The numerical case is not reliable enough to carry that structure.

Chemistry cannot finish on Friday under the stated bounds. Minimum service is 1.5 h, and the second analyst is free only from about 16:42, leaving at most 1.3 h. The “chemistry finishes Friday, join on Sunday” branch is impossible, yet it is used to support the early tail. The priority path is also overstated as finishing within about 0.5 h of Monday 08:00; with 0.5–1.3 h of Friday progress and modal service of 2.5 h, the usual remainder is closer to 1.5–2 h. “Either way, Monday 08:30–14:00” then erases the FIFO/priority split. FIFO is about 55% and, after roughly 8–9 analyst-hours still ahead of B-417, centers nearer Monday mid-afternoon, not inside that window. Scenario B’s Tuesday rerun spill requires a primary finish after about 15:00, which scenario A’s chemistry window does not allow.

Scenario C is mis-counted. “No weekend authorization and/or contamination” at 10–18% cannot be right: non-authorization alone is about 55–75% and mostly duplicates scenario A. The 5–12% contamination band is the only part that matches the inputs. Scenario E at 5–12% for the joint rerun, contamination, and investigation path is high relative to the base rates unless the excursion coupling is near-comonotonic; that strength is assumed, not shown.

The summary clocks do not match the stated hours. About 90 h is Tuesday morning, not Tuesday evening, so the P80 band of 90–125 h is not “Tuesday evening–Wednesday evening.” About 330 h falls around 2 October, not Tuesday 29 September. Electronic release at 85–92% also contradicts a rejection band of 3–8%. A documentation query of 4–12 QA working hours is stretched into “1–2 business days,” which pulls scenario D late.

Weekend on-call authorization is ranked second and described as tightening the whole distribution, while the same write-up shows that a Sunday microbiology result still waits on Monday chemistry and QA. That authorization matters mainly if contamination starts the extra 20–30 h clock early; it is not a central-path driver once Friday chemistry completion is ruled out. Friday QA capacity (15:30–20:00) is also left out of the backlog arithmetic, so Monday queue wait is only loosely supported.

The earliest time, central band, and severe tail are in the right neighborhood, and the ledger, overlap warning, and max-versus-sum reasoning are the right tools. Too many of the reported ranges contradict each other or the calendar arithmetic that is supposed to justify them.
