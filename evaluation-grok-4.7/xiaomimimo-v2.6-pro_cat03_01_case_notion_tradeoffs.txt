The final write-up is complete, identifier-heavy, and right on most of the hard accounting, but it is not internally consistent and it contains a few clear factual mistakes.

The event arithmetic is largely correct: Order yields 42 instances from 31 events (11 copies); Item 24 from 16 (8 copies); Package 12 from 10; Delivery 6, Invoice 7, and Payment 4 with no copying. O3’s 9 shared events out of 13, e16/e24’s three-order fan-out, the four distinct order activity sequences, and e16’s nine objects are all accurate. The required distortion types are present, Position B is fully argued, and the code names every required discovery routine.

The main failures are consistency and a few false claims. Section 1 defines convergence as distinct threads pulled into one case, then illustrates it with e13 being copied into both O2 and O3, which is the opposite direction. Divergence is defined as one thread split across cases, with Item-flattening of O1 as the example, yet the matrix rates Item and Package divergence as Low and Order divergence as Low. That Order rating also contradicts D9: O1’s two confirmations are exactly the repeated-activity distortion the matrix says is unlikely. D5 overstates the post-e19 overlap; O2 and O3 share e22, e24, and e25, but O3 still has e27 and the closes differ. D7 is simply wrong: V3’s issue-to-last-payment interval (~21.3 h) is longer than V2’s (~20.9 h, not ~20.5), and e21 at 12:30 is earlier than e22 at 12:40, so the parenthetical timestamps refute the sentence. S1’s claim that deduplication must drop Disp→Inv from 4 to 2 is not entailed; O1 still has unique e15, and the “e18/e19/e20 collapses” gloss does not explain the drop. Section 4 says Package and Delivery logs are not worth creating, while Section 5 makes them explicit secondary lifecycles. The flattening print treats `concept:name` nunique as “unique events,” which counts activities, and the later `event_id` filter is not something flattening reliably produces. Payment and Delivery rows also lack the identifiers the matrix was required to use for every rating.
