8.5

The interval arithmetic, time-zone and DST handling, SLA and profile classifications, and tolerance/zeta transitions are largely correct and carefully hedged. HUB ±1.5 s, OPS-BER [T−120 s, T+149.999 s], VENDOR-NY, SENSOR, LEGACY-BER fold pruning to (0,1,1), T-404 inferred starts, and the mobile entry-delay fork all check out, including the knife-edges (T-402 P1 at 958.5 vs 960, T-403 P1 at 1141.5 vs 1140, T-404 S2 at 2703 vs 2700, T-408 P2 at 3300).

Deductions are for precision and code semantics, not for a wrong primary verdict. The sensitivity matrix rounds T-405 to [720, 1080] and the constant-offset case to [840, 960] instead of [720.001, 1079.999] and [840.001, 959.999]. Section 4 never re-runs the HUB shared-offset case for the T-404 S2 and T-408 P2 knife-edges, even though independence is what creates the few-second overrun. The closing PM4Py note labels T-401/T-403/T-404/T-405/T-409 P1 as midpoint passes without a zeta; at ζ=1 those midpoints fall outside the band. The code also leaves pair-selection semantics (directly-follows versus all profile pairs, with T-404 `Investigation_Complete` in the log) unspecified, and it never executes the back-dated mobile model that the text itself says flips T-406 S2 and P2.
