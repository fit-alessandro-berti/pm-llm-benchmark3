7.1

The construction is complete and internally runnable-looking: 17 timezone-aware events, all 12 objects, joined E2O rows, the required O2O links, real integrity asserts, and the requested PM4Py calls are present and consistent with the chosen tables. The Order, Package, Delivery, and Invoice trace shapes, the shared-event set {e06, e07, e09, e11, e13}, and the Payment length-1 explanation match that E2O policy.

The analysis still has factual and conceptual errors. Section 4 calls P2/D2 “O200’s exclusive” path; I2, P2, and D2 belong only to O100, and those events are in the Order flattening, not omitted from it. The interaction summary gives closed partner lists that drop O100–I3, O200–I1, and O100–O200, even though e06 and e07 relate exactly those objects. That is the convergence the summary is supposed to show. P1’s cross-order structure is also not “event-level, not master-data”: there is no direct Package–Order O2O edge, but P1 reaches O100 through I1 and O200 through I3.

OC-DFG and the object-centric Petri net are described as if they contained instances and event ids. An OC-DFG will not show P1 or e07; `discover_oc_petri_net` yields a type-level net whose “Reserve Stock” / “Pack Package” / “Issue Invoice” transitions are not uniquely the bindings I1∧I3 or “O100 needs both confirmations.” The non-uniqueness caveat does not make those instance claims the expected result.

`related_order` is applied both to e07, where the narrative names both orders, and to dispatch/confirm and Pack P2, where the order link is inferred. That erases the source-versus-propagation distinction claimed in section 1. Confirm→Package (`confirmed_package`) is one-hop propagation along `carries`, while Items are deliberately not propagated, and the qualifier is not marked derived. `packs` and `bills` are also reused across E2O and O2O; only `packs` is flagged. M2 as `completes_settlement` asserts a total the story never gives, though the assumption is disclosed. Smaller slips: I2’s Item DFG edge is Add→Pack, not merely a skip; Record Payment→Record Payment is a self-loop, not an alternative to one; the source-minimal alternative omits that Package then loses Confirm.
