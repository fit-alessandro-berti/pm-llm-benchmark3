6.5

The answer covers all six required sections, keeps the main causal story multi-mechanism, and respects the hard constraints: early cases are treated as more complex, late speed-up is tied to pressure rather than learning, punishment is rejected, and quality checks are not recommended for removal. The mechanism table, resource splits, and WIP-by-complexity interaction are mostly faithful to the given figures.

It is not nearly flawless. Queue length from the sequence-position table (62 and 88, and the mid-shift 30–39 range) is repeatedly labeled as WIP, even though the log and the second table treat `wip_at_start` and `queue_length_at_start` as distinct. The pooled 76+ bin is treated as a clean within-shift clock; the answer never notes that only shifts long or busy enough to reach 76 events enter that bin. The cross-shift contrast that best separates pressure from fatigue is missed: day shift ends faster (10.9 min) with a 7.5% error rate as the queue grows from 38 to 75, while night ends slower (15.2 min) with a 5.1% error rate. Night’s 5.1% is still cited as fatigue support, which is a weak and poorly anchored comparison.

The PM4Py section is only partly an analysis plan. Within-shift position is named but not computed. The “last two hours” filter uses `shift_start_time`, so it does not select the end of each shift. `discover_batches` is followed by a filter on `is_batch`, a column that call does not create. Several APIs are invented or mis-signed (`clock_unit`, batch kwargs, `view_performance_dfg` on a raw discovery result). `resource_experience_months` is unused.

The dashboard has eight tiles but omits waiting time, which was explicitly required, and only weakly covers complexity mix and worker-load fairness. Intervention 6 lists throughput as a benefit and is never clearly rejected. A few causal lines are too strong for coarse, collinear data (“primary driver,” bot outputs less reliable, seniors’ selection “stabilizes” the aggregate 3.4% error). Arrow characters are also corrupted in several numeric claims, which makes the table harder to verify.
