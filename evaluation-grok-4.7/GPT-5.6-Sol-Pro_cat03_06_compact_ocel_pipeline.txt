The construction is internally consistent and arithmetically tight: 17 events, 12 objects, 12 O2O rows, and 53 E2O rows split 34/19 all reconcile with the link specifications, and the Order, Package, Delivery, and Invoice traces and DFG frequencies follow from that enriched relation table. Provenance via `relation_origin`, timestamp/activity denormalization from the event table, payment amounts kept on events, and the explicit non-uniqueness caveat for the object-centric Petri net are all sound. The V1→M2 inheritance observation is a precise reading of last-event/first-event co-occurrence, and the shared-event set {e06, e07, e09, e11, e12, e13, e15} is the right Order convergence set.

The score stays below the top band because of a few real slips. Section 3 says O100’s P1/D1 and P2/D2 branches reconverge at V1; those logistics branches never meet again. V1 converges O100 with O200, and P2 is not on the invoice. `order_context` is applied to adding, reserving, packing, dispatch, and delivery confirmation, so the qualifier no longer identifies the order’s role. `carried_package` on Confirm Delivery relocates the carries role onto the confirmation event. The OC-DFG item weights (for example Pack→Dispatch = 3) are object-flow counts and are easy to misread as event counts, unlike the careful ET–OT note that Record Payment–Order = 4 is two events times two orders. The OTG descendant wording also lumps O200 with O100 for later logistics objects, while O200 never meets P2 or D2. The 158-row interaction figure is correct only for an unaggregated ordered expansion; a true summary would collapse those pairs.
