7.5

The data model is coherent and the primary analysis is strong, but the required pipeline is not executable and a few later claims are imprecise.

The in-memory OCEL is internally consistent: 17 events, 12 objects, 35 E2O rows, and 12 O2O rows match the narrative. Qualifiers, UTC timestamps, payment attributes, referential checks, and the choice not to hang logistics or payments directly on orders are explicit and applied consistently. Order, Package, Delivery, and Invoice trace shapes and DFG edge counts follow from that model, including the I2 path that skips Reserve Stock, the P1/V1 convergence points, and the empty Payment DFG. The OC-PN caveat correctly refuses a unique behavioral reading.

The pipeline still breaks at the first required flattening. `pm4py.discover_dfg` returns only the DFG dict; unpacking it into `(dfg, start_activities, end_activities)` raises `ValueError`, so DFG, log-skeleton, OC-DFG, OTG, ET–OT, and OC-PN discovery never run. That call should be `pm4py.discover_directly_follows_graph` (or three separate calls). `discover_otg` and `discover_etot` are also invoked unconditionally while the text itself treats them as version-sensitive.

Secondary inaccuracies: the source-minimal Order trace drops O100’s second Add Item; full O2O propagation is written as Dispatch → Confirm → Record Payment → Close and said to “enforce” that order, although M1 and invoicing sit between dispatch and confirmation and O100 and O200 do not get the same events; “P1 carries I1/I3” uses the Delivery qualifier for a Package–Item link.
