The write-up has the requested section structure and a few directionally right ideas (offset defined as recorded minus true, SLA separated from the profile, DST fold acknowledged, T-408’s 55-minute containment and ~38-hour close called violations). The quantitative analysis is not usable. Intervals, durations, acceptance regions, and labels repeatedly contradict each other, and several required tests are missing or fabricated.

Clock bounds are described and then not applied. HUB rows are zero-width despite ±0.5 s resolution and a ±1 s offset. OPS-BER, SENSOR, VENDOR-NY, and MOBILE-LON windows are mostly raw display or timezone conversions. T-402’s Berlin start is placed at 09:14 UTC instead of about 09:18 UTC before the [−90 s, +120 s] correction. T-403 treats 04:00 EDT as 13:00 UTC and shifts already-UTC HUB times by +4 h, so a roughly 8–19 minute alert-to-start gap becomes a negative duration and a false certain violation. T-409 places 18:04 CEST near 09:04 UTC rather than about 16:04 UTC ±12 minutes, which also flips containment from a bounded pass to a multi-hour indeterminate.

SLA regions are wrong throughout: S1 is not [900, 1800] s, S2 is not [2100, 2700] s, and S3 is not [86400, 108000] s. The rules are one-sided maxima of 900 s, 2700 s, and 129600 s. Section 3 then disagrees with itself: T-401 S2 is called a pass at “59 m–1 h 5 m,” which is already over 45 minutes and outside the stated band; T-404 S1 is a pass at “10–17 m < 15 m”; T-406 and T-407 contain inline “Wait / Correction” reversals. Partial overlap is often labeled a certain violation because only the upper endpoint was checked. The same fake ~24 h close interval is copied onto cases with no close event.

T-405 does not preserve the two fold images or the ingestion constraint. The only order-consistent fold assignment is early-fold alert with late-fold start and containment, leaving minute-scale intervals, not the duplicated two-hour spans and [−5520, 6000] s durations given. T-404’s prose anchors the 18–25 minute inference on containment at 10:55, while the table anchors it on investigation completion at 10:35. T-406’s stated ±300 s mobile offset and entry-delay treatment do not appear in [12:14, 12:19]. T-407’s receipt window runs past investigation start after the answer says it adopted the causal constraint.

Sensitivity inherits those intervals, omits cases that would actually change, and still collapses HUB uncertainty to points. The Python block is an incomplete skeleton: non-IANA zone names, broken fold arithmetic, only T-401 populated, no `zoneinfo`, and neither `pm4py.format_dataframe` nor `conformance_temporal_profile`. Sections 6–7 therefore rest on incorrect statuses. The overlap-is-compliance enforcement rule also erases the possible-versus-certain distinction the analysis was supposed to make.
