5.5

The required nine-part structure is present, and the source reading is mostly right: register first, assess/prepare unordered, legal XOR with a silent skip, verification as a do-redo loop of at least one iteration, partial synchronization at release, and archive last. The non-block-structured N-shaped dependency (legal after assess only; verification after both assess and prepare; join only at release) is a sound reason to expect the process-tree pipeline to fail, and the canonical choice of POWL plus an accepting net, with BPMN kept derivative, is the right recommendation.

The audit overclaims results that were never produced. Several cells say `validated preserved` and `conversion failed` even though the code is only proposed. Allowed labels are also mixed (`conversion failed / not tested`, `approximated / ambiguous`). The initial marking is described as “after Register”; it is the marking that enables Register. “If loop condition” misstates the redo child as a data condition rather than an exclusive redo choice.

The required code would not carry the audit. `save_vis_bpmn(..., "*.bpmn")` asks a graph-visualization saver for a non-visualization extension. The bounded search calls undefined `pm4py.apply` and, on failure, returns the same marking, so both languages collapse to empty and the verdict can still pass. `max_loop_iter` is never used. The positive and negative witnesses are written and then ignored. Reachability is read from `.nodes` on a transition system that exposes `.states`, so that check is dead. Rejecting on reachability-node inequality is the wrong equivalence test for a BPMN round trip, which adds silent routing. The soundness branch is an empty `pass`, and a round trip that is unsound still passes unless the reference is sound and the round trip is not. Footprint mismatch is only a warning. Cross-net `fm` object equality in the rejection list is not a meaningful test. Criterion 9 also breaks down at “without reaching factory.”

Section 6 and Section 9 are the strongest parts: layout versus concurrency, invisible skip, and non-exhaustiveness of a bounded visible language are correctly separated. Those distinctions do not repair an audit that treats an unrun, non-terminating comparison as preservation evidence.
