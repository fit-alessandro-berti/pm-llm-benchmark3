8.3

The delay decomposition is analytically excellent and numerically reliable. The max-based fork–join identity is correct, the case table and the F02/F04/F07/F11 walk-throughs match the log, synchronization is kept out of the additive total, and B is ranked by criticality, tail offset, exclusive margin, and flow-time effect rather than by its shorter mean service. The three final-station readings are properly labeled as assumptions. Fixed slots, retained lag, and immediate dispatch are not conflated, and the reported savings check out, including A’s 15 join-minutes versus 5 flow-minutes, B’s 92.5 fully propagated minutes, immediate dispatch at 171 with only F11 still waiting 5, and the combined 268.5 from a real 5-minute interaction through F09.

The required code path is weaker than the prose. PM4Py is mislabeled optional and was not run. `get_service_time(..., aggregation_measure="mean")` is an unverified signature; only `ImportError` is caught, so a wrong keyword aborts the script after the pandas work instead of completing the required call. The sandbox download link points to no supplied file. The 10-minute acceleration column is calculated and then omitted from the printed influence table.

Two smaller presentation defects remain. F05’s B-queue gain is 10, not half of 25, because A caps it at offset 50, but unlike F10 that cap is never stated, so the table looks inconsistent. Mean savings are rounded to 7.71 while 92.5/12 and the stated mean flow 107.54 imply 7.708. The 09:00–12:40 occupancy denominator is also an arbitrary window, though the arithmetic and caveat are visible.
