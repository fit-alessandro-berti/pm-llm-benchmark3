8.8

The audit is unusually strong: the invariant table, the N-shaped (non-series-parallel) argument, the visible-language oracle, and the 15-marking net all cohere. A direct enumeration of the compact encoding (Register as the AND-split, one silent join of Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence before the loop, Legal Review/skip as a place-choice, Release Change itself competing with Correct Defect for the post-Inspect token) yields exactly 15 reachable markings, and the claimed 45 bounded traces with the three lost `R A L P …` words under `->(R, +(A,P), +(X(L,τ), *(->(C,I),D)), Rel, Arc)` are the right counts. Failure of Petri-net→tree is treated as the correct outcome, trace equivalence is separated from step semantics and from the moment of the loop-exit choice, and round-trip through the same library is not mistaken for proof.

Deductions are for remaining imprecision, not for the overall approach. The alignment rule (fitness 1.0 means “accepted”) is not the same test as exact final-marking membership and is unsafe for prefix negatives such as missing Archive unless the aligner is known to force the final marking; that assumption is never stated. The independent “soundness” check uses a place-set superset test rather than componentwise cover, so it is not quite the classical definition it is presented as. The 6-of-40 Woflan reruns, the scipy instability explanation, and the concrete Graphviz geometry (Correct Defect “above and to the right”) are reported as measurements but are not produced by the code shown. The claim that `convert_to_bpmn` swallows `BaseException` and rethrows a generic error is a specific library fact the rest of the argument does not need and does not establish. Section 6 calls the diagram behaviourally acceptable because the round trip validated, which is a step past the sharper caveat in Section 9.

Those are local gaps. The pipelines, witnesses, rejection criteria, and the choice of POWL as the authoritative model are sound.
