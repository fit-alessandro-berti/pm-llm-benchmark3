6.4

The design is a real discrete-event twin (calendars, emergent queues, non-stationary arrivals, latent objections, cross-case batching, multi-criteria decisions, no false optimality), but several material errors would mis-calibrate it if followed.

Process-mining and probability grounding is the weakest part. Variant shares are used as if they were conditional probabilities: the 19% “simple with missing documents” path is not P(completeness rework), and “18% + 13%” is not P(fire). The routing prose reverses the fire/environment rule (a `yes` flag is sufficient, not “necessary but not sufficient”), while the code does the opposite. D3/D5 longer travel is treated as environmental sensitivity, which the log summary does not say. The 40% environmental figure is the reviewers’ non-permit time, not “40% of the 8-day wait is third-party,” so capacity thinning and an extra delay double-count the same number. Parallel-branch linearization and the public-notice timestamp (a start of a statutory clock, not a completion) are never handled, so inter-event times will mix waiting, service, and notice duration. Planning-review rework and appeals are named in the problem and then mostly dropped. Treating the 7% rare variants as noise biases the tail the decision rule claims to protect. The provided process-tree string is never parsed; it is replaced by an ellipsis and dismissed as invalid PTML. That shorthand is close to PM4Py’s operator syntax (`->`, `X`, `+`, `*`, `tau`). Several cited APIs are wrong (`pm4py.discovery.dfg`, `pm4py.conformance.diagnostics_alignments`, `…log.statistics.get_case_arrival_average`, string `variant="performance"`, log-variant statistics on a dataframe). The skeleton does separate log handling from the engine and correctly refuses to split sojourn into service and wait on completion-only stamps.

Simulation mechanics are directionally right and quantitatively inconsistent. Peak months are 1.7× January–February, but the generator multiplies that factor by the overall mean (~57/day) and sets every other month, including June–August and December, equal to January–February, so annual volume and the seasonal shape both drift. Architect bursts are Hawkes-like in one section, Markov-modulated in another, and unimplemented in `next_arrival`. Monday is described as a pure arrival effect while one of five clerks is pulled to portal support. Initial WIP of 150 cases contradicts Little’s law: about 57 arrivals/day times a ~50-day median sojourn is on the order of a few thousand cases, and the mean is higher. “From pilot 5 runs, warm-up ≈ 90 days” reports an experiment that was not run. Deleting the first 90 days of a January start also cuts into the spring peak. Travel savings are 30% in the assumptions and 20% in the matrix. Commercial + complex is 27%, not the 21% used for D5. The D6 “same planner, −15% rework” effect is not what that intervention is. Clerk availability in code is 24/7. Case attributes required by routing are not sampled. CRN substreams are promised and not designed; variable event counts (D2 skips draws) would desynchronize a single stream. Case-level bootstrap under cross-case dependence is not distinguished from replication bootstrap.

The experiment matrix and decision section cover B, D1–D10, a second package, and more than three stresses, with real trade-offs. Effect sizes are still single invented points (`−40%` missing documents, `60%` of simple cases under €50k, `+15%` defect risk) even though `application_value` is in the log and violation history is not. Thresholds are often non-binding against the stated baseline (completeness rework “&lt;25%”), and “400 inspector-days” mixes labor with case-waiting and is far too small to matter. Applicant burden from D1 never enters the formal rule. Out-of-sample “policy A/B” confuses a historical holiday check with validating a counterfactual. Chi-square `p>0.05` on 18 months of daily counts will reject trivial misfit.

Uncertainty handling (Welch, seasonal WIP, paired CRN, BCa, Sobol, heavy-tail quantiles, Benjamini–Hochberg, no optimality claim) is the strongest part, but it sits on uncalibrated effect sizes and the volume/WIP errors above.
