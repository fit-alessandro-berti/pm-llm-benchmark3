7.5

The analysis is complete, well structured, and right on the main judgments: UI retries and telemetry are noise, silent `tau` fits, CL-609 and CL-610 are zero-cost policy failures, CL-604 is an irreversible pre-approval payment, denied and withdrawn cases are scope mismatches, and raw cost is not risk. Severity, confidence, owners, and remediation are mostly usable.

Defects keep it well short of nearly flawless.

Alignment counts are wrong in the semantics column and again in the code table. CL-604 has five synchronous moves (Register, Validate, Assess Loss, Manual Approve, Notify Claimant), not four. CL-611 has six synchronous moves, not five.

Section 6 misstates the repairs. CL-607 is `L(Deny Claim)` plus `M(Assess Loss)`, `M(Auto Approve)`, and `M(Pay Claim)`, with Notify synchronous. CL-612 already has synchronous Assess Loss and is missing Auto Approve, Pay Claim, and Notify Claimant. The parenthetical that both pay for `M(Assess Loss)` and `M(Notify Claimant)` cannot be true: that set plus the log move would cost 5, not the supplied 4.

Confidence is inconsistent. Section 1 calls CL-602 low/medium confidence and groups CL-606 with CL-608 as medium confidence. The case table then rates CL-602 medium-high and CL-606 high. Section 1 also says the 10:03/10:20 timestamps “prove beyond doubt” that payment preceded approval, while Section 4 still treats clock skew as a live hypothesis.

CL-609 is described as an “over-payment” and the auto-approve pattern as “direct financial leakage.” P1 forbids Auto Approve at EUR 18,000 and MEDIUM risk; it does not show that the assessed amount was excessive or that money was lost. P2 also does not require Fraud Screen for that band (not above EUR 50,000, not HIGH, no bank change). The pattern text still says these cases bypass a required fraud screen. The grey zone is never called out.

The “~80%+” share of high-cost false positives is not in the evidence; the supplied fact is only that denied and withdrawn cases account for most alignments with raw cost ≥ 4. Collusion is listed as a likely cause for CL-602 rather than as one unproven hypothesis. Severity 5 for the EUR 4,500 bank-change payout matches the EUR 480,000 case and flattens the amount dimension the framework itself defines.

The Python block is not executable: `pm4py. format_dataframe` is a syntax error. Trace reconstruction, separate technical-cost and business-severity columns, and the note that PM4Py may return a different optimal alignment are otherwise sound. The visible-move counter also assumes a flat `(log, model)` tuple and `None` for `tau`, which will mis-count under the nested alignment format some PM4Py versions return.
