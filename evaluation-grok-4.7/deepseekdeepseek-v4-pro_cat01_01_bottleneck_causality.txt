5.5

The verdict “mainly confounded,” the crude-versus-within-complexity reversal, the selection node, and the pilot-plus-compliance-capacity plan are the right skeleton. The write-up then mishandles the evidence those claims rest on.

The auditable decomposition is not reliable. An overall-complexity standardization of the published stratum means is about 17.1 h versus 19.6 h (Orion faster by roughly 2.5 h). The stated 20 h versus 24.9 h, and the “~5 h” contrast repeated as support in the hypothesis table, do not match that weighting. Night/day counts are wrong (17 night and 13 day, not 10 and 20). Nova’s day mean on the stated 12 cases is about 14.5 h, not 9.9 h, and Nova’s four night cases average 17.5 h, not 11.3 h. Medium Nova is 25% night, not 50%. Low Orion is one night and one day (C02); Orion is not night-only. High Nova is not “night/high for both”: C30 is day with normal load. The claim of no residual shift confounding in that stratum is therefore false.

Review service is systematically replaced by cycle time minus wait. Those are not equal. On the actual `review_service_h` field, high-complexity service is about 9.2 h in both groups, and the within-stratum cycle gaps are mostly `wait_before_review_h`. The mechanism story that Orion is faster on service is not what the columns show.

Required causal pieces are incomplete or mis-specified. No hypothesis assigns Orion itself a genuine harmful effect; shift/load is a separate required hypothesis and is already used. Compliance is treated as a possible mediator and as a front-door variable, but within low and high complexity it is fixed by case type, and within medium the compliance rate is similar across groups. The routing rule is proposed as an instrument even though it is driven by complexity. `unobserved_reviewer_skill` has no path into service or quality, and `resource_group → shift` is labeled as fact rather than a directional assumption. The closing recommendation drops the earlier hedge and treats the stratified reversal as a reason reassignment would raise cycle time.

The code would recompute the crude and complexity-weighted contrasts more correctly than the prose, and it warns on thin cells, but it fabricates timestamps, skips `parse_event_log_string`, and calls `view_dfg`, which can fail before the directly-follows comparison is printed. The five uncertainties and the three-phase safeguards are specific and usable.
