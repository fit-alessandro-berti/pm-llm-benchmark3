7.5

The final write-up meets the required structure, stays multi-hypothesis, and calibrates the absence result, segregation-of-duties concern, and “do not remove her” recommendation well. The reconciliation of high handover/subcontracting centrality with low working-together co-occurrence is substantively right, and the cited counts, the 65% tail shift, and the 120–180-word summary check out.

It is not nearly flawless on process-mining validity. Out-degree centrality 0.82 is treated as evidence that her events are “very frequently” followed by others; on a normalized SNA graph that score measures breadth of distinct targets, not handover volume. The handover section also says the network does not show whether handovers are fast or slow, despite mean elapsed times in the edge table. Working-together is misdescribed as inflated by long cases even though the given metric is a case co-occurrence rate, and the proposed geometric-mean correction inherits that mistake. Activity-based similarity is conflated with handover patterns when Mira is said to be likely to cluster with Bot_Rules, whose activity profile is different. The inference that her higher exception share includes the bot’s “errors” overreads a selection effect. Minor API slips (`["True"]`, `filter_case_values`) and an arbitrary &lt;10% re-routing cutoff add further imprecision.
