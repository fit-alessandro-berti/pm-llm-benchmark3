7.4

The design is conservative in the right places and the numeric scenario counts check out, but it is not nearly flawless. Several ordering claims are stronger than the facts, and the implementation does not actually do what the design says.

The decision tree covers every required class and maps each one to a legal terminal action. The pair table is consistent with that tree: e03/e04 and e11/e16 are the only merges treated as strongly supported; e05 and e14 stay distinct; e08/e09 is correctly kept as a harmonization collision; e06/e07 and e15 stay as abstraction-level observations in the conservative path. Retained counts are arithmetically right: 15, 14, 14 (guarded), and 10 (unguarded), with E2O counts 44, 39, 37, 36, and 28. The `have_common_object=True` warning is the right one: O1 and V1 do not separate M1 from M2. Provenance fields and the eight required failure modes are present, and the prose does not treat a total order as physical truth.

The ordering section still over-commits. e12 before e13 and e09 before e10 are the only hard semantic precedences in the 12:00 and 10:15 ties. Both “admissible” 12:00 orders still force e11 before e12 and e12 before e14, which the facts do not support and which creates the same false direct-follow story the microseconds warning describes. e08 before e09 is labeled intended and then hardcoded, despite the alternative order being equally consistent with the facts. Pick-before-pack is plausible, but it is stated more firmly in the intended sequence than in the rule table.

The code is the largest gap. `merge_verified_pair` unions object strings and writes provenance, but it never merges attributes, never records conflicts, and stores eids and `source_event_key`s in one pipe-delimited field. `validate` then requires every token to be a raw source key, so e03|e04|… fails the conservative pipeline it just built. It also never checks that relations still point at surviving events and objects. `order_key` is an unpadded string of the row index, so a lexicographic sort places `"10"` before `"2"`. The aggressive call is the unguarded library merge, not the guarded 14-event scenario analyzed in sections 5 and 7. Sensitivity names the right edges, but OC-DFG frequencies, ET–OT weights, and durations stay qualitative.
