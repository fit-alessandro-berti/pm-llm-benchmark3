8.2

The assessment is structurally complete and unusually disciplined. It separates facts from assumptions, uses an issue-specific hierarchy rather than recency or global wording, applies controls at commitment, refuses to treat late ERP stamps or later approvals as curative, and does not flatten every case into one label. P-501’s pre-ECD timing, P-502’s non-retroactive CFO email and late new-supplier clearance, P-503’s SoD/A3 result independent of scope, P-504’s unapproved-versus-new distinction, P-506’s expired directive, and P-507’s signed-PDF versus late ERP treatment are all handled correctly. Alternatives identify the premise that would change the result, and the remediation section properly separates breach, late act, logging defect, and inability to prove.

The score is held down by several non-cosmetic problems. The formal rule table drops the threshold operators for the EUR 50,000, 25,000, and 150,000 rules, so the catalogue is not self-sufficient even though the prose and pseudo-logic are correct. Plant approval under the local SOP is applied unevenly: it is central for P-501 and P-506, but P-502 never records the complete absence of Plant approval despite the email not yet being in force. The primary INDETERMINATE labels for P-505, P-507, and P-508 sit uneasily with the stated burden and with affirmative record facts—the separate 19:00 Finance approval in P-505, and “no separate Finance event” in P-507 and P-508. Those cases are well caveated, but the method would more naturally treat undocumented mandatory pre-commitment approval as noncompliance, with role evidence as a possible reverser. Confidence on the indeterminate cases is also ambiguous, and the replacement clause does not cleanly state which local approvals joint authorization actually replaces.
