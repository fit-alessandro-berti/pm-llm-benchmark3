6.7

The final JSON is structurally complete and often process-aware, but it is not nearly flawless. It correctly avoids copying the directly-follows graph, separates C6-A from C6-B, treats missing completions as non-proof, and handles the migration feed on C6-D with a real observation-model alternative.

The main defect is that several “next logged” predictions are later outcomes, not the next log line. On C6-B, Fast Close and Close — No Safety Issue are ranked as next events even though the reasoning itself places them after Evidence Review Completed. Fast Close is also the wrong closure label: it is the S1 triage path, while the evidenced post-review non-investigation outcome is Close — No Safety Issue, and this case already left triage by requesting evidence. C6-C’s Authority Notification and C6-E’s third evidence request have the same problem; marking `process_enabled: false` does not repair putting them in the immediate-next distribution. Disposition is marked process-enabled on C6-C and C6-D while the primary state still has Regulatory Assessment open. On C6-D that assessment has been open only about 80 minutes against a 2–14 hour completion range, so a 0.10–0.25 chance that disposition is next is too high.

Secondary issues: C6-B’s primary state wrongly calls the review a “second-cycle” review; C6-A gives Technical and Regulatory completions too much probability relative to an evidence review already inside its 1–5 hour window; and C6-E overweights Supplier Analysis completion when the supplier certificate arrived only at 13:20 and is more likely to open that analysis after the current review than to finish it first.
