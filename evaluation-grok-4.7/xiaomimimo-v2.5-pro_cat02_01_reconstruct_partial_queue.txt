The case reconstruction is largely competent: fully observed waits and services, completed sojourns (sum 2050 min, mean 146.4), censoring versus elapsed service, and the four missing-start windows tied to resource free time are mostly right, and the downstream-bottleneck reading is directionally defensible. It is not reliable enough to score higher.

The sensitivity table is internally inconsistent and partly impossible. Scenario C’s claimed mean S2 service of 28.6 min is below the fully observed 12-case sum of 480 min even after adding the shortest allowed imputed services (minimum mean about 35). The S1 scenario means are also off. The claim that utilization above 78% for every resource is stable across all three scenarios is false: shortening P07, P15, P05, and P11 to 5 min drops I1, C1, and C2 well below that line.

The Little’s Law “exact” check on S1 over 11:00–13:00 equates the in-window WIP integral to the full sojourns of seven departures (825 min). Most of that time is before 11:00, and P18–P22 are omitted, so the identity is circular rather than a validation. The stated snapshot-average WIP of about 9.3 does not match the printed snapshot table. At 10:00, P07 is dropped between stations; at 09:00, P08’s arrival is omitted while P05’s simultaneous start is counted.

Assumption A4 (FIFO, no overtaking) is contradicted by the log: P09 enters the S2 queue at 10:35 and starts at 11:00 on C1, while P08 has been waiting since 10:20 and does not start until 11:15 on C2. That undercuts both the FIFO claim and the attribution of P08’s wait solely to the C2 outage. The S1 coverage cell also says four missing starts; S1 has two (P07, P15).
