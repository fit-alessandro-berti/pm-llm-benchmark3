7.3

The role structure is largely evidence-based, covers every resource, and respects the required distinction between frequency, complexity, authority, and substitution. It is not nearly flawless: several quantitative and methodological claims are wrong or unsupported, and the code would not establish what the commentary says it establishes.

The 0.54 Hana–Mateo similarity is used as a reason naïve clustering would merge them. That figure is evidence they differ. A merge is plausible only because both touch A9, which is a different argument. Mateo’s core is defined as A13 (42) and A9 (44), while A12 (52), A2 (50), and A4 (45) are folded into “small counts in nearly every activity.” Those are his largest counts, so the core/peripheral split is not consistent with the matrix. Giorgio’s A8 (88) essentially matches Fatima’s (90), and only 18 of those 88 events fall in the absence window; 31 of 72 A7 events do. Treating commercial work as mainly substitution-driven, and sizing the desk as “1.5 people,” does not follow from those counts. “Only meaningful A6 performer” also overstates exclusivity: Chen has 24 and Mateo 16 against Erik’s 96. Fatima’s 16 A9 events are called “preliminary” with no evidence; that label is given only for Mateo’s week-5 stand-ins. Section 6 tells management to use Dina’s complexity trend, but the log summary is a 10-week snapshot with no trend. Chen’s late shift versus Dina’s day shift is an obvious alternative and is never considered, even though shift is used to split Ivan and Julia. The delay of two critical cases is only loosely tied to Mateo’s filter.

The code separates the bot, joins complexity and substitution, and warns against reading clusters as authority. It then breaks its own checks. Handover weight is passed to NetworkX betweenness, which treats weight as distance, so strong ties are penalized and the broker test is inverted. If these calls return an SNA object, `.items()` is the wrong accessor. The pricing filter is discarded, `get_service_time` is run on the unfiltered log, and `df_timed` is unused, so durations are never compared by performer. Complexity-banded cosine similarity is scale-invariant and need not fall in the top band if Chen and Dina keep the same activity mix. `get_variants` cannot show that a second A9 was another resource. Removing Nora before the handover network also hides the automation boundary the text says must stay visible.
