6.5

The required nine-part structure is complete, and the recommendation is robust rather than a base-case mean-delay optimum. The analysis still contains material calculation errors, an internal capacity inconsistency, and a preemption implementation that contradicts the stated rule.

Service-time means are wrong and then used throughout the offered-load math. With uniform midpoints, the mixture means are Intake 6.94, Electrical 32.82, Software 38.50, and Validation 15.74, not 6.86, 33.00, 38.00, and 16.62. Validation is about 5.6% high, so every validation load and several scenario ratios are off. Mechanical 26.30 and the doubled-tail U4 means are right.

Block capacity does not match the daily capacity in the same section. Ada’s 12:15–12:45 break is 30 minutes, but the 12:00–14:00 capacities (Mechanical 291, Intake 497.7, Electrical 380.1, Validation 503.1) only remove 15 minutes. Emil’s 16:00–16:30 break is omitted entirely: 16:00–18:00 Software is shown as 91.2/120 (76%) and Electrical as 212.3, which requires 120 Emil minutes. Daily Emil capacity correctly uses 450 minutes, so the tables do not reconcile. With the break, that Software block is about 91/90, slightly over scheduled capacity, not 76%. That reverses the stated base-case late-afternoon bottleneck.

The three policies, two objectives, U2 ambiguity, shift-aware skill coverage, and the conclusion that routing cannot meet the U5 P1 target are strong. The scenario table is explicitly a screening band, but the bands are too wide and too loosely tied to the load math to audit the claimed SLA gaps. Hybrid and “full-pooling surge” also overlap: the same 5-minute, 15-minute, and 85% triggers already define hybrid overflow.

The code encodes the tables, three policies, seeds, handover/setup/parallelism, and the requested PM4Py calls, and it correctly separates stage queue time from PM4Py case service. On reassignment after preemption, however, `remaining` is reset to the full base draw times the multiplier, so both partial progress and the 10% restart penalty are discarded. `pm4py.write_log` is also likely to throw before the required PM4Py analyses.
