The diagnosis of the regimes is right and well separated: April is a schema and feature-pipeline break, June is a routing-mix shift with stable within-variant breach rates, August is a supplier and V3-performance break before Partner Z exists, September adds population shift and censoring, and October’s 0.16 breach rate is not comparable to mature months. The table, tests, training gates, leakage warning, and alert dependencies all follow from that split.

The score is held down by concrete defects. The required sketch crashes on timezone-aware timestamps (`tz_localize(None)` after `utc=True`), treats `pm4py.get_variants` values as counts, and reads a `case_duration` column that `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` does not produce, so case duration is silently empty. Event-month performance DFGs also drop boundary-spanning waits. “Predicted ~0.32” for V3 is not in the data. August’s V3 share is a post-diagnosis mix statistic, not measured covariate drift, and volume growth is weak support for that row. October’s API backlog is double-filed as a feature-pipeline failure. April’s variant rates ignore selection from 18% unmapped cases. The Jan–Jun parts edge (28 h to 42 h) and the Aug-to-Sep V3 breach dip (0.64 to 0.61 while the edge slowed further) are unexplained. “Four regimes” does not match the seven-part narrative, ECE is equated with a 5-point mean gap, and the October calibration sign flip (0.21 vs 0.31, then 0.23 vs 0.16) is never stated cleanly.
