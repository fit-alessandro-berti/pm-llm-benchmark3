8.5

The audit is unusually rigorous: it separates future leakage from repairable lineage, keeps congestion and prefix age, treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as normative but inadmissible, and treats `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` as both future and order-ambiguous. It also refuses to subtract SHAP mass from a contaminated model and distinguishes prediction, causation, action, and fairness. Those are the decisions that matter here.

It is not nearly flawless. The validation suite’s A5 rule says training labels for a model applied at `t0` must come from cases whose outcome was already known before `t0`. Under the document’s own definition of `t0`, that is false: the breach label is future by construction. Read charitably, it is a mangled out-of-time backtest rule; as written, it would reject every valid supervised model. That is a material error in the section meant to be operationalized.

Section 4 also puts `claimant_channel` and `initial_reserve` in `credible_drivers` even though their as-of-`t0` lineage is explicitly unverified. The task required drivers that are defensibly available now; the hedges belong in `removed_or_repaired_drivers` until the timestamp is shown. `initial_reserve` is likewise left out of the “unusable until repaired” class while still depending on an unproven freeze.

Smaller overclaims remain. “Appeal is mechanically part of the label” overstates a duration effect. Week 52 is asserted to be the holiday and policy regime, although the prompt only aligns those shocks with unspecified weeks. “Relatively large” reserve and the fast-track mechanism for complexity go beyond the evidence, though the latter is at least suggested by the process text. `same_day_document_count_snapshot` is real post-`t0` leakage plus train/serve skew, and medium severity is soft. Footprint results collapse directly-follows, eventually-follows, and concurrency into “allowed.”

Coverage, severity structure, partial-order handling, and the X/Y versus Z distinction are otherwise at an expert level. The A5 defect and the premature credible drivers are why this stays well below a near-flawless score.
