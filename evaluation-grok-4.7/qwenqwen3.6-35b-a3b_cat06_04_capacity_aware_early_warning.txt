5.5

The final policy has the right shape: stage-gating, calibration shifts, spillover in the score, a reserve slot, I-03 excluded, I-05 suppressed, I-07 reopened for the new log, and all required fairness and monitoring topics covered. It is not decision-theoretically coherent, and the portfolio and impact numbers do not follow the rule it states.

The deployable rule is internally broken. It says a case advances only if adjusted probability exceeds 0.45 and only positive scores are staffed, with Strategic probabilities cut by about 0.085 and a €2,400 spillover charge per slot. I-04’s adjusted probability is about 0.40 even at the smallest stated Strategic bias, yet it is Tier 1 and staffed. I-07’s midpoint score is largely negative once that spillover charge, swarm cost, and false-alarm burden are included, yet it is also Tier 1. I-08 clears the midpoint score and is skipped. I-02 and I-09 have the same “second alert, no prior swarm” pattern and similar probability jumps, but only I-09 is staffed. “Both” is paired with an “or” in the re-entry rule, so the rule does not pick a unique action. The 2× urgency weight and the Non-Native shrinkage/manual-review safeguard are stated and then not used on the live portfolio. Calling a 10-hour runway a reason to spend a slot now reverses the option value of waiting.

The impact table is not auditable. Base “breaches avoided” of 0.4–0.6 sits below the midpoint absolute-risk-reduction sum of about 0.69; the adverse band of 0.1–0.3 sits below the lower-bound sum of about 0.48. Net ranges do not reconcile benefit, €1,350 labor, about €2,850 false-alarm burden, and the stated spillover. Spillover is a flat €20,000 loss on six unnamed diagnosis-stage cases, independent of who is treated and of the values actually in view. Optimistic confidence is rated medium-high partly because I-09’s data are “complete”; the case is the sparse Non-Native segment. Section 5 then lists I-07 as starved after Section 2 assigns it a slot.

Coverage keeps this above a pure probability ranking, but the allocation contradicts the rule and the scenario arithmetic does not check.
