6.5

The cluster ranking is directionally right often enough to be a real analysis: G1 as fixture-level simultaneity, G2 as a shared “begin group” start, G3 as folder-level completion, G4/G8 as zero-gap serial work, G5 as a shared login, G6 as a zero-duration bulk submit, and G7 as quarter-hour collapse. Sections 5–7 also keep identical timestamps from collapsing into one physical story, and the G6 wait-versus-service mechanism is the right one. The write-up is still not reliable. Arithmetic, the counterevidence column, the censoring model, and the PM4Py “results” fail in ways that change the conclusions.

Several reported figures do not match the table. G1 waits are 6, 5, 4, and 2 minutes, so the mean is 4.25, not 4.5. G6 arrival span is 14:05–16:25 (2 h 20 m), and the seven waits sum to 724 minutes, mean 103.4, not “2.5 hours” and 108.4. G8 waits are 38, 43, 48, 54, and 58 minutes, mean 48.2, not 47.6. G5’s stated 210% peak is the wrong quantity: from 13:12 to 13:20 all four intervals are open, so instantaneous load on that one resource id is 4; integrated load over 13:00–13:30 is 70/30 ≈ 233%.

The diagnosis table’s `strongest_counterevidence` cell is often evidence for the chosen label. G2’s one-minute recording lag after each distinct complete supports separate completion capture. G3’s 7–8 minute start spacing is the start-stagger of end batching, not a point against it. G7’s one-minute-apart `recorded_at` values support serial insertion under rounded lifecycle fields. G6’s contiguous `sequence_no` values are what ordered bulk entry produces. G5 is the cell that actually bites: four-way overlap exceeds the stated cap of three analysts. Section 5 then says treating the login as three people “resolves the physical impossibility,” which retracts that counterevidence. Durations there are also called accurate while the same intervals are used as if they were exclusive touch time.

The ±7.5 minute censoring interval is not implied by an undocumented down/up quarter-hour rule, and M05 rejects it. An arrival at 14:11 cannot sit inside [13:52.5, 14:07.5] around a displayed start of 14:00 if arrival precedes service. Flooring the start into `[14:00, 14:15)` is compatible with M02–M05 and with negative computed waits; symmetric nearest-bin censoring is not. That recommendation is repeated in §§5 and 7 as the G7 remedy.

Section 4 does not do the comparison it claims. The script never builds a granularity indicator or a queue-build-up series, never reads batch type, and scores overlap with a strict inequality, so G6’s point events at 17:00 get overlap 0. The following table assigns G6 overlap 1.00 and names PM4Py categories the code cannot print. “Parallel/Concurrent Batching” is not a PM4Py batch class; standard detection is simultaneous, start-batch, end-batch, and sequential. G5, with unequal starts and unequal completes, is exactly the overlap pattern those four labels miss. `(d=300, n=2)` also confuses `min_batch_size` with a discovered batch count. On exact adjacency, a zero merge distance already has a zero gap, so tying G4/G8 sequential detection to 300 seconds is unwarranted. `discover_batches` in current PM4Py takes a `parameters` dict; keyword `merge_distance=` is an untested call, and the category table is written as if it had run.

Hypotheses are written as measurements. “S04 physically waited until 10:21,” the G2/G3 reconstruction rules, and “proving real-time item exit scanning” are one serial reading of a group button plus separate completes. Under a real common setup, wait-until-10:00 can be the right queue definition while case service is still inflated. Section 3 states a single bias direction for each KPI; it does not carry conformance or throughput through the clusters, and it does not keep those directions conditional on the section 7 validation list. G4 is titled sequential batching at confidence 95 even though the same text later treats it as ordinary one-piece clearing; “conveyor” is not in the evidence. G8’s serial 7-minute scanner trace is strong, but the safety-check cause is not high-confidence: the clocks differ, and work starts at 15:38 against a 15:35 safety end. Adjudication puts G8 with G1 and G4 as directly usable.

The signature matrix and the redesign list cover the requested fields, including execution versus insert time, clocks, badges, fixture ids, capacity, pause events, and precision. The matrix still calls G2/G3-style nesting only “partial” overlap after the diagnostic table calls the same patterns overlap 1.00, and it folds multitasking into the shared-account row. Precision guidance says to abolish the 15-minute lattice rather than retain an explicit uncertainty field, which is the metadata needed once rounding remains.

The skeleton of the answer matches the task. The numbers, the counterevidence column, the G7 interval, the G5 capacity claim, and the PM4Py comparison do not.
