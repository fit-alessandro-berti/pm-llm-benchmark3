7.5

The final analysis is complete, well structured, and numerically careful on the main B1, S1, and CAL-3 intervals, with appropriate non-causal caveats and every required section present. It is not nearly flawless.

The clearest error is the treatment of RACK-7 on B2. J5 is ready at 14:40, while RACK-7 is free at 14:35, so rack cleaning is not the binding constraint on the 14:50 start. J4’s 1 h 40 min is mostly partner synchronization, plus about 10 minutes after both the rack and J5 are available. Calling this “RACK-7 cleaning delays B2,” and folding that claim into the top-ranked mechanism, overstates the tool’s role.

The FIX-A figure has a similar evidence stretch. CNC-2 is documented idle only from 10:05 to 10:47, yet 10:05–11:40 is treated as tool-attributable delay. The assumption is stated, but the limited idle window is not used to bound it. The CAL-3 “50 min” also includes 17:20–17:25, after recalibration ended. J2 is said to enter B1 at 12:05; the evidence says only that J2 entered before lot release.

The answer never reconciles OVEN-1’s 115-minute idle with a 73-minute longest event gap, or TEST-1’s 50-minute idle with a 39-minute gap. That tension matters for both delay attribution and the data-integration hypothesis. Direct versus inferred edges are discussed, but the edge list itself does not mark them. The PM4Py block names the requested functions and avoids gap-only waiting, while remaining only approximately executable.
