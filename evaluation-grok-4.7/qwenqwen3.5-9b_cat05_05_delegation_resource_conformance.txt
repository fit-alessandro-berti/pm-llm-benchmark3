The write-up follows the required headings and gets several clear calls right (CHG-501 as a valid QM substitution on the signed delegation, CHG-502 and alias-normalized CHG-508 as drafter/approver breaches, CHG-507 as Eva approving without QM authority, and CHG-510 as Clara approving her own emergency change). It also avoids treating the Clara–Ben pattern as proof of collusion. Those points are outweighed by systematic errors on the questions the scenario is built to test.

Identity evidence is flattened. CHG-503 is called a high-confidence unauthorized self-verification by Diego, even though the terminal is shared, there is no re-authentication, and CCTV is unavailable. That session does not reliably map `svc_qms` to Diego, so the case is a data/identity problem or indeterminate, not a proven segregation breach. CHG-509’s stronger badge and token evidence is not distinguished from that weak session. CHG-506 is treated as Ben simply being unavailable, with high confidence, although he denies the act and the office IP is already evidence that someone else used `u_bk`. The provided IP is listed as missing evidence. Technical resource conformance is never separated from the control objective, and none of the ten cases is classified as indeterminate, a data/identity problem, or a suspicious pattern.

Delegation scope and time are misapplied. D-QM-01 is production-equipment approval authority, but Ben’s cooling-system retrospectives on CHG-505 and CHG-510 are treated as if that delegation were general QM authority. CHG-505’s review on Friday 14 August is not tested against two business days from Tuesday 11 August; under a weekday count it is late, and the answer never defines the clock precisely enough to apply it. Ben’s denial is also attached to CHG-505 and CHG-508, where the facts do not place it. CHG-509 is a high-confidence valid substitution even though Asha’s documented leave ended on 14 August and D-QM-02 requires her absence. Diego is placed in the matrix as the D-IC-03 delegate rather than the principal. CHG-504 is high-confidence authorized despite the explicit credential-sharing gap.

The code does not meet the checking specification. It never calls `filter_four_eyes_principle` or the handover-of-work discovery, does not use the delegation table, discards `format_dataframe`, and would not run on this frame. Every `svc_qms` row is attributed to Diego, including CHG-509. Reciprocal approvals are omitted from the collusion section, and the case-level “alternative explanation” cells mostly restate the finding.
