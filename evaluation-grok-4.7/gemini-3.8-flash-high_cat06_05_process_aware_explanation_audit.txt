7.4

The audit is complete and often precise: every top-12 feature, sequence pattern, and local driver is covered; full-trace fields are rejected; queue length and elapsed time are kept; the appeal loop is treated as a later-legal cycle while `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as an ordering failure; and predictive, causal, actionable, and fairness standards are separated in the taxonomy. Those are the core distinctions this task requires. The score is held down by overclaims, one invented quantitative result, and several methodological mistakes.

The leakage table is slightly miscalibrated. `total_case_duration_hours` is critical post-closure leakage, but it is not identical to the 10-day settlement label, so “encodes the target” and “tautology” overstate it. `final_decision = Partial` is future outcome leakage, not the breach label itself. `final_handler_team` is unusable for every case because the warehouse has no as-of timestamp; “reassigned or escalated in 35%” is an inference from the 65% coincidence rate. `risk_score_current = 74` is only possibly post-`t0`; the write-up states that it is. `initial_reserve` is labeled a frozen intake signal even though no lineage is given and reserves are commonly revised. `Low/Medium` also breaks the required severity scale. `Documents Requested -> Documents Received` is the same class of post-`t0` pattern as damage-to-decision, but is rated only High.

Section 2 has the right checks, then overfits the process. Fraud investigation is conditionally triggered, not unconditionally enabled. Appeal is both currently impossible and later reachable, so the three-way “partition” is not a partition. `Absence3` imposes a two-decision cap the normative description does not state. Reordering timestamp ties by a normative topological sort can rewrite genuine deviations into the happy path, and the later recommendation to treat skips and inversions as ingestion errors would drop the deviant cases most relevant to breach.

Section 3 misuses Little’s Law: a snapshot of 18 is a congestion signal, not an application of \(L=\lambda W\) to this claim’s wait. The revised narratives then contradict the causal boundary. Region R4 is given a specific repair-density mechanism, the €25,000 reserve is said to mandate multi-tier authorization, and Broker is said to historically add communication hops. None of those mechanisms is established. The opaque “base and other features” mass (+0.21 / +0.20 / +0.17) is ignored, so retained SHAP signs from the leaked model are presented as if they were a recomputed explanation. “Maximal runway” for 1.2 hours and “immediate progression” for a queue of 4 are inflated.

The validation plan covers the requested controls, then introduces false precision. The counterfactual drop from 0.82 to 0.54 is not a result; it reuses the leaked probability and invents the effect. Requiring ablation to monotonically decrease predicted risk is not a valid SHAP faithfulness test. A named rank-stability cutoff of 0.85 is arbitrary.
