7.8

The pipeline is referentially sound and the flattened-trace predictions match the chosen relations, but the object-centric interpretation blurs type-level graphs with instance facts, and one payment qualifier overclaims the story.

The data model is the strongest part. All 17 events, 12 objects, timestamps, and O2O edges match the narrative, including the important exclusion of I2 from Reserve Stock. E2O rows repeat activity and timestamp by merge; orphan, uniqueness, type, and duplicate checks are real. Order traces are exact under this policy: O100 runs Create, two Add Item, Reserve, two Pack, two Dispatch, Issue Invoice, two Confirm, Close; O200 is the shorter P1/D1 path and never sees P2/D2 or payments. Package, Delivery, and Invoice shapes, copied shared events, and the one-event Payment flattening diagnosis are also right. Alternatives, failure modes, and the eight audit assertions are specific and mostly inspectable.

The main analytical error is in the object-centric section. OC-DFG, OTG, and the object-centric Petri net are type-level. They do not contain P1, V1, M1, M2, O100, or O200, and they cannot show that P1 is a stronger cross-order coupler than P2 or that O100 has a private branch while O200 does not. Both orders collapse into one Order lifecycle; two timed packs become a directly-follows self-succession, which hides divergence rather than revealing it. The interactions summary is the right place for those hubs, but O200’s neighborhood is understated: it co-occurs with O100 and I1 on reserve and on the P1 pack, which is the convergence itself.

Two smaller semantic slips matter. Both payments are described as partial, and no invoice total is given, yet M2 is qualified `completes_settlement`. `fulfilled_order` on the P1 pack also overstates O100, which still has I2 unpacked. Dispatch and confirm links to orders are a coherent policy choice, but calling them the narrative’s explicit shared behavior is too strong; the text is explicit for reserve, the P1 pack, and the invoice. Same-activity order DFG edges for Add Item and Confirm Delivery are omitted, though the edges that are listed do exist.
