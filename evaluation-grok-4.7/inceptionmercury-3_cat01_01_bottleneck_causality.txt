The answer is well structured, numerically careful on the main aggregates, and right about the decision: the crude Orion gap reverses inside every complexity stratum, stratification does not identify a group effect, and a 50% reassignment is not justified. Verdict, confidence, required sections, hypothesis types, and the learning-first pilot are all in place. It is not nearly flawless.

The headline standardized contrast is not auditable as written. `(7.509.64 + 17.1719.38 + 31.5735.00)/3 = 2.59` is not a valid expression, and even with restored minus signs it equals about −2.59, not +2.59. Crude Orion−Nova is reported as +6.72, while the standardized contrast is reported as a positive Orion advantage. The sign convention flips without definition. “High Nova n” in the high-complexity row is false; Nova has two high-complexity cases.

The DAG omits the dominant measured confounding path, `complexity → review_service` and `complexity → cycle_time`. `case_completed → quality_outcome` misstates selection as a causal effect of the selection indicator. `shift → resource_group` is labeled as fact rather than a directionally ambiguous staffing relation. Calling `routing_rule` a potential instrument is unwarranted: the stated rule uses case difficulty, so it is not exogenous.

Hypothesis 1 puts the complexity reversal in the contradicting column. That reversal is the evidence for case-mix confounding of the crude gap, not evidence against it. Hypothesis 2 attributes a beneficial Orion effect to shorter review service, but the within-stratum cycle gaps are mostly wait (about 1.6–3.3 h), while service gaps are only about 0.1–0.4 h. The crude residual of 0.71 h is mislabeled “overhead.” Cycle minus wait minus service is essentially 0.8 h with no compliance and no rework, about 2.2 h with compliance only, and about 3.6–3.7 h with both. That is compliance activity and request-info delay, both tied to case mix.

Total, direct, and mediated estimands are never stated as distinct contrasts. The executive success path is muddled: if Orion’s within-stratum speed is an artifact of easier unmeasured risk, moving those cases to Nova does not justify expecting Nova’s historical mean. The closing claim that the 6.7 h gap is “dominated by” mix, shift, load, compliance, and selection asserts more than the design identifies. The code meets the diagnostic spec, including a stated pooled-complexity weighting and small-cell warnings, but it cannot repair those interpretive errors.
