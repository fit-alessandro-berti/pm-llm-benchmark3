The write-up has the required headings, all 15 cases, and several defensible calls (SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-708, SUP-712, SUP-714). It is not a reliable conformance judgment. Load-bearing errors sit in authority, evidence, POWL semantics, and the mandatory revised model.

The burden test treats “M0/CS-9” as one rule. Fitness, the standard, overlays, and proof are different questions. SUP-712 fits M0 and still breaches CS-9; SUP-702 and SUP-704 break M0 and can still be authorized. Requiring the defense to show a “systematic” defect also misstates the case-level burden.

CS-9 requires both checks before activation, not before every rejection. The prosecution’s SUP-709 theory overreads that clause. The defense misreads concurrency: SUP-708 is activation before sanctions and before manual approval, and either order of the two checks is allowed by M0. The defense calls the alias SoD breach “not a process violation” and, elsewhere, noncompliant.

SUP-704 is inside FD-7 (2026-07-11). The matrix says the directive had expired and that an extension document would reverse the result. Section 5 then classifies SUP-705 as noncompliant for lack of a written extension and, in the same section, calls factual continuation a valid defense. That contradiction undercuts the authority analysis. SUP-711 is not indeterminate: both rows share a source transaction ID, yet the matrix treats that ID as missing. SUP-702 versus SUP-706 is the notification test the record was built to support (stable `notification_id` versus a mutable link written two days later); the answer never makes it. Also dropped: no spend before sanctions on SUP-708, PD-3’s DE and master-data conditions, the nine missing-eligibility cases, and the 47 information requests that never activated early. The system summary then omits SUP-705, SUP-714, and the ineligible auto-approval cluster.

The POWL does not implement the routes it claims. `sanctions`, `quality`, and `activate` are reused across parents, which is invalid. `checks` forces quality before every branch, so auto-approval and emergency activation cannot defer quality. `Request_More_Info` is mandatory and not repeatable, so the conforming SUP-701 trace fails, while a real loop is a partial order and therefore acyclic. `Operator.LOOP` is the PM4Py construct; the claim that loops need reset arcs is false. `SilentTransition(label=...)` does not match the API. Rejection still requires a full quality review, contradicting the abbreviated pre-screen and the proposed scope rule. Reactivation is described as modeled and is absent from the code. The permanent auto and emergency XOR children would accept the very post-expiry behavior judged noncompliant unless a versioned overlay gates them, and that adoption condition is not stated. The overlay table also loses the DE criterion, and several constraints are printed without a usable operator.
