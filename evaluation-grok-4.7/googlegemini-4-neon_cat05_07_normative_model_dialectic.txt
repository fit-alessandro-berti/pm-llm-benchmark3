The review is unusually complete and mostly well-controlled: all seven required headings are present, all 15 cases are classified with severity and confidence, both briefs concede the other side’s real points, and the layered split between POWL, temporal rules, data guards, identity rules, and overlay governance is the right architecture. Case readings of SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-708, SUP-709, SUP-712, SUP-713, and SUP-714 track the record. The guarded sub-POWL design correctly refuses to treat an unguarded XOR as authorization, and `LOOP(τ, Request_More_Info)` is a valid `0..*` encoding under PM4Py’s do/redo order.

It is not nearly flawless.

The burden rule in §1 says a justified exception requires proof that every overlay prerequisite was met. PD-3 requires pre-activation automated master-data validation. SUP-702 has no such event, yet §5 still labels it `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` at medium-high confidence and calls the gap minor. That is an internal contradiction, not a disclosed residual. The same slippage appears in the code: `select_authorized_powl`’s comment says the eligibility snapshot fails closed, but the predicate never checks validation, only risk, country, group, and dates.

“289 appear PD-3-eligible” is repeatedly hardened into “289 eligible” or substantively compliant pilot cases. Appearance of eligibility attributes does not establish sanctions order, validation, quality SLA, or notification for those cases.

The emergency route’s retrospective `Reject_Supplier` does not unwind provisional activation. That is an unsafe control-flow approximation, and §7 does not flag it, despite flagging other POWL limits. Reactivation is named `reactivation_route` in the overlay table and then omitted from the dispatcher, so those cases fall through to `CS-9-STD`. `CS-9-REACT` is dated `2026-09-01` as a proposed amendment even though that date is already past. RMI exists on the standard and prescreen routes only. The PD-3 row invents a “pilot threshold” spend cap the source does not state.

SUP-711 as `COMPLIANT` is a judgment call the text defends, but the logged trace is still a duplicate until deduplicated; `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` fits their own scheme better. SUP-703’s “deliberate” coloring goes beyond “operator selected pilot despite ineligibility.” SUP-715 as `MIXED` is explained, though medium-high severity overstates a missing CoI disclosure that CS-9 does not itself prohibit.

Dialectical structure, traceability, and the main conformance calls are strong. The self-contradictory exception standard, the over-read aggregate, and the unacknowledged emergency-reject gap are what keep it out of the top band.
