The memo reaches a defensible hybrid decision (E), covers every required section, and correctly refuses both national scale and a full rollback. That is not enough to clear a strict bar, because several claims that carry the argument are wrong or overstated.

The fitness/precision explanation inverts conformance. Billing before activation is a move-on-log: the log does it and the model does not allow it. Using that event to show that low precision means the model is overly permissive confuses fitness deviations with precision. The right precision evidence is the permissive combination space and move-on-model behavior, such as expected permit checks that never occur. Generalization (0.70 to 0.56) is ignored.

Aggregate results are treated as clean effects. The pilot mix was heavier in apartments and older buildings, segment and risk attributes are missing before week 3, and complaint cost stops after week 4. None of that is separated from the treatment effect. “Degrades performance for the remaining 66%” is also too strong: median duration still improved for apartments, business, and rural cases; the damage is tails, first-time-right, and complaints. Only old buildings got a worse median.

Cost is mishandled. EUR 23 is the overall visible-cost gap (184 to 161), not a saving “per simple case.” The rollout bar then asks full downstream cost, including complaints, to hit EUR 184, which is the baseline pre-complaint figure, not the baseline total of EUR 198. M4 is a 60-day cost window but is ordered “within the first 2 weeks.” The survey-skip rule is also fused with missing permit checks, which the evidence treats as a separate failure.

Value of information is only partly decision-linked. M7 mostly reconfirms a deviation the memo already pauses, yet it outranks M5, which could overturn the decision to scale coordinator ownership. Lower total cost is said to support scaling R “even with quality trade-offs,” which the constraints do not allow. Business and rural cases are never routed, and the 95% rule-accuracy gate is undefined.

The recommendation is usable. The supporting measurement logic and several numeric attributions are not.
