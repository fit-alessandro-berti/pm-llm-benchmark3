TX-302 is the determinate labeling error. The 09:20 enhanced review is after the only material baseline and before release, so R1 is satisfied without EX-77. The row still says deviations are none and then classifies the case as `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`. That label is for an unmet control saved by an exemption. EX-77 is a second sufficient ground, which the text itself admits could be `COMPLIANT`.

**Formalization.** The state function \(S\), the \((\text{timestamp}, \text{event_index})\) order, R6’s short-circuit (DE defeats R1 even with an unknown amount; PEP activates R2 even with unknown risk), and the separation of effective time, record time, and evidence time are right. Jurisdiction is forward-filled from every event, including `Sanctions_List_Update`.
