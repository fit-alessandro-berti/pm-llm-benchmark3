4.5

The verdict `mainly confounded` and the crude-versus-within-complexity reversal are the right central reading, and the refusal to endorse a blind 50% move is directionally sound. The deliverable is not reliable enough for a committee. Counts, means, and cell definitions are wrong often enough to change mechanisms, and several causal labels are misused.

Section 1 treats night queueing, compliance, and rework as partly genuine Orion effects. Within low complexity, night waits are not higher. Compliance and rework gaps are mostly the complexity mix. Within stratum, Orion’s rework is equal or lower (high 50% vs 50%, medium 1/6 vs 1/4, low 0 vs 1/10). The executive summary therefore reintroduces the crude association the stratification removes. “No direct harmful effect” is later placed in the allowed column; that is not identified.

Material data errors include: Orion day n=1, not 2, and night n=13, not 12; C02 is day, but it is put in the low-night cell; C01 is high load, but it is treated as normal and as “no high-load Orion low cases”; medium compliance is 3/6 and 2/4, not 83% and 75%; Orion compliance-required mean cycle time is about 21.0 h, not 25.4 h, and Nova’s is 27.0 h, not 25.5 h. All high cases require compliance, so a within-high compliance effect is not estimable. Within medium, compliance cases are not slower (Orion about 16.9 vs 17.4 h). The claim that compliance adds ~13 h inside complexity is the same confounding mistake the answer is supposed to catch. Crude wait means are about 12.8 h vs 8.8 h, not 11.4 vs 9.5; review service about 7.0 vs 5.0, not 6.4 vs 5.0. High Orion request-info is 3/6, not 2/6. Several “means” are single case values (31.2, 18.2, 8.5), and one displayed day-shift formula does not equal its stated result.

The graph is not a directed adjacency list, the routing arrow is reversed in the prose (`resource_group` does not cause `routing_rule`), and the explanation exceeds 120 words. Complexity is called a mediator of the group effect even though it causes assignment. `routing_rule` is called a collider without a collider structure. `review_service_h` already includes repeated review, while customer time around Request Info sits in the cycle-time gap; rework is still described as adding 7–8 h.

The hypothesis table has shift, compliance, harm, and selection rows, but not a first-class row for a beneficial Orion effect masked by case mix. Tests often predict from the bad counts. The learning plan does include a small randomization and a compliance-capacity phase, yet the pilot is only low-complexity non-compliance work, with little said about transport to the night, high-complexity cases management would actually move. The 10–20% improvement is an invented forecast. The recommendation again says Orion has longer waits and higher rework.

The Python does not use PM4Py, does not build group DFGs or directly-follows relations, and its printed sample wait and review means are not what that code would compute. Inverse-variance pooling is not a stated common population distribution, and it is unstable at n=2. The script then prints a causal conclusion the task forbade. Section 8 is the cleanest part: five concrete data gaps, correctly centered on selection, the routing rule, reviewer identity, timestamps, and appeal meaning.
