5.5

The case dispositions are directionally usable, but several conclusions that the problem is built to test are wrong, incomplete, or contradicted by the code.

The strongest calls are CHG-501 (Ben under D-QM-01 while Asha is on leave), CHG-502 and CHG-508 (RR1, with aliases merged), CHG-503 (unresolved `svc_qms`), CHG-504 (standing QM, identity still open), CHG-507 (Eva with no delegation), CHG-509 (D-QM-02 scope matches, absence not shown on Saturday), and CHG-510’s invalid subdelegation. Weekend exclusion, PDF-over-late-database-record, and refusal to treat the Clara–Ben pattern as proof are sound. Section 6 covers the requested controls.

Material errors remain in the final findings. D-QM-01 is limited to production-equipment approvals; it does not authorize Retrospective_QM_Review of a cooling-system emergency. CHG-505’s Friday review is correctly late, but Ben was also on sick leave then, and the “arguably in scope” alternative answers the wrong scope question. CHG-510’s SoD cell never states the independent RR1 breach (Clara drafted and approved) and never tests Ben’s 12 August retrospective, which is in time but outside D-QM-01’s scope. CI-44 is assumed from `cooling_system` with no missing-evidence note. CHG-506 is a contested identity, not a collusion pattern. CHG-508’s LOW-risk approval does not need a QM delegation. The 60-day benign story treats a five-day delegation as if it covered that history. Section 7 then counts “3 of 10” authority failures while listing five, and places CHG-504 in the Clara–Ben cluster even though Asha approved it. “Back-dated” misstates a record created late during migration; later sections describe that fact correctly, but the matrix does not.

The code would not reliably reproduce those judgments. It requires principal absence for every delegation, so valid D-IC-03 fails; both `allows_subdelegation` branches return the same “valid” result and the parent prohibition is never applied; LOW-risk approvals become `NO_AUTHORITY`; timezone-aware log times are compared with naive intervals; `svc_qms` is filled back in after being set to missing; handover and working-together graphs are built after `org:resource` is renamed; and the four-eyes results are never printed. Those are executability and logic failures, not style nits.
