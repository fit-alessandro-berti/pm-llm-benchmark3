The duplication counts are correct and are the best part of the answer. Convergence and divergence are defined in the standard way and illustrated with real objects. Position B is defended with phase coverage, an explicit counterargument, and change conditions. The required distortion categories are all present, and sensitivity checks 1–4 use the right weights and object counts.

The repeated timestamp claim is false. `e21` is `2026-02-03 12:30` and `e23` is `2026-02-03 14:00`. No case contains two events with the same timestamp. The only equal timestamps are `e29` and `e30` at `2026-02-04 11:05`, and those events belong to `O2` and `O3` separately, so they never create a within-trace tie. Section 5’s residual risk and section 7’s fifth check are therefore unsupported. That check is also incoherent on its own terms: event-id order and table insertion order both place `e21` before `e23`, so they do not vary the DFG. Under the real timestamps, `O1` is unambiguous: Record Payment → Confirm Delivery → Confirm Delivery, so the same-activity edge in distortion 7 is real and does not depend on tie-breaking.

Distortion 5 misstates `O3`. `O2` is `e19 → e22 → e24 → e25 → e29`. `O3` continues `e25 → e27 → e30`. Writing `e29/e30` drops `e27` and hides `O3`’s second confirmation after `M3`.
