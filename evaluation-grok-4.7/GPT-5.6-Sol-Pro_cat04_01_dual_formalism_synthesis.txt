8.4

The POWL model is the right canonical object and is nearly exact under the stated completion-event abstraction: cover edges match the split prerequisites, the risk XOR and optional supplier XOR are placed correctly, the trial is a do/redo loop `(Execute→Inspect)(Adjust (Execute→Inspect))*`, and release is a real three-way join. The process tree is a genuine conservative under-approximation, and A3 is the right witness for that barrier. Witness classification for A1–A5 and F1–F3 is consistent with those semantics, including F1 as a cross-branch failure and F3 as a broken iteration.

Deductions are for precision and executability, not for the core design. Section 4 never actually accounts for every incomparable pair: `Analyze`∥`Plan`, `Contain`∥`Design`, and `Contain`∥approval are left implicit, even though those are easy to mistake for missing edges. D17 treats “Failed Inspect Trial” as a dependency source after the answer has correctly said pass/fail is not an event. The tree is safe but coarser than “as faithful as possible”; a tighter under-approximation is dismissed by banning duplicate labels rather than shown to be unreasonable. `add_transitive_edges()` and `validate_partial_orders()` sit outside the guaranteed API, the snippets were not run, and a failure there aborts construction even though the cover relation alone is the model. A2’s gloss that approval “overlaps the trial” is loose: `Quality Review` completes after `Inspect Trial`.
