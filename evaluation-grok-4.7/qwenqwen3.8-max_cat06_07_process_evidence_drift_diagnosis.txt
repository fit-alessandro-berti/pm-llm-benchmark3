The diagnosis is right and well timed: April is a schema/pipeline failure with mature, stable breach labels; June is a V2 routing-mix change with stable remote-fix duration and no need to call concept drift; August–September combine supplier performance, V3 risk, and (from September) Partner Z; October’s 0.16 breach rate is censored and not comparable. Variant-mix checks (June ≈0.16, August ≈0.31, September ≈0.34) match the evidence, falsifiers are stated, and the retrain/recalibrate staging plus label-maturity gates are proportionate.

Points come off for definitional blur and a weaker code sketch. June is also filed under population shift, which the case does not support. Post-diagnosis variant is used as if it were a covariate at the prediction prefix without a clear information-set caveat. In the code, event-calendar part-lead medians drop cross-month edges, performance-DFG and temporal features are reduced to counts, and event-month duplicate totals are merged onto case-start rows. May is treated as broadly unsafe to train on, although the unfixed cache is specifically an April backfill gap.
