7.5

The policy is decision-theoretically serious and covers every required section: stage gates, calibration by segment, evidence-based re-entry, spillover in the objective, I-03 refused, I-05 and I-07 separated, three scenarios with auditable arithmetic, and concrete fairness and recalibration triggers. It is not nearly flawless, because several modeling choices change who gets the scarce slots and are not held fixed.

The largest problem is the exposure set. The stated spillover hits six non-intervened diagnosis-stage cases. I-08 is still in Triage. Treating its €100,000 as part of that set, and then prioritizing it partly to “remove” itself from the pool, is not supported. That assumption also flips the marginal test for I-01: private value is about €1.84k, which clears a €1.2k base charge and fails only the €3.2k charge that exists if I-08 is inside the exposed set. The write-up says I-01 is “below the charge (€1.2–3.2k).” That comparison is not true at the low end of the stated range.

The false-alarm term is probability-weighted, `(1−p)×F`. The suggested identity subtracts the full false-alarm burden. For most selected cases the sign is unchanged. For I-08 it is not: at the lower effect bound, `0.05×€100,000 − €450 − €5,000` is negative, so the downside gate that I-08 “passes” depends on the weighting. That sensitivity is not shown, yet I-08 is the conditional fourth slot.

Internal figures also drift. The rule states a 25% Strategic Enterprise effect haircut; I-08 is then scored with a 30% haircut. Section 1 defines the priority score net of spillover; the table reports private scores before spillover. I-09’s “base” effect is 0.20, not the midpoint 0.24, so the base row is not the scenario that was requested. The 5% live-capability holdout and the regression-discontinuity plan are stated without power, SLA risk, or a stop rule.

What keeps the score in the high 7s is the operational core. Diagnosis Complete blocks I-03. Refresh-only I-05 is suppressed and given a watch, while I-07 re-enters on the new log but is routed to a fast non-slot review because of runway and value density. I-06 is held for the imputed queue feature. Public-sector, SME, Strategic Enterprise, and non-native-language uncertainty are handled differently, and the portfolio net is reported under optimistic, base, and adverse spillover rather than by `p_now`.
