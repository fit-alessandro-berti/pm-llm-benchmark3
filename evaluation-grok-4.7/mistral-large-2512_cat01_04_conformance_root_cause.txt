The label-only repair is predicted to reach about 80% fitness and Apr–May-like variants. ## Diagnostic program
The four phases name DFGs, variants, alignments, remapping, model comparison, stratification, and audit. They do not isolate the three repairs that the evidence already separates. Label remapping and technical-event removal are one phase; source-sequence ordering is not a phase and is only a provenance input at the end. Precision is never a decision criterion, so an optional sign-off model can look successful while accepting low-value sign-off. Several method names (`parse_variants`, `map_labels`, `conformance_diagnostics`, `case_attribute_filter`) are not the PM4Py operations needed to perform those isolations.
