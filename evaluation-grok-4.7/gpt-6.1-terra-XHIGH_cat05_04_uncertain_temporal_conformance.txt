8.4

The interval arithmetic, timezone conversions, DST-fold filtering, SLA bands, and zeta sensitivity matrix are largely correct and internally consistent. HUB ±1.5 s, OPS-BER floor plus [−90, +120] s, SENSOR ±10 s, New York/London offsets, the T-404 Minkowski start, the single surviving T-405 fold triple, and the order-coupled T-407/T-409 durations all check out. Boundary calls are right, including T-402 S1 certain versus profile AI only possible, T-408 IC [3297, 3303] versus 3300, T-403/T-407/T-409 still possible at zeta 3, and T-407/T-409 still possible at +2 minutes.

The required PM4Py comparison is not semantically aligned. `conformance_temporal_profile` scores directly-follows pairs only. The supplied profile’s Alert→Containment pair is never adjacent, and inserting `Investigation_Complete` also destroys Investigation_Start→Containment_Complete for T-404. The code prints both outputs but never states that the diagnostics do not cover the same pairs as the interval analysis.

Clock offsets are assumed independent across events on one system, including near-boundary results, without a shared-offset check. That correlation cancels in differences and can move T-405 profile AI from possible violation to pass; T-404 S2 and T-408 IC sit only a few seconds over a limit for the same reason. Confidence is also uneven: some passes that still depend on a moderate or low source are marked high, T-407 AC is called indeterminate without order even though sent-plus-latency alone remains inside the zeta-2 band, and the text refers to a confidence dash the table never uses. The code comment claims a chronological recheck that is not implemented, and `fromisoformat` on space-separated strings is version-sensitive.
