7.5

The classifications are largely right, but the write-up is not reliable as a duration record, and the required code does not run through the comparison it promises.

The interval model is sound: resolution before clock correction, `true = recorded − offset` with offset in `[a, b]`, CEST/EDT/BST conversions, and the 2025-10-26 fold (CEST → 00:xx UTC, CET → 01:xx UTC). The only order-feasible T-405 combination is alert fold 0 with start and containment fold 1, and the resulting durations `[720, 1080]`, `[2040, 2400]`, and `[2940, 3300]` are right. HUB ±1.5 s, OPS-BER `[t−120 s, t+150 s)`, vendor `[t−180 s, t+240 s)`, sensor ±10 s, and mobile record-time ±330 s are applied correctly. T-404’s inferred start `[10:09:58.5, 10:17:01.5]` is right, and the observation that an S1 breach and an S2 breach cannot occur in the same world is right. T-406’s primary occurrence window `[12:03:30, 12:19:30]` matches “entered up to five minutes late” as an occurrence shift. SLA and zeta labels I checked, including the knife-edges (T-402 AI at zeta 2, T-403 AI at zeta 3, T-404 S2, T-408 IC at zeta 2, T-409 daily-NTP AI wholly below the zeta-1 band), do not flip.

The delivered duration intervals are not all the ones those labels were computed from.

- T-409’s primary start is trimmed to `[15:59:58.5, 16:17:00)`. Containment minus that lower end is `2103 s`, not `2581.5 s`. `2581.5` is containment minus the discarded raw lower end `15:52:00`. Section 3 still labels `[1078.5, 2581.5]` as the weak/primary S2 and IC interval. The class stays CP / PV because both numbers sit under 2700 and the zeta-1 failure is the short side (`1078.5 < 1500`). The published interval is still wrong, and it mixes trimmed and untrimmed endpoints inside one scenario.
- T-407’s no-causal receipt upper is `14:20:01.5`, so the alert-to-containment lower bound is `2097 s`, not `2037 s`. Class stays CERTAIN_PASS only because both values fall inside `[1980, 3420]`.
- Section 1’s occurrence formula extends the mobile upper end (`rec_hi + 300 s`). Section 2 and the code do not. A reader who follows the formula gets a different T-406 window from the one that was classified.
- Sensitivity prose calls T-403 and T-406 “close” at +2 minutes, then cites T-404 (`1023`), T-407 (`1023`), and T-409 (`1021.5`). T-403’s upper is `1141.5` and T-406’s is `1171.5`. The matrix itself is right; the discussion misidentifies the knife-edges. “CV at all tolerances up to +0” for T-402 S1 is also wrong: +2 and +5 minutes are only POSSIBLE_VIOLATION.

Section 2 does not use explicit ISO timestamps. Several instants are bare clock times, with the date only in a preamble, and the close events use `06-02` / `06-03`.

The code’s normalization, fold product, chain trim, and band/SLA classifiers match the stated semantics and would actually emit about `2103` for trimmed T-409, so it disagrees with section 3. It is not executable end to end. `itertuples` renames the `class` column to `_6` when the index is included; the comparison reads `r._7` and raises `AttributeError` once PM4Py imports. The author states that this block was never run. Missing Close events are skipped instead of recorded as INDETERMINATE, and the T-404 joint dependence noted in prose is not represented in the duration math.

Sections 6 and 7 are specific and usable: the pass/violation thresholds are the right boundaries, evidence types are separated, and enforcement is tied to certain versus possible overlap rather than to a midpoint. That does not repair the incorrect primary intervals or the broken comparison.
