5.7

The qualitative framing is the right kind of argument: the flag is treated as a policy bundle, natural direct and indirect effects are rejected because of exposure-induced confounding and shared queues, occurrence and waiting are separated in the pathway table, and P2 is a sensible pilot target. That structure does not survive the empirical and estimand errors.

The stage table is not a defensible approximation. Flagged-with-escalation is 6 versus 4, not 5 versus 5; flagged high-load versus normal-load is likewise 6 versus 4. Low complexity is 4 and medium is 8, not 5 and 7. Medium escalation is marked `NA` even though R06 escalates. Unflagged manual-review wait is about 1.9 h, not 2.5 h; flagged manual wait is about 6.5 h, not 6.0 h. Flagged high-load mean cycle time is about 18 h and normal-load about 14 h, so the table reverses the congestion contrast. High-complexity mean cycle time is about 20 h, not 18 h.

Several claims that drive the mechanism story are false on this log. Reject rates are 2/10 in both flag groups, not higher when flagged. Escalation is 60% versus 20%, not 50% versus 20%. `arrival_load` is only weakly related to flag (6/10 versus 5/10). Every high-complexity case is reviewed, flagged or not, so the occurrence pathway is concentrated in medium cases; that heterogeneity is missed. Triage resource is a perfect separator (T1/T2 flagged, T3/T4 unflagged) and is the clearest priority-lane signature; it is never used, and is then proposed as a negative control that should be independent of the flag.

Estimands are mis-typed. Effects on manual-review probability, review wait, and escalation are labeled interventional indirect effects; those are effects on mediators, not indirect effects on cycle time. “Not well-defined” is used for E7 because of unobserved risk, which is an identification problem, not a definition problem. The observed flag is a hard threshold between 0.61 and 0.64, so there is already no overlap, but the local-threshold design is centered on [0.65, 0.75] and lack of overlap is treated as a hypothetical reversal.

The pilot text assigns “standard triage routing,” which drops priority rather than preserving it as P2 requires, and the safety stop looks at adverse outcomes in the control arm. Section 9 later describes the correct contrast, then overstates that this randomization is robust to interference. The explanation under section 3 is under 100 words, and section 9 is under 180.

The Python does not supply the start-timestamp key, does not discover separate flagged and unflagged DFGs, will generally fail on `"-"` without coerced parsing, and the duration arithmetic is written on groupby Series rather than scalars. The graph also points `cycle_time` at `decision_outcome` and calls intake `complexity` a treatment–confounder feedback variable.
