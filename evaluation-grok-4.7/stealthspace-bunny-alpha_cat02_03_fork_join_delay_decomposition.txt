The write-up has the required section structure, a correct \(T=U+K+D+R\) skeleton for eleven cases, correct worked arithmetic for F02, F04, F07, and F11, and a serious limits discussion. The fork-join control results, counterfactual savings, and executable check are not reliable.

**Definitions.** \(r_c\) is introduced as fork release and \(f_c\) as final start, but upstream time, branch queue, and fork–join elapsed time are then written with \(f_c\). As printed, those formulas are final-start differences; the later minutes use fork release, and \(r_c\) is unused. Join-ready is defined as the latest branch completion, and a positive “completion overrun” is also defined for a branch that finishes after that maximum. Under the max, that overrun is identically zero. The F01 story depends on the impossible case: A at 09:00 is described as five minutes after an 08:55 join.

**Case table.** For F01 the latest completion is A at 09:00, so join-ready is 09:00, the critical branch is A, fork–join time is 50, downstream delay is 8, and synchronization is B 5 and C 22. The table gives join 08:55, critical branch B, \(K=45\), \(D=13\), BW 0, CW 17. The five-minute shift still satisfies \(15+45+13+20=93\), so the row adds up while the fork–join versus downstream split is wrong. The other eleven rows match the max rule. The summary then gives A only F11, C the set {F04, F06, F08, F11}, and B “the other eight.” Those lists overlap on F11, omit F01, misclassify F06 (B ends 10:30, C ends 10:00), and contain thirteen labels.

They sum to 13/12, and they match neither the case table (A 1, B 9, C 2 as printed) nor the log (A 2, B 8, C 2: A on F01 and F11; C on F04 and F08). **Counterfactuals.** A 20% service cut is a 10-minute move. It saves 10 minutes on F11 and 5 minutes on F01, and nothing where A is strictly early. The table has F01 at 0, and the text calls the F11 result “10 minutes, not 20.” For a 50% B-queue cut, flow time falls by the gap to the next branch even when B stops being sole critical: F02 saves 10, F05 saves 10, F06 saves 15, F09 saves 12.5, and F10 saves 5. The table sets all of those to 0, while the prose says F02 and F06 become earlier A–B ties. The printed B column sums to 42.5; the reported total is 52.5. A capped B-queue counterfactual on this log saves 82.5 minutes over eight cases. The C diagnostic assigns +5 minutes to all twelve cases. C binds only in F04 (margin 7) and F08 (margin 5); an earlier C leaves every other join unchanged. “Slack at least 5 in every case” is also false on the printed C-wait column, which is 0 for F04 and F08. Holding final completion fixed would make branch acceleration save zero flow time; the text calls that situation unmasked and still reports lower mean flow. Those claims do not coexist.

The final-station recommendation correctly treats removing all of \(D\) as an upper bound and asks for a ready-versus-start measurement. The 120/285 figure is arithmetically correct and uses a window that opens at the first arrival.
