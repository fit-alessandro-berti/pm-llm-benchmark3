The case-level authority analysis is largely careful and often correct, but the answer is not nearly flawless: it overstates several conclusions, misexplains the 60-day pattern, and ships a checking script that crashes before the required outputs.

The strongest reasoning is the event-time role work. Alias normalization of `u_bk`/`b.koch`, refusal to treat `svc_qms` as a person, Asha’s leave not revoking QM authority, Eva’s 9 August approval predating any delegation, D-QM-02’s unproven absence condition on 15 August, D-IC-04 as a prohibited subdelegation, and the production-equipment versus cooling-system scope mismatch are all well judged. CHG-501, 502, 503, 504, 506, 507, 508, 509, and 510 have defensible primary labels. The signed-PDF versus later migration-record distinction is the right answer to the delegation-evidence question, and business-day counting that makes 14 August late for an 11 August emergency is explicit and consistent. RR6 is mostly respected in the paired benign/adverse explanations.

The serious errors are these.

CHG-505 is called a valid IC substitution with moderate-to-high confidence even though D-IC-03 is limited to incident CI-44, the log only says `cooling_system`, and no safety rationale is in the record. Section 2 “accepts credibility at face value” while Section 3 lists that rationale as missing. The 14 August `u_bk` retrospective also falls inside Ben’s certified sick leave; the identity column’s “no alias issue” drops that attribution problem. Late and out of scope are enough to fail RR4, but the initial approval is not shown to be valid.

The 60-day Clara–Ben claim is factually wrong. D-QM-01 lasts five days and only covers production-equipment approvals while Asha is absent. It cannot make “most” of 18 prior HIGH-risk approvals valid, and the executive line that the pairing is “adequately explained” by cell assignment and leave cover infers a benign conclusion the known leave window does not support.

The code design is otherwise good (normalized humans before four-eyes, service account left unattributed, interval and scope checks, two-business-day deadline, networks not labeled as collusion). It is not executable. `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is called with two activities; the PM4Py function takes one activity and then a parameters dict, so this raises before the pandas flags and both network discoveries. The IC scope is also silently widened from CI-44 to every `cooling_system` change, and that approximation is not listed among the manual judgments. Ben’s 14 August sick-leave use is not held for identity review.

Smaller defects: CHG-509 is grouped with identity problems in the conclusion when its core gap is the absence condition; CHG-503’s delegation evidence is equated to CHG-501 although the QMS row existed before CHG-503 and not before CHG-501; and self-approval on LOW-risk cases is lumped into “authority failure” rather than kept as segregation-of-duties failure.
