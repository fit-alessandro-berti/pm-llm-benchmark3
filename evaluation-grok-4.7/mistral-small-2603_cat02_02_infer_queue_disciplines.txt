3.3

The write-up matches the required seven-part structure, but its dispatch reconstructions are systematically wrong and its policy labels are not supported by the choice sets in the data. Confidence of 75–90 is far too high for that evidence.

**Decision sets.** Almost every reconstructed opportunity includes jobs already completed and omits jobs that were waiting. That is a basic failure of the requested method, not a presentation issue. At QA 08:58, A01–A03 are finished; the real wait set is A04–A08, and FIFO, priority, and SPT all select A04, so the episode discriminates nothing. At 09:05 the wait set includes A06–A08, not A01–A03; A05 matches FIFO and contradicts both priority (A08) and SPT (A07). At QB 09:48, B04 and B05 are missing; priority selects B05 and SPT selects B04, while B03 was observed. The row labeled “10:14 (B05)” is the wrong event: B05 starts at 10:00, and at 10:14 B01 resumes while B06 (priority 2) is already waiting. At QC 10:10, C03 and C04 are omitted, which hides the real contrast: SPT selects C02, priority and earliest deadline select C03, and FIFO selects C01. At 10:54, C04 is finished and C06–C07 are missing. At QD 11:21, D01 is ineligible for D-F and D02 has just ended, while D05–D06 are ignored. At 11:35, D01 is finished and D08–D09 are missing. At 12:20, D07–D09 are finished and D05 is effectively the only legal job for D-F, so it is not a revealed preference.

**Diagnoses.** QA is not preemptive priority. A04 does not preempt A01 or jump A02/A03; A08, priority 1, waits through A05–A07. The coherent pattern is non-preemptive FIFO with appointment insertion: A03 starts exactly at 08:20, and A06 starts at 09:20 only because A05 occupies the server through its 09:15 appointment. Citing A08 as support and A04 as the strongest priority evidence reverses the record. The claim that A03’s appointment was not honored is false.

QB has one real priority-1 preemption, B02 over B01, followed by resume behavior that itself violates global priority: B01 resumes at 09:31 over B03 and at 10:14 over B06. B04 then precedes B06, and B07’s appointment is missed. “Preemptive priority, confidence 85” overstates a hybrid. The supposed contradicting episode, B01, is the same preemption used as support, and the recorded reason already says urgent arrival.

QC’s first and later choices contradict deadline-aware priority. C05 was eligible and already infeasible by 10:18 but was passed over for C03 and C04. C06 is chosen at 11:34 over higher-priority, tighter-deadline C07, and C01 is then chosen over C07, causing C07 to miss 12:00. Those episodes fit an SPT-like or batch rule better than priority or earliest deadline. Section 2 says the deadline rule predicts C05 at 10:54; section 4 says it predicts C01. The second claim is false: C05’s deadline is 10:50 and already overdue, and C07’s 12:00 is also earlier than C01’s 13:00.

QD correctly notices skills, but not the within-skill policy. D05 goes to D-F because no other resource is qualified, and it is deferred across the meeting, not preferred despite priority 2. D-M’s informative choice is D04 over waiting priority-1 D08 at 11:25; D08 does not pass any older waiting job. D06 starts exactly at 11:30 while D-X holds the slot. D-F’s idle period from 11:36 to 12:00, with D10 eligible and short enough to finish before the meeting, is never confronted. Calling QD the most identifiable queue is not justified.

**Tests, exceptions, and data plan.** Neither discrimination test uses an additional observable. Both reuse already-known facts, and both get the competing prediction wrong. The exception list names relevant phenomena, but calibration is incorrectly offered as an explanation of A06, QB’s interruption reasons are treated as missing when they are recorded, and scan-delay direction, resume-after-preemption, meeting fit, and estimate noise are not tied to altered choice sets. The collection plan restates generic fields instead of prioritizing the actual ambiguities: the 09:48 choice, C05’s earlier skips, C01 versus C07, and the D-F idle interval.

Observations, hypotheses, and recommendations are not separated. The skeleton shows awareness of the candidate mechanisms; the episode-level reasoning does not sustain the conclusions.
