7.4

The brief is disciplined, case-complete, and genuinely two-sided: authority, expiry, SoD, and post-pilot drift are separated from logging, label, and scope problems, and the judgment does not dissolve into a single compromise. The deductions below are where that quality breaks.

The revised POWL is the main failure. The standard route can only approve and activate. The reject route is only Register → Sanctions → Reject, so a full check cycle that ends in rejection is not in the language at all. That is a regression from M0’s decision after both checks, and it is never declared as an approximation. Mandatory `Master_Data_Validation` makes every historical auto trace, including the flagship justified case SUP-702, non-fitting, with no optional, silent, or logging-bridge treatment. The emergency route forces quality and manual approval after activation, which is stricter than FD-7’s permissive “may,” again without a limitation statement. Reactivation and withdrawal are specified in the architecture and then absent from the executable model.

SUP-711 is mislabeled. The same source transaction id is treated as established, confidence is High, and the reverse test is “show they were two real acts.” That is a determinate compliant execution plus a duplicate row, not `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` under the answer’s own definition, which reserves that label for unresolved performance.

Burden and matrix do not match the classifications. The stated test for `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` requires an immutable rule id, an eligibility snapshot, and proof that preconditions occurred. SUP-702 is still `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` at medium-high confidence while master-data validation is listed as missing and PD-3’s authority was marked disputed. Its “what would reverse” cell mixes confirming evidence with rebutting evidence. SUP-709’s prosecution cell claims a CS-9 breach; CS-9 ties quality to activation and expressly allows an abbreviated pre-screen, so the model over-constraint is the real issue. SUP-710 treats “no reactivation procedure” as enough to avoid the default quality-before-activation rule without saying why the burden falls that way.

The defense never addresses concurrency, which was a required topic. Section 1 calls alias resolution unproved, then SUP-712 is noncompliant at high confidence. Those are smaller faults beside the model and the SUP-711 category error.
