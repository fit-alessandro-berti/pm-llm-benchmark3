State reconstruction in the findings is correct for all eleven releases: blanks are held forward, settlement on TX-305 is not copied back to release, equality at EUR 100,000 triggers R1 on TX-308, the sub-20% increase on TX-306 and the decrease on TX-308 do not invalidate the prior enhanced review, TX-309’s post-review increase does, TX-307’s standard-packet approval does not satisfy R2, TX-304 fails R3, and TX-311 is judged on the post-`event_index` jurisdiction DE. Sections 6 and 7 cover the required failure modes and logging fixes with real case anchors.

The score is held down by defects in the final formalization and code:

- The R1 pseudo-logic requires an enhanced review with no material change before it (`no material_change m with before(m,e)`). That is the reverse of the rule and of the table in the same section, which correctly require the review after the latest material change. The direction error would reject a review that repaired an earlier material change.
- The checker treats every `Update_*` as material. Under that test TX-306 (about 4.5%) and TX-308 (a decrease) become R1 failures, while the findings call both COMPLIANT. R4’s 20% test is computed and then not applied to R1.
- Missing amount cannot trigger R1 in the code, because the trigger requires a non-null amount of at least 100,000. The `REVIEW_REQUIRED` branch is therefore unreachable, and TX-305 is labeled OK. That silently treats an unknown amount as below the threshold, which R6 forbids and which the findings correctly call INDETERMINATE.
- TX-309’s first `Update_Amount` row has one extra trailing field. With current pandas that row raises a parser error, so the script is not reliably executable. `Risk_Reclassify` is also omitted from material changes, and the diagnostics frame does not emit the review, approval, and sanctions timestamps the task asked to track.
- TX-302 is labeled JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION even though the findings admit the enhanced review already satisfies R1, so the exemption is not what makes the case pass. TX-310 is stated as NONCOMPLIANT at medium-high confidence without treating INDETERMINATE as a live outcome, despite contradictory approval evidence and an interested witness.

Temporal narrative and the rule table are strong. The inverted R1 quantifier and a checker that mislabels the sub-20% case, the decrease case, and the missing-amount case are not minor coarseness.
