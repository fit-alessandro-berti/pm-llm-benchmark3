The compliance comparison is misleading. Pooled required-case means (about 26.7h versus 27.0h) are called a negligible group difference, but Orion’s required cases are much more high-complexity than Nova’s. Within complexity, Orion remains faster, so that “negligible” result is residual case mix, not evidence that the group path is zero. Hypothesis 4 then predicts a direct effect of about zero, which conflicts with the within-stratum gaps and with hypothesis 1.
