The write-up follows the required outline and correctly treats machine capacity as the weakest explanation. It is not reliable on the timings and couplings the evidence actually supports.

The delay arithmetic restates the given B1 waits (J1 3 h 55 min from 08:10 to 12:05; J2 3 h 40 min from 08:25 to 12:05) and correctly gets J1’s pack-to-dispatch interval, 13:30 to 19:25 = 5 h 55 min. CAL-3’s recalibration is 16:35–17:20 (45 min). TEST-1’s idle stretch is 16:35–17:25 (50 min), and J6’s ready-to-start wait from 16:05 is 80 min, which also includes J5 occupying the tester from 16:10 to 16:35.
