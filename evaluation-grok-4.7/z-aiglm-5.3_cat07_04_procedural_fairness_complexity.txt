7.2

The assessment meets the required structure, respects the constraints, and reaches a calibrated conclusion: residual procedural burden is clearest for Groups B and C at the language-support and accommodation steps, raw approval gaps are not discrimination, and Group D’s fraud routing stays conditional on an unvalidated risk score. Most cited figures match the tables (15/9/20 pp raw approval gaps, adjusted residuals, 12.8 and 17.4 day raw p90 gaps, 21.6/5.8 ≈ 3.7×, supervisor waits of +0.9 and +0.6 days, the +9.5 pp document-request residual as the largest adjusted routing gap).

The grade is held down by repeated analytical overreach. The claim that `risk_score_band` “correlates strongly with prior-case count (49%)” treats two group-level shares (51% high risk, 49% high prior-case count) as a within-person correlation. That inference is not in the data, and it is reused as a load-bearing point in the path analysis, the mechanisms, and the bottom line. “13% of [Group D] cases include an appeal” confuses the V6 share with the stated 16% appeal rate. “Entirely conditional on the score’s neutrality” overstates a +0.9 day gap that is also adjusted for documentation, channel, household size, prior cases, region, and month. The 1.4 vs 0.8 day identity-verification contrast is a group median, not an in-person-only wait. Parallelizing language support is said to remove “much of” B’s +2.6 day adjusted gap without a decomposition that respects partial exposure or non-additive medians. Raw p90 gaps are set beside adjusted medians and called “more than double the median gaps” without separating adjustment levels. “Untestable with these data” conflicts with the same answer’s proposal to estimate caseworker fixed effects from `org:resource`.

The PM4Py plan covers filtering, variants, DFG and performance DFG, handovers, service/waiting insertion, and log-skeleton checks, but it is not executable as written. `filter_paths_performance` is called with an invented `comparison` argument and the wrong positional shape, and the inserted columns are assumed to be `case:service_time` / `case:waiting_time`. “Two-thirds to four-fifths” also fails to bound D’s absorption of the raw approval gap, which is about 85%.
