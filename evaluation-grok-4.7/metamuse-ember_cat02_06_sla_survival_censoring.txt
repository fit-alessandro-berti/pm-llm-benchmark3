7.4

The primary estimands, censoring-versus-competing-risk distinction, and most headline calculations are coherent and check out. Under transfer-as-competing, the 72-hour resolution CIF is about 30.4%, pending survival about 38.0%, and restricted mean pending time about 50.4 hours. Cohort figures (A 29.2%, B 31.6%), priority figures (P1 25%, P2 43.1%, P3 20%), transfer-censored 31.3%, and transfer-as-resolved-at-transfer 37.9% match a direct Aalen–Johansen recalculation. Case counts, early `AutoCheck` (12/17 in B, 0/15 in A), 27 variants with 22 singletons, leakage classification, and the open-case split at 72 hours are also right. The code retains open cases, encodes three transfer treatments, and avoids invented timestamps.

The sensitivity section states a false result. Direct standardization of the stratum CIFs to the pooled priority mix (8/32, 14/32, 10/32) gives about 31.3% for A and 32.5% for B. The gap shrinks and does not reverse. The reported 31.8% versus 31.0% reversal is therefore not the analysis described. The extra cohort-B transfer does not drive a 72-hour reversal either: S32 is at 96 hours, so each cohort has one pre-horizon transfer. Recoding transfers as censored leaves A at 29.2% and moves B to about 33.2%; treating pre-horizon transfers as resolutions gives about 37.7% versus 37.9%. In both cases B remains higher.

The net Kaplan–Meier figure is also off: censoring every non-resolution yields 1−S(72) ≈ 35.9%, not 36.1%. That 36.1% is repeated in §§1, 3, 6, and 8, and it disagrees with the supplied product-limit code. “Breach” is also used for any internal non-resolution, including withdrawal at 27 hours and at 70 hours, after the text has correctly said legitimate withdrawal or rejection is not automatically an SLA failure. The §5 diagram is a path list rather than an explicit state-transition graph, and “non-compliant” is defined as AutoCheck not being the second activity without labeling that rule as an assumption.

Those defects sit on top of an otherwise careful plan: Aalen–Johansen is an appropriate primary estimator at this sample size, ever-escalated and rework are correctly kept out of the baseline covariate set, and long gaps plus P1-only transfers are treated as possibly informative.
