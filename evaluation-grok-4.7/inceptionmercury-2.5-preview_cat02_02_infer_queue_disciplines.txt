3.5

The write-up keeps the required seven-part skeleton, and two macro labels are defensible: QA as FIFO with an appointment insertion, and QD as skill-constrained. Those labels are not earned by the decision reconstructions. Most eligible sets are wrong, several “supporting” episodes reverse the timestamps, and the required SPT comparison is missing from every decision table. Confidence and identifiability claims are then built on those mistakes.

**Decision sets, the core of the task, fail.** At QA 08:20 the waiting set is A02, A03, and A04 (A01 is completing; A04 arrived at 08:13). The table keeps A01 and drops A04, so it misses that a priority-1, shortest job was passed over for an appointment. At 08:58 A02 is already finished (08:52) and A06–A08 are waiting; the table still lists A02 and omits them. At 09:20 A05 is completing and A08 (priority 1) is waiting; FIFO’s next job is A06, while priority selects A08, not A06. The prose then says A04 started after A05. A04 runs 08:58–09:05 and A05 starts at 09:05.

QB at 09:16 includes B03, who arrives at 09:20. The stated support, “B03 completed before B05 arrived,” is false: B05 arrives at 09:46 and B03 runs 09:48–10:00. That episode contradicts priority; it is not evidence for it. QC at 10:18 includes finished C02 and excludes waiting C01 and C05, so the priority prediction (C03) is wrong; C05 is priority 1. On the 10:54 row, C01 is in the eligible set and is the oldest job, yet FIFO is predicted as C06. QD at 12:16 treats D07 as eligible after D07’s own segment ends at 12:16, so the row is not a choice. D08 is described as available at 11:00; D08 arrives at 11:16.

**Disciplines are mis-applied even where the label is plausible.** Non-preemptive priority still selects the highest-priority waiting job when the server frees. D-M’s choice of D04 over waiting D08 at 11:25 is a priority violation, not evidence of “P1 non-preemption.” The same error appears for B05, who is waiting while B03 is started. QB’s later choices also break the diagnosis: at 09:31 B01 (priority 3) is resumed ahead of B03 (priority 2); at 10:28 B04 is taken ahead of B06 (priority 2). Calling QB non-preemptive priority at confidence 70, and the most identifiable queue, is not supported.

QC is not “SPT with priority as a tiebreaker.” C02 at 10:10 matches SPT and contradicts both FIFO and priority, but at 10:32 C06 (estimate 6) is waiting and C04 (estimate 22) is chosen, and at 10:54 C05 (estimate 40, priority 1, deadline already infeasible) is chosen over C06. That is priority or deadline overriding SPT, which the prompt explicitly allows as a policy switch. The identifiability cell says C06 is priority 1 and invents a resource gap from 11:18–11:40. C05 runs until 11:34 and C06 starts at 11:34. “est222” is an uncaught data error.

Skill columns are largely arbitrary: on QA and QB every job is `general`, yet the table names a single skill winner. On QD, skill does not separate D04, D08, and D09. The informative QD decisions are absent: D-X idle 11:20–11:30 then taking appointment D06 while D10 (priority 1, electrical) waits; D-F taking D03 over D05 at 11:21 and then sitting idle 11:36–12:00 rather than starting D05 or D10; D-X taking D07 ahead of D10 at 11:48. “D08 started later” than D09 is false (11:35 vs 11:43).

**Counterfactuals do not discriminate.** Both tests ask the reader to observe facts already in the log, and they restate the timeline incorrectly. At 09:48 B03 is not “already started”; its segment begins then, with B05 already recorded. At 08:58 FIFO, priority, and SPT can all select A04, so that instant cannot separate them. A useful extra observable would be something not in this extract (remaining-time estimate at B01’s resume, whether B05’s scan was visible, appointment release rules, whether D-X was blocked for D06).

**Exceptions and data plan are thin.** Four exceptions are listed, but they are not ranked (calibration and skills are both “high,” with a “medium” between them). Calibration does not repair a FIFO breach by A04; after A03, A02 then A04 is FIFO around a known outage. Scan delay of up to two minutes, which the prompt flags and which matters at 09:46–09:48 and 10:10–10:11, is never used. Section 6 is a generic field list and never ties a field to a named ambiguous choice; queue-entry time, remaining estimate, and policy version are omitted.

The closing synthesis inherits the bad QB call and explains QC’s ambiguity with the nonexistent 11:18–11:40 gap. Assumptions are labeled, but they are not used to bound the wrong eligible sets. Format compliance is only partial: headings and three times per queue are present; the mandatory SPT prediction is not.
