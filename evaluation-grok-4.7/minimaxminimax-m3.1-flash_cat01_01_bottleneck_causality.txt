The assessment is strong on causal discipline and almost fully compliant with the required structure. The crude-versus-stratified reversal is correctly identified, complexity standardization is computed on the pooled 12/30–10/30–8/30 mix (17.14 versus 19.65 h), and that reversal is not treated as identification. Total, direct, and routing-policy estimands are separated; queueing is distinguished from service; positivity is exact; selection, rework, compliance, and the group-as-bundle problem are handled carefully. The hypotheses have real predicted observations, including a justified refusal to use compliance requirement as a front door for group. The phased plan is safer than a 50% transfer, and the word counts and table schemas fit.

Two concrete defects keep it short of near-flawless. First, the within-night standardized contrast is not internally consistent: Nova uses night-specific cell means, while Orion’s 21.39 h is reproducible only by inserting the overall Low mean 7.5, which includes the Day case C02, rather than the Night-only value 7.2. The Orion side is also not shown arithmetically, so the comparison is not fully auditable. The direction survives the correction, but the reported contrast does not. Second, the diagnostic code selects the `cycle_time_h` series and then names aggregations `mean_wait_h="mean"` and `mean_service_h="mean"`. Those columns are therefore repeated cycle-time means, not wait and service. The required cycle-time contrast still runs, but the extra output is wrong. `timestamp_key=None` is also a realistic PM4Py execution risk.

Smaller issues: “collider-biased” is slightly loose for a controlled direct effect, and “front-door-like” is loose for a compliance-policy change. The verdict is otherwise well scoped, and the numerical error does not overturn the conclusion.
