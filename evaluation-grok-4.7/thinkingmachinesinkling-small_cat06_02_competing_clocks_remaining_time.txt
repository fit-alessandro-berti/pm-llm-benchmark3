6.2

The forecast’s structure is sound: parallel branches are joined by a maximum, weekend closures are respected, incubation lands on Sunday 12:00–20:00, Saturday QA cannot release this batch, anomaly paths overlap rather than being added as independent means, and the temperature-excursion update is a defensible primary trigger. The central story (Monday afternoon through Tuesday midday, with a multi-day investigation tail) is in the right region.

The numerical layer is not reliable. Remaining hours and clock times contradict each other: Tuesday 08:00–12:00 is about 88–92 h, not 76–84 h; 120 h is Wednesday 23 September ~15:30, not Thursday 16:00; 120–165 h cannot be Friday 25 September–Monday 28 September (those dates are roughly 153–240 h). The same mismatch appears in the scenario tails and in the P80/P90 summary.

Queue discipline is mishandled. FIFO is the more common regime and four samples are ahead, so a Friday ~16:30 start is an optimistic case, not the center. The claimed “Monday 08:00 start adds ~14 h” is also wrong: Friday progress is only about 1–2 working hours, and under FIFO the batch still waits behind resumed and queued work rather than entering service at 08:00. The ledger’s “0.35/h over ~65 h ≈ 23 arrivals” applies the rate outside QA opening hours; actual pre-Monday opening time is about 8.5 h, or roughly three arrivals. Section 3 then uses the better figure without retracting the bad one.

On the answer’s own smooth timings, chemistry ends Monday ~09:00 and microbiology Monday ~08:30, so chemistry is the slower branch, yet the scenario table calls microbiology the dominant clock. “Chemistry cannot finish before Monday 08:00” and “48 h minimum” are both false: short service from the earlier analyst can finish Friday, and the incubation floor is 46 h (Sunday 12:00 is correct; 48 h is not). Scenario C mixes no weekend authorization, which only pushes readout to Monday morning, with contamination confirmation, which pushes it to Tuesday. Section 3 also never gives the required stage-by-stage calendar decomposition for each scenario.
