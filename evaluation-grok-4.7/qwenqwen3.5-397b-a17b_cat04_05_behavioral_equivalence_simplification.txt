The verdicts are right: A and B are not equivalent under P1 or P3, and they match under the stated P2 abstraction. The four counterexamples are real. A cannot fire two `Check Revision` events, B cannot complete two analysis iterations with only one check, and the loop reading (body once, then optional `Rework Case` plus body again) is applied correctly. T1–T4 all sit inside the P3 bound.

The rest of the write-up does not hold that standard.

P1 is defined too loosely. Equal check and analysis counts, plus an unspecified “ordering constraint,” is weaker than “each analysis has its own check before the next rework or close,” and it drops the requirement that approval count and placement stay visible. The observation is also indexed by model in a way that does not define equality of abstracted languages. Under that stricter view, approval placement already separates the models on a single iteration: in A, `Approve Revision` happens only after both `Analyze Revision` and `Check Revision`; in B, the one check runs beside the whole loop, so `Open Case, Analyze Revision, Approve Revision, Check Revision, Close Case` is in B only. Section 2 calls approval logic identical. It is not.

Section 5 would not perform the comparison it claims. Quoted `'tau'` is a visible label in the usual process-tree parser, not a silent leaf. `pm4py.fire_transition` is not part of the given API. A `Marking` is not hashable, so the visited set crashes. Pruning on `(marking, visible length, analyze count)` drops the other trace that reached that marking, so `Open Case, Check Revision, Analyze Revision, Close Case` is lost after `Open Case, Analyze Revision, Check Revision, …`. The search is therefore not exhaustive, and the text says that it is.

Section 7 reports exact figures that are wrong. Both trees have 12 nodes (5 operators and 7 leaves), not 13 and 11. Depth is 5 for A and 6 for B. Each tree has 6 visible leaves and 1 silent leaf, not 5 visible types. No label is duplicated in either tree; runtime repetition is not a duplicate-label count. Process-tree nets do not use reset arcs. `simplicity_petri_net` is never computed. A is not shown to have the larger reachability graph; with approval visible, B has the larger one-iteration interleaving set.

The simplified tree is a coherent P1 subset: one check after each analysis, optional approval, rework only as the loop separator. The regression and negative traces fit that claim, and the loss of `Check`/`Analyze` order is disclosed. The simplicity comparison still uses the wrong node count for A and does not compare structure with B. Footprints and EMD are discussed at the right level of caution, but the footprint snippet is not executable and misses differences those footprints would actually show, such as `Approve Revision` directly followed by `Check Revision`.
