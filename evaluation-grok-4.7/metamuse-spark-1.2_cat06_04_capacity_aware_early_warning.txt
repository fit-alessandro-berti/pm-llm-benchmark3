The policy is directionally serious and covers every required section, but it is not internally consistent, and several of the numbers that are supposed to justify the allocation do not survive an audit. Under a strict standard, those failures sit at the center of the task.

The decision rule correctly refuses a probability threshold, separates stage eligibility from observational risk, treats the supplied effect ranges as uncertain, and points calibration adjustments the right way: upward concern for SME, downward for Strategic Enterprise, unchanged for Public Sector, and wider uncertainty for Non-Native-Language Support. Excluding I-03 because the swarm is past its effective stage is the right call. Distinguishing I-05’s unchanged refresh from I-07’s new log is also the right distinction. I-06 is properly held for verification, and I-08 is properly kept out despite the €100,000 breach value. Spillover, segment bias, label contamination, and a holdout are all recognized.

The allocation then breaks the rule just stated. Robust benefit at the low end of absolute risk reduction is €3,550 for I-04, €2,350 for I-09, €1,500 for I-06, €1,200 for I-05, €950 for I-02, and €920 for I-01. The rule says to defer any case whose robust benefit is below about €1,500–€2,500 of marginal spillover harm. I-01 fails that hurdle, yet it is labeled Tier 1 and given a slot. On the answer’s own base assumptions, I-01’s marginal contribution is negative: midpoint benefit €2,385, swarm plus false-alarm cost €700, and one slot of spillover €2,160, for about −€475. Dropping I-01 raises the stated base net. I-02 is slightly higher on the same robust score, is already in Diagnosis Started, and has 3 hours rather than 7, but it is deferred. Its base marginal net is slightly positive. The tier labels do not order the scores, and I-08 is simultaneously “wait” and “do not intervene.”

I-09 is treated as if its published lower bound were a reliable conservative estimate. The same answer says this segment needs a widened interval and human review; neither adjustment enters the score before the slot is committed. I-06, which has a higher robust score if the imputed queue feature is confirmed, is not sequenced ahead of the marginal slot.

The impact table does not meet its own audit standard. False-alarm burdens of €250 + €2,000 + €800 equal €3,050, but the table repeatedly uses €3,250 and a €4,600 cash cost. The optimistic breach count is given as 0.81 from rounded effects 0.31 + 0.24 + 0.27, which sum to 0.82, while the euro components sum to €22,263 and the headline says €22,400. The adverse benefit range, €5,200–€8,860, does not match either all-lower-bound gross benefit (€1,620 + €6,000 + €3,600 = €11,220) or the zero-I-09 case the text actually calculates (€7,620). The claim that a fourth slot would move the base net to about €5,000 is not implied by the preceding arithmetic. Adverse congestion is also stacked on top of the high-end 0.03 probability spillover as an additional 0.02–0.04 breaches, although the delay and the probability shift are descriptions of the same queue externality.

Two further claims are unsupported. I-05 is described as already evaluated and deliberately not swarmed; the record only says that repeated alerts added no new event. I-07 would not outrank I-02 if it had six hours, because €500 remains below €950 on the answer’s own score.

The monitoring plan names the right classes of metric and gives concrete triggers, but the effect design is weaker than it appears. A comparison of swarmed cases with untreated diagnosis-stage cases attributes both the treatment effect and the spillover harm to the swarm, so it overstates absolute risk reduction. The post-diagnosis comparison is not a natural experiment. Forty swarmed cases per stage will not pin down heterogeneous effects tightly enough to authorize the threshold changes the plan contemplates. Full false-alarm burden is also subtracted as if it were incurred on every swarm, which over-penalizes high-burden cases relative to an expected-cost reading.

The answer therefore does the qualitative triage well and then spends one scarce slot, and parts of the justification, in conflict with its own arithmetic.
