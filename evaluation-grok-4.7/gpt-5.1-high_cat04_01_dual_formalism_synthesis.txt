The POWL model is the right canonical artifact and is largely faithful: registration is the unique source, containment and analysis are unordered, verification waits only on containment, design, the risk XOR, and optional consultation wait only on analysis, the trial waits on verification and design but not on approvals or consultation, release joins the trial, the chosen route, and the supplier block, and the loop is `(Execute, Inspect)(Adjust (Execute, Inspect))*`. The process-tree code is a real conservative under-approximation, parent links are set, and the eight witnesses have the right verdicts, including T3/T5 as tree losses and F1 as a cross-branch failure at the first `Execute Trial`.

The write-up then misstates that model. The tree is not only “approvals and supplier before the trial.” Its analysis branch is the sequence Design → risk route → supplier, so it also forbids design overlapping or following approvals, and forbids supplier overlapping design or approvals. Section 3 never lists the design/approval loss, yet it was required to identify every inexact requirement. Section 6 then claims the opposite: that supplier/approval concurrency “is preserved within the branch,” and that the tree is “essentially exact regarding approvals.” Section 4.3 incorrectly says the risk route depends on plan and design; the code correctly does not. Section 3.3’s loop remark is inconsistent with the author’s own abstraction: an iteration followed by `Adjust Parameters` is a failure, not a passing inspection. The dependency table and the “every unordered pair” discussion also never state design ∥ approvals or plan ∥ approvals, which is the main non-block pattern left implicit. `SilentTransition("skip_consult")` is an unforced API risk versus a label-less silent node. The self-critique notices the trial overlap and misses these tighter errors.
