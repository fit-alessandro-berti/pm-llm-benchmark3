The required seven sections are present, and most of the queue is handled coherently, but the recommended plan breaks its own rules and one hard compliance constraint.

The central failure is C015. Eva’s Security score is 0, which the matrix defines as not allowed. Security Review may be done by skill 3, or by skill 1–2 only under Gita or Hugo. Supervision does not authorize skill 0, and Jonas is also 0, so the case note does not override the rule. C003 is a second deviation. David is overtime-risk high, the case is normal priority with 18 hours left, and Celine is a Diagnosis specialist with low overtime risk and free capacity. The fairness rule allows that overload only when the SLA is urgent and no safer alternative exists. Continuity is a real counter-consideration because the case already has two reassignments, but the answer never shows that the “unless” test is met. Its own pseudocode would discard David here.

C001 is the weakest urgent decision. Reassignment has a stated median handover wait of 2.7 hours against 3 hours of SLA time, while Celine is skill 1 and therefore eligible for specialist supervision. The answer neither tests whether supervision is infeasible nor compares that option with a likely SLA breach. Keeping a weak owner is also costly, so the dilemma needed an explicit choice; listing the handover as an accepted trade-off does not resolve it.

The training claim does not hold. Nine cases are normal or low priority, so 20% requires two valid priority-matched assignments. C009 for Amal is valid. C015 is not. The algorithm also never enforces the quota; training is only an undefined weighted bonus. Several weights are unnamed numbers, and the greedy pass has no repair for the global quota, batching, or residual hours. Eva is given four cases, including a 7-hour high-complexity diagnosis, on 5.0 hours remaining.

The comparison covers every requested dimension and stays mostly qualitative, which is appropriate, but “best SLA protection” and “meets 20%” are stronger than the assignments support. The worker text is clear and non-punitive, yet it explains the non-compliant security assignment as intentional development.
