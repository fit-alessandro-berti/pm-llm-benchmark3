7.5

The final policy is decision-theoretically serious and hits the required structure: stage eligibility, calibration shifts, a non-threshold score, spillover, a full I-01–I-10 table, three auditable scenarios, distinct handling of I-05 and I-07, and concrete fairness and monitoring triggers. I-03 is correctly refused, and the chosen set (I-09, I-02, I-04) plus one reserved slot is a defensible robust allocation.

It is not nearly flawless. The published I-02 lower score, about −€1.3k, does not follow the stated \(S_{low}\) formula: that formula uses the high spillover penalty (€2,700) and a €500 Strategic penalty only if \(p_{cal}<0.40\). I-02’s adjusted probability is about 0.58, and with the high spillover term the score is about −€1.8k without the penalty or about −€2.3k with it. I-04’s lower score does use the high spillover term, so the two headline scores are internally inconsistent. The urgency exception is defined as time to SLA under 3 hours, yet I-02 is exactly 3.0 hours and is still labeled Tier 1 by that exception. The I-07 override (“reserve only before 13:15”) cannot be executed on the stated schedule, which occupies both specialists until 13:30 and places the reserve at 13:30–14:15. Optimistic effects are the top of each range rather than the specified upper half, and adverse spillover is stretched to 0.04 without a mapping from the 15–30 minute delay. Net-impact signs also rest on an assumed €15k value for the six untreated cases; that assumption is disclosed, but there is no sensitivity showing when the base or adverse net would change sign.
