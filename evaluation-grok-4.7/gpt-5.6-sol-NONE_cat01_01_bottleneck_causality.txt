The core causal reading is right: the crude Orion penalty (+6.72 h; 21.96 vs 15.24) reverses inside every complexity stratum (about −2.14, −2.21, and −3.43 h), Orion’s mix is much heavier on high complexity, night, and compliance, and that reversal is correctly treated as evidence against the 50% reassignment rather than as identification. Total, direct, and mediated effects are distinguished; queue time is not treated as pure service; completion is treated as selection; the hypothesis set includes harm, masked benefit, shift/load, compliance, rework, and selection, each with a predicted result; and the plan prefers instrumentation, a capacity change, and a small capped randomized pilot over moving half the book. Primary stratum counts and the supplied within-complexity means check out. The standardization, overlap grid, variant table, and PM4Py fallback in the code match the requested diagnostics.

The auditable arithmetic does not. Day versus night is not 15.54 h (n=13) versus 20.54 h (n=17). The case rows sum to about 13.98 h and 21.74 h, so the “+5 hours” night contrast is wrong by roughly 2.8 h. Compliance-required versus not is not 25.21 h versus 11.73 h (gap 13.48). Those means are about 26.78 h (13 cases, sum 348.2) and 11.95 h (17 cases, sum 203.1). The within-medium compliance contrast (17.76 vs 18.34) is exact, which makes the crude mis-sum more conspicuous. Low-Nova day is 9.825 h, not 9.80; that one is negligible. “Far more” rework overstates 4/14 versus 3/16, and “especially high” is only true in hours, not relative terms.

The graph reverses time: the 30-day outcome cannot cause `case_completed`, because appeals are observed only after closure. `shift -> arrival_load` is also a dubious direction, and reviewer skill is drawn only as a cause of group even though certification is part of the Orion bundle. The low-Nova night-versus-day contrast used against a shift effect does not hold load fixed: both night cases are High, while day mixes Normal and High. Every Night row in the file is High, so shift and load are completely separated nowhere. `warnings.warn` will suppress repeated small-cell warnings from the same line under Python’s default filter, so the required cell-level warnings are not reliably printed.

Confidence 78 is high for cells of size 2 and for a verdict that is still only a reading of a confounded crude gap. The decision, the reversal, and the learning plan are sound; the secondary calculations and one temporal arrow are not.
