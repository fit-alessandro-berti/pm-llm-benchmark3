The assessment is unusually rigorous: issue-specific authority, time-of-act application, separation of posting lag from the underlying act, refusal to treat later approval as cure, and differentiated outcomes across the eight cases. The main traps are handled correctly, including A3 on a new supplier below EUR 100,000 (P-502), A3 on an approved supplier above EUR 100,000 (P-503), ON-44 running only backward from the recorded P-501 time, the P-505 acknowledgment versus ERP clock, the P-507 PDF versus the 14:30 upload, and the invalid verbal extension in P-506. The hierarchy, rule catalogue, alternatives, and remediation track that method.

The score is held down by classification-relevant soft spots, not by missing sections.

P-507 is the largest one. B4 is not met on an eight-hour need, so C-scope is what avoids unjustified commit-before-PO and unapproved-supplier findings. The facts never name a sterilization line. Calling coverage “clear,” listing C1/C2 as satisfied, and omitting line identity from missing evidence is looser than the same answer’s treatment of P-503’s “critical” label. If that nexus fails, the primary result moves from INDETERMINATE to NONCOMPLIANT.

P-502’s calendar date is not in the record, but “D had not yet been sent” is stated as controlling. The A3 result survives either date; the unflagged inference does not. That row also treats a “new” supplier as an unregistered-supplier permission.

Smaller defects accumulate. P-501’s deviations cell includes a conditional Plant-substitution problem the justification then withdraws, and “no substantive breach” blurs the chosen JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION label. P-503 affirmatively lacks Plant approval, yet B2’s total omission is not itemized separately from A2. The later, distinct Finance event in P-505 is not weighed as evidence against the theory that the 17:50 authorization already was Finance approval. The formal Legal rule is stricter than the email’s stated weight in the hierarchy.

Those are contained defects in an otherwise coherent, evidence-sensitive analysis. They are enough to keep it short of nearly flawless.
