The OCEL arithmetic and the flattening mechanics are largely exact, and the lead-object decision is internally consistent. Order projections really are 42 rows over 31 events (11 extra rows from nine shared events). Item, package, delivery, invoice, and payment projections are 24/16, 12/10, 6/6, 7/7, and 4/4. The dispatch-to-confirm means, 10 h 13 min 20 s, 8 h 53 min 45 s, and 10 h 32 min 30 s, match equal weighting of D1–D3, P1–P4, and I11–I41. V2’s 35 min, 20 h 20 min, and 20 h 55 min intervals are right. So are the order multiplicities on e15, e16, and e17 (1, 3, 2), the four order-level Issue Invoice→Record Payment edges versus three invoice edges, O3’s e22→e24 and e25→e27, the 4/4/5 support split, and the +15 inherited item rows (24→39). Convergence and divergence are applied in the standard direction, copied events are treated as both participation and count inflation, and direct E2O is kept separate from qualified propagation. Decision C follows from D2 covering O1–O3 while V2 covers only O2–O3. The required distortion types are all present and tied to identifiers.

The concrete defects are smaller than the correct body of the answer, but they are real.

The flattening audit can abort the script. `pm4py.ocel_flattening` has long returned an `EventLog`, whose length is the number of cases. The assertion compares that length with `case_event_rows` (4 against 42 for orders, 6 against 24 for items). The outline says the named discovery functions must exist; it does not say flattening returns one row per case-event. Placed before `discover_ocdfg`, a failed assert stops the object-centric half. The separate `edge_support` construction itself is sound.

The propagation check is narrower than the transformation it defines. Inheriting e18–e22, e25, and e26 along `bills` then `contains` puts invoice events inside every item’s dispatch-to-confirm gap: e18 and e21 split I11 and I12, not only e19 and e22 for I21 and I31; e19, e22, and e25 split I32; e20 and e26 split I41. Citing only I21 and I31 makes a systematic break look local to V2. The +15 count is still correct if those paths are deduplicated.

A few formulations are looser than the evidence. Convergence, divergence, and suitability are not on one scale: “High,” “Present,” “None observed,” and “No observed opportunity” sit side by side. The order convergence cell names five shared events while the duplication cell depends on nine. “Equivalent-execution clustering” is then equated with connected-component grouping. One component is a sound reason not to compare execution clusters here, and skipping `cluster_equivalent_ocel` matches that snapshot, but those are different operations. “Customer” timelines and “the full payment” add a stakeholder and a settlement reading the log does not contain. “Handoff” is a broad gloss on divergence next to the accurate rework point.

None of that overturns the matrix, the nine distortions, or position C. The answer does not treat the longest traces as the true case notion, and the counting-unit reconciliation for e15–e17 is the right way to hold flattened and object-centric results together.
