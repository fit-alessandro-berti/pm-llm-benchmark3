The POWL model is the strongest part of the answer and is nearly language-exact: registration is the unique source, archive is the unique sink, containment and analysis are unordered after registration, planning waits only on containment, design and the approval and supplier blocks wait only on analysis, the trial loop waits on both design and planning, approvals and supplier consultation stay unordered relative to the trial, and release is the real join. The loop shape `(Execute Trial → Inspect Trial)` with `Adjust Parameters` as the redo child correctly keeps adjustment strictly between cycles. The witness set has the required coverage, and T6–T8 identify real earliest violations.

The process tree does not meet “as faithful as possible.” A still-simple permissive over-approximation can keep `Contain Material → Plan Verification` and `Analyze Root Cause` before design, the risk route, and supplier consultation, while leaving only the trial synchronization over-approximated. The delivered tree instead drops every intra-stream precedence at once. Section 1.6 treats full serialization as the alternative, which is a false dichotomy; section 8 later admits the tighter tree, so the submitted model is knowingly looser than necessary.

Section 6 contradicts the tree that was actually built. `Consult Supplier` sits inside the parallel block sequenced before `Release Material`, so the tree cannot accept supplier consultation after release. The same row says that it can. The release row then calls that same join an overconstraint and an “exact-superset,” which are opposite claims. Transitively, containment, analysis, planning, and design already have to precede release in the intended language, so that join is not an extra exclusion. Section 3.3 also muddles a pattern the tree does represent: approval activity may interleave with the trial precisely because those subtrees are parallel siblings.

Smaller faults accumulate. “Requirement 41” does not exist. The edge inventory omits D12 even though `SUP → RM` is present. The concurrency rationale misses incomparable pairs such as containment versus design, approvals, and supplier consultation. T4’s reason says supervisor review overlaps the trial, but in that trace the review occurs after the second inspection. The validation plan is concrete and appropriately non-absolute, but it inherits these semantic slips rather than testing them.
