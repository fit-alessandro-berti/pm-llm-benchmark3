The submission covers all eight required sections and builds a mostly coherent object inventory, but it is not semantically reliable, and the analysis does not match the log it constructs.

The April 7 timestamps are wrong. With the base at 2026-04-06 08:00 UTC, `days=1, hours=9/10` yields 17:00, 18:00, 18:30, and 18:35, not 09:00, 10:00, 10:30, and 10:35. Order is preserved, but the stated clock times are not. Calling M2 `completes_settlement` also goes beyond the narrative, which treats both payments as partial and never gives an invoice total.

Section 1 contradicts the code. It says logistics and financial events relate directly to orders and items, and it lists O2O qualifiers (`packs`, `carries`, `bills`) as the E2O vocabulary. In the relations table, dispatch, delivery confirmation, payment, and pack-P2 do not link to the orders or items those sentences claim. The real E2O set is different and leans on the vague qualifier `involved`. `added_to` is used for both the order and the item.

The pipeline would not perform the required analysis. Flattening is `pm4py.oce.flattening.OCFlattening(...)`, not `pm4py.ocel_flattening`. DFG and log-skeleton discovery are called on the OCEL with invented `ocel_types` arguments, not on the flattened logs. `get_activity_counts`, `get_object_type_counts`, and `pm4py.discovery` / `pm4py.oce` are not the specified API. Exceptions are swallowed. The commented plot is a fabricated DFG helper, not a view of the OC-DFG or the object-centric Petri net. Referential checks themselves are mostly sound, and O2O coverage of contains, packs, carries, bills, and the two payments is correct.

The predicted views disagree with this model. Order and package DFGs include Dispatch and Confirm even though those events are not tied to orders or packages. Both payment activities are `Record Payment`, so the invoice DFG is a self-loop, not Partial Payment → Complete Payment. Flattening on Payment produces two length-1 traces, not one shared trace. e7 is copied once into each order trace, not twice into each. e11 is convergence, not divergence; pack-P1, the main cross-order event, is not called out. OC-DFG is described as an instance graph, the OTG is reduced to a false chain that runs finance through delivery, and the Petri-net “merged place” for O100 and O200 misunderstands type-level places. Section 7 misstates ambiguous allocation and claims e16 and e17 share a timestamp. Section 8’s first check, “exactly 17 relation rows,” is false for this code (34 rows).
