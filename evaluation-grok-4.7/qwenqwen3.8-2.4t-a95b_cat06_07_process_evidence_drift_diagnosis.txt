The diagnosis is the right one and is well triangulated: April is a schema/pipeline failure, June is a routing-mix change with stable within-variant breach rates, August–September combine supplier-driven process degradation with V3 mix and Partner Z, and October’s 0.16 breach rate is not comparable because labels are only 55% mature. The six required sections, dual-hypothesis tests, training-month split (mature labels vs unsafe features), alert suppression so label delay does not trigger retraining, and the failure-mode mitigations all match the task. The mix-adjusted checks (June ≈0.16, August ≈0.31, September ≈0.34) are arithmetically correct and appropriately hedged.

It is not nearly flawless. Variant shares are treated as covariate evidence even though the score is taken immediately after diagnosis, so much of that mix is post-prediction process drift. Concept drift is still labeled “likely” through October, where variant outcomes are explicitly too immature to support that claim. Mix-adjusted rates multiply all-case shares by matured-subset breach rates without stating the denominator mismatch. In the code, the extra new-source duplicate clause is a no-op (it is already implied by the exact-duplicate mask), and `discover_performance_dfg` plus `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` are called but reduced to edge/row counts rather than auditable performance summaries.
