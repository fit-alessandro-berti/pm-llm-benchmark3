6.5

The final design covers every required section and respects the main constraints: calendars and queues are explicit, demand is non-stationary, cross-case effects are included, objections stay latent, service and waiting are not silently collapsed, and no unvalidated optimum is claimed. It is not nearly flawless.

The non-stationarity treatment contradicts itself. Section 3.8 randomly shifts the seasonal calendar across replications and then describes the ensemble as stationary, which smears the March–May and September–November peaks and misaligns D4, council weeks, and Monday staffing. Section 5 then seeds from a “long-run equilibrium,” which this process does not have, and treats a 90-day warm-up as covering a seasonal cycle.

Parallelism is represented badly. The tree’s `+` block can put one case in planning, fire, and environmental review at once, but case state has a single `current_activity`. Architect arrivals are Hawkes-like in the narrative and “not triggered” in the assumptions; the skeleton further multiplies an aggregate rate by an architect id that the arrival process has not yet drawn. Monday’s dedicated portal clerk is noted and then not turned into the obvious capacity drop against the 2.2× Monday surge. D2 depends on prior violations, which are absent from the log and from the data-gap assumptions. The “<0.5%” emergency share and D1’s “~50%” loop reduction are invented. Trade-off directions in the experiment table are blank, so the matrix does not actually communicate trade-offs, and most factors have only one untested level.

Validation and decision rules are weaker than the metric list. Fixed-timestamp replay cannot test simulated waits. A KS p>0.05 rule on 31,200 cases will reject trivial differences. Public-notice’s statutory 14 days is ranked as if it were a resource queue. Improvement thresholds in section 7 ignore the confidence intervals from section 5.

The skeleton is modular and uses `pandas` plus `pm4py.format_dataframe`, but it is not convention-faithful: `pm4py.get_variants_count`, `pm4py.discover_tree_inductive`, and `pm4py.organize_logs` are not PM4Py’s APIs (`discover_process_tree_inductive` is). After `convert_to_event_log`, `event_log['case:concept:name'].nunique()` crashes, and `TOP_20_ARCHITECTS` / `TOP20_BURST_MULTIPLIER` are undefined.
