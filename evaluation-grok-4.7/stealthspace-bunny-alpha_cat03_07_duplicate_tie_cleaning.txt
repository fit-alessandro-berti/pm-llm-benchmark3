Provenance and the failure-mode list cover the required ground.

Section 3 breaks its own rules. PSP `source_seq` puts e12 before e13 before e14, yet the “admissible” orders `e11, e14, e12, e13, e15` and `e12, e14, e13, e15, e11` both place e14 before e13, and the first also places e14 before e12. Section 2 is sloppy where it needed to be precise. The e06/e07 canonical-id cell says “e03–e15,” which is not a canonical id. Several confidence cells are not confidences. The implementation would not produce the datasets it describes. `verified_merge` subtracts the dict keys, so it drops e03 and e11 and keeps e04 and e16, while `ids` contains only the absorbed id. Nulling every differing field would blank `source`, `source_event_key`, and `payload_hash` on a real e03/e04 merge, after which `validate` requires those fields to be non-null. `linearize` then rewrites every timestamp, including next-day e17, to a one-microsecond grid from the first event; `synthetic_step_us` records steps of 1000 µs. The decision tree and audit design are usable. The formal alternatives, sensitivity figures, and executable block are not reliable enough to carry the rest.
