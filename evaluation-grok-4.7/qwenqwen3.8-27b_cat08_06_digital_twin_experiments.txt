The design covers all eight required sections and respects the main constraints: non-stationary demand, calendars, service versus waiting, latent objections, and no claim of optimality. It is not nearly flawless.

The case state never tracks parallel-review completion or a latent objection flag, even though decision drafting and cross-case objections depend on both. Section 1 includes appeals inside the boundary; section 3 then leaves appeals inside or outside that boundary.

The arrival model applies seasonal and weekday multipliers, whose average is above 1, on top of an already-average daily rate, so volume is inflated. Weekend rates are invented and unstated; the excerpt itself has a Saturday submission. Bursts are extra Poisson draws, so they are not tied to the stated 31% share of the top architects.

S1 restates the observed 1.7× season and 2.2× Monday pattern, so it is not a stress beyond baseline, and it disagrees with the code’s extra 1.2 factor. SLA targets are the historical p90s, so compliance is essentially the same fact as the tail metric. The robustness rule allows an intervention to be up to 15% worse under stress and still pass. “Discard the first annual cycle” conflicts with a 12-month minimum run. Fairness “≤ 20%” has no clear denominator. Utilization bands are absolute targets that a historically accurate baseline could fail. Decision rules never require the confidence intervals from section 5.

Validation blurs trace-driven replay with a fully generative model and proposes i.i.d. tests for dependent, non-stationary cases.

The skeleton is modular, but the routing placeholder never schedules fire, environmental, or public-notice work and never sets legal review, while `required_reviews()` is unused. `BundleManager` looks for interventions on the case, which arrivals never set. `part_time_fraction` is unused. PM4Py handling is not convention-faithful: `format_dataframe` is called with `activity` and `timestamp` rather than `activity_key` and `timestamp_key`; `pm4py.discovery.dfg.get_dfg` is not the real API; the DFG is treated as an adjacency dict rather than an edge counter; and replacing `X(` with `x(` contradicts PM4Py’s own uppercase `X` process-tree syntax, which the supplied string already uses.
