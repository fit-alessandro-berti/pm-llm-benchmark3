5.5

The write-up is organized, assumption-aware, and often numerically careful, but the required object of the analysis is the eligible set at each decision. Those sets are wrong in several load-bearing places, and the diagnoses inherit the errors.

**Queue QB is not reliably reconstructed.** At 09:31 the only other waiting job is B03 (arrived 09:20). B04 arrives at 09:35 and B05 at 09:46, so neither is eligible. Strict priority and SPT therefore both select B03, not B05 and B04. The 09:48 row inverts the log: B01’s segment ends and B03 starts (09:48–10:00); B03 is also missing from that eligible set. Calling 10:14 a second stop reverses the lifecycle: the stops are 09:16 and 09:48, and 10:14 is a resume. The 10:56 row chooses B06, but B06 was chosen at 10:36; at 10:56 only B07 remains and B07 starts. The prose even says B07 starts at 10:56. Protected-resume is already contradicted by the actual 09:48 switch to B03 and by starting B05 rather than B01 at 10:00. Confidence 80, and the synthesis claim of a clear repeated resume pattern, are too high. “29 minutes displace B01” also does not follow from the stated 41 minutes of suspension (15 during B02 plus 26 during B03 and B05). B04 ahead of B06 is FIFO among unstarted jobs, so it is not specific evidence of an SPT override.

**Queue QC’s SPT backbone depends on a missed arrival.** C06 arrives at 10:25 with estimate 6, so at 10:32 SPT selects C06, not C04 (22). C07 arrives at 10:40 and is eligible at 10:54. The interpretation then contradicts the decision table: C02 overtakes C01, so it is not a FIFO choice, and at 10:18 C05 is already waiting, so strict priority selects C05, not C03. The C01/C07 counterfactual is reversed. C07 then C01 finishes near 11:58 and 12:34, and both meet 12:00 and 13:00. C01 then C07 is what makes C07 34 minutes late. Slack figures of 44 and 2 minutes, and the C05 tardiness arithmetic, are right; the dominance claim is not. That also weakens the weighted-lateness story in section 5.

**QA’s decision table is mostly sound; the diagnosis labels are not.** After the A03 appointment insertion, A01–A02–A04–A05–A06–A07–A08 is arrival order. A04 does not overtake anyone, so it is not a priority intervention, and A04/A08 waiting is evidence for FIFO rather than the strongest contradiction of an appointment-aware FIFO rule. A06’s logged arrival is 08:29, so under the stated scan convention A06 is already waiting at 08:32; that does not change the 08:32 predictions.

**QD is the best structural analysis and still has eligibility and mechanism errors.** D-X has mechanical skill, but pure mechanical jobs are never placed in its feasible set, so the 11:20–11:30 idle set is too small. At 11:00 D04 has not arrived. At 11:21, D05’s 30 minutes fit before the 12:00 meeting (finish about 11:51), while choosing D03 on D-F consumes that window. D-X is already free and can take electrical work, so “selecting D03 protects scarce D05” reverses the capacity effect. D06 over D10 and D07 over D10 are also what local FIFO predicts; the tables show that and the support column still treats them as appointment and scarcity evidence. D08 and D10 lose whenever they compete, so they do not support local P1 exceptions. D04 before D08 remains a clean rejection of global priority and SPT. The 11:36 D-F test in section 4 is the one well-posed discrimination test: D10 fits before the meeting and D05 does not.

**Section 4’s QB test is invalid** because it compares B01 with B05 at 09:31. The data item that would discriminate is still the right kind of item; the next-job predictions are not. Section 6 is the strongest part: it is prioritized and tied to concrete clocks, resources, appointment tolerances, resume locks, and QC’s projected-miss state. Section 5 correctly treats missing reasons as missing evidence and separates scan error from the long QA bypass and the QD skill constraint. It still carries the false C01/C07 trade-off.

QD is a fair choice for most identifiable at the feasibility layer, and QC for least identifiable. The QB ranking is not, because the resume pattern used to place it second was read off the wrong jobs.
