6.5

The design correctly refuses to code rejected, withdrawn, transferred, or open cases as non-default, accounts for all 48,000 applications, and keeps an as-of prefix, a temporal split, and an explicit leakage table. Those are the right scientific commitments. The write-up is not coherent enough to carry them through.

The primary estimand is unstable. Section 1 defines a six-class distribution that includes both `NonDefault90` and administrative `Censored/Open`. Section 3 then treats non-default at 90 days as ordinary right censoring and says the operational score should be an Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence. Those are different estimands. A classifier that spends probability on “censored” does not estimate 90-day default risk, and the answer never fixes which object is primary.

Prediction time conflicts with the features. `t_pred` is the earliest of the third event, day 14, and the instant before activation or a terminal event. On the stated path, event 3 is the first Credit Review, so document loops, rework, and most timing features described in Sections 4 and 5 are not in the prefix. The 14-day prefix is described as a separate object and then cut off by the same `min()`.

The validation window does not match the maturity rule. With extraction on 15 February 2026, activation plus 90 days plus a 35-day feed lag is mature only for activations on or before about 13 October 2025. The primary test period starts on 15 October 2025, so under that rule it contains essentially no binary-evaluable 90-day labels. The July–September backtest is usable; the designated post-migration test is not. The text notes that many labels may be immature and does not move the window.

The skeleton contradicts the prose. Section 2 allows maturity if `label_available_at` has arrived; the code requires that timestamp and the full 125-day lag. The feature table is then merged with `category`, which encodes default, non-default, rejection, withdrawal, transfer, and open status, plus `label_matured`. The leakage check only searches a forbidden-column list, so it reports a clean audit while the label is in the matrix. Activity renames and `DefaultRecorded` are invented strings, and the survival handoff is a two-line comment rather than an event-time and censoring specification.
