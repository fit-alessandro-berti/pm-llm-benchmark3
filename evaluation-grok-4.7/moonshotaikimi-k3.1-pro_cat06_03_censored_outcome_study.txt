8.8

The design is statistically serious and avoids the main failure modes: it does not train on the 21,600 labeled rows alone, does not code rejected, withdrawn, transferred, or open cases as non-default, separates activation selection from ordinary censoring, and keeps an as-of view of labels, prefixes, and policy era. The nine required sections are present, the 48,000-case reconciliation is exact, and the maturity cutoffs (activation by 17 November 2025 for a completed 90-day horizon, and by 13 October 2025 for horizon plus 35 days) are arithmetically correct.

The primary estimand is coherent. \(R^{(e)}_{90,90}\) is an observed-policy compound pathway probability, reported with state probabilities and a separate activated-account risk \(Q\), plus an explicitly stronger counterfactual alternative. Competing outcomes, delayed feeds, administrative censoring, transfer linkage, and permanently missing partner feeds are distinguished, and the testable versus untestable assumptions are stated correctly. The leakage table, temporal split, and decision-use limits match that target. PM4Py use is real and appropriately limited: `format_dataframe`, `get_prefixes_from_log`, training-prefix `discover_dfg`, and `get_variants`, with no invented survival API.

The deductions are for remaining precision and implementation gaps. The skeleton builds labels once at the final cutoff and only then splits on start time; fold-specific `label_available_at` gating is commented, not implemented, so a verbatim run would train on labels unavailable at the historical fit date. The post-migration cohort is specified in the validation design but never materialized as a split. The code is also longer than the requested approximate 80–160 lines, and prefix construction depends on `_seq` surviving `format_dataframe` and `get_prefixes_from_log`. An empty activity crosswalk leaves renamed terminals and rework counts silently wrong. Cohort assignment can mark a pre-horizon \(Y_{90}=0\) as a usable non-default even though `usable_binary_label` does not. Finally, the claim that rejected or withdrawn cases must never be coded as zero is slightly too absolute for the compound event: they are observed non-events for source-platform \(R\), while still not being \(Y_{90}=0\). The multi-state factorization avoids misuse, but the wording overstates the prohibition.
