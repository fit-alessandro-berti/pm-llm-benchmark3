7.4

The case-level reconstruction is largely accurate and methodologically sound: missing, open, and blank timestamps are distinguished; missing starts are bounded from resource, queue-entry, and completion constraints; open service is treated as elapsed time; and the central handoffs for P05, P07, P11, and P15 are feasible. The checked waiting, service, sojourn, subperiod counts, WIP snapshots, busy-time totals, and utilization ranges are internally consistent, and the S1-early / S2-late bottleneck reading is supported by arrivals, completions, waits, and the C2 outage.

The score is held down by several concrete gaps. Little’s Law is required and is asserted in A9, but no interval, formula application, or numerical check appears. Section 2 also leaves contradictory candidate windows for P05 (“[10:45, 12:15]” and “[10:45, 10:15]”) before the correct [09:45, 10:15] bound. Queue length is not separated from station WIP, and arrival/departure rates are mostly left as counts. In the code, only the central imputation is computed: censored waiting lower bounds become missing values, the three uncertainty scenarios are not calculated, and `insert_case_service_waiting_time` is applied to one arrival-to-completion interval, so its service/waiting split is not meaningful even though it is correctly labeled case-level.
