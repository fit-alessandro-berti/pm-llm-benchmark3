7.4

The design is complete and generally competent: all eight sections are present, cases are not treated as independent, calendars and non-stationary demand are first-class, objections stay a duration proxy, service is not silently collapsed into waiting, PM4Py is kept out of the engine, and the write-up refuses an optimality claim. It is still materially short of a tight simulation design.

The legal intervention is mis-specified. D5 is a pre-check, but the matrix starts the existing post-draft Legal check after Planning, in parallel with Fire, Environment, notice, and inspection. A check of a decision that does not yet exist cannot start then; the design needs a distinct early activity plus a residual post-draft check, with an explicit rework arc. D3’s “20–40% travel reduction per empirical” treats an unimplemented policy effect as measured. D1’s “calibrated 40–60%” reduction is not calibrated to anything in the log. The “~35–45% eligible” figure for D2 is likewise unsupported.

Warm-up is inconsistent with the process being simulated. Complex cases have median 140 days and p90 260 days, so a 180-day discard does not clear initial conditions, and there is no rule for cases that cross the cut (left truncation, right censoring, or conditional inclusion). Welch stabilization to within 5% assumes a steady state the same section correctly says does not exist under seasonal load. A full annual cycle, not half a year, is the minimum initialization window, and measurement windows need to be aligned to whole years so March–May and September–November are not over-weighted by accident. S4 is not a stress: baseline and D10 already include seasonality, Monday bursts, and calendars, so S4 repeats D10. S1’s “2.5× all year or peak 3×” is two scenarios written as one. Synergy is claimed for D10, but the matrix is one-factor-at-a-time plus a single bundle, which cannot identify D1×D2×D3×D4 interactions. The 60–85% utilization band fights the 1.7× seasonal swing: staffing that stays under 95% at the peak will often sit under 60% off-peak, so the rule can reject the policies the stresses are meant to favor.

Process-mining grounding has gaps. `discover_performance_dfg` on a single timestamp is sojourn, not service, and the skeleton never builds service or waiting columns even as an explicitly conflated inter-event time. “Completeness rework” and “Inspection rework” are tree labels, not activities in the excerpt; alignments against that tree will look unfit unless those labels are mapped to repeats. Appeal and legal rework are given probabilities without a control-flow attachment. Council-week frequency, which removes most of a 2 FTE pool, is never parameterized. “2 FTE equivalent, Tuesday–Thursday only” is left ambiguous between two people on three days and two full-time-equivalent units compressed into three days. The excerpt itself shows Saturday/Sunday clerk work and a weekend fire review, which contradicts the HR calendars, and that conflict is never reconciled. Weekend portal arrivals (P002 is a Saturday submission) are a direct mechanism for Monday queues and are not in the arrival model. Same-planner continuity (planning review and decision drafting share `planner_3` / `planner_1`) is visible and unmodeled. No-shows are cited as material and then dropped.

Uncertainty treatment is directionally right (CRN, mixtures, sensitivity, holdout) but sloppy at the edges. A lognormal-plus-Pareto mixture can have infinite mean or variance; t-intervals on replication means are then invalid, and the text never constrains the tail index. “Frozen stochastic seeds” are not trace replay: historical arrivals and, where observed, historical service times have to be injected, otherwise the comparison is just another random run. With 31,200 cases, KS/AD rejection is almost automatic and is not tied to a practical equivalence margin. Global `get_case_arrival_average` is computed without a warning that it is the wrong rate under a non-homogeneous Poisson process.

The skeleton matches the requested PM4Py surface (`format_dataframe`, `parse_process_tree`, variants, DFG, performance DFG, case-arrival average, no `pm4py.read_csv`) and the module split is sound. Those are placeholders, not an estimable model, and they inherit the D5, label-mapping, and service/wait gaps above.
