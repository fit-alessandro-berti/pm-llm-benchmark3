7.5

The diagnoses for G1–G8 are coherent, numerically careful, and properly separate physical batching, logging batches, coarsening, shared accounts, and capacity loss. Bias directions, the G6 waiting/service split, the 4-open-window vs 3-analyst constraint, negative waits, and the `recorded_at` lag gradient are all sound, and assumptions are labeled.

Deductions are for real analytical errors, not polish. The tolerance story treats adjacency gap 0 and single-timestamp gap chaining as the same detector: the prose says G4/G8 batch at every tolerance and that 15 min merges every cluster, while the code’s own `chain_share` (start-to-start / end-to-end) does not batch G4 at 1 min (gaps of 4) or G8 until the gap threshold reaches 7. That also overclaims unrun PM4Py behavior, and the code drops batch type labels, so it never actually compares PM4Py categories. The G1 robustness claim is wrong: ±60 s censoring does not make four serial 45 s tasks compatible with a shared 09:00 start and 09:03 end; independent minute buckets still force overlap. “Up to 3” analysts is also used as exactly 3.
