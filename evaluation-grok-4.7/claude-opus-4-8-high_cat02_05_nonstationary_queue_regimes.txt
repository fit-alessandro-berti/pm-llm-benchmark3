5.5

The required eight-part structure is present, outage days are separated from recovery, median service is not used as mean flow time, and several local calculations (baseline arrival rate near 100/day, hourly totals, Fano near 2.9 and 9.3, implied \(W \approx L/\lambda\)) are in the right direction. Those merits do not offset multiple checkable errors in the quantities the argument depends on.

The pooled-balance claim is false. Over 28 days (the write-up says 27), arrivals sum to 2519 and completions to 2409, so net flow is about −110, not ≈0. Weekends cannot pull the pooled arrival median “far below” the weekday level: 20 of 28 days are weekdays, so that median stays near 103–104. The mean is the statistic near 90. The staffing Simpson story is also reversed: the 7.0 FTE days are exactly the \(C>A\) days, so pooled staff does not line up with a lower completion-to-arrival ratio.

Arithmetic and ranges slip in the same way. Unmet work on 2026-04-15 is \(105-58=47\), not 57, and the text later uses 47. Baseline weekday arrivals are 92–107, not 96–107. High-complex weekend completions are 39–51, not about 40–47. “Nine straight weekdays” of \(C<A\) is seven weekdays, or nine calendar days. Outage-recovery throughput is about 13–15% above arrivals; the intervention days are about 4–6%, not a 10–20% band. The service-median path is not a step from 26 to 34 at 04-20; it was already 29–33 through the outage and recovery.

Service medians and p90s are defined as active handling time excluding wait, so the outage increase is not identified as congestion. On the hourly vectors, 08:00–09:59 is 17/101 ≈ 17% on 2026-04-07, not the day’s `peak_08_10_pct` of 31, and 11/115 ≈ 10% on 2026-04-21 against a stated 47. The 04-21 spikes are the 10:00 and 13:00 bins themselves; calling them secondary and prescribing a morning 08:00–10:00 fast-lane does not follow from that vector. The Fano factors also mix a diurnal rate with extra-Poisson dispersion.

Little’s Law is applied too confidently to baseline weekdays. \(C \approx A\) says the backlog level is stable, not that opening backlog is small, which the audit itself says is unknown. With median service near 25 minutes and 6 FTE, nominal utilization is well under 1, while the implied sojourn is about 144 minutes; that gap is left unresolved while the verdict is still “applicable.” The rising-WIP ratio is then called a lower bound without a derivation. The code does not reproduce the stated segmentation: 04-18 and 04-19 fall into `high_complex`, the change-point comment says 1.5× while the test is 1.25, and that test mainly flags 04-16 rather than the mix shift. The complex-share coding-change caveat is also left outside the regime interpretation.
