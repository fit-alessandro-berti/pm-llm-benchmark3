The answer is a structured, process-aware forecast, but several control-flow and calibration errors are material.

C6-B’s alternative state is invalid. The prefix already contains Customer Evidence Requested and Evidence Received, so the case cannot be S1. The same write-up admits that Fast Close no longer fits, while still labeling Close — No Safety Issue and a further evidence request as process-enabled. Both become enabled only after Evidence Review Completed.

C6-E breaks activity order. A third Customer Evidence Requested cannot be the next logged event, or currently enabled, until the second review completes. Technical Investigation Completed is also the wrong third candidate: six complaints on one lot make Supplier Analysis the clearer Rule 5 obligation, and that analysis may already be running. Completeness 0.72 and “closure is blocked” do not contradict Evidence Review Completed being next.

C6-A overstates Rule 5. One complaint tied to two devices, plus three unconfirmed similar complaints, does not establish a mandatory Supplier Analysis. The primary state and the cross-case summary assert that obligation, while the unknowns withdraw it. The 16:00 authority batch is also irrelevant to evidence-review timing.

C6-D lists the source-system attachment as both a completed obligation and an open or suspected evidence cycle. “Opened on the worklist” is evidence of progress, not of delay. A chronological backfill would surface Customer Evidence Requested before Evidence Received. The S3-or-S5 alternative joins two states that conflict with a logged Technical Investigation and an assessment opened at 12:40.

Across C6-C, C6-D, and C6-E, queue duration and invisible starts are treated as if they change which event is next. The log never records starts, and a slow sole obligation can still be the next completion. That pushes Regulatory Assessment Completed to midpoint 0.50 and 0.40 even when competitors are weak, leaving about a third of the probability unnamed. C6-C’s Supplier Analysis Completed is marked enabled although no common component is established and no opening is known.

The A-versus-B split, the normative override of the direct Technical Investigation to disposition path, the migration caveat on C6-D, and the DFG limitations are sound. Those strengths do not repair the state, ordering, enablement, and probability-identity errors above.
