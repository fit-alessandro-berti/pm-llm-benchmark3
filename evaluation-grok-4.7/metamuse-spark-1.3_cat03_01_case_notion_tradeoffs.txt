6.5

The framework is strong: convergence versus divergence is defined correctly, copied events are treated as both evidence and duplication, direct E2O is kept separate from O2O propagation, and position B is argued with a real counterargument and explicit switch conditions. Most lifecycles and the order-row total (31 events to 42 rows) check out. The score is held down by repeated counting errors and several DFG claims that the traces do not support.

**Counts that fail.** Item flattening is 16 distinct events to 24 rows, not “13 to 24.” Six adds, four packs, three dispatches, and three confirms all touch items. Pack Package in the order log is 6 rows (e11, e12, e13×2, e14×2), not 5; the parenthesis even flags `e14x2` and then leaves the wrong total. e16 relates to 9 objects (O1–O3, I12, I21, I31, P2, P3, D2), not 8. The sensitivity band “total objects 4–8” misses both e16 (9) and e19 (3). Issue Invoice rises only from 3 events to 4 order rows (about 1.33×), so “1.5–3×” overstates that activity; no activity as a whole is 3×, only individual events such as e16 and e24.

**DFG claims that do not hold.** In every order trace, invoice or payment events sit between dispatch and confirm. The order DFG has Dispatch→Dispatch and Dispatch→Issue Invoice, and no Dispatch→Confirm edge, so distortion 4’s inflated Dispatch→Confirm support is not there. The same interleaving kills the Record Payment self-loop in the order log: O2 and O3 run e22 → e24 → e25, not e22 → e25. That self-loop exists only on V2. “Each of O2 and O3 was invoiced and paid twice” is half wrong: each trace contains one Issue Invoice (e19) and two Record Payments. Add Item→Pack Package has support 4, one per order, so calling that edge inflated is unsupported. Rating item convergence “Medium” on a stated 13→24 expansion (+85%) while rating order “High” on +35% is internally inconsistent; the corrected item ratio is still about +50%.

**Causal and temporal overreach.** P4 is packed at e14 (10:20), before D3 is dispatched at e17 (11:15). The 22h45 D3 interval does not contain that consolidation. e25 can be tied only to O2/O3; it does not “block” e31, which is O4 and follows M4 at e26. “Blocks” is used as fact where the log only shows order. Propagation would not “add little”: create, add, pack, invoice, and close events are far from fully linked, and pushing O2O through `contains` / `packs` / `carries` would materially lengthen item and delivery traces.

**Code.** The outline names the required routines and correctly expects a Pack→Pack residue after deduplication. The closing assert does not check that. It compares a DFG edge-weight sum with a made-up `ocdfg['event_frequency']` field; those quantities differ even with no convergence. The prose also runs past the 1,800–2,800 word limit.

What remains sound, and keeps this above a failing analysis, is the case inventory for orders, packages, deliveries, invoices, and payments, the split-versus-convergence distinction (O1’s e11→e12 survives deduplication; e16 does not), and the decision not to let trace length pick the lead object.
