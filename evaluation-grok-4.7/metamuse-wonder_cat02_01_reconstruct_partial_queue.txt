6.2

The write-up is conceptually strong and correctly structured, but it is not numerically reliable. Several required station metrics are impossible or inconsistent with the same answer’s own whole-window totals, and the Python does not reproduce the case bounds it claims.

What holds up is the framing. Missing starts, open activities, and blank waits are separated; station sojourn is held fixed when only the start is unknown; open cases stay in WIP and elapsed busy time; C2’s 15-minute outage is correctly treated as too small to explain 45–85 minute waits; and Little’s Law is correctly described as a censored accounting identity rather than evidence of steady state. Fully observed case arithmetic is mostly right, as are the whole-window busy ranges: S1 [535, 605] of 720 and S2 [515, 570] of 705. S2’s truncated area of 1340 minutes is right. The time-varying bottleneck story (S1 early, joint saturation, S2 dominant by the end) is the defensible reading.

The station table does not survive a capacity check. With a half-open convention, reported-time busy minutes are about S1 [165, 200], [190, 210], and [180, 195] across the three two-hour blocks, summing to the correct [535, 605]. The answer instead gives 210–245 of 240, 225–240, and 210–240. The first upper bound exceeds available resource-minutes, and the three lower bounds already sum to 645, above the answer’s own whole-window upper bound of 605. S2’s period ranges have the same defect: stated uppers of 190, 235, and 240 against feasible maxima near 160, 200, and 210, with 235 greater than the 225 available minutes after the outage. S1 WIP in 10:00–12:00 is not “roughly 5–6”; system WIP stays at 7–8 for that entire block. “Seven” unfinished S2 cases is six (P15–P20). P13’s total sojourn is 210 minutes, not 230. The S1 Little’s area is 2085 minutes (time-average WIP 5.79), not 2065 and 5.74. “Rising by arrival order” is also too strong: S1 and S2 waits trend up but are not monotonic.

The code would not catch these errors. `resource_bounds` treats a case’s own end as a previous end (`end <= end`), so every MISSING start collapses to zero service. Printed bounds and busy ranges would contradict Sections 3 and 4. The outage adjustment does not implement the stated no-span rule. The script never builds the three imputation scenarios or a queue/WIP trajectory, and the PM4Py calls are not shown as metric tables.
