7.5

The answer meets the required structure and constraints, and its mechanism separation is mostly faithful to the tables. Early complexity, late speed under queue pressure, fatigue as a non-exclusive factor, no punishment from raw late error rates, and no removal of quality checks are all handled correctly. The intervention review and dashboard are complete and usable.

It is not nearly flawless. The integrated explanation misstates the experience table: later low-WIP error rates are flat for seniors, mid-level, and night staff, and lower for juniors (5.1% to 4.4%), not “modestly higher for other groups.” The mixed model includes `shift_id` as both a fixed effect and a random intercept, and it stacks highly collinear time-in-shift terms without a real identification strategy. Experience is cut at invented 12- and 36-month thresholds that erase the night-staff group. Several PM4Py calls are wrong rather than merely version-sensitive: the distribution and dotted-chart functions do not take those column arguments, `filter_paths_performance` is given three-activity paths, and `filter_case_performance(..., 0, 10)` is inconsistent with the minute scale used elsewhere.
