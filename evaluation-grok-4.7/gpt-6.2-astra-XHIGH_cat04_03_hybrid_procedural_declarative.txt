8.5

The hybrid design is coherent and unusually careful. Legal versus Operations is kept apart, the accepting net is the right backbone, DECLARE directions are mostly right, the temporal arithmetic matches `zeta = 2.0`, and the T1–T8 layer split is substantively well judged. It is not nearly flawless.

The largest defect is `directly_follows: set()`. The text says that this prescribes no adjacency and tells the reader to pass that dictionary to `pm4py.conformance_log_skeleton`. In the default checker the set is an allow-list: every consecutive pair in the projected trace is a deviation. T1–T7 would then fail the skeleton, so the “pass” column is not reliable under the API the answer itself names. The other skeleton constraints are defensible, and the stated orientation (each first component of `always_before` needs an earlier second component) matches PM4Py, but that does not repair the empty allow-list. The same section already guards the `activ_freq` footgun with `skeleton_view` and then misses the twin footgun.

Smaller problems accumulate. Completion rules (`response` from activation to payment, count equivalence, `Close Onboarding` in `{1}`) are prefix-hostile, and the deployment order still runs them without saying how open cases should be treated. The net makes Capture, screening, and the pre-classification bank check non-repeatable and makes rejection unavailable once Enhanced Due Diligence or Manager Approval has consumed `classified`; neither limit is discussed. R8’s “should not” is hardened to confidence `1.0` with no remark. The R9 check correctly does all pairs and discloses that post-activation re-verification is included, but not the false-fail that creates. On T6 under Operations, the resource cell `not evaluated` flattens a mixed result: R9 is decidable and passes, the 10-hour opinion meets the hard deadline, and only joint authorization is missing. The explanation carries that; the cell does not.

What holds up under replay: the net’s token accounting on both close paths; clarification as a side place that is idle at Activate and Reject; R5 kept out of unconditional precedence so ordinary cases are not blocked; T4 failing only on Cora; T5 failing in the net and `altresponse` while the skeleton cannot see the request; T7’s 34 h gap at about 3.67 SD while the 2 h payment stays inside `[0, 144]`; T8 failing at Reject while each `{0,1}` count still holds; T6 not auto-passing under Operations. Those are the right calls. The empty directly-follows set and the unstated API caveat are why this stays well below a near-flawless score.
