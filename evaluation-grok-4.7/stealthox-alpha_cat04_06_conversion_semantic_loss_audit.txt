6.5

The document has the required structure, a correct primary pipeline, a sound N-poset argument for why a process tree should fail, and a strong treatment of silent skips, loop scope, joins, layout-versus-semantics, and residual uncertainty. Those strengths do not offset several concrete errors.

The semantic baseline is not fully reliable. There are nine labeled activities, not seven; that count is wrong in INV-10 and again in the preservation discussion. INV-06 says every Correct Defect is immediately followed by Configure Trial, but optional Legal Review is concurrent with the whole verification loop, so Legal Review can sit between Correct Defect and the next Configure Trial. That invariant contradicts INV-07.

Section 4 reports invariants as `validated preserved` and states that witness suites and footprints pass. The conversions and checks are only proposed, so those cells are expectations written as results. `expected preserved` was the available term.

The section 5 explorer is not executable as specified. `fire` copies the marking into a plain dict and drops zero-token places; `pm4py.get_enabled_transitions` indexes input places directly, so the next state raises `KeyError`. An inconclusive negative witness (`bad is None`) is printed as PASS and is not quarantined; an inconclusive positive witness is also left out of `QUARANTINE`. At `MAX_VISIBLE`, further silent steps are abandoned, so a complete trace that still needs a trailing tau to reach the final marking is dropped exactly on the boundary the text claims to cover (four iterations without Legal Review), and that cutoff is not recorded as truncation. `convert_to_reachability_graph` is then read with `number_of_nodes` / `number_of_edges`, which a PM4Py transition system does not provide; identical caught errors compare equal and look like agreement. Any exception from `convert_to_process_tree` is labeled `FAILED-AS-EXPECTED`, including an API or runtime failure that is not the N-poset result.

Section 6 states that the BPMN artifact contains one AND-split into the legal branch and the verification branch. A faithful graph also needs the Register split of Assess Risk from Prepare Evidence, and the loop must wait for both, while Legal Review waits only for Assess Risk.
