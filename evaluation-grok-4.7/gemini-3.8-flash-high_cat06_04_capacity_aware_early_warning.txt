The case-level midpoint arithmetic is mostly right. The policy is still not deployable as written, and the portfolio impact table does not audit.

The decision rule does not determine the allocation. Section 1 defines prioritization as net portfolio expected value, with a per-slot spillover of about €900–€2,700. On that rule, I-04’s midpoint direct value is about €8,950 and I-08’s about €4,550, both ahead of I-02, while I-07’s €800 direct value is about −€1,000 after the same €1,800 midpoint externality. Urgency and a fairness override can justify a negative-static-value rescue, but that term is absent from the formula, so two analysts applying Section 1 would not reproduce Section 2. The 8-hour triage deferral is likewise unused: I-01 at 7.0 h is also told to wait.

The reserve is enforced with invented inputs. \(€35{,}000 \times 0.40 = €14{,}000\) is presented as a hurdle the live book fails to clear. Neither the regulatory value nor the arrival probability is in the evidence. The untreated-queue value of €15,000 is also unsupported, and the sign of the base-case result depends on it. Optimistic “upper half” benefits are also a ±0.02 band around the chosen points, not the upper half of the supplied ranges. False-alarm burden is charged in full in every scenario, including the one in which the swarms work.

Several later rules contradict the earlier ones. Section 1 says a minimax widening of I-09’s effect; Section 5 ranks under-represented cases by an upper confidence bound. Neither adjustment is used. The monitoring plan names the right families of metrics, then weakens them. The effect trigger \(\Delta p < C_{\text{swarm}}/V\) ignores false-alarm cost and spillover, so it would miss the breakeven that actually governs this book. Slot exhaustion is treated as an exogenous instrument, although exhaustion is produced by the same demand shocks that raise breach risk. The X-learner is equated with a doubly robust estimator. Section 6 would suspend the program if adverse spillover dominates benefit, which is the adverse row already computed, and today’s three-case book is still dispatched.

Calibration direction, the I-06 imputation hold, the I-03 hard stop, and the I-05/I-07 contrast are sound. The score is limited by an objective the allocation does not follow, a required impact table whose ranges do not multiply out, unsupported parameters that decide both the reserve and the sign of base-case value, and later safeguards that contradict the cases actually selected.
