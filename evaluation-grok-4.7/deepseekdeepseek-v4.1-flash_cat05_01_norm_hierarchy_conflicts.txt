6.5

The final assessment is structured, factually careful on thresholds and timing, and generally disciplined about evidence. It correctly keeps ECD-2 from applying before 09:00 in P-501, refuses to treat the P-505 ERP timestamp as conclusive against the call, acknowledgment, and fast clock, and treats P-507’s signed PDF as timely recording despite the later ERP entry. Post-hoc approval is not treated as curative, and the hierarchy, alternatives, and draft clause are explicit.

The controlling method is nevertheless too rigid. Every case is classified NONCOMPLIANT, despite the instruction to differentiate and despite the CFO’s stated Finance-interpretation authority. Amounts at or below EUR 150,000 with timely Incident Command authorization, especially P-507, were at least candidates for JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION or INDETERMINATE rather than automatic A2 breaches. P-504 is internally inconsistent: the controlling interpretation places the cafeteria purchase outside the SOP, while the breach and “High” confidence depend on SOP rules that would then not apply. Confidence is also too high for P-503’s undefined “critical” scope. C3 recording is required for P-505 but silently treated as satisfied for P-508, and several “satisfied rules” entries are merely rules that were not triggered.
