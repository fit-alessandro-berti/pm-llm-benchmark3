The central error is the second-officer clock. The case gives a review of 35–75 minutes plus 5–15 minutes to record and, if approved, execute. The answer repeatedly replaces that with a 20–35 minute “accelerated” review once the alternate dossier exists. Nothing in the prompt supports that compression. With the stated ranges, S4 arriving at 35–65 minutes makes on-time execution the lower tail, not the central case: midpoint arrival plus midpoint review plus midpoint recording is about 115 minutes.
