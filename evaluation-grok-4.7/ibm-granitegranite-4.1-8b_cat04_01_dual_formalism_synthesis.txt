The write-up has the required sections and a few locally right ideas (register before the two opening streams, containment before planning, an exclusive low/high risk split, archive after release, POWL as the better target). Those ideas are not carried into a coherent model. Both implementations are invalid PM4Py, and both miss the cross-branch pattern the task is built around.

The process-tree code cannot run. `ProcessTree("Register Deviation")` binds the activity name to `operator`, not `label`. `op=`, `silent=`, `condition=`, and `body=` are not constructor arguments. `it == "Pass"` is evaluated once while the object is built, so the loop body does not encode failure. `Operator.LOOP` is binary (`do`, then `redo` repeated as `do (redo do)*`); there is no guard child and no `Operator.POOL`. The unused `pm4py.algo.process_tree` import is not an API. Parent links are only claimed, not established.

Even read as pseudocode, the tree is the wrong approximation. One outer sequence forces containment to finish before either review, root-cause analysis to finish before planning, design and planning to be ordered, every approval to finish before the trial, and supplier consultation to finish before the trial. Requirements 6 and 7 say the opposite: consultation and the selected reviews may overlap design, planning, and the trial, and the trial waits only on design plus the verification plan. The stated policy is permissive over-approximation; the tree is a strong under-approximation, and the loss paragraph does not say so. It instead denies that process trees have silent leaves (a `label=None` leaf is the ordinary tau) and treats low-risk versus high-risk parallelism as a defect. Those routes are exclusive. Consult Supplier is also both mandatory and silent, so it always occurs and never appears.

The POWL code is likewise not executable. `StrictPartialOrder` is constructed with no nodes, then edges are added. `SilentTransition("Consult Supplier", silent=True)` is not how a silent leaf is built, and silencing this activity deletes it from the language. `OperatorPOWL.XOR`, `OperatorPOWL(children=...)`, and `risk_op.apply(...)` do not exist. `OperatorPOWL` needs `Operator.XOR` or `Operator.LOOP` and the correct child count. Regulatory Review is constructed and never inserted. Because a strict partial order is conjunctive and acyclic, edges into both Supervisor Review and Quality Review make both mandatory, while edges from inspection to both Adjust Parameters and Passing Guard make failure and success both mandatory. There is no redo edge and no loop operator, so the repeat-until behavior is absent. Design Corrective Action has no predecessor, so it can precede registration. Consult Supplier is not ordered before Release Material and can follow Archive Case. The concurrency paragraph describes edges the code does not create.

The dependency table has duplicate IDs 5 and 6, introduces a visible RiskDecision, and places consultation after the risk route has finished, which forbids the required overlap with approvals. Release is not specified as a join of passing inspection, the selected route, and consultation when chosen.

The eight-row witness table misses its main job. No row has different intended, tree, and POWL verdicts, so the required approximation witness is absent. Pass and Fail are used as visible labels after the assumptions said outcomes are not labels. The three forbidden traces are all “the middle was skipped.” None isolates a cross-branch violation such as planning before containment while the other branch is lawful. Trace 8’s earliest violation is release without a passing inspection, not “the model forbids an infinite failure loop.” Every verdict is asserted. Against the written POWL, the supposedly allowed traces are missing mandatory nodes.

Section 6 marks broken constructions as exact and answers “split dependencies” with the risk choice instead of “planning depends on containment, design depends on root cause.” Approval–trial overlap is never assessed. The validation plan invents `depth=`, `apply_powl_inference`, and `disable_silent_transitions`, misdefines soundness as a source/sink check, and refers to eight allowed traces when three are forbidden. The self-critique invents an “as soon as possible” supplier rule and does not mention the dead review, the missing loop, or the block-structured collapse.
