7.3

The recommendation is the right one: conditional, segmented transfer, with Site A’s nine-hour median drop treated as confounded and Document Check kept behind Originals Receipt. Sections 1, 3, 4, 9, and 10 are careful and match the stated constraints. The answer is not nearly flawless.

The PM4Py block does not meet the model-fidelity requirement. `StrictPartialOrder` edges are set with `add_edge`, whereas the working API is `order.add_edge`, so the script fails before conversion. The same `Document Check` and `Assessment` objects are children of both the partial order and the supplement loop, which is an illegal multi-parent POWL. `Operator.SEQUENCE` is not the canonical POWL sequence constructor, and `play_out(net, im, fm, num_traces=5)` does not match the facade. The textual relation set also puts a cycle and a mandatory `Legal Review < Request Supplement` edge into what is presented as a partial order; supplement is optional (360 of 1,360 legal reviews).

The pilot overclaims support. Eight strata times multiple arms cannot yield 150–200 cases per cell from about 1,700 cases in 12–16 weeks. Team-level stepped-wedge does not remove interference through the shared pools of 3 assessors and 2 legal reviewers, which is exactly where a bottleneck shift would appear.

Scenario magnitudes are loose. Identity service is 3.4 h against an 18 h originals delay, and the DFG already shows 2,050 Register → Identity Check relations, so identity is often off the critical path for originals-required cases. An overall median gain of 2–5 h, and an eligible gain of up to 6 h, is not earned from the service times. Median “changes” are also unsigned, while p90 worsenings carry a plus. The transport graph sends originals, digital documents, and complexity into `treatment_parallelization`, which is selection rather than effect modification.

Those defects sit on an otherwise well-structured transport argument, so the score stays above a merely adequate analysis but well short of the top of the scale.
