4.8

The write-up has the right skeleton: labeled assumptions, wait/service/sojourn kept distinct, open and blank cases retained as censored, missing starts bounded by resource gaps, and PM4Py’s case-level times correctly fenced off from station queues. That framework is not matched by the numbers or by several interpretations those numbers are used to support.

**Impossible or contradictory aggregates.** Fully observed S1 waits already sum to 1240 minutes, so mean S1 queue length over 360 minutes is at least 3.44 before P07, P15, and P22. The reported mean queue 2.3–2.8 (and the pessimistic 2.0) is arithmetically impossible. The same integral gives mean S1 queue ≈ 4.1. System case-time is about 3425 case-minutes, so mean WIP ≈ 9.5 and the path peaks near 13 around 12:00 (21 arrived, 8 completed), then ends at 8. The stated mean WIP 5–6 and peak 9–10 are far too low, and “peak S1 queue at 13:30” is the wrong shape: by 13:30 only P22 is still waiting. The early-window S1 queue mean 0.8 / peak 3 is similarly low (overlap of waits in 08:00–10:00 is ~390 minutes, mean ≈ 3.3, peak about 6).

**Resource totals and rates.** Known I1 service, excluding both missing starts and P21, is 215 minutes, not 172–187. I2 = 290 is right; I1 is short by roughly P19 plus P07/P15, so S1 utilization in the high-60s/low-70s is biased down (completed central busy time is about 565/720 ≈ 78%, higher again once P21’s elapsed 30 minutes is included). C1’s known completed sum is 265 before P05’s 0–30, which does not fit “257–267 central 262.” S2 subperiod arrivals are mis-binned: ends at S1 are about 7 / 7 / 6 across E/M/L, not 4–5, 7, and 8–9. S2 throughput is about 14 completions in 6 hours (≈ 2.3/h, ≈ 2.9/h over the active span), so the “stable” finding of 3.5–4/h per station is false for S2. “Arrivals exceed departures throughout” is false after 12:00, when S1 is draining.

**Case arithmetic that should have been exact.** P06’s own components are 40+30+25+40 = 135, but total sojourn is given as 95. P05’s sojourn is exactly 10:15−08:25 = 110 for any imputation; 170 is wrong, and completed sojourns for P05, P07 (exactly 135), and P11 (exactly 180) do not depend on the missing start. P15’s S1 sojourn is 125, so W1 cannot approach 155. In the scenario table, P07’s “W1=60 at 09:50” is 65, P15’s “W1=125 with S1=10 at 12:05” is 115, and P11’s central W2=60 with S2=25 cannot occur because those must sum to 80. P05/P11 “central” service is the feasible maximum, so central and pessimistic are the same point while still being described as a midpoint. I1’s median is stated as both 27.5 and 30, and P07’s central service is variously 30, 35, and the max feasible fill. Fully observed S1 waits have median 75 and mean ≈ 65, not median 65 and mean ≈ 70. Observed S1 service includes 20, not a 25–30 band; observed S2 mean service is 40, not ≈ 39.

**Interpretation.** The outage reading is the wrong way around. P08 has been waiting since 10:20 and C2 is free at 10:50, before maintenance; the 10:50–11:15 gap is not S1 starvation. There is also a start-order inversion the assumptions say would be flagged: P09 starts on C1 at 11:00 while earlier arrival P08 waits for C2, and at 13:55 C1 takes P17 while earlier P16 is still queued at 14:00. End WIP is 6 at S2 versus 2 at S1, and S2’s service median (40) implies lower steady capacity than S1 (≈ 30). “S1 remains primary” and “outage explains at most ~25% because it delayed nobody” are stronger than the evidence. A time split is reasonable; the stated mechanism is not.

**Required checks and code.** Little’s law is never computed. Section 6 asserts a whole-window mismatch that A9 itself says is not an applicable regime. The Python distinguishes MISSING, OPEN, and blank, builds an interval log, calls `format_dataframe`, and correctly labels the two case-level PM4Py helpers. It does not implement the three scenarios (`scenario` is unused), does not compute station queue versus service WIP, and its completed-only busy totals cannot reproduce §4. The prose peak WIP is also inconsistent with the WIP function as written, which counts arrival through S2 end and would not yield a peak of 9–10.

What survives is qualitative and already visible from fully observed timestamps: case S1 waits trend from about 5 to about 100, S2 waits trend upward later, P11’s C2 gap cannot hold a typical ~40-minute service, censoring is material, and rounding is second-order next to imputation. The station metrics, utilization ranges, WIP path, throughput claim, and maintenance conclusion do not.
