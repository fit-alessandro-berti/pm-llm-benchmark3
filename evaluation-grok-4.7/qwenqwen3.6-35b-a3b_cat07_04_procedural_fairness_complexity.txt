6.5

The delivered write-up covers all eight required sections, uses the supplied figures accurately, and respects the main constraints: raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination, case-mix differences are not treated as a full excuse, risk scores are treated as suspect, and support steps are kept as sources of burden rather than steps to delete.

The inferential core is still too loose. Adjusted estimates do not control for language need or accommodation requests, so residual gaps for Groups B and C are not cleanly “beyond case mix.” The +9.5 point document-request gap is read as a stricter completeness check, although variant V4 already embeds that step and language need was not held fixed. The more telling adjusted residuals—supervisor review (+8.0 points for B) and appeal (+5.7 points for D, versus only +1.1 for C)—are barely used. Section 4 is almost entirely concerning mechanisms; a legitimate risk- or eligibility-based account is not developed as its own mechanism. Section 2 lists methods but never maps them onto disparity, discrimination, legitimate routing, and uncertainty as separate claims. The bottom line then calls the process procedurally unfair, which is stronger than the unresolved confounding supports.

The PM4Py section is not usable as written. `get_variants` is not a series, `variants_C` and `variants_D` are undefined, `filter_event_attribute_values` is given a string rather than a list, `filter_variants` is given a flat activity list, `get_all_case_durations` has no `.median()`, the performance DFG return value is indexed incorrectly, and `VariableConstant` is not a real parameter path. Service-time insertion is never actually measured, and the log-skeleton call checks no constraint.
