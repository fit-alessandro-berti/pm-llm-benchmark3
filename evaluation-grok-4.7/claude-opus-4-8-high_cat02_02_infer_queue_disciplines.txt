The write-up uses the required seven sections and often compares real decision sets, but several eligible sets are incomplete or reversed, and those mistakes carry the two highest-confidence diagnoses.

**QA.** At 08:20, A04 (arrived 08:13, priority 1, estimate 7) was waiting with A02 and A03. The table drops A04. FIFO selects A02, priority and SPT select A04, and the appointment rule selects A03, which is what happened. “Every pick equals FIFO” also contradicts the memo’s own 08:20 row, where FIFO selects A02. Appointments are not order-consistent or inert: A03 starts before A02. At 09:05, A08 (priority 1) was already waiting, so priority selects A08; the table says A06. Later skips of A04 (08:32) and A08 (09:20, 09:32) do support arrival order over priority. The trace fits FIFO plus a non-preemptive jump once an appointment is due, not FIFO at confidence 82. **QB.** The hybrid reading is the best part: B02’s labeled preemption, B05 ahead of older work at 10:00, and B04 ahead of higher-priority B06 at 10:28. The table is still wrong in places.
