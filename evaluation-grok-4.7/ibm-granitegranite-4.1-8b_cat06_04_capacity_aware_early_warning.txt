The answer covers the required six-section shape and gets two easy traps right: I-03 is stage-ineligible, and I-05 (repeat, no new event) is suppressed while I-07 (new log) can re-enter. The selection rule is \(S=(p_{\text{adj}}-\tau)V\) with \(\tau=0.55\) and a weight that is 1 in every case. That is a probability threshold times value. It omits absolute risk reduction, false-alarm burden, swarm cost, time to deadline, data quality, and spillover, which is the failure mode the task forbids. Observational \(p\) is treated as the object to act on; the supplied effect ranges are not. SME underprediction is later described as an “upward bias” that may make true risk lower, which reverses the stated bias.
