The write-up matches the required sections and most cited figures, but it is not close to flawless. The main defect is the reconciliation the question asks for. Working-together strength is defined as a case-level co-occurrence rate. Short routing events do not lower that rate; they only limit how intense or continuous the joint work is. The answer treats duration as the cause of peripheral co-occurrence and never separates event-level directed handovers from undirected joint cases, tie-spreading across many partners, or the denominator.
