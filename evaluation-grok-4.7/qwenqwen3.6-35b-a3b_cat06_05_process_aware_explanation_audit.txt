6.2

The audit has the right overall shape: all five required sections, a near-complete inventory of the top-12 features and sequence patterns, valid JSON, and correct rejection of the obvious full-trace fields (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, closure team, total document requests). Subtler lineage defects are also caught: the `prior_claim_count` extraction-date predicate, the nightly document snapshot, untimestamped `risk_score_current`, `last_resource_role`, and the missing sequence index. Queue length and elapsed time are kept, and region is mostly treated as a proxy.

Those strengths do not offset several material mistakes in the final conclusions.

The revised narratives misstate the local explanations. Case X’s 0.82 is still presented as driven primarily by queue length (+0.14) and complexity (+0.11), although `appeal_seen_anywhere` (+0.26) is larger and `final_decision` is also leaked. Case Y’s 0.71 is attributed to region and reserve even though the appeal-bearing variant (+0.31) dominates. A recompute/invalidate status cannot be paired with the old probability explained by the surviving features.

Process semantics are wrong where the task requires a three-way split. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is a normatively allowed reconsideration edge and is only inadmissible because it is after `t0`; calling it impossible collapses that into the same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` problem. `Documents Requested -> Documents Received` and `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` are future-possible (fraud may run in parallel), not impossible. Section 2 never cleanly separates enabled, impossible, and future-possible activities, and it omits fraud as potentially enabled after a trigger.

Required coverage is incomplete. `claimant_channel` never appears. `initial_reserve` is absent from the audit table and then used as a credible driver with no lineage. `prior_claim_count_before_t0 = 3` is both a “credible driver” and a leaked SQL result, so the count is not defensible. `total_case_duration_hours` is not identified as near-label leakage for a 10-day breach. The ±5-minute queue join is graded low and “valid” despite a window that can include post-`t0` state. Replacing total document requests with a prefix count ignores that, at this `t0`, that count is structurally zero.

Section 3 and the causal boundaries are only partly successful. Calendar week is filed under both fairness and drift. Queue length is called actionable through resource allocation, and Case Y recommends region-specific network reviews, which selects one correlate and skips the socioeconomic/fairness constraint. Section 5 covers the requested topics, but the appeal-to-`risk_score` repair example reintroduces a mutable, untimestamped feature.
