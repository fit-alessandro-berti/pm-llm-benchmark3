The analysis is numerically careful and conceptually strong: open-hour units, separated demand/mix/capacity/backlog effects, recovery not treated as sustainable throughput, and Little’s law refused where boundaries, proxies, or stationarity fail. Dozens of checked figures hold (week-1 491=491, pooled arrivals 90.0 and WIP 29.9, L/λ 3.98, stock-flow gaps, Fano values, χ²/df ≈ 0.21 and 5.36, mix-accounting solution ≈ 14 and 81 min, staff–ΔWIP correlation ≈ −0.75). Assumptions are labeled, and the Simpson reversal is real.

It is not nearly flawless.

- Regime boundaries overlap. P1’s row extends through 04-15 “about 10:00,” while P3a is 07:00–11:00 on that same morning, and the text then says the outage pieces are not pooled with P1. The 10:00 vs 11:00 cut is unresolved.
- The FTE-minutes/completions “upper bound” is used to discard the lognormal mean (≈44 vs ≈42.5) exactly in P5, where WIP is growing, so today’s staff-minutes and the service content of today’s completions are not the same stock. The cap is not a hard bound there. Lognormal CV ≈ 0.75 is still quoted after that mean is rejected.
- n_eff ≈ 4 applies weekday r ≈ 0.75 to n = 28. Weekday-only n_eff is nearer 3; all-days r ≈ 0.60 gives n_eff nearer 7.
- P6’s ρ “1.0–1.15” does not match the capped 36–42.5 min range (about 0.94–1.11). The extra “read ρ as ±0.05–0.10” sits on top of ranges that are already that wide.
- Section 7 turns 04-07’s 11–15 completions/hour at 10:00–12:00 into evidence of a fixable afternoon restart loss. The same-hours comparison is 5.6 vs 5.8. The staffing target 6.7–7.9 FTE also takes the high arrival count as real work, while threat 2 still allows double-counted arrivals; that sensitivity is not carried into the recommendation. The parked-work story is stressed; the phantom-arrival story is not.
- The endpoint-average identity behind the stock-flow test is only “about,” and it is not in the assumption list. The one-sided 10–16 case gap is still persuasive.
- Alternative A’s means describe no operating day, which is admitted, but the state-dependent service claim remains partly confounded with the mix shift. The change-point table is more date-specific than a 2-day minimum segment and an ad hoc penalty intersection can support.

The code matches the stated design (pandas summaries, dispersion, ACF, heuristic segmentation, windowed L/λ, PM4Py kept in comments and off the aggregate table). Remaining issues are identification and boundary discipline, not arithmetic.
