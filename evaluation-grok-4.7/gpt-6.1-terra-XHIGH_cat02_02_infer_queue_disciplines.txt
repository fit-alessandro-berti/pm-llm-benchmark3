7.5

The decision reconstructions are mostly accurate, assumptions are labeled, and the answer correctly rejects several tempting but false policies. It is not nearly flawless: the headline diagnosis table is internally inconsistent, one “strongest” episode does not identify the claimed rule, and a key QD idle interval is left untested.

The QA row treats 08:20 (A03 over A02, with A04 priority 1 waiting) as the strongest contradiction. That episode is the main support for the stated appointment component, and A04’s priority challenges priority scheduling rather than FIFO-plus-appointment. Section 3 itself calls A03 support. The real stress on the hybrid is 09:05, when a 15-minute A05 is started and pushes A06’s 09:15 appointment to 09:20. The cited support, A07 before A08 at 09:32, matches both FIFO and SPT (estimates 9 and 25). Their own QA table shows SPT selecting A07 there. The discriminating FIFO cases are 08:32 (A02, not shortest/highest-priority A04) and 09:05 (A05, not A08 by priority or A07 by SPT). Those are analyzed later, then ignored when the summary names the strongest evidence. “Interruption of A05 at 09:05” is also the wrong event: A05 is started then, not interrupted.

QD is over-identified. Skill feasibility is largely given by the resource table and the D05/D07 notes; it does not rank eligible work. Confidence 75 sits poorly with the answer’s own statement that skills do not determine ranking, and it leads Section 7 to call QD the most identifiable queue. QA’s post-08:20 choices are a cleaner FIFO pattern. The missed calculation is D-X’s idle stretch from 11:20 to 11:30. D08 is priority 1, mechanical, estimated at 8 minutes, so it would finish about 11:28, before D06’s 11:30 appointment. FIFO would start D03, and priority or SPT would start D08. Observed idleness therefore rejects ordinary ranking and is stronger appointment evidence than D06’s 11:30 start, which FIFO also predicts. A hard hold or an unstated refusal to give mechanical work to D-X is required; “do not overlap the appointment” is not enough. That omission blurs skill constraints, appointment reservation, and shortest-job rules, which is the main QD identifiability problem.

Elsewhere the arithmetic and eligibility sets hold up: B01’s 14/17/14 segments and remaining-time cases, C05’s priority/deadline tie, C01 at 11:40 making C07’s 12:00 deadline infeasible, scan lag bounded so that it cannot hide C06 at 10:32 or B05 at 09:48, and realized durations matching every `est_min` without treating that as proof of ex ante estimates. QB’s rejection of strict preemptive priority is well evidenced (B03 before B05 at 09:48; B01 resumed over B06 at 10:14). QC’s low confidence is appropriate. Those strengths keep the answer well above a middling reconstruction, but the diagnosis table and the QD idle test are central defects, not nits.
