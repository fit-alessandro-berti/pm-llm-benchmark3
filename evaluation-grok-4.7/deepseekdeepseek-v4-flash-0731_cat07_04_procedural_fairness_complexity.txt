7.5

The final assessment is substantively strong and well calibrated. It covers every required dimension and mitigation, uses the given rates, variants, waits, and resource facts accurately, and correctly refuses to treat raw approval gaps or `risk_score_band` as decisive. Justified routing is separated from residual burden at language support, accommodation review, handovers, and appeals.

The PM4Py plan is the main weakness. Group filters use `"A"`–`"D"` rather than `"Group A"`–`"Group D"`, so they would not match the stated groups. `filter_event_attribute_values` is called with a string instead of a value collection, and `filter_paths_performance` is used as if it selected one edge rather than filtering a path by duration. The code also compares groups without the stratification, within-variant controls, or sensitivity checks that section 2 says are necessary, so it does not operationalize the adjusted fairness analysis. The matching design also underplays sparse cells and the bad-control risk of putting possibly biased covariates in the primary specification.
