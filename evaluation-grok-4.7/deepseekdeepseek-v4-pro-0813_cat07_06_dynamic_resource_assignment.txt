7.2

The final assignment set is largely constraint-compliant and the seven required sections are all present, but the policy is not reproducible or fully consistent with its own rules, and a few high-stakes choices are under-justified.

The queue decisions correctly keep skill 0 off Security (Eva and Jonas are not assigned C015), keep Final Approval at skill 2–3, supervise the only high-priority skill-1 case, cap every resource at 4, give high-overtime resources no new work, preserve continuity on the high-complexity/high-handover cases C007 and C016, and hit about 22% documented training on normal/low cases (C003, C009). The worker explanation is clear and non-punitive, and the audit list covers every requested indicator. The baseline comparison correctly refuses a single optimum.

Those strengths are undercut by specification and feasibility gaps. In the pseudocode, the continuity branch writes a `penalty` that is never applied, and the condition is inverted: when the owner is weak and supervision is not feasible, leaving that owner is still heavily penalized, which contradicts the stated continuity exception. The 20% training quota and the Quality batching rule are hard constraints in section 1 but are not enforced by the algorithm. Weights stay symbolic, so an auditor cannot tell whether a case was the top-scoring candidate. Supervisor time is not reserved when the trainee is chosen, yet R8’s supervision of C001 and R3’s supervision of C003 and C009 are later counted against the cap. The workload formula barely sees the resulting load: it only penalizes under 4 hours remaining and more than 8 open cases, so R3 (6.0 hours, 6 open) can absorb an urgent Security case, a high-complexity Rework, and two supervisions without a score penalty. C001’s 3-hour SLA is not reconciled with the stated 3.3-hour service penalty for keeping a weak owner. C003 is given to R6, who is also given a 2-hour Approval and a 4-hour VIP Negotiation, and that same-day contention is not treated as a risk. Complexity is never turned into effort, so “enough hours” is not operational. The baseline comparison stays qualitative and is not shown on C001–C016. The PM4Py sketch names the right functions, but `filter_case_performance(None, None)` does not measure SLA misses, and the skill-mismatch and training-share code reads columns the merge never creates. Appeal rate is listed even though the log has no appeal field.
