7.5

The final answer meets every required section and is unusually well grounded in the case mix, DFG, path table, batching/transport evidence, and SP1/SP2/Q1/R1. Segment-weighted baselines are right (~7.4 kWh, ~10.3 kgCO2e, ~0.35 kg scrap per order; MTO ~87% of energy and ~85% of scrap; Critical ~41% of transport). Air and dedicated vans are separated by segment and slack, rework/returns/obsolescence stay in scope, and the wave plan plus rollback rules avoid a lowest-emission option that would break service or resilience.

It is not nearly flawless.

- Section 1 assigns cancelled, obsolesced, and returned work to the “denominator’s cost base.” Under the stated functional unit (per conforming delivered item) that waste belongs in the numerator. Section 7 treats it more correctly, but the definition itself stays muddled.
- “Production or picking already occurred” for all 530 delay cancellations is false for the 410 `Availability check -> Cancel due to delay` cases, which leave before pick or production.
- “Downgrading triples lateness” treats 9% vs 3% on two Critical paths as the causal effect of moving off dedicated vans. Those paths are selected differently; the gap is association, not an identified effect. The caution is directionally right; the multiplier is not.
- The S3 cell “avoids 32 kWh rework path” treats the whole CNC_B path average as avoidable rework energy. Shifting toward CNC_A does not remove 32 kWh.
- Several magnitudes are not supported by the log: S8’s 20–40% per pooled van, S9’s ~5–10% of fleet transport (no precautionary share is given, yet it is called the largest lever), S5 ~1–2%, S11 ~1%, and the stacked portfolio of transport −10–18%, energy −5–12%, scrap −10–20%. The prompt allows broad directions and forbids invented precision. Overlapping levers (S1 with S4; S3 with S7) are not netted.
- The reconciliation claim misses larger DFG gaps (ship-to-confirm 9,410 vs confirm-to-close 11,200; van events ~620 vs the 8% path ≈ 960) and never notes that Q1 needs part weight, which is not in the attribute list.
- Smaller slips: “re-heat-treat” on every rework loop, and one blended Service-contract late-delivery stop rule beside a ≤5% target that fights the +1 pp rollback.

The redesign, rebound list, and governance design would still be a strong advisory memo. The unsupported effect sizes and the causal overclaim are what keep it well below a near-flawless score.
