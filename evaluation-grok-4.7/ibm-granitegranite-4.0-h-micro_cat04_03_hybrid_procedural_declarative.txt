The answer is not a usable hybrid model. It names the right conflict and the right three temporal pairs, then encodes almost every constraint incorrectly, contradicts its own policy choice, and stops before the required verdicts and deployment analysis.

Policy resolution is incoherent. R5 is restated as R4 (opinion eventually after escalation) rather than “opinion precedes activation.” R6 is reversed. The procedural artifact is not a PM4Py process tree, POWL model, or accepting Petri net. `ProcessTree(activities, transitions)` and `filter_process_tree(..., filter_type="four_eyes")` are not the API. The edge list skips required approvals, lets classification go straight to activation or closure, ties the legal opinion to enhanced due diligence, and omits Request Clarification, Receive Clarification, and Sanctions Escalation while the prose claims a clarification loop. The DECLARE dictionary is not template-keyed. Rules sit under `"rules"` / `"activitys"`. Sections 8–10 are missing. The verdict table has no rows, so T1–T8 are not evaluated and T6 is not shown under both R5/R6 resolutions. There is no treatment of redundancy, vacuity, loops, missing attributes, or check order.
