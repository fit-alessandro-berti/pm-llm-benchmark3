8.0

The audit is complete and conceptually sharp. Every global top-12 feature, every sequence pattern, and every local driver in Cases X, Y, and Z is classified. Post-closure fields (`total_case_duration_hours`, `final_handler_team`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`) are separated from repairable as-of defects (prior-claim SQL, the 23:00 document snapshot, untimestamped `risk_score_current`, extraction-date tenure, the ±5 minute queue join). `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is correctly treated as a legal reconsideration loop that is still inadmissible at `t0`, while `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as normatively impossible and timestamp-ambiguous. Queue length and elapsed time are kept. Region and channel stay as fairness-sensitive proxies. The four standards — predictive validity, causation, actionability, and fairness — are applied explicitly, and Section 5 covers feature-store design, as-of timestamps, prefix recomputation, conformance tests, recalibration, explanation stability, and drift.

The grade stops at 8.0 because of concrete errors.

Case Y puts `prior_claim_count_before_t0 = 3` in both `credible_drivers` and `removed_or_repaired_drivers`. That value is produced by `claim_created_at <= extraction_date`, so it is not known to be the count before `t0`. `initial_reserve` is also listed as credible while its freeze time is only assumed, and the gloss adds an unsupported “senior review” step. The task allows only information defensibly available at `t0`.

The suffix-perturbation test cannot catch that prior-claim bug. Changing the current case’s post-`t0` suffix does not add or remove other claims filed before the extraction date. The test is valid for same-trace fields such as `final_handler_team` and `full_trace_variant_id`. Using the prior-claim query as a positive example of that test would give false assurance. A second method error: when the ingestion index is missing, a normative tie-break is allowed to create a directional bigram. Equal timestamps for Damage Assessment and Claim Closed are unordered; imposing the legal order invents a history.

`Fraud Investigation Opened` is placed in the enabled set immediately at `t0`. Under the stated model it is enabled only after a trigger, and it may run in parallel later. Week 52 is described as jointly the holiday window and the operating-policy change; the lineage note only says those regimes align with certain weeks.

Smaller overstatements accumulate. Closure duration is critical leakage, but it is not the same object as a 10-day settlement label. The queue join’s ±5 minute tolerance is an actual post-`t0` window, so “theoretical” and severity Low are too soft. The case narratives assign residual risk levels and operational mechanisms — iterative document cycles, Region R4 “structural delays,” document-request clarity — that the surviving prefix features do not establish. Mean-absolute importances are unsigned, and additive contributions from the leaked model are not a recalibrated probability.
