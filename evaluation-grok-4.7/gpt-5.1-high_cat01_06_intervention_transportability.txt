6.1

The advisory judgment is directionally right — conditional go, no blanket copy, no illegal early document check, Site A’s −9 h not treated as a causal effect — but the formal and quantitative core is not reliable enough for a high score.

The transport graph mis-specifies mechanisms. `digital_documents -> originals_required` and `case_complexity -> legal_capacity` point the wrong way; `cycle_time -> appeal` is an unsupported direct effect; `originals_required -> true_overlap` is missing even though the originals gate is the main reason parallelization cannot fire. The prose then calls API latency, huddles, and extra FTEs “shared” mechanisms while also encoding them as site-selection nodes. Those are Site A co-interventions, not a common structural backbone.

Scenario numbers are not derived from service times, the 18 h originals delay, the 20 h pre-assessment wait, or the pools (4/7/3/2). Eligible shares of 30–40% and ~60% do not fit 62% originals and 40% legal review: independent no-originals and no-legal volume is about 23%, and can reach the mid-30s only under an unstated near-perfect overlap of those flags. Portfolio p90 gains in the “easy segment only” scenarios are the wrong direction of effect; the untouched legal/originals tail still sets the portfolio 90th percentile. The one median-up/tail-down scenario is the right kind of case, but its −1 to −3 h / +5 to +20 h bands are asserted rather than mapped.

The candidate partial order is not a partial order. Unconditional edges among Assessment, Legal Review, Request Supplement, Approve, and Reject force every activity, including both decisions and a cycle `Request Supplement → Document Check → Assessment → Legal Review → Request Supplement`. XOR and the supplement loop are only described in prose. The PM4Py block repeats that error: non-API `Event` and `StrictPartialOrder(activities=, order=)`, a `POWL` wrap of an object that should already be a POWL, no `OperatorPOWL` loop or choice, and an unconditional originals→document edge, so the recommended digital path is not in the net. Playout and soundness checks sit on an empty or illegal order.

The pilot’s V1/V2 split is the right way to separate task creation from huddles and WIP, but 600–800 eligible cases per arm in 3–4 months is infeasible on 3,400 cases per six months once the stratum is digital, no-originals, and non-legal. Transport contrasts by originals and legal involvement are promised after those strata were excluded. The mechanism-failure example cites legal congestion inside a non-legal stratum.

Smaller faults add up. Huddles are placed in Site A’s before column. The directly-follows counts are unused: 2,108/3,400 and 1,360/3,400 simply reproduce the 62% and 40% constraints, while Register’s outgoing 2,050+1,860 exceeds 3,400 and is never questioned. The digital-claim “harmful signature” describes a small benefit, not harm. Assumptions and the evidence table are careful; they do not repair the graph, the arithmetic, or the model.
