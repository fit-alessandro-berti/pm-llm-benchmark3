7.4

The three candidate languages are the strongest part of the answer and check out by hand: the process tree is exactly the seven observed variants, the balanced partial order has size 12 (five justified extras), and the permissive partial order has size 18 (those five plus the six supplier-before-credit completions). Directly-follows bidirectionality is correctly treated as not proving concurrency, missing supplier events are separated from true skips, D1–D4 and D6 are enforced, and the positive/negative suite matches those languages. The data-collection plan (component attribute, start/complete timestamps, supplier reconciliation, and the specific falsifying traces) would actually separate routing, concurrency, optionality, and loss.

Those results are undercut by several concrete faults. S6 is absent from the evidence ledger even though every D-rule and every other stakeholder statement was required. The recommendation says that legitimate cases falling outside the seven-variant set measure the balanced model’s underfitting; that rate measures the conservative model’s underfitting and the value of the five extra balanced traces. Balanced underfitting is legitimate behavior that balanced still rejects. The same mix-up appears when permissive precision failures are filed as “underfitting risks,” and when D1–D2 are called medium confidence. “Smallest edge set that still fits” is the wrong fitness criterion: removing the credit-before-supplier edge still fits all 30 traces. The formal expressions are missing their operators, so they are not readable without the code. “7×8 = 56” is not the enumerated leaf count (the seven variant lengths sum to 49). “4 shown” SC-before-credit traces is wrong; the table shows five. The language DFS sorts place objects, which standard PM4Py places do not support, so the enumeration used to “confirm” the sizes is likely to crash. D5 is also overstated as unencodable: a completion proxy (“supplier after at least one check”) is expressible and was a better permissive boundary than supplier-first traces.
