7.5

The POWL model is the right canonical choice and is largely faithful: partial-order edges encode the cross-branch joins (`Plan`/`Design` before the trial loop, route and optional supplier before release) while leaving approval, supplier, planning, design, and the trial unordered. The process tree is a genuine conservative under-approximation, and T3 correctly separates intended behavior from that tree. Loop orientation is right in both models: `Execute`–`Inspect` is the do-body and `Adjust Parameters` is the redo, so a passing exit cannot be followed by `Adjust Parameters`. Parent links, the low/high XOR, silent supplier skip, and the required section structure are in place. Verdicts in the witness table match the models.

The dependency specification is incomplete for a table that was supposed to carry the concurrency, choice, and optionality relations on its own. There is no explicit row for `Contain Material` concurrent with `Analyze Root Cause`, for `Plan Verification` concurrent with `Design Corrective Action`, or for the approval route concurrent with the trial. Those are the non-block-structured facts the rest of the answer depends on. Supplier optionality is only folded into “if selected,” not stated as an XOR/skip relation.

F3’s reason misstates the earliest violation. `R C A P D S E I X` is still a legal prefix: `Adjust Parameters` is a valid redo. The first prefix outside the language is `… X Rel`, because release is not enabled until another `Execute Trial`–`Inspect Trial` cycle completes. Calling `X` itself the earliest violation, and calling the preceding `Inspect Trial` final, is incorrect. F1 is slightly loose in the same way: `D` has already occurred, so the broken condition is `Execute Trial` before `Plan Verification`, not an unsatisfied P/D join.

Smaller defects: `Transition(label, label)` and `SilentTransition("skip_supplier")` are version-fragile relative to the usual `Transition(label=…)` / `SilentTransition()` constructors, so the POWL snippet is not reliably standalone. The validation plan never says how a negative replay becomes a verdict, and it ignores that the unbounded trial loop can make a naive soundness or playout check fail to terminate. The release-synchronization cell mixes that join, which both models implement, with the separate decision to postpone route and supplier until after planning and design.
