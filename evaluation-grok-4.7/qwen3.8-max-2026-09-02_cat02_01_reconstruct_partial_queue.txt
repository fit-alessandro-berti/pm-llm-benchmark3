7.6

The final reconstruction is analytically strong and internally consistent: time base, case waits and services, missing-start bounds (including the C2/P08 constraint on P11), censored open and blank cases, subperiod rates, busy-time and utilization ranges, WIP identities, and the time-varying bottleneck argument all hold together. Assumptions, formulas, and the three imputation scenarios are explicit, and steady-state Little’s Law is correctly refused in favor of a finite-window identity.

It is not nearly flawless. “Eight uncensored-but-incomplete cases” reverses the censoring status of the eight unfinished cases. Zero-length service is carried into the case table and the lower utilization bounds even though an activity that occurred cannot have a serious service time of zero; that limitation is acknowledged later, but the reported ranges still treat it as an endpoint. The S2 capacity remark (“about 40–50 minutes”) is tighter than the observed known services, which run from 25 to 50 and center near 40. Rounding is discussed as a ±5-minute envelope but is not folded into the numeric ranges.

The required Python does not run. `pm4py.format_dataframe` is called with `activity` and `timestamp` rather than `activity_key` and `timestamp_key`, so it raises before any result table is printed and before the PM4Py demonstrations. Those demonstrations are also wrapped so broadly that a signature mismatch only prints a failure, and the subperiod rates are hardcoded instead of calculated. The analytical conclusions survive; the reproducibility requirement does not.
