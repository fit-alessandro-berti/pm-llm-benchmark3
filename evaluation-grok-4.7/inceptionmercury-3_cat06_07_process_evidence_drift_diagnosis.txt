6.5

The month-level story is the right one: April is a schema and feature-lineage failure, June is a routing-mix change with stable per-variant breach rates, August is a real V3 performance and outcome shift, September adds Partner Z, and October’s 0.16 breach rate is not comparable at 55% maturity. The six required sections, the alert hierarchy, and the five risk cases are present and mostly usable. The score is held down by test logic that contradicts the proposed mechanism, a few operational recommendations that would not be valid at the diagnosis-time cutoff, and a code sketch that would not produce the monthly evidence it claims.

The concept-drift tests are specified backwards. Sections 1 and 2 treat the `Order Part -> Part Received` slowdown as the supplier mechanism that raised matured V3 breach from 0.35 in June to 0.46 in July and 0.64 in August. Tests 4 and 5 then say concept drift is confirmed if the V3 month effect remains after adjusting for that delay. Under the stated mechanism the delay is the mediator, so the month effect should shrink once delay is included. A residual effect would indicate an additional channel. As written, full mediation would be misread as evidence against the supplier explanation.

September is under-called. “September possible,” with V3’s 0.61 listed as contradicting evidence, does not follow the mix. Partner Z is 24% of September starts and 58% V3, against 36% V3 for everyone else, so about two-thirds of V3 volume is still non-Z. Non-Z V3 breach must also have risen; Partner Z alone cannot produce a matured V3 rate of 0.61. Similarity to August supports persistence of the July–August shift. Censoring at 82% maturity and the new population confound the increment, but they do not make the level uncertain. The covariate row has the complementary problem: it calls August–September the strongest covariate period. A variant-weighted reconstruction puts August’s latent breach near 0.31 versus about 0.21 from August’s mix at June’s V3 rate, so the conditional V3 change is the larger piece.

The remediation path has internal conflicts. Stage 1 fits a calibrator on January–July and then names March, June, and July as holdouts. July is grouped with safe training data once labels are mature, even though July is the first month with a higher matured V3 rate. Variant-specific production calibrators, variant features, and case-level supplier lead time are recommended for a score issued immediately after `Initial Diagnosis Completed`, before the branch into remote fix, dispatch, or parts is executed. Retrospective stratification by eventual variant is valid; using that variant at score time is future information unless the route is stored on the diagnosis event. Variant-by-month interactions likewise do not carry forward to the next month. Stage 1 also never says that recalibration cannot repair the August discrimination drop from roughly 0.81 to 0.68 AUROC.

Smaller evidence problems accumulate. The label-delay row calls August moderately implicated, while the narrative correctly says 93% maturity cannot explain a 0.28 observed breach and that the missing tail would likely raise it. The documented unrecomputed cache is an April backfill defect, but May is described as if its own derived features were broadly stale. The January–June parts edge already moved from 28 h to 42 h before the July break. The maturity “curve” is defined as cumulative breach rate rather than fraction of cases with a known outcome. Falsifiers sit inside the test section, and the pipeline row’s cleaning check is placed in the contradicting-evidence column.

The code sketch uses the requested PM4Py entry points and does separate an event-calendar slice from a case-start cohort, but it would not run as monthly audit evidence. `format_dataframe` yields a dataframe, while the case-start step calls `pm4py.filter_log` with a trace lambda and `time:timestamp` keys. The missing-source fallback does `df.get("source", "old").map(...)`, which fails when `source` is absent. Deduping keeps exact `(case_id, activity, time)` matches only, so renamed-versus-`UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` pairs and timestamp-jitter duplicates remain. `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` is called on the full raw log inside the month loop, so every month receives the same global frame. The returned objects are full variant maps, DFGs, and feature frames, with no median for `Order Part -> Part Received` and no variant shares.
