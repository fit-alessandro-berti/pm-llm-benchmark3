7.5

The final document is a strong, mostly correct comparison: the languages are incomparable, the P1 / P2 / P3 verdicts match the right observation boundaries, the P2 homomorphism is valid, and the bounded cardinalities 76, 85, and 4 are derived correctly. The grade is held down by definite technical errors, not by presentation.

The counterexample memberships are right, and so are the invariants `#C = #A = #R+1` in A and `#C = 1` in B. T1 (length 7) is a minimal A-only trace, and the infinite families show unbounded inequivalence rather than a bound artifact. Tree metrics (12/12/11 nodes, depths 4/5/4, no duplicate labels) match the trees. The simplified model is honestly a strict subset of A: it keeps per-round checks and the dashboard projection, and it drops the check-before-analyze interleavings. The footprint claim also checks out: with approvals, the directly-follows sets differ only by `Approve → Check`; without approvals, they coincide while the languages stay incomparable.

The length of T4 is wrong. `Open, Analyze, Approve, Check, Close` has 5 visible events, not 4. That mistake is repeated in the verdict table, the counterexample table, and the minimality paragraph. Under the answer’s own formula `2k+2+a`, this trace (`k=1`, one approval) has length 5. Every length-4 trace (`Open, Analyze, Check, Close` and `Open, Check, Analyze, Close`) is in both models, so the “shortest difference has length 4” proof is false. T4 is still a valid, and in fact shortest, B-only witness, but only at length 5.

The Earth Mover’s Distance example is also wrong. Replacing `+(Analyze, Check)` by `X(→(Analyze, Check), →(Check, Analyze))` does preserve A’s visible language, but under the declared uniform-over-enabled-transitions policy, and under the usual process-tree net encoding, the visible-trace distribution does not change: silent split/join transitions are deterministic, and the order choice remains a fair coin flip. So EMD is 0, not > 0. The further step “T3 has tiny mass, so EMD can be small” does not follow; every `k ≥ 2` trace is disjoint between A and B, so the symmetric difference carries substantial mass.

Those faults sit on top of an otherwise careful equivalence argument, which is why the score stays in this range rather than falling further.
