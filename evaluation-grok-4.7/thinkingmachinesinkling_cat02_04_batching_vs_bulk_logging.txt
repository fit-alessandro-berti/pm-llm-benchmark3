The cluster diagnoses are mostly directionally right (fixture batch, group start, folder submission, serial packing, shared pool, bulk close, 15-minute rounding, downtime versus clock skew), and the signature matrix, G6 zero-service/inflated-wait account, redesign list, and adjudication follow the required headings. That substance is undercut by material errors.

Waiting time is misdefined for G8: Q01’s 38 minutes is queue-to-start, while “65 minutes” for Q05 is queue-to-complete (16:13−15:08). The true waits are 38 to 58 minutes. The adjusted “~3–13 minutes” does not follow from a 15:35 release. G2’s mean service is 17.5 minutes, not 18.75. G7’s decisive evidence is missed: M02–M05 have start 14:00 before queue arrival, so observed waits are negative. G5 never confronts four-way overlap against a stated cap of three analysts. G7’s ±7.5-minute censoring is treated as fact even though the rounding rule is undocumented and may be floor/ceil rather than nearest.

G4’s top and second explanations are the same serial pattern twice, so operational serial work is not cleanly separated from a detected sequential batch. The required code does not run: `equality_df` has no `resource` column, so the merge raises before the diagnostic table; `resource_key` is not a `format_dataframe` argument; the PM4Py “comparison” is the same unparsed note on every row; granularity is computed and never reported; and the overlap test uses a strict inequality, so G6’s identical zero-duration events score as no overlap.
