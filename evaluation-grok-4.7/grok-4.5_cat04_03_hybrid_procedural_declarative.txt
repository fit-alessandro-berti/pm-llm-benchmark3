Under that definition, both `("Manager Approval", "Activate Vendor")` and `("Compliance Approval", "Activate Vendor")` are mandatory. Every activation must therefore be preceded by both approvals. The published skeleton also contains `("Receive Clarification", "Request Clarification")`, which forbids a first request. T2 fails that pair.
