The conservative judgments are mostly right, and the event/relation arithmetic is consistent. The write-up still contains several internal contradictions, one misclassified pair, and an implementation that does not carry out the stated rules.

The retained-event math holds: 17 raw, 15 after merging only e04→e03 and e16→e11, 12 after also merging e07, e09, and e15, and 10 after a same-activity-and-timestamp collapse (picks 3→1, packs 2→1, dispatches 2→1, invoices 2→1, captures 3→1). Relation totals 44, 39, 32, and 28 follow if those unions add no new object links. e05 and e14 stay in the conservative and evidence-based scenarios. e08/e09 are correctly treated as a harmonization collision, e06/e07 and e13/e15 as abstraction mismatches, and e13/e14 as distinct payments. The explanation of why a synthetic microsecond gap becomes a fake measured duration is sound, and the 10:00, 10:15, and 12:00 orders respect the stated facts, with admissible swaps noted for the two picks and for e13/e14.

The decision tree does not cover e09/e10. Those events already have different labels and the same objects (O1, P1, D1), so they are neither a T5 disjoint-object pair nor a T4 same-label collision. Fact 5 makes them distinct ordered events; the tree would otherwise drop them into quarantine. T1 is also too wide: same `source_event_key` with a different payload is dropped, while the tree claims that drop loses no attributes.

Section 5 says the evidence-based merges erase Pick→Pick, Pack→Pack, and Capture→Capture. Merging e07 and e09 removes the pack and dispatch loops only. e03 and e05 remain, and e13 and e14 remain, so Pick→Pick and Capture→Capture stay. Section 7 then says the conservative log still shows Pack→Pack, even though e07’s activity was changed to “Pack Package (ERP status update).” The claim that OTG topology is stable is wrong once e13/e14/e15 become one event: M1 and M2 co-occur on a single event for the first time. Authorize→Capture still touches only M1; the aggressive capture does not extend authorization to M2. Calling M2’s lone capture a data-quality anomaly adds a defect the facts do not state.

The ordering policy lists within-source `source_seq` above ingestion time, but the sort uses domain edges plus `(ingested_at, eid)` only. In scenario B, merging e09 into e08 deletes e09→e10, so the required handover-after-acceptance constraint disappears. `verified_pair_merge` applies one source rank to every column, not the per-attribute authority given for ERP versus billing; when the donor wins, the conflict record stores the opposite kept/discarded values; when the canonical row wins, the donor value is not logged. Provenance columns are not protected. Label changes never write audit rows, and `relation_contributions` is specified and then never populated. `validate_integrity` requires e05 and e14, so the aggressive 10-event path fails its own assertion. `condition=` is not the PM4Py argument these helpers actually expose (`have_common_object` is), and `ocel_sort_by_additional_column(con, "order_key", TS)` passes the timestamp column in the ascending position.
