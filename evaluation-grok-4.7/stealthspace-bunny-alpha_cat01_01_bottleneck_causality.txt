The causal skeleton is strong: the crude-versus-stratified reversal is real, stratification is not treated as identification, queueing is separated from service, Orion is handled as a routing bundle, and the pilot-plus-capacity plan is safer than a 50% transfer. Those strengths do not offset material evidence errors.

The headline standardization is wrong. Pooled complexity weights are Low 12/30, Medium 10/30, and High 8/30, not 16/30, 10/30, and 4/30. The reported standardized means (Nova 16.27, Orion 13.93, gap 2.34) are therefore not the stated common-weight contrast. The correct pooled contrast is still a reversal, about 2.5 hours, so the direction survives, but the audited number does not.

Nova’s overall mean wait is 140.6/16 = 8.79 hours, not 7.66. The crude wait gap is about 4.0 hours, not 5.14. That 7.66 figure also contradicts the answer’s own stratum waits (5.06, 11.12, 22.75). Orion high-complexity service is 55.0/6 = 9.17, not 9.00, so overall Orion service is about 7.04, not 6.96. These errors are repeated in the role table and in hypothesis 2, and they distort the wait-versus-service split.

The data audit is false. Nova’s 16 cycle times have median (10.4+10.8)/2 = 10.6, matching the supplied 10.60. The claimed CSV median of 9.95 is an off-index calculation. Nova rework 3/16 = 18.75% rounds to the supplied 18.8% shown as 18.80. “Repair the aggregate audit” is not a real first step. Medium Nova is also misdescribed as three day/normal cases; it is two day/normal and one day/high. Signs are inconsistent with the stated Orion-minus-Nova convention.

The graph does not support the residual-confounding claim. `unobserved_case_risk` reaches `resource_group` only through `complexity`, so conditioning on complexity blocks that backdoor, yet the text still treats risk as open after stratification. `arrival_load` is discussed as a possible mediator and left disconnected from routing. Hypothesis 4’s prediction is reversed: vanishing shift/load coefficients would weaken, not favor, a shift/load explanation.

The diagnostics would not run. The overlap groupby uses `resource_group`, which is not a column. `discover_dfg` is treated as a DataFrame and passed `variant="parallel"`; the real return is a frequency map, and `noise=1.0` plus comma-joined traces is not a reliable `parse_event_log_string` call. The code’s `value_counts` weights also disagree with the prose. Confidence 88 is too high for n=2 cells and almost no complexity–shift overlap.
