The answer is strong and largely requirement-complete: all ten sections match the requested schema, the nine-hour Site A shift is treated as confounded, unconditional parallel document checking is rejected for the 62% originals-required share, and the pilot separates nominal task creation from true overlap. It is not nearly flawless.

The transportability graph has real causal errors. `legal_review_required` and `legal_capacity` point directly at `waiting_before_assessment`, although legal review is downstream of assessment; any effect on a later assessment wait runs through supplement rework and shared queues, which the graph already partly represents. `case_complexity -> legal_review_required` conflates the 48% complexity share with the distinct 40% statutory public-sector/high-value flag. Most importantly, `originals_required` reaches cycle time only by blocking `true_overlap`. The 18-hour receipt delay lengthens waiting before assessment and cycle time even when no parallelization is attempted, and that direct path is missing. Unsigned edges also make inhibitors indistinguishable from promoters.

The scenario arithmetic is only partly anchored. The `min(3.4, 8.1) = 3.4` service-overlap bound, the 38% non-originals share, and dilution into a roughly 0–3 hour portfolio effect are sound, and the bottleneck-shift row does let the center improve while p90 worsens. The specific p90 bands (+4 to +15, +1 to +8, +5 to +20) are not derived from the supplied directly-follows counts, the 360/1,360 supplement rate, pool sizes, or the 20-hour pre-assessment wait. The first-wave 15–30% share is loose once digital, non-legal, and lower-complexity filters are multiplied. Mean improvement is asserted beside a large p90 worsening without a joint distribution.

Design details conflict. A lane cap of six paired cases is incompatible with two paired cases per identity reviewer across four reviewers, and that six is borrowed from Site A’s per-reviewer rule, which section 1 also misplaces as a post-intervention change rather than general Site A context. Section 6 recommends lawful Identity–Originals concurrency, but the pilot leaves originals observational, so half of the proposed intervention is untested. The prose partial order re-receives originals on supplement; the POWL redo path is only Request Supplement → Document Check → Assessment, so the executable model drops a dependency the specification treats as mandatory. “Highest complexity,” “low-to-medium,” and the “pre-specified” overlap threshold are never operationalized. Assumption 8 is a decision rule, not a factual assumption.

The PM4Py fragment otherwise meets the requested construction: both Site A trees, a non-sequential Site B partial order with an explicit dual join into Assessment, an optional legal branch, a supplement loop, net conversion, soundness checks, and an explicit limits comment. Format, word counts, and the conditional-go decision are compliant.
