8.0

The design is statistically serious and meets the core bar: activation is treated as selective observation, rejected and withdrawn cases are not coded as non-defaults, censoring is separated from undefined outcomes, and validation is temporal rather than a random case split. Dates, cohort arithmetic, and the maturity boundary are right (125 days before 2026-02-15 is 2025-10-13; 90 days before extraction is 2025-11-17; 2,700/21,600 = 12.5%; the seven strata sum to 48,000). The two-stage estimand, leakage table, and refusal to validate 90-day default on post-migration activations are defensible.

The score stays at 8.0 because several design and skeleton gaps are real.

The primary Stage-2 IPW strategy needs a mature-but-unlabeled activated stratum. The export defines all 3,600 unlabeled activated accounts as not yet mature. If that definition is calendar-based, nobody with activation on or before 2025-10-13 sits in that bucket, the IPW sample is empty, and permanent partner missingness is only a prospective sensitivity case. The write-up treats that split as part of the current analysis without stating that its size is unidentified and may be zero.

Cause-specific hazards and Fine-Gray models are offered as substitutes. They target different estimands. The reported early-warning quantity is \(P(D=\text{Activated}\mid\mathcal{F}_{t_p})\,P(Y_{90}=1\mid D=\text{Activated},\mathcal{F}_{t_p})\). That product is coherent only for cause-specific activation risk, and only if the two fitted models are jointly calibrated. Separately trained models are not.

Policy regime is assigned by case start. Applications opened before 2025-07-01 and decided after the threshold change are still pre-policy in the split, so the policy break is not isolated. The 60-day disposition horizon in the estimand is not the censoring rule used in the cohort or the code.

The skeleton follows the requested PM4Py path and blocks the obvious post-decision fields, but it does not implement the as-of rule it argues for. `build_case_outcomes` is called once at 2026-02-15, so train labels are final-extract labels, not labels mature at the training cutoff. Rejected, withdrawn, and transferred cases share the status `selectively_unobserved`, which collapses an undefined outcome with loss to follow-up. Canonicalization is not restricted to post-2025-10-15, and deduplication keeps one row per clock second rather than a one-second delta. Prose puts Test 1 through 2025-09-30; the code puts every start before 2025-10-15 into that test. The block is also longer than the requested 80–160 lines and never builds an elapsed-time prefix.
