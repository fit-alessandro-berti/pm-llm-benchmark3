7.5

The reconstruction is rigorous and mostly numerically faithful: wait/service/sojourn are separated correctly, open and blank cases are kept as elapsed or censored time, missing starts are bounded from resource and queue constraints rather than dropped, and the central “previous end + 5 min” rule is empirically justified. Station WIP areas (S1 2085 case-min, mean 5.79; S2 1340, mean 3.72), the scenario-invariant split between queue and service, resource busy times (I1 245/305/315, I2 290, C1 270/295/300, C2 245/265/270), the 13:00 queue means (1.75 vs 4.2), and the post-14:00 clearing projection all check out. The sample-path versus stationary Little’s Law distinction, the C2 outage as a local shift rather than the cause of the backlog, and the transient-S1 / structural-S2 bottleneck reading are the right conclusions and are tied to evidence.

Those strengths do not make the write-up nearly flawless.

- Fully observed S1 services are 18, not 17 (sum 505, mean 28.1). Section 6 then uses n=19 and means 25.2 / 28.4 / 28.8; the correct completed-or-imputed set has n=20 and means about 25.3 / 28.3 / 28.8. The code’s own `summarise` path would not reproduce the prose.
- “Monotonically increasing” completed sojourns is false (P08 180, P09 155, P10 175), as is “rising monotonically” for the 08:00–10:00 S1 waits (P12 85, P13 80). Both claims contradict numbers printed in the same section.
- The “stable” claim that S1 wait exceeds S2 wait for every case through P14 is false: P01 and P02 tie, and P08 is 50 vs 55.
- The 08:00–10:00 S1 service mean of 27.7 matches neither the arrival cohort (~30, with or without P07 at 30) nor the six completions in that block (~25.8). It is undefined or wrong.
- “−2.6%, exactly the residual of P21/P22” is not exact. Residual time is 240 min (0.67 of WIP); the 5.79 vs 5.64 gap is about 0.15 and comes from mixing 22 arrivals with a 20-case mean.
- C2’s post-08:35 denominator is 325 min, or 310 after maintenance, not 320, so the “83%” starvation-adjusted figure is off. “3.75/h while saturated” is the first-to-last span rate; the saturated blocks in the same answer are 3.5/h. Calling 40–45 the C1 norm ignores observed C1 services of 30 and 25, which softens the P05 anomaly.
- Section 4’s utilization bands (I1 68–88, C1 72–86, C2 68–81) and Section 6’s imputation table are only loosely reconciled, and the start-up slice of the “125 idle server-minutes” counts both S1 servers while a single job is waiting.

The Python meets the required PM4Py surface (status-aware parsing, `format_dataframe`, both insert functions on the 11 fully observed cases, `convert_log_to_time_intervals`, printed scenarios). The interval unpacking assumes epoch-second 5-tuples and `@@arrival_rate` / `@@finish_rate` column names; if the installed PM4Py differs, that block throws. Pandas WIP and busy-time logic itself is aligned with the stated half-open convention.

Main operational conclusions survive the mistakes. The grade is held down by repeated summary-stat errors, two contradictions with the case table, and one false item in the stable-conclusion list.
