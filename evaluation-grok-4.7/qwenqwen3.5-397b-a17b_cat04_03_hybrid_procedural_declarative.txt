The write-up identifies the R5/R6 conflict, keeps temporal deviations as warnings, and names the intended issues in T4, T5, T6, and T8. Those parts do not survive contact with the formal artifacts. The dictionaries and tree are not internally coherent, and the verdict matrix does not follow from them.

The procedural code is not executable PM4Py. `ProcessTree(None, activity)` stores the activity as the parent and leaves the label empty. `Operator.OPTIONAL` is not a process-tree operator. The loop is described as zero-or-more, but a process-tree loop executes its do-child at least once, so every clarification-free trace is out of the language. `Sanctions Escalation` and `Obtain Legal Opinion` are sequenced before `Classify Risk`, while T6 and T7 place them after classification and around approval. T5’s unmatched request and T8’s activate-plus-reject are also outside that tree. The matrix still marks those procedural cells `pass` (T8 only with an unjustified parser hedge).

DECLARE does not implement the stated policy. `at_most_one` is not an allowed template. `precedence(Receive Clarification, Request Clarification)` is the reverse of the needed rule and conflicts with `response` in the other direction; under standard precedence, T2 fails. `precedence(Obtain Legal Opinion, Activate Vendor)` and the same log-skeleton `always_before` pair require an opinion before every activation, so T1, T2, T4, and T5 fail. R5 is conditional on escalation; that limitation is never stated. R3 needs alternate response and a bar before a final outcome, not plain response. R8 omits Enhanced Due Diligence versus Manager Approval. R11 is encoded as immediate `chain_response` and drops the later re-verification.

The log skeleton’s `always_after` pairs are oriented inconsistently: `(Close Onboarding, Receive Onboarding Request)` and `(Activate Vendor, Release First Payment)` cannot both be right. `directly_follows` neither matches the concurrent backbone nor T2’s actual successor of receipt, yet T1 and T2 are marked pass. Section 9 notices vacuity and the missing emergency attribute, but not these self-contradictions, and it treats normative confidence 1.0 as if it should be relaxed because of noise.

Temporal values, seconds conversion, `zeta = 2.0`, and the T7 escalation delay (34 h versus a 24 h upper bound) are right. The resource checks catch T4’s four-eyes clash, but the four-eyes call is unused, the 24-hour emergency deadline is never checked, and a missing `resource` has no defined outcome. `N/A` is outside the allowed verdict vocabulary. Deploying the model as written would reject the low-risk reference trace.
