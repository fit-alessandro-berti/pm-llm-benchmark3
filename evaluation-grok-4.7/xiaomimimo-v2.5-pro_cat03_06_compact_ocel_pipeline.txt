7.5

The final construction is internally consistent and covers every required section: 17 timezone-aware events, 12 objects, denormalized E2O rows, the required O2O links, validation, flattening, and the named discovery calls. Order, package, delivery, and invoice traces match the chosen relations, and the Order DFG weights are arithmetically right (including Add Item → Reserve Stock = 2 and Pack → Dispatch = 2). Payment traces are correctly treated as single-event and analytically useless. P1, V1, M1, M2, O100, and O200 are used with the right convergence roles.

Deductions are for concrete errors, not incompleteness. E07 is called the largest multi-type event, but E09 and E13 each have four object types and six relations, against E07’s three types and five relations. Issue Invoice → Confirm Delivery is blamed on a coincidental parallel ordering; under this model the edge exists because Record Payment at 11:30 sits between invoice and D1 confirmation and was deliberately left off the order traces. The source-minimal alternative’s “~25 versus 49” relation count does not follow from the policy just described (that policy removes about 19 rows, leaving about 30). Section 1 also calls the rich logistics links “direct source-system facts,” while dispatch and confirm never name orders or items; those links are operational propagation, which Section 6 itself treats as a different policy. The object-centric net is then drawn as one linear chain per type, which that discovery procedure does not determine.
