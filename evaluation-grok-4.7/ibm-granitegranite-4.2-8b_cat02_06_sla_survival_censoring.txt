4.5

The required nine-part structure is present, and some of the survival framing is real: time starts at `Submit`, open cases are kept as right-censored, withdrawal/rejection are competing events rather than ordinary censoring, transfers are given multiple labeled assumptions, escalation is recognized as post-baseline, and the limitations list matches the missing data that actually matter. That is not enough to carry the analysis.

The primary estimand is internally contradictory. It is defined as \(P(\text{Resolve is first and occurs after 72h})\), then called “equivalent” to \(1-\mathrm{CIF}_E(72)\), then described as the probability of not resolving by the SLA. Those are three different quantities. \(1-\mathrm{CIF}_E(72)\) also includes competing events by 72h and cases still unresolved at 72h. The case accounting follows whichever definition is convenient: a withdrawal at 27h is said to contribute to Estimand 1, which fits \(1-\mathrm{CIF}\) and contradicts the late-resolution probability. The same mix-up is repeated in the analysis plan, sensitivity claims, calibration rule, and code, where `1 - survival_function_at_time(72)` is printed as \(P(\text{Resolve}>72)\).

Supplied cases are misread. S15 is open at 35h, not 9h. S31 is B/P2 and 23h old with a 3h gap, not a 3h-old P1 case. The “2/12” non-resolve count lists five IDs, including cohort-B S30. The later “5/16” and “6/16” counts are also wrong: A has 15 cases and five non-resolve terminals, all before 72h; B has five, with S32 at 96h.

The state diagram omits observed transitions, including `Screen→AutoCheck`, `Screen→Resolve`, `Screen→Escalate`, `Screen→Reject/Withdraw`, `AutoCheck→Escalate`, and `Rework→Resolve`. Leakage is identified and then waived: full-prefix `has_escalate` and repeated-review indicators are still treated as acceptable baseline CIF covariates.

Section 8 does not construct the supplied table. Lengths and values disagree (`duration_or_age_h` has 33 entries and scrambles S29–S32; gaps and status lists are short or shifted), so the frame cannot be built. The lifelines call is not the real CIF API, `get_index` is not valid, competing events are collapsed into `event_observed=False`, and the PM4Py object is not a usable `parse_event_log_string` / `get_variants` construction. Turnbull is the wrong name for this censoring problem. Withdrawal/rejection and gap-driven informative censoring are not actually varied in the sensitivity design.
