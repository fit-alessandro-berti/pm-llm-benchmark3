3.5

The write-up has the required headings and a few correct point estimates, but the reconstruction of the hard cases, the station metrics, the bottleneck claim, and the code are not reliable.

Fully observed simple durations are often right: P02–P04, P06, P08–P10, and P12–P14, plus S1 for P16, P17, P19, and P20. Wait is separated from service in the definitions, open activities are described as right-censored, and C2’s 10:55–11:10 outage is noted. That is the usable part.

The mandatory cases are wrong or internally inconsistent. P11’s S1 interval is observed (10:40–11:05; wait 75, service 25); the table treats the start as missing and gives S1 wait [75, 100] and service [25, 50]. P05’s S2 service is given as 55 minutes, which would start near 09:20, before S1 ends at 09:25 and while C1 is still on P03 until 09:45; feasible S2 service is at most 30 minutes (start no earlier than 09:45, end 10:15). P11’s S2 service of 60 minutes overlaps P08 on C2 (11:15–12:00); the feasible start is no earlier than 12:00, so service is at most 25 minutes. P07’s S1 end is known, but the flag says the end is missing and both S1 columns are left as `MISSING` instead of bounds. I1 is not free from P03’s end at 08:55 until P09: P05 occupies it through 09:25.

Censoring is mixed up with sojourn. P15 has finished S1, so S1 service is finite; the row treats it as `[elapsed, ∞)` and the note calls 125 minutes of arrival-to-end time “service,” while the cell says 105. P15’s S2 start is known (13:30), so S2 wait is 75 minutes and elapsed S2 service to 14:00 is 30; the table gives wait `[90, ∞)`. P17’s S2 wait is the observed 65 minutes (12:50–13:55) and elapsed service is 5; the table censors wait at 75 and the note calls those 5 minutes waiting. P16, P18, P19, and P20 are waiting, not in service, yet S2 service is `OPEN`. Their wait lower bounds are 85, 50, 35, and 15; the table repeats 75, while some notes happen to be right and the P16 note says 15. P18’s S1 wait is 100, not 90. P21’s S1 wait is 100 and elapsed service is 30; the row says 120 and the note says 70. P22’s censored S1 wait is 110, not 135, and service has not started. P01’s wait is the reported 5 minutes; `[0, 5]` is neither that value nor the full ±2.5 rounding range, and rounding is not applied anywhere else.

Section 4 does not describe the log. External arrivals are 13, 8, and 1 in 08:00–10:00, 10:00–12:00, and 12:00–14:00, not 10, 6, and 6. Those counts are also copied onto S2, whose arrivals are S1 completions. S1 completions in those windows are about 6, 7, and 7, not 10, 4, and 2. S2 completions after 12:00 include P10, P11, P12, P13, and P14, not zero. Busy time of 380–400 minutes in a two-server, 120-minute window is about 160% under the stated utilization formula, not 95–100%. The 12:00–14:00 S2 row then calls 0–5% utilization an overwhelmed bottleneck. Queue length is never separated from WIP. Single WIP figures such as 2.5 and 4.0 are not tied to a shown trajectory.

The downstream-shift story is only a hypothesis. I2 runs almost continuously from 08:10 to 13:45 in 25–30 minute jobs with about 5-minute gaps, and observed S2 services are often longer than S1, so both stations are loaded and S2 has less capacity. The outage is about 15 minutes and is not shown to reorder the constraint. Section 6 names optimistic, central, and pessimistic scenarios but recomputes nothing, then declares the bottleneck ranking stable.

The code does not meet the reproducibility spec. `MISSING` and blank both become `None`. P17’s S2 start `13:55` is stored on P18. P11’s observed S1 start 10:40 is overwritten with 10:50 and called a central imputation; P05’s 08:55 is the edge of the feasible window, not its midpoint. `pm4py.insert_case_service_waiting_time` and `pm4py.insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` are never called. `pm4py.convert_log_to_time_intervals` is neither called nor justified in a comment. The WIP test compares datetimes with the string `"OPEN"`, counts missing-start cases after completion, and the busy-time subtraction includes null starts. Little’s Law is applied to 08:30–09:30, which still contains P05’s missing start, uses 4 arrivals against the wrong case list, and never compares `L/λ` with observed sojourn. A9 also writes `L = W`.
