The POWL model is a faithful completion-trace encoding of the narrative: direct edges capture the split prerequisites, the route and supplier stay unordered with the trial, the release join is complete, and the loop is `(Execute, Inspect)(Adjust (Execute, Inspect))*`. The process tree is a real conservative under-approximation, T5 actually separates the two languages, and the other witness verdicts match the models. The comparison, canonical choice, and validation caveats are precise.

The construction code still calls `powl.order.add_transitive_edges()` and `powl.order.is_strict_partial_order()`. Those methods are not part of PM4Py’s `BinaryRelation` API, so the script fails after an otherwise valid `powl` is built. Transitive edges are also unnecessary: precedence is the closure of the stored relation, and materializing it does not change the order.

The forbidden witnesses miss the required cross-branch case. N1 breaks the local edge Contain → Plan. N2’s earliest visible failure is the loop exit after Adjust, with approval-after-release only as a second fault at the same event. N3 is an incomplete high-risk AND. None has, as its first violation, Execute Trial after only one of Plan and Design, which is the distinctive join.

Section 2 never states the essential incomparabilities of route and supplier with Plan and with Design. The tree is coarser than the allowed under-approximation policy: the self-critique admits a duplication-based tree would keep more safe traces, including T5. N2’s reason, “no passing inspection has occurred,” is slightly loose, because an Inspect event did occur and is only interpreted as a failure because Adjust follows it.
