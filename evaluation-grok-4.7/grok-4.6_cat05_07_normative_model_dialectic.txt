The brief is unusually strong: both sides are actually argued, concessions are specific, the matrix covers all 15 cases, authority is ranked instead of averaged, and MIXED is used only for SUP-715 with a stated reason. That is not enough for a near-flawless score.

The revised POWL misreads PD-3 and FD-7. “Quality may occur up to two business days after provisional activation” and “provisional activation may precede” quality and manual approval are permissions with deadlines, not a mandatory after-activation order. M1 forces quality after `Activate_Supplier` on the auto route and both quality and manual approval after activation on the emergency route. A legal trace such as sanctions, quality, `Auto_Approve`, activate, or an emergency trace with quality before activation and retrospective manual approval, fits no branch. That under-generation is presented as deliberate route semantics, not as a disclosed approximation. The same answer’s data rules are closer to the texts than the net is.

The required executable model is not reliably executable. `standard_route.order.add_edge(standard_route.nodes[0], decision_std)` assumes insertion-ordered, indexable `nodes`. In PM4Py, `StrictPartialOrder.nodes` is commonly a set, so this raises `TypeError` before M1 exists. The RMI node should have been bound to a name. The silent skip on `Register_Supplier` is also global, so every route, including standard onboarding, can omit registration; only the reactivation quality-skip is clearly marked unsafe.

SUP-702 is classified `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` at high confidence even though the answer itself treats automated master-data validation as a PD-3 residual control and as unproved. The stated burden requires residual controls to have run, but the reversal conditions omit a missing validation. The system judgment likewise drops the nine auto-approvals with missing eligibility attributes after section 1 correctly says missing eligibility is indeterminate, not compliant and not noncompliant.

Smaller traceability slips: SUP-703 is not the same token pattern as SUP-702, because 703 contains `Notify_Requester` and 702 does not; FD-7 is “those 72 hours” only by rounding; the EUR 180,000 / EUR 20,000 comparison imports a cap from a directive that did not apply in June; SUP-710’s certificate “remained valid” on the facts given, rather than merely being “offered.” SUP-706’s date is never stated, though the mutable late link independently supports an indeterminate notification finding. SUP-711 as `COMPLIANT` is defensible only after deduplication; the raw trace still repeats a non-looped activity.

Case outcomes for SUP-701, 703, 704, 705, 707, 708, 709, 712, and 714 are well supported, expiry is not excused, and CS-9 four-eyes is not confused with the POWL language. The overlay table, alias/delegation/COI split, and immutable notification and eligibility snapshot proposal are the right architecture. The grade stops at 7.5 because the normative net contradicts the overlay language it is supposed to implement, and the code that is required to construct that net can fail on the RMI edge.
