8.1

The pipeline is complete, internally consistent, and unusually careful. The 17 events, 12 objects, 35 source E2O rows, 12 O2O rows, and 18 derived rows all match the narrative. Flattened traces, DFG edge counts (12 Order edges for 14 rows minus 2 cases), the 250-vs-500 payment double count, ET–OT weights summing to 35, the e09 expansion from 2 to 30 ordered pairs, and the 58-to-158 interaction jump all cross-check. Source vs derived relations are separated cleanly, the e08→O100 inference is flagged, and the OC-DFG distinction between one event couple and two Order traversals on Reserve Stock→Pack Package is correct.

It is not nearly flawless.

- Alternative 5 says item-level dispatch/confirm propagation yields traces “with no convergence.” That is false. I1 and I3 already share Reserve Stock and Pack Package, and both would also share P1’s dispatch and D1’s confirmation. That confuses “each item has one package” (no divergence) with convergence.
- Section 5 says the activities “where colours meet” are Pack, Dispatch, Issue Invoice, and Record Payment. Add Item and Reserve Stock are also multi-type, and Reserve Stock is the first O100/O200 synchronization. The sentence presents an incomplete set as the set.
- The OTG descendant list (Order→Item, Order→Package, Order→Invoice, Item→Package, Package→Delivery, Invoice→Payment) is asserted as the result of a run, but the predicate is never defined tightly enough to justify excluding the instance-level inversion I1 starts before O200 even though they co-occur. Inheritance, by contrast, is exact under the stated hand-off rule, including V1→M2 only.
- The interactions “summary” is described as 58 unaggregated ordered event-pairs. That arithmetic matches a self-join of `relations`, but a summary keyed by object pair would collapse repeated co-occurrences (O100–O200 three times) and would not have 58 rows. The qualitative co-occurrence claims remain true.
- Smaller nits: alternative 1 flattens O100’s two Add Item events into one schematic trace; “one event node per activity” is loose; the propagated Order trace omits the new Confirm Delivery→Confirm Delivery self-loop; `discover_otg` / `discover_etot` fallbacks are described in prose and not isolated in code.

Primary model, validation, and flattened/OC-DFG reading are strong. The convergence slip and the over-precise library claims keep this below a near-flawless score.
