Those merits are undercut by concrete defects.

The piece substantially overshoots the 1,700–2,600-word target (excluding code). Section 4 is also not the requested compact list. Length is not neutral here: several rows restate the same conditional classification three or four times.

There is a real count inconsistency that is never confronted. Directly-follows successors are exclusive, yet `Create Order -> Validate Order` is 98 of 100 newly created orders while `Create Order -> Manual Compliance Review` is 3 orders. 98+3 cannot sit inside 100 exclusive immediate successors. The write-up treats the 3 reviews as a clean unregulated path and never asks whether they fall outside the cohort, whether the summary double-counts, or whether the model is inconsistent. That is exactly the population/abstraction question the task demands.

Related sloppiness: rank 11 says the Validate counts “exceed 100,” but 72 and 83 do not. Only a mutually exclusive sum does, and that point is not made cleanly. Rank 4’s lead interpretation that two packages “shipped unpacked” is too strong when ET–OT already shows 200 `Pack Package`–`Package` and 200 `Dispatch Package`–`Package` links; the better hypotheses (intervening event, timestamp order, different package sets) are buried under the weaker one. `Validate Order -> Issue Invoice` is a present, N2-anticipated intermediate, yet its deviation class is “typical-pattern absence,” which names the wrong phenomenon.

T6 is “Forbidden behavior (or Insufficient evidence)” at medium confidence in the table, then a “robust” genuine anomaly in section 8, while the batch-merge alternative is dropped. `Sensor Heartbeat`–`Invoice` is correctly a suspected join collision, but Medium-High and rank 3 overstates operational harm relative to the clearer mandatory gaps at N4 and E6. The code sketch does call the six required functions, keeps raw diagnostics, and parks deontic status outside the bare graphs. The classifier itself is weaker than the prose. Presence is Boolean, so “98 of 100” cannot be represented. Any mandatory `eventually_follows` miss is routed to abstraction mismatch, including true absence. The worked example passes `observed_present=True` with `scope_fully_met=False` for N1 and still lands on abstraction mismatch. T6’s data-quality uncertainty is not encoded, so the sketch hardens a conclusion the essay leaves open.

Section 6’s seven gaps are real and each has a risk, a clarification, and a status. Section 8 does name robust claims, norm holes, data-quality dependence, and what summaries cannot decide, but it overstates how robust T6 and the cancel-then-dispatch root cause are.

Net: normative separation and uncertainty handling are good; calibration, count logic, classifier fidelity, and the length constraint are not.
