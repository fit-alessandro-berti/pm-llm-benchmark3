The clearest logical fault is internal inconsistency on G4. Confidence 96 is attached to “ordinary serial processing,” while the same row’s counterevidence says nothing distinguishes that from deliberate sequential batching, and section 7 later admits intentional batching is unproved. G1’s 85 for a “genuine” fixture batch and several other scores in the mid-90s are similarly tight for fragments of four to seven cases; the non-calibration disclaimer does not remove the overclaim. G5 and G7’s “counterevidence” cells mostly limit the second story or an over-reading, rather than challenge the stated top explanation. For G1 and G2, the second-ranked explanation is largely a refinement of the first, not a real competitor. The two `merge_distance` settings cannot separate these clusters, because every within-cluster gap is zero or negative; that limitation is interpreted honestly, but the sensitivity test still adds little. Conditional serial reconstructions for G2 and G3 are labeled as scenarios, yet they read more identified than the exclusive-worker bounds they actually are.
