4.5

The memo has the required seven-part shape and several defensible keeps (C002, C005, C007, C008, C011, C016, and C001 to a security specialist). It is not a reliable policy: the worked assignments contradict the scoring rule, break stated hard constraints, and misstate the skill matrix.

The score function cannot implement the objectives. The SLA term is identical for every candidate, so its 40% weight never changes a choice. Training adds at most 0.05, which cannot enforce a 20% quota or beat a one-step skill gap. Overtime is a small multiplier inside a 10% workload term, and it is waived for “high” as well as “urgent,” so it does not encode “avoid high overtime unless the case is urgent and no safer resource exists.” Hours remaining, batching, supervision, complexity, reassignment history, and bottleneck avoidance are absent from selection. Continuity is listed as a hard constraint and then implemented as a 15% preference. Tie-breaks and the four-assignment cap are described, but exception handling does not update counts, and skill 0 is not consistently excluded.

The queue table does not follow that algorithm, and several rows are factually wrong. C004, C012, and C014 give non-urgent work to high-overtime resources (Bruno, Iris, David) while safer skilled capacity exists; David already has 14 open cases and 3.0 hours. C012 calls Iris the current owner; the owner is Jonas. C006 is normal priority, not low, and Jonas’s training priority is security, so it is not a quality-training assignment. C009 is the diagnosis training case owned by Amal, but it is given to Eva, whose training priority is security. C003, a repeated diagnosis with two reassignments and complexity 0.66, is given to Amal at skill 1 even though Celine is a low-overtime diagnosis specialist. C010 treats Bruno, approval skill 2 with training priority quality, as a supervised approval trainee; skill 1 may prepare and may not finalize, and his overtime status is not urgent. C015 assigns Jonas, whose security score is 0, not 1; supervision is allowed only for security skill 1–2, and Eva is also security 0. The two batchable quality cases are split while both reasons call them batch candidates. Section 4 then says the policy avoids loading Bruno and David.

The comparison covers every requested dimension but does not apply either baseline to C001–C016, and its “low rework / high fairness / 20% training / avoids overtime” claims are not true of this table. The worker note is clear and non-punitive, then teaches the illegal Jonas security assignment and the overtime claim the table breaks. The audit list names the right phenomena, but several thresholds are arbitrary, handover is confused with reassignment count, and the working-together network is not a workload Gini. The PM4Py sketch uses real discovery and SNA calls, yet `filter_event_attribute_values` and `filter_case_performance` are given illegal arguments, service time is called with the wrong signature, training and deviation rates are event counts rather than cases, the skill matrix is never joined, and `discover_performance_dfg` and `discover_batches` are missing. There is no procedure that learns handover, rework, or service-time penalties from the log.
