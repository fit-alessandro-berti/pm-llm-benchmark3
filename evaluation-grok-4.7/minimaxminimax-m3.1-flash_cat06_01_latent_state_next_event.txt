7.5

The analysis is strong on state identification, attribute-sensitive divergence between C6-A and C6-B, migration delay in C6-D, and the limits of the directly-follows graph. It is not nearly flawless.

C6-A treats Supplier Analysis as mandatory. Rule 5 requires two or more related complaints on the same lot or supplier component. This case is one complaint linked to two devices of one lot, plus three “similar” complaints that are not stated to share that lot. Calling the obligation mandatory, including in the cross-case discussion, overstates the evidence and can wrongly block disposition.

The C6-B claim that another evidence request occurs in “only 101 of roughly 1,000 cases” uses the wrong denominator. Those 101 transitions are about 30% of the listed Evidence Review successors (101 + 173 + 68), so the graph is used to understate repeat evidence cycles.

`process_enabled` is true on every prediction, including Close, Disposition, and a third evidence request that the prose itself says cannot occur until a predecessor completion is logged. That blurs the required distinction between an enabled action and the next visible event. Downstream outcomes are also given sizable probability as the immediate next event (especially Close at 0.08–0.24 beside review at 0.62–0.86). C6-C and C6-D midpoint masses near 0.78 leave a large residual unnamed, and C6-D’s rank 2 bundles several different log lines into one probability.
