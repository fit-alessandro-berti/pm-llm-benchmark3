7.3

The control analysis is strong and mostly faithful to the log, but the required checking code is materially wrong and the write-up overclaims a few facts.

Sections 1–6 reason from objectives, timing, independence, coverage, recoverability, and collusion rather than from amount or raw four-eyes form. The six threat rows, the four-tier sufficiency test, and the rejection of expected-loss equivalence are concrete. Case labels are generally well chosen: AP-202 and AP-208 fail despite very different outcomes; AP-204 fails despite distinct user IDs and a fabricated receipt; AP-205 is stopped before completion; AP-209 stays indeterminate; AP-203 and AP-207 can meet the objective through ED-1. Timestamp arithmetic (AP-201 at about 25 hours, AP-206 at 18h52m, AP-207’s 35-minute and 80-minute gaps, AP-208 at 24 hours after settlement) matches the log. Treating post-release, pre-settlement manager action as hybrid, with a settlement-hold caveat, is the right semantics. Monitoring covers every requested signal and separates alerts from proof.

The code does not do what section 7 claims. In current PM4Py, `filter_four_eyes_principle` keeps cases in which the two activities have different resources. The script treats that result as the violating set and states that polarity as a 2.7+ fact. The flag is therefore inverted: AP-204 and AP-209 become the “violations,” and the same-user releases drop out. The task asked for violating completed payments and for an explicit version caveat; this note asserts the wrong contract. The `groupby(..., as_index=False).apply(..., include_groups=False)` plus `rename` to `case_id` is also not reliable: in common pandas versions the group key remains the index, so the merge raises `KeyError` or drops every event field. That breaks an executable requirement. AP-208’s token time of 14:50 and several vendor ages are invented; the later disclaimer does not make the evidence table safe. `POST_SETTLEMENT_OR_LATE` is also the fallback when settlement time is missing, so AP-202 is labeled that way without a settlement comparison.

Smaller judgment problems: AP-203 and AP-207 are said to satisfy all seven ED-1 conditions at high confidence, while L3+ rank is simultaneously missing evidence. AP-201’s corrective capacity is called high even though m04’s review depth is unknown and the audit is outside the recall window. AP-208’s residual risk is “Critical” and its severity only “High,” with no scale that makes those words compatible. AP-206 is a reasonable `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET`, but the logged token is after `Prepare_Payment`, so the formal ED-1 gate failed on the record that exists.
