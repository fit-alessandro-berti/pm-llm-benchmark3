6.5

The assessment covers every required heading, uses most figures accurately, and respects the main constraints: raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination, case mix is not treated as a full excuse, risk scores are not treated as neutral, and support steps are kept as possible burdens rather than targets for removal. The dimension-by-dimension discussion, mechanisms, limitations, and mitigation trade-offs are generally on target.

It still makes unsupported and internally inconsistent claims. The statement that the Completeness Check → Request Missing Documents wait “remains elevated after controlling for documentation_completeness” is not supported: the waiting times are raw group medians, while the adjusted result is only a higher probability of that step. Section 2 never clearly separates disparity, discrimination, legitimate routing, and uncertainty, and “average treatment effect” language is inappropriate for this observational comparison. Variant analysis underuses V2 and V6 and the variant cycle times that connect path mix to delay.

The PM4Py plan would not run as written. `discover_performance_dfg` is unpacked into four values, `filter_event_attribute_values` is called with the wrong argument and a bare string, variant IDs are undefined, and `get_all_case_durations` is mislabeled as waiting time. The closing assessment says the result is indeterminate and then says the process “is not procedurally fair in practice,” while attributing a “substantial portion” of the residual to measurement bias. Measurement bias undermines the adjustment; it does not identify the cause of the residual.
