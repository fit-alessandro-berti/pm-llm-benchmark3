**6.0**

The pipeline is complete and referentially usable: all 17 events, all 12 objects, timezone-aware timestamps, copied activity/timestamp on E2O rows, the required O2O families, and the requested validation and discovery calls are present. Order, Delivery, and Invoice trace shapes follow from those E2O rows, and the weak Payment flattening is identified correctly. The write-up is not reliable as an analysis of that same model.

Qualifier semantics do not hold together. Section 1 defines `container_of` as the logistics container and says `shared_by` covers P1 packing; the code puts `container_of` on Orders, uses `shared_by` only to make O100 primary on an equally shared reservation, and labels Orders as `operand` on Add Item. Record Payment attaches `partially_settles` / `completes_settlement` to the Invoice row, while O2O attaches those verbs to the Payment. The audit then calls the invoice role `settles`. Section 1 also calls the type names snake_case, then uses `Order`, `Item`, and so on, and states an “upstream on the left” O2O rule that Invoice→Order and Payment→Invoice violate. `total: 250` is added so M2 can be treated as completing settlement, although both payments are only given as partial amounts.

Flattening and interaction claims contradict the relations. Package cases are only events whose E2O object is that package, so P1 is Pack Package → Dispatch Package; Confirm Delivery stays on D1. The predicted Package trace adds Confirm Delivery, and the Order “misleading readings” (O200 packed as P2, O100 confirms D2) are events those Order traces do not contain. Items on pack/reserve are direct E2O links, not O2O-only facts. Dispatch does not touch Order, but the ET–OT list says it does. Co-occurrence counts are wrong: (O100, I1), (O200, I3), and (O100, O200) each share three events, including e07, yet the summary reports the first pair twice and the order pair only at reserve and V1. The Petri-net alternative in which one Pack Package splits into both deliveries does not match two pack events and two deliveries. Source-minimal and O2O-propagated alternatives, and the claim that an OC-net still recovers dropped E2O links, overstate what those relation edits would leave in the tables.
