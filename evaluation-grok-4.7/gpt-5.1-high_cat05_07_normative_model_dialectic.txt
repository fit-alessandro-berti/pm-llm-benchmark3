6.5

The case analysis is serious and often well-judged, but the answer is not close to flawless. Internal standards conflict, several evidence claims are stronger than the record, and the revised POWL contradicts the overlays the judgment itself treats as valid.

The classifications of SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-707, SUP-708, SUP-712, SUP-713, and SUP-714 are defensible. Expiry is treated as legally meaningful, desirability is refused as a substitute for amendment, and resource breaches that fit M0 are not ignored. Aggregates are cited with the right counts. Prosecution and defense both concede real points, and section 5 does reject specific overclaims.

The burden rule is incoherent. Section 1.3 says behavior that matches an overlay in intent, despite weak documentation or ambiguous legal status, is still a justified exception. That rule would pull SUP-705 and SUP-714 toward justification. The later judgments correctly go the other way, so the stated standard is not the standard applied. “Preponderance” is also a poor fit for a controlled standard whose exceptions need proved authority, eligibility, and time window.

The prosecution brief undermines itself. It first treats PD-3 and FD-7 as real exceptions, then calls SUP-702 formally noncompliant because PD-3 supposedly cannot override CS-9, and describes PD-3 as barely applying. SUP-702 is squarely inside the stated window and eligibility: low-risk, DE, approved group, 2026-06-15, next-business-day quality review, complete eligibility fields. The compliance observer’s later statement was that she had not approved weakening CS-9, not merely that she had not intended a permanent weakening. The prosecution also fails the required concession on data defects: SUP-706 and SUP-711 are used mainly as possible concealment, while the CRM break and duplicate source transaction are established independently of misconduct.

Model semantics are blurred on rejection. CS-9 requires sanctions and quality before activation. It does not say quality is required before rejection, and it expressly allows an abbreviated pre-screen while leaving M0’s scope ambiguous. M0 is stricter because both checks sit before the XOR. The matrix collapses those into one “M0/CS-9” breach. SUP-710 as MIXED has the same problem: absent a reactivation rule, the proved defect is scope, and the control breach remains conditional. SUP-709’s low downstream risk is established; its governance classification is not.

Evidence is over-read in places that matter. The SUP-702 trace does not contain automated master-data validation, yet the defense asserts that it occurred. “Initial spend” below EUR 20,000 does not prove the FD-7 cap held until retrospective approval. SUP-706 has no case date, so attributing it to the 1 July CRM cutover is an assumption. The 289 “eligible” auto-approvals are then generalized into sound handling without proof of validation, quality timing, notification, or separation of duties. Identity resolution for SUP-712 is stipulated as an assumption and then given high confidence.

The formal revision is the largest failure. The low-risk route places quality inside `checks_choice` and orders that block before `Auto_Approve` and activation. PD-3’s distinctive permission is quality up to two business days after provisional activation. Added DECLARE rules cannot remove a POWL edge, so the layering cannot repair it. The emergency route requires a second, unobserved `Activate_Supplier` after retrospective quality and approval; FD-7 and SUP-704/705 have one activation, followed by quality and approval. The model therefore excludes the two cases judged `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`.

The same `Transition` and submodel objects are reused across XOR branches, so the construction is not a sound POWL even if the objects instantiate. `Request_More_Info` is not repeatable, although `Operator.LOOP` is the direct encoding; `SilentTransition` is imported and unused. The limitation note does not disclose the post-activation quality error. The reject branch still demands quality, leaving the pre-screen scope fix outside the executable model. The overlay artifact is an empty schema, not a normalized table of CS-9, PD-3, and FD-7. The event proposal has no spend or transaction amount, so the EUR 20,000 rule is not checkable. “Withdrawals” are reduced to reversing provisional activation and do not cover abandoned onboardings.

Section 4 and section 5 also disagree. The SUP-702 reversal condition is a future amendment that would create a justified exception, after section 5 has already assigned that label. That is a traceability defect in the required matrix, not a wording quibble.

The result is a capable narrative judgment with a normative model that cannot express the authorized pilot or emergency languages, plus a proof standard the author does not follow. Those are central faults under the stated criteria, not polish.
