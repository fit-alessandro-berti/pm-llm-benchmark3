The write-up covers the required six sections and avoids the weakest failure modes: it excludes I-03 on stage grounds, separates I-05’s unchanged repeat from I-07’s new log, and tries to price queue harm rather than rank on `p_now`. Those merits do not make the policy coherent or auditable.

The decision rule is not the rule that is applied. Expected net benefit never uses the segment probability adjustments, yet I-02’s rationale claims Strategic Enterprise overprediction was “corrected.” The two-alert gate (effect midpoint above 0.20) would block I-02 (midpoint 0.15) and allow I-09 (midpoint 0.24); the allocation does the opposite. Time to deadline and the option value of waiting are absent, so 8–10 hour triage cases outrank a 1.5 hour case.

Spillover is not the stated model. The brief is about six non-intervened diagnosis-stage cases per slot; the scores monetize only the two or three visible cases, and Section 2’s flat €1,200 charge does not match Section 3. The impact footnote (3 × 3 × 0.01 × €17,500 = €1,575) does not generate the printed €1,400–€2,800 band. Optimistic and base share that same spillover range, so they are not the required scenarios. Adverse never sets one selected case to little or no benefit. False-alarm burdens (about €7,600 for I-04, I-08, and I-02) are deducted in ranking and then omitted from “net impact,” overstating portfolio value by that amount. Labeling the optimistic row high confidence treats the supplied ranges as known effects.

I-08 is selected on a midpoint even though its lower bound is negative before spillover (0.05 × €100,000 − €450 − €5,000). All three chosen cases are in the segment the model overpredicts; SME underprediction never changes a score. The fairness safeguards do not acknowledge that today’s book starves every other segment, and a 5% slot-share trigger would not catch that. The causal plan is matched before/after comparison under selection on the same score, including a “real-time” effect from single swarms, which cannot identify the intervention effect the monitoring section says it will use to change the rule.
