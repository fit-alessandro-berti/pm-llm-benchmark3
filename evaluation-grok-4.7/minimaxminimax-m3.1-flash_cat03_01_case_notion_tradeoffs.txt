The final write-up is specific, identifier-heavy, and right about most of the hard counts: order traces 12/9/13/8 (42 rows), item 24/16, package 12/10, delivery 6/6, invoice 7/7, payment 4 singletons, and the activity inflations (Dispatch and Confirm 6 vs 3, Pack 6 vs 4, Invoice 4 vs 3, Payment 6 vs 4). Timings check out (order cycle times ~26h, D1 5h, D2 4h55m, D3 22h45m, V1 30m, V2 20h55m, V3 21h20m, P4 23h40m). D1’s false pack→dispatch adjacencies (O1 e12→e15, O3 e14→e16), D2, D4, D6–D10, the multiplicity identity, and decision B are strong and internally usable.

It is not nearly flawless. D5 misstates both order traces: O2 and O3 are Issue → Pay → Confirm → Pay, not Issue → Pay → Pay, so there is no order-level Record Payment self-loop; that loop exists only on V2. Section 2 even describes Confirm→Pay, then D5 and S1 contradict it, and S1 has the suppression effect backwards (dropping e24 onto O1 would create Pay→Pay on O2, not collapse it). Delivery is called the only non-copying view in the same table that correctly shows Invoice 7/7 and Payment 4/4. D3’s “truth” of 4 package–order incidences is wrong (P1–O1, P2–O1, P3–O2, P3–O3, P4–O3, P4–O4 = 6), so the 1.5 packs-per-order figure is the incidence rate, not a false one. Conformance is inconsistent: prose says order 0% and invoice 33%, the coded formula yields 25% and 67%, and the hardcoded table repeats 0.0 and 0.333. “Near-clones” for O2 vs O3 overstates two clearly different sequences. Confirm→Pay is listed under “self-loops.” The main loop calls `pm4py.ocel_flatting`, which would crash, and the interaction-summary counts are the O2O edge counts, not event co-occurrence. Convergence/divergence are defined explicitly, but “divergence” is used for loss/splitting, so the matrix under-rates the false directly-follows problem that D1 actually found.
