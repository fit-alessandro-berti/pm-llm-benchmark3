6.5

The framework is serious and covers every required section: stage ineligibility, calibration bands, an externality hurdle, alert-state logic, label contamination, and quantitative monitoring triggers. I-03 is correctly refused, and I-05 is correctly suppressed while I-07 is re-opened. The allocation then breaks the rule that was just stated, and one gate-relevant net-value sign is wrong.

The Tier A rule requires \(NDE_{low}>0\), \(NDE_{mid}>MSC\), and \(T\le 4\)h or imminent loss of eligibility. I-04 has \(T=10\)h and is still labeled Tier A and given the 13:30 slot. That is not a close call under the written rule; it is a long-horizon Triage case that the same rule puts in Tier B. The withdrawal condition (drop only if \(p_{adj}<0.30\)) does not repair it, because a better claimant cannot displace I-04.

I-06 is the case that should. It is already in Diagnosis Started with 4.0h left, and verification is supposed to finish within 30 minutes, before slot 2. The plan instead makes it first claimant of a reserve released at 15:00. At 15:00 only about 1.0h remains; a 45-minute swarm ends around 15:45, leaving about 15 minutes for resolution and customer validation. That collides with the feasibility gate, yet the reserve row in the impact table still adds the unhaircut midpoint effect 0.225. Time-criticality ordering is stated and then not applied.

I-08’s reported lower net of about −€0.2k is inconsistent with the stated inputs. With \(p_{adj}\) midpoint \(0.345\), scale \(0.345/0.43\), low adjusted absolute reduction about 0.040, \(V=€100{,}000\), and false-alarm term \((1-0.345)\times€5{,}000=€3{,}275\):

\[
0.040\times 100{,}000-450-3{,}275\approx +€275.
\]

The upper figure (€8.3k) and midpoint (€4.3k) match that same false-alarm term, so the minus sign is a local arithmetic error. The text then uses the wrong sign to say the robust gate fails. Under the formula, the lower bound is small but positive and the midpoint (€4.3k) clears the €2.4k hurdle; the valid reason to wait is \(T=8\)h, which is not the reason given.

The hurdle itself is not robust to the spillover range used elsewhere. \(MSC\approx 6\times 0.02\times€20{,}000=€2{,}400\) is a midpoint. At the top of the stated base band, \(6\times 0.03\times€20{,}000=€3{,}600\) per slot, I-02’s €2.5k midpoint fails and I-09’s €3.9k barely survives. Section 3 still says three slots are justified because I-09 stays positive at its lower bound. I-09’s lower direct net is about €1.9k, which is already below €2.4k before any adverse congestion. I-02 clears the point hurdle by about €100 and is negative in the adverse scenario; committing it ahead of a pending I-06 check is fragile.

Two further policy inconsistencies are smaller but real. The 1.0h eligibility floor leaves about 15 minutes after the swarm, while the I-07 discussion treats 45 minutes as not enough. The €40k value cap is offered as a fairness control and is not used in the allocation that the cap was meant to discipline. Scaling absolute risk reduction by \(p_{adj}/p_{now}\) also conflates outcome-model miscalibration with bias in the effect range; it happens not to flip today’s selected set, but it is an extra penalty, concentrated on Strategic Enterprise, that the evidence does not identify.

Section 3’s main arithmetic otherwise tracks: adjusted effects, \(3\times€450\), probability-weighted false-alarm terms (€250, €255, €1,210), 0.52 expected direct breaches, and the €7.2k spillover point are reproducible. “After the €2k false-alarm term” for I-04 is still the wrong description of a €1.2k term. Stacking low benefit with high spillover, and stretching \(s\) to 0.05, makes the adverse envelope harsher than the stated 15–30 minute congestion band strictly supports.

Sections 4–6 are the strongest. The I-05 versus I-07 contrast is the right distinction (refresh noise versus a new log), with suppression kept visible. Fairness safeguards are operational: a lower-value slot share, treatment flags, training that does not treat swarmed non-breaches as ordinary negatives, segment-specific calibration, and spillover booked to the groups who absorb it. The monitoring plan has usable tripwires (bias, imputation rate, reserve utilization, fairness ratio, borderline randomization, discontinuity and doubly robust checks, shadow running before a rule change). Those parts do not offset an allocation that mis-signs a gate value and then violates its own timing and tier rules on the two cases where those rules change who should receive a slot.
