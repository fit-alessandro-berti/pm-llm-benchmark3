The forecast is structurally strong: the join is treated as \(\max(C,M)\), Friday analyst release times (15:54–16:24 and 16:42–17:30), the 2.1–3.4 leftover analyst-hours, the Sunday 12:00–20:00 incubation window, the Monday chemistry floor, and the Saturday QA hours used only to drain the queue are all defensible. The quantile section is not coherent. The P80-like window runs through Wednesday 17:30, while the P90-like window starts Wednesday 10:30, so the same clock time is both an 80th-percentile and a 90th-percentile outcome. The 140–307 hour band is the support of a severe tail, not a P90 location; if laboratory investigations are about a tenth of outcomes, P90 sits near the start of that band, not out at 1 October. That conflation matters because the task is uncertainty communication.

Smaller envelope problems accumulate. Monday 09:00 needs QA plus release in roughly half an hour after an ~08:10–08:30 join, against a stated median service of 1.5 h. QA clearance is median-based for a right-skewed service law (median 1.5 h, 80th percentile 3.5 h), so Monday backlog is biased low even though the shortcut is disclosed. The smooth-path spill is cut at Tuesday 11:00–13:00, short of a late FIFO start plus a long review. “Tuesday 11:00 centrally” for a late rerun is near the 3 h rerun maximum, not the center. Weekend call-out is correctly minor on the smooth join, but bundling dispatch with readout is then under-weighted on the confirmation clock, where it shifts the extra 20–30 h incubation.
