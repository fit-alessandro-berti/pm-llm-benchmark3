4.8

The diagnosis table is directionally useful for G2–G8, and the bulk-submission account of zero service with inflated waiting is the right mechanism. That is not enough to carry the required analysis.

The performance numbers are unreliable. G4 waiting is not a constant 7 minutes: arrivals bunch from 11:58–12:02 and waits rise from 7 to about 19 minutes under a 4-minute single server, so “steady state” is the wrong second explanation. G8 waits are about 38–58 minutes, not 138–158, and only part of that is the safety hold; the rest is serial queueing. G6’s range is about 35–175 minutes, not 35–155. G3’s service figure of 286 minutes is simply false (the intervals are 28, 20, 13, and 6). G7’s recording lag is about 1–5 minutes, not 1–4 seconds.

Several interpretations contradict the evidence. G1 matches a four-item fixture and genuine simultaneous processing; calling physical concurrency implausible, advising a sequential split, and later grouping G1 with G6/G7 as a logging batch erases the distinction the task requires. For G2, completions 8, 14, 21, and 27 minutes after a shared start are cumulative if work is serial, so service-time variance is not “accurate.” G7 never notes that M02–M05 arrive after the recorded start, which is the clearest sign of coarsening and makes ordinary waiting times negative. G5 never notes a peak of four overlapping cases against a stated capacity of three. Section 7 then withholds “high confidence” from G6 and G7 despite the table’s own scores of 92 and 95.

The signature matrix is cluster-by-feature, not the requested hypothesis-by-signature matrix. Bias coverage skips utilization or bottleneck detection for several clusters, and the G7 interval `[14:00, 14:15]` is not a valid censoring model for an undocumented round-to-quarter-hour rule.

The required program cannot run. List multiplication binds to the wrong timestamp lists, so the frame lengths do not match. Second-precision parsing is applied to minute-level `recorded_at` values. `format_dataframe` is called with invalid argument names and then on an event log; traces are built as nested `EventLog`s; `BehavioralBatchesAlgorithm` is not a valid argument to `discover_batches`. Batch categories are never computed, and the “queue build-up” metric is an interarrival gap. The printed comparison is hardcoded and misstates G1.
