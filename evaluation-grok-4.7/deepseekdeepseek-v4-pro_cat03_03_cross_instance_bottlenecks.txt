6.4

The answer covers all eight required sections, uses concrete object IDs and timestamps, keeps machine capacity off the top of the ranking, and separates several counterfactuals from causal proof. Those strengths do not offset multiple factual and logical errors.

The FIX-A calculation is the clearest miss. CNC-2 is documented idle only from 10:05 to 10:47. J4’s process starts at 11:45, five minutes after FIX-A is released at 11:40, but nothing shows the machine stayed free from 10:47 to 11:45. Treating 10:05–11:45 as 1 h 40 min of tool delay, then restating it as “~1.7 h,” overclaims the evidence. The “extra 52 min” also mixes that interval with the separate 48-minute longest machine gap.

“No more than 2% rework” is false. Inspect → Rework has frequency 2 and Inspect → Pack frequency 4, so 2 of 6 jobs rework.

The adjacency list assigns J1 and J2 to CNC-2 without evidence. Only J4’s Machine Process is tied to CNC-2 and FIX-A. Narrative 1 invents an 08:00 staging time (readiness is 08:10) and states that OVEN-1 was available during the morning wait. The B2 record shows the oven idle; the B1 record does not.

Job Dispatch and shipment dispatch are conflated. The job DFG’s Pack → Dispatch median is 1.1 h for all six jobs, and no job duration exceeds 1.8 standard deviations above the mean. That makes it unlikely that J1’s 5 h 55 min S1 hold sits inside the job case. The 5 h 55 min figure is arithmetically right (13:30 to 19:25) but is not adjusted for the ordinary 25–35 minute post-pack dispatch lag given for S2 and S3.

RACK-7 is under-ranked. J4 is ready at 13:10, the rack is blocked until 14:35, and the oven is idle from 12:55, while the answer’s own text says the L7 release is probably not on B2’s critical path. Ranking that lot coupling above the rack constraint is inconsistent.

The CAL-3 perverse-incentive claim is backwards. TEST-1 is idle during recalibration, so removing that gap would raise TEST-1 utilization, not lower it.

Section 8 does not do what was asked. Six observations were required that would weaken the top-ranked explanation, batch synchronization. Five of the six attack other mechanisms.
