8.5

The audit is strong, complete, and correctly structured. It covers every top-12 feature, every sequence pattern, and every local-only feature; keeps queue length, elapsed time, and frozen complexity; does not delete region or channel; and separates predictive association, causation, actionability, and fairness. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated as later-legitimate but unavailable at `t0`, while `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as normatively impossible and same-timestamp-ambiguous. Case X and Y are recomputed without future outcomes, Case Z is invalidated, and the JSON matches the required schema. The validation suite and prioritized recommendations are concrete and include the limits of DFG, alignment, and footprint checks.

Deductions are for small but real defects. Total case duration is called a direct encoding of the 10-day settlement label, although closure time can diverge from settlement time once appeals and closure lag are included; it is still critical leakage, but the wording is too strong. `claimant_channel` is accepted as a credible driver while `initial_reserve` is quarantined, despite neither having an explicit as-of timestamp. One appeal cell says “after a settlement decision” rather than only after Partial or Deny. Section 2 leaves open an “unusual but valid” `Closed -> Damage` order, which the normative model does not allow. Reverse causality and post-treatment bias for appeals and final handler team are labeled unsupported rather than explained. Those gaps keep it short of nearly flawless.
