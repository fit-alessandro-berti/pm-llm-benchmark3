The decomposition is arithmetically exact and conceptually sound. Wall-clock flow is correctly \(T_c=U_c+H_c+D_c+P_c\) with \(J_c=\max_i e_{ci}\) and \(H_c=J_c-r_c\). Summing branch lags plus synchronization waits is correctly rejected because each branch satisfies \(L_{ci}+W^{sync}_{ci}=H_c\), so the sum is \(3H_c\). The F02 overlap example is right.

All 12 case rows check out, including F01’s 15-minute upstream and the four worked cases. Component means sum to the stated 115.25 minutes (1383/12). Critical labels are A in F01 and F11, C in F04 and F08, and B in the other eight. Service, queue, lag, synchronization, and conditional stage summaries match the data at the reported rounding (B queue \(230/12\), B lag \(680/12\), C sync \(365/12\)). A is second in the other ten cases. Critical margins sum to 147 minutes (mean 12.25); the B-critical mean of 15 and the stated 5/10 and 7/5 margins are correct.

The counterfactuals are correct under the stated assumptions. A service 50→40 saves flow time only in F01 (5) and F11 (10), total 15. Halving B queue saves 92.5 minutes, with zeros exactly on F01, F04, F08, and F11, and the fork–join maximum falls from 75 to 60. Setting \(D_c=0\) saves 176. The fixed-slot bound of zero is valid because every accelerated join is still at or before the observed final start. The always-available two-server FCFS contrast is also right: only F11 waits, for 5 minutes, so observed \(D\) is not reproduced by that congestion model. Rankings, masking, and the two redesigns stay consistent with those results, and the limits section covers causality, unknown branch capacity, preemption, and non-queue interpretations of \(D_c\).

Deductions for remaining defects:
- “A capacity” is the wrong lever for the A scenario. A’s queue is 0 in every case, so the modeled change is a shorter service interval.
- “Strongest modeled improvement leverage” compares a 20% service cut with a 50% queue cut. Those are not effort-normalized.
- “Tail” is only the sample maximum. That is disclosed, but it is a thin reading of tail contribution on n=12. C’s critical-stage total (112 minutes) slightly exceeds A’s (100), which deserves an explicit place in the A-versus-C ranking.
- F1/F2 span occupancy is shown as about 66% and about 67%. The exact ratios are \(120/182\approx65.9\%\) and \(120/180\approx66.7\%\).
- The PM4Py call meets the required API, but `get_service_time` values are left in the library’s native seconds with no unit label, so the printed service table does not match the minute decomposition.
