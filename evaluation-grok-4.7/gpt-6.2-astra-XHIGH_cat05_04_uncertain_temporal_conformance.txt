8.5

The interval arithmetic, DST reduction, coupled-constraint durations, SLA/profile classifications, and the tolerance/ζ matrix are correct under the stated semantics. The three strict certain SLA breaches are real lower bounds: T-402 A→I is at least 958.5 s, T-408 I→C at least 3297 s, and T-408 C→K at least 138297 s. Fold filtering for T-405 to (CEST, CET, CET), non-rectangular A≤I handling for T-407 and T-409, and the lossy-midpoint counts (10 / 2 / 0 profile flags at ζ = 1 / 2 / 3) all check out. Assumptions that move results are stated and re-run.

The remaining defects are limited but real. The PM4Py log appends inferred T-404 start and T-407 receipt after later events and never sorts explicitly; a forward eventually-follows scan then misses I→C or A→I/A→C unless `format_dataframe` sorts for that version. `zeta=` is outside the diagnostic try/except, so a parameters-dict API crashes before the version fallback. T-407’s main table reports POSSIBLE_VIOLATION / CERTAIN_PASS for receipt pairs even though delivery is unacknowledged; that model belongs only under the conditional heading, with INDETERMINATE as the unconditioned result. S3 and C→K indeterminates are summarized in prose rather than given the required rows. Section 6’s “duration ≤ 1497 s” claim certifies T-404 profile I→C only if the 18-minute lower bound is kept.
