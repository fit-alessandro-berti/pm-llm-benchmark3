6.5

The audit covers every required item, uses the right five-section structure, and correctly kills the obvious full-trace leaks (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`, `risk_score_current`). It also keeps queue length, elapsed time, and initial complexity, and it does not dump region or channel. That is not enough for a high score. Several judgments the task specifically hinges on are wrong or internally inconsistent.

`Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is process-legitimate: an appeal after Partial or Deny can produce a second `Settlement Decision Recorded`. It is inadmissible at `t0` because it has not happened yet. Calling it “impossible at `t0`” collapses that distinction. The backwards `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` edge is the one that is normatively impossible and, in Case Z, timestamp-ambiguous. Those two patterns are labeled differently, then explained as if they were the same kind of defect. `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` is the same error in the other direction: process-feasible as a later step, but unobserved in a prefix that ends at coverage check, so it is future-trace leakage. Medium and “plausible future signal” understates it. Section 2 then calls settlement and closure “structurally unreachable” from `t0`. They are not enabled; they are still reachable later. A DFG is treated as a conformance oracle without the limitation that a frequent edge is not a normative rule.

Lineage is diagnosed and then ignored. `prior_claim_count_before_t0` is a high-severity SQL defect in section 1 and a “defensible” driver of Case Y in sections 3 and 4, with the value 3 read as real history. `initial_reserve` has no stated point-in-time lineage and is still declared legitimate. `final_handler_team` is always a closure field; “35% leakage” only describes how often it differs from the `t0` team, not whether the feature is admissible. `num_document_requests_total` cannot be repaired into a useful signal at this prefix, because the count is structurally zero.

The revised narratives invent mechanisms the evidence does not support: broker “communication overhead,” reserve-driven “scrutiny,” and region R4 as “network capacity,” while region is also tied to language and socioeconomic mix. Queue length “severely limits capacity” and elapsed time “leaves minimal slack” are causal claims, contradicting the causal-boundary sentences. Original SHAP directions are carried forward as if they would survive removal of the leaked features, and the large base-plus-other residual is omitted. For Case Z, identical timestamps are called “timeline corruption,” and the fix is to rebuild order. Both events sit after this prefix, so order repair does not make the pattern admissible.

Section 5 names every requested control, but it tells the team to retrain and “ensure robust performance” instead of treating current AUC and the 0.82 / 0.71 / 0.64 scores as leakage-contaminated. The as-of-extraction employee-experience defect is never picked up.
