2.5

The write-up keeps the required seven headings and names a few real phenomena (A03’s appointment, B02’s preemption of B01, skill limits on QD). Those are not enough to carry the analysis. The core requirement was to rebuild eligible sets at real decision epochs and test competing rules. That reconstruction is systematically wrong, several “supporting” episodes invert the log, confidence is far too high, and §7 contradicts §1.

**Decision tables do not follow the log.** Observed selections are largely job-ID order, not service-start times. In QA, A02 is treated as chosen at 08:04 while A01 is in service; A04 is treated as chosen at 08:30, but A02 runs 08:32–08:52 and A04 starts at 08:58 after calibration. At 08:20 the eligible set is not {A03}: A02 (FIFO) and A04 (priority 1, estimate 7, shorter than A03) are already waiting, and A03 is still chosen. That is the informative appointment test; the table never states it. Later, priority is predicted as A01 after A01 has finished. At 09:05 A05 is started over waiting A08 (priority 1) and A07 (shorter estimate); FIFO fits, priority and SPT do not. Skill-based routing is not identified here: one resource, all jobs `general`.

In QB the second and later rows are mis-timed. At 09:31 B01 resumes, B03 does not start; B03 starts at 09:48 while B05 (priority 1) is already waiting. B05 starts at 10:00, B01 resumes at 10:14, B04 starts at 10:28 ahead of B06 (priority 2) and of B07’s 10:30 appointment. A preemptive-priority-plus-appointment diagnosis does not survive those epochs. Priority-1 preemption explains B02 only. It does not explain B05 failing to preempt, B03 being chosen over B05 under a stated reassignment, or B04 before B06. The only appointment is explicitly missed, so appointment scheduling is not a supported secondary rule.

In QC, C01 is not selected at 10:00. C1 is busy until 10:10, and C01 runs 11:40–12:16. The note says the oldest job was executed last among the initial backlog. Calling C01 the SPT support case reverses that fact (estimate 36 is the longest of C01–C04). Online SPT matches C02 at 10:10 and C03 at 10:18, then fails: at 10:32 C06 (estimate 6) is waiting and C04 is chosen; at 10:54 C05 (estimate 40, priority 1, deadline already missed) is chosen over C06; at 11:40 C01 is chosen over C07 (estimate 18, deadline 12:00 still feasible). Confidence 80 for SPT is not earned. Estimates here match realized durations exactly, so “noisy estimates” are not available as a rescue.

In QD the table again follows IDs. D05 does not start at 11:36; D-F is idle until the 12:00–12:20 meeting ends and D05 runs 12:20–12:50. A 30-minute non-preemptive job does not fit before that meeting. D06 runs 11:30–11:48, not at 12:16; D-X is held idle from 11:20 despite D08 (priority 1, estimate 8) fitting before the appointment. On D-M at 11:25, D04 is chosen over D08, which is both higher priority and shorter, so this resource contradicts SPT and priority and fits FIFO. At 11:48 D-X chooses D07 over D10 (priority 1, shorter). That is consistent with FIFO and with “only D-X can do mechanical+electrical,” and inconsistent with SPT. Primary SPT at confidence 90, with skill also labeled primary, is internally inconsistent and factually unsupported. Parallel servers are ignored throughout.

**§3–§7 do not repair this.** Support and contradiction lines are single sentences and often attack a rule other than the stated primary, or restate a misread. A06 starting late is what non-preemption of A05 predicts; it is weak evidence against appointments. The §4 “extra observables” are already in the log (A06’s segment length equals its estimate; B07’s appointment, start, and completion are known) and would not separate the real competitors. §5’s four exceptions are generic, unranked by evidence, and not tied to calibration, the C1 busy-until-10:10 constraint, the D-F meeting, scan lag, or annotator notes versus resource calendars. §6 copies the prompt’s field list and does not target QD eligibility, appointment tolerance, frozen-backlog versus online SPT, or priority-1-only preemption. §7 calls QA most identifiable and QD least, while §1 assigns QD 90 and QB 75. FIFO is called the dangerous inference; the dangerous one is the high-confidence SPT/priority/skill labels drawn from mis-ordered traces and observationally equivalent constraints.
