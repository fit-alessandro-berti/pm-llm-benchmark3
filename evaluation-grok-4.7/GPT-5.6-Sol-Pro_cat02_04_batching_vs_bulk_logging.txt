The main defects:

- `pm4py.discover_batches` is called with `merge_distance` and `min_batch_size` as keywords. The public API takes those inside a `parameters` dict, so this raises `TypeError` before either print. The later unpacking `for actres, _, batches_by_type` and `len(batch[2])` is also tied to one assumed return shape. The sensitivity story (unchanged from 0 to 300 seconds; `min_batch_size=5` drops the four-row clusters) is plausible, but it is an unexecuted prediction, and touching chains at merge distance 0 depend on `<=` rather than `<`.
- G1’s second explanation is the same fixture cycle described as fan-out, not a competing mechanism. “Exactly four” is stronger than “can hold four.”
- G4’s top cell is the disjunction “sequential batch or ordinary serial run,” and the second cell repeats the second disjunct. Confidence 98 therefore does not rank a batch claim. G4 service time does not depend on upstream clock alignment; only waiting does.

- Minute fields are parsed as `:00`, so recording lags become exact second counts the precision does not support. That caveat appears only later, not in the diagnostic definition.

Sections 5–7 are the most reliable part of the answer. The score is held down by miscalibrated rankings and a required analysis script that does not get as far as printing the comparison it promises.
