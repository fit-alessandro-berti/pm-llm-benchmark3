The answer meets the required structure, keeps availability, intensity, selection, and co-occurring shocks distinct, and refuses an unsupported “reduces cycle time by X%” claim. Estimands, staggered-adoption assumptions, median-versus-tail separation, shared fraud-desk interference, and the embedded-evaluation rollout are all handled carefully. The checked pre/post and comparison arithmetic is accurate and explicitly separated from causal interpretation.

It is not nearly flawless. The cleanest month, April—before the May complexity shock and Alpha’s staffing loss—is not isolated; April cycle times were roughly flat and rework fell, so the “early tail harm” narrative overstates the immediate post-adoption window. The rework interval is mislabeled “control-adjusted” even though its upper end is a raw change. Arrivals per FTE, the main measurable capacity confounder, are never quantified. Bounded ranges also mix Alpha/Beta-only figures with all-adopter figures, and the code prints pooled event-time means that confound calendar time.
