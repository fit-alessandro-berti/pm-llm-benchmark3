8.3

The final diagnosis is coherent, decision-set based, and factually careful: competing FIFO, priority, SPT, appointment, and skill predictions are generally correct, remaining-time arithmetic for B01 checks out, and uncertainty is labeled rather than collapsed. Format, counterfactuals, and the cross-queue ranking (QD most identifiable, QC least, QB preemptive priority the riskiest inference) are defensible.

Points come off for emphasis and equivalence, not for invented events. QA is labeled appointment-primary even though only the 08:20 choice of A03 uniquely rejects FIFO, priority, and SPT; A06 is later cited as appointment support although, once A05 finishes, FIFO selects A06 as well. QB’s headline treats B05’s selection at a free server as the same “preemptive” mechanism as B02’s interruption of B01. The QD reconstruction misses the sharper 11:20–11:30 idle test: D08 (priority 1, 8 minutes) could finish before D06’s 11:30 appointment, so idleness discriminates skill reservation from appointment protection. The QC summary also counts C06 as SPT support even though C06 was already waiting and was passed over at 10:32 and 10:54. The two-minute scan bound is not applied consistently to jobs whose recorded arrival falls just after a decision.
