7.3

The answer covers every required heading, stays within the constraints, and uses the supplied rates, variant shares, waits, and resource facts accurately. The bottom line is properly calibrated: residual procedural concern, not a finding of intentional discrimination. That is the right claim strength.

It is not nearly flawless. The sensitivity logic in §2 is reversed. Over-controlling for a tainted or downstream covariate shrinks the gap; removing `risk_score_band` or `documentation_completeness` should typically enlarge it. Disappearance after removal is suppression or a specification artifact, not evidence that the preliminary model over-controlled.

§4 then contradicts §2 and §5: “if disparities disappear within strata, the mechanism is largely legitimate” treats those same possibly biased covariates as neutral proof of legitimacy.

§3 places Group C’s +4.1-day adjusted cycle-time gap next to a 4.7-day Accommodation Review → Eligibility wait as if the edge wait were the group effect. That wait applies only to cases that traverse the edge (V5 is 31%; accommodation is requested in 62%), so it cannot be read as explaining the group-level residual. The same looseness applies to Group B’s 3.8-day language-support wait versus +2.6 days.

V6 is mislabeled an “appeal/rework” path. Group C has the highest reopen/rework rate (24%) but a V6 share near Group A (5% vs 4%) and only +1.1 points of adjusted appeal probability. C’s rework is not the appeal path, and the answer never separates those facts. D’s small routing residuals (+2.0 and +1.5 points) are also lumped with B’s much larger ones.

§1 states the adjusted approval residuals without signs (“5.5 percentage points”), and several activity pairs lose the arrow, so the direction and the edge are harder to read than the source table.

§7 is the weakest required part. It is labeled pseudocode, but the measurement design is wrong in ways that matter: inter-start time is called waiting time and includes service time; `insert_case_service_waiting_time` and `get_service_time` are computed and never used; handover, working-together, similarity, and role networks are discovered and discarded; `top_resource_share` divides by the sum of per-resource case counts, which double-counts multi-resource cases; the order check conditions on both activities occurring, so omitted language support or accommodation review—the compliance failure that matters—drops out of the denominator. Assumed codes (`"minority language"`, `True`, `"high"`) are not in the log description. Variant values are treated as counts, and `filter_paths_performance` is called without the performance bounds that define it. The plan also never computes the stratified outcome comparison §2 says is the point.

Attenuation from raw to adjusted gaps is never quantified (B’s approval gap falls from about 15 points to 5.5; D’s cycle-time gap mostly disappears). The conclusion still holds, but the answer is stronger on policy judgment than on the identification and measurement steps it prescribes.
