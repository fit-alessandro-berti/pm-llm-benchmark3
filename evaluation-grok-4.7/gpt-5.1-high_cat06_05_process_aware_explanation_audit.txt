6.5

The audit is well structured and gets the main leakage calls right: full-trace duration, appeals, variants, closure patterns, the prior-claim SQL predicate, the nightly document snapshot, queue tolerance, and calendar confounding. It also keeps congestion and prefix age, separates a normatively valid later appeal from the impossible same-timestamp closure edge, and does not treat SHAP rank as causation. Those strengths do not make the write-up nearly flawless.

The required case JSON contradicts itself. For Case Y, `prior_claim_count_before_t0 = 3` is both a credible driver and a removed driver, and the narrative states as fact that the claimant had several prior claims. The stored value is not known to be a pre-`t0` count, so it is not admissible. `initial_reserve` is likewise treated as a confirmed prefix signal and given an investigation/negotiation mechanism, even though no as-of lineage is supplied and section 1 itself says to verify it first. “Relatively high” is unsupported.

`final_decision = Partial` is misdescribed as tautological and as revealing the label. The target is a 10-day breach, not the decision class. Partial is serious future-outcome leakage, but it does not determine duration. That error is repeated in the table and in Case X.

Several interpretations smuggle unsupported mechanisms back in: broker “extra coordination,” region “likely due to” service differences (while dropping the stated socioeconomic correlation at the point of use), appeals as “typically delayed,” and prior claims as “more scrutiny.” Section 3 draws the predictive/causal line more carefully than section 4 actually follows.

Process distinctions are incomplete. Appeal versus closure is differentiated, but enabled, impossible, and merely future-possible activities at `t0` are lumped together. A repaired document-request count at this prefix is typically zero, and “fraud already open at `t0`” is a weak substitute if investigation opens only after coverage. “Exactly one appeal between decisions” over-constrains the stated model. Case Z correctly discards the impossible pattern, mutable score, and last role, but ignores the still-knowable, confounded `calendar_week = 52` and the only partly leaked same-day document count. Employee experience joined as of extraction date, the hinted residual leakage inside “base and other features,” is never mentioned. The prefix-equality test would also falsely fail external as-of features such as queue length.
