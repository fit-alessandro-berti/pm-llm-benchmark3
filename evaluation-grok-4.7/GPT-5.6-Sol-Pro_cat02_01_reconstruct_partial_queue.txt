8.2

The reconstruction is numerically strong and methodologically careful: missing starts stay interval-censored, open work stays elapsed, station sojourn is separated from the wait/service split, and the early-S1 / late-S2 bottleneck reading is supported by invariant WIP. It is not nearly flawless, because one central flow account disagrees with the answer’s own snapshots.

The case table, feasible start windows, scenario means, resource busy times, and midpoint snapshots check out. P05/P07/P11/P15 bounds follow the same-resource neighbors (C1/I1/C2/I1), and the three scenarios preserve station sojourn and WIP while moving only the wait/service split. Busy time correctly includes P21, P15, and P17; completed-service means correctly exclude them. Capacity (720 and 705), full-window utilization (about 74–84% and 73–81%), and the reported-clock case and station summaries are coherent. Censoring, the 15-minute C2 loss versus the 25-minute C2 gap, and the refusal to invent a maintenance counterfactual are handled well. The Python distinguishes blank, MISSING, and OPEN, keeps PM4Py on a completed fully observed projection, and labels those outputs as case-level.

The material inconsistency is the “net WIP” column and the transition narrative built on it. Intervals are half-open, while snapshots are post-batch, and events exactly at 08:00 and 10:00 fall in different buckets. S1 snapshot WIP goes from 1 at 08:00 to 7 at 10:00, but the text says it grows by seven. S2 snapshot WIP is 4 at 10:00, 10:55, and 11:10, but §5 says that over 10:00–11:10 S2 “begins to accumulate” because the interval net is +1. That +1 counts P07’s 10:00 arrival, which the 10:00 snapshot already contains; within the published snapshots, S2 WIP is flat and earlier in the hour it dips to 2. The late shift (S1 7→2, S2 4→6 after 11:10, cutoff 2 vs 6) still stands. The transition claim does not.

Smaller defects: successor starts are listed as binding for P05, P07, P11, and P15 when the upper bound is each case’s own completion; “50–75 minutes around P07/P08” compresses a 40–75 range and a point value of 50; the 10:00 S2 queue of 2 is the midpoint scenario (service-light leaves P05 queued); I1’s 87.5% is reported as 88%, and midpoint utilization as 79% in §4 versus 79.2% in §6. Many exact 5-minute handoffs sit on the rounding boundary and are not discussed, while only the 11:00 C1 tie is. The code also never enforces the C2 outage as a start constraint, though that constraint does not bind these four missing starts.
