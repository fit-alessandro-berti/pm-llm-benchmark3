7.5

The role model is complete and largely well grounded: all six required sections are present, roles are not assigned by raw frequency, complexity and substitution limits are used, and shadow roles are treated as useful but risky rather than improper. The main deductions are uncorrected factual and methodological errors. Erik’s critical-case base is 166 (5+24+82+55), not 162. Section 2 states a pricing duration of 3874 minutes, which contradicts the later, correct 38-to-74-minute account. Fatima’s 16 A9 events are treated as core while Giorgio’s larger count of 20 A9 events is peripheral, without a justification for that authority split. The PM4Py code would not run as written (`resource_key` is not a `format_dataframe` parameter; `case_id_key` and `timestamp_key` are not parameters of `discover_organizational_roles`), and the complexity join is event-weighted rather than case-weighted while conflating `escalation_level` with critical `case_complexity`. Shift pairing in the source table is also unused.
