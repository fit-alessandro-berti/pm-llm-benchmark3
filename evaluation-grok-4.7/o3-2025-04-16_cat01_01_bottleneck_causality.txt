4.5

The crude-versus-stratified reversal is real and the “mainly confounded” reading of the proposed 50% shift is the right family of answer, but the write-up is not reliable enough to brief an operations committee.

Section 1 states the verdict, a confidence level, and a backfire path, yet it treats the reversal as evidence that Orion is not slower. That oversteps the brief: within complexity, Orion is still almost entirely night and high-load, several cells have two cases, and stratification is not identification. The appeal count is wrong. The file has two appeals, C14 (Orion) and C24 (Nova), so 1/14 versus 1/16, not 1/14 versus 2/16. The later 7.1% versus 12.5% and “three events” repeat that invention. The service gap is also overstated: within strata it is about 0.1–0.4 h, not a general 0.4 h advantage. Total, direct, and mediated estimands are never cleanly separated here.

The graph is an edge list rather than an adjacency list, and it does not encode the confounding story it needs. `unobserved_case_risk` has no outgoing arrows, so it cannot confound anything. `unobserved_reviewer_skill` never touches `resource_group`, despite the certification fact. `quality_outcome → case_completed` points the wrong way for sample selection. Stating that `queue_wait + review_service = cycle_time` is “supported by facts” is false: residuals are about 0.8 h on the short path and about 2–3.7 h once compliance or rework is present, and `review_service` already includes the repeated review, so `rework → cycle_time` double-counts.

The role table names confounders, mediators, a selection node, and proxies, but several roles are reversed. `routing_rule` causes group assignment; it is not a mediator of `resource_group`. Conditioning on `complexity` blocks a backdoor; it does not remove a legitimate indirect effect of the group. `shift` is not a collider in this graph, so the collider warning is not earned.

Section 4 is where the audit fails. Pooled complexity standardization is about −2.5 h, so −2.4 h is acceptable, and the three stratum gaps match the supplied aggregates. The other arithmetic does not. Night versus day is about 21.7 h versus 14.0 h, not 19.5 versus 15.3. Within night, Orion is about 23.0 h and Nova about 17.5 h, not 18.3 versus 18.6; the “little night effect once group is held constant” claim is both numerically false and still confounded by complexity. Compliance-required means are about 26.8 h versus 12.0 h, and within required cases Orion is about 26.7 h versus Nova 27.0 h, not 23.5 versus 26.0. Overall wait is about 12.8 h for Orion versus 8.8 h for Nova, so the crude claim that Orion waits less (12.5 versus 13.4) reverses the data. Overall service is about 7.0 h versus 5.0 h, not 6.9 versus 6.6. Rework does not add 3–5 h within strata. These are not rounding issues; they change the night, queue, and quality conclusions.

Section 5 has five hypotheses and predicted observations, but none assigns Orion a genuine harmful effect. The night hypothesis cites the false “about 4 h” and “within night, Orion ≈ Nova” results. The compliance “instrument” (“required but policy-exempt”) is not an instrument. The negative-control prediction is backwards: equal pre-review times would not demonstrate selection bias. Front-door language is used where `queue_wait` is also hit by load, shift, and compliance, with no assumption check.

The intervention shape is the strongest operational part: a limited routing pilot plus a queue action that does not relabel the group, plus a compliance-capacity action. Phase 1 still does not randomize both groups inside one stratum, so it does not deliver the “unconfounded estimate” claimed. Phase 2’s rollback rule is cut off at “or cycle_time.” The closing recommendation is about 100 words, short of the required 120–200, and calls `queue_wait` the “clearly dominant driver.”

Section 7 does not meet the reproducibility bar. The CSV is replaced by a placeholder, so the script cannot run. It never uses `parse_event_log_string`, passes a `StringIO` to `read_csv`, prints no variant comparison, and only warns on cells under three cases. Section 8 is the cleanest part: five concrete data gaps, each tied to one field.

The skeleton tracks the decision, but wrong counts, a non-executing diagnostic, a confounder with no arrows, and a missing harmful-effect hypothesis are disqualifying for a high score.
