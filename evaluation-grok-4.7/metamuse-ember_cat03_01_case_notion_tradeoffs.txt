6.5

The write-up is specific, complete, and often numerically exact, but it is not nearly flawless. Convergence and divergence are defined and then applied inconsistently, and several DFG claims are factually wrong.

What holds up: trace lengths (O1=12, O2=9, O3=13, O4=8), 42 incidences versus 31 events (+11), item 24 versus 16, package 12 versus 10, delivery and invoice duplication of zero, D1/D2/D3 durations (3h, 4h55, 22h45), e16’s nine objects, and four invoice incidences versus three issue events. The V2 Pay→Pay self-loop, payment singletons, item truncation (I11 09:15–14:00 versus O1 through e28), and the ban on treating trace length as correctness are all handled correctly. Sections 4–7 match the requested decision structure, and decision B is argued with real counterevidence rather than by trace length.

What fails:

- Convergence and divergence shift meaning across the matrix. Section 1 defines them as object-graph merge and branch. Order’s “High” convergence is then justified by event copying, while Delivery and Invoice are “Medium–High” and “High” despite zero copied events. Those ratings are not comparable.
- Item “divergence” is justified by I12/I21/I31 merging on D2, which is fan-in. Each item has one Add, Pack, Dispatch, and Confirm, so divergence is low, not low–medium. “P4 and D3” is not a branch: D3 only carries P4. Payment “convergence” reverses the arrow: M2 and M3 are separate successors of V2, and neither payment event is shared.
- Distortion 9 says O1 and O3 have Confirm→Confirm self-loops. O1 does (e23→e24). O3 does not: e24 Confirm → e25 Pay → e27 Confirm. Calling that a self-loop that “encodes order size” is false.
- Distortion 6 treats O4’s e26→e27 Pay→Confirm as part of the partial-payment effect. O4 is on V3/M4, a full settlement. The edge exists because D3’s confirm is late. The real instalment effect is on O2/O3: e22 Pay → e24 Confirm and e24 Confirm → e25 Pay. Only the second of those is stated.
- O3’s Pack→Pack is described as only a merge of foreign items. It is also a split of I31 and I32. That is the distinction the Order DFG erases; the write-up blurs it before saying the DFG cannot tell the cases apart.
- “Dispatch→Issue Invoice appears synchronously for three orders at 11:05” is wrong. e16 is at 11:05, but invoices are e18 at 12:00 and e19 at 12:05. For O3, e16 is directly followed by e17 Dispatch, not by Issue Invoice.
- The e29/e30 11:05 tie is not a within-case ordering artefact: those events belong to O2 and O3 separately.
- “D13h, D24h55, D322h45” is not readable as three durations.

The code matches the requested call list and correctly refuses to treat pictures as proof. The clustering comment overclaims: `cluster_equivalent_ocel(ocel)` is not restricted to orders. Under a strict reading, the central comparison matrix and three distortion claims are unreliable even though the surrounding inventory is strong.
