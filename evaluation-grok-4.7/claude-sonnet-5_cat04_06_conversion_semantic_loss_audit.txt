6.2

The document is a serious invariant-led audit with the right canonical choice (POWL), a real account of the asymmetric join, and failure handling around the process-tree probe. It is not reliable as a validation result: several required checks are mis-implemented, some “validated” cells are unearned, and the required residual-uncertainty section is cut off.

The baseline is mostly right: registration first, `Assess Risk` ∥ `Prepare Evidence`, legal review only after assessment, the loop joined on both, `Configure Trial` before `Inspect Trial`, redo only through `Correct Defect`, at least one iteration, and release as a join before archive. INV-10’s observability cell is wrong: after hiding τ the join is not visible; only its effect on `Release Change` is. Calling `Assess Risk` and `Prepare Evidence` “children” of registration also blurs partial-order successors with operator children.

The pipeline choice is directionally sound, and rejecting an invented direct POWL→BPMN call is correct. The risk model for the primary pipeline is too optimistic. In standard PM4Py, `convert_to_bpmn(net, im, fm)` is implemented by converting the workflow net to a process tree and then to BPMN. The same non-series-parallel “N” (`Assess Risk` precedes both the legal branch and verification; `Prepare Evidence` precedes only verification) that should make pipeline 2 fail is therefore also a likely hard failure of pipeline 1, not merely a gateway-layout risk. The audit table still marks many Petri→BPMN cells `expected preserved`.

Section 3 follows the requested call sequence, guards null markings, catches the tree conversion, round-trips both BPMN results, and runs workflow-net and soundness checks. It still passes possibly null markings into `save_vis_petri_net`.

Section 4 uses `validated preserved` for conversions that were not executed, including “no duplicate-label collapse observed” and “confirmed by trace search.” That contradicts the task’s distinction between expectation and validation. `not tested until round-trip check runs` is outside the allowed assessment vocabulary. Binary-loop behavior is called `approximated` for visualization reasons; that mixes rendering ambiguity with language preservation. Marking the tree column `lost` also skips the equally likely, and safer, outcome `conversion failed`.

Section 5 does not work as written. `visited` keys contain a PM4Py `Marking` (`Counter`), which is unhashable, so the search raises on the first state. `discover_footprints((net, im, fm))` does not match `discover_footprints(net, im, fm)`. The reachability “comparison” only prints sizes. Model and trace playout are omitted. The bound cannot support the claims made about it: `max_visible_steps=9`, but the redo witness has 10 visible labels (`Register`, `Assess Risk`, `Prepare Evidence`, `Configure`, `Inspect`, `Correct Defect`, `Configure`, `Inspect`, `Release`, `Archive`). The source net itself would be reported as missing a required positive witness. No positive witness places `Legal Review` after `Configure Trial`, so INV-6 is untested; no negative witness fires two iterations without `Correct Defect`. `max_silent_run=3` can also cut the silent chains POWL translation inserts and yield false missing traces. The note then treats a missing positive witness as conclusive divergence. It is not, when the witness does not fit the bound.

Section 6 is the strongest part: AND-gateway re-pairing, empty XOR branch, redo versus a generic loop icon, silent skip, and layout order are separated from behavior, with concrete annotations.

Section 7 has enough technical and semantic gates, but the lead-in applies every condition to every artifact. Condition 8 then quarantines the result whenever the optional tree conversion raises, which the task defines as an acceptable outcome. Condition 4 inherits the false-negative witness problem.

Section 8 correctly keeps POWL authoritative, BPMN derivative, and the process tree non-canonical, with regeneration and re-validation against drift. It does not say what is shown to stakeholders when `bpmn_from_net` is null, which is the likely case if BPMN conversion shares the process-tree failure.

Section 9 stops at “Even if every check in” and states none of the three required unprovable claims. Passing the proposed tests still would not establish step-concurrency versus interleaving, language equivalence past the bound, or that a BPMN picture’s gateways match the net.
