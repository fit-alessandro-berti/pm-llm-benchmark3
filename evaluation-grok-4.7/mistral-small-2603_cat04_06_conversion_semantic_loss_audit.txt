4.5

The document has the required nine-part shape and a defensible primary pipeline, but it is not a reliable audit. Several required semantic claims are inverted or missing, and the validation code cannot establish the equivalences it reports.

The baseline table reverses invariant I4: the positive witness is verification before both predecessors finish, and the negative witness is verification after both finish. That swaps the synchronization invariant the rest of the audit depends on. The redo rule is also missing as its own invariant. “Correct Defect” never appears in the table, so binary-loop scope, the mandatory first iteration, and the separator between iterations are not specified. I2’s negative witness only restates “not before Register,” so it does not police the missing order between Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence. Every row is marked observable after hiding silent transitions. Sequential traces can show both orders and the presence or absence of Legal Review; they cannot show true concurrency, which is exactly the distinction the task requires.

The preservation table then treats those invariants as largely `expected preserved` or even `validated preserved` although nothing was executed. `validated preserved` is unsupported. Petri-to-tree cells say `lost` or `expected preserved` where the appropriate result for this partial order is `conversion failed`: the order contains an N-shape (Prepare Evidence before verification, Assess Risk before both verification and optional legal review, and no edge between Prepare Evidence and optional legal review). A process tree cannot represent that order. “XOR collapsed” is the wrong failure diagnosis. Process trees can represent XOR; they cannot represent this partial order.

Section 6 contradicts Section 4. The audit says the Petri net preserves true concurrency. The visual review says the source only allows arbitrary interleaving and that parallel gateways would be a misreading. In this POWL model, absence of an edge means the activities may overlap. Parallel gateways are the right reading of Assess Risk versus Prepare Evidence. The dangerous misreading is a block-structured diagram that adds a causal edge, especially Prepare Evidence before Legal Review, or a layout that looks sequential while the gateway is an AND-split. Layout and control-flow semantics are repeatedly treated as the same thing.

The conversion code misses explicit requirements. Markings are never tested for `None` before soundness checks or saves. Only the POWL-derived net is diagnosed in the conversion section. `bpmn_from_net` is not converted back there, and `bpmn_from_tree` is never converted back at all. The source POWL is not visualized. Exceptions are printed and then re-raised, but the expected rigid / non-block-structured failure is never interpreted.

The round-trip explorer is incorrect. `pm4py.petri_semantics` is not a PM4Py function, so firing a transition cannot work. Silent transitions are discarded with `continue` instead of being executed and omitted from the visible trace. POWL-derived nets move tokens through silent transitions, so this search stalls and can yield two empty trace sets. Empty equality would be reported as a match. The footprint call for the second net uses `im1`, not `im2`. Trace search uses the globals `net_from_powl`, `im_from_powl`, and `fm_from_powl` instead of the function arguments. The shortest accepting visible trace already needs seven labels when legal review is skipped; the search bound of five generations cannot contain one complete trace. Reachability graphs, witness traces, and playout are absent. The required statement of whether comparison is exhaustive under that bound is absent. The imported `pm4py.simulation.playout.comparator` is unused and is not a sound public API.

The canonical-representation choice stores the converted accepting Petri net as the authority and edits that net in place. That freezes the first conversion, including any loss of the N-shaped partial order, and leaves the source POWL outside the edit path. No invariant suite is required before that promotion. The residuals discuss unstated business intent and other POWL models. They do not state the limits that apply here: a bounded interleaving set is not pomset equivalence, a depth bound that cannot reach the final marking is not language equivalence, soundness is not language inclusion, and footprint equality is not a complete invariant.
