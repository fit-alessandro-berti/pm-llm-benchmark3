7.2

The forecast is complete, well segmented, and appropriately conditional: all seven required sections are present, East is treated differently from North and South, and the recommendation is a monitored pilot rather than a full rollout. The listed-handover totals (343 to 359, +4.7%), the specialist-path sum of management’s median waits (9.8h to 12.0h), and the 9-to-12 legal/risk increase are arithmetically right, and the mechanism map correctly separates fewer organizational entities from more touches.

It is not nearly flawless. The sum of edge medians is labeled a path median, and that path is called standard even though the specialist edge occurs only 36 times per 100 cases. “+15 percentage points” misreads “15% higher,” and “Legal/Risk utilization >95%” is presented as arithmetic although referral volume is not shown to be their whole workload. “Tech loop” means 67→77 in the mechanism map, about 74 in the optimistic case, then 44–47 and 50+ later. The ping-pong rule counts the designed caseworker–specialist–caseworker return as failure. The priority-inversion proxy does not compare service order with priority, the absence “shock” deletes events instead of modeling backlog, and window cuts censor cases before cycle time is calculated. In the sketch, the performance DFG is unpacked into four values, roles use `.resources` rather than originators, handover and working-together results are treated as edge tables, and event-log variants are treated as counts. Those errors would break or mislead the proposed monitoring.
