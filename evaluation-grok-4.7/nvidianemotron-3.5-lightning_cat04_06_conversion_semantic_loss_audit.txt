The write-up covers all nine required sections and correctly treats the Petri-net path as primary, expects the process-tree path to fail, and wraps that attempt in exception handling. That structure does not offset several core technical mistakes.

The bounded explorer never fires label-`None` transitions, so it hides tau by refusing to execute it. POWL operator and partial-order nets depend on those silent steps; the search therefore cannot reach the visible behavior it claims to enumerate. Firing is also delegated to `pm4py.apply_marking_change`, which is not a provided API. The loop counter increments on every verification label and resets elsewhere, and any nonempty trace set is flagged as exhaustive. Each net is explored separately, but the source and round-tripped trace sets are never compared, and no positive or negative witness traces are checked.

`validated preserved` is used without any execution. `conversion failed` is applied where the real judgment is semantic loss or ambiguity. Cross-net equality of final markings is impossible after place identities change, yet it is a rejection criterion.

Tau-hiding is misstated throughout: a silent skip makes Legal Review optional in the visible language, rather than collapsing the choice into one path. Incomparable Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence activities are true concurrency in the partial order, not “not truly parallel.” Verification is also described as enabled by Assess Risk alone, despite still waiting for Prepare Evidence. LOOP semantics are treated as unspecified even though do-redo LOOP means at least one trial body and Correct Defect only between repetitions. The canonical-model section names both the derived Petri net and the source POWL as authoritative.
