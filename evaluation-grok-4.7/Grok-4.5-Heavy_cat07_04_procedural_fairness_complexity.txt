7.2

The answer covers every required heading, respects the constraints, and is generally careful not to treat raw approval gaps as discrimination or support steps as optional delay. The numerical citations are mostly faithful, the mechanism set includes both legitimate routing and capacity-driven burden, and the mitigations include real trade-offs. It is not nearly flawless.

The adjusted-evidence interpretation is the main analytical failure. The preliminary model does not control for `language_preference` or `disability_accommodation_requested`, yet sections 1, 3, and 8 treat its residuals as disparity beyond measured complexity and legitimate need. Section 2 adds those variables to the matching set and never uses that fact to qualify the published residuals. That is exactly the inference the question asks for, and it is missed. Section 3 then calls the variant mix “largely consistent with legitimate routing” while barely treating the adjusted +9.5 percentage-point Request Missing Documents gap for Group B — larger than the supervisor-review residual it does emphasize — as unexplained after documentation completeness is already controlled.

There are factual overstatements. Group D’s adjusted approval gap (−3.1 percentage points) is larger than Group C’s (−2.2), so “smaller residual approval and cycle-time gaps” is wrong as a joint claim. Reopen/rework is described as “partially residual after adjustment” even though no adjusted rework estimate is given. The 30.7-day figure is the median of variant V6 only, not of appeals as a class.

The PM4Py sketch would not run as written and blurs what the log can support. `format_dataframe` has no `resource_key` argument. `filter_paths_performance` is invoked without a path. Declarative and log-skeleton checks are comments, not constraint specifications or conformance checks. Service-versus-wait decomposition is presented as if it were observable, although the log has only `time:timestamp`; with no start time, service time is not identified. The code also never actually stratifies on risk or documentation completeness, despite section 2 requiring that.

The bottom line is directionally cautious but does not answer the requested fair / unfair / indeterminate classification. It substitutes “not demonstrably discriminatory,” which is a different claim, and it attributes B and C residuals “in large part” to support-step capacity while underweighting D’s adjusted appeal gap and B’s residual approval gap.
