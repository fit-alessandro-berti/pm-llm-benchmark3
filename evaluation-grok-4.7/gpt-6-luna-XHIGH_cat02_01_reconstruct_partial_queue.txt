The station-level picture is not reliable. Under the stated release-then-arrival convention, total WIP at 11:00 is 11 (P08–P18), not 9; every other hourly snapshot in that list is correct, so this is a counting error. S2 station WIP in 10:00–12:00 peaks at 5 (P08 with P10–P13), not “about 4.” That 4 matches the S2 queue peak, so station WIP and queue length are conflated in the period that matters for the bottleneck claim. The 08:00–10:00 S2 peak of “about 4” exists only if 10:00 is included, which contradicts the stated half-open bins; inside [08:00, 10:00) the peak is 3. The independent ±5-minute utilization envelope’s upper end of about 92% of 705 minutes is also looser than the stated rule produces (about 90%).

Section 5 then leans on a non-comparable pair: S1 mean wait 45–48 minutes over arrivals P01–P13 versus S2 mean wait 13–18 minutes over entries P01–P06. The S1 figure is inflated by late arrivals in that bin (P12–P13 wait 80–85 minutes, mostly after 10:00). Same-case S1 waits for P01–P06 average about 21 minutes. The rate gap and the later S2 buildup still support a time-varying constraint, but the cited 45-versus-15 contrast overstates how early the long S1 waits are.
