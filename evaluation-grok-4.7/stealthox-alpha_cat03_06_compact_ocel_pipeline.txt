7.5

The pipeline is complete, referentially careful, and strong on the parts that are easy to get wrong: all 17 events and 12 objects are present, E2O activity/timestamp values are joined from the event table, source versus `derived` is labeled, both payments stay `partially_settles` with amounts kept on the events, and the Order/Package/Delivery/Invoice trace shapes and DFG multiplicities check out (14 and 10 order events, 24 flattened rows, 22 directly-follows occurrences across 13 edges).

The analysis then misstates that same model. Undirected O2O reachability is treated as proof that every `derived` row is justified, but the O2O graph is one connected component, so the check accepts an arbitrary target and cannot fail. `subject` is defined as one per event while Reserve Stock is given two. e06 is not the only pre-invoice binding of the two orders’ items, and the cross pairs (O100, I3) and (O200, I1) also occur on e07. OTG weights are inconsistent with the relations: Order–Item is 6 shared events, not 5; Order–Package is 6, not 4; Order–Invoice is 3, not 1. The interactions paragraph repeats the Order–Item undercount, then says O100 meets 10 of 11 other objects and excludes O200 while also citing seven O100–O200 shared events; O100 co-occurs with all 11. The Petri-net reading also threads P1 then P2, and D1 then D2, as if distinct objects were one token. Lesser slips (about 22h45m called 23 hours, “60+ rows” for a propagation that adds far fewer) sit on top of those contradictions.
