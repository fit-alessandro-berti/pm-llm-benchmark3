6.5

The dataset and pipeline are largely complete and internally usable: all 17 events, the stated objects, timezone-aware timestamps, payment amounts on events, required O2O links, referential checks, and the mandated PM4Py calls are present. The chosen source-minimal E2O policy is defensible, and P1/V1 are correctly treated as the main convergence points.

The analysis is not reliable enough to score higher. Section 1 says Pack Package events are linked directly to orders, but e8 (P2/I2) is not linked to O100. Section 5 misstates the model: Reserve Stock does not touch Package, and only one Pack Package event touches both orders. The OC-DFG description treats the whole activity chain as co-occurring with Order, Item, and Package. The OTG account mixes event co-occurrence with O2O and gives Order a direct Package link that the O2O table does not contain. Section 1 also treats default flattening as O2O propagation; `ocel_flattening` follows direct E2O links.

Required flattened predictions are incomplete. Trace sketches for the four object types are mostly consistent with the code, but likely DFG edges are not stated, including the Add Item and Record Payment self-loops created by repeated activity labels. Package traces are called clean while omitting delivery confirmation, and the claim that shared events make traces “artificially linear” or create “loops” misidentifies duplication and frequency distortion. Failure modes are named rather than discussed, and the audit is a bullet list rather than the requested tables.
