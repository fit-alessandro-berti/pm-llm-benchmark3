The POWL model is the right canonical object and is essentially faithful. Top-level edges encode register-before-both-streams, containment before planning, root-cause before design, root-cause before the risk XOR and the supplier XOR, both planning products before the trial loop, and the three-way join into release before archive. Unordered pairs match the intended overlaps, including approval with trial and supplier with planning, design, approval, and trial. High risk is an XOR child that is itself an unordered pair, so both reviews occur in either order. Supplier optionality is an XOR with one silent skip, so the skip still joins at release. The loop body is Execute Trial then Inspect Trial, with Adjust Parameters only as the redo, which yields `(ET IT)(AP ET IT)*` and keeps Adjust Parameters off the exiting inspection. The process tree is a real block-structured under-approximation with the same hard joins, and it does not admit an unsafe release, a skipped approval route, or a broken trial cycle. Witnesses A1–A4 fit both models, A5 is the right separator, and F2 and F3 name the earliest broken condition.
