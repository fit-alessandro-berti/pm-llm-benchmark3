7.4

The forecast is structurally strong: it uses the parallel join as a maximum, separates queue wait from service, keeps the weekend from collapsing into a Friday release, conditions anomaly rates on the temperature excursion, and refuses to add stage means. Hour anchors from Friday 18 Sep 2026 15:30 are mostly arithmetic-correct, and the smooth-path insight (microbiology sets a Monday floor; FIFO chemistry often binds inside Monday) is the right decomposition. It is not nearly flawless. Several bounds are internally inconsistent or not the earliest case the stated assumptions allow, and two load-bearing quantities are used without being ledgered.

The contamination clock is the clearest temporal miss. With weekend authorisation, an indication at Sunday ~13:00 plus the short 20 h confirmation is technically ready Monday ~09:00 and readable Monday morning. A short QA/rejection path can then terminate Monday early-to-mid afternoon. Section 2 instead gives Monday ~19:00 as the earliest terminal time, which is what you get from Sunday 13:00 + 30 h with no readout and no QA, i.e. the long incubation end mislabelled as a terminal event. Section 3 then places the authorised confirmation result at Monday ~10:00–Tuesday ~09:30, and Section 2’s S6 “fast Batch Rejected” earliest is Tuesday 15:00. Those three statements cannot all be the earliest case of the same path.

The documentation-only investigation earliest time has the same corner-cutting. An investigation opened Monday ~11:00 for 8 QA working hours can be back with QA by Monday ~19:00 and released Monday ~20:00 or Tuesday ~08:30–10:00. Tuesday ~14:00 is a central-early case (open Monday afternoon), not the earliest plausible terminal time. Working-hour arithmetic drifts the same way elsewhere: 12 QA hours from a Monday ~18:00 query reaches about Tuesday 18:00, not 14:00–16:00; 20 hours from a Monday ~19:00 open reaches about Wednesday 15:00, not 09:00–13:00.

Probability bands are looser than the conditioning scheme. The ledger’s own conditionals imply an unconditional rerun rate of roughly 0.12–0.23, and S2 is rerun without a microbiology anomaly, so it should sit below that marginal. A 12–25% band overshoots it. S3’s 6–15% likewise sits above the stated 0.05–0.12 indication rate. The uplift under a confirmed excursion (rerun to 0.20–0.35, indication to 0.10–0.20) is an unguided judgment that, at the corner P(excursion)=0.40, pushes the marginals outside the ranges the scenario actually gave. Overlaps are disclosed, which prevents a summing error, but the individual bands are still inflated.

Two assumptions that move the forecast are missing from the ledger. Predecessor samples are given the same triangular service law as B-417; without that, the FIFO Monday start of ~11:30–14:30 is not identified. And “confirmed contamination ≈2–6%” silently assumes that only about half of indications become critical defects. The scenario describes a mandatory confirmation incubation, not a false-positive rate. If every indication is a critical defect, Batch Rejected is about 5–12%, the “refuted indication → laboratory investigation” branch in S3 should not be there, and the 90–96% Electronic Release share is high.

Section 6 over-updates a cleared excursion. Queries remain 0.15–0.30 even when tests are normal, so a P80 tightened to Tuesday ~16:00–19:00 (≈96–100 h) drops that mass. The directional claim (confirmed excursion fattens the tail and lifts investigation probability to 0.30–0.55; a clear flag does the opposite) is right; the tightened P80 is not.

Smaller issues, not enough to dominate: S1’s central column (Monday 14:00–19:30) is weighted toward the 45% risk-priority branch while FIFO is stated at 55%; the assumption-table FIFO finish (Monday 13:00–17:00) omits the long-service spill that Section 3 later admits; σ≈1 lognormal QA service makes Friday/Saturday throughput of ~1.8–2 completions optimistic in a 4–4.5 h window. The Monday queue band of 3–7 is wide enough to hide that. The QA fluid model itself is sound: net drain until B-417 joins, then wait = (number ahead) / two-reviewer rate, because later arrivals stand behind.

What keeps the score from falling further is the material that is right. Chemistry cannot clear on Friday if the urgent sample takes the first analyst (earliest second-analyst start ~16:42, minimum service ends ~18:12), and a Friday finish would not move a smooth-path join anyway because incubation is ready only Sunday 12:00–20:00 and QA is closed Sunday. Weekend readout authorisation is correctly treated as nearly worthless on the smooth path and worth about half a day to a day on the confirmation path. Reruns pause at 18:00. Doc-only rework is in QA working hours; laboratory investigation is in calendar days, including 26–27 Sep. Dependence is routed through the excursion instead of multiplied unconditionally. The driver ranking and the choice of the excursion flag as the next updating event are defensible.
