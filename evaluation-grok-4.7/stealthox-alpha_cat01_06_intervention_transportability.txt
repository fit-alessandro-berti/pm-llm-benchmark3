The answer meets the required structure and reaches the right decision (`conditional go`), correctly refusing to treat Site A’s nine-hour drop as an identified effect and refusing to start document checking before originals. The transport narrative, modifier table, tail-worsening scenario, and mechanism-versus-implementation split are substantively strong. It is not nearly flawless: several final claims contradict each other or the stated process.

The Site B net does not enforce the process it describes. Section 6 sequences `Assessment` before the decision/legal outcome, but the code never adds that edge. Under POWL partial-order semantics the outcome node is concurrent, so approval, rejection, notification, or legal review can fire before assessment (and even before registration). That breaks the required dependencies even though identity and document both precede assessment. Playout would emit illegal traces while still possibly passing a workflow-net check.

The quantitative layer overclaims and double-counts. The shorter verification task bounds digital overlap near 3.4h, yet the digital stratum is repeatedly given 3–6h. The 9h decomposition assigns the 6h pre-assessment reduction to huddles and staffing and then adds a separate ~3.2h parallelization residual. The 25h complexity premium and 1–3h handoff saving are inserted without data. “No originals and no legal review ≈ 38%” treats the 38% digital share as if it already excluded the 40% legal share. Those margins are separate; independence would put the joint group near 23%, and the site-level 1–2.5h claim depends on the larger figure.

The pilot contradicts itself. It specifies three versions (C, T2, T1) but a 1:1 randomization; individual assignment, arrival-day clustering, and dedicated pods at once; a 12-week pilot with a 10-week sample-size statement; and ~430 S1 cases per arm, roughly twice the two-arm yield of ~215 S1 cases per month. S1/S2/S3 are not a partition because originals and legal review can co-occur. The asserted 80% power has no variance or test. Section 9 then says T1≈T2 means the case lock is binding, which erases the fidelity distinction drawn in Section 7.

The transport graph puts `legal_capacity` on `waiting_before_assessment` although legal review is downstream of assessment, and it routes measurement bias into `true_overlap`. `originals_required → rework` is explained with the supplement loop, which is a legal-review mechanism. Those are material causal-specification errors, not wording nits.
