6.5

The final write-up is strong on estimands, competing risks, leakage, and format, but its reported survival numbers are systematically wrong and contradict its own code.

The definitions in §1 are the right ones: time from `Submit`, horizon 72, E1 as `1 − F_R(72)` under competing events, E2 as the post-72 resolution CIF, E3 as no internal disposition, and cause-specific CIFs. Withdrawal and rejection are treated as competing events, transfers get three explicit codings, and already-observed breaches (S09, S11, S25, S26, late resolutions, S32) are separated from open cases censored before 72 (S10, S15, S24, S29, S31). Method choice, immortal-time/leakage handling, the transition structure, and the non-causal operational section are all at a high level. Counts that do not depend on the Kaplan–Meier path are right: 32 cases, 9 still at risk at 72 (4 open), resolved-only medians 47.5 h vs 39 h, 3/8 P1 transfers, AutoCheck in 13/17 of cohort B.

The Aalen–Johansen/KM figures are not. Censored cases are not removed from later risk sets (the first break is t=23, S31: n at t=26 is 25, not 26). That error runs through every later increment. Under transfer-as-competing-event, the coherent values are about E1 69.6%, E2 30.1%, E3 38.0%, and at 72 h roughly 30.4% resolved, 14.0% withdrawn, 10.1% rejected, 7.5% transferred, 38.0% still open — not 71.5%, 13.9%, 42.9%, and 28.5/12.8/9.5/6.4/42.9. Cohort E1 is about 70.8% vs 68.4%, not 73.3% vs 70.6%; P2 E1 is about 56.9%, not 64.3%; the within-B AutoCheck contrast is about 57.5% vs 100%, not 61.5%. The naive resolution KM that censors competing events is about 36%, not 30%, so the competing-risk gap is understated. E2 is worse than a 2-point slip: the correct CIF puts a large share of “late resolution” on S13 at t=110 with one case left at risk, which the wrong 13.9% figure hides. The claim that only ~19% of the unresolved tail is later observed to resolve does not hold.

§8 makes this an internal contradiction, not just hand arithmetic. `km_surv` and `aj_cif` do decrement the risk set for censorings, so the script would print approximately 0.696 / 0.301 / 0.380. The text still says those outputs were hand-verified as 0.715 / 0.139 / 0.429. RMST ≈ 50.4 h happens to survive. Qualitative directions that remain intact — do not analyze completed cases only, E1 is much more stable than E2/E3 under transfer coding, the A-vs-B gap is small and confounded — are not enough to carry the numerical conclusions the document states.
