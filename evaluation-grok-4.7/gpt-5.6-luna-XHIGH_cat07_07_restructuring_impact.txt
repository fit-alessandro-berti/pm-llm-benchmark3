The forecast is complete, balanced, and unusually careful: every required heading and sub-element is present, East is treated differently from North and South, averages are refused as success criteria, and the recommendation is a conditional pilot rather than a rollout. The mechanism map also reads the supplied frequencies correctly (technical and approval handovers rise; only cross-team transfers fall) and correctly treats 91–93% Legal/Risk utilization as incompatible with a blanket utilization gain.

Deductions are for concrete errors, not tone. `filter_variants` is used to select case IDs, with a nonexistent `variants_filter` argument; that API filters activity-sequence variants, so the sketch’s only concrete case-selection call is wrong. The segment loop then depends on an undefined helper, while the defined `split_log` filters at event level and would truncate traces. Assigning period from each event timestamp splits in-flight cases across before/after and biases variants, handovers, and cycle time; that is never corrected. `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` is treated as an operational load and peak series, which it is not. In the pilot, “a fall in East’s average, caused by routing easier work into the pool” contradicts the stated rule that high-complexity work is centralized. The mechanism map also asserts higher arrival variability as an expected effect, then relies on pooling to smooth peaks, without separating absolute load from relative variability. Stubs are acceptable as pseudocode, but these are not merely stubs.
