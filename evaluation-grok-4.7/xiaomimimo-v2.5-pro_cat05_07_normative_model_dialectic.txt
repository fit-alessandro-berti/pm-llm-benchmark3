6.5

The briefs, matrix, and case rulings are serious and mostly traceable, but the required control-flow revision is not a valid or faithful POWL, and several classifications contradict the evidence or the answer’s own categories.

Dialectical work is the strongest part. Stipulated facts are separated from authority, scope, identity, and logging disputes. Both briefs are actually argued: CS-9 versus unincorporated PD-3, FD-7 expiry, hidden resource breaches, exception creep, and the point that desirability does not amend policy are on the prosecution side; loops, reactivation, rejected leads, CRM linkage, and label change are on the defense side. Each side makes the required concessions. The judgment does not wash out SUP-703, SUP-708, SUP-712, or post-expiry SUP-714, and it correctly refuses to treat SUP-705’s factual emergency as written authority.

Case handling is uneven. SUP-713 is not a justified policy exception; equivalent content and a later mapping approval support a label or model defect. SUP-715 is labeled both MIXED and NONCOMPLIANT. The system summary calls SUP-711 a model or scope defect after classifying it as a migration duplicate. HIGH confidence for in-window PD-3 is too strong: the observer’s statement that she had not approved weakening CS-9 leaves the authority proposition disputed. SUP-710’s “valid prior certification” sits poorly with “moderate” risk from the missing fresh review.

The formal model fails the semantics it is supposed to repair. `activate` and the silent transition are reused across partial orders and operators, so the POWL is not a well-formed tree. `M1.order.graph.edges()` is not executable on a NetworkX order. Quality review is placed before every branch, so the model rejects the pilot and emergency traces the judgment calls justified, including SUP-702 and SUP-704. New labels (`Provisional_Activate`, `Retrospective_Quality_Review`) do not match the log. The claim that post-activation quality cannot be encoded in one POWL is false: an exclusive choice among route-specific partial orders can express it, with eligibility, amount, and time left to data rules. `Request_More_Info` is forced before both checks, and constraint C8 repeats that error, but SUP-707 runs sanctions, then two info requests, then quality. The data schema and overlay table do cover notification IDs, versions, snapshots, delegation, and effective versus record time; they do not repair the control-flow model.
