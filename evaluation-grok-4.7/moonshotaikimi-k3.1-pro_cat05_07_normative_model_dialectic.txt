The final answer is a strong, evidence-tracked conformance judgment with the required structure, both briefs, all 15 cases, and a usable layered revision. It is not nearly flawless.

The dialectical core holds. M0 concurrency is read correctly: both checks are mandatory, and either order conforms. CS-9, PD-3, and FD-7 are kept distinct. Expiry is enforced for SUP-705 and SUP-714. SUP-703 and SUP-708 are treated as real activation breaches, while no-loss and no-transaction facts are mitigation only. SUP-712 is correctly a control-flow false negative. SUP-715 is limited to missing conflict-of-interest disclosure rather than an invented prohibition. SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-709, SUP-710, SUP-711, and SUP-713 are not collapsed into business misconduct. The 289/14/9 split and the notification-missingness jump are not converted into a fake compliance rate. Both sides concede the other side’s strongest points, and the system conclusion does not split the difference: material breaches exist, prevalence is unproved, and M0 remains a valid standard route but not a universal oracle.

The main technical defect is in the proposed POWL. `decision -> notify` forces every activity inside the chosen route, including post-activation `Quality_Review`, to finish before `Notify_Requester`. That contradicts the pilot pattern the model claims to allow: SUP-702’s stable CRM notification at +2 hours precedes next-business-day quality review. The task required awkward POWL limits to be stated. The information-request loop and external guards are stated; this ordering limit is not. The same structure also makes an early provisional notice impossible on the emergency route.

Smaller problems remain. SUP-713 is labeled `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT` even though the stated conclusion is substantive equivalence, so the category does not match the finding. The exception burden is applied more strictly to SUP-702 than to SUP-704, although cumulative pre-approval spend and the shutdown-prevention nexus are also less than fully proved. The overlay “table” is one wide prose row per instrument, not a normalized rule store. The June 15 CRM evidence for SUP-702 is accepted without noting that the CRM move is dated July 1.

The code is otherwise a defensible PM4Py construction: concurrent standard checks, manual-before-activation, guarded auto and emergency over-approximations, optional repeated `Request_More_Info`, rejection/withdrawal, and notification before closure. Case classifications and reversal conditions are specific enough to audit. Those strengths support a high score; the unstated notification-tail error and the smaller classification and burden inconsistencies keep it below excellent.
