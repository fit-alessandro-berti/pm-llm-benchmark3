3.5

The submission is an unfinished draft of the first two sections plus a broken opening of the third. Sections 4–7 are absent, so there is no case table, no station metrics, no bottleneck account, no three-scenario sensitivity analysis, and no Python or PM4Py implementation. The required output is therefore not delivered, and the analytical questions (rates, WIP path, utilization ranges, bottleneck, Little’s-law check) are not answered.

What is present is partly competent and partly inconsistent. Wait, service, and sojourn are defined separately, OPEN work is treated as elapsed rather than final, rounding envelopes are not called confidence intervals, the C2 capacity denominator \(2\times360-15=705\) is right, and the stated central S2 busy time of 565 minutes matches the listed imputations (515 minutes of fully observed or elapsed S2 service plus two 25-minute fills). The non-FIFO stance and the refusal to treat a short nonstationary window as Little’s-law validation are appropriate.

Those merits do not survive the imputation logic. The four missing-start intervals do not use one rounding rule: P07 and P05 take a lower bound of previous end \(+2.5\) minutes, while P15 and P11 take previous end \(-2.5\). The first pair is a “must hold for every rounding” interval; the second pair mixes a “possible” lower bound with a “necessary” upper bound of completion \(-2.5\). The inward five-minute grids inherit that split. A3 and A5 allow a start at resource release and nonnegative service, but the grids drop those endpoints with no reason. “Central” is a midpoint only for P07 (09:45) and P15 (11:55). For P05 and P11 it is the earliest grid point (09:50 and 12:00), i.e. maximum service, while still being described as central and as implying 25-minute services. The Little’s-law display equates station WIP \(L_s=Q_s+B_s\) to \(\lambda\overline W\) after \(W\) was defined as queue wait only; the matching sojourn is \(T=W+D\). A8 also puts the 565-minute result inside the assumption list. Section 3 stops mid-sentence and never gives the required rows for P05, P07, P11, P15, P16, P17, P21, and P22.
