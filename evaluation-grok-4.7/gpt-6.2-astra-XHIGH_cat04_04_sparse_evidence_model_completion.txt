8.7

The three candidates are genuine competing hypotheses, and the control-flow languages are internally consistent: conservative is exactly the seven observed words, balanced adds precisely `CEXS`, `ECSX`, and `CSEX` (10 words), and permissive is exactly the 14 words in which both checks occur, visible S is preceded by at least one check, and X, if present, follows both checks. Those languages nest, the positive/negative suite matches them, DFG counts are handled correctly (including 19 C-before-E completions versus 17 C→E edges), and completion order is not treated as proof of concurrency or causality. Missing supplier events are kept distinct from stocked skips, D5 is correctly read as a start/information rule rather than a completion rule, and the data-collection plan can actually separate XOR, sequence, overlap, optionality, and loss.

The required validation script is not reliable as written. `sound, diagnostics = pm4py.check_soundness(...)` assumes a tuple; the usual PM4Py facade returns a bool, so the script throws before fitness, precision, playout, or the suite. The claim that this passed on PM4Py 2.7.23.4, with all 30 cases fitting and every assertion succeeding, is therefore an unsupported empirical statement. That is a material failure in a section whose job is to reduce uncertainty.

Section 3 also blurs the requested risk types. The dominant conservative failure is overfitting (a one-off or bad export becomes legal; unseen legal words are rejected), but that point is placed under underfitting. “S on either side of the second check” understates the three S positions relative to `other · opt(X)`. Permissive duplicates C, E, S, and X across the mirrored XOR just as the catalogue does, but the duplicate-label and alignment cost is discussed only for the conservative tree. The 5–10% supplier-event loss rate is not pushed to its obvious consequence: with 23 observed S events it explains only about one to three missing events, so it cannot account for all seven S-free cases.

Those are precision and validation defects, not a collapse of the model argument. The inductive split itself—catalogue versus C-before-S with an X/S coupling versus either-check plus independent X—is sound and well calibrated.
