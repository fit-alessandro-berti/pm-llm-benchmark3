7.5

The assessment is substantively strong and meets the required structure: all eight headings are present, all eight fairness dimensions are covered, the adjusted-comparison design distinguishes disparity, legitimate routing, and uncertainty, and the bottom line stays calibrated. Raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination, case mix is not treated as a full excuse, `risk_score_band` is not treated as neutral, and support steps are kept as rights-preserving sources of burden rather than steps to delete.

The score is held down by concrete errors in the required analysis plan and a few overstatements.

In the PM4Py section, the regression is not runnable as written. `case_features` stores `outcome` and timestamps, but the logit formula uses `approval` and `C(month)`, neither of which is constructed. Waiting-time decomposition is only a pair of calls (`insert_case_service_waiting_time`, `get_service_time`); the plan never turns those into the step-level waits the assessment itself depends on, and handover counts are described earlier but never built into the case table. `filter_case_performance` and `filter_paths_performance` are called with a threshold of `10` and `max=None`. In PM4Py those thresholds are seconds, so this does not isolate slow cases, and `None` is not a reliable open upper bound. `filter_variants` is pointed at placeholder strings rather than activity-sequence variants. Discovery functions for handovers, working-together, resource similarity, and roles are invoked and then unused.

Two claims in the prose are looser than the tables. Section 1 says the evidence does not show whether language and accommodation support are timely or adequately staffed, while the same answer later uses the 3.8-day and 4.7-day waits and the counts of 7 bilingual workers and 5 specialists. Section 8 groups Group C with Group B on residual appeal burden; C’s adjusted appeal difference is +1.1 percentage points, while the large residual is D’s +5.7.

The fairness judgment itself is well supported: B and C carry residual time, document-request, supervisor, and capacity burdens that case mix does not absorb, and D’s fraud path is only partly justified until the risk score is audited.
