8.2

The pipeline is referentially sound and the flattened lifecycles match the stated relations, but the provenance story is internally inconsistent and a few interpretations go beyond what the chosen structures or the narrative actually support.

The construction itself is strong. All 17 events, 12 objects, and the required O2O edges are present, timezone-aware, and joined so relation activity and timestamp cannot drift from `events`. Directions follow a stable subject–verb–object pattern (Order `contains` Item, Package `packs` Item, Delivery `carries` Package, Invoice `bills` Order, Payment settles Invoice). Validation covers uniqueness, orphans, duplicates, and copied columns. Order traces are right: O100 carries both logistics branches; O200 only the P1/D1 branch; shared events are exactly e06, e07, e09, e11, e12, e13, and e15. Package, Delivery, and Invoice shapes, the one-event Payment projection, and the warning that 100 and 150 are not an allocation to O100 versus O200 are all correct. Required discovery calls, dictionaries, and commented viewers are in place, and the Petri-net section correctly refuses a unique causal reading.

The main flaw is the claim that qualifiers keep source facts distinct from analytical propagation. They do not. `operational_context` is used both for participation the narrative states outright (e06 and e07 on O100/O200) and for inferred participation (orders on e08, dispatch, and confirm). `invoice_context` is defined as the payment-to-invoice link, then also applied to both orders, so the primary V1 link and the propagated order links are the same qualifier. `billed` likewise covers V1 and both orders with no role split. A reviewer cannot tell, from the qualifier alone, which E2O rows were observed and which were added for analysis.

`completes_settlement` on M2→V1 is an extra assumption. Both M1 and M2 are described as partial payments, and no invoice total is given, so 150 does not establish completion. That reading then leaks into the Invoice DFG discussion as if it were given.

Section 5 slightly misplaces instance convergence. An OC-DFG is a type-level directly-follows graph; it does not itself show that P1 binds I1/O100 with I3/O200. That is an interaction/event-level fact, and M1–M2 connectivity is only indirect (they never co-occur). The OTG and ET–OT discussions are more careful. The parent/context gloss also fits the structural O2O edges better than Payment→Invoice, even though the concrete payment direction is clear and allowed.

Audit counts, activity-to-type coverage, failure modes, and the alternative policies are coherent with this code. The gaps above are what keep it short of a nearly flawless score.
