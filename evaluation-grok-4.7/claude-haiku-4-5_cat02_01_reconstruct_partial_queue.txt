3.8

The write-up follows the requested section order and correctly computes many fully observed S1 waits and services, and the resource-predecessor windows for P05, P07, P11, and P15 are mostly the right idea. Those pieces do not survive contact with the station-level results, the sensitivity claims, or the code. The reconstructed operating picture is not defensible.

The case table contradicts its own bounds. P05’s feasible S2 start is [09:45, 10:10], so wait is [20, 45] and service is [5, 30]; the table gives wait up to 50 and service [5, 15, 25], while the midpoint is stated as 10:00 instead of 09:57.5. P07’s constrained start is [09:25, 09:55], so wait is [40, 70] and service is [5, 35]; the table gives wait [0, 55, 75]. P11’s central wait is 65 minutes in the note and in §6, but 55 in the table, whose lower end of 35 would overlap P08 on C2. P15’s S2 start and P17’s S2 start are observed, so their waits are 75 and 65 minutes; the ranges [75, 95, 115] and [65, 85, 105] are invented. P16’s censored S2 wait is already ≥85 minutes, so a lower value of 75 is impossible. P21 has already been in service for 30 minutes, so service [0, 30, 60] treats elapsed service as optional. P18, P19, P20, and P22 are shown with no wait, which drops censored waits of at least 50, 35, 15, and 110 minutes.

Completed sojourn does not depend on missing starts: it is S2 end minus external arrival. For P01–P14 that mean is about 146 minutes and is invariant. §6 instead reports a scenario-sensitive mean of roughly 95/105/115 over “22 cases.” Only 14 cases finished both stations. S1 completions are 20, not 22; S2 completions are 14, not 16.

§4’s subperiod counts are wrong in essentially every row. External arrivals are 7, 6, 4, 4, 1, and 0 across the six hours, not 5, 4, 3, 2, 2, and 3; those figures sum to 19. “P01–P05 complete” by 09:00 is false, as is “only P14 ends” in 13:00–14:00. Busy time is divided by the full 360 minutes inside one-hour rows, so 120 busy minutes is labeled 33%. Over the window, observed S1 busy time is on the order of 550–580 resource-minutes out of 720, about 75–80%, not the stated 28–36%. S2 is similarly around three-quarters utilized, not 40–58%, and hypothetical post-14:00 duration must not be counted as in-window busy time. Reported queue lengths of 1–4 are far below the S1 queue, which is already about six cases waiting near 12:00, and below end-of-window S2 WIP of six.

§5 then describes 08:00–10:00 as light load at about 4.5 arrivals per hour. Those two hours contain 13 arrivals, about 6.5 per hour, above S1’s roughly 4.4-per-hour capacity at a 25–30 minute service time. S2, at about 40 minutes, is the tighter capacity constraint, but “S1 has spare capacity” is not a stable finding. The C2 outage is 15 minutes; P08 was already waiting on P06 until 10:50, so the incremental delay is about 25 minutes, not a 55-minute cascade. Little’s Law is only named. The code version keeps cases wholly contained in a bucket, uses one midpoint snapshot, and for S2 filters on service start while measuring sojourn from S1 end.

The Python also misses required semantics. Blanks for P16 and P18–P22 are stored as `MISSING`, then imputed as S2 starts at S1 end, including cases still waiting at 14:00. Mixed strings and numbers will break the wait arithmetic. `format_dataframe` is called, but `insert_case_service_waiting_time`, `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`, and `convert_log_to_time_intervals` are absent, with no comment justifying the last omission. An extra `# Summary` heading violates the required heading set. Rounding is declared as ±2.5 minutes and then ignored in the bounds.
