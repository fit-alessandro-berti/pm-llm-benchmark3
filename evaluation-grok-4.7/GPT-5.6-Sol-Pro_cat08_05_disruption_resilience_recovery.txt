The answer is complete, numerically careful, and strong on the required distinction between service protection and control failure. It uses the phase metrics, fitness figures, and the cited DFG edges accurately; it treats partial recovery as deceptive, separates operational close from compliance close, and builds a concrete mode-based recovery model with product gates, evidence provenance, backlog rules, and residual risk. Those are the core demands of the task.

It is not nearly flawless. The T2/T3 cycle-time tails are largely dropped: hospital p90 15h→21h→18h→16h and the much larger pharmacy and wholesale p90 path (49/118/90/74 and 72/180/160/120) are the clearest temporal signal that median recovery overstated restoration, yet only the T4 pharmacy and wholesale p90s appear. The stated temporal deviation that Backlog queue → Manual pick list exceeded baseline p95 is never used.

Two causal claims are ahead of the evidence. Dispatch without scan is said to have helped preserve hospital delivery, but the DFG does not connect that activity to the hospital fast lane; hospital protection is evidenced by fill rate and the fast-lane edge. Temporary release is said to have likely reduced controlled-substance errors even though missing dual-control rose from 0.2% to 2.9%, and the answer never reconciles that worsening with the claim. It also misses the composition that matters: 1,450 of 1,650 temporary-release cases still flow into Dispatch without scan, so F mostly fed G rather than containing it. About half of packed orders bypassed release entirely (1,800 of 3,600).

The “evidence of resilience” cells for C and G record throughput effects of patterns the answer itself retires, which blurs the column the question asked for. B, J, and K are marked Keep while the rationales require formalization, provenance, and new constraints that are modifications. Section 3 sorts patterns into resilient, shortcut, and conditional, but it does not explicitly isolate which condition is risk appetite rather than timing or evidence quality. “Several times baseline” is loose for T3 rework at 9.0% versus 3.5%. The recovery XOR lists standard electronic fulfillment without binding that choice to mode, so the model as drawn can select the normal path during a full manual outage.

No required section is missing and no reported source figure is misstated. The deductions are for unused temporal evidence, a few unsupported causal links, and classification softness.
