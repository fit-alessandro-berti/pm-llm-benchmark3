6.5

The semantic reading of the source is mostly right: Register Change is the unique start, Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence are unordered, Legal Review is an XOR with a silent skip after Assess Risk only, the verification loop is a do/redo loop with at least one `Configure Trial → Inspect Trial` iteration and `Correct Defect` between repeats, and Release Change is a join of the legal branch and the loop before Archive Change. The non-block cross-dependency (verification waits for both Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence, while Legal Review waits only for Assess Risk) is a sound reason to expect process-tree conversion to fail, and the choice of POWL as canonical storage with BPMN as a derivative view is justified. Sections 6–8 are careful about layout versus behavior.

The required validation is not reliable. `fire_transition` returns a plain `dict` with zero places removed, while `pm4py.get_enabled_transitions` depends on `Marking`/`Counter` indexing that yields 0 for absent places; the explorer therefore raises `KeyError` after the first firing. `discover_footprints` returns a dict, so `ref_fp - cand_fp` raises `TypeError`. When exploration throws, the handler substitutes empty trace sets, and every negative witness is then reported OK because it is absent. N2 and N3 are the same tuple, so the “no iteration” check does not exist. N4 also omits verification, so it does not isolate archive-before-release. No positive witness puts a verification activity before Legal Review, despite INV-08 requiring that interleaving. Reachability is reduced to a transition count mislabeled as states. “Validated preserved” and a blanket “conversion failed” are stated without executing the pipeline, and a truncated `max_states` search can still be treated as a match. The extra top-level title also breaks the required heading set.
