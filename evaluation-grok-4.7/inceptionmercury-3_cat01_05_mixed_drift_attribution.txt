6.5

The write-up covers every required section, correctly treats March as mostly semantic drift, and correctly rejects a single global change point. The later supplier, staffing, priority, and seasonal account is directionally sound, and the January DFG distances that can be checked are arithmetically right. It is still not a reliable diagnosis: several quantitative claims are inconsistent or stronger than the aggregates allow, and the event-level plan would not reproduce the normalization it depends on.

The baseline table is internally inconsistent. March case growth of +27.5 matches a January–February mean of 612.5, but the January row is all zeros, which that baseline does not produce. Fitness “changes” are unsigned drops (March raw 0.375) beside explicitly signed increases, so the same table reads as both gains and losses. Many timeline and hypothesis cells are unreadable as printed (`911` labels, `1.14.8–5.2%`, `40.844.2`, `1543%`, `1224%`, `0.920.55`).

The cycle-time split assigns 8–12, 3–6, 2–4, and 0.5–1.5 hours without stratum cycle times, loop durations, or measured queue time. Those ranges also overlap: extra rework mainly appears as extra queueing and service, while the ±3 hour interaction term does not make the pieces a partition. That violates the instruction not to invent precision. The Euclidean fragment distance double-counts the nearly collinear Request Info pair and gives that loop most of the May squared distance without saying so. Full-month June figures are assigned to a window ending 14 June.

July is not a logging change point. Missing-resource and duplicate rates jump in March and then drift; July is only a peak. The priority rule is also conflated with Senior Assessment. Queue reordering need not raise that rate, the May rate was already 7 before the rule, and the large jump is October’s 22 per 100 cases, where inconsistent historical recording remains a live alternative. “p90 peaks at 110 h in August” is false; November is 121 h. September’s residual delay is attributed partly to seasonal load, but renewal season begins on 1 October. June 15 to July 1 is about two weeks, not three.

The Gini alert “exceeds 2” cannot fire for a coefficient on [0,1]. Several other limits are “2 or 20%” or “5 points or 2,” with the unit unspecified. An absolute mapped-fitness floor of 0.85 would not have flagged the observed trough of 0.86. Making resource-complete, non-supplier-Z, standard cases the primary population hides the supplier and mix shifts that dominate this incident. Rule 5 treats priority as preserving total delay, which unequal service times and nonlinear queues do not guarantee.

In Part B, `format_dataframe` is called with `resource_key`, which is not part of the standard PM4Py signature. The month window ends at midnight on the last day, so that day’s events are dropped. `mapped_copy` walks traces on an object that `format_dataframe` returns as a dataframe, and duplicate removal is an empty `pass`, so the required raw-versus-normalized comparison is not actually implemented.
