The answer meets the required structure, stays multi-hypothesis, and is generally well calibrated: centrality is not treated as good or bad, absence effects are limited to exception cases, compliance risk is not inflated into wrongdoing, automation and vendors are included, and Mira is not removed without a replacement. The PM4Py proposals cover every requested family with real functions, and the executive summary is within length.

The main failure is the reconciliation the question asks for. The claim that working-together weights are “naturally small” because they are computed against all cases is inconsistent with the same table: Alice–Carla at 27% cannot be a share of all cases when Alice appears in only 665 of about 4100 cases. Similar handover volumes (Alice→Carla 320 versus Mira→Carla 350) with very different co-case rates are left unexplained. “Counts as co-occurrence but weakly” is also wrong for this metric: a shared case counts fully; brevity and gaps do not down-weight the edge.

Smaller problems add up. “Queue cleaner” is used as support for a formal desk role even though that remark cuts against formality. A unique role cluster is called “likely” despite activity overlap on reopen and missing-information work. Temps are “partially shielded” without evidence. Inter-event delays to specialists are not separated from Mira’s own 4.2-minute service time. The summary then offers one brief-touch story as the explanation.
