7.5

The three candidates are genuinely different hypotheses, the ledger covers every variant and rule, and the balanced POWL and permissive tree languages do admit all 30 observed cases while keeping D1–D4 and D6. Missing supplier events are treated as a control-flow abstraction rather than true optionality, and the recommendation and data-collection plan match the real ambiguities.

The audit is factually wrong on a frequent case. `R, E, C, S, A, P, Z` is the 7-case variant and is one of the seven sequences in `model_conservative`. Section 6 lists it as excluded, and the stated reason — that no conservative branch continues `R, E, C` with Supplier Confirmation — is false. Section 8 then puts that same trace in both the conservative positive suite and the conservative negative suite, so the check contradicts itself. Only one of the two conservative exclusions is real.

Smaller defects: the permissive script assigns `model_permissive` and immediately overwrites it; validation prints literal `\n` because of over-escaped newlines; playout uses `no_traces=` and only reports failure if the installed signature differs; the suite prints alignment fitness and never asserts expected accept/reject. “High” fitness for the balanced and permissive models understates inclusion that the constructions already guarantee. The conservative underfitting line about “unseen orders of Credit Check and Engineering Review” is loose: both orders are already in that model; what it rejects is new combinations around them.
