8.6

The final analysis is numerically reliable and process-mining-sound. Case-level times, critical branches (A 2, B 8, C 2), sync waits, fork-join spans, and the three counterfactuals (15, 92.5, and 176 minutes) all check out. Fork-join time is correctly the max of branch completions, total flow time is upstream + fork-join + downstream queue + final service, and the write-up correctly shows that summing branch sync waits counts overlapping branch-minutes rather than calendar time. B is ranked above A despite A’s larger mean service, with frequency, q+service tails, and the counterfactuals used as the basis. Zero-benefit cases and final-station masking are handled explicitly, and the queue-versus-release-policy limit is stated rather than assumed away.

The score is held down by a few rigor gaps. The 57% final-station occupancy figure is used to suggest a release policy; moderate utilization with bunched fork-join arrivals can still produce queues, and the stronger check—that a work-conserving two-server schedule would have started several cases earlier, notably F03—is not shown. The 12.5-minute B-queue result is not labeled as a continuous-time assumption against one-minute timestamps. “Adding final capacity” is evaluated only as the upper bound of deleting all downstream queue, which is labeled, but it is not a capacity model. Aggregate-table notation is slightly hard to read. Those are presentation and inference limits, not calculation errors.
