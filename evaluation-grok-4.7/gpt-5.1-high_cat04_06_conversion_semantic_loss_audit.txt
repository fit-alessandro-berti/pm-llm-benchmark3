6.4

The write-up covers every required section and understands the POWL control flow well: registration first, unordered assess/prepare, optional legal after assess, do-while verification, and a join before release. Pipeline choice, exception handling, and the visual-versus-semantics discussion are largely on target. Under a strict reading it still overclaims validation and ships a checker that cannot support those claims.

The audit marks several rows `validated preserved`, and the whole Petri→tree column `conversion failed`, without any execution. The evidence text even says the tree conversion fails only “if” the partial order is not block-structured. That contradicts the cell. `validated preserved` is not justified by unrun converters, soundness prints, or a planned trace diff. `expected preserved`, `not tested`, or an explicitly expected failure were the defensible terms.

Invariant identity is unstable. Section 1’s I5 is “verification waits for both assess and prepare”; section 4’s I5 is “no order between legal and verification,” and later IDs are shifted again. The overlap the source actually states — legal review concurrent with prepare-evidence and with the verification loop — never becomes its own invariant with a positive interleaving witness. Section 5’s “with legal” trace puts legal review entirely before `Configure Trial`, so a converter that serializes legal before the loop still passes every named witness.

The bounded explorer is incorrect for language comparison. Visited states are `(marking, visible length)`, not the visible sequence. Independent transitions that commute (assess/prepare, legal/configure) reach the same marking at the same length; the second interleaving is dropped. Enabled-transition order is not stable, so two faithful nets can show spurious trace diffs, and a serialized net can look equivalent if the surviving order is the only one it has. The exhaustive flag also stays true when the length bound cuts traces. `MAX_VISIBLE_LENGTH = 10` excludes legal-review plus two iterations (11 visible labels). The two “different” negatives `release_without_verification` and `no_iteration` are the same trace. There is no negative for inspect-before-configure, correct-defect inside a trial body, correct-defect with no following iteration, or legal after release.

True concurrency is treated as if footprints and interleaving traces established it. For this net, both orders in the language (and a parallel footprint) are also produced by an exclusive choice of orders. No check looks for a marking where both transitions are enabled. Custom firing ignores arc weights and does not use PM4Py’s execute semantics. `check_soundness`’s boolean result is ignored. Model/trace playout is omitted with no reason. Round-tripped nets are not visualized.

The canonical-model section names the converted Petri net as authoritative and the source POWL as a human-readable spec, then tells future editors to change “the POWL/Petri” model. If those diverge, authority is undefined. The source POWL should remain authoritative; the net is a derivative that must be re-derived and re-checked. Process-tree use is correctly limited to a non-authoritative side path.

Residual uncertainty correctly includes bounds, data, resources, readers, and toolchain bugs. It does not state the larger hole already present when every listed test passes: language and footprint agreement still do not establish step/pomset concurrency, joint enablement, or behavior past the visible bound.
