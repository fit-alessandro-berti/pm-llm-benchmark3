6.8

The audit is structurally complete and gets most of the hard calls right, but several final conclusions are over-specific, internally inconsistent, or thinner than the task requires.

What holds up is the core inventory. Full-trace and post-closure fields (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_handler_team`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, `last_resource_role`) are rejected. The 65% coincidence of final team with the team at `t0` is correctly treated as unusable without an as-of timestamp. The extraction-date SQL bug, the 23:00 document snapshot, the mutable `risk_score_current`, the ±5-minute queue window, and the missing sequence index on Case Z are all identified. Queue length and elapsed time are kept, with the short 1.2-hour prefix read in the right direction. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated as a later, normatively possible sequence, while `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as an ordering artefact. Region and calendar week are kept as predictive signals with proxy and drift limits rather than deleted. The five required sections, the validation ideas, and the Case Z JSON shape are present, and the revised narratives do not retell the leaked appeal or final decision as facts.

The final product still has material errors. `prior_claim_count_before_t0 = 3` is listed as a credible driver for Case Y “after repair,” but the broken window can only add claims, so the repaired count is not known to be 3. `initial_reserve` is used as a defensible `t0` driver and given a severity mechanism, although its lineage is never established and reserves are often revised. Case X is marked `invalidate` while the more contaminated Case Y is marked `recompute`, with no basis for that split. `total_case_duration_hours` is called future leakage only; for a 10-day settlement target it is also near-label leakage, and that circularity is never stated. Section 3 says `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` is usable “after repair,” which the lineage does not support. The appeal remediation suggests eligibility logic at `t0`, although an appeal is not enabled after coverage check. Several driver interpretations still use causal wording (“due to resource contention,” “historically shown”) that the later causal boundaries withdraw but do not fully replace. Predictive validity, causation, actionability, and fairness are not separated cleanly; actionability in particular stays thin. The table also breaks the required severity scale (`None`, `Low–medium`, `See above`), and the bundled Case X row falsely points `claimant_channel` to an assessment that was never made. Employee experience joined as of extraction date is never carried into the control plan.
