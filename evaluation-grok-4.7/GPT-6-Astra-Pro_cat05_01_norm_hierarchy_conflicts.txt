The assessment is unusually rigorous: issue-specific rather than document-ranked, timed to the binding act, careful about new versus merely unapproved suppliers, and unwilling to let ERP order outrun recordings, acknowledgments, or signed PDFs. Threshold direction (“at least” versus “above”), the B4 split between PO sequence and Shift Lead substitution, A6 non-cure, ON-44’s 14 July limit, and the survival of the four-hour recording duty past 18:00 are all handled correctly. Classifications are differentiated, and the alternatives would actually change results for stated reasons. The prospective clause covers authority, scope, thresholds, segregation, new suppliers, record timing, and expiry, and it includes a real anti-normalization control.

It is not nearly flawless.

The clearest evidential breach is P-502. The case record gives clock times only. The analysis nonetheless states as a controlling fact that the CFO email “had not yet been sent,” treats C1 and C3 as satisfied, and builds that assumption into the method section. Case order makes 14 July a plausible inference; it is not established. The A3 noncompliance finding survives either way, because the reported clearance is hours after commitment and D cannot waive it, but satisfied-rule claims that depend on the ECD window do not.

The functional mapping is applied unevenly. Plant approval is provisionally both final business approval and budget-owner approval, and ECD is treated as able to replace the Plant route. On that premise, P-503’s joint authorization is solid enough to make PROC-3 a final business approver. The same premise would also mean a non-independent person exercised the budget-owner function, which is an A2 failure, not an unproven capacity. P-508’s “A1 satisfied” depends on the same unstated equivalence. The section 5 alternative shows the author sees the hinge, but the primary table treats it as established.

C4 is narrower than the hierarchy and pseudo-logic. It displaces ordinary timing and supplier-onboarding. Replacement of the Plant-approval route is at most a B7 argument about undefined “ordinary” steps, yet `overrides_for` lists `ordinary_Plant_route` as a normal ECD effect. R6’s “functional equivalents” widens B4’s absent-and-delegated Shift Lead rule in the same direction. D2’s pre-commitment timing is likewise imported; it is labeled a preventive reading, and on these facts Legal happened to precede commitment.

Smaller defects accumulate. P-503’s deviations cell lists absent Compliance as a deviation after the method says a missing event is unproven; the later prose repairs this, the table does not. P-503 is given provisional ECD scope while engineering substantiation is still missing, against the stated burden on the party claiming the exception. P-506 calls a Finance approval that preceded commitment “mitigating,” which collides with A6’s term for a later act. P-505’s severity is framed around a Compliance failure the amount and supplier status do not support. P-504’s “satisfied rules” cell records the absence of a trigger. P-501’s unknown approver identities are left inside JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION, while similar role gaps elsewhere produce INDETERMINATE; separate approval events make that more defensible, but the confidence gap is still large.

Arithmetic, source restatement, and the main outcome pattern hold. The score is held down by the unsupported P-502 dating, the uneven A1/A2 inference, and the over-broad ECD approval override.
