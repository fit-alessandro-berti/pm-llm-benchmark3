5.4

The diagnosis and redesigns cite many real artifacts (fitness 0.91, 68% fitting traces, R1–R3, 14.1% vs 3.3% complaint rates, segment medians, 82% utilization, portal 30–45%, inflow +15–35%, gate 6–18h), and the required sections, sacrifices, and stop/go structure are present. Those merits do not offset several errors that would mislead an executive.

The fast path is miscounted as ~26% of cases while 2,550 auto-screen fast approvals are already 51% of 5,000. “14.1% of all cases” is wrong: 14.1% is the rate among fast-approval cases (overall reopen rate is 9.2%). The 26% repeat-evidence figure is treated as abandonment. Repair rework (12.5% of repaired high-value devices) is tied to wrong replacement parts, which the log links instead to dispatch→complaint. Edge times are described as activity durations.

Redesigns A and B are the same policy: portal check, fast approval only for low-risk accessories, devices forced onto repair quote, manual work only for evidence or fraud. C adds a gate only on that low-risk fast lane, then still credits the gate with fixing high-value dispatch (EUR 410, 18–22% complaints) and with Premium high-value complaint cuts. High-value cases never enter the gate. A’s rules also contradict themselves and R1: accessories above EUR 400 are sent to fast approval on portal completeness alone, while R1 requires manual or fraud review before fast approval with no accessory exception. R1 permits fast approval after review; banning replacement for all high-value claims is stricter than policy and ignores the observed 96h/120h reviewed-repair medians and 12.5% high-value repair rework. Invented medians (50–80h, 10–14h, 5h vs 10h gates) soften that trade-off. The Pareto table then asserts unsupported “5–10%,” “7–12%,” and “3–8%” cost savings, including a self-contradictory cycle-time cell for C.

Failure modes are specific, but B reverses the fraud flow (fraud precedes fast approval). The pilot mixes segments, mis-sizes 14% as ~70 of 200 cases, and uses 2-week complaint thresholds despite complaint edges at median 50–70h and p90 150–180h, so late reopens are censored. The result is one routing idea written three times, with the compliance and quality mechanisms pointed at the wrong cases.
