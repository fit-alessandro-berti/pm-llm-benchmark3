7.4

The final write-up is a strong estimand-first survival plan: time origin, 72-hour horizon, competing risks versus right censoring, and the four worked cases (resolved at 76, open at 92, withdrawn at 27, transferred at 68) are handled correctly. Method choice is justified for n=32, immortal time and prefix leakage are treated carefully, transfer is not silently called non-informative censoring, and the single code block implements a real Aalen–Johansen/Kaplan–Meier risk set without dropping open cases or inventing timestamps.

It is not nearly flawless. Cohort A is misread: every A prefix starts with `Screen`, including S01 and S15, so they are not exceptions to Screen-first routing. S10 (open at 47h, gap 19h, escalated) is omitted from the below-72 open contrast. The “worst-case” transfer scenario is encoded identically to transfer-as-competing, so it cannot change \(F_R(72)\). Most importantly, S11, S25, and S26 are censored after 72 and are already known non-resolutions at the SLA time; independent-censoring bias applies only after a censoring time, so those long gaps do not drive the 72-hour breach or backlog estimates. The cases that actually leave 72-hour status unknown are the pre-72 censored opens (S10, S15, S24, S29, S31) and pre-72 transfers if censored.
