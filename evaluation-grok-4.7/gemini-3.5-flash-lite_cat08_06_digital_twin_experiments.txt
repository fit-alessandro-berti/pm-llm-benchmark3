4.8

The memo covers the requested outline, but it is not a reliable simulation design. Several methods are internally inconsistent, some claims misstate the process and the tooling, and the code omits required measurements.

The scope treats D3 and D5 as adjacent districts that should be batched together. The evidence given is only that those districts have longer travel times. Bundling is described for cases that share an architect and a district. A deterministic delay of up to 48 hours also conflicts with the later 3-day batching window. Fork-join behavior is missing: parallel planning, fire, and environmental work can hold several resources at once, and decision drafting must wait for every applicable branch. Rework after planning, legal review, and appeal is outside the supplied tree and is never added back as an explicit state transition. “FIFO with dynamic re-ranking” is not a coherent queue discipline. Districts are written as D1–D5 without support, and `urgency_score` has no source.

Arrival intensity is estimated from all event timestamps. That mixes case starts with later activity volume. Architect bursts are added on top of a base Poisson process without removing the 31% of volume those architects already contribute, so demand can be double-counted. Service-time identification contradicts itself: section 2 uses EM or regression, while the assumptions subtract a median service time from the completion stamp. With only one timestamp, that subtraction does not identify waiting time under congestion, and the limitation is not stated. Performance-DFG times are treated as separate waiting and processing distributions; those edges are aggregate inter-event times. `parse_process_tree` is described as recovering routing probabilities. It only parses a string. Routing is then reduced to a multinomial logit that includes `rework_count`, which is a consequence of the path rather than a cause, and that probability model does not implement the rule changes in D2, D5, and D8.

The experiment list includes the baseline, D1–D10, and three stresses, but the trade-off cells state unevidenced effects, including a −45% cycle-time cut. D2 depends on prior violations, which are not in the log. D3 introduces a 5 km radius with no coordinates. D9 changes an unobserved objection process while the heavy tail of public notice is assigned entirely to objections, although council calendars and applicant delay can produce the same tail. Policy-by-stress crosses and common random numbers are absent, so interactions such as D2 reducing the value of D3 and D4 are not identified.

A 90-day warm-up is shorter than the complex-case median of 140 days and its 260-day p90, and the write-up treats burn-in as a path to steady state in a seasonal system with no stated calendar origin. Antithetic variates are named without stream assignment. The bootstrap is described in a way that resamples dependent in-run output as if it were iid. Sobol indices are proposed for the same inputs that may be Pareto or otherwise heavy-tailed and untruncated, where variance need not be finite. Thirty replications do not stabilize a p99 under that tail.

Validation uses token replay fitness and precision as if those scores checked simulated durations. They check language conformance. The temporal split is Year 1 versus Year 2, but the log covers 18 months. Cross-case patterns, the service-versus-wait split, and objection-mixture calibration are not validation targets. “Timesheet summaries” are assumed to exist beyond the reconstructed calendars.

The decision thresholds are presented as statutory limits even though no legal SLAs were supplied. A single 75–85% utilization band is a poor target for part-time fire staff, council blackout weeks, and seasonal peaks. The fairness rule mixes absolute disparity with “15% variance.” Inspection-skip risk is a trade-off in the table and then disappears from the criteria. Weights for the stated MCDA are not given.

The skeleton correctly avoids `pm4py.read_csv` and calls `format_dataframe`, `discover_dfg`, and `discover_performance_dfg`. It still fails the stated code contract. It does not compute case-arrival rate or service and waiting columns. It has no routing placeholder. It parses a short sequential tree rather than the supplied tree and does not state what that simplification removes. `pm4py.case_statistics.get_case_duration` is not a PM4Py API. The arrival placeholder stores seasonality and then samples a stationary exponential. Calendar checks always return true. The numeric claims and the engine are not yet a calibrated basis for preferring an intervention.
