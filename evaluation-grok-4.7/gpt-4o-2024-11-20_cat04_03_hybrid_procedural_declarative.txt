3.5

The write-up has the required ten sections and a few locally correct pieces (the R5/R6 split is named, the three temporal pairs are converted to seconds correctly, and temporal hits are described as warnings). The models, template semantics, and verdicts do not cohere, and several required analyses are missing or contradicted by the code.

The procedural program is not a runnable PM4Py process tree. `ProcessTreeOperator.SEQ` / `PAR` and `identifier=` are not the ProcessTree API (`Operator.SEQUENCE` / `PARALLEL`, leaf `label`). Parent links are unset. As written, the tree starts at the parallel block and ends at reject or activate. `Receive Onboarding Request`, `Release First Payment`, and `Close Onboarding` are absent, while the prose claims they are on the backbone. Under that tree every T1–T8 trace is unfit, so the matrix’s procedural passes are unsupported. Clarification is omitted with the claim that it is not block-structured; a `LOOP` is the ordinary encoding, and dropping it makes T2 unfit while the matrix marks T2 procedural pass. `Sanctions Escalation`, `Obtain Legal Opinion`, `Change Bank Details`, and `Suspend Vendor` are also absent, so T6/T7 are not “policy-dependent” on this model. Risk is an unlabeled XOR, with no disclosure that the class is not a log attribute.

DECLARE does not match the stated rules or the required dictionary shape. Unary `existence` values use `min`/`max` and omit `support`/`confidence`. R3 needs alternate response (each request answered before the next request and before a terminal outcome); `responded_existence` only demands that the reply occur somewhere, and the table calls that same template bidirectional on one row and unidirectional on the next. R4 is response (“eventually after”), not responded existence. The dictionary encodes only one R8 pair; `Compliance Approval` with `Manager Approval` is named in the mapping and missing in `declare_model`. R10’s cap on `Release First Payment` is missing. The rule table covers two constraints and never separates response from precedence, alternate response from chain response, or noncoexistence from nonsuccession. Confidences 0.8–0.95 are attached to mandatory rules.

The chosen R5/R6 policy is Resolution 2, but R5 is then mapped as precedence inside the procedural model, which contains neither activity. The emergency function flags only the case where both timestamps exist and the gap exceeds 24 hours. T6’s gap is 10 hours, so that function does not flag T6, and a missing opinion, a missing `emergency` attribute, or missing joint authorization is silently skipped. Section 8 never gives the two T6 verdicts the task requires.

Log-skeleton `directly_follows` is a closed whitelist in conformance. A singleton `(Manager Approval, Activate Vendor)` rejects every normal successor, including T1. Orientation of `always_before` / `always_after` is never stated. `always_after` is a crisp constraint; the matrix’s “Warning” has no skeleton meaning. R11 appears in no skeleton pair and in no executable check.

`validate_four_eyes` discards the filtered log, never checks `Compliance Approval` as the applicable approval, and never states `org:resource` or the missing-attribute rule. `filter_four_eyes_principle` keeps conforming cases; it is not itself a verdict.

Temporal bounds at `zeta = 2.0` are 96 h, 24 h, and 144 h (lower bounds collapse to 0). T4’s 61 h and T6’s 17 h / 77 h are inside the bands; T5 has no clarification interval to score. The matrix uses `N/A`, `Pass`, and `Policy-dep`, omits cells so several rows do not match the header, and treats T7’s late opinion as “timely but late.”

Against the dictionaries actually written, activity-level results are: T4 DECLARE and log skeleton pass (the only miss is four-eyes); T5 resource passes; T6 DECLARE passes and the 24 h check does not fire; T8 procedural fails and this DECLARE model passes, because reject/activate noncoexistence was never declared. The matrix asserts the opposite on those cells.

Section 9 does not address vacuity, loop and post-activation inconsistency (alternate clarification, R11 re-verification versus at-most-once and a closed directly-follows set), or the risk of reading support/confidence as normative. Section 10 makes temporal profiles authoritative and tells users to surface conflicts as warnings, including contradictions such as T8.
