6.5

The analysis is strong and internally consistent: the evidence ledger covers the variants, rules, and stakeholder claims; the three languages are genuinely different; completion order is not treated as concurrency; missing supplier confirmation is separated from true optionality; and the audit, comparison, recommendation, and uncertainty statement are careful.

The required models are not executable. `Operator.CHOICE` is not a PM4Py operator (`Operator.XOR` is), so the conservative POWL fails on construction. The balanced and permissive trees use `(...)` / `[...]` with unquoted multi-word labels, which is not `parse_process_tree` syntax (`->`, `X`, `+`, `*`, and quoted labels). The validation script depends on those objects and, when playout fails, checks membership against the hand-written expected language, so the positive/negative suite does not independently test the nets. All three candidates are also XOR enumerations of total orders, including the cases where a partial order or optional operator was the stated idea.
