4.5

The memo covers every required heading and, in places, uses the scenario’s real figures correctly (raw rates, V3/V4/V5 shares, the 3.8-day and 4.7-day queues, residual approval gaps). It also avoids the forbidden moves of treating raw approval gaps as proof of discrimination and of deleting language or accommodation steps to shorten cycle time. Those merits do not offset several material errors.

The empirical core invents or swaps statistics. Supervisor review is stated as “80%+ of B vs 50% of A.” The only supervisor figure given is an adjusted difference of +8.0, +5.5, and +2.0 percentage points. That claim also contradicts section 1. “13–24% reopen rates” mixes B’s 13% appeal rate with reopen/rework rates that actually run 20–24%. “31% supervisor overlap” is Group C’s V5 share, not the fact that 2 of 5 accommodation specialists also supervise. The handover story changes across sections: general → bilingual → general in the scenario, then “bilingual → regional officer → bilingual.”

Path interpretation is too coarse. V4’s 19.2 days versus V1’s 5.8 days is treated as the language-support penalty, but V4 also contains a missing-document loop, and V2 already takes 14.9 days. Variant medians are overall, not within-group. The adjusted cycle-time gaps (+2.6, +4.1, +0.9 days) are much smaller than those path gaps, so most of the raw delay is mix. That decomposition is missing. Group D’s largest residual, appeal probability at +5.7 points, is sidelined while the conclusion says D’s routing “works.”

The adjustment strategy does not identify the effects it names. The preliminary controls omit language need and accommodation request, so residuals are not “beyond legitimate complexity.” Sensitivity “tests” do not vary biased covariates. Section 7 would not run and would not answer the question: `discover_performance_dfg` has no `variant_id`; comparing A-on-V1 with B-on-V4 confounds group and path; `filter_case_performance` filters duration, not outcome; `discover_declare` does not take a log skeleton; `resource_attribute` is the wrong parameter; Ledoit–Wolf covariance on categorical fields is unrelated to adjusted disparity. Service time for Language Support Contact is the wrong target for a 3.8-day queue.

Mitigations add unsupported precision ($150k, five hires, a 7-day target that may be looser than the observed 3.8-day median) and monitor approval rates, which returns the design to outcome parity. Section 8 leads with “procedural unfairness” and “unnecessary” friction. The evidence supports a capacity-and-handover concern plus unresolved residual gaps, not that stronger finding.
