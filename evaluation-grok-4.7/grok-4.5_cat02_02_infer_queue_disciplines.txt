6.3

The diagnosis is in the right neighborhood and the decision-table method is real, but the write-up is not close to exact. Several claims are false or internally inconsistent, and the queue that most needs an eligibility reconstruction (QD) has a broken eligible set.

**Factual error on processing times.** Every job’s total service time equals `est_min` exactly, including B01 (14+17+14 = 45). Section 5’s claim that realized lengths differ, with B01’s segments as the example, is wrong. That mistake keeps “noisy/strategic estimates” alive as an explanation and weakens the SPT tests. Those tests are clean: every SPT miss is a true dispatch miss.

**QB FIFO is redefined mid-table.** At 09:31 and 10:14, FIFO means resume B01. At 09:48, FIFO suddenly means “B03 among new jobs,” which drops B01 so the column can match the dispatcher move. A stable FIFO rule predicts resume B01 there too. The observed B03 is a violation (labeled reassignment), not a FIFO hit. The primary label “preemptive priority” is also stronger than the evidence the answer itself reports. Resume of B01 over waiting B03 at 09:31 refutes ordinary preemptive priority, and B05 does not preempt B03 across 09:48–10:00. If B05 was not yet visible at 09:48, B03 actually matches priority over B01 and B04, so the reassignment label is not automatically anti-priority. Section 7 later calls clean preemptive priority the most dangerous inference, which fights the section 1 primary label.

**QC contradicts itself on the decisive fork.** C01 before C07 at 11:40 is exactly FIFO. Section 1 says that choice “fits none of the pure rules”; section 3 says only FIFO/aging explains it. Live SEPT also fails more often than the primary label admits: it fits 10:10, 10:18, and 11:34, and it fails at 10:32 (C04 over C06), 10:54 (C05), and 11:40 (C01). The “SLA override once the deadline is at risk” story is timed wrong. C05 was already infeasible at 10:18 (10:18+40 > 10:50) and was skipped again at 10:32; it was taken only at 10:54, after the deadline.

**QD eligibility is inconsistent.** D-X has mechanical as well as electrical skill, so at 11:20 the waiting set includes D04, D08, and D09, not only D06/D07 and maybe D03. The table omits the mechanical jobs, then says FIFO would start D07 even while noting D03 may be open. D03 arrived earlier, so FIFO would not be D07. The sharp missing comparison is D08 (est 8): it fits before D06’s 11:30 appointment, so idle time supports either an absolute appointment hold or specialist-only routing, and those two were not separated. The “reserve D-F for D05” explanation of the 11:36–12:00 idle is weak. D05 needs 30 minutes and the meeting is in 24, so that idle cannot be used for D05; D-F also does not split the job. Sticky assignment of D10 to D-X is the coherent account. Confidence 88 sits poorly next to “pool vs sticky cannot be fully separated.”

**Smaller problems.** QA’s “strongest contradicting episode” (A05 over A08) contradicts pure priority, not appointment+FIFO. The real stress on that diagnosis is 09:05: A05’s estimate of 15 guarantees A06’s 09:15 appointment is missed, while A07 (est 9) would finish in time, and A06 is also the FIFO choice, so only A03 independently identifies appointments. Scan delay is misused for the 08:30–08:32 QA gap; A02 had been visible since 08:04. Section 5 says it is ranked by plausibility, then places a “high” non-preemption item below a “medium-high” scan-delay item. Episode A in section 4 is already resolved by the observed choice, so it is a weak ambiguity test. Episodes B and C are the ones that earn their place.

What holds up is the macro picture: QA is reactive appointment then FIFO, with priority and SPT refuted between appointments; QB is a hybrid with one urgent preemption, resume, a manual label, and a late FIFO tail; QC is not a stable pure rule; QD is skill-constrained and not global priority or SPT; inferring textbook preemptive priority from QB is unsafe. Format, coverage, and most timestamps are in good shape. The grade is held down by the false estimate audit, the moving FIFO definition, the FIFO self-contradiction on C01/C07, and the incomplete D-X decision set.
