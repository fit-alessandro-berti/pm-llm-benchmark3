8.5

The formal core is strong: loop scope, the visible languages, both-direction counterexamples, and the bounded cardinalities (76, 85, intersection 4) are right, and the P1/P2/P3 verdicts follow from those facts. The bounded-versus-unbounded distinction is also handled correctly: a difference inside the bound proves non-equivalence, while agreement would not.

The write-up is not clean enough for a higher score. The closing claim in §4, that Model A cannot place `Check Revision` after a visible approval, is false and contradicts A-only-2, where the second check follows the first approval. What A forbids is approval before that same iteration’s check. Calling Model A’s `Analyze`/`Check` parallelism a “semantic ambiguity” is also wrong; both orders are specified behavior, and the simplified tree deletes them. Smaller gaps: the P2 relation is not stated as equality of abstraction images as sharply as the other notions, and “not ordered after the global check” is easy to misread.

The simplified tree, preservation table, regression/negative traces, footprint/EMD caveats, and refusal to invent a Petri-net simplicity number are sound. Those strengths do not cancel the ordering error.
