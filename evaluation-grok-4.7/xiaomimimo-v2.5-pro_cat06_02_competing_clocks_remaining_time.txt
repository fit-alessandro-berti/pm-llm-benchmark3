4.5

The forecast is a structured scenario analysis with the right section layout, a max-of-branches join, chemistry’s Friday 18:00 pause, and a Monday release as the modal outcome. Those merits are outweighed by calendar and coherence errors that move the decision-relevant numbers.

The required remaining-hour figures are off by a full day. Friday 18 September 15:30 to Monday 21 September 13:00 is about 69.5 elapsed hours, and Monday 16:00–17:30 is about 72.5–74 hours. The stated 45.5 and 48.5–50 hours land on Sunday, not Monday. The same 24-hour shortfall appears on the P80 band (Monday 18:30 is about 75 hours; Tuesday 09:00 is about 89.5 hours). The Thursday–Friday tail hours are roughly consistent with those dates, so the near-term conversion is internally inconsistent as well as wrong.

QA’s calendar is mishandled. One reviewer is still on duty Friday until 20:00 (4.5 h) and Saturday 09:00–13:00 (4 h). That is about 8.5 reviewer-hours before Monday, enough at a 1.5 h median to work through much of the five-batch queue, while opening-hour arrivals (0.35/h, overdispersed) continue on both of those shifts. Treating the five batches as frozen until Monday 08:00 and cleared only at 11:45 by two reviewers is not a defensible reading of the stated roster. The sensitivity note adds weekend arrivals on top of an unprocessed queue and never subtracts Friday/Saturday service, so the error and the sensitivity point the same wrong way. That invented Monday backlog is what makes scenario 1’s dominant clock “QA backlog” and what pushes the earliest terminal time to Monday 13:00.

The scenario set misses a required case. The four paths are priority-versus-FIFO, a documentation query, and a deviation/confirmation tail. A chemistry rerun (probability 0.10–0.20, priority, triangular 1.0/1.8/3.0 h, hard stop at 18:00) is not its own path; it is only a tail remark on the “normal tests” FIFO row. Weekend readout delay is dismissed as moot rather than shown as its own clock.

The percentile summary does not match the scenario masses. Bands of roughly 15–20% early Monday, 35–40% Monday afternoon, 15–20% Tuesday–Wednesday query, and 10–15% multi-day investigation put the 80th percentile in the query or investigation region. Reporting P80 as Monday 18:30–Tuesday 09:00 describes a rerun that crosses closing, which is too small and too short to be the 80th percentile of this mixture. Scenario 4’s own tail runs to Monday 28 September, while the summary’s severe tail stops on Friday 25 September. Priority is quoted at about 45% but the “smooth” band is 15–20%; the FIFO “normal” band stays near 35–40%. The same clean-path filter is not applied to both, and the non-overlapping “normal” labels plus the anomaly bands leave a large gap.

Other quantitative choices are asserted rather than derived. “Triples” the borderline and contamination rates is not in the evidence; the dependence is real, but the factor is not, and no total-probability mixture is shown. “Batch Rejected <2%” and the 0.5–1.0 h rejection step are not connected to any stated critical-defect probability. Ahead-of-queue samples are given B-417’s service law without saying so. “Urgent” outranking “high priority” is what stops a Friday chemistry finish (the first free analyst has about 1.6–2.1 h before 18:00, and the minimum service is 1.5 h); that ordering is absent from the ledger, yet it is also what makes weekend on-call almost irrelevant. QA’s 3.5 h upper service percentile never enters the ranges, so Monday afternoon is tighter than the given service law allows. Confirmation incubation is collapsed to “Tuesday ~09:00”; from a Monday morning indication, 20–30 h plus a staffed readout runs from Tuesday morning into Tuesday afternoon, and an on-call Sunday indication would be earlier. Scenario 4’s earliest terminal time of Thursday 08:00 does not follow from a Tuesday join plus a 3-day laboratory investigation.

The parallel-join logic, the Friday chemistry cutoff, and the separation of queue time from service time are the parts that hold. The elapsed-hour arithmetic, the QA Friday/Saturday capacity, the missing rerun path, and the quantile–scenario mismatch are the parts that do not.
