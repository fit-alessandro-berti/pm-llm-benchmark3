6.5

The final answer is complete, well organized, and reaches the right decision: conditional go, with no universal copy of Site A, no document check before originals receipt, and a segmented pilot. Co-interventions are treated as confounders, and the nine-hour median drop is not treated as an identified effect. That core judgment is sound.

It is not nearly flawless. Required comparison coverage omits loops as their own process feature. The transport graph includes a spurious `digital_documents -> originals_required` edge and does not show originals, the case lock, or role split as modifiers of the treatment-to-overlap path. The section 2 explanation sits on the 180-word floor and stays shallow on why a similar control flow fails to transport.

The scenario table is the weakest quantitative part. Optimistic median change is −6 to −9 hours, while the same answer’s transfer bound and recommendation are 4–7 hours. Eligible shares are not derived from the given directly-follows counts or the 4/7/3/2 staffing pools. Both “tail worsens” scenarios have median ranges that cross zero, so none cleanly shows average improvement with a worse tail.

The pilot names the right primary outcome and separates mechanism failure from implementation failure, but case-level randomization on tiny shared pools does not control interference, legal cases are both inside the primary stratum and “observation only,” and “16 weeks ≈ 860 cases” treats 16 weeks as four months. Power is asserted, not calculated.

The PM4Py fragment has the right outline, but the POWL constructors (`OperatorType`, `add_order`, activities wrapped as one-child XORs) do not match PM4Py’s POWL API, and the legal branch always re-executes document check and assessment before any supplement. That model would not faithfully encode Site B.
