The answer is unusually strong: it respects the required seven-part structure, stays on the observed paths and attributes, and refuses the main failure modes (blanket removal of air or dedicated vans, ignoring rework and obsolescence, treating missing SP2 documentation as proof of waste, and adding incompatible units into a fake lifecycle score). The portfolio arithmetic checks out. Share-weighted segment averages do put made-to-order at about 87% of recorded energy and 85% of scrap on 34% of orders, and critical-downtime at about 41% of transport emissions on 11% of orders. The 4–6× air factor converts correctly to roughly a 75–83% drop, and 2.8 kWh to 1.3 kWh is about a 54% per-item drop. Policy thresholds, variant statistics, and the baseline late-rate table are quoted accurately. Rebound coverage is specific, including batch-induced express recovery, obsolescence, packaging-versus-damage, inspection bottlenecks, machine concentration, partial-shipment withholding, and customer rebound.

Deductions are for small but real slips. “A small processing-time saving” on CNC_B is not supported; the log only says CNC_B is faster, while the cited CNC_B path is slower because it includes rework. In the effects table, “Transport ↑ versus air” sits in the cycle-time column and can be read as an emissions increase, contradicting the transport cell. The 54% and 75–83% figures are endpoint or mode ratios, and although later caveats say not to apply them to all orders or treat them as causal, the cells still read like expected intervention effects. Setup and idle energy are placed inside the boundary but omitted from the uncertainty list, so it is unclear whether `energy_kwh` contains them. The partial-shipment target uses the 7% variant while the directly comparable backorder edge is only 220; the general DFG-reconciliation warning does not identify that clash. Section 6 also never states a non-additive net direction for the combined package after correctly warning that the rows must not be summed.

No constraint is violated, and no major figure is invented. The remaining gap is precision of wording and a few unclosed data tensions, not the reasoning.
