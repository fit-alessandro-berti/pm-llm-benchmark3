6.2

The write-up is complete, cautious about Site A’s confounded before/after package, and right to refuse an across-the-board immediate Document Check. Those strengths do not offset broken formal structure in the places the task treats as decisive: the transport graph, the quantitative baseline, and the POWL net.

**Transport graph.** Required node names are present, and the prose is inside the word limit, but the graph does not encode the claimed mechanism. `treatment_parallelization` reaches `true_overlap` and then only `recorded_overlap`. There is no edge from treatment, eligibility, or true overlap into `waiting_before_assessment` or `cycle_time`, so parallelization is causally inert for the outcomes. `legal_review -> eligible_parallel_start` is the wrong gate: legal review is downstream of Assessment. `assessment_capacity` and `legal_capacity` point into `true_overlap` without a queue-control story. `cycle_time -> rework` together with `rework -> cycle_time` makes the graph cyclic, which standard transport formulas do not support. The narrative says overlap removes idle time; the adjacency list does not.

**Quantitative scenarios.** The 3.4 h clock is not the Site B intervention effect. Site B already allows Identity Check and Originals Receipt to start after Register. Identity service (3.4 h) sits inside the 18 h receipt delay, so original-required critical path is about `max(3.4, 18) + 8.1 = 26.1 h`, not `3.4 + 18 + 8.1` versus that quantity. The 3.4 h “savings” on that subgroup is an already-legal overlap, and it is exactly zero if that overlap already happens. Only the original-exempt slice has a real identity–document service overlap of about 3.4 h, and only if those checks are sequential today and queues are ignored. The optimistic row still claims a 3–6 h all-case median cut, above both that arithmetic and the digital-only row’s 0–2 h all-case effect. Sign convention breaks inside the table: optimistic “3 to 6 h” means a reduction to 38–41 h, while bottleneck-shift “1 to +3 h” is paired with 41–47 h, which is −3 to +3 h. Confidence is undefined (probability of the world versus precision of the range). The tail-worsening case is present, and the 360/1,360 and 360/3,400 supplement shares are right, but the headline structural number used again in the recommendation is the wrong baseline.

**Model.** Under `pm4py.objects.powl.obj`, `Activity`, `Choice`, and `ProcessModel` are not constructors; activities are `Transition`, exclusive choice and loops are `OperatorPOWL`. The net would not import. Even if those names existed, the edge list is the wrong process. `identity_receipt_join` is fed only by Identity Check and then precedes Document Check, so the two verifications are sequential. `receipt_branch` has no edge to Document Check, so the statutory originals gate is missing. In a strict partial order every listed node executes; edges from each assessment/legal `Choice` into the next legal decision and into the unrolled supplement chain make Legal Review and both supplement cycles mandatory on every case, while direct edges to Notify do not create XOR exits. The filter `if (source, end) and target is notify` is always true for Notify targets because a non-empty tuple is truthy; later code puts those edges back by accident. Comments say identity and document stay independent and that originals gate document work. The edges do the opposite. Soundness of an always-run AND skeleton would not repair that. `convert_to_petri_net(..., variant="strict")` and `play_out(net, n=5)` are not a reliable match to the library’s signatures.

**Pilot and WIP.** The factorial split of scheduling versus huddles, stratum guardrails, and fidelity-versus-shift interpretation are the right design idea. The support targets are not feasible. About 3,000 of 3,400 six-month cases, four arms, and 300 original-exempt high-complexity cases per arm exceed the base rate: 38% × 48% of 3,400 is only about 620 such cases in total. Team-week assignment on shared pools of 4/7/3/2 people, especially 2 legal reviewers, leaves interference larger than the admission-ceiling note admits. Pool caps of 6/8/4/2, copied from Site A’s per-reviewer limit of six, will not carry anything like 18.9 arrivals a day. Counting blocked original-required document tasks against the same document cap as executable work lets the 62% fill the cap and starve the exempt cases the pilot is supposed to learn from. Stop-on-p90-worse-than-+20 h versus expand only if the p90 bound is under +5 h leaves a wide unexplained band. Calendar-day throughput of 18.9 also blurs elapsed cycle time with staffed capacity.

**What holds up.** Section 1 covers the required features and correctly separates permission, execution, measurement, and case mix. Section 3 keeps the nine-hour median in the confounded-package column and treats API, FTE, marketing, huddles, rollout, and timestamp changes as separate rows. Section 4 names the right failure signatures, including median-up/tail-down and back-dated overlap. The recommendation is an appropriate conditional go: exempt/digital cases first, statutory order untouched, no promise of Site A’s nine hours, expansion tied to a concurrent contrast and verified overlap. The eight assumptions are testable. None of that repairs the inert transport graph, the sequential originals baseline, or a candidate net that cannot start Document Check in parallel and does not wait for originals.
