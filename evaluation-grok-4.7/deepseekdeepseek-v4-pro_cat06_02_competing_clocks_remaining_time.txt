The required scenario structure is present and several local clocks are right: incubation ready Sunday 12:00–20:00, Friday chemistry capacity about 2.1–3.4 analyst-hours, risk-priority B-417 cannot finish Friday and resumes Monday about 08:12–12:00, the join is a maximum rather than a sum, and rerun, contamination, query, and investigation are separated with overlapping probabilities.

The forecast summary is not usable. Elapsed time from Friday 15:30 to Monday 21 Sep 16:30 is 73 hours, not ~49 hours / “2.0 days.” Tuesday morning is about 89–95 hours, not 55–80; Wednesday afternoon through Friday midday is about 120–165 hours, not 80–130; Monday 28 Sep morning is about 233 hours, not inside 130–220. The hour column and the clock column describe different futures.

That error sits on a deeper contradiction. The decomposition correctly makes chemistry, especially FIFO, the smooth-path gate, so weekend on-call readout (Sunday) almost never moves the join—savings are about 0–1.5 hours when chemistry is already done at Monday open. Section 5 then ranks that authorization first and claims it can shift the join by up to 20 hours.

Other bounds are too tight or illegal. Four ahead samples at the long end of triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) push FIFO chemistry to Tuesday morning, but the smooth path caps chemistry at Monday 16:30. Scenario 1’s “2–6 hour” QA wait-plus-service conflicts with the same section’s ~4.8-batch, ~6-hour wait and a 16:00–20:00 finish. Contamination readout at Tuesday 05:30 ignores microbiologist hours (08:00–18:00). The contamination scenario’s earliest Wednesday also assumes an investigation whose stated probability is only 0.30–0.55. Excursion dependence is discussed, but never turned into a joint rerun-plus-contamination timeline, and P(no on-call) is multiplied into the smooth path even though that event barely changes its terminal time.
