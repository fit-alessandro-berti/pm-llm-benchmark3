4.5

The recommendation is the right class of decision — conditional, segmented, and unwilling to treat Site A’s 41-to-32-hour drop as an identified effect — but the answer is not reliable enough to act on. Required prose is short of both length floors (the transportability explanation is about 110 words against 180–280; the recommendation is about 180 against 250–400). Several tables are numerically unusable, the causal graph does not connect modifiers to the effect path, the pilot’s arithmetic contradicts itself, and the Site B model violates its own partial order.

The evidence-transfer table fails its coverage rule. Completed volume, p90, pre-assessment wait, both service times, appeals, the absent negative control, and the separate “two reviewers” co-intervention are missing or collapsed. The figures that remain are concatenated into values that are not the data: “4132 h,” “871 %,” “4840 %,” “1820,” “11.812.4 %.” Confidence scores attached to those strings cannot be audited. Co-interventions are named, which is the right instinct, and then left unquantified against the only decomposition the data support: serial identity-plus-document service is about 10.7 hours, parallel makespan is about 7.2, so overlap itself is worth on the order of the 3.5-hour identity duration, while the observed 6-hour wait cut and 9-hour cycle cut are larger than that mechanism.

The transportability graph includes the requested names and some `S_` nodes, then breaks as a causal object. `role_specialization -> identity_capacity, document_capacity` and the reversed `cycle_time <- ...` line are not an adjacency list. `digital_documents -> originals_required` points the channel effect the wrong way. `API_latency -> identity_capacity` treats a query-time change as a change in headcount. `assessment_capacity -> waiting_before_assessment` sends a downstream resource to an upstream clock. `case_complexity -> legal_capacity` confuses demand with capacity. Originals, complexity, and role specialization never meet `treatment_parallelization -> true_overlap -> cycle_time`, so the diagram does not show non-transportability. The prose states a shared mechanism of “earlier identity completion.” With document service at 7.2 hours against identity at 3.5, the critical path is the document activity; identity overlap removes the shorter summand. Site B’s 18-hour originals delay, when required, dominates both checks, and that comparison is absent.

Scenario ranges inherit that gap. Signs are mixed (“6 to 9 h” versus “+10 to +25 h”), and there is no mapping from Site A’s service times, Site B’s 20-hour pre-assessment wait, the 18-hour originals delay, or the pool sizes 4 / 7 / 3 / 2 into the intervals. The digital-only row assumes no IT change and carries the highest confidence (55), while the rest of the answer says the single-owner lock blocks true overlap. Eligible share is repeatedly “38%” for “digital and non-legal.” Those filters are 38% and 60%. Their intersection is 38% only if every digital case is outside legal review; under independence it is about 23%, and the lower bound is near zero. The optimistic 6-to-9-hour band reproduces the confounded Site A cycle-time change on a subset the service-time bound does not support. The bottleneck-shift row does supply an average-gain / tail-loss case, which the specification required, without a capacity calculation that would make +5 to +15 hours on the p90 a result rather than a label.

Process structure is right on the main prohibition: Document Check cannot be created at registration when originals are legally required, and 2,108 originals-to-document follows match 62% of 3,400. It then slips. One sentence makes Assessment wait for Legal Review; the DFG and the later order both put Legal Review after Assessment (1,360 assessment-to-legal versus 1,220 and 720 assessment-to-decision). The textual “strict partial order” adds `supplement -> document` on top of `document -> assessment -> legal -> supplement`, which is a cycle. Rework of 17% is used as if it were the supplement loop; supplements are 360 cases, about 26% of legal reviews and about 11% of volume. The originals segment is treated as ineligible residual sequence. Rules 2 and 3 already allow Identity Check to run while originals are outstanding; the lock, not the statute, is what serializes that pair. Copying Site A’s control flow is illegal for 62% of cases; transporting the overlap mechanism would mean concurrent identity during the 18-hour wait, with Document Check still gated. That larger, lawful slice is never estimated. Assigning Originals Receipt to the document pool treats an external delay as reviewer work. The copied WIP cap of six is not resized to pools of 4 and 7.

The pilot has the right primary contrast inside a legally feasible stratum, plus logging that could separate real overlap from back-entered timestamps. It does not survive its own numbers. Six hundred cases per arm in eight weeks on roughly 38% of a 3,400-case half-year is about three times the accrual that stratum can produce; “minimum four weeks” adds a third accrual rule. Case-level randomization shares four identity reviewers, seven document reviewers, three assessors, and two lawyers, so SUTVA fails. “Separate team queues” would be a different unit of assignment and cannot be staffed at the legal pool. Originals cases are then called a within-site control even though they are ineligible and have a different critical path. Supplement-loop monitoring and a 20% supplement stop rule sit on a stratum defined to exclude legal review. The expansion rule, as written, advances the rollout when median reduction is 4 hours, p90 change is +5 hours, and rework rises by 2 points — the tail and quality clauses are harm thresholds with the inequality missing. Abandonment on supplements above 20% does not match a non-legal pilot.

The code parses both Site A trees and draws Assessment after identity and document. It does not build the required Site B model. `OperatorPOWL` and `SilentTransition` are imported and unused, so legal review is mandatory, originals are mandatory, and Approve and Reject are both ordered rather than exclusive. The supplement edge cycles a strict partial order. `po.add_edge` is not the usual POWL order API, and soundness is invoked through `pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils.check_soundness` instead of `pm4py.check_soundness`. A net that must approve and reject, must visit legal review, and can return to document check is not a sound workflow net of this process. The closing comment correctly lists data-dependent gates, capacity, locks, and durations as unencoded; the model also fails to encode the optional branch and the loop it was asked to encode.

The eight assumptions are in the requested form and several are testable (lock removal, timestamp audit, assessment load). The eligibility assumption that digital cases carry no legal flag is untested in the design that depends on it, and the 38% intersection is never listed as an assumption. Assumption 4 concerns supplements in a pilot that excludes them. The register does not state the critical-path claim the scenarios require: that a 4–7 hour median gain fits inside a 3.4-hour identity activity once the document activity is already the longer parallel branch.

Taken together, the answer recognizes confounding, statutory order, and the case for a limited pilot, then supplies corrupted inputs, a disconnected selection diagram, inconsistent eligible shares, an infeasible sample-size plan, and a cyclic process model. That is a structured draft with the correct decision label, not an analysis whose quantities or model can be transported.
