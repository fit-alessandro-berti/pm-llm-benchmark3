5.5

The headline is right and the decision advice is usable: Orion’s crude +6.72 h gap reverses inside every complexity stratum, so a 50% handoff to Nova is not justified and a small routing pilot plus a queue/compliance-capacity change is safer. That is not enough for a high score.

Section 1 treats night-shift share and compliance-required share as evidence that Orion causes delay. Those are mix differences, i.e. reasons the crude contrast is confounded. It never separates total, direct, and mediated effects, and it never says that within-stratum contrasts are still shift-imbalanced (high and medium Orion are entirely night). Confidence 78 for `mainly confounded` is too firm given n=30 and failed positivity. The later claim that the penalty is “largely explained” by case mix, shift, and compliance treats complexity stratification as identification.

The graph labels assumed arrows as facts (`resource_group → shift`, `shift → arrival_load`) and adds `wait_before_review`, which is outside the required variable set. Treatment paths from `resource_group` into queueing and service are missing, so the graph cannot represent the harmful-Orion hypothesis the text still entertains. The variable table has no delimiter row and a duplicated header. Several roles are tied to the wrong estimand, and `rework` is called a collider without a group arrow into it.

Section 4 copies the supplied stratum means correctly, then asserts that within complexity, wait is higher when compliance is required. In the only stratum with compliance variation, medium, the opposite is true (Orion compliance-yes mean wait about 8.6 h versus about 9.9 h without; Nova shows the same direction). High complexity cannot inform that contrast: compliance is universal in both groups. “Despite more compliance” in the high stratum is therefore false. Day-versus-night and wait-versus-service rows contain no auditable arithmetic.

Two falsification predictions point the wrong way. If Orion were intrinsically slower, matched pairs should show Orion slower, not “no within-pair difference.” A negative-control outcome affected by the same confounders should differ by group if confounding is real, not show no difference. The compliance/rework hypothesis is not confronted with identical high-complexity rework rates (50%) plus universal compliance.

The recommendation is about 90 words, short of the required 120–200. The Python block loads the cases and can reproduce crude, stratified, and complexity-standardized means, but it changes C10 from `No appeal` to `Appeal`, never builds directly-follows relations, and does not use PM4Py (`parse_event_log_string` / `discover_dfg`) as required. The five residual uncertainties are the cleanest part of the answer.
