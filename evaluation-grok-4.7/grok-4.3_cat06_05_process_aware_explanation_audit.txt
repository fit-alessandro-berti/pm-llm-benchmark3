6.0

The audit correctly drops the obvious post-closure and full-trace fields, catches several real lineage bugs, and keeps queue length, registration age, and initial complexity as non-causal associations. Those strengths are undercut by systematic mistakes about what is actually knowable at `t0` and by revised narratives that still explain the contaminated scores.

The sequence block is the main failure. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is future at `t0` but normatively legitimate after Partial or Deny. `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is different: it is process-impossible and, on Case Z, same-timestamp with no sequence index. The table uses different labels, then gives both the same “critical / remove” treatment and never states that distinction. Worse, `Documents Requested -> Documents Received`, `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened`, and `Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded` are marked valid, low-severity prefix patterns. At a cutoff immediately after the first coverage check, none of those events has occurred. They are future-possible paths, and the damage-to-decision edge is full-trace leakage of the fast path. Section 3 then promotes the document and fraud edges to “defensible predictive signals.” A prefix restriction in the remediation column does not repair that classification.

Other lineage calls are too coarse or invented. `final_handler_team` is a closure-time field, not merely a mutable attribute; the 65% overlap with the team at `t0` does not make the warehouse value admissible. `initial_reserve` is treated as frozen at registration although no lineage is given. `calendar_week` is known at `t0` and is a policy/holiday confounder, yet it is repeatedly placed with region under disparate-impact review. `num_document_requests_total` is correctly rejected, but a prefix recount is not an early rework warning: at this `t0` it is normally zero. The ±5-minute queue join is real residual timing risk and is under-weighted as low.

Section 2 names the right check families, then blurs them. “Reject any feature whose value changes after `t0`” would discard legitimate snapshotted congestion. “Reachable without future events” collapses enabled-now, future-possible, and impossible, which is the distinction the audit was supposed to enforce. Mined DFG edges are correctly denied normative force in that section, then recommended again in section 5.

The case rewrites cite 0.82 and 0.71 as if the surviving features produced them. On Case X, `appeal_seen_anywhere` (+0.26) outweighs queue (+0.14) and complexity (+0.11); after removal, those probabilities are not interpretable. `claimant_channel` is judged valid and then omitted. On Case Y, `prior_claim_count_before_t0` disappears despite the `extraction_date` predicate being a high-severity defect. Case Z’s uncertainty note misses the same-day document snapshot and week 52. Section 5 covers the requested topics, but rank-stability against the old explanation would anchor the clean model to leaked rankings, and “recompute every top-12 feature on the prefix” is impossible for duration, appeal, final team, and full-trace variant.
