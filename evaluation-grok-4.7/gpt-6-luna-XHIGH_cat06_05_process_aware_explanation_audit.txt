The audit covers every global top-12 feature, every sequence pattern, and every local driver, including the opaque residual and the extraction-time employee-experience join. It separates direct label leakage (`total_case_duration_hours`), complete-trace fields, closure-populated attributes, mutable scores, the `<= extraction_date` prior-claim predicate, the nightly document snapshot, and the ±5-minute queue join. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated as a later-legitimate reconsideration edge that is simply unavailable at `t0`, while `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as unavailable, timestamp-ambiguous, and normatively inconsistent. Congestion, elapsed time, frozen complexity, and region are kept under different standards, and prediction, causation, actionability, and fairness are distinguished. The conformance suite and the section 5 plan hit the requested checks, including repeated settlement decisions and the limit of a frequent directly-follows edge.

The revised explanations undercut that standard. Section 1 says removing leaked inputs changes the model and that the displayed attributions must be recomputed rather than relabeled. Section 4 then places the contaminated magnitudes (+0.14, +0.11, −0.03, −0.05, +0.12) inside `credible_drivers`. Those coefficients come from a fit that also used the appeal flag, `final_decision = Partial`, the full-trace variant, and an unaudited residual. A revised t0 account can name candidate inputs and withhold a probability; it cannot keep those numbers as the explanation.

Secondary defects keep this short of a nearly flawless audit. The claim that closure-before-assessment fails the normative check unless ordering “resolves” the tie is ambiguous: confirming that order would still be a violation, and reversing it would remove the edge rather than make it valid. Immediate damage assessment is described as generally enabled at coverage completion, without the low-complexity fast-track guard. `prior_claim_count_before_t0` and `final_handler_team` are rated a step too mildly: the SQL cutoff is a systematic as-of failure, and the team field is a closure attribute even in the 65% of rows where it later equals the t0 team. The queue guidance both allows a documented ±5-minute tolerance and tells the reader to reject every future snapshot inside that tolerance.
