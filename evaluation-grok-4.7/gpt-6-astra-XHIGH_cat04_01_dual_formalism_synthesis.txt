The POWL model is exact for the stated completion-trace language, and the witness table is internally correct. The process tree is a real under-approximation, but it is not shown to be the most faithful compact unique-label tree, and the reported PM4Py execution results are asserted rather than evidenced.

The dependency specification, activity counts, trial projection `ET IT (AP ET IT)*`, and release join match the narrative. Risk is an XOR between supervisor review and an unordered mandatory quality/regulatory pair. Supplier consultation is zero-or-one, enabled after analysis and joined before release. Binary loop children are in the right order, so adjustment sits only between iterations and cannot follow loop exit. The N-poset argument on AR, PV, SR, and ET correctly shows why unique visible leaves under sequence and parallel cannot be exact. A5 is a genuine separator: intended and allowed by the POWL, rejected by the tree at SR. F1 violates the cross-branch containment-to-planning edge; F2 and F3 correctly isolate premature release and a second trial without adjustment. The incomparable root pairs are exhaustively and correctly classified. Nesting is sound: trial, approval, and supplier are composite nodes, so labels are not duplicated in the root. Soundness is properly distinguished from inevitable termination, and the validation plan targets silent skip, empty redo, and label copying.

The main modeling gap is the tree policy. `SEQ(RD, AND(CM, AR), AND(...))` adds AR before planning, and containment before design, reviews, and consultation. That erases the split the scenario highlights. Another compact conservative tree, `SEQ(RD, AND(SEQ(CM, PV), SEQ(AR, DC)), AND(TRIAL, APPROVAL, SUPPLIER), RM, AC)`, preserves that split and approval–trial overlap, but delays approval and supplier until both work products finish. The two languages are incomparable. The write-up treats the barrier as the faithful unique-label choice without making that tradeoff. The equality `L_tree = L_int` plus those extra precedences is nevertheless accurate, as is `L_powl = L_int` under the visible-trace abstraction.

Section 7 states that both models were converted in PM4Py 2.7.23.8, both nets were sound, and all 16 model verdicts matched alignments. No diagnostic output is shown, and those checks are then redescribed as a future plan. The language argument does not depend on that claim, but an unevidenced runtime result should not have been stated as fact. The self-critique correctly covers hidden data and the operational cost of the barrier, yet it never identifies the uncompared tree alternative as a residual modeling weakness.
