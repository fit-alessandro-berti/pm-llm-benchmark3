The final answer meets every required heading, mechanism row, intervention, dashboard element, and constraint, and its numbers match the tables. It correctly treats the pattern as a mixture: early improvement is confounded by falling complexity, priority, and queue; later speed with rising errors is concentrated at high WIP and is stronger for juniors; fatigue and circadian effects remain possible but are not identified. Mediator caution on service time, position endogeneity, complete-case DFGs, detection-versus-origination, and the rejection of raw-rate penalties and quality-check removal are all sound.

Deductions are for smaller gaps, not a wrong conclusion. `cluster_log` is never used, and the four PM4Py filters are only named, not shown. Enrichment calls are not carried back into the modeling frame. Medium WIP is barely used, so the dose-response is thinner than the table. “Early work is already relatively low-error” is loose: positions 1–5 are 3.4%, above the 2.5% middle, and no first-hour error rate is given. “Late inherits queue 70” also smooths Day’s end queue of 75 and the overlapping Day/Late hours. Those are precision and completeness issues, not a failure of the argument.
