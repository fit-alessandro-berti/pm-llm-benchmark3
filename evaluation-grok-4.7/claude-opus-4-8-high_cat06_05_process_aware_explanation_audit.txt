7.4

The audit is conceptually strong on the main distinctions, but it is not nearly flawless: one required pattern is missing, several severities are inconsistent, and the case rewrites still treat contaminated values and leaked attributions as if they survive.

The required inventory is incomplete. All 12 global features and the local-only fields are present, and appeal-after-decision is correctly separated from the same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` artifact. `Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded` (−0.09) is never audited. That pattern is normatively legal and entirely post-`t0`, so it is future leakage of a fast path, not an impossibility and not an early signal that “direct progression avoids breach.” Employee experience is an unrequired but sound extra.

Severity and enablement are not stable. `final_handler_team` is only high, while `last_resource_role` is critical, despite the same defect: the stored value is end-of-case state, and a 65% coincidence with the `t0` team does not make the closure field partially valid. `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` is only medium and labeled “not enabled.” Occurrence of that next activity is unavailable at `t0`; enablement is unknown and trigger-dependent, not known-disabled. It belongs with the other future patterns at high or critical. `appeal_seen_anywhere` is called process-impossible, while the appeal pattern is correctly called later-legitimate; those phrases fight each other. `prior_claim_count_before_t0` (`<= extraction_date`) can include the current claim and later claims and is closer to critical than high. `same_day_document_count_snapshot` repaired to uploads before `t0` is not “≈0”: registration attachments can exist even though `Documents Requested` cannot yet have occurred. The ±5 minute queue join is accepted inside tolerance; a snapshot after `t0` is still forward-looking and should be last-snapshot-at-or-before-`t0`.

Section 2 covers availability, order, ties, whole-trace fields, enablement versus future-possibility, and a second `Settlement Decision Recorded` only after appeal following Partial or Deny. The DFG-versus-norm caveat is right. The aggregation rule “strictly `< t0`” is not: admissible state is at or before `t0`, with a sequence index to break equal timestamps. Name-suffix checks would also miss `risk_score_current` and the SQL bug; those depend entirely on the timestamp rule.

Section 3 keeps the right classes and the four standards (predictive, causal, actionable, fairness). Residual errors: `handler_team_at_t0` is staffing state, not congestion; “disparate treatment” should be disparate impact or proxy risk; `initial_reserve` has no lineage and, until a timestamp exists, belongs in “unusable until repaired,” not in defensible signals.

Section 4 has the right status split and does not narrate X/Y’s appeal, partial decision, or variant as known futures. It then overclaims. In Y, `prior_claim_count_before_t0 = 3 (after SQL repair)` preserves a value the broken predicate does not justify. In both X and Y, directions and “elevated risk” / “primarily congestion and complexity” are SHAP pieces from a leaked model whose base-plus-other term is large and uninspected; those signs are hypotheses until a prefix refit. Z’s note hits the tie, the mutable score, and the end role, then omits `same_day_document_count_snapshot = 6`, which is on that explanation and can contain post-`t0` uploads. Suggesting queue, complexity, and elapsed time as Z’s replacement drivers invents features the case listing does not show. Week 52 is a confounded but time-valid control, not an ambiguity on par with ordering failure.

Section 5 hits every requested control in a sensible order: point-in-time feature store, as-of timestamps, prefix refit, conformance gates, recalibration, stability, proxy review, and drift. It still does not require a temporal evaluation split so the repaired features are not re-scored on extraction-time rows.

The answer would be an 8.5–9 audit if the missing transition, the preserved count of 3, the Z document snapshot, and the leaked-SHAP residual story were fixed. Those are material, so the score stays well below excellent.
