7.5

The report covers every required heading and constraint, uses the supplied figures carefully, and keeps the disparity-versus-discrimination distinction mostly calibrated. The bottom line is appropriately hedged, but it then treats Group C’s small adjusted approval gap as evidence the process is “relatively accurate,” which the same answer correctly says cannot be inferred without eligibility ground truth. It also labels higher risk and incomplete documentation as “legitimate case-mix” after arguing those measures may themselves be biased.

Elsewhere, group-level Submit → Identity Verification waits are misread as in-person waits, and the negative control is a step the data already show differs by group. The PM4Py plan names the requested techniques, but several calls are wrong or misleading: `filter_variants(['V6'])` is not a variant filter, `filter_paths_performance` is missing `paths` and is described as if it finds slow edges, `discover_declare(..., min_support=0.8)` uses the wrong argument, and service-versus-wait insertion is proposed on a single-timestamp log without noting that service time is not identified. `get_all_case_durations` is also treated as a case-feature table.
