The answer reaches a defensible `conditional go` and names real confounders (mix shift, added reviewers, API latency, huddles, timestamp quality). Those merits are outweighed by structural mistakes, invalid quantities, a non-identifying pilot, and PM4Py code that does not implement the required models.

**Process comparison.** Twelve rows exist, but several cells are wrong or mis-filed. Site B is described as a single sequence. Its rules already allow `Identity Check` and `Originals Receipt` to start after `Register`. Legal review and role specialization are marked low transport relevance even though both are large effect modifiers present only at B. Queue discipline is “no explicit discipline” at Site A after the huddles. Case mix, tail, rework, and capacity put the before/after change in the “before” cell and then mark “after” as “Same.” The supplement loop is only a rework percentage, so the control-flow loop is thin.

**Transport graph.** The block is Graphviz, not an adjacency list. `identity_capacity`, `document_capacity`, `assessment_capacity`, `API_latency`, and `appeal` are absent. `S_digital_documents` points at `originals_required`, and complexity points at `rework`, so selection nodes do not select their own variables. The explanation is about 140 words, below the 180-word minimum, and treats the core idea as transportable once a legal gate is open.

**Evidence table.** p90, wait-before-assessment, appeals, and the co-interventions as their own rows are missing. Service-time drops of 0.4h and 0.7h are partly credited to parallelization; overlap changes waiting on the critical path, while those service changes track the API upgrade and extra document reviewers. The FTE row treats the capacity co-intervention as the transferable component. The nine-hour median drop is still carried forward as parallelism.

**Modifiers.** All ten topics appear, but originals are assigned to “high-value” cases; that 40% split is legal review, while originals are 62%. Several “beneficial signatures” are “no improvement” or failure modes. Specialization is treated as something that blocks overlap. Separate identity and document pools can run together; the lock and the originals prerequisite are what block simultaneous execution. “POWL lock constraints” are not a POWL construct.

**Scenarios.** Five rows are numeric, and none has a better median with a worse tail. Eligible share 38% is then applied as an overall move from 44h to 35h or 36h. Critical-path arithmetic does not support that: identity service is about 3.4h and document service about 8.1h, so full overlap saves roughly the shorter service plus queueing, while a median originals delay of 18h dominates the 62% stratum. The −9h / −20h figures reuse Site A’s confounded before/after gap. Bottleneck, lock, and legal rows invent +5h to +50h without a mapping from the DFG or pool sizes. The DFG counts are unused.

**Intervention.** Eligibility is the 38% without originals, which is the safe slice, and then the spec also starts document checking before originals “if applicable.” That ordering is unlawful for the 62% stratum. The useful legal concurrency—identity during the originals wait—is omitted, and the text later says physical cases cannot benefit. `Reject` is folded into the supplement restart. Rule 7 also repeats assessment after `Request Supplement`. The partial order is one chain plus contradictory `is_parallel` edges, so assessment is not a join of identity and document, and legal review is mandatory rather than optional.

**Pilot.** The unit is “case type,” and originals-versus-digital is described as randomizable. That requirement is statutory, so this compares different populations, not treatment with control. Shared reviewer pools are called an interference control while remaining a shared queue. The stop rule halts on a cycle-time gain above 10h. The write-up does not separate mechanism failure from implementation failure: there is no fidelity measure for simultaneous task creation, lock workarounds, or back-entered timestamps versus outcomes on high-fidelity cases.

**Code.** `parse_process_tree` is not used. Site A is built with a nonexistent `time_tree` API and `"+IdentityCheck"` labels, dropping the `X(Approve, Reject)` branch. `convert_to_petri_net(...).show()` does not match the `(net, im, fm)` return. POWL is built from `Place`, `PartialOrder`, and `Edge(..., is_parallel=True)`, which are not the `StrictPartialOrder` / `OperatorPOWL` / `Transition` constructors, so there is no strict partial order, no optional legal XOR, and no supplement loop. `play_out` is given a handmade trace instead of generating one. Case locks are omitted from the limitation comments. The snippet would not run and would not encode the candidate process if it did.

**Recommendation and assumptions.** `conditional go` matches the evidence, and the 250–400 word band is met. Claimed digital-claim gains of 8–10h median and 15–20h at p90, plus “no rework increase,” overstate Site A, where rework and appeals ticked up under several co-interventions. Expansion is tied to a 10h gain and then to high-complexity cases, which mixes originals, legal review, and difficulty. Assumption 1 says Site A’s gain was driven by digital cases equal to 38% of Site B; Site A was already about 90% digital, and that 38% is Site B’s non-originals share. Assumption 2 treats the originals delay as deterministic although the brief calls it highly variable.
