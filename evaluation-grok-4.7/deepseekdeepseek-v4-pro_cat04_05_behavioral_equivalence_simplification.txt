6.2

The final verdicts are right: A and B are not equivalent under P1 or P3, and they match only after the P2 projection. The four counterexamples are valid in both directions, loop scope and the one-check versus one-check-per-iteration distinction are handled correctly, and the P2 simplification really does preserve that dashboard projection and is smaller than both trees. Those are the core of the task.

The supporting material is not reliable enough for a high score.

The bounded search is not exhaustive. Once the Analyze count hits 3, expansion stops, so later Check, Approve, Rework, `tau`, and Close firings are never taken. Complete traces with exactly three analyses, which are inside the P3 bound, are dropped, yet the write-up still says the search finds every trace in the bound. `pm4py.fire_transition` and `pm4py.is_final_marking` are not justified APIs; marking update and final-marking equality are exactly what that section had to get right. The playout fragment treats each `play_out` result as one trace and then builds a stochastic language from a list of logs.

The complexity counts are wrong. Model A has 5 operators and 7 leaves, 12 nodes, the same node count as B, not 13 nodes and 6 operators. “A is larger” and “B is structurally simpler” both come from that miscount. The simplicity claim is not computed. `pm4py.vis_process_tree` does not yield the nodes the metric code walks.

Footprints are not the same directly-follows relation. Approve can be immediately followed by Check in B and cannot in A, because A finishes both Analyze and Check before the optional approval. The Earth Mover’s Distance paragraph also says a zero distance could coexist with different supports, then correctly contradicts that.

The P1 notion blurs “every trace obeys the rule” with equality of compliance-relevant languages, and the single-iteration caveat misses that approval/check order already separates the languages when there is no rework. Optional approval and hidden `tau` are noted, but not tied back to the verdict: the K-cardinality split survives even if approval is hidden, while visible approval placement is an independent P1 split.
