7.5

The forecast meets the required structure and stays balanced: it treats the change as conditional, separates East from North and South, and ties structure to handover, queue, rework, role, resilience, learning, and fairness measures. The handover arithmetic is sound, and the recommendation correctly stays at a monitored pilot.

The analysis sketch still has material errors. Rework after a handover is taken from the handover event itself, so it does not measure later rework. A flat 7-day SLA sits inside the observed medians (6.8–8.6 days) and would mark a large share of baseline cases as misses. Team and segment fairness gaps are computed after filtering to a single team or segment, which forces the gap toward zero. Directly-follows graphs are treated as graphs with edges, while these PM4Py calls return edge dictionaries. Inter-event time is labeled as handover wait.

The pilot is also inconsistent with the forecast. Phase 1 keeps North and South local, so it cannot observe the local-expertise loss the forecast treats as a main risk for those teams. Scenario effects stay directional and are not tied back to the baseline cycle-time, rework, and utilization figures. Rework is present in the mechanism map, but it is thinner than the other required mechanisms.
