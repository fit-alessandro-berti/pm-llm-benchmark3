8.2

The final document meets the required structure and treats the three candidates as genuinely different hypotheses. The evidence ledger covers every variant, D1–D7, and S1–S7, and the bidirectional DFG discussion is correct: the 17/11 `Credit Check`/`Engineering Review` counts are exactly the sequential observed mix, so they do not prove concurrency. Shared assumptions correctly separate completion order, true skips, and missing `Supplier Confirmation` events.

The languages are internally consistent and nested. The conservative XOR is exactly the seven observed traces. The balanced POWL admits those seven plus five controlled extensions (expedite without supplier confirmation, and extra expedite/supplier interleavings) while keeping `Credit Check → Supplier Confirmation`. The permissive enumeration adds only the two engineering-triggered supplier traces. Observed traces V1–V7 are admitted by all three intended languages, and the excluded-trace audit matches those constraints. The recommendation, uncertainty estimates, and data-collection plan are specific enough to distinguish sequence, partial order, concurrency, and optionality.

Deductions are for implementation and validation gaps, not for the modeling argument. The POWL builder calls `order.add_node` and never supplies nodes to `StrictPartialOrder`; depending on the PM4Py version, edges must be added only after nodes exist, so `model_balanced` may not construct. The process-tree strings use `sequence(...)` / `xor(...)`, which is not the usual PM4Py `->(...)` / `X(...)` grammar, so the conservative and permissive trees may fail to parse. The validation suite also never checks the traces that actually separate the models: `Engineering Review, Supplier Confirmation, Credit Check, ...` is not a balanced negative, and expedite-without-supplier is not a conservative negative. Alignment fitness is only printed, with no expected accept/reject outcome. The precision gap is also overstated: balanced already adds five unobserved traces and permissive adds only two more.
