The role analysis in sections 1–4 is substantively strong: it separates routine specialization from complexity, authority, automation, contingent work, and bridging, and it treats shadow roles as useful but risky rather than improper. Most activity shares, complexity medians, and substitution facts check out.

It is not a complete answer to the required format. Section 5 stops after `format_dataframe` and never runs organizational-role discovery, activity-based similarity, handover or working-together networks, or a join to case complexity and temporary substitution. Section 6 is absent, so staffing, training, risk, resilience, and the required cautions are missing.

The finished sections also contain concrete errors: Amira’s technical/commercial tail is about 1.5× Ben’s, not 4–5×; human A2 volume is 1,641, not 881; temps account for about 31% of A4, not 27%; Giorgio is the 8th-largest event producer, not the 6th; his complexity mix is not the flattest (Mateo’s is flatter); 21 of Mateo’s approvals are about 10% of all A9, not “15% of a quarter’s”; 38/74 minutes is about 51% throughput, not 49%.
