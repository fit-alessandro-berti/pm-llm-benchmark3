The regime structure is sound, but the quantitative sections are not reliable enough for a higher score. Outage, recovery, mix shift, and the later staffing change are separated correctly; weekend composition, carry-over, and median service versus flow time are handled in the right way; and the pooled “cut FTE because average productivity looks normal” policy is a real segmented risk. Those parts meet the task.

The reported figures do not. There are 8 weekend days, not 10 of 28. Pooled arrival intensity is about 7.50 per open hour, not 7.66; mean completions are about 86 per day, not 88.4. The cases-per-FTE figures (14.7 pooled; 14.0 / 13.6 / 13.0 / 14.6 by regime) do not match completions divided by recorded FTE, except the outage day. In the mix split, low-mix completions are below arrivals because the outage sits in that window; the table shows the opposite, and both intensities are off. R3 weekday throughput is about 8.38 per hour with WIP near 44.3, not 8.18 and 44.5, so the 326-minute flow time uses an inconsistent window. R0 and R4 weekday arrival rates (8.49 and 9.45) are also wrong. Complex-case lag-1 autocorrelation is near 0.9 for this step change, not 0.62. The robust median arrival rate is a weekday value near 8.6 per hour, not 7.96, and median WIP is 23, not 24.5.

The same evidence is then misclassified. R3 has arrivals above completions and weekday WIP rising from 37 to 50, but the Little’s Law row calls the window stationary and applicable. That contradicts the regime write-up and the requirement not to force a queueing-law claim when the boundary is moving. Utilization is also a bit low relative to the stated median-service formula.

The code would recompute several of these quantities correctly, and it does include dispersion, a declared non-definitive split scan, chosen-window flow times, and an explicit note that these aggregates are not a PM4Py log. It does not correct the narrative tables, and the index-of-dispersion proxy is just the Fano factor again. Under a strict standard, conceptual structure cannot offset that many incorrect final figures.
