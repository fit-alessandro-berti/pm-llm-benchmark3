The six-section structure is complete, and the main forecast is a serious calendar-aware mixture: parallel join as a max, Friday chemistry pause to Monday 08:00, microbiology ready Sunday but unreadable without staff, QA closed Sunday, release able to finish after QA acceptance, excursion treated as a common cause, and a Monday-evening versus Tuesday-morning cliff. Headline bands (earliest Monday morning, P50 Monday late day, P80 Tuesday, P90 Wednesday, multi-day lab tail, release far more likely than rejection) are internally usable.

They are not tight enough for a high score. The FIFO wait is written as backlog divided by net drain after future arrivals, but those arrivals queue behind B-417, so they do not add to its wait. That error produces the overstated claim that strict FIFO moves the whole central band to Tuesday 09:00–14:00; morning drain plus a 5–7 h wait still leaves much of the mass on Monday. S3b’s earliest auth-case terminal (Tuesday 11:00) disagrees with the stated minima: Sunday readout plus 20 h confirmation can be usable Monday morning. S4 and S5 earliest times are anchored on a Monday-afternoon join, so a short query or 8 h documentation investigation after a priority-chemistry morning join can still finish Monday. In §6, 78–90 h is labeled Tuesday morning–afternoon; from Friday 15:30 that interval is Monday evening through Tuesday morning. Smaller slips: the priority-chemistry finish cap of 10:40 omits the 0.9 h Friday-credit case, and S2’s table earliest (Monday 12:30) conflicts with the prose release window (Monday 14:00 onward).
