7.5

The forecast is a strong, usable remaining-time analysis: weekend and shift calendars are applied correctly, the pre-join time is a maximum of the chemistry and microbiology clocks, rework is branched rather than averaged in, and the temperature-excursion flag is treated as a shared cause. The six required sections are present, shortcuts are stated, and the summary is given as ranges with a clear most-likely window of late Monday 21 September or early Tuesday 22 September.

The score stays well below a near-flawless mark because the probability accounting contradicts itself. Scenario A is described as overlapping the weekend-deferred readout entirely, yet that smooth path still includes the 0.25–0.45 on-call cases whose readout is Sunday. The “mutually exclusive core” puts rerun-only near 12%, while the stated marginal rerun rate of about 0.21 and the conditional investigation rate of 0.30–0.55 imply closer to 8% once contamination and queries are removed. Rejection at 3–7% does not follow from “25–40% of laboratory investigations” when those investigations are only 3–9% of paths. The P50-like band is 75–95 hours, but the stated median of about 96 hours is outside that band, and 96 hours after Friday 15:30 is Tuesday 15:30, not midday. The claim that a confirmed temperature excursion moves the central outcome to Wednesday–Friday overstates a probability shift that still leaves a substantial Monday–Tuesday mass. Monday analyst count and later chemistry arrivals are also left out of the assumption ledger, and the rerun triangular mean is given as 1.87 hours rather than 1.93.
