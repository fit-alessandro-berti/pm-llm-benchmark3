7.1

The answer meets the required structure and the core quality bar: estimands are defined before estimators, open cases are retained, withdrawal and transfer are not casually treated as ordinary censoring, and escalation/rework/full prefixes are correctly barred as baseline covariates. Case coding, the 5-vs-3 P1 split, and the Aalen–Johansen target are largely right. It is not nearly flawless.

The transfer-as-censoring sensitivity is misidentified. Censoring at transfer estimates a latent internal time-to-resolve under an independence assumption. It does not mean that external resolution “counts as success.” That is a different, unidentified estimand, and the write-up conflates the two.

The proposed bounds are logically invalid for this table. S32 is transferred at 96 hours, so at 72 hours it is a known non-resolution under both an internal-resolve definition and an external-success-by-72 definition. Assigning “every transfer” to success or failure by 72, including S32, does not bracket the 72-hour endpoint. Only S08 (68h) and S21 (26h) have unknown external status relative to hour 72, and even those are not known external successes.

The informative-censoring discussion uses the wrong cases and, in §6, the wrong direction. S09, S11, S25, and S26 are already older than 72 hours, so their censoring cannot change \(F_R(72)\) or \(S_A(72)\). Only opens younger than 72 hours (S10, S15, S24, S29, S31) leave the 72-hour status unidentified. Saying that future withdrawal of long-gap opens “understates the probability of no terminal disposition” reverses the relevant bias and contradicts the answer’s own correct below-72 vs above-72 distinction.

The state diagram is not a faithful reading of the prefixes. Observed edges are missing, including Screen→Reject (S05, S23), Screen→Withdraw (S12), Screen→Escalate (S04, S11, S21, S25, S32), Screen→Resolve (S16), AutoCheck→Resolve (S01), AutoCheck→Review (S15, S17), and Rework→Resolve (S27). The text admits incompleteness but only patches AutoCheck→Escalate, and the S18 citation does not identify an additional missing edge. The arrow-less list is also not an executable diagram.

Smaller defects: S10 is omitted from the first open-case list; “open patients” is the wrong unit; in-sample stratified Aalen–Johansen is treated as something that still needs predicted-versus-observed calibration; unconditional \(S_A(72)\) is loosely equated with current backlog; and `len` on `pm4py.get_variants` values will break if the installed version returns counts, with that failure mislabeled as PM4Py being unavailable.
