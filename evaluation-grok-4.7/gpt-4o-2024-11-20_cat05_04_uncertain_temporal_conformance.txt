The write-up names the right topics and gets the offset identity and a few nominal zone conversions right, then stops. Most required results are missing, several numeric intervals are wrong, and at least two of those errors reverse conformance.

Section 2 does not give explicit feasible UTC intervals. `±` notation leaves the bounds unresolved, HUB omits the stated ±0.5 s measurement uncertainty, and OPS-BER is treated as ±90 s even though the given offset is [−90 s, +120 s]. SENSOR `08:51:10` rounded to the nearest 10 s is rewritten as `08:51:05 ± 5 s`, which keeps only part of the pre-offset bin and then adds only the clock bound. VENDOR-NY `04:00` is `08:00Z ± 180 s`, so the minute floor never enters the interval. T-402 containment is HUB `10:02:00` and is reported as `08:02:00Z`. T-405 `02:50` Europe/Berlin on 2026-10-26 is the fold `00:50Z` (CEST) or `01:50Z` (CET), not a span from `01:50Z` to `02:50Z`; Investigation_Start and Containment_Complete are absent, so the ingestion constraint that leaves only one fold combination is never applied. T-404 never shows an inferred start. T-406, T-407, T-408, and T-409 are incomplete or missing.

The assumption that investigation start equals containment minus 18–25 minutes is the wrong pair; that duration is tied to Investigation_Complete. The mobile-delay note both adds five minutes to the occurrence window and says the delay does not affect occurrence time. T-407 turns an unlogged causal story into a fact while still not producing an Alert_Received interval. The claim that ambiguity yields POSSIBLE outcomes erases one-sided intervals: under the stated bounds, T-402 S1 and T-408 S2 and S3 are certain violations at zero tolerance, and T-408 remains a certain SLA violation at +5 minutes.

Section 3 is one unresolved T-401 S1 row plus a promise that the other rows exist. The acceptance region is a point rather than a duration predicate. Section 4’s “no certain violations” claim is false, and there is no zeta 1/2/3 interval matrix. Section 5 is a non-runnable stub: it does not normalize intervals, represent the DST union, classify durations, build a labeled midpoint log, or call `pm4py.format_dataframe` and `pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile`. Sections 6 and 7 are slogans and do not supply, per weak result, the minimum synchronizing, activity, duration, or semantic evidence, nor an enforcement policy for interval overlap.
