The final model pair is coherent and largely faithful. The POWL is an exact control-flow encoding of the stated dependency DAG: registration opens both streams, containment gates planning, root-cause analysis gates design, the approval block, and optional consultation, both work products gate the trial loop, and release is the three-way join. Loop, XOR, and silent-skip semantics match the intended repeat-until, risk split, and at-most-once supplier behavior. The process tree is a real conservative under-approximation, not an accidental serialization: approval and consultation still overlap the trial, while the phase-1 join is explicitly charged with excluding only early planning, design, approval, and consultation relative to the other initial stream. The N-pattern argument for why no exact tree exists is sound, the eight witnesses have the right intended and model verdicts, and W4 actually exposes that loss.

The witness analysis is not exact. In F3, `Adjust Parameters` is still a legal prefix of `(Execute Trial, Inspect Trial)(Adjust Parameters, Execute Trial, Inspect Trial)*`; the first event outside the language is `Release Material`, not Adjust. The stated indices are also wrong (Adjust is event 9, not 8; in F1, Execute is event 5, not 6). That misses the required “earliest violated condition” on a trace whose verdict is otherwise correct. The playout invariant is weaker than the claim: it does not enforce Execute/Inspect pairing across cycles, and a trailing Adjust raises `IndexError` on `trio[k+1]` instead of failing the check. Those are localized defects in an otherwise strong answer, not a broken model.
