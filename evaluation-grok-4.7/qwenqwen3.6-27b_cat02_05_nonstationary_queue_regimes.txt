5.5

The regime story is directionally sound, but the quantitative core is not reliable enough for a higher score.

The required eight-part structure is present, outage/recovery is correctly kept out of steady state, and the Little’s Law verdicts are appropriately cautious. Baseline, mix-shift, intervention, and weekend differences are real features of the table. The risky pooled staffing policy is also the right kind of conclusion.

Those merits do not offset the calculation and consistency failures. Pooled means are wrong: arrivals are about 90.0 per day, not 92.4; completions about 86.0, not 94.6; mean WIP about 29.9, not 28.1; mean FTE about 5.14, not 5.32. The reported “median service” of 31 matches the mean of daily medians; the median is 32.5. Outage net backlog is about +47 cases, not +14. Intervention throughput is 121 completions per day, not 119.

Dispersion results are likewise off. April 7’s hourly mean is 8.42 with a Fano factor near 3, not 8.25 and 4.2; April 21’s mean is 9.58 with a Fano factor near 9–10, not 8.5 and 13.9. The arrival peak is mis-binned by about two hours on both representative weekdays. Full-series arrival CV is roughly 0.3 because of weekends, so the claim of a 0.09 CV and “stable daily volume” is false. The complex-share rolling mean crosses 35 on April 21, not April 19. Several regime ranges borrow neighboring days, and cells such as “58129112” and “475235” are not readable estimates. The high-complexity window is also mislabeled “(Mon–Tue).”

Little’s Law multiplies calendar-day sojourn by 24 despite a 12-hour opening window, without resolving whether WIP snapshots include closure. Implied wait is then compared with median service, which the answer itself says is not mean service. Nominal utilization is never bounded, so the claim that a multi-hour wait is “moderate queueing” is unsupported. The alternative segmentation says it cuts on mix and FTE, then still pools high-mix weekends into one low-volume class. The prose figures would not match the supplied code, which also omits a pooled summary and two of the four hourly vectors.
