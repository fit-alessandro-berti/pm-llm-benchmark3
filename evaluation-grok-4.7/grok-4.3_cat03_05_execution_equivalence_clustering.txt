The write-up has the required skeleton and a few defensible choices (X1 with X2; activity bags for X3, X4, and X8; external sharing pulled out of the baseline). It is not a reliable analysis. Definitions, the signature, the table, the borderline section, and the perturbation claims contradict one another, and several of those claims are false on the given logs.

The business-semantic “equivalence” is not an equivalence: equality of the stated multisets would be transitive, yet the text says transitivity holds only inside a motif family. The fourth relation’s “not transitive with the other three” is a category error. Typed structure is muddled (“one canonical name per type” versus isomorphism that keeps multiplicities). The signature then fails the definition it claims to implement: `tuple(sorted(activities))` plus global type counts discards timestamp order and per-event incidence, while the prose says events stay in timestamp order. That sketch is not reproducible.

X7 is mishandled throughout. Its `Dispatch` event carries `Item:I72`; X1’s does not. Under the stated E2O-preserving typed relation, X1 and X7 are not equivalent. Section 3 puts X7 in `TS6`, section 5 says typed structure equates them, and section 7 claims that dropping qualifiers merges X7 into X1. Those cannot all be true. The `replaces` link is also structure, not only the `removed_before_dispatch` label.

Section 5 misses the assigned job for X3–X4 and X4–X8. Every relation the author actually defined separates both pairs, so no disagreement is shown. X1–X6 is worded as if typed structure and semantic equivalence conflict; under the author’s own rules both separate them. Flattening claims are wrong: an order-level trace does not merge the two packs, does not collapse two payments into one event, and does not truncate X8 after the first dispatch.

Robustness is mostly incorrect. Collapsing repeated labels merges X3 and X4 into the baseline as well, not into a distinct “multi-pack” cluster. A partial order on pack and dispatch does not erase X4’s second delivery or second payment. Treating payment count as an amount does not turn SEM-C into SEM-A, because X4 still has two packages and two deliveries. Only the external-object collapse of X5 and X6 is sound.

The PM4Py outline would not run as written (`lead_object_type`, and values treated as sub-logs). It asserts raw-identifier isomorphism and singleton clusters as fact, which overclaims the key and misses that `max_objs` truncates how many lead objects are keyed while a large connected component blows up each key. Section 8 then recommends, for descriptive reporting, a payment relation the author already said is only a financial collapse and not transitive.

Clusters of the eight logs are therefore not stable under the author’s own criteria. The form is complete; the equivalences are not.
