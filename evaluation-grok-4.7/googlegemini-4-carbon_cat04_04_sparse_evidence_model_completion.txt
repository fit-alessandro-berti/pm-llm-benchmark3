6.6

The models in §§1–7 are unusually careful and mostly correct, but the required document is incomplete and the validation code is not runnable. That is enough to keep this well below a near-flawless score.

What holds up: the 30-case counts, DFG frequencies, and the 23/23 “CC before SC” regularity are right. The language claims are right: 7, then 12, then 18, with \(\mathcal{L}(M_{cons})\subsetneq\mathcal{L}(M_{bal})\subsetneq\mathcal{L}(M_{perm})\). The balanced Hasse diagram really is an N-poset (ER ≺ XR, CC ≺ XR, CC ≺ SC, ER incomparable with SC), so the POWL choice is justified rather than cosmetic. Missing SC is treated as a control-flow skip that mixes S4 optionality with S5 loss, and the DFG bidirectionality discussion correctly refuses to infer concurrency. The audit traces and earliest-violation arguments match those languages.

What fails:
- §§9–10 are absent. The ledger even points to a §10 compliance alternative that is never written, so there is no recommendation, no data-collection plan, and no calibrated confidence statement.
- §8 dies mid-literal at `"Unobserved D5 symmetric:`. The suite is unfinished, `main` is never closed or called, and the script is a syntax error.
- `bounded_language_playout` puts PM4Py `Marking` objects (a `Counter`) in a set. That raises `TypeError` before any bounded language is explored. `ClassicSemantics.weak_execute` is also an unverified internal API.
- “Overfitting/precision tradeoff” for the permissive model confuses overfitting with over-generalization. Allowing SC to complete before both checks is also wider than D5; it is disclosed as risky, but the boundary is looser than the rule cited to justify it.
- The precision bands (~0.95–1.00, ~0.80–0.92, ~0.70–0.85) are not derived. Alignment precision is not \(|L_{obs}|/|L|\), so those intervals are decorative.

Fitness “1.00 by construction” is structurally plausible, and the three candidates are real competing hypotheses. The missing recommendation, uncertainty analysis, and broken validation still leave two required sections undone and the only empirical check non-executable.
