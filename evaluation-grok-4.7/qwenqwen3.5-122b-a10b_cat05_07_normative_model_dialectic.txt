4.5

The write-up has the required shape and gets the clearest holdings right, but it is not a strong conformance opinion. Several conclusions rest on misread facts, a wrong rule, or a model that cannot express the judgment it just reached.

The clean calls are SUP-701 (compliant), SUP-703 (ineligible auto-approval), SUP-704 (inside FD-7), SUP-705 (after FD-7 expired), SUP-708 (activation before sanctions), and SUP-714 (after PD-3 expired). SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-711, and SUP-712 are directionally sound. That is not enough to carry the review.

Section 1 treats PD-3 as an established valid derogation. The record is the opposite: it was never incorporated into CS-9, and the compliance observer later denied approving any weakening of CS-9. That authority question is the hinge of SUP-702 and of the 289 apparently eligible auto-approvals, and it is left unexamined. The burden of proof never separates control-flow fitness, CS-9 breach, exception authority, and logging failure.

The briefs are short and often identical. The prosecution does not really acknowledge the model and data defects it was required to concede, misstates SUP-706 as having no CRM evidence, and never uses the aggregate counts (312 auto-approvals, 14 ineligible, 9 unknown, 47 information requests, notification missingness from 2% to 29%, six post-pilot cases). The defense minimizes SUP-708 as a logging artifact even though the order is explicit, and it does not develop the strongest data point it has: SUP-702’s missing log notification is backed by a stable `notification_id`. It also calls SUP-702’s eligibility fields missing when the case says they are complete, and treats SUP-711’s source transaction ID as missing when it is given.

SUP-715 is misclassified in substance. Spouses are different humans, so the CS-9 requester/approver rule is not what failed. The conflict is between quality reviewer and approver, and the proved breach is missing disclosure. SUP-709 is also under-analyzed: in M0 the checks partial order must finish before the XOR, so quality is required even on the reject branch, while CS-9 only requires both checks before activation. Scope is unproved; calling it a determined model defect settles an open question.

The system judgment then says 9/15 cases are compliant or justified. The classifications themselves are 1 compliant and 2 justified exceptions. “M0 is invalid” is too broad: M0 still correctly describes the ordinary CS-9 path. Rejecting the prosecution because M0 does not encode the resource rule confuses language fitness with the underlying duty.

The revised model does not implement the decision. Quality and sanctions are forced before every route, so the model still rejects the pilot and emergency traces just classified as justified. There is no emergency/provisional branch. `Request_More_Info` is an XOR alternative that never reaches activation, although `Operator.LOOP` is the POWL construct for a repeatable redo. `manual_approval` and `activate` are reused across branches, and the later node swap leaves the old edges in place. Withdrawals are absent from the scope rules. Delegation evidence and immutable effective versus record timestamps are absent from the collection proposal.
