8.4

The final answer meets every required section, stays inside the stated policies, and does not treat air or dedicated vans as uniformly unjustified. Boundaries, indicators, all twelve options, the effect table, rebound coverage, the phased package, and conformance governance are path-specific and usable. True-downtime vans are retained, reinforced packaging stays risk-based, and rework, returns, and obsolescence are carried through the recommendation.

The deductions are for quantitative looseness that a strict reading should not ignore. The 5% stock-to-air path is treated as about 9% of transport CO2e and as Standard-stock service-contract “abuse,” although the 22% SP1 violations are only about 3.5% of orders and are not shown to be that entire path. “5,100 ground confirms” is the standard-pack-to-ground arc, not the 6,550 ground confirmations, and it is not only Regular stock. The partial-shipment path is called roughly double the clean ground path on both 7.2 kgCO2e and 1.4 kWh; only transport is double (3.6 kgCO2e), while energy is 1.4 versus 0.9. Halving the CNC_B rework rate (18% to 9%) is then treated as halving rework scrap and rework energy, which the path averages do not establish. S9’s mechanism allows expedited air, but the effect claims use the stock/ground exception (about 23h and 9% late). The claim that one air recovery “erases” batch energy savings compares +15–20 kgCO2e with kWh savings without a grid factor. The “late rates within 1–2 points” expectation is a target, not a result supported by the 1.7× heat-treat late multiplier or consolidation waits. Batch “≥12” is also flattened to “batch 12” in places.

Those are local errors. They do not break the segment logic or the recommendation’s guardrails.
