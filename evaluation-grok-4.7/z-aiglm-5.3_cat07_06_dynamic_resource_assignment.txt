7.3

The policy design is complete, constraint-aware, and explicit about trade-offs, but it is not nearly flawless. A wrong baseline trace and a few internal contradictions keep it well below a top score.

The seven required sections are all present and usable. Hard rules are separated from preferences. The algorithm covers eligibility, SLA urgency, skill match, handover, workload, overtime, training, resilience, tie-breaks, and exceptions. All 16 cases are assigned with a reason, a trade-off, a training flag, and a risk. Urgent and high-priority work stays at skill 2 or above. Security reviews go to skill 3. Approvals are finalized at skill 3. C007 stays with Celine and C016 stays with Eva, which matches the continuity rule. The Eva/Jonas security-0 conflict on C015 is handled correctly, and the 2/9 training share is arithmetically right. The worker note is clear and non-punitive. The audit list covers every requested indicator and ties it to PM4Py.

The comparison is not reliable. Under the stated first-available rule (static lowest open cases, skill above 0, cap of 4, case order), C009 goes to Farid, C010 to Farid, and C011 to Celine. The published trace sends C009 to Celine and C010–C013 to Eva, then claims 8 of 16 cases land on Jonas and Eva. That count is false. Several qualitative breach examples still happen to be right, but the worked allocation is not. Specialist-only is simulated only after imposing the cap of 4, which the baseline definition does not include. The headline result that C016 falls to David is an artifact of that cap; without it, Celine remains the diagnosis tie-break. That weakens the SLA critique of specialist-only.

Other problems are smaller but real. Section 1 calls the objectives lexicographic, while Section 2 optimizes a weighted sum of all soft terms. Constraint 4 allows high-overtime work only when the SLA is urgent and no safer alternative exists. C012 gives Bruno, a high-overtime resource, a low-priority 48-hour case even though Jonas is safer; the “time-shifted exception” is declared rather than authorized. David, the most overloaded high-overtime resource, is also used as C009’s reviewer. The SLA audit treats cycle time above 24 hours as a breach, which misses short-SLA misses and flags long-SLA cases that are still on time. The PM4Py sketch is broad, but `filter_event_attribute_values` never appears in the code, the skill-mismatch check ignores the security and approval floors, and the `@@duration` fallback is not created.
