7.5

The role model is data-faithful and meets the required structure: specialization, complexity, substitutions, shadow roles, ambiguity, and staffing cautions are all covered, and the cited counts, medians, and similarities match the evidence. It is not nearly flawless.

The core/peripheral distinction contradicts itself. Hana’s A13 is both a core reopen decision and a peripheral activity. Giorgio’s A7/A8 are placed in the core column of the role table and then called peripheral in the next section. Mateo’s A9 is defined as preliminary approval, although only 21 events in one week are documented that way and 13 of those were rechecked, while his total is 44; that later ambiguity does not undo the earlier assertion. Fatima’s 16 real A9 events are softened into “approval-adjacent,” and Erik’s A3 count (16) is omitted while smaller peripheral activities are listed. Giorgio’s standing commercial volume (72 A7, 88 A8) is also not reconciled with the 31 and 18 substitution events.

The PM4Py section names the right functions and builds useful complexity and substitution features, but the join would not work. PM4Py roles expose a set of `originators`, not a singular `originator` and `total_events`, so resources never attach to the mined roles. Handover and working-together results are SNA objects with connection maps; the DataFrame conversion does not inspect them. The median aggregation can also fail when `case_complexity` is categorical, despite a numeric conversion being computed and then unused.
