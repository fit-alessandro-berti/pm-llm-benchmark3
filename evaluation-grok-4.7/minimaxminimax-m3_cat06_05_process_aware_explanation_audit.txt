7.5

The audit is strong on the central distinctions: full-trace and post-decision fields are rejected, queue length and prefix age are kept, region and calendar week are retained as proxy and drift signals rather than causes, and `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is treated as a future but process-legal pair while `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as order-ambiguous and normatively implausible. Coverage of the top-12 features, sequence patterns, and local-only fields is complete, the conformance suite matches the requested checks, and the three claim standards (predictive, causal, actionable) are separated.

It is not nearly flawless. Case Y invents `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` as a credible driver, with direction `neutral/low` and a claim about a raw value the case does not provide. `initial_reserve` is only conditionally legitimate in the lineage table, then unconditional in the JSON. The appeal discussion overstates causality by saying the team reverses a known true direction and that appealed cases have already used up the slack; an appeal can still extend time to final closure, and SHAP does not identify that direction. The broker explanation adds an unsupported mechanism (documentation style and rework). Week 52 is asserted to be the holiday week. Employee-experience-as-of-extraction, a stated lineage defect, never appears. Severity labels also slip outside the required scale (`Medium-High`, `Low–Medium`), and document-count leakage is Critical while the matching document-cycle pattern is only High.
