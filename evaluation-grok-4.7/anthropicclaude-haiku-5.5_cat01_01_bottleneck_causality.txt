6.8

The memo is structurally complete and causally cautious in the right places: it flags the crude-versus-stratified reversal, refuses to treat that reversal as identification, separates queue time from review service, and replaces the 50% reassignment with a capacity pilot plus a narrow randomized routing pilot. Those strengths do not offset several concrete errors.

The complexity-standardized arithmetic is not auditable as written. With the printed weights, \(0.40\times7.50+0.33\times17.17+0.27\times31.57=17.19\), not 17.14, and the Nova line is 19.70, not 19.65. The stated 2.51 h gap matches exact shares \(12/30\), \(10/30\), \(8/30\), so the result is right and the displayed equation is wrong. The Nova medium day contrast is simply wrong: C26, C27, and C28 average 19.77 h, not 19.43 h, and the night-day gap is about 1.57 h, not 1.23 h. The compliance residual claim (“about 2.2 h versus about 0.8 h”) is the no-rework mode, not the compliance average; compliance-plus-rework residuals sit near 3.6–3.7 h.

Causal roles conflict. The graph sends `resource_group` to `unobserved_reviewer_skill`, which makes skill a mediator or part of the treatment bundle, while the role table calls skill a confounder. `arrival_load` is labeled a mediator of the group total effect even though the graph has no `resource_group -> arrival_load` path; at case level it is a pre-assignment confounder or modifier. `rework -> queue_wait` contradicts the definition of `wait_before_review_h`, which ends before review. Calling `queue_wait` a mediator of the direct effect inverts the estimand. Total, direct, and mediated effects are never cleanly separated.

“The reversal is not explained by complexity alone” misstates Simpson’s pattern: complexity mix is exactly what reverses the crude gap. What complexity does not explain is the remaining within-stratum difference. Hypothesis 5’s prediction is also illogical: a large indirect effect would decompose the within-stratum contrast, not show that the contrast is “wrong.”

The script does not run. In the rework/appeal `print`, `.round(3)` is attached to the tuple `("case_id", "count")`, and the `print(` is never closed, so execution dies before the variant and DFG sections. The DFG path also fabricates timestamps and uses `format_dataframe` rather than `pm4py.parse_event_log_string`, against the stated process-mining instruction, even though those stamps are not used for durations.
