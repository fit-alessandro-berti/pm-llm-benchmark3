7.5

The pipeline is semantically coherent and largely complete: all 17 events and 12 objects are present, E2O rows copy activity and timestamp from the event table, O2O covers the required relationships, validation is real, and the flattened-trace and failure-mode discussion matches the chosen relations. The modeling policy is explicit and mostly consistent.

It is not nearly flawless. `pm4py.ocel_object_type_activities` returns a dict of activities for every object type and takes an optional parameters dict, not an object-type string. The loop `pm4py.ocel_object_type_activities(ocel, object_type)` would raise before flattening or object-centric discovery, so the required analysis pipeline does not actually run. Version sensitivity is discussed only for the `OCEL` constructor.

Section 5 also conflates levels. An OC-DFG and an OTG are type-level; they do not themselves show `P1`, `O100`, or `O200` as hubs. An object-centric Petri net has one `Record Payment` transition that can fire twice, not separate transitions for `M1` and `M2`. Calling `M2` `completes_settlement` treats an unknown invoice total as a fact, despite both payments being described as partial. Linking `E08` to `O100` is also a quiet propagation from `O100 contains I2`, which sits awkwardly against the claim that O2O traversal is not stored as observation.
