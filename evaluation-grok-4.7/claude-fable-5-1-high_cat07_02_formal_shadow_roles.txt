7.5

The role model is substantively strong: all 14 resources are placed, the required columns and six headings are present, and the main arithmetic (activity totals, organizational shares, complexity mixes, 72−31=41, 13/21, Ivan+Julia’s 608/684 and 73 A10 events) checks out. Complexity, certification, and substitution are used as separate evidence, and the shadow-role discussion covers benefits and risks for Giorgio, Mateo, and Erik.

The write-up still asserts more than the log shows, and it contradicts itself. Chen’s 24 A6 events are treated as late-shift, uncertified coverage, and in §3.7 as overnight coverage, while §4 correctly says time of day and later review by Erik are what would decide that. Chen’s 24 and Mateo’s 16 A6 events are 10-week stocks, but they are written as if they were the weeks 7–8 filter. Nora’s 760−610 gap is called registrations she cannot validate and “the hardest” entitlement cases, which fights fragment 2 (Nora registers, a human validates) and the alternative in §4. Amira and the temps are called the same or near-identical activity vector; the given similarities never include that pair, and Amira’s diagnose, negotiation, configuration, and pricing counts are not in the temp profile. §2 calls Mateo’s other ~23 A9 events routine pre-screening; §4 still treats that as undecided. Commercial load is “understated by about a third” in §3 and “Fatima’s 291 events plus Giorgio’s ~160, about 50% larger” in §6. Those are different bases: 291 counts all of Fatima’s events, while 160 counts only Giorgio’s A7+A8.

The code is a real attempt at the requested workflow, and several API details are right (`timest_format`, role-object vs pair handling, `beta`, baseline vs substitution views, the certification overlay, the guard-rails in the last block). It is not coherent as runnable analysis. `get_variants(..., **KEYS)` and `get_service_time(...)` pass `resource_key`, which those calls do not take. Similarity pairs are subtracted without a canonical `(r1, r2)` order. The recheck `groupby.apply` does not reliably reshape to the three columns it then renames. High/critical is cut at both 0.6 and 0.75, and critical waiting time assumes `escalation_level` ∈ {3, 4}; none of those encodings are in the evidence. Summing `case_complexity` across events re-counts long cases, and the resource figure later used is a median of per-activity medians. Unknown activity labels fail open to “certified,” so a name mismatch would hide the shadow work the script is meant to surface. Working-together and subcontracting networks are built and then never inspected.

Staffing and control advice is usable, and the five cautions match the constraints. The commercial-capacity number and the late-shift security backup are still stated as planning facts after the text itself marks them as open.
