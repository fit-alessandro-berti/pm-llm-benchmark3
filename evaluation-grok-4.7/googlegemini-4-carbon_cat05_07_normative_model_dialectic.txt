The memo is a serious conformance opinion: all seven required headings are present, all fifteen cases are classified with a dominant reason, both briefs concede the points they must concede, and the POWL sketch is largely valid PM4Py. It is not nearly flawless. Several authority, scope, and model-semantics calls are tighter than the record, or looser than the author’s own burden of proof.

The burden section is the right frame. Syntactic divergence from M0 is not itself a breach, and an exception needs prior authority, eligibility, compensating controls, and non-repudiable evidence. The case holdings do not always apply that frame.

SUP-710 is the clearest misweighting. CS-9 requires a quality review before every activation and contains no reactivation carve-out. The trace omits Quality_Review. A still-valid prior certificate and a six-month dormancy are mitigation and a policy gap. They do not discharge the burden the memo itself states. MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT with severity “Low” treats an uncodified shortcut as if the missing rule were an authorization. The dominant holding should have been unauthorized activation, with model incompleteness as a second finding.

SUP-709 overreads the same standard. CS-9 says rejected leads may use an abbreviated pre-screen and expressly leaves open whether those leads are M0 cases. “Expressly permits,” “aligns with CS-9,” risk “None,” and confidence “High” convert a stipulated ambiguity into a permission to skip Quality_Review. The right status is unproved scope, not a settled model false positive.

SUP-702 is a reasoned reliance holding, but it sits above the memo’s own “empowered sign-off” test. PD-3 was never taken into CS-9. The observer’s sentence is a monitored-exception remark, later paired with a denial that CS-9 was weakened. Medium-High confidence, and the system-level claim that the pilot was a valid overlay, are more charitable than that record. The pre-1 July CRM `notification_id` is correctly flagged and then still used as if it cured notification.

SUP-715 is a real disclosure breach. “Collusive control-gate risk” and severity High state a collusion finding the file does not contain. The proved fact is an undeclared spousal relationship under a disclosure rule, with no rule barring the pairing itself.

Smaller factual stretches: SUP-705 is dated 2026-07-13, not shown to be “hours after midnight”; “immediate” EUR 180,000 spend is stronger than “first purchase”; matrix severity for SUP-706 is “Very Low” and the judgment says “Low.”

The revised POWL is the right shape: partial-order concurrency on the standard route, XOR of standard, auto, emergency, rejection, and withdrawal, LOOP(τ, Request_More_Info) as a Kleene star, distinct transition objects per branch, and no external I/O. The limitations that are disclosed (label reuse, reactivation not coupled to the entry XOR, data rules outside POWL) are real and correctly left outside the net.

The emergency route is not the most defensible approximation, and the miss is undisclosed. FD-7 permits quality review and manual approval to follow provisional activation. It does not require that order. The edges from Provisional_Activate_Supplier to Quality_Review and Request_More_Info reject a legal emergency trace in which sanctions and quality finish first, activation is provisional, and manual approval is retrospective. That trace fits neither the emergency route nor the standard route, because the standard route still requires manual approval before activation. “May” has been encoded as “must.”

The overlay table invents history. PD-3, as given, has no EUR 50,000 cap, yet `PD3-v1.0` records one beside a historical authority id. Naming the compliance observer as a dual signatory also overstates a contested minute. Those rows are written as the registry of what was authorized. Proposed controls and transcribed instruments are different things. The 12-month reactivation window and the 2026-08-15 effective date are design choices presented with the same tone as source rules.

Concurrency is only implicit. Reordering quality around activation is discussed; M0’s unordered sanctions/quality block, and what that partial order does and does not forbid, never gets its own argument.

What holds up is the rest of the disposition. SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-705, SUP-707, SUP-708, SUP-711, SUP-712, SUP-713, and SUP-714 are classified on the dominant fact, with sunset cases kept noncompliant and in-window FD-7 kept justified. SUP-706 versus SUP-711 correctly separates a mutable late link from a proved source-transaction duplicate. Alias self-approval is treated as a CS-9 breach that M0 cannot see. The system finding — weak preventive enforcement, and M0 unfit as an oracle — follows from the severe cases rather than from a split-the-difference average. Prosecution and defense each contain the concessions the task demanded.

The deduction from a near-full mark is for the SUP-709/SUP-710 scope overclaim, the softer-than-stated PD-3 authority holding, the undisclosed emergency over-constraint, and the fabricated overlay fields.
