The write-up is complete, internally consistent, and unusually careful about approximation: the dependency table, the conservative process-tree policy, the single-`Analyze Root Cause` POWL partial order, the T5 approximation witness, and the release/cross-branch counterexamples are all real strengths. It is not nearly flawless, because both executable models misuse the operator the task told them to use.

The central defect is `Operator.LOOP`. In PM4Py, a loop has exactly two children: the body is executed at least once, and the second child is the redo between repetitions. `*(seq(E, I), J)` is the language `(E I)(J E I)*`, i.e. `(E I J)* E I`, with the same leaves reused and `J` unable to follow the exiting inspection. Both the tree and the POWL instead build a one-child loop and treat it as a Kleene star, then append a second `E;I`. That object fails process-tree/POWL validation, so the “allowed” verdicts for T1–T4 and the “forbidden” verdict for F3 are claims about a model the stated API will not run. The caveat that LOOP might mean “one-or-more” does not describe the actual operator, so the assumption does not bound the error.

Two smaller API problems sit on top of that. `SilentTransition("skip supplier")` is unsafe: a silent transition’s label must stay empty, and a string argument can either be rejected or leak a visible skip label, which would make every no-supplier trace wrong. The validation sketch assigns `net = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(...)` even though that call returns `(net, im, fm)`.

The process-tree approximation itself is an allowed policy and is not disguised: forcing `Plan Verification` to finish before design, the risk route, and supplier consultation is a genuine under-approximation, and T5 shows it. It is coarser than necessary once a correct two-child loop is used, but that is a fidelity choice, not a hidden contradiction. The POWL edge set for the non-block pattern (one `A`, `P` and `D` both before the trial, reviews and supplier only before release) is the right design and is better than the tree.

Section coverage, witness inventory, and the self-critique are otherwise in good shape. The score stays at 7.0 because the repeat-until construct, which every trace depends on, is encoded with an invalid operator in both models.
