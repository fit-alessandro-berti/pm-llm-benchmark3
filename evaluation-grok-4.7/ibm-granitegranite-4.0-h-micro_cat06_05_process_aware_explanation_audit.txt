The audit identifies some real leakage, then mishandles it: several fixes still use future information, valid prefix signals are discarded, required items are missing, and Case Y is explained with a full future trace.

Section 1 does not cover the required set. `full_trace_variant_id` is absent, as are all five sequence patterns and the local-only features `claimant_channel`, `final_decision`, and `initial_reserve`. `same_day_document_count_snapshot` is repeated three times at three severities. Finding types never separate temporal leakage, process impossibility, mutable lineage, proxies, and legitimate signals.

Remediations are often wrong. `total_case_duration_hours` cannot be repaired with the final resolution timestamp; that is still the outcome. `final_handler_team` is not fixed by a closure timestamp; the warehouse has no as-of team at `t0`. `num_document_requests_total` is complete-trace leakage, not a sum that ignores receipt. Legitimate `t0` features are then rejected: `initial_damage_complexity` is frozen before `t0`, and `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` is a valid slack signal. Excluding `claimant_region` and `calendar_week` outright also ignores the instruction not to delete every correlated feature. The extraction-date bug in `prior_claim_count_before_t0`, the nightly snapshot leak, mutable `risk_score_current`, and closure-time `last_resource_role` are the clearer calls, but severity and lineage are still coarse.

Section 2 names the requested checks without making them operational. “Alignment” is used as a label rather than as trace-to-model alignment, and footprint, prefix, DFG, and declarative checks are never given roles or limits. The suite calls `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` impossible, then later a legitimate loop. A second settlement decision is allowed only after Partial or Deny; unavailability at `t0` is not process impossibility. `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is the opposite case: normatively backward, and here the equal timestamps plus a missing sequence index make it an ordering artifact. Enabled versus future-possible versus impossible at the post-coverage prefix is not specified. Fraud investigation, document cycles, and damage-to-decision are future-possible full-trace patterns, not prefix evidence.

Section 3 misses the required classes and the four standards (predictive validity, causation, actionability, fairness). Duration and the document snapshot are called calendar proxies; appeal and document-request totals are called fairness proxies; the final team is called a process-state signal; frozen complexity is called a lineage defect and dropped. What may and may not be claimed is not stated per class. Queue length is the only classification that is roughly right, and even that is left as “approximate” rather than a congestion signal whose ±5-minute join must be disclosed.

Section 4 is not a credible revision. Statuses are not one of retain / recompute / invalidate. Case X keeps queue and complexity, which is reasonable, but omits the negative elapsed-time signal, never removes `final_decision = Partial`, and drops `final_handler_team` and `initial_reserve`, which are not Case X drivers. The narrative treats 0.82 as justified by the two surviving terms. Case Y lists `full_trace_variant_id` ending in appeal, second decision, and closure as a credible driver, which reproduces the leaked future path. It omits the negative queue signal and the repairable prior-claim count, and removes Case Z features instead. `claimant_channel` and `initial_reserve` have no stated lineage and are handled inconsistently rather than flagged. Case Z notes the tied timestamps and mutable risk score, then adds the wrong variant id, omits last role, the same-day snapshot, and week 52’s holiday/policy confounding, and again proposes recomputing total duration from the final timestamp.

Section 5 touches every requested heading but stays generic and repeats the leakage error: prefix-only recomputation is described as using the final resolution timestamp. Calibration is framed as keeping performance stable after leakage removal. Explanation stability is reduced to version control. Fairness is a region audit, not a separation of association, causation, actionability, and disparate impact. Schema drift, sequence indexes, as-of joins, and refitting explanations on a prefix-only model are missing.

The write-up is structured and catches the obvious post-closure and extraction-time bugs, but it contradicts itself, assigns features to the wrong cases, and would still ship future-path explanations. That is not a usable audit.
