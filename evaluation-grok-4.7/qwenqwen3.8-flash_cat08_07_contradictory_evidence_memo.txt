The memo is strategically sound and meets the required structure. It recommends a segmented hybrid rather than national scale or full rollback, keeps facts, interpretations, and assumptions apart, explains all five metric conflicts with the process-mining evidence, and ties M1–M8 to decisions that could change.

The rollout thresholds are not reliable enough for a higher score. Simple-house first-time-right of 85% treats the overall baseline of 82% plus a +3pp segment change as a level; the prompt does not give that segment’s baseline. Complaints are held to “no worse than baseline” in the memo, then set at 6.5% and 7.5% against a 6.1% baseline, and the 30-day complaint metric is described as a 60-day result. The simple-house all-in ceiling of EUR 184 reuses the baseline pre-complaint average, not a segment all-in cost. “Normalized to the pilot mix” is ambiguous and likely reverses the needed mix adjustment. Several other bars (p90/p95, manual activation, second visits, precision) quietly allow results worse than baseline without a clear justification.
