The forecast is operationally unusable: its central claim is that B-417 is released around Friday 20:30, which the incubation clock makes impossible.

Incubation started Friday 14:00 and becomes technically ready only 46–54 h later, so Sunday 12:00–20:00. Routine readout is Monday 08:00 onward unless an on-call microbiologist is authorized (probability 0.25–0.45). QA has no Sunday hours, and Saturday QA closes at 13:00, before the earliest technical readiness. A Friday terminal time cannot occur on any path. Section 4 then repeats “48–48 h” for Friday evening, Monday midnight, and the severe tail, so the remaining-time figures do not match the clock times.

Chemistry is treated as already in service at 15:30 and finished by 18:00. At 15:30 one urgent and three ordinary samples are ahead, and both analysts are occupied (about 1.2–2.0 h and 0.4–0.9 h). Only 2.5 h of laboratory time remains, and the service distribution is triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) h, whose mean is about 2.8 h. Under either FIFO or priority, B-417 is unlikely to finish Friday; the unfinished-work rule pushes residual service to Monday 08:00. The smooth-path join is therefore not “microbiology at Monday 08:00–09:00, QA done Friday.” It is the later of a Monday chemistry completion and a Monday (or, only if chemistry had already finished, Sunday) microbiology readout. That maximum is never calculated. The rerun scenario is also faster than the smooth path (central 20:00–22:00 versus 22:00–24:00) and, in the same write-up, puts QA acceptance on Friday while placing the microbiology result on Monday.

The temperature-excursion dependence is reversed. Confirmation raises the chance of a chemistry rerun and of a contamination indication; it does not release the microbiology result early. Section 1’s sensitivity and Section 6 both say confirmation shortens the path. Contamination’s extra 20–30 h and a laboratory investigation’s 3–7 calendar days are named and then dropped: the stated P90 remains Monday 00:00–04:00. QA’s future queue, overdispersed arrivals, Saturday staffing, two Monday reviewers, and the median 1.5 h / P80 3.5 h service time are not turned into a waiting-time estimate; acceptance is repeatedly parked at 20:00 without a queue calculation.

The six headings and the requested columns are present, a few inputs are copied correctly, and overlap is acknowledged. Those points do not repair an impossible earliest time, an inverted dependence, a missing parallel join, or a tail that excludes the long paths the scenario defines.
