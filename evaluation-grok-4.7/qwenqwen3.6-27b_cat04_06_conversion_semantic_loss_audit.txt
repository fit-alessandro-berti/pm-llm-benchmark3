4.5

The write-up covers every required section and reads the source partial order correctly in outline: registration first, risk and evidence unordered, legal optional after risk only, verification joined on both predecessors, binary loop with at least one trial, release as a join, archive last. Choosing the Petri-net route as primary, and expecting the process-tree route to fail because the dependency is not block-structured, is the right call. Rejection criteria, the visual-misreading discussion, and the POWL-as-authority recommendation are substantively usable.

The validation core is not. Section 5 does not hide silent transitions; it refuses to fire them. On a POWL-derived net the first enabled steps are typically τ, so the search never reaches the visible behavior it claims to audit. The shortest accepting visible trace already has seven labels (register, risk, evidence, configure, inspect, release, archive), while `visible_bound=6`, so the positive witness is unreachable even under a correct τ-abstraction. The negative witness is an unused `Ellipsis`, not a trace. `input_arcs`/`output_arcs` are not PM4Py arc attributes (`in_arcs`/`out_arcs`), markings are used as dict keys although `Counter` markings are unhashable, and `reachability_graph.markings` is not a real attribute. Comparing place-identity markings across two nets would be meaningless even if that attribute existed. The printed “exhaustive under the bound” claim is therefore false.

Section 4 marks several invariants `validated preserved` and says round-trip marking equality was verified. Nothing in the conversion code checks those invariants; soundness is not trace equivalence. Invariant IDs are swapped between §1 and §4, and §6 and §7 then use `INV-08` for different properties (overlap versus the release join). Optionality of Legal Review is observable after hiding τ, via presence versus absence of the label; the baseline marks that invariant as not observable. `check_is_workflow_net` is never called, despite being required on every accepting net. Playout is omitted with no reason. §9 both asserts that the tests confirm trace equivalence and, later, that unbounded equivalence remains open.

Taken together, the document is a serious semantic sketch wrapped around a validation procedure that would crash or return empty trace sets, while describing those results as confirmation.
