The document is a strong, structurally complete audit: the nine required sections are present, the source partial order and binary-loop language are read correctly, the process-tree route is isolated as fallible, markings are null-checked, tau is hidden, completion uses exact final-marking equality, and POWL is kept authoritative over derivative BPMN. The deductions below are for concrete validation and consistency flaws, not for missing structure.

The bounded explorer’s exhaustiveness claim is stronger than the code. `exhaustive_under_declared_bound` ignores `visible_bound_pruned`, yet acceptance treats that flag as proof that every completed trace with at most three `Configure Trial` occurrences was compared. That length bound is proven only for the source alphabet and loop shape. A derivative can add extra visible firings, stay within three iterations, exceed length 14, and still be marked language-equal. The prose slides from a source-only counting argument to a general exhaustiveness claim.

Playout is presented as enforcing exact final equality through `add_only_if_fm_is_reached` and `fm_leq_accepted`. Those keys are not part of the stated playout contract and are likely ignored. Basic playout can then emit incomplete traces; the later exact replay would mark a correct net as failing the smoke test. The exception handler does not repair that false failure.

The preservation table is internally uneven. The same asymmetric join that makes legal and verification non-block-structured is `ambiguous` for ordering, but `expected preserved` for the release synchronization, the A/P precondition of verification, and label uniqueness under Petri-to-BPMN. PM4Py’s net-to-BPMN step is exactly where cloning, gateway restructuring, and label duplication are plausible. `not tested` on the round trip is honest; several sibling cells are more confident than the evidence policy allows.

Smaller defects: the primary `convert_to_petri_net` call is outside the failure recorder; the coded negative set omits legal-before-assess even though invariant I-03 depends on it; reachability-graph construction has no termination bound on a cyclic net; the length formula is broken LaTeX inside Markdown; citation [2] is attached to save functions that are not what that citation supports. “Partly” in I-02 also answers a different question than the column asks: both A/P orders are visible after hiding tau, while true concurrency is a separate, correctly discussed limit.

Semantics of the source itself are handled well: XOR-with-skip, `(C,I)(D,C,I)*`, at least one iteration, the N-shaped dependency, interleaving of legal review with the loop body, and the distinction between trace inclusion and step/pomset equivalence. Rejection criteria, canonical-source policy, and residual uncertainty are specific and mostly well aimed. This is not a near-flawless 9–10: the main validator can certify a derivative the stated bound did not fully explore, and one diagnostic path can fail a correct model.
