The memo reaches the right decision class (hybrid, not national scale or full rollback) and covers fitness/precision, alignments, DFG shifts, temporal profiles, resource networks, segments, A–E, and M1–M8. It is not reliable enough for a high score.

The lead claim that everyone except simple houses became “slower and more expensive” is false on the memo’s own numbers. Median duration improved for apartments (−2 days), business (−3), and rural (−1); only old buildings worsened on the median. “More expensive” is not shown by segment. “34% nationally” is the pilot share. Those regions were richer in apartments and older buildings, so the national simple-house share is likely higher. That error changes how large the hybrid win is and how bad national scaling would look.

“Field-survey utilization fell 52%” is wrong. It fell from 78% to 52%. Failed-visit 13%→19% and second-visit 10%→17% are DFG edge shares, not the case rates (second visit is 11%→18%). “Full estimated cost” overstates an incomplete estimate. “Field interviews” are not in the evidence. “Proves” and “three design choices drove this” treat a short, non-randomized, mix-confounded pilot as identified causation.

Process-mining reasoning contradicts itself. The pilot model is described as over-general (precision 0.51, too many skip/rework/billing combinations) and, in the same cell, as overfitting because generalization fell to 0.56. Those are opposite faults. Using discovered precision ≥0.70 as a rollout gate ignores miner settings. Section 3 also says the median gain means the “average” customer feels better; mean duration is unreported, and median is not the mean. “Fewer handovers  more robust” is a broken sentence in a required explanation.

The evidence table is the strongest part, but week-3 missing segment/risk attributes are parked under cost uncertainty instead of under the segment effects that justify immediate routing. M4 is ranked first without requiring segment-level cost, so an aggregate 60-day figure can be dominated by the harmed segments and still not test the simple-house case. Thresholds are directionally unclear where comparison operators dropped out, and the conjunctive bar (0% early billing, precision, utilization) can block a net-good segment for a mining artifact.

Strategically competent, factually loose on the points that set scale and scope.
