The final answer meets the required structure and stays balanced: it maps all eight mechanisms, separates optimistic, central, and adverse cases, treats East differently from North and South, and recommends a monitored pilot rather than a full rollout. The indicator table, tail-risk discussion, and conditional recommendation are substantively on target.

The quantitative forecast is not tight enough for the data given. Summing frequency times median wait shows management’s own routing model slightly raises total handover waiting time, by roughly an hour per case, against cycle times of about 7–9 days. An optimistic median improvement of 5–8% is therefore unsupported, and the answer never makes that hours-versus-days point. Legal and risk specialists are already at 91–93% utilization while referral volume rises from 9 to 12 per 100 cases; accepting a 10.5-hour optimistic wait ignores how sharply queues degrade near full utilization. Aggregate rework is also not rebuilt from the supplied rework-after-handover rates. The staff warning that the same central technical pool would absorb legal/risk triage and difficult quality checks is not carried through as a workload-composition effect.

The pilot is directionally sound, but a Technical Shared Service-only trial cannot validate legal/risk capacity or shared-approval accountability, while the recommendation still treats that pilot as the gate for broader rollout. An 85% utilization guardrail also sits on top of the stated 15% utilization gain, and North is a weak control for East because the baselines differ sharply.

The PM4Py sketch names the requested analyses, but several comparisons are computed and then unused. `.query('concept:name in ...')` is invalid pandas syntax, edge weights are not normalized per 100 cases, and activity service/waiting time is treated as resource-handover waiting time. The log has only `time:timestamp`, yet the sketch requires `start_timestamp`. The example 7-day SLA is inconsistent with medians of 6.8–8.6 days and observed miss rates of about 7–10%.
