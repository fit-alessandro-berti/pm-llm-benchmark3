7.5

The required seven-section structure is complete and the recommendation is appropriately conditional: pilot first, with design changes, and no scale-up from early averages. Mechanisms, scenarios, indicators, hidden-average risks, and the pilot all cover the requested dimensions, including East versus North/South, fairness, learning, tails, and accountability. The PM4Py sketch uses the relevant organizational, variant, DFG, rework, and filtering functions and correctly refuses to treat completion-only timestamps as pure queue or utilization time.

The opening quantitative claim is wrong. The eight listed handover frequencies sum to 343 per 100 cases now and 359 after, not 443 and 459. The increase is 16, so about 4.7%, not 3.6%. That erroneous 459 is repeated in the central scenario. The component comparisons (67 to 77 and 81 to 92) and the 588.3 to 722.2 wait index are correct, and the directional conclusion still holds, but the headline totals do not.

The scenarios also stay too qualitative relative to the given team baselines, and the sketch leaves SLA comparison and service-time calculation as comments while introducing a confusing `case_case_*` attribute prefix.
