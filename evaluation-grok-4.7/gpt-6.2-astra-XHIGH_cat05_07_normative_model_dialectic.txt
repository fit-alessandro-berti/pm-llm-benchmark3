8.2

The answer is a strong, case-level audit: it separates CS-9 from M0, treats PD-3 authority as unproved rather than invented, keeps FD-7 inside its written window, and does not let CRM loss, duplicate registration, label drift, or `Request_More_Info` become false violations. The matrix is complete, falsifiable, and the system finding correctly refuses a compliance rate. Those merits are real. They do not make the answer nearly flawless.

The clearest logical break is SUP-704. Section 1 puts the burden on the party claiming an exception to substantiate compensating controls. FD-7’s cap is cumulative spend until retrospective approval. The record shows only EUR 18,000 initial spend. The judgment nonetheless returns `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`, and the matrix reverses the burden: a breach appears only if someone later proves spend above EUR 20,000. Moderate confidence notes the gap; it does not repair the contradiction. On the stated standard this is at best a qualified, still-open exception.

The POWL is not a faithful encoding of the prose. `sequence(information_requests(), Auto_Approve)` is merely shuffled with `Sanctions_Check` and `Automated_Master_Data_Validation`, so auto-approval can precede both and still enable the pilot branch. The standard route is stricter than the pilot route for no stated reason. `Automated_Master_Data_Validation` is also a mandatory visible transition, yet no observed pilot trace contains it and the data proposal treats validation as linked evidence, not a log activity. M1 therefore false-fails the very traces the pilot route was built to admit. `M1.validate_partial_orders()` is not a demonstrated PM4Py API, and the transitive edges in `sequence()` are an unnecessary fragility. Exceptional routes also have no reject or suspension exit.

Smaller faults accumulate. “Continuing flood conditions establish need” treats an operations assertion as a stipulated fact. SUP-715 is `MIXED` even though the stated fact is that required disclosure was not recorded; channel speculation is thinner than that record. The fourteen clearly ineligible auto-approvals are softened to “review,” although they fail CS-9 even if PD-3 is wholly valid. Section 5 rejects “the defense” for a claim Section 3 already disclaims; that overclaim belongs to Operations. SUP-711 is cleaned into `COMPLIANT` while the equally reconciled rename in SUP-713 remains `MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT`, without a sharp rule for when reconciliation changes the classification.

Dialectically the briefs are fair, specific, and usually well caveated. The misses are local but concrete: one burden contradiction that changes a classification, and a normative model that does not actually accept its own justified pilot language.
