7.2

The design narrative is strong and covers the required structure, but it is not nearly flawless. Several concrete modeling and code errors keep it well below a top score.

The scope, assumptions, experiment matrix, uncertainty plan, validation strategy, and decision criteria are generally coherent. Non-stationary demand, calendars, latent objections, service-versus-wait separation, cross-case links, and the refusal to claim optimality are handled correctly in the prose. PM4Py use is mostly conventional: pandas loading, `format_dataframe`, DFG, performance DFG, variants, and a guarded `parse_process_tree`.

The main defects are:

- The arrival skeleton is not a valid non-homogeneous Poisson process. Acceptance probability is capped at 1, so the stated 1.7× seasonal and 2.2× Monday effects are erased. The supplied month multipliers are also all 1.0.
- Missing-document routing is hardcoded to false, which removes a major observed delay path rather than leaving it as a real placeholder.
- Objection assignment is independent per case, contradicting the cluster mechanism described in the text.
- Specified resource facts are incomplete: FTE counts and the Monday portal-support clerk are omitted, despite their direct effect on the Monday completeness queue.
- Baseline dispatch is said to include aging priority, which blurs the incremental effect of D6.
- Public-notice interaction with holidays and council weeks remains thin.
- A six-month warm-up is short relative to complex-case tails near 260 days, even with a pre-seeded backlog.
- Most interventions and stress scenarios are named but not connected to parameters, and `is_working` ignores hours of the day.
