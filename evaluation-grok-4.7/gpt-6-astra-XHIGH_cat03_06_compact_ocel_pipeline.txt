The construction is semantically faithful, internally consistent, and unusually careful. All 17 events, 12 objects, O2O edges, and the chosen E2O policy reconcile: 34 source rows plus 15 derived rows equal 49; per-type incidence is Order 20, Item 14, Package 6, Delivery 4, Invoice 3, Payment 2. Flattened shapes and directly-follows counts match that policy, including O200 not inheriting P2/D2, I2 having no reservation, payments staying off orders, and both installments remaining `partially_settles` without an invented total or allocation. Provenance, validation, and the refusal of a unique object-centric Petri-net reading are all sound.

The score is not higher because a few required interpretations stay slightly soft. The Order DFG table states P→S with count 2, but O100’s only P→S pair is Pack P2 followed by Dispatch P1, so that count mixes a same-package succession with a cross-branch one; the self-loop warning does not identify this edge. B→D likewise skips E12, which sits between invoice and D1 confirmation, and D→X on O100 skips M2. The five O100–O200 co-events are correct only for the enriched log, yet that bullet does not say E09 and E13 are derived while E06, E07, and E11 are source. `relation_origin` is passed into the constructor without noting that some PM4Py builds are sensitive to extra relation columns, and the commented `view_oc_petri_net` name is likely not the real entry point. None of these breaks the model; together they keep it short of flawless.
