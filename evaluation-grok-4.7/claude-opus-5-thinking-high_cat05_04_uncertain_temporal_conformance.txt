7.5

The interval arithmetic, timezone conversions, DST-fold pruning, SLA labels, and both sensitivity matrices are largely correct and carefully reasoned. That core is not enough for a near-flawless score: several claims are wrong or overstated, and the required program does not do what the write-up says it did.

**What holds up.** Clock-offset direction (`true = recorded − offset`), resolution-before-offset, CEST/BST/EDT conversions, and the 2025-10-26 Berlin fold are right. The sole surviving T-405 branch (alert in CEST, investigation and containment in CET) is the only order-feasible fold combination, and the surviving windows stay about three minutes wide. Checked duration intervals match the tables, including the knife-edges: T-402 S1 `[958.5, 1231.5]` (certain breach), T-404 S2 `[2277, 2703]` (3 s over), T-406 S2 primary `[1948.5, 2911.5]` versus alternative `[1948.5, 2611.5]` (possible breach versus certain pass), T-408 S2 `[3297, 3303]` and S3 `[138297, 138303]`, and T-403 still sticking out of the ζ=3 alert-to-start band by about 1.5 s. Tolerance and zeta cells that were recomputed agree with the matrices. Separating SLA deadlines from the two-sided profile, refusing to treat `Alert_Sent` as receipt, and keeping the mobile entry delay as an explicit semantic choice are the right structure.

**Material defects.**

- The script cannot have been “executed end to end.” After `pd.to_datetime(..., utc=True)`, `.dt.tz_localize(None)` raises on tz-aware data, so control never reaches `format_dataframe` or `conformance_temporal_profile`. Section 5.2 still reports concrete run output (ζ=2 hits at 2.75 sd and 2.40 sd, 26 of 87 cells, 6 over-calls, 20 masks). Those figures are a hand simulation, not this program’s output. The simulation also assumes every profile pair is checked even when it is not a direct succession. PM4Py temporal-profile conformance is defined on directly-follows pairs, so `Alert_Received → Containment_Complete` would not be scored in these traces; T-403’s alert-to-containment “over-call” and the 26-cell total depend on that unchecked assumption. The version note isolates tuple shape only.

- The claim that no zeta can classify T-403, T-406, T-407, and T-409 alert-to-start, because those intervals are “wider than the ζ=3 band,” is false. All four widths (about 423, 963, 1023, 1021 s) are narrower than the 1080 s ζ=3 band, and each becomes a certain pass once zeta is only slightly above 3 (roughly 3.01, 3.18, and 3.34). The ζ=1/2/3 matrix itself is right; the invariance conclusion is not.

- The “most damning” ζ=3 midpoint silence on T-408 close is a category error. At ζ=3 the interval analysis also marks that profile pair a certain pass (138303 ≤ 151200). Midpoint and interval agree. The real point, made correctly in §4, is that a ζ=3 profile miss is not an SLA miss.

- The unpruned T-405 duration hull is not “roughly `[−3480, +1080]`.” Cross-fold pairs reach about `−6480 s`. The surviving branch is unaffected.

- The T-407 send-versus-receipt paragraph states `CERTAIN_PASS`, then the correct send-anchored interval `[1017, 1023]`, which is a certain violation. The final interval is right; the sentence contradicts itself. The intermediate window `[14:16:57, 14:17:03]` is not the investigation-start interval.

- “Eight of the eleven unresolved SLA findings” does not match the tables (10 possible SLA breaches, or 17 if indeterminate S3 rows are included).

Smaller nits: “a 5-minute tolerance destroys” T-402 S1, which remains a possible violation on a ~31 s sliver; causal clipping of T-409 is stronger than anything the evidence states (classifications happen to be stable without it); open bounds are approximated by 1 ms. None of those move the main labels.
