6.7

The document matches the required structure and is unusually careful about invariants, witnesses, tau-hiding, and not treating a picture or a successful call as equivalence. Those parts are not enough to carry the grade: the validation code that is supposed to decide preservation does not run, and a central conversion claim is wrong.

The firing primitive is invalid for PM4Py. `Transition` exposes `in_arcs` and `out_arcs` (`arc.source`, `arc.target`, `arc.weight`), not `.input` and `.output`. Every `fire` call raises, and the explorer swallows that exception and continues, so both nets yield empty trace sets. Empty sets compare equal, so `trace_sets_identical_under_bound` can pass while no witness was ever replayed. `pm4py.PetriNet.Marking` is also not a real type; `Marking` is a top-level class, not nested in `PetriNet`. The fallback import `pm4py.objects.petri.utils` is the pre-2.3 path.

`discover_footprints` on a net returns a dict (`sequence`, `parallel`, start and end activities), not `(activity, footprint-list)` pairs. Iterating it and unpacking `item[0], item[1]` does not compare footprints. The cycle test `n_arcs > n_states` is false for directed graphs: a DAG can have more arcs than nodes. Probe C3 is a tautology: `visible_labels(...)[:0]` is always empty, so the first conjunct is always true.

The repeated claim that process trees cannot express “both pending at once,” and that they have no parallel operator, is incorrect. PM4Py trees have `Operator.PARALLEL` (`+`), and an AND-split of Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence is series-parallel. What a tree cannot represent faithfully is the N-shaped partial order: Legal Review depends only on Assess Risk, while verification waits for both Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence and may still overlap Legal Review. Pipeline B should fail or approximate for that reason, not because concurrency itself is inexpressible. Marking POWL→Petri concurrency as `approximated` overstates the risk; `convert_to_petri_net` on POWL is the path intended to keep that partial order, and the audit should have left it at `expected preserved` until C1.

The invariant table, rejection list, visual misreading notes, and canonical-model split are strong. The executable audit does not establish them.
