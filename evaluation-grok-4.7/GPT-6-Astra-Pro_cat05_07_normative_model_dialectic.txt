The case analysis is unusually precise, but the answer is not nearly flawless. The control-flow revision under-approximates a permission it claims to encode, and the aggregate judgment softens a finding the same brief has already proved.

The stipulated language of M0 is right: both checks are required, either order counts, and only SUP-701, SUP-712, and SUP-715 fit on exact labels. The 14/289/9 split is arithmetically correct. SUP-703 is correctly severed from the EUR 20,000 cap. Expiry is applied to new activations in SUP-705 and SUP-714 without invalidating follow-up on SUP-704. SUP-712 is not excused by trace fitness. SUP-715 is not inflated into a four-eyes breach. SUP-702’s June notification is not blamed on the July CRM migration. SUP-706, SUP-711, and SUP-713 are separated by linkage strength. The burden distinction between an unproved exception and a proved breach is coherent, and MIXED is explained rather than used as a shrug.

The emergency route is the clearest modeling error. FD-7 permits activation to precede quality review and permits it to precede manual approval; it does not require manual approval to be retrospective. The code puts an edge from the activation block to `Manual_Procurement_Approval`, while the standard route requires `Quality_Review` to finish before manual approval. The union therefore rejects a compliant emergency trace such as sanctions, manual approval, activation, then quality. That interleaving is ordinary partial-order behavior, not an awkward construct that had to be sacrificed. Calling the coded route a “selected variant” names the choice and still leaves the missing rectangle unstated. The witness set never includes that negative, so “22 witnesses passed” does not test the hole. PM4Py itself is also admitted to be unrun; the harness claim is narrower than it sounds.

The pilot profile is a better reading of PD-3, including master-data validation and quality before closure, and the separate profiles correctly stop the union model from becoming an authorizer. Transitive edges are an unnecessary compatibility risk. The dangling `sandbox:/mnt/data/...` path is not a delivered artifact.

At system level, “eligibility categories, not compliance rates” is the wrong caution for the 14 clearly ineligible auto-approvals. The 289 eligible cases are not a compliance rate, and 312 is not the onboarding denominator. The 14 are the same failure as SUP-703 even if PD-3 was valid: the exception they invoked does not apply. The six post-expiry uses are treated more directly than those 14. Section 5 then rejects prosecution and defense positions that sections 2 and 3 had already disclaimed.

SUP-704 as `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` with an open cap ledger is consistent with the stated burden because the sequencing waiver is proved and excess spend is not. SUP-715 is only loosely a data-quality defect; it is an unproved disclosure omission. Those are smaller calls. The architecture, overlay table, and collection proposal otherwise keep authority, identity, spend, and raw-versus-canonical evidence apart without inventing missing approvals.
