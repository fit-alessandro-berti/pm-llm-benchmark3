7.5

The six-section policy is complete and hits the main traps: I-03 is ineligible after Diagnosis Complete, I-05 is suppressed as an unchanged repeat while I-07 re-enters on the new log but is not swarmed, and untreated-case spillover is priced into both ranking and the scenario table. Segment calibration, label contamination, and capacity monitoring are all specified with usable safeguards. The base arithmetic checks (midpoint effects, €4,750 intervention burden, €7,200 spillover, about €8k net).

The allocation is not consistent with its own score. Base marginal net ranks I-08 (about +€2.2k) above I-09 (+€1.2k) and I-02 (+€0.3k), yet I-08 is only a backup and I-02 is deployed. The lower-bound rationale is plausible—I-08 is negative even before spillover—but that robust score is never shown, so the tier order contradicts the displayed priority score. The stated conservative treatment of Non-Native-Language Support is also not applied: I-09’s lower-bound direct net is about €2,350, at or below the €2,400 base spillover penalty, yet it is Tier A2. Calibration shifts are defined and then unused in every numeric score.

The impact ranges are only partly auditable. Optimistic €24k and 0.70 are not derived. Adverse spillover of €11k–€16k adds an unspecified delay-euro amount on top of the 0.01–0.03 probability spillover, which may already represent that delay. Causal monitoring proposes IPW and encouragement designs without an interference correction, despite spillovers being central to the policy.
