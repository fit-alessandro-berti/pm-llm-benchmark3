8.1

The audit is strong: it covers every required feature and pattern, separates the later-legal appeal reconsideration path from the same-timestamp close-before-assessment pattern, keeps queue length, prefix age, and frozen complexity, and rejects causal readings of SHAP. The feature-store, conformance, recalibration, stability, fairness, and drift plan is concrete. The remaining defects are real.

The conformance section mislabels the required status split. After `Claim Registered -> Coverage Check Completed`, damage assessment and `Documents Requested` are enabled, `Documents Received` and settlement are only later-possible, and appeal is conditional on a future Partial or Deny. Calling settlement, appeal, and closure “impossible at t0” conflicts with that trichotomy and with the answer’s own later-feasible reading of `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded`.

`total_case_duration_hours` is correctly removed, but the stated reason is backwards. A 10-day breach label is a threshold on duration, so the feature is definitional target leakage, not something downstream of the target. `final_handler_team` is the same end-of-case defect as `last_resource_role`, yet one is only high and the other critical. The ±5 minute queue join is treated as noise; a snapshot after `t0` is forward leakage and needs a last-snapshot-at-or-before-`t0` rule. The planted as-of extraction join for employee experience is never picked up.

Case Y treats `initial_reserve` as the defensible upward driver while its own lineage finding says availability is unproven. That violates the “defensibly available at t0” rule. Both narratives also reuse SHAP signs from a model whose largest terms are leaked, and they ignore residuals of about +0.21 and +0.20, which dominate the surviving attributions. Region and channel are temporally available proxies with mixed operational and fairness content, especially repair-network coverage, but they are dropped from the case stories rather than retained under a non-causal, governance-limited reading. A prefix document-request count at this particular `t0` is essentially degenerate, and that limit is not stated.
