7.6

The assessment is structured, temporally aware, and generally disciplined: GPIP-4 is kept as the integrity baseline, ECD-2 is confined to timing and supplier-register effects inside a doubtful subject-matter scope, the CFO email is limited to Finance interpretation, post-commitment approvals are treated as mitigation, and ON-44 is used only as an evidence rule. The issue-specific hierarchy, defeasible catalogue, four-way evidence split, and replacement clause are all usable. Those strengths do not remove several concrete errors.

The clearest mistake is P-505. At EUR 88,000 from an approved supplier, A3 never triggers: the threshold is above EUR 100,000 or a new supplier. The controlling interpretation nevertheless says “C3/A3 satisfied,” and Compliance is listed as though it were a required satisfied control. The A2 failure can still support NONCOMPLIANT, but the rule identification is wrong. The same row also omits the unrecorded Create_PO and Plant_Approve, which matter if commitment is found to fall after 18:00.

P-502 is undated. Clock times alone do not place it inside 14–18 July. The row nonetheless treats C1, C2, and the four-hour C3 record as satisfied and assigns high confidence. Late Compliance on a stated new supplier can sustain NONCOMPLIANT without that assumption; crediting the directive cannot.

Local Plant approval is applied unevenly. L-B2 is enforced for P-501 and P-506, then dropped for in-window cases. That gap is material for P-508: EUR 155,000 is above the CFO email’s EUR 150,000 cap, ECD-2 never mentions Plant_Approve, and no Plant event is recorded. P-503’s total absence of Plant approval is likewise folded into a vague “local rules also apply” clause. L-B6 is checked only for P-501; every other emergency commitment is left silent on the 24-hour form.

“New supplier” and “unapproved supplier” are used as if settled. Only P-502 is labeled new. If “unapproved” means “new,” A3 reaches P-504 even outside the SOP, and the section 5 INDETERMINATE alternative is much weaker. If it does not, that assumption needed to be stated.

All eight primaries are NONCOMPLIANT. P-501 depends on reading a timing requirement into B2 that the SOP does not state. P-504 depends on treating cafeteria repair as maintenance or production support when the stated facts exclude sterilization and production effect. Medium confidence and later alternatives do not cure a primary label that the text itself leaves open. INDETERMINATE was available and, on the stated burden of proof, fits those two rows better. Severity is also compressed: same-day late Finance on P-505 is “High” alongside P-503’s missing Compliance, missing Finance, and requester-committer-authorizer overlap.

Smaller overclaims sit in the same direction. P-501 marks A1 satisfied although the Plant and Finance approvers are unnamed. P-508 treats the 16:05 authorization as complete C3 compliance without a record time. P-503’s approved supplier is described as if a supplier-register deviation needed excusing.

The alternative section, remediation split, and draft clause are the best parts of the answer. They show the author can see the flips that the primary table then underuses. The grade reflects that unevenness: strong method, real threshold and proof errors, and too little classification diversity where the sources are actually silent.
