7.5

The source semantics are largely right: registration first, unordered assessment and preparation, optional legal review after assessment, verification only after both prerequisites, no order between the legal branch and the loop, a do/redo loop with at least one `Configure Trial` then `Inspect Trial`, a release join, and archive last. The witnesses for legal review before preparation and between trial activities follow the partial order. Both pipelines are identified correctly, tree failure is treated as a possible valid outcome, and the canonical choice—authoritative POWL, derivative BPMN, no silent round-trip promotion—is sound. The bounded search is also carefully designed: `None` labels are hidden, final markings use exact equality, repeated silent states are cut, and the length-11 claim is properly limited to non-truncated runs.

The required soundness diagnostic does not work. `pm4py.check_soundness(...)` returns a Boolean, but the code unpacks it into `result["sound"], result["details"]`. That raises `TypeError` for every net. The handler records an exception rather than a verdict, so workflow-net/soundness output cannot support acceptance or rejection, and the reachability gate would always see soundness as unestablished.

Section 5 only partly meets its comparison list. Footprints and witness traces are real checks. The reachability call is disabled and, if enabled, prints only graph size. Playout is omitted. Those limits are acknowledged, but the delivered comparison is narrower than the required set.

The preservation table is honest that it was not executed, yet it is too coarse to be an audit: every Petri-to-BPMN cell is `ambiguous` and every tree or round-trip cell is `not tested`, with no converter-specific expectation for this non-block-structured join. S2’s negative cell is a converter prohibition rather than a prohibited trace. Rejection item 12 also quarantines a model for unresolved drawing ambiguity even when the net behavior is acceptable, mixing layout with semantics.

The residual-uncertainty section correctly refuses to treat bounded visible-language equality as concurrency, silent synchronization, engine semantics, or unbounded loop equivalence. Those cautions do not offset the broken soundness call or the incomplete comparison code.
