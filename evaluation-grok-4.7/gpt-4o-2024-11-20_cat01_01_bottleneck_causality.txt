3.5

The memo is not usable for the decision. It keeps the required section skeleton, but the load-bearing empirical claims reverse the supplied tables, the causal graph cannot express an Orion effect, and the diagnostics do not run.

Section 1 picks `partly causal through mechanisms` at 70% on a false pattern. Orion’s crude mean is higher (21.96 h vs 15.24 h), and that gap reverses inside every complexity band, not only Low: Low 7.50 vs 9.64, Medium 17.17 vs 19.38, High 31.57 vs 35.00. Mean wait is also shorter for Orion in every band (3.45 vs 5.06, 9.25 vs 11.12, 19.47 vs 22.75). The memo instead says the medium and high gaps merely narrow and that Orion has longer median waits. Those two errors are what the 70% verdict is built on. Total, direct, and mediated effects are never separated. Cells of 2 cases and near-empty shift overlap are ignored, so the confidence is far too high. A supportable reading of these tables is `mainly confounded`, with the remaining group contrast `not identifiable`.

The graph has no directed path from `resource_group` to `queue_wait`, `review_service`, `rework`, or `cycle_time`, so it cannot represent the estimand. `resource_group -> unobserved_reviewer_skill` is a dead end. `unobserved_case_risk` never points at routing, yet Section 3 calls it a confounder. `case_completed -> cycle_time` reverses selection. `review_service -> rework` reverses the process fact that a repeated review is inside `review_service_h`. `queue_wait -> review_service` and `cycle_time -> quality_outcome` are treated as supported facts; they are assumptions. Shift and load are wired inconsistently around `routing_rule`.

Compliance is mislabeled a mediator of group assignment. Policy requires it from case features, and Orion’s higher compliance rate is the case mix. Within Medium, the only band with both compliance levels, required cases are not slower (Orion about 16.9 h vs 17.4 h; Nova 19.0 h vs 19.8 h). Review service is almost the same across groups once complexity is fixed, so it is not evidence of an Orion service penalty. The role table never says which variables are confounders of the total effect versus mediators that must be left open, and it never names a collider.

Section 4 is not auditable. It allows “Orion handles low complexity efficiently” and “skill mix aids high complexity” from stratum means, which is exactly treating stratification as identification. Day-versus-night is left crude. Night is slower overall only because night cases are harder; within Low, Medium, and High the night means are shorter. Rework is higher for Orion only in the crude 28.6% vs 18.8%; within complexity it is lower or equal. Appeals are 1/14 vs 1/16 and are not quantified. `cycle_time_h` is not wait plus service; that residual is never examined.

The hypothesis table ranks a harmful night-queue effect first even though the stratified waits point the other way. The “contradiction” that low-complexity Orion is no faster is false. The compliance row predicts similar delays across groups, which would weaken that hypothesis, not support it. The load row predicts that matching will fail, which is a positivity result, not evidence of a load effect. None of the five hypotheses centers on completed-case selection or on the measurement problem that `wait_before_review_h` mixes queueing with compliance waiting. Tests omit the conditioning set and the result that would favor versus weaken the claim.

The pilot structure is the one salvageable piece: a small randomized reroute, plus a capacity change that does not only relabel the group. It still randomizes the group label while leaving night coverage and certified-reviewer capacity unspecified, uses appeals as a short-run stop rule when only two appeals exist, and sets no numeric threshold. The closing recommendation is about 65 words against a required 120–200, and it still treats night and compliance queues as Orion’s delay mechanism.

The Python block is not reproducible. The CSV is replaced by `...`, `pd.compat.StringIO` is invalid, and the PM4Py calls are not `pm4py.parse_event_log_string` and `pm4py.discover_dfg`. Group filtering on the parsed log cannot work, trace variants are not compared, empty overlap cells are dropped, the standardized weights are not stated, and there is no warning when a cell has fewer than three cases. Section 8 is the only part that meets its brief.
