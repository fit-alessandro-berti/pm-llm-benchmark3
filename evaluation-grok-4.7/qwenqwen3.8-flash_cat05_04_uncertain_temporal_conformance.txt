The latest corrected conclusions get the clock-offset direction, resolution-before-correction order, and the main UTC conversions right, including the Berlin DST fold and the order-constrained survivor set. The SLA bands, profile bands, and most duration intervals are arithmetically consistent with those assumptions, and T-402 S1, T-408 S2/S3, and the mobile entry-delay split are the right qualitative calls.

That is not a finished answer. The required seven sections, tables, sufficiency write-up, governance recommendation, and executable PM4Py block are never actually produced; the text stops while still drafting. Several classifications that flip on 1–3 seconds (T-403 profile ζ=3, T-404 S2, T-408 profile ζ=2, T-409 S1 at +2 minutes) are stated as firm while the closed-versus-half-open endpoint rule is still unsettled. T-407 is left as both a conditional possible violation and indeterminate, so it has no single classification.
