7.5

The assessment is complete, well structured, and correctly refuses to treat raw approval gaps as discrimination, to treat case mix as a full justification, or to treat the risk score as neutral. The eight required sections are present, the mechanisms and mitigations are specific, and the bottom line is mostly well calibrated.

It is not nearly flawless. The Group C bound treats V4 as a possible Accommodation Review path, but the given V4 summary is a language-support path, so the “~34%” figure is mis-specified. The claim that C’s entire adjusted +4.1 days comes from the 4.7-day accommodation queue compares a group-level adjusted effect with a median edge wait without incidence weighting, and medians do not add that way. “Self-review” is stated as a present defect, while the handover pattern (specialist → supervisor) only shows dual roles and a risk of self-review. The PM4Py block does not run: the `viol` dictionary is invalid syntax, `violates()` indexes the case table incorrectly, `get_variants` is treated as if it returns case lists, and `filter_event_attribute_values` and `filter_paths_performance` are called with the wrong argument structure. Capacity ratios also mix all-group need counts with specialist headcount and are stated more precisely than the assignment data support.
