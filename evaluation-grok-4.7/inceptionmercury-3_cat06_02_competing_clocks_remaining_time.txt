5.5

The write-up has the right skeleton: join as a max, weekend calendars, FIFO versus priority, overlapping scenarios, and temperature dependence. It is not a mean-stacking forecast. The numerical story is still too early, too tight, and in several places inconsistent with its own assumptions.

The incubation window is wrong. Readiness 46–54 h after Friday 14:00 is Sunday 12:00–20:00, not Sunday 12:00–Monday 20:00. The later Monday-morning readout quietly uses the correct bound, so the decomposition contradicts itself.

Conditional probabilities are mislabeled. Rerun 0.25 and contamination 0.18 are outside the given bands, not midpoints. Marginal rates happen to land back inside the stated ranges, but the ledger’s justification is false. Confirmed temperature is also defined as an investigation trigger even with clean assays. A 0.30–0.55 investigation probability is then treated as a tail on the rerun and contamination rows, while those rows’ central times assume prompt release. That understates the conditional center by about a day and skips the documentation-investigation band.

The summary quantiles do not mix the scenarios. Smooth is only 45–60%, with a 30–45% rework band whose own center is Tuesday–Wednesday, yet the P50 remains Monday 16:00–20:30. The P80 interval starts inside the P50 interval, and the P90 interval starts before the P80 ends. Hour totals are about 30 minutes short of the stated clocks (Monday 14:00 is 70.5 h, not 70; Monday 16:00 is 72.5 h, not 72).

Chemistry and QA are compressed. Risk-priority “usable Monday 08:30–11:00” drops the long service draw after a late Friday start. FIFO “Monday 14:00–17:00” omits a long-service spill past 18:00. Monday’s QA backlog of about 4 is asserted with no Friday–Saturday drain, and a 2–3 h wait is the low side of two reviewers, a multi-batch queue, and stated overdispersion. Weekend on-call authorization is bundled with contamination as driver 2 even though the answer’s own join logic says chemistry is usually slower, so Sunday readout often does not move the release.

The temperature flag as the main updating event is directionally sound, and rejection is correctly kept as a secondary terminal state. Those strengths do not repair the arithmetic slip, the center-versus-tail error under dependence, or the optimistic quantile mixture.
