The dialectical casework is strong: both briefs are argued at real strength, concessions are explicit, the matrix covers all fifteen cases, and the final classifications are mostly decisive rather than averaged. Authority, expiry, scope, logging, and non-control-flow rules are separated carefully, and SUP-702 versus SUP-706 is a good distinction.

The required executable model is not sound. PM4Py’s loop operator has exactly two children (do, then optional redo); the code builds a three-child `LOOP(silent, Request_More_Info, silent)` and would fail construction or not implement the stated 0..n semantics. The hedge about “reordering children” does not fix arity. The correct approximation is an XOR of a skip and a binary loop. The low-risk route also forces `Quality_Review` before `Notify_Requester`, which can reject a valid pilot trace in which notification is hours after activation and quality is the next business day; that ordering limit is not disclosed.

SUP-715 is classified NONCOMPLIANT while the same answer treats whether the relationship is a reportable conflict as unproved, which is stricter than its own burden. SUP-711 is labeled COMPLIANT without stating in the judgment cell that this assumes deduplication. The aggregate of forty-seven `Request_More_Info` cases is not cited in either brief. Architecture, overlay table, and data-collection fields are otherwise thorough.
