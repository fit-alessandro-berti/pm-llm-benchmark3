6.5

The final assessment is substantively strong and well structured, but it is not numerically or technically reliable enough for a higher score.

The refusal of a single “automation reduces cycle time by X%” claim is right, and the final ladder correctly separates observed dose/load patterns, a suggestive relative improvement, a narrow conditional median effect, and unsupported universal, tail, rework, CSAT, and cold-start claims. Availability versus use, staggered selection, regression to the mean, fraud-desk interference, staffing, case mix, and median-versus-p90 are all treated seriously. The estimands, mechanism table, threat list, and “expand with an embedded evaluation” choice match the decision problem. The embedded phase-in, event logs, queue data, spillover checks, and guardrails are concrete.

The required comparison table is not disciplined. Alpha/Beta “Delta/Epsilon” control changes of +4.8h and +16.9h are Delta’s changes, not the two-control average (about +3.6h and +14.7h), so those DiDs are inflated. Gamma’s p90 contrast pairs a June baseline (−16.1h) with a January–June control change and produces an invalid −22.4h DiD. Those figures conflict with the later −6.9/−2.8/−3.9h scenario bounds and with §7’s “−3.9 to −4.9h.” The −21.6% Gamma figure is the August-only change, not the July–August mean versus June. Percent intervals also disagree across §5, §7, and §10 (−4% to −13% versus −13% to −2% versus 3–10%).

Identification is sophisticated but imperfect: adjusting the program-level effect for post-treatment arrivals is a bad-control problem, era dummies are redundant with month fixed effects, and 120 permutations should be 30 distinct cohort assignments. The rollout rule is infeasible as written: a two-branch coin toss cannot yield a randomization-inference p-value below 0.10. The code’s event-study dummies are never joined to the regression frame, so with statsmodels installed the script errors before the intensity model and the required warnings. Section 1 also overstates complexity and arrival changes as applying to every adopter and drops the sign on Alpha’s and Gamma’s FTE losses.
