The pipeline is substantively strong: the 17/44, 15/39, 13/35, and 10/28 event and E2O counts are arithmetically consistent, e05 and e14 are kept, e03/e04 and e11/e16 are the only high-confidence merges, abstraction and harmonization are separated, and the `have_common_object=True` failure on M1/M2 is explained correctly. Provenance, ordering layers, and the eight failure modes are all present. It is not nearly flawless.

The decision tree disagrees with the later decisions. Q4 (different source responsibility) fires before Q5, so e06/e07 and e13/e15 are harmonization collisions whose only terminal is “retain both refined,” while the evidence-based merge/retire path is unreachable. e09/e10 have different activities and the same source; they miss Q2–Q5 and land in quarantine, yet section 2 retains them unchanged with high confidence.

Section 3 treats e08 → e09 → e10 as a strict physical order with no alternative. The facts only establish that handover follows carrier acceptance. Release-before-acceptance is a domain assumption. That overclaim sits next to an otherwise careful semantic-versus-deterministic split. The microseconds warning is right in mechanism, but “payment capture precedes invoicing” overstates a tie in which e11 is already an invoice event.

Section 7 states a raw Order/V1 edge `Issue Invoice → Issue Invoice` (e11 → e16) and counts Invoice among five rework self-loops. Under directly-follows semantics those two invoices are not consecutive in eid order, ingestion order, or the proposed business order; intermediates sit between them. Equal timestamps also mean the raw log has no precedence inside each tie until a total order is imposed. “Self-loops on … Invoice (2x)” mixes activity counts with DFG edges. The Order-flattened phrase “single-item, single-payment” also fights the later, correct ET–OT claim of two items and two payments on the merged events.

The implementation is PM4Py-shaped and does not mutate `ocel_raw`, but it does not carry its own policy. `secondary_authoritative_cols` is never passed, so the ERP/billing field merge is not performed. `precheck_duplicate_candidates` is printed and then ignored. `ocel_drop_duplicates` still runs on the whole conservative log while e03/e05 and e13/e14 share activity and timestamp; safety is only an event-count assert plus an undocumented assumption about the library key. Relation timestamps are not resynced after the timedelta, and qualifier-aware `drop_duplicates` can keep duplicate E2O rows when qualifiers differ, breaking the hard-coded 39. Aggressive damage (union, ET–OT of 2, M1–M2 co-occurrence) is stated as library behavior rather than as one possible merge implementation.

Prose is also well above the 1,900–2,900 word aim, with the same decisions repeated across the tree, the pair table, the pipelines, and the sensitivity section.
