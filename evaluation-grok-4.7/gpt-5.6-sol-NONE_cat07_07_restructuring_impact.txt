7.5

The forecast is complete, scenario-faithful, and correctly refuses a full rollout. It treats East differently from North and South, separates medians from tails, and ties pooling, learning, fairness, and accountability to log measures. The deduction is for concrete defects, not missing sections.

The mechanism map misstates two signatures. Higher utilization is not “more events per specialist-hour,” and it does not by itself make the working-together network denser; that density is a pooling effect, which the same map later describes as weaker pair ties. “Greater activity-based resource similarity” is also too coarse: pooled specialists may converge while local staff diverge from technical work. Resource-level handover centrality appears only if the log has a shared-queue resource or resources are aggregated; keeping N_Tech, S_Tech, and E_Tech as separate IDs would show cross-team edges, not one hub. The listed frequencies support a sharper claim than “interactions do not clearly fall”: the eight reported types sum from 343 to 359 per 100 cases. Pooled tech utilization is never estimated from 71%, 68%, and 82% before the extra 15% and the added legal, risk, and quality demand.

Garbled phrases (“caseworkerlocal-tech,” “Specialist ReviewCustomer ContactSpecialist Review,” “Ashared serviceA”) make otherwise sound points unreadable. Several “days” indicators, especially network centralization and role discovery, will be too thin at these volumes.

The pilot is directionally right but not identified cleanly. Putting East fully in the pilot conflicts with “non-pilot cases within each team,” and randomization, matching, and stepped-wedge are left as alternatives rather than one primary design.

The PM4Py sketch calls the right family of techniques, then would fail or mislead in places. `get_service_time` is invoked with `case_id_column`, `activity_column`, and `timestamp_column` instead of `case_id_key`, `activity_key`, and `timestamp_key`. Activity-based similarity is given case and timestamp arguments it does not take. `insert_case_service_waiting_time` and the manual pre-activity gap are both computed and never reconciled. Queue “cases” counts events. The sketch never builds the actual decision metrics: handover type per 100 cases, wait after that handover, and rework after that handover, by team, complexity, specialty, and segment. Performance DFGs are stored without noting that their default aggregates omit the tail the rest of the answer treats as decisive.
