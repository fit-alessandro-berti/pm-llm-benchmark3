8.2

The analysis is strong, internally consistent, and numerically reliable on the quantities that matter. Regime boundaries, outage-versus-recovery treatment, weekday/weekend composition, the case-mix shift, the intervention confound, and the opening-hour Little’s Law caveats are all defensible, and the single Python block implements the required summaries, dispersion measures, lag-1 dependence, change-point heuristic, and PM4Py limitation.

The required Little’s Law matrix is damaged by missing operators, so several expressions are false as printed: `12*21.5/416.29`, `12*37/1213.67`, and `12*20.5/495.02` do not equal the intended 6.29 h, 3.67 h, and 5.02 h. Two verdicts also leave the required three-way label set. Hourly Fano factors are treated as stochastic burstiness without separating deterministic intraday seasonality, and a median-workload ratio below 1 is not explicitly reconciled with right skew and the growing high-mix backlog. The pre/post arrival contrast is slightly apples-to-oranges, though the weekend-composition conclusion is still directionally sound.
