7.8

The design is a serious discrete-event twin: case and shared state, calendars, latent objections, non-stationary and architect-clustered arrivals, service kept separate from waiting and external delay, paired comparisons, snapshot initialization, and no claim of an optimum. The eight required sections are present, the hard constraints are respected, and the PM4Py path (pandas load, `format_dataframe`, variants, DFG, performance DFG, defensive tree parse) matches current conventions. The score stays below the top band because several design choices that decide the experiment are unspecified or inconsistent.

The baseline is not operationally closed. B0 includes “current batching” while D3 is an off/on batching factor, so the reference policy is not identified. Baseline queue discipline is never stated, yet D6 is a priority reservation and D3 changes who waits. Planner capacity is not defined as a citywide pool versus district assignment, even though planning delay depends on district and two planners are restricted to simple renovations. The Monday clerk dedicated to portal support is omitted, so the busiest arrival day is under-constrained. Fire Tue–Thu, inspector Tue–Fri, legal council weeks, and the environmental 40% reservation are only partly restated.

Policy mechanics do not meet the experiment matrix. D1 and D7 change submission timing, abandonment, and pre-submission delay, but `ArrivalGenerator` is not policy-aware. D4 and D6 change seasonal headcount and reserved hours, but `ResourceCalendar` is shared unchanged across runs and only `Router` reads `policy`. The runner loops seeds, not the outer parameter draws required in the uncertainty section, and it keeps a single RNG despite the common-random-number stream split. Those are structural gaps, not missing polish.

Timing and decision rules have smaller but real slips. The prose measures waiting from prerequisite completion; the code subtracts the previous flattened event, which misstates parallel planning, fire, and environmental branches. “Completeness rework” and “Inspection rework” in the tree are not reconciled with the log, where rework appears as repeated real activities. S2’s “+10 percentage points Commercial/Complex” can mean each type or the combined share. The duration rule never says whether the whole 95% interval must clear the 10% / 2-day threshold. Many matrix “levels” are only “vary uptake/window/accuracy,” and the large policy-by-metric screen has no primary endpoint or multiplicity control. Provisional thresholds and the 80–85% utilization band are labeled as such, but they are still sharper than the unidentified service/wait split can support.
