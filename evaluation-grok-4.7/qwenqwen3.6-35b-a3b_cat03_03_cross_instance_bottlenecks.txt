The write-up hits the required section skeleton and gets several raw intervals right, but the coupling analysis is not reliable enough to score higher.

The required waits are arithmetically present: J1 3 h 55 min and J2 3 h 40 min before B1, J1’s 13:30–19:25 post-pack gap, and a stated CAL-3 counterfactual.
