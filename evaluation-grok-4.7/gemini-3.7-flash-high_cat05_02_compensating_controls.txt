Section 5 contradicts Section 3 on AP-204: the table correctly says nominal conformance, then the counterfactual says nominal FEP-1 failed and the diagram marks the funds LOST. Section 3 also parks the AP-201 and AP-207 pre-settlement manager actions under detective controls while Sections 4 and 5 treat those same actions as the gate that stops loss.

The case table’s severity labels mix Low, Minor, Medium, and Critical without a defined scale, and residual risk is largely restated exposure.

The code meets the broad PM4Py/pandas checklist and is correctly labeled as evidence rather than a verdict, but its evidence flags are unreliable. Version caveats are appropriate; they do not repair those logic errors.
