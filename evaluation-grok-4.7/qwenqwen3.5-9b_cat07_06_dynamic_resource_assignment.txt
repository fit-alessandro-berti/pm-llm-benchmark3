C003 is described as a diagnosis skill match for R6, who is level 1, and it becomes a third reassignment on a known diagnosis loop; R3_Celine is the low-load level-3 alternative. Urgent VIP C011 is taken off its level-3 owner, R6, for no continuity or capacity reason.

Training flags are wrong on C006, C012, and C015. R10_Jonas’s priority is security, not quality, and R8_Hugo is already a security specialist whose priority is approval. R5 and R10 have security skill 0, so the C015 supervision note cannot be executed; that exception is never stated. No assignment is a supervised pair, despite the required output and the security, approval, and high-priority rules. C001 (skill-1 owner, high complexity, high handover, supervision feasible) is the clearest missed pair.

The algorithm does not implement the policy. The worker text is too generic to explain these choices and incorrectly implies that anyone left without a new case was protected from overload.
