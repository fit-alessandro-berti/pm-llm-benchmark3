6.5

The interval framework is mostly right: offset direction, resolution-before-correction, HUB ±1.5 s, Europe/Berlin and America/New_York conversions, and the T-405 fold reduction are handled correctly. Only Alert in CEST plus Investigation_Start and Containment_Complete in CET can satisfy ingestion order, and the surviving UTC windows are the right ones. Most SLA and ζ=1 profile rows match those windows, including genuinely tight calls such as T-404 S2 at [2277, 2703] and T-408 IS→CC at ζ=2, where 3303 s sits just above 3300 s.

The certain/possible distinction still fails in a few places that the task is specifically about.

T-407 Alert_Received→Containment_Complete is wrong. With receipt constrained by the sent time, latency, and investigation start, the feasible duration is [2277, 3303] s. Even the looser unconstrained latency bound is about [2097, 3303] s. Both sit wholly inside [1980, 3420], so the ζ=1 result is CERTAIN_PASS. The table instead gives [≈2280, ≈3300] and labels it POSSIBLE_VIOLATION. Those approximate endpoints are already inside the band written in the same row, and §4 repeats the error by treating T-407 as a ζ=1 possible violation that only clears at ζ=2.

T-409 is classified incoherently. The same weak [−12 min, +12 min] bound produces INDETERMINATE for S1, because negative lag is allowed, and CERTAIN_PASS for S2. Under an upper-bound SLA, a negative Alert_Received→Investigation_Start lag still satisfies “no later than,” so the testable result is POSSIBLE_VIOLATION on [−481.5, 1021.5], not an untestable case. Section 4 then silently switches to an ordered [0, 1021.5] reading and promotes that case toward CERTAIN_PASS as tolerance grows. That drops the §3 semantics without saying that tolerance does not cure the indeterminacy claimed there.

The ζ sensitivity write-up is not case-complete. T-403, T-404, and T-405 are collapsed to “CERTAIN_PASS or borderline POSSIBLE” at ζ=3. The AR→IS band at ζ=3 is [60, 1140]. T-404 [597, 1023] and T-405 [720, 1080] become CERTAIN_PASS. T-403 [718.5, 1141.5] stays POSSIBLE_VIOLATION by 1.5 s. The hedge avoids computing the boundary the rest of the analysis is precise about.

The mobile entry-delay assumption is disclosed and then not tested. Shifting occurrence earlier by up to 5 minutes is what makes T-406 S2 and the IS→CC profile possible violations. If that delay only affects recording time, those two results become CERTAIN_PASS. That is a material assumption with no classification delta.

Section 6 misstates what T-405 still needs. Ingestion order already removed the fold. The remaining S1 window [720, 1080] is minute-flooring plus the ±60 s clock bound. A fold flag or “store UTC” does not, by itself, force that window entirely below or above 900 s. T-409 IS→CC is also a POSSIBLE_VIOLATION and is not given its own evidence requirement. Section 1 calls inferred events low-strength; the T-404 start row is labeled medium.

The code does not implement the required analysis. Normalization is partly hardcoded, the DST helper is never applied, and only T-401 S1 is classified, with the rest “omitted for brevity.” It never calls `pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile`. The midpoint log keeps Investigation_Complete, so a directly-follows checker will not score the eventually-following pairs the profile defines, and the “comparison” is a fixed print statement rather than a case-level contrast. SENSOR is half-open in §1 and closed in §2 and in the code.

Governance coverage is otherwise on target, and the bulk of the UTC arithmetic is reliable. The grade is held down by a false possible-violation on a wholly interior interval, an unresolved T-409 semantics split, a hedged ζ=3 result that is actually a miss, and a code block that does not perform the analysis it claims.
