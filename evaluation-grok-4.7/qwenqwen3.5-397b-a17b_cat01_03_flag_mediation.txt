4.5

The write-up has the right overall story and the required section structure, but it is not reliable on the data, and several causal labels are misapplied.

The qualitative pattern is real: flagged cases reach triage much sooner, almost all then enter manual review, and total cycle time is much longer. That is not enough to carry the answer, because the timestamp table and the rates built from it are wrong. Flagged manual-review occurrence is 10/10 versus 4/10 unflagged, not 50%. Escalation is 6/10 versus 2/10, not 50% versus 30%. Rejects are 2/10 in both groups, not 30% versus 20%. Mean risk score among flagged cases is about 0.80, not 0.86. High complexity is 8 cases and low is 4, not 6 and 5. Flagged high load is 6 and normal load is 4, not 5 and 5. Flagged triage-done-to-manual-start averages about 390 minutes, not 360, and manual-done-to-escalation-start averages about 420 minutes, not 270. Total cycle time is about 991 versus 490 minutes, not 1054 versus 454, and non-escalated flagged cases average about 549 minutes, not 828. “NA (5 cases)” also miscounts absent stages. Those are not acceptable approximations, and the false reject gap is used as evidence of a quality tradeoff.

The estimands name interventional and controlled effects, and the caution about cross-world natural effects is appropriate, but the labels do not match the interventions. P2 and P3 change review rules or capacity directly; those contrasts are not indirect effects of the flag. E7 calls the quality estimand ill-defined because the outcome is unobserved, which confuses definition with identification. P1 is treated as if hiding the flag removes priority routing, despite the required distinction between display, the flag bit, and downstream behavior. In the pathway table, P2 is described as removing priority even though P2 retains priority triage.

The proposed RDD cutoff of 0.7 has no treatment variation: the observed switch is between 0.61 and 0.64, and every case near 0.7 is already flagged. The pilot then randomizes individual cases in a shared queue after the text itself flags interference. Evidence strength for P2 is called “Medium (RDD)” although no such analysis was done. The synthesis is under the required 180 words, repeats the incorrect headline times, and calls the P2 trial an encouragement design.

The Python sketch correctly avoids inventing absent stages and warns against causal DFG readings, but it is not reproducible. `format_dataframe` is called with `case_id_key`, `discover_dfg` is never called, and `discover_performance_dfg(..., measure='performance')` is unpacked as two objects. That call fails on standard PM4Py, and escalation waits and services are never summarized.
