6.5

The redesign is structurally complete and often sharp: case-mix stress is separated from pure volume, each bundle names a downstream station, urgent and complex are not jointly first, tails and SLAs are preferred to mean cycle time, and the dashboard is tied to on/off triggers. Those strengths do not offset concrete quantitative and control errors.

The lead scenario misstates the urgent load. Weekly volume is 2,300 to 3,300 (+43%), and complex-panel volume is correctly 299 to 660/week (+121%). Urgent volume is 414 to 957/week, about +131%, not “+61%/wk.” +61% is only the mix ratio (29/18). The same sentence uses two different methods, and “+10% severity” is never applied.

Machine policy does not close. Under M2 degradation, P2 dedicates M1 to urgent and complex, stops routine-to-M2, and also batches routine to free M1. Routine, the largest class, has no coherent machine. B3 has the same hole once dedication and the M2 health gate are both on.

Cross-training is reversed relative to the evidence. The note and I4 make technical validators cross-trainable, in about four weeks, toward accession correction. The package instead cites that note to train preparation staff into technical validation. The conditional rule not to raid technical validation below 90% utilization is sound; the staffing claim is not.

Several effects are overstated. Outsourcing does not “remove” the 48h hold or the 22h medical queue; it diverts some complex volume and adds 12h plus reconciliation. An evening medical shift does not relieve the single specialist reviewer. Moving one unnamed medical validator from 93% to “>110%” assumes an unknown headcount. Peak routine p90 is 120h against a 72h target, so tail headroom is already negative, not “thin.” “Little’s law” is used as a label for upstream spillback.

Process-mining use is otherwise real (first-pass yield, cancel share, batch-wait and repeat shares, hold incidence, 74% to 58% urgent bypass). It still misses peak flow imbalance: assay inflows are about 10,250 against about 8,500 outflows, direct evidence of WIP buildup. Repeat-after-failure remains the worst tail (p90 132h/240h) without its own policy. Several red bands (<15 open recollections, urgent share ~35%, z>2) are presented as operational limits without a basis in the log.
