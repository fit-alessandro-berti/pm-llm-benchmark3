7.5

The redesign is complete and operationally coherent: all seven required sections are present, the four mandated stress types are distinct, interventions are conditional rather than blanket capacity, and downstream overload is discussed for each major lever. Bottleneck sequences, on/off triggers, the trade-off table, redesign-induced residual risks, and a leading-versus-lagging dashboard are all usable.

The score stays well below excellent because several evidence claims are wrong or mis-aimed.

The peak “direct technical release” share is calculated as 4,050/7,240 (~56%). 7,240 is assay-to-technical-validation arrivals, not technical-validation exits. Peak exits are 3,420 + 4,050 + 610 = 8,080, so the direct-release share is about 50%, not 56%. The normal-period denominator was the true exit total (15,890), so the comparison is internally inconsistent. The direction (more medical validation in the peak mix) still holds; the cited percentage does not.

The 42h/60h edge is `Request recollection -> Receive sample`, the wait for a replacement specimen. `Pre-analytical check -> Request recollection` is already 3h/7h. Setting a “recollection decision within 4h, not after 42–60h” treats the long loop as a decision delay. Early triage starts that loop sooner; it does not remove most of the 42–60h.

Repeat volume is compared as 1,260 versus 1,140 “despite lower total case volume.” The peak window is 3 weeks against 8. Weekly repeat events are roughly triple, and the per-case repeat rate roughly doubles. The raw-count contrast understates the shift the logs actually show.

Routine acceptability is set at a peak p90 of 96–120h. Observed peak routine p90 is already 120h, so that guardrail largely ratifies the degraded tail. A few mechanisms also sit outside the evidence: preparation staff doing technical validation, and tele-review as specialist backup. The daily “few” indicators expand to ten leading metrics plus five lagging ones.

The package would still be a sound operating design if those measurements were corrected. As written, the process-mining support is strong in structure and uneven in arithmetic.
