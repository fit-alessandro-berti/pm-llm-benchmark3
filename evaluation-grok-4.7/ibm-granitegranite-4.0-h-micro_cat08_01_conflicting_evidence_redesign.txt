The response follows the required section skeleton, but it misreads key evidence, under-specifies the redesigns, and treats conflicting objectives as jointly improvable. That falls well short of a defensible process-mining redesign.

**Diagnosis.** The contradiction is restated from the prompt and then left unanalyzed. The fast path is never quantified (2,550 auto fast approvals; 2,300 dispatches), and the central cost fact is absent: fast approval is 31% cheaper before rework and only 9% cheaper after complaints, reshipments, and credits. The 12.5% rework-repair rate is attached to the fast path; it applies to repaired high-value devices on the repair loop (45 rework edges). The cited “long delay” at close is the dispatch-to-close edge, whose median is 3.2 hours and mean is 5.0; that is a short edge, and it is not high-value-specific. The actual long tail is dispatch to complaint/reopen (mean 96, median 70, p90 180), tied to wrong parts.
