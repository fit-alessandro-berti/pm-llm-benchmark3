7.5

The policy is decision-theoretically serious and operationally recognizable. It separates stage eligibility from raw breach probability, refuses I-03, treats I-05’s unchanged refresh differently from I-07’s new log, adjusts segment calibration, prices false alarms in expectation and at full burden, reserves one slot, and puts queue spillover, label contamination, and segment equity into the ongoing rule. Most case-level midpoint values are reproducible, and the optimistic and base euro bounds largely tie out.

The required audit trail still fails in several places. I-03’s “direct EV ~€150; flat FA €250” does not follow the stated formula: midpoint lift gives about €600 of gross benefit, so expected-burden net is near €40 and full false-alarm net is about −€250. The base breach-count range is written as +0.04 to +0.40; crossing the same effect and spillover bounds gives about −0.04 to +0.40, and the prose calling that count “mixed” conflicts with the printed signs. The adverse net of “€12.7k to €4.5k” and “0.72 to 0.24 net breaches” is not recoverable from that row’s benefit, cost, and spillover ranges; the outer euro bound is closer to roughly −€17k to about break-even, so “net harmful” across the whole adverse cell overstates the row. Minus signs are dropped in the same way on I-03 and in the adverse interpretation.

Several judgment claims are stronger than the arithmetic. I-02’s midpoint direct net clears only the lower portion of the stated per-slot spillover band, not “base spillover” as such. Optimistic false-alarm cost is lowered without a reason tied to baseline risk. Adverse spillover of 0.03–0.05 per case, and optimistic spillover below 0.01, sit outside the given band without a stated delay-to-probability mapping. The reserve is not compared with the option value of the idle slot. The later protected SME/public floor is not reconciled with a live portfolio of I-09, I-02, and I-04. Dispatching I-09 and I-02 “immediately” is ambiguous if both specialists are required for one swarm. PANEV still depends on an unquantified waiting penalty and equity bonus, and the adverse loss is not tied back to a scenario weight or loss limit.
