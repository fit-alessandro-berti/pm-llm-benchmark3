7.5

The pipeline is complete and internally consistent: all 17 events and 12 objects are present, timestamps are timezone-aware, payment amounts stay on events, E2O rows copy activity and timestamp from `events`, O2O covers every required business link, and the integrity checks match the tables. Order, Package, Delivery, and Invoice trace shapes, shared-event copying, and DFG edges follow the chosen E2O policy, and the failure modes and alternatives are real.

The main analytical error is in the OC-DFG account. That graph is per object type, and the stated chains do not hold for every type named. Items have no Create Order, and I2 never participates in Reserve Stock (only I1 and I3 do). Deliveries start at Dispatch Package; they have no Pack Package. Each Payment has a single Record Payment, so Record Payment → Record Payment exists only on V1, not on the Payment type. Instance coupling among P1, O100, and O200 belongs to the interaction summary, not to OC-DFG nodes.

`completes_settlement` on M2 is not supported. The narrative calls both M1 and M2 partial payments and gives no invoice total, so completion is an added assumption. Section 1 also never states that single-order packing links the owning order, while E08 still relates P2 to O100; the later trace predictions depend on that unstated choice.

The keyword `OCEL(...)` call is the right constructor. The `TypeError` fallback passes `o2o` in the fourth positional slot, which in current PM4Py is `globals`, so that path would drop object-to-object relations. The interaction summary also treats the O2O qualifier `carries` as if it were an observed event interaction.
