The write-up follows the required section structure and often compares real decision sets with FIFO, priority, SPT, appointment, and skill predictions. QA is mostly reconstructed correctly, and several QB continuation-versus-re-rank points are sound. Those merits do not offset eligibility mistakes that change headline episodes.

The QC diagnosis is internally inconsistent. The 10:32 table correctly has dynamic SEPT select C06, yet the prose says decisions through 10:32 “align strongly with SEPT,” and both the diagnosis and §3 call C02–C04–C01 “exactly” SEPT. That subset order is not the decision-time prediction once C06 is waiting. C07 (arrived 10:40) is omitted at 10:54 and 11:34. At 11:34 the answer therefore treats priority as a tie and EDF as C01; C07 is P2 with deadline 12:00, so priority and EDF select C07, while C06 uniquely favors SEPT. Strict priority at 10:18 is also softened to “C05 or C03” even though P1 dominates P2. The §4 claim that 8→14→22→36 is observed adds a 36-minute step that does not occur.

QD misapplies the skill rule the answer itself uses for D05. `mechanical+electrical` is a conjunction, so D07 is not eligible for D-M; only D-X is. The supposed 11:35 FIFO violation (D08 over D07) is illusory: among D-M-eligible jobs, FIFO, priority, and SPT all select D08. The real anti-priority fact, D07 before waiting P1 D10, is cited as ordinary FIFO support. The D-X FIFO prediction names D07 although older D-X-eligible work includes D03 and D04, and D08 is ignored even though an 8-minute job fits the 11:20–11:30 gap. D10 is said to arrive just after D-F’s meeting begins; it arrives at 11:25, and D-F is free 11:36–12:00, long enough to run D10 before 12:00. That gap is never examined.

Smaller factual problems accumulate. QA’s “strongest support” groups A07 and A08 into the choice of A02, before either has arrived, and treats A06 as an appointment override of FIFO when FIFO selects A06 as well. In QB, the 09:48 non-preemptive-priority cell states the preemptive prediction, and §3 says B04 runs before B06/B07 “become relevant,” although both are already waiting. B05 is used as clean P1-preemption support despite B03 being chosen over already-waiting B05 at 09:48.

Uncertainty language, the data-collection plan, and the warning against inferring a formal priority or deadline policy are useful, but they sit on corrupted eligible sets. For this task, those sets are the analysis. The resulting confidences, QD’s contradicting episode, and QC’s primary SEPT claim are not reliable enough for a higher score.
