The policy is coherent and operationally serious, and it meets the main quality tests: I-03 is refused because Diagnosis Complete is past the effect window, I-05’s unchanged refresh is suppressed while I-07’s new log is reviewed immediately, effects are not set equal to `p_now`, and untreated-queue harm enters both selection and the scenario table. The planning scores are reproducible from the stated 75/25 rule, and the scenario arithmetic is internally consistent. It is still short of a near-flawless decision analysis.

The impact accounting mixes an ex ante penalty into ex post scenarios. The full €7,800 false-alarm burden is subtracted in Optimistic and Base even though those rows assume the risk reductions occur, so the alarms were not false. That charge is larger than the €1,350 swarm cost and is what pulls the optimistic net down to about €16.7k–€25.1k. The table’s Intervention cost column shows only €1.35k, so the net is not auditable from the table alone. Calling the charge “conservative” does not fix the conditional-cost error. Ex ante, P(false alarm) is also not 1; for I-08 the €5,000 burden is large enough that a realistic probability would change the margin over the spillover hurdle.

Optimistic effects are not the upper half of the given ranges. Upper halves are about 0.19–0.28, 0.10–0.15, and 0.24–0.30. The row uses 0.23–0.28, 0.12–0.15, and 0.27–0.30, a top-quartile band with no justification. That lifts the optimistic floor (about 0.62 breaches and €31.2k) above the scenario that was requested. Adverse incremental risk of 0.03–0.05 is likewise an unmapped step beyond the stated 0.01–0.03 model. The €20k loss used to turn spillovers into euros is labeled illustrative, but it is still the number that decides whether I-08 and I-09 clear the hurdle and whether the base net stays positive.

The decision rule promises uncertainty adjustments that the ranking does not implement. Non-Native-Language Support is scored with the same 75/25 weights as better-measured segments, then placed first, despite sparse calibration and effect evidence. A lower-bound score for I-09 is about €2,350, essentially at the reference queue cost. I-04 (10 h) and I-08 (8 h, calibrated risk only 0.31–0.38) are marked Intervene, while I-02 (3 h, diagnosis already started, score about €2,260) is only a backfill. That underuses the rule’s own latest-useful-start and “confirm when there is time” tests. Serial 45-minute swarms can consume the early window of the diagnosis-stage cases the spillover model says are harmed.

Smaller gaps remain. I-07’s action label is Wait even though the text correctly demands immediate ordinary escalation inside a 1.5 h window. Section 5 states safeguards but does not apply them to today’s pattern: I-05 has the largest effect range and is passed over on value. The five-point calibration trigger does not say whether it is residual error after the SME and Strategic bands already in use. Those bands themselves are applied only as commentary, not in the score.
