8.3

The audit correctly catches the major leakage, keeps the real t0 signals, and separates the impossible close-before-damage pattern from the legitimate but future appeal loop. The remaining defects are real and keep it short of nearly flawless.

Section 1 covers every top-12 feature, all five sequence patterns, and the local-only fields, with the right critical calls: post-close duration, anywhere-appeals, closure team, full-trace counts and variant IDs, the extraction-date prior-claim predicate, the 23:00 document snapshot, mutable `risk_score_current`, and complete-trace `last_resource_role`. Queue length and elapsed time are retained, with the ±5 minute lookahead tightened rather than discarded. Region and calendar week are treated as proxies, not deleted and not called causes. The fraud edge is correctly placed strictly after t0. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is future leakage but process-legal; `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is future, impossible, and a missing sequence-index artifact. Case narratives do not restate leaked outcomes, and X/Y recompute versus Z invalidate is the right split. Section 5 hits feature-store timestamps, prefix recomputation, conformance tests, recalibration, stability, fairness, and drift.

The footprint check is wrong. `#` means the two activities never directly succeed each other in either direction. Damage assessment and claim closure are not in that relation, and a direct-succession footprint cannot express “Claim Closed is terminal” or eventual precedence. The later DECLARE end/precedence rule is the right test, but the footprint paragraph would mis-specify the suite. Alignment and declarative limitations are thin; a conformant full trace can still be pure t0 leakage, and that limit is not stated.

Two remediations are degenerate at this cut. After coverage check, a conformant `num_document_requests_at_t0` is structurally zero, and X, Y, and Z share the same prefix, so `prefix_variant_at_t0` does not discriminate. The `< t0` repair for prior claims still counts the current claim; exclusion is only in the prose. `initial_reserve` and `claimant_channel` are used as credible drivers with no lineage proof, and the €25,000 reserve is called higher exposure without a baseline. Directions for the surviving drivers are taken from SHAP on a model that still contains the leaked features, so those signs are not yet attributions from a valid t0 model. The residual “base and other features” mass is never flagged as possible hidden leakage. Historical `final_handler_team`, experience, and risk score cannot be backfilled from values as of extraction; the plan still talks as if SCD2 history can be created for past rows. The positive global document-count story and the negative request-to-receive pattern are not reconciled.

Those are precision and methodology misses on an otherwise complete, well-targeted audit.
