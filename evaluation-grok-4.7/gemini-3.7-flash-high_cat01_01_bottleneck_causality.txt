6.5

The headline Simpson reversal is real and mostly computed correctly, but the write-up is not reliable enough for an operations decision. It treats a confounded descriptive pattern as a demonstrated Orion speed advantage, mis-specifies mechanisms, and reports several secondary figures that do not match the case data.

The central comparison is right. Orion’s crude mean is about 21.96h versus Nova’s 15.24h (+6.7h). Within complexity the sign flips: about −2.14h (Low), −2.21h (Medium), and −3.43h (High). Direct standardization to the pooled mix (40% / 33.3% / 26.7%) gives roughly 17.14h versus 19.65h (−2.51h). Within-stratum waits and services are also right, and wait, not touch time, is most of the within-stratum gap. Section 4’s “not allowed” cells correctly refuse to read that standardization as an ATE. Overlap failure is stated accurately: one Orion day case, two Nova high cases, four Nova night cases.

That discipline is abandoned in the verdict. `mainly confounded` at 85/100, “demonstrates that Orion is 2.51 hours faster,” and “will backfire” are stronger than the design supports. Stratification on a coarse complexity label does not block unobserved risk, shift, or load. Backfire needs a causal claim that Nova would do worse under Orion’s mix; the same answer says that effect is not identified. “Uncertified staff” overstates the prompt, which only says Orion has more certified reviewers. The −0.13h high-complexity service gap is also too small to attribute to certification, and most of the within-stratum cycle gap is pre-review wait, which certification does not obviously produce.

The graph is not a usable DAG. `review_service → rework → review_service` is a cycle. `quality_outcome → case_completed` is temporally impossible: the 30-day appeal is after close. `complexity → unobserved_case_risk` runs the more natural severity path backwards. Compliance is treated as a mediator of `resource_group`, including a “front-door-like” path. Policy requires compliance mainly by complexity, and the prompt says it is performed whenever required, so group assignment does not cause it. Conditioning on it is confounding control, not blocking a group mechanism.

Secondary arithmetic is not auditable and is sometimes wrong. All 17 night cases have high arrival load, not 15/17, so night and high load are perfectly collinear here. Compliance mean wait is about 16.05h versus 6.54h without, not 16.48 versus 6.21. “~1.4h direct compliance service” and “~10.3h upstream queue” are not in the data; compliance is 100% of high, 50% of medium, and 0% of low, so the +14.8h crude compliance gap is mostly complexity. The same Simpson problem is missed for rework: the crude +9.68h is case mix, while within-stratum rework gaps are roughly 0.3–2h, not “+1.5h non-service and +2.4h service.” Those two numbers neither match the file nor add to 9.68. Overall wait is about 58% of mean cycle time, not 56%, and the recommendation then mislabels that share as compliance queueing. Appeal counts are 1 and 1; calling Orion’s count higher is false. Within non-compliance, Orion’s +2.12h is still mix (more medium cases) and is not flagged.

The hypothesis tests are only partly discriminating. Predictions are mostly one-sided (“significant 2–3.5h,” “>80% of variance,” “>70% of variation”). Hypothesis 5’s “falsification” is the already observed n=1 day case. The selection story uses Nova’s two completed high cases as if they were abandonment rather than routing. Queue interference is ignored, so a case-level pilot is called a “true unconfounded ATE” of a routing policy.

The intervention shape is the right kind—do not move half the book, change compliance/queue capacity, randomize only where there is overlap—but the safeguards are mis-set. Nova medium rework is already 25% (1/4), so a stop at “>25%” fires at baseline. High cases are 6/8 Orion, not “remain 100%.” A zero-tolerance compliance rollback is not an operable pilot rule.

The Python sketch does the required job: embedded CSV, crude and stratified means, stated pooled weights, complexity×shift×group support with n<3 warnings, variant table, and group DFGs via `parse_event_log_string` and `discover_dfg`, without fake timestamps. It does not repeat the bad compliance and rework decompositions. Residual uncertainties are the right five threats; several rows offer two data fields after asking for one.

Format, coverage, and the main reversal are strong. Causal role errors, impossible graph arrows, overclaiming, and incorrect secondary magnitudes keep it well short of a committee-ready analysis.
