6.8

The answer is well organized and reaches the right decision, but it is not nearly flawless. Several load-bearing quantitative claims are statistically or operationally wrong, and the required Site B model does not actually contain a supplement loop and is unlikely to be a valid POWL tree.

**What holds up.** The recommendation `conditional go` is the only defensible choice. The answer refuses the unsegmented copy, keeps originals before document check and the assessment AND-join, treats Site A’s −9 h as confounded, and uses true overlap as a mediator to separate implementation failure from mechanism failure. Sections 1, 3, 4, 9, and 10 are substantively strong: co-interventions are itemized, transport confidences are appropriately low, failure signatures include median-up/tail-down behavior, and the eight assumptions are testable. Format and word-count constraints are met. The transport explanation correctly says a shared control-flow picture is not transportability.

**Model fidelity (largest defect).** In §8 the supplement loop is built as `legal_loop_body` and then never attached to the net that is converted. The converted model is only skip-versus-one-shot `Legal Review`. The same `Transition` objects for `Legal Review`, `Document Check`, and `Assessment` are inserted under more than one POWL parent. PM4Py POWL is a tree; that reuse either raises or silently re-parents nodes out of the concurrent fragment. The comment that the candidate “captures the legal loop” is false. `Operator.LOOP` is never used. Nested `Register → (Identity ∥ Document) → Assessment` does satisfy the AND-join, and the Site A trees match the given strings, so the section is not a total loss — but it misses an explicit requirement and is not a sound self-contained construction. §6’s non-eligible order `(Identity ∥ Originals Receipt) ≺ Document Check` also adds an identity-before-document edge the statute does not impose. Only originals receipt gates document check; identity gates assessment.

**Quantitative scenario analysis.** Overall median and p90 changes are computed as if they were eligible share times the subgroup effect. Medians do not decompose that way. No-originals cases are the fast mass (they avoid the 18 h originals delay), so they sit left of the median and far from the p90. A real digital-only effect should move the overall median by less than the stated −2 to −4 h and should move p90 by about nothing, not −3 to −7 h. The subgroup −6 to −10 h essentially reimports Site A’s confounded cycle-time drop. The structural critical-path saving, if identity (3.4 h) and document (8.1 h) are sequential today, is about 3.4 h of service plus whatever handoff queue exists — not a demonstrated 6–10 h. The optimistic row’s 70–90% eligibility requires “few cases require originals,” which contradicts the given 62% statutory share. The pilot asks for 800–1000 eligible cases per arm in 3–4 months while also stating that six months yield only about 1,300 eligible cases total. Those numbers cannot be true together. Ranges such as p90 +8 to +20 h are not derived from the service times, pool sizes, or DFG counts.

**Causal graph and modifiers.** `API_latency → true_overlap` is the wrong mechanism; latency changes identity service time and cycle time, not whether intervals overlap. `digital_documents` and `originals_required` are drawn as main causes of overlap, but at Site A about 90% digital documents coexisted with 8% overlap. They are treatment-effect modifiers. Parallelization does not increase identity demand: identity may already start at registration, and the pools are specialized, so earlier load hits the document pool. The graph omits the supplement loop as structure. The 40% legal and 62% originals margins are never crossed, even though 0.62 × 3,400 = 2,108 matches `Originals Receipt → Document Check` exactly and 1,360 matches legal review. That DFG evidence is unused. Case-level randomization on four shared specialist pools leaves interference in place; monitoring it is not a design that identifies a case-level effect under congestion.

**Smaller issues.** “No originals required” is treated as “digital documents available at registration.” The 38% figure is only the complement of the originals rule. Huddles and the API upgrade are scored as transport confidence in a way that mixes copyable practices with the parallelization effect. Expansion at a 4–5 h stratum median does not match the 3–4 h pilot target. A logging reform is itself a Site A-style co-intervention and is not isolated.
