7.5

The answer meets the required seven-section structure and is strong on lifecycle scope, path-grounded indicators, multi-criteria trade-offs, rebound, and a segment-differentiated package that keeps true Critical-downtime speed. It is not nearly flawless.

Cancellation accounting is inconsistent: Availability check (410) plus backorder wait (60) is treated as 470 and also as the 530 `Cancel due to delay -> END` cases, with no reconciliation. Those 410 cancellations occur before pick or production, so claiming energy and material were already emitted is unsupported.

Emergency, van, and variant volumes are collapsed into one chain: `Emergency production (760) -> Dedicated van (610)` is called the 8% path. Those are different objects (8% of 12,000 is about 960 cases; dedicated-van arcs are about 610–620). The expected gains from shifting CNC_B to CNC_A plus an inspection gate (about 40–50% energy, 50–60% scrap, 30–40 hours) treat the full gap between two non-comparable variants as the intervention effect. Those variants also differ in batching and heat treatment.

Section 4 stays mostly qualitative, but Sections 3, 6, and 7 still invent precise effects and targets (late +1–2 pp, 70–80% of “unjustified air,” rework 11% to ~7%, scrap 0.90 to ~0.60 kg) that the log does not support. Quality is folded into rework, and S10–S12 are only sketches. The redesign logic itself is coherent and appropriately refuses a lowest-emission-everywhere rule.
