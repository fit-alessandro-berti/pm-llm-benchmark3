7.5

The equivalence analysis is largely right: the observation boundaries are explicit, the loop-scope distinction is correct, neither language contains the other, and the P3 cardinalities 76, 85, and 4 are correct. The answer is not nearly flawless because several reported measurements and required code paths are wrong.

The four counterexamples are valid complete visible traces. A1 and A2 need one check per attempt; B cannot emit a second `Check Revision`. B1 puts the single check after `Approve Revision`, which A’s `->(+(Analyze, Check), X(Approve, tau))` cannot do. B2 ends the first attempt at `Rework Case` with no check. Hiding `tau` does not erase those differences, and optional approval is correctly treated as visible when the approve branch is taken. P1 non-equivalence, P2 equivalence only after the stated projection, and P3 non-equivalence follow. The P2 image really is the same for both models: open first, close last, `n ≥ 1` analyses, a check present, and rework exactly when `n > 1`. The bounded-versus-unbounded caveat is also right.

The P1 relation is softer than the others. “Same complete visible behaviors under that compliance interpretation” never defines whether compliance is a filter, a projection, or full `L_vis` equality. The verdict still happens to be the right one.

Section 7 reports false exact values. Both trees have the same operator multiset: one ternary sequence, one binary sequence, one loop, one binary parallel, one binary choice, six visible leaves, and one `tau`. The standard process-tree conversion therefore yields the same place, transition, and arc counts: 10 transitions and 11 arcs, eight transitions of arc degree 2 and two of degree 3. Mean transition arc degree is 2.2, so `1 / (1 + max(mean − 2, 0))` is `5/6 ≈ 0.8333333333` for both nets. The figures 0.8181818182 and 0.7777777778, and the claim that A produces a larger net, are incorrect. The hand counts for nodes (12/12), depth with root 1 (5/6), leaves (6 visible, 1 silent), and duplicate labels (0) are correct. The simplified tree’s 7 nodes, depth 3, no silent leaf, and simplicity 1.0 are also correct, so it is simpler, but the comparison repeats the wrong original scores.

The bounded search would reproduce 76 and 85 on these nets: `Close Case` reaches the final marking directly, so the length cap does not hide a trailing silent suffix here. The implementation is still not reliable as written. `pm4py.check_soundness` returns a boolean, and indexing `[0]` raises `TypeError` before any comparison. Once the visible length hits 12, silent transitions are discarded even if they are the only way into the final marking. Section 6’s reasoning about footprints and Earth Mover’s Distance is sound, but the snippet is not: `tree_a` is never bound, and `get_stochastic_language` is invoked on a Petri net with playout parameters instead of on a log produced by playout. Equal footprints or a small distance would still not prove equal supports; that part is stated correctly.

The P2 simplification preserves the dashboard projection and correctly records the loss of per-attempt checks. The regression and negative traces match that model. Model A remains the compliance model, and neither original should be replaced by this dashboard tree for P1 or P3.
