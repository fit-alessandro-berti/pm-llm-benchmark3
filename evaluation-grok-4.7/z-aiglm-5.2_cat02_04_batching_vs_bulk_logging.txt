6.5

The cluster ranking is mostly sound and the report keeps logging batches distinct from physical work, but the performance section contains several incorrect quantities and one direct internal contradiction, so it is not close to flawless.

The primary diagnoses fit the evidence. G1 is a four-slot fixture with shared start and end. G2 matches a group-begin control with staggered finishes. G3 matches folder submission after staggered review starts. G4 and the post-gap portion of G8 are exact serial handoffs. G6 is bulk stamping (`start = complete = 17:00` on `manual_bulk_form`). G7 is quarter-hour censoring, including arrivals after the recorded start. G5 is correctly left uncertain because a shared login and interleaved completions support both parallelism and multitasking. Section 7 also separates “the diagnosis is reliable” from “the times are usable,” which is the right adjudication for G6 and G7.

Those strengths are undercut by the calculations in §3. Case-level queue-to-start waits are about 7–19 minutes for G4, not 3–19; about 15–16 minutes for G5, not 3–15; and about 38–58 minutes for G8, not 30–38. The G8 range misses residual queueing after the safety stop: only the first case waits roughly the downtime, and later cases also wait for the serial predecessors. G6 is not “2–3 hours per case”; recorded sojourn runs from about 35 minutes to 2 hours 55 minutes, so “100% SLA violation if the SLA is under 3 hours” is false. G2’s service range is described as “end-to-start,” which reverses the interval. G3 says service is overestimated for later items, while §7 says it is overestimated for early-started items. The later statement is the coherent one if `11:30` is submission rather than finish, but both remain as conclusions.

Second-place explanations for G4 (concurrency) and G6 (one-minute coarsening) are weakly competing: the data essentially rule them out. G7’s censoring model is also unstable: an undocumented up/down quarter-hour rule is treated both as `[14:00, 14:15)` and as ±15 minutes, and non-negative waiting is asserted too strongly. Assumptions are rarely labeled. The code covers the requested PM4Py and pandas checks and does not drop G6 or G7, but it never maps batch categories back to clusters, leaves the granularity field unused, and likely parses `discover_batches` results as the wrong structure.
