The write-up follows the required section structure and often compares real candidate sets with FIFO, priority, SPT, appointment, and skill predictions. That is not enough to carry the diagnoses. Several headline claims are propped up by incomplete eligible sets, selective use of the scan-delay bound, and missed decisions that falsify the preferred rule. Confidence is badly overstated.

## Material errors

**Scan delay is used as a fact, in the helpful direction, at fake precision.** Arrival scans “can” lag by up to two minutes; minute timestamps cannot support 09:48:00 versus 09:48:01 or 09:48:05. If the recorded arrival is the scan, B05 was already visible at 09:46, so the lag makes the priority violation worse. If the column is physical arrival, visibility is only somewhere in [09:46, 09:48], not established at the extreme that saves preemptive priority. The same maximum lag is not applied to other decisions. The recorded second-stop reason, “dispatcher reassignment,” is the direct evidence and is demoted to a side comment. Ranking this explanation “Very High” and building Section 4 Hypothesis A on it is not uncertainty handling.

**Eligible sets drop the job that breaks the preferred rule.**

- QB at 09:48 omits B04 (arrived 09:35, priority 3, `est_min` 8). SPT/SRPT then selects B04, not B03. Section 4’s SRPT alternative (12 < 14) is false once B04 is included.
- QC at 10:32 omits C06 (arrived 10:25, `est_min` 6). Pure SPT selects C06, but C04 was started. The 8 → 14 → 22 story is only a prefix.
- QD at 11:36 lists only D05. D10 (electrical, priority 1, `est_min` 12) fits on idle D-F before the 12:00 meeting. Idle time is not explained by “the only eligible job does not fit.”

**QC is not a 90-confidence SPT-plus-SLA regime.** C05 was already unschedulable at 10:18 (10:18+40 min = 10:58 > 10:50) and was still passed over for C03 and then C04. An emergency override that waits through those jobs is not identified. The unanalyzed 11:40 choice starts C01 (`est_min` 36, priority 3, deadline 13:00) over C07 (`est_min` 18, priority 2, deadline 12:00). That single decision rejects SPT, static priority, and earliest-deadline simultaneously, and C07 could still have finished by about 11:58. Section 7’s “bypassed twice for shorter Priority 3 jobs” is also wrong: the earlier bypass was C03, which is priority 2.

**QB is not identified as preemptive priority at 75.** B02 preempting B01 matches both priority-1 preemption and SRPT (15 versus about 31 minutes left); that equivalence is missed. Resuming B01 at 09:31 over waiting B03 (priority 2), and again at 10:14 over waiting B06 (priority 2), falsifies ordinary preemptive priority. Those resume points fit “resume the interrupted job” better than the headline label. After B01 finally ends, B04 is chosen over higher-priority B06 and due/short B07, which the table correctly scores as FIFO, while the prose wrongly attributes B07’s lateness to priority dominating appointments. The clean priority-over-SPT start of B05 at 10:00 is omitted.

**QA’s primary label is the weaker component, and one “explanation” is timed wrong.** A03 is a real appointment insertion over FIFO and priority. A06 is what non-preemptive FIFO predicts if there is no lookahead; calling it the strongest contradiction of “appointment with FIFO fallback” depends on an unstated reservation rule. Priority is repeatedly falsified (A02 over A04, A05 over A08, A07 over A08), so the stated FIFO/priority equivalence problem is the wrong main ambiguity. Calibration is 08:52–08:58, after A02’s 08:32–08:52 service; it cannot explain the 08:32 start.

**QD skills are real; “zero structural ambiguity” and confidence 92 are not.** D05 is D-F-only and D07 is D-X-only, so a skill filter is required. Order inside the filter is not identified: D04 over D08 is one clean FIFO-over-priority/SPT case; D03 over D05 ties FIFO and SPT; D07 over D10 ties FIFO with least-flexible-first. D06 at exactly 11:30, with D-X idle from 11:20 despite D08 (priority 1, 8 minutes) fitting before the appointment, is the clearest appointment evidence and is not in the decision table. Starting D03 at 11:21 also consumes the only pre-meeting window in which D05 (30 minutes) could have finished before 12:00. “Correctly blocked until 12:20” describes the gap, not shown lookahead.

The Python does not verify the claims above it. The QA script only builds a frame; the QC assert only checks that C02 is shortest at 10:10.

Under a strict reading this is a complete but unreliable diagnosis, not a near-miss on a strong one.
