9.1

The three candidates are internally consistent, the trace arithmetic checks out, and the uncertainty handling is unusually careful. A few local inaccuracies keep it short of flawless.

The ledger covers every variant, D1–D7, and S1–S7. Both DFG directions are correctly treated as non-proof of concurrency: an XOR of `C·E` and `E·C` yields the same pair of edges, and the walk `R C Q A P Z` is a valid DFG path that still omits Engineering Review and breaks the expedite rule. Observed counts, the 175 directly-follows instances, and the 205 events are consistent with the 30 cases. `C` precedes every recorded `S` (23/23); that is kept as a hypothesis.

Language claims are right. The visible languages are strictly nested, with sizes 7, 12, and 18. Balanced splits as 2 / 3 / 2 / 5 across the four S/Q presence combinations; permissive splits as 2 / 6 / 2 / 8. The five balanced novelties and the six further permissive traces (those with `S` before `C`) are exactly the unobserved residues. All 20 suite rows, hence all 60 model/trace expectations, match those languages. The balanced partial order is the N-poset `C≺S`, `C≺Q`, `E≺Q`, which a series-parallel tree cannot express exactly; using POWL there and process trees for the other two is the right split. Transitive edges match PM4Py’s partial-order validation requirement. Optionality is `X(a,τ)`, not a probability and not independence. The seven absent supplier events are not treated as a 23% loss rate; the 5-skip / 2-loss sketch is labeled illustrative and does equal an 8% loss inside the stated 5–10% band.

The recommendation is calibrated: balanced for descriptive monitoring, permissive for sensitivity, conservative as a catalogue, with `C≺S` deviations graded as unresolved. The overlap test `max(start) < min(complete)`, the point that more `CE`/`EC` orders cannot separate `C∥E` from `X(CE, EC)`, and the warning that non-overlap does not forbid concurrent enabling are all correct.

Defects:

- The D5 ledger cell says the balanced Credit-completion gate is the modeling consequence of D5. That gate is the 23/23 completion pattern. D5 cuts the other way: supplier work may follow information from either check, including an intermediate milestone, so it does not support a universal `C≺S` rule. Section 4 states the narrower reading correctly, and the ledger therefore disagrees with the body.
- Permissive officially accepts supplier completion before either principal completion (`R S C E A P Z`, `R S E C Q A P Z`, and two relatives). D5 still requires enough information from a check. The text marks these traces risky and existential, but they remain inside the permissive language rather than outside the stated “reasonable” boundary.
- The validation script was not executed. Its oracle, bounded firing explorer, and refusal to invent metric values are sound design. The surrounding prose still asserts two unverified API facts: that soundness returns a `(bool, diagnostics)` tuple, and that `add_only_if_fm_is_reached` drops incomplete playout. The `check_is_sound` fallback is not the documented entry point. Unknown playout keys are often ignored, so that assertion can fail open, and the metric section is unestablished.
- The sandbox link is not a self-contained artifact; only the inlined script is.

No language-size, suite, fitness-by-construction, or identifiability claim above failed an independent recomputation. The deductions are for the D5 mismatch, the soft permissive boundary, and unverified API assertions.
