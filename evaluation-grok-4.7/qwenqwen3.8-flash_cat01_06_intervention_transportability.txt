The advisory core is strong and mostly well structured: Site A’s nine-hour median drop is treated as confounded, Document Check is not started before legally required originals, the intervention is segmented, a median-up/tail-down bottleneck case is included, and the pilot separates intention-to-treat from true-overlap adherence. Required headings, tables, scenario count, and the conditional-go recommendation are present and within the stated length bounds.

Those strengths do not make the answer nearly flawless.

The assumption register systematically mishandles “consequence if false.” For the claims about the nine-hour effect, originals, role specialization, case locks, supplement loops, and segment-specific effects, the stated consequence is what follows if the assumption is true and ignored, not what follows if it is false. That is a repeated logical reversal in a required field.

The transport graph has causal errors. Site B is wired to the same “API latency improved” selection node as Site A, although only Site A had that co-intervention. `appeal -> cycle_time` runs backward: a 90-day appeal is downstream of completion. `digital_documents -> originals_required` is unsigned and reads as the wrong dependence.

Model fidelity is weak. The POWL code guesses `Activity`, `ExclusiveChoice`, and `Loop`, then tries several constructor signatures. Under the usual `StrictPartialOrder(nodes)` plus `add_edge` API, `StrictPartialOrder(order)` can succeed while silently dropping edges. If `Loop` is missing, the fallback strict partial order contains `supp → … → legal_rep → supp`, which is a cycle, and it orders both Approve and Reject after Legal Review instead of an exclusive choice. The crash fallback wraps only the top-level partial order, so earlier constructor failures never reach a Petri net. Power is stated as 90% without a variance, “no originals” is treated as “digital,” the DFG counts are barely used, and the section 6 supplement cap of 1.2 per legal case conflicts with the pilot’s 3-point stop rule and with Site A’s six-task WIP rule that section 9 says not to copy.

6.7 reflects a usable transport recommendation with material errors in causal direction, assumption logic, and the executable process model.
