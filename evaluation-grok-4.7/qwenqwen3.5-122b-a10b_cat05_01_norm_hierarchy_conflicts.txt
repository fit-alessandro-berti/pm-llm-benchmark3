The final conclusion classifies every case as NONCOMPLIANT and never delivers the required assessment. That single label violates the instruction not to treat all deviations as noncompliant, and it is not supported by the case facts.

The required product is missing. There is no interpretive method, no issue-specific hierarchy, no rule catalogue or defeasible logic, no case table with severity, confidence, controlling interpretation, satisfied rules, deviations, failed exceptions, decisive evidence, and missing evidence, no alternative interpretations, no remediation split among breach, logging defect, late proof, and unprovable compliance, and no replacement clause. “ANT” is not a conclusion.

The repeated conclusion is therefore both formally incomplete and analytically false as a uniform result.
