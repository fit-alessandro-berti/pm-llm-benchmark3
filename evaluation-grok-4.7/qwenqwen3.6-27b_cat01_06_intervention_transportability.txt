**5.5**

The recommendation is the right one, and the write-up does recognize confounding, statutory gates, downstream congestion, and a segmented pilot. It is not close to the required standard of transportability and model fidelity.

The quantitative core is under-derived. Identity and document service at Site B are about 3.4 h and 8.1 h, so true overlap can remove roughly the shorter task from the critical path, not 7–9 h. Originals delay (median 18 h on 62% of cases) already dominates waiting, and rules 2–3 already allow identity to run during that wait. The directly-follows counts support this split (about 2108 originals→document versus 1160 identity→document) and were never used. Because the median case sits in the originals majority, a digital-only change on 38% of volume is unlikely to move the population median by 4–6 h. The optimistic band essentially reimports Site A’s confounded −9 h. Section 9 then pairs a 4–6 h cycle-time cut with only a 10–15% waiting cut (about 2–3 h on a 20 h baseline), which cannot add up. Expansion is allowed when p90 worsens by less than 5 h and does not require a cycle-time gain.

The pilot is infeasible. About 3,400 cases in six months is roughly 1,000 cases in eight weeks, not 1,200 per arm, and a third arm makes the gap worse. Appeal outcomes need a 90-day window the design never adds. Case-level randomization on pools of 4, 7, 3, and 2 people violates independence; “queue isolation” is named, not designed. The power statement (“80% power at =3 h”) is incomplete.

The partial-order material is formally wrong. Section 6’s order edges put Approve, Reject, and Legal Review in parallel after Assessment, omit Notify, and encode the supplement loop as a cycle, which a strict partial order cannot contain. The code does not use PM4Py’s real POWL constructors (`StrictPartialOrder`, `OperatorPOWL`, `Transition`); `PowlModel` / `PowlRelation` will not run, the same cycle and AND-split are hardcoded, and the net would not be the optional legal branch requested.

The transport graph includes every required node, but `appeal → cycle_time` reverses a post-decision quality outcome, `API_latency → identity_capacity` misstates a latency effect, and originals versus digital documents are given the same unsigned edge into overlap. The Section 2 explanation is about 150 words against a 180-word minimum; Section 9 is about 170 against a 250-word minimum. Site B is also described as sequential even though identity and originals receipt may already proceed together. Assumption 6 treats role separation as evidence that cross-training is unnecessary, which does not follow.
