4.0

The final write-up keeps the required seven-section structure, reads the missing fields correctly for the flagged cases, and gets the feasible windows for P05, P07, and P11 right. That is not enough to carry the analysis. The quantitative reconstruction is unreliable, several required checks are only asserted, and the code does not implement the claims in the prose.

**Case table.** Fully observed early cases are mostly right, but later arithmetic is not. S1 wait is wrong for P11 (75 min, reported as 85), P16 (100 vs 90), P19 (100 vs 80), P20 (105 vs 75), P21 (100 vs 40), and P22 (110 elapsed vs 50). P11 S1 service is 25 min, not 30. Elapsed S2 queue time at 14:00 is wrong for P16 (85 vs 35), P18 (50 vs 30), and P19 (35 vs 25). P15’s own note says I1 is free only after 11:40, so the S1 wait lower bound is 90 min, not 75; the printed range [75, 125] conflicts with that resource constraint and would start P15 while P13 is still on I1. Zero-length service is left as a live bound with no plausibility discussion. Completed-case sojourn is defined and then never reported, even though it is fixed for P05, P07, and P11 once the known endpoints are used.

**Station table.** Interval counts do not match the timestamps. External arrivals are about 14 / 7 / 1 across 08:00–10:00, 10:00–12:00, and 12:00–14:00, not 10 / 6 / 6. S1 completions are 20, not 7+6+4=17. S2 completions are 14, not 11. Subperiod utilization divides slice busy-time by the full 360-minute horizon (and once by C2’s 345-minute day), so 185/360 = 51.4% is not a two-hour, two-resource utilization. Afternoon S2 busy time of 85 minutes is far below the work still visible on C1 and C2. Mean/peak WIP is stated without a trajectory, so the simultaneous-event convention is never applied. The displayed precision is not earned.

**Bottleneck, Little’s Law, sensitivity.** S2’s fully observed services really are longer than S1’s (about 40 vs about 28 minutes), so a lower S2 capacity is defensible. The temporal story is not. S1 waits stay near 80–105 minutes into the early afternoon; S1 does not “catch up” until arrivals stop after 12:10. “1.0 case per 15 minutes per resource” misstates a ~30-minute service. C2’s outage removes one of two resources, not 25% of station capacity. Little’s Law is promised for 10:00–12:00 and then replaced by an unexplained residual of 0.8–1.4 cases. The optimistic/central/pessimistic utilization band (48.1 / 55.6 / 62.3%) is not computed from the actual missing starts; those starts barely move S2 busy time inside 10:00–12:00. The claim that sojourn for P05, P07, P11, and P15 shifts by 10–20 minutes is wrong: missing starts only reallocate wait and service between known endpoints, while elapsed sojourn stays fixed. Rounding is named and then not propagated; the 11:00 C1 handoff from P07 to P09, which rounding can turn into an overlap, is never examined.

**Code.** The table is transcribed correctly, but `parse_time` maps MISSING, OPEN, and blank to the same `NaT`. S2 “wait” is `s2_start - arrival_s1`, so it mixes S1 sojourn into S2 queue time. Blank waiting cases pass the supposedly valid case filter. There is no WIP series and no imputation scenario. `format_dataframe` is called with an invented variant, and the required PM4Py helpers are imported from modules that do not match the requested `pm4py.insert_case_*` entry points; both paths are swallowed by `try/except`. The script therefore cannot reproduce sections 3–6. The comment declining `convert_log_to_time_intervals` is the one code requirement that is actually satisfied.
