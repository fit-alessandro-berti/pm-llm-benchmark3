7.5

The final answer meets the required structure, covers every mandated dimension and mitigation, and reaches a calibrated conclusion that residual procedural burden remains after case-mix adjustment while raw approval gaps are not treated as proof of discrimination. Rights-preserving steps are kept, and risk scores are not treated as neutral.

It is not nearly flawless. Group C’s accommodation path is compared with “2–8%” for the other groups, but those shares are 2%, 3%, and 3%. The adjusted approval gaps are written without their negative direction. The matching strategy uses excess use of the language-support path as evidence of unexplained disparity without conditioning on language need, which would mislabel legitimate routing; later sections partly repair that distinction. The PM4Py sketch would not implement the stated plan: variant filtering is given joined strings rather than activity sequences, case-performance uses `min_duration` instead of `min_performance`, path filtering omits the required path and both performance filters treat day-scale cutoffs as if they were the library’s second-scale durations, and service/waiting-time insertion is applied to an event log rather than a dataframe. “Fraud review has more staff” and a neighborhood-trained risk model are also unsupported.
