4.5

The write-up is complete and often conceptually alert, but the implemented models contradict the verdicts, the chosen R5 policy, and PM4Py’s actual constraint semantics. Those are core failures, not polish issues.

The decisive break is `precedence(Obtain Legal Opinion, Activate Vendor)`. The text itself defines it as unconditional: every activation requires a prior opinion, including cases with no sanctions escalation. That rejects T1, T2, T4, T5, and T8. Section 8 still marks T1, T2, and T4 DECLARE `pass`, and calls T4’s control-flow layers clean. Resolution A is therefore neither what was specified (“conditioned on escalation”) nor what was scored. Confidence `0.85` does not soften `conformance_declare`.

The process tree does not support the procedural column. `clo` is one node under two parents, so the object is not a tree. `Sanctions Escalation` and `Obtain Legal Opinion` are absent, but that omission is not treated as a conformance deviation, so T6 and T7 are marked `pass`. The clarification loop is `LOOP(τ, Request→Receive)` after `Classify Risk`, while the compact formula instead loops on `Classify Risk`. T2 and T5 request clarification before classification; T5 also has no reply. Both are outside the language, yet the matrix uses `pass*`, which is not an allowed cell. Section 3 even admits a procedural fail and Section 8 walks it back.

Log Skeleton would not yield those passes under PM4Py. `directly_follows` is a permitted-succession whitelist; the partial set omits most successions that T1–T8 actually contain, so every trace deviates. Pair orientation is also off: PM4Py reads `always_before (A,B)` as “B occurs before A,” so pairs such as `(Receive, Capture)` are reversed. `always_after (Activate, Capture)` is likewise the wrong way around. `activ_freq` allows `Receive Clarification` to be 0 while `Request Clarification` is 1, so the skeleton cannot see T5.

Other concrete defects: Section 2 writes `precedence(Receive, Request)` while Section 4 encodes the opposite pair; noncoexistence is called weaker than nonsuccession, which reverses the templates the task asked to distinguish; R2 is declared authoritative in an external counter that Section 7 never implements; missing resources are skipped and therefore look like passes; the emergency check drops escalation cases that never obtain an opinion; Section 9 claims the skeleton allows a second `Release First Payment` when `activ_freq` is `{0,1}`. The R3 vacuity remark is wrong: `precedence(Receive, Activate)` fails when activation occurs without a clarification, rather than holding vacuously.

What holds up is the R5/R6 split, the temporal arithmetic (`zeta = 2` gives 0–96h, 0–24h, and 0–144h), T7 as a warning, T3’s rejection path, T4’s four-eyes failure, T5’s missing reply, T8’s mutual exclusion, and the warning that support and confidence are not normative truth. That discussion cannot repair models whose own happy paths fail.
