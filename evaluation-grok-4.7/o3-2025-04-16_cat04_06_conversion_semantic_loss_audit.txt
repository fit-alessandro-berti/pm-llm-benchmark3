4.5

The document has the right nine-part shape and a defensible primary pipeline, but it is not a reliable audit. Several required invariants are missing or mis-stated, preservation is marked “validated” from code that cannot run, and the round-trip checker does not implement the tests it claims to perform.

The baseline omits the central partial-order fact: optional legal review and the verification loop have no edge and may overlap. It also never states that every iteration is Configure Trial before Inspect Trial, or that Correct Defect cannot be the exit of the loop. INV-2’s negative witness (a duplicate Assess Risk) does not test concurrency. INV-4 marks the skip invariant as unobservable after hiding tau; traces that finish with no Legal Review make that option observable. Shortened labels and prefix “witnesses” leave the join and the do-then-redo loop under-specified. A converter that forced legal review to finish before verification, or that allowed zero iterations, could pass this table.

P1 versus P2 is the right split, and expecting the process tree to fail on this non-block partial order is right. The failure reason (“mixed parallel/XOR”) is loose: Assess Risk precedes both the XOR and the loop, Prepare Evidence precedes only the loop, and both join at Release. That is a non-series-parallel dependency, not merely a nested gateway mix. Section 4 then treats that failure as something that already happened.

The conversion script imports `source_powl` from a module that does not exist, checks only the net for `None`, and never checks initial or final markings. `json.dump` of footprints will fail because those relations are sets. `diagnose` prints soundness and does not record or enforce it. Allowed audit terms are not used: cells say “preserved”, “n/a”, and “conversion failed / ambiguous”. Concurrency is called “validated” because “footprints show ordering”, which is the opposite of the parallel footprint. Layout is scored as semantic approximation. A back-edge is treated as proof of at least one iteration; a while-loop also has a back-edge and allows zero. Activity-label uniqueness, true concurrency versus interleaving, binary-loop exit, and the missing legal/verification edge are not actually audited.

Section 5 is pseudo-code. The reachability graph is built and ignored. `fm` is omitted. The firing line subtracts arcs from a marking, discards the result, and never recurses. Transitions with label `None` are not hidden, silent cycles are not cut off, and exact final-marking completion is not implemented. Positive and negative witnesses are not replayed, footprints are not compared, and playout is absent. The bound of 12 is not shown to cover two full iterations plus legal/evidence interleavings, and the text calls the check sufficient to catch early release and lost optionality.

The visual section correctly warns that layout, a hidden skip, and a loop arrow can be misread. It asserts, without evidence, that a parallel gateway and an undrawn silent branch are what PM4Py emits. Rejection criteria cover unsoundness and early release, but they inherit the missing overlap invariant and do not require a failed exact-final-marking test or collapsed optionality as separate gates. A visual defect is also used to quarantine the model itself.

Making the converted Petri net the authority, while also allowing edits on the POWL source, creates two sources of truth. The POWL model is the authored semantics; the net, BPMN, tree, and pictures are derivatives. A process tree should stay disallowed for this model unless equivalence is shown, including when conversion returns a tree instead of throwing. Residual items 1–3 are real, but passing every stated test would still not establish pomset equivalence, silent choice timing, or that bounded language equality survives another iteration.
