3.5

The write-up follows the required 10-part outline and correctly notes that a bidirectional directly-follows graph does not prove concurrency, but the three models — the actual deliverable — are invalid, mutually inconsistent, and do not do what the prose claims.

The conservative tree is not legal PM4Py (`->` is missing; bare comma-lists are not a process tree). As written it sequences `Approve Configuration` twice, so it accepts unobserved double-approval traces and rejects the log. Its bias also reverses the brief: it drops observed `Engineering Review`-before-`Credit Check` behavior in favor of S1, which the data contradict, and it makes `Supplier Confirmation` mandatory despite 7 observed skips. Duplicate-label XOR enumeration and its scalability are never discussed. The “excludes 16/30” count omits the `Engineering Review, Credit Check, Expedite Review, Supplier Confirmation` case, and the claim that expedite was “only observed before” supplier confirmation is backwards: the log has one order each.

The balanced object is not POWL. `OperatorPOWL.SEQUENCE / PARALLEL / OPTIONAL`, `Transition(..., silent=False)`, and `StrictPartialOrder.add_edge` are not the API (`order.add_edge` on a node set is). Edges from both checks into `Supplier Confirmation` forbid the observed `Credit Check, Supplier Confirmation, Engineering Review` variant, while the text claims the model fits all 30 cases and that supplier confirmation may depend on either check.

The permissive string is also unparsable (`+` is parallel in PM4Py, not OR; `×` and `?` are not operators). Interpreted charitably, supplier confirmation and expedite review are fixed after both checks, so it rejects the interleaving and the expedite-before-supplier trace that were observed, contradicting both D5/S6 and the “fits all 30” cell. The three languages are not a conservative–balanced–permissive ladder.

The audit treats observed variants (the 4× no-supplier trace, the 2× mid-supplier trace, and the 3× reverse-check trace) as unobserved, and one “trace” still contains a `×(...)` operator. Validation code does not reconstruct the 30-case log (it leaves “repeat Nx” placeholders), never soundly converts the balanced model, clobbers `im`/`fm`, skips `precision_alignments` and `simplicity_petri_net`, imports a misspelled heuristics module, uses `pandas` without importing it, and treats an unobserved interleaving as a positive test. Sampled playout is correctly described as non-exhaustive, but the script cannot be run.

The matrix, the choice of the balanced model for immediate use, and two of the four “future” distinguishing traces inherit those false fitness claims; expedite-after-supplier and approval-without-supplier are already in the log. The evidence ledger and the uncertainty table are the only parts that mostly track the brief, and even there D7’s “low reliability” confuses a solid logging caveat with a weak rule, and S1 is rated far too high.
