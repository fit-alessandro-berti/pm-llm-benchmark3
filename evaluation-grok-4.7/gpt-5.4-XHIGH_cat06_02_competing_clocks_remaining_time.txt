6.5

The forecast gets the main structural facts right: chemistry cannot finish on Friday, the join is a maximum, incubation is continuous and lands on Sunday, Saturday QA cannot release this batch, and the temperature excursion is a common-cause dependence rather than an independent multiplier. Hour-to-clock conversions check out, and the scenario set covers the required branches. Those strengths are real. The numerical forecast built on top of them is not tight enough, and several clocks are shifted late in a way that is not supported by the same answer’s own stage times.

The largest logic error is Scenario E. The 20–40% band is the probability that the excursion flag is confirmed, but the row’s central window (Wednesday–Friday) and tail (28–29 September) are the clocks of coupled rerun, confirmation, and investigation. Confirmation only raises those probabilities; it does not make double rework the typical outcome. Assigning the marginal flag probability to the double-trouble timeline overstates the upper half of the distribution and helps explain why the summary median sits later than the smooth path.

Scenario C has a concrete calendar error. A Monday 08:30–09:30 primary readout plus 20–30 h and a 0.5–1.5 h second readout finishes on Tuesday between about 08:30 and 17:00, inside microbiology hours. The claimed Wednesday-morning spill does not follow, so the scenario’s central window (Tuesday noon–Wednesday noon) and Thursday tail are late for the confirmation branch itself. The 20–40% probability is also a poor match to the stated 0.25–0.45 authorization chance: no weekend readout is the majority case (about 55–75%). Overlap with the smooth path is noted, but the band is still attached to the wrong event rate.

Scenario D’s earliest terminal time, Tuesday 10:00–15:00, misses feasible Monday releases. A 4 h documentation query, or an 8 h documentation-only investigation, opened after an early Monday join can still be finished before QA closes at 20:00, with release the same evening. The second QA pass after a query returns is also left implicit.

Section 4 is not reconciled with Section 2. The modal scenario (about 35–55%) is centered on Monday 15:00–21:00, while the unconditional P50-like band is Monday 19:30–Tuesday 10:30. Right skew can move the median toward the edge of the modal scenario; it does not, without a mixture, justify centering it on Tuesday. The P80 and severe-tail bands are so wide that they cover both a reasonable quantile and a substantially later one. The far tail also stops on Tuesday 29 September, short of a 7-calendar-day laboratory investigation opened midweek.

Chemistry FIFO, which is the majority discipline, is asserted rather than calculated. The ledger admits “rough sample-scale assumptions” for the four waiting samples and never states them. With two analysts and only about 2–3.5 analyst-hours of Friday capacity against four unserved samples, a Monday 09:00–15:00 chemistry band is plausible only for moderate service times; the arithmetic that would show that is absent. Weekend readout is then ranked above chemistry queue position even though, on the answer’s own join rule, Monday 08:30–09:30 microbiology loses to FIFO chemistry. QA service is capped near the stated 80th percentile, so the one-in-five longer reviews that decide Monday-versus-Tuesday spillover are thin.

The earliest Monday 09:30 release is only the conjunction of minimum residual chemistry, minimum readout, an empty QA queue, and a sub-median review. That is labeled as a plausible earliest, which is acceptable, but it is an extreme conjunction rather than a robust lower band.
