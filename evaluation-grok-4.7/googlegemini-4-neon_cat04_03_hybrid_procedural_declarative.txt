**8.3**

The hybrid design is strong: R5/R6 is treated as a real conflict, Resolution A is implemented without silently encoding R6, the accepting net replays T1–T8 as claimed, and the DECLARE split among `response`, `precedence`, `altresponse`/`altprecedence`, `succession`, `noncoexistence`, and `nonsuccession` is the right one. Temporal arithmetic is correct, including T7’s payment interval at \(z \approx 1.94\), which stays inside \(\zeta = 2\).

The log skeleton is not executable as written. PM4Py treats `directly_follows` as a whitelist of permitted successor pairs. An empty set rejects every consecutive pair, so T1–T4, T6, and T7 do not pass `conformance_log_skeleton`. The matrix still marks those cells `pass`. Empty is defensible only as “no adjacency constraint,” and that exception is never stated. The same gap applies to `activ_freq`: `Verify Bank Account` and the loop activities are omitted. That matches a partial constraint set, but it is not stated, and a closed alphabet would fail every provided trace.

Two smaller modeling problems sit under that. The escalation place is 1-bounded, so a second `Sanctions Escalation` cannot start before `Obtain Legal Opinion`. R4 is only eventual response; one later opinion can cover several escalations. DECLARE uses `response`, while the net and `equivalence` enforce alternation and equal counts. Section 9 never identifies that disagreement, although it does so for clarification. The sentence that `always_before (A, B)` is “the exact reverse” of DECLARE `precedence(B, A)` is false: those are the same constraint with reversed tuple order. The R7 example is encoded correctly; the gloss is not.

Elsewhere the over-constraints are mostly disclosed (opinion before rejection, no suspended close without payment, post-activation-only bank changes). Calling the terminal structure “acyclic” is still wrong: clarification, escalation, and the R11 loop are cycles. `not evaluated` for T3/T8 is a reasonable vacuity label, but the tool result is an empty deviation list, i.e. a vacuous pass, and T5 is already scored `pass` under that same rule.

Resource checks are the right layer and correctly fail closed on missing `org:resource`. T4, T6, and T7 are judged correctly there. Overall verdicts for the procedural, DECLARE, temporal, and resource columns hold under the stated policy. The log-skeleton column does not hold as a PM4Py model.
