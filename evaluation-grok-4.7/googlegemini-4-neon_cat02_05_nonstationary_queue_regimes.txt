The write-up is strong: regimes are explicit, most arithmetic checks out, outage recovery is not treated as normal capacity, and Little’s Law is refused where boundaries or conservation fail. It is not nearly flawless.

The flow audit is the best part. Totals 2,519 arrivals and 2,409 completions, cumulative net +110, +88 through 2026-04-24 and +128 through 2026-04-28, and the WIP path (bounded 12–52, weekend drops despite positive net) are correct. The peak-column mismatch is also real: 08:00–09:59 is about 16.8%, 18.1%, 9.6%, and 11.8% on the four hourly days, not the tabulated 31/32/47/22. Hourly Fano factors, CVs, and peak-to-mean ratios match the vectors (for example 3.23, 10.12, 4.14, 1.29). Regime means, the empty weekday/weekend gap, and both Simpson contrasts (FTE vs WIP; complexity vs completions) are numerically right. Marking even the balanced 2026-04-06–04-10 window as approximate only is justified.

The main quantitative overclaim is traffic intensity. For post-mix standard-staff weekdays the median floor is about 0.93, so ρ>1 is not established. The lognormal means (~44 minutes, ρ≈1.17) are also hard to reconcile with staff-minutes: 100.6 completions at ~5.93 FTE cannot have a mean handling time above ~42 minutes if those minutes are single-server active time. The unexplained lower edges (1.07, 0.84, 1.10, 0.88) are not derived. Chronic net +13.4/day and rising WIP still show imbalance; they do not by themselves prove ρ>1, especially with the reported arrival bursts. “Guaranteeing” that deficit overstates the result.

Section 4 then mixes signs. Outage net flow is +47 under the paper’s own definition, but the pooling bullet calls it an acute −47 and says that term “cancels” against R5’s +94, R4’s −30, and R7’s −18. Those pieces sum to about +93, not to the mild +3.9/day. The average looks small because +110 is spread over 28 days.

Smaller faults: 25→35 minutes is +40%, not +38%, and 35 / 83 / 46% are regime centers, not 2026-04-20 (34, 78, 42). The 129 completions on 2026-04-16 are not “only” the extra FTE; handling time was shorter than in the overloaded regime and WIP was already high. “7.0 FTE Mon–Thu” extends a Wed–Thu observation across unobserved Mondays and Tuesdays. Abandonment is called the likely cause of the +110 gap after the paper itself gives equally open alternatives (WIP scope, off-hours exits). The one-FTE-per-active-minute assumption is never stated.

Format, segmentation, uncertainty labels, and the PM4Py caveat are otherwise in good shape. The grade is held down by the ρ inconsistency and the sign/cancellation error, not by missing structure.
