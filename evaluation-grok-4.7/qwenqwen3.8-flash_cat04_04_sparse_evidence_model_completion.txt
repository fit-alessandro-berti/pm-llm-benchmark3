The three candidates are genuinely different, internally consistent hypotheses, and the completion-order reasoning is largely right: the conservative language is exactly the seven observed variants, the balanced POWL linearizations cover those variants while excluding `E,S,C`, and the permissive tree’s 14 prefixes match the stated D3–D6 boundary. Evidence handling, the concurrency caveat, missing-versus-optional `S`, and the recommendation are strong.

Points come off for concrete defects. `fitness_alignments` returns a dict in PM4Py, but the validation code casts it with `float()`, so the required fitness check fails on the normal API. The positive/negative suite is accepted or rejected by all three models alike, so it never separates them. Overfitting is filed under “underfitting risks,” and S1 (credit before engineering starts) is overused as support for `C → S`.
