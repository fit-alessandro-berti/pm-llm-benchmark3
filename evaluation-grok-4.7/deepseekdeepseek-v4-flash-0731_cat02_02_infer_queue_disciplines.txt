7.4

The final diagnosis is directionally right and well structured: QA is FIFO with a narrow appointment overlay, QB is FIFO/resume plus a narrow priority-1 override, QC is not a stable single rule, and QD is skill-constrained and largely FIFO within resources. Decision sets, not raw start order, drive the argument; priority, SPT, EDF, and appointments are separated where they coincide; confidence is mostly calibrated; and the required seven-part format is met.

The score is held down by several concrete gaps.

The QC reconstruction skips its two sharpest decision epochs. At 10:18, C05 (priority 1, deadline 10:50, estimate 40) was already waiting, yet C03 was chosen — SPT among the feasible set, and a direct rejection of both priority and earliest-deadline. At 11:34, C06 was chosen while C07 (priority 2, earlier deadline, estimate 18) was waiting — again SPT only. Those rows are absent, so the write-up uses C06’s delay as anti-SPT evidence without using C06’s eventual selection as anti-priority and anti-EDF evidence.

The section 4 SPT test overreaches. C06’s ready time discriminates the 10:32 choice of C04, but it does not rescue SPT for the queue. Even if C06 was ineligible, C07 (estimate 18, arrived 10:40) was eligible at 10:54 and is shorter than C05 (40), so SPT still predicts C07, not the observed C05. The same independent failure recurs at 11:40, where SPT predicts C07 and the outcome is C01. Treating C06’s eligibility as what “rescues the SPT interpretation” is too strong.

On QB, the decision table is internally better than the prose. At 10:00 the table correctly says FIFO/resume would take suspended B01, while the observed job is B05. The diagnosis and supporting/contradicting sections instead say only that B05 overtakes B04. Under the answer’s own seniority rule, B05 also jumps the interrupted job, which is the sharper violation of “resume the interrupted job.” The 09:48 insertion of B03 remains correctly treated as an exception.

Supplied constraints are not fully used. A1’s calibration from 08:52–08:58 exactly explains the gap between A02 and A04 and is never cited. On QD, the 11:20–11:30 D-X idle window is discussed for D03, but not for D08 (priority 1, mechanical, estimate 8), which D-X could have finished before D06’s 11:30 appointment. Assumptions that suspended jobs keep arrival seniority and that remaining time equals estimate minus elapsed service are used throughout QB without being labeled.

QA’s later FIFO chain, QB’s post-10:28 arrival order, the B07 missed appointment, D05/D07 skill locks, and the refusal to promote every overtake into priority scheduling are all sound. The cross-queue conclusion — QA most identifiable, QC least, SPT-for-QC the most dangerous single-policy claim — follows from the evidence.
