What is present is mostly careful. Stage means were recomputed from the timestamps and match the table, including conditional handling of absent stages, flag splits, complexity splits, load splits, and escalation splits. Shared queues, exposure-induced confounding, and treatment–confounder feedback are all named with case-level evidence.

The main analytical error is the M2 “single-server bottleneck.” M2’s manual intervals overlap (R01/R03/R09/R10 and R13/R18/R20), so exclusive single-server queueing and the R09-over-R03 FIFO claim are not identified from these timestamps. “High-complexity cases … (100% escalated)” is also too strong: R17 is high complexity, reviewed by M1, and not escalated.
