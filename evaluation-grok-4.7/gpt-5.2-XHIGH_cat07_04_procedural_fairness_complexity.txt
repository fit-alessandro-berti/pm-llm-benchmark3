7.8

The assessment is substantively strong and constraint-compliant: raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination, case-mix is not used to dismiss residuals, risk scores are treated as suspect, and rights-preserving steps are analyzed as burden rather than proposed for removal. Dimensions, adjustment strategy, mechanisms, limitations, mitigations with trade-offs, and the calibrated bottom line are all present and mostly well supported by the given figures.

It is not nearly flawless. The clearest reasoning error is in the Group B routing discussion: the +8.0 pp and +9.5 pp figures are already the adjusted residuals, so the claim that they “should shrink more” if complexity explained them is incoherent. Unadjusted routing rates are not provided, and because documentation completeness is already in the control set, the residual document-request gap is more anomalous than the write-up makes clear. Variant shares are also treated a bit loosely as evidence of step use (V4 at 24% versus 57% language need; V5 at 31% versus 62% accommodation requests) without stating that a single variant is not the occurrence rate of the activity. “Concentrated in overloaded regions” and “batching” go beyond the stated evidence.

The PM4Py section is only a sketch and would not run as written. `discover_performance_dfg` returns a triple, not a mapping, so `.get` on edges is wrong; `filter_case_performance` does not take `min_dur`/`max_dur`; dataframe `get_variants` is typically variant-to-count, so `len(v)` is unsafe; `get_service_time` is invoked without a start timestamp despite the narrative’s consecutive-event approximation. `filter_variants` and `filter_paths_performance` are never used, so slow-path composition is not operationalized. Those defects are material in a required methods section, even framed as pseudocode.
