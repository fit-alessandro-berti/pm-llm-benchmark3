Deductions are for softness in the labels and a few over-grouped claims, not for broken timelines.

The prose still calls the discipline “FIFO-biased” and describes the post-B05 tail as the backbone, while the supporting episode mixes an anti-FIFO preemption with a later resume. These are precision and calibration problems on an otherwise coherent reconstruction. They keep the answer short of nearly flawless.
