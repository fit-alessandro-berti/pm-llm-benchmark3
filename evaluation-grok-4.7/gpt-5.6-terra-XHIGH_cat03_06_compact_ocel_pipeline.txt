7.4

The construction is complete, internally consistent, and careful about provenance. The interpretive sections misstate several object-centric notions that the task specifically asks for.

The dataset matches the narrative. All 17 events, 12 objects, UTC timestamps, and payment amounts 100 and 150 are present. E16 closes O200 and E17 closes O100. O2O covers containment, packing, carriage, billing, and two `partially_settles` links. Refusing `completes_settlement` because no invoice total is given is the right call. E2O is coherent: E06 links O100, O200, I1, and I3; E07 links P1, I1, I3, O100, and O200; E08 links only P2 and I2; dispatch, confirmation, and payment stay off the orders. Activity and timestamp are copied from `events`, and the checks cover uniqueness, orphans, timestamp awareness, activity/timestamp equality, and duplicate rows. The mandated discovery calls, dictionary storage, and commented visualizations are present. Flattened trace shapes for Order, Package, Delivery, Invoice, and the one-event Payment case are right, including duplication of E06, E07, and E11.

The analysis then drifts. E08 is not a divergence point. Convergence here is E06, E07, and E11: one event tied to multiple objects of the same type. Divergence is O100 carrying two `Add Item` events, which the Order DFG turns into an `Add Item → Add Item` edge. That symptom is described later as fake rework, but section 3 attaches the term to the wrong event. The OC-DFG is type-level. It cannot distinguish I1 and I3 from I2; Item is one node type, with mixed edges `Add Item → Reserve Stock → Pack Package` and `Add Item → Pack Package`. The same slip appears when the Petri net is said to make P1 itself appear, and when the OTG credits Item–Package only to E07. E08 is the same type pair. The interaction list also skips P2–I2 while enumerating weaker pairs. On Order flattening, `Issue Invoice → Close Order` is a spurious directly-follows edge: payments and delivery confirmations sit between those timestamps and were omitted by policy. That is the main misleading DFG result, and it is listed as an ordinary edge. Qualifiers are stable per role but not one scheme (`dispatched_package` versus `dispatch_delivery`, `added_item` versus `receiving_order`). The O2O “cardinality” column is a row count, not a ratio. `pm4py.view_oc_petri_net` is the wrong commented entry point; the public wrapper is `view_ocpn`.

Sections 6–8 are strong: the alternative policies change discovery without changing the story, the required failure modes are real, and the twelve manual checks match the tables.
