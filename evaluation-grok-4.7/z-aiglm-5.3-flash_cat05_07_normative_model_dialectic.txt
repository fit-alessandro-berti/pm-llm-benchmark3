6.5

The final write-up is a complete, case-complete review with real briefs, a usable burden split, and mostly defensible classifications. It is not nearly flawless: the required model is neither executable nor faithful to the required standard-route semantics, and the final judgment contradicts the answer’s own evidence findings.

The strongest material is the case disposition. SUP-703 and SUP-708 are correctly treated as critical breaches; SUP-704 is correctly separated from SUP-705 by FD-7’s hard sunset; SUP-714 is not excused by eligibility or “imminent amendment”; SUP-712 and SUP-715 are correctly read as control-flow-conformant but rule-noncompliant; SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-709, SUP-710, and SUP-713 are not inflated into misconduct. Both briefs concede what they must. The overlay table and collection schema cover eligibility snapshots, aliases, delegation, instrument IDs, notification IDs, and immutable timestamps.

The revised POWL fails its own deliverable. `t("Quality_Review")` is constructed again inside `add_edge` for both the standard and auto routes, so those edges refer to objects that are not the nodes. In PM4Py, transition equality is by object id, and `add_edge` rejects non-members, so the script raises before `M1` exists. Those were the edges meant to enforce quality and manual approval before activation. The emergency route, which reuses bound variables, is the only route whose order is actually encoded.

The same model also misses an explicit requirement: concurrent sanctions and quality on the standard route. Sanctions sit in a prefix block that must finish before the route that contains quality, so the two checks are ordered, not concurrent. New labels (`Auto_Approve_LowRisk`, `Provisional_Activate_Emergency`, `Retrospective_Procurement_Approval`) are not mapped back to the logged names, so the justified traces SUP-702 and SUP-704 do not replay. That limitation is not stated.

The final judgment then overclaims. Section 1 correctly leaves PD-3 master-data validation unproved for SUP-702, and the matrix lists it as missing evidence, but the classification table says “All PD-3 predicates met.” SUP-702 is also 2026-06-15, before the 2026-07-01 CRM move, yet the system judgment attributes that gap to the post-migration 29% drop. SUP-711 is labeled indeterminate while the same row says deduplication already resolves it to compliant. The burden paragraph both presumes CS-9 where authority is disputed and directs the adjudicator to record a justified exception. Those are not cosmetic inconsistencies; they are the authority rule on which the 289-case pilot population depends.
