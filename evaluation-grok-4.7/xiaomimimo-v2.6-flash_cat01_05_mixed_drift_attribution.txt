7.5

The diagnosis is substantively strong: March is correctly isolated as logging/semantic drift, later rework, queueing, priority redistribution, and seasonality are given separate change points, and the monitoring design can separate recurrence from new drift. It is not nearly flawless. Under a strict reading, several quantitative, design, and reproducibility errors are material.

The baseline-relative table and the total-variation / cosine distances check out, and the raw-to-mapped recovery range (about 85–96%) is right. The supplier-implied rework arithmetic does not match its stated assumption: the text says non-Z rework stays at 11.5%, but the published rates (especially September at 45%) match a 12% baseline. November cycle time is decomposed with `Assess → Request Info` at 29/100, which is July’s rate; November is not in the DFG fragment. The 2.5 h collect-documents service time is invented, and calling the residual split “Shapley-like” overclaims the method. Section 9’s “complexity +45%” treats a level (45%) as a change; the staffing paragraph also uses the November p90/median of 2.47, which is not the June–August effect. The senior/complexity “baseline” of 0.23 is March (about 0.23), not January (about 0.19).

The L0 rule requires 1,000 cases per month for DFGs, but every month has 605–790 cases, so the primary population fails its own support rule. Decision rule 5 fires only if the median rises by less than 10% while p90 rises by more than 20%, yet its rationale is the observed +17.9 h (+57%) median increase. That rule would miss the incident it cites.

Part A calls `DataFrame.mean()` on a frame that still contains the month string, which raises in current pandas before change points and caveats are printed. Part B calls `pm4py.filter_time_range(..., start_time=, end_time=, mode="both")`. The manual uses `dt1`/`dt2` and modes such as `events` and `traces_contained`, not `both`. The default raw-versus-mapped conformance path also rediscovers a separate model per labelling, so both fitnesses can look fine and the comparison fails.
