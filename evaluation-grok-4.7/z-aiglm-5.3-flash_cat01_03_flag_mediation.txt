7.2

The causal framing is strong, but the timestamp table and several factual claims are not reliable enough for a higher score.

The intervention section correctly rejects natural direct and indirect effects because of shared queues, interference, and exposure-induced mediator–outcome confounding, and it uses separable and interventional effects instead. The nine estimands cover the required contrasts, keep “hide the flag” distinct from changing routing in the estimand definitions, and treat a controlled direct effect as a different policy. The mechanism discussion, identification designs, falsification tests, reversal conditions, and P3 pilot are specific and process-aware. The main flag contrast is also right: triage wait about 0.2h versus 2.5h, review almost universal versus 4/10, escalation 6/10 versus 2/10, and much longer pre-review waits.

The stage table does not meet that standard. R03’s manual service is 3.5h (18:18–21:48), not 3.0h, so the flag=1 manual-service median and mean are wrong. Many subgroup medians are wrong even allowing approximation: flagged-medium arrival, service, review wait, and manual service; high-load arrival and escalation wait; normal-load review wait, escalation wait and service, and total cycle (16.45 is the overall flagged median; the subgroup median is 12.25). Escalated and non-escalated totals mix means and medians after the text says the entries are medians. The claim that high-load and normal-load differences are small rests on that incorrect 16.45. Low complexity is omitted despite n=4, while an n=2 row is included. Flagged high complexity is exactly the five escalated high-complexity cases, and only one medium flagged case is escalated, so the complexity and escalation contrasts are nearly the same partition and are treated as separate evidence.

The rejection count is wrong: 2/6 flagged escalations are rejected, not 3/6. That figure is reused to argue that escalation looks necessary. P1 is defined as display suppression, but the policy table assumes the priority lane is removed. The code’s structure is sound and does not zero-fill absent stages, but the small-subgroup warning sits outside the loop and never fires for partial stages, and the frequency DFG is computed and discarded.
