7.4

The verdict, crude-versus-stratified reversal, and refusal to treat complexity stratification as identification are sound. The +6.72 hour crude gap (21.96 versus 15.24), within-stratum means, pooled weights 12/30, 10/30, and 8/30, and the approximately −2.51 hour standardized contrast check out, as do most day/night, compliance, wait, service, and rework totals. Total, direct, and mediated estimands are separated; completion is treated as selection; the 50% transfer is correctly rejected in favor of instrumentation, a capacity change, and a blocked pilot.

The medium-Nova cell is wrong. Those four cases are two day/normal, one day/high (C28), and one night/high, not three day/normal plus one night/high. That is the main cross-group medium comparison, so the mislabeled load mix is material.

Queueing and service are reported separately, but the stratified cycle-time gaps are almost entirely wait (about 1.6, 1.9, and 3.3 hours) while service gaps are about 0.4, 0.2, and 0.1 hours and shrink as complexity rises. Hypothesis 2 still cites lower service time as support for a certification effect. That overstates the touch-time evidence and blunts the mechanism contrast the data actually support. Within-stratum rework is lower or equal for Orion in every stratum (0/2, 1/6, 3/6 versus 1/10, 1/4, 1/2); calling it mixed understates the same composition pattern.

The DAG conflicts with the role table. Complexity is described as a proxy for latent risk, but `unobserved_case_risk` and `complexity` are disconnected, so the graph makes them independent confounders and stratification does not touch risk. `compliance_required` is labeled a confounder although it has no arrow into routing or group. `shift -> resource_group` is presented as supported and bypasses `routing_rule`, despite the stated mechanism that the engine assigns the group after Classify; coverage could run in the other direction.

The diagnostic script’s pandas contrasts, weights, full complexity×shift×group grid, and n<3 warnings are appropriate. The DFG path is not: it builds a minimal XES document and passes that to `parse_event_log_string` instead of the trace strings that function is specified to parse, so the directly-follows output is not a reliable group comparison.
