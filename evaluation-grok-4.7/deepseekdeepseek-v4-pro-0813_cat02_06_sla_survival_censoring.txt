7.5

The final write-up is a strong, mostly coherent survival analysis: time origin and the 72-hour horizon are right, late resolution is separated from “still open at 72” and from “not resolved by 72,” withdrawal and transfer are not treated as ordinary censoring, and the hand Aalen–Johansen figures (CIF of resolution by 72 ≈ 0.304, competing CIFs ≈ 0.101 / 0.140 / 0.075, event-free survival ≈ 0.380, late-resolution mass ≈ 0.301) match the 32-case risk sets. Method choice, leakage warnings, and the non-causal operational section are also sound.

It is not nearly flawless. The state diagram drops observed transitions (`Screen→Resolve` on S16, `Rework→Resolve` on S27, `AutoCheck→Review` on S15/S17). Immortal time is only implied via leakage, not stated as guaranteed survival until `Escalate`. Section 5 adds a second fenced block after an explicit one-code-block rule. The “breach” scenario in code only relabels transfer (resolution CIF unchanged) and does not implement the composite or open-after-72 failure the prose says raises breach risk. S09 is described as contributing only through 72 hours even though, for the late-resolution estimand, it stays in the risk set until 92.
