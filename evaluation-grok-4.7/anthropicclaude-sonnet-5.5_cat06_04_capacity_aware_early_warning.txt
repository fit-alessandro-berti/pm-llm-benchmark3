7.4

The answer is a serious policy memo with the right headline calls, but it is not internally executable. The written rule and the portfolio diverge, and several gates contradict the cases they are applied to.

What meets the brief: I-03 is refused because the swarm is past its effective stage, not because `p_now` is low. I-05 (unchanged refresh) is suppressed, while I-07 (new log) is re-opened and still denied a swarm on value and time left. Calibration is segment-specific, effects are not set equal to observational `p`, spillover is monetized, one slot is reserved, and I-06 is gated on the imputed queue field. The base net-value arithmetic checks: with `S = 6 × 0.02 × €15k = €1.8k`, I-04 is about +€6.2k, I-02 about +€0.7k, I-09 about +€0.8k, I-08 about +€3.0k, and I-01, I-05, and I-10 sit near zero. Section 3’s optimistic and base benefit totals are reproducible. Fairness safeguards are concrete (equipoise randomization, segment recalibration, a low-value floor only when robust-positive, spillover caps). Monitoring includes owners, drift checks, and numeric change triggers.

Coherence failures:

- The deployable rule does not produce the stated portfolio. I-08 clears both written gates (`NV_mid ≈ +€3.0k`, `ARR_cons ≈ 8.5%` versus `1.25 × ARR* ≈ 6.9%`, and `NV_low ≈ −€1.3k > −S`). Its own risk-averse score, `NV_mid − 0.5(NV_mid − NV_low)`, is about +€850. I-02’s score on that same formula is about −€60, yet I-02 is waved through and I-08 is held. Time-to-SLA and value of information can justify that override, but then the rule is not the rule that is run.
- Suppression (`no new event` and `Δp_adj < 0.10`) plus escalation (`Δp ≥ 0.10`) leaves no path for I-02 (`Δp = 0.08`) or I-09 (`Δp = 0.07`). Both are second alerts with no stated new event, and both are wave-1 interventions.
- I-07 has 1.5 h left. After a 45-minute swarm, 45 minutes remain, which fails the Step 0 requirement of 60 minutes post-swarm. The table still marks it eligible.
- The congestion stop can cancel I-04, the only robustly positive case, after the two knife-edge swarms have already run. Congestion is not checked before those marginal slots.
- “I-09 and I-02 in parallel” assumes two swarms can run at once. The problem says two specialists deliver the swarm, which reads as one pair per slot. Parallelism is unjustified.
- In the adverse row, partial risk reduction on I-02 and I-04 is counted as benefit while their full false-alarm burdens are also charged. Those are mutually exclusive states. `δ ≈ 0.04` is also outside the stated 0.01–0.03 band. Both choices inflate the loss.
- “About two-thirds of the net comes from I-04” is too low. After equal per-slot cost and spillover, I-04 is closer to 85–90% of base net. The €15k exposed-case value drives every borderline call and is not sensitivity-tested for who is selected. Tiers A/B/C are never defined. Haircuts 0.85 / 0.70 / 0.60 and the 1.25× margin are arbitrary, and SLA time is used as if it were time until Diagnosis Complete.

The qualitative judgment is strong and the main table is auditable. The decision rule, as written, would not reproduce the allocation, so this is well short of nearly flawless.
