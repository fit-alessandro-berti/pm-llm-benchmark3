6.5

The final diagnosis is directionally sound for QA (FIFO plus appointment insertion), QB (FIFO with priority-1 preemption and a resume rule), and QD (skill routing with per-resource FIFO), and the confidence levels, equivalence caveats, and data plan are real process-mining work. It is not nearly flawless, because several reconstructed decision sets are wrong and those errors change the rule predictions.

The QA table at 08:20 lists only A02 and A03. A04 arrived at 08:13, so it was waiting. Priority and SPT both predict A04 (priority 1, `est_min` 7), not a priority tie and not A03. The row therefore understates the appointment override and misstates two predictions. The later claim that a 2-minute scan delay might hide A04 at 08:30 is impossible: A04 had arrived at 08:13. Calling A06 a 5-minute tolerance is also imprecise; 09:20 is exactly A05’s non-preemptive overrun (09:05 plus 15 minutes). A08-last correctly rejects global priority and fits FIFO, but that episode does not contradict the stated primary rule, yet it is entered as the contradicting episode.

QB is the best reconstruction: B02’s preemption, B05’s jump, B04 before B06, and B01’s resumes are read from the right eligible sets. Two gaps remain. Resume-at-09:31 is not reconciled with the 09:48 “dispatcher reassignment” stop of B01 in favor of B03, who was already waiting at 09:31. B07 is not explained by the resource being busy: at 10:36 the server is free, the 10:30 appointment is already late, and B06 is still chosen over B07. B04-before-B06 is support for FIFO, not a contradiction of the primary diagnosis.

QC is the weakest table. At 10:10, EDD predicts C03 (deadline 12:30), not C01 (13:00). At 10:32, C06 has been waiting since 10:25 with `est_min` 6, so SPT predicts C06, not C04; “SPT wins” is false on the true eligible set. The 10:18 choice of C03 while C05 (priority 1, arrived 10:11) is already waiting is omitted, so priority insertion of C05 is overstated. The 11:40 reading is right: FIFO takes C01, while priority, SPT, and EDD take C07. It is also unstated that C07 could still finish by 11:58 and meet 12:00 if started then. Confidence 50 is appropriate; the mechanism claimed for the middle of the trace is not.

QD’s headline is right, and D04 over D08 correctly separates FIFO from priority and SPT on D-M. The tables are still incomplete. At 11:30, SPT’s prediction is left as an unclear comparison and D08 is not tested against D-X, which has mechanical skill. At 11:48, D-X starts D07 (priority 3, 28 minutes) ahead of D10 (priority 1, 12 minutes); that clean FIFO-versus-priority-versus-SPT test is missing. D-F is idle from 11:36 to 12:00, long enough for D10, while D10 waits for D-X; that scarcity/reservation issue is not discussed. D06’s appointment-versus-FIFO equivalence is correctly flagged.

Section 7 names both QB and QD as most identifiable instead of one queue. QC as least identifiable, and global priority as the most dangerous inference, are the right conclusions.
