7.5

The operational comparison is largely right and unusually careful: visible trace equivalence is separated from branching and causal notions, duplicate labels are positional rather than identities, loop scope is interpreted correctly, and the P1/P2/P3 verdicts follow. Model A pairs one check with every attempt; Model B has exactly one check concurrent with the whole loop; the languages are incomparable; P2 collapses them to the same family of observations; a bounded mismatch proves unbounded non-equivalence. The closed languages, the bounds 76 / 85 / 4, and the five witnesses are correct, including the role of hidden `tau` as optional approval only. The bounded search is a real exhaustive marking search, and the simplified tree is a genuine reduction that preserves the stated P1 projection and P2 image while dropping check-before-analysis traces.

The complexity section does not meet that standard. Under the usual process-tree Petri-net translation, A and B are the same size: 10 places, 6 visible and 4 silent transitions, 22 arcs, and equal arc-degree simplicity. The reported 9/20 versus 7/16 figures, the claim that B is smaller but denser, and the claim that the simplification ties B at 7 transitions are incorrect. They are internally consistent with omitting the loop-exit silent transition and, for B, the parallel split/join as well. The exact Earth Mover’s Distance is asserted to ten digits without a derivation that could be checked, and `get_stochastic_language(tree, parameters={"num_traces": ...})` is not a sound way to obtain that sample. Several defining equations are also broken by stray heading markers, so the formal statements are harder to read than the underlying reasoning deserves.
