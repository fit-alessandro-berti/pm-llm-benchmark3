The redesign meets the required structure and is grounded in the normal versus peak logs: mix shift, rework loops, queue growth, and variant behavior are used to show how local fixes move the constraint. Lanes, pull limits, conditional interventions, on/off triggers, case-type trade-offs, redesign-caused residual risk, and a leading-versus-lagging dashboard are all present and internally consistent.

The main analytical gap is S2. Combined assay utilization is already 98%, and M2 is the faster machine, yet the scenario still treats an 18-hour urgent target as achievable by reserving M1 without estimating how much throughput an M2 outage removes. Concurrent trigger conflicts are also thin, a few targets are hedged (“where possible”), and stuck batch-wait volume (entries that never reach assay) is not used. Those limits keep it short of a near-flawless score.
