The verdicts, loop-scope analysis, and both directions of counterexamples are right: A repeats Check once per attempt and cannot put Approve before that attempt’s Check, while B has exactly one Check interleaved with the whole loop. P2 equivalence under the stated abstraction holds, the tree counts are correct, and the P2 simplification is actually simpler and does not pretend to preserve P1.

The write-up is not reliable as a formal artifact. Section 4 falsely says every A trace takes the silent approval branch; A2 has two visible approvals, so the required tau/approval explanation contradicts the table. The P3 caveat is backwards: those bounded witnesses are in the full languages, so they prove unbounded inequality; only a bounded match would leave unbounded equality open. Section 9 states that direction correctly, which makes §3 and §9 inconsistent. The bounded explorer calls `petri_utils.execute`, which is not PM4Py’s execution API (`semantics.execute`), so the required exhaustive comparison crashes. `play_out(..., num_simulations=10000)` is also the wrong parameter and will not do what the text claims. “Weakened” mislabels the simplified Analyze-then-Check order, which is a stricter representative of an abstraction P2 discards.
