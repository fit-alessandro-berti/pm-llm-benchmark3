The completed analysis (sections 1–8) is strong: fitness is not treated as ground truth, Model S’s `tau`/parallel permissiveness and Model C’s eight-trace memorization are traced to concrete languages, B1–B8 are judged as language membership rather than thresholded fitness, and the third model is a real compositional alternative with explicit data rules instead of silent optionality.

The deliverable is not acceptable as a board review. The required document stops mid-statement in section 9 (`format_dataframe` is cut off and will not parse). Sections 10 and 11 are absent, so there is no governance recommendation and no dissenting rebuttal. The validation program required by the prompt—negative suite, perturbations, unseen-combination tests, cost sensitivity, soundness, separate metrics, comprehension protocol, and a reject-despite-good-aggregates rule—is not actually specified in the final text.

The policy code that is present has a real false-positive: `pos = {a: i for i, a in enumerate(activities)}` keeps the last index, so a later recheck of Credit Check makes a legitimate earlier Expedite Review look like it preceded the check (R5 CRITICAL on traces the model itself calls valid, including X3-style cases). Skipped mandatory checks are also filed under R2, whose stated meaning is “repeat without correction,” so severity routing is wrong. R3 flags only under-scoped rechecks and treats a both-check recheck as fine even when the affected set is unknown.

Smaller overclaims: 0.973 training fitness is treated as evidence of variants outside the eight branches (noise or extra events would do the same), and “33 matches no obvious duplicate-label definition” is stronger than the leaf count of 48−9=39 can support.
