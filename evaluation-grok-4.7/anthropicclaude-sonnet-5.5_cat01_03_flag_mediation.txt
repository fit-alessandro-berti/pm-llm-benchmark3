7.5

The stage arithmetic is accurate across every subgroup I recomputed (flag, complexity, load, escalation, reviewer), absent stages are not zero-filled, and the required contrasts, headings, estimand count, and word limits are met. The flag is correctly treated as a score-threshold policy bundle rather than a manipulable cause, occurrence is separated from waiting, and NDE/NIE are rejected for the right reasons (no independent manipulation, exposure-induced queue confounding, interference). That is not enough for a near-flawless score.

The interference argument overreaches. T1/T2 serve only flagged cases and T3/T4 only unflagged cases, so the 14 vs 149 minute triage-wait gap is an own-lane contrast, not evidence that flagged cases push unflagged cases back. The prompt locates sharing in the review and escalation queues. Section 2 states the displacement as fact, the P0 spillover cell repeats it, and the synthesis says unflagged cases probably pay for the lane. Dedicated lanes can create interference only through prior staff reallocation, which these timestamps cannot show. The M2 overlap and the R09-before-R03/R10 ordering are the actual interference evidence, and they are later hedged by the possibility that M2 is a pool.

The recommended pilot does not match the delays it is built from. Review waits are about 390 minutes, yet assignment is by 12-hour arrival block with only a 3-hour opening washout, and analysis follows the arrival block. Arrivals in roughly the second half of a block are reviewed under the next regime, so the primary contrast is heavily contaminated. Exposure should have been the regime in force at review entry, or the blocks should have been much longer. The 3 pp, 10 pp, and 60-minute stopping rules are also arbitrary relative to an unknown baseline.

Smaller faults: the +370/+290 split is numerically a Kitagawa decomposition at unflagged conditional durations, but “stage durations as observed” does not say that. Section 1 conditions the interventional mediator draw on queue state, which is post-treatment, while Section 5 then switches to a g-formula. “Separable effects” is used without the modified-treatment assumption. The residual is called a fixed administrative negative control even though it is exactly 60 minutes in only 14 of 20 cases. The P3/P4 minute ranges look more precise than the disclaimer supports.

The code is careful and does not invent missing stages, but the retrospective queue snapshot is not annotated with the assumption that routing is known at `triage_done`. None of this breaks the descriptive tables. It does break the claim that the mechanism and the pilot are identified tightly enough to act on.
