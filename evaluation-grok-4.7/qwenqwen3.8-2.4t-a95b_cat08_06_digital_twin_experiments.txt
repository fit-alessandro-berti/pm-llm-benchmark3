7.5

The design is complete and well aligned with the brief: non-stationary demand, calendars, latent objections, service versus sojourn, cross-case coupling, a real experiment list, and a separated PM4Py versus simulation skeleton. It is not nearly flawless.

The public-notice clock is mishandled. The problem states that holidays and council meetings affect the public-notice period, but the model lets that clock run through weekends and holidays unless “legally paused,” so a stated calendar dependency is dropped. SLA targets are also mis-anchored: 12, 45, 60, and 120 days are treated as case-type medians, while those figures are variant-conditional (clean simple path, fire-plus-notice residential, full commercial, complex-with-appeal). Overall simple-renovation duration is higher once the missing-document variant is included.

Warm-up is too short relative to the tails the same answer cites. A 3-month burn-in cannot fill a pipeline whose complex p90 is about 260 days; “3 to 6 months” also does not match “discard 6 months of a 24-month run and score 18.” Parameter estimation never treats right-censoring of open cases, so duration fits can be biased. D2 depends on prior violations, which are not in the log and have no imputation plan. Combined policies are described as synergistic without substitution (D2 lowers the value of D4; D1 changes whether clerks stay the Monday bottleneck). Stress rows are not crossed with D1–D10.

Validation says historical waiting times can be compared, after the same answer has established that many start timestamps are missing and only sojourn is observed. The skeleton then writes that sojourn into `waiting_before_activity_days`. D7 is documented as a shift of Monday demand, but the only implemented effect deletes Monday arrivals. PM4Py use follows `format_dataframe` and `parse_process_tree`, yet DFG and performance DFG are requested via `get_directly_follows_graph` and `get_performance_dfg` rather than `discover_dfg` and `discover_performance_dfg`, and a failed performance DFG becomes `None` with no fallback. Common random numbers are named, but there is no stream design for policies that skip activities and therefore consume randomness differently.
