The advisory judgment is right: conditional go, limited to legally startable concurrency, with Site A’s −9 h median treated as confounded. The comparison, co-intervention inventory, modifier table, and expand/modify/abandon triggers are concrete and structurally grounded. The score is held down by failures in the three places the task weights hardest: executable model fidelity, median transport arithmetic, and pilot support.

The Site B POWL does not build. `loop_body` parents `Doc` and `Assess`, which are then inserted again into `digital_core`. On the originals side, the discarded `StrictPartialOrder(nodes=[Supp_o, Doc_o, Assess_o])` still parents `Doc_o` and `Assess_o` before `originals_core` is constructed. PM4Py’s single-parent rule raises there, so the Petri-net conversion, soundness check, and playout never run. The digital redo body also aliases the outer assessment node, which would cycle `Assess` before the legal loop and again inside it. Section 6’s sketch disagrees with the code: one routing puts `Approve`/`Reject` on the edge out of `Assessment`, another puts legal review and the supplement loop after assessment. The compact relation set never closes `Supplement → Doc → Assessment`.

The scenario ranges scale stratum effects into the overall median as if it were a mean. About 62% of Site B cases carry an 18 h originals delay, so many digital cases already sit below the overall median of 44 h; speeding only that left tail can leave the overall median nearly unchanged. “2 to 4 h overall” from a 35–40% digital arm, and “3 to 6 h” in the optimistic arm, are therefore too large. The hybrid line is also inverted: when originals delay masks identity work, identity is off the critical path, so p90 movement should shrink, not rise to −5 to −10 h. Pure service overlap is about `min(3.4, 8.1) ≈ 3.4 h`, and Site A’s −6 h wait drop is already confounded, yet the digital stratum is given −5 to −8 h at confidence 55. The directly-follows counts (2,108 originals→document, 1,360 assessment→legal, 1,160 identity→document) are not used to pin those ranges. The bottleneck-shift row does correctly separate a better median from a worse p90.

The pilot’s mechanism-versus-implementation split is sound, but the support math is not. Roughly 3,400 cases in six months and a ~38% digital share yield about 25 digital cases per arm per week, so 400–600 per arm is about 16–24 weeks, not 6–10. Shared pools of 3 assessors and 2 lawyers also make case-level assignment interfere across arms; team-week clustering does not separate those pools. The graph adds the required nodes, but `S_legislation → legal_capacity` conflates a statutory gate with a staffing level, and `role_specialization → identity_capacity` treats non-substitutability as a change in headcount. Assumption 8 mixes an immovable legal constraint with flag misclassification.
