7.5

The six-section forecast is structurally complete and substantively strong: it uses the slower parallel branch rather than a sum, separates queue time from service, keeps weekend and QA-hour pauses, treats FIFO versus risk-priority as material, and links the temperature-excursion flag to reruns, contamination, and investigations instead of multiplying anomalies as independent. Calendar anchors are mostly right (readiness Sunday 12:00–20:00, Monday 21 Sep as the next chemistry/QA day, hour conversions through about 170 h).

It is not nearly flawless. The plausible earliest, Monday about 09:30–12:30, stacks an early chemistry finish with a short QA wait, while the same write-up puts a 4–5 batch backlog at Monday 08:00. An early join hits that backlog, so those favorable extremes are negatively coupled; a completion before late morning is not plausible under the stated queue. The P50 band starts at Monday 18:30 even though the smooth path is only 30–45% and the other scenarios mostly land Tuesday or later, so the lower edge is early relative to the scenario probabilities. The delayed-microbiology row mixes a common, often non-binding Monday readout with uncommon contamination, but its 10–22% band and Tuesday–Wednesday center fit only the latter. Risk-priority wording (“after urgent work clears”) conflicts with the Friday start that the Monday-morning chemistry finish assumes. “Start QA before 17:00 and finish Monday” is tight against a 20:00 close when service is near 3.5 h plus release. The 250-hour mark is early Tuesday 29 Sep, not Monday 28 Sep evening. Excursion dependence is acknowledged but the probability uplifts are never specified.
