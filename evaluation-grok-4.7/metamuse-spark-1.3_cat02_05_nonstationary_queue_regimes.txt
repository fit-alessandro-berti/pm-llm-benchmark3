6.5

The regime story is coherent and the required eight-section structure is met: outage, recovery, mix shift, and the staffing intervention are separated; Little’s Law is refused where arrivals and completions diverge; and the pooled-FTE cut is a real Simpson-style trap. Those strengths sit on several concrete errors.

Net accounting is not internally consistent. Baseline weekdays are 701 arrivals and 697 completions, so arrivals−completed = +4, yet the prose says “net −4,” while the same section’s weekend “net +1” uses the opposite sign. Recovery is 241−211 = +30 completions, not +29, so the three-day outage block is +17 arrivals over completions, not −18. Section 4’s signed pieces (−47, +29, −94, +18) do not add to the stated +110 backlog. The code’s `net = arrivals−completed` disagrees with the prose.

Dispersion claims in the section that is supposed to establish them are wrong. On 2026-04-15, completion Fano is about 2.1 and CV about 0.65, so completion Fano is below the arrival Fano of 3.8 and CV is not above 1. The zero bins are 11:00–14:00, not 12:00–15:00. Weekday daily arrivals have Fano about 0.63 (mean 108), so they are under-dispersed relative to Poisson; “index of dispersion ≫ 1” is not true for that series. WIP lag-1 autocorrelation is about 0.61, not 0.7–0.8, and lag-1 of arrivals−completed is about 0, not negative. The 2026-04-21 completion range is 3–13, not 7–13. Mean staff is 144/28 = 5.14, not ~5.3, and the mean of daily service medians is 31.0, not ~31.5.

The hourly vectors sum to the daily totals, but they contradict `peak_08_10_pct` under the stated 08:00–09:59 definition (2026-04-21 is about 10% in those two hours, not 47%). The write-up treats 47% and the 10:00 and 13:00 spikes as the same concentration fact.

Flow time minus median service is used to claim “~120 min wait.” Mean service is unobserved; the later “+~35% mean service” both names a mean and understates the median ratio of 25 to 35 minutes (+40%). The cleaner two-day Little’s Law rate 9.83 does not match 2026-04-30 and 2026-05-01 (arrival rate about 9.54 at L = 34). A weekday surplus of ~6/day with balanced weekends clears a ~90–100 backlog in about three weeks, not ~2. The change-point “heuristic” is a printed rolling mean plus a sentence, and section 8 opens by pasting the task spec.

Arrival Fanos, the main regime means, and the stable-window L/λ arithmetic are largely right, and recovery is correctly barred from being treated as sustainable capacity. The numerical and sign failures are still large enough that the calculations are not reliable as written.
