6.5

The diagnosis is directionally competent and follows the required decision-set format, but the central tables contain factual eligibility errors and several mechanisms are stated more strongly than the episodes support.

**Correct core claims.** QA is FIFO with one non-preemptive appointment jump (A03 at 08:20); later free-server choices repeatedly reject priority and SPT. QB is a hybrid, and B03 at 09:48 matches none of FIFO, priority, or SPT and is explicitly a dispatcher reassignment. QC’s early choices (C02, C03, C04, C06) are SPT, while priority and EDD lose at 10:18 and 10:32. On D-M, D04 before D08 rejects both priority and SPT. Observational equivalence is handled well for B02 (priority vs SEPT), C05 (priority vs EDD), and A06/D06 (FIFO vs appointment). The section 7 warning against inferring a general preemptive-priority rule is justified by B05’s failure to preempt at 09:46.

**Factual errors in the decision sets.** At QB 10:14, B07 (arrived 10:08, est 6) is omitted. SPT then selects B07, not B04. At QD 11:48, D09 is listed as waiting and D-X-eligible, but D09 has been in service on D-M since 11:43. At QC 10:10, priority treats C05 as maybe present while EDD ignores that same possibility; if C05 is present, EDD selects C05, not C03. These are membership mistakes in the method the task requires.

**Mechanism overclaims.** Every realized service time in these tables equals `est_min` (segment sums included). Section 5 still ranks noisy or strategic estimates as a medium threat to the SPT reading; this sample does not show that distortion. QC’s “override when SLA is endangered” misses that C05 already had negative slack at 10:18 and 10:32 and was still passed over. QB’s primary label, preemptive priority for priority 1, rests on one “urgent arrival” preemption; the next priority-1 arrival did not preempt. For QA, A03 is used as both the strongest support and the strongest contradiction, so the contradiction column does not test the hybrid actually claimed.

**Under-analyzed constraints.** D-X’s 11:20–11:30 hold is informative because D08 (est 8) fits before D06’s 11:30 appointment; that fit is not used. D-F’s 11:36–12:00 idle (24 minutes) fits D10 (est 12) and cannot fit D05 (est 30) before the meeting. Attributing the idle to “D10 was assigned to D-X” is weak: D-X was busy, then started D07, and D10 waited until 12:16. The resume-to-head assumption for preempted B01 is material to every QB FIFO prediction and is not labeled. Section 5 calls A1’s recorded calibration undocumented, then says the calibration explains the QA gap. It also says removing overrides would make QD look like FIFO/SPT; the D-M sequence contradicts SPT.

**Uncertainty and format.** Confidence 60 on QB is plausible; 85 on QA and 70 on QD are high given a single clean appointment case and an unexplained specialist idle. Counterfactuals and the data-collection list are specific and usable. Format compliance is complete. The answer clears the bar of not treating every FIFO violation as priority, but the wrong eligible sets and the untested estimate-noise claim keep it well short of a nearly flawless reconstruction.
