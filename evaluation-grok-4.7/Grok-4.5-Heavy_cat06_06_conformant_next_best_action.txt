The answer has the right overall shape: all six required sections, every action ranked, lawful labels, A and B sunk by hard controls and asymmetric regulatory harm, rescreening not treated as a substitute for independent review, and P-901 kept in view. It is not nearly flawless. The primary plan’s own timing bounds contradict the ranking.

The Rank-1 rule waits for S4 if arrival is about 45 minutes or less. Review is 35–75 minutes and recording/execution is 5–15, against a 90-minute deadline. At a 45-minute arrival the fastest path finishes near 85 minutes and a middling review misses. At 65 minutes the minimum is about 105 minutes, so success is not “low”; it is outside the stated bounds. Section 5 softens this to “may” miss and still calls that cutoff robust. The 10–30 k€ benefit then attaches the 0.55–0.75 approval range to “timely + approve,” which overstates the joint probability.

The fallback is also under-modeled. S2 is available now only by interrupting P-901, whose breach increase of 0.12–0.28 is for an interrupt now, with a 70-minute deadline. Deciding at 30–40 minutes can lose S2 and makes a late interrupt worse for P-901. Under a uniform 35–65 minute ETA, the “wait unless later than ~45” rule interrupts more often than not, so “low–medium” spillover is too light. Immediate C is therefore closer to the deadline-preserving conformant option than the table’s gap between ranks 1 and 2 admits.

Smaller defects accumulate. Benefit cells mix gross ranges for A/B with probability-scaled ranges elsewhere. Ultimate approval probability is used as a false-rejection probability. The continuation says a score under 70 still needs a second review “while prior conditions [are] logged,” then allows a “lighter” auto-clear path without restating the actual test: no alert and risk below 40. Historical logging is not the rule. Section 4 states the current auto-clear bar correctly, so the trace is looser than the constraint section.

Conformance conclusions for A, B, D, E, and G are sound, and the dominance argument against unlawful execution is the strongest part of the answer. The operational recommendation that is supposed to beat immediate S2 is not earned by the numbers given.
