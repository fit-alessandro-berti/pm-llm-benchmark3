6.5

The review is complete, case-complete, and often precise, but it is not nearly flawless. Several judgment, authority, and implementation errors are material.

The strongest work is the separation of control-flow fitness from policy breach, the expiry analysis for SUP-705 and SUP-714, the sanctions-before-activation finding for SUP-708, and the refusal to treat SUP-707, SUP-709, and SUP-710 as misconduct. Both briefs make real concessions. The matrix is traceable, the calendar arithmetic checks out, and the POWL sketch does encode concurrency, XOR routes, a zero-or-more `Request_More_Info` approximation, rejection, and notification before closure.

The judgment then breaks its own rules. Section 1 says an unproved exception fails and the accuser must prove the breach. SUP-702 has a proved CS-9 deviation (no manual approval, quality after activation) and an unproved excuse (no logged master-data validation, contested PD-3 authority). That is noncompliance with an open evidence question, not `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE`. SUP-715 is also misread: the record states that no conflict-of-interest disclosure was recorded, not that the onboarding log merely failed to copy one. `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` is the wrong class. The system claim that M0 “flags the five NONCOMPLIANT cases” is false: SUP-712 fits M0 and is noncompliant only under the resource rule M0 omits. The “factual emergency extends FD-7” argument is rejected under the prosecution heading even though that is the defense’s claim. The two-source corroboration rule and the D5 alias caveat are announced and then ignored for high-confidence findings.

The revision contradicts itself. Layer 0 requires quality review before every activation, while the pilot and emergency routes put it afterward; only manual approval is given an exception. Layer 5 says overlays are not hard-coded, then M1 hard-codes both routes. The standard partial order allows manual approval before sanctions and quality, a real weakening of M0, with no justification. `Activate_Provisional` makes the justified FD-7 traces non-fitting unless relabeled, which is not specified.

`check_overlays` is not a sound implementation of the stated policy. `EVIDENCE_GAP` replaces `FAIL` whenever any gap coexists with any hard failure, so ineligibility can be hidden by an unrelated missing field. `fillna(False)` turns missing group, relationship, and similar attributes into definitive pass/fail values. Two business days are not computed; `quality_due_ts` is trusted input. Notification-before-closure, daily monitoring, reject, and reactivation cannot pass the checker. Spend is treated as a permanent cap rather than a cap until retrospective approval. The four-row demo behaving as described does not redeem those semantics.
