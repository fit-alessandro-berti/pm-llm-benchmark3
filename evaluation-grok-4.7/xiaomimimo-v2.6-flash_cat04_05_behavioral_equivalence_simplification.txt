8.5

The final write-up is formally strong: `+` is parallel and `X` is exclusive choice, the loop runs the body at least once, and the visible languages are correctly shown to be incomparable. Model A pairs one Check with each Analyze; Model B has exactly one Check interleaved with the whole loop, so Check can sit after Approve. The four witnesses are valid in both directions, include two-analysis traces, and fall inside the P3 bound, so the P1 and P3 “not equivalent” verdicts and the claim that bounded non-equivalence refutes unbounded equivalence are right. P2 equivalence only after collapsing checks to a Boolean and hiding Approve/`tau` is also right, as are the hand-counted tree sizes (12/12/11 nodes, depths 4/5/4) and the refusal to invent probabilities.

The deductions are for smaller faults. The preservation table calls the forced Analyze-then-Check order both “strengthened” and “Weakened,” and the recommendation then says P1 is preserved “exactly,” which blurs a real loss of CA traces. The P1 notion is phrased over reachable states rather than complete observations. The footprint note says Model A never puts Approve before Check, but a later iteration’s Check still follows an earlier Approve (only direct succession fails). The BFS is the right exhaustive design, yet it is unrun, and the footprint/EMD snippets assume set-valued PM4Py results and are looser than the trace argument.
