The required six-part structure is present, and the write-up does use a parallel join, ranges, and a temperature-excursion dependence note. The forecast itself is not usable.

The primary incubation clock is wrong by a full day. Friday 14:00 plus 46–54 h is Sunday 12:00–20:00, not Monday 14:00. Weekend readout and the 0.55–0.75 chance of no on-call microbiologist are therefore the main smooth-path branch, not a 10–15% side case built on an invented 40 h incubation. Because chemistry is closed over the weekend and QA has no Sunday hours, Sunday authorization also barely moves the join; that interaction is missed.

Chemistry is internally inconsistent: four full services are treated as about 10 h against about 5 analyst-hours left on Friday, yet B-417 is still assumed to start Monday 08:00. In-progress residuals, the 1.5 h minimum service versus the short Friday windows, pause-and-resume, and the 45% risk-priority case are not carried into the join. FIFO spillover can make chemistry the slower branch on Monday; the scenarios never test that.

The QA backlog is modeled as a frozen pile of five plus arrivals, with no service by the Friday-evening, Saturday, or Monday reviewers before the join. Friday QA is also cut at 18:00 instead of 20:00, review is allowed to run until 22:00, and the spillover arithmetic does not follow from those inputs. Scenario C’s table (Tuesday) contradicts its own decomposition (Monday 19:15). The rerun case is the same clock in the text and a later clock in the table. The investigation case uses 10–20% instead of 0.04–0.10, adds 8 days rather than 3–7, and places an endpoint at 03:30 outside QA hours. Contamination confirmation (extra 20–30 h) is never timed. The stated remaining hours (about 36–64) do not match the stated clocks (Tuesday–Wednesday is about 90–115 h), and “Tuesday, 18 September 2026” is still Friday 18 September.
