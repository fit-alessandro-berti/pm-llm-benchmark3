The answer covers the candidate list and most required headings, but it is not a reliable process redesign: one required section is missing, several mechanisms are inverted or miscategorized, and the effect table invents unsupported precision.

**Missing required section.** There is no Validation and governance plan. A single closing sentence about “event-log evidence” does not cover SP1/SP2/Q1 conformance, segment-level service (median, p90, late rate by case type and customer type), exception governance, or before/after path comparison. That omission alone blocks a high score.

**Boundary.** Scope is “the whole order lifecycle,” with no clear inside/outside split (machine kWh, transport, scrap, and package mass versus upstream materials, customer downtime, empty returns, and end-of-life). The assumption that external factors are absent contradicts carrier disruption, forecast error, and the stated low model precision during disruptions.
