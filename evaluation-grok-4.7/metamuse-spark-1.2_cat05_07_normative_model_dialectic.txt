## Model semantics

The RMI encoding does not accept the case it claims to legitimize. SUP-707 is `Sanctions_Check`, then `Request_More_Info` twice, then `Quality_Review`. `M_rev` places the loop entirely before the checks block. The text calls that “the observed desirable position” and an over-approximation. It is the wrong position and an under-approximation. SUP-707 remains deviant under the revised model.

`sanctions`, `quality`, `manual`, and `activate` are single objects reused across XOR children. A POWL node has one parent; this net is ill-formed even if construction does not throw. The reject child is only `Reject_Supplier`, so sanctions-then-reject (SUP-709) is not in the language, while rejection with no sanctions is. That inverts both CS-9 and the abbreviated pre-screen.

“May occur up to two business days after provisional activation” is a lateness bound, not a ban on quality before activation. FD-7’s EUR 20,000 cap is then applied to the auto branch as well as the emergency branch. The code comment calls `master_data` optional; the auto partial order makes it mandatory, so the exemplar pilot trace still fails `M_rev`. Withdrawal is named and not specified. The 12-month reactivation cutoff is an unexplained new rule.

LOOP(τ, `Request_More_Info`) as zero-or-more requests, standard-route concurrency, notify-before-close, and moving caps, identity, and windows out of POWL are sound. They do not repair a model that mis-orders the loop, shares nodes, and mis-states the pilot and emergency predicates.
