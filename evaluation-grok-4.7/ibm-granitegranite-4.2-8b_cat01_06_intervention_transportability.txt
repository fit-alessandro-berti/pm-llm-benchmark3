4.5

The write-up follows the required scaffold and correctly refuses a blind copy: Site A’s before/after change is confounded, Document Check cannot start before originals when they are legally required, and a segmented pilot is the right posture. Those judgments do not survive the operational detail.

The transportability “graph” is not a causal adjacency list. Required nodes mostly point at site labels rather than a path from parallelization through overlap and waiting into cycle time, with `S_` nodes entering the modifiers. Selection-diagram reasoning is asserted in prose and not represented.

Effect sizes recycle the confounded nine-hour drop. Identity and document service are about 3.4 h and 8.1 h, so fully overlapping them removes roughly the shorter service from the critical path, not 7–10 h. For the 62% that need originals, the median receipt delay is 18 h and Document Check still cannot start; the only feasible overlap is Identity Check with that wait, and the rules plus the directly-follows counts already allow much of it. Scenarios 1–2 treat nearly the whole Site A median shift as transportable. The bottleneck scenario is also internally inconsistent: the same downward change mark used for improvement is later described as a worse tail.

The legal loop is reversed. Directly-follows counts put Legal Review after Assessment, and Request Supplement then leads into Document Check (345 cases). Blocking Document Check while a supplement is “pending,” and restarting it only after the supplement is “resolved,” stalls the rework the rule requires. That error is repeated in the intervention, the pilot’s supplement checks, the recommendation’s eligibility clause, and assumptions 3 and 6.

The pilot randomizes only the stratum where originals are not required, then defines the control as waiting for an Originals Receipt that this stratum does not have. Shared reviewer pools make case-level assignment interfere; the “interference” row only freezes co-interventions. Power is a headcount with no variance, primary analysis drops supplement cases after treatment, and the expansion trigger “rework ≤ 5%” against a 17% baseline is not a transport criterion.

The PM4Py block does not call `parse_process_tree`, invents `POHLModel` / `POHLNode` instead of `pm4py.objects.powl.obj`, never builds a real strict partial order plus loop operator, leaves Legal Review without an incoming edge from Assessment, and claims soundness and playout succeeded. Service-time, p90, appeal, volume, and negative-control evidence are also missing from the transfer table, so the best within-process signs of confounding never constrain the numbers.
