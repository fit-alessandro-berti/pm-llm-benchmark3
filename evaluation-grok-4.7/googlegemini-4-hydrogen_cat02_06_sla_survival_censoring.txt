6.5

The estimand design, competing-risk distinction, and headline Aalen–Johansen/Kaplan–Meier figures are largely right, but the write-up is not nearly flawless: the control-flow map is internally inconsistent, one required comparison conflates different estimands, and the sensitivity code does not implement the scenarios the prose describes.

What holds up:
- Time origin, 72h horizon, and the split among resolution, competing exit, and administrative censoring are defined before the estimator. `S03` (resolved at 76), `S09` (open at 92), `S06` (withdrawn at 27), and `S08` (transferred at 68) are assigned coherently under E1 and E2.
- E1 as \(1-F_{\text{Resolve}}(72)\), E2 as \(S_{\text{any}}(72)\), and late resolution as \(F(\tau)-F(72)\) match different decisions (contract compliance, queue load, recovery). Withdrawal, rejection, and transfer are not silently treated as ordinary censoring.
- The reported primary numbers check out on the 32 rows: naive \(4/13=30.8\%\); pooled \(1-F(72)=69.6\%\); cohort A/B \(70.8\%/68.4\%\) and \(S(72)=33.9\%/41.6\%\); T2 for B \(66.8\%\). Open cases are retained. Priority imbalance (A \(3/15\) P1, B \(5/17\)), non-universal `AutoCheck`, and the E1-vs-E2 reversal are real and useful.
- Immortal time and leakage for `Escalate`, `Rework`, full prefixes, and `last_event_gap_h` are handled correctly. Recommending stratified Aalen–Johansen plus RMST, and rejecting a timed multi-state fit without stage clocks, fits \(N=32\).

Material problems:
- The state diagram does not conserve flow. There are 15 `AutoCheck` visits and every one has a successor, but the stated outs are \(9+2+2+1=14\). `AutoCheck→Screen` is 10 (`S16`, `S19`, `S21`, `S22`, `S24`, `S25`, `S27`, `S28`, `S31`, `S32`), not 9. `Review→Resolve` is 6, not 7; `Review→Escalate` is 5, not 4.
- `S28` is `Review→Escalate→Resolve`, not `Review→Escalate→Review`. `S13` loops through rework, then `Review→Escalate→Resolve`. `S20` (74h, no loop, no `AutoCheck`) is omitted from the post-72 resolution account.
- “Raises 72h non-resolution from \(30.8\%\) to \(69.6\%\)” treats a conditional completed-case ratio and the marginal CIF as one corrected estimand. The \(9/32=28.1\%\) figure is a third, different quantity. That undercuts the estimand-first standard.
- T3 is described as identical to T1 for E1, but the code censors transfers and moves `S21` and `S08` to a fabricated time `72.5`, which changes the risk set and the CIF. The stall rule does the same for `S10`: extending follow-up to 72.5 with `cr_code=5` is not “zero hazard after the observed cutoff,” and 72.5 is not a supplied timestamp.
- “Only `S31` is \(<24\)h” is false (`S05`, `S23`, `S18`, `S12`, `S16`, `S01`, `S31`). Point estimates are given to \(0.1\) percentage point with no interval, despite the uncertainty discussion.
- `breached_72h_confirmed` is tautological and unused. If `pm4py.get_variants` returns counts, `len(traces)` raises; only `ImportError` is caught.
