6.8

The review is complete, use-case-aware, and generally right about both submitted models: the probe matrix matches the process-tree languages, the refusal to average the metrics is sound, and the governance split (Model S only as a labeled exploratory envelope; neither original for enforcement) follows from the probes. Those strengths do not make the deliverable reliable.

The third alternative’s control-flow backbone is a reasonable reading of P1–P5, but the P6 checker is wrong. In `validate_case`, the recheck window is `events[ci:nxt]` while `nxt` is the first check after `Correct Dossier`, so that slice never contains a check. Every scoped comparison is against the empty set, and even an inclusive fix would still stop at the first recheck activity, so a two-check recheck cannot match. Scope mismatch is also only a review flag, despite the prose saying the sets must be equal. `DECLARE_RULES` is never applied. The backbone further forces every Supplier Review and Expedite Review to follow all correction cycles; P4 does not say that, and the “hence” in §7.4 does not establish it. That restriction is not in the limitations, unlike the narrower A4 assumption.

Two earlier claims are false. Training fitness 0.992 does not imply that correction events were filtered; a small share of log moves is enough. U6 is a representational gap, not an admitted trace. The sequential misreading of `+` would still accept B7, so the §5 caveat is wrong. U5 is undesirable because Technical Review is missing; treating expedited auto-approval as the open question conflicts with A2, which the same review later treats as allowed.

The validation program does not measure the hybrid it proposes. E10 gates on alignment fitness, while much of the claimed policy behavior sits in untested Python. The variant-stratified split parks rare variants entirely in training (`round(0.2 * n) = 0` for small n), so it cannot expose the unseen-combination failure attributed to Model C. Several skeletons are not executable (`EventLog()()`, the dead `if False` branch, a non-existent `"costs"` alignment parameter). The dissent is real, but it equates the hybrid’s attribute flags with Model S’s fail-open skips, which the backbone does not do.
