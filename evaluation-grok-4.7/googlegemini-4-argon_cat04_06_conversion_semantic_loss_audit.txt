6.5

The write-up is complete and often sharp: the N-shaped partial order, honest process-tree failure, tau-hiding, exact final markings, unidirectional POWL authority, and layout-versus-language separation are the right frame. It still asserts unrun results as facts and gets a required concurrency argument wrong.

The audit table marks POWL→Petri and the BPMN round trip `validated preserved`, and §5 states that both nets are 1-safe and that `check_soundness` passes. Nothing was executed. Witnesses and language diffs use `net_from_powl` as the oracle, so they cannot validate the POWL→Petri step. That should have been `expected preserved` or `not tested`. §9 never lists this circularity.

Completed-trace lengths are off by one. Skip-legal one iteration is RC, AR, PE, CT, IT, Rel, AC (7), not 6; with legal review it is 8. Two iterations are 10 and 11. Three are 13 and 14. `max_visible_len = 12` therefore covers every 1- and 2-iteration interleaving and no completed 3-iteration trace. §5 and §9 claim the opposite for three iterations and call that “exact language equivalence.”

Footprints do not show true concurrency. A parallel footprint is also produced by an XOR of both orders. Joint enabledness after registration is the structural test; the bounded explorer only fires one transition at a time. §4’s claim that footprints rule out an XOR of permutations is false, including on the point the task requires.

Other concrete defects: playout leaks are printed and then omitted from `passed`; §8 calls the derived Petri net both authoritative and strictly derivative; the “Trial Passed?” / “Legal Review Required?” annotations invent data decisions the nondeterministic XOR and LOOP do not have. The invariant table, Pipeline B handling, rejection list, and most of the explorer design remain strong.
