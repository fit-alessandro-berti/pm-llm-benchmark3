7.5

The forecast is structurally strong: the join is \(\max(C,M)\), the weekend is a hard floor, chemistry discipline and QA congestion are kept inside that floor, and excursion dependence plus the 2–12% laboratory-investigation band are used to explain why a P90 cannot be pinned. Hour-to-clock conversions (66.5 h → Monday 10:00, 73–97 h, 100–140 h, 145–290 h) and the worked QA examples (Monday 18:00 plus a 4 h query → Tuesday 10:00; Monday 16:00 plus 20 QA hours → Wednesday 12:00) check out. Rejection is correctly left unquantified.

The main quantitative error is the FIFO chemistry narrative. After the analysts’ residual work, Friday has only about 2.1–3.4 analyst-hours for the queue. Mean service is about 2.8 h, so finishing two full samples, and starting a third, is an extreme short-service corner, not the usual case. “Starts Monday 10:00–13:30 and Tuesday only in the tail” is therefore early and too dismissive of a Tuesday primary-chemistry spillover. That bias leaks into the smooth-path join.

Driver 3 is mislabeled. Contamination, not weekend on-call staffing, is what moves the join; the answer itself says on-call readout rarely changes the join because chemistry is already closed. That driver is mainly path uncertainty. The written investigation mixture also treats \(p\) and \(1-p\) as independent intervals, though 10–30% happens to be close to the valid range.

Smaller faults: contamination cannot credibly terminate at Monday 10:00 (earliest confirmation readout is about Monday 09:30, then QA and release); “early Sunday on-call” is used for the full Sunday 13:00–23:00 window; the rerun row is titled as excluding a laboratory investigation but assigned the full 10–20% marginal; earliest bounds are padded in one row and slightly aggressive in another. The 55/45 discipline split never becomes an explicit weight in the P50 range.

Not higher, because those are real decomposition and classification errors. Not lower, because the controlling clocks, calendars, overlap warning, and tail structure are right, and the FIFO mistake shifts Monday/Tuesday timing rather than the scenario logic.
