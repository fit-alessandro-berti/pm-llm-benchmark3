Deductions are for concrete defects, not tone.

The PM4Py blocks are written as runnable code but several calls do not match the library. `filter_four_eyes_principle`, `get_service_time`, and `insert_case_service_waiting_time` take a `parameters` dictionary, not `activity_key`, `positive`, `timestamp_key`, `start_timestamp_key`, or `start_timestamp_column` as keywords. `filter_paths_performance` uses `positive`, not `keep`. As written, those calls fail or, for `positive=False`, retain every case that fails to show two different resources, including cases that never contain both activities. The retrospective routine has the same gap: quarter-close rows with identical minute timestamps can be scored as timely, even though the prose says those timestamps are unreliable.
