6.5

The final document meets the required nine-section structure and is strong on source semantics, the non-series-parallel argument for why a process tree may legitimately fail, tau-hiding, the release join, and the split between an authoritative POWL source and a derivative BPMN picture. Those parts are careful and mostly accurate.

The required validation logic does not work on a faithful model. `bounded_visible_traces` sets `truncated=True` whenever any enabled visible step would pass `VISIBLE_BOUND`. The verification loop is unbounded, so that flag is always raised. `ref_traces` is then cleared and `validate_candidate` demands `not truncated`, so the source net and every round trip are marked `FAIL / QUARANTINE`, and the bounded trace-set comparison never runs. Section 7 criterion 7 then quarantines that inevitable outcome. Bound enforcement is treated as search failure. A correct cutoff would prune over-bound paths without voiding exhaustiveness inside the bound.

Section 4 compounds this. It defines `validated preserved` as “established by executing §5,” states that the code was not executed, and still labels the POWL→Petri and BPMN round-trip columns `validated preserved`. That is an evidence claim the document itself says it does not have. Footprint `parallel(Assess Risk, Prepare Evidence)` is also used as concurrency evidence, although an XOR of the two orders yields the same footprint and the same traces; section 9 later says this correctly, but the audit table does not.

Smaller defects: footprint or playout errors do not fail the verdict; negative witnesses for pure absence-of-order are set-properties rather than traces; and “assess_risk subtree” is a loose description of a partial-order crossing. The semantics, rejection list, canonical recommendation, and residual limits are otherwise good, but the central round-trip gate is incoherent and the preservation verdicts overclaim.
