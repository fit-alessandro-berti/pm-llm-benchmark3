7.4

The recommendation is the right one: conditional go on a narrowed mechanism, with Site A’s 9-hour median shift treated as confounded and Document Check kept behind originals where the statute requires it. Format, coverage, co-intervention accounting, and the max-versus-sum bound are strong. The score is held down by quantitative and design errors that affect the effect-size and pilot claims.

The transport bound itself is sound: full overlap saves the shorter verification, about min(3.5, 7.2) = 3.5 h at A and min(3.4, 8.1) = 3.4 h at B, so most of A’s 9 h median drop has to come from waiting, mix, staffing, the API, huddles, or timestamps. FTE throughput falling from about 267 to 260 cases, the exact 2,108 = 62% × 3,400 and 1,360 = 40% × 3,400 matches, and the legal-outflow gap (1,340 versus 1,360) are handled carefully. S0 is correctly marked as illegal and unidentified. S4 does include an average-improvement / tail-worsening case.

The scenario math then overclaims. DFG frequencies are treated as case shares (1,160 / 3,400 = 34% eligible). Register → Identity (2,050) plus Register → Originals (1,860) is 3,910, above 3,400, so these edges are not a partition of cases; assumption 8 flags only the small legal mismatch. A 0.34 × 1.5–3.4 h mean shift is then called a 0.5–1.5 h median shift. Non-original cases are the fast stratum (no 18 h originals delay), so speeding them moves the left tail and the mean; the overall median and p90 can stay nearly still. S1’s p90 range of 0–4 h hides that. S4 adds the baseline 345 supplement returns as if they were new delay, and “34% plus 40%” double-counts cases that are both non-original and legal. The sign of “change” flips between unsigned improvement and explicit worsening.

Those shares then contradict the pilot. Phase 1 excludes originals, legal-review, and the complexity tail, but 215 eligible cases per month is about 38% of 3,400 / 6, i.e. all non-original volume. Twelve weeks and “about 650” cases, and the 2,000-per-arm effect target, use that inflated pool. T2 (originals request at registration) is listed on a stratum that has no originals. Pods of 2 identity, 3 document, and 1 assessor do not tile pools of 4, 7, and 3; a stepped wedge on two clusters is a weak assignment design. Design effect 1 + (20 − 1) × 0.05 ≈ 2 is right only if the cluster is a pod-week, while assignment is by pod. Primary outcome “cycle time minus parallel saving” builds the estimand from the treatment. The closing claim that a null in originals cases with a clean overlap ratio shows the statutory gate has the signature backwards: a binding originals gate prevents identity–document overlap.

The causal graph has the same looseness. `role_specialization → true_overlap [+ if dual-qualified]` flips the node’s meaning; specialization at B blocks concurrency. Back-entry is not B-only: A’s overlap jump is already described as partly a recording change. `legal_review_required → legal_capacity` mixes demand with capacity.

Section 6’s control-flow is otherwise the right partial order: Register before identity and originals, originals before document when required, both checks before assessment, legal and supplement after assessment, decision before notify. The prose line that originals may run unordered against “any other node not on the chain” can violate `O → D → Assessment`. The PM4Py sketch matches that order, uses a real strict partial order plus XOR and a two-child LOOP, gates assessment on both checks, and states the right non-encodings (guards, capacity, locks, durations). Residual code risk is version fragility (`order.is_edge`, the POWL converter variant), not a sequential substitute.

Section 9 repeats the stratum error: 0.5–1.5 h is described as the segment effect, while that figure was the diluted population figure; the eligible-segment service bound is about 1.5–3.4 h. The 34% handoff group is also wider than the phase-1 rule (non-original, non-legal, below the tail). Expansion gates of 0.5 h, 60% system overlap, and the stop rules are operationally usable.

The eight assumptions are real and testable. Assumption 6 is almost non-falsifiable because a larger A effect still would not justify copying the magnitude. Assumption 2 is the one that most needs the missing DFG check.

Conditional go, statutory gating, co-intervention skepticism, and a mechanism-versus-fidelity split are the right skeleton. The effect sizes, eligible volume, and pilot arms do not yet form one consistent design.
