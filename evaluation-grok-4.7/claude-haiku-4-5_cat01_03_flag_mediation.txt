4.5

The write-up has the right skeleton and several real causal ideas, but the empirical core is unreliable, and those errors change the mechanism.

The stage table does not match the timestamps. There are 10 flagged and 10 unflagged cases, not 9 and 11. Unflagged mean cycle time is about 8.2 hours (median about 5.8), not 16.6; flagged mean cycle time is about 16.5, not 19.6. Manual-review occurrence is 10/10 versus 4/10, not 9/9 versus 5/11. Escalation is 6/10 versus 2/10. Conditional on review it is about 60% versus 50%, not “55% of reviewed flagged versus 18% of reviewed unflagged.” That denominator mistake collapses the distinction the task asks for: extra escalation is mostly extra entry into review. Medium-complexity cycle time is about 9 hours, not 16.4. Flagged high-load versus normal-load totals are not both about 17.6 hours, and flagged triage waits are about 0.2 hours, not the diagram’s “≈3 hours.” Escalation queue waits peak near 10 hours, not 20. Approval counts are wrong (8/10 and 8/10, not 7/9 and 8/11; escalated flagged cases are 4/6 approved, not 2/5). R01 and R02 are calculated correctly, so the aggregates were not produced from the case arithmetic.

Two design facts in the same 20 rows are missed. The flag is a sharp threshold of `risk_score` between 0.61 and 0.64, so the proposed RDD at 0.70–0.75 is the wrong cut and overlap is already empty. T1/T2 handle every flagged case and T3/T4 every unflagged case, so triage resource and flag cannot be separated. Triage service is also shorter for flagged cases, so it is not a negative control that “should be zero.”

Estimands mix frameworks. P0 versus P2 is called a natural direct effect, a capacity change is called an interventional indirect effect, and an accounting split is called a separable effect. E2 defines a priority-lane contrast on triage service for cases already in the priority lane, which conditions on a post-policy variable. P1 is elsewhere correctly treated as ambiguous, but E2 marks that contrast well-defined. The adjacency list chains flag into complexity and also reverses that arrow.

The pilot and identification discussion are more careful than the tables, but the pilot still uses a 0.70 eligibility cut, bundles discretionary review with dedicated capacity, and the synthesis then claims that bundle identifies review-occurrence mediation. Section 9 runs far past 260 words and repeats the false 19.6-versus-16.6 contrast. Timestamp semantics never become a reversal condition, despite overnight gaps. The Python path is the strongest piece: absent stages stay missing and causal DFG language is warned against. It still discovers a performance DFG only on the pooled log, calls `view_dfg` before the summaries, and omits an arrival event, so the DFG cannot show the triage-wait contrast.
