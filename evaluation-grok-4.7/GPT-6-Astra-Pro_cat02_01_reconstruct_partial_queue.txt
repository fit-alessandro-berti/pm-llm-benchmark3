The reconstruction is internally consistent and process-mining-literate. Case waits, services, sojourns, and ages match the timestamps; missing-start windows for P05, P07, P11, and P15 respect queue entry, neighboring jobs, completion, and the C2 outage; waiting and service are treated as coupled; OPEN and still-waiting time are elapsed, not final. Station areas close exactly (S1 2085 = 1490 + 595; S2 1340 = 780 + 560), subperiod counts and central queue averages reconcile with those areas, and nominal peaks (S1 WIP 8, S2 WIP 7, system 13) follow the stated right-continuous convention. Little’s Law is correctly refused as a steady-state estimator and replaced by a finite-window identity that accounts for the unfinished 360 case-minutes. The bottleneck claim is temporal and appropriately hedged: early S1 overload, slower fully observed S2 service, maintenance as a local amplifier, afternoon S2 congestion, non-FIFO handoffs, and the residual I2 idle beside P22.

The score is held down by bound and presentation problems, not by the nominal arithmetic. Section 4’s utilization floors (74.3% and 73.0%) are the zero-duration late limit; that limit is labeled elsewhere, but in the main station table it still reads like an operational range. The rounded station utilizations, about 61.5–96.9% and 63.8–89.4%, are sums of separately optimized resource extrema, so they can be wider than any jointly feasible station total. The stated ±5-minute envelope on mean completed station sojourn is the marginal one-case shift, not a joint envelope for the mean, and P01 is clipped by the window. The “central” start, five minutes after the lower bound, is motivated by recorded five-minute handoffs that rounding can reduce to zero idle; the scenario table contains that choice, but the justification does not. The S2 service-only benchmark of about 3.2/h uses the imputation-affected completed mean 37.5 rather than the fully observed 40 minutes. The required PM4Py block is honestly unrun: zero-duration arrival markers and the arrival/finish helper’s units (gap versus rate) are therefore unchecked, even though the calls are restricted to completed cases and labeled case-level.
