8.4

The reconstruction is unusually careful and mostly numerically exact: missing-start bounds, central waits and services, busy time, WIP areas, hourly flows, Little’s Law identities, and the S1/S2 capacity split all check out. Status semantics, censoring, and PM4Py’s case-level limits are handled correctly, and the required seven-part format is met.

Deductions are for real but local flaws. P05’s S2 service bound of ≤30 is not “shorter than 11 of the 12” fully observed calibrations (only 10 are ≥40; one is 25 and one is 30). Section 6 says both stations ran at capacity “all morning,” which contradicts the documented 08:00–09:00 S2 utilization of about 0.42. “System output equals S2’s rate” is true by the two-stage structure and is not bottleneck evidence. The Monte Carlo draws only on the FIFO-feasible interval even though S2 overtakes are observed, so those percentiles are too narrow; scenario X does not fully repair that. The claim that every quoted figure is printed by the script is false (the 26/29 hand-over count and the jitter-only effect are not). A5’s “clock runs late” wording does not pin down the sign of the offset.
