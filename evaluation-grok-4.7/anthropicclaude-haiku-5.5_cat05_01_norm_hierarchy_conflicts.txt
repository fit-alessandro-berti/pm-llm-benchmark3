6.5

The assessment is organized, time-aware, and often right on the hard controls, but it is not reliable as written. It invents evidence, breaks its own severity and classification rules, and overstates several deviations.

The hierarchy is the strongest part. GPIP-4 A1–A3 and A6 are kept above ECD-2, the SOP, and the CFO email. A7 is correctly confined to a written Chief Risk Officer exception. ECD-2 is limited to timing and supplier-register use inside its window. Late approval is treated as mitigation rather than cure. P-505’s supplier acknowledgment at 17:59:31 is correctly preferred to the 18:06 ERP stamp, and P-507’s 12:40 PDF is correctly preferred to the 14:30 ERP entry. P-502’s post-commitment compliance clearance, P-503’s missing compliance clearance and requester-committer overlap, P-506’s expired directive, and P-508’s position above the EUR 150,000 email threshold are defensible outcomes. Section 7 covers authority, scope, thresholds, segregation, new suppliers, records, expiry, and emergency-normalization safeguards.

Those merits do not remove the following errors.

P-501 is misclassified. SOP B4 permits commitment before the PO in a qualifying emergency, and separately permits substitution for an absent Plant Manager. It does not say that only the Plant Manager, or a delegated Shift Lead, may commit. The stoppage was predicted in four hours, the form fell inside 24 hours, the supplier was approved, and EUR 38,000 triggers neither A2 nor A3. Plant and Finance approvals exist, and B2 states no pre-commitment timing. NONCOMPLIANT rests on an added actor restriction. The decisive-evidence cell then cites “recorded calls and access logs for 08:38–08:50” and a “PM contact record.” The file gives neither. 08:38 is only the outer bound of a possible 12-minute recovery lag. Section 6 drops this supposed substantive breach and calls P-501 a logging defect, so Sections 4 and 6 do not describe the same case.

“Unapproved” is treated as “new” at high confidence. The file uses “new supplier” for P-502 and “unapproved supplier” for P-504, P-507, and P-508. That assumption is doing real work only in P-504: at EUR 24,000, A3 applies only if the supplier is new. B1 and B3 can still support NONCOMPLIANT if the cafeteria repair is inside the SOP, but the SOP’s maintenance and production-support scope is never tested, and Medium severity depends on a GPIP breach that the text has not established.

Severity contradicts the stated scale. Low is defined as no GPIP-4 control bypass. P-506 is rated Low while the same row finds the A2 budget-owner limb absent. The summary repeats that error by grouping P-506 with P-501 as procedural. Section 6 also lists a C3 breach for P-506 even though, on the controlling view that the verbal extension is invalid, C3 never applied.

Section 5 does not do what it announces. P-503’s alternative changes severity only and leaves NONCOMPLIANT in place. P-505’s header says the case could be compliant, while the body says discovery of the missing pre-commitment approvals would move it to INDETERMINATE. Finding those approvals would remove uncertainty. The known 19:00 Finance approval would still be late, so the stated reversal does not follow. P-506’s header says compliant sequencing, and the body then says the case would still fail C3. P-508 says a contemporaneous pre-commitment finance record would move the case away from compliance because that record would show a pre-commitment approval. That reason supports the opposite inference. P-507’s claim that the budget-owner limb fails “under any reading” is withdrawn by the later budget-owner premise.

The closing note says pre-commitment finance and budget-owner records would be the strongest reversal for P-502. They would not. Compliance clearance at 18:30 remains after the 10:15 commitment, and no stated override reaches A3.

Smaller defects accumulate. P-502 has no calendar date, but it is treated as certainly on 14 July and before the CFO email. Finance_Approve is repeatedly used as the A2 Finance Controller limb after the hierarchy treats those roles as distinct. The hierarchy columns slide between “can override” and “cannot be overridden by.” The catalogue and pseudo-logic lose the operators needed to distinguish “at least,” “before,” and “or,” and the pseudo-logic runs past the requested 12 lines. INDETERMINATE is defined for facts that would flip a result and then never used, while every primary label is NONCOMPLIANT. X.8’s post-expiry PO exception describes purchases whose PO and acknowledgment already predate expiry.

The central ordering is good enough that the paper is a serious draft. The evidential invention, the P-501 rule error, the broken severity scale, and the reversed alternative reasoning keep it well short of a dependable assessment.
