The final analysis is conceptually strong: estimands are defined before estimators, competing risks are separated from right censoring and from already observed breaches, transfers are not treated as ordinary non-informative censoring, future activities are kept out of baseline predictors, and the operational section does not treat scores as causal effects. Format, sensitivity coverage, and process-state reasoning are also largely on target.

It is not nearly flawless. The final text is internally inconsistent on the simplest count: it says 12 of 32 cases are resolved, while the same paragraph’s discarded groups (9 open, 4 withdrawn, 3 rejected, 3 transferred) imply 13 resolutions. “12 resolutions” is repeated in the analysis plan. Cohort B is said to have 11 of 17 `AutoCheck`-first paths; the prefixes show 12. The landmark risk sets, “27 alive at 24 hours” and “23 at 48,” omit observed terminals (including S01 at 18 hours and several events before 48). S03 and S13 are described as disappearing from a completed-resolved analysis even though both are resolved. The state list omits the observed `Screen → Withdraw` path.

The code matches the requested design, but `risk_set_counts` reads `analysis_cause`, a column created only inside `aj_cif`. On the primary encoded table that raises `KeyError` before the risk-set printout and the PM4Py extraction, so the script does not complete the required demonstration.
