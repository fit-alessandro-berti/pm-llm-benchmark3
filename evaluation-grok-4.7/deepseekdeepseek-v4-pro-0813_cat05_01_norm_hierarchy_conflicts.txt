6.5

The assessment is well structured and often sharp on timing, but it is not stable enough to score higher. The central hierarchy contradicts itself, and that contradiction drives two of the eight outcomes.

The strongest work is evidential and temporal. P-501 is correctly kept outside ECD-2. P-505 correctly prefers the 17:57 call and 17:59:31 acknowledgment over the 18:06 ERP stamp and the possible fast clock. P-507 correctly treats the 12:40 signed PDF as better evidence than the 14:30 ERP entry. P-506 correctly rejects an unwritten overnight extension. P-508 correctly separates Legal_Clearance and the EUR 150,000 email from the still-missing Finance Controller approval. Post-hoc approval is not treated as a cure, and the results are not uniformly compliant or noncompliant. Section 5 identifies premises that would actually flip several classifications.

The hierarchy does not hold together. The GPIP-4 row says the CFO email cannot override GPIP-4 without an A7 exception. The CFO row, Rule R2, the pseudo-logic, and the P-505 and P-507 verdicts then treat that same email as substituting for A2’s budget-owner and Finance Controller approvals up to EUR 150,000. That is a substitution of specified approvers, not an interpretation of ambiguous text. D4’s warning against removing essential integrity controls, and the fact that Incident Command members are not established budget owners, are left unused. Under the answer’s own GPIP row, P-505 and P-507 are missing pre-commitment A2 approval and should not be COMPLIANT.

Other defects are smaller but real. The first pseudo-logic line states the default backwards: commit is described as allowed unless the PO came first. The rule table puts source citations in the actor-constraint column, and several triggers lose the threshold operator. B6 is a deviation for P-502, merely missing evidence for P-505, and ignored for other emergency commitments, without a decision on whether ECD-2 supersedes it. C6’s scope test is used to narrow P-503 but is never adopted in the assumptions. P-506’s “Finance satisfied” line collapses A2’s two approvers into the finance event even though Plant_Approve is later. Section 6 also blends late evidence of a timely act into a generic logging defect.

The policy draft is the cleanest part: it separates CRO, CFO, and Incident Command authority, preserves segregation and compliance clearance, caps the email, makes ERP time rebuttable, and adds a real anti-normalization safeguard. That does not repair the inconsistent hierarchy applied to the cases.
