The interval arithmetic, timezone conversions, DST-fold elimination, SLA-versus-profile split, and zeta/tolerance matrices are largely correct, including several borderline calls (T-402 S1 stays a violation at +5 minutes; T-404 S2 and T-408 P2 flip on a few seconds; T-403 P1 stays possible at zeta 3). Assumptions about mobile entry delay and T-407 receipt are explicit, and the alternative that changes T-406 S2/P2 is reported.

The T-409 `Alert_Received → Investigation_Start` interval is not the full feasible set. Independent bounds allow about `[-481.5, 1021.5]` seconds; the reported `[0, 1021.5]` clips the negative tail without stating that constraint. Classifications happen not to change, but the interval itself is wrong. Sensitivity is a matrix rather than a discussion of every classification change. The PM4Py block is directionally compliant, but normalization is not done in pandas, and midpoint diagnostics are not actually compared with the interval results.
