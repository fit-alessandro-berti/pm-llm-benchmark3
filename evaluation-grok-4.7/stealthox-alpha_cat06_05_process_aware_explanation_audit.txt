The audit is thorough and mostly right: fixed-time leakage is separated from repairable lineage, the appeal bigram is treated as legal but future, the same-timestamp close→assessment pair is treated as an ordering artifact, congestion and prefix age are kept, and region/channel stay proxies rather than causes. Remediations and the case statuses (recompute / invalidate) follow from that.

The score is held down by repeated category errors. `final_decision = Partial` is future outcome leakage, not the breach label and not “direct label leakage”; the label is the 10-day threshold. `total_case_duration_hours` determines that label, but it is not “a monotone function of the label,” and the 0.44 importance is not that function either. In the conformance suite, `Settlement Decision Recorded` and `Claim Closed` are future-possible, not impossible at t0; Appeal is the structurally impossible activity. The example of a rare legal DFG edge, `Decision → Decision`, is the opposite of the stated rule (a second decision is legal only via `Appeal → Settlement Decision`).
