7.5

The written design is a serious calendar-aware twin: shared pools, seasonal and Monday demand, architect bursts, latent objections, separate municipal service / queue wait / external delay, and an explicit refusal to treat an unvalidated run as optimal. The PM4Py path matches the requested conventions (pandas load, `format_dataframe`, `parse_process_tree` with a fallback, variants, DFG, performance DFG, case-arrival average) and leaves service and wait as missing when the excerpt has only completion times.

The executable skeleton contradicts that design in ways that would distort a comparison if run.

- D2 cannot fire. `needs_inspection` skips only when `prior_violations is False`, and every generated case leaves that field as `None`. D10 inherits the dead branch.
- The D7 comment says Monday volume is preserved. The code multiplies Monday’s rate by `1 - reduction` and never moves that mass to other days, so the intervention deletes demand.
- D4 adds one inspector for the whole horizon. The policy is peak months only.
- Marks are drawn independently. Every New residential case is forced through fire review and public notice. The assumptions text limits public notice to some of those cases and requires correlated marks. Missing-document probabilities (25% for simple renovations) also sit poorly against a 19% overall “simple with missing docs” share inside a 45% simple mix.
- D3, D5, D6, and D8 are stored and then unread. S2 adds no council blackout weeks. `run()` still returns duration tables, so the matrix looks executed. The path that does run walks each case alone, adding service draws plus a few external delays, with no queue, calendar, bundle, or shared resource. Arrivals are generated on Saturday and Sunday at weekday factor 1.2, timestamped at midnight. That cannot produce the Monday completeness spike or the 12-day simple-path median, which is mostly wait.

The experiment plan is richer than the runner and still not a design that can be executed unambiguously. Multi-level cells (for example D1’s three reduction rates × three effort levels × three take-up rates) sit beside a later instruction to test conservative, central, and aggressive settings, with no statement of which factors are crossed, no run budget, and no confirmation sample after screening. Replication counts of 100 and then 300–500 are not tied to a target half-width on paired tail differences. Shared seeds are described as common random numbers; separate streams for arrivals, routing, and service are missing, so policies that take different branches desynchronize the draws.

Several identification issues stay open. Completion-only timestamps cannot separate an AND-split from short sequential work, so the parallel review block is assumed from the tree. Performance-DFG arc times mix queue, service, and applicant delay; the estimation section warns against calling inter-event time “service,” and still lists the performance DFG as a primary diagnostic without a rule for how those aggregates may be used. Emergencies deleted from the log still occupied specialists; dropping the cases and keeping full HR capacity makes the pools look freer than they were. Objection parameters would be tuned on the same long public-notice durations later used as validation targets. A 6-month warm-up is short next to a 260-day complex-variance tail, and neither the metric section nor the code fixes arrival-cohort follow-up versus in-window completion, warm-up deletion, or right-censoring.

Batching is parameterized as an exogenous travel-time cut (10/25/40%) beside a waiting window, detached from tour size and the inspector-day hour budget. Council meetings zero out ordinary legal work; alignment of a statutory notice period to a hearing date is left unspecified. The tree note covers parser/version fragility and skips the limits of this particular string: `Completeness rework` and `Inspection rework` are not labels in the excerpt, the XORs have no data guards, and appeal sits outside the tree. The 70–85% utilization band is not derived from these calendars or from the 1.7× peaks, and “beneficial under at least three stress scenarios” does not name which metrics must move.

The narrative would be a strong basis for a real discrete-event model. The internal contradictions and the unidentified control-flow and capacity biases are large enough to keep it well short of nearly flawless.
