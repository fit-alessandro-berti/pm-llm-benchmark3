7.5

The assessment is a serious, mostly coherent compliance opinion: issue-specific authority, time-of-act testing, non-curative later approvals, and the ON-44 direction of error are handled at a high level. It is not nearly flawless. Several formal rules contradict the case results, one high-value classification rests on an unproven scope assumption, and a few cells are internally inconsistent.

What holds up. GPIP-4 is correctly kept above ECD-2 and the CFO email on segregation and compliance; A7 is not smuggled in through the COO’s signature. ECD-2 is confined to its window and to local timing and supplier-register rules, with B5 distinguished from B7. The CFO email is not applied before 2026-07-15 07:20, so P-502’s finance gap survives. P-501 cannot be pulled into ECD-2, because an ERP time of 08:50 during ON-44 can only be later than the real act. P-505 correctly prefers the 17:59:31 supplier email over the 18:06 ERP stamp and separates that clock issue from ON-44. P-507 correctly treats the 12:40 PDF as the C3 record and the 14:30 ERP entry as a later mirror. A3 is applied to amount and to new-supplier status independently; post-hoc compliance is not treated as a cure on P-502. A1 is read as written: requester and committer may be the same person if they are not the approver, which is why P-508 is clean on segregation and P-503 is not. The hierarchy is issue-specific, the alternatives would actually change results, and remediation tracks the findings.

Consistency failures. Pseudo-logic line 5 satisfies the finance element by joint Incident Command authorization only if the amount is ≤ EUR 150,000, then adds Legal above that cap. I-2 and the P-508 row do the opposite: they treat “Legal as well” as extending the finance substitution above EUR 150,000. R-D2 admits the ambiguity and does not resolve it. The highest-value justified case therefore does not follow from the author’s own formal rule. The same burden rule that refuses P-506 — every exception predicate must be evidenced — is not applied to C3’s four-hour record on P-505 and P-508, where record time is simply missing, or to P-507, where sterilization-line scope is not in the facts. Those are classification-decisive under rules 6, 10, and 12, yet both P-505 and P-508 are JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION and P-507 is medium-high confidence. P-501 lists R-B2 as satisfied because Plant and Finance approvals were “eventually obtained,” while I-8 and the deviation cell say R-B2 is a pre-commitment rule that was missed. P-504 lists “no approval” as a deviation after stating that EUR 24,000 is below every approval threshold. P-503 lists R-C1 and R-C3 as satisfied under a controlling interpretation that the purchase is outside ECD-2.

Scope, evidence, and small inaccuracies. P-507 says only “critical replacement seals” needed within eight hours. Eight hours fails B4’s six-hour test, so if the seals are outside ECD-2 the missing finance approval, register breach, and PO sequence are uncovered. That classification-changing alternative is absent from section 5. I-4 equates “unapproved” with A3 “new supplier” despite the scenario’s deliberate split in wording; this does not flip P-504, but it inflates severity. The P-503 missing-evidence cell says a Chief Risk Officer A7 exception would only lower severity; a genuine A7 waiver of A1 and A3 would change the classification. Section 6 files P-501/P-502 outage timestamps, and P-502’s timely 12:55 record, under logging defects and late evidence. The 12:40 PDF is 3 hours 45 minutes after 08:55, not 3 hours 40 minutes, and 14:30 is 5 hours 30 minutes after the 09:00 commitment, not 5 hours 35 minutes. Severity labels such as “Moderate-high” sit outside the stated scale, and I-8’s “timing deviation rather than a missing approval” blurs the P-501 theory.

The policy draft is the cleanest part of the paper: it resolves the D2 cap, keeps compliance and segregation outside the overlay, fixes record time to the first binding communication, and adds real anti-normalization controls. That quality is why the case-level contradictions do not pull the grade lower. They do keep it well short of a near-flawless mark.
