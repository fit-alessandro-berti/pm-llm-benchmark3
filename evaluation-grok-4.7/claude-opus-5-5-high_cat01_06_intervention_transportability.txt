7.4

The recommendation is the right one: conditional adoption of the concurrency mechanism only where both checks can legally start, with Site A’s −9 h treated as confounded and the naive all-case copy rejected. Format, required nodes, statutory ordering, co-interventions, and the mechanism-versus-implementation pilot logic are largely in place. The score is held down by quantitative and design errors that the response then treats as established.

The 1–5 h “parallelization residual” is not pinned down by the stated decomposition. The confounder ranges sum to about 3.3–10 h, so the residual can be near zero or larger than 5 h. That invented band is then reused as the prior for the scenarios, the recommendation, and assumption 6. The median arithmetic conflicts with itself: if eligible cases mostly already sit below 44 h, speeding them up barely moves the site median, yet the ranges still apply a 0.2–0.5 multiplier and report site effects as large as −3 h. The 15–30% “no originals and no legal” band is attached to the wrong dependence direction. If public-sector cases need originals more often, that joint share rises above the independence value of about 23%, not down toward 15%.

The 20 h pre-assessment wait is handled correctly in the transfer table and assumption 4, then misused in S4 as evidence that three assessors are saturated. In Site A that same metric fell when verification was parallelized, so it is mostly verification critical path, not an assessor queue. No assessment service time is available to convert 46 assessments per assessor-week into utilization. S5’s calendar-hour Little’s-law sketch is only an illustration, and the confidence column switches meaning across rows.

The directly-follows counts are described as a closed, internally inconsistent graph. The problem gives selected frequencies. Register’s listed successors already exceed 3,400, which is a real anomaly, but Originals Receipt’s in-count versus out-count, and 1,940 + 980 decisions versus 3,400 cases, cannot be called gaps without the missing arcs. Assumption 5’s “>100% utilization” is also not shown: median × volume is not total effort, and 11,560 h exceeds capacity only under an unstated business-hours denominator.

The pilot’s purpose is strong, and the two-arm power arithmetic is correct (about 115 per arm for d ≈ 0.37). The design as written cannot be run that way. Phase A is 1:1, while T0, T1, and T2 require at least three arms; splitting about 240 cases three ways falls short of the power claim. A 3-day washout does not clear a process whose median is 44 h and whose p90 is 110 h. Case-level originals chasing inside a weekly switchback contaminates the shared document pool. The “+3 pp and posterior probability > 0.9 at n ≈ 100” stop rule is statistically miscalibrated for a base rate near 17%.

The transport graph contains the required nodes and the right shared path, but the invariance sentence is not the graphical criterion it cites. `digital_documents → originals_required` reverses the statutory mechanism. The POWL candidate does preserve the originals-before-document edge and the assessment join, and the limitations are honestly listed. “When run… sound” and the universal trace claims are asserted rather than shown; ten playout traces could not prove them.

These flaws do not reverse the conditional-go judgment, but they make the hour ranges and the pilot allocation unreliable as written.
