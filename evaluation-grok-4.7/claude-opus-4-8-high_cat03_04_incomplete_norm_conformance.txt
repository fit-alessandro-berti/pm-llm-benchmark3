The answer is a strong open-world reading with one material normative mix-up and several concrete inaccuracies. The central failure is N4. The observed `Pack Package -> Dispatch Package` shortfall, 198 of 200 externally shipped packages, is a distinct mandatory control-flow gap. Section 3 identifies the 198/200 figure as N4, then wrongly suggests those two packages might fall outside external-shipment scope even though the denominator is already externally shipped packages. Section 4 then reassigns N4 to “Dispatch Package lacking Delivery,” and the severity table never lists the pack-to-dispatch gap.
