7.5

The answer meets the required six-part structure and respects the main constraints: complexity is used, substitution is not treated as permanent competence, similarity is not equated with authority, and shadow roles are given both benefits and risks. Most cross-resource shares check out (A1, A2, A6, A7, A8, A9), and the role split is generally plausible.

It is not nearly flawless.

- Within-resource shares used to define core work are wrong. Giorgio’s A2+A4+A12 is 470/820, about 57%, not “about 68%” (repeated in sections 1 and 2). Amira’s A2+A4+A12 is 960/1375, about 70%, not about 80%. Ben’s is 1030/1366, about 75%, not about 85%. The direction survives, but these are load-bearing figures in the core-versus-peripheral section.
- Nora is overclaimed. Fragment 1 is Register → Nora Validate → standard response → close, with no human re-validation, yet sections 1 and 2 state that humans still re-validate and that human validation remains the entitlement control. Section 4 then treats that same point as undecided. That is an internal contradiction.
- “Highest critical-case exposure” for Mateo is the highest critical count (76), not the highest intensity. Erik’s critical share is about 33% and his median is 0.86, against Mateo’s roughly 18% and 0.77.
- The substitution rates (about 5 A7/week outside, about 15/week during absence) assume the 6-day absence is a clean 2-week block and that the remaining 41 A7 events are uniformly spread over the other 8 weeks. The aggregate matrix cannot show that weekly pattern.
- The PM4Py sketch is thoughtful but would not run as written. `discover_handover_of_work_network`, `discover_working_together_network`, and `discover_subcontracting_network` are called with `activity_key`, which those wrappers do not accept. Betweenness is computed with handover weight as distance, so strong handovers are treated as long paths and the broker metric is inverted. Joining role rows on `resource` breaks when one resource falls in more than one mined role. `get_service_time` is never actually compared across substitution and baseline, despite the comment claiming that comparison.
