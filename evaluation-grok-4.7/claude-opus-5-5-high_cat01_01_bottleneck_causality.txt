7.5

The analysis is quantitatively careful and structurally complete: the crude Orion–Nova gap reverses inside every complexity stratum, direct standardization is computed under explicit weights, queue time is separated from review service, positivity failure is taken seriously, and the 50% reassignment is rejected in favor of measurement plus a capped two-way pilot. Those are the central requirements, and the arithmetic behind the reported means, gaps, and standardized contrasts checks out. The score is held down by repeated causal overclaims and a few unsupported specifics.

The intervention story is wrong in a way that matters. A proportional reassignment of Orion’s cases leaves Orion’s expected complexity mix unchanged, so Orion’s mean would not fall “because its mix gets easier.” That claim appears in both the executive assessment and the final recommendation. Congestion relief could lower Orion’s mean; selective transfer of harder cases could too. Neither is the mechanism asserted. “Would probably backfire” and “would probably lengthen them” also treat the confounded within-stratum gaps as transportable effects after the same text says those gaps are not identified.

Hypothesis evidence is uneven. The Rosenbaum claim that a Γ below about 1.3 would explain away −2.5 h is an uncomputed number. The selection hypothesis cites Orion’s longer crude times, but within strata Orion is shorter, so ordinary right-truncation would tend to hide Nova’s long cases and shrink Orion’s apparent advantage, which is the wrong direction for the stated harmful-effect story. The measurement hypothesis treats the near-deterministic residual (about 0.8 h, plus 1.4 h for compliance, plus 1.5 h for Request Info) as support for group-specific logging error even though that pattern is shared across groups. The night negative control is also weak: a real “fewer interruptions at night” mechanism would speed Register→Classify as well, so a faster pre-routing segment does not discriminate that mechanism from a clock artifact.

Smaller discipline problems accumulate. “That gap is what case mix predicts” understates the result: complexity mix accounts for more than the observed +6.72 h. Compliance is not perfectly collinear with complexity; it is only deterministic at Low and High. In the decomposition table, the queue-versus-service row allows the causal reading that any real group difference “probably” operates through capacity or priority, while shift and load remain unbalanced. The DAG note leaves several arrows, including complexity→queue_wait, shift→queue_wait, and compliance_required→routing_rule, neither in the supported list nor in the assumption list, and it places an explicitly assumed compliance arrow in the supported sentence.

The variable-role table, overlap discussion, process semantics, and phased rollback rules are strong. The deduction is for those localized but genuine failures of causal identification, not for the overall design of the answer.
