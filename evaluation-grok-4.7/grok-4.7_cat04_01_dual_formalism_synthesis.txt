The control-flow reading, dependency table, single-copy under-approximation, and POWL partial order are largely right: block structure cannot express the two different joins, the tree delays approval and supplier consultation until both Plan Verification and Design Corrective Action finish, and the POWL edges encode those cuts without that delay. Loop shape `(ET→IT, AP)`, the risk XOR, the silent supplier skip, parent wiring, and the T5 split (tree rejects, POWL accepts) match the stated semantics. The concurrency table at the POWL root is complete, and the unsafe orders checked (trial before both work products, release after Adjust Parameters, high-risk with only Quality Review) are in fact excluded.

The score is held down by concrete faults, not by missing sections.

The T7 oracle does not test the claim attached to it. T7 omits the approval route as well as the second trial body, so Release Material is blocked for two independent reasons. A wrong loop that could exit after Adjust Parameters would still refuse release. Under ordinary alignment costs, the cheaper explanation is a log move on Adjust Parameters plus a model move inserting Supervisor Review, with Release Material synchronous — not a failure at release. The trace is still illegal, and the longest legal prefix does end at Adjust Parameters, but “the failing move is at RM” is not a sound check that redo cannot fall through.

That witness is also a poor isolator. A clean loop-exit counterexample needs the chosen approval (and the supplier obligation) already satisfied before the premature release. As written, T7 confounds the two gates. T8’s reason has a smaller version of the same looseness: once Quality Review has occurred, the low-risk branch is already closed, so “SR has not occurred” is not a second open alternative at release time. The fitness remark later in the validation plan is the accurate one.

The playout check “each IT immediately explained by a preceding ET” is ambiguous in a way that contradicts T2. Quality Review between Execute Trial and Inspect Trial is intended and accepted by both models; an adjacency reading of that sentence would reject it.

“Finite process tree” misstates the obstruction. The language is infinite because of the loop; the obstacle is that the dependency graph is not series-parallel. The self-critique chain “tree ⊂ intended ⊂ POWL” is also sloppy next to the claim that the POWL language equals the control-flow specification. Properly, the tree is a proper subset of that specification and the POWL model is meant to match it; POWL is larger only relative to the data-aware business rules, which the tree does not under-approximate either, because a high-risk case that took only Supervisor Review is still a perfect tree trace.

The duration remark in the self-critique overreaches. For a pair the partial order actually orders, a reversed completion order cannot come from a legal overlap; it is proof the start constraint failed. The real blind spot is the other direction: a legal-looking completion order can hide an early start. That is the limitation worth stating.

No deduction for leaving the constructors unexecuted: that limit is disclosed, and the construction follows the stated PM4Py interfaces, including partial order rather than `Operator.PARALLEL` inside POWL and do/redo child order. The residual doubt is real, but it is not an internal contradiction.
