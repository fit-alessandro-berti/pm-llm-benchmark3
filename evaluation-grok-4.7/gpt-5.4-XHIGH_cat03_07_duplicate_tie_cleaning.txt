6.2

The design is structurally complete and often careful, but it is not reliable as a conservative pipeline. Several ordering claims are stronger than the evidence, and the executable path contradicts both the stated partial order and the constraint against dropping distinct events.

The decision tree covers the six required classes and assigns sensible terminals. The pair table is mostly right: merge only e03/e04 and e11/e16, relabel e07, e08, e09, and e15, and keep e05, e13, e14, and e10. The warning that `have_common_object=True` cannot separate e13 from e14, or e03 from e05, is correct. Raw 17 events and 44 E2O links, conservative 15 and 39, and evidence-based 14 and 37 are arithmetically right. The naive raw collapse to 10 events and 28 links is also right. Provenance fields and the failure-mode list match the requested schema.

The 10:15 order is overclaimed. Fact 5 establishes only e09 before e10. e08 versus e09 is not a known precedence, yet both the prose and the rank table force warehouse release before carrier acceptance. At 10:00, scanner `source_seq` 551 before 552 means the physical scan shared by e03/e04 precedes e05; the two “admissible” pick orders are therefore not equal. PSP sequence 400 < 401 < 402 and ERP sequence 40 < 41 are likewise underused: e13 before e14 and e11 before e15 are within-source constraints, not open ties. e05 before e06 is only a lifecycle assumption.

The ordering key then diverges from the written policy. It omits original timestamp as the primary key, inserts alphabetical source ahead of the stated fallbacks, and gives Issue Invoice rank 80 against Capture Payment rank 90. That manufactures e11 before e14, which section 3 explicitly leaves unordered. Index-based microseconds are applied after this rank order, so the synthetic clock encodes those unsupported precedences. Nothing in the code flags them as technical.

`pm4py.ocel_drop_duplicates` is misunderstood. It does not delete duplicate E2O rows; the custom merge already does that. Applied to activity-plus-timestamp groups, it removes one of e03/e05 and one of e13/e14. The conservative program therefore cannot retain 15 events and violates the requirement not to drop e05 or e14. Relations are also left stale because timestamps are not resynchronized after the sort and timedelta. The aggressive program relabels before `ocel_merge_duplicates`, so it will not collapse e06/e07, e08/e09, or e15, yet sections 5 and 7 analyze the unlabeled raw collapse. The sensitivity narrative describes a different dataset from the code.

Smaller defects accumulate. The e11/e16 “authority map” is not implemented; conflict logging loses the kept value when the dropped source wins; OC-DFG is partly confused with object-type coupling; and a merge does not make I2 lose its pick, it makes I1 and I2 share one. Quarantine is redefined as leaving candidates in the production log. Full raw reconstruction is not mechanical from the stored columns.
