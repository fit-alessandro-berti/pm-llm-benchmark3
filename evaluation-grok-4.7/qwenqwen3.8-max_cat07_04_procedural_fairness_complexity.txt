The assessment is accurate, well calibrated, and complete against the required dimensions, constraints, and evidence. Raw approval gaps are treated as non-diagnostic, residual adjusted gaps are kept distinct from proof of discrimination, `risk_score_band` is not treated as neutral, and language support and accommodation review are analyzed as sources of burden rather than steps to remove. Variant shares, waits, resource concentration, mechanisms, limitations, and mitigations with trade-offs all track the given figures.

The score is held down by section 7. `filter_paths_performance` is called with a list of edges rather than one directly-follows pair; `insert_case_service_waiting_time` is given nonexistent `service_time`/`waiting_time` flags; case durations are joined to `unique()` case ids with no guaranteed order. The fraud check is internally inconsistent: the constraint says Fraud Review must follow Risk Screening, but the code tests whether Fraud Review occurs before Eligibility Assessment, an activity absent from the main high-risk path V3, so that check would miss the cases it is meant to audit. Path “summaries” are also hardcoded as exact variant tuples, without noting omitted activities such as closure. Those are real defects in a required analysis plan, not cosmetic issues.
