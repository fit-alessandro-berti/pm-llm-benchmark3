8.1

The three candidates are genuinely different hypotheses, the Process Tree / POWL split is satisfied, and the admission and exclusion claims that can be checked against the stated partial orders are internally consistent. The concurrency caution is also right: bidirectional directly-follows arcs are treated as completion-order evidence, not as proof of overlap. The score is held down by several concrete inaccuracies.

The evidence ledger misstates the counterexample to “Credit Check before Engineering Review.” Variant V1 says this order is unproven because 18/30 cases are not that full path. Most of those 18 still have Credit Check before Engineering Review. The actual contrary count is 11/30. The conclusion is later repaired, but that cell is a non sequitur.

Shared assumptions say every model represents optionality with XOR and silent transitions. The conservative model does not. It is a plain XOR of seven full sequences and contains no silent transitions.

The balanced “underfitting” list includes the possibility that the finance sequence rule is true and the Engineering-before-Credit traces are logging artifacts. The text itself calls that overgeneralization, so it is filed under the wrong failure mode. Section 4 also says Supplier Confirmation “starts” after one check, while the net only orders atomic completions. That slips past the completion-only semantics declared in Section 2.

The balanced model is an XOR of three partial orders, so Credit Check, Engineering Review, Supplier Confirmation, and Expedite Review are duplicate labels. That cost is explained for the conservative tree and only mentioned generically in the validation limitations. The same scalability and alignment issue applies to the balanced net.

DFG arcs Credit Check → Approve Configuration (3) and Engineering Review → Approve Configuration (6) are never named. They appear only indirectly through V3 and V4. The permissive audit trace Register Order, Credit Check, Engineering Review, Expedite Review, Approve Configuration, Release Production, Archive Case is also in the balanced language, but the table presents it only as a permissive admission, which blurs the comparison. The simplicity cell, “simplest POWL after the conservative enumeration,” does not say which ranking is intended.

The manual fitness predicate is wrong on a percentage scale. After missing the unit-interval keys, it accepts a trace when `percentage >= 100` or `percentage >= 1`. Any reported percentage from 1 through 100 is therefore treated as a perfect fit. On current PM4Py this is partly masked because `log_fitness` is checked first, but the fallback as written misclassifies non-fitting traces. Sampled playout is correctly described as non-exhaustive.
