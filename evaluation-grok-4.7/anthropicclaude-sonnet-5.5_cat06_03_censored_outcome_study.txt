7.5

The design is strong on the central identification problem: default is defined only after activation, omitted cases are not recoded as non-default, the 48,000-case flow adds up, and the 10.7–25.0% bounds on the activated default rate are right. As-of labeling, the policy-change and migration timing, and the refusal of a random split are all handled seriously. It is not nearly flawless.

The primary landmark equates “event 3” with first Credit Review. Under the stated process that prefix is almost control-flow invariant, so the study’s main estimand barely uses the process signal the design later celebrates. Later landmarks are only secondary.

The missingness plan overclaims. Clipped, stabilised IPW is called unbiased under MAR; clipping is already a bias. The weight model is allowed unspecified post-activation administrative fields, which can include descendants of default (`collection_status` is the obvious one). Positivity is named as testable, but not tied to a rule that the weight model is fit only inside the mature activated subpopulation, so administrative immaturity and permanent partner missingness can be mixed.

The survival hook conflicts with the binary rule. Truncating everyone at `as-of − 35 days` drops defaults already known through `label_available_at` and will understate the hazard. “Post-migration cases have almost no labels” is too strong: with a 7-day lag, activations from 15 Oct through about 10 Nov 2025 can still be labeled by 15 Feb 2026.

Notation is damaged in the prose (missing relations, and the decomposition collapses to `P(A=1|x) · (x)`), which is unacceptable in an estimand section. Linked-transfer dedup is one-sided (test only).

The skeleton is the right shape and length, uses a real temporal split, keeps an allowlist, and correctly refuses whole-case temporal features and lifecycle waiting times. Concrete defects: `GroupBy.last()` is not last-non-null for `default_90d` or `risk_score`; `nunique` is not an update count; `time_prefix` does not drop decision or post-decision events; the DFG is computed and then unused; the audit print mislabels every case that fails the landmark. `get_prefixes_from_log` is asserted rather than shown to return a dataframe that still carries the engineered columns.
