7.2

The analysis is strong on dialectic, expiry, and case distinctions, but it is not nearly flawless. Several model and evidence claims are internally inconsistent or unsupported.

The briefs, burden of proof, and matrix are serious and case-specific. In-window PD-3 and FD-7 cases are separated from post-expiry use, ineligible auto-approval, alias self-approval, missing COI disclosure, CRM mutability, duplicate registration, label drift, and the pre-screen scope dispute. Both sides concede the required points, and MIXED is not used as a dodge. SUP-701, 703, 704, 705, 706, 708, 712, 714, and 715 are classified with defensible dominant reasons. The layered split among POWL, temporal rules, data guards, identity controls, and organizational scope is the right architecture, and the schema covers notification IDs, versions, snapshots, aliases, delegation, overlay authority, and dual timestamps. The POWL is syntactically in the requested style: n-ary XOR, per-branch checks, LOOP(τ, Request_More_Info) as zero-or-more requests, notification inside every branch before Close_Case, and stated limits on spend, SLA, evidence coupling, and reactivation.

The model does not do what the prose says. Automated_Master_Data_Validation is mandatory in the pilot partial order, but it appears in no observed trace. M1 therefore rejects SUP-702, SUP-703, and SUP-714, not only the ineligible or post-expiry ones. Section 6 is wrong that this POWL removes false positives for SUP-702, and wrong that SUP-703 and SUP-714 remain control-flow valid and need only data guards. SUP-713 is also not repaired by the POWL; QA_Clearance still needs the mapping layer. The pilot edge Activate_Supplier → Quality_Review additionally forbids a PD-3-legal quality review before activation, and that over-constraint is not disclosed. PD-3 says quality may follow activation, not that it must.

Evidence handling slips in the same places. “Eligibility fields are complete” is treated as if master-data validation occurred. The source states that SUP-710’s prior certification remained valid; calling its adequacy unproved contradicts the record. The overlay table asserts Compliance sign-off for PD-3 and named approvers and document IDs for CS-9 and FD-7 that the file does not contain. That sign-off is the central authority dispute identified in Section 1. CS-9’s abbreviated pre-screen permission is also left out of the stipulated facts and appears only as a dispute.

Those are material errors under the stated tests of model semantics, traceability, and authority, even though the control-flow judgment itself is mostly well reasoned.
