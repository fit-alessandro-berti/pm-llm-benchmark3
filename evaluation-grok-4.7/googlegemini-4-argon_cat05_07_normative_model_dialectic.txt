The answer is complete, case-specific, and often precise about expiry, scope, and control-flow versus resource rules. It is not nearly flawless: several claims are untraceable, one classification is the wrong category, and the revised model disagrees with its own architecture.

Stipulated fact 1 calls CS-9 the sole formally controlled instrument, while the same section treats FD-7 as a formal directive and later accepts PD-3 as operative authority. That is an internal contradiction on the central authority question. The burden of proof also treats an M0 deviation as prima facie noncompliance, which prejudges the model-defect cases the judgment later excuses.

The briefs are strong and cite the right cases. The prosecution correctly separates in-window FD-7 use from post-expiry drift, alias self-approval, and the EUR 180,000 ineligible auto-approval. The defense then fails the required concession of genuine risk: “3 genuine errors and 9 missing-attribute cases” omits the rest of the 14 clearly ineligible auto-approvals, the six August post-pilot cases, and SUP-705. SUP-702 is described as meticulously satisfying PD-3 even though the trace does not show the required automated master-data validation. SUP-706’s mutable, late text link is grouped with SUP-702’s stable `notification_id`.

The matrix is thorough and the refusal to use MIXED is disciplined. SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-705, SUP-707, SUP-708, SUP-709, SUP-712, SUP-713, and SUP-714 are classified with defensible dominant reasons. SUP-711 is not. Identical source transaction IDs make that trace a deterministically resolvable logging duplicate, and the matrix itself calls it operationally compliant, yet the judgment labels it MODEL_OR_SCOPE_DEFECT. SUP-702 is JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION at high confidence while master-data validation is unproved, and the matrix reverses the burden by demanding proof that validation did not run. SUP-715’s missing disclosure is a real adjacent breach, but “nullifies dual control” overstates a policy that requires disclosure and does not prohibit the relationship; severity also shifts from Moderate-High in the matrix to Moderate in the judgment. The system claim that about 90% of M0 deviations are explained is not computable from the given aggregates.

The overlay table invents a PD-3 pre-approval cap of EUR 25,000. No source states that cap. Placing it on an EXPIRED PD-3 row presents a new rule as historical policy. The 12-month reactivation window and fictional authority document IDs are likewise unanchored, although the PROPOSED status partly limits the reactivation row.

Section 6 and the code diverge. The prose puts sanctions before route choice and describes the emergency path as activation followed by retrospective checks, so the standard route is sequential. The code instead repeats sanctions inside each branch and makes sanctions concurrent with quality only on the standard branch. Entry is an unconstrained XOR over register versus reactivate, independent of the five routes, so the POWL accepts registration-plus-reactivation-shortcut and reactivation-plus-pilot or emergency. That cross-product is unsafe and is not stated as a limitation. The loop docstring allows zero or more `Request_More_Info` steps, which matches `LOOP(silent, Request_More_Info)`, but the prose also says one or more and mislabels the silent transition as the redo child. The event-schema line “unique index per case_id + canonical_activity” can forbid legitimate repeated `Request_More_Info`, conflicting with the later deduplication key that includes `source_tx_id`.

The required headings, fifteen-row matrix, layered allocation, notification-before-close structure, and most PM4Py constructions are in place. The grade is held down by untraceable policy data, the SUP-711 category error, the SUP-702 burden error, the defense’s undercount of systemic failures, and an architecture that does not match the executable model.
