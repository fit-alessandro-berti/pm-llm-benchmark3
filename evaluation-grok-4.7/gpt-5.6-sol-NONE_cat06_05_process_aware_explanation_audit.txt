8.2

The audit is strong: it catches direct label leakage and the subtler lineage bugs, keeps queue length and prefix age, separates the legitimate appeal-then-redecision order from the unordered `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` tie, and does not treat correlation, SHAP, or region/channel as causes. Coverage of the top-12 features, sequence patterns, and local-only fields is complete, and the conformance suite and release gates match the decision point.

The main flaw is internal. Section 1 correctly says a model fit with critical future fields does not yield a valid prediction or trustworthy attributions, including for “other features.” Section 4 then puts contaminated local signs into `credible_drivers`: Broker and region R4 have no lineage or domain basis for direction, and queue = 4 is called “relatively light” without a baseline. Complexity = High, a high queue, and short elapsed time are available facts; their displayed signs are not yet re-estimated effects. `claimant_channel` is also treated as credible while only “presumed” to be a registration attribute, whereas `initial_reserve` is correctly quarantined for the same missing timestamp. That is a category error.

Smaller inaccuracies: `queue_length_at_t0` is rated low even though a ±5-minute join can take a post-`t0` snapshot; the appeal pattern is described as usable “after an appeal has occurred,” but `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is observed only once the later decision exists; received-before-validated is left coarse. Those do not undo the audit, but they keep it short of a nearly flawless one.
