8.7

The three languages, the evidence arithmetic, and the modeling choices are unusually careful and largely correct. The ledger accounts for all seven variants, D1–D7, and S1–S7; the 19/11 completion split is properly separated from the 17/11 directly-follows split; and the bidirectional DFG is not treated as proof of concurrency. Conservative is exactly the seven observed strings (25 labeled leaves after factoring, versus 49 unfactored). Balanced is exactly the 12 linear extensions of the stated partial order, including V5, which correctly kills an atomic parallel of the two checks before Supplier Confirmation. Permissive is exactly the 14 traces allowed by symmetric D5 plus D6, and P0–P2 match the balanced language. Nesting, the five balanced-only traces, the two P3 traces, the missingness/optionality split, and the lead-time confound are all right. The recommendation and the start-versus-complete collection plan follow from those distinctions. No metric values are invented.

The score is held down by inconsistent use of the terms the task asks to analyze. “Underfit” is used both for a language that is too small (the catalogue “underfits recombinations,” the Credit Check→Supplier Confirmation edge “underfits D5”) and for a language that is too large (“may underfit a real coupling,” “P3 underfits precision”). “P3 overfits the wording” then calls overgeneralization relative to the 23/23 pattern an overfitting. The phenomena are identified; the labels contradict each other. “More cautious generalization” for the independent Expedite Review skip has the same problem: it adds behavior the coupling hypothesis forbids, and the stated credence for independence is only 0.45.

The validation script has real defects. `play_out` is parameterized with `no_traces` and `max_trace_length`; pm4py reads `noTraces` and `maxTraceLength`, so the intended bound is silently ignored. `mark_key` uses `place.name`, and `getattr(..., id(place))` does not replace a present `None`, so unnamed places collapse and a non-truncated enumeration can falsely agree or disagree with the oracle. Depth counts silent steps against a cap of 24, which POWL translations can exceed; the truncation flag prevents a false proof, but the check is weaker than it appears. The suite prints PASS/FAIL and never fails the process. The claim that permissive precision takes an extra penalty “from duplicated labels” confuses simplicity with alignment precision, and the token-based generalization note misstates what that score penalizes.

Those are defects in terminology and in the verifier, not in the candidate languages. The models themselves are the right three hypotheses.
