The forecast is structurally strong: the Monday 08:00 floor, the uselessness of Saturday QA relative to a Sunday incubation-ready time, the limited value of weekend microbiology on-call on the smooth path, join-as-maximum, the FIFO versus risk-priority split, QA’s partial absorption of a later join, and positive dependence through the temperature-excursion flag are the right mechanisms, and most clock anchors (Friday 15:30 to Monday 08:00 = +64.5 h, Sunday 12:00 readiness, QA close at +76.5 h) check out. It is not numerically coherent enough for a higher mark.

The headline remaining-time summary contradicts itself. Section 4 puts a P50-like point at about +74 h (Monday ~17:30) and a P50-like band at +71 to +78 h, then states that only ~40% of the mass has finished by +78 h and ~58% by +92 h. That cumulative places the median on Tuesday morning, inside the lobe the same section calls secondary. “A coin flip before Monday 20:00” is a third, incompatible claim. The wide span “+71 to +92 h” covers the median only by swallowing the overnight gap. The same pattern is milder at P90: ~82% by +125 h and “nine in ten” by +165 h do not support a point near +148 h.

Scenario timings slip in the same way. In S3 the join is given as early as Monday 10:00 after an authorized short confirmation, while the earliest terminal time is Tuesday 11:00 (+91.5 h, which is 11:30, not 11:00). With a 0.30–0.55 investigation probability, a non-investigated confirmation can release Monday afternoon. The no-authorization confirmation window derived in the same section ends by Tuesday ~15:30, so the Wednesday 08:00 readout spill is outside that window. In S2, a first result after 16:00 is called ~35–45% of the rerun path; with a 0.55 FIFO share and a risk-priority completion by ~11:36, that fraction is closer to one fifth. The FIFO start envelope 10:45–13:15 also cannot miss Monday 18:00 under a 4.5 h service cap, yet ~10% of that branch is said to pause again until Tuesday.

Two requirement-level gaps sit under those arithmetic problems. A confirmed temperature excursion raises the investigation probability to 0.30–0.55 even when both assays are normal; S4 still uses 0.04–0.10 for normal branches, so the clean-assay, excursion-driven investigation is not a real path. Batch-rejection probability 3–7% depends on an unstated P(confirmed critical defect | indication) of about 0.3–0.5, which is not in the ledger. The conditional doubling (rerun 0.15→0.30, contamination 0.08→0.18) is disclosed, but the rejection factor is used as if it were given. Friday 18:00 to Monday 08:00 is a 62 h pause, not “14 h,” and “~50 of the ~74 central hours” neither matches that pause nor the separate microbiology staffing gap inside it. Those two figures are not what the terminal clocks were built from, but they are still wrong. The disjoint partition’s ~13% “other” bin is also where most documentation-only investigations disappear, despite that branch dominating P80–P90.

Queue-clearing means, the lognormal QA fit, the Monday two-analyst assumption, and the declared fluid shortcuts are acceptable approximate technique. The answer already knows the shape of this forecast: nothing terminal before Monday, smooth mass split across Monday’s QA close, query and rerun mass on Tuesday–Wednesday, and a laboratory-investigation tail into the following week. The reported P50 does not sit on that shape.
