6.8

The briefs, matrix, and system judgment are specific, case-cited, and mostly well calibrated, but the required model is not executable and mis-states several control-flow facts the judgment depends on.

The POWL construction fails PM4Py loop arity: `Operator.LOOP` takes exactly two children, do then redo. The three-child form `[τ, Request_More_Info, τ]` raises on construction, and the comment’s “exit” child is not part of the operator. Emergency ordering is then wired through `emergency_tail.nodes[i]`. `StrictPartialOrder.nodes` is a set or node view, not a stable list, so those edges are not a reliable encoding of provisional activation before monitoring, quality, and approval. Escaped quotes inside the fenced snippet also keep the block from running as written.

Even if repaired, the language is the wrong approximation. The information-request loop is entirely before route selection, so it cannot generate SUP-707 (`Sanctions_Check`, then `Request_More_Info`, then `Quality_Review`), which is the trace used to justify the loop. Rejection is a bare `Reject_Supplier` with no sanctions, so the model cannot express sanctions-then-reject, contradicts the intake path in section 6, and is a regression from M0. The emergency branch adds `Provisional_Activate`, a mandatory monitoring event, and a separate `Retrospective_Approval`, so the FD-7 trace treated as justified (SUP-704) does not fit without relabeling and extra events. That limitation is only partly disclosed.

Semantics slip in the argument as well. Partial-order concurrency does not explain SUP-702: quality there is after activation, not an unordered check. The matrix claim that SUP-709’s reject branch “fits M0” ignores that M0 still requires both checks before the XOR. Section 1 correctly puts an affirmative burden on authority for exceptions, then section 5 labels SUP-702 `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` while saying PD-3’s authority to displace CS-9 is not established. SUP-715’s dominant finding is a missed conflict disclosure, so `MIXED` remains a hedge despite the instruction not to use it in place of a primary call.

What keeps the score from falling further is the rest of the analysis. Expiry versus ineligibility is separated cleanly for SUP-703, SUP-705, and SUP-714. SUP-708 is treated as a preventive-control breach despite no pre-clearance spend. Stable `notification_id` evidence is distinguished from SUP-706’s late mutable link. SUP-711, SUP-712, and SUP-713 are correctly split across duplication, resource rules, and label equivalence. The layered architecture, overlay table, and append-only event schema cover scope, identity, caps, and versioned authority at a level the POWL itself does not.
