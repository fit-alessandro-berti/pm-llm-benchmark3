8.5

The models are largely faithful, internally consistent, and explicit about approximation. The process tree is a genuine conservative under-approximation, and the POWL model correctly encodes the non-block cross-branch pattern. The remaining deductions are for misplaced deviation analysis and a few over-sharp exactness claims.

The POWL construction is the strongest part. Registration forks into containment and analysis; planning waits only on containment; design, the risk XOR, and the supplier XOR wait only on analysis; the trial loop waits on planning and design; release synchronizes the loop, the whole risk submodel, and the supplier submodel. High-risk reviews are an unordered partial order under an XOR, so both are mandatory and neither order is forced. The loop body is Execute Trial before Inspect Trial, with Adjust Parameters only as the redo child, so a passing exit cannot be followed by adjustment. Parent and child links on the tree are maintained explicitly, labels are not cloned, and the silent supplier branch is a real skip rather than a missing path.

The tree matches that reading except for the loss it declares: because sequence and parallel are block-structured, approvals and supplier consultation are pulled into the pre-trial join. That forbids not only overlap with the trial, but also the valid order in which the trial finishes and those obligations complete only afterward, before release. Section 3 states the stronger “must finish before Execute Trial” constraint, yet Sections 6 and 8 repeatedly reduce the loss to overlap. That understates the excluded language.

Witness coverage meets the required categories, and A2 is a real separator: the tree rejects it and the POWL accepts it. F1 correctly localizes a cross-branch failure at Execute Trial before Plan Verification. F2 does not. Under the silent XOR, the prefix through Release Material is still a legal no-supplier prefix; the first event outside the prefix closure is Consult Supplier after release. Naming Release Material as the earliest violation uses future knowledge that the supplier branch was selected, which the control-flow model does not have at that point. A2’s commentary is also slightly loose: Regulatory Review sits after Inspect Trial, so it does not itself overlap the trial.

The dependency table, semantic comparison, POWL-first recommendation, and validation plan are concrete, including soundness, bounded playout, positive and negative replay, silent behavior, and duplicate-label checks. The third self-critique is only a version caveat. Mandatory participation of both initial streams is implemented but not stated as a resolved ambiguity. Those are secondary to the F2 slip and the incomplete statement of what the tree excludes.
