6.1

The core timeline is right—April is a pipeline break, June is a routing-mix change, August–September are real performance and population changes, and October’s 0.16 breach rate is not comparable—but the required deliverable is incomplete and partly wrong.

The diagnosis table merges covariate drift and process drift, so it does not give one row per requested type. Parts-edge time (65 h in July to 155 h in October) and the August V3 jump from 22% to 35% are process/performance drift, yet the table calls that mix change “slight” and files it under concept drift. August is also listed under label delay even though its 0.28 breach rate is high and mostly mature. Partner Z’s 0.45 rate is treated as settled despite slower maturity. Duplicate-event handling, which the audit explicitly flags, never enters the diagnosis.

The tests collapse six explanations into a pipeline-versus-real-drift pair. They omit duplicate cleaning, source and month stratification, discrimination as well as calibration, and a same-path check of whether P(breach | path) changed. Falsifiers are not stated per explanation.

Remediation contradicts itself: section 1 would retrain on August–September, while section 4 marks those months unsafe and calls early July safe. Recalibrating on January–June would lock in the pre-shock base rate and worsen the August–September underprediction (predicted risk about 0.19–0.21 versus observed 0.28–0.31). Variant-specific modeling is dismissed mainly via Partner Z’s share, not via the V3-only break.

Monitoring bundles or drops required monitors; there is no population monitor, and the maturity rule would not hold September (82%). The code sketch never calls `pm4py.format_dataframe`. `filter_time_range` is given the wrong argument names, `discover_dfg` and `discover_performance_dfg` return tuples that the code indexes like a table, `measure='median'` is not a valid argument, and `perf_dfg.index` will fail. The immature-label risk also assumes missing outcomes are stored as non-breaches, whereas the metrics are defined on labels available at cutoff, with long paths disproportionately missing.
