The abductive spine is the part that works. Those strengths are undercut by concrete errors.

Manual Override is already in the June–July variants (20 cases). The 2026-08-12 patch only changed how an existing action was logged. Evidence E10’s claim that it “appears after” that patch is false, and tying it to noncompliance overreads both IT and the reviewers’ routing-correction account. It is at least as much an unmodeled exception, hence a model gap, as misconduct.

“4–5% of enhanced-review cases” lack `Compliance Check` is the wrong denominator. 25/650 and 30/620 are about 4–5% of all cases; among enhanced-review traces the rates are 25/170 (14.7%) and 30/270 (11.1%), about 12.5% combined. Useful diagnostic outline, unreliable on facts, chronology, rates, and the program that was supposed to make the diagnosis reproducible.
