6.7

The spine of the analysis is sound and specific: direct E2O case membership is applied consistently, the main multi-object events are cited correctly (e13/e14 across orders, e16/e24 in O1–O3 and in P2/P3, e19/e22/e25 on V2, singleton M1–M4), the required distortion types are all present, and Position C is argued with a real counterargument and exit conditions. The grade is held down by several concrete reasoning and method errors, not by structure.

Divergence is defined as one object taking part in repeated sub-flows of the same activity, then misapplied. D2’s trace is only e16 then e24, so Delivery divergence is low. “D2 consolidates three orders” is order/package convergence onto one delivery event. That matrix cell contradicts the stated definition. Invoice distortion rated High has a similar problem: V1–V3 have no cross-invoice copying, and V2’s e22→e25 repeat is the real instalment sequence, so the invoice DFG is mostly faithful and merely narrow.

The Pack→Dispatch claim for e13 is not how directly-follows works. In O3, e13 (10:12) is followed by e14 (10:20), another Pack; only O2 contributes e13→e16. Duplicating e13 inflates Pack frequency to 2 and creates a Pack→Pack edge, which is not the same as doubling Pack→Dispatch. For D2, 11:05–16:00 is 4h55m, and it is not a Dispatch→Confirm DFG sojourn for O1, O2, or O3: e18/e19/e21/e22 (and e17 for O3) sit between e16 and e24. Summing overlapping case durations can multiple-count clock time; an average of order lead times does not “add that interval three times.” Section 3.8 also attributes parallel gateways and multi-instance markers to DFG discovery. On O1 the DFG artifact is self-loops (Add, Pack, Dispatch, Confirm), which is the right divergence symptom.

The propagation example is vacuous for orders. Every invoice and payment event already lists its orders (e18–e22, e25, e26). An Order flattening after “attaching invoices and payments to the orders they bill” is identical to the direct E2O flattening, so sensitivity check 2 cannot show the effect it describes. Propagation would matter for Item, Package, or Delivery, which are omitted from that check. Check 1 is also confounded: O2’s only pack, dispatch, confirm, invoice, and payments are shared events (e13, e16, e19, e22, e24, e25). Keeping only E2O-cardinality-1 events deletes O2’s logistics and settlement instead of isolating convergence weight.

Section 4 recommends equivalent-execution clustering for the O2+O3 pattern; Section 6 omits `cluster_equivalent_ocel` on the ground that clustering presupposes one lead object. Those claims conflict. The code comparison is the wrong statistic: `len` of DFG edges counts distinct activity pairs, not duplicated frequency, and it is labeled “unique events.” Activity weights (Pack 6 vs 4, Dispatch 6 vs 3, e16’s weight 3) are what would show convergence. `ocdfg.graph.edges` is also an implausible return shape for `discover_ocdfg`.

Smaller overclaims: packages “waiting for items from other orders” is not supported (I21/I31 exist by 09:22 and P3 is packed at 10:12; I32/I41 likewise precede e14), and “variant space explodes” overstates a four-trace log. Item truncation, payment singletons, P3/P4 copying, and the three-way partial-payment comparison are accurate and should be treated as the answer’s strongest evidence.
