8.5

The final answer is specific, internally usable, and factually careful against the given log. Event–object membership is consistently right: `e16`/`e24` on `O1`–`O3`, `I12`/`I21`/`I31`, `P2`/`P3`, and `D2`; `P3`/`P4` as the cross-order packages; `V2` with `e19`/`e22`/`e25`; payments as one-event objects. Required sections, distortion types, the B-style lead decision, the named PM4Py calls, and the four mandated sensitivity checks are all present and tied to identifiers. It does not treat the longest traces as the true case notion.

The score stays below the top band because the core vocabulary is looser than the analysis needs. Convergence is sometimes event replication (`e13` in `O2` and `O3`) and sometimes hidden consolidation (`D2`, `V2`), so the matrix ratings are not on one scale. Divergence mixes genuine multiplicity (`M2` then `M3` on `V2`, which exists even without flattening) with flattening artifacts and with fragmentation across cases. Calling `Payment` a secondary lifecycle in the lead decision also sits poorly with the earlier conclusion that payment cases are degenerate singletons. A few effects are slightly blunt, notably treating `e16`–`e24` as if it were the delivery performance of all three orders when `O1` also has `D1` and `O3` also has `D3`. Directly-follows self-loops such as `Pack Package → Pack Package` on `O3` are only implied.
