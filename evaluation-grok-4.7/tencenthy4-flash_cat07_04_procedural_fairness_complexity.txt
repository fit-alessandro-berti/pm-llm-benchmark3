8.3

The final assessment is complete, well-structured, and numerically faithful to the scenario. All eight required sections are present, the listed fairness dimensions and mitigations are covered with trade-offs, and raw approval gaps are correctly separated from residual procedural disparities. Legitimate routing, biased covariates, support-step burden, and uncertainty are handled with appropriate caution.

The main weakness is the PM4Py plan, which is presented as runnable code but misuses several required APIs. `get_variants` on a formatted dataframe returns counts, so `len(cases)` fails; `filter_paths_performance` expects one path tuple rather than two activity arguments; and `discover_declare` takes `min_support_ratio`, not `min_support`. `max_performance=None` is also unsafe. “Multilingual” slightly misstates Group B’s minority-language need, and the opening verdict “not procedurally fair” is a little stronger than the later, better-qualified conclusion that burden is concerning while discriminatory causation remains indeterminate.
