The assessment is strong, case-specific, and generally faithful to the log and the case facts. Nominal four-eyes status is kept separate from the control objective. AP-204 is failed despite different users, AP-205 is treated as stopped before completion, AP-202 and AP-208 are failed on bypass, timing, and coverage, and AP-206 is not forced to a verdict the token record cannot support. Timing arithmetic, the release-versus-settlement boundary, and the hybrid reading of a pre-settlement manager review are coherent. The sufficiency test covers timing, independence, coverage, override, recovery, evidence, and collusion, and it gives a real reason that equal expected loss is an incomplete standard. Monitoring is distinguished from proof of misconduct. The code reconstructs the log, calls the required PM4Py operations, separates AP-205, checks filter polarity, and labels its output as evidence.

The code still misstates known facts. m09’s performance authority over u23 is an established independence failure, but `manager_independent` is coded as unknown. “New vendor” is coded as an unknown 180-day status rather than a failed established-vendor gate. Other gates still make AP-202’s screen read as failed, so the label survives, but the required evidence flags drop two adverse facts that the prose itself uses.

AP-206’s single classification overloads two different questions. Independent pre-release approval, a clean validation, and next-morning audit are present on either reading of the token. What is indeterminate is whether ED-1 permitted the exception. The rationale describes that fork, but the classification column treats permission uncertainty as uncertainty about the whole control objective. AP-203 and AP-207 are explicitly conditional on unproven L3 authority and, for AP-207, on an unproven stop right, yet both are entered as `OBJECTIVE_MET`. That is a disclosed judgment, and it is permissive relative to the decision rule stated in section 2.

The business-day flag is coarser than the narrative. Date comparison through the next weekday can accept an audit well past 24 hours and, for a Friday release, can accept a weekend timestamp. The separate 24-hour columns and the AP-201 start-only mask reduce the harm. AP-201’s “High” confidence is also firm given an unspecified review scope, and AP-204’s confidence cell mixes confidence in the control failure with uncertainty about intent.
