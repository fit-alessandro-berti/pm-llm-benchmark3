The reconstruction is numerically careful and structurally compliant, but it is not nearly flawless. The main inventory identities check out: S1 exposure is 2,085 case-minutes, S2 exposure is 1,340, total WIP exposure is 3,425, and the central means 4.14 / 1.65 / 2.14 / 1.58 / 9.51 follow. Completed counts (22, 20, 14), the half-open rate table, the 14 outer-endpoint sojourns (mean about 146, median 145), central resource busy times (I1 305, I2 290, C1 300, C2 270), and the post-event peaks, including S2 station WIP 7 at 13:45–13:50, are consistent with the stated ledger. Open cases are kept, elapsed service is separated from final service, and the finite-window \(\lambda W\) identity is correctly refused as evidence of stationarity.

The required PM4Py demonstration is the clearest failure. Collapsing each completed case into one arrival-to-S2-end interval makes `insert_case_service_waiting_time` report service equal to total sojourn and waiting equal to zero or missing. That projection does not have valid case-level service/waiting semantics, and the code never says so. Calling the function and labeling the printout “not station-level” does not repair the misuse.

Section 6 is narrower than the claims that depend on it. Optimistic and pessimistic poles do not move P05 or P11 S2, and pessimistic S2 busy time is almost the central value. The reported swings are busy-time only. Started-case S2 wait moves by roughly five minutes under the short-service pole, but that is not shown. The 0–5 minute services are allowed by the prompt, yet they are far below every fully observed service, so the 0.68–0.80 band is a weak guide to operational uncertainty.

Hard bounds are also uneven. P07’s S1 bounds are expanded to the ±2.5-minute endpoints, while the stated P07 S2 service cap of 45 minutes is only the displayed 11:00–10:15 gap, not the same rounding expansion. The case table prints marginal intervals that cannot be attained jointly, without saying so. C2 utilization stays on a 360-minute denominator even though 15 minutes are known maintenance; the outage is discussed, but P08’s 55-minute wait is not split into time behind P06 (about 30 minutes), the 15-minute outage, and the residual idle around 10:50–10:55 and 11:10–11:15. The early-S1 / late-S2 bottleneck reading survives these gaps, which is why the score stays above the middle of the scale.
