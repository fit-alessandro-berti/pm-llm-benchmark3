The required causal scaffolding is real and often careful: interventional rather than natural effects, flag-as-policy-bundle, occurrence versus waiting, interference, exposure-induced confounding, P0–P4 as distinct interventions, and a guarded pilot. That does not rescue the empirical core.

Stage times for flagged cases are systematically misread. Triage-done to manual-start is about 390 minutes on average (420, 300, 540, 390, 240, 480, 210, 600, 420, 300), not ~0. Manual service is about 162 minutes, nearly the same as the unflagged reviewed mean of 165, not ~391 versus ~165. Escalation queue and service are swapped: flagged escalation waits average about 420 minutes and service about 115, not ~185 and ~420. Residuals are about 60–90 minutes, not ~210. The “queue skip, then very long service” story is the opposite of the log. The delay is mostly manual-queue wait plus escalation-queue wait, with similar service times conditional on the stage occurring.

That error is repeated as “directly observed” in the pathway table and the synthesis, so the mechanism, the P3 rationale (“flagged review waits stable”), and the claim that service duration is the long stage are not supported. Headline flag=1 cycle time is also wrong (~991 minutes, not ~1051); the listed case times sum to 9906. High-complexity review support is 8/8, not 7; medium is 6 reviewed, not 5. `flag` is not a deterministic function of `risk_score`: scores overlap (for example 0.61 unflagged versus 0.64 flagged).

The embedded CSV drops R01’s `escalation_done`, so `read_csv` does not match the 16-column header. `discover_performance_dfg` values are aggregate dicts, not lists, so the median/length print is wrong. The synthesis also runs long of the 180–260 word limit.
