7.5

The assessment hits every required section, uses the supplied figures accurately in most places, and stays within the constraints: raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination, case mix is not treated as a full excuse, risk scores are not treated as neutral, and support steps are treated as sources of burden rather than steps to delete. The adjusted-comparison design, mechanism split, limitations, and mitigations-with-trade-offs are substantive and well calibrated, and the bottom line correctly lands on procedural unfairness in timeliness and burden with uncertainty about discrimination.

The score is held down by concrete defects. In §1.2 the adjusted approval differences are stated without their negative signs (“approval probability 5.5 percentage points”), so the direction of the core outcome result is wrong or ambiguous. The PM4Py plan mixes data frames and event logs, would mis-count variants if `get_variants` returns counts, treats performance thresholds as days where PM4Py uses seconds, and marks a case as a policy violation whenever eligibility never occurs. It also never reconciles 57% language-support need with only 24% on V4, or 62% accommodation requests with only 31% on V5, and §2 briefly equates an unexplained residual with discrimination before §8 correctly walks that back.
