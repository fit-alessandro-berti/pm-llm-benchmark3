7.5

The final write-up is numerically careful, correctly structured, and strong on the main queueing points: outage versus recovery, weekday versus weekend, mix shift versus the late staffing change, and refusal to treat median service time or pooled \(L/\lambda\) as a steady-state flow time. Regime means, weighted complex shares, hourly Fano/CV figures, and the selected Little’s Law arithmetic check out.

It is not nearly flawless. Pooled daily arrivals are called overdispersed because variance/mean is about 9.7, but that figure is mostly mixture variance; within the stable weekday blocks the daily index of dispersion is well below 1. The Apr 20 break is sharp for complex share, p90, and WIP, and is overstated for arrivals. “High-complex phase” peaks of 40–50% do not apply to the high-complex weekends. The staffing reversal is Simpson-like only loosely, and the line about needing ~6 FTE “to keep … p90-load above 2” confuses a stressed observed state with a target. Small-n regimes are reported as precise means, assumptions are not labeled as such, and one Little’s Law verdict is left as a hybrid rather than a single required category. The code matches the prose calculations, but the pooled summary and PM4Py illustration are thinner than the requested specification.
