**Calculation 9 overclaims.** With sample standard deviation and n = 6, one point against n−1 equal points has z = (n−1)/√n ≈ 2.04. That algebra is right. The sentence that “nothing above 1.8 SD” is “almost guaranteed and says little” is not.
