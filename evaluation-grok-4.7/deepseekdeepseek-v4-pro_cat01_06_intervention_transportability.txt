5.5

The recommendation is directionally sound — conditional go, digital-only first, statutory ordering left intact, Site A’s nine-hour drop treated as confounded — but several material errors keep this well short of a high score.

The transport graph asserts `appeal -> cycle_time`, which reverses the outcome relationship, and the prose claims Site A’s post-change diagram is isomorphic to Site B’s. It is not: originals, legal review, and the supplement loop are absent at A. Capacities affect waiting, but parallelization never loads assessment or legal review, so the later bottleneck story is not in the graph. DFG counts are barely used (for example, Originals Receipt → Document Check = 2,108 = 62% of 3,400).

The formal model is inconsistent. For originals cases the partial order forces Identity Check before Document Check, which the rules do not require. On the supplement path, Request Supplement precedes both Document Check and Assessment, so the repeated assessment need not wait for the repeated document check, and those edges cycle with the forward order.

Scenario arithmetic does not match the recommendation. Overlapping 3.4 h and 8.1 h of service saves about 3.4 h on the critical path, yet the digital-only scenario claims a 10–15% case-level cut (about 4.4–6.6 h) and a 2–3 h overall median drop, while §9 says 2–4 h for eligible cases and 1–2 h overall.

The pilot’s unit is a case, but assignment is Monday–Wednesday versus Thursday–Friday, which confounds weekday mix and shares the same pools. Volume is also wrong: about 50 eligible cases per week yields ~400 eligible cases in eight weeks total, not 400 per arm.

The PM4Py block does not meet the specification. POWL objects are built with invalid constructors (`OperatorPOWL.XOR`, `Transition(name, label=...)`, edges from nodes outside the order), then abandoned. The converted model is a process tree, not that POWL net. The tree is a single pass, not a supplement loop, and the comment that POWL has no loop operator is false (`Operator.LOOP`). `max_traces` and the properties import are unlikely to run. What remains is an unsound control-flow sketch that would not check the candidate intervention.
