The write-up keeps the required seven-part outline and a few fully observed values (P01, P02, and some raw service durations), but it is not a defensible reconstruction. Mandatory case math, station counts, utilization, missingness, and the code all contradict the supplied log.

The case table fails the cases it was required to treat explicitly. P05 and P11 have observed S1 intervals and missing S2 starts; the answer treats S1 as missing. P05’s S1 wait is 35 minutes (09:00−08:25), not about 5. P03’s S1 wait is 20, not 10; P04’s is 20 and its S2 wait is 15, not 5 and 10; P06’s S1 wait is 40, not 5. P07’s feasible S1 start is after P05’s 09:25 completion, so S1 wait is roughly 40–75 minutes, not 15–25, and the cited “P05 completes at 10:00” is P07’s own end. P11 cannot start on C2 before P08 ends at 12:00, so S2 wait is about 55–80 minutes and service at most 25; the reported 25–35 and 55 overlap P08. P15’s S1 start cannot be 13:28 when S1 already ended at 12:15. P16’s S1 end is observed at 12:35; 12:05−10:25 is 100 minutes, not 40. P22 has been waiting since 12:10, about 110 minutes by 14:00, not 0–2. P08–P10, P12–P14, and P18–P20 are neither reported nor aggregated, and completed-case total sojourn is never given.

Station metrics do not describe this window. S1 arrivals are 7, 6, 4, 4, and 1 across the stated bins, not 5, 5, 2, 2, and 3. S1 is busy for most of 12:00–14:00; zero completions and 0% utilization confuse “waiting for S2” with an idle inspection station. S2 completions and the claim that P14 is still open are false (P14 ends at 13:50). P07 is on C1, so the C2 outage does not move it. No recorded C2 start lies inside 10:55–11:10; shifting observed timestamps as if they were plans is unwarranted. Cited busy minutes do not add up to the intervals named beside them. The WIP formula counts queue and service twice. Queue length is never separated from WIP, simultaneous handoffs such as P07 ending as P09 starts at 11:00 have no convention, and Little’s Law is only named, not calculated. The suggested 08:00–10:00 check is also a filling period, not a balanced one.

The bottleneck story is internally inconsistent: section 4 gives S1 about 0% utilization after 12:00, while section 5 calls S1 the late bottleneck and S2 only 35–40% utilized around 11:00, when C1 and C2 are in fact heavily occupied. Growing S1 waits, S2 waits, the 15-minute outage, and the 14:00 stock (2 cases still in S1, 6 in S2) are not actually measured. Section 6 repeats the wrong missingness labels, calls P21 an open S2 case, and declares the conclusions robust without recomputing waits, WIP, or utilization.

The Python does not implement the requested procedure. The S1-start list has 23 entries; later S1 starts are shifted; P07’s S2 start is marked missing and its end becomes 10:20; blanks, `MISSING`, and `OPEN` all become `NaT`; statuses and resources are invented. It never calls `pm4py.format_dataframe`, `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate`, or `convert_log_to_time_intervals`, and it does not explain that omission in a code comment. The interval log lacks `case:concept:name` and `start_timestamp`. Several expressions are not runnable (`time` subtraction, nonexistent columns, a DataFrame passed to an XES importer, and a waiting-time call whose arguments do not match PM4Py). It cannot reproduce sections 3–5.
