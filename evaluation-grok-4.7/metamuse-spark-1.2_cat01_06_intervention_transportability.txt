6.8

The answer is a serious transport analysis with the right high-level decision, but it is not nearly flawless. Several quantitative, causal, and formal claims are wrong or internally inconsistent, including in the sections the task weights most.

What holds up. The refusal to copy `+(Identity Check, Document Check)` onto every Site B case is correct, and the 62% originals rule is treated as a hard partial-order constraint rather than a soft delay. Site A’s −9 h median is explicitly treated as confounded, and the co-interventions (API, +2 FTE, mix shift, huddles, timestamping, no negative control) are separated from the concurrency mechanism. The selection-node framing, the digital-first subgroup, the median-improves / p90-worsens scenario, and the ITT versus true-overlap contrast are the right transport questions. All ten required sections and the required table columns are present.

Material flaws.

The recommended population effect is mis-aggregated. `38% × ~5 h = 1.9 h` is a mean calculation, then labeled a population median. Originals are required for 62% of cases and add a median 18 h delay, so those cases dominate the center and the upper half. Speeding the faster digital minority can easily leave the population median near zero. That same −1.5 to −2.5 h figure is repeated in the recommendation as an expected benefit.

The causal graph puts `legal_capacity` on `waiting_before_assessment`. Legal review is after assessment (DFG: Assessment → Legal Review 1,360), so it lengthens cycle time downstream of that wait. `case_complexity → *_capacity` conflates workload with headcount. `API_latency → identity_capacity` likewise turns a service-time change into a staffing node.

The mix-shift arithmetic does not match itself: 8 pp × 20–30 h is 1.6–2.4 h, not “~2–4 h,” and the 20–30 h excess is invented. No complexity-specific cycle times are in the evidence.

The “equivalent” order-edge list is not a partial order. `Document Check < Assessment < Legal Review < Request Supplement < Document Check` is a cycle. Loops need a loop operator; they cannot be encoded as strict order edges. The prose POWL also puts Assessment outside the node set while still drawing edges into it, and it always orders Originals before Document while the eligibility rules say digital cases have no Originals task.

The supplement story overclaims. Document check already precedes assessment and legal review in both the sequential and the parallel designs. A supplement redo exists for 360/1,360 legal cases whether or not identity and document overlap. Parallelization raises that rate only through a quality or illegal-early-start channel, which is assumed more than shown. Section 5’s “negligible” originals effect also conflicts with section 9’s claim that Identity ∥ Originals wait is the transportable kernel for 62% of volume. Site B rules 2–3 already allow both to start after registration, and Identity → Originals Receipt occurs 1,020 times, so much of that overlap may already be in the log. The marginal gain is IT-enabled execution, not a new legal partial order. Register’s outgoing DFG counts (2,050 + 1,860 > 3,400) are never reconciled.

The pilot’s power claim is not credible. An SD of ~12 h cannot describe cycle time when p90 − median is 66 h at B and about 46–55 h at A. A parametric 3 h effect at that dispersion needs far more than 500 per arm, and “130 eligible/week” contradicts stratum D at n≈380. “Case-level randomization clustered by arrival day” never settles the assignment unit. The expansion rule’s “rework +1.5 pp” has no inequality; read literally, an increase is a condition for expansion.

The code would not validate the candidate. `check_is_workflow_net` for Site A before is called with `fm_A_after`. The optional legal branch uses `None` instead of a silent transition. The same Document, Assessment, and Legal transitions are reused in the main order and the redo fragment, and the loop body plus redo both contain Legal Review, so the happy path and the supplement path are mis-counted. `POWL(powl_main)` does not wrap a model in standard PM4Py; operators belong to `Operator`, not `OperatorPOWL`. The net always enforces Originals → Document, so it is not the segmented intervention. Playout is passed `no_traces` and only survives by catching the exception. The closing comments on data guards, capacity, locks, and durations are the sound part of this section.

Smaller defects. “At At 62%,” “intentexecution,” and “17%20%+” are sloppy. The digital-row “harmful signature” is “no harm if capacity adequate,” which is not a signature. Assessors at WIP 5 and the 12-case throttle are unexplained. Section 9 is at or just over the 400-word cap. Assumption 5 is the right hypothesis, but several confidences (digital transfer 55, overlap transport 25) are tighter than the unidentified Site A effect allows.

`conditional go` is still the correct choice, and the first-wave subgroup is the right one. The score is held down because the headline effect size, the legal timing edge, the partial order, the power calculation, and the executable model are unreliable under the standards this question sets.
