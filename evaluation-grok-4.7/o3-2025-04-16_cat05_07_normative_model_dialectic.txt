The write-up covers every required heading and all 15 cases, and several classifications are defensible (SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-711, SUP-714). It is not reliable. Invented authority, inverted evidence, a non-executable POWL, and missed concessions undermine the five things this review is supposed to test.

## Evidence and authority

Stipulated facts over-close contested points. PD-3 is minutes plus email, and the compliance observer later denied approving any weakening of CS-9. FD-7’s power to derogate is not shown as a signed, competent override. Treating both as settled, competent, time-bounded authority makes SUP-702 and SUP-704 look cleaner than the record allows.

The prosecution quotes a nonexistent CS-9 §9 (“records shall be complete and tamper-resistant”). SUP-703’s EUR 180,000 is compared with the FD-7 cap, which did not apply in June and is not an M0 rule. SUP-702’s quality review is called late even though the next business day sits inside PD-3’s two-business-day window. SUP-708 is described as “no spend,” but the record only says no transactions occurred before sanctions clearance. The defense turns “about 70% of notifications are mirrored” into a “70% CRM integration loss,” invents a policy draft and an intent to amend CS-9, and treats a mutable link written two days later as a reasonable case link. PD-3’s separate automated master-data validation is never checked, yet SUP-702 is said to meet every pilot condition.

## Dialectic

The prosecution concludes that deviations are undisciplined practice and not model error. That refuses the required concession: M0 has no loop, no resource rule, no reactivation path, and no pilot or emergency overlay, and notification missingness rose from 2% to 29% after the CRM move. It also never uses its best authority point, the observer’s repudiation.

The defense does not, in the brief, accept SUP-703 or SUP-708 as indefensible, and it treats an expired directive plus expected permanence as a reason to extend policy. Matrix concessions partly repair this, but they are not the briefs the task demanded. The judgment then says prosecution wins on record-keeping while SUP-706 remains indeterminate.

## Case reasoning

SUP-708 also places Manual_Procurement_Approval after activation. That second CS-9 breach is missed. SUP-709 equates M0’s mandatory pre-decision quality check with CS-9. CS-9 requires quality before activation; a reject path with no activation can violate M0 and still be textually compatible with CS-9. SUP-712’s reversal test is wrong: a delegation record would not make one human into two. SUP-713’s mapping was approved a week after the case. SUP-715 separates poorly. The trace fits M0, CS-9’s different-human rule concerns requester and approver, and the proved gap is an unrecorded conflict disclosure, not a prohibited relationship. Dominant reasons are not stated where a mixed reading was available. Withdrawals, required in the scope rules, never appear.

Expiry handling is sound: SUP-705 and SUP-714 stay noncompliant, and SUP-704 is at least facially inside FD-7’s window, amount, monitoring, and timing facts.

## Model revision

`Operator.AND` is not a PM4Py process-tree operator, so the script is not executable. Concurrency in this API is an edgeless `StrictPartialOrder` or `Operator.PARALLEL`, and `Operator.LOOP` already exists. The claim that POWL has no loop is false. The substitute adds `loop_back → loop_entry`, which destroys a strict partial order, and it places `Request_More_Info` beside the approval choice rather than before it. One shared `activate` node is reused under two XOR branches. `checks_parallel` always precedes the decision, so the model cannot generate the quality-after-activation traces just classified as justified under PD-3 and FD-7. The declarative layer then relaxes sanctions for FD-7 (“0 minutes lag”). FD-7 keeps sanctions before activation. Aliases, delegation evidence, and emergency sign-off artifacts are missing from the event proposal. The overlay table also ignores the PD-3/FD-7 overlap from 10–12 July.

The layered split is the right idea, and the case labels are often in the right neighborhood. The reasoning and the proposed model are not accurate enough to enforce or to replace M0.
