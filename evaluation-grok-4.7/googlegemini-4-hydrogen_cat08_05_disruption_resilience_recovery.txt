The repeated “10–15×,” “5–7×,” and “2–3×” risk claims fold rework and cancellations into multipliers that fit only the traceability, cold-chain, and dual-control rates. Pattern D is also said to have caused the entire 12.5% rework rate, which the data do not isolate from skipped release checks and manual picking.

Frequency arcs are treated as a closed case ledger. The 650 “unaccounted” dispatches, 400 “unclosed” proofs of delivery, and 200 “still open” audit cases are differences between displayed arcs, not proven lost or open cases. The same answer ignores other imbalances, including 1,400 reconciliations versus 1,200 outgoing arcs and 750 stabilization cancellations versus the stated 3.0% rate.
