The write-up has the right six-part shape and correctly refuses a single point estimate, a sum of stage means, and an independence assumption. The calculated timelines are not reliable enough to carry that structure.

Chemistry is treated as two serial blocks totaling about 5 h, then a Monday 08:00 start. Two analysts are in parallel, the two in-service samples are in addition to the one urgent and three ordinary samples ahead, and FIFO is the more common discipline (about 55%). Under FIFO, B-417 is still behind that residual queue on Monday, so the smooth-path join is later than Monday 10:30. That case is only mentioned as sensitivity, not put into the central clock.

The QA wait is also mis-counted. Friday QA runs until 20:00, not 18:00, and the five batches already queued can be worked Friday evening and Saturday. Section 3 still has all five, plus arrivals, waiting at the Monday join, with no throughput removed and no Monday-morning arrivals added. The reported 5 h QA wait does not follow from the calendar given.

Scenario 3 is not a distinct delayed-microbiology case. No-authorization readout is shown to finish before chemistry, then given the same terminal times as the smooth path. The contamination confirmation of another 20–30 h of incubation, which can move the join to Tuesday or Wednesday, is never given its own clock. Scenario probabilities are also inconsistent with the ledger: no-authorization is about 55–75%, and a documentation query alone is 15–30%, but those scenarios are banded at 25–35% and 10–20%. A confirmed temperature excursion itself raises investigation probability to 0.30–0.55; the decomposition still uses about 0.10.

Section 4’s hours do not match its dates. From Friday 15:30, 86 h is about Tuesday 05:30, not Tuesday 10:00, and 96–110 h is Tuesday afternoon to early Wednesday, not Wednesday 10:00–Thursday 10:00. Those are the headline forecast numbers, and they disagree internally.

The excursion trigger, weekend gate, and use of a maximum at the join are sound. They do not offset the queue arithmetic, the collapsed microbiology scenario, or the hour-to-clock errors.
