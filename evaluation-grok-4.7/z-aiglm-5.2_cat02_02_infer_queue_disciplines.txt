4.5

The final write-up follows the required seven-part structure and is directionally right on QA (non-preemptive FIFO, with appointments only weakly overlaid) and QD (skill eligibility plus per-resource FIFO, not priority or SPT). Those conclusions are supported by real decision contrasts, especially A02 over A04 and D04 over D08. The rest of the document does not hold up under the decision-set standard it was asked to meet.

The QC reconstruction is factually wrong and then overconfident. C03 and C04 were already waiting at 10:10, and C05 (priority 1) was waiting when C03 and C04 were chosen; C02 before C03 falsifies priority as the primary rule. C04 runs 10:32–10:54 and C05 starts at 10:54, yet the diagnosis cites C05 starting at 10:32 as its strongest support. The 11:34 table still treats C04 as waiting, and the 11:40 contrast is C01 versus C07, not C01 versus C04. Priority, SPT, and earliest-deadline all pick C07 there; only an oldest-initial-backlog or FIFO story picks C01. Confidence 70 is therefore attached to a mechanism the cited episodes do not show. The second counterfactual is built on that nonexistent eligible set.

Other errors are smaller but compound. At 08:20, SPT selects A03 (10 < 20); the table’s “20<10” reversal hides the appointment/SPT confound. QA’s “contradicting” episode actually supports FIFO, and QD’s “contradicting” episode is later described as appointment compliance. On QB, B05 was already present when B03 was started at 09:48, and B01’s resume at 10:14 beats waiting B06; “P1 preemptive priority” does not survive those sets. Resume priority is also misused to explain B04 over B06 after B01 had already finished. The first counterfactual makes FIFO and priority agree, so it cannot discriminate them. Every `est_min` in the log equals realized duration, so the noisy-estimate exception is much weaker than presented. The D-F idle gap from 11:36 to the 12:00 meeting, despite D05 waiting and D10 fitting that gap, is never examined.

Format compliance and the two better queue diagnoses keep this above a failed attempt. The eligible-set mistakes, inverted discrimination test, and miscalibrated QC conclusion keep it well below a competent process-mining answer.
