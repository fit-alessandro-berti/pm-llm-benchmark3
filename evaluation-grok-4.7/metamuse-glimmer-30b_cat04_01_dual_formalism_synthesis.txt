4.5

The required eight-section structure is present, and several modeling choices are directionally right: risk as an XOR, supplier as an optional silent branch, release as a join, and T5/T6 as an approximation witness and a cross-branch violation. The delivered models and the claims about them do not hold up.

The process-tree program is not executable against the PM4Py parser. `parse_process_tree` recognizes `->`, `X`, `+`, and `*`, not `and`, `xor`, and `loop`, and the unquoted multi-word labels are not a reliable expression. The tree shape is also a stricter model than the write-up admits. Approvals and supplier consultation sit after the join of both `Plan Verification` and `Design Corrective Action`, so they wait on containment planning as well as design. The text only mentions a delay until design. Calling this a permissive over-approximation is backwards: delaying earliest start removes legal traces, so it is an under-approximation. The loop criticism is also wrong. A PM4Py loop `*(Execute→Inspect, Adjust)` performs `Adjust Parameters` only between iterations, so it does not place `Adjust Parameters` after the exiting inspection.

The POWL program violates the stated API and drops a core requirement. `OperatorPOWL` accepts only `XOR` and `LOOP`, and a loop needs exactly two children. The code builds `Operator.PARALLEL`, `Operator.SEQUENCE`, and a one-child loop, so it fails on construction. `Adjust Parameters` is never inserted, so repeat-until behavior is absent. T3 is nevertheless marked POWL-allowed, while T8 is marked forbidden; every trace containing `Adjust Parameters` is outside this model. The partial-order edges among register, containment, analysis, planning, design, approval, supplier, and release are a sound sketch, but they do not make the model faithful.

Section 6 repeats the same inversion: later approvals and a supplier that cannot overlap design are labeled underconstraint. Those are overconstraints. The validation plan then says to expect T1–T5 allowed, which contradicts T5’s role as a tree-forbidden approximation witness. The second self-critique is not a real defect: one XOR already yields at most one supplier consultation per case.
