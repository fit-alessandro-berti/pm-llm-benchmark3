8.2

The portfolio policy is coherent, auditable, and unusually careful about stage eligibility, label feedback, and interference. It still has several decision-level gaps that keep it short of nearly flawless.

The arithmetic that is shown checks. Segment bands are applied in the right direction and capped correctly. Midpoint effects 0.19, 0.24, and 0.26 sum to 0.69. Gross midpoint value is €17,500. Full false-alarm charges are €2,850 and swarm cost is €1,350, so the pre-spillover midpoint net of €13,300 is right. The upper-half bounds, the €147,778 break-even on \(W\), the single-nonresponse nets (€400 / €2,800 / €5,400), and the illustrative €20,000-average scenario all follow from the stated definitions. I-03 is correctly ineligible. I-05 is suppressed as a duplicate refresh while remaining first-swarm eligible. I-07 is reopened because of new evidence. Spillovers are carried into both breach counts and money, and the adverse case is labeled as a stress test rather than a known bound.

The main logical soft spot is the false-alarm term. Charging the full listed burden on every treated case is disclosed, including the alternative \((1-p^0)F\). That choice is then allowed to decide a case. I-08’s lower-end score of −€450 is an artifact of treating €5,000 as certain. Under the answer’s own alternative, with Strategic Enterprise risk nearer 0.31–0.43, expected false-alarm cost is roughly €2,850–€3,450, and the lower-end direct value turns positive by about €1,100–€1,700 before spillover. I-08 can still be deferred: its lower effect is only 0.05, one slot’s expected spillover is about 0.06–0.18 breaches, and eight hours remain. But the stated reason that it fails before any externality is not stable under the more coherent accounting, and that sensitivity is not shown.

The release rule is stricter than what is actually verified. Positive midpoint value at the upper base spillover requires \(W<€147{,}778\), yet \(W\) is unknown, and the table still marks I-07, I-09, and I-04 as intervene. Provisional language and a queue check repair some of this, but the selected portfolio is presented more firmly than the gate allows. The reserve has the same hole: holding it until 15:00 is a reasonable reading of management’s preference, but the forgone midpoint value of the next economic candidate, I-02 at about €2,700 and 0.15 breaches, is never set against even a rough arrival chance.

I-07 is the fragile selection. After a 45-minute swarm, about 45 minutes remain for resolution and validation, so the supplied 0.20–0.32 effect may be physically unavailable. Its midpoint direct margin is only €800, which one slot of spillover exceeds under commonplace queue values. At the lower effect and \(q=0.03\), net breaches from that slot are about 0.02. The answer states the €1,900 / 0.11 tradeoff against I-02 and calls the choice a fairness rule, but it does not say what happens if the owner cannot show a feasible post-swarm path. That fallback is the actual decision.

Calibration and segment uncertainty are discussed more than they are used. The euro scores depend only on supplied effects, cost, and the full false-alarm burden, so the SME and Strategic Enterprise bands do not change any rank. That is defensible only if those bands say nothing about effect error. The write-up itself says the workflow redesign may invalidate both predictions and effects, and that Non-Native-Language Support lacks a usable correction for either. I-09 is nevertheless ranked as a firm second economic slot on an unadjusted 0.18–0.30 range. I-06 is properly frozen because the missing queue field can both manufacture the jump and make another interruption harmful. I-09 does not receive a comparably wider hurdle.

Smaller unclarities remain. Alert-count wording on I-05 is slightly punitive in the table and correctly non-punitive in the temporal policy. Delay minutes and probability spillovers sit in one cell even though the net figures use only the probability channel. The steady-state “one of three” non-monetary lane is not tied back to today’s one protected slot plus two economic slots. Triage cases that will later enter the congested diagnosis queue are outside the six-case spillover term.

Section 6 is the strongest part: separated treated and untreated calibration, clustered repeated prefixes, interference-aware assignment, an explicit break-even effect, and portfolio-wide resolution as the governing outcome. Those safeguards do not remove the instability in the I-08 screen, the unverified \(W\) gate, or the under-specified I-07 commitment.
