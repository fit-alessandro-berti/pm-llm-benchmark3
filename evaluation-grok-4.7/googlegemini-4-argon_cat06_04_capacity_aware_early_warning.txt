6.5

The memo covers every required section and gets several hard calls right: I-03 is ineligible once Diagnosis Complete has passed, I-05’s unchanged refresh is suppressed while I-07’s new log re-enters and is then rejected on value and horizon, I-06 is held for imputation, I-08 is not bought with a €100k label, and one slot is reserved. The main scenario sums for the chosen trio are mostly reproducible. The decision rule is not actually followed, and several “auditable” figures do not recompute.

The allocation rule says a slot is used only when the Hurwicz score exceeds the €1,800 spillover hurdle and lower-bound direct net benefit is positive. I-01 scores €1,417, with base direct net benefit €1,685, so the marginal slot is about −€115 at the stated midpoint spillover (6 × 0.02 × €15,000). It is still assigned Slot 3. I-04 scores €4,683, clears both gates, and is deferred. Deferring a 10-hour triage case is reasonable; replacing it with a case that fails the hurdle is not. The claim that I-01 beats I-04 on downside is false on these numbers: I-04’s lower-bound direct net is about €2,050, versus €920 for I-01. Under the same 0.65/0.35 weights, the three-slot portfolio’s own base-to-adverse blend is about break-even to negative, which the case rule was written to avoid.

Calibration is mostly non-operative. Segment offsets are computed, then unused in \(\Delta p \times V - 450 - F\) except when \(\tilde{p}<0.45\) triggers an extra, arbitrary 0.75 factor. That knife-edge is what pushes I-04 and I-08, after the Strategic haircut, into a second penalty the supplied effect ranges already purport to represent. The false-alarm formula \(F(1-0.5\tilde{p})\) is defined and then ignored in favor of charging full \(F\) on every swarm.

Side scores presented as auditable are wrong: I-06’s unadjusted score is about €2,231, not €2,261; undiscounted I-04 is about €7,060, not €6,580; undiscounted I-08 is6.5

The answer is a serious portfolio policy with the right targets: stage eligibility, I-03 excluded, I-05 suppressed, I-07 reopened on new evidence, I-06 held for imputation, spillovers priced, and feedback loops given operational safeguards. It is not coherent enough, and several “auditable” figures are wrong.

The allocation contradicts the rule that is supposed to govern it. Section 1 allocates a slot only if \(S_i > €1{,}800\) and lower-bound direct net benefit is positive, unless an SLA-equity floor applies. I-01’s own score is \(S = €1{,}417\), with base direct net benefit \(€1{,}685\) against the stated per-slot spillover of about \(€1{,}800\). Marginal value is negative, yet I-01 is Slot 3. The equity floor is not what the write-up invokes, and it is a rolling quota with no binding history. I-01 also has 7 hours of slack, so the same option-value logic used to defer I-04 applies. The three-slot base result of about \(+€2{,}535\) is I-09 and I-02 subsidizing a value-destroying third slot. The text uses that spillover logic to reject a fourth slot and then does not apply it to the third.

Calibration is computed and then mostly unused. \(\mathrm{DENB} = \Delta p \times V - 450 - F\) does not depend on \(\tilde{p}\) except through the ad hoc \(\gamma = 0.75\) cut when \(\tilde{p} < 0.45\). That cut binds only for I-04 among cases where the calibration shift itself crosses 0.45, and I-04 is deferred for time, not for \(\gamma\). I-02’s rationale says net value survives the \(-0.085\) Strategic adjustment; that adjustment never enters I-02’s score. The repeat-alert gate is also not applied to I-02 or I-09, both second alerts, while it is applied strictly to I-05.

The scoring appendix is not reliable. Under the stated Hurwicz weights, I-06 is about \(€2{,}231\), not \(€2{,}261\); undiscounted I-08 is \(€2{,}800\), not \(€2{,}725\); undiscounted I-04 is \(€7{,}060\), not \(€6{,}580\). In Section 3 the selected-portfolio endpoints mostly check out, but the base gross band \(€9{,}100\text{–}€12{,}700\) and the breach band \(0.55\text{–}0.75\) are not derived. The hurdle itself rests on an untested \(\bar{V}_{\mathrm{queue}} = €15{,}000\) and an unexplained regulatory option value of \(€4{,}500\).

Elsewhere the machinery is uneven. \(\gamma\), \(\lambda = 0.65\), the 0.45 cutoff, and the 15% NNLS haircut are preference parameters presented as procedure. “Mondrian isotonic calibration” confuses two different methods. Boundary randomization is called a regression discontinuity. The effect plan does not deal with interference, even though spillover is the quantity that flips the portfolio. The fairness value blend is never used on these ten cases. “Three slots deployed at 12:00” is ambiguous with only two specialists; the later throttle note implies sequencing, but the capacity constraint is never stated.

Sections 4–6 are the strongest part: static refresh versus a new log, post-swarm lockout, label censorship with a positivity plan, segment calibration triggers, and a reserve-slot expiration rule. Those do not repair a live portfolio that breaks its own hurdle.
