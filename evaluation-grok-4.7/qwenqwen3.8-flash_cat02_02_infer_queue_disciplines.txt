The diagnosis is strongest on QA and QB, and the required seven-part structure, labeled assumptions, and low-to-moderate confidence scores are in place. FIFO-plus-appointment for QA and FIFO-plus-priority-1 preemption/resumption for QB are reconstructed from actual eligible sets, including the correct rejection of SPT at 08:20 (A04 is shorter than A03) and of pure priority when B01 resumes over B03 and B06. QC is appropriately treated as a low-confidence hybrid, and the data-collection plan is specific to the real ambiguities.

The decision-set method fails on QD. At 11:20, idle D-X can do electrical and mechanical work, so waiting D03 (electrical, arrived 11:03) and D04 (mechanical, arrived 11:05) are eligible; FIFO does not predict D06. That wrong baseline also hides a better D05 test: at 11:21, D05’s 30-minute estimate would finish about 11:51, before D-F’s 12:00 meeting, so the later “cannot fit” story is an after-effect of choosing D03, not an explanation of the whole delay.

The QB counterfactual is internally inconsistent: “priority-1 exceptions only” predicts B01 at 10:14, while the stated alternative prediction is B06, which requires full priority ranking. The QC aging component is not checked against 11:34, when C01 had already waited about 94 minutes and was skipped for C06, nor against the fact that starting C07 at 11:40 would still have met C07’s deadline. C06’s absence at 10:32 is correctly said to exceed the two-minute scan bound in one section and then treated as ordinary scan latency in another.
