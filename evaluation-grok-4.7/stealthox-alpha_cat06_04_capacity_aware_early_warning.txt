6.5

The memo gets the core traps right: I-03 is ineligible because the swarm is past `Diagnosis Complete`, I-05 is suppressed as a no-new-event repeat while I-07 re-enters on the new log but still fails the economic test, and untreated-queue spillover is inside the allocation rule rather than a footnote. Segment offsets, the imputation hold on I-06, the reserve slot, and the monitoring/fairness machinery are substantive. It is not nearly flawless, because the deployable rule and the portfolio that follows it do not match, and several audited numbers are wrong or unjustified.

The decision rule requires a case to clear pessimistic absolute-risk-reduction economics and to beat spillover, and after two slots the next slot must beat 1.5× spillover. The selected book does not obey that rule. I-09, I-02, and I-06 are approved on midpoint break-evens (about €29.6k, €22.5k, and €22k). At the low end of the given effect ranges those direct values fall to roughly €2,350, €950, and €1,500, so they lose to base spillover at any plausible queue value. I-08 is kept as a live trigger even though its lower-bound effect makes direct value negative (0.05 × €100,000 − €450 − €5,000). Slot 3 also fails the stated congestion brake: at the €20k queue value used later, 1.5× spillover is about €3,600 against I-06’s €2,625 direct value.

Section 3 then changes the queue-value assumption. Section 2’s own prior is the €29.2k portfolio average; at that figure the base three-slot net is negative (about €8.9k direct net minus about €10.5k spillover). The table instead centers €20k and headlines about +€1.7k base and +€1.3k probability-weighted. The sensitivity text admits the sign flip, but the summary still treats the favorable center as the result. Adverse accounting is also off-spec: I-02 at 0.08 is the stated floor, but I-09 is scored at 0.10 against a supplied floor of 0.18, with no stated haircut. The €10.8k note is consistent with 3 × 6 × 0.03 × €20k, yet the 0.36 breach figure is not derived and collides with the base row’s 3 × 6 × 0.02.

Factual and operational slips add up. I-06 is `Diagnosis Started`, not Triage, so the imputation delay sits inside the only stage where the swarm still works. I-02’s eligibility is treated as expiring at the 15:00 SLA rather than at `Diagnosis Complete`. I-07 is already on the 90-minute buffer at 12:00, so the 12:20 reserve checkpoint cannot authorize it under the same rule. The €50k value cap in the fairness section is not applied to today’s scores; applied at midpoint it would wipe out I-08. The 25% equity floor is concrete but can force slots that fail the spillover test and dump delay back onto the same low-value queue. Non-native-language effect uncertainty is discussed, then ignored in the ARR used to give I-09 the first slot.

Midpoint direct-value arithmetic for the ten cases is largely right, and sections 4–6 are specific enough to operate. The grade stops there because a policy whose gates, brake, central externality, and adverse effects do not survive its own arithmetic is not decision-theoretically coherent.
