6.5

The pipeline covers every required section, the 17 events, the 12 objects, the mandated O2O links, validations, and the requested PM4Py calls. The flattened traces for O200, Package, Delivery, and Invoice match the relations, and the payment-flattening critique is sound.

The construction still breaks its own source-versus-derived rule. Events 6 and 7 are stated business facts (“operationally shared” / “relates to both Orders”), yet both Order links are tagged `_derived`. Filtering derived rows would delete explicit observations. The stated qualifier vocabulary also disagrees with the code: `involved_order_derived` is never declared, and `delivered_package` is implemented as `delivered_package_derived`.

The audit table and ET–OT discussion treat `Order_derived` and `Package_derived` as object types. They are qualifiers; the types remain `Order` and `Package`.

The O100 trace is listed with 12 events and then called “~11.” The Order DFG notes the Pack self-loop but omits the same directly-follows self-loops on Add Item, Dispatch Package, and Confirm Delivery. The object-centric net claims Pack requires two Order tokens, two Item tokens, and one Package token, which cannot fit event 8 (one order, one item, one package) under one activity. Calling M2’s 150 a completion that “closes” a 250 invoice invents an invoice total the narrative never gives.
