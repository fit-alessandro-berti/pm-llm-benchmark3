3.5

The write-up has the requested six-part shape and does name parallelism, calendars, and path branches, but the forecast is not defensible. Clock arithmetic, closing rules, and the author’s own scenarios contradict each other, and the headline timing is impossible.

The earliest completion, Sunday 09:30–15:30 (42–48 h), cannot occur. Incubation started Friday 14:00, so technical readiness is Sunday 12:00–20:00 (46–54 h), not Sunday 12:00–22:00 and not Sunday morning. QA has no Sunday hours, and electronic release follows QA acceptance. Chemistry is closed after 18:00 and all weekend, with both analysts busy and an urgent sample plus three ordinary samples ahead, so a Friday finish is not available and a Sunday result is not available. Earliest release sits on Monday morning, about 65 h out, only on a fast risk-priority partial start.

Chemistry timing is internally impossible. Service is run through 18:30–19:30 after the laboratory has closed, then the same work is treated as resuming Monday and finishing 10:30–11:30. That both violates the pause rule and double-counts service. In-process residuals (0.4–0.9 h and 1.2–2.0 h) are treated as if they were the urgent sample. FIFO versus risk-priority is given a 1–2 h sensitivity; missing Friday start under FIFO moves the branch by several hours. The rerun case then finishes Monday 09:30–11:30, earlier than the smooth path, and does not raise the deviation probability to 0.30–0.55.

Weekend authorization is ranked as a 48–60 h driver and as the update that moves the terminal time between Monday and Tuesday/Wednesday. In the decomposition, both the authorized and unauthorized cases join at Monday 10:30–11:30 because chemistry is later, so authorization changes nothing. The real staffing gap is from Sunday readiness to Monday 08:00, about 12–20 h, and only if microbiology is the slower branch. On-call work is specified as 1–3 h of dispatch plus readout, not an immediate 0.5–1.5 h readout. Section 2 gives delayed microbiology a 15–25% band; the complement of 0.25–0.45 is about 55–75%. The overlap note reverses the authorization condition.

The QA queue uses 0.35/h over about 16 h and lands on 10–12 batches. Arrivals exist only in opening hours: Friday 15:30–20:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00, about 8.5 h, and one reviewer is on duty in both windows. Saturday clearing is never applied. Arrivals are called Poisson while overdispersion is acknowledged in the same cell. A documentation query is specified to add 4–12 working hours and then return to QA; the second review and release are omitted, and short queries that can finish Monday before 20:00 are placed on Tuesday.

Contamination time is started at primary readiness rather than at the readout that discovers it, so weekend staffing does not shift the start of the extra 20–30 h. The assumption ledger calls the temperature excursion independent and says there is no evidence of dependence unless a rerun or contamination occurs. The process states the opposite: confirmation raises both probabilities. Section 5 then treats that link as a main driver, so the ledger and the ranking disagree. Rejection is listed once and then given an unsupported 20–30% complement via a 70–80% release probability.

Section 4’s hour ranges do not match its clock labels (66 h is Monday 09:30, not Monday 14:00; 90 h is Tuesday 09:30, not Tuesday 12:00; 240 h is not the following Friday 12:00). Smooth-path terminals differ across the scenario table (Monday 09:00–10:30), the decomposition (Monday 14:00–16:00), and the summary (Sunday). Those are not uncertainty bands; they are inconsistent calculations.
