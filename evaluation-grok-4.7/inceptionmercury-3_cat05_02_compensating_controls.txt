7.5

The case judgments are mostly sound: nominal four-eyes failure is separated from objective failure, AP-204 is failed despite distinct users, AP-205 is stopped before completion, AP-206 stays indeterminate, AP-208 is failed despite a legitimate payment, and AP-209 is not treated as clean. The equivalence test, hybrid pre-settlement manager semantics, and alert-versus-proof monitoring rules are substantive. The required structure and PM4Py fallback are present.

Deductions are for concrete defects, not tone. AP-207’s “missing” bank-settlement timestamp is in the log at 2026-05-04T16:00:00Z. AP-201 is stated as outside a strict recall window because audit is 25 hours after release, without checking settlement at 10:00, which is about 23 hours before audit. Section 2 calls a post-release, pre-settlement manager only partially equivalent, then AP-207 is OBJECTIVE_MET with no reconciliation to ED-1 condition 6. Severity is incoherent: unrecovered related-party loss in AP-202 is High, while the legitimate EUR 900,000 outage case is Critical, and no scale is defined. “High” confidence on AP-204 blends a proven fabricated receipt with unproven collusion.

The code is not a reliable evidence layer. AP-206’s uncertain token becomes NO_EMERGENCY_TOKEN_PREP. m21 is coded independent although that is not given, and absent managers are coded not independent. Every warning is mapped to warning_overridden, and the 180-day vendor condition is omitted. Smaller flaws include the “unconlicted” typo, “recalled window,” a muddled shared-workstation rule, and counterfactuals that sometimes substitute an independent manager for an independent second releaser.
