The write-up is complete and several easy calls are right, but the authority traps that the facts are built around are mishandled, and the code does not implement the rules the narrative states.

Correct or defensible: CHG-502 and CHG-508 are real drafter/approver breaches after alias normalization; CHG-507 is an unauthorized high-risk approval by Eva before any QM delegation and before her later role change; CHG-510’s approval is unauthorized because D-IC-04 is a prohibited, email-only subdelegation and Clara also drafts; CHG-501 is a valid Ben substitution on production equipment while Asha is on leave, with the 9 August PDF able to support an effective start despite the 12 August migration row; CHG-503 is rightly not treated as proven self-verification. Material errors:

- CHG-509 is marked a high-confidence valid substitution. D-QM-02 requires Asha to be absent. Her recorded leave ends 14 August. 15 August is only a weekend, and the log shows the site processing a high-risk change that day. Scope and interval match; the absence condition does not. `VALID_SUBSTITUTION` with no missing evidence is not supportable. `INDETERMINATE` or `UNAUTHORIZED` is the event-time result.
- CHG-505 says D-QM-01 is valid. That delegation is QM approval for production-equipment changes only. CHG-505 is a cooling-system emergency, and `Retrospective_QM_Review` is not that approval. The matrix even limits Ben to production equipment, then the case row contradicts it. Lateness can still support `UNAUTHORIZED`: approval is Tuesday 11 August, and under the answer’s own “next business day is day 1” rule the second business day is Thursday 13 August, while the review is Friday 14 August. The label can survive; the delegation analysis cannot. The reviewer is also on sick leave, which the row treats as a side issue rather than an identity problem. The case row never shows that the log does not name CI-44, so Farid’s scope match is assumed.

The delegation instrument facially covers time, production-equipment scope, and Asha’s absence at 13:00. The actor does not. That is `INDETERMINATE` or unauthorized-by-unknown-user, not a proven breach by Ben with intact SoD.
- CHG-504 accepts Asha as the actor at high confidence because a VPN session overlaps 09:22. The same paragraph records that security cannot exclude credential sharing. Ambiguous identity is graded as settled authority.
- CHG-503’s SoD cell states that Diego verified his own implementation. Badge-into-a-shared-room, no re-authentication, and no CCTV do not reliably map `svc_qms` to him. Raw resources differ, so technical four-eyes passes; the control objective is unmet until a human is identified. Those are different findings, and the answer collapses them. CHG-510’s timely Ben retrospective review has the same out-of-scope cooling problem and is omitted.

Rule section: event-time role versus the current master is never formalized, despite Eva being the planted example. The PDF-versus-later-database position is sound. The business-day rule is one acceptable reading but is not applied to an actual deadline. “A principal VPN session suspends absence only for that act” is stated as a rule without tying it to the possibility that the session was not Asha.

Code: alias normalization before four-eyes is right, and hard-coding CHG-503 to Diego makes an uncertain judgment automatic. Interval checks ignore principal absence, risk, sick-leave identity, subdelegation, and the signed-versus-database effective time. They never evaluate `Retrospective_QM_Review` or two business days, so CHG-505’s real RR4 failure cannot appear. As written, CHG-506 and CHG-509 pass as valid delegations, while low-risk CHG-502 and CHG-508 are printed as invalid approvals even though RR3 does not apply. `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is computed and dropped. Handover and working-together networks are printed and never interpreted.

Controls and conclusion cover the requested topics, then overstate the result: sick-leave account use and “credential compromise” are treated as established authority failures. The better priority order is CHG-506’s actor, CHG-509’s unmet absence condition, and CHG-505/510 retrospective authority and timing, with CHG-503 attribution and CHG-504’s VPN session beside them.
