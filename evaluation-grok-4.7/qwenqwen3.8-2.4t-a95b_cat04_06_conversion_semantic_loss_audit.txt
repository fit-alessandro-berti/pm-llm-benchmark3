7.5

The final document meets the required nine-section structure and is strong on source semantics, pipeline choice, failure handling, visual ambiguity, and the refusal to treat BPMN as authoritative. The partial-order reading, loop/redo rule, optional silent branch, and release join are correct, and the witness set actually targets the risky interleavings.

It still overclaims. The audit marks many BPMN round-trip cells `validated preserved` even though nothing was executed; those cells should be `expected preserved` or `not tested`. `conversion failed` is likewise asserted for the process-tree route as an outcome rather than only as an expected result. The validator can also mislead: set differences are reported even when either exploration is truncated, silent-firing budgets can differ between the source net and a BPMN reimport and create false mismatches, and a `None` return from `convert_to_process_tree` is not handled. The BPMN helper also swallows the first conversion exception and retries other signatures. Those are concrete audit and code defects, not presentation issues.
