7.4

The diagnosis matches the evidence: April is a schema and pipeline failure, June is a routing-mix change with stable path-conditional breach rates, August–September combine a parts-delay shock with a September population shift, and October is dominated by immature labels. The staged response and the rule that a maturity alarm must block retraining are proportionate. The score is held down by concrete errors in identification, falsification, and the required code.

The concept-drift falsifier is confused. A flat breach-versus-wait gradient does not refute concept drift; a uniform rise inside wait bins would support a residual change in P(Y|X). “Delay-adjusted risk unchanged” would indicate performance mediation, not a “delay-censoring artifact.” Censoring and cycle time are separate mechanisms, and the answer otherwise keeps them apart. Section 2 then states as fact that V3 breach rose “beyond what wait alone predicted historically,” which the supplied tables do not show. Section 3 correctly treats that residual as untested.

The within-V3 claim that predicted risk is about 0.19 against an observed 0.64 misuses the overall mean score. Baseline ECE near 0.02 with a V3 base rate near 0.32 implies that scores already differed by eventual path. The transport test is also unreliable: expecting reweighted September risk to fall to 0.19–0.20 ignores August’s already elevated rate at zero Partner Z share, and the sentence beginning “non-Z Sep overall Sep” does not state a checkable result. June is labeled high-confidence covariate drift even though the measured change is post-diagnosis variant mix; that is process mix, and P(X) at diagnosis is not shown to have moved. Matured-variant mixtures for August and September imply roughly 0.31 and 0.34, against reported overall rates of 0.28 and 0.31, and that gap is never reconciled. The January–June parts edge moving from 28 h to 42 h is flattened into “normal.”

The code sketch uses real PM4Py calls and does not invent a drift detector, but it does not implement the requested case-start cohort. `traces_contained` keeps only traces that both start and end inside the month. With label delays of 33–52 days, that selects short cases and drops most V3 traces. `format_dataframe` renames `case_id` to `case:concept:name`, so `cal["case_id"]` fails. `discover_performance_dfg` returns a float per edge, normally the mean, so `.get("median")` fails when the edge exists. Deduping on `activity_raw` also leaves the same diagnosis stored under both the old and new names.

The monitoring gates, month-by-month split, and four action-risk scenarios are strong enough that the conclusion is still usable. The falsifier, the V3 score claim, and the cohort code are not.
