The required seven-section structure is present, and the analytical framework is genuinely strong: rounding, censoring, and missing starts are separated; open work is not dropped; S1 queue, busy time, and WIP reconcile; most fully observed case waits are right; and the three-scenario sensitivity design is coherent.

The final numbers are not reliable enough for a higher score. P13’s completed sojourn is 210 minutes, not 220, so the repeated completed-case mean of 147.1 is wrong. Both open S2 services are wrong: P15 has 30 elapsed minutes and P17 has 5, not 25 each. That error inflates S2 busy time from 542.5 to 557.5 minutes and distorts C1 versus C2 utilization.

The station bins contradict the data and the stated boundary rule. S2 does not have 11 completions in 10:00–12:00 and zero in 12:00–14:00; P10, P11, P12, P13, and P14 all finish after noon. The reported 10:00–12:00 S2 service mean of 23.4 is also a partial-sum division error. Section 5 then treats that false zero-throughput afternoon as bottleneck evidence, despite a later remark that the last completion is at 13:50.

Hourly snapshots miss P03 in the S2 queue at 09:00 and misstate 13:00 WIP. The Little’s Law check uses queue area 90 rather than 105, so the claimed 1.31-versus-1.38 agreement is overstated. Several of these prose figures would not be reproduced by the accompanying code, which computes open elapsed time correctly but can fail before printing if `pm4py.project` is unavailable.
