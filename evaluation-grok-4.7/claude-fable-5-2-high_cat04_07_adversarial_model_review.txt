The review is unusually strong: the metric autopsy, probe matrix, and third model are mostly right, and several quantitative claims check out exactly (130, 36, and 20 traces; 48/39/46/7 label counts; 580 language traces of length ≤ 12; per-trace fitness 0.889, 0.909, 0.727, 0.80, and 0.75). It is not nearly flawless.

The decision-relevant mistake is in §7.4. B6 does not become `CONFORMING` once the affected-check attribute matches Technical Review. With `sourcing is None`, D1 still returns `UNDETERMINED`, so the verdict stays `CONFORMING_UNCERTIFIED`. If the same trace is external, missing Supplier Review is a D1 `VIOLATION` regardless of the re-check. The §8 row (“decided by D2 once data exists”) repeats that oversimplification. The “[reviewer check]” tag makes the overclaim look verified.

“The gap between the two is exactly the REVIEW zone” is false. Rule-clean traces outside the backbone are only part of that zone. Traces that `policy_checks` itself marks `REVIEW` (H6b, H8, H9, H9b) are also in it.

The validation skeletons are not reliable enough for the “executed end to end” claim. `PARAM_TRACE_COST_FUNCTION` is passed a positional list; PM4Py looks up costs by activity name, so the duplicate-tolerant profile is silently ignored. Even a correct dict would cheapen every Correct Dossier log move, not a doubled event. `check_soundness(...)[0]` treats a boolean return as a sequence, and `check_is_workflow_net` is not the documented entry point. `activities_not_executable` checks label presence, not reachability.

Smaller overstatements: token-based generalization does not average in never-fired transitions (`1/sqrt(0)`); B8 at 0.889 does not clear a 0.90 threshold on Model S; the training residual of Model S is only conjectured to be rework. The DECLARE dictionary is rightly demoted, but a wrong `noncoexistence` key would make that layer fail closed rather than degrade visibly.

What keeps the score here is the actual governance reasoning. Model S’s skip-versus-ordering split, Model C’s memorized correlations and equal 0.75 scores for B3 and B5, the refusal to optionalize mandatory checks, the explicit uncertified state, P1–P7 and T1–T8, the rejection rule, and the dissent (about 208 words, and genuinely damaging) are all sound.
