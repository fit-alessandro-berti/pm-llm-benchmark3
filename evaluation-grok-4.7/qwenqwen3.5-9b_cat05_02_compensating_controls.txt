5.5

The nine headline classifications are mostly defensible and avoid a mechanical same-user/distinct-user mapping, but the supporting analysis, table semantics, and code contain repeated factual and logical errors.

Section 2 never defines fully equivalent, partially equivalent, merely mitigating, and ineffective, so the required sufficiency test is missing. Section 1 does not say, for each threat, which of AV-2, MG-4, RA-7, and ED-1 prevents, detects, corrects, or fails. ED-1 is treated as preventing single-actor fraud; it is a conditional permission to allow that pattern. Collusion and compromised-account control are largely omitted despite being explicit AV-2 gaps.

In the case table, AP-204 is given an MG-4 review that does not exist in the log, and collusion is stated as fact although it is denied and the two-person cell design is unknown. Corrective capacity is repeatedly filled with loss size. Overridden or unavailable AV-2 and ED-1 itself are listed as preventive controls. Pre-settlement manager review is called hybrid in section 4, then omitted from the preventive column for AP-201 and AP-207. Section 4 is not a table, and approval before release is wrongly described as stopping initiation.

Section 5 reads “which control breaks the causal chain” as “which control failed,” including on cases where loss was avoided. AP-204’s exposure is called an unknown amount although it is EUR 220,000, and AV-2 is said to have caught a fabricated receipt, contradicting the stated coverage limit. AP-206’s logging-delay ambiguity is flattened into a definite ED-1 breach.

The code misses the required bank-change flag, treats any emergency token as ED-1 eligibility, records AP-202’s audit as two days rather than about eight, and invents manager timing for AP-204 and AP-209 that also contradicts the prose. `format_dataframe` is called with nonexistent argument names, and the claim that standard PM4Py has no `filter_four_eyes_principle` is false; the default keep-versus-violate behavior is not handled. Alert thresholds are arbitrary, and control breaches are treated as proof of misconduct.
