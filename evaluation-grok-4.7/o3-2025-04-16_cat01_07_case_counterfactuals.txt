The write-up keeps the required section structure, preserves four-eyes and post-assessment compliance, and correctly treats K-337 as a decision-policy change and K-104’s C1 reservation as the intervention that fails to scale. Those strengths do not offset systematic failures of temporal consistency, which are the core of the task.

Across all four cases, the summary table, the propagation narrative, and the best/base/worst figures describe different clocks. K-104’s finish window (Wed 11:30–14:00) does not span the stated −4 h to −9 h, and a Tuesday 08:00 C1 skim is incompatible with the Tuesday snapshot’s 16 h compliance queue. K-219’s table ends the case Wed 03:30–06:00, while the derivation leaves assessment at Tue 18:30 and the manager at Wed 08:30; that misses the binding gate, so earlier document readiness can leave notify time unchanged. The −2 h to −5 h band also does not match those clock times. K-337 is worse: Fri 18:30 versus the following Wed 09:10 is on the order of 110 h, not −56 h, a 5 h queue from Fri 10:30 does not start at 14:00, and Mon 09:10, Mon 17:30–21:00, and −36/−44/−56 h cannot be the same scenario. Monday morning is also treated as a smaller saving than Monday evening. K-411 starts D2 at 10:00 against a 4 h backlog at 08:00, then reports notify at 13:30 (−1 h), a table finish of 10:00–11:30, and a −3 h to −7 h range, with a best case at 07:00 outside the assumed day.

Scale quantities inherit those errors: avoided K-337 work is about 3.5 h of D1 and 2.5 h of A1, not 12 h and 8 h, and compliance is moved rather than removed, so “1 000 h” and “−8 h C1” are unsupported. “300 h reserved versus 40 h available” also conflates earlier commitment of reviews the baseline already required with net new load. The 100-case discussion is only about 200 words, short of the required 250–400, and barely addresses timestamp fabrication versus true concurrency or external-response delay. Assumption 2 (no work outside 08:00–20:00) contradicts both the factual overnight document check and the K-219 night shift. The 25%, 60/30/10, +15%, and CV 0.6 figures are invented precision. K-411 is not timing-only; it changes task enabling. The ranking and pilot rows are conceptually pointed in a defensible direction, but the hour claims they rest on are not reliable.
