6.0

The normalization framework is sound and most of the hard interval work is right, but the published conformance table is not reliable. Several temporal-profile labels contradict the answer’s own intervals and its own two-sided rule, and at least one duration is arithmetically wrong.

Clock direction, ordering of resolution before offset, and the resulting UTC intervals are largely correct. HUB ±1.5 s, OPS-BER floor-minute plus [−90, +120] s, SENSOR nearest-10 s plus ±5 s, VENDOR-NY EDT, MOBILE-LON BST, and the T-404 Minkowski start [10:09:58.5Z, 10:17:01.5Z] all check out. T-405 is the strongest piece: all-CEST, all-CET, and AR-on-CET are impossible under ingestion order, and only AR-CEST plus IS/CC-CET survives, at [00:49:00Z, 00:51:59.999Z], [01:04:00Z, 01:06:59.999Z], and [01:41:00Z, 01:43:59.999Z]. Entry delay is stated as an occurrence shift and is not silently folded into the clock bound. Primary SLA calls at tolerance 0 are right, including the certain violations T-402 S1 [958.5, 1231.5] s, T-408 S2 [3297, 3303] s, and T-408 S3 [138297, 138303] s.

Profile classification then breaks. An interval wholly inside mean ± ζ·std is a certain pass; these rows violate that rule:

- T-404 AR→CC [3297, 3303] is inside [1980, 3420] at ζ=1, not a possible or certain violation.
- T-405 AR→CC [2940, 3300], T-406 AR→CC [3117, 3123], and T-407 AR→CC are inside the ζ=1 band. T-407’s lower end is also inconsistent: the capped receipt gives about [2277, 3303], not [2097, 3303].
- T-408 AR→CC [3897, 3903] is inside [1260, 4140] at ζ=2, so that cell is a certain pass. ζ=1 violation and ζ=3 pass are right.
- T-409 AR→CC [2097, 2103] is 117 s above 1980, not a ζ=1 boundary overlap.
- T-404 IS→CC [2277, 2703] exceeds the ζ=1 cap of 2700. The same 3 s overhang is correctly called a possible S2 breach and then called a certain profile pass.
- T-405 AR→IS max 1080 s is inside [60, 1140] at ζ=3.
- T-406 IS→CC [1948.5, 2911.5] is wholly inside both the ζ=2 band [900, 3300] and the ζ=3 band. “PASS-leaning” is not a permitted class.

T-401 S3 is numerically wrong. From CC [08:51:00Z, 08:51:20Z] and Close [06-02 08:59:58.5Z, 09:00:01.5Z] the duration is [86918.5, 86941.5] s, not [86938.5, 87061.5]. The certain pass is unaffected, but the interval was not produced by the stated Minkowski rule.

The tolerance matrix reaches the right SLA conclusions and the T-406 / T-409 assumption flips are real, yet many cells argue both classifications at once (“POSSIBLE … CERTAIN_PASS”). Profile sensitivity repeats the bad labels and misses flips that do occur, notably T-405 AR→IS at ζ=3 and T-406 IS→CC at ζ=2. Section 6 covers the main SLA possibles and separates sync, missing-activity, duration, and semantic evidence, but it does not do that for each possible or indeterminate profile result.

The code’s normalizers and `classify_profile` would correct many of the prose errors, and the midpoint log is explicitly lossy. That also means the written findings were not reconciled with the implementation. The DST search is unused. PM4Py is invoked with a version hedge, but the answer never says that conformance only scores directly-follows pairs, so AR→CC in the supplied profile is not what that call checks. Governance is specific and usable: UTC retention, raw strings, offset telemetry, fold bits, sent/received and start/complete separation, and no punitive use of mere overlap.
