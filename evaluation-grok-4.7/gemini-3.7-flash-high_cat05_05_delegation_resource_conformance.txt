The write-up is structured, covers every required heading, and gets several hard calls right: alias normalization on CHG-508, Eva’s event-time role on CHG-507, the void D-IC-04 subdelegation and RR1 breach on CHG-510, Farid’s D-IC-03 approval on CHG-505, the signed-PDF versus later QMS-row treatment of D-QM-01, Tuesday-to-Thursday business-day counting that makes the 14 Aug review late, and a non-conclusive reading of the Clara–Ben concentration under RR6. Those strengths do not offset decision-critical mistakes.

CHG-509 is cleared as a high-confidence valid substitution. D-QM-02 requires the principal to be absent. Asha’s leave ends 14 Aug; 15 Aug is only a weekend, and weekend is not recorded absence or formal unavailability. The approval is therefore unauthorized or indeterminate. The text simply asserts “principal absent.”

Both cooling retrospective reviews are mishandled. D-QM-01 is production-equipment QM approval only. Ben has no standing QM role and no cooling scope, so his reviews on CHG-505 and CHG-510 are not valid QM delegations. Section 2 states the scope test and Section 3 never applies it. CHG-505 is also over-weighted on sick leave: the file denies the CHG-506 approval specifically, gives no IP for the 14 Aug review, and does not make sick leave an automatic loss of delegated authority. The “logged in remotely” alternative is invented.

Identity findings violate the problem’s own constraint that ambiguous identity has no canonical answer. CHG-506 is written up as a high-confidence third-party impersonation. CHG-504 is high-confidence authorized, and credential sharing is described as refuted, although security cannot exclude it. Nominal QM authority if the actor was Asha should have stayed conditional.

CHG-508 is used as an example of Clara approving Ben’s drafts. In the log, Ben drafts as `b.koch` and approves as `u_bk`. The four reciprocal training approvals are background pattern, not this case.

The required code does not implement the analysis. Under ordinary pandas behavior, `true`/`false` remain strings, and a non-empty string is truthy, so every HIGH approval is forced down the emergency branch and valid QM approvals are flagged as RR4 failures. `requires_principal_absence` is stored and never tested, so CHG-509 would still pass if that bug were fixed. Retrospective reviews are never evaluated. CHG-503’s weak workstation evidence is hard-mapped to Diego and then printed as an RR2 violation. `resource_key=` is passed as a keyword to PM4Py helpers whose usual signature accepts only `parameters`, so the block is likely not executable. The different-resources filter is computed and discarded.

Secondary labels often blur a true authority failure into a data problem (CHG-502, CHG-507). The executive split assigns false precision, parks a plain self-approval in the identity bucket, and omits the missed CHG-509 and cooling-scope failures. Pair rotation is only implied by a dyad alert. “48 business hours” does not match the business-day rule defined earlier.
