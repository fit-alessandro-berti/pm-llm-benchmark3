8.1

The construction is semantically faithful and the pipeline is largely complete, but a few analytical claims are over-specific or loosely reasoned, so this is short of nearly flawless.

The in-memory model matches the narrative. All 12 objects and all 17 UTC events are present. Amounts stay on `E12` and `E15`. E2O coverage follows the stated policy: `E06` links `I1`, `I3`, `O100`, and `O200`; `E07` adds `P1`; `E08` links only `P2` and `I2`; dispatch, confirmation, and payments are not pushed onto orders. The 34 E2O rows and 12 O2O rows are referentially consistent, and the validation checks cover the required integrity conditions. Flattened traces and DFG frequencies are right, including O100’s single `Add Item → Add Item` edge, I2’s skip of `Reserve Stock`, shared copies of `E06`, `E07`, and `E11`, the 3-hour versus 22-hour-45-minute delivery gap, and the empty Payment DFG. The propagation, inflation, and double-counting warnings are concrete, including the incorrect path from `D2` to `O200` and the 500-versus-250 payment total.

The score is held down by Section 5. The Order–Order “weight 1” is justified by three shared events, while the Order–Package “weight 2” is justified by two pairs in one event. Those explanations do not define one aggregation. Interaction edges and the Invoice → Payment descendant edge are also presented as one OTG, although those are different graphs, and the O2O settlement direction correctly runs the other way. “The tested build marks multiple-object participation” treats an unexecuted miner’s variable arcs as observed output. The “54 rows” figure is arithmetically consistent only with a bidirectional per-event pair grain that `ocel_objects_interactions_summary` is not shown to use. Calling both invoice payments divergence is loose: the repeated label is real invoice behavior. Qualifiers are stable, but `dispatch_delivery` and the overloaded `involved_order` are coarser than the rest of the vocabulary. The timestamp check through `Series.map` can also fail on dtype even when the values match.
