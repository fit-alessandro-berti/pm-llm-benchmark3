7.5

The required seven-section structure is complete, and the redesign is genuinely multi-criteria: air and dedicated vans are split by segment and slack, Critical-downtime service is protected, rework, returns, obsolescence, SP1/SP2/Q1, and R1 are carried into a phased package with displacement checks. Most path statistics, the weighted baselines (~10.3 kgCO2e, ~7.4 kWh, ~0.35 kg scrap), and the rebound set are correctly grounded.

The grounding is not clean enough for a high score. Packaging rework is stated as 550 while the same sentences list 260 + 360 inflows (620); 550 is the outflow sum, mislabeled three times. Batch wait is reversed: 3,020 cases enter `Batch wait` and 2,910 leave it, yet the text says 2,910 of 3,780 enter. CNC_A’s 1.3 kWh/item is measured at batch size 12–20, but both the option and the effects table assign that figure to a size target of 8–12. The 760 emergency-production starts are treated as Made-to-order Regular over-promising, although the path evidence ties emergency production to Critical-downtime. Critical performance is also mixed internally (segment 15 h / 4% late versus the van path’s 14 h / 3% late).

Smaller logic slips remain. Standard-stock Service-contract speed is called picking-driven even though air cuts about 24 h and the stock air versus ground medians are 17 h versus 34 h. Returns are said to outweigh or dwarf doubled packaging despite the boundary statement that return legs are unevidenced. The effects table overstates partial-shipment savings as removing second legs for all 1,010 partials, and rework is described as routinely re-consuming heat-treatment energy. These are material attribution errors, not cosmetic ones, even though the final package would still be directionally defensible if the counts were corrected.
