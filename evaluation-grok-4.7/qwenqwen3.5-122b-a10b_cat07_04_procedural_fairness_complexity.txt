6.5

The required eight-part structure is complete, the five constraints are respected, and the split conclusion (procedural burden for B and C versus indeterminate outcome disparity for D) is directionally defensible. It is not nearly flawless.

The report misstates path durations: 19.2 and 14.9 days are variant medians, not time added by language support or document requests. It treats a +8.0 percentage-point supervisor-review gap as “8% higher,” calls other groups’ V4 share “1%,” and reads the supervisor wait (explicitly tied to a two-region backlog) as priority or implicit bias. Dropping `risk_score_band` or `documentation_completeness` and seeing a larger gap does not identify those covariates as biased; that sensitivity test cannot separate legitimate confounding, mediation, and measurement bias. Comparing V1 across groups also ignores selection into the straight path.

The most important residual is underused: Group B is still +9.5 percentage points more likely to be sent for missing documents after documentation completeness is controlled. The 57% language-need share versus 24% on V4, and the fact that two of five accommodation specialists also supervise, are likewise missed. Section 1 treats policy-required path differences as routing inconsistency and mixes raw outcome parity with adjusted disparity. “Violates the principle of timeliness in administrative justice” is stronger than evidence that shows long support queues and no stated service standard. The ownership redesign does not face its obvious side effect: putting whole cases on the same seven bilingual workers can deepen the queue.

The PM4Py sketch names the requested technique families, but several calls are wrong or non-operational: `format_dataframe` on a log, `read_event_log` rather than the dataframe/XES API, invented `start_event`/`end_event` arguments, `filter_variants(['V4'])` using a table label rather than an activity sequence, an unused filter, and a pooled log skeleton that cannot show group-specific violations. Handover discovery is not connected to roles, so it cannot identify the generalist–specialist ping-pong the text claims to test.
