7.5

The reconstruction is coherent and most of the hard arithmetic checks out: feasible windows for P05, P07, P11, and P15; central imputations; completed sojourns (mean 2050/14 ≈ 146, median 145); half-open subperiod counts and rates; imputation-invariant WIP (peak \(L_1=8\), \(L_2=7\), system 13; \(\bar L_1\approx 5.79\), \(\bar L_2\approx 3.72\), \(\bar L\approx 9.51\)); subperiod WIP; central busy time (S1 595/720, S2 570/705); and the O/P utilization bands in §4.4. Wait, service, and sojourn are separated, open and censored cases are kept, and the time-varying bottleneck reading is supported by queue growth rather than by completed cases alone.

The score is held down by rigor and consistency failures, not by the main story.

- Headline utilization in §4.1 (“S1 ~0.80–0.90”, “S2 ~0.75–0.90”) is not the scenario range the same answer computes. Optimistic/pessimistic busy time is about 0.74–0.84 (S1) and 0.73–0.81 (S2). The upper ends require closing inter-case gaps, including idle that rounding cannot justify, and that adjustment is never calculated. §4.4 then reports the full pessimistic envelope, including zero-length service that §6 itself calls implausible, without restating that limit.
- §7 does not compute queue length or WIP at all. The comment that station WIP “is computed via pandas above” is false, so the central \(\bar L\) and peak figures are not reproducible from the required script. Censored S2 waits are also folded into `describe()` as if observed.
- PM4Py is applied to a valid both-stations projection and labeled case-level, but the text never says `@@waiting_time` / `@@sojourn_time` start at S1 start and omit `arrival_s1`. Those outputs are not comparable to §3’s total sojourns.
- Little’s check multiplies the departure rate by the mean sojourn of the five [10:00, 12:00) departures (the 59 minutes and \(\lambda W\approx 2.46\) are arithmetically right) and compares that to total S2 WIP. That is not a cohort identity. The transient caveat is stated, but the diagnostic is weaker than claimed.
- Smaller defects: P05 and P11 cells omit the central point the table header promises; A9’s “40–50 min calibrations” overstates a sample that includes 25 and 30; “median S2 waits 5–20” is not a median; rounding \(\pm 5\) is not composed with the missing-start windows; wait and service rounding errors are reported as if simultaneously attainable; resource busy time is only station-aggregated.

Stable conclusions (fixed station sojourns, WIP path, S1-then-S2 congestion, outage too small to explain the late S2 queue) survive. The quoted utilization bands and the reproducibility claim do not.
