8.4

The paper is a genuine governance review: it separates uses, refuses to average incommensurable scores, judges S and C by language membership rather than by the leaderboard, and builds a third model that actually encodes selective rechecks, external supplier duty, expedite precedence, and exactly-one approval. Probe calls for S and C are correct, the 65×2 = 130 count for S is correct, C really is an eight-trace XOR, and 48 visible leaves versus 9 labels is correct arithmetic. The dissent is a real alternative (allowlist plus human exceptions), not a straw man, and it stays inside 150–250 words.

Those merits do not make the executable artifact safe enough to treat as nearly flawless.

The decision rule in `check_third_policy` ranks `evidence_complete=False` above violations, and that flag defaults to false. Any observed breach—B3, B4, a second approval, a repeat with no correction—therefore returns `indeterminate` unless the caller remembers to flip the flag. The violations list is retained, but an automated gate that reads `decision` never rejects. That cuts against the paper’s own rule that events which are present still prove ordering and occurrence failures. Incomplete evidence should block acceptance; it should not erase a proved violation.

The same function mis-parses phase errors. A late initial check after a premature `Correct Dossier` is labeled an unaffected recheck, because the parser resets the round even after `finish_round` has already failed. Verdicts often still fail closed, but the diagnostic strings an auditor would act on are wrong, and that limitation is not disclosed.

`model_third` itself still accepts B4. Expedite sits in parallel with the whole check/correction block, so forbidden expedite-before-check traces are language members. Safety exists only in the post-hoc checker. The prose says silent skips must be stopped at enablement, then delivers a completed-trace predicate with no transition-level guard. Empty correction rounds (`c? || t?` includes the empty subset) reintroduce, on the recheck phase, the same optionalization pathology the paper correctly ranks first for S.

Smaller faults: an external order with two supplier reviews is reported as “lacks Supplier Review”; “39 extra leaves, not 33” treats one undocumented definition as the refutation of the reported count; the P4 row reads as if the readiness guard were the ratified rule, while expedite between a correction and its rechecks is an extra hard failure, unlike correction-after-expedite, which is only indeterminate. Alignment skeletons are appropriately caveated and do not invent scores.

The recommendation—S exploratory only, C a frozen catalog, guarded composite in shadow until attributes, invariants, and negative suites exist—is the right board outcome. The checker as written is not yet the enforcement model that recommendation describes.
