The audit is strong on the distinctions that matter: post-closure and complete-trace leakage, the extraction-date SQL defect, the 23:00 snapshot, mutable `risk_score_current`, closure-time team and role fields, and the difference between a semantically valid appeal reconsideration and the impossible same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` ordering. Queue length and elapsed time are kept as congestion and slack signals, with a causal caveat. Region and channel are kept as proxies for fairness review rather than deleted or treated as causes. The conformance suite is concrete, and the DFG warning is right.

The revised case narratives miss the availability standard they otherwise apply. In Case Y, `initial_reserve` is both a credible driver and a removed driver. Nothing in the lineage shows it is frozen at `t0`; the claim that reserve fields are frequently overwritten is imported, not evidenced. The narrative then treats “repaired” prior-claim history as an upward signal even though the stored value uses `extraction_date` and the repaired count is unknown. Those two items are not defensibly available.

Several repairs are underspecified. `num_document_requests_up_to_t0` and `prefix_variant_at_t0` are correctly cut at the first coverage check, but under the normative process they are nearly constant there, so they do not preserve the reported predictive content. The fraud-pattern fix (“include fraud only if timestamped before `t0`”) does not match an edge that starts at coverage check and ends after it. `total_case_duration_hours` is severe outcome leakage; calling it a direct encoding of the 10-day settlement label is stronger than the process supports, because close time can include post-decision lag. Local directions for the surviving features are taken from a model whose largest attributions are leaked, without saying those signs can move after refit. Alignment is recommended without stating that it inherits any error in the reference model, and passing a future-erasure test is not tied back to the separate causal standard.
