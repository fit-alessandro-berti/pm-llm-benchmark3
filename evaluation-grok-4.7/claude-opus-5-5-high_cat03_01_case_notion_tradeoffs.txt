Material problems.

The validation rule reverses convergence and divergence. A gap between total objects and unique objects is repeated participation of the same object: divergence. Convergence is total objects above the event count while each object appears once. The answer’s own Invoice/Record Payment row shows this: 4 events, 3 unique invoices, 4 total objects, which is V2’s second payment and zero invoice-level convergence. The written rule would flag that gap as convergence. That contradicts the correct definitions in section 1 and would mis-train the sensitivity check.

Section 7 states package Pack→Dispatch as 50–55 minutes. The same answer already gives 53–60 minutes, and P1 is 60. The closing sentence about means that “hold across every lead” is also unclear, because the Order edge is observed and mispaired rather than absent.

T7 describes the Issue Invoice→Record Payment duration as an average of V2’s 35 minutes and V3’s 21h20m. That edge has three observations. V1 contributes a full settlement of 30 minutes (e18→e21). The understatement of V2’s 20h55m still follows, but the composition of the edge is wrong.

T5 says D2’s 4h55m transit is weighted three times in order-level averages. T9 and the later sensitivity table correctly say the Order DFG never has a Dispatch→Confirm edge, so that average is undefined there. Triple participation in e16 and e24 is real; calling it an order-level transit average is not reconciled with the missing edge.

Smaller issues. Order–Package many-to-many is presented as an OTG cardinality, while the supplied O2O graph connects those types only through Item; the direct edges are contains, packs, carries, bills, and the payment qualifiers. Item duplication is +50% and rated Medium-high, while Order at +35% is rated High, with no reason given for the reversal. Calling the three delivery cases identical flattens a 3h versus 22h45m difference that the same answer relies on elsewhere.

The PM4Py block matches the required calls, names its outputs, and separates direct, weighted, unshared, and propagated views. It does not correct the reversed total-versus-unique reading.
