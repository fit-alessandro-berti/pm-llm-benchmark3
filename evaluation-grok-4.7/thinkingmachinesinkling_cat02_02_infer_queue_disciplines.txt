The final write-up is a real decision-set analysis with the required structure, and several local calls are right: QA’s 08:20 appointment insertion and 08:32 rejection of both priority and SPT; QC’s 10:10–10:18 SPT matches and later breakdown; QD’s exclusive eligibility of D05 and D07; and the warning that B02’s preemption is a trap. Confidence is not uniformly inflated. Those merits do not offset conceptual mistakes in the discipline comparisons the task centers on.

The QB FIFO column is not a stable FIFO prediction. With B01 still incomplete, arrival order selects B01 at 09:48, 10:00, and 10:14. The table instead reports B03, B04, and B04 by quietly dropping the interrupted job, then treats B01’s resumptions as something FIFO cannot explain. That reverses the logic: resuming B01 is what FIFO predicts; not resuming it at 09:48, and serving B05 at 10:00, are the FIFO violations. “Mandatory interrupted-job resumption” is also the wrong label, because B03 and B05 both run between the second stop and the 10:14 resume. The primary label, the prediction column, and Section 3 therefore disagree with each other and with the usual definitions.

Scan delay is handled incorrectly. A delayed scan makes true arrival earlier than the stamp, by at most two minutes. Section 4’s discriminator—that B05 might truly have arrived after 09:48—is impossible, and B04 versus B05 cannot be reordered. Section 6 assumes the opposite direction, so the document contradicts itself on a stated data limitation.

QC contradicts itself in the same way. The 11:40 row correctly says only FIFO selects C01 over C07; Section 3 then says no documented discipline explains that pair. The 10:54 counterfactual also misstates the next decision: C05 is followed by C06 at 11:34, not by the C01/C07 choice at 11:40. Priority and an already-missed deadline are observationally equivalent for C05, but the competing test uses a vaguer hidden-eligibility story and an outcome the log already shows.

QD is over-identified. Not starting D05 before the 12:00 meeting is a coherent window constraint; D10 is 12 minutes, priority 1, and electrical, so it fits on free D-F at 11:36 without blocking D05 after 12:20. Calling that idle explained, assigning confidence 85, and ranking QD as more identifiable than QA are not supported. QA’s own “strongest contradicting” episode is admitted to be consistent with FIFO, so that cell does not do its job. The perfect match between every `est_min` and total realized service is never used, yet estimate noise is still ranked as a high-plausibility SPT escape.

The piece is well organized and often points at the right episodes, but baseline predictions and at least two cross-section claims are wrong. That is well short of a nearly flawless diagnosis.
