7.4

The pipeline is careful and mostly consistent: exact duplicates are separated from cross-system observations, e05 and e14 are kept, e08/e09 are treated as a label collision, e15 is not given M1/M2 links, and the 17/44, 15/39, and 14/37 counts follow from the stated unions. The `have_common_object` warning is right: e13 and e14 share O1 and V1 while remaining different payments, and the projected aggressive collapse to 10 events and 28 distinct E2O links is the correct connected-component result. Provenance, reversibility, and the listed failure modes cover the required ground.

The ordering section overreaches its own rule. Within-source sequence is declared semantic only when the source documents that `source_seq` is temporal, but e12 before e13 before e14 is then called a firm chain. Fact 7 fixes only e12 before e13. The same section says e03 and e05 may be physically unordered, while the rank table always places e03 first. e08 before e09 is a plausible logistics assumption, not a supplied constraint; only e09 before e10 is given.

The sensitivity write-up misstates a directly-follows edge. Under e03, e05, e06, e07, the new direct order edge is Pack Package to ERP Packing Status Update. Pick Item to that status label is a path with packing in between. Capture Payment to the ERP balance update is a true direct edge only from the later capture, and Capture to Capture remains. “Removes measurable status latency” and “destroys release-to-acceptance time” are also loose: those pairs already share a one-second timestamp, so the measured business gap is zero; the loss is the milestone, not a positive duration.

The code matches the required PM4Py calls, copies, signature pre-check, verified-pair helper, and relation checks, but it does not implement the partial order it specifies. Incomparable invoice and payment events still receive one microsecond total order, with no incomparability flag or cycle check. The global audit appends the e06/e07 merge without a scenario id, so replaying it cannot recover the conservative view alone. The attribute rule does not enact the stated ERP-versus-billing authority split. `tie_rank` restarts inside each timestamp and is safe only if `ocel_sort_by_additional_column` sorts by timestamp first; nothing asserts that the result is time-monotonic.
