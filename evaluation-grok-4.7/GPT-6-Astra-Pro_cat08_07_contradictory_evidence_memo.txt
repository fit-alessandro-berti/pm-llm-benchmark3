The memo is a strong, decision-ready synthesis: it keeps Alternative E, refuses unchanged national scale and blanket rollback, and separates segment gains from tail, rework, premature billing, and resilience losses. Facts, interpretations, and uncertainties are mostly kept apart, the five metric conflicts are explained with the right process-mining distinctions, and the measurement ranking is tied to actual decision changes.

It is not nearly flawless. “Independently verified” simple low-risk eligibility is undefined even though risk attributes are incomplete and the skip rule is untrusted, so the pathway recommended for immediate gradual scale is not operationally identifiable yet. Section 5 says to start M7, M3, M4, and basic M8 together, but section 6’s measure-next list drops M8 and elevates M2. The €9 net-cost figure is arithmetically correct, yet the memo never states that missing complaint costs after week 4 likely understate pilot harm. The rollout gates use tight, unpowered tolerances—especially a roughly 2-day bound on p95 and a €0 upper cost bound—without asking whether those tests can be passed. Simple-house SLA improvement is omitted, and the external citations do not establish the sentences they are attached to.
