6.2

The fork-join decomposition itself is mostly right, and the case table and the four worked examples check out. The formulas for upstream time, branch queue, service, join as the max end, sync slack, downstream wait, final service, and total flow time are correct, and the double-counting argument is sound: each branch’s queue + service + sync equals the same fork-join interval, so summing the three branches yields \(3 \times FJ\). Baseline critical branches are also right (A on F01 and F11, C on F04 and F08, B on the other eight, no ties). The ranking that B controls the tail, A is a 50-minute floor, and C is only episodic is the right conclusion, and several itemized counterfactual savings are right (CF1 saves 5 on F01 and 10 on F11; the capped CF2 savings of 10 and 5 on F05 and F10; CF3 saves 88 and 176).

The answer then asserts observations the timestamps do not support. Consecutive B intervals overlap, including three at once, and F08’s B starts before F07’s. \(B\_start_c = B\_end_{c-1}\) holds for one transition, F04 to F05, not for 8 of 11, so the single-server FIFO story and \(\rho_B = 2.5\) are not identified. The final-station census is also wrong and internally contradictory: F04 is not the unique moment when both F1 and F2 are busy, because F1 is idle then, while both are busy at F09’s and F10’s join. “Both idle in 7 cases,” “at least one idle in 11 of 12,” and “pooling drives every downstream wait to 0” do not follow. Those claims carry Redesigns 1 and 2.

Aggregate arithmetic does not match the answer’s own case figures. B’s mean sync is \(52/12 = 4.33\), not 2.67. Sample SDs are about 9.49 for B queue, 13.71 for B elapsed, 12.34 for C elapsed, and 8.38 for fork-join, not 9.73, 13.37, 12.69, and 8.28. The reported correlation is about 0.891, not 0.906. CF2’s itemized savings add to 92.5, not 85. CF1 leaves 9 strict B cases and one A+B tie, not 8 and 2 ties. CF2 leaves 4 strict B cases and 2 ties, not 5 and 1. Combined CF1+CF2 saves 117.5 with about 10 minutes of super-additivity, not 122.5 and 22.5. The F11 combined row (save 15, critical B) is impossible once A’s elapsed time is 40 and B’s is 35. The block labeled as script output repeats the wrong sync mean and that impossible row, so it was not produced by the code. The code’s own max logic is largely the correct one; B service IQR is also reported as 10 under a different quartile rule from the queue IQRs.
