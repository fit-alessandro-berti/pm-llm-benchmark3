The final answer is a strong, complete causal and process-mining assessment, but it is not nearly flawless. It correctly detects the Simpson reversal (Orion crude mean 21.96h vs Nova 15.24h, yet shorter within Low, Medium, and High), refuses to treat stratification as identification, separates queue wait from review service, treats resource group as a routing-policy bundle, and recommends a bounded randomized pilot plus a capacity intervention rather than a blind 50% reassignment. Required sections, tables, graph variables, and diagnostic code are present.

Deductions are for concrete errors. The graph is not a DAG: `rework` and `review_service` point at each other, and `quality_outcome -> case_completed` reverses the 30-day outcome’s timing. Nova’s within-shift figures are wrong (night is 17.50h, not 17.55h; day is 14.49h, not 14.05h), and “complexity-adjusted night excess remains” is asserted without a calculation and is not supported within Low. The compliance falsification test quotes 19.5h vs 22.8h as Compliance=Yes waits; those are approximately the High-stratum waits, whereas all Compliance=Yes waits average about 15.8h vs 16.6h. Medium compliance “means” are ranges mislabeled as means. The recommendation says the Medium-Day pilot is run “where overlap exists,” but Orion has no Medium-Day cases. Several comparison operators are missing, and several residual-uncertainty items name multiple data fields where one was required.
