7.2

The assessment is structured, temporally disciplined, and often precise, but several rule-mapping errors and one internal contradiction keep it well short of a near-flawless analysis.

The hierarchy, the decision method, and the defeasible catalogue are the strongest parts. Board-level integrity controls stay above the incident directive; ECD-2 is confined to timing and supplier-register issues inside its window and subject matter; the CFO email is given only prospective, finance-side effect from 2026-07-15 07:20; later approval is treated as mitigative; and ERP timestamps yield to contemporaneous proof. P-502, P-503, and P-506 are argued at the right moment of commitment, with scope, segregation, Compliance, and the unproven verbal extension kept distinct. The expiry analysis on P-505 and the signed-PDF analysis on P-507 follow the outage note’s evidential point. Section 5 actually changes premises rather than restating the same conclusion, and the draft clause covers authority, scope, thresholds, segregation, records, expiry, and normalization.

Those merits do not remove the following defects.

P-501’s primary NONCOMPLIANT label is built on an overreading of B4. The clause separately permits commitment before the PO in a defined emergency, and permits an actively delegated Shift Lead to stand in for an absent Plant Manager. A reachable manager with no delegation blocks that substitution. It does not, on the text, make SHIFT-1 an unauthorized committer. Plant_Approve and Finance_Approve both exist, the amount is below A2 and A3, the supplier is approved, and the emergency form falls inside 24 hours. The Section 5 alternative (clock drift or an implied standing delegation) is weaker than the grammatical one, so the required “strongest alternative” for this case is missed. P-501 is also absent from the case-level remediation list.

P-504 collapses “unapproved supplier” into A3’s “new supplier.” The case table uses those terms differently, and the rule catalogue itself keeps R3 and R7 apart. Solo commitment is listed as a deviation although no in-force rule requires dual action for a EUR 24,000 purchase outside ECD-2. NONCOMPLIANT can still stand on B1 and B3, but the breach inventory is inflated, and SOP scope for a cafeteria repair is never tested.

The burden rule is then applied unevenly. Section 1 puts the burden on the party invoking an exception, and Section 2 admits that D1 has no clear delegate for A2’s independent budget-owner leg. P-505, P-507, and P-508 nonetheless become JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION, with P-507 at high confidence, while that leg is unmet and unwaived. Under the answer’s own burden rule those three results should be NONCOMPLIANT or INDETERMINATE. P-505 also marks R10 satisfied although the case gives an authorization time and no recording time. B6 is checked for P-501 and then dropped for the ECD cases even though the catalogue makes the emergency form a general requirement. P-507’s document outage is attributed to ON-44, which is a different outage with a different drift rule.

The result is a serious and usable opinion with visible assumptions, not a clean application of the norms it states.
