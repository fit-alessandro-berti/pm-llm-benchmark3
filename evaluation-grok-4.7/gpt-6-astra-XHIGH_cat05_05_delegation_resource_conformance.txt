The case reasoning is unusually strong, but the required checking code is not reliably executable, and a few evidence calls are stricter than the record supports.

The rule model is careful and mostly right. Aliases are normalized to Ben before segregation checks, delegates are not rewritten as their principals, and `svc_qms` is not collapsed into one person. D-QM-01 is correctly treated as valid from the August 9 signature rather than the August 12 migration insert. CHG-501 is a valid substitution; CHG-502 and CHG-508 are real drafter/approver failures, the latter only after alias normalization; CHG-507 is an unauthorized high-risk approval; CHG-510 fails both on self-approval and on prohibited subdelegation; CHG-505’s August 14 review is outside two business days under the stated Monday–Friday count; CHG-509 correctly stays indeterminate because weekend status does not prove Asha’s absence. Repeated Clara/Ben work is treated as an indicator, with benign and adverse explanations and discriminating evidence. The six Diego-to-Eva handovers and four attributed shared cases are counted correctly, and the 60-day 18/23 figure is not pretended to be testable from this extract.

The code does not meet the executability requirement. `df.loc[service_509, ["human", "identity_note"]] = [ ... ]` is a one-row, two-column list assignment that raises `ValueError` on current pandas. Even if that were fixed, `pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle(..., keep_violations=True)` passes `keep_violations` as a keyword; standard PM4Py accepts it only inside `parameters`, so this raises `TypeError` before any flag is printed. The assertions and “expected key outputs” are therefore unverified. `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is omitted, and the handover/working-together printers can fall through to an unparsed PM4Py object. The interval logic itself is well designed: unknown humans stay `UNKNOWN` rather than passing four-eyes, incident CI-44 is not invented from `cooling_system`, and retrospective review is checked separately from approval.

Smaller judgment problems: CHG-505’s reviewer is blanked as unresolved even though the explicit denial and office-IP contradiction are given only for CHG-506; sick-leave overlap justifies investigation, and lateness already fails RR4. CHG-504’s approval at 09:22 sits inside the stated VPN window and is supported by Asha’s account, so primary `INDETERMINATE` is more skeptical than that positive evidence, though the missing link to “one urgent case” is fairly noted. Coding every QM grant as approval-only is narrower than “valid QM delegate”; cooling-system scope would still defeat those reviews, but the code could not accept an in-scope review delegate. Endpoints at `23:59:00` rather than the end of that minute do not affect these events.
