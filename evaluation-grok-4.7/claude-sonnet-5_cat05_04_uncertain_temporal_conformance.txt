3.5

The normalization framework is largely sound, and several hard cases are handled correctly, but the memo is unfinished and the duration arithmetic is not reliable enough to trust. As a required seven-part analysis it stops mid-row in §3.

§1–§2 get the main mechanics right. Resolution is applied before clock correction; with `clock_offset = recorded − true` and offset in `[a, b]`, true time is `[r_lo − b, r_hi − a]`. HUB’s combined ±0.5 s measurement and ±1 s offset correctly yields a ±1.5 s window. OPS-BER floor-to-minute plus `[−90, +120]` is right (T-401 start `[08:12:00, 08:16:29.999]` UTC). VENDOR-NY `[07:57:00, 08:03:59.999]`, the T-404 start inferred as complete minus `[1080, 1500]` s giving `[10:09:58.5, 10:17:01.5]`, the T-407 latency proxy `[13:59:58.5, 14:20:01.5]`, and the T-409 weak `[−12, +12]` min substitution giving `[15:52:00, 16:16:59.999]` all check out. The DST result is the right survivor: only Alert on the CEST fold and both later events on the CET fold can satisfy ingestion order, and the ±60 s bounds remain. The mobile-delay choice is explicit and internally consistent with `true ∈ [recorded_lo − 600 s, recorded_hi + 300 s]`.

Those sections still have material defects. The offset formula is printed with the operators missing, so it does not stand on its own. “Other 3 fold-combos” is the wrong count: eight fold assignments exist and seven are impossible. Many §2 cells drop the date, so they are not explicit ISO intervals. Same-clock dependence is never stated. A shared HUB offset cancels in HUB–HUB durations; treating every timestamp as independent is what produces T-404’s 3 s S2 overhang, and that assumption is outcome-relevant. SENSOR/mobile rounding is closed at `…59.999` / `…29.999` rather than the full half-width tie boundary (`08:51:20`, `12:19:30`).

§3’s labels for the rows that exist mostly survive, but several feasible intervals contradict §2:

- T-401 S1 is `[718.5, 991.5]`, not `[719.5, 991.5]` (`08:12:00 − 08:00:01.5`).
- T-401 Alert→Containment is `[3058.5, 3081.5]`, not `[3058.5, 3321.5]` (upper bound is 4 minutes high).
- T-402 S1 / Alert→Start is `[958.5, 1231.5]`, not `[938.5, 1231.5]`.
- T-402 S2 / Start→Containment is `[2488.5, 2761.5]`, not `[1288.5, 2761.5]` (lower bound is 20 minutes low).

Profile bands themselves are right (`600±180`, `2100±600`, `2700±720`, `86400±21600`), and the wrong intervals happen not to cross a zeta or SLA boundary, so those particular labels stay POSSIBLE or CERTAIN. That is luck, not a checked interval proof. T-402’s z2 Alert→Start edge is only 1.5 s from flipping (`958.5` vs `960`).
