The fragment shows a sound offset convention and several correct UTC bounds, but it is an unfinished draft of a seven-part conformance analysis. Sections 3–7 are missing, and the event table stops inside T-403, so there are no duration intervals, SLA or profile classifications, sensitivity results, PM4Py code, sufficiency criteria, or governance controls.

What is present is mostly consistent with the stated clock model. Resolution is applied before the offset, and `true ∈ [rec_lo − b, rec_hi − a]` is the right inversion of `offset = recorded − true`. The HUB composition `[r − 1.5 s, r + 1.5 s]` matches ±0.5 s measurement uncertainty plus `[-1, +1]` s. The OPS-BER rows are right: 10:14 and 11:18 CEST, floored to the minute and then shifted by `[-90, +120]` s, become `[08:12:00Z, 08:16:29.999Z]` and `[09:16:00Z, 09:20:29.999Z]`. The SENSOR completion instant 08:51:10 becomes `[08:51:00Z, 08:51:20Z]` after ±5 s rounding and ±5 s offset. The partial VENDOR-NY bound, `[07:57:00Z, 08:03:59.999Z]`, is the correct EDT floor-minute image under ±180 s. CEST/BST/EDT and the 2025-10-26 Berlin fold direction are right, and only one of the eight fold triples (Alert on the first occurrence, the later two events on the second) can respect ingestion order. Alert_Sent is correctly kept distinct from Alert_Received, T-404 is inverted from completion minus `[18, 25]` min without treating completion as the start, and mobile entry delay is separated from the ±300 s clock bound.

Those local successes do not carry the required conclusions. The causal reduction `earlier.hi = min(earlier.hi, later.hi)` and `later.lo = max(later.lo, earlier.lo)` only clips marginal supports. The product of those margins still contains pairs with earlier after later, so a duration interval computed from the clipped margins is too wide and can include negative values that the constraint forbids. That error would have corrupted T-405, T-407, and any other ordered pair. T-409 is also mishandled in advance: the supplied `[-12, +12]` min estimate is a weak but finite bound, and declaring every dependent classification INDETERMINATE discards it instead of classifying under it at low confidence. Applying an unlogged Alert ≤ Investigation constraint to T-409 is an extra assumption the evidence does not state. The floor upper edge is approximated as 59.999 s rather than a half-open minute, and the T-404 start is left as an unreduced expression. None of the testable S1–S3 or profile pairs is actually evaluated, and midpoint handling is only promised.
