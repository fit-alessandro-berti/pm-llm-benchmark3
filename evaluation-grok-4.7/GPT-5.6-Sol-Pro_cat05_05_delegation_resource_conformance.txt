8.3

The case analysis is strong and mostly event-time correct: aliases are normalized, `svc_qms` is not given a global human identity, D-QM-01 is grounded in the 9 August PDF rather than the 12 August migration row, D-IC-04 is rejected as prohibited subdelegation, weekend status is not treated as Asha’s absence, and Ben’s sick-leave denial is confined to CHG-506. RR1 hits on CHG-502, CHG-508, and CHG-510, the RR3 failure on CHG-507, the conditional D-QM-02 result on CHG-509, and the late, out-of-scope CHG-505 review are all well supported. Collusion is kept as an indicator, and the ten-case partition in the conclusion is coherent.

The business-day rule is explicit and internally consistent. With the approval date excluded, Tuesday 11 August yields a deadline at the end of Thursday 13 August, so the 14 August review is late and the 12 August review is timely. That result also survives a working-hours reading.

These defects keep it short of nearly flawless:

- Section 2 defines RR1/RR2 as pass, fail, or unresolved, but the checker represents an unknown actor as a distinct resource. `filter_four_eyes_principle` therefore reports no violation for CHG-503 and CHG-506. The unresolved flag is separate, so the printed SoD columns contradict the formal rule.
- When an emergency approval fails both QM and IC checks, the basis string retains only the last route, so the QM failure disappears from the output.
- The script was not run with PM4Py. `keep_violations` on `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is an unverified call placed before the required network output, so an API mismatch aborts the whole check. The pinned claim that PM4Py 2.7.23.3 was published on 17 July 2026 is unsupported here.
- The handover commentary names self-handovers on CHG-502 and CHG-508 and omits the same Clara-to-Clara handover on CHG-510.
- CHG-508 is a resolved same-person SoD breach. The secondary `DATA_OR_IDENTITY_PROBLEM` label describes a system that compares raw IDs, not the classified case.
- Section 2 treats Asha’s 13 August VPN session as relevant to what “absent” means, while the CHG-506 row treats D-QM-01’s absence condition as simply satisfied.
- The delegation formula uses scope subset, while the code uses equality. CHG-509 notes approver=verifier and omits the parallel drafter=implementer concentration.
