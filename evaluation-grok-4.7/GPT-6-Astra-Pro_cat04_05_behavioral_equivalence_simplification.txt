The deductions are for concrete defects, not tone. B’s placement cell omits explicit slots such as approval-then-check-then-rework. The footprint paragraph is hedged, but “sequence” in this API is a loop-closed can-follow relation, so A can still relate \(p\) to a later \(c\); adjacency of \(pc\) is not what that relation means. None of these holes overturn the verdicts.
