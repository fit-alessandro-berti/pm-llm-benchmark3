These errors are material:

- Manual eligibility review → Repair quote is median 15h and p90 60h, a 4.0× ratio, not 3.5×.
- Auto → fraud (11h), manual → fraud (18h), and manual → repair quote (22h) are alternative edges. Adding them as one 11+18+22 sequential chain overstates what parallel review would remove.
- Complaint/reopen is 9.2% of 5,000, i.e. 460 cases, while the listed inflows are 140+80+260+30 = 510 events and the outflows are only 310+120. Treating a residual of about 30 as the R3 exposure uses mixed units and misses the larger gap: 120 reopens go to the escalation board rather than Manual eligibility review. “Complaint after close” is a move-on-log, which that residual does not demonstrate.
- Abandoned claims are described as exiting quickly and cheaply and therefore flattering cycle time. No duration is given for abandonment, and the segment with the highest abandonment is also the 140h median segment.
- Dispatch without risk review and wrong-part replacement are treated as the same cases. The log only shows both are frequent on fast fulfillment.
- The 15–35% manual-inflow increase is the side effect of a generic auto-rule update. Redesign A also widens straight-through processing for the 43% accessory volume, so that range is not its net load, yet the failure mode and pilot threshold treat it as the expected effect.
- High-value repair-first is projected toward the aggregate 3.3% repair-path complaint rate. For high-value devices the reviewed/repair rates are 5% and 6%, and the 3.3% is conditional on customers who already accepted a quote. That confuses path quality with case mix.
- “A few hours” end to end sits beside component means of 0.8+1.0+3.0+5.0h. “10–30× later” mixes complaint means of 72–96h with a path whose mean is about 10h. Premium manual cycle time of 46h median makes a service credit likely for many claims; it does not almost guarantee one.
- Rule enforcement can improve future fitness to the normative net. It does not, as claimed, repair the discovered model’s low precision. The prompt’s precision remark is about low-frequency policy-illegal combinations, not a quantity the new lanes automatically raise.

The redesigns remain defensible operating hypotheses, and the measurement caveats are often better than the point estimates. Under a strict reading, the arithmetic mistake, the false sequential path, the abandonment claim, and the 3.3% and 15–35% transfers are enough to keep this out of the high band.
