6.5

The fork-join framework is sound and the main conclusion holds: B controls completion despite A’s longer service, and downstream delay can mask branch speedups. The write-up is not numerically reliable enough to score higher.

Definitions in §1 are correct. `join_ready = max(end_i)`, flow time is `upstream + max(queue_i + service_i) + downstream queue + final service`, and summing all branch sync waits is rightly rejected as double counting. Critical-branch counts are right (A 2, B 8, C 2). Worked cases F02, F04, F07, and F11 check out, including the checks against total flow time. The required headings and minimum case columns are present. Limits of inference (capacity vs release policy, preemption, shared resources, n=12) are specific and well tied to the log, including the unexplained 8-minute delay on F01.

The aggregates do not cohere. In §2, F01 `C_service` is 38; 08:20–08:38 is 18. That error reappears as F01 C sync wait 2 instead of 22 (09:00−08:38) and as C’s mean sync 28.8 instead of 30.4. B service mean is stated as 32.5 (actual 34.2) and B queue mean as 21.7 (actual 22.5), so “queue + service” 54.2 contradicts the same table’s correct B end-relative-to-fork mean of 56.7. C’s end-relative mean 32.3 likewise contradicts its own queue+service mean of 30.6. Conditional sync means are wrong: B non-critical is 13.0, not 5.2 (52 was divided by 10 instead of 4), and C’s non-critical mean cannot equal the overall mean when two critical cases contribute 0.

Counterfactual 1 misidentifies the binding branch. After A service falls to 40, F01’s new completion times are A 08:50, B 08:55, C 08:38, so B remains binding and `join_ready` falls by 5 minutes, not by 2 with C binding. The stated 12-minute direct reduction is therefore 15. Counterfactual 2’s per-case `join_ready` deltas are right and sum to 95, but the simulated diffs listed in the text sum to 100, not the reported 105 / 8.75. The two-server simulation starts both servers idle at time 0, so it does not reproduce the observed 8–20 minute downstream queues; savings versus that baseline are then placed next to the 176-minute observed-queue upper bound as if they were the same kind of minute. The code would not reproduce the prose: it computes F01 C service and the CF1 join correctly, prints no per-case savings, and wraps `pm4py.get_service_time` (called without an activity) and the DFG call in one `try`, so a signature error skips the performance DFG. The DFG print also labels performance values as frequency.

Qualitative ranking and the two redesigns survive these mistakes, because B is still critical most often, A is still the near-floor, and CF1 remains small. Under a calculation-coherence standard, the contradictory table and the wrong F01 counterfactual keep this well below a near-flawless score.
