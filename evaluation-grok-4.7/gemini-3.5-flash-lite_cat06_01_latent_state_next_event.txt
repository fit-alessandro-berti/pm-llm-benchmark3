The primary state is right for every case, and the rank-1 next event is defensible for each, but the forecast is not reliable. Downstream activities are repeatedly scored as the next log event, `process_enabled` contradicts the accompanying reasoning, and several rule readings are overstated.

C6-A is S3, yet “actively under review” is unsupported: four items are waiting and only two reviewers are active, about 1.8 h after receipt. Technical Investigation Completed is marked not enabled while the text says it is already in progress or queued. A new Customer Evidence Requested event is marked enabled before Evidence Review Completed. Supplier Analysis is suspected from one complaint covering two devices plus three similar complaints whose lot link is unstated. The 7-item investigation queue and the unavailable engineer do not affect the 0.15–0.35 interval.

C6-B’s alternative, “S1 TRIAGED / Fast Close,” reverses the lifecycle. Evidence was already requested and received, so the triage fast-close branch was not taken. Fast Close and Close — No Safety Issue are separate terminals; the historical close-without-investigation path is Evidence Review Completed → Close — No Safety Issue. Closure at 0.15–0.35 as the next log event skips the review completion that the prefix still lacks. Contradicting evidence is recorded as “None,” omitting the triage decision to collect evidence and the assignment to R-T4. A and B also sit in the same review queue, so B is not independently “swift.”

C6-C correctly keeps Regulatory Assessment ahead of disposition, but “country rules dictate repeated malfunctions are reportable” changes “can be reportable.” Disposition is marked enabled while the primary state still has that assessment open. Authority Notification at 0.05–0.20 cannot be next: it follows a reportable conclusion and disposition, and the batch is at 16:00. “Root cause plausible but not confirmed” never becomes an extra evidence or investigation candidate. Different lots are handled correctly by leaving Supplier Analysis out.

C6-D uses the 12:40 worklist opening well, but S5 is temporally implausible: assessment has been open about 80 minutes against a 2–14 h completion range. Authority Notification at 0.10–0.25 has the same unmet prerequisites. Because the feed is migration-affected and a source attachment exists, a late evidence event is a serious candidate for the next observation and is absent from the top three. The missing evidence events are filed as contradictions of S4 rather than as an incomplete prefix.

C6-E’s second receipt is only 40 minutes old, with completeness 0.72, yet Supplier Analysis Completed carries 0.20–0.40 and is marked enabled. The supplier certificate has just arrived, and supplier analysis takes 6–36 h, so completion can precede review only if analysis was already open. That unknown is named and then not reflected in the lower bound. A six-complaint lot is a suspicious common-lot signal: fast close is barred, and a possible reportability obligation for Regulatory Assessment is omitted. Technical Investigation Completed is marked disabled while described as possibly concurrent.

Across A–E, midpoint mass on second and third predictions often describes an eventual path, not the next logged activity. E’s midpoints sum to about 1.13. The cross-case note correctly separates A’s injury and lot context from B’s duplicate profile, and it correctly refuses to treat the DFG as normative. It still equates “different eventual obligations” with “different next log event,” which is the distinction the cases require.
