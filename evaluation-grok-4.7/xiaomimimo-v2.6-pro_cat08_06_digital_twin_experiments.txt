7.5

The design is strong and covers every required section: non-stationary clustered arrivals, calendars, latent objections, service/wait separation, cross-case bundling, a full experiment and stress matrix, warmup sized to the annual cycle, CRN, and validation that does not pretend the twin is already calibrated. It also correctly refuses an optimality claim.

It is not nearly flawless. Concrete errors keep it well below a top score:

- PM4Py process-tree conventions are wrong. `Operator.LOOPEXEC` and `Operator.TAU` are not PM4Py operators (the loop operator is `Operator.LOOP`; silent nodes are unlabeled leaves). The documented enum (`LOOPEXEC='*'`, `LOOP='R'`, `TAU='-'`, `INTERLEAVING='O|'`) is fabricated. One shared `tau` node is also reused under several parents, which is an invalid tree.
- The Poisson check is statistically wrong. Index of dispersion as variance/mean of inter-arrival times is not 1 under a Poisson process (that ratio equals the mean). Clustering should be tested with CV² of inter-arrivals, or with variance/mean of counts.
- Uncertainty methods are mislabeled. The text promises t-intervals and BCa bootstrap; the skeleton uses 1.96 and a percentile bootstrap. `hash(exp_id)` also contradicts the bitwise-reproducibility claim under Python’s salted `hash`.
- The decision rule (must improve p50 and p90 and CVaR95) conflicts with the answer’s own expected effects for D3 and D6, which trade a worse median for a better tail, without a separate acceptance path for those designs.
- The skeleton is not plausible Python: `"case:concept:name": str` is illegal as a dataclass field, so the module does not parse. Several PM4Py names in the estimation plan (`discover_variants`, `extract_start_activities`, `pm4py.convert.convert_event_log_to_dataframe`) are not the real API. `__main__` calls an undefined `_build`.

Those are localized failures in an otherwise careful twin design, not missing sections. Under a strict reading they are enough to withhold anything in the 9–10 range.
