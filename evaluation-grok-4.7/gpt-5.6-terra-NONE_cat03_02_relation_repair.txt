The repair judgment is directionally sound, and the required sections, bundles, uncertainty labels, and PM4Py outline are all present. The score is held down by factual slips, an inconsistent proposal table, and a lifecycle audit that misses the main cardinality consequence of the chosen package repair.

The evidence model is usable. Two classifications are loose: relations already stored in the log are observed facts, while the write-up files them as a “strong default,” and “the object still needs a package/delivery” is used as positive evidence in A1 after the same section says lifecycle completeness counts against a repair.
