7.4

The interval arithmetic, time-zone conversions, and SLA/profile classifications are largely correct and carefully separated from the midpoint log. That core is not enough for a higher score: several stated conclusions are internally inconsistent or factually wrong.

The offset direction is reversed in the T-401 S1 rationale. With `offset = recorded − true`, a fast OPS-BER clock moves `Investigation_Start` earlier and shortens the gap; a slow clock lengthens it. Violation sits on the slow side. Section 3 says the opposite (“clock fast by > ~30 s”). Section 6 then gives a pass condition (“at most ~30 s fast”) that is neither the right direction nor the right threshold: with the minute still unresolved, a certain pass needs the offset to be at least about +1.5 s, while a certain violation needs it slower than about 62 s, not “≥ 90 s and late in the minute.” T-402’s “fastest clock” wording is the correct direction, so the report contradicts itself.

T-409 breaks the rule in A8. S1 and the AR→IS profile are INDETERMINATE when the weak ±12 min estimate and the nominal bound disagree, but IS→CC at ζ = 1 is reported as POSSIBLE_VIOLATION even though those same two hypotheses disagree (possible vs certain pass). The “~10 min slow” gloss is also off; the low side of that band opens once the clock is only about 5–6 minutes slow. Calling S2 a CERTAIN_PASS is defensible only inside the stated hypotheses, and the confidence label understates how weak the ±12 min estimate is.

Section 5 misstates PM4Py. `conformance_temporal_profile` flags only durations above `μ + ζσ`, not `|duration − μ| > ζσ`. The claimed ζ = 1 list of 12 flags includes T-409 AR→IS at 270 s, a low-side midpoint that this checker does not flag. Those “run” results do not match the tool the code calls. The fallback is also brittle: a `TypeError` on `return_diagnostics_dataframe` retries with the same `zeta=` keyword and will not survive a second signature mismatch. The code never builds the nominal T-409 bound used in the prose.

Smaller faults: at ζ = 3 the text says three AR→IS pairs remain possible and then names four (T-402, T-403, T-406, T-407). “Every other S1” is false while T-402 is a certain violation. Common-mode cancellation does not clear T-408 IS→CC at ζ = 2; independent ±0.5 s measurement noise still straddles 3300 s. Severity labels such as “Low-Medium” do not match the scale defined in A11.

The required structure, DST pruning (only AR on CEST and the later two events on CET), dual mobile-entry readings, and tolerance/ζ matrices are otherwise tight and reproducible to about 0.5 s.
