7.5

The equivalence verdicts are right: A and B are not visibly trace-equivalent, not P1-equivalent, and not P3-equivalent, and they are P2-equivalent only after the stated abstraction. The counting invariants are right (`#Check = #Analyze = n` in A, `#Check = 1` in B), the four counterexamples are genuine, the single-iteration inclusion `L₁(A) ⊂ L₁(B)` and the `n ≥ 2` disjointness argument hold, and the bounded cardinalities `|L≤(A)| = 76`, `|L≤(B)| = 85`, shared `4` (hence 72 / 81) match an exhaustive enumeration. The P2 image `{(n, true, n≥2) : n ≥ 1}`, the footprint difference (sequence `(Check, Approve)` only in A; both parallel directions only in B), and the simplified language size 13 are also right. The simplified tree is a clearly declared strengthening of P1, and the recommendation correctly refuses to replace A unless check-after-analysis is required.

The required net-complexity figures do not. Under the standard process-tree conversion (leaf = one transition; sequence = intermediate places; AND = silent split/join plus two places per branch; loop = one mid-place, one silent exit, redo back to the loop entry), the nets are A: 10 places / 10 transitions / 22 arcs, B: the same 10 / 10 / 22, simplified: 7 / 8 / 16. Inverse arc-degree simplicity with the usual threshold 2 is then about 0.833, 0.833, and 0.882, not 0.8182, 0.7778, and 0.8667. Those printed decimals are exactly `1 / (1 + (2|A|/|N| − 2))` applied to the stated sizes, so they are not an independent measurement. “B converts to a smaller net and scores lower because the AND-join concentrates arcs” is therefore false; A and B are the same size and equally simple on this metric. The simplified model is still smaller than both, so the qualitative replacement claim survives, but §7 reports wrong exact values as executed results.

§5’s script is not reliably executable as written: `pm4py.check_soundness` returns a bool, so `diagnostics[0]` raises before any language is built. The BFS itself (silent steps, `(marking, visible trace)` visited set, visible-length and analyze bounds, final-marking equality) is the right exhaustive procedure. §9’s “157 of the 161 bounded traces belong to exactly one model” is arithmetic: the union has 157 traces, the sum of the two languages is 161, and the exactly-one count is 153. Smaller wording slips: approvals do distinguish some pairs (T3), and the redo activity `Rework Case` is visible even though the exit/`tau` choices are not.
