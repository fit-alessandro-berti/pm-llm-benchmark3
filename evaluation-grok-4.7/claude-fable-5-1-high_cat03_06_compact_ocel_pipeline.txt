7.5

The construction is coherent and largely audit-ready: 17 events, 12 objects, 35 source E2O rows, and 12 O2O rows match the narrative; timestamps, qualifiers, validation, and the source-versus-`derived_via_*` split are consistent. Flattened trace shapes are right (O100 length 8, O200 length 6; Package and Delivery are two-event twins; Invoice is one case with a payment self-loop; Payment is two length-1 cases). Order DFG edges and the event-couple versus unique-object reading of `Reserve Stock → Pack Package` are correct, as is the 58-row ordered interaction count and the non-uniqueness warning on the object-centric Petri net.

The object-type graph descendants are not. Interaction is counted over all co-occurring pairs, including I1–O200 and I3–O100, but descendants then report `Order → Item` as 3. Under the birth-order rule the text itself states, those cross pairs yield four `Order → Item` edges plus `Item → Order` (I1 starts before O200), and same-type edges `O100 → O200` and `I1 → I3`. Inheritance and co-death, by contrast, match the usual end-event/start-event and shared-end definitions, so the slip is local but it is exactly the convergence the log is built to show.

Section 6C is also incomplete. Propagated lengths 14 and 10 are right, and `Record Payment → Order` 4 and `Confirm Delivery → Order` 3 are right, but the enriched Order DFG does not merely gain payment and confirmation edges. Dispatch is inserted, so `Pack Package → Issue Invoice` and `Issue Invoice → Close Order` disappear, and O100 gains `Dispatch → Dispatch` and `Confirm Delivery → Confirm Delivery` while O200 does not.

Smaller faults: M2 as `completes_settlement` assumes an invoice total of 250 that the narrative never states; “revenue double-count of V1” refers to an event with no amount; the tie-break is described as `ocel:eid` while `events` is sorted only by timestamp; `always_before` is printed as dict keys; “variable token count” is overstated for single events whose multiplicity is fixed at 2; and the claimed delivery performance numbers are not what `discover_dfg` returns. The “tested on PM4Py 2.7” claim is hard to reconcile with the descendant counts.
