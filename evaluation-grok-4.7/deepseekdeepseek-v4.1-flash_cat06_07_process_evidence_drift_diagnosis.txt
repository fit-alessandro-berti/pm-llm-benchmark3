Points come off for imprecision and a thin sketch. July is listed under label delay despite 98% maturity and a median delay only a few days above the winter baseline. The monitoring section is a catalog plus one dependency paragraph, without evaluation windows, case-start versus event-time cohorts, or an explicit suppression order.
