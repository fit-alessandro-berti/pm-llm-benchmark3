The diagnosis is strong and correctly refuses a single-period story. April is treated as a schema and pipeline failure with mature labels, June as a routing-mix change with stable within-variant breach rates, August–September as overlapping parts-performance, conditional-risk, and population effects, and October as label-ascertainment confounding. The variant-mix arithmetic checks out (about 19.9%, 24.4%, and 31.3%), the Partner Z V3 increment is right, denominators are not confused with the dashboard rate, and falsifiers plus retrain gates are explicit.

Deductions are for smaller but real defects. Covariate drift still implicates June and August–October before any diagnosis-time X is shown, and label delay is said to start in July at 98% maturity. “Increasingly underpredicts” overstates the August-to-September gap, and September’s overall rise to 0.31 is firmer than 82% maturity allows. The sketch can break because timezone-aware timestamps are passed to `filter_time_range` as naive strings, deduping requires a shared `event_id` and includes `source` despite the prose claiming source ordering, and the return value keeps raw DFGs and feature frames instead of median edge times, variant shares, and equal follow-up.
