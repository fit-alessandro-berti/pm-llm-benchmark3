7.6

The cluster rankings, bias directions, and final adjudication are largely coherent and well supported. Identical timestamps are not treated as proof of simultaneous work, logging batches are separated from physical batches, and the G6 mechanism (arrival-to-bulk-close waiting plus zero `complete−start` service) is correct. Checked figures hold: G2 services 8/14/21/27 min, G4 waits 7–19 min with 4 min service, G5 work content 70 min (~233% as one resource, ~78% for three), G6 waits 35–175 min, and G8 waits 38–58 min with 7 min service.

The score is held down by concrete defects. Section 5 assigns G8 1-minute precision and says its second-level order should not be overread; G8 is a 1-second scanner log, and that sentence conflicts with the scanner/clock treatment elsewhere. The required PM4Py comparison never extracts batch categories (simultaneous, start, end, sequential); detection counts and parameter pairs are not that comparison, and a trailing category field would be dropped by `b[:5]`. Assumptions are not labeled. G8’s top rank is a bit strong given the prompt’s non-causal caveat and unsynchronized clocks. G7’s staggered `recorded_at` (14:16 through 14:20) is unused as evidence against true simultaneity. Rendered expressions such as `09:0309:00` and `completion10:00` are unclear, and the G6 censoring sentence can be read as assigning the whole arrival-to-recording span to service.
