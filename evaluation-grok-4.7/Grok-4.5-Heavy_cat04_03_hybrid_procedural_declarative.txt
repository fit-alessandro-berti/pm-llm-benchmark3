5.5

The hybrid split is coherent: a block-structured backbone, DECLARE for unconditional activity rules, the log skeleton for frequencies, R12 as warnings only, and an external layer for resources and for the escalation–activation order. The R5/R6 write-up states two real policies, selects strict legal precedence, and names the audit risk. That design does not survive its own dictionaries, checker code, or verdict matrix.

The temporal matrix is arithmetically wrong under the profile the answer itself defines. With `zeta = 2`, a pair deviates only outside `[mean − 2·sd, mean + 2·sd]`. Sanctions Escalation@6 → Obtain Legal Opinion@23 is 17 h, inside `[0, 24]` h. Activate Vendor@13 → Release First Payment@90 is 77 h, inside `[0, 144]` h. T6 is a temporal pass under both resolutions. The cell `warning` and the phrase “temporal also late” are false. T6’s only hard break under Resolution A is the data-layer order check; the 24 h emergency clock (opinion 10 h after activation) is also satisfied and is a different rule from R12. T5 is likewise a temporal pass: Activate Vendor@9 → Release First Payment@80 differs from the 72 h mean by 1 h. `not evaluated` erases a measured, conforming pair. T7’s 34 h escalation-to-opinion gap is a genuine warning (`|34 − 12| > 12`); the 2 h payment gap is not (`70 < 72`). The upper-bound formula is applied correctly only on T7.

The models disagree on the same activities. DECLARE `exactly_one` on Verify Bank Account forbids the second verification R11 requires. The log skeleton allows `{0, 1, 2}`, so it also accepts a trace that never verifies the account. The process tree executes that label once; projecting away Change Bank Details and Suspend Vendor leaves both Verify events in the tree alphabet, so a compliant re-verification trace fails the backbone. Section 9’s claim that DECLARE and the skeleton “will always agree” is false on this frequency, on `(Enhanced Due Diligence, Reject Vendor)` (DECLARE noncoexistence only), and on multi-cycle clarification: `altresponse` is per request, while `always_after` does not force counts to match (`equivalence` is empty; both clarification frequencies are an arbitrary `range(0, 5)`).

R11 is misread. “At most one Release First Payment” does not make “re-verify before any later payment” vacuous. The single payment can sit after Activate Vendor and after Change Bank Details. `response(Change Bank Details, Suspend Vendor)` also fires when the change is before activation, which R11 does not require. The re-verify guard is an ellipsis, not a check.

Section 7 does not implement the layer the matrix and the deployment section assume. `empty` is undefined, so the four-eyes function cannot run. As written, “cases that remain are failures” inverts `pm4py.filter_four_eyes_principle` (default `positive=True` keeps different-resource traces). That procedure would fail T1 and pass T4, the opposite of the matrix. The R5 function returns a combined Resolution A/B string and, if an emergency attribute were present inside 24 h, would accept the inversion the chosen policy forbids. No check implements “exactly one of the two approvals if and only if the case is accepted,” even though the mapping names that data-layer rule authoritative. The clarification walker would fail T5, but the matrix marks the resource/data cell `pass`.

`directly_follows: set()` is a whitelist in log-skeleton conformance, not an absent constraint. Every trace with two events contains a directly-follows pair outside that set, so the skeleton column is `fail` for T1–T4, T6, and T7 as well as for T5 and T8. Empty was defended as “no DF constraint”; under the API it is the strongest DF constraint.

Smaller defects sit on top of those. Several procedural cells use `pass (projected)`, which is outside the required vocabulary. The tree constructor is not shown setting parent links. R6’s 24 h deadline is described as if the R12 profile were that deadline. T8’s explanation stops at co-occurrence and omits the further DECLARE failure of `response(Activate Vendor, Release First Payment)` and of `noncoexistence(Manager Approval, Reject Vendor)`.

What holds is the backbone tree for the eight given traces after projection, the DECLARE pass/fail column on those traces (every test has a single Verify Bank Account), the T4 four-eyes outcome, the T7 warning, the T8 exclusion failure, and the decision to keep conditional legal order out of unconditional precedence. The required adjudication and the cross-layer model are not internally consistent.
