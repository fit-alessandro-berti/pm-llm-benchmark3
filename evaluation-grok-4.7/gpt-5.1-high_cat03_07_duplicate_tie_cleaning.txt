6.2

The prose design is structurally complete and, in its conservative choices, mostly sound: merge only the reconciled scan e03/e04, keep e05 and e14, keep summary and harmonization-collision events with refined labels, separate semantic precedence from tie-breaks, and spell out provenance and failure modes. The score stops there because the required PM4Py pipeline contradicts that design, misstates the library, and would violate the problem’s own constraints. Several ordering and sensitivity claims are also internally inconsistent.

The implementation is the largest failure. `add_business_order_rank` sorts and then assigns `reset_index().index` back onto the unsorted frame, so the rank collapses to the original row position and the sort is discarded. Even if that alignment were fixed, the key `(original_timestamp, source, source_seq, ingested_at, eid)` is alphabetical by source. At 10:00 that yields ERP pack, then the I2 scan, then the I1 pick, then the WMS pack, which breaks the answer’s own pick-before-pack rule. At 12:00 it places the ERP payment summary before PSP authorize/capture because `ERP < PSP`, contradicting Fact 8 and the order in §3. Domain constraints never appear in the code; `source_seq` is applied as a global column even though it is defined only within one source.

The PM4Py calls are not the functions’ real contract. `ocel_drop_duplicates` and `ocel_merge_duplicates` key off equal activity and timestamp, with `have_common_object` as the extra switch. The block instead passes invented keys (`consider_attributes`, `object_signature_key`, `keep_provenance`, `column`, `new_column`) and runs both routines on the whole log. Default drop/merge behavior collapses every same-label tie group, including Pick Item e03/e04/e05 and Capture Payment e13/e14/e15, which drops or merges e05 and e14. That is the opposite of §4’s “exact technical duplicates only” rule and of the stated aggressive policy (identical object sets, so e06/e07 and e13/e14 stay apart). `ocel_sort_by_additional_column` is called with `column=` rather than the additional-column argument, and `ocel_add_index_based_timedelta` is called as if it wrote a separate `timestamp_ordered` column; the real helper shifts the timestamp column itself. `merge_verified_pair` leaves `attribute_precedence` unused, unions every E2O link with no object-type guard, can overwrite the source-qualified backup on the canonical row, and writes a provenance record that does not match the §8 schema. The candidate pre-check groups on identical `object_signature` only, so it never surfaces e06/e07 or e13/e15, the pairs the prose says need judgment.

The decision tree disagrees with the later scenarios. Step 3A classifies e11/e16 as one physical event and requires a merge; the conservative pipeline keeps both under refined labels. Step 3’s “no contradictory object” gate makes branch 3D unreachable for different Payment or Item objects. Step 4 then labels any distinct lifecycle pair as a harmonization collision or a summary/detail pair, which those categories do not mean. The tie-break example orders WMS e03* before SCANNER e05, while lexicographic `(source, source_seq, eid)` puts SCANNER first. Scanner sequence 551 before 552, and PSP sequence 401 before 402, are within-source evidence; the write-up treats both orders as merely arbitrary concurrency. “Issue Invoice precedes every payment” is stated as a given domain constraint; the known facts do not say that. “Facts 7, 51” is an erroneous citation.

Sensitivity arithmetic for 17/16/14/14 events and about 44/41/37/36 relations is right for the prose policies, and the e08/e09 versus e06/e07 contrast is the right damage story. The comparison still misses the main conservative effect: raw I1 has two Pick Item events and a false item-level rework edge that disappears once e04 is merged. It also treats the arbitrary e11→e16 linearization as a real OC-DFG flow, and it says the aggressive logistics merge shortens lead time even though e08 and e09 already share 10:15:00, so measured duration is unchanged and only the stage count changes. Relation-level reversibility is incomplete: the provenance schema does not store per-source E2O sets, `source_seq`, `payload_hash`, or `ingested_at`, so the rebuild procedure cannot recover the raw log once a union has mixed links.

The essay also runs far past the requested 1,900–2,900 words of prose, repeating the same merge choices across §§2, 4, 5, and 7. The conservative policy in words is usable; the pipeline that is supposed to implement it is not.
