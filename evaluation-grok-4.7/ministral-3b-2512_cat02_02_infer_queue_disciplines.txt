The write-up matches the requested seven-part skeleton, but the dispatch reconstruction is not reliable enough to carry the diagnoses. Eligible sets are repeatedly incomplete or timed incorrectly, FIFO is applied backward, and several “episodes” are not in the log.

**QA is not non-preemptive priority.** A1’s order is A01, A03, A02, A04, A05, A06, A07, A08, with calibration from 08:52–08:58. A02 (priority 3, estimate 20) runs 08:32–08:52 before A04 (priority 1, estimate 7). A08 (priority 1) then waits through A05, A06, and A07 and starts at 09:41. That is FIFO, with A03 pulled in at its 08:20 appointment while the server is free, and A06’s 09:15 appointment missed because A05 is already in service. The cited support is false: A04 is not taken immediately after A03, and A08 does not bypass A05. The 08:32 row selects A04 from {A03, A04}; A03 has already finished and the job started is A02. At 09:05 the eligible set also includes A07 and A08, and FIFO selects A05, not A06. Confidence 85 is attached to a rule the same section says is contradicted.

**QB is not preemptive priority plus an SPT fallback.** B02 does preempt B01 at 09:16, and the log says that stop is an urgent arrival. The 09:31 mark is B02’s completion and B01’s resume, while B03 (priority 2) is already waiting — a direct violation of preemptive priority. At 09:48 the server starts B03 even though B05 (priority 1, arrived 09:46) is waiting and B04 is shorter (estimate 8 versus 12); the stated reason is dispatcher reassignment, not SPT. B05 runs 10:00–10:14, before B06, so “B05 resumed after B06” is the wrong order. B06 is not waiting at 10:00. After B05, B01 is resumed ahead of B06, and at 10:28 B04 is taken ahead of B06. B07’s 10:30 appointment starts at 10:56. Skill routing adds nothing: every QB job is general on B1.

**QC is not deadline-aware priority.** At 10:10 the waiting set is C01–C04, not {C01, C02}. Any order of those four meets their deadlines, and priority would take C03, but C02 (shortest estimate) starts. Realized durations equal the estimates. Later choices diverge: C04 at 10:32 over shorter C06 and priority-1 C05; C05 only at 10:54, after its 10:50 deadline; C06 at 11:34; then C01 at 11:40 ahead of C07, so C07 misses 12:00. The 10:32 and 11:34 rows name the job that is finishing, not the job selected. Calling the secondary mechanism an appointment window misstates deadlines. The FIFO tie-break claim is the same reversed FIFO rule.

**QD invents a preemption and collapses three resources into one queue.** No QD segment is split. D01 and D02 start together on D-M and D-F. D05 does not preempt D04. D10 does not bypass D09: D09 finishes at 12:03 on D-M; D10 runs later on D-X. D08 starts at 11:35, before D07 at 11:48. The real constraints are skill eligibility (only D-F can do D05, only D-X can do D07), D-X held idle until D06’s 11:30 appointment, D-F’s meeting 12:00–12:20, and D-F not starting D05 when it would run into that meeting. Within a resource, D04 is chosen over already-waiting D08 (priority 1 and shorter), and D07 over D10 (priority 1 and shorter). That contradicts both priority and SPT. Confidence 90, and naming QD the most identifiable queue on the basis of a nonexistent preemption, reverses the evidence.

The discrimination tests do not separate the live alternatives. A03 and A05 are not simultaneous candidates. “Record preemption cost” does not state the next job each rule would pick. The four exceptions are ranked, but the mechanisms are wrong: a two-minute scan delay cannot move B07 by 26 minutes; D-F’s calendar does not create a D05–D04 preemption; FIFO would have made C01 earlier, not later. The data-collection list repeats the prompt’s generic fields and never targets the actual forks (decision time versus previous-job start on QC, exclusive-skill versus idle D-F on D10, appointment hold versus D-X refusing mechanical work, resume-stack versus reassignment on QB).
