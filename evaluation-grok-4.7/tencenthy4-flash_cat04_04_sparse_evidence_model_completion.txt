The final document meets the required structure and is a strong, internally consistent three-hypothesis analysis. Conservative is an exact ProcessTree XOR of the seven observed variants; balanced and permissive are genuinely different POWLs; all seven observed traces sit in the balanced and permissive languages; SC-before-both-checks is excluded by balanced and admitted only by permissive; and D1–D7 plus S1–S7 are tied to concrete modeling choices without treating DFG reversals as concurrency.

Points come off for these defects. The manual acceptance search stops once every visible label is consumed, so any trailing silent transition needed to reach the final marking is never fired; bounded playout has the same cutoff at `max_events`. That can false-fail the required positive suite even though these particular nets may not have a trailing silent. In the conservative section, failure to admit legitimate unseen interleavings is listed as underfitting; under process-mining usage that is overfitting, and one bullet even says “Overfits” inside the underfitting list. The balanced XOR also puts every “SC after both checks” trace in two branches and never notes that redundancy. Those are real, but they do not break the candidate languages or the recommendation.
