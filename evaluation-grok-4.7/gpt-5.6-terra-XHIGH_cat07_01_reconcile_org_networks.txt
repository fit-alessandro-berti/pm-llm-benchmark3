7.5

The essay meets the required structure, stays multi-hypothesis, and is generally well calibrated: centrality is not treated as good or bad, removal is rejected unless the coordination function is replaced, bots and vendors are kept in view, and misconduct is not inferred. The subcontracting-name caveat, the `Assign Specialist` label gap, the absence-period confounds, and the split between immediate controls, monitoring, and deferred redesign are all sound. The executive summary is about 157 words.

The central reconciliation is only partly valid. Working-together counts case co-occurrence, so short routing events still count. Brevity can change the substantive reading of a tie; it does not lower the co-case rate. Spread across partners can explain weak individual edges and exclusion from the North/South clusters, but it does not explain Mira’s low weighted degree (0.16). More partners would raise a degree sum. The likely mechanisms are normalization — by her 905 cases or by the whole log — and one-off intersections that are large in handover counts but small as shares. That arithmetic is missing.

The handover account overreaches. Listed Mira outbound edges (Omar 210, Priya 180, Carla 350, Diego 330, Elise 290, Farid 280) sum to 1,640, and listed inbound edges sum to about 1,580, against 960 events. Under a direct-succession definition those totals cannot be raw event counts. Temps hand work to Mira; they are not shown as her destinations. No top edge links her with either team lead. Calling team leads and temps destination groups is unsupported.

`pm4py.filter_variants` is misapplied. The proposed “variants” are resource paths. Variant filters operate on activity sequences unless the activity classifier is explicitly redefined. Role discovery also collapses Elise and Farid into “assessment and negotiation,” although their stated work is damage assessment and missing-information requests. The summary’s “so the coordination function has operational value” is slightly stronger than one confounded six-day contrast supports, despite better hedging in the body.
