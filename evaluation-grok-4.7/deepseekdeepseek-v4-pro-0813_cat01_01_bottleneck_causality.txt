The final assessment correctly detects the crude-versus-complexity reversal, refuses to treat stratification as identification, separates queueing from service, and recommends a limited pilot plus capacity work instead of a blind 50% move. It is not nearly flawless. The diagnostic script calls `resource_group` although the embedded CSV column is `group`, so it fails before any comparison, overlap count, warning, or DFG is produced. Section 1 is slightly under 180 words and gives evidence against a harmful Orion effect rather than evidence against its own `mainly confounded` verdict. The graph makes shift a mediator of group, while the interpretation treats residual shift imbalance as leftover confounding of the complexity-stratified contrast; total, direct, and mediated effects are therefore not cleanly separated. “Shorter or equal” is inaccurate, the standardized contrast is left as the incomplete phrase “Orion 2.51 h,” and structural non-overlap—no Medium or High Orion day cases—is softened to “small cells.”
