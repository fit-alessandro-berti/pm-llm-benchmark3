7.2

The review is structurally complete and substantively strong: use cases are separated, every reported metric is interpreted without a fake composite score, S and C are judged from admitted traces, the listed pathologies are ranked, and the hybrid idea (mandatory block, conditional rules outside the tree, indeterminate rather than silent pass) is the right governance response. B1–B8 are correct for S and C under ordinary process-tree semantics. The dissent is a real counterargument and stays inside 150–250 words.

The centerpiece code is not sound. `*(tau, correction, tau)` is not a PM4Py loop. `*` takes exactly two children (do, then redo); exit is implicit. A third child is ill-formed and will fail tree-to-net conversion or soundness, so `model_third` does not reliably have the zero-or-more correction language claimed in §§7–8. The intended idiom was the binary loop `*(tau, correction_cycle)`.

`policy_check` does not implement the stated decisions. `names.index("Credit Check")` and `names.index("Technical Review")` raise if either activity is absent, so B3 crashes instead of failing. `external_order is None` returns `INDETERMINATE` before expedite and correction checks, which hides definite violations. The same function returns `PASS` for B8; rejection depends entirely on a tree that the snippet may not even compile. Sections 8’s three “passes” omit that those traces contain no Supplier Review, so the checker passes them only when `external_order` is false. One of the three “new” adversarial traces is B6 again, leaving only two distinct probes.

Supplier Review is sequenced after both mandatory checks although P3–P4 do not require that, and §3 treats Supplier between the checks as a defect without a policy basis. Credit Check and Technical Review are duplicated in the third tree, but that alignment/precision hazard is never listed as a limitation after being used against C. Alignment fitness/precision is also never contrasted with token-based-replay generalization. The design is close; the executable semantics and the audit of those semantics are not.
