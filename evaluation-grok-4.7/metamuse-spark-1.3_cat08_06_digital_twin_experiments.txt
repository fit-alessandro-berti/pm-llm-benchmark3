7.4

The design is strong on non-stationary demand, calendars, latent objections, cross-case coupling, paired uncertainty, and validation, but it is not nearly flawless. Several concrete inconsistencies and API/modeling slips keep it well below a top score.

The scope correctly rejects independent-case replay: NHPP arrivals, architect bursts, objection contagion, inspection bundling, skill-specific calendars, and an AND-join at decision drafting are all treated as first-class. Service versus waiting is argued carefully in the estimation plan, including calendar subtraction when only completions exist. Objections stay a latent mixture estimated from the public-notice duration distribution, not fake observed events. The experiment table covers B0, D1–D10, an extra combo, and three stresses, with trade-offs and a warning against additive overclaim. Warm-up, common random numbers, quantile-focused intervals, cluster bootstrap, and temporal holdout are appropriate. Optimality is not claimed.

The control flow is not stable. The tree is sequence(parallel reviews, optional notice, inspection loop, drafting, optional legal). Section 1 instead makes drafting a join over planning, fire, environment, notice, and inspection, and the engine comment parallelizes notice with the reviews. That changes induced waiting and how D5 overlaps legal. D6 is named as a reserved planner but implemented as age preemption. `prior_violations` is a case state variable even though it is absent from the log, while D2 depends on it; no-shows are named in the source data and then dropped. “Removed emergencies truncate the tail” conflicts with treating them as fast-track cases, which would truncate short durations and bias the log upward. “Peak days ~90–100” and “60–70% of Simple skip inspection” are stated as if estimated. Fire “2 FTE equivalent, Tue–Thu only” is reduced to two servers on three days without stating whether weekly capacity is 6 or 10 person-days. The warm-up line “60-day warm-up with peak-season arrivals discarded” muddles a transient seasonal system with classic steady-state deletion.

The skeleton follows the requested split and avoids `pm4py.read_csv`, but it is not faithful enough. The excerpt drops most events, so the sojourn column is not a real illustration. `waiting_days_est` is assigned the sojourn while the comment says it is the residual after service and calendar time, and service is left as NaN. `pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_token_replay` and `pm4py.statistics.traces.pandas.case_statistics` are not the current entry points (`conformance_diagnostics_token_based_replay`, `statistics.traces.generic.pandas.case_statistics`). The variant fallback calls `get_variants` on the pandas module, which exposes `get_variants_count`. The DES placeholders return a count and a dummy row, so the couplings specified in the design are not actually wired, and the per-replication RNG does not drive the arrival generator.
