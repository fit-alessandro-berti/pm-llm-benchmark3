5.5

The write-up covers every required heading and stays appropriately conditional (pilot first, East treated differently, fairness and learning included, averages not treated as proof). It is not a reliable forecast or a usable analysis sketch.

The central quantitative tensions in the data are never tested. Legal/Risk are already at about 91–93% utilization while referral volume rises from 9 to 12 per 100 cases; one local supervisor is removed while approval handovers rise from 42 to 49 per 100. Those changes can push queues past capacity, yet management’s small wait increases (9.6h to 10.5h, 2.4h to 3.0h) are adopted as the central case. Handover frequencies and the given rework-after-handover rates are not aggregated, so the claim of fewer handovers is not checked against the tables.

The optimistic scenario contradicts itself: it says waits are stable or lower, then cites 4.0h against a current 3.2h specialist handover and a 1.6h return. The 10–15% cycle-time improvement is asserted without a path from extra queue hours to multi-day cycle times. The baseline is also misstated when the existing 9-per-100 legal/risk flow is described as work previously handled locally.

The pilot cannot identify the proposed change. Pooling N_Tech, S_Tech, and E_Tech contaminates North and South, while leaving them intact means East is not using the proposed shared service. An East-only routing pilot also misses the main downside, loss of strong North/South local loops, and it sends more work into an already saturated legal/risk queue without a capacity guardrail. The stated 12-week design does not add up to the windows listed, and completed-case metrics are not adjusted for right-censoring when the 90th percentile is already 16–23 days.

The PM4Py sketch would not run. The “before” log is filtered to `"after"`, `log_after` is never defined, and later comparisons use the wrong object. `organising_key` and `case_id` are not valid parameters, handover results are treated as a dict of handover reasons, `get_service_time` is used for waiting time, role discovery is treated as a dict, filtered logs and subcontracting/working-together graphs are never compared, SLA misses are only a comment, and `format_dataframe` is called on a nested summary.
