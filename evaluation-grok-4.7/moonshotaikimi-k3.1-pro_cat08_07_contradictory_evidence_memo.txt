8.2

The memo is a strong, constraint-compliant decision: modified E with D embedded, no national scale, no blanket rollback, and an explicit speed-versus-tail trade-off. Facts, interpretations, and assumptions are separated cleanly; A–E are judged with conditions; M1–M8 are ranked by whether the result would change the decision; and the rollout gate covers tails, rework, cost scope, permits, and activation-before-billing. Process-mining evidence is used, not merely named.

Deductions are for accumulated precision problems, not for the decision itself. Apartments are described as having deteriorated within segment, while their median still improved by 2 days and only the tail, first-time-right, and complaints worsened; that flattens the median-versus-tail conflict inside the harmed group. Business and rural results, the complaint shift from waiting to “installed but not working” and billing-before-activation, NPS, and the simple-house SLA improvement are thin or absent. Lower generalization is treated too much like operational non-transportability, rather than a model-fit property that sits in tension with very low precision. The opening still leans on pilot deltas as gains R would sacrifice before the causality caveat. The numeric non-inferiority margins are appropriately labeled as risk appetite, so they are not treated as findings.
