5.5

The write-up has the required structure, uses a join rather than a sum, gets the incubation window right (Sunday 12:00–20:00), and keeps overlapping scenario probabilities and a multi-day investigation tail. Those merits do not offset several forecast-changing errors.

Friday work is not credited. A 15:54 start leaves about 2.1 h before 18:00, so a triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) service often finishes Friday; any spill resumes Monday with only the residual. The smooth path instead pauses every Friday start and restarts the full 1.5–4.5 h on Monday, producing “usable Mon 09:30–12:30.”

Priority is mis-ordered. One urgent sample outranks this high-but-not-critical batch, so the first free analyst should take that urgent sample. A second-slot start around 16:42–17:30 cannot finish a 1.5 h minimum before 18:00. The earliest Friday completion depends on jumping the urgent sample, which is never stated.

FIFO carryover is dropped. “No Friday start” is then treated as a Monday 08:00 start. The two in-process samples and up to four queued samples resume ahead of B-417, so a FIFO start is later on Monday and can spill to Tuesday.

The on-call effect is inconsistent with the rest of the answer. QA is closed Sunday, and the smooth path’s own chemistry completion is Monday morning, so Sunday versus Monday 08:30 readout often does not move the join. Section 6 still shifts the central and P80 terminal times by 18–30 h and folds contamination into the no-authorization branch. That is the wrong magnitude for the event chosen as most forecast-changing.

The Monday QA queue of about 5–7 copies Friday’s queue forward. One reviewer is still open Friday 15:30–20:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00, so the join-time queue can be much smaller. Weekend drain is the queue uncertainty the forecast was supposed to face.

The modal no-anomaly path is also mislabeled. On-call probability is only 0.25–0.45, but the “smooth” scenario requires it and names chemistry as the dominant clock. The common no-on-call case is merged with rare contamination and given a Tuesday–Wednesday center. Hour labels are off by a few hours (Monday 09:30 is about 66 h after Friday 15:30, not 64 h; Wednesday 12:00 is about 116 h, not 120 h). Rerun and microbiology scenarios never close the release step explicitly.

The overall window—earliest Monday, mass early next week, lab-investigation tail into the following week—is in the right neighborhood. The clock logic underneath it is not reliable enough to carry the forecast.
