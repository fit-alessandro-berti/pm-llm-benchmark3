8.5

The design is strong, coherent, and faithful to the constraints: it is a resource-constrained discrete-event twin with non-stationary demand, calendars, emergent waiting, latent objections, cross-case coupling, paired experiments, and no optimality claim. PM4Py use is conventional and the skeleton correctly avoids `pm4py.read_csv`, keeps service and waiting unidentified on completion-only timestamps, and separates mining from simulation.

Deductions are for remaining design gaps, not for the overall approach. Common-random-number coupling is overstated: named streams do not keep service and routing draws aligned once interventions change path length; per-case variate keys are missing. Council-calendar dependence, flagged as a driver of the Complex-variance tail and of public notice, is modeled mainly as legal-officer downtime rather than as a hearing-date constraint. The nested uncertainty budget (outer parameter draws versus 40–200 inner replications) is not specified, C3 is vague, and several decision cutoffs (5–10%, 85–90% utilization) remain round heuristics despite the caveats. The calendar scheduler is only an interface, so the next-available-slot rule is not actually designed.
