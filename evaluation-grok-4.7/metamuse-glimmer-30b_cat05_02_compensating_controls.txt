6.0

The case classifications are mostly defensible, and nominal four-eyes conformance is correctly separated from the control objective. AP-202, AP-203, AP-205, and AP-208 are judged in the right direction, and post-release but pre-settlement manager action is reasonably treated as hybrid. That is not enough to carry the paper. Several required analyses are incomplete, internally inconsistent, or factually off.

The case table drops a column on AP-209, so confidence is missing and the row no longer matches the required schema. Corrective capacity there is called “Limited” even though audit fell inside the recall window. AP-204 is marked `OBJECTIVE_FAILED` mainly for an unproven collusion story; the fabricated receipt, absent manager review, and late audit are the actual objective failure and are left out of the rationale. AP-208’s rationale omits the bank-account change three days earlier. Section 5 then claims an independent releaser would not have helped without automated validation, which is wrong: a second person is precisely the manual check when validation is down and the checklist is unsigned. AP-206 misses that the manager approved before validation ran. Collusion and credential sharing on AP-202 are stated as the loss mechanism although both are unresolved. Section 4’s hybrid semantics are not applied back to AP-201 or AP-207, where that manager step is filed as purely detective, and ED-1 is listed as if it were itself a preventive control.

The equivalence test covers the requested dimensions, but “fully equivalent” is loosely specified. Threat coverage in section 1 overstates what automated validation cannot do and does not give a consistent prevent/detect/correct/fail mapping for every control. The monitoring rule that alerts when approval is more than four hours before settlement is inverted: that is the stronger timing, not the late one. Evidence collection is thin relative to the monitoring list.

The code does not meet the specification. `pm4py` is never imported, so the four-eyes call is swallowed and every violation flag becomes false. `format_dataframe(..., org_resource=...)` is not a valid argument and can abort the script before any flags are emitted. Bank-change and repeated-pair flags are absent, emergency eligibility is a copied boolean rather than the seven conditions, and the blocked-release check is hardcoded to AP-205. The output label is correct, but the block is not reliable evidence.
