The design is strong, constraint-aware, and usable as a comparative twin specification. It keeps municipal service, queueing, calendars, statutory notice, travel, and applicant delay separate; treats objections as latent; refuses to read the process tree as the simulator; and does not claim an optimum from an unvalidated run. The remaining gaps are real and keep it short of nearly flawless.

The scope covers case state, skill-limited calendars, parallel reviews, joins, rework invalidation, appeals, architect bursts, neighborhood shocks, and inspection bundles with a hold limit. Fire FTE versus headcount, Monday clerk diversion, environmental time blocked once rather than twice, and legal council weeks are handled carefully. Bundle routing is still thin: there is no explicit inspector location, route, or no-show reschedule state, and the shared-shock mechanism does not say which activities are invalidated or how correlation is generated. D3/D5 longer travel is only implicit in “district.”

Parameter estimation correctly treats the 18-month summary as checks and priors, flags start/end truncation and open cases, and forbids stacking historical waits on simulated queues. Performance-DFG times are correctly called elapsed directly-follows times. Routing estimated only from complete traces can still bias loop rates. The arrival write-up stays at “overdispersed calendar counts” without a concrete cluster process that can reproduce the top-20 architects’ 31% share and within-week bursts.

Assumptions are labeled as testable and cover every requested class. FIFO within priority is a strong default for this office and needs a stated alternative. Business-day versus calendar-day service is settled only for the 14-day notice.

The experiment matrix includes baseline, D1–D10, constituent combinations, phased adoption, and four stresses, with paired scenarios and non-additive effects. D8 mixes priority triage with false-classification metrics even though legally required reviews are retained, so the measured error is not tied to a path change. D7 targets large architects and does not say what residual Monday load from other applicants remains. S1’s “50% peak surge” is ambiguous relative to the existing 1.7× seasonality.

Warm-up from the observed backlog, no fake steady state under growth, cohort follow-up with competing arrivals, censoring, paired replication intervals, and separate stochastic versus structural uncertainty are right. Common random numbers are not specified when a stress changes the number of arrivals. One hundred replications times parameter sets is stated without a feasible aggregation unit beyond the replication.

Validation correctly separates replay, distributional fit, utilization, bottleneck movement, and out-of-sample prediction from the causal effect of a new policy. The 10% median/p90 tolerance is tight relative to unidentified service/wait splits and can invite overfitting. Two peak seasons in 18 months give seasonal holdout very low power; that limit is noted only indirectly.

Decision metrics include tails, type-specific SLAs, district and architect fairness, utilization, rework, backlog, cost, and unresolved safety for D2/D8. The numeric gates are then softened with “such as” and “aim,” the headline duration test allows a median gain while p90 worsens by up to 5%, and an SLA gap can shrink because the best group gets worse. There is no primary endpoint, indifference zone, multiplicity control, or cost-per-day threshold. The 30/60/90/180-day examples are not checked against the supplied medians and p90s.

The skeleton correctly avoids `pm4py.read_csv`, uses `format_dataframe`, parses the tree, and separates discovery from the engine. Variants, both DFGs, arrival average, and conditional service/wait columns match PM4Py’s dataframe conventions, including the three-way DFG return and seconds for interarrival time. The embedded log drops most of the given P001/P002 events, so the discovered variants contradict the stated process, and the code does not say those traces were cut. Cross-case behavior is only a comment, and the single sequential RNG cannot implement the keyed streams the text requires.
