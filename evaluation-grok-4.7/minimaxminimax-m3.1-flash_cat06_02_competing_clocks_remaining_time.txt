The final forecast is a strong calendar-aware decomposition: parallel branches are combined with a maximum, weekend closures are applied, Saturday release is correctly ruled out, and the temperature excursion is treated as correlated rather than multiplied through as an independent anomaly. The main hour-to-clock bands in section 4 check out, and the microbiology staffing floor versus chemistry co-limitation is a defensible refinement.

It is not nearly flawless. The severe-tail extension equates 266–291 hours with Wednesday 30 September–Friday 2 October; those hours land on Tuesday 29 September evening through Wednesday 30 September, roughly one to two days earlier. The laboratory-investigation probability of about 0.07–0.25 is high relative to a 0.30–0.55 investigation chance and a 20–40% laboratory share, so the P90 is pulled too far out. Risk-priority chemistry at Monday 09:30–13:00 does not clearly credit Friday progress before 18:00, and the claimed Friday-completion edge contradicts the assumption that the urgent sample is ahead. With FIFO at 55%, the Monday 13:30–18:30 central band is also somewhat early.
