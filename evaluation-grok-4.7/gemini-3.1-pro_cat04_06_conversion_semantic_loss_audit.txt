4.8

The write-up has the required nine sections, the right primary pipeline, a justified expectation that a process tree will fail, and a sound storage rule (keep the POWL authoritative and regenerate diagrams one way). Those merits do not offset several errors in the semantic audit and in the code that is supposed to test it.

The invariant table does not pin down the behavior it names. INV-2’s positive witness is only one order of Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence, and its negative witness is Assess/Prepare before Register, which is an INV-1 violation. INV-5’s negative witness (Configure Trial immediately after Register) is illegal because verification has not been enabled, so a converter that forces Legal Review and verification into a sequence would still reject it. Optionality never has a witness in which Legal Review is absent. Every row is marked observable after hiding silent steps, including concurrency, which interleavings only approximate. Ellipsis traces are not executable witnesses.

Section 4 then misuses the required status words. Almost every cell says `expected preserved`, while the evidence column says “Validated via…”. Tree cells say `conversion failed` even though nothing was executed. `not tested` is the term that matches the work shown. The concurrency discussion treats the Petri net as if it only had interleaving semantics. In this translation the net is the POWL semantics: jointly enabled transitions are the concurrency. Unique visible labels are also described as preventing non-determinism; they only prevent aliasing.

The conversion code uses real PM4Py calls, catches a tree failure, round-trips BPMN, and draws the main artifacts. It never checks that initial or final markings are non-null before soundness, replay, or save calls, which the task explicitly requires. Workflow-net and soundness checks are not applied to every accepting net: the source net is omitted in the conversion section, and a tree round-trip net is never compared later.

The bounded explorer is not a valid equivalence check. Visited states are `(hash(marking), visible length)`, so two different visible traces that reach the same marking at the same length collapse. The second interleaving of Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence is dropped, which is the main concurrency fact under test. There is no separate silent-step bound. Positive and negative witnesses are never replayed. Reachability and playout are omitted with no reason. The note still calls the length-20 comparison exhaustive. Truncation plus this visited set does not enumerate the bounded language; enabled-set order can also make the equality result flaky.

Rejection item 2 is labeled “loss of required interleaving” but the sample trace is the forbidden order Inspect Trial then Configure Trial. Residual claim 3 says a passed suite still cannot rule out deadlock or livelock beyond length 20. A successful full soundness check is a state-space result, not a prefix of length 20. What remains unproved, and should have been stated, is unbounded language equivalence, pomset/step equivalence versus traces, and silent-branch bisimulation.

Visualization comments in section 6 are directionally useful, but empty XOR paths, cascading AND-joins, and backward loop edges are stated as if a diagram had been produced. Section 8 is the strongest part and matches the control-flow constraints.
