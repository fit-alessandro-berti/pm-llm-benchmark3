6.5

The design covers every required section and is serious about non-stationary demand, calendars, rework, cross-case effects, and unobserved objections. It is not nearly flawless: several claims that the simulation depends on are wrong or internally inconsistent.

PM4Py handling misses the stated conventions. `pm4py.parse_process_tree` accepts the operator string (`->`, `X`, `+`, `*`, `tau`); it does not require PTML XML. The skeleton never calls it. `pm4py.discover_dfg` returns a DFG, not `(dfg, starts, ends)`, so the three-way unpack in analysis and routing estimation is invalid. `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg` exists; the manual substitute is only completion-to-completion gaps, and those columns are not attached to the log. Case-arrival average is a span rate, not a mean inter-arrival. Activity labels are shortened (`Request missing docs`) so the excerpt, tree, and engine do not name the same events.

The narrative says queues, calendars, and dependencies drive the twin. In the engine, `_start_activity` samples service time and schedules completion. Availability, capacity, council weeks, Monday clerk loss, planner dedication, and travel are never applied. Architect bursts are a TODO. Objection draws are never fired, so public notice is just a lognormal “service” time, which contradicts the fixed 14-day minimum plus an unobserved objection extension. Inspection bundling usually sees a bundle of one, and travel is not reduced. Planning rework and appeals, both in the process context, are not executed. Routing and rejection probabilities are hardcoded and disconnected from the estimators. With `prior_violations` defaulting to false, D2 skips inspection for every cheap simple renovation.

Arrival rates multiply an average base rate by unnormalized seasonal and Monday factors, so volume is inflated and the Monday spike is double-counted. Identical seeds are treated as common random numbers even though D1/D2 change how many random draws a case consumes; legal and reject draws also use the global NumPy RNG. `compare_scenarios` reports the 2.5/97.5 percentiles of 30 replication medians as a confidence interval. Many scenarios are tested at p < 0.05 with no multiplicity control, then the winner is reused in stress tests.

Assumptions, the experiment matrix, warm-up, validation, and decision thresholds are the strongest parts, and the text correctly refuses to treat objections as logged events or to claim optimality. Those sections cannot carry the incorrect PM4Py API, the calendar-free event loop, or the broken uncertainty mechanics.
