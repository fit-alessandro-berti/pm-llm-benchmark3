4.5

The answer catches the obvious full-trace aggregates and writes a complete five-part structure, but it fails the distinctions this audit turns on. Several sequence patterns are treated as admissible prefix evidence, the appeal edge is given the wrong process meaning, and the revised case narratives state future events as if they were already known at prediction time.

Section 1 correctly flags `total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `same_day_document_count_snapshot`, `final_decision`, `risk_score_current`, and `last_resource_role` as unavailable at `t0`, and it correctly keeps `initial_damage_complexity`, `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0`, and `queue_length_at_t0` in the admissible set, with the prior-claim SQL defect identified. That is not enough. `total_case_duration_hours` is not merely a post-closure covariate: a 10-day settlement breach is a duration outcome, so this feature is near-label leakage, and the write-up never says so. `final_handler_team` is closure-time information with no historical as-of timestamp; “High” plus “flag as post-hoc if retained” still leaves a leaked field in play. `queue_length_at_t0` is over-penalized at medium severity for a timestamped snapshot whose real defect is a ±5-minute join tolerance.

The sequence block is substantially wrong. At this `t0`, the prefix is only Claim Registered → Coverage Check Completed. `Documents Requested → Documents Received`, `Coverage Check Completed → Fraud Investigation Opened`, and `Damage Assessment Completed → Settlement Decision Recorded` are later, process-feasible edges, not prefix patterns, and retaining them accepts full-trace attributions as early warnings. `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` is the legitimate reconsideration edge after Partial or Deny; it is future leakage, not an impossible order. The answer states the opposite: that an appeal cannot precede a settlement decision. `Claim Closed → Damage Assessment Completed` is the pattern that should be treated as normatively impossible or, given equal timestamps and a missing sequence index, as ambiguous. “Remove or invert” is not a repair. Document-request and appeal replacements are also hollow at this milestone, because those activities are not yet enabled, so a prefix count is structurally zero and cannot support the team’s “early rework” story. `claimant_channel` and `initial_reserve` are marked valid without a lineage proof. Employee experience joined as of extraction date is never audited.

Section 2 names the right check families, then encodes the wrong model. The constraint text drops the actual relations. It never states that a second `Settlement Decision Recorded` is valid only after an appeal, and that an appeal is valid only after Partial or Deny. Same-timestamp pairs with no sequence index are unresolved, not proven reversals. Fraud investigation is trigger-based and parallel, not “enabled at any time after coverage.” A frequent directly-follows edge is never distinguished from a normative rule. Alignment, footprint, and prefix replay are not given a role or a limitation.

Section 3 misses the required “unusable until lineage is repaired” class and instead blesses three future patterns as legitimate prefix signals, carrying their SHAP signs into the claim. “Due to longer processing,” “due to resource constraints,” and “due to external factors” are causal wording inside a section that also says the effects are not causal. `queue_length_at_t0` is listed twice and is declared non-actionable here, then used for resource-allocation advice later. Predictive association, causal effect, operational action, and fairness review are not separated as different standards. Region and channel are appropriately kept for review rather than deleted.

Section 4 violates the case record. Case X’s prefix cannot contain an appeal, yet the revised drivers and narrative say an appeal was already opened and recommend early dispute resolution. Short elapsed time, a real negative prefix signal, is dropped. Case Y is marked “retain” even though `full_trace_variant_id` (+0.31) encodes documents, damage assessment, two decisions, appeal, and closure. Renaming it `prefix_trace_variant_id` and narrating that path as already followed reproduces the leaked future. The buggy prior-claim count stays in; the protective queue value of 4 is omitted. Case Z is the best of the three: invalidation is justified, and the missing index, mutable risk score, complete-case role, and same-day snapshot are the right ambiguities. Week 52 as a holiday and policy-change confound is still absent, and “process-impossible” is asserted alongside an unresolved timestamp tie.

Section 5 does cover feature-store as-of timestamps, prefix recomputation, conformance tests, retraining and calibration, stability, proxy review, and drift monitoring. It also prescribes `appeal_opened_before_t0` and a prefix variant as if those repairs preserved the leaked stories, and it never requires as-of joins for employee experience or an explicit holiday/policy feature in place of raw `calendar_week`.
