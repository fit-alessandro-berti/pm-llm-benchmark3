4.5

The headline pattern is right: Orion’s crude cycle-time disadvantage (21.96 h vs 15.24 h) reverses inside every complexity stratum, so a blind 50% transfer to Nova is not justified. That finding is not enough to carry the rest of the assessment.

The executive verdict treats the reversal as showing the crude gap “is an artifact… not a causal penalty.” That is stronger than the design allows. Complexity stratification does not block shift, load, compliance, skill, or unobserved risk, and most complexity×shift×group cells have almost no overlap. Calling night coverage and compliance “evidence against pure confounding” also points the wrong way: those factors are additional reasons the crude contrast is not a group effect. Total, direct, and mediated effects are never separated.

Section 4 then permits the causal reading the task forbids (“Orion’s routing or capacity yields within-stratum efficiency”). Several mechanism sizes are invented and contradicted by the cases. Within medium Orion, compliance-required waits average about 8.6 h versus about 9.9 h when compliance is not required. Within high Orion, rework and non-rework service times are both about 9.2 h. Claims that compliance adds 2–3 h and rework adds 1–2 h are false. Day-versus-night and compliance contrasts are not calculated.

Causal roles are misassigned. `arrival_load` and `compliance_required` are not mediators of resource group; compliance is a case-policy variable. Shift is not a collider merely because it might affect completion. The graph omits the dominant empirical path from complexity to service and cycle time, and it omits quality.

The required code would not run. It never calls `pm4py.parse_event_log_string`, builds an invalid `EventLog`, and treats `discover_dfg` and `log.variants` incorrectly, so the DFG and variant comparison is not reproducible. The executive section is under 180 words and the recommendation is under 120. The pilot and capacity phases are directionally safer than the 50% move, but the rollback thresholds are not estimable at the proposed volume, and the write-up never uses the positivity failure the data already show.
