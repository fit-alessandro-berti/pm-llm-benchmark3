The required section structure is present and several assignments are defensible (C001 to R8, C002 kept with R6, C007 and C016 kept for continuity, C008 kept with R7, C009 used as a real diagnosis training case, and high-overtime R2/R4/R9 generally protected). The worker note is appropriately non-punitive, and the comparison does discuss trade-offs rather than a single optimum. Those merits do not offset hard failures in the assignment itself.

The batch violates its own cap. R3 is given C003, C004, C007, C013, and C014 — five new assignments against a maximum of four — while the write-up claims the limit is met and describes three different cases as the fourth. R6 is given four cases (C002, C005, C010, C011) but is reported as three. Hours remaining are never scored, so R3’s five cases in 6.0 hours and R6’s urgent cluster in 6.5 hours are not checked for feasibility.

C015 is illegal. R10’s Security score is 0, not 1, and skill 0 cannot perform Security Review even under R7/R8 supervision. The same is true of R5. That assignment is also one of the two cases used to claim the training quota. The “2/10 = 20%” claim is therefore wrong on both sides: C015 does not qualify, and C008 is high priority, so the normal/low pool is 9 cases, not 10. Valid training share is about 1/9.

Continuity is dropped without a sufficient reason on C004 (owner R1, Eligibility skill 3, low handover, routine, 30h) and C005 (owner R5, Negotiation skill 3, low handover, customer waiting). C003 is moved when its reassignment count is already 2, without facing the stated 1.8× rework / 1.4× appeal evidence. The scorer is not reproducible: weights are undefined, case order is unspecified, required skill is treated as a number, and higher skill is both rewarded and described as penalized.

The audit list omits rework/appeal after assignment. The PM4Py sketch misuses `filter_case_performance` as an SLA filter, treats `view_sna` as a statistic, and treats `get_variants` as workload. It does not cover role discovery, resource similarity, working-together networks, batches, waiting-time insertion, or attribute filtering, and it does not show how the historical time and rework effects would set the policy.
