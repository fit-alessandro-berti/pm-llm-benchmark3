6.2

The write-up is a real conformance opinion: every case is classified, both briefs cite the record, and authority, expiry, scope, and logging are separated. It is not reliable on the two points the task weights most heavily — POWL semantics and consistent application of its own authority standard.

The revised model does not accept the behavior it blesses. `checks` (both Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review) is ordered before the XOR, while the auto and emergency branches contain those activities again. SUP-702 and SUP-704 therefore need two quality reviews and two sanctions checks, plus, on the emergency path, an invented `Provisional_Activation_Emergency` before `Activate_Supplier`. The same `sanctions` and `quality` objects are inserted into more than one parent, which PM4Py rejects. Inside the emergency branch, quality and manual approval have no edge from activation, so the comment that they trail activation is false. `LOOP(τ, Request_More_Info)` is a fair zero-or-more pattern, but it sits after both checks, so it cannot generate SUP-707, where both requests fall between sanctions and quality. The limitation note blames amount, time, and identity predicates for this; those predicates are real POWL limits, and the control-flow encoding is independently wrong.

The authority reasoning contradicts itself. Section 1 puts the burden of proving a competent overlay on the party claiming an exception and treats PD-3’s power to suspend CS-9 as unresolved. Section 5 then labels SUP-702 a high-confidence justified exception. Section 7 sets that same overlay to `monitored_exception_only` and says that status is not a compliance override. One of those three positions can be defended; holding all three cannot. FD-7 is in better shape: SUP-704 meets the written window, cap, sanctions-before-activation rule, next-business-day review, and daily monitoring. SUP-705 and SUP-714 are correctly refused as self-renewals.

Case-level gaps remain. SUP-708 is also manual approval after activation, not only sanctions after activation. SUP-702 never shows the automated master-data validation PD-3 requires. SUP-710’s still-valid prior certification is given in the record and then treated as unresolved. The mapping date 2026-06-27 is invented. SUP-711 is labeled “COMPLIANT (data artifact),” outside the required taxonomy; after deduplication it is compliant, as logged it is a data defect. SUP-715’s dominant finding is an undisclosed conflict, which under the proposed rule is noncompliant, yet the verdict is MIXED. The defense rebuts an intra-check ordering attack the prosecution never makes. The prosecution brief never actually concedes the CRM break, the duplicate register, the label change, or the missing loop.

The layered split — route shape in POWL, eligibility and caps in data rules, identity and ratification in organizational controls, separate intake and reactivation scope — is the right architecture. The concrete POWL is not a sound implementation of it.
