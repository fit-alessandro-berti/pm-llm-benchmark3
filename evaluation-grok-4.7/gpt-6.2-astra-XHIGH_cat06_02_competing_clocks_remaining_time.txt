7.5

The forecast correctly treats the weekend floor, the parallel join as a maximum, chemistry spillover, QA congestion, and positive dependence from the unresolved temperature excursion. The incubation window, the 62-hour chemistry closure, the lognormal review mean near 2.5 h, and the Monday–Tuesday clock arithmetic are internally consistent, and the answer rightly refuses a rejection probability the evidence does not identify.

The scenario tails are not reliable 90th–95th windows. With a contamination indication, a laboratory investigation is only about 6–22% of that scenario, so the 90th–95th should be a multi-day band around the short laboratory cases, roughly Friday 25 through Monday 28. The table instead gives Monday 28, 09:00–11:00, a two-hour point near the extreme 7-day case. The same percentile definition places a confirmed-flag conditional P90 nearer Thursday–Friday; Saturday 26 is too late for an investigation probability near 42% and a laboratory share near one-eighth. Section 2 then ends laboratory cases around Tuesday 29 midday, while Section 4 extends the severe tail to Wednesday 30.

The earliest column is systematically early. A Monday 09:00–11:00 release, and the 10:00–12:30 rerun and confirmation variants, require an empty QA queue and a review far below the 1.5-hour median after the join. The chemistry-rerun “central” window, Monday 20:00 through Wednesday 16:30, mixes no-investigation and documentation-investigation modes without marking that bimodality, so it does not read as an interquartile range.

The claim of 90,000 simulated paths per parameter corner is not credible as reported evidence. The quantile endpoints are round clock times converted exactly into elapsed hours, which is hand-set envelope reporting. Gamma shapes, the 0.5–1.5-hour active QA job, and the 2–3× dependence multipliers are modeling choices, disclosed in the ledger, but they are presented as simulation output rather than as shortcuts.
