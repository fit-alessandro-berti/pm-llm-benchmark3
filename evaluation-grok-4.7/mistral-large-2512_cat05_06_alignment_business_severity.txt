Timing is missing as a dimension, despite P3 and the 24-hour notification rule. Evidence reliability is listed as a severity input and then defined as irrelevant to severity. ## Case findings
- **CL-609 is reversed.** EUR 18,000 and MEDIUM risk both forbid Auto Approve under P1. The tree being synchronous is the trap, not evidence of compliance. Severity 1 and “Compliant with P1” are the wrong conclusion. This is also one of the four synchronous auto-approve violations, and it is left out of that pattern.
- **CL-605 is mislabeled.** `T(tau,c=0)` is defined as technically fitting. Calling it a process-model mismatch, and later a hidden design flaw, contradicts the alignment notation.
- **CL-606 is not a P2 breach.** Fraud Screen and Manual Approve are synchronous and occur before Pay Claim. The gap is core-log evidence of Assess Loss against an external report. Severity 4 and “Non-compliant (P2)” overstate both the control failure and the policy result.
- **CL-608 contradicts itself.** The narrative says P4 compliance is uncertain because the CRM link is mutable; the policy column says “Compliant (P4).” Those cannot both stand. The +3 hour timing is inside 24 hours only if that weak link is accepted.
- **CL-610 is only half-read.** The bank change violates P1 and independently requires Fraud Screen and Manual Approve under P2. Severity 4 also conflicts with the framework’s “high exposure” definition for a EUR 4,500 payment.
- **CL-602 is directionally right but too coarse.** Payment without retained fraud-screen evidence on a EUR 480,000 HIGH-risk claim with a one-day bank change is a high-confidence control-evidence failure. Confidence should be low only for whether screening occurred verbally, not for the control gap. P2’s bank-change trigger is not stated in the policy result.
- **CL-611 is treated as settled.** The mapping table was updated after the case, so equivalence of Assess Damage and Assess Loss is not high-confidence, and “harmless” / “compliant (post-mapping)” is premature on a EUR 62,000 HIGH-risk claim.
- **CL-607 and CL-612 are collapsed.** P6 disputes denied pre-screen leads, not withdrawals. Scope is disputed, not decided. Model moves toward Auto Approve and Pay Claim must not be read as activities still required after denial or withdrawal. “Irrelevant to business risk” is too strong.

CL-601, CL-603, and the payment-before-approval reading of CL-604 are sound. CL-604 is still forced to severity 5 and called irreversible while the remediation says to reverse it if possible.
