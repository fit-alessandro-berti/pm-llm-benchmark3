7.5

The audit is well structured and substantively strong: every top-12 feature, sequence pattern, and local driver is covered; appeal-then-decision is correctly treated as process-legal but unavailable at `t0`; `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is correctly treated as impossible and post-`t0`; queue length, frozen complexity, and elapsed time are retained; region is kept under a fairness constraint; and the validation and remediation plans hit the required topics. It is not nearly flawless.

The revised Case Y narrative, including the removal reason, states that the trace contains an appeal and closure. That reproduces leaked future outcomes in the revised explanation, which the task forbids. Case X is cleaner on this point.

Several classifications are analytically off. `final_handler_team` is future state written at closure, so it is temporal leakage, not only a mutable-field defect; the team’s claim that the final team “determines” delay is left intact even though reassignment can be a consequence of a long case. `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` is grouped with a “harder or larger claims take longer” mechanism, which misstates a remaining-slack signal. `queue_length_at_t0` is called an actionable operational lever; importance at `t0` does not establish actionability. `claimant_channel` is asserted to be known at registration with no lineage, while `initial_reserve` is admitted to be unverified and still placed in credible drivers. Contaminated local attributions are reused as the revised drivers, with no statement that those directions can move once leaked covariates are removed. The large residual “base value and other features” blocks (+0.21, +0.20, +0.17) are never treated as unverified mass.

Severity labels slip outside the required scale (`Medium/High`, `Low/Medium`, “Critical for t0 use”). Future-but-legal edges (`Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded`, `Documents Requested -> Documents Received`) are not marked process-consistent, unlike the appeal edge. The same-timestamp defect is generalized from Case Z onto the global closed-before-assessment pattern. Fraud investigation is described as enabled immediately after coverage check, although the process requires a trigger. `total_case_duration_hours` is only a near substitute for a settlement-time label, because closure can follow later appeal activity. `same_day_document_count_snapshot` is correctly flagged, but the way a post-`t0` nightly count can manufacture the negative “more documents help” association is not examined. Replacing the full variant with a prefix variant ignores that, at this exact `t0`, conforming prefixes are essentially constant.

Section 2 is the strongest part: footprint versus alignment, DFG frequency versus normative permission, tied timestamps, enabled versus future-possible versus impossible, and the appeal precondition for a second decision are all specified with real limitations. Section 5 is complete and sensibly ordered, though it never says to withdraw the current explanations before the platform work lands.
