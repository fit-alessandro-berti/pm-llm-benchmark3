The write-up has the right outline and the right qualitative story (faster triage, longer downstream waits, flag as a policy bundle, natural effects not identified, interference). It is not reliable enough on the points this task is meant to test.

Stage math is mostly in the right neighborhood, but the flagged normal-load cycle time is reported as 1055 minutes when those four cases average 855 (486, 1512, 438, 984). That error makes high-load and normal-load look alike in the table (1081 vs 1055), while the synthesis still says delay is especially bad under high load. Reject rates are stated as 30% vs 30%; both groups are 2/10. Those are used as evidence, not as discarded scratch work.

The estimands do not match the interventions. P0 vs P2/P3/P4 are policy contrasts, not interventional indirect effects. The controlled direct effect fixes manual review and then targets triage service, which is earlier in time. Individual total effects are marked well-defined even though shared queues violate that. “Human judgment held” is smuggled into the flag-suppression contrast after the task warned that setting the flag to zero is not the same as changing post-flag behavior. Treatment-confounder feedback is explained with baseline `arrival_load`, which is not feedback from treatment.

The sample’s main identification fact is missed: flag and `risk_score` have no overlap (unflagged max 0.61, flagged min 0.64). The positivity reversal condition only mentions scores above 0.85. Triage resources are perfectly split (T1/T2 flagged, T3/T4 unflagged) and are never stated. The graph omits `decision_outcome` and does not send service times into `cycle_time`.

The code never builds DFGs for unflagged cases, never computes stage waits or support counts, and calls `.items()` on the performance-DFG return value. The required section 3 explanation is well under 100 words, and the synthesis is under 180. Policy cells for P2–P4 lack directions, evidence for those policies is called moderate, and the 4-hour pilot blocks are shorter than observed cycle times, so contamination is built in.
