8.3

The stage arithmetic, absent-stage handling, and occurrence-versus-wait split for manual review are correct and internally consistent. Policy contrasts are mostly well defined, natural cross-world effects are rejected for the right reason, and interference, feedback, and post-treatment conditioning are handled concretely. The pilot and falsification plan are serious.

Deductions are for real gaps, not taste. Escalation is not given the same decomposition as manual review: conditional senior entry is about 7.0 h versus 6.5 h, so the per-arrival gap is almost entirely incidence, but the write-up still frames it as a second long interval. E6 and E7 each bundle two outcomes, and E1 treats senior congestion, which is part of the total effect, as an identification obstacle. The sensitivity claim is one arbitrary point (60 percentage points times 13.9 h), not a contour. Resource IDs are not single servers far beyond the cited R01–R02 overlap on T1; M2, and also T3, T4, and M3, overlap, so that diagnostic is understated. The mechanism list includes a dubious `triage_resource -> triage_wait` arrow and conditional edges. The stated PM4Py edge means of 23400 s and 6750 s match end-to-next-start only under a particular library semantics and were not shown as executed output. Within-complexity means omit their small sample sizes in the sentence, and the pilot’s “matched P0” and +2 h rule are thin for a single-pool switchback.
