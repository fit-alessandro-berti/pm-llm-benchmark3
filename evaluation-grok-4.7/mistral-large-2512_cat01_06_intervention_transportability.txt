The write-up follows the required headings and reaches a defensible headline (do not copy Site A wholesale; restrict any trial; conditional go). That credit is overwhelmed by causal, quantitative, and model errors that would mis-specify both the intervention and the pilot.

**Process structure.** Site A prerequisites are not “None”: assessment still waits on both checks, and the before-model is ordered. Site B is not a single sequence ID → originals → document. Identity and originals receipt may both start after registration; document check is gated only when originals are legally required. The directly-follows counts support that partial concurrency and are never used. Recommending manual timestamp back-entry as the lock workaround confuses a measurement artifact with executed overlap. The same advice reappears in the modifier table and the recommendation, then contradicts the later ban on back-entry.

**Transport graph.** Required names and some `S_` nodes are present, but the explanation is about 110 words, short of the 180–280 required. `appeal → cycle_time` reverses an outcome into a cause. `API_latency → identity_capacity` confuses service time with headcount. Originals delay (median 18 h) never becomes a critical-path parent of waiting time, so the main Site B constraint is missing. Role specialization is treated only as a limit; separate identity and document pools are what make calendar overlap possible. One cross-trained reviewer cannot perform both checks at the same instant.

**Evidence and magnitudes.** The nine-hour median drop is still interpreted as parallelization and then scaled into Site B ranges, despite waiting time falling only six hours and service times about one hour combined, with mix, +2 FTE, the API, and huddles co-occurring. Volume, complexity share, pre-assessment wait, FTE, and the absent negative control are missing as transfer rows. Huddles are marked fully transferable. Confidence values around 60–70 on confounded rows are too high.

**Eligibility arithmetic.** “38% × 60% = 23%” is then described as also excluding high-complexity cases, which would shrink the share further if those factors are not nested. “Exclude legal-review cases (60% of digital claims)” misstates 60% non-legal volume as a digital-claim share. Legal review sits after assessment, so it does not make verification overlap illegal or useless; dropping all legal cases discards the transportable prefix. The binding legal design is identity concurrent with the originals wait, and document check only after receipt. Excluding all originals cases misses that fragment.

**Scenarios.** The median-improves / tail-worsens case is present, but optimistic −8 h to −12 h exceeds both Site A’s waiting-time change and the roughly 3–4 h of shorter-task overlap in Site B’s service times. The IT-lock scenario lets a false overlap measure change p90. The legal scenario asserts a +2 h to +8 h median without a congestion mechanism. Capacity (4 / 7 / 3 / 2) is never calculated.

**POWL and code.** The textual “strict partial order” contains XOR and a supplement cycle. A strict partial order cannot express exclusive choice or a loop; those edges would require both approve and reject, and would also force legal review. The Python block does not call `pm4py.parse_process_tree`, `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net`, `pm4py.check_is_workflow_net`, `pm4py.check_soundness`, or `pm4py.play_out`. Imports and constructors (`POWL("…")`, `Optional`, `add_edge` on the order object, `add_child`) do not match the POWL API (`Transition`, `StrictPartialOrder.order`, `OperatorPOWL` for XOR and LOOP). Approve is written “Append”. The net would not build, and it does not encode the proposed candidate.

**Pilot.** Case-level 50/50 assignment inside teams shares assessors and legal reviewers, so interference is built in while the text claims to control it. Three months at the stated eligible share cannot supply 400 cases per arm. The rework stop rule (>15%) is below Site B’s 17% baseline. A 90-day appeal rate is not observable for late entrants in a three-month window. There is one treatment cell, so mechanism failure is not separated from lock, timestamp, or role-implementation failure. The recommendation then says faster pre-assessment flow frees assessor capacity, which is the opposite of the bottleneck shift it also warns about.

**Assumptions.** Eight items are listed, but the central one is absent: that Site A’s before/after contrast identifies a parallelization effect. Assumption 3 treats back-entry as a way to achieve concurrency after stating that it does not execute parallel work.

Format compliance and a correctly cautious decision keep this above a failed paper. Invalid models, transported confounded effects, contradictory lock guidance, and a pilot that cannot identify the mechanism keep it well below a pass under strict scoring.
