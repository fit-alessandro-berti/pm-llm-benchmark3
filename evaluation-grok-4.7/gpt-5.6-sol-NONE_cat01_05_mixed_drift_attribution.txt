7.2

The analysis is structurally complete and causally careful: March is treated as semantic drift, later rework and queue growth are kept after normalization, priority redistribution is not over-claimed, and the monitoring design can separate recurrence, mix, logging, and behavior. The baseline-relative table, cycle-time ranges, required coverage, and PM4Py method names are largely sound. It is not nearly flawless.

The normalized DFG distances are wrong in every row. Under the stated mean-absolute and L1 formulas, the January gaps are about 0.43/3, 5.71/40, 8.43/59, 4.00/28, 9.14/64, and 5.14/36, not 0.71/5, 6.86/48, 10/70, 4.57/32, 9.86/69, and 5.86/41. October is farther than July; the table reverses that order. Edge-level deltas in the prose are right, so the story survives, but a required quantitative result is internally inconsistent with its own formula and with Part A.

Part A’s change-point rule misses the gradual performance drift it is meant to detect. For median and p90 cycle time, three times the median absolute month-to-month move exceeds every observed jump, so those series flag nothing. That contradicts the May, June–July, and October performance points in the narrative. The floor does not correct this because the nonstationary series inflates the threshold.

Part B slices months with `filter_time_range` on event time. Cases that cross a boundary are fractured, which can create false starts, ends, and loop rates. That conflicts with the registration-cohort denominator recommended in the segmentation plan.

Section 1 and parts of Section 4 drop the transition operator, producing unreadable strings such as “0.930.55,” “911,” and “18%43-46%.” Jan–Feb is labeled recurring without a repeated cycle. The October–November senior-assessment claim uses the October rate of 22 per 100 cases; November is not in the DFG fragment. Hypothesis rank is undefined, and the near-certain logging mechanism is ranked below less resolved operational hypotheses. The decision-edge decline is treated as structural rework displacement with too little weight on open-case censoring outside December. Subjective confidence near 99% is tighter than these aggregates can support.

The substantive mechanism account and the monitoring rules remain usable. The distance table and the change-point implementation should not be used as computed.
