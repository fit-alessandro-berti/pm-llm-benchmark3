The answer covers every required section, stays balanced, separates East from North/South, and treats averages as an incomplete success measure. The forecast itself is not anchored to the data given, and two later sections contain concrete errors.

The mechanism map names the required effects, but it does not use the handover frequencies, waits, rework rates, or utilizations. Management’s own medians imply only about 1.3 extra handover-wait hours per case (roughly 5.9 hours to 7.2 hours). Legal/Risk volume rises from 9 to 12 per 100 cases while utilization is already 91–93%; the answer never flags that a 0.9-hour wait increase is implausible near saturation, including added triage and difficult quality-check load. Volume-weighted tech utilization is about 73%, so a “15% higher utilization” claim needed a pooling-versus-extra-load calculation.
