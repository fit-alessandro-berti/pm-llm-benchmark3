7.4

The answer meets the required eight-section structure and the hard constraints: non-stationary demand, resource calendars, a waiting/service split, latent rather than observed objections, cross-case effects, and no claim of optimality. The experiment matrix, uncertainty plan, and validation outline are the strongest parts.

Deductions:

- Calendar behavior is incomplete for a calendar-driven twin. The design names shifts, council weeks, and part-time pools, but it never states whether service pauses at shift end, must fit in one contiguous window, or can be preempted. That choice changes fire, legal, and inspector queues.
- Public notice is not given an explicit statutory floor. The data specify a fixed 14-day minimum plus a longer objection tail. A latent objection mixture is the right idea, yet duration is not defined as 14 days plus a zero-inflated extension.
- Cross-case effects are named (architect bursts, district batches, neighborhood objections) without operational rules: batch window versus travel savings, how an objection on one case extends another, or how shared architect state changes routing.
- Appeal is listed as a rework source and then left without an activity, resource, or council-calendar path.
- Parameter estimation stays methodological. The given medians, p90s, variant shares, and Monday/seasonal multipliers are not turned into distribution parameters, conditional routing probabilities, or priors. `discover_performance_dfg` sojourn times are not distinguished from service time when only one timestamp exists.
- Decision SLAs (30/90/120/180 days) are not checked against the reported medians and p90s, so baseline compliance is unknown. Validation says “within tolerance” without numeric gates. Some experiment levels treat effect sizes (loop reduction, travel reduction) as policy settings.
- The skeleton is modular and uses `pandas` plus `pm4py.format_dataframe`, `discover_dfg`, `discover_performance_dfg`, and `parse_process_tree` correctly, with a variants fallback. Case-arrival rate by span is fine, but mean interarrival uses `sort_index()` on case id rather than chronological `sort_values()`, so the interarrival statistic is wrong on a general log. Routing is a flat activity list, and the parsed tree, parallelism, loops, and joins never drive the engine. Environmental effective capacity (60%) and the simple-renovation planner split appear in the prose and not in the resource model.

These are design and grounding gaps in an otherwise complete, constraint-aware answer, not a failed response.
