7.5

The clustering of X1–X8 is consistent and technically careful: activity traces, the X1≅X2 renaming (including the swapped Add Item order), and the separations for split delivery, external logistics, shared invoicing, pre-dispatch replacement, and return/repack are all justified. Borderline disagreements, qualifier-versus-relation handling for X7, and the perturbation analysis are precise and do not invent events.

The preferred signature is not fully reproducible. The executable canonizer implements typed graph isomorphism, while the business vector actually used for the preferred clusters is only partly specified. On these eight executions that vector also yields the same partition as typed structure, so the coarser relation is not shown in the assignments. The PM4Py section does not pin down the key: it asserts identifier-bearing E2O tuples and component-size truncation, and the outline iterates sub-logs, whereas the usual contract is per-type activity-lifecycle keys with object IDs as members and `max_objs` as a per-type sample cap. That makes X1 and X2 the same lifecycle cluster, contrary to the claim that raw IDs split them.
