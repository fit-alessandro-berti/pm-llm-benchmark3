5.5

The write-up hits the required headings, covers all 15 cases, and reaches a real split between bad behavior and a bad model. It is not nearly sound. Several classifications contradict the record or the answer’s own burden of proof, and the revised POWL does not mean what the prose claims.

Authority is mishandled from the start. PD-3 is stipulated as having “authorized” auto-approval. The materials only establish minutes and email, non-incorporation into CS-9, and a later observer statement that she had not approved weakening CS-9. That authority dispute is never judged. SUP-702 is then JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION at high confidence, and the matrix never notes that Notify_Requester is absent from the log (the CRM id is stronger than SUP-706, but it is still a second issue).

SUP-705 is a category error. FD-7 ended at 2026-07-12 23:59; there is no written extension. Calling post-expiry activation a JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION because operations said the flood continued treats an unproved fact as an exception and breaks the answer’s own rule that informal belief does not authorize a derogation. The “what would reverse” cell is also backwards: a written extension would confirm justification, not reverse it. SUP-711 is not indeterminate; the shared source transaction id establishes a duplicate of an otherwise M0-fitting trace. SUP-713 asks for equivalence evidence the case already gives, and the retrospective mapping is a governance point, not missing content. SUP-715 correctly sees missing disclosure, then overstates it as the same kind of breach as a prohibited relationship; no rule forbids the marriage.

Dialectical duties are only partly met. The prosecution never really concedes logging, vocabulary, scope, or the missing loop as defects that defeat some allegations. The defense brief never squarely concedes SUP-703, SUP-708, same-person approval, or the 14 ineligible auto-approvals; it softens SUP-714 as “not malfeasance.” M0 is misdescribed as lacking concurrency. It already leaves sanctions and quality unordered; the real conflict is order versus activation and routes the partial order cannot express. The 289/312 apparently eligible pilot cases are unused.

The code is not a defensible approximation. One StrictPartialOrder requires Sanctions_Check, Quality_Review, and QA_Clearance, so the alias becomes a third mandatory activity. Request_More_Info is mandatory and single-fire, not repeatable or skippable, despite an unused SilentTransition import and a comment that claims a loop. quality and activate objects are reused across branches, which is ill-formed POWL. Quality is forced before the XOR, so the auto and emergency routes cannot place Quality_Review after activation, contradicting the overlay table. Provisional_Activate is a new label the emergency traces do not use, and master-data validation from PD-3 is absent. The table invents a 12-month reactivation window and turns an undisclosed-COI disclosure duty into a hard block.

The data-collection list does cover notification ids, versions, eligibility snapshots, aliases, delegation, overlay authority, and timestamps. Case calls on SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-708, SUP-712, and SUP-714 are broadly right. Those merits do not offset the authority error, the expired-directive classification, or a revised control-flow model that rejects the compliant happy path and cannot replay the overlays it treats as justified.
