The audit is rigorous and correctly separates temporal leakage, process impossibility, mutable lineage, proxies, admissible prefix signals, and non-causal interpretation. It covers every required feature and pattern, treats the appeal edge as future but feasible while treating the tied closure-before-assessment pattern as unproven and process-inconsistent, and refuses to turn contaminated SHAP ranks into early causal stories. Queue length and elapsed time are preserved as repairable signals rather than discarded, and the case write-ups do not retell leaked outcomes.

The remaining defects are mostly calibration and claim strength. Queue length, channel, and reserve are rated as severely as fields that are definitely post-`t0`, even though the queue defect is only a symmetric tolerance and channel/reserve are unverified rather than shown to be future. That sits awkwardly against the answer’s own severity scale, which reserves “medium” for interpretation and robustness problems, yet rates complexity “low” despite flagging a causal overclaim. In the revised cases, region, complexity, and elapsed time still inherit increase/decrease directions from the leaked model. The caveats help, but Case Y’s sole “credible” driver is a fairness-sensitive proxy whose sign was estimated beside a full-trace variant. “Recompute” is also softer than the later, correct demand to refit, and Case X’s narrative slightly hardens the registration-clock assumption and the operational tone of complexity. None of these overturn the audit.
