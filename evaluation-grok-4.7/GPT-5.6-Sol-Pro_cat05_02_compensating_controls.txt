7.5

The assessment is careful and mostly well judged, but it is not nearly flawless. Several classifications, fact readings, and the required code are less reliable than the write-up claims.

The control-objective split is strong. It separates authorization, loss, and recovery, and it correctly limits AV-2 to its enumerated checks, treats ED-1 as conditional permission, and distinguishes rubber-stamping, override, collusion, and bank-change risk. The sufficiency test covers timing, independence, coverage, overrideability, recoverability, evidence, and collusion, and the rejection of “same expected loss” is well supported. Post-release, pre-settlement manager action is properly treated as hybrid. AP-202, AP-205, and AP-208 are classified convincingly, and AP-204 and AP-209 are appropriately left indeterminate rather than inferred from usernames or the fabricated receipt alone.

The case conclusions still overreach the author’s own test. AP-203 and AP-207 are marked `OBJECTIVE_MET` while L3 status is unverified and, for AP-207, authority to stop settlement is explicitly missing. That is a provisional pass, not demonstrated full equivalence. AP-202’s rationale calls manager independence unresolved, although m09’s rating authority already creates a conflict; what is unresolved is credential sharing. AP-201 treats the audit as outside the recall window because it is about 25 hours after release, without weighing that it is still about 23 hours after the stated 10:00 settlement and on the next business morning. AP-206’s counterfactual names backdating as the most plausible loss mechanism even though the facts leave logging delay equally open. AP-208’s facts table marks the emergency token as active before preparation although the scenario only says that a token was active.

The code is thoughtful and its manual resource-set check, blocked-release handling, and evidence labeling are sound, but it is not reliably executable against the PM4Py interface it cites. `keep_violations` and the activity, case, and resource keys are passed as direct keyword arguments. In the cited checker they belong in the `parameters` dictionary, and a `TypeError` would occur before the polarity fallback. The AP-208 token flag is also encoded as true without support. The script therefore cannot be treated as a dependable evidence generator across the versions the answer itself invokes.
