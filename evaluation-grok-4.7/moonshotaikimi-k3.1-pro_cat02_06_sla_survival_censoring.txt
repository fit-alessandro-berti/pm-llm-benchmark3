9.2

The final analysis is nearly flawless: estimands are defined before estimators, competing risks are separated from administrative censoring and from already-observed breaches, and open cases are retained. Case counts, the resolved-only median of 41 hours and \(4/13\), the nine-case 72-hour risk set, gap arithmetic, transfer/priority facts, and the all-location bounds \(16/32\)–\(23/32\) (internal \(18/32\)–\(23/32\)) all check out. Leakage, immortal time, cohort non-comparability, and non-causal use of risk scores are handled correctly, and the single code block meets the encoding, stratification, sensitivity, and PM4Py requirements without inventing timestamps.

Deductions are for small defects only. The prose table leaves the exact-72 boundary implicit while the code uses \(\ge 72\). The third transfer scenario duplicates the competing-risk time encoding and is distinguished only in the separate bounds. Stratified AJ probabilities are printed to four decimals for tiny risk sets, with less uncertainty attached than the prose itself demands. Those issues do not undermine the conclusions.
