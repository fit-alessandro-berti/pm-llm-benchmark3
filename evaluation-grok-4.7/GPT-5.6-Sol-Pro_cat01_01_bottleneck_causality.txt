The assessment is strong on causal discipline: the crude-versus-within-complexity reversal is correctly detected, stratification is not treated as identification, queue time is separated from review service, group is treated as a routed bundle rather than a clean treatment, and the 50% move is distinguished from a one-case effect. The required sections, role distinctions, falsification structure, and phased pilot are all present, and most arithmetic checks out.

It is not nearly flawless. The load row states that Nova’s high-load and normal-load means are similar because their complexity mix differs. Those eight-and-eight slices have the same mix (5 Low, 2 Medium, 1 High); what differs is shift. That is a false evidence claim in the decomposition the decision depends on. The displayed identity 21.96 − 15.24 = +6.71 is also arithmetically wrong (it is 6.72; 6.71 only matches unrounded means). Hypothesis 2’s “queue/time adjustment” is ambiguous enough to condition on post-treatment waiting and thereby mistake a mediated queue effect for no group effect, despite a better pre-assignment formulation later in the same row. Confidence 78 is high relative to n=30, almost no joint overlap, perfect Orion shift–load collinearity, and completion-only sampling.
