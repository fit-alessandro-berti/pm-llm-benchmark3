The offset direction, resolution-before-correction order, HUB ±1.5 s construction, OPS-BER/VENDOR floor expansion, and the single feasible 2025-10-26 fold assignment are mostly right. That is not enough. The required case-by-rule table is replaced by one sample row and a claim that the rest lives in a spreadsheet that is not here. The sensitivity section and the PM4Py block then assert conclusions the intervals do not support.

Section 2 is the strongest part, and it still has a material arithmetic error. T-404’s inferred start upper bound is given as 10:17:41.5. Completion 10:35:01.5 minus 18 minutes is 10:17:01.5. T-407’s receipt upper bound is cut at the earliest feasible investigation instant rather than the joint constraint \(t_{receipt}\le t_{start}\). T-409’s note says the start was causally clipped to the alert, but the printed interval still begins at 15:52. Timestamps are not explicit full ISO-8601 values. The independent-per-event clock-offset model is never distinguished from one shared source-clock offset, which changes same-system durations.

Section 3 misses certain violations that fall directly out of those intervals. At \(\zeta=1\), T-402 Alert→Investigation is wholly above [420, 780] (about [959, 1232] s), and T-408 Investigation→Containment, Alert→Containment, and Containment→Close are wholly above their bands (about 3300 s, 3900 s, and 138300 s). T-405 S1, about [720, 1080] s against 900 s, is a possible violation and is absent from the headline list. “No indeterminates” is too strong: T-407 has no receipt event, Alert_Sent is not Alert_Received, and the ordering constraint is explicitly unlogged. Negative durations are clipped to 0, which discards an ordering inconsistency instead of reporting it. The DST prose says the clock-regressive mapping was ruled out and then selects the only assignment in which local time falls back between alert and investigation.

Section 4 is not a reliable sensitivity analysis. T-401 Alert→Investigation stays a possible violation at \(\zeta=2\) because the duration still reaches about 991 s and the band ends at 960 s. T-402 Alert→Containment and T-408 Alert→Containment move from certain violation to certain pass at \(\zeta=2\), which contradicts the claim that no profile certain-violation becomes a certain pass for \(\zeta\le 3\). T-408’s close pair remains a certain violation at \(\zeta=2\) and passes only at \(\zeta=3\). Several possible violations remain at \(\zeta=3\), including T-402 and T-403 Alert→Investigation, T-406 Alert→Investigation, and any T-407/T-409 interval that still reaches 0 or below the lower band. On the SLA side, T-401 S1 becomes a certain pass at +2 minutes; T-402 S1 moves from certain violation to possible violation at +2 minutes; T-402 S2 is already a certain pass at +2 minutes, not only at +5; and the S1 tails for T-403, T-404, T-405, T-406, T-407, and T-409 become certain passes by +5 minutes. None of those transitions are reported.

Section 5 is not the required executable program. The event list and the classification loop are truncated. It uses `pytz` rather than the standard-library zone machinery. `format_dataframe` is called on the temporal-profile module and its return value is ignored. `conformance_temporal_profile` is never invoked with the supplied \((mean, sd)\) profile; a self-discovered profile of the same midpoint log would not test the given profile. DST is hardcoded to one `is_dst` flag, and the mobile, T-404, T-407, and T-409 adjustments are not actually implemented. The duration helper’s lower bound is not interval subtraction.

Section 6 names the wrong deciding evidence. For T-401, NTP tightened to ±2 s still leaves the minute floor, so S1 remains possible. For T-403 the nominal gap is already about 16 minutes; tighter symmetric synchronization removes the short tail and tends to confirm a violation, not clear it. A receipt acknowledgement does not by itself turn T-407 into a pass. T-405 is not covered. Section 7’s controls and whole-interval enforcement policy are directionally sound, but they do not repair the false conformance results above.
