The answer covers all seven required sections and keeps the final roster inside the hard rules (skill 0 blocked, no high-OT new work, batch cap, approval and security gates, batching only where Rule 8 allows). It also separates hard rules from preferences, refuses a fake optimum, and writes a worker-facing explanation that is not punitive. Those strengths do not survive execution. The scoring rule and the C001–C016 table are different policies, and several comparison claims are false.

The composite score does not produce the recommended assignments. With the published formula, a medium overtime penalty of 0.25 cancels ordinary continuity (0.25 × 0.3 = 0.075) and outweighs the skill-3 vs skill-2 gap (0.10). Greedy application therefore moves C005 from R5 to R6, C008 from R7 to R8, C004 from R1 to a lower-load eligibility resource, and C016 from R5 to R3. C009 does not go to R1 either: the +0.30 development bonus does not cover the skill gap plus R1’s medium overtime penalty. C003 does go to R6, because R6 is diagnosis skill 1, training priority diagnosis, normal priority, 18h left, and low overtime, which is exactly Step 6. The narrative then rejects that result. The table follows prose judgment while the pseudocode says `argmax`. That split is never declared.

C014’s reason is false on the answer’s own urgency order. C015 is scored after C014, so R8 is not “already at 3” when C014 is chosen. R8 (rework 3, low overtime, fewer open cases) dominates R7 (rework 2, medium overtime, 10 open). The cap is 4, not 3, so “at 3” would not block R8 anyway. The header count “R8: 3” also mixes supervision of C001 with performer assignments, while R3’s checkpoint on C009 is not counted the same way. C008’s “projected load at 11” ignores C014 also landing on R7.

C001 is allowed by Rules 2, 5, and 6 and is what the formula barely selects, but it fights the evidence the answer itself cites. Remaining SLA is 3h; keeping a weak owner costs a median 3.3h of service time. The handover alternative costs a median 2.7h of wait. Continuity is the worse clock. The case note says the enterprise security review needs a specialist. There is no SLA term in the score, so urgency only changes sort order, and a skill-1 performer can still win a 3-hour case. `est_hours` is never defined, so H7 cannot be checked for R3 (C001 plus C007) or R6 (C002, C011, and C010 on 6.5h).

The spec is also internally loose. `required_skill == Security and s == 1 or 2` binds as “(security and skill 1) or skill 2”; if R7 and R8 are absent, every skill-2 resource is dropped from every activity. `urgency_threshold` is undefined. High overtime is both “excluded” and left in the score. The resilience bonus of +0.15 applies to every eligible person whenever any backup exists, so it does not rank anyone. The weight table calls 0.25–0.80 an OT “weight” while the formula subtracts those amounts raw, which is why overtime swamps continuity. Rule 3’s 20% target is not tracked inside the loop. The 1-of-9 training result is honestly reported, but it is an override of Step 6, not an output of it.

Baseline facts are wrong or over-specific. Eligibility skill 3 is R1 and R2, so “R1 is the only eligibility specialist” is false. Under specialist-only, C007 is already owned by R3, the lowest-open rework-3; it does not “move to R3”. If open counts update, three diagnosis assignments push R3 past R8 and C007 moves to R8 instead. Under first-available, the lowest open security-eligible resource at the start is R3 (6), so C001 does not leave its owner. “R3 receives five” is true only if tie-breaks use a frozen snapshot, which is not stated. “Less rework rework-time loss” is broken wording.

The audit sketch names the right indicator families, but the code would not measure this policy. `first` resource and `(s != s.shift()).sum() - 1` collapse a whole case, not the assignment decision. SLA miss compares the last event with the deadline and treats open cases as successes. The skill-mismatch definition flags skill < 2 on normal work, which is the training the policy wants. `get_service_time` is only a comment, `get_variants` is absent, and `filter_case_performance` appears only in prose. Handover band `"none"` (C013) is not in the penalty map.

Section 5 and the hard/soft split are the parts that hold up. The queue decision, the formula, and the baseline comparison do not.
