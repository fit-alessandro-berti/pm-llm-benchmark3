6.5

The in-memory OCEL is largely faithful: 17 unique events, the 12 objects, timezone-aware timestamps, payment amounts as event attributes, required columns, narrative O2O (including both payments as `partially_settles`), and the stated integrity checks. The operational-involvement choice is disclosed, and the constructor fallback is isolated.

The analysis contradicts that model and misstates several discovery constructs.

- Section 3 correctly limits Reserve Stock to Item and Order. Section 5 then gives Reserve Stock degree Order+Item+Package.
- Order flattening is not the linear chain given. O100 is Create→Add→Add→Reserve→Pack→Pack→Dispatch→Dispatch→Issue→Close; O200 has one Add, Pack, and Dispatch. The missing Add→Add, Pack→Pack, and Dispatch→Dispatch edges are the O100/O200 divergence. Create→Add has frequency 2 because the cases are separate, not because an event was copied; that is conflated with true shared events (e06, e07, e09, e11).
- P2 is not a shorter package trace. P1 and P2 are both Pack→Dispatch→Confirm. “Plus the items/orders” is not how package flattening works.
- Payments are length-1 cases. Flattening on Payment does not pull Invoice events into those cases, so the length-2 caveat is wrong. The more important Order artifact is also missed: with payments and confirms unbound to Orders, both order DFGs invent Issue Invoice → Close Order.
- Section 6 calls the implemented policy full involvement of every downstream order-related event. Confirm Delivery is only Delivery+Package, and Dispatch omits Items, so the code, Section 1, and Section 6 disagree.
- The OC-DFG claim of two instance corridors (P1/D1 vs P2/D2) treats a type-level graph as an instance graph. The same confusion appears in the Petri-net claim of a distinct “Pack P1” transition and two Close Order transitions. Those are one activity type each; instances are tokens. “Fork” is also the wrong control-flow word for object convergence.

Qualifiers are consistent but weak: one verb is reused for every role on Pack and Dispatch. The semantic checks mostly restate counts and treat chosen order links as narrative-mandated facts. Failure modes cover the required list, but several repeat these same slips rather than tightening them.
