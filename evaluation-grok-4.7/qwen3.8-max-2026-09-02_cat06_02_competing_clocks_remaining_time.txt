7.2

The six required sections are present, the parallel join is correctly treated as a maximum, and the smooth path, chemistry spillover, QA congestion, weekend pauses, and temperature-excursion dependence are all discussed rather than collapsed into one summed mean. That structure is sound.

The forecast summary does not keep its own clocks consistent. From Friday 18 September 15:30, 145 h is Thursday 16:30, not Thursday 14:30; 160 h is Friday 07:30, not Thursday late; 280 h is Wednesday 07:30, not Wednesday 15:30. The P80 lower edge, Wednesday 07:30, also falls outside QA hours, so it is not a valid terminal time.

Scenario C assigns a central finish of Tuesday 10:00–20:00 to a 45–75% band that is mostly “no weekend microbiologist.” That staffing delay usually does not move the join, because QA is closed Sunday and chemistry is often still unfinished Monday morning. The contamination subset, roughly 8–25%, is what supports a Tuesday central time. The table therefore overstates how late the high-probability mass finishes and conflicts with the Monday-evening to Tuesday-afternoon P50.

The earliest window, about Monday 09:00–10:00, is not jointly plausible. Friday chemistry completion is nearly blocked once the urgent sample outranks B-417, and a five-batch QA queue plus Friday–Saturday arrivals is unlikely to be zero by Monday 08:00. The same parallel constraint is missed in the ledger: Friday chemistry overtime cannot pull the release left by 0.5–2 days while microbiology and QA remain closed. The microbiology driver is also mislabeled as mainly calendar uncertainty even though the stated 20–30 h contamination delay is path uncertainty. Dependence is acknowledged, but the conditional probabilities are not carried through numerically.
