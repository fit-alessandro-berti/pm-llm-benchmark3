The parallel reading of `+`, the binary-loop reading, and the three verdicts are right: A and B are not visibly equivalent, they match only after the P2 dashboard abstraction `(n analyses, some check, rework iff n≥2)`, and the P3 counterexamples are valid in both directions (`O A K R A K Cl`, `O K A R K A Cl` vs `O A R A K Cl`, `O A K R A Cl`). Approval/`tau` placement, structural counts (12/12 nodes, depths 5 and 6), and the simplified sequential model are also handled honestly, including what that simplification drops.

The required bounded-comparison code would not compute those languages on standard PM4Py. `+` is already parallel and `X` is XOR; `ProcessTreeOperator.PARALLEL_CHOICE` is not the real enum, and the fallback that rewrites `+` to the empty string makes `parse_process_tree` fail. Even if parsing succeeded, silent net transitions use `label=None` (plus extra silent loop/operator transitions), while the explorer treats a missing label as the transition’s visible name, so tau is not actually hidden. The footprint/EMD snippets are only sketches, and `simplicity_petri_net` is never given a result.
