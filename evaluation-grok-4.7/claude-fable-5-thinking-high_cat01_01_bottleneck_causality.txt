7.4

The assessment is complete, well structured, and causally careful on the main decision: the crude Orion penalty reverses inside every complexity stratum, stratification is not identification, service gaps are tiny, the 50% reassignment is unsafe, and the code, role table, and staged pilot meet the required form. The score is held down by concrete errors and one missed reversal of the same kind the task is built around.

The night analysis repeats the aggregate fallacy. “Night work is associated with longer cycles even within Nova” (+3.0h, 17.50 vs 14.49) is complexity mix. Inside Nova, night is shorter at every complexity level: Low 8.9h (n=2) vs day 9.83h (n=8), Medium 18.2 vs 19.8, High 34.0 vs 36.0. Orion’s only day case is also slightly faster than its low-complexity night case. Hypothesis 3 still treats the crude within-Nova gap as support for a shift effect. Conditional on complexity, these data do not show a night penalty.

Factual slips used as evidence: maximum wait is Nova’s 22.9h (C30) and 22.6h (C29), not Orion’s 21.4h, so “longest waits are Orion’s” is false. Nova has four night cases; “no night coverage” is false and is used twice to predict backfire. “92.9% night/high-load (vs 25.0%)” attaches Nova’s night share to load; Nova high load is 8/16 = 50%. Compliance gaps are not near-identical (about 13.3h Orion vs 15.7h Nova). Non-required mean is 203.1/17 ≈ 11.95h, not 12.1h. Stated weights 0.40/0.33/0.27 do not reproduce 17.14h and 19.65h; those levels require 12/30, 10/30, and 8/30.

The executive sentence that the crude +6.7h gap “lives in case mix and waiting” merges two contrasts. Case mix creates the crude Orion penalty. Within strata, Orion’s lower wait is the opposite-signed residual. “Every observable adjustment” is also overstated: only complexity was standardized. `shift -> routing_rule` is labeled a supplied fact; coverage makes the direction an assumption. Calling within-cell stratification “front-door-style” conflicts with the same answer’s collider warning on rework. Hypothesis 1’s predicted result (“implausibly strong” confounding) prejudges the sensitivity analysis, which is not credible while two strata have n=2.

The learning plan is directionally right but internally inconsistent. Section 8 wants a Nova night pod to create overlap; phase 3 bars night cases, so the pilot never studies the night/high-complexity population that is about 93% of Orion. Phases 2 and 3 overlap in weeks 6–8, so the capacity change can contaminate the randomized contrast. The appeal stop rule of +2 percentage points is not detectable at this volume.

What keeps the score in this range is the central discipline: sign reversal, refusal to treat standardization as a causal effect, queue versus service, group as a routing bundle, completion as a collider, falsification tests with predicted observations, no fabricated timestamps, and a recommendation against the blanket reassignment.
