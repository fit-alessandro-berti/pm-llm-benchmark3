7.3

The analysis is strong, specific, and mostly numerically reliable, but it is not nearly flawless. Several concrete DFG claims contradict each other or the event table.

The core reconstruction is right. Order flattening yields 42 trace rows from 31 events (11 extra copies of e13, e14, e16, e17, e19, e22, e24, e25, e27). Item, package, delivery, invoice, and payment coverages (24/16, 12/10, 6/6, 7/7, 4/4) and the dropped-event shares are correct. Transit math checks out: van mean 10h 13m, parcel mean 8h 54m, item/order-link mean 10h 33m. Dispatch→Confirm Delivery really has frequency 0 on every order trace, while Record Payment→Confirm Delivery is 5 and Dispatch→Issue Invoice is 4. O1’s trace and its four adjacent self-loops are exact. Fractional weights restore 4 packs, 3 dispatches, 3 invoices, and 4 payments. Definitions of convergence, divergence, and copied events are sound, and Position B is internally consistent with the portfolio.

These errors are material:

- Section 7’s “10/38 self-loop transitions (26.3%)” is wrong. Order traces have 38 directly-follows edges, but only 7 are adjacent self-loops (4 on O1, 3 on O3). The 10 counts non-adjacent repeats, including O3’s two Confirm Delivery events and the two Record Payment events on O2/O3, which are not DFG self-loops. 7/38 is about 18.4%, not 26.3%.
- That mistake repeats the matrix claim of Confirm self-loops on O3. On O3, e24 and e27 are separated by e25, so the edge is Confirm→Record Payment→Confirm, which distortion 5 itself describes.
- Distortion 5 then says that cycle has “frequency 2x each direction.” Record Payment→Confirm Delivery is 5 overall and 3 on O2/O3 alone; Confirm→Record Payment is 2. This contradicts the correct 5x figure in distortion 8.
- Removing the nine shared events does not sever O4. e20 and e26 are private, so O4 remains Create→Add Item→Issue Invoice→Record Payment→Close Order. Only O2 and O3 collapse to Create/Add/Close.
- e21→e28 is not a 22h 30m idle lag. e23 (14:00) and e24 (16:00) still occur after e21 (12:30). The comparison with O2’s genuine post-delivery gap e25→e29 (2h 05m) treats unfinished logistics as idle time.
- The cluster audit would not run as written if `cluster_equivalent_ocel` returns a list of OCELs: `.values()` and the nested loop assume a dict of lists. The sensitivity sentence that puts “Unique Payments = 4” on the e22→e25 self-loop mixes an activity-level count (M1–M4) with an edge that involves only M2 and M3.
- “Blocked on M3” / “had to wait” is stronger than the log. The timestamps are consistent with that story; they do not establish the rule. The prose also runs past the 2,800-word aim.

The lead-object argument, the Dispatch/Confirm erasure, the D2 triple-count, and the direct-versus-propagated O2O warning are still the right analysis. The grade is held down by false DFG frequencies and an incorrect sensitivity result, not by the overall approach.
