6.5

The design covers every required section and respects the main constraints: cross-case effects, calendars, a service/wait split, unobserved objections, and no optimality claim. It is not reliable enough to score higher.

The decision rule is incoherent. `Score = 0.3·Dur_90 + 0.2·SLA + 0.2·Fairness + 0.15·Backlog + 0.15·(1 - CostPenalty)` adds days, rates, a CV, and a count, and higher duration, backlog, and CV raise the score. The 60% synergy test treats non-additive bottleneck effects as a pass/fail for negative interaction.

Robustness is not actually tested. S1 and S2 stress only the baseline, while the selection rule requires each intervention to survive S1 and S2. S1–S3 also change demand, staffing, calendars, and objections together, so they cannot identify the binding constraint they claim to test. Factor levels are mostly on/off, with no common random numbers for paired deltas.

Warm-up seeks a steady state and a 30-day queue CV below 0.1 in a process whose arrival rate swings by 1.7× across seasons. Initialization bias and seasonal finite-horizon metrics are different problems; that rule can fail during ordinary peak transitions. Quantile bootstrap and sensitivity analysis are otherwise appropriate, but the decision thresholds ignore those intervals.

Process-mining details slip. `pm4py.parse.process_tree` is not the stated API; `get_variants` is treated as a DataFrame; and a performance DFG on completion-only timestamps is inter-event time, not service time. Section 2 still calls it activity duration. The given tree has no planning, legal, or appeal loops, and the state has no parallel-join token for the `+` block, yet decision drafting and D5 depend on that join. Inspection bundling is modeled by district only, not architect and district. Council calendars are applied to legal work but not to public notice, despite that being specified. D2 drops the unobserved prior-violations condition without an assumption.

Validation names replay but mostly compares generated variant frequencies. Face, distributional, utilization, bottleneck, and holdout checks are present and directionally sound.
