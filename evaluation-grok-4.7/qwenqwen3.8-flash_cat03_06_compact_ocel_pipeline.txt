7.5

The pipeline is complete, internally organized, and largely faithful to the narrative: all 17 events and 11 objects are present, the mandatory OCEL columns and validations are covered, timestamps are timezone-aware, payment amounts stay on events, and the direct-versus-propagated qualifier split is a coherent modeling policy. The flattened trace reconstructions, failure modes, and object-centric caveats are mostly careful, including the explicit refusal to treat the object-centric Petri net as unique.

Three substantive problems keep it from a higher score. First, `M2` is modeled with `completes_settlement`, and section 8 tells a reviewer to confirm that this matches the narrative. The narrative only says both payments are partial payments against `V1`; the invoice total is unknown, and that choice is never stated as an assumption. Second, the stated `O100` trace includes consecutive `Dispatch Package` events (`E09` then `E10`), but the listed Order DFG edges omit `Dispatch Package → Dispatch Package` while including the other consecutive pairs. Third, section 5’s ET–OT list of activities that touch `Order` omits `Dispatch Package`, contradicting the relation table and the propagated `E09`/`E10` links to `O100` and `O200`.

Smaller issues: `UTC` is unused; “propagated” is used loosely for `E11`, which is a direct `involves_order` link; and the order-view “rework loop” wording overstates a `Record Payment` self-loop that the traces do not contain.
