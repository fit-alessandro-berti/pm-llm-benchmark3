5.4

The write-up follows the requested eight-part shape, leaves `ev11` unresolved, keeps O2O direction straight, and does not invent objects or events. Those merits do not offset a selected repair that is not conservative and a process-mining section that misstates what the repaired log would actually change.

The financial argument is internally inconsistent. Bundle A rejects `130 + 100 = 230` as a reason to bill `ORD-B`, then accepts `230 − 100 = 130` as a reason to settle `INV-44` with `PAY-2` at 0.82. That residual only makes sense if the 230 already covers both orders, or if a 100 fee is assumed and never stated. The stronger competing reading is missed: `PAY-2` equals `ORD-A`’s declared value and `PAY-1` equals `ORD-B`’s. Arithmetic is treated as decisive for the payment after being treated as non-proof for the invoice.

The physical repairs are likewise over-closed. Domain rule 3 allows an item-only pack row after a scanner outage, and `PK8-2` may be a scanner id, yet both bundles accept `ev08`–`PK-8` and `PK-8 packs IT-B1`. Rule 4 allows a bulk dispatch, and `ev09` and `ev10` share a timestamp, yet `ev10`–`DEL-X` is rejected at 0.15 as a direct contradiction of `N-421?`. Bundle A is therefore not a minimum-change repair. The same O2O edges are marked “accept” while the prose admits they are derived from the new E2O links. The audit diagram then claims independent support from route and customer C17. `IT-B1` has neither attribute, and the following paragraph retracts the independence claim.

Bundle B is not a coherent lifecycle. Linking `ev11` to `ORD-B` and to `DEL-X` couples `ORD-B` to a delivery that, under the shared packing hypothesis, carries only `IT-A1` from `ORD-A`. `ORD-A`’s own goods are split across `PK-7`/`DEL-X` and `PK-8`/`DEL-Y`, which is never used to rank “both deliveries,” “`DEL-X` only,” and “order-only portal confirmation.” Section 3 calls the delivery lifecycles complete while confirmation is unresolved. The ASCII order trace puts Issue Invoice before Confirm Delivery; the prose and the timestamps have the opposite order.

Section 6 conflates instance gaps with type-level graphs. Unrepaired `ev06`/`ev07`, `ev09`, and `ev13` already connect Pack Item to Item and Package, Dispatch Package to Package and Delivery, and Record Payment to Payment and Invoice. Bundle A therefore does not create those OC-DFG, OTG, or ET–OT type edges, nor a Pack→Dispatch path for `IT-B1`, because `ev10` is never tied to the item. Standard flattening does not walk O2O, so the claimed `ORD-B` trace Create→Add→Pack→Dispatch is false, and Bundle B’s extra Pay/Pack/Dispatch steps are false as well. Confirm Delivery is also said to reach Package, which neither bundle adds. Equivalent-execution clustering on Order is absent. The code rebuilds the tables instead of copying `events`, `objects`, `relations`, and `o2o`, and checks object foreign keys only.

The scoring formula cites weights that are never set, then issues two-decimal accepts that ignore the conflicts the same answer lists. The over-repair warnings are the most careful part of the paper, and they describe failure modes that the chosen bundle itself commits.
