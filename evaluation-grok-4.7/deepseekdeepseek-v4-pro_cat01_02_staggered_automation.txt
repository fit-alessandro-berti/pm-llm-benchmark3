The answer’s causal posture is largely right: endogenous selection, staggered timing, partial adoption, median-versus-tail divergence, fraud-desk and routing interference, and non-identification of a single “reduces cycle time by X%” claim are all recognized, and “expand with an embedded evaluation” is a defensible decision. That does not offset material failures of quantitative discipline.

The central pre/post table is arithmetically unreliable. Gamma’s pre-period escalation mean is 8.7, not 8.2; Alpha/Beta rework post-means are about 13.7 and 12.2, not 14.1 and 12.0. Equal-weight never-treated p90 rises by about 16 hours over the Alpha/Beta window, not 12.4, and escalation rises by about 1.8 points, not 1.0. Those errors cut the escalation difference-in-differences roughly in half (about +0.7 to +1.1 points, not +1.5 to +2.0) and roughly double the relative p90 improvement versus controls. The claim ladder then states, as an “Observed” fact, an escalation increase of at least 1.5 points relative to never-treated branches. That contrast is neither raw observation nor numerically correct, yet it is treated as the most robust result, with confidence 75.

Ranges contradict the answer’s own contrasts. Alpha’s median difference-in-differences is −5.6 hours, but the effect table’s Alpha/Beta interval (−3% to +3%) excludes that estimate. Gamma’s median sensitivity is given as 0% to −10% and then as −5% to −12%. Mechanism evidence switches baselines without notice: Alpha’s escalation “+3.8 points” is January to August and includes the March fraud-desk jump, while Beta’s “+1.3” starts in March. The reported within-adopter intensity slope of about +0.2 hours per fast-path point is unsupported; the raw within-branch association is negative, and no scatterplot is shown.

The preferred synthetic control is never fit, so the “conditionally causal” 5–10% Gamma claim is a relabeled simple contrast. The code can crash on the first `read_csv`, pools Alpha with Beta instead of reporting adopter-specific contrasts, and estimates the naive two-way fixed-effects specification the narrative itself flags. The rollout correctly demands case-level logs, queues, fraud-desk waits, and response counts, but it nonrandomly pilots the easier branch and then allows already-treated branches back in as donors.
