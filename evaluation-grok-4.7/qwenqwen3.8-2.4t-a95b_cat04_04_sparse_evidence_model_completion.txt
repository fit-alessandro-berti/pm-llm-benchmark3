6.5

The prose models are differentiated, internally consistent, and tied to the evidence: the conservative model is an exact XOR of the seven observed sequences, the balanced model correctly keeps Credit Check and Engineering Review unordered while requiring Credit Check before Supplier Confirmation, and the permissive model adds the Engineering-only supplier trigger. The DFG-bidirectionality caveat, missing-versus-stocked disclosure, unobserved/excluded traces, and recommendation are largely sound.

The POWL code does not implement those models. `strict_po` never calls `order.add_edge`. `StrictPartialOrder(order=graph)` does not match the API, and the fallback `StrictPartialOrder(graph)` treats the graph as a node set, so the precedence edges are dropped. Both `model_balanced` and `model_permissive` therefore become choices among unordered activity sets and admit traces the text says are forbidden. The validation block would not detect that, because it only prints aggregate fitness.

The balanced risk statement that a supplier-after-both trace is explainable by more than one XOR branch is false: presence of Supplier Confirmation and Expedite Review selects exactly one branch. The 7/30 supplier absences versus the stated 5–10% missing-event rate is never used to separate true skips from missingness. Section 7 also states fitness as 1.0 despite the requested qualitative or interval assessment.
