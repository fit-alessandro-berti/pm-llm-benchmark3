The POWL model is the right canonical object and is essentially faithful: containment enables only verification planning, root-cause analysis enables design, the approval XOR, and optional supplier consultation, the trial loop is gated by both plan and design, and release is a real three-way join. Loop orientation is the correct PM4Py “do, then redo” shape, so `Adjust Parameters` cannot follow the exiting inspection. The process tree is a genuine safe under-approximation, and W-04 is a real witness of that loss. Forbidden traces W-06 and W-08 name genuine cross-branch failures.

The write-up then contradicts itself on the central approximation. Assumption 5 says the tree disallows “delaying Plan Verification until Analyze Root Cause completes.” That delay is what the tree forces. Section 3 and W-04 correctly say the excluded behavior is Plan Verification finishing while root-cause analysis is still open. Section 6 then marks optional supplier consultation as exact in the tree, despite Section 3 correctly noting that supplier consultation, design, and approvals are also blocked until containment finishes. Those are extra precedences, so “Exact” is false on a required comparison row. The unconnected-pair discussion never covers containment versus design, approvals, and supplier.

The validation sketch is not executable as written. `check_soundness` is indexed with `[0]` although the wrapper’s return shape is not that tuple, the prose claims 1000 playouts the call never requests, and integer timestamps are a fragile event log. Two of the three self-critiques are inherent control-flow limits the task required, not defects in this answer; the inverted example and the false “Exact” cell are the residual weaknesses that went unacknowledged.
