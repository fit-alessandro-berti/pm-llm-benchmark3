The forecast is complete, balanced, and well aligned with the required structure. All seven sections are present, East is treated differently from North and South, and the recommendation correctly conditions any expansion on a pilot and distributional monitoring rather than average cycle time. The mechanism map, scenarios, indicators, hidden risks, and guardrails are coherent and use the supplied baseline data plausibly.

The main weakness is the PM4Py sketch. Several calls add unsupported arguments (`resource_key`, `case_id`, `activity_key`) to functions that, in current PM4Py, accept a formatted log and only a few parameters. `get_variants` is treated as a Series via `.index`, although it returns a dictionary, and `filter_variants` is called with nonexistent column arguments. Summing `reassignment_count` across events also inflates a case-level field. The optimistic legal/risk waits assume adequate staffing despite utilization already at 91–93% and rising referral volume, without making that capacity assumption explicit enough.
