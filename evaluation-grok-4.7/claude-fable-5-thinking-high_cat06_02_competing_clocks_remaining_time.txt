7.5

The forecast has the right skeleton: the join is treated as a maximum, Friday chemistry completion is correctly ruled out, weekend incubation is mapped onto real calendars, and the excursion is handled as a common cause rather than as an independent multiplier. The hour anchors are mostly accurate, and the scenario set covers the paths the question requires. The aggregation and several calendar edges are not reliable enough for a higher score.

The decision number is mis-calibrated against the answer’s own probabilities. Scenario A is only 40–55% and is the fastest path, with its own center at Monday 15:00–20:00 and its congestion tail on Tuesday morning. A P50 band of Monday 14:30–21:30 (~71–78 h) sits on the fast half of that already-conditional center. FIFO at 55%, a query rate of 15–30%, and QA’s 20:00 close put the unconditional median on the Monday-evening / Tuesday-morning boundary (~76–92 h). The later remark that Tuesday 09:00–11:00 “sits just above the median” conflicts with a P50 interval whose lower end is 14:30.

The sensitivity ledger breaks the calendar the main text otherwise respects. If chemistry finished Friday, primary incubation is still ready only on Sunday 12:00–20:00. Saturday QA has already ended, and QA has no Sunday hours, so a Sunday microbiology result cannot be accepted or released on Sunday. The claimed Sunday-evening release, and the associated 12–18 h saving, is not available. Weekend authorization also would not “split the forecast” in that counterfactual: an authorized Sunday result waits idle until Monday 08:00, while an unauthorized readout is Monday 08:30–09:30. The remaining value of authorization is an earlier start of the 20–30 h confirmation clock. Scenario C never uses that branch. It starts every contamination case at Monday 08:30 and puts the earliest terminal time at Tuesday 18:00.

Scenario C’s center is also shifted later than its own probabilities support. An indication, not a positive confirmation, is what raises the investigation probability to 0.30–0.55, so the chance of no investigation is about 0.45–0.70. Calling the no-investigation case “low probability,” and centering the whole scenario on Wednesday because an investigation occurs, inverts that split. Confirmation becoming ready Tuesday afternoon also rarely misses the Tuesday microbiologist window; the Wednesday slip is a thin corner, not a second mode.

Smaller defects accumulate. Electronic Release at ~90–97% and Batch Rejected at ~1–5% do not partition the only two terminal events. A median of 1.5 h and a P80 of 3.5 h imply a mean QA service nearer 2.5 h than the 2 h used to clear the weekend queue, so Monday’s queue of 3–6 and the net drain of 0.65/h are optimistic. “Urgent outranks B-417 under both disciplines” is loose: under FIFO the three ordinary samples are ahead as well; under risk priority the ordinary samples are behind. The likely Friday partial start on the risk-priority arm is mentioned and then left out of the blended Monday 10:30–15:00 chemistry window. Section 2’s earliest smooth release, Monday 12:00, does not match section 4’s 11:30–13:30. A 7-calendar-day laboratory investigation opened on Tuesday extends past Monday 28 September, where the severe tail is cut.

Section 6 is directionally right that the excursion flag moves more than one clock, but the stated update is too large: confirmation raises rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities; it does not by itself move the conditional center from Monday evening to Wednesday–Thursday.
