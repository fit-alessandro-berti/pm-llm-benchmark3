7.5

The policy is complete and directionally strong: stage-gating kills I-03, I-05 is suppressed while I-07 re-enters on new evidence, effects are not confused with raw \(p\), and spillover is allowed to reverse the breach outcome. Case-level midpoint arithmetic is correct. It is not nearly flawless.

The deployable rule is not the rule that is applied. \(NB_i\) subtracts a probability-weighted burden and a spillover share \(P_i\), but every score subtracts the full listed burden and ignores \(P_i\). Calibration offsets are described and then never computed, so Strategic overprediction and SME underprediction do not actually change a number used to rank cases.

The allocation table contradicts the decision. I-04 is marked **Intervene** while the text holds that slot until 15:00. I-02 (\(\Delta p=+0.08\)) and I-09 (\(+0.07\)) are second alerts with no stated new event, yet both stay eligible under a later 0.10 re-entry filter that would exclude them. Three cases are activated at once with two specialists and no sequence, despite I-07 having 1.5 hours and a 45-minute swarm. I-09, already in Diagnosis Started with 2.5 hours and a higher midpoint net than I-01, can displace only the reserved slot, not the immediate 7-hour case. Section 5 warns against blanket NNLS discounting while the portfolio does exactly that pending review.

Section 3 is not auditable at the bounds it prints. Upper-half gross is about €22,965, not ~€23,200. €20–26k after €1,800 and €2,900 is about €15.3–21.3k, not €15–24k. €5–11k after the same deductions is about €0.3–6.3k, not €0.3–7.4k. The table also books all four swarms, so the strong base-case euros are mostly contingent I-04, not the three swarms actually released at 12:00. “Clinical” effect is the wrong domain.

Sections 4–6 are the best part: episode suppression, I-05 versus I-07, label contamination, equipoise randomization, and versioned change control are concrete. Those strengths do not remove the inconsistent rule, the mislabeled slot, the timing gap, or the impact-table arithmetic.
