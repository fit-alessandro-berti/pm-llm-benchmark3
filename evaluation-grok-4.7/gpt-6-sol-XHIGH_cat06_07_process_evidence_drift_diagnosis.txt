The diagnosis is month-specific, numerically careful, and operationally proportionate. April is treated as a scoring-point and feature-lineage failure with mature labels; June as a routing-mix change whose lower breach rate is largely recomposable from stable within-variant rates; August–September as overlapping parts-delay, within-V3 risk change, and Partner Z composition, with maturity still incomplete; October as process exposure continuing under a selected, 55% mature label set. Variant-matured rates are kept distinct from cutoff-available metrics, post-diagnosis paths are not smuggled in as model inputs, and retraining is gated on replay plus fixed-vintage labels. The six tests each name two divergent outcomes, and the alert design keeps a label-delay alarm from launching retraining while leaving the parts-edge signal live.

The required code sketch does not run. After `to_datetime(..., utc=True)`, `.dt.tz_localize(None)` raises on a timezone-aware series; the correct removal is `tz_convert(None)`. `discover_dfg` is unpacked as a triple, but `discover_performance_dfg` is used as a dict, so `.items()` fails on the `(dfg, starts, ends)` tuple this API returns. The calendar-month parts median is also length-biased: `filter_time_range(..., mode="events")` drops the far side of cross-month `Order Part -> Part Received` pairs, so that view systematically keeps shorter waits. `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` contributes only column names, which do not vary by month.

Two decision points stay softer than the evidence. May’s residual AUROC/ECE gap is left beside the unrecomputed April cache, yet that cache does not score May cases; May still needs its own account (about 2% unknown activity, 3% missing queue, source-uneven dedup). A June intercept fit would lower scores, while August–September under-predict (about 0.19 vs 0.28, then 0.21 vs 0.31); “validate on a later month” is present, but the sign reversal should be an explicit stop rule. Grouping August’s 93% maturity with July’s 98% as slight understates how much a long-path backlog can already move August’s cutoff metrics.

Those defects keep the write-up short of a near-flawless mark. The drift split, falsifiers, and training-month gates are otherwise sound.
