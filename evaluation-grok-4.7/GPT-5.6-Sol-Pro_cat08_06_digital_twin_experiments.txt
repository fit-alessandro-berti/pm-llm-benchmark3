7.3

The design is a serious calendar-aware, cross-case discrete-event twin and it meets the required section structure, but several methodological and consistency errors are large enough that the experiment results could mis-rank interventions.

The measurement window stops arrivals at month 36 and then runs the system until open cases finish. That removes right-censoring, and it also drains queues. Cases that arrive late, especially Complex variance and any policy that lengthens sojourn, spend part of their life in an unrealistically light system. With a stated p90 near 260 days, that slice of the 24-month measurement window is material, and it is differential across case types and policies. A cohort measured under continued arrivals would avoid that bias. The 12-month warm-up, Welch check, and snapshot initialization are otherwise sound, and the paired-replication precision targets are declared in the right direction.

The arrival design specifies an overdispersed negative-binomial daily count plus a separate architect-cluster process. The generator draws Poisson counts and then calls a burst model whose interface does not say whether bursts replace part of that count or are added on top. Addition double-counts the top architects, who are 31% of historical volume. D7 and pre-submission delay sit on `PolicySpec` but are not inputs to `ArrivalGenerator`, so the two arrival-side interventions have no path into the process that is supposed to create submissions.

Routing treats `requires_fire` / `requires_environment` false as decisive. The stated rules are OR rules: fire can still be required from building area, and environmental review from a sensitive district. Both latent fields exist on `CaseState` and are never consulted. Unknown flags are handled carefully, and D2 correctly refuses eligibility when prior violations are unknown. Negative flags plus an unmet OR condition are still dropped.

The estimation narrative says queue wait is `start − enabled_at`, and that a performance DFG on completion timestamps is only a transition gap. The implemented timing path then calls PM4Py service/waiting insertion, whose wait is based on consecutive timestamps, and presents those columns as service and waiting once starts exist. Parallel planning, fire, and environmental review make directly-follows gaps the wrong identification strategy even with start timestamps. The lognormal fallback from median and p90 is the right two-quantile map (`1.2816` is Φ⁻¹(0.90)), and it is correctly labeled a fallback.

The resource story contradicts itself. The text rejects a flat 1.8 FTE environmental pool and asks for three people with blocked non-permit time. The skeleton sets `availability_fraction=0.60` on capacity 3. Fire and inspector daily hours are invented and do not appear in the assumptions. Inspection scheduling has no scheduler pool despite a named scheduling resource in the log, and Reject application is on no pool even though its service time is not the automated issue-permit pattern. Completeness rework is given to general planners without a reason. District batching releases as soon as the minimum size is reached, so the design cannot form the larger routes that the travel-reduction factor is meant to represent. It only delays the first cases until the minimum is filled.

The experiment matrix is broad and rightly treats D1–D9 effect sizes as scenarios, includes interactions, and warns that D10 is not the sum of its parts. “Low / central / high” does not have a stable direction: D1’s missing-document multiplier becomes more favorable along the row, while pre-submission effort, audit rate, and misclassification become less favorable. Those rows cannot be read as one response surface. D1’s own trade-off of abandonment is absent from the factor list. D6 never says whether the reserved planner is general or simple-only. A simple-only planner cannot serve the aged commercial and variance cases that dominate the tail.

Secondary statistical issues: a Wilson or beta-binomial interval on pooled cases ignores within-replication queue dependence. Many policies, case types, and tail metrics are compared without a pre-declared primary endpoint or simultaneous interval. Council-week and holiday effects on public notice are named and then left as an unspecified calendar residual. Appeal reopening has no filing deadline or return point.

PM4Py use is largely the right separation. The log is loaded with pandas, `format_dataframe` uses `case_id` (not `case_id_key`), variants, both DFGs, and case-arrival average are computed, missing booleans stay missing, and the tree’s missing guards, calendars, and rework are stated. `business_hours` on `discover_performance_dfg` is an extra keyword that many PM4Py versions do not accept. The global arrival average is correctly demoted to a diagnostic.

What holds the score up is the boundary: pre-submission effort is inside the twin so D1 and D7 cannot look good by pushing delay off the log; objections stay latent; emergency deletions are not treated as ordinary cases; service is not sampled from historical waits; validation includes replay, rolling-origin holdout, bottleneck perturbation, and an independent-case diagnostic; the decision rule is an explicit robust Pareto screen with a pilot path, and it does not claim optimality.
