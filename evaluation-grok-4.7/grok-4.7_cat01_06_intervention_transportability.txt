The answer is a complete, internally consistent transport memo: it keeps Site A’s nine-hour shift confounded, refuses blanket parallelism where originals are legally required, segments digital / originals / legal work, and ties the pilot to fidelity versus mechanism. The remaining faults are quantitative and logical, and they sit in the sections that carry the decision.

The service-overlap ceiling is too tight. Sequential identity-then-document removes the shorter activity’s service and the precedence-induced wait before the second check. Median identity service (about 3.1–3.5 h at A, 3.4 h at B) is therefore not an upper bound on a pure sequencing effect. Section 3 treats the gap between that ceiling and the 9 h / 6 h drops as identified confounding. Section 5 then anchors every transported range to roughly 0–5 h. Co-interventions make a large share of Site A’s change non-transportable, but the decomposition overstates how much of the residual is outside sequencing.

Assumption 4 states that if the originals delay is shorter than the identity sojourn, the critical-path gain is zero. If identity currently precedes receipt, overlapping them still saves about the length of the delay. The “do not expand” consequence is stronger than that case supports. The same section’s claim that the portfolio median case is originals-required because 62% exceeds half is only true under a stochastic ordering; the hedge does not repair the stated reason. The optimistic row then applies nearly a full identity sojourn to the all-case median, while the directly-follows graph already shows 1,860 Register → Originals Receipt edges against 1,020 Identity Check → Originals Receipt edges, so much of that nesting may already exist.

Stratum D is described as about one fifth to one third of volume. With originals at 62% and legal review at 40%, the feasible share of digital non-legal cases runs from 0 to 38% until the joint distribution is known. Section 7 marks the joint as unknown; section 9 presents the narrower band as a consequence of the 1,160 edge and the 62% figure.

The pilot’s appeal stop rule cannot fire on the stated cadence: 90-day appeals are not observable at the 2- and 4-week reviews, and no interim quality proxy is named. The sample-size floor of 150 per arm matches a mean-difference calculation at SD 12 h, while the named primary outcomes are medians and the same paragraph says SD 20 h needs about 400 cases. Overlap is defined as the identity–document intersection, which misses the originals-wave mechanism (identity nested in the receipt wait). The partial order and the code also drop the prose requirement that a supplement repeat the originals gate when the statute still requires it.

The process-tree contrast, selection-node graph, co-intervention inventory, bottleneck-shift scenario, role-specific WIP, and POWL construction (assessment joined to both verification branches, channel XOR, legal loop) are sound. `conditional go`, limited to digital non-legal cases after a logging repair, follows from the evidence. The score is held down by the bound, the zero-gain claim, the stratum-size slip, and the pilot’s inoperable safety rule.
