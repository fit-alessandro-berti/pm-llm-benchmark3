7.5

The dataset, O2O directions, provenance split, validations, and the four flattened trace shapes are internally consistent and match the narrative, including the 17 events, 12 objects, shared P1/V1 structure, and unallocated payments. Section coverage is complete, and the Petri-net caveat is appropriately non-unique.

The pipeline is not reliable against actual PM4Py behavior. `ocel_flattening` returns one traditional log, not a list of per-object frames. Wrapping that frame and keying `dfgs_by_case` by `case:concept:name.iloc[0]` stores an all-cases model under the first object and prints the wrong case count. The required type-level DFG still works only because of the later `concat`.

The Order DFG prediction is incomplete in the place that matters. O100’s activity sequence has consecutive Pack, Dispatch, and Confirm pairs, so the flattened DFG has those self-loops as well as Add→Add. Only the Add self-loop is stated, which underplays how flattening collapses P1/P2 and D1/D2 into repeated labels. The OC-DFG discussion also drifts toward global interleaving and type-touch degree rather than object-typed directly-follows.
