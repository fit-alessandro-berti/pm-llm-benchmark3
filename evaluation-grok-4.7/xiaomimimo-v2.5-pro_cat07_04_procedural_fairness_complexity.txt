The assessment meets every required section, respects the constraints, and reaches a calibrated conclusion: residual gaps remain after case-mix adjustment, support steps impose real burden, and the evidence does not establish intentional discrimination. The mitigation options include trade-offs, and the PM4Py plan covers filtering, variants, performance DFGs, handovers, waiting time, and constraint checks.

It is not nearly flawless. Group A’s 42% V1 share is called a majority, and “more than double the V1 share of any other group” is false for Group C (42% vs 22%). The waiting-time “decomposition” treats path-specific waits (3.8 and 4.7 days) as additive group-level contributions even though they exceed the adjusted gaps (+2.6 and +4.1 days) and are not weighted by how often those paths occur. Service-time differences are called modest with no service-time evidence, and Group D’s extra fraud-review path is treated as roughly zero additional steps. Resource ratios assign all Group B and C cases to the specialist pools despite the source only saying those cases are disproportionately assigned, and the dual-role claim is generalized from two of five accommodation specialists to the specialists as a group. The closing summary also compresses a −5.5 point approval gap into “2–5 percentage points.” The code sketch leaves `approved` undefined and would not run as written.
