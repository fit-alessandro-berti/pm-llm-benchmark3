7.5

The design correctly refuses to treat non-activated cases as non-defaults, accounts for all 48,000 applications, separates censoring mechanisms, and uses a temporal split around the policy change and migration. Those are the decisions that determine whether the study is honest. The score is not higher because the primary estimand and the skeleton both have concrete defects.

The written estimand is \(P(T^{\text{act}}<\infty,\ T^{\text{def}}\le T^{\text{act}}+90\mid X(t_0))\). With extraction on 15 February 2026, that “eventually” probability is not identified for late starts. What is identified is a cumulative incidence by a finite time, or among cases with enough potential follow-up. The 90-day clock starts at activation, while rejection, withdrawal, transfer, and administrative censoring run on the application clock. The state list is compatible with a multi-state model, but transitions, clock reset at activation, and censoring at 90 days post-activation are never specified. A standard competing-risk fit from application start would not implement this target.

The fixed split is weaker than the rolling backtest. Training labels are not required to have `label_available_at` before the validation window, so the main scheme can use outcomes that would not have been known at deployment. “First Credit Review” and “first \(k=3\) events” are called equivalent, then later treated as different rules. Impossible-order exclusions are not given a row, so the flow table’s claim to exhaust 48,000 is slightly loose. Last-event service time is not flagged as censored at the prediction instant.

The skeleton matches the design’s primary label rule and does not invent a PM4Py survival API. It is still outside the requested length, and two failures are real: timezone-aware `AS_OF` compared with naive `read_csv` timestamps, and `merge` on a named Series whose case id is an index rather than a column. `post_prefix_events` cannot fail, because `prefix_end_time` is the max timestamp of the rows being checked. `get_prefixes_from_log` is swallowed by a bare `except`. Elapsed-time prefixes and the short-prefix flag stay in prose only.
