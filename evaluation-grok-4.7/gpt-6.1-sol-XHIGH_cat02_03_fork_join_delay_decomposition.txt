8.4

The delay identities, the 12-case table, the F02/F04/F07/F11 arithmetic, the exclusive-excess figures, and the counterfactual savings are consistent with the timestamps. Fork-join time is handled as a maximum, synchronization is kept out of the additive flow total, and the A and B scenarios correctly separate readiness gains from flow-time gains, including F11’s 10-minute readiness gain that the final replay absorbs. The score is held down by a contradictory ranking sentence, unused resource evidence, and one loosely defined utilization figure.

The bold ranking in section 3 says A and C are “close behind” B. The same section’s table gives B a mean exclusive excess of 10.00 minutes and critical frequency 8/12, against 1.25 and 1.00 minutes and 2/12 for A and C. The following paragraph correctly treats A and C as close to each other. The headline and the table do not make the same claim about the gap to B.

`final_resource` alternates F1, F2, … by case order for all 12 cases. That pattern is relevant to whether downstream waiting is a pooled queue, a pre-assignment, or a release hold. The F03 and F08 idle-server examples are accurate, but the alternation organizes them: F08’s start lines up with F2 still occupied by F06, while F03 and F06 still wait after their alternating resource is already free. The mechanism discussion stays more generic than that column allows.

The 56.6% figure uses one 09:08–12:40 denominator (212 minutes) for both servers. F2’s first recorded start is 09:40 and F1’s last recorded completion is 12:10, so 212 minutes is the union span. The memo does say this is not measured utilization; the denominator is still arbitrary beside the precision of the rest.

A’s queue is 0 on every case. The secondary-effect paragraph on a 20% A service cut still invokes possible cross-case availability gains without using that fact. With no observed A waiting, a shorter A interval cannot remove an A queue from a later case in this log.

The eligibility-lag replay is labeled as illustrative and is bracketed by fixed final appointments and a zero-wait ceiling. Those bounds are the right uncertainty treatment. The Python matches the stated quantile, tie rule, and savings, including B’s flow p90 of 114.5 and the 171-versus-176 final-station split. A higher score would require the ranking sentence to match the table and the alternating resource column to be used as evidence. The numerical core has no material calculation error.
