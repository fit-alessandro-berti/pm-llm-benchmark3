The answer is strong on partial-order transport, confounding, and pilot logic, but it is not nearly flawless. The headline quantitative results are more assumed than derived, and the required model-check code is likely to crash.

The process comparison, causal graph, and refusal to copy Site A’s fork onto legally gated originals are correct. Site A’s −9 h median is treated as a confounded bundle (API, +2 document FTE, mix shift, huddles, possible timestamp improvement), not as an identified effect. The Site B design keeps `Originals Receipt ≺ Document Check`, joins assessment on both verifications, and nests legal review with a supplement loop. The pilot separates implementation failure from mechanism failure (audited overlap and lock conflicts versus join time, assessor/legal queues, ineligible spillover, and intention-to-treat). Word counts, table schemas, eight assumptions, and `conditional go` all match the requested form. The mixture arithmetic is internally consistent: the synthetic bands do reproduce median 44 and p90 110; within-band weights 45/55 and 10/90 match 38% no-originals and 62% originals; 35/10 match 30% eligibility; 30/80 match 40% legal; and the bottleneck case really does improve the mean by 1.54 h while moving p90 from 110 to 115.70.

The quantitative section still overstates identification. The 3.4 h figure is the only term taken from Site B service times. The 1.6 h handoff, 2 h originals saving, 8 h assessment penalty, 12 h legal penalty, and 1 h lock cost are round assumptions, then reported to two decimals (−3.35, +5.70, +7.30) as if the transport effect had been calculated. The legal scenario adds that penalty on top of the originals-weighted shift without a joint classification, so legal cases that are also originals-heavy are mis-weighted. Phase-1 eligibility of 30% is only an upper-bound story: it must also be non-legal and lower-complexity, and digital readiness is unknown. Independence of the stated margins is closer to the low teens before that filter, yet 30% is what the power sketch uses.

`sound, _ = pm4py.check_soundness(...)` does not match the usual boolean return, so the soundness check and playout never run. `add_transitive_edges()` is an extra API dependency. The POWL shape itself is the right one. Transport-confidence scores also switch meaning across rows: 90 on the no-control row is confidence that the before/after effect is not transportable, which the column header does not say.
