The reconstruction is numerically strong and mostly internally consistent: case waits, services, sojourn sums, WIP areas (2085 / 1340 / 3425), means, subperiod rates, resource bounds, and the four imputation scenarios check out, and open or waiting cases are kept in WIP and busy time. Missing starts are bounded from resource and queue constraints, censoring is separated from completed time, and the bottleneck claim correctly follows queue growth rather than utilization alone.

Deductions are for defects that a strict reading should not ignore. The code sets total WIP peak to the sum of the station peaks, so it would report 15 where the text correctly reports 13. The station service means fold right-censored elapsed times (P15, P17, P21) into the same average as completed services. For P05 and P11 the “central/median” service is the feasible maximum, so the headline S2 utilization of 80.9% is the top of the stated range. The claim that utilization rank reverses is not shown by the aligned scenarios, all of which keep S1 slightly above S2, although the independent ranges do overlap. S1 station sojourn is defined but never summarized as a number.
