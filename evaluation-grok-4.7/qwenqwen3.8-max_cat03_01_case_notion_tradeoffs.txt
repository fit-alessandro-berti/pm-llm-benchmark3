6.2

The flattening comparison is specific and mostly faithful to the E2O graph: shared-event copying for `e13`, `e14`, `e16`, `e19`, `e22`, and `e25`, the short package, delivery, invoice, and payment lifecycles, the `V2` instalments, and the decision to keep Order as a qualified lead with secondary lifecycles are all well supported. The required sections, matrix, distortion categories, portfolio, code outline, and sensitivity checks are present and grounded in identifiers.

That score is held down by a systematic misreading of the log clock. `e23` is 2026-02-03 14:00 and `e24` is 2026-02-03 16:00, both before `e25`–`e31`. The answer dates `e24` to 2026-02-04 16:00 and then builds a false “close-before-confirm” loop (`e28`–`e30` before `e24`) into the Order row, distortion 9, the reconciliation advice, and the residual risk. Under the real order, every confirmation precedes closure; the directly-follows pattern is Confirm Delivery toward Close Order, not a loop.

The same clock error warps performance. `D2` runs about five hours (`e16` 11:05 to `e24` 16:00). The long transport is `D3` (`e17` 2026-02-03 11:15 to `e27` 2026-02-04 10:00). Calling `D2` the long delivery, and saying payments occur before `e24`, is wrong for `e25`: the partial payment `e22` is before `e24`, but `e25` is the next morning, after `D2` has already been confirmed and before `e27`.

Convergence and divergence are also redefined as path similarity and variant count. In this flattening problem, convergence is replication of one event across several objects of the chosen type, and divergence is repeated or misleading directly-follows inside one object’s trace. By that standard, Delivery has almost no convergence, yet the matrix rates its convergence risk “very high,” while Order, which has the most replication, is only “medium.” The separate duplication column partly repairs this, but the two risk columns do not measure the hazards the comparison is about.

The non-temporal relation analysis would have been in the high range. The inverted chronology is used as a finding in four sections, so the answer is not close to flawless.
