The claim that a 3–4 point quality gain cannot justify the slower path is therefore not established for Standard low-risk, which is the distinction the data most clearly supports.

Flow accounting is treated as closed when it is not. Listed terminals are 3015 closes + 220 denials + 390 abandonments = 3625, so 1375 of 5000 cases have no END edge. Summing complaint edges as “460 minus overlap” obscures that 140+260+80+30 = 510, while 460 is simply 9.2% of 5000.

Path outcomes are used causally. “Shifting” high-value complaints from 18–22% toward 5–6% treats the observed reviewed/repair path as the effect of forcing repair. Redesign C’s fast reship for minor reopens can close a reopened claim without manual eligibility review, which cuts against R3. The pilot labels the high-value group a “control” while the stop/go test is whether blocking auto-approval for that group reduces complaints.

The sacrifices, conditional dominance language, bottleneck migrations, and end-to-end anti-shift checks are concrete and non-generic. The grade is limited by the flow misreads, the segment conflation, the causal path comparison, the R3 hole, and the mis-specified pilot.
