The narrative is a serious regime analysis: all 28 days are partitioned once, outage is separated from recovery, most means match the table, median service is not treated as flow time, and uncertainty is labeled. It is not nearly flawless.

The required Python block stops after building the two frames. It does not compute regime summaries, dispersion, serial dependence, a change-point, Little’s-law values, PM4Py comments, or printed tables, so six of the seven code requirements are missed.

Other defects are material. Printed backlog bridges omit signs (`Apr 16–17 30`, `Apr 29–May 1 18`), so they do not add to the stated +110. R6’s evidence cell says `WIP 4332`. R5 and R6 are judged “approximate only” in the same rows that mark boundary stability and stationarity as failed. Hourly Fano factors are not separated from the ordinary diurnal schedule. Weekday and weekend lag-1 correlations are taken on non-contiguous subsequences (Friday with Monday, Sunday with the next Saturday) without saying so. `peak_08_10_pct` is never checked against the hourly vectors, which do not reproduce “08:00–09:59.” May 2–3’s step from 2.5 to 3.0 FTE is blended into the pre-intervention high-mix weekend. Section 4’s weekend-versus-weekday flow times still pool outage, mix, and intervention inside “weekday.”
