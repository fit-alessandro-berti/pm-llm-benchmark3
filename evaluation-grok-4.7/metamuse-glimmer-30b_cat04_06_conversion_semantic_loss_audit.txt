4.5

The write-up has the required nine-part shape, a defensible primary pipeline (`POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN`), and a mostly real PM4Py script for that path, including a `try/except` around `convert_to_process_tree`. Sections 6–8 are directionally sound. The core semantic and validation deliverables are not.

The baseline witnesses contradict the invariants they are meant to pin down. For unordered Assess Risk / Prepare Evidence, the other legal order is listed as a negative witness. For optional Legal Review, the branch that takes Legal Review is listed as negative, so optionality is reversed. For the missing edge between optional legal and verification, one allowed interleaving is listed as negative. Several “positive” traces also start Configure Trial before both Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence have occurred, so they are illegal in the source and cannot witness the claimed order. The loop witness shows two iterations separated by Correct Defect and never shows the required exit after exactly one body, nor forbids ending on Correct Defect.

The audit then marks many of those invariants `validated preserved` and marks every process-tree cell `conversion failed`, citing soundness, footprints, and reachability. None of that was run. That collapses the distinction the task requires between expected preservation and checked preservation. Initial/final-marking preservation and binary `LOOP(body, redo)` semantics are only sketched.

The bounded explorer does not implement the required check. It never fires a transition (`new_marking = marking.copy()`), never executes silent transitions (it drops them instead of hiding them), never increments the loop bound, and leaves both witness checks as `pass`. The closing claim that this search hides tau, demands exact final-marking equality, and blocks silent cycles describes a different algorithm. Footprints and reachability graphs are computed and then not compared; state-count comparison would not establish visible-language equivalence anyway. The BPMN produced from a process tree is never converted back, and workflow-net checks are not applied to every accepting net. Playout is omitted with no reason.

Rejection criteria cover unsoundness, early release, collapsed optionality, and final-marking failure, but not loss of a required interleaving. Residual uncertainty overstates the concurrency point: this loop does not make the state space unbounded, so “undecidable in general” is the wrong limit; the real gap is that a length bound plus direct-follows footprints cannot prove full-language equivalence.
