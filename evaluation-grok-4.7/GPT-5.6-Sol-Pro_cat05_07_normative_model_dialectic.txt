8.2

The brief is unusually strong: both sides are actually argued, concessions are real, the fifteen-case matrix is complete and traceable, expiry is not treated as a vibe, and the layered redesign correctly refuses to stuff amount, identity, and authority into POWL. The deductions below are why it is not nearly flawless.

The SUP-702 label is the clearest analytical slip. Complete eligibility fields, an in-window date, next-business-day quality review, and a stable `notification_id` are affirmative case facts. The missing pieces are a master-data validation event that the source log never records for any case, plus the system-wide PD-3 authority dispute. Calling that `DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE` misnames a burden-of-proof failure. Applied consistently, the same gap would make all 289 “apparently eligible” pilot cases indeterminate, which the system narrative never does.

SUP-713 is over-called. Section 1 correctly says equivalence is strongly supported and not certain, and the mapping was approved a week after the case. Section 5 then marks the case `COMPLIANT` at high confidence. Substance can still carry the classification; high confidence cannot.

The POWL matches the required envelope (concurrent standard checks, manual versus auto versus emergency, `A*` via `LOOP(τ, Request_More_Info)`, optional quality on rejection, notify-before-close). It is also looser than the prose admits. `Auto_Approve` is unordered relative to sanctions and master-data validation, and on the auto and emergency routes `Quality_Review` has no edges at all, so it may precede sanctions. Those are material language facts, not covered by the generic “permissive envelope” caveat. The declarative layer also names withdrawal and cancellation as terminal outcomes while Section 6 admits no such procedure exists.

Executable status is overclaimed. Syntax is not construction. `add_transitive_edges()` and `validate_partial_orders()` are asserted as current PM4Py APIs and cited to source, but the code was never run. If either method is absent or the stored relation is expected to be a transitive reduction, `root` does not build. Historical pilot traces also lack `Automated_Master_Data_Validation` as a visible activity, so this normative net would reject the very in-window cases the brief treats as potentially justified unless a mapping assumption is added.

Smaller traceability nits: SUP-706 is undated, yet the defense applies the 2026-07-01 CRM break as if it were established for that case; SUP-711’s duplicate is real data contamination even though the underlying business trace conforms. Authority, FD-7/PD-3 expiry, SUP-703, SUP-708, SUP-712, and the four-eyes versus disclosure split are handled correctly and should not be flattened into those defects.
