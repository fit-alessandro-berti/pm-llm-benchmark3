The primary plan overstates deadline feasibility. With S4 free only at 35–65 minutes, a 35–75 minute review, and 5–15 minutes to record and execute, S4 at 65 minutes cannot finish inside 90 minutes even on the shortest review. S4 at 35 minutes meets the deadline only on the short tail of review time (on the order of a quarter of that range, not a “high chance”). Across the full availability window the on-time probability is small. Section 5 still calls the early-S4 path a high chance of completion and says that, if S4 is at ~65 minutes, C “can move above E/F/G,” even though C is already ranked second. The €20k–€35k on-time benefit attached to rank 1 is therefore mostly the speculative fast-clear branch, not the S4 second-review branch the plan prefers.

The fallback is also mis-aimed. If alternate screening raises suspicion, the value of rushing execution collapses, so interrupting P-901 becomes harder to justify, not easier. The write-up sends that case to C anyway. The “cutoff-driven” plan never states a cutoff, and a late switch to S2 (after a 15–30 minute screen plus news that S4 is late) itself misses the window except on an optimistic review.
