The audit is complete and often well-aimed: full-trace and post-closure fields are rejected, queue length and elapsed time are kept, region is treated as a fairness proxy rather than a cause, and `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is correctly separated from the impossible same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` pattern. Case Z’s uncertainty note is the strongest part.

The applied conclusions are not reliable. Case X is marked `retain` and the narrative still attributes the 0.82 score to complexity and congestion, even though leaked drivers (`appeal_seen_anywhere` +0.26, `final_decision` +0.06) plus the base account for most of that figure; admissible named effects sum to about 0.29. Case Y has the same error: `recompute` is right, but the narrative still presents 0.71 as driven by region and reserve when the full-trace variant alone is +0.31. Those probabilities should not be retained.

`Documents Requested -> Documents Received` is rated low and “defensible if prefix-only.” At this `t0` the prefix is only registration and coverage check, so that transition is future information. That rating also conflicts with the later “future sequence patterns are unusable” class. `final_handler_team` is softened to high because it matches the `t0` team 65% of the time, and the row wrongly ties it to Case X’s `final_decision`. Agreement rate does not make a closure-time field available. `initial_reserve` and `claimant_channel` are treated as frozen registration facts without any lineage; silence is not provenance. The stated as-of-extraction join for employee experience is never picked up.

Section 2 lists `Appeal` and `Claim Closed` as both future-possible and impossible, and it treats evidence collection as required before settlement even on the fast track. Narratives also recommend accelerating assessment and managing workload after the causal boundary correctly says these associations are not shown to be actionable. Severity values such as “Low / Medium” fall outside the required scale.
