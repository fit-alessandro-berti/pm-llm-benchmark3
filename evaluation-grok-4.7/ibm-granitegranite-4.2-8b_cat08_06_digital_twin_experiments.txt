4.7

The write-up covers every required section and correctly treats the twin as a capacitated, calendar-aware, non-stationary system with separate applicant delays and unobserved objections. Those strengths are outweighed by checkable errors that would mis-specify the model and the decision.

Baseline performance contradicts the supplied variant table. Simple clean-path median is 12 days (p90 24) and missing-document median is 22 days (p90 45), so an overall simple median of about 8 days and p90 of about 12 days is impossible. New residential is likewise pulled down to about 35 days against a stated 48-day median for the dominant fire-plus-notice variant. SLA rates, rework rates, and the “baseline minus 2 days” rule are built on those figures, so the decision criteria are not usable.

Resource assumptions conflict internally. Environmental capacity is given as both 0.6 FTE and 1.8 FTE. Fire review is limited to one case per FTE per day despite a 2.5-hour median service time on an 8-hour Tuesday–Thursday calendar. Objection probabilities, a 90% “no prior violation” rate, a 60% document-loop reduction, and “20.7” missing documents are presented as estimates although they are not identified from the log. Prior violations are unobserved and should have been a sensitivity factor.

Cross-case behavior is incomplete. Inspection bundling is present, but architect submission bursts are not an arrival process, and objections are explicitly reduced to a single-case duration draw even though the brief says they can affect several related cases.

The experiment table includes baseline, D1–D10, and three stresses, but the stress rows are malformed, historical-versus-stress crossing is asserted rather than designed, and common random numbers are absent. Warm-up of four months from an empty system is too short for cases whose p90 is 260 days; censoring and initial WIP are ignored. The lognormal quantile formula is wrong. Replay “validation” uses two illustrative cases and then both adjusts parameters to that replay and refines the objection proxy on the holdout, which breaks the out-of-sample claim. Utilization cannot be read off a completion-only log, yet it is treated as observed.

The skeleton misses the required PM4Py contract. It never calls `format_dataframe`, never computes variants, a performance DFG, arrival average, or timestamp-based sojourn columns, and it falsely says `parse_process_tree` does not exist. The APIs it does call (`pm4py.log.normalize`, `pm4py.models.extract_dfg`) are not the library’s conventions. The arrival routine double-counts the 1.7× seasonality and does not generate the observed case volume; council-week logic compares weekday names to date tuples; the calendar has hours but no server counts; and the example policy dict does not match the keys the runner reads. The process tree is never connected to routing.
