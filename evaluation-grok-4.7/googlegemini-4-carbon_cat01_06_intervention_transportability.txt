7.2

The answer is a serious transportability analysis with the right decision, the right legal constraint, and a usable partial-order design. It is not nearly flawless. Several quantitative claims are internally inconsistent or directionally reversed, and those claims sit in the sections the task weights most.

The process comparison covers the twelve required features and correctly blocks universal `Document Check` at registration. The transportability write-up stays inside the word limit and separates critical-path overlap, join synchronization, originals, locks, specialization, and co-interventions. The recommendation is a genuine `conditional go`: digital claims first, originals kept as `Identity Check` concurrent with `Originals Receipt`, statutory gates left intact, and expansion tied to overlap, cycle time, tail, and rework. The POWL sketch and the PM4Py code match that design. `Assessment` is a real join of identity and document verification, originals are ordered before document checking when present, and legal review is an optional loop whose redo repeats document check and assessment. The comments correctly say the net cannot encode data guards, capacity, locks, or durations. That is the strongest part of the answer.

The quantitative layer overclaims identification. Site A’s −9h median is properly refused as a pure treatment effect, then replaced with a false decomposition: 3.5–4.5h “structural” and 4.5–5.5h “confounders.” The second piece is the residual, not an estimate. A net capacity gain of about 2.6% per completed case and a 0.4h identity-service drop cannot be shown to produce several hours. The −6h drop in pre-assessment wait is also left partly inside both buckets. The post-period 3.1h identity time is used as if it were a pre-period overlap ceiling.

The originals scenario reverses a directly-follows arrow. `Identity Check -> Originals Receipt` (1,020) means identity precedes originals. The text calls this “~30% of originals cases” in which originals precede identity. 1,020/3,400 is 30% of all cases, and 1,020/2,108 is about 48% of originals cases, in the opposite order. The 0.8–1.8h originals savings range happens to look plausible under a different mechanism, but it is attached to the wrong subset. Scenario S5 then adds the 8.1h document median, the 20h assessment-wait median, and legal queueing into a p90 increment. Those are baseline medians, not incremental tail delays. Assumption 4 correctly notes that 3,400 × 8.1h cannot be touch time for 7 document FTEs, yet Scenarios S1–S2 still subtract those elapsed medians as clean critical-path savings.

The pilot’s causal idea is good: C versus rule-only versus rule-plus-unlock can separate implementation failure from mechanism failure, and shared assessment and legal pools are acknowledged. The power claim is not. With median 44h and p90 110h, a lognormal scale is about 0.72. At roughly 216 digital cases per main arm, the standard error of a median difference is on the order of 4h, so a 3.2h shift is far below 80% power. The 10-week, 1,420-case figure also treats 10 weeks as 2.5 months and mixes a digital-first rollout with a site-wide sample. Stratum sizes of ~23% and ~15% are presented as volumes in the recommendation, then listed as an untested independence assumption.

Smaller defects accumulate. `digital_documents -> originals_required` is an unsupported causal direction. Supplement frequency switches between 360 and 345. Digital savings are −5.2h in the text and −5.0h in the table. Co-interventions are folded into metric rows, and the absent negative control never becomes its own evidence row. S1 and S2 confidences of 70 and 75 are high relative to the unidentified Site A residual, the lock risk, and the service-time paradox. The WIP caps, +25% join boost, and 18-case throttle are unestimated constants.

Format, coverage, legal caution, confounder awareness, bottleneck-shift scenario S3, and the candidate net are strong enough to keep this well above a merely adequate answer. The reversed DFG reading, the circular hour split, and the impossible power claim are material, so the score stays out of the high band.
