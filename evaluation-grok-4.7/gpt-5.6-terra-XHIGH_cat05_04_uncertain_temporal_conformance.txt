8.3

The primary interval arithmetic, DST-fold resolution, SLA and profile labels, and the tolerance/zeta matrix are accurate and internally consistent. Offsets are applied in the right direction after resolution uncertainty, Berlin/New York/London conversions are right, and the only ingestion-consistent 2025-10-26 fold assignment is the one used. Certain versus possible versus indeterminate is handled carefully, including T-404 inference, T-407 sent-versus-received, and the T-406 entry-delay split. I did not find a baseline classification that the stated bounds actually reverse.

It is still short of nearly flawless. The T-409 causal clip to zero is not supported by the case facts; it is disclosed and does not flip the label, but the profile row reports the clipped interval as the feasible duration. The record-time-only mobile model changes Investigation_Start to Containment at zeta 1 from possible violation to certain pass, and that profile change is absent from the sensitivity matrix even though the SLA change is discussed. The PM4Py section builds a labeled midpoint log and calls the requested APIs, but it only dumps the two results: it never shows concrete reversals, such as a midpoint pass where the interval still straddles the limit. The SLA acceptance “region” is written as a bare threshold, and “confirmed breach” is stronger than the model-conditional certainty claimed in the assumptions. The legacy fold filter in code is an existence test, weaker than the hard order described in prose, though that does not change this dataset.
