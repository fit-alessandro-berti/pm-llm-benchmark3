7.5

The design is complete, constraint-aware, and unusually careful about explainability, ownership versus execution, and process-mining bias. It still has enough specification gaps and internal inconsistencies that it is not nearly flawless.

The policy covers every required mechanism, separates hard rules from preferences, and refuses to waive Security, Approval, skill-0, and overtime protections merely to hit an SLA. The queue table respects the hard rules: no high or urgent executor is below skill 2, high-overtime resources receive no new execution, Quality batching meets the 24-hour rule, and the four-duty cap holds under the stated counting rules. C001, C007, C008, C011, and the decision to keep C016 with Eva are well justified. The worker text is plain and non-punitive. The audit section correctly separates executor handoffs from ownership changes, requires versioned skills, and warns that specialists and training cases are selected, so raw durations are not performance scores.

The recommendation is not shown to be the output of the scoring rule. Component F is never defined, resilience is prose rather than a score, urgency flattens every SLA above 24 hours to the same value, and the post-hoc repair procedure has no swap rule. A supervised skill-match cap of 0.8 can outrank an unsupervised executor whose skill/target ratio is lower, which inverts the intended penalty. The 3.3-hour weak-skill prior is narrowed to unsupervised skill 1 without support. C001’s owner is skill 1 and supervision is feasible, so the continuity rule still points toward Celine; moving execution to Hugo is defensible for a 3-hour specialist Security case, but it is not marked as an exception.

Eva’s package is the weakest allocation. She has 5.0 hours left and medium overtime risk, yet receives C005, C016, C010, and the C015 shadow. C010 and the shadow are avoidable: Farid has more residual time, and the policy’s own prepare-versus-finalize split is never used. Jonas reaches four heterogeneous duties while his documented Security priority is given to the more loaded person. Coordination for retained owners on C001 and C003 is excluded from the cap even though the policy says coaching must not be invisible labor. The 20% training result is 2/9 only because skill-0 shadowing counts; that reading is disclosed, and the stricter 1/9 shortfall is real.

The baseline comparison is directionally right and correctly calls literal first-available non-compliant, but the concrete counts are tie-break and order dependent. In C001–C016 order, Farid gets four specialist assignments only if he wins the open-case tie on C011; otherwise he gets three. Celine exceeds four only under particular ties. Those outcomes are stated more firmly than the assumptions support.

The PM4Py sketch uses the requested organizational, performance, batching, service-time, and filtering functions and correctly refuses to treat a single timestamp as active service time. The audit code is looser than the prose: any skill below 2 on Final Approval is a violation, although skill 1 may prepare; rework is any Rework Correction in the case, not rework after the decision; and the SLA uses the last event timestamp, including open cases. The training detector is a reason-text regex.
