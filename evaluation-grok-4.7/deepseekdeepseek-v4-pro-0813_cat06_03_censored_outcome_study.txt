7.2

The design is strong on cohort honesty, leakage awareness, and temporal validation, but the primary estimand is not statistically closed, and the skeleton reintroduces the binary collapse the prose correctly rejects.

The observed-policy target is the right primary choice. All 48,000 applications are accounted for; rejected, withdrawn, transferred, and open cases are not coded as non-default; delayed labels, transfer linkage, partner missingness, and what is testable versus untestable are separated cleanly. The leakage table covers every required field, the split is by case start with policy change and migration called out, and the three deployment feedback loops are concrete.

The central flaw is the time scale. Default is defined on a clock that starts at activation, while rejected, withdrawn, and transferred compete with activation, not with post-activation default. Truncation at 365 days from start then cuts off late activations whose 90-day window extends past that horizon, and that edge is never resolved. Section 3 tells you to censor the 18,900 mature non-defaults at activation+90, but the primary strategy never places “survived 90 days” in the state space. On an application-time multi-state model that censoring is informative: those cases are known not to enter the default state, yet they are removed as if their remaining hazard matched cases still in process. Aalen–Johansen or Fine–Gray cannot be dropped on top of that encoding without bias. Administrative censoring at day 90 is valid only for a clock that resets at activation.

The code contradicts the design. `default_event` is 1 only for mature defaults and 0 for competing exits, open cases, immature labels, missing feeds, and true non-defaults alike, which is exactly the convenient label the study forbids. Censoring time is never built, despite the requirement for case-level censoring as of the cutoff. Prefixes may contain Rejected, Withdrawn, or Transferred, so `last_activity` leaks a completed competing outcome, and this is not in the leakage table. `final_limit_available` and `decision_reason_available` still reveal that a final decision has occurred. The test window starts 1 October 2025, so it mixes pre-migration cases with the 15 October migration. The skeleton is also well past the requested length, and `get_prefixes_from_log` is only counted, not used to build the feature table.
