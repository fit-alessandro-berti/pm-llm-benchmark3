5.4

The case spine is coherent, but the briefs, matrix, and POWL revision contain material semantic and evidentiary errors, so this is not a strong answer under a strict reading.

The dispositions for SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-705, SUP-706, SUP-707, SUP-708, SUP-712, and SUP-714 are defensible, and the judgment correctly treats in-window PD-3/FD-7 authority as real while refusing post-expiry normalization. Burden of proof is separated from mere fitness. That is the main credit.

Dialectical fairness fails an explicit instruction. The prosecution brief never concedes the genuine model and logging defects (the missing loop, CRM mirror loss, duplicate register, label drift, ambiguous lead/reactivation scope). The defense brief never honestly concedes SUP-703, SUP-708, or SUP-712; “timing error” and “fits M0” are straw defenses. SUP-715 is grouped with a CS-9 “letter” breach even though the two approvers are different humans and the proved gap is an unrecorded conflict disclosure, which the source says no rule flatly prohibits.

M0 semantics are mishandled in the matrix. The reject branch has no manual approval, so SUP-709 is “missing Quality_Review,” not “missing approval.” SUP-702, SUP-703, and SUP-714 are reduced to “missing manual approval,” omitting Quality_Review after Activate_Supplier. SUP-702 also omits the stable `notification_id` at +2 hours and the unlogged master-data validation PD-3 requires; “missing evidence: none” is incorrect. SUP-708’s trace also activates before manual approval. Concurrency is named and then not analyzed. SUP-711, with two register rows sharing one source transaction ID, is determinate duplication, not an indeterminate case. Confidence is overstated for the scope, identity, and authority disputes the answer itself flags. Severity is not given for every case.

The revision is structurally incomplete. Withdrawals are absent. The overlay “table” is a column list, with no PD-3/FD-7 rows for window, LOW-risk DE group, sanctions-before-activation, two-business-day quality, EUR 20,000, or daily monitoring. Section 6 filters pre-screens out of the model; the code then admits an early-reject branch. The sample eligibility rule hard-codes 2026-07-31 while the registry is supposed to own windows.

The POWL does not implement the author’s own constraint. `checks_loop`, `activate`, and `reject` are reused across XOR parents after the text says reuse must be avoided. `LOOP(checks, Request_More_Info)` has language checks (Request_More_Info, checks)*, so it cannot emit SUP-707’s consecutive requests between Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review. The auto route still requires both checks before Activate_Supplier, so it rejects the documented pilot pattern it is meant to represent. `SilentTransition` is unused, and no limitation states these mismatches. Data fields are the best part of section 7, but they cannot repair the control-flow model.
