7.5

The design meets every required section and the hard constraints: non-stationary arrivals, calendars, latent objections, service versus wait, cross-case effects, and no optimality claim. PM4Py use is mostly conventional (`pandas.read_csv`, `format_dataframe`, variants, DFG, performance DFG, arrival average, guarded `parse_process_tree`). That is not enough for a near-flawless score.

The process-tree reading is wrong in a way that would change the simulator. `+` already AND-joins planning with optional fire and environment, and `->` already places public notice, inspection, and decision drafting after that join. The tree does encode control-flow join semantics. It does not encode planning, legal, or appeal rework, only completeness and inspection loops. Calling those gaps “missing rework probabilities” understates missing structure. “Join all applicable and started branches” is the wrong enablement rule: decision drafting must wait until every applicable branch has completed, including a required review that is still queued and not started. In-flight parallel branches versus planning or legal rework are unspecified.

Baseline batching is ambiguous. Section 1 allows architect–district bundles, while D3 is district batching, but the assumptions never fix whether B0 already batches. Without that, D3’s incremental effect is unidentified. Travel is not separated from site-inspection time, yet missing-data assumptions never say so. Open or censored cases in an 18-month window with 260-day tails are ignored, so completed-case quantiles can be length-biased. Weekend and after-hours portal arrivals (the excerpt has a Saturday submission) are not modeled as Monday queue buildup. Whether the six non-simple planners also take simple work is never stated. Reject is given a duration but no resource. Council meetings are tied to legal unavailability, not to the stated public-notice effect. Environmental 40% non-permit time and D4’s peak-only FTE need time-varying capacity; `ResourcePool.capacity` is a scalar and `is_available` is boolean.

The shift-based “sojourn” is not a predecessor sojourn under parallelism, and `discover_performance_dfg` is left on the mean default despite the heavy-tail warning. Warm-up is “6 months” without pinning seasonal phase. Stress rows say B0 and the best policy; the experiment dict does not cross them. D2 can meet the adopt rule on duration and SLA while compliance risk stays off-ledger. Adoption cutoffs (10%, 15%, 5 pp, 80%, 90% utilization) are presented as decision rules without showing they were pre-committed by the municipality rather than fit to hoped-for effects.

Estimation, CRN streams, per-replication quantile intervals, hold-out versus replay, and non-additivity of D10 are sound. The remaining holes are design errors, not omitted headings.
