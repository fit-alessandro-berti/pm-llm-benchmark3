6.2

The write-up has the required structure, separates assumptions from estimates, and handles the four missing starts with defensible resource-and-queue bounds rather than a silent point fill. Most fully observed waits and services, the S2 busy-time arithmetic, the completed-case sojourn list except one case, and the qualitative morning-S1 / afternoon-S2 story are in good shape. It is not close to exact.

The final numbers still contain several concrete errors. P20’s S1 wait is 105 minutes, not 100. P06’s arrival-to-completion sojourn is 135 minutes, not 115, so the reported mean of 145 is slightly low. Section 6 then says completed sojourn grows monotonically; the same answer’s own series falls from 180 (P08) to 155 (P09). I1 in-window busy time double-counts P21’s observed 30 minutes, so the stated range [275, 345] and the 76–96% utilization should be [245, 315] and about 68–88%. That error is repeated in the optimistic, central, and pessimistic scenarios and shifts combined S1 utilization up by about four points. Peak queue lengths are high: S1 peaks at 7, not 8–9, and S2 peaks at 5, not about 6.

The subperiod table is not a consistent partition. Arrivals are totaled as 14+8+1=23 for 22 cases because P14 is counted twice. P15’s 12:15 S1 completion is placed in 10:00–12:00, and P10/P11’s S2 completions at 12:10 and 12:25 are also placed there. The afternoon claim that S2 receives 5 arrivals against 3 completions is therefore wrong; under a half-open split the late window is about 7 S1 completions against 6 S2 completions. The backlog at 14:00 is real, but the rate evidence used to explain it is not. Start counts are also off by one at both stations (21 S1 starts and 16 S2 starts, not 22 and 17).

The Python does not implement the printed analysis. `feasible_missing` compares a datetime with the raw time string, so the first missing start raises `TypeError` before any table is printed. Even if that were fixed, OPEN elapsed service is stored as NaN and dropped from busy time, censored S2 waits are dropped from the mean, the first `format_dataframe` result is discarded, and the “S1 WIP peaks” print is `wip_s2`.
