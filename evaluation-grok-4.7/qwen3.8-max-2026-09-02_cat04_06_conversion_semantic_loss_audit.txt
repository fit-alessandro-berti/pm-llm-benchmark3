The document meets the required nine-section structure and is substantively strong: the POWL invariants, primary Petri-net path, allowed process-tree failure, silent optionality, binary loop, release join, and visual-versus-behavioral distinction are mostly right. The validation design also covers witnesses, exact final markings, tau-bounded search, soundness, and footprints.

The audit is not reliable as written. Several cells are marked “validated preserved” even though nothing was executed, including BPMN round-trip behavior. That directly conflicts with the required distinction between expected preservation and actual validation. The table is also inconsistent: invariants with the same witness evidence are variously “validated preserved,” “expected preserved,” and “approximated,” and the process-tree column is declared “conversion failed” before the conversion is run.

The code has concrete defects. Silent hiding goes beyond labels of `None` to strings such as “start” and “end,” so it can erase or invent visible behavior. `accepts_visible_trace` drops truncation, letting negative witnesses pass when search is incomplete. Footprint hashes use `default=str` on sets, so set order can make equal footprints compare unequal. A bounded-language exception is stored and then omitted from the report. These are small individually, but they undermine the proposed equivalence check.
