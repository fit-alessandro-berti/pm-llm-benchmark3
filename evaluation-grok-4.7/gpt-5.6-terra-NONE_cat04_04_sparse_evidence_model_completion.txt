6.5

The evidence discussion is careful and the three candidates are genuinely different hypotheses, but the required models are not faithfully implemented. That is a core failure, not a cosmetic one.

The conservative tree is not valid PM4Py. `parse_process_tree` recognizes `X`, not `xor(`. As written, `model_conservative` does not encode the seven-variant XOR, and section 8 uses the same string with no guard, so the validation script can fail before the other models are measured. Duplicate-label and scalability remarks do not repair that.

The balanced formalization and the section 4 program disagree with each other. The expression puts `A` outside the partial order while the constraints mention `A`, and the code puts `A` inside the order and then sequences only `P` and archive. Worse, `balanced_middle` builds its own `Credit Check` and `Engineering Review`, then `add_edge` is called on a different `Transition` passed in from outside the node set. That edge does not implement “`C` before `S` or `E` before `S`.” NetworkX will silently add the stray node, so the credit branch can require two Credit Check transitions. Section 8 rebuilds the intended net correctly and never states that it replaced the earlier model. The claimed language, the audit, and `model_balanced` are therefore three different objects.

Section 2 asserts, as a shared modeling choice, that `Q` may occur before or after `S` once both checks have completed. The conservative language allows only the two observed expedite traces, so that assumption is false for one of the three models. The permissive inductive-bias paragraph says `S` may occur after either check or after both; the formula, code, and audit also allow `S` before both checks. The later boundary statement corrects this, but the bias statement does not.

The conservative exclusion list is partly unreadable (“`S` after both checks but followed by no `S` abstraction”) and mixes the `C,E` order with the trace `R,E,C,Q,S,…`. The positive suite treats `R,C,E,Q,A,P,X` as positive for every model, including the conservative model that is defined to reject it. Results are only printed, not checked against per-model expectations.

What keeps the score from falling further is the ledger: variants, D1–D7, and S1–S7 are all present, and both DFG directions are correctly denied as proof of concurrency. Missing `S` is treated as an observation abstraction rather than as proven optionality. The intended languages, excluded traces, and the immediate-use versus sensitivity split are coherent, and the collection plan (sourcing attribute, start/complete timestamps, integration status, expedite reason, and overlap versus non-overlap cases) would actually separate the live hypotheses. Those strengths do not make the delivered programs match the prose.
