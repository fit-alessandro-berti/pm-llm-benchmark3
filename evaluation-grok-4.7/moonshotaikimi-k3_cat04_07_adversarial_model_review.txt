8.3

The final review is a strong, usable governance memo: probe verdicts for B1–B8 match the process-tree semantics, the metric table refuses a composite score, the pathology ranking is decision-relevant, and the third model is genuinely safer than S and less brittle than C. The dissent is a real counterargument within the required length.

Deductions are for concrete defects, not tone. The alignment-cost skeleton calls `PARAM_MODEL_MOVE_COST_FUNCTION` and `PARAM_LOG_MOVE_COST_FUNCTION`; PM4Py’s alignment parameters are `PARAM_MODEL_COST_FUNCTION` and `PARAM_TRACE_COST_FUNCTION`, so that skeleton raises `AttributeError`. The stratified-split helper labels any case missing a check as `CCfirst` and buckets missing approvals as `MA`, which contaminates the strata it is meant to protect. Reachability is asserted from stochastic `play_out`, which can both miss a live label and fail on a reachable one. The correction region silently forces both initial checks to finish before any `Correct Dossier`, so a plausible mid-review correction is rejected and never listed as a limitation. Smaller slips: “at least five” admissions are followed by seven traces, and a fitness of 0.973 is described as “missing 2.7% of cost.” None of these overturn the recommendation, but they keep the paper short of nearly flawless.
