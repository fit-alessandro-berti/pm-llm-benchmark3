7.5

The pipeline is complete and the in-memory OCEL is largely faithful: 17 events, 12 objects, copied activity/timestamp on every E2O row, coherent qualifiers, correct O2O direction, amounts kept on events, and validations that actually check uniqueness, orphans, duplicates, and event–relation consistency. Trace shapes for Order, Package, Delivery, and Invoice match the chosen direct-link policy, and I2 is correctly left out of stock reservation.

The interpretation then makes several false claims.

- Order flattening is 8 + 6 = 14 incidences over 11 distinct events. “17 source events become 20” assumes every event is in the order log; six are not (both dispatches, both confirmations, both payments). Failure mode 4 repeats this by saying those flattened counts exceed 17.
- Reserve Stock touches only Item and Order. Only Pack Package has degree 3. Calling Reserve Stock a three-type activity is wrong.
- O100’s event partners are I1, I2, I3, O200, P1, P2, and V1. The listed set drops I3 and O200. O200’s partners are I1, I3, O100, P1, and V1, so “subset centered on I3, P1, and V1” is false and misses the I1 convergence.
- “Before D2 closed the second order” mixes delivery D2 with order closure. M2 is after both confirmations, including D2. Check 7’s “despite” is also backwards: D2 being confirmed after D1 agrees with P2 being packed and dispatched later.
- Section 1 says order links on packing are only the narrative-explicit P1 case, while the qualifier list and E08 also attach O100 by inference from containment. That is the provenance slip the failure-mode section itself warns about.
- “One place per object type” does not match PM4Py’s usual per-type workflow net with merged transitions, and that sentence also mixes type-specific transitions with a single shared transition. The non-uniqueness caveat only partly repairs it.
- The order DFG discussion flags Pack Package → Pack Package and omits the same label collapse on Add Item → Add Item for O100.

Modeling alternatives, payment-trace weakness, and the manual audit checks are otherwise strong. Those factual slips in the expected-behavior sections keep it well short of nearly flawless.
