3.2

The submitted analysis is incomplete and internally inconsistent, and its treatment of the cases that actually require reconstruction is largely indefensible.

Sections 5–7 are absent, so there is no bottleneck argument, no three-scenario sensitivity analysis, and no reproducible Python/PM4Py implementation. Section 4 stops mid-cell. Those are required deliverables, not optional extras.

Fully observed waits and services for P01–P04, P06, P08–P10, P12–P14, P18–P20, and P22 are mostly right. The mandatory hard cases are not. Final claims still violate resource single-tasking or basic arithmetic:

- P05’s final S2 wait of [35, 45] assumes C1 becomes free at 10:15. C1 is free after P03 at 09:45 and is next occupied by P07 at 10:20, so the feasible start is about [09:45, 10:15] and the feasible wait is about [20, 50]. A service of 40 minutes, as in the table, would start at 09:35 and overlap P03.
- P07’s final S1 service of [60, 75] is impossible. I1 is free only after P05 ends at 09:25 and P07 ends at 10:00, so service is at most 35 minutes. The same paragraph states that start window and then reports a duration longer than the window.
- P11’s final S2 start in [11:00, 11:10) is before P11’s own S1 end (11:05), falls inside the C2 outage, and overlaps P08 (11:15–12:00). The feasible start is after 12:00 and before the recorded end 12:25.
- Censored durations are wrong where they are not later corrected: P16’s S2 wait is at least 85 minutes (14:00 − 12:35), not 145; P17 has 5 minutes of elapsed S2 service, not 65; P21 has 30 minutes elapsed in S1, not 90. The table also gives P15 an elapsed S2 service of 90 minutes; only a later sentence corrects that to 30.
- P15’s eventual S1 service of [25, 35] sits inside the feasible [0, 35] after P13 releases I1 at 11:40, but the required table still shows service [60, 80] and was not reconciled with that correction.

Station metrics repeat the confusion the task forbids. S1 completions by 10:30 are 8, not 13, and an in-service case is counted as a completion. S1 WIP by 10:30 is about 8, not a peak of 5. Busy time near 280 of 300 resource-minutes, and utilization [85%, 100%], overstates I1/I2 occupancy once P07 is constrained. The 08:00–10:30 Little’s Law window is declared free of missing starts even though P05 and P07 fall inside it. The second S1 row’s completion count is also low (6 observed S1 ends in 10:30–12:00, not 4).

Assumption labels and the fully observed rows show some process-mining structure, but the final numerical picture of missing starts, censoring, utilization, and WIP does not survive the resource calendars in the data.
