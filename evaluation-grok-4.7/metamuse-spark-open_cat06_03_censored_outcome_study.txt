7.3

The study is a serious design: the factual joint estimand is the right primary target, rejected and withdrawn cases are not recoded as non-defaults, the 48,000-case arithmetic closes, censoring mechanisms are separated, and validation is temporal rather than a random split. It is not nearly flawless. Several design and implementation choices are statistically loose or internally inconsistent.

The primary estimand is incomplete. “Activated within observation window W” never defines W, so two prefixes with different residual follow-up do not share a common activation horizon. The landmark itself is also not a single rule: section 1 uses “3 events or 72h, whichever…,” while section 4 later declares elapsed time primary. The product formula \(\pi=P(\text{Activated}\mid\text{prefix})\,P(\text{default}\mid\text{Activated},\text{prefix})\) is right only after censoring and missing feeds are removed from both factors; section 1 writes it as if the conditional were directly observed.

Cohort accounting is mostly exact (9,100+5,700+2,900+5,100+21,600+3,600=48,000; 60% of 2,900 is 1,740). The claim that permanently missing partner feeds include “some of the 21,600” is not. That export is defined as already labeled (18,900 non-defaults and 2,700 defaults). Those feeds belong in the immature or omitted activated mass, not in the labeled 21,600.

The missingness strategy names the right mechanisms, then mixes estimators. Putting censored cases in the multi-state outcome and also IPCW-weighting them does not have one clear probability interpretation. The maturity rule requires both `label_available_at ≤ C` and a full 125 days after activation, so valid labels that arrive in 7–20 days are discarded and the labeled sample is pushed toward fast activations. Channel and region are offered as exclusion restrictions for a doubly robust counterfactual without saying that both can affect default directly. That sensitivity analysis is not partially identifying under a credible instrument.

Prefix construction still lets the label enter the features. Fixed prefixes of length 5 can contain Rejected, Withdrawn, or Transferred, and those activity names determine the competing outcome. Only Contract Signed, Account Activated, collections, and default events are barred. Short but already-terminal cases are retained and will inflate discrimination. Deduping the same activity within one minute can also delete real document-loop events.

The leakage table itself is the right repair list for `final_limit`, `decision_reason`, `collection_status`, future events, `documents_total`, versioned `risk_score`, extraction-time tenure, and `default_90d`. It conflicts with section 5 on `source_system` (static feature versus drop unless debiased). `remaining_sojourn_proxy` is an unsafe name for a backward-looking timing feature.

Temporal validation recognizes policy change, migration, calibration, and shift, but the regime boundary is case start rather than decision time. Applications started in April–June 2025 and still in credit review on 1 July 2025 are labeled pre-policy while their activation was generated by the new thresholds. Test 1’s parenthetical cutoff “or +35d beyond” 15 February 2026 is impossible: the extract ends that day. Test 2 (starts from 15 October 2025) has almost no mature 90-day labels except very fast activations and fast feeds, so any default metric on that slice is itself a selected sample. “Five months maturity” for a 31 March to 30 September cutoff is also the wrong interval.

The skeleton is concrete and uses the requested PM4Py entry points without inventing a survival API, but it does not finish the required split: train and validation are built, test is only a comment. `get_prefixes_from_log` is unused. Discovery and variants run on full train-case histories, including post-outcome events, while the comment says pre-cutoff prefixes. `documents_total` repair is `pass`. Timestamps are parsed as UTC and then compared with naive `Timestamp` cutoffs, which fails in pandas. Mature `default_90d` is never written onto the case table, so the audit prints flags rather than a mutually exclusive flow.

Section 8 is the cleanest part: factual association, selection, and counterfactual risk stay distinct, and the three deployment feedback loops are real. Those strengths keep this well above a convenience-cohort answer. The undefined window, policy-time misalignment, prefix-terminal leakage, mixed censoring estimator, and code that does not implement its own as-of rules keep it out of the high 8s.
