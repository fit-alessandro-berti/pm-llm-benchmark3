The role model is well grounded in the counts, complexity, similarities, sequence fragments, and substitution episodes. All 14 resources are placed without equating activity volume with authority, Chen and Dina are kept distinct by risk, and Giorgio, Mateo, and Erik are treated as shadow or bridging roles with both benefits and risks. The required sections, table columns, ambiguities, and staffing cautions are present and numerically accurate.

The code section is the main weakness. Study “weeks 3–10” are treated as ISO calendar weeks, so the substitution analysis would not isolate the described episodes. Resource complexity is aggregated on events rather than distinct cases, overweighting long cases relative to the case-based evidence. The 0.75 critical cutoff is invented, and the handover helpers assume a dictionary return that PM4Py does not reliably provide. Sections 1–3 also label Mateo’s and Fatima’s A9 events as preliminary or preparatory more firmly than the log supports; only the week-5 rechecks are evidenced, although section 4 later treats that authority as unresolved.
