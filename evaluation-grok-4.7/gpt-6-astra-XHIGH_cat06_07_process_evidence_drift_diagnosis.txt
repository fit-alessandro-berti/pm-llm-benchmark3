8.6

The audit correctly separates the mechanisms by period and meets every required section: April is treated as a schema and feature-pipeline failure, June as a routing-mix change, August–September as overlapping execution, conditional-risk, and population change, and October as label-maturity confounding. Falsifiers, training gates, and alert dependencies are specific, and the PM4Py sketch uses the requested APIs, keeps rework distinct from verified duplicate deliveries, and separates clipped event-calendar windows from case-start cohorts.

Deductions:

- The September claim that a slightly lower matured V3 breach rate (0.61 versus 0.64) “specifically argues” against within-V3 worsening as the source of the aggregate rise compares matured variant rates with partially labeled aggregate rates. The same answer later says those tables are not safely composable. A within-path reconstruction also does not match the reported aggregates (about 0.31 versus observed 0.28 in August; about 0.34 versus 0.31 in September), which is noted in principle but not used to temper that sentence.
- August’s V3 share jump from 22% to 35% is left unexamined. Partner Z is still absent, and longer `Order Part → Part Received` times do not by themselves reclassify cases into V3, so that mix shift needs its own hypothesis.
- Concept drift is dated as “emerging” in July at moderate-to-high confidence, while July’s aggregate calibration is still tight (mean predicted risk 0.18 versus observed 0.19, ECE 0.042). The V3 rate jump supports an early stratum change; the row confidence is coarser than that evidence.
- April’s small increases in matured V1/V2/V3 breach rates are treated as possible real operational movement. With 18% unmapped variants and wrong diagnosis landmarks, those stratum rates can themselves be selection-biased.
- The extraction sketch can fail before its half-open refilter if `filter_time_range` rejects timezone-aware bounds, does not re-sort by case and time before `shift`, and still emits `extract_temporal_features_dataframe` on month-clipped traces. `grouper_freq="M"` is a fragile frequency alias. Adjacent-edge medians on the calendar frame also drop edges that cross the month boundary; that limitation is stated in prose, but the returned calendar object does not mark those medians as truncated.

These are precision and robustness defects. They do not overturn the period diagnoses, the containment decision not to recalibrate October downward, or the separation of label-delay alarms from retraining.
