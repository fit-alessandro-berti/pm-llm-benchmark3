7.5

The audit judgment is well calibrated: routine Aline/Elena and Boris/Felix pairs are treated as a baseline, Carla/Gita and Dan/Hugo are tied to emergency specialization, and Lena/Kim on V-77x is isolated because four-eyes breaches, ~27× faster bank-change-to-payment, high value, vendor concentration, and manual upload coincide without an emergency channel. Constraints are respected, required sections and indicators are present, benign explanations have support/refute tests, and the report wording (~280 words) does not assert fraud.

Deductions are for concrete errors, not tone.

The identity step in §4.0 is unsafe. `effective_actor` always prefers `entered_by`, then `approved_by`, despite the comment saying it should apply only when the audit trail differs. On a Create PO event, a populated `approved_by` labels the creator as the approver and can manufacture the very self-approval under study. On other events, a clerk in `entered_by` replaces `org:resource`. That “resolved” view is then the basis for re-running four-eyes. The legacy-ERP suspect filter is similarly blunt: if `approved_by` is case-level, it flags ordinary legacy PO creations, not just bot/human collisions.

Other code does not do what the prose claims. Handover results are treated as a weight dict; PM4Py SNA returns a graph. `fast_cases`, `approver`, and `segment_minutes` are never built. `get_service_time` is called with one timestamp column, so service time is degenerate, with no warning. The hand-built DECLARE object is not a conformance model. The loop test diffs `amount_band`, `vendor_id`, `contract_type`, and `purchase_category`, which behave as case attributes here; a null delta would be uninformative, but the code still reads it as an empty loop. Path-performance filters are not flagged as directly-follows measures.

Factual and presentation slips: the R19 row is missing a column, so scale, severity, and rationale are shifted. D1–D4 are cited and never defined. M1 applies the 31% and 42% high-amount shares of the full Dan/Hugo and Carla/Gita subgroups to “these” 42 four-eyes cases. L3 calls Marc/Iris’s 1.8h median “near” the 7.8h bank-change benchmark; it is not, and the paths differ. H1 calls overlapping indicators “independent.” Section 6 says “Management indicates” a legitimate V-77x migration; the pack states that fact directly, and §2.3 quotes it correctly. The claim that the five non-batch justifications are outside the migration is stronger than the 12-of-17 timestamp evidence.
