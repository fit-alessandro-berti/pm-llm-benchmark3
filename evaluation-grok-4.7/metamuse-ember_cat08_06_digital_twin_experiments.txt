7.5

The design is unusually complete: non-stationary arrivals, shared resources, architect bursts, district shocks, inspection bundling, calendars, latent objections, paired comparisons, and an explicit refusal to treat the run as optimal. Those strengths do not cancel the concrete errors below.

Process-mining grounding is weaker than the surrounding prose. `*`, `X`, `+`, and `tau` are standard PM4Py process-tree operators, and `->(+(…), 'Decision drafting')` already encodes the AND-join. Calling that notation informal, and claiming the tree does not encode the join, is incorrect. `'Completeness rework'` is a defined redo label; the real issue is that the log shows a repeated `Completeness check`, not that activity. The skeleton then calls `pm4py.discovery.get_dfg`, `get_performance_dfg`, and `get_variants`. Those are not PM4Py entry points; the simplified API is `discover_dfg` / `discover_performance_dfg` / `get_variants`, and the DFG calls return tuples, so `len(dfg)` and `.items()` do not do what the script claims. `format_dataframe` and the attempt to call `pm4py.parse_process_tree` match the requested conventions.

The simulation reasoning is strong in text and inconsistent in the model mechanics. `Router.route` returns a flat sequence, so Planning, Fire, and Environmental are serial in the only executable specification, while the narrative depends on them running in parallel and joining before drafting. The missing-document branch, which dominates the second-largest variant, is not in that route. D2 drops the “no prior violations” condition. Rejection rate is a veto metric, but neither assumptions nor routing define how `Issue permit` versus `Reject application` is generated. Appeals are cited in validation and absent from the state model. There is a `Calendar` placeholder and no resource-pool placeholder, despite pools, FTE, and queue discipline being load-bearing. Inter-completion gaps are described as an “upper proxy for busy time”; a gap is idle plus service, so it upper-bounds service only under a saturation argument the text does not state.

Assumptions are separated cleanly, and objections stay latent. A few are over-precise or over-certain: calendars are called known even though they were reconstructed from HR; building area is imputed from `application_value`, which couples the fire rule to the D2 value rule; emergency-case truncation is set at “<2%” with no basis; the Monday clerk reserved for portal support is omitted, so the Monday arrival spike has no matching capacity cut.

The experiment matrix does the required job: B0, D1–D10, an extra combination, factor levels, trade-offs, and four stresses applied to the baseline and finalists. D1’s pre-submission delay and D3’s first-in-bundle penalty are treated as open effects. That part is sound.

Uncertainty treatment is serious (WIP initialization, two seasonal cycles, common random numbers, separate streams, aleatory versus epistemic loops, quantile reporting, tornado/Morris/Sobol on finalists). The sample-size expression is garbled. More importantly, unfinished cases are “treated as censored” without an estimator: using the censoring time as duration, or dropping those cases, both pull the long-case p90 down. Kaplan–Meier or another survival quantile is required and not specified. A KS test on tens of thousands of cases will flag trivial CDF gaps; the tolerance bands are doing the real work, and the test is not qualified.

Validation covers the requested modes and correctly keeps replay separate from the arrival model. “Months 15–18 include a peak” is not given. “Fit without D5” collides with intervention D5. Expected bottlenecks are named as environmental and planning queues even though inspection’s median wait matches environmental review at 8 days.

Decision thresholds are labeled provisional and the selection rule avoids an optimality claim. The Simple SLA of 30 days is not tied back to the mixture of the clean path (p90 24 days) and the missing-document path (p90 45 days), so it is unclear whether that gate binds.

The skeleton is modular and does not use `pm4py.read_csv`; it builds the excerpt, formats the three required columns, derives timing proxies with an explicit service/wait caveat, and separates diagnostics from the engine. It would not run against PM4Py as written, and it does not carry the dependency structure the design says is mandatory.
