7.5

The POWL model is a faithful partial-order encoding, and the process-tree under-approximation is real, explicitly chosen, and directionally safe: complete tree traces stay inside the intended language, while A5 correctly shows the lost early enablement of review. Loop shape, exclusive risk routing, optional supplier consultation, release join, and the ban on duplicate labels are consistent across the assumptions, both constructions, and the witness table. That core is strong.

The required process-tree expression is not PM4Py parse syntax. `*->('Execute Trial', 'Inspect Trial'), 'Adjust Parameters')` transposes `*(->(...), 'Adjust Parameters')`, so the redo child falls outside the loop and the surrounding `+(` closes too early. The object-construction code has the right tree; the fenced expression does not match it and would not parse.

F1 is a genuine negative, but the reason is wrong. `Plan Verification` before `Contain Material` breaks the containment sequence (R5), not a cross-branch join. The cross-branch obligation is carried by F2 (`Execute Trial` before `Design Corrective Action`). The requirement is met only by that other row.

The soundness step is internally inconsistent. Mutually exclusive XOR branches are not dead: `Consult Supplier` and the skip are each reachable, and classical soundness allows no dead transitions at all. The parenthetical states the right liveness claim and then the preceding sentence retracts it. The alignment oracle is also too sharp: on F1 an optimal alignment can model-move `Contain Material` before synchronizing `Plan Verification`, so “the first non-synchronous move is PV” is not established. `play_out(..., no_traces=500)` is likewise only a guess at the parameters-dict API, and none of the constructors was run.

Smaller slips: concurrency is not inherited by every successor “therefore,” even with the later qualifier; no witness shows supplier consultation completing while planning or design is still open, so that half of the same late join is untested; A1’s “exactly one” supplier-skip move needs to be read as one skip, not one tau, because loop-exit and XOR routing are also silent.

Section 6 and the POWL edge set do not repeat those mistakes. The preferred model, the inclusion claim for the tree, and the self-critique of late binding versus data-free XOR choices are justified. The score drops for a broken required expression, a mislabeled counterexample, and a confused soundness criterion, not for the partial-order design itself.
