The write-up is complete and often directionally right about spillovers, but several counterfactuals are temporally impossible or control-flow infeasible, and the numerical claims contradict each other.

The largest defect is K-337. After Assessment 2 the case is still high-risk and high-value, so a final Compliance Review by someone other than A1 must still occur before Decision. The intervention stops the second information request; it does not waive compliance or four-eyes. The stated path `Assessment 2 → Decision` skips that review, and the feasibility paragraph only checks the rework rule. The time math is also inconsistent: a move from Wednesday morning to Friday is on the order of 110 hours, not the reported −72 to −96 hours, while the 100-case discussion then claims only 300–500 hours saved in total.

K-104’s best case of −24 hours finishes before Assessment ends plus final compliance, decision, and notification. A reserved C1 slot also cannot honestly be treated as free at Tuesday 20:00 while Tuesday’s compliance backlog is still about 16 hours and other urgent cases were added. Preliminary work of about 2 hours largely offsets the assumed shortening of the final review, yet the scale-up counts 200–300 new C1 hours. The interference table incorrectly says the identity and document queues shrink.

K-219 says D2 starts immediately at Monday 17:30 even though D2’s queue is about 5 hours, and the propagation never reaches A2 or M1. The table’s outcome band (−10 to +4 hours) excludes the same write-up’s +24-hour rework case. K-411 correctly separates earlier readiness from earlier completion, but a Tuesday 15:00 document finish ignores D2’s morning queue, and the synthesis then claims this parallelization does not shift work onto other people after Section 4 says it loads the assessment queue.

Paths omit Notify; K-104 omits the S1 packet the intervention actually sends; ranges, worst cases, and rollout totals do not match. Ranking K-411 and K-219 above the compliance-reservation and rework-suppression policies is reasonable, and the pilot and data recommendations are relevant, but the arithmetic and the illegal K-337 path are too central to treat the answer as reliable.
