5.5

The design covers the required sections and correctly treats objections as latent, applicant delays as external, and results as comparative rather than optimal. Those strengths do not offset several material technical failures.

PM4Py handling is not convention-faithful. `format_dataframe` takes `case_id`, not `case_id_key`. `pm4py.discover_variants` is not a PM4Py function. The given tree already uses PM4Py operators (`->`, `X`, `+`, `*`, `tau`); rewriting them to `SEQUENCE`/`XOR`/`AND`/`LOOP` makes a valid tree unparsable, and the stated syntax limitation is false. Real limits of that tree (no data guards, no appeal/planning/legal rework, no probabilities) are missed. The return type `pm4py.objects.petri.tree` is wrong.

Timing logic is off. Shifting to the next timestamp assigns the following sojourn to the current activity and never produces separate service and waiting columns. The idle/busy split is not identifiable from completion-only stamps. Given medians and p90s are not used via a quantile fit.

The operational model is too thin for the stated process. A single `determine_next_activity` cannot represent the parallel review join. The 14-day public-notice floor is absent, so notices can finish before the legal minimum. Given capacity structure is not encoded: Monday portal clerk, simple-only planners, fire Tuesday–Thursday, environmental 40% non-permit time, and planners shared by review and decision drafting. A 60-day “stabilization” warm-up fights the non-stationary demand the twin is supposed to study; long cases (p90 about 260 days) are still in transient, and seasonal load does not converge to a flat CV.

The experiment and decision layers are internally weak. D3 is contrasted with a no-batching baseline even though batching already occurs. Effect sizes (40%, 50%, 30–50% travel) are single invented points, stresses are not crossed with policies, and paired common-random-number comparisons are absent. Thresholds conflict: Gini ≤ 0.25 versus a “fairness index” ≥ 0.85. An 80% SLA inside 120 days is already failed by the observed complex-variance median of 140 days, with no feasibility check. D2 depends on violation history that is not in the log and has no assumption.
