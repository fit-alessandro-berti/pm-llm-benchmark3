5.5

The activity-sequence partition is sound, and the business readings of X5–X8 are mostly right, but the required formal machinery is not internally reliable. Typed-structural and “semantic” equivalence induce the same partition, while the semantic definition claims to ignore order that the signature actually keeps. The canonical code, table, and X4 qualifier notes do not match. X3 is misdescribed as having the same package–dispatch incidence as X4 and even as having two dispatches. Those errors carry into the borderline and robustness sections, which then merge unrelated executions (including X1 with the split-shipment cases, and the return case with baseline once repeats are collapsed). The PM4Py outline never passes lead type `Order`, misstates `max_objs`, and is not an executable use of the real API.
