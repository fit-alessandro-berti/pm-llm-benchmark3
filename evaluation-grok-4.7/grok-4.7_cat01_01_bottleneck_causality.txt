The answer is rigorous and numerically reliable, but it is not nearly flawless. Several local causal and measurement slips keep it well below a top mark.

The crude-versus-stratified reversal is correctly identified and not over-interpreted. The Kitagawa split checks out: composition at Nova rates is about +9.44 h and the Orion-mix rate component about −2.72 h, summing to the +6.72 h crude gap. Within-stratum gaps (−2.14, −2.21, −3.43 h), the wait shares of those gaps, service gaps, residuals (about 0.8 / 2.2 / 2.3 / 3.6–3.7 h), overlap, and appeal counts are accurate. Total, direct, and queue-mediated estimands are distinguished; queueing is separated from service; completion selection, positivity, interference, and the two-appeal quality outcome are handled with discipline. The three-phase plan correctly refuses the 50% transfer and separates a capacity trial from a small randomized routing pilot. Required sections, columns, hypothesis types, and word bounds are met.

Deductions:

- The within-complexity night-minus-day contrasts (−1.27, −2.45, −4.09 h) are still largely group contrasts. Medium and high day cells are entirely Nova, while the night cells are mostly Orion. Section 4 treats this as the crude night penalty not surviving inside complexity, and hypothesis 2 uses those figures as evidence against a shift effect. The collinearity caveat does not repair that comparison.
- The DAG omits `unobserved_case_risk → queue_wait` (and any direct path into cycle time). That is the main backdoor the prose itself relies on, so the graph understates confounding of the dominant time component.
- The graph note treats the stratified wait gap as support for `resource_group → queue_wait`. That gap is the confounded association under examination, not identification of the arrow.
- “Case mix accounts for roughly 9 hours in the opposite direction” is ambiguous in the decision paragraph. Composition (+9.4 h) is the same direction as the crude Orion penalty; the rate gap is the opposite one. Section 1 has the signs right, but the recommendation can be misread.
- Hypothesis 2’s main test (group coefficient vanishes once queue length and shift are controlled) is also what a beneficial capacity-through-queue effect predicts, so it does not discriminate. The low-complexity Nova load contrast (9.66 vs 9.62 h) mixes night cases into the high-load arm.
- `parse_event_log_string` is called on `>`-delimited traces without a separator. Under the usual comma default, each trace collapses to one activity and the directly-follows comparison is silently empty. Statistical diagnostics in the same script are sound, and no performance timestamps are invented.
- Smaller issues: “only the first is positive” treats an unestimated direct effect as signed; “positivity fails” is slightly absolute when some crossed cells are merely sparse; uncertainties 2 and 5 bundle several fields after asking for one.

No arithmetic failure or forbidden causal leap was found. The mark is held down by the shift contrast, the incomplete DAG, the sign ambiguity, and the process-mining call.
