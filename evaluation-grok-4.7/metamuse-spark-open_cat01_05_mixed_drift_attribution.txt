The July-to-August supplier change is −6 percentage points, not −8, but −8 is used with the +0.8 hour median. Section 9 also compresses several contrasts into unreadable figures (for example “911”, “1224”, “722”). The primary segment is wrongly sized from the fast-variant rate, and the second logging change point is only a small drift in an already elevated missingness rate.
