The write-up matches the required seven-section skeleton and a few broad intuitions are real: A03 is an appointment jump, B02 is a documented preemption, C05 is where priority and deadline agree, QD is skill-constrained, and QC is a bad place to declare a pure priority rule. Those points are not enough. The required decision-set analysis is repeatedly factually wrong, and several diagnoses are the policies those sets falsify.

**Decision tables.** Eligible sets regularly include the job that just ended and omit jobs that were waiting. FIFO is often the later arrival. SPT loses comparisons inside the answer’s own sets.

- QA at 08:20: A01 is finished; A04 (priority 1, `est_min` 7) has been waiting since 08:13 and is missing. FIFO is A02, priority is A04, SPT is A04. The answer predicts A01, A01/A02, and A03. That row hides the cleanest appointment test in the data.
- QA at 08:58: A06, A07, and A08 are also waiting. A04 is the FIFO job, and FIFO, priority, and SPT all select A04. The answer’s FIFO pick, A05, invents a discrimination.
- QA at 09:20: A05 has just finished; A07 and A08 are waiting. Priority selects A08, SPT selects A07, and both FIFO and a late appointment select A06. The answer predicts priority = A06 and SPT = A05.
- QB at 10:00: B04 (`est_min` 8) is in the answer’s eligible set and is shorter than B05 (`est_min` 14). The SPT cell still says B05. B01, with 14 minutes left, is also omitted.
- QC at 10:10: C03 is the only priority-2 job, so priority is C03, not “C02/C03/C04.” The observed job is C02, which is SPT. The deadline cell never names a candidate among C01–C04.
- QC at 10:54 and 11:34: C01 and, at 11:34, the full set are missing. Among the jobs the answer does list, FIFO is the earlier arrival (C05, then C06). The table gives the later job both times.
- QD at 11:35: D04 runs 11:25–11:35 and D08 starts at 11:35. The observed cell says D04. At the real 11:25 choice, D09 is also waiting, FIFO is D04, and both priority and SPT are D08 (`est_min` 8 < 10). The SPT cell says D04.
- QD at 12:20: D07 completed at 12:16 and cannot be done by D-F. D05 is the only eligible job. Even a counterfactual comparison is backwards: D05 arrived earlier and is priority 2, so FIFO and priority both select D05. The answer has FIFO and priority select D07.

**Diagnoses that fight the episodes.** QA is service in arrival order except A03 inserted at 08:20, with A1’s 08:52–08:58 calibration sitting on the gap. That rejects “priority override when no appointment is active”: at 09:05 A05 starts ahead of A08 (priority 1), and at 09:32 A07 starts ahead of A08. A04 waiting while A03 runs is support for an appointment override, but the diagnosis table lists it as the strongest contradiction. A06 after A05 is ordinary FIFO; the 09:15 appointment was already overrun by A05’s known 15-minute estimate.

QB is not non-preemptive priority at confidence 70. B02 preempts B01; B05 does not preempt B03. At 09:48, B05 is already waiting and B03 is chosen anyway. At 10:28, B04 (priority 3) starts ahead of B06 (priority 2). B07’s 10:30 appointment is missed and B07 runs last. The 09:31 resume is the return from the logged “urgent arrival” stop; “dispatcher reassignment” is B01’s later stop at 09:48. Section 3 treats the resume as that reassignment and then declares no violation. Every QB realized duration equals `est_min`, so the priority-versus-SPT contrast at 10:00 is informative once B04 is scored correctly.

QC’s own 11:34 row has priority select C07 and the log select C06, and the diagnosis is still priority. The worse omitted decision is 11:40: C01 starts ahead of C07, whose deadline is still feasible (start then would finish about 11:58; C07 actually finishes 12:34). Early choices C02 then C03 line up with SPT and conflict with priority. Priority and earliest-deadline agree with each other on several later choices and with the log mainly on C05; they are not “identical for most jobs” in the sense that both fit the trace.

QD skills are real constraints, especially single-resource D02, D05, and D07. That is not the same as identifying the discipline. On D-M, D01→D04→D08→D09 is FIFO, and D04 ahead of priority-1, shorter D08 is exactly the comparison skill does not explain. Section 3 says it does. D06 at 11:30 is jointly predicted by FIFO, priority, SPT, and the appointment. D-F is idle from 11:36 until the meeting while D10, priority 1 and 12 minutes, fits before 12:00; confidence 80 does not survive that gap.

**Later sections.** The QB counterfactual misstates the logged reason and uses an eligibility check that the timestamps already settle. The QC counterfactual compares priority with deadline risk, then admits both predict C05, so the extra observable never separates the two stated competitors. Section 5 ranks speculative `est_min` gaming as more plausible than documented availability, even though every queue’s estimates match realized service exactly, and it places A1’s calibration on QD and QC. Section 6 restates a generic field list, without priority or a tie from each field to a live ambiguity such as B01’s remaining time, C05’s visibility at 10:10 under a two-minute scan lag, C07’s slack at 11:40, or D-F’s idle gap. The synthesis is right that a pure QC priority claim is dangerous, and it is the claim the diagnosis table still makes. QA, read from corrected decision sets, is more identifiable than QD’s within-class rule.
