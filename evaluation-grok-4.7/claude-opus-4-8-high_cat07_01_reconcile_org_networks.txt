6.5

The write-up is complete, multi-hypothesis, and generally well calibrated, but it contains concrete metric errors and a few unsupported flow claims. That keeps it well short of a near-flawless mark.

The exception-rate comparison is wrong. Mira’s 0.48 is the share of her own cases with `exception_flag=True`. Omar (0.62) and Priya (0.71) are higher, so “highest of any human” and “the hardest mix” are false. That 0.48 figure is also placed beside “64% of reopens” and “71% of complaints,” which use the opposite denominator (coverage of a subset, not composition of her caseload). Bot_Rules touches almost every case at 0.31, so the base rate is about that high; Mira is enriched, not extreme. Specialists’ higher rates are what a broker would produce by routing hard cases onward, and that point is missed.

The H1 mechanism says she re-integrates work and hands it back to a team lead for approval. The top handover edges do not show Mira→Alice or Mira→Bruno. They show Mira→adjusters/specialists, then Carla→Alice and Diego→Bruno for approval, then team leads→Vendor_Pay. Low working-together with the team leads is what that bypass predicts. Treating it as evidence against a formal desk is backwards, and “Assign Specialist before almost all specialist work” is already strained: Mira→Omar is 210 against 316 medical cases, and Mira→Priya is 180 against 251 legal cases.

Working-together is misstated. Binary co-occurrence still counts a one-event touch. Short events do not make a resource peripheral “by construction.” Pairwise rates are low because her cases are spread across many partners and because the metric is normalized; duration would matter only for an intensity-weighted metric. The scenario’s short-event note is used without that distinction.

Smaller overclaims: Alice also performs Reopen Claim, so Mira does not “own” reopen; the Bot_Rules loop is a manual exception plus a fraud-screen update, not a demonstrated override; 8.4→13.9 days is about 1.65×, not “nearly doubled,” and P90 is not “the slowest.” The absence result is also widened from ordinary low-value claims to ordinary claims generally. An immediate second check on the same path whose absence already moved exception P90 is recommended without the delay tradeoff.

The network definitions, the refusal to treat centrality as good or bad, the absence experiment, vendor/specialist time sinks, no-wrongdoing compliance language, distinct hypotheses with predictions, and the do-not-remove-until-replaced recommendation are all sound. Those strengths are why the score stays in the competent band.
