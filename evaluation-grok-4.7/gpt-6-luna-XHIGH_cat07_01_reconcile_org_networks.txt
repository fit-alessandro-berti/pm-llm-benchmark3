The answer meets every structural constraint, stays multi-hypothesis, and is unusually careful about causality, denominators, lifecycle semantics, and not reading centrality as performance or misconduct. It is not nearly flawless. The handover section asserts Mira → specialist/adjuster → Mira return patterns.
