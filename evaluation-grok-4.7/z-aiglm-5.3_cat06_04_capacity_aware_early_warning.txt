7.5

The final policy is decision-theoretically serious and meets the main quality tests: I-03 is refused with arithmetic despite high \(p\) and value, I-05’s unchanged repeat is suppressed while remaining eligible, I-07 is re-opened because of new evidence and then refused on feasibility and value, and untreated-case harm is priced into both ranking and the scenario table. Calibration is adjusted in the right directions, effects are not equated with observational \(p\), and the monitoring section is concrete.

The score is held down by real errors and rule slippage. I-09 is called the second-best midpoint EV, but I-08’s midpoint net is about €4.5k versus I-09’s €3.6k. I-01 is called the strongest effect case; I-05’s range is higher. Case scores subtract the full false-alarm burden, while the portfolio table charges only an expected burden, so the two layers are not on the same basis. I-02 and I-09 do not clear the stated conservative score against the ~€2.4k slot price, and both flip negative if untreated-case value is €25k, a figure inside the answer’s own sensitivity band; the “expiring option” clause is doing the real selection work. The escalation “action threshold” and the I-04 abort trigger are not numeric, and the 25/50/25 weights are unexplained.
