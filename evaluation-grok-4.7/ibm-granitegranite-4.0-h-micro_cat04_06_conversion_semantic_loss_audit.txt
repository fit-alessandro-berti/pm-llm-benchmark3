3.5

The outline matches the requested nine sections, and the invariant names roughly restate the intended control flow. The substance does not hold up as an audit.

The witness column often names behavior the model must allow as a negative witness. Unordered Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence, and the missing edge between optional legal review and verification, are the clearest cases: “one occurs before the other” is permitted, not forbidden. Invariant 4 invents `verify_defect`, and marks a fully visible precedence as unobservable after hiding tau. The same error is applied to Configure Trial before Inspect Trial. Optionality is not stated as exactly one XOR branch, so the silent skip is treated as if both activities could simply be absent.

The pipelines name the right two routes, then misuse the API. `convert_to_petri_net` is treated as returning a net rather than `(net, im, fm)`. BPMN, process-tree, soundness, footprint, reachability, and Petri-net save calls omit markings. The primary row also folds the process-tree attempt into the Petri-to-BPMN pipeline. The stated reason that pipeline can fail — process trees cannot express concurrency or optionality — is wrong. Process trees have AND, XOR, and LOOP. This partial order is not series-parallel: legal review depends only on Assess Risk, while verification waits for both Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence. Failure or behavioral loss is the expected outcome for that N-shaped causality, and the answer never says so.

The conversion code is not runnable and misses most of the required procedure. Transitions and operators are undefined, markings are never unpacked or null-checked, the tree is never turned into BPMN, `check_is_workflow_net` is absent, soundness is not run on every accepting net, and the saved filenames do not cover the POWL source, the source net, or a process tree. Round-trip unpacking is also missing.

The preservation table uses `validated preserved` although nothing was executed, and several evidence cells describe a different invariant from the row they sit on. Independence is repeatedly called XOR. Initial and final markings are identified with the Register and Archive transitions. Label uniqueness ignores `None` on silent transitions and on taus a converter may insert.

The bounded exploration does not explore. It calls `get_enabled_transitions` without a marking, then passes a transition as a marking, takes only the first successor, and never hides tau, cuts silent cycles, checks exact final-marking equality, compares nets, or states whether the bound is exhaustive. Witness traces and playout are comments.

The visual review notices layout risk, then says the legal XOR makes both branches optional and pairs the wrong activities as the layout example. Rejection criteria omit release-before-verification and an exact final-marking failure, and they describe soundness as temporal logic. The canonical choice is an unmarked Petri net, with the process tree encouraged as a semantic check rather than a fallible derivative. One residual claim is real — trace tests do not establish true concurrency — but the second residual effectively assumes the equivalence it says remains open.
