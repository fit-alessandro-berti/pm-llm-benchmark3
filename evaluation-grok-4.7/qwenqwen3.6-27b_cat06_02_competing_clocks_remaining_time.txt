The write-up has the required six sections, scenario bands, an explicit parallel join, and a qualitative temperature-excursion dependence. The clock arithmetic that the forecast depends on is wrong, and several later figures contradict each other.

Friday 14:00 plus 46–54 h is Sunday 12:00–20:00 (50 h is Sunday 16:00). The ledger and the stage decomposition place technical readiness on Monday 10:00–18:00 and treat weekend on-call readout as irrelevant. The entire readiness window falls outside routine microbiology staffing. Readout is either a Sunday on-call visit (probability about 0.25–0.45, then 1–3 h) or Monday from 08:00. QA is closed Sunday, so a Sunday result still waits until Monday 08:00. On the no-on-call smooth path, the 46–54 h spread does not move the readout: staffing quantizes it to Monday 08:00. The claim that this window shifts the join by 12–24 h describes a Monday-afternoon incubation that the timestamps do not produce.

That shift produces the wrong dominant clock. With microbiology available Monday morning, or already waiting if on-call was authorized, the chemistry backlog is a real competitor for the join. The smooth path instead holds the join until Monday afternoon for microbiology and treats chemistry as finished near Monday 09:00.

The chemistry start is also internally inconsistent. FIFO, given as about 55% of shifts, leaves B-417 behind one urgent sample and three ordinary ones. The ledger still says that under FIFO the batch jumps ordinary work and is in service by about 16:30. A late-Friday start is only a risk-priority possibility. With four samples ahead, Friday’s remaining 2.5 h, and a minimum service of 1.5 h, the majority discipline points to a Monday start after paused and queued work.

The QA load does not follow from the stated rate. One sentence uses “~48 h open hours,” a parenthetical Friday 4.5 + Saturday 4 + Monday ~3 (about 11.5 h), and “~10 new arrivals.” At 0.35 arrivals per open hour, 11.5 h yields about 4 arrivals and 48 h yields about 17. Monday hours before a 15:00 join are about 7, not 3. The five batches already waiting are then added without subtracting Friday-evening and Saturday reviewer throughput, so 8–12 at the join is not a stock-flow result.

The smooth-path hour total is patched. Friday 15:30 to Monday 08:00 as 64.5 h is right, and Monday 15:00 to Tuesday 09:00 as 18 elapsed hours is right, but “5 h Mon + 3 h weekend pause + 10 h Tue morning” calls Monday night a weekend and assigns Tuesday morning 10 h. Clock labels drift by roughly 1.5–6.5 h: Monday 16:00 is about 72.5 h from Friday 15:30, and Wednesday 14:00 is about 118.5 h, against the printed ~71 h and ~112 h.

Section 4 lists a documentation query or deviation investigation as a terminal event alongside release and rejection. Those are intermediate paths; release and rejection are the terminal events, so the 85–90% / 3–5% / 5–10% split does not partition outcomes. The severe tail also stops on Friday, while the same answer’s laboratory investigation of 3–7 calendar days, opened around Tuesday, runs into the following week.

The temperature-flag update is directionally usable, and the overlap warning on scenario probabilities is appropriate. They sit on a baseline that misses the Sunday readiness cliff, the on-call fork, and a coherent queue balance, so the reported Monday-evening to Tuesday-morning center is not a defended decomposition.
