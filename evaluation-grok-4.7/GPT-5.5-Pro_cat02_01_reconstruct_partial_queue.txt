The reconstruction is unusually careful and mostly correct: missing-start feasibility, half-open ties, censored waits versus elapsed service, invariant WIP areas (S1 2085, S2 1340), busy-time ranges (S1 535–605, S2 515–570), resource splits, hourly counts, and the central snapshots all check out, and the three imputation scenarios are internally consistent with those areas. Required case rows, bottleneck timing, and the PM4Py-versus-pandas split are handled properly.

It is not nearly flawless. The Little’s Law table mixes per-hour rates with minute sojourns while still printing the per-minute product, so the displayed identity does not multiply as written. The S2 started-case median is given as about 35–40 minutes, but across the stated start bounds it runs from 35 to about 47.5. “No initial WIP” is easy to misread against the 08:00 snapshot of one just-arrived case. The 11:00–12:00 “low S2 output” reading leans on a bin edge (P08 completes at 12:00). Utilization bands overlap, so they do not identify the bottleneck, but that limit is never stated; the concrete S2 overtaking cases that justify dropping FIFO are only alluded to.
