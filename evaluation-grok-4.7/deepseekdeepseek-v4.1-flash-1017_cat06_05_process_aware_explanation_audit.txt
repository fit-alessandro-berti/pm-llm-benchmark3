8.4

The audit is strong and largely credible. It catches both obvious post-outcome leakage and the subtler lineage defects, keeps the legitimate congestion and prefix-age signals with the correct local signs, and treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as a later-legitimate repeat rather than the same-timestamp `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` ordering failure. Predictive, causal, actionable, and fairness standards are separated, sensitive features are reviewed rather than blindly dropped, and the case write-ups do not smuggle future outcomes back into the narratives.

The remaining issues are precision gaps, not missed leakage. Fraud investigation is labeled “not enabled,” and document request is grouped with trigger-gated activities, although after coverage those activities are enabled or future-possible while appeal and closure are not. Section 2 states that taxonomy but does not apply it activity by activity. `calendar_week` is a known-at-`t0` drift proxy, yet it is listed among the reasons Case Z is temporally invalid. “Recompute after excluding fields” is also softer than the training-time leakage warrants: dropping SHAP terms does not repair a model fit on future information, even though Section 5 later says retrain. Elapsed time is slightly conflated with queue, and complexity is filed in two classes.
