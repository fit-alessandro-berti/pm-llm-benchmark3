The required structure is present, and the high-level reading is partly right: S2 has longer service, C2’s 10:55–11:10 outage is real, both stations run hot, and open cases are kept. The supporting numbers and the code do not hold up.

The aggregate row for the 11 fully observed cases is wrong. Their S1 waits run from 5 to 95 minutes (mean about 48), not a mean of 20 on [0, 40]. Their S2 waits run from 5 to 65 (mean about 32), not 15 on [0, 30]. P08, P12, P13, and P14 are inside that aggregate and already falsify it. Section 4 then says late S1 waits are 95–110, so the document contradicts itself.

S1 arrival counts in the station table are 7, 4, and 1. That is 12 cases. The log has 22: 08:00–10:00 contains P01–P13 (13), and 10:00–12:00 contains P14–P21 (8). S1 WIP peaks of 5 and 6 are low; the queue reaches about 7–8. S2 completions in 10:00–12:00 omit P04 (4 instead of 5), so the “13 completions” total is short. C2 “120 busy minutes” in 08:00–10:00 counts work outside the window, including P06 at 10:10–10:50. Right-censored waits for P16 and P22 are closed intervals around 85 and 110; those are lower bounds, not two-sided estimates.

S2 as the tighter exit constraint is defensible (about 38–40 minutes versus about 28, two servers). The claim that the S1 queue is only a symptom of S2 is not. Pre-service S1 delay is fixed by S1 arrivals versus S1 completions; morning arrivals are about 5 per hour against S1 capacity near 4.3 per hour, so S1 is independently overloaded.

Scenario arithmetic does not match the starts given in the code: 09:30 minus 08:45 is 45 minutes, not an optimistic P07 wait of 35. Central figures also disagree across the case table, the scenario text, and the code. Little’s Law is aimed at two different windows, and the code multiplies an hourly rate by a wait in minutes.

The Python block does not run: `Series.notin` is not pandas. `MISSING`, `OPEN`, and blank are all parsed to the same `NaT`. The interval busy-time print repeats one full-log total, 14:00 WIP is hardcoded, and the PM4Py columns it prints are unlikely to exist under those names.
