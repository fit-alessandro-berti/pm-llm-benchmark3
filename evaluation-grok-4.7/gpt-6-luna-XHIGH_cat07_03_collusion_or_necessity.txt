7.2

The answer is careful, well structured, and generally faithful to the evidence. It avoids accusing anyone, separates routine co-working from real control exceptions, and treats emergency handling and data-quality problems as live explanations. It is not nearly flawless: a few evidential compressions and technical inaccuracies are material in an answer whose value is calibration.

The strongest defect is population stacking. The 9 bank/payment four-eyes cases are same-resource violations; the 44-case Lena/Kim attributes (72% V-77x, 68% high amount, 83% manual upload) describe a co-occurrence subgroup; the 17-minute figure is the median of a 16-case fast handover subgroup. Section 3 labels the 9 as “Lena→Kim … four-eyes cases” and then cites those other statistics as if they described the same set. The arrow notation used elsewhere means an ordered pair of different resources, which is the compliant handover pattern, not a same-person violation. The opening caveat and the more careful wording in section 6 reduce the damage but do not remove it. A reviewer following section 3 could sample the wrong cases.

The fast-approval row has a similar compression. Carla/Gita at “8–11 minutes” and Dan/Hugo at “10–14 minutes” blend the Create PO → Approve PO fast medians (8 and 10 minutes) with different path medians (Emergency Override → Approve PO at 11 and 14 minutes). Those are not one range. The 17-minute Lena/Kim figure is stated correctly.

Section 4 is directionally right, especially the warning that a mined Declare or log-skeleton model is frequent behavior rather than the three-working-day rule, and the insistence on canonical actors, full traces, and denominators. The executable sketch is not reliable. `attribute=` and `keep_same=` are not the stable `filter_four_eyes_principle` interface (`parameters` with a resource key and a positive/negative flag); `discover_subcontracting_network(..., n=2)` treats the scenario’s “n=2” definition as a keyword. The version hedge does not make those calls correct. The retrospective pseudocode also accesses `override.timestamp` / `override.audit_actor` from `itertuples()`, which will not expose `time:timestamp` or `audit:actor` under those names. Emergency-versus-non-emergency comparison and path-performance timing are specified in prose more than in the required code, and duration units are left implicit.

Smaller gaps: the 31 same-person Vendor Check + bank-change cases never appear in the risk table despite being larger than the 9-case cluster; “remaining V-77x cases” has no defined universe and overlaps the 16 fast cases; late retrospective justification at 18% and 21% in the emergency cohorts is only a stratification factor, not its own review slice, even though it is an explicit breach of the three-working-day rule and far above the routine 1% rate. Severity labels are otherwise differentiated and defensible, section 6 is within 200–300 words, and the increase/reduce-concern lists are concrete.
