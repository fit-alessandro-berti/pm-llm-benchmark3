8.5

The interval arithmetic, time-zone and DST-fold handling, joint duration bounds, and SLA/profile labels are almost entirely correct, including the 1.5 s and 3 s boundary cases. The score is held down for the issues below.

Section 1’s mobile summary, “±330 seconds,” describes only rounding plus clock offset. The primary T-406 model actually used in Sections 2–4 is asymmetric, about −630 s to +330 s around 12:14Z, after the extra [0, 300] s occurrence delay. The case note repairs this, but the general rule does not.

The findings table treats acceptance as closed [0, L]. That adds a non-negativity filter the literal “no later than” rules do not state. It is disclosed, and it does not flip the reported baseline labels, but the table presents it as the rule.

“Approximately 24 h 8 min 50 s” for T-401 is the midpoint of [86918.5, 86941.5] s. The feasible window is 24 h 8 m 38.5 s through 24 h 9 m 1.5 s. The exact interval is present, so this is loose rather than decisive.

Severity marks every missing close INDETERMINATE as High, the same band as T-402 S1 and T-408 S2/S3. The definition allows that, but it flattens assurance gaps and certain breaches.

The code’s “integer nanoseconds avoid floating-point errors” claim is overstated: `inf` is a float, durations are divided by `1e9`, and `59.999` is not decimal-exact. Error is far below the classification margins. The midpoint “consistency” checks cover order and two special constraints, not full joint feasibility of every projected center. PM4Py’s `zeta` keyword and deviation layout are version-sensitive; that part is at least isolated and labeled.

No material mis-classification showed up under independent checking of the UTC intervals, duration unions, tolerance matrix, or zeta matrix. T-405’s single surviving fold triple, the T-407 [0, 1023] versus naïve [−3, 1023] distinction, the T-408 zeta=2 straddle at 3300 s, and the T-409 ordinary-NTP counterfactual [118.5, 391.499] are all right. Midpoints are kept secondary and explicitly lossy.
