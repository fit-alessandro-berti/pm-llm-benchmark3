The final assessment is strong on the central pattern and on numerical auditability. The crude Orion penalty reverses inside every complexity stratum, queue time is separated from review service, positivity failure and completed-case selection are explicit, and stratification is not treated as identification. The `mainly confounded` verdict, the 65 confidence, the refusal of a blanket 50% move, and the day-stratum pilot plus a compliance/queue intervention all fit the evidence. The standardized contrast, overlap logic, and most stratum arithmetic check out.

The score stays well below excellent because several causal claims are wrong or unclear. The within-complexity night-versus-day comparison pools groups, so medium night is almost all Orion and medium day is all Nova; that comparison cannot contradict a shift effect, yet it is used as contradicting evidence. `routing_rule` is called instrument-like even though it is the confounded assignment mechanism. `compliance_required` is folded into a front-door-style test although it is a pre-assignment case attribute, not a mediator that intercepts the group effect. Conditioning on shift is described as blocking part of the Orion mechanism, which mixes a confounder with a post-treatment path. `rework -> queue_wait` conflicts with the stated meaning of `wait_before_review_h`, which covers pre-review and compliance waiting, not the Request Info loop. Total, direct, and mediated estimands are named but not defined operationally. The beneficial-effect prediction is missing its comparison operator (“Orion Nova”).
