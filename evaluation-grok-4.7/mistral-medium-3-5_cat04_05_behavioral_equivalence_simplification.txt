3.5

The headline verdicts happen to be right, but the supporting semantics, counterexamples, code, and simplification are not reliable.

The three view verdicts are the strongest part: P1 and P3 are not equivalent, and P2 is equivalent only after the stated abstraction. Model A is also the right compliance model, and the bounded-versus-proof warning is directionally right. Almost everything used to justify those claims is wrong or internally inconsistent.

Model B contains exactly one `Check Revision`. The parallel child is a single leaf, so the maximum is 1, not unbounded. Section 2 says unbounded; the Section 4 note says one check for the whole loop. Model A is not sequential inside the loop: `+` lets `Check Revision` precede or follow `Analyze Revision` in the same iteration. Section 2 misses that concurrency, and several later claims depend on the miss.

The counterexample table does not establish either language difference. Its membership cells are empty, and no trace is shown to be in A but not B. T1–T3 are B-only traces, but the written reasons say the opposite: T1 is legal in B precisely because the single check can sit between two analyses, and T3 is legal in B with the check delayed until the end. T4 is in neither model, because both loops require `Rework Case` between two analyses. `Approve Revision` is visible. P1 keeps its placement relevant and P3 retains it; treating it as irrelevant is incorrect. A trace such as `Open Case, Analyze Revision, Approve Revision, Check Revision, Close Case` separates B from A, and any two-check trace separates A from B. Neither appears.

The equivalence definitions drift. P3 still compares sequences of at most 12 visible events and at most three analyses; it does not collapse duplicates to label identity. P1 does not require each check to follow its analysis immediately. The inequality signs in the P3 bound are also missing.

The bounded code would not answer P3. The Model A string is not the given tree: the inner sequence is gone, `Rework Case` is mis-nested, and `'Close Case'` falls outside the call. Firing is not preset/postset execution, markings are visited without the visible history, and that visit test blocks later iterations. Markings are also likely unhashable. The search is therefore not exhaustive, despite the claim that it is.

The leaf counter never counts a `ProcessTree` leaf, duplicate-label count is omitted, and the “~10 / ~9 nodes, 4 visible leaves” figures are invented. Both trees have 12 nodes, 6 visible leaves, 1 silent leaf, and no duplicate labels.

The proposed simplification does not preserve P2. The expression uses `+`, while the prose calls it a choice. As written, it forces exactly one analysis, exactly one check, and a mandatory rework, so analysis counts and optional rework are lost. The three regression traces are not all accepted by that tree under either a parallel or a loop reading, and the negative traces are misclassified. Recommending it as the dashboard replacement is unjustified.
