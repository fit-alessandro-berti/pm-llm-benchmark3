The write-up keeps the required section structure, separates a decision-policy change from a pure timing cut, and correctly treats K-337 as high-risk and K-104’s C1 reservation as non-scalable. Those conclusions are not enough to carry the timelines. The counterfactual clocks repeatedly contradict the snapshots, the case table, and each other.

K-219 is the clearest break. The factual decision is already Wednesday 08:50. The table then claims a 4–12 hour cycle-time cut from a counterfactual decision “Wednesday morning instead of later,” which does not move the endpoint. The derivation’s best case saves about 16 hours only by deciding Tuesday evening, while its base case is roughly 0–4 hours. The same case assumes D2 works through Monday night and finishes around Tuesday 05:30, but Tuesday 08:00 still shows a 4-hour D2 queue, so that overnight clearing is not compatible with holding the rest of the system fixed.

K-411 does not implement its own intervention. Identity is set at 11:35–13:35 and Document Check at 13:35, so they do not overlap, yet the text calls that concurrency. The best case also starts assessment at Wednesday 08:00 despite a 5-hour A2 backlog at that snapshot. K-104 has the same maneuver: C1 is started at Wednesday 08:00 even though that snapshot shows an 8-hour compliance queue. That is preemption, not a single-case change with other behavior held fixed, and the stated saving drifts among 4–12, 6–8, 5.5, and 7.5 hours.

K-337 is conceptually right to drop the second information request, keep a post-assessment C1 review, and allow the decision to become conditional or a rejection. “3–5 business days” still overstates the move from Wednesday morning to Friday evening or Monday. The 100-case section misses an explicit weekend and external-response discussion, and “30–40 hours” of D1 relief for 100 document checks is far too small. The pilot assignment is both within-week randomization and alternating weeks. The assumption audit never states the overnight-work and manager-availability assumptions that drive the extreme savings.
