The answer covers every required section, chooses a sensible `conditional go`, and correctly refuses to treat Site A’s nine-hour median drop as an identified effect or to start document checking before originals. Those are the right structural conclusions. It is not nearly flawless: the causal graph, the numeric scenarios, and the executable model each contain hard errors.

The transport graph points `originals_required`, `digital_documents`, and `role_specialization` into `treatment_parallelization`, so constraints look like causes of the treatment rather than modifiers of whether parallelization produces overlap. `appeal -> cycle_time` is reversed: a 90-day appeal cannot generate the 44-hour cycle time. `treatment_parallelization -> rework` is missing. The prose then describes mechanisms the edges do not encode, including originals blocking document start and role specialization adding handoff delay. “A identical” is also ungrammatical.

The scenario table contradicts its own reasoning. The text caps aggregate median gain at about 2–5 hours because a median 18-hour originals delay blocks 62% of cases, but the optimistic row assigns −5 to −8 hours to 100% of volume. Digital-only treatment of the shorter 38% is also given a −5 to −10 hour p90 improvement, although Site B’s 110-hour p90 is driven by originals, legal review, and complexity, not by the non-original cases. Overall medians are treated as share-weighted subgroup effects. The pilot’s SD of about 12 hours cannot sit next to a median of 44 and a p90 of 110, so the claim that 300 cases per arm detects a 4-hour median shift is unsupported. Copying daily huddles into the candidate intervention also reintroduces one of Site A’s confounders.

The POWL model is the largest fidelity failure. A strict partial order executes every node, and the code places both `Approve` and `Reject` on every path. Legal review is mandatory rather than optional, the supplement redo is only `Request Supplement` and does not repeat document checking and assessment, and the non-original branch from section 6 is absent. Unquoted multi-word labels are also unlikely to survive `parse_process_tree`. The comments note missing capacity and locks, but not that the net decides both ways.

The comparison table, confounder inventory, segmented eligibility rules, and mechanism-versus-implementation pilot logic are the parts that hold.
