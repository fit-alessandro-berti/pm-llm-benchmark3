7.5

The audit is structurally complete and gets the central distinctions right: end-of-case fields and full-trace aggregates are leakage, `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is future but legal, `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is impossible and same-timestamp-ambiguous, and queue length, elapsed time, and frozen complexity stay as non-causal predictors. Region, channel, and calendar week are kept as proxies rather than deleted or treated as causes. All five required sections, the case JSON, and the eight validation topics are present.

Those strengths do not make the write-up nearly flawless.

The local revisions are less reliable than the global audit. Case X’s narrative names the appeal and the partial decision, which repeats leaked future outcomes inside the revised story. Its removed-driver list adds `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_handler_team`, and `total_case_duration_hours`, none of which appear in that case’s local attribution; the listed contributions plus the base/other bundle already sum to 0.82. Case Y likewise drops `appeal_seen_anywhere`, which is not a listed local driver. The bundled “base value and other features” term is never separated, so an unaudited residual remains in both explanations.

Several factual or methodological slips are clear. `elapsed_since_registration_at_t0` is called the only time-based prefix feature even though `calendar_week` is known at `t0`. The queue remediation tells the reader to avoid a nightly snapshot, but that defect belongs to `same_day_document_count_snapshot`; queue length is a timestamped snapshot with a ±5 minute tolerance. The prefix-truncation check is said to catch `risk_score_current`, which is a mutable external score and will not change merely because the event log is cut. `initial_reserve` is graded Low and described as an early static signal although no freeze time or lineage is given. “Queue length of 4 is below typical congested levels” asserts a baseline the explanation does not contain. Replacing `full_trace_variant_id` with a prefix variant also misses that, at this fixed `t0`, a conforming prefix is essentially `REG-COV` and will carry almost no variation.

Severity and class boundaries are softer than the analysis. Complete-trace `num_document_requests_total` is High while the same defect class is Critical for the variant id and the anywhere-appeal flag. Queue length, prior-claim count, and the document snapshot sit in more than one interpretation class, so “unusable until repaired,” “defensible after repair,” and “never repairable” are easy to mix. Section 5 treats a before/after drop in AUROC, PR, and log-loss as a measure of probability inflation; those metrics can move either way, and only the calibration check actually speaks to inflation. The conformance suite states the right appeal and terminal-close rules, but it does not make evidence-cycle order, legal document rework, withdrawal, or parallel fraud explicit enough to be the concrete normative checker it describes.
