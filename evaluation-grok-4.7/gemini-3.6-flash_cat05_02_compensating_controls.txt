The case judgments are the strongest part of the answer, but the write-up is not reliable enough to score higher. Several required checks are missing, a few unresolved facts are treated as established, and the sufficiency test contradicts the classifications.

The nine labels are mostly defensible and not a mechanical four-eyes mapping. AP-203 as met, AP-206 as only partial because the token is logged after preparation, AP-205 as stopped before completion, AP-202 and AP-208 as failed packages, and AP-209 as indeterminate are the right kinds of distinctions. AP-204 is rightly failed on the fabricated receipt and the weak second person, and AP-201 is rightly kept tolerable rather than equated with AP-202. That is real control reasoning.

Those judgments are undercut by overclaim and inconsistency. Whether m09’s credential was shared is explicitly unresolved, yet the answer calls it theft and stolen credentials. AP-204’s pair “deny collusion,” and a dedicated cell is still possible, but the rationale states pair collusion as the cause while assigning high confidence. AP-208 was a legitimate payment with failed contemporaneous evidence; the table reads like an unmitigated loss parallel to AP-202. AP-205 was never a completed payment, so nominal FEP-1 does not make it “nonconforming.” Pre-settlement `Manager_Ratify` is asserted to halt clearing, but every such case still settled, and ratify is not shown to be a gate distinct from `Manager_Approve`. The corrective-capacity column repeatedly describes prevention that worked.

Section 2 does not survive its own cases. Post-release, pre-settlement ratification is defined as only partially equivalent, then AP-207 is `OBJECTIVE_MET`. “Fully equivalent” demands zero added exposure plus proof of delivery, which is stricter than FEP-1 and is never actually applied. AV-2 is said not to check bank-account ownership, which contradicts the control definition and the later claim that the AP-208 outage left ownership unproven. “Exploit probability approaches 1.0” is an invented certainty inside an argument against crude loss math.

Section 6 omits two requested monitors: same-user rate and audit-before-recall-window. The other rules are real alerts, and the alert-versus-proof distinction is stated, but the numeric cutoffs are arbitrary and AP-202 is used as conclusive misconduct despite the open credential question.

The code block covers the requested skeleton, yet it is not sound evidence. Mainstream PM4Py `filter_four_eyes_principle` keeps cases that respect four eyes; the note asserts the opposite for 2.x and 3.x, so a successful call would invert the violation set. The fallback polarity is right, but it runs only on an exception. AP-208’s token time and several tenure figures are invented, so `Token_Pre_Prep` can fire without a logged token time. Unknown settlement becomes a false comparison with `NaT`, and the repeated-pair flag required in the output is never computed. The closing disclaimer is correct and does not repair those defects.
