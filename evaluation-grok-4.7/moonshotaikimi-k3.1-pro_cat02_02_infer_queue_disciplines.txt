The final diagnosis is rigorous, decision-set based, and internally consistent. It correctly treats QA as ordinary FIFO with only a soft appointment override, rejects a queue-wide priority reading, and flags A04, A06, and A07 as observationally equivalent. Arithmetic that matters checks out: segment sums match every `est_min`, B01’s remaining work is 31 then 14, and the appointment and calendar timings are right. Assumptions are labeled, scan-delay bounds are used only at the margin, and realized-duration noise is not misused to rewrite visible-estimate SPT.
