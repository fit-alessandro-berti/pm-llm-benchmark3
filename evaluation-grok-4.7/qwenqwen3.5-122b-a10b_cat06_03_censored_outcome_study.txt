4.5

The primary target is a reasonable one: 90-day default only after activation, with rejected, withdrawn, and open cases left unlabeled, plus a competing-risk alternative. The leakage table also drops the worst post-decision fields (`final_limit`, `decision_reason`, `collection_status`). Those choices do not carry the rest of the design.

The cohort table does not account for 48,000 applications. The four rows sum to 42,900. The 17,700 “competing events” cell equals rejected + withdrawn + transferred only, while the text also folds in the 5,100 still-open cases. Open cases are then described as ordinary right censoring in the censoring section and as competing events in the table. Still-open applications have no terminal state yet; rejected and withdrawn applications were never exposed to post-activation default. Those are different mechanisms, and the flow never separates labeled, administratively censored, delayed-feed, competing, and selectively unobserved cases. Transfer linkage (about 60%) and permanently missing partner feeds never appear as counts or dispositions.

Prediction time, population, and decision use do not describe one estimand. Scoring is set at the end of credit review, before Accept/Reject, but training keeps only cases that later activate, and the decision boundary says the score is for loans already approved. Conditioning on a post-prefix activation event makes the fitted probability unavailable for an application still at review. That selection problem is named, then left out of both the primary analysis and the sensitivity analysis.

The primary analysis is complete-case binary classification on the 21,600 matured activated accounts. Maturity is defined as activation plus 90 days before 15 February 2026, ignoring `label_available_at` and the extra 7–35 day feed lag. The proposed inverse-probability weighting is aimed at the 3,600 censored activated cases and would not recover their labels; it also does not probe activation selection, transfer loss, or permanently missing feeds. Administrative censoring given activation time is largely deterministic from the calendar. Missing partner feeds and transfer outcomes are not.

The validation window is not a usable backtest. Training runs to 30 June 2025, validation is starts from 1 July through 31 December 2025, and the test set is starts from 1 January through 15 February 2026, called “latest matured data.” An account activated on 1 January 2026 cannot finish a 90-day horizon before the 15 February 2026 extract, even with no feed lag. The same section then says late-2025 starts whose horizon passes the extract should be excluded, which empties that test window. There is no label embargo, no migration-period check after 15 October 2025, and no subgroup assessment. Calibration metrics and the objection to a random split are sound; they do not repair the split.

Prefix rules conflict: first 5 events, first 14 days, and all pre-decision events capped at 10. Migration duplicates are not handled. Cohort and calendar features are absent despite the policy break. Resource target encoding on default rates is proposed and then omitted from the leakage audit.

The skeleton does not implement the written study and would not run as specified. `format_dataframe` is called with `case_id_col`, `activity_col`, and `timestamp_col` rather than the PM4Py arguments `case_id`, `activity_key`, and `timestamp_key`. The split is on event time, the test frame is unused, and `MIGRATION_DATE` is unused. `filter_traces` is not used to truncate traces at the decision; dropping only an `Account Accepted` event would still leave later contract, activation, collection, and default events. `get_prefixes_from_log` is computed and then ignored. Feature rows are built from `prefix_log._cases`. Maturity ignores the feed delay, `drop_duplicates` can duplicate a case whose `default_90d` is missing on some events and filled on others, and the leakage print only checks a hand-built frame that never had the forbidden columns. Cohort totals, as-of censoring classes, and a case-start temporal split are not produced.
