The audit is structurally complete and mostly right: every top-12 feature, sequence pattern, and local-only field is classified; appeal-then-decision is kept distinct from the impossible close-before-assessment pattern; queue length and prefix age are preserved, including opposing signs; region and channel are kept as proxies rather than causes; Case Z is invalidated; and the JSON matches the required schema without restating leaked outcomes.

It is not nearly flawless. `total_case_duration_hours` is treated as ordinary post-closure leakage, not as near-label leakage of a 10-day breach. The prefix check is written as equality with `t0` rather than “at or before `t0`.” Directions in the revised explanations are taken from SHAP values computed with leaked features, without saying those signs can move after removal. `initial_reserve` is used as a credible driver although its freeze time is not established, and €25,000 is called moderate-high without support. `prior_claim_count_before_t0` is only medium despite a window that reaches the extraction date. The team’s causal lines (appeals cause breaches, the final team determines delay, region drives delay) are removed more than dismantled, the unexplained residual mass is ignored, and most of the published ranking is never summed up as inadmissible.
