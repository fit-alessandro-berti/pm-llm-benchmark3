7.5

The audit correctly separates the period instead of forcing one drift label. April is treated as a schema and feature-pipeline failure on mature labels; June as a real V2 mix shift with stable within-route breach rates; July as the supplier-timing break; August–September as overlapping V3 performance, conditional risk, and, from September, Partner Z composition; October as selectively immature. Training gates, falsifiers, alert ordering, and the refusal to read October’s 0.16 breach rate as comparable are all sound. The six required tests are present, and each has competing expected results.

The deductions are for concrete inaccuracies, not for the overall diagnosis.

The June standardization is useful, but two figures are off. A constant prediction at the base rate has Brier score \(p(1-p)\): 0.1476 at 0.18, not 0.144. June’s Brier of 0.139 is worse than the 0.15 climatology of about 0.1275, so Brier skill changes sign; calling 0.130→0.139 merely “not a pure discrimination measure” understates that. “Roughly 25.5%” at August’s mix with July’s V3 rate is about 24.3% using July’s own variant rates, or 25.0% if August’s V1/V2 rates are held fixed. June’s fully mature mix check is also grouped with August–September as “not a mature reconstruction.”

The process sketch names the requested functions and the right cohorts, but it would not produce the audit it describes. `format_dataframe` takes `activity_key` and `timestamp_key` and renames those columns to `concept:name` and `time:timestamp`; the call uses `activity` and `timestamp`, and later code mixes `case_id`/`activity` with `concept:name`. `filter_time_range` is `dt1, dt2` with modes `events`, `traces_contained`, and `traces_intersecting`, not `start`/`end` and `mode="trim"`. `discover_dfg` and `discover_performance_dfg` return `(graph, start_activities, end_activities)`; `len` on that tuple is 3, and `.items()` does not walk edges. Performance medians are in seconds, so dividing by \(3.6\times10^{12}\) is a nanosecond conversion and is off by \(10^9\). Duplicates are dropped before the monthly count, and retained rows have `_duplicate_removed` false, so `duplicate_event_share` is identically 0. Calendar trimming also drops cross-month `Order Part → Part Received` pairs instead of recording them as censored.

Those defects sit in a required artifact and would mis-state duplicate rate, edge counts, and waiting times. The narrative diagnosis itself is strong enough that the answer remains clearly above a merely adequate audit.
