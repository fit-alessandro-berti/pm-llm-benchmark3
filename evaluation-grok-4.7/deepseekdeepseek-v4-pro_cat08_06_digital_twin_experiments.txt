6.5

The design covers every required section and correctly treats non-stationary arrivals, calendars, emergent waiting, unobserved objections, and non-optimality. Those strengths do not offset several concrete design and API errors.

The process tree is misread. Its `*` loops cover only completeness and inspection. Planning-review, legal-check, and appeal rework are not in the tree, yet the routing assumptions say those loops sit where the tree indicates. Appeal is never given an activity, resource, or re-entry rule. Site-inspection no-shows, which the timing summary flags, are absent. The parallel block’s AND-join (which branches apply, and what decision drafting waits for) is not a state variable.

Baseline inspection batching is built into the core model, while D3 is the batching intervention. That confounds the comparison. D2’s “no prior violations (from log)” invents a field the log does not have. Service times are one stationary distribution per activity, despite variant medians from 12 to 140 days; case type should drive service time. Public notice has a 14-day floor, but the generic lognormal fit can draw below that floor. Travel times (30/45 minutes), a 15% batch saving, a 60% document-loop reduction, and a 5% rework increase are stated as operating facts rather than uncalibrated scenario settings. The urgent-case exception on council weeks is dropped, and council-week frequency is not a sensitivity factor.

A 6-month warm-up is shorter than the complex-variance tail (p90 about 260 days), so long cases started in warm-up still dominate the measurement window. The uncertainty plan runs 100 replications and, separately, a 50×10 Bayesian loop, without saying which interval the decision rule uses.

PM4Py usage is only partly faithful. `format_dataframe` and `parse_process_tree` match the requested conventions, and the log is built as a DataFrame. `dfg_discovery.apply` returns a DFG, not `(dfg, start_activities, end_activities)`. `variant="performance"` is the wrong type in current PM4Py (it expects the performance variant enum). `pm4py.stats.get_arrival_distribution` and `pm4py.algo.filtering.log.rework` are not real entry points. Quantile matching is mislabeled “method of moments.” The excerpt DataFrame contains one event, and the skeleton never builds service or waiting columns. Routing and calendars are comments inside stubs, not placeholders. Conformance of the simplified tree (fitness/precision) is missing before simulation, so control-flow error would be absorbed into duration error.
