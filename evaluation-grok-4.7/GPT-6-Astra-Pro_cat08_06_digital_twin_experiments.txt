The design is unusually strong: it keeps a shared-resource, calendar-aware DES with cross-case shocks, separates service from waiting, treats objections as latent, and refuses to call an unvalidated policy optimal. The capacity bound, Saturday calendar clashes, CRN keying, censored tails, and observation-equivalent replay log are all sound. Remaining issues are real but secondary.

The planner-hour bound is valid only as a conditional steady-state impossibility argument, yet FTE is turned into headcount more firmly for planners and clerks than for fire review. Baseline queue discipline is never fixed, and Monday peaks are not identified against architect bursts, so those two mechanisms can be double-counted. D6’s age threshold refers to a target duration the source never gives. Quantile intervals are described as paired Monte Carlo intervals without a procedure that is valid for censored, dependent, heavy-tailed p90s.
