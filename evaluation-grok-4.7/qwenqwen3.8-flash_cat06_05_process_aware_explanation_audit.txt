The audit is strong and meets the structural requirements: every global feature, sequence pattern, and local-only feature is covered; legitimate congestion and prefix-age signals are kept; the appeal edge is treated as future-but-legitimate while the same-timestamp closure edge is treated as ambiguous and possibly impossible; and prediction, causation, actionability, and fairness are separated. It is not nearly flawless. `prior_claim_count_before_t0` and `same_day_document_count_snapshot` are systematic temporal defects but are rated only high, while similar defects are critical. Section 3 puts the same features in both “defensible after repair” and “unusable until repaired,” and it places irreparable complete-trace fields in a class that implies repair. The Case Z note stresses an ordering artifact without stating that a confirmed reverse order would still be normatively impossible and that both events are post-`t0` anyway. `calendar_week` is correctly called non-leakage, then loosely described as encoding future conditions and used as a reason to invalidate Z. “Negative contribution cannot be observed” is imprecise, and `total_case_duration_hours` is overstated as something that may directly determine the settlement-breach label rather than post-closure duration entangled with it.
