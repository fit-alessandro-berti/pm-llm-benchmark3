The write-up has the required eight-part structure, cites norm IDs and counts, keeps the four explicitly protected relations (Validate Order → Issue Invoice, Manual Compliance Review–Customer, Package–Delivery codeath, Reopen Order → Close Order) out of the violation set, and correctly treats N3 (2 cancelled orders later dispatched) as the clearest forbidden behavioral breach. That is not enough to carry the reasoning the task is testing.

The central norm misread is E8. The exception covers only events explicitly typed as unapplied cash, and the snapshot says none are so typed. The one Record Payment tied to a Payment but not an Invoice is therefore an unmet mandatory binding, or at best insufficient evidence, until it is typed. Sections 2–4 call it a high-confidence conforming typical exception (“E8 explicitly permits this”). Sections 6–7 then recommend tagging it so it will not be flagged. Those cannot both be right.

Two different gaps are fused. N4 is 198/200 externally shipped packages with Pack Package → Dispatch Package. The carrier-reference note applies to two Dispatch Package events that lack a Delivery object (E6), not to the missing directly-follows edges. Rank 5 and the OC-DFG discussion attach the carrier evidence to N4. The same pairs are then double-listed: Dispatch–Delivery 198/200 and Add Item–Order 1448 vs Add Item–Item 1450 are both “findings” and later “benign mandatory” conformance. E2 (every Add Item relates to an Order) is a real mandatory miss of two events, not a benign edge with a minor tracking footnote.

Closed-world scope is mishandled. The norm is closed for forbidden relations and for mandatory columns inside scope. Sections 3 and 8 say open-world reasoning applies to mandatory gaps such as N1 and N4, and section 8 calls E10 a definitive violation that does not depend on data quality while also calling that same edge a likely join artifact. T6 is given data-quality alternative “None” in the table and then treated as undecidable. The 14 invoices with no successor are high-confidence “open credit” without evidence they are open-credit terms rather than unpaid invoices; N5 makes that absence non-forbidden, not proven legitimate.

The plan does not contain the table’s own top two (N3 and T6); immediate containment is N3 and the suspected sensor join. The code sketch names the required PM4Py calls and keeps a semantic layer apart from raw diagnostics, but it discovers the “norm” from the same observed log and then conforms the log to itself, so the sketch does not check an external norm. Section 6 misses the stated absence of frequency or tolerance bands, and it reopens N6 and “external shipment,” which the norm already marks unspecified and scoped. Cross-abstraction conformance (OTG weight 200 vs ET–OT Delivery 198) is noticed and then dismissed as extraction variance rather than a mandatory E6 gap.
