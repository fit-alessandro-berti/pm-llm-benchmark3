7.5

The reconstruction is strong: station sojourn is kept separate from the wait/service split, open and queued cases stay in WIP as elapsed bounds, busy time includes open work, and the early-S1 / late-S2 bottleneck shift is supported by the counts. Several reported figures still contradict the same document or the file.

The required P11 row breaks a quantity the row itself treats as known. \(Y_2=80\) is fixed, and Section 6’s imputations are \((W_2,B_2)=(55,25),\ (60,20),\ (60,20)\). Section 3 instead gives wait **60 (55–65)** and service **20 (20–25)**. Those intervals are not complements of 80. The same slip does not appear for P05, P07, or P15.

Completed sojourns are listed correctly (mean \(2050/14\approx 146\), median 145) and then called monotone in arrival order. They are not: P08 is 180, P09 is 155, P10 is 175. “S2 wait steps up to 45–75” also skips P09’s observed wait of 25. On 12:00–14:00, S2 WIP falls to 4, so the claimed 5–7 band is too narrow. The S1 started-case means in Section 6 (~67 / ~68 / ~69) do not match the waits in Section 3: scenario C sums to 1380 over 21 starts, mean \(65.7\), and O/P only move that by about \(0.5\). The code would print that lower mean, so the sensitivity table is not reproduced by Section 7.

The 12:00–14:00 Little check is not a real equilibrium test. \(\bar L\approx 5.4\) is the in-window integral (645 case-minutes / 120 is right), but the completers’ mean sojourn of ~92.5 minutes is mostly time accrued before 12:00, and arrivals (7) are not completions (6). The product matching \(\bar L\) is not evidence that \(\lambda\bar W\) holds. Full-window sample-path checks are the part that is actually sound; completed-only S2 \(\bar L\approx 2.7\) versus \(3.7\) is correctly diagnosed.

Section 4’s S2 utilization “~72–80%” is not the O/C/P range in Section 6 (77–79% of 720). The 72% floor is never derived. The claim that a one-count hourly flip cannot change the sign of late \(\lambda-\mu\) is too strong: that phase is about one job over capacity. A4 is also a little strong: the reported 5-minute gaps are compatible with zero true idle, but the 11:00 C1 handoff can overlap once \(\pm 2.5\) minute rounding is applied.

The script is otherwise aligned with the task (distinct MISSING/OPEN/blank parsing, scenario imputations, `format_dataframe`, case-level PM4Py only on fully stamped completions, pandas WIP). It still mislabels P21. Setting the open S1 end to 14:00 makes `s2_queued` true, so the printed case table gives P21 an S2 wait of 0. The prose correctly says S2 was not entered. Peak queue is argued in the text and never computed in the code. Central busy times (S1 595/720, S2 560/720), WIP integrals (2085 and 1340), and the hourly arrival and completion counts do check out.
