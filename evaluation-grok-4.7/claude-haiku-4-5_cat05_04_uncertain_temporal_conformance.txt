The write-up has the right overall shape: offset direction is defined correctly, summer offsets and the 2025-10-26 Berlin fold are identified, T-405’s ingestion order is used to keep only the first-fold alert with second-fold start and containment, and the clearest SLA breaches (T-402 S1, T-408 S2, T-408 S3) are found. That is not enough. The numerical layer is unreliable, two required sections are absent, and the code does not run.

Published HUB windows keep only ±0.5 s and drop the ±1 s clock bound, so they should be about ±1.5 s. SENSOR “nearest 10 seconds” is treated as ±10 s; the pre-offset bin is ±5 s. For asymmetric OPS-BER bounds the narrative subtracts the wrong endpoint, shifting starts later (T-401 start should be about [08:12:00, 08:16:30), not [08:13:30, 08:16:40)). Several later subtractions are simply wrong by minutes: T-401 start→containment is forced into a fictional [2414, 2416] s after an inverted difference; T-402 alert→containment is ~3660 s instead of ~3720 s; T-403 S1’s lower bound is written as ~960 s when 08:16−08:04 is ~720 s; T-405 alert→start is given a 240 s floor when 01:04−00:52 is 720 s.

Zeta is systematically one step too wide: the ζ=1 bands are mean±2σ, ζ=2 is mean±3σ, and ζ=3 is a broken ±4σ with an ad hoc clamp at 0. That alone flips T-408 start→containment at ζ=1 from CERTAIN_VIOLATION (duration ~3300 s versus [1500, 2700]) to POSSIBLE_VIOLATION. Other verdict errors follow from the arithmetic: T-402 S2 is POSSIBLE_VIOLATION (about [2489, 2762] s versus 2700), not CERTAIN_PASS; T-402 alert→containment is a ζ=1 CERTAIN_VIOLATION, not a pass; T-403 S1 is POSSIBLE_VIOLATION, not certain; T-404 S2 is computed against Investigation_Complete (~18–25 min) rather than Containment_Complete, and the summary table even stores containment as 10:35. The true T-404 start→containment window reaches just past 45 minutes, so S2 is POSSIBLE_VIOLATION. T-409 start→containment extends to ~18 minutes, below the ζ=1 floor of 1500 s, so that profile result is POSSIBLE_VIOLATION, not CERTAIN_PASS.

T-406 states three incompatible investigation windows and then describes entry delay as tightening the lower bound while the chosen interval [12:08:30, 12:14:30] cuts the upper tail. Under an additive reading (rounding, ±300 s, and up to 5 minutes late), the window reaches both before 12:08:30 and after 12:14:30, and S1 is no longer a certain pass. Sensitivity repeats the bad bands and misses changes that follow even from the answer’s own intervals (T-401 S1 becomes a pass at +2 minutes because 1000.5 s < 1020 s). T-408 S3 is variously “8.5 minutes,” “>500 s,” and “2.4 hours”; only ~8700 s (~2.4 h) matches 138300−129600. The ζ=3 summary cell “Pass (all)” also clears SLA breaches that zeta does not touch.
