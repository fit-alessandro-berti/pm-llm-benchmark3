The write-up has the right skeleton: join as a maximum, queue time split from service, weekend gates, overlapping scenario bands, temperature dependence, and release as the modal terminal event. The clock arithmetic that those claims rest on is not reliable.

The incubation bound is mis-set. Readiness 46–54 h after Friday 14:00 is Sunday 12:00–20:00. The ledger and Scenario C place the upper end at Monday ~20:00 and then send an unstaffed late readout to Tuesday 08:00. Inside the stated window, every primary incubation ends on Sunday, so without on-call the first readout is Monday 08:00 plus 0.5–1.5 h. Incubation spread alone does not create a Tuesday join.

The left tail is outside the process support. Earliest technical readiness is Sunday ~12:00, QA is closed Sunday, and Saturday 09:00–13:00 is already over. A Saturday 19 Sep terminal (~19–28 h) cannot occur. Friday chemistry completion is also outside the given residuals: the urgent sample still waits, the first free analyst is occupied by it, and the second analyst is free at 16:42 at the earliest, which is too late for a 1.5 h minimum service before 18:00. On-call Sunday readout does not open a Saturday or Sunday release; with chemistry unfinished and QA shut, it only removes the Monday morning readout (about 0.5–1.5 h).

Elapsed hours and named clocks disagree by about a day. Monday 11:00 is ~67.5 h after Friday 15:30, not ~43–48 h. Monday afternoon–Tuesday evening is roughly 75–100 h, not 45–70 h or 42–55 h. The same compression appears in the Scenario A tail (~55–70 h labeled as Tuesday evening–Wednesday). The 0.4–0.9 h analyst is free at 15:54–16:24, not 16:10–16:24. Quantile bands are not ordered: the P90 floor (~5 days / ~120 h) starts before the P80 clock range (Wednesday–Thursday).

Scenario C’s 0.15–0.30 mass treats that phantom late readout as a main path. The real micro delay is contamination (baseline 0.05–0.12, higher if the excursion confirms), which from a Monday morning read lands on Tuesday, not a central Tuesday–Thursday span. Scenario B mixes the ordinary Friday spill, which is the base case under the 18:00 stop, with the rerun. Smooth-path chemistry “typically” usable Monday 09:30–13:00 assumes B-417 is near the front at Monday 08:00; under FIFO (~55%) most of the one urgent plus three ordinary samples can still be ahead, so Monday afternoon spill is ordinary, not a side case.

Conditional rerun and contamination lifts (about 0.20–0.35 and 0.10–0.25) and a “single-digit” rejection probability are stated as numbers the prompt does not give. The structural ranking in §5–§6 is still aimed at the right objects, but the readout-timestamp branch “ready only late Monday” is the same incubation error again.
