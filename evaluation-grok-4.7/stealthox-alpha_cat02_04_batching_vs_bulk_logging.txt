The cluster diagnoses are largely coherent and evidence-tied: precision, recording lag, source type, and the fixture/button/testimony notes are used as corroboration, identical timestamps are not treated as physical simultaneity, and the G6 waiting-up/service-down mechanism is correctly specified (35–175 min waits, zero durations, uniform 1 min lag). Most checked quantities match the table (G2 18/14/11/6 and 8/14/21/27 with 70/27 ≈ 259%; G3 22/18/19/19 and 28/20/13/6; G5 70 min over 30 min ≈ 233%; G7 waits −1…−11 min and 5×15/15 = 500%; G4/G8 exact chains and 3–6 s / 2–6 s lags). Assumptions are labeled, and Sections 5–7 keep resource-trust and censoring regimes separate.

The required PM4Py comparison is the main failure. `discover_batches` is invoked with `merge_distance` and `min_batch_size` as keywords; the public API takes those inside a `parameters` dict, so the four runs are expected to raise, and the `except` path then prints an empty comparison. The flattener treats `b[0]` as case ids, while batches are `(events, BatchType)`, so cluster attribution and `",".join(cases)` do not work even if a call succeeded. The prose also collapses categories: equal start and end is Simultaneous (G1, G7, and possibly G6), but G2 is start-batching, G3 is end-batching, and G5 is concurrent. Predicting SIM for G2/G3/G5 misstates the detector the section is supposed to compare.

Other final-statement errors: G5 is not non-nested (A02 sits inside A01; A04 sits inside A01 and A03). G3’s last observed service is 6 min and the last start gap is also 6 min, so “last understated” does not follow from start spacing. The code sets G6 `naive_util` to NaN when the span is 0, while the reading guide says 0. G7’s recorded bins are uninformative; the true intervals do not overlap “regardless of ground truth.” Grouping G3 with G6 as one point-mass pattern is wrong — G3 has 6–28 min durations. “5 in 5 min” for G4 is a 11:58–12:02 span. Section 2 is an observed cluster-by-signature table, not a hypothesis-by-signature matrix of expected patterns, and throughput/conformance bias is only sketched. G3’s second rank, minute-level coarsening, is a weak alternative to parallel completion or folder submit.
