2.5

The write-up matches the required section outline and correctly reads a few early, fully observed cases (P01–P04, P06, P08). Almost everything that the benchmark asks to reconstruct is then either inconsistent with the supplied log or not actually computed.

The case table discards the given timestamps. P09, P10, and P13 are rewritten as “typos,” and P09–P22 are assigned starts, ends, waits, and services that are not in the data. That violates the evidence rule and makes the later station totals, subperiod rates, WIP peaks, and utilizations non-estimates. Mandatory rows are present but wrong: P05’s S2 wait is given as [0, 10] min while the note says the start lies between 09:25 and 10:10; a start at 09:25 also overlaps P03 on C1. P07’s start window includes times when I1 is busy with P05. P11, P15, and P17 use invented S2 clocks. P16 and P18–P20 are waiting for S2, but they are coded as open S2 service; P21–P22 likewise treat “not started” as open service. Reported S1 completions (18) and S2 arrivals/completions (18/12) do not match the log (20 S1 completions; 20 entries to S2; 14 S2 completions).

Bottleneck reasoning contradicts itself. Growing S2 waits are cited and then concluded to show that S1 is the bottleneck. P06 (10:10–10:50 on C2) is attributed to the 10:55–11:10 outage, which occurs after P06 has already finished. No queue-length path, simultaneous-event convention, rounding band (±5 min on a difference), or numerical Little’s Law check is produced, despite A5 promising one. Section 6 names optimistic, central, and pessimistic scenarios but reports no recomputed tables; the ±10% and ±20% claims are unsupported. “Lower-bound start” and “shortest service” are also incompatible when the end timestamp is fixed.

The Python block does not implement the method. The S2 columns have 23 entries against 22 cases, so the frame will not build. `parse_time` maps MISSING, OPEN, and blank to the same null. Datetime midpoints use `(lower + upper) / 2`, which is invalid, and blank S2 rows are sent through that imputer. `format_dataframe` is never called. Open work is dropped, so utilization is completed-case busy time only. `insert_case_service_waiting_time` and `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` are case-level operations, yet their results are printed as station means and the arrival/finish call is not shown. The “WIP by hour” group counts S1 starts; it is not WIP. No uncertainty scenario is calculated.
