7.3

The answer meets every required section, column set, node list, and length constraint, and its main judgment is sound: Site A’s 9-hour median drop is confounded, document checking cannot start before statutory originals, and only a gated, segmented pilot is defensible. The deductions are for quantitative inconsistency, a few causal-graph errors, an under-specified pilot sample, and code that likely fails when it checks soundness.

The process comparison is accurate on concurrency, the assessment join, originals, legal review, supplement loops, role pools, the one-owner lock, the six-task cap, huddles, mix shift, tail, rework, appeals, and timestamp bias. Completions rising from 4,800 to 5,200 are treated as an 8.3% throughput gain; completions can move with arrivals and backlog, so that label is stronger than the table supports. Transport-confidence scores are defined as confidence in the “transferable component,” which assigns 90 and 80 to methodological lessons and co-interventions. That definition avoids treating the 9-hour contrast as an effect, but the column still reads as transportability of the Site A result.

The adjacency list contains every required node and a real selection layer. Two edges are not justified by the evidence. High complexity is 48% and legal-review status is 40%; they are different classifications, yet `case_complexity -> legal_review` collapses them. `cycle_time -> appeal` makes duration a cause of 90-day appeals, while the measured appeal risk is a quality outcome already linked to rework. `identity_service_time` reaches cycle time only through pre-assessment waiting, so a shorter identity service on the critical path has no direct path. The prose itself is within 180–280 words and correctly separates the shared partial-order mechanism from lock, role, capacity, and originals modifiers.

Scenario direction is right, including a case where the center improves and the p90 worsens. The interval endpoints are not derived from the constraints the prose states. Service overlap on a ready case is about the shorter check, 3.4 hours, and an 18-hour originals delay can absorb that identity work when identity already runs during receipt. Those bounds sit poorly with the optimistic sitewide median of −3 to −6 hours and p90 of −3 to −9 hours when at most 38% of cases can overlap the two checks and the tail is originals plus legal review. Multiplying 38% by nine hours is correctly rejected, and 360/1,360 is correctly about 26%, but that arithmetic never produces the printed ranges. The IT-lock row also puts “0 to 38% effectively overlapped” in a column defined as eligible share. The Register outgoing counts 2,050 and 1,860 already sum above 3,400, so these cannot be ordinary directly-follows frequencies; that inconsistency is not flagged.

The modified control flow is the right intervention: ready no-originals cases may run the two checks together; originals cases may run identity with receipt; document execution stays behind receipt; assessment joins both; legal review and supplement repetition stay downstream; ownership, WIP, and immutable logging are preconditions. The partial order does not formally cover no-originals cases whose documents are not yet ready, and the supplement loop’s return to the approve/reject choice is left implicit.

The pilot randomizes the shared queue in two-week blocks, keeps V0 against V1, stages originals as V2, and separates implementation failure from mechanism failure by audited overlap, pre-assessment wait, and downstream queue shift. That causal structure is strong. The support calculation is not. About 590 cases per arm is the no-originals split before the stated exclusions for low complexity, non-legal status, readiness, and independence. The analyzed stratum is substantially smaller and is still only 12 clusters, with no washout, while p90 sojourn is 110 hours and V2’s entry “after the first gate” can fall inside the same 24 weeks. Stop rules mix a 10-hour p90 trigger with unquantified “material” rework and queue growth.

The PM4Py fragment builds both Site A trees, converts them, and builds a hierarchical POWL model whose digital and originals partial orders both make Assessment wait for identity and document completion, with document work after receipt only on the originals branch, plus an optional legal loop and a supplement redo. That is the right net. `sound, diagnostics = pm4py.check_soundness(...)` treats a boolean API as a pair, so the script fails on the first net before soundness of the Site B model is obtained. Playout is optional and omitted. The closing comment correctly excludes data-dependent guards, capacity, locks, and durations.

`conditional go` matches the evidence. The first subgroup, the refusal to copy universal execution and the six-task cap, and the expansion/abandon rules are coherent with the earlier sections. “Three-to-seven-hour” subgroup benefit repeats the under-derived digital scenario, and “upper-level descriptive contrast” is vague, but the 9-hour Site A change is not used as a Site B effect. The eight assumptions each have a use, a test, and a consequence. Assumption 3 asks for legal sign-off on identity during transit even though Site B’s stated rules already allow identity and originals receipt to start after registration; the binding constraint is the lock and practice, not an unstated prohibition.

The answer is analytically mature and operationally safe. It is short of nearly flawless because the scenario magnitudes conflict with its own critical-path bounds, the pilot’s minimum support describes a broader population than the estimand, the transport graph merges distinct case attributes, and the soundness check as written does not run.
