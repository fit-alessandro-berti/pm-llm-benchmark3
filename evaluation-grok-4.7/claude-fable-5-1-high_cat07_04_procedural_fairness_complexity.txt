7.3

The essay meets the required structure and constraints, and its core judgment is appropriately cautious: raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination, case-mix is not treated as a full excuse, support steps are treated as a burden, and `risk_score_band` is not treated as neutral. The adjusted-versus-raw distinction, mediator/confounder problem, and mitigation trade-offs are handled well. It is not nearly flawless.

The main inferential errors are quantitative and structural. “Roughly two thirds” of the raw approval gap is explained for B (about 63%) but not for D (about 85% of the 20-point gap; residual −3.1). The 3.8-day Language Support Contact → Eligibility Assessment edge is not on V4 as specified (V4 inserts Completeness and Request Missing Documents), so it cannot be “of which 3.8 days” of the 4.3-day V4–V2 difference. V5 versus V1 does not isolate accommodation cost: V1 is the short approved path, and a 1.6-day supervisor wait does not account for the remaining ~11 days, yet §8 still calls these “comparable paths” and cites “+4 to +16 days.” Calling 4.7 days times the 31% V5 share the “dominant” part of C’s +4.1-day adjusted gap fails the arithmetic (about 1.5 days).

Fraud routing is over-read. V3 share over high-risk share is about 0.50 (A) and 0.55 (D), but about 0.38 (B) and 0.33 (C). A mandatory Fraud Review rule for the high band would also not be satisfied by covering only about half of high-risk cases with V3, and V3 is only one listed path. “Rule-driven” and “consistent with its recorded risk band” are stronger than that comparison.

§8 then tightens the wrong bound: it sets the adjusted residual as a lower bound and the raw gap as an upper bound. §1.2 correctly says the residual may be unmeasured legitimate complexity, so the unjustified component can sit below the adjusted figure. Those two claims conflict.

The PM4Py plan uses the right family of techniques, but several pieces would mis-estimate the fairness quantities: logit coefficients are not percentage points; `get_service_time` is called on logs that never received service-time columns; `prob_edge * median` does not decompose cycle time and can exceed 1 when rework repeats an edge; hardcoded V2/V4 tuples omit activities the summaries themselves suggest are incomplete; and `supervisor_no_trigger` treats non-high risk as the violation, although the stated policy ties high risk to Fraud Review, not Supervisor Review. Exact strata on raw `prior_case_count` also contradict the coarsened matching described in §2.
