6.5

The final policy is structurally complete and gets the three traps right: I-03 is ineligible, I-05 is suppressed for a no-event refresh while I-07 is reopened on the new log and then rejected on value and time, and untreated-case spillover is carried into the scenario table. The portfolio I-04 / I-02 / I-09 plus one reserve is a defensible euro-value choice, and the midpoint benefit arithmetic (€8,950 / €2,700 / €3,550; totals €19,800 / €15,200 / €5,050) checks out. It is not coherent enough to score higher.

The deployable rule is not the rule that produces the allocation. Tier 1 is defined as midpoint NEIV above €2,500, which puts I-08 near €4,550, ahead of I-02. I-08 is then dropped as Tier 2. The stated reason, that Strategic calibration to ~0.34 makes the lower bound negative, is wrong: the lower bound is negative because \(0.05 \times 100{,}000 - 450 - 5{,}000 = -450\), and 0.15 still fits inside a 0.34 risk. Repeat alerts are suppressed when \(|\Delta p| < 0.10\) with no new document or stage change, yet I-02 (\(+0.08\)) and I-09 (\(+0.07\)) are both treated as valid and selected. The 0.75 Non-Native factor and the imputed-data factor are not applied: I-09 is reported as a “conservative” ~€3.6k, which is the undiscounted midpoint, and I-06’s “discounted” ~€2.6k is the pre-discount figure. Discounting \((rV - C - F)\) rather than \(rV\) alone also shrinks certain costs.

Safeguards in §§5–6 contradict the action. The equity floor would keep a slot for an SME/Public case with adjusted risk above 0.55 and a material event; I-07 qualifies, and none of the three selected cases is SME or Public Sector. The Strategic-share review fires above 50 percent; this portfolio is two-thirds Strategic. I-04 is supposed to need a double-driver check before a swarm and does not get one. A spillover index defined as the sum of probability increases freezes allocations above 0.15, but three slots at about 0.02 on six cases is about 0.36.

Scenario ranges are not auditable against the formulas printed beside them. Optimistic spillover is given as €2,500–€6,000 while \(3 \times 6 \times 0.01 \times €20\text{k}–€25\text{k}\) is €3,600–€4,500. Adverse net impact is stated as about −€8k to −€12k, while €5,050 minus the stated €11k–€17k spillover is about −€6k to −€12k. Adverse delay euros are stacked on the high end of the probability spillover the delay is already meant to represent. `p_adj` never enters the score, the 10-hour option value of waiting on I-04 is treated only as a reason to swarm now, and two specialists delivering one swarm is never sequenced against I-09’s 2.5-hour deadline.
