6.5

The write-up covers every required section, uses concrete object IDs and timestamps, and correctly refuses to treat machine capacity as the lead bottleneck. It is not reliable on the calculations and critical paths that the data actually support.

The headline waits that are simple subtractions are right: J1 08:10→12:05 is 3 h 55 min, J2 08:25→12:05 is 3 h 40 min, J1 13:30→19:25 is 5 h 55 min, and J4 09:50→11:45 is 1 h 55 min. Several derived results are not.

The B1 counterfactual sentence is false. Starting at 08:25 would leave J1 waiting 15 minutes, not 0–10. Starting at J3’s 11:55 readiness would leave J1 waiting 3 h 45 min. The later “3 h 55 min − 10 min = 3 h 45 min” line is the right difference, but it is attached to a counterfactual that also assumes RACK-7 and OVEN-1 were free at 08:10. Nothing in the evidence shows that. Oven idle time is given only for 12:55–14:50, after B1. RACK-7 is related to the 12:05 cure; the only cleaning/quarantine timestamp is 14:35, before B2. Folding the rack into the morning batch wait is unsupported.

CAL-3 is mis-timed. Recalibration runs 16:35–17:20, which is 45 minutes, not 50. The 50-minute span is 16:35→17:25, including five minutes after recalibration ends. The upper figure of 80 minutes is J6’s whole 16:05→17:25 wait, which includes J5 occupying TEST-1 from 16:10–16:35. The stated “50 min recalibration + setup ≈ 50–80 min” mixes those intervals.

The S1 residual is also wrong. 5 h 55 min minus 0.3–0.8 h is about 5 h 7 min–5 h 37 min, not 5 h 15 min–5 h 45 min. Those shipment-DFG medians are Create→Load and Load→Dispatch, not pack-to-dispatch. The clean partner wait is 13:30→19:10 = 5 h 40 min, then 15 minutes to dispatch.

FIX-A is over-attributed. CNC-2 is idle only 10:05–10:47 (42 min). The log does not say it was idle from 10:47 to 11:45, and it was not idle at 09:50. The claim that the 42-minute gap “understates” a 1 h 40 min–1 h 55 min tool block treats unobserved time as waiting. That inflated range is then reused as the “~1.5–2 h” benefit of a second fixture.

The B2 critical path is reversed. RACK-7 is free at 14:35, J5 is ready at 14:40, and B2 starts at 14:50. For the actual two-job batch, J5 is later than the rack. A second rack, or faster cleaning, does not move B2 to 13:10 unless the batch is also allowed to start without J5. Intervention I4 states that it would. The same error drives Narrative B: J4 is already batch-ready at 13:10, so the FIX-A hold has slack relative to the 14:50 start. The fixture→batch→S1 cascade equates succession with a critical path the timestamps contradict, despite the speculation label.

Ranking inherits these mistakes. Rank 1 bundles batch membership with a shared rack even though the rack is not shown to bind B1 and is not the last gate on B2. The rationale says the largest waits are B1 and S1, then ranks tools above shipment using the overstated FIX-A duration. S1’s 5 h 55 min dwell is larger than either tool episode.

Smaller compliance and clarity problems remain. The motif header says three object types while the motifs use four, and the fourth motif splices CAL-3/TEST-1 with S1. Lot–job links are called inferred in one place and directly observed in another; the events already relate to J2 and J5, while the job extract drops them. The code names every requested function, but the required comparison of two object-centric frequency metrics is only a comment. “Pack→Dispatch median 1.1 h masks the tail” is not reconciled with “no job is more than 1.8 SD above the mean,” nor with the alternative that shipment-partner wait is absent from the job log entirely. J3 holds FIX-A until 11:40 and is batch-ready at 11:55, while CNC-2 is idle during part of that hold; that tension is never examined. Two rework cases are available as an alternative account of J4’s 19:10 pack and are not used.

The flattening account of idle machines versus waiting jobs, the lot-override asymmetry, the logging intervention, and the falsification list are the parts that hold up.
