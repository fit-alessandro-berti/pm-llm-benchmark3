Object-centric expectations slide from type-level graphs to instance IDs. Forward context from package/delivery events is consistent, reserve is not pushed onto not-yet-packed packages, and invoice lines are not pushed onto items. Qualifiers such as `observed:order`, `observed:delivery`, and `observed:settled_invoice` are also thinner than the rest of the vocabulary, and “settled” overstates M1.

The code itself is the strongest part: one self-contained block, authoritative activity/timestamp copies, extra `relation_origin` kept out of the business keys, isolated constructor, required discoveries stored, and visualizations commented. Those strengths do not erase the unsupported settlement completion, the assumption/code mismatch, or the misstated divergence.
