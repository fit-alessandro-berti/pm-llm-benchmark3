8.3

The analysis is technically strong: time origin, competing risks, administrative censoring, and already-observed breaches are distinguished correctly, and the main Aalen–Johansen figures check out (pooled \(F_{res}(72)=0.304\), E1 \(=0.696\), E3 \(=0.380\), E2 \(=0.143\), RMST \(=50.4\); cohort E1 \(0.708\) vs \(0.684\); priority E1 \(0.750/0.569/0.800\); counting bounds \([0.563,0.719]\); T2/T3 and best/worst open-case sensitivities). Naive completed-case bias, immortal time, and prefix leakage are handled at the right level, and the code implements those estimands without dropping open cases.

These local failures keep it short of nearly flawless.

- `n_review ≥ 2` is given as S03, S07, S13, and S29. S29’s prefix is `Submit>AutoCheck>Escalate>Review` (one `Review`). Only S03, S07, and S13 repeat `Review`.
- S29 is an “active” open case in the encoding table (gap 6 h, under the 12 h rule) and a “stalled” imputation case in §3. Those cannot both be right under assumption A2.
- “All four” post-SLA open cases are called dormant at 72 h, with inactivity “11–46 h.” S09’s inactivity at 72 h is \(72-(92-31)=11\) h, below the stated 12 h threshold.
- The T2 imputation pool is named as S11, S25, and S29. That is not the risk set at the transfer times. At S21 (26 h), S04 is still in process and resolves at 29 h. S29 is already censored at 51 h, before S08 at 68 h.
- SA12 says reclassifying long-idle open cases as unlogged closures leaves E1 unchanged. Last-event times for S11, S25, S26, and S09 are 26, 31, 32, and 61 h, all before 72. An unlogged closure inside the gap can remove them from the pre-72 risk set and, if that closure was a resolution, can change their own breach status. E1 is unaffected only under an extra assumption that is not stated.
- The bootstrap interval is cohort-stratified resampling of the crude E1 contrast, but the prose places it immediately after the priority-standardized 0.688 vs 0.675 contrast, so it reads as a CI for the standardized difference.
- “The distortion is larger in the priority strata” is true for P3 (KM resolution about 0.29 vs CIF 0.20) and false for P1 (about 0.27 vs 0.25), where the gap is smaller than the pooled gap of about 0.06.
- “B has shorter potential follow-up” is stated as a fact. Later entry plus a common cutoff makes that plausible, but entry dates are absent, and B includes S26 at 104 h.

The remaining design (primary AJ under T1, RMST and landmarking as secondary, multi-state deferred until timestamps exist, transfer scenarios T1–T6, and non-causal alert language) is coherent and matched to \(n=32\).
