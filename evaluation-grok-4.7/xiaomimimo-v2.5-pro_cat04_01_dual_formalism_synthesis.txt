The process tree and the POWL model are the right constructions, and the witness table is internally consistent with them. The tree is an explicit conservative under-approximation: `CM→PV` is parallel to `ARC` then `DCA`, optional supplier, and the risk XOR, and the trial loop is only after that join, so approvals and supplier consultation cannot overlap the trial. The POWL partial order correctly leaves `risk_route` and `optional_cs` incomparable with `trial_loop` while still joining all three into release. Loop shape `(ET→IT)` redo `AP`, low-risk vs concurrent high-risk, and skip-or-`CS` are the right control-flow encodings, and W5 is a genuine exposure of the tree gap. W6–W8 name the earliest broken dependency, including cross-branch trial gates.

The prose then misstates XOR. An exclusive choice cannot emit `SR` and `QR`/`RR` in one case; playout selects one branch. Treating supplier traces as “semantically impossible” also fights the task: unobserved case data is supposed to be a control-flow alternative, not a defect. Section 3.3’s claim that the loop cannot stop `Adjust Parameters` after a passing inspection is false under the same `A (B A)*` semantics the model uses: the exiting `IT` is not followed by the redo. That false claim is repeated as a residual weakness, so one of the three required critiques does not hold. Smaller nits: “true concurrency” is looser than the incomparability actually implemented, and the compact tree string is not PM4Py parse syntax.
