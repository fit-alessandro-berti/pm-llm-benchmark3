The case analysis is complete and often careful, but it is not nearly flawless. The revised POWL contradicts the answer’s own judgments, and several authority and evidence calls are overconfident or internally inconsistent.

The required structure is met: both briefs concede real points, all 15 cases appear with the requested matrix columns and labels, burden of proof is explicit, and the layered split among POWL, overlays, data checks, and organizational controls is the right architecture. SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-709, SUP-712, and SUP-714 are classified on defensible dominant grounds, and expiry versus a mere “still an emergency” claim is handled correctly.

The model is not a sound revision. The same transition objects are reused across XOR branches, so the object graph is not a well-formed POWL. `Request_More_Info` is mandatory exactly once and is placed before route selection, yet `SilentTransition` is imported and unused; a loop with a silent child was the obvious approximation. That encoding rejects SUP-701 (no request) and SUP-707 (two requests interleaved after sanctions). The emergency path forces notification before quality and retrospective approval and renames the approval, so it rejects SUP-704, the case called a justified exception. The pilot path forces notification before quality, which PD-3 does not require and which SUP-703’s trace violates. Master-data validation and daily monitoring never become enforceable activities or rules. Overlay rows overlap through 2099 without a precedence rule, and reactivations and withdrawals are promised in prose but not given routes.

Evidence handling slips in the same places. Automated master-data validation is treated as done for SUP-702 although the trace and context do not show it. SUP-702 is tied to the July 1 CRM change even though the case is June 15. SUP-703 is called structurally the same as SUP-702 despite having a logged notification. SUP-708’s post-activation manual approval is omitted beside the sanctions inversion. SUP-711’s identical source transaction ID and SUP-713’s audited label equivalence are stronger than “indeterminate.” Section 1 correctly leaves PD-3’s authority disputed, then Section 5 marks SUP-702 a high-confidence justified exception without resolving the observer’s disavowal. Section 5 also rejects prosecution claims the prosecution brief never made.
