The required skeleton is present, but the empirical core is unreliable and several causal claims are mis-specified.

Stage times are not a defensible approximation. Flag 1 versus flag 0 median cycle time is about 16.5 h versus 5.8 h, not 19.5 versus 17.5. Unflagged triage wait is about 150 minutes, not 105; triage service medians are about 36 and 57 minutes, not 30 and 45. The manual gap among unflagged cases that actually reach review is about 1.75 h, not 4.5 h. Totals appear to be sums of component figures, including escalation for cases that never escalate, so median-of-sums is treated as cycle time. Flagged high load is n=6, not 5, and flagged normal load is n=4, not 5. The required complexity split is missing. The 100–180 word explanation is short and then calls these cycle times “similar.”

Other counts are wrong: every flagged case is reviewed, not 90%; unflagged escalation is 20%, not 30%; reject rates are both 20%, and all four rejects are high complexity. “Reject rate higher in Flag 1” is false. Triage staff are perfectly separated (T1/T2 versus T3/T4), score overlap exists around 0.55–0.67, and many downstream gaps cross overnight. None of that is used.

Estimands name interventional effects but then label policy contrasts as controlled direct effects, condition on post-treatment review, and mark interference-prone case-level effects well-defined. The graph’s `arrival_load → priority_lane` edge and reviewer-adjusted complexity contradict the stated intake process. Identification rows confuse binning with regression discontinuity, call `arrival_load` an instrument while describing adjustment, and use the decision itself as a negative control. The P3 pilot conflicts with itself: 50/50 assignment cannot also be capped at 20% capacity without an overflow rule, and “error rate” is undefined.

The Python block does not parse stage timestamps, treats `-` as present, drops Register and Close, omits the start-timestamp key, does not build flagged and unflagged frequency and performance DFGs, and does not report case durations or waiting times. The synthesis repeats the false “comparable cycle time” claim and is under 180 words.
