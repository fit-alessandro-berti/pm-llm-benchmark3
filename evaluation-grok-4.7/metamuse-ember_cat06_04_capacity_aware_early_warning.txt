7.5

The policy is coherent, operational, and correctly built around effect size, stage, value, false-alarm cost, calibration, and queue harm. It still has decision errors and internal inconsistencies that a deployable rule cannot carry.

The stage gate correctly excludes I-03, and the arithmetic behind that exclusion is right: a 0.00–0.03 effect on €40,000 cannot cover €850 of direct cost. I-05 is suppressed for a repeated refresh with no new event, while I-07 is re-opened because of the new log and then rejected on value. Calibration shifts match the stated biases (SME about +0.065, Strategic about −0.085, no point shift for Non-Native-Language Support). Case-level midpoints, lower bounds, and the €1,800 base spillover charge (6 × 0.02 × €15,000) reproduce the reported scores when negative results are read as negative. The base gross benefit of €19,950 and the €4,750 direct-cost total are correct. Sections 5 and 6 name concrete logging, shadow scoring, segment pauses, delay caps, and causal-estimation safeguards.

The portfolio decision is less robust than the write-up claims. I-02’s midpoint surplus is only about €900 after the €1,800 charge. At the €25,000 untreated value used later in the same answer, that score turns negative and I-02 fails the stated Tier 1 test. The allocation nevertheless commits a slot to I-02 rather than making dispatch conditional on the live untreated-queue value. I-06, which has a higher lower bound (€1,500 versus €950) and cleaner segment calibration, stays behind that commitment even though verification is due within 30 minutes.

The adverse row aims the failure at the wrong case. I-02 is cut to 0.03, while I-09 — the sparse Non-Native-Language Support case whose effect range is the least identified — remains at 0.18. That conflicts with the earlier statement that I-09 itself may receive near-zero benefit. The sentence that keeps spillover “at 0.03” and still shows a net up to about +€3,000 is wrong: at 0.03 the three-slot externality is 0.54 breaches, or about €8,100–€13,500, so the €5,600 post-cost benefit stays negative across that value range. The positive upper end appears only at the low spillover of 0.01.

The fairness rule is not applied to this portfolio. I-07 has adjusted risk near 0.79, 1.5 hours to deadline, below-median value, and a positive upper-bound direct benefit, which is the safeguard’s own trigger. Even its upper-bound direct benefit is about €1,100, below the €1,800 spillover charge, so refusing the swarm can be right, but the safeguard is written without that spillover test. The €15,000 anchor, the 120-minute cool-down, the ±0.15 Non-Native-Language Support band, and the 1.5× congestion multiplier are usable operating choices, yet none is tied back to how the selected set changes when they move. The regulatory reserve is also unpriced, and the impact table never shows the four-slot outcome in which verified I-06 takes that reserve.
