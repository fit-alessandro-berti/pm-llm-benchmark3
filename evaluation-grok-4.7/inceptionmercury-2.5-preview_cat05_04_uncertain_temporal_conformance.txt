**Event intervals.** ISO endpoints are not explicit (`20260601T08:12:0008:16:29` is not a parseable interval). HUB rows sometimes are ±1 s, sometimes a one-sided second, and sometimes a single instant (`08:16:00–08:16:00`), so duration endpoints are not comparable across cases.
