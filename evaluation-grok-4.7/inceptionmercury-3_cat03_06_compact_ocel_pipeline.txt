The pipeline is complete and mostly faithful: 17 timezone-aware events, all 12 objects, payment amounts on the events, E2O activity/timestamp copies, the required O2O edges, and real uniqueness, orphan, and consistency checks. The write-up then contradicts that model. Section 1 and the “source-minimal” alternative say an E2O edge exists only for objects the narrative names, and that pack events touch both orders because the text says so. Event 8 names only P2 and I2, yet `e8` still links O100. The same sections say no E2O row is added merely from an O2O path; `e8`–O100 is exactly that. Assertion 6 and the OTG paragraph repeat the error by saying the package–order link exists only through items, while `e7` co-relates P1 with O100 and O200 and the interaction list even records that co-occurrence.

Section 5 misstates the OC-DFG. No object type walks Create Order through Confirm Delivery. Order directly-follows matches section 4 and stops at close, without dispatch, confirmation, or payment.
