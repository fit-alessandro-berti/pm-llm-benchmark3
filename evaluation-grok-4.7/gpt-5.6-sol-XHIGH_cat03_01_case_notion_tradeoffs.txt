7.8

The analysis is specific, internally organized, and unusually careful with identifiers. The incidence arithmetic checks: 42 order relations over 31 events, 24 item relations over 16 events, 12 package relations over 10 events, and 6, 7, and 4 one-to-one relations for delivery, invoice, and payment. The D2 interval e16→e24 is 4 hours 55 minutes, the V2 gap e22→e25 is 20 hours 20 minutes, and the dispatch denominators (3 executions, 4 orders, 6 order incidences, 4 package incidences, 3 deliveries) are right. Copied events are treated as both impact evidence and execution duplication, propagation is bounded, and position C is supported by the orthogonal splits P3/P4, D2, and V2 rather than by trace length. The required distortion types, portfolio, sensitivity checks, and PM4Py calls are present, and omitting `cluster_equivalent_ocel` matches the lead-object decision.

The score is held down by a hard identifier error and a few classification problems.

In distortion 10, the range “e18–e26 finance” is false as written. e23 and e24 are Confirm Delivery, they belong to D1 and D2, and they are the second half of the delivery lifecycle the same paragraph is describing. Finance in that span is e18–e22 and e25–e26. The exclusion claim is therefore wrong for two of the nine IDs.

Divergence is defined as false serialization of separate branches, then applied to something else. V2’s e19→e22→e25 path is a real instalment sequence under `partially_settles` and `completes_settlement`. Calling that “divergence risk: medium” in the matrix conflicts with that definition and with distortion 9, which correctly says the invoice order should be preserved and the rework reading resisted. The Payment row then reuses the same column for fragmentation of M2 and M3. Order, invoice, and payment “divergence” are no longer the same measure. Order is also rated uniformly high, while O4 has no repeated activity and O2’s only repeat is those same instalments, broken by e24 so that Record Payment does not directly follow itself.

Smaller imprecisions: “identical item traces” is true of activity variants only; I11 (e15→e23), the D2 items (e16→e24), and the D3 items (e17→e27) are different timed traces, so item-level performance still exposes consolidation. Add Item→Add Item is multi-item repetition; it is not produced by the package split, even though O1 and O3 happen to be the split orders. Delivery’s convergence cell folds “aggregation semantics” into a column that section 1 reserved for same-type event copying. The code names the required models and separates execution counts from relation rows, but it never computes the durations, \(1/k\) weights, or qualifier distinctions on which sections 3 and 7 depend.

Those are local defects in an otherwise consistent reading. They are still defects in a task whose value is identifier-level control of convergence, divergence, and frequency.
