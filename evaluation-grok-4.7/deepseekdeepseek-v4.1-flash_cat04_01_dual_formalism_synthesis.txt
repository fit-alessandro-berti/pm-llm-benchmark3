The final model is coherent and mostly faithful. The POWL partial order correctly encodes the cross-branch pattern: containment before verification planning, root-cause analysis before design, approvals, and optional supplier consultation, the trial after both design and planning, and release as the join of trial, approval, and supplier. The loop language `(Execute Trial, Inspect Trial)(Adjust Parameters, Execute Trial, Inspect Trial)*` matches the repeat-until reading, the risk XOR and high-risk concurrency are right, and the process tree is an honestly labeled conservative under-approximation rather than a disguised exact tree. Witness verdicts, including A5 as the approximation counterexample and F1 as a cross-branch violation, check out.

It is not nearly flawless. The five intended-allowed traces share one skeleton and never actually exhibit the concurrency the models are built to allow: containment overlapping root-cause analysis, approval overlapping the trial, supplier consultation overlapping trial work, or either order of the two high-risk reviews. Section 6 asserts those overlaps, but Section 5 does not witness them. The unconnected-node argument is only a sample, not a justification of every intentionally unordered pair. “Exact” in the comparison table is slightly loose for the process tree, whose early join delays reviews and supplier consultation until both planning and design finish. Those are semantic and evidential gaps, not syntax nits, and they keep this below a top score.
