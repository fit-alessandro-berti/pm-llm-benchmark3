4.5

The final write-up has the right skeleton (four calendar regimes, outage not treated as steady state, median service not used as flow time, pooled Little’s law rejected), but the quantities a reader would act on are often wrong, internally inconsistent, or left unjustified.

Regime means do not match the stated date ranges. R1–R4 include weekends, yet arrivals/completions are reported near weekday levels (R1 ~100/100 vs about 87/86; R2 ~105/103 vs about 81/77; R3 ~112/101 vs about 99/87). R2 is not a net clearing regime: completions are 386 against 403 arrivals, so the claimed ratio of 1.02 is false. Pooled totals are also wrong (stated 2489/2517 and 98.9%; the table sums to 2409/2519, about 95.6%). R2’s “WIP ~37” and “p90 ~68” describe pieces of the window, not the regime.

Dispersion math is off. For 2026-04-07 arrivals the hourly Fano factor is about 3.0 (population) or 3.2 (sample), not 3.96; completion mean is 8.25, not 7.5. For 2026-04-21 the Fano factor is about 9–10, not 7.65, so “doubled” understates the change. Lag-1 WIP autocorrelation is about 0.61, not >0.8, and it is not computed in the code. Fano on raw hourly bins also mixes the diurnal wave with true bursts; 2026-04-07 is a smooth peak, while 2026-04-21 is the bursty day.

Little’s law multiplies \(L/\lambda\) by 1440 without resolving whether `mean_wip` is a 12-hour open-window snapshot or a 24-hour average. That choice can change implied flow time by 2×. R1 is marked `applicable` despite unknown 07:00 backlog, weekday/weekend mix, and that ambiguity. An open-hours reading (about 2–2.5 h) is the more coherent one; 276 minutes is hard to reconcile with ~25 min median service and roughly 6 FTE.

Required comparisons are incomplete. Section 4 never shows weekday vs weekend composition or the staffing-intervention contrast, even though both are requested. `complex_pct` is treated as a real mix shift; the coding-change alternative is dropped, and service times were already elevated on 2026-04-15–19 before the jump past 40%. The hourly vector for 2026-04-21 sums to the daily total but does not reproduce `peak_08_10_pct` of 47, yet the staffing advice still targets 08:00–10:00. The “pooled policy” example contradicts itself (simple cases called the long tail, then complex cases). The code omits completion vectors, a second dispersion metric, and any serial-dependence statistic, and its weekend-inclusive means contradict Section 2.
