6.3

The transport decision is right, but the quantitative mapping and the formal model are not reliable enough for a higher score.

The structural comparison, confounder handling, and conditional-go recommendation are strong. Site A’s −9h median is correctly treated as unidentified. Originals, the case lock, role specialization, legal review, and the supplement loop are correctly treated as barriers to copying `+(Identity Check, Document Check)` for every case. The pilot distinguishes implementation failure, bottleneck shift, and mechanism failure, and the statutory order is preserved in the recommended design.

The scenario arithmetic contradicts itself. The write-up caps theoretical overlap at about 3–4h, then assigns the eligible subgroup −6 to −9h in the optimistic case and −3 to −5h in the digital-only case, while its own formula (`−6h × 0.5 × 0.6`) yields about −1.8h. Population median changes are computed as eligible share times subgroup change. With originals required in 62% of cases, the overall median is likely a slow case; speeding the faster minority need not move it. Eligible cases are also assigned the population baseline of 44h despite not carrying the 18h originals delay. Legal utilization “>80%” and the Kingman claim are not identified: no legal service time is given, and 1,360 cases over six months is about 5 cases per legal reviewer per day, not evidence of saturation.

The causal graph includes every required node, but several edges have inconsistent meanings. `rework -> legal_capacity` and `S_legal_mandate -> legal_capacity` treat a capacity stock as demand, while `legal_capacity -> waiting_before_assessment` only makes sense if capacity reduces waiting. `treatment_parallelization -> queue_huddles` turns a co-intervention into an effect of treatment. Originals delay has no direct path into cycle time except through overlap.

The PM4Py fragment parses Site A correctly and does put both verification activities before Assessment. The Site B POWL is not a valid model: the same transition objects are reused across branches, the ineligible order contains a cycle through supplement, `legal_loop` and the approve/reject XOR are never connected, and Notify is unordered. A strict partial order would also execute both Approve and Reject. The result does not encode the partial order the prose specifies.
