5.8

The conservative bundle is a real repair thesis, and the write-up keeps the required shape: ev11 is not forced onto one delivery, arithmetic is not treated as proof, derivative O2O edges are later called out as non-independent, and the validation sketch copies the tables, checks referential integrity and duplicates, builds an OCEL, calls the three discovery routines, flattens Order and Delivery, and refuses to treat O2O enrichment as semantic proof. Those merits do not carry the analysis. Several claims that the repair depends on are false, invented, or inconsistent with the repair’s own premises.

The evidence model states that an edge is accepted only with two independent channels, then accepts PR-02 and PR-04 at the same confidence as the E2O edges they merely restate. `PK-8 packs IT-B1` and `DEL-Y carries PK-8` have no manifest, weight, or second observation of their own. Equal 0.85 and 0.80 scores double-count one inference. The repeated claim that 2.9 kg “fits both cables” is not evidence: no item masses are in the log, and `gross_weight` is a static package attribute already compatible with the observed `PK-8 packs IT-A2` edge. “Resolves DEL-Y’s orphan status” and “scanner dropout” are also the wrong justifications. ev08 still has `scanner_ref=PK8-2`; the strong analogy is `PK7-1`↔`PK-7` and `PK8-1`↔`PK-8`. Orphan completion is the motive the answer elsewhere classifies as a reason not to repair.

The ev11 scoring does not propagate Bundle A’s own packing hypothesis. Under that hypothesis `IT-A2` (ORD-A) sits in `PK-8`, so ORD-A is already split across `DEL-X` and `DEL-Y`. “ORD-A is logged, therefore DEL-Y is less plausible” points the wrong way: the observed order is positive evidence for every delivery that carries an ORD-A item. “ORD-B dispatched at 10:00” is likewise unavailable until PR-01 and PR-03 are assumed, and “parcel 1” is not in the data. Leaving ev11 unresolved is still a sound conservative act; the reasons attached to PR-05–PR-07 are not.

The numeric ambiguity is flattened into one story. 100 matches both `PAY-1` and ORD-B’s declared value; 130 matches both `PAY-2` and ORD-A’s declared value; 230 matches the invoice and the sum. Calling 130 the “unpaid balance” already assumes that `PAY-1` is a partial settlement of a consolidated 230 invoice. That alternative, and an ev14↔ORD-A amount match, never appear as candidates. Section 5 then describes INV-44 as issued “for ORD-A ($230),” which quietly assigns ORD-A a value the object table sets at 130.

Bundle C invents `PK-9`. The task forbids new objects. It also never says what happens to ev11, so it is not a complete alternative bundle. Bundle B’s bulk confirmation of both deliveries plus ORD-B is possible under rule 5, but it is adopted because it finishes lifecycles, with almost no account of why the wildcard is both carriers rather than neither, or why the portal row that already names only ORD-A should gain ORD-B.

The downstream section misstates the mechanics it is supposed to compare. Standard `ocel_flattening` keeps events with a direct E2O link to the chosen type. Bundle A never adds ev10 to ORD-B, so Order flattening does not give ORD-B `Dispatch Package` “via PK-8.” ORD-A’s direct events are ev01, ev03, ev04, ev11, and ev12: five, not seven, and that count does not stay stable if the same transitive rule is applied to ORD-A through `IT-A2`/`PK-8`. In Bundle A, Confirm Delivery is not a successor of Dispatch on DEL-X or on ORD-A, because ev11 is related only to ORD-A and neither object carries both activities. The unrepaired Order trace of ORD-B is Create plus Add Item; labeling it an aborted execution, then a new “in-flight shipped” cluster after Bundle A, is completionist and, under direct flattening, factually unchanged. Bundle B’s added ev11 and ev12 edges would change ORD-B; the two Add Item events on ORD-A still block activity-sequence equivalence. The claimed OTG cycle through Order, Package, Item, Invoice, and Payment is not in the O2O graph: Package meets Order only through Item, and Invoice meets Order on a separate branch. “Record Payment → Record Payment” on INV-44 in Bundle B is a real invoice self-succession; calling it a cycle in the OC-DFG, and earlier a Petri-net join, mixes models the outline does not discover.

Smaller compliance and calibration problems add up. Provisional rows in the candidate table are the relations Bundle A then refuses to add, so status does not match the selected repair. The two-channel rule, the anti-completion rule, and the “no new objects” rule are all breached in the artifact that claims to follow them. The code outline itself is the most reliable part of the answer, and it contradicts the transitive lifecycle story in sections 5 and 6.
