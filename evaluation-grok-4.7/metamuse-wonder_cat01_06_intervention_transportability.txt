The transport write-up is disciplined and largely complete: Site A’s −9 h is treated as confounded, statutory ordering is not copied onto the 62% originals cases, legal review and the supplement loop are kept as modifiers, and the pilot distinguishes non-delivery from a failed mechanism. Format, required nodes, column sets, word bounds, and the `conditional go` choice are met. It is not nearly flawless.

The formal model contradicts the intervention it is meant to specify. In §6 and §8, `Originals Receipt -> Document Check` is unconditional, so every case waits for originals and Identity ∥ Document is not enabled. The comment that the edge applies only when the node is “actually executed” is false for this POWL: a strict partial order executes every node. The legal “supplement loop” is `LOOP(Legal Review, Request Supplement)` and never returns to Document Check and Assessment, despite that being the stated dependency. Segment 1 in prose and the partial order are therefore different processes.

The code is unlikely to run. `StrictPartialOrder` exposes `add_edge` / `order.add_edge`, not `add_order`, so construction fails before conversion, soundness, or playout. The surrounding PM4Py calls are otherwise in the right shape, and the comments do name capacity, locks, durations, and data-dependent rules.

The scenario numbers are not tied to a critical path. Identity service is 3.4 h and A’s cleaner signal is a 6 h drop in pre-assessment wait, itself mixed with the API change, +2 FTE, huddles, and mix shift. An eligible cycle-time band of −5 to −8 h, and a population band down to −4 h on a 38% share, sits above that ceiling. The “20–30%” digital, non-legal, low/moderate share is not reconciled with 38%, 40% legal, and 48% high-complexity. Median shifts are also treated as if they dilute linearly.

Smaller faults: `cycle_time -> appeal` is not a mechanism; non-originals is equated with digital; team-week randomization is a poor fit to pools of 4, 7, 3, and 2; power is “several hundred” rather than an MDE. Those do not erase the causal segmentation, but the model error and the unbound scenario math keep this well below a near-flawless mark.
