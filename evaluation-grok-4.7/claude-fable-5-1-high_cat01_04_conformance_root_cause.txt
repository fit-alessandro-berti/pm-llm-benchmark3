The answer is a strong, structured abductive analysis, but it is not nearly flawless. Several quantitative claims contradict each other or the variant census, and those errors sit in the ranking and the conclusion.

The ledger, six-way ranking, staged-repair logic, refusal to treat employees as the primary cause, data-aware versus optional-sign-off distinction, falsifiers, four-phase isolation plan, and governance rules are all substantively good. Fitness figures implied by the variant tables are arithmetically right: June–July 0% → 13.8% → 82.3% → 93.1%, August–September normative 55.6%, optional sign-off 89.5%, over-permissive 94.4%. The process tree places sign-off correctly, and the over-permissive control is the right warning.

The central flaw is attribution. The conclusion says that “on the supplied aggregates” about 26, 23, and 8 points belong to the label change, `Queue Routed`, and ordering. Those shares come from the alignment summary. The same answer’s variant expansion shows about 14, 68, and 11 points, and for August–September a label map alone fits 0% because every trace also has `Queue Routed`. Percentage-point recovery is order-dependent; Phase 2 even warns about that, then Sections 2 and 7 still rank the label change first because it is the “largest” recovery step. That rationale is false on the complete variant counts (620/650 relabeled, 560/650 containing `Queue Routed`).

Related factual slips: E4 treats the DFG count `Risk Screen → Queue Routed` = 530 as the number of traces containing `Queue Routed` (actually 560). E12 says events per case “reproduce almost exactly”; April–May is 5.43 and August–September is 6.78, but June–July is 6.12, not 6.3. Posterior 80 for the ordering defect is called “medium” while it explains roughly one tenth of traces. Section 3’s “joint product” and “each repair removes exactly the move type and nothing else” overstate independence. Section 7 runs past the 400-word limit (about 440). The reported August–September sequence `0% → 48.4% → 55.6%` collapses two repairs and hides the 0-point label-only stage. Claimed alignment precisions (0.921, 0.936, 0.894) are asserted as an execution result; the increase when adding optional sign-off is unexplained and weakens the caveat that permissiveness shows up as a precision drop.

Qualitative verdict (measurement artifact first, residual missing checks and overrides still open, optional sign-off not a compliance proof) survives. The numeric scaffolding used to rank mechanisms does not.
