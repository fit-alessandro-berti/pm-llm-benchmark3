The final recommendation is coherent and well grounded: A4, A5, A6, and A9, with registration deferred because a 1.5h back-office wait and afternoon slack are not the constraint, and with activation kept behind KYC, decision-match, and four-eyes gates. All seven required sections are present, the matrix covers every candidate on the requested dimensions, and the three portfolios plus the hybrid make real trade-offs on cycle time, rework, specialist load, and controls.

Those merits do not make the analysis tight. The directly-follows reading is wrong in places that matter. `KYC check → Credit analysis` (4200) is treated as cases that reach credit analysis without statements, despite the stated concurrency of KYC and document review. `Resolve entity mismatch → Contract preparation` (380) is called an illegal skip without evidence that KYC clearance was absent. Incoming edges to committee packaging are added to 4680 against an activity frequency of 3900 and left unresolved. Waits of 4h, 31h, and 22h are summed as one unlocked chain.

Several claims are arithmetically or causally off. Cutting register wait from 1.5h to 0.2h is called a ~0.13h gain (it is 1.3h). A3, a KYC-hit pre-classifier, is credited with cutting four-eyes breaches, which are a credit-analysis versus committee-packaging segregation failure. Confidence cutoffs are described as “precision.” Section 4 then assigns unsupported point estimates (arrival +15%, remediation 8.5%→~3%, four-eyes 5.8%→<2%, cycle time 25–35%). The final section repeats that class of claim: the hybrid “attacks” the 58h valuation wait and lifts collateralized variants 25–30% even though A7 is not in the four modules, and it asserts ~5,000 extra waiting hours and more freed FTE-hours than A1 without a feasibility-adjusted basis.

The rollout also slips the budget. Phase 3 adds A7 and a full A12 while still counting only A4/A5/A6/A9, and the two deep core-banking slots are scheduled in a way that may cross the first-six-months limit, with an “exception” offered rather than a clean sequence. Exception queues, audit trails, and resource displacement are discussed seriously, but the invented magnitudes and DFG overclaims keep this short of a disciplined process-mining recommendation.
