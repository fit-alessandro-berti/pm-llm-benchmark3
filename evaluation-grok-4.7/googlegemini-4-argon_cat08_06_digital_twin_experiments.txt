6.5

The design covers every required section and is structurally a real digital twin: calendars, endogenous contention, latent objections, architect bursts, district batching, seasonal demand, and a separated PM4Py layer. Several quantitative and methodological errors would still make the twin miscalibrated and would bias the intervention ranking.

**Arrival and mix calibration.** `base_rate_per_day = 57` is the historical average, then peak months multiply by 1.7 and off-peak months stay at 1.0. Under the added assumption that unlabeled months match January–February, that multiplier averages about 1.35, so the simulator generates roughly a third more volume than the 31,200-case log. The Monday factor is closer to neutral only by accident. Top-20 status is drawn for 31% of batches, while those batches have geometric mean size \(1/0.55 \approx 1.82\), so those architects account for about 45% of cases, not 31%. One lognormal value distribution for every case type makes the EUR 50,000 rule in D2 unrealistic for simple renovations. `requires_environment` is true for every D3/D5 case plus every commercial and complex case, which cannot reproduce the variant shares the estimation section says to match.

**Misread empirical inputs.** “19% missing-doc rate on Simple cases” treats a variant-group share of all cases as a conditional probability. Simple renovation is 45% of volume and the two simple groups are 26% and 19%, so the conditional missing-document share is about 42%. The same 19% is reused as the completeness-rework baseline in the decision table. The public-notice mixture (point mass at 14 days, objection probability 0.25, median extra delay 25 days) has a 90th percentile near 44 days, not the stated 60. S3’s “−15% capacity” for one planner and one legal officer is arithmetically wrong: planners lose 12.5%, legal loses 50%.

**Non-stationary experiment design.** Welch deletion assumes a steady state this system does not have. Seasonal phase of \(t=0\), right-censoring of cases still open at the horizon, and length bias from 140–260 day tails are unspecified. A 180-day warmup does not fix that. Common random numbers are specified as separate streams, but the skeleton uses one generator whose draw count changes with policy, so equal seeds do not synchronize policies. D7 changes the global Monday multiplier from 2.2 to 1.3 even though appointment slots apply to architects, who are a minority of submissions. Effect sizes (70% fewer missing documents, 55% less travel, 65% of simple cases exempt, 40% fewer objections) are single invented points, not factors with levels. D2 shrinks the inspection pool that D3 and D4 act on; the combined-policy row does not treat that interaction as a design risk.

**Assumptions stated too strongly.** Council cadence (one week in four), “top 5% urgent,” Friday architect bursts, emergency volume under 1%, a 1.45 district-wait ratio, peak utilization above 92%, and statutory SLAs of 20/60/90/150 days are not in the evidence. Calling those SLAs statutory turns policy choices into fake legal gates. The claim that missing start timestamps do not bias the supplied service-time quantiles conflicts with the stated data gap and with the deconvolution plan. Travel uniforms and the 50% rework-service reduction are usable only as wide sensitivity parameters. Holidays and council dates are state variables, but public notice is never aligned to the council calendar even though complex-variance duration is described as council-dependent. Planning review and decision drafting share planners in the log; the resource model never makes that shared pool explicit.

**Validation and code.** A KS gate on tens of thousands of cases will reject operationally small differences. Utilization cannot be checked against HR calendars that were inputs, and cross-case batch size, same-week architect correlation, and the service-versus-wait split are not validation targets. The PM4Py path is the right shape: `pandas.read_csv`, `format_dataframe`, `parse_process_tree`, variants, both DFGs, and case-arrival average, with an explicit limit on the simplified tree. The run path then samples independent delays, never calls `get_available_capacity`, and leaves district queues empty, so the executable skeleton does not implement the dependency model the prose describes.
