7.5

The portfolio decision is coherent and meets the main quality bar: I-03 is excluded on stage and near-zero effect, I-05 is suppressed as a no-new-event repeat while I-07 re-enters on the new log but still loses on value net of spillover, I-06 is verify-first, and I-04/I-02/I-09 are chosen with one reserve held. Calibration, externalities, label feedback, and monitoring triggers are treated concretely, and the central expected-value arithmetic checks out.

The deployable rule does not match its own application. Section 1 sets Tier 3 at scores in (€500, €1k) and Tier 4 at scores below €500 or suppressed, yet I-01 (+€0.2k), I-10 (+€0.1k), and I-05 (+€0.3k and suppressed) are labeled Tier 3. As written, those cases would not enter the post-15:00 fallback the allocation later uses. Step 2 also says probabilities are adjusted before scoring and that Non-Native-Language cases are scored on a widened lower effect bound; the score uses unadjusted effect midpoints, and I-09 is not widened.

The impact table’s centers are auditable (base gross ≈ €19.9k, cost €4.75k, spillover ≈ €4.3k, net ≈ €10.9k). The published net ranges are not: base “+€6.5k to +€16k” cannot be recovered from the stated benefit, cost, and spillover band, and the optimistic floor is similarly loose. “Consistent with backtests” misuses calibration backtests as support for causal swarm effects. Sequential dispatch (I-02 starts at 12:45) is also not reflected in I-02’s effect. These are decision-relevant defects in a rule and table that claim to be operational and auditable, even though they do not overturn the three-case choice.
