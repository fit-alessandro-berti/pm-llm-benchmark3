7.4

The core language analysis is right and unusually careful: binary-loop semantics, \(L(A)\) and \(L(B)\), invariants \(\#C=\#A\) versus \(\#C=1\), the four-trace intersection, both directions of counterexamples, the P3 counts 76 / 85 / 4, and \(|L_W(\text{simplified})|=13\) all check out. The BFS design is actually exhaustive under the stated bounds, the footprint difference on Check versus Approve is real, and P2 equivalence under the stated \(\alpha\) is proved. Net sizes 9/9/20, 7/7/16, and 6/7/14 match a reduced workflow-net encoding. The simplification target is explicit, and the loss of Check-before-Analyze is disclosed honestly.

Those strengths do not make the write-up nearly flawless.

The structural table claims B’s per-\(n\) trace count grows faster, citing 5/20/60 versus 4/16/56. Unbounded, A has \(4^n\) traces and B has \(2.5n\cdot 2^n\); already at \(n=3\), A has 64 and B 60. The 56 is only the length cutoff inside the P3 window, and even 4→16→56 does not grow faster than 5→20→60. That sentence is false on the answer’s own numbers.

P1 is internally inconsistent. \(\beta\) stores only per-attempt counts, so it also forgets where Approve sits relative to Check, and the formal `compliant` predicate accepts `Open, Analyze, Approve, Check, Close`. Section 3 nevertheless treats that trace as a P1 violation. The non-equivalence verdict still holds because of attempts with \(\#C=0\), but the stated conformance witness and the claim that \(\beta\) forgets “only” Analyze/Check order do not match the definition given in §1.

The simplicity figures 0.8182, 0.7778, and 0.8667 are not a uniform function of the nets described. The first two are exactly \(|P|/(|A|-|P|)\); the third is 13/15, not \(6/(14-6)=0.75\). They are presented as `pm4py.simplicity_petri_net` results. The same problem appears in the “300-trace pilot” EMD \(\approx 0.15\) with supports 93 and 102: specific run output, no derivation. Section 9 then leans on the simplicity gaps.

Smaller faults: the dashboard tree is 7 nodes and therefore 2 operators, not 3; “simpler on every measure” ignores ties on depth, leaves, and B’s transition count; §1 says window agreement lower-bounds distinguishing-trace length, while a 10-event, 4-analysis B trace sits outside \(W\). Section 9 states that limit correctly, which makes the §1 wording a real slip rather than the final position.
