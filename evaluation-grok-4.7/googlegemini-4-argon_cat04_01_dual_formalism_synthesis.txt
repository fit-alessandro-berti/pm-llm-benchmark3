8.5

The POWL model is the right canonical object and is essentially exact under completion-event semantics: asymmetric edges (`Contain → Plan`, `Analyze → Design/approvals/supplier`), trial join only on plan and design, approval and supplier shuffled with the trial, release as the three-way join, high-risk as XOR against an unordered pair, supplier as XOR with `tau`, and `LOOP(Execute→Inspect, Adjust)`. The process tree is a genuine safe under-approximation, the loss is named precisely, and W4–W5 actually separate the two languages. All eight witness verdicts match the models. Loop shape, “at most one supplier,” and “both reviews, either order” are right.

Points come off for precision, not for a broken model.

The witness reasons overclaim overlap. W2 places `Regulatory Review` entirely after both trial cycles; W3 places `Consult Supplier` entirely before plan, design, and the trial. Those are legal linearizations of concurrency, not evidence that the activities overlapped the region. W7’s earliest illegal event is `Release Material` (loop exit after the redo). `Adjust Parameters` is still a legal failure step; the missing `Execute Trial → Inspect Trial` is what breaks D11.

The dependency table stuffs XOR and concurrency into a directed source/target schema. D6 reads like a flow from supervisor review to the high-risk pair. Mutual concurrency of the approval route and supplier consultation is only implied by D12’s grouping.

“Sacrificing only” the two early-start orders is accurate for this cut, but it is not the unique conservative cut. `Register → ((Contain → Plan) ∥ (Analyze → Design)) → (Trial ∥ Approvals ∥ Supplier) → Release → Archive` keeps the cross edges the task highlights and still lets approvals overlap the trial, at the cost of rule 6’s supplier/planning overlap. The chosen cut is defensible and safe; calling it the faithful tree without that comparison oversells it. “100% behavioral fidelity” has the same tone: the argument shows language agreement under the stated atomic-completion abstraction, it does not prove it, and section 8 correctly retreats from that claim.

Smaller nits: unused `pm4py` imports; D11’s source/target layout can be misread as “adjust follows every inspection,” with the exit condition only in the rationale; the validation plan treats token-based replay `trace_is_fit` as decisive on silent transitions and an unbounded loop, where alignments are the real test (they are mentioned, then used as if equivalent).

No major control-flow bug showed up in either construction. The deduction is for over-sharp claims and a few witness/table imprecisions, not for admitting an unsafe trace or missing a required case.
