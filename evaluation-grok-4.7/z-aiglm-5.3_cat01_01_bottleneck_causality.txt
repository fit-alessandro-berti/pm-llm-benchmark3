The assessment is strong on causal discipline: it catches the crude-versus-within-complexity reversal, refuses to treat stratification as identification, separates queue wait from review service, treats the resource group as a routing bundle rather than a clean treatment, and pairs each hypothesis with a falsifying prediction. Format, estimands, and the phased plan are complete.

It is not nearly flawless. The reproducible CSV drops `Review>` from C30, so the directly-follows graph invents a Nova-only `Compliance Check → Request Info` edge, while the script still prints that the edge sets are identical. Nova Low-day cycle time is given as 10.08 (80.6/8) instead of 9.83 (78.6/8), and that figure is reused in the decomposition and the shift hypothesis. Low Nova service is 3.61 rather than 3.64, which slightly shifts the crude and standardized service contrasts. The capacity wedge and the routing pilot overlap in time with no isolation, and a successful capacity change is over-read as proof that the group label was never a cause. The “direct effect is small” wording also blurs an accounting split of cycle time with an identified direct effect.
