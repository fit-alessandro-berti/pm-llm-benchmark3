The policy is structurally strong and meets the required six-section format. It correctly refuses I-03 after Diagnosis Complete, separates I-05’s unchanged repeat from I-07’s new-log re-entry, reserves one slot, and prices spillovers instead of ranking on `p_now`. Case-level lower/midpoint nets and the optimistic and base arithmetic are auditable and mostly accurate.

Material gaps keep it well short of nearly flawless. Calibration offsets are stated and then not used in the scores, so overpredicted Strategic cases I-04 and I-02 are still Tier 1 on raw effect × value. The lower-bound gate ignores the same spillover the rule says to subtract, so I-02’s roughly €1.0k lower-bound net does not clearly survive a per-slot externality, and the marginal third slot is never tested. Adverse spillover is pushed to 0.03–0.05, outside the given 0.01–0.03 model, which makes the adverse net look uniformly largely negative. False-alarm burden is treated as certain on every swarm. Minus and threshold operators are missing from the deployable formulas, and two specialists delivering serial 45-minute swarms are not scheduled. The NNLS “haircut” in monitoring also conflicts with I-09’s unadjusted selection.
