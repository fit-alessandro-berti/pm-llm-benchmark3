4.7

The audit covers the required pipeline shape and correctly treats the source POWL as authoritative, BPMN as a lossy view, and a process-tree intermediate as something that may fail. It is not reliable as an audit. Several central claims and the validation code itself are wrong or vacuous, and results are labeled “validated” without any execution.

The process-tree diagnosis is incorrect and is repeated in the pipeline table, the conversion script, and the canonical-representation section. Assess Risk ∥ Prepare Evidence is ordinary block-structured concurrency. A process tree can express that with AND. What is not series-parallel is the N-shaped dependency: Assess Risk precedes both the optional-legal branch and verification, Prepare Evidence precedes only verification, and those two branches join only at Release. Blaming “unordered concurrency” mis-states why `convert_to_process_tree` is expected to fail and would wrongly predict failure for any AND-split.

The preservation table misuses the required vocabulary. Multiple POWL→Petri cells are marked `validated preserved` while the summary still says conversion is “assumed” and soundness is “to be tested.” `to be tested` and `n/a` are not allowed assessment terms. INV-8 is called `approximated` for the standard LOOP→net construction, which is built to keep the do-once-then-exit-or-redo behavior. Concurrency is also described as places holding multiple tokens; the usual POWL net is 1-safe, with concurrency as several places marked at once.

The invariant table is uneven. INV-2’s negative witness is only “Assess Risk before Register,” which is INV-1. INV-6’s negative witness is “Neither.” INV-12 invents a silent loop inside the legal XOR; `skip_legal` completes that branch. The binary-loop obligation that Correct Defect cannot be the loop’s exit is not given its own invariant. The same visible trace is reused as the positive witness for five different IDs.

The round-trip code would not establish the comparisons it claims. `discover_footprints` returns a dict with keys such as `activities`, `sequence`, and `parallel`, not `.activities` and `.directly_follows`. `convert_to_reachability_graph` returns a transition system, not a `(states, edges)` pair. Both comparisons fall into the exception handlers. Cross-net `im_from_powl == im_from_bpmn` compares markings keyed by distinct place objects, so a behavior-preserving round trip fails it; rejection criterion 6 would quarantine that success. Different reachability-graph sizes are treated as over- or under-approximation even though extra silent routing changes state count without changing the visible language.

The bounded explorer does not do the job specified. Depth counts silent steps, and more than five consecutive `label is None` steps are cut off. POWL operator expansion routinely produces silent chains longer than that, so accepting runs can be discarded before the final marking. Visited markings are stored and never used to cut cyclic tau paths. Negative witnesses are proper prefixes, while only traces that hit the final marking are recorded, so those negatives are absent from the set even when the forbidden prefix is firable. The tests pass vacuously. Witnesses are run only on the source net, not on every round-tripped net. `bpmn_from_tree` is never converted back, and `check_is_workflow_net` is not run on every accepting net. Playout is omitted with no reason. The two scripts are separate; the second uses nets that exist only as globals from the first.

Section 6 and the edit policy are the strongest parts: layout versus gateway semantics, silent routing, loop-exit misreading, POWL as the editable source, and generated Petri/BPMN as derivatives. Those points do not offset an audit whose equivalence evidence is mis-labeled, whose block-structure claim is false, and whose witness, footprint, marking, and reachability checks do not mean what the write-up says they mean.
