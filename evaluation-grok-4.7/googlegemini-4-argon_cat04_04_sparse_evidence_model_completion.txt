6.8

The three candidates are real competing hypotheses, the POWL crown (CC before SC, CC unordered with ER, both checks before ExR, SC unordered with ExR) is the right balanced choice, and the ledger, concurrency-versus-XOR-versus-loop argument, completion-semantics disclosure, and data-collection plan are strong. The write-up is not reliable on the question it is built to answer: which unobserved traces each model admits.

Language sizes are wrong, and the same undercount is repeated as fact. The balanced partial order admits 12 visible traces, not 10. Besides the three extras listed, it also admits `RO, ER, CC, ExR, AC, RP, ArC` and `RO, ER, CC, SC, ExR, AC, RP, ArC`. Those are the ER-before-CC closures of behaviors the text already accepts. The permissive tree admits 18 traces, not 16: shuffle of `X(SC, τ)` with `→(+(CC, ER), X(ExR, τ))` gives 2 + 6 + 2 + 8. The two missing ones are SC before both checks and then ExR (`SC, CC, ER, ExR` and `SC, ER, CC, ExR`). Section 7’s “3 unseen” and “9 unseen,” and Section 8’s `|L_bal| = 10`, `|L_perm| = 16`, inherit that false casework. The state spaces are tiny; this should have been enumerated exactly.

Those counts also do not support the precision intervals. Set precision would be 7/10 = 0.70 and 7/16 ≈ 0.44, both below the stated bands ≈0.80–0.92 and ≈0.65–0.80. Alignment precision can differ, but the intervals are not derived from either definition.

Section 5 calls every interleaving of SC with CC, ER, and ExR a reasonable generalization. Section 6 then labels `RO, SC, CC, ER, …` clearly undesirable and a violation of D5. The permissive boundary therefore does not exclude behavior the audit itself rejects. The “unavoidable” block-tree excuse explains the overshoot; it does not make the boundary consistent. The sensitivity claim is likewise too strong: `L(permissive) \ L(balanced)` contains six traces, and only two are the clean D5 pattern `ER, SC, CC`. Shadowing the permissive model does not isolate that hypothesis.

Smaller faults: the conservative tree has 25 labeled leaves (21 inside the XOR plus RO, AC, RP, ArC), not 23; the ledger says the balanced CC→SC edge is justified “per V5,” but V5 is the evidence that SC need not wait for ER, while CC→SC is the 23/23 pattern; the formal POWL edge set omits edges the code adds (they are transitive, so the language matches, but the two specifications differ); “Overfitting (Inverse Underfitting)” muddles the risk the conservative section is supposed to name. S5’s 5–10% is treated as a share of all 30 cases rather than of supplier-confirmation events.

Fitness 1.0 on the 30 cases is credible for all three models, the excluded-trace constraints that are checked are right, duplicate-label cost is discussed, and the recommendation of the POWL model for use, with a looser model only as a probe, is directionally sound. The repeated admission-set errors are still central, so this cannot score as nearly flawless.
