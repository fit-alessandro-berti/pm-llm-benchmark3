Correct calls include CHG-502 and CHG-508 (same human drafts and approves, with `b.koch` normalized to Ben), CHG-507 (Eva approves a HIGH change on 9 August with no QM delegation in force), CHG-510’s prohibited subdelegation, CHG-501 under D-QM-01, and the conclusion that CHG-505’s retrospective is outside two business days. The serious errors are on the ambiguous cases. CHG-506 is classified AUTHORIZED at high confidence even though Ben was on sick leave, denies the approval, and the session came from an office machine.
