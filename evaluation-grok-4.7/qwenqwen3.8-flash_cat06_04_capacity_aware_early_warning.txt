7.2

The final policy is structurally complete and substantively strong: all six required sections are present, every case is classified, I-03 is correctly excluded because the swarm is ineffective after Diagnosis Complete, I-05 is suppressed as a refresh with no new evidence while I-07 remains eligible because of the new log, and spillovers, segment calibration, and training-label feedback are treated explicitly. The scenario arithmetic is mostly auditable and internally consistent (base direct net about €16.6k, cost €1,350, midpoint spillover €7.2k, net about €8k; adverse case correctly goes negative).

The score is held down by decision-rule contradictions and an under-specified ranking function. Section 1 says a repeated alert with no material new event and calibrated change below 0.10 is suppressed for swarm allocation. I-02 moves only about 0.08 and I-09 about 0.07, with no stated new event, yet both are selected. The same section’s deployable score (median net minus spillover minus an unquantified uncertainty penalty, times an arbitrary confidence weight) is not computed per case, so the table’s tiers are not reproducible. On that median-net basis, I-08 is about €4.6k before spillover and still ahead of I-02 after a €2.4k spillover share, while its lower bound is negative; excluding it by a 0.35 probability floor is a different rule from the one used to rank the selected cases, and that conflict is not shown. Section 4’s escalation trigger (`p_adj_low ≥ 0.70` and under 4 hours) also fits I-07, whose allocation is “wait.”

Smaller issues: the €20k untreated-case value, ±0.05/±0.10 calibration widenings, 0.10 evidence delta, and 4-hour cool-down are policy choices presented as if they were tighter than the evidence; the base “benefit range” is a point; the optimistic net range is built from the midpoint rather than the stated €17k–€25k benefit band; false-alarm burden is deducted in full even in the optimistic scenario; and non-monetary public-sector exposure on I-01 is thin relative to the reserve logic used for the unnamed regulatory case.
