The three models are genuine competing hypotheses, the trace audit is arithmetically correct, and the validation oracle matches those languages. The positive/negative suite separates the three languages at the right boundaries.

The permissive inductive-bias paragraph is wrong about its own construction. Deleting only the balanced edge `CC → SC_opt` also deletes the transitive constraint `RO → SC`. That poset allows `Supplier Confirmation` before `Register Order`. The process tree does not: it keeps `RO` before the parallel block. The tree is the right artifact; the “single edge collapses into this tree” claim is not.

Section 10 relocates the 32% calculation from 8 `ER`-first `SC` traces onto “a 30-trace log.” The precision bands 0.85–0.98 and 0.70–0.90 are presented as if estimated; they are guesses (alignment precision is also not \(|L_{log}|/|L_{model}|\)). “Minimal” partial-order generalization overclaims uniqueness. Those defects are local. The delivered nets, the 7/12/18 counts, the excluded-trace constraints, and the data-collection plan stay coherent.
