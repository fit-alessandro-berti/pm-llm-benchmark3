7.5

The design is careful and largely fact-faithful: verified merges are limited to e03/e04 and e11/e16, e05 and e14 are kept, e08/e09 stay split as a harmonization collision, and e15 is not given inferred payment links. Event counts 15 / 14 / 10 and E2O counts 39 / 37 / 28 match those decisions under a union policy. Semantic rules are separated from ingestion and stable tie-breaks, and the `have_common_object=True` failure on e13/e14 is explained correctly.

The implementation does not fully carry that design. On the stated `eid` / `timestamp` / `activity` schema, alias copies of `ocel:eid` and `ocel:activity` go stale: relation replacement updates only the alias, so `drop_duplicates` does not collapse merged E2O rows, and label refinements can miss the original activity column. Validation checks foreign keys only, so those duplicate relations pass. The stated “namespace and retain” rule for scanner and billing attributes is not implemented; same-name conflicts are dropped from the event and kept only in the audit. Ordering is a hardcoded rank, not the partial-order procedure described, and `tie_group_id` is the whole timestamp, including pairs already ordered by domain rules. The synthetic microsecond export is flagged, but it still rewrites every tied timestamp, including edges that are only a serialization.
