The review is strong, disciplined, and largely accurate. It separates exploration from enforcement, refuses to collapse the metrics into one score, and evaluates both models from concrete traces rather than headline numbers. Probe judgments for B1–B8 match the process-tree languages: Model S optionalizes the mandatory checks and allows Expedite Review first, while Model C is an eight-trace catalog that misses valid combinations such as B2 and B5.
