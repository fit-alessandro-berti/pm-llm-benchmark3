7.6

The final answer is a serious, well-structured conformance review: all seven required sections are present, all 15 cases are classified with a dominant reason, both briefs are genuinely argued, and the numerical evidence is traced accurately. It is not nearly flawless.

The main logical break is SUP-702. Section 1 puts the burden of an exception on documented authority and complete eligibility, and it treats PD-3 approval as disputed and automated master-data validation as unproven. Section 5 nevertheless classifies SUP-702 as JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. Medium confidence does not repair that mismatch. The same gap reappears in the system claim that the 289 apparently eligible auto-approvals are justified only if master-data validation is proven.

The revised POWL has a concrete semantic miss. `Request_More_Info` is placed entirely before route selection, so sanctions and quality cannot interleave with it. SUP-707, the case used to justify the loop, is Register → Sanctions → Request_More_Info → Request_More_Info → Quality. M1 rejects that trace. The code discloses only the zero-or-one cardinality fallback, not this ordering limitation, despite the instruction to state awkward approximations explicitly.

Making `Automated_Master_Data_Validation` a mandatory visible transition has the same effect: the pilot traces treated as justified do not conform to M1, and that consequence is not stated. SUP-711 is labeled DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE while the text says, with high confidence, that the duplicate is a known migration artifact and the underlying process is compliant. Those cannot both be the conclusion. Rejection analysis also blends two different questions: whether rejected leads are in scope, and whether M0’s quality-before-decision rule is stricter than CS-9’s quality-before-activation rule.

The architecture, overlay table, identity and notification schema, and separation of POWL from data and resource rules are strong. Those merits do not offset the inconsistent exception burden or the revised model’s failure to accept the behavior it was built to allow.
