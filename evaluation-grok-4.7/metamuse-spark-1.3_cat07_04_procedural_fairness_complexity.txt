7.5

The fairness argument is careful, numerically faithful to the tables, and consistent with the constraints: raw approval gaps are not treated as discrimination, case-mix is not treated as a full excuse, `risk_score_band` is not treated as neutral, and language/accommodation steps are analyzed as sources of burden rather than as steps to remove. All eight required sections are present, the adjusted residuals are used in the right places, and the legitimate-versus-concerning split (especially B’s language queue and C’s accommodation queue versus D’s mostly risk-linked fraud path) follows the evidence.

The score is held down by concrete defects. The PM4Py plan would not implement the analysis it describes. Within-variant durations are computed on the unfiltered group log, with the V1 restriction left as a comment. `filter_case_performance` is called with `min_case_performance` / `max_case_performance` rather than the actual performance arguments. `get_all_case_durations` is imported from a non-existent `pm4py.stats`, and `discover_declare(..., min_support=0.8)` on the full log cannot test the attribute rule that high risk requires Fraud Review. Handover networks and `get_service_time` are named, not stratified or applied. `filter_paths_performance` is not aimed at the bottleneck edges that carry the fairness claim.

There is also a reasoning inconsistency. Section 3 says adjustment “should have absorbed” B’s incompleteness, while Section 5 correctly warns that conditioning on `documentation_completeness` can absorb a language-barrier pathway. `prior_case_count` is used as a matching variable even though, especially for D, it may be downstream of earlier denials, appeals, and rework; the sensitivity design never treats it as a bad control. Adjusted gaps are ranked as if they were precise (“largest,” “largely attenuate”) without noting that no standard errors are available, and “extra-review” attenuation is not directly observable because raw supervisor-review and missing-document rates are not given. “Near-parity” for C’s −2.2 pp approval gap is looser than the estimate. Section 8 never actually lands on fair, unfair, or indeterminate; “concerning” is a hedge beside an otherwise well-calibrated residual claim.
