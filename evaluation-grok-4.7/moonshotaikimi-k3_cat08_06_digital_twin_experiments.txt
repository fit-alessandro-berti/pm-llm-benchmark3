The design covers every required section and the main constraints: non-stationary clustered arrivals, resource calendars, a waiting/service split, latent objections, a baseline plus D1–D10 with levels and trade-offs, four stress overlays, periodic warm-up, replication and sensitivity design, and a PM4Py layer kept apart from the simulation. The lognormal quantile match, fork/join before decision drafting, and refusal to treat the run as an optimum are sound.

The score stays at 7 because several design and code claims are wrong or internally inconsistent.

Arrival volume is double-counted. The NHPP is fit to all submissions, then cluster offspring are added on top of those parents, so generated demand exceeds the calibrated rate. The top-20 architects’ 31% share is never imposed as a constraint.

Missing start times are treated as missing at random within an activity. The brief says some resources record only completions, so missingness is systematic by resource. The removed emergency cases are also asserted to bias the tail downward without evidence of their direction.

Common random numbers are the basis for the paired confidence intervals in the selection rule, but each policy changes how many random draws the routing and rework logic consumes. Without separate streams, those paired intervals are not actually paired. Welch/MSER warm-up checks on raw weekly series will follow the seasonal cycle, so they do not identify the end of the transient unless the series is de-seasonalized.

The reference tree’s redo labels (`Completeness rework`, `Inspection rework`) do not appear in the log; the observed pattern is a repeated completeness check. Conformance is named later, but the skeleton still treats that tree as the process backbone. Appeal for complex variance is folded into legal rework, so council-calendar dependence is only a legal blackout. District bundling is described as baseline behavior and as intervention D3. Two simple-only planners and the Monday portal clerk are named, then implemented as undifferentiated pools. The public-notice component is “14 days plus noise,” which can fall below the fixed minimum. Case ownership visible in the excerpt (the same planner drafts the decision) is ignored. Fire “2 FTE equivalent, Tuesday–Thursday only” is implemented as two people on those three days, with no statement of which reading of FTE was chosen. The fairness cap of 1.25 is applied as a hard filter even though D3/D5 travel can make the baseline violate it. The temporal holdout does not handle cases that cross the split, despite tails of 260 days.

In the skeleton, `{"arrival_params": {...}}` builds a set, so `ArrivalGenerator(**...)` cannot run. `wait_from_prev_event_s` is the inter-completion gap (waiting plus service), which contradicts the identification argument in the estimation section. The router fires a review when the flag is true or a fresh probability draw succeeds, so flagged and unflagged cases are both inflated relative to the stated routing rule. Weekday is `day % 7` from an origin of 2026-01-01 (a Thursday), so the calendars do not land on the stated working days. The clerk calendar line includes a dead `Calendar(8.0 and 8.0, 16.0)` branch. PM4Py calls themselves (`format_dataframe`, `get_variants`, `discover_dfg`, `discover_performance_dfg`, `get_case_arrival_average`, guarded `parse_process_tree`) match the requested conventions.

The result is a usable consulting outline with the right experiment and uncertainty vocabulary, not a coherent, calibrated twin specification.
