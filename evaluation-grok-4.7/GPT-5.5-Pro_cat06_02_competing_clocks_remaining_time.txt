The forecast is structurally sound: the join is treated as a maximum, Friday chemistry is correctly shown to spill, Sunday microbiology cannot be actioned by QA, and the temperature-excursion dependence is not multiplied through as independence. Clock arithmetic for the headline ranges (about 66–68 h, 75–93 h, 100–123 h, 136–267 h) matches the stated dates. The deductions below are why this is not a near-flawless forecast.

The earliest terminal time, Monday 21 September 09:30, stacks boundary outcomes whose joint probability is negligible: triangular chemistry at its minimum (density zero at 1.5 h), a Friday start late enough that minimum service still clears only by assuming the second analyst is free early, a zero QA queue despite roughly three expected arrivals on top of five resident batches and only 8.5 reviewer-hours before Monday, and QA service well below the 1.5 h median. A credible lower edge is later on Monday, closer to late morning or midday. Section 2 (09:30–11:00) and Section 4 (09:30–11:30) do not even agree on that edge.

Scenario “central” ranges blend nested paths. The chemistry-rerun center runs to Wednesday 23 September, and the microbiology-confirmation center to Wednesday evening, by folding in deviation investigations that occur only in a 0.30–0.55 subset. The conditional center of a rerun or a confirmation without an investigation is earlier. Tails are also unreconciled: the rerun tail stops Monday 28 September, the severe-path tail reaches Wednesday 30 September, and the P90 band stops Tuesday 29 September 18:00, with no statement of which quantile is which.
