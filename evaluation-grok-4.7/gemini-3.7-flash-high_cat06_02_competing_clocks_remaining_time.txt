The write-up has the right skeleton: weekend gates, a parallel join as a maximum, separate rework paths, and ranges rather than a single timestamp. Those choices are then undercut by arithmetic and consistency failures large enough to move several scenario clocks by hours to a day.

The chemistry story is over-compressed. Triangular service is replaced by a mode remnant of about 1.7 h, so risk-priority completion is stated as Monday 09:30–11:30 and FIFO as 14:30–17:00. Those are interior slices. Fast risk-priority service can be back near Monday 08:30, and a long draw still fits before 18:00 only for some starts. Section 3 then treats chemistry as the smooth-path maximum in both disciplines. With no weekend readout (probability about 0.55–0.75), microbiology finishes Monday 08:30–09:30, so the fast chemistry branch is co-dominant or microbiology-limited. The scenario table says “co-dominant”; the decomposition does not.

The FIFO rerun clock is wrong. A primary finish near 15:30 plus a modal 1.8 h priority rerun ends about 17:18, inside Friday-to-Monday lab hours, and a 14:30 primary finish absorbs even the 3.0 h maximum before 18:00. The claim that the rerun “cannot finish before 18:00” and therefore resumes Tuesday 08:30–10:00 describes only a late-primary, long-rerun tail, but that tail is written into the central range (through Tuesday 14:00).

Microbiology confirmation drops the weekend on-call branch. Readiness is Sunday 12:00–20:00, and authorization probability is 0.25–0.45, with dispatch plus readout of 1–3 h. An indication found Sunday afternoon plus 20 h of confirmation can be read Monday morning, and the join is then often still the chemistry queue. The tabulated earliest terminal time, Tuesday 14:30, is therefore not the earliest plausible confirmation path. The Sunday readout window 14:00–21:00 also misses a 54 h incubation that is ready at 20:00 and read as late as 23:00.

QA congestion is asserted and then not used. Five batches, 8.5 weekend reviewer-hours, mean service near 2.2–2.5 h, and about three arrivals leave on the order of four batches at Monday 08:00. The stated net drain of about 0.55 batches per hour with two reviewers takes most of Monday to clear, so a morning join waits several hours. The decomposition instead uses a 0.5–2.0 h wait for Monday arrivals generally. That understates the risk-priority and early-rerun paths and makes Monday 11:30–13:00 release look more available than the answer’s own queue model allows. Overdispersion is named and then ignored. “QA closed for ~60+ hours” is also wrong: Saturday 09:00–13:00 is staffed; the 62 h closure is the chemistry lab.

Rework calendars slip. Twenty QA working hours from Monday 15:00 are 5 h Monday, 12 h Tuesday, and 3 h Wednesday, so Wednesday about 11:00, not Wednesday 16:00–19:30. Documentation-query terminals in §3 (Tuesday 11:00–18:30) do not match §2 (central Tuesday 11:00–16:30, tail through Wednesday 11:00). Section 4’s earliest release, Monday 11:30, is earlier than every scenario earliest in §2, including the smooth path’s Monday 13:00.

Dependence is acknowledged, then over-precision and contradiction replace it. The uplift of rerun probability to 0.25–0.40 and contamination to 0.15–0.30 is an unlabeled invention, and “tail underestimated by 40–60%” has no shown basis. Section 5 says the excursion “triples” reruns and “doubles” contamination; those factors do not match either the problem baselines (rerun 0.10–0.20, contamination 0.05–0.12) or the ledger. Section 6 is stricter still: clearing the flag drops reruns to ≤10% and contamination to ≤5%, then lifts Monday release above 75% and, if confirmed, moves the center to Wednesday/Thursday with Monday release under 15%. Residual baseline rates alone forbid ≤5% and ≤10%. A 0.30–0.55 investigation probability still leaves a large non-investigation mass on Monday/Tuesday, so the median does not move to Wednesday/Thursday.

Rejection at 7–12%, and release at 88–93%, is presented as an aggregate probability without a path calculation. A contamination indication is not yet a confirmed critical defect.

The useful core that remains is the Monday floor, FIFO versus risk-priority as a same-day swing, investigation length as the far tail, and the excursion flag as a real update trigger. The secondary Monday handoff trigger is well chosen. The forecast ranges in §4 are in a plausible neighborhood only because they are wide enough to cover the decomposition errors, not because the stage clocks reconcile.
