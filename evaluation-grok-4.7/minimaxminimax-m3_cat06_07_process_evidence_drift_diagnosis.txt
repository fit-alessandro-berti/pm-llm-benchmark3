The audit correctly separates the period into distinct regimes: April is a schema and pipeline failure, June is a V2 mix shift that does not require concept drift, August–September combine a parts-path performance change with variant mix and (from September) Partner Z, and October’s 0.16 breach rate is not comparable because maturity is 55% and long paths are censored. The six required sections, falsifiers, training-month split, alert dependencies, and wrong-diagnosis risks are present and mostly well aimed.

The label-maturity analysis is not consistent. The same censoring mechanism is treated as pushing Partner Z’s September rate of 0.45 upward, while October is correctly described as biased downward because longer, breach-prone cases are unlabeled. Slower maturity under-represents those cases, so 0.45 is not evidence of an inflated gap; equal-vintage comparison is required before signing the bias. That error sits on the criterion the task emphasizes.

ECE is repeatedly treated as a signed quantity (“turns negative,” “flips negative,” “ECE |.|”). The reported ECE values are positive; the signed fact is mean prediction minus observed rate (over-prediction in April and June, under-prediction in August–September). June’s ECE of 0.060 and July’s AUROC of 0.76 are also called near baseline despite baseline ECE near 0.02 and AUROC near 0.81. Discrimination held up better than calibration in June, but the mix shift was not fully absorbed: predicted risk stayed near 0.20 while the observed rate fell to 0.15. May’s residual gap is asserted as the unrecomputed April cache, although the audit only establishes that the cache was not fully backfilled for April.

The code sketch names the requested pm4py calls and avoids a fake drift detector, but the case-start cohort is not implemented: `mode="traces"` is not a filter on first-event month, every month is cut off on the 28th, `get_variants` values are used as counts, temporal features are computed after discarding the prefix and keeping only the diagnosis row, and the emitted summary stores edge counts rather than performance-DFG times. Immediate containment by rolling back to the March artifact also misses the live problem: August–September under-prediction is stale-model concept drift, so rollback does not add a V3 safety margin.
