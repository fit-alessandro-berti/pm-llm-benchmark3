7.5

The assessment meets the required structure and the main constraints: it refuses to treat raw approval gaps as discrimination, does not wave away residual gaps as case mix, treats risk scores as suspect, and keeps language support and accommodation review as possible sources of burden rather than steps to delete. The adjusted figures, variant shares, queues, and staffing concentrations are generally used accurately, and the split conclusion—indeterminate on discriminatory intent, unfavorable on timeliness and procedural burden—is appropriately calibrated.

The score is held down by a real identification error and several overstatements. Mechanism 3 attributes Group D’s +5.7 pp adjusted appeal gap to high prior-case count, but that variable is already in the adjustment set, so it cannot explain the residual. Group D’s fraud-path justification is said to rest entirely on `risk_score_band`, although prior cases, documentation, channel, region, and month are also controlled. The 3.8-day Language Support → Eligibility wait is tied to variant V4 even though V4 does not contain that direct edge. “Near-zero” notification differences overstate a 0.5-versus-0.8-day gap.

The PM4Py plan covers the requested techniques, but the code would not run as written: performance-DFG edge values are unpacked as dictionaries and then referenced as `wait_A`/`wait_B`, `v4_signature` is undefined, the path filter is given a list rather than an activity pair, and `get_service_time` is described as per-activity output. The plan also claims a single completion timestamp can separate agency queues from applicant waiting, which this log cannot support.
