7.5

The answer is complete, well calibrated, and structurally compliant: every required network, hypothesis, risk, analysis, and recommendation is present, centrality is not treated as inherently good or bad, bots and vendors are kept in view, and removal is refused unless the coordination function is replaced. The handover-versus-co-presence reconciliation is conceptually right, the absence result is mostly kept in the tail, and misconduct is not inferred from discretion.

It is not nearly flawless. The main quantitative reconciliation is invalid under the denominator it uses. If co-case rates are shares of about 4,100 claims, Alice–Carla at 27% is about 1,107 cases, which exceeds Alice’s 665 cases; the same bound fails for the other team-core pairs. The conditional “8% ≈ 328 cases ≈ 36% of Mira’s cases” is therefore not an available reading, yet it is used to soften her apparent peripherality. The same answer is strict about handover counts (outgoing edges sum to 1,640 against 960 events) but does not apply that skepticism to subcontracting: 126 and 98 return loops require far more repeat Mira events than 960 events across 905 cases allow. Those two figures are then used together as if both were reliable.

Smaller validity problems add up. `filter_variants` is aimed at resource paths, although it filters activity sequences. Subcontracting betweenness is treated as evidence of Mira’s own send-and-return loops. “Primarily the exception-to-specialist boundary” does not match the edge weights, which run about 1,250 to adjusters and 390 to Omar and Priya. The absence finding is also widened from an unchanged median for ordinary low-value claims to ordinary or routine claims “remaining stable,” including in the executive summary.
