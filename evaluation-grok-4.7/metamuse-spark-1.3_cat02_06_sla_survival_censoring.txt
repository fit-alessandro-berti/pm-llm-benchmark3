Defects that keep this well below a top score.

1. Process diagram versus the prefixes. The text says observed moves, then states `AutoCheck -> Review` only “via Screen.” S15 and S17 are direct `AutoCheck>Review`. That quantity is not “higher than actual CIF_resolve”; survival and cumulative incidence are different functions, and net survival can sit below the resolve CIF. The next sentence has the operational bias direction right (hypothetical resolve probability inflated, E1 breach understated), but the comparison itself is wrong.

3. Transfer leverage on the cohort contrast is overstated. Pre-horizon transfers are one per cohort (S08 at 68h, S21 at 26h). S32 at 96h is already a known E1 non-resolution at 72h under both censor-at-transfer and competing-event coding. “B has more transfers” (2 vs 1) therefore does not, by itself, flip the 72h classification contrast. S21’s earlier censoring can reweight the curve; that is a different and smaller claim than the one written.

4. S10 (gap 19h) is grouped with “active” sub-72h cases beside gaps of 2–6h, while the same answer treats gaps above about 12h as a stall signal and flags gaps ≥30h as informative. S25 (open at 79h) is omitted from the concrete open-breach list that otherwise names S09, S11, and S26.
