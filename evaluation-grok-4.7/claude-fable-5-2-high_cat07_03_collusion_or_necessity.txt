7.5

The assessment is well calibrated and meets the required structure. It separates clusters, treats co-working frequency as non-indicative, keeps emergency handling and data-quality problems in play, and does not accuse anyone. Priorities, benign explanations, and the change-the-assessment evidence are concrete and mostly tied to the given figures. Section 6 is within length and uses appropriately cautious wording.

The score is held down by analytical defects, not tone.

- The adjusted-identity logic overwrites resources too broadly: every approval with `approved_by` and every other event with `entered_by` is reassigned. That does not correct the stated `legacy_erp` bot/human mapping, and it can manufacture differences. The claim that cases unique to the adjusted view “were masked by data quality” is stronger than the method supports.
- The retrospective-justification check takes the minimum timestamp but the resource of the first unsorted row, so the same-person flag can attach to the wrong event. Inverted justification-before-override order is not flagged.
- The mined DECLARE model is never replayed, despite the comment that it will be. `always_after` is described as if it contained pairs; in a log skeleton it is a map from activity to a set.
- Several calls are written as executable PM4Py but pass arguments the library expects inside `parameters` (`resource_key`, `keep_violations`, `keep`, `n`, `return_diagnostics_dataframe`). `filter_between` plus max-minus-min is also a weak measure of the bank-change-to-payment gap.
- Section 6 says all 44 Lena/Kim cases had a bank change and payment “shortly afterwards,” and it presents 17 same-user justifications as procedural failures before making clear that 12 are batch-upload artefacts. Section 1 itself warns that the 19-minute and 17-minute figures may measure different things.
- Indicator 14 reads “Emergency Override → Approve PO” as evidence that the buyer performed the override. The fragment only shows the path in those joint cases.
- High-priority clusters 1–3 overlap inside the same 44 cases, and the plan never says to review the union once.

Numbers that can be checked (shares, 510/660, 44/240, loop rates, 17−12, non-emergency residuals) are accurate, and the V-77x cluster is correctly ranked as the least explained exposure rather than as collusion.
