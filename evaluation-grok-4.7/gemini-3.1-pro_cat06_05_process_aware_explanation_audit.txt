The audit correctly drops the obvious post-outcome fields (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `full_trace_variant_id`, `final_decision`, `num_document_requests_total`), keeps queue length and elapsed time with the right signs, and repairs the extraction-date count, the mutable risk score, and the nightly document snapshot. Coverage of the required rows, the JSON shape, and the recompute-versus-invalidate split are sound. It is still not a credible process-aware audit, because several central distinctions are wrong or invented.

The sequence findings flatten three different situations into “critical future leakage, remove.” `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is unavailable at `t0` and is not a cause of breach, but it is a normative reconsideration path after Partial or Deny. `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is the opposite: a terminal-activity violation, and in Case Z an unordered same-timestamp pair with a missing sequence index. `Documents Requested -> Documents Received` and `Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded` are ordinary later flow; their negative contributions are retrospective associations, not prefix signals and not conformance defects. `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened` is a future-possible parallel branch. Calling fraud investigation “the target event” misstates the label, which is the 10-day settlement breach. Section 5 then recommends filtering “future” patterns out of the sequence explainer, which would delete the real process from any retrospective model. Section 2 does say that a second `Settlement Decision Recorded` is legitimate after appeal and that a frequent DFG edge is not a rule, so the write-up contradicts itself.

`initial_reserve` is treated as frozen at or before coverage check and then used as a credible driver for Case Y. Nothing in the lineage supports that timestamp. `final_handler_team` is told to be deleted, with no account of the 65% overlap with the team at `t0` or of a point-in-time assignment as the repair. `total_case_duration_hours` is called a mathematical copy of the target; closure duration can extend past the settlement event that defines the 10-day breach. `prior_claim_count_before_t0` is only described as leaking later claims; `claim_created_at <= extraction_date` can also count the current claim. `calendar_week` is cleared in the table as a low-severity legitimate proxy, while the notes tie weeks to a policy change and holidays, and Case Z’s week 52 never appears in the uncertainty note.

The revised narratives keep admissible candidates, but they reuse directions from the contaminated model after marking the probabilities for recomputation. Those directions are not identified as provisional. Queue length is called actionable for load balancing; importance at `t0` does not establish that lever. Region is narrowed to a resource-bottleneck mechanism even though it also tracks language, channel, and socioeconomic mix. The four standards — predictive validity, causal interpretation, operational actionability, and fairness — are only implied by the may/may-not lines.

The validation suite names the right checks and is not concrete: no as-of assertions, no null-sequence-index quarantine, no temporal split, and no separation between “illegal in the normative model” and “legal but after `t0`.” Alignment, footprint, and DFG limits are mentioned thinly. Schema and process drift are reduced to watching week and queue distributions.
