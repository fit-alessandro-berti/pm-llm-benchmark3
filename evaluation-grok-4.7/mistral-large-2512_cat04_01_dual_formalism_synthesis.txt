3.5

The write-up covers every required section and roughly understands the narrative, but both models are semantically unreliable, the prose repeatedly describes a different model from the code, and several verdicts are false.

The process tree is not a conservative under-approximation. As constructed, `Plan Verification` is not after `Contain Material`, the risk route and `Consult Supplier` are not after `Analyze Root Cause`, and `Execute Trial` / `Release Material` do not wait for `Design Corrective Action`. `Contain Material` can even finish after `Release Material`. That admits unsafe orders while Section 6 calls the initial streams, split dependencies, and risk route exact. The silent node is an empty `XOR`, not a leaf, and the same object is shared by the supplier choice and the low-risk sequence, so the value is not a tree. Parent links are explicitly left incomplete. The fenced expression does not match the Python: the code places supplier and risk in parallel, while the approximation paragraph says supplier must finish before the risk route.

The POWL code does not run and would not mean the right thing if the operators were rewritten by hand. `Operator` is never imported; `OperatorPOWL` accepts only `XOR` and `LOOP`, not `PARALLEL` or `SEQUENCE`. Optional consultation is a two-cycle between `Consult Supplier` and a silent node, which is not an XOR skip, so consultation is mandatory and the order is not partial. `tau_risk` is unused and unconstrained. Children of `risk_choice` and `trial_loop` are also top-level nodes, so reviews, `Execute Trial`, `Inspect Trial`, and `Adjust Parameters` are not bound by those operators and need not follow `Register Deviation` or precede `Archive Case`. The claim that this POWL is exact is untenable.

The witness table inherits those errors. A3 is a legal interleaving of the tree they wrote, but it is marked forbidden. A3 also cannot expose “supplier before risk,” because consultation already precedes `Supervisor Review`. A5’s reason calls an after-the-trial approval an overlap. F2 is forbidden by the tree only because the approval route is missing, not because of containment; the tree does not enforce the cited cross-branch edge. Every “POWL allowed” cell is wrong once mandatory consultation, the extra top-level copies, and the invalid operators are taken into account.

Section 2 mislabels ordinary precedence as start-start, uses `N/A` as a relation, and omits the approval–trial and consultation overlap constraints. Section 7 then says both models must accept all five intended traces, contradicting Section 5, and treats an optional transition as allowed to be dead. The self-critique says the loop can be bypassed and that partial-order edges cannot keep `Adjust Parameters` inside the loop before release; neither claim is how these operators work.
