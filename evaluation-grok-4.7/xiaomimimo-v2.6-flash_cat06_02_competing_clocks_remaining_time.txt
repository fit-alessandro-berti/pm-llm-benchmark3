7.4

The forecast is structurally strong: the join is treated as a maximum, weekend calendars are applied separately to chemistry, microbiology, and QA, Friday chemistry completion is correctly ruled out, queue discipline is kept as a mixture, excursion risk is handled as a common cause, and the Monday/Tuesday bimodality plus the Wednesday–Thursday dead zone are real insights. The QA reviewer-hour ledger and the point that weekend on-call authorization does not move the smooth-path join are defensible.

It is not nearly flawless. The triangular chemistry service median is stated as 3.23 h and called a correct quantile; for triangular(1.5, 2.5, 4.5) the median is about 2.77 h, and that wrong value is then used as the central service time. Several required scenario-table hour labels do not match their own clock times by 1–2 h (for example Tuesday 10:00–11:00 is 90.5–91.5 h, not 88.5–89.5 h; Tuesday 14:15 is about 94.8 h, not 92.8 h; the smooth-path Tuesday-morning tail is about 89–90.5 h, not 87–88.5 h). On-call readout is given as Sunday 13:00–Monday 02:00; readiness at Sunday 20:00 plus 3 h ends Sunday 23:00. The 0.02–0.08 rejection probability is not supported by the scenario, and the ledger’s justification does not actually defend it. Scenario S2 also blurs ordinary FIFO, which still finishes Monday, with the smaller Tuesday spill while assigning the whole band a Tuesday-midday terminal.
