**Quantitative errors (section 2).** J1 and J2’s B1 waits (3 h 55 min, 3 h 40 min) match the table, but the other required calculations do not.
- J1’s shipment delay is computed as 13:30 − 08:10 = 5 h 20 min, i.e. pack time minus morning batch readiness. Consolidation acts after packing: S1 dispatches at 19:25, so the pack-to-dispatch wait is 5 h 55 min. The counterfactual (“packed earlier”) reverses the mechanism.
- CAL-3 for J6 is given as 17:25 − 17:20 = 5 min. That is the gap after recalibration. J6 is ready at 16:05, TEST-1 is idle from 16:35, and CAL-3 is busy 16:35–17:20. The plausible CAL-3 block is about 45–50 minutes, not 5.
