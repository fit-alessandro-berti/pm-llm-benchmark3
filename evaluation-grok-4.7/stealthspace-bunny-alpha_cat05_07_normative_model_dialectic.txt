6.4

The briefs, burden of proof, and most case calls are strong, but the answer is not nearly flawless. It contradicts itself on a core POWL fact, misstates one trace, invents a deadline, and the required model is not a valid executable POWL.

**What holds up.** Authority, expiry, and burden of proof are handled carefully. PD-3 and FD-7 are treated as real, conditional, time-bounded overlays rather than as amendments to CS-9 or as informal habit. SUP-701, SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-706, SUP-712, SUP-713, SUP-714, and SUP-715 are classified with the right dominant reason, including non-control-flow failures that fit M0. Aggregate counts are not inflated into “all 312 auto-approvals were unlawful,” and desirable RFI behavior is not treated as a retroactive policy change. The prosecution concedes model and logging defects; the defense concedes SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-708, SUP-712, and SUP-714. The data-collection proposal (immutable `occurred_at`/`recorded_at`, stable `notification_id`, eligibility snapshots, alias and delegation records, overlay binding) matches the evidence problems actually in the file.

**Model semantics, wrong.** Section 1.1 correctly says M0 requires both Sanctions_Check and Quality_Review. In the given POWL, `checks` is a `StrictPartialOrder` over both activities and must complete before the XOR. The reject child does not skip quality. Section 3.1 then says M0 lets a sanctions rejection omit Quality_Review, and sections 3.5, 4, and 5 say SUP-709 “precisely matches” M0. It does not: the trace has no Quality_Review. That case is an M0 misfit whose CS-9 and scope status is still unproved, because CS-9 ties both checks to activation and leaves rejected leads ambiguous. Calling it MIXED because “the trace fits M0, so no control violation is proved” rests on a false fitness claim. This is also an internal contradiction, not a close interpretive dispute.

**Trace error.** SUP-708 is Register, Quality_Review, Activate_Supplier, Sanctions_Check, Manual_Procurement_Approval, Notify, Close. The matrix says quality, activation, and approval all precede sanctions. Approval follows sanctions. The sanctions-before-activation breach is real and correctly judged NONCOMPLIANT, but the write-up drops the independent CS-9 breach that manual approval also occurs after activation.

**Invented rule.** N3 requires Quality_Review within two business days, daily monitoring, and a EUR 20,000 cap until retrospective approval. It does not set a two-business-day deadline for retrospective approval. Sections 6.3 and 7 hard-code `quality_and_retrospective_approval_deadline_business_days: 2`. That changes the obligation.

**Smaller classification pressure.** SUP-710 is mainly an absent reactivation/reuse rule, not a logging indeterminate; DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE is the weaker label. SUP-702 is a plausible PD-3 exception, but the trace never shows the required automated master-data validation, so medium-high confidence is a notch too high. SUP-711 as high-confidence “indeterminate” is awkward: the shared source transaction id is strong duplication evidence, while the allowed label set pushes the name toward indeterminacy.

**Section 7 does not do what it claims.** The block is not executable: `ProcessModel` is not a PM4Py POWL class, and that import fails before `REVISED_POWL` is reassigned. The RFI “loop” puts a cycle into a `StrictPartialOrder` (no linear extension, hence not a partial order) and also places `Request_More_Info` both inside an XOR and as a mandatory sibling, so the choice cannot exit. POWL repetition is `Operator.LOOP`; that operator is never used. The same node objects are parented twice (`sanctions`, `emergency_activation_join`, `emergency_decision_preconditions`, pilot `activation`). The root partial order executes the standard, pilot, and emergency routes together; mutual exclusion is only promised in a side validator. Pilot and emergency postconditions are sequenced after the XOR, so rejection still demands activation and post-activation work. The emergency branch is built twice, and the discarded builder still shares objects with the survivor. Notification is forced after deferred quality and emergency closeout, which would reject the answer’s own reading of SUP-702 (notification at +2 hours, quality the next business day). The prose in §6 is much better than this artifact, but the task required one defensible POWL, with awkward parts approximated and declared. This model has no sound execution and would not accept the cases the judgment calls compliant or justified.
