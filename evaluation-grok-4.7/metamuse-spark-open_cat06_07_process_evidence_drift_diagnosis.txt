7.5

The diagnosis is directionally strong and matches the intended separation of causes: April is treated as a schema and pipeline incident, June as a routing-mix change with stable variant-conditional breach rates, August–September as overlapping supplier performance, V3 concept change, and Partner Z mix shift, and October as label censoring that makes the 0.16 breach rate incomparable. The six required sections are present, falsifiers are stated, remediation is staged, and label-delay alarms are gated off retraining.

It is not nearly flawless. The concept-drift row calls July–September predicted risk a severe under-prediction of “mature” observed rates 0.19/0.28/0.31. July’s gap is 0.18 versus 0.19, and the August–September headline rates are cutoff rates at 93% and 82% maturity, not the matured variant rates. V1 is called flat at 0.15–0.17, but matured V1 is 0.18 in August and 0.19 in September; V3’s January–May band is given as 0.30–0.33 while April is 0.34. July is placed in the safe baseline training window even though V3 breach has already moved from about 0.33 to 0.46. May-started cases are too readily blamed on the April feature-cache miss. The Partner Z sentence on maturity bias “in the opposite direction versus October” is unclear. Section 4 states an unsupported recovery of AUROC to about 0.78 versus 0.74.

The required code sketch would not run as written. `format_dataframe` renames `case_id`, `activity`, and `timestamp`, but later lines still use the old names. `discover_performance_dfg` returns a three-tuple, yet the code calls `.get` on it. The duplicate rate is computed after `drop_duplicates`, so it is approximately zero, and the claimed 60-second near-duplicate rule is not implemented. Median edge times are labeled as hours without converting PM4Py’s seconds. `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` is nulled before discovery, and temporal-feature missingness is mislabeled as the queue feature. Performance DFGs are built on month-truncated event cohorts, which biases long edges such as `Order Part -> Part Received`, without that caveat.
