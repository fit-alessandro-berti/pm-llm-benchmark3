6.5

The portfolio correctly refuses I-03, separates stale repeat alerts from evidence-changing ones, and prices queue harm instead of ranking on `p_now`. The scenario arithmetic is largely reconstructible and internally consistent with a midpoint-effect score. The policy still does not implement its own rules, and several operational choices are under-specified or mutually inconsistent.

The capacity table’s signs, tiers, and actions do not form one rule. Restoring the intended subtractions, I-01, I-05, I-07, and I-10 have negative spillover-adjusted scores, while I-04 (€6,550), I-08 (€2,150), and I-09 (€1,150) are the only clear positives that pass the gates. That ordering is not what the numeric column communicates, because the negative scores are printed as positive euro amounts. I-07’s displayed €1,600 sits above I-09’s €1,150. I-08’s lower-bound net is \(0.05 \times 100{,}000 - 450 - 5{,}000 = -450\), so the stated robustness rule (negative lower bound means deprioritize or confirm before a slot) does not support “Intervene” on the current book. The same rule’s 1.5× spillover test is also not applied: I-09’s direct net of about €3,550 is only about 1.5× a €2,400 hurdle and fails a strict reading.

I-05 versus I-07 is directionally right and then dropped. I-05’s +0.02 refresh with no new event is suppressed. I-07’s new log is treated as material and still loses on value. The escalation rule that follows is ignored: I-07 has `p_adj` about 0.785 and 1.5 h to the deadline, which is exactly the stated “escalate even without a slot” case. Section 4 ends on the capacity decision and never fires that escalation.

The Non-Native-Language gate is likewise split. Section 1 requires human review before a swarm and Section 5 proposes a cap until more data; Section 2 still assigns I-09 a slot now. The fairness uplift (1.25× for SME and Public Sector) and the daily low-value reserve would qualify I-07 (`p_adj` ≥ 0.70, value €5,000, positive lower-bound net) for the spare slot. That slot is already reserved for a possible regulatory case. Today’s allocation therefore uses a different policy from the safeguards.

The spillover hurdle is a hidden parameter. Six untreated diagnosis-stage cases are given; €20,000 per case is not. It is what makes €2,400 the cutoff and what places I-02 only €300 above the line. There is no sensitivity to that value, to congestion, or to the fact that I-02 and I-06 are identifiable untreated diagnosis cases that absorb the delay. Additive application of 0.01–0.03 per slot is a literal reading of the prompt, but three slots at the high end are +0.09 on each of those cases, which is large relative to I-02’s own claim on a slot.

Adverse results do not feed the decision. The audited ranges are coherent: optimistic gross €32,000–€37,800, base gross €26,200, adverse gross €9,600–€12,600, then −€7,800 false-alarm penalty, −€1,350 labor, and spillover €3,600 to €10,800. Base net stays positive; adverse net is about −€7,350 to −€10,350. The text’s adverse net figures lose their minus signs even though the prose says the portfolio is overwhelmed. No risk preference, scenario weight, or option value is stated, yet three slots are committed, including I-08, whose 8 h horizon and Strategic over-prediction (`p_adj` about 0.345) make waiting the natural robust alternative. Only I-04 remains clearly positive if the lower effect and the midpoint spillover are charged together.

Operational timing is thin for a same-day policy. Two specialists can run one 45-minute swarm at a time, so four slots consume about three hours, into the 15:00 regulatory window. Release-at-14:30 is not shown to be feasible after three swarms, and order is unspecified even though I-09 has 2.5 h left while I-04 and I-08 have 8–10 h. The 1.5 h eligibility floor leaves 45 minutes after the swarm for resolution and customer validation, which is not a credible downstream window. The reserve rule (“release to I-02 if its effect is confirmed or if I-08’s is not”) couples those checks so that a confirmed I-08 blocks I-02; that is not a value-of-information rule. The cool-down line mixes “or” with “whichever is later,” so a deployer cannot tell whether a new event inside four hours reopens the case.

Section 6 is concrete on cadence, ECE, shadow mode, and a review board, but the causal plan undermines itself. Slot availability is proposed as an instrument while the same document’s spillover story violates the exclusion restriction. Weekly ECE triggers with no minimum sample size will churn the SME and Strategic adjustments on noise. The point calibration shifts (+0.065 and −0.085) are midpoints used as constants, with no uncertainty carried into the score.

The piece meets the required skeleton and avoids the weakest failure modes. It is not a single deployable policy: scoring, eligibility, fairness reserves, escalation, and the adverse scenario do not select the same portfolio.
