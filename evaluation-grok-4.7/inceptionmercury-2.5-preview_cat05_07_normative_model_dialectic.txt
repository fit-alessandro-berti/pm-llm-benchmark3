4.5

The write-up covers every required heading and all 15 cases, and several hard calls are directionally right: SUP-701 and SUP-713 as compliant, SUP-703 and SUP-708 as real control failures, SUP-704 versus SUP-705 split on FD-7’s expiry, SUP-706 as indeterminate logging, and SUP-714 as post-window auto-approval. That is not enough. The answer stipulates the main contested authority question, misstates evidence, breaks its own arithmetic, and delivers a POWL that is neither executable nor faithful to the routes it claims to add.

Section 1 treats PD-3 as an established permission during its window. The record does not. PD-3 is minutes and email, never folded into CS-9, and the compliance observer later denied approving any weakening of CS-9. That dispute is the center of the case; marking it stipulated collapses the dialectic before the briefs start. Other “facts” are invented or blurred: traces are not shown to be immutable, resource identity is not sourced to HR tables, missingness is 2% to 29% rather than a flat 30% mirror failure, and noncompliance is wrongly defined to require material residual risk. SUP-708 is noncompliant even though no transaction occurred. The burden also never separates an applicable-norm breach, a model/scope mismatch, and insufficient data, nor does it rank CS-9 above an unincorporated pilot and a pilot above operational habit.

The prosecution is not the strongest available case. It never attacks PD-3’s authority, never notes that PD-3 still required automated master-data validation, which SUP-702 does not show, and never mentions SUP-715. It calls Request_More_Info noncompliant even though the extra cycles break M0 only and fit CS-9. The defense is likewise not at full strength, and it misses the required concessions. SUP-703 and SUP-708 are barely acknowledged. The matrix’s defense of SUP-708, that M0 “ignores order,” is false: M0 orders both checks before the decision. Desirability and “normalization” are treated as if they were legal theories. SUP-711 is pinned on N4, which is the CRM notification change, not the register-event duplication.

The matrix invents gaps the file already closes. SUP-711’s shared source transaction ID is present, not missing. SUP-702’s minutes and complete eligibility fields are present. SUP-704’s daily monitoring is stated as existing. SUP-712’s alias resolution is given. SUP-707’s prosecution cell contradicts the later classification. SUP-710 is filed entirely as a scope defect, with no conditional finding if reactivation is in scope and no written exemption covers the skipped quality review. SUP-715 is treated as a prohibited spousal approval; the proved breach is missing COI disclosure, and the text says the relationship itself is not banned.

Section 5’s “60% / 20% / 20%” split does not match the answer’s own labels: 2 compliant, 2 justified exceptions, 6 noncompliant, 3 model/scope, 2 data. Severity is not given per case despite being required. Confidence is soft where the record is clear (SUP-705, SUP-707, SUP-712) and high where authority and master-data validation remain open (SUP-702). M0 is called obsolete because practice diverged. Practice does not supersede a controlled standard after the only overlays have expired. The rejection paragraph also blurs its own voice, reading as though the prosecution rejected M0.

The architecture omits withdrawals entirely. It does not set an authority order, bitemporal effective versus record time, or master-data validation. The code then encodes the wrong COI rule: spouse or same reporting line is a hard failure, so a disclosed relationship would still fail. The POWL reuses one `Activate_Supplier` in three partial orders and draws an edge from `decision` to `t_notify`, which is not a node of `M1` and already sits under `notify_close`. Under ordinary PM4Py parent and membership checks that script raises. `t_rmi` is never inserted. The claim that POWL cannot loop is wrong: `Operator.LOOP` exists; only `StrictPartialOrder` is acyclic. As written, sanctions and quality both precede every route, so the model rejects the lawful shapes of SUP-702 and SUP-704, while `Emergency_Provisional_Activation` is a label no log contains. The overlay dates do not repair a net that always offers those branches and still demands pre-activation quality.
