The diagnosis of the four regimes is right and well separated: April is a schema/pipeline failure with intact outcomes, June is a routing-mix shift that does not require concept drift, August–September stacks a real V3 hazard change with mix and Partner Z, and October’s 0.16 breach rate is informative censoring. The six required sections, falsifiers, stratified tests, training-month quarantine, and the rule that a label-delay alarm must not open retraining are all present and mostly precise. The at-diagnosis scoring constraint (realized variant is post-decision, so a V3-gated model leaks) is a genuine strength.

The score is held down by quantitative and logical errors that are stated as findings. The May–June mix-versus-rate split is given as 92% / 8% in both the table and the narrative. The components implied by the same arithmetic are about 0.014 mix and 0.007 rate, which is roughly two-thirds mix, not 92%. September volume growth is +22% (4,180 to 5,100), not +24%. More serious, V3 matured breach of 0.61–0.64 is compared with the global mean prediction of 0.19–0.21 and turned into a “~40 pp” segment under-prediction that then sizes the containment override. Aggregate predictions in August–September are also consistent with a model that still prices V3 near its pre-drift rate of about 0.32; variant-level scores are not in the data, so that gap is not identified. The July claim that V2 over-prediction masks V3 has the same unsupported jump. In the table, duplicate-inflated event counts are stated as the April mechanism rather than as a hypothesis.

The required process-evidence sketch meets the API checklist and correctly refuses a nonexistent PM4Py drift detector, but it is not reliable as written. The “unmapped” share is actually the share of labels that were successfully renamed. Near-duplicate removal does not apply the resource condition the comment describes. Temporal features are computed on the case-start frame after the loop while the comment says event-calendar. `discover_performance_dfg` is treated as a median of raw durations; the usual return is an already aggregated mean, and event-window DFGs are not flagged as truncated. Those defects do not overturn the audit, but they are material under a strict reading.
