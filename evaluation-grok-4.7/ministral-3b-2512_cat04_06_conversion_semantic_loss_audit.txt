3.5

The write-up has the required nine-section shape and a defensible primary pipeline (POWL to accepting Petri net to BPMN, with the process-tree route allowed to fail). That is where the credit ends. The substance inverts the source language, then treats imagined diagrams as validated evidence.

The invariant table does not witness the stated semantics. Several “positive” traces release after `Correct Defect` with no following trial, or start verification before `Prepare Evidence`, or put the silent skip in the visible trace. The negative witness for the loop is a legal one-iteration trace: `Configure Trial` then `Inspect Trial`, then exit, with no `Correct Defect`. In this operator, the redo child separates a repeated iteration; it is not required to leave the loop. “Observable after hiding tau?” is answered “No” for the initial and final visible activities, whose order remains visible once tau is hidden. Optionality, the missing edge between legal review and verification, and the release join are therefore not pinned down by legal witnesses.

The conversion code does not start from the authoritative `source_powl`. It builds a new partial order with no top-level edges, duplicate labels, fresh transition objects on edges that are not the nodes of those orders, and `.order.add_edge(...)` used as a node (that call returns `None`). Markings are not taken from `convert_to_petri_net`, which returns `(net, im, fm)`. `convert_to_immediate_marking`, `convert_to_final_marking`, and `get_next_marking` are not part of the given API. BPMN-derived nets are diagnosed with the original markings, null markings are never checked, and a failed tree conversion is not applied uniformly: later rows still say the tree preserved the loop. Visualization filenames are serialization extensions, and the source POWL view is never saved.

Section 4 marks Petri-to-BPMN and the round trip `validated preserved` without a run. `N/A` is outside the allowed assessment vocabulary. If tree conversion fails, every tree cell is `conversion failed`, not a mix of validated, ambiguous, and N/A. Activity-label uniqueness and initial and final markings are essentially unaudited. Calling the result preservation of binary-loop semantics repeats the redo error.

The bounded explorer is not an exploration. It skips silent transitions instead of firing and hiding them, mutates one marking while iterating the enabled set, records enabled labels rather than the path, and never checks exact final-marking equality on a completed visible trace. There is no visited-state or tau bound, so silent cycles are not prevented. Footprints, the reachability graph, witness traces, and playout are not used to compare nets. The three-step cap is not exhaustive even inside that bound.

The visual review mislabels the verification loop as an AND gateway and invents a BPMN “LOOP gateway.” Rejection criteria omit a failed exact-final-marking test and treat a sequential drawing as if it were loss of an interleaving. The canonical choice of POWL, with BPMN as a non-authoritative view and the tree intermediate disallowed, is the right direction, but the reason given (silent transitions) is wrong: process trees can represent tau; this partial order is not series-parallel. The residual uncertainties invent a skip that might “reset” Assess Risk and suggest the two post-registration activities are not really concurrent. They miss what passing these tests still cannot show: unbounded language equivalence, true concurrency versus interleaving, and footprint or soundness agreement versus full trace equivalence.
