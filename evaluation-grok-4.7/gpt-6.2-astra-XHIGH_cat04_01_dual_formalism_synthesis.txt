8.5

The models, witnesses, and bounded-language counts are unusually strong, but the answer is not nearly flawless: one reported validation result is numerically false, and one residual weakness is mis-scoped.

The process tree is a genuine conservative under-approximation. `SEQ(R, AND(SEQ(C,P), SEQ(A,D)), AND(XOR(V, AND(Q,G)), XOR(τ,U), LOOP(SEQ(E,I), J)), L, Z)` preserves the work-product split, the trial gate, approval/trial overlap after that gate, supplier cardinality, and the release join. It excludes exactly the intended traces in which a review or Consult Supplier completes before both Plan Verification and Design Corrective Action. The N-poset argument on {A, P, V, E} correctly explains why a single-label sequence/parallel tree cannot do better. Parent links are set explicitly, and the silent supplier skip is a real tau leaf.

The POWL cover is the right one: `C ∥ A`, `C < P`, `A < D,B,O`, `P,D < T`, and `B,O,T < L < Z`, with `Q ∥ G` and `E < I` inside the operators. Adding the transitive closure does not change the language. The 13 incomparable root pairs are all accounted for, and free interleaving inside concurrent submodels is the correct reading of the cross-branch overlaps.

The eight witnesses match those semantics. Traces 4 and 5 are allowed by the specification and by POWL, and they are the first events at which the tree barrier fails. Trace 6 fails at Execute Trial because the plan is still missing. Trace 7 is still extendable until Release Material, which is the right earliest violation. Trace 3 is a real two-iteration high-risk supplier trace.

The bounded-language totals are also right. With at most three executions and two adjustments, the partial-order language has exactly 20,952 traces, and the tree’s extra barrier leaves exactly 10,152. Those figures are strong evidence that the models and the playout comparison are faithful.

The clear factual error is the reachable-marking claim. Under the standard process-tree Petri-net translation, the parallel region alone has 6 × 4 × 2 = 48 markings, but the registration, work-product, and closure phases add 15 more, for 63. Reporting 48 as the size of a complete traversal means that check did not cover the net it describes. The soundness and trace-count claims can still be true; the marking statistic is not. Self-critique 2 also presents the pass/fail abstraction as a POWL limitation, although the tree uses the same loop abstraction.
