The diagnosis is strong and correctly refuses a single drift label: April is a schema and prefix-construction failure, June is a routing-mix change with stable within-variant rates, August–September is mostly a within-V3 rate shift tied to the parts edge (with Partner Z absent in August), and October’s 0.16 breach rate is not comparable. The Kitagawa-style split, falsifiers, maturity gating, and the refusal of a Partner Z model are all well supported. The score is held down by over-precise claims and a process-metric sketch that would mis-measure the edge it exists to watch.

The June claim that 0.15 is “exactly” the mix-only value 0.158 is loose, and the within-variant effect is only approximately zero (V2 0.09, V3 0.35). Mean predicted risk of 0.20 is also not the pre-June base rate of about 0.18, so part of that gap is the May residual, not only stale routing propensity. The mix effects of −0.02 / +0.02 are rounded against inconsistent baselines (reconstructed ~0.17 versus observed ~0.18). “Prefixes built at the wrong (usually later, longer) event” is a plausible mechanism stated too firmly. “October is evidence of a label-pipeline problem and nothing else” contradicts the same answer’s label-free evidence: the parts edge at 155 h and V3 at 41% still indicate ongoing performance drift. Test 1 bundles “real improvement” and “real model collapse” into one expected result.

In the code, default `discover_performance_dfg` values are numeric durations, so `.get("median")` is not valid unless aggregation is explicitly `"all"`. Filtering to in-month events before building the DFG drops cross-boundary `Order Part → Part Received` pairs and invents directly-follows edges; that bias grows as lead time lengthens. Dropping `UNKNOWN_ACTIVITY` before discovery has the same edge-fabrication effect. Near-duplicate logic only sees the previous row, and `source_system` is assumed. Cohort interpretations and the warning against `traces_contained` are right; the implementation does not actually produce an unbiased monthly parts-edge median.
