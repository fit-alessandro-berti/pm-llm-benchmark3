5.5

The write-up has the required sections, a full C001–C016 table, and several defensible calls (C002, C004, C005, C007, C008, C009, C015, C016). It is not a reliable policy.

Constraint 4 and the algorithm in §2 both bar new work for high overtime risk unless the case is urgent and no safer resource exists. C012 is low priority with 48h left, and R10 is the owner, skill 3, low overtime, and lightly loaded. Assigning it to R2 is a direct violation, and it is also the second “training” case used to claim the 20% quota (2/9). Without that illegal assignment the quota is missed, while the explicit trainee-approval note on C010 is ignored.

R3 is given four new cases (C001, C003, C007, C014) with 6.0 hours left, including an urgent skill-1 security review at a 3h SLA and a high-complexity rework at a 6h SLA. That batch cannot fit. R8 (rework skill 3, low overtime, almost unused) was the safer place for C014. C001 follows the continuity rule, but the stated +3.3h service penalty for a weak owner is not reconciled with a 3h deadline. Several “Risk to Monitor: None” cells hide real load (R6’s two urgent cases; R7 supervising C001 while owning C008 on 4.5h).

The scoring model is not executable. SLA points do not differ across resources, `required_skill` is treated as a number, handover-cost bands are not scored, tie-breaks are unspecified, and the training boost is circular. §2 hard-filters high overtime, then §3 breaks that filter. “Batching” is the reason for C006, but C006 is not batched.

The baseline comparison is factually wrong. First-available excludes skill 0, so it cannot send Security C001 to R10. Specialist-only tie-breaks by open cases, so security concentrates on R8, quality on R10, and diagnosis plus rework on R3; the claimed R7/R9 bottlenecks do not occur.

Audit definitions are loose: “Gini” is actually standard deviation over mean, handover frequency is conflated with reassignment count, and targets are arbitrary. The PM4Py sketch omits working-together network, service time, performance DFG, and variants; `filter_case_performance(..., 'sla_violation')` and the batches call are not real APIs; denominators count events, and nothing learns weights from the handover/rework evidence.
