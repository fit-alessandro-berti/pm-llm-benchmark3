The operational verdicts are right: A and B are not visible-trace equivalent, not P3-equivalent, and they match only after the P2 collapse. Loop scope, exactly-one versus one-check-per-iteration, approval-before-check, and both language non-inclusions are handled correctly, and the six witnesses are real complete traces within the P3 bound. The required exact tree metrics are wrong and internally inconsistent. Each tree has 12 nodes (5 operators + 6 visible leaves + 1 `tau`), not 13. With the root at depth 0, A has depth 4 and B depth 5, because `Approve Revision` and `tau` sit under the loop’s inner sequence. “Equal depth” is false, and §8’s “8 versus 13” and “depth 3 versus 4” reuse those figures. The later “before rework or close” wording is the real rule, and the equivalence sentence is only an “only if,” not an iff. Footprints are over-specific: pairwise directly-follows between Check, Analyze, Rework, and Approve can look alike across the loop, so the claimed qualitative split is not established. The BFS is otherwise a real bounded search, but `semantics.execute` is called without a defensive marking copy, which is unsafe if that call is in-place.
