7.4

The dataset, provenance split, and flattened control-flow predictions are largely consistent and checkable. The OCEL tables contain all 12 objects and all 17 events, the O2O facts match the narrative, and the stated sizes hold: 41 E2O rows, 7 derived, 20 Order rows, traces of length 12 and 8. The Order directly-follows multiset is right, including Create→Add (2), Pack→Dispatch (2), Dispatch→Issue (2), and no Dispatch→Confirm edge. Package, Delivery, and Invoice shapes, the payment-leaf argument, and the decision not to allocate M1/M2 to orders are sound. Validation covers the required integrity checks, and the constructor is isolated.

The analysis then asserts several things the same model does not support.

- O200’s P1/D1 time is treated as a wrong attribution “determined by I1.” I3 belongs to O200 and is in P1, so crediting E09 and E13 to O200 is the correct propagation (D1→P1→I3→O200). Section 8 even gives that path, which contradicts sections 4 and 7.
- O100–O200 co-occurrence is “entirely due to P1 and V1.” E06 is one of the five shared events and is a joint reservation, earlier than packing and independent of V1.
- M2-before-close is said to “exist only through V1.” The timestamps establish that order without any invoice link.
- Alternative A says source-only discovery removes an Order Dispatch→Confirm edge and that P2 leaves both order traces. That edge is absent in the full Order DFG as well (Issue sits between them). P2’s events were never on O200. The real change, Pack→Issue and Issue→Close, is not stated. Section 1 also says every result can be compared with and without propagation; the code only prints flattened row counts.
- Failure mode 2’s example of a missing source link is Order–Dispatch P1, which this model classifies as derived.
- The Petri-net passage turns the activity Pack Package into the instance “Pack P1” and “two Order places.” A variable arc would consume a variable set of tokens from one Order place. Close-versus-payment “gating” is also unlikely under this E2O policy, because payment never enters the Order lifecycle.

Smaller defects: the activity audit mixes source and derived touches, ET–OT is described as if it encoded the package path, Pack is called the unique most central activity while Dispatch also touches three types, and `validate_ocel_tables` is documented as pure while reading module globals. Those do not break the construction, but together with the false structural claims they keep the write-up well short of a near-flawless analysis.
