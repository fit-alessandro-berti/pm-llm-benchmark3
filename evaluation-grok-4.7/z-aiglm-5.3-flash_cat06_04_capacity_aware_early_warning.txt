7.4

The final policy is structurally complete and mostly decision-theoretically serious: I-03 is correctly barred, I-05 is suppressed as a stale repeat while I-07 is reopened on new evidence, calibration shifts are applied in the right directions, and spillovers, label contamination, and segment fairness are given concrete controls. The direct-net arithmetic for N is largely auditable and internally consistent.

The allocation still breaks its own rule. R6 says not to spend a slot when marginal value after the ~€2,400 externality is non-positive, yet slot 1 goes to I-07, whose direct net is only about +€0.8k (about −€1.6k after spillover). Urgency is cited, but no term in the rule makes a negative social trade positive just because the window is closing. That choice is also operationally thin: a 45-minute swarm inside a 1.5-hour residual leaves little time for resolution and validation, and that clock cost is never scored.

I-02 is mishandled relative to the same timing logic. It is already in diagnosis, about three hours from the deadline, with a positive base net, but the only path back in is a 15:00 rebid — at the deadline, after a 45-minute swarm would be too late. Slot 4 is meanwhile locked to I-04, which has about ten hours of slack. That contradicts the stated “H ≤ 3h, act while diagnosis is active” tiering.

Section 3’s scenario point estimates check out, but the ranges do not. Optimistic and base “breaches avoided” extend to 1.1 and 0.95, while the sum of the three stated upper bounds is 0.90. The base benefit band also swallows the optimistic and adverse cases, so the scenarios do not separate cleanly. The adverse row further inflates untreated-case value to €30k without a basis in the stated spillover model. I-08’s quoted downside (~+€1.5k) is not the consistent low case (~+€1.1k).

Non-monetary duties are thinner than the task requires, the I-06 haircut to 0.45 is undefined by the rule, and the reserve’s 30% / €3–10k illustration is speculative. Monitoring and fairness safeguards are specific and usable. Those strengths keep this well above a merely compliant answer; the rule-versus-action conflict, the missed I-02 window, and the impossible impact ranges keep it out of the high band.
