The document is a rigorous, internally consistent competing-risk analysis. Time origin, the 72-hour horizon, timely resolution at exactly 72, and the four estimands are defined before any estimator is chosen. Individual contributions for S03, S09, S06, and S08 match those definitions, including the important split between an already-observed breach and a still-censored eventual disposition. Withdrawal and rejection are treated as competing terminals, and transfer is given three genuine encodings (competing internal exit, censoring, and immediate hypothetical resolution), with the pessimistic external reading separated from independent-loss censoring.
