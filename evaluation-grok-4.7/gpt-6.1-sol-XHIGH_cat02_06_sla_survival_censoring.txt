The estimands, censoring distinctions, logical bounds, and Aalen–Johansen figures are internally consistent and match the extract (including 18/32–23/32 for internal non-resolution, pooled N72 ≈ 69.6%, U72 ≈ 38.0%, the 63.9% net Kaplan–Meier, and the cohort and transfer sensitivities, including the standardized ranking reversal). Competing risks, leakage, and immortal time are handled correctly, and open cases are retained.

Deductions: the PM4Py branch is not reliably executable. `variants_filter.get_variants` returns comma-separated string keys in current PM4Py, while the code indexes `tuple` keys and joins those keys, so a successful import raises `KeyError` before the survival summaries; the fallback never actually calls PM4Py. “Partly” misstates the late-resolution gap under immediate transfer imputation: that gap equals S32’s jump at 96, because earlier transfers are absorbing events in both scenarios and do not change S(t) or the resolution increments on (72, 96). “The latter net curve” is ambiguous between the 63.9% net curve and actual-world N72. “Slightly favoring B” is stronger than these cell sizes support, though it is later qualified.
