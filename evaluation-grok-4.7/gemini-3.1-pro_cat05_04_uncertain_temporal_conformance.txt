5.5

The core UTC normalization is mostly coherent and often numerically right, but the required sensitivity, code, and evidential sections are incomplete or internally inconsistent. Under a strict reading, that keeps the answer well below a strong score.

Section 1 states the offset direction correctly (`true = resolved − offset`, so bounds flip) and applies resolution before correction. CEST, EDT, and BST conversions are right. The mobile rule is an explicit one-sided occurrence shift of `[0, 300]` s earlier, separate from the `±300` s clock bound. The T-405 claim that ingestion order leaves only Alert in CEST and both later events in CET is right: every other fold pairing makes `Alert < Start < Complete` impossible even after floor and `±60` s. What is missing is the assumption that actually drives borderline results: event errors are treated as independent, so HUB–HUB durations pick up about `±3` s. That choice is material for T-408’s profile test and is never stated. T-409’s negative delays are also left uninterpreted.

Section 2’s intervals match that model. HUB is `±1.5` s. OPS-BER floor plus `[-90, +120]` gives `[T−120, T+149.999]`. VENDOR-NY `04:00` EDT becomes `[07:57:00Z, 08:03:59.999Z]`. T-404’s inferred start `[10:09:58.5Z, 10:17:01.5Z]` correctly subtracts `[18, 25]` min from the uncertain completion. T-409 correctly replaces the daily NTP bound with `±12` min and gets `[15:52:00Z, 16:16:59.999Z]`. SENSOR/mobile rounding is a slightly asymmetric half-open window (`+4.999` / `+29.999`); it is documented and does not move the classifications. Endpoints are sometimes rounded by `0.001` s when durations are formed.

Section 3’s baseline (zeta `2`) classifications follow from those intervals. Real certain results are preserved: T-402 S1 is `[958.5, 1231.5]` against `≤ 900`; T-408 S2 is about `3300` s against `2700`; T-408 S3 is about `138300` s against `129600`. Profile bands `[240, 960]`, `[900, 3300]`, `[1260, 4140]`, and `[43200, 129600]` are the right zeta-2 intervals. Partial overlap is labeled possible, including T-402’s thin TP1 overlap `[958.5, 960]` and T-408 TP2’s `[3297, 3303]` against an upper edge of `3300`. No row is indeterminate. T-407 is testable only because an unlogged causal cap is hardened into `[0, 1023]`; T-409 S1 is scored as possible while allowing start before the alert. Both needed an explicit alternative semantics.

Section 5 does not implement the analysis it accompanies. The script hardcodes one Legacy fold, never builds duration intervals or interval classifications, and never compares them with the midpoint log. T-404 is left as `Investigation_Complete`, T-407 as `Alert_Sent`, and T-409 uses the ordinary OPS-BER `[-90, +120]` window, so the code’s T-409 interval contradicts the table. The PM4Py call is only a zeta loop around a lossy midpoint log, with the comparison left as comments.

Section 6 names the right evidence classes in general, then gives the wrong minimum evidence in the tight cases. For T-401, cutting the `+120` s offset to `<28` s moves the earliest start, not the late start that produces the `991` s upper tail. For T-405, the fold is already fixed by the order constraint; the remaining `[720, 1080]` gap is floor and clock width, so more fold metadata does not resolve S1. Section 7 covers UTC storage, raw retention, offset telemetry, resolution, start/complete meaning, DST fold, correlation ids, and an overlap-versus-certainty enforcement rule, without tying those controls to the cases that actually flip.
