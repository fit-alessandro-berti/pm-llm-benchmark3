6.3

The answer matches the required structure and is substantively grounded in segments, DFG paths, SP1/SP2/Q1/R1, slack, rework, and rebound. The recommendation correctly refuses a blanket ban on air and dedicated vans. It is not reliable on magnitudes or causal attribution.

The indicator `energy_kwh / (1 - scrap + rework rate)` is invalid: scrap kilograms and a rework rate cannot be combined that way, and the expression is not a yield. The rework rate is also miscalculated. `(470 + 390) / 3670` is about 23%, not 12.8%; 12.8% is only `470 / 3670`. That denominator drops emergency and rework entries, and `2910 + 760 = 3670` is not total CNC activity. `410 + 60` does not equal the 530 cancellations. Batch wait `3020` is labeled “MTO regular,” but MTO regular is only about 2,640 of 12,000 orders, so that queue necessarily includes other segments. Standard-stock is also given a CNC wait cap even though it leaves through pick-from-stock.

The effect table violates the instruction not to invent unsupported precision, and several figures contradict the cited evidence. Air at 4–6× ground is about an 75–83% transport reduction, not the stated 20–40% for the SP1-violating cohort. “Avoids 32 kWh” treats the whole CNC_B path average as incremental rework energy; the same 32-versus-16 kWh and 1.8-versus-0.70 kg gaps are then reused for both the machine shift and the inspection gate. Those paths also differ in heat treatment and routing, so the gaps are not the effect of either intervention alone. The reinforced-packaging 40% damage reduction is misassigned to the compatibility check. Shared-van savings of 40–60%, precautionary volume of 30–50%, obsolescence falling from 6% to 2–4%, and many hour and late-rate deltas are unsupported. S10’s late rate is 13% in the text and 12% in the table. The rebound claim that mode shift moves lateness from 8% to 21% compares unrelated paths.

The package adopts nearly every candidate, so overlapping transport and rework effects are not kept distinct. The 15-hour critical figure is a median, not a demonstrated SLA. A minimum of 2,000 cases per segment is impossible for the smaller segments inside a 12,000-order log. Boundary setting, exception governance, phased pilots, and the refusal to strip true-downtime capacity are sound, but the quantitative core is too error-prone for a higher score.
