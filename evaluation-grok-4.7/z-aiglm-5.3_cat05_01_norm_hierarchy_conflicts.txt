The assessment is a serious, well-structured compliance opinion: issue-specific hierarchy, time-of-act analysis, non-curative treatment of later approvals, and real differentiation across the eight purchases. It is not nearly flawless. The classifications do not follow the author’s own rules, and that break is large enough to hold the score down.

The central failure is internal. G2 and the hierarchy treat the independent budget-owner approval as non-waivable by ECD-2 and by the CFO email; only a Chief Risk Officer exception can remove it, and none exists. Unproven exception elements are defined as unsatisfied. JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION is defined as a deviation covered by a valid or credibly operative exception. P-505, P-507, and P-508 are then labeled JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION even though the author states that the budget-owner element was never satisfied and was not waived. That is noncompliance under the stated catalogue, not a justified exception. Section 6 repeats the error by omitting those purchases from policy breaches. P-506, which at least has a late Plant approval, is NONCOMPLIANT, while P-508 (EUR 155,000, no Finance event and no budget-owner event) is not. Section 5 also misses the strongest classification-changing reading already available inside the author’s framework: budget-owner failure does not require holding D1 invalid.

Two narrower evidential mistakes sit on top of that. P-502’s 12:55 record is treated as outage-skewed, which only fits 14 July during ON-44, while D1 (sent 15 July 07:20) is still used to cover the Finance element. Those cannot both be true, and the source never dates P-502. P-507 is placed inside ECD-2 scope because the seals were needed within eight hours, but the record never ties them to a sterilization line.

The stronger parts are real. P-505 correctly prefers the 17:59:31 supplier acknowledgment over the 18:06 ERP stamp. P-507 correctly treats the 12:40 signed PDF as the recording and the 14:30 ERP entry as lag. P-501 is kept on B4 because commitment preceded 09:00. P-503 and P-504 are robust NONCOMPLIANT results. P-506 correctly refuses an unwritten extension after expiry. Thresholds, segregation of duties on P-508, A6, and the split between the local register and GPIP-4 clearance are handled carefully. The replacement clause covers authority, scope, thresholds, segregation, new suppliers, retrospective records, expiry, and anti-normalization.

COMPLIANT for P-501 is defensible only under the author’s explicit “B2 states no time” reading, and it sits uneasily with a reachable Plant Manager and a four-hour runway. That choice is disclosed. The budget-owner contradiction is not a disclosure; it changes three primary labels.
