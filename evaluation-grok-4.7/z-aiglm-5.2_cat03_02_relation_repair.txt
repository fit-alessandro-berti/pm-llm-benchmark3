5.5

The write-up has the required eight-part shape, two bundles, explicit non-additions, and the right candidate edges (ev08–PK-8, ev10–DEL-Y, invoice/ORD-B, PAY-2/INV-44). It does not invent objects or events, and it does say arithmetic is not proof. Under the stated criteria — abductive fit, lifecycle coherence, calibrated uncertainty, and downstream effects — the reasoning is not reliable enough to carry those candidates.

The evidence model is internally inconsistent and incomplete. Identifier match is filed as a weak heuristic, then called a “strong hint,” then used to **accept** ev10→DEL-Y at 0.90 despite `N-421?` and rule 9. Temporal lifecycle is filed as a hard invariant even though the domain text treats scanner outage and portal loss as normal exceptions. Route, amount-vs-customer cross-structure, cardinality, and already-observed O2O are not actually combined. The ten domain rules are not classified one by one. “Biologically impossible cardinality” is not a usable rule.

The central observed fact is missed. ev06/ev07 and the existing O2O already split ORD-A: IT-A1 is in PK-7/DEL-X, IT-A2 is in PK-8. Once PK-8 is carried by DEL-Y, ORD-A’s fulfillment sits on both deliveries. Bundle A still treats ev11 as a DEL-X link because “ORD-A’s item” is IT-A1, and the JSON says `N-42*` “likely” means N-420. That assigns ev11 without a sound reason the alternatives are weaker. The given reason — the event already names ORD-A — points the other way: ORD-A’s second item is the one on the unresolved delivery. Bundle A is also not a minimum-change repair; it closes the invoice and both payments and only leaves ev11→DEL-Y open.

Bundle B’s rejection of PK-8 is weakly motivated. `scanner_ref=PK8-2` one minute after PK8-1 is positive package evidence, not the item-only outage pattern in rule 3. Route=NORTH cannot support a bulk confirm, because every package and delivery in the log is NORTH. The better tension is never used: the payment already applied to INV-44 is 100 (ORD-B’s value), while the open payment is 130 (ORD-A’s value), on an invoice that currently bills only ORD-A. Both bundles still **accept** PAY-2→INV-44 at 0.90, against rule 7’s unapplied-cash case and against the answer’s own fifth warning.

Circularity is committed and then denied. P2, P4, P7, and P9 are O2O copies of E2O guesses at the same confidence, with “Evidence against: None.” Section 5 then says the invoice O2O is independently supported and that circular reasoning was avoided. That is the exact pattern the audit was required to flag.

Section 6 misstates the graphs these additions would change. PAY-2 is already on ev14; its payment lifecycle is one `Record Payment`. The invoice change is a second payment on INV-44, i.e. a `Record Payment → Record Payment` directly-follows edge. PK-8 already has Pack and Dispatch via ev07 and ev10; DEL-Y is the object with no events. Confirm Delivery is not added to any package, so a Pack→Dispatch→Confirm package flow does not appear. At type level, Package–Item, Delivery–Package, Invoice–Order, Payment–Invoice, and Dispatch–Delivery already exist; the new event-type/object-type edge is Confirm Delivery–Delivery. Order flattening, without O2O propagation, does not insert Pack Item: unrepaired order events are create, add, confirm (ORD-A only), and invoice. The claimed `Create → Add → Pack → Invoice` path is not produced by this repair. ORD-A and ORD-B are not equivalent executions in Bundle A: item count, confirm, and direct event sets still differ. Event vs unique-object vs total-object frequency is only gestured at.

The code sketch does copy, append, check referential integrity, mark a constructor, call the three named discovery functions, and flatten Order and Delivery. `pm4py >= 2.3.0` is an unsupported version pin, and the column-aligned append is fragile, but that is secondary to the modeling errors above.
