8.5

The audit is strong and hits the distinctions that matter: post-closure and full-trace leakage, the `extraction_date` and nightly-snapshot defects, preservation of queue length and elapsed time, Appeal → decision as future but process-legal versus Claim Closed → damage assessment as impossible or same-timestamp noise, and predictive versus causal versus fairness standards. Coverage of the top-12 features, sequence patterns, and local-only fields is complete, the JSON is usable, and the remediation plan is concrete.

It is not nearly flawless. Appeal and Claim Closed are described both as future-possible and as impossible at `t0`, which blurs the required enabled / future-possible / impossible split. `claimant_channel` is treated as known at registration without lineage, while `initial_reserve` is correctly withheld for the same lack of proof. Local SHAP signs on the retained features are reused without stating that attributions from a model trained with leaked features are not transportable. Calendar week is folded into Case Z’s invalidating ambiguities even though it is known at `t0` and is a drift proxy, not temporal leakage.
