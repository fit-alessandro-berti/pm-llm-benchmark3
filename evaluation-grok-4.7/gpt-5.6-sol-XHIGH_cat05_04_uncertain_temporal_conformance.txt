8.7

The interval arithmetic, time-zone conversions, and baseline classifications are almost entirely correct under the model stated in Section 1. HUB bounds are ±1.5 s, the asymmetric OPS-BER window for 10:14 CEST is UTC `[08:12:00, 08:16:29.999]`, SENSOR rounding-plus-offset is `[08:51:00, 08:51:20]`, and the EDT, CEST, and BST conversions match the stated dates. The only ordered DST survivor is alert on the first Europe/Berlin fold and the later two events on the second fold; the resulting T-405 durations `[720.001, 1079.999]`, `[2040.001, 2399.999]`, and `[2940.001, 3299.999]` are right. T-402 S1 is a real certain SLA breach (minimum 958.5 s), and T-408 S2 and S3 remain certain breaches well beyond five minutes. The zeta bands and the 0/2/5-minute tolerance matrix match those intervals, including the one-second edges: T-404 S2 at 2703 s, T-408 investigation→containment straddling 3300 s, T-403 still over the zeta-3 band by 1.5 s, and T-409 still over a +2-minute SLA by 1.499 s. Sent versus received, the T-404 duration subtraction, and the two mobile-entry readings are separated cleanly. The code follows that same model, keeps the DST union until the order filter, labels midpoints as lossy, and does not pretend PM4Py consumes interval uncertainty.

The score is held down by analysis that the write-up itself makes material and then does not test. Section 1 says same-source offsets are treated as independent and that a shared offset would narrow durations, but Section 4 never measures that choice. For T-405 it changes results: a common legacy offset cancels, leaving alert→investigation at about `[840, 960]` s, which is a certain profile pass at zeta 2 and a certain violation at zeta 1, not a possible violation at both. Several headline edges are the same kind of artifact. T-404 S2 clears 2700 s by 3 s only because two HUB corrections are added separately; T-408’s six-second straddle of 3300 s is partly the same effect, though measurement uncertainty still crosses that boundary even if the offset cancels. Those cases are reported as definite tolerance or zeta transitions without that caveat.

T-409 is also hardened too far. The ±12-minute figure is described as a weak engineering estimate, and the stated indeterminate category explicitly covers evidence that is too weak. The write-up uses it as a closed hard interval and then issues possible and certain verdicts. Low confidence is noted, but that is not the same as an indeterminate classification, or a side-by-side “if this estimate is only advisory” result.

Smaller defects remain. The opening offset sentence is missing the operator in “recorded true,” and several later constraints are likewise missing “minus” or “no later than,” so the prose formula does not stand on its own even though the displayed intervals are consistent with `true = recorded − offset`. The T-407 receipt row still runs to 14:20 Z, after investigation start; the causal cap is applied only in the duration override. The code implements only the primary mobile-delay reading and only the baseline call, so it does not reproduce the alternative S2 result or the sensitivity matrix given in the prose. The midpoint comparison also omits the indeterminate flag used elsewhere, although those T-407 pairs happen to be skipped. None of those code gaps overturns a robust certain breach.
