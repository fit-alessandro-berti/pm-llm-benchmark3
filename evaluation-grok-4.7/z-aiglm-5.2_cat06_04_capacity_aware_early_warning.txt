7.4

The final policy is structured, auditable, and meets the main quality bar: I-03 is ineligible despite high risk, I-05 is suppressed as a no-news refresh while I-07 re-enters on the new log, and spillovers are large enough to make the adverse portfolio negative. The scenario arithmetic for the chosen effects, costs, and €20,000 spillover assumption checks out, and the monitoring plan is concrete.

The decision rule is not the rule actually used. Segment adjustments never enter REV, so calibration does not change ranking. The promised ±50% widening for Non-Native-Language Support is not applied to I-09; doing so could drop that case below the stated €1,500 bar and change the portfolio. The claim that I-08 “flips” from positive to negative once Strategic Enterprise bias is applied is false under this formula, which ignores \(p\). The modified-score table also shows I-08 as €450 while the raw score and allocation row are −€450.

Other decision problems are material. The proposed “under 2 hours and \(p_{adj}>0.70\) gets manual review” safeguard is not applied to I-07. Rejecting I-07 because residual risk stays above 50% reintroduces a probability threshold the task forbids; value is the risk reduction, not crossing 0.5. Lower-bound REV is an undefended maximin rule that discards I-08 even though its midpoint value is strongly positive. Two specialists make three swarms serial, but there is no order, so a 10-hour case can be queued ahead of cases with 2.5–3 hours left. Adverse impact adds about €2,000 of delay harm on top of the high-end 0.03 spillover, which double-counts congestion. Non-monetary duties, especially public-sector harm beyond the euro values, are thin.
