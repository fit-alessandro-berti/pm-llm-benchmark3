6.5

The design covers the required sections and respects the main constraints: non-stationary arrivals, calendars, endogenous waiting, latent objections, cross-case effects, and no optimality claim. It is not nearly flawless. Several concrete modeling and statistical errors would change the twin or the policy ranking.

The process tree in the skeleton is not the tree that was supplied. `*('Completeness check', X(tau, missing-document path))` turns the document loop into the redo branch and deletes `Completeness rework`. `*('Inspection scheduling','Site inspection')` makes site inspection a redo of scheduling and deletes `Inspection rework`. The note about “tau escaping” is wrong: unquoted `tau` is valid PM4Py syntax. If that string parses, the fallback never runs and the engine is built on the wrong control flow. Appeal and planning/legal rework from the narrative are also never reconciled with the simplified tree.

Service-time estimation contradicts itself. The performance DFG is described as a direct service-time source, but with only completion timestamps a PM4Py performance DFG stores inter-activity sojourn time (wait plus service). The later convolution warning is the correct one; the earlier instruction is not. The proposed fix—“completion gaps conditional on resource availability, waiting is the residual”—is circular, because availability and waiting are what the simulation is supposed to generate from service times.

D2 and D10 depend on “no prior violations,” which is not in the log, and the case object defaults `prior_violations` to 0. That silently treats every low-value simple renovation as eligible. Removed emergency decisions are also dropped from the missing-data assumptions, so the log is treated as an unbiased population.

The experiment table lists S1–S4 as separate rows rather than re-running baseline and D1–D10 under those stresses, so robustness is not actually identified. Common random numbers are claimed, but different control flow (skipped inspection, fewer document loops) changes the number of draws; same-seed CRN is then misaligned. The selection rule uses non-overlapping marginal confidence intervals, which ignores pairing and is stricter and less valid than a CI on the paired difference. Fairness, rework, and backlog have no numeric thresholds despite the request for thresholds. An 85% utilization cutoff can reject a policy that productively uses idle capacity or keep one that is already overloaded in peak season.

The calendar object cannot represent constraints the prose lists: Monday portal-clerk diversion, two simple-only planners, or urgent legal work during council weeks. A 12/6 chronological holdout ignores that March–May and September–November are 1.7× seasons, and completed-case cycle times are not protected against right-censoring of the 260-day tail. “Replay validation” is only conformance and DFG comparison, not trace-driven replay of historical arrivals through the resource model.
