The final answer is complete and often directionally sound, but it is not reliable on model semantics or evidence traceability.

Both briefs exist, and each side makes the required concession. Case labels are mostly defensible: SUP-703 and SUP-708 are rightly severe noncompliance, SUP-705 and SUP-714 are rightly tied to expired authority, and SUP-707, SUP-709, and SUP-710 are rightly treated as scope or model problems. The layered architecture covers control flow, data rules, resources, overlays, and scope. Those strengths do not offset the concrete errors below.

The revised POWL misses the behavior it claims to encode. `Request_More_Info` is mandatory and unordered relative to registration, so it is neither optional nor repeatable. PM4Py POWL does support `Operator.LOOP`; the claim that it cannot express a loop is false. Quality review is globally required before the decision, so the auto-approval route cannot represent PD-3’s delayed quality review. The emergency route makes provisional activation concurrent with quality and manual approval and then requires another activation after all three, which contradicts FD-7. The same transition objects are reused across exclusive branches. Sanctions-before-activation is only an accidental consequence of the global checks block.

M0 itself is misread. Sanctions and quality are concurrent, so quality before sanctions is not a violation. SUP-708 is noncompliant because activation precedes sanctions and approval follows activation. The matrix also contradicts the record: SUP-702 already has a stable `notification_id`, and SUP-711 already shares a source transaction ID, yet both are described as missing the very evidence that would settle them. SUP-706’s log trace omits notification, so “compliant flow” is wrong. SUP-704 is inside FD-7, not conditionally post-expiry. Master-data validation, the 14 ineligible and 9 incomplete auto-approvals, and the compliance observer’s refusal to weaken CS-9 are largely unused. Several sentences drop the relation between trace conformance and policy compliance, and “requesterapprover” is garbled.

The judgment therefore rests on a flawed control-flow model and several inaccurate evidence cells, even though many individual classifications happen to land correctly.
