The diagnosis is right and the required structure is complete: June’s collapse is treated as stacked logging and provenance failure, August sign-off is separated as policy-plus-stale-model, missing compliance checks stay unresolved, and an optional sign-off model is flagged as precision-risky. Variant counts, DFG edges, alignment steps (32% → 58% → 81% → 89%, then 66% → 86%), and the audit fractions match the brief. The four-phase program isolates instrumentation before behavior, and the governance rules are concrete.

Those strengths do not make it nearly flawless.

The synthesis repeatedly calls fitting-trace rates “fitness.” 32%, 58%, 81%, 89%, 66%, and 86% are perfect-trace shares from the alignment table. Alignment fitness is a different quantity; with mean costs of 0.74 down to 0.12 it would not be 0.32. That slip is repeated in the ranking, the causal write-up, and the conclusion, and it overstates how broken the log is.

The “contradicting evidence” cells are partly misused. Partial `Classify` retention and incomplete `Queue Routed` coverage are what the chronology and the ledger itself describe as a mixed rollout. They support logging and extraction change and weaken a single behavioral shift. They are not evidence against those hypotheses. The same table gives “real behavioral process change” a posterior of 40 even though the 1 August sign-off rule is a documented process change. Section 7 corrects that; the ranking does not. Posterior and explanatory share are also blurred: the timestamp defect is narrowly scoped but strongly confirmed, yet it is scored as if limited scope were disconfirmation.

The adjacency list omits the 3 June gateway that introduced `Queue Routed`, one of the three mechanisms the prose relies on, and it draws `measured_fitness` into observed business outcomes. Fitness does not cause cycle time, approvals, or appeals. In the counterfactuals, extending the normative model is predicted to change cycle time. A model edit does not change operations; that cell confuses an intervention effect with a prediction of the policy.

The code follows the requested PM4Py calls and states the €50,000 and overgeneralization caveats, but normalization returns plain lists of dicts with no conversion back to an event log, so the later DFG and alignment calls are not reliably executable. It also never notes that every listed April–May variant is in the language of the normative tree while reported perfect fit is 93%.
