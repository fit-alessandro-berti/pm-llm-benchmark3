**8.0**

The stage arithmetic is unusually careful and mostly exact: flag-specific means, the path-weighted cycle-time identity (16.51 h and 8.17 h), and the Kitagawa split (−2.59 / +2.78 / +4.62 / −0.05 / +3.40 / +0.25 / −0.07) all reproduce from the timestamps. Absent stages are kept as conditional `NA`, occurrence is separated from waiting, natural effects are rejected for the right reasons (no overlap, exposure-induced mediator–outcome confounding, shared-queue interference), and P0–P4 are mapped to separable components rather than to “set flag = 0.” The identification menu and the added-versus-reserved P3 pilot are concrete enough to change a conclusion.

Those strengths do not make the answer nearly flawless.

The display-only intervention is not held fixed. Section 1 defines P1 as hiding `A_D` while leaving lane, mandate, score, and capacity in place, so its triage effect should be about zero if the lane is a system rule. Section 6 then gives P1 a −2.24 h triage-wait and −0.35 h service effect, which is the effect of switching the whole bundle off. E2 puts both contrasts in one row. E4 calls a capacity intervention an interventional indirect effect and “equivalently” a controlled direct effect; those are different estimands, and E9 correctly says a controlled wait is not well-defined.

Two resource claims are false. Manual service is not 2.0–2.5 h for every M1/M3 review: R17 is M1 and lasts 3.0 h, the same as other High cases. “High complexity requires M2 regardless of the flag” is also false: R17 is High and was reviewed by M1. The de-prioritisation evidence is weaker than written. R05 (M3) versus R01/R03 (M2), and R17 (M1) versus R13 (M2), are cross-label; M2 intervals overlap, so those starts are not one server choosing a later case. The same-label inversion that does exist, R05 before R08 on M3, is not the example used. The synthesis still treats “3 of 4 overtakes” as the mechanism clue.

Smaller faults: the local window `[0.55, 0.70]` contains five cases (R17, R09, R20, R15, R08), not four; conditional escalation wait is 7.00 h versus 6.50 h, so the diagram’s “lengthened to 7.00 h” is the level, not the contrast; flag-0 Low triage service is 0.775 h, not 0.77 h; `complexity → flag` and a direct `flag → escalation` edge conflict with a deterministic score threshold and with escalation only after review; the negative-control gaps are described as if the test had already passed. The performance DFG is completion-to-completion time, and the warning never says that this mixes the next service into the edge.
