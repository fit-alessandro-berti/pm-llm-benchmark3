7.5

The main account is right: the fitness collapse is mostly measurement and model lag, and the residual missing compliance checks are still an open provenance question. The staged 32→58→81→89 and 66→86 repairs, the refusal to treat log moves as employee blame, the precision warning on an optional sign-off, and the four-phase isolation are all strong. The write-up is not nearly flawless, because several concrete claims are wrong.

The ranking treats standard-versus-enhanced routing as roughly stable. It is not. Enhanced review is 180/600 (30%) in April–May, 170/650 (26%) in June–July, and 270/620 (44%) in August–September. That shift is used as evidence against a real process change, and section 7 then says flat cycle time, approval, and appeals show that day-to-day operations did not materially change. Those KPIs do not measure path mix. The same variant tables show `Request Info` in 40 April–May cases and in none later. Because the loop is optional, fitness cannot see that disappearance, so recovered fitness does not imply behavioral stability. Neither fact is analyzed.

The event-count story is also over-closed. Expanding the June–July variants gives 3975/650 = 6.12 events per case, not the published 6.3. Losing the `Request Info` loops should have offset part of the `Queue Routed` increase, so “+0.85 and +0.34, consistent, nothing unresolved” is not a reconciliation.

Two counterfactuals misfire. Mapping `Risk Screen` to `Classify` alone does not restore pre-June shapes; `Queue Routed` remains, and `Receive → Classify` becomes 650, not about 620. Optional sign-off does not make the 210 sign-off traces fit if the log is unchanged, because those traces still contain `Risk Screen` and `Queue Routed`. The 66%→86% figure already assumes the earlier repairs, so that row contradicts itself.

Smaller overstatements remain. The mechanisms are date-locked and separable, but not independent. `Queue Routed` was emitted and wrongly kept, not fabricated. Section 7 states that the ETL reversed all 70 traces after the ledger correctly left 59 unverified. The causal list also sends inserted technical events through `recorded_labels`, which blurs relabeling with event insertion.

The code, audit plan, EUR 50,000 caveat, and six governance rules match the requested apparatus and do not repeat those numerical errors. The grade is held down by false variant arithmetic used as discriminating evidence, not by the overall causal direction.
