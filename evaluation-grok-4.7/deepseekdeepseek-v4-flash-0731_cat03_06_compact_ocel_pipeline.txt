The dataset construction is largely complete and internally checkable, but the required analysis does not describe the log the code actually builds, and the pipeline as written would fail at the object-centric Petri-net call.

The four tables cover all 17 events, all 12 objects, timezone-aware timestamps, payment amounts as event attributes, and the required order–item, package–item, delivery–package, invoice–order, and payment–invoice links. Integrity checks match the stated constraints, and sections 3 and 8 agree with the code. Qualifiers are stable, and the source-fact versus propagated-relation distinction is set out in sections 1 and 6.

That policy is not applied consistently. Section 1 limits each event to objects named in the narrative, yet event e8 (Pack P2 with I2) is also tied to O100. Package–item O2O uses `contains`, the same qualifier as order–item containment, so the two business relations cannot be separated by qualifier alone.

Section 4 then predicts a different log. Under the coded relations, O100 is only Create Order, Add Item, Add Item, Reserve Stock, Pack Package, Pack Package, Issue Invoice, Close Order. O200 is the shorter analogue without the second add and second pack. Dispatch, Record Payment, and Confirm Delivery are not order relations, and Confirm Delivery is not a package relation. The predicted order and package traces include those events and justify them as direct relations. The same mismatch appears in section 5: Record Payment does not share an object with Close Order, and items are never linked to Dispatch, so those OC-DFG edges are not implied. The interaction summary also drops the Add Item co-occurrence of O200 and I3. Likely per-type DFG structure is thin: O100’s repeated Add Item and Pack Package activities, and V1’s repeated Record Payment, are self-loops that are not identified.

`pm4py.discover_oc_petri_net` returns one object-centric net, not a net plus two markings. Unpacking it into three variables stops the script. The commented `view_oc_petri_net` call does not match the usual `view_ocpn` entry point. The individual-object token story for the net is also stronger than what an object-centric net determines.

Failure modes, alternatives, and the non-uniqueness caveat are present and mostly on target. The grade is held down by the break between the constructed relations and the predicted flattened and object-centric behavior, plus the non-runnable discovery step.
