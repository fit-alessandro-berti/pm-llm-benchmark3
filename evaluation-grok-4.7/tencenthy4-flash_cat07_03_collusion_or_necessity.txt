The audit judgment is well calibrated and meets the required structure. It treats the evidence as a mixture, separates routine specialization, emergency handling, data-quality artifacts, and the V-77x bank-to-payment cluster, and it does not accuse anyone. Priorities, benign alternatives with support and refute tests, and the report wording are specific and within the 200–300 word range.

The score is held down by section 4. Several calls are not valid PM4Py usage: `format_dataframe` is given `case_id_key` rather than `case_id`; `filter_paths_performance` is called with two activity strings instead of one path tuple, so the bounds bind to the wrong arguments; `insert_case_service_waiting_time` uses the wrong parameter names; and `n=2` is unlikely to be a direct argument of `discover_subcontracting_network`. The quarter-close window `2024-03-28` to `2024-04-05` is invented and inconsistent with a last-quarter review as of 2026-11-17. The working-day check also treats a justification timestamp before the override as compliant because a negative `busday_count` is `<= 3`. The report’s phrase “documented emergency … processes” is slightly stronger than the stated specialization and rush-channel notes.
