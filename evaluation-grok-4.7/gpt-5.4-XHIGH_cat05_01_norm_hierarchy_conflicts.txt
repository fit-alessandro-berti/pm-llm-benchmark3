7.0

The memo is structurally complete and often precise on time, scope, and non-curative later approvals, but several classifications are stronger than the author’s own rules and the texts support.

Section 1 correctly separates formality from issue-specific override, refuses to treat ECD-2 as an A7 Chief Risk Officer exception, and keeps A1 and A3 outside the CFO email. The A2 “functional satisfaction” move is disclosed, yet it still does too much work. Dual Incident Command authorization is treated as the independent budget-owner approval even though the charter does not make Incident Command budget owners, and a blanket email is treated as the transaction-level Finance Controller approval that A2 requires before commitment. D1’s own cap and D4’s “essential integrity controls” sentence are noted in the hierarchy and then under-enforced in the compliant cases. The stated burden—that the organization must prove the exception—is later relaxed without saying so.

The hierarchy and rule catalogue are the right shape, but N6’s “normally pre-commitment” becomes, in application, an unqualified pre-commitment duty. B2 requires Plant and Finance approval at EUR 25,000 and does not time them before commitment; A2 does that only from EUR 50,000; B5 preserves Finance and the supplier register, not a pre-commit clock for both approvals. N11’s scope column also drops the critical-lot limit that Section 1 adopts, so the pseudo-logic can satisfy finance for any in-window purchase.

P-502, P-504, and P-506 are well decided. P-502 correctly keeps new-supplier Compliance pre-commitment and correctly refuses to backdate the 15 July email. P-504 fails on register and ordinary PO timing even if cafeteria spoilage were forced into B4. P-506 correctly rejects an oral extension after an express expiry, and A2 still fails because Finance alone is not the independent budget-owner approval.

P-501 is the clearest overcall. NONCOMPLIANT at high confidence depends on pre-commit Plant and Finance controls the local emergency text does not state. The four-hour stoppage fits B4, the form is inside 24 hours, the supplier was approved, and both approvals occurred the same day. Delegation is a problem only if SHIFT-1 was purporting to be the Plant approver. This was the case that most needed an alternative, and it receives none.

P-505 and P-507 show good timestamp discipline: the 17:59:31 acknowledgment beats the ERP stamp, and the 12:40 PDF is inside four hours of 08:55. Calling both COMPLIANT does not follow the author’s burden or N11. Requester and authorizer independence is unproved in both, P-507 never ties the seals to a sterilization line, and Plant approval is simply absent. P-508, where separation is actually proved, is the case labeled JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. That inverts evidential discipline. JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION is also never defined against COMPLIANT and INDETERMINATE, so P-508’s middle label functions as undisclosed uncertainty. No case is classified INDETERMINATE.

The P-503 alternative is invalid on the author’s own catalogue. Moving the pumps inside ECD-2 and relaxing A1 still leaves A3 unmet: there is no Compliance clearance on a EUR 140,000 purchase, and N3 is not waived. The P-507 alternative attacks the weaker ERP-versus-PDF point rather than the A2 and scope problems that would actually flip the result. The P-508 alternative only explores a more lenient COMPLIANT reading, not the stronger adverse reading that D1’s sufficiency stops at EUR 150,000 and D2’s “as well” adds Legal on top of A2 rather than replacing it.

Section 6 draws the right cure line for P-502 and P-506, but it misfiles P-508 as a proof failure when the residual problem is authority, and it omits P-507 from the unproved-compliance category despite the same missing identity map cited for P-505. Section 7 is the cleanest part: CRO authority, SoD, new-supplier clearance, register suspension, finance concurrence by a finance actor, evidence channels, written extensions, non-cure, and a concrete anti-normalization trigger are all drafted. That clause would reverse the email-substitution theory used to clear P-505 and P-507, and the memo never says so.
