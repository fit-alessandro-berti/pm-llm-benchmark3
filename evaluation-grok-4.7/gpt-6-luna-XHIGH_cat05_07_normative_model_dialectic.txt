6.5

The briefs, matrix, and burden allocation are unusually careful, but the required control-flow revision is not executable and several classifications contradict the answer’s own standard of proof.

The LOOP is invalid. PM4Py’s `Operator.LOOP` takes exactly two children (do, then redo); exit is implicit. The three-child construction, with a silent “exit” child, misunderstands the operator and fails at construction time. The information stage is also in the wrong place: it must finish before every route, so it cannot generate SUP-707 (`Sanctions_Check`, then two `Request_More_Info`, then `Quality_Review`). That is the exemplar the loop was supposed to cover, and the limitation is not stated. The same model then over-constrains permissive rules and adds events the endorsed traces do not contain. PD-3 says quality may follow provisional activation, but the pilot partial order requires it afterward and also requires `Automated_Master_Data_Validation`, which SUP-702 never logs. FD-7 likewise permits activation to precede review and approval; the emergency partial order requires post-activation `Daily_Transaction_Monitoring`, which is context, not a trace activity. SUP-702 and SUP-704 therefore fail the revised language that the judgment treats as their fit. Section 6 promises distinct pre-activation withdrawal and post-activation termination paths; the code has one `Withdrawal_Recorded` sequence.

Authority handling is internally inconsistent. Section 1 correctly says an unproved exception does not displace CS-9, and the matrix says SUP-702 becomes justified only once waiver authority is proved. Section 5 nonetheless labels it `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION`. Medium confidence does not repair a settled primary label. SUP-711 is the wrong bucket: identical source transaction IDs support a migration duplicate, so this is data quality (and, if confirmed, an otherwise ordinary compliant route), not a model or scope defect. “CS-9 is effective for all cases here” is also too strong beside the unresolved rejected-lead and reactivation populations.

The dialectical work is the strongest part. Both briefs concede the other side’s real points; 703, 705, 708, 712, and 714 are treated as genuine violations; 706, 709, and 713 are not inflated; notification weakness, alias separation, and label equivalence are traced to evidence rather than to replay mismatch. That quality cannot carry a normative POWL that does not run and does not accept the cases the judgment itself relies on.
