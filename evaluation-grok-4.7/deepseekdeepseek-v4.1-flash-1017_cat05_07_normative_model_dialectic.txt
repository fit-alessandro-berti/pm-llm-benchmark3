7.5

The dialectical analysis is strong: both briefs are serious, model and data defects are admitted on the prosecution side, real control failures are admitted on the defense side, and the case matrix is complete and traceable. The classifications are mostly well supported, especially SUP-703, SUP-704, SUP-705, SUP-708, SUP-712, and SUP-714, and the burden discussion is usable.

The score is held down by the revision, which is a core requirement. The POWL puts repeatable `Request_More_Info` only before route selection, so it cannot represent SUP-707’s requests between sanctions and quality. New labels (`Activate_Supplier_Provisional`, `Manual_Procurement_Approval_Retrospective`, `Automated_Master_Data_Validation`) are introduced without a mapping from the observed log, so justified historical traces will fail for labeling rather than behavior. Forcing every route activity, including deferred quality review, to finish before `Notify_Requester` is also unexamined: PD-3 allows quality up to two business days after provisional activation, while SUP-702’s notification is recorded at +2 hours. Reactivation and withdrawal stay in prose only. Those are semantic gaps, not presentation nits.
