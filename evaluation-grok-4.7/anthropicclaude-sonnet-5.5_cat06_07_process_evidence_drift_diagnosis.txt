The diagnosis is strong and matches the evidence: April is a pipeline failure, June is a routing-mix shift, July–September combine a parts-edge slowdown with V3-specific outcome change, September adds Partner Z, and October is not comparable because labels are immature. The mix-implied rates, constant-predictor Brier scores, and the September composition check (non-Z V3 staying near 36%) are arithmetically right, and the six required sections, falsifiers, training gates, and leakage warning are all present.

It is not nearly flawless. July’s claim that ECE looks fine “only because” V2 offsets V3 is stated as fact with no stratum-level scores. The covariate-drift row implicates August–October largely on features the same row admits are not model inputs. “About 2 SE” for April’s variant-rate uptick is high (closer to 1.5 SE), and “about 0.33” for the October projection is closer to 0.34. May’s residual is tied too tightly to the April cache backfill.

The sketch would also mis-audit. `events_before_dx_mean` drops cases with zero pre-diagnosis events, which is exactly the April failure mode. `get_variants` is treated as tuple-to-count, which is not stable across PM4Py versions, and variant shares are still computed on event-calendar slices whose traces are truncated. Those are concrete defects in an otherwise proportionate audit.
