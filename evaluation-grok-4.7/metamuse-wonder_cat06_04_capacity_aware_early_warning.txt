The memo covers all six required sections, refuses I-03 on stage grounds, separates I-05 from I-07, and prices spillovers, calibration, and label feedback. The allocation still contradicts the memo’s own rule, and that is the central defect.

The deployable score is `ARR_low × value − €450 − burden`. Checked figures: I-01 €920, I-02 €950, I-04 €3,550, I-05 €1,200, I-06 €1,500, I-07 €500, I-09 €2,350, I-10 €720. I-08 is **−€450** (`0.05 × €100,000 − €450 − €5,000`), but the table prints **€450**. I-03’s **€850** does not follow that formula at any point in `0.00–0.03` (the low end is −€850; the high end is €350). Step 4 says a slot is committed only if the robust score beats marginal spillover, given as about €700–€2,200 and, at the Section 3 central case, `6 × 0.02 × €12,000 = €1,440`. I-01 (€920) and I-02 (€950) miss €1,440. Joint robust surplus is `€920 + €950 + €2,350 − 3 × €1,440 ≈ −€100`. The base-case claim that the portfolio “clears its spillover hurdle mainly because I-09/I-01 carry it” is false for I-01 on the robust score. I-02 is marginal only at the low ARR; at the midpoint its pre-spillover surplus is about €2,700, so “marginal under base spillover” mixes two scenarios.

I-04 dominates I-01 inside the stated ranges. Even I-01 at the top of its ARR (`0.35 × €9,000 − €700 = €2,450`) loses to I-04 at its floor (€3,550). Both are first alerts with almost the same jump (+0.19 vs +0.18). Deferring I-04 for a persistence check while swarming I-01 immediately is special pleading. I-01 also has 7 hours left, so “capacity goes where windows are closing” does not describe this portfolio. A reserve plus I-09 and I-02, with I-04 first in line, would follow the written rule; `{I-01, I-02, I-09}` does not. Urgency can reorder near-ties. These scores are not near-ties.

Section 3’s midpoint arithmetic is mostly auditable: gross €2,385 + €3,750 + €4,800 = €10,935; burdens €1,650; cost €1,350; spillover €2,160 / €4,320 / €6,480; base net about +€3,600; adverse illustration about −€4,300. Two caveats change the interpretation. The sign of the base net is an assumption that untreated diagnosis cases are worth €12,000. At €25,000 the same `0.02` spillover is about €9,000 and the base net turns negative; the ±€5,000 note does not show that flip. The adverse band “€3,570–€5,900” does not match the lower-bound gross of €7,220 − €1,650 = €5,570. Full false-alarm burden is subtracted inside the optimistic row, where a high ARR makes a certain burden least plausible. The prompt’s template does this; calling the result an optimistic benefit overstates the downside already embedded in it.

Section 1 is not operational as written. Minus signs and several comparison operators are missing, so the robust-score line does not parse. “Segment watch level” is never defined. Strategic adjustment is described as “0.05 to 0.12” without a minus, although later ranges correctly subtract it (I-02 → 0.54–0.61, I-04 → 0.36–0.43, I-08 → 0.31–0.38). SME shifts (I-05 0.61–0.66, I-07 0.76–0.81) match the stated +0.04 to +0.09 band. The two-specialist, 45-minute team constraint is ignored: three “intervene now” decisions may be serial, and a third slot can push I-09 (2.5 h) or I-02 (3 h) toward `Diagnosis Complete`.

Sections 4–6 are the strongest. Cool-down, silent monitoring, re-entry on a new log or a real probability move, and escalation so suppression does not become neglect are concrete. I-05 (refresh, no new event) stays out of the slot contest; I-07 (new log, probability down slightly) re-enters and still loses on value and time. That distinction is right. Fairness controls are specific: duty-floor human review, a 60% segment cap, a value-blind review of the highest-risk non-selected case, separate treated/untreated training labels, propensity logging, and compensatory queue priority for spillover victims. Monitoring has owners’ metrics in substance: segment calibration triggers, a marginal-case lottery or stepped wedge, spillover stop-rules, risk-adjusted selection and delay ratios, and versioned threshold changes. The 60% cap, 0.8× selection ratio, and €12,000 externality value are unestimated policy parameters. The plan never says how that externality value will be measured, even though it decides whether the base case is positive.

I-03 is correctly barred. I-06 is correctly held for the imputed queue field. I-08 is correctly kept out on a negative robust value despite €100,000 at stake, except that the table’s positive €450 undoes the prose. The reserve and the adverse case where one selected file does nothing are sound. Those strengths do not repair a portfolio the memo’s own numbers reject.
