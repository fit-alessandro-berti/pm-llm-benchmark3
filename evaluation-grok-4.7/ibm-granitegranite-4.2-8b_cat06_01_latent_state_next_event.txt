The JSON is complete and the rank-1 calls are mostly the right local events (evidence review for A, B, and the second cycle in E; regulatory assessment for C and a defensible choice for D). A and B are not treated as the same case, and migration, queues, and reportability are used in places. Those strengths do not offset several control-flow and calibration failures.

Midpoints are not a coherent top-three distribution. Approximate midpoint sums are about 1.13 (A), 1.25 (B), 1.05 (C), 1.15 (D), and 1.50 (E). The calibration note treats that overflow as acceptable uncertainty; it is not. Several forecasts also confuse “could finish later today” with “this is the next log line.”

`process_enabled` is true on every row, so the required distinction between an enabled action and the next visible completion is never made. That matters: Customer Evidence Requested before review, Fast Close before review, Close — No Safety Issue before regulatory assessment, and technical/regulatory completion before the second review are not currently enabled.

Case-level errors: C6-A understates supplier analysis (same lot plus three similar complaints) and ranks a second evidence request and a technical-investigation completion ahead of the review that would enable them. C6-B’s contradicting-evidence list actually supports Fast Close; historically Fast Close leaves triage, while this case already entered the evidence cycle, so the post-review terminal is Close — No Safety Issue, not an immediate Fast Close (range up to 0.50). C6-C treats supplier-analysis completion as rank 2 even though the complaints are on different lots and that activity takes 6–36 h; closure is predicted while a reportability signal still blocks disposition. C6-D calls regulatory assessment “pending” although R-R2 opened it at 12:40, so it is in progress and its completion is early relative to a 2–14 h clock; a delayed evidence event is at least as plausible as the next feed line. C6-E inverts the state: the second receipt at 13:20 with no review puts the case in evidence review, yet the primary label is an open investigation fork. Technical and regulatory completions at 0.30–0.50 and 0.20–0.40 ignore the 40-minute-old evidence package, the low-severity queue (no overtake), and the mandatory supplier analysis, which never appears in the three predictions.

The cross-case DFG caveat is sound. The forecasts still over-weight slow or normatively blocked completions and mis-file supporting facts as contradictions.
