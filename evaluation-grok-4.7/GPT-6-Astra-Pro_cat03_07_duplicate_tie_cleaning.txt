8.5

The pipeline is conservative, arithmetically consistent, and unusually careful about provenance and false directly-follows structure. It is not quite tight enough to treat as nearly flawless.

The duplicate tree separates technical replay, cross-system identity, summaries, harmonization collisions, true concurrency, and unresolved cases, and the required pairs follow that policy. Keeping e05 and e14, refusing to attach M1/M2 to e15, splitting release from acceptance, and merging only the reconciled scan and invoice are the right minimum-change calls. The counts follow: 17/44 raw, 15/39 after dropping e04’s three identical links and e16’s two, 14/37 if e07 is absorbed as a subset of e06, 13/34 for the guarded collapses, and 10/28 if picks and captures are also union-merged. The `have_common_object` warning is exact: O1 and V1 are shared context, not payment identity.

Ordering is also mostly right. e12≺e13 is correctly limited to M1; e09≺e10 is the only logistics precedence actually supplied; pick-before-pack is not invented; original timestamps stay authoritative; and the selected extensions match a lexicographic linearization. 3×3×4=36 is the right extension count for the stated conservative partial order. The sensitivity claims that depend on those selected orders—Pick→Pick falling from two Order occurrences to one, I1’s self-loop disappearing, Pack and Dispatch “rework” becoming label or merge artifacts, and V1/M1 cobirth appearing only if authorization is placed first—are real.

The gaps are in completeness and classification. Under the stated 10:15 constraint, e09, e10, e08 is also admissible, so Handover→Release is a possible DFG edge, but section 3 only names e08, e09, e10 and e09, e08, e10 while section 7 still counts three extensions. Noon likewise has four positions for invoice issuance, not the two listed. Within-source sequence is used as a hard constraint, including e04’s sequence transferred onto merged e03 and e13≺e14 for distinct payments, yet it is never clearly marked semantic or merely deterministic, which was required. The “both captures feed e15” edges are disclosed in prose and then unconditionally hardcoded, so that assumption silently shapes the noon DFG. The implementation is careful and fail-closed, but it was not run, assumes e2e/o2o/object-changes tables, and uses the library merge mainly as a relation-collapsing subroutine before restoring canonical rows. Those are omissions and coupling risks, not wrong duplicate decisions.
