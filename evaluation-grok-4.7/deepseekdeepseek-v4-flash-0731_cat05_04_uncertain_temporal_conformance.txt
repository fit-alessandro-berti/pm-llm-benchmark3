The interval arithmetic, DST-fold filtering, SLA and temporal-profile classifications, and the 0/2/5-minute and zeta 1/2/3 sensitivity results are consistent and almost entirely correct. Clock-offset direction, resolution-before-offset, CEST/EDT/BST conversions, the T-405 order constraint (only alert in CEST and the later two events in CET), T-404’s [18, 25] min back-calculation, and T-407’s send-plus-latency window are handled properly, including one-sided SLA passes that still miss a two-sided profile band (T-409 S2 vs ISCC; T-408 across zeta).

The required PM4Py block is not executable. HUB intervals stay timezone-naive while OPS, vendor, legacy, and mobile intervals are timezone-aware, so the first duration subtraction raises `TypeError`. SENSOR times are naive and then passed through `astimezone`, so they are interpreted in the host zone rather than as UTC. `pm4py` is imported before any interval output, so a missing or incompatible library aborts the analysis, and the midpoint path only prints a disclaimer instead of comparing midpoint durations with the interval classifications.
