6.5

The write-up is complete and often arithmetically careful, but it is not causally disciplined enough for a high score. The crude-versus-complexity reversal is real and correctly computed (about +6.7 h crude; about −2.1 / −2.2 / −3.4 h within Low / Medium / High; pooled-mix standardized contrast about −2.5 h). Positivity failure, the group-as-bundle problem, completed-case selection, and a phased alternative to the 50% move are all present. Those strengths are offset by repeated over-identification.

The verdict `mainly confounded` at 85/100 does not match the prose. The executive assessment and the closing recommendation treat the stratified gaps as evidence that Orion is faster and that reassignment will almost certainly raise cycle time, daytime congestion, and appeals. The high-complexity contrast rests on two Nova cases; night-by-complexity contrasts include cells of size 1; appeals are one per group. Stratification is reported with caveats, then used as if it identified a team effect. “Entirely spurious,” “not inherently slower,” and “eliminates operational risk” go past the evidence.

The same Simpson structure is missed where it cuts against the narrative. Compliance is treated as a structural +14.8 h driver. Complexity almost determines compliance: every High case requires it and every Low case does not. In Medium, the only stratum where compliance varies, required cases average about 17.8 h versus about 18.3 h when not required, and their waits are shorter. The crude compliance gap is case mix. Rework is said to add about 7–9 h. Within High Orion, rework cases average about 32.0 h versus about 31.2 h without rework; other within-stratum increments are roughly 1–2 h, not 9 h. The crude residual (cycle − wait − service) of 2.12 h versus 1.41 h is offered as support for a harmful Orion close-delay. Within complexity those residuals are about 0.8 versus 0.9, 1.8 versus 1.9, and 2.93 versus 2.95. Hypothesis 4’s main quantitative support is the same composition artifact the answer correctly diagnosed for the headline mean.

Causal vocabulary is loose in ways the task specifically required to be sharp. Compliance composition is called mediation of the group effect; `review_service` is called the direct effect even though it is a mediator; a randomized pilot is labeled a LATE. The graph sends `quality_outcome` into `case_completed`, reversing the 30-day outcome, and sends `rework` into `queue_wait` although measured wait is pre-review and includes compliance waiting, not the information-request loop. `unobserved_reviewer_skill` is downstream of `resource_group` in the graph and a confounder in the table. “M-bias” is the wrong name for conditioning on a post-treatment collider. Hypothesis 5’s contradicting “evidence” asserts active Nova pipelines that the extract does not contain. The pilot never treats queue interference: moving cases changes the other group’s backlog, so a 15% Medium randomization is not a scale-free effect of moving half of Orion’s work.

Section 7 is the cleanest part: pooled complexity weights are explicit, overlap is tabulated, small cells are warned, traces and DFGs are compared without invented timestamps. Section 8 names useful fields. Secondary slips remain: overall wait is about 58% of cycle time, not “over 60%” or a general 58–64%, and “92.9% night-shift under high load versus 25%” pairs Orion’s joint pattern with Nova’s night share alone.
