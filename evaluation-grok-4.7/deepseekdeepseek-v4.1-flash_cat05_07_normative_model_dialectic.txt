6.8

The final answer is complete, case-complete, and genuinely dialectical: both briefs concede the other side’s real points, all 15 cases are classified with a dominant reason, and the serious breaches (SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-708, SUP-712, SUP-714) are separated from model, scope, and logging problems. Traceability to CS-9, PD-3, FD-7, the CRM change, and the aggregates is mostly precise, and dates, amounts, and eligibility status are handled correctly.

The revised POWL has a material semantics error. `Operator.LOOP` with `Request_More_Info` as the do-child executes that activity at least once, and the loop is a required node of the standard partial order. Every standard trace without a request, including the canonical compliant case SUP-701, is therefore outside the proposed normative language. The note discloses an interleaving limitation and leaves this cardinality consequence unstated. `LOOP(silent, Request_More_Info)`, or an XOR skip around a one-or-more loop, was the defensible approximation. The auto route also turns PD-3’s permission to defer quality review into a requirement that quality follow activation.

Authority and burden are applied unevenly. Section 1 correctly treats PD-3’s force as disputed—the decision was never incorporated into CS-9, and the compliance observer later denied approving a weakening—yet SUP-702 is a high-confidence justified exception without resolving that hierarchy or the missing master-data-validation event. SUP-704 is likewise high-confidence while the matrix still lists retrospective approval as missing. SUP-715 is marked noncompliant from “none was recorded,” which is a supportable control finding only if failure to record is itself the breach; the architecture then slides into treating the spousal relationship as prohibition-plus-invalidity, beyond the stated disclosure rule. The required concurrency point is barely argued: M0 already leaves sanctions and quality unordered, so their order is not a deviation.

Several quantitative and segregation statements are unreadable because the comparison operator is missing (“requester  approver,” “spend  EUR 20,000,” “Quality  2 business days”). The pre-screen rejection route is also baked into the single POWL while the prose still says rejected leads may belong in a separate model. Those defects keep a strong, well-structured review well short of nearly flawless.
