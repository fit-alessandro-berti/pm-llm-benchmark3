The interval arithmetic, time-zone conversions, DST-fold filtering, and baseline pass/violation calls are largely correct and carefully bounded. The main defect is the SLA acceptance region `[0, threshold]`. “No later than” is only an upper bound, so a start before the anchor still meets S1. That does not change the zero-tolerance labels, but it falsifies the +5-minute sensitivity cells and the accompanying prose for T-407 and T-409, which would be certain passes once every feasible duration is at most 1200 s. The mobile entry-delay choice is stated, yet its material effect is not: treating it as occurrence uncertainty is what pushes T-406 S2 over 45 minutes; as record latency only, that pair is a certain pass. The PM4Py block also never actually compares midpoint diagnostics with the interval results.
