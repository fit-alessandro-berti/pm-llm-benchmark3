The repair is coherent, conservative where it matters, and unusually careful about circular E2O/O2O confirmation, flattening duplication, and unique-object versus incidence counts. It is not nearly flawless: the main ambiguity is miscalibrated, and a few justifications are softer than the statuses and confidences attached to them.

That status is stronger than the epistemology; provisional would match the circularity warning. R15’s “evidence against” cell is really just weak evidence for. Those are calibration and clarity defects, not broken lifecycles or bad arithmetic. They are enough to keep the answer out of the top band.
