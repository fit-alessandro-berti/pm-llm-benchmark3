The equivalence analysis is strong: the visible languages, both-direction counterexamples, P1/P2/P3 verdicts, and the bounded cardinalities are correct. The score is held down by incorrect exact Petri-net metrics and a non-defensive exploration implementation.

The language claims check out. With \(Q\in\{AC,CA,ACP,CAP\}\) and \(D=AP?(RAP?)^{n-1}\), Model A is \(O\,Q(RQ)^{n-1}F\) and Model B is the shuffle of that \(D\) with exactly one \(C\). Those sets meet only in \(OACF\), \(OCAF\), \(OACPF\), and \(OCAPF\). A1, A2, B1, and B2 are valid complete witnesses inside the P3 bound, so neither language contains the other, and P1 and P3 are not equivalent. P2 really does collapse both models to the same family of observations: \(O\) first, \(F\) last, \(n\ge 1\) analyses, rework exactly when \(n>1\), and the Boolean “a check occurred.” The 76/85/4 counts follow from the length formulas \(3n+1+k\) and \(2n+2+k\): A loses only the \(2^3\) fully approved \(n=3\) traces, and B contributes \(5+20+60\).

Section 7’s converted-net figures do not. Under the standard process-tree translation, size depends only on the multiset of operators and leaves. A and B have the same multiset (sequence of 3, sequence of 2, parallel, loop, XOR, six visible leaves, one \(\tau\)), so both nets are 10 places, 10 transitions, and 22 arcs, and arc-degree simplicity is the same value, \(5/6\). The reported 9/9/20 versus 7/7/16, the simplicities \(0.81818\) versus \(0.77778\), and the claim that B is a smaller net with a worse score are false. The simplified tree is genuinely smaller (8 nodes, depth 4, no \(\tau\), net 9/8/18), but the stated 6/5/12 and \(0.84615\) are the same kind of unsupported exact values. The counting code would not print those figures.

The bounded search is the right algorithm only if `semantics.execute` copies the marking. Classic pm4py mutates and returns the same object; the loop then fires later enabled transitions from a corrupted marking and stores aliased markings on the queue. A copy is required for the claimed exhaustive result. The trace-set arithmetic itself does not depend on that run.
