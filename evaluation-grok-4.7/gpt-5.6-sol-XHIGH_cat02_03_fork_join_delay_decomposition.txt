8.3

The delay decomposition is internally consistent and arithmetically reliable. Upstream time, branch queue, service, synchronization, fork-join elapsed time \(R_c=\max_i R_{ic}\), downstream delay, final service, and total flow all reconcile case by case, including the worked cases F02, F04, F07, and F11. The double-counting identity is right: \(\sum_i(Q_{ic}+S_{ic}+W_{ic})=3R_c\), so adding every branch’s path to one case clock yields \(T_c+2R_c\). Critical-branch counts (A 2, B 8, C 2), critical leads (15, 120, and 12 minutes), and the three counterfactual savings (15, 92.5, and 176) check out. Zero-benefit cases, partial masking when another branch takes over, and the fixed-appointment versus movable-final bounds are handled correctly. The two-server FCFS diagnostic is also right under its stated assumptions: the only capacity wait is 5 minutes for F11, so 171 of the 176 downstream minutes are not explained by pooled two-server contention. Format, tie-breaking, and the pandas-versus-PM4Py split meet the requested structure.

The score is held down because supplied interval and resource evidence that changes the queue interpretation is left unused. `final_resource` alternates perfectly, odd cases to F1 and even cases to F2, which is direct evidence against pooled dispatch. B and C are not FCFS either: later cases overtake earlier ones, and C is observably idle during part of the F04 and F08 launch delays, including a gap from 10:03 to 10:20 before F08 starts. Concurrent overlap also lower-bounds capacity (A at least 4, B and C at least 3). Section 7’s claim that branch capacity is unknown is therefore too absolute, and the “B queue might be insufficient capacity” recommendation is weaker than the timestamps allow. The reproducible aggregate still labels a 176-minute saving as a third final resource even though the prose correctly attributes almost all of that to dispatch policy. Smaller nits are the rounded mean equation 19.6+61.0+14.7+20.0=115.3 against an exact mean of 115.25, and calling an exact 10.0-minute sample P90 drop “about 10.”
