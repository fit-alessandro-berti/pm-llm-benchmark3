The final analysis is strong: severity is separated from raw alignment cost, silent tau is treated as a fit, and the zero-cost P1/P2 failures (CL-609, CL-610) are not mistaken for compliance. Pay-before-approval (CL-604), the unevidenced high-value fraud screen (CL-602), telemetry/retry noise, label mismatch, external assessment evidence, and denial/withdrawal scope mismatch are classified correctly, with confidence distinguished from severity and missing events not treated as proof of non-occurrence. Remediation order, caveats, and section 6’s counterexamples match the control facts.

It is not nearly flawless. The materiality framework never incorporates recurrence, and timing is only implicit. “Four monthly” auto-approve failures adds a time scope the evidence does not state. Pattern 2 can be read as attaching the bank-change fact to CL-604. In the code, CL-607 is given amount 0 and risk “DENIED,” CL-604 is flagged `high_risk_missing_fraud` despite MEDIUM risk, and the `M(Assess Loss)` total mixes an external-evidence gap, a denial-model artifact, and a label mismatch. “P4 likely met” for CL-608 is a bit stronger than a mutable link supports.
