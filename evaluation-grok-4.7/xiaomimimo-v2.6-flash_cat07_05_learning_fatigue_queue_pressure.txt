The final answer meets the required six-heading structure, covers every requested mechanism and intervention, and respects the constraints: fatigue is not treated as the sole cause, early complexity and late queue pressure are both retained, raw late-shift punishment is rejected, and quality checks are not dropped to raise throughput. It is not nearly flawless. In the integrated explanation, 4.6% to 7.9% and 8.2% to 13.7% are called “more than doubles”; neither change doubles.
