7.5

The forecast is complete, well structured, and numerically careful on the main handover comparison (343 vs 359 events; about 588 vs 722 frequency-weighted hours). It covers every required heading, keeps East distinct from North/South, treats fairness and learning as first-class effects, and correctly recommends a conditional pilot rather than a full rollout. The central claim that management’s own table does not show fewer total handovers is sound.

Points come off for concrete flaws. The pilot guardrail requires Legal/Risk p90 wait to stay at or below the current 9.6h median; a p90 will normally exceed that median even if performance improves, so the trigger is mis-specified. “Median wait exposure” is a sum of medians, not a median. Phase 0 is both 8–12 weeks and “at least 12 weeks.” The 14-day SLA used in the sketch is inconsistent with the reported 6.5–9.8% miss rates given p90 cycle times of 16–23 days. In the PM4Py sketch, `discover_organizational_roles` is unpacked into two return values, and `get_variants` is handled as if keys are activity sequences and values are collections; either assumption fails on common PM4Py return types and would break the comparison. Segment labels are also invented without being marked as examples.
