7.5

The audit is substantively strong: it catches label-adjacent leakage, complete-trace aggregates, point-in-time SQL defects, the nightly document snapshot, closure-time team and role fields, and mutable scores; it keeps queue length and elapsed time; and it treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as later-feasible while treating `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` as infeasible and timestamp-ambiguous. The five required sections, the table coverage, and the case JSON are all present, and leaked futures are not retold as drivers.

Those merits do not make the write-up reliable enough to score higher.

The process check misstates the required three-way status of activities. Appeal, settlement, and closure are labeled impossible at `t0` because their preconditions are unmet, while the same appeal–decision pattern is then called future-possible. Unenabled and impossible are different claims. `NotCoExistence(Claim Closed, Damage Assessment)` is the wrong declarative constraint: those activities co-occur on conforming traces; precedence is the rule, and that constraint would reject normal cases. Damage assessment is named in the enabled set and then omitted from the enabled-but-unobserved list.

Several sentences lose the contrast they depend on (“Correlation causation,” “Predictive causal actionable,” “measure prediction,” “validity justification”), so the predictive-versus-causal standard is clear only from neighboring sentences. `breach = total_duration > 10d` overstates the label: duration is measured to closure, while the target is settlement. A non-zero document count at this prefix is treated as a logging error rather than a possible conformance deviation. Appeal’s correlation is attributed to “the long appeal cycle,” which is the sort of causal story the audit otherwise rejects.

Case narratives drop `claimant_region` and `claimant_channel` even though both are available at `t0` and section 3 correctly keeps them as reviewable proxies. `initial_reserve` is listed as a credible driver while its as-of time is still unverified. Directions for queue, complexity, and elapsed time are taken from SHAP values conditional on leaked covariates, without saying those signs can move after a refit. “Refit or recalibrate” leaves a calibration-only path open for a model that has effectively seen the outcome. Elapsed time is also filed as congestion, and the Case Z recompute list casually includes dropping queue.
