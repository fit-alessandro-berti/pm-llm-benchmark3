7.3

The portfolio judgment is directionally sound: I-03 is correctly excluded on stage grounds, I-05 is suppressed as unchanged refresh churn while I-07 is reopened because of the new log, imputed data blocks I-06, and capacity is not exhausted. The optimistic and base arithmetic is reconstructable and checks (I-04 midpoint 0.19 and I-09 midpoint 0.24 give ~0.43 breaches and ~€13.4k before the €900 labor cost). Those are real strengths. The write-up is still not internally consistent enough to treat the rule as deployable.

The stated sort is conservative value first, using lower-bound effect × breach value − false-alarm burden − €450 − a €1.5k spillover charge. On that formula the floors are about +€2,050 (I-04), +€850 (I-09), ~€0 (I-06), −€550 (I-02), −€580 (I-01), and −€1,950 (I-08). Promoting I-02 ahead of a cleared I-06 contradicts that rule. I-08, which has the second-best midpoint net (about +€3,050), is left off the promotion list with no statement of whether the idle slot is governed by maximin or by expected value. The €1.5k hurdle is also lower than the base spillover the impact table itself implies (~€1.8–2.4k per slot at 2 × 6 × 0.02 × €15–20k). The €15–20k untreated value is load-bearing and unjustified; if the “six” are the broader book rather than the two or three visible diagnosis-stage cases, the sign of the base net can move, and that sensitivity is never shown.

Calibration is described and then not used. Adjusted probabilities never enter the score, and there is no check that absolute risk reduction cannot exceed calibrated risk. For this book the cap happens not to bind, but that is luck, not a demonstrated control. Non-Native-Language Support is supposed to be prioritized on the lower half of an already unreliable effect range; I-09 is then selected because the published floor still clears €1.5k, with a fairness sentence standing in for a shrinkage or human-confirmation rule. Section 3 evaluates that same case on the full 0.18–0.30 range. The adverse row (0.10–0.15 breaches, €3.2–4.2k) is the right stress — I-04 at its floor and I-09 near zero, false-alarm cost still paid — but the path is not written down, and “Confidence: Low” is then used as the reason to stop at two slots.

Smaller integrity problems accumulate. I-05 is stage-eligible and has never been swarmed; “Eligible now? No” conflates suppression with ineligibility. I-10 is tier C (“do not intervene”) but the action is “Wait.” I-01’s uncertainty note blames a moderate effect when 0.18–0.35 is the widest lift in the set and the constraint is the €9k value. A 90-minute cool-down is not reconciled with I-02’s 3-hour deadline. Acting now on I-04, which still has 10 hours, is not compared with the option value of the slot. The reserve is asserted rather than priced against the marginal case. Randomizing live swarms for doubly robust learning has no exclusion for regulatory or public-sector cases and no sample-size guard, so the ECE > 0.05 trigger can thrash on the sparse segment.

The required topics are all present, and the main yes/no calls are defensible. The policy as written does not match its own ranking rule, its own externality price, or its own calibration adjustments, so it is not nearly flawless.
