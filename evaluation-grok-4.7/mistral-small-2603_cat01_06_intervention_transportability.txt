The recommendation is directionally usable — conditional go, limited to cases that do not legally need originals — but the supporting causal, quantitative, and formal models are not reliable enough to treat the answer as a transport analysis.

Section 1 invents a Site A legal-review requirement of 10%. The source states that Site A high-value cases do not require a separate legal review. The mandated loops feature is absent. Site B’s existing concurrency is misstated: identity check and originals receipt may already both start after registration, while document check is blocked only when originals are legally required. The rework row calls 11.8% to 12.4% “no change.”

Section 2 does not meet the 180–280 word requirement, and the graph omits `API_latency`. `true_overlap` has no edge to `waiting_before_assessment` or `cycle_time`, so the graph does not represent the mechanism the prose asserts. `originals_required`, `digital_documents`, and the two `S_` nodes are dangling. Capacities are drawn as causes of overlap rather than modifiers of whether overlap reduces waiting. `cycle_time -> appeal` is an unsupported causal claim.

Section 3 still gives the confounded nine-hour median drop the causal reading “parallel verification reduces waiting,” at transport confidence 60. Missing rows include completed volume, the 18h-to-12h pre-assessment wait, both service-time changes, appeals, the absent negative control, and the timestamp-recording artifact. Service time fell at Site A, so part of the nine hours is not concurrency. The overlap row does not treat the measurement change as non-transferable.

Section 4 covers the required modifiers, but “38% digital” equates two different facts: originals are required for 62%; a digital-channel share is not given. Role specialization is treated only as a barrier. Separate roles are what make simultaneous work possible; the lock and the lack of slack are the barriers. Several “beneficial signature: none” cells erase partial gains, and “lower rework” contradicts Site A, where rework and appeals both rose.

Section 5 has no derivation from Site A’s six-hour wait reduction or Site B’s 3.4h and 8.1h service times. Overlapping those checks can remove on the order of the shorter branch, not 8–12 hours. The digital-only scenario is more optimistic than the scenario labeled optimistic. The IT-lock failure still predicts a 1–3h gain. “10% of legal cases get supplements” is false: 360 supplements out of 1,360 legal reviews is about 26%. The DFG counts are otherwise unused. One scenario does correctly let the median improve while p90 worsens, but the bottleneck-shift row itself improves both.

Section 6’s prose correctly keeps the originals prerequisite and the assessment join. Its partial order does not. Physical cases omit `Originals Receipt` and `Notify`, serialize identity before document, and never place identity before assessment on the digital branch. The supplement path returns to document check and drops the required repeat of assessment. Adding huddles bundles a Site A co-intervention into the treatment.

Section 7 contradicts itself: digital-only eligibility, stratification by physical cases, and a physical control group cannot all be the design. Case-level randomization on shared pools does not identify a rollout effect, and capping treated volume at 20% prevents observation of the saturation failure the pilot needs to learn. σ = 12h is incompatible with a median of 44h and a p90 of 110h; under the stated normal-means formula those assumptions imply about 90 cases per arm, not 300. High overlap with no cycle-time gain is a downstream-bottleneck signature, not evidence that the case lock blocked parallelism. The prose maps that signature backwards.

Section 8 does not call `pm4py.parse_process_tree`. The Site A roots that are converted have no children; the constructed nodes are never attached, and leaf nodes are given children. The POWL classes and `OrderingRelation` API do not match PM4Py’s `StrictPartialOrder` and `OperatorPOWL`. Identity is ordered before document in every case, which is the sequential simplification the task forbids. The supplement edge creates a cycle, approve/reject nodes are reused under two XOR parents, and `check_soundness` is never called. The limitations note correctly lists data conditions, capacity, locks, and durations, but the model itself is not an executable candidate.

Section 9’s conditional-go choice and the refusal to copy full-population parallelization are the strongest parts. The expected 6–12h gain and the claim of possible rework reduction are not supported by Site A. The expansion rule “rework ≤ 15%” is stricter than Site B’s 17% baseline, so an unchanged quality result would block expansion. Assumption 3 says specialization both prevents dual-skilled work and is required for parallelization, and then says that falsifying it would enable parallelization but still be infeasible. Assumption 8 builds huddles into the causal package. The central untested assumption — that Site A’s before/after contrast is not an identified effect — is not in the register.
