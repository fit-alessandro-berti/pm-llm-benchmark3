7.5

The analysis is structurally complete and, on the hardest points, disciplined: issue-specific hierarchy rather than a single ranking, prospective application of ECD-2 and the CFO email, non-curative treatment of later approvals, and correct preference for the supplier acknowledgment and signed PDF over skewed ERP times. P-502, P-503, P-504, P-506, and P-508 are robust under the stated method, and the alternative readings actually identify the premise that would flip them.

The score is held down by internal contradictions, not by missing sections. R1 is marked satisfied for P-501 while approver identities are listed as unknown, so segregation of duties is asserted rather than shown. R11’s four-hour recording element is an “apparent deviation” for P-505 and treated as satisfied for P-508 on the same kind of silence. B6/R9 is adopted as a persisting duty and then checked only sporadically. P-501’s primary NONCOMPLIANT result depends on reading B4’s permission to substitute an absent Plant Manager as a prohibition on SHIFT-1 committing; that is a strained reading of a permissive clause, even though the COMPLIANT alternative is disclosed. “Unapproved” is equated with A3’s “new supplier,” and local Finance_Approve is equated with the GPIP Finance Controller, without argument. The COMPLIANT and JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION definitions overlap, and the claimant’s burden is applied strictly to scope but not to the unresolved budget-owner element in P-505 and P-507. Those are defects of consistency and evidential discipline in an otherwise strong assessment.
