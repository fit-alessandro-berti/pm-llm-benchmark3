5.5

The baseline UTC intervals and most included SLA/profile labels are numerically careful, including the Berlin DST fold filter and the T-404 start inference. That core is outweighed by errors on the exact certain-versus-possible distinction, incomplete pair coverage, and a code section that does not do the required analysis.

The sensitivity matrix treats T-402 S1 at +2 minutes as a certain violation. The feasible duration is about [958.5, 1231.5] s, so a 1020 s limit is only a possible violation: part of the interval still passes. The follow-up claim that the minimum (~958 s) exceeds 900+180 s is false, and “about 6.5 minutes” to reach a certain pass should be about 5.5 minutes. “All others: no change” is also false. At +5 minutes, S1 becomes a certain pass for T-403, T-404, T-405, T-406, and T-407. Across zeta, omitted flips include T-402 and T-404 Investigation→Containment, T-409 Investigation→Containment, T-402 and T-408 Alert→Containment, and T-408 Containment→Close (certain violation at zeta 2, certain pass at zeta 3). Several zeta cells contradict themselves before settling.

Testable Alert→Containment rows are missing for T-403, T-406, and T-407. T-407’s unconstrained duration is written as [183, 1023] s; the lower end is about −183 s. Section 1 says reversed order stays in the duration interval, but T-407 and T-409 replace that with a hard zero, and T-409 then uses the untruncated start for S2. Section 6 labels T-408’s zeta-2 Investigation→Containment profile as a certain violation after Section 3 correctly called it possible. The mobile “up to five minutes late, in addition to the clock bound” is set aside as logging delay only, without showing that putting it on occurrence time can turn T-406 S2 from a certain pass into a possible violation.

The Python block does not implement interval normalization, standard-library zone conversion, DST alternatives, or interval classification (`to_utc_interval` is `pass`). The “midpoint” log parses Berlin, New York, and London wall times as UTC, so it is not a midpoint of the feasible UTC intervals, and the PM4Py call is only wrapped so its failure can be printed.
