The top plan is mis-specified. “D + C (parallel)” does not run C in parallel. C is a late fallback: wait past 45 minutes, then interrupt S2. That trigger is dominated. P-884 has 90 minutes; an S2 review is 35–75 minutes plus 5–15 minutes to record and execute. Interrupting at minute 45 leaves about 45 minutes, so the deadline is met only on the fastest review tail. P-901 has a 70-minute deadline. A 45-minute interrupt started at minute 45 lands after P-901’s deadline. The stated +0.12–0.28 spillover is for an immediate 45-minute interrupt, and it is applied unchanged to this later, harsher intervention. S4’s 35–65 minutes is also treated as the review duration. That is the arrival window. Review time is additional, so waiting for S4 often misses the 90-minute deadline even when screening runs concurrently.

The continuation model misstates the control. Risk at or above 70, or an unresolved fuzzy match, requires a second officer before approval or execution. It does not mandate a hold or a suspicious-activity report, and it does not bar approval. The given approval range, 0.55–0.75, is the prediction for a conformant review of this case, which already has score 72 and an unresolved 0.46 match. Section 2 parks that probability on a “match resolved” branch and sends the unresolved branch to “Reject or SAR (no execution).” The clearance cutoff “confidence < 0.2” is invented.
