5.5

The fork-join framework and the main ranking are sound, but the required numerical work and counterfactuals are not reliable enough for a high score.

The definitions are the strongest part. `join_ready` is the latest branch completion, synchronization waits are concurrent, and the explanation that summing those waits double-counts elapsed time is correct. Total flow is implicitly upstream + fork-join elapsed + downstream queue + final service. Critical-branch labels and frequencies are right: A 2/12, B 8/12, C 2/12. Ranking B above A, despite A’s longer mean service (50 vs 37.5), follows queue plus service and critical frequency rather than service alone. The four worked cases (F02, F04, F07, F11) match the data. The limits section covers causality, unknown branch capacity, preemption, and release policy versus a true queue.

The case table is wrong for F12: `join_ready` is 12:10 and `final_start` is 12:20, so downstream queue is 10, not 20. That error carries into the capacity counterfactual (176 minutes total and about 14.7 minutes mean, not 186 and 15.5). Section 3’s synchronization means are also wrong. Correct means are about 11.0, 4.3, and 30.4 for A, B, and C, not 10.2, 3.3, and 32.3. The listed C waits sum to 360, not the stated 388. C’s queues sum to 125, so the mean is about 10.4, not 9.6; the reported C-queue standard deviation does not follow from either total. Several 5-minute near-ties (F01, F08, F10) are never stated.

The counterfactuals miss the slack test the task requires. Cutting A’s service from 50 to 40 saves only 5 minutes on F01, because B ends at 08:55 and becomes critical; it does not save 10. Halving B’s queue does not save the full half-queue on every B-critical case: F05 saves 10 rather than 12.5, and F10 saves 5 rather than 10, so the total is 92.5 minutes of `join_ready`, not 100. F01 and F11 are also zero-benefit cases for the B-queue cut, not only F04 and F08. Downstream masking is asserted but not measured, even though the F1/F2 schedule shows it. F05 is held to 10:20 by F03, and F09 is held to 11:30 by F07, so earlier branch completion need not move `final_start`. The final-resource column is otherwise unused. The redesign claim that adding F capacity without fixing B would accumulate WIP reverses the congestion mechanism. Assumptions are not labeled, and `join_ready` savings are reported as flow-time savings.

The Python does not implement the claimed results. The timestamp parser double-counts minutes, is applied to case IDs and resource labels, and would fail. PM4Py is called with invalid argument names (`timestamp`, `time`, `case_id`). Per-case counterfactual savings are computed in columns but never printed. The narrative totals also disagree with the max-based logic the code was attempting.
