7.3

The design meets the required eight-section structure and respects the hard constraints: non-stationary demand, calendars, cross-case coupling, latent objections, and no optimality claim. It is not nearly flawless.

PM4Py handling is the clearest defect. `pm4py.discover_dfg` returns only the DFG, and `pm4py.discover_performance_dfg` returns only the performance DFG. Unpacking both into three values is inconsistent with current PM4Py: the frequency call crashes, and the performance call is swallowed by the bare `except`, so the required performance DFG is never produced. The fallback for variants is fine, as are `format_dataframe`, `parse_process_tree`, and pandas `read_csv`. The lifecycle timing logic also collapses repeated activities (both completeness checks) onto one service time and defines waiting from the previous event rather than prerequisite ready time, which contradicts the correct definition in the estimation plan. Performance-DFG edge times are not flagged as sojourn times that mix wait and service.

The simplified tree is treated as structural only, but its real limits are understated. The loop operator encodes “Completeness rework” and “Inspection rework” as atomic redo activities, while planning, legal, and appeal rework from the narrative are absent. The narrative is also sequential, while the tree is parallel; that conflict is not identified, even though P001’s timestamps cannot confirm overlap without start times.

Decision criteria are partly ungrounded. Simple ≤30 days and Complex ≤180 days sit poorly against the given p90s (45 days with missing documents, 260 days for complex variance), and the “+10 points or ≥85/80/70/60%” rule is ambiguous. Safety and missed environmental harm are decision criteria, but no outcome process is specified, so the twin cannot score them. Utilization bands of 65–85% are not tied to the long observed waits.

The experiment matrix covers baseline, D1–D10, and five stresses with plausible trade-offs, but effects are not bounded by variant shares (19% missing documents, 9% inspection rework, 8% complex/appeal). Warm-up for seasonality, common random numbers, separate streams, quantile intervals, and heavy-tail mixtures are sound. Gaps remain: no initial WIP for replay, no check that events fall inside HR calendars, a six-month holdout that can confound seasonality, and no multiplicity control across ten policies and many metrics.
