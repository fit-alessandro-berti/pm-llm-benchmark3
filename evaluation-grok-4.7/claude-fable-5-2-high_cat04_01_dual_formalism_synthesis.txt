The models are faithful, the trace-language argument is sound, and the quantitative claims check out. Both constructions match the narrative: low versus high risk is an unguarded exclusive choice after root-cause analysis, supplier consultation is an at-most-once silent skip on that same window, and the trial is `ET IT (AP ET IT)*`, so a passing inspection is loop exit and `Adjust Parameters` cannot follow it. The POWL graph is the right canonical model. Its twelve edges are the covering precedences, and the thirteen incomparable root pairs are exactly the required overlaps, including approval and supplier against the whole trial block.

The process tree is a genuine duplication-based, language-preserving approximation rather than a hidden relaxation. `CM < PV` puts `Analyze Root Cause` in one of three positions, and each branch is the series-parallel order obtained by adding that case and closing transitively. The branches are disjoint and exhaustive, the artificial XORs are resolved by the next visible label, and same-label leaves sit under XOR ancestors. Bounded counts confirm the claim: 7,896 traces with at most one adjustment and 20,952 with at most two, with the stated baselines at 4,588, 4,815, 1,408, and 37,367 unintended extensions. The eight witnesses are correctly classified, W5 exposes the duplication without pretending the languages differ, and W6 is a real cross-branch failure at `Execute Trial` before `Plan Verification`.

Defects are small. Assumption 2 points to point 7 for missing concurrency, but point 7 is about duplicate labels; the concurrency loss is point 8 and §3.4. The repeat-until row treats unbounded iteration as an over- or under-constraint even though the narrative requires it; only the pass/fail abstraction is a real loss. The milestone alternative is written out, while the 4,815 and 1,408 trees are only described, and the reported net sizes and `add_transitive_edges` check are not produced by the validation script itself. None of that changes the behavior of `tree` or `powl`.
