5.5

The core control-flow distinction is right: in A, each loop iteration is `(Analyze ∥ Check)` then optional `Approve`; in B, exactly one `Check` is concurrent with the whole analysis/rework loop. The P1/P3 non-equivalence and P2 equivalence-under-abstraction verdicts follow from that and are defensible. The rest of the deliverable does not hold up under the stated requirements.

The counterexample table leaves the Model A and Model B cells blank, and only one trace is actually in A and not B. `T-A1` is in both languages, so it is not a counterexample. That misses the required two A-only witnesses. B-only traces are valid, but all of them put `Check` first, so they never show that B may place that single check in the middle or at the end.

The bounded explorer would not substantiate those verdicts. It never calls `pm4py.parse_process_tree` on the given strings (which are already pm4py tree syntax). It imports a nonexistent `ProcessTreeNode`, calls a nonexistent `pm4py.fire_transition`, and may fail when sorting place objects. Marking-only visited state is the deeper error: it drops the other interleaving of `Analyze` and `Check` and refuses to re-enter the loop marking, so multi-iteration traces inside the P3 bound are not enumerated. Presenting that search as exhaustive bounded comparison is incorrect. Section 6 states the right caveat that footprints and EMD do not prove language equality, then undermines it: B does not make `Check` consistently precede `Analyze` (they are concurrent), impossible traces have probability 0 rather than “exponentially lower” probability, and no stochastic or EMD code is given.

Complexity figures stated as exact are wrong. Both trees have 12 nodes, not 13; neither tree has duplicate labels (repetition comes from the loop operator); B is deeper than A once the XOR under the inner sequence is counted. `simplicity_petri_net` is never used. Calling approval placement “identical” is also too strong: in A, `Approve` is after both `Analyze` and `Check` of that iteration; in B, the single `Check` can sit before, between, or after `Analyze` and `Approve`.

The “simplified” model is copied from A, with no `simplified_model` assignment and a preservation table that marks every property exact because nothing changed. That does not meet the requirement to show a simpler structure against both originals and to say what is exact, weakened, or lost. P1 is a reasonable target, and the recommendation not to execute B as the operational model is sound, but the supporting code, counts, and simplification do not earn more than a partial score.
