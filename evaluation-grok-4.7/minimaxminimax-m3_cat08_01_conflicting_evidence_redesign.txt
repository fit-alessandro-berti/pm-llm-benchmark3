The diagnosis correctly frames the fast-path paradox and cites real artifacts (72% auto-screen volume, 2,550 fast approvals, fitness 0.91 with 68% fitting traces, the three move-on-log deviations, 14.1% vs 3.3% reopen rates, the 34 h / p90 88 h evidence loop, segment complaint gaps, and the fraud-review and 82% manual-review constraints). The three redesigns are genuinely different mechanisms, sacrifices are stated, and the experiment plan looks for downstream shift rather than edge-level speed alone.

Those strengths are undercut by repeated quantitative and rule errors. 14.1% is applied to the 2,300 dispatch edge (~324) as if that were all fast approvals; fast-approval inflows are 2,790 (2,550 + 240), and the observed complaint edges on that path are 140 + 260. “~14” repair-path complaints is undefined and sits poorly next to Repair device → complaint = 30 and Repair quote → complaint = 80. Segment share × conditional fast-path complaint rate (14% × 22% ≈ 150) is treated as the count of misrouted cases, which the log does not support. The 11,200-hour claim misreads a 30–45% cut in repeated evidence requests as deleting a full 34 h loop for 30% of 1,100 “cases/year”; the log window is never given as a year. The current-process “median ~10 h” is about the sum of means on one happy path (0.8 + 1.0 + 3.0 + 5.0) and conflicts with segment fast-path medians of 8, 13, 20, and 28 h.

Redesign A’s compliance claim does not match its own flow. R1 requires manual or fraud review before fast approval above EUR 400, but “Standard fast” sends Standard low-risk accessories above EUR 400, and mid-risk devices, to fast approval with only an evidence token. The table still says this yields a strong R1 reduction. The 11 h auto → fraud edge is also treated as review duration to be cut to 2–4 h, with an unsupported 60% / 5-minute split. “Pays back multiple times” asserts a payback the data cannot show: EUR 410 is an expected wrong-replacement cost, the ~340 figure again assumes every high-value case is both on the fast path and a complaint, and gate labor is not costed.

The Stage 1 halt rule is internally impossible. Standard high-value fast-path median is already 28 h, and the pilot adds 6–18 h, yet halt is “median cycle time on gated cases exceeds 24 h” (a Premium low-risk service-credit threshold, misapplied). Go (<10%, a ~44% relative drop from 18%) and adjust (relative drop <30%, i.e. still above ~12.6%) also leave the band between those rates with no decision. Repair-path rework (12.5% on high-value vs 4% on low-risk) and the slow Premium manual → repair-quote edge are barely used, so the reviewed path is still treated too much as the quality benchmark.
