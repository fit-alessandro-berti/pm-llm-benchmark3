The decisive defect is in the complexity numbers. For any Petri net, inverse mean arc degree is exactly \((|P|+|T|)/(2|F|)\). The reported sizes therefore force simplicity \(18/40=0.45\), \(14/32=0.4375\), and \(10/20=0.5\), not \(0.818\), \(0.778\), and \(1.000\).
