3.5

The write-up has the required six-part shape, uses a join maximum in the worked scenarios, and gets the primary incubation window right (Sunday 12:00–20:00). The forecast itself is not defensible: the headline remaining times, several calendars, and the parallel-update logic fail.

The central conversion is wrong by two days. Friday 18 September 2026 15:30 to Monday 21 September 12:30 is 69 hours; the summary states about 21 hours and “Mon 18 Sep.” The same 48-hour shortfall appears in the 22.5–25.5 hour “P50” band. Within the P80 line, 22.5 hours and 99 hours are not the same clock convention: Tuesday 22 September 18:00 is about 99 hours, while Monday 14:00 is about 70.5 hours. Monday 28 September 20:00 is about 244.5 hours, not about 234. “Mon 18 Sep” and “Tue 19 Sep” are impossible labels; the 18th is Friday and the 19th is Saturday.

Section 6 contradicts the join arithmetic in Section 3. There, smooth-path chemistry finishes Monday about 10:30 and microbiology about 09:00, so the join is chemistry. A Sunday readout then leaves the terminal time unchanged. The claimed ~15 hour pull-forward would require chemistry to be finished before the microbiologist arrives. The same error makes weekend authorisation look like a top driver of the smooth path after the decomposition has already made it nearly irrelevant there. It does matter for the contamination path, because it moves the start of the extra 20–30 hour incubation; the ledger’s claim that the contamination tail is unchanged is false.

Other material breaks:

- Scenario 3 starts a microbiologist readout at Tuesday 05:00, outside Monday–Friday 08:00–18:00. A 20 hour confirmation ending overnight is read from Tuesday 08:00, so the earliest terminal time is earlier than Tuesday 14:30 and the “05:00–16:00” readout range is not a staffing-feasible interval.
- B-417 is given a full triangular-mode 2.5 hour service from Monday 08:00. That drops the one urgent and three ordinary samples still ahead, invents an urgent duration of about 1.6 hours, and treats “cannot finish by 18:00 Friday” as “cannot start,” so any Friday partial service is lost.
- The QA story says five older batches plus about three weekend arrivals and then a steady-state M/M/2 wait of about 1 hour with two reviewers. Those cannot hold together. Friday 15:30–20:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00 are never used to clear that backlog. Mean service is taken as 2.2 hours; a lognormal with median 1.5 hours and 80th percentile 3.5 hours has a mean nearer 2.5 hours. “Six-month ticket data” and “internal KPI reports” are not in the evidence.
- A 12 working-hour documentation query opened Monday 12:30 consumes 7.5 hours by 20:00 and resumes Tuesday 12:30, not 10:30. A 14 working-hour documentation investigation from a Monday morning join finishes Tuesday, not “into Wednesday.” The stated 5 day laboratory centre is then reported as Monday 28 September, which is a 7 day offset from Monday 21 September.
- Overtime is rated high-sensitivity for every smooth path, although a Friday chemistry finish still waits on microbiology. The 60–80% documentation-versus-laboratory split is rated low-sensitivity while it is what creates the multi-day tail. The excursion effect is hard-coded as a doubling, which the brief does not state.
- Release probability 0.96 and rejection 0.04 are asserted without a path calculation. The P80 interval starts at the same instant as the median interval, so it is not an upper quantile. Smooth-path probability mass is not mixed over the 0.25–0.45 on-call branch.

The scenario menu, overlap note, and excursion as a secondary trigger are the parts that hold. They do not repair a remaining-time forecast whose elapsed hours, dates, queue, and critical-path update disagree with each other.
