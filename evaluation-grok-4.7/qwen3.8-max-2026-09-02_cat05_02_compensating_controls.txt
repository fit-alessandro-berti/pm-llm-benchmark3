8.2

The final assessment is strong, complete, and properly reasoned from control objectives rather than from nominal four-eyes counts or payment size. Same-user cases are split across met, partial, and failed outcomes, and the formally separated u12/u13 cases are not treated as automatically compliant. Timing, independence, coverage, override, recoverability, and collusion are applied consistently, including the hybrid reading of manager action after release but before settlement. ED-1 is handled correctly for AP-203 and AP-207, and realized legitimacy is not treated as control sufficiency in AP-208. All required sections, columns, monitoring signals, and the evidence-only code boundary are present.

Deductions are for remaining precision gaps. AP-204 is classified as a definite objective failure even though collusion is denied, recovery is unknown, and the log has no settlement event; that is a defensible judgment, but it is firmer than the evidence. “Merely mitigating” is defined and then left unmapped to the required classification labels. “Catastrophic” in the sufficiency discussion does not match the High rating later given to AP-208. In the code, PM4Py’s result is labeled as the violating set without stating that common versions keep four-eyes-compliant traces; the mismatch warning only partly repairs that. Vendor ages are invented approximations, one business day is reduced to 24 hours, and timestamp handling after `format_dataframe` sits outside the exception guard. None of these overturn the main conclusions.
