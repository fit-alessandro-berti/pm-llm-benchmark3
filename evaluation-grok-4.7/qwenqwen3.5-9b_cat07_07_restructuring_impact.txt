The response covers all seven required headings and stays appropriately cautious (pilot first, East treated differently, averages not trusted). It is not a reliable forecast: several core claims misuse the given data, the pilot cannot test the proposed change, and the PM4Py sketch would not run and omits required analyses.

The mechanism map treats “nominal handover reduction” as an expected drop in local specialist handovers. Management’s own table raises technical handovers from 36 to 41 per 100 cases and the return leg from 31 to 36; summed listed handovers rise from about 343 to about 359 per 100 cases. Role discovery is described as detecting ownership ambiguity, which that algorithm does not do. Legal/Risk, already at 91–93% utilization with a 9.6h wait and 14% post-handover rework, are given more volume (9 to 12 per 100) and barely drive the scenarios.

Baselines are repeatedly North-only figures presented as organizational: rework “8%” (volume-weighted rework is about 9.3%; East is 11.5%), cycle time “6.8d” (volume-weighted team medians are about 7.5d), and a 90th percentile of 16.4d (South is 18.2d and East 22.8d). Fairness text says North/South have similar case volumes to East (820 and 760 versus 520). Service time is repeatedly used to mean queue wait.

The pilot is internally inconsistent. Pooling N_Tech, S_Tech, and E_Tech cannot be tested by changing only East while North and South stay embedded, yet the recommendation watches for expertise loss in North/South. The window is “8 weeks” but sums to 2+6+1=9, and two pre-pilot weeks cannot observe p90 cycle times of 16–23 days. The East rework rollback is not calibrated to East’s already-higher 11.5% baseline.

The code sketch misses subcontracting, organizational role discovery, and filters on `required_specialty` and `customer_segment`. `format_dataframe` is called with XES arguments it does not take; handover and working-together results are treated as dataframes; `case_id='case'` does not match `case:concept:name`; `filter_event_attribute_values` is given a string instead of a value list; service time is requested for a nonexistent activity “Technical Review”; and the “cross-team” filter keeps every edge with frequency > 0. Learning is only a clause inside knowledge loss, not a separate observable (narrowing activity profiles, falling `local_expertise_flag`, heavier subcontracting).
