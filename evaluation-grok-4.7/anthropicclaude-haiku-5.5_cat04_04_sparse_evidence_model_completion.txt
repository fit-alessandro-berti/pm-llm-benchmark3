4.5

The delivered write-up is a strong partial draft of the model-construction half and an unfinished answer to the assigned task. Sections 6–10 are absent, and Section 5 stops mid-bullet, so the required audit table, comparison matrix, validation program, recommendation, data-collection plan, and calibrated uncertainty statement are all missing. Forward references to a Section 8 count check and a Section 10 sequence alternative are therefore unsupported.

What is present is largely careful. Variant totals, all 16 DFG weights, the 23/23 CC-before-SC regularity, the 21/23 vs 2/23 ER/SC split, and the closed languages of 7, 12, and 14 middles all check out. The DFG two-cycle discussion correctly refuses to treat mutual edges as concurrency. Completion-only semantics, stocked-skip versus lost SC, and the missing external/stocked attribute are disclosed rather than smuggled in. Conservative is a real closed-world XOR, balanced is a real POWL partial order, and permissive is a real process tree; the three are different hypotheses, and at least one of each formalism is used. Duplicate labels and factorial XOR blow-up are discussed.

The errors that remain are material, not cosmetic. The balanced section says a fully parallel block “admits SC before CC, which violates … D5,” while the same section correctly says D5 permits SC after ER alone. Those are the same family of orders; the justification contradicts the paper’s own balanced/permissive split. The balanced edge list is called “transitively closed” but is only a generating set: RO→CC and CC→AP do not list the implied RO→AP. “The 5 balanced-admissible traces and the 7 permissive-admissible traces” double-counts, because the 5 are a subset of the 7. Calling those conservative rejections “underfitting” inverts the fitness/precision/generalization distinction: that model is overfit, and its precision risk is low. Permissive never receives the required underfitting analysis, and its exclusion list is cut off. Concatenations such as “ERSCCC” are not tied to the declared abbreviations.

So the inductive core is credible, but the document does not meet the required format, does not validate anything it asserts, and contains an internal contradiction on the decision that is supposed to separate balanced from permissive. That is far from a complete, nearly flawless solution.
