8.0

The review is unusually strong on authority, scope, and dialectic, but it is not nearly flawless. Several internal contradictions and one unsupported empirical claim keep it well below a top mark.

The best work is the separation of M0 from CS-9. Reading M0 as exactly the four linear extensions of Register, then concurrent sanctions and quality, then XOR(manual approval before activation, reject), then notify, then close is right. So is the claim that M0 is stricter than CS-9 on registration, quality-before-rejection, checks-before-approval, and notification, and weaker on requester/approver segregation. Calendar derivations (15 Jun 2026 Monday through 2 Aug 2026 Sunday) check out, and the 2%→29% notification gap versus ~70% mirroring is calculated correctly. Both briefs are actually argued: the defense concedes SUP-703, SUP-708, SUP-712, expiry, and the nine incomplete eligibility cases; the prosecution concedes RMI, pre-screen rejection, duplicate registration, and label equivalence. The judgment does not split SUP-703, SUP-705, or SUP-714. Case-level classifications are mostly traceable, and MIXED is not used as a dodge. The overlay registry, frozen eligibility snapshot, alias/delegation/COI fields, and `event_ts`/`record_ts` proposal are concrete.

Defects that matter:

The system-level arithmetic is wrong. The table yields six NONCOMPLIANT cases (703, 705, 708, 712, 714, 715). Section 5.2 says five, while naming 712 and 715 as the two that fit M0. The 12-deviation breakdown (4 + 2 + 4 + 2) is otherwise consistent, so this is a summary error, but it sits in the conclusion.

The revised standard route reintroduces a constraint the review itself treats as illegitimate. Section 1 says CS-9 requires sanctions and quality before activation, not before manual approval. M1 still has `{S ∥ Q ∥ RMI} → MPA → ACT`. Register, S, MPA, Q, ACT, Notify, Close can satisfy CS-9 and still fail M1. That false fail is unexplained.

Layer 3 and Layer 4 disagree on pilot quality timing. PD-3 says quality may occur up to two business days after provisional activation. The DECLARE `response(Activate, Quality_Review)` requires quality after activation. The pandas helper returns 0 and passes if the review date is before activation. FD-7 does require post-activation review; PD-3 does not. The same rule is applied to both.

SUP-706 is classified DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE even though the review’s own norm says notification is not a CS-9 control. Under that framework, resolving the mutable CRM link changes M0 fitness only. Compliance with the binding standard does not turn on it. The matrix even calls the risk low because it is not a CS-9 control.

“Passes the soundness check” is not supported by the code block. The script converts to a Petri net and aligns; it never calls a soundness checker. The alignment narrative is semantically plausible, including emergency-route acceptance of SUP-704/705 and rejection of SUP-708, but the soundness sentence is an untraced extra claim. Naming Request_Reactivation rather than Register_Supplier for SUP-710 is also arbitrary: both XOR branches cost one model move.

Lesser issues: F14 (“FD-7 anticipates post-expiry completion”) is an argument filed as a stipulated fact; “weaker than CS-9” wrongly includes exception data conditions that are not in CS-9; PD-3 segregation is dismissed as n/a although a human still selects the pilot path; the exception burden says compensating controls must be shown, while SUP-702 is JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION with master-data validation unproven. That last point is partly rescued by the observation that master-data validation was never in the collected vocabulary, so absence is not a closed-world failure. The code’s loop orientation, fresh node objects, dedup by source transaction id, label map, and window checks are defensible, and the stated limits on route XOR, RMI-without-receipt, and withdrawal are the right approximations.

Dialectically this is close to expert work. The grade drops because the proposed oracle repeats a diagnosed M0 error, the declarative and data layers conflict on a control the review hinges on, and the final tally plus the soundness claim are not reliable.
