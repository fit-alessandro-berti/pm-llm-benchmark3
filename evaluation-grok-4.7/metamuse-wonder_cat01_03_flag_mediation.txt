7.5

The causal framing is strong, but the required timestamp decomposition is unreliable, and a few estimands and policy claims are looser than the rest of the argument.

The intervention section correctly treats the flag as a policy bundle, rejects natural direct and indirect effects as primary because of cross-world assumptions and post-treatment confounding, and separates display, review occurrence, review waiting, and escalation. E6 is not one intervention: “P0 vs P1/P2 combined” does not name a single contrast. Calling escalation both a mediator and a confounder is also imprecise.

The mechanism graph, interference discussion, occurrence-versus-waiting distinction, and identification plan are substantive. Designs say what would be identified and what result would weaken the claim. The P2 pilot has eligibility, cluster assignment, a queue guardrail, outcomes, data needs, and stopping rules, and it does not treat the shortest cycle time as automatically best. The code follows the requested PM4Py shape, keeps absent stages missing, and warns against causal DFG readings.

The stage table does not. Headline flag contrasts for triage wait, manual wait, escalation counts, and total cycle time check out, but several other cells do not: flag 1 manual service is 2.70 rather than 2.75 and residual 1.12 rather than 1.07; medium manual service is about 2.17 rather than 2.25 and residual about 1.24 rather than 1.09; flagged high-load manual service is about 2.83 rather than 2.92; escalated flagged triage wait is 0.20 rather than 0.18 and manual service about 3.08 rather than 3.25; non-escalated flagged triage service is 0.68 rather than 0.65. Residual mass also disagrees across the flag and complexity partitions, so the table is internally inconsistent. “Slightly shorter” triage service understates 0.60 versus 0.95 hours, and that gap is completely aligned with a T1/T2 versus T3/T4 split that the resource pathway never states. Low-complexity review is unobserved in this sample, not structurally impossible. Anchoring P2’s cycle-time gain on the selected flagged-but-not-escalated mean mixes a review-occurrence policy with an escalation contrast.
