The write-up has the right skeleton — parallel join as a maximum, weekend pauses, FIFO versus priority, excursion dependence, and separate rework tails — but the clock times and several branch calculations are not reliable.

Calendar labels are shifted by one day. Friday 18 September 2026 is followed by Monday 21 September, Tuesday 22 September, and Friday 25 September. The answer calls those days 22, 23, and 26 September. The early elapsed-hour figures match the true Monday (about 65 h to Monday 09:00 and about 72 h to Monday 15:00), so the hours and the written dates describe different instants. Later labels also pick the wrong weekday: 27 September is a Sunday, 29 September a Tuesday, 30 September a Wednesday, and 1 October a Thursday. The stated 171 h lands near Friday 25 September 18:30, not Friday 26 September 18:00 (about 194 h).

The earliest path is internally impossible. The ledger says B-417 cannot pass the urgent sample, so the first analyst to free (15:54–16:24) takes that urgent sample. B-417 can start only when the second analyst frees, at 16:42–17:30. Minimum chemistry service is 1.5 h, and 16:42+1.5 h is 18:12, after the laboratory closes. A Friday chemistry finish, including the cited 15:54–16:12 start, contradicts that rule. The “Mon 09:00–11:30, requires Friday chemistry plus Sunday readout” case should not be in the forecast. The same block lists a 16:10 start with only 0.5–1.3 h worked before 18:00; 16:10 to 18:00 is 1.8 h. The 0.5–1.3 h band matches a 16:42–17:30 start only.

Confirmation incubation is started from Sunday technical readiness (Sunday 12:00+20 h = Monday 08:00). The extra 20–30 h begins after a microbiologist records the indication. With no on-call, that readout is Monday morning, so confirmation is ready Tuesday morning–afternoon and is read Tuesday. The published Monday 09:00–Tuesday 10:00 usable window is about half a day to a day early on the common path, and it also understates how much the first-read on-call decision moves the second incubation.

The Monday QA wait is not rolled forward to the join time. A backlog of about 4–7 at 08:00 with two reviewers and a 0.35/h arrival rate is largely gone by mid-afternoon (utilization is only about 0.4). Adding a 4 h wait to a 15:30 join and spilling acceptance to 21:30, then Tuesday, does not follow from the answer’s own backlog. That bias is what pushes the stated median into Monday night–Tuesday morning. The worked query example is also wrong: 8 QA hours from Tuesday 10:00 ends Tuesday 18:00, while “Tuesday 10:00–20:00 plus Wednesday 08:00–10:00” is 12 h.

Driver ranking conflicts with the stage math. On the smooth path the answer’s own joins are Monday chemistry times, QA is closed Sunday, and a Sunday microbiology result usually does not move release. Ranking on-call authorization as the top driver, with a 10–16 h join shift, contradicts that, and section 6 then demotes the same decision to a co-secondary trigger. The excursion flag is a reasonable primary update for the tail; the claimed +1–3 day mean shift is large relative to the modest movement of the rerun and contamination rates only to the tops of the given ranges.

The percentile bands are too wide to be percentiles. The P80 interval runs from Wednesday morning through Friday evening, while the prose also says about 80% have released by Friday evening. S3a (probability 0.55–0.75) mostly duplicates the smooth Monday join rather than a distinct delayed-microbiology terminal time.
