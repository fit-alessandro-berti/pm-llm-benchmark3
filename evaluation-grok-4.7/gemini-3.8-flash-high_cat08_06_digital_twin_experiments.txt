6.1

The write-up covers every required section and is often technically serious, but it is not a reliable twin design. Several structural mistakes, invented “empirical” figures, and a simulation path that contradicts the prose keep it well short of a high score.

The process structure is wrong in a way that changes cycle time. The supplied tree is sequential after a three-way parallel block: planning with optional fire and environmental review, then optional public notice, then the inspection loop, then decision drafting, then optional legal check. Section 1 instead puts public notice and inspection into the same fork-join as the reviews. That shortens the critical path relative to the model the team actually handed over. Appeal, planning rework, and legal rework are mentioned, then collapsed into a vague 8–9% bucket.

The variant table is misread. Those rows are a partition of cases (they sum to 100%) with end-to-end duration, not transition probabilities or rework delays. “Simple with missing docs = 19%” is not \(P(\text{completeness rework})\); missing documents are an XOR inside the loop body, and that row is only one case-type group. “Inspection rework ≈ 0.09” with “median delay 80d / p90 160d” treats whole-case cycle times as the rework delay. Service-time lognormal matching is done correctly (\(\mu=\ln p_{50}\), \(\sigma=(\ln p_{90}-\ln p_{50})/1.28155\)), and sojourn versus service is explained in the estimation section. That does not repair the routing probabilities the simulator would be fed.

Many quantities are presented as measured when they are not in the log or the brief: applicant-response p90 of 22 days, clerk/planner/fire/environmental/inspector/legal utilization bands, statutory SLAs of 14/45/90/120 days, planning waits moving from 3 days to 9 days, emergency volume under 0.5%, council recess as days 15–21 every month, travel uniforms, a 25% objection contagion, and a 60% travel saving. Districts D1–D5 and urban/rural labels are filled in beyond “D3 and D5 take longer.” Objections are correctly treated as a latent mixture rather than observed events; that is then undercut by using those invented constants as if they were calibrated.

The experiment design names baseline, D1–D10, and three stresses, with real trade-offs (batch hold time versus SLA, legal pre-check waste, Monday smoothing versus architect resistance). It then states effects as if they were known: rework from 19% to about 3%, D10 cutting median by more than 35% and p90 by more than 50%. D2’s “no prior violations” rule has no violation state. D4’s temporary inspector is specified as peak-only, but the code increments inspector capacity for the whole run. D6 does not say which planner pool loses the FTE. The fairness ratio of citizen median to top-20 median ignores the assumption that top architects submit harder case types, so mix is confounded with favoritism.

Uncertainty treatment names the right toolkit (Welch warm-up, historical seeding, CRN streams, BCa, Beta–Binomial tails, Sobol, GPD, CVaR) and then overclaims it. A 60–90 day warm-up is not “typical” when complex-variance medians are 140 days and p90s are 260 days. \(R=50\) to \(100\) does not guarantee a p90 half-width under 5% without a variance estimate. “Kingman’s formula dictates exponential queue explosion” as \(\rho\to 1\) misstates the \(1/(1-\rho)\) approximation. A Kolmogorov–Smirnov test at \(\alpha=0.05\) on tens of thousands of cases will reject trivial differences. Utilization “empirical targets” used as validation gates are fabricated. Full-sample summary quantiles are not clearly barred from the months 1–12 fit, so the hold-out can leak. Token-based replay is invoked on a process tree; that diagnostic expects a Petri net plus markings.

The skeleton meets the narrow PM4Py I/O rules: `pandas.read_csv`, `format_dataframe` with the three standard keys, `parse_process_tree`, variants, DFG, performance DFG, an inter-arrival, and an explicit sojourn column. The engine is not coherent with the design. `sample_service_time`, applicant delay, public-notice mixture, calendars, and capacities are never used on the event path. Completion is a deterministic lead time by case type, so median and p90 coincide and resource changes cannot matter. D1 and D3 are no-ops. Environmental reviewers still contribute 3 FTE, not 1.8. Architect “bursts” are i.i.d. Bernoulli labels, not clusters sharing a district and a week. There is no routing function. Section 5’s CRN streams are a single seed. The prose forbids an independent-case model that collapses waiting and service; the executable path is exactly that, with no limitation stated at the point of use.

What remains is a thorough outline with correct lognormal quantile algebra, a sensible exogenous boundary for applicants and objections, calendars written down carefully, and a conditional decision rule that at least refuses to crown D10 without a paired test. The outline cannot be implemented or believed as written until the control-flow join, the variant-to-probability map, the invented inputs, and the execution semantics are corrected.
