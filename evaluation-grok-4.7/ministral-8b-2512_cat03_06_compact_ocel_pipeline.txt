3.2

The pipeline is not executable and does not preserve the business facts. Both `relations` and `o2o` are built from parallel lists of unequal length (`relations`: 35 eids, 30 activities, 33 timestamps, 38 oids/types, 30 qualifiers; `o2o`: 24 endpoints vs 23 qualifiers), so the DataFrame constructors raise before any check or discovery runs. The columns are also semantically shifted: duplicate object ids, qualifiers attached to the wrong activities, and false links.

Fact errors in the tables include Confirm Delivery D2 at 2026-04-06 14:00 instead of 2026-04-07 09:00, which reorders D2 relative to M2 and the closes. O2O does not encode the stated graph: O200–P1 is `contains`, P2–P1 is a link, D1 carries P2 and D2 carries P1, I1 and I2 are `reserved_by` O200, and both M1 and M2 receive both `partially_settles` and `completes_settlement`. Pack P1 does not connect I1, I3, O100, and O200; issue-invoice has only two relation slots for V1 plus two orders; each close is tied to two orders. Required checks for activity/timestamp agreement and duplicate relation rows are absent. `from pm4py.objects.ocel import ocel` and `ocel.get_activity_frequencies` / `get_object_type_frequencies` are not the PM4Py API, and `flattened.head()` does not exist on the event log returned by flattening. The `try/except` hides those failures. Section 1’s qualifier list (`issued`, `is_billed_by`) does not match the code.

The audit disagrees with the code (`packs` counted as 4 while the qualifier list has 3) and skips the main convergence event, packing P1 from O100 and O200. Flattened predictions contradict the stated E2O policy (order DFGs include dispatch and confirm; payments are described as attached to orders). Qualifiers are treated as DFG activity names. One invoice cannot duplicate e11 inside its own trace. Payment flattening is weak because each payment object has a single event and therefore no directly-follows relation; that point is missed. OC-DFG, OTG, and the object-centric Petri net are described as if P1, V1, M1, and M2 were type-level nodes, and the net is given one determined reading. The interactions summary is treated as a sequence model even though M1 and M2 never co-occur. The “source-minimal” alternative drops O2O `contains` instead of restricting E2O. Failure modes are one-line labels and do not note that this log has no timestamp ties. Semantic check 1 is false (O200 contains only I3). Check 4 invents an invoice total of 250 that is not stored in any table.
