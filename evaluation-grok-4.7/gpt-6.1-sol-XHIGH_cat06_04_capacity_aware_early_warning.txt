The policy is complete and arithmetically reliable, and it makes the hard case calls correctly. I-03 is refused because diagnosis is already complete. I-05’s unchanged 30-minute refresh is suppressed while a never-treated case can still compete later. I-07’s new log reopens review and still does not buy a slot. I-06 is held until the imputed queue feature is recovered. Calibration bands, midpoint and lower-bound margins, gross values (€19,950 base; €28,300 at the upper endpoints; €5,600/€8,000/€9,600 in the one-failure adverse splits), and the 18q spillover identity all check out. Effects are not treated as equal to `p_now`.

It is not nearly flawless, because the allocation it names is looser than the release rule it states, and the required impact table’s euro nets are not identified by the data.

The spillover money figures rest on T = €120,000, an invented total for six unnamed cases, and the adverse row further uses a 0.03–0.05 probability increment that the problem does not supply. Those two assumptions generate the headline nets (base about €4.4k–€11.6k; adverse about −€22.8k to −€6.0k). Under the same illustration at q = 0.03, I-09 and I-02 fail the marginal test while the three-case total stays positive — the subsidy pattern section 1 correctly forbids. Section 2 still names I-09, I-02, and I-04 as the noon portfolio. The sequential gate is real, but there is no observable stop rule for cancelling the next start once the first swarm shows the adverse regime.

Robustness is stacked unevenly. Charging the full false-alarm allowance in every scenario, including the optimistic one in which the swarms work, plus a pass/fail test at the extreme lower endpoint, is what drops I-08 (midpoint margin €4,550, lower −€450) and keeps I-02, whose illustrative marginal net is about €300. On that same pre-spillover lower bound, I-05 (€1,200) beats I-02 (€950). Deadline pressure can justify the swap; the comparison is never drawn. I-09 is booked first even though the rule itself puts Non-Native-Language Support under a zero-benefit stress, so the least validated effect creates the first spillover and delays I-02 by 45 minutes.

The reserve is not operational. Both specialists are booked 12:00–14:15, so a regulatory arrival during that window cannot use the held fourth slot unless a named swarm is preempted. Preemption order is unspecified.

Smaller defects: tier letters are not ordered by the scores beside them (I-08 is “C” at €4,550; I-02 is “A” at €2,700); the optimistic breach range starts at the base midpoint 0.58; “clinically” / “clinicians” is the wrong setting for this queue. The calibration bands are computed and then do not constrain τ. That is acceptable only because the effect ranges were supplied separately; the compatibility check the rule promises is not shown case by case.

Sections 4–6 are concrete enough to run (60-minute cool-down, distinct I-05 vs I-07 re-entry, segment service floor, propensity logging, queue-level randomization, untreated-risk calibration kept separate from policy calibration). Those sections and the correct dispositions are why the score stays high. The gap between the marginal release rule and the three-case commitment, and the fictional euro nets in the impact table, are why it is not higher.
