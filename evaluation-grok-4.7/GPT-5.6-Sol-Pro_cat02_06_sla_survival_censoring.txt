8.7

The analysis is strong, estimand-first, and numerically reliable. Time origin, the 72-hour horizon, competing terminal causes, and right censoring are separated cleanly, and the worked cases are right: S03 is an observed breach that is still active at 72, S09 is censored for eventual resolution time but already a known breach, S06 is a competing withdrawal, and S08 is an internal non-resolution whose external 72-hour result is unknown. The identified bounds (18/32–23/32 non-resolution; 9/32–14/32 still active), the primary Aalen–Johansen split (about 30.4%, 14.0%, 10.1%, 7.5%, 38.0%), cohort and priority stratifications, restricted mean active time (about 50.4, 49.6, and 51.1 hours), transfer sensitivities (69.6%, 68.7%, 62.1%), the net Kaplan–Meier figure (63.9%), and the landmark contrast (4/9 versus about 79.3%, with about 37.8% at 88 hours) are internally consistent. Open cases are retained. Withdrawal, rejection, and transfer are not treated as ordinary non-informative censoring. Escalation, rework, AutoCheck, and the frozen prefix are correctly kept out of baseline predictors. Cohort is treated as a non-randomized, incompletely applied policy proxy, and the P1-only transfer pattern is used properly as evidence of informative loss.

The deductions are for accumulated unclarities, not for a wrong primary estimand. The restricted-mean display is malformed (`RMST*` / `S*`), so that definition is not technically readable as written. Section 7 writes the alert target as \(P(T_R>72\mid T>t,\mathcal H(t))\) after \(T\) was defined as time to the first terminal event; that reintroduces an undefined latent resolution time beside the competing-risk quantities the rest of the paper uses. Sensitivity 2 censors withdrawal, rejection, and transfer together, so the withdrawal/rejection change is not isolated from the transfer change already measured at 68.7%. The 72-hour recovery set is the nine cases still observed after 72; the effect of informative censoring before the landmark on who enters that set is thinner than the discussion of censoring after it. The state diagram collapses Screen and AutoCheck into “preprocessing,” leaving observed transitions such as Screen→Resolve, AutoCheck→Resolve, and Screen→Reject/Withdraw implicit. Several headline secondary figures are argued in prose and are not emitted by the single code path. The PM4Py branch is conceptually compliant and does not invent timestamps, but `get_variants` is assumed to return trace lists, which will break if the installed version returns integer counts.
