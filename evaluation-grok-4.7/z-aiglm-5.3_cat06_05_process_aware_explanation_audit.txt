8.4

The audit is strong, complete, and correctly structured. It catches both obvious leakage and the subtler lineage defects, keeps the lawful congestion and prefix-age signals, treats `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` as process-legitimate but post-`t0` while treating `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` as footprint-impossible and timestamp-ambiguous, and separates prediction, causation, actionability, and fairness. The required table, conformance suite, class-wise claim limits, JSON, and prioritized validation plan are all present and mostly precise.

The score is held down by a few real lapses. `initial_reserve` is placed among Case Y’s credible drivers even though its freeze time is unverified, which violates the rule to use only information defensibly available at `t0`. The repaired `prior_claim_count_before_t0` is given direction “increase” although the corrected count is unknown. `total_case_duration_hours` is called a near-deterministic function of the settlement-breach label, which overstates the link because closure can lag the decision that defines the target. The 10-day clock is treated as starting at registration in the table, then later flagged as undefined. The negative `Documents Requested -> Documents Received` contribution is glossed as claimant responsiveness, which is the same kind of unsupported mechanism the audit otherwise rejects. A few availability predicates are also unreadable where the comparison operator dropped out.

Those are judgment and wording errors, not a failure of the audit’s main distinctions.
