The answer meets the required structure and stays conditional: it maps restructuring moves to process-mining signals, separates optimistic, central, and adverse paths, treats East differently from North and South, and refuses a full rollout without a pilot and guardrails. Leading versus lagging indicators and the hidden-risk discussion are concrete and tied to the log.

It is not nearly flawless. The mechanism map never treats rework as its own effect, and team-level fairness is only a brief aside. The optimistic case compares a high-complexity median of about 9 days with East’s overall median of 8.6 days, and it shortens Legal/Risk waits while volume rises from 9 to 12 per 100 cases on resources already at 91–93% utilization, with no added capacity. The pilot borrows North/South specialists into the East treatment, so the control is contaminated, and the “≤35% high-complexity shared variants” guardrail can fire simply because the pilot routes all high-complexity work centrally. In the sketch, `get_service_time` and `filter_variants` are not actually applied, SLA misses are not computed, and details such as `format="dict"`, `case_duration`, and `len` on the handover graph are unreliable.
