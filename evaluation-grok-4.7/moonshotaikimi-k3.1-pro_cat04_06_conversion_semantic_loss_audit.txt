The audit is strong on source semantics, pipeline risk, and refusal to treat a successful conversion as proof of equivalence. The validation explorer, however, fires Petri-net transitions backward, so the required round-trip comparison does not work as written.

Section 1 correctly reads the partial order and the POWL loop as `C I (D C I)*`, with at least one body, no trailing `D`, and an exclusive legal/skip branch. Witnesses for both `A`/`P` orders, legal review before and after verification, the join before release, and finality are right. Treating `R A L P C I X Z` as legal is also right: optional legal depends only on Assess Risk. The distinction between visible interleaving and true concurrency is maintained instead of being collapsed.

Sections 2, 4, and 6–9 are disciplined. P1 is the right primary pipeline, P2 is allowed to fail without being hidden, and a tree result is not treated as fidelity. Statuses stay in the permitted vocabulary and are not upgraded to `validated preserved` without a run. Rejection conditions cover unsoundness, lost interleaving, early release, collapsed optionality, and failed final-marking acceptance. The canonical choice—editable POWL, immutable derived net, annotated BPMN only after screening—is sound, and the residual limits are real.

The serious defect is in `bounded_visible_language`. Arcs from a transition to a place are stored in `pre` and then consumed; arcs from a place to a transition are stored in `post` and then produced. That inverts the preset and postset. The first enabled transition will usually try to remove tokens from an empty output place and raise `AssertionError`, or else build a meaningless marking. Enabledness still comes from `pm4py.get_enabled_transitions`, but every subsequent state is wrong, so witness checks, bounded-language equality, playout samples, and the exhaustiveness claim do not validate the nets.

Smaller gaps do not change that result. The witness set never includes legal review between Configure Trial and Inspect Trial, even though the source allows that overlap. Reachability output is only a state/edge count, and `graph.states` may not match every PM4Py return shape, though that failure is caught. `exhaustive_under_bound` can also become a false negative when the total-step budget trips on transitions that the visible bound would have skipped anyway.
