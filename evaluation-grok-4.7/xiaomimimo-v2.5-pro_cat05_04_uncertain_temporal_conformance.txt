4.5

The final write-up has the right offset direction, correct civil-time offsets, and several real baseline calls (T-402 S1 certain breach, T-408 S2/S3 certain breaches, T-404 inferred start, surviving T-405 fold pair). Those do not carry the submission. Required classifications are incomplete, three published sensitivity calls are arithmetically false, the DST intervals are inflated, and the code does not do the analysis it claims.

**False classifications.** T-401 A→I is about [718.5 s, 991.5 s]. At ζ=2 the band is [240 s, 960 s]; 991.5 > 960, so this stays POSSIBLE_VIOLATION. Section 4 calls it CERTAIN_PASS and states that 991 fits in 960. T-402 A→C is about [3717 s, 3723 s], wholly inside the ζ=2 band [1260 s, 4140 s], so CERTAIN_PASS; Section 4 calls that overlap a POSSIBLE_VIOLATION. T-406 S2 is about [32 m 28.5 s, 48 m 31.5 s]. A +2 minute SLA is 47 minutes; 48 m 31.5 s still exceeds it, so the result stays POSSIBLE_VIOLATION. Section 4 marks +2 minutes CERTAIN_PASS while only justifying a 50-minute limit.

**T-405 bounds.** LEGACY-BER is floor-to-minute plus ±60 s, i.e. about [minute_start − 60 s, minute_end + 60 s]. The table uses ±120 s: alert [00:48:00, 00:52:59.999] instead of about [00:49:00, 00:51:59.999], and the same extra minute on start and containment. S1 is therefore not [10 m, 20 m]; it is about [12 m, 18 m]. Fold selection itself (alert on CEST, start and containment on CET) is right, but the surviving intervals are not, and that width changes later ζ/tolerance edges.

**Missing required rows.** Section 3 does not give every testable case–rule pair. Certain passes, most profile pairs, and every missing Close_Incident are absent. S3 and C→Close with no close event are INDETERMINATE; that label is never used. Section 4 also skips real changes, including T-408 C→Close (still a certain profile violation at ζ=2, pass only at ζ=3) and T-408 A→C (certain violation at ζ=1, pass at ζ=2). T-409’s start is clamped to the alert with no logged order constraint.

**Code.** The block hardcodes UTC points, does not normalize with zoneinfo, does not build DST alternatives or duration intervals, and does not classify them. `conformance_temporal_profile` is called without the supplied profile; a profile discovered from the same log is not that check. Several “midpoints” are displayed timestamps, not interval midpoints (T-403 alert, T-401 start). The comparison prints are handwritten, and one of them repeats the ζ=2 mistake.

Section 7 covers the requested controls, and many unclamped HUB/OPS SLA windows in Sections 2–3 are numerically right. The sensitivity section is where possible and certain outcomes are decided, and that section is not reliable.
