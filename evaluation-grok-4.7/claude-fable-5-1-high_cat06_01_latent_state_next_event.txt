7.5

The forecast is process-aware and usually well calibrated to the log, but several claims are internally inconsistent or stronger than the evidence, so it is not nearly flawless.

C6-B is the weakest next-event model. Close — No Safety Issue and Fast Close are downstream of Evidence Review Completed. At 12:16 the review is still inside a 1–5 h queue and a 2.8 h median, so those terminations have generally not occurred; an 8–15% feed delay cannot surface events that have not happened. A direct close only works if the review already finished off-feed and closure was recorded without it, which is a narrow path for a 0.05–0.15 band. Fast Close is tied to post-triage behavior, and R-T4 already chose an evidence cycle. Similarity 0.93 to a resolved duplicate is used as a reason to escalate; that fact supports duplicate closure. The S1-like alternative is not a state in the abstraction once evidence has been received.

C6-A treats Supplier Analysis as already mandatory. Rule 5 needs two or more complaints on the same lot or supplier component. This record is one complaint on two devices of one lot, plus three “similar” complaints that are not stated to share that lot. That is a suspected obligation, and the same answer applies the rule correctly and cautiously to C6-C’s different lots and C6-E’s six-complaint lot.

C6-C says Supplier Analysis may have been opened “days ago” while also saying the case is about 23 h old. “Regulatory Assessment … probably queued or in progress” turns an unmet duty into hidden execution; unlike C6-D, nothing shows assignment. Rank 3 favors that completion over rework even though the root cause is unconfirmed and the lots differ. Disposition at 0.20–0.35 with `process_enabled: false` is only coherent if the primary state is assumed; the text also calls the S5 reading genuinely open, and the intervals do not overlap.

C6-D is the strongest case: the 12:40 worklist start, the 14:40 earliest completion, migration backfill, and a disabled disposition are all sound. Rank 2 still packs Customer Evidence Requested and Evidence Received into one prediction. A possible unlogged evidence cycle is placed under completed obligations. The 5 h DFG median is anchored at 12:40, but that median is from Technical Investigation Completed (13:05), while the 2–14 h clock is from assignment.

C6-E’s primary state and rule-5 duty are right, and the lower rank-1 midpoint is justified. “Four items ahead” overreads “4 items waiting,” and this case may itself be in that queue. The rank-1 interval is 0.50–0.70, width 0.20, the same width as C6-A and C6-B, so it is not wider. The calibration note repeats that mistake: every rank-1 band is width 0.20, and C6-B is not the narrow one. C6-C and C6-D are only slightly wider on rank 2 (0.15 versus 0.10–0.12).

The cross-case contrast between C6-A and C6-B, the completion-only observation model, and the DFG audit are accurate: triage outflow 824 versus 972, evidence requests 513, review outs 342, disposition inflow 266 versus outflow 229. Those checks do not remove the ranking and width errors above.
