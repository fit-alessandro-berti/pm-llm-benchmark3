7.5

The core interval arithmetic, time-zone conversions, DST pruning, SLA labels, and zeta/tolerance tables are largely right and internally consistent under an independent-offset model. The write-up still contains material quantitative errors, one inconsistent feasible interval, and code that is weaker than the prose.

**What holds.** Clock correction is applied in the right direction (`true = recorded − offset` with offset in `[a, b]`), after resolution expansion. June offsets are right: CEST = UTC+2, EDT = UTC−4, BST = UTC+1. The 2025-10-26 Berlin fold is the real ambiguity, and only (Alert = CEST, Start = CET, Complete = CET) is jointly ordered; the surviving windows `[00:49, 00:52]`, `[01:04, 01:07]`, `[01:41, 01:44]` and the durations `[720, 1080]`, `[2040, 2400]`, `[2940, 3300]` follow. Headline certainties are robust: T-402 S1 stays above 900 s even at the favorable end (958.5 s), and T-408 S2/S3 (about 3300 s and 138300 s) stay violations at +5 min. A1 vs A2 for T-406 is the right semantic split, and A2 really does make S2 a certain pass (`[1948.5, 2611.5]` ≤ 2700) while A1 does not. Profile bands and the sensitivity flips that are only 1.5–3 s wide (T-402 at ζ=2, T-404/T-407/T-409 at +2 min) match the stated intervals. The midpoint contrasts (855 s, ~2490 s, 270 s) are real and correctly show a point log flipping POSSIBLE cases.

**Section 6 claims that do not survive recomputation.**
- T-401 S1 is not resolved by a symmetric ±45 s OPS-BER bound, nor by a raw second inside 10:14 with the existing `[−90, +120]` s bound. Any such second still straddles 900 s. Even a perfect clock plus the minute floor still gives about `[838.5, 901.5]`.
- T-402 S2 is not settled by a raw second either. At 09:18:59 the duration interval is still about `[2489, 2702.5]`.
- T-404: a signed duration of 20–21 min still leaves S1 hi at 903 s, so it does not make both S1 and S2 certain.
- T-407: latency `[0, 4]` min does not move S1 hi. That end is `start_hi − sent_lo = 1023` s as long as the lower latency bound is 0. A certain pass needs a higher minimum latency (about 123 s), not a tighter maximum.
- T-408 at ζ=2: sub-second stamps do not remove the ±1 s HUB offset. Independent offsets still produce a window around 3300 s.

**Other flaws.** For T-409 the causal floor is applied to Alert→Start, then ignored for Start→Containment. With start forced to be at least the alert, the feasible S2 interval is about `[1078.5, 2103]`, not `[1078.5, 2581.5]`. Labels happen not to change; the interval is still wrong under the answer’s own rule. The T-407 Alert→Containment note is also wrong: the uncapped latency window is already about `[2097, 3303]`, inside the ζ=1 band, so the causal cap is not what produces the pass. The code’s fold filter only checks adjacent endpoint pairs, then stores the convex hull of survivors; that happens to be correct on this log and is not a sound union-preserving test. `pm4py.conformance_temporal_profile` scores directly-follows pairs, while the supplied profile is eventually-following, and the comparison never says so. S3 and Containment→Close are merged into one row wherever Close is missing.

Governance and the separation of certain, possible, and indeterminate results are in good shape. The grade is held down by false sufficiency thresholds on a required section and by the T-409 interval inconsistency, not by the main UTC normalization.
