6.5

The design correctly refuses to treat non-activated cases as non-defaults and separates selection, censoring, delayed labels, and counterfactuals. It is not statistically coherent end to end: the operational estimand, the primary missingness fix, the backtest, and the skeleton contradict each other.

The displayed identity is not the estimand defined above it. Stage 1 is a 60-day cumulative incidence, while Stage 2 conditions on activation in a different, effectively eventual sense. Their product is therefore not \(\Pr(S=\text{Activated}, Y_{90}=1 \mid \mathcal{H}(t_p))\). Open cases, activation after 60 days, and the extra 90 days plus feed delay are collapsed into one number that is then recommended for loss reserves. That is a decision-relevant error, not a notation slip. IPCW is also the wrong primary fix for the pooled 3,600. Activations after about 2025-10-13 have structural non-positivity if the feed delay really runs to 35 days, and partner non-response is allowed to be MNAR. One inverse-probability model cannot repair both. The administrative-buffer-plus-Manski approach mentioned in parentheses should have been primary. The cohort table makes the same mistake by labeling all 3,600 MAR. Withdrawal is asserted, without evidence, to remove low-risk applicants.

The maturity boundary is calculated correctly, then ignored in validation. A default label is generally knowable only about 125 days after activation, not after start. Cases that start in October 2025, especially after the 15 October migration, are mostly immature on 15 February 2026, yet that window is the out-of-time default test. November–December cases are restricted to a 30-day process outcome even though Section 1 uses 60 days. The prose split, purge, and as-of training date are not what the code does.

The skeleton would not implement the study safely. `default_90d` and `label_available_at` are taken with `groupby.first()` on a time-sorted log, which keeps the earliest event, where those fields are described as missing. The case table is built once at the final extract, so training sees labels that were unknowable at the stated 2025-06-30 as-of date. The code split is start-time only through 2025-02-10 / 2025-08-31 / later, with no maturity purge. `insert_case_service_waiting_time` is called on a log with no lifecycle start and complete timestamps, and its output is unused. The modeling hook sets competing-risk duration to last event time minus prefix end, which is not time to terminal state or time to the cutoff, and it sets IPCW weights to \(1/\hat\pi\) using prefix elapsed time rather than the stabilized weights and activation-time model in Section 3. The policy flag is attached to prefix end in code and to application start in Section 5. Deduping is not limited to consecutive duplicates. The block is also well over the requested length.

Leakage rules, prefix eligibility, feature timing, and the three deployment feedback loops are strong. Those parts do not offset an estimand that does not identify the stated probability, a backtest that cannot observe the post-migration default it claims to test, and a pipeline that leaks mature labels into earlier training folds.
