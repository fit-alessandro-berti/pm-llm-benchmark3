The final review is structurally complete and often directionally right about use cases, positive-only precision, overfitting, and not deploying either original model for enforcement. Those parts do not offset decisive technical failures.

Model S is misread. In this PM4Py process-tree notation, `+` is parallel interleaving, not a loop, and each `X(activity, tau)` allows that activity at most once. Model S therefore cannot emit a repeated Credit Check or Technical Review, and Correct Dossier is absent entirely. The claimed traces with a repeated check are not in its language, and the B6/B8 “accept” judgments are false. The probe matrix is wrong on 4 of 8 rows: Model C also rejects B2 (Technical Review before Credit Check with Auto Approval) and B5 (that order plus Supplier Review, Expedite Review, and Auto Approval). Section 4 correctly says C misses Supplier Review plus Auto Approval, then Section 5 says C accepts B5. That contradiction shows the variants were not actually checked.

The third model does not implement the policy it describes. The PM4Py code uses nonexistent `pnet` APIs, string “places,” no marking, and labeled sync transitions. Both “parallel” blocks are token competitions, so only one mandatory check can fire and exactly one of Supplier Review or Expedite Review is required. B1, the simplest valid trace, cannot be replayed. Correct Dossier loops in place and does not re-enable either check. The DECLARE precedence/response rules reverse P7 and would forbid ordinary checks unless a correction occurs. The comparative table never states acceptance for the three adversarial traces, and two of those traces do not separate a correct third model from both inputs.

Validation skeletons split with `train_test_split` without a case grouping, build negative traces as single strings, and invent alignment-cost parameters. The governance recommendation then treats this broken net as the compliance candidate. The dissent is long enough and genuine, but it cannot repair the operator, probe, and implementation errors.
