4.5

The final judgment and the revised model contain outcome-changing errors, so the complete required structure cannot carry the score.

The holdings that are sound are SUP-701 (compliant), SUP-703 and SUP-708 (noncompliant), and SUP-704 (FD-7 exception: sanctions first, EUR 18,000 under the cap, next-day quality and approval, monitoring present). SUP-702 is also fairly treated as a PD-3 exception. Those calls show the control-flow and the emergency window were partly understood.

They are outweighed by conclusions that contradict the record and the answer’s own burden:

- PD-3 is renamed PD-2 throughout, and FD-7’s EUR 20,000 cap is written into the pilot overlay, the SUP-702 reversal test, and the code. PD-3 has no spend cap; it does require automated master-data validation, which is never applied.
- SUP-714 is held a justified exception because a permanent amendment was expected. The facts say the pilot had expired and no amendment existed. That is normalization, not authority.
- SUP-705 is called data-indeterminate even though absence of a written extension is stipulated. Post-expiry activation before quality and manual approval violates CS-9; the defense’s claim that CS-9 does not forbid that sequence is false.
- SUP-712 (one human, two aliases) is merged with SUP-715 (spouses; disclosure required, relationship not banned). CS-9’s different-human rule is then implemented as a waivable disclosure flag, so the revision would permit the SUP-712 breach.
- SUP-707 is marked compliant although `Request_More_Info` is outside M0. SUP-709 is marked compliant although scope of rejected leads is expressly unresolved. SUP-706’s mutable, late link is called a model-scope defect rather than unreliable identity.

The mandatory POWL is still M0. It has no auto-approval branch, no provisional emergency branch, and no repeatable request. Traces the answer itself calls justified cannot fit it. `can_activate` returns success on the standard path without checking that manual approval or quality review occurred, and it references snapshot fields it never creates. Aliases, delegation evidence, and immutable effective versus record timestamps are missing. System “percentages” are computed from 15 illustrative cases, not from the 312/14/9 and 2%→29% aggregates.
