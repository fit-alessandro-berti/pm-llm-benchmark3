The delay identity is right and the case arithmetic checks out. Flow time is split once as upstream + fork–join stage + downstream wait + final service, with the stage equal to the maximum branch span. Synchronization is defined as slack to that maximum, and the F02 overlap example correctly shows why branch waits cannot be added to the critical path: A’s 10 minutes sit inside C’s 35, and those 35 already lie on B’s 60-minute span. Summing each branch’s queue, service, and sync to \(3T_c\) is the right general statement. Every required case figure I recomputed matches, including F01’s 15-minute upstream, the 75-minute stages on F07 and F12, F10’s 5-minute B-over-A margin, the four worked cases, critical counts 2/8/2, and the means 19.58, 61.00, 14.67, 20.00, and 115.25.

B is correctly identified as the controlling branch by last-completion frequency, mean and maximum span, and intervention effect, without treating A’s flat 50-minute service as influence. The three counterfactuals use a stated fixed-downstream-wait rule, give zero when another branch stays last, and cap the gain when the runner-up catches up. F01 saves 5 of A’s 10 minutes; F11 saves all 10 and ties B; F10 saves 5 of B’s 10-minute queue cut. Totals 15, 92.5, and 88, and the new maxima 130, 115, and 122.5, are correct. The fixed-final-start mask, the F11/F09 overlap on F1 under both the A shift and downstream halving, and F01’s apparent idle gap are all real features of this log. Assumptions, non-causality, missing branch capacity, preemption, and a possible release policy are labeled rather than asserted. Both pilots are tied to those limits.

The reproducible script’s pandas decomposition and tie handling are consistent with the tables, but `pm4py.get_service_time` is called without the required activity argument. The service-time comment also promises per-activity results the call could not return. Two smaller analytical nits remain. F05 is also capped (a 12.5-minute B-queue cut yields only 10 once A finishes at 10:00), but the prose describes capping only for F10. Ranking A strictly second is sharper than the evidence: A and C are tied at 2/12 critical cases, and C’s span maximum is 57 against A’s 50; the body text admits the ordering depends on the measure, while the table collapses that to second and third.
