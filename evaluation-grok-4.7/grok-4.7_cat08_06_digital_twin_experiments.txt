7.5

The design is a real digital twin: shared calendars, seasonal and weekday demand, architect bursts, a decision-drafting join, latent notice extensions, and a validation gate before any ranking. Sections 1–3 and 6 are strong, and the PM4Py layer is mostly faithful (`pandas` plus `format_dataframe`, variants, DFG, performance DFG, `get_case_arrival_average`, `parse_process_tree`, with the space-stripping parser hazard handled). The score is held down by internal contradictions and several rules that would mis-rank policies.

The 24-month scoring claim does not survive the stated initial condition. A 1 January WIP snapshot plus 90 days of warm-up starts the scored window near 1 April. That window contains two autumn peaks and only one intact March–May peak, plus two spring fragments. Fixed-horizon scoring, a Welch check, and an empty-start 12-month check are the right tools; the calendar alignment as written is not.

The decision screen is asymmetric. A benefit must show a paired interval entirely below zero, while harm to another case type counts only when that type’s p90 interval lies entirely above +5%. An interval from about −2% to +20% still passes. The 85% peak-utilization cap is absolute for candidate arms and is not applied to E0, so an arm that cuts overload from well above 85% to 90% and improves the tail is discarded and the overloaded baseline is retained. “Worst-case p90 regret” has no comparator, and the many arms and metrics have no multiplicity control. D8 has misclassification levels but no short-path service reduction, so that arm is not identified. Appeal, planning, and legal rework have rates and no return activities. Planner routing never says whether simple renovations may use the six generalists or only the two restricted planners; those are different bottlenecks. Inspection scheduling (median wait about 3 days, `scheduler_1` in the log) has no server and no statement that this wait is only the inspector queue. Precedence is specified at the decision join and left open elsewhere, so public notice and inspection can be read as parallel with planning even though the nominal flow is sequential after the review AND. “D3–D5” mixes intervention names with districts and, as a range, pulls in D4, which is not a long-travel district.

The outer uncertainty loop says “weekly block bootstrap” without a unit. Blocks of event rows fracture cases; the interval is not identified until the resample is case-preserving and season-stratified. Validation gates are numeric comparisons to one simulated path; at this volume a single run’s variant shares move enough that the 3-point gate needs a replication envelope.

The skeleton contradicts the design in code that is actually executed, not merely left empty. Objection shocks are i.i.d. per case, while the twin’s cross-case mechanism is a district-week shock. `generate_case_arrivals` advances `ArrivalProcess.rng` and ignores the replication seed, so replications are neither independent nor restartable. Stream ids use Python `hash()`, which changes across processes. D7 multiplies Monday’s rate down and never moves that mass to Tuesday–Thursday, so weekly volume falls. Environment capacity is specified as 1.8 FTE and implemented as `round(1.8) = 2`. Pre-submission delay is added after `arrival` while `end` is `arrival + 1 day`, which can make cohort duration negative and puts pre-submission on the municipal clock. `demand_multiplier` is a whole-horizon scalar, so S1’s six-month shock is inexpressible; absence, extra peak inspectors, legal parallelization, aging priority, and triage are stored and never read. Common-random-number keying by `(case_id, activity, attempt)` does not survive policies that change the arrival sequence, and the engine does not key draws that way.

No optimality is claimed, missing objections are not written back into the log, and service is not silently equated with inter-completion time. Those are the right epistemic calls. The remaining gaps are operational: an implementer following the text and the code together would mis-time the horizon, under-couple objections, mis-count environmental and Monday capacity, and apply a screen that can keep a worse baseline.
