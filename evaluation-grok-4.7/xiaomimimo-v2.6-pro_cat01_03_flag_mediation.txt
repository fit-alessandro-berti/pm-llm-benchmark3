The delivered analysis is strong on the hard parts and incomplete on the required close. Sections 1–6 correctly treat the flag as a policy bundle rather than a manipulable cause, reject natural direct/indirect effects because of interference, truncation, and exposure-induced confounding, and separate review occurrence from review waiting. The timestamp table is largely accurate (flag means 16.51 h vs 8.17 h; triage wait 0.24 h vs 2.48 h; review wait 6.50 h vs 1.88 h among reviewed unflagged cases), absent stages are kept as NA, and the P3 lottery pilot is a well-specified next experiment.

Those merits do not make the answer nearly complete. Section 8 stops at three conditions and never covers timestamp semantics, positivity/overlap, or reviewer behavior change. Section 9 is missing entirely. Reviewer M2 escalated 7/7 reviewed cases (R01, R03, R09, R10, R13, R18, R20), not 6/7; that error is repeated in the pathway table and the roster-instrument row. The competing-explanation cell says “3 unflagged” and then lists four cases (R05, R09, R17, R20). “32% escalation-step time (25% of that decision/queue latency)” misstates 25% of total flagged case-hours as a share of the 32%. In the Python, `discover_performance_dfg` returns three objects, but the script unpacks four, so DFG printing crashes after discovery.
