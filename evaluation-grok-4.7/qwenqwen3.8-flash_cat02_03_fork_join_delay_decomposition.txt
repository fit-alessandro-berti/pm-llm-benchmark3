The delay decomposition is internally consistent and numerically accurate: upstream, branch queue, service, join-ready as a max, non-additive synchronization waits, fork-join elapsed time, downstream queue, final service, and total flow time all reconcile on the worked cases and the aggregates (including 1383 total flow minutes, 732 fork-join minutes, and 549 synchronization minutes treated as diagnostic overlap). Critical-path ranking correctly refuses to equate A’s longest mean service with control, and the three counterfactual totals (15, 92.5, and 176) follow the stated assumptions, including zero-benefit cases and downstream masking.

The main defects are small but real. In the B-queue counterfactual, F02 is an A/B completion tie, yet it is described as a shift to A, unlike the explicit tie reported for F06. The two-server claim that observed downstream waits are not simple congestion is only gestured at, not shown. The PM4Py block attempts the required calls, but both `format_dataframe` signatures are unlikely to match the library, so that part is not reliably reproducible even though the pandas calculations are.
