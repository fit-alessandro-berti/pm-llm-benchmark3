6.8

The review is complete and often sharp: use cases are separated, B1–B8 are read correctly off both source expressions, Model S’s skip-and-interleave failures are real, Model C is rightly treated as a memorized whitelist, and the third tree does keep Credit Check and Technical Review mandatory. It is not board-ready. Several claims are factually loose, one audit table reverses the author’s own error polarity, and the proposed model is stricter than the stated policy while being described as exact enforcement.

The most serious reasoning break is internal. Section 1 defines a false negative as failing to flag an illegal bypass and a false positive as blocking a valid order. Section 8 swaps those labels: Model S accepting B3/B4 is called a fatal false positive, and Model C rejecting valid B2/B5 is called a false negative. Section 10 then uses the Section 1 definitions again. A governance reader cannot tell which error the recommendation is weighting.

The third model is oversold against P1–P7. P4 forces Expedite Review to follow both mandatory checks; it does not force Supplier Review to wait for both, nor does it forbid Supplier Review from interleaving with the checks (`Credit Check`, `Supplier Review`, `Technical Review`, …). ADV3 is therefore policy-ambiguous, not a demonstrated fatal violation, and “Fully Enforced” for P3 is a design choice presented as a requirement. The same model reintroduces duplicate Credit Check, Technical Review, Supplier Review, and Expedite Review labels, repeats the optional reviews inside every correction, and cannot express a correction with no recheck. Those are the same ownership and precision problems used to condemn Model C, and Section 7 does not account for them. The DECLARE layer also invents an `order_value < 50000` auto-approval rule that no stakeholder stated; applied literally, it can reject B2 and B5.

Metric reading is uneven. Training fitness 0.973 is not “97.3% of training variant weight.” Alignment costs are described as identical even though the dossier says they were never supplied. “33 duplicate labels” and “Supplier Review on 4 paths” are stated as structure facts; the tree has Supplier Review on three branches, and a direct extra-copy count of visible nodes is not 33. “Zero silent transitions” ignores the silent splits a Petri-net translation of an 8-way `X` introduces. “Livelock-free by construction” is false for an unbounded `*` loop under ordinary soundness. A flower net does not have near-perfect arc-degree simplicity. Holdout 0.744 is too often attributed only to overfitting, despite F4’s missing-event alternative.

The adversarial traces are not self-contained: ADV1–ADV3 omit `Register Dossier`, but the accept/reject cells are true only if it is present; taken literally, Model S would reject them too. ADV4 is a description, not a trace. Validation does not match its own protocol: stratification is only on trace length, so `StratifiedKFold` can crash; there is no duplicate-event or custom alignment-cost skeleton; `check_soundness(...)[0]` assumes a return type the call does not establish. The 0.05 fitness-drop and 0.050 s/case gates ignore the missing-event and hardware confounding discussed in Section 6. Exploration never gets an explicit deploy/do-not-deploy decision for `model_third`.

The dissent is real, within length, and correctly attacks the B5 generalization. The core S-versus-C probe matrix is the part that holds.
