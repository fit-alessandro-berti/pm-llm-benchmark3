The write-up has the required six-part structure, uses a parallel join rather than a sum of branch times, and keeps reruns, contamination, queries, and investigations as separate tails. The load-bearing clocks are still wrong, and several of those errors are internal contradictions.

Incubation started Friday at 14:00, so 46–54 h of technical readiness is Sunday 12:00–20:00. The ledger and smooth-path table place readiness as early as Saturday 16:00 and the earliest terminal event at Saturday 17:00. That instant is impossible twice: the culture cannot be ready, and QA’s Saturday window has already closed at 13:00, with no Sunday QA. Weekend micro on-call therefore cannot move a smooth release off Monday. It matters mainly by letting a contamination indication start its extra 20–30 h incubation on Sunday rather than Monday morning. Section 3’s own smooth path ends near Monday noon, which conflicts with the Saturday earliest time in both the scenario table and the forecast summary.

Chemistry is treated as usually finished by Friday 17:30 after a 16:30 start. A 16:30 start plus the stated minimum service of 1.5 h ends at 18:00, and the same paragraph also computes a modal finish at 19:00, after the lab closes and after the no-overtime rule. The two analysts are effectively serialized behind the urgent sample. Under FIFO, about 55%, B-417 is unlikely to start on Friday at all. Friday spillover is a base case, not a catastrophe, so microbiology does not “universally” dominate the join.

The rerun path drops the required jump in deviation-investigation probability to 0.30–0.55. The confirmation path assumes no Sunday readout and still has the extra incubation finished on Monday afternoon. Conditional excursion probabilities (about 0.35 and 0.20) are stated as if given, then not carried into a joint scenario. The 10–15% rejection share has no support in the problem. Remaining-hour bands do not match the clock times attached to them: Monday 21 September 12:00 is about 68.5 elapsed hours from Friday 15:30, not 54, and Tuesday 22 September 15:00 is about 95.5 hours, not 72. The secondary trigger also refers to a microbiology readout before Friday 18:00, which cannot occur.
