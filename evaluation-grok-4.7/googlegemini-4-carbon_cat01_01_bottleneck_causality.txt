The assessment is structurally complete and causally cautious: the crude-versus-stratified reversal is real (Orion +6.72 h overall, and about −2.14 / −2.21 / −3.43 h within Low / Medium / High), positivity failure and completed-case selection are treated as identification problems, queue time is separated from review service inside strata, and the three-phase plan correctly refuses a blind 50% transfer in favor of instrumentation, a capacity change that does not relabel groups, and a small stratified routing pilot. The required graph, role table, five hypothesis types, PM4Py diagnostics, and five uncertainties are all present, and the word limits are met.

The score is held down by auditable numerical and role errors. Day load is 9/13 Normal, not 10/13. Night and day mean cycle times are about 21.74 h and 13.98 h, not 21.58 h and 13.79 h. Compliance-absent cases average about 11.95 h, not 11.98 h. Crude Nova wait is about 8.79 h and Nova review service about 5.04 h, not 8.16 h and 4.86 h, so the crude residual gap is about +0.7 h for Orion, not −0.09 h. The joint rework-and-compliance residual averages 3.675 h, not 3.63 h. The “85–95% from wait” claim fails for Low, where wait is about 75% of a 2.14 h gap.

Those figures matter because the equal-residual claim hides Orion’s heavier compliance and rework mix. In the authors’ own graph, `compliance_required` and `arrival_load` are descendants on backdoor paths, not common causes, yet both are labeled confounders of the group effect. The graph also has `rework -> review_service` after the text shows Orion High service stuck at 9.17 h with and without rework; the extra time sits in the residual. The lead paragraph ties the wait advantage to certified reviewers, while the later queue analysis points to staffing and WIP. “Ecological fallacy” is the wrong name for this case-level Simpson reversal, and appeal harm is asserted from two appeals after the decomposition correctly says quality is not identifiable. The pilot and code are sound; the slips are in execution and in keeping one estimand story consistent across sections.
