7.4

The design correctly refuses the fatal errors: it does not train only on the 21,600 matured activations, does not code rejected, withdrawn, or open cases as non-default, and does not use a random split or post-outcome fields as features. Target semantics, competing outcomes, delayed labels, transfer linkage, and deployment feedback are handled seriously, and the nine required sections are all present.

The score is held down by concrete design and implementation inconsistencies.

The primary estimand is written as a plain conditional probability of activation within 180 days and default within 90 days, while the data are censored. That quantity is a cumulative incidence under competing risks, and the two clocks (time since application start and time since activation) are never aligned. Section 3 then lists administrative censoring as a model state and treats “activated, no default” like a competing transition. Censoring is a missingness mechanism, and surviving the horizon is a non-event, not a competing event that precludes default. Unlinked transfers are left as “competing or missing follow-up,” so the primary analysis does not actually choose one mechanism.

The 180-day window is not applied to the cohort accounting. All 2,700 defaults and 18,900 non-defaults are still treated as labeled primary outcomes, even though activations after day 180 fall outside the stated estimand. The as-of date of 31 January 2026 also does not match the 15 February 2026 extract that defines the 21,600.

Section 7 and the code disagree on the temporal split. The prose trains through 31 December 2024 and validates from 1 January through 30 June 2025. The code trains through 31 March 2025 and validates only April–June, and starts from 1–14 October fall into `other`. Maturity is discussed for the 90-day outcome plus feed delay, but not for whether a 180-day activation window is even observable in the July–September test.

In the skeleton, a missing `label_available_at` is treated as known, which contradicts the delayed-label rule. `cohort_status` is placed in `feature_df` while the leakage assert only checks a name denylist, so the outcome column would pass the audit. `analysis_end_time` omits `last_event_time` even though the comment says it is included, and the activity rename map invents source names that are not in the data. Global variant and DFG discovery is run on the full prefix log, including later regimes, rather than on the training period only.
