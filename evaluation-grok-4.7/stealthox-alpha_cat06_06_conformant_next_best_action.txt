The final recommendation is directionally sound: a conformant independent second review, with parallel rescreening, A and B excluded, SAR nondisclosure preserved, and P-901 treated as a real spillover. The required structure and conformance labels are largely right. It is not nearly flawless.

The Rank 2 plan labels “S4 (R17)” as the second reviewer while marking that plan `CONFORMANT NOW`. R17 is the first analyst, so this identity slip makes the second-ranked plan read as a four-eyes breach. Section 6 later names S4 correctly, but the ranking table itself is wrong.

Timelines and probabilities conflict. Action C is specified as 35–75 minutes of review plus 5–15 minutes to record and execute (about 40–90 minutes). The table instead uses a decision at T+45–95 and execution at T+50–110, while the Rank 1 trace uses execution at T+45–70. The same row states on-time completion of 0.35–0.65 and a breach risk of 0.30–0.55. Those are not the same claim, and both sit poorly against a hard upper time of about 90 minutes.

Benefit reasoning overclaims dominance. Deadline value (€20k–€35k) is not strictly less than false-rejection or hold harm (€15k–€80k); the ranges overlap. Action A’s maximum upside is also not shown to be €35k once avoided hold harm is included, so it is not strictly below the €100k regulatory floor. P-901’s €25k–€60k breach cost is invented. Branch probabilities such as match confirmation at 0.5–0.7 and approval rising to 0.7–0.85 are unsupported.

Rule 6 is overstated. Second review is required while the score stays at least 70 or the fuzzy match remains unresolved. It is not unavoidable in every branch if a refreshed score falls below 70 and the match is cleared. A confirmed sanctions match is also left open to “approve with mitigating controls,” which is a materially unsafe continuation. G is not strictly dominated by the slower S4 path: a genuine override at 20–40 minutes can be faster. The claim that S4 at 65 minutes puts Rank 2 near parity with immediate S2 review is not credible. Immediate rejection is ranked above a certainly conformant evidence request even though the answer itself says the rejection threshold is unverified and that rejection would pre-empt the control.
