**Errors that change identifiability**

- Scan delay is defined two incompatible ways. A1 says `arrival` is the scan time, so visibility begins at that timestamp. The QB rescue, Test 1, and the top-ranked exception then treat B05 (`arrival` 09:46) as possibly invisible at 09:48, and also treat the 09:14–09:16 lag as scan delay. The same rule had already failed at 09:31: B03 was waiting and was skipped in favor of resuming B01, then B01 was stopped at 09:48 for that same B03. Counting 09:48 as priority support is not tenable.
- At 09:31, B01’s remaining time is 45−14 = 31 minutes, not ~14. The ~14 figure is right only at 09:48, after the 09:31–09:48 segment. SPT still selects B03, so the direction survives, but the arithmetic is wrong.
