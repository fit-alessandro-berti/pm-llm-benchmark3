8.4

The analysis is strong and largely faithful to the evidence: the eight required sections are present, the arithmetic checks out, machine capacity is correctly demoted, lot-to-job linkage failure is treated as structural, and succession is separated from cause. The best parts are the conjunctive-constraint results (rack-only ≈ 0 min, lot-only ≈ 5 min, both ≈ 90 min), J4’s FIX-A dwell being slack-absorbed before B2, and J1’s 235 + 355 = 590 min of documented waiting being invisible to the flattened views.

Deductions are for real but limited defects. The prose is likely past the 2,800-word aim. In the code, `ocel_objects_interactions_summary` is probably already aggregated, so `groupby(...).size()` does not compare interaction frequencies; object-id columns are inconsistent with the comment; and several discovered objects (`pdfg`, `ocdfg`, `otg`) are unused. “Job Ready For Batch” is an invented activity, though the closing assert handles that honestly. A few claims are tighter than the data: the “+10 min rule,” the 33% oven ceiling versus 44% utilization, and calling S1’s 15 min overhead “consistent” with S2’s 25 and S3’s 35. The ranking table compresses “observability in each flattening” into slogans, even though section 4 later covers the six views properly.
