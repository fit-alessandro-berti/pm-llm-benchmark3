7.4

The analysis is strong on authority, case splitting, and adversarial balance, but it is not nearly flawless. The briefs and matrix are specific and mostly traceable; the revised POWL contains real semantic holes, one false claim, and a few burden inconsistencies.

What holds up. M0 is read correctly: concurrent sanctions and quality, manual approval strictly before activation, XOR against rejection, then notify and close. CS-9, PD-3, and FD-7 are kept apart, including the observer’s later disclaimer and the hard expiries (704 inside FD-7, 705 and 714 outside). Both briefs actually concede the other side’s best facts. 701, 703, 705, 706, 708, 711, 712, and 714 are classified with a dominant reason rather than averaged away. 712 versus 715 is a real distinction (explicit segregation rule versus an unprohibited relationship plus a missed disclosure). The overlay table and the collection proposal cover notification IDs, rule versions, eligibility snapshots, aliases, delegation, authority tokens, and effective versus record time. The standard, auto, and emergency partial orders are individually coherent, and n-ary XOR plus separate transition objects is the right PM4Py shape.

Model defects. The Request_More_Info loop is only a block between registration and the whole XOR. SUP-707, the motivating trace, is Register, Sanctions, Request_More_Info, Request_More_Info, Quality, then approval. That trace is not in the language, and the limitation is never stated. Interleaving was available: put the loop concurrent with the checks and before manual approval. The comment says a full sanctions-and-quality-then-reject path exists; the code has no such branch, so a trace that M0 and CS-9 allow cannot be replayed. `Response(Activate, Quality within 2 business days)` also fights the auto route, which deliberately allows quality before activation; a prior quality review would still fail that response rule.

Judgment defects. The stated burden for a justified exception requires contemporaneous eligibility conditions. PD-3 requires automated master-data validation before activation; it is absent from SUP-702, listed as missing, and then omitted from the reversal test while the case is still JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION. “Prior quality certification remained valid” is given for SUP-710 and then treated as a mere claim with missing proof. SUP-712’s same-human resolution is given, yet confidence is hedged as if identity were still open. The defense sentence “Trace deviation  business misconduct…” is missing the relation and is not grammatical. DECLARE is invoked with a non-constraint, `ExactlyOnce(Register, Notify, Close)`, which also conflicts with the reactivation path that drops registration. Withdrawal is named and then left without a mechanism, and section 6’s separate intake model is quietly merged into M1.

These are not presentation nits. They undermine the proposed normative model and the burden the answer set for itself. The dialectical core is still well above a middling split-the-difference review.
