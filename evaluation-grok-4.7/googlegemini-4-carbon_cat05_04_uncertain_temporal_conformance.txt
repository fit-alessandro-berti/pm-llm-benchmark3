7.5

The core interval arithmetic, timezone/DST handling, SLA-versus-profile split, and zeta/tolerance flips are largely correct and carefully labeled. That is not enough for a near-flawless score: the assumptions section contradicts the tables, same-clock dependence is never fixed as a global rule, and several “what would make this certain” claims are numerically wrong.

Sections 2–4 are the strongest part. HUB net ±1.5 s, OPS-BER `[displayed UTC − 120 s, displayed UTC + 150 s]`, SENSOR ±10 s, VENDOR-NY `[07:57:00Z, 08:04:00Z]`, and the mobile upper bound `12:19:30Z` are right. T-405 is correctly reduced to folds `(CEST, CET, CET)`: every other fold pair makes ingestion order impossible even with independent ±60 s offsets. Baseline labels match those intervals, including the robust results (T-402 S1 and T-408 S2/S3 certain violations; T-401/T-403/T-405 S1 only possible) and the fragile ones (T-402 P1 overlaps `[240, 960]` by 1.5 s; T-404 S2 reaches 2703 s; T-408 P2 straddles 3300 s while S2 is already a certain breach). Sensitivity thresholds at +120/+300 s and ζ ∈ {1, 2, 3} are computed correctly, including 1021.5 s versus 1020 s and 1141.5 s versus 1140 s.

The write-up then undercuts itself. Section 1 builds T-404 `Investigation_Start` as `[10:09:59.5Z, 10:17:00.5Z]` from a completion window that omits HUB clock offset; Section 2 and all later durations use `[10:09:58.5Z, 10:17:01.5Z]`. Pairwise durations treat every clock draw as independent, but that assumption is not stated in Section 1 even though it is the sole reason T-404 S2 and T-408 P2 are not pointwise passes. Section 6 then gives the wrong certainty conditions: T-401’s “offset ≥ +31.5 s” confuses the P1 excess over 960 s with an offset threshold; T-405’s `|Δoffset| ≤ 1 s` produces about `[839, 961]`, not `[840, 960]`, so P1 is still a possible violation; T-407’s `[2 min 3 s, 12 min 58 s]` still leaves a P1 lower tail (12 min 57 s is the inclusive edge); restoring T-409’s normal NTP bound yields roughly `[118.5, 391.5]` and certifies S1 only, while P1 remains possible because of the lower edge. T-402’s “≥ 1.5 s into the minute” lands on 960 s, which is still inside a closed band.

Section 5 is a real interval pipeline, keeps DST alternatives, labels midpoints as lossy, and calls `format_dataframe` plus `conformance_temporal_profile` at ζ = 1, 2, 3. It does not match the prose: `SLA_RULES` is unused, T-407 marginal truncation yields a negative duration instead of the stated `[0, 1023]`, and only the occurrence-shift mobile model is implemented. T-409’s ±12 min estimate is also promoted to a hard feasible set (`POSSIBLE_VIOLATION`) rather than left indeterminate, despite the problem marking that bound as weak.
