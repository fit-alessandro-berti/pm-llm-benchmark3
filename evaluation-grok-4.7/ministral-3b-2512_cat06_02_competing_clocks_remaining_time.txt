The write-up has the six required sections and names the right mechanisms, but the forecast itself is not temporally defensible. The headline times are impossible under the stated clocks and calendars, and the scenario table does not separate paths.

Incubation started Friday 14:00 and needs 46–54 h, so technical readiness is Sunday 12:00–20:00. QA is closed Saturday after 13:00 and all day Sunday, and chemistry has no weekend hours. A terminal event on Saturday is therefore not available on any path. The earliest plausible release is Monday morning, only if the microbiology result is already in hand, chemistry has cleared, and QA is nearly free. The reported earliest time (Saturday 18:00 / 23:00) and the central band (18–24 h, Saturday–Sunday) are about two days early.

The supporting arithmetic does not match those clocks. “46 h + 1.5 h” is equated to Saturday 18:00; 18–24 h is equated to Saturday 18:00–Sunday 18:00; Monday 08:00 plus 1.5 h of QA is reported as Monday 16:00. The same central and tail windows are pasted onto all four scenarios, and the smooth-path lower bound is “now.”

Chemistry is treated as finishing Friday after about 3.5 h. Only 2.5 h of lab time remains, two analysts are occupied, and one urgent plus three ordinary samples are ahead. FIFO waiting time is not the sum of the two residual services, and that sum also omits the queued samples. The sensitivity note reverses discipline: FIFO is the slower regime for a high-but-not-critical batch; risk priority lets it pass the ordinary samples. A rerun is then placed on Saturday, when the laboratory is closed, and the rerun distribution is replaced by the original service maximum of 4.5 h.

The QA queue is incoherent. Five to ten batches ahead are assigned both “first in queue” and a 0–2 h wait, and current inventory is added to two calendar days of arrivals without subtracting service or restricting arrivals to opening hours. QA service is capped at the stated 80th percentile, so the tail beyond 3.5 h disappears. Documentation-query and investigation paths are collapsed, “Saturday 26:00” appears, and the 3–7 calendar-day laboratory investigation is never scheduled. The severe tail therefore stops around Tuesday instead of late that week or the next.

The temperature excursion is listed and then unused. Rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities are never recomputed jointly, and the extra 20–30 h confirmation incubation is absent. Weekend on-call authorization is ranked as the dominant smooth-path driver; once QA’s Sunday closure is respected, it only moves Monday’s start by the readout, not the terminal event from Saturday to Monday.

The next-event section inherits the same clock. Incubation cannot finish before Saturday 18:00, and primary readiness cannot slip past Sunday evening. Higher-leverage observations are resolution of the excursion flag and whether chemistry actually starts before Friday 18:00.
