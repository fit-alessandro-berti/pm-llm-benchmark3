The write-up covers every required heading and correctly down-ranks telemetry, retries, silent routing, label drift, and disputed denial/withdrawal scope. It also correctly treats CL-610 as a zero-cost P1/P2 failure and CL-602 as high-severity missing evidence. Those strengths do not offset several material errors.

Policy logic is wrong on two control cases. P1 allows auto-approval only when amount, LOW risk, and no recent bank change all hold. CL-609 (EUR 18,000, MEDIUM) therefore violates P1; the text says the P1 restriction “does not explicitly apply” and treats the breach as unstated intent. CL-604 is EUR 90,000, so P2 requires Fraud Screen and Manual Approve before payment. Calling the missing fraud screen a “near-miss,” and citing the EUR 5,000 line, contradicts the quoted rule. Severity for CL-609 then drifts from 2–3 to “compliant / 1” in section 6.

Alignment semantics are misread on the main sequencing failure. In CL-604, `L(Pay Claim)` is the early log payment and `M(Pay Claim)` is an unmatched model expectation, not a second payment at 10:20. That false duplicate is repeated in the narrative, hypothesis H3d, and the event log. Section 6.2’s heading also says the sequencing is reversible while the body says it is irreversible. CL-602 is assigned bank acceptance and a 10:20 timestamp the case does not provide.

Population math is invalid and then used for prioritization. The four synchronous auto-approval violations are a full-month count, not 4/12. “~33% of auto-approvals,” “~75/month,” and “10–20%” denial prevalence are unsupported. Remediation 1 would block every payment until fraud screen and manual approval, including lawful auto-approvals. The P1 gate checks only bank change, not amount or risk. Investigating the EUR 480,000 case is ranked last.

The code misses three explicit requirements: it never calls `pm4py.parse_process_tree`, never builds the log with `parse_event_log_string` or a pandas dataframe, and never calls `pm4py.conformance_diagnostics_alignments`. Events are appended directly to an `EventLog`, CL-604 is given two pay events, confidence is derived from raw cost, and `Cost_Severity_Ratio` replaces judgment with a formula.
