5.5

The memo is well structured and avoids the crude failure modes (it bars I-03, separates I-05 from I-07, and prices spillovers), but the live allocation contradicts its own rule and its own arithmetic.

The decision rule is not deployable and is not applied. Segment-adjusted probabilities, the €50k concave value cap, the Non-Native effect discount, and the gate “base net > 0 and still non-negative under a modestly adverse effect” never appear as numbers. Tiers are asserted. An operator could not reproduce them.

Using the memo’s own formula, midpoint absolute reduction × breach value − €450 − false-alarm burden − ~€2,400 spillover, direct nets are approximately: I-04 +€6.6k, I-08 +€2.2k, I-09 +€1.2k, I-02 +€0.3k, I-06 +€0.2k, I-01 −€0.7k, I-05 −€0.7k, I-10 −€0.9k, I-07 −€1.6k. I-07 is the worst eligible case even before spillover (about +€800 direct). Its upper effect, 0.32 × €5,000 − €500 ≈ €1,100, still loses to one slot of spillover. It also has 1.5 h left and is still in Triage, so the 0.20–0.32 effect is the least credible runway in the book. Labeling it Tier 1 and spending a slot violates the memo’s similarity clause for urgency weights and its economic threshold. I-05 is correctly stood down, but the write-up credits cool-down rather than the fact that even the top of its effect range fails after spillover, so the same machinery would mishandle the next I-05-like case.

Only I-04 clearly survives the memo’s adverse gate. I-09 is about zero at its lower bound before any uncertainty haircut, yet it is Tier 1 partly as a learning intervention on a four-slot production queue. I-06, the imputation case, is named first backup despite failing that same gate. I-01 is called moderate EV while its net is negative. The €20k “typical” harmed case, which sets every threshold, is never varied; at ~€30k even I-09’s base net flips sign.

Section 3’s base arithmetic checks out (≈€17.5k gross, €4.2k swarm plus false-alarm, 0.18–0.54 extra expected breaches, €3.6–10.8k). The interpretations do not. Adverse gross benefit in the worked example is ~€7k, so €7k − €4.2k − €8–15k is about −€5k to −€12k, which is not “close to zero or mildly negative.” Optimistic spillover of €2–5k undercuts the model’s own low end (~€3.6k) without a derivation from 15–30 minutes of delay. Scenario results are not fed back: the chosen three-slot book is dominated by dropping I-07 in every scenario, and it fails the memo’s own “still non-negative when adverse” exception for using scarce slots. No weights, minimax, or value-of-waiting criterion connects the three scenarios to the portfolio.

Sections 4–6 are the stronger part. Evidence versus refresh, treatment logging, boundary randomization, segment offsets, and spillover monitoring are concrete. They still do not repair the decision. Fairness devices that were supposed to bind (value cap, segment floors) are idle, while a negative-EV SME swarm is justified in fairness language and can push delay onto untreated diagnosis-stage cases. One Non-Native case is treated as both a fairness floor and an experiment, with no value of information and no duty-of-care limit on randomizing contractual deadlines.

The qualitative skeleton would be a usable draft. The capacity choice, which is the actual decision, does not follow from the rule or the table.
