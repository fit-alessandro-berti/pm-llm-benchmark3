7.5

The written delay decomposition is coherent and numerically reliable. Upstream time, branch queues, service, max-based `join_ready`, synchronization waits, downstream delay, final service, and total flow time reconcile for the worked cases and the full table. The double-counting argument is exact: each branch’s queue + service + sync equals the fork-join interval, so summing branches counts that interval three times. Critical-path ranking correctly separates A’s largest service time from B’s control of completion (8/12 critical, highest queue-plus-service and tail). Counterfactual join-ready savings are right, including partial effects when another branch becomes critical, and final-station masking is treated as an assumption rather than a fact.

The required Python does not reproduce that analysis. `join_minus_free` is computed as `resource_free - join_ready`, so estimated total savings equal `min(delay reduction, resource wait)`. That is the opposite of the stated rule and of the reported table: running the code would give 0 where Section 4 reports positive savings (F01, F02, F03, F07, F12) and positive savings where Section 4 reports 0 (F05). `pm4py.format_dataframe` is called with `activity` and `timestamp` instead of `activity_key` and `timestamp_key`, and the failure is swallowed, so the required PM4Py step does not run. The system-wide 45-minute total also holds later resources fixed even though earlier final completions would move.
