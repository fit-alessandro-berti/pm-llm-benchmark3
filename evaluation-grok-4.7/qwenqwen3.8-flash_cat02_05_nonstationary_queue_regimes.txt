6.4

The regime analysis is substantively strong: opening-hour units, median service versus flow time, outage versus recovery, mix shift versus staffing, carry-over, and Little’s law limits are handled carefully, and most regime means (arrival/completion rates, WIP, load proxies, implied \(L/\gamma\)) check out.

It is not nearly flawless. The required Python block stops mid-function: no hourly table, no printed summaries, no serial-dependence statistic, no change-point heuristic, no Little’s-law window, and no PM4Py commentary, so that deliverable does not run. Alternative B mis-assigns the 25–26 April weekend into the post-29 April window, so post-period arrival intensity (~8.95/h), completions (~9.05/h), staff (6.3), and net (−1.2/day) are wrong (about 7.4/h, 7.7/h, 5.3 FTE, and −3.6/day). The claim that post-intervention days have higher arrivals is therefore false. The outage “9.33 cases/hour over 9 service-available hours” is also wrong (105/9 ≈ 11.7), and several net-change cells lose their sign or collapse “43→36→32” into “433632,” which obscures accumulation versus drain in the primary table and Little’s-law matrix.
