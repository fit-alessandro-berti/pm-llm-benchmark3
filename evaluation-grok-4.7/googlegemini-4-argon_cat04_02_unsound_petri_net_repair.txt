6.5

The core diagnosis is right: the net is a workflow net, it is unsound, and `t_join_waived` synchronizes only `p_waived`. The waiver firing tables are accurate, leftover tokens in `p_analysis` / `p_analysis_done` are correctly shown to be stuck because `t_join_normal` still needs `p_review_done`, and Repair R1 (one arc `p_analysis_done → t_join_waived`) is the right intent-preserving fix. The standalone repaired net matches that edit, and the recommendation of R1 over the free-choice merge R2 is justified.

The formal bookkeeping is not reliable enough for a higher score.

The claimed reachability sizes are wrong. Direct enumeration gives 14 reachable markings in the original net and 10 after R1, not 12 and 8. The four improper markings listed (`{p_analysis, p_decision}`, `{p_analysis, sink}`, `{p_analysis_done, p_decision}`, `{p_analysis_done, sink}`) are real, but `{p_analysis, p_waived}` and `{p_analysis_done, p_waived}` were omitted from both totals. That violates the instruction not to assert undervived state-space sizes, and it is presented as an enumeration.

Soundness is misclassified. No transition is dead from `im`: `t_join_normal` fires on the standard-review path. The original net fails option to complete and proper completion only. Calling that a dead-transition fault, and calling the repaired transitions “live,” confuses quasi-liveness with van der Aalst soundness.

Repair minimality does not hold together. Under the stated unit cost, R3’s “exact edits” omit `(p_waived, t_join_waived)` and `(t_join_waived, p_decision)`, so the cost is 8, not 6, if the result must be a well-formed net. The justification then admits a sound policy-changing variant at cost 2, while still treating R3 at cost 6 as tied with R2, and inserts an unrelated two-arc addition that would deadlock the waiver path. R1 at cost 1 is fine; the comparison among the other repairs is not.

The bypass argument overclaims. The place set `{source, p_ready, p_analysis, p_analysis_done, p_decision, sink}` is a P-invariant of R1, but it is also preserved by the R3 bypass `p_ready → t_waive_review → p_decision`, which reaches `sink` without `t_analyze`. It does not prove that authorization consumed the analysis branch. The LTL sketch uses `executed(...)` as if it were a state predicate.

Smaller slips: “isomorphic” visible language instead of equal language, and a free-choice remark in the R1 row that is only cleaned up later. Sections 1–3 and the R1 code are strong; the false counts, the soundness clause, and the edit-cost argument keep this well short of nearly flawless.
