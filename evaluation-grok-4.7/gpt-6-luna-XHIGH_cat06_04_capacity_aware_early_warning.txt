The answer meets every formal requirement and handles the hard qualitative tests correctly: I-03 is rejected on stage grounds, I-05 is suppressed as an unchanged refresh while I-07 is reopened because of the new log, effects are kept separate from observational `p`, segment calibration is adjusted in the right direction, and spillovers, label feedback, and fairness are treated concretely. The scenario arithmetic is internally consistent and auditable. It is not nearly flawless, because the allocation rule and the chosen portfolio do not close.

The decision rule says to rank on conservative direct contribution—(lower-bound risk reduction × breach value) − €450 − false-alarm burden—then adjust for time, evidence, and queue harm, using deadline bands only to break close ties. The implied conservative nets are about €3,550 for I-04, €2,350 for I-09, €1,500 for I-06, €950 for I-02, and only €500 for I-07, with I-08 negative. The selected set is I-02, I-07, and I-09. That can be defended as preserving options on a 10-hour case, but the answer never shows the adjusted score, never prices I-07’s marginal spillover against a €500 surplus, and omits the euro figure exactly for the weakest pick. “Close ties” does not describe a €3,550 versus €500 gap, so the rule is not deployable as written.

Section 3 then reports that the same three-slot portfolio can be net-negative in the adverse case and that the base case is not assured once unpriced peers are included, without revising how many slots are committed or stating a robust stopping rule. Full false-alarm burden is also deducted in every scenario, including the optimistic one. That follows the prompt’s sketch formula, but it is not an expected cost: it is largest precisely where a breach is least likely, and for I-08 it is what flips the sign. There is no comment on that.

Operationally, two specialists deliver each 45-minute swarm and I-07 has 90 minutes left, yet there is no dispatch order. I-06, which is already in diagnosis and has a higher conservative contribution than I-02 or I-07, is left behind a reserve that may be held until 15:00. The 60-minute suppression window is also ambiguous about whether an unchanged alert can re-enter once the clock expires. Fairness and monitoring are concrete and cover interference, drift, and threshold changes; those sections are the strongest part of the answer.
