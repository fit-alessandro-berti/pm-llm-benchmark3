7.2

The design is a real calendar-aware, resource-constrained twin with non-stationary arrivals, cross-case coupling, a latent objection layer, and an explicit refusal to treat the run as an optimum. That is the right problem. It is not nearly flawless: the measurement plan biases the metrics used to choose policies, public notice is generated from the wrong mechanism, and several implemented parameters contradict the written design.

The serious statistical flaw is in the run design. Scoring cases that arrive and complete in months 4–15 of an 18-month replication gives late arrivals only about one quarter of follow-up. Complex variance has median 140 days and p90 260 days, so conditioning on completion drops the right tail and pulls median, p90, and SLA compliance downward for the case types D5, D6, D8, and D9 are supposed to change. “Arrived and completed in the core window” is also ambiguous; if both timestamps must fall inside that window, long cases are structurally truncated. Backlog accounting for open cases does not repair the duration metrics. A 90-day warm-up is short relative to those sojourns unless the seasonal snapshot is already correct, and the snapshot is not required to match the simulation’s starting season. Warm-up deletion, CRN, intervals on differences, heavy-tailed service, and a ban on exponential draws are otherwise sound. The NHPP \(\lambda_k s(t) w(t) h(t)\) has no normalization or identifiability constraint, so seasonality and weekday effects can be double-counted once \(\lambda_k\) is an average rate. Multiplicity across D1–D10, case types, and tail metrics is unchecked. A t-interval on replication medians or p90s is a weak default even with BCa offered as an alternative.

Public notice is the main process miss. The log summary ties that sojourn to holidays and the council calendar, and complex variance is described as council-calendar dependent. The generator is only a 14-day point mass plus a latent objection extension. That misses hearing dates that synchronize many cases onto the same release week, which is a different bottleneck than a heavy-tailed mixture and a different object from legal officers being off during council weeks. Holidays “where policy says so” never become an operational rule. Objection events are correctly kept latent, and D9 is correctly barred from winning on that layer alone.

The skeleton contradicts the design in code that actually runs, not only in stubs. D7 lowers Monday’s multiplier from 2.2 to 1.15 and does not move that mass to Tuesday–Thursday, so weekly volume falls and cycle-time gains are confounded with hidden demand loss. The prose says the opposite. `prior_violations` defaults to false, so D2 skips inspection for every cheap simple case; the assumptions say the base imputation is conservative and treats some of those cases as ineligible. Simple-renovation missing-document probability is hardcoded at 0.28. The variant table partitions simple renovation into 26% clean and 19% missing documents, so the implied conditional probability is about 0.42. D1 then multiplies by 0.4, a 60% cut, while the experiment grid is −40% and −70%. D9’s code cuts the extension by 20% against a stated 10/30 grid. Jun–Aug and December are fixed at 1.2 and 0.9 while the text says to interpolate them from the log. Seeds use process-salted `hash()` and differ by experiment, which breaks both reproducibility and the stated CRN scheme \(s+r\) with shared substreams. `experiment_matrix()` drops C2 and retargets the stress cells relative to the table.

Resource detail is uneven. The entity list promises individual clerks and planners, including one Monday portal clerk and two simple-only planners. The calendar object is a pool: `reserved_fraction` is never applied, so the 40% environmental haircut does nothing in `is_open`, and council weeks block urgent legal work that the assumptions keep open. Fire and inspector hours are invented and not listed as assumptions. Routing hardcodes a few Bernoulli draws and omits the fire, environment, public-notice, and legal gates that actually determine path mix. Rework is independent given case type, while architect-level document quality is also described as correlated; those cannot both be the base model without a specified copula or shared latent.

PM4Py use is largely conventional: pandas load, no `pm4py.read_csv`, `format_dataframe` with the three standard keys, variants, DFG, performance DFG, case-arrival average, and `parse_process_tree` with the tree’s real limits stated. Completion-only sojourn is not falsely split into wait and service. The twin is correctly separated from replay, and validation is staged in the right order, including transfer risk for policies that change unobserved mechanisms.

The experiment grid and decision section are strong on non-additivity, applicant burden, district and architect fairness, and robustness gates. They still set SLA levels and a +5 percentage-point rule without checking them against the given medians and p90s, and D8 never settles whether triage is a second queue, a shorter service time, or a skipped consultant. Comparative claims are appropriately caveated. The answer would be in the high 8s if the follow-up window, council-pinned public notice, and the implemented parameter contradictions were fixed.
