7.5

The equivalence analysis is strong: the observation boundaries are explicit, the visible languages are characterized correctly, the P2 record-set proof holds for every finite revision count, and the four witnesses plus the four-trace intersection argument are valid. The bounded counts 76, 85, and 4 also match an independent enumeration, and the BFS is a genuine exhaustive exploration rather than playout. The P2 simplification preserves exactly the dashboard language it claims, and the recommendation not to let it replace Model A is sound.

The complexity results are not. Under the standard process-tree encoding, size depends only on the multiset of operator arities, and both originals have the same multiset, so both nets have 10 places, 10 transitions, and 22 arcs. The simplified net has 6, 6, and 12. The reported 9/9/20, 7/7/16, and 5/5/10 figures are false, and the simplicity values 0.818182 and 0.777778 are just the arc-degree formula applied to those false sizes. Model B is not smaller than Model A, so the claim that it has fewer nodes yet a worse simplicity score is a false comparison. The ten-digit Earth Mover’s Distance is presented as an executed result beside those incorrect measurements and is not reproducible from anything derived in the answer. Tree-node, depth, and leaf counts are correct, and the simplified tree is still smaller, but the required net comparison is numerically unreliable.
