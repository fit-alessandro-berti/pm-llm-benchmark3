4.7

The final write-up keeps the required seven-part structure and reaches a defensible headline for QA (time-triggered appointments, otherwise FIFO, priority not in force) plus a sound cross-queue warning that priority is unsafe to infer. Those are outweighed by factual failures in the required decision-set reconstruction.

Eligible sets are wrong at most tabulated epochs. Completed or in-service jobs are treated as candidates, and jobs that were actually waiting are dropped. At 08:58, A02 has already finished (08:52), yet FIFO is said to select A02; A06–A08 are omitted, so FIFO, priority, and SPT all select A04 and the row does not discriminate. At 09:20, A05 is finishing, not waiting, and priority selects A08 (priority 1), not A06. At QB 10:00, B03 is finishing; the real set is B01, B04, and B05, so FIFO does not predict B03. At 10:28, B01 is finishing and B07 (estimate 6, appointment 10:30) is missing, so SPT and appointment are never tested. At QC 10:10, C03 (priority 2) and C04 are omitted, and priority is incorrectly called a tie. At 10:54, C04 has been in service since 10:32, while C01 and C07 are absent. At 11:40, C06 has just finished and the real alternative is C07, which SPT, priority, and earliest-deadline all prefer to C01. On QD, the informative D-M choice is 11:25, when D04 is taken over D08; the 11:35 row reverses that and treats a moment when FIFO, priority, and SPT agree as a priority win. At 12:16, D05 and D10 do not compete for one resource.

The QD story depends on a false resource state: D-F’s meeting is 12:00–12:20, so D-F is idle at 11:48 and could take D10 (estimate 12) before the meeting. D-X’s idle 11:20–11:30 and D06’s exact 11:30 start, the clearest appointment evidence on QD, never enters the diagnosis. D-F’s idle 11:36–12:00 is also unexamined.

Support and contradiction are misaligned with the stated diagnoses. A04 waiting behind A02 supports appointment-plus-FIFO; it is not a contradiction. For QB, the listed “support” is priority behavior (B02’s preemption, B05 after B03), while B03-before-B05 and B04-before-B06 support FIFO and contradict priority. The prose then switches the QB mechanism to non-preemptive priority, which still fails at 09:31 (B01 resumes over B03), 09:48 (B03 over waiting B05), and 10:28 (B04 over B06).

QC as SPT with a deadline override, at confidence 70, is not earned. Dynamic SPT already fails when C04 (22) starts at 10:32 ahead of waiting C06 (6). C05 was already impossible to finish by 10:50 at 10:18, yet was not pulled then. Starting C01 at 11:40 misses C07’s 12:00 deadline and contradicts SPT, priority, and earliest deadline. Deadline-versus-priority equivalence is named once and not carried through those cases.

The counterfactuals do not discriminate the real competitors. The QA test still assumes A02 is waiting at 08:58. The QD test treats “if D-F were free” as hypothetical when D-F was free and D10 was not sent there. Calibration is credited for A04’s delay, but it only covers 08:52–08:58; the anti-priority choice is A02’s start at 08:32. B07’s missed appointment, resumption cost for B01, the two-minute ambiguity around B05 versus the 09:48 stop, and estimate noise on D08 (8) versus D04 (10) are thin or absent. The collection list drops queue-entry time, resource state, and policy version, and it does not target QC re-optimization versus batching or the D-F idle.
