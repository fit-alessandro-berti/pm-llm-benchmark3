3.5

The write-up has the required nine sections and a recognizable specialization–pooling–hybrid frame, but the quantitative core is not auditable and several claims contradict the given skill matrix and the code.

Offered load is not a time-block or skill analysis. The ~50-minute case time and ~2,100-minute day ignore the second ME/ES branch, multipliers, handover, setup, and coordination. Gross shift minutes are then called “effective” capacity after arbitrary speed haircuts, and those haircuts do not add to the stated ~2,500 minutes. Farah is treated as unable to do software, and Ada is omitted from electrical, despite the matrix. U1–U5 effects stay qualitative; U2 is not renormalized, and U4’s 15–20% uplift is not shown.

The comparison table misses p90/p95 delay, P2 SLA, utilization fairness, resilience, and failure mode, and it gives false-precision point estimates that the code never produces. Policy C reserves Ada/Ben/Deepa as “core,” but that pool cannot do software, so the recommended rule is infeasible for S/ES. Policy text also says software stops if Chen is absent, while Emil and Farah are qualified. Fairness complaints about pooling conflict with the table’s own max-utilization column. The SLA and fairness scores in section 1 are never computed, so robustness is not actually measured.

The Python is not a valid three-policy, six-scenario model. Durations are divided by multipliers that the prompt defines as slowdowns; breaks, shifts, the 7-minute handover, team-change delay, coordination, parallel branches, and the 10% preemption penalty are not applied; U1’s 1.7 factor is applied all day; U2 does not double E/ES; activities are logged as “Diag,” which is absent from the service table; and the next stage is not forced to wait for the previous end. Only Base and U1 under one policy are printed. `insert_case_service_waiting_time` and `discover_handover_of_work_network` are not executed, and `pm4py.get_bottleneck` is not a real analysis. Omitted mechanisms are not identified, so this does not qualify as an honest scaffold.

Monitoring omits queue entry, the eligible set, skill used, pause/resume, and an SLA clock. The case-type A/B pilot confounds policy with mix. Assumptions and secondary risks are directionally sensible, but they do not repair the load, policy, and reproducibility failures.
