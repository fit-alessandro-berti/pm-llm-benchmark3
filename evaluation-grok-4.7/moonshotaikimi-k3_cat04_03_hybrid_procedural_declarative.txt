The conceptual core is strong: R5/R6 is treated as a real conflict, Resolution B is chosen with an explicit residual risk, T6 is scored under both resolutions, and DECLARE directionality is mostly right (`noncoexistence` rather than one-way `nonsuccession`, `altresponse` rather than `chainresponse`, and no binary encoding of “opinion between escalation and activation”). Temporal conversions, \(\zeta = 2.0\), and the T7 window (34 h flagged, 2 h payment at \(z \approx 1.94\) not flagged) are correct. The process tree admits T1–T4, T6, and T7 and rejects T5 and T8 for the right structural reasons.

The log skeleton is not safe against PM4Py. `conformance_log_skeleton` treats `equivalence` and `always_before` / `always_after` as constraints the model imposes on the trace, not as whitelists of pairs the trace may exhibit. This model puts both orders of the concurrent checks into `always_before`, and both `(Request Clarification, Receive Clarification)` and the reverse, then sets `always_after` to the exact dual. Under the real checker those pairs contradict each other, so T1–T7 would fail the skeleton; the matrix column that marks them `pass` is wrong. `directly_follows` and `never_together` are closer to the actual checker, but they do not rescue the other relations.

Section 7 does not match Section 8. `check_clarification_before_outcome` is inverted: `all(tx2 >= rc)` is the compliant case (answer before `Activate Vendor` / `Reject Vendor`), yet the function returns `fail`. It therefore rejects T2 and accepts a clarification that arrives only after the outcome. `check_approval_cardinality` returns `fail` on T8, and the unanswered-request branch fails T5, while the matrix marks the resource/data layer `pass` for both. The segregation check also treats a missing activity as a missing resource.

The tree and the skeleton disagree on counts. The tree has two bank-change slots, so one case can contain two `Change Bank Details` and three `Verify Bank Account` events; `activ_freq` allows CBD in `{0,1}` and VBA in `{1,2}`. That contradiction is not called out in Section 9.
