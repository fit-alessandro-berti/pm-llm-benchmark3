7.5

The role model is numerically faithful and meets the required six-part structure, but it is not nearly flawless. Several overstatements and a role-discovery script that would not actually join PM4Py’s results keep it well below a top score.

The activity counts, medians, critical-case totals, similarities, and substitution figures match the evidence. Separating the bot, temps, certified front office, technical pair, security, commercial specialist, Giorgio, Hana, quality, and Mateo is justified, and the answer correctly refuses to treat frequency, similarity, or cover as authority. Benefits and risks of the shadow roles are both stated, and sections 4 and 6 keep the main cautions.

The reasoning still overreaches. Week 5 is called “decisive” proof that Hana holds final authority, while section 4 still treats Mateo’s approvals as unresolved; the other 23 of his 44 A9 events are never accounted for, yet the role table labels his approvals “preliminary” in general. Mateo’s filtering is said to have “contributed to” the two critical waits; the log only places those waits in the same period. “Highest-risk” for Erik is defensible on median and concentration, but it is asserted without the absolute comparison that Mateo handled more critical cases (76 versus 55). Usual shift is ignored, so the resilience discussion misses a real single-point exposure: security, pricing, and final approval are all day-shift, with no late counterpart. “Uploaded benchmark” and Mateo doing “almost every activity except pure specialization” are vague; he has nonzero counts on all 14 activities.

The code uses the right high-level names and does try to attach complexity and substitution, but it is not executable as written. `discover_organizational_roles` yields `Role` objects whose resource field is `originators`, which `normalize_roles` never reads, so the join is empty. `activity_key` is passed into handover and subcontracting discovery, whose signatures do not take it. The network adapter does not handle the NetworkX graph those calls return, and the later column filter would fail. Activity similarity is computed and then discarded. Those are failures of the required discovery-and-join step, not cosmetic style issues.
