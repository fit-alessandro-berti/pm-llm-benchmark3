6.4

The required six-part structure is present, weekend calendars are applied, the join is treated as a maximum, and the excursion is recognized as a shared latent state. The headline forecast is still not internally reliable enough for a higher score.

The forecast summary disagrees with itself. S1 is given probability 0.45–0.55 with a central completion of Monday 16:00–21:00, and Monday evening release is then described as that same 45–55% outcome. The reported P50 is nevertheless Tuesday 08:00–14:30 (88–95 h). Those claims cannot be read as one distribution: if about half the mass finishes Monday evening, the median is still Monday night or the Tuesday 08:00 restart, not a band centered near Tuesday midday. The P80 row is also mislabeled. 105–125 h from Friday 15:30 is about Wednesday 00:30–20:30, not “Tuesday evening to Wednesday midday” (about 99–117 h).

Scenario probabilities and clocks are inconsistent across sections. The ledger raises contamination, given a confirmed excursion, to about 0.10–0.20; S4 uses 0.08–0.15. S7 says dependence multiplies the compound tail by roughly five relative to independence, while the ledger says the joint tail is “understated by roughly 2×” if independence is wrong. That sensitivity sentence also points the wrong way: if the events are independent, this forecast’s compound tail is too heavy. S2 is named “rerun only” but is given the full marginal rerun band, even though a rerun raises investigation probability to 0.30–0.55 and that investigation is absent from S2’s Tuesday timing. S6 assigns a confirmed excursion a central terminal time of Wednesday–Friday, although the flag only raises rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities; a large share of confirmed-excursion paths still release on the normal Monday–Tuesday clocks.

S4’s earliest terminal time, Tuesday 09:00, starts at the confirmation readout and omits QA waiting, QA service, and release or rejection. The same scenario later places fast rejection on Tuesday evening or Wednesday. The 4-hour end of the documentation-query range is similarly dropped: a short query opened Monday afternoon can finish Monday night or early Tuesday, but S3’s earliest time is Tuesday noon. Section 6 then overstates the Friday 17:00 update: being in service by 17:00 leaves about an hour of chemistry before 18:00, so Monday’s finish is near the microbiology readout only for a short service time, and a 15:00 join is the FIFO case rather than the automatic consequence of missing that start.

Load-bearing chemistry times (priority about Monday 10:00, FIFO about Monday 14:30–15:00) are asserted from “2.7 analyst-hours” without showing residual work for the urgent and ordinary samples ahead. The implied assumption that those samples share B-417’s service law is not in the ledger. The QA backlog arithmetic to about 4–5 batches by Monday 08:00 is sound in expectation, and the lognormal check (median 1.5 h, p80 3.5 h, mean about 2.5 h) is right, but that backlog is then collapsed to a 2–5 h wait without a stated join time, so the smooth-path Monday-evening center is not reproducible.

Microbiology readiness (Sunday 12:00–20:00), the Monday 08:00 staffing gate, pause-and-resume at the chemistry close, and the refusal to publish a single mean are defensible. Those strengths do not remove the contradictory percentiles, mismatched P80 clock, or overlapping scenario bands that do not describe the paths they name.
