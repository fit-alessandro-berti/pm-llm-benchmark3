Sections 1–4 and 6 are strong. Giorgio’s 20 A9 events are only “some approval,” even though they are not explained by Hana’s week-5 absence and exceed Fatima’s count. Mateo’s profile is also slightly shaped: A2 (50) and A4 (45) are omitted beside A12 (52), A9 (44), and A13 (42), and his 16 A6 events are described as handoffs. Section 5 names the right PM4Py functions and the right caution, but the shadow filter is wrong. `&` binds tighter than `|`, so any pair with an authority-share gap above 0.05 is kept even when similarity is low, which floods the list the comment says should be similarity-gated. Substitution overlap is mentioned in the comment and never applied. Case complexity is aggregated at event level, so busy cases outweigh others, and the 0.33/0.66 cuts are invented. `escalation_level` counted with `notna()` treats a filled field as an escalation. Network flattening assumes nested dicts while the similarity branch assumes tuple keys, so the two paths are inconsistent with each other and with typical SNA objects.

Operational advice in section 6 is sound and includes more than the required two cautions. The grade is held down by the authority relabeling and by code that does not actually implement the interpretation rule it states.
