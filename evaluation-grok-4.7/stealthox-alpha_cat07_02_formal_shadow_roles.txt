7.1

The role model is substantively strong: all six required sections are present, resources are not collapsed by raw frequency, complexity and substitution are used as real constraints, and the shadow-role discussion (Giorgio, Mateo, Erik, plus automation and temps) gives both benefits and risks. Most headline ratios check out (Fatima ~52% of A7, Erik 96/146 of A6, Hana ~63% of A9, Nora ~46% of A1 and ~27% of A2, Erik ~33% critical).

The score is held down by concrete errors and overclaims in the final text.

- Kai has 2 high-complexity cases and Lina 4. “4 high cases each” is false.
- Similarity 0.98 is Kai–Lina, not temps versus Amira/Ben. Section 6 treats 0.98 as evidence that temps mirror the generalists.
- “Cases do not proceed past A6 without Erik” is too strong: about a third of A6 events belong to Chen, Mateo, Dina, and Ivan.
- “Reversal rate (13/21)” equates Hana’s rechecks with reversals. The log only says those events were rechecked.
- Fatima’s 16 A9 events are performances of Supervisor Approval, not proof that she merely triggers approval. Giorgio’s 20 A9 events are left out of that authority discussion.
- The role table assigns Giorgio the commercial core (A7/A8) even though his volume core is still front-office work. Section 2 corrects this, but the table itself does not.
- “Fewest knowledge-base updates” does not fit Erik (9), who out-documents several humans; Fatima (0) and Hana (0) are the empty cases.
- “38→74” is rendered as “3874 min” in the staffing recommendation, so one action line is numerically unusable even though 38 and 74 appear correctly elsewhere.

The PM4Py sketch names the right functions and states the right cautions, but it is not coherent as written. `format_dataframe` is given a `resource_key` it does not take. Role objects are read through `.resources`, `.role_name`, and `.origin_ev` instead of originators and activities. Activity similarity is indexed as a DataFrame and passed `measure="cosine"`, while the real call returns a graph and has no such parameter. `cluster_log` is computed and then ignored, despite the comment that role mining should be per segment. Weighted betweenness treats handover counts as distances, which inverts strength, yet the comment expects Mateo on top. The A9-only handover, especially with `level="events"`, does not show an authority sink. Incumbent-versus-substitute service time is described and not computed, and no start timestamp is supplied for service time.

Operational guidance is otherwise usable: protect Erik, do not treat temp volume as capacity, and do not promote mined clusters into authority. Those cautions are undercut by the 0.98 mis-cite and the 3874 figure.
