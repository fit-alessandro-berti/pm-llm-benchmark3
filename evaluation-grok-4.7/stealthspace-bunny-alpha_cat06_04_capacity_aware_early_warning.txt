6.5

The answer is a complete six-part policy with the right traps identified: I-03 is refused, I-05 and I-07 are treated differently, effects are kept separate from observational probabilities, calibration is segmented, and spillovers are monetized only for cases that can actually be identified. Those strengths do not offset several consequential internal failures.

The allocation contradicts the answer’s own rule. Section 1 says a case should not take a slot when credible marginal queue harm exceeds expected benefit, and it sets the preferred test at \(e_{\text{low}}V-(€450+B)>0\). Applied uniformly, conservative direct nets are about €3,550 (I-04), €2,350 (I-09), €1,500 (I-06), €1,200 (I-05), €950 (I-02), €920 (I-01), €720 (I-10), and €500 (I-07). One slot at the stated 0.01–0.03 spillover on the three known diagnosis-stage cases (combined value €60,000) costs about €600–€1,800. I-07 fails that test on both the conservative and midpoint figures (€500 and about €800). I-01 clears it only at the low end of spillover. The selected portfolio is nevertheless I-07 and I-01 immediately, then I-04, with I-09 only as a reserve alternate. The spillover check is postponed until the second wave, which is the case that most easily survives it.

I-07 also fails the answer’s own completion-path gate. It is still in triage with 1.5 hours left. A 45-minute swarm leaves about 45 minutes for the rest of triage, diagnosis completion, resolution, and customer validation. The write-up records that uncertainty and still assigns the first slot. I-02 and I-09 are already in Diagnosis Started, inside the only window where the swarm works, with shorter deadlines and better conservative nets, and they are told to wait. “Diagnosis already active” is used as a reason to withhold the intervention that is defined for that stage.

Two factual errors prop up that ordering. I-01 is supported by “potential Public Sector underprediction,” while the stated evidence is that Public Sector calibration is broadly acceptable and Section 1 correctly refuses a directional Public Sector adjustment. I-07’s “0.20–0.31 adjusted risk range” is neither the SME-adjusted probability (about 0.76–0.81) nor the supplied effect range (0.20–0.32).

The adverse row is not auditable. For effects \((0.18,\ 0.10,\ 0\text{–}0.20)\),

Using €5,400 of known-case harm, the corrected adverse net is about −€1.4k to −€0.4k. The published upper end has the wrong sign. Optimistic and base gross benefits, the €3,650 cost, the 0.95 and 0.72 case-equivalents, and the €1,800–€5,400 known-case spillover arithmetic are otherwise correct. Optimistic effects are the top endpoints; the requested scenario is the upper half of each range.

Section 4 draws the right I-05 / I-07 distinction, including that I-05 has had no prior swarm and that I-07’s small decline does not erase the new log. The same section conditions I-07 on a usable log and an open path, which the 12:00 slot assignment does not wait for. I-05’s SME-adjusted risk is omitted, and a positive review has no defined contest against the I-09 alternate.

Section 5 names the right mechanisms and gives operational safeguards, with two weak spots. The training-label paragraph says a naive model will learn that treated cases “would have breached anyway, even though the swarm helped.” Observed post-swarm non-breaches teach the opposite contamination: prevented breaches are labeled as non-breaches. The proposed logs and pre-intervention snapshots are the right controls for that problem. The 0.80 “adjusted treatment-rate ratio” has no defined numerator or denominator.

Section 6 is specific on operations, calibration, versioned snapshots, and capacity, and randomization within oversubscribed tiers is a real identification plan. That plan conflicts with the value- and obligation-weighted ranking, because Tier A mixes roughly €5k and €60k cases. A 15-minute start standard for accepted swarms is incompatible with two specialists and a 45-minute treatment. Fairness monitoring stays at the undefined ratio from Section 5.

Case-level figures that do check out include I-03 (€600 versus about €850), I-04 (€3,550; adjusted risk about 0.36–0.43), I-08 (−€450 to €9,550; break-even near 0.055), and the midpoint nets cited for I-02, I-05, I-06, and I-10. The reserve slot is conditional rather than automatic. Those are real merits. The grade is held down because the portfolio violates the answer’s own economic and eligibility tests, one calibration claim is factually reversed, and the adverse net-impact range is arithmetically wrong in a way that changes its sign.
