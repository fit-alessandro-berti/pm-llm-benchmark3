7.5

The formal core is strong: the four observation boundaries are defined clearly, duplicate labels are treated as position and multiplicity rather than transition identity, loop scope is interpreted correctly, and the four complete traces are genuine two-sided witnesses. A-only traces are impossible in B because B has a single check; B-only-1 puts approval before that check, which A’s per-iteration join forbids; B-only-2 crosses `Rework Case` before any check, which A’s mandatory first-iteration parallel forbids. The P1/P3 verdicts of not equivalent, the P2 projection `(n, check occurred, rework iff n > 1)`, the tau/approval discussion, and the warning that a finite bound can refute but cannot prove unbounded equality all hold.

The required program does not. `MODEL_B_STRING` is unbalanced: the outer sequence is closed before `'Close Case'`, leaving an extra parenthesis, so `parse_process_tree` fails and the soundness check, bounded search, and EMD block never run. The BFS design itself is the right exhaustive construction (silent labels hidden, length and analysis bounds applied only to visible labels, acceptance only at the exact final marking, state identity including the visible trace). Tree counts 12/12/7, depths 4/5/2, and leaf counts are correct, and withholding an unexecuted `simplicity_petri_net` number is appropriate.

Smaller faults remain. “The first two traces use the silent branch” is false of A-only-2, which contains `Approve Revision`. The P2 write-up shows that both models can realize each record `(n, true, n > 1)` but does not explicitly close the other direction, that no complete trace maps outside that set. The footprint paragraph overstates blindness: directly-follows footprints miss check cardinality, but they do expose B’s `Approve Revision`–`Check Revision` interleaving. The structural “before approval, Rework, or Close” wording is ambiguous, and `marking_key` assumes unique `place.name` values. The P2 simplification is honestly lossy and structurally smaller, but two of its three regression traces are not behaviors of Model A.
