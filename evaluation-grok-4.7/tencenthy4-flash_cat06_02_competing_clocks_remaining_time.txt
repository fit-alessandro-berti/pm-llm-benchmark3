7.5

The forecast is structurally complete and analytically serious: parallel branches are joined by a maximum, weekend calendars are applied to chemistry, microbiology readout, and QA, queue time is separated from service, reruns stop at lab close, and the temperature excursion is treated as a dependence rather than an independent multiplier. Hours and dates are internally consistent, ranges are used instead of a false point estimate, and release versus rejection is distinguished.

The score is held down by several concrete weaknesses. The “delayed microbiology” scenario, given a 50–80% band and a central span from Monday afternoon through Wednesday, mixes a common non-binding Monday readout with the much rarer contamination-confirmation path, so the table overstates how often Wednesday occurs. Monday QA capacity is under-modeled: two reviewers from 08:00 are not carried through the queue drain, which makes the 4–10 h wait tail too strong. The smooth-path chemistry window of roughly 10:30–15:00 is only loosely tied to Friday partial progress, and short documentation queries that can still finish Monday evening are pushed into a Tuesday-only tail. The 1–5% rejection band is not derived from the 5–12% contamination indication, and the Saturday QA “narrow window” is effectively shut once a 46 h incubation plus at least 1 h dispatch lands at 13:00.
