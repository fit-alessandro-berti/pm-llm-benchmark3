8.2

The dialectical analysis is strong, case-faithful, and unusually careful about authority, but the revised POWL misses the observed behavior it is meant to legitimize and applies the answer’s own exception burden unevenly.

Sections 1–5 are the high-quality part. M0 is read correctly: sanctions and quality are unordered, both are mandatory before the XOR, and that forces `Quality_Review` before rejection even though CS-9 states quality in relation to activation. PD-3 versus FD-7, expiry versus ongoing facts, maker-checker versus the spouse disclosure rule, stable `notification_id` versus a late mutable CRM link, and duplicate registration versus two business events are kept distinct. Both briefs concede real points. The matrix covers all 15 cases and the reversal conditions are specific. `MIXED` for SUP-702 is explained rather than used as a tie-breaker: pilot predicates are present, while derogation authority and a recorded master-data validation are not. System-level separation of “material control and exception-governance failures” from “M0 is only a baseline flow” is justified by SUP-703, SUP-705, SUP-708, SUP-712, SUP-714, and SUP-715, without treating the 15 cases as a compliance rate.

The model revision is where the score drops. The normative loop is `Request_More_Info` then `Evidence_Received`, one or more times. SUP-707, the case used to justify the loop, is `Request_More_Info`, `Request_More_Info`, with no evidence event. Loop semantics also forbid reordering those pairs, so the motivating trace is still outside M1. The limitation note discusses unresolved-request counting and business days, and does not state this fitness failure. `Automated_Master_Data_Validation`, `Daily_Transaction_Monitoring`, `Reactivation_Assessment`, and `Validate_Prior_Quality` are mandatory visible activities, so the SUP-702 and SUP-704 traces also fail M1 even on the answer’s favorable reading. Those controls belong in the design, but the text never says the historical traces remain non-conformant until new events or an explicit mapping exist. Monitoring is both a required POWL activity and a daily data rule, with no account of how those two layers interact.

The same strictness is not applied in classification. The stated exception test requires recorded safeguards. SUP-704 is still `JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION` although only initial spend of EUR 18,000 is shown, not cumulative spend before retrospective approval. SUP-702 stays `MIXED` partly because validation is unlogged. That distinction is only partly earned by the weaker PD-3 authority record. SUP-711 as `COMPLIANT` assumes a dedupe step the raw trace does not satisfy. The standard route still requires quality before every post-acceptance rejection, while Section 6 leaves that policy question open. Withdrawal can occur only immediately after registration. Auto and emergency routes have no quality-failure or refused-retrospective path into the offboarding process described in the architecture. DECLARE is named and not instantiated. The two-business-day rule has no calendar, holiday, or timezone definition.

The PM4Py shape itself is largely sound: distinct transition objects, n-ary XOR, a real two-child loop, silent skip for zero repetitions, concurrent standard checks, manual-before-activate, sanctions-before-provisional-activation, and notification before closure. The policy-overlay and event-field tables include rule versions, eligibility snapshots, aliases, delegation, authority, and immutable timestamps. Those strengths keep this well above an ordinary split-the-difference review.
