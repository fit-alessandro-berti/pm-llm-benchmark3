The JSON is complete and ordered, and the primary labels for C6-A, C6-B, C6-C, and C6-E are the right abstractions. Those strengths do not carry the forecasts. Several rankings contradict the answer’s own control-flow claims, one case invents evidence, and the required split between an enabled action and the next log observation is missed on every row because `process_enabled` is true fifteen times.

C6-A is the best case and still flawed. Evidence Received at 12:10 puts the case in S3; Evidence Review Completed is the right first successor, and high severity, injury, two devices from one lot, and three similar complaints do support later Technical Investigation, Regulatory Assessment, and Supplier Analysis. The alternative is not S2. S2 requires a request with no receipt, and the receipt is already logged. High-severity queue priority would speed review, not return the case to evidence-pending.
