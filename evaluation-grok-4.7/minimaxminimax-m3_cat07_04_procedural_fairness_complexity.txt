6.8

The fairness reasoning is careful and mostly well calibrated: raw gaps are not treated as discrimination, case mix is not treated as a full excuse, risk scores are treated as suspect, support steps are analyzed as burden, and mitigations keep those steps while naming trade-offs. All eight required topics are covered, and most figures match the tables.

The central claim is still overstated. Language-support and accommodation waits are descriptive path delays, not residuals from the adjusted model, yet the key insight and bottom line present them as adjusted residuals. Group D is then grouped with B and C on handovers and missing-document burden, although D’s adjusted step gaps are small (+1.5 and +2.0 pp) and the handover evidence is about B and C. “11% elsewhere” for variant V3 is wrong (A 9%, B 11%, C 8%), and C and D are mislabeled “minority groups.”

The PM4Py plan would not perform the stated analysis. `get_all_case_durations` is treated as waiting time; `filter_variants` is called with a flat activity list and a nonexistent `positive` argument; `filter_case_performance(..., max_performance=30)` keeps shorter cases, not the right tail, and ignores second-based units; the service-time insertion result is discarded; and log-skeleton/DECLARE cannot test the attribute rule described. Extra headings also violate the required heading set. The conclusion is directionally cautious, but those factual and methodological errors keep it well short of nearly flawless.
