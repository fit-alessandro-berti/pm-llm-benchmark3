6.5

The control analysis is structured, uses the required labels, and usually separates nominal four-eyes form from the underlying objective. ED-1 checks for AP-203, AP-206, and AP-208 are counted carefully, AP-205 is correctly treated as stopped before completion, and the post-release/pre-settlement manager step is reasonably classed as hybrid. Those strengths do not make the conclusions reliable.

The case table contradicts itself. AP-204 is OBJECTIVE_FAILED because the u12/u13 pairing and shared workstation undermine independence, yet AP-209, the same pair the same day, is OBJECTIVE_MET with the claim that “form and substance both satisfied.” No detected defect does not prove an independent challenge, and the missing cell-design evidence is the same in both cases. AP-209 should be indeterminate or only partially met. AP-204’s failed label is also firmer than the unresolved collusion-versus-negligence record.

Factual slips accumulate. The audit for AP-201 starts about 25 hours after release, outside the usual 24-hour recall window, but the facts table marks recall as still available. Eight months of vendor history is not “borderline” against a 180-day rule. m21 is coded as independent although independence is stated only for m04, m02, m11, and m15, and m09 is explicitly conflicted. AP-202’s manager action is described as after settlement even though that case has no settlement event.

The required code does not join the case-facts frame to the log, hardcodes the repeated-pair flag, and treats `filter_four_eyes_principle` as definitely returning violators. That direction is version-dependent; the known behavior often keeps cases that satisfy four-eyes, which would invert the printed “violations,” and the write-up does not flag or cross-check that risk.
