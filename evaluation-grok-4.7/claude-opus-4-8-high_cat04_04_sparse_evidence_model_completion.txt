The document is complete and often conceptually sharp, but two of the three models do not implement the languages the text claims, and several headline conclusions are therefore false.

The evidence ledger’s treatment of bidirectional DFG edges is correct: both CC↔ER directions are necessary for concurrency and also for an XOR of sequences or an unordered partial order, and completion timestamps cannot show overlap. Missing Supplier Confirmation versus legitimate skips is disclosed as one control-flow abstraction. The conservative XOR of the seven observed pre-approval skeletons, with the shared tail factored out, really does generate exactly the observed language, and the duplicate-label and scalability discussion is appropriate. The validation script’s limitation statement about sampled playout is also right.

Those strengths are outweighed by failures in the models that are supposed to generalize.

The balanced POWL is internally inconsistent. The prose says Supplier Confirmation is unordered with the checks, approximates “after either check,” and admits `ER, SC, CC`. The edges `CC → SC_opt` and `ER → SC_opt` do the opposite: both checks must finish before SC. That rejects observed variant V5 (`Register Order, Credit Check, Supplier Confirmation, Engineering Review, …`, 2 cases) and also rejects the audit trace claimed as admitted. The same section then says this join is stricter than D5 and, two sentences later, that `⟨ER, SC, CC⟩` is admitted. In POWL, incomparable activities are concurrent; leaving CC and ER unordered is an AND, not a way to avoid asserting concurrency. XOR of the two sequences would have been the non-concurrent encoding. Fitness is therefore not ~1.0, and `⟨ER, SC, CC⟩` does not separate this model from the conservative one.

The permissive tree has the same class of error. `^` is not a PM4Py operator (`+` is), so `model_permissive` does not parse. Even rewritten with `+`, SC sits inside the parallel block that must finish before optional Expedite, so observed V7 (`… Expedite Review, Supplier Confirmation, …`) is rejected. The bias statement also says Expedite is concurrent with the checks; the tree sequences it after them. “Subsumes all observed interleavings” is false.

Those mistakes propagate. Shared assumption A6 (Expedite as a silent XOR after both checks) is not true of the conservative tree. SC is present in 23/30 traces, not 22/30. “V1 17” confuses a DFG arc with a variant count. The matrix’s ~1.0 fitness row, the positive suite comment expecting balanced to accept `ER, SC, CC`, and the recommendation of balanced because it “fits all 30 cases” all restate the same unchecked claim. The data-collection plan’s supposedly decisive `⟨ER, SC, CC⟩` trace is rejected by both conservative and balanced.

A sound balanced model had to accept V5 while keeping both checks before approval; a sound permissive model had to accept V7 and S6’s Expedite-before-or-after-SC rule. Neither candidate does. The write-up discusses uncertainty well and then recommends a model that contradicts its own log.
