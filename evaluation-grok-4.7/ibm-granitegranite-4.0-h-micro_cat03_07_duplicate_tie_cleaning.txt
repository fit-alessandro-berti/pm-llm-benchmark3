The answer is not a usable cleaning design. It covers only part of the required structure, contradicts its own decisions, and mishandles the cases the brief exists to protect.

Sections 8 and 9 are absent, and section 7 stops mid-sentence. The required provenance schema, raw-view reconstruction, and at least eight failure modes are never specified. Prose length also falls short of the requested range, largely because those sections and the concrete sensitivity discussion are missing.

The decision tree is not operationally reliable. Exact duplication is defined as matching `source_event_key` and `source_seq`, which this table does not contain. Same-physical-event detection requires an equal `payload_hash`, so it misses e03/e04, whose payloads differ because WMS added a location. Concurrent same-label events on different objects are illustrated with e11/e16, which share O1 and V1 and are the same invoice, and the prescribed action is to merge them. That rule would also collapse e05 into e03 and M2 into M1.

The event table repeats that confusion. e03/e05 is called the same physical scan. e06/e07 is both “merged” and “both retained.” e13/e15 is collapsed onto e13, and e14/e15 onto e14, so e15 is assigned to two different canonical events and M1/M2 are mixed into an invoice-level balance update. Every relation-merge policy is “N/A,” including pairs whose object sets differ (I1 versus I2, items present only on e06, Payment objects absent from e15). Ambiguous pairs do not get separate conservative and aggressive scenarios. Confidence is “medium” for facts the brief states directly and “high” for merges the brief leaves open.

The 10:00 order is e03, e04, e06, e07, omitting e05. The 12:00 order is e11, e13, e14, e15, e16, omitting e12, so the required Authorize Payment → Capture Payment constraint on M1 disappears. Ingestion time is described as evidence of the latest business activity. A global rule that finance precedes logistics would place the 12:00 invoice before the 10:15 dispatch. The microseconds discussion talks about excess precision and does not explain that synthetic fractions, applied before a semantic partial order and written over the business timestamp, become false directly-follows relations and false durations.

The conservative pipeline gives “around 80%” rather than a count derived from stated merges.
