5.5

The framework is sound: offset direction, resolution-before-correction, CEST/EDT/BST, and the T-405 fold reduction to one order-feasible triple are right, and several headline calls hold (T-402 S1 certain violation; T-408 S2/S3 certain violations; most SLA tolerance flips). The required certain-vs-possible distinction is still wrong in a central case, and several published intervals are arithmetically false.

T-404 start is not [10:09:58.5, 10:14:01.5]. Completion minus a duration in [18, 25] min is [10:09:58.5, 10:17:01.5], so Alert→Start reaches about 1023 s. S1 is a possible violation, not a certain pass, and the zeta=2 profile band is only partially covered. T-407 receipt is Sent plus [0, 20] min, ending near 14:20:01.5, not 14:12:01.5; that upper end can fall after investigation start, so the causal constraint is binding and the published [297, 1023] s duration is too narrow. T-401 containment→close is about 86919–86942 s, not 83319–83342 s. T-408 close is about 138297 s, not 137097 s, and Alert→Containment is about 3897–3903 s, not 3657–3663 s. Those last errors do not flip the baseline labels, but they are still wrong feasible intervals.

Section 3 marks T-408 Investigation→Containment a zeta=2 certain pass on [3297, 3303] against a band ending at 3300; section 4 later calls zeta=2 a possible violation. Sensitivity omits material zeta changes, including T-402 and T-408 Alert→Containment (certain violation at zeta=1), T-403 Alert→Containment, T-405 Alert→Investigation, T-406 and T-409 Investigation→Containment, and T-404’s profile pairs. Section 6 does not cover each possible violation. The PM4Py block is not executable: event rows are a comment, DST alternatives are not built, `timedelta(seconds=59, 999999)` is a syntax error, interval classification and the midpoint comparison are not implemented, and `format_dataframe` is called with the wrong positional argument.
