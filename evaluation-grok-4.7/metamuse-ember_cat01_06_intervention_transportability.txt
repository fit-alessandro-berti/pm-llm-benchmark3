6.6

The transport argument is strong and the required format is complete, but the required executable model is not faithful, and several quantitative claims are internally inconsistent.

The process comparison covers the required features and correctly treats statutory ordering, role specialization, case locking, mix shift, and timestamp quality as transport barriers. Co-interventions are not mistaken for the treatment: the nine-hour median drop is explicitly unidentified, the 6-hour waiting drop is contrasted with the roughly 1.1-hour service-time drop, and maximum pure service overlap is correctly bounded near the shorter check, about 3.5 hours at A and 3.4 hours at B. The modified intervention correctly refuses universal document-task creation, keeps originals and legal review mandatory, and separates mechanism failure from implementation failure. The conditional-go decision, stratified pilot, interference controls, and eight testable assumptions fit the evidence.

The PM4Py block does not meet the model-fidelity requirement. `Activity`, `PartialOrder`, `Choice`, and `Loop` are not the POWL constructors in `pm4py.objects.powl.obj`; the working types are `Transition`, `StrictPartialOrder`, and `OperatorPOWL`. Order is assigned as a plain list rather than through the partial-order edge relation, so the Assessment gate is not actually established in PM4Py. The top-level `StrictPartialOrder` has no edges. Under real POWL semantics those nodes are concurrent, so Register, verification, decision, and Notify are unordered. The supplement loop contains only Legal Review and Request Supplement; Document Check and Assessment sit outside it and cannot repeat, contradicting both Site B’s rules and Section 6. `play_out` is invoked on the POWL object with nonstandard parameters, while soundness failures are suppressed.

The causal graph has an unjustified `digital_documents -> originals_required` arrow and a weak `cycle_time -> appeal` arrow. “No-originals” is repeatedly equated with “digital” even though Site B reports only the 62% originals rule. Originals Receipt is an external delay, but the design assigns it to document reviewers and can consume their WIP. Population-median effects are also too large for treatment confined to 38% of cases: improving the faster no-originals mass need not move the overall 50th percentile by the stated 1–3 or 2–5 hours. Section 9’s population-median claim conflicts with Section 5, and the power target is asserted without a variance or quantile calculation.
