7.4

The fork-join arithmetic, case table, worked examples, and the A-service and B-queue counterfactuals are largely exact. The score is held down by a false final-station overlap claim, a wrong median, a reversed bound statement, a mistaken FIFO argument, and a unit error in the required code.

The case decomposition matches the log. Upstream, queues, services, `join_ready = max` branch end, critical branch, downstream wait, final service, and total flow time are right for all 12 cases, including F02, F04, F07, and F11. The no-double-counting identity is the right one: elapsed fork–join time is the max sojourn, every branch’s queue + service + sync slack equals that same span, and summing the three slacks counts overlapping calendar time. The F02 illustration (slacks 10+0+35 = 45 versus elapsed 20+60+15+20 = 115, and the inflated sum 235) is correct. Mean flow 115.25, mean fork–join 61, mean downstream 14.67, critical counts 2/8/2, and the 100/520/112 attribution split are correct. Ranking B first by last-completion frequency, sojourn, and tail, while treating A’s flat 50-minute service as a floor rather than as proof of influence, is the right distinction.

CF1 (A service 50→40) is right: only F01 (ΔJ = 5, B becomes last) and F11 (ΔJ = 10, A+B tie) move, total 15 minutes, and F11 is fully masked if F09 still occupies F1 until 11:50. CF2 is right case by case, including partial gains on F05 (10 of 12.5) and F10 (5 of 10), exact A-floor ties on F02 and F06, zero gain on F01, F04, F08, and F11, and total 92.5. The fixed-previous-completion floor of 70, with F05 and F09 masked and knock-on restoring 92.5, is also right. Assumptions A1–A5 are labeled, and half-minute cuts are flagged against a one-minute clock.

The serious error is CF3-pool. Immediate two-server FIFO by join-ready does not reproduce zero downstream wait. If each case starts at join-ready and runs 20 minutes, F09 (11:15–11:35), F10 (11:20–11:40), and F11 (11:30–11:50) overlap on [11:30, 11:35). F11 waits 5 minutes, so pooled savings are 171, not 176. “Join-ready gaps never put three cases in service at once” and “earlier F starts do not queue two pooled servers here” are false on this sample. Calling that construction “Hypothesis H1 plus” pooling also mixes dedicated odd/even assignment with a shared queue. The separate policy-only figure of 171, with F11 keeping 5 minutes because F09 finishes at 11:35, is correct, and “do not buy a third server for 5 minutes” can still be a reasonable judgment, but it is not what the pool paragraph claims.

Secondary faults:

- B service median is reported as 35. The ordered sample is 30, 30, 30, 35, 35, 35, 40, 40, 40, 45, 45, 45, so the median is 37.5. Other even-n medians in the same table (B queue 20, C sync 32.5) use the two-middle average, so this is an inconsistent miss, not a declared lower-median convention.
- “This is an upper bound if F can start earlier one-for-one” has the condition reversed. Pass-through ΔT = ΔJ is achieved when the final station advances one-for-one; masking makes realized savings smaller. The later A5 numbers show the intended idea, but the sentence does not.
- “F08 starting before F07 rejects a single FIFO combined queue” is the wrong test. F08 is join-ready at 10:50 and F07 at 10:55, so readiness-FIFO serves F08 first. What rejects one server is overlapping F1/F2 occupancy (for example F03 on 10:00–10:20 and F04 on 10:10–10:30). Start order itself follows join order.
- The code comment says `pm4py.get_service_time` should match 50, 37.5, 20.17, and 20 “(seconds).” Those figures are minutes; interval service time from timestamps is in seconds (3000, 2250, 1210, 1200). The comment would fail its own check. The unused floor assignment and unused `datetime` import are sloppy but do not change results.
- The required case fields are present in substance, under symbols (`U`, `Q_A`, `S_A`, …) rather than the requested names. Sync waits are defined, illustrated for four cases, and averaged, but not shown for every case.

Sections 5 and 7 are otherwise careful: B’s gain is flow time rather than demonstrated throughput, A becomes the fork–join floor after a B-queue cut, C’s slack is not extra elapsed time, and capacity, preemption, causality, and the queue-versus-release-policy reading of downstream wait are properly left unidentified. Those strengths do not remove the false pooled-server result.
