The case dispositions are the strongest part of the answer, and they are largely right: TX-301, TX-303, TX-304, TX-307, TX-309, and TX-310 are noncompliant; TX-302, TX-306, TX-308, and TX-311 are compliant; TX-305 is indeterminate. Threshold handling is also right, including EUR 100,000 as inclusive, a sub-20% increase as non-invalidating, a decrease as non-invalidating, the 118,000 to 145,000 increase as about 22.9%, EX-77 as timely, EX-88 as not proved at release, settlement amount as not a release payload, and TX-311’s `event_index` order. Sections 1 and 7 are substantively useful.

The formalization does not match those holdings. Section 1 defines every amount, risk, jurisdiction, or customer-type update as material, and the R1 pseudo-logic requires the enhanced review to fall strictly after that change. Under that definition, the post-review updates in TX-306 and TX-308 defeat R1. The findings instead treat R4 as preserving those reviews. That reconciliation is never written into the rule, so the central amount-change rule is inconsistent across the answer.

Section 4 then contradicts the TX-302 finding. The enhanced review already satisfies R1, so removing the qualified-signature evidence would not make the case noncompliant. Final-state leakage is also misstated: the TX-311 example describes control-flow blindness to attributes and use of the create-time jurisdiction, not copying the final case state backward.

The checker does not implement the written conclusions. Review rows do not carry `amount`, but R4 reads the amount from those rows, so a known release amount is treated as an unknown baseline. `R4_invalidates_review` never becomes true, and every such case is forced to `REVIEW_REQUIRED`. The code also compares only timestamps, embeds 13:00 instead of 16:00 for TX-311’s create event despite noting the error, and would reject TX-306 and TX-308 on the strict “any later update” reading. Several formal operators are missing or unreadable, including the jurisdiction/amount conditions and the “118145” comparison.
