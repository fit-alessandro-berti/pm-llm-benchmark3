6.5

The write-up covers the required sections and is directionally sound on calendars, latent objections, service-versus-wait separation, and paired replications. It is not a reliable simulation design: several core choices are inconsistent or unspecified, and the code misses required outputs.

The non-stationary demand is handled with a rate formula, seasonal factors, and Monday and architect bursts, but the run design then applies steady-state warm-up deletion (Welch or MSER, drop 180 days). In a seasonal calendar system the “transient” is the regime of interest, and a fixed deletion window shifts which peak is discarded depending on the start date. Finite-horizon runs anchored to a calendar date, with a shared initial backlog, are the appropriate setup; that correction is absent.

Service time is never defined as net working time or as elapsed time. Applying elapsed-time estimates on top of resource calendars double-counts nights, weekends, and holidays. Public-notice duration is partly tied to council and holiday calendars even though it is a statutory elapsed wait, while legal unavailability is a true resource constraint. The zero-truncated split “waiting = sojourn − expected service” systematically pushes negative residuals into zero and biases the wait distribution.

The process tree is treated as official routing. It is a simplification: appeal is not an activity, “Completeness rework” and “Inspection rework” may not be logged event names, and the AND-join before decision drafting is not specified as state. Queue discipline, preemption, and no-shows are also left out of the state model.

The experiment list names baseline, D1–D10, and four stresses, but stresses are separate rows rather than a policy-by-scenario matrix, so interactions are not actually estimated. D1 shortens post-submission response time; the intervention mainly moves effort before arrival. Effect sizes (50%, 20%, 30%, 25%) are stated as if they were known impacts. Decision thresholds (30/60/90/180 days, 10% p90, 95% utilization) are unanchored, the selection rule does not say whether the median is pooled or by case type, skipped-inspection risk and cost never enter the rule, and multiple comparisons across policies are ignored. Out-of-sample validation is a 12/6 time split that confounds season.

The skeleton uses pandas plus `format_dataframe`, `parse_process_tree`, variants, a DFG, and a performance DFG, and it keeps PM4Py off the event loop. It never builds service or waiting columns, and it has no experiment runner over policies, seeds, and stresses. Token replay is named for a process tree without a Petri-net conversion.
