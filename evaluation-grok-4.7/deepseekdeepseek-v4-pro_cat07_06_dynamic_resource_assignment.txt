6.5

The final write-up covers every required section and keeps most queue decisions inside the hard rules (skill floors, security and approval limits, the four-assignment cap, and no new work for high overtime-risk staff). The worker note and the audit list are usable. The policy is not internally reliable enough to score higher.

The scoring rules contradict their own intent. Continuity “penalties” are written as positive terms, and the resilience term adds score to the sole specialist when a backup exists, which concentrates bottlenecks instead of avoiding them. SLA urgency is identical for every candidate on a case, so it never changes who is chosen. With the stated weights, a skill-3 resource beats a skill-1 trainee by far more than the training bonus can offset, yet C003 and C009 are still given to R1_Amal. The published queue therefore does not follow the published algorithm; the later 20% repair step is not shown producing those choices either.

Two assignment judgments are weak. C001 is high-complexity with high handover cost and an owner at security skill 1; supervision by R7_Gita or R8_Hugo is feasible, so the continuity rule still applies, and a supervised pair is never scored. C003 is a repeated diagnosis loop at complexity 0.66 with two prior reassignments; using it as R1_Amal’s training case fights the rework evidence the design itself cites. C009 was the safer training case.

The comparison overclaims. “22% documented training (Amal, Jonas)” cannot be both: R10_Jonas’s documented priority is security, not approval, and 2/9 is Amal alone. It also says supervised pairs spread security capacity, but every security case is given to an unsupervised skill-3 resource, with two of the three landing on R8_Hugo. Hours remaining never enter eligibility or scoring, despite driving the first-available baseline and making R8_Hugo’s three new cases (5.5h) and R6_Farid’s two urgent cases look capacity-blind. Amal’s resulting load is stated as both 12 and 13 open cases.

The PM4Py sketch names the right audit targets, but similarity is routed through an old internal factory instead of `discover_activity_based_resource_similarity`, and `get_service_time` is absent. Batching is declared a hard rule and then never applied, even though C006 and C012 both qualify and share an assignee.
