6.4

The write-up meets the required seven-section structure, ranks all twelve candidates on process leverage rather than volume, and lands on a four-module set that respects the budget, includes a real control module, and withholds loan activation. The case against automating registration first is directionally sound, and most cited frequencies, waits, and control rates match the evidence.

The reasoning is not reliable enough to score higher. Speeding registration is treated as if it would raise arrivals into KYC, entity resolution, and credit analysis. With exogenous demand and only a 1.5-hour registration wait, steady-state throughput does not rise; at most a small queue would drain once. That error is repeated in the portfolio comparison, the effects, and the recommendation.

The recommended set is A4, A6, A5, and A9, but the effects section never analyzes that set. It analyzes A4, A6, A5, and A7, then the rollout drops A7. The 58-hour external valuation wait and the 44-hour signing wait, the two largest synchronization delays, therefore disappear without a trade-off. Weekly committee cadence is also ignored, so high-exposure cycle time still has an unstated floor.

Other material gaps: recorded-versus-actual concurrency of KYC and financial-document review is never used, despite the split after classification. A6 is described as effective only after A5, which overstates the dependency. A8 is said to depend on the A3 classifier rather than on KYC status. A6 is then credited with cutting the 110-minute credit-analysis effort and the 5.8% four-eyes violations, neither of which it directly controls. No recommended module actually enforces four-eyes at analysis and packaging. The dependency map assigns deep core-banking integration to A1, A9, and A11, while the rollout spends those two slots on A6 and A9. Gate language such as “waiting … 15%” and “remediation loops 20%” is ambiguous, and the latter can be read as worse than today’s 8.5%. A6’s known failure mode on poor PDFs is missing from the risk section even though A6 is central.
