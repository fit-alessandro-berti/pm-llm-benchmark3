7.2

The decision-set reconstructions are largely accurate, the required seven-part structure is met, and the main diagnoses (QA: myopic appointment override on FIFO; QB: FIFO plus inconsistent P1 preemption; QC: an index rule plus a C05 exception; QD: skill filters plus FIFO) are defensible. Durations, B01 residuals (31 then 14), C05 slack (−8 at 10:18, −22 at 10:32), and the `arrival+est` keys (11, 19, 29, 31, 36, 58) check out. Confidence is appropriately conservative, and competing rules are usually compared on the same eligible set.

Those merits do not make the write-up nearly flawless.

The opening bound is false: arrival gaps are not all ≥3 minutes. C02/C03 and C03/C04 are 2 minutes apart, and D01/D02 are 1 minute apart. The later “every contested FIFO pair” sentence repeats the overclaim; the listed examples happen to be safe, but C05 is not the only ≤2-minute boundary.

The synthesis claim that priority 2 never beats priority 3 is false as written. C03 (P2) is chosen over waiting C01 and C04; B03 (P2) is chosen over B01 and B04 at 09:48; B06 (P2) is chosen over waiting B07 at 10:36. The intended point—that a later P2 does not jump an earlier P3—is narrower than the sentence, and it is used in the “most dangerous inference” conclusion.

Test 1 is internally inconsistent. H2 cannot both be a locked initial batch that would run C01 before C06 and a 100-minute starvation cap whose whole point is that C06 runs at 11:34 while C01 has waited only 94 minutes. The counterfactual (“C01 runs at 10:54”) silently switches back to batch lock. H1 is also mislabeled: `est − α·wait` with constant α is a static index (`est + α·arrival`). With α≈1 it never reorders over time, so it is not a starvation safeguard that “activates” at 11:40. The fitted range is about (0.89, 1.2), not a uniquely identified α=1, and the displayed chain omits C05, whose score of 51 breaks the full observed order.

QD at 11:21 understates a known constraint. D05 is 30 minutes and would finish at 11:51, before the 12:00 meeting; after D03 it no longer fits. That consequence discriminates lookahead bottleneck routing from FIFO/SPT and is not in the 11:21 row. The 09:46–09:48 gap is grouped with preemption even though B05 does not take the server. Test 2 places D-F “in its 12:00–12:20 meeting” at an 11:48 decision. The C05 rule is described as firing once `t` passes 10:50, but C04 is not preempted; the override appears only at the 10:54 completion epoch.

Assumptions (static index, D-X eligibility for pure mechanical work, minute-exact timestamps rather than ±1 minute plus scan lag) are often used without being marked as assumptions. The core queue stories survive these defects; the universal claims and the QC discrimination test do not.
