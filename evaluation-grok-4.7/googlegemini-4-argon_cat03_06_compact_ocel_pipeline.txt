7.5

The pipeline is complete and largely faithful: all 17 events and 12 objects are present, E2O rows carry matching activities and timestamps, O2O covers the required business links, validations match the checklist, and the Order, Package, Delivery, and Invoice trace shapes and DFG edge frequencies are right. The source-versus-propagated distinction, payment-flattening weakness, and most failure modes are also sound. It is not nearly flawless.

The clearest factual error is in Policy 3. Under the propagation rules stated there, O100 has 14 events and the union is all 17, so O200 has 10 events and the two traces share 7. The claim that O200 has 11 does not reconcile with those rules or with 14 + 11 − 17. Section 8 announces eight assertions and lists seven.

The Pack Package self-loop is misexplained. On the Order flattening it is O100’s true E7→E8 sequence (P1 then P2), which is divergence. Copying E7 into O200’s trace is a separate convergence effect and does not create that loop. Section 5 then drops edges that Section 4 correctly counted: Add Item→Add Item and Pack Package→Pack Package. It also never says that I2 skips Reserve Stock, so the Item OC-DFG is not the single path implied for I1 and I3.

Policy 2 calls the baseline “full” operational involvement and treats the E8–O100 link as explicitly stated. The narrative explicitly shares only E6 and E7 across orders; E8→O100 is an inference. That fights Section 1’s “direct source relations plus explicit sharing” wording. The OTG fallback only aggregates O2O type pairs, while its comment and Section 5 also claim E2O co-occurrence bridges. `partially_settles` / `completes_settlement` are labeled N:1 from a single row each, and “V1 totals 250” is an inference from `completes_settlement`, not a stated amount.

Referential integrity in the four tables is good, qualifiers are used consistently, and the constructor is isolated. Those strengths keep the answer well above a merely partial solution, but the counting error, the convergence/divergence mix-up, and the internal policy contradictions are material under a strict reading.
