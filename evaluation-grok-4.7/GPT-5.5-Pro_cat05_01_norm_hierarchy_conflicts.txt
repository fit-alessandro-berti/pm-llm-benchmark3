6.9

The memo is complete, temporally careful, and often precise, but it is not nearly flawless. Its own decision method is broken in the two highest-value streamlined cases, and several rule statements are looser than the holdings that depend on them.

The controlling inconsistency is in the burden of proof. Section 1 says an invoked exception must be proved on scope, time, authority, and independence, and that an unproved exception falls back to the ordinary rule, with genuine ambiguity marked as uncertainty. P-508 is still labeled COMPLIANT. EUR 155,000 sits above D1’s “up to EUR 150,000” ceiling; D2’s “as well” does not clearly preserve Finance substitution; there is no Finance Controller or independent budget-owner approval; and the missing-evidence cell asks for the very confirmation the burden rule required before compliance could be found. Under that stated method the primary label is NONCOMPLIANT, or at best INDETERMINATE. Calling the stricter reading merely “credible” in Section 5 does not repair a primary holding that the author’s own rule forbids. P-507 has the same budget-owner hole below the ceiling. Medium-high confidence and a “low residual” severity label understate a missing GPIP-4 A2 prong.

That error rests on an authority expansion. The CFO is described as owning Finance controls only. The memo treats the uncontrolled email as also replacing Plant_Approve and the independent budget owner. The materials say Incident Command’s charter does not call its members budget owners, and D4 keeps “essential integrity controls.” The hierarchy notes that this substitution is contestable, then the case table applies it as if it were established. ECD-2, which is silent on Finance, is also blurred with the CFO email in the A2 row (“ECD/CFO may substitute”).

P-501 is classified JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION while the justification cell says B4 does not clearly waive Plant/Finance timing or authorize an undelegated Shift Lead when the Plant Manager was reachable. That is a failed exception, which this framework should call NONCOMPLIANT. The Section 5 alternative moves only toward COMPLIANT and does not test that stricter premise. The defined JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION category (“not clean enough”) becomes a residual bucket rather than a successful exception.

The pseudo-logic does not implement the catalogue. The Finance `unless` clause omits the 15 July 07:20 effective time and the EUR 150,000 cap, so it would excuse P-502’s Finance timing and P-508’s missing Finance approval. R10 states both limits. R6’s “preferably before commitment” softens B2 without a textual basis and then helps P-501 look satisfied.

Smaller defects: P-502’s date is not in the case row, yet “the CFO email had not yet been sent” is stated as fact (A3 still saves the NONCOMPLIANT result). The prior practice of treating signed incident directives as temporary overlays, expressly withheld from new-supplier Compliance cases, is unused. P-503’s alternative does not actually change the classification once A3 is held. Satisfied-rule cells include conditionals (“if PROC-3 counts”) and non-triggers. The GPIP row answers “what cannot override GPIP” in a column headed “what it cannot override.” Severity mixes breach gravity with residual risk and has no stated scale.

What holds the score up is real analytical quality. The hierarchy is issue-specific. GPIP-4 is not allowed to erase ECD-2 on PO timing and the approved-supplier register, and ECD-2/CFO wording is not allowed to erase A1 or A3. Time-of-act analysis is right on P-501 before 09:00, P-506 after expiry, and P-505 at the boundary. P-505 correctly prefers the 17:59:31 acknowledgment and the fast ERP clock over 18:06. P-507 correctly treats the 12:40 PDF as a timely record and 14:30 as a logging artifact. Later Compliance or Plant/Finance approval is not treated as a cure. P-502, P-503, P-504, and P-506 are robust NONCOMPLIANT results. New-supplier A3 is kept distinct from the local register rule. Section 7 covers authority, scope, thresholds, segregation, new suppliers, retrospective proof, expiry, and an anti-normalization safeguard.

Those strengths are the core of the assignment. The P-507/P-508 holdings contradict the announced burden and over-read the CFO’s authority, so the memo cannot score as nearly flawless.
