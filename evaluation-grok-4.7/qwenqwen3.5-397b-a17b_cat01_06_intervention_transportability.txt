5.5

The directional judgment is sound: conditional go, no blanket copy, and no legally impossible document-check start. Completeness of the ten sections does not make the analysis reliable. The load-bearing quantitative, causal, and model-construction work is wrong or unsupported.

The transport graph does not identify a coherent effect. `originals_required` points at `document_capacity` rather than blocking document start; `API_latency` and `queue_huddles` point at `identity_capacity`, which has no outgoing path, so the Site A confounders never reach cycle time. Complexity is wired as if it created legal capacity. The prose then claims those confounders explain why the cycle-time result may not replicate, which the graph itself cannot express.

The nine-hour Site A shift is not fully treated as unidentified. Pre-assessment wait is given a transferable “parallel start” component and a non-transferable component of “None,” despite the mix shift, two added reviewers, API change, and huddles. Overlap transport confidence of 70 ignores the case lock and the timestamp artifact.

The scenario ranges are not derived from the evidence. Identity service is about 3.4h and originals delay about 18h, so parallelizing identity with document check cannot produce a 5–10h median gain, and it cannot move the critical path on the 62% originals cases. The 38% eligibility figure is also used for a “digital and no legal review” population without intersecting the 40% legal-review share. Scenario 2’s realistic −3h median contradicts the recommendation’s −5 to −10h. The DFG counts and the 4/7/3/2 staffing pools are never used. “The intervention worked” overclaims the confounded before/after result.

The pilot names the right fidelity contrast, but case-level randomization shares the same specialist pools, so interference remains. A 90-day appeal stop rule cannot fire inside an 8-week pilot, and “200 cases” is not a power calculation. Assumption 2 is already inconsistent with 345 observed supplement returns.

The PM4Py block does not build a strict partial order, does not set order edges into Assessment, and calls a fictional `powl_op.Sequence/Parallel/Loop` API. The supplement is one optional pass, not a loop; Originals Receipt is absent; and the exception path abandons POWL for a process tree. That misses the required model-fidelity test even though the final recommendation remains the right kind of decision.
