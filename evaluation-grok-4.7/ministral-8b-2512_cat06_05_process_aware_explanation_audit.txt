The audit correctly flags several post-closure and full-trace fields (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_handler_team`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`, the `prior_claim_count_before_t0` predicate, and `same_day_document_count_snapshot`) and it keeps elapsed time, initial complexity, queue length, and region in view. That is not enough. The revised explanations restate future case content as if it were known at `t0`, and the sequence audit reverses the distinction the task depends on.

Section 1 is incomplete and mis-typed. `initial_damage_complexity` is absent. Local-only `final_decision`, `claimant_channel`, `initial_reserve`, `risk_score_current`, and `last_resource_role` are absent. `employee_experience` is dismissed as irrelevant even though the as-of join is extraction date. `total_case_duration_hours` is described only as late measurement; it is essentially the 10-day label. `Claim Closed -> Damage Assessment Completed` is treated as a timestamp tie. Under the stated process that order is impossible, and a tied timestamp with no sequence index makes the recorded order unusable. `Appeal -> Settlement Decision Recorded` is labeled a low-severity legitimate prefix signal. A later appeal followed by a second decision can be a legal full trace, and it is still unavailable immediately after coverage check. `Documents Requested -> Documents Received`, `Coverage Check Completed -> Fraud Investigation Opened`, and `Damage Assessment Completed -> Settlement Decision Recorded` are future-feasible transitions, not prefix observations; the SHAP sign is used as the finding type. Queue length is over-penalized for a stated ±5-minute snapshot tolerance. Remediation often invents `*_at_t0` copies of activities that the prefix cannot contain. At this `t0` the observed prefix is only registration and coverage check, so document, damage, settlement, appeal, and closure counts are structurally still ahead.

Section 2 names the requested check families and then applies them incorrectly. The SQL called an alignment check does not bind lineage to a case or a feature. Damage assessment before any document request is flagged as illegal, but the fast track allows that path. The appeal query flags a decision after an appeal when no second appeal sits between them, which is the permitted reconsideration path. “Footprint” is redefined as a ban on cross-claim inputs, which would throw out queue length and a repaired prior-claim count. Enabled, impossible, and merely future-possible are not separated. Dropping every same-timestamp pair would also drop legitimate parallelism. PyWorkflows is cited as if it were an AWS process-mining library.

Section 3 does not state separate standards for predictive validity, causal wording, operational actionability, and fairness review. Future activities are placed in the process-state class. `final_handler_team`, the mutable risk score, the final role, and `final_decision` are left out of the unusable class. The closing tradeoff says to replace region with a process-irrelevant substitute.

Section 4 is the decisive failure. Both X and Y are marked `retain` although the published probabilities are dominated by full-trace inputs and must be recomputed. For X, `appeal_seen_anywhere = 1` is rewritten as a prior partial decision and an appeal already triggered. The prefix contains neither, and `final_decision = Partial` never appears among the removed drivers. For Y, the variant is shortened to `REG-COV-DOC-DOC-DMG` and the narrative still explains risk by a multi-document path. That path is after `t0`. The low queue (`4`, negative contribution) is dropped. The broken prior-claim count is kept as a credible positive driver and glossed as “experience.” `initial_reserve` is treated as known without a lineage. Case Z’s note correctly records the tie, the mutable score, and the final role, then still omits the contaminated same-day document snapshot and the week-52 policy/holiday confound. `invalidate` is a reasonable status for that explanation.

Section 5 lists every requested topic and then sets the wrong success condition: hold the AUC loss under 5% after removing leakage. That rewards substitutes for the leaked label. Thresholds of 10% SHAP change and 15% event-distribution change are arbitrary. Explanation stability is framed as later drift of retained features rather than invariance to unseen future events. There is no stop on serving the current ranking or these local explanations.
