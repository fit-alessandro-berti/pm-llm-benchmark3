The write-up keeps the seven required headings and a few correct labels (`join_ready` as the latest branch end, a named critical branch, the three counterfactual titles, and a short note that a final wait may be a release policy). Almost every quantitative claim, the influence ranking, and the code are wrong, internally inconsistent, or missing.

**Definitions.** There are no formulas in case index \(c\) and branch index \(i\). Upstream is later written as arrival minus fork release. Branch queue is written as \(\max(0,\text{fork}-\text{start})\), which reverses the interval. Downstream queue is defined as waiting after `final_start`; that interval is final service. Synchronization wait is named and then never given as \(\text{join\_ready}-\text{end}_i\). Elapsed fork-join time, \(\max_i(\text{queue}_i+\text{service}_i)\), is absent. The double-count argument does not show that each branch’s queue + service + sync equals the same stage length, so summing sync waits adds overlapping slack. The worked flow-time sums then do that double count by adding every branch service into calendar time.

**Case table and worked examples.** The table is not computed from the log. Upstream is a fabricated 10, 15, 20, … sequence; the log is 15 minutes for F01 and 20 minutes for every other case. A always starts at fork release and lasts 50 minutes with queue 0; the table uses 60 or 75. Every final service is 20 minutes; the table uses 30–72. Critical branch in the table is right for only 5 of 12 cases. Truth is A on F01 and F11, C on F04 and F08, and B on the other eight. Worked examples contradict the table and their own maxima: F02’s stated max is 09:25 (B) while the text calls C critical; F02 final service is written as 10:00 − 10:00 = 40; F04’s sum includes the total 132 inside the sum that is claimed to equal 132; F07’s narrative says B and the table says C; F11’s narrative says downstream queue 0 and the table says 20. Clock times are mixed with durations. `11:72` later appears as a timestamp.

**Critical-path section.** Service means, queue means, and frequencies are wrong, and mean synchronization wait is missing. The frequency lists are not a partition: F02 and F04 are assigned to both B and C, three cases are omitted, and 3+3+3 does not equal 12. A is given mean queue 25 although A never queues. C is ranked first on a three-way frequency tie, which the instructions said to report as a tie. The controlling branch is B (8/12), including near-misses such as F10, where B finishes only five minutes after A. No tail contribution of queue-plus-service to `join_ready` is computed.

**Counterfactuals and redesigns.** Savings are stated as a flat 20% or 50% of an incorrect mean, applied to the wrong critical cases. Margins are ignored: on F01, A leads B by only five minutes, so a 10-minute service cut cannot all become flow-time reduction. There is no per-case check of whether an earlier join is absorbed by `final_start`. F1/F2 are never used. Several final waits occur while the assigned resource is already idle (F01, F02, F03, F07, F12); others match back-to-back occupancy (F05, F08, F09, F11). “Average downstream queue of 45 minutes” and “every case benefits from more final capacity” are both false. Redesign 1 speeds C and claims a flow-time cut in every case, which is the non-critical-branch fallacy the task forbids. Throughput, flow-time tail, utilization, the next bottleneck (A’s flat 50 minutes once B’s queue falls), synchronization slack, and a pilot metric are not analyzed.

**Python.** The embedded table corrupts B and C timestamps and omits `final_resource`. `branch` is undefined, so the apply crashes. Tie-breaking, all three counterfactuals, a PM4Py interval log, `format_dataframe`, and any of `get_service_time`, `discover_performance_dfg`, or `convert_log_to_time_intervals` are absent, as is the required comment that directly-follows performance does not recover synchronization wait.

**Limits.** Causality, branch capacity, preemption, and queue versus release policy are named in three sentences. Sample size, minute censoring, the idle-versus-blocked final station, and why A has zero queue are not treated as open identification problems. No assumption is labeled, and the rest of the document states false figures as facts.
