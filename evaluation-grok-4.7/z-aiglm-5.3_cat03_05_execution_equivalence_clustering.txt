The clustering is internally consistent and the borderline analysis is strong: X1≡X2 under renaming, X5/X6 separated by externality rather than labels, X7 separated by the Dispatch–item incidence plus qualifiers, and X8 by the return/repack motif. Preserved vs discarded information, RST, and the robustness perturbations are mostly right.

Points come off for reproducibility and the PM4Py section. `qualifier_names` is never defined, and O2O qualifiers are not in the event structure the signature consumes, so the preferred key is not fully reproducible. `canonical()` drops cross-event identity of `EXT` objects while being labeled a full E2 incidence key. `max_objs` is described as truncating keys and causing collisions; in PM4Py it caps how many objects are clustered. The key itself is treated as an event-level (activity, raw ids) fragment key rather than an object-lifecycle key plus related object identifiers, which overstates “literal E2O incidence.” E2p is also defined with timestamp cuts, then later used as if fulfillment order were purely partial. The preferred partition is nearly discrete, though the rollups mitigate that.
