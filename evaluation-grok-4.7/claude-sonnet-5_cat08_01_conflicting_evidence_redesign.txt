The diagnosis misreads the conformance evidence. Fitness 0.91 with 68% fully fitting traces means many traces deviate, often slightly; the prompt separately attributes low precision to low-frequency disallowed combinations. The answer merges those into one claim that about a third of cases are structurally different low-frequency variants, and that hidden risk sits there rather than in the frequent path. That conflicts with its own thesis and with the alignment findings, which locate frequent log moves on fast approval before evidence completeness and dispatch without risk review. “2,300 of those” also treats all dispatch edges as coming from the 2,550 auto-screen fast approvals, although 240 fast approvals arrive from fraud/risk review. The 0.4–1.8h “per hop” range drops the dispatch-to-close median of 3.2h. Routing 400 auto-screened cases to fraud/risk review is treated as proof of misclassification; that edge is also what a working screen would do.
