The construction is complete and internally referential: 17 unique events, all 12 objects, timezone-aware timestamps, payment amounts on events, required O2O links, and flattened Order/Package/Delivery/Invoice traces and DFG counts that match the chosen E2O table. Validation of IDs, orphans, timestamp/activity agreement, and duplicate relations is real.

Those strengths are undercut by concrete errors. The mandated frame names `events`, `objects`, `relations`, and `o2o` are replaced by `*_df` names. Section 1 says Confirm Delivery is linked to both Delivery and Package; `e13`/`e14` link only the delivery. Both remittances are specified as partial, yet M2 is encoded as `completes_settlement` and later treated as closing an invented invoice total of 250. `Reserve Stock` touches two object types and is filed under tri-type activities. `packs` is labeled N:M although each item sits in one package. The object-centric net is given one token game, including payment tokens being consumed at birth and a claim of no deadlocks, with no non-uniqueness caveat. The interactions summary is described as a per-event directed clique of 20 pairs, which over-specifies that aggregate. Linking `e8` to O100 also crosses the source-versus-propagated boundary the answer itself draws for delivery confirmation.
