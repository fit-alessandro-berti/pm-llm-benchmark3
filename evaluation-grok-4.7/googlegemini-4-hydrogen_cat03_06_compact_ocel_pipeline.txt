The pipeline is complete, internally consistent, and faithful to the 17 events, the stated object set, and an explicit “name what the text names” E2O policy. Flattened traces and DFG edge counts for Order, Package, Delivery, and Invoice match that policy, including the non-obvious fact that I2 skips Reserve Stock. Referential checks, timestamp copying, payment attributes, and the refusal to treat the object-centric Petri net as unique are all sound.

Deductions are for concrete defects, not missing sections. `carried_in` is attached to Delivery while the gloss says the delivery executes the dispatch, so the qualifier reads backwards. `completes_settlement` on M2 treats 100+150 as full settlement of V1 even though no invoice total is given and both payments are described as partial. The OTG write-up states an Order–Package edge via E07 as fact; that edge exists only under event co-occurrence, not in `o2o`, and the algorithm’s edge source is never qualified. `pm4py.view_oc_petri_net` is not the usual public call (`view_ocpn`). “Divergence” is also stretched to sequential Add Item and Record Payment events that are not single-event multi-object splits, and `packs` / `bills` are reused with different meanings in E2O and O2O.
