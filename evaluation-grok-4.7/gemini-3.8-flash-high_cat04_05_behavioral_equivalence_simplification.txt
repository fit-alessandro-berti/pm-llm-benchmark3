The equivalence analysis is largely right: visible-trace, P1, P2, and P3 verdicts match the loop semantics, the two-sided counterexamples are valid, and the P3 cardinalities 76 / 85 / intersection 4 are correct. The answer is not reliable on the quantitative claims it presents as derived.

The P1 definition adds an approval-after-check rule the stated compliance view does not make. “Must execute concurrently” overstates the parallel operator, and calling the simplified net deterministic is false: the XOR and the loop exit are conflicts. Tree size, depth, and leaf counts are correct, and `Analyze` before `Check` is a documented refinement rather than trace preservation of Model A.

Petri-net sizes are wrong. Under the standard process-tree translation both originals are the same net size, \(|P|=|T|=10\), \(|F|=22\), because they have the same operator multiset; the claimed 9/9/20 versus 7/7/16 and the higher simplicity of A are unsupported. The simplified net is \(|P|=7\), \(|T|=8\), \(|F|=16\) (the silent loop exit is missing from 6/7/14), so \(13/15\) and the percentages are false even though the net is still smaller. Section 6 asserts \(\mathrm{EMD}\approx 0.1457\) and \(p<0.05\) for two- and three-revision behavior without a derivation. Under the stated fair loop choice those classes have mass \(1/4\) and \(1/8\). Equal footprints or a small EMD would still not give trace equivalence; that caveat is right, the number is not. Bounded inequality is correctly distinguished from an unbounded proof.
