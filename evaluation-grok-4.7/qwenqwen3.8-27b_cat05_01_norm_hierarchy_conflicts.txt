7.6

The assessment is coherent and usable, but it is not nearly flawless. Primary labels, timing, and the GPIP-4 / ECD-2 split are mostly stable; several internal mismatches and evidential overclaims keep it well below a top score.

The hierarchy is explicit and issue-specific: GPIP-4 integrity controls stay in force unless the Chief Risk Officer issues an A7 exception; ECD-2 can relax ordinary timing and supplier-register rules only inside its window and scope; the CFO email is interpretive and cannot erase segregation of duties, Compliance clearance, or the independent budget-owner limb. Facts, assumptions, and recommendations are separated. Time-of-act analysis is generally careful: P-501 is correctly kept before 09:00, P-506 is correctly kept after expiry, P-505 prefers the 17:59:31 supplier acknowledgment over the 18:06 ERP stamp, and P-507 prefers the 12:40 PDF over the 14:30 ERP entry. Post-hoc approvals are not treated as curative. Thresholds (€25k / €50k / €100k / €150k), new-supplier Compliance, and PROC-3’s combined requester–committer–authorizer role are applied in the right direction. Section 5 actually changes classifications by naming the premise that would have to move, and sections 6–7 separate policy breach, logging defect, late proof of a timely act, and failure of proof, with a real anti-normalization safeguard.

The main logical defect is that the hierarchy and the case table do not use the same rule for local operational approvals. Section 1 and the D1 row allow the CFO email to cover operational approvals that are not GPIP-4 integrity controls. Plant_Approve is an Aachen SOP approval, not an A2/A3/A1 control. For in-window purchases at or under €150,000, P-505 and P-507 still list missing Plant_Approve as an unwaived deviation, while the same P-505 row says D1 may cover operational approvals. That is a direct inconsistency. It does not flip those primaries, because missing pre-commitment budget-owner and Finance Controller approval still fails A2, but it inflates the deviation set.

A second gap is the CFO’s finance-interpretation authority. D3 withholds waiver of Compliance and segregation of duties while expressly leaving finance interpretation with the CFO. The case rows usually bundle “Finance/budget” as one unwaivable GPIP-4 block. The budget-owner half is not established by Incident Command; the Finance Controller half is the part D3 puts in question. Collapsing them makes P-505, P-507, and P-508 look cleaner than the sources are.

B2 is also upgraded without enough disclosure. The SOP requires Plant_Approve and Finance_Approve at €25,000; it does not say those acts must precede commitment. GPIP-4 A2 and A6 supply that timing only at €50,000 and only for approvals that were already pre-commitment requirements. The rule catalogue quietly makes B2 pre-commitment, and that assumption does real work on P-501. The unauthorized SHIFT-1 commitment still supports NONCOMPLIANT, because the Plant Manager was reachable and had not delegated, so the classification survives, but the stated confidence is higher than the text supports.

C3 is treated as fully satisfied wherever an authorization time appears. The four-hour recording limb is independently shown for P-502 and P-507. For P-503, P-505, and P-508, a single authorization time is counted as both the prior authorization and the required record. P-501’s outage uncertainty is also slightly overstated in the wrong direction: recovery stamps run later than the real act, so a recorded 08:50 commitment is even more clearly before 09:00, not less.

All eight primaries are NONCOMPLIANT. That follows this hierarchy, and satisfied rules are distinguished from failed ones, so it is not a crude blanket. It still underuses INDETERMINATE where the open question is institutional authority rather than a missing document, especially on whether D1 can speak to the Finance Controller limb during the ECD-2 window.

The required structure, scope cuts, and evidential categories are strong. The Plant/D1 contradiction, the unsplit A2 limbs, and the pre-commitment reading of B2 are enough to hold this at 7.6.
