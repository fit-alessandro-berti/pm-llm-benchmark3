5.5

The required nine-part structure is present, Pipeline A is the right primary path, the process-tree attempt is correctly isolated in `try/except`, and no fake direct POWL-to-BPMN API is invented. The load-bearing semantics and the validation that is supposed to police them are not reliable.

The source order is misstated. `optional_legal` is ordered only after Assess Risk. It is unordered with Prepare Evidence and with the verification loop, so Legal Review can precede Prepare, follow the whole trial, or sit between Configure Trial and Inspect Trial. INV-05 instead says Legal and the loop both occur after Assess and Prepare. That drops a real concurrency and invents a synchronization the POWL does not have. INV-04’s negative witness is then muddled: a trace that fires Configure before Prepare is invalid, but the cell treats that pattern as conditionally valid. INV-10 mixes two different claims, unique transition labels and at-most-once occurrence. The second is false for Configure Trial, Inspect Trial, and Correct Defect.

Section 5 does not test the concurrency Section 1 itself calls most fragile. Every positive witness that contains Legal Review places it before Configure Trial. A converter that forces the legal branch to finish before the loop accepts the whole suite. The prose witness with Legal after Inspect is never encoded. `accepts` also cannot run: `pm4py.objects.petri_net.utils.petri_utils.fire_transition` is not a PM4Py operation; firing is `pm4py.objects.petri_net.semantics.execute`. `convert_to_reachability_graph` returns a graph, so `rg[0]` / `rg[1]` as “states” and “edges” is an unsupported unpacking. Freezing markings by `p.name` collapses places whose names are missing or duplicated and can prune distinct states.

The bound claim is arithmetically false. A completed two-iteration trace has at least ten visible labels (Register, Assess, Prepare, Configure, Inspect, Correct Defect, Configure, Inspect, Release, Archive), and eleven with Legal. `max_visible_len=9` does not cover two iterations. The length and Correct Defect checks are also off by one, and stopping at `max_states` means the search is not exhaustive inside the length bound. The exhaustiveness sentence asserts that it is.

The audit table reports `validated preserved` and “checked via bounded exploration; exact FM equality holds” for conversions that were not run, while neighboring cells say the same step is still only `ambiguous` or “not validated until witness tested.” Petri→Tree is entered as `conversion failed` as an accomplished result. That is the failure mode the task is about: a conversion object, or an unexecuted checker, is treated as behavioral evidence. The same split appears for the release join, which is marked validated in one row and ambiguous in another, and for final markings, marked both validated and approximated.

Section 6 then describes “the correct” BPMN as a second parallel fork that starts Optional Legal and the verification loop together. That drawing is the block-structured over-synchronization: it makes Legal wait for Prepare, or, if the fork is placed earlier, lets the loop start before Prepare. The asymmetric join in the source is exactly the N-shaped partial order that a single fork/join pair cannot represent. Layout warnings, tau hiding, do-versus-redo, rejection criteria, and residual uncertainty are otherwise substantive, but they sit on an invariant set and a witness program that would accept a loss of that concurrency.
