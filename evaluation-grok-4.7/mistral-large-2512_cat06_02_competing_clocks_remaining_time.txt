The write-up keeps the required six-part shape and does not collapse the case into one summed mean, but the forecast is not usable. Clock times contradict each other across sections, the hour figures do not match those clocks, and the critical-path arithmetic is wrong.

The hour column in §4 is disconnected from the clock column. Friday 15:30 to Monday 10:00 is 66.5 hours, not 18.5. Monday 18:00 is about 74.5 hours, not 40. Tuesday 12:00 is about 92.5 hours, not 60. Wednesday 10:00 is about 114.5 hours, not 70–90. The following Monday 10:00 is about 234 hours, not 168. No single convention (wall-clock, weekend removed, laboratory hours) reproduces the pairs.

Those clocks also disagree with the decomposition. §3’s smooth path joins at Monday 10:30 and releases Monday 16:15–20:45, while §2’s earliest terminal time is Monday 10:00 and §4 repeats that as the earliest completion. A terminal time cannot precede the join plus QA plus release. The chemistry-rerun central case is Wednesday in §2 and Tuesday afternoon in §3. The delayed-microbiology central case is Tuesday 10:00–14:00 in §2 and Tuesday 16:00–Wednesday morning, before release, in §3.

Microbiology timing is internally impossible. §3 correctly places technical readiness at Sunday 12:00–20:00 (Friday 14:00 plus 46–54 h), then schedules the on-call readout for Saturday 13:00–15:00. Primary incubation cannot be read on Saturday. Because QA is closed Sunday and chemistry is closed all weekend, a Sunday on-call readout usually does not move the join; it mostly changes a Monday 08:30–09:30 readout. Ranking weekend staffing as the main driver, and pricing a denied on-call at +16–24 h, follows from that Saturday error.

Chemistry is mis-queued. Four samples are ahead, and two analysts are still occupied; only about 2–3 analyst-hours remain before 18:00, against a minimum new service of 1.5 h. B-417 finishing, or even starting with a chance to finish, on Friday is a thin risk-priority case, and only if “high” outranks the urgent sample already waiting. The stated FIFO wait of “~3 h” does not equal 1.2–2.0 + 0.4–0.9 + 2.5. Adding the two analysts’ residual work treats parallel servers as one server and still accounts for one queued sample, not four. Monday 08:00 + 2.5 h = 10:30 restarts a full service after also claiming a Friday start, and it leaves the rest of the queue unexplained. The ledger’s claim that discipline changes the wait by only 0.5–2 h is far too small.

QA waiting is not a queue. “~6 arrivals” is neither 0.35 times the ~67 elapsed hours nor 0.35 times the roughly 11 opening hours before Monday 10:30. The five batches already waiting are added to future arrivals, while Friday 15:30–20:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00 capacity is not subtracted. Median service of 1.5 h is then used as the fast end, even though half of reviews are shorter than the median.

Path probabilities are asserted rather than tied to the given rates. A clear path (no rerun, no contamination, no query, no investigation) is closer to one half before calendar splits; 30–40% smooth, 25–35% “delayed readout,” and 20–30% QA rework are neither a partition nor a quantified overlap. Conditional rerun ~30% and contamination ~20% given a confirmed excursion are invented. Confirmation is then treated as moving the whole forecast by +1–3 days, rather than reweighting rerun, contamination, and investigation probabilities. Rejection at 5–10% has no source in the scenario, and 70–80% release leaves an unexplained remainder. The 60/40 split of investigation types sits on the heavy edge of the stated 60–80% documentation-only band.

§6 names a real high-leverage uncertainty, but the update sizes are not conditional-probability shifts. The extra “Key Notes” block also breaks the six-section constraint. Parallelism is named, including in the notes, yet the smooth path calls microbiology dominant while its own join is set by chemistry.
