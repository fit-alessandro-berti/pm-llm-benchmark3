6.3

The case judgments are differentiated and mostly defensible, but the memo contradicts itself, overstates unresolved facts, and the required checker would mis-flag the very cases the prose treats as meeting ED-1.

The control split is real and the case table does not collapse to “same user = fail, different users = pass.” AP-202 as `OBJECTIVE_FAILED`, AP-205 as `LOSS_PREVENTED_BEFORE_COMPLETION`, AP-206 as `INDETERMINATE`, AP-204 as failed despite distinct users, and AP-208 as failed despite a legitimate payment are the right shape of answer. Section 4’s hybrid reading of post-release, pre-settlement manager action is coherent, and section 6 treats the requested monitors as alerts.

Those strengths are undercut by contradictions and factual overclaims. Section 2 calls AP-207 only partially equivalent and AP-206 merely mitigating; section 3 calls them `OBJECTIVE_MET` and `INDETERMINATE`. AP-201’s own rationale says the objective was “substantively met” while the label is `OBJECTIVE_PARTIALLY_MET`. ED-1 is described as suspending the four-eyes objective; the rule states that the objective does not disappear. O6 marks RA-7 as unable to address collusion even though the same memo credits RA-7 with finding AP-204’s fabricated receipt, and O3 is overstated as a structural blind spot of the whole package when a substantive MG-4 review is exactly the receipt check. “Borrowed” credential, “no genuine organizational independence,” and AP-208’s “freshest” bank change and “unverified” legitimacy are stronger than the record: credential sharing is unresolved, the u12/u13 cell may be a designed pair, AP-202’s bank change was two hours old, and AP-208’s payment is given as legitimate while bank-ownership evidence at release is what is missing. AP-209’s corrective capacity is marked N/A despite an audit inside the recall window, which is inconsistent with the unused-but-available treatment of AP-203.

The code follows the requested skeleton and labels itself as evidence, but its ED-1 flag is not usable. `None` bank-change values become NaN, and `is None` then fails, so every no-change case, including AP-203 and AP-207, is ineligible. Eligibility also ignores token-before-prepare timing and treats a missing `Bank_Settlement` event as failure, so AP-203 fails again and AP-206’s central defect is not tested. “One business day” is hardcoded as 24 hours. `filter_four_eyes_principle` is called and its result is never emitted. Vendor ages such as 30 and 1000 days are invented, and `manager_independent=True` is assigned where no manager exists or independence is unknown.
