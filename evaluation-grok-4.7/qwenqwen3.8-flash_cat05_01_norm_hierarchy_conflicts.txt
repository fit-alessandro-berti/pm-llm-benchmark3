7.5

The assessment is structured, case-complete, and generally disciplined: rules are applied at the time of the act, ERP timestamps are not treated as conclusive, later approval is not treated as a cure, and the classifications are differentiated rather than uniformly compliant or noncompliant. The hierarchy is issue-specific, the alternatives actually change results by changing a premise, and the evidence/remediation split is usable.

It is not nearly flawless. Under the answer’s own burden of proof and its conclusion that Incident Command is not shown to be an independent budget owner, P-507 should be NONCOMPLIANT, not INDETERMINATE; leaving it indeterminate hedges a legal conclusion the hierarchy had already narrowed. The CFO email’s effect on the Finance Controller element is also applied unevenly: it is a live defeater for P-507 but is simply counted as a missed pre-commitment Finance approval in P-502. P-508 marks C3 satisfied although the file shows authorization, not the required recording within four hours, while P-505 correctly treats that same gap as unresolved. P-504 is held NONCOMPLIANT at high confidence under the Aachen SOP without testing whether a cafeteria-freezer repair is even within that SOP’s maintenance and production-support scope. Those are classification and evidential errors, not cosmetic ones.
