4.6

The diagnosis table picks a defensible primary label for every cluster, and it correctly separates fixture-level simultaneity (G1), group start (G2), folder submit (G3), zero-duration bulk close (G6), quarter-hour coarsening (G7), and post-blockage serial drain (G8). That skeleton is undermined by reversed performance biases, unused contradictions in the data, an unusable signature matrix, and a code section that does not meet its own specification.

Section 1 overstates several runners-up and counters. G3’s second explanation, a shared account, is not supported; T-C is one worker and the folder note already explains the common completion. G6’s minute-rounding counter is impossible: 1-minute precision cannot collapse arrivals from 14:05–16:25 onto one 17:00 point. G4’s “hidden overlap” counter is likewise empty at 1-second scanner precision on 4-minute jobs; zero-gap serial work is ordinary single-piece flow, so confidence 60 on “sequential batching” needs that counter, not an overlap story. G8 is marked physically concurrent even though the five intervals abut and never overlap. G7’s proposed censor window, 13:53–14:00 and 14:15–14:22, is an undocumented bound and later conflicts with the ±7.5-minute claim.

The strongest evidence in the table is missed. In G7, M02–M05 have start 14:00 before queue arrivals 14:01–14:11, which rules out a real common start; recorded_at then steps 14:16–14:20 in sequence order. In G5, all four intervals overlap from 13:12 to 13:20, so three analysts cannot cover the log without extra multitasking or bad times. In G8, the safety clock and the scanner clock are stated to differ, so 15:35 versus 15:38 is not a measured three-minute handover.

Section 2 does not function as a matrix. The legend’s three markers are blank, and the start-equality, end-equality, and adjacency cells are blank with them. Readable cells are weak: every hypothesis is given a small recording lag, including bulk backfill, and downtime arrivals are described as a “spike after gap” when G8’s arrivals spike before the idle gap.

Section 3 gets G1’s summed-duration inflation and G6’s zero service plus huge arrival-to-17:00 wait right, then reverses other signs. With G2’s completion buttons treated as real, later logged services are 14, 21, and 27 minutes against completion gaps of about 6–8 minutes, so later service is overstated and later waiting is understated; the text says later service is understated. For G3, delaying only the completion moves sojourn and service by the same amount, so waiting defined as start minus arrival stays put; the text says waiting is inflated, and it calls E04’s 6 minutes “almost zero.” For G8, waits of 38–58 minutes contain the safety blockage, so ignoring downtime inflates process waiting; the text says measured waiting shortens. G5’s 70 person-minutes inside a 30-minute wall are not “low” utilization under a single-analyst reading. G1 is also described as needing no correction, which is unsafe for any utilizer that sums overlapping intervals.

Section 4 embeds the rows and calls `format_dataframe` and `discover_batches` on a 2×2 parameter grid, but it does not do the required comparison. Granularity and queue build-up are never computed. Overlap uses a closed `<=` test, so G6’s seven identical zero-duration events contribute an overlap rate of 0. `merge_distance` is passed as a `Timedelta` where this API compares against numeric seconds, and the result is treated as a dict with `.items()` although `discover_batches` returns a list. Those two calls do not run as written. Suspect lifecycles are kept only by accident of not filtering.

Section 5 names three ambiguous patterns, which meets the count, but the G5 branch says one trusted analyst yields low utilization, and the G7 branch uses a different censor than Section 1. G4’s abutment, G6’s 17:00 stamp, and G1’s four equal intervals are the clearer multi-explanation cases and are not developed. Assumptions are not labeled as assumptions.

Section 6 hits badge identity, NTP and precision, per-item execution times, batch id, capacity, and pause/resume. It never says to keep database insertion time distinct from execution time, which is the field that separates G6 logging from work.

Section 7 calls G6 a high-confidence physical interpretation when the confident claim is only that 17:00 is not a measured service interval. The “hence” then clears G1 and G4 for simulation. G4’s times are usable; G1’s are not, unless fixture capacity is applied so the robot is not counted four times. G4 also never appears in the high-confidence list that the “hence” is drawn from.
