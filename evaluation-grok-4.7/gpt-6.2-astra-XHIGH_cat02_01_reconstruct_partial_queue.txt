The reconstruction is numerically strong and methodologically careful. Case times, missing-start windows, completed-case totals (2050 minutes, mean 146.4, median 145), station means, busy-time and utilization ranges, queue areas (1515 and 797.5), WIP areas (2085 and 1340), the cleared-queue Little’s Law check, the 08:00 state path, and the two-hour flow counts all agree with the extract under the stated half-open, after-ties convention. Open and still-queued cases are kept in WIP, elapsed service, and lower bounds, and are not folded into completed-service means. Imputation is monotone and corner-bounded, the C2 outage is not turned into a fake lost-throughput number, and the time-varying bottleneck claim is tied to queue buildup, service length, and the closing stock rather than to a single utilization ratio.

Deductions for concrete defects:

- The PM4Py prose says the arrival and finish features are successive gaps in seconds. `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` stores inverses of those gaps, in per-second units, and the first case in each order is undefined. That misstates the required demonstration.
- The section 4 coverage cells (“21 starts, 2 missing start times, 1 open, 1 queued” and the S2 analogue) are overlapping counts that read like a partition.
- “Assume neither FCFS nor immediate dispatch” is the wrong epistemic label. Overtaking (P17 ahead of P16) and I2 idle after 13:45 while P22 waits are observations; the text later treats them correctly, but the assumption list overstates them.
- Section 6 never classifies the claim that finished S2 service exceeds finished S1 service. That ranking is stable across the three on-clock imputations (about 25–29 versus 34–38), but the aligned ±2.5-minute stress tests move service by about five minutes and can close or overturn it. The stability list should have said so.
- The rounding envelope “at most about 2.5 × (entries + exits)” is only a loose area bound. It is not derived under the same-case precedence and shared S1-end/S2-entry constraints the rest of the answer is careful about.
- The reported-clock utilization band in section 4 is easy to take as the uncertainty, even though section 6 later shows rounding dominates it. The caption does limit the parentheses to missing starts, so this is presentation, not a false formula.

No checked case duration, resource total, or queue-identity was off. The score is held below 9 because those labeling, stability, and PM4Py-semantics faults are real, not because the station arithmetic failed.
