The completed analysis is strong on control objectives, timing, independence, coverage, recoverability, and non-mechanical case judgment, but the required deliverable is incomplete and a few findings overstate what the facts support.

Sections 1–5 meet the brief. The decomposition covers single-actor diversion, error, collusion, credential substitution, bank redirection, emergency-path abuse, recoverability, and evidence. The harm-referenced hybrid reading of post-release, pre-settlement manager action is consistent and applied in the later sections. Counterfactuals for AP-202, AP-204, AP-208, AP-201, AP-206, and AP-209 identify the loss mechanism, the broken or missing control, residual exposure, and the limited value of a second releaser.
