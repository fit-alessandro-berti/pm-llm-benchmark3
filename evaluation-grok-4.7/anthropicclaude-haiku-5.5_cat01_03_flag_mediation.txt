6.4

The conceptual frame is strong, but the timestamp arithmetic and several mechanism claims are wrong, so the answer is not close to flawless.

The intervention section correctly rejects natural direct and indirect effects under interference and cross-world queue mediators, separates the priority lane from the review mandate, and distinguishes occurrence mediation (E3) from waiting-time mediation (E4) and escalation waiting (E7). P1 is correctly treated as a display change rather than “set flag = 0.” The graph records shared-queue interference and treatment–confounder feedback, and the pilot names eligibility, assignment, a capacity guardrail, outcomes, data, and stopping rules.

The stage table does not match its own description or its own code. The code computes medians and leaves absent stages as missing; the table reports means and calls them medians “from the code.” That gap is material: unflagged cycle time has mean 8.2 h and median 5.75 h; unflagged high-complexity cycle time has mean 15.4 h and median 18.4 h; flagged normal-load cycle time has mean 14.3 h and median 12.25 h. Flag-0 residual time is 1.19 h on average, not 1.3 h, and the unflagged non-escalated residual is about 1.24 h, not 1.4 h. Flag-1 triage service is 0.60 h, not 0.59 h.

Two process facts are stated incorrectly. Flag and load have the same split (6 high / 4 normal), so load does not confound the flag contrast and does not “drive the flag.” Triage resources are perfectly separated: flagged cases use only T1/T2 and unflagged cases only T3/T4. The claim that T4 handles more flagged cases is false, and this collinearity of lane and resource pool is the main obstacle to the triage effect. M2 reviews 5 of 10 flagged cases, not 7 of 10; all five flagged high-complexity cases go to M2 and are escalated, which is the relevant assignment pattern.

Smaller identification problems remain. “Well-defined?” is sometimes answered with “no data” (E9). The proposed negative control, arrival-to-register lag, is not in the log because registration is the arrival timestamp. The local band 0.58–0.80 has no score overlap (highest unflagged 0.61, lowest flagged 0.64). In the pilot, a treatment-only 15% audit adds reviews that dilute the primary cycle-time contrast. Reversal condition 2 treats a failed P2 spillover as a reason to prefer P3, whose defining effect is to worsen unflagged waits. The edge `triage_wait → triage_service` is not a justified causal link, and the complexity pathway cites long cycles as evidence that complexity causes the flag, which these scores do not show.
