The defects are limited but real, and they keep this off a near-flawless score.

The required PM4Py skeleton unpacks `pm4py.check_soundness(...)` into `(sound, diagnostics)`. The public call returns a boolean; that line raises. `sync_cost_function` is also a fragile, version-sensitive parameter and omits silent transitions. The negative-suite rates the text relies on are not computed by the skeleton.

`any(pending)` works only because a nonempty `frozenset` is truthy. That is the wrong idiom for “some hypothesis still has a due check,” and it is easy to break while editing the oracle. `policy_verdict` also returns hard `fail` for an extra check such as B8, while the prose says to investigate a possible duplicate log event first. Log-quality uncertainty is not the same three-way outcome as a missing source or impact.

`model_third` is only the attribute-erased net, so it still exact-accepts an external `R C T M L` and any impact explanation of a correction trace. The surrounding rules say not to deploy that projection alone, but the formal paragraph blends net language with the supplier obligation, and the section 8 “Pass/Fail” cells are easy to read as net acceptance.

Those are specification and harness issues, not a wrong recommendation. The dissent is a genuine alternative (authorized catalog plus escalation), within the 150–250 word bound, and the evidence that would reverse the decision is concrete.
