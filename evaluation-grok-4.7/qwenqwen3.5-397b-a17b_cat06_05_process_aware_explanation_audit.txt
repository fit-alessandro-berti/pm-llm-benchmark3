6.5

The audit correctly rejects the dominant full-trace leaks (`total_case_duration_hours`, `appeal_seen_anywhere`, `final_handler_team`, `num_document_requests_total`, `full_trace_variant_id`), keeps queue length, registration-time complexity, and prefix age, and flags the extraction-date SQL, the nightly document snapshot, the mutable risk score, and end-of-case resource role. The five-part structure, causal caveats, and Case Z hold are credible. It is not strict enough on its own rules.

Section 1 misses local-only `claimant_channel` and `initial_reserve`, and never flags employee experience joined as of extraction. `total_case_duration_hours` is treated as the label itself rather than post-closure information that may determine a 10-day breach. Remediation is process-naive: a prefix variant at this `t0` is the same `Claim Registered → Coverage Check Completed` path for X, Y, and Z, so it has no discriminating value; document requests are not yet enabled, so a “0 or 1” replacement is wrong; “prior appeal history” on this claim is impossible because appeal follows Partial or Deny. `Appeal → Settlement Decision Recorded` is only called future leakage. It is a legal reconsideration path and must be separated from `Claim Closed → Damage Assessment Completed`, which is either non-conforming or an unidentified same-timestamp order. The ±5-minute queue join is marked a clean valid signal.

Section 4 then violates the `t0` constraint. `prior_claim_count_before_t0` is “unusable until repaired” in Section 3 and still a credible driver for Y. `initial_reserve` has no point-in-time lineage and is given an unsupported “requires more validation” story. Original local signs are presented as the revised explanation without stating that those attributions come from a leaked model and can change after refit. Case Z’s note omits the contaminated same-day document snapshot and the time-legal but confounded week-52 signal.

Sections 2 and 5 cover the requested checks and plan, including appeal-before-second-decision, but overstate a second decision without appeal as a data error and stay generic. The result is a useful leak list with an internally inconsistent, partly inadmissible case rewrite.
