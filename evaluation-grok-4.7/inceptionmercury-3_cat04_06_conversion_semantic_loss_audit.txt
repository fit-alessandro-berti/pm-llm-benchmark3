6.5

The document covers all nine required sections, understands the POWL (partial order with loops) as authoritative, and correctly treats BPMN as a derivative. The pipeline split, loop reading, silent-skip versus visible optionality, and storage-versus-visualization recommendation are sound. It is not a validated audit.

The central fault is epistemic. Section 4 marks many cells `validated preserved` and states that traces match, witnesses are rejected, and the label multiset “passes.” Nothing was executed. Those cells are at most `expected preserved`. `conversion failed` for the process tree is likewise an unrun outcome, even though failure is a plausible expectation. That conflation is exactly what this task forbids.

The witness suite does not support the invariants it is said to prove. The coded negatives omit Assess Risk missing before Configure Trial, Release between Configure and Inspect, Correct Defect inside the trial body, Archive before Release, and Legal Review before Assess Risk. The two-iteration witness has no Legal Review, yet the text claims it covers two iterations with legal review. L wholly before or after the loop does not show overlap inside Configure/Inspect/Correct. Full bounded-language comparison would catch some of those differences only between nets; it does not audit `net_from_powl` against the source. The baseline is the converted net, so round-trip equality cannot by itself prove POWL-to-Petri preservation.

Section 5 is incomplete relative to its own claims. Footprints and the reachability graph are computed and printed, not compared. Playout is absent with no reason. Non-final deadlocks are a rejection condition but are never recorded. `check_soundness` is only in the conversion script. The visited key uses place names, so same-named places can collapse distinct markings; the silent budget is also tied to whichever path is dequeued first. For this small net those hazards may not fire, but the write-up still calls the search exhaustive for visible length ≤ 12. Exact final-marking equality is local to each net, which the marking row admits, while other rows still speak as if round-trip behavior was shown.

Smaller issues: INV-SYNC-2’s negative repeats the redo-then-exit case; several concurrency negatives are “N/A”; the tau wording is blank; and Section 5 is not a self-contained script. Sections 6–9 are the strongest: gateway and layout misreading, quarantine rules, POWL as the only editable source, and unbounded language, silent synchronization, version dependence, and diagram reading as residuals. Even there, trace equivalence is not listed as failing to establish pomset concurrency or branching-time bisimulation.
