The primary states and rank-1 next events are sound for all five cases. C6-A and C6-B are both placed in evidence review, with review completion as the next logged event, and they are separated afterward by severity, injury, lot structure, and the duplicate signal. C6-C keeps regulatory assessment open under the repeated-malfunction rule. C6-D uses the 12:40 worklist opening and the migration flag. Intervals are wide, residual mass is left outside the top three, and the directly-follows counts are treated as unconditioned frequencies.

The score is held down by concrete faults in the lower ranks.

C6-D’s second prediction is labeled “Customer Evidence Received,” which is outside the log vocabulary. The logged event is Evidence Received. Both the request and the receipt are absent. A delayed backfill in causal order would surface Customer Evidence Requested first. The range 0.14–0.28 is also high against an 8–15% delay rate and against the attested direct path from Initial Triage to Technical Investigation, which can explain a missing evidence cycle with no delayed event at all.

C6-D’s third prediction, Disposition Recorded at 0.08–0.18, needs regulatory assessment to have finished and disposition to have been recorded within about 80 minutes of the 12:40 opening. Regulatory completion is given as 2–14 hours after assignment, and disposition follows it by a median of 2.2 hours. The enablement flag is correctly false; the probability mass is too large for that pathway.

C6-C marks Supplier Analysis Completed as process-enabled. The related complaints are on different lots, and no shared supplier component is stated, so the supplier-analysis rule is not satisfied. A low interval does not correct the flag.

C6-E ranks Technical Investigation Completed above Supplier Analysis Completed. Low severity and no injury leave technical investigation optional. Six complaints on one lot make supplier analysis mandatory before disposition. The overlapping intervals soften the ranking, and review completion remains the right first prediction only 40 minutes after receipt.

Smaller issues accumulate. C6-A calls supplier analysis “strongly suspected” when the same-lot link is one complaint on two devices and the three similar complaints have no confirmed lot. C6-D puts an unlogged source attachment inside completed obligations. C6-A and C6-B are likely two of the four waiting reviews and are still scored as independent completion chances. On normal-feed cases, non-trivial probability remains on successors that can be next only if Evidence Review Completed is skipped in the feed.
