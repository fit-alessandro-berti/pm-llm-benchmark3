The diagnosis is serious, decision-set based, and mostly faithful to the log, but it is not nearly flawless. Several internal inconsistencies and one false equivalence claim are enough to keep it well below the top of the scale.

What holds up: eligible sets and rule predictions are reconstructed rather than read off start order; QA’s rejection of priority and SPT is earned at 08:30, 09:05, 09:20, and 09:32; QB is correctly scored as about 6/7 for “FIFO plus a priority-1 preemptive lane” versus 3/7 for pure preemptive priority and 1/7 for SPT; the B02 preemption is flagged as SRPT-equivalent; C05 is correctly shown to be infeasible from arrival (10:11+40 > 10:50); the 11:25 D-M choice really does separate FIFO from both priority and SPT; skill eligibility on QD is handled as a constraint rather than silently treated as the dispatch rule; all 32 realized durations equaling `est_min` is a real, useful observation; assumptions, confidence, and overrides are labeled; the required seven-part structure is met.

What pulls the score down:

- QB remaining time is inconsistent. At the 09:48 switch B01 has 14 minutes left (14+17 served), yet the decision table says 16, then 14 again at 10:00 with no intervening service. Predictions do not flip, but the arithmetic is wrong.
- Section 5 claims priority plus resume bias would explain QB without FIFO. That is false. At 10:28 no job is partial and no priority-1 job is waiting, so priority selects B06 and the observed job is B04. FIFO is doing real work there.
- The appointment rule is applied unevenly. In QA an appointment counts only when it is due at the decision instant; at QB 10:28 B07’s 10:30 appointment is still two minutes away and is still marked as the appointment prediction. The 10:14 “look-ahead” check is marked as a hit for B01 even though B04 also fits before 10:30.
- QA’s “strongest supporting” episode, 09:32, is also exactly what SPT predicts. The three-way split at 09:05, or 08:30, is the episode that actually identifies FIFO.
- QC’s “5/6 with two overrides” counts two single-point patches as support. The 10:10 row flags C05 as borderline but still lets priority and EDF predict C03, which is only true if C05 is excluded.
- QD contradicts itself. “No backfilling” is part of the stated discipline, yet the D-F idle period that this rule predicts is listed as the strongest contradiction, and the identifiability cell says idle time is unexplained by any candidate rule. Section 3’s claim that the D-X idle is explained only by a mechanical home-queue rule ignores head-of-line blocking by D03 and then D04, which the same answer uses elsewhere.
- The first discrimination test does not discriminate: the offered counterfactual makes both competing explanations predict B05. The back-fill story is also over-ranked. Universal estimate/realization equality is equally consistent with perfect ex ante estimates, which would strengthen the SPT/SRPT tests rather than dissolve them.
