The write-up has the required seven sections and the right classification vocabulary, but the timing arithmetic is not reliable enough to use. Feasible intervals, durations, and labels frequently contradict each other, and several clear SLA results are reversed.

**Clock model.** Resolution is applied after clock correction, and often not at all. The stated rule is true time = recorded time minus an offset in [a, b], after resolution uncertainty. HUB is treated as an exact point despite ±0.5 s display uncertainty and a [−1 s, +1 s] offset, so every HUB endpoint is about 1.5 s too tight. OPS-BER [−90 s, +120 s] is not applied as an asymmetric subtraction. The mobile paragraph says entry delay does not move occurrence time, then builds an interval that does.

**Section 2 intervals.** Base zone offsets for June (CEST −2, EDT −4, BST −1) are right; the windows are not. OPS-BER 10:14 CEST is about [08:12:00, 08:16:30) UTC, not [08:14:00, 09:04:59]. VENDOR-NY and LEGACY-BER “floor to minute” is expanded to roughly an hour. T-402 containment 10:02 is written as 09:02. SENSOR 08:51:10 keeps only the ±5 s rounding window and drops the separate [−5 s, +5 s] offset. T-409’s [−12 min, +12 min] window drops the minute floor. T-407 containment is missing. The 2025-10-26 fold is reversed: the first 02:xx (CEST, UTC+2) is 00:xx UTC, and the second (CET, UTC+1) is 01:xx UTC, not the other way around.

**T-405 order.** Ingestion order leaves one fold triple, not two parallel hour-scale ranges. Alert must be the first fold, near [00:49, 00:52); investigation and containment must be the second fold, near [01:04, 01:07) and [01:41, 01:44). S1 is then about [720 s, 1080 s] (possible versus 900 s). Fold logging alone would not make it certain.

- T-401 S1 is possible, about [719 s, 992 s], not a certain violation at [600, 1800]. S2 is a certain pass, about [2070 s, 2360 s], not a certain violation. Alert→containment sits inside the zeta=1 band.
- T-402 S2 is a possible violation around 42–46 min. Negative duration is an artifact of the 09:02 transcription.
- T-403 S1 is possible, about 12–19 min. S2 is a certain pass near 42 min, not 70–80 min.
- T-404’s 18–25 min record anchors to Investigation_Complete (10:35), while the note says containment. S2 reaches about 2700 s once HUB uncertainty is kept, so “certain pass at ≤2400 s” is false. 36 h is 129600 s, not 259200 s.
- T-406, after BST, nearest-minute rounding, and ±300 s, gives a possible S1 and a certain-pass S2 near 32–44 min. The negative-duration violation is a local-versus-UTC mix-up.
- T-408 S2 is 55 min (3300 s), not 3900 s; the violation label is still right. S3 is about 38 h 25 min and is a certain breach of 36 h. It is reported as 86400 s and a certain pass.
- T-409’s own [15:52, 16:16] window implies durations from about −8 min to +17 min, so S1 is testable and possible. The findings interval is entirely non-positive and called indeterminate. S2’s profile band at zeta=1 is not a certain pass.

Most required rows are absent. Every supplied profile pair that has both ends, including inferred T-404 and both close pairs, needed a row. Only a handful of SLA rows and one profile row appear.

Real flips are missed: T-401 S1 becomes a certain pass at +2 min; T-402 S2 becomes a certain pass by +2 min; T-408 investigation→containment is a certain violation at zeta=1, borderline at zeta=2, and a pass at zeta=3; containment→close stays a profile violation through zeta=2 and falls inside only at zeta=3.
