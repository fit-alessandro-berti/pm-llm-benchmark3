6.3

The write-up meets the required eight-part structure and gets the main mechanism roughly right: label remap, `Queue Routed` removal, and source ordering account for most of the measured fitness drop, the August sign-off gap is model obsolescence, the optional control-flow repair can overfit, and stable appeals do not clear the missing `Compliance Check` cases. It is not a precise abductive analysis. Several central claims are internally inconsistent or stronger than the evidence.

The ranking does not discriminate. Posterior scores of 98, 96, 94, and 92 are effectively tied, and real behavioral change is scored 85 while ranked below employee noncompliance at 75. Obsolete-model obsolescence is treated as a cause of the fall from 93% to 32% fitting traces. That fall is a June–July result. The EUR 50,000 rule is dated 2026-08-01, so it cannot explain the earlier collapse. The June recovery to 89% is produced by label mapping, technical-event removal, and source ordering alone. Section 2’s claim that hypotheses 1–4 explain about 90% of the 93-to-32 drop repeats that timing error.

The policy is misread. Nothing in the mandate limits sign-off to enhanced review or forbids it below EUR 50,000. “Standard-review sign-offs contradict an enhanced-review focus” is an invented contradiction. The same error reappears in the data-aware recommendation, which treats sub-threshold sign-off as illicit delay. The correct data condition is mandatory sign-off above EUR 50,000. High-value status was omitted from the alignment extract, so neither perfect compliance nor illicit low-value sign-off has been shown.

Calibration and provenance are overstated. Release notes only claim that the business function was unchanged. The ledger upgrades that to application-team verification and calls the supplied alignment table a replay of 650 cases. The 11-of-70 screenshot sample and the explicitly unaudited 25 and 30 missing-check traces do not support 99% confidence in the collapse explanation or 85% confidence that an active compliance breach exists. Section 7 says those 55 cases remain unconfirmed and then assigns 85%. “Matching baseline” is also false: 89% and 86% are not 93%. “Catastrophic regulatory liability” and “harmless measurement noise” are both stronger than the record.

The causal list asserts edges the evidence leaves open. UI 4.2 is tied to routing-error rate, and reviewer behavior is tied to skipped checks. The chronology supports a rename, a gateway event, an ingestion-time change, an unupdated model, and an unresolved bypass-versus-missing-event question. `Manual Override` also had a 2026-08-12 logging change, which is underweighted relative to the “real user action” story. The August enhanced-review share rises from about 26–30% to about 43.5% of cases and is never used. The missing-resource jump from 1.2% to 5.9% is too small to show that essentially all `Queue Routed` events are null-resource technical events. One extra event per case with a null resource would have moved that rate much further.

Counterfactuals mostly restate the alignment table already given. The hard-block row confuses a future system control with a historical log repair and still quotes a return to about 93% fitness while `Manual Override` and, in August, sign-off remain. The diagnostic phases isolate the right layers, but Phase 2 cites `pm4py.sort_timestamp_attribute`, which is not a real PM4Py entry point, and Phase 4 leans toward discipline before the audit result.

The code follows the requested pipeline: process tree, Petri net, `parse_event_log_string`, DFGs, label map, optional `Queue Routed` removal, candidate `X('Supervisor Sign-off', tau)`, fitness and precision, and an explicit EUR 50,000 overgeneralization caveat. It does not compute alignment diagnostics or move types, despite that being required. The inversion “repair” swaps activity names on adjacent rows rather than ordering by a source sequence attribute. That can look right on this synthetic log and is not the stated provenance fix.

Governance rules cover the six requested topics and are the cleanest part of the answer. The analysis is usable as a hypothesis memo and unreliable as a ranked causal conclusion.
