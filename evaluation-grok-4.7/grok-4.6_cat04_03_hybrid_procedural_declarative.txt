7.2

The hybrid design is analytically strong: R5 and R6 are kept apart, the Legal-strict choice and its false-fail risk are explicit, and the test matrix mostly follows from those choices. Temporal arithmetic, the refusal to encode R5 as unconditional `precedence(Obtain Legal Opinion, Activate Vendor)`, and the observation that `altresponse` still allows `Request Clarification → Activate Vendor → Receive Clarification` are all correct. The score is held down by concrete model and logic errors, not by disclosed approximations.

The process tree is not valid PM4Py. `Operator.LOOP` has exactly two children, do then redo; exit is the implicit choice after do. The formal term `*(τ, →(Request Clarification, Receive Clarification), τ)` and the three-child constructor invent a `*(do, redo, exit)` shape PM4Py does not use. A strict converter or validity check rejects it. Lenient semantics that read only the first two children happen to yield the intended \((Request\ Receive)^*\), so the T1–T8 procedural column is unaffected, but the required executable artifact and the claim that this matches PM4Py are wrong. Section 10 then tells a deployer to convert that tree with `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net`.

`log_skeleton["directly_follows"] = set()` contradicts the pass cells. The write-up itself says a present empty directly-follows set is a universal ban and would fail T1–T8, then still records skeleton `pass` for six traces. The later “disable the check” instruction is a wrapper around the dictionary, not part of the dictionary the matrix claims to evaluate. Upper bounds in `activ_freq` (`Request`/`Receive` ≤ 5, `Verify Bank Account` ≤ 3, bank-change activities ≤ 2) are likewise undefended: R3 allows arbitrarily many closed cycles, so the skeleton false-fails traces the DECLARE layer accepts.

Two semantic claims are tighter than the rules. `always_before(Activate Vendor, Change Bank Details)` plus `always_after(Change Bank Details, Suspend Vendor)` forbid every change that is not post-activation and unsuspended. R11 is conditional: only a change after activation must be suspended, and only a later payment must be re-verified. Calling equivalence of the clarification pair “stricter than `altresponse`” is false. Equal counts do not imply alternate response (`Request, Request, Receive, Receive`), and alternate response does not imply equal counts (an extra `Receive Clarification`). They are incomparable; T5 fails both, which is all that was shown.

The authoritative R5 predicate is not the stated obligation. `min(opinion) < min(activate)` accepts a pre-escalation opinion even when the only post-escalation opinion is after activation. With `activ_freq` capped at one opinion, R4 masks the usual miss, but the check does not implement “after escalation, an opinion precedes activation,” and the same `min` shortcut is reused for the 24-hour SLA. Missing-attribute behavior is specified in prose and not implemented: absent timestamps or resources throw instead of returning `not_evaluated`. The three `filter_four_eyes_principle` calls discard their results; route-dependent four-eyes is entirely the hand-rolled intersection.

Smaller issues: `response` on the clarification pair is redundant once `altresponse` is present; `noncoexistence(Reject Vendor, Enhanced Due Diligence)` and `noncoexistence(Reject Vendor, Release First Payment)` are backbone projections, not R1–R12; “agrees with the XOR” overstates a skeleton that enforces exclusion but not “an accepted case has an approval”; vacuous temporal pairs are labeled `pass` rather than `not evaluated`. None of those flip T1–T8 under the stated policy.

T1–T5, T7, and T8 verdicts match the design, including the easy-to-miss result that Activate→Pay of 2 hours in T7 is inside \([0, 144]\) while Escalation→Opinion of 34 hours is a warning only. T6 correctly splits into Legal-strict `fail` versus Operations-permissive `warning`, and the two clocks (escalation→opinion versus activate→opinion) are not collapsed. That case analysis is the best part of the answer; the tree arity bug, the directly-follows contradiction, and the inexact R5/R11 encodings are why it is not higher.
