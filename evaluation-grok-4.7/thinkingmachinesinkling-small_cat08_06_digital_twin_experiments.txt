6.5

The design covers every required section and the main constraints: non-stationary arrivals, calendars, latent objections, service versus wait, cross-case effects, no optimality claim, and mostly conventional PM4Py use. It is not close to a reliable twin specification.

The process state cannot represent the model it cites. A single `current_activity` cannot hold the parallel planning, fire, and environmental branches, and there is no AND-join that holds decision drafting until the applicable reviews, notice, and inspection finish. Public-notice and applicant delays are not clearly separated from resource seizures. Calendar facts that drive bottlenecks are missing: environmental reviewers lose 40% of time to non-permit work, inspectors are field-only Tuesday–Friday, and one clerk is reserved for Monday portal support. Capping rework at three loops with a made-up 1.2× service factor suppresses the long tail used later as a decision metric.

Estimation and validation are weaker than they look. Two quantiles do not identify a service distribution; subtracting a random service draw from completion-to-completion gaps can go negative and is not a waiting-time estimator. The rate formula is garbled. Variant medians are treated as case-type baselines: 12 days is the clean simple path, not simple renovations, and 140 days is one complex variant, not the complex type. Objection “means” of 30 and 46 days are not in the data. Replay through a process tree is conformance, not simulation validation, and fitting service times then checking those same quantiles is not out-of-sample proof. Warm-up both starts from empty queues and seeds historical state; 90 days is shorter than complex-case p90s. Batch means are proposed for a seasonal, non-stationary process.

The experiment matrix lists D1–D10, a combination, and three stresses, but effects such as “40% fewer loops” or “notice falls from 46 to 20 days” are assumed outcomes, not mechanisms. Stresses are not crossed with interventions, yet selection depends on robustness under those stresses. D2 needs violation history the log does not contain. The skeleton formats the excerpt correctly and keeps PM4Py off the engine, but it never defines the required arrival generator, and the simulation classes do not encode calendars, joins, or dependencies.
