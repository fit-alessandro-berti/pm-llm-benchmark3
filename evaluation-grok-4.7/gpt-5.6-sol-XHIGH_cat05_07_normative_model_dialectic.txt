7.4

The memorandum is a serious conformance opinion: M0’s block semantics are read correctly, trace fitness is separated from CS-9 compliance, PD-3 and FD-7 are not collapsed into one another, and the revision keeps amounts, identity, expiry, and authority outside POWL. It is not nearly flawless. Several local errors sit on the exact points the analysis treats as decisive.

The stipulated-fact list includes a normative conclusion: that a continuing flood and an expected amendment did not extend FD-7 or PD-3. That is a shared legal premise later conceded by the defense, not a fact established by the record. Placing it above the dispute table biases the burden analysis.

SUP-702 is the clearest failure to judge. The memorandum’s own exception burden requires competent authority, a proved derogation, and the compensating controls. Automated master-data validation is absent, and PD-3’s power to waive CS-9 is expressly disputed. Those findings support NONCOMPLIANT under a fail-closed ledger, or an authority-indeterminate finding. MIXED restates the unresolved conflict and is the split the task forbids, despite the accompanying explanation.

SUP-704 is classified JUSTIFIED_EXCEPTION at high confidence as “validly timed” with every compensating control met. Activation on 2026-07-11 was inside FD-7, but that date was a Saturday, so “next business day” for quality review and retrospective approval is Monday 2026-07-13, after the directive’s stated end. The two-business-day deadline can still be met while the performing acts fall outside the written window. The opinion never confronts that collision. It also treats initial spend of EUR 18,000 as cap compliance, although the cap is cumulative until retrospective approval and no full ledger is supplied. High confidence overstates what the exception burden allows. The revised POWL then makes this worse by requiring a visible `Start_Daily_Transaction_Monitoring` before activation. FD-7 does not say monitoring is a pre-activation event, the historical trace has no such activity, and the write-up does not acknowledge that its own justified exemplar is outside M1.

SUP-706 is correctly left unresolved, but for the wrong established mechanism. The case has no date. The 70% mirror rate and the rise from 2% to 29% apply only from 2026-07-01. The mutable text field is described as the older linking method, so it does not prove the post-migration defect. The indeterminacy should rest on linkage and an unknown case date.

SUP-715 imports a rule the facts do not invoke. The relationship is between quality reviewer and manual approver. CS-9 separates requester from manual approver. “N1 identity rule remains separately applicable” and the demand for requester-identity confirmation are unsupported. The disclosure failure itself is fairly classified.

System-level noncompliance is then slightly overcounted. Fourteen ineligible auto-approvals, six post-expiry uses, SUP-703, SUP-708, and SUP-712 support a material finding. The nine cases with missing eligibility attributes do not. The memorandum’s own proof rules class those as indeterminate or fail-closed operations, yet section 5 lists them as facts that establish noncompliance.

The model is otherwise the right kind of approximation: n-ary route choice, concurrent standard checks, silent-do/visible-redo for `Request_More_Info*`, notification on every terminal branch, and an explicit statement that route structure does not confer authority. Smaller defects remain. Emergency information requests are forced to finish before activation. The DECLARE wording that auto-approval and validation need only “coexist” with activation drops the precedence the POWL actually encodes. SUP-711 is highly confidently a duplicate whose canonical trace conforms, which sits awkwardly under DATA_QUALITY_INDETERMINATE. Withdrawal is specified as requested, but it can bypass sanctions without a stated anti-evasion rule.

The prosecution and defense are both real, and the rejection paragraphs name concrete overclaims. The grade is held down by mis-timed emergency follow-up, an exception burden the central classification does not apply, one misattributed identity rule, and a normative model that silently fails its best emergency case.
