The write-up follows the requested nine-part outline and correctly prefers `POWL → accepting Petri net → BPMN`, with the process-tree route allowed to fail. That is where the reliability ends. The baseline, code, audit, and recommendation repeatedly contradict the source semantics or the stated PM4Py API.

The invariant table is not a usable baseline. Witnesses are ellipses rather than concrete visible traces. I2 claims Assess Risk and Prepare Evidence occur before every other activity, which is false: Legal Review can occur after Assess Risk and before Prepare Evidence. I5’s negative witness treats an allowed ordering as forbidden. I6 recasts the nondeterministic do-redo loop as “repeat until defect fixed,” inventing a data condition the POWL operator does not have, and it never isolates Configure Trial before Inspect Trial or the rule that Correct Defect cannot be the exit. I8’s positive witness is phrased as Legal Review or the verification loop, which is an OR-join; release requires both. IDs then drift in §§4 and 7, so later claims do not refer to a stable invariant. The tau column is garbled and often marks a silent choice as merely “hidden” even when omission of Legal Review is visible.

The conversion code would not run. `Operator` is imported from the wrong module. It calls undefined entry points (`powl_to_petrinet`, `save_vis_petrinet`, `convert_to_petrinet`, `save_vis_footprint`) instead of `pm4py.convert_to_petri_net`, `convert_to_bpmn`, `convert_to_process_tree`, and `save_vis_petri_net`. BPMN conversion is invoked without markings and unpacked as a `(bpmn, im, fm)` triple. Markings are never null-checked. Workflow-net and soundness checks are not applied to every accepting net. The required names `net_from_powl`, `im_from_powl`, `fm_from_powl`, and `bpmn_from_net` are absent.

The preservation table marks many rows “validated preserved” although nothing was executed. Concurrency is described as an exclusive gateway or swimlanes; an XOR would destroy the legal/verification overlap. “Not applicable” is outside the allowed assessment vocabulary. The likely process-tree outcome, failure because the cross-dependency is not block-structured, is softened into “ambiguous” or “approximated,” while §8 later says that intermediate is disallowed.

The explorer does not implement the required test. Finality is “no enabled transition,” so a deadlock counts as success; exact final-marking equality is never checked. The visited-marking set drops other interleavings, trailing silent steps are not consumed, and the silent bound is not counted on the path. `compare_nets` is called on the POWL object, soundness is called without `fm`, and `pm4py.apply_transition` is undefined. Footprints, the reachability graph, playout, and positive/negative witness replay are missing. Calling the bounded run exhaustive is wrong: the loop language is infinite, and this search is not even complete inside the bound.

Rejection criteria and the visual discussion touch the right themes, but “missing ordering” for the unordered pair is backwards, POWL is said to have markings, and a BPMN “while card” is assumed rather than an unstructured gateway cycle. Storage authority is given to the derived Petri net, with future edits made there and claimed to prevent drift. The authoritative model is the source POWL; the net and BPMN are derivatives. Two of the three residual doubts concern hypothetical multi-token extensions and “rare” tau orders, not the real gaps: bounded trace equality does not give language equivalence, step/concurrency equivalence, or proof that a sound BPMN drawing preserves the partial order.
