8.4

The stage arithmetic, absent-stage handling, and refusal of natural direct/indirect effects are correct and unusually careful. Flagged versus unflagged means, supports (10/10 versus 4/10 reviewed; 6/10 versus 2/10 escalated), and the 0.24 h versus 2.48 h triage-wait and 16.51 h versus 8.17 h cycle-time contrasts all check. Occurrence is separated from waiting, interference is built into the estimands, and the code keeps missing stages as missing. The score stays below a near-flawless mark because of the gaps below.

The complexity evidence is partly circular. High versus low cycle time (19.77 h versus 4.35 h) largely restates the flag, because all four low-complexity cases are unflagged and unreviewed, while seven of eight high-complexity cases are escalated. The flag contrast inside complexity is missing. It still exists, about 4.6 h for medium and about 7 h for high, on very thin support.

The triage mechanism is under-read. Flagged cases use only T1/T2 and unflagged cases only T3/T4, and triage service is also shorter (0.60 h versus 0.95 h). The graph sends `priority_lane` only to `triage_wait`, so the pathway table treats acceleration as waiting and leaves the dedicated-lane evidence implicit.

Wording slips. Scores do not overlap (maximum unflagged 0.61, minimum flagged 0.64). Section 4 calls that “nearly” separate and Section 9 calls overlap “sparse”; Section 5 states the separation correctly.

The P2 pilot never says whether high-complexity flagged cases in a treated shift keep mandatory review. That choice changes both the quality risk and the shared-queue contrast. Stopping a block because backlog worsened can also distort a shift-level intention-to-treat estimate.

Smaller defects: `manual_review -> manual_queue_wait` still reads like a duration effect; separable effects and controlled direct effects are not named cleanly; P3 is defined as new capacity and then judged as if it might be reallocation; low-complexity triage service is 0.775 h, not 0.77 h, and high-complexity cycle time is 19.775 h, not 19.77 h; daylight-saving does not fall on 2–3 March 2026. Section 9’s body is about 255 words, or just over 260 if the labels count.
