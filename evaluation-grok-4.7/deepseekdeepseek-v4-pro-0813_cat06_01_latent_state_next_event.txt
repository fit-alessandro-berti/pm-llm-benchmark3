6.2

The latent-state reading is generally strong, but the prediction lists repeatedly treat later activities as the next logged event.

C6-A and C6-B are correctly separated: high severity, injury, and a shared lot keep investigations open, while the low-severity near-duplicate points toward non-investigation closure. C6-C correctly holds disposition for a reportability-driven regulatory assessment. C6-D uses the worklist and the migration flag instead of treating missing evidence events as proof that evidence work never happened. C6-E correctly makes supplier analysis mandatory for a lot tied to six complaints and does not fast-close it. Queues, priority, the 16:00 notification batch, and directly-follows frequencies are used as evidence rather than as the model. Intervals are wide, and the cross-case discussion is coherent.

The main failure is control-flow timing. In every case, rank 2 or rank 3 is an activity the write-up itself says is not enabled until an unlogged predecessor completes, yet those activities still carry substantial probability as the next log event. That is explicit for another evidence request and for Close — No Safety Issue before Evidence Review Completed, for disposition and authority notification before Regulatory Assessment Completed, and for a third evidence request only 40 minutes after the second receipt. `process_enabled: false` records the distinction and then the ranking ignores it. On a normal feed, those upper bounds (about 0.18–0.30) are too high for paths that need one or more missing completions.

Other judgment errors add to that. C6-A ranks another evidence request ahead of regulatory completion even though injury and high severity make concurrent Technical Investigation and Regulatory Assessment the more plausible open work. C6-C’s Authority Notification still depends on an unseen reportable conclusion, an unseen disposition, and the batch clock. C6-D names delayed Evidence Received specifically; if a missing evidence cycle is flushing in order, Customer Evidence Requested would surface first, and the source attachment may only be an intake file on the direct triage-to-investigation path. Technical Investigation and Supplier Analysis completions are marked process-enabled without evidence those tasks have started. Elapsed time is cited but does not discipline the ranges: regulatory work open for about 80 minutes against a 2–14 hour band, and a review only 40 minutes old, are given floors similar to reviews already near the median.
