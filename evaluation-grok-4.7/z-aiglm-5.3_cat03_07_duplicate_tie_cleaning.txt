The final answer is a strong, constraint-respecting design: the six-way decision tree is complete, the required pairs are decided conservatively, e05 and e14 are protected, e15 is correctly refused as a merge target for either capture, ingestion time is not used as business order, originals are retained, and the 15 / 14 / 10 event counts and 44 / 44 / 28 E2O counts are arithmetically right.
