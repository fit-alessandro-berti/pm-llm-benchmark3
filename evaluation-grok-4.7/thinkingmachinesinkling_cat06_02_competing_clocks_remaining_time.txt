6.5

The six-section forecast is structured and covers parallelism (`max`, not a sum), weekend calendars, queue-versus-service time, path branching, temperature-flag dependence, and QA-queue uncertainty. Those are the right mechanisms, but the numerical forecast is not internally consistent and several clocks are wrong.

The headline mapping in §4 is arithmetically off. From Friday 18 September 15:30, 90 h is Tuesday 22 September 09:30, not 06:00, and 110 h is Wednesday 23 September 05:30, not 08:00. The earliest “~67 h ≈ Monday 11:00–12:00” is similarly slipped (67 h is about 10:30). For a remaining-time forecast, that is a direct error, not a rounding choice.

Scenario B contradicts itself. The table treats an overnight pause as the central case (terminal Tuesday 09:00–16:00) and calls Monday 17:00 the earliest terminal time. The decomposition’s own central case finishes the priority rerun Monday at 17:18 and then still needs QA. Monday 17:00 is a chemistry-completion time, not a terminal release. Scenario C has the same slip: Tuesday 09:30 is about when a confirmation readout could exist, not when QA and electronic release could finish.

Risk-priority queueing is serialized incorrectly. With two analysts and only the urgent sample strictly ahead, the second analyst can start B-417 while the urgent sample is in service. On Monday that is an 08:00 start, not a ~2.5 h wait for the urgent job to finish. The “<1 h Friday partial start, then a full service from scratch Monday” story is also inconsistent: an earliest start near 16:42 could leave more than an hour of work done by 18:00, which would pull Monday chemistry earlier. The assumption ledger and the stage narrative do not use the same rule.

Probability mass is shifted late. A rerun is only 0.10–0.20 at baseline, and the mode rerun in §3 finishes the same afternoon; contamination is 0.05–0.12 unless the flag is confirmed. Treating those as 0.15–0.25 and 0.10–0.20 delaying scenarios, then placing the P50 window entirely on Tuesday–Wednesday and the P80 out to Friday 18:00, overstates the smooth-path tail. The P80 interval also starts at 90 h, so it is not an 80% range; it drops the Monday outcomes. “Batch Rejected or release past Friday (~15–20%)” conflates a rare critical-defect rejection with a calendar delay.

Dependence is acknowledged and the temperature flag is a defensible primary update trigger, but the lifted rerun (~0.30) and contamination (~0.20) probabilities are invented, and no scenario actually runs the correlated case (flag confirmed, both branches slow, investigation probability 0.30–0.55) as its own clock. The investigation tail is instead anchored on a near-normal Tuesday join.

The skeleton would be a high score if the clocks, terminal-event definition, and scenario quantiles agreed. They do not.
