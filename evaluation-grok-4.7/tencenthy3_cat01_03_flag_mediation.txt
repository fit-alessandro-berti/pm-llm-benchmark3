The stage table is mostly faithful to the timestamps, absent stages are kept as missing rather than zero, and the policy split between priority triage, review occurrence, review waiting, and escalation is the right causal structure. The choice of interventional effects over natural direct and indirect effects is justified by shared queues, cross-world mediator assumptions, and treatment–confounder feedback. The score is held down by concrete errors. Flagged normal-load mean cycle time is 14.25 h, not 14.0 h. The prose then calls tabulated means “medians” and describes total cycle time as about three times larger; the tabulated means are 16.5 h versus 8.2 h, about twice as large. In this sample the flag is a hard threshold of `risk_score` (maximum unflagged score 0.61, minimum flagged score 0.64), so the proposed 0.70–0.80 within-score contrast has no unflagged cases. That empty-band design is offered as an identification strategy.
