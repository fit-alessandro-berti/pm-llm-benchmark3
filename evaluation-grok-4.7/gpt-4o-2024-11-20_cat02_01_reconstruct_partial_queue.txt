The write-up keeps the required seven-part outline, states usable base formulas, transcribes the source table, and gets the fully observed early S1/S2 durations for P01–P03 right. P16’s S1 wait (100 min) and service (30 min) are also right, and a full-window S2 service mean near 40 minutes matches the twelve fully timestamped S2 services. Beyond that, the reconstruction is not reliable.

The case table misses required rows P17, P21, and P22, and it drops censored S2 waiters P18–P20. Several reported ranges violate the timestamps and the single-resource constraint. P05’s S2 sojourn from 09:25 to 10:15 is 50 minutes, so a wait of [50, 60] plus a service of 30 minutes cannot occur together; with C1 free only after P03 ends at 09:45, the feasible split is a wait of about [20, 50) minutes and a complementary service of about (0, 30] minutes. P07’s S1 start is bounded by P05 ending at 09:25 and P07 ending at 10:00, so service cannot be 45 minutes; the feasible service is at most about 35 minutes and the wait is about [40, 75) minutes, not “[TBD]” paired with a point service. P11’s S1 start is observed: wait is 75 minutes and service is 25 minutes. Its missing timestamp is the S2 start. C2 is occupied by P08 until 12:00 and P11 ends at 12:25, so S2 service is at most about 25 minutes and S2 wait is about [55, 80) minutes. The reported wait [40, 50] and service 40 minutes overlap P08. P15’s open activity is S2, not S1. S1 has already ended at 12:15; the 125-minute figure is arrival-to-S1-end sojourn, mislabeled as open S1 service. S2 wait is observed at 75 minutes (13:30 − 12:15), and elapsed S2 service by 14:00 is 30 minutes and still censored. P16’s S2 wait is likewise censored at a minimum of 85 minutes, not N/A. P07 and P15 are left unresolved in the same section that claims missing starts were addressed.

Station counts for 08:00–14:00 do not match the log. S1 has 22 arrivals and 20 completions (P21 is open; P22 has not started), not 21 departures. S2 receives those 20 S1 completions and completes 14 services, not 19 arrivals and 18 departures. The full-window S1 mean wait of [15, 30] minutes is far below the observed starts: the 19 known S1 waits average about 65 minutes. The 18 known S1 services average about 28 minutes; a 35-minute mean only appears if the infeasible P07 and P15 imputations are included. WIP peaks and the S1 utilization band are stated without a timeline. The S2 utilization band of roughly 70–80% is plausible once C2’s 15-minute outage and the open services are included, but it is not derived. No subperiod arrival or departure rates are computed. Little’s Law is named and then never applied, and no interval is explicitly rejected as non-stationary.

The bottleneck narrative, S1 congested while the morning arrival surge is absorbed and S2 holding most of the WIP later, is a defensible hypothesis. End-of-window state supports the second half: S1 has one open service and one waiting case, while S2 has two open services and four waiting cases. The text presents that shift as an observation without queue paths, throughput, or subperiod utilization, and it treats a 15-minute one-resource outage as a clear propagating shock. P08 was already waiting well before 10:55; C2 was free at 10:50 and the outage removes only 15 resource-minutes.

Section 6 names optimistic, central, and pessimistic scenarios and does not compute them. No imputed starts, alternate waits, WIP bounds, or utilization ranges are compared, so the stability claims are untested. The central case is also inconsistent with section 3, where P07 is still “[TBD]” and other missing starts are replaced by single infeasible services.

The Python builds the right table and does not collapse MISSING, OPEN, and blank into one sentinel at parse time. It then fails the rest of the code specification. Open ends are passed through as event completions, missing starts are dropped rather than bounded, and there is no pandas queue, service, WIP, or scenario calculation. `format_dataframe` is called, but `insert_case_arrival_finish_rate` is not. `insert_case_service_waiting_time` is run on a mixed S1/S2 frame that still contains non-timestamps, without a valid case-level projection or a case-level-versus-station-level label. `convert_log_to_time_intervals` is absent, with no comment explaining a pandas alternative. An unused import sits on a module path that is not the PM4Py entry point for these helpers. The script does not print the station or uncertainty results the earlier sections assert.
