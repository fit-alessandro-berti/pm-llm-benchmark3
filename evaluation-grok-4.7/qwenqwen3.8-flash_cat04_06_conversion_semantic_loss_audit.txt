The document hits the required structure and is unusually careful about what POWL actually commits to: partial order rather than layout order, silent XOR optionality, at-least-one loop with redo-not-last, and the asymmetric join before release. Pipeline choice, exception handling, null-marking checks, assessment vocabulary, visual-misreading analysis, rejection criteria, and the POWL-versus-BPMN authority split are all sound.

The round-trip explorer does not implement the token game. PM4Py transitions expose `in_arcs`/`out_arcs` (`arc.source`, `arc.target`, `arc.weight`), not `.pre`/`.post`. With those attributes missing, every firing is a no-op, so completed traces stay empty, positive replays fail, negative replays pass vacuously, and an empty-versus-empty trace diff looks like equivalence. That breaks the central validation claim. The audit also marks every Petri-to-tree cell `conversion failed` as an outcome that was not run, and the comparison block never reuses `check_soundness` even though soundness is part of the required comparison.
