The final document is a strong governance review: process-tree readings of S and C are right, B1–B8 language membership is right, metrics stay disaggregated, and the third model is a real alternative rather than a restatement of either submission. Mandatory checks stay mandatory, approval is exclusive, and missing source and affected-check data are treated as unknown rather than as a license to optionalize every activity. The dissent is a genuine rebuttal and stays within the required length.

The score is held down by concrete gaps and one analytical slip. Section 9 never builds duplicate-event perturbations, leaves unseen-combination testing as a comment, and substitutes a variant split that keeps singleton variants out of holdout while still using a 0.90 holdout-fitness rejection rule. Attribute stratification and a documented comprehension protocol are missing. In the metrics table, C’s holdout drop is tied to label-splitting; that drop is primarily a finite-variant language mismatch. The audit marks P4 as fully met, but putting the repair loop in parallel with Supplier/Expedite lets Expedite sit inside the correction window, including before the affected recheck. The third tree also duplicates Credit Check and Technical Review and does not discuss the alignment ambiguity that creates, after criticizing C for duplicate labels. The PM4Py skeletons are directionally usable, but negative traces are built from bare dicts rather than events, and alignment-cost keys are only guessed.
