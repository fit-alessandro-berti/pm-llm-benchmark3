The forecast is structurally complete and gets the main mechanism right: the join is a maximum, the weekend incubation plus Monday microbiology staffing sets the smooth-path floor, chemistry is a queue-and-calendar problem rather than a same-day finish, and the temperature excursion is treated as a shared cause rather than ignored. The hour anchors that can be checked directly are right (Monday 08:00 = 64.5 h, incubation ready Sunday 12:00–20:00, Friday residual analyst time 2.1–3.4 h, pre-Monday QA capacity 8.5 h, triangular means ≈ 2.8 h and ≈ 1.9 h). The clean-path band of about 0.40–0.55 is consistent with the constructed conditionals, and the scenario bands are correctly left overlapping.

The QA-query clock is arithmetically wrong. QA is open until 20:00, so a Monday afternoon opening still has about 5–7 working hours left. A 4-hour query opened at 14:00 finishes Monday evening; opened at 17:00 it spills only to about Tuesday 09:00, and a resumed 1.5-hour review releases around mid-morning, not “Tuesday midday to afternoon.” A 12-hour query from mid-afternoon Monday consumes roughly half of Tuesday, not “most of Tuesday.” The scenario table’s earliest query terminal time, Tuesday 12:00–18:00, inherits that slip. Because queries are one of the larger branches (0.15–0.30), this pushes both the query row and the claim that queries belong in the Wednesday–Thursday P80 band later than the calendar supports.

Several smaller defects sit beside that error. The FIFO paragraph says 7–11 analyst-hours remain, which with two analysts is a start near 11:30–13:30, but the same paragraph then uses a 15:30 start, which needs about 15 analyst-hours. The constructed rerun and contamination conditionals are only “near” the stated marginals: the corners fall outside 0.10–0.20 and 0.05–0.12. The rerun row’s earliest terminal time, Monday 17:00–20:00, is later than the row’s own premise that a primary result early Monday lets the rerun finish before 18:00; with a short QA queue that path can release mid-afternoon. “A review that starts after about 16:30 often stops at 20:00” overstates spill for a 1.5-hour median. Conditional on a confirmed excursion, a fully clean release is still on the order of one quarter and a laboratory investigation on the order of one tenth; calling Wednesday–Thursday the new central mass, and rejection or a laboratory investigation “routine,” overstates the shift. The secondary trigger says a Friday priority start pulls chemistry to Monday late morning, but under priority a Monday 08:00 start is already about 09:30–12:30; the fragment only pulls that toward early morning.

The headline ranges are wide enough that the true smooth-path mass still sits inside them, and the dependence, rejection caveat, and rerun close-rule are explicit. The query-calendar error and the inconsistent queue arithmetic are still concrete failures in a required decomposition, so this is not a near-flawless forecast.
