The review is rigorous, use-aware, and mostly faithful to the process-tree semantics, but it is not nearly flawless. Several concrete errors and one self-contradictory validation design keep it well below a top score.

The strongest material is the separation of language membership from alignment fitness, the reading of Model S’s `X(activity, tau)` block, and the probe verdicts for S and C. B1–B8 are decided correctly from the expressions: S admits B3 and B4 and cannot express correction; C admits B1 and B7, rejects B2–B6 and B8, and contains only one auto-approval script. The metric table refuses a composite score, and the ranked pathologies correctly put optionalized mandatory checks, positive-only precision, and catalog memorization ahead of runtime and arc-degree simplicity. The third model keeps both initial checks mandatory, uses `*(A, B)` in the usual do/redo sense, confines `tau` to Supplier Review and Expedite Review, and refuses a clean pass while sourcing or affected-check data are absent. Retiring both submitted nets for enforcement, with an explicit probe gate, follows from that analysis. The dissent is a real counterargument and stays inside 150–250 words.

The arithmetic in section 4 does not hold. Two check orders, five supplier/expedite patterns, and two approvals do make 20 traces, and listing 7 inside Model C is right, but “every auto-approval except `CC→TR→AA`” is 9 traces, not 7. The complement is therefore not auditable from the text that claims to enumerate it.

Section 8 contradicts section 7. The checker sets P3 to PASS for a known internal order whether or not Supplier Review occurs, and to FAIL for a known external order that lacks it. The B1 cell says PASS once sourcing is known, which is false for a known external B1. The B5 cell says P3 PASSes only when sourcing is external, which is false under the same checker. Those are the canonical probes, not edge commentary.

Section 9 does not implement the experiment it describes. The prose says stratification on an activity multiset plus proxy flags. The code keys each stratum on the full ordered trace, then forces every singleton variant into training. That split cannot measure unseen variants; seed sensitivity is almost empty; and the later sentence about proxy-stratified holdout is a different design from the function above it. Cost grids and perturbation tests remain prose around default API calls.

Smaller faults add up. “A flower model is simple” is a poor illustration in the arc-degree row, because that metric penalizes high degree. The internal-supplier reading is an extra policy choice but is not named with A-order, A-approval, and A-recheck. Of the added traces, only A3 separates the third language from both originals at once; A2, A4, and A6 are rejected by all three. The minimum-length guard of 4 is looser than the shortest legal trace, which has length 5.

The governance conclusion survives these mistakes. The mistakes are still numerical, logical, and methodological, in exactly the sections a board would rely on.
