7.5

The final answer meets every required section and constraint: all seven mechanisms are separated, early complexity and late queue pressure are treated as real confounders, fatigue is not the sole cause, punishment and dropping quality checks are rejected, and the dashboard includes the required indicators.

The integrated account is the strongest part. Service-time decline is split into early complex carryover, a partial mid-shift warm-up that is still mixed with falling complexity, and a late speed-up under queue pressure. The claim that errors rise only in some conditions is supported by the WIP-by-complexity table and by the experience split (juniors at 12.4% under high WIP versus seniors at 4.9%). Intervention risks and the ethical cautions are specific and operational.

The score is held down by uncorrected defects in the required analysis, not by the narrative. In the mechanism table, mean queue lengths of 62 and 88 are labeled as WIP; a later sentence assigns those numbers to queue length, but the table itself remains wrong, and those columns are distinct. The proposed code would not implement the comparisons it describes: `C(org:resource)` is invalid in a Patsy formula because `:` is the interaction operator; the within-resource group-by uses the raw complexity score and drops shift, so it does not hold complexity and shift constant; `first_error_activity` is the first activity in the case, not the first error; and the experience summary groups on raw months while the text claims a junior/mid/senior/night comparison. `discover_performance_dfg` is also unpacked into four values, which does not match the usual three-value return. Those are methodological errors in a section that was supposed to show how to test the mechanisms.
