The final answer has the required seven-part structure and the right core thesis: A/B/J can be resilient, C and G are concealed noncompliance, D decays with time, and K/L are a post-recovery compliance tail rather than real-time control. That is not enough to carry the grade. Several factual and causal errors sit on the exact judgments the task asks for.

The timeline invents baseline fill rates (~95/90/85) and T3–T4 fill rates (~85 and ~75). No such figures exist, and “hospital rebounds to ~85%” contradicts the only reported hospital fill rate, 91% in T2. T3 cycle times are miscompared: pharmacy median 38h is set against baseline p90 49h, and wholesale median 84h is set against baseline p90 72h. T4 is called near baseline while pharmacy p90 is 74h versus 49h, wholesale p90 120h versus 72h, and traceability 2.2% versus 0.8%. Conformance (0.71 fitness / 44% fitting traces; move-on-log versus move-on-model) is barely used.

The table misreads the logs. Baseline compliance-hold counts are treated as manual-phase evidence for B (“520900”), although the workaround DFG has no compliance-hold path. The 1,800 count is Manual pack → Dispatch without scan, not the largest dispatch path (Dispatch without scan → Phone POD is 2,600). C, an ambient authorization skip, is tied to the 2.9% controlled-substance dual-control gap. That 2.9% figure is then called “controlled temp doc missing,” merging it with the separate 4.8% temperature-documentation failure. G is blamed for the wholesale p90 of 180h even though skipping the scan was an acceleration path; the delay sits in deferral and Backlog queue → Manual pick list. E and J are labeled “keep” while the rationale materially changes them.

The redesign repeats those problems. A rule of two manual authorization checks per compliance staff per shift cannot coexist with a 30/60-minute check on essentially every hospital and pharmacy order; T2 already sent only 2,500 of 5,600 cases through manual authorization because capacity was scarce. Bulk customers are promised 24h in the notification and 48–72h in the priority rules. Dual control becomes optional supervisor initials. The partial-recovery regime—intake back, sync and scanning unreliable, fitness only 0.82—is not a distinct mode. Indicators omit a true throughput or cycle-time measure despite that being required, and the fairness gap is stated as both 1.5 and 2.5 points. Targets such as 1.0% traceability exceptions by day 6 are asserted as expected benefits even though stabilization was still 2.2% through day 21.

The trade-off paragraph is directionally sound, but it does not clearly state the service trade-off management must accept: slower pharmacy and bulk flow under explicit rules, never silent loss of authorization, release, temperature, or custody evidence.
