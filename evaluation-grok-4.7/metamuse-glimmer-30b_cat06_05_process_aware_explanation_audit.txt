The audit correctly catches the major leakage and the subtler lineage bugs, and it keeps the right at-t0 signals with the right signs. It is not nearly flawless: the process check contradicts itself, several required distinctions stay blurry, and one revised case drops a usable feature.

Section 1 is the strongest part. The gaps in that table are still material. `total_case_duration_hours` is not just a future covariate; it is adjacent to the 10-day breach label, and that near-tautology is never stated. `num_document_requests_total` is not fixed by a prefix count: immediately after coverage check a conforming trace has had no document cycle, so the team’s “early warning” reading is structurally empty, not merely windowed wrong. `initial_reserve` is labeled a low-severity defensible signal when its timestamp is never given. Overall this is a competent leakage audit with the right headline calls, not a weak one that treats full-trace SHAP as an early explanation. The self-contradictory process order, the blurred repair and fairness standards, and the inconsistent Case X feature handling keep it well short of a high score.
