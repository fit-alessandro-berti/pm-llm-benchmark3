7.5

The diagnosis is directionally strong and often precise, but it is not nearly flawless. Several decision sets are incomplete, one headline hit-rate is arithmetically impossible on the answer’s own terms, and a few summary claims are stricter than the evidence.

What holds up. QA as FIFO plus a soft appointment insert, with priority inert, is well supported: 08:32, 09:05, and 09:32 really do pick the oldest job over both higher priority and shorter estimates, and A03 at 08:20 is the only pure-FIFO miss. QB is correctly treated as under-identified: B02’s interrupt and B01’s resume at 09:31 support a priority-1 exception plus a FIFO/resume backbone, while 09:48 (stop timed like B05, selection = B03, log says “dispatcher reassignment”) blocks a clean preemptive-priority label. QC’s scoreboard checks out. At 10:10 and 10:18 the chosen job is the SPT/`age − est` job and not the FIFO, priority, or EDD job; at 11:40, C01 over C07 makes C07 finish at 12:34, 34 minutes past 12:00, whereas C07 first would have met both deadlines. C05 is infeasible even at arrival (10:11 + 40 min = 10:51 > 10:50). QD’s skill map is right, and 11:25 (D04 over priority-1, est-8 D08) and 11:48 (D07 over priority-1 D10) really do refute priority and SPT inside the eligible set. The `est_min` = realized duration identity on all 32 jobs, the zero idle at QB switches, and the warning against reading 08:58 or 11:35 as priority evidence are the right caveats. The pandas reconstruction matches the QC table, and PM4Py is correctly declined.

Material problems.

1. Eligibility sets violate the answer’s own rule (scanned and not yet started). At 08:32, A06 arrived at 08:29 and is omitted; the “three jobs queued” claim at 08:30–08:32 is therefore wrong. At 08:58, A08 (arrived 08:40, priority 1) is omitted. Winners do not change, but these are exactly the decision sets the task requires. At 11:20, D03 is electrical, D-X is free, and D-F is still busy until 11:21, so D03 is eligible for that instant; recorded FIFO is then D03, not D04. The overlap is only one minute, so the idle verdict survives, but the FIFO column does not.

2. “FIFO wins 6 of the 7 contested selections” is inconsistent with the same paragraph. The seven non-forced QB rows include 09:16 and 10:00, both scored as priority-1 wins and FIFO losses. That leaves at most 5/7. The 09:48 parenthetical (“B04, or … B03 under SRPT”) also muddles a comparison that, once B04’s remaining 8 is included, is simply SRPT = B04.

3. Section 7’s “three of the four queues” and the waits 45, 51, and 61 are wrong as a cross-queue claim. Those waits are A04 (45), D10 (51), and A08 (61): two queues, not three. QB’s priority-1 waits are about 2 and 14 minutes. C05’s wait is 43, not 51.

4. The dismissal of pre-calibration packing at 08:32 is invalid. “A04 would also have fit” only rejects “A02 is the unique job that fits.” From 08:32 to the 08:52 calibration, the window is 20 minutes; longest-that-fits uniquely selects A02 (20) over A04 (7), A05 (15), and the omitted A06 (12). That rival is not identified by one window, but it contaminates the episode the answer treats as a clean FIFO signature. The stated reason for ignoring it does not work.

5. “Calendar-aware” scheduling of D05 is stronger than the log. At 11:21 D-F has 39 minutes before the meeting and D05 needs 30, so D05 finishes at 11:51 if started then. FIFO, priority, and SPT all take D03 instead and only then make D05 miss the pre-meeting window. Non-splittability explains the 11:36 refusal; it does not show a forward-looking hold. The 11:36–12:00 idle also cannot be a hold for D05, because idling does not create a 30-minute block. That part the answer gets right, which makes the secondary label inconsistent with its own contradicting episode.

6. Test 3 misstates the insertable set. D09 at est 20, started at 11:20, finishes at 11:40, so it is not insertable before 11:30. The jobs that actually fit are D08 (done 11:28) and, at best, D04 (done 11:30). Both are mechanical, so H1 versus specialist-protection is still not separated, but the written set is wrong. Exception 1 then says early commitment removes aging’s “best” support by dissolving 10:32. The answer’s own 11:40 case is the stronger aging evidence and is untouched by a pre-10:25 commitment. Elsewhere, “priority refuted a third time” at 09:32 under-counts the QA refutations already listed.

7. Identifiability and confidence disagree. The table’s confidence order is QA 74, QD 70, QC 66, QB 55, but §7 calls QC the most identifiable and QA a close second. The prose distinction (QC rejects whole rule classes; the weight-1 index is a 7-point fit) is real, and the index is not unique: weights roughly in (0.23, 1.125) can keep the same QC winners. The table nevertheless promotes “maximise waiting time − est_min” to the primary discipline, and the C05 “policy switch” is a single boundary after the same infeasible job was skipped at 10:18 and 10:32. “Shelve doomed jobs” explains those skips and does not explain the 10:54 selection.

The collection plan, the labelled assumptions, the QA aging-versus-FIFO split at 08:32, and the refusal to promote site-wide priority are at the level the question asks for. The grade is held down by incorrect eligible sets, an impossible 6/7, a false cross-queue wait claim, and secondary labels that the answer’s own episodes do not identify.
