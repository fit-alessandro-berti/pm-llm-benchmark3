6.4

The write-up is organized to the required headings and is right on several hard calls: alias collapse makes CHG-508 a real drafter/approver breach; D-IC-04 is void; D-QM-01 does not cover cooling-system retrospective review; the 9 Aug PDF can support D-QM-01 while the 12 Aug migrated row cannot create earlier authority; Eva has no QM authority on 9 Aug; Asha’s leave does not by itself revoke her QM role; and `svc_qms` is handled as non-human, with only strong session evidence substituted. Collusion is not inferred from the Clara–Ben pattern. Those strengths do not offset the errors below.

CHG-505 is mishandled on two independent RR4 facts. Section 2 defines the deadline as the end of the second business day after implementation (11 Aug → end of 13 Aug). The retrospective review is 14 Aug 16:00, so it is late. The case table says “08-11→08-14 = within,” and the code’s inclusive `bdate_range` count is 3 and would print not timely. The same review is also inside Ben’s certified sick leave (13 Aug 12:00 through 14 Aug 23:59). The prompt’s account-misuse evidence covers that event as well as CHG-506; the table treats `u_bk` there as Ben and never raises impersonation. Scope failure is correctly found, but timeliness and identity of the control that is supposed to cure the emergency are both wrong or missing. Calling the Farid approval a valid substitution is also too firm: D-IC-03 is CI-44 only, and CHG-505 is not shown to be CI-44. The safety-rationale prong is unassessed.

CHG-509’s primary label should not be `VALID_SUBSTITUTION`. D-QM-02 requires Asha’s absence; leave ended 14 Aug, and a Saturday is not absence. That is `INDETERMINATE` until unavailability is shown. The code does the opposite of the prose: `asha_absent()` is false on 15 Aug, so the same delegation is marked `INVALID`.

The checking code would not reliably reproduce the SoD claims. `filter_four_eyes_principle` keeps compliant cases unless `keep_violations=True`; the prints label that output as same-person breaches, which inverts RR1/RR2 (and drops CHG-502, CHG-508, and CHG-510 from the “violation” list). `emergency` is computed and never used, so IC authority is not gated on RR4. `STANDING_IC` is unused. D-IC-03 is coded as all `cooling_system`, not CI-44 only. `filter_activity_done_different_resources` is described as a cross-case view; it is within-case repeated activity. Section 5 then overclaims that this script deterministically evaluates RR3/RR4.

Smaller gaps: RR5 and RR6 are not actually formalized in section 2; technical SoD is not consistently separated from control purpose (Eva both approves and is the strong verifier on CHG-509; Grace verifies CHG-507 with no role analysis); CHG-504 severity “Low” underweights an unresolved HIGH-risk credential-sharing question. The improvement list and the refusal to treat repeated pairs as proof are sound, but the three priority investigations omit the sick-leave status of the CHG-505 review itself.
