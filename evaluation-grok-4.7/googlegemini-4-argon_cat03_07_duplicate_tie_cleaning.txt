6.8

The duplicate decisions, PM4Py misuse analysis, and provenance design are largely sound. Exact counts, one ordering rule, and a safeguard contradict that design, so the write-up is not close to flawless.

The decision tree names every required class and the right terminal actions: drop only an exact technical duplicate, merge a reconciled same-physical observation, relabel a harmonization collision or a summary, keep distinct focal objects, and quarantine the rest. The pair table follows that policy. e03/e04 and e11/e16 are merged with lineage. e05 and e14 stay. e08/e09 are de-harmonized. e09/e10 stay ordered. e15 is treated as an N:1 rollup and is not folded into e13 and e14. Conservative retention is 15 (17−e04−e16). Evidence-based retention is 13 (also remove e07 and e15). Mechanical `ocel_merge_duplicates(..., have_common_object=True)` retention is 10, and the collapsed groups are the right ones: picks e03–e05, packs e06–e07, dispatches e08–e09, invoices e11–e16, captures e13–e15. The intersection argument is correct: `{O1,V1}` makes `have_common_object` true while `{M1}` and `{M2}` stay disjoint. The same holds for `{O1,P1}` versus `{I1}` and `{I2}`.

Event-count and activity-frequency claims match the log. Pick/Pack/Dispatch/Invoice/Capture frequencies of 3/2/2/2/3 raw, 2/1/refined/1/2 conservative, and 1/1/1/1/1 after the library merge are right. Item traces, the fabricated `(I1,I2)` pick co-occurrence, and the fabricated `(M1,M2)` capture co-occurrence are the right sensitivity story. The six named patterns are the ones whose meaning actually changes. Original timestamps, sources, and keys are retained in the design. Ingestion time is demoted. Microseconds are applied only after a sort, with a clear account of why an earlier index timedelta freezes an arbitrary row order into later DFGs and durations. The code deep-copies scenarios, qualifies activity before `ocel_drop_duplicates`, uses a custom verified-pair merge, calls `ocel_merge_duplicates` only in the warned scenario, sorts on an extra column, then calls `ocel_add_index_based_timedelta`, and checks eid/oid integrity. `ocel_raw` is left alone.

The relation arithmetic is wrong. The log has 44 E2O rows: ten events with 3 objects (30), e06 with 4, four events with 2 (8), and e01–e02 with 1 each. The answer’s 42 omits e01 and e02. After identical-set dedup, conservative is 44−3−2 = 39, not 37. Evidence-based removal of e07 and e15 removes four more subset rows, leaving 35, not 33. The aggressive unions are 4 (picks), 4 (pack), 3 (dispatch), 2 (invoice), and 4 (captures O1, V1, M1, M2), plus 11 rows on e01, e02, e10, e12, and e17, totaling 28, not 25. Those cells are presented as measured comparison results.

Ordering is inconsistent with itself. The prose says `source_seq` is incomparable across systems, then uses absorbed scanner sequence 551 versus 552 to place merged e03 before e05. The ranker never stores 551. It sorts every remaining row by global `source_seq`, so e03 (WMS 100) precedes e05 (scanner 552) only because those unrelated integers compare that way. The same paragraph cites ERP `source_seq` 40 versus 41 as a reason e11 precedes the PSP payments. That comparison orders only e11 before e15. Invoice-before-authorization is an unlabeled domain assumption. Calling e08→e09→e10 the only admissible physical chain treats a custody inference as observed fact. On this particular file the domain priorities still emit the intended conservative order, so the bug is latent.

Step 2 of the tree exits when focal instances are disjoint. For Capture Payment the focal type is Payment; e15’s payment set is empty and therefore disjoint from `{M1}` and from `{M2}`. Those pairs would be kept unchanged and would never reach the summary test, which is how section 2 actually classifies them. The empty-focal case is unspecified.

The section 9 equality safeguard conflicts with the earlier, correct e08/e09 decision. e08 and e09 already have the same activity, the same timestamp, and the same object set `{O1,P1,D1}`. Requiring `Objs(ea)=Objs(eb)` still merges the warehouse release with carrier acceptance. Equality is necessary for an identical-observation merge and insufficient for one.

Reconstruction is overstated. A bit-for-bit return to `ocel_raw` will not survive column order, relation order, dtypes, and hash concatenation. e15 is attached to both e13 and e14, and the code writes the non-eid `"e15 (shared_summary)"` over `prov:absorbed_eids`. The unpack rule `len(source_eids)>1` can emit that summary twice. The audit schema itself has the right fields; the implementation’s “field-level” merge only concatenates `payload_hash`.

Smaller distortions: flattened “17 / 15 / 13 / 10 nodes” are trace lengths, not DFG activity nodes. Raw already contains e11 before the payments; the false edge is the later duplicate e16, not a full reversal of invoicing. M2’s missing Authorize event does not establish that M2 bypassed authorization. The WMS-to-ERP ingestion gap from e06 at 10:00:05 to e07 at 10:01:00 is 55 seconds, not 60. The prose is also far past the requested 1,900–2,900 words outside the code.
