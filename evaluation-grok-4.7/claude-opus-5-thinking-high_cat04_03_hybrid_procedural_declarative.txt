6.5

The submission is a serious hybrid design: R5/R6 is treated as a real policy conflict, the layers are mostly assigned to the right kind of constraint, DECLARE direction is careful, the temporal bands and z-scores are right, and the alignment fitness figures for T5 (1 − 1/17), T6 (1 − 2/19), and T8 (1 − 2/16) match standard unit costs. It is not nearly flawless. Several claims are false, and two of them change the model.

The R10/R11 “genuine inconsistency” is not in the text. “Any later Release First Payment” means a payment after the bank change, not a second payment. One payment after re-verification satisfies both rules. That mistake is then used to drop post-activation behavior from the backbone and, in §9, to say the re-verification clause is not actually enforced, while §7’s `check_post_activation_bank_change` does enforce it inside the case.

`directly_follows` is the wrong relation. In PM4Py it is the allowed directly-follows whitelist, not “every A is immediately followed by B.” With only `(Release First Payment, Close Onboarding)` and `(Reject Vendor, Close Onboarding)`, a real `conformance_log_skeleton` run fails every test trace. The second pair is also inconsistent with this tree: `∧(escalation, Reject Vendor)` can put `Obtain Legal Opinion` between rejection and close. The statement that the matrix was produced by running the skeleton is therefore not credible, and the “consistent with the tree” claim is false.

The T8 resource/data cell is `pass`, but §7 states that `check_outcome_consistency` returns `fail` when both terminal outcomes occur. Those cannot both be the layer verdict.

Smaller technical mistakes: XOR already yields at most one `Activate Vendor` / `Reject Vendor` / `Release First Payment`, so the skeleton is not authoritative for R10 because the tree “only bounds a branch.” T5 is not in the language, but an optimal alignment is a single log move on `Request Clarification` or a single model move on `Receive Clarification`; fitness 0.941 means a final marking is reached, and `Classify Risk` is not the unique deviation. The 122400 s “deviation” on T7 is the 34 h elapsed time; the residual is 22 h (79200 s). The z-score 3.67 is correct. Assumption A9 (an opinion need not follow an escalation) is respected in DECLARE and the skeleton and silently violated by the tree, where `Obtain Legal Opinion` exists only after `Sanctions Escalation`. Resolution B’s missing-evidence result is `unverifiable` in the code and `fail` in the §8.1 summary. The explicit four-eyes routine does not implement the stated null-resource policy.

What keeps this above a weak score is the part that is actually right: strict versus exceptional R5/R6 without merging them, T6 under both variants, vacuity versus activated satisfaction, confidence ignored by `conformance_declare`, the 24 h deadline not stuffed into a two-sided profile, and T4 failing only in the resource layer.
