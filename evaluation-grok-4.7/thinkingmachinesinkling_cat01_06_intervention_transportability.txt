6.4

The advisory judgment is directionally sound: conditional go, no blanket copy, statutory ordering kept, Site A’s before/after change treated as confounded, and a digital non-legal slice proposed first. The required sections and tables are present, and the mechanism-versus-implementation split is intelligible. It is not close to flawless.

The transport graph includes the required nodes and `S_` selectors, but unsigned edges make inhibitors (`originals_required`, `IT_case_lock`, `role_specialization`) look like causes of `true_overlap`. The prose then mislabels `originals_required` itself as a selection node. `API_latency` dead-ends on an extra node and never reaches cycle time. Section 1 covers the required themes, but tail behavior is only a clause inside case mix, and dropped arrows turn comparisons into strings such as “871% overlap increase.”

Quantitative transport is the clearest failure. “8 to 12 h (18–27% of A’s observed reduction)” is arithmetically false: 18–27% of the 9-hour change is about 1.6–2.4 hours. The same write-up then scales a median reduction by eligible share to get an overall median, which medians do not do, and the optimistic row (8–12 h, 55% eligible) contradicts the later 3–6 h structural and 1.5–2.5 h overall claims. The joint distribution behind the 20–25% “digital and non-legal” share is not in the data and is not carried as an assumption.

The Site B partial order is formally invalid: XOR outcomes and a supplement loop are written as strict-order edges, including a cycle through document check and assessment. The PM4Py block repeats that semantics. Legal review is mandatory, both Approve and Reject are ordered after the loop, and the loop does not return to document check or assessment. Constructors are also non-executable against the real POWL API (`order=` on `StrictPartialOrder`, `OperatorPOWL.LOOP`, labeled `SilentTransition`, `POWL(nodes=...)`), and the core order object is never used.

The pilot’s identification story is better than its design math. Two hundred cases per stratum per arm cannot fit in about 1,100 six-to-ten-week cases, especially in a ~20% stratum. Case-level randomization on shared specialist queues does not control the interference the scenarios themselves predict. Section 9 is roughly 700 words against a 250–400 limit.
