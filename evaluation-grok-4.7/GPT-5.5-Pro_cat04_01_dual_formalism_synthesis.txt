The models are largely the right ones: the POWL partial order matches the cross-branch narrative, the loop is the correct do/redo shape `Execute–Inspect (Adjust Execute–Inspect)*`, optionality and the risk split are control-flow choices rather than data, and the process tree is an explicit conservative under-approximation with a witness (A4) that actually separates the two languages. Verdicts on A1–A5 and F1–F3 are consistent with those semantics, including shuffle of reviews inside a trial body, and the code follows the stated constructors (`parent`/`children`, `OperatorPOWL`, `order.add_edge`).

Points lost for imprecision, not for a broken model. The concurrency write-up does not cover every unordered pair: containment versus design, the risk route, and supplier consultation are left implicit. A2 and A5 overstate overlap — in A2 regulatory review is after `Inspect Trial`, and in A5 `Consult Supplier` is after the trial body — so the reasons are looser than the traces. “Duplicate-label underconstraint” muddles labeling ambiguity with language inclusion. The self-critique mostly restates limits already declared (phase cut, missing pass/fail data, completion interleavings) and never tests whether another duplicate-free series-parallel cut would drop less intended behavior while staying safe.
