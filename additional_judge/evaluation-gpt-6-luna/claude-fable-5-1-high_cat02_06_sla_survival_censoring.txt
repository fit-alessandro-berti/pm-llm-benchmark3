The submission directly addresses the requested process-aware survival analysis and follows the required nine-heading format. It distinguishes unresolved-by-72-hours from resolution timing, separates censoring from competing outcomes, discusses transfer assumptions and open cases, and covers process features, time-dependent leakage, sensitivity analyses, operational use, code, and limitations.

However, the numerical results and code contain material inconsistencies. The script’s transfer scenarios and reported E1 estimates do not align: the “transfer is resolution” scenario is reported as 0.687, lower than the competing-risk scenario’s 0.696, although counting transfers as timely success should not increase the estimated breach rate under its stated definition. The bound calculation also treats every transfer as a known non-breach, even when transfer is not being counted as resolution. The reported cohort and priority summaries are asserted without showing their output, and the small sample does not support the precision implied by some of the point estimates.

These issues weaken the quantitative conclusions, but the conceptual framework is strong and appropriately cautious about causal interpretation and informative censoring.
