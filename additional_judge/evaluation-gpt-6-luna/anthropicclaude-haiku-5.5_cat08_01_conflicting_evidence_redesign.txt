79/100 — **A strong, evidence-based set of redesigns with explicit trade-offs and useful implementation detail.**

The response offers three materially different approaches: segment-based triage, evidence completion before decisions, and pre-authorized repair options with complaint recovery. It ties them to specific process evidence, including the 22% complaint rate for Premium high-value fast-path cases, repeated evidence requests, wrong-part complaints, repair rework, and resource bottlenecks. It also addresses compliance rules R1–R3, risks of moving bottlenecks, and a staged pilot and measurement plan. The response appropriately avoids claiming that one redesign dominates without the missing cost and routing data.

The main weakness is that some proposed thresholds and implementation details appear more precise than the supplied evidence supports, and the recommendation could synthesize the three options into a clearer target operating design. Still, it recognizes the contradictory evidence and makes the trade-offs concrete rather than optimizing for speed alone.
