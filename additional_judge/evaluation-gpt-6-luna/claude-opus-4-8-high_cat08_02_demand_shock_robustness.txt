6.5/10 — The response gives a strong, scenario-aware redesign with explicit triggers, bottleneck migration, trade-offs, and monitoring. It uses the process data to identify several important risks, including repeat loops, validator shortages, specialist absences, and downstream overload.

The main weakness is that several recommendations are under-justified or potentially unsafe as stated. In particular, routing all Routine work to M2 risks worsening repeats, while the proposed fallback and outsourcing rules do not establish enough capacity or quality controls. Some thresholds and claims about which targets can absorb a 12-hour delay are asserted without support from the supplied data. The response also omits a clear, end-to-end operating policy for balancing all four case types when multiple disruptions overlap.
