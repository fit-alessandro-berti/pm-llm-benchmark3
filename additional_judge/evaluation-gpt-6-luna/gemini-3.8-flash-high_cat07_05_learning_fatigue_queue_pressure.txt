The code also assumes PM4Py function signatures and batch object structure without qualification, risks incorrectly mapping discovered batches to dataframe indices, and does not specify right censoring for seven-day rework. The intervention section is decisive where the evidence warrants more caution. In particular, rejecting complex-case redistribution and random assignment as categorically harmful overlooks skill-based routing with safeguards and balanced allocation as alternatives. The dashboard invents thresholds and formulas without support, and the Gini measure can confuse workload fairness with equal distribution of complexity. Some service-time and waiting-time calculations also risk conflating event duration, case waiting, and queue delay.

Overall, this is comprehensive and well organized, but overconfident causal interpretation and technical imprecision substantially weaken it.
