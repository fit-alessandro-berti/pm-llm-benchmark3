The answer is organized and engages directly with the representational tradeoff, but its central claim that the POWL is exact is not supported by the model it constructs.

The main semantic problem is that `APP` and `SUP` are atomic child nodes in the root partial order. The edges `ARC → APP → RM` and `ARC → SUP → RM` constrain each whole submodel as a unit. That requires the entire approval route and supplier consultation to occur after `Analyze Root Cause` and finish before `Release Material`. The narrative permits supplier consultation to overlap with corrective design, planning, approvals, and trial work; representing it as one atomic submodel prevents that overlap in the POWL. Similarly, the approval route is constrained as a whole relative to other root nodes, rather than exposing its constituent reviews to the cross-branch ordering needed to express the intended interleavings. The answer’s claims of exactness and its concurrency explanation overstate what those edges encode.

There is also a specific error in the process-tree discussion: it says the tree can admit `Consult Supplier` after `Release Material`. The supplier XOR is inside the parallel obligations block, and that entire block must complete before release, so this tree cannot admit that ordering. It does admit supplier consultation before `Analyze Root Cause`, which is the actual missing constraint.

The witnesses are mostly useful, but T5 is not the requested tree-approximation witness; T6 serves that purpose. T8 gives the earliest violation as release before approval, but because `Consult Supplier` is optional, omitting it is valid and does not affect that verdict.

The answer meets the requested structure and includes substantial specification, code, comparison, validation, and self-critique. However, its dependency table sometimes treats relations at a submodel level as if they were independently realized activity-level constraints, and the POWL construction does not preserve the stated supplier concurrency. Those are consequential modeling errors, so the “language-exact POWL” conclusion is not warranted.
