The answer uses the DFG and queue evidence to identify changing constraints across intake, correction, assay, technical validation, medical validation, and specialist review. It addresses Urgent SLA risk while protecting minimum capacity for Routine and Complex-panel work, and it includes disruption triggers, monitoring signals, and residual vulnerabilities.
