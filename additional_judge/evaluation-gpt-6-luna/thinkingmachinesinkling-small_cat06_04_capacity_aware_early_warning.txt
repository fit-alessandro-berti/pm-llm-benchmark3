The response follows the requested six-section structure, addresses every case, and discusses capacity, repeated alerts, calibration, fairness, spillovers, and monitoring. It also correctly distinguishes breach probability from intervention benefit and recognizes that I-03 is past the swarm’s useful stage.

However, the decision rule and calculations have material inconsistencies. Several probability-adjustment formulas are malformed or unclear, and the response uses an ad hoc adjustment of `p_now` that is not justified by the supplied calibration notes. The portfolio’s benefit arithmetic is also unreliable: the stated selected cases and scenario totals do not consistently account for effect ranges, false-alarm burdens, intervention costs, or the spillover exposure to six untreated cases. Some safeguards and thresholds are asserted without a clear basis, and the proposed policy sometimes treats uncertain observational estimates as if they support precise operational cutoffs.

Overall, this is broad and operationally aware, but the core allocation and expected-impact analysis are not sufficiently auditable or internally coherent.
