4.2/10. The answer follows the requested structure and gives case-by-case conclusions, but several material rule errors undermine its analysis.

- The rule catalogue reverses or muddles key conditions. Its pseudo-logic says Finance approval is required “unless amount >= 50000,” and Compliance clearance is required “unless new_supplier OR amount > 100000”—the opposite of the triggers in GPIP-4. It also treats requester and committer being the same as the A1 violation, although A1 prohibits either from being the *final business approver*.
- It omits the independent budget-owner requirement in A2 and mishandles the local Plant approval requirement. That materially affects P-501, P-503, P-506, and P-508.
- It treats CFO email D1 as clearly substituting for Finance approval, despite its ambiguity, D4’s integrity caveat, and the absence of an express rule that Incident Command authorization replaces the required independent budget owner. P-508 is especially weak: D1’s threshold is “up to EUR 150,000,” while this purchase is EUR 155,000. Legal clearance under D2 does not establish that Finance approval is waived.
- P-501’s “satisfied rules” list says the PO timing rule was satisfied, though B4’s emergency exception is subject to its own conditions and the Plant Manager was reachable with no delegation. The assessment should distinguish the timing exception from the failed substitute-approver condition.
- The pseudo-logic contains additional unsupported reversals, such as treating post-commitment approval as normally curative and verbal directive extensions as normally valid. Those contradict the source rules and the answer’s stated method.
- Some remediation is overconfident: terminating a supplier engagement or reallocating costs is recommended without establishing that those actions are available or appropriate.

The answer does handle the outage evidence sensibly for P-505 and P-507, and it recognizes ECD-2’s limited scope and the difference between late evidence and late action. But the rule errors and unsupported treatment of Finance authority leave several primary classifications insufficiently grounded.
