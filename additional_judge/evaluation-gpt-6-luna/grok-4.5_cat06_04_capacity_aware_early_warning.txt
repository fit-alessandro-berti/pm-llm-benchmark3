6/10

The response follows the required six-section structure and addresses eligibility, repeated alerts, calibration differences, uncertainty, spillovers, fairness, and monitoring. It makes a deployable portfolio decision: intervene on I-01, I-04, and I-07, and reserve one slot. It also gives scenario ranges and describes safeguards for selective labels and spillover harms.

The main weakness is that several calculations and rules are not fully auditable or consistently applied. The portfolio’s “direct breaches plausibly avoided” figures do not clearly match the selected cases’ stated effect ranges, and the economic ranges mix direct benefit, false-alarm burden, and spillover assumptions without showing the underlying case-level arithmetic. I-04’s Strategic Enterprise overprediction is acknowledged, but the proposed calibration adjustment is not specified enough to reproduce its priority score. The fairness floor and escalation criteria are also asserted without a clear rationale for their thresholds.

Overall, it is operationally thoughtful and covers the requested risks, but the quantitative decision rule and impact estimates need tighter, more consistent support.
