The response is a strong, decision-oriented memo. It recommends a segment-limited hybrid rollout, explains why faster median time and fewer handovers can coexist with worse tail outcomes and quality, and separates observed facts from interpretations and uncertainties in a useful evidence table. It also compares all five alternatives and prioritizes measurements around the main uncertainties.

The recommendation is defensible, but it presents some operational choices and rollout thresholds—such as an 85% coordinator-utilization cap and specific performance gates—as if they were established, without deriving them from the supplied evidence. The memo could also distinguish more clearly between evidence that suggests a cause and evidence that demonstrates one, given the differing regions and incomplete early-pilot attributes.
