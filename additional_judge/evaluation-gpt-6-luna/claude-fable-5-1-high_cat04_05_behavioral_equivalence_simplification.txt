Several errors materially undermine the claimed precision:

- The P1 abstraction is misdefined. P1 requires a check for each analysis before the next rework or close; it does not say to forget the order of analysis and check within an attempt. Also, “position of P relative to A/C” cannot be preserved while their relative order is forgotten without a more precise abstraction.
- The assertion that the two models have “duplicate-labelled leaves” count 0 is reasonable structurally, but the explanation that each event maps to exactly one leaf overlooks repeated executions of the same leaf in a loop. Occurrences are position-distinguished in traces, not uniquely attributable to a leaf occurrence.
- The stochastic section claims a uniform playout assumption and gives specific observed EMD values without establishing that the code declares or implements those probabilities. The PM4Py calls shown also do not specify the claimed sample count or uniform decision policy.

Counting the displayed trees gives 12 nodes, but the listed depths are inconsistent with the operator nesting: Model A’s deepest leaf is at depth 5 (root, loop, sequence, XOR, leaf), and Model B’s deepest leaf is at depth 5. The claimed simplified-model depth of 2 is also inconsistent with its nested sequence/loop tree.
