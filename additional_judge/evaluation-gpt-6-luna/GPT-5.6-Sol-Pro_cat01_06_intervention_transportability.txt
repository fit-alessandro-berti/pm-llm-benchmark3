Several issues keep it short of excellent:

It introduces `legal_review_required`, `timestamp_reliability`, and `observed_overlap` without listing them among the required variables, while omitting `appeal` as an outcome’s direct causes beyond `rework` and not including `treatment_parallelization -> cycle_time` except through mediators. The provided evidence does not establish the proposed median or p90 changes, and some scenarios make confident-looking subgroup or tail predictions without an explicit derivation from available distributions. The answer appropriately labels them as assumptions, but could more clearly distinguish illustrative planning ranges from evidence-based estimates.

These limitations are acknowledged, but the code’s checks and playout cannot validate the real conditional process.

The sample-size thresholds lack a stated power calculation or baseline variance, and the diagnostic policy-only arm may expose cases to the known case-lock problem.

If those sections were absent in the actual answer, that would be a major format and completeness failure.

Overall, the reasoning and practical design are thoughtful, but uncertain quantitative claims, incomplete graph specification, and a control-flow model that abstracts away essential routing rules prevent a higher grade.
