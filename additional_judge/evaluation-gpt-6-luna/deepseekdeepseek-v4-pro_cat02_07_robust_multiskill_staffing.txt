The response follows much of the requested structure and proposes three operational policies, a hybrid recommendation, scenario-specific bottlenecks, monitoring indicators, and a validation plan. It shows some useful load calculations and acknowledges uncertainty and simulation limitations.

The main weakness is that the scenario comparison does not provide the required policy-by-scenario table with estimates for mean and tail delay, SLA attainment, utilization, fairness, handover/setup burden, resilience, and failure mode. Several conclusions about SLAs and utilization are therefore asserted without auditable supporting calculations. The offered-load analysis is uneven across skills and shocks, and the simulation scaffold omits key mechanics—including true parallel execution, preemption penalties, handovers, and breaks—so it cannot substantiate the comparative claims. There are also inconsistencies in the proposed pilot, including keeping all P1 cases under the current policy while evaluating the new policy’s P1 performance.
