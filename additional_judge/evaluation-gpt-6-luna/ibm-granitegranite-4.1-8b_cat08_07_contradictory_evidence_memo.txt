5/10 — The response presents a defensible hybrid rollout: keep the redesign for simple, low-risk homes, pause risky changes elsewhere, and gather more evidence before broader expansion. It connects the worsening tail, rework, complaints, and model precision to risks hidden by improved medians and fewer handovers. Its measurement priorities and segment-level reasoning are useful.

It also does not clearly address the pilot’s regional and case-mix differences as limits on causal attribution. A tighter experiment plan with explicit comparison groups, decision thresholds tied to business impact, and a clearer distinction between observed facts and hypotheses would make the memo more actionable.
