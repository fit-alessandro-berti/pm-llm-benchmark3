4/5 — The prompt gives detailed case facts, policy rules, alignment notation, and repeated-pattern evidence, while correctly warning against treating raw cost as business severity. It also supplies a clear required structure and asks for case-by-case interpretations, confidence, remediation, and prioritization.

The main weakness is that the requested output is very large: twelve full case rows plus pattern analysis, remediation, and prioritization. That raises the chance of inconsistent severity judgments or omitting a required distinction. The prompt also leaves room for reasonable disagreement in severity, though it explicitly tells the evaluator to accept that rather than expect a canonical ranking.
