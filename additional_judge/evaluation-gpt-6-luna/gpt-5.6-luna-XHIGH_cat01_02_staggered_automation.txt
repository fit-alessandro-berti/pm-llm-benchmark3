This is a strong, careful evaluation: it follows the required structure, distinguishes availability from uptake, recognizes endogenous rollout and interference, separates median from tail outcomes, and recommends a randomized phase-in with operational guardrails. It also gives useful arithmetic contrasts and repeatedly cautions that they are not causal estimates.

The main deduction is for quantitative and methodological precision. The stated adopter changes and control comparisons are not consistently auditable from the response: for example, the claim that Gamma’s median change exceeded the never-treated comparison by about 3.9 hours is questionable against the supplied branch values and chosen windows. Some scenario ranges are also presented with more precision than these aggregate data support. The primary event-study plan is sensible in principle, but five branches and the short pre-period leave little practical support for its diagnostics or conventional inference. Finally, the reproducible code provides a descriptive TWFE event-time regression, but does not implement the proposed cohort-specific estimator, and branch-clustered standard errors with five clusters remain unreliable despite the warning.

The answer is appropriately cautious overall, but the arithmetic discrepancies and gap between the proposed primary design and the code prevent a higher grade.
