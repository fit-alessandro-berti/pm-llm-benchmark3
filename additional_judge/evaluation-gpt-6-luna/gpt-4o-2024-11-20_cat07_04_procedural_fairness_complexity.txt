The strategy also needs clearer separation of descriptive disparity from causal claims, better handling of covariates that may be affected by inequity, and comparisons within comparable paths and resource conditions. The PM4Py pseudocode is too loose to be reliably executable: it treats variant IDs as filter inputs, uses performance and waiting-time functions without showing their required data or parameters, and does not demonstrate group-specific filtering or declarative checks.
