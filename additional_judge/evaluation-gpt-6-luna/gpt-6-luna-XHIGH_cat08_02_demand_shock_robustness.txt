The response follows the required seven-section structure and gives a grounded, multi-scenario redesign. The response could also distinguish more carefully between order counts and actual assay workload throughout, given the flattened log.

These are meaningful but limited issues. The answer is coherent, cautious about downstream effects, and substantially meets the request.
