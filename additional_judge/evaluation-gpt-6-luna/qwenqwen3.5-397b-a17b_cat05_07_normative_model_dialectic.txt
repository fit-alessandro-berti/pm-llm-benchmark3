8/10 — The response gives a substantial, evidence-linked analysis, distinguishes policy authority from operational practice, and recognizes uncertainty around scope, logging, and reactivation. Its proposed policy overlays and data improvements are useful.

The main weakness is the revised POWL construction: it acknowledges that its control-flow encoding is approximate, but the final model still has conflicting or duplicate decision structures and does not faithfully encode the approved-versus-reject XOR, optional repeated information requests, or the different timing rules for standard, pilot, and emergency cases. The response’s case-by-case judgments are generally careful, though the model proposal falls short of its stated semantic goals.
