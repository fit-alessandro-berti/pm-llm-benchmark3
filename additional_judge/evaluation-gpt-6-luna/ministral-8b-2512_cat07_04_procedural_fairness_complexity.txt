4/10 — The response recognizes that raw outcome gaps alone do not establish unfairness and considers adjusted disparities, routing, delays, and resource allocation. But it overstates the evidence by declaring the process procedurally unfair while also saying the causes are indeterminate. It does not adequately distinguish justified procedural safeguards from avoidable burdens or explain how to test that distinction. Its PM4Py plan also contains likely errors: `filter_event_attribute_values` is used as though it filters cases, and the variant filtering and `discover_declare` calls appear incompatible with the described inputs. The recommendations are relevant but generic and not tied to a clear audit design or measurable fairness criteria.
