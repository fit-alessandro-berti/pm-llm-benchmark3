**4/5** — Strong, well-grounded multi-criteria answer. It proposes phased changes tied to observed paths and attributes, protects service with lead-time slack and reliability thresholds, and addresses quality, resilience, obsolescence, rebound, and displacement risks. Its validation plan includes segment-level guardrails and conformance monitoring.

The main weakness is a few unsupported quantitative claims, especially that rework and oversized batching account for “≈85–90%” of energy and scrap; the provided evidence does not establish that share. Some proposed mechanisms, such as forward stocking and shared emergency lanes, also need clearer estimates of their costs and trade-offs.
