The answer shows strong awareness of stage eligibility, stale versus evidence-changing alerts, calibration differences, intervention uncertainty, and spillover harms. Its monitoring and feedback-loop discussion is extensive. However, the final allocation and impact arithmetic are not sufficiently reliable for a high grade.

The expected-impact table and summary claim a base net value of about €12.4k and spillover harm of about €3.7k, but the supplied material does not make those figures readily auditable. The answer’s proposed spillover cap, randomization rates, and several threshold triggers are presented with unwarranted specificity and are not justified from the case data. Its calibration adjustments are also applied to breach probabilities without clearly distinguishing those adjusted probabilities from the supplied causal-effect ranges.

Overall, it addresses most required topics, but the decision and its quantitative justification are too ambiguous and over-precise to demonstrate dependable portfolio-level reasoning.
