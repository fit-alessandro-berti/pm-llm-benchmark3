4/5 — Strong design: it separates structural process mining from simulation, accounts for calendars, nonstationary arrivals, conditional routing, rework, linked cases, censoring, paired scenarios, and uncertainty. The policy experiments and assumptions are grounded in the supplied context, and the proposed PM4Py use is appropriately limited to diagnostics and process structure.

The main weakness is that much of the simulation is left as `NotImplementedError`, so the answer is a detailed architecture and experiment plan rather than an executable digital twin. It also relies on calibrated parameter providers without showing how they would be estimated or validated against the log.
