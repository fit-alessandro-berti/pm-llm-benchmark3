The response follows the required seven-section structure and gives a clear conditional recommendation to pilot first. It connects the proposed changes to plausible process effects, distinguishes optimistic, central, and adverse scenarios, and covers the requested risks, indicators, pilot design, and PM4Py analysis.

The main weakness is that some forecasts and thresholds are asserted without enough grounding in the supplied baseline. The pilot comparison is also confounded: East differs from North and South before the change, so a simple team comparison would not isolate the restructuring’s effect. A phased design with matched cases or a staggered rollout, plus a clearer method for measuring fairness and role clarity, would strengthen the evaluation.
