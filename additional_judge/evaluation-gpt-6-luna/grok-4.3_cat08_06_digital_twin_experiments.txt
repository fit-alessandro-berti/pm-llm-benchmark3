It also correctly treats PM4Py as a source of process-mining analytics rather than a discrete-event simulator.

The proposed warm-up and 24-month run also need reconsideration for a finite-horizon replay and projection, where initial backlog and seasonal phase matter. Calibration is described generally, but the design should more clearly distinguish observed service time from waiting time given missing start timestamps, and specify how arrival bursts and correlated cases will be estimated and validated.

The experiment matrix is useful, though some interventions need explicit policy constraints and outcome safeguards, especially inspection removal and environmental triage.
