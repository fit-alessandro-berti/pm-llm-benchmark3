The response follows the requested seven-heading structure and covers the main mechanisms, scenarios, risks, pilot design, recommendation, and a PM4Py sketch. It connects several organizational changes to observable process measures and appropriately recommends piloting before wider rollout.

The forecasts are mostly generic and sometimes assert unsupported numbers, such as a 10% cycle-time improvement and a 2% rework reduction. They do not use the supplied handover frequencies and waits to quantify the expected tradeoffs—for example, more referrals to technical and legal/risk queues alongside fewer cross-team handovers. Several measures are also mismatched to the claims: resource similarity does not measure utilization, and rework-per-activity does not measure SLA misses. The pilot lacks a clear allocation method and its rollback thresholds are not grounded in the provided baseline. The analysis sketch also has issues, including filtering event attributes for a case-level complexity field and omitting the requested decision, fairness, and queue measures from the analysis.
