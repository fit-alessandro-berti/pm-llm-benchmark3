8.5/10 — Strong, evidence-rich response that follows the requested structure and offers three meaningfully different redesigns: policy-based routing, assisted evidence completion, and fulfillment quality control. It uses specific DFG, performance, conformance, rework, segment, and capacity findings; handles trade-offs; avoids unsupported ROI claims; and proposes a staged pilot with useful monitoring and stop/go criteria.

A few issues keep it from a higher score. The response is very long and repeats some findings across sections. Some redesign mechanisms overstate what the supplied evidence establishes: for example, a pre-dispatch gate is said to support R1 and R2, though those controls must be enforced at approval and evidence-completion stages as well. The claim that the 1,430 missing-evidence entries are “entries” also sums two incoming edges that may not represent distinct cases. Finally, the experiment thresholds are mostly qualitative, and the plan could be clearer about how to evaluate the third redesign independently from the first two.
