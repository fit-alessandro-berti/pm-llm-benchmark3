9/10 — The response is coherent, operationally grounded, and directly addresses the request. It compares eligible cases using expected-value ranges, adjusts for calibration and data quality, handles repeat alerts, accounts for spillovers and a reserve slot, and lays out optimistic, base, and adverse scenarios alongside monitoring and causal-effect safeguards.

The main weakness is that the final allocation is not fully decisive: it recommends two routine swarms (I-02 and I-09) and a conditional third (I-07), but does not clearly specify which cases get the four available slots under each scenario or give a compact portfolio comparison of the alternatives. It also leaves some calibration adjustments qualitative.
