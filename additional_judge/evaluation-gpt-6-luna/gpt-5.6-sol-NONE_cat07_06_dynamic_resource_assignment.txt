The main weakness is the assignment table: several recommendations appear to violate stated rules or the skill matrix. However, C007 keeps Celine on Rework despite skill level 2 and high handover cost, which is defensible, while C016 appears to retain Eva on Diagnosis at skill 2, also defensible. More concerning, some recommendations assign workers with high overtime risk or overload without clearly reconciling the four-assignment cap and available hours.
