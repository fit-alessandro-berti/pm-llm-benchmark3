6.5

The response is strong and covers all six required sections, with a case-by-case allocation, explicit scenario arithmetic, repeated-alert handling, fairness safeguards, and a detailed monitoring plan. It adjusts probabilities for known calibration differences and distinguishes model predictions from uncertain intervention effects.

The main weakness is that the allocation rule and selected portfolio appear overly dependent on midpoint expected value and uncapped case value, despite acknowledged spillover and fairness concerns. The adverse scenario and spillover arithmetic are useful but rely on uncertain assumptions, and the response could more clearly distinguish direct expected benefits from portfolio-wide risk changes. It also proposes randomized withholding and several specific monitoring thresholds without much discussion of sample-size or operational feasibility.
