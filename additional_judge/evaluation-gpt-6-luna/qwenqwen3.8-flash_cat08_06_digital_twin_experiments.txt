8.2/10 — The answer covers the required sections and handles non-stationary arrivals, calendars, rework, cross-case dependencies, missing data, uncertainty, and the D1–D10 experiments in a grounded way. The PM4Py skeleton uses the requested conventions and clearly separates log handling from simulation.

The main weaknesses are that the calendar and resource classes do not actually enforce working hours or interruptions, and the simulation engine is only a placeholder. Some decision thresholds and intervention effects are asserted without calibration, and the timestamp proxy is described imprecisely: completion-to-completion time includes the current activity’s service time as well as waiting and calendar gaps.
