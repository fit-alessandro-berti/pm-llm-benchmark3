The main deductions are for technical and semantic gaps in the proposed third model:

For example, the answer calls C’s B6 rejection a false rejection without clarifying that C’s exact language simply lacks that conditional case, and describes some interpretations as hard enforcement rules despite unresolved stakeholder detail.

These are meaningful shortcomings in the most consequential part of the answer: whether the proposed alternative actually enforces the policy it claims to encode. The analysis of the original models, metrics, and governance trade-offs is otherwise strong.
