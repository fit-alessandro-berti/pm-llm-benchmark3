6.5/10 — The response is unusually thorough and meets the six-section format, covering stage eligibility, calibration adjustments, capacity and reserve choices, repeated alerts, fairness, spillovers, and a detailed monitoring and causal-estimation plan. It gives concrete safeguards and acknowledges that the intervention effects are uncertain.

The main weakness is that its elaborate scoring and portfolio arithmetic appear more precise than the supplied evidence supports. It imposes extra assumptions—such as runway and stage discounts, a €1,800 spillover charge per slot, and fairness quotas—without clearly grounding them in the prompt, and the selected portfolio’s net impact depends heavily on those assumptions. The adverse scenario also seems to mix worst-case effect bounds with the proposed gates, making its interpretation difficult to audit. Overall, it is operationally thoughtful, but the decision rule and impact estimates would be stronger with fewer invented parameters and clearer sensitivity analysis.
