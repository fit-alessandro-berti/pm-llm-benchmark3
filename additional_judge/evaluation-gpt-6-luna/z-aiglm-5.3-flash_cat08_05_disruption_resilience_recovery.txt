**9/10**

The response gives a strong, evidence-based account of the incident’s timeline, distinguishes governed workarounds from concealed control failures, and proposes a detailed recovery design with operational gates, metrics, and trade-offs. It uses the process data to identify where throughput was preserved and where risk accumulated, especially around authorization, dispatch evidence, inventory reconciliation, and the persistent audit-remediation queue.

The main limitation is that the response’s many precise thresholds and some case-level estimates are not clearly derived from the supplied data. Those recommendations are plausible, but should be labeled as proposed targets or assumptions rather than established findings.
