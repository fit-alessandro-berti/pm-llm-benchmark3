Several material issues keep it from a higher grade:

F01 saves 5 minutes, but F11 saves 0 because B’s 40-minute offset still exceeds A’s new 40-minute offset only if the tie rule favors A; either way the join time remains at 40. The aggregate saving is 5 minutes, not 15.

The response cites 42% in one place and suggests utilization is low based on “240 min against ~570 min of two-resource capacity,” without establishing the capacity window or denominator. The claimed cases likely to reflect congestion are also speculative.

The code also omits a separate printed aggregate branch-comparison table and does not print synchronization summaries.

The central interpretation is defensible, but counterfactual arithmetic and some aggregate claims are wrong, and the PM4Py portion is uncertain.
