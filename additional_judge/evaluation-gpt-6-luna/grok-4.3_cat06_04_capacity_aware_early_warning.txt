The answer follows the required six-section structure and gets several central points right: it excludes I-03 because the swarm is ineffective after Diagnosis Complete, suppresses I-05’s unchanged repeat alerts, treats I-07’s new log as relevant evidence, and acknowledges spillovers and segment uncertainty. But the policy and impact estimates contain serious inconsistencies.

The priority formula is malformed: it omits an operator between risk reduction × value and the subtracted costs, and it divides by “expected spillover cases affected” without defining that quantity or relating it to spillover harm. The stated scores and decisions are therefore not auditable. The calibration adjustments are also applied as fixed corrections without explaining their limits; the “0.10 conservative discount” for Non-Native-Language Support is ambiguous and is not a sound substitute for handling uncertainty.

The selected portfolio does not reconcile with the stated one-slot reserve. Four cases are selected while one of four remaining slots is reserved. The required scenario arithmetic is also unreliable: the base midpoint reductions listed for the selected cases sum to about €20.2k in gross benefit using the given case values, not €23.5k. The spillover amounts are asserted without showing how many untreated diagnosis-stage cases are affected or how their breach costs are valued. The adverse case does not clearly translate the specified effect and congestion assumptions into its estimates.

The temporal policy is internally inconsistent: the decision rule says suppressed alerts are reconsidered only when a new event occurs, while the 90-minute rule allows reconsideration after a cool-down. The fairness safeguards are concrete, but an arbitrary daily reservation and an undefined “0.10 discount” are not justified. Monitoring also relies on a matched observational comparison that may remain confounded, and it omits a clear method for handling intervention effects and labels in causal estimation.

These issues undermine the core decision-theoretic and operational reasoning, despite the answer’s useful structure and recognition of several key risks.
