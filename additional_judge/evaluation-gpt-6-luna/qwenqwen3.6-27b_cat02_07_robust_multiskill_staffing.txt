The answer follows the nine-heading format and covers the requested policy types, operational risks, monitoring fields, and pilot design. Its main weakness is that the quantitative analysis and code do not support its conclusions.

Overall, the response is broad and well organized, but unsupported scenario metrics, incorrect calculations, and a simulation scaffold that omits or misimplements core mechanics substantially reduce its reliability.
