The response also labels operational policy contrasts as separable effects without establishing the required component interventions or assumptions.

The code preserves absent stages as missing, but its `last_stage_done` calculation and event-level DFG intervals need careful interpretation; DFG edge performance is not itself a clean stage waiting-time measure.
- The pilot has unsupported operational assumptions and arbitrary thresholds, including the risk-score eligibility cutoff, queue limit, false-approval stopping threshold, and claims about resource hours. These are not justified by the supplied data.

It does satisfy much of the requested structure and discusses interference, mediation, quality, falsification, and reversal conditions. But the fabricated rule and numerical errors affect core conclusions and policy advice, so the answer is only partially dependable.
