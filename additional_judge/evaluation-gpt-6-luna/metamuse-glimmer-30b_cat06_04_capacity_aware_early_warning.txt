The response follows the required six-section structure, covers all ten cases, selects a portfolio of three cases with one slot reserved, and addresses repeated alerts, calibration, fairness, spillovers, and monitoring. It shows scenario arithmetic and acknowledges uncertainty.

The main weakness is that some calculations and decisions are inconsistent or insufficiently supported. For example, its base spillover estimate does not clearly account for the number of used slots and affected cases, and the stated adverse direct benefit range conflicts with the accompanying lower-bound calculation. It also intervenes on I-07 despite a low expected net benefit and does not fully reconcile that choice with its prioritization rule.
