6.5/10 — The response covers the requested survival-analysis concepts in detail and follows the required nine-heading format, but it has important inconsistencies in its event definitions and code.

It correctly distinguishes resolution from other terminal outcomes, discusses competing risks and informative censoring, warns about immortal-time and leakage bias, and proposes process-aware features and operational checks. It also notes the limits of the small, single-cutoff extract and the lack of event timestamps.

However, the code does not implement its stated “probability of not being resolved by 72 hours” as a standard survival estimate. It calculates one minus the Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence of resolution, which includes competing outcomes; that is not generally the same as the probability of unresolved status at 72 hours. The code also labels transfers as an event in the “no disposition” calculation under some modes, although transfer may end internal observation without being an internal disposition. The response’s treatment of transfers as “success” in one scenario is also hard to reconcile with the scenario’s definition of operational success as reaching `Resolve`.

The result is a thoughtful and useful analysis plan, but the estimands, labels, and implementation need tighter alignment before the numerical outputs can be trusted.
