9.0 — Excellent and unusually thorough. It follows the required structure, addresses all seven mechanisms, evaluates all eight interventions, and gives detailed analysis and dashboard plans. It is especially strong on confounding, case mix, queue pressure, measurement limits, and fair attribution.

A few issues keep it from a near-perfect score: the PM4Py examples may rely on version-specific signatures and custom helper functions, so they are not directly runnable; the proposed models and many dashboard indicators require data not listed in the event log; and the answer is so extensive that its main conclusions can be hard to locate. These are modest shortcomings in an otherwise careful, well-supported evaluation.
