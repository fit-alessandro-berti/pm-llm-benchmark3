It also misses stronger alternatives, including G1’s fixture batch versus common start, G2’s group-start workflow, and G4/G8’s clear sequential timing patterns.

The recommendations are generally sensible, but the final adjudication inherits the unsupported certainty and does not consistently distinguish what can be inferred from the fragments from what requires validation.
