The answer is thorough and handles event-time state, blank cells, late evidence, unknown values, same-timestamp ordering, and the limits of control-flow-only conformance well. Its case analysis and logging recommendations are generally thoughtful.

The largest weakness is the checking code: its coarse logic does not faithfully implement several rules. For example, it marks R3 as untriggered when there was no prior screen, despite the required screening logic being underspecified; its R1 validity test uses a broad “last material change” marker that includes changes not necessarily relevant to R1; and its R2 check does not reliably ensure the approval follows the relevant risk or PEP change. The code also treats a valid R1 exemption as a justification even when a qualifying review independently satisfies R1, which can blur the requested classification. These gaps matter because executable checking code is a substantial part of the requested output.

There are also judgment calls presented too confidently, especially around review scope and which changes make a review stale. Those choices are disclosed, but the formalization and code do not always stay aligned with them. The response is strong overall, but not close to flawless.
