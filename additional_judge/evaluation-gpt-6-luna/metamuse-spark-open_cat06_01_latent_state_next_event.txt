5.8 — The answer is detailed and generally process-aware, but several material errors weaken its reliability. It treats supplier analysis as mandatory or likely mandatory in C6-A based on three similar complaints, although the rule requires two or more complaints concerning the same lot or supplier component; that link is not established. It also says C6-E’s six complaints “transform” the case into mandatory Technical Investigation, though the stated rule mandates Supplier Analysis and does not itself require TI or RA for a low-severity case. Some predictions are not plausible immediate next log events: for example, C6-E ranks Supplier Analysis Completed despite the 6–36 hour duration and a second Evidence Review still pending. The answer also misuses DFG counts as branching evidence in places, including an unsupported “~15% branching” claim for Review → Customer Evidence Requested.

It does well to distinguish process state from feed visibility, especially for migration-affected C6-D, and to contrast C6-A with C6-B. However, it contains internal contradictions about whether missing investigation events indicate delay or that work has not started, and makes unsupported assumptions about queue priority and timing. These issues prevent a higher grade despite strong coverage and a broadly coherent structure.
