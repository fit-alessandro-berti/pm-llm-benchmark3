The shared mechanism is straightforward: when both checks can truly start and proceed independently, parallel execution may reduce time spent waiting for one check to finish before the other begins. Site A’s intended process change targets that waiting. Site B also has an identity check and document check before assessment, so this mechanism may apply in some cases.

Several conditions differ. Originals create a legal prerequisite for Document Check in 62% of B cases; separate roles, a single-task case lock, smaller specialized pools, and required legal review can constrain actual concurrency or shift the queue downstream. Site A also had an API upgrade, added document reviewers, queue huddles, and a simpler incoming mix. Those changes could explain part of its before/after improvement.

Effect modifiers include originals requirement, document channel, case complexity, legal-review status, pool capacity, case locking, and queue conditions. They influence whether parallelism is possible and whether reduced verification waiting translates into shorter total cycle time. A similar control-flow diagram does not establish similar task durations, staffing, legal gates, rework paths, or system behavior. Nor does it establish that the same observed change would result: Site A had no randomized control, and its evidence mixes the intervention with concurrent changes and changing case mix.

## 6. Candidate Site B intervention

Eligibility: begin with cases confirmed to be digital, not legally subject to originals receipt, and not requiring Legal Review. The reported 38% is only an upper bound; confirm actual eligibility from case-level data.

After Register, create Identity Check and Document Check tasks concurrently for eligible cases. For cases requiring originals, Identity Check and Originals Receipt may proceed concurrently after Register, but Document Check must wait for Originals Receipt. Assessment must wait until both checks are complete. Where Legal Review is required, it remains downstream of Assessment; supplements must retain the required repeat Document Check and Assessment path before the final decision.

Identity and document teams own their respective tasks; assessors and legal reviewers retain their specialist work. Use role-specific WIP limits and monitor queue length, utilization, and service time before increasing release rates. Support simultaneous task ownership in the case system for treatment cases. Record real-time task creation, start, completion, owner, prerequisite, and loop iteration events.

POWL-style partial order:

```text
Register < Identity Check
Register < Originals Receipt
Originals Receipt < Document Check    [when originals are required]
Identity Check < Assessment
Document Check < Assessment
Assessment < Legal Review             [when required]
Legal Review < (Approve XOR Reject)
Legal Review < Request Supplement     [optional]
Request Supplement < Document Check < Assessment  [repeat path]
(Approve XOR Reject) < Notify
```

## 7. Pilot for transport learning

| design element | specification | causal purpose | operational safeguard | process-mining measurement |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unit of assignment | Case, with assignment blocked within team and week | Estimate case-level treatment effect | Avoid assigning shared queues to different modes where spillover is likely | Case-level trace and assignment |
| Randomization or phased assignment | Randomize eligible cases 1:1 to parallel or current flow | Balance measured and unmeasured case factors | Keep assignment concealed until registration data are captured | Intention-to-treat comparison |
| Eligible strata | Digital, no originals gate, no legal-review requirement; record other strata observationally | Test the feasible shared mechanism first | Validate eligibility before assignment | Stratum-specific cycle and overlap |
| Treatment versions | Parallel task creation with system-supported dual ownership; control retains current sequence | Distinguish task ordering from IT implementation | Version and log any deviations | Treatment version, owner, timestamps |
| Primary and secondary outcomes | Primary: cycle time; secondary: pre-assessment wait, p90, service times, rework, appeals, and throughput | Capture speed, tail, and quality | Do not expand based on median alone | Full distributions and loop counts |
| Interference controls | Keep separate treatment and control queues or monitor shared-pool exposure | Detect displacement and spillover | WIP caps and queue alerts | Queue length and utilization by pool |
| Power or minimum-support logic | Pre-specify a power calculation using baseline variance; require at least 600 cases per arm as a provisional minimum, then verify support by strata | Avoid unsupported subgroup claims | Extend accrual if minimum support is unmet | Confidence intervals and minimum cell counts |
| Duration | Eight weeks or until minimum support is reached, whichever is later | Cover operating variation | Weekly safety review | Accrual and time trend |
| Stop rules | Pause if p90 rises over 15% versus control, rework rises by more than 2 percentage points, or a statutory prerequisite is breached | Limit operational and quality harm | Independent review of triggers | Alerts by arm and event audit |
| Transportability analysis | Estimate effects by channel and complexity; compare eligible pilot cases with noneligible cases descriptively | Identify effect modification and limits of transport | Do not extrapolate randomized effects to unsupported groups | Stratum-specific effects and case-mix weighting |

Randomization within the feasible digital, no-legal stratum tests whether concurrency reduces waiting when it can actually occur. System-supported ownership and audited event timestamps separate a failed process mechanism from a failure to implement real overlap. Pool utilization, queue lengths, and service times show whether any time saved upstream is displaced into assessment or document queues. A concurrent rise in recorded overlap without audited starts would point to a logging problem. A rise in true overlap without a reduction in waiting would suggest that the targeted wait is not the main constraint. The other strata should be logged for future transport analysis, but this pilot does not identify their causal effects.

## 8. PM4Py model-construction code

```python
import pm4py
from pm4py.objects.powl.obj import StrictPartialOrder, Transition, SilentTransition

# Site A before and after, using PM4Py process-tree syntax.
tree_a_before = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register','Identity Check','Document Check','Assessment',"
    "X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')"
)
net_a_before, im_a_before, fm_a_before = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_before)

tree_a_after = pm4py.parse_process_tree(
    "->('Register',+('Identity Check','Document Check'),'Assessment',"
    "X('Approve','Reject'),'Notify')"
)
net_a_after, im_a_after, fm_a_after = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(tree_a_after)

# Site B candidate: strict ordering for prerequisites and a modeled supplement
# repeat path. Legal and originals conditions are data-dependent in reality.
register = Transition("Register")
identity = Transition("Identity Check")
originals = Transition("Originals Receipt")
document = Transition("Document Check")
assessment = Transition("Assessment")
legal = Transition("Legal Review")
supplement = Transition("Request Supplement")
approve = Transition("Approve")
reject = Transition("Reject")
notify = Transition("Notify")
silent = SilentTransition()

nodes = [
    register, identity, originals, document, assessment, legal,
    supplement, approve, reject, notify, silent
]
powl_b = StrictPartialOrder(nodes=nodes)

# Explicit prerequisite edges: Assessment waits for both checks.
powl_b.add_edge(register, identity)
powl_b.add_edge(register, originals)
powl_b.add_edge(originals, document)
powl_b.add_edge(identity, assessment)
powl_b.add_edge(document, assessment)

# Legal review follows assessment; supplement repeats document check and assessment.
powl_b.add_edge(assessment, legal)
powl_b.add_edge(legal, supplement)
powl_b.add_edge(supplement, document)

# Decisions follow legal review in this simplified candidate.
powl_b.add_edge(legal, approve)
powl_b.add_edge(legal, reject)
powl_b.add_edge(approve, notify)
powl_b.add_edge(reject, notify)

net_b, im_b, fm_b = pm4py.convert_to_petri_net(powl_b)

print("A before workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_before))
print("A after workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_a_after))
print("B workflow net:", pm4py.check_is_workflow_net(net_b))
print("B soundness:", pm4py.check_soundness(net_b, im_b, fm_b))

# This partial model does not encode data-dependent eligibility, resource
# capacities, case locks, or event-time reliability.
```

## 9. Recommendation

**Conditional go.** Pilot system-supported parallel verification for the digital, no-originals, no-legal-review stratum, whose share is at most 38% on the reported marginal counts. Keep the statutory receipt-before-document gate and the legal-review and supplement paths intact. Use randomized assignment, role-specific WIP controls, and audited real-time events. Treat a median reduction as insufficient for expansion if p90, rework, or downstream queues worsen. Site A’s nine-hour median change is not an identified treatment effect and should not be used as a point forecast for Site B.

## 10. Assumption register

1. Digital cases have no unobserved originals prerequisite — test via legal audit; otherwise cases may be assigned ineligible work.
2. The case lock can support dual ownership for pilot cases — test in a system trial; otherwise true overlap may not occur.
3. Assessment capacity remains adequate — test via utilization and queue length; otherwise the bottleneck may shift.
4. Supplement frequency is not increased by parallelization — test loop rates by arm; otherwise rework may rise.
5. WIP limits are followed — test open tasks per reviewer; otherwise queues may saturate.
6. Timestamps reflect actual work — audit start and completion events; otherwise overlap estimates are biased.
7. Case mix remains stable — monitor complexity and channel; otherwise comparisons may drift.
8. External API conditions remain stable — monitor query latency; otherwise identity service changes confound the estimate.
