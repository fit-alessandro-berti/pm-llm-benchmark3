8/10 — The answer gives a grounded, segmented package of process changes and supports it with path-level evidence, policy findings, and service, quality, resilience, and environmental guardrails. It handles trade-offs well: it preserves emergency options, limits batching by waiting time and demand confidence, addresses rework and packaging, and identifies displacement and rebound risks. The validation plan is specific and tied to the event attributes and observed baselines.

The main weakness is that some recommendations are more prescriptive than the evidence supports. For example, moving precautionary Critical orders toward the stock/ground exception assumes those orders resemble the observed 3% path, and priority picking could shift delays to Regular customers. The answer recognizes these risks, but does not quantify how much change is safe or specify a pilot threshold for each intervention.
